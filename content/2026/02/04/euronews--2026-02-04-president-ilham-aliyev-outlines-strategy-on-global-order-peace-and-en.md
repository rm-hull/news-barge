## Speaking at Davos, President Ilham Aliyev outlines Azerbaijan’s response to global geopolitical shifts, progress towards peace in the South Caucasus and expanding energy cooperation with Europe.

President Ilham Aliyev joins Euronews in Davos to discuss the changing global order and Azerbaijan’s response to growing geopolitical uncertainty. He stresses the importance of strong regional ties, strategic independence and respect for international law. The president points to new opportunities for peace and cooperation in the South Caucasus, alongside Azerbaijan’s increasing role in European energy supply. The conversation highlights Azerbaijan’s focus on stability, long-term security and constructive engagement at a time of global transition.
