Eleven people have been killed, including four children, in an overnight attack on holiday camps in a Russian-held part of Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia region, the Russian-installed local authorities said on Saturday.

Kyiv has not immediately responded to the incident.

A Russian ballistic missile strike on Friday killed 10 people and injured close to 100 at the site of a defence industry event in Ukraine's Kyiv region, Ukrainian prosecutor general Ruslan Kravchenko said.

Ukraine's emergency services said its crews had completed rescue operations at the site north of the capital. Russia is yet to comment on the attack.

This comes as five people were killed and nine injured in the city of Sloviansk in eastern Ukraine in a Russian aerial bomb attack killed, regional governor Vadym Filashkin said on Friday.

Earlier Ukraine attacked Russian online retail giant Wildberries’ warehouses in St Petersburg on Friday, which Kyiv said is part of infrastructure supporting the Russian military.

## Recap: Eleven people killed in attacks on holiday camps

Eleven people have been killed in attacks on holiday camps in the partly Russian-occupied Zaporizhzhia region, the Russian-installed local authorities said on Saturday.

The strikes followed a major Russian attack on Friday near Kyiv that killed at least 10 people and wounded around 100 others.

Four children were among 11 people killed in the attack on the resorts in Zaporizhzhia, Yevgeny Balitsky, the Kremlin-installed regional head, said, adding that 16 others were wounded.

## Romania shoots down suspected Shahed drone in its airspace for first time

Romania shot down a foreign drone in its airspace on Friday in the first interception of its kind, amid growing concern over UAVs straying into Nato territory.

An F-16 fighter jet shot down the drone near the town of Padina, in the east, at around 11am after it entered the country’s airspace, officials said, adding that an investigation was underway.

## Recap: Romania shoots down second drone in two days

Romania’s defense ministry said its forces had “safely shot down” a drone that breached its airspace in an “unpopulated area” near the Ukrainian border.

The drone was “shot down by a Romanian pilot from an F-16 aircraft,” President Nicușor Dan said in a post on Facebook.

It came after a drone was shot down around Padina, northeast of the Romanian capital, Bucharest, on Friday.

## Recap: Italy defence minister offers ousted Ukraine counterpart Fedorov adviser role

Italian Defence Minister Guido Crosetto said he has offered Mykhailo Fedorov, recently sacked as Ukraine's defence minister, a role as an adviser in Italy.

"I called him the day after his dismissal and said: When do you want to come to Rome and work with me as an adviser?" Crosetto told Italian daily la Repubblica in an interview published on Saturday.

Asked how Fedorov had responded, Crosetto said the former minister was "moved" and saw the offer as "a demonstration of esteem and friendship".

Crosetto praised Fedorov as a driver of military innovation, saying he was "one of those people who completely rewrote the rules of the game on the battlefield". Fedorov had helped Ukraine first resist Russia's invasion and then regain the initiative by reshaping battlefield practices through new technologies, Crossetto said.

Fedorov, a 35-year-old tech-savvy reformer credited by supporters with boosting Ukraine's drone capabilities and pushing defence-sector reforms, was ousted as defence minister this month, a move that sparked rare wartime protests in Ukraine.

He was offered alternative roles by President Volodymyr Zelenskiy, but said he would not accept any other government role besides his former job.
