*(Image: North Yorkshire Weather Updates)*

Firefighters are battling a blaze at a farm building close to a North Yorkshire air base.

Several crews from across the area have responded to tackle the fire which broke out this afternoon on a farm near RAF Leeming.

People living in the area are being advised to keep their doors and windows shut as putrid, thick black smoke can be seen for miles.

  North Yorkshire crews tackle fire near RAF Leeming at an agricultural building. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Weather Updates)*

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: "We currently have five fire appliances from Bedale, Northallerton, Richmond, Thirsk and Ripon and a water bowser on scene at an agricultural building fire.

Recommended reads:

"Crews currently have six breathing apparatus and four hose reel jets in use to extinguish the fire. The building measures approximately 50 x 30 metres.

"We have received reports that smoke is blowing over the A1, please try avoid the area if possible."

  Several crews are tackling a large fire at an agricultural building by RAF Leeming in North Yorkshire. 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Weather Updates)*

RAF Leeming - home to the Hawk Mk 167 and Grob Tutor T1 - is not thought to be affected by the agricultural building fire.

Drivers are being urged to try to avoid the area if possible due to reports that smoke is blowing over the A1.
