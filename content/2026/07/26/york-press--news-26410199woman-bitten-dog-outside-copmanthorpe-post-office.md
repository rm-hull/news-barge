## Woman bitten by dog outside York village post office - police want to trace owner

North Yorkshire Police want to trace a dog's owner after it bit a woman  outside Copmanthorpe post office. *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A woman was bitten by a dog outside a post office in York.

The dog bite pierced the woman's skin, resulting in her needing antibiotics and tetanus jabs at the hospital.

Police are now appealing for information about the incident happened on Friday, July 3, at about 11.40am, outside the Post Office in Copmanthorpe.

  The incident happened on Friday, July 3, at about 11.40am, outside the Post Office in Copmanthorpe. 

*(Image: Google Maps)*

They want to find the dog's owner so they can speak to her about what happened and have shared a description of the owner as there is limited CCTV footage of the incident.

  **Recommended reads:**

The owner is described as elderly, white, slim, and using a walking frame.

The dog, which was on a lead, has been described as a small bichon frise, possibly a crossbreed.

Anyone who has information that could help with the investigation should email [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

People can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101, choose option two, and ask for Nick Harrison.

Anyone who wants to remain anonymous can pass on information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

When providing information, please mention reference number 12260125248.
