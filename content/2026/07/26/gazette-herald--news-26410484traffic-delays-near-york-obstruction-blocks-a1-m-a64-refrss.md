Drivers are being warned to expect delays on a major road near York due to an obstruction on the carriageway.

  Traffic delays near York after obstruction blocks A1(M) to A64 - pictured, traffic on the affected route. 

*(Image: Google Maps)*

The partial blockage is affecting traffic in both directions between the M1 and junction 44 of the A1(M), heading onto the A64 (York/Tadcaster) .

The Highways Agency has reported that traffic is moving slowly through the affected area.

The disruption began shortly before 11am and is expected to be cleared by 11.24am.

The incident comes as westbound traffic on the A64 near Tadcaster is delayed by an accident that emergency services are dealing with.
