Bordeaux City Council is considering a total evacuation of the city, a spokesperson told Sky News on Sunday.

The Council is "currently studying scenarios for all possible ways the fire could evolve", they said, which includes a total evacuation of the city, if needed.

Surrounding areas have been evacuated as the fire advanced amid high winds.

French interior minister said that the blaze was “extremely virulent and unpredictable again, generating its own wind and spreading erratically toward the Bordeaux metropolitan area” on Saturday night but had calmed “somewhat” overnight. The situation “remains highly unfavourable”, he added.

Bordeaux is the sixth largest in France when including both the city proper and surrounding metropolitan area and is home to over 1.2 million people.

One man died in a wildfire in Manises, near the Spanish city of Valencia, the country's interior ministry said. He was found in a car in a ravine hundreds of kilometres away from the severe wildfires near Madrid.

Around 300,000 people have been displaced including some 70,000 people evacuated in central Spain.

“The fires which are hitting our country have reached a level that has never been seen before,” French prime minister Sebastien Lecornu wrote on X on Saturday.
