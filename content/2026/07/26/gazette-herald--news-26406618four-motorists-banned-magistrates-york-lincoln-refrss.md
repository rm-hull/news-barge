Four motorists from York and North Yorkshire are off the road after they appeared before magistrates for driving offences.

Three appeared before York Magistrates Court.

Kirsty Harrison, 46, of Dixon Gardens, Selby, pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified and without insurance in Selby.

She was banned from driving for a further six months, fined £120 and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £48 statutory surcharge.

David Udakis, 5, of Grange Road, Tadcaster, pleaded guilty to failure to provide a specimen of blood when suspected of drink or drug driving and failure to provide a breath test at the roadside.

He must wear an alcohol abstinence tag for 80 days, banned from driving for 17 months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

William Stubbs, 30, of Murton near York, pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified, cocaine driving, driving with a cocaine derivative in his body, driving without insurance and driving a vehicle without a valid MOT, all committed at the same time on Water End, York.

He was given a 12-month community order with 10 days’ rehabilitative activities and banned from driving for three years.

Alfie Purvis, 21, of Chudleigh Road, off Leeman Road, York, appeared before Lincoln Magistrates Court. He pleaded guilty to failure to provide a breath specimen in Tattersall, south Lincolnshire, when suspected of drink driving.

He was given a 12-month community order with 120 hours’ unpaid work and banned from driving for 38 months. He must pay £85 prosecution costs.
