Drivers are facing increasing delays on the A64.

Traffic is building on the westbound carriageway near Tadcaster between the A162 and Paradise Lane.

Motorists are currently being delayed by more than 17 minutes, with an average speed of 5mph due to the congestion heading towards the A1(M).

  The traffic situation on the A64 westbound at 11.35am, Sunday, July 26. 

*(Image: Google Maps)*

One person who has been held up has posted on social media: "Stuck on A64 in a massive traffic jam - nothing moving."

Another motorist has reported that the outside lane is closed just before junction 44 to the A1, with slow-moving traffic.

She wrote on social media: "Just one car as we've just gone past, only police there and driver still in car, traffic moving freely once passed."
