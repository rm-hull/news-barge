Thieves have stolen tools, a generator, a water pump, and a cement mixer from a North Yorkshire farm.

Police are urging witnesses to come forward following a burglary at The Barn, Ruddings Farm, Wetherby Lane, Kirk Deighton.

It happened sometime between Monday, July 20 and Friday, July, 24, when thieves broke into a metal storage container.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "This is a rural location, but we're keen to hear from anyone who was in the area and may have seen anything unusual. Did you notice any suspicious vehicles, people, or activity around Ruddings Farm during this period?"

Police are also asking people to check any CCTV, doorbell, or dashcam footage from those dates.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting incident number NYP-24072026-0230.
