# Gatwick Airport travellers hit by major water supply issue

- Gatwick Airport is currently experiencing a severe water outage affecting both its North and South terminals.
- The disruption means passengers are unable to flush toilets, and hand-washing facilities are out of use.
- Bottled water is being distributed to travellers and staff across the airport.
- SES Water, the airport's supplier, has identified an issue at its Bough Beech Water Treatment Works as the cause of the problem, leading to low pressure or loss of supply for some customers.
- The airport has apologised for the inconvenience, with a spokesperson saying that contingency measures are in place for passenger welfare.
