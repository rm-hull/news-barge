# Urgent warning after 'injectable end of life medication' stolen in Redcar

## An urgent warning has been issued by Cleveland Police after potentially lethal medication was stolen from an address in Redcar.

The midazolam and morphine - which were reported as stolen from the property on Charles Street in Redcar on Saturday July 25 - are both injectable end of life medication.

They could cause serious harm in the wrong hands and anyone who may have taken it should report to A&E immediately, police said.

Anyone with information should call 101, quoting reference SE26147136.
