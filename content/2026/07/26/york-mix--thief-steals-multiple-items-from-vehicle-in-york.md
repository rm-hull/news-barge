Police have issued this CCTV after a theft in York.

They want to talk to this man after “multiple items” were stolen from a car on **Blue Bridge Lane**.

It happened at **3.42am** on**Thursday, 16 July**.

#### Blue Bridge Lane, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the male pictured on CCTV, as he may have information that will assist our investigation.

“We would also ask if anyone has any CCTV around this area to please check if you have captured the male also around the same time.”
