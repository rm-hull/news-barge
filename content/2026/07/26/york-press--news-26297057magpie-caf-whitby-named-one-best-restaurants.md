A seaside restaurant at a popular resort in Yorkshire has been named one of the best in the region.

The Telegraph has released a list of the best restaurants in Yorkshire with the Magpie Café in Whitby getting a mention.

The restaurant is said to be "known" throughout North Yorkshire and is described as being "one of the finest fish restaurants" in the region.

Many people consider the Magpie Café to be a must-visit if you are in the area with it being mentioned in the Good Food Guide.

  The restaurant is a must-visit if you are ever going to the seaside town. 

*(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## Why do people love the Whitby Café?

On the seaside restaurant, The Telegraph said: "There's nothing small, quiet or dainty about this long-established, family-run fish restaurant that sprawls over three higgledy-piggledy floors in a black-and-white building on Whitby's quayside.

"Fish portions are as huge as the menu (around 20 varieties, from monkfish to turbot) while the waitresses are disarmingly motherly.

"And you really need to save room for their puddings; the crumble (with custard, naturally) is one of the best, and one portion is easily big enough for two.

"If fully booked, their takeaway fish and chip shop next door cooks to restaurant standards."


The restaurant has a huge variety of fish dishes from its starter including calamari, fishcakes, and a seafood pot served with clams, scallops, salmon, and mussels in a wine, butter, and garlic sauce.

The fish dishes continue to the mains with the classic fish and chips that is a seaside staple and comes with Magpie tartare sauce.

You can also create your own fish platter with a range of sides and fish available from haddock and plaice to sea bass.

For those who do not like fish, there are a few meat and vegetarian dishes, so everyone is catered for.

The restaurant even does a cream tea if you fancy enjoying some scones and a pot of Yorkshire tea by the seaside.

## What do visitors of the Magpie Café say?

On Tripadvisor, one reviewer said: "This restaurant provides the best experience of traditional fish & chips in Whitby.

"Excellent menu with multiple choices at reasonable prices.

"Definite visit when you are next in the town."

Another reviewer said: "The best fish in the country!

"Have been several times and never disappoints.

"Classic fish and chips is the best of the best but they have such a wide range of speciality dishes which will be sure to make you return."
