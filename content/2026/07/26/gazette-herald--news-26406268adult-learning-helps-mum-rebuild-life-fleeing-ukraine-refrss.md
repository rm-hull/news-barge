A mother-of-two who fled Ukraine has been praised for rebuilding her life through adult learning.

Olha Honcharuk, now living in Boroughbridge, arrived in the UK in December 2022 with her two children and mother after escaping the conflict with Russia.

She began learning English and completing qualifications through North Yorkshire Council’s adult learning services.

Mrs Honcharuk said: "When I arrived in the UK, everything was new and unfamiliar.

"I had to rebuild my life with my children and mother from the beginning."

She started at entry level and worked her way up to achieving a Level 3 qualification in accounting.

She now works as a finance officer at Boroughbridge Primary School and plans to continue her studies with a Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA) qualification.

Named one of 22 winners in North Yorkshire Council’s annual adult learning awards, Mrs Honcharuk received the Employability and Work Readiness Award.

Cllr Annabel Wilkinson, the council’s executive member for education, learning and skills, said: "The award winners demonstrate the life-changing impact that adult learning can have."

In Whitby, coastguard volunteer Paul Thomas was also recognised for his commitment to learning.

He enrolled on a Level 2 functional skills maths course after seeing it on social media.

Mr Thomas said: "I would highly recommend the council’s adult learning courses to others.

"The combination of structured learning and in-person tutor support is ideal for adults looking to develop their skills and opportunities."

Since August last year, the service has supported more than 3,050 residents with over 6,000 course enrolments.
