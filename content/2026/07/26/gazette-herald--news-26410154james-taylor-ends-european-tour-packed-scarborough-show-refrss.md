James Taylor closed his European tour with a two-hour set of timeless classics to a sold-out crowd at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Saturday night (July 25).

The legendary American singer-songwriter, performing with his acclaimed All-Star Band, told thousands of fans it was his first time at the coastal venue.

  James Taylor at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, Saturday, July 25, 2026. 

*(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

He said: “This is our first time here at this beautiful venue for what is the final date of our European Tour. Thank you for coming out tonight Scarborough, it means so much.”

  James Taylor at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, Saturday, July 25, 2026. 

*(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

Many in the audience had travelled from across the UK and beyond, and were rewarded with a career-spanning performance featuring some of Taylor’s most celebrated songs. Highlights included Mexico, Don’t Let Me Be Lonely Tonight and T-Bone, alongside enduring favourites Sweet Baby James, Carolina In My Mind and Fire and Rain.

One of the standout moments came with a moving rendition of You’ve Got A Friend, written by Taylor’s long-time friend Carole King, followed by another King-penned classic, Up On The Roof.

  James Taylor at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, Saturday, July 25, 2026. 

*(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

The show marked another major night in the TK Maxx presents Scarborough Open Air Theatre 2026 season, which has already welcomed a string of big names this summer, including Rick Astley, Madness, Paul Weller, Alanis Morissette, Michael Bublé, Bastille, and Richard Ashcroft.

The series continues tonight, Sunday (July 26) with Sir Tom Jones taking to the stage, before further headline performances from The Streets, Sex Pistols, Scissor Sisters, Hollywood Vampires and Nile Rodgers & Chic in the weeks ahead.
