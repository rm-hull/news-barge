# Marvel Studios announces new Ghost Rider film starring Ryan Gosling

- Marvel Studios has announced that a new *Ghost Rider* film is in development and will be released in 2028.
- The film will star Ryan Gosling as the motorbike-riding hero, and will be directed by Shawn Levy, who also directed *Deadpool & Wolverine* and*Free Guy.*
- The pair are currently working together on the upcoming *Star Wars: Starfighter* film, and Levy said that he and Gosling spent a lot of their downtime on set swapping ideas about*Ghost Rider.*
- “As Ryan started talking about his vision for this character, we started riffing ... and I finally said, “Bro, let’s ride’,” Levy said.
- The announcement was made at the San Diego Comic-Con, with the audience loudly cheering when Gosling and Levy stepped on stage.
