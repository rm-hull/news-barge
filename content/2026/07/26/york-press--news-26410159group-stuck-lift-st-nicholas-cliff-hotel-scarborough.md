Firefighters went to the rescue after five people got trapped in a lift in a North Yorkshire hotel.

  **Recommended reads:**

  North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said it was called to St Nicholas Cliff in **Scarborough** to help the group who were unable to get out of a lift in a hotel.

The alarm was raised at 6.26pm on Saturday evening, July 24.

Crews used lift keys to free the occupants who were unharmed.
