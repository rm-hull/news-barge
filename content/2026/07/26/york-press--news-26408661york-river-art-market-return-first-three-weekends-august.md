## Award-winning York event set to return to city centre next weekend

 *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

AN AWARD-WINNING free art event in York is expecting more exhibitors than ever before as it returns to the city centre very soon.

York River Art Market (YRAM) is set to return to Dame Judi Dench Walk for three consecutive weekends of art in the heart of the city next week.

Running over the first three weekends in August, the open-air market will feature a range of artists - some attending every day and others just once, offering visitors the chance to explore and purchase original artwork.

As reported by The Press in January, the event was originally going to end after last year's event due to former owner Charlotte Dawson's work commitments - before it was sold to Caroline Turner, a professional potter and ceramics teacher.

She said: "I'd helped Charlotte with the event a couple of years, and then she ended up having work commitments and wasn't going to run it. She asked me if I'd like to buy it, so I did and she handed the whole thing over.

"I'm learning how much work goes into creating an award winning event like this - Charlotte had very big boots to fill and it's been a learning curve, but I feel excited now it's so close and we're really looking forward to it."

Caroline said the event has attracted a wide mix of artists and makers, with a number of new artists expected to sell their work for the first time at the market.

The line-up includes everything from contemporary paintings and watercolours to ceramics, jewellery, woodwork, and glass, with some artists who will be creating work on the day as they sell.

  Caroline Turner in her Walmgate studio 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

There are new changes to the layout of the market, which means that they will be able to accommodate more artists by having people on both sides of the path.

She said: "We're planning to open up both sides of the pathway this year and we've got quite a few additional pitches.

"On one side, we'll have our 2D artists and prints on the railings, with jewellers and ceramics on the other side - we've looked at the type of art they do online to work out where to place them and what will suit their work better.

"I think that should be successful and it'll create a more immersive environment as people walk through through the market."

The market runs from 10am to 5.30pm on each of the six dates.

  The art market runs along Dame Judi Dench Walk in York 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

In the lead-up to the market, York arts venue Spark: York is hosting a special launch event and exhibition on July 30, from 6.30pm to 9pm, which will give visitors a preview of what to expect during the market weekends.

Caroline said: "Spark are very kindly hosting a launch event next Thursday (July 3) from 6.30pm till 9pm where people can meet the artists and talk to them.

"I feel the pressure of the years before and I want it to be as successful as it was under Charlotte, but I'm just looking forward to running my first one and see what will work well."

The York River Art Market will take place over the first three consecutive weekends in August, with stalls open from 10am to 5.30pm each day.
