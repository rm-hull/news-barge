Parents looking to keep children fed over the long summer holidays can once again pick up free fruit for youngsters at Tesco, as the supermarket revives one of its most popular family initiatives.

Until August 30, children can collect a free apple while shopping in more than 800 Tesco supermarkets and 77 large Tesco Express stores across the UK. Families using Click & Collect will also receive free apples for children at selected locations.

The move comes as millions of families face the extra cost of feeding children during the six-week school break, when free school meals are unavailable for many households.

But the free fruit isn't the only support on offer.

## Tesco's Kids Eat Free offer returns

Tesco is also extending its popular Kids Eat Free café promotion throughout the summer holidays.

Families can claim one free Kids Hot Meal Deal, Kids Breakfast or Kids Pick 'N' Mix meal with the purchase of any adult café item from just 60p, provided they scan a Tesco Clubcard or Clubcard app.

The offer is available in 317 Tesco cafés across the UK and now runs every day of the week, including weekends, until September 1.


## Extra support for families

Tesco has also announced several new initiatives aimed at helping households struggling with the cost of living this summer.

These include:

- Pre-filled food donation bags costing between £2 and £4, available in larger stores to support food charities Felix and Trussell.
- A Round Up at the Till campaign running from 3 August to 9 August, allowing shoppers to donate spare change at checkout.
- Continued investment in its Free Fruit & Veg for Schools programme, which already supports more than 500 schools.

The supermarket says the measures are designed to help children who may miss out on free school meals during the holidays.

## Why Tesco is offering free fruit

The announcement comes amid growing concern about holiday hunger.

According to Trussell, food bank support for children remains 34% higher than before the pandemic, while more than 900,000 emergency food parcels for children were distributed through its network last year.

Research from Felix also found that 41% of charities receiving redistributed food have seen demand rise over the past year, with many expecting even greater pressure during the school holidays.


## Tesco: 'Every little bit helps'

Christine Heffernan, Tesco's Chief Communications and Sustainability Officer, said: "We want to be able to support our charity partners to help those needing a little extra support this summer. Our partnerships with Felix and Trussell are central to this aim.

"When you're out doing your weekly shop this summer, dropping a few tins into a food collection point, picking up a food donation bag in our stores or rounding up your grocery bill can make a difference to the lives of thousands of children in communities across the UK."

## How to claim Tesco's free fruit

Children can pick up a free apple at participating Tesco stores until August 30.

To claim the Kids Eat Free café offer, customers simply need to:

- Buy any adult café item from 60p
- Scan a Tesco Clubcard or Clubcard app
- Have a child present
- Visit before September 1

With supermarket prices and childcare costs continuing to squeeze household budgets, the offers could help families save money on trips to Tesco throughout the summer holidays.
