Gatwick Airport experienced a significant water supply issue on Sunday, leading to the closure of most toilets and many food and beverage outlets across both terminals.

The problem, caused by an issue at the Bough Beech Water Treatment Works, resulted in taps either not running or having very low pressure.

Airport staff distributed bottled water to travellers, and additional personnel were deployed to manage the situation and minimise disruption.

Despite the widespread inconvenience, all flights at Gatwick Airport continued to depart as scheduled throughout the incident.

By Sunday evening, water supply had been largely restored, with most toilets reopened and food and beverage outlets expected to be fully operational by Monday morning.
