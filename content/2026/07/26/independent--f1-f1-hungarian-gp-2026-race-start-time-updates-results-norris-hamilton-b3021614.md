Lando Norris landed the first pole position of his world championship defence after he beat Lewis Hamilton to top spot for the Hungarian Grand Prix by just 0.012 seconds.

Hamilton appeared on course to end a three-year run without taking pole only to be usurped by Norris in the closing moments of a thrilling qualifying session at the Hungaroring.

It seemed like Hamilton had to settle for second, a place ahead of his team-mate Charles Leclerc, until he was docked three places by the stewards for impeding Oscar Piastri in what looked like a slam-dunk incident.

But he still has a strong chance of taking a record-extending ninth win at a venue he has so often ruled, and slash Kimi Antonelli’s advantage at the summit of the world championship standings - who was also hit with a three-place grid penalty after qualifying.

*Follow live updates from the Hungarian GP with The Independent*

## Hamilton pits again (Lap 32/70)

Ferrari would’ve liked to get Lewis Hamilton out on fresh tyres in front of Red Bull’s Isaac Hadjar... but that is not the case. Max Verstappen’s team-mate could end up really holding him up here.

## Norris goes wide again (Lap 29/70)

Lando Norris has been going wide on a fair few turns and Oscar Piastri is now opening the gap on his McLaren team-mate.

## Russell pits amid fightback (Lap 28/70)

George Russell has fought back to seventh but has opted to pit to get on the hard tyres and get some grip.

He gets away pretty slowly, hinting at maybe more issues with the Mercedes car.

## Gap opens behind McLarens (Lap 24/70)

Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris are now comfortably leading the chasing trio of Max Verstappen, Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc.

Kimi Antonelli, who has just received a warning for track limits, is five seconds behind Leclerc in sixth.

## Antonelli pits (Lap 23/70)

Kimi Antonelli has now decided to switch to the hard tyres, putting Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris back into the one-two.

The Mercedes man now has six laps of fresher tyres than Piastri.

## Bottas out (Lap 22/70)

In case there was any questions over Valtteri Bottas’ involvement in this race, he will DNF after his Cadillac developed a break cooling issue.

## Piastri and Norris stay ahead of Verstappen as Antonelli takes lead (Lap 19/70)

Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris are back in the action and came out ahead of Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton, with the pair blasting past Isaac Hadjar.

Kimi Antonelli is in the lead by 12.4 seconds but still needs to pit.

## Cadillac issue delays Piastri pit (Lap 18/70)

There’s a yellow flag in the pit lane after Valtteri Bottas' Cadillac very slowly enters the pit lane slip road with his car’s brakes on fire.

He uses the wall to slow him down and is still not out.

McLaren had called Oscar Piastri into the pits from the race lead but quickly changed their minds, delaying it for another lap. Both Piastri and Lando Norris have now changed to the hard tyres.
