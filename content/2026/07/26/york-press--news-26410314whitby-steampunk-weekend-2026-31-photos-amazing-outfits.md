## Whitby Steampunk Weekend: 31 photos of amazing outfits as crowds gather

Whitby Steampunk Weekend 2026. *(Image: Simon McCabe Photography)*

Thousands of people have descended on Whitby for the town's colourful Steampunk Weekend.

Visitors in elaborate Victorian-inspired costumes have gathered for the return of the popular festival.

  Whitby Steampunk Weekend 2026. 

*(Image: Garry Hornby)*

  Photographers were spoiled for choice as Whitby’s streets and the pavilion filled with corsets, goggles and top hats, with many of the most eye-catching outfits captured on camera by The Press Camera Club's talented members **Garry Hornby** and**Simon McCabe** throughout the weekend.

The three-day event, held at Whitby Pavilion from Friday, July 24 to today, Sunday, July 26, is widely regarded as one of the largest free steampunk gatherings in the UK, attracting crowds from across the country.

  Whitby Steampunk Weekend 2026. 

*(Image: Simon McCabe Photography)*

Organisers said the family-friendly festival, first launched in 2017, once again transformed the seaside venue into a hub of “fanciful extravagance”, with around 70 traders offering clothing, accessories, and handmade curiosities.

  Whitby Steampunk Weekend 2026. 

*(Image: Simon McCabe Photography)*

Alongside the retail emporium, visitors are being treated to a packed programme of talks, workshops, and live entertainment.

Highlights have included presentations on Whitby’s ghosts and legends, Vikings and vampires, Viking mythology, the town's links to Bram Stoker, and the history of Victorian funeral customs.

  Whitby Steampunk Weekend 2026. 

*(Image: Simon McCabe Photography)*

Experts and guest speakers have included author and parapsychologist Paul Fitz-George, funeral director Emma Sparre-Newman covering how and why Victorian social conditions gave rise to the modern day funeral director and shaped so many funeral customs, and curators from Whitby Museum showcasing unusual artefacts from their collections.

Entertainment extended beyond the pavilion over the weekend, with performances from Ravens Morris from East Yorkshire, and sea shanty group Whitby Tillermen Friends drawing crowds outdoors.

  Whitby Steampunk Weekend 2026. 

*(Image: Simon McCabe Photography)*

Evening events included a charity quiz and a tribute concert at the Whitby Pavilion Theatre featuring the Duran Duran Experience and Ant Trouble – Adam & The Ants Tribute.

Organiser Andy from WSW Events encouraged visitors to attend, saying: "Don’t wait for all the pictures to appear on social media and wish you’d come—make the effort and enjoy a day to remember."

The event, which offers free daytime entry, is held every February and July.
