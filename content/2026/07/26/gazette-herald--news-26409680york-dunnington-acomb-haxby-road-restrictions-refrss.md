Road closures will be put in place in and around York next month due to essential works.

Eastfield Lane in Dunnington will experience a temporary traffic restriction starting at 7.30am on Monday, August 3. The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until 5pm on Friday, September 11, as drainage mains works are carried out.

Water Lane in Dunnington will also be affected by road closures. The restrictions are set to begin at 9am on Monday, August 3, and will end at 4pm on Tuesday, August 4, for highway maintenance works.

In York, Bishopthorpe Road and Ebor Street will see temporary traffic restrictions starting at 9am on Monday, August 3. The restrictions are expected to remain in place until 7pm on Saturday, August 15, during traffic signal renewal works.

Yearsley Grove and Whenby Grove will have restrictions from 9am on Tuesday, August 4, until 4pm on Wednesday, August 5, for highway maintenance works.

  In Acomb, there will be a temporary restriction for Birkdale Grove from 8am on Tuesday, August 4 and ending at 6pm on Monday, August 10 during fuse upgrade works, along with temporary restrictions in West Bank, Acomb from 8am on Tuesday, August 4 until 6pm on Wednesday, August 12 during fuse upgrade works.

  

  In Haxby, Wheatfield Lane will be affected from 8am on Wednesday, August 5 until 5pm on Friday, August 14 during gas mains works.

Motorists are advised to plan their journeys accordingly to avoid delays.
