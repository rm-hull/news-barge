*Love Island USA* season eight star Kenzie Annis has fired back at rumors that she supports Donald Trump.

Speculation about the 24-year-old Georgia nursing school graduate’s political views has swirled for months after pictures surfaced online that appeared to show Annis standing in front of Trump flags after she was cast in the Peacock dating show.

Annis responded to the rumors in an expletive-filled rant during an Instagram livestream Thursday, saying: “That’s so funny ‘cause I’m not a Trump supporter, so you guys can f***the f*** off.”

“Just because I’m blonde and white doesn’t mean I’m a f***ing Trump supporter. What the hell?” she said.

Annis joined the wildly popular season of*Love Island USA* on the first day, and was dumped from the villa by America’s vote just days before the finale.

![Kenzie Annis has denied rumors that she supports Donald Trump](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/05/28/22/14/Peacock_LoveIsland_S8_CharacterPortrait_TitleSocial_TEXTLESS_1920x1080_KENZIE.png)

She left the show with her final connection, Dylan Wrona, after previously being coupled up with Zach Georgiou, Corbin Mims and Caleb McDaniel.

Since leaving the show, Annis and Wrona have made their relationship official.

Wrona, 24, wrote in an Instagram post Friday: “Getting to know you in the villa was unforgettable, but falling for you in the real world was all too easy. It didn’t take me long to realize there was no other way — I had to make you my girlfriend! And what better place to do it than on the top of NYC.”

“I’m so excited for all the memories we are going to make,” he added.

Before Wrona entered the season as a Casa Amor bombshell, Annis was known on the show for her sex positivity as well as her tendency to drop into the splits.

Toward the end of the season, Annis faced backlash as the contestant who had explored the most connections throughout the season, which she addressed on Alex Cooper’s podcast *Call Her Daddy*.

![Annis and Dylan Wrona made their relationship official after leaving the villa](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/06/23/19/45/ScreenShot-Tool-20260623150813.png)

“I don’t know why the girls were holding me to a different standard,” Annis said on the podcast earlier this month. “Especially after getting out, it does feel, like, sexist almost, because I’m not a male and it’s like I can’t move like them maybe.”

She continued that she “didn’t feel that supported at that time,” and that the criticism likely affected her friendships “a little bit” on the show.

“But we did get over it and we talked about it. But yeah, it definitely hurt,” she said, continuing: “I was feeling like I wasn’t allowed to talk to two people at once. It just made no sense in my mind. I’m like, ‘Where are we right now?’ I’m very confused and feeling like I was just getting s*** on over and over for exploring when no one was closed off.”

Annis is next set to appear on the *Love Island USA* season eight reunion, which airs August 31 on Peacock.
