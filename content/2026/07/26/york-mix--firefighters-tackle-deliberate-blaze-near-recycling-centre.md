Crews tackled another fire believed to have been started deliberately yesterday (Saturday).

Firefighters were called to the blaze, in a field off **Garden Lane, Sherburn in Elmet**, at** 4.45pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Tadcaster, Selby and Pontefract responded to a stubble field fire believed to have been caused deliberately.

#### Garden Lane, Sherburn in Elmet

“Fire measured 100m x 30m and was spreading towards a recycling centre. Crews extinguished using two hose reels and some farm machinery.”
