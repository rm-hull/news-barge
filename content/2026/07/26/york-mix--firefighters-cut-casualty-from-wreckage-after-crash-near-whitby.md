There was a two-car crash near Whitby last night (Saturday).

Emergency services were called to **Guisborough Road, Aislaby**, at** 6.58pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Whitby ad Lythe attended a two vehicle road traffic collision.

#### Guisborough Road, Aislaby, Whitby

“One person was trapped.

“Crew stabilised the vehicle and removed the side of the car to allow the casualty to be released from vehicle.”

Two people in the other vehicle had minor injuries. All three casualties were taken to hospital.
