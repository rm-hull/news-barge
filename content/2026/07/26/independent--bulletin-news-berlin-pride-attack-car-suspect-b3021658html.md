# Police hunt 21-year-old after car driven into crowd at Berlin Pride event

- One person was killed and at least 17 others were injured after a car drove into a crowd near Berlin's LGBTQ+ parade on Saturday evening.
- Three of the victims sustained life-threatening injuries, while eight others were seriously injured.
- German police identified a 21-year-old suspect who they named only as Abdul B, who is known to authorities as a member of Berlin's “Islamist scene”.
- A wanted notice has been issued for Abdul B, who has not yet been arrested and is considered potentially armed and dangerous.
- The incident occurred during Christopher Street Day, one of Europe's largest LGBTQ+ celebrations.
