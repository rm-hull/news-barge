Search the Public Notice PortalA series of road closures have been announced due to various repair and maintenance works.

Broadlea Mount in Bramley will be closed between its junctions with Broadlea Street and Broadlea Terrace for carriageway and footway resurfacing work.

The closure will be in effect from Monday, July 27, to Friday, August 28, between 9.30am and 3pm on weekdays.

A diversion will be available via Broadlea Terrace, Broadlea Hill, Broadlea Road, and Broadlea Street.

Britannia Road in Morley will also be closed to facilitate gas main replacement works.

The closure will be in place from July 27 to August 11, with a diversion available via Britannia Road, Bridge Street, Bruntcliffe Road, Bruntcliffe Lane, and Corporation Street.

Magnolia Road in Seacroft will also be closed for gas main replacement works from July 29 to August 19.

The diversionary route will be via York Road and Selby Road.

Oakwood Lane and several other roads in Roundhay will be closed for water mains and services rehabilitation works.

The closures will begin on July 27 and will be staggered, with the final road closure ending on November 22.

Concordia Street in Leeds will be closed northbound for improvement works associated with the Leeds City Links scheme.

The closure will be in place from July 27 to October 2, with a diversion via Concordia Street, Sovereign Street, and Swinegate.

Windsor Crescent in Rothwell will be closed outside number 18 for new electrical connection works.

The closure will be in place from August 4 to August 10, with a diversion via Churchfield Lane.

Skeltons Lane in Whinmoor will be closed eastbound for the installation of new storm drainage.

The closure will be in place from July 27 to September 4, with a diversion via Ring Road Whinmoor Fields, York Road, Old Ring Road Seacroft, and Coal Road.

Hyde Park Road in Hyde Park will be closed for gas mains replacement works.

The closure will be in place from August 3 to August 14, with a diversion via Hyde Park Road, Moorland Road, Belle Vue Road, and Woodsley Road.

The Inner Ring Road and New York Road in Leeds will be closed for maintenance works to retaining walls.

The night-time closures will be in place from August 3 to November 27, with a diversion via various city centre roads.

Woodhouse Lane in Hyde Park/Woodhouse will have its northbound bus lane suspended for the installation of a cycle track and associated footway and carriageway works.

The closure will be in place from July 23 to September 24.

Lowfields Road will be closed from Elland Road to the M621 overpass for Leeds United friendly fixtures on August 8 and 15.

The road will be closed an hour before and after the matches.

A number of footpaths will also be closed for safety concerns and repair works.

Definitive Footpath Garforth No.25 will be closed from July 27 to August 7, Monday to Friday, from 8am to 5pm.

Definitive Footpath Leeds No.103 will remain closed until December 12, and Definitive Bridleways Bramhope No.2 & Arthington No.5 will remain closed until January 8, 2027.

All closures will have diversions in place, and access to premises and for emergency vehicles will be maintained.
