Ten years of environmental excellence have been marked with awards along Yorkshire’s historic waterways.

The Canal & River Trust has secured Green Flag Awards for all 10 of its sites across Yorkshire and the North East, including stretches of the Leeds & Liverpool Canal, Ripon Canal, and Sheffield & Tinsley Canal.

These awards form part of a decade-long achievement by the trust in maintaining high environmental standards across its network.

Campbell Robb, chief executive at Canal & River Trust, said: "For ten years, Green Flag Awards have helped showcase the important contribution canals make to communities across England and Wales.

"Whether in the heart of our cities or in the countryside, canals provide places where people can improve their wellbeing, be active, and spend time with family and friends."

The Green Flag Award scheme is managed by Keep Britain Tidy and recognises well-managed parks and green spaces.

Unlike many recreational areas, canals are freely accessible and woven into the fabric of the communities they serve.

The trust, which maintains 2,000 miles of waterways across England and Wales, manages 320 miles in Yorkshire, highlighting the importance of canals in promoting wellbeing and offering sustainable transport options.

Details of how to support local canals can be found at canalrivertrust.org.uk.
