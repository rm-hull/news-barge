A care home in York is inviting families to join in a day of food and festivities.

Taking place at Care UK’s Chocolate Works in York on August 6, from 10am, as part of the "A Taste of Home" initiative.

The food festival aims to celebrate the UK’s rich food culture while bringing together the wider community.

Ella Bond, deputy home manager at The Chocolate Works, said: "We’re delighted to be welcoming the local community to our very own food festival.

"We are part of a strong community here in York, and days like this are a wonderful way of bringing people together to celebrate our shared love of food, while giving residents the opportunity to share their favourite recipes and traditions with younger generations.

"The day not only promises a lot of laughter, but it is also an excellent way for residents to meet and mingle with lots of new faces."

To find out more, visit: careuk.com/a-taste-of-home
