# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after two electric bikes stolen in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after two electric bikes were stolen in Harrogate.

The incident happened at 4.20pm on July 17 and involved the theft of two high-value mountain electric bikes from Fodder at the Great Yorkshire Showground.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you have seen the two bikes around Harrogate or have any information that will assist our investigation.”
