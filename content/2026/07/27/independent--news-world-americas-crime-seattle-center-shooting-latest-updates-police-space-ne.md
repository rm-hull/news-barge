A suspect has been taken into custody after three people were killed and four wounded – including a two-year-old boy – during a mass shooting at a popular Seattle food festival Sunday evening.

Two died at the scene and a third victim, who was taken to Harborview Medical Center, later succumbed to their injuries.

A manhunt for a second suspect has been launched, according to Seattle’s Assistant Police Chief Tyrone Davis.

“There were two people who we believe were shooting at each other,” he said.

The Seattle Fire Department earlier said the child and three other injured people were rushed to hospital in stable conditions, with a fifth person treated at the scene for minor injuries, according to *The Seattle Times*.

No details have yet been released about the suspect in the attack on the Seattle Center, home to the city’s iconic Space Needle, which was hosting the annual Bite of Seattle event over the weekend.

Estan Wakonabo, 25, told*The Seattle Times* he was at the event with his girlfriend when he heard gunshots at around 6 p.m. local time.

“It was just pure chaos,” he said.

## ‘Pop, pop, pop’: Witnesses describe screams, chaos and crowds fleeing after shooting at Seattle food festival

Bite of Seattle attendees have described the scenes of terror they saw as gunfire tore through the popular food festival, sending panicked crowds scrambling for cover, parents desperately grabbing children and baby strollers toppling as people fled for their lives.

Faith Adia Hunter said she and her friends had just picked up food from a crepe stand when the shooting erupted.

“We were right next to him when it started, so we took off running,” she said, explaining that she and dozens of others had rushed into the nearby Seattle Children’s Museum to escape the gunfire.

As bullets rang out, vendors were forced to abandon their food stalls and flee alongside festivalgoers.

## Four wounded in shooting ‘in stable condition’, police chief says

The four injured in the shooting, including a toddler, are in stable condition, according to assistant police chief Tyrone Davis.

Since the shooting, which erupted at 6.01 p.m. on Sunday, one suspect – described as a “young person” by police – has been taken into custody, while a manhunt has been launched for a second suspect.

Police now believe two people exchanged gunfire during Seattle’s popular annual food festival that left three people dead and four others injured.

The shooting erupted at the Seattle Center, near the Space Needle, where thousands had gathered for the annual food festival.

## Police chief says no threat to the community

Seattle assistant police chief Tyrone Davis has said that police now believe there is “no outstanding threat to the community”.
