# Watch moment drink-driver is arrested as part of crackdown in Yorkshire

## This is the moment police in Yorkshire arrested a drink driver - as part of a spate of drivers being held for testing over the legal limit behind the wheel.

Five drink-drivers have been stopped in recent days by officers and South Yorkshire Police has warned: “Don't make the same mistake.”

A spokesperson said: “There is never an excuse to drink and drive - and getting behind the wheel when you've had a drink is one of the four leading contributory factors in serious or fatal collisions.

“If officers suspect a vehicle is being driven by someone who is over the legal drink-drive limit, they will request for the vehicle to stop and carry out a roadside breath test.

“Between 2am and 6am on 18 July, officers on patrol in Doncaster stopped five drink-drivers, all of whom provided positive roadside breath tests which indicated they were not legally safe to drive.

![Police arrest a drink driver in Doncaster.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/6/08/chrome_kTxkqHgdL9.jpeg?trim=34,0,34,0&width=640&quality=65)



“The highest reading of 110 micrograms of alcohol per 100 millilitres of breath was over three times the legal drink-drive limit of 35.

“All five who were arrested have since been charged with driving a motor vehicle while over the legal drink-drive limit and are set to appear before the courts.

“If you suspect someone is drink or drug-driving, then please report it to us so we can take action.”
