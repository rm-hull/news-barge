Arsenal are plotting an audacious move for Real Madrid superstar Vinicius Jr and want to sign the Brazil winger this summer. The Premier League champions are preparing to offer the 26-year-old the biggest contract in their history but have yet to make contact with Real Madrid over the move, who has just one more year on his contract, while incoming boss at the Bernabeu, Jose Mourinho, would need to sanction the transfer.

It could be a busy few weeks for Mikel Arteta’s side as they also look to sign Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes and add another centre-back, with England internationals Ezri Konsa and John Stones both of interest. Newcastle manager Eddie Howe confirmed that he wants to keep Guimaraes, but added that his future at the club is out of his control.

Elsewhere, Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola has set out his targets for the transfer window and has confirmed the Reds “definitely need to sign a winger”, while France international Bradley Barcola is reportedly open to leaving Paris Saint-Germain this summer. PSG, who are set to miss out on the signing Yan Diomande to Real Madrid, would demand a record fee, however.

Manchester United are still interested in adding another midfielder and have “reached an agreement in principle” with France international Manu Kone, according to reports in Italy. Aurelien Tchouameni remains a target, too.

## Juventus consider Joshua Zirkzee

Juventus are considering Manchester United's Joshua Zirkzee as a potential forward option, *Sky in Italy* reports. 

Randal Kolo Muani, who spent last season on loan at Tottenham Hotspur, remains Juve's priority for the attack, but a valuation gap persists with Paris Saint-Germain.

Should an agreement with PSG not be reached, Zirkzee is among the alternative options.

## Huge fee set for Liverpool target Bradley Barcola

Widespread reports suggest that Paris Saint-Germain are open to selling Bradley Barcola – but only for a fee of around £145m or more. Pricey!

*The Times***suggest that Arsenal and Liverpool both have interest, though may be unwilling to pay the terms PSG desire with the French capital club using the Morgan Rogers and Elliot Anderson deals as a benchmark.

It comes after *L’Equipe* reported that Barcola had shut down contract talks with PSG to try and force a move away.

## Fulham handed boost in race for Real Madrid starlet

Real Madrid are set to sell Gonzalo García to Fulham.

Marca reports that, despite Jose Mourinho admiring the striker, the pull of the Premier League side making the 22-year-old their primary target could be enough.

Fulham would need to table a big bid, but also give up a percentage of the rights.

## Arsenal 'prepared' to go big for Julian Alvarez

Arsenal are prepared to break the British transfer record to sign Julian Alvarez from Atletico Madrid, according to Teamtalk.

*The Independent* first reported on Arsenal’s interest in Julian Alvarez in April and a three-way battle between the Gunners, Paris Saint-Germain and Barcelona was expected this summer. Barcelona’s approach for Alvarez angered Atletico and they have reportedly set their LaLiga rivals a deadline of July 31 to accept their offer, which will need to be around €150m.

Having missed out on Morgan Rogers, Arsenal would arguably have more resources available should Barcelona fail to land their priority target and Gunners boss Mikel Arteta is prepared to take a patient approach for the former Manchester City forward and Argentina international. Alvarez’s preference would be Barcelona, and it would take a record bid from Arsenal to get the deal over the line.

## Vinicius Jr wants Real Madrid stay amid Arsenal interest

Vinicius Jr wants to stay at Real Madrid and is ready to begin discussions on signing a new contract, according to reports in Spain.

*AS* report that Vinicius, who is out of contract in 2027, will resume talks on a new deal once he returns from his summer holiday.

News broke yesterday that Arsenal were keen to explore a transfer this summer as Mikel Arteta looks for a marquee signing and new left winger.

Real Madrid’s agreement to sign RB Leipzig’s Yan Diomande also added to the speculation - but the club want Vinicius to extend his deal, too.

## Arsenal line up centre-back options after Saliba injury

Arsenal are looking into a significant investment at centre-back with William Saliba expected to be out long-term following a major injury blow.

Aston Villa’s Ezri Konsa and John Stones, available on a free transfer after his Man City contract expired, are both being considered.

## Kane ready to begin Bayern contract talks

Harry Kane will begin contract talks with Bayern Munich when he returns from his summer holiday

That’s according to *Sky Sports News,* who say that Kane is garnering interest from Barcelona but is very happy in Germany.

Kane’s deal expires at the end of the 2026/27 season and the England captain is said to be very relaxed about the situation but Bayern are keen to start negotiations given the interest surrounding him.

## Joe Gomez injury takes shine off Liverpool’s first win under Andoni Iraola

Andoni Iraola said the injury to Joe Gomez was the “worst news” after he began his Liverpool reign with a 4-2 win over Sunderland in Nashville.

Gomez, 29, was forced off within 10 minutes of Liverpool’s first friendly of pre-season after appearing to suffer a muscle issue

"Probably the worst news has been straight away the injury of Joe,” Iraola said after the win.

“We were happy because we were kind of going through all the training without losing any player. Unluckily for us, straight away we've lost Joe."
