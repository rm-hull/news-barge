# How to apply for Love Island 2027? Applications open for next series of ITV reality show

- The application process for Love Island 2027 is now open.
- Anyone over the age of 18 can apply. Candidates should head to ITV’s applications page and fill out their details.
- “ITV2 & ITVX are looking for vibrant singles from across the UK who are looking for love in the sun!” a post on ITV’s website said.
- “The chosen cast will spend time in a luxury villa, getting to know one another, but to remain in paradise they must win the hearts of the public and their fellow Islanders who ultimately decide their fate on Love Island. If you think you’ve got what it takes, then we want to hear from you!”
- Winners of the show could win £50,000. The closing date to apply is **19 July 2027**, but ITV’s website says it “may change at their discretion and without notice”.
