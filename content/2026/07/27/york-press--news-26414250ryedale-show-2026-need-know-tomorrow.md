One of Yorkshire's biggest celebrations of farming and rural life is set to return to Duncombe Park, Helmsley, tomorrow (Tuesday) promising an action-packed day of competition, entertainment and family fun.

Ryedale Show, which has been at the heart of the county's agricultural calendar since 1855, attracts thousands of visitors each year and features one of the region's largest displays of livestock, horses, local produce and traditional rural crafts. This year's show also introduces a number of new features designed to make the visitor experience even better.

A major focus for 2026 has been improving traffic management following last year's event. Working with professional traffic management specialists, the show has invested heavily in measures to reduce delays, including advance roadside signage, clearer routes into Helmsley, alternative routes for non-show traffic, improved on-site access roads and a dedicated professional parking team managing vehicle movements throughout the day. Organisers hope the changes will significantly reduce queue times while ensuring visitors arrive safely and efficiently.

For those travelling into Helmsley by bus or parking in the town, a new "Tractor and Trailer Taxi" service will run throughout the day, transporting visitors between the Duncombe Park main entrance and the showground, making it even easier to enjoy the event without the walk. This will also enable showgoers to visit the Sheepdog Section, located by the Park Entrance.

Once inside the showground there is something to entertain every member of the family. Hundreds of cattle, sheep, pigs, goats and other livestock will compete for championship honours, alongside an extensive horse section featuring more than 90 classes, show jumping, Heavy Horses and the popular Mounted Games entertainment in the main ring.

Visitors can also browse an impressive array of produce, horticulture, arts, crafts and photography exhibits. And, with over 250 trade and craft stands there is everything from local delicacies and artisan products to country clothing and agricultural machinery to shop for!

Animal lovers won't want to miss the ever-popular Fun Dog Show, open to dogs of all shapes and sizes, alongside gundog displays and sheepdog demonstrations that celebrate the skills of working dogs in the countryside.

Families can enjoy free children's activities throughout the day, including competitions, races, treasure hunts and hands-on experiences, making the Ryedale Show one of North Yorkshire's favourite family days out.

For those wanting to round off the day in style, the celebrations continue after the show closes. Members are invited to stay for the new Members' After Party, taking place from 4.30pm until 6.30pm, featuring live music from local rockabilly favourites The Rhythm Burners and a delicious hog roast—a perfect opportunity to relax, catch up with friends and enjoy the unique atmosphere that has made the Ryedale Show such a special event for generations.
