Police are investigating a theft from a York garden centre.

It happened on **Thursday**(23 July) between** 3pm and 3.30pm**at** Skelton Garden Centre**, off Shipton Road.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Around this time, the owner of the garden centre noticed that the till drawer was missing and there was a crack in the till screen.

#### Skelton Garden Centre, York

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to come forward if they saw anything on this day that may be of assistance to the investigation.”
