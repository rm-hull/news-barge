# Trump demands GOP scrap filibuster to ram through voting bill

- Donald Trump is pressuring Senate Majority Leader John Thune to pass the "SAVE America Act," a restrictive voting bill, or eliminate the Senate filibuster.
- Trump demanded on Truth Social that the Senate forgo its August recess until the bill passes or the filibuster is terminated, allowing Republicans to pass legislation with a simple majority.
- The "SAVE America Act" currently lacks the 60 votes required to overcome the Senate's supermajority rule for most legislation.
- Senator Thune has repeatedly stated there is insufficient support, even among Republicans, to pass the bill or change the filibuster rules.
- Trump's press secretary, Karoline Leavitt, indicated his "patience was running out," prompting Thune to suggest she gather the necessary votes instead of blaming Republicans.
