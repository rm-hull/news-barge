A NEW entertainment centre is ready to reopen to the public after a major refurbishment over the school summer holidays.

Tenpin York, located at the Clifton Moor Centre, will open its doors on a new entertainment area on Monday (August 3) - which will see updated attractions and new entertainment options.

The site now features two private karaoke rooms, interactive dartboards, a soft play area for children, and an arcade with a variety of different games.

Marc Couget, regional manager at Tenpin, said: "We’re incredibly excited to unveil our refurbished centre and share these new experiences with our customers.

"With the addition of brand-new karaoke rooms, interactive darts, and a soft play area, we’re proud to be one of York’s leading indoor entertainment venues.

"We’re not just about bowling lanes at Tenpin, our guests can now enjoy a wide range of activities all under one roof, making it the perfect place for family days out."

Other popular features, including Houdini’s escape rooms, the laser tag arena, and the pool tables are still available to visitors.

Marc added: "We can't wait to welcome both new and returning visitors from York and the surrounding area, including communities such as Pocklington, Ripon, and beyond."
