# Spiderman: Brand New Day premiere takes over Hollywood with film’s stars set to hit the red carpet

![Spiderman: Brand New Day officially opens Friday but a promotion offers early screenings at select theaters](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/18/53/GettyImages-2283298349-(1).jpg)

- A section of Hollywood Boulevard was closed Monday for the premiere of the new Spider-Man movie, *Spiderman: Brand New Day*, starring Tom Holland and Zendaya.
- The premiere and its red carpet event were scheduled for Monday evening at the El Capitan Theatre, located at 6838 Hollywood Blvd.
- The road closure affected the stretch of Hollywood Boulevard from Highland Avenue to North Orange Drive, a prominent tourist area along the Walk of Fame.
- The movie, which is the fourth installment featuring Holland as Peter Parker, is set for an official release on Friday, with some early promotional screenings available.
- Red carpet entrances for Monday night’s premiere will be aired live on Amazon's Prime Video beginning at 5:30 p.m.
