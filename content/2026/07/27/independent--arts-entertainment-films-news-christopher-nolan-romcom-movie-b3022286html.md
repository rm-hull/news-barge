Christopher Nolan fans should not expect a romcom from the acclaimed director anytime soon, if ever.

The Oscar winner, 55, whose latest epic, *The Odyssey*, is dominating box offices, admitted that while he loves watching romcoms, helming one would be “incredibly difficult.” 

“I think people who direct comedy, people who do comedy, I think that’s one of the hardest things in the world to make people laugh that way,” Nolan said on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon*.

“Particularly, the risk they take when you make a film — if I show a film to people and maybe they don’t respond to bits of it, I’m like, ‘Ah, they don’t get it. Whatever,’” the *Oppenheimer* director added. 

“There’s no hiding behind that when you do comedy. So, I would be terrified of doing anything like that.”

Nolan’s repertoire includes a long list of acclaimed action, drama and psychological thrillers. He made his directorial debut in 1998 with *Following*, a crime thriller starring Jeremy Theobald about a young writer living in London who follows strangers’ lives for material. His hobby soon turns into an obsession that leads him down a dark path. 

In 2000, the British director came out with his Guy Pearce-led psychological thriller *Memento*, which earned him his first Oscar nomination for Best Original Screenplay. 

From there, he continued his ascent to become one of Hollywood’s most in-demand directors, releasing *Insomnia*(2002),* Batman Begins*(2005),* The Prestige*(2006),* The Dark Knight*(2008),* Inception*(2010),* The Dark Knight Rises*(2012),* Interstellar*(2014),* Dunkirk*(2017),* Tenet*(2020) and* Oppenheimer* (2023), which landed him his first Oscar wins for Best Director and Best Picture. 

![Anne Hathaway plays Penelope, the wife of Odysseus and mother of Telemachus](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/22/11/15/Screenshot-2026-07-22-at-12-28-42.png)

Just over a week ago, Nolan premiered*The Odyssey*, an adaptation of Homer’s epic poem. Starring Matt Damon as Odysseus, King of Ithaca, the story follows his perilous journey home after the 10-year Trojan War. Anne Hathaway, Tom Holland, Zendaya, Charlize Theron, Robert Pattinson and Lupita Nyong’o also feature. 

*The Odyssey* has already become a critical and box office success, raking in $639.6 million globally so far. 

“As a feat of pure adaptation, Nolan has achieved something I admittedly thought was near impossible,” *The Independent*’s Clarisse Loughrey wrote in a five-star review. “His stamp is all over the film — this is intellectual, brutalist, muscular Hollywood fare — yet it never wavers in its commitment to, and comprehension of, its source text.”

Declaring it “his best film to date,” Loughrey further applauded the performances, arguing that “there’s not a weak link.”
