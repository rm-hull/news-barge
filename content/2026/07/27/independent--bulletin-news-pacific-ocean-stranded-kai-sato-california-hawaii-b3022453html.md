Australian navy rescues adventurer struck by cyclone while rowing across Pacific Ocean

Sailor Kai Sato survived over a month adrift in the Pacific Ocean after his solo journey from California to Hawaii went awry.

Two weeks into his trip, his boat's mast snapped, and he was left stranded after running out of fuel and his phone dying.

Sato ingeniously collected drinking water by placing plastic bags over buckets of seawater to gather condensation after his supplies ran out on 11 July.

He sustained himself on oatmeal, fish, and squid, and temporarily repaired his sails using kayak paddles and PVC pipes to reach a shipping lane.

Sato was eventually rescued by a Pasha Hawaii ship last Tuesday, expressing immense relief and gratitude for the crew's help.
