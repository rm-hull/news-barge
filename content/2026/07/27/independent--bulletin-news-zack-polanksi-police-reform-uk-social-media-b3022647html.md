The image, which Mr Polanski later unshared, depicted a man wearing a T-shirt featuring a guillotine and the phrase 'We're only making plans for Nigel'.

Reform UK leader Nigel Farage and home affairs spokesperson Zia Yusuf accused Mr Polanski of inciting murder, calling for police action.

The Green Party stated that Mr Polanski 'condemns the image' and accepted a tag in a multi-image post without realising the controversial content was included.

The Metropolitan Police confirmed they are conducting inquiries into the matter after receiving a third-party report.
