Applying one of the best face sunscreens should be as second nature as brushing your teeth, rather than the last, laborious step in your skincare routine. Yet many only save SPF for beach days and holidays, despite UV rays being present all year round in the UK.

“Broad-spectrum sunscreens protect skin against UV light produced by the sun,” says dermatologist Dr Alexis Granite. “The two main types that impact skin are UVA and UVB, and together they are responsible for producing changes within the skin that can lead to sunburn and skin cancer, along with things like pigmentation, fine lines and wrinkles.” She adds that nearly 80 per cent of visible ageing is linked to UV exposure, even on cold or cloudy days.

## How much sunscreen should I be applying to my face?

“The ‘two-finger’ rule is a good one to know,” advises Dr Granite. It’s simply a case of squeezing two strips of sunscreen along your index and middle fingers to get adequate coverage for your face, and remember to reapply every couple of hours if you're in the sun.

SPF moisturisers and foundations can help, but it’s rare for them to provide the amount of SPF protection listed on the bottle, unless applied generously. The good news is that modern facial sunscreens have come a long way and are far more pleasant to apply and wear than older formulas – especially if you grew up slapping on a thick, mud-like body sunscreens from top to toe in lieu of a dedicated formula for face.

After whittling down a long list of popular brands and products, I tested a wide range of cream, gel and lotion face SPFs to find the ones that sit well under make-up, feel comfortable all day and are genuinely easy to use every morning. These were my top picks.
