THIEVES have stolen an electric bike from the garden of a home in East Yorkshire.

The burglary happened between 9pm and 11.30pm on July 22 in Water Lane in Hook.

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “The offender(s) approached a secure shed in the rear of the victim’s garden, broke the lock, carried out an untidy search, and made off with an electric bike and the padlock."

  


They added: “If you have any CCTV footage (including video doorbell), saw anyone acting suspiciously in the area around this time or have any information about the offenders please contact the police on the non-emergency number 101 quoting occurrence ref: 26*103190.

“Alternatively you could call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111. You don’t have to leave your name and you may even receive a reward.

“You can also provide information anonymously on the Crimestoppers website”
