# £389 ‘hotel-style’ memory foam mattress slashed to £69 with free delivery

![The Deep-Sleep Memory Foam Mattress has dropped to £69 in a GoGroopie voucher deal](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/11/14/ChatGPT-Image-Jul-7-2026-04_06_31-PM.png?trim=46,0,46,0&crop=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)



## Five sizes are included in the promotion, combining an open-coil support core with memory foam, medium firmness and a breathable cover

A new mattress does not have to cost hundreds of pounds, as this extra-thick memory foam model is currently available from just £69 with free delivery.

The Deep-Sleep Memory Foam Mattress is listed with an 82% saving, reducing the price of a single from £389 to £69. An additional 10% discount is advertised when shoppers enter the code MARKDOWN10 at checkout.

If the code applies to the £69 deal price, it would bring the single mattress down to £62.10. Buyers should check the reduction appears in their basket before paying, as promotional codes and availability can change.

The discounted mattress combines an open-coil spring foundation with a memory foam comfort layer. This hybrid-style construction is intended to provide the underlying support of traditional springs alongside the cushioning feel associated with memory foam.

At approximately 11 to 12 inches deep, the mattress has a more substantial profile than many budget alternatives. The retailer describes it as offering a plush, hotel-style sleeping surface, which could make the deal particularly tempting for anyone replacing a thin or ageing mattress.

![The Deep-Sleep Memory Foam Mattress starts at £69 and is available in five sizes](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/11/07/ChatGPT-Image-Jul-7-2026-04_03_52-PM.png?trim=26,0,27,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



Its medium firmness is designed to balance support and softness rather than sitting at either extreme. The memory foam layer adapts around the body and is intended to relieve pressure, while the open-coil core provides the structure beneath it.

Another feature of the Deep-Sleep mattress deal is its cool-touch breathable cover. This is designed to help manage excess warmth and maintain a more comfortable sleeping temperature throughout the year.

The interior is also described as hypoallergenic and antibacterial. Although individual sensitivities vary, those qualities may appeal to households looking for a cleaner and fresher sleeping environment.

Five sizes are available. A small double costs £89, while the standard double is £99. The king-size version is £129 and the largest super king costs £179 before any further reduction from the checkout code.

![Deep-Sleep medium-firm mattress featuring open-coil support and a memory foam comfort layer](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/11/27/ChatGPT-Image-Jul-7-2026-04_08_28-PM.png?trim=13,0,15,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)



That means even the super king memory foam mattress comes in below £180 at the advertised price. The range could therefore cover anything from a child’s room or guest bed to a larger main bedroom without forcing buyers into a one-size-only promotion.

More than 1,000 mattresses have reportedly been sold through the deal. The product is supplied and dispatched by Cloud 9YN, rather than GoGroopie itself, so buyers should read the merchant details and delivery terms before ordering.

Free delivery is included, removing one of the extra charges that can make buying a mattress online more expensive than expected. Anyone interested can view all five Deep-Sleep mattress sizes and apply the discount code at checkout.

Mattress comfort is subjective, so it is worth checking the dimensions, firmness description, returns policy and full fine print before committing. Prices, discount codes and stock can also change at short notice.
