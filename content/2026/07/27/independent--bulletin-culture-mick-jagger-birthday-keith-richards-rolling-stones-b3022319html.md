# Mick Jagger turns 83 as Rolling Stones bandmates send warm wishes

- Rock legend Sir Mick Jagger celebrated his 83rd birthday, receiving warm wishes from current and former Rolling Stones bandmates.
- The milestone comes just weeks after the group unveiled their latest studio album, *Foreign Tongues*, on 10 July.
- Guitarists Ronnie Wood and Keith Richards, along with former bassist Bill Wyman, posted tributes on social media.
- Sir Mick's fiancée, Melanie Hamrick, and the official Rolling Stones Instagram account also shared birthday messages, the latter writing: “You continue to inspire generations with your energy, creativity and unmistakable spirit – there is no one quite like you. Wishing you an amazing day.”
- The new album, *Foreign Tongues*, features guest appearances from notable musicians including Sir Paul McCartney, Robert Smith, and Steve Winwood.
