# Trump vows to confront Putin over Iran war claim as ‘good talks’ with Tehran resume

![Trump, on Air Force One on his way to Michigan, said he has ‘plenty of time’ to deal with Iran](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/17/40/GettyImages-2287248718.jpg)

- President Donald Trump said Monday that the United States is engaged in "good talks" with Iran, indicating a potential for a deal to resolve the conflict, but warned that US air strikes would resume if negotiations fail.
- This announcement came after Washington abruptly suspended a two-week campaign of air strikes against Iran on Saturday, marking a significant strategic shift in the ongoing five-month conflict.
- Despite Trump’s comments, Tehran appeared to immediately test the pause in US military action, with Saudi Arabia, Jordan, and Iraq reporting drone attacks on Monday.
- Saudi Arabia confirmed shooting down drones aimed at petroleum targets, including in its capital Riyadh, and attributed these attacks to Iran-backed armed groups operating from Iraq. Separately, Iran's Houthi allies in Yemen claimed responsibility for targeting the 1,200-km East-West Pipeline, which transports oil to Saudi Arabia's main Red Sea port of Yanbu, in retaliation for alleged Saudi drone incursions.
- Trump also said Monday that he will ask Russia about reports it provided satellite imagery to assist Iran. "I'll ask Putin about it," Trump said. "We'll find out."
