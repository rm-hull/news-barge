# Anthony Joshua makes ‘unstoppable’ claim after narrowly avoiding disastrous defeat

- Anthony Joshua secured a second-round knockout victory over Kristian Prenga in Saudi Arabia on Saturday, despite being floored twice in the opening round of their bout.
- Joshua's win was crucial for preserving his highly anticipated all-British clash with Tyson Fury, which is expected to take place later this year.
- Reflecting on the challenging first round, Joshua stated on the 5 Live Boxing Podcast that he felt "unstoppable" after overcoming the adversity, viewing the experience as character-building.
- This was Joshua’s first fight since a fatal car crash in December that killed two of his teammates, Sina Ghami and Latif “Latz” Ayodele, adding emotional weight to his performance.
- Joshua emphasised the spiritual aspect of fighting, crediting the support of others and his team for helping him overcome adversity in the ring.
