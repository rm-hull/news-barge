Contact details for numerous celebrities, including Angelina Jolie, Robert De Niro, and Martin Scorsese, were reportedly exposed in a data breach.

The leak occurred when a Tribeca Film Festival database, simply named “contacts”, was accidentally made public online.

Cybersecurity expert Jeremiah Fowler discovered the exposure days before the festival began on 3 June, calling it the “biggest collection of celebrity data” he had ever seen.

The exposed data included names, phone numbers, email addresses, and device information such as iPhone versions and web browsers used by the celebrities.

Fowler notified the Tribeca Film Festival, co-founded by Robert De Niro, which promptly removed the databases and said it is actively investigating the incident.
