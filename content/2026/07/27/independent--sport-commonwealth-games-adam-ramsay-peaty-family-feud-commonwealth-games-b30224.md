Olympic champion swimmer Adam Ramsay-Peaty has hit back at critics "who want to hurt me and my family with comments" after securing a "bittersweet" bronze medal at the Commonwealth Games.

The 31-year-old, who married Holly Ramsay-Peaty, 26, last December and recently announced they are expecting their first child, publicly thanked his wife for her unwavering support.

Ramsay-Peaty, who took home bronze in the 100-metre breaststroke on Saturday, shared several photos from the event on Instagram, including one of him and his wife sharing a kiss.

He described his latest achievement as a "proud moment" and explained his continued dedication to the sport.

"I still love standing on the blocks, hearing the roar of the crowd, fighting inch by inch in every stroke, putting myself on the line, and, because I’m not finished," he wrote. "Ultimately, I know these are the stories I will be telling my children and their children."

![Holly Ramsay-Peaty has been cheering on Adam at the Commonwealth Games](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/17/01KYG0VAWPHRQHHEM2S5CHPYB4.jpg)

Despite the medal, the swimmer admitted placing third was a "bittersweet moment." He added: "I know in my soul that I am capable of much more, as all athletes, I push myself to the extremity in training for better results. The race just didn’t come together unfortunately and I am building my race skills back together."

In his lengthy post, Ramsay-Peaty also addressed those who have criticised his family.

"To the people out there who want to hurt me and my family with comments, I have seen this story a thousand times with other athletes from many sports," he stated.

He called for a more positive environment, adding: "We are athletes, doing something we love and that is our deepest passion. As it’s said, be the change you want to see in the world, and, I want a better world for the next generation of athletes. Love always wins."

The couple, with Holly being the daughter of celebrity chef Gordon Ramsay, have both adopted the surname Ramsay-Peaty since their marriage. Their relationship previously garnered attention last year amid reports of an ongoing family feud with Adam’s parents, Mark and Caroline, who missed their lavish wedding.

Expressing gratitude, Ramsay-Peaty said he was "thankful for my wife, my friends, my team, and the people in the stands who cheer my name," describing competing in his fourth Commonwealth Games as "the greatest honour."

The athlete, a winner of three Olympic gold medals, is set to compete in the 50-metre breaststroke event on Monday, declaring, "tonight I go again."

His wife, Holly, echoed the sentiment of support, commenting on his post, "so proud of you, always," and sharing it to her Instagram story.

The couple reportedly met through Holly’s sister Tilly when both participated in *Strictly Come Dancing* in 2021, announcing their engagement in 2024.
