# Ryan Reynolds makes surprise appearance at San Diego Comic Con – here’s what happened

- Ryan Reynolds made a surprise appearance as Deadpool at San Diego Comic-Con, asking Robert Downey Jr. for a role in the upcoming *Avengers: Doomsday* film.
- Robert Downey Jr., portraying Doctor Doom, informed Reynolds that the film had already been shot in a humorous exchange.
- Despite Deadpool not being officially listed, rumours persist about Reynolds' character having a role in *Avengers: Doomsday*.
- An extended trailer for *Avengers: Doomsday* premiered at Comic-Con, showcasing the film’s massive crossover appeal, according to*Variety*.
- The film, a sequel to *Avengers: Endgame* and the 39th MCU movie, is scheduled for release in cinemas on 18 December.
