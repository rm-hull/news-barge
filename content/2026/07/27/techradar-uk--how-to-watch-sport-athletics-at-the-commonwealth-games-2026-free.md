## Commonwealth Games Athletics 2026: Preview

Two-time Olympic medalist and 2023 world champion Kerr is arguably the star attraction when it comes to the athletics and para-athletics at Glasgow 2026, especially after breaking the men's mile world record in a time of 3mins 42.66secs. The Scot goes for gold in the return of the historic Commonwealth Mile, so expect an electric atmosphere if he reaches the final on Saturday evening, with fellow home hopes Jake Wightman and Neil Gourley also targeting medals.

The host nation boasts a similarly strong line-up of female track stars, with Jemma Reekie (800m and Mile) and Laura Muir (5,000m) among their 39-strong party, as well as Paralympic gold medalist Ben Sandilands and Owen Miller (both T20 1,500m).

Several more big names are set to light up the track, including England's Zharnel Hughes in the 200m and 4x100m relay, and Georgia Hunter Bell (800m), with Jackson and De Grasse representing Jamaica and Canada, respectively, in the 4x100m relays, and 21-year-old Kanyinsola Ajayi, who this year broke the Nigerian 100m record with a time of 9.84.

The pick of the field events is arguably a highly anticipated javelin showdown between India's Neeraj Chopra and Pakistan's Arshad Nadeem, the two most recent Olympic champions. Reigning Olympic, world, and Commonwealth high jump champion Hamish Kerr represents New Zealand as he aims to add yet another medal to his collection, while Australian wheelchair racing legend Angie Ballard competes at a record-breaking fifth Commonwealth Games.
