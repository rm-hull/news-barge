A New Jersey man has been charged after a 2-year-old boy allegedly overdosed on cocaine while in his care during a Jersey Shore vacation.

Firefighters responding to a call in North Wildwood on July 20 found the toddler struggling to breathe inside Alexander Moles' vehicle before EMS revived him with Narcan.

Hospital tests later detected cocaine in the child's system, according to investigators, prompting a criminal investigation.

Authorities said Moles, 39, was staying at a vacation rental and was responsible for supervising the children when the boy became critically ill.

Moles is charged with child endangerment, cocaine possession and related offenses. He was released following a detention hearing on July 24.
