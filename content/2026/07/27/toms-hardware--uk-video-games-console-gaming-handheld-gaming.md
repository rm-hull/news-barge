It has the performance muscle that is unmatched in this class, but the Claw 8 EX AI+’s $1,799 price tag and lack of OLED leave us scratching our heads.

The MSI Claw 8 EX AI+ brought comfort and performance to Computex with massive prongs and Intel Arc G3 Extreme.

![Lenovo G02](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MnGTq7gv6VYtv9CWS75eDK-320-80.jpg)

![Anbernic RG34XXSP](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Laa2Kaz2pWKBLuTVWU4oub-320-80.jpg)

Anbernic silently ships some retro gaming handhelds with less memory than initially advertised, without notifying customers.

Intel has revealed the Arc G3 and G3 Extreme chips, which are built on Panther Lake dies with up to an Arc B390 iGPU. The chips will show up in devices from partners like Acer and OneXPlayer.

![Acer Predator Atlas 8 on an RGB VU meter background](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/f5ECBRfXC3jZ97zGqGJjMf-320-80.jpg)

Acer's Atlas 8 takes on AMD’s dominance in the space with the new Intel Arc G3 and G3 Extreme processors, packing Arc B370 or B390 iGPUs.
