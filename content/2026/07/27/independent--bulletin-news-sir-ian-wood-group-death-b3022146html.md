Sir Ian Wood, a Scottish billionaire who built his fortune in the North Seaoil industry, has died peacefully at his home at the age of 84.

He transformed his family's Aberdeen-based fishing operation into an engineering and consulting giant, later forming a separate engineering company servicing the rapidly growing North Sea oil business.

At its height, the Wood Group was valued at more than £5 billion. Sir Ian retired as the group's chair in 2012 and was knighted in 1994 for his contributions to the oil and gas sector, later becoming a member of the Order of the Thistle.

He established the Wood Foundation in 2007, through which he engaged in philanthropic work.

His family described him as “a devoted husband, father, grandfather and friend”, acknowledging “his kindness, generosity, determination and unwavering commitment to his family”.
