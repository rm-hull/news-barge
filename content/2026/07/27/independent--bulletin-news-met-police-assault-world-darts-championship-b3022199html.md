Police are searching for a man who punched another man outside Alexandra Palace following a World Darts Championship game on 13 December.

The victim, in his twenties, was knocked unconscious and is still suffering from “significant injuries”.

The Met Police have released CCTV footage of the suspect, who was part of a larger group wearing lobster costumes.

An initial argument between the victim and the group at the venue's entrance was broken up by security, but they encountered each other again shortly after.

The assault occurred on the venue's concourse, and the victim was taken to hospital with serious but non-life-threatening injuries.
