# Inquest opens into death of man allegedly murdered at solstice event in the Peak District

![Isaac Clare-Watts was found dead at a Bronze Age stone circle in the Peak District](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/24/20/newFile.jpg.jpg)

- An inquest has been opened into the death of Isaac Clare-Watts, 26, who was allegedly murdered "in the most brutal way" at a summer solstice event near the Nine Ladies Stone Circle in the Peak District.
- Mr Clare-Watts' body was discovered on Monday, June 22, near the Bronze Age stone circle in Stanton Lees, Derbyshire, but the cause of his death has not yet been determined as post-mortem examination findings are still awaited.
- Szymon Babynko, 23, a Polish national from London, has been charged with Mr Clare-Watts' murder and is also accused of attempted murder in relation to a separate incident in Chesterfield.
- Coroner Susan Evans formally opened the inquest at Chesterfield Coroner’s Court, confirming Mr Clare-Watts, a carpenter from Carrington in Nottingham, was identified by his mother.
- The inquest has been suspended pending the conclusion of criminal proceedings, with Mr Babynko scheduled to appear at Derby Crown Court on September 25, and a trial anticipated for November this year or March next year.
