# Usher fan called on-stage in viral video breaks silence after singer halts interaction

- Usher's performance in Nashville took an unexpected turn when a fan brought on stage for a serenade appeared visibly uncomfortable, leading him to stop the interaction.
- During his 'R&B Tour', Usher typically selects a fan for a 'sexy serenade' during his song 'Can U Handle It?', but this particular instance did not go as planned.
- A viral video showed Usher pausing and looking surprised before the woman was escorted off stage, prompting a divided reaction from social media users.
- The fan later defended her decision to go on stage, stating that 'nobody is declining to go on stage' and that she had hoped to be serenaded by Chris Brown instead.
- She explained that she and her mother were moved to VIP seats before the show and that she enjoyed the attention, dismissing criticism from online users.
