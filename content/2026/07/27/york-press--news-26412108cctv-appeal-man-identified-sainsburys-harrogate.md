A man captured on CCTV following a theft from a North Yorkshire store has been identified.

North Yorkshire Police had shared the CCTV footage, saying they wanted to speak to the man in relation to their investigation into a theft from Sainsbury's on Cold Bath Road in Harrogate on Saturday, June 13.

The force said the man featured had now been identified.

They thanked members of the public for sharing the appeal.

The incident reference was 12260109331 .
