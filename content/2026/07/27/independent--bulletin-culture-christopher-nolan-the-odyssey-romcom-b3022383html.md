Christopher Nolan on his 20-year journey to The Odyssey

Oscar-winning director Christopher Nolan has said helming a romcom would be “incredibly difficult.”

Nolan said on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon: “I think people who direct comedy, people who do comedy, I think that’s one of the hardest things in the world to make people laugh that way.”

His acclaimed filmography primarily features action, drama, and psychological thrillers, including Oppenheimer, which earned him his first Oscar wins for Best Director and Best Picture.

The Odyssey, Nolan’s adaptation of Homer's epic poem starring Matt Damon as Odysseus, has achieved critical and box office success, raking in $639.6 million globally so far.

The Independent’s Clarisse Loughrey awarded The Odyssey five-stars, praising Nolan's adaptation and declaring it “his best film to date”.
