A BURGLAR stole a cash till from a popular York garden centre during a 30-minute window.

The incident took place at Skelton Garden Centre, Shipton Road, Skelton, on Thursday, July 23, between 3pm and 3.30pm.

  **Recommended reads:**

The owner noticed during this period that the till drawer was missing and there was a crack in the screen.

Police are seeking witnesses who may have seen anything to assist the investigation.

If you have any information, contact the police on 101 and ask for PC 564, or visit their website and refer to case reference 12260140934.
