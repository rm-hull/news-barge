                                    A new community garden is taking shape in the heart of Knaresborough as part of a partnership aimed at creating a 'greener, more wildlife-friendly' town.

The garden, named Park Corner, is being developed at the junction of High Street and Park Row through a collaboration between Renaissance Knaresborough's BuzzLines initiative and Knaresborough in Bloom.

The site has been designed and planted by volunteers with support from North Yorkshire Council's gardening team.

Many of the plants have been grown from seedlings first cultivated for the community garden at the Terraced Garden in Conyngham Hall.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a670ecc09df871340c.jpg)


The project forms part of the BuzzLines initiative, which aims to create 10 kilometres of pollinator pathways across Knaresborough, linking green spaces and habitats to support bees, butterflies and other pollinating insects.

As well as encouraging biodiversity, the scheme also aims to improve the town's streetscape, encourage walking and promote active travel.

Hannah Gostlow, Chair of Knaresborough in Bloom, said:

"When we learned that the previous planting scheme on the site was coming to the end of its life, we saw an opportunity to work with Renaissance Knaresborough's BuzzLines project to create something that would have a lasting impact.

"We wanted a garden that would be beautiful, sustainable and provide greater benefits for local biodiversity."


She added:

"Knaresborough in Bloom has some amazing gardening volunteers and they jumped at the chance to be involved.

"By working together with BuzzLines, we can create spaces that not only help wildlife but also make our town centre a more welcoming and enjoyable place for residents and visitors.

"Over time, I'm sure Park Corner will become a feature that the town can be really proud of."


The partners hope Park Corner will become a gateway feature for Knaresborough town centre while contributing to a growing network of wildlife-friendly spaces across the town.

Liz Baxandall, from Renaissance Knaresborough, said:

"Park Corner perfectly captures what BuzzLines is all about. Our ambition is to create 10 kilometres of pollinator pathways across Knaresborough, linking habitats and helping nature thrive, but we're also creating greener, more attractive streets and spaces for people.

"By improving the environment for pollinators, we're making the town a more pleasant place to walk, encouraging active travel and helping people connect with nature in their everyday lives.

"It's a wonderful example of what can be achieved through partnership working and community action."
