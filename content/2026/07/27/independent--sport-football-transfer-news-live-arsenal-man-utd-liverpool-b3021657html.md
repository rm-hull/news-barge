Arsenal are plotting an audacious move for Real Madrid superstar Vinicius Jr and want to sign the Brazil winger this summer. The Premier League champions have yet to make contact with Real Madrid over the move for the 26-year-old, who has just one more year on his contract, while incoming boss at the Bernabeu, Jose Mourinho, would need to sanction the transfer.

It could be a busy few weeks for Mikel Arteta’s side as they also look to sign Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes and add another centre-back, with England internationals Ezri Konsa and John Stones both of interest. Newcastle manager Eddie Howe confirmed that he wants to keep Guimaraes, but added that his future at the club is out of his control.

Elsewhere, Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola has set out his targets for the transfer window and has confirmed the Reds “definitely need to sign a winger”, while France international Bradley Barcola is reportedly open to leaving Paris Saint-Germain this summer. PSG, who are set to miss out on the signing Yan Diomande to Real Madrid, would demand a record fee, however.

Manchester United are still interested in adding another midfielder and have “reached an agreement in principle” with France international Manu Kone, according to reports in Italy. Aurelien Tchouameni remains a target, too.

## Kane ready to begin Bayern contract talks

Harry Kane will begin contract talks with Bayern Munich when he returns from his summer holiday

That’s according to *Sky Sports News,* who say that Kane is garnering interest from Barcelona but is very happy in Germany.

Kane’s deal expires at the end of the 2026/27 season and the England captain is said to be very relaxed about the situation but Bayern are keen to start negotiations given the interest surrounding him.

## Joe Gomez injury takes shine off Liverpool’s first win under Andoni Iraola

Andoni Iraola said the injury to Joe Gomez was the “worst news” after he began his Liverpool reign with a 4-2 win over Sunderland in Nashville.

Gomez, 29, was forced off within 10 minutes of Liverpool’s first friendly of pre-season after appearing to suffer a muscle issue

"Probably the worst news has been straight away the injury of Joe,” Iraola said after the win.

“We were happy because we were kind of going through all the training without losing any player. Unluckily for us, straight away we've lost Joe."

## Eddie Howe gives update on Bruno Guimaraes and his Newcastle future

Newcastle boss Eddie Howe has admitted he “does not know” if captain Bruno Guimaraes will remain at the club next season but insisted he wants his best players to stay.

Arsenal are prepared to step up their pursuit of Guimaraes but have been told that a fee of £100m will be needed to sign the Brazil international midfielder.

The *BBC* report that Arsenal are preparing to test their resolve with a £70m offer for Guimaraes, who is said to be keen on a move to London.

Newcastle believe the fee Tottenham paid for Sandro Tonali should set the benchmark for any interest in their captain, yet the Gunners are still said to be poised to “accelerate” their interest, according to the *Telegraph*.

And Howe told *Sky Sports*: "We've had some really good conversations, before the World Cup, during the World Cup and after the World Cup. What we speak about obviously has to remain private, but just a great player, a great person.

“I don't know what's going to happen with Bruno's future. That's for other people to speculate about, but also conversations that I'm not part of.”

## Inter Milan confirm Cristian Romero interest

Cristian Romero has seemed like a good candidate to leave Tottenham this summer and Inter Milan have now confirmed their interest in buying the centre-back.

According to *Fabrizio Romano*, Inter’s sporting director Piero Ausilio has acknowledged that the Argentine defender is of interest to them this summer.

“We are interested for sure, it’s true,” Romano claims Ausilio said. “We spoke to the agent and Tottenham. We will see what happens.”

## Man City, Arsenal and Liverpool out of running for Yan Diomande

Yan Diomande has verbally agreed personal terms with Real Madrid, leaving just the Spanish giants and PSG in the running for the sought-after winger, with Man City, Arsenal and Liverpool having moved on.

That’s according to Fabrizio Romano,, who says that Diomande has now sorted terms with both European giants and it is now up to one of them to agree a price with RB Leipzig.

Real’s latest bid, that was rejected by Leipzig, was €90m plus €10m and the 19-year-old Ivorian while PSG have again been in contact trying to sort a fee.

## Man Utd set-back in Manu Kone pursuit

Roma are becoming frustrated by Manchester United’s approach for their France international midfielder Manu Kone, according to *Corriere dello Sport* in Italy. 

The same outlet reported that United had “reached an agreement in principle” with the 25-year-old, but are yet still to make a bid.

After signing Andrey Santos and Youri Tielemans, Michael Carrick’s side still need reinforcements following the departure of Casemiro and Manuel Ugarte suffering a long-term injury at the World Cup.

They also missed out on the signing of Elliot Anderson, who joined rivals Manchester City on a £116m deal.

Kone, who played for France at the World Cup, is valued by Roma at around €50m, with *Corriere dello Sport* reporting that Atletico Madrid, Chelsea, and Al-Hilal are also interested.

## Forest submit improved bid for Read

Nottingham Forest have submitted an improved £17.5m bid for Feyenoord right-back Givairo Read, according to the *Daily Mail*.

The 20-year-old has spent three seasons at the Rotterdam club and has competed at youth level for the Netherlands.

## Why Arsenal are plotting audacious Vinicius Jr move

Arsenal are plotting an ambitious move for Real Madrid superstar Vinicius Jr and will explore whether a deal is possible this summer.

While there have yet to be talks between the clubs, Arsenal want to land a major signing to improve their attack this summer and Mikel Arteta has been on the hunt for a superstar left winger for years.

On his day, Vinicius Jr is the best left winger in the world and the 26-year-old only has one year left on his Real Madrid contract.

If there would be a time to move for him, it would be now, given that the Spanish side would not want to lose him on a free next summer.

Vinicius would instantly add firepower to Arsenal. He scored 22 goals last season for Real Madrid but talks over a new deal have stalled.

## Juventus explore Fernandes move

Juventus are exploring an ambitious move for Manchester United’s Bruno Fernandes, according to *CaughtOffside.*

Fernandes, who broke the Premier League assist record, enters the final year of his United contract this term but has the option to extend for a further year, and it is believed talks are progressing over a renewal.

However, Juve have apparently held talks with his representatives over a shock exit, though financing his wages could prove a major stumbling block - beyond United simply not wanting him to leave.

## Jose Mourinho wants to keep Vinicius Jr amid Arsenal interest

Real Madrid manager Jose Mourinho does not want to sell Vinicius Jr and wants the superstar winger to line up in attack with Kylian Mbappe and Jude Bellingham next season, according to *The Telegraph*.

Arsenal are in the early stages of exploring a move for the 26-year-old Brazil international, who has one year left on his contract.

Mourinho was controversially reappointed as Real Madrid manager this summer. Mourinho was criticised for comments he made after Vinicius accused Benfica’s Gianluca Prestianni of racially abusing him during a Champions League tie in February.

However, Mourinho is set to tell Real Madrid president Florentino Perez that he wants to keep Vinicius and does not believe there will be a problem in their relationship once he takes charge.
