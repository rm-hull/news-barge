Brighton has perfected the art of radical acceptance. This seaside city doesn't just tolerate difference; it feeds on authenticity like it’s morning coffee.

Yet beneath the surface lies a crucial truth: sanctuary isn't automatic. The wrong accommodation can leave you feeling like a visitor to your own experience, carefully measuring laughter and second-guessing affection. That's why choosing the right place to rest your head matters profoundly.

We've mapped Brighton's most affirming accommodations – places where the staff understand something fundamental: queer hospitality transcends amenities. These hotels recognise that for LGBTQ+ travellers, a hotel room should represent a guaranteed safe space.

When you're planning your Brighton escape, remember that where you sleep shapes how you dream. Choose spaces that see you completely, celebrate you consistently, and send you into each day feeling invincible.

If you’re in town for Brighton’s 2026 Pride, you can expect a community parade along the seafront, a street party featuring your favourite LGBTQ+ performers, and a Pride festival in Preston Park headlined by Raye and Diana Ross.

In Brighton, every sunrise is an invitation to be boldly, brilliantly, unapologetically you.

**1. The New Steine Hotel**

Step outside and you're immediately part of the neighbourhood's rhythm: from the morning coffee run, where regulars discuss last night's drag performances, to an afternoon spent browsing at gay fashion store Prowler, where conversation flows as freely as recommendations.

## FAQs

### When is the best time of year to visit Brighton?

The summer months are the best time to visit this seaside city.

### What percentage of Brighton are LGBTQ+?

The council says that around 11 per cent of the city (over 25,000) identify as lesbian, gay or bisexual. It does not have statistics on the transgender community in Brighton.

### Where is the best LGBTQ+ nightlife in Brighton?

Kemptown has plenty of LGBTQ+ nightlife venues, especially on St James’s Street and St George’s Road. The Marine Tavern and Revenge are among some of the most popular bars and clubs.

### When is Brighton Pride?

Brighton Pride is split into three major events, with the LGBTQ+ Community Parade taking place on 1 August, which sees over 300,000 people participating in the carnival that winds its way from Hove Laws to Preston Park, where the Pride on the Park festival is held. Expect headliners such as Raye and Jessie J, as well as RuPaul doing a DJ set. On Sunday 2 August, Pride on the Park continues with Dianna Ross, Paris Hilton and Five.

On the same weekend, you can also catch the Pride Street Party on the seafront with multiple stages hosting the likes of Gok Wan, Bimini and Bailey J Mills. You can find out more on the Brighton Pride website.
