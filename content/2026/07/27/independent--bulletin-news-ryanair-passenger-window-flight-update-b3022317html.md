Ryanair plane after passenger was partially sucked out of broken plane window

Ljubisa Karovic, 61, was partially sucked out of a broken Ryanair plane window at 20,000ft during a flight from Thessaloniki to Memmingen on 10 July.

He sustained neck and shoulder injuries, and his wife, Svetlana Grković, reportedly pulled him back into the aircraft as he lost consciousness.

Karovic claims Ryanair has only offered to rebook his flight via emails to his son and has not contacted him directly to show concern.

Svetlana Grković also alleged that the crew members “didn't do anything” and “looked scared” during the mid-air emergency.

Ryanair's group chief financial officer, Neil Sorahan, said that their customer care team has been in touch with the family and praised the crew for doing a “phenomenal job” in returning the aircraft safely.
