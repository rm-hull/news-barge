With only three more episodes to go, *X-Men 97* season 2 is fast approaching the finish line — and there are a few big story threads that the Marvel TV show needs to wrap before it takes another extended break from our screens.

How will Professor X establishing X-Corp affect the current world order? What impact will Gambit's resurrection as one of Apocalypse's Four Horsemen have on this season's plot? And where is the Disney+ TV series setting up things to come for season 3? I can't say for sure — but, what I do know is when season 2 episode 7 will air on Disney+, one of the best streaming services. So, scroll on for more details!

## What time can I watch X-Men 97 season 2 episode 7?

 ![Cyclops, Jean Grey, Morph, Forge, and Storm sitting down in X-Men 97 season 2 episode 6](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/WZnqzH9vRXj2zKCsSgtzbH.jpg) 


Episode 7 of *X-Men 97*'s sophomore season will make its Disney+ debut on**Wednesday, July 29**. That's its global launch date, too, so viewers in North and South America won't be able to watch it on the evening of July 28.

Unless the Marvel Phase 6 project's release time is suddenly changed, the entry titled 'Strange Land, Savage Heart' will drop at the same time that its six forebears have:

- **US**— 12am PT / 3am ET
- **Canada**— 12am PT / 3am ET
- **UK**— 8am BST
- **India**— 12:30pm IST
- **Singapore**— 3pm SGT
- **Australia**— 5pm AEST
- **New Zealand**— 7pm NZST
