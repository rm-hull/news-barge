Police have launched an investigation after dangerous driving near York.

It happened at **3.55pm** on**Sunday**(26 July) near the** closed down Jinnah restaurant**, between Flaxton and Barton Hill.

A red Seat car was tailgating a Volvo XC40, as they both drove towards Scarborough.

#### Former Jinnah restaurant, A64

According to police, the Seat was “using the horn and flashing its lights, before overtaking along the grass verge of the central reservation and driving into the Volvo causing it damage and to swerve into the other lane”.

The Seat then drove off at speed overtaking and undertaking other vehicles.

Police are asking for any witnesses to the crash, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact them.
