A 2-year-old boy is among the four people who were injured in a mass shooting that took place at a popular Seattle food festival Sunday.

Gunfire rang out at the annual Bite of Seattle event around 6 p.m. local time, sending crowds of people running for safety. At least three people were killed in the shooting – two were declared deceased at the scene while another succumbed to their injuries at a local hospital.

Police have apprehended one suspect, who surrendered at the scene, and are currently searching for a second, Assistant Police Chief Tyrone Davis said.

“There were two people who we believe were shooting at each other,” he said.

As of Monday morning, two adults who were rushed to Harborview Medical Center with injuries had been discharged while the 2-year-old was in ‘satisfactory’ condition and still being cared for, Harborview Medical Center spokesperson Susan Gregg told *The Seattle Times*.

No details have yet been released about those involved in the attack.

## Recap: Three killed as Seattle food festival devastated by mass shooting

- Three people were killed and five wounded – including a two-year-old boy – during a mass shooting at a popular Seattle food festival Sunday evening.
- The shooting occurred at Seattle Center, a 74-acre entertainment and cultural complex in downtown Seattle, which is home to the iconic Space Needle.
- The venue was hosting the annual Bite of Seattle festival, which draws hundreds of thousands of visitors to its food stalls and live music.
- The victims have yet to be named but among the injured was a 56-year-old woman in serious condition, the two-year-old boy, a 40-year-old woman, a 39-year-old woman and a 23-year-old man.
- City Mayor Katie Wilson said one suspect had been taken into custody while Assistant Police Chief Tyrone Davis said a manhunt was underway to find a second person thought to have been involved.
- Harborview Medical Center, which is treating several victims, said it has gone into “partial lockdown” following guidance from the Seattle Police Department.
- The attack is the latest of at least 271 mass shootings that have been recorded in the U.S. this year, according to the Gun Violence Archive.
