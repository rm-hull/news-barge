A MAN is wanted by police after he skipped sentencing for possessing incident images of children.

West Yorkshire Police is appealing for information to help its officers locate 37-year-old Andrew Marsh who is wanted for failing to appear in court.

Marsh – who is from the Wetherby Area – was due to appear before Leeds Crown Court in April for sentencing in relation to possession of indecent images of children.
