Scaffolding collapses from high-rise building as Typhoon Noul looms over Hong Kong

Scaffolding on a high-rise construction building in Hong Kong collapsed due to high winds from the incoming Typhoon Noul.

The incident occurred on Cheung Sha Wan Road on Saturday, 25 July, with footage showing the bamboo scaffolding buckling and crashing onto a building below.

No injuries were reported following the scaffolding collapse.

Typhoon Noul hit southern China, including Hong Kong, on Sunday, 26 July, bringing powerful winds and heavy rainfall.

Reported as the strongest typhoon to make landfall this year, Noul led to the evacuation of over 715,000 people from Guangdong province.
