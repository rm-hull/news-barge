# Emma Roberts ties the knot with actor Cody John

- Emma Roberts, known for her roles in *American Horror Story*, has married fellow actor Cody John.
- The couple tied the knot on Saturday in Idaho, according to *Vogue* magazine.
- Roberts wore an off-white Monique Lhuillier wedding gown, telling the publication: "I didn’t really know what I wanted, I just knew that I wanted it to feel very vintage-inspired."
- She reflected on the difference between her real-life nuptials compared to her on-screen weddings.
- Roberts and John began dating in 2022 and announced their engagement on Instagram in 2024.
