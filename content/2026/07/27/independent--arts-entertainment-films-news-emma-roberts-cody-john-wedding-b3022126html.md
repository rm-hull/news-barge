Actress Emma Roberts, known for her roles in *American Horror Story*, has married fellow actor Cody John. 

The 35-year-old, daughter of Eric Roberts and niece to Hollywood icon Julia Roberts, married John on Saturday in Idaho, according to *Vogue* magazine.

Speaking to the publication during a fitting for her off-white Monique Lhuillier wedding gown, Roberts reflected on the significance of the occasion.

"I’ve been married in a movie before, and I tried out wedding dresses for that, which feels so weird. So now, it’s the real deal," she shared, highlighting the difference between on-screen and real-life nuptials.

Describing her vision for the dress, Roberts explained: "I didn’t really know what I wanted, I just knew that I wanted it to feel very vintage-inspired."

She added, "I’m obsessed with antique books and treasure hunting, and I love collecting old ephemera, so I really wanted that to shine through in the dress."

Despite her husband's laid-back approach – Roberts noted John "wouldn’t care if I wore jeans" – she was confident he would appreciate her chosen attire, stating he would "definitely die" for the elegant gown.

She reportedly wore a black dress by the same designer for the after-party.

Roberts shares a child with her ex-boyfriend, actor and musician Garrett Hedlund, who she was in a relationship with between 2019 and 2022.

She began dating John in 2022, and announced their engagement on Instagram in 2024.

The announcement included a picture of the couple smiling with their arms around each other as she flashed a large engagement ring.

Roberts has appeared in FX’s *American Horror Story’s Coven, Freak Show, Cult, Apocalypse, 1984* and*Delicate* seasons from 2013 onwards, and will star in its upcoming season 13 later this year.

She has also appeared in films such as*Nancy Drew (2007), Scream 4 (2011)* and*We’re The Millers (2013).*

John has appeared in shows such as*Wu-Tang: An American Saga* and*In The Dark.*
