Outlander star Sam Heughan revealed that actors on the popular historical drama were "perhaps forced" into filming nude scenes during the show's early production.

Heughan stated that intimacy coordinators were not present in the initial seasons, leading to negotiations around nudity that were "not particularly nice."

The actor, who played Jamie Fraser, noted that the television industry had fewer guidelines and protections when Outlander began in 2014.

He highlighted that intimacy coordinators were introduced in later seasons, which he described as providing "great protection" for performers.

Heughan also discussed the challenging experience of filming a scene where his character was raped, reflecting on how the industry has since changed.
