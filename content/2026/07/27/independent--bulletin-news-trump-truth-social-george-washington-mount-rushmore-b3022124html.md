Donald Trump engaged in a late-night posting spree on Truth Social, sharing a series of bizarre A.I.-generated images and messages.

The posts included a mocked-up movie poster depicting Trump as a superhero saving George Washington at Mount Rushmore, with a tagline suggesting he would save history.

Trump's social media activity also featured several hints at a potential 2028 presidential bid, including images with 'TRUMP 2028' hats and mugs.

A significant portion of the posts focused on the U.S.war with Iran, showing A.I.-generated images of military jets attacking Kharg Island and Trump overseeing military operations with captions like 'Guardians of the World'.

Other posts included an 'air filter barrier' at the U.S.-Canada border, referencing complaints about wildfire smoke, and a manipulated image of CNN anchor Kaitlan Collins related to a Budweiser promotion, echoing grievances from a recent speech.
