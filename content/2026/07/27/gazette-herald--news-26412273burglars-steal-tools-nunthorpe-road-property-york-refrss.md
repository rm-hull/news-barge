*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Tools worth £3,500 were stolen during a burglary in York at the weekend.

The incident occurred at a property on Nunthorpe Road, York, between 5pm on Saturday, July 25, and 9am on Sunday, July 26.

Police officers are asking anyone who witnessed anything out of the ordinary in the area, or who may have CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage from the relevant time period, to come forward.

  **Recommended reads:**

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who saw suspicious individuals or vehicles on Nunthorpe Road or the surrounding streets around the time of the offence."

Anyone with information is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260142956, or Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Information can also be emailed to Brandon King at [Brandon.King@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Brandon.King@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
