Jeremy Clarkson, star of the docuseries Clarkson’s Farm, revealed he left 200 acres of his land uncultivated this year, stating it has become impossible for farmers to make money from growing food.

He emphasised the urgent need for government intervention to address the severe financial difficulties faced by the farming sector, asserting that farmers cannot currently profit from their crops.

Clarkson voiced strong concerns regarding the appointment of Dame Angela Eagle as Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, questioning her qualifications due to her perceived lack of farming or business background.

He criticised the new prime minister, Andy Burnham, for this decision, suggesting it would exacerbate challenges for farmers and likening it to other inappropriate leadership appointments.

The article also mentioned Clarkson's recent health update, confirming his aggressive cancer diagnosis last year, which was detected early, and his subsequent remission following successful treatment.
