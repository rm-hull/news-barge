THIEVES have targeted a popular North Yorkshire food hall and made off with two "high value" e-bikes.

North Yorkshire Police said two electric mountain bikes - one a gold-coloured Focus model - were stolen at Fodder in the Great Yorkshire Showground in Harrogate, around 4.20pm on Friday, July 17.

  


The force is for information to help recover the stolen bikes.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Email PC 1824 Chatland on [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

"Or please call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

"Please quote reference is 12260136500 when providing information."

This year's Great Yorkshire Show finished at 6pm on the same day.
