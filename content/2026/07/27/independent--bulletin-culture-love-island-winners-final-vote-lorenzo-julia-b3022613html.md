# Winners of Love Island 2026 revealed after dramatic finale

![Julia and Lorenzo react after winning the show](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/21/34/Love-Island-winners-revealed.png)

- Lorenzo Alessi and Julia Majchrzak have been voted as the winners of Love Island season 13.
- The pair overcame Kavan Murphy and Jasmine Muller to win the £50,000 prize.
- Simba Kudyiwa and Angelista Gunda finished third.
- In a final twist, Yasmin Hadlow and Tommy Stagg were dumped from the island at the beginning of Monday’s episode.
- They were eliminated by Mica Harris and Samraj Toor – contestants who had been dumped themselves during Sunday’s episode by fellow love islanders who had been eliminated earlier in the series.
