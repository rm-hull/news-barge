LONG delays are being reported on a main road in North Yorkshire.

Temporary traffic lights on the A64 at Rillington are leading to traffic queues with delays of around 40 minutes.

Vehicles are queuing all way back to Scagglethorpe in the west and West Knapton in the opposite direction.
