Thousands of pounds worth of stock has been stolen during a series of shop raids in and around York.

A group of men targeted various shops in York, Kirkbymoorside, Sherburn in Elmet and the surrounding area over two days, February 25 and 26, resulting in the theft of more than £2,780 worth of goods.

North Yorkshire Police said this was an organised event involving several unidentified males.

They are now appealing to the public for help in identifying the men captured on CCTV as they may have information to assist their investigation.

  **Recommended reads:**

Any information can be sent to [Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk), or shared through their website.

Alternatively, people can contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 or reach out to Crimestoppers anonymously at 0800 555 111, quoting reference 12260035570.

The authorities are keen to solve this case and bring the perpetrators to justice.
