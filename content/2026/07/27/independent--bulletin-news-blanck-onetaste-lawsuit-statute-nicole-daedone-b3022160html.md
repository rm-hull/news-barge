A former OneTaste saleswoman is asking a judge to dismiss the company's lawsuit, arguing it was filed after California's three-year deadline for fraud claims had already expired.

OneTaste alleges Ayries Blanck violated a 2015 settlement agreement by publicly discussing allegations against the company and disclosing confidential settlement terms despite agreeing to keep them private.

Blanck's attorney argues the company knew, or should have known, about her public statements beginning in 2018, years before it sued her in October 2022.

The court filing contends OneTaste failed to investigate the alleged disclosures within the legal time limit and could have preserved its claims by initially suing unnamed "Doe" defendants.

The legal fight comes months after OneTaste co-founder Nicole Daedone was sentenced to nine years in federal prison following her conviction on a forced labor conspiracy charge related to the company.
