Robert Downey Jr. is set to play Doctor Doom, the primary antagonist, in the upcoming Marvel film, Avengers: Doomsday.

Downey Jr. expressed significant excitement over the return of original X-Men stars Rebecca Romijn and Kelsey Grammar as Mystique and Beast.

The film boasts a large ensemble cast, including Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Pedro Pascal, and Florence Pugh, alongside other returning X-Men actors like Ian McKellen and Patrick Stewart.

An extended trailer for Avengers: Doomsday premiered at Comic-Con, which fans preferred over the previously released “disappointing” first trailer.

The film, which is the first installment of a two-part Avengers finale, is scheduled for release on 18 December.
