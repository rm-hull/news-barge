## ‘Exceptional’ period home hits the market in York for £1.1 million – look inside

Outside at the six-bedroom terraced house for sale in York Road, Acomb, for £1.1 million *(Image: Carter Jonas/Zoopla)*

An “exceptional” period home has hit the market in York – for £1.1 million.

The six-bedroom terraced house is for sale in York Road, Acomb.

Carter Jonas, which is marketing the property, said it is a “magnificent period residence” which has been “lovingly restored and thoughtfully enhanced by the current owners”.

  “Great care has been taken to preserve and reinstate many of the home’s original period

  features to an exceptional standard, resulting in a truly elegant and versatile family home that seamlessly combines timeless character with modern convenience,” a Carter Jonas spokesperson said.

  Inside the six-bedroom terraced house for sale in York Road, Acomb, for £1.1 million 

*(Image: Carter Jonas/Zoopla)*

The property’s design was overseen by renowned York architect Walter Green Penty, whose designs include the Lighthorseman pub building in Fulford Road.

Inside, a entrance hallway leads to four reception rooms – one of which is currently used as a ground floor bedroom.

Three of the reception rooms have open fire plans, while the sitting room provides open access to the modern kitchen.

The kitchen leads out to the garden, offering a space for outdoor dining.

A three-piece shower room completes the ground floor.

  


Four double bedrooms, many retaining original fireplaces, are on the first floor, along with two en-suite bathrooms.

The home’s main bathroom has a freestanding bath and separate walk-in shower.

Upstairs, the second floor features self-contained living accommodation and includes two more double bedrooms, a modern shower room and an open-plan kitchen/living area.

Outside, the home has a south-facing garden, as well as a garage and secure driveway to the rear.

There is also a large outhouse, which is currently used as utility room.
