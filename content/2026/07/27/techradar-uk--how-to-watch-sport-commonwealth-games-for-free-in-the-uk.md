Less prestigious than the Olympics but much more of an occasion than the various international governing bodies' world championships, the Commonwealth Games has its roots in the past but its eyes on the future.

74 Commonwealth Games Association member states, spanning Europe, Asia, Africa, the Caribbean, the Americas and Oceania, are participating at Glasgow 2026, many of which barely get a look-in on the global stage.

For instance, Antigua and Barbuda, Bangladesh, Belize, the British Virgin Islands, Brunei, the Cayman Islands, the Cook Islands, Eswatini, the Gambia, Kiribati, Lesotho, Malawi, the Maldives, Malta, Nauru, Papua New Guinea, Rwanda, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, the Seychelles, Sierra Leone, the Solomon Islands, Tuvalu and Vanuatu are yet to win Olympic silverware, yet they're being strongly represented at the 2026 Commonwealth Games.

Eyeing their first Commonwealth Games silverware in Scotland this summer are Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Belize, Brunei, Eswatini, the Falkland Islands, Gabon, Gibraltar, the Maldives, Montserrat, Rwanda, Saint Helena, Sierra Leone, Togo, and the Turks and Caicos Islands.

It's this scarcity of acclaim that makes the event so special, so read on as we explain how to watch the Commonwealth Games for free in the UK.

### Can you watch Commonwealth Games for free in the UK?

Yes. **S4C** and**BBC Alba** are each providing free Commonwealth Games coverage in the UK, with live streaming available via BBC iPlayer. However, the catch – and it's a big one – is that S4C and BBC Alba are, respectively, Welsh-language and Gaelic-language channels.

Another free-to-air option is **5**, which is airing a nightly highlights show.

Extensive free Commonwealth Games coverage, meanwhile, is available in many countries around the world, with the best English-language options being **7Plus** in Australia and**CBC Gem** in Canada. 

Free-to-air **ThreeNow** in New Zealand is also showing select events from Glasgow 2026 live.
