# Power your summer adventure – Jackery’s guide to choosing the right portable power station for every trip

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/7/35/Main-Image.jpeg?trim=0,150,0,150&crop=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)



## Not every outdoor adventure needs the biggest battery. Understanding how much power you actually need can help you choose a portable power station that's practical, portable and perfectly suited to your summer plans.

From family camping trips in the Lake District to music festivals, campervan tours of the Scottish Highlands and weekends by the Cornish coast, more Britons are embracing outdoor adventures this summer. While escaping the grid has its appeal, most people still want to keep everyday essentials powered – whether that's charging a phone, running a portable fridge or making a morning coffee.

That's where portable power stations come in. And Jackery, a global leader in portable power solutions, can help you choose the right one.

Unlike traditional petrol generators, portable power stations provide clean, silent and emission-free electricity, making them ideal for campsites, festivals and family holidays. They're easy to use, require little maintenance and can even be recharged using portable solar panels, offering greater independence during longer stays outdoors.

But with a growing range of sizes and capacities available, how do you know which one is right for your trip?

**Match the power station to your adventure**

The biggest mistake many first-time buyers make is assuming they need the largest battery available. In reality, the best portable power station depends on how you travel and what you plan to power.

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/7/47/PIC-1.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)



**Day trips, picnics and festivals**

If you're heading to the beach, spending the day at a country park or camping overnight at a festival, your power needs are likely to be fairly modest. Charging smartphones, tablets, cameras, drones, Bluetooth speakers and LED lighting requires relatively little energy.

For these lighter-duty adventures, the Jackery Explorer 500 v2 is an ideal entry-level option. With a 512Wh capacity and 500W output, it provides enough power for everyday essentials while remaining compact and highly portable at just 5.7kg. It also supports ultra-fast charging, reaching full capacity in as little as one hour when using combined AC and solar charging, while its long-lasting LiFePO₄ battery is designed for years of reliable use.

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/7/17/PIC-2.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)



**Weekend camping and family holidays**

Families spending a few nights camping or travelling in a campervan often need to power a wider range of equipment. Portable fridges, laptops, coffee machines, lighting and small cooking appliances all add to your daily energy requirements.

For these scenarios, the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 strikes an excellent balance between portability and performance. Offering a 1070Wh capacity and 1500W output, it's capable of running multiple devices simultaneously while remaining compact enough to transport easily. Whisper-quiet operation makes it particularly well suited to campsites where noise can disturb neighbouring campers, while its durable LiFePO₄ battery and advanced battery protection technology are built to withstand years of regular use.

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/7/57/PIC-3.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)



**Extended off-grid adventures**

For motorhome owners, caravanners or anyone planning several days away from mains electricity, a larger power station provides greater flexibility. Whether you're using an induction hob, electric kettle, portable grill or charging several devices at once, higher capacity means fewer compromises.

The Jackery Explorer 2000 v2 is designed for these more demanding situations. With a substantial 2042Wh capacity and 2200W output, it can comfortably power most everyday appliances while remaining significantly lighter and smaller than many comparable units. Multiple charging options – including AC, vehicle charging and solar – make it easy to top up wherever your journey takes you.

**Get even more from solar charging**

For longer camping holidays or extended off-grid stays, pairing a portable power station with Jackery SolarSaga solar panels allows you to generate renewable electricity throughout the day. Instead of relying solely on campsite hook-ups or returning home to recharge, you can harness solar energy to keep your power station topped up while reducing your environmental impact.

It's a practical solution for anyone spending several days outdoors, particularly during the longer daylight hours of the British summer.

**Power your holiday with confidence**

Portable power stations have become an increasingly valuable travel companion, helping families, campers and outdoor enthusiasts stay connected and comfortable without sacrificing the peace and quiet of the outdoors.

The key is choosing the right capacity for your style of travel rather than simply buying the biggest model available. Whether you need lightweight power for festival weekends, reliable energy for family camping or a higher-capacity solution for extended motorhome adventures, Jackery offers a portable power station to suit every journey.

With fast charging, durable LiFePO₄ batteries, intuitive operation and the option to recharge using SolarSaga panels, it's easier than ever to enjoy a comfortable, sustainable and worry-free summer adventure, wherever the road leads.
