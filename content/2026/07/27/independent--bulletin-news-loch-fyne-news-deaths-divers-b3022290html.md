Two women, aged 26 and 24, have died while diving at Loch Fyne in Argyll and Bute on Sunday.

Emergency services were called to reports of divers in difficulty at about 3.30pm on Sunday, with one woman pronounced dead at the scene and the other's body later recovered following a rescue operation.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: “The deaths are being treated as unexplained and a post-mortem examination will take place in due course.”

Separately, two people died after getting into difficulty at sea off Fistral Beach in Newquay on Sunday night.

Another man died and a second was hospitalised after being pulled from the water in West Wittering on Sunday.
