Search intensifies for missing Elizabeth Waddell who vanished on vacation in Grenada

Elizabeth Waddell, 44, of North Carolina, disappeared in Grenada on July 22 (Royal Grenada Police Force)

Elizabeth "Liz" Waddell, a 44-year-old woman from Cary, North Carolina, has been missing since Wednesday, July 22, when she vanished during a vacation in Grenada.

Waddell was last seen at Grand Anse Beach in St George's, Grenada, where she reportedly went for a swim, leaving her towel on a pier, and did not return.

Her husband, Cailen Waddell, has traveled to the Caribbean island to assist in the search and has issued a public plea on Facebook for anyone with connections in Grenada to share information.

The Royal Grenada Police Force has requested public assistance, specifically appealing to fishermen, boat operators, and residents along the southern coastline for any sightings or relevant information.

A friend, Amanda Romano-Harmon, described Waddell as an "amazing person" and "free-spirited," urging anyone with even the smallest detail to come forward to help bring her home.
