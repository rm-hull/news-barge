Police have issued photographs of a man officers want to speak to after a "criminal damage incident” at a North Yorkshire pub.

North Yorkshire Police said it is appealing for help to locate Jamie Alan Marsden after the alleged incident at the Black Bull pub in Thirsk last Thursday (July 23).

  Police want to speak to this man about the reported incident at the Black Bull pub in Thirsk last Thursday (July 23) 

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

The force said officers want to speak to Marsden, 34, about the reported incident at the Market Place pub which reportedly happened between 5.30pm and 5.45pm.

Police urged anyone who sees Marsden to phone 999 and those with information about his whereabouts to dial 101.

Information can be reported anonymous via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Quote incident number 12260140970 when providing information.
