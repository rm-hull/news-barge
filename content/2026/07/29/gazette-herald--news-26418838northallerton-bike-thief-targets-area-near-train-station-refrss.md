A BIKE thief has targeted an area close to a North Yorkshire railway station.

The county's police force said the cycle was stolen from Ferguson Close in Northallerton on Thursday, July 2.

  



Police have issued CCTV pictures of a man the force said it would like to identify saying he may have information which could assist its investigation.

A force spokesperson said: "Can you help us identify the man in these images?

"Please contact us online in the first instance by emailing [joe.simms@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joe.simms@northyorkshire.police.uk.)"

Information can also be shared by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101 or anonymously through Crimestoppers at 0800 555 111.

The police reference is 12260124404.
