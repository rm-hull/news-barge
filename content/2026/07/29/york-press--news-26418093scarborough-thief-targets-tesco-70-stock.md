A THIEF has targeted a supermarket in a North Yorkshire town and made off with £70 of stock.

The county's force said the theft happened at the Tesco supermarket in Westwood, Scarborough on Saturday, July 4.

  


Police have released a CCTV image of a man they want to speak to, saying he may have information which could support its investigation into the theft.

Anyone who recognises the man or has information is urged to email [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively contact the force on 101 or Contact Crimestoppers to provide information anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260125468.
