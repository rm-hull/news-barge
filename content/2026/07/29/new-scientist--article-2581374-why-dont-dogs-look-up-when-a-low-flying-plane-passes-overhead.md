**Why don’t dogs look up when a low-flying plane or helicopter passes overhead? I’ve never seen one do this.**

*Pam Lunn**Kenilworth, Warwickshire, UK*

Advertisement

This isn’t an answer but a deepening of the mystery. Rauer Island in Norway is a military site with significant natural importance. Near the shoreline, atop a tall tree, is an osprey nest. The birds display significant and prolonged alarm if boats come near the shore or if people on foot approach the tree. They show not the slightest concern at low-flying military helicopters in their airspace.

*Richard Maddra**Queensbury, West Yorkshire, UK*

Our most recent dog, Blu, regularly looked up at low-flying aircraft. He would stand still and turn his head to track it, then resume whatever he had been doing beforehand when it disappeared from sight.

He was the most intelligent dog I have ever known, teaching himself to open doors and letting himself out of the vet’s
