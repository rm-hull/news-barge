# Driver taken to hospital following three-vehicle collision on A1(M) near Knaresborough

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following a three-vehicle collision on a major road near Knaresborough.

The incident happened at around 11.19am on Friday, July 24 on the A1(M) northbound, just prior to Junction 47.

It involved a white Tesla, a white Mercedes Sprinter and a white Volkswagen Transporter.

The driver of the Tesla was taken to hospital by ambulance for treatment following the collision.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses following a three-vehicle collision on the A1(M) northbound, just prior to Junction 47, near Knaresborough](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmQ0YzUzYTYwLTYwNWQtNGQ2ZC1hY2NlLWE2NWIwOTM5YWZiZToyY2E1MmFmZS1mNjYyLTQ3ZjUtOTg2Ni04YzJiYTg5YmIzOWM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision, seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dash cam footage.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260141509 when providing any details regarding the incident.
