Thousands of runners, volunteers, and supporters are set to descend on York This weekend – as the York 10K returns to bring another day of energy, fundraising and celebration.

Delivered by Jane Tomlinson’s Run For All in partnership with Arla Protein, the popular event attracts participants of all ages and abilities each year, alongside hundreds of volunteers and spectators who line the route to cheer them on.

The charity race will take place through the heart of our historic city this **Sunday 2 August**.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/york-10k-road-closures-2026-300x200.webp)

**YorkMix** are proud to be the official media partner for the event once again, and we’ll be cheering you all on from the start and finish line!

Showcasing some of York’s most iconic landmarks, including Clifford’s Tower, York Minster and the city’s ancient walls, the scenic 10K is set to **start at 9:30am on Knavesmire Road**, near York Racecourse before looping around and finishing back on Knavesmire.

## Route map for York 10k

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/york-10k_route-map-new-1000x1167.webp)

As the event acts as a fundraising vehicle for many charities, the day is expected to raise substantial sums for a host of good causes. Participants are encouraged to fundraise for either a chosen charity, or one of the event’s partner charities, including:

- Jane Tomlinson Appeal
- Family Fund
- RSPCA
- St Leonard’s Hospice
- The Hut York
- York Against Cancer
- York Carers Centre
- York City Foundation
- York Down Syndrome Support Group
- Mind York

There’s also plenty on offer for younger runners, with the **York Mini and Junior Runs** getting underway from 12pm. Designed for children aged 3-8, the 1.5km Mini Run sees youngsters take part alongside a parent or guardian, while the 2.5km Junior Run is open to children aged 9–14.

## Route map for York Mini and Junior runs

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/york-mini-junior-map-1000x1300.webp)

Every young participant will receive a commemorative medal at the finish line, along with a selection of post-run goodies to celebrate their achievement.

Tristan Batley-Kyle, operations director at Run For All commented: “Excitement is already building for the return of the Arla Protein York 10K this weekend.

“With thousands of participants set to take to the streets of York, it’s shaping up to be another memorable day of achievement, fundraising and celebration.

“The event is about much more than crossing the finish line. It’s an opportunity to bring communities together, support incredible charities and inspire people of all ages to get active.

“We can’t wait to welcome runners, volunteers, supporters and families to what promises to be a fantastic day.”

Stuart Ibberson, Arla brand director, comments: “We’re proud to be returning as title sponsor of the Arla Protein York 10K for 2026. The event perfectly reflects what Arla Protein stands for – encouraging people of all ages and abilities to challenge themselves, stay active and celebrate what they can achieve.

“It’s fantastic to see thousands of runners, volunteers, families and supporters coming together in the heart of York, while also raising vital funds for charities making a real difference in local communities. We can’t wait to cheer everyone on this August and help make it another memorable day.”

Bottled water will be available on the course and at the finish. There will also be an array of food and drink outlets offering refreshments in the Event Village.
