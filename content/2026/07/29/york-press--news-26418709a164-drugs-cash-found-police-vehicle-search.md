THREE men have been arrested after a vehicle was stopped by police after being seen on a busy main road in East Yorkshire.

Officers from Humberside Police’s Kinetic Roads Crime Team said the vehicle was allegedly connected to criminality, and it was sighted on the A164 near Eastrington, close to Howden, on Tuesday, July 28.

  



A search led to the "discovery of a large amount of cash and suspected class A drugs", police said.

A spokesperson for the force said two men - aged 24 and 25 - and a 30-year-old man were arrested for allegedly possessing criminal property and being concerned in the supply of class A drugs.

The 30-year-old man was also arrested for allegedly being concerned in the offer to supply class A drugs.

Humberside Police said the three men remain in police custody and the force enquiries remain ongoing.
