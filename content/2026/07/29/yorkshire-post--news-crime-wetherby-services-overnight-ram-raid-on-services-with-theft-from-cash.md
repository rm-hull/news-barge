# Wetherby Services: Overnight ram raid on services with theft from cash machines

## Detectives are appealing for witnesses and information following a ram raid involving cash machines at Wetherby Services overnight.

A black vehicle, believed to be an older-model Nissan Juke, was driven into the services through doors near the M&S Food store.

The vehicle was then used to repeatedly ram a standalone ATM.

The incident occurred at approximately 2am on Wednesday July 29.

![In particular, detectives would like to identify and speak to three men who may have witnessed the incident and could hold information relevant to enquiries.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjIyMWM5ZTNmLTFjOWItNGUzZi05ZDZjLTgxMjQzMmM4ZmU3NDo0MjEwOWVlYi1kZGIwLTRiMWQtOTYxMy1jODdlYTAyMzEwZWM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Four men wearing face coverings and gloves were involved, police said.

Two of the suspects then attached what is believed to have been a chain to an “&PLAY” cash payout machine, which was pulled free using the vehicle.

The machine was subsequently loaded into the rear of the Nissan Juke.

A Nissan Juke has since been recovered in the Leeds area, police said.

The vehicle had been set on fire and the stolen cash machine was found inside.

Officers are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident, saw the Nissan Juke travelling between Wetherby and Leeds between 1am and 5am, or has any information that could assist the investigation.

In particular, detectives would like to identify and speak to three men who may have witnessed the incident and could hold information relevant to enquiries.

They are urged to come forward as soon as possible.

North Yorkshire Police said: “We would like to emphasise that, at this stage, we believe these men are witnesses and do not believe they were involved in the offence.

"Their information could prove important in helping us establish the full circumstances surrounding the incident.

“Witnesses or anyone with information are asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101. Alternatively, call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555111.

“You can also use our live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

“Please quote incident number 12260145086 when providing any information to make sure it gets to the right person as quickly as possible.”
