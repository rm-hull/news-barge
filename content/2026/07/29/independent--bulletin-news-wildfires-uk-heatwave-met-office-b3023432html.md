Wildfires sweep southern Europe forcing thousands from their homes

Fire chiefs have warned that the risk of wildfires across the UK remains high, with over 200 blazes recorded in the past two weeks.

The country is preparing for its fourth heatwave of the year, with temperatures expected to reach 35C in south-east England by Wednesday.

The Met Office indicates that fires igniting in London, the South East, and eastern England could be exceptionally severe due to hot, dry conditions.

The UK Health Security Agency has issued an amber heat-health alert for several regions, effective from Tuesday to Thursday, highlighting increased health risks for vulnerable groups.

The National Fire Chiefs Council emphasised that continued dry conditions and warmer temperatures are creating an environment where fires can start and spread rapidly, urging public caution.
