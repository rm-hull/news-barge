Two North Yorkshire towns will host special Yorkshire Day celebrations this summer, promising a weekend of artisan markets.

The three-day event, organised by Little Bird Made, will begin at Knaresborough Castle on Friday, July 31 and continue on Saturday, August 1 — Yorkshire Day itself — before moving to Harrogate on Sunday, August 2.

The markets will feature regional food and drink, crafts, and live music, offering a full weekend experience for visitors of all ages.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "Yorkshire Day is all about pride, community and great food, paired with a full weekend of artisan markets, you have the perfect family day out.

"Whether you’re donning your favourite flat cap or enjoying a picnic in the park, there is something for everyone this Yorkshire Day weekend."

Stalls will be open from 10am to 3pm each day.

Artisan trader Ryan Pearson, owner of Bird and Bramble, said: "Knaresborough Castle is a stunning venue for a diverse artisan market.

"Come along, browse the stalls, treat yourself, and enjoy the views and a great day out."

For further information on Little Bird Artisan Markets, or how to book a stall at one of the events, email [info@littlebirdmade.co.uk](mailto:info@littlebirdmade.co.uk)
