Footage from Los Angeles takes viewers inside the 'Hospital of Emotions', an immersive art exhibition housed in the former St. Vincent Medical Center.

Once-clinical rooms have been transformed into colourful installations featuring flowers, hospital equipment, projections and interactive displays that invite visitors to reflect on memories, grief and resilience.

The exhibition features around 80 installations created by 70 artists across four floors of the long-closed US hospital.

Each room explores a different emotion, turning former wards, emergency rooms and treatment spaces into artworks inspired by personal and shared experiences.
