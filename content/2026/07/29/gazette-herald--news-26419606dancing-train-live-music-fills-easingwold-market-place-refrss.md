A DANCING train wound its way around a North Yorkshire town's market place as a rousing summer concert pulled in the crowds.

Easingwold's regular free Summer of Music concert in the Marketplace featured a captivating performance by vocalist Carla, better known as the Northern Soul Sista, who filled the air with Tamla Motown and Northern Soul classics.

Carla's set included 'Get Ready' by The Temptations and 'Needle in a Haystack' by The Velvelettes, alongside favourites like Tommy Hunt's 'Loving on the Losing Side', Shirley Ellis's 'Soul Time' and Arthur Conley's 'Sweet Soul Music'.

  A dancing train formed in Easingwold during its weekend of live music. 

*(Image: Submitted)*

The musical afternoon concert concluded with an encore of The O'Jays' 'Love Train', with attendees forming a dancing train that wound through the Marketplace.

Sunday saw the town come alive once more, as Easingwold hosted its annual 10K road race, with the event drawing hundreds of runners and supporters who cheered them on.

  Easingwold Town Band entertained the runners after the annual 10K race. 

*(Image: Submitted)*

After the race, the live entertainment resumed, with performances from the Easingwold Town Band, followed by The Young Ones.

Their lively set of 1950s and 1960s favourites, including classics by The Beatles, The Tremeloes, The Hollies, and The Everly Brothers, provided the perfect soundtrack to wrap up the weekend.

  The Young Ones entertained onlookers with firm favourites. 

*(Image: Submitted)*

Town Councillor Frank Johnston-Banks said the Summer of Music programme, with its mix of genres and talented performers, had been a hit with the community, with the Northern Soul Sista's performance hailed a highlight of this year's programme.

He said: "Weekends such as this remind us what makes our town of Easingwold so special: great music, a wonderful community spirit and people coming together to enjoy everything Easingwold has to offer."
