Police are appealing for information in connection with a fraud investigation in York city centre

It happened on Merchant Gate at 12.30pm on Friday 5 June 2026 and involved three Asian women aged between 40 and 60 approaching an elderly woman.

The victim reported being coerced into giving up thousands of pounds in jewellery and cash.
