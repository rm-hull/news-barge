## FAQs

### Does Heathrow Airport Parking offer discount codes?

Heathrow Airport Parking occasionally releases discount codes and promotional offers that can help reduce the cost of your booking. However, not every deal requires a code. Many of the best savings are available as automatic discounts when you book online, particularly if you reserve your parking in advance.

### What is the cheapest Heathrow Airport parking option?

Park and Ride is usually the most affordable Heathrow Airport parking option. You'll park your car in a secure car park before taking a complimentary shuttle bus to your terminal, with journey times typically taking around 15 minutes.

### Is it cheaper to book Heathrow Airport parking in advance?

Yes, booking in advance is one of the easiest ways to save money on Heathrow Airport parking. Prices tend to increase closer to your departure date, especially during school holidays and other busy travel periods. You can often combine advance booking rates with a discount code or promotional offer.

### Can I amend or cancel my Heathrow Airport parking booking?

In most cases, you can amend or cancel your booking, but this depends on the option you've selected and booking conditions. Flexible bookings generally allow changes up to a certain point before your arrival, whilst some lower-priced options may have more restrictions. It's worth reviewing the cancellation policy before confirming your booking.

### Is Heathrow Airport parking secure?

Parking facilities at Heathrow Airport have high security measures with CCTV, secure fencing, regular security patrols and controlled entry and exit points. Security may vary depending on the parking option you choose, but all official Heathrow parking facilities are monitored to help keep vehicles safe while you're away.
