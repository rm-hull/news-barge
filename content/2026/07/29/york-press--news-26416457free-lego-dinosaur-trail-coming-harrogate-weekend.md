A free dinosaur-themed LEGO trail is coming to Harrogate this weekend.

The Dinky Dinosaurs Trail will launch in the town centre from August 1, featuring 13 large LEGO dinosaur models displayed at businesses and venues.

Participants can collect a trail map from The Crown Hotel or Games Crusade in Oxford Street.

By finding each dinosaur, scanning QR codes, and collecting letters to reveal a secret word, families can complete the trail.

Bethany Allen, operations manager at Harrogate BID, said: "We're incredibly excited to reveal the locations for this year’s Dinky Dinosaurs LEGO Trail.

"By placing the dinosaurs across the town centre, we're encouraging visitors to discover different streets, support local businesses, and enjoy everything Harrogate has to offer during the summer holidays."

Once completed, the trail map can be returned for a small prize.

Those who complete the trail can also enter a prize draw to win a £50 Harrogate Gift Card via the Harrogate BID website.
