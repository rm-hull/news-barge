A restaurant found in a Yorkshire seaside town is worth a visit if you are looking for somewhere to escape to by the coast.

While many people will be jetting off on holiday, it can be nice to spend some time exploring the local area during the summer.

Saltburn-by-the-Sea is a hugely popular seaside resort with plenty of things to do and great restaurants, including the Seaview Restaurant.

The "outstanding" restaurant has "unrivalled views of the sea" thanks to its terrace area where you can enjoy a meal while watching the sunset.


## What is the Seaview Restaurant in Saltburn-by-the-Sea like?

The Seaview Restaurant featured on the Remarkable Places to Eat show where Fred Sirieix and Nadiya Hussain tried the spot’s famous Crab Brioche.

The restaurant is described as offering "an extraordinary setting" with floor-to-ceiling windows so guests can enjoy watching the pier and sea.

The restaurant’s website says: "With a laid-back old-school Ibiza vibe, our outdoor, open-air terrace with chic furnishings comes with jaw-dropping views across the dramatic coastline of Huntcliff."

The Seaview Restaurant has a range of starters including moules mariniere, seafood cocktail, and sardines on toast.

The famous Saltburn crab brioche features on the main menu alongside dishes like lobster linguine, lemon sole, and market fish.

For those wanting a more traditional experience, you can find classic cod, haddock, and Whitby scampi served with chips, as well as mushy peas and curry sauce available to add on.

### 
  **What do visitors of the Seaview Restaurant say?**

Guests on Tripadvisor have said the restaurant is in a "superb location" with "friendly" and "efficient" service.


One reviewer said: "Great restaurant with great service and excellent food.

"We ate there twice and both times was amazing.

"We had cod and chips, tuna, oysters (which we got the younger waiter to try).

"The homemade taramasalata with bread was beautiful.

"Afterward we had some cocktails, espresso martini and a new one on the menu called Amalfi Sour.

"Well worth a try."

Another person praised the restaurant and said: "Food is outstanding.

"The fish was perfectly cooked and had so much flavour.

"The lobster mash is expensive but worth it!

"Staff were absolutely lovely and service was excellent.

"It was a lovely evening watching the waves while we ate."
