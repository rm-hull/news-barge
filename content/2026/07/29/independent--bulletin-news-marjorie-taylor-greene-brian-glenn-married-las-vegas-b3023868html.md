Marjorie Taylor Greene and pro-MAGA journalist Brian Glenn married in Las Vegas this week.

The couple wed at the Bellagio Hotel on Tuesday, with Greene in a white gown and Glenn in a tux, and were accompanied by a cameraman.

Clark County online records confirm the couple applied for a marriage license last Friday, following their engagement last December after about three years of dating.

Greene and Glenn met in 2022 while both were going through divorces, with Glenn describing Greene as "mystical" after seeing her at a Trump rally.

Greene resigned from Congress in November, with her final day in office on January 5, following a public rift with Trump.
