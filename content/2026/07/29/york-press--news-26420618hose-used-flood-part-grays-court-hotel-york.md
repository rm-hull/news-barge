A hose has been used to flood part of a top York hotel, police said.

Officers have issued a photograph of a man they want to speak to about the incident at Grays Court Hotel in Chapter House Street, near York Minster.

North Yorkshire Police said an investigation is underway into the “criminal damage” which happened on Wednesday, June 17.

The force on Wednesday (July 29) issued the photograph, saying the man pictured “will have information that could help our investigation”.

Anyone who recognises the man is asked to email [richard.coulthard@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:richard.coulthard@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260113645 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
