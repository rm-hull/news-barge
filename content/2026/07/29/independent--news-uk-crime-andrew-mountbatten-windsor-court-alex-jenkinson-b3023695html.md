Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor will give evidence against the man accused of threatening him near his Sandringham home.

Alex Jenkinson, 39, allegedly accosted the former prince, 66, on 6 May.

Andrew was walking the late Queen Elizabeth’s corgis in King’s Lynn, Norfolk, at the time.

The charge says that Jenkinson “used towards Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor threatening, abusive or insulting words or behaviour with intent to cause that person to believe that immediate unlawful violence would be used against him by any person, or to provoke the immediate use of unlawful violence by him whereby that person was likely to believe that such violence would be used, or it was likely that such violence would be provoked”.

Jenkinson faces a second like charge, relating to a man named Stephen Terry, in the same location the day before.

A one-day trial will take place on 21 December, Chief Magistrate Paul Goldspring said on Wednesday.

![Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, formerly Prince Andrew, pictured in 2022](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/08/15/1245816367.jpg)

Andrew is due to give evidence at the trial via video-link.

Two other witnesses for the prosecution are also due to give evidence.

Jenkinson did not appear at Westminster Magistrates' Court on Wednesday. He is currently living in temporary accommodation in a hotel.

Jenkinson, formerly of King Street, King’s Lynn, is charged with two charges of using threatening, abusive or insulting behaviour with intent to cause fear of or provoke unlawful violence.

He is on conditional bail on the condition he does not contact Andrew or Mr Terry, enter Norfolk, or go near the royal residences in Sandringham, Balmoral, Windsor, Highgrove or Buckingham Palace.

![Andrew now lives in Marsh Farm on the Sandringham Estate in Norfolk](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/05/00/39bc68d7cd15e8a617b01404e3483517Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzgwNjAzMzc0-2.83642423.jpg)

Andrew himself was arrested in March on suspicion of misconduct in public office over his connections with paedophile financier Jeffrey Epstein. He was later released under investigation.

Andrew, who became the first senior royal in modern history to be arrested, is accused of sharing sensitive information with Epstein while serving as the UK’s trade envoy.

The former duke was detained on his 66th birthday following allegations that he shared reports of official visits to Hong Kong, Vietnam and Singapore with Epstein.

He has vehemently denied any wrongdoing over his Epstein links.

Andrew moved to Marsh Farm on the Sandringham estate earlier this year after his older brother, King Charles, evicted him from Royal Lodge in Windsor.
