A man wanted for recall to prison may be in North Yorkshire, police said.

North Yorkshire Police said Paterson Prentice is wanted for allegedly breaching his licence conditions.

The force said it is believed that Prentice, 36, could be in Northallerton or Thirsk.

Prentice, of Northallerton, also has links to the Hambleton area, police said.

He is described as white, 5ft 11in tall, short dark brown hair, stocky build, brown eyes, dark beard, and he has a Yorkshire accent.

Police urge anyone who sees Prentice to call 999 and those with information about his whereabouts to dial 101.
