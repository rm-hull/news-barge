Former Republican congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene quietly married her fiancé, pro-MAGA journalist Brian Glenn, in Las Vegas this week, according to a report.

Greene, 52, was seen in a white wedding gown while Glenn, 57, donned a tux on Tuesday as they wed at the famous Bellagio Hotel in Las Vegas, TMZ reported.

The couple was spotted holding hands as they walked through the hotel, accompanied by a cameraman who captured the entire celebration. There was also a group of children with the couple during their celebrations, according to the report.

On Monday, Glenn appeared to tease their upcoming nuptials, sharing a photo of him and Greene kissing on X and writing, “The clock is ticking.”

Neither Greene nor Glenn have publicly commented on their wedding. However, Clark County online records confirm the couple applied for a marriage license last Friday.

![Marjorie Taylor Greene and Brian Glenn tied the knot Tuesday in Las Vegas, according to a report](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/08/18/2213616582.jpg)

The former Georgia congresswoman and Glenn got engaged last December after roughly three years of dating.

At the time, Glenn, a staunch supporter of President Donald Trump, broke the news on X, sharing an image of the two beaming at a restaurant, with Greene showing off her ring, and writing, “She said yes.”

Greene later reshared the post, adding, “Happily ever after!!! I love you @brianglenntv!!!”

Greene and Glenn were previously married, but met while they were both going through divorces in 2022, *The Washington Post* reported. 

Greene first caught Glenn’s eye at a Trump rally, with Glenn telling the*Post*, “She was, like, mystical — like a unicorn or something like that, you know?” 

He told the newspaper he was immediately drawn to her Georgian accent and “very gentle, almost angelic soul.”

![Marjorie Taylor Greene and Brian Glenn announced their engagement on X in December 2025](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/12/16/1/40/G8PtnITXoAQzxUo.jpg)

“People don’t see the side of Marjorie that I do,” he later told the*Post*. “When you take her out of politics, she’s sweet. She’s sweet!” 

Despite his love for Greene, Glenn, a fervently pro-Trump reporter, was left in a tough spot after the president and Greene had a nasty public rift, with Trump dubbing her a “Traitor” for criticizing him over his handling of the Epstein files.

In November, Glenn shared a photo online of himself standing alone after a Trump campaign rally, writing that the image, “pretty much represents where I’m at right now.”

In May, Glenn left his role as a journalist for conservative outlet Real America's Voice to spend more time with Greene.

Greene, previously one of Trump’s closely allies, also announced her resignation from Congress in November, writing in a statement, “I have too much self respect and dignity, love my family way too much, and do not want my sweet district to have to endure a hurtful and hateful primary against me by the President we all fought for, only to fight and win my election while Republicans will likely lose the midterms.”

“It’s all so absurd and completely unserious,” she added. “I refuse to be a ‘battered wife’ hoping it all goes away and gets better.”

Greene’s final day in office was January 5.
