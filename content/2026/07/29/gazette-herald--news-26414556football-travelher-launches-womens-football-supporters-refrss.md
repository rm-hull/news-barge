A new York-based travel company has launched to make it easier for fans to support the women's game.

Football TravelHER is a specialist tour operator dedicated to supporters of women's football in the UK and beyond.

Founded by Corinne Wray and Heather Raimes, the company was created to remove barriers for fans attending matches, especially away and European games.

Ms Wray, co-founder and co-director, said: "After many years of talking about working within the women’s football industry, Heather and I recognised a real gap in the market and decided to take the leap.

"As long-time supporters who have travelled extensively to follow women’s football, we know first-hand the challenges fans can face when trying to attend matches, particularly away fixtures and European games.

"We created Football TravelHER to remove as many of those barriers as possible and make attending matches easier, more accessible and more enjoyable.

"Ultimately, we hope that by helping more supporters get to games, we can play a small part in the continued growth of the women’s game and contribute to stronger attendances across leagues and competitions."

The company offers match tickets, accommodation, transport and curated cultural experiences as part of its packages.

For more information, or to view upcoming packages, contact the team directly.
