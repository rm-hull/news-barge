Police are investigating reports of anti-social driving in a Selby car park.

Officers say they are aware of the ongoing issues relating to anti-social driving at the **Three Lakes Retail Park**.

In a community message, PCSO Mason Midgley said: “We recognise the impact this behaviour is having on local residents and businesses.

#### Three Lakes Retail Park, Selby

“The manner of driving being reported is completely unacceptable and poses a significant risk to members of the public and other road users.

“This type of behaviour will not be tolerated.”

A community event is being organised where residents will have the opportunity to discuss their concerns with officers and learn more about the measures being implemented to tackle the behaviour.

PCSO Midgley added: “We appreciate the continued support of the community and encourage anyone with information relating to anti-social driving to come forward and report to North Yorkshire Police.”
