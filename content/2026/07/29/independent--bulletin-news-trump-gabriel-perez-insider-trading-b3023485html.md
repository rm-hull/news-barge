# Trump’s White House teleprompter operator accused of insider trading leaves job

- Gabriel Perez, Donald Trump's long-time teleprompter operator, is no longer working for the government following allegations of insider trading.
- He was placed on unpaid leave after federal investigators examined claims he used advance knowledge of Trump's speeches to win over $100,000 on the prediction market Kalshi.
- White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt confirmed Trump personally decided to place Perez on leave, describing the allegations as "deeply unfortunate and frankly a disgrace."
- While federal prosecutors reportedly declined a criminal investigation, regulators have allegedly reached a settlement requiring Perez to return his profits and refrain from similar betting.
- The incident has raised concerns about government employees exploiting sensitive information on prediction markets, leading to White House warnings and potential legislative measures.
