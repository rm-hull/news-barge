                                    There's one last chance to win a full tank of fuel this week as Your Harrogate's popular Free Fuel Friday returns on Friday (31st July).

The promotion, which launched on 24th April, has become a favourite with listeners looking to scoop a full tank as fuel prices continue to soar.

So far, 13 lucky motorists from across the Harrogate district have had their tanks filled for free, with winners coming from Harrogate, Knaresborough, Ripon and surrounding areas.

This Friday, one more listener will join that growing list by tracking down the famous Free Fuel Fairy after a series of clues are revealed on air.

Your Harrogate Breakfast presenter and Free Fuel Fairy Ricky Durkin said:

"It's always the highlight of my week! With fuel costs still a concern for so many people, it's fantastic to be able to put a smile on someone's face every Friday.

"If you're out and about this week, keep listening to Your Harrogate – you never know where the Free Fuel Fairy will pop up next!"


To be in with a chance of becoming winner number 14, make sure tune in to Your Harrogate on Friday morning and follow us on social media.

Listen out for the clues, find the Free Fuel Fairy and ask the magic question:

"Are you Your Harrogate's Free Fuel Fairy?"


One lucky winner will drive away with a full tank of fuel – just in time for the weekend.
