## York pub has chance to reclaim its crown in popular annual competition

The Crooked Tap in Acomb won Best Pub in the 2025 York Press competition and could retain its crown this year *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

A PUB in York has the chance to regain its crown as winner of a popular annual competition.

The Crooked Tap, in The Green, Acomb, has been shortlisted by readers of The York Press as one of the best pubs in the city in 2026.

Press readers previously voted the Crooked Tap as their Pub of the Year 2025.

The pub boasts a healthy 4.4 out of 5 on Trip Advisor and a 100 per cent recommendation from Facebook reviews.

Recent comments have praised The Crooked Tap for its vibrant atmosphere, live music each weekend, and hosting of matches from the men's football World Cup.

Performances on stage included Micky Johnson, The Glass Collectors and in support of July's AcombFest 26, a "bumper line-up of music" was laid on at the venue.

  The Crooked Tap in Acomb won Best Pub in the 2025 York Press competition and could retain its crown this year 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

Summer food offers have included Asian street food from Little Somboon Box and recently, Verdant Brewing Company "took over the taps" at The Crooked Tap.

And signs outside the window on Tuesday, July 28 showed pop-up offerings from Clucking Oinks, Philly's Wood Fired Pizza York making eight weekend appearances in 2026, as well as Yuzu street food on the first weekend in September.


As reported by The Press, when readers voted The Crooked Tap as its best pub in 2025, manager Jimmy Hebbron said: "The theme with a lot of pubs in the competition seemed to be the community feel that they’d created for their locals, so it looks like Acomb spoke the loudest this time.

“We want everyone to be able to have a great time here – whether they’re coming in the good or bad times, they can pull up a stool and have a chat."

At the turn of the year, Jimmy said 2026 was shaping up nicely with new music acts, the pop-up food options, the launch of running clubs, plus events including Cinco De Mayo, Oktoberfest and its Christmas lights in June.

Jimmy added: “This to us is more than a pub it's a community, friends have been made here for life and whilst we would love you to visit us, our message is - visit the pub!"

Owned by Crooked Brewery, the Crooked Tap first opened its doors around seven-and-a-half years ago.

Specialising in craft beer, the pub serves as a taproom for Crooked Brewing, offering a range of beers from across Yorkshire.

The pub is also dog-friendly, with some visitors even bringing their cats along for the evening.

  *Voting slips for The York Press Best Pub 2026 are in copies of the newspaper up to and including Saturday, August 5 and must be posted off to the address provided to count.*

  *The winner will be announced shortly afterwards.*
