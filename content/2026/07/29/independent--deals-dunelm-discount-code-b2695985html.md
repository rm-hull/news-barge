## Free delivery and returns

Standard UK delivery is free when you spend £60 or more (large items excluded), and there are plenty of other options available, depending on how quickly you need your order. If you’re in a hurry, Dunelm’s free click & collect service lets you pick up your items in as little as two hours.

Returns are also free, either by post or in-store, depending on what is most convenient for you. In-store tends to be the quickest way to get your money back, however, if you can’t make it to your local store, you can arrange a parcel collection or drop-off for small items through your online account. For larger items, you’ll need to contact the customer service team directly to arrange a collection.
