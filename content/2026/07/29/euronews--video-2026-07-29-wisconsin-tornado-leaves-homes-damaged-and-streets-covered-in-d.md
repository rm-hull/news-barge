Residents in Menasha, Wisconsin, are assessing the damage after a tornado tore through the US Midwest region, leaving homes in pieces and without power.

The tornado left widespread destruction in its wake across Menasha and neighbouring Appleton, with debris scattered across streets and utility poles damaged.

Fallen trees blocked roads, while residents cleared branches and debris from their properties.

The storm has left residents facing costly repairs as emergency teams continue to assess the impact.
