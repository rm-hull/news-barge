A major search was launched when a child went missing in Market Weighton last night (Tuesday).

Emergency services were called in at **10.43pm**.

The search centred on the River Lane area.

#### River Lane, Market Weighton

A Humberside Police spokesperson said they carried out a “special service to assist the police search for a missing child.

“A drone and thermal image camera in use in the surrounding area including water courses and ditches.

“Police found the child safe on land.”
