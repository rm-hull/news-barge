## A key figure of the French touch house scene, Kavinsky wrote "Nightcall", the track made famous by the film "Drive", performed at the Paris 2024 Olympic closing ceremony alongside Angèle and Phoenix.

French DJ Kavinsky was found dead at his Paris home on Tuesday evening, the Paris prosecutor's office said on Wednesday 29 July. Aged 50, Vincent Belorgey, his real name, was one of the leading figures of the French touch, the house music movement, notably alongside Daft Punk, Justice, Cassius and DJ Mehdi.

The prosecutor's office said that "*an investigation to establish the causes of death*" had been opened, noting that no suspect was found at the scene on Tuesday evening.

"*France has lost one of its most distinctive voices*", culture minister Catherine Pégard said in a message on social network X. "* Both danceable and nostalgic, his music will go on crossing generations and borders.*"

One of his most popular tracks remains *Nightcall*, the dark, slow-tempo synthwave hit included on the soundtrack of* Drive* (2011), the successful thriller starring Ryan Gosling. The track enjoyed a new lease of life during the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

The DJ performed at the Stade de France alongside Belgian singer Angèle and pop-rock band Phoenix, who reworked *Nightcall* for the occasion during the Olympic closing ceremony.

A self-taught pianist born in Seine-Saint-Denis, Kavinsky launched his career in the early 2000s, opening in particular for Daft Punk and sharing the stage with other emerging names on the electronic scene, such as SebastiAn. Kavinsky would have turned 51 on 31 July.
