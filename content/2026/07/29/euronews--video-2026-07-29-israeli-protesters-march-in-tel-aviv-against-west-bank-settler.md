Footage from Tel Aviv shows Israeli protesters marching through the city before police block their route. Demonstrators chant, hold signs condemning settler violence in the West Bank and gather around a participant wearing masks depicting US President Donald Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

The rally was organised to denounce violence by Israeli settlers in the West Bank, which protesters say has intensified in recent months. They accused the government of failing to protect Palestinian communities and called for stronger action.

Police maintained a heavy presence throughout the march, blocking parts of the planned route as the demonstration continued in central Tel Aviv.
