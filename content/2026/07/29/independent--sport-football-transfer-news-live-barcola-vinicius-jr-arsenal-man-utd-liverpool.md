Liverpool have agreed terms with Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola, according to reports in France, but are still a considerable distance away from agreeing a fee for the French international. Barcola wants to join Liverpool, according to***L’Equipe*, but the European champions will demand £140m this summer. 

Liverpool may also face fresh competition from Arsenal after Vinicius Jr made it clear that he wants to remain at Real Madrid this summer, according to *Sky Sports*. The Gunners have been plotting an audacious move for the 26-year-old Brazil star while he has one year remaining on his contract, but Vinicius Jr wants to agree new terms at the Bernabeu. 

Chelsea FC appear set to strike a surprise double deal for England veterans Danny Welbeck and Jordan Henderson as Xabi Alonso seeks to add experience to his squad, while Manchester United could be running out of time to meet Roma’s deadline to sign France international Manu Kone, according to reports in Italy.

*Follow the latest**transfer news**, rumours and reports with our live blog below*

## Vinicius Jr set to stay at Real Madrid

Arsenal will have to turn to other transfer targets after it was reported by Sky Sports that Real Madrid do not want to sell Vinicius Jr this summer.

Real Madrid are reportedly confident that the Brazil winger, who emerged as a shock target for the Gunners as they aim to strengthen their attack, will agree a new contract after entering the final year of his deal.

*AS* also reported that Vinicius, who is out of contract in 2027, will resume talks on a new deal once he returns from his summer holiday.

Real Madrid’s agreement to sign RB Leipzig’s Yan Diomande also added to the speculation - but the club want Vinicius to extend his deal, too.

## Bradley Barcola 'agrees to join Liverpool'

Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola has agreed to join Liverpool, according to a report in the French sports daily *L’Equipe*. 

It is undoubtedly a boost to Liverpool ahead of potentially facing competition from Premier League rivals Arsenal for the France international, but a deal remains some way off.

That’s because PSG have set a price tag of £140m on the 23-year-old, and do not want to sell - especially as Real Madrid look likely to sign RB Leipzig’s Yan Diomande.

As previously reported, PSG are using the Morgan Rogers and Elliot Anderson deals as a benchmark, while Barcola remains under contract with the European champions.

When asked about his targets this summer, Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola said a wide player was top of his list.

## Arsenal 'prepared' to break British transfer record to sign Julian Alvarez

Arsenal are prepared to break the British transfer record to sign Julian Alvarez from Atletico Madrid, according to Teamtalk.

*The Independent* first reported on Arsenal’s interest in Julian Alvarez in April and a three-way battle between the Gunners, Paris Saint-Germain and Barcelona was expected this summer. Barcelona’s approach for Alvarez angered Atletico and they have reportedly set their LaLiga rivals a deadline of July 31 to accept their offer, which will need to be around €150m.

Having missed out on Morgan Rogers, Arsenal would arguably have more resources available should Barcelona fail to land their priority target and Gunners boss Mikel Arteta is prepared to take a patient approach for the former Manchester City forward and Argentina international. Alvarez’s preference would be Barcelona, and it would take a record bid from Arsenal to get the deal over the line.

## Why do Arsenal want to sign Vinicius Jr?

Arsenal want to land a major signing to improve their attack this summer and Mikel Arteta has been on the hunt for a superstar left winger for years.

On his day, Vinicius Jr is the best left winger in the world and the 26-year-old only has one year left on his Real Madrid contract.

If there would be a time to move for him, it would be now, given that the Spanish side would not want to lose him on a free next summer.

Vinicius would instantly add firepower to Arsenal. He scored 22 goals last season for Real Madrid but talks over a new deal have stalled.

## Eddie Howe offers update on future of Bruno Guimaraes

Newcastle boss Eddie Howe has admitted he “does not know” if captain Bruno Guimaraes will remain at the club next season but insisted he wants his best players to stay.

Arsenal are prepared to step up their pursuit of Guimaraes but have been told that a fee of £100m will be needed to sign the Brazil international midfielder.

The *BBC* report that Arsenal are preparing to test their resolve with a £70m offer for Guimaraes, who is said to be keen on a move to London.

Newcastle believe the fee Tottenham paid for Sandro Tonali should set the benchmark for any interest in their captain, yet the Gunners are still said to be poised to “accelerate” their interest, according to the *Telegraph*.

And Howe told *Sky Sports*: "We've had some really good conversations, before the World Cup, during the World Cup and after the World Cup. What we speak about obviously has to remain private, but just a great player, a great person.

“I don't know what's going to happen with Bruno's future. That's for other people to speculate about, but also conversations that I'm not part of.”

## Huge fee set for Bradley Barcola

Widespread reports suggest that Paris Saint-Germain are open to selling Bradley Barcola – but only for a fee of around £145m or more. Pricey!

*The Times***suggest that Arsenal and Liverpool both have interest, though may be unwilling to pay the terms PSG desire with the French capital club using the Morgan Rogers and Elliot Anderson deals as a benchmark.

## Inter Milan join race for Tottenham captain Cristian Romero

Cristian Romero is expected to leave Tottenham this summer and Inter Milan want to sign the Argentina defender.

Spurs would be willing to let Romero leave if they receive the right offer, according to *Sky Sports*, following a tumultuous season.

Inter’s sporting director Piero Ausilo has confirmed the Italian club’s interest in the player, who previously played in Serie A with Alatanta.

"It's definitely a deal we are trying to sign with the player and the club [Tottenham],” Ausilo said. “These things take some time but there's definitely interest. Let's see."

## Chelsea set to sign Maxence Lacroix as medical planned before £52m move

Maxence Lacroix will have a medical at Chelsea before a £52m move to leave Crystal Palace, but what could the France international bring to Stamford Bridge?

The 26-year-old centre-back is set to sign a six-year deal to boost the Blues’ defence. Lacroix played in a back three under Oliver Glasner at Crystal Palace, a system previously favoured by Xabi Alonso, but the right-footed defender is also versatile and can play in a back four.

He brings authority to defence as well as excellent recovery pace, and his arrival at Chelsea could lead to the departures of Trvevoh Chalobah and Axel Disasi.

## Juventus boss claims Emiliano Martinez 'wants to leave Aston Villa'

Emiliano Martinez wants to leave Aston Villa this summer, Juventus boss Luciano Spaletti has claimed.

According to *Gazzetta dello Sport,* Spaletti said: “He's a goalkeeper who wants to change clubs, and we're looking for competitiveness. But we currently have two [keepers]."

Juventus have placed Argentina and Aston Villa goalkeeper Martinez at the top of their summer wish list, but Villa director of football operations Damian Vidagany revealed earlier this month that Martinez would not be sold.

Aston Villa have already sold key players Morgan Rogers and Youri Tielemans this summer.

## Chelsea make surprise move for Brighton striker Danny Welbeck

Chelsea are in talks to sign veteran striker Danny Welbeck from Brighton, according to Press Association sources.

The 35-year-old has emerged as a surprising target to reinforce the Blues’ attacking options, following an impressive season where he netted 13 Premier League goals in 37 appearances.
