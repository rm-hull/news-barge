A serious crash has closed a road in North Yorkshire this morning (Wednesday).

It’s affecting the A170 near Pickering.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are currently dealing with a two-vehicle road traffic collision on Wrelton Cliff Road between Pickering and Wrelton.

#### Crash location

“The road is closed in both directions and is expected to be for some time.

“Motorists are advised to find alternative routes where possible in the meantime.”

People living in the area have been told the road could stay closed until this afternoon.
