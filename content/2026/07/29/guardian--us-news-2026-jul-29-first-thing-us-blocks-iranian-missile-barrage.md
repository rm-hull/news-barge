The US military has claimed it knocked down an Iranian missile barrage and worked with Saudi forces to strike sites in Iraq that Tehran-backed militias have used to launch attacks in recent days. In what Washington cast as “an attempted surprise attack” by Tehran, Iran had launched ballistic missiles at US forces in the Middle East, shattering a brief pause in fighting. US Central Command (Centcom) said all Iranian missiles launched on Tuesday were intercepted.

Iran’s attack came as the Israeli prime minister, Benjamin Netanyahu, visited Washington. Netanyahu said he had an “excellent” meeting with Donald Trump. The closed-door meeting lasted nearly an hour and a half and was “positive and productive,” the White House press secretary, Karoline Leavitt, said.

**Japan earthquake death toll rises as rescue teams search for missing**

![Aerial image of a rescue operation in a damaged factory building](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/35ae27d4922455463426cd4d83302b49c8630935/102_0_2448_1958/master/2448.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

At least 13 people were killed and hundreds injured when a 6.8-magnitude earthquake struck southern Japan on Tuesday. The prime minister, Sanae Takaichi, said on Wednesday: “There are people who are still waiting to be rescued, and it’s a race against time.”

The Japanese military mobilised 3,600 personnel for disaster relief operations and sent 20 aircraft to assess the damage from the air, the defence minister said. Hundreds of first responders from neighbouring prefectures arrived in the affected areas on Tuesday evening. The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) warned that areas with the biggest tremors would remain at risk of further strong quakes and landslides for about a week.

Donald Trump is pushing the health secretary, Robert F Kennedy Jr, a longtime vaccine opponent, to be more aggressive on cutting vaccines in the US, according to reporting from the Wall Street Journal. Kennedy recently cut the routine childhood immunization schedule in an unprecedented move.

Trump has a long record of anti-vaccine statements. As senior leaders have downplayed the safety and importance of vaccines, measles in the US has surged to its highest rate in 30 years, and there has been a rise in deaths from whooping cough, flu and tetanus in recent years.

**US bans humanoid robots from China, citing ‘unacceptable risks’**

![A worker on a line producing humanoid robots](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/c27c6f20216c7e2a4c0c695e887ecc0fd0c4fd41/2_0_6631_5305/master/6631.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

The Federal Communications Commission has announced a ban on humanoid robots from China, saying the technology poses “unacceptable risks” to the country’s national security. The FCC defines the banned robots as “advanced robotic devices”, which include humanoids and animal-like quadrupeds.

“Advanced robotic devices collect data that could be leveraged by malign actors to surveil Americans, enhance the capabilities of foreign intelligence services, or to remotely commandeer the robots,” FCC officials wrote. In addition, the FCC barred connected power inverters, which enable renewable energy sources and batteries to connect to grids and datacenter equipment.
