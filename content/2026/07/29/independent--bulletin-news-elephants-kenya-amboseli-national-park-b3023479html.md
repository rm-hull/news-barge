An investigation has been launched after 15 elephants died mysteriously in Kenya's Amboseli National Park region over the past month.

The Kenya Wildlife Service reported that ten elephants exhibited signs of partial paralysis before succumbing, while five others were found dead and partially consumed by scavengers.

The majority of the deceased were female elephants and their calves, with only one male among the fatalities.

Preliminary laboratory tests by the University of Nairobi detected a “potential toxic substance”, though separate analyses by the Government Chemist found no evidence of the specific toxins tested.

Further environmental assessments are underway to determine the cause, with authorities reassuring the public there is currently no evidence of human transmission risk.
