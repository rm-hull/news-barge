A Yorkshire brewery is teaming up with a running club to host a pair of scenic trail races this autumn.

Wold Top Brewery at Hunmanby Grange is working with Bridlington Road Runners to organise the Wold Top 5k and 10k.

The events will take place on Saturday, September 5 and follow all-terrain routes across the Yorkshire Wolds.

A 1k children’s fun run will also be held as part of the event.

Both routes start and finish at the brewery.

Alex Balchin, brewery director and a keen runner, said: "Starting from the brewery, you'll run around the Wold Top farm, finishing back at the brewery bar for a well-deserved pint and some food."

Entry for the 5k race costs £12, or £10 for affiliated runners, while the 10k costs £22, or £20 for affiliated runners.

Finishers will receive a medal and a pint, with food, music, and access to the brewery bar throughout the day.
