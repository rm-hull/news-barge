# Watch dramatic moment smoke billows from 20-acre Yorkshire crop fire as farmers help to contain blaze

## Firefighters worked alongside farmers to contain a crop fire in Yorkshire on Tuesday.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said they were called about the fire - which involved standing crops in a field off Bridlington Road - at 4:19pm.

![Farmers helped firefighters to contain a crop fire in Hunmanby on Tuesday 28 July.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/29/16/47/Red-And-Black-Modern-Tips-Trick-YouTube-Thumbnail-(1600-x-1080-px)-(1600-x-1080-px)-(1600-x-1080-px).jpg?trim=7,0,7,0&width=640&quality=65)



Firefighters worked alongside local farmers using water bowsers to contain the fire - which covered approximately 20 acres - and prevent it from spreading further.

The service said crews remained at the scene into the evening damping down hotspots.
