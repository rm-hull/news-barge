Harrogate Town AFC has confirmed Reece Smith has joined League Two side Walsall for an undisclosed fee.

Reece signed from Maidenhead ahead of the 2025/26 season and would be an almost ever-present in Simon Weaver’s side for the entire season, making a total of 52 appearances.

Reece departs having also made a big impression off the pitch, being named PFA Community Champion for his work with our Foundation in and around the local community.

The club would like to thank Reece for his efforts and wish him well for the future.
