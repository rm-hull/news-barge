# Teenage girl allegedly sexually assaulted in Yorkshire supermarket

## Police are appealing for a woman to come forward following a reported sexual offence in a supermarket in Wakefield.

Officers were called to the ASDA supermarket at around 3.45pm on July 23 to reports that a man had sexually assaulted a teenage girl within the store.

A 28-year-old man was arrested at the scene on suspicion of sexual assault and also assaulting emergency services offences, police said.

In a statement, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “We understand that a woman who was shopping in the kitchen aisle confronted the suspect and officers are urging her to come forward to assist with the ongoing enquiries into this incident.
