## 'It's pretty scary' - residents shocked as man 'stabbed' near York city centre park

There was no cordon in place when The Press visited the scene today *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

YORK residents have spoken of their shock after a man was allegedly attacked with a knife outside a busy city centre park.

As reported by The Press this afternoon, North Yorkshire Police says a 37-year-old man was arrested last night (Thursday, July 23) and the area around Museum Gardens was cordoned off after a cyclist was allegedly attacked.

The cyclist, who is in his 50s and from Leeds, suffered slash wounds from a carpentry-type knife in Museum Street in York at 8.08pm, police said.

The alleged victim was discharged from hospital after receiving treatment for injuries to his head, face, and hand, the force added.

A 37-year-old man was arrested at 8.17pm – nine minutes after the attack took place, according to the police.

The day after the incident, people gathered in Museum Gardens as usual with the busy park seemingly unaffected by the incident, as families and tourists made the most of the good weather.

  Museum Gardens remained busy 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Outside, people said they were shocked by incident – with several stating that they were alarmed that it had happened so close to the popular gardens.

Two women, who were visiting the city from Sheriff Hutton, said that while the incident was alarming, it wouldn’t stop them visiting York.

One of the women, who didn't want to be named, said: “We don’t come to York often, mostly because it’s just so busy, but it’s a lovely city and we'll still come back."

The other added: "This is really shocking to hear about though – especially considering it’s grown men and not teenagers. People should know better.”

  Museum Street 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Also shocked was a bartender from a pub in nearby Lendal who said she and her colleagues knew nothing about the alleged incident until she was approached by The Press.

She said: “It’s pretty scary that something like that could happen so close – we had no idea. I wasn’t working last night, but one of our other staff members was and he didn’t say anything.”

Another member of staff added: "You think we'd have heard something, it's really crazy."

Sam, a bartender at nearby Museum Street Tavern, said that the incident was shocking and that it had been a ‘weird night’ for those out for the pub's Thursday night karaoke.

  The area in front of Museum Gardens 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

He said: “I wasn’t working last night, but I came in here for the karaoke. When I walked up Museum Street, there were loads of blue lights – we saw three police vans and an ambulance there. One of our regulars was there and he said he thought someone might have been attacked.

“The ambulance left quite quickly, so we just thought that it was nothing. In the months that I’ve worked here, we’ve seen a few things happen nearby, but this is definitely the most worrying.”
