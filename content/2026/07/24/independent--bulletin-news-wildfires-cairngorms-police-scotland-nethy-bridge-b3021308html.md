The blaze's behaviour has become increasingly unpredictable due to changing weather conditions, including stronger winds, leading to concerns it is spreading towards Nethy Bridge.

Homes around Nethy Bridge have been evacuated as a precautionary measure, with residents directed to a respite centre and access roads closed.

Over 500 firefighters, specialist resources, and helicopter water-bombing operations have been involved since the wildfire was first reported on 15 July.

First Minister John Swinney urged the public to respect exclusion zones, noting that the fire's direction has worsened due to weather changes, with smoke reaching as far as Aberdeen.
