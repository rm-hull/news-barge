Search the Public Notice PortalParts of a Grade II-listed country estate near Ripon could be transformed into a new private events venue under plans submitted to North Yorkshire Council.

Copt Hewick Hall, in Dishforth Road, dates back to the 18th century.

The estate includes gardens, woodland and a collection of buildings which historically supported running of the Hall.

  


The proposal for the site, validated on July 21, seeks planning permission to use part of the grounds, together with the stable block, garage, glasshouse, outdoor kitchen, garden stores and garden room as an events venue.

A maximum of 75 to 79 events would be held each year, Planning Potential Ltd said on behalf of the application.

It added that of these, around 69 would be car club/launch events, along with a smaller number of weddings - predominantly in the summer - and occasional other events of a "similar or lesser size".

A planning statement submitted with the application said: "The proposed change of use will operate in a manner sympathetic to the historic character of the estate, making use of existing buildings with no physical intervention within the wider landscape.

"All activity would be contained and managed within the site."

The remainder of the estate, including formal gardens and woodland, would remain unaffected.

Access to the site via car would continue to be taken from the hall’s existing entrance off Dishforth Road.

The applicant has proposed separating guest and service access routes to help manage arrivals and reduce on-site congestion.

Event parking would be provided on areas of existing ground and lawns near the main hall.

To manage access and reduce potential disruption, the applicant has suggested several options.

These include a one-way traffic system within the site, clear visitor directions, and on-site parking and drop-off areas.

The application remains under consideration by North Yorkshire Council.
