FANS of a much-loved range of soft toys are being reminded that they only have a few weeks to stock up on their favourites.

Castle Howard's dedicated Jellycat shop in its Stable Courtyard, featuring the summer collection of the playful and highly collectable soft toys only runs for another month.

Located in Castle Howard’s free-to-enter Stable Courtyard, the shop is open until Monday, August 31.

Adam Barber, head of retail at Castle Howard, said: "We’re absolutely delighted to host a Jellycat shop.

"Their jolly ranges make for beautiful gifts and souvenirs from a day out exploring the House and Gardens.

"We’re excited to continue to surprise visitors with top-quality shopping experiences in the Stable Courtyard, which permanently houses our Farm Shop and Gift Shop, and Garden Centre nearby.

"We hope Jellycat fans old and new will pay us a visit and add something lovely to their collection."

The shop features popular Jellycat characters along with some items that were sold out on the official Jellycat website at the time of release.

Opening hours may vary and are available at www.castlehoward.co.uk.
