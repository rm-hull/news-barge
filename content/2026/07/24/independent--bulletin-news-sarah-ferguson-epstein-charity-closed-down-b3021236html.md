Sarah Ferguson's charity, Sarah's Trust, has formally closed down, with its status now marked as 'removed' on the public register of charities.

The decision to close the charity was announced in February, days after new revelations emerged about her friendship with convicted paedophile Jeffrey Epstein.

Documents released by the US Department of Justice indicated Ms Ferguson visited Epstein with her daughters, Beatrice and Eugenie, shortly after his release from prison in 2009.

Email exchanges from 2009 showed Ms Ferguson referring to Epstein as 'the brother I have always wished for' and seeking £20,000 for rent after a business venture failed.

Sarah's Trust, launched in 2020, provided aid for NHS, care home, and hospice staff during the Covid pandemic and sent computers to Ukrainian refugees.
