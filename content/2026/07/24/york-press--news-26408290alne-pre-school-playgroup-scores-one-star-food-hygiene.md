A North Yorkshire playgroup has scored one-star for food hygiene, meaning major improvement necessary.

Alne Pre-school Playgroup is based at Alne Primary School, Main Street, Alne, and caters for children aged 2-5.

Inspectors from North Yorkshire Council visited the venue, which was established in 1971, on June 15.

The Food Standards Agency website says that for hygienic food handling, standards were good.

However, it was a matter of improvement necessary for the cleanliness and condition of the facilities and the building.

And for management of food safety, inspectors determined major improvement necessary.

The Press approach the pre-school, a registered charity, for comment.

A statement responded: “No children were at risk. All points were rectified within a couple of days. We look forward to being reinspected.”

No further comment was given.

The Press will report on any future re-inspection.
