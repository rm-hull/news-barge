Cole Hauser, who plays Rip Wheeler in the new spin-off series Dutton Ranch, said “there were challenges” while launching the show.

Speaking to Film Inside NYC, Hauser added: “Not having Taylor [Sheridan] there, for us to really kind of be able to work with these new writers, these new directors, these people... everything had changed.”

Hauser said the “only normality that we had whatsoever on set” was having Kelly Reilly, their Yellowstone co-star Finn Little and the show’s former director Christina Alexandra Voros.

Nevertheless, Hauser expressed pride in the work accomplished and “look[s] forward to doing it again”, adding: “We worked our asses off this year.”

The Yellowstone franchise has become a TV juggernaut, spawning four spin-offs, including 1883, 1923, Marshals and Dutton Ranch.
