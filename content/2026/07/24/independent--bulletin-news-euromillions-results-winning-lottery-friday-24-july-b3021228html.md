# EuroMillions results: Winning lottery and Thunderball numbers for Friday 24 July

- Tonight’s EuroMillions draw has offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £59million.
- To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.
- In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.
- The winning EuroMillions numbers were **8, 10, 30, 36** and**47**. The Lucky Stars were** 1**and** 4**. The Millionaire Maker code was** TGVM82413.**
- The winning Thunderball numbers were **10, 16, 27, 29** and**34**. The Thunderball was** 3.**
