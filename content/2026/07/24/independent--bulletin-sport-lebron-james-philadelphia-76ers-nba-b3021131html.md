LeBron James has announced his decision to join the Philadelphia 76ers for what will be his record-extending 24th NBA season.

The league's all-time leading scorer is moving from the Los Angeles Lakers, where he spent the last eight seasons and won a title in 2020, having also played for the Cleveland Cavaliers and Miami Heat.

"This is my last decision. I’m not going for money. I’m not going for family. What am I really playing for at this point? I still want to sacrifice. I still want to work. I still want to grind. I still want to compete, to win and to have a chance at the feeling of winning another championship," James wrote on social media.

He continued, "I believe I can help make the Philadelphia 76ers a championship team and I am so excited to energize a new fan base and start this incredible journey one last time."

Despite being 41 years old, James continues to perform at an elite level, having surpassed Robert Parish's record for most career regular-season games last March. He earned a record 22nd consecutive All-Star selection last February, averaging 20.9 points, 6.1 rebounds, and 7.2 assists over 60 games in the previous season.
