# Firefighters battling large blaze in Essex after ‘massive explosion’

![Firefighters are tackling a blaze in Essex](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/29/08/83fa52bfe1ee7ca5d72544c367249968Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzgwMTIwMzYz-2.75490513.jpg)

- More than 100 firefighters are currently battling a large blaze at an industrial unit in Essex.
- The fire, located on farmland in Southend Road between East Hanningfield and Rettendon, followed a "massive explosion".
- Emergency services, including Essex County Fire and Rescue Service and the East of England Ambulance Service, were called to the scene on Friday afternoon.
- Local residents reported that the explosion shook houses in the area and created a huge mushroom cloud visible in the sky.
- Authorities have described the ongoing situation as a "significant incident", with further updates expected.
