A York street next to where a water main burst will remain closed over the weekend as crews work to repair and clear the site after flooding, the council has said.

Salisbury Terrace has been closed since the pipe burst last Sunday evening (July 19), with diversions in place for drivers.

A hole has been left in the ground outside the former Jubilee pub with crews working to repair the damage caused by the burst.

  Cars underwater in Leeman Road, York, on Sunday night (July 19) 

*(Image: Jay Loft)*

Yorkshire Water said the road closure in Salisbury Terrace was needed to repair the “significant burst on a 21-inch water main”, adding that it will “open the road as soon as it is safe to do so”.

The company said the main has been repaired and its crew is working to repair the damaged road.

City of York Council has urged drivers heading towards the city centre to use the diversion via Water End, Boroughbridge Road and Blossom Street.

The council also said it is “working with partners to try and address people driving the wrong way up Livingstone Street”.

Meanwhile, a free shuttle bus service to York station for Leeman Road residents whose routes were disrupted due to the closure will remain for “as long as the closure is in place”, the council said.

The bus, which the council said is being supported by Yorkshire Water, will run from 6.30am until 11pm. The bus runs a circular route from York Station via Leeman Road, Park Street, Garfield Terrace, Livingstone Street, Salisbury Terrace, Water End, Poppleton Road, Holgate Road, Blossom Street and back to York Station around every 30 minutes.

Waste collections were also disrupted by the burst water main, with the councils saying it will return to collect recycling from Stephenson Way residents “asap”.

The council added that its team was on site on Friday to support the clean-up left by floodwater after the main burst.

“We are grateful for your patience and understanding as we continue to work with Yorkshire Water and partners to resolve this as quickly and safely as possible,” a council spokesperson said.
