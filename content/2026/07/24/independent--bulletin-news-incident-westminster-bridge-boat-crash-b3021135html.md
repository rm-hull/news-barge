Westminster Bridge incident as five police officers are injured in boat crash

Emergency services responding to the incident at Westminster Bridge in London (PA)

A police boat from the Metropolitan Police's Marine Policing Unit, carrying armed officers, collided with Westminster Bridge in central London on Friday afternoon.

The incident, which occurred around 3.40pm, resulted in five police officers sustaining injuries, with several initially entering the water before being recovered and receiving treatment.

Emergency services, including police, fire crews and ambulances, rapidly responded to the scene, supported by a police helicopter and an air ambulance circling overhead.

The Metropolitan Police confirmed that no members of the public were involved or injured in the collision, and stated that the circumstances of the incident would be thoroughly reviewed.

As a result, a section of Westminster Bridge was cordoned off, traffic was brought to a standstill, and entrances three and four to Westminster Underground station were temporarily shut.
