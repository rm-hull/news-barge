CBS analyst and former Dallas Cowboys quarterback Tony Romo was arrested on suspicion of operating a vehicle while under the influence (OWI) on Thursday.

Romo was stopped while driving south on Interstate 43 in Milwaukee County, Wisconsin, and performed poorly on field sobriety tests, police say.

Following his arrest, Romo was taken into custody, booked, and subsequently released by the Milwaukee County Sheriff’s Office.

A court appearance for the incident has been scheduled for September 21.

Romo has served as the lead game analyst for CBS' NFL coverage since 2017, after a 13-year NFL career spent entirely with the Dallas Cowboys.
