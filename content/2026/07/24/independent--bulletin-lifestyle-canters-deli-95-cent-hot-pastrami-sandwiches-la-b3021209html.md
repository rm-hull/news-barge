# Iconic deli will celebrate 95 years in Los Angeles with 95-cent sandwiches

![Founded in 1931, Canter's has become one of Los Angeles' best-known Jewish delicatessens](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/24/18/44/GettyImages-1071543440.jpg)

- Canter's Deli will celebrate its 95th anniversary in Los Angeles next month by offering its signature hot pastrami sandwich for 95 cents during a one-night promotion.
- The anniversary special will be available on August 3, from 6 p.m. to midnight, exclusively for dine-in customers at its Fairfax District restaurant, with a limit of one sandwich per customer.
- The 95-cent meal includes a hot pastrami sandwich, cole slaw, a pickle, and a chocolate chip rugelach, with an option to substitute Mrs. Goldfarb's plant-based corned beef.
- Founded in 1931, Canter's Deli is a well-known Jewish delicatessen in Los Angeles, recognized for its pastrami sandwiches and continuously operated by the third and fourth generations of the Canter family.
- Fourth-generation owner Alex Canter recently announced plans to launch Maggie's Refuel, a new chain of upscale gas stations and convenience stores, which is expected to debut in Beverly Hills.
