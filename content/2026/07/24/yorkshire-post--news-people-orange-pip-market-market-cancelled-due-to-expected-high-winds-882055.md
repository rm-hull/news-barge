# Orange Pip Market: Market cancelled due to expected high winds

![The Orange Pip Market in Middlesbrough Middlesbrough Council free for LDR use](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjFlMmE5MTk0LWE4N2EtNGJiNC04YWY1LTYxN2JkZDc5NGY3ZDpiMWQxODZmYy04N2QwLTQ4OWYtYjRmNi0wZjg4NzVhMWM1NDI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)



## This month’s Orange Pip Market has been cancelled due to weather concerns.

Saturday, July 25 will no longer see Middlesbrough’s streets lined with market-goers, due to expected wind gusts exceeding the safe level for operating. The decision was made with the safety of traders and visitors in mind.

As recently as Thursday, July 23, trader announcements were being made for this weekend’s market. However, Orange Pip’s Facebook page confirmed in the early afternoon on Friday, July 24 that the market would not be operating as planned on Bedford and Baker Street.

Market organisers have been unable to source an alternative venue, with the team making the “very difficult” decision to cancel the event. Middlesbrough Council has apologised for the disappointment caused and the impact the decision will have on traders, artists and local businesses. Residents of Middlesbrough and beyond are still encouraged to visit the town centre as “many local businesses will have organised events to coincide with the market”.

Orange Pip is celebrating its tenth birthday this year, with five events in the calendar running from May until September. Following this weekend’s cancellation, the next event will be held on Saturday, August 29, during the extended bank holiday weekend.
