  **QUEUES of 45 minutes are expected after a crash in a major North Yorkshire road**

Two lanes of the A1(M) southbound are currently closed after a crash that took place close to Tadcaster at 4pm today (July 24).

Traffic is stretching back to J45 (Boston Spa) according to reports by the AA.

A spokesperson for National Highways said: "Two lanes (of four) are closed southbound on the A1(M) between J44 and J43 near Tadcaster due to a traffic collision.

“Traffic officers are working on scene. Current delays up to 45 mins on approach, five miles of congestion.”
