## York city centre street remains closed to motorists

George Street is closed to motorists but the footpath remains open for pedestrians *(Image: Newsquest)*

A BUSY section of York city centre remains closed to motorists.

George Street, off Walmgate, closed for a wastewater pipe repair on Thursday (July 23).

The planned work is being done by Yorkshire Water, who have until Wednesday (July 29) to complete the repair.

  


It is doubtful that the work will take that long, a spokesperson for the company has said.

Pedestrians are still able to access the road, with the footpath unaffected.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Water said the company will be replacing just under nine kilometres of mains in the city by April 2027, with more to come by April 2030, as part of a £406 million mains project.
