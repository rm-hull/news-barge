# Former youth worker jailed for sex offences against teenage girls

![Jacob Agnew, 30, was jailed for five and a half years at Hull Crown Court](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmVjYTBhZjMyLWUxMDMtNGE3Yy1iZDBiLTQ2Mzk1MGM4YjgwOToxYjhhYjg3Mi1hMjJkLTQ2NzctYjdjYy1iYzllOTg2Mjg0NTU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)



## A former youth worker has been jailed for five and a half years after a string of sexual offences against girls aged 14 to 17.

Jacob Agnew, 30, from Hull, was around 16 when his offending started in 2012 and it continued when he was in a position of trust working in youth support services.

He was convicted of six charges against three girls following a 13-day trial at Hull Crown Court.

Sentencing Judge Mark Bury said Agnew “didn't grow out of a predilection for sexual activity with underage girls”.

He said the victims had shown "immense courage", adding: "Each of them has more courage in their little finger than you have in the whole of your body."

One told the court: "Since 2022 I don't trust anybody any more. Someone can be nice but I'm sure they have a hidden agenda.”

Another said she had been groomed to "get what he wanted": "Looking back I can see he preyed on me."

After Agnew was suspended in 2022, he applied for a role at a school. Asked for references he created a fake e-mail address, purporting to be from a former colleague, describing himself in glowing terms. However former colleagues got wind and reported it to the council, who launched an investigation.

Detective Inspector Tim Hudson said Agnew used his position of trust to target vulnerable young girls, adding: “When concerns were raised, he used his manipulative charm to claim the children were attracted to him, that they were fabricating stories, and that he was the victim.”

Agnew was found guilty of four counts of sexual activity with a child, sexual assault and causing a child to engage in sexual activity. He pleaded guilty at a previous hearing to fraud and four counts of sexual activity with a child.

He will be on the Sex Offenders Register for life.
