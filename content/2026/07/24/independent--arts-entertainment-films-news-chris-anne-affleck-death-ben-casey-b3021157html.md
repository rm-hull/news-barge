Christopher Anne Affleck, the mother of Oscar-winning actors Ben and Casey Affleck, has died at the age of 83.

Her obituary, published in The Boston Globe, confirmed she passed away "peacefully in her sleep" on 2 June.

Reports indicate Ms Affleck had been diagnosed with pancreatic cancer before her death, with her obituary noting she was "given six months to live in December of 2025".

Born in New York City in 1942, Ms Affleck was a Harvard University graduate who pursued a 35-year career as a public school teacher, retiring in 2008.

She was also a dedicated civil rights activist, influenced by her stepfather Sam, and taught literacy classes in Mississippi during the 1964 Freedom Summer campaign.

She was married to Timothy Affleck, and they welcomed their sons, Ben in 1972 and Casey in 1975.

![Christopher Anne Affleck (left) was married to Timothy Affleck and they welcomed their sons Ben (centre) and Casey in 1972 and 1975, respectively](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/24/16/24/GettyImages-2992264-107f4b68.jpeg)

The obituary details how her sons Ben and Casey were her “favourite young people” compared to her “cherished” students, and her five grandchildren were described as “the animating force of her last two decades”.

“Chris was suitably proud of her descendants’ achievements, but she was delighted when they questioned, defied, or cleverly engaged authority,” it added.

Ms Affleck continued her activism after her retirement, and set out “to both see the world and change it” – having volunteered on the campaign trail for former US president Barack Obama.

She also travelled to Palestine and Israel and returned from her travels “as an organiser with the Alliance for Water Justice in Palestine”, it said.

Her obituary states she was a founding board member of the Sustainable Cape project in Truro, Massachusetts – which is a non-profit organisation that builds local food systems, runs children’s gardens, and operates educational farmers’ markets.

![An obituary details how her sons Ben and Casey were her ‘favourite young people’ compared to her ‘cherished’ students, and her five grandchildren were described as ‘the animating force of her last two decades’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/24/16/21/GettyImages-2992294-171d19e9.jpeg)

“When her granddaughter’s student activist group needed a space for trainings, she invited them to Truro and attended every single workshop herself, taking notes,” the obituary said.

It also detailed how she received a terminal diagnosis last year, and said that her “greatest wish” was “to live to see her grandson graduate high school”.

“She did so, attending with her family on May 31, 2026. She died peacefully, in her sleep, two days later,” the obituary said.

Ms Affleck’s eldest son, Ben, is best known for his breakthrough role in Good Will Hunting, which he co-wrote with his friend and fellow actor Matt Damon, who also starred in the film.

Casey also appeared in the film, and starred in his brother’s directorial debut Gone Baby Gone.

He has since appeared in other projects such as the action-packed Oceans franchise.
