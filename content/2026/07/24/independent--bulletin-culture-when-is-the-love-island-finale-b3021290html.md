# When is the Love Island finale? What to know as 13th season nears conclusion

![The final will be available to watch live on TV2 and ITVX](https://static.independent.co.uk/2023/01/04/15/newFile-1.jpg)

- Love Island is nearing its finale as the couples in the villa compete for the £50,000 prize.
- The final of the 13th season of the reality TV show is on Monday, July 27.
- It will be available to watch live on TV2 and ITVX.
- The remaining couples are: Samraj Toor and Mica Harris, Simba Kudyiwa and Angelista Gunda, and Priya Jaswal and Aidan Murphy.
- Lorenzo Alessi and Julia Majchrzak, Kavan Murphy and Jasmine Muller, and Yasmin Hadlow and Tom Stagg complete the line-up.
