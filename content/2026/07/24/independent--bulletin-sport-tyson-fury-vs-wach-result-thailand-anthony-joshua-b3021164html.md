# Tyson Fury beats Mariusz Wach after seventh-round TKO in Anthony Joshua warm-up clash

- Tyson Fury has beaten Polish fighter Mariusz Wach after a seventh-round TKO as he continues to gear up for his fight with Anthony Joshua later this year.
- The British heavyweight proved to be too strong for the former world-title challenger, who was retired by his corner after round seven during the bout in Thailand.
- It is now back-to-back wins for Fury, who beat Arslanbek Makhmudov on points in April at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.
- Joshua, 36, will face Albanian Kristian Prenga on Saturday in Saudi Arabia.
- Should Joshua also win, he will share a ring with Fury later this year, potentially at Wembley Stadium in November.
