A POPULAR market has been cancelled this weekend due to 'forecasted high gusts'.

Malton’s monthly Little Bird Artisan Market, organised in partnership with Little Bird Made, was due to take place on Saturday, July 25, from 10am in the town’s central market square.

The event was promoted as featuring a vibrant selection of independent traders offering handcrafted goods, food, and drink, capturing the heart of Malton’s famed “Made in Malton” spirit.

However, a spokesperson for Little Bird Artisan Market, said: "Due to forecasted high wind gusts, we’ve made the tough decision to cancel the Malton Artisan Market for Saturday, 25 July.

"The safety of our visitors, traders, and staff is always our top priority.

"We appreciate your understanding and can’t wait to welcome you on Saturday, 22 August."

For more information about Little Bird Artisan Markets, to book a stall, or to apply, contact [info@littlebirdmade.co.uk](mailto:info@littlebirdmade.co.uk), visit www.littlebirdmade.com, or follow @LittleBirdMade on Instagram or Facebook.
