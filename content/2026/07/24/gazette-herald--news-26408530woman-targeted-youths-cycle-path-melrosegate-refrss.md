A CYCLIST says she was targeted by two youths in York who were allegedly throwing sticks from a bridge.

Two girls are said to have targeted a woman riding her bike on the cycle path beneath Melrosegate in Tang Hall at around 2pm on Saturday, July 11.

The youths were reported to be throwing sticks from the bridge and when challenged by the woman, they are said to have laughed at her, making her feel “intimidated”.

  


The York woman said she could have suffered a serious head injury had she not been wearing a cycle helmet.

But she said that when she stopped to tell the girls how dangerous their actions had been, they just laughed.

And when she said she was going to call the police, they responded: “Go ahead, they won’t come and they won’t do anything.

“We can do whatever we want to do, they won’t stop us.”

The woman, who does not want to be identified, said she was riding to an appointment and felt something heavy falling on her head.

She said: “I felt very confused and pressed on my brakes.

“My first thought was that a branch had fallen off a tree as I saw it lying on the ground; I thought I was just slightly unlucky.

“However, I suddenly remembered reading on Facebook about a female cyclist being targeted under a bridge and went back to look up.

“Two girls were sitting on the bridge.

“I noticed that they had more sticks and branches with them. I cycled on to the other side of the bridge and started to call the police.

“The police were on the phone with me for 17 minutes and told me to share my location as they were going to dispatch a local unit.

“They told me there had been three reports of teenage girls throwing things.

“They asked me to describe what happened and what the girls looked like.

“In the meantime, I kept warning passers-by, especially cyclists.”

The woman said she was called back by another police officer, who asked whether she wanted to press a criminal charge.

“He mentioned that because the offenders were underage, even if that happened, the law was lenient on them. He said they were well known to police and repeat incidents had been reported already.”

She said she told him she didn’t want to press a criminal charge in this case because of the stress involved.

“He then asked me what did I want them to do?"

I said in a bit of disbelief: “Stop them! They could hurt someone seriously next, as if I wasn’t wearing a helmet, I would’ve been hurt.

“He reassured me that a unit was in the area and told me I could go home.”

The woman added: “My confidence has really dropped down and I don’t think I’ll venture out like that again.

“I’m planning to talk to the councillors for the area and York Cycling Campaign to escalate this.”

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said that on Saturday (July 11, 2026), police received several reports of anti-social behaviour involving two girls throwing sticks from a bridge onto the cycle path below.

"A woman cyclist also complained that she had been struck on the head by a tree branch," the spokesperson said.

"When the victim challenged the girls, they reportedly laughed at her and made her feel intimidated.

"Officers attended and the located the girls who are aged 14 and 12.

"They challenged them about their behaviour and explained the potential consequences of their actions."

Anyone who witnesses or is a victim of this type of behaviour is urged to report it on 101 or 999 if an emergency response is required.
