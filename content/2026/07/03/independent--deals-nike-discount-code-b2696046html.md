## Helpful FAQs

### Where can I apply my Nike discount code?

You can apply your Nike discount code at checkout on the Nike website. Head to your shopping bag and look for the “Do you have a promo code?” dropdown in the order summary. Click to expand it, paste your Nike promo code into the box provided and apply it to see your savings before paying.

### How much can I save with a Nike promo code?

A Nike promo code typically saves shoppers around 25 per cent off full-price items. Many codes also come with a minimum spend of £50 to £100, depending on the offer. The best Nike deals usually appear in the run-up to Black Friday, as well as during major seasonal sales.

**Does Nike ever do 20 per cent off?**

Nike occasionally offers 20 to 25 per cent off promotions during major sales events such as Black Friday, but these are usually limited-time and often restricted to selected items.

### Is there still a Nike birthday discount?

Yes, Nike still offers a birthday discount for members. All Nike members receive 25 per cent off as a birthday reward. You’ll need to sign up at least 10 days before your birthday and the discount is valid for 30 days from the date it’s issued.

### Is there a Nike NHS discount?

NHS workers can access an exclusive 10 per cent off Nike through Health Service Discounts, which can be used stacked with existing on-site Nike deals where eligible. Alternatively, there is an extra 5 per cent off for medical professionals by verifying your work email address directly through the Nike website.

### Can you stack Nike discount codes?

No, Nike typically only allows one discount code per order. However, you can combine codes with sale items or member offers to maximise your overall saving.

### When is the best time to buy Nike?

The best time to buy Nike is during seasonal sales and key events like Black Friday, when discounts of up to 50 per cent are common across trainers and clothing.

### Why are Nike discount codes rare?

Nike doesn’t often release sitewide promo codes, with most savings coming from seasonal sales, student discounts and member-exclusive offers. This means the best way to save is usually to combine these deals rather than rely on a single code.
