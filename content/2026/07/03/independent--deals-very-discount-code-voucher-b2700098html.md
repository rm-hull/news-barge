To redeem your discount, add the items you want to your basket and head to checkout. You’ll need to log in or create an account and select your preferred delivery option before proceeding to payment. On the right-hand side of the page, you’ll find a promo code box where you can enter and apply your code.

On average, shoppers can expect savings of between 10-25 per cent. Very discount codes always apply to selected ranges, such as designer fashion, computing or electrical beauty. New codes are released each week, so it’s worth checking regularly and acting quickly when a strong offer is available.
