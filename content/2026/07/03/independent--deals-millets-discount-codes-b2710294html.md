**Save 10 per cent with the Millets student discount**

If you’re a student, you can get 10 per cent off full-price items at Millets by verifying your student status with Unidays or Student Beans. Once you’ve done this, you’ll receive a unique code to use at checkout.

**Key workers save 10 per cent at Millets**

If you’re part of the NHS, emergency services, or hold a Blue Light Card, you can get a 10 per cent discount at Millets (in-store and online). Register through Blue Light Card or Health Service Discounts to receive a unique promo code.

**Exclusive discounts for Duke of Edinburgh’s Award participants**

For those involved in the Duke of Edinburgh’s Award (DofE), Millets provides a discount on expedition kit in-store and online. Participants will get 15 per cent off, while leaders and adult volunteers get 20 per cent off.

To claim your discount in-store, simply present your DofE card at the checkout. When shopping online, you’ll need to use the discount code printed on your card.

**More discounts are available through the Scouts, Girlguiding and more**

Millets also offers discounts to members of various groups, including the Scouts, Girlguiding, the Camping and Caravanning Club, and more. How you can access this discount depends on which group you’re a part of, so head to the Millets website for the full list of partners and details of how to sign up.
