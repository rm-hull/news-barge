## Helpful information

### Price match promise

Under its ‘lowest price promise’, Mountain Warehouse says that it’s ‘never’ beaten on price on its own products. If you find a lower price on another website, like Amazon or eBay, it’ll match the price and give you a 10 per cent discount.

This still applies if you’ve already bought the item from Mountain Warehouse, but only for up to 30 days after you’ve made your purchase. If you see a Mountain Warehouse product elsewhere for cheaper than it's listed on its own site, email [support@care.mountainwarehouse.com](mailto:support@care.mountainwarehouse.com) with ‘Price Match’ in the subject line. Then follow the instructions on the Mountain Warehouse website.

### Returns policy

Mountain Warehouse also allows you to return an item within 60 days if you change your mind. In-store returns are free, but online returns will cost £2, which will be deducted from your refund. You can see the full return policy on the Mountain Warehouse website.
