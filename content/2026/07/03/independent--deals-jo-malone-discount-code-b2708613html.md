## Best ways to save at Jo Malone

**Get 10 per cent off with Jo Malone’s newsletter**

One of the easiest ways to get money off your purchase is by signing up for Jo Malone’s newsletter. Once you’ve done this, you’ll receive a 10 per cent off code – but bear in mind that you can’t use this in conjunction with any other offers.

If you decide you don’t want to carry on receiving marketing emails, just unsubscribe once you’ve used your discount.

**Free gifts with selected purchases**

Jo Malone discount codes may not always be available, but one thing you can count on from this retailer is free gifts. These typically appear during key events such as Christmas, Mother’s Day and Black Friday, and usually require either a minimum spend or the purchase of a specific fragrance.

Free gifts range from mini fragrances and candles to full-size body and bath products. Most recently, they have included the Jo Malone cologne discovery set and the miniature luxuries trio, worth up to £40.

**Complimentary gift wrapping**

If you’re buying a present for someone else, you can add Jo Malone's gift wrapping to any order, at no extra cost. Simply choose the option at checkout and your order will arrive neatly packaged in a signature cream box and black velvet ribbon. You can also add extra details to your gift and choose to engrave any colognes or candles, though this comes at an additional price of £10 per item.

### Save 10 per cent with the Jo Malone Blue Light Card discount

Key workers such as NHS employees, teachers and those working in emergency services can get 10 per cent off Jo Malone, in-store and online. You can even save on gift cards, though at a reduced rate of five per cent.

## Jo Malone FAQs

### Can I use multiple Jo Malone discount codes on my order?

Jo Malone allows only one discount code per order. But some coupons can be applied to baskets that already contain discounted items.

### How much can I save with a Jo Malone discount code?

Jo Malone promo codes offer an extra 5 or 10 per cent discount on full-price items. During major sale events such as Black Friday, using a coupon can help you save up to 25 per cent. Some coupons also offer gift-with-purchase bundles, such as mini candles or travel samples, when you spend above a certain amount.
