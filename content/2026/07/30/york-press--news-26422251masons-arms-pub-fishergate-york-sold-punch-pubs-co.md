A popular pub just outside York’s city walls has a new owner.

  Major pub company Punch Pubs & Co has added to its growing portfolio with the acquisition of The Masons Arms pub in York**.**

The Grade II listed building, which sits on York’s historical Fishergate, will sit within Punch’s Leased & Tenanted estate and will be run by existing Publican, Terry Cooney.

The Masons Arms is highly-regarded ‘traditional’ pub, with Tudor styling, good food and a tranquil beer garden overlooking the Ouse. It also offers accommodation.

Google awards the venue 4.6 stars out of five, based on 1,827 Google reviews.

Tripadvisor awards 4.3 stars out of five based on 1,094 reviews, ranking it 107th out of 888 restaurants in York. It received a Traveller’s Choice Award for 2025.

Punch Pubs Operations Manager Jason Moran said: “I am really looking forward to working with Terry and the team at the Mason’s Arms. Terry has done a superb job creating a welcome atmosphere at the pub during his tenure and I’m excited to work alongside him as he continues his journey.”

The community pub is well-known for its historic features, including its fireplace and panelled bar. It also boasts a popular beer terrace overlooking the River Foss, making it a favourite destination for locals and tourists alike.

Punch Pubs & Co Acquisitions Manager, Henry Boshier, said: “We’re thrilled to have acquired this fantastic pub in the centre of York, which already has a great reputation locally. I do not doubt that with Punch’s industry-leading training and support, this pub will continue to thrive.”

Punch added the company is continually on the lookout for additional high-quality acquisitions, including packages and single-site opportunities that align with its modern, progressive vision and growth plans.
