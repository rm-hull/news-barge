A York pub has been bought by a leading pub company.

Punch Pubs & Co has added to its growing portfolio with the acquisition of The Masons Arms pub in York.

The Grade II listed building, located on Fishergate, will sit within Punch’s Leased & Tenanted estate and will be run by existing publican, Terry Cooney.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/masons-arms-fishergate-york-gsv.webp)

The Masons Arms is a historic site first recorded in 1835, before being completely rebuilt in Tudor style in 1935, and designated a Grade II listed building.

Today, it’s a popular community pub with four double rooms, and serves food from 8am every day.

Speaking about the acquisition, Punch Pubs operations manager Jason Moran added: “I am really looking forward to working with Terry and the team at the Mason’s Arms.

“Terry has done a superb job creating a welcome atmosphere at the pub during his tenure and I’m excited to work alongside him as he continues his journey.”

The community pub is well-known for its historic features, including its fireplace and panelled bar. It also has a beer terrace overlooking the River Foss, making it a popular destination for locals and tourists alike.

Punch Pubs & Co acquisitions manager, Henry Boshier, commented: “We’re thrilled to have acquired this fantastic pub in the centre of York, which already has a great reputation locally.

“I do not doubt that with Punch’s industry-leading training and support, this pub will continue to thrive.”
