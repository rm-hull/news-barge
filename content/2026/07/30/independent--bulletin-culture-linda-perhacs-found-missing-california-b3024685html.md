Folk legend Linda Perhacs, 82, has been located safe after being reported missing in California.

Perhacs had been out of contact with loved ones for eight months since her discharge from an assisted living facility in Northridge by her legal guardian.

Her former manager, Laurel Stearns, issued a public plea for help to locate Perhacs due to unanswered attempts to contact her guardian.

The LAPD Missing Persons Unit confirmed on Wednesday that Perhacs was found and is safe, though details about her location and health status cannot be shared due to HIPAA.

An update on Perhacs's Instagram confirmed she is in a new care facility, thanking the community for their support.
