Marcus Rashford could leave Manchester United this summer with Tottenham Hotspur being rumoured as a potential destination for the 28-year-old forward. Rashford spent last season on loan at Barcelona having been exiled by former coach Ruben Amorim and returned to Old Trafford when the La Liga champions failed to take up his £26m option. Michael Carrick is an admirer of Rashford and could reintegrate him into the first team squad but there are a few Premier League clubs in line for his signature.

United, meanwhile, could be running out of time to meet Roma’s deadline to sign France international Manu Kone, according to reports in Italy.

Elsewhere, Liverpool have agreed terms with Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola, according to reports in France, but are still a considerable distance away from agreeing a fee for the French international. Barcola wants to join Liverpool, according to***L’Equipe*, but the European champions will demand £140m this summer as the Reds are now set to open talks with his club. 

In one of the bigger stories of the summer Vinicius Jr has made it clear that he wants to remain at Real Madrid, according to *Sky Sports* despite interest from Arsenal. The Gunners have been plotting an audacious move for the 26-year-old Brazil star while he has one year remaining on his contract, but Vinicius Jr wants to agree new terms at the Bernabeu. 

Chelsea appear set to strike a surprise double deal for England veterans Danny Welbeck and Jordan Henderson as Xabi Alonso seeks to add experience to his squad, with the former taking a medical and the latter having agreed a two-year contract after officially leaving Brentford.

## Liverpool prepare Bradley Barcola bid

Liverpool are preparing an opening bid for Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola, according to *BBC Sport.*

PSG have set a price tag of £140m on the 23-year-old, and do not want to sell - especially as Real Madrid look likely to sign RB Leipzig’s Yan Diomande.

However, it comes amid reports from France that Barcola has agreed to join the club ahead of Premier League rivals Arsenal.
