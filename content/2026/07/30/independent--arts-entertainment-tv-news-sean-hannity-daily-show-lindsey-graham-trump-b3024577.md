*The Daily Show* host Jordan Klepper has roasted Fox News host Sean Hannity’s bizarre eulogy at Lindsey Graham’s funeral.

Graham, who died earlier this month at the age of 71, was eulogized by Donald Trump, J.D. Vance, and Hannity, among others, at his funeral this week.

Part of Hannity’s tribute included his vision of the MAGA heaven he believes Graham might now be enjoying.

“Lindsey, Mr. President, I’ll argue in my mind, probably has a heavenly version of Mar-a-Lago,” Hannity said. “He may have a little more gold than in the Oval Office in his mansion in heaven. And I wouldn’t be surprised if he added one big, beautiful ballroom and an arch somewhere on the property,” he said.

Klepper tore into Hannity’s odd description and didn’t mince words.

![Jordan Klepper roasted Sean Hannity's eulogy at Lindsey Graham's funeral](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/07/30/12/57/MixCollage-30-Jul-2026-08-23-AM-754.jpg)

“OK. OK, you know, let me, let me get this straight. You’re saying that when God asked Lindsey Graham, ‘Where do you wanna spend eternity?’ Lindsey said, ‘You know that tacky club in Palm Beach that’s so ugly it became a nickname for botched plastic surgery? I want you to stuff a ballroom in it, slap an arch in there, and then pack in an Oval Office so I’m living in a Trump version of *Backrooms**,”* Klepper said.

“That’s, that’s my little slice of heaven.’ I mean, OK. With all due respect, with all due respect, why would anyone want that?”

Klepper then continued playing Hannity’s tribute as the Fox host said, “Because he would wanna obviously duplicate his dearest friend in life. You, Mr. President.”

The *Daily Show* host then continued to lay into Hannity. “Wow. Wow. That’s really what it has all come down to for Republicans. Your entire life is so thoroughly and abjectly reduced to the ass-kissing of one man.

“And then when you die, other Republicans use your eulogy to continue the ass-kissing in a perpetual motion machine of chapped lips and lubricated cheeks.”

Trump was roasted in his own right not only for his own rambling tribute, but also for appearing to nod off during the hours-long funeral.

Seated in the front row of the packed cathedral, Trump appeared at times to be on the verge of nodding off, video footage indicates. Social media commenters were quick to express frustration, but not necessarily astonishment.

“Yes everyone, this is the man that holds the nuclear codes. Let that sink in,” wrote one X user, while another chimed in: “No surprise. How long did he go before he went to slumberland?”

When reached by *The Independent*, White House spokesperson Davis Ingle said, “President Trump was reflecting on the loss of his dear friend Lindsey Graham, who he just delivered a beautiful eulogy to. Anyone suggesting otherwise is a depraved moron.”
