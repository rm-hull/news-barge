Dog and cat rescued from 98F heat after being left in back of truck

Police officers in Boise rescued a dog and a cat trapped inside a sweltering moving truck where outside temperatures reached 98F.

The animals were found showing signs of dehydration and stress after residents alerted authorities to their plight.

Officers provided the dog with water and removed the cat from the vehicle to allow it to get air.

Evidence indicated that the pets had been confined in the truck for at least 20 minutes before officers arrived.

Two individuals were apprehended upon returning to the truck and have since been charged with two misdemeanour counts of carrying animals in a cruel manner.
