A PUB brought back to life after years of closure is celebrating an award shortlisting.

The Knapton Lion in Main Street, Knapton, is one of 10 York licensed premises shortlisted by readers of The Press for its popular Best Pub competition, which is running again this year.

The pub was also nominated as a best restaurant or gastro pub in a similar competition which ran in April and May this year.

The pub is owned by Tom Brown, who also has The Fox Inn in Stockton-on-the-Forest and The Lord Nelson in Nether Poppleton.

The Knapton Lion, formerly The Red Lion, was closed for several years before Tom took over, refurbished, and renamed it.

When the April shortlisting came in, Tom said: "The premises has gone from strength to strength since opening, and it's a real honour to be nominated.

"We pride ourselves on fresh-made seasonal menus with beautiful fish dishes, steaks, and proper pies.

He added: "We're a completely free-of-tie pub group, so we're able to work directly with local breweries and distilleries, stocking a vast range of unique drink offerings.

"We love being able to support the smaller local producers who are often overlooked by the bigger pubs and restaurants."

The York Press business editor Darren Greenwood reviewed The Knapton Lion in May last year and commended the pub for its top quality and tip top beers, and the food was generously portioned.

49 Tripadvisor reviewers recorded up to July 29 average out to give the The Knapton Lion a hearty 4.9 out of five, with specific plus points for consistency, lovely staff who are friendly and accommodating and a laid-back atmosphere.

Tom Brown said: "I love that our pubs become community hubs and boost the local community spirit.

"I believe that our staff knowing the locals on a more personal basis creates such a beautiful atmosphere."

The Knapton Lion recently invested in a Le Verre de Vin wine preservation system, allowing for an elevation of its wine list.

The system allows staff to re-seal bottles of wine using CO2, meaning everything on the wine list is offered by the glass.

The list is compiled in conjunction with Majestic Wines.

Paying tribute to the pub's core customer base in April, Tom said: "We're in quite a fortunate position that we are so well supported by our core customer base that we're fully booked with 50+ covers every evening and regularly serve 120+ covers on a Sunday.

"Our booking system collects reviews from our guests after their visit, which allows us some great insights into what we're doing right and, more importantly, what we can improve on.

"Seeing these 5* reviews and regularly full booking diaries is our main gauge of success."

  *Voting slips for The York Press Best Pub 2026 are in copies of the newspaper up to and including Saturday, August 5 and must be posted off to the address provided to count.*

  *The winner will be announced shortly afterwards.*
