PASSENGERS of vehicles involved in a three vehicle crash on a North Yorkshire road have been taken to hospital.

The crash, which involved a blue Ford Puma, a black Tesla and a black Nissan, happened on the A165 Hunmanby Road, near Reighton, at approximately 12.45pm on Tuesday (July 28).

  


North Yorkshire Police said some of the occupants attended hospital for treatment for potentially minor injuries.

A force spokesperson said: “The road was closed for a short time while officers investigated the scene and recovery of the vehicles took place.

“We're appealing for anyone who witnessed the collision, saw any of the vehicles prior to the incident, or has relevant dashcam footage, to get in touch.

“If you have information that could assist our investigation, please contact us quoting TC 1142 Upton:

• Email: [Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk)

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.”
