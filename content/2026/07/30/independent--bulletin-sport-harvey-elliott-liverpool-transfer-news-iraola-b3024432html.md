# Liverpool star Harvey Elliott speaks out on his Anfield future

- Liverpool midfielder Harvey Elliott is seeking a "second chance" to establish himself at the club following a challenging loan spell at Aston Villa last season, hoping the arrival of new manager Andoni Iraola will provide this opportunity.
- Elliott's limited appearances at Aston Villa, where he played only nine matches, were due to a specific loan clause that would have obligated Villa to sign him for £35 million had he made a tenth appearance.
- Despite ongoing speculation about another potential move, Elliott expressed his deep connection to Liverpool, stating it is a "childhood dream" to represent the club and he wants to give 100 per cent every time he wears the shirt.
- The 23-year-old returned to action for Liverpool in their 1-0 pre-season friendly victory over Wrexham in New York on Wednesday, marking his first game back with the squad.
- Elliott remains pragmatic about his Anfield future, acknowledging interest from clubs like Leeds, Sunderland, and RB Leipzig, and stating he wants to be "selfish" in deciding whether to fight for his place at Liverpool or potentially move elsewhere to enjoy his football.
