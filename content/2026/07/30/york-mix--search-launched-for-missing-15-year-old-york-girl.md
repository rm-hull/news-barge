A 15-year-old girl is missing from her York home.

Lexi is from the Acomb area, was last seen at home at around 3pm yesterday (Wednesday, 29 July).

She is described as

- white
- around 6ft tall
- of slim build
- with shoulder-length, straight brown hair.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/lexi-missing-york-2-858x1500.webp)

She may be wearing a black top with white logo on the front, black trousers and carrying an satchel-style shoulder bag.

She may be carrying a black walking stick or cane with stickers on it.

Lexi is known to spend time in the Hob Moor area of Acomb but could be anywhere in York.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “If you see Lexi, please call 999. If you have any other information that could help us find her, please call us on 101 and select option 1 to speak to our control room.”
