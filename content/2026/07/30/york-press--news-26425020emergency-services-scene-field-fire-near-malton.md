## Emergency services currently at scene of a wildfire in North Yorkshire

Farmers & Fire Crews are on scene at a field fire near West Lutton/ Wintringham area *(Image: Max Dunning)*

EMERGENCY services are currently at the scene of a wildfire in North Yorkshire.

Farmers and fire crews are at the scene of a field fire in the West Lutton and Wintringham area.

Several appliances and tractor tankers are understood to have brought the fire under control
