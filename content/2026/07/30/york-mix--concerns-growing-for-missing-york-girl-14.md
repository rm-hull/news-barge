Police are searching for a 14-year-old York girl who’s missing from home.

Nancy was last seen at home at around lunchtime yesterday (Wednesday, 29 July).

She is described as

- white
- 4ft 7in tall
- of medium build
- with long, brown hair.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/nancy-14-missing-york-july-2026-full.webp)

She may be wearing a black jumpsuit and white trainers.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are growing concerned for her welfare.

“We believe she may have travelled to the Scarborough area. If you have have seen Nancy or know where she is now, please call us on 999.

“If you have any other information that could help us find her please call us on 101 and select option 1 to speak to our control room.”
