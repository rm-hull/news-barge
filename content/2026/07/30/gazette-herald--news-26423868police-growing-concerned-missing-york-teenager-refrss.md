POLICE are searching for a missing teenage girl from York.

Nancy, 14, was last seen at home at around 12pm on Wednesday (July 29).

North Yorkshire Police say they are growing increasingly concerned for her welfare.

  


She is described as white, 4ft 7in tall, of medium build with long, brown hair.

She may be wearing a black jumpsuit and white trainers.

A police spokesperson said: "If you have have seen Nancy or know where she is now, please call police on 999.

"If you have any other information that could help us find her please call 101 and select option 1 to speak to the control room.

"Please quote 12260145874 when sharing information."
