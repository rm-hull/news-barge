**National League side Harrogate Town have signed experienced defender Tom Lees following his summer exit from Peterborough United.**

Lees, 35, has made more than 600 league appearances during his career, including spells with Leeds United, Sheffield Wednesday and Huddersfield Town.

He told the club website:, external "I've had a few offers in the Football League, but after sitting down and talking with the club, there's an exciting couple of years in store here and the start of a good project.

"I've lived in this town for most of my life, this is my local club, it means a lot to me to be involved and hopefully have a positive impact on my local team."
