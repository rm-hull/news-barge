# Deadly wildfires rage on Crete as crews tackle blazes for second day

- Greek firefighters are battling deadly wildfires on the island of Crete for a second day, following devastating blazes across Europe.
- Three Greek firefighters died on Wednesday while tackling fires on Crete and the mainland, with two others sustaining light injuries.
- Hundreds of residents and tourists were evacuated from Krya Vrysi and other villages on Wednesday, as high winds exacerbated the fire near popular holiday destinations.
- On Thursday, over 200 firefighters, supported by engines and aircraft, tackled two wildfires on Crete, where agricultural crops, olive groves, warehouses, and some houses have been damaged.
- Giorgos Tsapakos, deputy regional governor for civil protection on Crete, said on SKAI TV: “Unfortunately there is no improvement, and the latest information indicates that winds will increase later in the day.”
