Irish authorities are actively preparing to extradite Daniel Kinahan, an alleged leader of the Kinahan organised crime group, from Dubai back to Ireland.

Kinahan, in his late 40s, was arrested in the United Arab Emirates in April following an Irish court-issued warrant for alleged serious organised crime offences.

Preparations for his return are intensifying after he reportedly failed in legal attempts in Dubai to block the extradition process.

While the legal process may have concluded, Irish authorities state that diplomatic steps are still necessary to finalise the extradition plans, with no set timeline for his transportation.

The High Court in Dublin previously identified Kinahan as a senior figure in an organised crime gang involved in international drug trafficking and firearms offences, with US authorities offering a reward of up to five million dollars for his arrest in 2022.
