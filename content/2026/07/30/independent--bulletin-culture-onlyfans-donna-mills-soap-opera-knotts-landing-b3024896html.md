# Iconic soap star joins OnlyFans at 85 years old

- Longtime soap opera star Donna Mills, 85, has announced she is joining the subscription platform OnlyFans.
- Mills is widely recognized for her roles in "Knots Landing," "General Hospital," and the 2022 Jordan Peele film "Nope."
- She stated her intention to use OnlyFans for a more personal and direct connection with fans, sharing daily life moments and behind-the-scenes glimpses.
- OnlyFans is a subscription site known for allowing creators to post adult content, though Mills emphasized sharing unscripted, candid moments.
- Mills previously claimed in 2022 that she was the original inspiration for the character of Sandy in the musical "Grease."
