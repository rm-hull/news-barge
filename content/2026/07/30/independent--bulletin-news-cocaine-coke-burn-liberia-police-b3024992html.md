Liberian authorities burned 4.2 metric tons of cocaine, valued at $336 million, following the country's largest-ever drug seizure.

High-ranking police officials, including the chief of highway patrol and head of the major crimes unit, face charges for allegedly escorting the illicit drugs.

The massive haul, totaling nearly 4 metric tons (8,818 pounds) with an estimated street value of $317 million, was recovered during a raid in Duazon, near Monrovia.

Two foreign nationals, a Serbian and a dual Colombian-Spanish national, were arrested at the scene and are currently being questioned. Investigators identified officers allegedly involved in escorting the cocaine after its arrival.

The seizures highlight West Africa's growing role as a major transit route for cocaine trafficked from South America to Europe, with at least 30% of Europe-bound cocaine now passing through the region.
