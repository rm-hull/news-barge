# Trump announces $100B plan to transform former nuclear site into AI data center

![](https://cdn.jwplayer.com/v2/media/yf3OUFzD/poster.jpg?width=720)

- The Trump administration is pushing to accelerate AI data center construction, including plans to redevelop former nuclear enrichment sites like the Department of Energy’s Paducah Site in Kentucky.
- The Kentucky project, backed by over $100 billion in private investment, is projected to create 8,000 construction jobs and 600 permanent jobs and will include a new two-gigawatt natural gas power plant.
- The administration aims to leverage federal assets and preempt state AI regulations, arguing these facilities will create jobs and help the U.S. win the AI race against competitors like China.
- Environmentalists and local leaders have raised concerns about these projects, citing a lack of community input, potential environmental impacts and the use of fossil fuels for power generation.
- Critics argue that the administration's push for fossil fuel-powered data centers exacerbates the mismatch between high AI energy demand and the availability of renewable energy, hindering climate crisis efforts.
