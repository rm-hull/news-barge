## Twelve North Yorkshire campsites named among UK's best family holiday sites

St Helens in the Park, near Scarborough *(Image: Campsites.co.uk)*

A dozen North Yorkshire campsites have been named among the UK’s top family holiday destinations.

Each one has been featured in Campsites.co.uk’s annual Outstanding Family Sites list for 2026, which celebrates the best camping, glamping and touring sites that deliver a “reliably great holiday” experience.

Standout locations include St Helens in the Park, located 15 minutes from the seaside town of Scarborough, which was singled out for its adventure play area, scenic countryside walks, and family-friendly facilities.

Martin Smith, founder of Campsites.co.uk, said: "It's a real pleasure to launch our list of outstanding sites for 2026.

"The chosen campsites are all trusted favourites with our campers, and sites we're proud to put our name to, so you can book with confidence."

St Helens in the Park is part of the 7,500-acre Dawnay Estate and boasts a trim trail, climbing frame, and zip wire, as well as private bathrooms and accessible facilities.

Children can enjoy spotting alpacas and donkeys on nearby walking trails, while parents can relax at Hitch and Brew, the on-site coffee shop serving award-winning coffee, hearty breakfasts, brunches, lunches and homemade sweet treats.

This summer, the park will also feature a changing line-up of food trucks and a fully stocked bar for families to enjoy.

Campers have given the site a 4.81-star rating, with 99 per cent saying they would recommend it to others.

The full list of North Yorkshire sites featured in the top 12 are:

- St Helens in the Park
- Thornbrook Barn, Ingleton
- Hillside Caravan Park, Knayton
- Street Head Caravan Park, Leyburn
- Cliff Farm Holidays, Pickering
- Keld Knowle Farm Holidays, Pickering
- Vale of Pickering Caravan Park, Pickering
- Little Seed Field, Ripon
- Riverside Caravan Park, Bentham
- Catgill Farm Camping and Glamping, Skipton
- Howgill Lodge, Skipton
- Ravensdale Glamping Retreats, York

The compilation highlights sites with facilities such as swimming pools, farm parks, direct beach access and ice cream huts, as well as entertainment teams and organised activity programmes for children.

Combining award-winning destinations with sites that have earned glowing guest reviews year after year, the list offers travellers a trusted source of inspiration for their next holiday in the great outdoors.
