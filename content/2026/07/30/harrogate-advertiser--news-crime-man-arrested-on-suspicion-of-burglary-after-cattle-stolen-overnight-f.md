# Man arrested on suspicion of burglary after cattle stolen overnight from farm near Ripley

## North Yorkshire Police has arrested a man after recovering a number of cattle stolen from a farm in the Ripley area earlier this week.

The livestock was reported stolen overnight on Sunday, prompting an investigation by officers after the theft attracted widespread attention across the local farming community.

Police have now confirmed that the cattle were recovered on Wednesday night and that a man in his 30s from the West Yorkshire area has been arrested on suspicion of burglary.

He remains in police custody while enquiries continue.

![The police have arrested a man in his 30s on suspicion of burglary after recovering a number of cattle stolen from a farm in the Ripley area](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmVjN2IwOGQzLTFkOWQtNDRiYy05YzVjLThlMGJhZWM3NzA5MzpmODIzMTQ4Ny1jM2FlLTRlYzItYWVjZS0yZjEzOWNhM2RhODQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



The farmer has been updated and arrangements are now being made for the safe return of the cattle.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Rural crime can have a significant impact on farming communities, causing financial loss, disruption to businesses and considerable emotional distress.

"Livestock theft is far more than a property crime.

"For many farmers, their animals represent years of hard work, investment and dedication.

“North Yorkshire Police takes rural crime extremely seriously and remains committed to protecting our farming and rural communities.

"Through proactive patrols, intelligence-led operations and close partnership working with farmers, landowners and neighbouring forces, we continue to target those involved in offending across our countryside.

"We would like to thank everyone who has shared information and supported this investigation."

Enquiries remain ongoing.
