Police are appealing for witnesses after an apparent road rage incident at an agricultural show.

The incident was reported to have happened at about 4pm on Tuesday (28 July) in one of the car parks at the Ryedale Show, held at Duncombe Park, Helmsley.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It involves an allegation that a green Land Rover Defender was driven towards event stewards.

“All parties involved have been spoken to and enquiries are ongoing.

“We’re particularly appealing to anyone who witnessed the incident, or who may have dashcam footage from the area at the time, to come forward.”
