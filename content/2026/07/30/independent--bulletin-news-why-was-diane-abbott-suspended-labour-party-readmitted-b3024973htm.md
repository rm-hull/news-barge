Diane Abbott has been readmitted to the Labour Party following an internal investigation, after being suspended for a year over comments about racism.

Upon her readmission, Ms Abbott received a formal warning for a separate tweet that referred to the Israel Defence Force as the 'Jewish Defence Force', for which she apologised.

Fellow Labour MP Joani Reid was also readmitted to the party on Thursday evening, having voluntarily suspended herself in March.

Ms Reid's suspension followed the arrest of her husband on suspicion of spying for China, though she clarified she was never part of a police investigation herself.

Both Ms Abbott and Ms Reid have had the Labour whip restored, allowing them to sit as Labour MPs once more.
