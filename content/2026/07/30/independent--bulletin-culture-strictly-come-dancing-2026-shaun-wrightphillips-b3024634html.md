Strictly unveil five new professional dancers for 2026

The BBC's Strictly Come Dancing has begun announcing its celebrity line-up for the 2026 series, with several notable figures confirmed.

Confirmed contestants includeEastEndersstar Lacey Turner, Love Island's Dani Dyer, Australian singer Delta Goodrem, celebrity hairstylist Chris Appleton, reality star Cach Mercer, Paralympian Dame Sarah Storey, TV presenter Will Best and actor Jaime Winstone.

Recent additions to the cast feature House of the Dragon actor Bethany Antonia and former footballer Shaun Wright-Phillips.
