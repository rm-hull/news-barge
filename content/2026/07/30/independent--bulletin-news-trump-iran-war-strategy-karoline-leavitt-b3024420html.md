# Trump ‘erupted’ during meeting on how to end Iran war – report

- President Trump is reportedly exasperated by the lack of progress in the Iran conflict and the absence of a unified strategy among his top military advisers.
- An anonymous source claimed Trump 'erupted' during a national security meeting last week, expressing his frustration with the ongoing situation.
- White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt denied the alleged incident, asserting that Trump trusts his team and is prepared to continue punishing Iran.
- The conflict, which began in February, is described as a strategic stalemate with tactical victories but no clear long-term policy guidance.
- Divisions exist within Trump's administration regarding the approach to Iran, with some advocating for increased military force while others question its efficacy and resource drain.
