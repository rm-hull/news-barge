Harrogate Town AFC can confirm Anthony O’Connor has left the club by mutual consent.

The 33-year-old year-old signed in January 2023 and would make 149 appearances during his three-and-a-half years at the club.

The club has said they would like to place on record their thanks to Ant for his efforts at the club.
