TWO men have been arrested following a series of vehicle break-ins in North Yorkshire.

It comes after North Yorkshire Police said two vehicles were entered at 2am in Park Road, Scarborough on Thursday (July 30).

The suspects are also believed to have been in the areas of Cambridge Place, Scalby Road, Falgrave Road, Park Road and Highfield around that time.

  


A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It may be that they have also gone further afield in the Scarborough area.

“We are asking for anyone in these areas to check their vehicles for any sign of interference, and to wear gloves if possible, to preserve any forensic opportunities.

"A suspected stolen dash-cam has been recovered during the investigation."

Police are appealing for anyone who believes they have had their device/s stolen, to get in touch.

If you have any other information that could assist the investigation, please call 101 and ask for PC Evie Walters or email [evie.walters@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:evie.walters@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, you can report details on the police’s website, or call 0800 555 111 to pass information to Crimestoppers.

Please quote reference 12260145931 when providing information.
