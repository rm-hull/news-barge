A primary school federation has introduced lockable phone pouches as part of a new digital safety initiative.

The Heritage Coast Federation is believed to be among the first in North Yorkshire to take this step, working in partnership with Harrogate-based technology firm Nynet and North Yorkshire Education Services.

The move aims to support Year 5 and Year 6 pupils with a smartphone-free school environment, in line with the growing trend of children carrying devices for safety as they begin to walk to and from school independently.

Headteacher Helen Thompson said: "We don't have a smartphone problem and we'd like to keep it that way.

"We recognise that many families choose these devices for communication and safety reasons.

"Our aim is simply to ensure that, during the school day, pupils can focus on learning, friendships and play without the distractions and pressures that connected devices can bring.

"This is about normalising a smartphone-free school culture before smartphones become an issue."

Nynet, which specialises in school-focused technology solutions, developed the pouches as part of a broader strategy supporting schools to address emerging digital challenges and adhere to government guidance.

Chantal Anselme of Nynet said: "Having worked with more than 200 schools across North Yorkshire as their trusted technology and network partner, we've developed a real understanding of the unique challenges schools face.

"The smartphone-free pouches were developed to provide a practical, education-focused solution that supports both pupils and staff."

Keren Wild of NYES Digital welcomed the federation’s “proactive approach.”

She said: "Planning ahead helps schools create the best possible learning environment, allowing pupils to focus on learning and wellbeing.

"Smartphone-free pouches are just one of a suite of solutions developed by Nynet.

"Every solution includes implementation support, training and ongoing guidance, giving schools the confidence to introduce this new initiative successfully and sustainably."
