A CAT has died after a crash with an alleged illegal e-bike in North Yorkshire.

The incident, which killed the family pet, happened in Overdale in Eastfield near Scarborough at about 7.50pm on Monday, July 20.

Police are urging eyewitnesses to come forward, along with those who may have seen any bikes in the area at the time, or who has CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage.

  


A force spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could help our investigation, please contact us quoting reference 12260138692:

• Email: PCSO 1578 Sam Piercy at [sam.piercy@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:sam.piercy@northyorkshire.police.uk)

• Online: Visit our website, select 'Tell us about', then choose 'An existing case or report'

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote reference 12260138692 when providing information.”
