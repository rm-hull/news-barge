## York attraction wins four awards just 18 months after opening

The unique Puzzling World of Professor Kettlestring in York has won a fourth award. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A visitor attraction in York has collected four major awards just 18 months after opening.

The family-owned Puzzling World of Professor Kettlestring has earned recognition for excellence in visitor experience, innovation and customer satisfaction since launching on January 10, 2025.

Matthew Tritton-Hughes, founder of the attraction, said: "We're absolutely delighted and incredibly grateful.

"To receive four major awards in just our second year is far beyond anything we expected.

"As a family-run business, every award means the world to us.

"It's a real testament to our amazing team, whose passion and hard work shine through every day, and to the thousands of visitors who have supported us, left wonderful reviews and recommended us to others.

"We can't thank everyone enough."

Its latest accolade is the Emerging Tourism Business of the Year 2026, awarded at the SME UK Enterprise Awards.

This adds to its growing list of honours, which includes New Tourism Business of the Year at the Visit York Tourism Awards, Best Tourist Attraction at the YorkWorks Business Awards, and Tripadvisor's Travellers' Choice Award 2026.

The attraction, located in the heart of York at 14 Merchantgate, features immersive illusion rooms, interactive exhibits, and hands-on puzzles.

It has become a favourite with families, couples, and groups looking for a fun and unusual day out.

The awards highlight the attraction’s focus on delivering memorable, high-quality experiences for visitors of all ages.

With the school summer holidays under way, the attraction says families are being encouraged to book in advance due to increased demand following its recent successes.

The Puzzling World of Professor Kettlestring offers a range of inclusive events and experiences throughout the year, including Calm Hours and Dog Days.

Visitors can find out more, book tickets, and plan their visit by going to www.puzzlingworldyork.com or by following the attraction on social media.
