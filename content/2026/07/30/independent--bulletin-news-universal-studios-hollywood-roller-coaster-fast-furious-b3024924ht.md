Noise complaints reported at Universal Studios

Universal Studios Hollywood's new Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift roller coaster is generating noise complaints from residents in the nearby Toluca Lake neighborhood.

The high-speed outdoor coaster, which spins cars up to 72 mph, is currently in technical rehearsals, with residents describing rider screams as sounding like “bloody murder” or a “horror film.”

Residents report constant and intrusive noise, even with windows closed, despite Universal's attempts to mitigate sound.

Universal Studios has installed sound shields and pea gravel along the track and is actively monitoring sound levels during the attraction's evaluation period.

The theme park has also opened a hotline and installed microphones in the neighborhood to address and track residents' concerns about the noise.
