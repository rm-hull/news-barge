# All Creatures Great and Small: Discover Grassington hiring team for new exhibition

## Discover Grassington is hiring a new team for its All Creatures Great and Small exhibition.

Following the funding from the Vibrant and Sustainable High Streets Fund, supported by York and North Yorkshire Mayor David Skaith, Discover Grassington will be recruiting a ‘friendly and enthusiastic team’ for the new All Creatures Great and Small Exhibition at Grassington Town Hall.

They are currently recruiting for one supervisor position for up to 20 hours per week across four days.

The pay rate will be £13.71 per hour, they will be self-employed and will work daytime hours only.

Discover Grassington will also be recruiting for exhibition assistants (up to three positions) for one to two days per week (up to 10 hours).

The pay rate will be £12.71 per hour, they will also be self-employed and will work daytime hours only.

According to the Discover Grassington Facebook post: “You'll help create a fantastic visitor experience, welcome guests from across the UK and beyond, support events and marketing, and become part of an exciting new attraction celebrating one of Yorkshire's most beloved TV series.

“No previous experience is required. Full training, guidance and ongoing support will be provided.”

The attributes they are looking for in their recruits are:

- Friendly and welcoming

- Reliable and organised

- Confident communicating with visitors

- Comfortable working independently and as part of a team

- Computer literate

- Passionate about showcasing Grassington and delivering excellent customer service

These positions are a minimum 18-month contract and for those who are interested in applying, they must contact Lynette Brammah either via email: [email protected] or phone: 07786 914546, Agi Rusinek (Black Horse Hotel) or Julia Metcalfe (The Flower Loft).

The closing date for applications is: Friday, August 7, 2026.
