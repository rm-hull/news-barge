## Today’s best Lenovo discount codes and deals

From specs to brands and operating systems, navigating the world of tech can be a difficult task. But if you’re an Android user who’s looking for a budget-friendly buy, it’s safe to say tech titan Lenovo is a good place to start. Here, we’ve rounded up the best Lenovo discount codes to help you save on your next upgrade.

## Ways to save at Lenovo

**Get £30 off your first Lenovo order**

A quick and easy way to save on your first order with Lenovo is by signing up for the brand’s email newsletter. Once you’re registered, you’ll receive a unique discount code straight to your inbox. Apply the code at checkout to get £30 off any order of more than £300.

As a subscriber, you’ll also receive weekly emails with the latest offers, Lenovo promo codes and product launches.

**Get a £25 voucher when you refer a friend**

Lenovo’s referral programme is an easy way to get money off your next order, simply by spreading the word. For every friend you refer who purchases £300 or more, you’ll get £25 in the form of a voucher. You can refer 10 friends every year and stack the vouchers to use on future purchases.

### Students and educators save up to 20 per cent at Lenovo

You can get 10-20 per cent off PCs, phones, accessories, monitors and more by verifying your status as a student or teacher through Unidays or Student Beans.

First, sign up for Lenovo’s education store, using your school email address. You’ll be asked to verify your identity through Unidays or Student Beans, after which you’ll get access to the discounts available on Lenovo’s education store.

Not all offers will be available for everyone. For example, some will only be available to university students, some to teachers, and so on. Go to the banner of the page and make sure you have selected either ‘all education level’, or whichever one applies to you.

**Join the Lenovo Pro Rewards programme**

The Lenovo rewards programme is designed to help individual shoppers, as well as small and medium-sized businesses, get more value from their purchases. By joining, you can earn points on eligible orders, enjoy bonus rewards and even benefit from referring other businesses or friends and family.

**Here’s a breakdown of the key perks:**

- **Earn points on purchases:** Get up to nine per cent back in reward points when you shop with Lenovo online. The points can then be used at checkout to get money off your orders.
- **Birthday bonus:** Earn double points on any purchases made during your birthday month.
- **Referral rewards for businesses:** Refer other businesses and, if they join (spending at least £1,200), both you and your referral get 100,000 points. You can make up to 10 referrals each year.

**Is Lenovo Rewards worth it?**

Lenovo Rewards is free to join and may be worthwhile if you buy tech from Lenovo regularly. For most purchases, you’ll earn between 3-9 per cent of the value of your purchase (before VAT) back in points. It can be tricky to figure out exactly how many points you’ll get from each purchase, but when you’re at the checkout, it should say in a purple box on the bottom right-hand side.

You’ll also get double points on your birthday, plus you can earn £25 in points for referring a friend who purchases £300 or more.

However, remember that third-party retailers such as Amazon, Currys, AO and John Lewis can sometimes beat Lenovo’s own website on price, so it’s always worth shopping around for the best deal.
