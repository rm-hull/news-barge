**Earn and redeem Viator Rewards**

Once registered with Viator, you will access its free loyalty program where you will automatically earn around five per cent back in rewards for every qualifying booking made directly through Viator’s website or app.

These credits appear in your account 24 hours after your activity begins and can be used towards your next booking. You have 180 days to redeem them, and they will be automatically applied at checkout, reducing the total amount you pay (a minimum £1 spend still applies).

Keep in mind that you can’t combine rewards with promo codes. If you’re choosing between a discount code and Viator rewards, compare the value of each before checking out.

**Save 10 per cent with the Viator student discount**

Viator also offers exclusive student discounts through both Student Beans and Unidays, allowing verified students to save on tours and experiences around the world. Both platforms offer 10 per cent off most bookings by logging in, retrieving the unique code and applying it at checkout.

**10 per cent discount for NHS employees and Blue Light Card holders**

NHS employees can also enjoy 10 per cent off Viator bookings through Health Service Discounts. Simply verify your NHS status on the platform, access the offer and apply the promo code at checkout.

Similarly, Blue Light Card holders – including NHS, emergency services, armed forces, and social care staff – also receive a 10 per cent discount when booking through the "Holiday discounts" section of the Blue Light Card website or app.

**Download the Viator app for more discounts and codes**

The Viator app offers a range of exclusive benefits designed to make planning and booking activities easier. You can access app-only promo codes, sometimes as much as 35 per cent on top of regular discounts. By downloading the app and turning on notifications, you’ll also activate real-time alerts about flash sales, seasonal offers and last-minute availability.
