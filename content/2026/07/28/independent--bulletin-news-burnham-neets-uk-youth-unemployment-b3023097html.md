# The reasons why Neet numbers are rising as Burnham reveals major shake-up

- Andy Burnham has unveiled a significant overhaul of England's education system, aiming to tackle youth unemployment and align curricula with local business needs.
- This initiative includes the introduction of new, 'prestigious' technical education routes to provide alternatives to purely academic paths.
- The number of 16-24 year-olds not in education, employment, or training (Neet) in the UK rose to 1.01 million in January-March, a 6% increase from the previous quarter.
- This figure, representing 13.5% of the age group, marks the highest Neet estimate since late 2013, primarily driven by a record number of economically inactive young people.
- Contributing factors include the cost-of-living crisis, increased economic inactivity among young men, social media anxiety, and a reduction in entry-level job opportunities.
