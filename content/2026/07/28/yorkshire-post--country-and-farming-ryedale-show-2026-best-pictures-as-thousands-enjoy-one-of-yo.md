The Ryedale Show returned to Duncombe Park on Tuesday (Jul 28), bringing together farmers, exhibitors and visitors for one of Yorkshire's best-loved agricultural events.

Established in 1855, the annual show is renowned for its high-quality livestock competitions, traditional rural crafts and bustling trade stands.

Held on the last Tuesday of July, the event featured eight rings showcasing cattle, sheep, pigs, horses and goats, alongside produce, children's classes, sheepdog trials and a host of family attractions.

The picturesque Duncombe Park estate once again provided the backdrop after the show's return to one of its historic homes following more than 60 years away.
