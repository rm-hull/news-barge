A CRASH has closed a major road close to a popular North Yorkshire beach.

North Yorkshire Police have confirmed that the A165 is currently blocked after a crash that took place earlier today.

  


- 
    **Emergency services called to incident in centre of North Yorkshire town**
- 
    **Three young people rescued after getting lost in North Yorkshire woods at night**

A spokesperson for the force said: “It has been reported to be between the roundabout at Sands Road (Hunmanby Gap) and the junction at Reighton Nurseries to Hunmanby

The road is reported to be currently blocked and road closures are in place. Please divert through Reighton Village.”
