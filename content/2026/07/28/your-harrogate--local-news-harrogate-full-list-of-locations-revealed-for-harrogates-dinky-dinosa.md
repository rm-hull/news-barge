                                    With just days to go until the launch of Harrogate's much-loved Lego Trail, Harrogate Business Improvement District (BID) has revealed where visitors will be able to find each of this year's prehistoric displays.

The free family trail, proudly sponsored by The Crown Hotel, launches on Saturday (1st August), with 13 large-scale Lego dinosaur models set to appear in businesses and venues across Harrogate town centre.

Families, residents and visitors are invited to collect a trail map from The Crown Hotel or Games Crusade on Oxford Street before setting off around the town centre.

By scanning the QR code at each dinosaur location and collecting every letter, participants can uncover the secret word and return their completed trail map to Games Crusade to claim a small prize. Those who complete the trail can also enter a prize draw to win a £50 Harrogate Gift Card via the Harrogate BID website.

The trail is completely free to take part in and has been designed to be accessible, encouraging people of all ages to explore Harrogate while discovering the town's fantastic retail, hospitality and leisure businesses.

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said:

"We're incredibly excited to reveal the locations for this year's Dinky Dinosaurs Lego Trail.

"The trail has become one of our most anticipated annual events and we know families look forward to exploring Harrogate in search of each model.

"By placing the dinosaurs across the town centre, we're encouraging visitors to discover different streets, support local businesses and enjoy everything Harrogate has to offer during the summer holidays.

"Don't forget to pick up your trail map, scan the QR code at each location, work out the secret word and head to Games Crusade to collect your prize!"


Dan Siddle, General Manager at The Crown Hotel, said:

"We're delighted to be sponsoring the Harrogate Lego Trail once again.

"It's a fantastic event that brings families into the town centre, supports local businesses and creates a real buzz throughout the summer holidays. We're proud to continue supporting an event that has become such a popular part of Harrogate's events calendar."


The official Dinky Dinosaurs and their locations are:

- The Crown Hotel (2 models)
- Turkish Baths, Parliament Street
- Victoria Shopping Centre (2 models)
- Wetherspoons, Parliament Street
- Jespers, Oxford Street
- Games Crusade, Oxford Street
- Artizan Café, Cambridge Road
- Bettys, Parliament Street
- Primark (Oxford Street window)
- Yorkshire Soap Company, James Street
- Otto Café, Oxford Street
- Everyman Cinema, Albert Street

The Harrogate Lego Trail forms part of Harrogate BID's wider programme of events designed to increase footfall, support local businesses and encourage residents and visitors to explore the town centre throughout the year.

*More information, including the trail map and entry into the £50 Harrogate Gift Card prize draw, can be found on the Harrogate BID  website.*
