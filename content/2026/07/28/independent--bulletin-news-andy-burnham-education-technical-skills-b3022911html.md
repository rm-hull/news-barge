# What we know about Andy Burnham’s major education changes

- Andy Burnham, the new prime minister, has announced a major overhaul of England's education system, aiming to tackle youth unemployment and further the government's devolution drive.
- The reforms will mandate schools to adapt their curriculum to align with the needs of local businesses, introducing new 'prestigious' technical education routes.
- This initiative represents the most significant change to the schooling system since the 2011 reforms, with a central aim to shift the focus from university-centric education and equally value technical skills.
- From age 14, students in Year 10 will have the option to combine core academic subjects with technical education directly linked to available jobs in their area, including practical work experience with employers.
- Expected to commence by September 2028, the changes will be funded by an existing £570 million investment for college places and a £4.7 billion Department for Education settlement, with school performance measures also set to be revised to include technical education provision.
