# BBC reports from outside court after judge rules singer D4vd will stand trial for murder

A judge has ruled that US musician D4vd will go on trial for murder over the death of 14-year-old Celeste Rivas Hernandez.

The decision came after a five-day preliminary hearing, in which Los Angeles prosecutors questioned a dozen witnesses and displayed photographic evidence.

BBC's North America correspondent Shaimaa Khalil was inside the courtroom.
