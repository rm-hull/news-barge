Emergency services were called to a serious crash near a North Yorkshire village.

It happened on the B1363 Main Street near Gilling East, south of Helmsley, at around 11.15am on Thursday (23 July).

A black Mercedes-Benz and a blue BMW motorbike collided.

#### Map location

The rider of the motorbike was taken to hospital for treatment by ambulance and has since been discharged.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the incident or anyone who has dashcam footage of the area during this time.”
