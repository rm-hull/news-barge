Firefighters were called to another fire started deliberately in York last night (Monday).

The incident, off **Burnholme Drive** in Heworth, was reported at**8.48pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to reports of a small fire consisting of sticks and leaves.

#### Burnholme Drive, York

“Crews extinguished using knapsack sprayer. The cause was deliberate.”

Later, at **9.44pm**, Acomb firefighters put out a campfire left unattended in** Knavesmire Woods**.
