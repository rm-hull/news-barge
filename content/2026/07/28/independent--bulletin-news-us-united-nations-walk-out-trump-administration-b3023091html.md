# Why the US walked out on France at a United Nations meeting

- U.S. diplomats staged a walkout during a United Nations Security Council meeting on Ukraine after French counterparts criticized the U.S. human rights record, reflecting heightened tensions between the two nations.
- The walkout was triggered by France's criticism of the U.S. opposition to the reappointment of U.N. human rights chief Volker Turk, who received overwhelming support from the General Assembly despite U.S. dissent.
- A social media post from France's U.N. Mission in Geneva, which stated the U.S. was no longer a 'beacon of human rights' and stood 'isolated' with countries like North Korea, further inflamed the situation.
- Deputy U.S. Ambassador Dan Negrea accused France of feigning 'moral outrage' and lecturing the world, asserting that the U.S. would no longer tolerate such 'condescending and disrespectful rhetoric.'
- The incident underscores broader strains in U.S.-Europe relations during the Trump administration, including disagreements over NATO commitments and other international policies.
