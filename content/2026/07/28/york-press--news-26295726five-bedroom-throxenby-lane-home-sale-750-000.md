A large family home, with five bedrooms and a sunroom overlooking the garden, is up for sale in Scarborough.

The detached house, in Throxenby Lane, is on the market with a guide price of £750,000.

Estate agents, Colin Ellis Property Services, said it is an "executive detached family home set within an exclusive development of just three properties".

  Open-plan living and dining room with feature fireplace 

*(Image: Colin Ellis Property Services)*

There are versatile options to convert one of the bedrooms into an office.

The principal bedroom is described as exceptionally spacious and has an en-suite bathroom, and there is also a second en-suite bedroom.

The house has a reception hall, which leads to an open-plan living and dining room with a feature fireplace.

  Sunroom overlooking the rear garden 

*(Image: Colin Ellis Property Services)*

There is also a lounge and a sunroom, which overlooks the garden.

The kitchen is fitted with a range of cabinets and is complemented by a utility room and ground-floor cloakroom.

Upstairs, there are four double bedrooms, including a spacious principal suite with a well-appointed en-suite bathroom.

The second bedroom also has an en-suite, while the other bedrooms are served by a contemporary family bathroom.

  Kitchen with fitted cabinets 

*(Image: Colin Ellis Property Services)*

There is also a further versatile room, which could be used as a nursery, study, dressing room or hobby room.

Outside, the mature rear garden has an expansive lawn, established trees, and well-stocked borders.

There is also a double garage with driveway parking, with the property approached via a private road.

  Rear garden with lawn, established trees and planted borders 

*(Image: Colin Ellis Property Services)*

Colin Ellis Property Services said the property occupies a generous plot with mature gardens and has been thoughtfully designed to provide spacious and versatile accommodation, perfectly suited to modern family living.

According to the estate agent, the house has an EPC rating of C and is freehold and is in council tax band G.
