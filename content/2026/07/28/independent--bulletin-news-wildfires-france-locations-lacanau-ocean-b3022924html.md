# Thousands more forced from their homes as heatwave threatens to intensify France wildfires

- An additional 4,000 people have been ordered to evacuate campsites, tourist residences and leisure parks in France, specifically in the Lacanau Ocean area and along both shores of the strip.
- These new evacuation orders are a precautionary measure ahead of an anticipated heatwave expected to impact the region later today.
- The latest evacuations contribute to a larger total, with over 360,000 people already having been evacuated across France and Spain due to ongoing crises.
- Firefighters in both France and Spain are engaged in a critical effort to bring raging wildfires under control.
- This urgent action is necessary before the return of severe heatwave conditions to the two countries, which officials fear would exacerbate the current wildfire situation.
