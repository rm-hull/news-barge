FARM machinery has been completely destroyed by fire in an East Yorkshire village.

  


Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said its crews were called to Burnbutts Lane in Hutton Cranswick at around 4.50pm yesterday (Monday, July 27).


A service spokesperson said a combine harvester was on fire inside an agricultural building.

The spokesperson added: "Breathing apparatus, two hose reel jets and a thermal image camera were put to use.

"The combine was completely destroyed by the fire."
