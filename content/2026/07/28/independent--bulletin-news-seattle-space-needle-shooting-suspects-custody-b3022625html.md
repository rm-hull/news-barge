# Victims identified after deadly Seattle shooting as police hunt for remaining suspect

- Three individuals, including a 19-year-old believed to be a suspect, were killed in a mass shooting at the annual Bite of Seattle food festival on Sunday evening.
- The victims were identified as Carlos Israel Sanchez Villalba, 44, Ashley Whitehead, 56, and Junior Cee Niko Semo, 19.
- Four other people, including a two-year-old boy, were injured; three adults have since been discharged from the hospital, and the toddler is in satisfactory condition.
- Police believe at least three suspects were involved, with a 15-year-old detained for firearms violations and first-degree assault, and one suspect (Semo) killed in the gunfire.
- Authorities are actively searching for at least one additional unknown suspect involved in the shooting, which occurred as the festival was winding down.
