Deep within one of the most remote wilderness areas of the Kruger National Park, two safari expedition camps appear for just a few months of the year before disappearing without a trace. Kruger Untamed's Satara Plains Camp and Tshokwane River Camp are seasonal, low-impact camps designed to immerse guests in the wilderness while leaving the lightest possible environmental footprint.

Intended exclusively for private excursions in small groups, Tshokwane River Camp overlooks the tranquil Ripape Riverbed, shaded by towering trees, while Satara Plains Camp commands sweeping views across one of the Kruger's most celebrated big cat territories. Each stay includes guided game drives as well as optional walking safaris led by expert trackers and guides.

Rates for the 2026 season, per person sharing, per night: Satara from £249 and Tshokwane from £363. Rates include luxury tented accommodation, meals, beverages and safari activities.

This summer, Forestry England is trialling a time-limited series of overnight stays across seven woodland parking locations around the country. The scheme allows a limited number of campervans and motorhomes to park for up to two nights at some of England's best-loved forests, including Cardinham Woods in Cornwall, Kielder Forest near the Scottish border and Gisburn Forest in Lancashire.

For an affordable overnight stay, park up beneath the stars, surrounded by pine and oak trees and enjoy a peaceful escape deep within some of the country’s most cherished forests. Wake up to the sounds of birdsong, explore woodland trails throughout the day and spend quiet evenings watching the sun set above the forest canopy. Most sites require advance booking, with stays limited to two nights. Basic facilities are available.
