EMERGENCY services are currently in attendance at a major North Yorkshire town.

Multiple vehicles from North Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been spotted in Commercial Street. Norton today (July 28).

The images – which were shared at 11.30am – show three vehicles in place and several orange barriers up, but by 1.30pm, they have all left the scene.

North Yorkshire Police have been contacted for comment.
