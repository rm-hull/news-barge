                                    A driver has been taken to hospital following a three-vehicle collision on the A1(M) near Wetherby.

The collision happened at around 11:19am on Friday (24th July) on the northbound carriageway just before Junction 47.

It involved a white Tesla, a white Mercedes Sprinter and a white Volkswagen Transporter.

The driver of the Tesla was taken to hospital by ambulance for treatment following the collision.

North Yorkshire Police are now appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage as enquiries continue.

A spokesperson said:

"We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision, seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage."
