# Italy set to reappoint Roberto Mancini after missing out on Pep Guardiola

- Roberto Mancini is set to return for his second stint as Italy’s national team manager, a decision confirmed by Italian Football Federation (FIGC) President Giovanni Malago.
- Mancini, who previously led Italy to victory in the European Championship in 2021, replaces Gennaro Gattuso, who resigned in April after Italy failed to qualify for the World Cup for the third consecutive time.
- Alongside Mancini, Claudio Ranieri has been appointed as the new FIGC technical director, taking over from Paolo Maldini, and will oversee technical operations and elite football development.
- These appointments follow a period of turbulence for the FIGC, which saw former technical director Paolo Maldini and adviser Leonardo resign, and first-choice candidate Pep Guardiola turn down the role.
- FIGC President Malago emphasised the importance of this new leadership structure, with Italy scheduled to play Belgium in the Nations League on 25 September.
