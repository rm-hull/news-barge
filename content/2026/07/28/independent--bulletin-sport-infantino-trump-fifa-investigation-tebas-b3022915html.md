Fifa president Gianni Infantino's recent Instagram post, which controversially accused his critics of spreading "hate" during the World Cup, has been strongly criticised by La Liga president Javier Tebas.

Infantino's extensive 15-page message suggested critics should "meditate, pray, or watch a football match”, prompting Tebas to label it "pathetic" and assert that transparency and accountability are obligations, not hate.

The US House Judiciary Committee has confirmed an investigation into Infantino, citing "mounting evidence of potential corruption and a possible quid pro quo between Fifa and the Trump Administration."

The Committee alleges Fifa engaged in a "sustained campaign to secure special favours from President Trump”, including the dismissal of high-profile corruption cases and actions like awarding a "hilariously fake" Fifa Peace Prize and renting office space at Trump Tower.

The US House Judiciary Committee has requested documents and information related to the investigation to be submitted by 10pm BST on August 9, with Fifa having been approached for comment.
