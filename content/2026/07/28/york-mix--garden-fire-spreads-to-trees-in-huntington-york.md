Firefighters tackled a blaze in Huntington, York, this afternoon (Tuesday).

They were called to **Brockfield Road** at**3.58pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from York and Acomb were mobilised to a large fire in the rear garden of a property that spread to the tree line and neighbouring properties’ garden.

#### Brockfield Road, Huntington

“Crews used one hose reel jet to extinguish.”
