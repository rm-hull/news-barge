A detached four-bedroom house in York is on the market for £1.1 million.

The home, located in Bootham Crescent, features a spacious living room, an open-plan kitchen and dining area, a utility room and a ground-floor WC.

The master bedroom has its own en-suite, while the other three double bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bathroom.

  Living room with feature fireplace 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

Outside, the property has a private rear garden with a patio and off-street parking with an electric gate.

The estate agent, Hudson Moody, said the house is in an "enviable city centre location" with easy access to transport links and local amenities.

The house was recently extended and fully renovated by the current owners.

  The home is on sale for £1.1m with estate agent Hudson Moody 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

The reception hall leads to a large living room with a feature fireplace and bi-fold doors opening onto the garden.

The open-plan kitchen and living area has a central island with a breakfast bar, integrated Bosch dishwasher, Rangemaster gas cooker, fridge freezer, wine cooler, pull-out pantry cupboard and built-in waste and recycling storage.

  Open-plan kitchen and dining area with island, breakfast bar and integrated appliances 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

The utility room has space for a washing machine and tumble dryer and there is also a separate downstairs WC.

On the first floor, the principal bedroom has dual-aspect windows, fitted wardrobes and a large en-suite bathroom with both a bath and separate shower.

  Private rear garden with patio area 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

The other three double bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bathroom with a shower over the bath.

The house is in easy reach of York city centre, local amenities and schools including Clifton Green Primary School and Vale of York Academy.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact Hudson Moody on 01904 918594.
