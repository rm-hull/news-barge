People are being invited to explore a retirement community during an open week.

Riverside Gardens in Knaresborough is hosting the event every day until Saturday, August 1, from 10.30am to 4pm, offering a chance to tour the development, meet homeowners, and chat with the team.

Visitors can explore the show apartments and shared facilities and enjoy a guided stroll through the landscaped gardens.

Phil Holyoak, sales director for Adlington Retirement Living, said: "Many people are curious about retirement living but aren't quite sure what to expect.

"Our open week gives them the chance to look around at their own pace, meet the people who live here and experience the welcoming atmosphere for themselves.

"We know that deciding whether to move is a big decision, so we'd encourage anyone who is curious to come along, take a look around and discover what life here is really like.

"There's absolutely no pressure, just a warm welcome and the chance to ask questions."

Guests can enjoy complimentary refreshments, including a glass of Prosecco.

Riverside Gardens offers one, two, and three-bedroom age-exclusive apartments, alongside amenities such as a homeowners' lounge, restaurant, hair salon, and activities studio.

Visitors are welcome to drop in or book an appointment by calling 01423 222478.
