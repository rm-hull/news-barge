                                    ABBA fans are being invited to grab a picnic blanket and sing along under the stars as Mamma Mia! heads to Harewood House next month.

The popular Adventure Cinema tour is bringing its *Mamma Mia! ExtrABBAganza* to the stately home's grounds on Saturday 22nd August, promising an evening of music, dancing and outdoor entertainment.

Gates open at 6pm, with the film starting at 7:30pm on a giant outdoor screen.

Before and after the screening, visitors can enjoy an ABBA disco, with organisers encouraging guests to dress in their best 70s-inspired outfits and sing along to the band's biggest hits.

Guests are welcome to bring picnics, blankets and camping chairs to make the most of the evening.

A selection of street food vendors and a licensed bar will also be available on site, although Harewood House has reminded visitors that glass is not permitted.

The event is rated PG and is suitable for families, forming part of a packed weekend of outdoor cinema at Harewood House.

The venue will also host screenings of *Dirty Dancing*,* Encanto*,* Star Wars: A New Hope*,* Andrea Bocelli 30: The Celebration*and* Bing and His Friends at the Cinema!* across the weekend.

*Adventure Cinema says the event offers "the ultimate ABBA and Mamma Mia! experience", with tickets available to book online  here.*
