If your head is still spinning from the first episode of *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe,* I can't say that I blame you.

This week, we're going to officially universe jump for the first time. So, when does *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* episode 2 arrive on HBO Max?

## What time can I watch Stuart Fails to Save the Universe episode 2 on HBO Max?

![Stuart Fails to Save the Universe | Weeks Ahead Trailer | HBO Max - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/orv-w9ewCps/maxresdefault.jpg) 

For US viewers, *Stuart Fails to Save the Universe* episode 2 will drop on**Thursday, July 30 at 6pm PT/ 9pm ET**.

Internationally, you're looking out for these timings:

- **US**– 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **Canada**– 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **UK**– Friday, July 31 at 2am BST
- **India**– Friday, July 31 at 6:30am IST
- **Singapore**– Friday, July 31 at 9am SGT
- **Australia**– Friday, July 31 at 11am AEDT
- **New Zealand**– Friday, July 31 at 12pm NZDT
