This Sunday (**2 August**), runners of all ages and abilities will hit our streets as the York 10K returns.

The route will explore some of the city’s most iconic landmarks, including York Racecourse, the city walls, Clifford’s Tower and York Minster.

And **YorkMix** are once again the official media partners for the event, and we’ll be there with our summer tour cheering you on – so come along and say hello!

Delivered by Jane Tomlinson’s Run For All in partnership with Arla Protein, the popular event attracts participants of all ages and abilities each year, alongside hundreds of volunteers and spectators who line the route to cheer them on.

As always, the Arla Protein York 10K will require some roads to be closed for the duration of the event.

Here are the details. Access for emergency service vehicles shall be retained throughout the duration of the event, and all times are approximate and subject to change.

### York 10K road closures

**Saturday 1 August**

Knavesmire Road will be closed between its junction with Tadcaster Road and Campleshon Road from 12:00 on Saturday 1st August 2026 until approximately 15:00 on Sunday 2nd August 2026.

Tristan Batley-Kyle, operations director at Run For All, said: “The safety of our participants, spectators and local communities is always our top priority. As with any major road running event, temporary road closures are required to ensure the event can take place safely.

“We would like to thank residents and businesses for their patience and understanding, and apologise for any inconvenience these restrictions may cause. Our team will work hard to reopen roads as quickly as possible once it is safe to do so.”

The day is expected to raise thousands for charity, with participants encouraged to raise money for a cause close to their heart or support one of the event’s partner charities, which include; the Jane Tomlinson Appeal, Family Fund, RSPCA, St Leonard’s Hospice, The Hut York, York Against Cancer, York Carers Centre, York City Foundation, York Down Syndrome Support Group, and Mind York.

There’s also plenty on offer for younger runners, with the York Mini and Junior Runs getting underway from 12pm. Designed for children aged 3-8, the 1.5km Mini Run sees youngsters take part alongside a parent or guardian, while the 2.5km Junior Run is open to children aged 9–14.

Every young participant will receive a commemorative medal at the finish line, along with a selection of post-run goodies to celebrate their achievement.

The 10K will kick off the day at 09:30, followed by the Junior and Mini races starting at 12:00, with the start and finish lines all located at Knavesmire Road.

The Arla Protein York 10K is supported by City of York Council, York Racecourse, YorkMix, Applied Nutrition, Up & Running, Coopah, Shokz, Gradwell, Marathon Photos Live, Project Servator and Jog On.
