MORE than £100 of fuel has been stolen from a petrol station on a major North Yorkshire road.

North Yorkshire Police have asked for help in identifying the man in this image who the force believe could help its investigation.

It happened on 1st June 2026 in 7.55am when £102.00 worth of fuel was stolen from the A19 Southbound Services in Ingleby Arncliffe.

“We believe the person in the image will have information that could help our investigation,” said a spokesperson for the force.

If you have any information, please contact PC 662 ABEL at [samantha.abel@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:samantha.abel@northyorkshire.police.uk), online or using live chat, or by calling 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.
