## New pick-your-own flower field near York opens to public with summer events

Dunnington Flower Field recently welcomed its first visitors to Church Balk in Dunnington, near York *(Image: Supplied)*

A pick-your-own flower field near York recently opened to the public.

Dunnington Flower Field welcomed visitors to Church Balk in Dunnington, near York, from July 17.

The new attraction allows guests to hand-pick their own bouquets from a field in full bloom.

Oliver Medforth, who is behind the project, said: "We're incredibly excited to welcome everyone to Dunnington Flower Field.

"It's been wonderful watching the flowers grow over the past eight weeks, and we can't wait to share them with the community.

"We hope visitors will enjoy creating their own unique bouquets while spending time outdoors in a beautiful setting."

Created as a space for families, friends, and flower enthusiasts of all ages, the flower field features thousands of blooms, including Medieval Carpet, Vale of York Bloom, and other seasonal varieties.

Guests can pick their own flowers, enjoy refreshments from local businesses, and take part in a range of events throughout the summer.

Planned events include a yoga night on August 5 from 7pm to 8pm, a sound bath session on August 11, also from 7pm to 8pm, a live music night with Ross Mcwhirter on August 14, and a special breathwork session on August 26, from 7pm to 8pm.

For more information about the yoga night, call Emily on 07715694656, and for the breathwork session, call Anna on 07580045969.

The Dunnington Flower Field team said they are passionate about supporting local suppliers and creating a welcoming atmosphere for visitors.

More events are expected to be announced throughout the flowering season.

The field will be open throughout the summer, with visitors invited to follow the project on Facebook for the latest updates.

The address for the field is Dunnington Flower Field, Church Balk, Dunnington, York YO19 5PR.

The site is accessible by car and public transport, with the number 10 and X46 bus routes stopping directly outside.

Mr Medforth and his team said they look forward to welcoming visitors throughout the season and sharing the spectacular colours of Dunnington Flower Field.

For more information and event details, visitors are encouraged to follow the Dunnington Flower Field Facebook page.
