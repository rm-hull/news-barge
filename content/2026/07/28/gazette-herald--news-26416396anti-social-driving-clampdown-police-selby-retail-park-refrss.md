POLICE said they are clamping down on anti-social driving at a leisure and retail area of a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police said the "completely unacceptable" driving in Three Lakes Retail Park, off Bawtry Road in Selby poses a significant risk to the public and other road users.

A force spokesperson said: "This type of behaviour will not be tolerated.

"A community engagement event will be arranged in the near future, where residents will have the opportunity to discuss their concerns directly with local officers and learn more about the measures being implemented to tackle the issue.

"We appreciate the continued support of the community and encourage anyone with information relating to anti-social driving to come forward."

North Yorkshire Police said details of the community event will be released "in due course."
