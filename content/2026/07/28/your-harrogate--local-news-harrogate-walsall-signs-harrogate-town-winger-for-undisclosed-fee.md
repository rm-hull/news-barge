                                    Harrogate Town has confirmed that Reece Smith has joined League Two side Walsall for an undisclosed fee.


Reece signed from Maidenhead ahead of the 2025/26 season and would be an almost ever-present in Simon Weaver’s side for the entire season, making a total of 52 appearances.

Reece departs having also made a big impression off the pitch, being named PFA Community Champion for his work with the club's Foundation in and around the local community.

A club spokesperson said:

"The club would like to thank Reece for his efforts and wish him well for the future."
