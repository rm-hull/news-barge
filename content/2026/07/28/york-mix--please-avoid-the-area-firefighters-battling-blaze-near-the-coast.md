Firefighters are battling a large field blaze out near the North Yorkshire coast this evening (Tuesday).

Several crews are in attendance at the fire in a field off Bridlington Road, Hunmanby.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The fire covers approximately 20 acres.

#### Bridlington Road, Hunmanby

“Firefighters are working alongside local farmers using water bowsers to contain the fire and prevent it from spreading further.”

It comes 24 hours after someone deliberately started a fire on cliffs at Filey.
