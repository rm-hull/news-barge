A barn conversion in rural Yorkshire is on the market for £1,150,000.

The property in Norwood, near Harrogate, is built from stone and has been extended.

It has five bedrooms, three bathrooms, three reception rooms, and a detached double garage.

  The property is on sale with Nicholls Tyreman for £1.15m 

*(Image: Nicholls Tyreman)*

It is situated in a small development and has views of the surrounding countryside.

The home is laid out over three floors and has double glazing as well as underfloor heating throughout the ground floor and bathrooms.

  A separate sitting room area 

*(Image: Nicholls Tyreman)*

The accommodation comprises a large entrance hall, a living room, and a sitting room.

There is also a large open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area with bi-folding doors.

The kitchen area was refitted in 2021 and has high-quality Leicht fitted units including a fridge-freezer, separate fridge, induction hob, and a central island with a breakfast bar.

  Kitchen with island, breakfast bar, and integrated appliances 

*(Image: Nicholls Tyreman)*

There are three bedrooms on the first floor, one of which has an en-suite, and a family bathroom.

The second floor has two further bedrooms and a shower room.

The rear garden is designed for relaxation and entertaining, featuring several patio areas, including a raised Jacuzzi terrace beneath a timber pergola, a greenhouse, and a sunken patio.

  Garden with patios, pergola, greenhouse, and access to paddock 

*(Image: Nicholls Tyreman)*

The garden leads onto a paddock which could be used for grazing or recreation.

The property is accessed by a long private lane leading to a spacious driveway and communal courtyard, providing off-street parking and access to the detached double garage, which benefits from electric up-and-over doors, power, and lighting.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact estate agent Nicholls Tyreman on 01423 578039.
