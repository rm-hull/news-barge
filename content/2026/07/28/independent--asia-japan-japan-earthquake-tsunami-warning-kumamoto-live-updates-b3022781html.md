Multiple deaths are being reported after a shopping mall collapsed during today's 7.1-magnitude earthquake in southern Japan’s Kumamoto Prefecture.

Fire officials say an upper storey of the Aeon Mall Kumamoto in Kashima Town collapsed as a result of the explosion, as emergency crews rushed to the scene.

Mall operator Aeon said customers were being evacuated and it was still assessing the situation.

The earthquake caused powerful shaking and a tsunami warning was issued for Kyushu's south-western coastlines, though it was later lifted.

Prime minister Sanae Takaichi said people had been injured and that officials were evaluating reports of collapsed buildings, fires, damaged roads and bridges, and power outages. She urged residents to evacuate to safe locations and remain alert.

Public broadcaster NHK showed burning and collapsed buildings, large cracks in roads, including an elevated highway, and a derailed freight train.

Mobile phone services were also disrupted in some areas and around 40,000 homes lost electricity.

## Multiple people confirmed dead in shopping centre explosion in Kashima

Multiple people are confirmed dead as a result of the shopping centre explosion in Kashima town, according to Fuji TV.

Witnesses say the explosion occurred shortly after the earthquake struck Kumamoto province, though its cause is yet to be established.

Fire officials say part of the second floor of the building collapsed due to the explosion, trapping many people inside.

## Quake reminds residents of 2016 earthquake

Residents in Kumamoto said the earthquake reminded them of the 2016 disaster that left 278 people dead across the prefecture and surrounding areas.

Yozo Kai, a priest at a Shinto shrine in the nearby town of Mashiki, said he saw a few stone lanterns fall down.

"I worried if the main hall and the gate would tilt again," he told Kyodo.

Hiroki Shimoda, an official at the Mifune town hall, who saw rooftiles of nearby homes crash down to the ground, said: "The shaking reminded me of the Kumamoto quake (10 years ago) and I was frightened."

## 'Quite a few' feared dead after Japan mall explosion

Police in Japan's Kumamoto prefecture said there are "quite a few" presumed dead following an explosion at an Aeon shopping mall that occurred after the 7.1-magnitude earthquake, broadcaster TBS reported.

Emergency crews are continuing search and rescue operations at the site, where multiple people were reported trapped after part of the building collapsed.

Authorities have not yet released an official death toll or reason for the explosion.

## Aftershock jolts Kumamoto

A magnitude 4.2 aftershock was reported in Kumamoto around 7pm, according to the Japan Meteorological Agency.

## Eyewitness recounts huge explosion at mall

"It was a huge impact," eyewitness Kazuya Tsurunaga said after an explosion ripped through an Aeon shopping mall in Japan's Kumamoto prefecture following a powerful earthquake, trapping multiple people inside.

Tsurunaga, who was cleaning up dishes broken by the quake inside a pub about 300 metres from the mall, told broadcaster TBS he saw a massive cloud of smoke rise into the air.

"It was a huge impact," he said. "A large cloud of smoke billowed up, and because the wind happened to be blowing and the wind direction was towards the shop, the area around us was in a state like volcanic ash was falling."

Emergency services said several people were trapped after the explosion at the Aeon shopping mall.

An Aeon spokesperson said customers and employees had been evacuated immediately after the initial earthquake and that the cause of the subsequent explosion remained unclear.

## Many people trapped in Kumamoto mall after explosion - report

An explosion has been reported at the Aeon Mall Kumamoto in Kashima Town.

The company said customers have been evacuated and it is still assessing the situation.

A part of the second floor of the building collapsed after the explosion, trapping many people inside, fire officials said.

Broadcaster NHK’s helicopter recorded parts of the shopping centre's exterior walls damaged, exposing the interiors.

## Aerial footage shows damage to major expressway during earthquake

Aerial footage broadcast by NHK shows extensive damage to the Kyushu Expressway after a powerful 7.1-magnitude earthquake struck.

Several bridge sections appear to have shifted and separated at expansion joints.

The earthquake has caused significant damage to buildings and roads and also sparked fires.

## Seven fires have broken out in earthquake-hit region

Japan’s disaster management agency has said that at least seven fires have broken out in Kumamoto Prefecture as of 6pm local time.

Most were in Kumamoto City, it said.

## At least 50 people being treated in a town in Kumamoto

A hospital in the town of Hikawa in Kumamoto Prefecture was treating at least 50 people injured in the earthquake, according to public broadcaster NHK.
