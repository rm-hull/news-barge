A motorcyclist was taken to hospital following a serious collision in North Yorkshire.

The accident happened on Main Street on the B1363 near Gilling East at around 11.15am on Thursday July 23, and involved a Black Mercedes-Benz and a Blue BMW Motorcycle.

The rider of the motorcycle was taken to hospital for treatment by ambulance and has since been discharged.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the incident or anyone who has dashcam footage of the area during this time.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance, email: Joshua Barnes on [Joshua.Barnes@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Joshua.Barnes@northyorkshire.police.uk),if you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

"Please quote reference 12260140674 when providing information."
