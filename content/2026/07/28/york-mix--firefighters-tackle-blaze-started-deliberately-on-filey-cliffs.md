A deliberate fire on Filey cliffs was tackled by firefighters last night (Monday).

The Filey crew was called to the incident off **Ravine Road** at**5.45pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Filey responded to a fire on the cliffs measuring 5 by 10 metres.

#### Ravine Road, Filey

“Crews used two hose reel jets to extinguish the fire whilst wearing respiratory protection.

“The cause of this fire was believed deliberate.”
