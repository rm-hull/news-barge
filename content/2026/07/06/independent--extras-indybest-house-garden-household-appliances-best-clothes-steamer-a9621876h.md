Tackling a mountain of laundry can feel like a relentless chore. Even when you’ve used the best laundry detergents or taken to creases with the best steam irons, pressing out stubborn wrinkles on an traditional ironing board remains a time-consuming slog.

That is where the best clothes steamers come in, offering a faster, highly convenient alternative by smoothing out hanging garments in seconds. They are exceptionally gentle on delicate materials, freshen up fabrics between washes, and use intense heat to eliminate odours and bacteria.

When selecting an optimal model, professional fashion stylist Lauren Paxton advises prioritising water capacity: “The more water it can hold, the better the steam power is likely to be, and you’re more likely to get through a whole rail or basket of clothes in one go.”

You also need to look at how you’ll use the steamer. Professional fashion stylist Rebecca Kahane shares: “I also think about how I’ll be using the steamer. For travel, I opt for something lightweight, which usually means a smaller water capacity between refills. For everyday home use, a medium-sized option with a larger water tank is ideal so I can keep it filled and quickly steam pieces before wearing.”

To uncover the best clothes steamers, I rigorously tested a mix of budget and luxury models. Here’s which ones came out on top.
