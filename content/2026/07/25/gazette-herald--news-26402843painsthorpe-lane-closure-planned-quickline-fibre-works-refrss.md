Search the Public Notice PortalEast Riding of Yorkshire Council has announced a temporary road closure for part of Painsthorpe Lane.

The closure is set to take place from Monday, August 10, at 8am until Monday, September 18, at 6pm, to facilitate the installation of ducting and chambers for the new BDUK fibre network by Quickline Communications.

The affected stretch of the road runs between its junction with Roman Road, Painsthorpe, South Wold, Garrowby Hill, Garrowby Street, Barf Lane, Main Street, Town End Hill, and Howe Lane.

The council said that the closure is necessary to ensure the safety of the public during these works.

While the order is valid for 18 months, it is expected that the works will be completed within five days.

Traffic affected by the closure will be redirected via a signed diversion route at the time of closure, passing through Painsthorpe Lane, Roman Road, South Wold, Garrowby Hill, Garrowby Street, Barf Lane, Main Street, Town End Hill, and Howe Lane.

Despite the closure, access will be maintained for residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians.

For more information, the public can contact the council, asking for Highways Customer Care on 01482 393939, with reference CB.
