There’s been a police incident at a York shopping centre this afternoon.

Witnesses have seen a lot of police at the Vangarde Shopping Park, particularly near Hollywood Bowl.

Fans at the LNER Community Stadium for the York Knights game were held in place after the final whistle for their own safety.

They have now been allowed to leave.

People also say a road has been closed by police at Monks Cross.
