Anyone selling on eBay is being urged to look out for an important change landing from next month, as the online marketplace rolls out a major update designed to put listings in front of millions more overseas buyers.

Despite the overhaul, eBay says the process for sellers will remain largely unchanged.

The rollout will happen in phases throughout August 2026, with eligible account holders receiving a notification before they're automatically switched over to the new service.

It will see eBay International Shipping (eIS) will begin replacing the current Global Shipping Programme for eligible listings on eBay.co.uk, giving sellers access to buyers in more than 195 countries - almost double the 105 countries currently covered.


Just like the current Global Shipping Programme, sellers will simply send their sold item to a UK domestic shipping hub. From there, eBay takes care of the international delivery.

The biggest difference is the potential reach. Eligible listings will become visible to buyers in almost 200 countries, opening up significantly more opportunities for international sales.

There could also be savings for overseas customers, with eBay saying international delivery costs through the new service can be up to 30% cheaper.

The company is also removing international selling fees on orders completed through eBay International Shipping, meaning sellers won't pay extra to access overseas buyers.

Another benefit is added protection once an item reaches the domestic hub. After the parcel has been scanned in, sellers will be protected by eBay against claims for lost, damaged or delayed items during the international leg of the journey.

The buyer, rather than the seller, will continue to pay international postage costs and any import charges, meaning there are no additional shipping fees for UK sellers using the service.

Eligible accounts will be moved across automatically from August. Before the change takes place, affected users will receive a separate notification confirming exactly when their account will be enrolled.

Once switched over, all eligible listings will automatically be updated to use eBay International Shipping, allowing sellers to continue selling internationally while potentially reaching millions more customers around the world.
