Don’t count on a Pamela Anderson cameo in the *Baywatch* reboot.

Anderson, 59, rose to fame playing C.J. Parker on the 1990s series*.* A reboot in the works from Fox is slated to star Stephen Amell, Shay Mitchell and Brooks Nader. 

When asked by *Deadline***if she would be making an appearance, Anderson responded: “No one’s reached out to me.”

“I don’t know much about it,” she continued. “I know that they were talking to my sons at some point about producing, but I don’t think they had a meeting of the minds. I’m not sure what happened there, but I wish them well.

Anderson maintained it was a “great job” and she “loved going to work every day on the beach.”

![Pamela Anderson starred on ‘Baywatch’ in the 1990s](https://static.the-independent.com/2025/09/23/15/16/ezgif-458cab2a58ad64.jpeg)

“At the time, it taught me a lot, and it gave me worldwide exposure,” she said. “Everything I’ve done has led me to here, which is a blessing. But I have no plans of going back to *Baywatch.*”

Prodding further, *Deadline* asked if Anderson was curious at all about what had become of her *Baywatch* character. But Anderson said she’s not interested in returning to the past. 

“Now is my time to really play with different characters. I don’t want to just fill my time until the end of the year. I have options, and some things I’m thinking about, including some theater again,” she hinted.

“I want to make really bold choices. I want to surprise people. I want to surprise myself. And so I’m trying to do things I haven’t done in the past.”

*Baywatch* first aired in 1989 and went on to become one of the most-watched television series in the world.

The original series starred David Hasselhoff and featured dozens of stars during its 12-year run, including Anderson, Jeremy Jackson, Michael Newman, Gregory Alan Williams, Nicole Eggert, Carmen Electra, and more.

The show was most recently revived as a movie in 2017, starring Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson, Zac Efron, and Alexandra Daddario.

The *Baywatch* reboot will be co-produced by Fox Entertainment and Fremantle, and will run for 12 episodes during the 2026-2027 broadcast season. *Burn Notice* creator Matt Nix will serve as showrunner.

In a statement to *Variety*, Michael Thorn, President of Fox Television Network, said: “In its first run,* Baywatch* defined an entire era of beach life and elevated lifeguards to an iconic status. Now, with our partners at Fremantle, this television juggernaut is set for a modern-day comeback.

“Together, Fox and Fremantle, along with Matt Nix and original co-creator Greg Bonann, will bring the California dream to a whole new generation of fans with fresh stories, rising stars and all the spectacle that make the *Baywatch* franchise a global sensation.”
