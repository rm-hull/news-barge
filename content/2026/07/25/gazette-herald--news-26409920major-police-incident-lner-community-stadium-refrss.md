- A major police incident took place outside York LNER Community Stadium this afternoon.
- People have been told to stay inside the venue part-way through the second half of the York Knights Vs Huddersfield Giants clash while police dealt with a situation. Supporters were then asked to wait inside following the final whistle for a brief period.
