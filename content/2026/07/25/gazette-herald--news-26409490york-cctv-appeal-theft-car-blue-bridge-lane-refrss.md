Police have issued CCTV footage of a man they want to speak to after a theft from a vehicle in York.

The incident happened in the early hours of July 16 on Blue Bridge Lane in the city when several items were stolen from the victim's car.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing to the public for help in identifying the man.

  **Recommended reads:**

A police spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the male pictured on CCTV, as he may have information that will assist our investigation.

"We would also ask if anyone has any CCTV around this area to please check if you have captured the male also around the same time."

Anyone with information is asked to email PC 1824 Chatland on [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101, quoting reference 12260136620.

Alternatively, people can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
