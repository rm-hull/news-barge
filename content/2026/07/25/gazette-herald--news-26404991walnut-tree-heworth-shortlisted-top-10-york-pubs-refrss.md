A YORK pub said it has "worked incredibly hard over the last 12 months "to make it the heart of the community once again."

The Walnut Tree in Heworth Village has been named among the top 10 in the running for the York Press Best Pub 2026 award.

Sharing the news on its Facebook page, The Walnut Tree spokesperson thanked the community for their support.

The post to announce the pub's inclusion in the shortlist said: "Over the last year, we've worked incredibly hard to get The Walnut Tree back to its best and make it the heart of the community once again.

"None of this would have been possible without you, all guests old and new, and of course our incredible team.

"They're the ones on the front line, working long shifts, late nights, and even through heatwaves.

"When we have our big events, I sometimes take a step back, look around, and think, wow... the beer garden is packed, the lounge is full of diners, and the bar is buzzing with people playing darts, laughing, and enjoying themselves.

"That's what this team has created, and I couldn't be prouder of them."

"I would also like to thank the local community for being patient with us and understanding, it is appreciated."

Tripadvisor scores The Walnut Tree 4.1 out of 5 at the time of writing from 436 reviews.

The pub's nomination Facebook post added that it has raised hundreds of pounds in funds for the York Neonatal Unit.

It has also donated vouchers for food to local charities, hosted family events, and advertised local businesses for free.

The pub has supported sport by sponsoring a football team and the Big Screen at York City LNER Stadium, and by holding youth football presentations and running darts tournaments for adults and children.

Family entertainment has included live wrestling, family fun days, and regular events are advertised on its website such as Friendship Lunch Fridays, where the pub serves a two-course meal with bingo and live entertainment on the last Friday of each month.

  *Voting slips for The York Press Best Pub 2026 are in copies of the newspaper up to and including Saturday, August 5 and must be posted off to the address provided to count.*

  *The winner will be announced shortly afterwards.*
