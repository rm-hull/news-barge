Police have shared an update on their search for a missing woman whose disappearance on Friday afternoon sparked concerns for her welfare.

The 50-year-old at the heart of a police appeal for sightings earlier today, Saturday, July 25, has been found.

  **Recommended reads:**

She had last been seen at about 4pm yesterday, Friday, July 24, near Northallerton, and was thought to possibly be in the Osmotherley and Ellerbeck area, near the A19.

A spokesman for North Yorkshire Police said: "The missing 50-year-old woman we published an appeal for in the early hours of this morning has been found safe and well.

"Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal."

Police had appealed for anyone who saw her to phone on 101 or 999 for immediate sightings.
