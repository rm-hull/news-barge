**How will Sam Smith pubs be run now Humphrey has died?**

FOLLOWING the recent death of Humphrey Smith, owner of the brewery, his son inherits the title of Lord High Brewer of Tadcaster.

Many people will be wondering what changes, if any, he will make to the brewery and its chain of nearly 300 pubs.

If he is less secretive than his father, he might agree to be interviewed by the Press, and explain any changes he has in mind.

Some of Humphrey Smith's policies were good ones, such as the ban on music and televisions in his pubs.

This makes for a civilised pub. But about half of the pubs stand empty because he could not find managers who met his exacting standards.

This may be because potential managers have been reluctant to apply for pubs, knowing that under the old regime they could be turned out of their pubs without warning because of some minor infringement of the rules.

This is no way to run a business, or to win the loyalty of customers.

I hope the new man will feel able to tell the Press how his brewery and pub chain will be run.

  *David Martin,*

  *Rosedale Ave,*

  *Acomb,*

  *York*
