# Commonwealth Games shock as Adam Peaty-Ramsay beaten in 100m breaststroke

- Three-time Olympic champion Adam Ramsay-Peaty secured a bronze medal in the men’s 100m breaststroke at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow.
- Despite being the world record holder and a strong favourite, Ramsay-Peaty was beaten by Sam Williamson of Australia, who took gold, and Filip Nowacki of Jersey, who claimed silver.
- Williamson finished in 59.17s, with Nowacki 0.17s behind, while Ramsay-Peaty recorded a time of 59.65s.
- This marked Jersey's first Commonwealth medal since 1990, with 18-year-old Nowacki expressing his privilege in racing against Ramsay-Peaty.
- The event was significant for Ramsay-Peaty, who described it as a "full circle moment" to reignite his career after a challenging 2023 focused on mental health.
