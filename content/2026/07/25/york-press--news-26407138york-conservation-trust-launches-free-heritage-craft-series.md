## Traditional heritage crafts showcased at free York demonstrations series

Traditional craft skills will be showcased in a summer demonstration series *(Image: York Conservation Trust)*

Traditional craft skills will be showcased in a summer demonstration series open to the public.

The free events, organised by York Conservation Trust, will take place at St Anthony's Gardens in York from 12pm to 3pm between July 24 and August 14.

Visitors will be able to see heritage crafts in action, including joinery, woodcarving, masonry, stained glass, and work with faience and terracotta.

Guy Bowyer, chief executive of York Conservation Trust, said: "Heritage skills are essential to the future of our historic buildings, but many people never get the opportunity to see these remarkable crafts being practised.

"These demonstrations are a chance to meet the talented craftspeople who care for York's built heritage, learn about traditional techniques and discover why these skills remain as important today as they have been for centuries.

"We hope the events will inspire people of all ages to look more closely at the craftsmanship around them and encourage more people to engage with the city's rich heritage."

Additional demonstrations in woodcarving and plastering will take place each week.

Thin Ice Press will also showcase traditional letterpress printmaking.

The events form part of the Trust’s 50th anniversary programme and are free with no booking required.
