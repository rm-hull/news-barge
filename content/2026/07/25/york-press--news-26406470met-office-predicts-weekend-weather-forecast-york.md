## York weather forecast: Warm sunshine on Saturday before cooler Sunday

The Met Office has forecast a sunny Saturday for York, with a cloudier Sunday 

The Met Office has forecast a warm and sunny Saturday for York, with a cooler, cloudier Sunday.

Saturday will start off cool, with temperatures between 15°C and 16°C, and light winds around 6–7 mph.

The morning will see clear skies initially, but clouds will increase as the morning progresses.

The chance of rain is very low, mostly under 10 per cent, briefly rising to around 20–30 per cent mid-morning.

The afternoon will see temperatures steadily climb to a warm 23°C, with clouds breaking to give plenty of sunny intervals.

Rain chances remain very low, less than 10–20 per cent, and winds will strengthen through the afternoon, reaching 18–19 mph.

The evening will remain warm at 23°C, gradually cooling to 16°C by midnight.

  


The skies will be mostly cloudy during the evening before clearing later, leading to a fine, dry night.

Winds will ease steadily from around 19mph to seven mph.

Sunday will be slightly cooler, with more cloud cover.

The morning will see temperatures rise from 14°C to around 20°C, with a 20–40 per cent chance of a light shower, particularly towards midday.

Winds will stay light at 4–8 mph.

The afternoon will see highs of around 21°C, with cloud remaining dominant through early afternoon before sunny intervals develop later in the day.

Shower chances will remain around 30–40 per cent, but any rain is likely to be brief and scattered.

Winds will be light to moderate at 9–11 mph.

The evening will be pleasant, with sunny intervals giving way to partly cloudy skies after sunset.

Temperatures will gradually fall from 20°C to 15°C overnight, and winds will remain light, easing from around 11 mph to 7 mph.
