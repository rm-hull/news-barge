A motorbike could 'face the crusher' after being dumped in North Yorkshire following complaints from local residents about nuisance riding.

The motorbike was found abandoned in Middle Deepdale, in Scarborough, following regular complaints from residents about off-road bikes, e-bikes, and scooters being used in the area.

Eastfield's Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) previously launched Operation Bungalow, to clampdown on anti-social behaviour, noise complaints, criminal damage and collisions or near misses caused by these vehicle users.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said concerns also included dangerous riding and speed.

"Today, (Friday, July 24) officers tried to engage with the rider and their passenger of the motorbike in the picture. Unfortunately, both people didn't want to hang around to speak to officers, and ran away.

"The motorbike was recovered and may have a future appointment with the crusher."

The neighbourhood policing team is urging anyone with information that could assist in maintaining the safety of the area to contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Non-emergency incidents can also be reported via 101 or the North Yorkshire Police website.

"By providing information, or reporting incidents, it will allow your NPT to patrol the right places, at the right times, to combat anti-social behaviour," added the spokesperson.
