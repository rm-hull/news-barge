DRIVERS in York are being warned of multiple road closures in and around York on Monday, July 27, due to essential works.

Tudor Road in Acomb will be closed from 9.30am to 2.30pm on Monday for highway maintenance. The restrictions are expected to remain in place throughout this period.

  **Recommended reads:**

Outgang Lane in Osbaldwick will face closures from 8am on Monday, July 27 until 6pm on Wednesday, July 29, for telecoms works.

The restrictions are set to remain in place during this time.

Moor Lane in Woodthorpe will be closed from 7pm on Monday, July 27, until 6am on Wednesday, July 29 for water mains works.
