# Brazil denies entry to two US officials ‘wanting to influence October presidential election’

- Brazil has reportedly rejected visa applications from two US officials associated with the Trump administration.
- The officials, Riley M. Barnes and Samuel Samson, are reported to have intended to travel to Brazil to cast doubt on the integrity of its electoral system.
- Brazilian officials perceived this US initiative as an attempt to influence the country's forthcoming October presidential election.
- The election will see incumbent Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva contend with Senator Flavio Bolsonaro, an ally of Trump.
- In a related development, Argentine President Javier Milei travelled to Brazil to endorse Senator Bolsonaro's presidential campaign.
