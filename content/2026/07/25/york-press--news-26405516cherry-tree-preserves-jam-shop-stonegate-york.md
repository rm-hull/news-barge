## New shop welcomed by York locals and tourists alike

The outside of the Stonegate shop *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A NEW shop in York city centre has received a warm welcome as locals and tourists alike flock to try something new.

The Cherry Tree, which opened its doors last month at 14 Stonegate, offers a range of preserves, chutneys, jams, and spreads, alongside cheeses and accompaniments.

First founded in 1997, the shop is one of seven owned by the brand across the UK in reaches 250,000 customers each year, with 74 different products on offer.

Master-chutney and preserve maker Gavin has received over 200 awards and was responsible for creating the Supreme Champion product at the Great Taste Award - the highest accolade possible in the ‘food industry’s Oscars’.

In the York shop, the best-selling items have been the cheeses (which were all sold out aside from the hot chilli cheddar) and the hot garlic chutney (which is also the brand’s best seller online). All items are also available to taste.

  Shelves of preserves 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A member of staff said the store had been ‘very busy’ since they opened their doors on June 20.

He said: “It's been nice and plenty of people come in and try different things and we're really happy that people enjoy our preserves. Here in York, the Cherry Bakewell and all the curds have been really popular.”

  A display screen at the front of the shop 

*(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

The Cherry Tree is open at 14 Stonegate every day from 10am to 5pm.
