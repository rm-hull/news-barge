Cairngorms wildfire burns through night into third day

Residents of Nethy Bridge, a Scottish village, have criticised the emergency response to a wildfire in Cairngorms National Park, likening it to "closing the stable door now that the horse has bolted".

A precautionary evacuation order was issued for homes around Nethy Bridge on Friday night as the wildfire, which has been burning for days, intensified due to shifting wind conditions.

One resident, Richard Eccles, described being given three options by police: stay, go to friends or family, or use emergency accommodation, with most residents opting to stay unless the fire significantly nears.

The fire, which started in heathland near Ryvoan Bothy on Wednesday morning, led to the cancellation of a wedding at Castle Roy and earlier closures of properties and businesses in Glenmore Forest Park.

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service has urged people to avoid the area near Aviemore and Glenmore within the Cairngorms National Park and to remain vigilant as firefighting efforts continue.
