# Our Cow Molly: 'We've had to move a footpath on our farm due to the success of our ice cream shop'

## Yorkshire ice cream firm Our Cow Molly has paid to move a footpath because the sheer number of visitors is disturbing neighbours.

The popular dairy farm, in Dungworth, near Sheffield, is a big attraction for visitors enjoying its many ice cream flavours made using milk from its herd of cows. The farm has a shop and cafe.

Sheffield City Council’s planning and highways committee agreed on Tuesday (Jul 21) to divert a footpath away from neighbouring homes off Cliffe Hill.

![Open Farm Sunday at Our Cow Molly](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmQzNzViNzE5LTY5NDctNDBlYS1hZjRkLTRiYTUwNjljYzNjMjo4NmM0MGY1Yi0yZGE0LTRkZjYtOWRjMC1lZDIyOTA2NGExMjI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



A report to the committee said that the footpath became very popular during the Covid pandemic.

It said: “On numerous occasions walkers would linger around the cottages, even stopping to sit on a garden bench owned by one of the residents, to consume ice cream.

“To help the residents, the dairy owner (the applicant for this diversion) provided an additional permissive route to encourage walkers away from the two cottages.

“This has proved a popular alternative, particularly during the summer.”

However, the entrance to the alternative path was extremely muddy, due to the frequent herding of cattle to and from the field.

The report added: “Following discussions with the dairy owner it was agreed that to alleviate this issue the new public path would be constructed from crushed stone and moved away from the cattle entrance/exit.”

There have been no objections to the change.

Councillor Richard Williams said that the change solves two issues very well.
