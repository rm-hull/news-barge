The Met Office has forecast a sunny start in Malton, before a cloudier end to the weekend.

Saturday will begin cool and sunny, with temperatures at 14–15°C and a very low chance of rain.

Light winds of 6–10 mph will accompany the morning sunshine.

By the afternoon, temperatures will rise to a warm 22°C, with plenty of sunny intervals.

The wind will pick up, reaching 19–22 mph, especially in exposed areas, and cloud cover will increase towards the early evening, but remain dry.

The evening will start warm, with temperatures around 21–22°C, gradually cooling to 15°C overnight.

Cloudy conditions will persist for a time before clearer skies return later in the evening.

The chance of rain remains low throughout, and winds will ease from 22 mph to around 7 mph overnight.

Sunday will be cooler and cloudier than Saturday, but it should stay largely dry.

Temperatures will rise from 12°C to 19°C in the morning, with a small chance of a light shower.

The afternoon will see highs of around 20°C, with a 40–50 per cent chance of a passing light shower.

Winds will remain light to moderate at 10–11 mph.

In the evening, the cloud will gradually break, giving some sunny intervals before becoming partly cloudy overnight, and temperatures will ease from 20°C to 15°C.

The chance of showers will decrease through the evening, with dry weather becoming more likely.

Light winds will ease from 11 mph to 7 mph.

Overall, the weekend promises a mix of sunshine and cloud, with temperatures reaching up to 22°C on Saturday.
