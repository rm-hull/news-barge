Fire crews tackled a 'deliberate' fire in woods in Ryedale.

Kirkbymoorside firefighters extinguished the fire in Peaslands Lane, Thornton Le Dale, at 8.19pm last night, Friday, July 24, using two knapsack sprayers.

  **Recommended reads:**

A thermal imaging camera was used to check for any hotspots.

A spokesperson for the Fire & Rescue Service said the initial reports were of a 'deliberate fire in the woods', and the incident was left with the police.
