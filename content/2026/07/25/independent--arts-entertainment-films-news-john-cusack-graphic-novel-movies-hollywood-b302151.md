John Cusack has offered some insight into why he has mostly moved away from major studio films in recent years.

The actor rose to fame in the 1980s with roles in films such as John Hughes’s *Sixteen Candles*(1984) and* Better Off Dead* (1985). 

He went on to enjoy a successful film career with credits including *High Fidelity*,* Being John Malkovich*,* Serendipity*,* Con Air*,* 1408*,* Say Anything*, and* Grosse Pointe Blank. *

More recently, however, Cusack has largely turned away from Hollywood films in favour of smaller projects. He has also focused on his interests beyond acting, such as writing.

Promoting his forthcoming graphic novel *Momo* at Comic-Con this week, Cusack suggested that an increase in film studio intervention was a key reason behind his pivot. 

“[This graphic novel] was just like I wrote it. And I said, ‘I like this story,’” he said, as per *People* magazine. “Nobody edited anything I wrote or that he [Ignacio Noé] drew. So it’s really a piece of work that was just done by two people.”

“It was just me and Ignacio’s story, and that doesn’t happen today,” Cusack said, noting that he appreciated there was “no committee” on the project.

“I didn’t want to negotiate telling a story really,” continued the actor. “When you ask somebody to put like $20 to $30 million into a bank account [for a film budget], they want to have an opinion, and they’re afraid and all that stuff.”

Cusack’s recent comments echo those he gave more than 10 years ago. Speaking to *The Guardian* in 2014, he said: “My friend Joe Roth ran Disney [until 2000]. He made things like*The Rock* and*Con Air* to make shareholders happy, but then he also gave six or seven slots to people he liked. 

“I got to make *High Fidelity* and*Grosse Point Blank*. Spike Lee got to make* Summer of Sam*. Wes Anderson got to make* Rushmore*. I had that memory of film and that’s gone.”

He explained that there was once a “one for you, one for them” system. “Now it’s six for them – with a committee cutting the film who weren’t part of making it – and maybe one for you,” the actor said.

While Cusack does not star in as many high-profile films as he once did, he did appear in two movies last year: *Fog of War* and the Mandarin-language hit film*Detective Chinatown 1900*. In 2024, Cusack appeared in one movie, the war drama* Decoded*, which was preceded by 2022’s* Pursuit*. 

His sister, fellow actor Joan Cusack, has similarily shunned the spotlight in recent years. She recently made her first public appearance in 11 years at the London premiere of *Toy Story 5*, for which she reprised her role as cowgirl Jessie. 

As a writer, Cusack co-wrote the 2016 book *Things That Can and Cannot Be Said* with Arundhati Roy about his conversations with whistleblower Edward Snowden.
