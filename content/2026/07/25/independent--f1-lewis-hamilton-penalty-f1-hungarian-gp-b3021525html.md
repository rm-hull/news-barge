Lando Norris landed the first pole position of his world championship defence after he beat Lewis Hamilton to top spot for the Hungarian Grand Prix by just 0.012 seconds.

Hamilton appeared on course to end a three-year run without taking pole only to be usurped by Norris in the closing moments of a thrilling qualifying session at the Hungaroring.

Hamilton had to settle for second, a place ahead of his team-mate Charles Leclerc, but he still has a strong chance of taking a record-extending ninth win at venue he has so often ruled, and slash Kimi Antonelli’s advantage at the summit of the world championship standings.

Antonelli, who holds a 45-point lead over Hamilton heading into the final round before the sport’s summer break, starts only fourth after Mercedes’ exclusive run of poles at every round so far came to a stuttering end. Antonelli’s team-mate George Russell was even further back in seventh as his title assault threatens to combust.

## What time is the race on Sunday?

The Hungarian Grand Prix starts at **2pm (BST)** on Sunday. 

## NEW: Hamilton receives penalty

As expected, Hamilton has received a three-place grid drop for impeding Oscar Piastri in qualifying.

As a result, he will drop from second to fifth on the grid, with Charles Leclerc bumped up to the front row.

## Oscar Piastri after finishing P5:

"It was just Lewis not looking in his mirrors, the yellow flag wasn't my problem. A shame because it meant I didn't get a lap in either way. Clearly the car's been quick today, I've certainly been chasing it. It's not been easy but clearly there's performance there."

**On changing conditions:** "That's definitely not helped but the wind conditions have been quite different today so it was a bit of a reset in some ways. I feel like I've been chasing it all weekend to an extent, I feel like I need a few practice sessions to get on top of things, which we don't have."
