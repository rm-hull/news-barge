## TV star buys iconic North Yorkshire pub made famous by hit show

Shooting a scene in Heartbeat days. *(Image: The Press)*

A star of the small screen has returned to familiar territory after buying an iconic North Yorkshire pub made famous in one of television's most popular series.

Tricia Penrose, who played barmaid Gina Ward in Heartbeat's Aidensfield Arms for 18 years, has bought The Goathland Hotel – the real-life setting used in ITV's long-running show.

The news marks a new chapter for the York stone pub in the pretty North York Moors village of the same name where so many village goings-on and rural crimes were mulled over.

  Flashback: The Aidensfield Arms, aka The Goathland Hotel. 

*(Image: The Press)*

The Goathland Hotel is popular worldwide for its role as the Aidensfield Arms in the TV series which ran from 1992 to 2010.

Announcing the news on Instagram, Tricia Penrose, whose character Gina Ward married PC Bellamy in the TV series, said: “I have some exciting news to announce.

“I am now the proud owner along with 2 local businessmen of The Goathland Hotel well known as The Aidensfield Arms where I played Gina Ward for 18 years.

“Gina’s going home!! Woohoo!! I will be setting up a YouTube channel to let you know what events will be taking place etc etc.

“I will be over there in my pub next Tuesday 28th from about 11.00. It’s very exciting xxxx.”

  The Aidensfield Arms from Heartbeat. 

*(Image: Submitted)*

The recognisable pub in Goathland, on the North York Moors, has long been a draw for Heartbeat fans and tourists. It previously ran under the same family ownership for more than 41 years before being sold to Penrose and her business partners.

  Heartbeat actress Tricia Penrose buys The Goathland Hotel. 

*(Image: The Press)*

Most of the internal scenes in the pub were filmed there and many of the features seen in Heartbeat can still be seen today. As a result of its fame, The Goathland Hotel welcomes thousands of visitors throughout the year from fans of Heartbeat from across the globe.

Heartbeat, set in the fictional village of Aidensfield and filmed across North Yorkshire, became one of ITV’s most popular dramas. The Aidensfield Arm, as depicted in the show, was a central hub for village life, with Penrose’s character Gina Ward a firm fan favourite.

  The bar in the pub from Heartbeat. 

*(Image: Submitted)*

The actress, now 56, appeared in the series from 1993 until its final episode in 2010, becoming one of its longest-serving cast members.

Since leaving the show, she has worked as a radio presenter on Radio Wirral and appeared on Celebrity Big Brother in 2013. She also released two music singles in the early 2000s.

The purchase caps a notable year for the actress, who married her second husband, Chris Hargreaves, in May.

Her return to the pub marks a full-circle moment for fans of the show.
