# Laura Kuenssberg goes behind the scenes with Andy Burnham in his first week as PM

Prime Minister Andy Burnham has just completed his first week as prime minister, with new policy decisions being made and the much discussed No 10 North taking centre stage.

The BBC's Laura Kuenssberg met Burnham for his first sit-down interview since taking charge and got an exclusive behind the scenes look at the new Manchester office.

Andy Burnham: The Laura Kuenssberg Interview will be available across BBC News on Sunday.
