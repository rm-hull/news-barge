Cricket’s Hundred takes centre stage on Sunday as the men’s and women’s competitions slip into top gear.

Manchester Super Giants, Birmingham Phoenix, Trent Rockets and London Spirit are all in action in the second round of fixtures.

In Formula One, the championship reaches Hungary with Mercedes’ Kimi Antonelli leading the drivers’ championship.

The Commonwealth Games continue in Glasgow and the Tour de France reaches its conclusion on the Champs-Elysees.

Here’s what is happening in the sporting world on Sunday – and where you can catch the action in the UK.

## Giants tackle Phoenix

![Manchester Super Giants welcome Birmingham Phoenix to Old Trafford on Sunday (Adam Davy/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/25/15/a3dece4bcd31e3d44416829fc8cbf707Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1MDY4Njk2-2.85392936.jpg)

**Manchester Super Giants** and**Birmingham Phoenix** go head-to-head at**Old Trafford**, with the women first to enter battle on Sky Sports Cricket at 10.45am and on Sky Sports Main Event at 11am before the men take over on Sky Sports Cricket at 2pm.

BBC Two has **Trent Rockets Women** v**London Spirit Women** at Trent Bridge from 2.15pm, while the men’s clash between the same two franchises features on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Cricket from 5.30pm and BBC Two at 6pm.

## What else is happening on Sunday?

![The 2026 World Matchplay reaches its climax in Blackpool on Sunday (Bradley Collyer/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/25/15/186085bbf432254817dbd707151b82e2Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1MDY4Nzg2-2.84774294.jpg)

It is final day at the **World Matchplay** darts in**Blackpool** with some of the game’s biggest names fighting for the title.

The winner of Saturday’s last-four clashes, pitting world champion **Luke Littler** against**Dirk van Duijvenbode** and number three**Gian van Veen** against**Gerwyn Price**, lock horns at the Winter Gardens on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Action from 7.30pm.

**Antonelli** will attempt to increase his 45-point lead over**Lewis Hamilton** at the**Hungarian Grand Prix** with coverage on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports F1 starting at 1.55pm.

All the action from the **Commonwealth Games** will be screened by TNT Sports 2 from 8.30am, while the**Tour de France** draws to a close in Paris on TNT Sports 1 from 2.30pm with**Tadej Pogacar** set to complete a record-equalling fifth win.

In golf, it is the final day at the **ISPS HANDA Women’s Scottish Open** from 12pm, the**Senior Open Championship** from 4pm and the**3M Open** in Minnesota – where American**Michael Kim** shot a 59 on Friday – from 7pm, all on Sky Sports Golf.

## On this day in history

![Muhammad Ali, right, won the North American belt after his return to boxing on this day in 1971 (PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/25/15/638e292445f97f7f4849af9a132a43e0Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1MDY5MDA3-2.1662801.jpg)

**1971:** Muhammad Ali won his first title since returning to boxing after being stripped of his world heavyweight crown four years earlier for refusing, on religious grounds, to be drafted into the US Army. He beat Jimmy Ellis for the North American belt.

**2021:** Adam Peaty won Great Britain’s first gold medal at the Tokyo Olympics by retaining his title in the men’s 100 metres breaststroke. Tom Daley and Matty Lee later won gold in the men’s synchronised 10 metres platform.

**2022:** England reached the Women’s Euro 2022 final after a 4-0 victory over Sweden at Bramall Lane. They went on to beat Germany in the final.
