# San Francisco 49ers coach Kyle Shanahan involved in car crash

- San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan was involved in a car crash earlier this month, sustaining multiple injuries.
- His injuries include a concussion, a broken nose, several fractured ribs, and hand injuries, though they are not life-threatening.
- The incident will limit his participation as the team's training camp commences, which was announced by the team on Saturday.
- Assistant head coach Chris Foerster, supported by coordinators, will oversee the initial practice sessions.
- Shanahan, highly regarded for his offensive acumen, has led the 49ers to two Super Bowl appearances and two NFC Championship games during his tenure.
