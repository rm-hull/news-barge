# Trump demands CNN anchor smile more in ‘rambling, unhinged’ White House Correspondents’ Dinner speech

- Donald Trump delivered a roughly hour-long speech at the White House Correspondents’ Association dinner, where he largely savaged members of the media.
- He specifically targeted CNN's Kaitlan Collins, demanding she smile more, and branded Jake Tapper "Fake Tapper," while also criticizing *Wall Street Journal* reporters.
- Trump made crude jokes about critics and Democrats, and claimed that the media would be "broke" without him.
- The speech also featured jokes about his White House ballroom project, Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy, Jr., and President Joe Biden's mental fitness.
- Critics described the speech as "rambling, unhinged" and "full of hate."
