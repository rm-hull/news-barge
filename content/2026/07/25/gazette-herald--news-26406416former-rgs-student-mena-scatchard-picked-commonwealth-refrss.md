A former Ripon Grammar School student has been selected to represent Scotland in the 800 metres at this month’s Commonwealth Games.

Hutton Conyers native Mena Scatchard, 23, has been named in the star-studded Team Scotland squad for the Glasgow-based event, thanks to her Scottish family heritage, with three of her four grandparents hailing from north of the border.

The Stanford University graduate, who left Ripon Grammar School (RGS) in 2021, will compete in the 800m heats on July 27, aiming for the semi-finals two days later.

This marks her first senior Commonwealth selection.

Her selection is a significant milestone in her athletic career, which began flourishing while competing for RGS, eventually earning her an athletics scholarship to the United States.

Only last month, Mena graduated from Stanford University, having completed her postgraduate studies after transferring from Princeton University, where she was awarded the Top Women's Athlete Award.

During her time in the US, she continued to enhance her reputation as one of Britain’s leading middle-distance athletes, achieving several standout performances and personal bests.

Mena has represented Team GB in Europe and was ranked No. 2 in Britain in the 1,500m and No. 3 in Europe.

She was also ranked No. 2 in Britain and No. 4 in Europe in the 800m.

Following the Commonwealth Games, Mena hopes to turn professional and is already in discussions with professional athletics teams about the next stage of her career.

At RGS, Mena was a standout sporting talent, excelling in cross country and track events, regularly representing North Yorkshire and England Schools, before securing her scholarship to study and compete in the United States.

Her latest international selection is a proud moment for the RGS community, showcasing how dedication, determination, and hard work can propel young athletes from school competitions to the world stage.

The Games will be broadcast on Channel 5.
