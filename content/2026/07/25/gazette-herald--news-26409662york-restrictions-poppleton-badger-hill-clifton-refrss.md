Essential works could cause possible delays for drivers in and around York as temporary traffic restrictions are put in place.

Low Mill Close in Badger Hill will face a temporary traffic restriction starting on Monday, July 27, at 8.30am.

The restrictions are scheduled to remain in place until Friday, August 14, for water mains works.

Millfield Lane in Poppleton will also be affected, with a temporary traffic restriction also beginning on Monday, July 27, at 8am.

These restrictions are expected to last until Sunday, August 30, for electrical mains works.

  There will also be temporary traffic restriction for Rawcliffe Drive and Rawcliffe Lane, Clifton from 8am on Tuesday, July 28 until 6pm on Thursday, July 30 for highway repairs.
