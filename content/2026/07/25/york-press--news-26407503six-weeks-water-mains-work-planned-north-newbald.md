Major upgrades to the water network will begin this summer, aiming to improve long-term reliability for residents and reduce the risk of supply issues.

Yorkshire Water has announced plans to replace 4.4km of ageing water mains in North Newbald, East Riding, as part of its £8.3 billion investment programme to strengthen infrastructure and improve services for customers.

Work will begin on Monday, July 27, with Yorkshire Water’s contractor OCU Group starting on site, and should take up to six weeks to complete.

Victoria Corbett, project manager at Yorkshire Water, said: "This is part of our largest ever investment programme of £8.3bn to strengthen infrastructure and improve services for our customers.

"Replacing old pipes, prone to bursts and leaks, will lower the risk of issues in the future, which helps us to save water and reduces supply interruptions for our customers.

"We’ve worked closely with local highways to plan our rolling programme of work and keep disruption to a minimum."

A rolling road closure will be in place in Beverley Road from the junction of Stoneknowle Hill to the junction of Bushey Lane until early September.

Further construction will take place at Walkington Heads, between Bushey Lane and Littlewood Farm.
