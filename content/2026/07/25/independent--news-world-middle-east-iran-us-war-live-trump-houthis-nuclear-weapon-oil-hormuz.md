## Experts warn the real pinch from the Iran war is about to strike

## Houthis and Saudi Arabia exchange strikes as conflict spreads

Saudi Arabia has Yemen’s Houthi rebel group has claimed responsibility for an attack on Saudi Arabia on Saturday.
