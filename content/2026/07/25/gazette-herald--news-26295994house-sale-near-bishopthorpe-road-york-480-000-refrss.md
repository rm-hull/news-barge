A “beautiful” home has hit the market in “one of York’s most desirable locations”.

The three-bedroom end terrace house is for sale in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £480,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it combines “period character with contemporary styling”.

“This is a beautifully presented home in one of York's most desirable residential locations, perfectly placed to enjoy the independent lifestyle of Bishopthorpe Road,” a spokesperson for the estate agent said.

  Inside the three-bedroom house for sale in Nunmill Street, York, for £480,000 

*(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside, an entrance hallway leads to a living room with a large bay window and cast iron fireplace.

To the rear, the property opens into an open plan living dining kitchen.

Also on the ground floor is a utility room and a modern shower room.

Outside, the property has a private rear courtyard and on street parking to the front.
