Police are investigating a fire started deliberately in York last night.

Firefighters were called to **Leeman Park** at**9.13pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb’s pump was mobilised to a deliberate fire that had been set in a wooded area by youths.

#### Leeman Park, York

“Crews extinguished the fire using a knapsack sprayer and the incident was passed over to police.”
