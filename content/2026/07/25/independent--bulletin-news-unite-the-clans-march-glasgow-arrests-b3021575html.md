More than a dozen people were arrested in Glasgow after violent clashes erupted between anti-immigration and anti-racism protesters.

Police Scotland confirmed 15 arrests were made for various offences, including possession of an offensive weapon, assault, and breach of the peace.

Weapons recovered at the scene indicated some attendees arrived with an "intention for pre-meditated disorder, violence and intimidation".

Protesters from the anti-immigration group, Unite the Clans, were observed performing Nazi salutes and chanting "send them home", while anti-racism campaigners shouted "Nazi scum".

A significant policing operation was deployed across the city centre, with authorities condemning the behaviour as unacceptable violence and promising further arrests.
