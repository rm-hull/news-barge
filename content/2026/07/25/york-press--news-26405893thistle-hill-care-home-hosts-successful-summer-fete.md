## Knaresborough care home brings community together for summer fete

Entertainment included a musical performance by Karen Clegg *(Image: Barchester Healthcare)*

A care home in Knaresborough recently welcomed neighbours, friends and residents for a summer celebration.

Thistle Hill Care Home hosted a summer fete in its grounds on July 18, bringing together families, staff, residents and members of the community.

Guests enjoyed a barbecue with burgers, sausages, BBQ chicken, and blueberries.

Steph, a resident at Thistle Hill, said: "I thoroughly enjoyed the day surrounded by lots of great people.

"Thankfully the weather held out until we finished.

"Staff did a great job, not just on the day, but organising the event and getting everyone together."

Entertainment included a musical performance by Karen Clegg and stalls from local businesses offering the chance to browse, shop and support the local community.

Pratheesh, general manager at Thistle Hill, said: "Staff at Thistle Hill are dedicated to making sure that the home is a hub of the local community, and this event, along with all their hard work, has definitely paid off.

"It was a resounding success."

Thistle Hill is run by Barchester Healthcare, one of the UK’s largest care providers.

The home offers residential, nursing, and dementia care for 94 residents, from respite care to long-term stays.
