## Take a look inside this £600,000 refurbished bungalow in Haxby

A three-bed detached bungalow in Haxby, York, has been put on the market for £600,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody)*

A detached bungalow in York has been put on the market for £600,000.

The property, located in Ashwood Glade, Haxby, is said to have been comprehensively refurbished and extended by the current owner since 2022 to an exceptional standard.

The three-bedroom, freehold property lies within a quiet cul-de-sac and comes with an extensive rear garden, off-road parking, and a garage.

  Bay-fronted living room with flame-effect gas fire and stone surround 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

The estate agent, Hudson Moody, said the bungalow has more than 1,800 sq ft of well-planned accommodation and is sure to appeal to a wide range of buyers.

In terms of location, the property is said to offer good access to local amenities, including schools, retail parks, and York city centre.

The A64, servicing the motorway network, is also said to be easily accessible.

  Kitchen/dining room with integrated appliances and dining space 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

Inside the property, there's a bay-fronted living room at the front, featuring a real flame-effect gas fire with a stone surround, and a library area with fitted book shelving.

The home boasts an open and spacious kitchen/dining area, fitted with quality units and integrated appliances.

A garden room, which is accessible from the kitchen, has dual-aspect windows and a lantern roof light with a solar-powered blind.

  Family bathroom with separate shower 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

The bungalow has two double bedrooms, with a large office that could be turned into a third bedroom.

There is also a quality family bathroom with a separate shower room, as well as a utility room and a boot room.

Outside, the driveway leads to a garage that opens into a rear store with direct access to the garden.

  Rear garden with lawn, sheds and access to beckside green space 

*(Image: Hudson Moody)*

The enclosed rear garden is mostly lawn, with a potting shed and storage shed.

A brick-built versatile space can be used as a sunroom or garden office, and there is a further lawned area that adjoins a beck and open green space.
