Free events are being held across York designed to help protect bicycles from theft.

Cyclists are being invited to take their bikes along on Sunday, July 26, from 10.30am to 12pm outside the Little Yorkshire Coffee Co, The Knavesmire, York.

A second bike register event will then be held outside the Super Sustainable Centre in Derwenthorpe on Tuesday, July 28, from 10am to noon.

The events will see bikes registered on a national database to help owners recover them if they are stolen.

The event, run by the local Neighbourhood Policing Team, will also provide free bicycle security marking, cycle security advice, and the chance for the public to ask officers about community safety.

The policing team is encouraging all cyclists to take advantage of the free services.
