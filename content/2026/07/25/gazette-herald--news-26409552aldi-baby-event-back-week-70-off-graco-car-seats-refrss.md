Parents looking to stock up on baby essentials without blowing the family budget are in luck, as Aldi's popular Baby & Toddler Event returns to stores this week.

Landing in the middle aisle from Wednesday, July 23, the latest Specialbuy event is packed with everything from baby clothing and books to nursery furniture, toys and feeding essentials, with prices starting from just 79p.

As always with Aldi Specialbuys, once the products are gone, they're gone - meaning shoppers will need to be quick if they want to bag the biggest bargains.


## Graco car seat is £70 cheaper than Boots

The standout deal is Aldi's Graco 360 Car Seat (£89.99), which the supermarket says is £70 cheaper than the same model at Boots, where it sells for £160.

Suitable from birth up to four years old, the seat features a 360-degree rotating base, a five-point safety harness and built-in ventilation to help keep little ones comfortable on long journeys.
