The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra has better camera specs and a higher-capacity battery than its smaller sibling, but its dimensions aren't as well-suited to video streaming, and it costs more.

#### Pros

- Includes a telephoto camera
- High-capacity 5,000mAh battery
- Big screens

#### Cons

- Very expensive
- Large size won't suit everyone

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 is smaller than its Ultra counterpart and lacks a telephoto camera, but it also costs less, has just as much power, and boasts a compact display that may suit some users better.

#### Pros

- Lower price than the Z Fold 8 Ultra
- Top-end power
- More pocket-friendly than the Ultra

#### Cons

- No telephoto camera
- Smaller battery than the Ultra

Samsung has unveiled three new foldable phones — the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8. And while the last of that trio falls into its own category, the former two phones have a lot in common.

Aside from having similar names, they’re both book-style foldables, and they also share plenty of specs. But between very different form factors and differences in their respective camera setups, there's lots to differentiate them, too.

So, below, we’ve compared the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, so you can easily understand how they stack up. We haven't reviewed either model just yet, but we have been hands-on with both, and so have been able to draw some early conclusions as to who they're best suited to.

## Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8: design

![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 behind held in the hand](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/LChiJEynyygvzF5Z5uQ2rM-1200-80.jpg) 

![Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/RXn7AQeoPp8TwKchY3estT-1200-80.jpg) 

![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 behind held in the hand](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/LChiJEynyygvzF5Z5uQ2rM-1280-80.jpg) 

![Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/RXn7AQeoPp8TwKchY3estT-1280-80.jpg) 

In some ways, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 have similar designs — after all, they’re both foldable phones that fold and unfold horizontally like a book. However, their shapes and dimensions differ a lot.

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra looks a lot like the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7, with a tall, narrow design, leading to dimensions of 158.4 x 143.2 x 4.1mm when unfolded, 158.4 x 72.8 x 8.9mm when folded, and a weight of 215g.

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8, on the other hand, is shorter and wider, looking more like a passport than a book, with dimensions of 161.4 x 123.9 x 4.5mm when unfolded, 81.9 x 123.9 x 9.7mm when folded, and a weight of 201g.

 ![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold 8, and Galaxy Z Flip 8](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Gx2pmBDWtf34PsEQVmn2td.jpg) 


Both phones, though, have an IP48 rating, meaning they’re water-resistant to depths of 1.5 meters for up to 30 minutes, and also resistant to dust and other objects greater than 1mm in size.

They also both have aluminum frames, and share Samsung's Flex Titanium technology, meaning their hinges are similarly invisible (you will notice their respective hinges at certain angles, but Samsung has come a long way in this department since the days of the original Galaxy Fold).

As for colors, while the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra is sold in Graphite, Cream, Green Shadow, and Violet Shadow, the Z Fold 8 can be bought in Graphite, Cream, Lavender, and Pistachio shades.

## Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8: display

![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 being held in the hand](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/RtKVyYQ3GWFEQJ5hrZmSPa-1280-80.jpg) 

Now we get to the biggest difference between these two phones: their displays. Or rather, the shape and purpose of those displays.

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra has an 8.0-inch 2256 x 2504 foldable AMOLED screen and a 6.5-inch 1080 x 2520 AMOLED cover screen, both with a 120Hz refresh rate and around 422 pixels per inch.

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 has a 7.6-inch 1828 x 2448 foldable AMOLED screen with around 403 pixels per inch and a 5.5-inch 1248 x 1972 AMOLED cover screen with roughly 428 pixels per inch.

So, both screens on the standard Z Fold 8 are noticeably smaller, but the aspect ratios arguably make a bigger difference, with the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra having an almost square 1.11:1 foldable screen and a narrow 21:9 cover screen.

![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 resting on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/JNwFqoYUVAPWwn26seYn8j-1200-80.jpg) 

![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 resting on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/tetZ8ygV3AeiFwezDGoH9j-1280-80.jpg) 

![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 resting on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/JNwFqoYUVAPWwn26seYn8j-1280-80.jpg) 

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8, on the other hand, has a 4:3 foldable screen, which is ideal for widescreen content, and a 10:16 cover screen, which is a bit more square than a typical phone and can be easily covered with one hand from top to bottom.

The taller, narrower Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra does have size benefits of its own, mind. It's harder to stretch your thumb to the top of this phone's display, but it's more easily covered with one hand from left to right. And when open, the Ultra is better suited to productivity apps that display square-shaped content like spreadsheets.

For video streaming, you'll want to go for the Galaxy Z Fold 8, since most video content is displayed in 16:9 (you'll see smaller black bars on a 4:3 screen), and it's arguably the better-shaped phone for gaming, too.

## Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8: cameras

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra HANDS ON](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/e7XMpm53KKgnFnuP7iFz9h-1200-80.jpg) 

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8jVvSrCbnKVe4EJrbBANFd-1200-80.jpg) 

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra HANDS ON](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/e7XMpm53KKgnFnuP7iFz9h-1280-80.jpg) 

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8jVvSrCbnKVe4EJrbBANFd-1280-80.jpg) 

There are big differences between the cameras on these two phones, with the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra coming out on top. This device has a 200MP f/1.7 wide camera, a 50MP f/1.9 ultra-wide with a 120-degree field of view, and a 10MP f/2.4 telephoto with 3x optical zoom. It also has a 10MP selfie camera on each screen.

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 has one fewer camera and fewer megapixels in its wide one. Specifically, you’re getting a 50MP f/1.8 wide camera, a 50MP f/1.9 ultra-wide with a 120-degree field of view, and two 10MP selfie cameras. So, it lacks the telephoto snapper found on the Ultra.

Both phones, though, are capable of recording video in up to 8K quality at 30fps, or in 4K at up to 120fps.

We haven't had a chance to test both phones' cameras just yet, but we'll update this article with images when we have. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra will, for obvious reasons, be the superior zoom shooter, but there isn't likely to be too much to choose between the phones' 200MP and 50MP wide cameras, since both will default to smaller photo sizes anyway, as is standard practice for modern smartphones.

There’s nothing to choose here, with both the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and the Z Fold 8 having a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chipset and 12GB of RAM — unless you get a 1TB model, in which case you’ll get 16GB of RAM.

This combination of specs will ensure top-end performance regardless of what you're doing on your phone, and in our short time with both devices, they're equally capable gaming phones and productivity powerhouses (notwithstanding their respective display sizes, which makes each better suited to different tasks).

Both the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 also run Android 17 overlaid with One UI 9, and come with the promise of seven major Android upgrades.

## Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8: battery

![Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/uRgKFzh2jBmFJqxeMRHotT-1200-80.jpg) 

![Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra on a table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/uRgKFzh2jBmFJqxeMRHotT-1280-80.jpg) 

Samsung has finally started putting silicon-carbon batteries in its phones, allowing for higher-capacity batteries in the same footprint. As a result, it's managed to pack a 5,000mAh battery into the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra (up from 4,400mAh in the Z Fold 7).

Unsurprisingly, the smaller Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 features a lower-capacity battery (4,800mAh), which should allow the Ultra to last slightly longer — but not by much.

Samsung claims you'll get 27 hours of video playback from the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, while the standard Galaxy Z Fold 8 is rated for 26 hours. In our early anecdotal testing, we've managed to use both phones for multiple days without needing to charge them, so those playback estimates are likely accurate.

Both phones also support up to 45W wired charging and 20W wireless, so you won't be losing out on charging speed by picking one phone over the other.

## Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8: early verdict

 ![The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 on a wooden table](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/m3etkDt4JgVkpNizVZYW7U.jpg) 


The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra has bigger, more productivity-friendly screens than the Galaxy Z Fold 8, along with an extra camera, more megapixels in its wide camera, and a slightly higher-capacity battery. However, it also costs $200 / £200 / AU$300 more, and offers identical power.

Its larger screens also won’t be desirable for everyone — indeed, the Galaxy Z Fold 8's screens are much more video-friendly, and may soon become the norm for foldables — so the only indisputable advantages of the pricier model are its cameras and larger battery (though on the latter, we're talking fine margins).

Whether those extra features are significant enough to justify the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra's higher price will, of course, be subjective, but you might want to wait for our full reviews to see how these two phones compare in practice.
