# Everything you need to know about the Anthony Joshua vs Prenga fight

- Anthony Joshua is scheduled to fight Kristian Prenga in a warm-up bout this evening, ahead of his highly anticipated clash with Tyson Fury.
- The fight against the relatively unknown Albanian, Prenga, will be Joshua's first since he survived a fatal car crash in December.
- The bout takes place on Saturday 25 July at the Jeddah Superdome in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, with main-event ring walks expected around 10.45pm BST.
- This warm-up is deemed necessary after Joshua's previous fight against YouTuber-turned-boxer Jake Paul, which was not considered a sufficient preparation for the Fury showdown.
- The event will be streamed live exclusively on DAZN pay-per-view for £19.99, and the undercard features British stars Hamzah Sheeraz and Josh Kelly defending their world titles.
