# Trump says Iran cannot have nuclear weapons as strikes intensify across the Middle East

- Donald Trump insisted that Iran must not possess a nuclear weapon, despite earlier threats, and indicated a willingness to engage in discussions.
- Trump stated that while Iran is not yet ready for a deal, he is open to listening to their proposals.
- He highlighted the severe danger of nuclear weapons to Washington DC, other US cities, Israel, and the wider Middle East.
- Conflict has intensified across the Middle East, with Yemen's Houthi rebels and Saudi Arabia exchanging overnight strikes.
- The Houthis claimed an attack on Jizan, Saudi Arabia, in response to Saudi strikes on Hodeida, Yemen, and threatened a Red Sea maritime blockade.
