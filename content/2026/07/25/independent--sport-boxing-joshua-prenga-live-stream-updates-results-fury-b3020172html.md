Anthony Joshua will fight Kristian Prenga in Saudi Arabia tonight, in a warm-up for his long-awaited clash with Tyson Fury.

Heavyweight star Joshua takes on Albanian unknown Prenga at Jeddah Superdome, one day after Fury’s bizarre, non-televised warm-up against Mariusz Wach in Thailand – which the “Gypsy King” won via seventh-round TKO.

Joshua, 36, last fought in December, stopping YouTuber-turned-boxer Jake Paul in six rounds, 10 days before surviving a fatal car crash in Nigeria, which claimed the lives of two of his teammates.

Should Joshua win here, he will share a ring with fellow Briton Fury later this year, potentially at Wembley Stadium in November – provided the ring walks begin at 2am GMT, according to Saudi matchmaker Turki Alalshikh.

## AJ's Saudi Arabian record

Tonight’s bout in Jeddah represents Anthony Joshua’s fifth fight in Saudi Arabia - and the Middle East has been a happy hunting ground for AJ.

He’s won three of his four previous bouts in Saudi Arabia, including his rematch with Andy Ruiz in 2019 at the Diriyah Arena.

In the Kingdom Arena in Riyadh AJ defeated Francis Ngannou and Otto Wallin, in 2024 and 2023 respectively.

## Final prelim bout stopped early

We're into the second round of eight between**Oleksandr Khzyniak** and** Lenny Patrach**. Ukranian Khzyniak has caught the eye with a high volume of his punches, and his latest hook has his French rival on the canvas.

Patrach is back to his feet but has work to do - too much, as he is knocked down moments later. The fight is called off - light heavyweight Khzyniak records the second professional win of his career, in front of compatriot **Oleksandr Usyk**.

## The world titles up for grabs this evening

Unfortunately for AJ, there are no world titles on the line during the headlining bout against Kristian Prenga.

However, we will see two world championship fights as part of the chief support acts on the under card.

The penultimate fight of the evening sees **Hamzah Sheeraz**(23-0-1, 19 KOs) make the first defence of his WBO super middleweight belt against German challenger** Simon Zachenhuber**(* 28-1, 19 KOs*). Zachenhuber’s latest fight - a fifth-round stoppage of Armin Ajrulai - came less than 30 days ago.

Sheeraz’s fellow Brit **Josh Kelly**(18-1-1, 9 KOs) has his own defence, taking on unbeaten Irish fighter ** Caoimhin Agyarko**(18-0, 7 KOs) for the IBF version of the super welterweight crown.

## Who is Kristian Prenga?

We’ve known Anthony Joshua’s next opponent for a few months, but Kristian Prenga remains a relative unknown in the heavyweight division.

The 35-year-old boasts a record of 20 wins and one loss in 21 professional fights. Remarkably, all 20 of those wins came by way of knockout - either straight KO or TKO victories.
