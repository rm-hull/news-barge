A national exercise programme has been launched to help people with Parkinson’s get moving and improve their quality of life.

The Parkinson’s Coach, developed by Wilmslow-based personal trainer and exercise coach Katie Thompson, was inspired by her father-in-law’s diagnosis in 2018.

The online platform offers a library of more than 50 pre-recorded exercise videos tailored for people living with Parkinson’s.

Ms Thompson said: "I know that getting started with exercise can feel daunting, particularly if someone is worried about their balance or ability to keep up in a traditional class.

"I wanted to create something simple and easy to follow that gives people the knowledge, support and flexibility to exercise in a way that works for them, whatever their starting point."

Sessions focus on key areas such as balance, strength, mobility, coordination, and agility, as well as stretching and meditation.

The programme includes practical advice on how to safely incorporate exercise into daily routines and address common symptoms.

A seven-day free trial is available to new users.

Ms Thompson added: "Most importantly, I want to help people find a way of moving that feels achievable and that they genuinely enjoy – something they look forward to and can make part of everyday life."

For more information, visit: [https://www.theparkinsonscoach.co.uk/](https://www.theparkinsonscoach.co.uk/)
