Anyone living or working near the site who believes their use or enjoyment of the land could be affected had 21 days from June 7 to submit written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

Representations should clearly state reasons and be sent to Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UA.

Copies must also be sent to the applicant.
