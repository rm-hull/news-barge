A wooden board blew from nearby scaffolding and hit the roof of a ground floor kitchen during high winds in North Yorkshire today.

Firefighters were called to North Marine Road, Scarborough, at 11:53am after the alarm was raised.

Crew members removed the board using triple extension ladder and a saw, and gave advice to the homeowners of the property.
