# Troy Jackson secures Democratic nomination for US Senate in Maine

- Troy Jackson has secured the Democratic nomination for the US Senate in Maine, setting up a crucial contest against long-serving Republican Senator Susan Collins.
- His nomination followed the withdrawal of Graham Platner, who stepped down earlier this month amid an allegation of sexual assault, which he denies.
- Over 600 delegates from across Maine convened in Bangor to select Jackson as the replacement candidate, a process necessitated by Platner's departure.
- Jackson, a progressive figure and former state lawmaker, advocates for policies such as Medicare for All and the dismantling of Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
- The outcome of this race is considered pivotal for control of the Senate, with Democrats viewing Maine as a key opportunity to flip a seat despite Collins' fundraising advantage.
