Firefighters responded to a "deliberate" fire started in a wooded area in York last night, Friday, July 24.

A fire engine from Acomb went to the scene in Leeman Park, York, with a fire service spokesperson saying it had been set light by youths.

The incident occurred at around 9.13pm.

"Crews extinguished the fire using a knapsack sprayer and the incident was passed over to police," said the spokesperson.
