## Winner of best pub in York prize two years ago has the chance to reclaim its crown

The Lord Nelson in Nether Poppleton has been shortlisted by readers of The York Press for Best Pub - a title it held in 2024 *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

THE only pub in a York village has been named as one of the top of its kind in the city.

The Lord Nelson in Main Street, Nether Poppleton, has been shortlisted by readers of The York Press as one of their favourites in an annual Best Pub competition.

The 300-year-old pub took the title after a reader vote in 2024.

As reported by The Press recently, the premises is the subject of an application by Nether Poppleton Parish Council to designate it an Asset of Community Value (ACV) under City of York Council’s scheme.

  The Lord Nelson in Nether Poppleton has been shortlisted by readers of The York Press for Best Pub - a title it held in 2024 

*(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

If approved, this status would formally recognise the pub’s contribution to social wellbeing and give the community the opportunity to bid for it if it is ever put up for sale.

The application stated: "The Lord Nelson is of significant community value, being the only public house serving Nether Poppleton village.

"The pub is a flourishing, welcoming social facility.

"It is family-friendly and is a meeting point for local residents, community groups and sports teams."

Landlord Tom Brown, who took over in 2022, has played a key role in revitalising the pub.

The 2024 crown was put down at the time by Mr Brown in part by efforts to create what he described as a "busy, welcoming, community pubs" environment.

The pub is known for its local ales, Yorkshire Tapas menu, live music nights, and weekly pub quiz.

The Press popped in after work on Friday, July 24 and the pub was busy inside and out, especially in the large beer garden which features a shaded, decked patio at one end.

Food is also served outside in this garden.

Pizza and quiz evenings take place every Wednesday.

The pub recently paid tribute to all the locals who joined it for its screenings of the England Men's team run to the semi-final of the 2026 Fifa World Cup, saying: "We had a cracking laugh and many a debate over several pints.

"Been amazing to share the highs and lows with you all!"

The Lord Nelson has received a substantial 4.5 out of a possible five from 46 Tripadvisor reviewers.

Over on Facebook, reviewers spoke of "absolutely delicious food and drinks, and "the best tapas I've ever had."

The pub made its way into The Campaign for Real Ale's Good Beer Guide for 2025.

  *Voting slips for The York Press Best Pub 2026 are in copies of the newspaper up to and including Saturday, August 1 and must be posted off to the address provided to count.*

  *The winner will be announced shortly afterwards.*
