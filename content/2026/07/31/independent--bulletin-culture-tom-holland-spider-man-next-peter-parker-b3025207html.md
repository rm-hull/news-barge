# Tom Holland opens up on Spider-Man successor plans

- Tom Holland has revealed that plans for his successor as Spider-Man have been in development since 2021's *Spider-Man: No Way Home*.
- Speaking on the *Happy Sad Confused* podcast, Holland said: “It’s laid out. It’s gonna change for sure, but I have a really clear vision to the passing on of the baton, and I think it’s really exciting.”
- Holland is reprising his role as Peter Parker in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, which was released in cinemas on 31 July.
- Directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, the film follows Peter four years after *No Way Home*, living anonymously and investigating a new threat as his powers evolve.
- Holland, who is the longest-serving live-action Spider-Man actor, previously told *Esquire UK* that he would like*Adolescence* star Owen Cooper to take over the role of Peter Parker.
