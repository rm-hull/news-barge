The changes at the National Jamboree, which ends Friday, reflect shifts made by Scouting America since the organization became a target of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth over what he calls “woke” policies (Reuters)

Scouting America eliminated dedicated spaces for LGBTQ+ scouts, girls, and scouts of color at its National Jamboree in West Virginia.

The decision came after Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth threatened to end the military's partnership with the organization unless it scaled back its diversity initiatives.

Some scouts and volunteers, including Eagle Scout John Andrew Segebarth and assistant scoutmaster Layla Grace, expressed disappointment or chose to skip the event, citing concerns about inclusivity.

Gay rights activist James Dale, who obtained the agreement between the Pentagon and Scouting America, accused the Pentagon of exerting constant pressure on the organization.

Scouting America, which rebranded in 2024 to emphasize inclusion, stated it remains committed to serving all youth but declined to explain the removal of the community spaces.
