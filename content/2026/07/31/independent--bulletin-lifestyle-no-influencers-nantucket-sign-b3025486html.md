A Nantucket antiques store, Four Winds Craft Guild, has caused controversy by displaying a sign reading “No Influencers” in its premises.

Content creators, including Paige Lorenze, criticised the sign, accusing the shop of “mocking women” and delegitimizing their professional work.

Store owner John Sylvia explained the sign was a “tongue-in-cheek” joke and a commentary on influencer culture, where people use his shop for photos without making purchases.

Some commentators, such as blogger Erin Gates, supported the store, viewing the sign as a stand against the changing, increasingly crowded nature of Nantucket due to viral online content.

The incident highlights a broader debate about the impact of influencer culture and increased tourism on the Massachusetts island.
