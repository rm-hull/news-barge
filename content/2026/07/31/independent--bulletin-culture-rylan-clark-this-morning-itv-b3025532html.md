ITV's This Morning has announced its roster of guest presenters for the 2026 summer period, as regular hosts Cat Deeley and Ben Shephard take their holidays.

Hosting duties will be split among a variety of familiar faces, including Rylan Clark, Paddy McGuinness, and Josie Gibson.

The stand-in presenters will appear in pairs, with combinations such as Paddy McGuinness alongside Josie Gibson, Andi Peters with Rochelle Humes, and Craig Doyle partnered with Angela Scanlon.

Further pairings include Rylan Clark with Josie Gibson, Sian Welby with Joel Dommett, and Craig Doyle once more with Christine Lampard.

Rylan, who first appeared on the show in 2013, previously took an unofficial six-month absence following controversial remarks about immigration in 2025.
