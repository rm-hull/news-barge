# Pilot arrested after 26kg of MDMA allegedly found in luggage

![Malaysia Airlines said it is conducting an internal review into the matter](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/04/03/8/49/iStock-1353887266.jpeg)

- A Malaysian pilot was arrested in Jakarta for allegedly smuggling 26kg of MDMA, comprising over 70,000 pills, and 4g of methamphetamine.
- The drugs were allegedly found in his luggage after he flew a plane from Kuala Lumpur, with officials saying he was under the influence of meth, MDMA, and cocaine during the flight.
- Officials said the pilot, whose flight number suggested he worked for Malaysia Airlines, is believed to have smuggled the MDMA for a reward worth around 50,000 ringgit (£9,092).
- He has been named a criminal suspect and faces severe penalties, including the death penalty or life imprisonment, under Indonesia's strict drug laws.
- Malaysia Airlines said it does not tolerate misconduct and is conducting an internal review while cooperating with Indonesian authorities.
