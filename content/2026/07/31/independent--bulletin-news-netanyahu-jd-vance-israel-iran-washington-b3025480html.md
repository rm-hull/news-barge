# Why Netanyahu confronted Vance during ‘frank and candid conversation’

- Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confronted US Vice President JD Vance over his criticisms of Israel during a recent state visit to the US.
- The meeting, held at Blair House in Washington, was described by an Israeli official as a 'frank and candid conversation' and by a US official as 'direct'.
- Vance has been a prominent critic, previously stating Israel was 'trying to kill your way out of solving every single national security problem' and suggesting Israeli influence on US policy towards Iran.
- His past criticisms also include hitting back at Israeli politicians for attacking a US deal with Iran and implying connections between Jeffrey Epstein and Israeli intelligence.
- Despite these tensions, Vance recently reiterated the close US-Israel relationship, acknowledging Netanyahu as a 'good partner' and affirming continued cooperation where interests align.
