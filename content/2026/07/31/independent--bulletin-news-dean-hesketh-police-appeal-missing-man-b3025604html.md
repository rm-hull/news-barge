Police launch urgent appeal to find man who 'may have contagious condition'

Preston Police have launched an appeal to locate Dean Hesketh, 51, a missing man who may have a contagious health condition.

CCTV footage released on Thursday, 30 July, shows Mr Hesketh leaving Royal Preston Hospital at 11:16 am on 28 July.

Mr Hesketh is also known by the names Tim, Dean, or Anthony Gledhill.

He is described as 5ft 4in tall, of slim build, with black hair and blue eyes, and was last seen wearing a khaki green and black jacket, black trainers, a Lyle and Scott jumper, and a blue face mask.

Police are increasingly concerned for his welfare and urge anyone who sees him to call 999 immediately, or 101 with information, quoting log 1051 of 28 July.
