North Yorkshire Police has issued an update to its appeal to find a missing teenage girl from York.

Yesterday afternoon (Thursday, July 30), the force asked for the public's help in locating the 14-year-old last seen at 12pm on Wednesday.

In a statement issued around 6.30pm yesterday, a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said the missing teenager had been found safe and well and thanked everyone who shared the appeal details.
