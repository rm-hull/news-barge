# Franco Baresi: Legendary AC Milan and Italy defender dies aged 66

- Former AC Milan and Italy defender Franco Baresi has died at the age of 66, his former club announced on Friday.
- AC Milan expressed profound sadness, writing on X: “His example and integrity will be forever etched into the Club's DNA, just as his iconic number 6 shirt is.”
- Baresi spent his entire 20-year career with the club, winning six Serie A titles and three top European trophies, and captaining the team for 15 seasons.
- He was a member of Italy's 1982 World Cup-winning squad and captained the national team to the 1994 World Cup final.
- Renowned for redefining the sweeper role, Baresi was a cornerstone of Milan's defence, helping perfect the high defensive line and offside trap.
