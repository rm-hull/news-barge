# Chelsea handed huge fine over FA agent rules breach – here’s what we know

- Chelsea have been fined £10m by an independent appeal board.
- The club have also received a suspended two-window transfer ban.
- These sanctions were imposed after Chelsea admitted to breaching Football Association agent rules.
- The Football Association officially announced the penalties on Friday.
