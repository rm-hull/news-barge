Stockings, tree baubles and the best beauty advent calendars might seem worlds away, but with summer now approaching its second half, the season for securing the year’s ’must have’ Christmas countdown is officially here.

Waitlists and pre-orders are dropping across the board while, for a select few brands, their beauty advent calendars are available to buy now. Yankee Candle has kicked off proceedings, with the Lush advent calendar a close second and offering delivery over the next few weeks.

For the London ‘big four’ of department stores (Liberty, Harrods, Selfridges and Harvey Nichols), the early details are plentiful, with Augustinus Bader making a return appearance in multiple advent calendars. Selfridges and Harvey Nichols are keeping their treats a secret for now, but you can rest assured I’m working on it!

Based on my years of experience reviewing and buying advent calendars (the latter for more than 10 years), I can promise you the next few weeks will be rife with launches and key dates to make note of, particularly from the likes of Sephora, Space NK and Boots. Lucky for you, I'll be here every step of the way – and I've even got my hands on two of the biggest launches already to bring you my tried and tested reviews (watch this space). Here's everything I know so far about 2026's best beauty advent calendars, including contents, prices and much, much more.
