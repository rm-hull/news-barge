Hearts begin their 2026-27 William Hill Premiership season with a trip to Aberdeen.

Things are hotting up at the AIG Women’s Open while tennis, rugby league and the Commonwealth Games are also on the agenda.

Here is where you can watch the major sporting events taking place on Saturday.

**Hearts head to Aberdeen**

![Hearts were pipped to the title by Celtic (Andrew Milligan/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/15/0d03941bfa3b0428b4954dc3c68a2bb1Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NTg3MTk1-2.84639334.jpg)

**Hearts** begin their bid to bounce back from their final-day title heartbreak last season when they travel to**Aberdeen**.

The Edinburgh club were minutes away from winning the Premiership at Celtic Park before the late drama kept the title in Glasgow.

The signs are not encouraging so far after new head coach Wouter Vrankcen watched his side beaten 6-0 on aggregate by Sturm Graz in the Champions League qualifiers.

The match is on **Sky Sports Main Event** at 1715, with**Falkirk** and**St Mirren** providing an aperitif on**Sky Sports Football** at 1500.

**Day three drama at Lytham**

![Jeeno Thitikul is in the hunt at Lytham (Peter Byrne/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/15/21d160fc7be41d7aaa6fdef8f61a4b25Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NTg2Nzgy-2.85459302.jpg)

It’s moving day at the **AIG Women’s Open** at Royal Lytham and St Annes with some big names vying for the title.

Thailand’s **Jeeno Thitikul**, the world number two, is bidding for a first major win after an amazing 11 top-10 finishes, while** Ryu Hae-ran** is in contention for a third straight major crown.

Home hopes are likely to land on the shoulders of **Charley Hull** and**Lottie Woad**, with the third round starting at midday on** Sky Sports Golf**.

**What else is happening on Saturday?**

![Welsh Fire’s Phil Salt will be looking for boundaries (Adam Davy/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/15/5b47da237e5bc448c0a05cb49e3ff525Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg1NTg2NzAx-2.85386874.jpg)

The Hundred rumbles on with **Birmingham Phoenix** and**Welsh Fire** men and women locking horns along with**London Spirit** men against**Southern Brave** men, all starting on**Sky Sports Cricket** at 1045.

It’s semi-finals day at the **Washington Open** on**Sky Sports Main Event** from 2000, and in rugby league**Catalans Dragons** take on**Wakefield** on**Sky Sports Action** at 1955.

The **Commonwealth Games** continues on**TNT Sports 2** from 0830, and the racing from**Goodwood** is on**ITV1** and**STV** at 1315.
