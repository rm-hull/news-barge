# Spider-Man: Brand New Day’s post-credit scene features no characters

***Warning – spoilers ahead for***Spider-Man: Brand New Day**

- *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, starring Tom Holland and Zendaya, has been released five years after the previous film.
- The plot follows Peter Parker as he protects New York City from a new threat, with the world having forgotten his existence following Dr Strange's spell.
- The film ends with Parker defeating villain Jean Grey and Ned Leeds suddenly remembering him, despite MJ still having no memory.
- An unusual post-credits scene, featuring no characters, shows Ned's tracking device locating Spider-Man in outer space, teasing future adventures.
- Critics have largely praised the film, with *The Independent* calling it “the best Spider-Man movie in more than 20 years”, though Holland revealed an earlier cut was scrapped.
