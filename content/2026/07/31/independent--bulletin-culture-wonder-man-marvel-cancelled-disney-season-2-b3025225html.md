Disney has cancelled the MarvelseriesWonder Man, despite previously renewing it for a second season.

The show, which received high acclaim and a surprise Emmy nomination for lead star Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, was initially renewed despite lower-than-hoped streaming figures.

However, Disney has now backtracked on its decision, with Variety reporting that the show’s writers have been released so they can pursue other projects.

Fans expressed being “crushed” and “heartbroken” by the unexpected news of the cancellation.

Yahya Abdul-Mateen II acknowledged the cancellation on Instagram, writing: “That’s life, right? Everything will shake out.”
