Betfred is to shut 132 shops, affecting 600 workers (Jordan Pettitt/PA)

Betfred is set to close 132 of its betting shops and cut over 600 jobs from September.

The company attributes these significant reductions to increased taxation, higher employer national insurance contributions, wage inflation, and wider economic uncertainty.

This move will affect more than a tenth of Betfred's retail estate, leaving approximately 1,100 shops across the UK.

Other major betting firms, including William Hill and Paddy Power, have also closed numerous stores recently due to similar economic pressures.

The closures follow an increase in remote gaming duty from 21 per cent to 40 per cent from April 2026, and a new 25 per cent online sports betting duty (excluding horse racing) from 2027.
