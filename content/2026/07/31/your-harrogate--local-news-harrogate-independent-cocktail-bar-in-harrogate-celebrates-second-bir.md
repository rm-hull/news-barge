                                    An independent cocktail bar in Harrogate is celebrating its second birthday this weekend.

Elysian Harrogate will mark the milestone on Saturday (1st August), thanking customers for supporting the business since it first opened its doors in 2024.

The celebration will include live music from Ritch Austin as the venue marks two years in business.

A spokesperson said:

"A big shoutout to all of our customers that have visited and continued to support our independent bar for the last two years… we love you all."


Located on Cheltenham Crescent in Harrogate town centre, Elysian Cocktail Bar specialises in premium cocktails, wines and spirits.

The venue regularly hosts live DJs and music events, and is also available for private parties, cocktail masterclasses and celebrations.
