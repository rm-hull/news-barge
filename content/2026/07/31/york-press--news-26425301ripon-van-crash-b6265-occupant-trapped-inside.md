A VAN has crashed on a main road in a North Yorkshire city.

The county's fire and rescue service said two crews from Ripon were called to the B6265 Boroughbridge Road around 6.50am (Friday, July 31).


A service spokesperson said: "Crews released the person using crew power who was taken to hospital by the ambulance service."
