*House of the Dragon* season 3 doesn't have long left to deliver on the action spectacle front. Indeed, only two more episodes of the*Game of Thrones* prequel's latest installment remain, so we're going to get the endlessly teased Battle of Tumbleton before it ends, right?

Considering *House of the Dragon 3*'s mid-season trailer confirmed we would, that's a given — but will we get to see it in this week's chapter, or is it being held back for the season 3 finale? That, I don't know. What I can tell you, though, is when the high-fantasy show's next entry will be released, so read on to learn more.

## What is the launch time for House of the Dragon season 3 episode 7?

![House of the Dragon | Episode 7 Preview | HBO Max - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/ZkYyuP4PKYw/maxresdefault.jpg) 

Episode 7 of *House of the Dragon*'s latest season will launch at**6pm PT / 9pm ET on Sunday, July 26** in the US and Canada.

Where viewers in other nations are concerned, it'll premiere on Monday, July 27 — and the below release times are when you'll need to tune in to see it as soon as it goes live:

- **US**— Sunday, July 26 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **Canada**— Sunday, July 26 at 6pm PT / 9pm ET
- **UK**— Monday, July 27 at 2am BST
- **India**— Monday, July 27 at 6:30am IST
- **Singapore**— Monday, July 27 at 9am SGT
- **Australia**— Monday, July 27 at 11am AEST
- **New Zealand**— Monday, July 27 at 1pm NZST

## Where can I watch House of the Dragon season 3 episode 7?

Just like every other episode of season 3, this week's entry will air on HBO Max, aka one of the world's best streaming services.

Warner Bros. Discovery's (WBD) super streamer isn't available worldwide, though, so some of you will have to locate it on another streaming platform or TV network. To help you out, the list below reveals where you can catch it in your neck of the woods. You're welcome in advance:

- **US**— HBO and HBO Max
- **Canada**— Crave
- **UK**— HBO Max, Sky Atlantic, and Now TV
- **India**— JioHotstar
- **Singapore**— HBO Max
- **Australia**— HBO Max
- **New Zealand**— HBO Max
