'Stuart Fails to Save the Universe' cast reveals funny BTS stories

HBO Max's new spin-off series, Stuart Fails to Save the Universe, follows Stuart Bloom as he accidentally destroys a multiverse device created by Sheldon Cooper and Leonard Hofstadter.

Stuart, aided by familiar faces Bert, Barry Kripke, and Denise, embarks on a mission across alternate realities to restore order to the universe.

The latest episode features a major cameo from Kaley Cuoco, who played Penny in The Big Bang Theory, but in an entirely new role.

This alternate version of Penny is a brunette resistance leader in an AI-controlled world, a significant departure from her original character.

Co-creator Chuck Lorre said that the show's design allows original Big Bang Theory characters to recur with entirely different backstories in the multiverse.
