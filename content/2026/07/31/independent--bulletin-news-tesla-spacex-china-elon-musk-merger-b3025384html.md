# Could Tesla and SpaceX ever merge? Elon Musk weighs in after explosive report

- A *Wall Street Journal* report indicated that Tesla executives discussed options for separating the company's China business to facilitate a potential merger with SpaceX.
- Elon Musk publicly denied the report on X, stating that such discussions had 'never even come up' and calling it 'absurdly fake news'.
- The alleged discussions were aimed at addressing geopolitical and regulatory hurdles, especially given SpaceX's status as a major U.S. defense contractor and Tesla's significant operations in China.
- Speculation about a merger between Tesla and SpaceX has intensified following SpaceX's recent $75 billion fundraising and Musk's comments about growing overlap between the companies.
- Tesla's Shanghai Gigafactory is its largest manufacturing plant, and China represents the company's second-largest market, making any potential separation of its Chinese operations a complex issue.
