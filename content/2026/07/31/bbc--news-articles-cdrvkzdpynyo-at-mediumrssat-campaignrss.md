![The headline on the front page of the reads: "Spanish border crisis is 'wake-up call' for UK."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/d7d0/live/62f4b000-8d27-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

The crossing of 60,000 migrants into the Spanish North African enclave of Ceuta from Morocco is featured prominently across Saturday's papers. The Daily Express says the crisis should be a "wake-up call" for the UK, quoting shadow Home Office minister Katie Lam's warning to Prime Minister Andy Burnham.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: "Ministers must stop the Spanish border invaders reaching UK."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/810/cpsprodpb/7033/live/6e568180-8d27-11f1-b8ee-9b3c26ad07bb.jpg)

The Daily Mail follows with calls from the Conservatives and Reform UK for ministers to take immediate action to strengthen the UK's borders to prevent the migrants from entering Britain. The PM says the situation is a "cause for concern" and government officials were in contact with their Spanish counterparts.

![The headline on the front page of the FT Weekend reads: "Sanchez condemns 'attack' on Spain.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1056/cpsprodpb/8bb6/live/7dcf13c0-8d27-11f1-b8ee-9b3c26ad07bb.jpg)

Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez has condemned the influx of migrants as an "attack" on the country's "territorial integrity", according to the FT Weekend. Sanchez was speaking from the enclave of Ceuta, which has been a longstanding flashpoint in relations between Spain and Morocco, the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: "Migrant crisis as 60,000 storm Spanish border."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1536/cpsprodpb/d93a/live/377d9640-8d30-11f1-b8ee-9b3c26ad07bb.jpg)

The Independent spotlights fears of the impact of the migrant crossing across Europe, with Italy suspending the EU's border-free Schengen arrangements with Spain.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: "Healey the iron chancellor."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/809/cpsprodpb/4e5b/live/20e58630-8d27-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

The Times leads with an interview with Chancellor John Healey, who says he and the PM have told ministers they must make cuts to their budgets to pay for the PM's spending pledges. Healey - who is described as the "iron chancellor" in the paper's headline - says economic stability is a "fundamental" priority for him and added he could not rule out tax rises in the October's budget.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: "Rapists face ban from early release."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1040/cpsprodpb/aac1/live/588e5580-8d27-11f1-b2ab-0dd01740f9f6.jpg)

Rapists are set to be banned from Labour's prison early release scheme in England and Wales after the PM ordered a review of the plans last week, the Daily Telegraph reports. It adds that Andy Burnham and Justice Secretary Alex Norris were presented with a list of options which included exempting all rapists and child rapists from the early release scheme.

![The headline on the front page of the i Weekend reads: "The Burnham bounce: Labour poll lead grows as Reform and Greens stumble."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/831/cpsprodpb/f122/live/83938480-8d27-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

A poll by the i Weekend shows Labour is now ahead of Reform UK less than a fortnight after Andy Burnham became PM. "The Burnham bounce" is the headline.

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: "Burnham 'risks his first revolt' over North Sea oil."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/1024/cpsprodpb/24bb/live/33e3f4c0-8d30-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

In more Burnham news, the PM has been warned that he could face the first revolt of his premiership after Labour MPs and climate experts criticised his apparent support for renewed drilling in the North Sea, says the Guardian. It comes after Burnham gave a speech saying he would be "pragmatic" about oil and gas and decisions on new drilling would be made "in due course".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: "Go, Gianni, go, go, go."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/d9c3/live/9a3854e0-8d27-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

The Daily Star continues its coverage of Fifa boss Gianni Infantino's move to sell World Cup stakes, reporting that his closest adviser has quit over the row.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: "Never again."](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/802/cpsprodpb/1603/live/94e19a10-8d27-11f1-a7ab-8b30adf0800a.jpg)

Finally, the Daily Mirror reports on families calling for a public inquiry into the funeral director who failed to bury or cremate people and gave bereaved relatives the ashes of strangers. Robert Bush has been jailed for 20 years for defrauding nearly 200 families, with the court saying he "caused anguish and pain on a scale beyond comprehension".

The Daily Telegraph reports, external that Prime Minister Andy Burnham is considering plans to exempt rapists, from the government's early release scheme from prisons in England and Wales. The paper understands that no final decision has been made, and the option is part of a list under review, ahead of an announcement early next week.

In his interview with the Times, external, Chancellor John Healey hinted at benefit cuts when he said that the UK's "inexorably rising welfare bill must be controlled". Healey also echoed the prime minister in declining to commit to spending 3% of GDP on defence by 2030, saying a "target date" will instead be set at the next spending review in 2027.

The Guardian says, external Andy Burnham has been warned he faces the first revolt of his premiership, after indicating he would support fresh drilling in the North Sea. Many Labour MPs are, according to the paper, worried about how that will sit with an electorate alarmed by this summer's soaring temperatures, drought and wildfires. The Guardian quotes the UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres, who warned on Friday that every new fossil fuel project makes "tomorrow's heatwaves more dangerous".

The Financial Times leads, external on the influx of migrants into the Spanish territory of Ceuta, saying right-wing leaders including US President Donald Trump have seized on the incident. The paper notes that the 60,000 people who crossed into the exclave is five times the daily peak set in 2015 when hundreds of thousands of refugees entered Europe via Turkey. Ministers in the UK are under pressure from the Conservatives and Reform UK to strengthen Britain's borders, according to the Daily Mail, external. Nigel Farage is quoted saying: "This is a battle for our civilisation." The Daily Express, external, also leading on the story, cites a Conservative MP, Katie Lam, as describing the crisis as a "wake-up call".

The Daily Mirror reports, external that grieving families are calling for a public inquiry into the actions of a funeral director in Hull, who mistreated the remains of dozens of people. Robert Bush was jailed for 20 years. The paper's headline is, "Never Again".
