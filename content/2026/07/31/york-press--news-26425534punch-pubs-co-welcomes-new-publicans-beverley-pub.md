A historic East Yorkshire pub has welcomed new publicans with big plans.

The Royal Standard, located in Beverley’s Georgian Quarter, is now in the hands of Paul and Deborah Perkins, who bring 15 years of experience to the role.

The couple has signed a long-term agreement with pub company Punch and is keen to become a lasting part of the community.

Deborah Perkins said: "I am originally from South Africa.

"I have lived in the UK for 20 years and am now looking to put down some roots.

"We’re committed to the pub and want this to be a place people can relax, socialise and enjoy their time."

The new publicans aim to "elevate" the pub’s profile with plans for regular events.

Paul Perkins said: "We are working hard to elevate the pub amongst the community and restore an element of pride into what we believe is a fantastic pub.

"I understand there has been a bit of upheaval previously but we’re here to stay."

Plans include quiz nights, bingo, disco, karaoke, and the formation of a darts team.

The Royal Standard is a Grade II-listed building at North Bar Within featuring original beams, exposed brick, and a central bar that divides a lounge and cosy bar area.

It also boasts an outdoor courtyard, expected to be a popular spot during the summer.

Paul Hutchinson, operations manager at Punch Pubs & Co, said: "Paul and Deborah are the perfect Publicans to take the reins at the Royal Standard.

"This is a fantastic pub, and I’m excited to be supporting them on their journey and look forward to witnessing their success."

Updates and event details can be found on the Royal Standard’s social media pages.
