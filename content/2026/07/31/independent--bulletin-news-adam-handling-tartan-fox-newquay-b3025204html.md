Pub's chicken coop destroyed by children while parents eat lunch

Michelin-starred chef Adam Handling reported that guests at his Tartan Fox pub near Newquay, Cornwall, allowed their dogs to attack chickens and their children to smash fertilised eggs.

The incident, which occurred during last week's Sunday lunch service, resulted in the deaths of several chickens and the destruction of about 30 to 40 fertilised eggs.

Handling, a former Masterchef: The Professionals finalist, expressed his disappointment and hurt in an Instagram video, describing the guests as “evil” and the children as “selfish little brats”.

He criticised the parents for permitting such behaviour, saying that it “says more about the parents than the child”.

In response to the damage, the pub is implementing new security measures, including installing cameras and motion sensors in the chicken coop, and adding more signage.
