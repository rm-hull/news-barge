Emergency services are at crash in Ripon this morning (Friday).

They were called to the incident on **Boroughbridge Road** at**6.48am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Two crews from Ripon are responding to reports of a single vehicle road traffic collision, where an occupant is thought to be trapped in a van.

#### Boroughbridge Road, Ripon

“This incident is ongoing.”
