Rob Key, the ECB’s managing director of men’s cricket, confirmed Joe Root's return as England Test captain, alongside the appointment of Stephen Fleming as the new head coach on Thursday.

These leadership changes follow the retirement of Ben Stokes and the departure of Brendon McCullum from their respective roles.

Harry Brook, who served as vice-captain and leads England's white-ball teams, was overlooked for the Test captaincy, with Key stating on Friday that it was “too soon” for him to take on the role.

Key explained that he and Fleming agreed Root was the best choice, highlighting his extensive experience having previously led England in a record 65 Tests, and the opportunity for him to work with Fleming.

While acknowledging Root's past “unhealthy relationship” with the captaincy, Key believes this is the right time for him, and views Brook as a long-term candidate who needs further development before assuming the Test captaincy.
