The F-35B is one of several versions of the advanced stealth fighter jet that is flown by the Marine Corps, Navy and Air Force (Luke Johnson /The San Diego Unio)

The pilot was rushed to a hospital and suffered non-life-threatening injuries, according to the Marine Corps, which classified the crash as a "Class A mishap."

The advanced F-35B jet, valued at approximately $109 million, is a variant designed for short takeoffs and vertical landings, utilized by the Marine Corps, Navy, and Air Force.

A witness described seeing the aircraft fly over Interstate 15 with its cockpit open before crashing in a "big fireball" and observed a parachute carrying the ejected pilot.

The crash occurred near the flight line of Marine Corps Air Station Miramar, a base historically known as "Fightertown USA" for previously hosting the Navy fighter pilot training school.
