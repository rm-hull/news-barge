North West's debut headline tour, the Kimokawaii Tour, has been cancelled just days before its scheduled start.

The 13-year-old rapper, daughter of Kim Kardashian and Kanye West, was due to perform 14 dates across North America to promote her debut EP, N0rth4evr, alongside Molly Santana.

West confirmed the cancellation in a statement, expressing disappointment but promising “something special” for fans.

The tour, which was set to begin on 5 August in Dallas, Texas, had already seen some dates listed as cancelled on Ticketmaster, and its official website has since been taken down.

North’s EP, released on 1 May, received positive reviews for its exploration of growing up as a celebrity child, and she performed solo at Chicago's Summer Smash Festival in June.
