A former village swingers club known as “Chunky Muffins” is for sale and could be yours - if you have a spare £1.1m to splash.

The “unique” property in Boston, Lincolnshire, was put up for sale just nine days ago, and has already attracted eight times more than the expected views for a popular listing per day.

Pictures in the listing offer a glimpse inside the club, revealing a number of play rooms, complete with a pole, a St Andrew’s cross, and a spanking bench, as well as beds fitted with restraints and plastic sheeting.

Speaking to *The Independent* Shelby Ebbitt, sales manager at William H Brown, said people have been “intrigued” by the listing, which has racked up hundreds of views a day online.

She added the club is a “well-established” business that had come to market because its owners are retiring, saying its a “great investment for someone”.

![The Chunky Muffins swingers club is up for sale](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/10/50/dadfaf5c644885eb636a23e447dccfe8.jpeg)

But while the property has been popular among online viewers, she said its high price point and a lull during the school holidays means interest has been limited so far.

“I don’t think people will want to view it with children,” she said.

The property also boasts a heated outdoor swimming pool, jacuzzi, and a bar room, as well as a number of commercial outbuildings, offices and garages.

![The spacious property boasts three bedrooms, a swimming pool, and a number of commercial outbuildings](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/10/43/4476c2fc1ee7c152a4e4ce8a810536ff.jpeg)

Advertised as a three-bedroom “character property” on Rightmove, the listing boasts a “truly unique” combination of “residential accommodation, extensive commercial facilities and impressive leisure amenities”.

Chunky Muffins describes itself as “Lincolnshire’s premier swingers venue”, and runs party nights every Friday and Saturday.

The listing added: “Occupying a substantial plot behind double electric gates, this truly unique detached three-bedroom cottage offers an exceptional combination of residential accommodation, extensive commercial facilities and impressive leisure amenities.

![The pictures show a number of beds with plastic sheeting](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/31/10/55/65b39b05e0f4c5238f818ad02961b83f.jpeg)

“The cottage comprises three double bedrooms, a family bathroom, lounge, kitchen with pantry and store, together with a separate WC. The commercial element includes offices, a triple garage, single garage and extensive outbuildings, currently arranged to provide a versatile entertainment venue.

“Facilities include a welcoming reception hall, spacious bar and entertainment room, games room, lounge, catering kitchen, sauna, changing facilities, shower room with walk-in shower, WCs and stores. The first floor provides further flexible rooms, a lounge area, shower room, additional storage and WCs, offering excellent versatility for a variety of uses (subject to any necessary consents).

“Outside, the property continues to impress with extensive parking suitable for commercial vehicles, a heated outdoor swimming pool, jacuzzi and a beautifully handcrafted timber bar beneath a pitched tiled roof, creating an outstanding space for entertaining. A rare opportunity to acquire a property offering a unique blend of home, business and lifestyle potential.”
