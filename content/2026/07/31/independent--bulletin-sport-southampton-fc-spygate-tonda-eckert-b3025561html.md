Southampton manager Tonda Eckert is currently awaiting a verdict from the Football Association regarding potential further disciplinary action, as the 'Spygate' scandal continues to impact the club's pre-season.

The Saints were previously expelled from last season’s Championship play-off final and handed a four-point deduction for the upcoming campaign after admitting to three spying charges.

The FA initiated its investigation on 21 May and subsequently charged Eckert last Wednesday with three breaches of Rule E3.1 for "misconduct" between December and May, with his response due by Tuesday.

Eckert, who retained his position despite the controversy, acknowledged the ongoing investigation is a concern but stated he is focusing on pre-season preparations, including training and the transfer window.

The FA's decision is anticipated in August, potentially disrupting Southampton's build-up to the new season, which commences next weekend with a Carabao Cup fixture, although bookmakers still rate them as fifth favourites for the Championship.
