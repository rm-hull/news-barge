The Southport killer Axel Rudakubana serving life in prison for the murders of three girls at a Taylor Swift-themed dance event in 2024 has been charged with multiple assaults on prison staff, police have said.

On 29 July 2024, Rudakubana, then 17, launched a frenzied knife attack in Southport, north-west England, killing Bebe King, six, Elsie Dot Stancombe, seven, and Alice Dasilva Aguiar, nine.

Rudakubana also wounded 10 others in the attack, which was followed by days of rioting across the country. He was jailed for a minimum of 52 years after he admitted the killings shortly before trial last year.

![Left to right: headshots of Elsie Dot Stancombe, Bebe King,  Alice da Silva Aguiar smiling](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/8b75c468fdca264e375401c254a7da644c8d830a/0_0_3000_1210/master/3000.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Rudakubana, now 19, has been charged with attempted grievous bodily harm relating to an incident in May 2025 and assault on an emergency worker two days earlier, the Metropolitan police said on Friday.

He is also charged with possession of an offensive weapon in prison and assault on an emergency worker and will appear at Westminster magistrates court on 11 September.

Rudakubana was recently moved from prison to the Broadmoor top security psychiatric hospital after it was concluded he could not be managed safely in a prison environment. The families of the three girls described the move as an attempt “to manipulate the system”.
