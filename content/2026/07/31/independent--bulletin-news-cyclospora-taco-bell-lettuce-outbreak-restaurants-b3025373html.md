Restaurants heavily reliant on lettuce are experiencing a dual challenge from a nationwide Cyclospora outbreak and significantly rising prices.

The Cyclospora outbreak, which started in late June, was initially connected by federal health officials to shredded iceberg lettuce served at Taco Bell in several states.

Taco Bell voluntarily removed the implicated lettuce from its U.S. restaurants, resulting in a 21% decrease in customer visits by late July.

Other salad-focused restaurant chains, including Chopt and Sweetgreen, have also reported declines in customer traffic despite not being directly linked to the outbreak.

These food safety concerns are compounded by months of increasing lettuce prices, attributed to factors such as unusually hot weather impacting crops and higher fuel costs for refrigerated transportation.
