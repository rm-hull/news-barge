Nancy Guthrie ransom notes released by Arizona police

Arizona authorities released ransom notes concerning the disappearance of Nancy Guthrie, 84-year-old mother of Today show star Savannah Guthrie, who vanished six months ago from her Tucson home.

The first note, addressed to Savannah, demanded $4 million in Bitcoin for Nancy's safe return, threatening her death if the ransom was not paid by a specific deadline.

A subsequent note claimed Nancy died shortly after being abducted, possibly due to heart-related issues, and expressed regret, stating she was buried “in nature.”

Nancy had a pacemaker, and her family had previously voiced concerns about her fragile health and need for medication.

Authorities hope the release of these notes will generate new leads, while Savannah continues to appeal to the kidnappers for information on her mother's whereabouts.
