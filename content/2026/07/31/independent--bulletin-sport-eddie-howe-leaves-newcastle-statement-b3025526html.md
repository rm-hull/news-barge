Eddie Howe has announced his decision to step down as head coach of Newcastle United, citing a need for personal reflection, to recharge, and take a break after nearly five years at the club.

The timing of Howe's departure, just weeks before the new Premier League season and two months after the previous one concluded, was unexpected.

During his tenure, which began in 2021, Howe led Newcastle to Champions League football and secured the 2025 Carabao Cup, marking the club's first domestic trophy in 70 years.

In an emotional statement, Howe expressed profound gratitude to the club's ownership, staff, players, and particularly the supporters, stating his affinity and love for Newcastle will always remain, as he described managing the club as the “privilege of my lifetime”.

Newcastle United confirmed Howe's decision, thanked him for his “extraordinary contribution”, and announced that the process to recruit a new head coach is at an advanced stage, with Matthias Jaissle widely reported as his likely successor.
