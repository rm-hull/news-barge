A YORK sports centre was forced to close its swimming pools after a burst water pipe.

York Sport Village in Lakeside Way, Heslington said it had to close its main and small pool today (July 31) after a burst main had affected its hot water supply.

A spokesperson said: “As pool temperatures will begin to drop, it will no longer be safe for customers to use the pools.”

  


All swimming lessons have been cancelled today, along with the centre’s café.

Users have been warned that the showers in the changing rooms will also be cold, due to the lack of hot water.

The centre said it was hopeful of the hot water supply being back up and running this afternoon but confirmed this hadn’t been restored at 4.30pm.

A spokesperson said it is unknown whether the pools will be reopened this weekend.

They added: “We will provide a further update as soon as we have more information.

“We sincerely apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience and understanding.”
