Suffolk firefighters making 'good progress' as wildfire breaks out in Dunwich Heath

A helicopter has been deployed to help tackle an "unpredictable" wildfire in Suffolk, while firefighters respond to another blaze at a nature reserve in North Essex.

The Suffolk wildfire at Dunwich Heath has spread to cover an area the size of 210 football pitches, leading to a major incident declaration and the evacuation of around 200 coastal properties and caravans.

The Sizewell B nuclear power plant, a few miles from the Suffolk blaze, is on alert, though nuclear chiefs state there is currently no risk to the facility.

Around 30 crew are battling a fire at Middlewick Ranges nature reserve in Colchester, Essex, amidst tinderbox conditions and drought across England and Wales.

Prime minister Andy Burnham has pledged all necessary resources for firefighters in Suffolk, while Green Party leader Zack Polanski called for an emergency Cobra meeting.
