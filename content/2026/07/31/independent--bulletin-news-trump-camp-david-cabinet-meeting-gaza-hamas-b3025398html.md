# Trump convenes his Cabinet at rarely-visited Camp David country residence

- President Donald Trump is holding a Cabinet meeting at Camp David on Friday as the ongoing conflict with Iran and rising gasoline prices threaten Republican prospects in the upcoming November midterm elections.
- Unlike some past presidents, Trump has largely stayed away from the mountaintop presidential redoubt in western Maryland, preferring to spend time at his golf resorts when not at the White House. This will be his third trip to Camp David in his second term.
- The Cabinet meeting, the 13th of Trump's second term, is expected to heavily focus on foreign policy, particularly the five-month-old conflict with Iran, which was initially projected to last only a few weeks.
- The war has led to a significant increase in gasoline prices and has negatively impacted Trump's public standing, causing concern among Republicans ahead of the midterms. A recent Reuters-Ipsos poll indicates that only one in three Americans support the war, marking the lowest approval rating since the conflict began.
- Following the Cabinet meeting, Trump is scheduled to spend the weekend at his golf club in Bedminster, New Jersey.
