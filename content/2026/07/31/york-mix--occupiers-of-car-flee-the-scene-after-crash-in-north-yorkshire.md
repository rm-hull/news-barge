Emergency services were called to a crash early this morning (Friday).

It was on **Fox Lane, Chapel Haddlesey**, south of Selby, at** 12.26am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Selby responded to reports of a single vehicle road traffic collision.

**Fox Lane, Chapel Haddlesey**

“Crews isolated the high voltage and 12v supply to the vehicle.

“The occupiers were out on arrival and had left the scene. Crews left the incident in the hands of police.”
