Harrogate Community Festival will take place at Valley Gardens, Harrogate, on Tuesday, 11 August, 2026, from 10am to 2pm.

A free family-friendly event organised by Safer North Yorkshire, it will bring together local organisations, emergency services, charities and community groups.

There will be opportunities to meet representatives from organisations including North Yorkshire Council, North Yorkshire Police, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, Yorkshire Air Ambulance, North Yorkshire Sport, Harrogate Town AFC, IDAS, Crimestoppers, Ivison Trust, North Yorkshire Youth, NY RISE and others.

It will feature a range of information stands, offering advice, guidance and details about local services and support available to residents.

There will also be interactive activities and demonstrations designed to engage visitors of all ages as well as opportunities for families to learn more about the services and organisations that help keep communities safe, healthy and supported.

The festival aims to strengthen community connections and promote safety, wellbeing and healthy lifestyles during the summer holidays.
