President Donald Trump has done a U-turn and will now rally in Kansas and Iowa this weekend after Republican officials previously pulled the plug on the in-person events.

The reversal comes after the president lashed out at a reporter who asked if the decision related to his health.

“You know what the reason is? I don’t feel I have to go to Kansas,” Trump snapped at the *Reuters* reporter on Thursday. “I feel perfect. You are disgusting.”

“Put yourself in shape. You look like hell,” Trump fumed.

Trump was due to hold a series of telerallies in 19 states before Tuesday’s midterm elections. The Iowa and Kansas rallies, taking place Sunday, have been rearranged to accommodate the telerallies added to Trump’s schedule, a source familiar with the plans told *The Independent.*

“Rallies are BACK ON!” the source said. “No one works harder, fights harder, or loves the American people more than President Donald J. Trump!”

The president is also scheduled to appear in person at a rally in Daytona Beach, Florida, on Friday night.

## Rallies back on as Republicans are 'feeling bullish about both states'

Trump and the Republicans are said to be “feeling bullish” about their prospects in Kansas and Iowa, reportedly one of the reasons behind the U-turn, according to *Politico*.

But polls show the Senate races in each state are close.

“Back by popular demand. Feeling bullish about both states, but it’s about giving the people what they want,” a person familiar with the plans told the outlet.

In Kansas, Democrat Adam Hamilton has a narrow lead over Republican Roger Marshall, according to Emerson College Polling.

In Iowa, Republican Ashley Hinson is ahead of Democrat Josh Turek by two points, according to the *NYT* poll tracker.

## Pundits: 'Trump didn't like the optics of canceling'

Pundits have been weighing in on the significance of Trump’s exchange with the reporter who riled the president on Thursday.

## President will take part in back-to-back rallies on Sunday

President Donald Trump will take part in back-to-back rallies in Kansas and Iowa on Sunday after holding telerallies on Saturday.

Both are home to battleground Senate races that will help determine whether Republicans hold onto their majority in the chamber in the midterm elections.

Trump has already traveled to both states this month to boost GOP Sen. Roger Marshall in Kansas and Republican Iowa Senate candidate Ashley Hinson, who currently serves in the House.

Those stops were among the 25 rallies that the president has headlined so far in October.

## New: Trump WILL now rally in Kansas and Iowa in person

President Donald Trump has reversed course and will now hold midterm rallies in Kansas and Iowa this weekend after he was called out by a reporter for canceling them.

Before the U-turn, the rallies in Kansas and Iowa were pulled at the eleventh hour by Republican officials who announced the events would be replaced with telerallies.

Now the rallies are back on, sources told CNN and the *Associated Press,* citing a White House official. It follows pressure from a reporter who asked Trump if the cancelations had anything to do with his health.
