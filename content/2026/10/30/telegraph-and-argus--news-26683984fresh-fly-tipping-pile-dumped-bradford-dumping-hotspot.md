Residents have voiced their anger after yet more waste was dumped at a persistent fly-tipping hotspot, marking the second incident reported at this location in Lidget Green this week.

The latest pile of rubbish, believed to include flooring, plasterboard, wall materials and other construction debris, has been left on the public footpath near the junction of Clayton Road and Beckside Road.

The new dumping comes just days after concerns were raised about a separate pile of timber, pallets and wooden boards left at the same spot.

## Resident says community has had enough

A local dog walker, who first alerted the Telegraph & Argus to the problem, said frustration within the community is growing.

They said: "As a community we will fight back. Enough is enough. We will not let this slip.

"We have suffered enough for the past several years. We are fed up.

"We will fight back and will get our community back."

The resident said people living nearby were tired of repeatedly seeing waste dumped in the area and wanted stronger action to be taken against those responsible.

## Concerns over impact on pedestrians

The resident also raised concerns about the effect the fly-tipping is having on people using the busy route.

They said: "Days are getting short. This is outside a bus stop. Look at the impact this is having.

"The smell is attracting mice and rats. I use the bus route regularly. I feel for people getting off."

## Calls for action

The latest incident has renewed calls for action to prevent further dumping at the site.

Residents hope those responsible can be identified and measures put in place to stop the location becoming a repeated target for fly-tippers.

Bradford Council has been contacted for comment and has been asked whether the latest incident has been reported, whether an investigation is under way and what action may be taken.
