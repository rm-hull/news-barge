A long-standing Bradford restaurant that says it “never takes customer loyalty for granted” has been shortlisted for a major award.

Kiplings Restaurant on Harrogate Road, Greengates, is one of ten finalists in the Telegraph & Argus Best Curry House 2026 competition.

The family-run Indian restaurant has served the city since 1994, crediting its success to strong customer relationships and consistent hospitality.

## 'More than serving good food'

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21646305.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An aerial picture of Kiplings Restaurant at Greengates with a stunning sky in the background

Mohsan Mahmood, front of house at Kiplings, said: "Kiplings was established in 1994 with a vision of bringing authentic Indian cuisine, genuine hospitality and a memorable dining experience to the local community.

"From the very beginning, it was about more than serving good food.

"It was about creating a place where people could come together, celebrate life’s special moments and feel welcomed as part of the family."

## Curry house treasures legacy

Mr Mahmood said the restaurant’s focus on building long-term relationships has led to generations of families returning over the years.

He said: "Customers who visited us in our early years now return with their children and grandchildren, and that is a legacy we treasure.

"For us, longevity is not simply about how long a restaurant has been open.

"It is about earning people’s trust and giving them reasons to keep coming back."

## Who runs the kitchen?

The restaurant is led by head chef Tariq Mahmood, whose culinary experience includes cooking for prestigious events involving the House of Lords and more than 200 MPs.

Mr Mahmood said: “Our kitchen is led by my father.

"His knowledge, passion and dedication to his craft are a huge part of our identity.

“But a great restaurant is about much more than what comes out of the kitchen.

“At the front of house, Mohsan and Ehsan play a vital role in creating the warm, personal atmosphere our customers know us for.

"We believe that hospitality should feel genuine, never rehearsed.

"Whether someone is visiting for the first time or has been coming to us for 30 years, we want them to receive the same welcome, attention and care.

"Our greatest achievement is not simply the number of years we have been open, but the trust and loyalty we have earned along the way.

“We never take loyalty for granted.

"In a competitive industry where customers have so many choices, the fact that people continue to choose Kiplings and recommend us to their friends and families means everything to us."

## What it means to be nominated

Readers can vote for their favourite finalist using voting slips in the newspaper until November 7, with the winner receiving a special feature in the Telegraph & Argus.

Mr Mahmood called the nomination a proud moment for his family and the team.

He said: "Being named among the top 10 finalists for the T&A’s Best Curry House 2026 is an incredibly proud moment for our family and the entire Kiplings team.

“After more than 35 years in business, recognition of this kind means a great deal.

"Whatever the final result, this is a moment to celebrate the family, the team and the community that have supported us since 1994."
