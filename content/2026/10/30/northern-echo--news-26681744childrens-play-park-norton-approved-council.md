A new play area is coming to the North East, with fresh equipment planned for a patch of open green space.

Stockton Borough Council is backing plans to turn a 50-square-metre patch in Norton into a children’s play area.

New play equipment will be installed alongside safety surfacing, although the application does not reveal exactly what children can expect to find when the play area opens.

The site is part of what the council calls a “large area of amenity open green space”, and the play area will occupy only a small section.

With its village green and open spaces, Norton already offers plenty of opportunities to get outdoors, and the new play area will add another spot for local families to enjoy.

The playpark is not expected to affect any trees or hedges on the site, has a biodiversity plan in place to protect nature and animals in the area, and is not in an area considered at risk of flooding.

The wider layout will remain largely unchanged, with no alterations to vehicle or pedestrian access, roads, parking or public rights of way.

The application was submitted earlier this year and has now been approved, clearing the way for the equipment and safety surfacing to be installed.

Children in Norton will soon have another outdoor space to play, make friends and burn off some energy, with families keen to see what the new play park will bring.
