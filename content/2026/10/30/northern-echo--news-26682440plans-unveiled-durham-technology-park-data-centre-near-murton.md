Plans have been revealed for a major new data centre in County Durham.

Developer Tract has unveiled proposals for Durham Technology Park between South Hetton, Murton and Easington Lane.

The project would include up to seven main data centre buildings, a logistics building, car parking, internal roads and an on-site electricity substation connected to the National Grid at Hawthorn Pit.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648402.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The site location *(Image: TRACT)*

Tract is now inviting residents to have their say before an outline planning application is submitted to Durham County Council.

A spokesperson said: "Tract is excited to share our vision for the Durham Technology Park that will bring new jobs, infrastructure and community benefits to the region.

"We are committed to establishing a long-term partnership with the local community, Durham County Council and the North East Mayoral Strategic Authority, starting with clear communication and transparency about this project."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648405.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Illustrative masterplan *(Image: TRACT)*

The developer said the project could create employment opportunities across a range of roles, including electrical engineers, technicians, operations management, customer services, security and welfare.

It also plans to work with local education institutions and employment agencies on training and recruitment programmes, with opportunities ranging from apprenticeships to degree-qualified positions.

The plans remain at an initial design stage, with Tract saying further information will accompany the planning application.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648403.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Viewpoint B *(Image: TRACT)*

The developer said it was working with Northumbrian Water to establish the most resource-efficient cooling system and that any necessary upgrades to water supply infrastructure would be funded by Tract.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648404.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Viewpoint A *(Image: TRACT)*

Residents can view the proposals and speak to the project team at drop-in events at Robin Todd Community Centre, Front Street, South Hetton.

The events will take place on Friday, November 6, from 3.30pm to 7pm, and Monday, November 9, from 3pm to 7pm. Further details are available at durhamtechparkinfo.co.uk/.

Tract said feedback would be considered before an outline planning application was submitted to the council, with further opportunities for the public to comment once a formal application had been lodged.
