A nine-year-old North Yorkshire boy died in hospital eight years after he was attacked as a baby by his father.

Bradley Warren Nelson, of Whitby, died in Scarborough Hospital in October 2024, an inquest heard today (Friday).

Opening his inquest, coroner Sarah Hudson said that Bradley was born in Scarborough before living in Whitby.

He was admitted to Scarborough Hospital on 21 October 2024 “and treated for worsening respiratory distress. Despite efforts he died on the 22nd of October 2024.”

The provisional cause of death was pneumonia and a traumatic head injury.

She then adjourned the inquest, which would be suspended to await the outcome of the ongoing police investigation.

The BBC reported that Bradley was born fit and well. But he was left blind, severely disabled and unable to walk after being assaulted by Darren Spreadbury in April 2016, when he was seven months old.

Spreadbury was sentenced to four years in prison in May 2018, after scans found Bradley’s brain injuries had been caused by shaken baby syndrome.

He pleaded guilty to causing grievous bodily harm with intent and was handed a 10-year restraining order, as well as being sent to prison.

North Yorkshire Police says this is a complex investigation and may take some time to complete.
