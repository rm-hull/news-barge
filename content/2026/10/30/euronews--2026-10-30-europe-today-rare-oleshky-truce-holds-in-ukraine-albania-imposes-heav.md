## A rare ceasefire in Russian-occupied Oleshky enters its fourth day as aid is expected to arrive, after a Russian drone killed at least 14 at Dnipro's station. In Albania, protesters face fines up to €5,500. We also look at Israel's deadlock and the Ebola epidemic in Congo.

## On today's show:

**Sasha Vakulina**, Euronews correspondent, explains the humanitarian operation and civilian evacuation expected in Oleshky, and shares her interview with former Ukrainian prime minister** Arseniy Yatsenyuk**.

**Ardit Hoxha**, Euronews correspondent in Tirana, reports on public reaction to the heavy fines for demonstrators, and on the flares and chaos in the Albanian parliament.

**Dr Mustafa Barghouti**, Secretary-General of the Palestinian National Initiative, asks what Israel's political deadlock means for Palestinians, and whether the two-state solution is dead.
