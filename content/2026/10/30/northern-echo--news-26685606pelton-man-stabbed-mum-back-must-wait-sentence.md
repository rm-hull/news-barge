A man who stabbed his mum in the back must wait to learn his fate as the court seeks an update on her injuries.

Cameron Green was arrested after his mother was found with a single stab wound to her back in Alder Close, Pelton, on Thursday, August 6.

The 21-year-old was arrested on suspicion of attempted murder while his mother was rushed to Newcastle’s RVI.

Green admitted causing grievous bodily harm with intent and was due to be sentenced at Durham Crown Court.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21289614.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police cordoned off five homes in Pelton.

Judge Nathan Adams was told the court had no up-to-date medical evidence.

The judge adjourned sentencing until December 17 to give prosecutors time to obtain the information.

He said: "The sentence won't go ahead today. We are missing pieces of information for the medical evidence pertaining to the lasting impact of the injuries to your mother.

"That could have an impact on the sentence that you face."
