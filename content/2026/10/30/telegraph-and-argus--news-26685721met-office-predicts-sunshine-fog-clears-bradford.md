THE Met Office has forecast a foggy start to the day, with sunny intervals expected later in Bradford.

The morning will begin with mist and fog, with temperatures around 6C.

The fog is expected to persist until 12pm, when it will start to clear, giving way to mist, then overcast skies.

By 3pm, sunny intervals are expected, with temperatures rising to 7C.

The afternoon will see more sunshine, with temperatures peaking at 12C by 8pm.

The evening will be clear, with temperatures gradually dropping to 5C by midnight.

Winds will remain light throughout the day, with speeds of up to 9mph.

The chance of rain is less than five per cent throughout the day.

Looking ahead to Sunday, the day will start off overcast, with temperatures around 6C.

The sky will remain overcast throughout the morning, with temperatures rising to 10C by midday.

Sunny intervals are expected from 1pm, with temperatures peaking at 11C by 2pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 5C by midnight.

Winds will be slightly stronger than Saturday, with speeds of up to 19mph.

The chance of rain is less than five per cent from the afternoon onward.

The fog is expected to clear by 1pm, but some areas may see it linger for longer.
