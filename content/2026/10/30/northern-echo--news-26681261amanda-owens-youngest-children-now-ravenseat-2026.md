Amanda Owen's youngest children have continued growing up in front of television audiences, with the latest series of Our Farm Next Door showing how family life at Ravenseat is changing.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess first became a familiar face to viewers through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed Amanda, Clive and their nine children at their remote home in Swaledale.

Years later, the family remains a fixture on television through Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which documents the restoration of Anty John's farmhouse.

While much of the programme focuses on the ambitious building project, viewers have also been given further glimpses into the lives of the younger Owen children.

The series has shown the children continuing to experience a childhood shaped by the changing seasons, animals and the responsibilities that come with living on a working farm.

In one episode, Annas was seen dealing with the chaos caused by the family's new puppies.

The arrivals followed the loss of the family's much-loved dog Chalky, whose death was discussed during the opening episode of the latest series.

Elsewhere, Clemmie helped care for a lamb, while the younger children enjoyed an Easter egg hunt organised by Amanda.

The latest programme has also featured the girls spending time around the family's horses and taking part in activities at Ravenseat.

For long-standing viewers, these moments offer a reminder of how much the family has changed since the children first appeared on Our Yorkshire Farm.

The older siblings are increasingly developing their own lives and interests, while the younger children continue to experience the distinctive upbringing that made the family popular with audiences.

The restoration of Anty John's could also bring further changes.

Amanda and Clive have spent years working on the neighbouring farmhouse, with the property intended to provide additional opportunities for the family's future.

As the fourth series approaches its conclusion, the renovation is reaching another significant milestone, with furniture being moved into the building.

The project has raised questions about how the family's living arrangements might evolve as the children grow older.

However, the precise long-term arrangements have not been publicly established.

There is also uncertainty about whether viewers will continue following the children on television.

The fifth and final scheduled episode of Our Farm Next Door series four will air on Monday, November 2.

It remains unclear whether another episode or a fifth series will follow, with no confirmed plans for further programmes.
