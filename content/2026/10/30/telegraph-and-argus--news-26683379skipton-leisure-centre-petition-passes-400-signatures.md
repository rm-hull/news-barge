More than 400 people have signed an online petition about unpopular changes to the booking system at a leisure centre.

Members of Craven Leisure and Wellbeing Hub, now known as Active North Yorkshire, in Aireville Park, Skipton, say while the centre provides a great service, is clean and well run, and has quality instructors and staff, recent changes to the membership structure have left people feeling disappointed and frustrated.

Christine Solloway, organiser of the petition, dubbed Challenge North Yorkshire's two-tier leisure membership.

## 'Many members are deeply concerned'

She said: "Some longstanding members already saw a fairly substantial increase to their membership price in September.

"Now, North Yorkshire Council has introduced an added tier of membership which gives preferential booking access to those willing to pay an additional £6 a month.

"Many members are deeply concerned about this policy shift.

"These are local authority-run facilities, meant to promote communal access to health and wellness resources.

"It feels unjust and contrary to the spirit of a community-driven environment to prioritise those who can afford to pay more to get better access.

"It is not relevant to say the extra £6 also provides additional benefits - the one fundamental thing we all want is fair access to our local leisure facilities, which are now even less available when a privileged tier gets early access to book exercise classes which are already over-subscribed."

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21646302.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Active North Yorkshire Skipton *(Image: Lesley Tate)*

## Council defends decision

North Yorkshire Council has defended the decision to bring in a new pricing model and adds it is doing all it can to resolve temporary issues with booking systems.

Its corporate director for community development, Nic Harne, said: "We recognise that any increase in prices or changes to memberships can have an impact which is why we have worked hard to keep services as affordable and sustainable as possible, while continuing to provide the high-quality leisure facilities and experiences that our customers expect.

"The new pricing model is based on industry standards and has been designed to make sure concessionary rates support the residents who need them most.

"Benchmarking shows our concessionary membership prices are in fact lower than those offered by most providers - both locally and nationally.

"We are also doing everything we can to resolve the temporary issues with our booking systems as quickly as possible.

"We recognise that our customers in Skipton value the strong sense of community at Active North Yorkshire and the role it plays in supporting not only physical activity, but also social connection and wellbeing.

"Those benefits will always remain an important part of the service as we provide a welcoming and supportive environment for our customers.

"In the meantime, we apologise for any inconvenience caused and thank customers for their patience and understanding."
