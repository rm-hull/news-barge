A gym chain will open a 24-hour fitness centre in North Yorkshire, creating five jobs and offering round-the-clock workouts.

PureGym Catterick Garrison will open at 12pm on November 27, 2026, at Princes Gate Retail Park, giving members 24/7 access.

The gym will offer PureGym’s low-cost, no-contract membership.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646589.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A typical representation of a PureGym *(Image: PureGym)*

A spokesperson for PureGym said: "We’re excited to be opening a new PureGym in Catterick, bringing affordable, high-quality fitness to even more people across North Yorkshire.

"Open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, the new gym will provide members with the flexibility to work out whenever suits them, alongside a fantastic range of equipment and facilities.

"As we continue to grow, our focus remains on helping more people lead healthier, more active lives.

"We’re looking forward to welcoming the local community to PureGym Catterick Garrison and encourage everyone to keep an eye out for our special opening offer."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646590.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A typical representation of a PureGym *(Image: PureGym)*

The gym will create five jobs and is expected to bring more visitors to the retail park.

The 7,500 sq ft gym will have a functional training zone, free weights, resistance machines and cardio equipment.

Qualified personal trainers will be available, and every member will receive a free initial induction.

Membership will include group exercise classes, while free parking will be available subject to restrictions.

The opening is part of PureGym’s UK expansion.

PureGym also runs a Personal Training Academy for people starting careers in fitness.

The academy promises personal-trainer certification in 14 weeks and a job after completion, subject to an interview.

The spokesperson said: "The PT Academy also provides ongoing PT business support and opportunities for career progression into management roles."

The new gym will follow PureGym’s TrainSafe safety programme.

Automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are installed in all PureGym gyms nationwide.

A spokesperson said: "AEDs can be lifesaving in a critical situation, which is why we believe they are as essential as any other piece of equipment in the gym."

PureGym is also working with the British Heart Foundation to promote heart health nationwide.

PureGym describes itself as the UK’s largest gym operator, offering flexible, budget-friendly fitness.

The Catterick Garrison gym aims to serve the local community and support nearby businesses.

Every member will get a free induction to help them start safely and confidently.

Personal trainers will offer tailored advice and support.

Membership will include group exercise classes at no extra cost for a range of abilities and interests.
