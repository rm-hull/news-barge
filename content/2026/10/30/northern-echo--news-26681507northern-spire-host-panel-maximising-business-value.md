A wealth management firm is bringing together leading young North East business figures to help entrepreneurs build stronger companies and maximise their value.

Sunderland-based Northern Spire will host Under 40 Panel – Maximise Your Business Value at Ramside Hall Hotel on Wednesday, November 4, from 8.30am to 10.30am.

The breakfast is open to business owners and professionals of all ages, but is aimed particularly at people under 40.

David Cook, managing partner at Northern Spire, said: "Building a successful business is about more than growth alone; it’s about creating sustainable value and making the right decisions for the future.

"By bringing together four of the region’s most successful young business leaders who have experienced these challenges first-hand, we hope to give attendees fresh perspectives, practical ideas and the confidence to think differently about the next stage of their business journey."

The panel will examine the challenges and opportunities of building a business, increasing its value and planning for the future.

Each panellist will share their experience of entrepreneurship and leadership.

Jen Hallam, managing director of strategic brand communications agency Gardiner Richardson, is among the speakers.

Ms Hallam will speak about leadership and business development.

Utilink Consulting founder Sam Riches will discuss starting a company from scratch and building a team of more than 50 business energy brokers.

Adeel Bashir, legal director at Nouveau Legal, will explain the legal and structural steps needed to maximise a business’s value.

Mr Bashir is also a non-executive director and board member at several organisations, specialising in EDI and ESG.

Flooring Superstore founder Dan Foskett will talk about growing the business from regional beginnings into a nationwide operation.

Mr Foskett began selling computer parts at 15 before launching Flooring Superstore, which now has 50 stores across the UK.

Simon Oxley, a financial adviser at Northern Spire, will chair the panel.

Mr Oxley said: "The experience and knowledge of our panel is breathtaking.

"Sam is a hugely impressive Sunderland entrepreneur who’s spoken at a previous Under 40s panel.

"Jen is another excellent example of a successful young North East business leader, combining commercial leadership with a strong focus on brand development, social impact, and business resilience.

"Dan’s remarkable journey from teenage entrepreneur to building a substantial national business, with a reported annual turnover of about £50m, is truly inspiring.

"Meanwhile, Adeel will give our audience his own informed and unique perspective."

The morning will start with 30 minutes of networking, followed by an hour-long panel and a final 30-minute networking session.

Northern Spire advises individuals and owner-managed businesses across the region, while supporting long-term relationships and the wider North East business community.
