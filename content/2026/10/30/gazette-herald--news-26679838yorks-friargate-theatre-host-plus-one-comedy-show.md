An improvised comedy show is set to entertain folks in York later this year.

Fool(ish) Improvisation's "My Plus One" features two performers filling an hour with unscripted comedy, creating unique characters and stories in real time.

The show will take place at Friargate Theatre on Thursday, December 17, at 8pm.

The performance is led by York improv veteran Ned Lunn, who has been performing in the city for 15 years.

"My Plus One" promises to deliver an evening that seamlessly transitions from the humorous to the heartfelt, without any script or plan.

Each show is different, offering an intimate and unpredictable experience, with the audience witnessing a story that unfolds in the moment.

As Christmas approaches, the show provides a break from the festive hustle and bustle for an hour of live entertainment.

Tickets are priced at £10, plus a booking fee, and can be purchased online.

Fool(ish) Improvisation has previously performed at York Theatre Royal, Theatre@41, and various other venues across Yorkshire.

"My Plus One" looks to be a distinctive feature of York’s performance scene, offering an entire hour of unscripted theatre carried by just two performers, ensuring every moment is significant and unique.
