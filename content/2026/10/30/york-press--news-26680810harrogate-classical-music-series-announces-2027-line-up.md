## World-class musicians to perform in Harrogate concert series

Jess Gillam *(Image: Robin Clewley)*

A much-loved classical music series is returning with internationally acclaimed artists and rising stars.

The Harrogate International Sunday Series will run from January to April 2027 at the Old Swan Hotel, featuring world-class chamber music in an intimate setting.

The series opens on January 24 with violinist Emily Sun and pianist Alim Beisembayev, followed by performances from the Kleio Quartet on February 7, pianist Callum McLachlan on February 28, the Jess Gillam Trio on March 14, and cellist Maxim Calver with pianist Kumi Matsuo on April 4.

Sharon Canavar, chief executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said: "The Sunday Series has been part of Harrogate’s cultural calendar for more than three decades, bringing some of the world's finest musicians to our town.

"What makes these concerts so special is the opportunity to experience extraordinary musicianship in such an intimate setting.

"This year's programme brings together established international performers and exciting new talent, offering audiences a fantastic way to spend a winter Sunday."

The concerts, which include post-performance talks with selected artists, offer a chance to enjoy morning coffee and music in a relaxed environment.

Tickets go on general sale on 5 November at 10am from harrogateinternationalfestivals.com or by calling the Box Office on 01423 562 303.
