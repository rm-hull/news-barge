Tea Time Bradford Limited has applied for a premises licence to operate at 4-8 John Street.

The application is for late-night refreshments from 11pm to 2am.

The application, made under the Licensing Act 2003, was submitted on October 26.

Residents and businesses can submit written representations to the licensing team at Shearbridge Depot, Shearbridge Road, until November 23.

The licensing register is available for inspection at the same address from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Thursday, and 9am to 4.30pm on Friday.

False statements in connection with the application are punishable by an unlimited fine.
