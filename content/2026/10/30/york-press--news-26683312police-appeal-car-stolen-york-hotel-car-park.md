*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A car was reported stolen from a hotel car park in York.

The alleged theft took place sometime between September 3 and September 4, 2026.

North Yorkshire Police is now appealing for information and have released CCTV images of a person they want to identify.




A spokesman for the force said: "As part of our investigation, we are releasing CCTV images of a person we would like to speak to, as they may have information that could assist our enquiries.

"We are asking anyone who recognises the individual pictured, witnessed the incident, or has any information that could help the investigation to get in touch."

Officers are especially keen to hear from anyone with CCTV, doorbell, or dashcam footage from the area, or who saw any suspicious activity nearby.

Information that could help identify the person in the images is also sought.

Details can be reported by emailing [keilee.wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:keilee.wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk), calling 101, or reporting online via the North Yorkshire Police website.

The reference number 12260173182 should be quoted.
