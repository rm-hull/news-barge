A popular Whitby beach café and gift shop is up for sale at a reduced price.

North Beach Café and The Little North Beach Seaside Treats, which are situated on the West Cliff Beach promenade in Whitby, are seeking new ownership after 15 years with the current proprietors.

The seasonal businesses, which run from March to November, promise a unique combination of café services and a separate gift shop situated in a container unit.

The leasehold of the property is listed on Rightbiz for £95,000, with the rent being £15,000 a year, or £1,250 a month.

Rightbiz says the café provides a range of food and drink options, including breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a selection of hot and cold beverages.

The gift shop offers a wide array of beachwear, toys, souvenirs, and holiday-themed gifts.

Both businesses have no direct competition in the area, contributing to their appeal among tourists.

The businesses are located in a prominent beach front spot, surrounded by scenic sea views and overlooked by Whitby Abbey.

The café is well-known for its fresh, quality food and friendly service, with a menu that caters for various dietary requirements, including gluten-free, vegetarian, and vegan options.

The gift shop, meanwhile, is stocked with everything needed for a day on the beach, from buckets and spades to body boards and fishing nets.

The businesses have been noted for their personable touches, which have been appreciated by both the local community and tourists, and have helped to build a strong reputation.

The businesses have an independent website and a significant presence on social media, and hold impressive ratings on Google, TripAdvisor, and Facebook.

The owner and her mother currently run both businesses, with the help of five part-time employees.

The businesses are currently reporting a weekly sales average of £3,470 from April 1, 2026, to June 22, 2026.
