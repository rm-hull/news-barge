Demonstrators gathered near **Termini railway station** before setting off from**Piazza dei Cinquecento** at around 10.40 am, led by a large banner denouncing those accused of profiting from workers' labour while jeopardising their future.

Dancers performed in front of the banner as the procession began, with tractors and a taxi following behind. Firefighters, farmers, students and other demonstrators joined the march, carrying placards and banners through the **Italian capital**. The protest brought together several groups voicing frustration over the government's economic and spending priorities.

**Students have been demonstrating for weeks**, demanding greater investment in schools and more support for housing, while** farmers and truck drivers have protested against rising fuel prices and the cost of living**.

**Increased defence spending** has also become a focus of opposition among demonstrators, who are calling for public resources to be directed towards social needs.

The **USB-led mobilisation** brought these demands together in the streets of**Rome**, with younger protesters marching alongside workers and agricultural representatives.
