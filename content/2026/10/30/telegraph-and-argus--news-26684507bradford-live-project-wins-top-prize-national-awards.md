A restoration project has earned national recognition for its craftsmanship and attention to detail.

Ornate Interiors Ltd won the Ceilings category at the National Gypsum Trophy 2026 for its work on the Bradford Live regeneration, which saw the former Odeon cinema transformed into a new entertainment venue.

The award, presented at a ceremony in Birmingham on October 20, recognises the exceptional craftsmanship, technical complexity and visual impact of the company's work.

Jim Dodd, contracts manager at Ornate Interiors, said: "In 2025, we entered the Bradford Live project for the annual FIS Contractor Awards.

"I had a good feeling that we would do well with the project.

"It turned out to be a very good day for Ornate Interiors, as we were awarded the trophy for Best Heritage Project in the UK and also received the highest accolade of Project of the Year.

"It was a fantastic result for our whole team."

The company was later encouraged to enter the National Gypsum Trophy by its Saint-Gobain Formula representative, Laura Wilson.

After winning the UK award, Ornate Interiors will now represent the country at the international Saint-Gobain Gypsum Trophy in Madrid in April 2027.

The judges praised the "exceptional craftsmanship, complexity and visual impact" of the work, highlighting the skill involved in reviving a historic building while maintaining its original character.

Ornate Interiors restored intricate plasterwork throughout the venue, including the ballroom, bars, balcony fronts, staircases and turret ceilings.

The work involved manufacturing replacement mouldings to match the original designs and adapting them to suit modern lighting and ventilation needs.

Much of the original fibrous plasterwork had deteriorated due to years of dereliction and water damage, requiring careful assessment and specialist restoration techniques.

Mr Dodd said: "Tuesday, October 20 was another outstanding day for Ornate Interiors, as we scooped the top award in the Ceilings category for Bradford Live.

"It has only just sunk in that we are now going to represent the UK at the international awards in Madrid in April 2027 with this project.

"I cannot express enough how proud I am that Ornate Interiors has won this accolade.

"I am looking forward to the international awards next year, whatever the result.

"It is an immense achievement."

This latest award follows the company’s double win at the 2025 FIS Contractor Awards, where Bradford Live was named Best Heritage Project and overall Project of the Year.
