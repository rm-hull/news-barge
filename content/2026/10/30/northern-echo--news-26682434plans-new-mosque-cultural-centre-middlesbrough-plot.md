A striking new mosque crowned with a stone-coloured dome could be built on a vacant site in Middlesbrough.

Plans have been submitted for a new Abu Bakr Mosque and Cultural Centre at 7 Park Road North by the Muslim Federation of Cleveland.

The organisation currently operates from Cannon Park after its former Park Road base was demolished in the early 2000s.

The building would be faced in warm yellow-buff brick, with sections of grey and buff brickwork.

But one of its most distinctive features would sit above the building, with a stone-buff coloured dome planned for the roof.

The windows would also feature grey aluminium frames and artist-designed translucent patterned glazing.

Together, the details give an early picture of how the new mosque and cultural centre could look if the scheme gets the go-ahead.

The plans also include 26 parking spaces and 18 cycle spaces, with new or altered entrances for vehicles and pedestrians created from the public highway as part of the development.

Early discussions about the scheme have already taken place with Middlesbrough Council

Parking, plus the area's conservation area as well as the impact on neighbouring homes are among the issues highlighted during those conversations.

The council will now consider the proposals.

If approved, the scheme would turn the vacant plot into a purpose-built mosque and cultural centre with warm brickwork, patterned windows and a domed roof.
