Passengers travelling on the East Kilbride rail line are facing disruption after emergency services responded to an incident in the Hairmyres area.

ScotRail said the incident is affecting train services to and from East Kilbride, with services currently terminating and starting at Busby while the situation is dealt with.

Replacement buses have been arranged to operate between Busby and East Kilbride to help passengers complete their journeys.

In an update issued on Friday morning, the rail operator said: "The emergency services are dealing with an incident in the Hairmyres area which is affecting our trains to and from East Kilbride. We'll keep you updated."
