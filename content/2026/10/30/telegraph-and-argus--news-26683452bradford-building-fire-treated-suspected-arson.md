An investigation has been launched after a fire at a building in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was called at around 2.30pm on Thursday to reports of a fire at a disused building on Ivy Lane, Allerton.

Four fire crews and an aerial ladder platform attended the scene to tackle the blaze.

## Crews used large jets to extinguish fire

The fire service said the incident involved a two-storey derelict building measuring approximately 25 metres by 10 metres.

On arrival, crews found around 10 per cent of the building affected by fire and used three large jets to bring the blaze under control.

## Blaze recorded as suspected deliberate ignition

In an update, West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the fire has been recorded as a suspected deliberate ignition.

That means investigators believe the blaze may have been started intentionally, although enquiries into the exact cause are ongoing.

No injuries have been reported.
