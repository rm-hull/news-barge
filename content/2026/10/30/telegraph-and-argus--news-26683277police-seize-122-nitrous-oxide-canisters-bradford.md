More than 120 nitrous oxide canisters have been seized after police spotted what they described as a suspicious exchange between two vehicles in Bradford.

Officers from Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team were carrying out proactive patrols last Sunday.

According to police, officers saw individuals passing boxes between the vehicles and decided to stop and investigate.

## Searches uncover canister haul

Both vehicles, parked off Spartan Road in Low Moor, and their occupants were stopped and searched by officers.

During the searches, police recovered 122 nitrous oxide canisters.

The occupants have since been reported for relevant offences.

Nitrous oxide, often referred to as laughing gas, has become a focus of police enforcement in recent years.

## Patrols aimed at tackling crime

Bradford South NPT said the seizure was made during proactive patrols designed to identify and disrupt criminal activity in local communities.

The force said targeted operations such as these also help officers address concerns raised by residents and keep neighbourhoods safe.

## Public urged to report concerns

Police are continuing to encourage members of the public to report crime and suspicious activity in their area.

Anyone with information about illegal activity can contact West Yorkshire Police through its online reporting services or by calling 101.
