PUBS are getting into the Halloween spirit with a Medusa-themed weekend of spooky cocktails and ghoulish fun.

Bradford’s Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn, both part of the Proper Pubs group, will take part in the three-day event running from Friday, October 30, to Sunday, November 1.

Guests will be able to enjoy a special edition 'Venom' cocktail, a blend of Southern Comfort, Smirnoff vodka, VK Blue, and orange juice.

Two themed shots will also be on offer at two for £5.

With each Venom cocktail, customers will get a lucky dip for the chance to win a Hiss shot, a Bliss shot, or other treats.

A national costume competition is also being held, with a Butlins Big Weekender for four up for grabs.

To enter, customers simply need to take a selfie in costume at one of the participating pubs and share it on social media using the hashtag #ProperPubsHalloween.

A spokesperson for Proper Pubs said: "Round up your mates, pick your costume and head to your local for a fang-tastic Halloween."

Proper Pubs is part of Admiral Taverns and operates more than 200 community pubs across England, Scotland and Wales.

The Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn are the two Bradford pubs taking part.
