A predatory pervert was caught after videos of himself and an underage girl were found on the victim’s mobile phone.

Alby Hunt carried out a sex act in front of the child before encouraging her to engage in the same behaviour.

Prosecutor Shaun Dodds said the 21-year-old was fully aware of the girl’s age as he had wished her happy birthday.

Teesside Crown Court heard how the abuse came to light after social services were alerted.

Mr Dodds said videos stored on the girl’s mobile phone captured Hunt’s abuse.

Hunt, of Rochester Road, Stockton, admitted inciting a child to engage in sexual activity, engaging in sexual activity with a child and attempting to engage in sexual activity in the presence of a child.

His lawyer, Tom Bennett, said Hunt had recently become a father and expected a lengthy prison sentence.

Recorder Caroline Goodwin KC sentenced Hunt to three years and nine months in prison.

She said: “This was quite appalling behaviour by you, a young man aged 21 and the victim of your predatory behaviour was so young.

“You decided to take gross advantage of her. You engineered and manipulated a situation where she found herself alone and subject to your predatory conduct.”

Hunt was given an indefinite restraining order and a sexual harm prevention order. He must also sign the sex offenders’ register for life.

Detective Constable Anna Liddle, of Cleveland Police’s Protecting Vulnerable People Department, said: “Understandably, this vulnerable young victim was initially fearful and reluctant to speak to police and other professionals.

"However, the child showed great bravery in engaging with all the services and providing us with evidence, enabling us to secure charges against Hunt.
