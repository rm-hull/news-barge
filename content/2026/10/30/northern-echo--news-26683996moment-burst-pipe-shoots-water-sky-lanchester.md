Dramatic footage shows a burst water pipe shooting water meters into the air on a County Durham street.

Supply was affected around West Drive in Lanchester after the pipe burst on Thursday (October 29) afternoon.

![Burst pipe - Lanchester](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21650180.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Sharon Gardiner)*

Pictures show the water towering above the road, even shooting higher than a telegraph pole, while the street began to flood.

Passing cars turned on their wipers to clear their windscreens having been showered with water.

The burst was fixed and supply restored within 50 minutes, Northumbrian Water said.

A spokesperson said: "Our teams responded to a small burst on a water main at the junction of West Drive in Lanchester.

"The teams arrived on site and quickly located the burst, and all customer supplies were restored within 50 minutes."
