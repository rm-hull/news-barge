*Now celebrating its 25th anniversary, Edinburgh’s One Spa is building on its reputation with new treatments, upgraded facilities and innovative ways to enhance wellbeing*

For 25 years, One Spa has been a leader in Scotland’s wellness landscape, offering city residents and visitors a place to relax and invest in their wellbeing.

While many know One Spa for its iconic rooftop hydropool, 19-metre infinity-edge pool and thermal suite, the past year has seen exciting additions and upgrades, reinforcing its position as one of Scotland’s leading city centre spa destinations.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650481.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One Spa offers a large selection of exclusive treatments *(Image: Supplied)*

Recent developments include the launch of reformer Pilates and a newly upgraded gym featuring the latest Technogym equipment.

The spa has also introduced the Rasul Mud Bothy, a private self-guided experience combining exfoliation, mineral-rich mud treatments and steam for a deeply restorative ritual.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650482.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One Spa is a leading city centre spa destination *(Image: Supplied)*

Skin Rocks facials, developed by renowned skincare expert Caroline Hirons, now feature on the treatment menu, while the NOW Nail Bar, in partnership with OPI, offers guests another way to enjoy a little self-care.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650484.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Investment continues throughout the spa *(Image: Supplied)*

Investment continues throughout the spa, with enhancements to the thermal suite including a new steam room and cold room experience.

Twenty five years since opening its doors, One Spa remains true to the experience guests have always valued while continuing to evolve, offering more ways than ever to move, restore and prioritise wellbeing in the heart of Edinburgh.
