A North Yorkshire village pub just outside Tadcaster is back open to great reviews.

The White Swan at Wighill opened its doors last weekend, several days earlier than planned.

Tom Brown has taken the reins of the village pub, adding to The Knapton Lion at Knapton, the Lord Nelson in Poppleton and The Fox at Stockton-on-Forest.

At the moment, Tom is running the pub along with acting manager Peter Thompson.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647955.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Acting manager Peter Thompson and bartender Jess Stead *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The re-opening follows several months of closure for a refresh.

The pair re-opened the pub early as the work was complete and to allow the locals to enjoy the pub again.

It has fresh carpets, a lick of paint, new furniture, including tables and chairs to give it a new lease of life and to bring it more into line with Tom’s other pubs.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647968.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Acting manager Peter Thompson by the front door. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

A chef called ‘Jack’ has been recruited from Grantley Hall, with menus similar to the others, pub classics and gastropub offerings, with Jack introducing his own items such as a white onion velouté soup.

The White Swan will use local suppliers and promises big portions and value for money.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647969.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Acting manager Peter Thompson by the sign advertising the reopening, but the White Swan opened on Saturday. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The three staple beers are Yorkshire Blonde from Ossett Brewery, Timothy Taylor’s Landlord and Three Swords from Kirkstall.

Peter says since the opening, the White Swan has been “nice and busy”.

And when it opened last Saturday at noon, there were five villagers waiting to get in.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647993.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the dining rooms *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

He added: “People are really excited to be back in and all are enjoying the pub. There’s been quite a few locals plus others coming from neighbouring villages to see what the fuss is about.”

When the Press called on Thursday lunchtime David Wood of Norfolk, plus David and Julie Wood of Pocklington, were about to order lunch.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647993.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the dining rooms *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

David Wood from Bickerton and Julie Wood from Pocklington had been to the White Swan when it had other owners.

Julie Wood said her brother had come up from Norfolk to see their uncle and a neighbour in Bickeron recommended they visit.

They agreed the White Swan has good parking, a pleasant atmosphere and a warm welcome.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21648034.jpg?type=mds-article-620)David Wood, David Wood and Julie Wood *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

They were looking forward to their lunch.

Ron and Margaret Winter from York had also been to the pub two years ago and were keen to see what it was like under new ownership.

They had been to one of Tom Brown’s other pubs, the Knapton Lion.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21648035.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ron and Margaret Winter of York *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Ron added: “We like the chap who has taken it over. We thought we would give it ago.”

And they too praised the pleasant interior, friendly service, adding they too were looking forward to their lunch.

And in just a few days, TripAdvisor is reporting five-star reviews.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21648001.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another dining room *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The latest reported a “Great Sunday roast.”

It continued: “We had a lovely Sunday roast today. The White Swan opened under new management yesterday. Lovely fresh and warm decor and absolutely delicious food, along with a kids menu. As a coeliac needing gluten free options, I was really well catered for. Thank you to Tom and the new team.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21648007.jpg?type=mds-article-620)And another dining room *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Another also gave five stars on TripAdvisor for a “lovely village pub with fantastic food.”

They added: “We visited The White Swan on their opening night Sat 24th Oct 26. Landlord Tom and his team made us feel very welcome. The decor is very smart, comfortable and the food is amazing, making our evening even more fabulous. We will most definitely be back.”
