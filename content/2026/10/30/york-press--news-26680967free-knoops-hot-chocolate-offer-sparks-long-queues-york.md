**Some Yorkshire traditions never change!**

According to legend, a Yorkshireman's favourite two words are an incredulous "'Ow much?" and his favourite single word is "Free".

So it was reassuring to see queues outside Knoops when the shop handed out free cups of hot chocolate.

In a chaotic world thank goodness some things don't alter.

**Paying the cost of bus replacement service**

For the period October 24 to November 1, trains between York and Leeds are being replaced by bus services due to major engineering works.

During this period of only bus travel availability the rail operators are still charging normal train fares.

This begs the question. Shouldn’t the replacement buses be charging a flat fare of £3 in accordance with the government’s introduction of the national bus cap fare of £3 outside London until the end of December 2026?

**Good luck James Brennan and thank you**

It was a pleasure working with James on so many racecourse stories over the years before I retired as Chief Reporter at The Press three years ago.

He always made sure he was available when I needed information, such as attendance figures at the end of a big meeting.

He defended the racecourse robustly when it came under criticism, for example over the behaviour of some racegoers.

I remember his insistence to me on a number of occasions that people causing drunken trouble in York city centre after a day at the races should not be described as race goers, because they had left the course!

He also argued a compelling case about the economic and employment benefits which the course brought to York, which I was happy to report, but always accepted I had a right to write critical stories, provided they were fair and balanced, and never took it personally.

Wishing James a happy retirement.

**Great news about Healthwatch**

IT is fantastic news that Healthwatch is to be retained, as announced in the Budget. While it's a pity that it took a threat of demise for its high value to be recognised, recognised it has been by an expansion of its remit leading to greater influence on the whole health and social care sectors.

Healthcare in York is a professional, trusted, independent body that represents the interests of ordinary citizens in the health and social care sectors, in a similar way to how the Money Saving Expert represents ordinary citizens in the financial sector. If you are not familiar its work, I recommend visiting the website (Healthwatch York.co.uk). I confirm that I have no link with Healthwatch other than as one of the ordinary citizens that it represents.
