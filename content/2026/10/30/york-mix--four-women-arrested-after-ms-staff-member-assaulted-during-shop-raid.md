A member of staff was assaulted during a ‘high-value theft’ at a North Yorkshire Marks & Spencer store.

Police received a report that a significant quantity of goods had been stolen from the branch on Leeds Road, Harrogate, at 3.36pm yesterday (Thursday).

During the incident, a member of staff who attempted to stop the women was assaulted.

#### Marks and Spencer, Leeds Road, Harrogate

Officers were given details of a getaway vehicle which was located travelling northbound on the A1.

Roads policing unit officers intercepted it. Inside the vehicle were four women, two of whom were wanted under another warrant.

The vehicle has been seized and officers recovered £1,672.49 worth of suspected stolen goods.

All four women were arrested and enquiries are continuing today.

PC Michelle Telford from North Yorkshire Police said “At a time when the cost of living continues to rise and businesses are under increasing pressure, shop theft remains a key priority for North Yorkshire Police.

“When incidents like this happen, we will respond immediately.I would urge retailers, staff and members of the public to always report crimes of this nature.

“If a shop theft is in progress, or there is any threat of violence or assault, please call 999 straight away. The information you provide can be crucial in helping us identify and locate suspects quickly.”
