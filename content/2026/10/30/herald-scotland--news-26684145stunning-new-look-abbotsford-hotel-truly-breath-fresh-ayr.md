There is something special about discovering a hotel that combines the charm of traditional Scottish hospitality with a fresh, contemporary approach to comfort.

At The Abbotsford Hotel in Ayr, a new chapter is unfolding, with investment, imaginative design and a renewed focus on giving guests a memorable experience.

Originally built in 1916 as a private residence, the building has a long history, having previously operated as the Iona Hotel before becoming The Abbotsford in 1966.

Now under the ownership of Brian and Tina Daly, who took over in 2023, the hotel has undergone a thoughtful transformation, retaining its distinctive character while introducing a more contemporary boutique feel.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650382.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Rowan ballroom *(Image: Supplied)*

Central to that investment has been the refurbishment and redesign of its accommodation. The hotel boasts 16 individually designed bedrooms, including two suites, with each offering its own personality and sense of place.

Rather than opting for a uniform hotel-room aesthetic, the owners have embraced a collection of distinctive themes celebrating Scottish culture, heritage and the surrounding area.

Guests can choose from rooms including the Whisky Room, Thistle Room, Rabbie Burns Room and Hairy Coo Room, while the Fairways Corridor pays tribute to nearby golf courses and their associated clan tartans. The result is accommodation with plenty of character, complemented by the comforts expected of a modern stay.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650381.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Brèagha Suite *(Image: Supplied)*

Food is another important part of the experience. Epicurious at The Abbotsford brings its own creative flavour to the hotel, reimagining Scottish favourites while introducing international influences and inventive dishes.

With daily specials showcasing locally sourced ingredients and menus that evolve with the seasons, it offers something for both hotel guests and those simply looking for somewhere different to dine.

And this winter, there is an especially tempting reason to pay a visit.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650384.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the hotel's stunning bedrooms *(Image: Supplied)*

The Abbotsford's Winter Dinner, Bed and Breakfast package offers two people a three-course dinner, an overnight stay and breakfast the following morning, with prices starting from just £175.

Available Sunday to Thursday until December 31, 2026, it is an ideal opportunity to enjoy a cosy escape, a midweek change of scenery or simply some well-deserved time together.

With its welcoming atmosphere, distinctive accommodation and inviting restaurant, The Abbotsford offers a little more than just a place to stay. It offers a reason to make a winter break of it.
