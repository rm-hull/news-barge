Halloween doesn't have to be just for trick-or-treaters; while the spooky season is often associated with children, venues across the North East are proving there's plenty for adults to enjoy too.

From bottomless brunches and fancy dress parties to immersive horror experiences and late-night scares, here's our pick of the best Halloween events for grown-up ghouls this weekend.

### **Newcastle/GatesheadRocky Horror Halloween Bottomless Brunch, The Rebel Yell, Newcastle**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 12pm to 5pm (performance starts at 2pm)
* Price: Free entry or £44 for 2 hours of bottomless brunch
* What’s on: An afternoon of cult cinema, drag, bottomless drinks and Halloween chaos, hosted by drag stars Vick The Prick and Mea.T.Cleaver. Includes live entertainment, audience interaction and a screening of The Rocky Horror Picture Show.

**Stranger Things Club Night, Soho Rooms, Newcastle**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 9pm to 4am
* Price: Varied
* What’s on: A multi-floor club night inspired by the world of Hawkins, with themed music and immersive decor across four floors.

**Nerd Fright Fest 2026, Gateshead International Stadium**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 10am to 4pm
* Price: £6-10
* What’s on: Spooky entertainment for a range of ages, including movie props, photo ops, retro gaming, cosplay competitions and more.

**Halloween Punk Night, The Cluny, Newcastle**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 7.30pm
* Price: Starts at £10
* What’s on: A horror punk takeover headlined by The Fiend Club Band, performing classic Misfits tracks and supported by Carbona Not Glue, a Ramones tribute band.

### **Sunderland**

Horror Car Wash, Sunderland Live, Houghton-le-Spring

* Date: Every day until Sunday, November 1
* Time: Varied
* Price: £27 per vehicle
* What’s on: A drive-through scare experience featuring live actors and special effects as ghosts and ghouls surprise you from the outside of your car.

**Clubland Halloween, Elysium Nightclub, Sunderland**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 10pm to 5am
* Price: £22  
  What’s on: A night of bounce, club classics and hardstyle with a full Halloween club experience.

**Halloween Bottomless Brunch, The 3 Stories, Sunderland**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 1pm to 3pm
* Price: £40 per person
* What’s on: 2 hours of themed bottomless brunch with spooky drinks, Halloween anthems and interactive entertainment.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18714333.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

### **TeessideThe Terrace Asylum, Teesside University Students' Union, Middlesbrough**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 10pm
* Price: From £4.99
* What’s on: Middlesbrough’s biggest Halloween event, transforming The Terrace into a nightmarish asylum for one night only. Fancy dress strongly encouraged.

**Retro Halloween Scare Fest, Middlesbrough Empire**

* Date: Friday, October 30
* Time: 8pm to 3.30am
* Price: Start at £21
* What’s on: A retro club night with a Halloween twist- fancy dress “practically compulsory.”

**Scream Factory, Kirkleatham Museum, Redcar**

* Date: Friday, October 30 and Saturday, October 31
* Price: £27 to £35 for the full tour, or £10 for the food, DJ and firepit experience only.
* What’s on: An immersive scare tour featuring killer clowns, zombies, an abandoned mine shaft and other horrors.

![Scream Factory, Kirkleatham.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601219.jpg?type=mds-article-620)While the spooky season is often associated with children, venues across the North East are proving there's plenty for adults to enjoy too. *(Image: Newsquest)*

**Halloween Cocktail Masterclass, Curious Mr Fox, Durham**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 6pm to 8pm
* Price: £45
* What’s on: A one-off cocktail masterclass with spooky drinks, mezze boards and a certificate of completion. Learn classic cocktail techniques with a Halloween twist.

**Terror On The Terrace, The Black Lion, Sedgefield**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 5.30pm to midnight
* Price: £9
* What’s on: A night of house music, face painting and body art. Fancy dress optional.

**Trop Or Treat, Home & Botanic, Durham**

* Date: Saturday, October 31
* Time: 9pm to 2am
* Price: £10
* What’s on: A tropical-themed Halloween party with music and immersive entertainment.

**After Dark Halloween movie night, 3 of Cups, Durham**

* Date: Friday, October 30
* Time: 6pm-10pm
* Price: £3-13
* What's on: A spooky movie night with games, prizes, fancy-dress and food.
