BRADFORD is among the most expensive places in the UK for young drivers to insure their cars.

Drivers under 25 in the city now face an average annual premium of £1,933, according to new data from MoneySuperMarket.

This makes Bradford the sixth most expensive city in the UK for young driver insurance.

Alicia Hempsted, car insurance expert at MoneySuperMarket, said: "With insurance premiums for young drivers now averaging over £1,400 a year, it’s more important than ever for young motorists to be savvy when budgeting for their first car and any associated costs.

"It’s always worth getting a sense of likely costs ahead of time, so we’ve got a quick car insurance tool online to help young drivers cut through the confusion and get a clearer picture of what they can expect to pay."

East London topped the list as the most expensive area, with average premiums reaching £2,413 a year.

The high costs are partly due to Bradford’s inclusion on the Insurance Fraud Bureau’s list of "crash for cash" hotspots.
