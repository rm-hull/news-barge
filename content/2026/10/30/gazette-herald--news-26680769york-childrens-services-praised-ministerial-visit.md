An MP has welcomed a ministerial visit to see first-hand how children’s services are transforming lives in her constituency.

Rachael Maskell, MP for York Central, welcomed Georgia Gould, Minister of State for School Standards, on a visit to York Central to see recent improvements in children’s services led by City of York Council.

During the visit, Ms Gould toured SEND Central, York’s dedicated support hub for families, and visited Clifton Green Primary to understand how integrated services are delivered on the ground.

Children’s services in York have risen from ‘requiring improvement’ to ‘outstanding’ in just one inspection period, and the city has now been appointed by the Government as a Children’s Social Care Improvement Team to support other local authorities.

Ms Maskell said: "It was fantastic to have the minister in York, where we are paving the way in children’s services.

"York is a shining example of what happens when we can get this right.

"The minister was able to see this work, and we hope that the York model can be rolled out more widely to improve the lives of all our children."

York’s approach centres on co-located, family-focused services delivered through a network of hubs across the city.
