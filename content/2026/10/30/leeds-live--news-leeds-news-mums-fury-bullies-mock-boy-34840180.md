# Mum's fury as bullies mock boy, 7, with autoimmune condition at Leeds Light Night

## 'I spoke to the parents - but they just shook their head at us like we was in the wrong'.

A Leeds seven-year-old boy has been left in tears after he was targeted by bullies while out in the city.

Seven-year-old Cole Mercer was out at the Leeds Night Light festival in the city centre alongside his mum Ellie-mae Robertshaw. The family, from Cross Gates, were attending an event inside Leeds Trinity when a group of children began pointing and laughing at Cole, who suffers from alopecia and has no hair.

Ellie-mae kept an eye on them, and intervened when they pulled out their phones to take pictures. However, when she approached their parents, they did not appear to care.

She said: "We spoke with the parents, and the parents basically wasn't bothered, they didn't care. The little girl started crying but there was no sorry, there was no awareness, no I'll speak to them or whatever.

"They just didn't care and then they just shook their head at us like we was in the wrong."

Cole was left upset by what had happened, and he started crying when he got home. Ellie-mae added: "It made me cry.

"So we were both crying together because I don't want him to feel that way, and I know the older he's getting, the worse it's going to become."

Ellie-mae hopes other parents can learn from this to explain alopecia to their children, to make the world a more comfortable place for people like Cole. She explained: "Kids are kids, aren't they?

"Kids can be mean, but the parents should have pulled them up on it and sorted it out there and then."

She said if they had been, then they may not do it in future to Cole or other children. Ellie-mae also hopes for more awareness on the condition, and how it impacts people who suffer from it.

Ellie-mae hopes other parents make sure their children are "treating everyone with respect".
