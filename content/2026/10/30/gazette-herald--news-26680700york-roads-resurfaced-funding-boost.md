More roads and pathways across York will be resurfaced and improved as part of a newly expanded highways maintenance programme.

The updated plan, published by City of York Council, covers the 2026/27 financial year and follows confirmation of four years of transport funding from the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Recent works have already taken place in areas including Stockton on the Forest, A1079, A19, Haxby Road, Acomb and Elvington.

Councillor Kate Ravilious, executive member for transport, said: "It’s fantastic that more routes across the city are set for improvements.

"This has been made possible by the confirmation of funding at a recent Combined Authority meeting, and we’re acting quickly to get these repairs planned in and completed as soon as possible."

The expanded programme also includes street lighting repairs, drainage improvements and maintenance of York’s historic city walls.

Newly added schemes feature resurfacing of walkways in Wheldrake, safety upgrades to pedestrian crossings, and road resurfacing in Holgate, South Bank, Elvington and Osbaldwick.

David Skaith, mayor of York and North Yorkshire, said: "We’re investing record amounts to fix our roads and make them safer for everyone who uses them."

Longer sections of road have also been resurfaced thanks to increased funding.
