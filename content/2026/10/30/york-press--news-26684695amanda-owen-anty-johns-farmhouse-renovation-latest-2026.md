Amanda Owen's ambitious Yorkshire Dales farmhouse renovation is taking another step forward as the historic building begins to resemble a family home.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess and her former husband Clive have spent years working to transform Anty John's, a neglected farmhouse near their famous Ravenseat Farm in Swaledale.

The project has become the central focus of Channel 4's Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, with viewers following the family as they tackle the challenges of restoring an old building in one of the most remote parts of North Yorkshire.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618051.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anty John's *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Now, the latest episode reveals further progress inside the property, with the upstairs space being divided into individual rooms.

The work represents another important stage in a restoration that has required considerable patience, specialist skills and help from local tradespeople.

## What has changed at Anty John's?

His task is to transform the open upstairs space into separate rooms, helping the building take another step towards becoming suitable for family life.

The work follows earlier stages of the renovation, which have included plastering, painting and laying new flooring.

However, the latest work shows how the interior is gradually being transformed.

## Why are Amanda and Clive renovating Anty John's?

Anty John's is a historic farmhouse situated close to Ravenseat, where Amanda and Clive have raised their nine children.

The family has spent many years farming at Ravenseat, which is a tenanted property.

Restoring Anty John's offers an opportunity to create a more permanent base for future generations.

Amanda has previously explained that the project is intended to provide security and opportunities for her children as they grow older.

The family has been working alongside local craftspeople to preserve the building's traditional character while making it suitable for modern living.

## What is the history of Anty John's?

The farmhouse is associated with Anthony Clarkson, a historic mapmaker who lived in Swaledale.

His connection to the property has become another part of the story explored in Our Farm Next Door.

In the latest episode, Amanda reflects on Clarkson's life while travelling on horseback to the Tan Hill Inn.

## When will Anty John's be finished?

Although significant progress has been made, the family's long-term plans for the property are still developing.

The fourth series follows further work on the interior as the farmhouse moves closer to becoming habitable.

The programme's final episode, scheduled for November 2, is expected to show Amanda and the girls spending their first night at the property.

The family will also begin moving furniture into the building.

For viewers who remember the farmhouse at the start of the restoration, the latest developments represent a significant transformation.
