Police are appealing for help to find a missing Bradford man who was last seen in the Little Horton area of the city.

David, the force has not released his surname, has been reported missing, with officers urging anyone who may have seen him or knows where he is to come forward.

A public appeal has now been issued as efforts continue to trace his whereabouts.

## Description released by police

David is described as a white man, approximately 5ft 8in tall, with a slim build, short dark hair and glasses.

Police say he usually wears joggers or jeans, along with a T-shirt and jumper.

He also uses a white cane when walking, which officers hope may help members of the public identify him.

## Appeal for information

Anyone who has seen David, or who has information that could help locate him, is being urged to contact police.

Members of the public are asked to call 101 and quote log 671 26/10/2026.
