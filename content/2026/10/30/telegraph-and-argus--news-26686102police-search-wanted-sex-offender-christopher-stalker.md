Police are hunting wanted sex offender Christopher Stalker.

Stalker, 41, of Leeds, has reportedly breached the conditions of the sex offenders register.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for information to help locate Christopher Stalker from Leeds who is wanted for breaching the conditions of the sex offenders register.

"Stalker aged 41, has recently been released from prison.

"He has failed to comply with the conditions of the sex offenders register.

"Extensive enquiries have been ongoing to locate Stalker.

"It’s believed he could be in the Armley or Holbeck area of Leeds.

"Anyone with information that may assist in the whereabouts of Christopher Stalker is asked to contact police via 101 or go online at westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260499664.

"Alternatively, you can call the independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously via 0800 555 111."
