Work has begun on transforming a former County Durham pub ahead of its planned reopening under new management.

The Gate in Framwellgate Moor closed earlier this year, but the new managers announced plans to reopen it before the end of 2026.

It's the next chapter for the pub after previous operator Kira Leisure confirmed in June that it would be stepping away from the business.

Now, an update shared by the pub's new team has revealed that the major makeover is underway, to give the building "a fresh new look".

In a post on the venue's Facebook page, operators said the transformation had officially begun.

They wrote: "You may have noticed things are starting to look a little different at The Gate, and this is just the beginning.

"Our decorators have officially started work, and we're kicking things off by giving the outside of the pub a fresh new look."

The work includes new outdoor lighting, additional plants and greenery, and improvements to the building's exterior appearance.

The team added: "We're working hard behind the scenes to give The Gate the TLC it deserves, and we can't wait for you all to see the finished result.

"Same Gate, fresh new look, exciting things ahead."

The update comes days after the new pub runners announced plans to reopen the venue following its closure.

At the time, they said they were aiming to welcome customers back before Christmas and promised a community-focused pub offering plenty of entertainment, food and drink.

The owners previously told The Northern Echo they wanted to restore the pub's place at the heart of the local community while creating a welcoming venue for families, sports fans and regular customers alike.

Residents have reacted positively to the latest progress update on social media.

One commenter said they were "so pleased it's opening again", while another expressed hope that the new operators could "make a go of it".

Others described the transformation as "looking good" and said they were excited for the reopening.

The business is also recruiting staff for several roles as preparations for reopening continue.
