A teenage girlband with millions of TikTok likes has hit the road in the North East for its first music video.

Northern Daughters, a Sunderland-based group aged 14 to 17, filmed the video on an open-top bus.

Go North East donated the bus for the shoot, which featured the band exploring landmarks across Sunderland.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644517.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The video launched with a premiere at Sunderland City Hall *(Image: Go North East)*

Members Izzy Gettins, Lola Waite, Lola Cairns, Annette Ambang, Isabelle Dutton, and Molly Daymond formed the group in January 2026 after meeting at Inspire Stage School.

The video, released on October 16, features the band visiting Penshaw Monument, the Stadium of Light, the Northern Spire Bridge, and Seaburn.

Paul Waite, CEO of I-Stage Group (ISG), said: "Go North East were absolutely brilliant from start to finish.

"It’s genuinely special to see a proud local business backing local talent and getting behind a citywide community project that celebrates Sunderland’s people, culture and growth.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644520.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The band already have more 130k followers on TikTok *(Image: Go North East)*

"We’re really grateful to the Go North East team for their support, their energy on the day, and for helping us create something that will live on as a legacy piece for the city."

Go North East also supported a flashmob event at Keel Square as part of Sunderland City Council’s One Big Summer launch.

The band’s debut single, Open Arms, was released in May on streaming services.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644521.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The band filmed the video for Open Arms in August *(Image: Go North East)*

Lorraine Petch, the Go North East bus driver who drove the band’s open top bus, said: "It was a real pleasure to drive an up-and-coming girl band, Northen Daughters around Sunderland as they filmed their music video, helping them capture the character, energy and spirit of our city.

"It was fantastic to be part of something so exciting and to play a small role in supporting their musical journey. It’s not every day that driving a bus takes you behind the scenes of the music industry, and it’s definitely a day I won’t forget!"

Northern Daughters have already gained more than 130,000 followers and 10 million likes on TikTok.
