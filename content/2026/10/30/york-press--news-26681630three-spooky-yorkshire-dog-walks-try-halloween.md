## Yorkshire dog walkers urged to try three spooky Halloween routes

Trollers Gill, Wharfedale, where the ghostly Barguest is said to lurk *(Image: Rydale)*

Dog walkers are being urged to visit three Yorkshire spots with links to ghost dog legends.

Yorkshire country clothing brand Rydale is encouraging dog owners to try the three walks this Halloween.

The walks are at Trollers Gill, Wharfedale, where the ghostly Barguest is said to lurk; Ivelet Bridge, Swaledale, where a headless hound supposedly haunts the crossing; and Stainforth Bridge, near Settle, where a ghostly man and his dog are said to linger together.

Each walk is around four miles long, offering a mix of rocky terrain, riverside paths, and scenic routes through the Dales.

Walkers are advised to bring a lead for certain areas and be prepared for the terrain, with some routes requiring confident walkers and sure-footed dogs.

The guide, which includes route links, parking information, and practical advice for walking with dogs, can be found on Rydale's website: [https://www.rydale.com/blogs/news/yorkshire-ghost-dog-walks](https://www.rydale.com/blogs/news/yorkshire-ghost-dog-walks) and offers a unique way to enjoy the autumn season.
