Prince Harry and Meghan Markle are leaving the UK for a TV job in Australia after it was claimed that King Charles considered stripping their titles.

The Duchess of Sussex, who moved back to the UK with her family this year, is reportedly set to appear as a judge on the Australian version of Junior MasterChef next year.

A source close to the production has reportedly confirmed that the Duchess will appear on the highly anticipated upcoming season, slated for release in 2027.

This will require her to be away from Britain for "weeks", and it is speculated that Harry will take children Archie and Lilibet to Melbourne to accompany Meghan.

Journalist Lauren Tomasi told Australia's breakfast news show, Today: "It's not going to be any short period of time either.

"This is going to be a weeks-long stint, which I think begs the question: Are Harry and the kids going to come too?

"I would hazard a guess at maybe, yes. They have become quite the globe-trotting family. They have moved from the US to the UK; they love a bit of a trip.

"I think all four should head down under to Melbourne. That will be for the 2027 season of Junior MasterChef."

This comes after it was reported that King Charles, who has a house in the Cotswolds, considered stripping the couple of their titles.

InStyle reported this week: "When Prince Harry and Meghan Markle decided to step back from their senior royal roles and move to the U.S. in 2020, the move seemingly strained their relationships with King Charles and Prince William.

"And while Harry and Meghan were continuing charitable work independently, one trip in particular reportedly infuriated Charles, leading him to consider stripping them of their titles.

"In May 2024, Harry and Meghan travelled to Nigeria for a three-day tour as the country expressed interest in hosting the prince’s Invictus Games—which are next set to be held in Birmingham in 2027.

"Fans noticed that the trip closely resembled an official royal visit, despite the pair no longer working as royals, which in turn upset the king."
