Two footpaths in a North East town are to close temporarily while essential gas main repair work is carried out.

The City of Sunderland has issued a temporary traffic regulation order for the closure of the footpaths at Houghtonside in Houghton-le-Spring.

The affected paths are the entire length of Houghtonside and the unnamed footpath adjacent to number 53 Houghtonside, stretching from its junction with Houghtonside to its junction with the A690 Subway.

The closure will begin on November 5 and is expected to last for 11 weeks, although the order allows for a maximum closure period of six months.

The closure is necessary to allow for gas main repair work to be carried out.

This means all pedestrians will be temporarily prohibited from using these footpaths.

Only personnel involved in the works will be allowed access until the repairs are completed.

The council has announced that a pedestrian diversion route will be provided via the nearby highway network.
