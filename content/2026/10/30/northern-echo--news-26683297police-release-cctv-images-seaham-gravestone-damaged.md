A gravestone was damaged at a County Durham cemetery earlier this month, as police have released CCTV images of people they want to speak to in connection with the incident.

Durham Police officers are investigating the incident, which happened at Seaham Cemetery on Sunday, October 18.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649344.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Images of the men at the cemetery *(Image: DURHAM CONSTABULARY)*

In a bid to catch those responsible, the police force has released CCTV images of men they wish to speak to in connection.

It's believed the men arrived at the cemetery in a white car.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649345.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Images of the men at the cemetery *(Image: DURHAM CONSTABULARY)*

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "We’d like to speak to them as they may have information that could help an investigation after a gravestone was damaged at Seaham Cemetery on Sunday, October 18.
