# North Yorkshire Police launch appeal after keyless theft of Range Rover from property in Harrogate

## Police launch appeal after keyless theft of Range Rover in Harrogate

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a Range Rover was stolen from a property in Harrogate.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after a grey Range Rover was stolen without keys from a property on Swarcliffe Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/30/10/01/868058980_2108878503084328_952506795971267592_n.jpeg?trim=202,0,171,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The grey Range Rover, with registration DF66 OYS, was taken from Swarcliffe Road at around 9.45pm on Sunday, October 25.

Police say the vehicle was stolen without the use of keys, and officers are now carrying out enquiries to establish the circumstances surrounding the theft.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Officers are carrying out enquiries and are asking anyone who witnessed any suspicious activity in the area, or who may have CCTV, doorbell, dash cam or other footage that could assist the investigation, to get in touch.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260210498 when providing any details regarding the incident.
