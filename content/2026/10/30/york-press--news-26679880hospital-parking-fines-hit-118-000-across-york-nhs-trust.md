## Parking fines top £118k at hospitals trust - as York spaces fall by nearly 200

The parking facilities at York Hospital were revealed to be the most expensive in Yorkshire at £22.50 for a stay over five hours *(Image: Newsquest)*

Money raised by parking fines at the NHS trust that runs York Hospital has risen by more than 50 per cent in just over a year, new figures show.

Data obtained through a Freedom of Information (FOI) request shows that drivers paid almost £120,000 in car parking penalties across three hospitals in the York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundations Trust from March 2025 to July 2026.

This is up from more than £76,000 generated between March 2023 and February 2025.

The figures cover the trust’s hospitals in York, Scarborough and Bridlington.

It comes as the number of parking spaces available at York Hospital has fallen by 192 bays in the last three years, according to the FOI response to The Press.

A spokesperson for the hospitals trust said: "Our parking charges are broadly in line with local council rates, and we offer a wide range of concessions and exemptions, including for patients attending hospital regularly, parents of sick children and visitors to patients receiving end-of-life care.

“We recognise the importance of accessible parking for patients and visitors.

"While investment in new buildings and facilities to improve patient care has affected parking availability at York Hospital, we continue to manage the space available carefully to support access to our services."

![Money raised by hospital parking fines at York, Scarborough and Bridlington Hospitals has risen by more than 50 per cent in little over a year, latest figures have revealed](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21488233.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Money raised by hospital parking fines at York, Scarborough and Bridlington Hospitals has risen by more than 50 per cent in little over a year, latest figures have revealed *(Image: Newsquest)*

The trust spokesperson added: “Parking charge notices help prevent inappropriate parking and ensure safe access around our hospitals. Income from parking is reinvested in the maintenance and security of our car parks, as well as sustainable travel initiatives, including subsidised bus travel for staff and improved cycling facilities.

“It is important to note that the income figures provided cover York, Scarborough and Bridlington hospitals combined, rather than York Hospital alone.”




The hospital’s “expensive” parking – at £22.50 for a stay of more than five hours, making it the costliest in Yorkshire – was criticised by patients.

Speaking to The Press for a special report in July, patient Julie Tucker said she had paid £18.60 to visit a loved one in hospital for four hours.

She said: “I queued for almost 15 minutes to get in, parked up just before 3pm and left just after 7pm.

“I would have been out just before 7pm, which would have made it cheaper, but we had to wait to be let out of the ward for quite some time.

“I don't expect it to be free, but how can that amount be justified?”

![The Trust said new buildings and facilities to improve patient care has affected parking availability at York Hospital](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21136792.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Trust said new buildings and facilities to improve patient care has affected parking availability at York Hospital *(Image: Newsquest)*

A spokesperson for the trust said at the time: “Parking charges for patients and visitors at our hospitals are broadly aligned with those charged by local authorities in the areas we serve and are reviewed regularly."

They added that a range of exemptions and concessions provided support to those who needed it.

## Over £2.72 million raised in parking fees

Managed by the trust and an external contractor, Nexus T/A CP Plus Ltd, car parks at York, Scarborough and Bridlington hospitals made more than £2.72 million from parking fees in the last financial year.

This was up from £2.57 million in 2024/25 and £2.05 million in 2023/24.

York Central MP Rachael Maskell has called for an end to hospital parking charges, describing them as a “tax on serious illness”.

![Rachael Maskell MP said the parking fees placed an ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/16775411.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rachael Maskell MP said the parking fees placed an "unnecessary burden" on families, patients and NHS staff *(Image: Supplied)*

She said: “Labour created the NHS to be free at the point of use, so hidden charges of car parking fees are an unnecessary burden on families, patients and NHS staff.

“The underfunding of our NHS for so long under Tory rule meant that many NHS England hospital trusts relied on parking income to try and plug the gap.

"The Government must now bring about a plan to cover the cost of removing hospital car parking charges.”
