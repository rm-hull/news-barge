# Your ultimate guide to the best bonfires and spectacular fireworks displays across the Harrogate district

## Your guide to the best bonfires and fireworks displays in the Harrogate district 🎆

Bonfire season is almost here, and communities across the Harrogate district are getting ready to light up the skies with spectacular fireworks displays and traditional bonfire nights.

![Bonfire season is almost here, and communities across the Harrogate district are getting ready to light up the skies with spectacular fireworks displays and traditional bonfire nights](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/30/14/34/beautiful-colorful-firework-display-night-celebrate.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


From family-friendly events and dazzling displays to food stalls, fairground rides and autumn evenings spent wrapped up under the stars, there are plenty of ways to enjoy bonfire night across Harrogate, Knaresborough, Ripon, Wetherby and Nidderdale.

Whether you're looking for a big fireworks spectacular, a local community celebration or a fun night out with the family, there are plenty of events to choose from.

To help you plan your bonfire night celebrations, we've rounded up some of the best bonfire nights and fireworks displays taking place across the Harrogate district...

## **HARROGATEBonfire Night Party at The Queen’s Head InnWhere?** The Queens Head Inn, Kettlesing

**When?** Sunday 1 November from 4pm

**Cost?** £9 per adult and £3 per child

**Harrogate Stray Charity Bonfire & Fireworks**

This free event, organised by Harrogate Round Table, has become an annual tradition for many families, with generations returning year after year to watch the bonfire and fireworks

**Where?** The Stray (opposite Oatlands Drive)

**When?** Saturday 7 November

**Cost?** Free

**TIMETABLE:**

5pm – Music Starts

6.30pm – Bonfire Lit

7pm – Fireworks

7.25pm – UK Foo Fighters

9pm – Event Finish

**Bonfire Night at Rudding ParkWhere?** Rudding Park, Rudding Lane, Follifoot

**When?** Saturday 7 November

**Cost?** £15 per adult and £7 per child

**TIMETABLE:**

5pm - Gates Open

5.45pm till 6.15pm - Family Fire & LED Show

6.30pm - Bonfire Lit

7pm - Fireworks

## **KNARESBOROUGHKnaresborough Firework DisplayWhere?** Knaresborough Cricket Club, Aspin Lane

**When?** Wednesday 4 November from 6pm

**Cost?** £5 per adult and £2.50 per child

## **RIPONRipon Rowels Charity Bonfire and Firework DisplayWhere?** Ripon Racecourse, Boroughbridge Road

**When?** Friday 6 November from 5pm

**Cost?** £10 per adult and £5 per child

**TIMETABLE:**

5.30pm - Gates Open

7pm - Bonfire Lit

7.30pm - Fireworks

## **WETHERBYWetherby Bonfire & FireworksWhere?** The Ings

**When?** Saturday 7 November from 5pm

**Cost?** £5 per person, under 2’s are free but still require a ticket

**Walton Village Hall Bonfire EventWhere?** Walton Village Hall, School Lane

**When?** Saturday 7 November from 6pm

**Cost?** £9 per adult, £3.25 per child, under 4’s are free

**Boston Spa Community Bonfire & FireworksWhere?** Primrose Lane Primary School, Westwood Way

**When?** Saturday 7 November from 5pm

**Cost?** £5 per adult and £3 per child

## **NIDDERDALEPateley Bridge Bonfire & FireworksWhere?** Nidderdale Showground,

**When?** Saturday 7 November from 6pm

**Cost?** Entry by donation
