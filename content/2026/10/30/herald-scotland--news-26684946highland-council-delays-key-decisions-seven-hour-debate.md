A number of key Highland Council decisions have been pushed back until next week after councillors debated for around seven hours and just made it to half way on their meeting agenda.

The full council meeting was scheduled to start and finish on Thursday, October 29, but after seven hours of debate only a handful of meeting items were discussed meaning the rest have now been pushed back until next Thursday, November 5.

Several of the delayed items were reported by the press, including a call for more to be done to improve Royal Mail services in the region, a bid to reduce “over-reliance” on Chromebooks in schools and to decide whether or not to proceed with an independent review into council licensing after the rapist taxi driver scandal.

But now these decisions will not be made until next week as council rules state they have to call it a day at 6pm and re-schedule the remaining items for the same day the following week.

The council’s communities and place committee meeting is scheduled for next Thursday too, but this is set to go ahead as planned with the remaining full council items to be discussed at 2pm after that meeting is finished.
