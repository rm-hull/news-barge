Police have issued a major update in their hunt for a wanted man and two missing children.

Brothers Yousaf, 15, and Mohammed Ahmed, 12, were reported missing from their home in the Liversidge area on Saturday, October 24, but have since been located.

West Yorkshire Police are still appealing for information to help locate their father, 42-year-old Kibriya Ahmed, who is wanted for a breach of his non-molestation order.

The order prevents Mr Ahmed from having unsupervised contact with his two children."

A West Yorkshire police spokesperson said: "Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal to find them."

He is known to have links to Rochdale and Manchester.

## Police appeal

Councillor Jane Barraclough, (Reform, Liversedge and Gomersal), urged the public to support the ongoing investigation.

Cllr Barraclough said: "We urge anyone who might be able to assist to contact West Yorkshire Police as soon as possible."

Anyone with information about Mr Ahmed’s whereabouts is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or using the force’s live chat facility, quoting reference 13260586098.

Alternatively, information can be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111.
