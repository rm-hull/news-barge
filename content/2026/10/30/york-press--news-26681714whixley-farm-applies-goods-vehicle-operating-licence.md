A farm in York is seeking permission to operate a goods vehicle.

R. Winn & Son, based at Low Farm, Starra Field Lane, Whixley, has applied for a licence to use its own premises as an operating centre for a single goods vehicle.

Richard William Winn and Benjamin Richard Winn, trading as R. Winn & Son, have not applied to operate any trailers.

The application has been submitted to the Traffic Commissioner.

* **For more information about this public notice, click here.**
* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal at publicnoticeportal.uk/york-press**

People who own or occupy land near the farm and believe their enjoyment of the land may be impacted are invited to make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

These must be sent to Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, within 21 days from the notice's publication.

A copy of the representation should also be sent to the applicant.

Further guidance on making representations is available at www.gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.
