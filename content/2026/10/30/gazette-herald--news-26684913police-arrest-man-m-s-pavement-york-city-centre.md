POLICE have been called to an incident outside M&S in York city centre.

Multiple officers were seen in Pavement shortly after 1pm on Friday (October 30).

An eye-witness said a man appeared to have been arrested from inside the shop.




![Multiple officers and two police vans were called to Pavement in York](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21651476.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Multiple officers and two police vans were called to Pavement in York *(Image: Newsquest)*

Two police vans were called to the scene, and officers were seen liaising with two people outside the van at 1.30pm.

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Police for comment.
