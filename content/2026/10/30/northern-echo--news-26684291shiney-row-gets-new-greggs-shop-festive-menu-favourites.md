A fresh new Greggs shop has opened its doors to North East customers.

The new branch opened today (Friday, October 30) on Westbourne Terrace in Shiney Row, Houghton-le-Spring, bringing 12 new jobs to the area and offering a full menu of freshly prepared options throughout the day - from breakfast to evening meals.

Customers can enjoy popular items such as sausage rolls, steak bakes, and vegan alternatives, as well as seasonal favourites like the Festive Bake and Salt & Chilli Chicken Roll.

Douglas Seaman, shop manager, said: "We're excited to be opening our doors in Shiney Row and becoming part of the local community.

"Whether customers are stopping by for breakfast, lunch or a coffee break, we're looking forward to serving them from our new shop on Westbourne Terrace."

The shop also serves a range of over-ice drinks, including the Candy Cane Mint Iced Matcha Latte, and a wide selection of hot food such as pigs in blankets and Southern Fried Chicken Goujons.

Customers can choose to dine in, take away, or order ahead using the Greggs App.

Delivery is also available through Just Eat and Uber Eats.

Gillian Long, retail operations director for Greggs, said: "Our new shop in Shiney Row has brought 12 new jobs to the local area, as well as providing customers with a modern, convenient new place to experience their Greggs favourites."

Greggs App users can access exclusive rewards, including a free hot or iced drink when they sign up and a complimentary sweet treat on their birthday.

The shop opens with a modern look and operates Monday to Saturday from 6am to 5pm and Sundays from 8am to 4pm.
