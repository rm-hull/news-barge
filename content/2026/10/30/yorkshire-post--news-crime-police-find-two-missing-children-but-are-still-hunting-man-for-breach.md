# Police find two missing children - but are still hunting man for breaching non-molestation order

## Police have issued an update to an appeal for two missing children and a wanted man.

West Yorkshire Police appealed on Thursday for information regarding the whereabouts of Kibriya Ahmed, 42, and his two children Yousaf and Mohammed.

Both of the children were reported missing from Liversidge in Kirklees on Saturday, while Ahmed has a non-molestation order preventing him from having unsupervised contact with his two children.

Yousaf, aged 15, and Mohammed, aged 12, were located by police, an update from the force said.

However, officers are still looking for Ahmed.

![Kibriya Ahmed.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmJhYTNlMDdlLTVkYjYtNDNhNi1hMGYxLTkzMDdjOTUwMmYxNzoyYjA1MWRmMC1jMGFlLTQ1MDctODgxYi0wNzkwOTYzY2Y2ZjE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


A statement said: “We are still appealing for information to help locate Kibriya Ahmed who is wanted for a breach of his non-molestation order.”

He has connections in Rochdale and Manchester.

Anyone with information which may assist to locate Kibriya Ahmed is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at here, quoting reference 13260586098.

Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
