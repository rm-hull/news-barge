Medivet Harrogate Knaresborough Road is inviting pet owners, families and neighbours to a festive open day on Sunday 22 November, with all donations raised during the event going to Make-A-Wish UK, the charity that grants life-changing wishes for children with critical illnesses.

The free event will run from 11am to 3pm at the veterinary practice on Knaresborough Road. It will give local residents an opportunity to meet the team behind their pets’ care, explore the practice and enjoy a range of Christmas activities, while helping raise money for a charity supporting seriously ill children and their families.

Medivet provides veterinary care for pets, and the open day will offer visitors an opportunity to get to know the people working at its Harrogate Knaresborough Road practice in a relaxed, festive setting. Pets are welcome, making it an event for the whole family.

Inside, the practice will be decorated with Christmas lights and tinsel, with Santa welcoming children to his grotto in a consulting room. Children will be able to make a wish with Santa and take home a Medivet goodie bag.

Visitors can also explore the teddy theatre, discover more about what happens behind the scenes at a veterinary practice and speak to the team about pet first aid.

Mince pies, mulled wine and fizz will be available, alongside a raffle and quiz. Visitors are invited to make a donation to see Santa or enjoy a mince pie, with all money raised on the day going towards Make-A-Wish.

Make-A-Wish UK supports children living with critical illnesses by granting wishes that can provide special experiences and lasting memories during difficult circumstances. The charity aims to bring joy and create positive moments for children and their families when they may be facing challenging times.

> **Marrianne Griffiths, co-owner and partner of Medivet Harrogate, said:**
>
> Christmas is a truly wonderful time of year where wishes can come true, but it can also be quite tough too. That’s why we are wanting to give a little back with a bit of festive fun and our very own Santa, pulling together as a community to fundraise for Make-A-Wish to help make children’s wishes a reality.

![Marrianne Griffiths, co-owner and partner of Medivet Harrogate](https://harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/medivets-1.webp)

The practice hopes the event will bring the local community together for an afternoon of festive activities while raising money to help make wishes come true for children living with critical illnesses.

### How to attend

No booking is required. The open day takes place from 11am to 3pm on Sunday 22 November at Medivet Harrogate Knaresborough Road, 43 Knaresborough Road, Harrogate, HG2 7SR.

For more information, contact the practice on 01423 884074 or email [harrogate.knaresborough.rd@medivet.co.uk.](mailto:harrogate.knaresborough.rd@medivet.co.uk.)
