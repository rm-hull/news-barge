Firefighters remain at the scene of an ongoing fire at a student accommodation building close to the University of Bradford.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was called to Horton House on Great Horton Road at 10.47am on Friday, October 30.

The incident is currently listed as ongoing by the fire service.

## Crews tackle kitchen fire

According to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, the blaze started in the kitchen of a flat on the second floor of the high-rise building.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21651052.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Firefighters on the scene on Great Horton Road. *(Image: T&A)*

Firefighters used two breathing apparatus and a hose reel jet to extinguish the fire.

No information has yet been released regarding any injuries or the cause of the blaze.

## Student accommodation near Bradford campus

Horton House is a purpose-built student accommodation block situated close to the University of Bradford campus.

The development offers en-suite rooms and studio apartments for students, along with communal facilities and managed accommodation.

The block is a prominent part of Bradford's student quarter and is home to students during the academic year.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21651101.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Firefighters remain at the scene of an ongoing fire at Horton House on Great Horton Road. *(Image: T&A)*

## Incident remains ongoing

Fire crews continued to deal with the incident at the time of the latest update from West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service.

Further details about the extent of the damage, any evacuations, or the cause of the fire have not yet been confirmed.

A WYFRS spokesperson said: "Kitchen fire in flat on 2nd floor of highrise building. 2 breathing apparatus and 1 hose reel used to extinguish fire."
