An arrest warrant has been issued for an arsonist after he failed to turn up at court to be sentenced.

Anthony Ovington admitted starting a fire in the toilets of a city centre pub.

The 53-year-old set fire to toilets and bathroom fittings at The Water House on North Road, Durham, opposite the city bus station.

Ovington, of Park View Gardens, Glasgow, admitted reckless arson over the fire on Thursday, January 30, last year.

The judge heard that Ovington had not travelled from Glasgow to attend court.

His barrister, Paul Abrahams, told Teesside Crown Court he could not oppose the application for an arrest warrant.

Judge Peter Armstrong said: “I shall issue a bench warrant not backed by bail.”

At an earlier hearing, he was granted bail and warned that a warrant would be issued if he failed to attend sentencing on May 29, 2026.
