John Swinney said he “understands” the concern over the allegations relating to a visit to Balmoral by Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and the paedophile Jeffrey Epstein.

Detectives are investigating the time the disgraced royal spent with the late paedophile financier and convicted sex trafficker Ghislaine Maxwell at the King’s private estate in Scotland.

Police Scotland said it had received “information” and a probe is at a “very early stage”.

The Daily Express reported the inquiries relate to claims that women were trafficked to the estate.

Speaking to the Press Association on Friday, the First Minister said: “I’ve seen the media report on the allegations that have been made and I understand the seriousness of the points that have been made and the concern that has been expressed.

“This is an operational matter for Police Scotland to fully and comprehensively investigate and I’m very confident that’s exactly what Police Scotland will do.”

Andy Burnham would not comment on the reports. Asked about the matter, the Prime Minister told reporters: “Well, those are matters for the police, and it’s not for me to comment on police investigations, which should properly be independent.”

Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor was arrested on suspicion of misconduct in public office on February 19, his 66th birthday, following allegations he shared sensitive information with Epstein during his time as the UK’s trade envoy.

The former Duke of York was interviewed under caution before being released under investigation, and denies wrongdoing, with the police probe ongoing.

Andrew was the UK’s special representative for international trade and investment from 2001 until 2011, when he stepped down amid controversy over his friendship with Epstein.

![Prince Andrew arriving for the Requiem Mass service for the Duchess of Kent,](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20321659.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor *(Image: PA)*

He received no salary for travelling around the world and at home promoting Britain’s business interests, but criticisms were made about the thousands of pounds spent each year on his expenses and travel costs.

His decision to quit the role came in the same year he was pictured with his arm around his primary accuser, Virginia Giuffre, who said she was trafficked to the former duke at the home of Ghislaine Maxwell.

Andrew paid Ms Giuffre, a woman he has claimed never to have met, millions of pounds to settle a civil suit in the US in 2022.

Ms Giuffre died by suicide aged 41 in April 2025.
