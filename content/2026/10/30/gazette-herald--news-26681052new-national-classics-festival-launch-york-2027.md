A new national festival celebrating the classical world will take place in York in 2027.

The Classics Festival, focused on the ancient Greeks, Romans, and related civilisations, will run from September 3 to 6 at the Yorkshire Museum and Hospitium.

It will feature around 30 events exploring classical themes through books, music, theatre, and the visual arts.

The Classics Festival director, Adrian Turpin, said: "Our aim is to create a festival that will lead new audiences to enjoy the classics as well as attracting those who already identify as classicists."

The festival is designed for a general audience and is being launched on a not-for-profit basis, with core funding from the Fountainhall Trust and a three-year development plan.

Confirmed partners include The British School at Athens, The Classical Association, and the charity Classics for All, which supports the teaching of classics in state schools.

The advisory board features leading classical authors, including Charlotte Higgins and Sir Peter Stothard.
