A woman who was believed to be carrying a knife has been arrested on suspicion of assault after a man was left injured in the street.

The incident happened on Thursday (October 29) at about 8.15pm, in the Bishopton Road area of Stockton.

Cleveland Police say they were called to reports of a woman in possession of a knife.

After 25 minutes of searching, officers arrested a 36-year-old woman in Vicarage Street just after 8.40pm.

She was arrested on suspicion of assault and is being questioned.

A 31-year-old man was discovered with injuries on St Paul’s Road and an ambulance was called.

The extent of his injuries hasn't been confirmed.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "Enquiries are ongoing and we would appeal for anyone with information or any relevant footage to contact Cleveland Police via the force website or by phoning 101. Please quote ref 216668."
