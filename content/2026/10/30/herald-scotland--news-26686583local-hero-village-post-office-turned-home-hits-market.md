A house that was originally the village post office and store in a quaint coastal village made famous by classic Scottish comedy-drama Local Hero has been listed for sale.

The Aberdeenshire village of Pennan acted as the movie’s fictional Ferness, and its iconic red phonebox took centre stage in the 1983 movie.

Now a "rare opportunity" has emerged to acquire a former fisherman's cottage within the historic village.

The property is being offered for sale by Lanark-based estate agents Morison & Smith for offers over £148,000.

The listing notes that the property combines "traditional fisherman's-cottage charm, modern conveniences, superb sea views and an extraordinary connection with Scottish film history".

The accommodation comprises entrance hallway, living room, dining kitchen, two double bedrooms and shower room, together with a stone-built garage/boathouse.

The dual-aspect living room boasts a red sandstone open fireplace with log-burning stove and front-facing windows overlooking the North Sea and Moray Firth, while the principal bedroom also has a front-facing window with sea views and a side-facing window directly overlooking Pennan's famous red telephone box.

Fraserburgh and Macduff provide the nearest larger-town amenities, while local deliveries are available. The village also has a seasonal coffee hut on the pier and the well-known Pennan Inn, offering high quality dining and a small bar.
