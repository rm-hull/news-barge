A nine-year-old boy from North Yorkshire died in hospital eight years after his father attacked him leaving him blind and brain-damaged.

Bradley Nelson, of Whitby, died in Scarborough Hospital on October 22, 2024, an inquest heard.

Coroner Sarah Hudson gave Bradley’s cause of death as pneumonia followed by traumatic head injuries.

She adjourned the hearing to a later date due to an ongoing police investigation.

North Yorkshire Police said its investigation into the case remains ongoing.

“What is a complex investigation remains ongoing and is likely to take time,” a force spokesperson said.

Bradley was left severely disabled and unable to walk after being assaulted by his father, Darren Spreadbury, in April 2016 when he was seven months old, according to the BBC.

The broadcaster reported Bradley was born fit and well but was later diagnosed with cerebral palsy and epilepsy after the attack.

Spreadbury was jailed for four years in 2018 after pleading guilty to causing grievous bodily harm with intent.

He was also handed a 10-year restraining order.

He was sentenced after scans found Bradley’s brain injuries had been caused by shaken baby syndrome.
