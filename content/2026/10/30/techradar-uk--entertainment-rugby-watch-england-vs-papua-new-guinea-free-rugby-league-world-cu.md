Herbie Farnworth and Dom Young both scored hat-tricks as the three-time runners-up trampled all over the French, but they are still relying on results elsewhere in this final round of pool matches. England are third in Pool B/C and are guaranteed to finish below Tonga on the head-to-head rule after their defeat by the South Pacific Islanders in their opening match. After a 42-18 win over Lebanon in their opening game they were crushed 64-6 by Samoa, so the Kumuls need to beat McDermott's men and for Tonga to lose to Samoa, with a points swing of 88.
