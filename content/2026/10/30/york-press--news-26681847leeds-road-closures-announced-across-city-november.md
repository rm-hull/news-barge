Leeds City Council has announced a series of road closures.

Temporary road closures are set to impact various parts of Leeds as gas, water, and electric works get underway.

The council has provided details on the affected roads and the nature of the works.

In Armley, Back Springfield Mount will be closed from Wednesday, November 4, for new electric connection works.

A diversion will be set up via Town Street, Springfield Mount, and Whingate.

The works are expected to last until Tuesday, November 10.

In Hunslet, Westbourne Mount will be closed between its junctions with Stratford Street and Rowland Road for gas main replacement works.

The closure will begin on Thursday, November 5, with works expected to be completed by Thursday, December 17.

Similarly, Church Close in Pool, Wharfedale, will be closed from November 10 for the renewal of water mains.

The works are expected to last until December 11.

Victoria Mount in Horsforth will be closed for new connection works from November 5.

The works are expected to be completed by November 13.

In Roundhay, Barlby Way will be closed for new connection works from November 5 to November 13.

No diversion is necessary due to the road being a dead end.

Fearnville View will be closed for water mains works from Monday, November 2, to Friday, November 20.

The road will be closed between 8am and 5pm each day and will change to a one-way closure between 5pm and 8am.

Spen Approach in West Park will be closed for fuse upgrade works from Tuesday, November 3, to Monday, November 9.

In Garforth, Sturton Avenue will be closed for new connection works from November 11 to November 19.

Lawrence Gardens in Roundhay will be closed for water main works from November 16 to November 29.

In West Park, Spen Lane will be closed for sewer connection works on behalf of Yorkshire Water from Tuesday, November 10, to Friday, December 11.

Gerard Avenue in Morley will be closed for new connection works from November 12 to November 18.

George Street in Leeds will have parking prohibited for borehole works from Monday, November 9, to Wednesday, November 11.

Kent Crescent in Pudsey will be closed for new electric connection works from November 11 to November 17.

The Grove in Horsforth will be closed for new connection works from November 12 to November 18.

In Hunslet, Severn Road will be closed for the installation of ducts from November 2 to November 9.

Definitive Footpath 82 will be closed for resurfacing works from November 16, for up to six months.

Definitive Footpath Wetherby No.8 will be closed for resurfacing works from October 29, for up to six months.

For the Northern Lights event at Temple Newsam, temporary traffic management measures will be in place from November 20 to December 31.

The Leeds City Council will also introduce a no waiting at any time restriction on several roads in the Chapel Allerton Ward.

These restrictions are part of the Chapeltown Healthier Streets initiative.

The council has assured that essential access to and from premises and for emergency vehicles will be maintained on all affected roads.
