The certification comes as Strive Group Holdings marks its 25th year in business. Both businesses are based at Freemans Way in Harrogate.

Strive Group Holdings acquired natural wellbeing brand Harrogate Organics in June 2025. The group says the certification reflects its commitment to social and environmental responsibility, ethical business practices and accountability.

B Corp certification is administered by B Lab, an organisation that assesses businesses against standards covering areas including governance, workers, communities, the environment and customers.

Alistair Grant, managing director of Strive Group Holdings, said the certification marked both recognition of the group’s progress and a commitment to further improvement.

> **Alistair Grant, managing director of Strive Group Holdings, said:** I am incredibly proud that Strive Group Holdings has achieved B Corp certification, a globally recognised standard that challenges businesses to meet the highest levels of social, environmental and ethical performance.
>
> While this accreditation celebrates the progress we have already made, it also marks the beginning of our next chapter. Achieving B Corp is not a destination; it is a commitment to continually raising the bar, challenging ourselves to do better, and ensuring that growth is achieved responsibly and sustainably.
>
> This certification reinforces our commitment to making thoughtful, transparent and accountable decisions that benefit our people, our clients, our communities and the environment.

Grant said the group’s values of accountability, integrity and respect had guided the business over the past 25 years and would continue to shape its approach.
