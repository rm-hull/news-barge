## Are Halloween favourite green smoothies tasty or grisly?

Green smoothies are popular Halloween drinks

ON THE cusp of Halloween, what better than a clutch of grisly green smoothies to whet your appetite.

Popular on October 31, the drink’s often vibrant colour easily transforms into spooky themes like swamp potions, witches' brew and ghoulish cocktails to serve at parties.

Seasonal joking aside, with their sometimes vibrant pea colour, green smoothies are not the most appetising-looking drinks, but they can bring a refreshing taste.

Blending leafy greens such as spinach or kale with fruits, they are widely believed to deliver health benefits through micronutrients such as vitamins A, C and K, though in some products this can be offset by a high sugar content. But this test is all about taste.

I pitted several supermarket own brands against other well-known favourites. Here’s how they compare:

\*Energise Super Smoothie by Asda, 750ml, £1.80

Fruit and veg contents: apple, kiwi, mango, matcha and cucumber.

A pea green colour, this one had a thinner, more watery consistency than the others. When I poured it into the glass it had a slight smell of kiwi fruit, but that did not come through in the taste. As a drink it was, to me, quite cardboard-like, with a dominant taste of cucumber - in fact it was very much like liquid cucumber. I didn’t like it, but for anyone who does, It’s a not a bad price.

Pros: price.

Cons: consistency, taste.

Mark: 2/5

\*Innocent Green Energise Super Smoothie,750ml, £2.50 (Asda)

Contains kiwi, cucumber, apple and matcha.

Dark green in colour, this was fairly thick and had an aroma of apple which I could taste along with kiwi. As I drank it other flavours came through, which I liked but could not identify (I am not sure what matcha tastes like). This one is more pricey but was nice and is worth the extra outlay.

Pros: consistency, taste.

Cons: price.

Mark: 4/5

\*Sainsbury’s Cucumber, Kiwi and Lime Smoothie, 750ml, £1.95

Quite a thin consistency - more like a fruit juice. This pea-green smoothie had a fresh smell, slightly grassy, but freshly-mown. Like the Asda drink, I found the taste rather boxy - I could not pick out any ingredients, and it had a lingering, rather unpleasant aftertaste.

Pros: aroma.

Cons: consistency, smell, taste.

Mark: 1/5

\*Naked Fibre Machine Supercharged Smoothie, 750gml, £4.50 (£2.50 with Tesco Clubcard)

Contains: kiwi, apple, spinach, apple cider vinegar.

A jade green colour, this one was more watery in consistency than the others. It has a bit of a grassy aroma. As for taste, I found it far too sweet - it was like drinking from a can of syrup. The sweetness dominated, and I found it hard to detect any of the individual flavours. Very costly if you don’t have a Tesco Clubcard.

Pros: none.

Cons: consistency, smell, taste, price.

Mark: 1/5

\*M&S Smoothie, 750ml, £2.00

Fruit and veg contents: Mango, kiwi and kale blended with apple.

This one was more khaki in colour, which wasn’t appetising at all. It was smooth in texture, with a pleasant, slightly mango aroma. It had an odd taste - very vegetably, despite it being mostly fruit. I wasn’t very keen.

Pros: consistency.

Cons: appearance, taste.

Mark: 2/5

\*Enliven Super Smoothie, The Juice Company, Aldi, 750ml, £1.59

Contains kiwi, lime, matcha, cucumber and flax seeds.

With quite a pea green colour and fairly thick consistency, this one smelled quite grassy - the aroma remined me of our green wheelie bin after the grass cuttings have been collected, which wasn’t a great first impression. The taste surprised me though - it was nice and fresh; I could pick out the kiwi, flax and lime flavours. It was very refreshing and a decent price too.

Pros: consistency, taste, price.

Cons: smell.

Mark: 3/5

\*Morrisons Fruity Greens Smoothie, 750ml, £1.95

Contains: apple, pear, lime, courgette and lemon with apple, mango, pear, kiwi, cucumber and kale puree.

The pea-green drink had a decent consistency. It didn’t have any smell at all. It tasted far too sweet, but not of any fruit in particular (I didn’t know that courgette was a fruit). It had a medicinal tinge to it. I really didn’t like it.

Pros: consistency.

Cons: taste.

Mark: 1/5
