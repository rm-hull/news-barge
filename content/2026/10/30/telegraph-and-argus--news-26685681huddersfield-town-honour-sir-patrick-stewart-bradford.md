Bradford City’s derby with Huddersfield Town will be a poignant occasion.

Lifelong Town supporter and Star Trek actor Sir Patrick Stewart will be remembered, after his death this week at the age of 86.

And the Bantams and Huddersfield will mourn John Vaughan, who represented both clubs as a goalkeeping coach.

He died earlier this month at the age of 62.

Posting on their official Facebook page, Town wrote: “Tomorrow, we'll join Bradford City in a minute’s applause ahead of kick-off to remember John Vaughan, who sadly passed away earlier this month.

“Representing both Town and Bradford across his coaching career, we'll come together to honour him before the first whistle.

“We'll be wearing black armbands in memory of 'Vaughany' and lifelong Town supporter Sir Patrick Stewart, who sadly passed away on Wednesday.”
