DIFFICULT driving conditions are leading to motorists driving through road closure signs following a serious accident on a main road in North Yorkshire.

Motorists are said to be driving into and through the police road closed signs, lights and cones due to foggy conditions on A169 near Low Marishes between Pickering and Malton.

The road is closed due to a previous road traffic collision.

Earlier this evening North Yorkshire Police said: "Emergency services are at the scene of a serious collision on the A169, Malton Road between Pickering and Malton in the area of Marishes.

"The road is closed and motorists are asked to find an alternative route while emergency services work at the scene. "
