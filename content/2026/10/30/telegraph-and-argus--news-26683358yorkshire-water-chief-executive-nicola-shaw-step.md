The chief executive of Yorkshire Water is set to step down following four-and-a-half years leading the company, hours after regulators handed the utility company a poor one-star environmental rating.

Nicola Shaw will leave her role at the end of the year, with Nicola Medalova appointed as chief executive designate ahead of taking over on January 1, 2027.

Yorkshire Water said Ms Medalova will join the company on November 2 and work alongside Ms Shaw during a structured handover period to ensure a smooth transition.

## Leadership change at critical time

The announcement comes as Yorkshire Water faces increased scrutiny over its environmental performance.

A joint report published this week by water regulators found environmental performance across the sector had fallen to its lowest level on record, with Yorkshire Water among four companies to receive a one-star rating, classed as "poor".

The watchdogs found eight of England's nine water companies were either rated "poor" or "requiring improvement", with only Severn Trent achieving the highest rating.

The report found there were 2,666 pollution incidents across England during 2025, with regulators warning that permit breaches and pollution incidents remain a significant concern.

## Chair thanks outgoing chief executive

Yorkshire Water chairman Jon Lewis praised Ms Shaw's contribution to the business during her tenure.

He said the company had secured £500 million in new shareholder equity under her leadership, with a further £600 million committed, helping fund what Yorkshire Water describes as the largest investment programme in its history.

Mr Lewis said Ms Shaw leaves the business with strong foundations for future investment and improvement.

He also described Ms Medalova as an accomplished leader with significant experience delivering major infrastructure projects and improving customer services in complex organisations.

## Who is Nicola Medalova?

Ms Medalova joins Yorkshire Water from National Grid, where she most recently served as chief of staff to the group chief executive.

She previously worked as chief operating officer for National Grid New England, overseeing a 4,000-person operation across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont and Quebec.

Yorkshire Water said her experience spans operations, regulation, environmental management, customer service and major capital investment programmes.

She has also previously served as a non-executive director on Yorkshire Water's board.

## 'Huge challenges' ahead

Announcing her appointment, Ms Medalova said she was excited to lead the company through its next chapter but acknowledged the challenges facing the sector.

She said her immediate priority would be listening and learning while maintaining a strong focus on delivering for customers, communities and the environment.

Meanwhile, Ms Shaw said it had been a privilege to serve Yorkshire and thanked staff across the business for their dedication and commitment.

She said the move felt like the right time to begin an orderly transition to the company's next chapter.

## Bonuses ban after poor rating

Under rules introduced last year, Yorkshire Water's one-star rating means the company will automatically be blocked from paying executive bonuses.

The Government has also summoned water company bosses to explain their performance and outline how they plan to improve services, reduce pollution incidents and tackle leaks.

Yorkshire Water says its next phase will focus on improving customer service, reducing pollution and strengthening the resilience of the region's water network through major investment.
