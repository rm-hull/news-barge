Glasgow Prestwick is to host a Christmas market inside its main terminal building.

The transport hub, which is owned by the **Scottish Government**, has confirmed that it will host the event on Saturday, December 5.

According to flight scheduling data, Ryanair is due to operate two flights that day from the airport to Lanzarote (Arrecife) and Tenerife (South).

A post on the airport's official Facebook page announcing the event read: "Christmas is coming to Glasgow Prestwick Airport!

"We're looking forward to hosting our Christmas Fair on Saturday, 5 December 2026, with plenty of festive shopping and entertainment for everyone to enjoy!

**READ MORE:**

"From Christmas gifts and handmade crafts to festive treats, we're bringing together a fantastic selection of local traders for a day filled with Christmas cheer.

"Whether you're looking to tick a few presents off your list, support local businesses or enjoy a day out with family and friends, there'll be plenty to look forward to."

News that Prestwick will host a Christmas Market comes after the airport took to Facebook to invite airlines such as easyJet and Ryanair to **reinstate a long-lost connection** to London.

The social media appeal was **met with criticism from the Scottish Conservatives**, who called on the** SNP** to intervene and support the return of domestic air links.

**Scottish Conservative** transport spokesman, Tim Eagle MSP, told The Herald: “It says a lot that an airport owned by the SNP Government is having to take to Facebook to ask airlines to restore a London route.

"Prestwick should be benefiting from a proper strategy to connect Scotland to the rest of the UK, not having to beg airlines to fill the gaps left by SNP ministers".
