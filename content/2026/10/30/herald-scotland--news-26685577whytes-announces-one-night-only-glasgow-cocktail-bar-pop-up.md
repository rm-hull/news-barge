A cult London restaurant is bringing its international pop-up tour to Glasgow next month, with a menu that puts its own spin on Scottish cuisine.

Chef Whyte Rushen and restaurateur Livi Akers will return to the city for a one-night takeover at Next Door in Strathbungo on Thursday, November 5.

The pair are behind Whyte’s, the former Hackney restaurant which attracted rave reviews and a Michelin Guide listing before closing its permanent site in 2025 to focus on a series of international pop-ups and residencies.

The Glasgow event follows a previous visit last year and comes as part of a wider tour which has seen the team travel to 35 cities and host more than 55 events over the past 18 months.

Rather than serving the same dishes at each stop, Whyte’s creates menus inspired by the produce, flavours and food traditions of each destination.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21653024.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The team behind Whyte's creates new menus for each pop-up destination *(Image: Supplied)*

For the latest Glasgow pop-up, the team has turned its attention to Scottish ingredients.

Among the dishes will be caramelised swede and haggis croquettes served with whisky peppercorn sauce, roasted Orkney scallop with white pudding, confit leek and parsnip, and grilled langoustines paired with house Lorne sausage and lentil butter curry.

An à la carte menu will be served alongside a selection of cocktails created exclusively for the event.

Following the Glasgow pop-up, the Whyte’s team will continue its Scottish tour with stops in Dundee and Edinburgh.

Booking enquiries can be made directly with Next Door via social media, while limited walk-in spaces will be available at the Pollokshaws Road venue on the night.
