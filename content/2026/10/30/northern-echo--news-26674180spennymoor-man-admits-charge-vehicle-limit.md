A Spennymoor man has been ordered to pay a fine and costs of more than £400 after admitting being in charge of a vehicle while over the alcohol limit.

Martin Bear, of North Road, Spennymoor, appeared before Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 26.

The offence happened on Belle Vue Villas, Spennymoor, on August 15.

The court heard Bear was in charge of a silver Ford Transit Connect after consuming alcohol.

A breath test gave a reading of 118 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath.

Bear, 59, pleaded guilty on October 26 and his guilty plea was taken into account.

Magistrates fined him £230 and ordered him to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £92 surcharge.

His driving record was endorsed with 10 penalty points.

The court made a collection order.
