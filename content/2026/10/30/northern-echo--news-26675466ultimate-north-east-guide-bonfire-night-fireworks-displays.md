Whether you're looking for a free community event close to home, a traditional bonfire night, or a ticketed fireworks extravaganza featuring lasers, special effects and pyromusical displays, organisers across the North East are preparing for one of the busiest fireworks weekends of the year.

Here's your guide to every major display taking place across the region this year.

### Teesside

Sunday, November 1- Hartlepool, Seaton Carew promenade

* Time: 2pm-7pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Entertainment, children’s fairground, food stalls and a music-set finale.

Sunday, November 1- Stockton Fireworks, Stockton Riverside

* Time: Funfair opens 12pm, Fireworks 6.30pm, event ends 6.50pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Funfair, live music, food and drink stalls, and entertainment across the town centre.

Wednesday, November 4- Yarm Rugby Club Fireworks Night

* Time: Gates 5.30pm, fireworks 6.45pm
* Ticket prices: Adults £7, children £4, family £17.50
* What’s on: Food and entertainment before the display.

Thursday, November 5- Acklam Rugby Club Fireworks Display

* Time: Gates 5pm, fireworks 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £6, children £4, family £20
* What’s on: Firework display.

Thursday, November 5- Old Boys Fireworks, Hartlepool Rugby Club

* Time: Gates 5.30pm, fireworks 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £2, under-16s £1, family of four £5
* What’s on: Family evening with fireworks and community entertainment.

Thursday, November 5- Middlesbrough Rugby Club Fireworks

* Time: Gates open 6.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £8 in advance or £12 on the day, children £5 in advance or £7 on the day
* What’s on: Firework display at Easington Road.

Thursday, November 5- Saltburn Cricket Club

* Time: 8pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £6, children £4, toddlers £1
* What’s on: Fireworks, live music, food vendors and a bar.

Thursday, November 5- Billingham Town Fireworks Night

* Time: Gates open 5:30pm, display begins 7:30pm.
* Ticket prices: £6
* What’s on: Fireworks, free football activities for children, cross bar challenges.

Friday, November 6- Nunthorpe and Marton Recreational Club

* Time: 6.30pm.
* Ticket prices: £6 per person, under-threes free
* What’s on: Fireworks, fairground rides, food stalls and a bar.

Saturday, November 7- Brass Castle Country House Fireworks, Nunthorpe

* Time: 4.30pm to 10pm.
* Ticket prices: £7
* What’s on: Fireworks, food, disco, dancing, children’s stalls and a licensed bar.

Saturday, November 7- Guisborough Cricket Club

* Time: Gates open 5pm, fireworks 7.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £7, children £5, under-fives free
* What’s on: Fairground rides, food, disco, bars and free face painting.

Saturday, November 7- Redcar Rugby Club Fireworks

* Time: Gates open 5:30pm, fireworks 7.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £7, children £4, under-fours free
* What’s on: Funfair rides, food vendors, children's activities and a bar.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20152661.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MARLON BONILLA)*

### Southern County Durham

Saturday, October 31- Kynren, Bishop Auckland

* Time: Show begins 7pm.
* Ticket prices: From £11 for under-18s and £14 for adults
* What’s on: A 25-minute pyromusical, fire pits, food and Halloween touches.

Thursday, November 5 & Saturday, November 7- Hardwick Hall Fireworks Extravaganza, Sedgefield

* Time: 5pm to 8.30pm.
* Ticket prices: £7, VIP benches £75, car parking £3.50
* What’s on: Bonfire, fireworks, rides and street food.

Thursday, November 5- Spennymoor Fireworks Display

* What time: 5pm to 8pm.
* Ticket prices: Free, but tickets are required
* What’s on: Live performances, food, rides, a flame show and fireworks.

Friday, November 6- Fantastic Fireworks and Bonfire Brandon, Brandon Sports Club

* Time: Fairground opens 4pm, fireworks 7.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £3, children and pensioners £2, pushchairs free
* What’s on: Fairground and fireworks.

Friday, November 6- Barnard Castle Bonfire Night, The Witham, Barnard Castle

* Time: Opens 6pm, bonfire 6.30pm, fireworks 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Advance tickets £6 adults, £4 children, £15 family. Prices will be higher on the gate.
* What’s on: A 15-minute display with more than 2,000 effects.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20149677.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

### Northern County Durham and Durham City

Sunday, November 1- Fireworks by the River, Durham Amateur Rowing Club

* What time: Gates open 5pm, display 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £4.50, children aged four to 12 £3.50, under-fours free
* What’s on: Fireworks by the Wear in Durham.

Sunday, November 1-November 4- Firework Extravaganza at Beamish Hall

* Time: Arrival 6pm, fireworks 7.30pm.
* Ticket prices: From £45.95 per car, complimentary for hotel guests.
* What’s on: Fireworks for visitors arriving by car.

Thursday, November 5- Derwent Manor Bonfire Night, Derwent Manor, Allensford

* Time: 6pm to 10pm.
* Ticket prices: £20 per car
* What’s on: Bonfire, fireworks, food, drinks and marshmallows.

Thursday, November 5 & Saturday, November 7- The Big Blaze, Lintz Hall Farm, Burnopfield

* Time: 4pm to 11pm.
* Ticket prices: £13 to £15
* What’s on: Bonfire, fireworks, fire and angle-grinding show, and fairground.

Saturday, November 7- Stanley Fireworks, Oakey’s Park, StanleyTime:

* Entry 5pm, fireworks 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Live music and fireworks set to music.

Saturday, November 7- Blanchland Village Hall Bonfire Night Celebration, Blanchland

* Time: Doors open 5pm, bonfire lit at 6.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Hot drinks, licensed bar, sweets and tombola.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20152701.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MARLON BONILLA)*

### East Durham and Sunderland

Friday, October 30- Sunderland Spooktacular, Barnes Park, Sunderland

* Time: Entry 4pm, low-noise display 6pm, pyromusical 8pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £8, children £6
* What’s on: Low-noise fireworks, a pyromusical, live entertainment and Halloween activities.

Sunday, November 1- Seaham Fireworks, Seaham Harbour Marina

* Time: 5pm to 9pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Angels versus Demons theme, food and craft stalls, rides, stage entertainment and symphonic fireworks.

Wednesday, November 4 to Friday, November 6- Firework & Bonfire Spectacular, Rainton Arena, Houghton-le-Spring

* Time: 4pm to 9pm each day.
* Ticket prices: £5, under-twos free
* What’s on: Music, DJs, food, rides, stalls, bars, bonfire and fireworks.

Wednesday, November 4- Ashbrooke Sports Club Fireworks, Sunderland

* Time: Gates open 4.30pm, fireworks 8pm.
* Ticket prices: General admission £7.50, VIP £25, under-twos free
* What’s on: Outdoor display, VIP ticket includes a drink and hot buffet.

Saturday, November 7- Durham City Cricket Club Fireworks Spectacular, Riverside Stadium, Chester-le-Street

* Time: Gates open 4.30pm, fireworks 7.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £11, children £7, family £29
* What’s on: Live music, rides, fireworks and a laser show.

Sunday, November 8- Peterlee & Horden RFC Fireworks Night, Peterlee and Horden Rugby Club

* Time: Doors open 4.30pm, fireworks 6.30pm.
* Ticket prices: £4
* What’s on: Food, drinks and family entertainment.

### Newcastle, Gateshead and Tyneside

Sunday, November 1- South Shields Firework Display, Ocean Beach

* Time: Displays at 6pm and 8pm, attractions open until 10pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Rides and attractions from midday until 10pm.

Sunday, November 1- Gateshead Rugby Club Fireworks

* Time: Gates open 4.30pm, fireworks at 6.30pm.
* Ticket prices: £7, group ticket £25, under-fives free.

Thursday, November 5- Blue Flames Fireworks Display, Blue Flames Sporting Club, Benton

* Time: Gates open 5.30pm, display 7.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £10, children £7
* What’s on: Rides, food stalls, vendors, characters and three indoor bars.

Friday, November 6- Friday Floodlit Firework and Funfair Racenight, Newcastle Racecourse

* Time: Opens 1pm.
* Ticket prices: Under-18s free with an adult, adults £21-29
* What’s on: Racing, rides, character appearances, family entertainment and a fireworks and laser finale.

Friday, November 6- Barmoor Hub Fireworks, Barmoor Field, Ryton

* Time: Fairground from 4pm, fireworks 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Fairground, food and drink stalls.

Friday, November 6- Rutherford AFC Fireworks, Lobley Hill

* Time: Doors open 6pm, fireworks 8.30pm.
* Ticket prices: Adult £12, children £9, under-twos free
* What’s on: “The Greatest Showman” theme, fancy dress, food, bars, face painting and tombola.

Saturday, November 7- Gosforth Fireworks Extravaganza, Broadway West Playing Fields, Gosforth

* Time: Doors open 5pm, fireworks 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £8.50, children £6.50
* What’s on: Music, fire performers, character appearances, food, rides and more than 120 effects per minute during the display.

Saturday, November 7- Bonfire and Firework Extravaganza, Blaydon Rugby Club, Blaydon

* Time: 4pm to 9pm.
* Ticket prices: Adults £9, children £5.70, family £25
* What’s on: Pyro-musical display and gourmet food village.

Saturday, November 7- Gateshead Stadium Firework Frenzy, Gateshead International Stadium

* Time: Doors open 5pm, fireworks 8.30pm.
* Ticket prices: £6
* What’s on: Fairground, local performers, tribute acts and live music.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/16581863.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

### North Yorkshire

Thursday, November 5- Richmond Fireworks, Richmond School

* Time: Gates open 6pm, display 7pm.
* Ticket prices: Free, donations encouraged

Thursday, November 5- Fireworks at the Farm, Monk Park Farm, near Bagby

* Time: 5pm to 8pm.
* Ticket prices: £12, under-ones £3
* What’s on: Bonfire, low-bang Disney fireworks, games, marshmallows, food, bar and animal barn.

Thursday, November 5- Northallerton's bonfire and fireworks, Applegarth car park, Northallerton

* Time: To be announced.
* Ticket prices: Free
* What’s on: Town council bonfire and fireworks display.
