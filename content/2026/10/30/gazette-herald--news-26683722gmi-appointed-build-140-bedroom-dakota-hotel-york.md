A new luxury hotel in York has moved a step closer to reality.

Leeds-based GMI Construction Group has been appointed as the principal contractor for the latest Dakota hotel in the city centre, which will become the sixth Dakota hotel to be delivered by GMI.

The 140-bedroom hotel, delivered on behalf of Evans Property Group, owner of the Dakota brand, will be built within the medieval city walls in York’s historic heart.

This follows GMI’s successful delivery of Dakota hotels in Manchester city centre, Manchester Airport, Leeds, Glasgow, and Newcastle Quayside.

Ed Weston, regional director for Yorkshire at GMI, said: "We are delighted to be continuing our long-standing relationship with Evans Property Group and the Dakota brand on a sixth hotel development.

"This is a highly prominent and constrained city-centre site, surrounded by neighbouring buildings and shaped by York’s historic character.

"With the demolition phase now complete, our focus is on progressing the new hotel.

"We will bring our understanding of the Dakota brand and the experience gained across the previous five hotels to deliver a distinctive new destination for York."

The hotel will feature Dakota’s signature brasserie-style grill, centred around a feature bar.

It will combine the brand’s industrial-inspired design with red-brick façades, bronze-effect detailing, and a courtyard.

Guest access will be available from both Rougier Street and Tanners Moat, with terraces offering panoramic views across the city.

The project aims to achieve BREEAM Excellent certification.

It will incorporate air source heat pumps, electric vehicle charging points, and other sustainable travel facilities.

The main construction phase is now underway following the completion of demolition work.

The wider project team includes KPP Architects, structural engineer JPG Group, planning consultant O’Neill Associates, heritage specialist Montagu Evans, employer’s agent Rex Proctor & Partners, and commercial property adviser CBRE.

Marc Banks, development director at Evans Property Group, said: "This development represents the next chapter for the Dakota brand and a significant investment in one of the country's most popular and historic destinations.

"Our ambition has always been to create a hotel that is unmistakably Dakota while responding positively to the character and heritage of York.

"We believe the finished development will become a distinctive new addition to the city's hospitality sector."

The hotel is planned to complete in spring 2028.
