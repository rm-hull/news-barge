A CHARITY has donated £100,000 to support the creation of a new family facility at a hospital.

Kashmir Orphan Relief Trust (KORT) has contributed the sum to the Bradford Hospitals Charity’s Home from Home Appeal, which will help fund the creation of a dedicated space for families of newborns receiving care at Bradford Royal Infirmary’s Neonatal Intensive Care Unit.

Ch. Mohammed Akhtar, founder and chairman of KORT, said: "The people of Bradford have supported KORT from the very beginning and we have the opportunity to give something back.

"The Home from Home Appeal will make a real difference to families facing incredibly difficult circumstances, helping parents stay close to their babies when they need each other most.

"We are proud to support such an important project for our city."

The Home from Home facility will include en-suite bedrooms, a kitchen, dining room, living area, laundry facilities, and a private garden and play area.

It will support 500 families each year by offering a comfortable environment close to the neonatal unit during what are often lengthy and stressful hospital stays.

The £100,000 donation brings the total raised for the appeal to £2.6 million, moving the project closer to its £3.4 million target.
