Police are looking into reports of modern slavery after officers descended on a North East pub and a cordon was put in place yesterday (October 29).

Vehicles and officers from Northumbria Police were spotted inside the old Mountain Daisy pub on Hylton Road, Sunderland.

During the incident, a police cordon was put in place and residents reported seeing blue lights and police vehicles in the area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646487.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MC Turtle)*

A spokesperson for the police force said: "As part of an ongoing investigation, we are carrying out planned activity at a premises on Hylton Road in the Millfield area of Sunderland.

"This is in relation to concerns about modern slavery. There is no wider risk to the public and investigations are ongoing.

""Members of the public can also get in touch by sending a direct message on social media, or by using live chat and report forms on the Force’s website.

"Those unable to make contact in these ways can call 101."

The road has since reopened to traffic.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646485.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police were carrying out planned activity at a premises on Hylton Road in the Millfield area of Sunderland when a gas leak was discovered at the property. *(Image: MC Turtle)*

Dozens of police officers could be seen inside the pub, while a number of police vehicles were stationed outside.

While police were at the scene, a gas leak was identified.

Northern Gas Networks said it was contacted by police at 8am.

A spokeswoman for Northern Gas Networks said: "Our teams responded to a call at 8am this morning (29 Oct) from the Police regarding a potential gas escape at a property they were attending in the Hylton Road area of Sunderland."
