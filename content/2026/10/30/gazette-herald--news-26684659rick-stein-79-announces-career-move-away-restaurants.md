Celebrity chef Rick Stein has announced a career move away from his restaurants this week.

The 79-year-old restaurateur and television presenter has turned his attention to writing with the release of a new cookbook, Rick Stein’s Cookery Course, on Thursday, October 29.

Mr Stein's latest book draws on 60 years of cooking and is described by publisher Penguin as his most extensive book to date.

The 432-page book features 150 classic recipes alongside photographed step-by-step techniques aimed at teaching readers the basics of cooking.

Its chapters focus on different ingredients, including eggs and dairy, vegetables, fish and shellfish, meat and poultry, as well as rice and grains.

Mr Stein also includes personal anecdotes and tips gathered over his long career in professional kitchens.

The publication marks another chapter in a career which has taken Mr Stein from rural Oxfordshire to becoming one of Britain's best-known chefs.

He was born and brought up on a farm in Churchill, near Chipping Norton, before later studying English at New College, Oxford.

Mr Stein graduated from Oxford University in 1971 before moving to Padstow in Cornwall, where he went on to establish the restaurant career which made him famous.

He opened The Seafood Restaurant in Padstow in 1975 and has since built a food business encompassing restaurants and other ventures, while becoming a familiar face on television and publishing numerous cookbooks.

His previous books include French Odyssey, Rick Stein's India, Rick Stein's Long Weekends and Rick Stein's Christmas.

Rick Stein’s Cookery Course has a recommended price of £30 and is available in hardback and ebook formats.
