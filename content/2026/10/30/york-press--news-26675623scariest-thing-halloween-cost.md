## The scariest thing about Halloween is the cost of it

An animated display of witches on sale in a store

I’M A big fan of Halloween. I love seeing pumpkins glowing on gateposts, windows draped with cobwebs and bats hanging from porches. I miss the parties we used to have when my daughters were growing up – we would transform the garden into a ghostly playground, with all manner of spooky objects emerging from hedges, bushes and trees.
