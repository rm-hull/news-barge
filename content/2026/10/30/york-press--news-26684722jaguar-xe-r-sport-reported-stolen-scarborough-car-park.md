## High-value car stolen from North Yorkshire car park

Police have launched an investigation after a Jaguar XE R-Sport has been reported stolen from a car park in Scarborough *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A Jaguar XE R-Sport has been reported stolen from a car park in North Yorkshire.

The high-value black vehicle was allegedly stolen from Cunsey Gate Car Park in Rainscliffe Woods, Scarborough between 11am and 11.30am on October 22.

It carries the registration number MX66WBY.




![The car carries the registration plate MX66WBY](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21651234.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The car carries the registration plate MX66WBY *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please email PC Sander on [mickey.sander@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:mickey.sander@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”

Alternatively, please call 101 or Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 if you would like to remain anonymous.

Please quote reference 12260207707 when providing information.
