Birds are being temporarily rehomed while their aviary undergoes a full survey and options appraisal.

Bradford Council is moving the residents of Myrtle Park aviary in Bingley to larger facilities at Cliffe Castle in Keighley to ensure their welfare during the winter months.

Councillor Ian Walker, executive member for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "The welfare of the birds is our primary concern and that is why we have taken the decision to temporarily relocate them to the larger facilities at Cliffe Castle.

"This move will ensure they are cared for in the best possible environment during the winter months while we carry out the detailed work needed to understand the condition of the aviary and the options available for its future."

The council says Myrtle Park aviary is an ageing structure and no longer provides the optimum living environment for the birds.

It adds that significant upgrades are required before it could reopen as an aviary, including the installation of electricity and lighting, alongside repairs and improvements to the building.

No formal decision has been made on the future of the aviary.

Cllr Walker said: "We commit to looking at options and working with local community groups to explore any support they could provide to allow the birds to return to upgraded facilities in the future."
