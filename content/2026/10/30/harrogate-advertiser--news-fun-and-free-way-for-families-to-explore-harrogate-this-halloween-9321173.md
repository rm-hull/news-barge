# Harrogate's Halloween Trail will invite families to uncover magical ingredients this weekend

## “The Halloween Potion Trail is a fun and free way for families to explore Harrogate”

Fans of Halloween in Harrogate are being invited this weekend to spot trail clues and collect the secret ingredients to create heir very own imaginary potion in a free fun event.

![The Harrogate Halloween Potion Trail will run until Sunday, November 1, taking participants on a journey to ten locations across the town centre. ](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/30/17/55/trick-and-treat.jpeg?trim=1368,0,648,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Presented by Harrogate Business Improvement District (BID), the Harrogate Halloween Potion Trail will run until Sunday, November 1, taking participants on a journey to ten locations across the town centre.

At each stop, participants will need to find a window sticker and record the Harrogate-themed potion ingredient it reveals.

Once all ten ingredients have been collected, completed trails can be returned to The Crown Hotel to claim a prize.

**Harrogate Halloween Potion Trail participating locationsThe Crown Hotel, Crown Place.Starling, Oxford Street.Søstrene Grene, Cambridge Street.Verity Frearson, Albert Street.Tap on Tower Street.Mama Doreen’s, Station Square.On the Rhodes at the Victoria Shopping Centre Piazza.Althams Travel, Cambridge Street.Harrogate Theatre, Oxford Street.Harrogate Town AFC Shop, Commercial Street.**

Generously sponsored by Harrogate’s Crown Hotel, the trail is designed to encourage residents and visitors to explore different parts of Harrogate town centre while supporting local businesses during the half-term break.

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said: “The Halloween Potion Trail is a fun and free way for families to explore the town, discover local businesses and enjoy a little Halloween magic along the way.

“We hope plenty of curious potion seekers will pick up a map, hunt down all ten Harrogate-themed ingredients and create an imaginary potion of their own.”

Dan Siddle, General Manager of the Crown Hotel said: "As one of Harrogate's most historic hotels, we love finding new ways to engage with the community throughout the year, and the Halloween Trail has become a wonderful way to welcome people into the hotel and showcase the spirit of the town.”
