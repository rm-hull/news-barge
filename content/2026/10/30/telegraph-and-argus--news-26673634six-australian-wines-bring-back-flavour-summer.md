AFTER a long, extended summer, if the balmy temperatures are starting to feel like a distant memory, and you’re after a fruit-forward wine that’s delicious from word go, you can’t beat a top drop from Down Under to put you in a sunny mood.

And if the classics such as a chardonnay, sauvignon blanc, cabernet sauvignon and shiraz come to mind, Australian winemakers have nailed these popular grape varieties - and put their own spin on wines we sometimes associate with vineyards closer to home.

Moreover, if you’re thinking about stocking up the wine rack with something a little bit special, we’ve rounded up an iconic Australian red for good measure.

Here’s what’s sparking a smile and worth seeking out.

1. Sainsbury’s Western Australian Sauvignon Blanc 2023, £9.50, Sainsbury’s

A suave sauvignon blanc to rival a French Sancerre, a minerally freshness elevates the flavours and vibrancy. Expressive and zingy with attractive tropical fruit, gooseberry and herbaceous notes, similar flavours unfold with a touch of guava and peach, offset by a fine line of citrusy acidity and crisp, refreshing finish.

2. Wildflower Chardonnay 2023, Western Australia, £13.49 (was £16.99), Ellie’s Cellar

With a label to love and bouquet of blooms to mirror the spring wildflowers the region’s famous for, this is a fresh, balanced, elegant chardonnay - and a glass which is hard to put down. With a gorgeous nose of buttercups and white peaches, followed by ripe stone fruits with peachy accents, a pleasant roundness from gentle oak ageing, there’s a lovely freshness on the finish.

3. Robert Oatley Finisterre Cabernet Sauvignon 2019, Margaret River, Australia, £12.25 (was £16.50), Ocado

A broad-shouldered cab sav, a rhapsody of black forest fruits laced with cassis and a hint of menthol waft from the glass, giving way to baskets of ripe brambly fruit, bolstered by enticing leafy nuances, hint of liquorice, black pepper and sweet spice. Silky smooth with a generous profile and long lingering finish, it’s a beautiful expression from a very good vintage and award-winning winery.

4. Wirra Wirra Church Block 2022, McLaren Vale, Australia, £16, Waitrose Cellar

Offering the best of three worlds - cabernet sauvignon, shiraz and merlot - this is a deftly blended Aussie classic from another highly respected, trophy winning producer, best known for its Church Block blends. Seductive and completely delicious from word go, it’s aromatic with characteristic blackberry leaf, bushy notes, plummy tones with hints of herbs and violets, supported by the pure succulence of shiraz and mixed berries galore. Dangerously easy to drink.

5. The Black Pig The Prize Shiraz 2023, McLaren Vale, Australia, £19.99, Virgin Wines

A multi-awarding, top drop from the same region, this is a rich, smooth shiraz to swoon over. Perfumed and resplendent with broad flavours of ripe black fruits, loads of cassis, violets, alongside peppery spice, with a well-cushioned, concentrated palate, finishing very long and velvety smooth. Vivid and memorable, it’s the prize in the winemaker’s portfolio.

6. Penfolds Bin 28 2024, South Australia, £36 (£31 mix six), Majestic

A class act from Australia’s most iconic winery, Penfolds Bin 28 is cited by many wine critics as the label which put Australian shiraz on the map - and one of their more affordable wines. First made in 1959, it pays homage to warm climate Australian shiraz; made from multi-region fruit, with grapes from famed Barossa Valley at its heart. Intense on the nose with complex aromas of baking spice, blackberry and mulberry aromas; on the palate, black and blue fruits combine with soft spice, liquorice, and hint of cocoa, lending layers of flavour, finishing long and beautifully balanced. Worth seeking out.
