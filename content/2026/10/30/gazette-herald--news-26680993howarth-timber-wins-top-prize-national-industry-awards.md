A York timber firm has been named Timber Merchant of the Year at the Builders’ Merchants Journal (BMJ) Awards.

Howarth Timber received the award, while also winning Apprentice/Trainee of the Year for Bethanie Jarvis-Green and being highly commended in the E-Commerce Award of the Year category.

The awards recognise the company’s continued commitment across the business, from its work supporting and developing employees to its approach to e-commerce and digital development.

Gavin Knowles, head of marketing and digital at Howarth Timber, said: "We’re incredibly proud to have been named Timber Merchant of the Year.

"This recognition reflects the hard work and dedication of our teams across the business and our continued commitment to delivering for our customers.

"Being Highly Commended in the E-Commerce Award of the Year category is fantastic recognition of the work we’ve been doing to strengthen our digital offering and improve the customer experience."
