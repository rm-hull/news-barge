PADDY McGuinness is hitting the road with a brand new UK tour.

The comedian and presenter will bring his latest show, Blast from the Past, to Scarborough Spa Grand Hall on Friday, October 29, 2027, as part of a 33-date tour.

Mr McGuinness said: "This new tour is all about nostalgia – the telly, the tunes and the daft little things that instantly take you back.

"This is me doing what I love: making people laugh!

"It’ll be a big, funny warm trip down memory lane, but with plenty of modern life woes along the way.

"Grab your rose tinted spectacles and a ticket!"

The show promises a night of stand-up comedy mixed with Paddy’s signature humour and reflections on pop culture, music, and television.

It follows the success of his Nearly There tour, which sold out more than 50 dates across the UK and Ireland.

Mr McGuinness is best known for hosting ITV’s Take Me Out, BBC’s Top Gear, and raising £10.5 million for BBC Children in Need.

He continues to host his Sunday morning show on BBC Radio 2 and co-presents the podcast The Good, The Bad and The Football.

Tickets go on general sale at 10am on Friday, November 6.
