## Our live feed has now finished.

* Motorists are being warned of a crash on a main road in North Yorkshire.
* The A64 is blocked both ways along a stretch of Scarborough Road near Scampston on Friday (October 30).
* Drivers are being reminded of severe delays along the road.

## Person helped out of vehicle by paramedics after crash

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service says one person was helped out of a vehicle after the crash.

The fire service says the crash involved two vehicles and two people involved in the crash were out of their vehicle when the crews arrived shortly after 3.30pm.

In a statement, the fire service says: "Crews from Sherburn and Scarborough responded to a two-vehicle road traffic collision.

"Two occupants were out on fire service arrival, one was assisted out the vehicle by paramedics.

"Fire crews made the vehicles safe using small tools and left the incident with the police on scene."

## Road likely to be blocked for next hour, police say

North Yorkshire Police says the A64 is likely to be blocked for the next hour.

In an update at 4.30pm, a force spokesperson says: "The A64 is currently blocked near Rillington in the Malton area due to a collision.

"Please avoid the area at this time while emergency services work at the scene.

"Thank you for your patience, we hope to have the road clear in the next hour subject to vehicle recovery."

The road remains blocked as of 4.30pm with lengthy traffic queues. Motorists are being advised to avoid the area if at all possible.

## The Press has contacted emergency services

The Press has contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Services, the fire service and police for further updates.

In the meantime, you can send news where you are to [newsdesk@thepress.co.uk](mailto:newsdesk@thepress.co.uk)

## Severe delays on nearby roads

The crash is currently affecting the A64 Scarborough Road from Scagglethorpe to the road's junction with Malton Road.

A spokesperson from AA Traffic said: "Road blocked and queueing traffic due to accident on A64 Both ways from Sands Lane to Scampston turn."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21652951.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: AA Traffic)*

## 'Traffic is chaotic'

An eye-witness said he had been diverted from the A64 to the A169 due to the incident near Rillington and was now stuck in queuing traffic.

He added: " I can see a number of blue lights in the distance and the traffic is chaotic.

"It must be a bad crash as everyone is being turned round now and diverted via Kirkby Misperton."
