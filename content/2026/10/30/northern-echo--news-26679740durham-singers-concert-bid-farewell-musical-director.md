A concert celebrating the many moods of the sea will mark a fond farewell to a choir’s musical director after four years at the helm in Durham.

The Durham Singers will perform at St Oswald's Church in Durham on November 21 in tribute to Tom Edney, who is leaving the area.

Ruth Durbridge, chair of DurhamSingers, said: "We will really miss Tom's inspiring leadership, innovative programming and generous-spirited sharing of his love of music with singers and audiences alike."

The programme features Edward Elgar's Sea Pictures, alongside the dramatic setting by Samuel Taylor-Coleridge of Robert Buchanan's Meg Blane, the story of a woman leading a shipwreck mission.

Soprano soloist Katy Thompson will perform nautical-themed songs, including Rebecca Clarke's The Seal Man.

The concert begins at 7.30pm.

Tickets cost £15 for general admission, £10 for students and are free for under-18s.
