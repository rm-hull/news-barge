Calling all lovers of vinyl – a huge record fair is returning to York this weekend.

York Record Fair is back at York Racecourse on **Sunday 1 November**, and its bigger and beter than ever.

With over 70 top sellers and 220 tables – and about 200,000 records – there’ll be something for everyone: rock ‘n’ roll, soul, punk, prog, indie, reggae, metal, electronic, jazz, classical and much more.

In addition to mostly vinyl records, there will also be some collectable CDs and 78s, music-related books, ephemera and posters.

For 2026, the event will take place across two floors in the Knavesmire Stand exhibition space, making it the biggest record fair in the UK this year.

Since its inaugural event in 2022, York Record Fair has become a staple bi-annual destination for vinyl collectors. Stalls will include rare first pressings, as well as recent releases of current music – and of course lots of bargains!

Admission into York Record Fair is £3 on the door, with under 16s going free. Happy hunting!
