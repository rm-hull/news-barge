## Two North Yorkshire restaurants go on the market

A waiter carves a rump steak *(Image: NQ)*

Two well-known steak houses in York and Selby have gone on the market.

The Caramba steakhouse in York closed earlier this year but the Selby restaurant is understood to be still operating.

The sale follows the previous owners of the restaurants - AD Restaurants Ltd - going into administration on July 30.

Administrators BTG were able to transfer the ownership of the Selby restaurant to a new company called JA Restaurants.

However, the York restaurant closed earlier in the summer and remains closed.

In August, BTG said: “The sale of the Selby restaurant’s assets to a new company, which is now exploring options for a future trade at new premises, will enable that part of the business to continue trading, saving three jobs.”

A listing on RightBiz says both branded restaurants are available for leasehold at £164,900.

They have a turnover of £1,040,000 a year or £20,000 a week between them.

The York venue is a 100 cover, purpose-built restaurant of around 358m2 “within a huge retail and leisure park with a colossal amount of footfall.”

RightBiz has that venue listed separately as well for £94,950 for the leasehold.

The Selby restaurant in Brook Street has 70 covers and covers 230m2 in a “rapidly expanding” and “charming” market town.

The listing on RightBiz said: “Each restaurant has an annual turnover in excess of £500,000 and each are being offered individually or as a group. The sale of the group would be at a much discounted asking price and would include the trademarked brand (if required) and all intellectual property, websites, social media and reviews.”

As The Press previously reported, both the York and Selby restaurants had received rave reviews online.

York received 4.5 stars out of five on Google and 4.4 stars out of five on Tripadvisor.

Selby also received 4.5 stars on Google and 4.4 stars out of five on Tripadvisor, ranking it 7th out of 102 Selby restaurants.
