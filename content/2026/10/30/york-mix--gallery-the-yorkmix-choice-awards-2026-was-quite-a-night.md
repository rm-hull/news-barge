Well, that was a blast.

York and North Yorkshire came together at The Milner Hotel last night to celebrate our amazing businesses at the **YorkMix Choice Awards 2026**.

And it turned into a proper knees-up!

We were thoroughly entertained by Belle Velvet, enjoyed a spectacular dance-off, cheered all our award winners, and then partied into the night.

Thanks to our sponsor, recruitment specialist PJ Staffing, to The Milner York and of course to all our fabulous businesses.

You can find out all our Gold, Silver and Bronze winners here. And enjoy our gallery of the night below.

Same again next year?
