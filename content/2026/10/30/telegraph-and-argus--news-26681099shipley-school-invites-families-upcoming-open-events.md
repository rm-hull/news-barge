FAMILIES are invited to explore a primary school and nursery at two upcoming open events.

High Crags Primary Leadership Academy in Shipley is welcoming prospective Reception pupils and their families to visit the school on Wednesday, November 11 from 4pm to 5pm and Thursday, November 12 from 9.30am to 10.30am.

Visitors will be able to tour the modern facilities, meet staff and pupils, and learn more about the school's curriculum and enrichment activities.

A spokesperson for the school said: "Visitors can explore the school’s modern facilities and discover what makes High Crags Primary Leadership Academy a welcoming place for children to begin their school journey."

The open events will also include information about the school’s 60-place nursery for children aged three and four.

Both the nursery and Early Years settings have been rated 'Outstanding' by Ofsted.

High Crags Primary Leadership Academy is part of Star Academies, one of the UK’s leading education providers.

The deadline to apply for a Reception place for September 2027 is January 15.

Families who cannot attend the open events but are interested in the school can contact High Crags Primary Leadership Academy to arrange a visit by emailing [info@highcrags.staracademies.org](mailto:info@highcrags.staracademies.org) or calling 01274 584 068.
