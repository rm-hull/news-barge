**Pannal Players will stage their final pantomime in February 2027, bringing a 40-year tradition to an end with a production of Snow White.**

The amateur theatre group has announced that its 2027 production will be its last, citing declining audiences, an increasing administrative burden and the fact that few members now live in Pannal itself.

The group’s first pantomime, Aladdin, was performed at Pannal Village Hall in 1987. Since then, Pannal Players has staged a pantomime every year apart from 2021, when the Covid pandemic prevented a production.

Over the decades, the group has performed traditional pantomime favourites including Cinderella and Snow White, alongside less conventional productions based on Toy Story and The Sound of Music.

### Declining audiences and increasing demands

Michael Newby, chair of Pannal Players, said the decision to end the productions had been a reluctant one.

> **Michael Newby, chair of Pannal Players, said:** Although a lot of us have connections to Pannal, very few of us actually live in the village and with declining audiences and an increased administrative burden we have reluctantly decided to call it a day.

The group hopes its final production will attract people who have been involved with Pannal Players over the years, as well as audiences who have supported its performances.

> **Michael Newby, chair of Pannal Players, said:** We hope that this will be a special production for us, and for our audiences. We certainly want to go out with a bang rather than a whimper and hope that many of the people who have been involved in the past will come along to support our final production.

### Final production to support cancer ward charity

Pannal Players has also raised thousands of pounds over the years for the Teenage and Young Persons Cancer Ward at St James’s Hospital, in memory of former member Joanna Luty.

The group hopes to make its final year another significant fundraising effort.

> **Michael Newby, chair of Pannal Players, said:** It is important that we make one last big effort in this our final year.

### Snow White tickets on sale

*Snow White* will run from Wednesday 10 February to Saturday 13 February 2027 at Pannal Village Hall.

Early-bird tickets are on sale for £6 for the Wednesday and Thursday performances and £7 for Friday and Saturday, including the Saturday matinee. These prices are available until 31 December 2026, after which ticket prices may change.

Tickets can be purchased through the Pannal Players Facebook page or online at [https://buytickets.at/pannalplayers/2499936](https://buytickets.at/pannalplayers/2499936).
