A man has been jailed for three years and eight months after carrying out a sustained and violent assault on his former partner at her home in Harrogate.

David Thompson, of The Square, Hillam, was sentenced at court on Thursday 29 October 2026. He was also given a 10-year restraining order to protect the victim.

The assault took place in June 2024, when North Yorkshire Police received a 999 call from a concerned neighbour reporting a serious domestic incident at an address in Harrogate.

The caller described seeing a woman hanging from a window and shouting for help. As the call continued, the situation appeared to escalate, with the victim heard screaming and apparently injured.

Members of the public went to help and saw Thompson leave the address before police arrived. Neighbours reported seeing blood inside the property and the victim covered in blood. She immediately told them she had been assaulted by Thompson.

The court heard that Thompson had arrived at the woman’s home uninvited and forced his way inside by kicking the door. He verbally abused her before launching a sustained attack.

During the assault, Thompson jumped onto a bed and stamped on the victim’s head twice, leaving distinctive shoe marks on her face. He also smashed household items, including a vase and stereo, and used objects from inside the property as weapons.

The victim later told officers she genuinely believed Thompson was going to kill her.

Investigating officers carried out extensive enquiries and gathered evidence that led to Thompson being charged and brought before the courts.

## Police welcome sentence

> **Detective Constable Rebecca Foster, the investigating officer from North Yorkshire Police, said:**
>
> This was an horrific and sustained attack which left the victim with significant injuries and fearing for her life.
>
> Domestic abuse can have a devastating and lasting impact on victims. We would like to commend the bravery of the victim in this case, as well as the members of the public who intervened and contacted police when they realised something was wrong.
>
> We welcome the sentence handed to Thompson and hope it provides the victim with some reassurance as she continues to recover from her ordeal.
>
> If you are experiencing domestic abuse, please know that you are not alone and support is available. We will always take reports seriously and work to protect victims and bring offenders to justice.
