## Acclaimed restaurant in York named finalist in curry house award

Award-winning Bengal Lounge is up for a Best Curry House in York award *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A critically acclaimed restaurant in York has been named a finalist in a prestigious competition to find the city’s best curry house.

Bengal Lounge, in Acomb Wood Shopping Centre, opened in 2019.

The top restaurant has been named as one of the top 10 venues in the Press’ Best Curry House in York competition 2026.




The recognition is Bengal Lounge's second award shortlist in two years.

Speaking to The Press in 2025, owner Riyadh Ali said: “Dishes at Bengal Lounge combine the rich flavours and traditions of Indian and Bangladeshi cuisine, with a fun section in its menu dedicated to ‘Mad Chef Specials’.

“Since opening, we have continually made enhancements to both our menu and the restaurant itself.

“At Bengal Lounge, we believe in keeping things fresh and exciting for our guests, which is why our menu is regularly updated to introduce new and innovative dishes.

“If a customer has a particular dish or flavour combination in mind, our talented chef is always happy to accommodate — using his culinary expertise to create a bespoke, one-of-a-kind dish tailored specifically for that individual.”

Customers can opt for chicken or lamb as their protein of choice, with the specials’ dish No.4, garnished with a “delicately poached egg” and finished with a topping of charred, caramelised and crispy onions.

Riyadh said: “It is an extraordinary dish that perfectly embodies our chef’s passion for innovation – a true must-try for anyone visiting Bengal Lounge.

After picking up the keys in 2019, just before the pandemic, Riyadh and his team of staff have transformed the restaurant with a full refurbishment.

Riyadh has worked in the hospitality industry since he was a teenager, when he worked alongside his family.

Speaking about this, he said: “This early exposure provided me with a strong understanding of how the industry operates, as well as valuable insights into what was lacking in the market.

“At Bengal Lounge, our aim is to provide guests with a warm, welcoming, and memorable dining experience that combines authentic cuisine with genuine hospitality.

“We want every customer to feel valued and comfortable from the moment they walk through our doors.

“Our focus is on offering high-quality, freshly prepared dishes that showcase the rich flavours and traditions of Indian and Bangladeshi cuisine, all served in a relaxed and intimate setting.

“Guests can expect attentive service, a friendly atmosphere, and a commitment to excellence in every aspect of their visit — from the food and presentation to the overall dining environment.”

You can vote for Bengal Lounge by filling out the slip included in each edition of the newspaper.

For more information, please visit bengalloungeyork.com.
