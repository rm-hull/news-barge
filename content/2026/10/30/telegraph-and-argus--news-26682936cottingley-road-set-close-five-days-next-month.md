A ROAD in Cottingley is set to be temporarily closed for electrical works.

March Cote Lane will be shut between numbers 127 and 132.

The closure, for electrical works, will not apply to vehicles used in the works, and pedestrian access will remain.

Alternative routes will be via unaffected sections of March Cote Lane, Airedale Avenue, Ryecroft Avenue, and Coppy Close.

The closure is due to commence at 8am on November 9 and is expected to remain in place until 5pm on November 13.

However, if necessary, the closure could be extended for up to 18 months.

The works are being carried out under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, Section 14.

City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council gave notice of the closure.

The closure is for electrical works proposed to be executed on or near March Cote Lane.

The closure was announced on October 29.
