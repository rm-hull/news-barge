# Four women arrested after goods stolen from M&S store in Yorkshire

## Four women have been arrested after more than £1,500 worth of goods were stolen from an M&S store in Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police said it was contacted shortly after 3.30pm on Thursday (Oct 29) following reports that a ‘significant’ amount of items had been stolen.

During the incident, a member of staff who attempted to stop the woman from making off with the goods was assaulted.

A vehicle believed to be involved in the theft was spotted travelling north on the A1 following extensive searches and was pulled over by a roads policing team.

Four women were inside the vehicle, two who were already wanted on a warrant.

Inside the car, officers found goods worth £1,672.49 which had been stolen from various stores, including M&S.

The four women were all arrested.

![Four women have been arrested after goods were stolen from an M&S store in Harrogate](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/30/12/05/MS-stolen1.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


PC Michelle Telford, from North Yorkshire Police, said “At a time when the cost of living continues to rise and businesses are under increasing pressure, shop theft remains a key priority for North Yorkshire Police.

“When incidents like this happen, we will respond immediately. I would urge retailers, staff and members of the public to always report crimes of this nature.

“If a shop theft is in progress, or there is any threat of violence or assault, please call 999 straight away. The information you provide can be crucial in helping us identify and locate suspects quickly.”
