A North Yorkshire man has been jailed for harassing a teenage boy.

Officers found 500 images of the boy on Paul Davey’s phone, as well as a printed image of the boy “stuck to a chair” in Davey’s bedroom, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said Davey followed the boy for months, driving alongside him as he walked along the street, on occasions filming him and would slow his car down so the victim had to walk past it.

“He knew the routes the victim would take, and despite the victim changing his routes, Davey still found him,” a police spokesperson said.

They said Davey had carried out the acts while being under a sexual risk order that prevented him from contacting anyone under 18 directly or indirectly.

Davey, 57, of Scarborough, pleaded guilty to breaching a sexual risk order and also pleaded guilty to harassment without violence.

He was jailed for 29 weeks after appearing at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on Thursday (October 29).

After the hearing, Detective Constable Jack McCabe, of Scarborough CID, said the victim was “frightened and distressed by Davey’s actions, which effected his day-to-day life”.

“Davey not only caused emotional harm but also wilfully breached the court order that was in place to protect children,” DC McCabe said.

“The court’s decision to impose a prison sentence reflects the seriousness of those breaches.

“Our thoughts remain with the victim, and I hope this result helps him move forward with the knowledge that Davey has been held responsible for the harm he caused.”
