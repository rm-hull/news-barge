A MENSTON woman undergoing treatment for breast cancer has launched a new project to provide free care packages to people facing the disease, with colleagues taking on a 1,000-mile challenge to help make the idea a reality.

Lyn Lyons, who is currently receiving treatment, created Join the Huddle after drawing on her own experiences of navigating breast cancer care.

The initiative aims to provide specially tailored care packages to patients at different stages of treatment, including chemotherapy, surgery, radiotherapy and hormone therapy. The packages would be distributed directly through NHS hospital care teams, removing the need for patients to apply for support themselves.

Join the Huddle is currently in discussions with NHS teams in Leeds as it looks to launch the scheme locally, while also working towards becoming a registered UK charity.

To support the project during Breast Cancer Awareness Month, her colleagues at Gusto Research in Roundhay have spent October walking, running and cycling a combined 1,000 miles, roughly equivalent to the journey from Leeds to the Outer Hebrides and back.

The team initially set out to raise £1,000, working on the basis of £1 for every mile completed. However, the challenge has already exceeded expectations, with nearly £3,000 raised so far.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21653650.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lyn Lyons *(Image: submitted)*

Lyn said: "I founded the Huddle following my own breast cancer diagnosis and treatment. I'm still on my journey, but I wanted help others going through theirs.

"I know how much the little things can matter during treatment. That's why the Huddle's care packages are being developed around the practical needs and comforts that can make a difference at different stages, including chemotherapy, surgery, radiotherapy and hormone therapy. Every detail, right down to what goes into each box, is shaped by my own experience."

She added: "I'm incredibly grateful to Gusto Research for supporting me throughout my diagnosis and treatment, and now for getting behind the Huddle and helping us raise funds.

"Our goal is simple: £1 for every mile we cover. Raising £1,000 would give the Huddle a brilliant start towards getting everything in place and preparing our very first care packages.

"Every donation, whatever the amount, will help us take another step towards supporting people receiving breast cancer treatment.

"If you're able to donate, share our challenge or cheer us on, we'd be so grateful.

"Together, we can help bring a little more comfort to treatment, one box at a time."

Donations can be made through the project's JustGiving crowdfunding page.

More information about Join the Huddle is available at www.jointhehuddle.org.uk, while updates can also be followed on Instagram at @join\_thehuddleuk.
