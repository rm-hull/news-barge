York building company has gone into liquidation with debts topping £60,000.

Virtue Developments was registered at Foxwood Lane, York, with directors listed as Jake Michael Andrew Calam and Reef Karl Wilson.

The specialist construction business was incorporated in April last year and the business was wound up on September 30.

Insolvency One of Leeds were appointed liquidators.




* **York hospitality firm liquidated owing over £235,000**
* **York employment agency liquidated owing more than £31,000**
* **JP Commercial Vehicles registered in York in liquidation**

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs outlines the financial situation.

The statement says the company had realisable assets, namely motor vehicles worth £2,500.

It had no preference creditors - those who are paid first.

But it did owe HMRC £3,500 in PAYE as a secondary preference creditor - someone paid next.

This left a deficit or shortfall of £1,000.

The report also outlines the unsecured creditors, who were owed a further £63,971.29 between them.

There were trade and expense creditors owed £7,922.29.

A director’s loan account was owed £34,353.14 and Banks/institutions were owed £21,695.86.

This gave a total deficit or shortfall of £64,971.29, the report added.

There was no issued or called up capital.
