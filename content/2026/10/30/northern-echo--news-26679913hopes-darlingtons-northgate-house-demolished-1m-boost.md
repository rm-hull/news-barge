Hopes that a derelict Darlington building could finally be demolished have been raised after the town secured £1 million in Government funding.

Darlington MP Lola McEvoy has singled out long-vacant Northgate House as a potential demolition target after years of stalled regeneration plans.

The Labour MP said Darlington Borough Council would receive £1 million to tackle derelict buildings across the town after the Autumn Budget on Wednesday, October 28.

Council representatives say that the authority is "awaiting more information before any decisions are made around how it will be used".

Ms McEvoy made clear her hopes that it could help bring down the prominent Northgate building, regularly branded an eyesore.

She said: "We got £1m for Darlington Council to get rid of derelict buildings in the town. Here's hoping it goes to pulling down Northgate House."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644849.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Northgate House in Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

But contractual disputes, communication problems and delays have repeatedly blocked efforts to acquire the property.

Talks between the owners and the council appeared to stall last year, despite Northgate House being central to the authority's redevelopment plans.

The council and its development partner, CityHeart, have proposed buying and demolishing Northgate House to create a new residential and commercial gateway into the town.

The plans include up to 205 homes near Gladstone Street.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644848.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lola McEvoy, MP for Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

A Darlington Borough Council spokesperson said: “It’s great news that Government funding is being made available to help improve our town centre.

"As the funding has only just been announced, we are awaiting more information before any decisions are made around how it will be used.”

The MP said 44,950 Darlington households in council tax bands A to D would receive £100 cashback towards winter energy bills while changes to business rates were expected to benefit 222 Darlington businesses.
