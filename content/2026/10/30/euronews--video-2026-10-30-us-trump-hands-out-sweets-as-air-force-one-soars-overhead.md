Dressed as everything from police officers and soldiers to dolls and racing drivers, young visitors lined up to receive sweets from the presidential couple. **Trump** also signed autographs, including on the costumes of two children dressed as racing drivers, while he and**Melania** posed for photographs with families. Among the more unusual outfits was a**baby dressed as a paper ballot**.

The festivities featured an unexpected spectacle when **Air Force One flew over the White House**, prompting the president and first lady to look up as the aircraft passed overhead, with the** Washington Monument** visible in the distance.

Several senior members of the Trump administration also attended the celebrations with their families, including **Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth**, White House Deputy Chief of Staff** Stephen Miller**and US Trade Representative** Jamieson Greer**. The president and first lady greeted them alongside other guests during the event, which brought together children in an array of colourful costumes.

The **White House Halloween celebration** is an established tradition, giving invited families an opportunity to meet the president and first lady while taking part in the American custom of trick-or-treating. Held**two days before Halloween itself, on 31 October**, this year's gathering combined the familiar ritual of handing out sweets with photographs, autographs and a** flypast by the presidential aircraft**.
