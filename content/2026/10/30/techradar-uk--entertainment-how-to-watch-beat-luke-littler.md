### Who is Luke 'The Nuke' Littler?

Littler is a darts prodigy who, at just 17 years old, became the youngest world champion in the sport’s history. The holder of 30 PDC titles, including 14 majors, he was named in TIME’s 100 Most Influential People in Sports 2026 list.

He was also featured in the Netflix sports documentary series *Matchroom: The Greatest Showmen*.

### Who will compete against Luke Littler?

There will be 24 contestants taking on Luke Littler and they range from 15 to 57 years old. The most high-profile are women's world number three Fallon Sherrock, two-time World Youth Championship winner Mitchell Lawrie, and 14-year-old darting sensation Jayden Walker.

Other competitors include a bin worker from Bedford, a Nando's restaurant manager and a former British Army captain.

The 24 contestants will compete for 20 spots, then be split into groups and randomly selected to take on Littler. The competition ends the moment somebody defeats Littler.
