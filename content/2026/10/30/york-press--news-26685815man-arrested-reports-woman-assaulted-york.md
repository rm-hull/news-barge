A man in his 60s has been arrested after reports of a woman allegedly being assaulted in York.

The incident happened in Piccadilly at 12.56pm on Friday (October 30).

North Yorkshire Police said officers arrived a short time later and a man in his 60s was arrested in connection with the incident.




A force spokesperson said: “No reports of injuries were received at the time of report.

“Anyone who witnessed it and has not yet spoken to the police is asked to get in touch either through our website.

“Or by calling 101. Please quote reference NYP-30102026-0206 when passing on information.”
