East Riding Libraries are set to host a brand new series of live, interactive events throughout November.

The libraries will launch their BOOKED! programme, featuring theatre, comedy and storytelling across multiple venues.

The programme includes a murder mystery performance, The Death of the Great Detective, created by theatre group Highly Suspect.

Councillor Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "Our libraries are about so much more than books, and the launch of BOOKED! is a fantastic opportunity to showcase the wide range of creative, cultural and community experiences they offer.

"From interactive theatre and environmental storytelling to Shakespeare and stand-up comedy, there is something for everyone to enjoy."

The show is suitable for ages eight and above, and will take place on November 9 at Beverley Library from 2pm to 3.30pm and at Driffield Centre from 7pm to 8.30pm.

The schedule also includes Sowing Sounds: Beverley’s Climate Story, an environmental audio event developed with Ergon Theatre.

The free event takes place at Beverley Library on November 12 from 6pm to 7.30pm and is suitable for those aged 12 and over.

For more details, pricing, and bookings, visit the East Riding Libraries website.
