A body of a man has been found outside a city centre building in Leeds.

It is understood West Yorkshire Police were called to a building in Water Lane at 3.32am on the morning of Friday, October 30. It is understood that a body has been found. Pictures from the scene show a blue tent has been erected outside the building.

A police cordon was put in place earlier today as police carried out enquiries. The cordon resulted in traffic problems in the area this morning.

### Body found after 'fall'

It is understood West Yorkshire Police were called to a building in Water Lane at 3.32am on the morning of Friday, October 30. It is understood that a body has been found and is believed to be connected to a fall.

The force has opened an investigation into the circumstances of the man's death, and enquiries are ongoing.

### 'Large' police cordon

Earlier today eye-witnesses reported seeing a 'large' police cordon in place. They said: "A large police cordon is causing major traffic issues around Bridgewater Place in Leeds city centre this morning.

"Officers were called to a building on Water Lane at 3.32am following a concern for safety report. Road closures remain in place. "
