# Zero Carbon Harrogate offers exclusive event this weekend inside house before it goes fossil fuel free

## “The event on Saturday will enable the public to see inside the 1920s house in Harrogate before it undergoes a major renovation to improve its energy efficiency”

There is a one-off opportunity to look around a 1920s home in Harrogate this weekend thanks to a local volunteer-led climate change charity.

![Zero Carbon Harrogate is offering a one-off opportunity to look around a 1920s home in Harrogate this weekend before it undergoes a major renovation to improve its energy efficiency.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/30/16/19/unnamed-(90).jpeg?trim=85,0,85,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Zero Carbon Harrogate’s exclusive event on Saturday, October 31 will enable the public to see inside the house in the Harlow Hill area before it undergoes a major renovation to improve its energy efficiency and move away from fossil fuels.

The free tour round the property will last an hour and will be available for a maximum of 15 people per time slot.

There will be two tours at 2pm and 3pm.

Zero Carbon Harrogate was founded in 2016 to act as an independent, trusted voice that collaborates with public bodies, local businesses, and communities to drive rapid decarbonisation in the Harrogate district.

If you’d like to come, there are a few pieces of advice in advance.

No building site safety equipment / PPE is needed.

The address is accessible by public transport.

The property does not have step-free access.

Zero Carbon Harrogate advises to dress for the weather.
