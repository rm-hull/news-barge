# Council's 'scandalous rip-off tactics' as volunteers handed £400 bill to put up Christmas lights

## A volunteer group were told they’d have to pay '£400+' for each of the 11 decorations

A Leeds town will be getting Christmas lamp decorations despite a team of volunteers accusing the council of “scandalous rip-off tactics”.

A volunteer group in Wetherby, who have been putting up the festive lights decorations for more than 60 years, was told by the Leeds City Council they’d have to pay “£400+” for each of the 11 decorations. Wetherby Town Council will now be footing the bill.

The hefty payments are needed for Leeds City Council’s Lights Team’s safety regulations. This comes after the Wetherby Christmas Lights Team took to social media to vent their frustrations at being handed such a large bill for each lamp decoration.

The team shared a Facebook post from community leader Allan Pearson, which said: “Unfortunately, we have been stopped from deploying decorations onto lamp posts this year, eleven in total, something we have been doing for many years. Leeds lights authority did however offer to do it for us at £400+ per display! Scandalous.”

Adding their own comment, Wetherby Christmas Lights Team said: “Deplorable! When a volunteer group of 62 years standing who selflessly provide time and energy for the benefit of all, have been faced with council attempting rip off tactics.”

The Facebook statement was met with outrage online by the community and local councillors. In response, work went on behind the scenes, directed by Wetherby ward City and Conservative councillor Norma Harrington, who is also a former town councillor, to ensure the North Leeds market town would be getting its lamp decorations.

A spokesperson for Leeds City Council said: “The Leeds Lights team have been in discussions with Wetherby Parish Council to provide additional support and guidance that will ensure compliance with strict safety regulations required to install Christmas lights on lampposts this year, as is the case with all other community led displays.

“Following further discussions with all partners it has been agreed that the lights install can now be carried out and any additional costs will be met by the local parish council.”

In a statement Wetherby Town Council said: “Following discussions and negotiations with Leeds City Council’s Lights Team, Wetherby Town Council will be paying for the installation of the Christmas lights on the street lamp posts for 2026, helping to ensure that the necessary requirements are met and that our town can continue to enjoy its Christmas lights.

“We want to make it clear that this arrangement is about ensuring the installation can take place in accordance with the required regulations. It does not diminish, in any way, the outstanding contribution made by the volunteers who work so hard to bring Christmas cheer to Wetherby by putting up the Christmas trees and motifs around the town.

“The Town Council fully appreciates and values the dedication, time and effort of the Wetherby Christmas Lights Team. Their commitment plays a vital role in making Wetherby a beautiful and welcoming place during the festive season, and we sincerely hope their wonderful work will continue for many years to come.”
