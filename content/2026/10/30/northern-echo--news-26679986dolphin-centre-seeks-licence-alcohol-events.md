Darlington's Dolphin Centre is seeking permission to extend its licensed area and allow alcohol sales and entertainment until 1am.

Darlington Council has submitted an application to extend the Dolphin Centre's licensable area and amend its operating hours.

The proposal, submitted under the Licensing Act 2003, aims to permit the sale of alcohol both on and off the premises, as well as to host indoor sporting events, boxing or wrestling, live and recorded music, and dance performances until 1am.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

The proposed extension of licensable hours would also include late-night refreshments until 1am.

The application has been published, and any objections or representations must be submitted in writing by November 17.

People can inspect the register of licensing applications at the Darlington Borough Council Licensing Authority, located in the Town Hall, between 10am and 4pm, or online at darlington.gov.uk.
