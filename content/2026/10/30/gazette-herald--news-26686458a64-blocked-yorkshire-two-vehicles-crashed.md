Two vehicles have crashed on a major road in North Yorkshire with emergency services called to the scene.

The A64 is blocked both ways near Rillington after the crash on Friday afternoon (October 30).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said one person was helped out of a vehicle by paramedics and two other people were outside when its crews arrived at 3.30pm.

"Fire crews made the vehicles safe using small tools and left the incident with the police on scene,” a service spokesperson said.

North Yorkshire Police has urged drivers to avoid the area.

"Thank you for your patience, we hope to have the road clear in the next hour subject to vehicle recovery,” a force spokesperson said at 4.30pm.

The crash has resulted in delays for drivers with traffic moving “very slowly”, according to the AA.

An eye-witness said he had been diverted from the A64 to the A169 due to the incident and was stuck in queuing traffic.

"I can see a number of blue lights in the distance and the traffic is chaotic,” he said.

"It must be a bad crash as everyone is being turned round now and diverted via Kirkby Misperton."
