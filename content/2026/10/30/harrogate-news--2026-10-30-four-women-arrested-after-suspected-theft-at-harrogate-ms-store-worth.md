**Four women have been arrested following a suspected theft of goods from the M&S store on Leeds Road in Harrogate, during which a member of staff was assaulted.**

North Yorkshire Police received a report at 3.36pm on Thursday 29 October 2026 that a significant quantity of goods had been stolen from the store.

Officers were given details of a vehicle believed to be involved and carried out searches across the area. The vehicle was later located travelling northbound on the A1, where roads policing officers intercepted it.

Four women were inside the vehicle, two of whom were wanted on warrants.

Police seized the vehicle and recovered £1,672.49 worth of suspected stolen goods from various stores.

All four women were arrested and enquiries are continuing.

### Police urge retailers to report thefts

PC Michelle Telford of North Yorkshire Police said shop theft remained a priority for the force, particularly as businesses faced financial pressures.

> **PC Michelle Telford, North Yorkshire Police, said:** At a time when the cost of living continues to rise and businesses are under increasing pressure, shop theft remains a key priority for North Yorkshire Police.
>
> When incidents like this happen, we will respond immediately. I would urge retailers, staff and members of the public to always report crimes of this nature. If a shop theft is in progress, or there is any threat of violence or assault, please call 999 straight away. The information you provide can be crucial in helping us identify and locate suspects quickly.

Police have confirmed that enquiries are continuing.
