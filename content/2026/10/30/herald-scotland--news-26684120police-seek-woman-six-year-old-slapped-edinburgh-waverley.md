Police are seeking a woman alleged to have slapped a six-year-old across the face during a train journey to Scotland.

Detectives investigating the incident say it happened on board a train arriving at Edinburgh Waverley that had travelled from Morpeth, Northumberland.

The incident is being treated as an assault, and officers are appealing for witnesses to come forward to support their investigation.

The six-year-old boy, who was accompanied by a relative, is said to have been assaulted at around 3pm on Saturday, 24 October, by a woman unknown to him.

No arrests have been made in connection with the incident, and enquiries are ongoing.

Several people are thought to have witnessed the incident, and detectives are appealing for them to get in touch to support the investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to contact BTP by texting 61016 or calling 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 481 of 24 October.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
