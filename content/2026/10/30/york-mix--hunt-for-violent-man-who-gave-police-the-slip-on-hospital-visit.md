Police are tonight searching for Ethan Platt.

Platt, 22, is on the run after giving officers the slip while on a hospital visit.

He was arrested earlier today for breaching a domestic violence protection order.

“While receiving treatment at hospital, he managed to evade officers and left the hospital grounds,” a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said.

“Extensive enquiries and searches are now underway to locate him, and we believe he may be in the Harrogate area.

“Although Ethan is not believed to pose a risk to the wider public, officers are keen to locate him as quickly as possible.”

He’s described as

* white
* about 5ft 11in (180cm) tall
* with a slim build
* blue eyes
* and brown hair.

If you see Ethan, or have any information about his whereabouts, you’re asked to call 999 immediately quoting North Yorkshire Police reference NYP-30102026-0071.

The force spokesperson added: “Please do not approach him. Instead, contact police straight away with any sightings or information.”
