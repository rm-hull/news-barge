Sophie Ellis-Bextor will headline Live At The Piece Hall in Halifax.

The disco-pop icon will perform on Friday, July 2, as part of the 2027 series of summer shows at the historic venue.

She will be joined by special guests Scouting For Girls.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "Move over kitchen disco, it’s time to crack out the sequins for our very own courtyard disco.

"Sophie Ellis-Bextor was an absolute joy when she supported Nile Rodgers & CHIC and so many people suggested she come back— from original fans to those who fell in love with her during lockdown, and the younger crowd who discovered her through that iconic Saltburn moment.

"Her unique sound is a perfect fit for our unique venue, and this show is guaranteed to lift your spirits and get those feet moving."

Ms Ellis-Bextor’s return follows her previous performance at The Piece Hall in support of Nile Rodgers & CHIC.

She has enjoyed more than 25 years in music with five Top 10 albums and eight Top 10 singles, including Murder on the Dancefloor, which recently found renewed popularity after featuring in the film Saltburn.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, November 6, via ticketmaster.co.uk.
