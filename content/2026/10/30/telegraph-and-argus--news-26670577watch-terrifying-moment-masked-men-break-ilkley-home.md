THIS VIDEO shows the terrifying moment three masked men break into a home in the early hours of Sunday (25 October) morning.

Ring Doorbell footage, captured by homeowner Georgina Jones, sees three men in balaclavas approaching the property on Colbert Road in Ilkley.

One suspect seems to be assisted by another as they attempt to gain entry to the home through a downstairs window.

### 'This was a terrifying invasion of our home'

The incident, which Ms Jones described as "terrifying", took place at around 4.50am on Sunday, October 25.

She said: "The experience has left us shaken and our sense of security has been taken away.

“This was a terrifying invasion of our home, not simply an attempted theft.

"Everyone deserves to feel safe in their own home, and we deserve to be kept informed about what is happening."

### 'The support from the community has been incredible'

Ms Jones thanked the local community for their support during and after the ordeal.

She said: "I want to say a huge thank you to the local people who have shared their CCTV footage with us and helped us piece together the men’s movements as they tried to escape through people’s gardens.

"The support from our community has been incredible, and we are genuinely grateful to everyone who has taken the time to help."

### 'The emotional impact of something like this should never be underestimated'

Ms Jones implores anybody with CCTV footage to come forward to help identify the three men.

She said: “I hope anyone with CCTV or Ring doorbell footage will come forward to help identify these men, and I hope the police will contact us with an update.

"We are thankful nobody was physically harmed, but the emotional impact of something like this should never be underestimated.”

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were called to a report of a burglary at a residential property on Colbert Road, Ilkley.

"The burglary is reported to have happened around 4.50am on Sunday morning (25 October).

"Enquiries are currently ongoing."
