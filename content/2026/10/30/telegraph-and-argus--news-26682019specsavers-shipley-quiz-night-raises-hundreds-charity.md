A CHARITY quiz night has helped raise hundreds of pounds for good causes.

The event, organised by Specsavers Shipley and held at Baildon Woodbottom Working Men’s Club, featured several rounds of sponsored bingo and was the second games night hosted by the opticians since earlier this year.

Dominic Doran, director at Specsavers Shipley, said: "We’re always looking at new ways to give back to the community and it’s clear that our most recent endeavour is a hit, bringing the people of Shipley together while raising hundreds of pounds for a good cause.

"We want to thank our co-hosts, Shipley Memories, and the Baildon Woodbottom Working Men’s Club for offering us the space free of charge.

"It also couldn’t have happened without all the players on the day, who helped us raise the much-needed funds."

The night was organised in partnership with Shipley Memories, a dementia support group that runs bi-weekly socials at the club.

These events are funded by Specsavers Shipley and feature sponsored entertainment, refreshments, and free glasses cleaning and adjustments.

Funds raised from the night were donated to Shipley Memories and Diabetes UK.

Mr Doran encouraged residents to look out for future events both in-store and on social media.
