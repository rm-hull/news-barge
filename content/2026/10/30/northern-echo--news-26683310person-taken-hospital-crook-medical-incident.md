A person has been taken to hospital after a medical incident in County Durham that saw the air ambulance attend the scene.

Emergency services were called to Crook at around midday yesterday (October 29), following reports of an incident.

An air ambulance was seen landing in the streets of the town, just after 12pm.

The Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS) has confirmed that a person was taken to hospital via road ambulance.

A spokesperson for GNAAS said: "Our critical care team was activated at 12pm to reports of a medical incident in Crook.
