Paddy McGuinness is bringing his latest stand-up tour to Bradford.

The comedian and television presenter will perform at Bradford Live on Saturday, October 30, 2027, as part of his new 33-date UK tour Blast from the Past.

The show promises McGuinness’ trademark humour alongside a nostalgic look back at the television, music, and pop culture moments that defined a generation.

McGuinness said: "This new tour is all about nostalgia – the telly, the tunes and the daft little things that instantly take you back.

"This is me doing what I love: making people laugh.

"It’ll be a big, funny warm trip down memory lane, but with plenty of modern life woes along the way.

"Grab your rose-tinted spectacles and a ticket."

A familiar face on British television, Mr McGuinness is best known for hosting ITV’s Take Me Out and the BBC’s Top Gear.

In 2026, it was announced that he would co-host a revival of Big Break for BBC Two and BBC iPlayer.

His last tour, Nearly There, sold out more than 50 dates across the UK and Ireland between 2024 and spring 2025.

Tickets for Blast from the Past go on general sale at 10am on Friday, November 6.
