Berlin security authorities say they have no information regarding specific calls for violence at this Saturday’s protest, but they are preparing for a major operation. This is due to a call to protest by the initiative ‘Gen Y regelt’ (Gen Z rules), which is planned to take place all day on Halloween. As a result, approximately 1,500 additional police officers will be deployed on October 31. According to operations manager Stephan Katte, water cannons will also be on standby.

According to police, two ‘Gen Z’ rallies are currently scheduled for Saturday. The one at the Brandenburg Gate, which is set to begin at 2 pm simultaneously with numerous other events nationwide, is expected to draw 10,000 people, a figure considered realistic by the police. Students, university students and parents are expected to attend. Police anticipate little potential for conflict. The organisers intend to peacefully express their concerns.

![](https://www.the-berliner.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/imago0871332664h-scaled.jpg)

*IMAGO / STEINSIEK.CH*

Police Chief Barbara Slowik Meisel is convinced that there will be no scenes of looted shops like those seen in France. “We have no concrete evidence of any coordinated looting,” she said. However, the police have received an increasing number of inquiries from concerned business owners in recent days. Many of whom have started to board up their shop fronts and windows.
