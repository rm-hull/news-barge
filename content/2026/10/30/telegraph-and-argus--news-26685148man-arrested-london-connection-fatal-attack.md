A murder suspect has been arrested in connection with a fatal attack in Leeds.

The 26-year-old man was detained in London by the Metropolitan Police on suspicion of murdering Rahiem Jackson, 38, in New Wortley.

Mr Jackson was fatally assaulted by four suspects outside a property on Hedley Chase at around 10.23pm on Saturday, October 24.

Emergency services attended the scene and despite treatment by paramedics Mr Jackson was sadly pronounced dead a short time later.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates, of West Yorkshire Police’s Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, said: "This arrest is significant development in the murder investigation of Rahiem Jackson.

"Officers are continuing to support the family.

"Extensive enquiries remain ongoing and we continue to appeal for information around the attack.

"We continue to ask anyone who may have seen people arrive or leave the scene on Hedley Chase on Saturday night to come forward."

The suspect remains in police custody.

West Yorkshire Police are urging anyone with information to come forward.

People can contact police by calling 101 or using the live chat facility at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat, quoting reference 13260606745.

Information can also be provided anonymously through Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 11 or via online.
