A Bradford KFC is applying to stay open until 2am.

Intracave Limited, the operator of the Odsal Road branch, has submitted an application to the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council to serve late-night refreshments from 11pm to 2am, Monday to Sunday.

The application is open for written representations until November 18 and can be inspected at the Licensing Team, Shearbridge Depot, Shearbridge Road, Bradford, BD7 1PU.

The public can also view the application by visiting bradford.gov.uk, calling 01274 432240, or emailing [licensing@bradford.go.uk.](mailto:licensing@bradford.go.uk.)

Making a false statement in connection with the application carries an unlimited fine upon summary conviction.
