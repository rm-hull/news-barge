A village pub on the outskirts of Darlington says it has been overwhelmed by customers' support after reopening its doors this week.

The Tuns at Sadberge officially welcomed customers back on Wednesday, October 29, following months of preparation behind the scenes.

The reopening in Hill House Lane marks a significant comeback for the pub, which built a strong reputation for its food, Sunday lunches and friendly atmosphere.

Just a day after reopening, the team thanked customers for turning out in force and helping make the launch a success.

In a social media update, the pub said: "Good morning! We couldn't be happier with the opening yesterday.

"It was great to meet everyone who popped in, said hello and wished us all the best with the venue.

"It really means a lot and we appreciate all of your welcomes and good wishes."

The business added that it still had tables available and encouraged people to visit over the coming days.

The Tuns has reopened with both a lunchtime deli menu and a main menu featuring a range of pub favourites.

Staff were also keen to draw attention to another popular part of the menu.

The post continued: "But let's talk desserts. We serve homemade cheesecakes and chocolate cakes.

"And let us tell you now, they are something special. This week's cheesecake is a lemon."

The Tuns announced plans to reopen in August, telling customers work was taking place behind the scenes to prepare the venue.

At the time, the pub asked residents what food, drinks and desserts they would like to see on offer when the doors reopened. Diners praised its freshly prepared food, hospitality and locally sourced produce.

Previous reviews highlighted its Sunday lunches, steak and curry nights, as well as gluten-free and dairy-free options.

The Tuns has also confirmed it will be serving cask beer from local firm Three Brothers Brewing Company.

Following a successful first day back in business, staff said they are looking forward to welcoming both familiar faces and first-time visitors through the doors.

"We would love to see you," the post added.

Customers who wish to book a table can do so through the pub's online booking system.
