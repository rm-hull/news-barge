A weekly online dance programme is helping people with Parkinson’s stay active from home.

Bounceback Exercise, a national provider of exercise and wellbeing services, runs the sessions to support people living with Parkinson’s and other long-term health conditions through dance and movement.

Beth Boxall, founder at Bounceback Exercise, said: "Delivering the online dance programme has meant that we can offer even more opportunities for people to lead active lifestyles with Parkinson’s.

"Each online class is designed to be easy to follow and accessible, and I’m delighted to see how it’s already supporting people to be active with the condition."

The classes are led by Steph Still, a professional dancer and teacher with a Master’s degree in Clinical Exercise Science.

She said: "Dance can be a really expressive and creative way for people to live well with long-term health conditions.

"I’m really pleased to be able to teach the new online dance classes for people with Parkinson’s, and I hope that many more people will be able to take advantage of the offer."

The programme is open to all abilities, and no previous dance experience is needed.

Relatives and loved ones are also welcome to take part.

More information can be found at www.bouncebackexercise.com/dance.
