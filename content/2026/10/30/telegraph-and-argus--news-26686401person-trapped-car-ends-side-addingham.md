A PERSON was trapped inside a car after it ended up on its side.

The incident happened on Main Street in Addingham at 5.16am this morning (Friday).

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service attended the scene and helped free the person before handing them over to paramedics.

A spokesperson for the fire service said: "Single vehicle road traffic collision with car on its side, one person trapped in vehicle, extricated by fire crews and handed over to paramedics."

The T&A has reached out to West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service for further comment.
