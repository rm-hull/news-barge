A cancer charity supporting patients and families across the west of Scotland has received a £100,000 donation from a foundation established in memory of a woman who died from cancer.

The Beatson Cancer Charity received the gift from the Emmie Smillie Charity Foundation, which has raised and donated more than £5 million since it was founded in 2017.

The foundation was set up in memory of Emmie Smillie and distributes funds to cancer charities, hospices and families affected by cancer and life-limiting illnesses across Scotland and the UK.

The donation will support the Beatson Cancer Charity’s ongoing work with patients and their families.

Andrew Smillie, co-founder of the Emmie Smillie Charity Foundation, said: "We started the charity around ten years ago after my sister passed away from cancer.

"Since then, we've tried to raise as much money as possible to support cancer charities.

"Beatson Cancer Charity became especially important to us this year as my mum was receiving treatment here.

"We witnessed the work that the charity does, and we just thought it was amazing."

Andrew Smillie said: "The treatment and care that patients get here in general is fantastic, but I think the wellbeing support that people receive is unbelievable.

"From their hair to their nails and everything else, it helps people feel more comfortable and feel better about themselves."

Gillian Hailstones, chief executive of Beatson Cancer Charity, said: "We are incredibly grateful to the Smillie family and everyone involved with the Emmie Smillie Charity Foundation for this remarkable donation.

"Their continued commitment to supporting people affected by cancer has made a meaningful difference to our work and to the many individuals and families we support."
