Four women have been arrested following a suspected high-value theft, which resulted in the alleged assault of a member of staff, from a popular supermarket.

Police were called to M&S in Leeds Road, Harrogate, at 3.36pm on Thursday, October 29, after a large quantity of goods was reported stolen.

During the incident, a member of staff was assaulted while trying to intervene.




Officers were given details of a vehicle believed to be involved and carried out extensive searches in the area.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "The vehicle was eventually located travelling northbound on the A1, where roads policing unit officers intercepted it.

"Inside the vehicle were four women, two of whom were wanted warrant.

"The vehicle has been seized and officers recovered £1,672.49 worth of suspected stolen goods.

"All four women were arrested and enquiries are today continuing."

PC Michelle Telford said: "At a time when the cost of living continues to rise and businesses are under increasing pressure, shop theft remains a key priority for North Yorkshire Police.

"When incidents like this happen, we will respond immediately."

Police have urged anyone who witnesses a theft in progress, or where violence is threatened, to call 999.
