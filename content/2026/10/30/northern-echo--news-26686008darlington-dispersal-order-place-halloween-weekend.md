"Enjoy Halloween responsibly"- that is the message from police in Darlington as anti-yob order to prevent anti-social behaviour and disorder is brought in over the weekend.

A 72-hour dispersal order covering Darlington town centre came into force at 3.30pm on Friday (October 30).

The order gives police the power to move on anyone suspected of causing trouble in the and prevent them from returning. Anyone who ignores an order could commit a criminal offence and may be arrested.

Extra officers will also be out on patrol throughout the weekend.

Neighbourhood Inspector David Littlefair said: “Our message is simple: enjoy Halloween responsibly, respect your community, and do not spoil it for everyone else.

“The vast majority of young people enjoy Halloween responsibly. It is a small minority whose behaviour causes concern and has a disproportionate impact on residents, businesses and visitors.

“We are taking people's concerns seriously and will have an increased police presence across the town centre.”

Durham Constabulary is also urging parents and guardians to make sure they know where their children are and what they are doing over the Halloween weekend.

“Too often we deal with incidents involving young people whose parents have no idea where they are or what they have become involved in," the neighbourhood inspector added.
