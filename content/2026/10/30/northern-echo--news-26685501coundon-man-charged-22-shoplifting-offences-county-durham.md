A man has appeared in court charged after an alleged shoplifting spree.

Alex Ferguson, 45, of Coundon, Bishop Auckland, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on Friday (October 30) charged with 29 offences.

He faces 22 counts of shoplifting after around £3,500 worth of stock is alleged to have been taken from businesses in Spennymoor, Sedgefield, Newton Aycliffe, Shildon and Bishop Auckland between October 3 and October 25.

Ferguson faces a further four charges of burglary.

He has also been charged with handling stolen goods, driving while disqualified and driving without insurance.

Durham Police said Ferguson was remanded in custody and is due to appear at Durham Crown Court on Friday, November 27.
