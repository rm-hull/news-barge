A man has been arrested after he was reportedly spotted carrying a knife in Teesside.

Council enforcement officers spotted the weapon while watching two men near the car park on William Street, Stockton, on Tuesday, October 13.

Officers continued to watch the pair as they made their way along Yarm Lane, where they saw that one of the men had a knife.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651510.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Yarm Lane, Stockton *(Image: Google)*

Police were called, and the Stockton CCTV operators were quickly alerted to help monitor the men’s movements and help officers track them down.

Police were then able to stop the pair on Yarm Lane, arresting a 56-year-old man on suspicion of a knife offence and recovering the weapon.

Sergeant Chris Williams, of Stockton Neighbourhood Team, said: “This is an excellent example of the partnership work between Cleveland Police and Stockton-on-Tees Borough Council to tackle crime and keep the area safe.

“We always work closely with partners, and the communication channels we have in place ensure that as soon as a problem or an issue is spotted, we can quickly resolve it.”

This incident comes as efforts to tackle knife crime across Teesside are being stepped up with extra Government funding for Cleveland Police through the Knife Crime Concentration Fund.

Councillor Norma Stephenson, the council's Cabinet Member for Access, Communities and Community Safety, said: “Our officers on the ground and in the CCTV Control Room were key to this arrest and quick removal of the knife.

“Operation Shield, a multi-agency operation designed to combat anti-social behaviour and improve safety in Stockton Town Centre sees our teams listening to people, working together in joint patrols and taking action on issues raised by our communities."
