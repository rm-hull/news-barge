An application for a new premises licence has been submitted for a Selby premises.

Curly Pepper Trading Ltd has applied for a licence for The Summit, in Scott Road, to sell alcohol from 8am to 2am, Monday to Sunday.

The venue also plans to play recorded music until 2.30am and offer late-night refreshments until 2am on Sundays.

The full application can be viewed at the North Yorkshire Council offices in Doncaster Road, Selby, between 10am and 4pm.

People can comment on this application until November 17.

This must be done in writing to North Yorkshire Council at [licensing.sel@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.sel@northyorks.gov.uk.)

Anyone who makes a false statement in connection with an application can be fined an unlimited amount.

The application was made under the Licensing Act 2003.
