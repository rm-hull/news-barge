A Glasgow-born eyewear brand is set to open a new flagship showroom in the heart of the city’s West End as part of its continued expansion across the UK.

Independent eyewear company IOLLA has announced plans for a new 790 sq ft showroom at 711 Great Western Road, which it says will become its flagship Glasgow location and a major milestone in the firm's growth.

The new space represents a continued investment in the city where the business was founded 11 years ago and has been designed to create a relaxed and welcoming environment for customers browsing prescription glasses and sunglasses.

Founded in 2015, IOLLA has grown from a single Glasgow showroom to a network of nine locations across six UK cities, including Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Manchester, Cambridge and London.

The company said the Great Western Road showroom has been shaped by more than a decade of customer feedback and is intended to feel more like a home than a traditional retail space.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21647980.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Founded in 2015, IOLLA has grown from a single Glasgow showroom to a network of nine locations *(Image: Storyshop)*

Stefan Hunter, founder and chief executive of IOLLA, said: "We genuinely wouldn’t be where we are today without the West End. Finnieston gave IOLLA its start and the response we had there gave us the belief that we could take a Glasgow business into new cities across the UK.

"Eleven years of feedback has gone into this space. We know that our West End customers love somewhere that’s social enough to feel relaxed, but private enough that you can take your time choosing, with no one looking over your shoulder and no pressure to make a sale.

"We’ve designed the new showroom to feel less like a traditional shop and more like walking into a trusted home."

Hunter added that the firm was proud to be investing in physical retail at a time when many high streets were facing challenges.

The showroom has been created in collaboration with Scottish interior designer and television presenter Anna Campbell-Jones, who is known for presenting BBC Scotland’s Scotland's Home of the Year.

Campbell-Jones said: "Working with IOLLA on the design of the new showroom was an opportunity to create a space that feels welcoming, confident and unmistakably Glasgow.

"Every detail was considered to create a showroom people genuinely want to spend time in, from the overall atmosphere through to the bespoke design elements."

IOLLA is known for its direct-to-consumer model, designing its eyewear in-house and working directly with manufacturing partners to offer prescription glasses and sunglasses at transparent prices.

The opening forms part of the company's ongoing investment in bricks-and-mortar retail and reinforces Glasgow's position at the centre of its operations as it continues to expand across the UK.

Further details, including an opening date and first-look images of the new flagship showroom, are expected to be announced in the coming weeks.
