They are the best of the best.

Meet the winners of the **YorkMix Choice Awards 2026**, now officially crowned the finest businesses in York and North Yorkshire.

At a glam and glittering night at The Milner York Hotel, we gave out 20 awards, from best bar to business champion.

Sponsored by recruitment specialists PJ Staffing, the YorkMix Choice Awards brought the best in business together, alongside special guests, the Lord Mayor of York Cllr Margaret Wells, and the Lord Mayor of Leeds, Cllr Stephen Holroyd.

Getting the party started in rip-roaring fashion were country singer Belle Velvet and her dancers.
