*Award-winning kitchen and fitted-furniture manufacturer Jackton Moor will showcase its design and manufacturing capability at the Raer Christmas Market on 5 and 6 December, with a new charity fundraising initiative forming part of the two-day event*

East Kilbride-based Jackton Moor, recently named Scotland’s Best Kitchen Company for the second consecutive year, will attend the Raer Christmas Market with a specially prepared display highlighting the company’s bespoke manufacturing and design capabilities.

The business is also developing a charity raffle for the event, with plans for a Quooker tap & System including installation to form the main prize, subject to final confirmation.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650576.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jackton Moor will be exhibiting throughout the event *(Image: BK PHOTOGRAPHY)*

All proceeds from the raffle are intended to support a Rett syndrome charity, a cause with a personal connection to Jackton Moor General Manager David Gillen.

David said: “Raer is a fantastic environment for us to showcase the wider capability of Jackton Moor.

“As a Scottish manufacturer, we are proud that our kitchens and fitted furniture are designed around the individual customer and manufactured here in East Kilbride.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650577.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raer Christmas market *(Image: BK PHOTOGRAPHY)*

“This year we wanted to add something more meaningful to our involvement in the Christmas Market. Rett syndrome is a cause particularly close to me personally, so we are currently working with the relevant organisations and our suppliers to create a fundraising element that can generate both awareness and a worthwhile contribution to the charity.”

Jackton Moor has an established relationship with Raer Distillery and has previously been involved in a number of bespoke projects across the facility, including bar and display cabinetry, laboratory areas and accommodation fit-outs.

David added: “The relationship with Raer is a good example of how the capability within Jackton Moor extends far beyond a traditional kitchen environment.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650580.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raer Christmas market *(Image: BK PHOTOGRAPHY)*

“The Christmas Market gives us the opportunity to meet people in a much more relaxed setting, showcase some of that craftsmanship and introduce more customers to what can be achieved through bespoke Scottish manufacturing.”

The company is currently updating its event display ahead of December, incorporating new finishes and exploring the inclusion of the same Quooker model that could ultimately become the raffle prize.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650680.jpg?type=mds-article-620). *(Image: Supplied)*

Alongside the fundraising activity, Jackton Moor will use the event to promote its East Kilbride and Prestwick design studios and its wider offering across kitchens, bedrooms and bespoke fitted furniture.

**A Period of Continued Investment and Development**

The December event comes during a significant period of development for Jackton Moor, with the business continuing to invest in upgraded systems and technology while also strengthening its wider sales and commercial structure.

As part of that development, Jackton Moor is restructuring its senior commercial team and is preparing to welcome a new **Head of Sales & Commercial Development** to the business.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650579.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Raer Christmas market *(Image: BK PHOTOGRAPHY)*

David said: “We have taken the opportunity to look carefully at the next stage of Jackton Moor rather than simply replace an existing position.

“We have been fortunate to secure a very experienced senior appointment who will work across Retail, Contracts, new business development and the wider commercial strategy of the company.

“The objective is to strengthen the connection between our customers, our sales teams and our manufacturing capability, while giving the business additional leadership as we move into the next stage of our development.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650581.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The market is always a popular event *(Image: BK PHOTOGRAPHY)*

“Alongside our investment in upgraded systems and technology, we believe this will put Jackton Moor in a stronger position to develop both our existing customer relationships and new opportunities across Scotland.”

David added: “The Raer Christmas Market gives us a really positive way to finish the year - supporting a local event, showcasing Scottish craftsmanship and hopefully raising a meaningful amount for a cause that deserves greater awareness.”

Further details on the charity raffle, prize and entry arrangements will be announced ahead of the event.
