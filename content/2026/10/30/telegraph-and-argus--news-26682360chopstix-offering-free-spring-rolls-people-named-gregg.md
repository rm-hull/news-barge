CHOPSTIX is giving away free spring rolls as part of a new campaign.

The UK noodle bar chain is offering the treat to anyone named “Gregg” or similar—either as a first or last name—as part of its “Punch Up Your Lunch” campaign.

The offer runs from October 30 to November 23.

To claim the free spring rolls, eligible customers must sign up through the Chopstix website to receive a voucher.

The campaign aims to encourage people to break away from dull desk lunches and enjoy something more flavourful.

It follows research commissioned by Chopstix, which found that 55 per cent of Brits have avoided bringing certain foods into work to avoid offending colleagues.
