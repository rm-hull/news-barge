Crowds queued in long lines as eager shoppers raced to get a first look inside Durham's newest homeware store this morning.

The Danish retailer Søstrene Grene officially opened its doors at The Riverwalk at 9.59am on Friday (October 30).

Queues stretched all the way from the new shop to the end of the street and around the corner.

A ribbon-cutting ceremony was held by staff, alongside performing ballerinas, before the first customer was let through the doors.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651430.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Crowds gather for the official opening of Søstrene Grene's new Durham store at The Riverwalk *(Image: Adam Shanley)*

The launch marks the Scandinavian brand’s arrival in Durham, with shoppers eager to explore its range of home décor, crafts, furniture, gifts, and lifestyle products.

Many visitors arrived early to take advantage of launch-day offers, including complimentary tote bags filled with selected Søstrene Grene products.

Mikkel Grene, group chief executive and co-owner of Søstrene Grene, said: "It's always special to open a Søstrene Grene store in a new city, and we're particularly pleased to be bringing our universe to Durham.

"We hope the new store will become a destination for those looking for something a little different, whether they're searching for a new craft project, something for the home, or simply enjoying an afternoon of browsing."

The retailer announced earlier this month that it would be opening at The Riverwalk, describing Durham as an exciting addition to its growing North East presence.

Founded in Denmark in 1973, Søstrene Grene has built a loyal following through its ever-changing collections and Scandinavian-inspired shopping experience centred around the concept of "hygge" – creating comfort and enjoyment in everyday life.

The company now operates more than 400 stores across 19 countries and continues to expand in the UK.

The strong turnout on Friday suggests that the shop will be busy over Halloween weekend.
