Greens Contractors Ltd has applied to operate one goods vehicle from a site in Wetherby.

The proposed operating centre is at Valley View, Trip Lane, Linton, LS22 4HX.

The applicant is trading as Greens Contractors and is based at 15 Northgates, Wetherby, LS22 6NX.

Owners or occupiers of nearby land, including buildings, who believe their use or enjoyment could be affected may make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

* **For more information about this public notice, click here.**
* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal at publicnoticeportal.uk/york-press**

Representations must be sent to Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, stating the reasons for the objection.

They must be made within 21 days of this notice.

Representors must also send a copy to Greens Contractors at the Northgates address.

A guide to making representations is available at www.gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.
