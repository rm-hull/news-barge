# Met Police arrest man in connection with murder of 'beautiful soul' in Yorkshire

## The Metropolitan Police have arrested a man in London in connection with a murder in Yorkshire.

The 26-year-old man was arrested overnight in connection with the murder of Rahiem Jackson in Leeds on October 24.

Detectives from West Yorkshire Police’s homicide and major enquiry team are continuing to investigate the incident, the force said.

Mr Jackson was named as the victim of an attack which happened outside a property on Hedley Chase in New Wortley.

Police were called to the scene following reports of a man being attacked with weapons.

A statement was released by Mr Jackson’s family - who said he was known as Reema.

![Rahiem Jackson has been named as the man killed in Leeds](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/27/17/14/Leeds-murder-victim-named.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


It said: “Rahiem was loved and adored by his sisters and family. He had a beautiful heart and beautiful soul; his smile was so infectious, and he lit up the room with his presence.

“Rahiem would have gone above and beyond for anybody he cared for."

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates, of West Yorkshire Police, said: “This arrest is significant development in the murder investigation of Rahiem Jackson.

“Officers are continuing to support the family. Extensive enquiries remain ongoing and we continue to appeal for information around the attack.

“We continue to ask anyone who may have seen people arrive or leave the scene on Hedley Chase on Saturday night to come forward.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact police online or on 101, quoting incident number 13260606745.

Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
