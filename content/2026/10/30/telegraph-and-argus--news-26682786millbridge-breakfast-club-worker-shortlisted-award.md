A BREAKFAST club champion has been named a finalist in a national competition.

Sharron Ryan, who runs the breakfast club at Millbridge A SHARE Primary Academy, has been shortlisted as one of five finalists in Tiptree's Raise a Toast to Britain's Morning Heroes.

Ms Ryan begins her day at 7.30am, welcoming pupils with a smile and offering them toast, cereal, yoghurt and fruit, along with activities to help them start the day positively.

She said: "I feel very proud and honoured (and totally surprised!) to have been nominated for Breakfast Club Hero.

"I absolutely love my job and feel privileged to be able to make a positive difference to the lives of children every day.

"For some of our children, breakfast club is about so much more than just having something to eat; it's about starting the day with a warm welcome, a friendly smile and knowing that someone is there for them."

Ms Ryan was nominated by Lauren Wyke, who praised her dedication and care for the children at Millbridge.

Voting for the competition opens on Thursday, October 29, and closes at midnight on Tuesday, November 10.
