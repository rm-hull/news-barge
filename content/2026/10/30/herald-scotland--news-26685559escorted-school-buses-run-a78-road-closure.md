School buses between Skelmorlie and Largs are being escorted through the A78 closure, helping pupils travel to school again without a lengthy diversion.

Buses carrying pupils to Largs Academy will use a restricted lane on the southbound carriageway in the morning and afternoon, at agreed times only.

Teams at both ends of the closure will manage each movement, with buses travelling under vehicle escort at reduced speed.

The lane is narrow, with voids beside it, and every run will be monitored. Road maintenance firm Amey says it can suspend the service at short notice if site conditions or repair work creates a safety concern. Parents should contact the school for information about times.

High winds, heavy rain and large waves damaged the road surface and sea wall south of Skelmorlie during Storm Boelo on Sunday, October 25.

Investigations and emergency repairs continue, while engineers assess the carriageway. Amey cannot say when the road will reopen.

A restricted access lane has been operating on the southbound carriageway since Wednesday, October 28 to help residents within the closure access their properties without travelling the full diversion.

The lane is coned off, with designated passing places, and vehicles are taken through under escort at a maximum of 10 mph.

Access is limited to residents and carers, and the lane remains closed to general traffic, including larger and heavier vehicles.

The closure points are staffed at all times and access for emergency service vehicles is being maintained.

An Amey spokesperson said: "We know how disruptive this closure has been for the communities of Largs, Skelmorlie and Wemyss Bay, and for families in particular.

"Getting the school buses through safely has been a priority for the team this week, and we are pleased that the escorted arrangement is now running."

A signed diversion remains in place through Port Glasgow, Kilmacolm, Johnstone, Kilbirnie and Largs. Drivers should allow extra time.

Routenburn Road, Brisbane Glen Road and Meigle Road are not part of the diversion and should not be used for through traffic.
