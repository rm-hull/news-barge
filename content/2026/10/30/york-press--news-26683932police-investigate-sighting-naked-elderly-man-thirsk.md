POLICE have launched an investigation after reports of a sighting of a naked elderly man close to a North Yorkshire school.

The reports indicate that the man was spotted putting his bins out at an address in School Garth, close to Thirsk School and Sixth Form College, on Wednesday (October 28).




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: “We received a report relating to this incident at 8.40pm on Wednesday 28 October and enquiries remain ongoing.

“Anyone with information that could assist our enquiries is asked to contact us online via our website.

“Please select ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘An existing case or report’. Live Chat is also available through our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote reference 12260212514 when providing information.”
