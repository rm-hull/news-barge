# Leeds Bradford Airport station plans 'absolutely critical' as leaders issued update

## West Yorkshire councillors have discussed plans for an estimated £65m parkway station to serve Leeds Bradford Airport.

A proposed parkway station serving Leeds Bradford Airport has been described as "absolutely critical" for the region at a recent meeting.

Councillors from across West Yorkshire backed plans to press ahead with a rail station to serve the airport, with Bradford Council's Leader declaring he was "delighted" to lend his support to the project.

West Yorkshire Combined Authority received a progress report on the proposals at a meeting on Thursday, October 29, during which West Yorkshire Mayor Tracy Brabin stated she was "determined" to ensure the station ultimately receives the green light. However, members were warned it could still take as long as two years for a full business case for the station to gain approval.

Proposals for a rail station serving the airport were first floated more than a decade ago, with an outline business case given the go-ahead in 2020, yet little headway has been made since. A report presented to members suggested the parkway station could be constructed on the Harrogate line, positioned between Horsforth Station and Bramhope Tunnel, with passengers then travelling to the airport via a shuttle bus service.

The station is estimated to carry a price tag of around £65m.

The report highlighted that the absence of a nearby rail station is acting as a "drag" on the airport's growth, adding that a new facility would substantially boost the number of passengers able to reach LBA via public transport. It would additionally support the development of a Northwest Leeds Employment Hub, a 36-hectare strategic employment site adjoining LBA, while also enabling further housebuilding in the surrounding area.

Introducing the report, West Yorkshire Mayor Tracy Brabin said: "This station has long been an ambition for our region. I know some have been frustrated by the pace of progress, but this development will be a game changer.

"I am determined to make sure that this project finally gets approved."

She revealed that she would be addressing an event alongside Leeds Bradford Airport executives the following week, where they will "set out the vision to get this delivered."

Members were invited to "endorse continued development of the LBA Parkway project including:

* "Completion of a Full Business Case.
* "Completion of necessary Ground Investigation work at the site.
* "Identification of a workable timetable for LBA Parkway, including definition of any network enhancements required, and.
* "Identification of a funding strategy for the station. "

Leeds Councillor Salma Arif (Labour) welcomed the proposal, saying: "I really welcome this, and what it will mean for jobs in the area."

Bradford Council Leader Stephen Place added: "I'm delighted to be supporting this. Leeds Bradford Airport is a vital hub for the region.

"I do believe a shuttle bus from Apperley Bridge Station would alleviate a lot of transport issues for passengers in the area."

Leeds Councillor Alan Lamb (Cons) said: "This is absolutely critical for Leeds and the wider region. It is a tricky project to deliver because of the topography."

He stated it would also create employment opportunities and release housing land, noting it would deliver a "huge impact" extending well beyond merely transport links to and from the airport.

He questioned officers about potential timescales for the works, asking: "Can you tell us when people will be able to start getting trains?"

Simon Pope, Transport Capital Programme Director, responded: "It will be at least two years to get to the point where we have a full business case. " He explained this would require investigations into the level of rail service the proposed station could accommodate.

Members approved proceeding with the proposals.

The West and North Yorkshire Chamber of Commerce has also voiced strong support for the scheme. Mark Casci, Head of Policy and Representation at the Chamber, said: "The Chamber is massively supportive of any efforts to build a Parkway Station for Leeds Bradford Airport.

"We would wish to see a positive and speedy decision made regarding its approval.

"The leadership team at Leeds Bradford Airport is very ambitious and its recent refurbishment is delivering a much better passenger experience, but the key next step is connectivity and new routes, something the station will enable."
