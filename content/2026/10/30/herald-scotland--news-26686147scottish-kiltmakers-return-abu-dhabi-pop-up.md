A Scottish kiltmaker is bringing a taste of Highland tradition back to the Middle East with an exclusive fitting service in Abu Dhabi.

MacGregor and MacDuff, will return to the UAE on November 14 and 15, offering customers the chance to experience its Highlandwear service without travelling to Scotland.

The fittings will take place at The Club Abu Dhabi on Saadiyat Island, with the company bringing more than 45 years of kiltmaking experience to the event.

Customers will receive a professional fitting with one of the company’s stylists, alongside measuring and styling advice for kilts, trews and full Highland outfits.

More than 4,000 tartans will be available, allowing customers to personalise their chosen look.

The visit follows the success of the company’s first UAE fitting service in 2025. Positive feedback from that event has prompted MacGregor and MacDuff to consider making Abu Dhabi a regular stop.

Lauren Canning, fittings service manager, said: "Following the success of our first UAE fitting service in 2025, we will be returning to Abu Dhabi to share our passion for craftsmanship and authentic Highlandwear once again."
