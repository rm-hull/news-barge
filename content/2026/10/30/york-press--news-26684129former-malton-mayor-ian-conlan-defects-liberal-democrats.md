A FORMER mayor of a North Yorkshire town has defected parties announcing plans to stand in next year's local elections.

Former Green Party Mayor of Malton, Ian Conlan, has defected to the Liberal Democrats and is set to stand as a candidate in the 2027 North Yorkshire Council elections.

Ian said he brings experience of local government and a track record of environmental campaigning, to the party and was joining a team committed to delivering practical change for communities across North Yorkshire.

Ian said: “The Liberal Democrats offer a practical, sustained approach to environmental policy, backed by strong local teams, community involvement and a proven track record of delivering change. While the Greens have centralised their own structures, the Lib Dems champion local accountability and empower communities to flourish.

“As the former Mayor of Malton for three years, I worked to deliver greener policies and strengthen local democracy. Amotherby and Ampleforth Councillor Steve Mason - a member of the Green Liberal Democrats - has been a consistent and active supporter of that work, demonstrating the value of effective cross-party collaboration.

“The Lib Dems are the leading opposition on North Yorkshire Council, with a real opportunity to deliver fairer school transport, better active travel policies and greater community influence over planning decisions. Having led successful local initiatives, including the county’s 20’s Plenty campaign, I know the importance of turning environmental ambition into practical action. I believe the Lib Dems offer the leadership and governance needed to deliver that change across North Yorkshire.

“I am happy to be standing as a candidate in next year’s elections as part of the Lib Dem campaign to take control of the council. I have been frustrated by the direction of the Green Party of late; it’s simply not the caring party I joined in 2015. I’m glad to have found a new home in the Lib Dems.”

Liberal Democrat Councillor Steve Mason, who has welcomed the defection, said: “I have known Ian since working together on the anti-fracking campaign here in Ryedale, and know from experience that his values match ours. His diligence and commitment to the community are unquestionable, and Ian is a great asset in helping us defend liberal values, support residents and communities, and protect the environment.

“As for being the greenest? I know that we have the best, most realistic policies to protect the environment. Ian will feel right at home; I look forward to working with him.”

Leader of the Liberal group in North Yorkshire, Councillor Peter Lacey, said: “We’re excited to welcome Ian to the team ahead of next year’s Council elections. His experience, skills and energy will be a real asset to our growing team, bringing fresh perspectives and a strong commitment to delivering for local residents.

“I’m convinced that, working together, we can build on the momentum behind the Liberal Democrats and give voters across North Yorkshire a positive alternative, putting us in a strong position to win through in May.”
