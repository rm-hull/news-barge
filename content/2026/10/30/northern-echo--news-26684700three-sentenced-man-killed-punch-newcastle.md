The family of a "deeply loved" dad killed in a single punch have said they will never get over his death.

Simon Beston, 55, suffered serious head injuries when he was struck during an altercation on St Andrew’s Street in Newcastle’s Chinatown shortly before 10.30pm on July 26, 2025.

Newcastle Crown Court heard he had been trying to break up a fight when Ethan Cockram struck him, causing the dad to fall and hit his head.

He remained in hospital for several weeks, but died from his injuries on August 16.

Appearing before the court on Thursday (October 29) three people, including Simon's killer, were sentenced.

Ethan Cockram, 21, of Durham Street, Wallsend, admitted manslaughter and was jailed for four years and ten months.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651277.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ethan Cockram *(Image: Northumbria Police)*

Shayne Cook, 20, of Winifred Gardens, Wallsend, admitted affray and was jailed for 18 months.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651280.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Shayne Cook *(Image: Northumbria Police)*

Lola Charlton, 20, of Fairhaven Avenue, Walker, was convicted of affray and given a nine-month sentence suspended for 24 months.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651278.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lola Charlton *(Image: Northumbria Police)*

Following the sentencing, Simon’s family spoke of how his death has devastated their lives.

His mother Mary said: "I was on holiday with my older son and family when I got the most devastating news I could possibly receive. My world just fell apart.

"We went straight to the hospital on our return and what I saw will stay with me for the rest of my life. My son lying there with so many tubes, monitors and all I wanted to do was give him a hug.

"The day Simon died part of me went with him and for the rest of my life, I’ll never get over losing him."

His daughter Chloe described the lasting impact of her father’s death and the difficulty of coming to terms with what happened.

She said: "I did not lose my Dad due to old age or ill health, I lost him as a result of someone else’s actions which has made the grieving process even more difficult and much more traumatic."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651360.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Simon was killed after an altercation near Chinatown, on St Andrews Street, Newcastle. *(Image: Google)*

Simon’s partner, Maxine, also spoke about the life they had shared and the future they had planned together.

She said: "He was the person I shared my home, my life and my future with.

"In one moment, everything we had planned and everything I knew was taken away from me."

The court heard that the incident began with a confrontation between a man, a woman and a small group.

Simon was punched in the head and fell to the ground, suffering injuries which ultimately proved fatal.

An investigation by Northumbria Police’s Major Investigation Team led to the arrests of five people.

Three were charged and two were released without further action.

Detective Inspector Ashley Loader of Northumbria Police said: "No result will change or reverse the tragic events leading to Simon’s untimely death, and our thoughts remain with all his loved ones as they continue to navigate life without him.

"This deeply sad case highlights the far-reaching consequences violence can have on individuals, families and communities.
