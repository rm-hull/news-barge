Police have said they are carrying out active enquiries into the time Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell spent at Balmoral, the King's private estate in Aberdeenshire.

The nature of the information received by officers has not been disclosed, and Police Scotland has not provided further details about the scope of its investigation.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "We have received information and active enquiries are ongoing but these are at a very early stage."

Police added that all enquiries are evidence-led and that it would be inappropriate to comment further.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20487237.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, seen here in a photo from the 'Epstein files', denies all wrongdoing *(Image: US DOJ)*

The development follows an appeal issued by the force earlier this year seeking information about a September 2006 flight into Edinburgh Airport on Epstein's private jet.

Flight records show Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Maxwell were among those on board.

Balmoral has featured in previous disclosures relating to Epstein and Maxwell. During Maxwell's 2021 sex-trafficking trial in the United States, prosecutors released photographs showing Epstein and Maxwell on the Balmoral estate during a 1999 visit.

Court evidence included images of the pair outside a log cabin on the Aberdeenshire estate.

Reports have previously stated that Epstein and Maxwell visited Balmoral in 1999 as guests of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, then known as Prince Andrew.

Further references to Balmoral emerged through the release of US Department of Justice files, which included a 2001 email sent to Maxwell by a person identified only as "A" from Balmoral.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/9463950.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Balmoral Castle

The documents also contained flight logs recording journeys involving Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Maxwell to and from Scotland.

Maxwell was convicted in New York in 2021 of recruiting and grooming teenage girls for Epstein's sexual abuse and is serving a 20-year prison sentence.

Epstein died in a New York jail cell in 2019 while awaiting trial on sex-trafficking charges.

Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor has repeatedly denied wrongdoing in relation to his association with Epstein and Maxwell.
