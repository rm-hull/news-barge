A woman breached a protective order brought in to tackle anti-social behaviour in Durham city centre by drinking alcohol concealed in a sports drink bottle.

Lindsey Smith was seen taking a swig from the bottle which was found to contain alcohol in North Road, Durham, at 12.30pm on August 7.

Peterlee magistrates were told Smith had earlier been seen with a can of cider at Tesco on North Road, which is within the city-centre protection order’s boundary.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633644.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Woman breached Public Spaces Protection Order by drinking alcohol concealed in a sports drink bottle in North Road, Durham

Josh Peacock, for Smith, said she knew about the order after a neighbourhood warden warned her a few days earlier.

Mr Peacock described it was a “fairly low-level breach” which could be dealt with by way of a fine.

He added that the defendant had told him she had never previously been in trouble with police.

Smith, 32, of Princes Street, Bishop Auckland, admitted breaching the Public Spaces Protection Order.

The magistrates gave Smith a six-month conditional discharge and ordered her to pay £26 in costs.
