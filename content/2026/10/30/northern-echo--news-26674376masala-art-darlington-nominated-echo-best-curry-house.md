A Darlington restaurant run by a chef with 24 years of experience is in the running to be named The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Masala Art has been welcoming diners to its Houndgate restaurant for the last four years - and is continuing to go from strength to strength.

The venue, which is run by director and head chef Sanjay Mohakud, prides itself on being the “epitome of authentic exquisite contemporary Indian cuisine” in the town.

Sanjay is originally from Odisha, state on the eastern coast of India, and moved to the UK in 2002 where he worked in a number of high profile venues across the region.

The 48-year-old enjoyed stints as head chef at popular street food spot Dabbawal, in Newcastle, and at the former Raj Bari in Yarm, which he left in 2022 to open Masala Art.

Now, Masala Art is competing to be named as the winner of the Echo’s Best Curry House Competition alongside nine other restaurants in Darlington and County Durham.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649581.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Masala Art *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

“For the last couple of years, we have been named in the top ten,” dad-of-two Sanjay said.

“We have very loyal customers here. We have been going for four years and have been going from strength to strength. It is nice to be nominated.”

With a 4.6 rating on Google, Masala Art is praised online for “absolutely delicious” food with an “authentic and modern” menu.

One diner said: “The service was great. I've been to some amazing curry restaurants and this is up there as one of the best. The food was absolutely incredible!”

Masala Art is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Arvelli Indian Restaurant, Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.

Readers will decide the winner in a public vote, and we will be featuring each of the businesses nominated in the next two weeks.
