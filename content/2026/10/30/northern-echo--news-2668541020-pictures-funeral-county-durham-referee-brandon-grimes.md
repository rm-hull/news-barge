A County Durham man killed in a hit-and-run in Florida was given an emotional final send-off as mourners gathered to celebrate his life, tragically cut short.

Brandon Grimes, 26, from Seaham, was remembered with tears and laughter at a packed funeral, where mourners heard his own rendition of Liverpool FC's anthem, You'll Never Walk Alone.

His performance brought laughter and tears before Trimdon Brass Band played the anthem again as mourners left the church.

Every guest was given a red badge reading 'You'll never walk alone Brandon' with the song's lyrics.

A sea of red filled the church as family and friends wore Liverpool shirts and colours, while football referees attended in their kits.

Brandon's closest family wore shirts bearing 'Brandon 26' on the back.

Brandon, from Seaham, was on holiday with a friend when a Ford Edge SUV hit him and fled. He was trying to cross the US192 highway on International Drive in Kissimmee, around 20 miles south of Orlando, in the early hours of Friday, September 26, Florida Highway Patrol said.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653417.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

A flower-covered hearse led the way at his funeral at St John's Church in Seaham on Friday (October 30), followed by a double-decker bus carrying Brandon and his family, decorated with banners and posters bearing his name.

Brandon's parents, Clair and Wayne, four siblings, young nieces and nephews and his friend of 10 years, Paul, paid emotional tributes at the service.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653416.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

His parents told mourners: "Our beautiful boy Brandon, our hearts are aching, shattered and quite frankly we are still struggling to comprehend the reality of a world without our cherished, beautiful boy. We adore you, and our lives will never ever be the same."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653420.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The priest called Brandon's death "a tragedy of phenomenal proportions."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653418.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

After the service, the family travelled to Durham Crematorium before celebrating Brandon's life at Seaham Town Hall.

The driver allegedly fled before police arrived. Henry Guerra-Pulgar, 54, was arrested on October 6 on suspicion of with leaving a fatal crash.

More than £19,566 was raised to help bring Brandon home and cover some of his funeral costs.

Brandon was a grassroots football referee for Durham County Football Association, having refereed grassroots football at weekends.
