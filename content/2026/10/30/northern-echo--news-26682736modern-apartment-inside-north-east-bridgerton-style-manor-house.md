A home set within an 18th century mansion house, surrounded by acres of landscaped grounds sounds like something from Bridgerton, but one North East property is making that dream a reality.

Aidan Flat is a two-bedroom ground-floor apartment in historic Belford Hall near Bamburgh, Northumberland, with a guide price of £295,000.

The property forms part of the main house at Belford Hall, a Grade I listed Georgian manor house designed in the Palladian style.

The mansion house stands among 28 acres of Northumberland scenery, with communal gardens, parkland, woodland and a small lake.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648536.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The ground floor apartment is approached from the east side of Belford Hall, with its own half-glazed double front door to enter through and views over the gardens and parkland to the east and south.

Architect James Paine designed Belford Hall, which was built in the mid-1750s before being restored and converted into apartments.

Aidan Flat offers a peek into the lesser-known past of the hall, its property description describing that "the echoes of its history resonate through its walls," by nature of once being part of the servants’ quarters.

The apartment combines modern facilities with original features including barrel ceilings, stone doorways and several original doors.

The ground-floor apartment has its own half-glazed double front door on the east side of Belford Hall, with views across the gardens and parkland.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648545.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Belford Hall is a Grade I listed Georgian manor house designed in the Palladian style, which stands among 28 acres of Northumberland scenery.

The home's two bedrooms are described as having excellent proportions, ideal for either a main residence or second home in the Northumberland Countryside. Residents also have access to the shared reception rooms within the hall.

These include the main hallway, which retains its original flagged stone floor and is entered through the portico entrance.

An Empire-style staircase sits in the entrance hall, while the south-facing drawing room is available to residents for special occasions.

The shared spaces give owners access to parts of the historic building beyond their own flats.

A stone-pillared entrance and long private drive lead to Belford Hall, its gravelled courtyard and parking area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648559.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alongside its modern facilities, many of the property's original features remain within the apartment, including barrel ceilings, stone doorways and several original doors.

The flat comes with a large garage and shared use of about three acres of gardens and grounds around the hall.

Maintenance and upkeep of the gardens and grounds are taken care of as part of the arrangement, giving buyers the opulent setting without full responsibility for managing the estate.

Belford village, with its local amenities, is within walking distance, while Bamburgh is roughly five miles away.

Its historic features, communal facilities and extensive grounds make Aidan Flat far more than a standard apartment.

Agents describe the property as a "rare opportunity to acquire a beautiful home with the grandeur of living within a magnificent manor house."

The guide price for Aidan Flat is £295,000. Prospective buyers can contact the selling agent for further information or to arrange a viewing.
