TWO ATTEMPTED burglaries have been linked to a break-in at a nearby home.

The incidents happened on Belmont Road in Ilkley, and are believed to be connected to a burglary on Colbert Avenue that occurred in the early hours of Sunday, October 25.

West Yorkshire Police said the two attempted break-ins on Belmont Road were reported at around 4.30am, 20 minutes before the burglary on Colbert Avenue.

CCTV footage from the Colbert Avenue burglary shows three masked men entering the home through a downstairs window.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police are investigating a report of a burglary at a residential property on Colbert Avenue, Ilkley, and two attempt burglaries at residential properties on Belmont Road, Ilkley.

"The burglary on Colbert Avenue is reported to have happened around 4.50am Sunday morning (25 October).

"The attempt burglaries on Belmont Road are reported to have happened at around 4.30am on Sunday morning (25 October).

"Enquiries are currently ongoing."
