# Man arrested in London on suspicion of murder of Rahiem Jackson in Leeds

## Raheim died after being attacked last weekend

A man has been arrested in London on suspicion of the murder of Leeds man Rahiem Jackson.

Rahiem Reema Jackson, 38, died in hospital after he was attacked with “weapons” outside Don Robins House, a supported living facility managed by St George’s Crypt, on Hedley Chase, New Wortley, at 10.23pm on Saturday, October 24. It’s suspected a gang of four were involved in the fatal attack.

West Yorkshire Police have now confirmed that a 26-year-old man was arrested overnight by the Met Police on suspicion of murder and remains in police custody. They said inquiries into Rahiem’s death remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates, of the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team said: “This arrest is significant development in the murder investigation of Rahiem Jackson. Officers are continuing to support the family.

“Extensive enquiries remain ongoing and we continue to appeal for information around the attack. We continue to ask anyone who may have seen people arrive or leave the scene on Hedley Chase on Saturday night to come forward.”
