Police are appealing after a 13-year-old on an e-scooter was seriously injured in a crash with a car.

The crash happened on Branwell Avenue, Birstall, at around 1.40pm on Saturday, October 24.

The boy on the e-scooter was seriously injured and was taken to hospital.

Police reported that the driver of the car failed to stop.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for information following a serious injury collision in Birstall.

"The collision happened at around 1.40pm on Saturday (October 24) on Branwell Avenue, Birstall, involving a silver car and an electric scooter.

"The rider of the electric scooter, a 13-year-old boy, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

"The driver of the car failed to stop and has failed to report the incident to police.

"The Roads Policing Unit is investigating the matter and is asking for anyone with information or footage to contact them using our Live Chat facility on our website quoting reference is 0816 of the 24/10."
