# North Yorkshire Police appeal to identify person after criminal damage incident on major road in Harrogate district

## Appeal after criminal damage incident on major road in Harrogate district

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information to help identify a person they would like to speak to following a criminal damage incident on a major road in the Harrogate district.

![The police are asking for the public's help to identify a person they would like to speak to following a criminal damage incident on Kex Gill Road, near Blubberhouses, in the Harrogate district](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/30/10/08/864869508_1654938912999473_7272638241803235394_n.jpeg?trim=376,0,584,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The incident happened on Kex Gill Road, near Blubberhouses, on Sunday, October 11.

Officers are keen to trace a person they believe may be able to assist with their enquiries into the reported criminal damage.

Anyone who recognises the individual or has any information that could help the investigation is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260202039 when providing any details regarding the incident.
