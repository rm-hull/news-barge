A young County Durham referee killed in a hit-and-run in Florida was given an emotional final send-off with a message to his mourning family that they will 'never walk alone'.

Brandon Grimes was remembered with tears and laughter at a packed funeral in his hometown of Seaham on Friday (October 30).

The service at St John's Church ended with a video of him singing his beloved Liverpool FC's anthem You'll Never Walk Alone.

His pitchy performance brought both laughter and tears from mourners as they said their final goodbyes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21511057.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Brandon Grimes was a grassroots referee for the Durham County Football Association on the weekends *(Image: FAMILY)*

The 26-year-old was on holiday with a friend in Florida last month whenhe was hit by a Ford Edge SUV on the US 192 Highway in Kissimmee in the early hours of the morning. A fundraiser to help his family bring him home raised almost £20k.

The grassroots referee's death sparked an outpouring of tributes from County Durham's football community.

Some attended his funeral in their referee kits while others including his close family wore Liverpool shirts with 'Brandon 26' on the back or a splash of the club's colour.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653428.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Trimdon Brass Band played the club's anthem and mourners were given red badges reading 'You'll never walk alone Brandon'.

His coffin, also marked with the club's liver bird was brought to the church in a bus adorned with posters and banners bearing his name, and its service number reading '26 Brandon Grimes'

The priest described Brandon's death as "a tragedy of phenomenal proportions."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653421.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

His parents Clair and Wayne added: "Our hearts are aching, shattered and quite frankly we are still struggling to comprehend the reality of a world without our cherished, beautiful boy. We adore you, and our lives will never ever be the same.

"And now we are completely lost without you. As we navigate this, suffocating agony, we also want to remember the incredible person that you were, you brought so much light, joy, laughter and pride into our lives.

"You loved with your whole unfiltered heart, always quick to forgive and eager to start a fresh.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653426.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"You are resilient, deeply kind, smart and protective of the people you love. Now at 26, Brandon you still have that baby face and the most infectious smile.

"To know you is to love you. You don't just walk into a room, you fill it. You turned into such an incredibly wonderful young man, you are the life and soul of the party. You brought so much laughter and joy to everyone fortunate enough to cross your path. You have a heart of gold."

The congregation heard how Brandon entered the world as a six-pound-two-ounce baby described by his family as 'pure magic', with the brightest and most beautiful eyes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653424.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

His family spoke of their treasured memories together, particularly their annual two-week trips to Flamingo Land with extended family.

Brandon's passion for sport and refereeing was also remembered, with his family saying: "You squeezed every drop of experience, joy and adventure out of the time we had before it was taken away.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653429.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"You are loved beyond measure and missed beyond words always our beautiful boy Brandon, forever and always."

His four siblings said he "brought so much sparkle" and "the room always brightened the second you walked in."

His friend of ten years, Paul, shared memories of their friendship, which began through sport and refereeing. He remembered Brandon as someone who could make people laugh even on their hardest days.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653417.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Paul said: "Brandon was one of the funniest lads you would ever meet, he was an absolute character.

"Brandon had that cheeky personality where you never knew what was going to come out of his mouth next but that's what made him Brandon.

"He would make you laugh on your hardest days and would make people laugh without even trying. He lived life to the absolute max and didn't do things by halves.

"Brandon was always making people laugh, always being cheeky and showed his affection in his own special way, and I think its those little things that hurt the most when someone is no longer here, its not just the big memories we miss but its the every day things."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653423.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The funeral of 26-year-old young referee Brandon Grimes in Seaham *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Florida Highway Patrol said Brandon was crossing the US192 near International Drive in Kissimmee when he was struck by a westbound vehicle.

He was pronounced dead at the scene.

The driver allegedly fled before officers arrived.

A 54-year-old man, Henry Guerra-Pulgar, was arrested on October 6 on suspicion of leaving the scene of a crash involving a death. Florida Highway Patrol has been contacted for an update.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21511056.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Brandon Grimes, 26, from Seaham was killed in a suspected hit-and-run while on holiday in Florida *(Image: FAMILY)*
