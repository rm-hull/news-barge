Winter announces itself softly before it arrives in earnest. There is the first frost silvering the garden, the first morning when breath hangs visible in the air, and the first evening when daylight has gone by late afternoon and the windows across the street glow gold. It is then that the wardrobe quietly rearranges itself, and the good scarf is lifted down from its shelf.

The most satisfying winter dressing has little to do with heaviness and everything to do with intelligence: soft layers against the skin, a considered silhouette over them, and one generous piece to gather it all together.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650717.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinburgh Cashmere *(Image: Angel Montellano)*

A cashmere wrap around the shoulders on the walk home, a jumper that holds its shape through a long season, a coat that carries the cold with composure. These are the quiet pleasures of the colder months, and they are the pieces we find ourselves returning to year after year.

At Edinburgh Cashmere, our winter clothing brings together handcrafted scarves, knitwear and outerwear in cashmere, lambswool and merino wool, chosen for warmth that feels as good as it looks.

## **Scottish heritage, handcrafted for the colder months**

Edinburgh Cashmere is a Scottish heritage brand, and our winter pieces are handcrafted from cashmere, lambswool, merino wool and fur, selected for the warmth and softness they bring to the season. Our knitwear is designed to last, with 4-ply construction, long-staple fibres and high-density knits that suit ultra-warm layering through the winter months.

Scotland's textile traditions, its tartans and checks and its long relationship with fine fibres, inform the character of our designs. They are quiet influences rather than loud statements: patterns that have been worn for generations, interpreted for the way people dress today.

Colour follows the season, from understated shades of brown, camel and grey to brighter tones for the days that call for them. A winter wardrobe may be as quiet or as spirited as its owner wishes, and the most satisfying ones tend to be a little of both.

A well-chosen winter piece becomes a long companion. It is worn on the first cold morning, reached for again in the depths of January, and carried gratefully into the first mild days of spring.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650722.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinburgh Cashmere *(Image: Angel Montellano)*

## **The art of winter layering**

Winter dressing rewards a good sense of proportion. It begins with the finest layer, a slim-fit cashmere sweater designed to sit effortlessly beneath a coat or jacket. Over it comes a jumper or cardigan in soft cashmere blends, then the outer layer, and finally the scarf that gathers everything together and keeps the wind at bay.

Whether the day holds a brisk commute, a long walk in the frost or an evening by the fire, there is a natural place to begin in our women's winter clothing and men's winter clothing.

## **Winter pairings, quietly perfected**

Some combinations feel inevitable once they have been worn. These are the pairings we find ourselves returning to every winter.

**Cashmere jumper with a lambswool scarf** - The quintessential winter ritual: a v neck or round neck cashmere jumper, soft and well loved, beneath a lambswool scarf in tartan or check. The textures differ and the effect is harmonious, a quiet play between the fine and the cosy.

**Fur coat with a cashmere scarf** - Wrapped around the neck for luxurious warmth, or allowed to fall naturally for an effortless layer, a cashmere scarf is the most graceful companion to a fur coat.

**Poncho with a scarf** - A cashmere or wool poncho sits beautifully beside a cashmere or lambswool scarf, one softly draped over the shoulders and the other gathered at the neck, for warmth that never feels heavy-handed.

**Wool wrap with a cashmere jumper** - A wool wrap and a cashmere jumper are a natural pairing for winter days, and the same wrap layered over a skirt or dress turns an evening out into something altogether more chic.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21650723.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinburgh Cashmere *(Image: Angel Montellano)*

## **Scarves, shawls and wraps for the coldest days**

Nothing says winter quite like a scarf. Ours are handwoven with long-staple fibres for softness and endurance, and each can be drawn up over the nose, mouth and ears when the wind sharpens, or left to fall loosely as an easy finishing line to a coat.

**Cashmere scarves** - Luxuriously soft and gracefully versatile, our women's cashmere scarves and men's cashmere scarves are enduring essentials for the season. Pure cashmere offers indulgent softness alongside a dependable layer of protection from chilly winds, and looks equally natural wrapped close at the neck or allowed to fall over a fur coat.

**Lambswool scarves** - Our women's lambswool scarves and men's lambswool scarves are handcrafted to be worn, loved and remembered through the winter months. Supremely cosy and designed to complement every ensemble, they are the scarves that become part of the daily routine: draped over a coat on the way out, and left on the hook by the door at night.

**Merino wool scarves** - A fine, soft fibre sits at the heart of our women's merino wool scarves and men's merino wool scarves, a refined choice for layering above or beneath a coat.

**Tartan scarves** - Tartan has symbolised identity, craftsmanship and tradition in Scotland since the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. Our women's tartan scarves and men's tartan scarves reinterpret that legacy in pure cashmere and lambswool for the modern wardrobe. Each drapes effortlessly around the neck, and rises over the nose and ears as the temperature falls.

**Check scarves** - The check is a hallmark of Scotland's textile weaving expertise, and our women's check scarves and men's check scarves pair that heritage with a modern, uncluttered look in cashmere and lambswool. Graphic and quietly confident, they sit as well with a charcoal overcoat as with a favourite knit.

**Wool wraps and shawls** - Crafted from pure wool, our women's wool wraps and shawls and men's wool wraps and shawls range from superfine and luxuriously light to exquisitely soft ultrafine. A wrap sits beautifully over a coat, across a skirt or dress for a winter evening, or alongside a cashmere jumper for slower days at home.

## **Knitwear for every winter wardrobe**

Knitwear is the heart of the winter wardrobe, the layer that lives closest to the skin and is worn most often. Softness matters, and so does shape: a knit that drapes well, holds its form and moves easily beneath a coat. We have given both equal attention, in cashmere blends and across styles that range from classic to relaxed.

**Jumpers and cardigans** - Woven from blends of natural cashmere fibres for supreme warmth, our women's winter jumpers and cardigans and men's winter jumpers and cardigans are coveted for their irresistibly soft touch and classic design. V neck and round neck styles form the foundation of a winter wardrobe, and with a cashmere or lambswool scarf they become a ritual, perfected, rather than simply an outfit.

**Sweaters** - Whether the mood is chunky and warm or sleek and lightweight, our women's winter sweaters and men's winter sweaters offer a generous range of colours, from staple neutrals to lively tones. The slim-fit cashmere knits are designed to layer effortlessly beneath coats and jackets.

**Ponchos and capes** - Part jumper, part jacket and part shawl, our women's ponchos and capes are a considered answer to winter layering. Handcrafted in cashmere and lambswool, in styles from plain to printed, they are at their most cosy beside a cashmere or lambswool scarf.

## **Outerwear of quiet refinement**

A coat is the final word in winter dressing, the piece that meets the weather first. It should carry warmth with grace and sit well over the layers beneath it.

Elegant, flattering and undeniably stylish, our women's fur coats and jackets and men's fur coats and jackets are crafted from pure fur to insulate against the cold while keeping a refined silhouette. A range of colours and styles is available, and a cashmere or lambswool scarf makes a graceful companion.

## **The quiet pleasure of a winter well dressed**

There is a particular contentment in being properly dressed for the cold: the scarf wound once more around the neck, the collar turned up, the hands tucked into soft sleeves, and the whole season ahead with something fine around the shoulders.

A winter wardrobe is built in layers, and so is the season itself. The first frost gives way to the hush of midwinter, to the long dark evenings of January, to the first thaw and the first unexpected warmth of the sun. Through all of it, the right pieces remain constant: familiar, comforting and quietly reassuring.
