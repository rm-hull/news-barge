York singer Twinnie has been announced as the live vocalist for the 2026 UK tour of Love Actually Live in Concert.

She will perform in every city except London, including a date at the Barbican in York on December 12.

The tour will feature a live orchestra performing the film’s score in sync with a high-definition screening.

Twinnie will sing six songs from the soundtrack, including Here with Me by Dido and God Only Knows by The Beach Boys.

Twinnie said: "Love Actually is such a huge part of Christmas, and these songs are so woven into the film.

"Both Sides Now breaks my heart every time I watch it.

"To sing them live with a full orchestra while the film plays behind me is a real dream – I can’t wait."

Love Actually, written and directed by Richard Curtis, tells interwoven stories of love and connection in the weeks leading up to Christmas.
