FORTY-FOUR vape shops are set to lose business rates relief following a recent Budget announcement.

The shops in Bradford are among 180 across West Yorkshire affected by Chancellor John Healey’s decision to remove the relief for vape shops and adult gaming centres.

This change follows the introduction of the Vaping Products Duty on October 1, which adds £2.20 to every 10ml of vaping liquid.

Shane Margereson, founder of UK online vape shop Ecigone, said: "Vapers have already seen prices go up since the new duty started on 1 October, and losing rates relief adds another cost for high street vape shops on top of that.

"Most of these are small, local businesses helping adults move away from cigarettes, so the worry is that higher costs for legal shops make illegal vapes look cheaper by comparison."

The number of vape shops in West Yorkshire is based on Ecigone’s analysis of Google Maps listings, with 55 in Leeds, 44 in Bradford and 32 in Kirklees.

A spokesman for the Government said the decision to pass costs on to customers is a commercial one.

Mr Margereson encouraged customers to compare prices, buy from UK-registered sellers, and consider switching to refillable kits to save money.
