Police are appealing to identify a cyclist following criminal damage in Blubberhouses.

The incident happened on Kex Gill Road on Sunday 11th October.

Officers would like to speak to the cyclist pictured in connection with the incident.

*Anyone with information is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference number 12260202039.*

*Alternatively, contact PC Tyson by emailing [Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.)*
