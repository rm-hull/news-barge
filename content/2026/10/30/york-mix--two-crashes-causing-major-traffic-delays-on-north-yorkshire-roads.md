Emergency services are dealing with two crashes close to one another in North Yorkshire this afternoon (Friday).

There’s been a serious crash on the **A169 between Malton and Pickering** in the Marishes area.

#### A169 crash location

North Yorkshire Police said at 4.22pm: “The road is closed and motorists are asked to find an alternative route while emergency services work at the scene.”

The AA says: “Road blocked and queueing traffic due to accident on A169. Both ways between Marishes Low Road and Thornton Lane.”

The second crash has blocked the **A64 near Rillington** in the Malton area.

#### A64 crash location

At 4.28pm, North Yorkshire Police said: “Please avoid the area at this time while emergency services work at the scene.

“Thank you for your patience, we hope to have the road clear in the next hour subject to vehicle recovery.”

The AA reports: “Road blocked and long delays due to accident on A64 Both ways from Sands Lane to Scampston turn off.”
