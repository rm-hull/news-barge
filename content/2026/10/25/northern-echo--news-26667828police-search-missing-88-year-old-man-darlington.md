Police are concerned for the welfare of a missing man in his 80s last seen in Darlington.

Durham Police said 88-year-old Kenneth was last seen in Darlington at around 7.20am on Saturday morning (October 24).

Kenneth is described as about 5ft 5ins tall, with grey hair and is believed to be wearing a checked shirt, green gilet, jeans and possibly a green jacket.

He is believed to be driving a silver Hyundai Tucson, registration plate EK08 XXN.

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “If you have seen him or know where he is, please call us on 101, quoting incident number 195 of October 24.”
