**More than a hundred flights across Scotland's airports have been cancelled as Storm Boelo disrupts travel across the country.**

Storm Boelo has brought 80mph gusts and travel disruptions to much of Scotland this weekend, shortly after Storm Austen brought heavy rain to many areas on Friday.

The Herald reported on Saturday that a Ryanair flight to Edinburgh was forced to divert over 600 miles away to Oslo after being unable to land due to strong winds.

Ryanair flight FR7644 departed Faro in Portugal at around 2pm local time on Saturday, October 24, bound for the Scottish capital.

Data from FlightRadar24 showed the plane attempted one landing at Edinburgh Airport before the flight crew made the decision to divert to Oslo Gardermoen Airport.

The plane then landed safely in the Norwegian capital at around 5:05pm local time.
