A woman from Thirsk has won £5,000 and secured a place on Millionaire's Row after a dramatic victory on ITV's Win Win with Postcode Lottery.

Vonnie claimed the final spot in next week's showdown during Saturday night's episode after defeating fellow contestant Faye in a tense head-to-head contest.

The pair had to name answers in the category "Top Ten Instructions or Rules Brits Ignore". Vonnie won, taking £5,000 and a place in the programme's final round.

![Vonnie from Thirsk.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21628048.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Vonnie from Thirsk. *(Image: HELLY DOLLY LTD)*

Speaking to hosts Mel Giedroyc and Sue Perkins about what a big win would mean to her family, Vonnie said: “I am a partner to my fiancé Sol, I've got two beautiful boys at home and a stepdaughter who's 16.

"It would just mean so much to all of us.”

After learning she had secured her place on Millionaire's Row, an emotional Vonnie spoke about sharing the moment with her family.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21628052.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mel, Sue and Vonnie. *(Image: HELLO DOLLY LTD)*

“Everybody here is so amazing and I mean that.
