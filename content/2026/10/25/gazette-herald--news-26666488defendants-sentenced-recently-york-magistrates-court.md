A man who assaulted two other men and a man who assaulted one woman twice were among defendants sentenced recently at York Magistrates Court.

Christopher Bullivant, 42, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to using homophobic threatening or abusive words or behaviour to a police officer in Whip-ma-whop-ma-gate.

He pleaded guilty to assaulting a man in Sainsbury’s Piccadilly branch.

He was jailed for six weeks suspended for six months and ordered to pay £50 to each complainant.

Arthur Broadley, 50, of Common Lane, Warthill, pleaded guilty to breaking a restraining order.

He was given an eight-week prison sentence suspended for 22 weeks on condition he does 25 days’ rehabilitative activities.

He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Scott Oliver, 49, of Foxcroft Drive, Brighouse, Calderdale, pleaded guilty to assaulting a woman twice and damaging a mobile phone.

Both offences happened in South Milford.

He was given 12 weeks’ imprisonment suspended for 18 months on condition he does a 26-day rehabilitative programme and 15 days’ rehabilitative activities.

He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £154 statutory surcharge.

Bartosz Wielgomas, 22, of Willow Grove, Boroughbridge, pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified and speeding on the A168 near Dishforth Airfield.

He pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified at Markington near Harrogate while on a community order.

He was given a 24-week prison sentence suspended for 12 months on condition he does 10 days’ rehabilitative activities and 120 hours’ unpaid work.

He was banned from driving for 12 further months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Jordan Ellerby, 31, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to assaulting a man and racially abusing him in Tower Street, York, and causing actual bodily harm to a second man.

He was jailed for 24 weeks.
