# 'Most beautiful walk in England' with waterfalls and dramatic views is hour from Leeds

## If you're looking for a scenic autumn walk this October, this woodland area has been dubbed the "most beautiful walk in England" right now and we can totally see why!

Autumn is a truly enchanting time of year, bursting with vibrant colours and a refreshing chill in the air. It's also the perfect time to get ready for some of the festivities like Halloween.

Of course we're well aware of all the gorgeous walks which are near to Leeds, like the Yorkshire Dales. And while it's often celebrated as a waterfall spot that "never gets old", we have another location which could be great for your bucket list this weekend. It's just over an hour from Leeds – and it has everything you need for an autumnal walk.

Content creator Shendrine Wijendra shared a glimpse of Padley Gorge in the Peak District, describing it as the ideal destination for those who "love autumn colours, woodland walks, streams and dramatic views".

She said: "Location: Padley Gorge, Peak District National Park. Trail: Padley Gorge Circular. Starting point: Car park at the corner of Hathersage Road and Owler Bar Road. Finish: Same point – circular route."

Shendrine noted the trail covers approximately 5.8miles or 9.3km and should take around 2-3 hours to complete. She rated the difficulty as "moderate" and advised walkers to "expect some steep sections, uneven paths and woodland terrain".

Highlighting some of the must-see spots, she recommended fans explore Burbage Brook, Padley Gorge, Millstone Edge and Oxhay Woods.

If you need further persuasion, Shendrine explained that autumn is the ideal season to visit, particularly when the trees display their golden hues. After finishing the walk, she suggested stopping in Grindleford for a warm cafe or pub visit.

Padley Gorge offers plenty of activities, including walking, paddling and wildlife watching among the ancient oak and birch woodland of the Peak District National Park.

The gorge itself is under a mile long, but walking the full loop paths, crossing wooden footbridges, and navigating tree roots and rocks add time.

### How to travel to Padley Gorge?

Located in the Peak District, Padley Gorge is just an hour and 15 minutes by car from Leeds. You can also opt for public transport which could take you less than an hour.

It's worth noting Padley Gorge is well worth visiting if you love fairy-tale-like woodlands, gentle streams and scenic nature walks.

You should also bear in mind that parking is limited there and mobile phone reception in the valley can be poor. So ahead of your trip there, ensure you download offline maps or pay via parking apps like RingGo in advance.
