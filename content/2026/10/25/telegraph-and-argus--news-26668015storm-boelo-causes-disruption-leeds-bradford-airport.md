Flights have been cancelled, diverted and delayed due to disruption caused by Storm Boelo.

A number of flights into and out of Leeds Bradford Airport have had to be cancelled, with others diverted or delayed due to strong winds.

In the latest update this afternoon, a spokesman for Leeds Bradford Airport said: "Our teams on the ground are working hard in challenging weather conditions to support customers and manage the ongoing impact of the disruption caused by Storm Bolelo.  
  
"Departures from LBA: Unfortunately, some departing flights have been cancelled. If you are affected, please contact your airline to discuss rebooking or refund options.

"If you are due to fly later today, please check your flight status directly with your airline before travelling to the airport.

"Arrivals into LBA: Some arriving flights have been diverted to other airports. Your airline should provide information about onward ground transportation back to LBA.

"Some arriving flights have also been cancelled.

"Please contact your airline for the latest information and details of any revised arrangements.

"If you are due to arrive at LBA within the next 24 hours, please check directly with your airline for the latest updates.

"If your return flight has been delayed or diverted and you have a car parked with us, you do not need to contact us.

"No additional parking charges will apply as a result of this disruption.

"When you return, please press the help button on the exit barrier to speak to our team.

"We appreciate that this is a challenging situation.

"Our teams are working in very difficult conditions to support passengers as best they can.

"Thank you for your patience and understanding.

"If you have any further questions, please email [customercare@lba.co.uk](mailto:customercare@lba.co.uk), and our team will do their best to assist.

"We will share further updates as more information becomes available."

A yellow wind warning is in force across northern England, with gusts of 50-60mph expected widely and up to 80mph in exposed areas.
