Disney Store is opening its first Outlet store in the UK.

The new Disney Store Pop Up will launch this autumn at McArthurGlen Designer Outlet Cheshire Oaks.

It will offer a mix of new and seasonal full-price products alongside discounted merchandise, multibuy promotions, and special offers to appeal to a wide range of shoppers.

Patrick Sager, VP, Global Disney Store Retail & Marketing, said: "Outlet stores have been a successful part of our retail business around the world for several years.

"As the UK retail landscape and customer shopping habits continue to evolve, we’re excited to open our first UK Outlet store at Cheshire Oaks.

"The Disney Store Pop Up will allow us to reach more Guests and offer a wider range of price points, helping more Guests bring home a little Disney wonder.

"By bringing together new and seasonal products, discounted merchandise and special offers, the store will provide families and fans with another way to shop official Disney merchandise in person, complementing our existing retail portfolio and online offering."

The store will stock a wide selection of official Disney merchandise, including products inspired by Disney, Pixar, Marvel, and Star Wars.

The range will include toys, apparel, accessories, stationery, plush toys, collectables, and dress-up collections.

Selected products will offer savings of up to 40%, with further value available through multibuy deals and other promotions.

The Cheshire Oaks Pop Up will become part of Disney Store’s retail network in the UK and Ireland, which currently includes standalone stores on London’s Oxford Street and Dublin’s Grafton Street, as well as locations inside Selfridges in London, Birmingham, and Manchester Trafford.

More information about the precise opening date will be announced closer to the opening.
