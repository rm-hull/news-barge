Emergency services have responded to dozens of incidents caused by Storm Boelo today (Sunday).

Fire crews have tackled everything from dangerous overhead signs to chimney stack collapses.

The Met Office yellow warning for high winds is in effect until 9pm tonight

Here are some of the emergencies North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service have responded to today.

* **6.40am,  A170**: Kirkbymoorside crew went to a tree fallen in the road. Crews a battery saw cut it up and then cleared it out the roadway.

* **7.23am, Hamilton Drive, York**: Acomb crew went to a fallen tree that had brought down telephone cables.

* **7.33am, The Boulevard, Eastfield**: Scarborough firefighters went to a shed roof hanging dangerously over a neighbouring property. They lifted off the roof using crew power.

* **7.41am, Finkle Street, Richmond**: Richmond crews attended a shop sign dangerously hanging over a pavement. Crews removed the sign using a triple extension ladder.

* **9.09am, Vangarde Way, York**: A sign damaged by the wind was at risk of falling onto the road. An Acomb fire crew secured the sign.

* **9.51am, Prospect Terrace, Newton on Ouse**: Acomb and Easingwold crews responded to reports of a barbecue that had blown over in the wind and become alight. The crew righted the barbecue and isolated the gas supply.

* **1.20pm,  High Street, Skipton**: Mortar fell from a building and onto the pavement. Crews used a triple extension ladder and small tools to bring down loose mortar.

* **1.28pm,  Robin Lane, High Bentham**: a power line came down over a road. North West power ensured the cable was no longer live and removed from the road.

* **1.49pm,  Main Street, Burnsall**: Grassington firefighters went to a tree that had fallen onto a public toilet block affecting the roof. Crews cordoned off the area to prevent access via the public.

* **2.31pm,  Broughton Road, Skipton**: Skipton firefighters attended part of a roof that had been blown off a property. Crews used ladder and ceiling hook to remove the remainder of the fibre glass roof from the second floor.

* **2.38pm, Fairfax Street, York**; York firefighters responded to a chimney stack that had collapsed, falling into the road. Crews used a roof ladder to remove loose debris and left in hands of responsible person on scene.
