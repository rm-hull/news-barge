Images have been released showing how a new £1.25 million "Grand Design-style" home in a York village could look.

The four-bedroom house is on the market in Model Farm, a gated community in Upper Poppleton.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it offers an "exceptional grand design opportunity".

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21605951.jpg?type=mds-article-620)How the new £1.25 million home in Model Farm, Upper Poppleton, York, could look *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

The estate agent said buyers will be able to create a "bespoke new-build home finished to their exact specification".

"‘Working alongside an architect, you can select bespoke finishes and design details, creating a truly one-of-a-kind home tailored to your vision," a Hudson Moody spokesperson said.

They said the development forms part of a "restored collection of Grade II listed buildings", adding: "Each has been thoughtfully renovated to retain its original character while seamlessly integrating modern design and contemporary comforts.

"Surrounded by picturesque countryside, the setting offers peace and tranquillity, while remaining conveniently close to a wide range of amenities within the nearby village.

"The developer provides a rare opportunity to customise the property to your exact specification, subject to budget and final purchase agreement, with a broad selection of high-quality finishes available to bring your dream home to life."
