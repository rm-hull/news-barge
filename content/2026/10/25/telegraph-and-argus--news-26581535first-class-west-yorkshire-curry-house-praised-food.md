When it comes to curry restaurants in and around Bradford, you are absolutely spoiled for choice.

The city is known as the curry capital with so many food spots to choose from offering a range of traditional curries.

Many of these curry houses have won multiple awards making it difficult to pick just one to try out.

However, if you are in the area and want to try somewhere new, you might like Lala’s Restaurant.

## What is Lala’s Restaurant like?

Lala's Restaurant can be found just outside of Bradford on Bradford Road in Pudsey.

The brand also has locations in Wakefield and Huddersfield.

The Pudsey site offers “a warm, magnetic atmosphere” and “traditional Kashmiri recipes passed down through generations”.

The restaurant has several dishes made specifically for the Pudsey location, including a Kashmiri Rogan Josh with slow-braised lamb chunks, yoghurt, garlic, ginger, and Kashmiri chillies.

There is also the masala fish and keema naan stuffed with spiced minced lamb.

Along with these specials, the menu features lots of classics including dopiaza, dhansak, korma and madras options with different meats and fish.

You can also get biryanis and baltis.

To enjoy alongside your curry, you can choose from a range of rice dishes, naans, and vegetable sides.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21500971.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lala's has three locations around West Yorkshire. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Lala’s Restaurant is no stranger to awards and has featured in the top 100 Asian Restaurants list released by the Asian Catering Federation.

It was also named the Pakistani Restaurant of the Year in the Oceanic Awards for 2025.

### What do reviews say about Lala’s Restaurant?

Lala’s Restaurant has a rating of 4.1 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 632 people leaving reviews.

One person said: “Another brilliant trip to Lalas.

“Food was fantastic, no complaints from any of the 11 of us.

“Murad and the rest of the servers couldn’t be more helpful.

“Service was seamless even with how busy it was and the atmosphere was pleasant enough.

“Price was very reasonable too, pleasantly surprised.”

Another person said: “First time visiting Lala's in Pudsey.
