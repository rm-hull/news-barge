# Watch moment Ryanair flight lands in Storm Boelo’s strong winds at Leeds Bradford Airport

## A Met Office yellow wind warning, which has a medium (25-50%) likelihood and a significant impact, is in force for Leeds until 9pm today (Sunday).

Passenger Patryk Korsak, who said the pilots did great, recorded the windy landing as he flew from Poznań to Leeds.

Gusts of 54mph are forecast for the city, with the wind expected to reduce slightly throughout the day but still remaining strong.

![Plane lands in Storm Boelo's strong winds at Leeds Bradford Airport on Saturday 24 October.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/25/13/46/YouTube-Thumbnail-00-x-1080-px)-(10).jpg?trim=7,0,7,0&width=640&quality=65)


An airport spokesperson said: “Our teams on the ground are working hard in challenging weather conditions to support customers and manage the impact of the disruption.

“If you are parked with us and your return flight is delayed, you do not need to contact us. No additional parking charges will apply as a result of the disruption.

“We'd like to thank all customers for their patience and understanding while our teams work through these challenging conditions. We will share further updates as more information becomes available.”

Gusts of up to 41mph and 45mph are forecast for Leeds on Monday 26 October and Tuesday 27 October. 30mph gusts are predicted for Wednesday 28 October, before they drop to 15mph on Thursday 29 October.
