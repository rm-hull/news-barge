**The body of a missing man believed to have fallen from a CalMac ferry has been found on a Scottish beach.**

The body, believed to be 82-year-old Jim Wither, was discovered on Sunday morning at Largs beach.

Jim Wither was last seen walking towards Beith Road around 12.18pm on Sunday, October 18.

Police previously said that enquiries have established that he may have entered the water after boarding a ferry from **Ardrossan** to Arran.

On Sunday at about 8.30pm, **an extensive search was launched** after a man fell from the MV Caledonian Isles near Arran.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "On Sunday, 25 October, 2026, we received a report of the body of a man having been found in the Largs beach area.

"Formal identification is yet to take place, however the family of 82-year-old Jim Wither, who was reported missing from Johnstone, have been informed.

"The death is not being treated as suspicious and a report will be submitted to the Procurator Fiscal in due course."
