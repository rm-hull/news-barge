Emergency services are in attendance at an area of flooding around a care home in Saltcoats.

The incident at a care home on the town's Sidney Street was reported to police on Sunday evening.

Police Scotland said officers are working with partners to ensure the area is made safe and will remain on scene overnight to assist.

"Everyone within the care home has been accounted for and every effort is being made to ensure their safety", the force added.

Road closures are in place on Sidney Street, Winton Circus, The Braes and Melbourne Road.

The public is asked to avoid the area.
