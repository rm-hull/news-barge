Amanda Owen's youngest daughter Nancy is taking on a very different challenge away from the family's Yorkshire Dales farm.

Viewers have become accustomed to seeing the Owen children helping with sheep, feeding animals and tackling the everyday demands of life at remote Ravenseat.

But Nancy's preparations show another side of growing up in the famous farming family.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560464.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nancy Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The youngster is shown rehearsing at a village hall as she works towards her ballet exam.

It is a change of scene from the muddy fields and windswept hillsides that have become familiar to fans of the Owen family's television programmes.

The programme documented Amanda and Clive raising their children at Ravenseat, near Keld in Swaledale.

The family's newer series continues to follow their lives while concentrating on the restoration of Anty John's, a historic farmhouse close to their original home.

Alongside the building work, viewers see the children developing new interests and taking on different responsibilities.
