* Major York attractions have closed and roads in North Yorkshire are blocked as Storm Boelo hits the county bringing gusts of up to 60mph.
* Clifford's Tower, the Yorkshire Museum and Museum Gardens are all closed today (Sunday, October 25) due to the weather.
* The A169 is also shut between Malton and Pickering due to a fallen tree.
