**One of the stars of the hit TV show Stranger Things has been forced to cancel his attendance at an event in Scotland as Storm Boelo continues to cause disruption across the country.**

The actor was due to be one of the main attractions at the event but has had to disappoint fans as his flight has been cancelled, along with hundreds more across the country.

David Harbour was due to attend the event at the Royal Highland Centre near Edinburgh on Sunday.

The event's organisers shared the news in a statement on social media, which read: "David Harbour’s flight has been cancelled and unfortunately David will be unable to attend.

"We have been working through the night to try and get him alternative flights but they either don’t get him here on time or have also been cancelled. We are sorry about this but have exhausted every avenue.

"The other one day guests Noah Schnapp and Tony Robinson are attending. Noah starts at 12pm midday."

Comic Con Scotland is one of Europe's largest pop culture conventions, attracting tens of thousands of fans every year.

Stranger Things fans can see fellow cast member Noah Schapp on Sunday.

In previous years Comic Con Scotland has hosted the likes of Ewan McGregor, Hayden Christensen, Christopher Lloyd, Jean Claude Van Damme, The Undertaker, Matt Smith, Ian Somerhalder, and Alicia Silverstone.
