Finding a good Sunday lunch is rarely difficult in County Durham, but locating one people are willing to travel is a different matter.

When Northern Echo readers were recently asked to share their favourite places for a Sunday roast, one name came up repeatedly: The Copper Mine in Crook.

The pub was among the most-mentioned venues in the discussion, with readers pointing to its reputation for generous portions, traditional cooking and consistently high standards.

Located on High Jobs Hill, The Copper Mine has spent years building a loyal following among diners from Crook, Bishop Auckland, Weardale and beyond.

While it offers a full pub menu throughout the week, its Sunday lunches appear to be one of its biggest draws.

TripAdvisor reviewers regularly single out the roasts when describing their visits, with many praising the homemade feel of the food and the quality of the ingredients.

A common theme among reviews is the size of the meals on offer.

![The Copper Mine, in Crook, has been named among the ten finalists in The Northern Echo Best Pub 2026 competition](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21227210.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Copper Mine, in Crook, was named among the ten finalists in The Northern Echo Best Pub 2026 *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Diners frequently mention towering Yorkshire puddings, generous servings of vegetables and plentiful gravy, alongside a choice of roast meats.

Others praise the balance between quality and value, describing the meals as hearty without feeling too expensive.

The atmosphere also receives regular praise; visitors often describe the venue as warm and welcoming, with friendly staff helping to create the sort of relaxed environment many people look for on a Sunday afternoon.

TripAdvisor's review summary notes that the pub is particularly well regarded for its inviting atmosphere, attentive service and homemade-style cooking.

For some customers, it has become a weekly tradition.

Several reviews reference repeat visits, while others recommend booking ahead because of the pub's popularity at peak times.

In a region packed with respected pubs, restaurants and country inns, The Copper Mine still managed to stand out.
