# Israel votes in first election since 7 October attacks and Gaza war – could Netanyahu lose power?

Israelis will head to the polls on Tuesday (27 October) in the first election since the 7 October attacks and subsequent wars in Gaza, Iran and Lebanon.

Prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu faces a big challenge from former army chief Gadi Eisenkot, with neither expected to secure a parliamentary majority.

But would a change in leadership bring peace in the region?

Both candidates oppose an independent Palestinian state, and support continued Israeli security control over Palestinian territories.

*The Independent’s* chief international correspondent Bel Trew reports.
