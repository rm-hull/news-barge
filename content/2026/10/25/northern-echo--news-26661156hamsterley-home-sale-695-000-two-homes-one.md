Originally dating back to the 1800s, Rose Cottage – a stone-built detached house – has been rebuilt and extensively extended from 2009 onwards.

The result is a spacious and immaculately presented home in a picturesque rural setting with a layout that’s tailor-made for multigenerational living.

In fact, the property’s clever design basically gives buyers two homes under one roof!

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618303.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Thanks to its clever design Rose Cottage offers two homes in one, making it perfect for an array of uses including multigenerational living *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

The ground floor of each side of the house has a fully equipped kitchen, separate living area and cloakroom.

Added to this, one side has a utility room and snug, while the other has a home office.

Each of the two individual first floor landings leads to a family bathroom and a pair of double bedrooms, one of which (on each side) benefits from an en-suite.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618306.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Thanks to its clever design Rose Cottage offers two homes in one, making it perfect for an array of uses including multigenerational living *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

That means everyone, from grandparents to grown-up children, can enjoy their own space and privacy.

One side of the house also has an accessible entrance which makes it a great choice for anyone with mobility needs.

Throughout the house large windows fill the rooms with natural light and frame lovely views across the surrounding countryside, giving the home a calm and welcoming feel.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618310.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Thanks to its clever design Rose Cottage offers two homes in one, making it perfect for an array of uses including multigenerational living *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

Outside, the wraparound gardens are a real highlight.

They include a lawned garden with a large potting shed and a raised gravelled seating area perfect for alfresco dining or relaxing on as you watch the sun go down.

On the eastern side a gentle ramp leads from one of the living rooms down to a stone-paved patio with decking and a further lawn.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618308.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Thanks to its clever design Rose Cottage offers two homes in one, making it perfect for an array of uses including multigenerational living *(Image: Weardale Property Agency)*

To the west, a sheltered, sunny courtyard garden enclosed by mature hedging holds a fascinating piece of history – a brick-built World War II air raid shelter.

Last, but certainly not least, is a driveway that sweeps up to a stone-built double garage which offers potential for further development, subject to obtaining the necessary planning consent.

Rose Cottage lies almost equidistant from Hamsterley and Witton-le-Wear.
