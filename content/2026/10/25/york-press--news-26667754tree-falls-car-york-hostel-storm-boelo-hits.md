A tree has fallen on a car at a York hostel as Storm Boelo hits the city with gusts of up to 55mph expected.

There are no injuries after the tree fell on the car at Astor Hostels in Holgate Road overnight from Saturday (October 24) into Sunday.

Tree surgeons were on site at 7.30am on Sunday removing the tree which had come to rest against the hostel.

![The fallen tree on a car outside Astor Hostels in Holgate Road, York, on Saturday morning (October 25)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21627646.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The fallen tree at Astor Hostels in Holgate Road, York, on Sunday morning (October 25) *(Image: Newsquest)*

A spokesperson for the hostel said the hostel remained open and “everything is fine”.

They said guests that were in rooms where the tree was resting had been moved.

A yellow warning for wind is in place in York and North Yorkshire until 9pm on Sunday.

The Met Office warned travel disruption is expected along with power cuts.

Flying debris is also "likely" and the storm will "probably" bring some damage to buildings, such as tiles blown from roofs, the forecaster said.

Storm Boelo (pronounced Boo-loh) follows Storm Austen, the first named storm of the season , which brought disruption across the country on Friday.
