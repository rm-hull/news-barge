Fans of Our Yorkshire Farm have watched Amanda Owen's nine children grow up on one of the most remote farms in the Yorkshire Dales.

When the Channel 5 programme first aired in 2018, the Owen family became household names thanks to their extraordinary lives at Ravenseat Farm in Swaledale.

Eight years later, the children who once helped their parents with lambing, feeding animals and everyday farm chores are growing into young adults with ambitions of their own.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21617959.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Owen family *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

And with Amanda, her former husband Clive and their children back on television in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, viewers are getting another opportunity to see just how much the family has changed.

## Who are Amanda Owen's nine children?

Amanda and Clive have nine children together: Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Their eldest, Raven, is now 25, while their youngest daughter, Nancy, is 10.

The children have spent much of their lives in the public eye, although each has followed a different path as they have grown older.

## Raven Owen

Raven, the eldest of the nine siblings, has pursued an academic career alongside her appearances on the family's television programmes.

She studied biomedical science at York St John University, graduating with first-class honours.

Although she has spent time away from Ravenseat, Raven continues to return to the family farm.

In the fourth series of Our Farm Next Door, she is seen helping with farm duties alongside younger sister Edith.

## Reuben Owen

Reuben, now 23, has established a television career of his own.

He subsequently fronted Life in the Dales, following his work with heavy machinery and his efforts to build a business.

His career has taken him beyond the family's traditional sheep farming activities, although his Yorkshire roots remain an important part of his story.

## Miles Owen

Miles, now 20, has also become more prominent in the family's television appearances.

The young farmer has shown a particular interest in developing practical agricultural skills.

In Our Farm Next Door, he helps his parents with the restoration of Anty John's, the historic farmhouse the family is renovating near Ravenseat.

He has also spoken about the opportunities to use farming skills beyond the Yorkshire Dales.

## Edith, Violet and Sidney Owen

Edith, 18, Violet, 16, and Sidney, 15, have grown up considerably since viewers first met them on Our Yorkshire Farm.

Edith features in the latest series as she balances her studies with responsibilities at Ravenseat, including preparations for the family's cream tea van.

Sidney is also taking on more practical farming responsibilities, with the latest episodes following his efforts to develop his tractor-driving skills.

## Annas, Clementine and Nancy Owen

The youngest Owen children are Annas, 13, Clementine, 11, and Nancy, 10.

Like their older siblings, they have grown up surrounded by animals, changing seasons and the demanding routines of hill farming.

## Where are the Owen children now?

Although the children are growing up and pursuing different interests, Ravenseat remains central to their family life.

The latest series of Our Farm Next Door follows Amanda and Clive as they work with their children to restore Anty John's, a neighbouring farmhouse intended to provide greater security for the family's future.

For viewers who remember the youngest Owen children from the early days of Our Yorkshire Farm, the new series provides a striking reminder of just how quickly the years have passed.
