**A man has died after being hit by a falling tree which came down due to high winds driven by Storm Boelo.**

The 60-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene at Mid Kelton near Castle Douglas around 10.30am on Sunday morning.

Officers say there are no suspicious circumstances.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: “We were made aware of a 60-year-old man having been struck by a falling tree on a minor road at Mid Kelton near Castle Douglas around 10.30am on Sunday, 25 October, 2026.

“The man was pronounced dead at the scene.

“There are no suspicious circumstances and a report will be submitted to the Procurator Fiscal in due course.”

Storm Boelo has brought 80mph gusts and travel disruptions to much of *Scotland* this weekend, shortly after Storm Austen brought heavy rain to many areas on Friday.

The Met Office has warned of potential injuries and danger to life from flying debris as well as a chance of damage to buildings, power cuts and travel disruption.

The amber wind warning came into force at 9pm on Saturday night, and will remain in place until 9pm on Sunday, with only Orkney remaining under a yellow warning.

The storm brought 80mph wind gusts to Islay, just off the West Coast in Argyll and Bute, overnight on Sunday.

Tiree saw 82mph gusts at about 4am, and in the Glasgow region, the Met Office recorded up to 62mph wind gusts at the same time.
