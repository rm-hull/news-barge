A total of 161,400 customers are currently without power in the UK due to the impacts of Storm Austen and Boelo.

The Energy Networks Association (ENA) has said that network operators have been working hard to "rapidly reconnect customers", but "access to certain areas remains difficult due to fallen trees and other transport disruptions".

The association said another 677,300 customers have been reconnected, following Storms Austen and Boelo.

Lawrence Slade, chief executive of the ENA, said: "Network operators had anticipated that Storm Boelo would bring disruption to households across northern areas of the UK and prepared by moving staff and equipment into place, ready to respond to the worst of the damage."

Anyone experiencing a power cut has been urged to visit powercut105.com to register it with their local network.

Scotland in particular has suffered from strong winds due to Storm Boelo.

Between SP Energy Networks (SPEN) and Scottish and Southern Electricity Networks (SSEN)’s latest updates, around 53,000 homes in Scotland are still without power.

## How long will the storms continue?

Storm Boelo continues to batter northern parts of the UK, with gusts reaching 84mph.

An amber wind warning remains in place until 9pm today (Sunday, October 25) and covers Scotland, parts of Northern Ireland and the far north of England.

There are currently no weather warnings in place for tomorrow (Monday, October 26), but conditions won't remain that way.
