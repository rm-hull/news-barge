Businesses across East Cleveland are getting ready to serve up limited time special deals on food and drink as part of a local council scheme.

Hospitality venues across Redcar and Cleveland will offer special deals to residents and visitors across the region from Wednesday, November 4 until Sunday, November 15.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21616382.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Real Meals, Saltburn. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

A Taste of Redcar and Cleveland will feature fixed-price offers ranging from £5 to £30 or more, depending on the venue.

The council is launching the scheme to showcase local food and drink, attract new customers and give residents more chances to eat out, try old favourites for less or discover somewhere new.

Participating businesses include Cookfellas, The Twisted Peel and Sergios in Guisborough, plus Daisy-Mae’s Vintage Tea Room, Armandos and Koha in Redcar.

Saltburn participants include Real Meals Deli and Café, Scoundrels Coffee Co and Brockley Hall, while Bello Bistro, The Brickyard and Holey Molies are among those taking part across East Cleveland.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21616350.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Twisted Peel, Norton *(Image: Tees Valley Combined Authority)*

Southbank-based NiteLight CIC, which supports homeless people and those struggling, is also taking part.

NiteLight is offering two meals, two sides and two drinks for £15, with all proceeds funding its “pay forward” service for people in need.

NiteLight founder Nadia Gibany said: “We are here because we want to help people, whether it's those unfortunately homeless or just people who are struggling.

“We joined the scheme so that we can give people a great deal for people to eat out. It helps us to spread the word about what we do and invites new customers to try our food.”

NiteLight volunteer Lise Addison said: “We have shower and washing facilities for the homeless and we offer free three course Christmas meals for the homeless and struggling families alongside other services.”

“We also run a free stall every Friday in Southbank among other areas across Teesside where people can get free tea, coffee, cakes and more. It all goes back into the community.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21616327.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nadia Gibany with staff members and volunteers from NiteLight CIC *(Image: Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council)*

Redcar and Cleveland Borough Council leader Cllr Alec Brown said: “It is very heartwarming to see that this campaign is not only supporting local businesses, but also helping organisations give back to the communities and those in need.

“A Taste of Redcar and Cleveland is a fantastic opportunity to celebrate the brilliant food and drink businesses we have right across our borough.

“From cafés to bars, these businesses make an important contribution to our local communities and help make Redcar and Cleveland a great place to live and visit.”

Participating venues also receive free promotion through the Enjoy Redcar and Cleveland tourism platform, social media and printed and digital marketing materials.

A full list of participating businesses and offers can be found on the Redcar and Cleveland council website.
