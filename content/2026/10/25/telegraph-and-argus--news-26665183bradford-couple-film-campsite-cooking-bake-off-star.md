A retired couple from Bradford are cooking up views on YouTube with their travel adventures and campsite cuisine.

Mick and Cath, who split their time between Ripon and Ciudad Quesada in Spain, document their journeys in a motorhome on their channel Our Turn Now.

In a recent episode, they teamed up with former Great British Bake Off contestant Karen Wright to film a special outdoor cooking segment.

Mick and Cath said: "Our lives have always revolved around life on the road.

"Cath worked as a travel agent for many years, and together with Mick spent time driving long-haul routes across the USA and Canada in massive American trucks, and then we spent 14 years working as professional campsite wardens in France, Bedfordshire, and Yorkshire."

Ms Wright prepared a Spaghetti Puttanesca using just a camping stove and basic equipment.

The couple described the experience as "a total joy".

They said: "Cooking a classic Spaghetti Puttanesca with a former GBBO contestant on a basic camping stove proves you don't need a luxury kitchen to make world-class food.

"After a hectic time travelling in Ireland and France this year, pitching up with a fellow Yorkshire foodie to make a fun, light-hearted video was just a perfect moment."
