A Bedale woman has celebrated her 100th birthday with an opera singer, a special cake and a family reunion that brought relatives together from across the world.

Hilda Sharp marked her milestone birthday on October 22 at The Millings Care Home in Bedale, surrounded by loved ones, care home staff, and friends.

The day included a live opera performance, a spread of cheese, fruit, and snacks, and a personalised birthday cake made by chocolatier Ben Ellis.

![Woman celebrating her 100th birthday](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623418.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hilda cuts her birthday cake *(Image: Supplied)*

A care home spokesman said: "A century of memories, family and friendship was celebrated in style as Hilda Sharp marked her 100th birthday at The Millings Care Home in Bedale on 22 October.

"Hilda enjoyed a very special birthday celebration surrounded by family, friends and the team at The Millings, with a day full of music, delicious treats and plenty of reasons to raise a glass.

"The celebrations included a live performance from an opera singer, specially booked for the occasion, while The Millings’ kitchen team prepared a birthday spread of cheese, fruit and nibbles.

![Woman celebrating her 100th birthday](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623414.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hilda’s birthday Cake *(Image: Supplied)*

"A beer pump helped get the party started, making sure there was something to toast this remarkable milestone."

Hilda’s family joined her for lunch, and the care home team gifted her a bottle of her favourite drink, Baileys.

Her birthday cake featured a photo of Hilda at 18, chosen by her daughter.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623416.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The room at The Millings decorated for Hilda’s celebration *(Image: Supplied)*

Her granddaughter travelled from Australia to join the celebration.

Born in 1926, Hilda continues to enjoy staying active and taking part in daily activities at the care home.

Her favourite pastimes include bingo, carpet boules, and spending time with friends and family.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623417.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The room at The Millings decorated for Hilda’s celebration *(Image: Supplied)*

Hilda shared her advice for a long life in just three words: "Just live your life."

The care home spokesman said: "Her words are a fitting reminder to enjoy the moment, treasure the people around you and make the most of every day."

The team at The Millings said they were honoured to help Hilda celebrate her centenary and were delighted to make the day so special.
