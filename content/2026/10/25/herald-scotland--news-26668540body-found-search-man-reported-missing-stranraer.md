A body has been found during searches for a man reported missing in Stranraer.

On Sunday, October 25, police said the body of a man was found in a wooded area in the town.

Formal identification is yet to take place, however the family of Craig Smith, who was reported missing, have been made aware.

The 40-year-old was last seen on the town's Old Port Avenue on Saturday, October 17.

Police said the death is not being treated as suspicious.

A report will be submitted to the Procurator Fiscal in due course.
