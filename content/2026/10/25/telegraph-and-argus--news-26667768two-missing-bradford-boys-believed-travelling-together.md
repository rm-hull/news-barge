Two Bradford teenagers who have been reported missing are believed to be travelling together.

Finley, 13, and Harry, 14, both from Bradford, have been reported missing.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Both Finley and Harry are missing from the Bradford area and are believed to be together."

Finley is described as a white male with brown hair shaved at the sides, around 5ft 1in tall, and was last seen wearing a navy blue tracksuit and a black coat.

Harry is described as a white male with dark brown hair, between 5ft 5in and 5ft 6in tall.

He was last seen wearing a grey and blue Mercier tracksuit with badges and grey and black Air Force trainers.

Any information or sightings please call 101 and quote log 1762 24/10 for Finley or log 0002 25/10 for Harry.
