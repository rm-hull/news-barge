Strong gusts brought by Storm Boelo resulted in a skip fire spreading to a caravan in North Yorkshire.

Five fire crews were called to the scene in Sherburn, east of Malton, at about 6pm on Saturday (October 24).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said there were concerns the blaze would spread to a nearby property due to the strong wind.

“Crews sectorised the incident to prevent fire spread and extinguish multiple seats of fire,” a service spokesperson said.

A yellow warning for wind is in place in York and North Yorkshire until 9pm on Sunday.
