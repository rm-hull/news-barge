Police have issued an update as Storm Boelo continues to cause problems for York and North Yorkshire today (Sunday).

As we are reporting, the high winds have brought trees down across the county, and there are widespread power cuts.

North Yorkshire Police posted: “Our officers and partner agencies are working hard to keep people safe, but we’re asking everyone to take extra care if you’re travelling or spending time outdoors today, and to plan ahead where possible.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/storm-boelo-tree-down-huntington-york-25-oct-2026-1.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/storm-boelo-tree-down-huntington-york-25-oct-2026-3.webp)

They offered this advice:

* Coastal safety and tide warnings – If you’re visiting the coast, particularly with children, please pay close attention to tide times and local safety advice. Parents and carers are urged to keep children well away from cliff edges, sea walls and areas that could be cut off by incoming tides and overtopping waves.

* •Travel and road closures – Before setting off, check the latest travel alerts, road conditions and closures

* Do not drive through flood water – Flood water is often deeper and faster-moving than it appears. Never attempt to drive through flooded roads and always obey road closure signs.

* Fallen trees and debris – Police have received some reports of fallen trees overnight. Thankfully, there have been no reports of any serious injuries. However, fallen branches and other debris may still be present, so please remain vigilant and take extra care when travelling or walking outdoors.

* Rail travel – Some rail services may be affected by the weather conditions. Please check with your train operator before you travel.

* Rural walks and higher ground – If you’re planning to go walking today, particularly in rural areas or on higher ground, please take extra care. Mountain rescue teams are advising people to avoid exposed high ground. Adverse conditions increase the risk of incidents and can place additional pressure on emergency services responding across the county.

* • Plan ahead – If you’re intending to visit an event, attraction or other destination today, please check that it is open and operating as normal before you leave home. There’s a list of the latest closures here.
