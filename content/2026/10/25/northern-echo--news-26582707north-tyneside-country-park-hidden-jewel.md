If you are looking for a way to escape daily life, taking a walk through the countryside can be a great way to relax for a few hours.

Described as a “hidden jewel” in Newcastle, the Rising Sun Country Park offers an oasis in the city for those wanting to enjoy some fresh air.

The park covers 400 acres and is a “haven for wildlife”.

## What is the Rising Sun Country Park like?

The nature reserve features ponds, woodlands, extensive grasslands, and the Countryside Centre.

The Rising Sun Country Park has a range of footpaths and bridleways for walkers and cyclists to use.

For those who want to do some birdwatching, there are a few bird hides you can use.

To keep children entertained, the park has a playground with swings and slides as well as water play features for the summer.

If you need something to warm you up after your walk, the park has a café with a terrace and outdoor seating overlooking the surrounding area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21502736.jpg?type=mds-article-620) The park's café has a lot of dishes on offer. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The café offers a range of hot drinks that can be enjoyed with a slice of cake or a fresh scone.

You can also enjoy lunch with options ranging from sandwiches and paninis to light bites and hot meals, including scampi and chips and halloumi fries.

The park has toilet facilities and a dog washing area to make sure your pet is clean before heading home.
