A new Mediterranean and Greek festive market is set to bring seasonal food, gifts and crafts to Durham.

Organised by Pegasos Events, the market will be held at Christchurch, Durham, on November 28, 2026, running from 10.30am to 4pm.

Traders will offer a variety of Mediterranean-inspired food, traditional sweets, handmade crafts, candles and Christmas gifts.

![Greek and Mediterranean event](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623237.jpg?type=mds-article-620)VOUNO Greek Products *(Image: Supplied)*

Visitors can expect to explore Mediterranean flavours, discover unique Christmas gifts and support independent traders in a festive setting.

A Christmas wreath-making workshop will also take place during the event, with pre-booking required through Blooming Good by Katie.

![Greek and Mediterranean event](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623238.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Orinoco Candle Company *(Image: Supplied)*

The market promises to offer a taste of Mediterranean tradition while celebrating the season and supporting small businesses in the community.

Businesses already confirmed for the Durham Greek and Mediterranean Festive Market include VOUNO Greek Products, Lady Gabriel's Kitchen, Greek Pies, Something Greek, Diavazo Greek Books, Orinoco Candle Company, Earth to Ellie Naturals, Lemonia Crafts, Blooming Good by Katie, Not a Lie Delicacies, HiNoon Bakery, Olfactory Baking Co, Cakepops et al, Dark Fairies Positive Petals, Lovers of the North Sea, Sniff and Savour, Fat Rabbit Crafts and Well Preserved in County Durham.
