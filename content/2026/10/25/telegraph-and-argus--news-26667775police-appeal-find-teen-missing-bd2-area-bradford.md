Police have launched an appeal to find a missing Bradford teenage boy.

Brandon, 17, has been missing from his home in the BD2 area of Bradford since 7pm on Friday, October 23.

Brandon is 5ft tall, slim and has dark brown afro-style hair.

He was last seen wearing a white hoody, black trousers and black trainers.

Anyone with information about Brandon’s whereabouts should call 101 and quote reference 321 24/10/26.
