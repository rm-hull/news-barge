A Harrogate-based marketing agency is in liquidation with debts topping £400,000.

Zelst Ltd was a business support services company registered at 52 East Parade, Harrogate.

It was incorporated in 2012 and it entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on September 22.

Companies House records the directors as Sarah and Peter Van Zelst.




* **Horsebox Window Centre Limited of Harrogate in liquidation**
* **Red Kite Recruitment Group of Harrogate in liquidation**
* **FK Building Ltd of Harrogate in liquidation owning over £3m**
* **Chico's Harrogate Ltd in liquidation owing over £30,000**

BTG Begbies Traynor (Central) LLP were appointed liquidators, and they produced a report known as a statement of affairs outlining the company’s financial situation.

The report says Zelst Ltd has estimated realisable assets of £103,064.15.

When it came to liabilities, there were ten staff as preferential creditors- those who are paid first- who were owed arrears/holiday pay of £2,641.72 and £1,785.64 in pensions.

HMRC was a secondary preferential creditor which was owed £112,464.40.

This left a deficit of £13,827.61.

However, there were further unsecured claims- those who are paid last- of £400,388.51.

This was made up of 12 trade creditors owed £29,90124 between them.

Ten employees were also owed £81,745.80.

Two banks/ financial institutions were owed £22,433.33.

HMRC was also owed a further £1,436.58 in non-VAT debt.

Employees were owed a further £1,3398.24 in pension contributions.

There were three finance creditors owed £143,660.45 between them.

The were two inter-company loans totalling £104,832.17.

Finally, £15,000 in accountant fees were also owed.

This gave a total deficiency with regard to creditors of £414, 216.12.

With Zelst Ltd having issued and called up capital of £1,000, this gave a total deficiency of £415,216.12.
