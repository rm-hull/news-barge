Across **Brazil**, voters made their way to polling stations on** Sunday 25 October**as** President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva**faced** Senator Flávio Bolsonaro**in a** runoff election**.

In the **Amazon rainforest**, residents of the isolated** Lago do Catalão**community** travelled by canoe and motorboat** to cast their ballots, relying on waterways to access polling facilities.

In Rio de Janeiro's **Rocinha favela**,** long queues formed**as residents waited to vote, reflecting the** deep divisions surrounding the election**.

In **Brasília**, voters showed** passionate support for both candidates**, highlighting the stark choices facing the electorate.

The **official result is expected** tonight between**23:30–00:00 CET** (around 20:30–21:00 Brasília time).
