Set behind electric gates on a plot of around a third of an acre, 28 Harlsey Road certainly knows how to make a great first impression – and it only gets better once you’re through the front door.

Step inside and you’re welcomed by a bright entrance hall with a lofty ceiling and contemporary tiled floor plus a cloakroom off.

The hall leads to a cosy snug with a generous bay window and a multi-fuel stove perfect for relaxing around on winter evenings, plus a semi open-plan dining room centred around a feature fireplace and with panelled walls.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618516.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stylish and contemporary, this family home in Hartburn Village is a stunner *(Image: The Yorkshire Property Agency)*

Stretching right across the rear of the property is a substantial extension that is home to a stunning open-plan kitchen, dining and living room that can’t fail to impress.

The bespoke Mick Jackson kitchen takes centre stage, with a double Belfast-style sink, large central island breakfast bar and a range of high-spec integrated appliances that includes a separate wine fridge.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618514.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stylish and contemporary, this family home in Hartburn Village is a stunner *(Image: The Yorkshire Property Agency)*

A striking atrium draws light into the room from above while bifold doors open into the garden.

Open-plan to both the dining room and kitchen is a generous seating area with a feature exposed brick wall.

A beautiful stained glass window lights the stairs up to the luxurious principal bedroom which encompasses a dressing area, plenty of bespoke storage and an en-suite shower room and has a bath at the foot of the bed.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618515.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stylish and contemporary, this family home in Hartburn Village is a stunner *(Image: The Yorkshire Property Agency)*

An elegant family bathroom and three further bedrooms, one of which has a bay window and feature fireplace, complete the accommodation.

Outside, the private landscaped garden has a large paved terrace and immaculate lawns, perfect for summer entertaining.

There’s also off-street parking for several cars, a detached garage with an electric door and additional garden storage.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618513.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stylish and contemporary, this family home in Hartburn Village is a stunner *(Image: The Yorkshire Property Agency)*

Located in Hartburn Village, on the edge of the Hartburn Conservation Area, this home pairs timeless architecture with a high-spec contemporary finish making it a superb choice for a growing family.
