Waitrose has been forced to apologise after making an error on its website with one of its new Christmas products.

The major UK supermarket chain launched its No.1 Yule Log Sausage Roll (0.85kg) in 2025 as part of its Christmas range.

Describing the festive product, the Waitrose website reads: "A sharing sausage roll, perfect for festive buffets.

"The succulent pork, turkey, ham and chestnut filling is made with rich pork stock and seasoned with sage, pepper, rosemary and thyme, and it's all wrapped up in crisp pastry."

![](https://www.bournemouthecho.co.uk/resources/images/21627640.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Waitrose's No.1 Yule Log Sausage Roll is back again for Christmas 2026. *(Image: Waitrose)*

## Waitrose "very sorry" for online error with Yule Log Sausage Roll

The Yule Log Sausage Roll has returned in 2026, once again part of Waitrose's festive offerings.

However, Waitrose has been forced to apologise after the wrong image was displayed on its website.

A Waitrose spokesperson said: "Yule never believe the pastry pickle we got ourselves into.

"Giddy with excitement about the upcoming festivities, we mixed up our Logs with our Wreaths, and while we may have been a bit flaky about the product image, the sausage inside is the same outdoor-reared British pork.

“Our customer care team has contacted everyone impacted to prevent anyone getting tied up in knots - we’re very sorry for any inconvenience caused.”

The correct image of the Yule Log Sausage Roll has now been added to the Waitrose website.

## Waitrose recalls

Waitrose shoppers are also being warned to keep an eye out for several products which have been recalled in recent weeks, including:

### Gilbert's Kosher Chopped Liver (250g)

![Gilbert's Kosher Chopped Liver (250g) from Waitrose.](https://www.bournemouthecho.co.uk/resources/images/21627642.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gilbert's Kosher Chopped Liver (250g) has been recalled because of an undeclared allergen. *(Image: Waitrose)*

Waitrose has recalled Gilbert's Kosher Chopped Liver (250g) because of an undeclared allergen: wheat (Gluten).

Shoppers have been told not to consume the product, and instead to package it up and return it for a refund.

The products affected have the following use-by dates:

* October 23
* October 28
* November 4
* November 13

### WR Tuna Pâté (115g)

![Waitrose's WR Tuna Pate (115g)](https://www.bournemouthecho.co.uk/resources/images/21627644.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Shoppers have been warned against eating Waitrose's WR Tuna Pâté (115g). *(Image: Waitrose)*

Waitrose is recalling WR Tuna Pâté (115g) due to an incorrect date code.

Once again, shoppers are being told to avoid eating the product and should instead return it for a full refund.

The products affected have a use-by date of December 17.

### Essential Unsweetened Oat Drink (1L)

![](https://www.bournemouthecho.co.uk/resources/images/21627643.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Waitrose's Essential Unsweetened Oat Drink (1L) has been recalled due to a risk of microbiological contamination and spoilage. *(Image: Waitrose)*

Waitrose has also issued a recall notice on its Essential Unsweetened Oat Drink (1L) due to a risk of microbiological contamination and spoilage.

Shoppers have been told not to consume the product, and instead to package it up and return it for a refund.

The products affected have the following use-by dates:

* In-store - August 14 to October 29
* On-Demand grocery delivery service - September 2 to September 21
