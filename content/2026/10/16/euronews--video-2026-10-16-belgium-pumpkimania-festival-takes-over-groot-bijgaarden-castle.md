Thousands of pumpkins have transformed the grounds of **Groot-Bijgaarden Castle near Brussels** into a colourful autumn wonderland for the annual**Pumpkimania festival**. This year's theme,**“The Magic of the Forest”**, features** towering sculptures**of foxes, owls and deer covered in pumpkins of every shape and size, sourced from** Belgium, Germany and Portugal**.

The star attraction is a **giant pumpkin weighing 772 kilograms**, grown for the castle's traditional weigh-off on 26 September. Visitors can also watch** giant pumpkins being turned into makeshift kayaks** for rides across the castle lake.

Organisers expect around **50,000 visitors** before the festival**closes on 1 November**. Families can take part in** pumpkin-carving workshops at weekends**, while the** Halloween programme**will see the castle** open daily until 7pm from 24 October**. The festivities will end with a** giant pumpkin-smashing event**.

Despite a **difficult growing season** following a hot summer, the displays have filled the estate with thousands of pumpkins. Located in**Dilbeek**, about 10 kilometres from central Brussels, the festival costs**€16 for adults** and**€9 for children aged 5 to 14**, with free entry for younger children.
