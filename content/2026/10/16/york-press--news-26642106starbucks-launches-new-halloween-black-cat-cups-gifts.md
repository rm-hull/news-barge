## Starbucks launches new Halloween black cat cups and gifts – and prices start at £3.95

Starbucks has a new Halloween drink, and cat-themed cake pops *(Image: Starbucks)*

Starbucks has unveiled a new collection of cat-themed Halloween merchandise, including a purple Cold Cup with cat ears and a black reusable cup decorated with a spooky feline.

The seasonal range features drinkware and accessories inspired by black cats, with prices starting at £3.95 for the Cat Whiskers Reusable Cup.

The black cup features a cat design with striking yellow eyes, pink details and a purple collar, while the £11.95 Cold Cat Ears Cold Cup comes with a dome lid topped with playful ears and a matching striped straw.

Also in the collection is the £18.95 Pine Straw Cat Tumbler, decorated with black cats and pops of orange, plus a £6.95 Winnie Whiskers Keychain featuring a plush black cat in a Halloween costume.

## Starbucks launches new Halloween Frappuccino

![](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/resources/images/21585717.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Starbucks has a new Halloween drink *(Image: Starbucks)*

The cat-themed collection arrives alongside Starbucks’ new Mummy Frappuccino, available in participating UK coffeehouses from October 15 to November 1.

Priced at £5.85, the drink combines white mocha flavour sauce and cookie crumb with milk and ice, finished with mocha drizzle, whipped cream and chocolate button eyes.

Fans can also pick up a matching Mummy Cake Pop for £3.05, made with red velvet cake, vanilla flavour frosting and biscuit crumb, coated in white chocolate and decorated with black icing eyes.

Both treats are available in participating stores, through the Starbucks UK app or via Starbucks Delivers, while stocks last.

## Starbucks Autumn Charm

There is another seasonal item that fans may want to look out for – but it cannot be purchased in stores.

Starbucks is offering a limited number of its Autumn Charm exclusively through a competition on its UK Instagram account, running until October 19.

The Halloween drinks and cake pop can be ordered in participating stores, through the Starbucks UK app or via Starbucks Delivers.

The Mummy Frappuccino and Mummy Cake Pop are available until November 1, subject to availability.
