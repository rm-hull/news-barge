Police are appealing for help to locate a 15-year-old girl who has been reported missing from her home in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police are appealing for sightings of Ummayah, who was last seen near the Premier store on Beckside Road at around 2.20pm on October 15.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "We are appealing for members of the public to report any sightings of a 15-year-old girl who has been reported missing from home in Bradford.

"It is understood that Ummayah was near the Premier store on Beckside Road, Bradford, at around 2.20pm yesterday (15 October).

"There are no confirmed sightings of her after that time.

"She is described as Asian, approximately 5ft tall, of slim build with mid-length black hair that she usually wears tied up.

"She is believed to be wearing white jeans, a black jacket, black crocs and a shoulder bag."

Anyone who believes they have seen Ummayah since yesterday afternoon or anyone with information about her movements or whereabouts is urged to contact Bradford CID via Live Chat online or by calling 101, quoting log 1274 of 15 October."
