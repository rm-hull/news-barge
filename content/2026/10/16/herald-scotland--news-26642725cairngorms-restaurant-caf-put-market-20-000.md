A restaurant and café in the Cairngorms near Aviemore has been put on the market, offering hospitality operators the chance to take over a 120-cover venue in a woodland setting.

Speyside Trust Restaurant & Café, located at the Badaguish Outdoor Centre near Aviemore, is being marketed by specialist business property adviser Christie & Co.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21589841.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Speyside Trust Restaurant & Café. *(Image: Supplied)*

The leasehold is on the market for £20,000, with an annual rent of £45,000.

The fully licensed venue includes private dining facilities, event space and extensive outdoor seating. Its location within Glenmore Forest Park, between Aviemore and Cairngorm Mountain, places it close to popular walking and cycling routes.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21589842.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Speyside Trust Restaurant & Café. *(Image: Supplied)*

The site is part of the Badaguish Outdoor Centre, a visitor destination operated by The Speyside Trust.

Simon Watson, senior business agent – hospitality at Christie & Co, said:"Opportunities like this rarely come to the market in the Highlands.

"Speyside Trust Restaurant & Café offers incoming operators the chance to take on a well-established hospitality business in a stunning Cairngorms setting, benefitting from year-round visitor demand and multiple revenue streams through food, drink, events, and private functions.

"We expect strong interest from experienced operators looking to build on the venue's existing reputation and exceptional location."
