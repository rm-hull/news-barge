# Major road in Harrogate closed in both directions following serious collision

## North Yorkshire Police is currently dealing with a serious collision on a major road in Harrogate, causing a temporary road closure.

Skipton Road is currently closed in both directions between the A59/A61 roundabout and Bilton junction.

![The police are currently dealing with a serious collision on Skipton Road in Harrogate, causing a temporary road closure](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/16/11/36/Police-Car-Siren.jpeg?trim=0,130,0,130&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please avoid the area and seek alternative routes while emergency services deal with the incident.”
