Police say they believe a missing woman "entered the water" off the North East coast in South Shields.

Shannon, 56, was last seen in the Cheshire Grove area of the town late last Friday (October 9).

Northumbria Police have now said that they believe that Shannon entered the water at South Shields.

Her family have been informed of developments in the investigation and continue to be supported by officers.

Sergeant Michael Wynes, of the Force’s Missing Investigation Team, said: “Our thoughts are with Shannon's family, friends and loved ones at this deeply difficult time.

“We have carried out a wide range of enquiries since she was reported missing to us, and we are continuing these enquiries.

“It is now our understanding that she entered the water off the coast of South Shields.

“We would like to sincerely thank all of the officers who have been involved in the searches for Shannon, and also thank our partner organisations who helped."
