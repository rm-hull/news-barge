A multi-platinum recording artist whose hits helped bring South Asian music into the mainstream is set to headline this year’s Diwali celebrations in Middlesbrough.

Raghav, known for blending soulful vocals with Bollywood-inspired beats, will take to the stage in Middlesbrough on Saturday, October 31, as the centrepiece of a day-long festival marking Diwali, the festival of light.

The free family event will be held in Centre Square and will end with a Bollywood-themed fireworks display.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21586232.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Traditional Indian dancers *(Image: Supplied)*

Kash Patel, producer of Diwali in Middlesbrough, said: "Seldom do you witness an artist of Raghav’s calibre who can produce hit after hit which remain relevant and contemporary almost a quarter of a century on.

"He has been instrumental in shaping the Asian music scene around the world.

"I am super excited to be flying him all the way from his hometown in Canada to perform exclusively for the people of Middlesbrough."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21586226.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Supplied)*

Raghav rose to international fame with his 2004 album Storyteller, which featured global hits such as Angel Eyes, So Confused, and Can’t Get Enough.

The album helped launch South Asian music into the mainstream and earned Raghav multiple awards, including a MOBO, two AMA's, and an Urban Music Award.

Diwali in Middlesbrough promises a full day of entertainment from noon, including live music, dance performances, street food, children’s activities, funfair rides, and market stalls.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21586230.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Diwali main stage *(Image: Supplied)*

The event is described as a “multicultural extravaganza” and is free for all to attend, with no tickets required.

Diwali is one of the major festivals celebrated by Hindus, Sikh, and Jains all over the world, with the five-day Festival of Light representing the triumph of good over evil.

Organisers are also inviting traders and exhibitors to book stalls for the event by calling 07776 210066.
