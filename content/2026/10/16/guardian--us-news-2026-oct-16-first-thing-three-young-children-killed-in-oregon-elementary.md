Good morning. A 16-year-old shooter opened fire at an elementary school in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday, killing three children and a teacher before he was shot dead by a community member. The children killed were five, eight and 11, officials said. The gunman, who was a Bend resident, has not been publicly identified.

In the aftermath of the attack, children were taken by bus to a nearby church serving as a reunification center for families. Parents hugged, kissed and held their children’s hands as they walked to their cars, with some bringing along favorite stuffed animals or other comfort items from home. Here is what we know so far.

## **Trump says Zelenskyy ‘more difficult’ than Putin, and calls Kyiv strikes on Russian oil refineries ‘disgrace’**

![A woman holds her daughter in front of a residential building on fire](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/208a961e12e9ec5f76b3c211e8d213e4668d17f8/2_0_7200_5760/master/7200.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Donald Trump has called Ukraine’s latest strikes on Russian oil refineries a “disgrace”, while renewing his criticism of Volodymyr ⁠Zelenskyy after an energy ceasefire announced by the US president collapsed. Trump said: “All he’s doing is making the world oil price higher. Well, I think that’s a disgrace. I think I find Zelenskyy to be more difficult ​to deal with ​than Putin.”

* **What have been the diplomatic moves in Europe?** Amid growing doubts about long-term US commitment to Ukraine, European leaders declared their support for Zelenskyy. The European Commission president, Ursula von der Leyen, said the EU intended to increase pressure on Russia, promising a 22nd round of sanctions. The French president, Emmanuel Macron, said it was “crucial” that Ukraine receive “all the means necessary to protect itself”.

## **Houthi missiles strike Yemen presidential palace, killing five**

![A displaced Yemeni family sits beside a makeshift shelter in Aden.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/a7659eb63722daa749964db86dbd8d9d32c08953/688_0_5050_4043/master/5050.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Yemen’s Houthis fired missiles at the presidential palace complex in the southern city of Aden, killing five people. The attack was the first on the Al-Maashiq palace ⁠in the current conflict. Yemen’s prime minister, Shaya al-Zindani, ⁠and cabinet members were said to be safe after the attack.

* **Why is Yemen saying the battle against the Houthis is crucial?** Yemen’s former PM Maeen Abdulmalik Saeed has said the Iran-backed militia’s use of drones represents a “new era of radical terrorism”. The adviser to the Yemeni president said he had no doubt that the Houthis intended to seize control of all of Yemen, and claimed their goal was “to help Iran to expand the chaos” in the region.

## **Trump cancels plan to livestream execution after global condemnation**

![Memorials honoring the 13 shooting victims at Fort Hood.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/f5d55450d680509a0d66ed72ba0f5820952e0536/684_0_1116_893/master/1116.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Donald Trump has shelved a widely condemned plan to livestream the execution of Nidal Malik Hasan, who was convicted of killing 13 people at a military base at Fort Hood in Texas in 2009. “He will die, as scheduled, by firing squad, but this event will not be streamed,” Trump said. “That said, the execution may be viewed in person by the immediate families of the victims, if they so choose.”

* **How did Pete Hegseth’s church allies react to the planned broadcast?** The de facto leader of Hegseth’s theocratic Communion of Reformed Evangelical Churches (CREC), Douglas Wilson, had welcomed the announcement, saying he prefers “public execution”. He said he favored “the older forms, like a firing squad, as opposed to attempts to ‘medicalize’ it”, and added that “executions in the scriptures were public”.

## **Holdout juror in Lindsay Clancy trial gives first interview since mistrial**

![Lindsay Clancy.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/9ba824737138292df065d602742e30c49574e79c/382_101_2374_1899/master/2374.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)
