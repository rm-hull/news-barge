A family-run hearing clinic in York has been shortlisted for two awards.

The Hearing Place is a finalist in three categories at the 2026 Yorkshire, Humberside and North East Family Business Awards, including Consumer Services, Small Retailers & E-tailers, and the People’s Choice award.

Jess Kyei-Yamoah, co-owner of The Hearing Place, said: "We’re absolutely thrilled to reach the finals of both of these awards.

"We pride ourselves on the excellent service we provide, so to be recognised for it is incredibly special."

The clinic has also reached the final round of voting in the YorkMix Choice Awards 2026, where it is shortlisted for Best Independent Shop, Best Customer Experience, and Best Professional Services Provider.

The Hearing Place recently celebrated one year at its Haxby Shopping Centre location.

The business operates clinics across Yorkshire and offers hearing tests, hearing aid fittings, and professional ear wax removal.
