A serious crash has closed a road in Harrogate this morning (Friday).

Skipton Road is shut in both directions between the A59/A61 roundabout and Bilton junction.

Motorists are reporting standstill traffic.

#### Skipton Road, Harrogate

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please avoid the area and seek alternative routes while emergency services deal with the incident.”
