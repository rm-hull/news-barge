Martin House has been named Hospice of the Year at the National Charity Today Awards.

The award recognises outstanding care, leadership and community impact, supporting patients, families and loved ones during some of the most difficult moments of their lives.

The charity was also highly commended in the People’s Choice Award and Partnership of the Year category, alongside its long-standing partner Haribo UK & Ireland.

Announcing the news, a spokesperson Martin House said:

> “This recognition is a testament to the dedication, passion, and hard work of our incredible team here at Martin House, as well as the unwavering support from our amazing community of supporters.
>
> “Thank you to everyone who champions our mission.”

Martin House provides specialist care and support for children and young people with life-limiting conditions, and their families, across West, North and East Yorkshire.
