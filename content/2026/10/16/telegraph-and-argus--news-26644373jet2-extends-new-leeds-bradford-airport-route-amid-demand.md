Jet2 is extending one of its new routes from Leeds Bradford Airport.

The airline said it had put more seats on sale after strong demand from customers booking early and late-winter holidays.

The change gives families more dates to travel during the October and February half-term breaks.

## Which route is it?

Jet2 first announced flights to Enfidha-Hammamet International Airport in Tunisia for summer 2027.

The route was originally due to run until October 30, 2027.

The expanded service will now run until November 27, 2027.

Flights will resume on February 12, 2028, and run throughout summer 2028.

Services will run weekly on Saturdays, giving passengers more options when booking a winter sunshine break from West Yorkshire.

Jet2 said the longer season would give Yorkshire customers and independent travel agents more chances to visit Tunisia outside the main summer period.

The Tunisia route is part of Jet2’s wider summer 2027 expansion from Leeds Bradford, which will take the airport’s total number of destinations on sale to 56.

## What Jet2 said

Steve Heapy, CEO of Jet2, said: "This expansion gives customers across Yorkshire even more opportunities to enjoy everything that Tunisia has to offer, whilst enjoying our industry-leading customer service.

"We know there is enormous appetite for this destination, and we are pleased to be responding by offering customers greater choice and flexibility when planning their holidays."
