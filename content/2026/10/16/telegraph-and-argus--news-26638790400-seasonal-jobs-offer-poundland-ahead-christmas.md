POUNDLAND is creating 400 seasonal jobs across the UK and Ireland to support its stores during the busy Christmas period.

The roles will help keep shelves stocked and stores running smoothly in the weeks leading up to Christmas, as part of the retailer’s continued recovery and return to like-for-like sales growth.

Alan Smallman, retail director at Poundland, said: "After a busy year getting the business back on track, our store teams are excited about delivering our best Christmas in years as our all-new seasonal ranges begin landing in store.

"Our people make the difference whatever the time of year, but at Christmas they play a special role looking after our customers.

"We look forward to welcoming new colleagues to our teams to help make this Christmas really special."

Poundland reported 3.3 per cent like-for-like growth in the final quarter of its financial year and 6.7 per cent in September.

The retailer has refreshed its product ranges and is now offering customers new toys and festive lines each week throughout October and November.

Those interested in seasonal roles can find opportunities at www.poundlandcareers.co.uk.

In recognition of staff efforts, Poundland will close all stores on Christmas Day, Boxing Day, and New Year’s Day to allow colleagues time off.

Poundland has stores in Bradford and Keighley.
