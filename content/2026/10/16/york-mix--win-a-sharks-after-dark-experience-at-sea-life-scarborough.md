You could win an after-hours VIP tour of Scarborough SEA LIFE Aquarium with an expert guide for up to 6 guests!

You’ll get exclusive access to the whole aquarium, during the evening when the attraction is closed to the public

To be in with a chance of winning, simply watch the video below, answer the question at the end correctly and you’re in the draw!
