# Five days, one briefing: How Katie Zacharia’s short tenure unfolded

The White House says Katie Zacharia is "transitioning out" of the role of press secretary just days after taking the job. It comes after US President Donald Trump said on Friday he suggested to Zacharia she should "reconsider" the job.

Trump went on to say that Zacharia has been treated "very, very badly" by the media in recent days.

The BBC's Sarah Smith, who was in the room at Zacharia’s first briefing, breaks down how her short tenure unfolded.
