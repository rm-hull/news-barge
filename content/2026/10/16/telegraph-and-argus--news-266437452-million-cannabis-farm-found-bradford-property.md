A man has been arrested and charged following the discovery of a £2 million cannabis farm.

The industrial-scale grow was found at a property on Little Horton Lane during an operation led by West Yorkshire Police’s Bradford City team, supported by colleagues from Bradford West.

## Who has been charged?

Nam Bao, 45, has been charged with production of a Class B drug.

He has been remanded in custody.

Sergeant Coleman from Team 1 City NPT said: "Cannabis is a harmful drug and tackling its production remains a significant priority for West Yorkshire Police.

"My officers remain dedicated to disrupting serious and organised crime and will pursue prosecution against those who choose to break the law.

"To those involved in these illicit activities, our message is clear: we will find you, we will seize the drugs, and we will put you before the courts.

"This result demonstrates our ongoing commitment to tackling organised crime, safeguarding our communities, and making Bradford a safer place for everyone."
