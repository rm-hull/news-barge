Kimberly Guilfoyle’s former Fox News colleague has penned a damning column about her time at the network with the recently ousted U.S. Ambassador to Greece, and claimed she shared sexually explicit images with co-workers.

Fox News commentator Lisa “Kennedy” Montgomery, who worked with the disgraced former ambassador at the conservative network from 2012 until Guilfoyle’s abrupt departure in 2018, let rip in her latest *Daily Mail* column, where she claimed that Guilfoyle “talked about sex so cavalierly that she made Samantha from*Sex and the City* seem like Greta Thunberg.”

“Kimberly claimed to me – and whomever else was within earshot – that just about everyone at Fox News was in love with her and wanted to sleep with her, especially the women,” Kennedy alleged.

The commentator said she “wasn’t shocked” when she first read reports that Guilfoyle had begged a Trump donor for $100,000 to pay off an Amex bill before her Senate confirmation hearing last year. More unflattering reports of Guilfoyle’s alleged conduct in Greece swirled, ultimately leading to her resignation earlier this week.

Guilfoyle, who was in a six-year relationship with Donald Trump Jr. until late 2024, said that the allegations against her are false in a statement posted Tuesday. “I intend to vigorously defend my name, my reputation, and my integrity,” Guilfoyle said.

![Lisa ‘Kennedy’ Montgomery, Kimberly Guilfoyle’s former Fox News colleague, has penned a damning column about her time at the network with the recently ousted U.S. Ambassador to Greece](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/16/15/23/GettyImages-1068914612.jpg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=40%2C315%2C1989%2C229&auto=webp)

Kennedy wrote that Guilfoyle was “already something of a rising star” when she joined Fox News in 2012. While they bonded over their shared contempt for Democratic California Gov. Gavin Newsom, Guilfoyle’s ex-husband, the 57-year-old put Kennedy in uncomfortable situations, she claimed.

“One unsettling night, she asked me to meet her at her apartment,” Kennedy wrote in her column. “She answered the door in a black, push-up corset and thigh-high stockings, and purred: 'You like what you see?'”

While at Fox News, Guilfoyle showed Kennedy and several other co-workers pictures of a man’s private parts whom she was dating at the time. “Kimberly showed me and several of our friends pictures of his Amex black card – front and back,” Kennedy claimed. “The images were matched with a corresponding picture of the card holder's private parts.”

The columnist claimed there were “at least three sets of these credit card-penis pairings.”

According to Kennedy, when allegations surfaced of sexual assault against the now-deceased network chief Roger Ailes, Guilfoyle “demanded” she “pledge my loyalty to Ailes and his wife.”

Guilfoyle worked for Fox News from 2006 until her sudden departure in 2018. *The New Yorker* subsequently reported that Fox News paid Guilfoyle’s former assistant approximately $4 million after she drafted a complaint alleging sexual harassment against her former boss. A lawyer for Guilfoyle previously told the outlet that “any suggestion” she had “engaged in misconduct at Fox is patently false.”

*The Independent* has contacted Guilfoyle’s attorney for comment.

Guilfoyle is now facing a defamation lawsuit brought by Eric Deters, the former Trump donor who published text messages where she asked him to pay off her Amex bill in exchange for influence in the Trump administration.

“Honey please I need you to get this done for me today,” she texted Deters via Signal on July 3, adding a praying emoji and a PDF containing payment instructions from Amex, according to the messages. “You could just send it here. It won’t show up anywhere if you wire money to American Express that’s all that will show. Please.”

Her lawyers have disputed the authenticity of the text messages Deters shared with media outlets. Deters told CNN earlier this week that he did not give Guilfoyle the money.

Deters said his lawsuit “might bring down the entire Trump administration — an administration which I have supported from day one, whose policies I still support.”

Guilfoyle has not commented on the lawsuit or her resignation beyond a social media post after it was announced she was asked to resign.
