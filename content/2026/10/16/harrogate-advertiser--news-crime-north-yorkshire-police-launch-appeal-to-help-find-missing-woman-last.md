# North Yorkshire Police launch appeal to help find missing woman last seen at Harrogate District Hospital

## North Yorkshire Police has released two images and launched an appeal to help find a missing woman who could be in the Harrogate area.

Rachel Lenton, 43, from Nottinghamshire, was last seen at 12.40pm today (October 16) at Harrogate District Hospital.

![The police have released two images and launched an appeal to help find Rachel Lenton, 43, who has gone missing and could be in the Harrogate area](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/16/15/54/CCTV-APPEAL-(1).jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Rachel is described as a white female, 5ft 6inch tall, with short, curly, greying hair.

She was last seen wearing a knee length dark coloured Karrimor jacket with a hood.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Rachel and as part of our enquiries, we are asking for anyone who may have seen Rachel, or who has information about his whereabouts, to contact us immediately.”

If you have an immediate sighting of Rachel, you should call 999.

If you have any other information that could help the police with their investigation, you should call 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 16102026-0212 when providing any details regarding her whereabouts.
