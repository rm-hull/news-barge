## **Why does replacing a bus shelter in York take so long?**

Several months ago they removed a perfectly good bus shelter (to my mind) in Clarence Street, York, reinstated the footpath and installed new electronic timetable board, and then nothing for weeks and weeks - nothing.

Fortunately, the weather was fine, although the lack of seats was a problem for some but it was a good while before the replacement bus shelter arrived.

Recently the same scenario but opposite to the other one arose, taking down the old, installing signage, then nothing.

But this time it's autumn, cooler, wet, and with no coverage or seating - miserable.

Why can't the people involved in the removal and reinstating of the bus stop / new shelter liaise, cooperate?

A week from start to finish should be easily attainable. Why does it have to take so long, and cause so much incontinence to bus passengers?

**D M DeamerPenleys Grove StreetMonkgateYork\_\_\_**
