Police have released this picture of a man in mid-kick after a car was damaged in York.

The image was captured on dashcam footage.

Officers want to trace the man as part of their investigation into criminal damage.

#### Burdyke Avenue, York

A stationary car was damaged on **Burdyke Avenue** at about**1.45pm** on**Sunday 27 September**.

Police ask anyone who recognises the man to contact them, as he “may have information that will assist our investigation”.
