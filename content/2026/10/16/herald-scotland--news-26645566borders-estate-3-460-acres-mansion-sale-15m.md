An estate boasting a Category A-listed baronial mansion is on the market for more than £15 million in the Borders.

Portmore Estate, near Peebles, extends over approximately 3,460 acres and is centred around the 1850-built Portmore House.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582125.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The estate also includes five additional residential dwellings, a farm, and a former pheasant shoot, according to the listing.

The "highly desirable" estate is described by the agents as providing "great seclusion and amenity" in the Borders countryside.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582131.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The baronial Portmore House was designed by Scottish architect David Bryce and is built of red sandstone.

It features a central tower, turrets on the western elevation, crow-step gables, and sash and case windows.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582120.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The mansion’s South Wing was partially rebuilt following a fire in the 1980s and has retained many period features, including wood panelling, fine cornicing, panelled doors, fireplaces, and the original hall organ.

The house includes principal reception rooms, a "most welcoming" kitchen, a billiard room, a playroom, and a two-bedroom caretaker's flat.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582121.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

It also has a range of outbuildings and a partly covered cobbled courtyard.

The accommodation is spread over three floors with a guest annex, and a two-bedroom flat on the lower ground floor.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582130.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The gardens at Portmore are also highlighted as a "notable feature of the estate."

The current owners have spent the last 40 years restoring and developing the gardens.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582119.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The estate also includes a Victorian glasshouse and brick outbuildings.

A woodland walk with azaleas, rhododendrons, and spring bulbs completes the horticultural features.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582123.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The other residences on the estate include Portmore Lodge, Primrose Cottage, and Hillhead Cottage, each with their own gardens.

Portmore Lodge is a traditional lodge located near the A703 public road.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582122.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

Primrose Cottage is a recently modernised stone-built cottage, while Hillhead is a traditional stone-built cottage that was previously occupied by the estate gamekeeper.

The estate is being offered in three lots, but lots two and three cannot be purchased individually.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582124.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

Lot one includes Portmore House, Portmore Lodge, Primrose Cottage, Hillhead Cottage, and the surrounding land.

Lot two consists of 932 acres of hill ground, a reservoir, and woodlands.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582126.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

Lot three comprises Boreland Farm, a productive farm focusing on a two flock sheep enterprise and a herd of 70 suckler cows.

Boreland Farm includes a farmhouse, farm manager's house, and a range of modern and traditional farm buildings.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582127.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The estate's farming activities are centred on Boreland Farm, located south of Portmore House.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582128.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

The farm includes a farmhouse, cottage, and a range of modern and traditional farm buildings.

Further there is the reservoir which extends to about 105 acres (42 hectares) and is maintained by Scottish Water.

![](https://www.bordertelegraph.com/resources/images/21582118.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Portmore Estate, Peebles, EH45 8QU *(Image: Galbraith)*

In addition the fishing is leased to a local fishing syndicate and have included in their lease the use of the timber fishing chalet which lies to the north west of the reservoir.
