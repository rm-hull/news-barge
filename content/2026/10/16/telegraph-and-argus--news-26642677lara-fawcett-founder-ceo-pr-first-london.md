Lara Fawcett, star of TV’s Gogglebox and sister of adventurer Bear Grylls, is an adventurer herself - in 2021, she took part in an open-air ice-swimming challenge for charity, raising £23k. As founder and CEO of PR First London, life is one big fashion moment for Lara, who lives near Leeds

**How did you get into style PR?**

I was unemployable! I really annoyed my last boss, who couldn’t understand my style of working, which involved racing around London on a multicoloured moped and roller blades, schmoozing and picking up clients.

I set up my own PR business in 1992 specialising in lifestyle PR. It was the height of the Ab Fab era and I was always compared to Edina. I managed to surround myself with amazing people in the agency and our work ethos was ‘presentation not persuasion’.

**Describe your look**

More is more! Bold is beautiful. I dress to brighten up the world. It is not about drawing attention, but about making others happy (including myself obviously). I love the conversations that start as a result of my whacky clothes. Four years ago I had to wear spectacles full time, so decided they were to become part of my style. I collect funky glasses in different colours to match my outfits and now feel naked without them. Laser surgery? I don’t think so.

![Lara Fawcett](https://www.greatbritishlife.co.uk/resources/images/21191304.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lara loves a pair of statement specs. *(Image: Lara Fawcett)*

**Favourite brand and designer?**

Azuzu in Wetherby is a total gem and so well priced.

I have to confess to being a TK Maxx addict, as I love the thrill of the chase. I do not buy expensive clothes. I also love a charity shop mooch and have bought some amazing one-off pieces. I love shopping abroad as it reminds me of my travels.

I very rarely buy mainstream brands and I love going to pop-up fairs. The Yorkshire Pop Up that happens every April and September is amazing. I always model in their fashion show and particularly love a brand called Sansom Reed. I love a pair of Fitflops too.

**Who is your style icon?**

Iris Apfel, which is exactly how I want to look in my dotage. Also, Hen Tatham, Queen of Galle in Sri Lanka. I bought a wonderful Iris Apfel dress from a local tailor called Karma in Unawatuna Sri Lanka. I went in to buy some material and it was already on the dummy! So I grabbed it. I also love the style of the Yorkshire interior designer Siobhan Murphy – so full of colour.

**Favourite fashion item?**

Buffalo shoes and wedged trainers with glitter. Colourful Rigby and Peller bras, which make me feel happy! Cowboy boots. My hat collection. I especially love Jonny Beardsall and Nicola de Selincourt.

**What do you wear to impress?**

Funky tights and jewellery. I am also particularly passionate about my Tatty Devine Chandelier earrings.

![Lara Fawcett](https://www.greatbritishlife.co.uk/resources/images/21191307.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lara Fawcett swims in the River Nidd in Knaresborough, North Yorkshire, to raise money for the Archbishop of York Youth Trus *(Image: Lara Fawcett)*

**What do you wear to relax?**

Kikili kaftans. I love fun loungewear for the evening and furry Fitflops.

**Worst fashion faux pas?**

I went for an interview with David Emanuel (designer of Lady Diana’s wedding dress). I was desperate to get into fashion and saw this as a way in. I turned up at the chic Mayfair salon dressed in a yellow zip dress with bumble bee striped tights. I never lived that down as they were all about minimalism. I did actually get the job but I was given a uniform!

Another one was turning up at boarding school as the only new girl aged 11, whose mother hadn’t sent her to school with her ‘own clothes, just uniform. I came down on the Saturday where all the girls of my age were dressed in cool OshKosh dungarees and Kickers shoes. I was wearing navy cords and brown Hush Puppies lace-ups and an Aertex shirt. Also I had waist-high white granny knickers. Everyone burst out laughing and mocked me.

The first time I went home after that I marched my mother down the King’s Road to buy some trendy clothes and I never looked back. I think that must have triggered my fashion sense.

**Favourite fashion trend of all time?**

Flared jeans and mini skirts.

**Pet hate of the moment?**

Flat Mary Jane shoes on adults.

![Lara Fawcett](https://www.greatbritishlife.co.uk/resources/images/21191311.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lara loves clothes that are a talking point! *(Image: Lara Fawcett)*

**Best style advice?**

Don’t follow the crowd. Find what suits your shape. If you find something you really love and they’re not too expensive then buy two of them.

**Most stylish celebrity?**

Annabel Croft always looks beautiful. Mary Portas never fails to impress. Also the beautiful Santa Montefiore.

**The look I wish would come back?**

In honour of Penelope Keith – the housecoat.

**What item has had its day and should be confined to the back of the wardrobe?**

Ballet pumps if you are not a ballerina. Elasticated trousers. Lady Diana high collars. Velvet headbands..
