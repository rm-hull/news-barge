BRADFORD moviegoers have a packed Saturday ahead, with ODEON Leeds Bradford offering a huge 23-film programme and The Light Bradford adding 15 titles, including family films, horror, blockbusters and special screenings.

The Light is located at The Broadway, Hall Ings, while ODEON Leeds Bradford is at Gallagher Leisure Park, Thornbury.

**ODEON Leeds Bradford**

ODEON has the broader schedule, with multiple daily performances for its biggest releases and several family-friendly morning slots.

Adult tickets for standard showings are listed from £12.50, although selected children’s screenings are cheaper.

**Saturday Picks**

For families: Forgotten Island, Tad and the Magic Lamp, Shaun the Sheep, Minions & Monsters, or the We’re Going on a Bear Hunt double bill.

For blockbuster fans: Street Fighter, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, or the three-hour Avengers Endgame: Encore.
