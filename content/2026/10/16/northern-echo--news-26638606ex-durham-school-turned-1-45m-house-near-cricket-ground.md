A historic Durham property with views across the city’s former cricket ground is on the market for £1.45million.

Durham House, on Old Elvet, dates back to the 18th century and was formerly used as a chaplaincy and school before undergoing an extensive restoration in 2002.

The five-bedroom property, which is a short walk from the Market Place, combines original period features with modern additions.

It is in a private area despite being in the heart of Durham city centre.

Approached through a grand archway, the house opens into a private courtyard with four large garages, workshop space and secure bike storage.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584024.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The rear of Durham House features an expansive riverside terrace, French doors and multiple levels of accommodation, all within walking distance of Durham city centre. *(Image: Urban Base)*

Original stonework has been retained throughout the restoration, while a striking two-storey bottle window fills the central staircase with natural light.

Inside, the ground floor includes a large entrance hall, study, kitchen, dining room, family area and a full-sized games room designed around a snooker table.

The study features mullioned windows overlooking what was once Durham's county cricket pitch.

The home's main living spaces are centred around a sunken lounge and dining area, complete with hardwood French windows, offering views towards the riverbanks and university playing fields.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584008.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Behind the historic stone façade of Durham House, elegant living spaces blend period character with modern luxury in one of Durham's most prestigious homes. *(Image: Urban Base)*

There is also a dedicated space for a baby grand piano.

Upstairs, the principal bedroom suite comes with a dressing room, ensuite bathroom and direct access to a balcony overlooking the River Wear.

A second bedroom also benefits from ensuite facilities, while a large family bathroom features an elevated jacuzzi bath, shower and dressing area.

A further three ensuite bedrooms are located on the second floor, two of which enjoy river views.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584039.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of Durham House's standout features is a full-sized snooker room complete with oak-panelled walls, making it an impressive space for entertaining guests. *(Image: Urban Base)*

At the top of the house is a south-facing lounge where floor-to-ceiling windows provide panoramic views across Durham.

Outside, the grounds include a raised seating area, sunken garden, summerhouse and an original well believed to be part of Old Elvet's history.

Despite its peaceful setting, the property is less than 400 metres from Durham Market Place, offering easy access to the city's shops, restaurants and historic attractions.

Estate agents describe Durham House as a rare opportunity to acquire a substantial period home that blends historical character with contemporary living in one of Durham's most sought-after locations.

The property is currently on the market for offers in the region of £1,450,000.
