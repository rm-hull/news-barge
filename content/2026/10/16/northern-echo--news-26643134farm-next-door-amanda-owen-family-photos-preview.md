New pictures from Channel 4's Our Farm Next Door offer a glimpse of what's next for Amanda Owen and her family as the popular Yorkshire farming series continues.

The latest episode, airing on Monday, October 19, sees the Owen children taking centre stage, with eldest daughter Raven returning to Ravenseat, Edith preparing for a major milestone and Annas getting stuck into the restoration of Anty John's farmhouse.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21590343.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raven, Clemmie and Annas look at a new lamb *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Meanwhile, Amanda makes an intriguing discovery about the history of the land surrounding the property, while Clive has his hands full looking after the family's animals.

## Raven returns to Ravenseat

The eldest Owen daughter is back at the family's remote Yorkshire Dales farm, where she joins younger sister Edith mucking out the stables.

New photographs show the sisters working together around the farm, while another captures Raven catching up with Edith.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21590341.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raven hears exciting news from Edith *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

It comes as Edith prepares for a particularly busy few weeks, balancing her exams with work at Ravenseat and the return of the family's cream tea van for walkers travelling along the Coast to Coast route.

Edith is also learning to drive, with her theory test approaching and her practical test to follow.

In new footage from the programme, she reveals to Raven that their mum Amanda is buying her a car.

## Clive prepares for new arrivals at Ravenseat

Elsewhere, Clive Owen is keeping a close eye on the family's dogs as more new arrivals are expected.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21590342.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Clive and Annas with Nellie the sheepdog *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

He checks in on puppies Patch, Rascal and Dora, while his beloved sheepdog Nellie is also preparing to give birth.

New photographs show Clive alongside daughter Annas as they tend to the animals at Ravenseat.

The family is also facing a challenging rescue after a poorly pregnant sheep becomes stuck at the bottom of a hill.

Amanda joins Sid and Miles in an effort to help the animal.

## Annas takes on unusual decorating challenge

Work is continuing at Anty John's, the old farmhouse being brought back to life by the Owen family.

Carpenter Dan and joiner Philip are busy laying oak floorboards as the restoration moves forward.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21590340.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda and historian Derek at Ravenseat *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

But it is Annas who takes on one of the more unusual jobs, using large quantities of tea and a spray gun on the newly plastered walls.

One of the new photographs shows her spraying the walls as other members of the restoration team look on.

## Amanda uncovers 200-year-old history

The latest episode also sees Amanda exploring the history of the land surrounding Anty John's.

She meets historian Derek to share an intriguing discovery about the fields around the property dating back two centuries.
