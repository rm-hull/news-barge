A MAIN road in North Yorkshire is currently closed both ways due to a serious crash.

The A64 is currently shut at Barton Hill between York and Malton.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Emergency services are at the scene and the road is expected to remain closed for some time while the incident is dealt with.

"At this stage, it is not known how long the closure will be in place.

"Please avoid the area and seek alternative routes where possible."
