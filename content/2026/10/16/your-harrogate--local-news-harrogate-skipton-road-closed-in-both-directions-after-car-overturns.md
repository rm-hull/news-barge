Skipton Road in Harrogate has been closed in both directions following a serious collision this morning (Friday).

North Yorkshire Police said the road is shut between the A59/A61 roundabout and Bilton junction while emergency services deal with the incident.

A car has reportedly overturned onto its side near St John’s Road, with delays in the area.

Early reports suggest the driver is unharmed, although this has not been confirmed by police.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said:

> “Please avoid the area and seek alternative routes while emergency services deal with the incident.”

*The police incident reference is NYP-16102026-0124.*
