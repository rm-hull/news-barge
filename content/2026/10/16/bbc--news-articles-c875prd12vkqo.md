**Medical records belonging to a man killed by a tiger at Yorkshire Wildlife Park may have been accessed "without a legitimate reason" by staff at Yorkshire Ambulance Service, a spokesperson has said.**

William Chapman, 24, was mauled to death at the park near Doncaster on Saturday.

In a statement, the trust said following Chapman's death it had identified two possible incidents of staff "accessing or attempting to access incident/medical information" about the case.

It said an investigation had been launched "to establish the full circumstances surrounding these potential breaches".

Chapman died as a result of "multiple traumatic injuries and animal interaction" after entering the tiger's enclosure at the park, an inquest into his death heard on Thursday.

An eyewitness took a photograph of the tiger at the wildlife park shortly before the incident

In a statement, a spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service NHS Trust said Chapman's family was aware of the potential breach and offered their condolences to his relatives.

They said: "We believe there may have been two instances involving two members of staff accessing or attempting to access incident/medical information following this incident without a legitimate reason to do so.

"We take the confidentiality and security of patient information extremely seriously and have robust systems in place to protect information, and this concern has been identified as a result of our proactive audits.

"The trust has commenced an investigation to establish the full circumstances surrounding these potential breaches and the actions of these individuals.

"We have also reported this matter to the Information Commissioner's Office and we will continue to keep William's family informed as our investigation progresses."

An inquest at Doncaster Coroner's Court on Thursday heard that Chapman had died of "multiple traumatic injuries".

Chapman had attended the park at about 13:30 BST on Saturday, climbed over two fences and entered the tiger enclosure.

After the attack, park staff secured the tiger and paramedics attended the scene but confirmed Chapman's death at the scene, the inquest was told.

Senior coroner Nicola Mundy said Chapman had a history of mental illness, a diagnosis of schizophrenia and had suffered previous bouts of psychosis.

The inquest heard that the 24-year-old was under adult mental health services in Doncaster, but was "highly functioning and independent with no care requirements".

The self-employed online trader was born in Chichester, West Sussex, and living in Doncaster at the time of his death.
