A man on the run after escaping police custody is being sought by officers.

The suspect, who was under arrest on suspicion of burglary, fled from Halifax Police Station in West Yorkshire shortly before 11am on Friday.

Officers conducted a search of the area but the man has not yet been found.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Shortly after 10.50am this morning a man who was under arrest on suspicion of burglary has made off from officers at Halifax Police Station.

"An immediate search of the area was made, including the use of specialist resources.

"Currently the man remains outstanding, and enquiries are ongoing to locate him and bring him into custody."
