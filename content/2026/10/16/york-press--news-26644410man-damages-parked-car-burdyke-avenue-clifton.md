A MAN has damaged a parked car in a York suburb.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after criminal damage took place in Burdyke Avenue in Clifton at the end of last month.

It happened at about 1.45pm on September 27 and ‘involved a male damaging a stationary car’.
