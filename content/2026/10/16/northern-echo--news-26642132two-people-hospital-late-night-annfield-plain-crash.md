Two people are in hospital after a late-night crash in County Durham that saw the road closed.

Emergency services were called to West Road in Annfield Plain at 10.55pm on Thursday (October 15), following reports of a crash.

The road was closed for around 30 minutes to allow emergency services to work at the scene.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) confirmed that two people were taken to the University Hospital of North Durham (UHND).

The severity of their injuries hasn't been confirmed.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on West Road in Anfield Plain at 22:55 on Thursday 15 October.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew and two patients were taken to University Hospital of North Durham (UHND) for further treatment."
