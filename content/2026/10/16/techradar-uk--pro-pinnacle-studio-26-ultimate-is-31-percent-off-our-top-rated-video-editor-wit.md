## Which video editing tool we'd recommend

Adobe Premiere Pro is an excellent choice for professional editors, but not everyone needs everything it offers. Pinnacle Studio 26 Ultimate is a much more affordable alternative with plenty of tools to explore. We rated it the best subscription-free video editing software in our guide.

In our review the previous edition, Studio 25 Ultimate, we found it "offers professional post-production polish without subscription costs." Minor niggles with the slow import section aside, we found it ticked all the boxes for general video editing workflows that don't need the pro-grade output of Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

On top of the features we've discussed, you can use masking to hide distracting objects, blur sensitive information, or apply effects to selected areas of a scene.

The software also includes keyframe controls, letting you adjust the position, size, speed, and appearance of elements throughout your video. For audio, there's voiceover recording, noise reduction, and audio ducking, which automatically lowers background audio to make narration easier to hear.

## Should you buy it?

✅ **Buy Pinnacle Studio 26 if...**

You want a Windows video editor with advanced features but don't want to keep paying for an Adobe subscription. It's particularly appealing if you regularly create YouTube content, tutorials, travel videos, or personal projects and want more control than basic editing software provides.

❌ **Skip Pinnacle Studio 26 if...**

You already rely on Adobe Premiere for professional projects, especially if you use other Adobe applications or need to exchange projects with collaborators. It's unsuitable if you use a Mac, as Studio 26 is designed for Windows.
