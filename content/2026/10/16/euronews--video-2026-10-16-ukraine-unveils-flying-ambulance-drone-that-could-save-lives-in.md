Built to reach casualties in areas inaccessible to ground vehicles, the remotely operated machine can reportedly lift **150 kg** and travel at speeds of up to**105 km/h**. Its ability to fly over** minefields**, rivers and damaged roads could help shorten dangerous journeys from the front line.

In the footage, a SkyFall representative steps out of the cabin after landing, while an operator remains at a distance. The manufacturer says StarGate could cut **evacuation times** from hours or days to just**minutes**, potentially allowing wounded troops to receive medical attention much sooner.

The aircraft uses **Starlink** and SkyFall's L-Link system, together with**AI-assisted technology** designed to operate despite satellite navigation interference. It was also shown deploying a net to intercept a**hostile drone**.

A Ukrainian combat brigade has now received the **prototype** for testing under frontline conditions. SkyFall has yet to publish the findings, and the drone's performance in real combat evacuations remains to be**independently confirmed**.
