A ROAD in North Yorkshire has been closed following a serious crash.

Emergency services were called to Skipton Road in Harrogate today (Friday, October 16).




North Yorkshire Police has closed the road in both directions between the A59/A61 roundabout and the Bilton roundabout.

A spokesperson said: “Please avoid the area and seek alternative routes while emergency services deal with the incident.”
