A legal team has been named Private Client Team of the Year at the Yorkshire Legal Awards 2026.

Harrogate-based Raworths, which employs 70 people, picked up the honour at New Dock Hall in Leeds on Thursday, October 8.

The firm was recognised for its Trusts, Wills and Estates (TWE) team, described by judges as “an outstanding field that made the decision particularly challenging".

Judges praised Raworths for its client care and community contributions, saying it “reflected the very best of private client practice".

Rachel Tunnicliffe, senior partner and head of private client services at Raworths, said: "We’re incredibly proud to see our Private Client team recognised at the Yorkshire Legal Awards.

"This reflects the expertise, commitment and collaborative spirit of our whole team, and the exceptional service they provide to businesses, families and individuals across the region and beyond."

Raworths was also shortlisted in several other categories, including Law Firm of the Year (medium).
