HAWORTH'S ghostly legends have earned it a place among the UK's most chilling destinations.

The Haworth Ghost Tour in West Yorkshire is featured in Spooky Britain: The 100 Most Eerie and Mysterious Places to Visit, a guidebook by travel writer Peter Naldrett.

The book invites readers to explore "the stories that refuse to die" by visiting the most haunted houses, sinister dungeons, and mysterious crypts across the country.

Peter Naldrett said: "Dare to visit Britain’s most haunted places – and see if you can keep your cool when the lights go out."

Each entry in the book offers a glimpse into what makes these locations unique and why they continue to attract thrill-seekers and ghost-hunters alike.

The Haworth Ghost Tour is one of 100 destinations featured, alongside Edinburgh’s ghost tours and Whitby’s Dracula experiences.

Mr Naldrett is known for his travel writing and guidebooks, including Around the Coast in 80 Days.
