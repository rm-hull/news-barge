**Russian strikes** sparked**major fires** in Ukraine's**Odesa and Zaporizhzhia regions** on**Friday 16 October**, leaving** industrial premises engulfed in flames**.

In Odesa, firefighters battled a blaze at a **factory producing household goods**, dragging hoses towards the burning building as products lay scattered across the ground.

Further east, in Zaporizhzhia, emergency crews used an elevated platform to tackle a **fire covering 500 square metres** at a**private business**. Footage released by** Ukraine's emergency services** shows firefighters directing jets of water into the flames at both sites.

The attacks came as **Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky** joined European leaders for talks. Separate footage shows him arriving alongside**European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen** and**European Council President António Costa**.** German Chancellor Friedrich Merz**,** Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni**and** Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk**were also among the leaders filmed at the gathering. The scenes underline the continuing impact of Russian strikes on Ukrainian businesses as Kyiv pursues** diplomatic discussions with its European partners**.
