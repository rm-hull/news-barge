A MAN has been found dead in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is assisting the Coroner’s Office to locate the next of kin of Stuart Andrew Holbrook, 57, who passed away at his home address in Scarborough on Wednesday (October 14).




A force spokesperson said: “There are not believed to be any suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

“We’re now looking to identify any next of kin for Stuart.

“If you can help us, please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk) and mark for the attention of Sandra Price.”
