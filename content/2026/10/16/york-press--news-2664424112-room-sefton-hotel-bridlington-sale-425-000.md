A prominent Bridlington hotel has gone up for sale for £425,000.

The Sefton Hotel on Flamborough Road is on the market as the owner plans to retire.

The freehold property offers 12 ensuite letting rooms, a top floor three-bedroom flat for owners, and a large dining/function room with a bar.

The building also houses a self-contained one-bedroom flat.

York-based property agents Barry Crux & Co say the hotel, a detached three-storey property, has been a consistent profit earner, drawing in regular annual visitors.

It has benefited from Bridlington's strong summer trade and steady winter market for contractors.

The owner has admitted not fully exploring the business's potential for hosting functions, indicating room for growth in this area.

The hotel is also licensed for a bar and has a large ground floor dining/function space.

There is provision for a maximum of 11 parking spaces on the side.

Notably, subject to planning permission, the property could be converted for other uses, such as residential or holiday lets.

The Sefton Hotel is situated in a prime location close to the seafront and town centre, surrounded by a mix of residential and guest house or bed and breakfast accommodation.

Bridlington, a well-established popular seaside resort on the east coast, attracts visitors throughout the season.

The town's usual population of 38,000 reportedly increases significantly during the holiday season.
