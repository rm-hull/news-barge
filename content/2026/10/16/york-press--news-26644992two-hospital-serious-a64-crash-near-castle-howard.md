Two people have been taken to hospital after a serious crash on the A64 in North Yorkshire.

Police, firefighters and paramedics, including an air ambulance, were called to the crash at Barton Hill, near Castle Howard, at about 1.40pm on Friday (October 16).

The road was closed in both directions after the collision and has since reopened.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said two people were taken to hospital by road after the crash.

"We received an emergency call at 1.37pm this afternoon to report a road traffic collision on the A64," an ambulance service spokesperson said on Friday.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including two ambulances and the air ambulance, and two patients were conveyed to hospital by road."

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said two fire crews were called to the crash and found the air bags deployed in both vehicles.

"Crews assisted with making the vehicles safe and isolating them using small tools," a service spokesperson said.
