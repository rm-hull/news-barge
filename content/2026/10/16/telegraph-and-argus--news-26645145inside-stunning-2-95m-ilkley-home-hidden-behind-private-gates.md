A substantial detached Edwardian home in a sought-after residential area in the Bradford district has been placed on the market with a guide price of £2.95 million.

The Lindens, on Rupert Road in Ilkley’s exclusive Middleton district, is being marketed by Yorkshire estate agent Dacre, Son & Hartley.

The detached property offers five double bedrooms and combines period features with modern family living.

At the centre of the house is an impressive reception hall leading to a spacious living room, family room, dining room, garden room and contemporary living kitchen.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21593258.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Lindens in Middleton, Ilkley *(Image: submitted)*

The first floor includes a principal bedroom with a dressing area and en-suite bathroom, while two further double bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bathroom.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21593227.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Lindens, on Rupert Road in Middleton, Ilkley *(Image: submitted)*

A fourth bedroom and house bathroom complete the floor.

On the second floor, there is a fifth double bedroom with an en-suite bathroom alongside an office suite.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21593259.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Lindens in Middleton, Ilkley *(Image: submitted)*

The Lindens is approached through remote-controlled gates and sits within gardens extending to around 0.6 acres.

The property also benefits from a detached garage block.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21593260.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Lindens in Middleton, Ilkley *(Image: submitted)*

Patrick McCutcheon, head of residential at Dacre, Son & Hartley, said: “The Lindens is a superb example of an Edwardian property, with large bay windows, decorative gable ends and airy rooms offering plenty of space and natural light.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21593261.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Lindens in Middleton, Ilkley *(Image: submitted)*

“The house retains an array of original features, including wood panelling, an elaborately carved staircase and landing and delightful fireplaces.

“It’s also in a highly desirable location, just a short walk from Ilkley town centre, with its shops, leisure facilities and restaurants, as well as the railway station and local schools.

"The property is also around a 30-minute drive from both the Yorkshire Dales and Leeds Bradford Airport, adding to its appeal.”

Further details are available from Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Ilkley office.
