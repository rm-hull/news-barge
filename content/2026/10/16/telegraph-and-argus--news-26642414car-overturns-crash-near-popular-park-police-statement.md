A car overturned in a crash near a popular park in Bradford.

Emergency services were called just after 7am on Thursday to reports of a crash on North Park Road near Lister Park.

Social media pictures showed the car had overturned onto its side.

North Park Road runs down one side of Lister Park.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call just after 7am on Thursday morning to report a collision on North Park Road, Bradford.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene but no-one required conveyance to hospital."

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for comment.
