Two vehicles were left badly damaged following a rush-hour crash on a busy Bradford road this morning.

The collision happened at around 7.47am on October 16 on Leeds road at the junction with Hemingway Road in Idle.

## Two cars involved in collision

A witness at the scene reported that two vehicles had been involved in the crash.

Images from the scene show both cars sustaining significant damage following the collision at the junction.

The circumstances surrounding the crash have not yet been confirmed.

## Traffic moving slowly through the area

Despite the damage to the vehicles, traffic was reported to be getting past the scene.

A witness said traffic was moving slowly as drivers navigated around the crashed vehicles during the morning commute.

The collision caused disruption for motorists travelling along Leeds Road at one of the busiest times of the day.

## No immediate reports of injuries

There were no immediate indications that anyone had been injured in the crash.

A witness at the scene said there was no obvious sign of injuries following the collision and no ambulance sirens had been heard in the area, although it was unclear whether emergency services had attended earlier.

One local resident said: "The two cars looked pretty smashed up. That junction can be quite busy during rush hour. I'm not surprised there's been a crash there. Hope the people involved are ok "

## Commuters face delays

Drivers travelling through Idle were advised to expect delays while the vehicles remained at the junction.

West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been approached for further information about the collision
