**Police and fire crews are attending an incident in an industrial estate in Perth.**

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service said that it was attending a fire on Whitefriars Crescent near Glasgow Road in Perth, alongside Police Scotland.

They urged drivers to avoid the area.
