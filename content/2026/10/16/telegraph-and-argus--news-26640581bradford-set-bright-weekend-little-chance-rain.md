THE Met Office has forecast a sunny weekend for Bradford.

Saturday will start off clear, with temperatures around 5C.

The sky will remain clear throughout the morning, with temperatures gradually rising to 5C by 9am.

Sunny intervals are expected from 4pm, with temperatures peaking at 12C by 9pm.

The evening will remain clear, with temperatures dropping to 5C by midnight.

Sunday will also start off clear, with temperatures around 5C.

The sky will remain clear throughout the morning, with temperatures gradually rising to 8C by 10am.

Sunny intervals are expected from 12pm, with temperatures peaking at 11C by 3pm.

The evening will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 8C by 9pm.

The chance of rain is less than 5 per cent on Saturday and between 10 and 30 per cent on Sunday.

Wind speeds will remain light, with a maximum of 8mph on Saturday and 9mph on Sunday.
