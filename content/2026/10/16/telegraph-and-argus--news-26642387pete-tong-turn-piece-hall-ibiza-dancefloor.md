PETE Tong is bringing the sounds of Ibiza to Halifax with his world-famous Ibiza Classics show.

The iconic DJ and broadcaster will headline Live At The Piece Hall on Sunday, August 29, as part of the Live At The Piece Hall 2027 summer series.

He will be joined by The Essential Orchestra, Grammy-nominated producer Mousse T, and DJ Kavita Varu.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "I'm beyond excited to be welcoming Pete Tong back with The Essential Orchestra as he once again turns our unique Courtyard into the coolest of dancefloors!"

"Epic club tunes, recreated by the most talented classical musicians, means a night of spine-tingling, hands in the air moments.

"Joined by Mousse T, one of the most highly regarded house DJ's and producers on the planet and Yorkshire's finest dance party starter Kavita Varu grabbing tickets for this this night is, well... essential!"

Pete Tong’s Ibiza Classics show blends iconic club tracks with orchestral arrangements for a unique live experience.

It is the latest addition to the 2027 line-up, which already features Suki Waterhouse, Rudimental, Bananarama, Slash, Rick Astley, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, and James Morrison.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, October 23.
