# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after e-bike stolen from property in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following a burglary at a property in Harrogate.

The incident happened between 7.10pm and 7.20pm on Saturday, October 10 when a garage at a property on St Hilda’s Road was entered and an e-bike was stolen.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after an e-bike was stolen from a property on St Hilda's Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/16/15/36/854458057_1825430381799737_7460918749724201668_n.jpeg?trim=133,0,174,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The bike is described as Como e-bike in black with a children’s seat on the back.

A yellow jacket, helmet and toddlers helmet that was on the bike was found discarded around the corner from victims address.

The suspect was seen heading towards the St Clements Road area.

If you have any information, including any CCTV, doorbell or dash cam footage capturing the incident or suspect, then you should get in contact with the police.

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260199828 when providing any details regarding the incident.
