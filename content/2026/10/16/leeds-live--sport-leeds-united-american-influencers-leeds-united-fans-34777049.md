# American influencer's Leeds United fans plea to 'adopt him' after travel blunder

## 'Daddy Wellness' Instagrammer, Richie Lovelace, rushed to Instagram to share his dilemma

An American fitness influencer has gone viral in Leeds after filming himself begging for a local family to "adopt him" for a day, after forgetting to book a hotel.

Richie Lovelace, better known online as Daddy Wellness, took to his 2.1 million Instagram followers, explaining that he had "forgotten" to book a hotel for his travel to Leeds this upcoming weekend. His short video explained that he was looking for "die-hard" Leeds fans to house him for his travels. Speaking to his phone, Lovelace said: "I am f\*\*\*\*\*. I forgot to book a hotel in Leeds, and my train arrives tomorrow at midday.

"I was wondering if there was a die-hard Leeds family out there who would adopt me for a day - pick me up from the station, feed me a meal, and let me crash on their couch and get my Leeds tattoo that I have to live up to... maybe we will share a few songs and teach me about the club's history."

He signs off the video by asking his followers to leave a comment below or send him a DM if they live near Leeds Station.

@daddywellness (real name Richie Lovelace) is a wellness entrepreneur and popular fitness/lifestyle creator based in Miami. He focuses on balancing physical fitness with a "social", fun lifestyle across his social media.
