Asda shoppers are sharing their excitement for a pink version of a classic biscuit.

The new treat is part of the supermarket’s Tickled Pink campaign, which raises money for breast cancer charities.

Costing £2.35 for a 162g pack, the Pink Choc Coated Custard Creams are covered in pink white chocolate and are suitable for vegetarians.

They've already caused a discussion on social media after being shared by the Facebook page Newfoodsuk.

One shopper said: "They are very nice."

Another commented: "We NEED to try these."

A third person said: "Need them!!"

Someone else joked: "Please pick me up 13 packets of these please."

A minimum of 10% of the price of products included in the supermarket’s Tickled Pink range is donated to its charity partners, as reported by What’s The Jam?

The money is divided on a 70/30 basis between Breast Cancer Now and CoppaFeel! respectively.

Tickled Pink has been running since 1996 and marked its 30th anniversary in 2026.

## Asda to make change to up to 700 UK stores

Around 700 Asda stores will see a major change at locations across the UK.

The popular supermarket chain has announced a partnership with parcel locker provider InPost.

This means up to 700 Asda stores will have an InPost locker installed across the UK.
