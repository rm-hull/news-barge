Firefighters were called to a vehicle fire in Ryedale last night (Thursday).

A crew from Pickering were called to the incident on **Penniston Lane, Ebberston**, at** 6.35pm**.

The fire was already out when crews arrived.

#### Penniston Lane, Ebberston

They dampened down hotspots and made the vehicle safe.
