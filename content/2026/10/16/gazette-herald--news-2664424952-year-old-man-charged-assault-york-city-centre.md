A MAN in his 50s is set to appear in court after allegedly attacking a worker in a busy York city centre street.

North Yorkshire Police say it has charged a man after an alleged ‘altercation’ that took place at 6.45pm on Wednesday evening (October 14) in Jubbergate, off Parliament Street.

A spokesperson for the force said: “We were called to a report of an altercation on Jubbergate in the city centre, wherein a man had been aggressive and attempted to attack a retail worker.

“A 52-year-old man was arrested and later charged with assault and public order offences.”

The has been released on unconditional bail until his appearance at York Magistrates' Court on Monday, November 30.
