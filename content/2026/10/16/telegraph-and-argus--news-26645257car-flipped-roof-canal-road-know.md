A shocking image has emerged after a car flipped on its roof on a major road.

The incident occurred on Canal Road, Bradford this afternoon.

When the T&A visited the scene at around 5pm, the car remained on its roof.

Images posted on social media show at least one police vehicle and ambulance in attendance.

However, West Yorkshire Police has confirmed there were no injuries.
