Drivers are facing severe delays after a crash on the M62.

The collision happened on the eastbound carriageway between junction 27 (Gildersome) and junction 29 (Lofthouse), causing reported delays of up to 34 minutes and average speeds of just 5mph.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21592829.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The M62 eastbound traffic build-up near J28 at Tingley *(Image: Highways England)*

Images from the scene show two vehicles blocking the first two lanes, with several others stopped on the hard shoulder.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21592817.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The site of the collision near J29 *(Image: T&A)*

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21592816.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The site of the collision near J29 *(Image: T&A)*

Traffic officers were in attendance.
