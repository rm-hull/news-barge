The new series of Celebrity Traitors has started with more lies and heated roundtables taking place.

21 celebrities are hoping they will either outwit the Traitors or take on the Faithfuls to win money for their chosen charity.

The Faithfuls got off to a good start after they banished Traitor Richard E Grant from the Scottish castle.

While the show feels very autumnal and cosy to us, here is when it was actually filmed.

## When was the Celebrity Traitors series two filmed?

To fit filming around all of the celebrities' schedules, the filming for series two of the Celebrity Traitors took place in the spring of this year.

It started at the end of May and lasted for around 16 to 18 days.

It finished filming in early June.

This means the celebrities have had to keep what happened at the Scottish castle completely secret for months.

The episodes of Celebrity Traitors will be broadcast on Thursdays and Fridays for five weeks.
