Dating from the late Victorian era – with the added bonus of attractive Arts and Crafts influences – number 41 Abbey Road has plenty of attributes to appeal to buyers, not the least of which is its location on one of Darlington’s most prestigious residential roads, just a short walk from the town centre.

The property combines striking architectural character with substantial accommodation which extends to around 4,316 sq.ft.

Carefully maintained and sympathetically enhanced by the current owners, this seamlessly blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557406.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This home on Darlington’s Abbey Road successfully blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living *(Image: Knight Frank)*

Added to this is a wealth of original architectural features including ornate fireplaces, decorative stained glass, timber panelling and high ceilings in rooms which boast magnificent proportions throughout.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557405.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This home on Darlington’s Abbey Road successfully blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living *(Image: Knight Frank)*

The property’s attractive facade is the first sign that this home is anything but average, a view that’s confirmed the moment you step from the porch into the magnificent reception hall which creates an unforgettable first impression and immediately establishes the quality and scale of the accommodation beyond.

Rising to a dramatic double-height ceiling, the hall features beautiful parquet flooring, decorative wall panelling, stained glass windows and an impressive staircase with richly detailed timber balustrading.

Radiating from the reception hall – which is currently used as a dining room – is the ground floor accommodation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557399.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This home on Darlington’s Abbey Road successfully blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living *(Image: Knight Frank)*

This includes a cloakroom; a dedicated study with a feature fireplace and a mullioned bay window with window seat; an elegant drawing room of superb proportions and with a wealth of period detailing which ensures it is perfect for both formal entertaining and family gatherings and an equally impressive sitting room of considerable character which features intricate full-height timber panelling, exposed ceiling beams, large garden-facing windows and an attractive fireplace, all of which combine to create an atmosphere of warmth and sophistication.

At the heart of the home is the beautifully appointed kitchen/breakfast room.

Fitted with bespoke handcrafted cabinetry with granite work surfaces plus a matching peninsular island breakfast bar and home to integrated appliances including an impressive AGA range cooker set within a traditional oak surround, the kitchen provides an exceptional environment for both everyday living and entertaining.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557401.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This home on Darlington’s Abbey Road successfully blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living *(Image: Knight Frank)*

Adjoining pantry and utility facilities offer plenty of practical storage, while the snug provides a social element to the room.

A magnificent conservatory – accessed from both the drawing and sitting rooms and enjoying views over the gardens – is a light-filled space perfectly suited to relaxation and informal entertaining.

Arranged around the first floor galleried landing, the focal point of which is a large stained glass window on the half landing, are a generous principal suite with a walk-in dressing room and four further bedrooms, one of which could be used as a dressing room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557397.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This home on Darlington’s Abbey Road successfully blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living *(Image: Knight Frank)*

Two further bedrooms – or a bedroom and dressing room – occupy the second floor, providing additional accommodation ideally suited to guests.

The gardens and grounds provide a wonderful setting for the house and contribute significantly to the property’s appeal.

Positioned within mature landscaped gardens, the property enjoys an exceptional degree of privacy and seclusion despite its convenient location within the town.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557408.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This home on Darlington’s Abbey Road successfully blends period elegance and craftsmanship with the practical requirements of modern family living *(Image: Knight Frank)*

Extensive lawns, established planting and mature trees surround the house, creating an attractive environment throughout the year.

A substantial detached garage extends to over 33 feet in length and is complemented by additional storage areas and ancillary accommodation including a utility room.
