A British-Asian restaurant with a “famously diverse” menu has been praised for its “bustling” atmosphere and “fragrant” food.

Bradford is known as the curry capital of Britain but if you are looking for a curry house that is a little bit different, you might want to try MyLahore.

Found in the centre of the city, the restaurant offers British-Asian dining with everything on its menu from traditional curries to fusion dishes.

No matter what your family’s preferences are, you should be able to find something to suit everyone at MyLahore.

## What is MyLahore in Bradford like?

MyLahore first opened its doors in 2002 and has expanded to other places in England such as Manchester, Leeds, and Blackburn.

The restaurant’s menu is known for its wide range of options including classic curries like butter chicken, karahi, dopiaza, and jalfrezi.

Alongside your curry, you can enjoy seasoned rice and some desi bread to mop up the sauce.

Before tucking into a curry, you can enjoy some “old school classic” starters ranging from different pakoras and chaats to dynamite prawns.

If you are not a fan of curry, there are also lots of burgers like the crispy chicken burger, smash burger, and grilled coley fish burger as well as pasta dishes, stir fries, and steak medallions.

To finish off your meal, you can enjoy something sweet from desi classics like falooda to British favourites including chocolate sponge pudding and jam roly poly.
