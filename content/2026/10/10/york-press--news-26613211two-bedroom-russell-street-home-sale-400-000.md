## Inside York home blending ‘original character and timeless appeal’ as it hits market

Inside the two-bedroom terraced house for sale in Russell Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £400,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

A York home that blends “contemporary style with its original character and timeless appeal” has hit the market.

The two-bedroom terraced house is for sale in Russell Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £400,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said the period home has been “thoughtfully modernised throughout”.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546655.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The two-bedroom terraced house for sale in Russell Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £400,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

Inside, a bright entrance hall leads to the kitchen/dining room and utility area.

The living room is “full of period character”, Hudson Moody said, with stripped-back floorboards, traditional timber folding shutters to the bay window, a cast iron radiator and an attractive fireplace with marble surround.

Meanwhile, the kitchen/dining area is complete with an electric Aga and double Belfast sink. There is also a separate utility room.

Upstairs are two double bedrooms, both featuring ornamental cast iron fireplaces, stripped-back timber flooring and built-in storage. The home’s bathroom is also on the first floor.

Outside is a rear courtyard – “ideal for alfresco dining”, Hudson Moody said.

The estate agent added that on-street permit parking is available for the home.
