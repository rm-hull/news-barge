Firefighters are dealing with a fire at a derelict building close to Oastler Market in Bradford city centre.

Emergency crews were called to John Street at 6.42pm on Saturday, October 10, with the incident classified as a property fire.

A significant emergency response remains in place as firefighters work to tackle the blaze.

## **Residents Urged to Keep Windows and Doors Closed**

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service has advised people living and working nearby to keep their windows and doors shut due to smoke coming from the incident.

The precaution has been issued to help prevent smoke entering homes, businesses and other buildings in the city centre.

A number of specialist fire service units have been sent to the scene, including an aerial appliance, aerial support, as well as a technical rescue unit and a welfare unit

## **Emergency Response Continues**

No information has yet been released regarding the cause of the fire, and it is not currently known whether anyone has been injured.

Crews remain at the scene this evening as efforts continue to bring the fire under control.

The Telegraph & Argus will provide further updates as more information becomes available.

## **FULL statement from Emergency Services**

A WYFRS spokesperson said: "Firefighters are currently dealing with a fire at a derelict building on John Street, Bradford.

"Residents in the surrounding area are advised to keep windows and doors closed due to smoke coming from the incident.

"Please avoid the area where possible to help maintain access for emergency vehicles and crews attending the incident.

"Thank you for your cooperation. #TeamWYFRS"
