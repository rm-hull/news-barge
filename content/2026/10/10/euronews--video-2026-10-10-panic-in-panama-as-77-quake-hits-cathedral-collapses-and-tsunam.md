A series of strong earthquakes hit **Panama** on Friday, 9 October, causing damage to buildings and triggering evacuations. Residents rushed into the streets of**Panama City**, while tremors were also reported in Colombia.

In **Penonomé**, part of a cathedral collapsed, including one of its spires, while footage showed extensive damage to a nearby hotel. Residents in** Herrera province** also reported cracks and damage to their homes.

**Tocumen International Airport** briefly suspended operations before reopening, while the**Panama Canal** continued operating normally.

According to the **US Geological Survey**, the first earthquake struck at a depth of** 10 kilometres**, around 10 kilometres southwest of Pitaloza Arriba in Herrera province.** Tsunami warnings** were issued for Panama and several neighbouring countries.

Panama's president said **no deaths had been reported** in the immediate aftermath, as emergency teams continued assessing the damage.

The country lies along the Pacific **Ring of Fire**, a vast zone of seismic and volcanic activity where most of the world's earthquakes occur.
