Do you recognise any of these four people?

They are wanted by police investigating crimes at a York holiday let.

They were staying at an Airbnb on Burton Stone Lane.

#### Burton Stone Lane, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cctv-airbnb-burton-stone-lane-york-damage-1.webp)![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cctv-airbnb-burton-stone-lane-york-damage-4.webp)![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cctv-airbnb-burton-stone-lane-york-damage-3.webp)![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cctv-airbnb-burton-stone-lane-york-damage-2.webp)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The two men and two women stole items from the property and caused over £1,000 of criminal damage before leaving in the early hours of their departure day without handing back the house key.

“The group used false names, contact numbers and addresses to book the property and did not register any vehicle details.”

If you can help police, you can contact them on the numbers below.
