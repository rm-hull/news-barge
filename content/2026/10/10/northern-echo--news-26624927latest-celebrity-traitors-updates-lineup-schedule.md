The second series of Celebrity Traitors is underway.

The beloved BBC series began on October 1, with famous faces heading to Scotland to play the ultimate game of trust, deception, and strategy in hopes of winning up to £100,000 for a charity of their choice.

TV star Claudia Winkleman will once again host, as 21 celebrities take part.

Here you can find everything you need to know about the new series of Celebrity Traitors, including the full celebrity lineup, updates on theories and who has been eliminated, new twists in this series, and more.

## Latest Celebrity Traitors news

## What is the schedule for Celebrity Traitors?

The new series of Celebrity Traitors starts on Thursday, October 1.

The show is airing twice weekly on Thursdays and Fridays at 8pm and is available to watch on both BBC One and BBC iPlayer.

The official video podcast and spin-off show, The Celebrity Traitors: Uncloaked, is also back on BBC One, BBC iPlayer, and BBC Sounds.

Hosted by Ed Gamble, the podcast features exclusive interviews, behind-the-scenes footage, and reactions from both banished and "murdered" players.

There will also be exclusive bonus episodes available immediately after the main show on BBC Sounds, with extended celebrity interviews and in-depth gameplay analysis.

New for 2026 is a special titled Traitor or Faithful? Claudia Decides.

It will reveal how Claudia selects her Traitors, offer a more personal look at the contestants and give viewers fresh insight into her decision-making process.

Traitor or Faithful? Claudia Decides will be available on Thursday, October 1, from 9.15pm on BBC iPlayer.

## Where is Celebrity Traitors filmed?

Celebrity Traitors, just like the main series, is filmed at Ardross Castle in the Scottish Highlands.

The castle is located near the town of Alness, about 30 miles north of Inverness.

It is a 19th-century Scottish baronial-style castle set in over 100 acres of parkland on the banks of the River Alness.

However, local sights like Loch Glass and nearby areas are often used for outdoor challenges and water-based missions.

The property is available as a wedding venue, with its popularity rising since its appearances in The Traitors.

## Celebrity Traitors FAQs

### Who hosts Celebrity Traitors?

Claudia Winkleman hosts Celebrity Traitors, just as she does the main series.

### How does the game work?

The show is based on the Dutch series De Verraders and features a group of contestants who get divided into "faithfuls" and a small number of "traitors".

It then works similarly to Mafia or Werewolf, where the faithfuls have to figure out the identity of the traitors.

Meanwhile, the traitors work to eliminate all the other contestants by 'murder' or getting them banished from the game at a daily roundtable vote.

### What do winners receive?

The winners of The Traitors get up to a maximum of £120,000 for the UK version.

The final amount depends on how much money the group successfully adds to the prize fund during challenges throughout the season.

It can be shared among several winners or all taken by one, but if any traitors are left at the end of the game, they will take all of the money.

### How many episodes of The Traitors are there?

In each regular series of The Traitors, there have been 12 episodes, airing over four weeks.

Last year's first series of Celebrity Traitors had nine episodes, also airing over four weeks.

### Is Celebrity Traitors different from The Traitors?

The celebrity spin-off is different in the fact that it sees celebrities take on the challenges and the Traitors game.

However, the game works the same way, aside from twists that may air during each series.

The key difference is that the prize money is for charity in the celebrity version.
