One Saltburn business offers the perfect afternoon browsing beautiful ceramics before settling down with a coffee and homemade cake, all under one roof.

Tucked away on Station Street, Lillian Daph has become one of the town's best-known independent shops, with a Scandinavian-inspired interior, stylish gifts and home accessories - from ceramics and kitchenware to furniture, textiles, lighting, stationery and seasonal decorations.

Many of its products are sourced from independent brands, complemented by curated gift hampers and own-brand collections that reflect the shop's unique style.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560534.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lillian Daph, Saltburn *(Image: Dawn McNamara /TVCA)*

Stepping inside, shoppers will find carefully styled displays of stoneware mugs, hand-painted bowls, cushions, candles and home accessories, creating the sort of interiors inspiration usually associated with far larger city stores.

Alongside the shop is Yard., an independent coffee shop serving speciality coffee, fresh pastries, cakes and other sweet treats in a cosy courtyard-style setting.

Many visitors now combine a browse around the store with a stop at the café, which was created using a thoughtfully renovated yard space on site.

It is also pet-friendly, making it a popular stop for dog owners exploring the coast.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560535.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lillian Daph, Saltburn *(Image: Dawn McNamara /TVCA)*

The shop has become a hotspot for visitors spending the day in Saltburn, adding to the town’s growing reputation for independent businesses, cafés and boutiques.

For shoppers looking for something a little different from the high street, it offers a distinctive blend of Scandinavian interiors, thoughtful gifting and speciality coffee that keeps customers coming back.

Whether it's a handcrafted ceramic, a birthday gift or a perfectly made flat white, there's plenty to tempt visitors through the doors of Lillian Daph.

In the heart of the town, the store is only a short walk from the seafront, pier and Station Square.
