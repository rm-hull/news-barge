## Man Utd

**Senne Lammens:** Made a major early error that could have had disastrous consequences but the Belgian recovered to make the save from Dominic Solanke. Mis-control shortly afterwards showed more signs of nerves and pass straight out of play summed up United's first half hour. Might have done better for equaliser.**5Leny Yoro (off 59, Dalot):** Given a start after making France debut and excellent tackle to deny Jan Paul van Hecke a clear sight at goal. Decent effort.**7Harry Maguire:** Usual solid presence at the heart of the home defence but no chance to threaten at the other end and fact he did try a shot from 30 yards suggested lack .**7Lisandro Martinez:** Partnership with Maguire a plus point for Man Utd but was totally outmuscled by Solanke for Spurs' equaliser.**6Luke Shaw:** One of the home side's more consistent performers and got forward to force a corner early in the second period and made a vital intervention at the other end. Lucky to avoid red card for his reactionary shove on Rodrigo Bentancur?**6Kobbie Mainoo:** Caught in possession on too many occasions, even if the passes he was receiving inside his own half were ill-advised. Tried to get on the ball in deep lying midfield role. Excellent block to deny Savio.**7Youri Tielemans (off 59, Baleba):** Lucky to get away with being robbed by Savio deep in the United half due to debatable free-kick but calm in heading the ball back to Lammens to relieve pressure on home goal.**6Amad Diallo (off 59, Zirkzee):** Took a long time to get into the game on his first appearance of the season but eventually had home side's first shot.**6Bruno Fernandes:** Shadow of the player who deservedly won all the player of the year awards last season. Didn't shoot when had the chance suggests confidence levels have dipped.**5Matheus Cunha:** Reaction to negativity from the stands shows he cares but, like many, yet to reach last term's levels. Provided the pass for Mbuemo's goal.**6Bryan Mbeumo:** Didn't have enough support to be effective in the lead striking role but led one counter and turned smartly to score United's goal.**8.Subs:Diogo Dalot (on 59, Yoro):** Nearly set up Bruno Fernandes with cross from the right.**5Carlos Baleba (on 59, Tielemans):** Introduction injected life into home fans and made plenty of tackles.**5Joshua Zirkzee (on 59, Amad):** Quickly into action to force a corner and let Tottenham know he was there.**6Ayden Heaven (on 90, Shaw). 5Marcus Rashford (on 90, Cunha). 5Subs not used: Karl Darlow, Mason Mount, Andrey Santos, Shea Lacey.**
