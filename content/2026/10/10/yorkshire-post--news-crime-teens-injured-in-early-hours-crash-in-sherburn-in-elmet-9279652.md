# Teens injured in early hours crash in Sherburn in Elmet

## Four teenagers were taken to hospital with serious injuries after a late night crash in Sherburn in Elmet last night.

Police are now appealing for any witnesses or anyone with dashcam footage to come forward.

The crash happened in the early hours of Saturday morning, around 2.50am, on Coldhill Lane.

Four people in a red Seat Ibiza, three 17-year-olds and an 18-year-old, were taken to hospital with serious injuries.

![Police officers are investigating](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmVkMGM3M2UwLTE2YzYtNDZkYy1iOTU3LWNkYTMwZjlmOGQ0YzplMGZkNDdkNi1lODE1LTQzNmMtYjc1Mi0zMWQ3MWFmNGNmZjg=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“We are urging anyone who witnessed the collision, saw the red Seat Ibiza in the moments leading up to it, or has relevant dashcam footage, to come forward,” a spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police (NYP) said.

“In particular, we are keen to identify and speak to the driver of a white pick-up truck with a yellow stripe along the side, which is believed to have been in the area and may have seen the vehicle shortly before the collision occurred.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact police quoting reference 12260199148.
