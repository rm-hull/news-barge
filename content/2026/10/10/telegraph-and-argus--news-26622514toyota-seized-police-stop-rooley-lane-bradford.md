Police seized a car after a roadside game of "Guess Who" revealed its driver did not have a licence.

Officers from Operation Steerside stopped the car on Rooley Avenue in Bradford.

Police eventually discovered the identity of the driver after what they described having to play a game of "roadside Guess Who".

A spokesperson for Operation Steerside said: "Seen driving on Rooley Avenue, this Toyota was stopped for a document check.

"After a short game of roadside "Guess Who" we eventually got round to establishing the correct identity of the driver, who was found to be a non-licence holder.

"The vehicle was seized under S.165 Road Traffic Act 1988 and driver reported for the offence."
