All Creatures Great and Small stars Rachel Shenton and Nicholas Ralph have given fans a special look behind the scenes at one of the show's most recognisable locations.

The actors, who play Helen Herriot and James Herriot in the hit Channel 5 drama, have taken viewers on a tour of Skeldale House, revealing some of the details that help bring the fictional Yorkshire veterinary practice and family home to life.

The new video comes as series seven of All Creatures Great and Small is currently airing on Channel 5, once again transporting viewers to the Yorkshire Dales.

Shenton and Ralph lead fans through the familiar Skeldale House set, taking in rooms which have provided the backdrop to some of the programme's most memorable moments.

Among the locations shown is the dining room, while Shenton also gives viewers a look around the living room and explains how the house has changed as the characters' lives have developed.

The tour also heads into the surgery, where Ralph points out some of the details surrounding James' working life as a vet.

One addition is particularly significant for the latest chapter of the story, with Ralph highlighting changes made following the arrival of James and Helen's son, Jimmy.

The pair also venture beyond Skeldale House to The Drovers Arms, another location which has become synonymous with the series.

The video gives viewers a much closer look at the sets than they normally receive on screen, revealing the attention to detail that goes into creating the world of Darrowby.

Based on James Herriot's much-loved veterinary books, All Creatures Great and Small stars Ralph as James alongside Shenton as Helen, Samuel West as Siegfried Farnon and Callum Woodhouse as Tristan Farnon.

Although Darrowby itself is fictional, much of the programme is filmed across Yorkshire, with the market town of Grassington transformed for filming.

The Yorkshire Dales have become almost as important to the programme as its characters, with locations used for the drama attracting fans keen to see the real-life places behind the series.

Grassington is regularly transformed into 1940s Darrowby, while locations including Broughton Hall and the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway have also featured during the show's run.

The behind-the-scenes glimpse comes as the seventh series continues on Channel 5, following the characters into another chapter of life in the Dales.

For fans who have spent years watching James and Helen's story unfold inside Skeldale House, the new tour offers a rare chance to step through its doors and see the home from the stars' perspective.
