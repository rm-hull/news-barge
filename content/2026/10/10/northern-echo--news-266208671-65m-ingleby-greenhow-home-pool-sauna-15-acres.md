*(Image: GSC Grays)*

Constructed in the 1970s to replace an earlier farmstead, High Farm House is positioned to take full advantage of an elevated position from where it enjoys a breathtaking backdrop and enchanting views.

A unique property in an equally unique location, High Farm House is home to some 6,592 sq. ft. of well presented and elegant accommodation which offers its new owners both flexibility and the opportunity to improve and update it should they wish to.

A meandering driveway that passes through a maturing woodland before entering a large paved courtyard at the front of the house creates an immediate sense of arrival.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557360.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A wealth of elegant accommodation and a stunning backdrop combine to make High Farm House a truly special home *(Image: GSC Grays)*

This sense of grandeur continues once you enter the property, where the formal central reception hall – with a cloakroom off – leads to a large and light-filled dual aspect formal drawing room centred around a feature fireplace then through double doors into a spacious dining room.

Like the drawing room, this has half panelled walls and a feature fireplace.

It also has a further set of double doors which open into a conservatory strategically positioned to catch the late afternoon sun.

Also on the ground floor are an elegant library-style study, a large utility room and ancillary storerooms, and a well proportioned breakfast kitchen with wooden flooring, exposed timber ceiling beams, a range cooker, windows overlooking the walled courtyard and room for a dining table.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557365.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A wealth of elegant accommodation and a stunning backdrop combine to make High Farm House a truly special home *(Image: GSC Grays)*

An inner hallway leads to a flexible studio/games room ideal for use as additional family space, with multiple double doors opening into the courtyard making it perfect for entertaining purposes.

A hidden doorway leads to a leisure suite comprising a gym area, sauna and an endless pool.

This space lends itself to the creation of further living accommodation if required for dependent relatives, for example.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557359.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A wealth of elegant accommodation and a stunning backdrop combine to make High Farm House a truly special home *(Image: GSC Grays)*

Arranged around the first floor landing are a family bathroom and four bedrooms, including the generous master suite comprising a bedroom, dressing room and bathroom plus a guest suite made up of a bedroom and bathroom.

Externally, High Farm House has an integral car port and storage plus a substantial general purpose building incorporating multi vehicle workshop space and generous garaging plus mezzanine space over.

There is also attached open-fronted storage which incorporates an inspection pit.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557364.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A wealth of elegant accommodation and a stunning backdrop combine to make High Farm House a truly special home *(Image: GSC Grays)*

The professionally managed grounds of High Farm House have developed over many years and now encompass formal lawned areas, a walled courtyard, a kitchen garden plus woodland and grassland, in all extending to some 15.05 acres.
