A remarkable home with a history stretching back more than 240 years is on the market near Durham, offering buyers the chance to own a property designed by one of Britain's most celebrated architects.

Soane House, a Grade II-listed home on the Burn Hall Estate, in Durham, is on the market for £1.35 million.

Sir John Soane designed the five-bedroom property in 1783, and it now combines historic features with modern family living.

![BRADLEY HALL](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559051.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559026.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Built for Burn Hall Estate owner George Smith, the crescent-shaped structure originally housed cattle, including bulls.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559027.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

It was later converted into a family home while retaining many of its historic features.

A double-height entrance hall floods the home with natural light, a hallmark of Soane's design.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559037.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

The standout feature is the circular dining room built inside the former bull house.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559036.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

The room sits beneath a conical roof, recalling the building's original purpose.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559034.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559048.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559035.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

The home also has an open-plan kitchen and living area with bespoke fittings, granite worktops and integrated appliances.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559050.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

A separate sitting room with a multi-fuel stove overlooks the gardens, and five bedrooms occupy the central section and wing pavilions.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559049.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

The principal suite has its own staircase, dressing room and en-suite, while another bedroom opens onto the central balcony overlooking the countryside.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559028.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Soane House sits in around half an acre of private gardens, with a large terrace overlooking the countryside.

A gated driveway and detached four-car garage complete the property.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21559029.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Residents can also use the wider Burn Hall Estate, including its woodland walks, parkland and routes to the River Browney.

Lauren Gill, branch manager at Bradley Hall Durham, said: "Soane House is a remarkable property and a rare chance to own a home with such a significant architectural history.

"Sir John Soane's influence can be seen throughout the property, from its distinctive crescent-shaped design and monumental arches to the circular dining room, central balcony and thoughtful use of natural light."

Sir John Soane, one of Britain's best-known architects, is famed for his work on the Bank of England and Dulwich Picture Gallery.

Soane House is one of Soane's earliest and most unusual surviving designs, making it a rare North East home.
