Bradford Bulls’ Big Bang Spectacular will bring two fireworks displays, live entertainment and fairground rides to the Odsal Stadium.

The event takes place on Wednesday, November 4.

Gates will open at 4.30pm, allowing visitors time to visit food and drink outlets, enjoy the rides and take part in activities for children.

## What to expect at Bradford Bulls' Big Bang Spectacular

A dedicated children’s fireworks display is scheduled for 6.30pm.

It will offer younger spectators the excitement of fireworks in a display designed with them in mind.

The main Big Bang fireworks display will follow at 8.30pm, lighting the sky above the stadium.

![Fireworks](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21383995.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fireworks *(Image: Pixabay)*

Entertainment during the evening will include live music, a DJ and tribute performances.

ABBA and Elvis tribute acts are due to appear before the fireworks begin.

## Entertainment for all ages

Organisers said the annual event has become a fixture in Bradford’s autumn calendar, attracting families and fireworks fans from across the district. They are promising an evening with entertainment for all ages.

Tickets are on sale now. Visitors are encouraged to book in advance to secure places at the Bonfire Night celebration.

The event is expected to offer a range of attractions throughout the evening, with food, drinks, rides and performances available before and between the two displays.
