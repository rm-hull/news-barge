A 39-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of being concerned in the supply of Class A drugs following an operation involving armed police.

The arrest was made on Waterhouse street in Halifax in the early hours of this morning.

No further details have been released regarding the circumstances surrounding the arrest, and officers have not said whether any drugs or other items were seized.

## **Investigation continues**

West Yorkshire Police has not indicated whether further arrests are expected as enquiries progress.

Anyone with information that may assist police is encouraged to contact the force through the appropriate reporting channels.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "A 39-year-old man was arrested by armed officers on Waterhouse Street, Halifax, in the early hours of this morning. He was arrested on suspicion of being concerned in the supply of Class A drugs and is currently in custody."
