A leading city centre venue has been crowned Pub/Bar of the Year for its exceptional drinks , welcoming atmosphere and commitment to bringing people together through memorable experiences.

Brew York Taproom and Beer Hall, on Walmgate, earned the prestigious title at the inaugural Press Food and Drink Awards which were held at the National Railway Museum in celebration of the talent and innovation within the sector.

The Pub/Bar award was given in recognition of Brew York's outstanding contribution to the food and drink scene and celebrates what has become one of the city's most popular destinations.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546648.jpg?type=mds-article-620)From left, Lee Grabham, Megan Gill and Wayne Smith, from Brew York, which won Pub or Bar of the Year, with Olly McClaren, from The Escapologist.

The business attracts locals and visitors alike with its brewery-fresh beer, first-class service and vibrant community spirit.

As the flagship home of Brew York, the Taproom and Beer Hall showcases an ever-evolving range of innovative beers alongside carefully-selected guest brews from respected breweries across the UK and beyond. Its commitment to quality and variety has helped establish it as a must-visit venue for both dedicated craft beer enthusiasts and those discovering the category for the first time.

Judges also praised the venue’s atmosphere, which combines the character of a working brewery with the energy of a modern social space. Despite its large scale, the Taproom and Beer Hall has retained a welcoming and inclusive feel, thanks in no small part to its knowledgeable and passionate team.

Community engagement is another key part of the venue’s success. Through collaborations, special events, brewery experiences and partnerships with local food vendors and businesses, it has become far more than simply a place to enjoy a drink. It has developed into a hub for York’s wider food and drink culture, playing an important role in showcasing the city’s thriving independent hospitality sector.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546315.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Meet the winners of The Press Food and Drink Awards.

The accolade comes during a landmark year as Brew York celebrates its 10th anniversary. Despite the challenges facing the hospitality industry, including rising costs and economic uncertainty, the team has continued to innovate, host successful events and deliver outstanding customer experiences.

Looking ahead, plans include new beer launches, collaborative events and further investment in the customer experience. For now, though, the team can celebrate a well-earned honour as one of York’s standout hospitality success stories.

Presented by Andrew Swift, CEO of Fera Science, the accolade celebrated Brew York's journey from a small independent brewery to an internationally-recognised brand that remains firmly rooted in York.

The Pub/ Bar of the Year Award was sponsored by The Escapologist.
