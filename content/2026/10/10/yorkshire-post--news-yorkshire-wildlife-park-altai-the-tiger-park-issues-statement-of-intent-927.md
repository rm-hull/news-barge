# Yorkshire Wildlife Park: Altai the tiger - park issues statement concerning tiger's fate

![Yorkshire Wildlife Park: rumours had been circulating about the fate of the tiger that killed a man who intruded on its enclosure](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/10/17/06/GettyImages-482258124.jpeg?trim=259,0,1343,0&crop=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## Yorkshire Wildlife Park has issued a statement following yesterday’s fatal incident.

“Many many people have contacted us about Altai, the tiger. We can reassure everyone that he is safe and well and will not be euthanised as a result of this incident,” the park said in a statement.

It went on: “We would like to thank everyone who has expressed their concern following yesterday’s tragic incident.”

“Our thoughts and heartfelt sympathies remain with the family and loved ones of the man who sadly lost his life. The park will be closed today while the staff team who were deeply affected by events receive specialist support.”
