Firefighters freed an occupant of a car after it crashed and ended up on its side today (Saturday).

Emergency services were called to the A170 at Sutton Bank near Thirsk at 3.13pm.

Fire crews from Northallerton and Helmsley worked to free one person trapped in the vehicle.

#### Sutton Bank

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews used a triple extension ladder, cutting gear and stabilisation equipment to secure the vehicle and allow the casualty to walk themselves out.

“The incident was left with police and paramedics.”
