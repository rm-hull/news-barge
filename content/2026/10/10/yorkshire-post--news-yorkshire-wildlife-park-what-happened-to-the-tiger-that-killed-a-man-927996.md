# Yorkshire Wildlife Park: what happened to the tiger that killed a man?

![Yorkshire Wildlife Park: rumours had been circulating about the fate of the tiger that killed a man who intruded on its enclosure](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/10/17/06/GettyImages-482258124.jpeg?trim=259,0,1343,0&crop=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## South Yorkshire Police dispels rumours as to fate of tiger that killed an intruder into its enclosure.

South Yorkshire Police has this evening moved to dispel rumours that the tiger involved in the death of a man today had been destroyed.

The animal was not injured in the incident in which a man gained access to its enclosure. Specialist officers are supporting the family of the man who lost his life.
