*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

This group of visitors to York trashed and looted an AirBnB before disappearing with the house key.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21563882.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the guests that trashed an AirBnB in York *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

The two men and two women gave false names, false address and false contact numbers when they booked their stay at the property in Burtonstone Lane in Clifton.

Nor did they register their vehicle.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21563881.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the guests that trashed an AirBnB in York *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

After they got the key to the address, they caused so much damage it will cost more than £1,000 to repair before leaving in the early hours of their last day, taking with them items from the property.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21563883.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the guests that trashed an AirBnB in York *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

But cameras at the address caught them on film.

Now police are appealing to the public to help them identify and locate the four.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21563884.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the guests that trashed an AirBnB in York *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

If you know who they are or have any information that can help police bring them to justice, contact North Yorkshire Police via its online website, 101 or Crimestoppers.
