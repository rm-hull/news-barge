Four teenagers have been taken to hospital after their car crashed early this morning (Saturday).

Emergency services were called to **Coldhill Lane** in**Sherburn in Elmet** at**2.50am**.

A Seat Ibiza had crashed. The four people inside, three 17-year-olds and an 18-year-old, were taken to hospital with serious injuries.

#### Coldhill Lane, Sherburn in Elmet

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are urging anyone who witnessed the collision, saw the red Seat Ibiza in the moments leading up to it, or has relevant dashcam footage, to come forward.

“In particular, we are keen to identify and speak to the driver of a white pick-up truck with a yellow stripe along the side, which is believed to have been in the area and may have seen the vehicle shortly before the collision occurred.”
