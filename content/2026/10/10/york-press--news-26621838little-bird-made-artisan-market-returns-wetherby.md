## Wetherby town centre to welcome artisan market packed with seasonal goodies

The Little Bird Made Artisan Market will take place in Wetherby town centre on Sunday, October 11 >(Image: Little Bird Artisan Markets)

Handmade gifts, seasonal treats and early festive finds are set to fill the streets as an artisan market returns this October.

The Little Bird Made Artisan Market will take place in Wetherby town centre on Sunday, October 11, from 10am to 3pm, featuring a curated mix of independent makers from across Yorkshire.

Visitors can expect handcrafted homeware, Halloween-themed crafts, seasonal bakes, cheeses, and small-batch spirits.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "October is such a wonderful time of year for our outdoor markets as the air turns crisp and everyone gets into the autumn spirit.

"We’re thrilled to bring our wonderful community of artisans back to Wetherby this Sunday, 11 October.

"We always encourage visitors to make a full day of it - explore Wetherby’s fantastic independent shops, enjoy a hot drink as you wander, and soak up the friendly town atmosphere."

Helen Quinn, owner of Helen’s Hampers, said the markets have helped her business grow, saying: "Helen’s Hampers is still a relatively new business, and Little Bird Artisan Markets have been such a lovely way to get out there, meet customers and become part of the local small-business community."

The market is dog-friendly and encourages visitors to explore Wetherby’s town centre, riverside walks and pubs.
