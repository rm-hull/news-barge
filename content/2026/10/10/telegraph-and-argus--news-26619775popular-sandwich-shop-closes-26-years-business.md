Brighouse’s Mushroom Sandwich Shop has closed after 26 years, with its owners thanking customers for their support.

The Gooder Street takeaway announced on social media that it had officially ceased trading after more than two decades in business.

Mandy and Philip, owners of the shop, shared the news in a post on October 6.

### Owners announce closure after 26 years

Owners Mandy and Philip said: "We have officially ceased trading as the Mushroom Sandwich shop after 26 years, it’s been quite the adventure and would like to thank all our customers for supporting us year upon year."

They added: "Thankyou to all the lovely people of Brighouse and Rastrick we will miss you all.

Love from Mandy & Philip."

The shop had served local residents with sandwiches and breakfast food, and was close to Brighouse bus station.

Its position made it a convenient stop for workers and early morning customers seeking breakfast or lunch.

Over its 26-year run, the business built a loyal following in the town and nearby Rastrick. It was known for freshly made food and friendly service.

### New owner takes over business

The owners also confirmed that the premises have been taken over by a new business owner.

The owners said: "The shop has been taken over by a lovely lady called Lisa (Itsy Bitsy's sandwich shop) so we hope you will support her in this new venture and continue to enjoy her food please give her a try you won’t be disappointed."

The announcement led to messages from customers, who shared memories of visiting the shop.

The shop's final trading day was not stated in the announcement, and no reason for the closure was given.
