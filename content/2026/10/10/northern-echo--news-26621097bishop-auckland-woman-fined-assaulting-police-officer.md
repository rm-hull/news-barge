A Bishop Auckland woman has been fined after admitting assaulting a police officer while she was carrying out her duties.

Helen Jones, 45, of Dent Street, Bishop Auckland, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 5.

She pleaded guilty to assaulting a police constable on August 4. The court heard that the officer was carryintg out her duty at the time of the assault.

Jones was fined £120 and was ordered to pay £50 compensation to the officer for the incident.

In addition, she was told to pay £85 in costs to the Crown Prosecution Service.

The charge of common assault of an emergency worker against Jones was withdrawn.

The guilty plea was taken into account during sentencing.
