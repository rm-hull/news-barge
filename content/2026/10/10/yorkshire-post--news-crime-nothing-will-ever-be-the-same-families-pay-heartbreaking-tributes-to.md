# 'Nothing will ever be the same': Families pay heartbreaking tributes to two teenagers killed in crash

## The families of two 19-year-old men who died in a crash in Rotherham have paid emotional tributes to their loved ones, describing the devastating impact of their deaths.

The families of Jacob Patching and Jacob Skidmore, both 19, have shared tributes following their deaths in a crash on East Bawtry Road in Rotherham.

At around 6.43pm on Saturday (Sep 26), emergency services were called to reports of a crash involving a white BMW 3 Series.

It is understood the car left the road and struck a lamppost.

Despite the efforts of members of the public, firefighters, paramedics and police officers, both teenagers were pronounced dead at the scene.

Their families continue to receive support from specialist family liaison officers.

![Jacob Skidmore (left) and Jacob Patching](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/10/14/58/MixCollage-10-Oct-2026-03-35-PM-1681.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Paying tribute to Jacob Patching, his family said: “Jacob was a loyal, hardworking, caring and kind young man with a contagious sense of humour who had a love for the outdoors.

“Our whole lives have changed beyond measure in an instant and the pain left behind is immeasurable. Nothing will ever be the same without him.

“We would like to express our gratitude to the emergency services and the wider community affected by this tragedy.”

Jacob Skidmore's family also shared a heartfelt tribute, remembering his love of fixing engines and the close relationships he had with his friends and colleagues.

They said: “We would like to thank all that are supporting us through this time. Jacob was loved by all who knew him, especially his work family at The Paint Factory, his girl-friend and his friends.

“He had a great love for mending anything with an engine as his mates know this well enough. He has left a big hole in our family that will never close. Forever in our hearts. Love Mum, Dad, Brothers and Sister”

Police continue to appeal for anyone with information to come forward.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said: “Our thoughts remain with the families, friends, and wider community affected by the loss of Jacob and Jacob.

“We are continuing to investigate the circumstances surrounding the collision and urge anyone with information or footage to contact us.

“If you have footage it can be shared directly with us here-[https://unitedkingdom1cpp-portal.digital-policing.co.uk/southyorks/appeal/footage-and-witnesses-sought-following-fatal-collision](https://unitedkingdom1cpp-portal.digital-policing.co.uk/southyorks/appeal/footage-and-witnesses-sought-following-fatal-collision)

“Any other information can be shared with us online, via live chat or by calling 101 quoting incident number 817 of 26 September 2026.”
