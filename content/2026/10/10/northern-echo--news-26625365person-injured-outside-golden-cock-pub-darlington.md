Police and paramedics rushed to a Darlington town-centre pub after reports that a person had been injured.

Two police cars and a dog-unit van were seen outside the Golden Cock pub on Tubwell Row shortly after 11am on Saturday, October 10.

![Police and ambulance crews on scene of an incident outside the Golden Cock pub in Darlington on Saturday (October 10).](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21563430.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and ambulance crews on scene of an incident outside the Golden Cock pub in Darlington on Saturday (October 10). *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

The North East Ambulance Service confirmed it had been called to reports of an injured person in the area.

The incident is not thought to be suspicious.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21563426.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and ambulance crews on scene of an incident outside the Golden Cock pub in Darlington on Saturday (October 10). *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

An ambulance service spokesperson said: "We received a call at 10.45am on Saturday 10 October to reports of a person injured outside an address on Tubwell Row in Darlington.

"We dispatched an ambulance crew who treated and discharged one patient on-scene."

The cause of the person's injuries is currently unknown.

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Constabulary for further details.
