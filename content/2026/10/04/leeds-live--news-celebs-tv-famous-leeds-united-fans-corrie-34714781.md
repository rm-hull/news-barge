# Famous Leeds United fans and Corrie stars spotted at Elland Road following invite from 'new best friend' James Trafford

## They have told fans about their Elland Road experience

Three Coronation Street legends have enjoyed a big day out at Elland Road.

Famous Leeds United supporters Colson Smith and Jack P. Shepherd, who are known from their roles as Craig Tinker and David Platt in the ITV soap, were joined by Ben Price - who plays Nick Tilsley. The trio host the On The Sofa podcast.

Colson, who left Corrie in 2025, posted a photo of the three at Elland Road ahead of Leeds United’s recent Premier League match against Newcastle United. The caption read: “It’s a boys trip out to the football.”

Colson and Jack being two of Leeds United’s famous supporters. While, Ben is a Newcastle United fan.

They talked about their trip on their podcast where they were guests of Leeds United goalkeeper James Trafford who Colson described as their ‘new best friend’. Ben said: “They’re nice fans and it is a nice atmosphere there.”

Colson added: “We had a little day out together - a social outing.”
