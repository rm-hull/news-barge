Drivers face temporary restrictions on the A66 in North Yorkshire, according to a public notice.

National Highways will close an eastbound lay-by on the A66 near Sedbury from October 26 to October 29.

The lay-by, about 440 metres east of Moor Road Junction, will close for kerb replacement works from 8pm to 6pm each day.

Only emergency vehicles, works vehicles, winter maintenance vehicles and traffic officer vehicles will be exempt.

Traffic signs will warn motorists about the closure.

The works are expected to last four days, but the order will remain valid for up to 18 months if there are delays.

For more information, contact National Highways on 0300 123 5000 or at [info@nationalhighways.co.uk.](mailto:info@nationalhighways.co.uk.)
