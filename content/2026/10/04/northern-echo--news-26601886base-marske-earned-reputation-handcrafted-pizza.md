Just a short stroll from the North East seafront, a family-run restaurant has earned a reputation for its handcrafted pizzas and laid-back coastal setting.

Base Pizzeria & Bar has served pizza and cocktails on Marske High Street since 2022 - quickly becoming a favourite on the village food scene.

Base is a contemporary-style pizzeria and bar, offering a menu built around in-house, handcrafted pizzas.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21532368.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Handcrafted pizza at Base in Marske *(Image: Base Pizzeria & Bar)*

The restaurant combines a modern restaurant atmosphere with a bar serving local craft beers, wines and cocktails, as well as an outdoor terrace for additional seating during the warmer months.

What sets Base apart is its commitment to making everything on the menu from scratch.

The restaurant's regularly changing specials reflect the broad culinary experience of its two head chefs, who have worked across a variety of international cuisines.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21530114.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Base Pizza. *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

The menu evolves throughout the year, with new Pizza specials introduced every fortnight and expertly paired with complementary cocktails.

The restaurant also runs a Lunch Club menu from 12pm to 4pm, Friday to Sunday, offering a 'bottomless brunch beating' range of smaller pizzas, sandwiches and sides, perfect for a light lunch.

Since opening, Base has become a staple on Marske’s food and drink scene, helping the village join neighbouring Redcar and Saltburn as a popular stop for foodies on the North Yorkshire coast.

Base is open Wednesday to Sunday, serving food throughout the afternoon and evening.

Located in the heart of Marske, the restaurant offers a contemporary take on the traditional pizzeria, combining fresh ingredients and regularly changing menus with the added bonus of being just moments from the coast - making a post-meal stroll along the beach hard to resist.
