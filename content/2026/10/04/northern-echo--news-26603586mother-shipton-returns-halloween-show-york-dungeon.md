Visitors can discover what the future holds this Halloween as legendary Yorkshire soothsayer Mother Shipton returns to a North Yorkshire attraction.

Mother Shipton will take centre stage in the Halloween show at The York Dungeon from October 10 to November 1.

Visitors will step back in time to explore superstition, fear and fate in Mother Shipton’s eerie hideout.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21532557.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mother Shipton returns to The York Dungeon, with a chilling Halloween show exploring prophecy, superstition and fate *(Image: Nick Mailer)*

A spokesman said: "Step back to the 16th century and watch as the line between prophecy and reality blurs, as Mother Shipton reveals her next fate at the York Dungeon.

"In the hands of this prophetess, there is no escaping what the future holds."

The show features chilling tales and flickering fires.

A spokesman said: "The York Dungeon is a uniquely thrilling attraction that will whisk you away to the city's most perilous past.

"See, hear, feel and (ahem) smell the chillingly amusing characters of the ‘bad old days’ as they come to life before you."

Tickets for the York Dungeon start at £19 and can be booked online.
