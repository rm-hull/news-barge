N-Dubz are set to return to the stage with an arena tour that will see them perform in the North East.

The London trio, known for hits like I Need You and Playing With Fire, will play at the Utilita Arena on February 27, 2027, as part of their newly announced Legacy tour.

The announcement was teased on social media before being confirmed in the early hours of Friday.

Their return follows a sold-out comeback tour in 2022, which saw Tulisa, Dappy, and Fazer perform together for the first time in a decade, filling venues across the UK including two packed nights in Newcastle.

The 2027 tour will begin in Glasgow on February 25, before heading to Newcastle, Sheffield, Nottingham, London, Cardiff, Manchester, and concluding in Birmingham on March 10.
