Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton has been appointed as Donald Trump’s AI czar, the president announced on Sunday, tasking him with coordinating federal government efforts to preserve American leadership in tech.

Trump, who has long advocated renaming artificial intelligence as "super intelligence," stated that Clayton will head the newly created Super Intelligence Force (SIF), managing government engagement across consumers, religious groups, AI companies, and other stakeholders.

In a post on his Truth Social platform, Trump wrote that the SIF would work to "ensure that America continues to lead the World in Super Intelligence" and to "protect the interests, and improve the lives, of all Americans."

Clayton, appointed intelligence chief in August, said Trump tapped him to direct the administration's push to keep the US at the forefront of AI.

![Jay Clayton appears before the Senate Intelligence Committee during his confirmation hearing on Capitol Hill July 15, 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/29/01/2286236842...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Clayton said the ‌task force would have 120 days to produce a report ‌assessing the risks and opportunities posed by AI and recommending what role the federal government should play.

The safety of AI has become a major issue in recent weeks, with some researchers warning that people building the technology believe it ⁠could kill humans within a decade. Several Democratic and Republican lawmakers have responded with alarm and calls for more action.

Trump has appeared to take a light touch toward AI regulation, arguing that excessive oversight could weaken competitiveness against China. He said on Tuesday that he does not want to work with Chinese President Xi Jinping on governing AI technology.

Clayton's panel will review AI-related risks and current government reporting mechanisms for breaches, hacks and other incidents, and recommend ways to strengthen ‌federal-response capabilities under existing authorities, the Journal said, citing the task force's charter.

This is the ​second time Trump has named an adviser to oversee AI-related decisions across the ‌administration. Venture capitalist David Sacks held the AI ⁠czar role at the beginning of Trump's second term.

In addition to Clayton, the ⁠task force's leaders will include Federal Trade Commission Chairman Andrew Ferguson, Pentagon Chief Technology Officer Emil Michael and Office of Personnel Management ‌Director Scott Kupor, Trump ​wrote.

The panel will report to the president ‌and White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles, ​he said.
