Amanda and Clive Owen are preparing to return to television together as Our Farm Next Door comes back for a fourth series.

The former couple first became known to millions through Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed their lives at Ravenseat with their nine children.

They announced their separation in 2022 but have continued appearing together in Our Farm Next Door, which focuses on their family, farm and restoration of Anty John's.

The new series will once again show them working alongside their children.

Wise Owl Films, the production company behind the show, said Clive and the Owen children will "give a helping hand every step of the way" as the Anty John's restoration moves forward.

The farmhouse is now entering its third year of renovation.

Its plastered walls have finally dried, which means floorboards can arrive, painting can start, and furniture can be moved into individual rooms.

The family will also tackle one of its biggest practical challenges yet, bringing piped water across the moors.

But the programme will not focus solely on Anty John's.

Wise Owl Films said life at Ravenseat "carries on as usual".

Amanda, Clive and their children will be seen celebrating Christmas, caring for livestock and moving through another Easter lambing season.

The children's changing lives will also feature prominently.

Nancy continues to dream of becoming both a ballerina and a farmer.

Edith is preparing for key exams and taking on the cream tea van.

Sid will face his tractor test.
