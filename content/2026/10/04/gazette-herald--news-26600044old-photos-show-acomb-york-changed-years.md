These old photos show how Acomb in York has changed over the years.

We've had a search through The Press archives to give a glimpse of life in this part of the city from the turn of the 20th century.

The images capture various aspects of community life, from a street party on Danesfort Avenue celebrating Queen Elizabeth II's coronation in 1952 to the opening day of the White Rose Pub on Cornlands Road in June 1957.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527236.jpg?type=mds-article-620)3 June 1957 - The White Rose pub in Cornlands Road, Acomb, on its opening day.

Other highlights include the thriving Methodist Church in Front Street is about 1900, which became Ebor Carpet Warehouse in 1978, and the new Acomb Working Men's Club, pictured in November 1970.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527240.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Circus Americano at Acomb Green in June 1973.

The archive also features the Circus Americano at Acomb Green in June 1973 - does anyone remember that? What a sight to behold that was.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527241.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new Acomb Working Men's Club in November 1970. *(Image: The Press)*

There's also the former Acomb Co-op building, pictured in June 1973, and the Spar in Beckfield Lane in December 1977, during the era of half-pennies.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527237.jpg?type=mds-article-620)21 June 1973: The former Acomb Co-op building.

The collection paints a vivid picture of the area's rich history and evolution over the decades, offering a nostalgic glimpse into the past.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527235.jpg?type=mds-article-620)1 December 1977: Spar in Beckfield Lane, Acomb, still in the days of half-pennies.
