Out-of-date vegan cheese and hummus were found at a grocers which received a one-star food hygiene rating.

Haworth Wholefoods, on Main Street, Keighley, was inspected on January 13 by Environmental Health Officers.

## Out of date cheese on sale

Inspectors found vegan cheese with a use-by date of November 20, 2025, for sale.

Out-of-date hummus was also found in the storeroom fridge, with use-by dates of November 20, 2025, and December 26, 2025.

Owners were told to discard the items as soon as possible.

## Walls and ceilings covered in mould

The report found the building structure needed attention, with mould in several rooms.

Inspectors instructed the business: "Refurbish the toilet room: mouldy, perished wall and ceiling surfaces."

They also said the wash-up room was damp and required attention.

Owners were told to "adequately pest-proof the former window areas to the storeroom."

## No food safety system for third year running

The business had failed to provide a written food safety system for the third visit in a row.

The report also noted gaps in food hygiene knowledge.

Inspectors said: "Food handlers should be supervised instructed and or trained in food hygiene."

They also said: "Provide allergen information for customers when requested for "loose" foods such as bread and cakes."

The business was instructed to dispose of waste legally.

Inspectors said: "Legally dispose of waste – obtain a business waste contract."

**The T&A reached out to Haworth Wholefoods for comment.**
