FIREFIGHTERS were called out to a fire set by young people close to a York retail park.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service say that one of their York crews were called out to Monks Cross Drive in Huntington at 10.59am today (October 4) after reports of ‘youths setting a fire in a field’.
