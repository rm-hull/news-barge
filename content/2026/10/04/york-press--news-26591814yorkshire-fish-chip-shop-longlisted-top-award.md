An “excellent” Yorkshire fish and chip shop has been longlisted for a prestigious award.

The National Fish & Chip Awards has released its longlist for the Takeaway Of The Year category.

The list features many coastal spots as well as fish and chip shops found in bigger cities.

The Fish Bank in Sherburn-in-Elmet made it onto the list and is one of the many amazing fish and chip shops that could win the award.

## What is the Fish Bank in Sherburn-in-Elmet like?

The Fish Bank can be found in a former bank in the town of Sherburn-in-Elmet and “proudly serves quality, sustainable and locally sourced fish and chips”.

All of the fish and chip shop's food is cooked fresh to order and offers “specially battered fish, fluffy chips and a range of specials that set the Sherburn in Elmet fish and chip shop apart from the rest”.

The fish and chip shop also works hard to minimise its environmental impact and promotes sustainability.

The Fish Bank offers traditional fish and chips alongside other familiar favourites including king prawns, scampi, and sausages.

You can also get lots of sides to go alongside your meal from curry sauce and gravy to peas and breadcakes.

The Fish Bank is no stranger to being recognised as the food spot has been awarded the Fry’s Magazine Top 10 Best Fish and Chip Takeaways Award in 2023.
