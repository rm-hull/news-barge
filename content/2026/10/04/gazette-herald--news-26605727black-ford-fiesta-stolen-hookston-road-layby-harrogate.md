A CAR has been stolen from a layby in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a black Ford Fiesta was stolen from a layby in Hookston Road, Harrogate.

The vehicle, registration LG09 XSM, was taken between 1.50pm and 3.37pm on September 18.

Officers are investigating the circumstances of the incident and are keen to speak with anyone who may have witnessed the theft or has information that could assist their enquiries.

A spokesperson for the force said: "We are particularly interested in hearing from anyone who has dashcam recordings or CCTV from the area at the relevant time."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing the officer in charge at [Keilee.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Keilee.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk), using the website, calling 101 or you can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260188868.
