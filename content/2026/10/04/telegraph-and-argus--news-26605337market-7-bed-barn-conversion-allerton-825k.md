A seven-bed barn conversion with countryside views is up for sale in Bradford.

The property, located on Haworth Road in Allerton, is on the market for £825,000.

This superb stone-built barn conversion offers spacious family accommodation, along with a self-contained barn for dependent relatives or guests - not to mention the breath-taking views.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535288.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A room with a view *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

Situated in a semi-rural location, the property provides ample parking, extensive gardens, and countryside views.

The main house retains original features including exposed stonework.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535287.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the barn conversion *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

In the house itself, there is a kitchen/diner, dining room, living room, WC, lounge, bathroom, four bedrooms, an en-suite, a walk-in closet, and two cellars.

In the barn, which is considered a highlight of the property, there is a living room, kitchen/diner, shower room, hall, and three bedrooms, one of which has an en-suite.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535289.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nice kitchen and dining area *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

The set up is ideal for elderly relatives, dependent family members, guests, or those seeking a degree of independent living.

Outside, there are extensive lawned gardens and parking space.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535290.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the many bedrooms *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

## Rural charm and modern convenience

The estate agent calls it the “perfect balance of rural charm and modern convenience”.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535294.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bird's eye view really captures the scale of the land available *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

While the owner can benefit from the rustic feel of the property and surrounding area, they would not be, by any means, isolated.

The Allerton barn is close to a number of schools and transport links.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535291.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Imagine owning all this land! *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

Sandy Lane Primary School, which caters to children aged two to 11, is just 0.2 miles away.

Belle Vue Girls' Academy, for students aged 11 to 18, and Beckfoot Upper Heaton, for students aged 11 to 16, are both 0.6 miles away.

Beckfoot Heaton Primary, for children aged two to 11, is 0.7 miles away.

![Allerton barn conversion](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535292.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Here's another perspective *(Image: Holroyds via Zoopla)*

The property is also close to several transport links.

Saltaire station is 2.1 miles away, Bingley and Frizinghall stations are 2.3 miles away, and Shipley station is 2.4 miles away.
