A country park is a great place to spend some time outdoors and enjoy the fresh air with your family.

Northumberland is lucky to have so much beautiful outdoor space to enjoy, including country parks.

Bolam Lake Country Park has been described as a "lovely" place for walks with a "wonderful" café to enjoy.

## What is Bolam Lake Country Park like?

Bolam Lake Country Park is "surrounded by historic landscapes and dramatic views" with woodland and grassy areas to explore.

Visitors can take a stroll around the lake or delve deeper into the woods.

While walking around the country park, you can look out for lots of wildlife such as roe deer, red squirrels, and nuthatches.

If you love birdwatching, there is the Bolam Bird Hide where you can sit and watch the birds around the lake.

After enjoying a walk around the lake and woodland, you can visit the Bolam Lake visitor centre and café.

The café sells soups, sandwiches, and homemade cakes and scones if you fancy something sweet.

The visitor centre also has a range of handmade gifts, books, and maps to have a look at.
