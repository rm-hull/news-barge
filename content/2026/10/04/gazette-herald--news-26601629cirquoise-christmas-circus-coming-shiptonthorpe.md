A Christmas circus show is set to bring festive magic to the big top.

Cirquoise will return to Langlands Garden Centre in Shiptonthorpe with its all-new Christmas Circus Spectacular, running from December 17, 2026 to January 3, 2027.

The family-run production, now in its tenth generation, promises a "magical blend of breathtaking acrobatics, comedy, dance, and classic circus artistry" in a heated big top transformed into a winter wonderland.

The Cirquoise production team said: "Christmas at Cirquoise is all about bringing families together for an hour of pure joy.

"Every year we push ourselves to create something new, even more magical, more exciting, and more memorable – but still completely affordable for every family."

This year’s show features a new festive storyline, dazzling aerial performances, family-friendly comedy, and immersive stage effects.

The cast includes gravity-defying aerialists, human foot jugglers, Guinness World Record-holding acrobats, and classic clowns.

The show is accessible, wheelchair-friendly, and enjoyable for all, whether people come as a family or as adults wanting a "magical escape".

Food and drink will be available inside the big top, including popcorn, candyfloss, doughnuts, mulled wine, and hot food.

Tickets can be purchased through the Cirquoise website.
