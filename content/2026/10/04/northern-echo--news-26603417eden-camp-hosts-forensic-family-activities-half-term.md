Young detectives can take on forensic challenges and Halloween fun at a North Yorkshire museum this October half-term.

Eden Camp, near Malton, is hosting hands-on, spy-themed family activities from October 24 to November 1.

Children can uncover clues, solve mysteries and try codebreaking and crime scene investigation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21532237.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Eden Camp near Malton will host spy-themed family fun, CSI workshops, fingerprint detectives and a Halloween trail during October half-term *(Image: Supplied)*

A spokesperson for Eden Camp said: "Your mission, should you choose to accept it… starts at Eden Camp.

"Whether you’re a WW2 history buff or just looking for a unique family day out, there’s something for everyone."

The museum’s CSI-themed drop-in sessions run on October 24 and 25.

The free, 50-minute workshops must be booked in advance and teach children how to examine clues, analyse evidence and follow a forensic investigation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21532238.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Eden Camp near Malton will host spy-themed family fun, CSI workshops, fingerprint detectives and a Halloween trail during October half-term *(Image: Supplied)*

From October 26 to 30, Fingerprint Detectives will show children how investigators collect and compare fingerprints and what makes each one unique.

A Halloween-themed I-spy Trail will run throughout the week, sending families around the museum grounds to find hidden clues and spooky surprises.

All activities are included in standard museum admission.

Collect a trail sheet on arrival and win a prize from the shop when you complete it.

Book online in advance for discounted tickets.

Adult admission for those aged 16 and over costs £17 online or £20 on the day. Children’s tickets for ages five to 16 cost £15 online or £18 on the day.

Concession and family tickets are available too. For details and tickets, visit edencamp.co.uk.
