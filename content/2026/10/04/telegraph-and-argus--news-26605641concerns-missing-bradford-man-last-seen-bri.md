Police are appealing for information to help find a missing 60-year-old man last seen at Bradford Royal Infirmary.

West Yorkshire Police have confirmed the missing man is called Paul, but as is often the case with missing persons appeals, have not provided a surname.

Paul, who is known to the Manningham area, was last seen at the hospital.

He is described as having short, shaved hair and skinny and was last seen wearing a blue jacket with grey sleeves and grey shorts with a white trim.

![Missing person Paul](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21535794.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Missing person Paul *(Image: WYP)*

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Concerns for missing 60 year old Paul, last seen at Bradford Royal Infirmary.

"He is known to frequent the Manningham area of Bradford.

"He is described as having short shaved hair and skinny and he was last seen wearing a blue jacket with grey sleeves and grey shorts with a white trim.

"Any sightings log 1936 03-10 refers."
