Aldi's sell-out wooden toys are back and they're now featuring new additions.

Aldi is bringing back its popular range of wooden toys, with new items including a mini kebab shop, a drum kit, and a vanity unit.

The toys, which start from £3.99, will hit the shelves on October 8.

The collection, which includes more than 50 wooden toys, is available nationwide.

## What wooden toys are Aldi selling?

Among the new toys are the Wooden 2-in-1 Market Stall and the Cinema Counter & Kebab Takeaway Food Cart, both priced at £29.99.

These toys come with a selection of wooden food crates and platters, which cost £8.99, featuring a variety of items like meat, cheese, pastries, and seafood.

Aldi is also introducing a new stackable wooden dishwasher, priced at £17.99, aimed at encouraging children to help keep their play kitchens tidy.

For aspiring vets or doctors, the new wooden medical and vet tables, each priced at £19.99, come with 21 pieces, including a table, a medical or vet bag, and accessories.

Little ones can also enjoy the new wooden bike or rocker for £24.99, available in Vespa or trike models, in blue or yellow.

Future store managers can play with the wooden Aldi supermarket set, priced at £29.99, where they can stack shelves, scan items, and run their own middle aisle.

Musically inclined toddlers can enjoy the wooden piano or drum kit, each priced at £29.99, aimed at encouraging early rhythm and music sessions.

The wooden activity board and cubes are available for £9.99, offering a variety of play options for budding artists.

Dress-up fans can use the wooden vanity table, priced at £34.99, which comes with a mirror and a matching stool.

For train lovers, the wooden railway set, priced at £14.99, provides hours of imaginative play.

## Aldi introduces strict customer limit for wooden toys

Due to the anticipated high demand, a strict purchase limit of two per product per person will apply to certain lines.

Aldi's wooden toys range, part of the Specialbuys collection, is popular and often sells out quickly.

The range is available while stocks last, with the store reminding customers that once they're gone, they're gone.

The toys are available in Aldi stores nationwide, providing plenty of choice for parents to treat their little ones without breaking the bank.
