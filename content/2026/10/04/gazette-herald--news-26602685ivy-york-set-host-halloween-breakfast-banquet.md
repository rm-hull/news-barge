A restaurant chain in York is set to offer a 'Little Monsters Breakfast Banquet' for Halloween.

The Ivy, in St Helen's Square, will host the event on Saturday, October 31, and Sunday, November 1.

Priced at £32.50 for adults and £27.50 for children, the breakfast includes seasonal treats and surprises, with fancy dress encouraged.

Each child will also receive a Halloween toy and treats to take home.

In addition, the chain is introducing two limited-edition 'Wicked Eye' creations for the spooky season.

The Wicked Eye dessert, costing £13.95, includes chocolate ice cream and dark chocolate mousse, honeycomb, pink angel hair, and milk foam.

The Wicked Eye cocktail, priced at £12.95, mixes Ketel One Vodka, Beesou honey aperitif, lemon, white chocolate, and raspberry.

Both will be available from Monday, October 26, to Sunday, November 1.

The Halloween offerings will be available at participating restaurants.
