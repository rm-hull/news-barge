An Antiques Roadshow expert is set to take centre stage in County Durham next week.

Mark Smith, a specialist in medals and militaria, will appear at Bishop Auckland Town Hall on Friday, October 9, for an evening titled For Valour: The Story of the Victoria Cross.

The event will explore the history of Britain’s highest military honour, dispel common myths, and share inspiring stories of bravery.

Lizzie Glazier, culture venues and programme manager at Durham County Council, said: "We are very excited to welcome Mark Smith to Bishop Auckland Town Hall.

"As one of the leading authorities on medals and militaria, Mark’s presentation will be a fascinating evening for collectors, history lovers and Antiques Roadshow fans alike.

"This event offers a rare chance to experience Mark’s expertise and storytelling live on stage."

"There will also be the chance for some audience members to get the real Antiques Roadshow experience and have their items appraised by Mark first-hand."

A limited number of VIP tickets are available, including a meet and greet with Mr Smith and the opportunity to bring medals or militaria for a personal description and valuation.

The event begins at 7.30pm.
