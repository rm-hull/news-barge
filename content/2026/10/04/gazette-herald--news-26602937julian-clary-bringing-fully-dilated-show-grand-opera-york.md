A comedy legend is coming to York as part of a brand-new UK tour.

Julian Clary's Fully Dilated will be showing at the Grand Opera House in York on Friday, May 28, 2027.

The show will run in its entirety from April 7 to June 5, 2027, and follows a successful 10-show stint at the 2026 Edinburgh Festival Fringe.

Mr Clary said: "I’m going to be talking about things on this tour that would get me flogged in Dubai.

"And pointed at in Peckham."

The comedian promises to reveal "intimate secrets" and the "traumas" that shaped his life and career.

Tickets go on general sale at 10am on Thursday, October 1, with a Ticketmaster and venue presale from 10am on Wednesday, September 30.

Mr Clary began his career on the cabaret circuit as The Joan Collins Fan Club and rose to fame in the late 1980s.

His television credits include Sticky Moments, Terry and Julian, Celebrity Big Brother, Have I Got News For You, and Celebrity Gogglebox.

On stage, he has appeared in productions such as Taboo, Cabaret, Le Grand Mort, The Dresser, and Bleak Expectations.

He also played Herod in the 2023–2024 touring production of Jesus Christ Superstar.
