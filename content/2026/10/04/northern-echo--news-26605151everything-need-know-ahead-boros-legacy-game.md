Middlesbrough is set to welcome back a host of football legends as a sold-out crowd prepares to celebrate 150 years of the football club at the Riverside Stadium.

The Legacy Game takes place on Sunday as part of the club's 2026/27 season celebrations, marking a remarkable story which stretches back to 1876.

Former players and managers from across the generations will return to the Riverside for the special fixture, with a packed schedule of activities planned before, during and after the game.

**Here is everything you need to know ahead of the special game today.**

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21534941.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Middlesbrough FC's new badge for this season to commemorate 150 years of the club *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The match will be played over two 45-minute halves, with the former players split into Erimus in red and Ironopolis in blue.

Kick-off is at 4pm, but supporters are being strongly urged to be in their seats by 3.30pm to take in the pre-match celebrations.

Team arrivals are expected outside the West Stand at around 1pm, with the stadium opening for general admission at 2.30pm.

Warm-ups begin at 3pm before the Legacy Walk at 3.40pm, when players will be introduced to the crowd.

At 3.59pm, there will be a minute of recognition as part of Boro Remembers for those who are no longer with us.

The game will then get under way at 4pm with Pigbag.

Half-time is scheduled for 4.45pm, when non-playing former players will be introduced to supporters before receiving their Legacy Number certificates.

The second half begins at 5.15pm, with full-time expected at around 6pm.

Playing former players will then receive their Legacy Number certificates before all former players take part in a lap of appreciation.

Among the confirmed names returning to the Riverside are Dimi Konstantopoulos, Franck Queudrue, George Friend, David Wheater, Colin Cooper, Daniel Ayala, Joe Bennett, Emilio Nsue, Craig Hignett, Christian Karembeu, Geremi, Lee Tomlin, Mikkel Beck, Brian Deane, Yakubu, Tuncay Sanli, Fabrizio Ravanelli, Aitor Karanka, Juninho, Jonny Howson and Gary Pallister.

Also confirmed are Tony Mowbray, Bernie Slaven, Barry Robson, Dean Windass, Julio Arca, Dael Fry, Mido, Robbie Mustoe, Joseph-Desire Job, Albert Adomah, Hayden Hackney and many more.

The club says some of those attending will feature in a non-playing capacity.

Tickets for the game have sold out.

Supporters are reminded that seasonal car park passes are not valid for the fixture and fans are being advised to arrive early.

The main Ticket Office opens at 10am, while the North Stand Ticket Office opens at 2pm for collections.

Fans must make sure their ticket is for the correct age category, while all fans under the age of 14 must be accompanied by an adult both inside and outside the stadium.

New turnstile scanners have also been installed at the Riverside ahead of the 2026/27 season.

Physical match tickets have a barcode which can be scanned at the bottom of the turnstile reader, while digital tickets and all Season Tickets use contactless NFC at the top of the reader.

Supporters are advised to arrive early as fans get used to the new process.

For those unable to attend, the Legacy Game will also be available to watch via a live stream, with passes available for £3.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/12253416.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

The FanZone opens at midday and closes at 3.30pm, with supporters encouraged to head into the stadium in time for the Legacy Walk.

Two ice cream vans, six EBar machines, a Upex Pies van, a Shawarma chicken van, a cookie stall and a can bar will be operating.

The FanZone will not reopen after full-time, although many of the food vendors will continue serving.

Gary Philipson is the host, while limited activities will be available in the GRFZ from the turnstiles opening at 2.30pm until 3.30pm.

Roary and River will also be in the Family Zone concourse.

A quiet space will be available for supporters who need to use it, with fans asked to speak to a Happy To Help member of staff for more information.

The club shop will open from 10am until kick-off, with new Castore replica kits, training and leisurewear available.

Legacy Game shirts will also be on sale.

A Legacy Game matchday programme will be on sale around the ground for £3. Card and cash are accepted.

The sold-out occasion promises to bring together generations of Boro history as the club celebrates 150 years at the heart of Teesside.
