This blurry image forms part of an investigation into vehicle interference in York.

It happened on **Royal Avenue, Huntington**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are aware that the image provided is not of the clearest quality.

#### Royal Avenue, York

“This is a third-party image and is not owned or captured by North Yorkshire Police.

“At this time, the image available does not allow us to establish any further identifying details about the person pictured.

“We are therefore asking residents and businesses in the area to review any CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage they may have from around the time of the incident.”

They’re asking anyone who thinks they have a clearer image of this person, or who can help identify them, to get in touch.
