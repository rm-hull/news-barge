A Bradford club has been named the best holiday programme provider in England.

Bradford Phab Club, which runs summer multi-activity sports camps, received the award at the National HAF Awards 2026 in London, celebrating its efforts to provide food, activities, and support for children and young people during school holidays.

The awards, presented by the Department for Education, recognise outstanding organisations, providers, and volunteers across England.

Akbar Khan, chairman of the Bradford Phab Club, said: "This thoroughly deserved recognition celebrates Bradford Phab Club's Summer Multi Activity Sports Camps, delivered every year for children and young people across Bradford.

"But this award is about so much more than a title.

"It belongs to the whole Phab family, especially the amazing volunteers who give their time, energy and commitment to making the holiday programmes such a positive experience for children and families."

Bradford Phab Club was named the national winner alongside other regional winners from across England.
