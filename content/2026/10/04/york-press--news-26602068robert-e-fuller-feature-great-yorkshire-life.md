A North Yorkshire wildlife artist and filmmaker will appear in the latest series of Channel 5's Our Great Yorkshire Life.

The programme, which celebrates Yorkshire's unique people, places, and passions, will visit Robert E Fuller's gallery and studio in Thixendale on Friday, October 9, at 8pm.

Mr Fuller is renowned for documenting the secret lives of Yorkshire's wildlife through his paintings, photography, and films.

He dedicates much of his time to the conservation and rehabilitation of animals.

In the episode, viewers will gain insight into his innovative method of rehabilitating rescued owls, following him as he places young tawny owl chicks into a wild nest to be raised by wild owls.

Our Great Yorkshire Life explores the traditions, landscapes, communities, and passions that make Yorkshire distinctive.

The current series features a variety of Yorkshire characters and stories, including farmers, craftspeople, conservation projects, and rural businesses.
