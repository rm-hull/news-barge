A period home in a “vibrant” area of York has hit the market.

The three-bedroom house is for sale in Thorpe Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £495,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the Victorian property, said it is a “beautifully presented” home “just a short walk from the vibrant amenities of Bishopthorpe Road”.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21532794.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the three-bedroom home for sale in Thorpe Street, off Scarcroft Road in York, for £495,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside, the home is accessed via an entrance vestibule with mosaic tiled flooring and a stained glass and timber door opening into the hallway.

The sitting room features a bay window, wood burning stove and stripped timber flooring.

Another reception room is to the rear of the house with tiled flooring, original built-in storage, shelving and under-stairs storage.

The renovated kitchen, meanwhile, is fitted with integrated appliances and has French doors opening onto the rear courtyard.

Upstairs, two double bedrooms, both with cast iron fireplaces and original storage cupboards, are on the first floor.

A three-piece bathroom is also located on the first floor.

The third bedroom, with an en-suite shower room, is on the second floor.

Outside, the property has a walled forecourt and a rear courtyard garden.

For more information about the property, visit its listing on Zoopla.
