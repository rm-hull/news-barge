Police are searching for a man who has gone missing from his York home.

Benjamin Robinson, who’s 41, is from Copmanthorpe.

He’s described as

* white
* about 6ft 1in tall
* of very slim build
* with light brown receding hair that is often shaved
* and light stubble.

He tends to wear sunglasses.

Police say he has links to Derbyshire and South Yorkshire.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are very concerned for Benjamin’s welfare, so if you see him, please dial 999.

“If you have any other information that could help us find him, call North Yorkshire Police on 101.”
