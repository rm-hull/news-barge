Youths started a fire in York this morning (Sunday).

Firefighters went to the incident near **Monks Cross Drive, Huntington**, at** 10.59am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to reports of youths setting a fire in a field.

#### Monks Cross Drive, Huntington

“This was a fire measuring 1m by 1m, consisting of branches and bushes. The fire was out on arrival. Crews damped down using buckets of water.”
