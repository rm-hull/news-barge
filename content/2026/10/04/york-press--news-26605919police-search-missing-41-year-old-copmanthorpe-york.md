AN URGENT search has been launched for a man missing from a York village.

North Yorkshire Police says it is ‘very concerned’ for 41-year-old Benjamin Robinson, who is currently missing from Copmanthorpe in York.

He also has links to Derbyshire and South Yorkshire.

A spokesperson for the force said: “He is 41, and described as a white man, about 6ft 1in tall, of very slim build, with light brown receding hair that is often shaved. He has light stubble and tends to wear sunglasses.”

They added that officers are ‘very concerned for Benjamin's welfare’.

If you see him, please dial 999, and if you have any other information that could help the police to find him, call North Yorkshire Police on 101.
