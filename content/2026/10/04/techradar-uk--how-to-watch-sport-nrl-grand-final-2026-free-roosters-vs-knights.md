The 2026 NRL Grand Final is serving up a David vs Goliath clash between the Roosters and the Knights at Accor Stadium. Whereas Sydney have reached eight previous Grand Finals and won four of them, including two in the past decade, for the past 15 years Newcastle fans have been more concerned with survival than the Provan-Summons Trophy. The Knights were wooden-spooners for the fifth time last season.

Newcastle's one and only previous Grand Final appearance came in 2001, an upset win over the Eels. Produce the goods again on Sunday and they'll go down in NRL lore – the last team to go from last to first were the 1934 Western Suburbs. You wouldn't rule anything out. Justin Holbrook's men finished seventh in the ladder but have taken down minor premiers Penrith and second-place finishers New Zealand en route to the Grand Final. They were trailing the Panthers and down to 12 men last weekend due to Phoenix Crossland's awful tackle on Nathan Cleary, but pulled off a stunning comeback thanks to the combined excellence of Tyson Frizell, Sandon Smith and Kalyn Ponga.

The Roosters, meanwhile, are very comfortable on this stage, and the NRL Grand Final is effectively a home match for Trent Robinson's men. They flew out of the traps to throw the Dolphins off their game last weekend, and if their forwards, led by Victor Radley, can capture that same intensity here it could be a long day for the Knights. On a personal note, Mark Nawaqanitawase is looking to sign off in style before jetting off to Japan to cross codes again.
