Largely dating from the 16th century, with earlier origins linked to a monastic settlement founded by monks from Old Byland in 1143 and later relocated to nearby Byland Abbey, Caydale Mill not only has remarkable provenance but also enjoys a magical setting within a tranquil dale deep in the heart of the North York Moors National Park.

The incarnation of Caydale Mill that stands today blends a wealth of original features including exposed stonework, deep window sills, inset cupboards, fireplaces, substantial beamed ceilings and heavy oak timbers that reveal evidence of early cruck construction with modern additions ranging from a Meridian surround sound system to heated LED mirrors and smart toilets.

One of many highlights of the main house is an orangery that spans the rear elevation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522306.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Original features and contemporary additions have been combined to create the ultimate in luxury living at Caydale Mill, with its stunning location being the cherry on the top *(Image: Blenkin & Co)*

Designed as the principal kitchen, dining and living space, it is flooded with natural light from lantern rooflights with rain sensors and French doors opening onto the terrace.

Underfloor heating lies beneath limestone flooring.

The handcrafted kitchen features a central island with breakfast bar, integrated Miele appliances, a double butler’s sink, an oil-fired AGA and Quooker taps, with a bespoke reinforced glass floor panel in the adjoining dining area revealing the mill stream beneath – a striking reminder of the building’s heritage.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522308.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Original features and contemporary additions have been combined to create the ultimate in luxury living at Caydale Mill, with its stunning location being the cherry on the top *(Image: Blenkin & Co)*

Two reception rooms lie just off the orangery: the snug featuring a drinks cabinet with wine cooler and an inglenook fireplace with wood-burning stove and illuminated seating nook, and the family room with exposed stone walls, a wood-burning stove and a timber-clad media wall.

Off the family room is a walk-in pantry.

Completing the ground floor accommodation is a boot, dog and laundry room which has French doors opening to a covered seating area beside the stream, and the sitting room defined by magnificent beams etched with historic dates, wall panelling, a substantial stone fireplace and an illuminated window seat.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522303.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Original features and contemporary additions have been combined to create the ultimate in luxury living at Caydale Mill, with its stunning location being the cherry on the top *(Image: Blenkin & Co)*

Upstairs are a library area and reading nook on the landing, framed by reclaimed timbers believed to originate from a sailing ship, plus three bedrooms – the principal suite and two with cast-iron fireplaces and en-suite shower rooms.

The principal suite encompasses a bedroom with a vaulted ceiling, far-reaching views and French doors onto a balcony, a generously proportioned dressing room and a luxurious en-suite bathroom with exposed stonework highlighted by ambient lighting, a freestanding copper bath, a walk-in shower, twin basins and bespoke shelving.

A detached stone-built cottage has been beautifully converted from a traditional outbuilding and provides stylish guest accommodation that comprises a spacious sitting room, well-equipped kitchen and two bedroom suites, plus an integrated garage and patio.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522302.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Original features and contemporary additions have been combined to create the ultimate in luxury living at Caydale Mill, with its stunning location being the cherry on the top *(Image: Blenkin & Co)*

Its accommodation is arranged across the ground floor, with ceilings rising into the vaulted roof space allowing for mezzanine loft areas that provide additional storage or play space.

Caydale Mill sits in 28 acres of gardens and grounds, with the focal point of the gardens being the mill stream that flows through the grounds with a diverted race forming a waterfall.

Gardens lie on both sides of the stream, connected by three bridges and rising to pasture and woodland beyond.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522305.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Original features and contemporary additions have been combined to create the ultimate in luxury living at Caydale Mill, with its stunning location being the cherry on the top *(Image: Blenkin & Co)*

Dry-stone walls, meandering pathways, shaped lawns and wildflower planting create an enclosed garden that blends beautifully with the surrounding natural landscape.

The gardens are also home to an outdoor kitchen skilfully created from a repurposed Nissen hut, with a south-facing patio that incorporates the original mill cog, a broad terrace that wraps around the rear of the house, vegetable beds, a small orchard, a brick-built bothy ideal for use as a summer house, a timber workshop, a greenhouse housing a Black Hamburgh grapevine originally from Hampton Court and a five bay oak-framed garage with a heated insulated concrete floor, motion-sensor lighting and integrated speakers.
