TEENS have reportedly smashed in the window of a takeaway in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for more information after criminal damage at a Chinese takeaway in Scarborough earlier this week.




* **Fire crews called to ongoing incident on North Yorkshire coast**
* **Emergency services called to crash in main York road**

The incident took place at Zen Kitchen Chinese Takeaway in Victoria Road between 11pm and 11.30pm on Wednesday (September 30) and involved two teenage boys reportedly smashed the door window and were seen banging on other premises along the street.

"One was wearing a black top and the other a yellow or reflective top," said a spokesperson for the force,

If you can assist, please contact PC 1141 Kelly at [laura.kelly@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:laura.kelly@northyorkshire.police.uk) and quote reference 12260192575.
