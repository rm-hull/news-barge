POLICE are investigating after a shed was targeted in an East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Police is investigating a reported burglary at a shed on Cliffe Road in Market Weighton earlier this week.

The incident took place between 3.32am and 4.05am on Thursday, October 1, 2026.




* **Car stolen from North Yorkshire layby**
* **Fire crews called to ongoing incident on North Yorkshire coast**

An offender or offenders entered a large, unsecured shed - located away from the main house - and disturbed items inside.

A spokesperson for the force said: "The entrance has caused a sensor to activate and linked to the owners phone, security lights have also illuminated.

"Nothing has been taken and no damage caused, nobody has been seen on this occasion but neighbours bolted gates have also been opened."

If you have any CCTV footage (including video doorbell), saw anyone acting suspiciously in the area around this time or have any information about the offenders, please contact the police on 101.
