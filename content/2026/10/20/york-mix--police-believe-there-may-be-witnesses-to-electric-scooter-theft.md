There’s been a theft of an electric scooter in York.

It happened approximately 9pm on Sunday 18 October, outside the Sainsburys located on Burton Stone Lane in Clifton.

The electric scooter is described as black and turquoise.

Police are particularly appealing for information from anyone who witnessed this theft take place or may have dash camera footage of the incident.
