# Reserve your free tickets for the popular Pavilions of Harrogate Decorative Antiques & Fine Art Fair

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/20/13/24/Teddy-Mirror-(1).jpeg?trim=689,0,689,0&crop=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## The hugely popular Pavilions of Harrogate Decorative Antiques & Fine Art Fair will take place this autumn from November 13th–15th.

This will be the major art and antiques fair of the autumn in the North of England and a highlight for connoisseurs, collectors and interior designers.

With a vast range of high-quality pieces for sale belonging to more than 45 specialist dealers, the fair has something for everyone. Prices range from £20 to £20,000 and the fair brings together many dealers from across the country.

![C Horley figures](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/20/13/44/C-Horley-figures-copy-(2).jpeg?trim=105,0,106,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Highlights include fabulous displays of fine art, decorative pieces, Mid-century Modern, ceramics, glass and sculpture and 18th & 19th century furniture, silver and jewellery. Look out for rare Chinese tea caddies, snuff boxes, early coffee grinders, paintings by Yorkshire artists and much more.

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/20/13/16/Modern-Aesthetic-Wedding-Photo-Collage-Instagram-Post.jpeg?trim=0,0,35,0&width=640&quality=65)


“Our Pavilions of Harrogate Fair always offers exhibitors specialising in all kinds of fine and rare pieces,” explains fair organiser Sue Ede. “Works of art, decorative accessories and fine furniture make this a must for collectors and home furnishers.”

The fair offers something for everyone, whether you’re looking to expand your porcelain collection, find talking-point decorative pieces for the home or garden, or seek rare collectables and works of art from the past 200 years.
