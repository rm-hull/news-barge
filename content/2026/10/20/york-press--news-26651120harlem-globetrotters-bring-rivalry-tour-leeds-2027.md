Gravity-defying dunks, jaw-dropping tricks and one of basketball’s most famous rivalries are heading to a venue near York.

The Original Harlem Globetrotters, a legendary exhibition basketball team, will bring their Rivalry Tour to Leeds in 2027, promising an action-packed mix of basketball skill, comedy and live entertainment

The world-renowned team will showcase their skills at the first direct bank arena on Friday, February 26, 2027.

Known for their basketball prowess and humour, the Globetrotters have entertained fans worldwide for more than a century.

Now a new generation is bringing their latest tour offers a fresh take on their classic style, featuring new challenges and more fan interactions.

The Globetrotters will face off against their long-time competitors, the Washington Generals, alongside a new set of challengers.

The audience can expect the Globetrotters' trademark ball-handling skills, long-range shots, and gravity-defying dunks with the show promising to be more than a basketball game, offering a live entertainment experience for the whole family.

Tickets for The Original Harlem Globetrotters at the first direct bank arena are now available.
