## Her family said she passed away in hospital surrounded by loved ones, just months after she welcomed her first child, daughter Snow.

Australian model and swimwear designer Sahara Ray has died aged 34. Her family said in a statement to US celebrity news website TMZ that she died on Sunday night surrounded by family at the hospital.

Her cause of death has not been made public.

The daughter of Australian surfer Tony Ray, she founded the label Sahara Ray Swim in 2014 and was photographed on holiday with Justin Bieber in 2016.

She gave birth to her daughter, Snow, in January this year.
