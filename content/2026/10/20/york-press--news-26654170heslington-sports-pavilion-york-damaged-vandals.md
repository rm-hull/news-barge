## Vandals wreck York sports clubhouse used by children and community teams

Vandals targeted Heslington Community Sports Centre in the early hours of Saturday, October 18. *(Image: Heslington Cricket Club Facebook)*

VANDALS smashed windows, ripped off shutters and forcibly damaged locks at a York clubhouse used by numerous community sports teams.

Heslington Community Sports Centre was targeted in the early hours of Saturday morning, October 17, to the shock and dismay of club members and supporters.

The clubhouse is used by football teams during winter and by cricket teams throughout the summer, benefitting children up to senior players.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21607928.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Locks on this internal door were forced off at Heslington Community Sports Centre. *(Image: Heslington Cricket Club Facebook)*

North Yorkshire Police issued an appeal following the incident which they described as a suspected burglary at a building near The Outgang, on Main Street in Heslington, York.

A spokesperson confirmed that no arrests had been made by Tuesday afternoon.

A fundraising appeal has been launched to help cover the costs of the extensive damage and to help improve security around the clubhouse.

**Recommended reads:**

Rob Stonadge set up the Go Fund Me appeal which swiftly attracted support from more than 60 people, raising £2,250 within days towards its £3,000 target.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21607926.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A Go Fund Me appeal was launched to help towards the repair costs of the damage caused to Heslington Community Sports Centre. *(Image: Heslington Cricket Club Facebook)*

Donations poured in including £100 from Fulford Vikings U14s team.

Rob wrote on the Go Fund Me page: “Funds are being raised to cover repairs necessitated by this completely unnecessary, but sadly becoming more common, attack.

“We will also aim to improve the security measures around the clubhouse in order to deter and prevent future vandalism.

Thank you for your ongoing support.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21607927.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Doors and locks at Heslington Community Sports Centre were deliberately damaged by vandals in the early hours of Saturday, October 18. *(Image: Heslington Cricket Club Facebook)*

Heslington Cricket Club which uses the facilities shared the news on social media, with a rallying plea for support for the fundraising bid.

The club wrote: “Heslington sports pavilion has been the victim of vandalism, causing huge damage to the clubhouse in the early hours of Saturday morning - damage to shutters, windows, plus changing room doors and internal locks were kicked in.

“Luckily nothing of value was taken, but damages will be expensive to repair.

“This damage will impact a large number of people from youngsters taking their first sporting steps, to veterans of their respective sport, plus parents and spectators who use the clubhouse for a drink or shelter during poor weather!

“Please consider helping by donating to our Go Fund Me.”
