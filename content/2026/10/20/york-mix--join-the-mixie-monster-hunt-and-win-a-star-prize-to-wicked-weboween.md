The Mixie Monster Hunt is ON!

This week, Mixie, the YorkMix Monster, is heading out across York and North Yorkshire for five days of Halloween fun!

Armed with trick or treat bags and one very special star prize, Mixie will be popping up at five different locations across the area.

**Here’s the challenge: be the first to find Mixie and claim the star prize!**

Even if you’re not first, don’t worry…Mixie will be staying put with trick-or-treat bags to give away.

We’ll be giving out clues on the radio and across YorkMix socials to help you track Mixie down, so keep listening, keep checking online, and get ready to join the hunt!

The Mixie Monster Hunt with The Web Adventure Park and their **Wicked Web’oween 2026!**

There’s so much going on during WEB’OWEEN, come along for a SPOOK-tacular time! Meet Elphaba and Glinda too! Our resident magic man **Dazzling Darren** will be offering Halloween-themed entertainment for the children to enjoy every day. He will showcase his daily**magic shows**, this time with a Halloween twist!

Whizz around our **unlimited fairground rides**, compete with friends in the** Laser Lightyear Game Zone**, hop around on our two** bouncing pillows**, race down our** giant astra slid**e, meet our** furry friends**during the animal encounter sessions, let off steam inside our award-winning** indoor play**, taste our delicious handmade pizzas, and so much more!

Keep your eyes peeled, York…. the hunt is on!

Follow the daily clues to find Mixie Monster.
