Police are searching for a teenage girl who disappeared from her house in County Durham.

Mikaylea, 16, was reported missing from her home in Seaham, and officers are asking anyone who may have seen her to come forward.

Police describe the teenager as being about 5ft 4in tall, with shoulder-length brown hair.

Mikaylea was last seen wearing a brown zip-up hoodie, black leggings and black Nike TN trainers.

Police are urging members of the public to get in touch as soon as possible if they have any information that could help locate her.

Anyone who has seen Mikaylea, or who knows where she may be, is asked to contact police on 101, quoting incident number 460 of October 18.
