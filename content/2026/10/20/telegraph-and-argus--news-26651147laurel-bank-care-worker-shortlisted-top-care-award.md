A CARE worker has been named a finalist in this year’s Bradford Cares Excellence Awards.

Emily Roberts, who has worked at Laurel Bank care home in Wilsden for four years, is one of three finalists in the Care Worker of the Year (Care Home) category.

The award recognises front-line care staff who make a positive impact in adult social care.

Bernie Suresparan, CEO of Summit Care Group, said: "We're incredibly proud of Emily.

"Her dedication, kindness and the relationships she builds with residents reflect the difference a great care worker can make to everyday life at Laurel Bank.

"This shortlisting is well-deserved recognition of her contribution and a moment for the whole team to celebrate."

The programme celebrates organisations and their teams who consistently maintain high-quality support, ensuring residents can live their lives with dignity across the Bradford district.

Winners will be announced at a ceremony on Thursday, November 5.

Ms Roberts said: "I feel incredibly honoured to have been shortlisted for this award.

"For me, the most rewarding part of the role is building relationships with our residents and making a positive difference to their day."

Laurel Bank was purpose-built in 2007 and supports up to 63 elderly residents, including those with dementia and palliative care needs.
