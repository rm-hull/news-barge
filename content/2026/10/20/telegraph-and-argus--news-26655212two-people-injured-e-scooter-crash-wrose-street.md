TWO people were injured in a crash involving an e-scooter and a parked car on a residential Bradford street.

The crash happened on Thornacre Road in Wrose just before 3pm on Sunday.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 2.52pm on Sunday afternoon to report a collision between an e-scooter and parked car on Thornacre Road, Wrose.

"Two ambulances, a specialist paramedic (critical care) and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to Leeds General Infirmary and one patient to Bradford Royal Infirmary."

Social media reports suggested a boy was seriously injured in the crash.

The Telegraph & Argus has also contacted West Yorkshire Police for more details.

A separate crash in Wrose Road, involving a motorbike and a car, was reported in October 2021, when one rider died and another was taken to hospital.
