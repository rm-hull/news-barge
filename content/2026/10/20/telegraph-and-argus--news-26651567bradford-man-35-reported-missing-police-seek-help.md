Concerns are growing for a missing man from Bradford city centre.

Robert, 35, was reported missing from the area on Monday, October 19.

He is described as a white male of slim build, around 5ft 5in tall, with short dark brown hair.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "Robert is missing from the Bradford City Centre area and has been reported missing this morning (19/10).

"He is a white male, 35 years old, slim build, 5ft 5 tall. He has dark brown hair cut short and was last seen wearing a dark coloured tracksuit.

"Police are concerned for his welfare.

"Any information or sightings please contact 101 and quote log 561 19/10/2026."
