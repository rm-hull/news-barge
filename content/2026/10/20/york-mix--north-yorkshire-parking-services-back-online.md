The recent issues with online parking services in North Yorkshire have now been resolved.

Once again, it is now possible to access and apply for parking permits, submit Penalty Charge Notice (PCN) appeals, and make Penalty Charge Notice (PCN) payments online.

A council spokesperson said: “Thank you for your patience during this outage.”
