Harrogate food hall Weetons has launched a Christmas hamper dedicated to pickle lovers.

The limited edition Pickle Christmas Hamper costs £95 and brings together eight pickle-themed food products, alongside a festive dinosaur decoration.

Weetons says it believes the hamper is a world first, with the launch tapping into a trend for pickle-inspired food and drink.

Presented in a 16-inch wicker hamper, the selection includes cucumber pickles, pickled onions, pickle seasoning and pickle-flavoured crisps.

Products include Goat Rodeo Goods’ Big Dill Energy Cucumber Pickles, Rodeo Disco Cucumber Pickles, Hell or High Water Hot Dog Pickles, The Pickle Tickle Pickle Salt and Sumac Sliced Pickled Onions.

They are joined by Drivers Bread & Butter Pickles, Drivers Dill Pickle Slices and a share bag of Torres Pickle Flavoured Potato Crisps.

The hamper also includes ‘Mr Pickle’, a 17cm green dinosaur wool decoration dressed in a Santa hat and red scarf.

Liam Gill, director at Weetons, said:

> “Pickles have certainly been the snack of the year, from pickle-topped everything on social media to pickle martinis at the bar, so we wanted to create a Christmas gift that is gloriously, unapologetically pickle-packed.
>
> “From Big Dill Energy pickles to Pickle Tickle seasoning, every item has been chosen to celebrate the humble pickle, and we have a feeling Mr Pickle will be the most talked about decoration on the tree this year.
>
> “It's made for the pickle obsessives, the adventurous foodies and those impossible to buy for people on your list. It really is one Christmas gift they'll never see coming. Dare we say it, it's kind of a big dill!”

*The Weetons Pickle Christmas Hamper is priced at £95.00 and is a limited edition item available exclusively at Weetons.*

*It can be ordered online at [https://weetons.co.uk/collections/christmas-hampers/products/the-pickle-christmas-hamper](https://weetons.co.uk/collections/christmas-hampers/products/the-pickle-christmas-hamper) and in store at Weetons, Harrogate, North Yorkshire.*
