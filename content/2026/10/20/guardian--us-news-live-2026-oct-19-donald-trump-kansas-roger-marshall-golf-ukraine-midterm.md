Trump's claim 'a million' Ukrainians have been killed contradicted by evidence

Before we move on from Donald Trump’s latest attempt to blame Ukraine’s president, Volodymyr Zelenskyy, for the death of “a million” Ukrainians since the full-scale Russian invasion in 2022, it is worth pausing for a moment to explain that this number, which Trump has been repeating for two years, is false.

While the exact death toll is not known, a study published in July by the Center for Strategic and International Studies in Washington, estimated that “Ukrainian forces have suffered somewhere between 525,000 and 625,000 casualties (killed, wounded, and missing) and between 125,000 and 150,000 fatalities between February 2022 and June 2026.”

The same study concluded that the Russian military “has suffered 1.4 million battlefield casualties and as many as 450,000 deaths since its February 2022 full-scale invasion”.

Despite the evidence, Trump has been wildly inflating the Ukrainian death toll in public comments for two years. On the campaign trail in October 2024, Trump claimed that there were “millions of dead people” as a result of the war in Ukraine at that time.

Four months later, after he returned to the Oval Office, Trump told a national prayer breakfast in Washington that there were “probably 800,000 Russians; 600,000 – 700,000 Ukrainians, young people, just laying on a battlefield dead”.

Two months after that, when Trump was asked to comment on Russia’s Palm Sunday attack on Ukraine, Trump said “This country would’ve never allowed that war to have started if I were president. That war is a shame. Millions of people are dead”.

Last week, Trump attacked Zelenskyy for resisting the Russian invasion, saying: “You’ve lost 30% of your country and you’ve had over a million people killed.”

In August of this year, the United Nations reported that more civilians were killed or injured in Ukraine in July than in any month since March 2022.

Overall, since 24 February 2022, the UN said it has verified that at least 16,874 civilians killed have been killed in Ukraine, including 820 children, and 51,273 injured, including 3,126 children.”
