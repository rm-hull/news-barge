Unemployment in Scotland has fallen in the last quarter, according to the latest figures.

Data from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) shows the unemployment rate for people aged 16 and over was 4.3% between June to August this year.

This was 0.4% down on the previous quarter and below the UK wide average of 4.7%.

There were 119,000 people aged 16 and over out of work in Scotland in the latest quarter, according to the figures.

The employment rate for people aged 16 to 64 was 74%, which was 0.4% down on the previous quarter.

The figures show 2.631 million people aged 16 and over were in employment in Scotland in the last quarter.
