Windows 98 devotees now have (experimental) support for USB4 and Thunderbolt 3/4. Vibe coded driver also supports Windows Me, XP, Vista, and 7.

![Asus ThunderboltEX 5 expansion card ](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8tVzNz3vwLaepNAqk42XKU-840-80.jpg)

An Asus-branded Thunderbolt 5 expansion card has appeared among the firm's product pages.

![Thunderbolt 5 cable](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qiwKLraJG9pvTKXcGfAtZn-840-80.jpg)

Thunderbolt 5 products are finally materializing.

![Intel announces Thunderbolt Share](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/iDy7iRidaVj5QC4QH3qH8F-840-80.jpg)

Using Thunderbolt Share, people can make the most of their multiple PCs, whether they're at work, watching videos, or gaming.

![Thunderbolt 5 connector on a blue and black background.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/jqf9QS8q5aPP7qfc6i4shS-840-80.jpg)

Intel has corrected a press release that stated that 14th Core desktop processors supported Thunderbolt 5.

![Thunderbolt 5](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/JPZFdMHgPEN2nE6X92gvwJ-840-80.jpg)

Intel announced its Thunderbolt 5 specification, which offers up to 120 Gbps of bandwidth, 3X its predecessor, employs universal 240W charging cables and steps up to PCIe 4.0.

![Plugable Docking Station](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/tigrfcxGuvgvac4DfogJ26-840-80.jpg)

Plugable’s new UD-4VPD dock supports USB 4 and Thunderbolt 4 with the ability to output to two 4K displays at 120Hz.

![Plugable Quad-Monitor Thunderbolt Dock](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/sL8Yg4A2a2zsL7EviamQge-840-80.jpg)

The new TBT4-UDZ can connect your Thunderbolt laptop to up to four different 4K displays.

![Intel Thunderbolt](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/P8vCZuuszuaVXeQVxvFn3M-840-80.png)

Intel is further detailing its next-gen Thunderbolt spec, which will support 80 Gbps transfers and up to 120 Gbps for video.

![Apple uses new USB4 retimer](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/g3kZaimnw2AfwgQSubsc5d-840-80.jpg)

Apple's refreshed M2 MacBook Air uses a custom U09PY3 USB4 retimer, putting Intel silicon in the rear view mirror.
