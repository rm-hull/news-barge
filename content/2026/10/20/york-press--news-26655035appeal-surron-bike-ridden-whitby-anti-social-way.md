Police are appealing for information after an e-bike was ridden in an anti-social manner in a North Yorkshire town.

The incident took place around 3pm on October 15 in the Helredale Road and Rohilla Close area of Whitby.

The red and black Surron bike may have been seen in the area on other days as well.

Officers are asking anyone with information, including video doorbell, dashcam, or CCTV footage, to come forward.

A police spokesperson said: "We’re appealing for information about a Surron bike which was driven in an anti-social manner around the Helredale Road and Rohilla Close area in Whitby.

"It happened on 15 October at around 3pm.

"The red and black Surron bike may have also been seen on other days prior to this incident."

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC Bethany Thompson at [bethany.thompson@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:bethany.thompson@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
