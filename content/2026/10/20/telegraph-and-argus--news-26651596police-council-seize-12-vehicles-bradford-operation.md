Twelve vehicles have been seized during a joint police and council operation.

Officers from Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team and Bradford Council’s Vehicle Enforcement team carried out the operation in the Holmewood and Bierley ward, targeting uninsured, untaxed, unregistered, and abandoned vehicles.

The vehicles seized included cars, flat-bed vans, and others found abandoned on the roadside.

A spokesman for Bradford South NPT said: "As a result of the operation, 12 vehicles were seized across the Holmewood and Tong areas.

"This included a number of cars, flat-bed vans and vehicles that had been abandoned on the roadside.

"These proactive operations help keep our roads safer, tackle vehicle-related crime and improve the local environment for residents.

"A big thank you to our partners at Bradford Council Vehicle Enforcement and Stoneywood Vehicle Recovery for their support and assistance throughout the operation."
