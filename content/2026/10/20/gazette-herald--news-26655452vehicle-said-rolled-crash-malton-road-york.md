A vehicle has reportedly rolled after crashing into a traffic light on a main road in York.

The incident is said to have happened in Malton Road, Huntington this evening (Tuesday, October 20).

The Press arrived at the scene around 10pm and the road was closed by police between the junctions with Elmfield Avenue and New Lane.




A bystander said two fire engines were at the scene, along with three or four police vehicles and paramedics.

One person seen by the bystander was said to have been helped from the vehicle by fire crews before being placed in the care of the ambulance service.

The traffic light was stationed on a small island with direction bollards close to a pedestrian crossing.

Malton Road was re-opened at around 10.15pm.

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and Yorkshire Ambulance Service NHS Trust.
