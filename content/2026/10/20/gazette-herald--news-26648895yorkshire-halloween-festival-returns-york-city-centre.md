York city centre will host the Yorkshire Halloween Festival, with a week of activities.

From October 22 to 30, Parliament Street will come alive with pumpkins, crafts and more, as the festival, hosted by Made in Yorkshire, takes over with something for families to enjoy.

This year's festival is centred around a variety of hands-on activities and workshops, offering visitors the chance to try new things and make memories.

A variety of crafting workshops will be available, ranging from spooky seasonal creations to unique artisan activities.

The festival also caters to families seeking a quieter experience.

The Sensory Tipi offers a peaceful, creative retreat where both adults and children can participate in calming craft sessions.

The Yorkshire Halloween Festival is not just an event, but an experience for all ages.

Whether you're a "little monster, witch, or wizard", the festival promises a fun-filled half-term for all.

With crafts, creativity, and a sprinkle of Halloween magic, the Yorkshire Halloween Festival invites everyone to Parliament Street this October half-term.
