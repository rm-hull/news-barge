Police are appealing for information about a Surron bike which was driven in an anti-social manner in Whitby.

It happened on 15 October around the Helredale Road and Rohilla Close area at around 3pm. The red and black Surron bike may have also been seen on other days prior to this incident.

If you have any information that could assist our investigation (including video doorbell, dashcam or CCTV footage), please contact us:
