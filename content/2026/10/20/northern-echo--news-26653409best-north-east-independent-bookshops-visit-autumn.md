Whether you're searching for a rare first edition, the latest bestseller or a cosy spot to spend a rainy afternoon, the North East has some of the best independent bookshops for every reader to enjoy this autumn.

From cafés and book clubs to antiquarian collections and children’s events, these seven shops each offer a different way to browse, buy and enjoy books.

## Collected, Durham

Collected on Durham's Riverwalk mainly sells books written by women. Visitors can browse its carefully selected collection while enjoying a warm coffee, cake, or even local beers and wines in the café.

Collected runs regular events, book clubs and social clubs, while its website offers subscriptions and book bundles, which can be bought as gifts or used to stock up bookshelves for the winter.

One online reviewer said: “Excellent customer service. Fantastic selection and welcoming space.

"Just a gem of a place for bookworms in Durham.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/19027828.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Collected stocks County Durham beer in their café for visitors who prefer a stronger drink with their books. *(Image: Jane Parker)*

## Book Corner, Saltburn

Book Corner is based on Saltburn's Milton Street and focuses on contemporary fiction for adults and children.

Its non-fiction shelves contain works about folklore, mythology, local interest, history and nature writing.

Bestselling authors are stocked alongside titles from independent publishers and small presses. Locals describe the bookshop as a "little gem" in the seaside town, mentioning helpful staff with indepth knowledge and suggestions.

An online reviewer said: “Local, friendly, excellent choices, great little town too.

"What's not to like?”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21606995.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Book Corner has attracted praise for its support of local authors and varied collection of books. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## Barter Books, Alnwick

Barter Books in Alnwick offers much more than shelves of books. The building includes a café serving food, tea, coffee and biscuits, as well as the Paradise ice cream parlour.

A model railway runs between the book columns in the central room, while poetry lines and three 40-foot murals form part of the setting. Restored station features, seating and more than 40 glass cases of antiquarian books are also included.

A loyal customer wrote: “This is a must for book lovers of all ages.

"You could easily spend half a day browsing the incredible range of titles, even more if you stop for lunch in the café."

Since opening in 1991, Barter Books has stamped its second-hand books in a tradition that has seen copies travel as far as Australia and the United States.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18843087.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Barter Books in Alnwick have seen copies of books they've sold on every corner of the globe. *(Image: Joshua Nichol)*

## Keel Row Books, Whitley Bay

Keel Row Books is on Park View in Whitley Bay town centre, and was founded in 1981.

The locally loved bookshop moved from North Shields to Whitley Bay in 2024, bringing its unique and eclectic selection of books with it.

Keel Row Books is known for rare first editions, signed books, and works written in or about the North East.

Its stock also includes local art and maps of the North East, some dating back to the 18th century.

An online reviewer praised the Whitley Bay business, writing: “Keel Row Books is full of delights.

"Seemingly ancient dusty tomes, spread amongst modern collectibles, first editions, some art and curios like maps and that sort of thing.

"It just sings with the variety of amazing stuff you can find.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21607047.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Keel Row Books has attracted praise for its wide selection of books ranging from historic first editions to modern fiction. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## Books and Bakes, Washington

Books and Bakes, sat inside Washington Galleries, stocks genres like crime thrillers, general and translated fiction, romance and fantasy, with both new and pre-loved books available.

Comfy seats allow for visiting readers to relax with a book before buying, and the café serves hot drinks and sweet treats.

Owner Charlotte King told Spark that the idea for Books and Bakes came from wanting a welcoming place for readers who may not feel comfortable in a traditional café.

Books and Bakes has attracted a variety of lifestyle and North East content creators since it opened, with visitors praising their stock and labelling the shop "adorable."

## Cognito Books, Hexham

Cognito Books is a cosy bookshop that has been in Hexham for over 25 years, found in a cobbled alleyway off St Mary’s Chare.

Staff select titles out of their over 10,000 strong collection each day, and offer recommendations for readers of all ages and interests.

Cognito regularly hosts events, including Where's Wally-style hunts around the town, and encourages customers to take their bookmarks with them while travelling.

One customer told Cognito their bookmark had accompanied them to Prague, Vienna, Bratislava, Sventendre and Budapest.

## 1b Books, Newcastle

Based on Bolingbroke Street in Heaton, 1b Books sells new and second-hand books, including classics and recent fiction.

The bookshop is a favourite among Newcastle's independent businesses for their selection of niche genres and ability to connect with their customer base.

1b's selection includes both classic and more modern works, with a special spotlight on LGBTQ+ fiction, working class memoirs and translated works, which reflect the interests of owners Mandy and Kate.

The Heaton bookshop also runs book clubs such as their baby and toddler book club every Friday, which is always open to new parents.

As autumn settles across the North East, these independent bookshops continue to provide spaces where readers can slow down, discover something new and support local businesses.

From Northumberland to North Yorkshire, each offers its own unique charm for those seeking comfort between the pages of a good book.
