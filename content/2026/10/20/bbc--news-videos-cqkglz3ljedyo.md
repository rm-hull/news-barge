# Shock and disbelief: How the attack on an Australian MP unfolded

The Australian MP Helen Haines has reportedly lost her leg and is in a critical condition after being hit by a car outside her electoral office in Victoria.

BBC's Katy Watson is in Wangaratta visiting the scene of the incident and the church where members of the community have held a vigil for the independent MP.
