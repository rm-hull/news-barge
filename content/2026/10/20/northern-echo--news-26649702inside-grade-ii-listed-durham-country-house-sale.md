A remarkable Grade II-listed country house set within more than four acres of immaculate grounds is on the market in one of County Durham's most sought-after villages.

The Cottage, on the edge of Castle Eden, offers nearly 10,000 sq ft of living space and has remained in the same family ownership for more than 60 years.

The house was once part of the historic Burdon Estate and dates back to the late 1700s, with later additions creating a distinctive home.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601849.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Behind a circular driveway and immaculate gardens, the house blends period character with modern luxury.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601703.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Inside are elegant reception rooms, including a large sitting room, formal dining room and high-specification Mark Wilkinson kitchen overlooking the grounds.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601774.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601752.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601751.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

The house also has a large family room for entertaining, a study, utility and boot room, extensive cellars and an upper-level drawing room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601772.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

There are five double bedrooms, a dressing room, family bathroom and shower room, plus attic rooms with further potential.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601750.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601749.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

The standout feature is a 1,700 sq ft leisure suite with a gym, sauna and steam room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601745.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601743.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21601742.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

The suite could also be converted into space for home working or multigenerational living.
