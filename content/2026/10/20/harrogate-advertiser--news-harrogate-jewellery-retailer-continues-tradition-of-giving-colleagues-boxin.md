# Harrogate jewellery retailer continues tradition of giving colleagues Boxing Day off – and extra week’s salary

## “Harrogate shop to give staff an extra day off to spend time with loved ones as a thank you for their dedication”

A Harrogate jewellers is giving colleagues the gift of time with loved ones this Christmas, announcing it will not open its doors on Boxing Day.

![Harrogate jewellers Beaverbrooks is giving colleagues the gift of time with loved ones this Christmas, announcing it will not open its doors on Boxing Day.  ](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/20/15/43/BeaverbrooksShopHarrStaff.jpeg?trim=120,157,6,160&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The well-known British retailer first opened its doors in Harrogate in April 2025 when it became its sixth store in Yorkshire.

Located on James Street, Beaverbrooks, Harrogate will continue the brand’s 107-year, longstanding tradition of giving colleagues an extra day off to spend time with loved ones, as a thank you for their dedication and hard work throughout the year.

In addition, the retailer will continue its tradition of gifting colleagues an extra week’s salary in December to show its appreciation.

Natalie James, Regional Head of Retail at Beaverbrooks Harrogate, said: “Here in Harrogate, the festive season is always a really special time for us.

“But it’s also one of the busiest periods of the year, alongside launching our new lab grown diamond collection and a handful of new fashion jewellery and watch brands stocked by Beaverbrooks.

“Our team works incredibly hard in the run-up to Christmas to help customers find those perfect gifts.

“The extra day gives everyone the chance to relax, recharge and enjoy some quality time with family and friends.

“Family values are at the heart of Beaverbrooks, and it's fantastic to see that reflected in initiatives like this.

“Our colleagues really value the company’s people-first approach and we’re proud to be part of a business that recognises and rewards its teams.”

Beaverbrooks is an award-winning, family-owned jeweller established in 1919, providing exceptional quality diamonds, jewellery and watches.

The jeweller spans a total of 82 locations across the UK, with 56 Beaverbrooks stores.

Beaverbrooks was recognised as the Number 1 Best Retailer to Work For in the UK at the Best Companies Awards 2025 and placed 22nd in Best Companies to Work For in the UK.

Other honours include Employer of the Year at the UK Jewellery Awards 2026, and Professional Jeweller Awards 2026.
