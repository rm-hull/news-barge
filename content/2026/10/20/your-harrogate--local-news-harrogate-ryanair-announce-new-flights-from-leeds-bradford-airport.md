Ryanair has announced four new routes from Leeds Bradford Airport for summer 2027.

The budget airline will offer flights to Budapest in Hungary, Bari in Italy, Corfu in Greece and Tirana in Albania.

The additions form part of Ryanair’s first release of 29 new routes across its European network for next summer, with flights now available to book.

The destinations offer a mix of city breaks and coastal holidays, from the Hungarian and Albanian capitals to the southern Italian port of Bari and the Greek island of Corfu.

Ryanair’s booking pages currently show flights from Leeds Bradford to Budapest and Corfu on Monday 29th March, with a flight to Tirana listed for Wednesday 31st March.

The airline says further additions to its summer 2027 network will be announced.

*Passengers can check travel dates, availability and fares through the Leeds Bradford Airport**website**.*
