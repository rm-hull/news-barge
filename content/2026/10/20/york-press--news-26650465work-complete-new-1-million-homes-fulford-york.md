Work on a new housing development in York featuring four homes all worth more than £1 million is now complete.

Just one home, the Dragoon, priced at £1.4 million, remains on the market at the Urban Renaissance site off Fulford Road.

The four and five-bedroom detached properties have been built in the grounds of the Grade II-listed Ousefield House.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21602811.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the homes for sale at the Urban Renaissance site off Fulford Road in York

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21602813.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside one of the homes for sale at the Urban Renaissance site off Fulford Road in York

Developer Mulgrave has worked on the scheme with York-based architects Vincent and Partners, Harrogate-based bathroom provider Abacus and Newcastle-based kitchen provider Nixons Project Exclusive.

Debbie Foster, sales director for Mulgrave, said the “luxurious” new homes “demonstrated the wealth of talent in the property industry in the north of England”.

“We are proud to bring construction work at Urban Renaissance to a close,” Debbie said.

“This has been an incredible project, creating modern, energy-efficient new-build homes which are inspired by their historic surroundings and in keeping with their charm.”

Naish’s managing director, Matthew Hendry, previously told The Press there has been a “fantastic” response to the Urban Renaissance properties – despite their high price tags.

"The sales demonstrate that exceptional homes in outstanding locations continue to attract discerning buyers,” he said.

“York remains one of the country's most desirable places to live, and there is clearly strong demand for beautifully designed, high-specification homes that are built to last."
