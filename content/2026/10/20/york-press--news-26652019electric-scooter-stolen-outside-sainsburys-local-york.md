An electric scooter has been stolen from outside a York supermarket.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information about the theft outside the Sainsbury’s Local in Burton Stone Lane, York, at 9pm on Sunday (October 18).

The force said the electric scooter is described as black and turquoise.

“We’re particularly appealing for information from anyone who witnessed this theft take place or may have dash camera footage of the incident,” a police spokesperson said.

Those with information are asked to email [david.ellison@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.ellison@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260205275 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
