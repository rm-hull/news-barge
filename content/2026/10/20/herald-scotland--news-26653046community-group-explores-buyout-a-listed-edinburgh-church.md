**The former Reid Memorial Church in Edinburgh could be purchased from the Church of Scotland in a community buyout.**

A local group has been set up to explore the possibility of buying the Category A-listed building on West Savile Terrace.

The Reid for the Community will hold its first meeting on Sunday October 25 at 4pm in the church’s Large Hall.

“The Church of Scotland is planning to put the Reid on the open market. We want the community to have a say in its future,” the group said on social media.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21606491.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The church opens up into a cloistered courtyard. *(Image: Wikimedia Commons)*

“A new local group, The Reid for the Community, is asking one question: Should the Reid be owned and run by the community?" the group went on to state.

"A building for music, arts, clubs, events for every generation, owned by and run for the people who live here.

“Nothing is decided. That's why we need you. Tell us what this area is missing, ask the hard questions, and help decide the Reid's future before someone else does.”

Reid Memorial Church, which opened in 1928, was commissioned by William Crambe Reid in memory of his father and designed by Leslie Grahame Thomson in the neo-perpendicular style.

The congregation at Reid Memorial united with Marchmont St Giles Church in January 2026, forming the new Blackford and Grange Parish Church.

The MP for Edinburgh South, Ian Murray, has thrown his support behind the group.

“Following the Church of Scotland's decision to sell the Reid Memorial Church, Edinburgh, a group of residents are looking into the possibility of a community buy out,” he said on Facebook.

“Currently the site hosts a number of activities including the local Scouts, community yoga, and mother and toddler sessions. A community buy out would protect these and offer other opportunities for local groups.

“It's a fantastic initiative, well worth attending on Sunday if you are able to.”

The Church of Scotland has been approached for comment.
