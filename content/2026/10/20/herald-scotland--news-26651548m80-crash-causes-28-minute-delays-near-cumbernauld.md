A collision on the M80 has caused significant delays for motorists this morning.

Traffic Scotland said the motorway was partially blocked between Junctions 6 and 6A following a crash near Cumbernauld in North Lanarkshire.

Long tailbacks have built up on the approach, with congestion stretching back towards Junction 4.

Drivers are facing delays of around 28 minutes, and are being urged to allow extra time for their journeys.
