One person has been taken to hospital following a crash in Bradford.

Emergency services were called to reports of a collision on Little Horton Lane at 6.45pm on Monday and the incident involved a single casualty.

Images on social media show a police car and ambulance at the scene.

A private hire car appears to be between both vehicles.

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 6.45pm yesterday (October 19) to report a road traffic collision on Little Horton Lane, Bradford.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
