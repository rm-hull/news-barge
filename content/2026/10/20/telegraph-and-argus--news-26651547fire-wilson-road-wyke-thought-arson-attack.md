A suspected arson attack has been recorded following a fire over the weekend.

The blaze occurred on Wilson Road in Wyke on Sunday, October 18, at around 8.44pm.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) were called to reports of a fire in the open.

One crew attended the incident, which has been recorded as suspected deliberate arson.

A spokesperson for WYFRS said: "We were called to reports of a fire in the open at Wilson Road in Wyke on the 18th at 20.44.

"One crew attended and extinguished it.

"It's been recorded as suspected deliberate arson."
