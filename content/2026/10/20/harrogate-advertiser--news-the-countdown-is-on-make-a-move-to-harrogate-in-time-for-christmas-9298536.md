# The countdown is on: Make a move to Harrogate in time for Christmas

## Harrogate new homes ready to move into before Christmas

House hunters have a final opportunity to secure a new home in Harrogate and be in before Christmas.

![Redrow's Kingsley Manor](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/ugc/2026/10/20/11/Kingsley_Manor_1792405499_43991e4c-70444e2d7dfdc182.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=65)


Redrow has a selection of four and five-bedroom Readymade properties at Kingsley Manor, on Kingsley Road, that can be moved into straight away, meaning buyers could be settled and decorated in time for Christmas 2026.

Steve Jackson, sales director at Redrow Yorkshire, said: “At this time of year, buyers are often looking for a stress-free move.

"A brand new home offers exactly that, with everything ready from the moment they move in. There’s no need to worry about renovations, DIY projects or costly upgrades, allowing homeowners to simply enjoy a warm, energy efficient home and focus on celebrating the festive season.”

Readymade homes are brand new properties, with a high specification interior chosen by Redrow’s interior designers.

The properties have no onward chain and everything in place, so they’re ideal for buyers who want to move quickly and easily.

Among the homes available include the Readymade Henley, which includes over £20,000 worth of extras, and is currently priced from £750,000.

Also available is the Readymade Highgate, which includes over £20,000 worth of extras, and is currently priced from £740,000.

Steve added: “These Readymade properties are part of a selection of our final homes here in Harrogate.

“Our Readymade properties all come with flooring throughout and a removal package and buyers can also choose from either a five per cent Deposit Boost or our Part Exchange scheme.”

Kingsley Manor is set on 20.3 acres of land and once complete will feature a total of 133 properties from the homebuilder’s Arts and Crafts-inspired Heritage Collection.

The development also includes green space, two play areas, cycle and footpaths linking to neighbouring developments, local facilities and into the town centre.
