Police are investigating a report of a public order incident which occurred on Cromer Street, Glencoe Street, Pembroke Street and Shipton Street and Newborough Street, York.

Between 12:41 pm and 1:14pm, on Thursday 8th October 2026, a black male in a white, Peugeot Partner van, registration MP19NPN, harassed a lone female as she walked along the streets.

The female is white, 20 years old, medium build, 5’2” tall, with long blond hair and was wearing a green t-shirt and cream joggers.

A police spokesperson said: “Do you have any information which would assist?

“Did you or anyone who you live with, hear, or see anything around the above time and date?

“Do you have any CCTV coverage capturing the offence, or anything happening in the area before or after?

“Do you have a vehicle with a dash-cam parked in the area?”

If you can assist, please contact Constable 1222 David Fulford at [email protected] quoting reference 12260198568.
