Metrocentre is promising a packed week of family fun this half-term, with gaming, beauty, magic, and circus entertainment.

Running in Gateshead from Friday, October 23, through to Saturday, October 31, the programme aims to brighten up the shorter autumn days with activities for all ages.

Helen Atkinson, marketing manager at Metrocentre, said: "As the days shorten and the nights draw in, Metrocentre offers lots of reasons to get out of the house this Autumn and avoid cabin fever over the half-term holidays.

"From gaming, beauty and circus fun to our free wizardry world event, there's something for all the family, big and small."

Here's what's on offer over half-term:

### **Nintendo Switch 2 experience**

Gamers can head to Exhibition Square from Friday, October 23, to try out the latest Nintendo Switch 2 games, including Mario Kart World, Donkey Kong Bananza, Splatoon Raiders, and Star Fox.

Fans of more relaxed gameplay can also enjoy Pokémon Pokopia and Yoshi and the Mysterious Book.

### **Beauty Carnival**

On Saturday, October 24, and Sunday, October 25, beauty lovers can visit the H beauty Carnival 5.0 in Town Square.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21605402.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There’s the chance to learn pro tips from some of the biggest beauty brands. *(Image: Metrocentre)*

The event offers professional tips from top brands, dry styling, flash facials, charm-making sessions, and exclusive discounts.

Visitors who spend £70 or more can build their own goodie bag with five deluxe samples.

Carnival passes can be booked in advance or on the day, subject to availability.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21605401.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Carnival passes can be booked in advance or on the day, subject to availability. *(Image: Metrocentre)*

### **Circus Spectacle**

Also launching on Saturday, October 24, is Gandey’s K-Pop Dragon Circus.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21605399.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gandey’s K-Pop Dragon Circus is bringing its famous big top to Gateshead. *(Image: Metrocentre)*

The high-energy show features acrobatics, comedy, and music under the big top.

Tickets start from £9.99, with family packages available and free admission for under-twos.

A 20 per cent discount is available on selected seats with the code METRO20.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21605398.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gandey’s K-Pop Dragon Circus is bringing its famous big top to Gateshead. *(Image: Metrocentre)*

### **Wizarding Week**

Starting Monday, October 26, Exhibition Square will transform into a magical space with replica movie props from the world of the famous schoolboy wizard, offering fans the perfect photo opportunity.

### **Halloween Fun**

The magic continues into Halloween weekend with wand-making craft sessions for young wizards and witches on Friday, October 30, and Saturday, October 31.

Costumed characters will roam the centre, and select retailers will offer exclusive discounts.
