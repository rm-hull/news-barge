Police are appealing for information following a suspected burglary in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened in the early hours of Saturday, October 17, when a building near Outgang Lane play area in Main Street, Heslington was damaged.




A force spokesperson said officers are particularly interested in hearing from anyone with CCTV footage from the area and reports of vehicles seen nearby at the time.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [Maximilian.Bothwell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Maximilian.Bothwell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or by calling 101.

Information can also be provided anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260204211.
