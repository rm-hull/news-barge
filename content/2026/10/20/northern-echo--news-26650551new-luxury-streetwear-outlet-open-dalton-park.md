Shoppers can snap up luxury streetwear brands for less when a new outlet opens at Dalton Park later this month.

Badge Outlet will open at the Murton shopping centre on Saturday, October 24.

The retailer, which has stores in Sunderland and Gateshead’s Metrocentre, is promising live DJs, refreshments, giveaways and an extra 10 per cent off sale prices all weekend.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602932.jpg?type=mds-article-620)BADGE in Sunderland. *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

Customers will also get a first look at the brand’s autumn/winter 2026 collection.

Announcing the opening on social media, the company said: “Dalton Park, here we come.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602931.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BADGE)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602930.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dalton Park. *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

“It’s official. Badge Outlet is opening its doors on Saturday, October 24, 2026, and we’re kicking things off properly.

“We’ve got some unreal brands, belta prices and plenty going on for opening weekend.”

Badge Clothing specialises in luxury streetwear and designer fashion, stocking Stone Island, Canada Goose and Burberry alongside menswear, womenswear and childrenswear.

The North East retailer has built a strong following with its stores and online presence, offering premium labels at outlet prices.

The new Dalton Park store is expected to offer shoppers the chance to pick up designer clothing, footwear and accessories at reduced prices while expanding the centre's fashion offering.

The company said: “Whether you're after some fresh gear or just fancy a nosey, get yourselves down and see what we're all about.”
