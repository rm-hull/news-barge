Ripon’s Market Place will host the latest Little Bird Made Artisan Market on Sunday, 25 October, with independent traders selling handmade goods, artwork, food and other products.

The outdoor market will run from 10am to 3pm, bringing together small businesses and craftspeople offering items including ceramics, skincare, bespoke gifts, artisan bakes and photographic art.

The event comes as Little Bird Made celebrates its eighth anniversary. The company has been running artisan markets in Ripon since 2018.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said the markets helped bring communities together and support independent businesses.

> **Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made:** Our markets are about much more than just shopping. They bring communities together and support small, independent businesses.

Alan Clamp, who sells limited-edition photographic art through the markets, said Ripon had consistently been his strongest trading location.

> **Alan Clamp, artist behind AC Landscapes:** Ripon is a special location for me as year on year it has been my top trading Artisan Market offering a fabulous trading location, top quality products and great customer footfall.

The market takes place in Ripon Market Place, within walking distance of the city’s cathedral, canal and independent shops and cafés.

Further information about Little Bird Made’s markets is available at www.littlebirdmade.com.
