Eyes down, a full house of food, games and good times is on the cards as bingo comes to the coast.

STACK Seaburn, Sunderland, is set to be transformed into a “Sunset Strip” on Friday, October 23, for Barmy Bingo - where fun is firmly on the cards.

The event puts a lively twist on traditional bingo, offering a party atmosphere with spot prizes.

Revellers can also take advantage of STACK’s variety of food outlets, with options ranging from Greek street food at Acropolis to Nashville-style fried chicken at Get Strip’d, and burgers and loaded fries at Brack Burger.

For those craving Asian flavours, Bao Down offers a taste of the East, while Zza Pizzeria serves up slices of pizza and Soju and Gogi brings Korean street food to the mix.

Doors open at 7pm and ticket holders are asked to arrive no later than 7.15pm for a prompt 7.30pm start.

Each ticket holder will also get a free drink voucher on arrival.

Tickets cost £6.50 including booking fee.
