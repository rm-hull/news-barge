### A Truly Historical Halloween: October Half-Term at York Castle Museum

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Nut-Crack-Night-at-York-Castle-Museum.-Image-Courtesy-of-History-Riot-1.jpg)

Step into York’s darker past this October Half-Term as York Castle Museum brings Halloween history to life with prison escapes, Victorian autumnal traditions and family storytelling.

Brand new for 2026, visitors can explore the museum’s atmospheric Georgian Prison Cells through an immersive adventure inspired by the real people once held at York Prison.

Armed with the jailer’s keys, uncover clues hidden inside the cells, beneath the flagstones and within the prison walls to piece together stories of daring escapes and secret plots. Two versions of the experience will be available: one for younger children and another for older teens and adults.

Then step forward into Victorian York on the museum’s iconic Kirkgate street and discover the traditions of an All-Hallows’ Eve from more than a century ago.

### Autumn Half Term at Yorkshire Air Museum

### The Concert That Must Not Be Named

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/786069966_1488916096610226_814348125829787287_n.jpg)

Immerse yourself into a spellbinding night of music as the iconic music of Harry Potter and more are transformed in a breathtaking performance on the magnificent Grand Organ of York Minster.

Iconic film scores by John Williams (Hedwig’s Theme and Harry’s Wondrous World), Alexandre Desplat (Statues and Farewell to Dobby), Nicholas Hooper (Ministry of Magic) and Patrick Doyle (Hogwarts’ Hymn), plus arrangements of orchestral music by Gounod, Holst, and Stravinsky, will be heard alongside a ‘thrilling’ classic by Michael Jackson and original pieces of fabulous organ music by J S Bach, Cecilia McDowall, and Louis Vierne.

Gounod’s Funeral March for a Marionette — the theme of Alfred Hitchcock Presents, Bach’s famous and thunderous Toccata in D minor, and Vierne’s phantasmagorical Feux Follets will be just some of the pieces used to display our Grand Organ’s formidable array of orchestral colours and demonstrate why the organ was cinema’s original surround-sound system.

* York Minster, Deangate, York, YO1 7HH
* Sat 31 Oct
* 7:30pm
* More info
