When it comes to Enshrouded base locations, Embervale real estate has no bad options, but I tend to favor scenic waterfront properties over any strategic positioning. The world has plenty of fast travel points, so I don't suffer much from my form-over-function choices, though I do appreciate conveniently placed mines or higher elevations to get a good running jump with the glider.

After hundreds of hours base hopping all over Embervale, my map is packed with markers highlighting my favorite spots, so I might as well share them. Here are some of the best base locations I've found in Enshrouded 1.0.

## Springlands

### Springlands map

![Enshrouded map highlighting base locations in Springlands](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/pjab7kR7m92GgRKQcGsFjN-1200-80.jpg)

### Fort Kelvin

![Enshrouded - Fort Kelvin, a town in the Springlands](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/b3jBrTz3zJyh2arLJ2qbAn-1200-80.jpg)

🏘️ **Pre-built town for renovating**   
Fort Kelvin sits on the Springlands' northern border, near Revelwood and just west of the Pillars of Creation. It's a sprawling abandoned town with a central square, underground tunnels, and even its own pre-built tower. Restoring the whole place is a massive undertaking, but it's a lovely fixer-upper if you're willing to put in the work.

### Pondside ruins

![Enshrouded - Fort Kelvin, a pond and ruins](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/nRnnMzNheX85wQdeov9NDn-1200-80.jpg)

**🌳Empty lot for building**   
This little pond just west of Fort Kelvin is a personal favorite, and I almost always wind up building something here in every world. I love the island in the middle of the water, so I spend a lot of time fixing up the pier and expanding it. Just be careful building near the path. Enemy patrols occasionally pass through, and they've caught me unprepared and killed me once or twice.

### Western waterfront

![Enshrouded - Pond near the shroud in the Springlands](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/jGKhePxUG3tLFemWHwL8Mc-1200-80.jpg)

**🌳Empty lot for building**   
Surprise, surprise—it's another waterside lot for your next build. This one's almost a little too close to the nearby Shroud, but it's too cute to pass up. The uneven terrain may put some players off, but I'd leave the terrain tool alone and embrace the challenge of building around it.

### Mountainside pond

![Enshrouded - Mountainside Pond south in the Springlands, next to the Hollow Halls](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/zcPPifuM5Ewncar6SQMtJZ-1200-80.jpg)

**🌳Empty lot for building**   
If you don't mind a deforestation project, there's more flat space here than what the screenshot shows. It's close to the Hollowed Halls, and there's easy access to a flint mine directly to your north.

## Revelwood and Blackmire

### Revelwood and Blackmire map

![Enshrouded map highlighting base locations in Revelwood](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/7KWZNoQni8hj8AuFLUzciN-1200-80.jpg)

### Glenwood's End neighboring waterfall

![Enshrouded - Revelwood waterfall](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/VfBsvzhow4gcwEzKT3wkZF-1200-80.jpg)

**🌳Empty lot for building**   
Look, I'm no fan of building in Revelwood—the thick foliage makes it too dark for my taste—but I'm obsessed with this lonely waterfall beside Glenwood's End. And it's not just any waterfall. It's a proper RPG waterfall, complete with a fancy secret alcove.

### Blue Goblet Tavern

![Enshrouded - Blue Goblet Tavern, a single large building sitting up on a hill](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/66P5YbiC56npM5DW5TULvk-1200-80.jpg)

**🏠Pre-built tavern for renovating**   
I understand renovating entire towns is a big ask, so here's a single building you can move into with considerably less hassle. What I really like about this old tavern is its hilltop location, which gets more sunlight than most places in Revelwood. There's also plenty of ore and clay nearby.

## Nomad Highlands and Kindlewastes

### Nomad Highlands and Kindlewastes map

![Enshrouded map highlighting base locations in Kindlewastes](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/x9fRzbSw4RG4Qn5QJdYejN-1200-80.jpg)

### Top of the Pillars of Creation

![Enshrouded - Pillars of Creation base location on top of the northern pillar](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/kC4QsFtWSuf6ngs67ySkN7-1200-80.jpg)

**🌳Empty lot for building**   
Even if I'm not building here, I always place a Flame Altar atop one of the two pillars for easy fast travel around southern Embervale. The northern pillar is easier to build on since the Engine Chambers occupy the southern one, but the latter has nearby copper mines to sweeten the deal.

### Southern foot of the Pillars of Creation

![Enshrouded - Pillars of Creation base location with a waterfall on the southern side of the southern pillar](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8iQWCRqjMfXYo5k3UxEhQ7-1200-80.jpg)

**🌳Empty lot for building**   
This adorable mountainside nook is tucked between two vast stretches of Shroud. It's not exactly overflowing with resources, but there's a limestone deposit within easy reach thanks to a handy hookshot shortcut.

### Shorewatch

![Enshrouded - Shorewatch, a massive town in the Kindlewaste desert](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/YTE2hkAxuBzQstwBnZSqnk-1200-80.jpg)

🏘️ **Pre-built town for renovating**   
Most Kindlewastes towns are a little too big for my liking, but Shorewatch is manageable. Southern Kindlewastes is pretty sparse on fast travel points, making this a handy spot to settle down. It even has a pre-built tower you can keep extending upward to get more mileage out of your glider jumps.

## Albaneve Summits

### Albaneve Summits map

![Enshrouded map highlighting base locations in Albavene Summit](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ZfR5RxyFTDpps3zyDeQehN-1200-80.jpg)

## Hermit Lodgings

![Enshrouded -](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/D6ZFRfxKezU5JoTyusbwqk-1200-80.jpg)

**🏠Pre-built cottage for renovating**   
Far to the east in the Albaneve Summits, tucked away on an unassuming mountainside, is one of Enshrouded's most heartbreaking journals. I won't spoil it, but since I can't bring myself to disturb the actual 'Hermit Lodgings' landmark, I settle a little northwest in this cute lakeside cabin instead. I like to imagine it belonged to the journal characters during happier times, though I'm sure they'd hate to see what I've done with the place.

## Veilwater Basin

### Veilwater Basin map

![Enshrouded map highlighting base locations in Kindlewood](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ga9r33zuZeDBZwJYHZttjN-1200-80.jpg)

### Dragondream Lake - Riverreach

![Enshrouded - Riverreach, a town in Veilwater Basin built over a waterfall](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/TfMc3dZzZyspbcCVn5fS5c-1200-80.jpg)

🏘️ **Pre-built town for renovating**   
Against my better judgment, I'm including the massive town of Riverreach. It sits in the northernmost corner of Dragondream Lake, stretching across the area's biggest waterfall. The town has three main levels, not counting the floors in each house, and renovating the whole place is a monumental undertaking. I'm sure none of us need this much space, but the allure of its enormous, working waterwheels and intricate underground cells is too good to pass up.

### Dragondream Lake - Driftwood Wake

![Enshrouded - Driftwood lake, a lakeside town in Veilwater Basin](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ka3729BRK3TPpFAAPPWXC7-1200-80.jpg)

🏘️ **Pre-built town for renovating**   
If Riverreach's dark wood and stone aren't your thing, Driftwood Wake sits across the lake to the southeast. It's closer to the water (and therefore better for fishing), with its own waterfall tucked against the western mountainside. It's smaller and probably more practical than Riverreach, so it earns a spot on the list, but its northern neighbor still has my heart.
