# Two men rushed to hospital after 'industrial accident' at Leeds unit

## The men were seriously hurt

Two men were rushed to hospital with serious injuries after an industrial accident at a Leeds industrial unit.

Paramedics were called to Knowsthorpe Lane, in Cross Green, at 4.15pm on Monday, October 19. Detectives from Leeds CID carried out initial enquiries on behalf of the Health and Safety Executive.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Emergency services were called to an industrial unit on Knowsthorpe Lane, Cross Green, Leeds, at 4.15pm yesterday following reports of an industrial accident. Two men were taken to hospital with serious injuries. Leeds CID conducted initial enquiries on behalf of the Health and Safety Executive."
