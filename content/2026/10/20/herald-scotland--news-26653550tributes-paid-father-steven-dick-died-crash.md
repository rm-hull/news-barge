A family has paid tribute to a "much-loved father, son and brother" who died in a crash in Fife.

Steven Dick, from Lochgelly, was pronounced dead at the scene after he was struck by a black Volkswagen Golf.

The 28-year-old male driver of the car was arrested in connection with a road traffic offence and enquiries are ongoing.

Police have appealed for witnesses to the incident, which happened at around 1.30am on Saturday, 17 October.

The road was closed for a number of hours while officers carried out their investigation and reopened at around 7.50am.

Steven’s family said: "Steven was a much-loved father, son and brother, who will be missed by all those who were close to him and knew him."

Sergeant Tom Aitken said: "Our thoughts remain with Steven’s family and friends at this difficult time.

"Enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances surrounding the crash and we are appealing for anyone with information to come forward.

"If you were on Lumphinnans Road and witnessed the crash, or may have dashcam footage of significance, please get in touch."

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident 0302 of Saturday, 17 October, 2026.
