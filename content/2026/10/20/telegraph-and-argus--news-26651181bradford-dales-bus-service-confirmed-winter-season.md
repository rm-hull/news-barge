A SUNDAY bus service connecting Bradford with the Yorkshire Dales will continue through the winter season.

The DalesBus 864, operated by Keighley Bus Company, will continue to run from Bradford to Malham every Sunday, serving Ilkley, Skipton, and Gargrave.

The service departs Bradford Interchange at 9.25am and Shipley Market Place at 9.40am, returning from Malham at 4.40pm, Skipton at 5.20pm, and Ilkley at 5.50pm.

At Ilkley, passengers can connect with DalesBus 874, which travels to Bolton Abbey, Burnsall, Grassington, Kettlewell, and Buckden.

The maximum single fare on each service is capped at £3, with under-19s travelling for £1.

Elderly and disabled bus passes are also valid for free travel.

Full details are available at dalesbus.org/cravenlink and in timetable leaflets soon to be distributed.
