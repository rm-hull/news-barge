Ryanair has announced its Winter 2026 schedule for Edinburgh Airport with 46 routes, including three new routes to Milan, Tirana and Warsaw.

The budget airline will also operate extra flights on 13 existing routes, including top winter sun, city break, and business travel destinations such as Barcelona, Budapest, Krakow and Prague.

The significant Ryanair growth at Edinburgh (+11%) is underpinned by the airline’s 11 Edinburgh-based aircraft.

Ryanair’s latest traffic figures showed it operated over 111,700 flights last month and carried 20.1 million passengers, up 4% year on year.

Ryanair’s Director of Comms, Jade Kirwan, said: “As the UK’s No.1 airline for low fares and reliability, we are pleased to announce Ryanair’s Winter 2026 schedule for Edinburgh, with 46 routes including 3 new routes to Milan, Tirana and Warsaw. This robust schedule, underpinned by Ryanair’s $1.1 billion investment in 11 Edinburgh-based aircraft, offers our customers throughout Scotland even more low-fare choice and regular connections for their winter getaways, whilst driving invaluable inbound tourism at the same time."

Lynsey Burns, Head of Aviation at Edinburgh Airport, said: “Winter might be on the way, but these new routes give passengers plenty to look forward to. Whether it’s a weekend in Milan, discovering Tirana or exploring Warsaw, these additions, alongside the many destinations already served by Ryanair, provide even more opportunities to travel from Scotland’s busiest airport and strengthen our links with key European markets.”
