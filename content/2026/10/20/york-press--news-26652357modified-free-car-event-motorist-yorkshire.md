A free event showcasing modified cars is set to take place near York this weekend.

Petrolheads are in for a treat as a huge variety of modified motors descend on The Motorist in Sherburn in Elmet Sherburn in Elmet for the free event on Saturday, October 24, celebrating car culture.

There will be modified cars from across the UK car scene, including those with subtle upgrades, restomods, and tuned performance cars, as well as stance builds, track-inspired machinery, and more.

The event follows a series of other themed days at The Motorist, including a popular Supercar Day earlier this month.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21537246.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Motorist hosts themed events throughout the year including the recent Supercar Day. *(Image: The Motorist)*

Modified will also feature a range of performance machinery, hot hatches, modern classics, custom builds, and other cars that defy categorisation.

Visitors will have the opportunity to meet the car owners and learn about the modifications made to each vehicle.

The event is open to anyone and is likely to appeal to all car enthusiasts, whether they have been working on their own build for years or are just starting out.

Any owner of a modified car is encouraged to bring it to the event to showcase the different aspects of modified car culture.

Modified car clubs or owners groups can also reserve an area together by contacting the organisers in advance.

The Drivers Lounge will be open throughout the event, serving breakfast, lunch, coffee, and drinks.

Everyone, including families and dogs, is welcome.

The Motorist hosts year-round meets and gets busy. Organisers are urging all visitors to enter and leave the venue respectfully to help keep it a safe environment for everyone to enjoy.
