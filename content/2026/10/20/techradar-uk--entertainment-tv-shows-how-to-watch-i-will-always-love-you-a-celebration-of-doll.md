## Where is I Will Always Love You: A Celebration of Dolly Parton taking place?

The tribute show will emanate from Belmont University’s Fisher Center in Nashville, Tennessee, the iconic 'Music City' and Parton's spiritual home.

Dr. Greg Jones, president of Belmont University says: "It is a privilege to welcome artists, friends and fans from around the world to celebrate her extraordinary life and legacy from this stage in Nashville, honoring a woman whose love for Tennessee was woven into everything she did."

## Who was Dolly Parton?

Primarily known as an icon of country music, Dolly Parton was also an actress, philanthropist, and businesswoman responsible for some of the genre's best loved songs including "Jolene," "I Will Always Love You," "9 to 5," and "Islands in the Stream."
