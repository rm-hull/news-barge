Train services between Balloch and Airdrie were disrupted after emergency services dealt with an incident in the Renton area.

ScotRail said it was assisting emergency services at the scene, with the incident affecting services operating between the two stations.

> NEW: We're assisting the emergency services who are dealing with an incident in the Renton area, which is affecting train services between Balloch and Airdrie. Train services between Airdrie and Balloch are now returning to run as scheduled. [https://t.co/AoNM5qsZBt](https://t.co/AoNM5qsZBt)
>
> — ScotRail (@ScotRail) October 20, 2026

Passengers faced disruption while the incident was being dealt with, although details of its nature have not been disclosed.

In an update, ScotRail confirmed that train services between Airdrie and Balloch were returning to their normal schedules.

Rail passengers have been advised to check their journeys for the latest travel information, as some services may still experience residual delays while the timetable recovers.

Scotrail said on social media: " We're assisting the emergency services who are dealing with an incident in the Renton area, which is affecting train services between Balloch and Airdrie."
