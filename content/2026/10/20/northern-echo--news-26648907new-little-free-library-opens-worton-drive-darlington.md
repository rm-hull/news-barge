Book lovers have a new reason to stop and browse while out and about in a Darlington neighbourhood.

A new Little Free Library has appeared on Worton Drive, giving residents and passers-by the chance to pick up their next read.

Resident Jennifer Gibbard launched the community book box to encourage sharing and bring neighbours together through reading.

Jennifer, a lifelong reader, said books are best enjoyed when they are passed on rather than left gathering dust on a shelf.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21600405.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jennifer Gibbard and the new book box. *(Image: JENNIFER GIBBARD)*

She said: “I've always loved the idea of books being passed from one person to another, rather than sitting unread on a shelf.

“I love sharing, swapping and passing books on, so a Little Free Library seemed like the perfect way to turn that into something the whole neighbourhood could enjoy.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21600406.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: JENNIFER GIBBARD)*

The library is open to everyone and stocks adult and children’s books.

There is no membership scheme or formal borrowing system.

Visitors can take a book and leave one behind if they wish.

Jennifer hopes the book box will become a familiar neighbourhood feature and a welcome surprise for passers-by.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21600407.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: JENNIFER GIBBARD)*

She said: “I wanted to create something small and simple that people in the neighbourhood could enjoy.

“Hopefully it will become one of those things that people just know is there – somewhere you might stop on a walk, find a book you weren't expecting, and take it home with you.”

The Little Free Library is now open to visitors.
