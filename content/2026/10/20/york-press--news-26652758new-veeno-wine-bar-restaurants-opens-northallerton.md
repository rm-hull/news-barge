A taste of Sicily has arrived with the launch of a new Italian restaurant and wine bar in the heart of a North Yorkshire town.

Veeno has opened at the Treadmills development in Northallerton, creating 10 jobs.

The restaurant, which is the first Veeno in North Yorkshire, welcomed its first guests following a VIP launch event attended by dignitaries, including the Mayor of Northallerton, and local businesses.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21606194.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sherryl Fernandes, Mayor of Northallerton, Cllr Andrew Hugill, Chris Fernandes and Chris and Sherryl's daughter *(Image: Submitted)*

Chris Fernandes, franchisee owner of Veeno Northallerton with his wife Sherryl, said: "We're absolutely delighted to have officially opened our doors in Northallerton.

"It's been a lot of hard work getting to this point, so to finally welcome people into the venue and see them enjoying the food, wine and atmosphere is a fantastic feeling.

"Northallerton is a brilliant town with a really strong sense of community, and the support we've received from local businesses and residents has been incredible."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21606285.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cheese and charcuterie board at Veeno in Northallerton. *(Image: Submitted)*

He added: "We wanted to create somewhere people could come together, whether that's for a relaxed drink, dinner with friends or a special celebration, and we're really proud of what we've achieved.

"We're looking forward to becoming part of the town and welcoming many more people through our doors."

The Italian wine bar and kitchen offers Sicilian wines, authentic cuisine and guided wine tasting experiences in a relaxed setting.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21606218.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pinsa bread at the newly-opened Italian venue Veeno in Northallerton. *(Image: Submitted)*

The menu features traditional sharing boards, pinsa (Italian flatbread), pasta dishes and desserts, including Veeno's signature tiramisu, made using the original recipe of founder Nino Caruso’s grandmother.

Wine is central to the Veeno experience, with a selection produced at the Caruso family’s vineyard and winery, Caruso & Minini, in Marsala, Sicily.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21606217.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rodrigue Trouillet Veeno UK Managing Director and Chris Fernandes, Franchisee Owner of Veeno Northallerton. *(Image: Submitted)*

Mr Caruso launched the first Veeno in Manchester in 2013, aiming to share his family’s wines and Italian hospitality with the UK.

He said: "It's fantastic to see Veeno open its doors in Northallerton and to welcome Chris and Sherryl into the Veeno family.

"When we launched Veeno in 2013, our ambition was to share our family's Sicilian wines and the Italian tradition of bringing people together over great food and drink.

"To see that vision continuing to grow in towns and cities across the UK is something we're incredibly proud of.

"Chris and Sherryl have put so much passion and hard work into creating this venue, and we're confident they'll make Veeno a much-loved part of the Northallerton community.

"We wish them every success as they begin this new chapter."

The Mayor of Northallerton, Councillor Andrew Hugill, said: "It's wonderful to see another new business opening in Northallerton and bringing something different to the town's hospitality offering.

"I'd like to congratulate Chris and the team on the opening of Veeno and wish them every success.

"It's a welcome addition to the town, and I'm sure residents and visitors alike will enjoy experiencing what it has to offer."
