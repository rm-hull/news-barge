Families are already making plans for half-term days out after a pumpkin trail returned to one of Bishop Auckland's most popular visitor attractions.

The Auckland Project's Pumpkin Trail is taking place at Auckland Palace and Gardens until November 1, inviting children to search for hidden pumpkins while exploring the historic grounds.

Young visitors can collect a free trail leaflet from Auckland Tower before following clues around the gardens.

Along the way, they're challenged to find special gold, silver and bronze pumpkins and complete a series of activities, with those taking part entered into a prize draw.

Among the prizes up for grabs is a family ticket to AGLOW, The Auckland Project's annual illuminated light trail.

The attraction is running from Wednesday to Sunday throughout the half-term period.

While the trail itself is free with admission, adults must pay £5 to enter the gardens.

Children aged under 16 can visit free of charge.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21599891.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Illuminated carved pumpkins on display as part of The Auckland Project's autumn and Halloween activities in Bishop Auckland. *(Image: Bishop Auckland Town Centre)*

Sharing details of the attraction on social media, Bishop Auckland Town Council described it as "a lovely autumn adventure right here on our doorstep".

The post struck a chord with local families, attracting dozens of interactions from residents keen to make the most of the school holidays.

Many commenters tagged friends and relatives to suggest a visit, while others discussed arranging to meet up with their children at the attraction.

The response reflects the growing appeal of family-focused events in Bishop Auckland, where attractions are preparing for an influx of visitors during the October break.

For those planning a visit, trail leaflets are available from Auckland Tower throughout the event, with organisers encouraging families to put their pumpkin-hunting skills to the test before the trail closes on November 1.
