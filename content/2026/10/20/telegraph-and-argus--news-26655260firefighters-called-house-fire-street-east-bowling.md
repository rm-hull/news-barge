A house fire broke out at a Bradford home in the early hours of this morning.

Crews from Bradford and Odsal were called to Warwick Road in East Bowling at 12.43am today (Tuesday).

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "Firefighters from Bradford and Odsal fire stations responded to the report of a fire in a two-storey semi-detached property.

"The fire was on the ground floor, with 10 per cent of the property involved.

"Four firefighters wearing breathing apparatus used two hose reels to extinguish the fire, and were able to confirm that no people were involved."

"A high pressure fan was used to clear smoke from the property.

"Fire investigators were consulted and are attending."
