Police have released an image of a man they want to speak to following an assault in Whitby.

The incident happened outside The George Hotel on Baxtergate at 1.45am on Saturday, September 12.

The victim was shouted at and spat at during the assault.

A police spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured as he may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference 12260188868.
