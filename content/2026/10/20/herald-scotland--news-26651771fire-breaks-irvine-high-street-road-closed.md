Fire crews were called out during the early hours of the morning to tackle a blaze that broke out on a Scottish town's main street.

Firefighters are currently at the scene, and police have closed off a road while the emergency in Irvine's High Street is being dealt with.

> Emergency services are in attendance at a fire at a premises on High Street, Irvine. We were called to the scene around 5.30am on Tuesday, 20 October, 2026. High Street is closed at Bank Street and a cordon is in place. Enquiries are ongoing. [https://t.co/T2TzYRLe8v](https://t.co/T2TzYRLe8v)
>
> — Police Scotland Ayrshire (@PSOS\_Ayrshire) October 20, 2026

The alarm was raised at 5.30am. Pictures from the scene show flames have taken hold of the building, with a plume of smoke rising above the site.

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service confirmed it was in attendance but has provided no further details.

A spokesperson for Police Scotland said: "Emergency services are in attendance at a fire at a premises on High Street, Irvine.

"We were called to the scene around 5.30am on Tuesday, 20 October 2026.

"High Street is closed at Bank Street and a cordon is in place. Enquiries are ongoing."
