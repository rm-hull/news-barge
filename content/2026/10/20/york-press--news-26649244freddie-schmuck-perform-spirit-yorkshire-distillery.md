An intimate acoustic gig is set to take place at a Yorkshire whisky distillery's coffee shop.

Scarborough musician Freddie Schmuck, former frontman of indie-rock band The Feens, will perform in Hunmanby at the Spirit of Yorkshire Distillery on October 31.

He will take to the stage as part of the venue’s Distillery Sessions.

Steve Marson, marketing manager at Spirit of Yorkshire, said: "If you love your guitar-based indie, Freddie’s Distillery Session will be right up your street: a stripped-back acoustic gig here in our super-intimate Coffee Shop."

"Freddie and The Feens spent nearly a decade building a massive reputation on the UK indie circuit.

"They racked up over a million streams, secured an iTunes Number 1 single and earned widespread airplay on BBC Radio 1, BBC 6Music and Radio X."

Tickets are £15, or £25 including a goulash or vegetarian supper, and are available online or by calling 01723 891758.
