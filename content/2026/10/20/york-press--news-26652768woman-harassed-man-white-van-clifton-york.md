A lone 20-year-old woman was "harassed" by a man in a white van as she walked along streets in York, police have said.

Officers are appealing for information about the incident in Clifton between around 12.30pm and 1.15pm on Thursday, October 8.

North Yorkshire Police said the woman, described as white, of medium build and 5ft 2in tall, was harassed in Cromer Street, Glencoe Street, Pembroke Street, Shipton Street and Newborough Street.

The force is appealing for information about the man, who is described as being black and driving a white Peugeot Partner van with registration MP19 NPN.

Anyone with information is asked to email [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101 quoting reference 12260198568.
