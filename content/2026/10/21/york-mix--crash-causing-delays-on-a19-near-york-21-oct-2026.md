A crash is causing jams on the A19 south of York this afternoon (Wednesday).

It’s between the Designer Outlet and Crockey Hill.

Emergency services are on the scene.

#### Crash location

Drivers report standstill traffic on the road both ways. There’s congestion as far south as Deighton.

The AA reports delays are increasing on the road.
