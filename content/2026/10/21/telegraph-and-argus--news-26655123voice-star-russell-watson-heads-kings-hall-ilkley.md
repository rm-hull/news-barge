RUSSELL Watson will bring an intimate evening of music and personal stories to audiences.

The performance will take place at King’s Hall Ilkley this Friday, October 23.

Watson, the original crossover superstar, rose to fame with The Voice, his debut album, which shattered records and won hearts across the globe.

He has since sold millions of albums, performed for royalty, presidents and popes, and shared stages with musical legends.

During this intimate tour, Watson will discuss the highs and lows of his extraordinary life, including surviving two life-threatening illnesses. The evening will combine warmth, humour and personal stories from behind the spotlight.

Audiences can expect powerful renditions of beloved classics, spine-tingling vocals and a rare opportunity to spend time up close with The Voice himself.

The performance begins at 7.30pm and runs for one hour and 50 minutes.

Standard tickets cost £37 to £88. Pre-show meet and greet, Friends Members, boxes and access tickets are listed as open.

Boxes are still available by calling 01274 432000 to book.
