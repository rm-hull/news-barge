Application forms are now available for York’s annual Christmas Cheer Receptions.

Paper application forms are available from York libraries, the council’s Customer Centre at West Offices, or St Sampson’s Centre.

Hosted by the Lord Mayor and Sheriff of York, the events are open to residents aged 65 and over and will take place at the Assembly Rooms on December 1.

Councillor Margaret Wells, the Right Honourable Lord Mayor of York, said: "What better way to start your festivities than with good company in a spectacular setting?

"And I’m honoured to be hosting the annual Christmas Cheer Receptions on Tuesday, December 1.

"The receptions offer our older residents top class entertainment, food and fun.

"It’s a chance to meet new friends and bring in the festive season in a truly memorable way.

"If you’re eligible to apply and are free to join us on Tuesday, December 1, please send off your application to enjoy this wonderful occasion."

Each event will welcome 200 guests, with a morning session at 10am and an afternoon session at 2pm.

Morning entertainment will include St Wilfrid's Catholic Primary School, the Shepherd Brass Band, and soloist Alexandra Mather.

The afternoon programme will feature Ralph Butterfield Primary School and the York Philharmonic Male Voice Choir.

Completed forms should be sent to The Christmas Cheer Draw, c/o Civic Services, West Offices, Station Rise, York, YO1 6GA, or emailed to [civic@york.gov.uk.](mailto:civic@york.gov.uk.)

Entries must be received by November 16.

Applicants can state a preferred session, though tickets will be allocated through a random draw.
