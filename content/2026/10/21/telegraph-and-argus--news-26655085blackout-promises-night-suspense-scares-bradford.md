AN immersive theatrical experience is set to captivate Bradford.

Blackout, an innovative production, promises to plunge attendees into a world where fear and imagination collide at St George's Hall this Thursday.

The narrative unfolds as a small cast gathers for a late-night rehearsal in an old theatre, only to find themselves caught up in the building's sinister reputation.

As the theatre doors close, reality and storytelling begin to blur, creating a shadowed world filled with unnerving twists and creeping shadows.

The production invites audiences to be part of the experience, with every movement, sound, and shadow pulling them deeper into the dark.

Tickets for this unique experience are priced between £22 and £30.

The performance is scheduled for 7.30pm.
