## This episode of Qatar in Motion explores how local talent is driving economic diversification. We reveal how innovation, enterprise and ideas are being proudly made in Qatar and finding its way to the global stage.

We meet the entrepreneurs behind a new generation of homegrown businesses redefining what ‘Made in Qatar’ means. From Mazzraty’s locally produced dairy and poultry products, to Raw Middle East’s plant-based food movement, we explore how innovation, sustainability and ambition are driving Qatar’s economic diversification, and turning local ideas into global impact. We also meet JTA International Investment, which is quietly showing how Qatar is investing and scaling global businesses and helping them reach new markets.
