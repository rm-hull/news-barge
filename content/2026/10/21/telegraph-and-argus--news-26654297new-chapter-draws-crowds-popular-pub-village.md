A popular pub once visited by former Prime Minister David Cameron has announced a major new chapter after reopening its kitchen to a strong response from customers.

The Ring O'Bells Inn, in Bradford, relaunched its food offering on October 10, with its first service fully booked.

Diners have since left a series of positive reviews, praising the food, friendly service and welcoming atmosphere.

The reopening marks a significant milestone for the historic venue, which has changed hands several times over the years.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21608135.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Ring O' Bells Inn has relaunched its kitchen, with dishes including burgers, loaded fries and a range of pub classics now available to diners. *(Image: Alex Mordey)*

## Famous pub with a rich history

The Ring O' Bells, which is on Hill Top Road in Thornton, occupies a building steeped in local history.

Originally a Wesleyan chapel, it is believed the pub takes its name from a bell loft that still exists within the building, despite not being visible from outside.

The venue enjoyed a reputation as a popular food destination and attracted national attention in March 2012 when Prime Minister David Cameron stopped by for lunch during a visit to the area.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/1954889.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Prime Minister David Cameron is unexpected guest at Thornton restaurant

## New team behind the revival

Its current operators hope the kitchen relaunch will help restore the pub's standing within the community.

The pub is now run by landlady Helen Toft in partnership with Alex Mordey.

Ms Toft previously managed staff and operations at Aldi and also ran the kitchen at the Victoria Hotel on Sandy Lane.

Leading the kitchen is head chef Aidan Hunton, with food now being served Tuesday to Sunday.

Ms Toft said: "We wanted to bring back the food this place was known for.

"Opening to a full room on the first night was a brilliant start, and we're grateful to everyone who has come in and supported us."

Mr Mordey added: "Our aim was to bring the food back to the standard this pub was known for, and seeing it so busy on the first night was a great start.

"Helen and I bring different strengths to the pub. Together we want to give it a future it can be proud of."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21608137.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Food is once again being served at the Ring O' Bells Inn after the Thornton pub reopened its kitchen under a new management team. *(Image: Alex Mordey)*

## 'A full circle moment'

For Mr Hunton, the role represents something of a homecoming, having started his culinary career in Thornton 13 years ago.

He said: "It feels like a full circle moment to be cooking in Thornton, 13 years from where I started my career.

"Now running the kitchen for the Ring O' Bells in the village, Helen, Alex and I share the same vision for the pub, which is great.

"Locally sourced food that people can enjoy with friends and family. I'm proud to be part of the pub's next chapter."

The pub is open from noon until 11pm Tuesday to Saturday and from noon until 10pm on Sundays.
