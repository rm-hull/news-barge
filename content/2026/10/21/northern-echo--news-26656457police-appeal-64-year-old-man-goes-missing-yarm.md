Police are concerned about a missing man last seen in Yarm using a walking frame.

Officers from Cleveland Police are currently looking for 64-year-old Paul Marshall, who was last seen on Mount Leven Road/Valley Drive at around 7.40am today (October 21).

He is described as a white male, balding and was wearing a light coloured top, light coloured pants and was using a double handed walking frame.

There is no photograph available of Paul.

Members of the public who see Paul are advised not to approach him but to call police immediately on 999.

Anyone who thinks they may have seen Paul this morning, or may know of his whereabouts, is also asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference number 210672
