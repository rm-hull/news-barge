Construction has finished on four new homes within the grounds of the Grade II-listed Ousefield House in Fulford, York.

The Mulgrave Properties houses come with either four or five bedrooms.

Three of the homes have already been sold, and one four-bedroom property – offering 3,199 sq ft of living space –is now on the market for £1.4 million.

The development features energy-saving technologies such as air source heat pumps and mechanical ventilation heat recovery.

Each home includes custom-designed kitchens , private gardens surrounded by mature trees, and first-floor roof terraces.

The development, called Urban Renaissance, was a finalist in this year’s Insider Media Yorkshire Property Awards.

Sales director for Mulgrave Properties Debbie Foster said: “We are proud to bring construction work at Urban Renaissance to a close.

“This has been an incredible project, creating modern, energy-efficient new-build homes which are inspired by their historic surroundings and in keeping with their charm.”

The project brought together York-based architects Vincent & Partners, Harrogate bathroom specialists Abacus, and Newcastle kitchen provider Nixons Project Exclusive.

More details about the homes at Urban Renaissance are available on the Mulgrave Properties website.
