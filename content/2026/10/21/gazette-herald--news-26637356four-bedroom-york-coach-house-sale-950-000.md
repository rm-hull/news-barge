A York home “with real presence” has hit the market – for £950,000.

The four-bedroom home, called the Coach House, is for sale in Millgates, off Boroughbridge Road.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it is a “truly individual and characterful” home which combines the “charm and architectural features of a traditional coach house with the space and versatility needed for modern family living”.

Inside, an entrance hall leads to a series of reception rooms.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21581451.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Outside at the four-bedroom home, called the Coach House, for sale in Millgates, off Boroughbridge Road, York, for £950,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

A dining kitchen sits at the heart of the home with views and access towards the courtyard garden.

Upstairs, four bedrooms are on the first floor, “offering flexibility for family life, guests or hobbies”, according to Ashtons.

Outside is a landscaped garden and a garage with a mezzanine above.

“Homes such as this are rarely available and increasingly difficult to find – space, character, versatility and a real sense of individuality,” an Ashtons spokesperson said.

“It has been lovingly presented by the current owners, with colour, furnishings and carefully chosen interiors complementing rather than competing with its original character.”
