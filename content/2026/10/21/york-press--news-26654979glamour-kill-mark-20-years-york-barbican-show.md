## 'Here’s to York' - band to celebrate anniversary with biggest ever headline show

Glamour of the Kill are set to play York Barbican *(Image: Provided)*

A YORK band is preparing to mark 20 years in music with their biggest headline show right back where it all began.

Popular York metal band Glamour of the Kill will celebrate their 20th anniversary with a headline performance at York Barbican on August 28. 2027.

The concert promises to be the biggest of their career and a homecoming celebration to mark two decades in the industry.

Opening with a a performance by the winners of The Fulford Arms Battle of the Bands, the anniversary event will feature an all-York line-up, including Beyond All Reason and The Bastard Sons.

A spokesman for the band said: "We’re incredibly excited to be celebrating 20 years of Glamour of the Kill, and there really couldn’t be a more fitting place to do it than The Barbican, right here in our hometown of York.

"This show is going to be a celebration of everything we’ve done over the past two decades.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21609160.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The band at a previous show *(Image: Provided)*

"We’ll be playing songs from across all of our releases, with a few surprises thrown in along the way, and we’re bringing some great friends along for the ride In - Beyond All Reason and The Bastard Sons.

"This will be our biggest headline show ever, and we genuinely can’t wait to get up on that stage, celebrate 20 years with everyone who has supported us, and make it a night to remember. Here’s to York."

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, October 23 via the York Barbican website.
