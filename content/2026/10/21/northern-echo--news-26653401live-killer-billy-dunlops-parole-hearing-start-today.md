* Welcome to The Northern Echo's live blog on double jeopardy killer Billy Dunlop's parole hearing.
* It’s Alex O'Leary here, reporting from London as Dunlop - who was jailed for life in 2006 after murdering Julie Hogg in Billingham in 1989 - bids for freedom.
* The "evil" murderer was the first person in the country to be charged twice with the same offence after Julie’s mother Ann Ming’s 17-year fight to overturn the 800-year-old double jeopardy law.
* The hearing is expected to last for two days.
