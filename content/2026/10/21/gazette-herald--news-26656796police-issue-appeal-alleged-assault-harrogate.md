Police are appealing for information following an alleged assault in a North Yorkshire town.

The incident occurred on Sunday, October 11 2026 between about 2pm and 2.30pm on Knaresborough Road in Harrogate, opposite Co-op Funeralcare.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "The incident involved an unknown man assaulting a member of the public."

The suspect is yet to be identified, and officers are keen to speak to witnesses or anyone with relevant information.

The spokesperson added: "If you saw what happened, have CCTV, dashcam footage, or any information that could assist the investigation, please get in touch with North Yorkshire Police quoting the relevant incident number."

For those with information, please contact North Yorkshire Police online, selecting “Tell us about” and then “An existing case or report”.

Alternatively, PC 1311 Young can be reached on 101, quoting collar number.

Those unable to use these options should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

People can also share information anonymously with Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Reference 12260200119 should be quoted when providing information.

The police did not provide any further details about the assault or the condition of the victim.
