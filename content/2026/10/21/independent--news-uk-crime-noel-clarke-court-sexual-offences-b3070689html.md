Actor Noel Clarke has pleaded not guilty to sex offences against five women.

The 50-year-old from Kensington, central London, is accused of two counts of sexual assault, three of voyeurism and one of exposure between 2007 and 2016.

Clarke, who has starred in *Doctor Who* and the movie*Star Trek: Into Darkness*, appeared at Westminster Magistrates’ Court on Wednesday.

Court officials said he pleaded not guilty to the offences, which are alleged to have taken place in London and Dorset.

Clarke received critical acclaim for writing, directing, and starring in the teen crime drama films *Kidulthood, Adulthood,* and*Brotherhood.*

The charges were brought after an investigation that began in September 2025.

He was conditionally bailed to next appear at Southwark Crown Court on 18 November.
