## Live

In line with the iconic game series' 30th anniversary, Sony has revealed a limited-edition Gran Turismo PS5 controller, and it's going up for pre-order tomorrow!

![A screenshot from the promotional video of the Gran Turismo 7 DualSense controller](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/FKuGHE4mwcDiA9JwbrbSxh-1200-80.jpg)

**1. US pre-order quick links2. UK pre-order quick links3. Live updates**

It's a PS5 DualSense controller with a lot going on across it, but one thing's for sure: it does evoke a Gran Turismo racing car and is sure to be popular with the series' long-standing fans and PlayStation hardware collectors.

With racing car badges, racing stripes, and some unmistakably Gran Turismo motifs across the white and black carbon-fiber-effect chassis, this really does a great job of evoking the famous racing series that has spanned every PlayStation generation.

This controller will cost the usual $84.99 / £74.99, and pre-orders go live at 10am local time (ET in the US) tomorrow, October 22. It appears from the wording on the PS Blog that this will be a PlayStation Direct exclusive too, but we're still including links to other retailers below, as well as other key info, as we set up our stall early.

### Gran Turismo Limited Edition PS5 controllers: cut to the chase

* **Price:** $84.99 / £74.99
