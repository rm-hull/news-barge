A Michael Jackson tribute concert is set to take place in Bradford.

Man in the Mirror, starring CJ, one of the world’s greatest Michael Jackson tribute artists, will be at St George's Hall next Tuesday, October 27.

The show will feature all of the King of Pop's greatest hits, including Thriller, Billie Jean, Beat It, Smooth Criminal, and Man in the Mirror.

The show will also have choreography, visual effects, and costumes.

"Join us for an unforgettable evening celebrating the legendary King of Pop. "This is the Man in the Mirror – the must-see, electrifying tribute concert to Michael Jackson.

"Whether you're a lifelong fan or experiencing the magic of Michael Jackson for the first time, join us for a night like no other. "Don’t miss out on this thrilling experience."

Tickets are priced at £34, with a maximum of 10 tickets available to book online HERE.

The show will start at 7.30pm.
