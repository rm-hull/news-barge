A Harrogate woman has won £220 after correctly identifying two songs in Your Harrogate's Double Decker competition.

Lauren named ‘Everybody’s Free’ by Rozalla and ‘Starlight’ by The Supermen Lovers to scoop the cash on George Dickson's Drive Time show.

The competition, run in partnership with The Harrogate Bus Company, challenges listeners to identify two songs merged into one short clip - naming both the titles and artists.

Each prize pot starts at £150 and increases by £10 every weekday until someone gives the correct answer.

Fancy having a go yourself? Double Decker airs at 4:10pm on weekdays with George Dickson.

Listeners can register their interest to play via the Your Harrogate **website**, with one entrant selected at random each day to answer on air.

*A new clip will be released tomorrow (Thursday) with another chance to win £150 thanks to The Harrogate Bus Company.*
