The son of legendary Hollywood director Rob Reiner is due back in court Wednesday as a bitter legal battle unfolds over whether evidence surrounding the killings of his famous parents should be made public.

Nick Reiner, 33, is charged with murdering his father, 78, and mother, photographer Michele Reiner, 70, who were found stabbed to death inside their Brentwood, Los Angeles, home on December 14.

His surviving siblings, Romy and Jake Reiner, have joined efforts to prevent the release of grand jury evidence detailing their parents' deaths, arguing that making the material public would inflict further trauma on the grieving family.

![Nick Reiner, 33, is charged with murdering his father, 78, and mother, photographer Michele Reiner, 70, who were found stabbed to death inside their Brentwood, Los Angeles, home](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/09/15/14/2026-08-17T212837Z_588297862_RC2LRJAB31LE_RTRMADP_3_PEOPLE-ROB-REINER.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The hearing comes as Nick Reiner separately fights to access more than $1.5 million from a family trust to fund his murder defense.

## Siblings plead for privacy as media demands access to evidence

Reiner's defense attorney, Kimberly Greene, has asked a judge to keep grand jury transcripts and exhibits sealed, arguing that their release could undermine her client's right to a fair trial.

Prosecutors say his siblings also strongly oppose the disclosure.

In court documents, prosecutors Habib Balian and Jonathan Chung said Romy and Jake wanted as much privacy as possible following the "horrific and unimaginable murders."

The siblings believe releasing details of their parents' killings would cause them "additional trauma and harm," prosecutors wrote.

![Rob Reiner and wife Michelle arrive at "Turn Up The Heat-Fight Global Warming", a benefit to help the Natural Resources Defense Council, featuring a concert performance by the Rolling Stones at the Staples Center on February 6, 2003](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/08/12/20/2267181...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

However, a coalition of more than a dozen media organizations has challenged the request, arguing that Reiner has failed to overcome the public's right to access court proceedings.

Los Angeles County Superior Court Judge Sam Ohta previously ordered the records to remain conditionally sealed pending a hearing.

Reiner was indicted by a grand jury in July on two murder counts, including special-circumstance allegations of multiple murders and lying in wait. Prosecutors also allege he personally used a knife.

The Los Angeles County medical examiner determined that both victims died from multiple sharp-force injuries.

District Attorney Nathan Hochman announced last month that prosecutors would not seek the death penalty. Reiner faces a maximum sentence of life imprisonment without parole if convicted.

He remains jailed without bail.

## $1.5 million inheritance fight adds new twist to murder case

A separate legal dispute centers on Reiner's attempt to access a trust fund his parents established in 1993.

His attorneys argue that he is entitled to more than $1.5 million under the trust's terms and needs the money to pay for his defense.

![Rob and Michelle Reiner shared three children: Jake, Romy and Nick. The director also had an older daughter, Tracy, whom he adopted during his first marriage to Penny Marshall](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/05/07/16/42/Rob-Reiner.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

According to a nearly 140-page petition, Reiner was entitled to receive half the fund when he turned 30, with the remainder due at 35.

His legal team maintains that those distributions are mandatory and cannot be withheld simply because he has been accused of murder.

The dispute involves California's so-called *slayer statute*, which generally prevents someone who intentionally kills another person from benefiting financially from that person's death.

Reiner's attorneys argue that he remains presumed innocent and that the law should not prevent him from accessing funds already designated for his benefit.

![Nick Reiner, wearing a blue protective vest, makes his first court appearance](https://static.the-independent.com/2025/12/17/19/30/Nick-Reiner-makes-his-first-court-appearance-on-murder-charges-for-the-killing-of-his-parents-Rob-an.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"Nick loved his parents, and he is devastated by their deaths," the petition states.

The filing also suggests that difficulties accessing the money contributed to prominent defense attorney Alan Jackson's departure from the criminal case.

Another hearing on the trust dispute is scheduled for Friday, October 23.

## Hollywood tragedy stunned family and friends

Rob and Michele Reiner were discovered dead at their home hours before their youngest son was arrested near the University of Southern California.

Reports subsequently emerged that Nick had argued with his father at a Christmas party hosted by Conan O'Brien the previous evening.

The family had previously spoken publicly about Nick's struggles with addiction, which inspired the 2015 film *Being Charlie*, directed by his father.

![Reports subsequently emerged that Nick had argued with his father at a Christmas party hosted by Conan O'Brien the previous evening](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/02/21/10/16/Conan-OBrien-Rob-Reiner.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Following their parents' deaths, Romy and Jake described their grief as unimaginable.

"They weren't just our parents; they were our best friends," they said in a statement, urging the public to show compassion and respect their privacy.

Rob Reiner first found fame playing Michael "Meathead" Stivic on *All in the Family* before becoming one of Hollywood's most celebrated directors.

His films included *Stand By Me*,* The Princess Bride*,* Misery*,* A Few Good Men*,* This Is Spinal Tap*and* When Harry Met Sally*.

He was also a prominent Democratic activist who campaigned for same-sex marriage rights and early childhood education.

Nick Reiner has not been convicted of any crime in connection with his parents' deaths.
