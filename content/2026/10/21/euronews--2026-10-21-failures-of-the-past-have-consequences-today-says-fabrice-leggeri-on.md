Fabrice Leggeri, MEP for Rassemblement Nationale in the Patriots for Europe group, speaks to Euronews on the recent violence and protests in France.

He claims integration policies have "failed" in France, in addition to the failure of the French government for a controlled migration policy, calling on the government to "draw conclusions from that failure." He believes the government, and the president Emmanuel Macron, has "not addressed the problem."

France has been embroiled in protests and looting across the country over the past weekend, and the government had imposed temporary curfews to help curtail the violence.
