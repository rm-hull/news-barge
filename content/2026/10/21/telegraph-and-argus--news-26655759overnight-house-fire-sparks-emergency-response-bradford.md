Firefighters were called to a house fire in Bradford during the early hours of the morning.

Crews from Bradford and Odsal fire stations were dispatched to a two-storey semi-detached home on Warwick Road in East Bowling at 12.43am on Tuesday.

The incident was initially reported as a "persons reported" fire, prompting an urgent response from emergency services.

## Fire broke out on ground floor

According to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, the blaze was located on the ground floor of the property and involved around 10 per cent of the area.

Four firefighters wearing breathing apparatus used two hose reels to bring the fire under control.

Crews later confirmed that nobody had been involved in the incident.

## Specialist team called in

Following the fire, a high-pressure fan was used to clear smoke from the property.

A specialist Fire Investigation Team was consulted and attended the scene to examine the circumstances surrounding the blaze.

## Investigation continues

The cause of the fire has not yet been confirmed.

Fire crews remained in attendance until the following morning while work at the scene continued.

An investigation into the circumstances of the fire is ongoing.

## **FULL statement from Emergency Services**

A WYFRS spokesperson said: "Firefighters from Bradford and Odsal fire stations responded to the report of a fire in a two-storey semi-detached property that was made persons reported on arrival.

"The fire was on the ground floor of the property 10 percent involved.

"Four firefighters wearing breathing apparatus used 2 hose reels to extinguish the fire, and were able to confirm that no persons were involved. A high pressure fan was used to clear smoke from the property.

"Fire Investigation were consulted and are attending."
