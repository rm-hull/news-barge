**A FRONTMAN of a top American band is set to perform in York tonight.**

The Killers frontman Brandon Flowers will perform to a sold-out audience at the Barbican in York tonight (October 21).

As reported by The Press in June, the show is part of his newly announced UK and Ireland tour and comes after the release of his third solo album, Thrasher, in August.

Other cities on the tour include Bournemouth, Bristol, Nottingham, Manchester, Glasgow, Birmingham, Dublin, and London – with the latter set to take place in Royal Albert Hall.

The musician will perform new material and tracks from his previous solo albums Flamingo and The Desired Effect as part of the show.

The outer doors for the show will open at 7pm tonight, with access to the inter audiotorium limited until half an hour later at 7.30pm.

Brandon will take to the stage at 9pm, with no support act scheduled.

All under 14’s must be accompanied by an adult aged 18+, with no under 14’s allowed on the standing floor.

Fans have been asked not to contact the Barbican regarding tickets as the gig is fully sold out.
