# Exciting new burger bar to open in Leeds city centre

## Work is currently ongoing ahead of the exciting new opening

Work is ongoing ahead of the launch of an exciting new Leeds city centre burger bar.

The new opening will replace a big name in a unit left vacant following its closure. We have previously brought you news of Five Guys, the business itself famous for its burgers, shutting.

The fast food giant previously operated out of the premises in Duncan Street. When we brought you news of Five Guys shutting, in March, a notice read: “We’re sorry, this Five Guys is now closed.

“Visit us in Trinity Leeds.” Boards have gone up which state Urban Burger will soon open in the former Five Guys unit.

Work is currently ongoing ahead of it launching. You can currently find Urban Burger branches across South Yorkshire.

We’ll bring you further details on the new Urban Burger branch in Leeds when we have further details.
