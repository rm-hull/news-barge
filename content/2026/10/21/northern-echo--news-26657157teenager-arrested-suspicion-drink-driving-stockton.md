A teenager has been arrested on suspicion of drink driving after a car overturned in Stockton.

Officers from Cleveland Police were called to the one-vehicle crash on Greens Lane in Hartburn at around 4.20pm on Tuesday (October 20).

The road was partly closed during the incident to allow recovery of the vehicle.
