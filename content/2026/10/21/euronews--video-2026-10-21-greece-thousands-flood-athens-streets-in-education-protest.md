Students, teachers and public-sector workers **marched through central Athens** on Wednesday, demanding**more funding for education**,** smaller classes**,** better facilities**and** affordable student housing**.

Aerial footage showed crowds filling major streets as protesters made their way from the University of Athens towards the **Greek Parliament**. Some banners written in French reflected the Greek protesters'** solidarity with**, and inspiration from, recent** student demonstrations in France**. Organisers presented the march as part of a** broader European movement**calling for** better-funded education**,** affordable housing**and** relief from rising living costs**.

**Police officers**, including riot police carrying shields, accompanied the march and** blocked nearby roads**. Some shops and cafés closed their shutters as a precaution.

The demonstration was part of a **nationwide protest movement**, with rallies also held in** Thessaloniki, Patras, Larissa and Crete**. Police estimated that around** 10,000 people**took part in Athens and its suburbs, although organisers claimed a** higher turnout**.

Some demonstrators had **camped overnight outside Parliament**, calling for** rent controls**,** greater housing support**and** more investment in public education**.

The rally **ended peacefully** shortly before 2pm, reflecting**mounting pressure on the Greek government** to address education funding and the financial difficulties facing students.
