Darlington's Indoor Market is preparing to host an afternoon of Diwali celebrations, with free entertainment and crafts and food on offer for visitors.

The event will take place on Friday, October 30, from 3pm to 7pm at the Market Hall and is open to all.

A Darlington Council spokesman said: "There will be the chance to create mini lanterns, Mandala designs and other Diwali crafts, food samples from Rumi’s Kitchen. Bollywood dancers from Navrang Arts and music and entertainment from NE4 DJs."
