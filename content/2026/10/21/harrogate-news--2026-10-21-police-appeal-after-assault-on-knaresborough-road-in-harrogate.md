North Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses after a member of the public was assaulted on Knaresborough Road in Harrogate.

The incident happened between approximately 2pm and 2.30pm on Sunday 11 October 2026, opposite Co-op Funeralcare.

Police say an unknown man assaulted the victim and officers are now carrying out enquiries to identify the suspect.

Investigators are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident or has information that could assist their investigation. This includes anyone with CCTV or dashcam footage covering the area at the time.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online by visiting the force’s website, selecting ‘Tell us about’ and then choosing ‘An existing case or report’.

Alternatively, information can be provided through the force’s live chat service, available Monday to Friday between 9am and 5pm, or by calling PC 1311 Young on 101 and quoting collar number 1311.

Anyone unable to use these options can call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

Anyone contacting police is asked to quote reference number **12260200119**.
