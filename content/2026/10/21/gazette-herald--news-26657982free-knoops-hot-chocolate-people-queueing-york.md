An offer from a York café has people queuing down the street.

Knoops in Parliament Street is giving away free hot chocolates on Wednesday (October 21).

Dozens of people have queued from outside the café in the former Boots Opticians to Shambles Market to get their hot drinks.

The offer is available for small hot chocolates, usually priced between £4.40 and £4.70, through the Knoops app.

Knoops opened in Parliament Street in 2024 and offers a fully customisable chocolate menu.

Pastries and takeaway chocolate flakes are also available, allowing people to remake their favourite drink at home.

Knoops was founded in Rye, East Sussex, in 2013 by German-born chocolate enthusiast Jens Knoop and has since expanded across the UK.
