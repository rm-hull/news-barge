Train services through the North East have been disrupted this morning (Wednesday, October 21) as emergency services deal with an incident.

Rail passengers faced disruption this morning after an incident prompted the closure of railway lines in the Newcastle area.

National Rail, Northern and TransPennine Express have warned that services running to, from and through Newcastle may be cancelled, delayed or revised while emergency services attend the scene.

The British Transport Police (BTP) have confirmed that a person was hit by a train and died this morning.

A BTP spokesperson said: "We were called to an area of the line close to Newcastle station at around 8am today (21 October) following reports of a casualty on the tracks.

"Officers attended along with paramedics, but sadly a person was pronounced dead at the scene.

"Enquiries are ongoing into the circumstances leading up to their death."

The disruption could affect passengers travelling between York and Newcastle, including those using services through Darlington and Durham.

Northern also warned passengers that all lines were blocked at Newcastle while emergency services dealt with the incident.

National Rail subsequently reported that some lines were closed, with trains running to, from and through Newcastle potentially affected.

The disruption may have knock-on effects for passengers travelling through Durham and Darlington, which are on the main railway route between Newcastle and York.

Passengers have been urged to allow additional time for their journeys and check the latest information before travelling.

## Ticket acceptance arrangements have been put in place to help affected passengers

TransPennine Express customers can use their tickets at no additional cost on CrossCountry, LNER and Northern services between Newcastle, York and Leeds.

CrossCountry has also confirmed that affected customers can use their tickets on alternative services, including:

* The two CrossCountry services operating before and after their cancelled service.
* Avanti West Coast services between Edinburgh and Birmingham New Street.
* LNER services between Edinburgh and York.
* Northern services between York and Sheffield via Leeds.
* TransPennine Express services between Edinburgh and Newcastle, York or Leeds.
