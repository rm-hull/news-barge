LEGO, chocolate, and booze are among the 'stolen' items that were found in a car pulled over by police in East Yorkshire,

Humberside Police says that it has arrested three men after police stopped a vehicle on the M62 near Goole yesterday (October 20)

Officers pulled over the car, which was believed to be linked to criminal activity, as it travelled westbound towards West Yorkshire and detained three men for a search.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Approximately £2000 worth of suspected stolen items including chocolate, alcohol, and Lego, was found inside, and initial checks also discovered the driver was believed to be driving without insurance, and was also disqualified."

The driver - a 29-year-old man - was arrested on suspicion of shop theft, disqualified driving, and driving without insurance.

The two other men were also arrested: a 22-year-old man on suspicion of shop theft and a 52-year-old man on suspicion of three counts of shop theft.

All three remain in custody, whilst police continue their investigation.
