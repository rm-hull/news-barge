*trentbarton is a Business Reporter client*

For more than a century, trentbarton has been helping people connect with the places, people and opportunities that matter most. As one of the East Midlands’ leading bus operators, we provide vital links across Derbyshire, Nottinghamshire and Leicestershire, serving communities with reliable, high-quality public transport that forms part of everyday life for thousands of customers.

At trentbarton, we see ourselves as much more than a bus company. We are a people business, focused on making travel easier, more accessible and more enjoyable. Whether it is helping someone get to work, connecting students with education, supporting local businesses or enabling people to spend time with family and friends, our services play a key role in keeping communities moving.

Our success has always been built on a commitment to customer service, innovation and investment. We continually look for ways to improve the travel experience, from introducing modern, comfortable vehicles to embracing digital technology that helps customers plan and track their journeys more easily. We are also proud of the strong relationships we have with local authorities, businesses, educational institutions and community organisations across the region.

We want to continue leading the way in sustainable public transport by investing in cleaner, greener vehicles and reducing our environmental impact. The introduction of new electric buses is an important step on that journey, helping us create a more sustainable future while maintaining the high standards our customers expect.

We also want to make public transport more inclusive and accessible for everyone. By working closely with customers, accessibility groups and industry partners, we are developing solutions that ensure our services can be used confidently and comfortably by people from all backgrounds and with differing needs.

At the same time, we remain focused on attracting and developing talented people, creating rewarding careers and providing opportunities for the next generation. Our colleagues are at the heart of everything we do, and their passion, expertise and commitment continue to drive the business forward.

Ultimately, our ambition is simple: to be the first choice for travel across the East Midlands. By continuing to invest in our network, our people, our communities and our environment, we will build on our proud heritage while delivering a modern, sustainable and customer-focused transport network for generations to come.
