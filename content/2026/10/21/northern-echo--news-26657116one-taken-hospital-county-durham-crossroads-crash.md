A person has been taken to hospital after a crash at a County Durham crossroads that blocked the road.

Emergency services were called to Waldridge Road in Edmondsley, near Chester-le-Street, at around 8.55am on Wednesday (October 21).

During the incident, the crossroads was closed off to drivers by police.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed it attended the scene.

A spokesperson for NEAS said: "We were called to a road traffic incident in Waldridge near Chester-le-Street at 8.54am on Wednesday 21st October 2026.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew who took one patient to University Hospital of North Durham (UHND)."
