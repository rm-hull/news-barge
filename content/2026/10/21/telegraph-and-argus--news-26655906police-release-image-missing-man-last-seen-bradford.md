Police have released an updated image of a missing man as concern for his welfare continues to grow.

Gareth, 59, was reported missing last week and officers are now renewing their appeal for information about his whereabouts.

West Yorkshire Police said recent enquiries had uncovered a new sighting of Gareth, captured on CCTV at Bradford Interchange on October 12.

## New CCTV sighting identified

The newly released image shows Gareth at the transport hub wearing what police described as an orange-red hoodie with white drawstrings, along with a dark-coloured jumper underneath.

He was also wearing black trousers and black shoes.

Officers believe he may have been carrying two bags, one black and one dark green, with a dark jacket draped over one of them.

## Police increasingly concerned

Police say they are becoming increasingly concerned for Gareth's welfare and are urging anyone who may have seen him to come forward.

Gareth is known to have links to the Brighton area, and officers are asking members of the public to remain vigilant.

## How to help

Anyone who believes they have seen Gareth or has information about where he may be is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police.

Information can be provided by calling 101 or via the Live Chat service on the West Yorkshire Police website, quoting log 1679 of October 13.

Anyone who sees Gareth is urged to contact police immediately.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A WYP spokesperson said: "Our officers in Bradford have issued an updated image of missing Keighley man Gareth as they renew their appeal for information to help locate him.

"Gareth, aged 59, was reported missing last week and officers are increasingly concerned about his welfare.

"Recent enquiries have identified a new last sighting of him, captured on CCTV, at Bradford Interchange on 12 October.

"He appears to be wearing an orange/red hoody with white strings and a dark coloured jumper underneath, black trousers and black shoes.

"He is thought to be carrying two bags, one black and one dark green, with a dark jacket over one of the bags.

"Gareth has links to the Brighton area.

"Anyone who thinks they have seen Gareth or knows of his whereabouts is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online via Live Chat on our website quoting log 1679 of 13/10. "
