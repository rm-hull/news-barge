A primary school in Stockton is set to close to all pupils tomorrow.

Stockton-on-Tees Borough Council has confirmed that Whitehouse Primary School, on Dunelm Road in the Elm Tree Farm area of the town, will be closed on Thursday, October 22.

The closure is due to problems affecting the school's gas supply.

Parents and carers have been informed that the school will not be open to any pupils while the issue is addressed.

The council said there is already a scheduled 'Personal Development' day on Friday, October 23, meaning pupils were not due to attend school on that date.

School leaders are currently hopeful that the issue will be resolved in time for the school to reopen after the half-term holiday.

In a notice issued by Stockton-on-Tees Borough Council, officials said: "Whitehouse Primary School on Dunelm Road, Elm Tree Farm in Stockton, will be closed for all pupils tomorrow, Thursday 22 October, due to problems with the gas supply.

"There is a scheduled PD Day on Friday 23 October, so the school is hoping to reopen on Monday 2 November after the half-term break."

Parents are advised to check for any further updates from the school or the council ahead of the planned reopening date.
