COUNTRY Hits Live is set to bring the atmosphere and energy of an evening in the home of country music to Ilkley.

The event, which promises to be a celebration of country music's greatest hits, will take place at King's Hall this Thursday, October 22.

The show will feature performances from Lars Pluto, UK country star Jade Helliwell, and a multi-award-winning band.

Country Hits Live aims to transport audiences to the heart of country music, with a setlist that spans decades of iconic songs.

Expect to hear tear-jerking classics like 'I Will Always Love You' and 'Always On my Mind', as well as high-energy anthems such as '9-5' and 'Beer Never Broke My Heart'.

With a huge list of tour dates already on sale and festival appearances already booked, Country Hits Live is set to take the theatre world by storm.
