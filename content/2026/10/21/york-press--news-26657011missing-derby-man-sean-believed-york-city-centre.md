## Concern for missing man, 57, last seen at York city centre pub

North Yorkshire Police is urging members of the public to dial 999 immediately if they see this man, named as Sean, who has gone missing in York. *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A MAN in his 50 has gone missing after last being seen in a York pub.

The 57-year-old, who has been named by police simply as Sean, is from Derby but is believed to be in York and staying in or around the city centre.

**Recommended reads:**

North Yorkshire Police is urging anyone who sees him to call 999 immediate, or dial 101 if they have any other details.

A spokesperson said: "The 57-year-old is believed to be in York, and was seen yesterday evening, Tuesday, October 20, in the Golden Fleece pub in Pavement, when this picture was taken of him."

The force is lending support to colleagues from Derbyshire Constabulary who issued the appeal.
