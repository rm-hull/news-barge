The FBI was investigating Wednesday after a “Molotov cocktail-style device” was thrown at a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement building in downtown Atlanta the night before.

The device, which was thrown around 8:15 p.m. Tuesday, caused minor damage, the FBI said in a statement. The investigation was ongoing and agents were “reviewing evidence and following leads.” No arrests had been made.

The building also houses an immigration court.
