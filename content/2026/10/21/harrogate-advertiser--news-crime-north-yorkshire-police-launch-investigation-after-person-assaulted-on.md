# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after person assaulted on major road in Harrogate

## Police launch appeal after person assaulted on major road in Harrogate

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following an assault on a major road in Harrogate.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after a member of the public was assaulted by an unknown man on Knaresborough Road, opposite Co-op Funeralcare, in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/21/9/32/Screenshot-2026-10-21-103948.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,77&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The incident happened between approximately 2pm and 2.30pm on Sunday, October 11 on Knaresborough Road, opposite Co-op Funeralcare, and involved an unknown man assaulting a member of the public.

Officers are carrying out enquiries and are particularly keen to identify the suspect and speak to anyone who witnessed the incident or may have relevant information.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “If you saw what happened, have CCTV, dash cam footage, or any information that could assist the investigation, you should get in touch.”

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260200119 when providing any details regarding the incident.
