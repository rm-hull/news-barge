**A POPULAR fast-food restaurant is set to open tomorrow on the outskirts of York.**

McDonald’s at Bilborough Top - located off the A64 between York and Tadcaster – will reopen its doors tomorrow (October 22) after a refurbishment as part of the brand’s ‘reimagining programme’.

As reported by The Press last week, the restaurant first shut its doors on September 21, with changes made to the design of the eatery to refit the shop to bring it in line with the brand’s long-term vision.

This includes net-zero standards, improving the waste process, and making the brand eco-friendlier.

A spokesperson for McDonald said: “The revamp will improve the experience of both our customers and our restaurant team, and we look forward to welcoming customers back on October 22."
