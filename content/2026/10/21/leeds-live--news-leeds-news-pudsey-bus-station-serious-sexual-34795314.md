# Pudsey bus station serious sexual assault investigation - man in custody

## A police scene remains in place at Pudsey bus station after a woman was reportedly sexually assaulted

A man has been arrested after a serious sexual assault on a woman was reported at Pudsey bus station last night.

Police are investigating the incident, which is reported to have taken place on Tuesday evening (October 20). A scene remains in place at the bus station as officers work to establish the circumstances surrounding the alleged assault.

The man arrested in connection with the incident remains in police custody, while the woman is being supported by specially trained officers.
