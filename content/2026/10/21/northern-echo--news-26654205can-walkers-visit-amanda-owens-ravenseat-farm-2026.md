Amanda Owen's Ravenseat Farm has become one of the most recognisable locations in the Yorkshire Dales thanks to the success of Our Yorkshire Farm and Our Farm Next Door.

The remote Swaledale property, where Amanda and former husband Clive raised their nine children, has attracted interest from television viewers and walkers for years.

## Where is Ravenseat Farm?

The working farm is surrounded by moorland and lies close to routes used by walkers tackling the famous Coast to Coast walk.

Its remote setting has been central to the Owen family's television story.

## Is Ravenseat open to visitors?

Ravenseat is a private working farm and family home, rather than a general visitor attraction.

Visitors should not assume that they can enter private buildings, farmyards or other areas outside designated public routes.

## Can you still get a cream tea?

The Owen family's cream teas have been a popular tradition among walkers passing through the area.

In the latest series of Our Farm Next Door, daughters Raven and Edith are shown preparing the family's tea van for its seasonal return.

Recent reports also suggest that refreshments have returned to Ravenseat.

However, opening arrangements may vary, and visitors should seek up-to-date confirmation before making a special journey.

While the cream teas have only just featured on our TV screens, they have been served since earlier this year when Edith resurrected the van, when the show was filmed.

## Can you walk near Ravenseat?

Yes. The surrounding area offers public walking routes through some of the Yorkshire Dales' most dramatic scenery.

The Coast to Coast route passes through the wider area, with Keld providing a popular stopping point.

Walkers should follow signed public rights of way, respect livestock and observe the Countryside Code.

## What is Amanda Owen filming now?

Amanda, Clive and their children appear in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which follows the restoration of nearby Anty John's farmhouse.

The programme also features everyday life at Ravenseat, including farming, family activities and the children's growing responsibilities.

For visitors inspired by the television series, the surrounding Swaledale landscape remains an opportunity to experience the scenery that made the family's story famous.
