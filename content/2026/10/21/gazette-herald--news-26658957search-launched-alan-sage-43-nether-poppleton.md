POLICE have launched an urgent search for a missing man from a York village.

Alan Sage, 43, was last seen in the Malton area on Monday (October 19).

He is from the Nether Poppleton area and is described as white of heavy build and is 6ft 2 inches tall, with short, light brown hair.




North Yorkshire Police said: “He may be wearing all black clothing.

“He is believed to be travelling in a Grey Volvo XC90 and is known to travel around multiple locations across the county such as Thirsk, Malton and Filey.  
  
“If you see Alan, please call 999. If you have any other information that could help us find him, please call us on 101 and select option 1 to speak to our control room.  
  
“Please quote 12260206777 when sharing information.”
