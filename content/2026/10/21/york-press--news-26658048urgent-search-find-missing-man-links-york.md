## Urgent search launched to find missing man with links to York

Matthew Thompson was last seen at Preston Railway Station *(Image: Lancashire Police)*

North Yorkshire Police has joined an extensive search for a missing man with links to York.

Matthew Thompson was last seen at Preston Railway Station at 2.30pm on October 7.




Lancashire Police, who first launched the appeal, said: “We’re really concerned about his welfare and so we’re asking for your help to bring him home."

![Matthew has links to York](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21613537.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Matthew has links to York *(Image: Lancashire Police)*

They added: “He is around 6ft2 and slight in build, with fair hair, some facial hair and he is usually wearing black.

“He is known to have links to York, London and Belgium.”

If you have any information or think you’ve seen Matthew, please get in touch with police by calling 999 and quoting log number LC-20261019-1017.
