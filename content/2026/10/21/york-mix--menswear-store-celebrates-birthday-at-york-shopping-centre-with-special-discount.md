A popular menswear store at York Designer Outlet is marking its second birthday – and shoppers can join the celebrations with a special discount.

Charles Tyrwhitt has been welcoming customers to its York Designer Outlet store since 2024, offering everything from its famous shirts and smart suits to casual clothing, knitwear and accessories.

Now, two years on, the team is celebrating the milestone **tomorrow (22 October)** by offering customers 20% off as a birthday treat.

The British menswear brand, which was founded in London in 1986, has become well known for its classic styles, quality clothing and smart looks for every occasion.

And the team at York Designer Outlet say the last two years have flown by.

Store supervisor Heather said: “It’s been an absolute whirlwind, but we really do feel right at home here at York Designer Outlet.

“Paul and the team have been fantastic to work with, and we’ve thoroughly enjoyed being part of the centre over the last two years.

“We’d like to say a huge thank you to all our customers for their continued support.

“We can’t wait to welcome everyone back and help them look their best, especially as we head into the festive season.”

The 20% birthday discount is available at Charles Tyrwhitt at York Designer Outlet.

You can find out more about the store on the Charles Tyrwhitt website.
