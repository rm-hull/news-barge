Here's why police in Bradford have released pictures of these four men.

These images are taken from CCTV camera footage and may show suspects or witnesses West Yorkshire Police officers wish to speak to.

Each individual is wanted in connection with a different crime, and police believe identifying them could help progress ongoing inquiries.

Police warn the public not to approach anyone shown in the images and to contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 instead.

![4 wanted faces by police, all male](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21614759.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The image shows, from left to right, top row, person 1 to 2, and then from left to right, bottom row, person 3 to 4. *(Image: WYP)*

## Why are they being sought?

Person 1 is sought in connection with an alleged Criminal Damage in Bradford City on October 10, 2026. Ref: BD9127.

Person 2 is sought in connection with an alleged Theft From Shop in Bradford City on July 15, 2026. Ref: BD9122.

Person 3 is sought in connection with an alleged Assault in Bradford City on September 24, 2026. Ref: BD9120.

Person 4 is sought in connection with an alleged Theft from Vehicle in Bradford City on September 17, 2026. Ref: BD9113.

## **What the police said**

West Yorkshire Police said: "Our CCTV gallery of people who have been caught on camera – do you recognise anyone?

"The identities of those shown are sought in connection with enquiries into the described alleged offences.

"Images may be of both potential suspects or witnesses.

"Members of the public should not approach anyone who they believe to be displayed in the images but should contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111."
