Police are searching for a teenager who has been missing for more than a week.

Lewis Pearson, 17, has been missing from Gateshead since Tuesday, October 13 - and is yet to be found.

Officers from Northumbria Police believe he may be in Stockton, particularly the Roseworth or Hardwick areas.

Lewis was last seen wearing dark tracksuit bottoms, a brown camouflage jacket and a brown baseball cap.

Officers are urging anyone who has seen Lewis or knows where he may be to come forward.

Anyone with information should call 101, quoting reference 210138.
