Harrogate tailor Rhodes Wood has thanked customers for their support as it approaches three decades in business.

The family business marked its first anniversary at its larger store on Prince Albert Row on 18th October, having moved from its original premises on Parliament Street in 2025.

Reflecting on the milestone, Jeremy Wood Beaumont paid tribute to customers, colleagues and his family, with his three sons now working alongside him.

Jeremy said:

> “To our wonderful customers, many of whom have become dear friends over the years, thank you for your loyalty, kindness and continued support. Without you, none of this would have been possible.
>
> “What makes this journey particularly special to me is that my three sons are now working alongside me, bringing their own ideas, enthusiasm and energy to help shape the future of our family business.”

Jeremy also thanked longstanding colleagues and friends Chris, David and Andrew, tailors Otto and Chris, and his original business partner, Mr Rhodes, for their support over the years.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad86985cce0a4c1219.png)

Since opening in Harrogate nearly 30 years ago, Rhodes Wood has developed an international customer base, regularly sending parcels to Iceland, Australia, Europe and the United States.

But Jeremy said the local customers who supported the business from the beginning remain an important part of its story.

He said:

> “Much has changed over the years, but our passion for beautiful clothing, craftsmanship, personal service and, most importantly, the people we meet along the way remains exactly the same.
>
> “Nearly 30 years on, I feel immensely fortunate to be doing something I love, surrounded by family, friends and colleagues who share that passion.
>
> “From the bottom of my heart, thank you all. Here’s to the next chapter of Rhodes-Wood.”
