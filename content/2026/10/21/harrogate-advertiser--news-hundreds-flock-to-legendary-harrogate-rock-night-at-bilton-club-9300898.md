# Iconic Harrogate rock music night is to return after an 'incredible turnout' for emotional 25th anniversary

## "It was all a vast amount of work but I an incredible team helped me and they absolutely delivered”

A legendary Harrogate rock night is expected to return - again - after an “incredible turnout” for an emotional 25th reunion which saw hugs and moshing to hard rock and metal at full volume and velocity.

![Bottom of the Bottle’s 25th anniversary event at The Bilton Club attracted almost 300 music fans in Harrogate.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/21/10/31/BottomoftheBottlecrowd2026BiltonClub.jpeg?trim=116,0,117,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


First launched by the legendary Harrogate DJ in 2001, Bottom of the Bottle’s 25th anniversary event at The Bilton Club attracted almost 300 music fans in Harrogate.

DJ Trev said: "What an incredible night. We got towards 300 people through the doors and from where I was it felt amazing.

"It was all a vast amount of work but I an incredible team helped me and they absolutely delivered.

“It all felt totally worth it."

Delivering choice cuts of Metal, Nu-Metal, Punk, Pop-Punk, Emo, Screamo and more, the energy in the room went beyond nostalgia, even as a giant screen showed vintage footage and photos of past Bottom of the Bottle nights and audiences over the last quarter of a century.

The vibes were so good at last Friday night’s Bottom of the Bottle 25th reunion, DJ Trev is now working on a return event with exciting plans and changes to up the ante on this legendary Harrogate music night.

DJ Trev said: "Attendance wise we sold 208 tickets and had a lot of people pay on the door, so we were getting towards 300 people there.

“It was really a great response. Plans are in motion for next year already."
