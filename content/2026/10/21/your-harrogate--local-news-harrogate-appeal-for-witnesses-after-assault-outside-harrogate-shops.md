Police are appealing for witnesses after a member of the public was assaulted on Knaresborough Road in Harrogate.

The incident happened opposite Co-op Funeralcare between approximately 2pm and 2.30pm on Sunday 11th October.

North Yorkshire Police said an unknown man was involved in the assault.

Officers are carrying out enquiries to identify the suspect and want to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident.

A police spokesperson said:

> “If you saw what happened, have CCTV, dashcam footage, or any information that could assist the investigation, please get in touch with North Yorkshire Police quoting the relevant incident number.”

*Anyone with information can contact police through the ‘Tell us about’ section of the force’s website, selecting ‘An existing case or report’.*

*Alternatively, call 101 and ask for PC Young, collar number 1311. Please quote reference 12260200119.*

*Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*
