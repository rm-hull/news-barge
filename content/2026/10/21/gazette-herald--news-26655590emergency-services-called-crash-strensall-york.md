Emergency services were called to a crash involving one vehicle in York last night (Tuesday, October 20).

The incident happened in Strensall at 10.54pm, with police and firefighters at the scene.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21015077.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and firefighters called to a late-night crash in Strensall. *(Image: Submitted)*

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said members of its Huntington crew ensure the scene was safe.

"Small tools and a thermal image camera were used to assist.

"The incident was then left with police."
