The UK’s biggest record fair is set to take place in York next month.

York Racecourse will host the event on Sunday, November 1, 2026, from 10am to 5pm.

Music lovers can explore two floors packed with around 200,000 records in the Knavesmire Stand.

The fair features more than 70 top sellers, offering a wide variety of LPs and singles, including rare first pressings and recent releases.

The event caters to all musical tastes, with stalls specialising in genres like rock 'n' roll, soul, punk, indie, reggae, metal, jazz, and classical.

Besides records, attendees can find a range of memorabilia, including original posters, music books, and collectible CDs.

Since its inception in 2022, York Record Fair has become a biannual event, attracting collectors from across the UK and beyond.

Admission is £3 on the door, but free for under-16s.

The venue offers free parking and is accessible via a 30-minute walk from York Railway Station.
