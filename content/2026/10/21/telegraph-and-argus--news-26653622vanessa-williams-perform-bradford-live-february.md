Vanessa Williams is bringing her upcoming UK tour to Bradford.

The singer, actress, and Broadway star will perform at Bradford Live on February 26, 2027, as part of her first full concert tour in the UK.

She will be joined by a band of world-class musicians and will perform hits from her 1988 chart debut through to her latest album, Survivor.

Vanessa Williams said: "I am so thrilled to be heading out on my first full concert tour here in the UK.

"This show brings together so many songs and moments that have meant something to me throughout my life, from Broadway and the West End to the records that people have made part of their own stories.

"There’s nothing like the energy of a live audience, and I can’t wait to share the music, the memories, and a few surprises with everyone in February."

The tour will feature songs from her stage career, including Into The Woods, Kiss of the Spiderwoman, and After Midnight, as well as chart-topping singles such as The Right Stuff, Colours of the Wind, The Sweetest Days, and Save The Best For Last.

Williams will also share stories from her decades-long career spanning music, film, television, and theatre.

For tickets, visit: www.fiery-entertainment.com and vanessawilliamslive.co.uk.
