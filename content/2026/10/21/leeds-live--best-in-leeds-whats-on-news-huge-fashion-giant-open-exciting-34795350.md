# Huge fashion giant to open exciting new Leeds city centre store

## The new shop is expected to open next month

A fashion giant will soon open an exciting new store in Leeds city centre.

We have spotted signs on a vacant Victoria Quarter unit. They are promoting the impending launch of a new store from Michael Kors.

The notice simply reads: “Michael Kors - opening soon.” There’s further information about the new shop on the retailer’s website.

It states: “This store is currently under construction and will be opening November 2026.” We will keep you posted on the exciting new opening.
