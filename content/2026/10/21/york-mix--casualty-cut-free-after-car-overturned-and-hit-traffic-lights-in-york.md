Emergency services were called to a bad crash which left a main York road closed last night (Tuesday).

It was on Malton Road between Elmfield Avenue and New Lane.

Police, fire and ambulance crews were called to the collision at 8.34pm.

#### Crash location

The road was closed for some time while they worked at the scene.It closed the road for some time while

Witnesses reported “an overturned car”. Another said: “They’ve crashed into lights. The light looked like it was flat to the floor – it’s been some speed to do that.”

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York and Acomb crews attended a single vehicle road traffic collision.

“Police and ambulance were also on scene.

“Crews used cutting equipment to assist with the safe release of a casualty from within the vehicle. Casualty left in care of ambulance.”
