Award-winning comedian Mick Ferry will headline the second Comedy in the Kitchen night.

The event will take place at Darley Street Market in Bradford on Thursday, November 26, starting at 7pm.

He will be joined by a packed line-up of comedians.

Liam Farrelly, Scottish Comedian of the Year and Live at the Apollo performer, will take to the stage.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21613427.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Liam Farrelly *(Image: Supplied)*

Karen Bayley, Komedia Award winner and Funny Women finalist, will also perform, along with UK Pun Championship winner Robby T, and Bradford’s own Comedy Roast-winning storyteller DeeJay Khalda.

Anthony J Brown will return as the evening’s compere.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21613423.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Compere - Anthony J Brown *(Image: Supplied)*

Cllr Ian Walker, executive member for neighbourhoods and communities at Bradford Council, said: "It's fantastic to see Comedy in the Kitchen returning to Darley Street Market after such a successful debut earlier this year.

"Bringing high-quality live entertainment into the heart of Bradford city centre helps make the market an even more vibrant destination, attracting people into the city and supporting our traders and businesses.

"With a great mix of established acts and local talent, including Bradford's own DeeJay Khalda, this promises to be another fantastic evening for residents and visitors alike."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21613422.jpg?type=mds-article-620)DeeJay Khalda *(Image: Supplied)*

The first Comedy in the Kitchen event, held in July, was a sell-out, with audience members describing it as “great” and the acts as “top drawer.”

Many called for more comedy nights at the market.

Food and drink will be available before the show and during the intervals, giving visitors the chance to enjoy offerings from Darley Street Kitchen’s independent traders.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21613425.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Robby T *(Image: Supplied)*

Mick Ferry, the headline act, is a former winner of the Comedian’s Comedian Award and is known for his deadpan delivery.

He has appeared on Comedy Central’s The Comedy Store, Michael McIntyre’s Comedy Roadshow, and The World Stands Up.

Liam Farrelly has been praised by comedy website Beyond The Joke, which said: "Get used to the Kevin Bridges comparisons."

Karen Bayley, who supported Joe Lycett on tour, was described by Alan Carr as "She has me in stitches."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21613424.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Karen Bayley *(Image: Supplied)*

Anthony J Brown, who will compere the evening, is a Yorkshire Comedy Awards Best Promoter winner.

The Metro described him as having "more stings in the tail than a scorpion with a migraine."
