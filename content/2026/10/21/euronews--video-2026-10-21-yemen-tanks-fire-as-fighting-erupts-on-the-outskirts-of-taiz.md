**Yemeni government forces** and allied Popular Resistance fighters**clashed with Houthi fighters** on the western outskirts of**Taiz** on Wednesday, 21 October, near the city’s old airport and Jabal Han mountain. The internationally recognised government said its forces**repelled the attack**, while reporting** air strikes**on Houthi positions, vehicles and a missile launchpad west, south and east of the city. Those claims** could not be independently verified**.

Footage showed a government tank firing towards Houthi positions as gunfire echoed across the area. The fighting comes as Taiz, Yemen’s third-largest city, faces mounting pressure from Houthi forces, which have **largely encircled it** and**cut key supply routes**, including the** main road to Aden**, the interim capital of the internationally recognised government.

The restrictions have also **disrupted access to the city’s main landfill**, leaving** rubbish to pile up**along major streets. Sanitation workers say they can no longer reach the dumping site because of the clashes and have resorted to** burning waste in the streets**.

Footage showed waste burning beside busy roads, thick smoke rising over neighbourhoods and goats feeding among the rubbish. Aid agencies said **fuel shortages** were also**disrupting water pumps, hospitals and waste collection**, deepening** fears of disease outbreaks**.

*“If we don’t die from Houthi shelling, we’ll die from this rubbish,”* one resident said.
