A NORTH Yorkshire doctors' surgery is to close tomorrow (Thursday).

Derwent Practice, in Norton, will be closed from 12 noon on Thursday, October 22, for staff training.

A spokesperson for Derwent Practice, said: "Please make sure any urgent requests or prescription needs are sorted before the practice closes at midday.

"Thank you for your understanding."
