A HUNDRED calves have been evacuated to safety after a fire in a North Yorkshire barn.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Services from Harrogate and Summerbridge were called to the barn in Blubberhouses at 12.27pm on Wednesday (October 21).




A service spokesperson said: “Crews responded to straw on fire in a barn containing 100 calves.

“No calves were injured and were evacuated safely.

“The fire was measuring two metres by three metres.”
