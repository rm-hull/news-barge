Police are urging anyone who has information about a man last seen in the Malton area to phone 999.

Alan Sage, 43, is from Nether Poppleton, York. He is described as a white man of heavy build, 189cm tall, with short, light brown hair. He may be wearing all-black clothing.

He is believed to be travelling in a grey Volvo XC90 and may visit locations including Thirsk, Malton and Filey.

Anyone who sees Alan should call 999. Information should be reported to police on 101, selecting option 1. Case reference 12260206777.
