President Donald Trump’s Oval Office outburst at Andrew Feinberg, *The Independent*’s White House correspondent, triggered a wave of online fury.

During a Tuesday press gaggle, Trump repeatedly lashed out at Feinberg as he attempted to ask questions, warning him to “be careful,” demanding that he be “quiet” and labeling him a “sick person.”

“Wait, wait, wait, wait. Quiet, quiet!” the 80-year-old president said from behind the Resolute Desk. “What’s wrong with you? Are you a sick person? I actually think there’s something wrong with you.”

The exchange prompted an outpouring of support - and some criticism - for Feinberg and *The Independen* t across numerous social media platforms. Here is some of what you thought about the president’s meltdown:
