Two North East firms have picked up national business awards in recognition of their work developing new technology and investing in future talent.

Care Messenger Holdings and Osbit, both members of the North East Chamber of Commerce, were named winners at the British Chambers of Commerce (BCC) Chamber Business Awards 2026.

The awards celebrate excellence in digital innovation and workforce development across the UK.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21604872.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Haviland, Director General, British Chambers of Commerce, Wayne Muter, Creative Solutions Director and Paul Slaughter, CEO, Care Messenger Holdings *(Image: PHILIP J.A BENTON)*

John McCabe, chief executive of the North East Chamber of Commerce, said: "To see two of our members recognised as national winners for the second year running is a fantastic achievement and a real reflection of the talent, innovation and ambition we have here in the North East.

"Care Messenger Holdings and Osbit are very different businesses, but both demonstrate what can be achieved through investing in people, embracing new ideas and finding practical solutions to real challenges.

"We're incredibly proud to see their achievements recognised nationally and congratulate both teams on this well-deserved success."

Care Messenger Holdings received the Digital Revolution Award for its Chatta TV platform, which helps older and vulnerable people stay connected with their families and care providers through their televisions—without needing smartphones, tablets, or apps.

Paul Slaughter, CEO of Care Messenger Holdings, said: "We are proud to receive the Digital Revolution Award.

"Chatta TV was created to ensure older and vulnerable people are not left behind by digital change.

"This recognition reflects years of development, testing, and partnership working, but this isn’t the end of the story; we plan to take simple, inclusive communication to more families and care settings."

Offshore engineering firm Osbit won the Future of Work Award for its commitment to developing talent and supporting the next generation of engineers.

The company has invested in apprenticeships, university placements, and continuous professional development.

Last year, Osbit employees dedicated hundreds of hours to STEM engagement, reaching more than 5,800 young people.

Nigel Ward, managing director of Osbit, said: "We are delighted to receive the Future of Work Award.

"Our people are central to Osbit’s success, our team have worked hard to create an environment where individuals can learn quickly, take responsibility, and contribute ideas.

"This award belongs to our whole team and reinforces our commitment to developing engineering talent and creating rewarding careers in the North East."

This marks the second consecutive year that North East Chamber of Commerce members have been named national winners at the BCC Chamber Business Awards.
