**Sam Fender will play Edinburgh's Scottish Gas Murrayfield Stadium next summer as part of what has been billed as his biggest headline tour to date.**

The Geordie singer-songwriter has announced a run of UK and Irish stadium shows for 2027, including two nights at London's Wembley Stadium, two hometown dates at Newcastle's St James' Park and a stop in the Scottish capital on July 8.

**Read More: Sam Fender announces huge Scottish Edinburgh Murrayfield gig 2027**

The tour, dubbed The Proper Massive Stadium Tour, will see Fender perform in six cities across the UK and Ireland, culminating in two nights at Manchester's Etihad Stadium later that month.

The Edinburgh concert is part of a huge summer for the 33-year-old musician, who will also headline Belgium's Rock Werchter festival for the first time.

**Read More:**

British alt-rock band Wunderhorse will support the Murrayfield show and join Fender across all UK stadium dates.

Tickets for the Edinburgh concert and the wider tour go on general sale at 9.30am on October 30, with a fan pre-sale scheduled to begin two days earlier.

Fender's latest album, People Watching, became a chart-topping success following its release and earned him the Mercury Prize, adding to a growing collection of BRIT and Ivor Novello awards.
