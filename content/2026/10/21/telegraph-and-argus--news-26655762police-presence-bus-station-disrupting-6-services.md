A police presence has been seen at a bus station.

Police tape has been put around a stand at Pudsey Bus Station this morning.

Passengers are facing disruption after two bus stands were closed, with several services forced to use alternative stops.

According to a travel update shared by Metro, stands A and B at Pudsey Bus Station are currently closed, affecting services 4, 4F, 14, 16, 91 and X11.

Passengers travelling on those routes have been advised to use the next available stands.

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted British Transport Police for further information and comment.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21610315.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The wider scene *(Image: T&A)*

## Passengers advised to check before travelling

Metro apologised for the disruption and advised passengers to make use of alternative stands while the issue is ongoing.

Anyone planning to travel through Pudsey Bus Station this morning is advised to check for the latest updates before setting off.

More information will be added to this story as soon as it becomes available.
