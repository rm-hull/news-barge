Everyone has their favourite fish and chip shop, whether it's the one you grew up with, a Friday night regular, or somewhere recommended by loved ones; chippy loyalties run deep.

That's certainly true in Consett, where one family-run takeaway has spent almost two decades building a loyal following among local customers.

Located on Durham Road in Blackhill, Blackhill Fish & Chips has been in the same family for 18 years and has become a popular choice for residents looking for freshly cooked fish, generous portions and all the chip shop favourites.

Over the years, the business has earned a strong reputation within the community, attracting hundreds of online reviews and being named among the best-rated fish and chip shops in Consett in 2024.

While fish and chips remain at the heart of the menu, customers can also choose from a range of burgers, kebabs, pizzas and homemade curry sauce.

For the family behind the business, the focus has always been on serving traditional fish and chips made using quality ingredients and cooked fresh to order.

That approach appears to have struck a chord with customers, who regularly praise the takeaway's crispy batter, generous portions and friendly service in online reviews.

On TripAdvisor, diners have described the food as "absolutely delicious" and singled out the quality of both the fish and the popular curry sauce.

The takeaway has also maintained a five-star food hygiene rating, further strengthening its reputation among regular customers.

Fish and chips remain one of Britain's best-loved meals, but independent takeaways continue to face challenges from rising costs and changing consumer habits.

Despite this, longstanding family businesses such as Blackhill Fish & Chips continue to play an important role in communities across County Durham.

For many customers, the appeal is simple: a freshly cooked fish and chip supper served by a business that has been part of the local community for nearly 20 years.

In Blackhill, that straightforward formula continues to prove successful.

With 18 years of family ownership, glowing reviews and a loyal customer base, Blackhill Fish & Chips has established itself as one of Consett's go-to destinations for a classic chippy tea.
