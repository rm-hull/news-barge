A Halloween-themed concert is coming to Bradford's St George's Hall.

The show, named Halloween In Concert, will take place on October 28.

The event will feature a "frighteningly good night of graveyard smash hits for all the family".

The show will feature a live band, visuals, and state-of-the-art production.

Attendees are encouraged to arrive in costume, with a prize for the best costume.

The concert will feature "some of the greatest spooky hits ever recorded", including Monster Mash, Highway to Hell, The Time Warp, Running Up That Hill, Hungry Like the Wolf, Maneater, Bat Out of Hell, It’s a Kind of Magic, She Wolf, and Thriller.

The show is suitable for those aged 10 and over.

Tickets are priced between £31 and £37.
