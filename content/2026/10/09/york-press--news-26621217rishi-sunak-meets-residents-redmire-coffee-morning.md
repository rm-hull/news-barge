Rishi Sunak has met residents in a North Yorkshire village as part of a series of community visits across his constituency.

The former Prime Minister and Richmond and Northallerton MP attended a coffee morning at Redmire Village Hall, where he spoke with more than 40 residents from Redmire and Castle Bolton.

Mr Sunak said: "It was great to spend time with so many residents from Redmire and to have the chance to sit down and chat with people.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21551235.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rishi Sunak meets more than 40 residents from Redmire and Castle Bolton at a coffee morning *(Image: Supplied)*

"I always value the opportunity to come into local communities, hear what people have to say and catch up with residents.

"Thank you to Anne and everyone who came along and made it such an enjoyable morning."

The event was organised by Anne Day, secretary of Redmire Village Hall, who welcomed residents.

Ms Day said: "It was a privilege and a pleasure to welcome our constituency MP, Rishi Sunak, to Redmire Village Hall.

"He came to give local residents of Redmire and Castle Bolton an opportunity to meet and chat with him, which he certainly did as he went round all the tables."

The coffee morning is part of a series of similar events Mr Sunak has been holding across the constituency to engage directly with residents.

It also served as a chance for neighbours to catch up and spend time together at the village hall.
