A person was taken to hospital after a crash in Redcar that saw the road cordoned off last night.

Emergency services were called to West Dyke Road at around 7.15pm on Thursday (October 7), following reports of a crash.

Police, ambulance crews and firefighters all attended the scene, and the road was cordoned off.

The North East Ambulance Service has confirmed that one person was taken to hospital.

The road has since reopened to traffic.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic collision on West Dyke Road in Redcar at 7.15pm on Thursday 8th October 2026.

"We dispatched a clinical team leader and an ambulance crew who took one patient to a local hospital."
