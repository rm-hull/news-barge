A VEHICLE ended up on its side following a crash in a North Yorkshire village.

The county's fire and rescue service was called at 5.26pm last night (Thursday, October 8) to the crash in Ampleforth and involved two other cars which were parked with nobody inside.

A North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue spokesperson said: "All occupants were out of the vehicle on arrival of the crews and no fire service action was required.

"Incident was left with Police and Ambulance."
