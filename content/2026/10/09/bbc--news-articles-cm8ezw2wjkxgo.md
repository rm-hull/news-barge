**This paper review contains spoilers from the third episode of The Celebrity Traitors, series two.**

!["Stark warning over rising demand for NHS autism and ADHD care" reads the headline on the front page of the Guardian.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/624/cpsprodpb/17b9/live/63bb56c0-c35e-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

Several of today's papers lead with the government's review of ADHD and autism diagnoses. A "stark warning over rising demand for NHS autism and ADHD care" tops the Guardian. Elsewhere, it reports that two Latvians have been arrested after they were "found inside RAF base". And, after it was revealed that the three sisters who died in the Brighton sea had taken their own lives, the paper describes their "final pilgrimage".

!["ADHD label risks 'harm for life' for youngsters" reads the headline on the front page of the Times.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/eb6f/live/44049850-c35e-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Times also leads with the report, highlighting one of its conclusions - that an ADHD label risks causing "harm for life" to young people. In defence news, following the series of arrests at UK air bases, the Times adds that the country's most senior military officer "insists" the bases are secure.

!["ADHD and autism review calls for medication and benefits shake-up as diagnoses surge" reads the headline on the front page of The i Paper.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/bb53/live/a2d02720-c370-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

The i Paper has picked up that the ADHD and autism review "calls for medication and benefits shake-up as diagnoses surge". It also notes that it has found mental health conditions are not overdiagnosed "at scale".

!["Rush to diagnose autism can damage children" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Mail. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/89c2/live/9d8ee9c0-c368-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Daily Mail also headlines on the government report on ADHD and autism, noting the "toll on those wrongly given label". It carries a picture of the Duchess of Sussex with Malala Yousafsai, saying the pair had a "cosy Claridge's tea".

!["Andrew wins legal fight over police raid on his homes" reads the headline on the front page of the Independent. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/89dc/live/ff803ce0-c36a-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Independent leads with Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor after he "wins legal fight over police raid on his homes". The High Court has ruled a "significant error" was made in the application for a warrant to search his residences, it writes.

!["Andy cops: no sweat" reads the headline on the front page of the Sun.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/ac90/live/f6fcf810-c35b-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

This is "no sweat" for the "Andy cops", according to the Sun. It says Thames Valley Police will continue their investigation into Mountbatten-Windsor after the "warrant gaffe". The picture of the former prince looking alarmed while lying back in a car after being released from custody is splashed across its front.

!["Bank chief: Don't risk repeat of Truss chaos" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/79c7/live/48f8c0c0-c35e-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

The Daily Telegraph has spoken to friends of Mountbatten-Windsor who say his "fightback starts now". A warning from the Governor of the Bank of England not to "risk repeat of Truss chaos" heads up the Daily Telegraph's front page ahead of the Budget. The paper also reports that Conservative Party leader Kemi Badenoch's name appeared "on list at home of Widdecombe murder suspect".

!["It's not over" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/898e/live/a1a26aa0-c372-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Daily Mirror says "Andrew cops blunder but.. it's not over". A picture of Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband hugging a child in Ukraine is captioned on his "fury at Putin".

!["Failed by a system that should protect you" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Express.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/f9b1/live/f9db7c60-c35a-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Daily Express leads with coverage of the early prisoner release scheme, saying domestic abuse campaigners "slam" the policy. "Failed by a system that should protect you" reads its headline, writing that survivors of domestic abuse are "living in terror".

!["Not 100% faithful" reads the headline on the front page of the Metro.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/62fb/live/793140e0-c35a-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

The Metro headlines with "not 100% faithful", writing that a Royal Navy sailor has appeared in court after "allegedly plotting to pass British secrets to Russia". Teddy Young, a specialist in warfare intelligence, has been "accused of being a spy".

!["OpenAI's $20bn cut to annualised revenues damps investor optimism" reads the headline on the front page of the Financial Times.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/48ac/live/1ec2a8f0-c35b-11f1-bc2e-018d645d8d21.jpg)

Investor optimism in OpenAI has been dampened, writes the FT, citing a "$20bn cut to annualised revenues" as the source of discontent. It also covers the bus bombing in Kramatorsk, Ukraine yesterday, in which a Russian strike killed "dozens".

!["You Only Drink Twice" reads the headline on the front page of the Daily Star.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/9e04/live/96fbbfb0-c35a-11f1-bc2e-018d645d8d21.jpg)

The Daily Star headlines with "You Only Drink Twice" as it says James Bond actor Daniel Craig has bought a pub. The paper also trails some spoilers for the third episode of Celebrity Traitors.
