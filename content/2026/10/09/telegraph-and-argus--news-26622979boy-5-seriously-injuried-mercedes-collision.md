A five-year-old boy has been seriously injured after being involved in a collision with a Mercedes.

The collision happened at around 5.42pm on Thursday, October 8, on Red Holt Drive in Keighley.

It involved a black Mercedes Vito.

The child was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

Police have confirmed they have spoken to the driver.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Our Roads Policing Unit is appealing for witnesses and footage following a serious injury collision in Keighley.

"It happened in Red Holt Drive at about 5.42pm yesterday (Thursday) and involved a black Mercedes Vito car and a five-year-old boy.

"The child was taken to hospital, where he was found to have suffered serious injuries.

"The driver was spoken to by officers.

"Our Roads Policing Unit is investigating this incident and is appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision to contact them, either by calling 101 or using our LiveChat facility on the website."

Anyone with information is urged to contact police on 101 or via the force’s LiveChat service, quoting log 1181 of October 8.
