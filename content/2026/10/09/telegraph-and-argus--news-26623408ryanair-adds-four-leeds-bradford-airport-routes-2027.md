Ryanair has added four new routes from Leeds Bradford Airport.

The airline's first 29 summer 2027 routes are now available to book on its website and app.

Ryanair will offer more than 2,500 routes to 230 destinations across its network next summer.

The schedule will offer travel options to more than 214 million customers a year across Europe.

## What are the new destinations?

The four Leeds Bradford Airport destinations are Budapest, Tirana, Corfu and Bari.

A spokesperson for LBA said: "Exciting news. We’re delighted to announce four new Ryanair routes launching next summer.

"Passengers will be able to fly from LBA to Budapest, Tirana, Corfu, and Bari, as part of the 82 destinations we’ll be offering for Summer 2027.

"With more destinations on offer than ever before, we’re already getting ready for our biggest summer ever."

Ryanair CMO Dara Brady said: “Ryanair is pleased to launch the first 29 new routes of what is set to be a bumper summer 2027 schedule, giving our cost-savvy customers an early opportunity to start planning next year's getaways.”

“These are the first of our new summer 2027 routes to go on sale, with more exciting additions to be announced as Ryanair continues to expand Europe's largest route network.”
