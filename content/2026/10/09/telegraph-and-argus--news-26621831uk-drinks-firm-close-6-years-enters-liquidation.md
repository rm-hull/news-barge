A UK-based drinks company is set to close after six years in business, having fallen into liquidation.

Straight and Narrow Drinks, established in October 2020, produced low-calorie, alcohol-free beverages made with natural ingredients and free from artificial colours and flavourings.

Customers regularly praised the company's drinks on Trustpilot, with one reviewer describing them as "brilliant".

Another fan on Trustpilot added: "Love, love, love these products.

"Bought the mixed case and every flavour is delicious. Love that there is no alcohol but no compromise on taste."

## Straight and Narrow Drinks to close after falling into liquidation

Despite the positive reception, Straight and Narrow Drinks has now entered liquidation and is set to close.

Nick West and Craig Povey of BTG Begbies Traynor (Central) LLP were appointed as joint liquidators on September 30, according to The Gazette.

Companies House records show that Straight and Narrow Drinks has also been served with a winding-up notice.

The notice states: "The Registrar of Companies gives notice that, unless cause is shown to the contrary, the Company will be struck off the register and dissolved not less than 2 months from the date shown above [September 29].

"Upon the Company's dissolution, all property and rights vested in, or held in trust for, the Company are deemed to be bona vacantia, and will belong to the Crown."

Accounts for the year up to October 2025 are overdue, according to Companies House.
