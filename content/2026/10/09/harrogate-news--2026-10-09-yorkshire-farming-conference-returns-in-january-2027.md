The Yorkshire Farming Conference will return to the Great Yorkshire Events Centre on Wednesday 27 January 2027, following an inaugural event attended by more than 700 farmers and agricultural workers.

Organised by the Yorkshire Agricultural Society (YAS), the conference will bring together people from across the farming industry for discussions on agricultural issues.

Farming content creators Tom Carlisle and Rebecca Wilson will lead a social media session, while Clare Wise, chair of the NFU Livestock Board, will be among the session chairs.

The inaugural conference also featured a trade show supported by more than 60 businesses and organisations.

Tickets for the 2027 conference will go on sale on Monday 19 October. Further details are available from the Yorkshire Farming Conference website.
