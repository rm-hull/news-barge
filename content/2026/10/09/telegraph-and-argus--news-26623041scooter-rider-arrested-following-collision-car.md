A scooter rider has been arrested after colliding with a car on Harrogate Road in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police said: "This appears to be a fail to stop involving a scooter, which has then collided with a car."

Police said: "Rider arrested and no injuries reported."

The incident happened this afternoon, and officers confirmed no injuries were reported. The rider was arrested after the collision, which involved the scooter failing to stop before striking the car.
