A vehicle crashed into parked cars in a quiet North Yorkshire village last night (Thursday).

Emergency services were called to the incident at **Ampleforth** at**5.26pm**.

A fire crew from Kirkbymoorside went to the scene.

#### Ampleforth

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The incident involved three vehicles, two of which were unoccupied and parked.

“The other vehicle had come to rest on its side. All occupants were out of the vehicle on arrival of the crews and no fire service action was required.

“Incident was left with police and ambulance.”
