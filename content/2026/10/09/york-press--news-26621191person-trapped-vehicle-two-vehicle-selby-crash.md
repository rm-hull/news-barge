A PERSON has been trapped in a vehicle after a crash in a North Yorkshire town.




The county's fire and rescue service said crews from Selby and Tadcaster were called to an incident involving two vehicles in Wistow Road, Selby at 7.41pm yesterday (Thursday, October 8).

The person was released by fire crews and ambulance service staff.

A North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said two other people were out of the vehicles when crews arrived.

The spokesperson said: "Crews made the vehicles and scene safe and two casualties were taken to hospital for precautionary checks."
