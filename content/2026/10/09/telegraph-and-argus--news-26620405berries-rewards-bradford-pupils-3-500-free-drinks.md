A drinks company has given out approximately 3,500 free drink vouchers to pupils as part of a school reward scheme.

Berries launched its School Reward Initiative in September, pledging to give away 10,000 complimentary drinks to primary school pupils across Bradford during the academic year.

So far, 52 schools have signed up to the scheme, which rewards academic achievement, effort, improved attendance, kindness and positive behaviour.

Raza Zulfiqar, speaking on behalf of Berries, said: "When we launched, we hoped schools would see this as a useful way to celebrate their pupils.

"To have 52 schools involved already is fantastic.

"Bradford has supported Berries as we’ve grown.

"We want to keep giving back and building relationships with the schools and organisations supporting local families."

The scheme has also drawn interest from other organisations, including the Bradford Children and Families Trust, which has been sent 100 vouchers.

Schools and community organisations can register their interest by emailing [info@berriesdrinks.co.uk.](mailto:info@berriesdrinks.co.uk.)
