Emergency services were called to a two-vehicle crash in Selby last night (Thursday).

It was on **Wistow Road** at**7.41pm**.

Firefighters from Selby and Tadcaster went to the incident.

#### Wistow Road, Selby

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Two people were out on arrival of the crews and one person was extricated by ambulance and fire crews.

“Crews made the vehicles and scene safe and two casualties were taken to hospital for precautionary checks.”
