Motorists in Bradford could save money by checking fuel prices on Google Maps before travelling.

Concerns about rising fuel costs have grown in recent months following conflict in the Middle East.

Higher global oil prices have led to warnings that forecourt prices could increase across the UK.

## Where is the highest fuel price in Bradford?

The highest listed diesel price in Bradford was 199.9p at Murco on Killinghall Road.

Shell on Rooley Lane had the highest petrol price, at 177.9p.

## Prime Minister Announcement

Andy Burnham has highlighted a new Google Maps feature allowing drivers to compare prices before setting off.

In a social media post, he said he understood concerns about rising costs. He also said the tool could help motorists find the cheapest nearby petrol station and save money at the pump.
