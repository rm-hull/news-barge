A road in Wilberfoss is set to close temporarily, beginning next month.

Bolton Lane will be closed from the A1079 Hull Road junction to the Newbridge Lane, Yapham junction on November 6, 2026.

The road will be shut from 8am to 4pm, with the closure in place for patching works.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council said the closure is to keep the public safe.

The order allowing the closure is valid for 18 months, but the council believes the work will be done in one day.

While the road is closed, traffic will be redirected via Newbridge Lane, Yapham, Sand Lane, Wilberfoss, A1079 Hull Road, and Bolton Lane, Newton Upon Derwent.

Signs will be in place to show the diversion route.

The road will be closed to all through traffic, but residents, emergency services, and pedestrians will still be able to use it.

For more information, people can call the council on (01482) 393939 and ask for Highways Customer Care - Ref: CB.
