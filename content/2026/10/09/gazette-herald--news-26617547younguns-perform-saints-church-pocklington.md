An award-winning folk trio is set to perform in Pocklington later this year.

The Young'uns, three-time BBC Radio 2 Folk Award winners, will play at All Saints Church on Tuesday, December 22, at 7.30pm.

Tickets are £25 and can be purchased from theyounguns.co.uk.

The group, comprising Sean Cooney, Michael Hughes, and David Eagle, has gained popularity for their mix of "glorious singing, heartfelt storytelling, and quick-fire humour".

The trio, who have roots in Teesside, have toured extensively throughout Britain and internationally, performing in various venues from village halls to concert halls and festivals.

Their discography includes albums like Strangers, which won a BBC Folk Award, and Tiny Notes.

The Young'uns have also produced a theatre production based on their album The Ballad of Johnny Longstaff, which tells the story of a young Teessider who fought fascism in the 1930s.

The show has toured both nationally and internationally.
