If you enjoy stunning views of the countryside or enjoy some classic literature, you might want to take a trip to the Brontë Waterfall.

Found just outside the pretty village of Haworth, the waterfall is worth a visit for an autumnal walk.

The waterfall has links to the Brontë sisters, as they spent a lot of time visiting this spot, which is said to have had a big influence on their writing.

It is said that a ruined farmhouse called Top Withens near the waterfall is said to be Heathcliff’s home in Wuthering Heights.

## What is the Brontë Waterfall like?

The waterfall is said to have “jaw-dropping” views around it with different walking trails you can follow.

There are several car parks where you can start your walk from, including Penistone Hill Country Park and the Parsonage Museum car park.

There is a walking trail that takes you from Haworth to the waterfall, and it is well-signposted along the way.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21555592.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There are a few trails you can follow. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

After admiring the views and beautiful waterfall, you can simply turn around and follow the same trail back.

If you want a bit more variety on your walk, you can follow the Brontë Falls Trail, which is a circular route.

As well as the waterfall, on this walk, you will see the Brontë Bride and parts of the Lower Laithe Reservoir.

After exploring the waterfall, you can easily head back to Haworth to enjoy the cosy town and check out its other links to the literary sisters.

### **What do reviews say about the Brontë Waterfall?**

The Brontë Waterfall has a rating of 4.4 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 363 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “What a beautiful place.

“Easy to get to and the local pubs are incredibly friendly so please support.”

Another person said: “Parked at penistone Hill Country Park and walked to the Bridge.

“The path is well signposted and mostly easy to walk there are just a few places with rocks/stones and a few puddles even in August.

“The surrounding area is beautiful with fantastic views and lots of purple heather.

“The waterfall is only small and fairly shallow, there were plenty of dragonflies around.”
