Ryanair has announced two new flight routes from the North East as part of its early release of summer 2027 holiday plans.

The airline will begin flights from Newcastle Airport to Reus in Spain and Rhodes in Greece from Sunday, March 28, 2027, with both routes operating six times a week.

Ryanair launched the first 29 new routes of its Summer 2027 schedule, giving cost-savvy customers the chance to get ahead of the flock and bag themselves a bargain early bird fare.

The new routes are part of a wider network expansion, with Ryanair planning to operate more than 2,500 routes across 230 destinations in summer 2027.

Additional routes announced include Leeds Bradford to Corfu, Norwich to Malaga, and East Midlands to Gdansk.

Ryanair CMO, Dara Brady, said: “Ryanair is pleased to launch the first 29 new routes of what is set to be a bumper Summer 2027 schedule, giving our cost-savvy customers an early opportunity to start planning next year’s getaways.

"These are the first of our new Summer 2027 routes to go on sale, with more exciting additions to be announced as Ryanair continues to expand Europe’s largest route network.

"Customers looking to secure the lowest fares for their Summer 2027 travel should head to the Ryanair app/website and book now to get ahead of the curve.”
