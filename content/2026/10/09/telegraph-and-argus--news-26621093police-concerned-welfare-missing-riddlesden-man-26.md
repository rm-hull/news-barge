Police are concerned for the welfare of a missing 26-year-old man from the Bradford district.

Stephen, 26, who is missing from Riddlesden in Keighley, was last seen at around 7.30pm on Thursday, October 8.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Stephen is 26-years-old and missing from Riddlesden, Keighley.

"He was last seen at approximately 7.30pm on Thursday, October 8.

"There are concerns for his welfare."

"He is a white male, 5ft 6 tall, of stocky build, he has mousey brown/light brown short hair with stubble and a moustache.

"He was last seen wearing a dark Levi's hoody with Levi written in white, black jeans and black trainers.

"Any sightings or information please call 101 and quote log 1648 08/10/2026."
