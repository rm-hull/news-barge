A popular pub has reopened its doors following a three-week refurbishment.

The Armytage, in Brighouse, has undergone a top-to-bottom revamp, with updated dining and bar areas, new furnishings and improvements to its outdoor space.

The venue, on Armytage Road, welcomed customers back this week, with management saying the refurbishment has given the pub a fresh new feel while retaining its community atmosphere.

## Fresh look for popular local pub

The refurbishment includes a redesigned dining space and modernised bar area, alongside updates throughout the pub.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21557971.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The team at The Armytage as the pub welcomed customers back after its refurbishment. *(Image: Leopard co.)*

Work has also been carried out in the beer garden, where the decking has been upgraded and new lighting installed to help make the most of views across the surrounding countryside.

Management said the changes are designed to enhance the experience for both regular customers and first-time visitors.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21557965.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Armytage's refurbished dining area following a three-week makeover. *(Image: Leopard co.)*

## Favourite dishes remain on the menu

Alongside its new look, the pub is continuing to serve a range of popular menu items, including the Dirty Clucker, Crispy Sweet Chilli Chicken and Pork Tomahawk.

Desserts such as the Caramelised Waffle also remain available.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21557970.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The newly refurbished exterior of The Armytage in Brighouse. *(Image: Leopard co.)*

## 'We've kept everything guests love'

Sarah McDougall, general manager at The Armytage, said: "We couldn't be more excited to show off the pub's new look.

"It's been amazing seeing guests walk in and instantly feel at home.

"Whether you're popping in for a meal, grabbing a bite with the kids, or just catching up over a drink, we've kept everything you love about the place with a bit of extra sizzle."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21557968.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The revamped bar area at The Armytage in Brighouse. *(Image: Leopard co.)*

## Packed calendar of events

The pub will continue to host a range of regular events, including its pub quiz, which takes place every other Thursday.

Live music and disco nights are also planned throughout the year, bringing together customers from across the community.

The Armytage is part of the Sizzling Pubs chain, which operates more than 200 pubs across the UK.
