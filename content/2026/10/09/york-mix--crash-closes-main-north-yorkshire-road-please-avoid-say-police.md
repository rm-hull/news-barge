A crash closed a main road in Ryedale this morning (Friday).

It was on the A169 at Saltergate between Pickering and Whitby.

Police wer at the scene as drivers report very slow traffic.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said at 7.27am: “The A169 is currently closed between Saltergate Bank and Lockton in Ryedale while the emergency services deal with a collision.

“Please avoid the area until further notice.

“If you are already on the route, please follow the diversions.”

The road has now reopened and traffic is back to normal.
