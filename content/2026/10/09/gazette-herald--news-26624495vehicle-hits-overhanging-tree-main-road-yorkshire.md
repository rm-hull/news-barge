An overhanging tree has been removed from a North Yorkshire road after being hit by a vehicle.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crew removed the tree on the B6165 at Scotton, near Knaresborough, on Friday afternoon (October 9).

A fire crew was called to the road about 12.45pm after the tree was hit by a passing vehicle.

The fire service said its crew removed the hanging branches and made the scene safe.
