## French students have “retaken the Bastille”, says Jean-Luc Mélenchon, after a mass rally in Paris. Volodymyr Zelenskyy accuses the US of “unwillingness” to help Ukraine. And former New Zealand PM Helen Clark joins us on critical minerals as MEP Rima Hassan faces fallout over flags row.

## On today's show:

**Emma Rafowicz**, French Socialist MEP, discusses the student protests that culminated in a mass gathering at the Place de la Bastille, after** Jean-Luc Mélenchon** declared "the Bastille has been retaken", and whether France is heading for a debt crisis.

**Helen Clark**, former New Zealand prime minister and outgoing chair of the Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative, joins the show as more than 1,000 delegates from over 50 countries gather in Brussels amid pressure on energy security and the scramble for critical raw materials.

**Yulia Klymenko**, member of the Ukrainian parliament, discusses** Volodymyr Zelenskyy**'s criticism of US "unwillingness" to help Ukraine, ahead of talks in Miami with** Jared Kushner**and** Steve Witkoff**, and the Russian glide-bomb strike that killed at least 33 civilians in Kramatorsk.

**Maia de la Baume** speaks to**Rima Hassan**, the French MEP of Palestinian origin, about the flags row in Strasbourg and the European Parliament procedure that could follow.
