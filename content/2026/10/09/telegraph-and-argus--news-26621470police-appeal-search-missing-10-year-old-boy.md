A search is under way for a missing 10-year-old boy.

Kaiden was last seen in the Chickenley area of Dewsbury on the evening of October 8, according to West Yorkshire Police.

He is described as white, around 4ft tall, of medium build, with short ginger-blond hair.

He was wearing a dark blue Hype puffer style bomber jacket, grey jogging bottoms, and silver Adidas trainers.

Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting log 1759 08/10/2026.
