* **The rumored name for Samsung's first over-ears in a decade was spotted**
* **They may be called Galaxy Studio, according to a leak and a trademark**
* **And also, they'll give Samsung's various audio brands something to do**

For almost a decade, Samsung has been the proud owner of a who's-who of legacy audio brands, and it finally looks like it's ready to do something with them. A potential AirPods Max 2 rival, possibly called the Galaxy Studio, is rumored to be on the way.

That Samsung has some over-ears in the works has been leaked before, but people are finally taking the rumors seriously now because of the mention of an actual name. These fresh leads stem from a leaker on Twitter called @evowizz, who also successfully leaked the Pixel 11 Pro Fold. It is they who gave us the Galaxy Studio moniker.

Now, the title 'Studio' might suggest professional uses for these headphones. Maybe they're made for music mixing and/or critical listening? This is Samsung though, a tech giant that typically sticks to the consumer product realm to support its ecosystem, so perhaps the name was simply chosen because it makes them sound premium.

In fact, Samsung trademarked this name a whole decade ago, and re-filed it in mid-September. So the Galaxy Studio is sounding increasingly likely. And with the brand's recent push into new audio form factors, including the Galaxy Buds On, it makes the new cans seem increasingly likely.

There's no indication of price, feature set or release date just yet. Some fans are hoping for a 2027 release date, so maybe they'll come alongside the Galaxy S27, Galaxy Z Fold 9s or another release that year. But it's impossible to say for sure.

## It's an audio party and everyone's invited

![Bowers &amp;amp; Wilkins PX8 over-ears in hand, on white background](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/5JxAEKegm8bUqcDR8qwuCL-1200-80.jpg)

Samsung bought Harman International in November 2016, which means the likes of Harman Kardon, JBL and AKG are all in its trophy cabinet. A later acquisition of Masimo's audio brands in late 2025 means that Bowers & Wilkins, Denon and Marantz are also on its roster.

Despite all that knowledge and R&D on its shelves, the brand has been content releasing in-ears only, including the Galaxy Buds 4 and Galaxy Buds 4 Pro. We haven't seen any over-ears since the likes of the Samsung Level On Pro, back when ANC was a new novelty, rather than a mandatory feature for any new wireless over-ears.

The shame is that some of the Harman International brands have languished recently. JBL and Bowers & Wilkins still push out glorious over-ears regularly, but Harman Kardon now seems to exist solely as a brand which tunes others' headphones, and when was the last time we saw a major AKG release? These are legacy audiophile brands, seemingly going to waste.

Hi-fi fans have been begging for an proper AKG revival for a long time, and the Samsung Galaxy Studio won't be that — they won't be designed and honed for audiophiles in the same way that AKG's cans might be.

But if there's even a small splash of juice from all of its well-known names, the Studio could turn out to be a pretty tasty smoothie. For now, we wait…
