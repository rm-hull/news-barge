Individuals with claims against the estate of the late Robert Wilfred Hills must submit them by December 7, 2026.

Mr Hills, who resided at 139 Holgate Road, York, died on November 3, 2025.

After the deadline, the estate will be distributed, considering only the claims and interests that were notified.
