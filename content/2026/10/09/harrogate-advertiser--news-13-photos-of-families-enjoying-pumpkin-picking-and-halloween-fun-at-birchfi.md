Visitors of all ages have been enjoying exploring the pumpkin fields, where thousands of home-grown pumpkins in a wide range of shapes, sizes and colours are ready to be picked.

From giant orange pumpkins to unusual white, green and warty varieties, there is plenty to discover.

Alongside the pumpkin patch, families can enjoy a host of seasonal attractions, including photo opportunities, animal encounters and outdoor activities, making it a popular destination in the run-up to Halloween.

The festival continues throughout the October half-term before coming to a close on November 1.

Entry to the pumpkin patch is free, with pumpkins available to buy from £2.50 to £15.

Visitors can grab a wheelbarrow and head out into the patch to find their perfect pumpkin.
