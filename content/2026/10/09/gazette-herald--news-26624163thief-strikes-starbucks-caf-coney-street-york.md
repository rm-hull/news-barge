A thief has struck at a York café.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a person officers want to speak to about the theft at Starbucks in Coney Street.

The force said the man may have information about the theft which happened on September 15.

“Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation,” a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 1226181560 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
