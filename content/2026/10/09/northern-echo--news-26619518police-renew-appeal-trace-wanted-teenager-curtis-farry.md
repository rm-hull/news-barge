A teenager wanted over allegations of two serious assaults and two burglaries remains at large nearly two months after police first appealed for help finding him.

Curtis Farry, 18, remains wanted by officers from Cleveland Police, who say they would like to speak to him regarding these incidents, which happened between May and August.

Police first appealed for information on August 14 and have now renewed the appeal as the search continues.

In a fresh statement, police said: “Police continue to appeal to trace 18-year-old Curtis Farry, as our officers would like to speak with him in connection with allegations of two serious assaults and two burglaries.

“Farry has links to both the Middlesbrough and the Darlington areas.
