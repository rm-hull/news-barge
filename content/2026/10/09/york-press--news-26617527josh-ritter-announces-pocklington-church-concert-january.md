A critically acclaimed singer-songwriter is set to headline a show in Pocklington.

Josh Ritter will take to the stage at All Saints Church on Friday, January 29, with tickets available now at joshritter.com.

A renowned singer, songwriter, musician, and best-selling author, Ritter has released 12 studio albums over a career spanning more than two decades.

The show is part of Ritter’s UK solo tour, which also includes a performance at the Howard Assembly Room in Leeds on Thursday, January 28.

His latest album, I Believe in You, My Honeydew, is out now.

Ritter has earned praise from NPR Music, which said: "Josh Ritter remains at the top of his game two decades into a highlight-strewn career.

"He’d be forgiven for loosening his grip, but his hand has never felt surer."

Tickets for the Pocklington concert are priced at £35 and can be purchased through joshritter.com.
