Charlie Maynard, the MP for Witney, has resigned from his role as a Treasury spokesman in a move reported to be aimed at removing leader Sir Ed Davey.

The Liberal Democrats confirmed the senior MP’s departure from the party’s front bench on Friday.

According to the *Financial Times*, Mr Maynard’s resignation is intended to prompt Sir Ed’s removal, with the politician reportedly asking parliamentary colleagues to support a vote of no confidence in the party leader.

Lib Dem deputy leader Daisy Cooper backed Sir Ed, saying the “vast majority” of the party’s MPs wanted him to remain as leader and were “furious at this distraction”.

![Charlie Maynard, Liberal Democrat MP for Witney, addresses delegates during the Liberal Democrat Spring Conference in 2025.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/09/16/2206274053...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

She said: “We Liberal Democrats are the last line of defence against a Reform UK government, and Ed is determined to lead a strong and united party into the next general election whenever it comes.

“As Ed said at conference we are in the fight of our lives against the right, who want to turn our country into their version of Trump’s America.”

Mr Maynard himself declined to comment.

His resignation comes amid reported frustration with the party’s lack of progress since the 2024 election, which saw it secure a record 72 MPs.

Since then, the party has gained little ground in the polls, with the latest YouGov survey putting the Liberal Democrats on 11 per cent – just below their share in 2024.
