A Bradford school has been forced to partially shut.

Oasis Academy Lister Park is located on North Avenue in Manningham.

The secondary school has 732 pupils on the roll.

## Why has the school shut?

The school has partially closed due to a substantial roof leak.

It has affected the first and second floor of the building.

## What the school has said

On the emergency school closures list, a spokesperson for the academy said: "We have had a substantial roof leak that is affecting first and second floor.

"The school is open for year 7, year 11 and phoenix area for SEN students.

"The classes have been re-roomed to ones in areas of the school that are unaffected."

Oasis Academy Lister Park was previously known as Challenge College, before becoming part of the Oasis academy trust.
