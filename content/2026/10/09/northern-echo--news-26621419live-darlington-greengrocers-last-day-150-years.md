* Good afternoon, welcome to this Northern Echo live breaking news blog.
* A historic Darlington greengrocer, considered one of the oldest in the world, will serve its final customers today after more than 150 years.
* JJ Blair and Sons, known to many loyal customers as Blairs, has traded in Darlington's Indoor Market since 1875 when Benjamin Disraeli was PM and Queen Victoria was monarch.
* But today, Friday, October 9, will mark the end of an era as the greengrocer stall officially closes, just months after the death of its long-standing owner Robin Blair.
