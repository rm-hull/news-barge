## Our Yorkshire Farm's Amanda Owen: out and about with the Yorkshire Shepherdess

Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen with Yorkshire Cheesemaker Mario Olianas

*Our Yorkshire Farm's Amanda Owen: out and about with the Yorkshire Shepherdess in 10 photos. By MAXINE GORDON*

FROM judging dog shows to taking part in cookery demonstrations, Amanda Owen, aka the Yorkshire Shepherdess and star of TV show Our Yorkshire Farm, has become a national favourite.

As shown in our gallery of photos of Amanda over the years out and about in Yorkshire, she is the first to support local events and celebrate all that's best of God's Own County.

![Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen as a star judge at the Harrogate Dog Show ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21556082.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen as a star judge at the Harrogate Dog Show

Amanda, former husband Clive and their nine children originally became household names through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed their unconventional family life at remote Ravenseat in Swaledale.

She first hit fame through her Twitter social media account where she used the name "The Yorkshire Shepherdess"; this led to her writing a book of the same title.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21556089.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda Owen know as the The Yorkshire Shepherdess speaking at the start of Wool Week at Salts Mill

Since then she has written four more books and become a regular on TV, fronting Our Yorkshire Farm from 2018–2021 and Amanda Owen's Farming Lives in 2023.

More recently, fans have been enjoying Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, following Amanda, her ex-husband Clive, and their children renovating a derelict farmhouse.

She has also appeared on TV in ITV's The Dales, Ben Fogle's New Lives in the Wild and Countrywise. She was voted Yorkshirewoman of the Year by the Dalesman magazine.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21556086.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Yorkshire Shepherdess Amanda Owen who gave a cookery demo in the Food Hall with Sue Nelson. Picture: Richard Walker / Yorkshire Agricultural Society

Her family life today is a world away from her own childhood. She grew up in the suburbs of Huddersfield – but always loved the countryside.

A favourite author was North Yorkshire's James Herriot and she read his books growing up.

*The new season of Our Farm Next Door began on October 5 on Channel 4*
