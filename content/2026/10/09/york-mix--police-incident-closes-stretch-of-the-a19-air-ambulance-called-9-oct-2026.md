A police incident closed a section of the A19 in North Yorkshire today (Friday).

The road was closed between Knayton and Thimbleby near Northallerton. It has now reopened.

Multiple emergency services went to the scene.

#### Crash location

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/police-incident-thimbleby-northallerton-9-oct-2026-3.webp)

The Yorkshire Air Ambulance was also called to the incident.

There were unconfirmed reports of a naked man running in the road.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We received a report of concern for the welfare of a man near the A19, north of Thirsk, at 10.29am this morning.

“Officers attended immediately and quickly located the man. He is now receiving the help, care and support he needs from the relevant agencies.

“A section of the A19 was temporarily closed to allow emergency services to deal with the incident safely.

“We would like to thank motorists and members of the public for their patience and understanding while the road closure was in place.”
