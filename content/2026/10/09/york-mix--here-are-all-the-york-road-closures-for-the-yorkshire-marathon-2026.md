More than 10,000 participants are set to take part in returning Yorkshire marathon festival this month.

Residents and visitors are advised that a number of road closures and access restrictions will be in place across York for the festival. The Yorkshire Marathon Festival will take place on **Sunday 18 October**.

The hugely popular event is for all ages and abilities, and features the Yorkshire Marathon, Yorkshire 10 Mile, and Yorkshire Marathon Relay.

The day will begin with the Altra Yorkshire Marathon and Yorkshire Marathon Relay at 9:30am, starting from the University of York campus. Participants will follow a scenic route through the historic city centre before heading into the surrounding countryside and returning to the university finish line.

The Altra Yorkshire 10 Mile will set off at 10:15am, covering much of the same route over a shorter distance, offering a more accessible challenge while still delivering the unforgettable atmosphere and support of marathon day.

Participants are invited to wheel, walk, jog or run their way round the course as organisers work to make the event as inclusive as possible.

The festival is organised by Jane Tomlinson’s Run For All – who have announced the series of road closures and restrictions that will be in place to ensure the safety of all those taking part.

## The road closures

### Saturday 17 October

* **University Road** in York will be closed between Innovation Way and Green Dykes Lane on ** Saturday 17 October from 10am**. It will re-open at 11pm on Sunday 19 October.

### Sunday 18 October

*Road closures on the **A166, Church Balk, Church Street, Eastfield Lane, Stockhill Close, Green Dykes Lane, Heslington Lane** and**Main Street (Heslington)** will be in place from ** 6am**.
  + There will be a crossing point at Thief Lane which will be manned for residents until 9:25am, before the start of the event. Local residents will also be able to access Main Street and Heslington Lane where required.
*City centre roads affected by the closures include **Hull Road, Barbican Road, Blake Street, Coney Street, Davygate, Deangate, Duncombe Place, Foss Bank, Foss Islands, Goodramgate, High Ousegate, Jewbury, Lord Mayor’s Walk, Melrosegate, Minster Yard, Monkgate, Parliament Street, Piccadilly, Spurriergate, St Denys Road, St Helen’s Square, St Maurice’s Road, Walmgate** and**A1079 Lawrence Street** with these in place from ** 8am**.
  + There will be crossing points at Walmgate Bar & Malton Road to allow vehicles across up until 9:25am before the event starts at 9:30am.
*Further afield, **A1036 Heworth Green, Hopegrove Lane South**,** Stockton Lane** and ** Sandy Lane** will be closed from ** 8:30am**.
* **Common Lane, Northgate Lane, Upper Helmsley** and ** Butttercrambe Moor Wood** will be closed from ** 9:15am**.
*Finally,**Holtby Lane, Rudcarr Lane, Straight Lane, Bad Bargain Lane, Moor Lane, Sandy Lane, Hull Road, Osbaldwick Lane, Osbaldwick Village, Tang Hall Lane** and**Murton Way** will be closed from ** 9:30am**.
  + On **Garrow Hill Avenue (on Hull Road)** vehicles can cross freely until 11am, while Heworth Road Roundabout will be under marshal supervision.

There will also be a number of **parking restrictions** in place from ** 4pm on Saturday 17 October** to ** 6pm on Sunday 18 October** with a park and ride service offered for all runners, spectators and visitors. Find the full details here.

All road closures will be lifted as soon as the safety of runners, spectators and visitors can be assured.

Tristan Batley-Kyle, operations director at Run For All said: “The Altra Yorkshire Marathon Festival is a fantastic event that brings thousands of runners to York each year, while also raising vital funds for so many worthwhile causes.

“We understand that an event of this scale can result in some temporary disruption, and we would like to thank residents, businesses and local communities for their patience and support throughout the weekend.

“Our team will work hard to keep disruption to a minimum and ensure roads are reopened as soon as it is safe to do so.”
