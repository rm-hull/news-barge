A car burst into flames on the M62 this morning.

Crews were called to the eastbound carriageway between junctions 27 and 28 at 5.03am today.

The car was fully alight and required two hose reels and a dry powder extinguisher to bring the blaze under control.

## What the fire service have said

The incident happened shortly after a multi-vehicle crash on the M62 eastbound on Friday morning.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to a car fire on the M62 motorway between junctions 27 and 28 on the eastbound carriageway.

"The car was 100 per cent involved in fire with crews using two hose reels and a dry powder extinguisher to get the fire under control.

"No people were involved."
