## Zelensky's comments about American inaction in full

Volodymyr Zelensky accused the US of “unwillingness” to help in the war after Russian strikes killed more than 70 people over two days.

Speaking to reporters in Pryluky after a Russian guided aerial bomb attack on Kramatorsk that killed at least 30 people, he pushed back against US secretary of state Marco Rubio’s earlier comment that the war was at a “stalemate” and that Washington would keep working toward a settlement.

“We have heard about many different moments during the war. Many hopeless situations, difficult corners and so on. We – people, leaders, politicians – drive ourselves into a corner… Even on the first day of the war, we were not in a corner. Because one person tried to drive an entire nation into a corner, and the nation rose up and defended its state,” he said.

“You see, you cannot negotiate economic projects for the future with the Russians on the one hand and, on the other, say that we are at a… diplomatic deadlock. We are not at a diplomatic stalemate. Shut down their entire economy. Shut Russia’s mouth. Do not feed them with your sales. Just impose sanctions on their weapons. Open Starlink to us, to Ukraine,” he added.

“So where’s the truth here, where’s the honesty? Where’s the stalemate? ... That’s called unwillingness,” he said.

“One cannot, on the one hand, negotiate with the Russians about future economic projects, whilst on the other hand say that we find ourselves in some sort of … diplomatic stalemate,” Zelensky ‌added.

## Former Ukrainian security officer claims military intelligence ordered Monaco bombing

A former Ukrainian security services officer linked to a bomb attack targeting a millionaire businessman in Monaco has claimed that the operation was authorised by senior figures in Ukraine’s military intelligence agency, HUR.

Vitalii Zhykovych alleged that an active-duty HUR officer, Vladyslav Reut, had proposed the operation against Ukrainian-born businessman Vadym Yermolayev, in an interview with the BBC.

Yermolayev and his 13-year-old son were seriously injured in the June explosion, while his partner suffered catastrophic injuries.

Zhykovych, who describes himself as a freelance operative for Ukraine’s SBU security service, said he believed the mission had official approval.

“As Reut informed me, this operation […] had been authorised and approved by Reut's superiors, who served in the Main Intelligence Directorate of the Ministry of Defence [HUR],” he said.

He denied involvement in planting the bomb or killing Anastasia Berezovska, the woman filmed placing the explosive device in Monaco. Her body was discovered with gunshot wounds in woodland near Kyiv a week later.

Zhykovych and Reut were subsequently arrested over her murder.

Reut initially confessed to investigators before changing his account in court to blame Zhykovych.

HUR has not commented on the latest allegations but previously said it was committed to a full investigation and would cooperate with law enforcement authorities.

## Ukrainian negotiators to travel to US for talks

A Ukrainian delegation will travel to the US on Friday and Saturday for talks aimed at advancing efforts to end the war, president Volodymyr Zelensky has said.

The talks, scheduled to take place in Florida, will involve Zelensky’s chief of staff Kyrylo Budanov, alongside senior Ukrainian officials Rustem Umerov, Sergiy Kyslytsya and David Arakhamia.

## Ukrainian drone strike sparks fire at industrial facility in Russia
