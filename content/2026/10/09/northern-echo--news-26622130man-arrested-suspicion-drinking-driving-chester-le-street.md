A man has been arrested on suspicion of drink driving after a crash last night (October 8).

Officers from Durham Police attended Waldridge Road, Chester-le-Street, around 9.15pm, after a two-vehicle crash.

A 40-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of drink driving.

A force spokesperson said: "Officers attended the scene, where a 40-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of drink driving.

"There were no reports of any injuries."
