In southern Albania, the **Vjosa River** burst its banks near Novosela, in the Vlorë region, prompting**evacuation** orders. Rescue teams used inflatable boats to reach residents trapped by rising water, including two elderly people whose home was flooded to a depth of around 1.5 metres.

At least **one person has died**in** Albania**. A 54-year-old man was found dead in an irrigation canal in Durrës after being swept away by floodwaters. Emergency services were working to** clear water from around 800 buildings**, while some** 300 people** had been given temporary shelter.

In neighbouring **Montenegro**, the** Zeta River**flooded residential areas in Danilovgrad, affecting around** 30 homes and several nearby villages**.

Near **Podgorica**, soldiers and engineers built a** temporary pontoon bridge**to replace a crossing destroyed by a torrent. Excavators also cleared drainage channels as emergency crews worked to** restore transport links**.

The floods followed days of severe winter weather across the **Balkans**. Parts of Albania recorded more than** 100 millimetres of rain in 24 hours,** while authorities warned that further snowfall and icy conditions could bring more disruption.
