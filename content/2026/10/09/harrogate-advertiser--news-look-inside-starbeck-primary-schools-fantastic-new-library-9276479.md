# Harrogate Mayor cuts ribbon at completion of school's exciting new project

## The Mayor has cut the ribbon to mark the completion of a Harrogate school's exciting project devoted to children’s literacy

The unveiling of Starbeck Primary Academy newly-refurbished school library was a big moment for teachers and pupils and will support literacy and learning for years to come.

![The Mayor of Harrogate Councillor Viv Poskitt cuts the ribbon to open the library at Starbeck Primary Academy School. (Picture Gerard Binks)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/09/9/11/16970636-library.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The opening ceremony was attended by civic dignatories, including Harrogate Town Council Mayor Coun Viv Poskitt and former Mayor Coun Chris Aldred.

“The new-look school library will be used to build community links between home and school and provide the children with an inspiring place to read,” said the Harrogate Mayor.

"It's a fantastic area and well done to all who facilitated this.

"I even had the children read to me including my favourite “girls can do anything “."

![Opening of Starbeck Primary Academy School library - Pupils Henry Bloor, Elenor Bloor, Sydney Dean and Sebastian Dean. (Picture Gerard Binks)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/09/9/56/16970633-library.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


As well as reading and learning, the aim of the impressively refurbished school library is also to reach out to the wider community.

Kris Starkey, English Leader/Senior Leader at Starbeck Primary Academy said: "The new library will not only benefit our pupils but will also strengthen links with the wider Starbeck community through future reading events, family activities, and community partnerships.”
