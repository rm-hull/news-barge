A car crashed into railings in York last night (Thursday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on **Wigginton Road** at**5.40pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from York responded to a report of a vehicle which had collided with railings.

#### **Wigginton Road, York**

“All occupants of the vehicle were out on arrival of the crews and being checked over by paramedics.

“Crews used small tools and spreaders to access and isolate the vehicle battery.”
