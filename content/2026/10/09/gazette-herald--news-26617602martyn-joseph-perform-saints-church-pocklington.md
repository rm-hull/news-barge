Welsh singer-songwriter Martyn Joseph is set to perform in Pocklington next month.

The concert, at All Saints Church, is scheduled for Thursday, November 19, at 7.30pm.

Martyn Joseph, known for his "poetic songwriting" and "razor-sharp social observation," is returning to reconnect with fans.

All of his previous performances in Pocklington have sold out.

The evening will feature a mix of songs from his expansive catalogue, ranging from "soaring anthems to quiet, reflective moments".

His performances are said to be characterised by "warmth, humour, and generosity".

The spokesperson added: "For long-time followers and new listeners alike, this show offers a chance to experience a songwriter at the height of his craft, performing in the intimate and historic setting of All Saints Church."

Tickets, priced at £22.50, are available through martynjoseph.net, and early booking is essential due to high demand.

The show promises to be a memorable evening of music.
