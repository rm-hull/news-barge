A person was taken to hospital after a crash on the A66 that saw the air ambulance, police and paramedics attend.

Emergency services were called to Stainmore, Cumbria, at around 1.25pm on Thursday (October 8) after reports of a collision between an HGV and a car.

During the incident, traffic on the A66 queued in both directions, heading towards Cumbria and County Durham.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/17712363.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An air ambulance *(Image: GNAAS)*

The Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS) attended the scene, and one person was taken to hospital via road ambulance.

A spokesperson for GNAAS said: "Our critical care team was activated at 1.27pm to reports of a road traffic collision on the A66 near Bowes.

"We had a paramedic and doctor on board our aircraft, and they arrived on scene at 1.45pm.

"Our team worked alongside the North West Ambulance Service to assess and treat a patient.

"The patient was taken to hospital by a NWAS road crew."

Police officers from Cumbria initially attended the scene but left the incident in the hands of Durham Police.

"Injuries unknown at the moment.

"The incident has been moved to Durham Constabulary."
