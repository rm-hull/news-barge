Motorists are facing delays after a car transporter lorry broke down on a busy Bradford roundabout this morning.

The vehicle became stranded on Staithgate Roundabout, which links the M606 with Rooley Lane and Staithgate Lane.

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene at around 8.20am today, the lorry remained in place and was blocking one lane of traffic on the roundabout.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21615539.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Delays reported this morning. *(Image: T&A)*

Traffic was still able to pass the vehicle, but motorists were experiencing delays as they navigated around the obstruction.

The cause of the breakdown is not yet known.

Drivers travelling through the area are advised to allow extra time for their journeys while recovery arrangements are made.
