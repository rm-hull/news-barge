**Australian Broadcasting Corporation reporter Will Murray** was covering the floods when a vehicle stalled after entering deep water on Buchanan Road. As it**began to sink**, Murray handed his phone to camera operator Liz Pickering,** called emergency services**and** waded into the floodwater**. Another bystander quickly joined him.

The pair first tried to open the nearest door, then climbed onto the vehicle and considered smashing the rear window. Murray eventually **forced open the rear passenger-side door**,** allowing the driver to escape**before the ute** disappeared beneath the water**.

Footage captured the **dramatic rescue**, followed by an emotional moment as the relieved driver embraced one of the men who helped save him. ABC camera operator Liz Pickering said the two men**“undoubtedly saved that man’s life”**.

**No serious injuries were reported**. About** 170 millimetres of rain**had fallen in** Morayfield**, where** severe flooding**forced road closures and left the shopping centre largely inaccessible.** Queensland authorities warned motorists** not to drive or swim through floodwaters, warning that vehicles can quickly become trapped or swept away.
