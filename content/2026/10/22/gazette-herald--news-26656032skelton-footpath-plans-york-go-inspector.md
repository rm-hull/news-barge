A public hearing is set to discuss a contested footpath proposal in York.

An inspector, appointed by the Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, will oversee the City of York Council's opposed public footpath Skelton 13 Modification Order 2021.

The hearing will take place at City of York Council, West Offices, Station Rise, York, on November 18 at 10am.

The proposed order seeks to add a public footpath between The Village and Moor Lane, across Skelton Moor, to the area's definitive map and statement.

If the order is confirmed without changes, the map and statement will be modified.

Interested parties can view the statements of case and related documents at the City of York Council West Offices, by appointment, Monday to Friday, 8.30am to 5pm.

For more information, contact Russell Varley on 01904 551500 or [rightsofway@york.gov.uk.](mailto:rightsofway@york.gov.uk.)
