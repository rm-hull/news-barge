Bishop John Keenan, the Bishop of Paisley and President of the Bishops' Conference of Scotland, has announced that he is stepping back from his daily duties following medical advice.

The Bishops’ Conference of Scotland confirmed that Bishop Keenan has been undergoing medical investigations and will require further tests and examinations over the coming weeks.

In the meantime, Vicar General Monsignor Joe Burke will oversee the day-to-day running of the Diocese of Paisley.

**READ MORE:**

In a statement, the Bishops’ Conference of Scotland said: "Bishop John Keenan, Bishop of Paisley and President of the Bishops’ Conference of Scotland, has announced that he is stepping back from his daily duties until further notice, following medical advice.

"Bishop Keenan has been undergoing medical investigations and will require further tests and examinations over the coming weeks.

"During this time, Vicar General Monsignor Joe Burke will oversee the day-to-day running of the Diocese of Paisley.

"Bishop Keenan hopes to provide a further update once these investigations are concluded and is grateful for your prayers at this time."
