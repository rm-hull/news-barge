## The studio behind Preston legends Wallace and Gromit are celebrating a milestone anniversary this year.

The iconic duo Wallace and Gromit were created by Preston-born animator Nick Park back in 1982, when he was still a student at the National Film and Television School.

Three years later, Nick joined Aardman Animations, which helped turn the pair into the world-renowned characters they are today.

As Aardman Animations turns 50 this year, what better way for Prestonians to celebrate than with some collectibles and gifts inspired by the cheese-loving inventor and his loyal dog

Take a look below at 15 Wallace & Gromit-themed items worth checking out as the studio celebrates its milestone anniversary.
