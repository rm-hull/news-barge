Stars have been spotted arriving on Tyneside.

The Mercury Prize 2026 ceremony is taking place tonight, Thursday, October 22, at the Utilita Arena in Newcastle.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21620340.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dove Ellis attending the 2026 Mercury Music Prize awards show at the Utilita Arena, Newcastle Upon Tyne. *(Image: owen humphreys)*

It is the second year running that the event has been held in the North East, following its move from London in 2025.

Excitement has been building in the North East since the Mercury Prize relocated from its long-time London home to Newcastle.

Among the celebrities arriving was singer RAYE, who was photographed in a striking mustard yellow gown.

Also turning heads on the carpet was Olivia Dean in an elegant white dress.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21620341.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Olivia Dean attending the 2026 Mercury Music Prize awards show at the Utilita Arena, Newcastle Upon Tyne. *(Image: owen humphreys)*

Other shortlisted artists include Sir Paul McCartney and North East acts JADE and Knats.

Each nominee will perform a single track during the ceremony as they compete for the Album of the Year title.

Returning performers include last year’s nominees Pulp, taking to the stage in support of their HELP(2) album.

The Mercury Prize has previously recognised artists including Arctic Monkeys, Little Simz, Dizzee Rascal, and Wolf Alice.

The 2025 ceremony was a standout moment for the region when North Shields’ own Sam Fender took home the award.

This year’s shortlist also features Where Is My Husband star RAYE and music legend Sir Paul McCartney.
