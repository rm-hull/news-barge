## The Press Best Curry House 2026 finalists revealed

The finalists in The Press Best Curry House (York) competition 2026 have been announced *(Image: Ben Egan)*

York’s top 10 curry houses have been revealed after readers of The Press nominated their favourites.

Earlier this month, we asked readers to tell us their go-to curry spots across York and the surrounding area in the opening round of our Best Curry House in York competition.




Following a flood of nominations, these restaurants secured a place in the top 10:

1. 29 States – Tower Street, York

2. Bengal Lounge – Acomb Wood Drive, Woodthorpe

3. Bombay Spice – Goodramgate, York

4. Kalpakavadi Restaurant – Fossgate, York

5. Mirchiz – Fishergate, Fulford

6. Spice Clay Oven – Hull Road, Wilberfoss

7. Sylhety Lounge - Kimberlow Lane, York

8. The Parvin Indian Restaurant – York Road, Haxby

9. The Viceroy – Monkgate, York

10. Woodthorpe Spice – Moorcroft Road, York

From Monday (October 26), The Press will be publishing a series of stories highlighting each finalist and their incredible restaurants.

![You can vote for your favourite by picking up a copy of The Press from Monday (October 26)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21617403.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can vote for your favourite by picking up a copy of The Press from Monday (October 26) *(Image: Newsquest)*

The stories will be published online and in print.

Readers will be able to vote for their winner in The Press’ Best Curry House York 2026 competition by picking up a copy of the paper and filling out the submission slip.

Speaking about the contest, Press Editor Ben Hardy said: “York is blessed with an incredible range of curry houses and these restaurants are a huge part of our city's food scene and deserve to be celebrated.

"That's why we're delighted to launch The Press Best Curry House 2026 award and put the decision in the hands of our readers.

"Whether it's your local takeaway, the restaurant you visit for family celebrations, or the place that never fails to impress, we want to hear which curry house you think deserves the title.

"This award is all about recognising the businesses that consistently deliver great food, excellent service and a warm welcome. The passion readers show for supporting local businesses makes these awards so special.

"I would encourage everyone to get involved, cast their nomination and help us find York's favourite curry house. Every vote counts, and we can't wait to see which restaurants make it through to the final."
