A road traffic collision has closed the A9 in both directions.

Police said the closure is in place between Helmsdale and Portgower due to a crash involving one vehicle.

The crash occurred at around 5pm on Thursday, October 22.

Emergency services are in attendance.

Motorists are asked to avoid the area.
