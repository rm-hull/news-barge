Police have issued an update following an appeal for information in its search for a man missing from a York village.

Yesterday evening (Wednesday, October 21), North Yorkshire Police launched an appeal to find a 43-year-old who was last seen in the Malton area on Monday, October 19.

In a statement issued at 9.25pm last night, the force said the man had been found safe and well.
