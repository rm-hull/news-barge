Two famous Teesside faces are set to come together for a special evening in their hometown next month.

Bob Mortimer and Steph McGovern will take to the stage at Middlesbrough Town Hall for the unique event hosted by Waterstones next month.

The event was only announced on Friday (October 16), but quickly sold out, with more than 1,000 people joining the waiting list.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21617868.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Waterstones)*

Mortimer will discuss his latest novel, Michael, at the event, which is due to take place on Thursday, November 19.

He will be joined by journalist and broadcaster Steph McGovern, who will host the event.

Mortimer, who was born in Middlesbrough, is best known for his work as a comedian and has also found success as a writer.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/3352621.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bob Mortimer *(Image: Provided)*

His books include the memoir And Away..., the novels The Satsuma Complex and Hotel Avocado, and The Long Shoe.

His latest work, Michael, is described as a "wise, funny and moving" story about a man given a second chance at life, only to find himself reliving a difficult time from his past.

McGovern, also from Middlesbrough, spent more than 20 years in financial journalism and eight years on BBC Breakfast.

She now co-hosts The Rest is Money podcast with Robert Peston and published her debut thriller, Deadline, in 2025.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21387694.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TV presenter Steph McGovern *(Image: BBC/Stellify)*

Audience members who secured tickets will have the opportunity to submit questions for Mortimer in advance, either at the time of booking or by emailing [events@waterstones.com.](mailto:events@waterstones.com.)

Due to time limitations, only a selection of questions will be asked during the event.

The event starts at 7pm on Thursday, November 19, with tickets costing £32, and each ticket includes an unsigned copy of Michael, which can be collected on the night.
