Durham County Council’s leader will ask the Government to tell all of its councillors where migrant housing is located, despite ‘safeguarding’ concerns raised during a heated debate.

Councillors backed a motion on Wednesday (October 21) which would see the council leader write to the Government to ask them to reveal data on where migrant housing is located.

Deputy council leader Darren Grimes said a lack of information about HMOs being used in County Durham was causing resentment in communities.

"It is a terraced house here. It is a flat there. We don’t know. They pop up like weeds," he said.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21614530.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cllr Darren Grimes said County Durham was becoming a 'dumping ground'. *(Image: DURHAM COUNTY COUNCIL)*

"[People] elected Reform to give them the transparency and the data they were crying out for. That is all we are asking for, to be informed with the facts."

The motion was heard late in the meeting after councillors twice agreed to extend its running time and debate became heated when Cllr Grimes accused Liberal Democrat Ellie Hopgood of calling him a ‘racist’ off-microphone.

Cllr Hopgood said she had called the motion a ‘disgrace’.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21614531.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cllr Ellie Hopgood. *(Image: DURHAM COUNTY COUNCIL)*

Cllr Dawn Bellingham, who submitted the motion, said the authority had already struggled to get information from the Home Office.

She said: "I have been in many meetings with Home Office representatives that will not provide us with any data that we have been asking for."

A Liberal Democrat amendment sought to ask for data to be provided to council officers, rather than councillors, because of safeguarding concerns.

Green councillor Jonathan Elmer said he had ‘huge concerns’ about the motion and warned against providing detailed information about where people were living and referred to a recent incident involving a refugee in County Durham.

He said: "There’s been an incident recently within County Durham. I’m not going to say where. But the police have records of it, of where a refugee who was into a property.
