POLICE are appealing for information to help locate Riley Lister, who is wanted on recall to prison.

Lister is from the Yeadon area of Leeds and also has connections in Bradford.

Despite ongoing enquiries, officers have so far been unable to trace him and are now asking the public for help.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or using the Live Chat service on the force’s website, quoting reference 13260416379.

Alternatively, information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
