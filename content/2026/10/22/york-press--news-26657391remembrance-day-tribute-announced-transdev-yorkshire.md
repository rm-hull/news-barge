## Dozens of Yorkshire buses display giant poppies for Remembrance tribute

Transdev colleagues who are former Armed Forces personnel with a poppy in front of a Transdev bus, for the Poppy Appeal 2026 campaign renewal *(Image: Transdev)*

Dozens of buses across Yorkshire have been decorated with giant poppies in tribute to the Armed Forces.

Transdev, which operates services across Yorkshire and Lancashire, has launched the display as part of its Poppy Appeal campaign.

The tribute also includes free travel for serving and former members of the Armed Forces on Remembrance Sunday, November 8, and Remembrance Day, November 11.

Henri Rohard, managing director of Transdev, said: "This Remembrance Weekend and Armistice Day, we are reminded of the sacrifice that the men and women of our Armed Forces have made for our freedom.

"We hope that by displaying these large poppies on our buses, many of our customers will join us in remembering those who serve and will donate to charitable causes that support our veterans.

"And for those current and former personnel who use our buses, we hope the free travel option allows them to visit friends, family, and local remembrance events on both of these important days."

The company is also supporting the British Royal Legion by encouraging passengers to donate to the charity.

Transdev has joined the UK Ministry of Defence’s Career Transition Partnership scheme, which helps service personnel move into civilian jobs.

The company regularly recruits engineers and drivers across the region.
