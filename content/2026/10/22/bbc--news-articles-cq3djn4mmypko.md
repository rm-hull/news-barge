![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: 'Drill, Andy, Drill!'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/b9b7/live/66850930-cd92-11f1-bc2e-018d645d8d21.jpg)

Prime Minister Andy Burnham is "facing a storm" for approving drilling at the Jackdaw gas field in the North Sea, which the Metro has headlined as "Drill, Andy, Drill!" The move has been praised by US President Donald Trump, the paper says, but not by those concerned about the project's environmental and economic impact.

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: 'Fahnbulleh minded to reject Rosebank oilfield, sources say.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/c816/live/404f06c0-cd98-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

Energy secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh could reject a giant Rosebank oil field, sources have told the Guardian, after the approval of the Jackdaw gas field on Wednesday. According to officials, Fahnbulleh is considering "blocking what would be one of the biggest oil fields in the North Sea", the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail: 'Pathetic.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/f81f/live/f14ff160-cd97-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

"Pathetic" reads the Daily Mail headline about the Foreign Secretary Ed "Miliband's Net Zero madness" on potentially rejecting the Rosebank oil field. The Daily Mail calls Labour's approval of the Jackdaw gas field as a "rare outbreak of common sense".

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: 'Chancellor: I'll cut cost of business to boost UK.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/8b92/live/8a462f80-cd96-11f1-bc2e-018d645d8d21.jpg)

The upcoming Budget will cut regulations and "back business in Britain making a profit", the chancellor has told the Times, in a move that will be for "bloody-minded entrepreneurs".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: 'Putin's trawlers spy on Britain.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/0c66/live/72ebeea0-cd92-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

The Telegraph has been analysing Russian fishing vessels above critical UK undersea cables and suggests the Kremlin is using the fleet to "spy on Britain". The paper found "more than 500 incidents in which Russian trawlers loitered above vital communications cables".

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: 'Schools face cutting SEND staff to fund 2026 pay rise for teachers.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/f84c/live/768a4b10-cd92-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

Headteachers have spoken to the i Paper over fears their schools will have to find money for a national 3.5% teacher pay rise, "despite claims it would be 'fully funded'". The UK government has denied that the education secretary said the pay rise would be fully funded, the paper reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: 'Musk's vision for SpaceX and Tesla combination welcomed by investors.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/fd4a/live/7e17e310-cd92-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Financial Times leads on the richest man in the world, Elon Musk, as he considers merging his two companies SpaceX and Tesla into a "sprawling conglomerate spanning rockets, satellites, social media, robots and cars". Investors are welcoming the idea, the paper reports, and shares in SpaceX surged on Wednesday.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: 'To deter war Britain must be ready.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/7def/live/b0fbda80-cd96-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

"Britain must be ready" the Daily Express reports, amid growing threats of war. Senior Conservatives are urging Burnham to increase spending on defence to combat a rise in Russian threats, the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: 'Kyle inks it's all over.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/444f/live/bde6a080-cd97-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

The Sun splashes on a picture of former Manchester City player Kyle Walker's "4 In A Row" leg tattoo that he said he will have removed if the club are stripped of its titles for breaching financial rules. The club was found guilty by an independent commission of more than 100 charges. City has denied any wrongdoing and has appealed the ruling.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: 'Ruled out.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/bd5d/live/aeae5620-cd98-11f1-a64c-550be9e3c66b.jpg)

The Prince of Wales faces off the Duke of Sussex on the front page of the Daily Mirror above the headline "Ruled Out". According to a royal insider, Prince William has "no intention" of engaging with Prince Harry, the paper reports, after the younger brother called for a reconciliation.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star: '£4m Rylan: Beeb don't pay enough.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/dcf8/live/7d57dfb0-cd98-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

The Daily Star leads on TV personality and presenter Ryan Clark who said he "carpeted for missing work because of an air traffic strike" at the BBC.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: 'How our reporter triggered Trump.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/a6c5/live/e15170d0-cd98-11f1-9a39-31b474234113.jpg)

And Donald Trump is the lead image on the Independent after he apparently "lashed out" at reporter Andrew Feinberg. The Independent reporter asked the president about fraud tip-off payments, which reportedly "triggered Trump".

The decision to approve the Jackdaw gas field is praised as "a welcome outbreak of common sense" in the Sun., external But, in what is described as "Net Zero madness", the Daily Mail , externalsays the energy secretary, Miatta Fahnbulleh, is poised to reject plans for the Rosebank oil field. "Pathetic" is the paper's headline. A source tells the Times that it would be hard to justify, external approving the project on climate grounds. Another says Fahnbulleh has long been opposed to it. But, according to the Guardian, she "could still be overruled", external by the prime minister, who "may hear different representations" from the chancellor.

John Healey gives clues about next week's Budget in an interview with the Times, external. He says that alleviating the cost of business is "in the end the only way to deal with people who have cost of living pressures". With the chancellor expected to cut regulations, a senior Treasury source likens them to "the sludge" which is "choking firms".

Separately, the Financial Times reports that Healey has suffered a "double blow" , externalwith increases in both inflation and government borrowing. One economist tells the paper the figures are "dismal" and signal that he will deliver a "cautious Budget that has little net impact on the economy and leaves the toughest fiscal decisions for next year".

Analysis by the Daily Telegraph, external suggests that Russia is using fishing vessels to spy on the UK's undersea cables. The paper says these could be attacked in any future war, potentially paralysing the country. A Nato source is quoted as saying: "We're absolutely aware of what Russian civilian fishing ships are up to and where... Some of them have never seen a single fish on their deck." Meanwhile, warnings about Britain's readiness for war are highlighted in the Daily Express, external, which calls for an increase in defence spending.

And a royal insider tells the Daily Mirror , externalthat Prince William "has no intention of engaging with" his brother, Prince Harry, who has called for reconciliation. The source says "there isn't a world where the Prince of Wales can imagine" a reunion. The headline is: "Ruled out."
