Six people were taken to hospital after a three-vehicle crash in Aberdeen.

The incident took place at around 5.05pm on Wednesday on Springhill Road.

Emergency services responded and the six people were taken to hospital.

The road was closed while inquiries took place, but has since reopened.

Police Scotland said on Thursday it is continuing to look into the circumstances surrounding the crash, and thanked motorists for their patience.
