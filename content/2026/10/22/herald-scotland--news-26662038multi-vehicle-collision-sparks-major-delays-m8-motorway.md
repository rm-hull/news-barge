A multi-vehicle collision is causing major delays for motorists on the M8 motorway in Glasgow.

Traffic Scotland took to X to alert road users to the crash on the M8 Eastbound at Junction 16 (Dobbie's Loan Interchange) at around 4pm on Thursday.

The crash means the motorway on-slip is closed and lanes 1, 2 and 4 are currently closed.

Heavy congestion is being reported on approach, with Traffic Scotland warning of delays of over an hour, with traffic queueing from Junction 26 (Hillington).

Motorists are being warned to allow extra travel time and seek alternative routes.

Police are on the scene.
