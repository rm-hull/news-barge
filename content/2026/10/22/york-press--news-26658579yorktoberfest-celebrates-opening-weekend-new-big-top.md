## Yorktoberfest returns with spectacular circus acts and Bavarian celebrations

Yorktoberfest returned to York Racecourse this weekend with a packed programme of Bavarian-themed entertainment *(Image: Jamboree Entertainment)*

Yorktoberfest returned to York Racecourse this weekend with a packed programme of Bavarian-themed entertainment.

The event is being held under a Big Top for the first time, adding extra energy to the festivities.

The opening weekend featured a dramatic aerial performance from artist Zoe Kulyk, who wowed the crowd with her rooftop acrobatics.

Velma Celli, a drag performer, kept audiences entertained with powerful vocals and playful commentary on guests’ outfits.

James Cundall, event organiser and CEO of Jamboree Entertainment, said: "Staging Yorktoberfest in a Big Top for the first time has really ramped up the party atmosphere.

"The feedback from our first weekend has been superb, with many people commenting on the high-energy festive vibe, not to mention the fantastic entertainment line-up complete with a circus-themed aerial display."

The Bavarian Strollers, a live oompah band, led the crowd in traditional drinking songs and had everyone dancing to their mix of oompah anthems and disco favourites.

Guests also enjoyed a selection of beers, wines, spirits, and German-inspired food from The Dog Haus.

Yorktoberfest returns for a final weekend of celebrations on Friday, October 23, and Saturday, October 24.

Standard tickets are priced at £25 per person, while VIP tickets cost £35 per person.

A booking fee applies to all online purchases.

Tickets can be purchased in advance at www.ticketsource.co.uk/yorktoberfest and will also be available at the entrance, subject to availability.

Entry is restricted to those aged 18 and over.

Yorktoberfest is produced by York-based Jamboree Entertainment, which has more than 30 years of experience in live entertainment and is known for producing Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland.

Full event details, including timings and ticket options, are available at www.yorktoberfest.co.uk.
