Around 800 homes have been left without electricity in Darlington this morning (Thursday, October 22), with dozens of postcodes affected by a major power cut.

Northern Powergrid has confirmed that properties across parts of the town are experiencing an outage, with engineers working to restore supplies as quickly as possible.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21615663.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Power lines *(Image: PA MEDIA)*

The electricity distributor has estimated that power could be restored by 11.30am, although this remains subject to change.

Northern Powergrid said its teams were attempting to reroute electricity supplies to get homes back online.

A spokesperson said: "Our team are trying to get the power back on quickly by switching electricity through alternative routes on our network."
