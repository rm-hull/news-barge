Shetland has been hit by flooding and a landslide after heavy rainfall on Friday.

Cafes, restaurants and shops in Lerwick flooded, with businesses around Commercial Street and Esplanade forced to clear water and reporting flood damage.

The Nomadic Pizzeria Shetland took to Facebook to write: "We have finally cleaned up the last of the flood water.

"We still have to put the shop back together so we will be closed for the rest of the week whilst we get sorted."

Optometrists iCare Shetland also wrote: "Due to the heavy rain we are dealing with flooding & will be closed today & tomorrow. Sorry for the inconvenience."

Shetland Islands Council said its roads staff are working to clear drains around the town.

Meanwhile, a diversion has been put in place due to a landslip affecting the B9071 road between Garderhouse and Reawick.

Traffic for Reawick and Skeld is being directed through the Houllmalees and Gruting.

Work to clear the road is underway, with the road expected to reopen within a few hours, the council said.

Scotland is set to be hit by powerful winds and heavy rain this weekend, with forecasters warning of gusts reaching up to 80mph.

The Met Office said strong westerly winds are expected to spread across northern parts of the UK on Saturday evening, bringing increasingly unsettled conditions brought by Storm Boelo.

A yellow weather warning for high winds has been issued for all of Scotland from 6pm on Saturday to 9pm on Sunday.

Forecasters say gusts of 55-65mph are likely across many areas, while some coastal and exposed locations could see winds of 70-80mph or even higher.
