**🚘** The treasure chest is in east Sotoyama on the shore of a big lake, north of Soni Circuit.

It's winter time in Forza Horizon 6's Horizon Meets series, so once again we have a new Treasure Hunt to find to get some seasonal points for earning the newest automobiles on offer.

This week, the chest is in east Sotoyama, the northernmost region, by a mountain lake. You can see it in the image above, but this one is exceptionally easy to spot on the map.

* Just pan north on the map from Soni Circuit in Takashiro and you'll spot the big pool in the far east of Sotoyama.
* The chest itself is on the beach that runs along its right side from northeast to southwest.

Head there and smash the chest to get 100,000 credits, 5,000 XP, and three Season Progress. Also make sure to complete the new photo challenge, too, if you're looking for extra points.
