# Police hunt wanted Yeadon man who's being recalled to prison

## Riley Lister also has connections to Bradford.

Police are hunting a Leeds man who is wanted on recall to prison.

West Yorkshire Police has issued an appeal for help locating Riley Lister, who is from Yeadon. He is also known to people in the Bradford area.

A police spokesman said: "Police in Leeds are appealing for information to help locate Riley Lister who is wanted on recall to prison.

"He is from the Yeadon area of Leeds and also has connections in the Bradford area "Enquiries have been carried out to try and locate Riley and officers are appealing to the public for information that could assist with their enquiries."

Anyone with any information is urged to call police on 101, quoting reference 13260416379, or report online here. Alternatively, call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
