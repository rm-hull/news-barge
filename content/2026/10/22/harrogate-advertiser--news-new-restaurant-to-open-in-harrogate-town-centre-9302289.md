# Mystery of empty restaurant unit in prominent Harrogate location solved as a new sign appears

## “The arrival of this new restaurant will bring a new entry to the growing battle to be the best Thai in Harrogate”

A new restaurant is to open in an empty unit in Harrogate town centre which provoked readers to post their worries for its future.

![A new restaurant is to open in Harrogate town centre.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/21/15/50/NewHarrRestaurStationBridge.jpeg?trim=0,0,307,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The location at at 7 Station Bridge in Harrogate has been lifeless since January this year when Dinos, an independent Mediterranean restaurant, closed its doors, the latest in a series of establishments at that address to do so.

The signs suggested a refurbishment was in the works but nothing appeared to be happening until the Dinos livery and signs came down and the windows were whitewashed.

But new signs have now appeared at 7 Station Bridge with the promise of the opening of a new restaurant.

Sila Thai is, as the name suggests, a Thai restaurant, bar, and pub.

Combining a traditional pub and bar atmosphere with authentic Thai cuisine, its arrival will bring a new entry to the growing battle to be the best Thai in Harrogate.

Others already engaged in that competition include Sukhothai Harrogate at 17–19 Cheltenham Parade, Giggling Squid at 1-2 John Street and Phranakhon Thai Tapas at 3 Cheltenham Crescent.
