West Yorkshire Police has released pictures of potential witnesses and suspects wanted in connection with alleged crimes.

The images are taken from CCTV camera footage and may show suspects or witnesses officers wish to speak to.

The image shows, from left to right, top row, person 1 to 2, and then from left to right, bottom row, person 3 to 4.

Police warn the public not to approach anyone shown in the images and to contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 instead.
