**Watch Bournemouth vs AC Milan live streams** as the Cherries welcome one of the most prestigious clubs in world football to the 12,000-capacity Vitality Stadium in the Europa League 2026/27 league phase.

Milan last visited the south coast of England in November 2008, just 18 months after they had been crowned European champions for the seventh time. As Ronaldinho, Kaka, Andriy Shevchenko and Co faced Portsmouth at Fratton Park, Bournemouth were battling at the bottom of League Two and minutes away from going bust.

Eighteen years on, the Cherries have been transformed. They are now Premier League regulars and finished sixth last season to qualify for Europe for the first time. Bournemouth are arguably favourites, too, having beaten Real Sociedad and Sturm Graz in their opening two matches of the league phase. The Cherries overcame Sunderland 2-0 on Sunday to claim their first Premier League win under Marco Rose and they will believe they have the quality to turn over Milan in what would be the most eye-catching victory in the club's 126-year history. Left-back Adrien Truffert is available despite suffering cramp on Sunday, but Eli Junior Kroupi and Alex Scott remain sidelined.

While Milan no longer boast the galaxy of superstars they had in years gone by, the Rossoneri remain a huge draw. Ruben Amorim's side are one of the favourites to win the competition and thrashed RB Salzburg 4-0 in Austria seven days ago to bounce back from their 2-0 home defeat by Benfica in gameweek one. The Italians have made a strong start under former Manchester United manager Amorim, who was appointed in the summer, and are yet to lose in Serie A this term. Rising star Alphadjo Cisse could start on the day he celebrates his 20th birthday, having hit a hat-trick against Salzburg.
