The fair’s fifth edition gathers **211 galleries from 41 countries**, with public days running from** Friday 23 to Sunday 25 October**. Visitors explored works by artists including** Yayoi Kusama, Julian Schnabel and Matthew Wong**, alongside new paintings by American artist** Drake Carr**.

The fair’s **Emergence section** also showcased more experimental installations, with**16 solo booths** dedicated to emerging practices. Artist Yann Stéphane Bisso highlighted the opportunity to present work beyond conventional commercial formats.

The event is taking place under **heightened security** following**violent protests in the French capital**.** Anti-ram barriers and security guards**surround the** Grand Palais**, while organisers are coordinating with Paris police over possible adjustments.

Art Basel Paris director **Karim Crippa** said a**comprehensive security plan** had been prepared and activated, adding that the**safety of exhibitors, staff, visitors and artworks** was the priority. Indian collector Saloni Doshi described witnessing**fires, protesters and scenes of panic** on her arrival in Paris, leaving her concerned about fellow visitors travelling from abroad.

Despite the **tense atmosphere outside**, the fair continued to** welcome collectors and art enthusiasts**, with works also displayed in public spaces around the Grand Palais.
