Good morning. An investigation has revealed that Israel is using US-made electroshock weapons on Palestinian detainees. The American company Axon is still supplying the Taser weapons despite evidence they have been used to torture prisoners. The news comes as US volunteers including a lawmaker helping Palestinians harvest olives were detained, and Heidi Levine revealed more details of her violent assault by Israeli settlers.

Human rights groups have condemned allegations of the repeated torture and ill-treatment of detainees held in Israeli detention centres and prisons using electric shock devices, with Amnesty calling for restrictions on exports of such devices. A spokesperson for the Israel Defense Forces said the abuse of detainees was strictly prohibited.

* **Who was detained by Israel near Nahalin in the occupied West Bank?** The Israeli military detained about 80 US, Israeli and British volunteers helping Palestinians harvest olives near Bethlehem, including the New York Democratic congressional nominee Brad Lander, several rabbis and Israel’s former deputy attorney general. Soldiers said the area had been declared a closed military zone.
* **What more has Levine said about the Israeli settler attack on her?** The Washington Post photojournalist who was attacked and seriously injured by Israeli settlers on Friday has described how she was saved with the help of local Palestinians. Witnesses said thee attack on her ceased only when young men used slingshots to aim stones at the settlers, forcing them back.
* **What is the latest development with US diplomats in the Middle East?** The US state department has ordered some diplomats and the families of US government employees to leave Saudi Arabia, as it faces a barrage of near-daily attacks from Yemen’s Houthis. Reports have emerged of similar US withdrawals in UAE, Jordan, Qatar and Bahrain amid concerns of a return to conflict with Iran after the midterm elections.

## **State attorneys general step up plans to counter Trump’s meddling in midterms**

![US president Donald Trump.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/28364452a7117aabdc84955bd634d79bdbcb3605/435_40_1926_1541/master/1926.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Democratic state attorneys general, other top state officials and civic groups have accelerated planning to thwart any federal meddling in the November midterms by Donald Trump as he continues his false charges of widespread voting fraud and veiled threats of interfering in the process. The enhanced cooperation is significant because many secretaries of state are responsible for supervising elections, and state attorneys general are charged with defending the laws governing them.

* **Where has Elon Musk been spending his election campaign money?** Elon Musk’s Super Pac has spent more than $500,000 in New York’s highly competitive 17th congressional district race to help Mike Lawler, the Republican incumbent, hold his seat. Lawler, a moderate Republican, is fending off a challenge from the Democrat Cait Conley in one of the most-watched races that could determine who controls Congress.
* **What has happened with letters containing white powder being sent to Congress?** Nancy Pelosi’s office received “a suspicious letter” on Wednesday in what appears to be the latest incident of ill-intentioned mailings targeted at US Congress members. The offices of more than three dozen members of Congress across the political spectrum have received envelopes containing white powder in recent days.
* **What is Trump’s polling as the election looms?** Donald Trump’s approval rating has sunk to 36%, the lowest recorded, with most respondents expressing frustration over his handling of the US economy, cost of living and Iran war. The president was heckled at least nine times during a campaign event yesterday, and reacted to a supporter punching a demonstrator by saying: “Man, I loved that.”

## **Flaw in PHP computer language shuts down websites around world**

![The PHP Foundation website.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/7b2ac9acb1cb8d1678d58a8d278df37914456a10/461_0_5417_4335/master/5417.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)
