A LAW firm has strengthened its team with two new appointments.

Gordons, based in Bradford, has added Hannah Moran and Mehsan Khalid to its commercial contracts team, bringing the total number of specialists in the division to eight.

Ms Moran joins from Irwin Mitchell, while Mr Khalid arrives from Hill Dickinson.

Andy Brian, partner and head of commercial at Gordons, said: "The continued growth of our commercial contracts specialism reflects the long-term, trusted relationships we are committed to building with our clients.

"We aim to operate as an extension of our clients’ teams and work hard to be available, responsive and pragmatic.

"Hannah and Mehsan bring valuable expertise and experience that will help us continue to deliver excellent client service and meet our ambitious targets for further growth."

The appointments follow a period of sustained financial growth for the firm.

Gordons recently reported its sixth consecutive year of overall growth, with net profit rising by 12 per cent to £14.6 million and annual turnover increasing by 11 per cent to £26.3 million for the year ending March 31, 2026.

The commercial contracts team has doubled its revenues over the past five years, serving clients including AO, Oak Furnitureland, Moss, and Wren Kitchens.
