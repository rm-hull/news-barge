A building has caught fire in an East Yorkshire town this morning (Thursday, October 22).




A spokesperson for Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said: "We received a call at 4.56am reporting a building fire in Mill Lane.

Four fire engines and an aerial ladder platform attended the scene and extinguished the fire just after 6am.

A fire investigation is taking place and no injuries were reported."

Humberside Police said the level crossing in Mill Lane, and nearby Athelstan Road in Beverley were closed this morning.

The two roads are north of the town centre.
