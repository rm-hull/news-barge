NordVPN is without a doubt, the top VPN service for most people based on our tests. It's fast, feature-rich, and does everything you'll likely need from a VPN. And currently, a range of NordVPN plans are on sale with up to 78% off.

These two-year subscriptions offer an extra three months free, for a total of 27 months' protection. Both Complete and Prime (called Ultimate Max in the UK) plans offer good value for money for professionals. They couple antivirus, scam and spam protection, ad blocking, and more security tools. There's also a password manager, and 1TB cloud storage. Prime/Ultimate Max also adds Cyber Insurance against identity theft.

• **Best for:** Professionals and frequent flyers who want to use public and hotel Wi-Fi securely, stream content, and get security tools for extra protection without a monthly subscription.

• **Why we like it:** consistently fast speeds and near-flawless streaming unblocking in our testing, plus antivirus, ad and tracker blocking, a password manager, and 1TB of encrypted cloud storage.

• **What you need to know:** Our review does flag that features vary across platforms, and some extras, like scam call protection, currently work only on Android.
