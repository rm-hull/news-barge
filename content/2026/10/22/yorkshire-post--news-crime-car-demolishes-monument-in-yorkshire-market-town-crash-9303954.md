# Car demolishes monument in Yorkshire market town crash

## A town has been rocked by a collision that has demolished its historic market cross.

Shoppers and businesses in the centre of Howden, East Yorkshire, were shocked when a gold Range Rover crashed into the local landmark shortly after 10.30am on Thursday (Oct 22), leaving the vehicle perched on the base of the wrecked monument.

One pedestrian is believed to have been taken to hospital following the incident outside the Shire Hall.

Local councillor David Howard said he was at home nearby when alerted to the crash and went to the scene to see the damage.

![A Range Rover has mounted a paved area and knocked down the Market Cross in Howden.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/22/12/27/01M5HP56PNTDY3G7Q2CW6R85R9.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)


Mr Howard said of the cross: “It’s in a real mess. We’re trying to work out who owns it, really.”

He said: “It marks the centre of the town and it needs to be put back in some form – restored, repaired, replaced.

“That’s for the future. At the minute, we just need it to be safe and conserve as much as possible.”

Mr Howard said: “It does look like a very, very interesting art installation at the motor show at the moment.

“In fact, it’s almost an album cover from an early 1980s rock band.”

The independent councillor said there was some confusion over the history of the cross in the Market Place, close to the town’s historic minster.

![The incident occurred on Thursday (Oct 22).](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/22/12/21/01M5HP55WDJ1E9JN81KVNJRKRM.jpeg?trim=140,0,141,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


He said he had believed parts of it were medieval, but had also been told it does not appear in some Victorian photographs.

According to the Historic England website, the structure is Grade II listed.

The heritage body says the base is medieval but the 16ft-high (5-metre) cross was erected in 1909.

Mr Howard said the town centre is a conservation area.

He said the accident could have been much worse and praised the work of the fire service.

A Humberside Police spokeswoman said: “Officers are in attendance following a reported road traffic collision in the area of Market Place, Howden, involving a car and a pedestrian at approximately 10.38am this morning.

“Road users are advised to avoid the area at this time whilst emergency services are dealing with the incident.”
