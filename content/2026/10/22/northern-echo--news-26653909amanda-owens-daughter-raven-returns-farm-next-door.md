Amanda Owen's eldest daughter Raven is back at Ravenseat as the family faces another busy period in the Yorkshire Dales.

The eldest of Amanda and Clive Owen's nine children returned to the family's remote Swaledale farm in the latest episode of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which aired on Monday (October 19).

Raven, who viewers watched growing up in the original Our Yorkshire Farm series, was seen mucking out the stables alongside her younger sister Edith.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21491864.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

But Edith has plenty of other responsibilities competing for her attention.

As well as helping with the animals, she is preparing for exams and getting the family's seasonal cream tea van ready for walkers passing through the Yorkshire Dales.

The van has previously been a familiar sight for visitors tackling the Coast to Coast route near Ravenseat.

Meanwhile, work continued on Anty John's, the historic farmhouse the Owens have been renovating close to Ravenseat.

Carpenters were laying oak floorboards as the ambitious restoration project moves another step towards completion.
