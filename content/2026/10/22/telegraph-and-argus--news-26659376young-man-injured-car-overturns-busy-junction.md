A young man was injured after a car overturned at a busy junction.

Police were called to the junction of Moorhead Lane in Shipley at around 6.45pm on Wednesday.

Officers attended the incident after receiving reports that a car had overturned at the junction.

A 21-year-old man suffered injuries as a result of the incident.

Police said the injuries are not believed to be serious.

Enquiries into the circumstances of the incident are ongoing.

Anyone with information about the collision is urged to contact police.

A spokesperson for the force said: "At 6.45pm yesterday (21 October), police received a report of an overturned car at the junction of Moorhead Lane, Shipley.

"Officers attended and a 21 year old man suffered injuries that are not believed to be serious."
