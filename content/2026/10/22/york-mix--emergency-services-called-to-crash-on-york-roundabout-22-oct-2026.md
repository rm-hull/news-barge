Emergency services were called to a crash on a York roundabout at lunchtime today (Thursday).

The incident was on the roundabout **near Kimberlow Rise** at about**1pm**.

A car and motorbike had collided.

#### Incident location

Firefighters from York and Acomb went to the scene.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “One male motorcyclist was treated at scene by police and nearby health centre staff.”
