A GROUP of primary schools is preparing to welcome families through their doors.

Six schools in Bradford’s Manningham area, all part of The Priestley Academy Trust, will host open days throughout November, December, and January to give prospective pupils and their families the chance to explore facilities and meet staff.

The open days are designed to showcase each school’s unique approach while reflecting the trust’s shared commitment to delivering “an outstanding, transformational learning experience for every pupil.”

Mathew Atkinson, chief executive officer of The Priestley Academy Trust, said: "We are so proud of our schools and would encourage any families looking for a school place for their child to come and have a look round.

"Each Priestley school is unique, with incredible facilities, rich indoor and outdoor learning spaces, dedicated staff and strong curricula, but what unites all of our schools is the number one aim to make sure that the pupils are central to everything we do.

"We think some visitors may be surprised to see what lies behind the schools’ gates—please do come along and see for yourselves."

The events are open to families seeking Nursery or Reception places for the next academic year, as well as those considering in-year transfers.

Open day details can be found on individual school websites.
