Firefighters have come to the rescue of a goat trapped in a North Yorkshire field.

The county's fire and rescue service was called to an area of Falsgrave, to the west of Scarborough, at 7.32pm last night (Wednesday, October 21).

A service spokesperson said firefighters used hydraulic cutters to release the goat and left it in the care of the farmer.
