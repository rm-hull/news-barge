A County Durham chippy has been named a national finalist in a prestigious fish and chip award.

Grandpa’s Fish Bar in Durham has been shortlisted for Middleton Foods’ Peter Hill Award for the second year running, a national honour that celebrates standout newcomers in the fish and chip industry.

Ryan Baker, sales manager at Middleton Foods, said: "Huge congratulations to Grandpa’s Fish Bar for once again being recognised as one of this year’s finalists.

"We launched The Peter Hill Award in memory of our great friend and CEO, Peter Hill.

"This year, we have a whopping £10,000 worth of prizes up for grabs!

"I’d like to thank everyone who has been involved with the Award – from the judges to those who took the time to enter.

"Once more, we have received a fantastic number of entries and are delighted to continue recognising more newcomers to the fish and chip industry; whilst encouraging new talent, promoting quality and excellence and giving something back to the trade we love so much."

The winner and runners-up will be announced by the beginning of November, following the next stage of judging by industry experts Fred Capel, Nigel Hodgson and Calum Richardson, and a personal visit from Ryan Baker, Sales Manager, Middleton Foods.

Rich Weatherburn, director of Grandpa’s Fish Bar, said: "We’re so excited to be shortlisted for The Peter Hill Award once again!"

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21617798.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rich Weatherburn *(Image: Provided)*

"It’s great to continue getting industry recognition for all our hard work and it would mean the world to us to go all the way, so fingers crossed!

"We have continued to work really hard to build something that didn’t feel like just another chippy.

"We have been supported so enthusiastically by our team and, even more importantly, our customers.

"We’re so grateful for this recognition once again and are hoping we can go all the way!"

**Read More:**

The winner will receive £1,000 worth of Middleton Foods stock, a factory tour with design of their ‘own label’ bespoke batter mix, a year’s membership to the National Federation of Fish Friers (NFFF), and three months of one-to-one ForkedUp training with Mr Richardson.

Other prizes include tickets to the 2027 Fish & Chip Shop of the Year Awards, travel and hotel accommodation, and a full day of business support from the Middleton Foods team.

Three runners-up will each receive £1,000 of Middleton Foods stock and an invitation to the NFFF media day.
