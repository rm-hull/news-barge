A passenger on a flight arriving in Scotland fell unconscious while the plane was mid-air.

EasyJet said that the traveller, who was on board a flight from Sharm el-Sheikh, lost consciousness as the plane was en route to Glasgow.

However, the holidaymaker was treated by cabin crew and the aircraft did not have to declare a medical emergency.

The flight, which left Egypt on Wednesday evening, touched down at around 2.30am on Thursday.

Glasgow Airport confirmed that medical staff were not required to meet passengers on arrival.
