York Theatre Royal have the perfect accompaniment to a cosy night at the theatre this Autumn.

To celebrate the launch of new discount membership scheme for those aged **16-30**, York Theatre Royal have partnered up with Ringtons for a special pre-show treat at an upcoming performance of new show* The Penelopiad.*

On **Thursday 29 October from 6:30pm**, 16-30 members are invited to join Ringtons and York Theatre Royal in the Lower Foyer before the evening performance of* The Penelopiad* for a free vegan hot chocolate and cookie.

The show is exuberantly witty retelling of *The Odyssey* from the perspective of Penelope and her maids, written by Margaret Atwood, author of the modern feminist classic*The Handmaid’s Tale*.

The show is directed by Juliet Forster and stars Amelia Bullmore (*Riot Women, Happy Valley, Scott & Bailey*) as Penelope.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/9-The-Penelopiad_Pamela-Raith-Photography-300x200.webp)

A 16-30s YTR membership is completely free and gives access to discounted £15 tickets for *The Penelopiad* and many of YTR’s Main House productions.

Tickets can be booked online or via the Box Office (add a 16-30 Membership to your basket when checking out).

Head to the Box Office on the night to collect your 16-30s Ringtons treat voucher.
