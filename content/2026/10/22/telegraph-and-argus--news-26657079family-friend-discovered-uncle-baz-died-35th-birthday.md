A man has paid tribute to a long-time family friend whose body was found.

Barry Jarvis, 82, from Wibsey, Bradford, was last seen leaving home at about 7.15am on Saturday, October 10.

West Yorkshire Police said a body was found at Royds Hall Dam, Bradford, at about 9.33am on Sunday, October 18.

The force said the death was not being treated as suspicious.

![Barry, 82, missing from the Wibsey area of Bradford.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21564329.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mr Jarvis, 82, missing from the Wibsey area of Bradford.

## Man pays tribute

Damian Bolton, 35, from Little Horton, told the T&A he had known Mr Jarvis all his life.

Mr Bolton said: "Barry has been around since I was born. He was a long family friend and I always called him Uncle Baz."

Mr Bolton learned Mr Jarvis had been found in the dam on Sunday — his 35th birthday.

He described the late Mr Jarvis as an "absolute gentleman".

He remembered Mr Jarvis making him a personalised wooden garage when he was a child.

Mr Bolton added: "He loved making dolls houses, antiques and old toys just like myself."

They also shared a love of charity shopping.

He admitted that they did not have any photographs together, because they both didn't like having them taken.
