Catholic bishops and archbishops gathered around US President Donald Trump in the Oval Office on Thursday, 22 October, to pray for the president and the country.

The footage, shared by White House official Margo Martin, shows the religious leaders surrounding Trump at his desk during the prayer. Denver Archbishop James Golka was among those present, according to Denver Catholic, which reported on the meeting.
