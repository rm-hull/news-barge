A Darlington motorist has been banned from driving after admitting he failed to provide a blood sample during a suspected drink or drug-driving investigation.

Sam Stockdale, 28, of Brinkburn Avenue, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 19.

He admitted failing to provide a blood specimen in Darlington on July 21 during a police investigation into suspected driving offences under the Road Traffic Act.

Stockdale was banned from driving for 17 months, although he can cut the ban by 17 weeks by completing a rehabilitation course by September 20, 2027.

He was also fined £1,166, ordered to pay £85 in Crown Prosecution Service costs and handed a £466 victim surcharge.

The court ordered Stockdale to pay £100 a month from November 16, 2026. His guilty plea was taken into account when setting the penalties.
