A rising star from The Voice UK is set to take the stage at this year’s Diwali in Middlesbrough.

Molly J, a semi-finalist on the ITV talent show and part of Team Tom Jones, will perform at Centre Square on Saturday, October 31, as part of the festival celebrating South Asian culture.

The event will feature live music, dance, street food, children’s activities, funfair rides, and a Bollywood-themed firework finale.

Molly J said: "I’m incredibly excited to be part of the Diwali Festival.

"I’ve grown up surrounded by different cultures and musical influences, and I truly believe music is one of the most powerful ways to bring people together.

"Diwali is a beautiful celebration of light, hope and togetherness, and I’m looking forward to bringing my own energy, culture and sound to the stage."

Kash Patel, producer of Diwali in Middlesbrough, said: "Once again we are delighted to bring another rising star to showcase her talent on our Diwali stage.

"Her performance promises to bring a fresh and exciting energy to the festival, combining her classical musical foundations with contemporary sounds and international influences."

The free family event will run from 1pm to 7pm and will be hosted by BBC Radio Tees DJ Shakk.
