BUSINESSES gathered for a day of networking, workshops and support.

The second annual Bradford Business Fair took place at Mercure Bradford Bankfield Hotel on October 15, bringing together companies from across West Yorkshire for a day of networking, learning, and business support.

The event featured a business-to-business exhibition with stands from organisations including Bradford Live, Cosurica, Your New Car Ltd, Green View Projects Electrical Division, Utility Warehouse, and Wizu Workspace.

Kathy Haines, director at Liverpool BA, said: "We've been holding annual B2B events across the North West region since 2002, and had been thinking about holding events in West Yorkshire for some time.

"Our partnership with Mercure Bradford Bankfield Hotel – via a connection made at one of our NW events – gave us the opportunity to do so."

The event also offered one-to-one advice, information on business support services, and a programme of free workshops delivered by local experts.

Sessions covered topics such as pricing, content marketing, and changes to unfair dismissal rules.

A speed networking session was hosted by Merewyn Sayers of Work Rest or Play.

The fair was organised by Liverpool BA, which is approaching its 25th year of running business events.
