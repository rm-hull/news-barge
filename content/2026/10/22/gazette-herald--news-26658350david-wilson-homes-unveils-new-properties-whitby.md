A housing developer has launched the next phase of a popular scheme on the North Yorkshire coast.

David Wilson Homes has announced the release of new properties at Chaloners Green in Whitby, following strong demand for the initial phase of the development.

Located just outside the town centre, the scheme offers three- and four-bedroom homes within walking distance of Whitby’s amenities.

Steven Ball, sales director at David Wilson Homes North East, said: "It is fantastic to see the community at Chaloners Green forming, and we cannot wait to build upon that success with this new phase of homes.

"We encourage prospective buyers in North Yorkshire, or looking to move to the area, to visit Chaloners Green, tour the homes and speak to our dedicated sales team about schemes and deals."

The new phase includes house types not previously available in the first phase, offering buyers additional choice.

All homes are designed to be energy efficient, and prices start from £289,995.

Chaloners Green has been especially popular with buyers looking to relocate to Whitby or settle in a coastal community.

More information about the development is available on the David Wilson Homes website.
