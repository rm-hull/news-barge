A NORTH Yorkshire school is celebrating following a recent inspection which recognised its 'loving community'.

Gillamoor CE Primary School are celebrating following their recent Statutory Inspection of Anglican & Methodist Schools (SIAMS) inspection on September 25.

The inspection praised the kind and encouraging relationships that are central to daily life at the school. These positive relationships create a deep sense of belonging, with pupils and adults alike feeling valued, nurtured and supported within a loving school community.

Inspectors were particularly impressed by the wide range of leadership and enrichment opportunities available to pupils. Through demonstrating courage, love and service, pupils are developing a strong understanding of justice, responsibility and the importance of supporting others.

Headteacher Samantha Bond, said: "The report highlights how our Christian vision and values are at the heart of school life, enabling us to serve our rural community well. Inspectors recognised the school's strong aspiration for every individual to flourish, including those who may need additional support, ensuring that all members of our community are valued and able to achieve their potential.

"The report also recognised the strength of our community partnerships, especially our close collaboration with the local church. These relationships enrich daily life and learning, contributing positively to the spiritual development and flourishing of both pupils and staff.

"I am incredibly proud of this positive recognition and would like to thank our pupils, staff, governors, families, church partners and wider community for their continued support. Together, we will continue to provide a nurturing environment where everyone is encouraged to flourish and achieve their very best."
