Though perhaps not the most memorable of Edgerunners 2's characters, Weak Kingsley, or "The King" has the best drip, including his spectacles and crown-emblazoned coat.

No wonder, then, that Cyberpunk 2077 added the new Clampdown quest that lets you get his outfit and gigantic hand cannon in the Edgerunners 2.5 update, similar to how it did for David's jacket and Rebecca's Shotgun for season one. To complete this quest, you need to:

* Complete The Heist, two gigs for Wakako, and then wait for her message, starting the Clampdown quest.
* Read the message, head to the meeting at BuryGer diner and speak to King, accepting the job.
* Go to the prison.
* When directed, head to the bathroom and interact with the duffle bag under the bench.

The quest will continue, but you'll have King's iconic Coronation Tech Revolver, plus his full outfit, including the glasses.
