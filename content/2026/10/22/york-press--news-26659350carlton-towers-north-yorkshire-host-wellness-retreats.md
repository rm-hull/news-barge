## Historic Yorkshire stately home to offer yoga and meditation retreats

Other activities include forest bathing, massage and Reiki treatments, and a historical tour of the stately home *(Image: Carlton Tower Retreats)*

A gothic-style stately home in North Yorkshire will host private wellness retreats.

Carlton Towers is offering the three-day retreats in 2027, focusing on yoga, nature, and meditation.

Limited to 20 people per retreat, the sessions will be held in various parts of the estate such as the ancient woodland and the Walled Garden Vineyard.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21615590.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Carlton Towers is offering three-day wellness retreats in 2027 *(Image: Carlton Tower Retreats)*

The sessions will be led by Mariska Gunter, a certified yoga instructor, sylvotherapist, Reiki practitioner, and holistic masseuse.

Other activities include forest bathing, massage and Reiki treatments, a historical tour of the stately home, and a lavender martini demonstration.

Guests will also enjoy locally sourced meals and a choice of bedrooms for relaxation.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21615591.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The sessions will be led by Mariska Gunter *(Image: Carlton Tower Retreats)*

The retreats have been designed to offer a more private house stay feel than a typical wellness retreat.

The retreats are priced at £950 per person or £750 per person for double occupancy.

The 2027 dates for the retreats are as follows: Friday, April 9, to Sunday, April 11; Tuesday, June 8, to Thursday, June 10; Friday, September 10, to Sunday, September 12; and Monday, October 25, to Wednesday, October 27.

For more information, visit www.carltontowers.co.uk/retreats.
