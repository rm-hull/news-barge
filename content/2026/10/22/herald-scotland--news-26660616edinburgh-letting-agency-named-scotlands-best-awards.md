Edinburgh-based Arden Property Management has won three awards at the ESTAS Awards, including Gold for Best Letting Agency in Scotland, as voted for by landlords and tenants.

The agency also received Gold for Best Letting Agency in Edinburgh. The ESTAS Awards are based on verified customer reviews rather than decisions made by a judging panel.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21617408.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Malcolm Brown, Catriona Smith, Phil Spencer and Kat Usher. *(Image: Simply Photography)*

More than 300,000 landlords and tenants from across the UK contributed feedback for this year’s awards, which were presented at a ceremony in London on Friday.

Arden also received the Best in Postcode Award for the EH10 area earlier this year. The agency has also been recognised with a Gold Award from The British Property Awards for letting agencies covering postcodes EH6 to EH12.

Established in 2006, Arden Property Management manages properties across Edinburgh and the Lothians.

The agency is also shortlisted for further awards due to be announced later in 2026.

Simon Brown, founder of The ESTAS, said: "The property industry is, and always will be, a people business.

"Technology may help us streamline processes, crunch data, and even predict the market, but it can’t replace empathy, trust, or the human touch that defines our profession, which is what The ESTAS Awards are all about."

Catriona Smith, senior partner at Arden Property Management, said: "We are thrilled with this triple win.

"We pride ourselves on a personable, professional, and people first approach, and having our clients judge our performance makes this recognition extra special.

"This success is the perfect way to mark our milestone 20th anniversary year.

"In the last two decades we have successfully let 10,500 properties and built a strong reputation for trusted service.

"We’re over the moon to receive this recognition in our 20th year, and couldn’t be more grateful to our community for their support.

"We look forward to continuing to nurture our relationships with our landlords and tenants as we move into 2027, here’s to the next 20 years."
