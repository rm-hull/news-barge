A North Yorkshire heritage railway will celebrate Halloween with a special family event featuring train rides, games and children's activities.

Families can catch a special Wensleydale Railway service from Bedale to Scruton Station on Saturday, October 31, with activities running from 2pm to 5.30pm.

Tickets cost £6 for children and £10 for adults, including return travel from Bedale to Scruton and the children's activities.

Wensleydale Railway runs through North Yorkshire between Leeming Bar and Leyburn, stopping at Bedale and Scruton.

Volunteers run most of the railway's special events and regular passenger services.

Scruton station returned to passenger services in 2024 after decades closed, adding another stop and helping the railway expand its family events programme.

The railway also stages seasonal events such as Santa trains, dining trips and themed family days.
