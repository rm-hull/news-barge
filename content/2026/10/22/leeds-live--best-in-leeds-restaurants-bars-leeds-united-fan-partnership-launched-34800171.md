# Leeds united fan partnership launched with popular city centre restaurant

## Leeds United fans get enjoy a great result

Leeds United fans can enjoy a new incentive with a city centre restaurant.

Leeds rooftop restaurant CrowdedHouse, which recently celebrated its first birthday, is showing backing to the Whites with an offer aimed at the club’s supporters. CrowdedHouse is offering Leeds United season ticket holders 15 per cent off its à la carte menu every day, giving fans the opportunity to enjoy dining at the restaurant throughout the season.

Additionally, when Leeds United secure a victory, there's will be even more reason to celebrate, with the discount increasing to 25 per cent off the à la carte menu after the game on matchdays. Supporters simply need to show their season ticket or season ticket ID to claim their discount.

Since opening in July last year, CrowdedHouse, based at Trinity Leeds, has become a popular destination for dining and drinks in the city, with its year-round rooftop terrace and focus on locally sourced Yorkshire produce. The new partnership aims to build on the restaurant's commitment to supporting the local community, while bringing together two Leeds names united by a shared pride in the city.

For CrowdedHouse owner and Leeds United fan, John Quinlan, the partnership is particularly meaningful. He said: "I know the whole CrowdedHouse team is really excited. Leeds United is such a huge part of this city's identity, and to see our name alongside such an iconic club is pretty special.

"At CrowdedHouse, backing local has always been important to us. Whether that's sourcing ingredients from Yorkshire farmers and producers or supporting other independent businesses, we want to play our part in making Leeds a brilliant place to visit.

"Football has an incredible way of bringing people together, and that's something we believe in at CrowdedHouse too. We're all about creating a place where people can come together, enjoy great food and make memories. We've achieved so much in our first year, and becoming a Leeds United business club partner feels like another big milestone for us. We're looking forward to getting behind the team, welcoming supporters to the restaurant and hopefully celebrating plenty of wins together this season."
