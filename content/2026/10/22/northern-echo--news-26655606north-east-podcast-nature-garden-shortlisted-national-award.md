A North East podcast celebrating wildlife, dialect and gardening has been shortlisted for a national award after attracting listeners in 127 countries.

The Morpeth-made Nature Garden is shortlisted for Best Regional Podcast at the 2026 Independent Podcast Awards.

Launched during the pandemic, the programme covers Northumbrian wildlife, gardening, heritage and dialect, reaching listeners in more than 3,800 cities across 127 countries.

![Group taking part in podcast](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21605298.jpg?type=mds-article-620)from left, Tom Pattinson, Tom Cadwallender, Carl Stiansen and Steve Lowe *(Image: North News & Pictures)*

Carl Stiansen, who lives on the Holy Island of Lindisfarne, said: "We're thrilled that The Nature Garden has been shortlisted for Best Regional Podcast.

"The programme is proudly rooted in Northumberland – our people, wildlife, gardens, dialect and heritage – so this recognition means a great deal to our small team of volunteer contributors.

"Since beginning during lockdown, we've grown into a global community while staying true to our unmistakably local voice."

The podcast’s informal chats cover seasonal gardening, birds, wildlife, conservation and local history, giving listeners the feel of a conversation over the garden fence.

Regular contributors are gardener Tom Pattinson, ornithologist Tom Cadwallender, and wildlife and conservation expert Steve Lowe.

The podcast also showcases regional and national wildlife charities and raises awareness of their work.

![Group taking part in podcast](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21605299.jpg?type=mds-article-620)From left, Tom Pattinson, Tom Cadwallender, Carl Stiansen and Steve Lowe *(Image: North News & Pictures)*

The Nature Garden has attracted more than 36,000 downloads across 80 episodes and regularly featured in the UK Top 20 and US Top 100 gardening and leisure podcast charts.

It has also charted in Japan, New Zealand, Canada and Australia, reaching number one in the Seychelles.

Mr Pattinson, from Lesbury, said: "When Covid restrictions kept us out of the Lionheart Radio studio in Alnwick, we could no longer present our programme, The Weekending Show.

"We began the podcast as a passionate offshoot, and it remains a homespun production rooted in Northumberland while reaching listeners around the world."

This year’s shortlist was chosen from a record 550 entries.

Winners will be announced at Kings Place in London on Wednesday, November 4, 2026.

The full 2026 Independent Podcast Awards shortlist is available at independentpodcastawards.com.
