Firefighters tackled a fire in a farmyard last night (Wednesday).

Crews from Pickering and Malton attended the incident near Newton on Rawcliffe, north of Pickering, at **6.47pm**.

A straw chopper was on fire.

#### Newton on Rawcliffe, Pickering

Two teams wearing breathing apparatus used hose reel jets to put out the flames.

With help from the farmer operating a telehandler, they also used pitchforks to turn over the straw and dampen down hotspots.
