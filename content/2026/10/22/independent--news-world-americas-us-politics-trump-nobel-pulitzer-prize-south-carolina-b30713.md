President Trump complained again about not winning the Nobel Peace Prize at a midterm rally on Wednesday - and claimed he should also have won a Pulitzer.

The commander-in-chief was at Clemson University in South Carolina to support Senator Darline Graham amid a tight U.S. Senate race, but then veered off onto one of his ongoing obsessions.

​“Last year, the person who got it, a very high quality woman, you know who that is,” he said, referring to Venezuelan activist María Corina Machado.

![President Trump complained about not winning a Nobel Peace Prize when he was meant to be drumming up support for a Republican senator](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/22/09/2300828259...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“And she said that, ‘No, no, I don’t deserve the Nobel Prize. There’s only one person in the world that deserves it.’

​“In fact, she said he deserves it more than any human being that’s gotten it because I settled all these wars.”

​Trump went on to claim that he was told that he could win the prize in 2026, only for someone else to receive it once again.

​“They’ve lost such respect,” he remarked, before claiming to have settled eight wars - a figure that has been widely disputed.

​Then, Trump took a swipe at 85-year-old Navanethem “Navi” Pillay - the winner of the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize.

​The Nobel Prize website says that she received the award in recognition “for her efforts to promote peace and international law.”

![Trump criticized Nobel Peace Prize winner Navi Pillay (pictured) during his speech, claiming that she hates Israel with a 'passion'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/22/11/2026-10-13T104304Z_118526138_RC2A2OALVU0S_RTRMADP_3_NOBEL-PRIZE-PEACE-PILLAY.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

“The woman that got it, I don’t know where the hell she comes (sic),” he began. “The only thing I know is she hates Israel. That’s the only thing.”

​“That’s the only thing; she hates Israel with a passion,” he claimed.

​“I didn't get the Nobel Prize. I didn't get the Pulitzer. I should have gotten the Pulitzer for writing, too,” he added.

Trump went on to take a swipe at previous winners of one of the most prestigious awards for writing.

​“The people that got it shouldn't have gotten it, you know?” he asked. “They got a Pulitzer Prize for their accurate and wonderful writing on the subject of Russia, Russia, Russia. But they had it wrong. They all had it wrong.”​

![Trump was speaking in support of Senator Darline Graham amid a tense South Carolina US Senate election](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/22/11/2301396347...jpg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Trump previously filed a defamation lawsuit against the Pulitzer Prize Board, demanding that they rescind prizes awarded to *The New York Times* and*The Washington Post* for covering alleged Russian interference in the 2016 election. The case remains ongoing.

​At another point in his speech, he took another jab at Pillay although he appeared to confuse the two prizes.

​“They gave the wonderful peace prize, right? Does anybody know the name of that peace prize?” he asked. “They gave the Pulitzer Prize to a young lady. I think she’s 80 years old.”
