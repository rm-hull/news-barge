## Grade II listed village pub goes up for sale for £850,000

The Oak Tree Inn, located in Helperby near Easingwold, is on the market for £850,000 and offers a blend of traditional Yorkshire pub charm and modern hospitality facilities. *(Image: Everard Cole)*

A Grade II listed village inn has been put up for sale.

The Oak Tree Inn, located in Helperby near Easingwold, is on the market for £850,000 and offers a blend of traditional Yorkshire pub charm and modern hospitality facilities.

The property features a restaurant, private dining room, and six en-suite guest bedrooms.

Inside, the inn boasts exposed timber beams, open fireplaces, wood-burning stoves, traditional wooden flooring, and a solid oak bar.

The main bar seats around 45 customers, while the restaurant can accommodate 40 diners.

A private dining room on the first floor offers space for up to 30 guests, making it suitable for events and celebrations.

The six guest bedrooms include a ground-floor accessible room, adding to the inn’s versatility.

A spokesperson for Everard Cole said: "The combination of traditional pub facilities, restaurant dining and guest accommodation provides opportunities for an incoming operator to build upon the existing business and explore further growth."

Outside, the property includes a beer garden and dedicated customer parking.

Helperby is located within easy reach of Boroughbridge, Easingwold, and York.

The Oak Tree Inn is being sold freehold.

Offers are invited in the region of £850,000 plus VAT.
