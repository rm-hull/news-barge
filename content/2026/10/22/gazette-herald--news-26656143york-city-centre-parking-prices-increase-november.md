York parking charges will rise at many city-centre locations from November 12 during the seasonal event period.

The City of York Council has announced changes to on-street and off-street parking charges, as well as the cost of a Minster Badge.

The revised charges will begin at 12.01am on November 12.

At the affected event locations, they will remain in place until midnight on December 21, when charges will return to those introduced on April 1.

The changes apply to parking places covered by the York Parking, Stopping and Waiting Traffic Order 2014 and the York Off-Street Parking Places Order 2013.

For several on-street locations, the charge for up to one hour will rise from £5 to £5.50 on all days during the event period.

This applies to Carmelite Street, Lord Mayor’s Walk, North Street, Piccadilly, Skeldergate, Tanner’s Moat and Walmgate.

At those locations, parking for one to two hours will rise from £10 to £10.90.

The charge for two to three hours will increase from £11.50 to £13.90.

Evening parking after 6pm will rise from £4 to £4.80.

The same event charges will apply to on-street parking in the inner Respark zones.

These include Bishophill Senior, Cromwell Road, Cumberland Street, George Street, Leadmill Lane, Lower Friargate, Lower Priory Street, Margaret Street and Walmgate Respark.

The charge for up to one hour will be £5.50, compared with £5.

Charges for large vehicles at Foss Islands Road will also increase.

Vehicles parked for more than two hours will pay £11.30, compared with £10.30.

Parking for two to five hours will cost £24.70, up from £20.60.

The five-to-12-hour charge will be £25.80, compared with £23.20.

Off-street charges will rise at Bootham Bar, Castle, Coppergate Esplanade, Nunnery Lane, Marygate, Monk Bar, St George’s Field, Union Terrace and Foss Bank.

At most of these car parks, parking for up to one hour will cost £5.50 during the event period, compared with £5.

Parking for one to two hours will cost £10.90, while two to three hours will cost £13.90.

Parking for three to four hours will rise from £15.30 to £18. Parking for four to five hours will increase from £19.20 to £22.10.

At Nunnery Lane, Marygate, Monk Bar, St George’s Field, Union Terrace and Foss Bank, the all-day charge will increase from £23.20 to £25.

The Minster Badge rate will also change at the listed locations.

During the event period, up to one hour will cost £3.90, compared with £3.50.

One to two hours will cost £7.60, compared with £7.

Two to three hours will cost £9.70, compared with £8.10.

For some on-street areas, including Lawrence Street, Micklegate and Priory Street, the listed standard charges remain £3 for up to one hour, £6 for one to two hours and £9 for two to three hours.

The evening charge is £4.

Minster Badge charges are £1.50, £3, £4.50 and £2 respectively.

The council will also reduce the Minster Badge cost from £40 to £15.

The badge will be valid for one year instead of two, and the new charge will apply from November 12.
