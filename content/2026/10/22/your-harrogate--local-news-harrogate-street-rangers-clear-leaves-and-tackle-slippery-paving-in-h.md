Harrogate BID's Street Rangers have been clearing fallen leaves and jet washing paving on Royal Parade and Crown Place.

The team has been tackling the build-up of autumn leaves, with wet weather making surfaces slippery.

The work aims to keep the two town centre streets cleaner and safer for pedestrians.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad9b824cf8d2b36967.png)

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad9b8259b18a7dc7bf.png)

A Harrogate BID spokesperson said:

> “With autumn leaves piling up and the rain making things slippery, the team has been clearing the leaves and giving the flooring a good jet wash.
>
> “The difference is amazing, leaving the area cleaner and safer for everyone."

They added:

> “A big thank you for keeping our streets looking their best, whatever the weather!”
