A disqualified County Durham driver has been banned from driving again after he was caught uninsured at the wheel of a van.

Jake Hopper, 25, of no fixed abode, Spennymoor, was sentenced at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 19, after admitting driving while disqualified and uninsured.

Hopper was caught driving a Ford Transit van on Middridge Road, Newton Aycliffe, on July 23, despite his ban and without third-party insurance.

Magistrates handed Hopper a further six-month driving ban and fined him £750.

He must also pay £85 in Crown Prosecution Service costs and a £300 surcharge.
