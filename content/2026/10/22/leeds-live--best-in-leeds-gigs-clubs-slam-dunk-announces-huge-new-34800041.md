# Slam Dunk announces huge new names for 2027 line up

## The huge rock festival takes places annually at Temple Newsam in Leeds

Slam Dunk will welcome some huge names to Temple Newsam in Leeds next summer.

The team from the event, which holds a southern edition in Hatfield Park, Hertfordshire, have revealed the latest batch of acts for Slam Dunk 2027. There are 18 new acts on the bill.

Some of these include Billy Talent, Cobra Starship, Superheaven, Joyce Manor, The Paradox, Amira Elfeky, Forever The Sickest Kids, The Family Business Ltd, Ben Quad, Games We Play and more. You can find ticket information here.

The latest additions join the previously announced Bowling For Soup, Simple Plan, Killswitch Engage, Beartooth, Sleeping With Sirens, The Used and The Front Bottoms - among others.

Slam Dunk Festival 2027 will take place in Leeds at Temple Newsam on Saturday, May 29. We will bring you more details about the event ahead of the big rock party.
