Amanda Owen and her former husband Clive are set to share a special family moment in the next episode of Our Farm Next Door.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess and Clive, who announced their separation in 2022, will be seen coming together after a demanding lambing season at Ravenseat.

The episode, scheduled for Monday, October 26, follows the family as they tackle one of the busiest and most challenging periods of the farming calendar.

With spring arriving in Swaledale, the Owens are working around the clock to care for their sheep and newborn lambs.

Clive and son Miles also face problems with a waterlogged field, while work continues at Anty John's, the neighbouring farmhouse being restored by the family.

But after weeks of hard work, there is an opportunity to celebrate.

The gathering offers viewers another glimpse of the family's continuing working relationship following Amanda and Clive's separation.

Although no longer together as a couple, the pair remain connected through their nine children and their shared responsibilities on the farm.

Their family life first captured the attention of viewers through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed the challenges of raising nine children in one of the country's most remote farming communities.

The newer More4 programme follows the Owens as they balance everyday life at Ravenseat with the restoration of Anty John's.

The forthcoming episode also features an Easter egg hunt for the younger children and further preparations at the family's cream tea van.

Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids continues on Monday, October 26, on More4.
