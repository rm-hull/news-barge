Surveillance footage captured the moment the tremor struck, showing people **rushing out of buildings** to escape the shaking. The earthquake’s epicentre was recorded at a**shallow depth of 10 kilometres**, with coordinates placing it at 32.40 degrees north latitude and 93.79 degrees east longitude. The depth and strength of the quake prompted an** immediate emergency response** from Chinese authorities.

The **China Earthquake Administration** activated a**Level-II emergency response** as rescue operations got under way. The**Nagqu Battalion** of the Lhasa Fire and Rescue Mobile Detachment dispatched an**advance team of seven firefighters** equipped with**life-detection devices** and specialist rescue equipment.

Footage showed emergency personnel preparing to leave and vehicles travelling towards the affected area. The teams were sent to **assess conditions and carry out rescue operations** following the earthquake.

Local authorities later said **casualties had been reported**, but did not immediately provide a confirmed death toll. At least** two people were injured**in a village near the epicentre, while** cracks were reported in several homes**in nearby communities. Officials were checking villages around the epicentre for** damage and possible casualties**.
