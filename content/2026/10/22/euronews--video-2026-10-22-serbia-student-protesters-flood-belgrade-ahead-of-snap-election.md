Carrying Serbian flags and burning torches, the marchers were **greeted by cheering crowds** who filled the streets of the capital. The**atmosphere was festive**, with students and supporters singing, dancing and applauding as the procession made its way into the city. Some demonstrators held signs declaring***"Students win!"***, while others displayed symbols of the** student movement**.

Images showed young demonstrators dancing together and carrying banners, including one featuring a **crossed-out portrait of a man resembling former president Aleksandar Vučić**.

The gathering marked the arrival of students who had travelled from different parts of Serbia to take part in the **final mobilisation before the parliamentary vote**. Thousands of supporters turned out to welcome them, waving national flags and joining in the celebrations.

The rally was scheduled to take place in Belgrade ahead of the **weekend elections**, with the student movement seeking to** make its presence felt** in the final days before voters head to the polls.
