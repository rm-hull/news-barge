Police are appealing for witnesses after a black Ford Fiesta was stolen from a layby on Hookstone Road in Harrogate.

The car, registration LG09 XSM, was taken between approximately 1:50pm and 3:37pm on Friday 18th September.

North Yorkshire Police officers are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the theft or has information that could help their investigation.

They are particularly appealing for dashcam or CCTV footage from the area at the time.

Anyone with information should email [Keilee.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Keilee.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting reference 12260188868.

Information can also be submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting “Tell us about” and then “An existing case or report”, or through its Live Chat service, available Monday to Friday from 9am to 5pm.

Anyone unable to use the online options can call 101.

Alternatively, information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
