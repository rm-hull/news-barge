POLICE are appealing for information following a theft from a discount retailer in a York shopping park.




North Yorkshire Police said it happened at Home Bargains in Foss Islands Retail Park in Foss Islands Road on Wednesday, September 9.

Officers have released a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to, saying he may have information which could assist its investigation into the theft.

North Yorkshire Police said anyone who recognises the person can email PC1488 Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk), or visit the force's website.

Alternatively, the force can be contacted by phone via 101 or Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
