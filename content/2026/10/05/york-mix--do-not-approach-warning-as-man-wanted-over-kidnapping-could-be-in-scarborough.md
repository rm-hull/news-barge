Police are searching for a wanted man who is believed to be in Scarborough.

Christopher Young, 44, is wanted in connection with kidnap and assault offences that happened in Burton-upon-Trent.

Young, who is from Staffordshire, is believed to be in Scarborough town centre or the South Cliff area. He also has links to Manchester.

He is described as a white man, balding and is 6ft 2in.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Young and we are now appealing for your help to find him.

“Members of the public are advised not to approach Young and to instead call 999 immediately should they have a sighting of him.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online.”
