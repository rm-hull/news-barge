Aldi is bringing back a £49.99 air fryer to stores, which is £220 cheaper than a branded alternative.

Described as "much loved" by the supermarket, the Vertical Air Fryer is back as part of Aldi's Winter Kitchen range.

The supermarket also highlights the air fryer as a cheaper option than some well-known brands.

For example, it cites the Ninja Double Stack XL 9.5L Air Fryer SL400UK, which is usually priced at £269.99.

![](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/resources/images/21536767.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Aldi's Vertical Air Fryer comes with nine pre-set programmes *(Image: Aldi)*

## What can Aldi's Vertical Air Fryer do?

Aldi describes the Vertical Air Fryer as a "real kitchen game-changer".

It adds: "Delivering delicious, crispy results in a clever, stacked design, it's perfect for everything from roasting root vegetables to giving Sunday dinner an air-mazing upgrade."

The air fryer features two independently controlled baskets, a touchable digital display and nine pre-set programmes.

It also includes a non-stick frying basket for easier cleaning.

The Vertical Air Fryer is in shops now, while stocks last.

## What else is in Aldi's Winter Kitchen range?

The full Winter Kitchen range includes a Premium Capsule Coffee Machine for £34.99.

Aldi describes the machine as "the perfect way to perk up the day without the high price tag" and says it serves "barista-style coffee at the touch of a button."

The retailer's Soup Maker (£24.99) also makes a return, blending and heating ingredients for quick, homemade soups.

It adds: "Perfect for creating hearty lunches with minimal effort, it blends and heats ingredients to perfection, allowing shoppers to ladle up a soup-er hearty meal in no time."
