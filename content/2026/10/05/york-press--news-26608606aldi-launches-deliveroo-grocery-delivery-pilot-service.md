Aldi is launching a new on-demand grocery delivery service.

In a partnership with Deliveroo, customers living near eight participating stores will be able to order around 2,000 Aldi products for delivery to their doorstep using the Deliveroo app.

Julie Ashfield, chief commercial officer at Aldi UK, said: "Customers tell us they want shopping to be as convenient as possible, so launching this service with Deliveroo is a great opportunity to make it even easier to shop at Aldi.

"From tomorrow, shoppers will be able to get their Aldi favourites delivered straight to their door, while enjoying the award-winning quality and market-leading value they expect from us."

## Aldi launches Deliveroo service

The pilot programme will begin in stores located in Ashford, Dover, Norwich, Ipswich, Letchworth Garden City, Margate, Strood, and Tamworth.

Available products include fresh fruit and vegetables, meat, seafood, dairy, bread, and household essentials.

Deliveroo riders will collect, pack, and deliver orders directly from Aldi stores, using the Deliveroo rider app to scan items before completing purchases with a prepaid Rider Card.

Rob Harris, VP Merchant and Chief Revenue Officer at Deliveroo, said: "We are excited to welcome Aldi to the Deliveroo platform through a brand-new service, Shop & Deliver.

"Consumers in several locations across the country will now be able to enjoy Aldi’s great value and selection delivered to their doorstep on-demand."
