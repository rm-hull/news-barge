Illegal tobacco, cigarettes and vaping products have been seized during an operation targeting suspected sellers in a town centre.

Neighbourhood Policing Officers joined Trading Standards on Friday as part of Calderdale Council's Operation Compass, which aims to tackle the sale of counterfeit and illicit products.

## Several premises visited

Officers and Trading Standards staff visited a number of locations across Halifax.

The team was supported by a specialist tobacco detection dog, which was used to help identify hidden illicit products during searches of the premises.

A quantity of illegal goods was seized during the operation, although authorities have not released details of the exact amount recovered.

## How to report concerns

Anyone with information about the sale of illegal tobacco, cigarettes or vaping products can report it anonymously to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111.

Investigations into the seized products are ongoing.
