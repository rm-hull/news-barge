A historic hotel with a luxury spa, wedding venue and conference facilities is on the market for £1.25 million.

Hall Garth Hotel & Revive Spa, in Coatham Mundeville, is set within 3.7 acres of grounds just outside Darlington.

The business includes 56 en-suite bedrooms, a restaurant, bar, function suites, meeting rooms and parking for more than 150 vehicles.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537715.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537718.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537716.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

The sale includes the recently refurbished Revive Spa, with a gym, sauna, steam room, hot tubs and treatment rooms.

Hall Garth has several income streams, bringing in money from weddings, conferences, corporate bookings, leisure guests, spa memberships and year-round events.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537717.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537722.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

North of England Estates has owned the venue since 2012 and invested in refurbishing the spa and several guest bedrooms.

The owners are now seeking a buyer for one of Darlington’s best-known hospitality venues.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537719.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

Robert Smithson, director in Colliers' UK Hotels Agency team, said: "Hall Garth represents an excellent opportunity to acquire a well-established hotel business with multiple income streams.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537720.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21537721.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: COLLIERS)*

"Its location between the historic cities of York and Newcastle, and proximity to the North York Moors and Yorkshire Dales National Parks, supports year-round demand from corporate guests, tourists, weddings and events.

"The hotel is a recognised destination within the regional hospitality market and will appeal to a wide range of buyers, from experienced owner-operators to investors looking to expand their presence in the UK hotel sector."

The same owners are also seeking a buyer for The Morritt Hotel & Garage Spa in Barnard Castle, on the market for more than £1.3 million.

The Morritt Hotel includes 26 bedrooms, a bar, restaurant, function suite, lounges and a standalone spa, alongside a recently converted seven-bedroom holiday cottage called The Burns.
