Not only does it warm a small- to medium-sized room in the colder months, it also serves as a cooling fan during summer heatwaves, and filters dirt and allergens out of the air all year round.

This three-in-one device got the seal of approval from our reviewer Becca Caddy, so I know it'll be a reliable buy.
