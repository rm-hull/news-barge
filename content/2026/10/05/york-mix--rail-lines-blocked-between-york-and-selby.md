There’s disruption on trains in our area this afternoon (Monday).

LNER is reporting a points failure between York and Selby / Doncaster.

It means all lines are blocked. As a result, trains will be delayed by up to 30 minutes or revised.

Disruption is expected until 4pm. LNER recommends passengers check your journey using the National Rail real-time journey planner.
