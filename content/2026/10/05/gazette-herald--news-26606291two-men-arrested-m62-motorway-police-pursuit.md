TWO men have been arrested after police stopped a suspected stolen vehicle on a Yorkshire motorway.




Humberside Police said it happened yesterday evening (Sunday, October 4) when officers spotted the vehicle being driven erratically on the M62 near Goole.

A force spokesperson said a 31-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of drink driving, drug driving, driving while disqualified, driving without insurance, and theft from a motor vehicle after failing a roadside breath test and drugs wipe.

The passenger, a 27-year-old man, who was subject to a stop and search by police was arrested on suspicion of possession of class A drugs and theft of a motor vehicle.

Both men are in police custody and Humberside Police said enquiries remain ongoing.
