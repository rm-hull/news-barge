From the outside this bungalow in Chester-le-Street might not stand out from the crowd... but, as the saying goes: never judge a book by its cover.

Inside it has been completely remodelled and upgraded with incredible attention to detail to create a stunning contemporary open-plan living space with a stylish interior and high calibre specification.

Situated in the sought-after Garden Farm development just a short distance to the west of the town centre, this bungalow is perfect for a variety of buyers, from those who can’t be bothered with stairs and prefer single-storey living to a single buyer or downsizing couple.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21521780.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Remodelled two-bedroom bungalow in Chester-le-Street with open-plan living, solar panels, west-facing garden, carport and 21ft garage. Offers over £220,000 *(Image: JW WOOD)*

Whatever their reason, the buyers of this superb home will have no need to compromise on anything because the property includes not only stunning accommodation but also a westerly facing back garden and ample off-street parking including a covered carport and a large 21ft garage.

The newly redesigned interior is beautifully presented, with the entrance hall leading to the main living area which extends to over 38ft from front to rear.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21521776.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Remodelled two-bedroom bungalow in Chester-le-Street with open-plan living, solar panels, west-facing garden, carport and 21ft garage. Offers over £220,000 *(Image: JW WOOD)*

This seamlessly blends three separate, but adjoining, areas into one – a newly-refitted kitchen with quartz work surfaces and integrated appliances, a dining area and the lounge, to the rear, which opens to a sun lounge overlooking the back garden and with a door opening into it.

Completing the accommodation are two bedrooms and a stylish newly-refitted shower room.

A useful utility room has also been created, which in turn leads to the attached garage.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21521772.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Remodelled two-bedroom bungalow in Chester-le-Street with open-plan living, solar panels, west-facing garden, carport and 21ft garage. Offers over £220,000 *(Image: JW WOOD)*

The specifications of the bungalow – which is available with no onward chain – have been improved to include gas central heating via radiators with a combination boiler, solar panels and uPVC double glazing.
