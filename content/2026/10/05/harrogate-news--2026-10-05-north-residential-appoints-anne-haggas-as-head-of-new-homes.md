Yorkshire estate agency North Residential has appointed Anne Haggas as its new Head of New Homes, securing a senior hire from property firm Savills to spearhead the growth of its residential development business.

Haggas joins the independent agency following a successful tenure within the residential development and new homes business at Savills. In her new role, she will take charge of North Residential’s New Homes division, managing developer relationships across Yorkshire from early-stage development appraisal and consultancy through to marketing strategies and sales launches.

The strategic appointment marks a major step forward in North Residential’s regional expansion, following the agency’s successful rebranding from the former Knight Frank Harrogate office.

> **Anne Haggas, Head of New Homes at North Residential, said:**
>
> I’m incredibly excited to be joining North Residential at such an important stage in the company’s growth. There is a real opportunity to build something special within the New Homes division, combining strong developer relationships, detailed market knowledge and a genuinely bespoke approach to the marketing and sale of new homes.

The independent agency, known for its personal service and high-quality property marketing, aims to position its specialised division as the premier choice for regional housebuilders seeking commercial and consultancy advice.

> **Harlan Pollitt, Founder of North Residential, said:**
>
> Anne joining North Residential is a significant appointment for us. She brings a wealth of knowledge and experience within the new homes sector, as well as an excellent understanding of developers and the Yorkshire property market. We’ve got ambitious plans for New Homes and want to establish North Residential as the first choice for developers looking for experienced, commercially minded advice alongside exceptional marketing and service.

The appointment comes amidst a wider expansion strategy for the firm as it looks to broaden its footprint across key Yorkshire property markets, building upon its network of branches in locations such as Harrogate, Pocklington, and North Leeds.
