### Travel disruptions

There is the usual traffic on the A66 between Stockton and Elton due to restricted 40mph speed limits.

The A1(M) between J58 (Burtree Interchange) and J56 at Barton has now reopened following a serious collision last night. Traffic approaching is reportedly light with all lanes now clear.

### What will the weather be like in the North East today?

Today is set to have a cloudy start, becoming brighter and slightly breezier by the afternoon.

#### **Today:**

Overcast with some spells of sunshine. A small chance of rain throughout the morning, drying up in the afternoon with light winds expected around midday. Cloud breaking with some sunshine and winds easing later. Maximum temperature 20°C.

#### **Tonight:**

Light showers are expected from around 10pm and into tomorrow morning, while temperatures remain fairly mild and winds ease overnight. Minimum temperature 11 °C.
