Today is James Bond day. And as a 007 super-fan and home office expert, there was only one item I wanted to see get a discount at Amazon: the iconic Parker Jotter ballpoint featured in *GoldenEye*. Now, this one won't explode after three clicks, but I can't fault the smooth writing experience here.

• **Best for:** Professionals, double-O agents, and those of discerning taste who want an affordable, elegant writing solution issued by Q Branch.

• **The deal:** The Parker Jotter refillable ballpoint pen is now $11 (was $13) at Amazon. In the UK, the Jotter is down to £15 (was £17).

• **Why we like it:** When it comes to movie memorabilia, I always like gear that looks like it's in-universe - not plastered with logos that remove the illusion. And this is the exact model Pierce Brosnan used in his 007 debut. I have more than one of these on my desk. It's a great price, and I especially like that it's refillable.

## Why we recommend it

This has to be one of the most affordable gifts to celebrate James Bond day. It's not just that this Parker pen was seen in a James Bond film that appeals to me. I genuinely enjoy writing with this pen. It delivers a smooth writing experience across the page, which is ideal you take a lot of notes at work. I also like that it's refillable, which means you can bring it back to pristine condition after the ink runs dry. Pair it with a Moleskine or Leuchtturm1917 notebook for the best experience.

## Price context & historical value

The Parker Jotter is already an affordable pen, so this discount is a welcome extra. From my investigations, this is about the average price to pay - it's a long time since I've seen this as cheap as its lowest ever price of $6, and it's around 50 cents more than it was during the July Prime Day sale. Of course, Q hasn't packed this one full of his 'special ingredients', which helps keeps the cost down.

✅ **Buy the Parker Jotter if..**.
