# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after Ford Fiesta stolen in broad daylight in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a vehicle was stolen in broad daylight in Harrogate.

The incident happened between 1.50pm and 3.37pm on 18 September when a black Ford Fiesta was stolen from a layby Hookstone Road.

The vehicle has the registration number LG09 XSM.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We are particularly interested in hearing from anyone who has dash cam recordings or CCTV footage from the area at the relevant time.”

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after a black Ford Fiesta was stolen in broad daylight from a layby on Hookstone Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/crime/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260188868 when providing any details regarding the incident.
