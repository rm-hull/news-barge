# 'Dial 999 if you see him' - Police issue urgent appeal over missing Yorkshire man

## Police have issued an urgent appeal to the public to help them track down a missing man.

Benjamin Robinson, who is missing from Copmanthorpe in York, has links to Derbyshire and South Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police says it is ‘very concerned’ for his welfare, and has urged anyone who thinks they may have seen him to call 999.

The 41-year-old is described as a white man, about 6ft 1in tall, of very slim build, with light brown receding hair that is often shaved.

![Benjamin Robinson, who is missing from Copmanthorpe in York](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/people/0&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


He has light stubble and tends to wear sunglasses, the force added.

A statement said: “We are very concerned for Benjamin's welfare, so if you see him, please dial 999. If you have any other information that could help us find him, call North Yorkshire Police on 101.”

Quote reference number 12260193477 when contacting police.
