Three fire crews were called to a van blaze on the York Outer Ring Road last night (Sunday).

It was on the eastbound carriageway of the **A64** between**Fulford and Grimston Bar** at**6.08pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York, Acomb and Huntington crews responded to a van on fire.

“Incident involved a van carrying very small lithium batteries that set alight. Crew used hose reels and breathing apparatus to extinguish the fire.”
