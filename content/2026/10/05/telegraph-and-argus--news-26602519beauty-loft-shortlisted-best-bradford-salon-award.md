A women-only beauty salon has been shortlisted for an award after just one year of business.

The Beauty Loft, based on Frank Street in Dudley Hill, is one of 10 finalists in the T&A Best Beauty Salon Award 2026.

Founded by Saniya Khan, the salon was created to give women in Bradford somewhere they could access beauty services.

## 'The first year was about building the foundations'

Ms Khan said: "I got the premises in spring 2025 and spent around a month getting everything ready from scratch before opening in June.

"The first year was about building the foundations, getting our name out there, growing our client base and learning what our customers wanted.

"We are now moving into the next stage and focusing on growing the team, expanding what we offer and developing the business into more than just a traditional salon."

## 'We are growing into a wider beauty, hair and wellness space'

Since opening, The Beauty Loft has built a foundation of clientele and is now expanding what they do.

Ms Khan said: "We are a women-only salon, but we are also growing into a wider beauty, hair and wellness space.

"Alongside beauty and hair, we are developing other services and collaborations around women’s wellbeing, including aesthetics, physiotherapy, mum and baby events, and partnerships with local businesses."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21530819.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Beauty Loft, Frank Street *(Image: Saniya Khan)*

## 'I'm really proud of how far the business has come'

Ms Khan said: "I honestly couldn’t believe it when I found out.

"There are so many established salons in Bradford, so being recognised this early in our journey feels like a huge achievement whatever the final result is.

"It has made me really proud of how far the business has come in such a short space of time."

## 'We build real relationships with customers'

She credited the salon’s success to its focus on customer care.

Ms Khan said: "We build real relationships with customers.

"We get to know them, remember what they like, have proper conversations with them and try to make the salon feel comfortable and welcoming."

"Some people have told me they had been meaning to visit for ages and when they finally came they loved it.

"Others have said the way they were spoken to when booking made them feel comfortable enough to come in.

"That kind of feedback means a lot because customer care is one of the biggest things I want The Beauty Loft to be known for."
