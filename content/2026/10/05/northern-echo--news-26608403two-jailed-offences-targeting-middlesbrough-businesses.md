Two people have been jailed after offences affecting businesses in the Greater Eston area of Middlesbrough.

Justine Jefferson, 36, and Jordan Pain, 31, were handed prison sentences of two weeks for their offending.

Anthony Power, 41, received a 12-month suspended sentence, while 25-year-old Robbie Crockett was remanded in custody until sentencing early next month.

Power and Pain were issued with criminal behaviour orders.

Jefferson and Power must take part in drug rehabilitation and complete a rehabilitation activity requirement.

Greater Eston Neighbourhoods Inspector Anthony Ford said: "NPT officers work tirelessly to target individuals who cause harm to our local business communities.

"We take shoplifting and business crime seriously and understand the significant impact this can have on retailers, staff and the wider community.

“We will continue to target those who repeatedly steal from stores, intimidate staff or cause disruption to businesses and members of the public.

"We will make use of all available enforcement powers, including criminal behaviour orders where appropriate, to hold those responsible accountable for their actions."

Last month, Crockett was in court after he had targeted Cleveland Retail Park, stealing more than £2,000 of clothing, £1,735 of meat, high-end fragrances, skincare products, sports jackets and four right-foot trainers.
