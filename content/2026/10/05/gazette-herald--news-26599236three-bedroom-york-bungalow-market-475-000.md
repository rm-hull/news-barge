A detached dormer bungalow in York has hit the market for £475,000.

The three-bedroom home is on Newlands Drive in Acomb and has two bathrooms, one reception room and an EPC rating of C.

It is being sold as a freehold property, with the listing saying the house is within walking distance of shops, cafés, schools and regular bus connections into York city centre.

It also has access to the outer ring road and routes further afield.

Inside, an entrance porch leads to an open-plan kitchen and breakfast room.

The kitchen has contemporary units, storage and worktop space, along with a seating area for dining.

A central hallway provides access to the living room.

Dual-aspect windows are fitted in the room, which the listing says brings in natural light.

A further reception room is currently used as a study.

French doors from the study open onto the south-facing rear garden.

One double bedroom is on the ground floor.

A three-piece family bathroom completes the ground-floor accommodation.

Upstairs, there are two further double bedrooms, both having built-in storage, while the principal bedroom also includes fitted wardrobes and a dressing area.

A second three-piece bathroom is located on the first floor.

Outside, the property has driveway parking and a garage, which includes skylights that provide natural light.

The rear garden is south-facing and enclosed by mature hedging and neighbouring low-level buildings.

It is mainly laid to lawn, with patio seating areas and a gravelled area.

There is also gated access between the garden and the front of the property.

The listing describes the house as having been upgraded by its current owners.

It also says the property is presented throughout and ready for its next owners to move into.

The home is in a residential area of Acomb, with local amenities nearby.

The listing says the property has three double bedrooms and two bathrooms.

The property combines ground-floor and first-floor bedrooms within its detached dormer bungalow layout.

It has a driveway, garage, and south-facing garden.
