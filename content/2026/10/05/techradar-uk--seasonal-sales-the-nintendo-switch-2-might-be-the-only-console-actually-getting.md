This has been the year of console price increases, with everything from the PlayStation 5 to the Xbox Series X getting steadily more expensive. It might seem all doom and gloom, but Nintendo is bucking that trend this Amazon Prime Day with a nifty Switch 2 discount.

A range of retailers have the system at 15% off, including Amazon, where it's on sale for £354.99 (was £419.99), matching its lowest ever price on the storefront.

That's less than the console's £395.99 launch price (the RRP went up to £419.99 last month), so a genuinely good deal – a rare sight in today's gaming market!
