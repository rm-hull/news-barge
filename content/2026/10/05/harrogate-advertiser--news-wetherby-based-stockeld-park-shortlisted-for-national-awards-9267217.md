# Wetherby-based Stockeld Park shortlisted for national awards

## Stockeld Park shortlisted for prestigious farm attractions awards

Wetherby-based family themed Stockeld Park has been shortlisted for the prestigious National Farm Attractions Network (NFAN) awards.

![Blast off into adventure in the space-themed Playhive – one of Europe's largest indoor play centres.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/17&crop=&width=990&quality=65.jpg)


The popular site, set in 2,000 acres, is in the running for the Jim Keetch Large Farm Park, Best Food and Beverage, and Best Retail accolades.

NFAN Chair Tom Pearcy said: “Farm attractions are constantly evolving, finding new ways to entertain visitors and develop their businesses to create fantastic days out.

“The NFAN Awards are our opportunity to recognise the people and businesses leading the way.

“Reaching the shortlist is a fantastic achievement in itself and reflects the passion, imagination and sheer hard work that goes into running a successful farm attraction.

“We’re really looking forward to celebrating with all our finalists in January.”

![Peter and Susie Grant, the co-founders of Stockeld Park, the award-winning attraction near Wetherby and Harrogate. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


Peter and Susie Grant, are the co-founders of the popular family theme park, on the outskirts of Wetherby.

It now attracts a quarter of million visitors a year and is listed as one of the top five theme parks in the UK on Tripadvisor.

The attraction started out by opening to sell home-grown fir trees, from the vast estate, at Christmas.

It has since expanded, to feature the 20,000ft Playhive indoor play centre and is home to a unique artificial Nordic ski trail and yew maze.

The NFAN awards shine the spotlight on the attractions and people helping to drive the vibrant sector forward.

The winners will be revealed at NFAN’s glittering Awards Gala Dinner on 27 January 2027 at the Hilton Hotel Doncaster.

Other categories include Best Digital Presence, Best Event, Shining Star, David Taylor Forward Thinking Business Award.
