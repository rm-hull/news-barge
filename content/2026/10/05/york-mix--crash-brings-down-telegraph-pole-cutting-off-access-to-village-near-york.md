A crash has knocked over a telegraph pole in a village near York.

It’s come down across **Main Street** in**Thorganby**, cutting off the road near to the junction with Ings Lane.

It happened at about 11.30am this morning (Monday).

#### Incident location

East Yorkshire buses said: “Live power cables have come down across the road, and emergency services are currently in attendance.

“As a result, significant delays and disruption are expected while the area is made safe.

“Please allow extra time for your journey and take care when travelling through the area.”

It affects their Service 18.

Villagers posted that there was “no vehicle access at all through the village” as the telegraph pole is “completely across the road”.

No one is believed to be hurt.
