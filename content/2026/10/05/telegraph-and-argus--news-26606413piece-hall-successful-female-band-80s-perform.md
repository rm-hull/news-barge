The most successful British female band of the 80s is coming to The Piece Hall.

Bananarama is the latest artist announced for Live at The Piece Hall 2027 which will also see headliner shows from Rick Astley, The Saw Doctors, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison as well as three nights with Simply Red.

The band will bring their Too Hot To Handle Greatest Hits Summer Tour to Halifax next summer as part of a run of headline dates across the UK.

The iconic group will headline Live at The Piece Hall on Sunday, July 18, and they’ll be joined by special guests Tony Hadley and Altered Images for an unforgettable night of favourites and pop classics.

Tickets go on sale at 10am Friday via ticketmaster.co.uk

Nicky Chance-Thompson MBE DL FRSA, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: “What a night this is going be – three 80s legends together on the same stage here at The Piece Hall!”

“Bananarama, Tony Hadley and Altered Images are a massive part of the soundtrack of a generation and this will undoubtedly be one of the biggest parties of the summer, and one personally I cannot wait for!”
