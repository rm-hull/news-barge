# 'Bettys To-Go' launched after first major refurbishment in 20 years at Bettys tearooms at RHS Garden Harlow gardens

## Bettys Café Tea Rooms at Harrogate’s RHS Garden Harlow Carr has re-opened after a month-long refurbishment just in time to enjoy the RHS Garden in its glorious autumn colours with new improvements for customers.

During the recent closure, work has been undertaken to refresh the Café Tea Rooms and visitors will now find a brand-new Bettys To-Go Hatch at the entrance, offering them the opportunity to pick up their picnic essentials before heading into the gardens.

Table bookings at will also now be available, helping customers plan their day with ease, particularly families visiting later in the afternoon.

Bettys Harlow Carr Branch Manager, Graeme Sharp said: ‘We are delighted to be welcoming people back into our new look Bettys at RHS Harlow Carr.

![After the recent closure of Bettys at RHS Harlow Carr gardens for a major refurbishment , visitors will now find a brand-new Bettys To-Go Hatch at the entrance. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/people/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


"We have a very unique location here on the edge of the RHS Garden and we hope the changes we have made will enhance our customers experience of both our Café Tea Rooms and the Garden itself.’

Liz Thwaite, Head of Site at RHS Garden Harlow Carr, said: “Bettys has long been an important part of the visitor experience at RHS Harlow Carr, so we're thrilled to see the new-look Café Tea Rooms unveiled ready for the autumn season.

"With the garden looking particularly spectacular at this time of year, visitors can enjoy a fantastic day out, taking in the seasonal colour before relaxing and enjoying all that Bettys has to offer.”

The temporary closure, which has involved the first significant changes at the popular cafe since it first opened at RHS Harlow Carr in 2004, is designed to create an even more welcoming experience for customers.
