A manhunt has been launched after reports of an attack in County Durham.

Police would like to speak to the man pictured in connection with an incident in Ferryhill on August 22.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: "Officers have conducted CCTV enquiries and are now asking for the public’s help to identify the person shown.

"If you can help, please call 101, quoting reference CRI00702680, or email [Rebecca.Hotle1@durham.police.uk.](mailto:Rebecca.Hotle1@durham.police.uk.)"

The image shows a man wearing all black and carrying a scooter.
