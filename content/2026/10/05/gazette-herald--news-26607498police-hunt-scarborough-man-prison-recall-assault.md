A HUNT has been launched for a man wanted on recall to prison and for assault.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information to locate 50-year-old John Paul Mclatchie from Scarborough on recall to prison and in connection with a domestic assault.

He is believed to be in York or Scarborough, with links to Hull and Leeds, and is described as a white man of slim build, 6ft 3in tall.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Mclatchie and we are now appealing for your help to find him."

If you have an immediate sighting of him, please call 999, and for any further information, report through the website, live chat, or call 101.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

Please quote reference 12260193366 when reporting information.
