## Theft at garage in North Yorkshire market town

*(Image: Police)*

Police have released CCTV images of two people they want to speak to following a theft in Helmsley.

The incident took place on September 17 at BATA Garage on Bondgate.

RECOMMENDED READING:

North Yorkshire Police believe the individuals captured on CCTV may have information that could help with the investigation.

"Please contact us if you recognise any of the persons pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police, quoting the reference 12260182500.
