## Historic York pub closes from today - plans revealed

Church Street in York city centre *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A HISTORIC pub in York city centre has closed its doors ahead of a refurbishment.

The Golden Lion, in Church Street, York city centre, has temporarily closed today (October 5) for a renovation, which will see improves to both the inside and outside of the pub,

The pub is undergoing a six-figure investment to update 'its facilities while preserving its historic charm' and will reopen later in the month.

A Greene King spokesperson said: "The much-loved pub will retain all its historic character while some modern-day comforts are added as part of the project.

"There will be improvements both inside and out to boost the pub’s appeal.

"The pub will remain a popular choice for tourists, friends, families and sports fans with a good range of food and drinks menus to suit all appetites, diets and occasions, whether customers are stopping by for a drink with colleagues, lunchtime refreshments after touring the city, looking to watch all the action on Sky Sports and TNT Sports, or party goers on a night out.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21538522.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Golden Lion, York *(Image: Dean Atkins)*

"Its popular live music and DJ nights will be back too. The Golden Lion’s roof terrace is a great place for friends to gather for alfresco drinks and dining."

The pub is currently recruiting for both full-time and part-time roles in its kitchen and bar teams.

More information, including booking details and opening hours, is available on the Golden Lion’s official website.
