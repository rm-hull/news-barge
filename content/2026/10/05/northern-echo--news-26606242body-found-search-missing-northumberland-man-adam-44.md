A body has been found in the search for a Northumberland man who had been missing for more than a week.

The discovery was made near the River Lyne in Lynemouth on Sunday evening (October 4).

It comes after more than a week of enquiries to trace 44-year-old Adam, who was last seen in the area on Saturday, September 26.

A spokesperson for Northumbria Police said: "A body has sadly been found in the search for missing Northumberland man Adam.

"While formal identification is yet to take place, it is believed to be Adam and his next of kin have been informed.

"Our thoughts are with Adam’s family and loved ones at this tragic time."

Adam's family is now being supported by specially trained officers.

A report will now be prepared for the coroner.

Police say there were no suspicious circumstances and do not believe anyone else was involved.

Officers thanked members of the public for sharing their appeals, providing information and supporting the search for Adam.
