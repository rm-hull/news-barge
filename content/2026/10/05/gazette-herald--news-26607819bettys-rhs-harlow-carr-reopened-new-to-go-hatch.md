A MUCH-LOVED North Yorkshire café inside a beautiful garden has reopened after a month-long refurbishment.

Bettys Café Tea Rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr welcomed back visitors with a refreshed look and new features designed to improve the visitor experience.

The revamped tea room now includes a Bettys To-Go Hatch, allowing guests to pick up picnic items before exploring the gardens.

Graeme Sharp, branch manager at Bettys Harlow Carr, said: "We are delighted to be welcoming people back into our new look Bettys at RHS Harlow Carr.

"We have a very unique location here on the edge of the RHS Garden and we hope the changes we have made will enhance our customers experience of both our Café Tea Rooms and the Garden itself."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21538843.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bettys in Harlow Carr *(Image: Provided)*

They have also introduced table bookings, which were created to help customers plan their day - particular families and larger groups hoping to visit the tearooms in the afternoon.

Liz Thwaite, head of site at RHS Garden Harlow Carr, said: "Bettys has long been an important part of the visitor experience at RHS Harlow Carr, so we're thrilled to see the new-look Café Tea Rooms unveiled ready for the autumn season.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21538844.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The inside of the new Bettys *(Image: Provided)*

"With the garden looking particularly spectacular at this time of year, visitors can enjoy a fantastic day out."

The tearoom will be open from 9am to 5pm daily.
