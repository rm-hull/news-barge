A van and a large skip were destroyed in a fire.

Fire crews were called to Forge Lane, Armley, at around 8.50pm on Sunday after reports of a blaze involving a vehicle.

## Van and skip engulfed by flames

According to a fire service incident log, a Volkswagen van was on fire when crews arrived at the scene.

A large skip was also fully involved in the blaze, with both objects suffering extensive fire damage.

## Firefighters tackle blaze

Firefighters used two large jets and breathing apparatus equipment to bring the fire under control.

Two fire engines from Leeds attended the incident and worked to extinguish the flames.

The incident was later marked as finished.

## Agencies informed

The fire service said Yorkshire Water and the Environment Agency were informed following the incident.
