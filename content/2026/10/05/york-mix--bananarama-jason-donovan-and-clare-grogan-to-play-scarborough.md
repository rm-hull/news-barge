A special vintage of pop superstars will stage a night of hits at the Scarborough Open Air Theatre.

The most successful British female band of the 1980s **Bananarama** will bring their * Too Hot To Handle Greatest Hits Summer Tour* to**Scarborough Open Air Theatre** on **Saturday 17 July**.

They’ll be joined on the night by special guests **Jason Donovan** and ** Altered Images** for an night of Eighties favourites and pop classics.

Tickets go on general sale at **10am on Friday** (9 October) at either the Scarborough theatre website or Ticketmaster.

This summer, Bananarama celebrated 40years since the release of their landmark studio album *True Confessions*, with a special anniversary edition reaching number six in the UK album chart.

As holders of the Guinness World Record for the highest number of chart entries by an all-female group, Bananarama have enjoyed a remarkable career spanning more than four decades.

Their *Too Hot To Handle Greatest Hits Summer Tour* promises to be a party to remember.

Founding member of Bananarama **Sara Dallin said:**“Summer 2027 we are so ready for you! We can’t wait to perform all of our biggest hit singles and bring the Bananarama party to some of the UK’s most brilliant outdoor venues.”

Bandmate **Keren Woodward added:** “We promise a night of real 80s nostalgia and along with our special guests, guarantee an evening that will have everyone dancing and singing along.”

With more than 30 million records sold and 10 UK Top 10 hits Bananarama are one of the most successful female groups of all time.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jason-donovan-by-steve-schofield.webp)

Since bursting onto the scene in the early 1980s, they have amassed a catalogue of timeless hits including *Cruel Summer, Venus, Love In The First Degree, Nathan Jones, Robert De Niro’s Waiting* and *I Heard A Rumour.*

Following the 2024 release of *Glorious: The Ultimate Collection*, which celebrated more than 40 years of chart success for lifelong friends **Sara Dallin** and ** Keren Woodward,** the duo continue to be one of the UK’s most beloved pop acts.

Joining Bananarama in Scarborough will be Australian pop icon **Jason Donovan** whose chart-topping music and acclaimed stage career have made him a firm favourite with audiences for decades.

Donovan first captured the hearts of UK audiences as Scott Robinson in the hit TV series *Neighbours* before becoming one of the biggest pop stars of the late 1980s. His debut album * Ten Good Reasons* was the best-selling album of 1989, producing a string of hits including * Too Many Broken Hearts, Especially For You* with Kylie Minogue, * When You Come Back to Me* and * Sealed With A Kiss*.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/clare-grogan.webp)

Opening both nights will be Scottish new wave favourites **Altered Images**, fronted by the charismatic ** Clare Grogan**. Bursting onto the music scene in the early 1980s, the band became synonymous with infectious pop melodies and a distinctive post-punk edge, scoring hits including * Happy Birthday*, * I Could Be Happy* and * Don’t Talk to Me About Love*.
