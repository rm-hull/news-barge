## Amanda Owen's farming show returns to More4 tonight

*(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Amanda Owen, her former husband Clive and their children are returning for a fourth series of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

The Yorkshire farming family's More4 show returns at 9pm on Monday, October 5, as work continues on their second Swaledale property, Anty John's.

Filmed during winter 2025 and spring 2026, the new episodes follow the farmhouse conversion as it enters its third year.

Viewers will also return to Ravenseat, the remote Yorkshire Dales farm where Amanda, Clive and their nine children first found fame on Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm.

The opening episode follows Christmas at Ravenseat, where the family open presents before dinner.

After New Year, snow blankets Swaledale as Amanda and son Miles paint Anty John's newly plastered walls.

It marks another major stage in the renovation, which has been filmed from the start.

Anty John's was a derelict neighbouring farmhouse when the Owens began to restore it, while the programme also followed daily life at Ravenseat.

The first series, which began in October 2024, showed the family restoring the listed property with local tradespeople while Amanda investigated its history.

Channel 4 commissioned two more five-part series in August 2025.

By then, the exterior was largely complete and work had moved inside, with plumbing, running water and room designs still to tackle.

Series three, which screened earlier this year, showed the walls being plastered, new doors fitted, an antique fireplace installed and the underfloor heating switched on.

But Anty John's will not be the only story when the programme returns.

The new series features Christmas at Ravenseat before another busy Easter lambing season with the wider Owen family.

Amanda will also continue researching the history of Anty John's and the Clarkson family who once lived at the property.

One of its best-known former residents was Anthony Clarkson, a Swaledale mapmaker whose story Amanda has traced throughout the renovation. Channel 4 has said uncovering the house's past would remain central to the third and fourth series.

A new storyline follows Edith and her sisters as they prepare the family's cream tea van for its summer opening.

The programme has brought Amanda and Clive back on screen since Our Yorkshire Farm ended.

The couple announced their separation in 2022 but have continued farming and raising their nine children: Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Fans can see previously unaired footage at a special Northallerton event before the fourth series begins.

The RTS calls Our Farm Next Door one of More4's most popular series in years and says it has charted the Owen children growing up during the three-year restoration.

Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids returns on More4 at 9pm on Monday, October 5.
