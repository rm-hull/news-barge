Police searching for this wanted man say he could be in York.

John Paul Mclatchie, 50, is wanted for recall to prison and in connection with a domestic assault that occurred in Hull.

From Scarborough, Mclatchie is believed to be there or in York, but also has links to Hull and Leeds.

He is described as a white man of slim build and is 6ft 3in.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Mclatchie and we are now appealing for your help to find him.

“If you have an immediate sighting of him, please call 999.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online.”
