A man suffered a facial injury after an alleged knife attack inside a Bradford takeaway.

Police were called to Amigos on Otley Road in Undercliffe at 9.02pm on Sunday following reports that a man had attacked another with a knife.

The injured man was assessed by paramedics at the scene and was found not to require hospital treatment.

## Damage reported at takeaway

Police said it was further alleged that the same man threw rocks at members of staff and caused damage to the takeaway's door during the incident.

Officers attended and enquiries into the circumstances surrounding the incident remain ongoing.

No further details about any arrests have been released at this stage.

Anyone with information that could assist the investigation is urged to contact West Yorkshire Police.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called at 9.02pm on Sunday 4 October to a report that a man had attacked another man with a knife in a takeaway restaurant on Otley Road, Bradford.

"It was further reported that the same man had thrown rocks at staff members and caused damage to the door of the restaurant."

They added: "The injured man was checked over by paramedics and found to have a cut to the face which did not require hospital treatment. Enquiries are ongoing."
