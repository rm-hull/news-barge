## Grade II-listed resort town hotel set for refurbishment under new plans

The Whiteley hotel as viewed from North Marine Road. Courtesy of applicant. *(Image: Applicant)>*

A GRADE II-listed hotel overlooking Scarborough's North Bay is seeking approval for a series of improvements, including the installation of electric vehicle charging points for guests.

Owners of The Whiteley, on Queens Parade, have submitted plans for works to the historic property, which forms part of a listed terrace within Scarborough’s conservation area.

The proposals are intended to secure the long-term future of the building, support the operation of the hotel and address maintenance issues affecting the property.

Among the plans are two electric vehicle charging points to be installed within the hotel's car park overlooking Queens Parade.

A planning statement submitted on behalf of owners, Jon and Emma Haddock, states that the charging facilities would help modernise the guest offer and encourage the use of lower-emission vehicles.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21541474.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Drone photo of roof taken from above front garden to west of property of The Whiteley hotel. Courtesy of applicant. *(Image: Applicant)*

It states: "The applicants consider that the facility will prove attractive to guests and promote the use of low-emission vehicles on trips to the town."

The application also includes repair and refurbishment works to parts of the listed building.

The Whiteley occupies a prominent position between Queens Parade and North Marine Road and was originally built as two separate houses before being combined into a hotel.

The property forms part of a group of Grade II-listed buildings dating from the early 19th century.

Planning documents describe the building as making a positive contribution to the character and appearance of the conservation area.

According to the applicants, parts of the building are suffering from water ingress, with existing roof coverings, dormers and rainwater goods requiring attention.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21541475.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Proposed elevations, The Whiteley hotel. Courtesy of applicant. *(Image: Applicant)*

The heritage statement submitted with the application argues the proposals would improve the appearance of the property, safeguard its historic fabric and help ensure its continued viability as a hotel.

North Yorkshire Council has not set a date for deciding on the plans which are open to representations from members of the public.
