A popular McDonald's near a motorway will reopen next week.

The fast-food branch on Rooley Lane, opposite the M606, shut its doors for a refurbishment last month.

Fencing surrounds the drive-thru, including the car park.

A huge sign outside the branch states: "We're closed to level up - Back October 15

"It's going to be iconic."

A banner points customers to the nearest McDonald's drive-thru on Ingleby Road.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21539599.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The McDonald's is closed for a refurb *(Image: Newsquest)*

It comes after the fast-food chain's Brighouse branch, owned by Yorkshire franchisee Pritpal Singh, had a makeover.

The refurbishment was part of McDonald’s reimaging programme, which aims to improve customer experience through updated layouts and new technology.

The redesigned restaurant now features a more comfortable and relaxed dining area.

It also offers better integration of digital ordering channels, including the McDonald’s app, alongside traditional walk-in service.

The changes are designed to meet consumer demand for greater speed, efficiency and choice.
