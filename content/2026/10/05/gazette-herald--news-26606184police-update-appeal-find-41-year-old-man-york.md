NORTH Yorkshire Police has issued an update to its appeal for help in finding a missing 41-year-old man from a York village.

Yesterday (Sunday, October 4), officers from the force said they were very concerned about the missing man from Copmanthorpe who they said had links to Derbyshire and South Yorkshire and had asked for the public's help to locate him.

In a statement issued this morning, North Yorkshire Police said the man had been found safe.
