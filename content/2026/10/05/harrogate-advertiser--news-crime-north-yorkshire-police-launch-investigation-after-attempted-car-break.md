# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after attempted car break-in on Harrogate street

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following an attempted car break-in on a street in Harrogate

The incident happened at about 4am on Saturday, October 3, on Dragon Road, where a man reportedly tried to break into a car parked opposite the Asda petrol station.

Officers are now asking anyone who was in the area at the time to come forward if they saw anything suspicious or have any information that could assist with the investigation.

Police are particularly keen to hear from anyone who may have recorded the incident on a mobile phone, has CCTV or doorbell camera footage, or was driving through the area with an active dashcam.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after a man reportedly tried to break into a car parked on Dragon Road, opposite the Asda petrol station, in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/crime/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260194160 when providing any details regarding the incident.
