🐦‍⬛ The medallion is along the path to the left of the scarecrow by a cross against a tree.  
🐦‍⬛ The letter with a drawing is on the upper floor of the big house to the left of the "Fields" lantern, accessible via the gate you open as a shortcut later.  
🐦‍⬛ Once you return both, you have to find the lover's hideaway in the attic of the barn near the big house.

In Valor Mortis's The Scarecrow quest, you have to collect two possessions belonging to the unfortunate soul tied up in the field: a medallion and a note with a drawing.

The video above shows the three locations you need; where to find both items and the hay loft once you've returned them to the scarecrow. You can find the medallion just to the left of the scarecrow, down a short path through the field that leads to a cross resting against a tree, with a corpse who looks to have dropped it. The note with a drawing is slightly more complicated, though:

* Progress through the level until you unlock the shortcut gate by the big house, leading back to the "Fields" lantern.

* From the lantern, head down the path until you reach the gate to the open area with the Volunteer enemy guarding the carriage—turn left before the gate instead.

* Continue through the haystacks towards the house in the distance—note the barn with the diamond-shaped window to your right, as you'll return here later. Enter the house through the back door.

* Head upstairs and grab the note with a drawing from the table.

Now, return both items to the scarecrow, after which you'll have to locate the lover's old hideaway. This is where that barn I asked you to remember comes in.

Follow the same steps as above, but after you turn left (before the gate leading into the area with the Volunteer guarding the carriage), you'll want to head towards that barn you can see on your right, making your way around the back of it and climbing into the hayloft.

You'll encounter an echo of the lovers meeting each other and will be rewarded with the Bountiful Harvest amber, which grants health when picking up Nephtoglobin remnants, as the completion reward.
