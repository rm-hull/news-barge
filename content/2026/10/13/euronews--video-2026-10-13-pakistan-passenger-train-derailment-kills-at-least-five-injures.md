The **Rehman Baba Express**, travelling from Karachi to Peshawar, came off the tracks at around** 5.15am local time**near Sher Shah railway station in Punjab province. All** 14 carriages derailed**, with several overturning and trapping passengers inside.

Rescue workers, police and local residents searched the wreckage, while cranes were brought in to remove damaged coaches. The injured were taken to **Nishtar Hospital in Multan**, where at least one person was reported to be in critical condition.

Pakistan Railways said all passengers had been evacuated. Train services on the affected line were suspended, and a relief train and medical unit were dispatched to the scene.

The cause of the accident remains unknown, and an investigation is under way. **Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif** ordered authorities to accelerate rescue efforts and ensure proper medical care for those injured.
