Firefighters from Harrogate and Knaresborough went to a crash on the A661 near Spofforth last night (Monday).

The incident happened at around 9.02pm after police requested assistance to free one person trapped in the vehicle.

#### A661, Spofforth, York

Crews used cutting gear, small tools, lighting and a spinal board to extricate the casualty.

The scene was then handed over to police and paramedics.
