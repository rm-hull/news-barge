Police are appealing for witnesses after two people were injured in a collision on the A1(M) near Boroughbridge.

The crash involved a Ford Ranger towing a caravan and a Toyota Yaris on the northbound carriageway at junction 48 at 12:19pm on Saturday (10th October).

One driver suffered a broken wrist, while a passenger in the same vehicle suffered a suspected fractured rib. All occupants have since been discharged from hospital.

The road was closed for several hours while officers investigated the scene and the vehicles were recovered.

North Yorkshire Police are asking anyone who witnessed the collision, saw either vehicle beforehand or has relevant dashcam footage to come forward.

Anyone with information should email [matthew.harvey@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:matthew.harvey@northyorkshire.police.uk) or visit the North Yorkshire Police website, select ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘An existing case or report’.

The force’s online live chat is also available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Anyone unable to use these options can call 101.

*Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111. Please quote reference 12260199312 when providing information.*
