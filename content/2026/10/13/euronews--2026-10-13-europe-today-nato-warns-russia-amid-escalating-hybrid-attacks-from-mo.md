## NATO's top commander has a message for Moscow: any attack on the alliance will be blocked, blunted and broken. In Warsaw, Zelenskyy says Russia has already broken Trump's energy ceasefire. And in France, student protests enter a new phase as the country's budget reaches the French Assembly.

**Maria Tadeo** reports from the Warsaw Security Forum, where NATO's Supreme Allied Commander Europe,**Alexus Grynkewich**, has warned Russia that the alliance is ready to deter any attack, and where** Volodymyr Zelenskyy** and European leaders say Moscow is not serious about an energy ceasefire.

**Sasha Vakulina** explains what Russia is demanding from Ukraine as Washington renews pressure for concessions, whether Moscow's position has changed, and what she heard about conditions for people still living in Oleshky under Russian occupation.

**Estelle Nilsson-Julien** reports from Paris, where the student movement enters a new phase today as unions join the protests, the 2027 budget reaches the National Assembly, and Prime Minister**Sébastien Lecornu** faces pressure to find compromises.

Interview with **Jaume Sanpera**, CEO of Spanish satellite company Sateliot, who discusses whether Europe can achieve space sovereignty and reduce its dependence on Starlink, the risk that "technological sovereignty" becomes protectionism, the EU Space Act, and whether Europe has regulated AI too much, too early.
