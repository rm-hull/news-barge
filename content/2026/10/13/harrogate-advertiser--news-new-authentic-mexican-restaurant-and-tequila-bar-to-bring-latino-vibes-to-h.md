# New authentic Mexican restaurant and tequila bar to bring Latino vibes to Harrogate town centre

## A new authentic Mexican restaurant and tequila bar is set to open in Harrogate, bringing traditional cuisine, handcrafted cocktails and a taste of Latin America to the town.

Casa del Alma has confirmed it is preparing to open its doors in Harrogate in mid-November, following the success of its restaurant in Darlington and its popular street food venture in Saltburn.

![Casa Del Alma, a Mexican restaurant and tequila bar, is set to open in Harrogate in mid-November, bringing traditional cuisine, handcrafted cocktails and a taste of Latin America to the town](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/13/14/40/1302x.jpeg?trim=11,57,9,74&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Diners can look forward to an authentic Mexican dining experience featuring traditional dishes, premium tequilas, signature margaritas and a vibrant atmosphere inspired by Latino culture.

Announcing the news on social media, a spokesperson said: "Expect a fantastic experience with great traditional food, fabulous margaritas and tequila, Latino vibes and much more.

“We are aiming to open mid-November, so keep an eye on our socials to see how we progress."

The restaurant's menu celebrates authentic Mexican cuisine, with a range of traditional dishes prepared using fresh ingredients, alongside an extensive tequila selection and expertly crafted cocktails.

The owners say they are passionate about sharing the flavours and hospitality of Mexico, creating a welcoming space where customers can enjoy everything from relaxed family meals to evenings out with friends over cocktails.

The new venue will become Casa del Alma's third venture and marks another addition to Harrogate's growing independent food and drink scene.

An exact opening date has yet to be confirmed, but Casa del Alma is aiming to welcome its first customers in mid-November.
