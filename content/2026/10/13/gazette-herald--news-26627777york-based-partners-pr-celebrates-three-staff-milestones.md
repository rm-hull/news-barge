Three PR professionals are marking a combined 40 years of service at a York-based agency.

Monica Green, director and co-owner of Partners PR, is celebrating 25 years with the company, while senior account executives Leanne Terry and Ellie Bilton are marking 10 and five years respectively.

Ms Green said: "It’s fantastic to be celebrating these milestones with Leanne and Ellie.

"Forty years of combined experience is a real testament to the strength and commitment of our team."

She joined Partners PR in 2001 as an account director and later became part of the management team that bought the business in 2006.

Ms Green now oversees finance and operations at the agency, which serves clients across manufacturing, IT, power, rail, and construction.

Former journalist Ms Terry and Ms Bilton help deliver PR campaigns that support client reputation and business goals.

Partners PR is a Prolific North Top 50 agency based at Independence House in Nether Poppleton.
