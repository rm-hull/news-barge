**\*\* The road has now reopened \*\***

From Tuesday 13 October 2026 at 11:50AM

Emergency services are dealing with a three-vehicle crash near York.

It’s near the North Road junction east of the Hopgrove Roundabout and the road is closed.

Police are asking people to avoid the area.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are currently dealing with a three vehicle road traffic collision on the junction of North Lane onto the A64.

“The road is currently closed while emergency services attend the scene.

“Motorists are advised to avoid the area and find alternative travel routes in the meantime.”

The AA is warning of long delays.
