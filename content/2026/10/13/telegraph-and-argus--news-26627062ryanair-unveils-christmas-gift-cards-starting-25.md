RYANAIR has launched its Christmas gift cards for 2026.

The cards offer travellers the chance to give the gift of travel this festive season, with both digital and physical options available.

Ryanair’s Director of Comms, Jade Kirwan, said: "With Christmas just around the corner, we’re delighted to kick start the festive season with the launch of our Christmas Gift Cards.

"Ryanair’s Christmas Gift Cards for 2026 are available in a range of festive designs, and from as little as £25, they make the perfect gift for family, friends, Secret Santa recipients, or pretty much anyone planning to get away in the new year.

"With both physical and digital gift card options, you can either wrap them up for underneath the Christmas tree or send to loved ones living away."
