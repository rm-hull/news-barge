An aircraft maintenance company operating at some of the UK's busiest airports, including Heathrow, Birmingham and Manchester, has collapsed into liquidation.

Luftavia, founded in May 2015, offered a range of services, including scheduled line maintenance, ad-hoc repairs, and rapid temporary line station set-ups.

The company positioned itself as a provider of "efficient, cost-effective maintenance solutions" using UK Civil Aviation Authority-approved products.

The Luftavia website added: "Our line maintenance engineers are committed to a 'right first time' quality standard, receiving specific training in risk assessment and SMS.

"Our partners are all respected industry operators, chosen specifically to add quality and value to Luftavia performance."

Luftavia operated at major airports in the UK and internationally, including:

* London Heathrow Airport
* Manchester Airport
* Birmingham Airport
* Saigon Airport
* Bangkok Airport
* Dhaka Airport
* Siem Reap Airport
* Phnom Penh Airport
* Hanoi Airport

## Luftavia collapses into liquidation after 11 years in business

After 11 years in business, Luftavia is set to close after falling into liquidation.

Ryan Michael Davies of Moore Kingston Smith & Partners LLP was appointed as liquidator on September 30, according to a notice published in The Gazette.
