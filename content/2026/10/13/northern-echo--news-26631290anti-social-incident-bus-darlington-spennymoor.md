A probe has been launched after an anti-social incident on a bus from Darlington.

It took place on the Number 8 bus service from Darlington to Spennymoor just before midday on September 18.

Durham Police said officers want to speak to anyone who may have seen two men with dogs board the bus or any passengers who may have information.

The force said it is unable to share any further details on the incident at this time.

A spokesperson said: “Police are appealing for information following an anti-social incident on the Number 8 bus from Darlington to Spennymoor last month.

“The service began in Darlington at 11.50am on September 18 and officers want to speak to anyone who may have seen two men with dogs board the bus or any passengers who might have information that could assist their enquiries.

“If you have any information, call 101 quoting incident reference number 215 of September 18 or contact PC Riddell at [callum.riddell@durham.police.uk](mailto:callum.riddell@durham.police.uk) or by calling 07929788400.”
