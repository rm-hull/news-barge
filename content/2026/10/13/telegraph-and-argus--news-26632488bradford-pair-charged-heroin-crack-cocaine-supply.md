Two men have been charged with drugs offences following an investigation by West Yorkshire Police.

It comes amid an investigation by officers from the Operation SWEETGILL team - a police operation targeting organised crime and drug supply across parts of Bradford.

Harris Moman, 25, of Ivanhoe Road in Little Horton, and Paul Martindale, 63 of Sharp Street, Wibsey, have been charged.

## When will they be in court?

Both men have been charged with being concerned in the supply of Class A drugs, specifically crack cocaine and heroin.

They have been remanded in custody and are due to appear at Bradford Crown Court on November 11.

Operation SWEETGILL focuses on areas including Holme Wood, Bierley, Tong and the wider Bradford district.

In a statement posted to social media, West Yorkshire Police said: "If you have information about drug dealing or related criminality within your community, please contact your local West Yorkshire Police Neighbourhood Policing Team.

"Information from the public plays a vital role in helping us disrupt criminal activity and keep our communities safe."
