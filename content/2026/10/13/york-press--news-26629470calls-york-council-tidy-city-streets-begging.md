## 'Council needs to up its game on city streets'

Praise indeed to D.A. Brown’s letter to The Press, printed in October 10 edition, but unfortunately it only begins to hit the nail on the head.

It doesn’t go far enough in order to give a true representation of the disgusting condition of the city centre streets in general with an overabundance of dirty, filthy, stained footpaths and pedestrianised areas, soiled rubbish-filled (empty) shop fronts and a strong chance of being harassed by drunks dossing down and begging on the streets, in doorways and public seating areas.

City of York Council really need to up their game if they ever want to be taken seriously and put some real effort and action into cleaning up this (what ought to be, could be, should be) amazing city, instead of dreaming up unpopular and expensive plans that tumble out of control in terms of timescale and public money.

More people who live in York need to stand and be counted and complain about the lack of care and attention this council are currently prepared to give to the streets of York.

## If it ain’t broke...

I WROTE recently about the problems regarding the proposed new recycling bins probably being too small to take a month’s plastic, glass and tins for many households.

It has been drawn to my attention by a neighbour that she and many others face the opposite situation - boxes were quite adequate for her household and where will she find the space to put two extra bins?

The recycling boxes served most of us well.

Wake up council, if a system ain’t broke for so many, please don’t fix it.

**Mary Morton,Hob Moor Drive,Holgate, York\_\_\_**
