A Bradford accountancy firm has been named one of the UK’s best small companies to work for.

Watson Buckle, based in Cottingley Business Park, ranked 68th in the national list compiled by workplace engagement specialist Best Companies, earning praise for its staff support and commitment to developing future talent.

The firm was also accredited as an ‘Outstanding Company to Work For’ based on anonymous employee feedback.

Mark Wilcock, managing director at Watson Buckle, said: "We realise that our power is in our people, those who have the dedication and drive to move us forward.

"To enable our long-term future success, we want to ensure our people are part of a happy and engaged workforce where their contributions are truly valued, where they can learn and grow and forge a long, rewarding career.

![Watson Buckle](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21574105.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mark Wilcock of Watson Buckle *(Image: Faye Hatton)*

"Our Best Companies achievement is something we’re really proud of.

"It demonstrates that we are living our values and taking feedback on board."

Founded in 1902, Watson Buckle has a long-standing reputation for its audit and accountancy services, as well as for training some of the region’s best accountants and tax advisers.

The firm’s culture is built on collaboration and integrity.

It encourages employees to approach challenges creatively and values diverse perspectives.

Professional development is a key focus, with the company offering flexible working options to help staff balance their careers with personal commitments.

This approach, Watson Buckle believes, is central to creating a fulfilling and inspiring workplace.

Staff are also encouraged to get involved in volunteering and community projects, with the firm sponsoring several charitable events and initiatives in the local area.

Regular team-building activities and social events further strengthen team connections.

Mr Wilcock said: "We are incredibly proud of the work culture that we have nurtured at Watson Buckle and it is reflected in our high rates of staff retention.

"We are bucking in the trend in our industry in many ways, not least in the fact that 58 per cent of our team are female."

Watson Buckle’s latest recognition highlights its ongoing efforts to create an inclusive and supportive workplace environment that values employee wellbeing and professional growth.

More information about the firm’s services and career opportunities can be found at www.watsonbuckle.co.uk.
