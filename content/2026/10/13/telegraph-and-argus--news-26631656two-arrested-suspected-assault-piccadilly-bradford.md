A man suffered serious head injuries in a suspected attack in Bradford city centre.

A 49-year-old man and 40-year-old woman have been arrested on suspicion of assault and remain in police custody.

The 62-year-old was found lying in the street on Piccadilly just after midnight.

West Yorkshire Police said he was taken to hospital.

A police cordon remains in place this lunchtime.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police are investigating after reports of a serious assault at an address on Piccadilly in Bradford.

"Officers attended shortly after midnight this morning (October 13) and found a man aged 62 with serious head injuries lying in the street.

"Enquiries are ongoing to establish how he sustained these serious injuries. The victim has been taken to hospital.

"A 49-year-old man and 40-year-old woman have been arrested on suspicion of assault and remain in police custody.

"Enquiries remain ongoing by Bradford CID.

"Anyone who witnessed the incident is asked to contact police via 101 or use the Live Chat options at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260582268."
