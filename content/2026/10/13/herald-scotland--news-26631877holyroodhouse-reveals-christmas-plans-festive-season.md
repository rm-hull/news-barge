The Palace of Holyroodhouse will welcome visitors for the festive season with illuminated wildlife displays, decorated Christmas trees and events inspired by a medieval Scottish legend.

The King’s official residence in Edinburgh will open its Christmas programme from November 19, 2026, to January 4, 2027.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21573852.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christmas Exclusive Guided Tour and Carols at the Palace of Holyroodhouse. *(Image: Royal Collection Enterprises Limited 2026 | Royal Collection Trust/Jane Massey)*

This year’s new display draws on King David I of Scotland, who is said to have encountered a stag with a glowing cross between its antlers while hunting in the 12th century.

According to the story, the king interpreted the vision as a message from God and built Holyrood Abbey on the spot. The abbey now stands in ruins within the palace grounds.

Visitors will be able to walk around the palace’s central quadrangle, where illuminated, life-size models of stags, does and fawns will bring the legend to life.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21573854.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Palace of Holyroodhouse. *(Image: Royal Collection Enterprises Limited 2026 | Royal Collection Trust/Jane Massey)*

The palace façade, gates and forecourt fountain will also be illuminated each evening, creating a festive display at the foot of Edinburgh’s Royal Mile.

Inside, a 15-foot Nordmann fir tree will take centre stage in the Great Gallery, while the Throne Room will feature two 12-foot Christmas trees, lights and ornaments.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21573853.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christmas Exclusive Guided Tour and Carols at the Palace of Holyroodhouse. *(Image: Royal Collection Enterprises Limited 2026 | Royal Collection Trust/Jane Massey)*

Garlands, berries and winter foliage will decorate the Great Stair leading to the historic State Apartments, with wreaths adorning the windows along the King’s Rooms. The Royal Dining Room will also feature seasonal foliage and objects from the Royal Collection.

Visitors will be able to enjoy a festive menu at the Café at the Palace, located in the historic Mews courtyard.

The programme includes Christmas carol performances by members of the Choir of St Mary’s Cathedral, Edinburgh, in the Great Gallery on December 8 and 9.

A festive wreath-making workshop will take place on December 10, followed by a Christmas activity day featuring arts and crafts on December 19.

Tickets for the Christmas displays are available for £1 for people receiving Universal Credit and other eligible benefits, with up to five additional members of their household also able to access the offer.

Further information about the displays, events and activities is available through the Royal Collection Trust website.
