Roadworks at a key York junction are causing traffic delays today.

Four-way temporary traffic lights have been installed at the crossroads between Micklegate, Skeldergate, Bridge Street and North Street.

Northern Powergrid is undertaking utility works at the junction.

#### Roadworks location

It’s causing traffic to queue along Micklegate and down George Hudson Street.

The work is set to continue until Friday (16 October).
