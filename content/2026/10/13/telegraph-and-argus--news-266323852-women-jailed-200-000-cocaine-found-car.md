Two women have each been jailed for six years and nine months after police found £200,000 worth of cocaine in their car.

Chelsea Kelly and Alexandra Lewis were sentenced at Leeds Crown Court after police found eight kilos of cocaine in a shopping bag.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Two women are now serving time after their car was found to be transporting cocaine disguised as packages of designer watches.

"Chelsea Kelly and Alexandra Lewis were sentenced to six years and nine months each after they were convicted of possession with intent to supply Class A drugs at Leeds Crown Court on Wednesday, October 7.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21574465.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cocaine which was discovered in designer watch packaging

"Kelly, 36, of Firbank Road in Manchester, and Lewis, 36, of Dunstall Road in Manchester, were stopped by officers from our Proactive Intercept Team (PIT) as they drove along Bradley Road, Huddersfield, in February this year.

"Their vehicle was searched, and a shopping bag containing eight kilos of cocaine – with a street value of around £200,000 - was found inside.

"The pair were arrested and later charged following an investigation by the Kirklees Precision Team."
