A POPULAR fast-food restaurant on the outskirts of York has closed its doors.

McDonald’s at Bilborough Top - located off the A64 between York and Tadcaster – has shut for refurbishment as part of the brand’s ‘reimagining programme’ with plans to reopen later in the month.

A spokesperson for McDonald’s said: “Our Bilborough Top restaurant closed on September 21 and is currently undergoing a refurbishment as part of our reimaging programme.

“The revamp will improve the experience of both our customers and our restaurant team, and we look forward to welcoming customers back on October 22."

On the website, the store is listed as closed with the nearest locations being York Designer Outlet and on the Poppleton roundabout in York.
