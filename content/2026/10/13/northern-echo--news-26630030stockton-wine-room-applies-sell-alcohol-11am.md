A new North East wine room and pantry wants a licence to sell alcohol, according to a public notice..

Lu Lu’s Pantry and Wine Room Limited has applied to Stockton Council for a premises licence.

If approved, the licence would allow alcohol sales on and off the premises.

The application seeks permission to sell alcohol from 11am to 11pm every day.

The venue would be at 9 Silver Street, Stockton.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

The council will accept comments or objections until November 4.

Comments and objections must be submitted in writing or emailed to [licensing@stockton.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@stockton.gov.uk.)

The application can be viewed at the council offices in Dunedin House, Thornaby, from 9am to 4pm on weekdays, excluding bank holidays, or online at stockton.gov.uk.
