A BANK card has been used fraudulently in York, police said.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened at around 3.50am on Saturday, September 12, when a card holder was taken and the bank card inside was used.

The force issued a CCTV image of a man it said it would like help in identifying, saying he may have information that could help its investigation.




North Yorkshire Police said anyone with information is asked to contact PC Stacie-Lea Franks by email at [stacie-lea.franks@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:stacie-lea.franks@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, information can be submitted online via the North Yorkshire Police website

Those unable to use these options can call 101, quoting reference 12260179069.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
