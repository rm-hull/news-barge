# Man killed by tiger at Yorkshire Wildlife Park 'named as William Chapman'

![Flowers left at the entrance of Yorkshire Wildlife Park in Doncaster (Dave Higgens/PA)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/13/14/v2-image-production-18867f925ce9d47ca41712777fb952beY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkxOTgzOTc0-2.86159517.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## A man who was fatally attacked by a tiger at Yorkshire Wildlife Park has been named in media reports.

The visitor who died after he was attacked by Altai the Amur tiger at the Doncaster attraction, apparently filmed the moment he approached the animal, according to footage posted in reports.

The Press Association said today the man has been named in media reports as William Chapman.

The short clip, apparently posted on a now-deleted Instagram account, was filmed inside the tiger’s enclosure and the large animal can be seen lying on top of a wooden platform, a few metres in front of the camera.

Yorkshire Wildlife Park was closed for a fourth day as an investigation into the incident continues.

![Paramedics rush to the scene after the tiger attack at Yorkshire Wildlife Park.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/13/00/4c95d4ef-46f6-4d1c-864e-8a2e638c52e5.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


In the audio, the person filming can be heard breathing heavily as distant voices shout “Oh my God” and “seriously”.

The park remained closed on Tuesday for a fourth day after the attack.

Emergency services were contacted shortly after 1.30pm on October 10 when Mr Chapman, who police said was visiting the park alone, entered the enclosure.

He was pronounced dead at the scene.

![Yorlshire Wildlife Park tiger Altai.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/8/06/chrome_1aZWNPw87W.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


City of Doncaster Council, which is responsible for licensing the park, said on Monday it had started a health and safety investigation.

Dan Swaine, executive director of place at City of Doncaster Council, said: “As the relevant licensing authority for the Yorkshire Wildlife Park, I can confirm that, in line with our statutory duties, the council has launched a health and safety investigation.

“We are also working with multiple agencies, including the police and health agencies, and are supporting the Yorkshire Wildlife Park in response to the incident.

“At this time, we won’t be commenting further on the individual or the investigation and we ask that the privacy of those involved is respected at this difficult time.”

In a previous statement on social media, the wildlife park said “many, many people” had contacted them about Altai, the Amur tiger involved in the incident, and it confirmed he is “safe and well” and will not be euthanised.
