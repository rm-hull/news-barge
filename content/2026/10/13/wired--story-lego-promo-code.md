If there’s anything the WIRED team loves, it’s Lego.

Just about everyone has heard of Lego and its block sets. You can use them to build just about anything. It’s great for all ages, a great gift, and even great for date night. My husband and I have gotten Lego Botanical sets for four years in a row for Valentine’s Day to build together. Lego sets are constantly fought over at every White Elephant party I attend, which is why you’ll always find a set recommended in our White Elephant gift guide. We love Legos as a gift so much that we have an entire gift guide dedicated to it. There are even Lego smart bricks that let you add sounds and light effects to your Lego set of choice.

If you’re shopping for a specific Lego set ahead of the holiday season, like a Star Wars Lego set or Harry Potter Lego sets (or, if you’re my husband, a specific One Piece Lego set that features an entire ship and all the characters), it isn’t always cheap, especially for the cooler, larger sets. That’s where WIRED’s Lego coupon codes come in handy, whether you’re shopping for a holiday or just for fun.

These Lego discount codes can help you with all kinds of sets. Here are all the Lego coupons you need to try the next time you’re shopping for a Lego set.

## Buy One, Get 20% Off With This Lego Promo Code

Why get just one Lego set when you can get two? This Lego discount code gives you 20 percent off your second Lego set. Stock up on the best Lego sets in one swoop, and check out Lego’s website for the current monthly discount.

## Piece Together Huge Lego Discounts With the Lego Sale

The smartest way to get a good Lego discount (without a Lego promo code) is to shop in the Lego sale section. You can always find on-sale Legos on Lego’s website, and right now there’s a handful of Harry Potter Lego keychains on sale, along with other Lego minifigures and accessories.

## Unlock Your Lego Student Discount Today

Students can always shop with their own special Lego promo code and discount options. Students can check here to see which discounted Lego sets they can currently purchase.

## Score Extra Savings With Lego Coupons and at Lego Insiders

Love Lego? It might be time to become a Lego Insider. Download the Lego Insiders app and sign up to get membership perks, early access to new Lego sets, and points on your purchases. It’s available on the iOS App Store and Google Play Store.

## Get More Value From Lego Gift Cards

You can stretch your shopping a little farther by getting a Lego gift card and adding another promotion. You can often add gifts and Lego sets to your purchase of a gift card, or after spending a certain amount; right now, you can add a Mixed Flowerpot set after spending $150.
