POLICE have launched a next-of-kin appeal after a man was found dead in North Yorkshire.

Gary Williams, 66, and from Selby, passed away on Monday (October 12).




North Yorkshire Police is supporting the Coroner's Office in helping locate his next of kin.

A force spokesperson said: "There are no suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

"If you have any information that would help the Coroner to locate any members of Mr Williams' family then please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)"
