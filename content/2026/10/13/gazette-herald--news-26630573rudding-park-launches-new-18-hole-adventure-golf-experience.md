A hotel, spa and golf resort has launched a new team-building experience in North Yorkshire.

Harrogate-based Rudding Park has unveiled The Great Yorkshire Golf Adventure, an 18-hole adventure golf course designed for corporate events and team-building days.

The course takes players on a journey through Yorkshire’s heritage, with each hole inspired by a landmark or legend from the county, including Vikings, Guy Fawkes, York Minster, Whitby, the Flying Scotsman and the Yorkshire Dales.

Janet Scally, sales director at Rudding Park, said: "Businesses are increasingly looking for experiences that bring colleagues together in meaningful ways.

"The Great Yorkshire Golf Adventure offers something different – a shared experience that encourages conversation, collaboration and friendly competition while celebrating the county we are proud to call home."

The adventure forms part of the Gimme Golf concept, allowing businesses to combine adventure golf with TrackMan Driving Range competitions, short-course golf, hospitality and networking.

For further information, visit www.ruddingpark.co.uk
