A Coldplay tribute concert is set to light up Bradford's St George's Hall.

Sky Full of Stars, described as "the most authentic tribute to Coldplay," will take place on October 16, 2026.

The show promises to be an "electrifying night of music and magic," with LED wristbands lighting up the crowd and creating an immersive experience.

The concert will feature Coldplay's greatest hits, including Viva La Vida, A Sky Full of Stars, Clocks, Yellow, Paradise, and Hymn for the Weekend.

Tickets are priced between £31 and £39, with a maximum of 10 tickets available for online booking HERE.

The show is recommended for those aged 12 and above.

A spokesperson said: "Whether you’re a lifelong Coldplay fan or just looking for an unforgettable night out, Sky Full of Stars delivers dazzling performances, stunning visuals, and an atmosphere that truly brings Coldplay’s greatest hits to life."
