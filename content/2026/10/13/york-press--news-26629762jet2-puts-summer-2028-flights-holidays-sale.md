Jet2 has put its summer 2028 programme from Leeds Bradford Airport on sale earlier than ever.

In response to customer demand, the airline and tour operator is offering flights and holidays to 50 destinations, with almost two million seats on sale.

This early release allows customers and independent travel agents to secure their preferred destinations, dates, accommodation, and rooms well in advance.

The announcement was made as Leeds Bradford Airport faces a weekend of potential disruption as almost 130 workers prepare to take strike action from October 16 to 18.

Jet2's 2028 summer programme from Leeds Bradford Airport includes flights to various destinations across the Canary Islands, Balearic Islands, mainland Spain, Portugal, Greece, and Turkey, among others.

With up to 192 departing weekly flights during peak periods, the programme represents a five per cent capacity growth compared to summer 2027, offering holidaymakers across Yorkshire more choice and flexibility.

The airline is the first to launch summer 2028 getaways, with some holidaymakers already planning their trips.

Customers can now book with a £60 per person deposit and spread the cost through interest-free monthly payments, similar to the options available for other seasons such as summer 2027 and winter 2027/28.

Steve Heapy, CEO of Jet2, said: "We are already hearing from customers asking when they can book for Summer 2028, so we’ve responded by putting the programme on sale nice and early.

“We know our Yorkshire customers work hard for their well-deserved holidays, and this exciting announcement gives them something to look forward to."

He added: "By booking early, customers have the best choice when it comes to where they want to go and when they want to travel.

"Early bookers also get the pick of the best hotels, which are always the first to sell out, as well as the best rooms such as family rooms, interconnecting rooms, and rooms with a sea view, which fill up quickly too.

“With 2028 being a particularly special summer for us as we celebrate 25 years of Jet2, we look forward to showcasing our industry-leading customer service and helping our holidaymakers create unforgettable memories."

Jet2's summer 2028 programme from Leeds Bradford Airport includes a variety of destinations on sale.

These include Agadir, Alicante, Almería, Antalya, Barcelona, Bergerac, Bodrum, Bourgas, Catania, Corfu, Crete (Chania), Crete (Heraklion), Dalaman, Dubrovnik, Enfidha, Faro, Fuerteventura, Girona, Gran Canaria, Ibiza, Izmir, Jerez, Jersey, Kefalonia, Kos, Lanzarote, Larnaca, Madeira, Málaga, Malta, Menorca, Naples, Nice, Palma de Mallorca, Paphos, Pisa, Preveza, Reus, Rhodes, Rome, Santorini, Sharm el Sheikh, Skiathos, Split, Tenerife, Thessaloniki, Tivat, Verona, and Zante.

For those booking a package holiday with Jet2holidays, the offer includes ATOL protection, a selection of 2 to 5-star accommodation, transfers to and from the airport, and Free Child Place Holidays.

Customers flying with Jet2 can also enjoy convenient flight times, a 22kg baggage allowance, and 10kg hand luggage.

In addition to selling holidays, Jet2 is also known for its customer service, which has won awards.

This announcement comes as the airline prepares to celebrate its 25th anniversary in 2028, marking a significant milestone in its history.
