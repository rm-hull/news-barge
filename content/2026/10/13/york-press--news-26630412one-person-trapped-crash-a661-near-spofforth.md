A CRASH close to a North Yorkshire village left one person trapped in a vehicle.

The county's fire and rescue service said crews from Harrogate and Knaresborough were called to the A661 in the area of Spofforth, near Harrogate, at 9.02pm yesterday (Monday, October 12).

A service spokesperson said: "Crews extricated the casualty using cutting gear, small tools, lighting and a spinal board.

"The incident was then handed over to police and paramedics on scene."
