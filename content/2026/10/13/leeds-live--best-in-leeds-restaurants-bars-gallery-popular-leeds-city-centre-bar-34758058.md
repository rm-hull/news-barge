# Popular Leeds city centre bar reopens following major makeover

## 'We wanted to give the people of Leeds and beyond new reasons to visit'

![The Leeds city centre venue has enjoyed a major makeover](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/incoming/article34757910.ece/ALTERNATES/s810/1_Manahatta-2.jpg)

Take a look inside a popular Leeds city centre bar which has reopened with an exciting new look.

The venue, based in Greek Street, has enjoyed a major makeover. We have lots of photos of the refurbishment of Manahatta Greek Street.

Marking the Greek Street venue’s 10th anniversary, the venue has been transformed and designed to take guests from day to night, including refreshed interiors, new seating areas for guests to enjoy and an updated bar area.

Martin Wolstencroft, Co-Founder and chief executive at Manahatta’s operator, Arc Inspirations, said: “Greek Street is now our longest-running Manahatta site and not only did we want to celebrate its 10th anniversary with a bit of a makeover, we wanted to give the people of Leeds and beyond new reasons to visit. As well as the new look, we’ve introduced a Manahatta meal deal, after work happy hour, new cocktails and food items on the main menu too.

“We can’t wait to welcome guests, both old and new, to celebrate this next chapter with us. Come as you are, stay as long as you like and check out the changes to Manahatta Greek Street.”
