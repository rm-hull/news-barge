POLICE are appealing for information after a window was smashed at a community centre in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the reported incident of criminal damage took place at Millers Yard Community Centre in Gillygate on Sunday, September 6, at around 7.40pm.

Officers have released a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to, saying they may have information which could help the force's investigation into the incident.




Anyone with information is asked to contact PC Franks by email at [stacie-lea.franks@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:stacie-lea.franks@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, information can be submitted online by visiting the North Yorkshire Police website.

If you are unable to use these options, call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Please quote reference 12260175227.
