A biker from County Durham has died after a crash with a car in Wales.

The crash happened in Pontardawe, South Wales, at 10.40pm on Friday, October 9.

Paramedics attended the scene, but the man, a 52-year-old from Crook, died at the scene. His next of kin has been informed.

An investigation into the crash is ongoing, South Wales Police said.

Officers are appealing for anyone with information or dashcam footage who has not yet spoken to police to come forward quoting reference 2600331858.

Information can be provided online at orlo.uk/7Pju3, or by calling 101.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online at [https://orlo.uk/ymBF2.](https://orlo.uk/ymBF2.)
