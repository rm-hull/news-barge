# 'These contributions make a very real difference': Harrogate primary school's Harvest appeal supports homeless community

## A primary school in Harrogate is marking this year's Harvest celebrations by supporting projects that provide food and essential supplies for homeless people in the town.

St Peter's Church of England Primary School has chosen to support the work of St Peter's Church through this year's Harvest appeal, with donations helping its six-day-a-week Breakfast Club and Thursday ‘mini supermarket’ for people experiencing homelessness and hardship.

![The Revd Dr Alan Garrow, Vicar at St Peter's Church in Harrogate, welcomes this year's Harvest appeal by St Peter's Church of England Primary School, which will support the church's Breakfast Club and weekly 'mini supermarket' for people experiencing homelessness and hardship in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/13/9/19/Harvest-at-St-Peters.jpeg?trim=335,0,1,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The initiative forms part of the school's commitment to helping pupils understand the importance of supporting their local community, with children exploring themes of kindness, belonging and compassion through Harvest.

Led by the school's student worship leaders, pupils have been learning how small acts of generosity can make a real difference to people facing difficult circumstances in Harrogate.

Paul Griffiths, Headteacher at St Peter's Church of England Primary School, said: "Harvest is a wonderful tradition, and we want our children to understand its real-world application right here in Harrogate.

"Through our close partnership with St Peter's Church, our children are learning that true community spirit means looking out for one another and supporting those who need a helping hand in our town."

St Peter's Church has long provided practical support for people in need in Harrogate through its Breakfast Club, which serves hot breakfasts six mornings a week, as well as its weekly ‘mini supermarket’, offering food and everyday essentials on a no-questions-asked basis.

The Revd Dr Alan Garrow, Vicar at St Peter's Church, said: "We are continually blown away by the compassion and generosity of the children and families at St Peter's Church of England Primary School.

"These contributions make a very real difference to our projects, helping us provide a lifeline and a warm welcome to those who need it most in our community."

The school hopes the Harvest appeal will encourage pupils to see how helping others can have a positive impact beyond the classroom, while making a difference to some of Harrogate's most vulnerable residents.
