A 23-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of assaulting emergency service workers in York.

The incident happened in Lowther Street at 7.43pn on Monday (October 12).




The man, North Yorkshire Police said, was wanted in connection with an ongoing investigation.

A spokesperson said: “He was further arrested on suspicion of assaulting emergency workers, namely three police officers who assisted with the arrest.

“He remains in police custody for questioning.”
