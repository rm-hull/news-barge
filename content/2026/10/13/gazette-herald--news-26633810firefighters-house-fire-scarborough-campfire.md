FIREFIGHTERS were called to what was believed to be a house fire with people trapped inside in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service were called to Esplanade, Scarborough at 11.41am this morning (October 13) after reports of a fire in a building, with persons believed to be inside.

“A crew inspected and found it to be a campfire in the open,” said a spokesperson for the force.
