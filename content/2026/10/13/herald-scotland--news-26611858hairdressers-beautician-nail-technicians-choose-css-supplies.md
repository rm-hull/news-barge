From professional hair, beauty and barber products to equipment and complete salon furniture, CSS Combined Salon Supplies has established itself as a trusted supplier to professionals across throughout the UK.

Based in Glasgow, CSS supplies salons, barbers, hairdressers, beauty professionals and businesses with a comprehensive range of professional products at competitive trade prices.

The company stocks an extensive selection of leading professional brands, including American Crew, Andis, BaByliss Pro, Color Wow, Dax, Gabri, Gamma+, Gellux, Gummy, Igora Royal, Immortal Infuse, Indola, JRL, L'Oréal, Nioxin, Olaplex, Red One, Refectocil, Salon System, Schwarzkopf, Selective Professional, Sinep, Style Craft, The Shave Factory, Vasso, Wahl, Wella, Zenix, Hive and many more professional brands.

From professional hair colour and treatments to clippers, trimmers, styling products, barbering tools, beauty essentials and everyday salon supplies, CSS aims to give professionals access to the brands and products they know and trust, all under one roof.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21575414.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Furniture, equipment, professional supplies, fit-out support and finance options are available through one local supplier *(Image: Supplied)*

*Fast UK-Wide Delivery*

CSS delivers throughout the UK, with many products available through a 48-hour courier service, helping salons, barbers and beauty professionals receive the essential products they need quickly and efficiently.

CSS also offers FREE Local Delivery on orders over £100 and FREE UK Mainland Delivery on all furniture orders over £1,500.

*Salon & Barber Furniture Specialists*

Furniture is another major part of the CSS business, with an extensive range of barber chairs, styling stations, backwash units, reception desks, barber units and complete salon fit-outs available.

Whether you're opening your first salon or barbershop, expanding your business or completely refurbishing an existing space, the CSS team can help customers find furniture to suit their style, space and budget.

CSS also specialises in customised furniture, allowing customers to create solutions around their individual requirements, including specific dimensions, colours, finishes and more. You envision it. We design it.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21575415.jpg?type=mds-article-620)CSS has introduced asset finance options allowing qualifying businesses to spread the cost of salon furniture, equipment and fit-outs over periods ranging from 12 to 60 months *(Image: Supplied)*

*Furniture Delivered with Care*

What sets CSS apart is its approach to furniture delivery.

Rather than relying solely on third-party couriers, CSS personally delivers its furniture whenever possible. This gives the team greater control over the delivery process and helps reduce the risk of damage during transportation.

“Our customers are making a significant investment in their businesses, and we want to make sure their furniture arrives safely and in the condition they expect. That’s why we take care of our own furniture deliveries whenever possible.”

*Flexible finance options for larger projects*

CSS also offers finance options on selected salon and barber furniture, helping businesses spread the cost of their investment rather than paying the full amount upfront.

For larger projects, eligible customers can access flexible finance options with terms ranging from 12 to 60 months, subject to approval and applicable terms. This can help businesses manage cash flow while investing in furniture, equipment or a complete salon or barbershop fit-out.

CSS also accepts PayPal and Klarna at checkout, with instalment options available for eligible customers.
