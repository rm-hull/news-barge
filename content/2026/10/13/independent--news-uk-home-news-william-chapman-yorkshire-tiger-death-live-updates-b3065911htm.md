The man who was fatally wounded after entering a tiger enclosure has been named as William Chapman.

Mr Chapman posted footage on his now-deleted Instagram account, minutes before he was killed, of him approaching the tiger, called Altai, at the Yorkshire Wildlife Park on Saturday.

In the audio, the person filming can be heard breathing heavily as distant voices shout “Oh my God” and “seriously”. The park remained closed on Tuesday for a fourth day after the attack.

Emergency services were contacted shortly after 1.30pm on October 10 when Mr Chapman, who police said was visiting the park alone, entered the enclosure.

He was pronounced dead at the scene. City of Doncaster Council said on Monday it had started a health and safety investigation.

In a previous statement on social media, the wildlife park said “many, many people” had contacted them about Altai, the Amur tiger involved in the incident, and it confirmed he is “safe and well” and will not be euthanised.

## Recap: Local councillors reflect on 'upsetting' incident

Councillor Steve Cox, who represents the Finningley ward covering the wildlife park, said: “I really feel for the staff, they’ve got protocols they have to go through.

“It’s really sad, but I’m sure everybody did what they could.”

Doncaster’s deputy civic mayor councillor Nick Allen, who represents a nearby ward, said: “We don’t know all the circumstances yet and the speculation online is obviously upsetting and unneeded.”

He said he had been “really shocked about the tragic incident”.

## Recap: Man fatally wounded in tiger enclosure filmed encounter

A man who died after he was attacked by a tiger in a wildlife park apparently filmed the moment he approached the animal, according to footage posted in reports.

William Chapman, believed to be the identity of the victim, posted footage online minutes before he was killed of him approaching the tiger, called Altai, at the Yorkshire Wildlife Park on Saturday, *The Independent* understands.

## Yorkshire Wildlife Park remains closed

Yorkshire Wildlife Park remains closed for another day since Saturday’s incident.

A statement on the website reads:

“We can confirm that a serious incident has occurred on Saturday at the tiger enclosure.

“Our emergency response procedures were immediately activated, and emergency services attended the scene.

“The park will be closed today whilst we are working closely with the authorities. The Yorkshire Hive will remain open as usual**.**

“The safety and wellbeing of our visitors, staff and animals remain our highest priority.”

## City of Doncaster statement in full

Dan Swaine, executive director of place at City of Doncaster Council, said: “As the relevant licensing authority for the Yorkshire Wildlife Park, I can confirm that, in line with our statutory duties, the council has launched a health and safety investigation.

“We are also working with multiple agencies, including the police and health agencies, and are supporting the Yorkshire Wildlife Park in response to the incident.

“At this time, we won’t be commenting further on the individual or the investigation and we ask that the privacy of those involved is respected at this difficult time.”

## What we know about Yorkshire Wildlife Park's tiger enclosure

Yorkshire Wildlife Park opened in 2009, with the Land of the Tigers attraction opening in 2011.

The attraction is a purpose-built, two-acre reserve for Amur tigers, an endangered subspecies with around 500 left in the wild.

Photos of the enclosure on the park’s website show a high walkway for visitors and fencing above the section where the tigers can roam.
