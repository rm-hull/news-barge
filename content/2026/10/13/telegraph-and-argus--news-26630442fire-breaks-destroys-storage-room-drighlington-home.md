A house fire in a Bradford home completely destroyed a storage room.

Firefighters were called to a house fire on Fairfax Avenue, Drighlington, just before 6pm on Monday.

The West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed that crews attended the blaze at 5.58pm on Monday.

A spokesperson for WYFRS said the storage room was "100 per cent involved".

Firefighters used two hose reels, four breathing apparatus sets and positive-pressure ventilation to put out the fire.

The spokesperson added: "Advice was also obtained from a fire investigation officer, but they did not attend.

"Smoke detection was installed and working."

Fire engines from Spen Valley, Morley and Leeds stations attended the scene.

Firefighters have stressed that checking smoke alarms can help limit the spread of fire.
