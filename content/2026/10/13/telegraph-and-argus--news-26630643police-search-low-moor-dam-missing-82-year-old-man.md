Police have searched a Bradford reservoir for a missing man.

West Yorkshire Police are looking for 82-year-old Barry, who was last seen leaving his home in the Wibsey area of Bradford at around 7.15am on Saturday.

Concerns are growing for his welfare, and officers have been searching Royds Hall Dam in Low Moor as part of their investigation.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Barry is known to walk in parks and other public spaces on the Bradford/Kirklees border including Wibsey Park, Harold Park, Royds Hall Dam, Spen Valley Greenway, Bierley Fishing Ponds and Cleckheaton Greenway."

More than 15 officers reportedly took part in the search at the dam, off Lingdale Road and Mendip Way.

The teams included the Regional Marine and Underwater Search Unit, divers, crime scene investigators and drone pilots.

Barry is described as a white man about 5ft 7in tall, with an almost bald head and tattoos on his chest and arms.

Police have urged anyone who has seen Barry or knows where he is to come forward.

A police spokesperson added: "If anyone has any information that could assist in the ongoing search for Barry, they are asked to contact Bradford CID via Live Chat on our website or by calling 101, quoting log 618 of October 10."

Police have urged anyone who was in those areas on Saturday to remain vigilant and report any sightings.
