# How did Katie Zacharia do at her first - very brief - White House briefing?

Katie Zacharia held her first press briefing as the new White House press secretary, just a day after beginning the job. During the media conference, Zacharia broke news that Kimberly Guilfoyle was asked to resign as the US ambassador to Greece.

The BBC's North America editor Sarah Smith was in the room and describes Zacharia's performance.
