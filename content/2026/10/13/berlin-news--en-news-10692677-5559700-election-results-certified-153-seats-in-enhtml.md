According to official results, Die Linke emerged as the largest party in the election held just over three weeks ago, with 25.7 percent of the vote. It was followed by the CDU (18.8 percent), the AfD (16.3 percent), the Greens (14.3 percent), and the SPD (12.1 percent). In the new parliament, Die Linke holds 45 of the 153 seats, the CDU holds 33 seats, and the AfD holds 29 seats. The Greens have 25 representatives and the SPD has 21. The State Election Commission consists of State Election Commissioner Stephan Bröchler, two judges from the Higher Administrative Court, and six eligible voters nominated by the political parties.
