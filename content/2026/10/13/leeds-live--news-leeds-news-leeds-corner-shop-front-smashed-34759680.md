# Leeds corner shop front smashed in early hours raid for cash and vapes

## Burglars targeted Della Mini Market in Harehills

Thieves smashed up a Leeds corner shop in an early hours burglary.

They made off with cash and vapes after smashing the front of Della Mini Market, on Ashley Road, Harehills, in the early hours of Sunday, October 11.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: The suspects made off with a quantity of cash and vapes. Anyone with information or footage that may assist this investigation is asked to contact police in Leeds."

You can contact the force on 101 with crime reference 13260578776. Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online.
