A popular Sunday lunch spot in Scarborough has applied for permission to offer al fresco dining.

Chameleon, in Bar Street in the coastal town, has applied for an updated licence allowing it to keep serving customers on the pavement outside the premises.

The well-rated restaurant would place six tables and 24 chairs on the pavement if William Miller’s proposal is approved.

On the customer feedback website TripAdvisor, Chameleon has a rating of 4.3 out of five and is ranked as the 88th best restaurant in the town out of about 300.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21573656.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A Bar Street Establishment In Scarborough Has Applied For A New Licence From North Yorkshire Council. Google Maps

The business, which serves breakfast, street food, and homemade cakes and scones, also offers bottomless brunches and cocktails.

Commenting on TripAdvisor, one recent visitor said: “Just rolled up by chance. Lovely food, all good. Will drop by again when up this way.”

The deadline for members of the public to comment on the pavement seating plans is Thursday, October 22.

Anyone wishing to make representations regarding the application should give notice in writing to: North Yorkshire Council, Licensing Services, Town Hall, St Nicholas Street, Scarborough, YO11 2HG.  
​  
​The authority can also be contacted by email at [licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.)  
​  
​The council’s website states: “It is an offence knowingly or recklessly to make a false statement in connection with an application punishable on summary conviction by an unlimited fine.”
