POSTERS have caused criminal damage around a York university campus, police say.

North Yorkshire Police has launched an appeal after the posters were positioned around York St John University at approximately 9.20pm on Tuesday (September 22).

The force believe the people in these images will have information that could help their investigation.




![Police have launched an appeal after the 'criminal damage'](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21573683.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police have launched an appeal after the 'criminal damage' *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A spokesperson said: "We would like to speak to them following a report of criminal damage that took place in York.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [stacie-lea.franks@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:stacie-lea.franks@northyorkshire.police.uk) or 101.

Please quote reference 12260186931 when providing information.
