A NORTH Yorkshire garden is preparing to host a three-day celebration of all things autumn this weekend.

RHS Garden Harlow Carr in Harrogate will hold its Autumn Weekend from Friday (October 16) to Sunday (October 18). offering visitors the chance to experience its seasonal displays at their peak.

The garden is known for its vibrant Japanese acers, copper-toned conifers and golden woodland walks during the autumn months.

There are also displays of late-flowering perennials, ornamental grasses, berries and seedheads throughout the grounds.

Fiona Slight, curator at RHS Garden Harlow Carr, said: "Autumn truly is one of the most rewarding seasons at RHS Harlow Carr.

"The changing colours bring an entirely new character to the garden, from the vivid reds of the acers around the Sandstone Rock Garden to the golden canopies in the Woodland landscape and the beautiful reflections on the Queen Mother's Lake.

![Trees at RHS Harlow Carr](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21573346.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Trees at RHS Harlow Carr *(Image: Provided)*

"There's something different to discover every week, making it a wonderful time to slow down, enjoy nature and experience the garden in a completely new light."

A key highlight of the weekend is the RHS Late Fruit and Vegetable Competition, which features more than 60 classes of prize-winning produce.

The Northern Fruit Group Apple Display will also return, offering visitors the chance to have their own homegrown fruit identified by experts.

![Autumnal flowers](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21573339.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Autumnal flowers *(Image: Provided)*

Those interested in fungi can join guided fungus forays led by the Mid Yorkshire Fungus Group, who will lead forays daily at 11.30am and 1.30pm, costing £3 per person, and talks on fungi will be given by Jenny Sergeant on Friday and Saturday at 11am.

All Autumn Weekend events are included with standard garden admission and are free for RHS members.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21573338.jpg?type=mds-article-620)RHS Harlow Carr *(Image: Provided)*

The garden’s Sandstone Rock Garden is also said to be a particular autumn favourite, with winding paths through vibrant foliage and mature trees.

More information and tickets are available at rhs.org.uk/harlowcarr.
