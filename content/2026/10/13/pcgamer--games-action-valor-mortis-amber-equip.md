💎 Ambers are precious gems you equip to gain passive bonuses and abilities.  
💎 You can slot them at the level up menu with any lantern.  
💎 You unlock a new slot every ten levels.

You'll most likely find your first amber in Valor Mortis before you're actually able to equip it. The game has a fairly simplistic skill tree that focuses around passive buffs, so the ambers you equip are more what add a bit of spice to your build.

You can **equip an amber once you reach Level 5** by leveling up at any lantern. When you enter the level up menu, you'll notice that a slot with a + symbol has unlocked on the bottom layer—click on this and select the amber you want to equip, and don't worry, you can swap them at any time. After this, you'll unlock a new slots every ten levels at:

* **Slot two**: Level 15
* **Slot three**: Level 25
* **Slot four**: Level 35
* **Slot five**: Level 45

This means there are five amber slots overall. You'll generally get new ambers from quests and chests, and any amber you already own will level up to provide a more powerful passive bonus when you acquire a copy of it.
