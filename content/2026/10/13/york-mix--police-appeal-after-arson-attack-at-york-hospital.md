Police investigating an incident of arson in York have released this image of a man they want to speak to.

It happened at the Nuffield Hospital on Haxby Road.

Officers were called shortly before 1am on Wednesday 23 September, to a report of bins on fire near the hospital entrance.

#### Nuffield Hospital, Haxby Road

Fire crews put out the flames.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Thankfully there were no injuries.

“We now want to speak to a man seen wearing a hooded top at the location.

“He may have information that could assist our investigation.

“If you know who this is, please contact North Yorkshire Police straight away.”

The man is described as white, in his early 50s, of scruffy appearance, wearing black tracksuit bottoms with stripes down the leg, and carrying a black backpack.
