## York police cadets tackle giant inflatable obstacle course for hospice charity

Police cadets took part in a giant inflatable obstacle run in York to raise money for St Leonard's Hospice *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Police cadets took part in a giant inflatable obstacle run in York to raise money for charity.

The event was held at Esrick Park on Saturday, September 26, 2026.

Each cadet was required to raise more than £100, with the proceeds going to St Leonard's Hospice.

Cadet leaders Chris Gill and Lyndsey Ibbotson also participated, encouraging the cadets throughout the course, while parents were present to offer support.

The activity was part of the police cadet programme, which promotes fundraising for various charities.

The obstacle course included inflatable tunnels, jumps, and swings, providing a fun challenge for all participants.
