A manhunt has been launched to find a Bradford man who is wanted for robbery and theft offences.

Police have made extensive enquiries to find Afraheem Javed, 30, from Girlington.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police in Bradford are appealing for information to help locate Afraheem Javeed who is wanted for several theft offences.

"Javeed, 30, from Girlington, is also wanted on suspicion of robbery and over two warrants for failing to appear in court.

"He is known to frequently attend Victoria Shopping Centre in Girlington and is known to the Bradford West area.

"Extensive enquiries have been ongoing to locate Javeed.

"Anyone with information that could assist in locating him is asked to contact officers via 101 quoting reference 13260451316 or online via Live Chat | West Yorkshire Police.

"Information can also be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111."
