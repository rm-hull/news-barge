RAF Association members recently raised funds in Pickering, Helmsley, and Malton.

The efforts support veterans, serving personnel, and their families.

The branch is well on the way to achieving its £5,000 target for the year, with the 1323 Ryedale Squadron Air Cadets assisting with the collection in Malton.

The cadets also participated in the annual Battle of Britain Service at St Peter's Norton, joining veterans, serving personnel, and their families in the event.
