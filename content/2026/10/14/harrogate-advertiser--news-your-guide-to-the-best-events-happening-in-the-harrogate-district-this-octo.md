# Your guide to the best events happening in the Harrogate district this October — including shows, gigs and comedy you won't want to miss

## Whether you're planning a night out this weekend or looking ahead to the rest of October, here's our pick of the best shows, gigs, comedy and cultural events happening across the Harrogate district.

## **What’s on in the Harrogate district during October...Thursday, October 15-31:** Sugar Rush exhibition by artist Nicholas Jolly at 108 Fine Art Gallery at 16 Cold Bath Road, Harrogate.

![Wednesday, October 20-24, 7.30pm: We Will Rock You musical at Harrogate Theatre. ](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/15/59/WeRocWIl.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


**Thursday, October 15, 9pm:** Live music with Drop Leg Steppers at the Blues Bar, Harrogate.

**Thursday, October 15-17, 7.30pm:** The Knaresborough Players Present 2:22 A Ghost Story at Frazer Theatre, Knaresborough.

**Thursday, October 15-17, 7.30pm:** Harrogate Dramatic Society presents Jerusalem, by Jez Butterworth at Harrogate Theatre. Also 2.30pm.

**Thursday, October 15, 8pm:** Yorkshire folk and Americana songwriters evening with Serious Sam Barrett, Lewis Pugh and David Broad at Roosters Taproom Hornbeam Park, Harrogate.

**Thursday, October 15, 8pm:** Tez Ilyas: Tez Things I Hate About You at Harrogate Studio Theatre. Part of Harrogate Comedy Festival.

**Friday, October 16, 7.30pm:** Bottom of the Bottle 25th Reunion Party with DJ Trev at The Bilton Club, Harrogate.

**Friday, October 16, 9pm:** Live music with Crosscut Saw at the Blues Bar, Harrogate.

**Saturday, October 17, 7pm:** Vocalis presents Missa Sancti Nicolai (Haydn St. Nicholas Mass) at Wesley Centre, Harrogate.

**Saturday, October 17, 7pm:** Ripley Live presents Man of the World – A Celebration of the Music of Peter Green + Georgette & Iain at Ripley Town Hall.

**Saturday, October 17, 7.30pm:** Ripon’s St Cecilia Orchestra presents Bernstein, Gershwin and Rachmaninov with soloist Martin Roscoe (piano) at Ripon Cathedral.

**Saturday, October 17, 7.30pm:** Live music with Definitely Maybeez Oasis Tribute at The Bilton Club, Harrogate.

**Sunday, October 18, 9pm:** Live music with Erin Harpe and Jim Countryman at the Blues Bar, Harrogate.

**Monday, October 19, 7.30pm:** Harrogate Film Society presents Sentimental Value (15 | Norway) at the Harrogate Odeon.

**Wednesday, October 20-24, 7.30pm:** We Will Rock You at Harrogate Theatre. Also 2.30pm.

**Thursday, October 22, noon:** Yorkshire Life Literary Lunch with Sophie Raworth at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Thursday, October 22, 4.30pm:** Sir John Curtice (Making Sense of British Politics) at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Thursday, October 22, 6.30pm:** Heather Morris (The Piano Teacher of Montparnasse) at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Friday, October 23, 5pm:** Strictly Speaking with John Sergeant at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Friday, October 23, 7.30pm:** Close Knit - A Drip Drop Theatre Musical by E.C.R Roberts at Frazer Theatre, Knaresborough.

**Friday, October 23, 8pm:** The Midmorning Murders with Julian Clary at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Saturday, October 24, 7.30pm:** Richard Pulsford: Gold Fashioned Comedy at Frazer Theatre, Knaresborough.

**Saturday, October 24, 7.30pm:** Queen of the Night – A Tribute to Whitney Houston at the Royal Hall, Harrogate.

**Saturday, October 24:** Raworths Family Literature Festival with children's stories, poetry, and outdoor activities at Pinewoods and The Crown Hotel.

**Saturday, October 24, 11am:** Alison Stankard - Totes Inappropes – at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Saturday, October 24, 12.30pm:** A Hard Day’s Night with Samira Ahmed at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Saturday, October 24, 3.30pm:** Fight to the Death with Paul Brand at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Saturday, October 24, 8pm:** Acoustic roots duo Mambo Jambo at Grewelthorpe Village Hall.

Tickets £12 from the Village Hall during café opening or online at: [https://www.wegottickets.com/grewelthorpe](https://www.wegottickets.com/grewelthorpe)

**Sunday, October 25, 7pm:** John Bramwell (I am Kloot) at Frazer Theatre, Knaresborough.

**Sunday, October 25, 7.30pm:** Things I Took From the Dark with Reece Shearsmith at the Royal Hall, Harrogate. Part of Harrogate Comedy Festival.

**Sunday, October 25, 12.30pm:** Kate Mosse - Autumn at the Crown Hotel. Part of Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival.

**Sunday, October 25, 7.30pm:** John Lydon: Talk Tour at Harrogate Theatre.

**Tuesday, October 27, 7.30pm:** Direct from the West End - Adam Garcia’s Emerald Storm at the Royal Hall, Harrogate.

**Saturday, October 31, 3pm:** Haunted House DJs House Music event at the Piano Harrogate.

**Friday, November 6, 7.30pm:** Opening concert of 2026-27 season with the Cuore Chamber Orchestra at the Royal Hall, Harrogate.

**Saturday, October 17, 7pm:** Ripley Live presents the Van Morrison Alumni Band at Ripley Town Hall. Sold out.
