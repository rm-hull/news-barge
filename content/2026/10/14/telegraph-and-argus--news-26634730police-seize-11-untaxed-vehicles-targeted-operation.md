11 untaxed vehicles have been seized by police during a targeted operation.

Officers from the Shipley Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) identified the road tax offences in a joint operation with Bradford Council.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Shipley NPT officers joined forces with Bradford Council and discovered 11 vehicles whose road tax had apparently taken an extended holiday.

"Sadly for the owners, those vehicles have now embarked on a different journey - straight to the vehicle compound.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21577929.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This vehicle seized by police in a Shipley operation

"If you use or keep a vehicle on the road, it's your responsibility to ensure it's properly taxed.

"Failing to do so could result in penalties, enforcement action and your vehicle being seized.

"Don't let your car become the next contestant on 'Gone in 60 Seconds – Shipley Edition.'

"Check your vehicle details, keep everything up to date and avoid an unnecessary bill."
