A LANTER parade will light up the night.

Hundreds of glowing lanterns, giant illuminated puppets, and live music will fill Lister Park in Bradford on Friday, October 30, as part of this year’s Lantern Parade.

Starting at 4.30pm outside Cartwright Hall Art Gallery, the parade begins at 5.30pm and will conclude with a theatrical finale at the Bandstand around 6.30pm.

Katie Jones from Cecil Green Arts said: "It is really great to be bringing this well-loved and spectacular event to Lister Park again and, after a year off, it feels particularly important to give the community an opportunity to come together and enjoy something special."

This year’s theme, 'Celebration of Life', will be reflected in lanterns created by schools, community groups, and artists.

The event is part of the Festivals of the Dead project, a collaboration between Cecil Green Arts, the University of Bradford, and Bradford Death Cafes.

Cllr Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s executive member for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "The Lister Park Lantern Parade is a fantastic celebration of creativity and community, bringing people of all ages together for a spectacular evening in the park."

A quieter lantern event will also be held at Northcliffe Park on Saturday, November 14.
