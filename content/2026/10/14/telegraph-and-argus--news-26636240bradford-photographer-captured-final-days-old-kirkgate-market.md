Bradford photographer Jerry Smith took many photos of Bradford markets.

His daughter, Jo Singh, shares these photos of the old Kirkgate Market, taken in January 1973.

The market was demolished later that year.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579806.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The hustle and bustle of the much-missed Kirkgate Market.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579808.jpg?type=mds-article-620)'There were many popular stalls and cafes, all with similar menus of hamburgers, soup, Horlicks, Bovril, ice creams, and of course tea and coffee,' says Jo. *(Image: Jerry Smith)*

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579807.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Waiting for a cuppa and a sandwich in one of the market cafes.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579809.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ice-creams and hot drinks were served up for shoppers taking a breather.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579814.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Shoppers heading in and out of the old market.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579813.jpg?type=mds-article-620) The market sold a range of household and clothing as well as food.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579812.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The ornate stonework on the market roof.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579811.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A staff area or security booth in the market.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579805.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jo's mum was one of many protesters against the market demolition. 'They stayed inside overnight to try and prevent it and were arrested in the morning,' recalls Jo. *(Image: Jerry Smith)*
