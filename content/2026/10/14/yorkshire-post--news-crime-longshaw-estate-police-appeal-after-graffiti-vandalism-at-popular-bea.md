# Longshaw Estate: Police appeal after graffiti vandalism at popular beauty spot

## Police are appealing for information after graffiti was discovered at a popular Peak District beauty spot, prompting officers to condemn the damage to the countryside.

Derbyshire Police have launched an appeal following an incident of criminal damage at Longshaw Estate, near Padley Gorge.

The incident was reported at around 10.30am on Saturday (Oct 10), off a footpath near the Padley Gorge Trail and Burbage Brook.

Officers are asking anyone who was in the area at the time to come forward if they witnessed anything suspicious or have information that could help identify those responsible.

![Graffiti found at the Longshaw Estate](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/14/10/54/846050662_1680248290209197_5185804604961675885_n.jpeg?trim=0,5,0,4&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Police have also urged the individuals responsible for the damage to come forward.

In a statement shared on social media, Derbyshire Police said: “Our countryside is not a canvas for vandalism!”

The force added: “Longshaw Estate is a beautiful natural area enjoyed by so many. This damage is completely unnecessary and has no place in our countryside.”

![Photos released by Derbyshire Police in the appeal for information on Longshaw Estate graffiti](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/14/10/57/844966694_1011057922008061_275122627618401379_n.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=530&quality=65)


Anyone who witnessed the incident, saw anyone acting suspiciously or has information about those involved is encouraged to contact Derbyshire Police.

Information can be reported by calling 101 or through the force's online reporting service, quoting reference 26\*614017.

Longshaw Estate, which is managed by the National Trust, has been contacted for a comment.
