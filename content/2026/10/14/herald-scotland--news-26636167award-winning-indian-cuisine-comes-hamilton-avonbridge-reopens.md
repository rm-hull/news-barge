*The chef behind a restaurant recognised as Scotland's Best Indian Restaurant brings award-winning culinary experience to the**Avonbridge Hotel**, alongside an exciting new European menu*

Hamilton's dining scene is set for an exciting new chapter as the Avonbridge Hotel confirms that its newly refurbished restaurant and public areas will open to the public on Thursday, 15 October 2026.

Under AMA Hotels, the landmark venue will introduce a brand-new dining experience combining Indian cuisine with European favourites, bringing two popular culinary traditions together under one roof.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21527481.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Avonbridge Hotel *(Image: Supplied)*

Management has confirmed that the chef behind the hotel's Indian food previously helped achieve a Best Indian Restaurant in Scotland award, bringing recognised culinary experience to Hamilton. Award-winning Indian food arrives in Hamilton.

The new Indian menu will showcase aromatic spices, rich flavours and carefully prepared dishes, while the European selection will offer familiar favourites for families, couples and groups.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21579721.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Avonbridge Hotel *(Image: Supplied)*

The dual-cuisine concept is designed to give diners more choice without compromising on quality. A Hamilton landmark transformed First opened in 1973 under the Martalo family, the Avonbridge Hotel has been a familiar destination for generations of local residents.

Its latest transformation includes refurbished restaurant and bar spaces, an updated entrance and conservatory, and contemporary interiors featuring walnut finishes, brass detailing and warm lighting.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21527483.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Avonbridge Hotel *(Image: Supplied)*

AMA Hotels looks ahead A proposed statement from AMA Hotels management reads: “We are absolutely delighted to announce the reopening of the Avonbridge Hotel on Thursday, 15 October.

"We believe we are bringing something special to Hamilton. Having a chef whose previous work has been recognised with a Best Indian Restaurant in Scotland award is a fantastic part of our new offering. Together with our European menu and refreshed surroundings, we want to create somewhere families, friends and visitors can enjoy exceptional food and a warm welcome. We cannot wait to open our doors.”

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21579722.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Avonbridge Hotel *(Image: Supplied)*

*Doors open Thursday, 15 October The newly refurbished restaurant and public areas at the**Avonbridge Hotel** will welcome the public from Thursday, 15 October 2026. AMA Hotels looks forward to welcoming returning guests and first-time visitors to experience the transformation.*
