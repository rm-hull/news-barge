A POWERFUL stage drama exploring the impact of child abduction is set to be performed this autumn.

Frozen, written by Bryony Lavery and directed by Shipley-based Abbe Robinson, will run at Bingley Little Theatre from Monday, October 26 to Saturday, October 31.

The play tells the story of a 10-year-old girl who goes missing on her way to her grandmother’s house, and the emotional aftermath experienced by her mother, the man responsible, and a psychologist.

Ms Robinson said: "Frozen is a powerful drama with big themes to explore; abuse, grief, loss, guilt, remorse and forgiveness."

A signed performance will take place on Tuesday, October 27 at 7.30pm.

Ms Robinson has a strong background in the arts, having served as artistic director and producer of Light Night Leeds for nine years.

She also worked as a senior producer for Bradford 2025 UK City of Culture.

The cast includes Julian Freeman, Kate Maddison-Greenwell and Vani Midgley.
