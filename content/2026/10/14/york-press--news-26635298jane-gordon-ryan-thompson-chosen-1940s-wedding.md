## Couple renew vows in wartime-themed ceremony at 1940s festival

Jane Gordon and Ryan Thompson won a competition for the ceremony as part of the Pickering 1940s weekend. *(Image: Tim Bruce)*

A COUPLE who share a love of the 1940s 'tied the knot' in a themed wedding at the weekend.

Jane Gordon and Ryan Thompson won a competition for the ceremony as part of the Pickering 1940s weekend.

The couple, from Yorkshire, both have family ties to military history, and were chosen as winners of the competition launched earlier this year by Eden Camp, based near Old Malton, and Pickering Town CIC’s.

The winning couple, who are already married, renewed their vows in a ceremony on October 10 at St Peter and St Paul’s Church in Pickering, before travelling through town in a vintage vehicle and continuing the celebrations at Eden Camp’s Heritage Hall.

Their story stood out for its strong military connections, passion for history, and resilience through personal challenges.

The bride, Jane, was raised by her grandparents, whose own wartime experiences inspired a lifelong interest in the Second World War.

Her grandfather served in the Polish Army, and after being unable to return home following the conflict, rebuilt his life in England.

Jane's grandmother had been evacuated to North Yorkshire during the war.

The winning couple met through an armed forces penpal website during the Covid-19 pandemic whilst the groom, a serving soldier, was deployed overseas.

Jane & Ryan said: “We’re absolutely over the moon to have been chosen.

"The 1940s have always been incredibly special to us because of our family history, our love of living history events, and the military connections that have shaped both our lives.

"Our own relationship began with long periods apart, exchanging messages overseas, which in many ways echoed a wartime story.

"This vow renewal will give us the chance to celebrate our journey, honour our grandparents, and create special memories with our family and friends.”

Luke Hudman, Marketing Manager at Eden Camp, added: “The winning entry perfectly captured everything we hoped the competition would celebrate - a love of history and a personal connection to the wartime generation. We were delighted to be able to give them the opportunity to renew their vows.”

The couple’s special day coincided with the Pickering 1940s weekend, an immersive two-day event that invited visitors of all ages to step back in time and experience the spirit, sights, and sounds of wartime Britain.

Carrie Brackstone, Operations Manager at Pickering Town CIC, said: “Their family’s wartime story spans generations and countries and the celebration was a truly memorable day that celebrated their love story and history.”
