Police were called to a 'burglary in progress' at a garden centre.

West Yorkshire Police was called just before 9pm on Tuesday to the scene of a reported burglary at a business on Canal Road in Bradford.

Social media reports show three police officers outside the ACW Garden Centre.

Police have explained why officers were at the business.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police received a report of a burglary in progress at 8.45pm yesterday at a business premise on Canal Road, Bradford.

"Officers attended and made an area search for the suspect.

"At the time of report, it was not confirmed if anything had been stolen."
