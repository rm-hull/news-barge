**Rescue workers combed through the wreckage** of damaged buildings in**Kyiv** on Wednesday after a wave of**Russian missiles and drones** struck the Ukrainian capital overnight, leaving at least**three people dead and 22 injured**.

In the **Desnianskyi district**, an attack on a** nine-storey residential block**killed two people and injured 21, including a child. Emergency crews rescued** 16 residents**, while firefighters tackled a blaze spanning** 400 square metres**. Sixteen of those injured were hospitalised, four in a serious condition.

Another person was killed and one injured in the **Holosiivskyi district**, where a non-residential building was hit. Psychologists from the emergency services assisted** 110 people** at the affected sites.

The strikes also targeted **Kyiv’s energy infrastructure**, with President** Volodymyr Zelensky**confirming that a ballistic missile hit the** CHP-4 heat and power plant**. He said Ukraine had received** no warning from its partners** before detecting the incoming missiles.

The broader overnight attacks on energy facilities left **more than 600,000 people without power** across Ukraine.
