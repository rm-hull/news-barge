A housebuilder is set to open a new sales office at its latest development in Dunnington.

David Wilson Homes will open the new office at its Eastfield Park site on Saturday, October 17, giving prospective buyers the opportunity to learn more about the homes on offer and speak with the sales team.

Eastfield Park will feature 44 homes in total, with a mix of one-, two-, three-, and four-bedroom properties aimed at a range of buyers, including families, young professionals, and first-time buyers.

Paul Hogan, sales director at David Wilson Homes Yorkshire East, said: "We’re looking forward to welcoming prospective buyers to the launch of the new sales office at our newest development at Eastfield Park.

"With an exclusive number of homes scheduled to be completed by next summer, this is a fantastic opportunity for prospective buyers in North Yorkshire to secure a new, energy-efficient home."

Located in the village of Dunnington, the development offers a semi-rural setting with easy access to essential amenities, well-regarded schools, and transport links to York city centre and beyond.

The homes are designed to appeal to those seeking a balance between village living and convenient connectivity.

More information about the development is available on the David Wilson Homes website.
