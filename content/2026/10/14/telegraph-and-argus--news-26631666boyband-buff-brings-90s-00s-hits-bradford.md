A CHEEKY nostalgia-filled concert is coming to Bradford for one night only.

Boyband in the Buff will bring its throwback show to St George's Hall tomorrow, October 15, 2026.

The performance starts at 7:30pm and has a running time of two hours.

Featuring pop heartthrob Gareth Gates and a cast of talented performers, the concert promises slick harmonies, energetic choreography and plenty of charisma.

The show celebrates the biggest hits of the 1990s and 2000s, with music associated with Backstreet Boys, NSYNC, Westlife and Take That.

Described as nostalgia turned all the way up, it combines pop and passion with a cheeky twist as the layers drop and the temperature rises.

The event is for audiences aged 18 and over. Tickets cost between £35 and £53, with standard tickets listed at that price.
