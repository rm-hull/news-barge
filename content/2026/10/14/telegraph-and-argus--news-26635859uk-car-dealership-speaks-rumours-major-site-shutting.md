A car dealership has commented on speculation that one of its major sites will be shutting.

The JCT600 branch on Sticker Lane in Bradford sells BMW, Audi, Lotus and Maserati vehicles.

The Telegraph & Argus was contacted by a source who claimed the dealership would close on November 30, describing it as a "sad day for Bradford" given the firm’s long-standing presence in the city.

## What has JCT600 said?

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579312.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The JCT600 dealership on Sticker Lane, Bradford *(Image: UGC)*

JCT600 confirmed to the T&A that it is in talks with staff about possible changes to the site.

The company is currently undergoing a formal consultation process with employees.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21579300.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The JCT600 dealership on Sticker Lane, Bradford *(Image: UGC)*

## Full JCT600 statement

A JCT600 spokesperson said: "We are aware of speculation regarding potential changes to some of our Sticker Lane, Bradford, operations.

"Our colleagues are our number one priority, and we are currently engaged in a formal consultation process with those individuals potentially impacted, and until that process has been completed, it would not be appropriate to comment on specific proposals.

"We remain committed to communicating openly with colleagues and will provide further information publicly once the consultation process has concluded."

## BMW speaks out

A BMW spokesperson said: "As an independent business, JCT600 is best placed to comment on its own operations.

"We can however reassure BMW and MINI customers in Bradford that they will continue to have access to aftersales support through our authorised repairer network across the region."

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted Audi, Lotus and Maserati for a comment.

## JCT600 started out on Sticker Lane

JCT600, which was found in Bradford back in 1946, is one of the UK's largest family-owned automotive retail groups.

What began as a single garage on Sticker Lane has grown into a major regional retailer representing multiple vehicle manufacturers across Yorkshire, Lincolnshire, Derbyshire and the North East.

The company's headquarters is located at Tordoff House in Apperley Bridge - where key corporate functions such as leadership, finance, marketing, human resources and strategic planning are managed.
