**A man has died after falling ill at a West Fife spa.**

Emergency services were called to the Keavil House Hotel on Friday evening, and transported him to hospital.

**Read More:**

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: “On the evening of Friday, October 9, officers were made aware of a man having taken unwell at a premises in Crossford.

“He was taken to hospital, where he was pronounced dead.

“The death is being treated as unexplained, however there are not believed to be any suspicious circumstances.”
