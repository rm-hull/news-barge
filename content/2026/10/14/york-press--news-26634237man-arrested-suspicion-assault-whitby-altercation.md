A MAN in his 50s has been arrested on suspicion of assault following a "violent altercation" in a North Yorkshire coastal town, police said.  
  
The county's police force said the incident took place in Upgang Lane, Whitby at around 7.45pm on Sunday, October 4, and involved an alleged assault on the driver of a stationary Land Rover Discovery.  
  
North Yorkshire Police said it believes the incident began outside Gino's Pizza on White Point Road.




A police spokesperson said: "The driver and another person, who was also driving a Land Rover Discovery, are said to have then followed the suspect on foot.

"The driver who was initially assaulted did not require hospital treatment."

The arrested man, who was on foot at the time of the incident, has been released under investigation while enquiries continue.

In a statement, North Yorkshire Police said they are appealing for information about a man it described as "A white male, over six feet tall, or broad build, and wearing a black and white striped sweatshirt."

The man was seen holding a dog on a lead during the alleged "altercation", which was not harmed, police said.

Both Land Rovers involved were described as dark in colour and muddy.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

Those who can assist are asked to contact officer Evie Walters by email at [evie.walters@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:evie.walters@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, information can be provided online via the North Yorkshire Police website.

The reference number is 12260195344 when providing information.
