One person was taken to hospital after a road incident in Stockton.

Emergency services were called to an incident on Harrowgate Lane at 8.22am today (Wednesday, October 14).

It is understood to have happened near the Outwood Academy Bishopsgarth school and the road was closed while emergency crews dealt with the incident.

One person was taken to hospital for further treatment.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Harrowgate Lane in Stockton at 8.22am on Wednesday, October 14, 2026.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and one patient was taken to University Hospital of North Tees."

The road has since reopened.

Cleveland Police have been contacted for more information.
