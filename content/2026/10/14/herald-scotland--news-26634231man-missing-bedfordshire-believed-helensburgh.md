Police are appealing for the public’s help to trace a man reported missing from Bedfordshire.

Caleb Thomas was reported missing around 3.45pm on Monday, October 12, and following enquiries, it is believed he may be in the central Helensburgh area.

The 40-year-old is described as having short black hair with some facial hair. His clothing when last seen is not known.

Inspector Annette McVicar said: “We are growing concerned for Caleb’s welfare and our enquiries are ongoing to trace him as soon as possible.

“We understand he may have travelled to Helensburgh and we are appealing for any sightings of him since 11 October.

“I would appeal to anyone who may have seen Caleb or who has any information on his whereabouts to contact us.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland via 101 quoting reference 3794 of 12 October, 2026.
