**Footballer Antoine Semenyo "froze" after he was allegedly racially abused by a Liverpool fan during a Premier League match at Anfield, a court has heard.**

The Manchester City winger, who was playing for Bournemouth at the time, was preparing to take a throw-in when Mark Mogan, 47, allegedly shouted a racist insult at him on 15 August 2025.

Prosecutors said Semenyo was "clearly hurt and upset" and reported the incident to referee Anthony Taylor when the ball next went out of play.

Mogan, of Dovecot, Liverpool, denies racially aggravated behaviour.

Liverpool Magistrates' Court heard Mogan, who uses a wheelchair, shouted an offensive slur about 30 minutes into the opening match of the Premier League season.

Prosecutor Simon Jones said: "Mr Semenyo briefly froze as he couldn't believe what the male had said."

![A man in a wheelchair wearing a dark navy hooded jacket with a quilted front is photographed holding a ciggarette towards his mouth. He is wearing dark sunglasses.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/4d2d/live/b2159be0-c7d8-11f1-9671-5b70c6e774ea.jpg)

Mogan, 47, denies racially aggravated behaviour during the Premier League match at Anfield

The court heard Mogan then shouted further abuse at Semenyo, who did not respond and went on to take the throw-in.

Mogan was removed from the stadium at half-time, the court was told.

Two short video clips were shown to the court, with Jones describing Mogan as being in an "agitated and angry state".

![Semenyo wearing Manchester City's sky-blue home shirt stands on the pitch with both arms raised above his head and his index fingers pointing upwards. His eyes are closed. A large crowd of spectators fills the blurred background.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/088a/live/132e0d70-c7d6-11f1-9671-5b70c6e774ea.jpg)

Semenyo now plays for Manchester City but was at Bournemouth when the alleged racist abuse took place

He said witnesses saw Mogan throw chewing gum at Semenyo, hitting the right side of his shorts.

In a statement read to the court, Liverpool supporter Neil Sherratt, who sits next to Mogan at matches, said he saw him approach the pitch from the disabled section of the Main Stand.

Sherratt said he heard Mogan shout something along the lines of "hurry up" and saw Semenyo turn towards him.

He said the footballer had a shocked expression and he believed he replied: "you what".

Another Liverpool fan, Nicholas Collins, said Mogan became "very cocky and brazen" after the incident and threatened to assault police if they approached him.

Mogan, who had an intermediary with him in court, told police during an interview the following day that he had shouted at Semenyo to hurry up.

He claimed the footballer had called him "a cripple", which had upset him, and said he had thrown chewing gum on to the floor rather than at Semenyo.

Jones told the court the prosecution's case was that Mogan had lied during his police interview.

Semenyo, who was born in London to Ghanaian parents and has represented Ghana in the World Cup, is expected to give evidence over a videolink.

Mogan, of Templehill Close, Dovecot, denies racially aggravated behaviour.

The trial continues.
