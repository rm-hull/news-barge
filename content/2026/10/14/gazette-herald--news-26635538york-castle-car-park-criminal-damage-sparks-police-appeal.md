A CAR was damaged in a York city centre car park, leading to a police investigation into criminal damage.

The incident at Castle car park on September 19 at about 10.50p, and police have released a CCTV image of a man they believe could have key evidence related to the incident.

Police are urging anyone with information to come forward.
