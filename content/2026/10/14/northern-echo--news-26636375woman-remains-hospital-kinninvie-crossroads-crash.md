A woman remains in hospital after a serious crash at the notorious Kinninvie Crossroads.

Police, paramedics, and an air ambulance were called to the B6279 near Barnard Castle on Tuesday (October 13) following a crash.

A BMW and an Audi collided before one of the cars smashed into a house just before 9am.

The Audi driver, a woman in her 60s, suffered serious injuries and was airlifted to James Cook University Hospital, where she remains today (October 14).

The BMW driver, a 35-year-old man, was arrested at the scene on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

Durham Police has today (Wednesday, October 14) confirmed that he has since been released under investigation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21576084.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pictures show extensive damage to the corner of the house overlooking the crossroads. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

The road was closed for several hours following the crash yesterday, with pictures showing firefighters working in the middle of the road shortly after it took place.

Pictures showed extensive damage to one vehicle as emergency crews remained at the crossroads, as well as the damaged corner of a home.

The junction has been the site of several serious incidents in recent years.

In February 2023, a car ploughed through the wall of the same house, leaving a gaping hole and destroying the family’s dining room.

The crash prompted calls for improved safety measures, with Durham County Council later introducing stop signs, new road markings, high-friction surfacing, and more.
