Police investigating criminal damage in a York car park want to trace this man.

At around 10.50pm on Saturday 19 September a car was damaged in the Castle Car Park.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are particularly appealing for information about the male in the image who may have key evidence in relation to the incident.”
