A BRITISH popstar is set to visit East Yorkshire as part of his first headline tour in a decade.

Chesney Hawkes - who is best known for his hit single The One and Only - will play at Pocklington Arts Centre on Friday, April 16.

Dave Parker, venue manager at Pocklington Arts Centre, said: "It's fantastic to have an artist of Chesney Hawkes' calibre coming to Pocklington Arts Centre.

"His iconic songs have really stood the test of time and we're delighted to be able to bring such a well-loved name to Pocklington."

The singer has recently 're-ignited' his career with new singles including Get A Hold of Yourself, Live Forever, and Loud.

His latest album, Living Arrows, was released in April 2025 and is described as his most authentic work to date featuring his signature pop-rock sound while also exploring deeper themes such as mental health.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday (October 16) and can be purchased through the arts centre’s website at pocklingtonartscentre.co.uk
