A serious crash has occurred at a busy junction on a major Bradford road this afternoon.

Police have cordoned off a section of Manchester Road following the incident.

It has happened at the junction with Mayo Avenue.

The crash appears to have involved three vehicles.

![Photos live from the scene](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21581180.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The T&A is live on scene - here's some photos *(Image: T&A)*

A T&A reporter at the scene confirmed that three cars were damaged and the traffic lights are out.

It is understood the crash happened around 4pm.

Multiple police officers are at the scene.

![Police at the scene](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21581769.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police at the scene *(Image: Newsquest)*

![Photos live from the scene](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21581179.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some pretty bad wreckage on the road *(Image: T&A)*

## What police could say

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "This has been recorded as a damage only collision with no injuries reported.

"There is damage to a traffic light and officers have remained at the scene while the council attend."

A driver nearby told the T&A: "A guy in a tanker truck totally unconnected to it stopped and is helping brush wreckage off the road."

Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been contacted for a statement.
