## Ukrainian authorities say some of the Russian strikes targeted energy infrastructures, a few days after US President Donald Trump announced an "energy ceasefire".

At least one person was killed and more than 20 others injured in a Russian attack on Ukraine’s capital Kyiv overnight into Wednesday.

A large fire broke out in a residential building after it had come under Russian shelling.

The strikes targeted a key power plant in the city, according to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Zelenskyy noted that the Darnytska CHP-4 plant, which supplies the capital with heating and electricity, was targeted by a ballistic missile.

The attack comes just days after US President Donald Trump announced an energy ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine, halting attacks on energy targets.

Since then, the Kremlin’s nightly attacks have nevertheless continued to target energy sites, according to Zelenskyy.

## What 'energy ceasefire'?

The attacks also come at a dire time for Ukraine, currently grappling with a crisis which has seen more than 600,000 people lose power in various strikes on energy grids.

"Energy facilities in Kyiv and the surrounding region have come under a massive ballistic missile strike. There are power disruptions in the capital," Mayor Vitali Klitschko wrote on Telegram.

The Ukrainian President accused Moscow of disrespecting US President Donald Trump and his "energy ceasefire".

"The strike was entirely deliberate and demonstrative in its scale –- Russia has never before shown such ostentatious disrespect toward the United States", Zelenskyy wrote on X.

Meanwhile Russia said it had shot down 900 Ukrainian drones overnight, including 180 near Moscow.

Zelenskyy has previously noted that Ukraine is prepared to abide by Trump’s truce if Russia reciprocates, a plea the Kremlin did not acknowledge.
