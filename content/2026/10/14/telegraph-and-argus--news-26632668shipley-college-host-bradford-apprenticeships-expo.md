BUSINESSES are being invited to meet the next generation of talent at a Bradford Apprenticeships Expo.

The event, organised by Shipley College, will take place on Tuesday, November 10, 2026 at Victoria Hall in Saltaire.

It aims to connect employers across Bradford and West Yorkshire with prospective apprentices.

Martin Silverwood, principal and CEO of Shipley College, said: "Bradford has an enormous amount of talent and ambition, and we want to help local businesses tap into that potential.

"Apprenticeships offer employers a fantastic opportunity to bring fresh ideas into their organisations, develop the skills they need and invest in their future workforce.

"This Expo is about bringing employers and prospective apprentices together, making those first connections and opening doors to opportunities that benefit both businesses and young people."

The expo is open to businesses of all sizes and sectors, whether they currently employ apprentices or are considering it for the first time.

Employers can reserve free exhibition space, including tables for displays and promotional materials.

The event supports Shipley College's broader mission to help employers meet recruitment and skills needs through closer collaboration and apprenticeship programmes.
