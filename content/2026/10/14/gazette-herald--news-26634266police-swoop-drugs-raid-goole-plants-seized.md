POLICE have seized plants and equipment in a drugs raid at a property in an East Yorkshire town.

Officers from Humberside Police's neighbourhood and Operation Kinetic teams carried out a Misuse of Drugs Act warrant in Jefferson Street, Goole yesterday (Tuesday, October 13).

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21577306.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Humberside Police said all plants and equipment have been removed from the property *(Image: Humberside Police)*

A force spokesperson said: "After smashing through all the doors barricaded in a desperate attempt to keep us or anyone else out, all plants and equipment have been removed from the property.

"The investigation continues."
