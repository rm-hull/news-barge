# A629: Motorcyclist dies in crash at Low Bradley as driver arrested by police

## The rider of the motorcycle died at the scene despite the efforts of other motorists and paramedics

Police are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage following a fatal crash involving a red Honda motorcycle and a green Land Rover Defender on the A629 Keighley Road at Low Bradley.

The rider of the motorcycle, a 65-year-old man, died at the scene at around 12.30pm on Monday October 12 despite the efforts of other motorists and paramedics to save him.

His family have been informed and are receiving support, North Yorkshire Police said.

The driver of the Defender, a 43-year-old woman, was arrested in connection with the investigation.

She has been released under investigation while enquiries continue.

The collision occurred as both vehicles were heading south from Skipton towards Keighley.

The road remained closed until 8.40pm while police enquiries were completed at the scene and to allow for the safe recovery of both vehicles.

To support the investigation, police are appealing for witnesses or anyone with relevant dashcam footage to come forward.

This includes of the collision itself as well as either vehicle prior to the incident.
