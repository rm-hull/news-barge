Police are searching for a County Durham man who is wanted on prison recall.

Ellis Nino de Guzman, 30, has links to Chester le Street, Middlesbrough and Newcastle.

A spokesperson for Durham Constabulary has urged anyone who has seen him or knows where he is to contact police rather than commenting on social media.

The force said: “If you have seen him or know where he is, please don’t comment here.

“Instead, call us on 101, or contact us online at Durham Police's website.”
