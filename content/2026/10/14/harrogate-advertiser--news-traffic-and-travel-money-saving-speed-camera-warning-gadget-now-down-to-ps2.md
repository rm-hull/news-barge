## The gadget can save drivers money by reducing the risk of absent-mindedly picking up speeding tickets and fines

A clever gadget that alerts drivers to speed cameras and helps avoid speeding tickets has been reduced to under £27 and has been described as a “no-brainer” and “amazing”.

The price for a double pack of OOONO Co-driver No.1 devices has been reduced to £54.78 on Amazon, bringing the price of each one down to £27.39. For one-car households, a single device currently costs £29.85.

One driver who left a five-star review on Amazon called the device “amazing value” for motorists, saying: “Excellent product, works really well, detects a camera well in advance, allowing you to slow down before the camera if necessary.

“A noise sounds when it detects a camera. Very easy to link your phone to the device once you have downloaded the app.

“Once it’s linked, you stick the device to your dashboard and that’s it, you don’t even have to remember to turn it on, it will recognise your phone and beep to tell you it’s working, then as you drive the alarm will sound.

“Amazing value at £30, it could save you points on your licence and insurance hikes. It’s a no brainer.”
