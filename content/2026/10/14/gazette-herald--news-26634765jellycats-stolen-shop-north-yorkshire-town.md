Police have released CCTV of two people they want to speak to following the theft of Jellycat plush toys from a North Yorkshire town.

The toys were stolen from Hoppers in Market Place, Malton.

The incident took place on September 20.

RECOMMENDED READING:

A police spokesperson said: "We’ve issued this CCTV of two persons that we would like to speak to, following a theft in Malton.

"It happened on the 20/09/26, at Hoppers, Market Place and involved the theft of Jellycat plush toys."

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC1488 Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
