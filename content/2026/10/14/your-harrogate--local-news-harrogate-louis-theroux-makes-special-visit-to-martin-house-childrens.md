Filmmaker and broadcaster Louis Theroux has paid a special visit to Martin House children’s hospice, spending time with children and families supported by the charity.

During his visit, Louis found himself on the other side of the questions as seven-year-old Hooriya took on the role of interviewer.

Hooriya, who is supported by the hospice, put the documentary maker in the hot seat with a mix of questions.

A spokesperson for Martin House said:

> "Hooriya kept Louis on his toes with a brilliant mix of questions, and we knew we had to share the full conversation.
>
> “Thank you Louis for taking the time to visit us and spending time with families that we support - this is a moment we won’t be forgetting anytime soon.”
