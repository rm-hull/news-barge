# New season saving: Country pub in Harrogate gives guests a complimentary main course

## “For this week only this dog-friendly country pub’s owners are offering a complimentary main course with each main purchase”

The offer has now been extended of a complimentary main course at a historic Harrogate country pub which was, reputedly, the home of the legendary inventor of the world's largest telescope in the 19th century.

![The original building of the Pine Marten  foodies pub in Harrogate dates back to the 1820s.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/14/11/40/PMartenPub.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The original building of the Pine Marten dates back to the 1820s when it was, the story goes, the home of Robert Wigglesworth, the historic figure credited with creating the world's biggest telescope.

For this week only, there is something else worth seeing.

This dog-friendly country pub’s owners are offering a complimentary main course with each main purchased at this rustic country pub located Otley Road in Harrogate with spacious; bar, lounge, dining, and outdoor areas.

Now available until Friday, October 23, the extended deal is redeemable across any pub within the Premium Country Pub Collection, when you dine in or order via the Click & Collect service.

Still set in a historic listed building, recent decades have seen the Pine Marten become the ideal place to relax, indulge, and enjoy al-fresco dining in exceptional surroundings.

In 2021, it closed temporarily for a major redevelopment, reopening four weeks later with updated interiors, a refreshed menu, and an open-plan lounge and bar area.

Owners Premium Country Pubs continue to make improvements, launching a brand new cocktail collection in the summer.

This real foodie country pub with an impressive beer garden, also boasts an all-encompassing menu has been designed to accommodate even the fussiest of tastebuds with a range of different dishes from international small plates to comfort food classics.
