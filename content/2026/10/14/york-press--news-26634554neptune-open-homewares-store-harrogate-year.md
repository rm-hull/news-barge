An upmarket homewares store is to open in Harrogate later this year.

Neptune will be opening in Princes Street, where ProCook used to be.

The retailer of homeware, furniture and kitchen items promises good design, exceptional craftsmanship, using natural materials.

Its products also come with a lifetime guarantee.

The company has posted online: "Work is well underway on our new Neptune Harrogate store, and we’re looking forward to opening our doors later this year."

Neptune has more than 30 stores across the UK and Europe.

These include Malton Road, York, on the A64 in the former Tanglewood public house.
