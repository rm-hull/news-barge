Big news for those with a passion for homes, interiors and artisan crafts – a glorious Yorkshire showcase is coming your way.

Yorkshire Life Home & Gift Show launches at the Great Yorkshire Events Centre, Harrogate in 2027.

This new premium lifestyle event is all about celebrating beautiful homes, gardens, artisans and exceptional food and drink producers.

![Cumbria Life Home and Gift Fair at Keswick Pencil Factory](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21579252.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nicola Brass from Whitemore and Thwaytes at the Cumbria Life event *(Image: Jonathan Becker)*

The two-day event promises indulgence for home lovers and those who relish the chance to support Yorkshire’s craftspeople and makers.

Yorkshire Life’s event follows the successful launch of the Cumbria Life Home & Gift Show and the sell-out debut of the Derbyshire Life Show.

Taking place on Saturday, July 31 and Sunday, August 1, 2027, the show will bring together an inspiring collection of carefully selected businesses, makers, designers and producers for a two-day celebration of style, craftsmanship and regional creativity.

The Cumbria show welcomed more than 145 traders and over 7,500 visitors in its inaugural year.

Meanwhile, the first Derbyshire Life Show quickly sold out of exhibitor space, underlining the enthusiasm for high-quality, lifestyle-led events linked to our Life magazines.

Set within one of Harrogate’s most prestigious venues and the home of the world-renowned Great Yorkshire Show, the Yorkshire Life Home & Gift Show will offer a superb setting for visitors seeking ideas, inspiration and distinctive products for their homes, gardens and lives.

The Homes & Interiors Section will create an inspiring destination for visitors with a passion for design, craftsmanship and beautiful living spaces.

Exhibitors will include interior designers, architects and specialists in kitchens, bathrooms and bedrooms, alongside makers of handmade furniture, fine décor, soft furnishings, designer lighting and more.

The Gardens, Outdoor Living & Leisure Area is perfect for those who love spending time outside.

It will showcase stylish garden furniture, outdoor accessories, plants, landscaping services, garden buildings and unique features designed to help visitors make the most of their outdoor space.

Handmade & Heartfelt Treasures celebrates the best of British creativity and independent craftsmanship.

Visitors can browse hand-poured candles, ceramic stoneware, jewellery, natural skincare, heritage keepsakes, eco-luxe products, knitwear, original artwork and thoughtfully made gifts.

A Food Lover’s Paradise invites visitors to meet passionate producers and sample and take home new favourites.

The area will feature small-batch spirits, local beers, award-winning cheeses, artisan sourdough, chutneys, sauces, luxury hampers, brownies, cakes and other irresistible treats.

INVITATION TO EXHIBITORS

The Yorkshire Life Home & Gift Show offers businesses the opportunity to meet new customers, engage with genuine buyers and build their brand in one of Yorkshire’s most important retail, tourism and lifestyle destinations.

TRUSTED BRAND, WIDER REACH

Yorkshire Life is part of the prestigious Life magazine portfolio, published across 17 UK regions and backed by Newsquest Media Group, the UK’s second-largest local media company.

That association gives traders the reassurance of an established, trusted editorial environment and the potential for wide multi-platform exposure.

Across the wider portfolio, the network delivers 68.5 million online unique browsers, 257 million page views, 11.4 million social media followers and 2.3 million print readers.

Yorkshire Life Home & Gift Show will give traders direct access to a highly engaged Yorkshire audience, particularly well-suited to premium home, lifestyle, leisure and hospitality retail traders.

With its strong editorial heritage, influential audience and premium Harrogate location, the Yorkshire Life Home & Gift Show is set to become a major date in the North’s lifestyle calendar.

Exhibitor applications are now OPEN!

We are inviting businesses offering exceptional home, interiors, garden, gift, food and drink products.
