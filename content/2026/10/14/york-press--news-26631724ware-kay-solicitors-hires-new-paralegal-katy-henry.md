A Yorkshire law firm has strengthened its team with the appointment of a new paralegal.

Ware & Kay Solicitors has welcomed Katy Henry to its litigation department, where she will support clients throughout the litigation process, including preparing court applications, managing cases and liaising with clients, courts and barristers.

Ms Henry said: "I am pleased to be joining the Litigation Department and taking the next step in my legal career.

"My previous experience has given me a strong foundation in working with clients, managing cases and dealing with court proceedings, and I am looking forward to developing those skills further within Litigation."

She joins the team with experience in family law and a strong academic background, having graduated from York St John University with a 2:1 in law before completing an LLM Legal Practice (LPC) with merit.

David Hyams, managing director, said: "We are delighted to welcome Katy to the Litigation team."
