A fire which tore through terraced homes in Newton Aycliffe leaving two women in hospital is being treated as arson.

Emergency services were called to a house in Greenlee Garth just after 1am on Wednesday, October 14.

The two women inside managed to escape with minor injuries and were taken to Darlington Memorial Hospital, where they remain.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21578108.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Neighbouring houses were also damaged and had to be evacuated, although the majority of residents have since been able to return home.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: "An investigation was immediately launched and officers remain at the scene today as enquiries continue."

Pictures show the homes badly damaged with windows charred and the rooves caved in.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21578219.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*
