These are three of the cheapest houses for sale in the North East this week, and they are all heading to online auction.

Located in County Durham and Hartlepool, the properties are available for £10,000 or less.

The first property is 44 North Road West in Wingate, listed by Under The Hammer with a guide price of just £9,000.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21571332.jpg?type=mds-article-620)44 North Road West, Wingate *(Image: Under The Hammer)*

The property is a 3-bedroom mid terrace house situated on North Road West in the County Durham village of Wingate.

The property requires full refurbishment, offering an opportunity for investors and developers.

The ground floor includes a living room, dining area, and kitchen, with three bedrooms and a bathroom on the first floor.

A rear yard is also included.

The house will head to online auction on Wednesday, October 21.

The second property is 12 Belk Street, Hartlepool, also listed by Under The Hammer at a guide price of £9,000.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21571325.jpg?type=mds-article-620)12 Belk Street, Hartlepool *(Image: Under The Hammer)*

It is a freehold mid-terrace house arranged over three floors, including a room in the roof space.

Belk Street lies just off Raby Road, around 250 metres from Morrisons.

The third property is 13 Lake View, Station Town, Wingate, listed with McHugh & Co at a guide price of £10,000.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21571326.jpg?type=mds-article-620)13 Lake View, Station Town, Wingate *(Image: McHugh & Co)*

This two-bedroom terrace house requires modernisation and is arranged over two floors.

The ground floor includes an entrance porch, living room, kitchen, and bathroom, while the first floor offers two bedrooms.

The property also has a rear yard.

It will also be sold by online auction on Wednesday, October 21.
