A five-bedroom barn conversion with valley views is for sale in West Yorkshire.

The detached property on Addersgate Lane in Northowram is listed at £735,000.

It is situated in the Shibden Valley, near Halifax.

The listing describes far-reaching views across the surrounding Yorkshire countryside.

![The property](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21561699.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The property *(Image: Hamilton Bower via Zoopla)*

Accommodation is arranged across a split-level ground floor and two further floors.

There are five bedrooms, two bathrooms and two reception rooms.

Four of the bedrooms are described as double rooms.

The principal bedroom has a private en-suite bathroom.

A first reception room includes an impressive fireplace and wood flooring.

It also has views across the Shibden Valley.

The second reception room is open plan and overlooks the garden.

Together, the rooms provide separate areas for sitting, entertaining and family activities.

A contemporary dining kitchen forms the main part of the living accommodation.

![The property](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21561700.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The property *(Image: Hamilton Bower via Zoopla)*

It has high-gloss dove-grey cabinets, granite work surfaces and Yorkshire stone window sills.

A breakfast bar is positioned within the kitchen.

The room also includes a Falcon five-ring range.

Integrated appliances include a microwave oven, dishwasher, fridge and freezer.

A utility room has matching cabinets, a sink and plumbing for a washing machine.

Modern flooring runs between the kitchen and utility room.

A door from the kitchen opens directly into the garden.

This provides access between the indoor and outdoor areas.

Outside, the property has off-road parking and maintained gardens.

The listing says the gardens include areas for outdoor dining and children’s play.

Views of the valley form a backdrop beyond the garden.

A substantial outbuilding is currently used as a home office.

![Pretty views!](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21561737.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pretty views! *(Image: Hamilton Bower via Zoopla)*

It could also be used as a studio, hobby room or storage area, subject to consents.

The property retains features associated with its former barn use.

These are combined with updated fittings intended for modern family living.

The listing describes the home as private while remaining close to local amenities.

Rural walks, green spaces and countryside are available around the property.

The house is presented as suitable for families or people working from home.

The two bathrooms provide facilities across the sleeping accommodation.

The home is being marketed as a detached property.

Its address is Addersgate Lane, Northowram, Halifax, HX3.

The property combines a rural location with five bedrooms and working-from-home space.

The listing says an early inspection is recommended to assess the property and its setting.
