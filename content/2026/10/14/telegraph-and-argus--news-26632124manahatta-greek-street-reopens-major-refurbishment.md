A bar has unveiled a brand new look in time for its 10th anniversary.

Manahatta, located on Greek Street in Leeds, has reopened with a bold refurbishment inspired by New York’s energy and streetscape.

The redesign includes a more open layout, refreshed interiors, and a new bar area, with a colour scheme of green, red brick, and earthy tones.

Martin Wolstencroft, co-founder and CEO of Arc Inspirations, said: "Greek Street is now our longest-running Manahatta site and not only did we want to celebrate its 10th anniversary with a bit of a makeover, we wanted to give the people of Leeds and beyond new reasons to visit.

"As well as the new look, we’ve introduced a Manahatta Meal Deal, after work Happy Hour, new cocktails and food items on the main menu too."

Mood lighting and diner-style seating have been added to create an inviting atmosphere for both daytime and late-night guests.
