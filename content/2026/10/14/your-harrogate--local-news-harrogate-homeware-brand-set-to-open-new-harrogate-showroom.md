British furniture and interiors brand Neptune is set to open a new showroom in Harrogate.

The company is restoring a terraced building on Princes Street, with furniture, home accessories and design services to be offered across several floors.

The new premises will combine a showroom with a design studio, helping customers plan kitchens and other rooms in their homes.

Announcing the plans on social media, a spokesperson for Neptune said:

> "Set within a characterful terrace on Princes Street, this beautiful building will be thoughtfully restored to create an inspiring destination.
>
> "Across several floors, you will be able to discover the full Neptune experience, from considered furniture and timeless accessories to our expert design services and our team who will bring it all to life.
>
> "“We can’t wait to open the doors and welcome you into our new Neptune home, watch this space!”

Recruitment is underway for the Harrogate team, with advertised roles including a Design Lead, Designer and Client Experience Lead.

The company’s designer role includes consultations in the showroom and home visits, alongside preparing room layouts, mood boards and design proposals.

Founded in 1996 by friends John Sims-Hilditch and Giles Redman, Neptune sells kitchens, furniture, lighting and home accessories.

The brand already has a Yorkshire showroom on Malton Road in York.

An exact opening date for the Harrogate store has yet to be announced.
