A law firm has reported an eight per cent rise in turnover.

Schofield Sweeney, which has offices in Bradford and Leeds, saw revenue reach £17.2 million in the year to March 31, up from £15.9 million the previous year.

Profits before members’ remuneration and profit shares also increased to £6.85 million.

Graham Sweeney, managing partner at Schofield Sweeney, said: "The last year was a success story for us with really strong results across the board, and specific areas of growth across our corporate, employment and litigation teams.

"This was driven by several really key appointments, to already excellent team, which introduced increased expertise and enabled us to enhance our offering to clients."

He also highlighted continued momentum into the current financial year.

Mr Sweeney said: "It is also pleasing to see that our growth is continuing in the new financial year, with August—usually expected to be one of the quieter months—actually reporting as one of the busiest this year so far."
