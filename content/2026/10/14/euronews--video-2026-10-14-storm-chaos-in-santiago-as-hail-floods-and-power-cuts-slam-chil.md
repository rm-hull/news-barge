Video recorded by local resident **Jennifer Vidal** shows**hail-covered roads and flooded streets**, while separate footage captures water spreading across the roadway as people shelter in a nearby building entrance.

Flooding hit several districts, including **Providencia, Las Condes and Estación Central**. Water entered residential buildings, municipal offices and the underground car park of the Alto Las Condes shopping centre.

The storm also disrupted **Santiago’s Metro**, forcing the closure of** Pedro de Valdivia station** and restricting access at Salvador, while Line 2 operated in separate sections.

Firefighters carried out rescues at a **flooded underpass in Peñalolén**, as authorities warned of further thunderstorms and urged drivers to avoid low-lying roads.
