POLICE are hunting for a man wanted for recall to prison after allegedly failing to comply with a sexual offence order.

Extensive enquiries remain ongoing to locate Ryan Richards, 24, who is originally from York.

Richards is also believed to have links to Selby, Goole and Hull.




He is also wanted in connection with a reported assault.

Humberside Police said: "We are do everything we can to find him including arrest attempts which have so far been unsuccessful.

"If you have seen Ryan, or have any information about his whereabouts, please contact us on our non-emergency number 101, quoting crime reference 26\*104002.

"Alternatively, if you wish to report anonymously, you can also contact the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111."
