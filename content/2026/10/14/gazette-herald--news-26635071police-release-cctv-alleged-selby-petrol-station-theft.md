A MAN allegedly asked for a substantial sum to be added to a top-up card at a petrol station and then left without paying.

Police have now released a CCTV image following the alleged theft from the Jet Fuel station on Bawtry Road in the Selby area, which happened at about 8.45pm on September 15.

Authorities are urging anyone who recognises the man in the image to come forward, as he may have information that could aid the investigation.

**Recommended reads:**

People can contact the police by emailing [Molly.Grace@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Molly.Grace@northyorkshire.police.uk), visiting the North Yorkshire Police website and selecting 'Tell us about' followed by 'An existing case or report', or via live chat on the website from Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Alternatively, information can be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260181741 when providing information.
