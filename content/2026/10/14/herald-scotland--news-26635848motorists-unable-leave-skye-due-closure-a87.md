Motorists are currently unable to leave the Isle of Skye following an accident.

Traffic Scotland took to X to warn drivers that the A87 is currently closed at Sconser in both directions as a result of a collision.

Police are in attendance at the scene to direct traffic.

Online reports indicate that a lorry has blown over due to high winds, with the road closure meaning that there is "no way off Skye at the moment".

A weather warning for strong winds covering parts of north-west Scotland, including Skye, is currently in place until 7pm on Wednesday evening.

The Met Office forecast strong south to south westerly winds with gusts of between 50-60mph expected widely, with some exposed coastal and hilly areas also potentially seeing gusts of around 70mph.

Sailings on a number of CalMac ferry routes have been cancelled or disrupted on Wednesday as a result of the weather, with some disruption continuing into Thursday.
