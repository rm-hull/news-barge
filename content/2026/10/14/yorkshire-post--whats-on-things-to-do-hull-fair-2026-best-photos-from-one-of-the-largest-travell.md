Hull Fair has more than 250 rides and attractions for visitors to enjoy, ranging from roller coasters and thrill rides to classic carousels and family-friendly games.

There are also a variety of food stalls offering the fair classics such as candy floss, toffee apples, patties and fresh doughnuts.
