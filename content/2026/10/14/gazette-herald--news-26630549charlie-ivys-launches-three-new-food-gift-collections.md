A Yorkshire food brand has unveiled three new themed gift collections.

Charlie & Ivy’s, the North Yorkshire family business, has launched the boxes in time for the festive season, each inspired by popular food trends.

Jason Thompson, new product development manager at Charlie & Ivy’s, said: "We’re very excited to launch these gift collections, which bring together a number of our customer favourites.

"The Pizza Pairings Collection is an ideal gift for foodies and pizza fans, the Fire & Feast Barbecue Collection is a delicious selection of barbecue essentials for outdoor feasts, and the Chilli Lovers Collection is a fiery selection of chilli treats for true spice lovers.

"All are presented in eco-friendly gift boxes."

The Pizza Pairings Collection features Chipotle Chilli Mayonnaise, Smoked Garlic Mayonnaise, Smoked Chilli Dressing, and a new Smoky Harissa Drizzle Oil.

Each box is priced at £24 per pack and is available from selected independent retailers nationwide and online at charlieandivys.co.uk.
