## Have a look inside this period home for sale in a popular York area

The three-bedroom house for sale in Murray Street, off Acomb Road, for £450,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

A period home has hit the market in a popular York area.

The three-bedroom house is for sale in Murray Street, off Acomb Road, for £450,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it is a “beautifully presented Victorian home, ideally positioned in the popular Holgate area”.

The estate agent said the property has been “thoughtfully modernised and extended by the current sellers”.

Inside, an entrance hall leads to an open-plan lounge/diner area.
