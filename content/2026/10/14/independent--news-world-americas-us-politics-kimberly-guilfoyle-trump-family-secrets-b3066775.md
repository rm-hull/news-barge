Kimberly Guilfoyle, the recently ousted U.S. Ambassador to Greece, has Trump insiders worried that she could spill the secrets of the first family because she allegedly keeps a “burn book” filled with juicy details, according to a report.

Guilfoyle resigned Tuesday after being at the center of a scandal accusing her of asking a MAGA donor to pay off a $100,000 credit card bill days before her Senate confirmation hearing, followed by other salacious allegations surrounding her inappropriate conduct at the helm of the U.S. Greek embassy.

The 57-year-old ex-girlfriend of Donald Trump Jr., has denied the allegations and said she would “always be a loyal supporter” of the president. “I intend to vigorously defend my name, my reputation, and my integrity,” she said in a statement Tuesday.

Since her ouster from the Trump administration, insiders are reportedly concerned about Guilfoyle’s next move. Her six-year relationship with Trump’s son made her privy to private conversations of key Trumpworld figures, according to the *Daily Mail.* The couple split up in late 2024, around the same time the elder Trump tapped her to be his Greece ambassador, and Don Jr. has since remarried Palm Beach socialite Bettina Anderson.

One anonymous source told the newspaper that Trump allies were worried she could release the explosive contents of a diary now that she has “nothing to lose.”

![Kimberly Guilfoyle’s proximity to the first family over the years has Trump insiders worried after her ouster, according to a report. She was previously engaged to Don Jr and the couple dated for six years before they split in late 2024](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/17/11/2161997603...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Having her back in America would be a nightmare. Kimberly knows where the bodies are buried,” the source said. “She knows the people, the secrets, the fights, the relationships.”

Another insider told the *Mail* that “Kimberly knows everything” and was “way inside that family and political operation for years.”

Trump distanced Guilfoyle from his family Tuesday when speaking to reporters. “No, she wasn’t a member of my family,” he said. “She, I guess, dated my son a long time ago.”

*The Independent* has contacted the White House for comment.

Before her dismissal, Guilfoyle had been concerned about her job for weeks, the *Wall Street Journal* reported Tuesday, citing people familiar with the matter. Some of Trump’s advisers were working to save her job before she was asked to resign by the administration over fears of what she would reveal outside the family’s orbit, according to the*Mail*.

Her troubles began when the *Journal* reported late last month that the U.S. ambassador had been promoting a Greek construction firm, where one of her advisers served as a lobbyist. After the story was published, Guilfoyle started calling influential Trump supporters and officials, asking them to back her up.

![One anonymous source told the newspaper that Trump allies were worried Guilfoyle could release the explosive contents of a diary now that she has ‘nothing to lose’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/14/15/1304267793...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Then, on Oct. 7, a second story reported that the ambassador had asked Eric Deters, a former Trump donor, to pay off her $100,000 American Express bill in the lead-up to her September 2025 Senate confirmation. “Honey please I need you to get this done for me today,” she reportedly texted Eric Deters, asking him to wire the money in a way that would not leave a paper trail.

The president's aides talked about cutting Guilfoyle loose when Secretary of State Marco Rubio was in Greece between October 6 and 8, the *Journal* reported. However, the secretary didn’t want to upstage his speech on western civilization, which he delivered from the Acropolis in Athens. At the time, Rubio defended Guilfoyle, telling reporters: “You guys walk around thinking that because some source told you something and you reported it, it’s the gospel. It’s not the gospel.”

Meanwhile, Trump had grown irritated at the shocking allegations against Guilfoyle, and he was bothered that she appeared to be profiting off his presidency.

Trump’s new White House press secretary Katie Zacharia announced that the president had asked the ambassador to resign at her first briefing.

Guilfoyle paid tribute to the president in her resignation statement and said her “gratitude and respect” for him and Rubio was “unwavering.”

“I will always be a loyal supporter of President Trump and his mission to Make America Great Again,” she said.
