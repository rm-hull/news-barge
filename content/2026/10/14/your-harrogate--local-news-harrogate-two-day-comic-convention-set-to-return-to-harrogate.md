Thousands of comic fans and cosplayers are expected in Harrogate next month as Thought Bubble returns to the town.

The comic convention will take place at Harrogate Convention Centre on Saturday 14th and Sunday 15th November, welcoming more than 600 exhibitors.

Now in its 20th edition, Thought Bubble is a week-long celebration of comics, with events across Yorkshire culminating in the two-day Harrogate convention.

Visitors will be able to browse and buy work from comic creators, meet international special guests and enjoy talks, exhibitions, workshops and signings.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6acf8b6f2b08a1308f3.png)

A dedicated children’s zone will also offer free activities and workshops, alongside exhibitors catering for families.

The event welcomes both established comic fans and those discovering graphic novels and comic art for the first time.

With one month to go, a spokesperson for Harrogate Convention Centre said:

> “We look forward to welcoming 600+ exhibitors and thousands of comic fans and cosplayers of all ages to our venue.”

*Ticket information is available through the Harrogate Convention Centre**website**.*
