The rail industry will continue its long-standing support for the Armed Forces community in Yorkshire.

Free travel will be offered to serving military personnel, veterans and bereaved families across Great Britain to help them attend Services of Remembrance this November.

The scheme covers travel to the national Service of Remembrance at the Cenotaph in London, as well as other services across the country.

Ola Ogun, co-CEO of Rail Delivery Group, said: "Remembrance gives us a chance to honour those who served and to stand alongside the people who remember them.

"The railway is proud to help serving personnel and veterans attend services across the country.

"Extending free travel to bereaved next of kin this year will help more families be there to pay tribute to their loved ones."

This year, the offer has been extended to include one nominated next of kin of a service person who died while serving in the Armed Forces.

Eligible family members with a Bereaved Travel for Remembrance 2026 complimentary ticket can travel on the same dates to attend a Remembrance service.

The scheme also supports children and young people helped by Scotty's Little Soldiers, who have lost a parent who served in the British Armed Forces.
