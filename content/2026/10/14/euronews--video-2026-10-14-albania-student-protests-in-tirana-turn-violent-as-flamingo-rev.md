Hundreds gathered outside the **Education Ministry** to demand better**university conditions**, increased financial support and improved job prospects. The protests escalated as demonstrators** threw stones, smashed shop windows and damaged businesses**, while police responded with** tear gas**and** water cannon**.

According to a police update on Wednesday, **16 people were arrested** and**49 others faced criminal proceedings**. Six police officers and at least one journalist were injured.

The student-led demonstrations have joined a wider anti-government movement known as the **Flamingo Revolution**, which is demanding Prime Minister** Edi Rama's resignation**over allegations of** corruption**. Protesters also oppose a** luxury coastal development**linked to** Jared Kushner**, US President Donald Trump's son-in-law, citing concerns about its** environmental impact**. The movement takes its name from the pink flamingos that campaigners say could be threatened by the project. The unrest has added to mounting political pressure on Rama's government.
