The chief executive of the Yorkshire Agricultural Society has stepped down from his role due to health issues.

Allister Nixon, CEO of the YAS, announced his departure after four years in the role.

During his tenure, he led the rollout of the society’s values, a rebrand, and the refurbishment of Fodder.

Mr Nixon said: "My recent health issues have given me cause and opportunity for much reflection, and I have decided that this is the right time for me to move on to my next chapter.

"It has been a real privilege to lead this incredible organisation and work alongside such a dedicated team.

"We do amazing things here, and I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together during my tenure.

"I wish the organisation and everyone in it continued success."

Paul Russell, chair of the board of trustees, said: "We would like to thank Allister for his commitment over the last four years and wish him well with his recovery and next chapter.

"The board is confident that David and Graham, supported by trustees and the SMT, will lead the charity successfully as the team prepares for a busy Christmas season ahead."
