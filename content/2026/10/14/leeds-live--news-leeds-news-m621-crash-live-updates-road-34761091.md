An overturned car is causing delays on a major road in Leeds this morning (Wednesday, October 14)

The M621 westbound has been shut from Junction 5 to Junction 4 with congestion backing up to Junction 43 for the M1.

INRIX says: "M621 Westbound closed long delays, queueing traffic for two miles due to overturned car and recovery work from J5 Tunstall Road to J4 A61 (Hunslet Distributor). Congestion to M1 J43."
