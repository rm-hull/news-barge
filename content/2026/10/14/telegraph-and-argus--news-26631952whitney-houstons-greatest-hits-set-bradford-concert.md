A Whitney Houston tribute show is set to return.

Queen of The Night - A Tribute to Whitney Houston will be at Bradford's St George's Hall this Saturday, October 17, 2026.

The show, which has been described as the UK's biggest Whitney Houston tribute, will celebrate the music and legacy of the late singer.

The production will feature vocalists and a live band performing hits such as I Wanna Dance With Somebody, One Moment In Time, I Will Always Love You, and many more.

The show will start at 7.30pm.

A spokesperson for the show said: "Join us for an unmissable evening filled with live music, captivating choreography, and powerful vocals, as we pay homage to the one and only queen of the night."

Tickets for the show are priced between £22.50 and £32.50 and can be purchased HERE.

The show is suitable for people aged 14 and over.
