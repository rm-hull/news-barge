## Scarborough Spa announces festive Christmas party nights this December

Scarborough Spa is hosting its 'Bows & Baubles' Christmas Party Nights this December *(Image: Supplied)*

Scarborough Spa is bringing festive cheer back to the coast.

The venue will host its 'Bows & Baubles' Christmas Party Nights on December 18 and 19, promising an evening of festive food and music.

Guests will be greeted with a glass of bubbly before sitting down to a three-course meal, all set in the popular surroundings of Scarborough Spa.

A spokesperson for Scarborough Spa said: "Our Christmas Party Nights have become a festive tradition for many local businesses, groups and friends.

"'Bows & Baubles' is all about bringing people together, creating special memories and celebrating the season in a fun and festive atmosphere.

"We can't wait to welcome guests for what promises to be two fantastic nights."

The evenings are designed for workplace gatherings, friends, and families to enjoy together.

After dinner, the party will continue with a lively dance floor and classic party tunes to keep guests entertained.

Organisers recommend booking early to avoid disappointment.

The event offers a complete festive experience under one roof, blending dining, entertainment, and seasonal spirit in a landmark setting.

Scarborough Spa, known for its year-round events and hospitality, has made its Christmas nights a staple of the local festive calendar.

Whether it’s for an office party, a night out with friends, or a family celebration, the 'Bows & Baubles' nights promise a festive start to the holiday season.

Tickets are available from the Scarborough Spa Box Office, via the official website at www.scarboroughspa.co.uk, or by calling 01723 376774.
