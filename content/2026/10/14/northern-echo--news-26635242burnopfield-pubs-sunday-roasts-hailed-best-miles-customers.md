A North East pub's roasts are drawing diners from across the region with their towering Sunday dinners.

The Owl and Otter, a gastropub off the A692 near Burnopfield, is a favourite among local Sunday roast fanatics.

Recently nominated for the North East's Best Sunday Dinner 2026, its Sunday roasts have gained a strong following, with many customers describing them as the best they have ever eaten.

The business announced its nomination on Facebook, saying it was “so excited,” before adding: "Thank you, as always, for your incredible support- it means the world to us! ”

Customers have travelled from across the North East to sample the Owl and Otter's most popular dishes, with many praising the food's quality, portion sizes and presentation.

One diner reacted to the nomination on social media, writing: “Been to the Owl and Otter a few times for Sunday lunch; this place will take some beating.”

Another customer said: “The best Sunday lunch by miles.”

Newcastle-based food reviewer Geordie Food Gals also visited and praised the tender beef and large portions. She said: “We can't wait to come back already!”

One customer described the meal as “an absolute bargain for what you're getting.”

A Tripadvisor review said: “Sunday lunch was fantastic. Called on chance after seeing reviews and so glad we did.

"Staff were attentive and very helpful. Seated quickly and served with good timing. Sunday beef and chicken very good and plenty of food.”

Another diner said: “ Brilliant roast this weekend. They were all huge.

"So big we had to take home in a takeaway box.”

The roast chicken and nut roast will set you back £17.95. The roast topside beef and roast topside pork are priced at £18.95, and the braised beef featherblade is £19.95.

The pub's new Christmas menu has added roast turkey to its list of meat options, joining chicken, beef, pork and nut roast for vegetarians.

The Owl and Otter also has a gluten-free Sunday menu featuring gluten-free Yorkshire puddings.

Customers have described the turkey as “gorgeous” and “beautiful.”

The pub advises customers to book ahead for Sunday visits, as the restaurant can become very busy. Bookings can be made on the Owl and Otter's website.
