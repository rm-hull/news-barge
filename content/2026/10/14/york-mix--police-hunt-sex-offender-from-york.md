Have you seen this man?

He is Ryan Richards and he’s wanted by police.

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “Officers are keen to locate him as he is wanted for recall to prison in connection with a reported assault and failure to comply with a sexual offence order.

“Extensive enquiries remain ongoing to locate Ryan, and we are do everything we can to find him including arrest attempts which have so far been unsuccessful.”

It is believed Ryan is originally from York. He’s also thought to have links to Hull, Goole and Selby.

If you have seen Ryan, or have any information about where he is, you can contact police on 101 quoting crime reference 26\*104002.

Alternatively, if you wish to report anonymously, you can also contact the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
