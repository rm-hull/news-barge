A man is fighting for his life following a three-vehicle crash which closed part of a major road.

The collision happened on Otley Road in Guiseley between the junction with the Bradford Road/Thorpe Lane and Ings Lane at around 11.20am this morning.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 11.20am officers were called to Otley Road, Guiseley to reports three vehicles were in collision.

"A man from one of the vehicles has been taken to hospital, where he remains in a critical condition.

"The road is closed whilst enquiries are carried out."

The A65 was closed in both directions at around 11.24am, with significant congestion building in the area as emergency services responded to the incident.

Bus services have also been affected by the closure.

A spokesperson for Metro said: "Services 33 and 34 are diverting from Aireborough Leisure Centre roundabout, Park Road, Bradford Road in both directions."

Motorists are being advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes while police carry out their investigation and recovery work.
