Bank customers have named the UK’s best and worst-rated branches, with more than one in five receiving fewer than three stars in a new analysis of Google reviews.

The ranking examined 849 bank and building society branches across the UK, covering 168,345 Google reviews.

And the most common complaint among customers leaving negative reviews was one that many people will recognise: queues and waiting times.

## Which are the UK's best bank branches?

Nationwide’s branch on South Coast Road in Peacehaven, East Sussex, came out on top with an almost perfect rating of 4.98 stars out of five.

At the other end of the table, Virgin Money’s branch on Northumberland Street in Newcastle received just 1.89 stars from 55 reviews, making it the lowest-rated branch in the country.

The research found that 187 of the 849 branches included in the analysis averaged fewer than three stars – more than one in five.

## Nationwide among best-rated bank branches

Nationwide dominates the top of the ranking, with three of the top eight branches belonging to the building society.

Its Peacehaven branch received a 4.98-star average from 118 reviews, including 117 five-star reviews and no one-star reviews.

Nationwide branches in Tooting High Street, London, and Upminster, also in London, came joint second with ratings of 4.97 stars.

Mandy Beech, Nationwide’s Group Director of Retail Services, said: “Nationwide prides itself on the service we give our customers, and was named as the UK’s top brand for customer satisfaction this year.

"Unlike the big banks, we have not closed significant numbers of branches as we recognise the need to give customers real choice in how they bank.

"Today, we have the biggest branch network in the UK and have promised to keep every single one of our 696 branches open until at least the start of 2030.”

Barclays also featured heavily among the highest-rated branches, including locations in Redhill, Kidderminster, Newport and Dudley.

A Barclays spokesperson said: “We’re pleased to see so many Barclays branches recognised positively by our customers. These reviews reflect the dedication of our colleagues and our commitment to investing in communities across the UK to ensure customers can reach our people when it matters most.

"Our ambition is to give customers the best of both worlds: leading digital services for day-to-day banking and access to in-person expert support for more complex needs.”

The top 25 included branches with ratings ranging from 4.84 to 4.98 stars.

The analysis calculated each branch’s exact average from its individual one, two, three, four and five-star reviews, rather than relying on Google’s rounded score. Where branches tied, the one with the greater proportion of one-star reviews ranked lower.

## The worst-rated bank branch in the UK

Virgin Money’s branch at 132-134 Northumberland Street in Newcastle came bottom of the national ranking, with an average of just 1.89 stars from 55 reviews.

It was followed by Santander branches in London, Nottingham and Edinburgh, which also featured among the lowest-rated locations.

Santander had the lowest average rating of the nine banks and building societies with at least 20 branches in the ranking, at 2.93 stars. The figures suggest that customer experiences vary considerably between branches, even within the same banking group.

However, the results reflect Google reviews rather than an independent assessment of service quality, and the analysis only included branches with at least 50 reviews.

A Santander UK spokesperson said: “We are committed to providing a good experience for our customers whenever they visit one of our branches.

“While online review sites reflect the views of individual customers, they are based on a relatively small number of reviews and may not be representative of the experience of the millions of customers who use our services each year.

“We continue to invest in our branch network and remain focused on providing customers with the support they need, whether in person, over the phone or through our digital services.”

## Queues are the biggest complaint from bank customers

Waiting times emerged as the most common grievance among customers posting negative reviews.

Researchers examined 388 one- and two-star reviews written since January 2024 across 30 of the lowest-rated branches.

The analysis found:

* 43% mentioned queues or waiting times.
* 25% complained about rude or unhelpful staff.
* 21% said they had been sent online, to an app or to the telephone.
* 17% mentioned machines that were not working.
* 12% complained about too few staff or closed counters.

Reviews could mention more than one issue, so the percentages do not add up to 100%.

The findings highlight the frustrations some customers experience when they need face-to-face help with their money, particularly when a visit ends with a long wait or an instruction to use digital banking instead.

Dawn Yates, a financial assistance expert at SingleMothersGrants.org, said: “People who still go into a branch are usually there because they can’t do it another way, whether that’s paying in cash, sorting out a card that’s stopped working or dealing with something after a death in the family.

"Waiting 45 minutes and then being told to go online is hard on anyone, and harder with children in tow."

## How the bank branch rankings were compiled

The researchers looked at Google Maps listings for UK bank and building society branches on 8 October 2026 - finding 849 branches that were open and had at least 50 Google reviews, covering 168,345 reviews in total.

Cash machines, head offices, business and private banking centres, mortgage offices, mobile branches, banking hubs and Post Office counters were excluded. Branches shown as closed on Google were also left out, alongside seven branches that had recently closed or were due to close by the end of November.

For branches named in the analysis, the figures came from live Google listings checked on 8 October 2026. Ratings for other branches reflected the last time their listings were refreshed, in most cases during September 2026.

The rankings were based on exact average star ratings, with the proportion of one-star reviews used to break ties. The researchers also flagged highly rated branches that had received their 20 newest reviews within 60 days.

The results provide a snapshot of how customers have rated their local bank branches, rather than a definitive measure of the service every customer can expect.
