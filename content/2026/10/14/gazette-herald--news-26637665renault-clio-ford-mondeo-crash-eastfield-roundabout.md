A car drove the wrong way around a North Yorkshire roundabout before crashing into another vehicle, police have said.

The crash happened at around 8.50am on Tuesday (October 13) at the Musham Bank roundabout near Eastfield.




A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It involved a grey Renault Clio which was travelling the wrong way around the roundabout before colliding with a silver Ford Mondeo.

“We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the collision or saw either vehicle in the moments leading up to it. We also ask anyone with dashcam footage of the incident, or the vehicles involved, to get in touch.”

If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please email [megan.smith2@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:megan.smith2@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Please quote reference 12260201324 when providing information.
