Standing out in a county famous for fish and chips is no easy task, but a chippy in one of County Durham’s prettiest villages has built a reputation far beyond Teesdale.

Nestled on Market Place, Middleton-in-Teesdale Fish & Chip Shop has become a firm favourite with locals, walkers and tourists exploring the Durham Dales.

Just a short drive from High Force and surrounded by the landscapes of the North Pennines, the independent business has quietly become a destination in its own right.

Customers regularly stop by after days spent exploring the countryside, while others make special journeys to visit the village chippy.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21157997.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Lydia Sunter, who has run the shop for 14 years, said quality food, affordable prices and loyal customers had driven its success after it reached The Northern Echo’s Best Chippy 2026 shortlist.

The traditional menu remains at the heart of its appeal. Alongside fish and chips, customers can choose from a range of pies, with the shop also earning wider recognition for its homemade creations.

The business was previously highlighted for winning top honours at the British Pie Awards and for speciality menu items that have attracted praise from visitors.

Online reviews say it is the consistency of the food and the friendly atmosphere that keep people returning.

Customers praise the quality of the fish, generous portions, freshly cooked chips and welcoming service.

That reputation has helped the business develop a following beyond Teesdale.

TripAdvisor reviewers describe it as a destination worth travelling for, with some making repeat visits whenever they find themselves in the area.

The site currently gives the business a rating of 4.7 out of five from hundreds of reviews.

The chippy has also expanded its reach through a mobile van, which serves surrounding villages and pubs across the region, helping bring its food to communities beyond Middleton-in-Teesdale.

Whether it's walkers finishing a day on the fells, visitors returning from High Force or locals picking up their Friday tea, Middleton in Teesdale Fish & Chip Shop has become part of village life.

In a county packed with excellent chippies, that combination of quality food, friendly service and stunning surroundings continues to make it one of Teesdale's most popular stops.
