Police have released CCTV footage of a person they want to speak to about a theft in Scarborough.

The alleged incident happened on September 21 at the One Stop on Falsgrave Road in the town.

Officers are urging anyone who recognises the person in the footage to come forward.

**Recommended reads:**

A police spokesperson said: “They may have information that will assist our investigation.”

Anyone with information is asked to email PC1488 Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260186066.

Alternatively, people can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
