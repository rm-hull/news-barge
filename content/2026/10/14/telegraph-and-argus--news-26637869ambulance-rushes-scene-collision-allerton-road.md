THE AMBULANCE SERVICE confirmed it attended a collision in a Bradford suburb this afternoon.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service received an emergency call just before 2pm today (October 14) to Allerton Road in Allerton.

The road runs through the Allerton area and connects with Thornton Road (B6145) and other local residential streets.

There had been a collision between a car and e-bike.

A paramedic checked the patient, who did not need hospital treatment.

## What the emergency service said

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 1.48pm on Wednesday afternoon to report a collision between a car and e-bike on Allerton Road, Bradford.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was checked over but did not need to be conveyed to hospital."

West Yorkshire Police did not provide a comment.
