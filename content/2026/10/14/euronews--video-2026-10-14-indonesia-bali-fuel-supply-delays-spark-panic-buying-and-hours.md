**Queues of motorcycles and cars spilled onto roads**across the Indonesian resort island. One Denpasar resident said she had waited about seven hours in the heat** to buy petrol**.

Officials blamed **distribution delays** rather than depleted island-wide stocks. Indonesia’s state-owned energy company**Pertamina** said a tanker’s**anchor problem** had delayed unloading, while**road congestion slowed deliveries**to stations.** Panic buying** added to the pressure, particularly for cheaper, subsidised Pertalite petrol.

Bali Governor **Wayan Koster** urged residents**not to panic**, saying fuel remained available at storage depots. Pertamina said on Wednesday that** an additional 57.9 million litres of petrol were scheduled to arrive** in Bali between 14 and 17 October. Deliveries were stepped up around the clock, with 60 stations in Denpasar operating 24 hours a day. Queues persisted despite the additional supplies.
