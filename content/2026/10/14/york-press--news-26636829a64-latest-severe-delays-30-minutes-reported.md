Long delays are being reported on a main road in North Yorkshire.

Traffic is queuing at various sections of the A64 between York and Malton.

The AA has said there are severe delays of 13 minutes on A64 Westbound between Whinny Lane and Malton Road, with an average speed of ten mph.

Traffic also queuing both ways at North Lane, near the Highwayman Cafe, just before the Hopgrove roundabout due to roadworks and temporary traffic lights.
