# Leeds Harehills crash latest as police and ambulance crews issue statement

## A person has been rushed to hospital

A person has been rushed to hospital after they were hit by a car in Leeds.

They were hit on Beckett Street in Harehills at 9.52am today on Thursday, October 29. A large cordon has been set up and surrounding roads have been closed. Drivers have been asked to avoid the area.

Harehills Road has been closed in both directions with slow traffic. All approaches to the mini roundabout are closed, from Beckett Street, Ashley Road and Stanley Road.

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 9.52am on Thursday morning to report a collision between a car and pedestrian on Beckett Street, Leeds. A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Police are currently in attendance at a serious road traffic collision involving a car and a pedestrian on Beckett Street. A scene is on whilst enquiries are ongoing. Motorists are asked to avoid the area."
