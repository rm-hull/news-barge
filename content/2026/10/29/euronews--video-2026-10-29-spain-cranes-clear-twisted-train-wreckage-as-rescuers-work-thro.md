Personnel from **Spain's Military Emergency Unit (UME)** remained at the scene, working among derailed carriages and scattered debris while**heavy cranes lifted sections of wreckage** from the tracks. Images released on Thursday show emergency workers examining the damaged trains and coordinating operations at the crash site in the**Castilla-La Mancha region**.

The disaster occurred when a high-speed service operated by state-owned **Renfe struck the rear of a stationary train** belonging to private operator**Iryo**. The collision left carriages derailed and badly damaged, with rescue teams continuing their work into the following day.

Meanwhile, authorities **established a temporary morgue** at the**Institute of Forensic Medicine in Toledo** to identify those killed in the disaster. Military vehicles and police patrols were stationed outside the facility as forensic identification procedures got under way.

The two operations continued in parallel on Thursday, with emergency teams clearing the wreckage near **Urda** while forensic specialists worked to establish the identities of the victims. The crash is**Spain's second major deadly railway accident of 2026**, following a** collision in January that killed 45 people**.
