A former car dealership on one of Teesside’s busiest retail strips could become a petrol station and shop.

Plans have been lodged to turn the vacant former JLR Stockton site on Portrack Lane into a petrol station, shop and electric vehicle charging hub.

The site was previously used as a forecourt and outdoor display area for the vehicle showroom.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644593.jpg?type=mds-article-620)85 Portrack Lane, the former JLR showroom, could soon become a petrol station *(Image: Google)*

IGP Investments Stockton LLP hopes to bring a new 313-square-metre building to the site, most of which would be given over to the shop.

If approved, the petrol station would open from 6am to midnight every day, including Sundays and bank holidays.

Drivers would also have four electric vehicle charging bays to choose from, alongside nine standard car parking spaces and two disabled spaces for those popping into the store without filling up.

Rather than a traditional brick-built shop, plans show a more modern finish for the kiosk, combining pale wall panels with chocolate-coloured vertical boards, a grey roof and dark grey-framed windows.

The development would use the existing access, with no new or altered vehicle or pedestrian entrance from the public highway.

The developers sought pre-application advice from Stockton-on-Tees Borough Council in October 2025 before signing the full application on October 20, 2025.

The plans will now be considered by Stockton Council.
