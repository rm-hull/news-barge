A County Durham firm is seeking permission to move part of its goods vehicle operations to a new base.

ARM Pipetek Ltd, based at Unit 8, Belmont Industrial Estate, has applied to amend its goods vehicle operator’s licence.

The company wants to add a new operating centre at Unit 3, Doxford Drive, South West Industrial Estate, Peterlee.

The new site would house 12 goods vehicles and one trailer.

The order would remove the undertaking that ARM Pipetek keep 12 goods vehicles and one trailer at its Belmont Industrial Estate site.

The proposal has been published and is open to public comment.

Owners and occupiers of nearby land who believe the site could affect their use or enjoyment of it can object.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Objections must be sent in writing to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, with clear reasons.

Objections must be submitted within 21 days of the notice being published.

A copy must also be sent to ARM Pipetek Ltd at its Belmont Industrial Estate address.

The deadline for making representations is November 17.

Guidance on making representations is available at gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.
