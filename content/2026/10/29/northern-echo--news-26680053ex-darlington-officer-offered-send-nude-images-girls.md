A Darlington policeman who thought he'd offered to send pictures of his penis to teenage girls was actually messaging online paedophile hunters, a court heard.

Haydyn Brunsdon, a now-former police constable, thought he was chatting to girls aged 13 and 15, but was actually speaking to decoys.

The 31-year-old former Royal Navy serviceman appeared at Teesside Crown Court this morning when he (Thursday, October 28) admitted two charges of attempting to communicate sexually with a child.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21505056.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Former Durham Police officer Haydyn Brunsdon leaving Teesside Magistrates' Court. *(Image: NORTH NEWS)*

The court heard the messages were sent between June 2 and 5, and September 14 and 24 this year when he was an officer based in Darlington.

Brunsdon, previously of an address in Darlington but now of Catterick, North Yorkshire, was arrested while off duty in the early hours of September 25 and taken into custody in another force area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21644562.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Haydyn Brunsdon pictured leaving court earlier this month. *(Image: North News and Pictures)*

He was suspended from duty, but Durham Police confirmed he has now resigned.

Brunsdon's barrister, Chris Moran, asked for a further charge of misconduct in public office not to be put to him at today's hearing.

The court heard his lawyers will ask for it to be dismissed at a hearing on March 25 on the basis the messages were sent outside Brunsdon's working hours.

If the application fails, a trial lasting up to three days will start on February 21, 2028.

He will only be sentenced once the misconduct charge is resolved.
