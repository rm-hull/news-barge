Former police station in Paisley has gone on the market with offers invited above £75,000.

Shepherd Chartered Surveyors is selling the former Ferguslie Park police station as a potential development opportunity.

The property is in Ferguslie Park, within Paisley and the Renfrewshire Council area. It is 8.5 miles south-west of Glasgow city centre.

The single-storey building measures 2,422 sq ft and stands on a secure site covering a third of an acre.

Access to the yard and building is available through secure vehicle access from Blackstoun Road and pedestrian access from Ferguslie Park Avenue.

Inside, the premises include a reception area and adjoining office near the Ferguslie Park Avenue entrance.

Two larger rooms, previously used as breakout spaces, are at the west and south ends of the building.

Several smaller storage and office spaces, along with toilets, are reached from a central hallway.

A secured rear parking area contains a storage shed and covered spaces for up to 10 vehicles. It also includes open space.

The property occupies a prominent corner position within the Ferguslie Park community hub.

The Tannahill Community Centre, Glencoats and Fergus’s Primary Schools, Mirren Park High School and Ferguslie Sports Centre are all within one mile.

The A737 is nearby, providing links to the M8, the south and Scotland’s wider motorway network.

Paisley St. James Train Station is less than a mile away, with frequent services to Glasgow Central Station and the West Coast.

Fraser McDonald at Shepherd Chartered Surveyors said: “This former police station with secure off-street covered parking offers a potential development opportunity with scope for alternative use, subject to planning. As such, we have already received keen interest in this opportunity.”

Offers in excess of £75,000 are invited. Interested parties can contact the sole selling agent on 0141 331 2807 for further information.
