As the Celebrity Traitors comes to its dramatic conclusion, VisitScotland has revealed that fans are driving online interest in Scotland – with UK searches more than double those during the run of the first Celebrity Traitors series.

The 10-episode show set at the privately-owned Ardross Castle in the Highlands will conclude on Friday, 30 October.

Actor Michael Sheen, presenter Maya Jama, singer James Blunt and comedian Miranda Hart are among the famous faces that entered the castle to play the game of deception, strategy and suspense and win money for charity.

While the castle is not open to the public, viewers have been treated to stunning scenes of Scotland's landscapes, from rolling moorland, lochs and forests, during the series’ outdoor missions, which appears to have inspired them to flock online.

![Claudia Winkleman.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20866196.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Celebrity Traitors host Claudia Winkleman *(Image: BBC/Studio Lambert/Euan Cherry)*

Research by Scotland's national tourism and events organization has found UK searches for "Where is the Traitors castle?" and "Where is The Traitors filmed?" have both increased by 124% in October 2026, compared with the same period in 2025 (when the first series aired).

Screen tourism is a popular travel trend. VisitScotland research shows that one in five visitors to Scotland are inspired by film, television or literature when choosing to take a trip.

To capitalise on the spike in interest in the show, a VisitScotland partnership with content creator Postcards by Hannah encouraged audiences to explore a range of attractions in the Highlands and Moray Speyside, after she travelled overnight from London to Inverness aboard the Caledonian Sleeper.

Across an Instagram reel and accompanying carousel, the activity generated more than 213,000 views, reached over 144,000 people, and achieved strong engagement. The reel has been one of VisitScotland's top-performing influencer posts of the year.

Jenni Steele, VisitScotland's Film and Creative Industries Manager, said: "The Traitors has become the perfect companion to autumn and winter in Scotland.

"The enchanting beauty of our moorland, forests and lochs, combined with the mystery, plotting and suspense of the show, is a powerful mix for viewers and a great showcase for Scotland.

"Screen tourism has the power to turn viewers into visitors, creating benefits for communities across Scotland long after the credits roll.

"Celebrity Traitors is helping introduce Scotland to new audiences while inspiring others to discover more about the places they see on screen.

"We look forward to seeing whether a traitor or a faithful comes out on top, but one thing is already clear: Scotland is once again stealing the show."
