A PRESENTER and musician is returning to host a major awards event.

Nova will host the Retail, Leisure and Hospitality Awards at The Broadway Bradford on November 15.

It will be his fifth time hosting the ceremony, which celebrates outstanding businesses across the region.

Nova said: "It's always a proud moment for me coming back and hosting this event."

Nova began his media career as the club presenter for Bradford City before hosting Loving Living Local on FOX21 in Southern Colorado.

Following the awards, he will return to the US to host several high-profile events, including the We Fortify Gala on November 21 at Hotel Polaris in Colorado Springs and, for the fourth consecutive year, the America the Beautiful Chorus Christmas Show on December 5.

Nova is also marking the first anniversary of The Brit Pub in Colorado Springs on October 24, which was named Pub of the Year in the Best of the Springs 2026 awards.

A former member of Yorkshire band Scars on 45, Nova has teamed up with fellow Bradfordian Tom Nowakowski to launch Midnight Movie Club, a new global music project.

Their debut single, "That's the Way," featuring US singer-songwriter AltarViolet, will be released worldwide on November 3, 2026.
