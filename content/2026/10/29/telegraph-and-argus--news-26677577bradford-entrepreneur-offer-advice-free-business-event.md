A YOUNG entrepreneur who turned his childhood hobby into a successful business will speak at a free event next month.

Charlie Lane, 24, will share his journey at the Shipley Business Enterprise Hub on Saturday, November 7, at 10am.

Mr Lane is the founder of Charlie's Woodland Wonders, a venture that creates handcrafted wooden products from reclaimed and recycled timber.

He began the business at just 12 years old and has since earned national recognition, including success in the National Market Traders Federation's Young Trader Competition.

Bradford Council’s Executive Member for Economy, Regeneration and Planning Councillor Andrew Judson said: "The event has been organised for aspiring entrepreneurs and market traders aged 12 and above, although anyone interested in starting a small business, launching a market stall or turning a hobby into an income is welcome to attend.

"We are working hard to boost businesses across Bradford and we hope this event will be inspirational for Bradford’s business people of the future."

At the event, Mr Lane will discuss how he started his business, the challenges he has faced, and the lessons he has learned along the way.

Attendees will also have the chance to ask questions and gain practical advice about self-employment and creative enterprise.
