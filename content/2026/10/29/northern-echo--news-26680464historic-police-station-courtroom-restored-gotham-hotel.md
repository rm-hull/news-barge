A luxury North East hotel is expanding into a former police station and courtroom.

Hotel Gotham Newcastle will open its restored Grade II-listed building on Pilgrim Street on November 12, marking the second phase of its city-centre development.

A former police station with historic courtrooms, judges' chambers and prisoner cells is reopening as part of a luxury Newcastle hotel.

The opening follows last year's launch of the neighbouring former fire station and adds 30 bedrooms, including eight suites, plus event and social spaces in the original courtrooms.

Many of the building's most distinctive features have been retained as part of the restoration.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21645099.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Newcastle's former police station as Hotel Gotham prepares to open restored courtrooms and luxury spaces *(Image: Hotel Gotham Newcastle)*

Developers aim to preserve the history of one of Newcastle's most recognisable civic buildings.

At the heart of the project are the former courtrooms, which have been transformed into event venues while maintaining much of their original character.

A courtroom retains its raised judge's dais and wall panelling, while its dock and witness box have been reinstated.

Refurbished chandeliers offer another reminder of the building's former purpose.

The spaces will host weddings, private celebrations and other events.

Guests will also find original details including a restored terrazzo staircase leading to the courtroom level and lobby.

The former judge's chambers still have their wall panelling, while three prisoner cells still have their glazed tiled walls.

History has also been incorporated into a number of the bedrooms, with sections of gallery seating from courtroom one repurposed as feature walls.

A museum will also showcase artefacts and features from the building's past, opening to the public on selected days.

Among the exhibits will be the former gun-safe door and prisoner cell-corridor gate, alongside historic photographs, architectural drawings and displays exploring the site's role in Newcastle's policing history.

Visitors will be able to learn more about former chief constables, Newcastle's mounted police and the horses once stabled on the site, as well as the history of the neighbouring fire station.

Chris Thompson, general manager of Hotel Gotham Newcastle, said: "Opening the police station feels like completing the story we started when Gotham first arrived in Newcastle.

"This building has an incredible history, and we've been very conscious of making sure that history isn't hidden away - it's part of the experience.

"We wanted this phase to feel connected to everything guests already know and love about Gotham, while having an identity of its own."

He added: "The fire station was the first chapter, but this opening brings the two buildings together and gives guests even more of Gotham to discover.

"It's dramatic, it's full of personality and, most importantly, it still feels Newcastle unmistakably."

The opening of the former police station will bring Hotel Gotham Newcastle's total number of bedrooms to 87.

There will also be three new event spaces and additional facilities linked to the hotel's private members' club.
