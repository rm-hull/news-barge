A COMMUNITY health event will take centre stage this Black History Month.

Impact Women Network CIC (IWN CIC) is hosting a free health and wellbeing outreach in Bradford on Saturday, October 31, as part of its Black History Month programme.

The event, held under the theme Honouring Our Communities, will offer blood pressure and BMI checks, wellbeing information, period health support, and free sanitary products.

Funmilola Peters, founder and director of Impact Women Network CIC, said: "Black History Month is not only an opportunity to celebrate our history, culture and achievements; it is also a time to invest in the health and future of our communities.

"Through this outreach, we want people to stop, check their health, ask questions and access support that could make a real difference to their wellbeing."

The event is designed to promote early awareness, prevention, and reduced health inequalities, particularly among Black, Asian and minority ethnic communities who may face barriers to accessing health information and support.

The outreach is part of IWN CIC’s wider Black History Month celebration taking place at Bradford Art Centre, St Peter’s House.

Running from 3pm to 6pm, the event will bring together community members, volunteers, and health professionals in a supportive environment.
