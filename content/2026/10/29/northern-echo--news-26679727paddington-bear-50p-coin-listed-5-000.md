A "rare" 50p coin is currently up for sale for more than £5,000.

The coin has been listed for sale for a grand sum of £5,086.70 by a Sheringham seller.

It features a Paddington Bear design, created by David Knapton, which was released in 2018 and 2019.

![](https://www.edp24.co.uk/resources/images/21632929.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The coin is just one in a series of coins about the bear *(Image: eBay/Domesticonly)*

This specific coin depicts Paddington at the Tower of London.

There are four different Paddington Bear 50p coin designs, featuring him at other London landmarks such as St Paul's Cathedral.

Coin Identifier lists the coin as the most valuable in the Paddington series.

More than nine million of the coins were minted.
