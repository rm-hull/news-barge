The Honor Among Thieves quest in Phantom Blade Zero is relatively short—you kill an enemy in the Crimson Forest who drops a letter, which you then deliver to his brother in the Thieves Camp a little further down the trail after Jing Ye.

The brother, however, can't read, and demands you answer some questions about its contents to prove it's genuine. The answers to his questions are:

* Stole a landowner's belt.
* Butterfly.
* The letter never said. His fate is unknown.

Don't worry if you get an answer wrong, he'll just end the conversation and you'll have to talk to him again. These answers are, of course, in the letter itself. Look for the Wulin Tales section of the archive and then the 8th entry, Blood Letter from the Nimble Thief.

Answer all three correctly and you'll get the Silent Wrap accessory, which reduces noise from assassinations and movement by 40%, though you can upgrade this further once you unlock polishing in the Village of Lu.
