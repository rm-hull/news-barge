# Huge popstar opens up on Leeds Festival headlining 'dream' as 2027 line up rumours build

## Big names including Tate McRae, Sabrina Carpenter, Phoebe Bridgers and The Strokes have been rumoured for Leeds Festival 2027

A huge pop star has spoken of their ‘dream’ to headline Leeds Festival.

We have started to see lots of rumours about who might appear at Bramham Park for Leeds Fest 2027. Earlier this week, we reported that Tate McRae, Sabrina Carpenter, Phoebe Bridgers and The Strokes are being backed for appearances at Leeds Festival and its sister event Reading Festival.

Leeds Festival 2027 will take place, as usual, over the August bank holiday weekend. The dates for the fun are Thursday, August 26 to Sunday, August 29. You can buy tickets from Ticketmaster.

Following on from a Leeds Festival 2026 performance, Little Mix star Jade Thirlwall has spoken of how she’d love to headline at Worthy Farm in the future. As reported by the NME, Jade said: “That’s definitely a dream of mine to headline more festivals, and there are no better ones than Reading, Leeds and Glastonbury.”

We’ve previously chatted to Melvin Benn, managing director of Festival Republic, which oversees the festivities, with our feature touching on next year and how he, and his team, go about booking headline acts.
