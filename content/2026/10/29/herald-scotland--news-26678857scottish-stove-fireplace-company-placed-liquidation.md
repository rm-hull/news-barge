A liquidator has been appointed to a Scottish stove and fireplace company.

The Dunblane-registered company was placed into voluntary liquidation.

Murray and McGregor Stoves and Fireplaces Limited moved to be voluntarily wound up at a general meeting when it was also agreed that Robert Cooksey, of Bridgestones Limited, should be appointed liquidator.

The company reported net assets of £10,000 in 2025, down from £25,000 the year before. The firm had an average of four employees, according to its latest accounts, set against five in the prior period.

![Wood stove heating with in foreground wood pellets - economical heating system concept](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21643082.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Liquidator appointed to Scottish stove and fireplace company *(Image: Getty Images)*

The creditors’ voluntary liquidation is related to a Duckburn Business Park-registered company.

It comes against the backdrop of a short-term rise in the number of such liquidations in Scotland.

The latest Insolvency Service figures show that in September, there were 117 company insolvencies registered in Scotland, 14% higher than the number in September 2025.

The total number of company insolvencies was made up of 68 creditors’ voluntary liquidations, 38 compulsory liquidations, 10 administrations and one company voluntary arrangement.

In August, there were 73 company insolvencies registered in Scotland, 23% lower than the number in August 2025. The total number of company insolvencies consisted of 30 creditors’ voluntary liquidations, 35 compulsory liquidations and eight administrations.

In England and Wales last month, there were 1,882 company insolvencies, 4% lower than in August and 5% lower than in the same period last year. September saw higher numbers of compulsory liquidations but lower numbers of creditors’ voluntary liquidations and administrations than in August south of the Border.

There were 1,946 company insolvencies in August in England and Wales, 1% higher than in July but 3% lower than the same month last year.

In August, there were lower numbers of creditors’ voluntary liquidations but higher compulsory liquidations and administrations than July south of the Border.

Online information about Murray and McGregor Stoves and Fireplaces describes the firm as family-run, and being involved in selling and installing wood burning stoves and chimneys.
