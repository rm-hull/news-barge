Serbia’s political landscape has been upended. On Sunday, record numbers of people went to the polls and voted to end the 12-year rule of Aleksandar Vučić. And who did they back instead? A list of candidates, half of whom have PhDs, put forward by student activists whose protests have rocked the Balkan country for the past two years.

**Helen Pidd** speaks to the Serbian-American journalist**Ana Milosavljevic**, the student activist** Tara Blagojević**and the MP-to-be** Milica Jovanović** about how the Student List managed to defeat Vučić’s increasingly authoritarian regime.

With victory secured, the hard part begins: how does a leaderless movement choose a prime minister and run a country?
