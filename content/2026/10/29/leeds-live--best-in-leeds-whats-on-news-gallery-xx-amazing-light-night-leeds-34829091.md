# 29 amazing Light Night Leeds 2026 photos perfectly summing up the opening evening

## Photogragher Lee McLean has captured some brilliant photos from the first evening of Light Night Leeds 2026

![](https://video.primis.tech/uploads/cn1/video/users/origin/31466/video_68f8c0e8d8a26009905662/vid6ae312d633620194886451.jpg?cbuster=1793266667)

Take a look at these wonderful photos from the opening night of Light Night Leeds 2026.

We’ve saw families take to the streets of Leeds city centre on Wednesday evening to savour the special festivities. Light Night Leeds 2026 running on Wednesday, October 28 and Thursday, October 29 - the event dates this year moved to coincide with the school holidays.

Buildings across the city have been lit up while lots of special installations have been put in place. This year’s spectacular festival has the theme of A City Speaks…which is exploring the stories, people and places that have shaped Leeds over the past four centuries while looking towards the city's future.

Councillor Salma Arif, Leeds City Council’s deputy leader and executive member for economy, said: “Light Night is a major event in our calendar every year and we are really looking forward to the wide variety of projections and performances that are on offer for this year’s festival. It’s great to have this opportunity to really celebrate Leeds and bringing people together to explore the stories and the people who make the city what it is.”

You can see photos, from the first evening of Light Night Leeds 2026, by scrolling below. The amazing photos have been taken by Lee McLean.
