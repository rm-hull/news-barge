Melbourne Road in Thornton is set to be temporarily closed next month.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has announced the closure to allow safe access to a telecoms pole for service provision.

The closure will take place on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, from 8am to 5pm.

The road closure is necessary to ensure public safety during the works.

The order is valid for 18 months, but the works are expected to be completed within one day.

Access for emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout the closure.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal at publicnoticeportal.uk/york-press**

A diversion route will be in place for all other traffic.

This will include Main Street, the road from Hagg Lane to General Lane, Hagg Lane, Common Lane, Main Street, Sandhill Lane, The Street, Sutton Lane, York Road, Main Street, Hodsow Lane Roundabout, Hodsow Lane, Main Street, and Melbourne Road.

The diversion route will be signed at the time of closure.

For further information, residents can contact the council and ask for Highways Customer Care on 01482 393939, using reference RJH.
