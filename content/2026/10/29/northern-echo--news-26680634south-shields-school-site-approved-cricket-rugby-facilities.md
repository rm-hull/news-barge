Plans to transform part of a school site into new cricket and rugby facilities for a South Tyneside sports club have been given the go-ahead.

The borough council’s planning committee has approved plans to change the use of playing fields at Epinay School in South Shields to make way for new outdoor sports grounds.

This includes two cricket squares, three rugby pitches and a pavilion building with hospitality facilities, with the council expected to construct the facilities and lease them to the Westoe Cricket & Rugby Club.

A council report noted that the sports club will be “looking to use the site primarily in the evenings and at weekends as additional facilities provision to their existing club facilities on their site at Dean Road in Westoe.”

There were six public objections to the plans raising concerns about drainage, increased traffic from players, spectators, parents and visitors, as well as concerns about the loss of open green space and noise impacts.

Meanwhile, there were three representations in support describing the plans as a “great addition to the local area” which would benefit young people and tackle obesity.

Councillors discussed the plans at a meeting on October 20 at South Shields Town Hall, and the application was approved by a majority vote.

Although there were questions about the development’s links with the South Tyneside College relocation project, committee members were advised that the application for part of the Epinay School site needed to be looked at on its own merits.

Council planning officers, recommending the scheme for approval, said it was acceptable in terms of the principle of development, green belt, visual and residential amenity, highway safety and parking, biodiversity, drainage and land contamination matters.

A council report added: “The lawful planning use of the site is school playing fields although the Epinay School do not currently use the pitches for sports activities.

“The site would be operated separately from the school by Westoe Cricket & Rugby Club and the only shared facilities would be the school car park (outwith the application site) on Nevinson Avenue which would be used by the club as well as the school, and this would be fenced off.

“The proposed facilities will compensate in part for the loss of playing pitches on the South Tyneside College Westoe campus site.

“They are part of a package of sports facilities associated with the Section 106 agreement which runs alongside the planning permission for housing development at the college site, and where this is also linked to the college’s relocation into their new campus in South Shields town centre.”
