Stars of stage and screen Tom Rosenthal and Adrian Scarborough will lead the cast in a new tour of *Jeeves and Wooster in Perfect Nonsense*.

The comedy from the works of PG Wodehouse, is written by Robert Goodale and David Goodale, and the tour, will be directed by acclaimed theatre director Phillip Breen.

*Jeeves and Wooster in Perfect Nonsense* will run at the Grand Opera House York from**Tuesday 4 to Saturday 8 May 2027.**

Expect laugh-out-loud comedy, mistaken identities, romantic entanglements, spectacular muddles and a colourful cast of wonderfully eccentric characters.

PG Wodehouse’s world of charm and mischief will burst onto the stage in a riot of impeccable comic timing, ingenious theatrical invention and gloriously British humour.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Adrian-Scarborough-and-Tom-Rosenthal-photo-by-Michael-Wharley-.png)

Tom Rosenthal, who plays the hapless Bertie Wooster, is an acclaimed British actor and comedian, best known for playing Jonny Goodman in the hit Channel 4 sitcom *Friday Night Dinner* and Marcus Gallo in ITV2’s*Plebs*.

Alongside his screen career, he is an established stand-up comedian, performing at venues and festivals across the UK.

Adrian Scarborough who plays Wooster’s unflappable valet Jeeves, is a two-time Olivier Award-winning British actor best known for playing Pete Sutcliffe in the hit BBC comedy *Gavin & Stacey* and the title role in*The Chelsea Detective*.

Alongside his screen career, he is an acclaimed stage actor, having won two Olivier Awards for his performances in After the Dance (National Theatre) and Leopoldstadt (Wyndham’s Theatre).

*Jeeves and Wooster in Perfect Nonsense* is produced by ATG Productions.
