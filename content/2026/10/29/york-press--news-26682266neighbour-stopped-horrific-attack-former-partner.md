## Neighbour praised for stopping 'horrific' attack on woman in her home

The neighbour stopped domestic abuse that a judge warned could have ended in murder. *(Image: Unsplash)*

The courage of a neighbour whose actions stopped a 'horrific' attack on a woman has been praised.

He was among people who acted when they heard the woman screaming as her former partner David Thompson attacked her in her own home.

North Yorkshire Police said one made a 999 call saying the woman was hanging out of a window shouting for help.

York Crown Court heard how the neighbour saw the door to the woman's flat ajar and went inside.

As he did so, Thompson ran out with blood from the victim on his clothes.

Inside, the woman had marks on her head where Thompson had stamped on her twice.

He had also smashed a vase over her head and thrown a stereo at her.

"You could have been up for murder. It was only him going in there that stopped you. You didn’t stop yourself,” he told Thompson.

![Domestic abuser David Thompson of North Yorkshire (Image: North Yorkshire Police)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21648104.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Domestic abuser David Thompson of North Yorkshire *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

After Thompson, 49, now of Hillam near Selby, was jailed for attempted grievous bodily harm and criminal damage, police praised the actions of the neighbour who went into the Harrogate flat.

Detective Constable Rebecca Foster, who brought Thompson to justice, said: "This was a horrific and sustained attack which left the victim with significant injuries and fearing for her life.

"Domestic abuse can have a devastating and lasting impact on victims.

"We would like to commend the bravery of the victim in this case, as well as the members of the public who intervened and contacted police when they realised something was wrong."

"We welcome the sentence handed to Thompson and hope it provides the victim with some reassurance as she continues to recover from her ordeal."

She said: "If you are experiencing domestic abuse, please know that you are not alone and support is available.

"We will always take reports seriously and work to protect victims and bring offenders to justice.

"If you prefer not to report to the police, you can get confidential help, support and access to emergency accommodation from the charity Independent Domestic Abuse Services IDAS or call 03000 110 110.

"Or call the national 24-hour domestic abuse helpline 0808 2000 247Refuge, the UK's largest specialist domestic abuse organisation."
