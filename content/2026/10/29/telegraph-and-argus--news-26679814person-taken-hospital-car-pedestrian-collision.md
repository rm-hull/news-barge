A person was rushed to hospital after a collision involving a car and a pedestrian in Bradford.

The incident happened on Beckside Road, near the Lidget Green Post Office, shortly before 2pm on Wednesday afternoon.

A police cordon was put in place and the road was partially blocked while officers responded.

## What happened?

The ambulance service has now revealed what happened.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 1.48pm on Wednesday afternoon (28 October) to report a collision between a car and pedestrian on Beckside Road, Bradford.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to Bradford Royal Infirmary."

![Officers attending the scene at Beckside Road](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21642771.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Officers attending the scene at Beckside Road *(Image: Supplied)*

A witness at the scene said: "At first, we thought the post office was getting robbed.

"There was a huge scene and there were loads of police cars.

"They started coming from every angle."
