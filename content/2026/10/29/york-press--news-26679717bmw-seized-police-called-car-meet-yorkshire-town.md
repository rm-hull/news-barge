A BMW has been seized by police after officers were called to a “large-scale car meet” in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police said the car was seized “due to the driver’s manner of driving” at the meeting in Selby on Sunday night (October 25).

The force said it was called to the meet up “following a number of reports of antisocial driving, beeping, revving of engines and traffic hold-ups”.

Police said the cars were dispersed from the area and details of the vehicles in attendance were obtained as “enquiries are continuing”.

PC Matt Harvey, of North Yorkshire Police’s Road Safety Team, said: “Illegal car meets, street racing and anti-social driving will not be tolerated in North Yorkshire.

“We are committed to tackling this type of behaviour through enforcement, engagement and partnership working, and any individuals involved can expect decisive action, including vehicle seizure and prosecution where appropriate.”
