City of York Council have reminded road users that a section of York Outer Ring Road will be closed tonight and tomorrow.

A section of the A1237 between the A19 Shipton Road Roundabout and the entrance to Clifton Moor Retail Park will be resurfaced on Thursday 29 October and Friday 30 October.

Works will take place overnight, from 8pm, with the road reopening each morning at 5am.

All works are due to be completed by 5am on Saturday (31 October), weather permitting.

A signed diversion will be in place during the closures.

Bus services will not be affected and emergency services will continue to have access at all times.
