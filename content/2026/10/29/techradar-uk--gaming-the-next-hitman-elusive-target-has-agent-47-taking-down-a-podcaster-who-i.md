* **The next*Hitman* Elusive Target has been revealed**
* **The limited-time mission will see Agent 47 taking down an annoying podcaster**
* **The target will be played by real-world content creator Seán McLoughlin**

The next Hitman Elusive Target has been revealed, and it's something that could happen in 2026.

As of October 29, players will be able to take out Finn Colton – a controversial millionaire content creator who goes by the online handle 'Finnvictus'. The mission will see the flashy mansion of the existing Hawke's Bay map turned into an even more elaborate space for content creation, with its own underground studio and cameras that live-stream everything 24/7.

Bald assassin protagonist Agent 47 has managed to gain entry thanks to an invite to appear on the content creator's flagship podcast and will have to contend with an environment that has eyes almost everywhere.

![Content creator Se&amp;aacute;n McLoughlin in the recording both for the latest Hitman DLC.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/7sRGV3FWHea2TgcfXVaGM7-1200-80.png)

That's not all, however, as Colton is played by Seán McLoughlin, better known as 'Jacksepticeye', with the popular streamer lending both his likeness and voice to the character.

"I’ve been playing *Hitman* with my community for years, so getting to actually step into the*World of Assassination* is unreal," shared McLoughlin.

"Finn is loud, paranoid, and completely obsessed with content, and playing him was an absolute blast. I can’t wait to see all the creative ways you take him down."

The mission will be available for free for a limited time, though die-hard *Hitman* fans can pick up a themed DLC pack as well. It costs $7.99 / £6.99 and includes permanent access to the mission via the Elusive Target Arcade mode plus a new suit and some unique equipment to try.
