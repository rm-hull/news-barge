Seven months of roadworks are set to cause disruption on a main York road from next week.

Main Street in Fulford will have restrictions from Monday, 2 November.

Yorkshire Water is installing an underground storage tank on Fordlands Road to help improve water quality in the River Ouse.

The extra storage is part of an £8 million investment to improve storm overflows across York. The tank is designed hold more wastewater back and stop it overflowing during periods of bad weather.

Work to install the tank will begin with a project to divert a water main between Monday  
2 November to Thursday 19 November.

Residents nearby are being warned that they may experience a temporary interruption to  
their water supply or reduced water pressure during this time.

A roadworks notice says there will be “some carriageway incursion” from 2 November 2026 to 28 May 2027.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/traffic-on-main-street-fulford-york.webp)

Yorkshire Water says: “We will work hard to minimise the disruption this causes for local residents and road users.”

The most severe disruption looks set to take place next year.

A letter to residents says: “To carry out the construction works safely, a road closure will be in place on Main Street from Monday 1 February to Friday 12 March, but don’t worry, a fully signposted diversion route will be in place.

“There will also be temporary traffic lights in place on Fulford Mews from Monday 2 November to Sunday 28 March, and overnight on Fordlands Road on Tuesday 19 January and Wednesday 20 January.”

The news has left residents in the area concerned about the impact on their lives.

One posted on social media: “Bang on time to compliment the car park that is Fulford when the festive stuff starts at the outlet.”

Another said: “Can we contest this start date – as others have said the traffic makes us trapped from November 1st to Christmas Eve near Fordlands/ Germany Beck.

“This is just going to make it even worse …. Surely starting after Christmas is the right thing to do?”
