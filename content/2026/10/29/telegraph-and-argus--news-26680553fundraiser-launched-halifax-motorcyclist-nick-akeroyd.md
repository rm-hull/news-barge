A fundraising campaign has been launched for the family of a motorcyclist who died following a crash in West Yorkshire.

Police confirmed a 32-year-old man died after a single-vehicle collision involving a motorbike on Moor End Road, Halifax, at around 2.40pm on Tuesday, October 28.

The man has since been locally named as Nick Akeroyd.

## Motorcyclist died at the scene

West Yorkshire Police said emergency services were called to Moor End Road shortly after 2.40pm following reports of a collision involving a motorcycle.

Despite receiving urgent treatment from emergency crews, the 32-year-old rider was pronounced dead at the scene.

Officers are continuing to investigate the circumstances of the crash and are appealing for witnesses to come forward.

Anyone who witnessed the incident, has footage of the collision, or saw the motorbike prior to the crash is asked to contact police, quoting reference 856 of October 28.

## Fundraiser launched for wife and three children

Following Mr Akeroyd's death, a GoFundMe page has been set up to help support his family and cover funeral costs.

The appeal states that his death came as a huge shock to those closest to him and that he leaves behind his wife, three children, family and many friends.

The fundraiser reads: "Nick sadly passed away yesterday in a motorbike accident leaving behind three children, a wife, family and many friends.

"As this came to us as a massive shock and something so sudden we are trying to piece everything together."

Organisers said donations would help the family arrange a fitting funeral and ease some of the financial pressures following his sudden death.

## 'The life and soul of any party'

Paying tribute, those behind the fundraiser described Mr Akeroyd as someone who brought joy to everyone around him.

The page states: "He was the life and soul of any party and his sense of humour would make you laugh your socks off."

It also describes him as "the best man anyone could know".

Friends, loved ones and supporters have been donating and leaving messages of condolence as the family comes to terms with the loss.

## Police appeal for information

West Yorkshire Police is continuing to appeal for witnesses as enquiries into the collision continue.

Anyone who saw the incident, has dashcam, CCTV or mobile phone footage, or saw the motorcycle before the crash, is asked to contact the force via 101 or online, quoting reference 856 of October 28.
