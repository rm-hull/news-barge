Fourteen men involved in a massive Race Night brawl in York city centre have pleaded guilty to unlawful violence.

All 14 had travelled together from their homes near Rotherham in South Yorkshire to York on July 25, 2025.

After spending the day at York races, they moved into the city centre for the evening, where they went to the Slug and Lettuce on Ouse Bridge.

Doormen ejected one of the 14 and that sparked violence that erupted both outside and inside the bar, York Magistrates Court heard.

After viewing CCTV of the brawl, Deputy District Judge Andrew Garthwaite said it had been such a major fight that his powers of a maximum sentence of 12 months were insufficient.

It was a “very serious incident with very clear targeting of a bouncer”, he said.

The majority of the violence had been directed at doormen, who had been performing a public function.

It had “clearly distressed members of the public within and outside” the bar.

He sent all 14 defendants to Leeds Crown Court for sentence.

They were released until November 25 when they must appear before a crown court judge for sentence.

He will have the power to jail them for up to three years each.

Deputy District Judge Andrew Garthwaite ordered probation officers to prepare pre-sentence reports on all fourteen.
