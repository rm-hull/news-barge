# Major Leeds festival announces 2027 big acts

## The event will take place next summer

A huge Leeds festival has announced its first acts for summer 2027.

MiNT Festival is set to return to Newsam Green Farm, in Leeds, on Saturday, May 1 and Sunday, May 2. The festivities aim to be two days of electronic music, underground sounds and atmosphere.

If you’d love to go to MiNT Festival 2027 you can find out ticket information here.

Set against the distinctive countryside surroundings of Newsam Green Farm, MiNT Festival 2027 will feature three stages, The Bunker, The Orchard and The Barn, each showcasing a varied selection of artists spanning electronic music and underground club culture. Saturday’s programme will feature performances from Captain Wallop, Lili, L.P. Rhythm, Kepler, Rossi., RTK Tarantino, Annie Errez and Bobby O’Donnell, alongside appearances from MiNT residents. The Orchard will host Ellia Jaya and Gaskin, while The Barn welcomes Morgan Seatree.

Sunday continues the celebrations with a fresh programme across all three stages. The Bunker will feature Boss Priester, Dr Banana, Enzo Siragusa, Annie Errez and Bobby O’Donnell, alongside MiNT residents. Over at The Orchard, Cam Stockman, Hidde van Wee and Sidney Charles lead the announced names, while The Barn brings together Burnski, Julian Anthony, Luke Dean and MJ Cole.
