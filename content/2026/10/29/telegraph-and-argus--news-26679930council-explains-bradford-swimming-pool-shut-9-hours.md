Bradford Council has explained why one of its swimming pools was shut for nine hours.

Bowling Pool and Gym, on Flockton Road in East Bowling, announced a number of cancelled sessions on Tuesday morning after what was initially described only as an “incident in the pool”.

The affected sessions included adult lane swimming between 6.30am and 8.30am and a Swim for All session from 9am until 10am.

## Safety concern prompted closure

Bradford Council has now confirmed the closure was due to a health and safety concern identified at around 6.30pm on Monday.

The decision was taken to shut the pool so it could be cleaned before reopening to the public.

A council spokesperson said: “Due to a health and safety concern at around 6:30pm on Monday, the decision was taken to close the pool at Bowling Pool and Gym so that it could be cleaned.

“It did not require emergency services, nor did any of our customers require medical treatment.

“The pool reopened at 12pm on Tuesday once it was deemed safe to do so, meaning it was closed for approximately nine operational hours.”

![Bowling Pool and Gym](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21644450.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bowling Pool and Gym *(Image: Bradford Council)*

## Popular community facility

Bowling Pool and Gym is a council-run leisure facility in East Bowling.

The site features a 25-metre swimming pool, gym, sauna and accessible changing facilities, including ramp access and a pool lift platform for swimmers with impaired mobility.

While the council has now confirmed the closure was linked to a health and safety concern, no further details about the nature of the incident have been released.
