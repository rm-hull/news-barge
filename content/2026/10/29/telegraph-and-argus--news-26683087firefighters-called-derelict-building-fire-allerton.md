A LARGE emergency response was at the scene of a derelict building fire in Bradford this afternoon.

The West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was called at 2.38pm on Thursday, October 29, after reports of a fire at a derelict building on Ivy Lane in Allerton, Bradford.

The fire service confirmed that three fire engines and one aerial appliance tackled the blaze, which social media users said was in an old farmhouse.

A spokesperson for the WYFRS said: "The derelict building was 25 x 10 metres and was 10 per cent involved in fire over two stories.

"Three large jets were used to extinguish the fire. "
