The company behind a much-loved East Coast family restaurant has gone into liquidation with debts exceeding £80,000.

Wiseguys Bridlington Ltd opened 10 years ago and over the years became a popular Italian American café restaurant on the resort’s Promenade.

The licensed restaurant received rave reviews on social media, with TripAdvisor awarding the business 4.7 stars out of five, based on 374 reviews.

Sadly, the venture, popular with families, both local and visitors, and noted for its burgers, pizzas, pasta, ice creams and extensive menu, closed on January 3.

Announcing the closure, the company posted on Facebook: “We have come to the end of our lease and decided it’s time to move on.”

However, Wiseguys Bridlington, which was incorporated in 2016, entered voluntary liquidation on August 25, with Driffield-based JW Butler appointed liquidator.

Companies House lists the directors as Kate Eloise Corrigan and Kirk Ashley Jemison as the two directors.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs outlines their company’s financial situation.

The report says the business had assets available of £659.

However, there were preferential creditors – those who are paid first – namely nine members of staff owed £5,252 between them.

HMRC was also owed £18,102 as a secondary preference creditor – someone who is paid next.

In addition, there were unsecured non-preferential creditors, those who are paid last – and were owed £60,485 between them.

They consisted of trade creditors owed £33,076 between them; HMRC owed a further £1; nine employees owed a further £27,127 between them and connected creditors owed £281.

This gave a deficit of £83,180.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £100, this gave an overall deficit of £83,280 outstanding, the statement of affairs concluded.
