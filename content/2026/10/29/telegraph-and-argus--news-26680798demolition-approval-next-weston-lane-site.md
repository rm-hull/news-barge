Residents are being invited to have their say on plans to redevelop an old shopping parade - with a new convenience store, two retail units and a dedicated customer car park.

Planning and development consultants Johnson Mowat have launched a public consultation on the proposals ahead of the submission of a planning application to Leeds City Council.

The scheme would see a modern retail building constructed on the site of the former parade at 12-22 Weston Lane in Otley.

The site was granted permission for demolition earlier this month through the council's prior approval process.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21645342.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A CGI image of the development of Weston Lane, Otley *(Image: Johnson Mowat)*

## What are the plans?

According to consultation documents, the development would comprise a larger convenience store alongside two smaller retail units aimed at supporting local businesses.

Developers say the store would provide a wider range of fresh food and everyday essentials, helping residents meet more of their day-to-day shopping needs locally.

The plans also include a dedicated off-street customer car park with separate entrance and exit points.

Developers say this would improve convenience for shoppers while helping to reduce parking pressures on neighbouring residential streets.

Consultation material states that the development is intended to improve access to everyday shopping for residents living on the Weston Lane side of Otley, reducing the need for some journeys into the town centre for groceries and household essentials.

Other anticipated benefits highlighted by the developers include the creation of new jobs, additional opportunities for small businesses through the proposed retail units, and a modern, energy-efficient building designed to serve the local community.

Illustrative images released as part of the consultation show a contemporary single-storey development featuring extensive glazing, landscaped areas and parking spaces positioned in front of the retail units.

A site layout plan indicates the development would sit between Weston Lane and Belmont Avenue.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21645353.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A site plan of the proposed Weston Lane development *(Image: Johnson Mowat)*

## Demolition approved

The proposals follow Leeds City Council's decision earlier in October to approve the demolition of the existing 1924 Neo-Georgian shopping parade under the prior approval process.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21645365.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Weston Lane shops which have been approved for demolition *(Image: Google Maps)*

The decision was made despite objections from local residents, Leeds Civic Trust and ward councillors.

Planning officers stated that concerns about the loss of the building could not be given decisive weight because the prior approval process does not allow authorities to determine whether a building should be retained on heritage grounds alone.

The application had stated that following demolition, the site would initially be cleared, levelled and grassed over pending redevelopment.

Residents can view the consultation material on the proposed redevelopment and submit comments at johnsonmowat.co.uk/public-consultations/weston-lane-otley or by emailing [consultation@johnsonmowat.co.uk.](mailto:consultation@johnsonmowat.co.uk.)

Feedback must be submitted by November 15.
