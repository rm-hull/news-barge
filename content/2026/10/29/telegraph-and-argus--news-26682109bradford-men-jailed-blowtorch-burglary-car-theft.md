Three men have been jailed after using a blowtorch to break into a home and steal a vehicle.

The men, all from Bradford, admitted to burglary and theft offences in Calderdale and were sentenced at Bradford Crown Court on October 28.

The incident took place in the early hours of April 28 at a home in Elland, where the men used a blowtorch to burn through the lock and gain entry.

Once inside, they stole the victim’s keys and wallet before fleeing in the victim’s vehicle.

The car was later abandoned, and all three suspects were arrested following a police chase supported by the Police Dog Unit.

Ellis Ashley, 20, of Lingdale Road, Woodside, Bradford, pleaded guilty to burglary with intent to steal and theft of a motor vehicle.

Luke Linden, 23, of Whitehall Avenue, Wyke, Bradford, and Nicholas Stones, 26, of Crossdale Avenue, Buttershaw, Bradford, admitted to the same offences.

They were sentenced to three years in prison at Bradford Crown Court yesterday.

Calderdale police were able to recover the vehicle stolen and the victim’s wallet and returned them to their owner.

Detective Inspector David Purcell of Calderdale’s District Crime Team welcomed the outcome.

He said: "We welcome the sentences handed down to Ashley, Linden and Stones.

"They are selfish individuals who targeted this home for their own personal gain.

"CCTV and doorbell footage assisted hugely in allowing officers to link these three individuals to the offence.

"Luckily we were able to return stolen property to its rightful owners and we hope the victims of this crime feel reassured that three burglars are back behind bars."
