A much-loved bonfire event is marking 40 years of community celebration.

The Ashlands Bonfire returns to Ilkley this November, promising its biggest and brightest event yet in honour of the special anniversary.

Taking place at Ashlands Primary School on Saturday, November 7, the event has grown from a modest school gathering into a major fixture in the town's calendar, attracting families from across the town and beyond.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21645719.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Photos of Ashlands Bonfire over the years *(Image: Colin Williams)*

A volunteer said: "When I first started helping with the bonfire over a decade ago, my grandson was still at Ashlands Primary School, and I never imagined all these years later we’d still be involved together.

"Back then, I got involved to support his school, but it’s grown into something that means so much more to me.

"To now be working alongside my grandson as an adult is really special.

"We’ve seen the event change and grow over the years, but the community spirit at the heart of it has always stayed the same.

"It’s lovely to think about how many children and families have made memories here over four decades, and how many volunteers have given up their time to keep it going."

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21645722.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Photos of Ashlands Bonfire over the years *(Image: Colin Williams)*

The bonfire is organised and run entirely by volunteers, including parents, school staff, former pupils, and members of the wider Ilkley community.

Volunteers build the bonfire, set up the fireworks display, run food and drink stalls, and ensure the evening runs smoothly.

Cllr Damian Kearns, Mayor of Ilkley, said: "Ashlands Bonfire has been bringing our community together for 40 years, and it’s a wonderful example of what makes Ilkley such a special place.

"It’s much more than a spectacular bonfire and fireworks display – it’s a volunteer-led event that’s created lasting memories for generations of local families while raising vital funds for Ashlands Primary School.

"I’d like to thank everyone who gives their time, skills and support to make the event possible, from the organisers and volunteers to the local businesses who continue to get behind it.

"I encourage everyone to come along, enjoy a fantastic evening and help celebrate this remarkable milestone for one of Ilkley’s most cherished community traditions.

"Organised events like Ashlands Bonfire offer a safer way for families to enjoy spectacular fireworks in a controlled environment, rather than risking the dangers associated with unorganised displays."

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21645723.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Photos of Ashlands Bonfire over the years *(Image: Colin Williams)*

This year’s event will feature the biggest fireworks display in its history, alongside the traditional bonfire, refreshments and a welcoming, family-friendly atmosphere.

Ste Hallam, from Friends of Ashlands, said: "Reaching our 40th anniversary is a real milestone, and it’s wonderful to think about how many families have enjoyed Ashlands Bonfire over the years.

"For some, it’s become a tradition they’ve enjoyed since childhood and now share with their own children.

"Events like this take a huge amount of work behind the scenes, and we’re incredibly grateful to all the volunteers, local businesses and supporters who help make it happen year after year.

"Every penny raised helps us support the school and create better opportunities for the children."

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21645725.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Photos of Ashlands Bonfire over the years *(Image: Colin Williams)*

Funds raised through ticket sales, food and drink, and other activities go directly to Ashlands Primary School, supporting projects, resources and opportunities for pupils.

Organisers are encouraging people to book tickets in advance, as previous years have sold out.

The Ashlands Bonfire will take place on Saturday, November 7, at Ashlands Primary School on Leeds Road, Ilkley.

Gates open at 5.30pm, the bonfire will be lit at 6.15pm, and the fireworks display begins at 7.30pm.
