Amanda and Clive Owen have continued working together on their family's ambitious Yorkshire Dales farmhouse renovation despite separating four years ago.

The couple, who became household names through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, have returned to television screens alongside their nine children in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

Their latest programme has followed the restoration of Anty John's farmhouse, close to the family's home at Ravenseat in Swaledale.

Amanda and Clive announced their separation in 2022, bringing an end to their marriage but not their shared involvement in the family farm.

Since then, viewers have continued to see the pair working together as they balance the demands of farming, family life and the restoration project.

Clive has been seen tackling farming problems alongside son Miles, including dealing with a waterlogged field.

Amanda, meanwhile, has continued her involvement in the farmhouse renovation while also taking part in everyday farming activities.

The series has shown the family dealing with everything from lambing and sheep rescues to the arrival of new puppies at Ravenseat.

Their children have also played an increasingly prominent role, with the older siblings taking on responsibilities and pursuing their own interests.

The family's decision to renovate Anty John's has provided a new focus for the television programme.

The old farmhouse has required extensive work, including improvements to the interior, water supply and accommodation.

As the fourth series reaches its final scheduled episode on November 2, the building is beginning to take shape as a home.

For viewers who followed Amanda and Clive during Our Yorkshire Farm, the new programme has offered a different perspective on family life following their separation.

It has also shown that, despite changes in their personal relationship, they remain connected through their children and farming responsibilities.

However, their continued professional and family cooperation should not be taken as evidence that the couple have rekindled their romantic relationship.

The future of their television programme also remains uncertain.

It is unclear whether another episode of Our Farm Next Door will be produced or whether the programme will return for a fifth series.

Although the fourth series is approaching its conclusion, there has been no confirmed announcement about a further run.

The final scheduled episode will air on More4 at 9pm on Monday, November 2, with previous instalments available through Channel 4.
