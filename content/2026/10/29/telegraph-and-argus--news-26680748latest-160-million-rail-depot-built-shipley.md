A £160 million development that will create 100 jobs has reached a major milestone.

The first steelwork has recently been installed on the TrainCare Centre that is being built in Shipley.

Once up and running, the depot will be the home of Northern Rail’s electric train fleet.

Last Friday, the Transpennine Route Upgrade, the huge infrastructure scheme the depot is a part of, hosted Anna Dixon, MP for Shipley, Councillor Eleanor Thomson, and Michael Sasse, Rail Policy Manager at WYCA, to view the first steelwork installed.

![Shipley MP Anna Dixon visits the Shipley depot site](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21645428.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Shipley MP Anna Dixon visits the Shipley depot site *(Image: TPU)*

## Where is it being built?

The development is being built on the former Crossley Evans scrap yard near Shipley Rail Station.

The purpose-built facility has already received over £160 million of funding, and TPU says it will “serve a critical role in delivering a resilient railway across the north of England.”

![The Shipley Depot site](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21645430.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Shipley Depot site *(Image: T&A)*

## What will it do?

It will enable trains to continue operating on the Airedale and Wharfedale lines while major work takes place in and around the Neville Hill TrainCare Centre during a future phase of planned TRU improvements.

Shipley TrainCare Centre will bring up to 100 skilled jobs to the area, which will remain after the conclusion of the Transpennine Route Upgrade.

![Work on the Shipley Depot](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21645479.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work on the Shipley Depot *(Image: T&A)*

## 'More people in well-paid jobs in the heart of Shipley'

After the visit, Shipley MP Anna Dixon said: “It was brilliant to again visit the Shipley TrainCare Centre and see first-hand the fantastic progress that has been made on its construction.

“Bringing the skilled, high-quality jobs of the future to the Shipley constituency has always been one of my top priorities as MP, and having this facility here will give local people the chance to build long-term careers right on our doorstep.

“And importantly, the benefits aren’t just confined within the depot gates.

"More people in well-paid jobs in the heart of Shipley will mean more spending in our shops, cafés, and businesses, further helping in the revitalisation of the town centre.

“From what I have seen today, I am confident that this development will make a real and lasting contribution to Shipley’s growth, and in unlocking the full potential of the wider region.”

![Work on the Shipley Depot](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21645481.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work on the Shipley Depot *(Image: T&A)*

Adam Sellers, TRU senior sponsor, said: “Great progress is being made our Shipley site.

"We were delighted to have our senior stakeholders see this firsthand, and to provide a detailed update on the benefits this site will bring to not just railway resilience in West Yorkshire, but the local economy.

“TRU is committed to supporting local communities and providing local jobs, and this new depot will ensure we leave a positive lasting legacy in the town.”
