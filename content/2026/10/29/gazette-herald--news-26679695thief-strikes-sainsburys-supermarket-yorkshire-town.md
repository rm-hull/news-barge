A thief has struck at a supermarket in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a person officers want to speak to in connection with the theft at the Sainsbury’s in Leeds Road, Harrogate, on Monday, September 28.

“Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation,” a force spokesperson said.

Those with information are asked to email [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference number 12260193543 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
