A woman diagnosed with breast cancer just weeks after moving to Teesside has praised the medical team who supported and reassured her throughout her treatment.

Fi Oakes, 65, found a lump in her right breast shortly after moving to the area and was later diagnosed with stage one breast cancer at the University Hospital of North Tees.

She said: "The breast unit was fantastic and so reassuring.

"Kerry, the nurse I was assigned to, was wonderful and my surgeon, Mr Dave Fung, was amazing too.

"We felt completely confident that I was in the best possible hands."

Ms Oakes had not yet registered with a GP when she noticed bruising, which she first thought was caused by moving boxes.

When the discomfort continued and she found a lump, she sought medical advice.

She said: "I’ve been incredibly lucky to have been under the care of the breast care unit.

"There have been times when I’ve been worried about how I was healing, so I’d call the unit and they would say, ‘Come on in.’

"I never once felt they weren’t there for me, and that means everything."

During treatment, a nurse recommended Maggie’s Middlesbrough, a cancer support centre at James Cook University Hospital offering emotional, practical and psychological help to anyone affected by cancer.

Ms Oakes already knew the charity after seeing how Maggie’s Newcastle supported her son.

She visited the Middlesbrough centre to find out what help was available.

She said: "The moment I walked in, it felt so welcoming.

"Someone came straight over, asked if I was okay, and made me a cup of tea."

Since then, Ms Oakes has received benefits advice, holistic therapy, wellbeing activities and peer support at the centre.

She said: "In those early days after a diagnosis, it’s so easy to feel vulnerable and isolated.

"I had only just moved to the area and didn’t know anyone locally; my family and besties were miles away.

"Coming to Maggie’s is brilliant because it’s a safe space, and you can be completely open about what you’re going through."

Ms Oakes said expert medical care and emotional support made a major difference during her cancer treatment.

She said: "What worked perfectly was having my expert medical care paired with the emotional and psychological support at Maggie’s.

"I was treated at the hospital in Stockton-on-Tees and have access to Maggie’s in Middlesbrough.

"Having the best medical team looking after your body, and Maggie’s looking after the rest of you, and knowing both teams are working together for you.

"The support offered here is awesome."
