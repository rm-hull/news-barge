The fire service has revealed the cause behind a fire that claimed the life of a much-loved man.

Emergency services were called to a ground-floor flat on St James Street in Heckmondwike on the morning of Saturday, October 17, where 59-year-old Paul Dawkins, known as "Docka" to friends and family, was found inside the property.

Mr Dawkins was pronounced dead at the scene.

## Fire service reveal cause

In an update following the tragedy today, fire investigators have revealed the incident is currently being treated as an accidental fire.

A spokesperson said: "Investigations are ongoing with West Yorkshire Police, but the incident is currently being treated as accidental fire."

Officers had previously confirmed that Mr Dawkins' death is not being treated as suspicious.

## Emergency services rushed to flat

Three fire engines, two from Dewsbury and one from Spen Valley, attended the scene and found the property well alight.

The blaze affected around 20 per cent of the flat and caused smoke damage throughout.

Firefighters wearing breathing apparatus used hose reels to tackle the flames before discovering Mr Dawkins inside the property and handing him into the care of ambulance crews.

Despite the efforts of emergency services, he was pronounced dead at the scene.

## Family's tribute to 'Docka'

Speaking previously, Mr Dawkins' sister, Denise Dawkins, described her brother as a "unique character" who was loved by those around him.

She said he lived life on his own terms and wanted him to be remembered for his kindness and sensitive nature.

Denise said: "He was very true to himself which was inspirational to those around him."
