A police investigation has been launched after bins and trees were set alight in a North Yorkshire village.

Three fire crews were called to extinguish the small fires in Eggborough, south of Selby, shortly after 10.40pm on Wednesday (October 28).

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21644152.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Westfield Close in Eggborough where fire crews were called on Wednesday night (October 28) *(Image: Google)*

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the fires in Westfield Close consisted of “multiple bins, trees and shrubbery”.

“Crews extinguished the fires using two hose reel jets, thermal imagining cameras and lighting,” a service spokesperson said.

“The incident was left for police to investigate further.”
