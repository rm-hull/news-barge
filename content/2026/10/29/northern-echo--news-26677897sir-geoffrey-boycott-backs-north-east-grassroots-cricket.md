Cricket legend Sir Geoffrey Boycott has gone the extra mile to support grassroots clubs and leagues in the North East.

The former England batsman invited league president Chris West and Mark Christon to join him at a recent event in North Shields after the league approached him about its awards evening.

He also recorded a promotional video and signed shirts to raise money for the league.

Mark Christon said: "We contacted Sir Geoffrey and asked if we could arrange to have some shirts signed to raffle at the event.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21169706.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sir Geoffrey Boycott earlier this summer with Maltby Cricket Club sponsor Gary Gill and club Chairman Chris Francis *(Image: Supplied by Goffy Media)*

"It was a joy to meet Sir Geoffrey, and he even made a promotional video for us to use for the event."

Mr Christon said Boycott’s support went far beyond signing shirts.

He said: "Sir Geoffrey is not only one of the very best cricketers and broadcasters, he thoroughly understands the importance of leagues such as the NYSD League and all they bring to the game.

"We are hugely proud of those that have progressed to play at the top level after starting out in our league."

This isn’t the first time Sir Geoffrey has supported grassroots cricket in the region.

Earlier this year, he backed Maltby Cricket Club’s 80th anniversary by signing shirts for a fundraising auction.

Former Darlington and Middlesbrough footballer Gary Gill made the connection through his Chadwicks Inn restaurant in Maltby, a key club supporter.

Mr Christon said: "He is deeply involved in his role as president of Scarborough Cricket Club, where he uses his vast knowledge of cricket heritage and his contacts to make things happen.

"It’s something that Sir Geoffrey does on a regular basis.

"He helped Maltby Cricket Club recently in its anniversary year, and he really is a bit special."

The NYSD League’s annual awards evening will take place at Sedgefield’s Hardwick Hall Hotel on Saturday, November 14.

Sky TV broadcasters Charles Dagnall and Natalie Sawyer will be special guests at the event.
