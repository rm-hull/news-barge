A balaclava-clad rider fled on foot after falling from an off-road bike in front of police officers, with the vehicle later seized by officers who described it as an "absolute menace to the community".

The incident happened in the Heights and Farnley Wortley area of Leeds while neighbourhood policing officers were on patrol.

## **Rider falls from bike in front of officers**

According to West Yorkshire Police's Leeds West Neighbourhood Policing Team, officers were assisting patrol colleagues when they spotted a rider on a Talaria Sting electric motorbike.

The rider, who was wearing a balaclava, reportedly came off the bike in front of officers.

Police said the male rider was not injured in the fall.

## **Suspect runs off**

After falling from the bike, the rider fled the scene on foot before officers could detain him.

Police have not released any details about the suspect.

Enquiries into the incident remain ongoing.

## **Vehicle seized by police**

Officers seized the Talaria Sting following the incident.

In a statement, Leeds West NPT described the bike as "an absolute menace to the community".

Off-road and high-powered electric bikes have become a growing concern for police forces across West Yorkshire, with complaints often linked to anti-social behaviour, dangerous riding and the use of parks, footpaths and residential streets.

## **Appeal over nuisance bikes**

Police regularly carry out operations targeting illegal off-road bikes and e-bikes, particularly in areas where residents have raised concerns about safety and nuisance riding.

Anyone with information about the use of illegal bikes in their area can report it to West Yorkshire Police.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "West Yorkshire Police - Leeds West NPT have assisted patrol colleagues this evening.

"The balaclava clad male riding this Talaria Sting in Heights, Farnley Wortley area unfortunately fell off in front of officers on patrol.

"The male was fortunately uninjured and ran off.

"WYP were obviously obliged to seize the vehicle that are an absolute menace to the community."
