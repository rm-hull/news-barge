Police are investigating after a car burst into flames in a North Yorkshire village.

Firefighters were called after the car caught fire in Barlby, near Selby, at 10.40pm on Wednesday (October 28).

It followed several reports of the vehicle being seen alight in Highfield View, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

A service spokesperson said the crews extinguished the fire using a hose reel jet and breathing apparatus.

"The incident was then handed over to police," they said.
