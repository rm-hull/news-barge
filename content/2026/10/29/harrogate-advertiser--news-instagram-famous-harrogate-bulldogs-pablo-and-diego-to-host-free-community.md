# Instagram-famous Harrogate bulldogs Pablo and Diego to host free community dog walk in Valley Gardens

## Instagram-famous Harrogate bulldogs to host community dog walk 🐶

Harrogate's best-known four-legged social media stars are inviting local dog owners to lace up their walking boots and join them for a free community dog walk.

![Instagram-famous bulldogs Pablo and Diego, from Harrogate, are inviting local dog owners to join them for a free community walk through Valley Gardens as part of Mountain Warehouse’s Great British Walkies campaign](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/29/11/47/PabloDiego.jpeg?trim=0,0,480,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Instagram-famous bulldogs Pablo and Diego, known to thousands of followers as **@bulldogpabs**, will be joined by their owner Ricky to lead the walk on Monday, November 2.

The event will run from 10.30am till 11.30am, setting off from the Mountain Warehouse store before following an approximately 1.5km route through the town's picturesque Valley Gardens.

The walk is part of Mountain Warehouse's new Great British Walkies campaign, which celebrates the everyday adventures dogs inspire their owners to take while encouraging communities to enjoy the outdoors together.

Pablo and Diego have recently been named official Mountain Warehouse 'Ambassadogs', joining a panel of social media-famous pups helping to uncover Britain's best dog walks for the retailer's new Great British Walkies Guide.

The guide, launched alongside Mountain Warehouse's new dog accessories collection, features walks across the UK rated from a dog's perspective, scoring routes on everything from sniff quality and stick availability to zoomie zones, roll-ability and water access.

The Harrogate pair have already reviewed several Yorkshire beauty spots, awarding three paws to Stainburn Forest, four paws to Aysgarth Falls and the coveted five-paw rating to Conyngham Hall.

Now they are encouraging local dog owners and their four-legged companions to come along to the free community walk, meet fellow dog lovers and enjoy one of Harrogate's best-loved parks.

The walk is free to attend, although places are limited and advance booking is required.
