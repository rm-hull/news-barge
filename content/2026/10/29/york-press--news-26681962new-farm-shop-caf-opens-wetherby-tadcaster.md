## 'Manic' start for bustling Yorkshire farm shop and cafe

Local lamb is also available *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

A bustling farm shop and café between Wetherby and Tadcaster has launched to many great reviews.

Shire Farm Shop at Smiddy Hill, Walton, close to Thorp Arch Trading Estate, is owned by the Rawlings family, who were keen to open such a business on the land they own.

A farm spokesman told the Press: “We rear our own meat, we grow our own crops, we employ locally. It’s field to fork in less than a mile.”

The business has been open for three weeks and in that time “it’s really being manic, really busy and everything has been well received.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647311.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plenty of pies on offer! Not to mention bread! *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

When the Press called on Thursday, the car park was nearly full, the inside part of the café was packed and there were also a few outside enjoying the sun.

The business combines a farm shop with a cafe serving hot and cold food, coffee and cakes.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647342.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bustling cafe on Thursday lunchtime. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The shop stocks many local items, larder essentials and drinks, encouraging local growers and makers to get in touch about supplying the shop.

The cafe menu includes an extensive range of dishes such as full English breakfast, eggs benedict, chorizo baked eggs and wild mushrooms on toast.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647310.jpg?type=mds-article-620)More of the shop interior. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Other options include sandwiches, burgers, homemade soup, loaded giant Yorkshire pudding, as well as deli salads and a selection of cakes and sweet treats.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647283.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The shop interior. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Those seeking something lighter can enjoy coffee, tea and cream tea, alongside other refreshments.

Shires Farm Shop aims to offer more than just a place to buy groceries, with facilities including outdoor seating and free parking for visitors.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647352.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The outside area was well-liked *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The site also has electric vehicle charging points and step-free access, while dogs are welcome on leads.

Among those enjoying the outside sun when the Press called were Robert and Jacqui Neilson of East Keswick and friends Howard and Jane McWilliam of Ramsbottom, Lancashire, plus labradors Barry and Betty.

The Neilsons had been to the farm shop before and loved it, which was why they brought their friends to see it.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647360.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jane McWilliam, Jacqui Neilson, Howard McWilliam and Robert Neilson *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Robert said he liked the freshness of the food, the selection in the shop and that you can bring dogs.

He continued: “You can add the location. You can see they have spent a lot of money on this and done a damn good job.”

Howard agreed the food was tasty, great value for money and the location was impressive.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647392.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Damian and Isaac Burns of Boston Spa. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Damian Burns and son Isaac said the business location was fantastic and they liked the café making it a little different to other farm shops.

They had enjoyed a smash burger and three light bites between them.

Damian added: “It’s a lovely place, and the shop offering when you come in. It’s great to sit out here.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21647361.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cafe interior *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

At present, Shire Farm Shop has four stars out of five on Google, based on 48 Google Reviews.

The latest review gave five stars out of five and said: “Had a lovely brunch. Nice little shop and cafe. Definitely will do well.”

It added: “The food was lovely and fresh. Will definitely go again.”

However, some have given fewer stars, complaining about the wait for food.
