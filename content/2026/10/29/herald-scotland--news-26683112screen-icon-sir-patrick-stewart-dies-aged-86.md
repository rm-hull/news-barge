Legendary Star Trek and X-Men actor Sir Patrick Stewart has died at his home in Los Angeles aged 86 following a “brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy Body Dementia”, it has been announced on his social media.

Born in Mirfield, Yorkshire in 1940, Sir Patrick’s more than 60-year career included movies, TV and the theatre including 16 years with the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC).

The actor shot to fame when he originated the role of Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation in 1987.

The sci-fi TV series ran for seven years, with films Star Trek Generations in 1994 and Star Trek: First Contact in 1996.

He also appeared in the musical Let It Be Me, romantic comedy Jeffrey, Julia Roberts-starring thriller Conspiracy Theory and animated film The Prince Of Egypt.

For a new generation, he took on his biggest role on the big screen as the Marvel superhero Professor Charles Xavier in all the first three X-Men blockbusters, which began life in 2000.

He would come back to the series in 2014’s X-Men: Days Of Future Past, 2017’s Logan, 2022’s Doctor Strange In The Multiverse of Madness and 2024’s Deadpool & Wolverine.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21649062.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Star Trek actor Sir Patrick Stewart has died at his home in Los Angeles aged 86 *(Image: Matt Crossick/PA Archive/PA Images)*

He earned a Grammy for best spoken word album for children for Prokofiev: Peter And The Wolf in 1996. He also earned four Emmy nominations for various work and two supporting actor Olivier Awards for Shakespeare plays, the first for Antony And Cleopatra in 1979 and the second for Hamlet in 2009.

Sir Patrick became an officer of the OBE in 2001. He then received his knighthood for services to drama in 2009, and was knighted by the late Queen in 2010.

Kelly Bush Novak, longtime spokesperson for Stewart, said in a statement on Thursday: “We announce with great sorrow the passing of Sir Patrick Stewart yesterday at his home in Los Angeles, in the loving care of his beloved wife Sunny, following a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s Disease and Lewy Body Dementia.

“He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayers, tears, laughter and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to all of us.

“We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace.”

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21649098.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sir Patrick Stewart attending the Star Trek: Picard Premiere held at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square, London. *(Image: PA)*

The statement continued: “A son of Mirfield in the West Riding of Yorkshire, he travelled from the Royal Shakespeare Company to an equally acclaimed career spanning six decades on Broadway, the silver screen and television.

“His sonorous voice and singular talent will forever be synonymous with indelible performances in every genre. He explored the human condition in all its poetic complexity and brought hope not only to the far reaches of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, but into science fiction’s most iconic final frontier aboard the Starship Enterprise.

“His professional accomplishments were humbly matched by his philanthropy and activism on behalf of human rights, survivors of domestic violence, global refugees, veterans’ mental health services, and animal welfare, particularly his cherished foster pitties. He was a Knight, Chancellor, motor sport enthusiast, and lifelong Huddersfield Football fan. He will be remembered for the dignity, compassion and humanity he brought to every role – in his art and life."

Marvel Studios paid tribute to Sir Patrick, describing the actor as “a true gentleman”.

In a message posted on X, the film and TV production company said: “All of us at Marvel Studios are deeply saddened by the passing of Sir Patrick Stewart.

“Sir Patrick brought Professor Charles Xavier to life with extraordinary grace, intelligence, and humanity, defining the character for generations of fans, from ‘X-Men’ to ‘Logan’ to ‘Avengers: Doomsday.’

“He was a singular talent, a generous collaborator, and a true gentleman.

“Our hearts go out to his wife Sunny, his children, his friends, and the countless fans around the world whose lives he touched with his art.”
