A man lost his temper when a woman stepped into an argument with his friend during a drinking night out.

Josh Hutchinson and his friend were rowing during the evening out in Bishop Auckland on Friday, May 2, last year.

The woman, who knew Hutchinson, stepped between the men to try to stop the dispute, Peterlee magistrates were told.

Prosecutor John Garside said Hutchinson apologised, but then pushed the woman and grabbed at her phone, making it fall and smash.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21482380.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Peterlee magistrates were told defendant pushed a woman whose phone dropped to ground and smashed during a night out in Bishop Auckland

The woman said she had never previously seen Hutchinson behave in that manner.

Hutchinson, 25, of Grizedale Road, Peterlee, denied assault by beating and criminal damage but was convicted after a trial in his absence in September.

The conviction out him in breach of a suspended prison sentence imposed in 2024 for theft and burglary, although that sentence has since expired.

The court was told Hutchinson was arrested on a warrant after failing to attend his trial and ended up briefly in the cells.

Amrit Jandoo, defending, said Hutchinson effectively pushed the woman causing her phone to drop.

He said Hutchinson was homeless at the time of the trial but now has settled accommodation, and is in “a better place”, not using illicit substances.

The magistrates asked for a Probation Service report to assess whether Hutchinson was suitable for unpaid work.

He was bailed to return to Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court for sentencing on November 19.

Bench chairman John Marr told Hutchinson to stay out of trouble in the meantime.
