Several North Yorkshire roads are set to temporarily close, beginning next month.

North Yorkshire Council has announced temporary road closures in Slingsby, Pickering, and Swinton for works.

Sycamore Close in Slingsby will be closed from November 2 to November 10 for fuse upgrade works, with a maximum closure period until November 13.

Traffic signs will indicate the closure, and a local alternative route will be provided.

The upgrade works in Pickering will also affect Meadow Road, which is set to close to vehicles from November 2 to November 13.

As in Slingsby, signs will indicate the closure, and an alternative route will be provided.

In Swinton, East Street will be closed for a week between November 2 and December 31 for works, with the same arrangements for traffic signs and an alternative route.

The council has also announced a six-month closure of a public footpath in Pickering.

The footpath, located at grid reference SE78048259, is deemed unsafe due to an unsafe bridge.

Closure signs will be in place, and the closure period may be extended if necessary.

The council has advised residents and road users to check the roadworks map on its website for updates on the closures.

All the closures are subject to access to premises, meaning residents should still be able to access their homes.

For further information, residents and road users can contact OCU Utility Services Limited on 0333 200 0011.

Alternatively, the North Yorkshire Council's roadworks map provides updates on the closures and the progress of the works.
