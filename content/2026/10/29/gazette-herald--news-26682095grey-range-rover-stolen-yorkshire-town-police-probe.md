A car has been stolen in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after the grey Range Rover was taken from Swarcliffe Road, Harrogate, at 9.45pm on Sunday, November 25.

The force said the car, with registration DF66OYS, was stolen without keys.

"Officers are carrying out enquiries and are asking anyone who witnessed any suspicious activity in the area, or who may have CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage that could assist the investigation, to get in touch," a police spokesperson said.

They urged anyone with information to email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be reported anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference number 12260210498 when passing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
