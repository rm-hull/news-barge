A Bradford dairy is set to give away 1,000 bottles of free milk this week as a thank you to the community that has supported its growth.

Levants Dairy will hand out two-litre bottles of farm-fresh milk, together with milkshakes and other products, at the triangle near Lister Park on Friday, October 30.

Explaining the reason for the giveaway, Levants Dairy manager Kaay Khan said: "It's a way for us to give back to the community and for the people that haven't tried us, it's a great opportunity for them to try us as well."

The event will begin at 1.30pm and continue until all products have been distributed.

## Dairy prioritises affordability

Levants Dairy delivers farm-fresh products to homes across Bradford and surrounding areas, offering everything from milk and eggs to cream, butter, honey and freshly baked bread.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21641585.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Levants Dairy *(Image: Levants Dairy)*

Mr Khan said the company's aim had always been to make quality farm products more accessible.

"Our mission has always been simple: to make fresh, high-quality farm products accessible and affordable for everyone, while supporting British farmers and our local community," he said.

The business says it has expanded steadily since its launch, building a loyal customer base through its doorstep delivery service.

Levants Dairy's milk is produced and bottled on the farm before being delivered directly to customers, often on the same day the cows are milked.

## Customers seeking 'less processed' foods

Mr Khan said increasing numbers of customers were seeking less processed food products.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21641581.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Levants Dairy *(Image: Levants Dairy)*

"We've noticed that there are a lot of households moving towards the natural way of life, obviously wanting their products to be less processed," he said.

He added that many customers choose Levants Dairy because of the freshness and quality of its products, and because they value knowing where their food comes from.

Mr Khan said: "For us, it's about more than simply delivering milk.

"It's about reconnecting people with where their food comes from, supporting farmers and bringing communities closer together."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21641583.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Levants Dairy *(Image: Levants Dairy)*

## Giveaway details

As well as collecting free products, visitors to Friday's event will be able to meet members of the Levants Dairy team and ask questions about the company's products, farming practices and home delivery service.

The giveaway is free to attend and no purchase is required.

People can collect a free two-litre bottle of milk, along with a selection of other products, on a first-come, first-served basis while stocks last.

The event will take place at the triangle, near the Manningham and Lister Park entrance on North Park Drive, from 1.30pm on Friday, October 30.
