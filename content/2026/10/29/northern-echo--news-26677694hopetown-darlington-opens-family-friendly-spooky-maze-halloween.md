A new Spooky Maze attraction is giving visitors a frightful start to their ghost train journey.

The family-friendly maze is part of Hopetown Darlington’s Halloween celebrations and is open until November 1.

Designed to entertain visitors of all ages, the maze promises gentle scares and seasonal fun for children and families during the half-term break.

Hopetown Darlington said: "Dare you enter the Spooky Maze?

"Admission is free because you won't need a refund where you're going."

The maze leads to the attraction’s popular ghost train and is filled with surprises along the way.

For those who prefer to skip the scares, an alternative route to the ghost train is available.

The announcement has been met with enthusiasm from the community, with many families already planning visits.

One person commented online that the maze "looks good," while others tagged friends and relatives to suggest a trip before Halloween.

Another wrote that they "have to take" a family member, with several using the comments section to organise group visits.

Hopetown Darlington confirmed that the maze is wheelchair-friendly, with level access and sufficient width for wheelchairs.

The maze is just one part of the site’s wider Halloween offerings.

Hopetown Darlington, located on McNay Street, is one of the town’s major family attractions.

It celebrates Darlington’s railway heritage while hosting seasonal events throughout the year.

Families still have a few days left to take part in the seasonal fun before the Spooky Maze closes at the end of the week.

Hopetown Darlington continues to attract visitors with its blend of history, entertainment, and special events, making it a favourite destination for families in the region.
