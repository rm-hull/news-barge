A Darlington motorist was banned from driving for 16 months after admitting drug-driving offences.

Darren Wheelhouse, 54, of Appleton Close, Westmoreland Street, Darlington, pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court to two counts of driving with controlled drugs above the legal limit.

The court heard that on March 10, he drove a Peugeot, on North Road, Darlington, while the proportion of cocaine in his blood was 11 micrograms per litre and the proportion of benzoylecgonine, a cocaine breakdown product, exceeded 800 micrograms per litre.

Wheelhouse entered guilty pleas on October 23.

Magistrates imposed the obligatory 16-month disqualification and fined him £120 for one offence, with no separate penalty on the second count.

He was also ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £48 surcharge.

A collection order was made for deductions from benefits following an application for benefit deductions in court.
