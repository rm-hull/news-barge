With the weather getting colder, you might be craving a hot Sunday roast to help warm you up.

A Sunday roast can take a long time to cook with many timings to deal with so if you want to make life easier for yourself, you can head to a pub or restaurant for one.

There are plenty of pubs and restaurants in and around Bradford to enjoy a roast in.

If you are heading to the Yorkshire Dales for a long hike soon, you might want to stop off at The Lister Arms for its delicious roast.

## What is The Lister Arms like?

Found close to Malham Cove in the Yorkshire Dales, The Lister Arms might be the perfect spot for a meal after exploring the area.

On Sundays, the pub offers a roast where you can choose from beef, lamb, roast porchetta, and a nut roast.

There is also a trio of roasts that you can enjoy for £29.

The roasts are served with a Yorkshire pudding, carrots, parsnips, roast potatoes, greens, and gravy.

You can also add some sides like cauliflower cheese, mash, pigs in blankets, and stuffing onto your meal.

Before your main, you can enjoy a starter ranging from the soup of the day and ham hock and parsley terrine to homemade sourdough focaccia.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21642393.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The pub can be found in a quaint location. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

You can finish off your Sunday roast experience with a sticky toffee pudding, a chocolate brownie or a cheese board served with oat biscuits, date chutney, and quince.

## What do reviews say about The Lister Arms?

The Lister Arms in Malham has a rating of 4.5 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 1,560 people leaving reviews.

One person wrote: “Excellent stay in the new Lister Farmhouse.

“Quality finish rooms and just a short walk across the road to the Lister’s Arms for delicious food and friendly staff.

“Great location to walk around Malham Cove and surrounding area. Highly recommend.”

Another person wrote: “Beautiful pub in a stunning location with amazing food.

“They were busy when we went and the service was still quick and efficient with friendly helpful staff.

“We went with dogs and it always makes us nervous as we appreciate not everybody loves dogs, but we were made to feel so welcome and the room that’s allows dogs was spotless and really comfortable.
