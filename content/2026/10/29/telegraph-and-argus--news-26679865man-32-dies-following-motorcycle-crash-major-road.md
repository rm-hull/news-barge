A motorcyclist has died following a crash.

Police said emergency services were called to Moor End Road in Halifax at around 2.40pm on Wednesday, October 28, following a collision involving a single motorbike.

A 32-year-old man was given urgent medical treatment at the scene, but sadly died from his injuries.

## Police appeal for information

Officers have launched an investigation into the circumstances of the crash and are appealing for witnesses to come forward.

They are particularly keen to hear from anyone who saw the collision take place or who may have information about the motorbike's movements beforehand.

## Dashcam and CCTV footage sought

Police are also asking motorists with dashcam footage, or anyone who may have captured the incident on CCTV, to contact them.

A spokesperson said: "Any witnesses including anyone who may have footage of the incident or saw the motorbike prior to the collision is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police."

## How to help

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or reporting information online, quoting reference 856 of October 28.

The man's family has been informed and enquiries into the collision are ongoing.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A WYP spokesperson said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a single vehicle collision on Moor End Road, Halifax yesterday (28 October) at around 2.40pm involving a motorbike.

"A 32-year-old man received urgent care by emergency services, but sadly died at the scene.

"Any witnesses including anyone who may have footage of the incident or saw the motorbike prior to the collision is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or reporting it online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 856 of 28/10."
