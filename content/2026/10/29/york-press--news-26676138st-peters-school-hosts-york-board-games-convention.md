## Latest York board games convention celebrates record turnout

A board games convention held at St Peter's School in York has recorded its highest-ever turnout *(Image: York Board Games Convention)*

A board games convention in York has recorded its highest-ever turnout.

York Board Games Convention, held at St Peter’s School, welcomed a record number of visitors in its biggest event yet, with footfall up 44 per cent compared to its biggest previous year.

The event featured expanded gaming areas, a larger board games library, and more trade stands to meet rising demand.

Ryan Willis, convention director, said: "We are blown away by the response to this year's convention.

"To see footfall leap by 44 per cent and sell out completely shows just how passionate and growing the tabletop community is, both locally in Yorkshire and across the UK.

"Hundreds of people of all ages came together to share space, make new friends, and enjoy playing games together."

The weekend featured a full programme of tabletop roleplaying games, including fan favourites like Crash Pandas, alongside social deduction sessions such as Blood on the Clocktower.

Visitors also enjoyed an exclusive preview of Counter Espionage, the upcoming social deduction title from game designer and former Traitor, Ivan Brett.

Mr Willis said: "Beyond the record numbers, the real highlight was the atmosphere."

Organisers have confirmed the convention will return to St Peter’s School in October 2027.
