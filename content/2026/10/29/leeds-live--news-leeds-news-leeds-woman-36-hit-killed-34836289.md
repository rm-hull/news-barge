# Leeds woman, 36, hit and killed by car being chased by police - full statement

## West Yorkshire Police have released details of the tragedy

A woman has been killed after a car involved in a police chase hit her and fled the scene in Leeds.

The woman, 36, was rushed to hospital with serious injuries but sadly died after she was hit on Beckett Street, in Harehills, at 9.52am today, on Thursday, October 29. Minutes earlier, West Yorkshire Police had spotted a black Vauxhall Corsa which failed to stop for officers on Harehills Road, Leeds.

A pursuit was authorised and the Corsa driver hit the woman and failed to stop at the scene. Emergency medical attention was given by officers at the scene.

The Corsa was then found abandoned on Nippet Lane, at least two people in the vehicle left the scene. The vehicle has been seized pending forensic examination. A large cordon has been in place ever since with multiple roads closed.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Enquiries are ongoing to locate the people in the vehicle. The Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have launched an investigation and are appealing for witnesses or anyone with dash-cam to come forward. Due to prior police contact a mandatory IOPC referral has been made."

Anyone who witnessed the collision or who saw the vehicle before or after is asked to contact police on 101 with reference 414 of October 29.
