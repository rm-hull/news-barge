Drivers may face long delays on the M62 this morning following a crash near Rishworth Moor.

The incident happened on the eastbound carriageway between Junction 22 for Rishworth Moor and Junction 23 for Huddersfield shortly before 7am on Thursday morning.

Traffic monitoring service Inrix reported that the crash had been moved onto the hard shoulder, allowing all lanes to reopen.

### Delays built up after collision

Long queues formed on the approach to the scene, with congestion stretching between Junctions 22 and 23.

In an update issued shortly after 8am, Inrix said: "Long delays and queueing traffic due to crash, now on the shoulder on M62 Eastbound from J22 A672 (Rishworth Moor) to J23 A640 New Hey Road (Huddersfield).

"Cameras show traffic has now been released and all lanes have reopened."

### Motorists warned of ongoing congestion

Despite all lanes reopening, delays continued in the area as traffic worked through the backlog.

Separate traffic data showed severe delays on the westbound carriageway, with average speeds of around 15mph and journey times increasing by approximately 13 minutes.

Traffic on the opposite side of the motorway was also said to be slow due to motorists looking over at the incident scene.

### Traffic map shows congestion

A traffic map from the area showed lengthy stretches of slow-moving traffic on the M62 between Rishworth Moor and Huddersfield, with red sections indicating the worst of the congestion.

The AA Traffic Map showed several sections of the motorway were highlighted in red and amber, indicating heavy congestion and slow-moving traffic.
