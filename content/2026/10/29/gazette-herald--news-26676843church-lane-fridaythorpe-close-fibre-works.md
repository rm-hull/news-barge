A road in Fridaythorpe is set to close for new fibre network installation.

Church Lane will be shut from Church Barns to St Mary's Church starting Wednesday, November 18, 2026, from 8am until Friday, November 20, 2026, at 5pm.

The temporary closure is to allow for the installation of ducting and chambers for the new BDUK fibre network by Quickline Communications.

The works are expected to be completed within three days.

An alternative route for affected traffic has been announced via Church Lane, Burdale Lane, B1251 Sledmere Road, A166 Wetwang Road, and Back Street.

Despite the closure to through traffic, access will still be available for residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians.

The order by the East Riding of Yorkshire Council will remain in effect for up to 18 months, or until the works are completed.

For further information, residents can contact Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance at 01482 393939, quoting reference CB.
