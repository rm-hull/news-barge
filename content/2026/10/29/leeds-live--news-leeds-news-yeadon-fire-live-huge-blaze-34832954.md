A large fire has broken out in Yeadon with residents being warned to keep their windows and doors shut.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service has said crews from across the region are at the scene of a a fire at the SBT Waste Transfer Station in Yeadon on Milners Road. Locals are being ordered to keep their windows and doors shut to avoid inhaling the smoke.

As a result of the fire, which started at around 8.30am, traffic disruption is expected in the area. The fire service said: "Crews are currently in attendance at a fire at SBT Waste Transfer Station in Yeadon (LS19).

"Residents in the area are requested to keep windows and doors closed. There may also be traffic disruption on the surrounding roads, so please avoid the area if possible.

"Thank you for your understanding and cooperation."

### Fire service issues update

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "Our crews are still in attendance, but will shortly be scaling back activity to two fire engines.

"Please continue to be mindful of any traffic impacts in the area.

"Thank you for your ongoing patience."

### Fire crews still in attendance

Fire crews are currently still in attendance at the source of the fire.

Crews were called to the scene at 8.30am this morning, on Milners Road in Yeadon.

### Full fire service statement

WYFRS said: "Crews are currently in attendance at a fire at SBT Waste Transfer Station in Yeadon (LS19).

"Residents in the area are requested to keep windows and doors closed.

"There may also be traffic disruption on the surrounding roads, so please avoid the area if possible.

"Thank you for your understanding and cooperation."

### Statement as fire burns at waste centre

A statement from Leeds Recycles, shared by the Guiseley and Rawdon Councillors on their social media page, said: "Yeadon Household Waste Recycling Centre is currently closed due to a fire in the industrial units further along Milner's Road.

"The Fire Service are using water suppliers near our site and do not want vehicles driving over their hoses. As a result, the whole road is currently closed.

"Our staff are on site and ready to open once the Fire Service have reopened the road."
