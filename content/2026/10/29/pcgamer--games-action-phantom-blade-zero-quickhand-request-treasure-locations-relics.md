You first meet Quickhand Zhang in Phantom Blade Zero's Crimson Forest and, assuming you free him, arriving at the Thieves Camp and his first hideout, he quickly recruits you to find his counterparts and unmarked treasure in each region.

I've included videos below of where to find Zhang's hideout and the jade treasure chest locations in each region, plus corresponding text entries that explain what you'll need to do to reach each in terms of battling enemies or grabbing any keys required.

## Crimson Forest treasure locations

* **Reward**: Flying Dagger (Phantom Edge) - Thrown knives that apply poison buildup.

* From the Outlaw Trap bell, head into the cave directly behind you with the butcher and the dogs to grab the Crimson Forest Tunnel Crank. Use it to unlock the grate in the floor near the bell, drop down, descend the ladder, cross the wooden bridge and defeat the second butcher and dogs, then take the left path up a slope to where you'll find the chest.

* This second chest is behind the locked Thieves Camp Dungeon gate. To get the key, progress through the Crimson Forest until you arrive at the Cliffside Lodge. Head upstairs to grab the cellar crank, unlock the cellar, then drop down to where you can spot the corpse with the glowing item (the key) below you. Unlock the gate back in the Thieves Camp to find the chest immediately on your right.

* The third chest is in a cave near the Thieves Camp bell. Exit the camp via the path heading towards Zhang's Hideout, follow the path down around the cliffside into the cave, then fight past the dogs and their master to find the jade chest on your left.

## Village of Lu treasure locations

* **Reward**: Fine Thread (accessory) - Increases the damage and Sha-Chi depletion of summoned objects by 50%.

* From the Well of the Three Puppets bell, head up the stairs to the big house at the very top with the mother puppet who attacks you in the entrance. Turn left, climb the ladder, open the door, and pass through the creepy puppet room to find the chest to the left of the altar at the end.

* Close to the Lady's Grounds bell, which unlocks after you defeat Master Bi Yuan of the Tianxuan Sect. Head right out of the courtyard, climb the wall to your left, and circle around onto the upper level of the Lady's Grounds where you'll find the chest.

* From the Survivors' Courtyard bell, head left out of the entrance, go down the stairs, then descend the rope down the shaft to your right. The room at the bottom is filled with puppet enemies, but also the third jade chest.

## Pangzhen treasure locations

* **Reward**: Goldblaze (Phantom Edge): Essentially a rifle.

* You'll need the Tianxeng Pavillion Key for this first one, which you get by talking to the puppet in the Order's underground tavern to start the Star Performer quest. From the Huang Manor Crossroads, head west down the road with the hanging lanterns and turn left to find the door to Tianxeng Pavillion. Unlock it, taking care of the puppet enemies inside, then take the left staircase to the first floor, and circle around the balcony to find the jade chest in the room at the back.

* For this one, you'll need the You Loong's Deluge Phantom Edge, which you get by defeating You Loong, Leader of the Blackwater Crew in the pond near the Banyan Tree in the far west of Pangzheng. From the Marketplace bell, use the deluge to put out the nearby flaming barrier, head inside, turn right, use the hunter sickle to get up on the rooftops and you'll find a jade chest on a platform just up ahead.

* From the Backstreet Archway bell at the start of Pangzhen's western section, defeat Wu Xuan, The Fallen General boss, then head through the circular gate behind him and up the stairs to find the chest in a small house at the far end of the garden filled with puppet enemies.

* From the Pond of Fading Song bell just before the well where you fight Gu Wanyue, The Wailing Wraith, backtrack along the street to just before the pool, and turn right up a set of stairs, through an archway, into a building where you'll find the final jade chest for this region.

## Meigu Lake treasure locations

* **Reward**: Sha-Chi Blood Catalyst (medicine) - Increases Sha-Chi combo damage by 20% for 15 seconds and restores 20% HP, but ignites you.

* From the Blackwater Crew Dock bell, on the northern island in the lake, head through the arena where you fought You Loong, The Frenzied Blackwater Crew Leader, out the right side via the ramp, then immediately through the house on your right towards a zipline you'll spot ahead of you. Ride this down, hop over the poles, and turn right onto the sand where the chest is located.

* The second two treasures in Meigu Lake require you to unlock a special area via the Secrets at the Heart of the Lake quest. In the northwest of Meigu Lake, you'll find a snowy area with a mirror which can only be unsealed by defeating the four acolyte scarecrow bosses around Wulin, including one in the Crimson Manor, which you'll have to fix the Illusory Pipa to ultimately access. It's quite elaborate, but once you're through the mirror, continue through the area until the second bell, then make your way along the cliffside coffins to find the jade chest on a platform at the end.

* I didn't include this final chest in the video because I didn't want to spoil the area, but it's on your main track through the secret region mentioned above, towards the end of the section where you're navigating across ships. The chest is on the final ship, just before the shrine to the lady that teleports you and is almost impossible to miss.

As mentioned, there is one final area where you'll find Quickhang Zhang, but it's close to the end of the game, and currently still embargoed, so I'll add those details when I can.
