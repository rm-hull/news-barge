Two men have been arrested after a “stolen” Jeep was snared by a stinger during a police chase near Bishop Auckland.

Officers were called to a farm on Tuesday night (October 27) following reports that two men were trying to steal a trailer.

When police arrived, a chase began after a Jeep fled the scene. The drivers tried to reverse ram officers, police said.

Anticipating the Jeep would make its way towards Bishop Auckland, an officer was deployed who stung the car, puncturing all four tyres.

Police continued to follow the Jeep through Bishop Auckland Market Place as its tyres began to shred and brought it to a stop with enough force to snap its axle.

Both suspects attempted to flee on foot but were brought to another stinging stop after being tasered.

Durham Police said two men were arrested on suspicion of theft and making threats to kill, while the one was further arrested on suspicion of dangerous driving, having no licence, no insurance, and failing to provide.

The pair were taken into custody where they remained on Wednesday evening.
