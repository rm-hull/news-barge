A man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted rape after a woman was attacked in the street by a stranger.

A man dressed all in black was said to have threatened the woman before sexually assaulting her in the St Peter's Road area of Wallsend, North Tyneside, at around 8.45pm on Tuesday, October 27.

Northumbria Police confirmed a man has since been arrested in connection with the attack.

A spokesperson for Northumbria Police said: "At about 8pm on Wednesday (October 28), following proactive patrols in the area, a man was arrested on suspicion of attempted rape.

"He is currently in police custody."

Police remained in the area as investigations continue and the force said officers had worked "at speed" to identify and arrest a suspect.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Northumbria Police via direct message, live chat, or by calling 101 quoting reference 26000482347.
