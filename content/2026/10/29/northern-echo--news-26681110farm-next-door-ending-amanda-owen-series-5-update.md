Amanda Owen's popular television programme Our Farm Next Door is approaching the end of its fourth series, leaving viewers wondering whether the Yorkshire Shepherdess and her family will return for more episodes.

The More4 programme, which follows Amanda, her former husband Clive Owen and their nine children, has continued to document the family's ambitious restoration of Anty John's farmhouse in the Yorkshire Dales.

However, with the fifth and final scheduled episode of series four due to air on Monday, November 2, questions remain over the programme's future.

Although there has been speculation that the fourth series could be the last, no confirmed plans for a fifth series have been established.

The programme first arrived on screens in October 2024, marking a television comeback for the family following their success on Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm.

Since then, viewers have watched the Owens tackle the restoration of Anty John's, a derelict farmhouse near their famous Ravenseat home in Swaledale.

The project has involved years of work, with the family battling difficult weather conditions, structural challenges and the demands of running a working hill farm.

Throughout the fourth series, Amanda and the children have continued transforming the property, including painting newly plastered walls, installing a water supply and completing work on the upstairs rooms.

The final scheduled episode will mark another significant milestone, with the interior of Anty John's beginning to take shape as furniture is moved into the property.

The programme will also feature more of the family's everyday lives, including the children spending time at the farmhouse and their continued involvement with animals at Ravenseat.

For viewers who have followed Amanda since the early days of Our Yorkshire Farm, the latest series has provided another opportunity to see how the family has changed.

Her nine children have grown up in front of television audiences, with the older siblings increasingly developing their own interests and responsibilities.

The restoration of Anty John's has also raised questions about the family's long-term plans and what the property could mean for future generations.

There has been no confirmed announcement establishing whether viewers will see another episode or a fifth series.

The final scheduled episode of Our Farm Next Door series four will air on More4 at 9pm on Monday, November 2.
