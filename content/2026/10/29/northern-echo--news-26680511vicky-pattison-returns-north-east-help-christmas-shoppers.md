Finding the perfect Christmas present can be a challenge, even for the most organised shoppers, but North East TV personality Vicky Pattison is stepping in to help for an important cause.

The Newcastle-born radio host will be returning to Newcastle next Wednesday (November 4) to launch a festive campaign in the city centre's popular shopping centre.

Eldon Square's festive campaign, Christmas Central, will see the former Geordie Shore star meet visitors in the shopping centre, helping them to find the right present for loved ones they are struggling to buy for.

Her visit also aims to support two community-focused initiatives, with donations from city centre shops benefitting Cash for Kids’ Mission Christmas and Newcastle City Council’s Evry programme which launched in 2022.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21645143.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Eldon Square has said its Christmas Central campaign is about "making the season easier to enjoy" for shoppers along with supporting important causes. *(Image: Eldon Square/BWP Group)*

Last year, Mission Christmas helped 304,690 children across the UK, raising more than £16.9 million in gifts and cash.

Pattison praised the initiative and said she was "really proud" to be supporting campaigns which help North East families over Christmas.

The former Queen of the Jungle added that she was excited to help Newcastle shoppers with gift ideas, adding: "We all have those people on our Christmas list that we really want to get the right gift for.

"Whether it’s a parent who says they don’t want anything, a friend who seems to have everything, or someone you just want to make feel special, it is easy to overthink it."

Pattison can be found outside Sephora and Next in Eldon Square at lunchtime next Wednesday, with shoppers able to meet the TV star and ask for gift inspiration ahead of the busy Christmas period.

Eldon Square is home to more than 100 brands, and is one of the region’s leading shopping destinations. Pattison said the range of options "makes such a difference" when shopping for gifts.

She said: "You can browse, find something unexpected, and leave knowing you have found a special gift for your loved one while getting plenty of the rest of your Christmas plans sorted too."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21645142.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pattison can be found outside Sephora and Next in Eldon Square at lunchtime next Wednesday (November 4). *(Image: BWP Group)*

The two initiatives supported by Pattison during the Christmas Central campaign, Cash for Kids’ Mission Christmas and the Evry programme, aim to bring festive cheer to young people and families in the North East facing hardship.

Eldon Square retailers such as Boots, MINISO, John Lewis and Menkind have already made donations to ensure that children across the region wake up to something special on Christmas morning.

Pattison said: "Christmas is about making people feel loved, so knowing that the gifts we donate will support so many children across the North East means a great deal.

"The donation box will be here until Thursday, December 17, and any contributions would be greatly appreciated by local families."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21645140.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pattison has praised the initiative, adding she was "really proud" to be supporting campaigns which help North East families over Christmas. *(Image: Eldon Square/BWP Group)*

Helen Cowie, centre director at Eldon Square, said Pattison’s involvement captures the essence of the centre’s Christmas campaign, adding: "We are delighted to welcome Vicky back to Newcastle, and especially proud that the activity will support Cash for Kids’ Mission Christmas and Evry at Newcastle City Council, supporting babies, children and young people.

"At a time of year when a gift can mean so much, we hope the donations will make a heartfelt difference to young people and families across the city."

The 38-year-old TV personality's visit forms part of Eldon Square’s wider Christmas Central campaign, which the centre says is about "making the season easier to enjoy" through its range of the nation's most popular shops throughout the festive season.

This campaign follows another recent fundraiser from Pattison in the North East, after she began her 250-mile Tyne to Thames challenge in Newcastle earlier this month to raise money for Global’s Make Some Noise.

Pattison's return to Newcastle next week will help shoppers with their Christmas lists while bringing hope and happiness to children and families across the region who need it most.

More information about Eldon Square's Christmas campaign, along with Cash for Kids’ Mission Christmas and Newcastle City Council’s Evry programme, can be found online.
