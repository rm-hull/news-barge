Fans are fearing that Harry Potter star Emma Watson may have been hacked after a strange post on social media.

The actress, who starred in the live-action Beauty and the Beast, attended Stagecoach Theatre Arts before finding worldwide fame as Hermione Granger in the Harry Potter films.

The 36-year-old joined Oxford University in 2023 to begin a master's in creative writing and later switched to a DPhil in literature.

It is rare that Ms Watson posts on her social media, with the actress preferring to keep her life private from the cameras.

The actress has uploaded an Instagram post this afternoon but has left fans fearing she may have been hacked.

This upload consisted of a carousel of three photos, all in low resolution and showing shots from the upcoming film Wicker.

The movie, which is released in UK cinemas on Friday (October 30), stars Olivia Colman, Alexander Skarsgård, Elizabeth Debicki, Marli Siu, Nabhaan Rizwan, Richard E. Grant, and Peter Dinklage.
