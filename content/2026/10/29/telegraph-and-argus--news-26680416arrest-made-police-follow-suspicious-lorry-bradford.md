A man has been arrested after two suspected stolen lorries were found in Bradford following a police operation.

Officers carrying out targeted patrols became aware of a suspected cloned DAF tractor unit near Bradford city centre and launched enquiries to trace the vehicle.

## Officers track suspected cloned vehicle

Police located the lorry and followed it to a site on Thornton Road.

When officers arrived, they discovered a second tractor unit at the location and detained the driver.

Further checks revealed that both vehicles had been reported stolen from different parts of the country.

## Two stolen vehicles recovered

One of the tractor units was confirmed to be from the Greater Manchester area, while the other was from Wakefield.

Both vehicles were recovered by police.

A man was arrested on suspicion of theft and enquiries remain ongoing.

The recovery was carried out as part of Operation Steerside, which targets vehicle crime involving commercial vehicles and heavy goods vehicles.
