ON this day last year, Reana Bashir was honoured as the Most Inspiring Businesswoman of the Year at the prestigious Best Businesswomen Awards, held at the Daventry Court Hotel.

Ms Bashir, who leads the award-winning insurance brokerage Coversure Keighley and Coversure Rotherham, expressed her gratitude for the recognition, stating: "I’m absolutely honoured to have received this award. It’s a wonderful recognition of the hard work and dedication that goes into supporting our customers and community every single day."

Her business network, which includes Keighley Financial Services and H&A Legals, aims to provide accessible expert advice and genuine support, helping clients build secure and successful futures.

Debbie Gilbert, the founder and organiser of the awards, remarked on the rigorous judging process, emphasising that each winner demonstrated "exceptional skill, innovation and commitment to excellence."

The Best Businesswomen Awards celebrated the achievements of female-led businesses and charities across the UK, highlighting talent in a diverse range of industries.
