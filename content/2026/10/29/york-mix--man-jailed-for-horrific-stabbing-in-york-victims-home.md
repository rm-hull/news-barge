A man has been jailed for three years for an horrific knife attack in York in which the victim was stabbed five times in his own home.

It happened at a flat in Burnsall Drive, Holgate, just after 6pm on Saturday 9 May when Matthew Watson, 37, was with a group of people who went to the named victim’s home and began kicking his front door and shouting threats through the letterbox

The victim went to investigate and opened the door, when Watson burst in and stabbed him five times with a jagged-edged knife, York Crown Court heard.

#### Burnsall Drive, York

Prosecutor Nick Peacock said that Watson, the victim and the rest of the group had known each other for some time.

The victim had called police to say that a baying group were outside his home “shouting names at him through the letterbox”.

When he looked through the spy glass on his front door, he saw the group, all of whom he knew.

“He decided to arm himself with a kitchen knife to defend himself because matters got out of hand,” added Mr Peacock.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/burnsall-drive-holgate-york-gsv.webp)

“Before he was able to get to the front door, his friend took the knife from him.”

When the victim opened the door, Watson “forced himself through the doorway and asked someone to hold his can of beer”.

He then grabbed the victim by the neck and stabbed him five times to the head, neck and arm using a knife with a jagged edge.

Watson then fled and an ambulance was called for the victim who was taken to Leeds General Infirmary for treatment to five separate wounds to his arm and the back of his head and two lacerations to his head and neck.

It took 24 sutures or stitches to close the wounds, but the victim was discharged from hospital the following day.

## ‘Extremely lucky’

Watson, of no fixed address, was arrested about an hour after the incident and charged with wounding with intent to do grievous bodily harm and carrying a knife.

He initially denied the offences, only to plead guilty to an alternative charge of wounding without intent, as well as the knife offence, on the day his trial was due to take place.

He appeared for sentence via video link yesterday (Wednesday) after being remanded in custody.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/leeds-general-infirmary-2014-wk.webp)

Prosecuting barrister Mr Peacock said that Watson had 42 previous offences on his record including violence.

In November 2023, he received a short jail sentence for two counts of battery.

Defence barrister Nicholas Hammond said there was evidence to show that the knife used by Watson was already at the flat and wasn’t taken to the property by the defendant.

Judge Sean Morris, the Recorder of York, told Watson: “You are extremely lucky that you didn’t cut this man’s jugular or any other vital organ or vein or artery.

“If you had and he had died, you would be looking at a minimum sentence of about 30 years.”

He noted that Watson had “multiple convictions for low-level violence”, but that the knife offence was “in a different league”.

Watson was jailed for three years.
