A man has been charged with burglary after goods worth about £300 were allegedly taken from a Darlington store.

Rhyss Bayley, 22, of no fixed abode, has been charged following an incident at the B&M store in Northgate in the early hours of today (Thursday, October 29).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646060.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Lisa Young)*

Police were called to the store shortly before 2.20am following reports that entry had been forced and goods taken.

**Read More:**

CCTV operators from Darlington Borough Council tracked a suspect to Victoria Road, where a man was arrested on suspicion of burglary.

Bayley has since been charged with burglary and remanded to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court.

He has also been charged with two counts of shoplifting in connection with alleged incidents at Sainsbury’s in Duke Street on Wednesday, October 28.
