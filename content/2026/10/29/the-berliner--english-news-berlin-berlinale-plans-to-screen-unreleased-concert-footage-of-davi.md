The Berlinale has announced a world premiere: its next edition will feature previously unreleased concert footage of pop icon David Bowie (1947-2016). Titled ‘David Bowie ‘Stage’, the film festival announced an “immersive concert experience”. The production is based on newly discovered and restored film footage from the Isolar II world tour of 1978. Fans will experience tracks from his album “Heroes”, which he recorded entirely in Berlin, as never before.

At the world premiere during the first Berlinale weekend in mid-February, the audience will have the opportunity to experience the show on three screens and transparent LED displays, combined with a “live light and sound experience,” the film festival announced. The idea is to place the audience at the centre of a Bowie concert.

Filmmaker Brett Morgen discovered the original camera negatives while working on his documentary “Moonage Daydream” (2022) about Bowie. Tickets will be available in an initial pre-sale on the Eventim platform starting Wednesday (October 28). General ticket sales begin on Friday.

Berlin is closely linked to the biography of the British singer and often appears as a pivotal point in his narrative from 1976 to around 1979. Bowie came to the divided city at that time as an escape from his problems, as well as to recover from drug abuse. The 2027 Berlinale will take place from February 10 through February 21.
