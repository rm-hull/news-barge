Albania's parliament descended into chaos again on Thursday as opposition **Democratic Party MPs set off smoke flares**, blocked the podium and displayed photographs of protesters allegedly injured by police. The disruption came during a vote on** central bank legislation**, which governing** Socialist Party**lawmakers continued despite the protests.** Four opposition MPs were expelled** from the chamber.

The parliamentary protest follows repeated demonstrations in Albania calling for the resignation of Prime Minister **Edi Rama**. Opposition lawmakers** accuse police of using excessive force**against people taking part in the protests, although the allegations are not independently established by the images presented. By bringing photographs of injured demonstrators into parliament,** Democratic Party MPs** sought to draw attention to their accusations against the security forces.

The unrest followed similar scenes on **22 October**, when opposition MPs surrounded the Speaker's podium, displayed photographs of** students allegedly beaten by police**and threw objects at Speaker** Niko Peleshi**. Clashes between rival lawmakers then** forced parliamentary guards to intervene**as** microphones were torn from desks and chairs overturned**.
