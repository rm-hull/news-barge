A car caught fire after its engine overheated on a West Yorkshire roadside.

The fire broke out on Tanhouse Hill in Elland at 12.40pm on Wednesday.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 12.40pm today to reports of a car on fire on Tanhouse Hill, and one crew attended.

"The car engine was alight, and firefighters extinguished this using breathing apparatus and a hose-reel jet.

"It’s been recorded as a result of the car overheating."

Social media images showed the vehicle’s engine compartment engulfed in flames at the side of the road.
