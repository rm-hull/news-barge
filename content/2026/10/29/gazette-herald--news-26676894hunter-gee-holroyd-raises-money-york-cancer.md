A charity quiz night has raised nearly £3,000 to help fund cancer care and support services.

The event, organised by York accountancy firm Hunter Gee Holroyd, took place at York Sports Club on October 8 and raised £2,882.11 for York Against Cancer.

The funds will help provide care for patients and families in York and East Yorkshire.

Mark Grewer, managing director at Hunter Gee Holroyd, said: "We’re very pleased with the turnout and everyone’s generosity.

"York Against Cancer offers vital care and support to children and families throughout Yorkshire, and after a few last-minute donations, we’re incredibly happy that our Quiz Night raised enough to assist with their important work."

A total of 72 guests across 18 teams took part in the quiz, which included challenges such as Beat the Buzz and Chopstick Challenge.

Raffle prizes were donated by businesses including Middlethorpe Hotel and Spa, Thirsk Racecourse, and Dean Court Hotel.

Nicola Doherty, a representative from York Against Cancer, said: "It was such a wonderful event to attend and be part of.

"We are immensely grateful to everyone at Hunter Gee Holroyd for organising such a fantastic event."

The money raised could fund a month of the charity’s radiotherapy transport service to Leeds.
