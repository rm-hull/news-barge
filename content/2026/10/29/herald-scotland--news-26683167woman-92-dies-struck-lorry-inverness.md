Police are appealing for information following a fatal crash involving a pedestrian and a lorry in Inverness city centre.

Emergency services were called to the incident on the city's Academy Street around 9:50am on Thursday, October 29.

The 92-year-old woman was pronounced dead at the scene. Her next of kin are aware.

Academy Street was closed at Crown Road and Chapel Street for seven hours while officers carried out enquiries. The road reopened at 5pm.

Sgt Calum MacAulay said: “Our thoughts are with the woman who died at this difficult time.

“Extensive enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances and we’re appealing for anyone with information to come forward.

“If anyone witnessed what happened and is still to speak to officers, or may have dashcam footage of significance, please get in touch.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 0879 of Thursday, 29 October, 2026
