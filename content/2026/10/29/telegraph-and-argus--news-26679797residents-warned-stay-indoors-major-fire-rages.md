Residents have been urged to stay indoors after a major fire broke out.

Crews from across West Yorkshire were called to the SBT Waste Transfer Station on Milners Road in Yeadon at around 8.30am on Thursday morning.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said firefighters remain at the scene tackling the blaze.

## Warning issued to nearby residents

People living nearby have been advised to keep their windows and doors closed while crews work to bring the fire under control.

In an update, the fire service said: "Crews are currently in attendance at a fire at SBT Waste Transfer Station in Yeadon (LS19).

"Residents in the area are requested to keep windows and doors closed."

## Traffic disruption expected

Motorists have also been warned of potential delays around the area as emergency services deal with the incident.

The fire service said there may be disruption on surrounding roads and urged people to avoid the area if possible.

## Crews from across West Yorkshire attend

A large emergency response remains in place, with fire crews from stations across West Yorkshire attending the scene.

At this stage, no information about the cause of the fire or any injuries has been released.

The incident is ongoing.
