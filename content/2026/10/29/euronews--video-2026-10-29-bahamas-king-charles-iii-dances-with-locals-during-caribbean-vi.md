The **British king** joined in the celebrations,**dancing to the rhythm of the traditional Junkanoo festival** before watching performers and decorated floats pass through the streets. Footage shows**Charles dancing during the parade** in an unusual moment of informality during the**royal visit to the Bahamas**.

After the parade, **Charles walked through Nassau's Parliament Square**, stopping at market stalls to** meet local vendors** and examine handmade goods such as woven raffia baskets.

The king also **greeted members of the public** who gathered to see him.**Junkanoo** is a longstanding Bahamian tradition celebrated through elaborate costumes, music, dancing and street processions.

The Bahamas **gained independence from Britain in 1973** but**remains a Commonwealth realm with Charles III as its head of state**. His visit brings together the country's cultural heritage and its** continuing constitutional ties to the British monarchy**.
