Three cars and a lorry crashed on the A64 in North Yorkshire this afternoon.

Emergency services were called to the incident at East Heslertonat** 2.59pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “All persons were out on arrival.

#### Crash location

“Crews completed scene safety and used an environmental pack to stem and contain fluid leaking from one of the vehicles.

“The incident was left in the hands of the police.”

The incident has caused long delays on that stretch of the road this afternoon.
