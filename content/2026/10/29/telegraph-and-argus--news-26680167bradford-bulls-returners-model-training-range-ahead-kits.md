BRADFORD Bulls have already announced a timeline for their three kits ahead of next season.

But they have taken advantage of some of their younger first-team players being back at Tong to reveal their training wear range first.

Pre-season has not officially restarted yet, but the likes of Jamie Gill and Eliot Peposhi, as well as new signing Nathan Connell, have been getting ahead.

And they have been modelling the Principal Training Range, which went on sale to fans this morning.

The range includes vests, t-shirts and jackets in club colours, and it helps whet the appetite for the bigger reveals.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21644675.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nathan Connell in Bulls' new training jacket. *(Image: @OfficialBullsRL)*

CEO Jason Hirst has already confirmed that the new home kit will be launched at next month's season launch event, as was the case last year.

The exact date for that event has not yet been confirmed, but it will also give supporters the chance to meet the first-team squad, as well as the chance to ask players, coaches and club officials questions.

Bulls have made eight signings so far for 2027, but more should be on the way, with the club in the market for key positions like half back and hooker.

Jayden Nikorima and Rowan Milnes are the only senior halves in the squad after the release of Joe Keyes, Riley Dean and Chris Atkin.

Names like Hull FC's Aidan Sezer and Manly Sea Eagles' Jamal Fogarty have been thrown around, but as Hirst told the T&A last month, all transfer rumours must be taken with a pinch of salt.

With Mitch Souter departing, leaving Andy Ackers as the only senior hooker, Cole Geyer had been reported as a possible replacement, but he has now joined Catalans for 2027.

The former Oldham man will therefore not be running out in Bulls' new away kit, which is due to be released in time for Christmas.

And Bradford will also be producing an LV27 kit ahead of their trip to face Leigh in Las Vegas next February.

That will be their third and final kit for 2027, instead of a new number for Magic Weekend like this year.
