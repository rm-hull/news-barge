Police are investigating the attempted kidnapping of a woman in York.

It happened on the **A19** into**Fulford Interchange** at**2pm on Monday** (26 October).

The driver of a white Volkswagen Polo was involved in the attempted kidnap of a woman in her 40s.

#### Fulford Interchange

A man in his 40s has been arrested and charged with kidnap and assault.

He has since been released on conditional bail while police enquiries continue.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It is understood that the suspect and victim are known to each other, and there is not believed to be any risk to the wider public.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed this incident or has any dashcam footage of the vehicle during this time.”
