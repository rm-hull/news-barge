Police are investigating following a number of fires in the same village street last night.

York, Tadcaster and Pontefract firefighters tackled several small fires involving bins, trees and shrubbery in **Westfield Close, Eggborough**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished the fires using two hose reel jets, thermal imagining cameras and lighting.

#### Westfield Close, Eggborough

“The incident was left for police to investigate further.”
