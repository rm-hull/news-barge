A woman has died following a hit-and-run involving a car that was in a police chase.

The incident happened on Beckett Street in Leeds at around 9.52am this morning after a black Vauxhall Corsa reportedly failed to stop for officers on Harehills Road.

Police say a pursuit was authorised, and the car was in collision with a pedestrian before being abandoned on Nippet Lane.

Despite emergency treatment at the scene, the 36-year-old woman later died in hospital.

The force added that at least two people fled from the vehicle, which has since been seized for forensic examination.

The Homicide and Major Enquiry Team has launched an investigation and is appealing for witnesses.
