A disqualified driver who led police on a chase has received a suspended prison sentence after admitting two motoring offences.

Nathan Hendry, of Wanstead Crescent, Chester-le-Street, pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court to driving while disqualified and using a vehicle without third-party insurance.

The offences happened on July 12, when Hendry drove a Vauxhall Meriva, on the A167 while disqualified from holding or obtaining a driving licence.

The insurance offence covered use of the same vehicle on the A167 and A1.

On October 26, magistrates imposed an eight-week prison sentence, suspended for 18 months.

The court said the offences were serious enough to justify custody and referred to a prolonged period of bad driving and a "police chase in a built-up area".

Hendry was also disqualified from driving for 24 months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

There was no separate penalty for the insurance offence.
