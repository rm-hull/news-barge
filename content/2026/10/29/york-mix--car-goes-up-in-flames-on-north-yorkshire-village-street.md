Multiple people reported a car fire in a village street last night (Wednesday).

Firefighters from Selby were called to the incident on Highfield View in Barlby at 10.40pm.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished the fire using two breathing apparatus and one hose reel jet.

“The incident was then handed over to police.”
