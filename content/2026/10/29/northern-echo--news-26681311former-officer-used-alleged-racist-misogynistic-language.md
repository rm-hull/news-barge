A former police officer who allegedly used racist and misogynistic language while interacting with women on dating apps is set to face a gross misconduct hearing.

Former Northumbria PC Tim Bartley allegedly made misogynistic comments and used disablist and racist language while using dating apps between 2019 and 2024.

The allegations concern the former officer's off-duty conduct.

The hearing notice alleges that, between 2019 and 2024, he used dating apps in his private life and made misogynistic comments while using disablist and racist language.

His alleged conduct breached professional standards on authority, respect and courtesy, equality and diversity, and discreditable conduct.

The allegations, if proven, are classed as gross misconduct.

The complainants have also applied for anonymity during the proceedings.

Assistant Chief Officer Joscelin Lawson, chair of the misconduct panel, has received an application from the Appropriate Authority to prevent publication of information identifying the complainants.

The chair will decide whether to grant the orders at the start of the hearing.

The public misconduct hearing at Houghton Police Station is due to begin at 10am on November 23 at Old Magistrates Court, Houghton Police Station, Dairy Lane, Houghton-le-Spring.
