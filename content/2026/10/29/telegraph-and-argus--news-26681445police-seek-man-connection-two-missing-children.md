An urgent hunt is underway to find a man wanted for breaching a non-molestation order.

Kibriya Ahmed, 42, is being sought by West Yorkshire Police in connection with his two missing children, Yousaf, 15, and Mohammed, 12.

The boys were reported missing on Saturday, October 24, from Liversidge in Kirklees.

Yousaf is described as 5ft 11ins, of medium build with short black hair and black facial hair.

Mohammed is described as 4ft 9ins, of slim build with short black hair and glasses.

A non-molestation order prohibits Mr Ahmed from having unsupervised contact with the children.

West Yorkshire Police believe Mr Ahmed may know their whereabouts.

A spokesman for the force said: "Officers believe Ahmed may know the whereabouts of his two children and have conducted extensive arrest enquiries in order to try and locate him but are now appealing for the public’s help."

Mr Ahmed is known to have links to Rochdale and Manchester.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police on 101 or via the Live Chat facility, quoting reference 13260586098.

Alternatively, members of the public can call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
