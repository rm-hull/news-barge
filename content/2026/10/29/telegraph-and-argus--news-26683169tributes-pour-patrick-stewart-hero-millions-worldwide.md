Tributes have been pouring in for celebrated actor Sir Patrick Stewart.

Sir Patrick, who was born and bred in Mirfield, has died aged 86.

He was best known for appearing and leading in the X-Men film series and the Star Trek franchise.

Brighouse man Stephen Naylor, who used to work at a radio station in Huddersfield, paid tribute to Sir Patrick on X.

Mr Naylor said: "Had the privilege of meeting Sir Patrick several times while working on the radio in Huddersfield.

"He was a huge global star yet always found time to return to West Yorkshire, and use his success for the benefit of his home and the community which, as he frequently said, made him. Interviewing him was one of those ‘pinch me’ moments and he was a true gent. RIP.

Sir Patrick was also a supporter of Huddersfield Town football club, and tonight the club paid tribute to him.

A spokesperson for Huddersfield Town said: "All at Huddersfield Town are deeply saddened to learn of the passing of lifelong supporter Sir Patrick Stewart.

"Our thoughts are with his family and loved ones at this sad time.

"A titan of his craft and a legend and hero to millions worldwide, his legacy will live on.

"Fiercely proud of his Yorkshire roots, Sir Patrick was a Terrier in every sense, and we’re proud to have counted him as one of our own.

"Once a Terrier, always a Terrier."

Residents on the on the Mirfield Matters Facebook page shared tributes of Sir Patrick.

One one post, a man said: "One of our own RIP Patrick."

Another person wrote: "Mirfield forever remembers the legend himself."
