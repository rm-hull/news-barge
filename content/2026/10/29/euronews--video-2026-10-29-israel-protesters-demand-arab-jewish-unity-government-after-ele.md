**Left-wing activists blocked roads** as they marched through the city, prompting**police officers to move in and disperse them**. Demonstrators confronted police, chanting slogans and sitting down on the road, while others** trampled on a campaign poster of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu**.

Some shouted **"Ben Gvir lost!"** in reference to far-right politician**Itamar Ben-Gvir**, while a placard in Hebrew declared**"Arabs and Jews - Only together will we win"**. The march reflected calls among the demonstrators for** Jewish and Arab political cooperation** to break the electoral deadlock.

The **inconclusive result** has left**Netanyahu and opposition parties searching for a parliamentary majority**, with parties representing** Israel's Arab population**potentially playing a decisive role in** coalition negotiations**.

Protesters argued that a **government involving both Jewish and Arab parties** offered a path to political change. Others carried a banner**accusing Israel of being an apartheid state** rather than a democracy, reflecting their criticism of the country's political system.

As demonstrators continued marching with megaphones and banners, police maintained a presence along the route. **No governing coalition had emerged** from the election result.
