York fish and chip favourite Millers have won a national award.

The popular fish and chip company, which locations in Haxby and Shambles Market, took an honour at the Marine Stewardship Council (MSC) and Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC) UK Awards at Fishmongers’ Hall in London last night (Wednesday).

They took the national sustainability communications award for championing sustainably sourced fish and having a huge impact through their sustainability work.

It’z the first time this award was introduced.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SPL-MSC-AWARDS-025-Picture-by-Daniel-HamburyStella-Pictures-Ltd.webp)

After receiving the gong on stage, David Miller, said: “We’re very proud to be MSC certified. It’s so important for young people to understand the importance of sustainability so we can continue to have fish into the future.”

Millers featured on Channel 5 News earlier this year when David and his team talked about sustainability in a piece about Icelandic cod and the importance of being MSC certified.

Millers only serve MSC certified fish. The company also regularly shares videos on their social media that champion the importance of protecting the ocean.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SPL-MSC-AWARDS-Picture-by-Daniel-HamburyStella-Pictures-Ltd.webp)

George Clark, programme director MSC UK and Ireland, said: “Millers is an outstanding example of how food service businesses can use sustainable seafood to make a real impact.

“Whether through innovative menu development, customer engagement, community outreach or national media coverage, they’ve gone beyond simply serving MSC certified seafood to actively championing sustainable fishing and helping consumers understand the impact of their choices.

“Their passion, commitment and leadership make them thoroughly deserving winners.”

Among the other awards presented on the night, Tesco was the Gold winner in the MSC UK Supermarket of the Year category while Sainsbury’s scooped ASC UK Supermarket of the Year.
