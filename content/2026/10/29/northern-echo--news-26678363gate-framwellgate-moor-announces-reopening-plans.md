A County Durham pub is preparing to reopen its doors under new management.

The Gate, in Framwellgate Moor, said it was “gearing up to open our doors very soon”.

The announcement marks the next chapter for the pub after previous operator Kira Leisure confirmed in June that it would be stepping away from the business.

The new operators said: “The Gate is back.

“We can’t wait to welcome you all back through the doors and bring this proper local pub right back into the beating heart of our community.

“Expect great vibes, a warm atmosphere, and your new favourite local hangout.”

The business is recruiting staff for a number of roles.

No opening date has been announced. Further updates are expected to be shared on The Gate's social media pages.

In a farewell message shared on June 6, KIRA Leisure thanked customers for their support and reflected on its time running the pub.

The company said: “This pub has been so much more than just a business to us – it’s been a community, and we’ve been privileged to be part of it.”

KIRA Leisure also paid tribute to its staff, thanking both past and present employees for their “hard work, dedication, passion, and commitment”.

At the time, the operator expressed confidence in the future of the venue, telling customers it would be handing the keys over to a “fantastic new operator” who would continue to help the pub thrive.
