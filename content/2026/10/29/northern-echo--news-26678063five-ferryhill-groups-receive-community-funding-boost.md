Five community groups in Ferryhill have received a funding boost to help improve facilities, activities and opportunities for residents.

The grants were awarded through Ferryhill Town Council’s Members Initiative Fund (MIF), which supports projects that benefit the community.

Councillor Curtis Bihari, Mayor of Ferryhill, said: "I am delighted that the Town Council has been able to support five worthwhile projects through this year’s Members Initiative Fund.

"Projects receiving funding this year are all very different in nature, but each offers a unique benefit to the Town."

Endeavour Woodcrafts CIC received £500 to purchase new tools for adults with learning disabilities and mental health needs, supporting both day services and community craft classes.

The Humble Garden CIC was awarded £1,500 for its Community Garden Improvement project, which will fund new fencing, pathway materials, and upgrades to seating areas. The improvements aim to improve health & safety issues across the garden and make the community garden a safer place for users to visit and enjoy.

Miner’s Utd Football Club received £1,000 to purchase a new electric spray line marker.

The equipment will allow the club to mark out multiple pitches in the same area in different colours, enabling more matches to be played and supporting the growth of its teams.

Friends of Astrid Academy of Movement also received £1,000 to cover training, coaching, travel, and competition costs for four young female athletes from Ferryhill who have been selected to represent Team GB at an international event in Portugal in July 2027.

Ferryhill Wheelers Cycling Club was awarded £500 to support its Centenary Presentation Evening and Charity Fundraiser.

The funds will help cover the cost of catering, room hire, and a guest speaker, with the event also raising money for the Great North Air Ambulance.

The Members Initiative Fund has now closed for this year but is expected to reopen for applications in May 2027.

Further details will be shared on the council’s website and Facebook page in the new year.
