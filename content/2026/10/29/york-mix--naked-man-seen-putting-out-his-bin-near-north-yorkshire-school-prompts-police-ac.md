Police are investigating reports of a man putting his bins out while naked.

A number of people have reported seeing the unclothed man yesterday morning (Wednesday).

It was on School Garth in Thirsk.

#### Map location

One parent said “a car full of kids” had seen him during their trip to school. Thirsk School and Sixth Form College is nearby.

Another said they had been to speak to him after their children were “traumatised” from seeing “a wrinkly old naked man parading around with his bin” when they looked out of their bedroom window.

The behaviour has been reported to police.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We received a report relating to this incident at 8.40pm last night and enquiries remain ongoing.

“Anyone with information that could assist our enquiries is asked to contact us online via our website.”

They asked people to select ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘an existing case or report’.

“Live Chat is also available through our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101. Please quote reference 12260212514 when providing information.”
