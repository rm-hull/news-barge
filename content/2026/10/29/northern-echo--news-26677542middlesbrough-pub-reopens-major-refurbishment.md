A popular Middlesbrough pub has reopened after a major facelift designed to improve the experience while keeping its community feel.

The Coulby Farm in Coulby Newham welcomed guests back after a three-week makeover on October 22.

It now has refreshed spaces throughout, while maintaining the pub's familiar atmosphere.

Management hopes the investment will appeal to both regulars and new visitors.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641352.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The newly refreshed bar at The Coulby Farm, offering a range of beers, lagers and ciders on tap.

Customers can enjoy a range of food and drink offers, with menu highlights including the 'K-POP Crunch', crispy sweet chilli chicken and pork tomahawk. Desserts on offer include the caramelised waffle.

Sarah Lowther, general manager of The Coulby Farm, said: "We're incredibly proud to welcome everyone back.

"The refurbishment has given the pub a completely new feel while keeping everything our guests love about it."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641350.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The refurbished darts area at The Coulby Farm in Middlesbrough following the pub's recent three-week makeover.

She added: "Seeing familiar faces return and new guests walk through the door has been fantastic, and we can't wait to make even more memories with the local community."

Alongside its new-look surroundings, the pub will continue to host a programme of regular events and entertainment.

Big screens will show live sport, and the weekly pub quiz starts at 7pm every Monday.

The venue also hosts live music and community events throughout the year.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641349.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A renovated seating area inside The Coulby Farm following its major refurbishment.

Rock and Roll Bingo will be held every Wednesday evening from 7pm, while bingo sessions are scheduled for Thursday afternoons from noon.

Poker Night returns on Sundays from 5pm, and families can also make use of the refurbished Fuzzy Ed's play area, which opens from 9am.

The upgraded children’s play area now includes TVs and a dedicated party space for hire for birthdays and other celebrations.

The Coulby Farm is part of the Sizzling Pubs chain, which operates more than 200 pubs across the UK.

Food is served daily from 9am until 9pm.

The pub has also been granted extended opening hours, closing at 11pm from Sunday to Thursday and midnight on Fridays and Saturdays.
