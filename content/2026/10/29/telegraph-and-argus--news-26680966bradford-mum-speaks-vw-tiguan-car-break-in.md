A Bradford mum says her family has been left living in fear after yobs targeted her car in the early hours of the morning.

Police are appealing for witnesses after two individuals tried to break into a Volkswagen Tiguan on Cooper Lane in Buttershaw at around 12.05am last Saturday.

The resident, who has asked to remain anonymous, said the incident is part of a wider pattern of vehicle crime that has left local residents frustrated and concerned.

## 'It was gone within minutes'

CCTV footage shows two individuals targeting the vehicle.

They then scarper the scene when the car's alarm goes off.

After sharing footage online, the owner said a number of other residents came forward claiming they had experienced similar incidents, including reports of thefts in the Low Moor area.

She said: "It just seems to keep happening."

The resident added that comments on social media suggested a number of Volkswagen owners had recently been targeted.

## Family left shaken by ordeal

The mum said the incident has had a lasting impact on her family.

She said: "I've got a terrified eight-year-old who wakes up because she's absolutely convinced they're going to come through the door next time."

The woman added that the emotional impact of vehicle crime is often overlooked because, while possessions can be replaced, the sense of security is much harder to restore.

## 'Enough is enough'

The resident praised the officers who initially attended the scene but believes more needs to be done to tackle repeat offending.

"It just doesn't feel like there's that urgency," she said.

"We work hard for the things we've got and people feel like nothing is being done about it."

She wants increased police patrols and a greater focus on gathering CCTV evidence.

## Police appeal for witnesses

West Yorkshire Police is appealing for information following the incident.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Officers would like to speak to anyone who has information about or footage showing the incident.

They added: "Two suspects, described as male, made off in the direction of a Londis store in the aftermath of the incident."

Anyone who has information or footage which could assist the investigation is asked to contact Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team via 101 or online, quoting crime reference 13260605306.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
