A Romanian man who made his FOURTH illegal entry into the UK has been jailed – and faces being sent back to his homeland on release.

Alexandru Rostas was driving a car on the M62 in September when he was spotted and recognised by police.

The 25-year-old is now beginning a 16-month prison sentence and faces being deported on release.

Bradford Crown Court heard how, after being deported in February 2024, Rostas illegally entered the UK on two occasions.

He was made subject to deportation orders on January 14 and July 6, 2025, but then returned to the UK last month.

How he came back into the country is unknown.

When he was arrested Rostas, of County Street, Oldham, provided a false date of birth, but his identity was later established and it was revealed that he was in breach of a deportation order.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21645532.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The M62 Summit on Saddleworth Moor *(Image: T&A Camera Club: Clockwork Photography)*

He appeared in court on a charge of having knowingly entered the UK before September 23 in North Yorkshire in breach of the order issued in February of 2024.

The court heard that he had 13 previous convictions for 23 offences including for dishonesty and drugs.

In mitigation the court heard that Rostas’s “unsophisticated” methods of coming illegally into the UK were by road or airport, and that it was accepted and conceded that such behaviour came at a cost.

The court heard that Rostas has lived in the UK for over 20 years and considers it his home as all of his friends and relatives are in this country, not in Romania where he has no links.

He was said to be married with young children, all of whom live legally in the UK.

In Romania he was homeless with no benefits or access to facilities such as a GP, and that being away from his family had affected his mental health.

He was said to have breached the order “out of sheer desperation”.

Sentencing Rostas, Mr Recorder Patrick Palmer said there had been “obvious planning” around his illegal activity and described his repeated offending as “persistent”.

He told Rostas: “You know that it is a serious thing to enter a country in breach of its regulations, and that is what you have done here again and again and again.”

He added: “It may be once again that you are made the subject of a deportation order [after serving your prison sentence].”
