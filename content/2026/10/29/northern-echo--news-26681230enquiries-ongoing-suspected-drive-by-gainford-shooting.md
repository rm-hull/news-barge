A police probe into a suspected drive-by shooting 'murder bid' in a quiet County Durham village which left a man critically ill remains ongoing.

The 46-year-old was shot in the abdomen with birdshot at a home on Balmer Hill, Gainford, on the evening of Saturday, August 29.

Police were called at about 8.10pm after reports that a car had arrived at the address and shots had been fired.

Six people were arrested in connection with the suspected attempted murder in the days following the shooting.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21389852.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and CSI at the scene on Sunday, August 30 *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Three men, aged 32, 31 and 26, were arrested on suspicion of attempted murder, while a 31-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of conspiracy to murder.

A 39-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy were also arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender.

A 20-year-old man and another 16-year-old boy were initially arrested following the shooting. Both have since been released while police carry out further enquiries.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21389797.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The smashed window after 'shots were fired at the home' *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The last update in September confirmed the man was in a 'critical but stable' condition, no further update has been issued on his condition.

Durham Police said that enquiries remain ongoing into the incident and no further arrests or charges have been made.

A red convertible Jaguar was found abandoned around 50 minutes after the shooting. Police believe the soft-top F-type cabriolet was used in the attack.

Neighbours reported hearing two loud pops before seeing a red soft-top car driving away.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21389847.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

One resident said: "I was just sat watching the TV. They were talking about Newcastle on Sky Sports News, then we heard like a pop.

"At first I thought it was a car backfiring. We jumped.

"And then there was another pop, and it was a case of 'What the heck was that?' Then the next thing we saw was a red soft-top car go down the road.

"After the pops, I looked along towards next door and we saw all the glass that was outside, and the front windows were gone. It is something you see in movies or television.

"You don't expect to see it in a little village, in your own of street."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21389828.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and CSI are at the scene of a house on Balmer Hill, Gainford *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Another neighbour, Jenny, who has lived on the estate since 1973, said: “It’s a very quiet estate usually.

"You never get anything like this.”

A spokesperson for Durham Constabulary said: "Enquiries remain ongoing."

Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting incident reference 479 of August 29.
