A County Durham councillor is upgrading play areas across his division, with the latest project giving a much-loved play train a new lease of life.

Cllr Craig Martin visited Riverside Park in Chester-le-Street with his son, wearing a railwayman’s uniform to inspect the improvements.

Cllr Martin said: "This is another step in my campaign to upgrade all of the playgrounds in the council division I represent—and now we have improvements for Riverside Park, which is used and loved by families across Chester-le-Street."

The Riverside Park revamp is part of his campaign to improve play areas for children and families.

He said: "Since becoming a councillor, I have campaigned to keep investment in playgrounds on track: It's one of the best-value things a council can do to provide free, fun, outdoor activities for our children.
