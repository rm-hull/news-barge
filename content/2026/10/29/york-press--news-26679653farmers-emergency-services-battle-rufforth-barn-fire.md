Farmers joined emergency services in tackling a barn fire in a village near York.

Three fire crews were called to the blaze in Bradley Lane, Rufforth, at 7.35pm on Wednesday (October 28).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the fire involved a grain dryer.

“Crews, with the assistance of farmers, transported the grain dryer to open space and emptied the contents in order to extinguish the fire,” a service spokesperson said.

They said the firefighters used two hose reel jets to extinguish the blaze and used thermal imaging cameras to check for hotspots.

“The incident was then handed over to a responsible person on scene,” the fire service spokesperson said.
