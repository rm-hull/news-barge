An automatic cat litter box will change your life. I’m not exaggerating. It’s the product I recommend every cat parent invest in above all else. These nifty machines do the work for you, using internal sensors to detect a cat and automatically start cleaning after they leave. They separate waste clumps and deposits them in a drawer below, leaving only fresh litter behind. This means far less day-to-day cleaning—you mostly only need to throw out the drawer waste bag when it's full.

I’ve tested over two dozen automatic cat litter box models, and my top pick is almost always a model from pet tech brand Petkit. They rarely goes on significant sale until the holidays, but I found most of my favorite models on sale at Shop.com right now, some for $100 off—comparable to the discount we usually see during Black Friday sales.
