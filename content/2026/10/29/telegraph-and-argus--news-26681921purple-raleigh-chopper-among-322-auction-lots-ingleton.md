A purple Raleigh Chopper is amongst the items due to go under the hammer at an auction next week.

The bikes - which came in four different colours - were the must-have status symbol bike for all young girls and boys in the 1970s.

Their high-rise handlebars, three-speed gear with T-bar shift, and long, padded seat, put them at the top of every self-respecting teen's Christmas list - even though they were not the easiest to ride.

It is one of 322 lots at Lords Auctions, which is now based at the antiques and salvage centre, off the A65, after relocating from The Courtyard, next to the A65, between Settle and Long Preston.

See the online catalogue at: [https://tinyurl.com/2j9nfuak](https://tinyurl.com/2j9nfuak)

The Chopper, in its original condition, currently has a bid of just £8.

Other items include a Lincoln Burnett black felt bowler hat, with a starting bid of £5; and Whitby jet jewellery.

People can either go along to the auction at 10.30am on Monday, November 2, or bid from home.

Viewing is from 10am to 5pm between now and the sale, and from 9.30am on sale day.
