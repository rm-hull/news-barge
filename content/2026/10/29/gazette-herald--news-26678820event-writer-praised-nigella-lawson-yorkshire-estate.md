A cooking writer praised by Nigella Lawson will host an event at an East Yorkshire estate.

Amy Willcock, the 'queen of the Aga', will hold cookery demonstrations and talks at Hotham Hall on Wednesday, November 4.

Amy, described by Nigella in Vogue as “hand holder to Aga owners everywhere”, will appear at the event which follows a multi-million-pound restoration of the site.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21642915.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amy Willcock *(Image: Supplied)*

Linda Kilburn, who owns Hotham Hall with her husband David, said they are “honoured to welcome Amy to Hotham”.

“She is a fabulous cook, with a tremendous reputation and we are looking forward to a very special day. This is one of a number quality events which we are organising at Hotham, as we share our lovely home with the public.”

Amy, who lives in East Yorkshire, said: “I am so proud to be hosting my Christmas guide to cooking and entertaining at such a magnificent venue as Hotham Hall.

“It is just so perfect and the hall’s spacious kitchen is ideally suited to the style and content of my demonstration.

“Essentially, I will be entertaining as well as educating and I am keen to emphasise that organising and cooking Christmas lunch, or for a major festive occasion, should be fun rather than stressful.

The key to this is to prepare well ahead, which I’ve learnt from my time in the restaurant trade.”

For more information and tickets (£75 per person), visit: hothamhallestate.com
