THE explosive science show, Brainiac Live, is set to thrill audiences at Bradford's St George's Hall.

The event, which won the 2025 Olivier Award for Best Family Show, will take place this Saturday, October 31, at 1.30pm.

Known for its mischievous and energetic performances, Brainiac Live promises a "breathless ride through the wild world of the weird and wonderful."

The show will feature a variety of explosive experiments, with the Brainiacs fearlessly delving into the mysteries of science.

Audience members are encouraged to watch from the safety of their seats as the Brainiacs perform daring feats not to be tried at home.

The live show is suitable for ages five and above, with a running time of one hour.

Tickets are priced between £16 and £22, with a maximum of 10 tickets available for online booking HERE.

Discounts are subject to availability.
