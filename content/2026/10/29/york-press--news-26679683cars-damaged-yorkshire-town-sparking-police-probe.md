Police are investigating after several cars were damaged in a North Yorkshire town.

The vehicles were damaged in King Edward’s Drive and Jesmond Road in Harrogate in the early hours of Monday (October 26).

North Yorkshire Police said it is investigating the “criminal damage” and urged anyone with information to contact the force.

“Officers are carrying out enquiries and are asking anyone who witnessed the incident, saw any suspicious activity in the area, or has information that could assist the investigation to get in touch,” a force spokesperson said.

They asked anyone with information to report it via the North Yorkshire Police website, email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260210752 when passing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
