Rail passengers are facing disruption after a fault on a busy commuter line into Glasgow.

Scotrail has warned that services on the Neilston line may be cancelled after a train broke down shortly before 7am between Whitecraigs and Neilston.

ScotRail has warned that services between Glasgow Central and Neilston may be delayed or revised while the issue is dealt with.

Passengers travelling on the route are being urged to check before travelling and allow extra time for their journey.

A ScotRail update said: "Glasgow Central to Neilston service has developed a fault between Whitecraigs and Neilston.

"Train services between Glasgow Central and Neilston may be cancelled, delayed or revised."

No estimate has yet been given for when services will return to normal.
