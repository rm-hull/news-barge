North East business leaders have hailed the success of a popular business event, with hundreds of people attending sessions across Sunderland.

Sunderland Business Week returned for its sixth year from October 12 to 16, featuring entrepreneur and TV personality Spencer Matthews and Paralympic gold medallist Jonnie Peacock.

The pair discussed their lives and careers in fireside chats at SunFest, the week’s one-day conference, on Thursday, October 15.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639855.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sunderland Business Week brought hundreds of people together for five days of events across the city *(Image: Supplied)*

The week featured expert presentations, panel discussions, fireside chats, networking and workshops.

Sunderland City Council leader Councillor Chris Eynon said: "Sunderland is a city that’s open for business and a place where companies can thrive.

"Sunderland Business Week shone a spotlight on the fantastic opportunities there are here for growth and the major part Sunderland can play in the regional and national economy.

It was a celebration of business and entrepreneurialism right here in our city and a great chance for people and businesses to connect and collaborate."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639856.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sunderland Business Week brought hundreds of people together for five days of events across the city *(Image: Supplied)*

Sunderland Business Partnership organised the week to help organisations of all sizes collaborate, grow and support the city’s development.

Rob Lawson OBE, chair of Sunderland Business Partnership, said: "Sunderland is a brilliant, dynamic place for enterprise and innovation and we are delighted to have been able to deliver another successful Business Week.

"It was truly inspirational and I’d like to thank all our wonderful speakers, sponsors and venues who made the event so enjoyable."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639858.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sunderland Business Week brought hundreds of people together for five days of events across the city *(Image: Supplied)*

SunFest, the week’s flagship event, brought Spencer Matthews and Jonnie Peacock together with senior representatives from the Bank of England, North East Mayoral Strategic Authority, Hays Travel, NatWest and the North East Chamber of Commerce at The Fire Station and Culture House Sunderland for talks, workshops and networking.

Sessions covered the national and regional economy, artificial intelligence, leadership confidence and employee wellbeing.

Paul McEldon, outgoing chief executive of North East BIC, said: "Sunderland is home to many successful businesses of all sizes and events like Sunderland Business Week offer the chance for them to build connections, spark ideas and collaborate.

It was a great week that showcased everything the city has to offer entrepreneurs and businesses."

Sunderland Business Week was sponsored by Sunderland City Council, North East BIC, the University of Sunderland, NatWest, Robson Laidler, Ward Hadaway, Sunderland College, FutureReady, Gentoo, Creo Comms, Sunderland AFC, Sunderland Culture, Procure Smart and Sunderland BIDs.

Craig Shields, Managing Director of Procure Smart, said: "SunFest 2026 was another fantastic event as part of Sunderland Business Week.

We were delighted to return as sponsors for a second consecutive year.

As a proud Sunderland-based business, it's rewarding to connect with so many local organisations, professionals and future talent in one place.

"Throughout the day, our team had some incredibly positive conversations with attendees about the challenges and opportunities facing businesses today.

"We were able to share insights, showcase our services and technology, and explore ways we can help organisations reduce costs, improve efficiency and plan for the future.

We’re confident many of the relationships formed during the day will lead to beneficial outcomes for everyone involved."
