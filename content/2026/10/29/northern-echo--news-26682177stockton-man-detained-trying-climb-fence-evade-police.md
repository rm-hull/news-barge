A man has been charged after police seized suspected Class A drugs and cash during a patrol.

The 29-year-old from Stockton was detained by Stockton’s Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) in Ragworth on Wednesday, October 28.

Officers were carrying out proactive patrols in the area when they spotted a man trying to evade them by climbing a large fence.

He was quickly detained, and a search uncovered what is believed to be Class A drugs, cannabis and a quantity of cash.

Further searches of connected addresses led to the discovery of thousands of pounds in cash.

The man has been charged with possession with intent to supply Class A drugs, resisting an arrest and possession of cannabis.

He was remanded in custody to appear before Teesside Magistrates’ Court this morning (Thursday, October 29).

Inspector Andy Vickers of the NPT said: "Intelligence from the community is crucial.

"Even if you don’t see immediate action, it helps us build a bigger picture and take the most appropriate action we can.

"I hope this reassures the public that we are listening to concerns and taking action."
