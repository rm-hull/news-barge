**🚘** The treasure chest is in central Hokubo at a greenhouse in the Shikisai-no-oka.

Spring has arrived in Forza Horizon 6's Horizon Meets series, which means a new Treasure Hunt chest to find for some seasonal points, which will help you earn some progress towards the new cars on offer.

This week's treasure chest is in central Hokubo by a greenhouse at the Shikisai-no-oka—the distinctive field of colourful strips you can see on the map. To find it:

* Drive along the road going northwest through Shikisai-no-oka's fields.
* When the road turns east, look for the greenhouses on your left.
* The chest is between these, as shown on the map above.

Head there and smash the chest to get 100,000 credits, 5,000 XP, and three Season Progress. Also make sure to complete the new photo challenge, too, if you're looking for extra points.
