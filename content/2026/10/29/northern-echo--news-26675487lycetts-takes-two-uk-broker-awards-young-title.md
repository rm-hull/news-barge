A North East insurance broker has scooped two national awards, with one of its rising stars also taking a top industry title.

Newcastle-based Lycetts secured the honours at the UK Broker Awards 2026, organised by Insurance Age to celebrate excellence across the insurance broking sector.

Amber Cartlidge, an account executive in Lycetts’ specialist bloodstock team, was named Young Insurance Broker of the Year.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639496.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amber Cartlidge with Mark Watson (award event host) *(Image: Supplied)*

Ian Barclay, chief executive at Lycetts, said: "To receive three accolades at such a prestigious industry event is a fantastic achievement and we're incredibly proud of everyone involved.

"Each recognises a different area of our business, but they all reflect the same commitment to delivering specialist expertise and exceptional personal service.

"Amber’s achievement is particularly special.

"Her rapid career progression is remarkable and reflects the importance we place on supporting and developing our people."

Ms Cartlidge joined Lycetts in 2023 with no prior insurance experience, having previously worked in horse racing.

She has since progressed from trainee account handler to account executive and has used her equestrian background to help raise awareness of key industry issues.

This is the second consecutive year that a Lycetts employee has won the young broker title.

Lycetts’ animal trades insurance business, Cliverton, was also named Schemes Insurance Broker of the Year.

Cliverton provides tailored insurance for animal-related trades, rural and equestrian businesses, and smallholdings.

Mr Barclay said: "Cliverton's win and the commendation for our claims team are equally well deserved.
