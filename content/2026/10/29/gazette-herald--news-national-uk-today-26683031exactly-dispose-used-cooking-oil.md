If you regularly cook at home, you will know how hard it can be to get rid of any cooking oil you might have left over from your meal.

It is commonly known that pouring cooking oil down the sink is a bad idea, as it can harden and lead to blockages in your sink.

It can be quite expensive to get an expert to unblock your sink after cooking oil has built up over time.

However, a binman has revealed exactly how to dispose of cooking oil after you are finished with it.

## How to correctly dispose of cooking oil

Ashley, also known as the No.1 Binman on TikTok, has posted a video telling people exactly how to get rid of used cooking oil.

You might think to put it in your food waste bin, but this is also wrong.

Ashley said: “Used cooking oil should go in your general waste.

“Do not put it in the food waste, and definitely not the recycling bin.

If you want to avoid the hot oil splitting your bin bag, you can easily put it into a container.

Ashley added: “If you can, put it in a container or something that can't be recycled and then put it in your general waste.

“It stops it from going anywhere in case a bag splits.”

You can easily put the oil in a carton or jar with a lid and throw it in the general waste bin.

However, Ashley mentioned if you have a lot of oil like commercial oil, you cannot put this in your general waste bin.

He added: “You’d have to either contact your council or get someone else to come and collect it.

“But if it’s just like a normal family-sized amount of cooking oil, then it’ll just go in your general waste.”

Many people took to the comments to share where they put their used cooking oil to dispose of it correctly.

One person said: “I put mine in an empty coffee jar then put it in the black bin bag with other stuff.”

Another person said: “I used to pour it into a milk jug, write on it what’s inside, and then leave it next to the bin.”

A third person added: “Empty jar or bottle and in the bin.”
