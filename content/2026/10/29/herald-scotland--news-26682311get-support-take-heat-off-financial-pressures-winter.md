*Are you worried about high energy bills this winter? Then seek expert support now, writes Josiah Lockhart, Chief Executive at Changeworks, a charity and social enterprise providing energy advice and retrofit support*

With colder weather approaching and many households feeling the impact of high energy costs, we are encouraging people across Scotland to act now.

Whether it’s accessing support with energy bills and debt or taking steps to make homes warmer and more energy efficient, getting prepared now can help households stay more comfortable and manage costs over the winter months.

With the ongoing energy crisis, many people may feel they’ve already done everything they can to reduce their bills, or don’t know where to turn to for support.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21647867.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nobody should feel they have to face energy worries alone *(Image: JAMIE WILLIAMSON)*

At Changeworks, our Affordable Warmth Services provide free, confidential support to people who are struggling with energy costs, helping them reduce bills, resolve fuel debt issues, access financial support and stay affordably warm at home.

*Making a difference*

OVER the last year, we provided advice and support to over 62,000 households. One client, Treasure, sought help after struggling with high energy bills and needing support with tariffs. She said:

“When you’re already dealing with financial pressure, energy bills become another constant worry. I work full time, but I was still struggling to keep up with energy costs.

“Trying to navigate complicated tariffs and changes to my meter was stressful and overwhelming. The support I received from Changeworks helped me understand my options and made me feel more in control.

“It wasn’t just about reducing costs – it helped reduce the stress that comes with not knowing where to turn.”

*Actions you can take*

SIMPLE everyday actions such as only boiling the water needed in a kettle, reducing shower times and washing clothes at lower temperatures can help reduce household energy consumption and lower bills.

If you are on a time-of-use tariff, shifting suitable electricity use to cheaper off-peak periods may also save money.

While small changes can help reduce energy use, households experiencing high energy costs should also seek advice on tariffs, financial support and improving the energy efficiency of their home.

If you’re worried about energy costs, consider taking the following steps:

■ Contact your energy supplier to check that you’re on the most suitable tariff and payment method for your circumstances.

■ Ask what support is available if you’re struggling with bills, including help with managing energy debt, spreading costs or agreeing a repayment plan.

■ Check whether you’re eligible for the Priority Services Register, which provides free additional support for households that may need extra assistance because of age, health or personal circumstances.

■ Review available tariffs, including options from your current supplier and others, and check prices, terms and any exit fees before switching.

■ Seek advice on improving your home’s energy efficiency, including insulation and appropriate draught-proofing measures that can help reduce heat loss while maintaining good ventilation and air quality.

■ Check whether you’re eligible for grants or support schemes for energy efficiency improvements or heating upgrades.

■ If you’re planning home improvements, consider energy-saving upgrades or renewable technologies, such as solar panels, which may help reduce energy costs over the longer term.
