After months of building work and menu planning, a new Teesside restaurant has revealed its opening date.

Crust K, at the former Billingham Beck Visitor Centre, will officially open on Thursday, November 26, bringing pizza, fresh pasta and Mediterranean-inspired dishes to the country park setting.

The family-run restaurant is the latest venture from the team behind Crust Norton and mobile pizzeria Flaming Dough.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642993.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Crust Norton *(Image: James Durham)*

Owner James Durham told The Northern Echo the visitor centre had no commercial kitchen, bar or restaurant infrastructure when the team first viewed it.

Walls were stripped out and a new kitchen and bar created, with much of the work carried out by the team themselves.

At the heart of the new kitchen is a two-tonne Marana Forni pizza oven, hand-built in Italy and transported from Manchester before being manoeuvred into the former visitor centre.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642990.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Crust K concept *(Image: James Durham)*

Alongside pizza, Crust K will serve brunch, sharing plates, fresh pasta, desserts and Sunday lunch, with a focus on relaxed Mediterranean-inspired food.

In the kitchen will be another James, the restaurant's head chef, who has spent 30 years in the industry and previously cooked at Kinloch Lodge on the Isle of Skye.

He grew up in an Italian family and has spent almost a year developing the menu with the Crust K team.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642992.jpg?type=mds-article-620)James Durham during Crust K renovations in Billingham *(Image: James Durham)*

The Billingham Beck site brings the story full circle for the owner.

James launched Flaming Dough in 2022 after converting a horse trailer into a mobile pizzeria that served customers at Billingham Beck.

He left his job as a senior planning manager in 2024 to open Crust Norton.

His daughter Amelia, who helped serve pizzas from the trailer at Billingham Beck in the early days, will also take a key front-of-house role at the new restaurant.

Crust K is expected to seat around 45 diners inside, with plans to use the outdoor space and surrounding country park too.

Ahead of the official opening, several soft-launch evenings will be held to give the team a chance to settle in and get everything running smoothly.

Bookings are due to open next week, with details set to be shared on Crust K's social media pages.
