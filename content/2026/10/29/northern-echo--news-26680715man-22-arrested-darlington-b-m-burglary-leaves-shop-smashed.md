A man has been arrested after £300 worth of items were stolen from a Darlington B&M store during an early morning break-in.

Durham Police were called to the Northgate bargain store shortly before 2.20am on Thursday (October 29).

It comes after reports that a man had forced entry to the store before making off with goods.

The suspect is then said to have fled the scene, but was tracked across the town by Darlington Borough Council CCTV operators, who followed his movements to Victoria Road.

Officers subsequently arrested a 22-year-old man on suspicion of burglary.

A spokesperson for the force said: "A man has been arrested following a burglary in Darlington in the early hours of this morning.

"Officers were called just before 2.20am following reports that a man had broken into B&M on Northgate.

"The suspect is believed to have stolen around £300 worth of items before fleeing the scene.

"Darlington Borough Council's CCTV operators tracked the suspect to Victoria Road, where officers arrested a 22-year-old man on suspicion of burglary."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646060.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene outside the shop on October 29 *(Image: Lisa Young)*

The 22-year-old remains in custody while enquiries continue.

A sign on the entrance of Northgate's B&M tells customers the branch is "closed until further notice".

A smashed front window can be seen cordoned off outside the shop.

The burglary comes just weeks after the same store was targeted on October 8.

On that occasion, two men were arrested after offenders smashed their way into the shop before making off with a quantity of stock.

Officers later detained two suspects after CCTV operators tracked a vehicle linked to the incident through Darlington.
