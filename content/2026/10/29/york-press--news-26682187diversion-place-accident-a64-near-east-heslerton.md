VEHICLES are now moving 'slowly' past the scene of an accident on a main road in North Yorkshire.

A crew from Sherburn responded the incident involving a HGV and three cars. at 2.59pm.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said everyone in the vehicles were out on arrival.

"Crews completed scene safety and used an environmental pack to stem and contain fluid leaking from one of the vehicles. The incident was left in the hands of the police.
