A road in Stamford Bridge is set to close for repairs.

Battle Flats Way will be shut on Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

The closure, which will be in place from 9am to 6pm, is to repair and replace a sunken frame cover.

The work is part of a maintenance effort by the East Riding of Yorkshire Council to ensure public safety.

The closure will affect the stretch of road outside numbers 1 to 6.

A diversion route will be in place for affected traffic.

This will guide drivers via Battle Flats Way, Moor Road, Church Road, Main Street, and Burton Fields Road in Stamford Bridge.

Although closed to through traffic, access will still be available for residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians.

The order for the closure will remain valid for up to 18 months, or until the repair work is completed, whichever is sooner.

It is anticipated that the works will be completed within eight hours.

For further information, people can contact the council's Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance on 01482 393939, quoting reference CB.
