Natalie Harp has taken to working out of President Donald Trump’s private dining room near the Oval Office to run her “human printer” operation ahead of next week’s highly consequential midterm elections, according to White House insiders.

Harp, 35, Trump’s devoted special assistant, has abandoned the desk where she usually sits outside of the Oval and is mostly working out of the private dining room, printing off hard copies of news articles and documents for the president to read, several administration officials told *The New York Times.* Harp referred to herself as “human printer” in letters to Trump that were leaked earlier this year.

The president has reportedly been consuming vast amounts of video clips and “AI slop” ahead of the midterms, put in front of him by aides.

Insiders described the mood in the White House lately as “grim” and the president “unpredictable” as the administration grapples with fears that Republicans could lose control of the House and Senate next week in the midterm elections.

Staffers are reportedly on edge as unfavorable polls and negative stories set Trump “into a dark mood” amid his midterms blitz of the country. Trump has been on tour, headlining rallies to galvanize his supporters despite not being on the ballot, but some GOP candidates have distanced themselves from the president.

![Natalie Harp has been working out of President Donald Trump’s private dining room near the Oval Office to run her ‘human printer’ operation, according to a report](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/06/09/2298782614...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Harp, who reportedly posts on Truth Social on the president’s behalf, has been pictured at Trump’s side on the campaign trail and is one of the small group the president has been “increasingly cloistered with” lately, according to the *Times*.

Trump reportedly dictated his furious 1,300-word rant about Bill Maher to Harp aboard Air Force One after watching Karoline Leavitt on the comedian’s show last week. The president was seething after Maher, who recently dined at the White House, referenced Trump’s “lowest approval rating of any midterm candidate ever” in his opening monologue.

“Bill Maher lied, but he lies about everything,” Trump fumed, labeling him a “COMPLETE AND TOTAL LOSER.” The tirade was posted on Truth Social on Sunday.

Another aide who has the ear of the president is Bill Pulte, director of the Federal Housing Finance Agency. Pulte has been making posters for Trump - including one of Ohio Republican Sen. Jon Husted, whose rear has been admired by the president.

![Housing official Bill Pulte has been making posters for Trump, including one of Ohio Republican Sen. Jon Husted, whose rear has been admired by the president](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/29/12/42/Screenshot-2026-10-29-at-8-39-12-AM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

One administration official compared Pulte to a “court jester” to the *Times*, as he is often in and out of Trump’s Air Force One cabin to show him memes and posters he has made for him, according to the newspaper.

Several current and former officials likened the atmosphere in the White House to Trump’s turbulent first term, characterized by infighting and a revolving door of staffers.

The White House said the president and those around him are united ahead of the midterms.

“There is only one team that matters in this White House, and that is the team focused on serving President Donald J. Trump and the American people,” White House spokeswoman Danielle Alvarez said in a statement. “The president’s team remains strong, united and focused on delivering his America First agenda.”
