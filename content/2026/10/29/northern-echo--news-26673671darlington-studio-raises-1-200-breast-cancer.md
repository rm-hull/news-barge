A Darlington Pilates studio has raised more than £1,000 at a breast cancer awareness event inspired by its owner’s cancer battle.

Space Pilates inside Equinox House in Darlington, run by mother and daughter team Lucy Hughes and Layla Hall, turned the studio pink and decorated the space with balloons and bunting on Friday (October 23).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21636391.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The breast cancer fundraiser at Space Pilates, Darlington *(Image: SPACE PILATES)*

Fitness instructor Lucy, who has more than 25 years’ experience, opened the studio last year after recovering from breast cancer.

But her breast cancer returned earlier this year, prompting her to launch the fundraiser during Breast Cancer Awareness Month.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21636385.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The breast cancer fundraiser at Space Pilates, Darlington *(Image: SPACE PILATES)*

Lucy said: "I set up the business after overcoming breast cancer; I turned the corner, but now the breast cancer has returned.

"We wanted to run the event to give something back. Ninety per cent of our clients are women, and now the stats say that one in three women are impacted by breast cancer; it was always important for us to do this fundraising."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21636390.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The breast cancer fundraiser at Space Pilates, Darlington *(Image: SPACE PILATES)*

Five classes were held on Friday, raising more than £1,200 in donations.

After her second breast cancer diagnosis, Lucy says daughter Layla has taken on many of the classes, while she keeps as active as possible.

The fundraiser also fell on the day of Lucy’s final session of her first round of chemotherapy.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21636389.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The breast cancer fundraiser at Space Pilates, Darlington *(Image: SPACE PILATES)*
