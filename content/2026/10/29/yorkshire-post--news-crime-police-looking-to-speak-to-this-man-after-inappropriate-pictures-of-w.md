# Police looking to speak to this man after inappropriate pictures of woman allegedly taken in shop

## Do you recognise him?

Police in South Yorkshire have released a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to in connection to a reported sexual offence in a shop in a Doncaster shopping centre.

It is reported that around 11.15am on Monday September 7, a man took inappropriate pictures a woman without her consent.

Following numerous enquiries, police are now releasing a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to as they believe he may be able to help us with their investigation.

He is described as white, of a medium build, around 5ft 8ins tall, with short brown hair and a beard, and is aged between 25 and 40.

If you can help, you can pass information to the force online or by calling 101.

Please quote investigation number 14/159217/26 when you get in touch.
