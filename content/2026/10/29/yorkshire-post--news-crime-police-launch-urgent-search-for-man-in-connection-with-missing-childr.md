# Police launch urgent search for man in connection with missing children

## Yousaf and Mohammed were both reported missing on Saturday

Police are appealing for information to help locate a man who is wanted for a breach of his non-molestation order in connection with his two children who are currently missing.

Kibriya Ahmed, 42, has a non-molestation order preventing him from having unsupervised contact with his two children who were reported missing from Liversidge, Kirklees.

Yousaf and Mohammed were both reported missing on Saturday October 24.

Yousaf, 15, is described as 5ft 11ins, of medium build with short black hair and black facial hair.

Mohammed, 12, is described as 4ft 9ins, of slim build with short black hair and glasses.

Officers believe Ahmed may know the whereabouts of his two children and have conducted extensive arrest enquiries in order to try and locate him but are now appealing for the public’s help.

Ahmed has connections in Rochdale and Manchester, police said.

Anyone with information which may assist to locate Kibriya Ahmed is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at Live Chat | West Yorkshire Police quoting reference 13260586098.

Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
