A person has been hit by a car in Leeds and traffic has been thrown into "chaos".

They were hit on Beckett Street in Harehills at 9.52am today on Thursday, October 29. A large cordon has been set up and surrounding roads have been closed, it has been reported. Drivers have been asked to avoid the area.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Police are currently in attendance at a serious road traffic collision involving a car and a pedestrian on Beckett Street. A scene is on whilst enquiries are ongoing. Motorists are asked to avoid the area."

Locals have said the large scene has resulted in "lots of emergency services and traffic chaos". Yorkshire Ambulance Service has been asked for further comment. This is a breaking news story. Scroll below for further details.

### Leeds woman, 36, hit and killed by car being chased by police - full statement

A woman has been killed after a car involved in a police chase hit her and fled the scene in Leeds.

The woman, 36, was rushed to hospital with serious injuries but sadly died after she was hit on Beckett Street, in Harehills, at 9.52am today, on Thursday, October 29. Minutes earlier, West Yorkshire Police had spotted a black Vauxhall Corsa which failed to stop for officers on Harehills Road, Leeds.

### Woman killed after car in police chase hit her - full police statement:

> At 9.50am this morning a black Vauxhall Corsa failed to stop for officers on Harehills Road, Leeds. A pursuit was authorised and at 9.52am the Corsa was involved in a collision with a pedestrian on Beckett Street, the driver failed to stop at the scene.Emergency medical attention was given by officers at the scene. The 36-year-old woman was taken to hospital with serious injuries. Sadly, the woman died in hospital.The Corsa was then found abandoned on Nippet Lane, at least two people in the vehicle left the scene. The vehicle has been seized pending forensic examination. Enquiries are ongoing to locate the people in the vehicle. The Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have launched an investigation and are appealing for witnesses or anyone with dash-cam to come forward. Due to prior police contact a mandatory IOPC referral has been made.Anyone who witnessed the collision or who saw the vehicle before or after is asked to contact police via 101 quoting log 414 of 29 October

### Leeds hospital urges patients to attend appointments 'as usual' and allow for extra travel time

A spokesperson for Leeds' St James's Hospital said:

> We are aware of a serious road traffic collision at a mini roundabout on Beckett Street near the front of St James’s University Hospital (circled on the map), and understand that emergency services are responding.Road closures and traffic disruption in the surrounding area may affect journeys to and from the hospital. Please allow extra time for your journey and follow police advice about roads to avoid. Our hospital services continue to support patients and if you have an appointment, please attend as usual.

### Leeds Harehills crash latest as police and ambulance crews issue statement

A person has been rushed to hospital after they were hit by a car in Leeds.

They were hit on Beckett Street in Harehills at 9.52am today on Thursday, October 29. A large cordon has been set up and surrounding roads have been closed. Drivers have been asked to avoid the area.

Harehills Road has been closed in both directions with slow traffic. All approaches to the mini roundabout are closed, from Beckett Street, Ashley Road and Stanley Road.

### All the surrounding roads affected:

Harehills Road in both directions closed, slow traffic due to accident from Bexley Terrace to Beckett Street.All approaches to the mini roundabout are closed, from Beckett Street, Ashley Road and Stanley Road.

### Yorkshire Ambulance Service Statement

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said:

> "We received an emergency call at 9.52am on Thursday morning to report a collision between a car and pedestrian on Beckett Street, Leeds. A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
