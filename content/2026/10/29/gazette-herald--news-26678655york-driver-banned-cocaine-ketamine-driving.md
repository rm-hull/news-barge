A young man’s weekend trip to a “rave” will have a major adverse effect on his new business after he was stopped by police, York magistrates heard.

Luke Kane, 23, was driving in Rougier Street late at night on June 8 with ketamine and a breakdown product of cocaine in his body, said Antony Farrell, prosecuting.

He was slurring his words and failed a roadside drug swipe.

Kane, of Irwin Avenue, Heworth, pleaded guilty to two charges of drug driving.

He had no previous convictions.




He was banned from driving for two years, fined £833 and ordered to pay a £333 statutory surcharge and £85 prosecution costs.

For him, Neal Kutte said Kane had been to a “rave” the previous weekend with friends and had taken some drugs there.

He was not a regular drug user.

He needed his driving licence because he had recently set up his own business as a self-employed plasterer and would now have difficulty getting to jobs outside his immediate locality.

He would also be paying very high insurance premiums when he had finished his driving ban.
