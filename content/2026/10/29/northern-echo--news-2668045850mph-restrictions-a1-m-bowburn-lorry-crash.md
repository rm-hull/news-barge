Fifty-mile-an-hour speed limits have been put in place on the A1(M) in County Durham for urgent repairs after a lorry overturned and slipped its load.

Barriers were smashed by a Warburtons HGV which ended up on its side across the central reservation at Bowburn on Wednesday (October 28) morning.

The HGV overturned at around 5.14am, scattering loaves, packaging and bread trays across the carriageways.

Repairs are now being carried out, with speed restrictions in place as work continues.

![CRASH](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639965.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Warburtons lorry damaged the central reservation barriers *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Dramatic pictures showed the orange lorry lying on its side across the motorway, with its load strewn across the tarmac and grass verges.

The crash caused major disruption during the morning rush hour, with the northbound carriageway closed between Bowburn and Carville Interchange and the southbound carriageway restricted to the hard shoulder.

The lorry driver was helped from the cab by firefighters before being taken to University Hospital of North Durham for treatment.

The road has since reopened with temporary barriers, with a 50mph speed limit in place within the junction.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640064.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The wreckage on the A1M after the HGV crash *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "Police were called just after 5.10am today following reports that a lorry had overturned on the A1(M) just after the Bowburn junction.

"The lorry has shed its load across both carriageways, resulting in a full closure of the northbound carriageway between Bowburn and Carville Interchange. The southbound carriageway is restricted to the hard shoulder only.

"Motorists are advised to avoid the area and find an alternative route.

"The driver of the lorry has been taken to hospital for treatment."

![aa](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639711.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The crash led to heavy tailbacks *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on the A1(M) at Bowburn at 5.14am.

"We dispatched a double-crewed ambulance, a clinical team leader and two vehicles from our hazardous area response team.

"One patient was taken to University Hospital of North Durham."

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service sent two crews from Spennymoor and Durham to help safely extract the driver.
