The death of a farmer working with fence post machinery is to be probed in a Fatal Accident Inquiry (FAI).

William Mark Callander, 55, from Crofthead Farm near Crocketford, died in hospital on May 11, 2023, three days after the incident happened while fencing repair work was being carried out.

An FAI is to be held into his death to establish the facts and find out f any lessons can be learned.

The Procurator Fiscal has now lodged a First Notice to begin the court process for a Fatal Accident Inquiry (FAI).

A preliminary hearing has been scheduled for December 7, 2026, at Dumfries Sheriff Court.

The inquiry will examine the cause of Mr Callander's death, the circumstances surrounding the incident, and whether any reasonable precautions could have been taken that may help prevent similar deaths in the future.

Unlike criminal proceedings, fatal accident inquiries are fact-finding hearings designed to establish the circumstances of a death rather than apportion blame.

Debbie Carroll, of the Crown Office and Procurator Fiscal Service (COPFS), said: “The death of William Mark Callander occurred while in the course of his employment and as such a Fatal Accident Inquiry is mandatory.

“The lodging of the First Notice enables FAI proceedings to commence under the direction of the Sheriff.

“Mr Callander’s family will continue to be kept informed of significant developments as court proceedings progress.”

The Fatal Accident Inquiry will take place before a sheriff at Dumfries Sheriff Court following the preliminary hearing later this year.
