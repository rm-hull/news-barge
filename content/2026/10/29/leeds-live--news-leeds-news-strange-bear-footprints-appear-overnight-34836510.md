# Strange 'bear' footprints appear overnight across Leeds

## The footprints appeared in Roundhay Park

A series of strange 'bear' footprints have appeared overnight in a region of Leeds.

The large paw prints appeared in Roundhay Park here in Leeds near to a Morrisons store during the early hours of this morning. While they appear to be bear prints, they have been clearly placed in the area.

These prints are larger than an average human hand and appeared along a path in the woods.

It is unclear at this time as to what exactly caused the prints to be placed in Roundhay Park, although it thankfully remains unlikely they were caused by a real bear.

Images from the scene do show a trail of the prints leading deeper into the woods as walkers pass on by. The prints are made up of a large foot pad with five toe marks.
