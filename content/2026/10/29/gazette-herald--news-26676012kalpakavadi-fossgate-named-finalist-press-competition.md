A YORK restaurant renowned for its speciality in authentic South Indian cuisine has celebrated after being named a finalist in the Press Best Curry House competition.

Kalpakavadi, in Fossgate, opened in November 2019 with the aim of bringing the flavours, traditions and hospitality of Kerala to York.

The independent restaurant was established by the team behind Coconut Lagoon and was inspired by a desire to introduce diners to a different side of Indian cuisine.




A former lead chef in the Indian Merchant Navy, Kalpakavadi founder Saji Kurian brings extensive culinary experience to the table after years of working in the restaurant industry.

![Popular dishes include Kottayam Beef Roast and Malabar Fried Prawns, highlighting the diversity of Kerala's regional and coastal cuisine](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21639504.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Popular dishes include Kottayam Beef Roast and Malabar Fried Prawns, highlighting the diversity of Kerala's regional and coastal cuisine *(Image: Kalpakavadi)*

A representative for the curry house said: “Customers can expect freshly prepared food, distinctive regional flavours and a varied menu featuring seafood, traditional meat dishes and vegetarian options.

“Above all, we want people to leave having enjoyed something memorable and feeling that they would like to return.

“One of our standout dishes is Seabass Pollichathu, a traditional Kerala preparation in which fish is marinated with spices, coated in a flavourful masala and wrapped in a banana leaf for cooking.

“The banana leaf helps retain moisture while imparting a distinctive aroma, allowing the flavours of the fish, spices and masala to come together.

“It represents what we aim to achieve at Kalpakavadi: traditional Kerala cooking that celebrates the ingredients and techniques of the region.”

![The independent restaurant was established by the team behind Coconut Lagoon and was inspired by a desire to introduce diners to a different side of Indian cuisine](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21639503.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The independent restaurant was established by the team behind Coconut Lagoon and was inspired by a desire to introduce diners to a different side of Indian cuisine *(Image: Kalpakavadi)*

Other popular dishes include Kottayam Beef Roast and Malabar Fried Prawns, highlighting the diversity of Kerala's regional and coastal cuisine.

A Kalpakavadi spokesperson added: “Our growth has been driven by our commitment to quality, consistency and authentic flavours, alongside the hard work of our kitchen and front-of-house teams.

“We have also been fortunate to receive recognition for our work, including an Outstanding Achievement award at the Yorkshire & North East Curry Awards 2025.

“Being named a finalist in The Press Best Curry House in York 2026 competition is another important milestone for us.”
