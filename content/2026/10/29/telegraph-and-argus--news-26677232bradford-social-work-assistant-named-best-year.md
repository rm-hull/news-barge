A SOCIAL work assistant has been named Social Work Assistant of the Year.

Samreen Akhtar, who works at Cygnet Hospital Bierley in Bradford, received the honour at the Cygnet Group’s annual Social Work Conference and Awards on September 8.

Ms Akhtar was recognised for her dedicated support for people with mental health needs and her commitment to improving the lives of service users and their families.

She said: "Receiving this award is an incredible honour.

"I absolutely love my role as a social work assistant because it gives me the chance to help people when they need it most.

"I am incredibly passionate about what I do because nothing is more rewarding than making a positive difference in people’s lives every day.

"Having once been in a position where I needed support myself, being able to give back means the world to me.

"Our entire social work team is absolutely fantastic, and I want to thank every single one of them for being such an amazing team.

"A massive thank you to my managers for always giving me the opportunity to learn, push myself, and grow."

Cygnet Hospital Bierley is a 62-bed hospital providing a range of acute and low secure mental health services for both men and women.

Ms Akhtar, who serves as the hospital’s Carers Lead, was nominated by colleagues who praised her commitment to involving families in patient care and improving community support for carers.

They said: "Samreen continues to demonstrate clear commitment and passion.

"She not only fulfils her role but day in day out goes above and beyond for the service users in our care as well as carers and colleagues.

"Samreen is always available for service users and colleagues and works tirelessly each and every day to make sure service users’ needs are met.

"She significantly improves their quality of life."

Colleagues also highlighted her work in developing new initiatives, including carers newsletters, and creating volunteering opportunities for service users to build confidence and life skills.

The nomination continued: "All staff at Bierley speak highly of Samreen and value all that she does, which speaks volumes regarding her positive attitude and outstanding work ethic.

"She has a can-do attitude and service users are always at the forefront of what she does.

"We are lucky to have her."

Cygnet Group is a leading independent provider of health and social care services for adults and young people with mental health needs, learning disabilities, autism, and eating disorders.
