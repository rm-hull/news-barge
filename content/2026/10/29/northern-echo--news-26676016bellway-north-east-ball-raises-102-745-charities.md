More than £100,000 has been raised for two charities after hundreds of guests attended a North East housebuilder's fundraising ball.

Bellway North East held the event at Gateshead’s Hilton Hotel in aid of Cancer Research UK and Variety, raising £102,745 including a £10,000 donation from the housebuilder.

Comedian Steve Walls hosted the ball, which drew 384 guests.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639519.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Host Steve Walls, Bellway North East Sales Director Emma Chesterton and Bellway North East Managing Direct Daniel Walker at the event. *(Image: Supplied)*

Emma Chesterton, sales director for Bellway North East, said: "This year’s charity ball was another fantastic night of fun, laughter and generosity which raised an incredible £102,745 – including a £10,000 donation from Bellway – for two very special charities.

"Both Cancer Research UK and Variety are Bellway’s national charity partners and each do invaluable work which we are so proud to support.

"I’d like to thank all staff, supporters and contractors who attended the event and helped make it a night to remember."

Entertainment included a charity auction with sporting and film memorabilia, a phone quiz, magic tricks and performances by BeatBox Trio and Groove Allstars.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639520.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nicky McKenna from Cancer Research UK. *(Image: Supplied)*

Bellway has raised more than £5.1 million for Cancer Research UK in the past decade.

Earlier this year, Bellway launched a partnership with Variety to support disabled and disadvantaged children.

Bellway has pledged to fund six specially adapted Variety minibuses, known as Sunshine Coaches, in areas where it operates.

The first Sunshine Coach went to a school in South Shields earlier this year.
