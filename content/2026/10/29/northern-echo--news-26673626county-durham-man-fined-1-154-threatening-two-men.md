A County Durham man has been fined for threatening two men and intending to make them fear violence.

Liam Armstrong, of Manor Grove, Cockfield, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on October 23.

The 39-year-old denied using threatening, abusive or insulting words or behaviour towards the men in Staindrop on April 11.

He was charged under the Public Order Act 1986 with threatening the men or provoking immediate unlawful violence.

Magistrates found him guilty.

Armstrong was fined £360 and ordered to pay £650 in prosecution costs plus a £144 surcharge, taking his total bill to £1,154.

The court ordered him to pay through deductions from his benefits.

His account was consolidated with existing accounts.
