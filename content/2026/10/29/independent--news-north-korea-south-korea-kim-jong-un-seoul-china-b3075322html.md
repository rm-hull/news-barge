South Korea said North Korea has launched an unidentified projectile off the North’s east coast.

South Korea’s military said the launch happened Thursday but gave no further details, including the type of weapon fired.

The launch comes five days after North Korean leader Kim Jong Un visited China via a newly inaugurated bridge connecting the two countries.
