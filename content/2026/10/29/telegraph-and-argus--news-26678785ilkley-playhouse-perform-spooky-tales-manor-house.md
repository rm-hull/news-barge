AN evening of ghostly tales will bring suspense and disbelief to local visitors.

Halloween: Stories of Suspense and Disbelief will take place at Ilkley Manor House this Saturday, October 31, from 7:30pm to 9:00pm.

Ilkley Playhouse, the Manor House’s Bard in the Yard partners, will read ghostly, unusual and unexplained tales and poems inside the atmospheric historic building.

Some stories include a sprinkling of horror, offering fascinating or frightening entertainment and the chance to expect the unexpected.

Visitors can attend whether avoiding trick or treat, celebrating the Celtic feast of Samhain, or enjoying mysterious tales of between-worlds realms.

Doors open at 7:00pm, with refreshments available to purchase, and the performance begins at 7:30pm.

Tickets cost £10 and may be purchased on Ticket Tailor HERE or in person at the Box Office on weekends when Ilkley Manor House is open.

Limited tickets may also be available on the door, subject to availability.
