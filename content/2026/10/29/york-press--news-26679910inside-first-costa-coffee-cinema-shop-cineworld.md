Costa Coffee has opened its first-ever store inside a Cineworld cinema, with plans to bring the coffee chain to up to 26 locations across the UK.

The new shop has opened at Cineworld Ashton-under-Lyne, giving film fans the chance to grab their favourite Costa drinks, toasties and sweet treats before or after watching a movie.

The opening marks the first step in a new nationwide partnership between Costa Coffee and Cineworld, with further locations planned in cities including Glasgow, Sheffield and Birmingham.

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21637857.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Costa Coffee in Cineworld Cinema, in Ashton-under-Lyme *(Image: Costa Coffee)*

The new store is designed to offer movie-goers somewhere to relax, with comfortable seating and Costa's modern shop design.

Customers can order the chain's full range of handcrafted hot and iced drinks, including its signature Mocha Italia coffee.

## Where will Costa Coffee open next?

The Ashton-under-Lyne store is the first of a planned rollout that could see Costa Coffee open in up to 26 Cineworld cinemas nationwide.

The next location is scheduled to open at Broughton Park on October 28, with further stores planned for sites including Glasgow, Sheffield and Birmingham.

Andrew Miller, Express and Corporate Sales Director UK&I at Costa Coffee, said: "We're delighted to open our first cinema store in Ashton-under-Lyne, giving customers another place to enjoy their favourite handcrafted drinks and food as part of a trip to the cinema.

"This is just the beginning of an exciting rollout that will bring Costa Coffee to movie-goers in locations across the UK."

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21637860.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cineworld Cinema, in Ashton-under-Lyme *(Image: Costa Coffee)*

Tim Sparrow, Retail Director at Cineworld, said: "Our partnership with Costa Coffee marks an exciting new chapter for Cineworld.

"We pride ourselves on elevating the guest experience, from the moment you walk through the doors to when you sit back and watch the magic unfold on the big screen."

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21637859.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cineworld Cinema, in Ashton-under-Lyme, home to Costa Coffee *(Image: Costa Coffee)*

## Costa Coffee Cineworld opening times

The new Costa Coffee store at Cineworld Ashton-under-Lyne is open at the following times:

* Monday to Friday: 12pm to 9pm
* Saturday and Sunday: 9am to 9.30pm

The new shop gives cinema visitors another option for picking up a coffee and a bite to eat as part of their trip to the big screen.
