THE Feeling are set to perform in Halifax as special guests for pop icon Rick Astley.

They will appear at The Piece Hall on May 28 as part of Astley’s headline show for Live at The Piece Hall 2027.

A spokesperson said: "The Feeling will join multi-million selling global star Rick Astley as special guests when he heads to Halifax next year to kick off a summer of live shows at The Piece Hall."

The Feeling, known for hits such as Sewn and Fill My Little World, rose to fame in the mid-2000s with their debut album Twelve Stops and Home.

Rick Astley, best known for his 1987 hit Never Gonna Give You Up, last performed at The Piece Hall in 2024 to a sold-out crowd.

His 2016 comeback album 50 reached number one, and his latest release, Are We There Yet?, reached number two in the charts in 2023.
