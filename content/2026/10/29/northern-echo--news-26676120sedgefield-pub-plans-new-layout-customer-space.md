A County Durham pub is set for a refurbishment under plans to alter its layout and create more space for customers, according to a public notice.

Punch Taverns Limited has submitted plans to Durham County Council for changes to the Golden Lion pub in Sedgefield.

The alterations include a new bar layout and the removal of the kitchen to expand the trading area.

There will also be new fixed seating positions and joinery, all part of a comprehensive refurbishment project.

The pub, located at 1 East End, Sedgefield, will also see changes to conditions regarding children on the premises.

The existing operating times, licensable activities, and conditions under the current premises licence will stay the same.

The details of these amendments can be viewed in the full application with the Licensing Authority.

The plan, drawing number 26102-502, can be inspected at the council's offices at Annand House, John Street North, Meadowfield, Durham.

This is by appointment only on weekdays between 9.30am and 4pm.

Any representations regarding the application must be submitted in writing by the end of November 17.

These can be sent to Durham County Council, Licensing Services, PO Box 274, Stanley, Durham, DH8 1HG, or via email to [licensing@durham.gov.uk](mailto:licensing@durham.gov.uk) .
