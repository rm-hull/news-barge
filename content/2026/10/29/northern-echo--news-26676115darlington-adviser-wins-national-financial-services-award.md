Darlington financial adviser Christine Tarran has won a national award celebrating outstanding women in financial services.

Christine Tarran, founder and director of Tarran & Co Financial Planning Ltd, won Independent Financial Adviser of the Year at the 2026 Women's Recognition Awards in London.

The awards honour women across the UK mortgage and financial services sectors who are driving positive change.

The award marks another milestone in Christine’s 28-year career, built around putting people first in financial planning.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20998846.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christine Tarran at the Women's Recognition Awards 2026 *(Image: Women’s Recognition Awards)*

Tarran & Co helps individuals, families and businesses feel more confident about their financial future through personal advice and client-focused service.

Its services include personal and family protection, savings and investments, retirement, inheritance and estate planning, plus Her Invest, which supports women seeking financial independence.

Christine said: "Winning this award is a real honour and one that means so much to me personally.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20998848.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Tarran & Co team *(Image: Supplied)*

"I've spent my career helping people navigate important financial decisions, and supporting clients through every stage of life is what drives me every day. To be recognised by my peers and the judges at the Women's Recognition Awards is incredibly humbling.

"This award is also a reflection of the fantastic team around me and the wonderful clients who place their trust in us. I'm truly grateful for their support."

The 2026 Women's Recognition Awards used the theme “50 Years Forward”, reflecting on progress since the Sex Discrimination Act was introduced in 1975 and looking ahead for financial services.

Christine said the awards give national recognition to women making a difference in financial services.

Tarran & Co said the team was delighted to see Christine’s dedication, passion and leadership recognised nationally.

A spokesperson for the company said: "We couldn't be happier or prouder to see Christine receive this award. Anyone who knows her will understand how much care, commitment and energy she brings to supporting clients and helping them achieve their goals.

"Her passion for improving people's lives through financial planning is at the heart of everything we do at Tarran & Co, and this recognition is thoroughly deserved."

The award caps a successful year for the firm and strengthens its reputation for personalised financial advice across the North East.

Christine has also been nominated for Financial Adviser of the Year – North East for four consecutive years and is a finalist again at the Professional Adviser Women in Financial Advice Awards on November 4.

The company is recruiting three people to expand its team: a financial adviser, paraplanner and marketing executive.

For more information, visit www.tarranandco.co.uk.
