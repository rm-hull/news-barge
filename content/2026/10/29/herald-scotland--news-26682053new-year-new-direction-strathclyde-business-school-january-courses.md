*A wide range of Master’s programmes at Strathclyde Business School in January offer opportunities to advance business careers*

The start of a new year can be a time when people make plans to change or improve their lives and one of the ways to do this is to embark on a new educational programme.

January may seem a long way from the traditional academic year but the good news is, while the majority of academic programmes start in the autumn, there are a number of courses available at Strathclyde Business School for those who’d like to kick off their academic learning in January – the traditional season of New Year Resolutions – and start a programme of learning with a clean slate and renewed motivation.

There’s a wide selection of programmes to choose from –Strathclyde Business School’s January Master’s programmes include:

Accounting, Finance & Data Analytics (MSc): This 12-month programme is for graduates looking to broaden their previous study of accounting or for those already working in accounting looking to expand their knowledge to include the latest developments in the profession.

You will undertake studies in financial management and securities markets and learn to apply cutting edge technology to strategic business decisions.

Business Analysis & Consulting (MSc): This programme will equip you with the skills to analyse organisational challenges and deliver practical solutions, applying business analysis tools through industry-focused projects. There’s plenty of experiential learning with industry-focused projects, external clients and the chance to work on a project which is carried out in collaboration with an external organisation.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21647414.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Why not start a programme of learning with a clean slate and renewed motivation? *(Image: Supplied)*

Business and Management (MSc): The MBM programme offers a broad, yet specific exploration of general management, and skills in management theories and practices. It provides a developmental process that builds your knowledge and confidence in understanding and undertaking hands-on analysis of management issues. It provides a springboard for those looking for careers in management, in business analysis, in consultancy, or even in starting a business. The Consulting in Practice module is a real-world consulting experience, enhancing employability by applying academic theory to live client challenges.

Digital Marketing Management (MSc): The DMM was developed in collaboration with industry in response to a growing need for graduates with both strategic insight and hands-on skills in a rapidly evolving, data-driven digital economy. Specialised digital modules combine academic theory with real-world application of tools and techniques such as SEO, social media and influencer strategy, digital PR, email campaigns, affiliate marketing, and analytics.

Finance (MSc): Finance has transformed faster than any other business function — global capital markets, technology, and competition have reshaped the landscape. This programme answers that evolution, blending both international and practical scope. With a curriculum that balances theory with real-world skills, the MSc Finance offers a dynamic launch pad for a career anywhere in global finance.

International Management (MSc): The MIM programme will develop the knowledge and skills required by international managers and leaders to operate in a global environment and students will benefit from a practical focus on managing and leading in various organisational settings.

Marketing (MSc): The Department of Marketing draws on more than fifty years of internationally leading research, innovation and impact, and global engagement to deliver an outstanding student experience. The MSc Marketing offers research-led teaching and industry input to shape students and prepare them to tackle grand challenges in marketing and wider society - it’s suitable for non-business graduates who want to pursue a career in this area.

Investment & Finance (MSc): Choosing this course will help you be industry-ready by learning the latest techniques and tools used by investment professionals while gaining invaluable practical experience with the AmplifyME Boot Camp and multiple case studies. Students will attain the Bloomberg Market Concepts certificate and gain a comprehensive education in the theory of finance and investments.

An advantage of starting in January is that graduates will be returning to the world of work alongside a smaller cohort than the September graduates, meaning entry into the job market when fewer new graduates are job hunting, helping a now improved CV stand out to employers and providing an extra edge in the search for a new career or promotion.

Strathclyde’s pedigree as a business school which offers ‘useful learning’ is undisputed. So far this year, SBS has performed well in the latest undergraduate rankings carried out by the Complete University Guide, the National Student Survey, the Sunday Times University Guide and The Guardian University Guide, with subjects ranking highly in each.

The QS Global MBA and Business Master’s rankings included Strathclyde’s full-time MBA and four other Master’s programmes recognising them as among the world’s leading postgraduate programmes, while CEO magazine ranked SBS 14th in the world for its Executive MBA programme.

Strathclyde’s programmes are backed up by academic research. Strathclyde was ranked 1st in Scotland and 6th in the UK for Business & Management research income, according to the Chartered Association of Business Schools’ analysis of analysis of HESA (Higher Education Statistics Agency) data, while the University overall was named Daily Mail Research University of the Year 2027, with nearly 90% of research rated as world-leading or internationally excellent.

Strathclyde Business School also holds triple accreditation from international accreditation bodies AMBA, EQUIS and AACSB while programmes across the business school feature endorsements from key organisations such as CIMA, ICAS and CIPD. Scottish postgraduate students may be able to access SAAS support, including a tuition fee loan and, for eligible applicants, a living cost loan. Full details on available support and how to apply are available through SAAS.
