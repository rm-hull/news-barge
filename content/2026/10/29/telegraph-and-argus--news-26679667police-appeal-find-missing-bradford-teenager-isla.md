Police are appealing for help to find a missing teenager from Bradford.

Isla, 16, has been reported missing from the Chellow Dean area of the city and officers are asking the public to help trace her.

The teenager is described as being around 5ft 2in tall, of thin build, with long dark brown wavy hair. Police have also said Isla is not currently wearing her glasses.

## **Description released**

A photograph released as part of the appeal shows Isla with long dark hair and round dark-framed glasses, wearing a dark quarter-zip sports top.

Officers are urging anyone who may have seen Isla, or who has information about her whereabouts, to get in touch.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101, quoting log 1608 of October 28, 2026.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "Have you seen missing Isla? Isla is 16 years old and missing from the Chellow Dean area of Bradford. She is 5ft 2 tall, thin build and she has dark brown long wavy hair. She is not currently wearing her glasses.

Any sightings or information please call 101 and quote log 1608 28/10/2026."
