A North Yorkshire-based television company has been named Production Company of the Year.

Air TV, based at Leeds East Airport, received the honour at the Royal Television Society (RTS) Yorkshire Awards.

The company produces hit series including Bangers & Cash and Yorkshire Air 999, the latter of which also won the High Volume Factual award.

Judges praised the show as "a show with amazing access; gripping and engaging" and "on the edge of your seat stuff with raw human stories".

Matt Richards, managing director of Air TV, said: "These awards are a great recognition for all of our team who work right through the year to deliver our programmes.

"We're really grateful to all of our contributors – from the Mathewson family in Bangers & Cash to auctioneer Angus Ashworth and the paramedics, doctors and pilots of the Yorkshire Air Ambulance."

The RTS Awards recognise the very best talent and programmes across the TV production industry.
