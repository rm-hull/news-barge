Bradford will get an extra £688,000 in funding to end rough sleeping in the district by Christmas.

And the council’s leader says the aim is to keep these people off the street by “tackling the root cause of homelessness.”

The West Yorkshire Combined Authority (WYCA) this week committed £3.7 million to provide accommodation and targeted support for people with the most complex needs across the region.

Of this, Bradford will receive £688,194.

## How many people in West Yorkshire are homeless?

According to government data, at least 141 people are thought to be experiencing rough sleeping every night across the region.

This funding follows Prime Minister Andy Burnham’s national drive to provide a route off the street for everyone by Christmas as part of a new nationwide rough sleeping programme.

## 'Rough sleeping has become far too commonplace'

Tracy Brabin, Mayor of West Yorkshire, said: “I’ve always said everyone should have a warm, safe, and secure place to call home, and with the winter fast approaching, it’s only right we step in with support for people who need it most.

“Rough sleeping has become far too commonplace, and we must do everything we can to make sure nobody is forced to sleep rough in West Yorkshire.

"This is a positive step towards ensuring people experiencing homelessness and rough sleeping can access the safe accommodation and support they need.

“Together with our partners, we are committed to strengthening local efforts to support people experiencing homelessness and rough sleeping.”

The short term funding comes as WYCA works to develop a strategic regional response to tackling homelessness and rough sleeping, backed by £10 million secured through West Yorkshire's first Integrated Settlement last year.

Working alongside local authorities, the funding announced this week will support tailored local approaches that help people experiencing rough sleeping access the accommodation and support they need.

## What the Bradford Council leader has said

Councillor Stephen Place (Reform UK), Leader of Bradford Council said: “We welcome this funding from the West Yorkshire Combined Authority, which will add real momentum to our work supporting people sleeping rough across the district.

"Alongside our partners, we are working tirelessly to make sure everyone sleeping rough in Bradford is offered a route off the streets by Christmas.

"Our Homeless Outreach Partnership won this year's LGC Award for Housing, which shows what can be achieved working in partnership.

"But our aim is not just to help people off the streets, it is to make sure they stay off them, by tackling the root causes of homelessness and supporting them into settled homes."
