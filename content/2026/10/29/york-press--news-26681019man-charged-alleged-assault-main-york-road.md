A man has been charged after an alleged assault on a main road in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the reported incident happened on the A19 near the Fulford Interchange at 2pm on Monday, October 26.

The force said it involved a driver of a Volkswagen Polo and a woman in her 40s.

“A man in his 40s was arrested and later charged with assault,” a police spokesperson said.

“He has since been released on conditional bail while our enquiries continue.”

They urged anyone with information about the alleged assault to come forward.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed this incident or has any dashcam footage of the vehicle during this time,” the police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [william.robertson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:william.robertson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260210759 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
