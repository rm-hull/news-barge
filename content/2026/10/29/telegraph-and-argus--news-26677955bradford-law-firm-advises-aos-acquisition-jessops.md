A HISTORIC photography retailer has been snapped up in a high-profile acquisition.

Bradford law firm Gordons advised long-standing client AO on its purchase of Jessops, the specialist photography chain founded in 1935 and once owned by Dragons’ Den investor Peter Jones CBE.

A multi-disciplinary team from Gordons, led by corporate partner James Fawcett, provided corporate, commercial property, and commercial contracts advice on the deal.

Julie Finnemore, company secretary and legal director at AO, said: "Jessops is a great addition to the AO family and an important step in our strategy of expanding into complementary categories.

"Gordons were a trusted partner throughout the transaction.

"They are highly commercial, responsive and pragmatic, with a real understanding of our business and how we approach risk."

Jessops was acquired by Mr Jones’ PJ Investment Group in 2013.

James Fawcett, corporate partner at Gordons, said: "This is an excellent strategic acquisition for AO.

"Jessops is an iconic British retail brand that will give customers and members more reasons to shop with AO."

Gordons has worked with AO since 2018 and employs 184 people across offices in Bradford and Leeds.

The firm’s clients include Iceland Foods, Morrisons, Ocado Retail, Wren Kitchens, Molson Coors, and Evri.
