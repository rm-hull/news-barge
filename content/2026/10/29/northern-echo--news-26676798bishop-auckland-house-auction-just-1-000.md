A County Durham house is up for auction for just £1,000 - but buyers will need to put in plenty of work to make it a home.

The two-bedroom mid-terrace property at 9 Garden Street, Newfield, near Bishop Auckland, is being sold by online auction with no reserve.

The property is in need of modernisation but could make an ideal first-time buy or investment once renovated, according to estate agents J W Wood.

The auction starts at 10am on Thursday, October 29, with bidding opening 24 hours earlier.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640681.jpg?type=mds-article-620)See inside this Bishop Auckland home, up for auction for just £1,000 *(Image: JWW)*

Inside, the house has two bedrooms, one bathroom and two reception rooms, alongside a kitchen and dining area.

There is also a front garden and a rear yard, complete with an outhouse for storage.

The property comes with no onward chain and UPVC double glazing throughout.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640688.jpg?type=mds-article-620)See inside this Bishop Auckland home, up for auction for just £1,000 *(Image: JWW)*

Newfield is described as a pleasant village a short distance from Bishop Auckland, Spennymoor and Willington.

The house has mains gas, electricity, water and sewerage, with an open fire, back boiler and radiators providing the heating.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640689.jpg?type=mds-article-620)See inside this Bishop Auckland home, up for auction for just £1,000 *(Image: JWW)*

However, prospective buyers should be aware that the property is listed as having non-standard construction and falls within a coal mining reporting area, meaning further searches may be required.

It is also in a selective licensing area and subject to an Article 4 Direction, which could restrict some planning changes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640682.jpg?type=mds-article-620)See inside this Bishop Auckland home, up for auction for just £1,000 *(Image: JWW)*

The property is in council tax band A and has on-street parking.

Despite its £1,000 guide price, buyers should factor in additional costs before making a bid.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640687.jpg?type=mds-article-620)See inside this Bishop Auckland home, up for auction for just £1,000 *(Image: JWW)*

The successful buyer must pay a £2,400 administration fee, including VAT, according to the auction listing.

Bidding starts at the guide price, but the final sale price could be higher. The listing says the reserve may be up to 10 per cent above the guide price.

Buyers should read the legal pack and seek professional advice before committing to the purchase.
