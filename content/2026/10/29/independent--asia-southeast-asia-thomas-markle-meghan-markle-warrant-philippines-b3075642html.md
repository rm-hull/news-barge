Police in the Philippines have been granted a warrant to examine alleged child sex abuse images belonging to the Duchess of Sussex’s father Thomas Markle.

The material, including digital devices and hard drives, will be inspected by officers in the country after a court granter permission to search them, according to media reports.

They were handed over to police by Meghan’s half brother, Thomas Markle Jr, who claims he found them in his father’s home in Cebu. However, police had to acquire a court warrant before inspecting the items.

Thomas Markle Snr denies all the allegations and has claimed the accusations by his son “sick lies”.

Cebu regional prosecutor Graeme Elmido told the BBC that Mr Markle Jr was "adamant" the case be investigated because he believed his father "has to answer for it".

![Thomas Markle Snr denies all allegations](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/23/8/30/Thomas-Markle-Sr.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Mr Markle Jr told *The Sun* last week he found a stash of child sex abuse images belonging to their father at his former home in the Philippines and reported it to local police and the FBI.

The US has an extradition treaty with the Philippines, which means Mr Markle Sr could face trial there if charged, and could face between 10 and 12 years behind bars if found guilty of accessing child abuse images.

Mr Markle Sr has admitted to paying for sex in the southeast Asian country but denies ever possessing a hoard of such material.

Meghan said last week she was shown a letter sent to Scotland Yard in 2018, that made “troubling” claims against her father.

The Metropolitan Police has said it passed the Duchess of Sussex a letter it had received in 2019, which contained a “number of claims about alleged offending outside the United Kingdom”, and was assured that they were investigated.

Meghan sent a final letter to her father in 2025 while he was recovering there from a leg amputation last year, according to reports in the *Sun on Sunday.*

She wrote to say she knows he is an abuser and that he will never see his grandchildren Archie, seven, and Lilibet, five, the outlet said.

In an interview with the *Mail on Sunday,* Mr Markle Sr said he has “never had sex with anyone under the age of 18”.
