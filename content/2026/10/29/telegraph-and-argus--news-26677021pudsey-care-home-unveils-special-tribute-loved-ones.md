A CARE home has opened a new memorial garden.

Hutton Manor Care Home in Pudsey officially opened the garden on Crawshaw Road on Saturday, October 3, with a ribbon-cutting ceremony and speeches attended by more than 30 people.

Guests included residents, families, and members of the community, alongside Councillor Trish Smith and members of the Pudsey and Farsley Royal British Legion.

Zaneta Balcerak, home manager at Hutton Manor, said: "We wanted to create a peaceful and welcoming place where people can come to remember those who were important to them.

"It was particularly meaningful to welcome back families of former residents and see them beside the plaques they had donated in memory of their loved ones."

The memorial garden includes a dedicated Royal British Legion area.

Councillor Trish Smith said: "It was a privilege to help open Hutton Manor’s memorial garden.

"It provides families and members of the community with a thoughtful space to remember loved ones, while the dedicated Royal British Legion area offers an important place for local veterans to pay their respects."

The opening took place during Hutton Manor’s weekly Veterans Café, which brings residents and local veterans together every Saturday morning for conversation and companionship.

Guests were invited into the home for refreshments following the ceremony.
