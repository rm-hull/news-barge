## Aldi supersize White Company candle dupes are back – £70 cheaper than the real thing

Aldi's Inclusion Candles return on November 1, with prices from £5.99 and savings of up to £70 compared with selected The White Company candles. *(Image: Aldi)*

Aldi is bringing back its popular Christmas inclusion candles, with prices starting at £5.99 and shoppers able to save up to £70 compared with The White Company.

The budget supermarket's statement candles will return to stores nationwide from Sunday, November 1, just in time for shoppers looking for affordable Christmas gifts and festive home decorations.

The range comes in three sizes, with the largest weighing a hefty 4.5kg and offering up to 200 hours of burn time.

Each candle is available in two festive-inspired fragrances – Winter Spice and Scandinavian Forest – and features dried fruit, berries and cinnamon sticks encased in the wax for a decorative finish.

![](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/resources/images/21636094.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The range comes in three sizes, with the largest weighing a hefty 4.5kg *(Image: Aldi)*

Aldi says its candles offer savings of up to 83% compared with selected alternatives from The White Company, making them a tempting option for shoppers who want a luxury-looking home accessory without the designer price tag.

## Aldi's Inclusion Candles - prices and savings

### XXL Inclusion Candle – £24.99

Weighing 4.5kg, Aldi's largest candle is designed to make a statement, with multiple wicks and an impressive 200-hour burn time.

* Price: £24.99
* Weight: 4.5kg
* Burn time: 200 hours
* Comparable White Company candle: Winter Indulgence 6 Wick Botanical Candle, £95
* Saving: £70.01, or 74%

### Small Inclusion Candle – £5.99

The smallest option offers a more affordable way to try the trend, with a 660g candle promising up to 60 hours of burn time.

* Price: £5.99
* Weight: 660g
* Burn time: 60 hours
* Comparable White Company candle: Fir Tree Botanical Candle, medium, £35
* Saving: £29.01, or 83%

### Rectangle Inclusion Candle – £12.99

The rectangular design is intended for display on dining tables, mantelpieces and festive arrangements, with a 1kg size and up to 100 hours of burn time.

* Price: £12.99
* Weight: 1kg
* Burn time: 100 hours
* Comparable White Company candle: Fir Tree Botanical Candle, large, £45
* Saving: £32.01, or 71%

## When do Aldi's 'White Company dupe' Inclusion Candles go on sale?

The Inclusion Candle range will be available in Aldi stores nationwide from Sunday, November 1, while stocks last.

With prices starting below £6, the candles could appeal to shoppers looking for an affordable Christmas present or a festive addition to their own home.

However, the products are available in stores only, and shoppers may need to move quickly if previous demand for Aldi's popular homeware ranges is anything to go by.
