An “exceptional” Indian restaurant that has become a “staple” of a County Durham town is going for gold in The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Namaste Spennymoor has been serving diners its “amazing” food from its home on Cheapside since October 2023.

Owned by Taj Qureshi, the restaurant was originally based in Meadowfield, where it operated from 2010 to 2018 before the 55-year-old decided to make the move to Spennymoor.

His son, Adam Qureshi, said the business is “here to stay” after a successful three years, having last year making the Echo’s shortlist for Best Curry House.

And the firm will now fight it out once again in a bid to be crowned the best of the best across County Durham and Darlington for 2026.

“It is nice to be nominated,” Adam, who lives in Darlington, said.

“It is the second time around now which is nice, it means we are consistently getting recognised which is what you want in a business.

“My dad has run the business since 2010 and after lots of success we ended up here in Spennymoor and it looks like we are here to stay.”

![Taj Qureshi, owner of Namaste Indian restaurant in Cheapside, Spennymoor, which has been nominated for The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21643684.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Namaste Indian restaurant in Cheapside, Spennymoor *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The 21-year-old said he feels people know they can rely on Namaste to provide them with good food and a good night.

“I think we have just made a staple in the community now, everyone is much more familiar with us now they know that it is a consistently good place,” he said.

Adam also revealed some of the restaurant’s best sellers: “If we are talking about numbers, it is going to be chicken tikka masalas, as it is everyone’s go-to.

“But the korahi and chilli chicken masala is up there as well.”

Namaste currently boasts a 4.9 star rating on Google and is regularly praised for its “absolutely amazing” food and “fantastic” service.

![Taj Qureshi, owner of Namaste Indian restaurant in Cheapside, Spennymoor, which has been nominated for The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21643685.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The dining area at Namaste, one of ten venues nominated for The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026 *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Namaste is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Arvelli Indian Restaurant, Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.

Readers will decide the winner in a public vote, and we will be featuring each of the businesses nominated in the next two weeks.
