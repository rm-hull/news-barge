*(Image: Middlesbrough Council)*

Final preparations are underway for a free festival of light, music and fireworks in the North East.

Diwali in Middlesbrough 2026 will take place in Centre Square this Saturday, October 31, from 1pm to 7pm.

The event promises an afternoon of music, dance, food and family fun to mark the Festival of Light.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640006.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A feast of entertainment for all is lined up when Diwali comes to Middlesbrough’s Centre Square at the weekend *(Image: Middlesbrough Council)*

Kash Patel, event organiser, said: "Diwali is about coming together with family and friends and embracing the values that Diwali represents such as gratitude, joy and hope for the future.

"It’s an event open to all regardless of faith so come and join us on the day and celebrate our diversity whilst strengthening our unity."

Entertainment will run throughout the day and will be hosted by DJ Shakk from BBC Radio Tees.

Main stage highlights will include king of multicultural pop Raghav and The Voice UK’s Molly J.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640008.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A feast of entertainment for all is lined up when Diwali comes to Middlesbrough’s Centre Square at the weekend *(Image: Middlesbrough Council)*

Street vendors from all over the UK will tempt visitors with a wide selection of sweet and savoury delights as well as ethnic fashion and fabrics and a whole host of other stalls.

Children will be able to take part in free arts and crafts, including lantern making, and enjoy a range of funfair rides.

Other family-friendly activities will include drumming workshops, rangoli design sessions and dance workshops.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640010.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A feast of entertainment for all is lined up when Diwali comes to Middlesbrough’s Centre Square at the weekend *(Image: Middlesbrough Council)*

The evening will end with a Bollywood-themed fireworks display.

The festival is organised by the team behind the Middlesbrough Mela and is promoted to be inclusive of all backgrounds, races, religions and beliefs.

The event is supported by Arts Council England and sponsors including Thirteen Group and Unison.

Diwali is celebrated by Hindus, Sikhs and Jains worldwide and symbolises the triumph of light over darkness and good over evil.

Updates are available by following @DiwaliinBoro on Facebook.
