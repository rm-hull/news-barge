Staff turned a cancelled heritage train into an act of kindness by supporting communities hit by Storm Boelo in County Durham.

Weardale Railway cancelled its popular Afternoon Tea Train on Sunday, October 25, after the storm brought power cuts, heavy rain and damaging winds to the region.

The team delivered the freshly prepared afternoon teas to elderly residents, care workers, emergency services and others affected across Weardale.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641616.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Weardale Railway staff delivered afternoon teas to communities hit by Storm Boelo after a popular train service was cancelled *(Image: Supplied)*

Claire Gibbons, general manager at Weardale Railway, said: "It was disappointing to cancel the train, particularly knowing people had booked to celebrate birthdays and other special occasions.

"But we had all these afternoon teas prepared and wanted to put them to good use.

"With the storm affecting homes and services across the dale, it felt like a good opportunity to do something for our neighbours.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641617.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Weardale Railway staff delivered afternoon teas to communities hit by Storm Boelo after a popular train service was cancelled *(Image: Supplied)*

"Our team headed out to make the deliveries, and the response was lovely.

"We met some very grateful people and were pleased we could bring a little cheer to what was a difficult day for many."

Afternoon teas went to staff at Stanhope Care Home, Weardale Community Hospital, the Angel Trust charity and the local police station, as well as elderly residents in Wolsingham and Stanhope.

Some recipients had been left without power by the storm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641618.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Weardale Railway staff delivered afternoon teas to communities hit by Storm Boelo after a popular train service was cancelled *(Image: Supplied)*

Ms Gibbons said customers who had planned to celebrate special occasions on board still received their afternoon teas, including one grandmother whose granddaughter collected hers.

She added: "We were also touched that so many of our customers chose to rebook their afternoon tea rather than accept a refund.

"We're grateful for that support and look forward to welcoming them on board soon.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641619.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Weardale Railway staff delivered afternoon teas to communities hit by Storm Boelo after a popular train service was cancelled *(Image: Supplied)*

"Weardale Railway has always been about connecting communities, and although our trains couldn't run that day, it was nice to be able to do that differently."

Weardale Railway is a 16-mile heritage line between Bishop Auckland West and Stanhope.

It offers driver days, heritage journeys and educational visits.

The Auckland Project rescued the railway from administration in 2020 and has since invested in repairs and improvements to secure its future as a visitor attraction.

The railway also runs themed events including gin and cheese trains, family events and the popular Santa Express.
