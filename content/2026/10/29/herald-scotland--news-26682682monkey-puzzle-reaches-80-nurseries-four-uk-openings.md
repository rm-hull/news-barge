Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries has reached 80 settings after opening four new nurseries across the UK.

The franchised nursery group opened Monkey Puzzle Colchester in August, followed by a new setting in Radlett in September.

October brought two further openings. Monkey Puzzle St Albans welcomed its first children after being opened by franchisees Tanvi and Anubhav Bhagat. Sam El-Labban and Laila Ibrahim also opened Monkey Puzzle Upminster.

The new nurseries form part of a period of expansion for the company, which describes itself as the UK's largest franchised nursery group.

Colchester is owned by franchisees Manjit and Sandeep Sandhu. Hassan Alquraini owns the Radlett setting, while the St Albans and Upminster nurseries are run by their respective franchisee teams.

Existing franchisee Nitish Patel has also expanded Monkey Puzzle Enfield. A new preschool space has opened in the building next door, providing additional space for children.

Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries managing director Grace Cole said the recent openings reflected work by the company's head office, franchisees and nursery teams.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21648364.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries)*

Grace Cole said: “It’s absolutely fantastic to see so many new settings welcoming children and families over the past few months.”

Grace Cole said: “Every setting brings a fresh opportunity to make a positive difference in each of the local communities they serve.”

The company said its expansion would continue alongside support for franchisees at each stage of running a nursery.

Grace Cole said: “As we grow, it’s critical every franchisee receives the right level of expertise at every stage of their journey.”

Grace Cole said: “For us, it’s about building strong, sustainable businesses that provide the best possible start for children, rather than just a series of increasing numbers on a spreadsheet.”

Mr El-Labban has opened his second setting with Monkey Puzzle. His first nursery is in Purley, and he said support from the company's head office had helped with the Upminster opening.

Sam El-Labban said: “The head office team have been a huge help, particularly when it came to finding the right property for Upminster.”

“Having their guidance and support with the practical side of opening a new nursery has meant we could focus on our team and growing the business.”

“Having been through the process with our first setting in Purley, we know how valuable that support is.”

“Being part of Monkey Puzzle feels like joining a family rather than simply buying into a franchise.”

“It's an amazing network to be part of, and knowing there's always someone to turn to makes a real difference.”

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21648365.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Monkey Puzzle Day Nurseries)*

The Monkey Puzzle network will meet in November for its annual conference at The Crowne Plaza Hotel in Gerrards Cross.

The event will allow franchisees and nursery teams to share experiences, exchange ideas and discuss the company's recent progress.

Grace Cole said: “After such a busy few months, the conference is a great opportunity for our newer franchisees to get to know the wider network.”

“We’ve got plenty to celebrate, and it’ll be exciting to look at how we can carry that progress forward into 2027.”
