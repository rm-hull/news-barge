One of Darlington's newest restaurants has received a seal of approval after a viral social media creator tried the trending spot on a visit to his hometown.

Darlington-born Shane Cooper, now based in Manchester, has amassed over 70,000 followers across his Instagram and Tiktok accounts.

He has gone viral multiple times and is best known online for his comedy and lifestyle videos.

Cooper recently returned home, visiting the Pepe's Piri Piri restaurant, which opened this summer in Darlington's Cornmill Shopping Centre, to try their trendy food.

In a video shared online, Cooper can be seen ordering mild chicken tenders and garlic mayonnaise before trying them in his car.

He said the chicken looked "so good" before praising the opening of the viral restaurant in his hometown, adding: "Darlo doing bits."

Cooper also said: "People say you can't buy happiness, you can."

Pepe's Piri Piri opened in Darlington on August 19 and created 15 jobs in the area, after signage appeared in the town centre teasing its arrival.

It is owned by Ammar, a hospitality operator with sites across the UK, and is one of seven Pepe's sites across the North East.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21296685.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pepe's Piri Piri opened in Darlington on August 19 and created 15 jobs in the area. *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Pepe's was founded in 2007, and serves flame-grilled Piri Piri chicken. The halal chain has recently enjoyed viral status on social media thanks to its popular Chick'n'Rice box.

Its menu includes whole, half and quarter chickens, wings, tender strips, burgers, wraps, rice boxes, salads, burritos and nachos.

Pepe's has grown through franchising and now has more than 250 UK stores, and branches across the world in Pakistan and the United Arab Emirates.

One Darlington customer commented on Cooper's video, echoing his praise for the new branch: "I've been there twice since it opened and it's good."

Another customer wrote on Pepe's social media announcement for their Darlington location: "Just ordered from here.

"Tasted amazing, arrived hot, and great value. Definitely coming here again."

The chain describes itself as one of the largest Piri Piri chicken operators in the UK, with customers dubbing it a 'Nando's rival.'

With praise from both a popular hometown creator and local customers, Darlington's newest Pepe's appears to have made a strong impression since opening its doors this summer.

As the chain continues to grow across the UK and social media users embrace its viral menu items, the Cornmill Shopping Centre branch looks set to remain a popular new addition to the town's fast-food scene.
