The best advent calendars make the countdown to Christmas extra special without breaking the bank.

We tested brands across the market and really put them through their paces. We were pleasantly surprised by just how good value many of the calendars we tested were, offering plenty of treats, surprises and festive fun for the price.

From affordable favourites to more luxurious options, each with prices listed, here are our reviews to help you find the perfect advent calendar.
