# Man, 74, dies after car crashes into lamppost on Yorkshire road

## A 74-year-old man has died after his car left the road and struck a lamppost in Leeds, prompting a police appeal for witnesses.

At around 5.05pm on Sunday (Oct 11), police were called to reports of a crash on South View Road in Yeadon, Leeds.

A black Land Rover Freelander was travelling towards Harper Lane when it left the road and struck a lamppost.

The driver, a 74-year-old man, received medical treatment at the scene but was pronounced dead a short time later.

![South View Road in Yeadon, Leeds](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/11/11/South-View-Road-in-Yeadon-Leeds.png?trim=0,52,0,51&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


West Yorkshire Police's Major Collision Enquiry Team is investigating the circumstances surrounding the crash.

Officers are appealing for anyone who witnessed the incident or has footage that could assist their investigation to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision, or who has footage which may assist is asked to contact the Major Collision Enquiry Team on 101, or by using our LiveChat facility.

“The log reference is 1143 of 11 October.”
