A HORSE and trap is reported to have damaged a vehicle during a crash in York.

The incident happened in Lawrence Street, between 4.25pm and 4.40pm on Sunday (September 27).




A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The horse and trap damaged a vehicle and road off.

“We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the collision or any person that had dashcam or has any information that could assist the investigation.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk) or by calling 101.

Please quote reference 12260190085 when reporting information.
