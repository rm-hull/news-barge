Police have today issued this photograph as part of their investigation into a racially-aggravated assault.

It happened at 11.50pm on Friday 28 August outside the Ainsty pub on Boroughbridge Road.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Racial slurs were shouted at the victim and an e-cigarette was also thrown at them which missed hitting them.

#### Ainsty, York

“Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured as he may have information that will assist our investigation.”
