The finalists of Celebrity Traitors 2026 are said to have been leaked just a few episodes in.

Newsquest will not reveal the details of which celebrities are supposedly going to make it to the final of the BBC show since these results have not been verified, and so as not to spoil the programme.

The 2026 series is still in its early stages at the moment, with only a few celebrities having been 'murdered' or banished from the show.

Fans have seen four episodes so far, with one traitor being caught and four Faithful players leaving.

The latest celebrity to leave the show is Julie Hesmondhalgh, known for playing Hayley Cropper in Coronation Street.

She was banished by the other players as they thought she was a Traitor.

Celebrities who have left so far:

* Richard E. Grant – Traitor (banished)
* Bella Ramsey – Faithful (murdered)
* Julie Hesmondhalgh – Faithful (banished)
* Myha’la – Faithful (banished)
* Amol Rajan – Faithful (murdered)

On Reddit, a viewer warned others to steer clear of any discussion about the supposed finalists online so as not to spoil it for themselves.

They said: “Please be careful online when looking for celebrity traitors season two discussion

“The finalists have been leaked by one of the celebrities friends.

## Who are the biggest stars on series 2 of The Celebrity Traitors?

“I haven't looked into it more than that because I don't want spoilers.

“Many news sites are talking about the leaks if you want to see it but I would discourage it.”

Other Reddit users reassured viewers that while there is said to have been a leak, it has not been made public.

They appreciated the heads up, with one saying: “I am not even looking for it but I appreciate the warning and heads up!

“I will be avoiding anything expect for this Reddit thread.”

Another commented: “Thanks for the heads up. There's always some (not so) bright spark spoiling things for others by trying to make out how clever they are.”

A source said to The Sun: “Because of the leak, who makes the final is now a hot topic at showbiz parties.

“It's also spread how certain players get on, not helped by the fact that another source has been suggesting who's been placed for interviews around the final.

“It's vital the leaks don't make it to gossip forums online and on social media, where fans can find the information.
