Residents at Wetherby Manor care home enjoyed an outdoor theatre performance when the Performance Ensemble brought its production *Bloomin’ in the Garden* to the Wetherby home.

Residents, relatives and staff gathered in the gardens to watch the production, which celebrated nature, community and wellbeing.

> **Caroline Bryon, Home Manager at Wetherby Manor, said:**
>
> Caroline Bryon, Home Manager at Wetherby Manor, said: “We were thrilled to welcome the Performance Ensemble to our home. *Bloomin’ in the Garden* was a fantastic production which was perfectly suited to our gardens and gave residents a wonderful opportunity to enjoy live entertainment outdoors.
>
> Activities like this support residents’ wellbeing, encourage social interaction and help us create meaningful experiences for everyone at the home.

The performance was part of the home’s programme of activities, which includes opportunities for residents to enjoy the arts and take part in shared experiences.

Wetherby Manor provides residential, dementia, assisted living and nursing care.

## About Anchor

Anchor is England’s largest not-for-profit provider of housing and care for older people. It provides housing to rent and buy, alongside residential care services, including specialist dementia care.

The organisation says it serves more than 65,000 residents across almost 55,000 homes in nearly 1,700 locations.
