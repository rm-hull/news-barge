## Why we recommend it

If you're looking for a reasonably priced ergonomic office chair, the Sihoo B100 offers an adaptive lumbar support that's designed to follow your lower back as you change sitting positions. The support moves with you when you lean forward or sit upright.

The chair also features a breathable mesh backrest, which allows air to circulate and helps reduce heat buildup. There's an adjustable headrest for neck support, and a widened seat cushion that distributes weight across your hips and thighs.

You can adjust the seat height from 18.3 to 21.9 inches to suit different desk heights and sitting preferences and the B100 also offers three lockable reclining positions at 110, 125, and 135 degrees.

We haven't reviewed this specific model yet, but we've tested plenty of Sihoo office chairs over the years and they always impress us with their comfort, adjustability, and value.

## Should you buy it?

✅ **Buy the Sihoo B100 Mesh Office Chair if...**

You spend several hours a day working at a desk and want a chair that offers plenty of adjustment options. The adaptive lumbar support, adjustable headrest, and three reclining positions give you flexibility and the breathable mesh backrest will keep you cool when your home office gets warm.

❌ **Skip the Sihoo B100 Mesh Office Chair if...**

You prefer a heavily padded chair or need more control over the armrest positioning. The B100 has flip-up armrests rather than fully adjustable 3D or 4D versions. You should also check the seat dimensions and height range before ordering if you're taller or shorter than average.
