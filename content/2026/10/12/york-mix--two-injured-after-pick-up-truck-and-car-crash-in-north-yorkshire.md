Two people were hurt in a crash on the A1(M) in North Yorkshire.

It happened at 12.19pm on Saturday (10 October) on the northbound carriageway at junction 48 near Boroughbridge.

A Ford Ranger pick-up truck towing a caravan and a Toyota Yaris collided.

#### Crash location

One of the drivers suffered a broken wrist, and a passenger from the same vehicle suffered a suspected fractured rib.

They have now been discharged from hospital.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.”
