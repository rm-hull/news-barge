From its stylish facade to its beautiful interior, this refurbished and updated semi-detached property within one of Nunthorpe’s most desirable residential locations has been thoughtfully remodelled to create a family home where contemporary styling effortlessly complements its character and charm.

The tiled porch – which adds to the kerb appeal of the house – opens into an impressive reception hall with decorative wall panelling that sets the tone for the stylish interior that lies within.

To the front of the property is an elegant living room where a feature fireplace and a large shuttered square bay window create a warm and inviting retreat.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557385.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cap: Looking for a contemporary home that oozes style? This could be it! *(Image: Roseberry Newhouse)*

To the rear of the property is a large part open-plan space comprising a versatile family room and the contemporary adjoining dining kitchen.

Perfect for relaxing and entertaining in, the family room is centred around a striking exposed brick fireplace with an inset log-burning stove, while the heart of the home – the stunning open-plan dining kitchen – has been beautifully appointed with a range of modern cabinetry and an array of integrated appliances alongside generous preparation space.

A vaulted ceiling with Velux roof windows floods the room with natural light, whilst French doors open directly onto the terrace, creating an effortless connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557381.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cap: Looking for a contemporary home that oozes style? This could be it! *(Image: Roseberry Newhouse)*

Upstairs are three well-proportioned bedrooms, each presented to an excellent standard, plus a luxurious family bathroom beautifully designed with a freestanding oval bath, separate walk-in shower and quality contemporary fittings to create a spa-like atmosphere.

The rear garden has been carefully landscaped to include a contemporary porcelain-tiled terrace with glass balustrading which provides an ideal setting for outdoor dining and entertaining alongside an attractive lawned area framed by mature trees, shrubs and established planting.

To the front is a generous driveway that provides ample off-road parking and leads to the detached garage.
