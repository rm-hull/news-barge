INDIE-POP star Suki Waterhouse is set to perform live in Halifax.

The London-born singer-songwriter will headline Live at The Piece Hall on Sunday, June 13, as part of The Loveland Tour.

She will be joined by rising indie-pop talents Nieve Ella and Ruby Roberts.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "We’re absolutely delighted to welcome Suki Waterhouse to Halifax for what promises to be a very special night.

"Suki is a fantastic addition to next summer’s Live at The Piece Hall series, and we’re thrilled to be bringing her distinctive sound and brilliant songs to this incredible setting.

"We’re very proud to continue attracting world-class artists to Halifax, giving audiences here and beyond the chance to experience amazing live music in such a unique and historic venue.

"We can’t wait for this one."

Suki Waterhouse has earned more than 1.5 billion global streams, with her hit Good Looking alone approaching one billion.

Her recent album Loveland features 14 new tracks and includes collaborations with artists such as Mick Fleetwood and Aaron Dessner.

She is also known for performing at Coachella and Lollapalooza, and for supporting Taylor Swift at Wembley Stadium during The Eras Tour.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, October 16 via this LINK.
