Three men who attacked other people on the North Yorkshire coast were among defendants sentenced recently at Scarborough Magistrates Court.

Kiaren Whitwell-Dormer, 20, of St Peters Road, Whitby, pleaded guilty to assaulting a man in Whitby.  
He was given a 12-month community order with 10 days’ rehabilitative activities and 80 hours’ unpaid work. He must pay £200 compensation, £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Nigel Kendrew, 62, of Cavalry Court, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to harassing a woman in Scarborough, and was given a 12-month community order with 10 days’ rehabilitative activities.  
He was fined £440, ordered to pay £500 compensation, £85 prosecution costs and a £176 statutory surcharge and made subject to a five-year restraining order to protect the woman.

Trevor Cordy, 44, of Cambridge Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to assaulting a police emergency worker, carrying a wood shillelagh as an offensive weapon in Scarborough, affray and possessing of cannabis.  
He was given a 24-week prison sentence suspended for 12 months on condition he does six months’ drug rehabilitative activities and 20 days’ rehabilitative activities. He must pay £85 prosecution costs.

Michael Joyce, 39, of Hampton Road, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to two assaults of a woman and threatening to damage her mobile phone.  
He was given a 12-month community order with 20 days’ rehabilitative activities, fined £96, ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £38 statutory surcharge and made subject to a two-year restraining order to protect the woman.

Kevin Jennings, 48, of Ash Road, Filey, pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified and driving without insurance in Filey in March.  
He was given a 12-month community order with 10 days’ rehabilitative activities and banned from driving for three months concurrent with his current ban.  
He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.
