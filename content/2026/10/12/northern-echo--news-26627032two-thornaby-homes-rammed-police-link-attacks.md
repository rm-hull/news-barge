Two homes were rammed overnight in two 'targeted attacks'.

Officers were called to Westbury Street and Northumberland Road in Thornaby just after 8.30pm on Sunday, October 11, after vehicles were driven into two properties.

Fortunately, no one was injured in either incident.

Cleveland Police is linking the incidents and has launched an investigation.

Detective Chief Inspector David Snaith, the senior investigating officer, said: “Cleveland Police is taking reports of incidents in Thornaby this weekend extremely seriously.

“We know that these incidents will be concerning to Thornaby residents, however, we believe that these are targeted attacks, and we do have officers working around the clock to identify and locate everyone believed to be involved.
