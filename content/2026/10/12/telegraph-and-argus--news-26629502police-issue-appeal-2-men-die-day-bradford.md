Coroner’s officers are appealing for help to find the relatives of two Bradford district men who have died.

Alan Kelsey, 74, of Keighley, and Peter Lawton, 69, of Bradford, both passed away on Wednesday, October 7.

A spokesperson for the coroner’s office said: "Coroner's officers are appealing for information to help trace the relatives of Alan Kelsey, from Keighley.

"Coroner's officers are appealing for information to help trace the relatives of Peter Lawton, from Bradford."

Anyone with information is asked to contact the Bradford Coroner’s Office on 01274 438800.
