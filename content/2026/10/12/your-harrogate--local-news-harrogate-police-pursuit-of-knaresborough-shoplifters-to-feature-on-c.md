An operation targeting suspected shoplifters in Knaresborough will feature on Channel 5’s Biker Cops: Elite Squad One tonight (Monday).

The latest episode follows North Yorkshire Police officers as they respond to intelligence that a group of prolific shoplifters are targeting stores across Knaresborough and York.

Biker and traffic officers quickly pick up their trail, with viewers able to see how the operation unfolds.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said:

> “After receiving intelligence that a group of prolific shoplifters are targeting stores across York and Knaresborough, our Biker / Traffic Cops quickly pick up their trail.
>
> "But will we be able to catch up with them?”

The programme offers a behind-the-scenes look at policing across the county, featuring officers and staff from the force.

Tonight’s episode also follows the response to a serious collision involving a car and a motorcycle near Selby.

Elsewhere, a rider on a Sur-Ron fails to stop for officers during a dedicated operation targeting the illegal use of bikes in York city centre.

Tune in to Biker Cops: Elite Squad One on Channel 5 at 8pm tonight to watch the Knaresborough operation unfold.
