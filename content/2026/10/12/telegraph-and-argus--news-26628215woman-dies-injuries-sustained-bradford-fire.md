A woman has died after suffering serious burns in a fire in Bradford.

Emergency services were called to Woodlands Street in the Black Abbey area just after 1pm on Sunday.

The woman, aged in her 80s, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries but died this morning.

## What police have said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A woman who suffered serious burns injuries at an address in Woodlands Street, Bradford, yesterday has sadly died.

"Police were called to the address by fire service colleagues at 1.14pm yesterday afternoon (October 11).

"The woman, aged in her 80s, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries.

"She died earlier this morning.

"The incident is not being treated as suspicious and a file has been prepared for the West Yorkshire Coroner."
