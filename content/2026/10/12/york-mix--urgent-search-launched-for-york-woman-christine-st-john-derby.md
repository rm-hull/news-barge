Police have launched an appeal after a woman went missing from her home.

They are asking York people to look out for Christine St John-Derby, 70, who was last seen at her home address on Byland Avenue at 6.50am today (Monday).

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re very concerned for her welfare, and asking for members of the public to report any sightings.

“She may have headed to the city centre either on foot or using public transport.”

Christine is described as white, about 5ft 1in tall, with mousey hair. She is believed to be wearing a below-the-knee-length brown coat.

If you see Christine, please call 999.

If you have any other information that could help police find her, call us on 101, quoting 12260200573.
