# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of man after vehicle stolen in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak after a motor vehicle was stolen in Harrogate.

The incident happened between 1.50pm and 3.37pm on Friday, September 18 when a black Ford Fiesta car, which was for sale, was taken from the layby on Hookstone Road.

![The police have released a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak after a motor vehicle was stolen from the layby on Hookstone Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/14/49/846395540_950496391009835_7940328321999579055_n.jpeg?trim=95,0,132,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured in the CCTV image as he may have information that will assist our investigation.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260188868 when providing any details regarding the incident.
