POLICE have launched an appeal after an alleged racially-aggravated assault outside a pub in York.

The incident happened at 11.50pm on Friday (August 28) outside Ainsty in Boroughbridge Road.

A spokesperson from North Yorkshire Police said the incident involved racial slurs toward a victim.




An e-cigarette was also reported to have been thrown at the person.

The force said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured as he may have information that will assist our investigation."

Please email [jonathan.gillespie@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:jonathan.gillespie@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Alternatively, contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260168880 when providing information.
