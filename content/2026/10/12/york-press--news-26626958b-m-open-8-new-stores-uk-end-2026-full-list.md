B&M is continuing its rapid expansion with eight new stores set to open by the end of 2026.

The discount retailer already operates 799 stores across the UK, which welcome more than four million customers each week.

The major retail chain has opened several new sites already in 2026, most recently in Carmarthen, Plymstock, and Waltham Cross.

## Full list of 8 new B&M stores opening before the end of 2026

B&M is set to open another eight new stores in the coming months:

* **Acton** - Unit 4C, The Oaks Shopping Centre, Acton, London, W3 6RE (October 24)
* **Newtown** - Pool Road, Newtown, Powys, SY16 3AF (November 7)
* **Isle Of Wight** - Unit 1, Newport Retail Park, Godric Road, Newport, Isle of Wight, PO30 2LF (November 27)
* **Middleton** - Unit S8, Middleton Shopping Centre, Middleton, Greater Manchester, M24 4EL (November 27)
* **Colchester** - Unit 1, St John's Walk, Colchester, Essex, CO2 7AL (November 28)
* **Kingswinford** - 779 High Street, Kingswinford, West Midlands, DY6 8AA (November 28)
* **Stirling** - Store 2, Thistles Shopping Centre, Goosecroft Road, Stirling, FK8 2ED (November 28)
* **Southport** - Unit 2, 41-49 Chapel Street, Southport, Merseyside, PR8 1AL (December 3)

## 110 former Whitbread restaurants set to reopen

Meanwhile, around 110 former Whitbread pubs and restaurants are set to reopen under new ownership.

Whitbread closed all of its 261 standalone branded restaurants last month as part of a five-year strategy to save £250 million, resulting in the loss of around 3,800 jobs.

The closures affected five Whitbread brands:

* Brewers Fayre (89 locations)
* Beefeater (106)
* Bar + Block (20)
* Table Table (32)
* Cookhouse + Pub (14)

Now, Queensway Inns has taken over 53 sites and plans to relaunch them as "value pubs".

Common Table Group, a newly established hospitality firm, also announced earlier this week that it had acquired 57 sites, including one Premier Inn hotel.

The new sites are expected to create more than 1,400 jobs.
