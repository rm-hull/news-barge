Police estimated the turnout at **45,000**, while organisers put the figure at** 60,000**. Among the banners and placards, protesters carried a giant inflatable screaming globe to give the Earth a human voice and demand** urgent climate action**.

Organised by the **Climate Coalition** and other environmental groups, the march ended near the**EU institutions**. Demonstrators called for an** end to fossil-fuel subsidies**,** higher taxes**on the industry, and a** fair transition to cleaner energy**.

The demonstration followed a summer of **record-breaking temperatures** and**repeated heatwaves** across Europe. Belgium also experienced**deadly heat**, while** wildfires**hit the High Fens nature reserve. Expectations for** COP31 remain low**. The** United States has stepped back**from international climate efforts under President Donald Trump, while** climate action has slipped down the political agenda** in Europe.

The European Commission is also proposing a **one-year delay**(from 2027 to 2028) to** reporting requirements for importers of fossil fuels**under the EU’s methane rules. The change would require approval by** EU lawmakers and member states**.
