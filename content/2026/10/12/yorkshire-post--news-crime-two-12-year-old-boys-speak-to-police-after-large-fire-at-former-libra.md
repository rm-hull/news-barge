# Two 12-year-old boys speak to police after large fire at former library

## A large blaze ripped through the former library building

Two 12-year-old boys have now come forward to speak to police in connection with a large fire in Billingham town centre on Tuesday (October 6).

The fire in the derelict, former library building on Queensway in the town caused significant damage to the building, and disruption to shops, business and members of the public with the closure of several roads whilst emergency services dealt with the fire.

A 14-year-old boy was arrested on Wednesday October 7.

He has since been bailed whilst enquiries continue, police said.

Two 12-year-old boys came forward and spoke to police voluntarily on Thursday October 8.

Detective Inspector Barnes of Stockton CID has said "We have now conducted a prompt and thorough investigation into the fire at the former library.

“I would like to pass on my thanks to the local community who have greatly supported the investigation by providing key information. I

“I can confirm that two 12-year-old boys have now been interviewed by detectives and we will now consider next steps."
