Scott Campbell is the head chef at Badger & Co in Edinburgh.

Named after one of the characters in Kenneth Grahame's classic children's novel The Wind in the Willows, Badger & Co occupies a site on the same street where the author was born in 1859.

Inspired by Mr Badger and The Wind in the Willows story, the Castle Street restaurant has recently 'turned a new page ' and is now taking its culinary adventures beyond the ‘Wild Wood’ with a creative dining experience that celebrates the best local and seasonal produce

Leading a team of eight in the kitchen, Campbell has overseen the launch of a new seasonal menu showcasing the quality and diversity of ingredients from Scotland’s land and sea.

This week, he takes on our Q&A.

**When did you realise you wanted to be a chef?**  
From a young age, I always wanted to be a plumber, but after a problem with my leg, that wasn’t an option anymore. Once I returned to school, cooking was something I enjoyed, so from there I really wanted to get into the industry.

**What was your first kitchen job like?**  
It was in a hotel with a lot of frozen food to begin with. After 12 months, our senior team completely changed, and it became more fresh food-focused, with lots of training, which I really enjoyed. I have a lot to thank the management for. At the time, they gave me an opportunity and really looked after and developed me.

**What does "good food" mean to you?**  
Good food to me is something fresh, seasonal and flavoursome. Something that would bring a smile to the faces of the guests or my family, depending on where I’m cooking.

**Can you share a memory of your worst kitchen disaster?**  
I think it's the worst, but also probably pretty common for most young chefs: not closing the fryer properly and having to clean up all the oil that's all over the kitchen. Thankfully, I never made a habit of that.

**Where is your favourite place to eat out?**  
I don't really have a favourite, but I’ve always been a fan of the Six by Nico and Lennox concepts. I also really enjoy my visits to Taisteal.

**What's the best thing you've cooked recently?**  
Recently, I'd say our venison and lamb specials. Some of our talented team have been creating some amazing specials, and it’s been a pleasure cooking and tasting these over the last few weeks.

**Read more:What do you think is the biggest misconception people have about working in a professional kitchen?**  
I think a lot of people don’t understand the heavy schedules you have and how they take their toll physically on individuals.

**If you weren't a chef, what do you think you'd be doing instead?**  
I do enjoy a little bit of gardening, so possibly a landscaper.

**Is there one ingredient or kitchen tool you think everyone should have at home?**  
I don’t have one myself, but I think a Thermomix. It’s almost like having an extra chef at times.

**What's been the standout highlight of your career so far?**  
I'd say being head chef at Badger & Co has been the best part of my career so far. The journey I’m on at this restaurant has been different to the rest.
