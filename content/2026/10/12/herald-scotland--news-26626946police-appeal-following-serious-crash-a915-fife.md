Police are appealing for information following a serious crash in Fife.

Around 10.20am on Sunday, October 11, a white BMW 3 Series and a blue New Holland tractor crashed on the A915 south of St Andrews.

The driver of the BMW, a 20-year-old man, was taken to Ninewells Hospital in Dundee for treatment to serious injuries.

Two male passengers, both 20, were taken to the same hospital. One with life-threatening injuries and the other as a precaution.

The driver of the tractor, a 46-year-old man, was also taken as a precaution.

The road was closed and has since been reopened.

Sergeant Phil Winning said: “Our enquiries remain ongoing to establish the full circumstances of this incident.

“I am appealing to those who witnessed the crash and have yet to speak to officers or anyone who was in the area prior and has information that could assist, to come forward.

“Motorists are also asked to check dashcam footage and share anything which may be relevant with officers.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 1034 of 11 October, 2026.
