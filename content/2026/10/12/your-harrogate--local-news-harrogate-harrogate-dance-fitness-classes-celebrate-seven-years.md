A dance fitness group is celebrating seven years of classes across Harrogate, Knaresborough and Boroughbridge.

Clubbercise with Gilly, run by Gilly McBride, will mark its seventh anniversary on Wednesday 21st October, with two half-price launch sessions also taking place this month.

The classes combine dance fitness with disco lights, glowsticks and club classics in a darkened room, welcoming participants from their late teens to over 80.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6acc872664fb0bc1899.jpg)

Gilly McBride said:

> “Our Clubbercise with Gilly classes are more than just a dance exercise class, as we bring friendships, fun, support and bright to each other’s weeks, workouts and lives.
>
> “We are a true community group exercise class, who are still glowing strong seven years on because of our community spirit and the fun and friendships we share with each other in and out of class.”

To celebrate the launch of new class nights, sessions at Coronation Hall in Boroughbridge on Wednesday 14th October and St John Fisher School in Harrogate on Monday 19th October will cost £3.45 each.

The Boroughbridge session starts at 7:15pm, while the Harrogate class begins at 6:30pm.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6acc87632715d55bccf.jpg)

The group will also take part in the warm-up and Moonlight Glow Walk for Saint Michael’s Hospice, continuing its involvement from previous years.

Gilly said:

> “Our classes are all about the warm welcome, enjoying fun fabulous exercise and feeling your best you… your ‘happiest you’ by sharing fun and support with others whilst dancing at your level to great tunes.”

Classes run on Mondays at St John Fisher School in Harrogate at 6:30pm, Tuesdays at St John’s Primary School in Knaresborough at 6:45pm, Wednesdays at Coronation Hall in Boroughbridge at 7:15pm, and Thursdays at St John Fisher School at 6:45pm.

Regular sessions cost £6.95, with class passes offering prices from £5.50 per session.
