# Exciting new restaurant opens at Yorkshire shopping centre

## We have lots of photos from the major new opening

![A look at the exciting new restaurant](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/incoming/article34751871.ece/ALTERNATES/s810/2_039A8251.jpg)

Take a look at this exciting new restaurant which has launched at a Yorkshire shopping centre.

We have seen Hills Kitchen welcome its first guests at Batley Plaza. The new eatery serves Caribbean cuisine.

A Hills Kitchen spokesperson said: “Our vision is to bring together bold Caribbean flavours, warm hospitality and a welcoming setting where people can enjoy great food with family and friends.”

Additionally, the team from Batley Plaza posted on their Instagram account: “Expect mouth-watering jerk meats, flavour-packed rice, fresh sides and plenty more, all served from their brand-new spot right here inside Batley Plaza.”
