POLICE are appealing for information following a road incident that left a driver fearing they would be forced off the road.

The incident happened between 2.30pm and 2.40pm on Monday, October 5, on the A170 Pickering Road, B1261 Seamer Road, Ayton Road, and Scarborough Road, between West Ayton and Seamer.

It involved a silver Kia Sportage, which was reported to have been driving carelessly towards another vehicle.




North Yorkshire Police said: "The victim felt the other car was trying to run them off the road as they kept swerving towards them."

Officers are appealing for witnesses and anyone with CCTV or dashcam footage.

Anyone with information can contact police online or by email at [kyra.marshall1@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:kyra.marshall1@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Those unable to use these options can call 101, quoting reference 12260196327.
