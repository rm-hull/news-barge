North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following multiple reports of cars being damaged in Harrogate.

The incidents are believed to have happened in the early hours of Monday (October 26), between 2am and 4am, on King Edwards Road and Jesmond Road.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Officers are carrying out enquiries and are asking anyone who witnessed the incident, saw any suspicious activity in the area, or has information that could assist the investigation, to get in touch.”

If you can help, you should email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk)

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260210752 when providing any details regarding the incident.
