Police are appealing for the assistance of the public to trace a 48-year-old woman who was last seen in Caithness on Tuesday, October 6.

Zuzana Gabrisova, who is from the Czech Republic, arrived in Wick by train around 5pm on Friday, October 2, and was last seen at holiday accommodation in John O’Groats around 4pm on Tuesday.

She is described as white, around 5ft 6ins with brown curly hair. It isn’t known what she may be wearing, but she is believed to be carrying walking equipment and rucksack.

Inspector Daniel Jack said: “We are very concerned for Zuzana’s wellbeing and we urge anyone who has seen her since 4pm on Tuesday, 6 October, to please contact us.

“We believe she headed to the Sannick Bay area and officers are carrying out extensive enquiries in the area, with support from the Coastguard, Dive and Marine Unit and local mountain rescue teams.

“Anyone with information that may assist our enquiry should contact 101, quoting incident number 1809 of Wednesday, 7 October, 2026.”
