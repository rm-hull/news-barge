Property consultancy Savills has announced two senior appointments in its Scottish residential division, naming new leaders for its country house team and west of Scotland business.

Harry Maitland has been appointed head of the Scottish Country House team, while Peter Walker will lead the residential business across Glasgow and the west of Scotland.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21570818.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Peter Walker. *(Image: Chris Watt Photography)*

The appointments form part of Savills’ strategy to develop talent from within the business and strengthen its Scottish leadership team.

Mr Maitland succeeds Jamie Macnab, who led the Scottish Country House team for 36 years and helped establish Savills as a leading adviser in Scotland’s prime country house market. Mr Macnab will now focus on the St Andrews market, where he remains a specialist.

Mr Maitland first joined Savills 16 years ago and returned in 2019 after a period in Canada. He has worked closely with Mr Macnab and is regarded as an experienced adviser in Scotland’s prime residential sector.

Mr Walker was promoted to head of residential in Glasgow earlier this year and will now oversee the wider west of Scotland business, covering an area stretching from Dumfries to Inverness. He joined Savills in 2018.

Cameron Ewer, head of residential for Scotland and the North West of England at Savills, said: "Harry and Peter are outstanding examples of the calibre of people we have across our residential business.

"They have built deep market knowledge and trusted client relationships over many years, and their appointments enhance our leadership team as we continue to support clients across Scotland."

Harry Maitland, head of the Scottish Country House team at Savills, said: "It is a real privilege to take on this role and build on the strong foundations already in place.

"Having spent much of my career at Savills, I am proud to lead such an outstanding group of agents and negotiators, whose expertise, professionalism and commitment to clients make the business such a rewarding place to work.

"Scotland’s country house market continues to offer exciting opportunities, and I look forward to working with colleagues across the country as we continue to provide the highest levels of advice and service for our clients."

Peter Walker, head of residential across Glasgow and the west of Scotland at Savills, said: "Having joined Savills in 2018, I am proud to have taken on this role and to lead such a talented team.

"The strength of our business has always been the quality of our people and their deep knowledge of the markets in which they operate.

"With my team, I look forward to continuing to deliver market-leading advice across Glasgow and the West of Scotland."
