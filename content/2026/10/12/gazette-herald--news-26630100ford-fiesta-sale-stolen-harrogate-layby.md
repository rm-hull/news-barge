A CAR for sale has been 'stolen' in North Yorkshire.

The black Ford Fiesta, which was listed for sale, was reported to have been stolen from Hookstone Road layby in Harrogate between 1.50pm and 3.37pm on Friday (September 18).

North Yorkshire Police has released an image of a man officers say they would like to speak to in connection with the incident.




A force spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured as he may have information that will assist our investigation.

"You can do this by emailing [keilee.wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:keilee.wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or calling 101.

"Please quote reference 12260188868 when providing information."
