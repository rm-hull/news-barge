A MAJOR running event in a North Yorkshire coastal town brought thousands of people together for a day of sport and fundraising.

Crowds gathered in Scarborough on Sunday (October 11) for the return of the McCain Yorkshire Coast 10K, along with 5K and fun runs, transforming the town into a vibrant hub of athletic achievement and community spirit.

The 10K and fun runs sold out ahead of the event, reflecting its popularity on the autumn running calendar.




![2026 McCain Yorkshire Coast 10K, 5K and Fun Run](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21567592.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Organisers said participants were cheered on throughout the course by enthusiastic supporters lining the route. *(Image: Contributed)*

Mike Tomlinson, CEO of organisers Jane Tomlinson’s Run For All, said: "A huge congratulations and thank you to everyone who took part.

"It was fantastic to see thousands of people come together in Scarborough, with both the 10K and Fun Runs selling out.

"The incredible demand helped generate even more funding for charities locally and beyond while creating a brilliant atmosphere along the coastline.

"From those chasing personal bests to first-time runners and fundraisers, every participant helped make the day special."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21567591.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The women's 10K race saw Sarah Platten take top spot in an impressive time of 00:38:54. Katie Alcock secured second place in 00:39:38, closely followed by Krista Keough, who finished third in 00:39:42. *(Image: Contributed)*

The 10K race began at 9.45am, starting from Scarborough Spa and following a coastal route through South Bay, past the harbour, and around the headland into North Bay.

Runners then passed the open air theatre before returning to the Spa for a scenic finish overlooking the Yorkshire coast.

Spectators lined the route, cheering on runners of all ages and abilities.

Harry Butterworth set a new men’s 10K course record, finishing in 33 minutes and 13 seconds.

Callum James took second place in 34:01, and Dan Applebee finished third in 34:24.

In the women’s 10K, Sarah Platten claimed victory in 38:54, with Katie Alcock second in 39:38 and Krista Keough third in 39:42.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21567587.jpg?type=mds-article-620)In the men's 10K race, Harry Butterworth set a new course record with an impressive 00:33:13. Callum James finished second in 00:34:01, while Dan Applebee completed the podium places with a time of 00:34:24. *(Image: Contributed)*

Participants were encouraged to raise money for charities of their choice or support the event’s partner charities.

Each finisher received a distinctive slate medal, and in partnership with McCain Foods, a tree will be planted for every participant to create an environmental legacy alongside the fundraising.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21567589.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Organisers said a vibrant atmosphere greeted runners as they crossed the finish line *(Image: Contributed)*

Mr Tomlinson thanked supporters and organisers for helping to make the event possible.

He said: "We’d also like to thank McCain Foods for their continued support as title sponsor and Scarborough Athletics Club, whose ongoing dedication and support play a vital role in making the event possible year after year.

"We’re proud that the event not only inspires people to get active and achieve their goals but also raises vital funds for good causes and leaves a lasting legacy in the community."

Andrew Hoff, managing director at McCain Great Britain and Ireland, said: "The Yorkshire Coast events not only inspire people to get active and connect with one another, but also help support important charitable causes."
