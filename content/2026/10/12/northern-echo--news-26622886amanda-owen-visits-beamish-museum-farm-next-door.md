Amanda Owen and her children are set to visit one of County Durham's best-known attractions as they seek inspiration for their new family home in the latest episode of Our Farm Next Door.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess and her family will head to Beamish Museum in Monday night's (October 12) instalment of the More4 series as work continues on their ambitious renovation of Anty John's.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560465.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda, Philip and kids look at the cupboard bed at Beamish Museum *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Amanda, joiner Philip and the children will explore the popular open-air museum in search of ideas for a traditional cupboard bed to be built at the farmhouse.

The visit comes as the Owens continue transforming the historic property into a new family home, with the latest episode showing the challenges of renovating a remote farmhouse in the Yorkshire Dales.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560463.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda inside water tank at Anty John's *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

February's difficult weather threatens to slow progress, with the family battling wind, mist and rain as they attempt to install a piped water supply at Anty John's.

However, there is some good news for the renovation project when the long-awaited floorboards finally arrive at the property on the back of a tractor.

The fourth series of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids follows the family as they continue restoring the farmhouse, with cameras capturing both the building work and everyday life at Ravenseat.

Away from the renovation, the latest episode will see Amanda's son Sid take his tractor exam, which could allow him to carry out more jobs around the farm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560466.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sid takes his tractor exam *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Meanwhile, Annas has her hands full trying to stop the family's new puppies from causing chaos, while Tony the Pony receives some much-needed attention with a brush down and hoof polish.
