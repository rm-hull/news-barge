There is plenty of drama in store for Emmerdale viewers this week, with family tensions rising, new alliances forming and several residents finding themselves at the centre of potentially life-changing decisions.

Joe narrowly avoids one mistake when Thea prevents him from driving while drunk.

However, Serena remains suspicious of Thea's motives and fears her actions are part of a wider plan to gain Kim's trust before betraying her.

Meanwhile, Joe sets another plan in motion by recruiting Sadie to spy on Caleb and Ruby Milligan. Although he warns her that the couple have a strong relationship, he hopes she will be able to uncover useful information.

## **Sadie causes concern among the Milligans**

Sadie's growing involvement soon attracts attention within the village.

Cain becomes increasingly worried that she is attempting to get close to his brother Caleb, but Caleb remains confident he can handle the situation.

His self-assurance may be tested, however, as Sadie's arrival threatens to create fresh complications for the Milligan family.

## **Gabby attempts to put things right**

Elsewhere, Gabby tries to make amends after missing Jimmy's funeral.

In an effort to repair relationships, she sells Hide to Robert and gives the proceeds to Nicola.

However, it is the revelation surrounding her recent hospital stay that has the greatest impact, helping to improve her relationship with her aunt and offering an opportunity for reconciliation.

## **The stakes rise at poker night**

Robert decides to host a high-stakes poker game, bringing together several familiar faces.

Joe and Jai are among those taking part, while Claudette's unexpected involvement is likely to raise a few eyebrows.

Kev is also keen to join the game, setting the scene for an evening where fortunes, loyalties and reputations could all be put on the line.

## **Fresh Dingle tensions emerge**

The Dingles continue to rally around Vinny as he prepares to face trial.

At the same time, Samson's release creates unease within the family, particularly for Sam, Lydia and Moira.

His return threatens to reopen old wounds and reignite tensions, leaving some family members questioning what the future may hold.

With secrets, rivalries and emotional confrontations unfolding across the village, viewers can expect another eventful week in the Dales.
