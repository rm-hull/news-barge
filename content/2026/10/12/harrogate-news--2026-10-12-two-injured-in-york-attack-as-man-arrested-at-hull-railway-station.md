**Two men were taken to hospital after an attack involving a bladed weapon on Leeman Road in York on Sunday afternoon. A man was later arrested at Hull Railway Station.**

North Yorkshire Police were called to Leeman Road at 2.19pm on Sunday 11 October 2026 following reports of an attack involving three men, during which a bladed weapon was seen.

Officers arrived within minutes and found two injured men. Both were taken to hospital for treatment, although their injuries are not believed to be life-threatening.

Police launched an extensive search across York city centre to find the suspect. The operation involved armed officers, police dog units and neighbourhood policing officers.

Following enquiries in York, with support from Humberside Police, a man was arrested at Hull Railway Station later that afternoon.

He was arrested on suspicion of serious assault and possession of an offensive weapon in a public place. He remains in police custody, according to North Yorkshire Police.

## Police appeal for witnesses

A cordon remained in place on Leeman Road while police continued their enquiries.

Anyone who witnessed the attack, or who has relevant CCTV, dashcam or mobile phone footage, is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference **12260200081**.

Enquiries are ongoing.
