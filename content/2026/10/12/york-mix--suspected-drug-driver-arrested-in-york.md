A suspected drug driver has been arrested in York.

The news of the incident came in a community message by PC Dave Ellison of North Yorkshire Police.

He wrote: “Based on information from the community officers from the York Outer Neighbourhood Policing Team located and stopped a vehicle of note.

“The driver tested positive for drugs at the roadside and was arrested on suspicion of drug driving.

“The driver has been bailed pending toxicology testing.”

He said if you have any concerns of anyone drug driving, or involved in dangerous driving, “please contact a member from your local Neighbourhood Policing Team”.

Alternatively, log onto to the North Yorkshire Police website and always dial 999 in an emergency.
