A third major parcel of farmland has been put up for sale.

Lister Haigh, a rural property consultancy, has listed nearly 30 acres of Grade 2 agricultural land near Tadcaster, following the successful sale of two other plots in the area earlier this year.

Located at Healaugh, four miles from Tadcaster, the land is currently grassed but has previously supported combinable and root crops.

James Neilson-Clark of Lister Haigh said: "This latest sale offers buyers the flexibility to purchase the entire field or one of three smaller lots.

"The land will no doubt appeal to a variety of purchasers, including those with equestrian interests, as well as offering opportunities for grazing, habitat management or sporting use, alongside its potential as a productive arable unit.

"We’ve already sold two other parcels of land in the area this year, so we expect plenty of interest from both owner-occupiers and investors."

The full 29.77-acre plot has a guide price of £450,000.

It is also available as three separate lots of 7.75, 7.80, and 14.22 acres, priced at £125,000 each for the smaller lots and £200,000 for the larger.

Earlier this year, Lister Haigh sold 78.40 acres at Wighill Park with a guide price of £840,000, as well as 16.15 acres off Wighill Lane at Healaugh, which was marketed with a guide price of £190,000.
