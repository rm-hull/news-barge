## York readers asked to nominate their favourite curry house for 2026 award

This is your chance to vote for your favourite curry house in York. *(Image: Newsquest)*

Who serves the best curry in York? It's time for readers of The Press to decide.

York is home to some outstanding curry houses, serving everything from traditional favourites to modern twists on classic dishes.

Now we're asking you to tell us which restaurant deserves to be crowned The Press Best Curry House 2026.

**READ MORE: Jaipur Spice, York, celebrates Best Curry House award**

Last year's award was won by Jaipur Spice, which celebrated after receiving fantastic support from readers across the city. Now the search is on to find this year's champion.

Whether it's your favourite takeaway, the restaurant you visit for special occasions, or the place that never disappoints, we want to hear from you.

Nominations are now open and will close at midnight on Sunday, October 18.

To nominate, simply complete our online form at www.surveymonkey.co.uk/r/YPBEST or scan the QR code featured in our Best Curry House promotion.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21562117.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scan this QR code to nominate your favourite curry house in York. *(Image: Newsquest)*

Once nominations have closed, we will count the votes and reveal the top 10 finalists on Monday, October 19.

The finalists will then battle it out in a public vote, with voting slips appearing in The Press from Monday, October 26 until Saturday, November 7.

Readers will be able to collect and submit voting coupons to support their favourite finalist and help decide who takes the title.

So don't miss your chance to have your say.

If there's a curry house you think stands out for its food, service and popularity, nominate it today and help us crown York's Best Curry House 2026.

Every nomination counts.
