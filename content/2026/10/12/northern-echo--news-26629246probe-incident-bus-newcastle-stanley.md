An investigation has been launched after an incident on a bus heading to County Durham.

Officers have been made aware of a public order incident on the bus from Newcastle to Stanley on Saturday (October 10) night.

Durham Police said officers are speaking to all parties involved as part of the probe, asking that people do not share the footage any further.

A spokesman said: “Police are aware of a public order incident which took place on the Newcastle to Stanley bus on Saturday evening.

“Officers are speaking to all the parties involved in the incident as part of an investigation.

“Police are aware of video circulating of the incident and would ask that the footage is not shared further as it may hinder the investigation”
