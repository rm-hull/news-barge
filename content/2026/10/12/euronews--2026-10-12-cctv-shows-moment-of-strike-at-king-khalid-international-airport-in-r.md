CCTV footage has captured the moment a missile struck King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on October 10, 2026. The video shows the strike hitting the terminal resulting in a fireball explosion, sending debris falling from the ceiling onto the travelers below.

The attack killed at least 13 people and injured more than 300, while forcing the airport to suspend operations and cancelling more than 600 flights, according to Saudi authorities.
