## Donald Trump says Russia and Ukraine have agreed an energy ceasefire. Volodymyr Zelenskyy says it was news to him. As he addresses the Warsaw Security Forum, we hear from Latvia's FM Baiba Braže, Doha after a deadly Riyadh airport attack, and France's debt fears.

**Sasha Vakulina** analyses Donald Trump's announcement of an immediate energy ceasefire, what has been agreed, and what his call for Ukraine to replace**Volodymyr Zelenskyy** says about US–Ukraine relations.

**Maria Tadeo** reports from the Warsaw Security Forum, where Zelenskyy addresses delegates as he seeks to shore up European support.

Interview with **Baiba Braže**, Latvian Foreign Affairs Minister, as Europe’s eastern flank is facing another moment of heightened tension amid growing concerns over Russian drones, airspace violations and a broader campaign of hybrid pressure against NATO.

**Laila Humairah** reports from Doha, Qatar, on this weekend's attack on Araba Saudi's King Khalid International Airport, which killed at least 12 people and injured more than 300. The Houthis, suspected to be behind the strike, are warning airlines and passengers to stay away from Saudi airports.

French economist and essayist **Nicolas Bouzou** discusses France's dangerous economic and political balancing act as thousands of students are taking to the streets, demanding more resources and better conditions, while the government struggles to repair public finances under severe pressure.
