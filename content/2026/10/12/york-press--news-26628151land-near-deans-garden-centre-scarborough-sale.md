## Land next to popular North Yorkshire garden centre up for sale

The land is next to Dean's Garden Centre *(Image: Howie Property, Northallerton)*

LAND close to a popular North Yorkshire garden centre with potential for a variety of different uses has been listed for sale.

An eight acre patch of land - located next to Dean's Garden Centre in Seamer Road, on the outskirts of Scarborough - has gone onto the market.

The "underutilised grassland", which is is currently split between two separate owners, is said to be potentially suitable for several uses, subject to planning permission.

These uses include residential, retail, leisure, a budget hotel, and a pub, however, according to the listing, a positive pre-application has been made for a 72-bed care home and 34 assisted living bungalows.

The site is said to be "highly visible" from Seamer Road, a main route into Scarborough, and it is located next to Seamer Road Park and Ride, and the Scarborough train line, which connects with York and Hull.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21569902.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An aerial shot of the land *(Image: Howie Property, Northallerton)*

According to the listing, most of the site falls within Flood Zone 1, with just a small section in the north-eastern corner in Flood Zone 2.

Access to the site is via the existing road used by Dean's Garden Centre, which is immediately to the south of the land.

The price of the land is available on application, however the listing states that individual plot sales will be considered.
