Kremlin rejects Zelenskyy's idea of a 60-day ceasefire and refuses to comment on Trump's 'energy truce'

The Kremlin spokesperson, Dmitry Peskov, has said Russia rejects the idea of a temporary ceasefire, telling reporters that Moscow wanted a long-term peace deal instead.

“In the Kremlin, both ‌President Putin and officials at other levels have repeatedly stated that ​we do not want a ceasefire,” said Peskov. “We want long-term peace and are prepared to do everything possible ⁠to achieve it.“

Peskov’s comments were a rejection of a ⁠60-day ceasefire idea proposed by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy ⁠on Sunday to allow ⁠his ​country to hold presidential elections. Kyiv has accused Russia of violating temporary truces when ceasefires between the two sides have been agreed to in the past.

Peskov was asked to comment on Trump’s self-declared “energy ceasefire” between Russia and Ukraine and refused to be drawn on it, only saying:

Contacts continue. As soon as we have something to say on the matter, we will inform you.

Merz’s government plunged into further crisis after key minister's surprise resignation

In other news, German Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s government came under renewed pressure this morning after a key minister from its junior coalition partner, the SPD, unexpectedly quit.

Baerbel Bas, labour minister and co-leader of the centre-left SPD, cited personal reasons for her resignation, saying she needed time to grieve after the death of her stepdaughter.

But she also criticised Merz’s conservative Christian Democratic Union (CDU), which is in crisis after heavy recent electoral defeats, notably the far-right AfD’s victory in Saxony-Anhalt last month.

“I must unfortunately note that the public attacks against me – regrettably including those from within the ranks of our coalition partner – have continued to intensify during this difficult time for me personally,” Bas said. She will reportedly replaced as labour minister by Alexander Schweitzer, one of the SPD’s vice-chairs.

The resignation comes amid turmoil for the government, which is grappling with a sluggish economy and low poll ratings.

We are ready to respect an energy ceasefire if Russia does, Zelenskyy says

Zelenskyy said Russia is trying to tactically use the period of cold weather against Ukraine, targeting the country’s power plants and power grids.

“This winter if Russia does not agree to an energy ceasefire, we will respond,” Zelenskyy said, adding that he supports Trump’s energy ceasefire proposal, but vows that if Russia doesn’t agree to it, then Ukraine “will respond”.

“If Russia wants us to stop our long-range strikes against its oil refineries and oil exports, then Russia must stop its strikes against our energy system and critical infrastructure, and stop killing our people. This is only fair,” Zelenskyy said.

“We are ready to respect an energy ceasefire if Russia is ready to do the same. We agree, if they agree. And we have already shown that we mean it. Last night, Russian oil refineries were not burning but at midnight a Russian jet powered Shahed strike against a production facility in Ukraine.”
