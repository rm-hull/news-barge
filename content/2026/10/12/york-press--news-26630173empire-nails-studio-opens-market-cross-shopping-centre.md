## Brand-new nail salon opens in North Yorkshire shopping centre

Empire Nails Salon has opened in Selby's Market Cross Shopping Centre *(Image: Market Cross)*

A NEW nail salon is bringing fresh glamour and style to the high street in North Yorkshire.

Empire Nails Studio is the latest addition to Selby’s Market Cross and offers gel and acrylic extensions, restorative treatments, and intricate nail art.

The salon features a clean, elegant interior and a calm and welcoming atmosphere.




To celebrate its launch, the studio is offering 30 per cent off treatments for the first 30 customers to book an appointment and 20 per cent off all services until October 25.

Trang Radola, manager at Empire Nails Studio, said: "Selby has such a warm and welcoming community, and we felt that it was the perfect place for us to open our studio.

"We’re really looking forward to welcoming clients into the studio and becoming part of the local community."

Melissa Clement, who manages Market Cross, said: "We’re delighted to welcome Empire Nails Studio to Market Cross.

"The studio is a great addition to the centre, and it’s encouraging to see another independent business choosing Market Cross and investing in Selby town centre.

"We wish the team every success and look forward to seeing the studio become a popular destination for our visitors."

Empire Nails joins a growing list of independent retailers and services in the area.

The salon is located on New Lane in Selby and is open to customers now.

More information can be found at www.marketcrossselby.co.uk.
