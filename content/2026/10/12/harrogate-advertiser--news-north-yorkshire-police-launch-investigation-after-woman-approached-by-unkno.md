# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after woman approached by unknown man in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a woman was reportedly approached by an unknown man in Harrogate.

The incident happened between 10.25am and 10.35am on Wednesday, October 7, at the rear of Queens Road, behind Duchy Hospital.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after a woman was reportedly approached by an unknown man at the rear of Queens Road in Harrogate.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/9/16/Police-1.jpeg?trim=0,1,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Police say a woman was walking in the area when an unknown man ran up to her and repeatedly asked for her personal contact details.

The man is described as Asian, tall and of a slim build, aged between his 20s and 30s.

He was reportedly wearing a red turban or hat, joggers, a jacket and trainers, and had stubble facial hair.

Officers are now appealing for anyone who witnessed the incident, or who has CCTV, dash cam or mobile phone footage from the area around the time of the incident, to come forward.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260197332 when providing any details regarding the incident.
