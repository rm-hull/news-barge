ITEMS have been taken from a car in a York city centre street overnight.

North Yorkshire Police said a grey-coloured MINI One was entered between Sunday (October 11) and today in Fairfax Street.

Sergeant Toby Gorwood, neighbourhood policing sergeant for York Inner - York City Centre, said any witnesses or anyone with information or CCTV footage can email [yorknpt@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:yorknpt@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference number 12260200617.

Alternatively, information can be provided anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online.
