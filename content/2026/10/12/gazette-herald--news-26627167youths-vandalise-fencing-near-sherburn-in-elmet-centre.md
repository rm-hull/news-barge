YOUTHS have vandalised fencing close to a community centre in a North Yorkshire village.




The county's police force said in a statement yesterday (Sunday, October 11) it is investigating reports of anti-social behaviour and criminal damage in Sherburn-in-Elmet.

A force spokesperson said: "Youths have been congregating around the community centre / Low Garth Road area and fencing has been damaged."

Anyone who has information or CCTV / doorbell camera footage, is asked to contact PC 1223 Irvine via email at [colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting North Yorkshire Police reference 12260196863.
