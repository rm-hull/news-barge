The opening date for a new restaurant in Giffnock has been confirmed, with Christmas Day bookings already in "high demand".

Richmond Palace, a new restaurant and entertainment venue from the team behind The Asian in Newton Mearns, will open at 164 Fenwick Road on Thursday, December 3.

The business has released first-look artists' impressions of how the space is expected to look once an ongoing refurbishment programme is completed.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21560741.jpg?type=mds-article-620)New first look images show how the new Giffnock restaurant is expected to look on opening day *(Image: Supplied)*

The new restaurant will focus on Asian-inspired food and drink, alongside live entertainment on Friday and Saturday evenings.

Owners say interest in the opening has been strong, with bookings already being taken for the launch day and festive period.

Richmond Palace has also confirmed details of its Christmas Day offering, with one of its three sittings already fully booked.

A festive menu will also be available in the run-up to Christmas, with the restaurant accepting bookings for seasonal lunches, dinners and celebrations.

The co-owners of The Asian previously said: "The Asian has been such a huge part of our lives and we’ve had so many amazing customers support us over the years, so it will always be incredibly special to us.

“We felt the time was right to create something new and take everything we’ve learned into a completely different concept.

"Richmond Palace is going to be much more than somewhere you simply come for dinner.

"We want it to feel like an experience from the moment you walk through the door, with incredible food, beautiful interiors, cocktails, live entertainment and a really special atmosphere.

"We genuinely feel Richmond Palace is going to be the perfect addition to Giffnock and we can't wait for everyone to see what we've been working on."
