A stamp card campaign is giving shoppers the chance to win up to £500 in vouchers simply by supporting independent businesses.

Keighley BID has relaunched its Keighley Independents Stamp Card Campaign, which encourages people to shop locally in the lead-up to Christmas.

The scheme runs from October 19 to December 31.

Ryan Troy, BID manager at Keighley BID, said: "Independent businesses are the heart and soul of Keighley.

"They provide unique products, fantastic customer service and make a huge contribution to the local economy.

"This campaign is a great opportunity to celebrate those businesses while rewarding shoppers who choose to support local."

Shoppers can collect a stamp card from participating businesses and collect a stamp when they spend £5 or more at three different participating independent businesses.

Completed cards can be entered into a prize draw to win £500, £250, or £100 in shopping vouchers.

The campaign highlights Keighley’s independent retailers, cafés, salons, and service businesses.

Mr Troy added: "We encourage everyone to get involved, discover new businesses and enjoy shopping locally."

Completed stamp cards must be returned by January 10, 2027.

Cards can be dropped off at participating businesses, the Keighley BID office, or the Civic Centre post box on North Street.
