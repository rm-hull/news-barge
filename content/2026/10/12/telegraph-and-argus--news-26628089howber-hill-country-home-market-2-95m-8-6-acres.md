A COUNTRY home has gone on the market for the first time in more than 30 years.

Howber Hill, a five-bedroom period property located in the exclusive hamlet of Langbar, is just four miles from Ilkley and close to Bolton Abbey.

The property is surrounded by 8.6 acres of gardens and grazing land.

It is being marketed by Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Ilkley office with a guide price of £2.95 million.

Patrick McCutcheon, head of residential at Dacre, Son & Hartley, said: "Howber Hill is a beautiful period country home with an abundance of character features, including stone-mullioned windows, grand fireplaces, exposed stonework and timber beams.

"It also enjoys a wonderful rural setting, overlooking its substantial gardens and grazing land to the south.

"Crucially, despite its idyllic and peaceful position approximately a mile from Beamsley Beacon and close to Bolton Abbey, it is just a short drive from the A59 and Ilkley town centre.

"It therefore offers a fantastic lifestyle opportunity, and we’re anticipating strong interest from anyone looking for an exceptional home in one of Yorkshire’s most sought-after locations."

The ground floor includes five reception rooms, a central hall, study, and a breakfast kitchen.

Upstairs, the main bedroom features a four-piece en-suite bathroom, with four additional double bedrooms and a house bathroom.

The property is accessed via a long driveway and includes a detached double garage with a large office suite above, as well as a barn and workshop.

Potential buyers can contact Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Ilkley office on 01943 600655 for more information.

Details are also available online at dacres.co.uk.
