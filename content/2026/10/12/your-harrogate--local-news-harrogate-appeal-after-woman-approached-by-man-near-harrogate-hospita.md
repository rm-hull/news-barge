Police are appealing for witnesses after a woman was approached by a man who repeatedly asked for her personal contact details in Harrogate.

The reported public order incident happened at the rear of Queens Road, behind Duchy Hospital, between 10:25am and 10:35am on Wednesday 7th October.

The woman was walking when an unknown man reportedly ran up to her and repeatedly asked for her details.

Police described the man as Asian, tall and of a skinny build, aged in his 20s or 30s, with stubble.

He was wearing a red turban or hat, joggers, a jacket and trainers.

Officers are seeking witnesses, information and any CCTV or video footage that could assist their investigation.

*Anyone who can help is asked to contact PC 1141 Kelly by emailing [laura.kelly@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:laura.kelly@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting reference 12260197332.*
