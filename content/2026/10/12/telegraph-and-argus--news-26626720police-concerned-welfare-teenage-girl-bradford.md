Police are concerned for the safety of a Bradford teenage girl who went missing at the weekend.

Patrina, 16, of Bradford, has been reported missing and was last seen at around 5pm on Saturday.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are concerned for the safety of 16-year-old Patrina from Bradford, who was last seen around 5pm on Saturday, October 10.

"Patrina is believed to be wearing a pink top and pink trousers.

"If you know where Patrina is or if you see her then please call 101 and quote log number 0853 of October 22, 2026."
