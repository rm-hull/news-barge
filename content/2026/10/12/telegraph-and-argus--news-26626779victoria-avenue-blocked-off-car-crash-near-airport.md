A road near Leeds Bradford Airport was blocked off after a crash at the weekend.

Police and fire crews attended the scene on Victoria Avenue in Yeadon, near Leeds Bradford Airport, just before noon on Saturday.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service initially responded to reports of a car on fire.

A spokesman for the service said: "We were called to reports of a car on fire at 11.26am on Saturday.

"One crew were sent but on attendance this was a car collision with no fire and no injuries, so the assisted in making the scene safe before leaving."

Upon arrival, they found a vehicle involved in a collision, but there was no fire and no injuries.

Two police vehicles blocked off the road while the scene was made safe.

Motorists were diverted away from the area.

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for further information about the crash.
