Italian jewellery brand Moriconi Italy is set to open a new permanent boutique at Glasgow’s Silverburn Shopping Centre in October, expanding from its existing kiosk.

The larger store will give customers the chance to explore the brand’s full range of collections, including new and exclusive pieces set to be unveiled at a launch event.

Founded by William Moriconi, whose family comes from the Tuscan town of Barga, the business brings Italian jewellery craftsmanship and heritage to customers in Scotland and across the UK.

The expansion marks the latest stage in the company’s growth, following more than 13 years of trading from its store at Glasgow’s St Enoch Shopping Centre.

Moriconi Italy also operates at Silverburn and through an international online store. The new boutique will give the brand two permanent Glasgow locations where customers can browse its collections.

The opening date and further details of the launch event are expected to be announced in the coming weeks.

William Moriconi, founder of Moriconi Italy, said: "I am absolutely delighted to be opening our new Moriconi Italy store in the heart of Silverburn, a highly prestigious centre that houses top retail brands.

"I’m sure that our existing – and new – customers will be as excited as we are about this new opportunity."

Diarmuid Donohoe, general manager of Silverburn, said: "Moriconi Italy has built a real following at Silverburn, and this expansion to a full boutique reflects that success.

"It's a perfect example of how we support brands to evolve and scale, working together to deliver a retail experience that meets their needs while adding variety to our jewellery and accessories offering that we know our guests love."
