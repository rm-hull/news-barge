A CAR has crashed into a lamppost in an East Yorkshire village.




Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Rawcliffe Road, near Airmyn at 7.32pm yesterday (Sunday, October 10).

A spokesperson for the service said: "All persons self-released prior to the fire service arrival.

"Crews made the vehicle safe using go-jacks, spreaders, cordon tape and lighting."

"The incident was left with police."
