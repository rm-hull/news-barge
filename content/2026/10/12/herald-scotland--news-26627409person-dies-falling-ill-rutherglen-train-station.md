A person has died after falling ill at a train station near Glasgow.

British Transport Police said officers were called to Rutherglen train station at around 6:20pm on Saturday, October 10, after reports that a person had fallen ill.

Officers attended alongside ambulance crews, however the person was pronounced dead at the scene.

The death is not being treated as suspicious.

A spokesperson for British Transport Police said: "Officers were called to Rutherglen railway station at 6.21pm on Saturday (October 10) following reports that a person had fallen ill at the station.

"Officers attended alongside paramedics, however sadly a person was pronounced dead at the scene.

"The incident is not being treated as suspicious."
