# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after SYM Mask moped stolen from street in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a moped was reported stolen from a street in Harrogate.

The black and yellow SYM Mask moped, with registration YD75 HCK, was parked on Regent Terrace between 8pm and 9pm on Sunday, October 4.

![The police black and yellow SYM Mask moped, with registration YD75 HCK, was parked on Regent Terrace between 8pm and 9pm on Sunday, October 4.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/12/10/38/844252868_1421876120087853_4843909440884858845_n.jpeg?trim=57,20,27,34&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The owner returned later that evening to find the vehicle was no longer there.

The police are now investigating the reported theft and are appealing to anyone who may have seen the moped, witnessed any suspicious activity in the area, or has information which could help with enquiries to get in touch.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number when providing any details regarding the incident.
