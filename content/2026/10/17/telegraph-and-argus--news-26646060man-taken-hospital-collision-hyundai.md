A MAN was taken to hospital following a collision.

Officers were called to reports of a road traffic collision between a pedestrian and a Hyundai on Emm Lane at 9.55pm last night (Friday).

The 48-year-old pedestrian was taken to hospital with non-life-threatening injuries.

The driver of the vehicle stopped at the scene and has been spoken to by officers.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "At 9.55pm last night, police were called to a road traffic collision involving a car and a pedestrian in Emm Lane, Bradford.

"The pedestrian, a 48-year-old man, was taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not believed to be life threatening.

"The driver of the Hyundai involved stopped at the scene and has been spoken to by officers."
