EMERGENCY services have been called to a main York road following a crash.

The incident happened in Wigginton Road at 5.38pm on Friday (October 16).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from York attended and had made the scene and vehicle safe before handing the incident over to North Yorkshire Police.




It comes after reports that a DPD van had crashed into a tree in the area.

A service spokesperson confirmed: “A crew from York responded to a report of a road traffic collision involving an electric vehicle.

“Crews made the scene and vehicle safe and left the incident in the hands of police.”

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Police.
