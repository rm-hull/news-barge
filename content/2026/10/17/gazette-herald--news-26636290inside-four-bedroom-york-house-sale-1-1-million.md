An "immaculate" York home has hit the market – for £1.1 million.

The four-bedroom detached house is for sale in Bootham Crescent.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it has been "thoughtfully extended and fully renovated by the current owners to create a stylish, turn-key family residence".

Inside, the property opens into a central reception hall, featuring the original staircase rising to the first floor.

A spacious living room is on the ground floor which flows into a rear section with doors opening onto the garden.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21579948.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the four-bedroom detached house for sale in Bootham Crescent for £1.1 million *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

There is also an open-plan kitchen, dining and living space – "beautifully extended to maximise light and space", according to Hudson Moody.

The estate agent said the kitchen has been designed with "both style and practicality in mind".

Doors from the kitchen also lead to the outside patio area.

Meanwhile, the four bedrooms are on the first floor of the home.

The main bedroom has an en-suite bathroom, while the three other double bedrooms are served by a ‘Jack and Jill’ family bathroom.

Outside, the property has a private rear garden with both lawn and patio areas, as well as a storage shed.

To the front is a walled garden and driveway offering off-street parking, accessed via electric gates.
