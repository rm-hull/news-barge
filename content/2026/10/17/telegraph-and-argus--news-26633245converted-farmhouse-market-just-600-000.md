A five-bedroom converted farmhouse is on the market for £600,000 after two price cuts.

The detached property is in New Houses on Back Lyon Street, Queensbury, and sits on about three-quarters of an acre.

Sugdens estate agents say it blends traditional features with contemporary living.

The price was cut by £25,000 in January 2026 and by a further £25,000 in June 2026, bringing it to £600,000.

![Kitchen area](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575778.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kitchen area

The house has spacious rooms designed for family life and entertaining.

Its family kitchen has wall and base units, granite worktops, a breakfast bar, an integrated fridge-freezer, wine cooler, oven, hob and extractor hood.

The sitting area opens on to the rear through bi-fold doors.

![Nice terrace area for when the sun is shining!](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575776.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nice terrace area for when the sun is shining!

Another sitting area has a multi-fuel fire set into the chimney breast, while the lounge features a window seat and ceiling beams.

The utility room has fitted wall and base units, a stainless-steel sink, tiled splashback, plumbing for a washing machine and a radiator.

The fifth bedroom and a cloakroom are also on the ground floor.

![Dining area](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575777.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dining area

A cellar provides extra storage.

The first floor has four more bedrooms, including the main bedroom with an en-suite.

The en-suite has a shower cubicle, low-level WC, pedestal basin and radiator.

Two further bedrooms have modern wardrobes with sliding doors.

![One of the many bedrooms](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575772.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the many bedrooms

The first-floor bathroom has a shower cubicle, panelled bath, low-level WC and twin vanity unit.

Outside, the property has well-maintained lawns and paved gardens, with borders and shrubs.

A gated driveway offers ample off-road parking.

![Bathroom](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575773.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bathroom

![A bedroom](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575775.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bedroom

![Another bedroom](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21575774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another bedroom

Several outbuildings offer scope for different uses, subject to planning permission.

Several schools are nearby: Trinity Academy Bradford is 0.4 miles away, Russell Hall Primary School is 0.5 miles away, and Foxhill Primary School and Shibden Head Primary Academy are about 0.7 miles away.

The house last sold for £190,000 in October 2015.

The house is listed by Sugdens on Zoopla. The agent is at 44 High Street, Queensbury, Bradford BD13 2PA, and can be reached on 01274 978448.
