A man is being sought following the alleged theft of items from a North Yorkshire supermarket.

The incident happened on September 29 at the Tesco store in Cambridge Road, Harrogate.




North Yorkshire Police is urging anyone who recognises the man pictured on CCTV to get in touch, as he may have more information that will assist the investigation.

Please email [Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 if you believe you can help.

Please quote reference 12260185198 when passing on information.
