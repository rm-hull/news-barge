Amanda Owen's eldest daughter Raven is returning to Ravenseat as the Owen family continues to adjust to the different paths being taken by its nine children.

Raven, who became a familiar face to viewers of Our Yorkshire Farm, features in the fourth series of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids as she spends time back at the family's remote Swaledale home.

The eldest of Amanda and former husband Clive's children has pursued a life beyond the everyday demands of farming, having studied biomedical science at university.

But her latest appearance shows that she has not lost her connection with Ravenseat.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21526503.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Details released ahead of the episode airing on More4 on Monday, October 19, reveal Raven returning to the farm and getting involved with mucking out the stables.

She is joined by younger sister Edith, who is juggling her own responsibilities as she prepares for exams and helps run the family's revived cream tea business.

The scenes offer another glimpse of how life has changed for the Owen children since viewers first watched them growing up together in the Yorkshire Dales.

## What is Amanda Owen's daughter Raven doing now?

Raven is the oldest of Amanda and Clive's nine children and has followed a different career path from some of her farming-focused siblings.

She studied biomedical science at York St John University, graduating with first-class honours in 2022, before continuing her studies at postgraduate level.

Her scientific interests have taken her beyond the agricultural world in which she grew up.

But she has continued to maintain links with the family farm, returning to Ravenseat and appearing in the family's television programmes.

Her return in the latest series comes as Amanda has been speaking about the increasingly different ambitions of her children.

At Our Farm Next Door: A Behind-the-Scenes Celebration at Everyman Northallerton on September 30, Amanda explained that some of the children were considering university, others were developing businesses and some wanted to continue farming.

The uncertainty over their futures has even affected plans for Anty John's, the centuries-old farmhouse the family has spent three years restoring.

Amanda revealed that belongings had already been moved into the property, but the family still had not decided who would eventually live there.

She said: "With some kids wanting to go to university, some starting their lives elsewhere and others wanting to farm, we're unsure of people's futures."

For Raven, returning to Ravenseat means stepping back into a world she knows well.

The family still faces the familiar demands of caring for animals, maintaining the farm and dealing with the unpredictable weather of Upper Swaledale.

*Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids continues on More4 on Monday, October 19, at 9pm.*
