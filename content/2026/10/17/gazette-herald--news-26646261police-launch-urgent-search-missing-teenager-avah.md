**UPDATE: The missing teenager has been found.A police spokesperson said: "Earlier today (17 October), we shared an appeal to find a missing 13-year-old from West Yorkshire area."We are pleased to confirm that she has now been found safe and well."Thank you for sharing our post and your continued support."**

NORTH Yorkshire Police has launched a search for a missing teenager.

The teenage girl, 13, was last seen in the West Yorkshire area on the evening of Friday (October 16).

She is believed to have connections in Scarborough and Filey.




She is described as 5ft 3 inches in height with blue eyes and shoulder-length brown hair.

North Yorkshire Police said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate her and as part of our enquiries, we are asking for anyone who may have seen her, or who has information about her whereabouts, to contact us immediately.  
  
“If you see this missing person, please call 999.

“If you have any other information that could help us find them, please call us on 101 and select option 1 to speak to our control room.

“Please quote 12260204102 when sharing information.”
