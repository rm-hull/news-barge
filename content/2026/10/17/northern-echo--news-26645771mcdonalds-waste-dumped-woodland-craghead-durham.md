McDonald's bags, food waste, cans and bottles have been dumped in a woodland in County Durham.

Neighbourhood Wardens working for Durham County Council received reports of a fly-tip discovered in the wooded area behind Holmside Terrace, Craghead, earlier this week.

Although evidence was recovered from the waste, the wardens are appealing for information from the public.

Images show fly-tipped McDonald's bags, food waste, tins, cans and bottles that have been dumped in the woodlands.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21594779.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The fly-tipping in Craghead, County Durham *(Image: DURHAM COUNTY COUNCIL)*

A spokesperson for Durham County Council's wardens said: "We are keen to hear from anyone who may have witnessed the incident or has information that could assist with our ongoing investigation.
