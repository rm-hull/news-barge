Two people have been arrested on suspicion of drug dealing after cannabis and £3000 cash were seized from a property in Teesside.

At around 8.30pm on October 14, Cleveland Police officers in the Newport area of Middlesbrough witnessed a suspected drug deal at the door of an address on Colville Street.

Officers stopped a man nearby and recovered a small amount of cannabis.

Police then went to an address, where they seized cannabis and around £3,000 in cash.

A 36-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply class B drugs and possession of criminal property.

A 64-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply class B drugs and being concerned in the supply of class B drugs.
