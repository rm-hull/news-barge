North Yorkshire Police have issued this CCTV image (see photo), of a female that we would like to speak to, following a theft from a farm in Suffield in Scarborough.

It happened on the 4th October 2026 at 12.32, at Suffield Farm, involving the theft of an honesty money tin in an egg stand.

Police are particularly keen to hear from anyone who:

* Witnessed the theft

* Has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam, or mobile phone footage that may have captured the incident or those involved.

* Has any information that could assist the investigation.
