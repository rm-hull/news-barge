A family-run farm shop café in North Yorkshire has attracted hundreds of reviews from visitors, with its hearty meals and spectacular countryside setting winning particular praise.

Toft Gate Barn is situated at Greenhow, above Pateley Bridge and combines a farm shop with a café serving breakfast, lunch and homemade refreshments.

The rural business holds an overall Tripadvisor rating of 4.6 out of five from around 370 reviews.

It has also received a Travellers' Choice award, placing it among businesses recognised for consistently positive visitor feedback.

Its elevated position provides sweeping views across the surrounding Nidderdale countryside, making it a popular stopping point for people exploring the region.

Visitors can combine a meal with browsing the farm shop, which stocks local produce and other food products.

But reviews suggest the setting is only part of the attraction.

## What do visitors say about Toft Gate Barn?

A customer visiting in September praised the café's food, particularly the steak pie ordered by their partner.

They described it as "incredible and stuffed full of beef".

Another visitor, who stopped with friends in March, was impressed by the countryside setting and the choice of food.

They described the location as having "amazing views", while praising the meals for arriving quickly.

A customer who visited in November 2025 was particularly enthusiastic about the steak pie, which was made using beef from the farm.

They said: "I don't think I've had a better Pie", praising the generous filling and flavour.

## Farm café in North Yorkshire

The reviews suggest that visitors particularly appreciate the countryside location, but that service and food availability can vary at times.

Toft Gate Barn's appeal lies partly in offering something different from a conventional town-centre café.

Its location high above Pateley Bridge makes it a destination for walkers, motorists and visitors exploring Nidderdale.

The combination of a working agricultural setting, farm shop and café also allows customers to browse local food products alongside their visit.

And for those fortunate enough to secure a window seat, the surrounding landscape provides a view which several reviewers have found just as memorable as the food.

Toft Gate Barn is at Greenhow, Pateley Bridge, North Yorkshire, HG3 5JE.

The café currently lists opening hours of 9am to 4pm daily.
