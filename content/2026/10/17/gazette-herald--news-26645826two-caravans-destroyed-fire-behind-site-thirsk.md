TWO caravans have been destroyed by a fire in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened at 9.32pm on Friday (October 16) behind a caravan site in Thirsk.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service crews from Northallerton and Thirsk were called the scene.




Firefigters managed to extinguish the blaze, which caused 100 per cent fire damage to the caravans.

A service spokesperson said: "Crew from Northallerton and Thirsk attended a fire to a caravan behind a site.

"On arrival crews used 1 hose reel jet to extinguish and dampen down 2 caravans.

"Both suffered 100% fire damage."
