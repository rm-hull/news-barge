A cyclist has died after a crash near Easingwold.

It happened on **Alne Road** at around**5pm yesterday** (Friday).

A blue Volvo XC60 and the cycle collided.

#### Alne Road near Easingwold

The collision happened as both parties were travelling away from Easingwold, just before where Alne Road crosses the A19 Easingwold Bypass.  
The cyclist, a 60-year-old man, died at the scene as a result of the collision. His family have been informed and are receiving support.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are particularly interesting in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision or anyone with relevant dashcam footage of the area during this time.

“This includes of the collision itself as well as both parties prior to the incident.”
