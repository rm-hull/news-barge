Bradford is known as the curry capital of the UK thanks to the huge range of curry houses available to try out.

From takeaways to sit-down restaurants, you can find a curry house in West Yorkshire that suits you.

If you are struggling to choose the perfect restaurant to visit, you might want to take a trip to Chowdreys.

## What is Chowdreys in Bradford like?

Chowdreys can be found along Great Horton Road in Bradford and has been described as “one of the best curry eateries” in the city.

The restaurant is loved by locals and could be worth a visit the next time you are in Bradford.

The restaurant’s menu features lots of chef specials like karahis, handi chicken, and paya as well as old favourites like kormas, dopiazas, and makhanis.

There are also plenty of vegetable dishes that can be added to the side of your curry as well as naans, rotis, and parathas.

Alongside the classics, Chowdreys offers some great options for lunch, including naan rolls and the viral naanwich.

If you need something sweet to finish off your meal, there are a few desserts to choose from, including the kunafa brownie and different milk cakes.

You can also get traditional desi desserts like kheer, kulfi, and rasmalai.
