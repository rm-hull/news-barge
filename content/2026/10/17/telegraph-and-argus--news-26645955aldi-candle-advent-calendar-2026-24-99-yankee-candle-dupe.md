Aldi is bringing back its dupe candle advent calendar for Christmas, with 25 festive fragrances for less than £25.

And there's a big saving compared with a popular Yankee Candle alternative, which costs £59.99, or Jo Malone's pricy £375 advent collection.

The gingerbread house-inspired design has 25 mini candles, offering shoppers a different festive scent to enjoy throughout December.

## Aldi's £24.99 candle advent calendar

The Aldi calendar features 25 candles, each weighing 50g and offering a burn time of up to eight hours.

Festive scents include Warm Gingerbread, Hot Cocoa, Frosted Fir, White Christmas, Cinnamon Spice and Iced Berries.

The calendar's nostalgic gingerbread house design taps into the retro Christmas trend, with the supermarket pointing to the popularity of #NostalgicChristmas on TikTok.

Shoppers in previous years have snapped up the candles as soon as they hit the middle aisle, with one posting: "Aldi’s viral candles are back!! I had to stop myself from buying all of them."

Another said: "A much cheaper dupe for the Jo Malone advent!".

Another added, of this year's design: "So far my favorite Aldi candle advent calendar! Love the smells and packaging!".

Shoppers who tried the scents in larger sizes said: "The viral candle dupe. Totally worth the hype! The smell is just divine."

## How much can you save compared with Yankee Candle?

Aldi's calendar costs £24.99, compared with £59.99 for the Yankee Candle Fragrance Lovers Advent Calendar.

At £24.99 for 25 candles, each one works out at around £1, making it an affordable way to fill your home with seasonal scents.

It will be available nationwide from October 25.
