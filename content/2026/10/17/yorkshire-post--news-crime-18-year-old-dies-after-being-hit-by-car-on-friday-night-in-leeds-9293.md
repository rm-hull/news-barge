# 18-year-old dies after being hit by car on Friday night in Leeds

## The collision caused the death of an 18-year-old pedestrian

Police are appealing for witnesses to a collision in Leeds city centre on Friday night (Oct 16) which caused the death of an 18-year-old pedestrian.

At 9.12pm last night, the man was hit by a black BMW i40 M50 in Park Lane, near to the split for the A58 entry slip road.

He was taken to hospital for emergency treatment but passed away from his injuries, West Yorkshire Police said.

The BMW, which had been travelling away from the city centre at the time of the collision, stopped near the scene.

Enquiries have established a silver Toyota Yaris T-sport was present in the area at the time of the collision.

Both vehicles have been recovered.

Two 22-year-old men and a 24-year-old man have been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in relation to the incident.

They remain in custody, police said.

Officers from the Major Collision Enquiry Team are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed any part of the incident or saw either of the vehicles being driven in the area in the time leading up to the collision, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage.
