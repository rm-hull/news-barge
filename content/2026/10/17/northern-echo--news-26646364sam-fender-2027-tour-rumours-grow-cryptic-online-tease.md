Sam Fender has sent fans into a frenzy after appearing to tease something big for 2027 amid rumours of another huge UK tour.

The North Shields star and his team have shared a mysterious 'SF27' design on social media, with the branding also appearing online and prompting speculation over what Fender could have planned for next year.

No new UK tour has been officially announced, and Fender's official tour page currently does not list confirmed 2027 dates.

However, the tease comes as speculation continues to build around the singer's plans for 2027, including reports linking him with one of the headline slots at Glastonbury.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21596186.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Geordie rock star's website displays a mysterious 'SF27' logo. *(Image: SAM FENDER WEBSITE)*

## Sam Fender fans react to 'SF27'

Fans have been quick to interpret the cryptic branding as a sign that a major announcement could be approaching.

One wrote on Instagram: "Best news EVERRRR", while another said: "we are so back!"

A third added: "Does not matter the time and place, I am there."

The speculation comes with Fender riding another wave of success following his collaboration with Olivia Dean on Rein Me In.

His official website lists the track among the additional songs included on the deluxe edition of People Watching.

Fender has also recently been spotted at St James' Park dressed as a hobbit, adding another unusual clue for fans attempting to work out what the singer might be planning.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21596171.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fender was spotted in St James' Park recently... dressed as a hobbit. *(Image: TIKTOK)*

## Could Sam Fender play Glastonbury 2027?

Reports have separately linked Fender with a headline appearance at Glastonbury when the festival returns in 2027.

Reports published this month claim Fender could take one of the coveted Pyramid Stage headline slots, although this remains unconfirmed.

Fender previously performed on the Pyramid Stage in 2022.

Festival organiser Emily Eavis has confirmed that all three Glastonbury 2027 headliners have already been booked, but has warned that the overwhelming majority of rumours surrounding the festival are inaccurate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/16848957.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: PA)*

## Sam Fender's huge St James' Park homecoming

Any new tour would follow Fender's enormous homecoming shows at St James' Park in Newcastle in June 2025.

The North East singer returned to Newcastle United's stadium for concerts on June 12, 14 and 15 as part of his People Watching era, with Olivia Dean among the supporting acts.

The shows also have a connection to Fender's latest success, with a live version of Rein Me In recorded at St James' Park subsequently released.

Fender had previously played two sold-out concerts at the stadium in June 2023, making St James' Park an increasingly significant part of his rise from North Shields to some of Britain's biggest stages.
