A popular Chester-Le-Street café known for its indulgent pancakes and waffles has unveiled a spooky new addition to its menu just in time for Halloween.

Miss Elephant, based on North Burns near the town's market place, announced the launch of its limited-edition Halloween pancake in a recent Facebook post.

The café teased the seasonal special with the message: "We've officially reached the point in October where even the pancakes are dressing up for Halloween."

Topped with orange and green decorations, chocolate and whipped cream, the colourful creation has been described by the business as an "absolute spooky mix on a plate" and will only be available for a limited time.

Miss Elephant has built up a loyal following since opening in Chester-Le-Street, specialising in pancakes, waffles, cakes, milkshakes, smoothies and brunch dishes.

TripAdvisor lists the café as serving breakfast, lunch, dinner and drinks, with vegan options available.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21591154.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new Halloween pancake is available for a limited time at Miss Elephant as part of the cafe's seasonal menu. *(Image: Miss Elephant)*

The restaurant currently holds a 3.9-star rating on TripAdvisor, with many customers praising both the food and service.

One reviewer called the café a "hidden gem" and said they were "surprised and delighted" by its range of sweet and savoury dishes.

They added: "There was a great selection of savoury and sweet dishes, including cakes and waffles.

"The staff were very helpful and friendly. Well worth a visit."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21591152.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Miss Elephant has launched a limited-edition Halloween pancake featuring orange and green toppings, whipped cream and chocolate drizzle. *(Image: Miss Elephant)*

Another customer highlighted the atmosphere and service during a festive visit, writing: "Our drinks were of exceptional standard with brilliant presentation.

"There were lovely festive decorations and atmospheric music. Good value and a warm café."

A separate review praised the venue’s sweet treats, especially its pancakes and waffles.

The reviewer also praised the "nice service" and "nice coffee".

Located at 14 North Burns, Miss Elephant is open daily and has become a familiar stop for those looking for everything from loaded waffles and pancakes to hot drinks and light lunches.

For anyone looking to embrace the Halloween season, the cafe's latest pancake special may offer a suitably spooky excuse for a weekend treat.
