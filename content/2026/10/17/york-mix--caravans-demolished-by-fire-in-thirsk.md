Firefighters were called to two caravans alight in Thirsk last night, Friday 16thOctober. A crew from Northallerton and Thirsk attended the scene behind a caravan site in the town.

On arrival they used 1 hose reel jet to extinguish and dampen them down. Both suffered 100% fire damage.
