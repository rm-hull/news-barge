It will get darker this month as the clocks go back and North Yorkshire Police are advising drivers to have a walkaround check of your vehicle. They suggest focusing on ensuring bulbs are working and documents are up to date. Ensure address changes are reflected on your insurance and driving licence.

It’s as officers patrolling on Royal Albert Drive in Scarborough sighted a vehicle with several lights not illuminated. It was seized and the driver reported for the offences.

The officers from Scarborough’s Neighbourhood Policing Team stopped the Hyundai on Dean Road on Wednesday night (14 Oct 26) where it was found not to have a valid MOT. Subsequent check also revealed a lack of insurance for the driver.
