A PUG was reportedly stolen in broad daylight from a busy Bradford street.

The incident took place on Market Street in Bradford at around 11.30am yesterday, with the suspects described as two Asian men approximately in their twenties.

Enquiries are ongoing.

A spokesperson for the force said: "We have received a report of a black pug dog being stolen in Market Street, Bradford, at about 11.30am yesterday.

"The suspects were described as two Asian men aged approximately in their twenties.

"We are currently in the process of contacting the victim to confirm details of the incident."

Anyone with any information that could assist the investigation is urged to contact officers online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting crime reference 13260590747.
