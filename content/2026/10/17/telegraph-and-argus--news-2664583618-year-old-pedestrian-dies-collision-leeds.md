An 18-year-old man has died following a collision in Leeds city centre.

The incident happened at 9.12pm last night (Friday) on Park Lane near the A58 entry slip road.

The pedestrian was hit by a black BMW i40 M50 and was taken to hospital but later died from his injuries.

West Yorkshire Police said the BMW was travelling away from the city centre at the time of the collision and stopped nearby.

A silver Toyota Yaris T-Sport was also reported to be in the area.

Both vehicles have since been recovered.

Three men, two aged 22 and one aged 24, have been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

They remain in custody.

### West Yorkshire Police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses to a collision in Leeds city centre yesterday evening which caused the death of an 18-year-old pedestrian.

"At 9.12pm last night, the man was involved in a collision with a black BMW i40 M50 in Park Lane, near to the split for the A58 entry slip road.

"He was taken to hospital for emergency treatment but sadly passed away from his injuries."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police by calling 101 or using the live chat facility on the West Yorkshire Police website.
