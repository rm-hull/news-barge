Co-op is bringing back its Christmas sandwiches, with four returning favourites and two new festive fillings joining the lunchtime range.

The supermarket's festive sarnies land in stores and online from October 21, with classic options including Turkey Feast, Boxing Day Feast, Pigs Under Blankets and Brie & Cranberry.

There are two new premium sandwiches on the way, too - but shoppers will have to wait until December 2 to try them.

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21590931.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Co-op Christmas sandwiches *(Image: Co-op)*

The new Christmas Club Sandwich combines roast British chicken, French Brie, truffle mayonnaise, cranberry sauce and spinach on lightly grilled white bread, while the Posh Prawn Cocktail features king prawns, cold-water prawns and zesty Marie Rose sauce.

The four returning sandwiches cost £3.60 each, while the new premium options are priced at £4.75.

## Co-op Christmas sandwiches included in £3.85 meal deal

Shoppers can also pick up the festive sandwiches as part of Co-op's lunchtime meal deal, which includes a main, snack and drink.

The deal costs £3.85 for members and £4.25 for non-members, while premium sandwiches are included in the Premium Meal Deal at £5.50 for members or £6 for non-members.

Helen Jagger, Product Development Manager for Food to Go, said: "For many shoppers, the arrival of Christmas sandwiches is the moment the festive season really begins.

"We know our customers look forward to picking up their favourites every year, which is why we're bringing back our most-loved classics. And for those keen to try something new, we’ll also introduce two exciting premium additions we're sure will be a hit when they land in December."

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21590933.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Co-op Christmas sandwiches *(Image: Co-op)*

Co-op says all its own-brand meat is 100% British, including the fresh turkey used in its core Christmas sandwich range.

Proceeds from the core Christmas sandwiches will be donated to Barnardo’s as part of Co-op's national charity partnership. The partnership has raised more than £7million and supported positive futures for one million young people across the UK.

The festive sandwich range has raised more than £500,000 for Co-op's national charity partners over the past decade.

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21590934.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Co-op Christmas sandwiches *(Image: Co-op)*

## Co-op's Christmas sandwich range and prices

* **Turkey Feast – £3.60:** British turkey breast, British bacon, sage and onion stuffing, and onion mayonnaise on malted bread.
* **Boxing Day Feast – £3.60:** Smoked British chicken, oak-smoked British ham, coleslaw and balsamic onion chutney on soft white bread.
* **Pigs Under Blankets – £3.60:** British sausage, smoked British bacon, balsamic onion chutney and onion mayonnaise on malted bread.
* **Brie & Cranberry – £3.60:** Brie, cranberry chutney, seasoned mayonnaise and spinach on malted bread.
* **NEW Christmas Club – £4.75:** Roast British chicken, French Brie, truffle mayonnaise, cranberry sauce and spinach on lightly grilled white bread.
* **NEW Posh Prawn Cocktail – £4.75:** King prawns and cold-water prawns in Marie Rose sauce with lettuce on soft malted bread.

The four returning sandwiches are available from October 21, while the two new premium options arrive on December 2.

![](https://www.theboltonnews.co.uk/resources/images/21590935.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Co-op Christmas sandwiches *(Image: Co-op)*
