## North Yorkshire pub with six en-suite bedrooms on sale for £850,000 plus VAT

The exterior of The Oak Tree in Helperby *(Image: Everard Cole)*

A rural North Yorkshire pub is on the market for £850,000 plus VAT.

The Oak Tree, located on Raskelf Road, Helperby, is being marketed by Everard Cole.

The freehold property, currently owned by Provenance Inns, includes a bar, restaurant, private dining room, and six bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms.

The pub is also equipped with a large commercial kitchen, a car park, and an outdoor trading area.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21592148.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The plush interior of The Oak Tree at Helperby *(Image: Everard Cole)*

Everard Cole says The Oak Tree is situated in a rural setting but has good road links to Boroughbridge, Easingwold, and York.

The building, which is set over two storeys, features a bar area with a solid oak bar, an open fireplace, and a cast-iron wood-burning stove.

The separate restaurant has polished wood flooring and a feature fireplace.

The first floor houses a private dining room that can accommodate up to 30 people and is self-contained with its own toilets.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21592149.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The plush interior of The Oak Tree at Helperby *(Image: Everard Cole)*

All the bedrooms are furnished with jacuzzi baths.

The property is Grade II Listed and situated in a conservation area.

The sale includes all trade fixtures and fittings.

The business currently operates seven days a week, with food served from Wednesday to Sunday lunchtime.

Currently, the pub is trading in the region of £10,000 to £12,000 per week.

However, the agents add it has been noted that the business is not operating to its full potential, as it has previously traded in the region of £17,000 to £20,000 per week.
