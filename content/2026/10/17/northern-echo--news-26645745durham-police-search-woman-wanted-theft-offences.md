Police in County Durham are searching for a woman with links to several areas of the region who is wanted for several theft offences.

Georgia Little, 28, is wanted by Durham Police in connection with theft offences in Ferryhill and Chilton.

She has recent links to Ferryhill, Durham City and Peterlee.

The force are asking anyone who has seen her to get in touch as soon as possible.

If you have any information regarding Georgia's whereabouts, call Durham Police on 101, quoting reference CRI00711275, or email [Joshua.Dillon@durham.police.uk](mailto:Joshua.Dillon@durham.police.uk)

Alternatively, you can call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
