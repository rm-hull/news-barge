Tucked away on Greenbank Road in Darlington’s Denes area is this mid-terrace house that has been upgraded and improved by its current owner to create a home that’s stylish, practical and ready for its new owners to move into and enjoy.

Step in through the entrance vestibule and you’ll find yourself in a welcoming lounge filled with natural light thanks to a double-glazed box bay window to the front.

Family meals and evenings with friends are well catered for in the dining kitchen, which has a range of modern fitted units, an electric hob and oven, and space for a fridge freezer and washing machine as well as ample room for a dining table.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584021.jpg?type=mds-article-620)a *(Image: Venture Properties)*

The kitchen has a door to the side garden and another into the shower room with a heated towel rail that completes this level.

Upstairs are a separate WC and two bedrooms, including the good-sized principal bedroom with a feature fireplace.

Outside, a forecourt adds kerb appeal to the front, while to the rear an enclosed yard offers a private spot for sitting out, along with an outhouse and gated access to the rear lane.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584022.jpg?type=mds-article-620)a *(Image: Venture Properties)*

With local amenities close by, this beautifully maintained home is a lovely choice for first-time buyers and downsizers alike – and one that really needs to be seen inside to be appreciated, as the saying goes.
