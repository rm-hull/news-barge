There'll be festive fun at Bramham Park this Christmas as the estate continues to develop new ways for the public to enjoy its historic setting.

Best known as the long-standing home of Leeds Festival and the Bramham International Horse Trials, the estate, near Wetherby, is expanding its public events programme with a series of festive experiences.

On November 14 and 15, Bramham Park will welcome more than 100 artisan traders for its inaugural Christmas Market.

Set against the backdrop of the house, the market will bring together a variety of independent makers and producers of quality artisan products ideal for Christmas gifts and decorations.

In the stable yard, visitors can enjoy a selection of local food stands.

On November 14, the Yorkshire Rock Choir will perform carols in front of the main house from 12pm until 2pm, adding to the festive cheer.

James Hamilton-Goddard, commercial director at Bramham Park, says: 'We are looking forward to welcoming everyone to The Beginning of Christmas at Bramham Park.

'We are planning a great day out of festive cheer for all the family, filled with a variety of trade stands, music and food. Keep following us on social media as there are more announcements to come.'

Early-bird parking is £10 per car until October 31, and from November 1, it will be £15 per car.

Entry to the market is free

## Christmas house tours

In December, Bramham Park is also launching Christmas House Tours, giving visitors a rare opportunity to enjoy an informative tour of the private historic, Grade I-listed home, followed by tea, coffee and mince pies in the Old Kitchen.

Bramham Park is a much-loved family home, and Christmas is traditionally celebrated with a 25ft tree in the hall and many decorations.
