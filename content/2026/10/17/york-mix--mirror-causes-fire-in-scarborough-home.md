Firefighters from Scarborough responded to a report of smoke in an upstairs room of a residential property on Stepney Road in Scarborough last night, Friday 16th October.

On arrival of the crew it was found to have been caused by a small fire on a windowsill which was a result of sunlight reflecting off a mirror.

The fire had self extinguished prior to fire service arrival.
