A brunch spot in Durham has been praised for its “friendly and helpful” staff and “stunning” views.

If you are looking for somewhere in the city to go for breakfast or a relaxing coffee, you might want to try out the Riverview Kitchen.

The café can be found right next to the Framwellgate Bridge and overlooks the River Wear.

## What is the Riverview Kitchen like?

The Riverview Kitchen website says: “Located on the beautiful riverside in Durham, our café has become one of the city’s most loved destinations, famous for its breathtaking views and picture-perfect brunches and lunches.

“Guests travel from across Durham and beyond to capture stunning riverside photos while enjoying beautifully presented dishes that are made to stand out both on the table and on Instagram.”

On the breakfast menu, the restaurant offers a huge range of dishes from a full English breakfast to egg dishes and sourdough toast topped with different ingredients.

You can also get a breakfast stottie served with fried eggs, bacon, or sausage.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21499436.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can enjoy your meal with a riverside view. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The Riverview Kitchen also offers a whole pancake menu where you can choose from classic toppings such as bacon and maple syrup and fresh strawberries and Nutella as well as the special banoffee pancakes and Eton mess pancakes.

There is also a lunch menu offering things like the popular Philly cheese steak sandwich, burgers, wraps, and freshly made salads.

The café has both indoor seating that looks over the river as well as outdoor tables if you are lucky with the weather during your visit.
