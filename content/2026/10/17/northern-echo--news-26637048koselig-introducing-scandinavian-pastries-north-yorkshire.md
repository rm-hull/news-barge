From cardamom buns and Danish pastries to sourdough loaves and community events, one independent bakery is bringing a taste of Scandinavia to North Yorkshire.

Koselig Bakeri, which now has branches in Guisborough, Saltburn and Stokesley, has become well-known for its Nordic-inspired approach to baking.

The bakery takes its name from the Norwegian word "koselig", meaning comfort, contentment and enjoying other people's company.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21581018.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Koselig Bakeri *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

That philosophy shapes both the food and the cafés, where cosy interiors and a relaxed atmosphere are central to the experience.

Popular choices at Koselig include cardamom buns, cinnamon buns, Danish pastries, handcrafted sourdough and other artisan bakery classics.

As autumn settles across North Yorkshire, Koselig Bakeri has become the sort of place locals stop after a weekend stroll, calling in for a warm coffee and a freshly baked pastry before heading home.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21581017.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Koselig pastries *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

The bakery stands out for introducing locals to lesser-known Scandinavian sweet treats, including Koselig's Cherry Streusel Cheesecake Brioche.

The business began in Guisborough before expanding to Saltburn's Milton Street with Koselig By The Sea.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20253958.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Koselig Bakeri *(Image: Koselig)*

Its third and newest location is in the heart of Stokesley, reflecting growing demand for handmade pastries across North Yorkshire.

Koselig also regularly hosts community-focused events, including Wellness Wednesdays and Live Music Sundays, helping transform its cafés into social spaces where people can meet, relax and spend time together.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20253960.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Koselig Bakeri *(Image: Koselig)*
