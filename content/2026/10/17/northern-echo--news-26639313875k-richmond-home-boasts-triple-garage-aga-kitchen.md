First impressions count – and Meadowfield certainly makes a memorable one, but its wow factor doesn’t stop at its attractive facade, beautiful front garden and triple garage that give it kerb appeal Kirsty and Phil would be thrilled to shout about.

Inside it’s pretty special too!

The property’s tiled entrance vestibule opens into an elegant panelled reception hall off which the extensive ground floor accommodation radiates.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584512.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There's plenty to impress even the most discerning of buyers at Meadowfield *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Briefly, this includes a bay windowed living room centred around a marble fireplace housing a gas fire; a generous dining room perfectly suited to formal dinners and family meals alike and which is warmed by a wood-burning stove; a utility room; a rear boot lobby; two cloakrooms and a study fitted with bespoke furniture, making it ideal for those who work from home.

Keen cooks will be in their element in the kitchen, where bespoke hand-built cabinetry topped with granite work surfaces, a double oven AGA and a curved island unit incorporating breakfast bar seating are sure to impress.

Completing the ground floor is a garden room extension that’s a real showstopper, with underfloor heating, a wood-burning stove, four Velux rooflights and bifold doors that open onto a patio.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584516.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There's plenty to impress even the most discerning of buyers at Meadowfield *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Off the garden room is a rear hall that provides internal access to the garage.

Upstairs, a feature window floods the panelled landing with light.

Off the landing are a stylish family bathroom and four double bedrooms, two of which have en-suites.

These include the principal suite which also benefits from a fireplace and an extensive range of built-in wardrobes.

To the front of the house is a lawned garden with planted beds and mature trees, alongside which is a large block-paved driveway that provides parking for several vehicles and access to the garage which has three electric doors.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584518.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There's plenty to impress even the most discerning of buyers at Meadowfield *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

To the rear are patio terraces with lighting perfect for alfresco dining.

Beyond them steps lead up to further seating, an expansive lawned area, vegetable beds, fruit trees and a Victorian-style greenhouse – a real retreat for those with green fingers.

This characterful extended home sits on a wonderful plot in a prime Richmond location from where it offers the kind of space growing families dream of.
