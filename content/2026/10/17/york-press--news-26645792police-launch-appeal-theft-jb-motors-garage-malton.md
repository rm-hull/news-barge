POLICE have launched a CCTV appeal following an alleged shop theft in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened on September 13 at the JB Motors Garage in Wentworth Street, Malton.




North Yorkshire Police is urging anyone who recognises the man pictured on CCTV to contact the force, as he may have more information to assist the investigation.

If you believe you can help, please email [Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Ben.Hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Please quote reference 12260180987 when passing on information.
