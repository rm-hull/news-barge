A popular daytime disco celebrating the sounds of the 70s, 80s and 90s is returning to a North East town this weekend, with organisers promising an afternoon of nostalgia for music lovers.

Decades Day Disco Volume 3 will take over KU and The Social Room in Stockton on Saturday, October 10, offering three rooms of non-stop music and retro fun from 3pm to 8pm.

Designed for the over-30s crowd, the event aims to deliver a high-energy experience without a late-night finish.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21562260.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Decades Day Disco Volume 3 returns to Stockton bringing 70s, 80s and 90s hits across three rooms at KU and The Social Room *(Image: Supplied)*

A spokesperson for Decades Day Disco said: "We can’t wait to bring Decades back to Stockton this Saturday.

"The response to the first two events has been brilliant and there’s clearly a real appetite for getting together, having a dance and enjoying the music people grew up with.

"The whole idea is simple – three rooms, three brilliant decades and five hours of songs everyone knows.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21562261.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Decades Day Disco Volume 3 returns to Stockton bringing 70s, 80s and 90s hits across three rooms at KU and The Social Room *(Image: Supplied)*

"You can spend the afternoon moving between the 70s, 80s and 90s, have a proper day out with your mates and still be home at a reasonable time."

Each room will be dedicated to a different era.

Room One is themed around the 1970s with The Busstop Show, featuring DJ Tony Loveshaft playing favourites from Fleetwood Mac, Elton John, Hall & Oates, Stevie Wonder, Boney M, and ELO.

Room Two takes guests to the 1980s with the return of the legendary Club Visage.

DJs Matt Gloss and Timmy Mullet will spin hits from David Bowie, Duran Duran, Wham!, Gary Numan, Pet Shop Boys, INXS, and Depeche Mode.

Room Three brings the 1990s to life, with the KU DJ team delivering a mix of dance, Britpop, R&B, and pop anthems from Madonna, Britney Spears, Robbie Williams, Take That, Spice Girls, Salt-N-Pepa, and Backstreet Boys.
