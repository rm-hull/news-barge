Two people were taken to hospital after a road traffic collision on the A168 near Dishforth last night (Saturday).

It happened at 7.10pm.

Fire crews from Harrogate and Ripon attended the scene. Both casualties were freed from their vehicles and transported by ambulance.

#### A168, Dishforth

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service handed the incident over to police once their work was complete.
