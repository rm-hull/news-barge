Since winning in Chicago last year, Kiplimo has become the third-fastest marathoner in history – although the achievement was overshadowed by the fact that the only two men to run quicker did it in the same race, at April's London Marathon. Further back, Americans Charles Hicks and Clayton Young may not be in contention to win, but they will both have their eyes on Conner Mantz’s American record, which stands at 2:04.43.

Kosgei's Kenyan counterpart Sharon Lokedi is another one to watch as she makes her debut on this course, having won the Boston Marathon in each of the past two years and claimed victory in New York in 2022.

The course is famous for producing fast times, with seven world records set across the men's and women's races over the years, and this year should be no different.
