A County Durham man was fined after admitting he kept a prohibited stun gun at home.

Michael Snowdon, 45, had a stun gun at his Redwood Flats home in Brandon on May 31.

Snowdon admitted the offence at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates' Court on October 7.

The court ordered the stun gun forfeited and destroyed under the Sentencing Act 2020.

Snowdon was fined £120 and ordered to pay £85 in prosecution costs and a £48 victim surcharge.

The court took his guilty plea into account when sentencing him.
