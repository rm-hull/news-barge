# Watch moment plane aborts landing at Leeds Bradford Airport and performs go-around in strong winds

## A video shows a plane aborting landing and performing a go-around in strong winds at Leeds Bradford Airport on Friday.

Footage shows the moment a plane aborted landing and performed a go-around manoeuvre at Leeds Bradford Airport on Friday 9 October.

The video shows the plane wobbling from side to side in the wind and briefly touching down on the runway, before ascending back into the sky again.

According to Flightradar24, the Titan Airbus A320 departed London Stansted Airport at 12:39pm and landed at Leeds Bradford Airport at 1:27pm on its second attempt after performing the go-around.

![A plane aborts landing and performs a go-around in strong winds at Leeds Bradford Airport on Friday.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/11/9/12/YouTube-Thumbnail-00-x-1080-px)-(2).jpg?trim=7,0,7,0&width=640&quality=65)


A go around is a manoeuvre where a pilot decides not to complete a landing attempt and instead takes the aircraft back into the air to make another approach or divert to an alternative airport. The go-around manoeuvre allows pilots to abort a landing if they judge that conditions are no longer suitable for a safe touchdown. Go-arounds are a standard procedure and are practiced regularly by pilots to ensure proficiency.
