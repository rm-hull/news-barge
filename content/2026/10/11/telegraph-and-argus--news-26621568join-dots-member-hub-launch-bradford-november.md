A new networking hub for the construction industry is launching in Bradford.

Join The Dots Member Hub, founded by business development expert Angela Bray, will bring together investors, developers, professional services and supply chain businesses to foster collaboration across the sector.

The hub will officially launch with a breakfast event on Tuesday, November 10, at Darley Street Market’s Food Hall.

Angela Bray, who has organised construction events across West Yorkshire for several years, said: "I’m holding this event in Bradford because I want to increase the professional footfall into the city to support the local economy.

"Bradford is a big city with huge potential and is undergoing a real transformation from a construction perspective.

"I want the local construction community to benefit from the opportunities this is creating, and the best way to do that is to bring all these professionals together so they can work collaboratively."

Councillor Stephen Place, leader of Bradford Council, will address attendees at the event, alongside Richard Hollinson, the council’s assistant director of planning, transport and highways, and Steven Jenks, senior project director.

Join The Dots Member Hub offers two membership tiers:

A free Connect Membership and a paid Dot Membership, which includes access to additional events and resources.
