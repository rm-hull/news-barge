# Man, 21, fighting for life after serious crash involving e-scooter and car

## A 21-year-old man has been taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries following a crash involving an e-scooter and a car in Doncaster.

At around 1.45pm on Saturday (Oct 10), police were called to reports of a crash at the junction of South Parade and Albion Place in Doncaster.

The crash reportedly involved a blue Kia Sportage and an e-scooter.

![The junction of South Parade and Albion Place in Doncaster](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/11/9/22/The-junction-of-South-Parade-and-Albion-Place-in-Doncaster.png?trim=0,96,0,95&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Officers and paramedics attended the scene, and the rider of the e-scooter, a 21-year-old man, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries.

The driver of the Kia remained at the scene to assist police with their enquiries.

Police have launched an appeal for any witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said: “If you witnessed the incident or have dashcam footage of the collision, please get in touch online or by calling 101, quoting incident number 456 of 10 October 2026.”
