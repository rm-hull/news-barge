A North Yorkshire village nursery has reopened after a major transformation.

Jigsaws Childcare Nursery in Church Fenton has welcomed children back following a major £750,000 redevelopment project over the summer.

The project has transformed its facilities and created additional childcare places for families in Church Fenton and the surrounding area – increasing its capacity from 51 to 77 children.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/L-to-R-Amy-Ross-Operations-Manager-Helen-Smith-Business-Manager.webp)

The two-month programme of works, which began in July, involved the removal of the original prefabricated portable building, which had served the nursery since it was established in 2007, and was no longer fit for purpose.

Working in collaboration with architects Henszel Designs and modular building manufacturer Phoenix Building Systems, the new facility was manufactured off-site before the 14 individual modules were transported and craned into position. The new energy-efficient two-storey building provides modernised facilities and an improved environment for children’s learning, development and wellbeing.

To celebrate the milestone, Jigsaws hosted a free open day for the local community, giving families the opportunity to explore the new facilities, meet the team and learn more about the nursery’s childcare provision.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jigsaws-childcare-nursery-5.webp)

Established in 2007, Jigsaws is an inclusive, community-rooted, not-for-profit organisation providing full-day nursery care, wraparound before- and after-school clubs, and support for children with special educational needs.

As it approaches its 20th anniversary in 2027, the redevelopment represents a significant investment in the organisation’s future and its role in supporting local families.

The move follows the April 2026 announcement that Jigsaws scooped the Healthy Early Years Gold Award, recognising its work to support children’s physical, social and emotional wellbeing, promote healthy eating, and encourage outdoor learning.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jigsaws-childcare-nursery-7.webp)

Helen Smith, business manager at Jigsaws Childcare, said “This has been a landmark year for Jigsaws. From investing £750,000 in a new nursery to securing our Healthy Early Years Gold Award, we have achieved some really significant milestones as an organisation, and we are incredibly proud of what they represent.

“As we approach our 20th anniversary, this is about more than celebrating our past – it is about shaping what comes next. This investment is future-proofing Jigsaws for the next 20 years and beyond, giving us the foundations to grow, innovate and continue making a real difference to children and families across our community.

“Behind all of this is an exceptional team of 28 members of staff who bring our values to life every day. Their dedication, alongside the support of our families and the wider community, has helped make Jigsaws what it is today.”
