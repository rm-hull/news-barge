Proving that you don’t have to have the biggest house in the street to have a beautiful home that ticks all the boxes on your wishlist is this stylish semi-detached property in Newton Aycliffe.

Perfectly suited to modern family living, its ground floor comprises an entrance hall with cloakroom off that opens into a stylish open-plan kitchen and dining room with contemporary fitted units and a range of appliances as well as a central island breakfast bar.

The living room, located at the rear of the house, has a feature panelled wall housing a contemporary fireplace plus French doors that open onto the rear garden, allowing for an abundance of natural light and a view of the outdoor space.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557305.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This Newton Aycliffe home may be petite but it packs a big punch when it comes to appeal *(Image: Carvers)*

Upstairs are the family bathroom and three bedrooms, including the principal double bedroom with an en-suite shower room.

To the front of the property is a driveway with off-street parking, while to the rear is a beautiful enclosed garden that is one of the standout features of this home.

Designed to be a low-maintenance outdoor oasis ideal for relaxation or entertaining, it includes composite decking with planted beds, artificial grass and a seating area under an awning.

uPVC double glazing, gas central heating and solar panels make this property perfect for buyers looking for a stylish home they can move into and enjoy without doing anything except unpack their belongings.
