Go Ape Dalby Forest is launching a new Halloween event for families this half term.

The Potion Hunt will take families up into the trees at Go Ape Dalby Forest from October 24 to November 1.

The hunt involves finding clues to potion ingredients in the trees and uncovering a secret potion recipe.

A medal and sweets are up for grabs as a prize.

Go Ape said the event is ideal for "young explorers looking for more thrills than scares this spooky season".

The event is designed to provide an alternative to traditional Halloween activities, encouraging families to work together outdoors.

The Potion Hunt is available for £5 per person and can be added to the Treetop Adventure and Treetop Adventure Plus experiences.

Go Ape was founded in 2002 by Rebecca and Tristram Mayhew.

The adventure company has 36 locations across England, Scotland, and Wales.

Find out more at goape.co.uk/potion-hunt.
