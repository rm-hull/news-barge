Police have launched an investigation after a woman was harassed in Harrogate.

The public order incident took place on Wednesday (7 October) between 10.25am and 10.35am on Queens Road behind the Duchy Hospital.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A female was walking when an unknown male ran up to her and repeatedly asked her for personal contact details.

#### Incident location

“The unknown male is described as an Asian male, tall, skinny build, aged 20s to 30s, wearing a red turban/hat, joggers, jacket and trainers.

“The male reportedly had stubble facial hair.”

Police are seeking any information, witnesses and any CCTV or video footage.
