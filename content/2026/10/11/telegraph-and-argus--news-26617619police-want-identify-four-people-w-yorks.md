Police are seeking potential witnesses and suspects linked to alleged crimes across West Yorkshire.

West Yorkshire Police regularly release CCTV images of people they want to question about alleged offences.

The people shown may be suspects or witnesses police want to question.

The image shows four people: Person 1 is top left, Person 2 top right, Person 3 bottom left and Person 4 bottom right.

Police have warned the public not to approach anyone shown, but to contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
