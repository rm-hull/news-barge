Buyers in the market for a home that sets their heart aflutter might find everything they desire... plus quite a few things more... in The Burrow, an exceptional contemporary family home in the sought-after village of Crathorne, approximately four miles from Yarm.

An immaculate and beautifully appointed modern house completed in around 2020, The Burrow has subsequently been enhanced by a substantial kitchen extension that lies at the heart of the property.

![Property](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557350.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Burrow is no ordinary village home thanks to a high-spec finish which offers the height of luxury *(Image: Savills)*

Designed for both family life and entertaining, the open-plan kitchen, sitting and dining room is filled with natural light and features extensive bifold doors that open directly into the lawned gardens.

The three separate areas that make up this room each have their own character, yet an overarching style that ties them together.

This includes wood trusses and an exposed brick wall that runs the full length of one side of the room, providing a stark contrast to the kitchen’s contemporary fitted units.

![Property](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557344.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Burrow is no ordinary village home thanks to a high-spec finish which offers the height of luxury *(Image: Savills)*

Alongside the kitchen – which is home to a double Belfast sink, a range of appliances and a large central island breakfast bar with an integrated dining table at one end – is a dining area with space for a large table, then the extensive double seating area, one part of which is centred around a stove.

Also on the ground floor is a utility room; the stunning principal suite made up of a dressing room featuring fitted wardrobes and a dedicated dressing area, a beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom and a spacious bedroom which enjoys lovely views across the gardens; a study; a snug/playroom with a cloakroom off and a generous sitting room/family room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557336.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Burrow is no ordinary village home thanks to a high-spec finish which offers the height of luxury *(Image: Savills)*

This has two large rooflights and extensive sliding doors into the garden that together allow natural light to flood into its three separate zones – a play area, seating area and a stylish bar.

A particularly striking feature of the house is the suspended floating staircase with glazed display area beneath which creates an architectural focal point and a dramatic sense of space between the ground and first floors.

The first floor is home to a family bathroom plus three bedrooms, two of which benefit from en-suite shower rooms.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21557347.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Burrow is no ordinary village home thanks to a high-spec finish which offers the height of luxury *(Image: Savills)*

Upstairs again on the second floor are two further bedrooms and a shower room that could be used as a suite for visiting guests or would be perfect for families requiring flexible accommodation.

The property benefits from underfloor heating throughout, supplied by an air source heat pump which provides an efficient and comfortable heating system, complemented by air-conditioning units in the south-facing bedrooms to ensure they are kept at a comfortable temperature no matter the weather.

Its lawned gardens provide an excellent setting for outdoor entertaining and family enjoyment, with a substantial garden room – currently arranged as an entertaining area with a bar and WC – offering a versatile additional space that’s perfect for gatherings.

There is also a detached double garage with a gym/studio above which could be used for a variety of purposes, including as an office.

The Burrow, Crathorne, is on the market at a guide price of £1.1m.
