A North East road is set to close for up to two weeks, according to a public notice.

The closure on Cowpen Bewley Road, Billingham, will begin on Monday, October 19 for roadworks, Stockton Council has confirmed.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

It will stretch from around 930 metres south of Macklin Avenue junction for approximately 670 metres in a southerly direction.

The works are expected to last until Monday, November 2, and will affect all traffic, including emergency vehicles, which will not be granted access.

The council says a diversion route will be in place and clearly signposted for drivers to follow.
