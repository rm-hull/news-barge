## Japan reclaimed the Mixed Team World Championship title in Baku, defeating Russia in the final to secure their eighth gold. After overcoming China in the semi-final, Japan sealed victory through Akari Omori, while Russia reached their first-ever mixed team final.

Today in Baku at the World Judo Championships, the Mixed Team competition is about more than medals. It’s about team spirit, unity and athletes fighting not just for themselves, but for each other.

In the semi-final, Japan went up against the People's Republic of China and **UCHIMURA** earned Japan an early lead.

Double World Champion **MURAO** continued their momentum with another ippon.

**ARAI** executed a lovely turn over and held Japan into the final. After last year’s team bronze, could Japan reclaim their title?

The other semi-final was between Uzbekistan and the Russian Federation.

An early ippon from **AHADOV** put Uzbekistan in the lead.**LISHCHENKO** evened the score with a yuko.**MURTOZOEV** took the score 2 - 1. But**RECHKALOVA** forced a submission. 2 - 2.

**TASOEV** scored big, sending his team into the lead.**AMINOVA** equalised to 3 - 3, forcing a Golden Score contest. +90kg was drawn!

TASOEV didn’t disappoint, throwing for ippon a second time to put his team into their first ever mixed team final.

In the final, tensions were high with the mixed team gold medal on the line.

In the first contest, a yuko was enough for **TANAKA Shiho** to push Japan into an early lead. MURAO Sanshiro was up next and continued the momentum. 2 - 0 to Japan. Mao ARAI continued the trend to take Japan to 3 - 0.

**Tamerlan BASHAEV** flipped the script to earn a win for his team. But**OMORI Akari** clinched the title with her win, claiming an eighth Team gold for Japan.

International Judo Federation President Mr Marius VIZER awarded the gold trophy.

CEO of Harvest Group Mr Almaz Alsenov awarded the silver trophy.

The two bronze trophies were awarded by International Judo Federation Vice President Dr. Laszlo Toth and International Judo Federation Vice President Obaid Al Anzi.

What an incredible celebration of judo it’s been at the World Judo Championships, thank you for watching.
