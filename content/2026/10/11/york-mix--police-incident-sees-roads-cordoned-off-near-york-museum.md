One man has been arrested after an incident near the National Railway Museum in York this afternoon.

North Yorkshire Police have issued this statement.

“We were called to Leeman Road, in York, at 2.19pm today (Sunday 11 October) following a report of an altercation involving three men during which a bladed weapon was also seen.

“Officers attended within minutes and located two victims who have sustained injuries.

“Both have been taken to hospital for treatment. Their injuries are not believed to be life-threatening.

“An extensive search was launched immediately across York city centre to trace the suspect. This involved armed officers, police dog units and neighbourhood policing officers.

As a result of swift enquiries in York, with the support of Humberside Police, a man was arrested at **Hull Railway Station** later this afternoon, on suspicion of serious assault and possession of an offensive weapon in public place.

“He is currently in police custody.”

#### Incident location

A cordon remains in place on Leeman Road which affects both cyclists and those on foot.

Anyone who witnessed the incident, or who has relevant CCTV, dashcam or mobile phone footage, is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference 12260200081.

Enquiries remain ongoing.
