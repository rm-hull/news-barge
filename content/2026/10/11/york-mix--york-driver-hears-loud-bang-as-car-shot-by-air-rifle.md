A car was damaged while driving in a York suburb.

It happened near to the junction of **Tudor Road** and**Rosemary Road** in Acomb on**Friday, 2 October** at about**10.45pm**.

A silver Vauxhall Astra was targeted.

#### Incident location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim reported hearing a loud bang and subsequently discovered damage to a glass panel of the vehicle.

“The damage is described as being consistent with that which may have been caused by an air weapon.

“At this stage of the investigation, we are seeking any information, CCTV, dashcam footage, or doorbell footage that may assist in identifying those responsible.

“We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who was in the area at the time of the incident and may have captured relevant footage.”
