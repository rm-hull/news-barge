A "wholesome" café in Wakefield has been praised for its "fun themed drinks", with some being inspired by a popular cosy TV show.

If you are looking for a cosy spot to visit this autumn, you might want to try Buttons in Wakefield.

Buttons aims to create "a welcoming destination" for visitors, offering "exceptional food" and "outstanding service".

If you are a fan of the show Gilmore Girls, the café is currently offering a menu themed around the show.

## What is Buttons in Wakefield like?

The family-run café offers a range of breakfast items that are served all day, including hearty fry-ups.

You can also get breakfast sandwiches, lots of bagels with different fillings, and eggs cooked in nearly any way you’d like.

For those with a sweet tooth, you might prefer picking between pancakes and French toast with a range of toppings available from Nutella to berries and pistachios.

The Gilmore Girls-themed menu features lots of special drinks like Sookie’s cookie butter, a coffee that can be served hot or cold and comes with cookie butter, milk chocolate, and is topped with a chocolate chunk cookie.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21545678.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The café is currently offering Gilmore Girls themed drinks. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

There’s also the Luke’s waffles drink that can be made as either a latte or matcha and comes with vanilla maple, brown sugar, caramel, and is topped with mini waffles.

You could opt for the movie night at Lorelai’s, which is a coffee that is made using milk chocolate, caramel, and marshmallows, and is topped with pretzels, popcorn, caramel drizzle, and homemade whipped cream.

## What do reviews say about Buttons in Wakefield?

Buttons has a rating of 4.6 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with 128 people leaving reviews on Tripadvisor.

One person said: “I love Buttons. I've been on several occasions for breakfast, to one of their events, and for pizza Saturdays.

“This place is a gem, lovely wholesome atmosphere, lovely staff who are clearly passionate about Buttons, and absolutely fantastic food and fun themed drinks.

“Came today with the family too and enjoyed every second.

“I’ll be there as often as I can, see you soon!”

Another person said: “I visited this morning with my teen daughter and if course she had to try a Gilmore girls themed latte, she got Luke's waffles one and was absolutely delicious (I sneaked a taste).

“She also had the Nutella pancake stack, again delicious and enough for 2.

“The place had the most wholesome autumn vibe with a lovely chilled playlist.

“It's not cheap and can be a wait at busy times but we most certainly will be back.”
