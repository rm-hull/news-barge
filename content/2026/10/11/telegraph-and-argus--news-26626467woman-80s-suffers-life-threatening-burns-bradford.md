A woman in her 80s has been taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries after suffering serious burns at a property.

Police were called by fire service colleagues to reports that the woman had sustained serious burn injuries at Woodlands street on Sunday afternoon.

The incident prompted a significant emergency response, with multiple police vehicles, fire engines and ambulances attending the scene.

## Woman taken to hospital

The woman was taken to hospital for treatment after the incident.

West Yorkshire Police said the woman, who is aged in her 80s, suffered serious burns injuries and remains in hospital with life-threatening injuries.

No further details regarding her condition have been released.

## Enquiries remain ongoing

A police scene remains in place as enquiries continue to establish exactly what happened.

Emergency services were first alerted to the incident at 1.14pm on Sunday, October 11.

Residents reported a large emergency services presence in the area throughout the afternoon as officers and firefighters carried out their investigations.

## Incident not being treated as suspicious

Police have confirmed the incident is not currently being treated as suspicious.

Enquiries remain ongoing to determine the circumstances surrounding the incident.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said officers were called by fire service colleagues to reports that a woman had suffered serious burns injuries and that a scene remains in place while investigations continue.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "Police were called by fire service colleagues to reports that a woman had suffered serious burns injuries at an address in Woodlands Street, Bradford, earlier today.

"The woman, aged in her 80s, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries.

"A scene remains in place at the address as enquiries continue to establish exactly what happened.

"The incident is not currently being treated as suspicious.

"The report was received at 1.14pm today (11 October)."
