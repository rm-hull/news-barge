Even the most seasoned globe-trotter likely finds traveling stressful. Between panic-packing, navigating cramped terminals, and living out of a suitcase, each phase of being away from home can be overstimulating. That’s why the best gifts for travelers are ones that focus on ease and peace of mind.

Jet-setters may think they have their travel routine down to a science, but the perfect gift can enhance any experience, whether it's a three-day business retreat or a two-week adventure across Europe. Below, find my favorite gifts for frequent travelers, from an essential suitcase set and a portable e-reader to comfortable compression socks and the perfect neck pillow.

For more travel gifts, check out our guides to the Best Travel Tote Bags, Gifts for Women, and the Best Stocking Stuffers.

## Featured in This Gift Guide

## A Matching Luggage Set

Every traveler needs a luggage set that includes a checked case and a carry-on. Chances are, the jet-setter in your life already has one they love. But if they’re due for a refresh, this set from Quince is an excellent (and expandable) gift. The brand makes some of my favorite travel gear, with pieces that look remarkably similar to designs from premium brands—but at a fraction of the price. This set comes in a range of minimalist colors and has all the features you could want in luggage, including 360-degree spinner wheels, a TSA-approved lock, and high-quality zippers.

## Vacation Reading

For the bookworm who’s always traveling, the Kindle Paperwhite is the obvious gift. Reviewer Nena Farrell says the 12th-generation model has the highest contrast ratio of any Kindle and lasts up to three months on a single charge. Whether you're reading in direct sun or a dim plane cabin, its 7-inch display is crisp and easy on the eyes thanks to its warm backlighting. Plus, its IPX8 waterproofing rating means you can confidently read poolside without worrying about water damage. The Signature Edition adds wireless charging, auto-brightness, and double the storage at 32 GB.

Check out our guides to the Best Kindles and the Best E-Readers for more recommendations.

## Passport Control

If you’re traveling abroad—or just don’t yet have your Real ID—a travel wallet is a must. The puffy Luka from Calpak comes in 23 colors, so you’re sure to find an option that fits your giftee’s vibe. It’s got plenty of room for cash, cards, your passport, and other documents, and it zips securely for peace of mind. Just note that it is quite large—about the size of a mass-market paperback. On the plus side, this makes it easy to find at a glance in a big bag or backpack. On the minus, a flight attendant once asked me to put it in the overhead compartment when she saw it on my lap. *—Kat Merck*

## Travel Organizer

Frequent travelers who don't use collapsible cubes when packing are *struggling*, and they don't even know it. Give them this travel tip: Eagle Creek's set is the best, made of durable fabric with compression and high-quality zipper pulls. Editor Kat Merck says there's just enough mesh for air circulation to prevent odor buildup or fabric degradation, and the bottom and back of the cubes are opaque for privacy. They're water-resistant and designed with a top handle. The largest cube in this set is also split into two sections for clean clothes and dirty laundry. Eagle Creek also sells larger sets for extended stays and slim cubes to help you maximize narrow spaces in your luggage.

Check out our guide to the Best Packing Cubes for more recommendations.

## Sun Block

Do you know a frequent traveler who’s always headed somewhere sunny and always comes back with a sunburn as a souvenir? Solbari’s hats make the perfect gift, as they're all designed for sun protection with built-in Ultraviolet Protection Factor (UPF). As a resident of sunny San Diego, I wear this UPF 50+ hat often to protect my face and scalp, but it would work well anywhere. It travels well, too, since it’s soft, flexible, and doesn't take up much space. I regularly stow it in the bottom of my beach bag or stroller, and it would also be easy to pack in a carry-on. *—Nena Farrell*

## Glam to Go

This is my all-time favorite travel makeup bag. It comes in eight colors and three sizes (small, medium, and large). The medium has enough room for all the makeup I need for a two-week trip, and I'm an overpacker. It has 15 small pockets in addition to four larger mesh pockets, three flexible compartments, and two adjustable dividers, meaning you'll have a designated spot for nearly every product you own, including your makeup brushes. The customizable layout lets you configure it around what you actually pack. And it also feels and looks luxe. For everyday needs, get the small. The medium is my pick for short trips, and the large has enough space to double as a toiletry bag for longer trips.

## Baggage Tracker

Airports are chaotic enough without needing to worry about losing your luggage. AirTags can be a bit divisive, but they’re the best way to keep tabs on your valuables if you have an iPhone. Plus, with busy flights often leading to last-minute gate checks and delays, AirTags are well worth the extra security. If your friend or loved one is an Android user, I recommend the Chipolo Pop instead.

Check out our guide to the Best Bluetooth Trackers for more recommendations.

## Lights Out

Traveling means sacrificing the comfort of your bed and sometimes facing unpredictable sleeping conditions. An eye mask for sleep is a small luxury that can make even the least ideal sleep arrangements more tolerable. I love Bearaby's full temple-to-temple coverage and gentle fit that doesn't squeeze your head. Made from natural eucalyptus fibers, it's breathable and anti-frizz, which is perfect for anyone who styles their hair before flights.

Check out our guide to the Best Sleep Masks for more recommendations.

## Stay Cozy

If I’m traveling away from home, whether for a day or a week, it’s a sure bet that I have Rumpl’s machine-washable Travel Puffy with me. I’ve had to use it for countless unexpected situations—sitting on something cold or wet, napping or relaxing at the airport or on a cold airplane, using it as a beach or picnic blanket, or just wearing it as a poncho because I forgot a jacket. (It has a little clip in the front for this purpose.) It’s also super easy to return it to its 4-by-10-inch dry-bag-style stuff sack, which can slip into a travel backpack’s water bottle slot or clip onto a loop or MOLLE webbing. *—Kat Merck*

## A Portable Charging Station

This portable 3-in-1 charger is a great gift for on-the-go Apple lovers. It delivers up to 15 watts to an iPhone or any Qi2-certified device via an adjustable magnetic pad, so you can doomscroll *while* you charge. There's a second pad tucked behind for AirPods (or other wireless earbuds) and a fold-out Apple Watch charger. Anker even includes a 5-foot USB-C cable and a wall adapter, all backed by a two-year warranty.

Check out our guides to the Best Apple 3-in-1 Wireless Chargers, Best Power Banks, and Best Portable Chargers for more recommendations.

## Comfort in Coach

You don’t normally realize you need a travel pillow until 45 minutes before an international flight, when you’re standing in the Hudson News debating whether the overpriced U-shaped bolster is worth it. (It isn’t.) Save your friend the agony and get them the Trtl Neck Pillow. It may look corny, but it’s my favorite travel pillow. Instead of constricting your neck in a doughnut, these scarf-style travel pillows have an internal frame that cradles your head without physical strain. It's lightweight—just half a pound—and packable; you can stuff it into its accompanying storage bag and clip it onto your travel bag when not in use.

Check out our guide to the Best Travel Pillows for more recommendations.

## Stay Charged Abroad

This 100-watt universal travel adapter covers more than 200 countries and can charge multiple devices at once. The classic slider design pushes out EU, UK, and US plugs, and you can rotate the pins for sockets in Australia and China. There’s a universal input socket, an additional side socket for US or Japanese plugs, and two USB-C ports and two USB-A ports on the bottom. Reviewer Simon Hill especially loves the retractable USB-C cable, which is around 25 inches long, delivers up to 100 watts in total, and saves you from having to pack an extra cable.

Check out our guide to the Best Travel Adapters for more recommendations.

## Happy Feet

No matter how short or long the flight, I don’t fly without the Bombas compression socks. Available in men’s and women’s styles, these medium-compression socks (15 to 20 mmHg) support healthy circulation, reduce and relieve swelling, and help speed up recovery after long days of travel and sightseeing. Bombas also gives back with every purchase, donating a pair of socks to someone in need.
