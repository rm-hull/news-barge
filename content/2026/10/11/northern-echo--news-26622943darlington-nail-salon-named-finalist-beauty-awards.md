A Darlington nail salon specialising in builder gel and intricate nail artistry has been named a finalist in the Northern Echo's Best Beauty Salon Awards.

Bella Nails, on Duke Street in Darlington, made the shortlist for its beautifully finished nails, attention to detail and personalised service.

The salon specialises in builder gel (BIAB), gel nails and hard gel extensions, creating strong, durable, natural-looking nails tailored to each client.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21562360.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

Bella Nails says it prioritises nail health, precise shaping and long-lasting results.

Hayley, the technician behind Bella Nails, is known for detailed nail art and creative designs.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21562363.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

Clients have also praised the salon’s customer service.

One customer, who left a five-star review, said: "First time and definitely will return, lovely girl and very chatty.

"Thank you so much for the gorgeous nails."
