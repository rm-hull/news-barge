Firefighters from Acomb were called to Beckfield Lane last night (Saturday) after reports of a tree on fire.

They responded to the incident at 9.50pm.

Crews arrived to find a small blaze at the base of the tree and used knapsack sprayers and small tools to extinguish it fully.
