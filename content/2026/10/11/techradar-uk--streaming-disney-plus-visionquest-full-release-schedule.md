![Ultron standing menacingly behind Vision in VisionQuest](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/NNbWMtEsCSpy7RaADYFBGL-1920-80.jpg)

It's Vision's time to shine. After playing a supporting role in myriad Marvel movies and TV shows, the superpowered synthezoid is finally set to star in his own project in *VisionQuest*.

Set around a year — in-universe, that is — after *WandaVision*, the Disney+ TV Original will reunite us with Vision (Paul Bettany), who's suffering from an identity crisis following the events of* WandaVision*'s finale. Searching for meaning, he consults the A.I. programs in his programming for guidance and advice, including Ultron (James Spader), aka the villainous android last seen in* Avengers: Age of Ultron*.

![Marvel Television’s VisionQuest | Official Trailer - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/sXKnmgmbkoE/maxresdefault.jpg)

Soon, though, Vision is forced to go on the run with Tommy Shepherd (Ruaridh Mollica), a mysterious individual who claims to be one of Vision's reincarnated twin sons, when nefarious forces come gunning for the highly-advanced tech that Vision possesses.

With that somewhat lengthy story synopsis out of the way, we can move onto the real reason for you clicking on this article. That being: when will every episode of *VisionQuest* be released on one of the world's best streaming services?

## What are the release dates for every episode of VisionQuest?

![Vision and Thomas Shepard at night in VisionQuest](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/77jruM49PJv5PF7KJwTuch-1200-80.jpg)

The penultimate Marvel Phase 6 TV series will arrive with a two-episode premiere on **Wednesday, October 14**. That's a worldwide launch for the sci-fi comic book show, too, so viewers in North and South America won't be able to watch them earlier than everyone else.

As for when the other six chapters should be released, read on for more details:

* ***VisionQuest* episode 1** — Wednesday, October 14
* ***VisionQuest* episode 2** — Wednesday, October 14
* ***VisionQuest* episode 3** — Wednesday, October 21
* ***VisionQuest* episode 4** — Wednesday, October 28
* ***VisionQuest* episode 5** — Wednesday, November 4
* ***VisionQuest* episode 6** — Wednesday, November 11
* ***VisionQuest* episode 7** — Wednesday, November 18
* ***VisionQuest* episode 8** — Wednesday, November 25

Why do I say "should" in the above paragraph? Marvel fansite Nexus Point News claims that episodes 3 and 4 will be released on the same date, i.e. October 21. The same is apparently true of episodes 5 and 6, which are rumored to debut together on October 28. Lastly, episodes 7 and 8 will supposedly launch separate to one another, meaning they'd come out November 4 and 11.

## What time can I watch VisionQuest?

![F.R.I.D.A.Y, J.A.R.V.I.S, U, and D.U.M-E looking at Vision in VisionQuest](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/KgRDUVpWMG5BXEvSrE8xJh-1200-80.jpg)

With *VisionQuest* due to air worldwide on October 14, I expect the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) project's first two episodes to drop at**12am PT / 3am ET in the US and Canada**. If they do, the remaining entries should do likewise.

Live elsewhere? Here's when new installments of *VisionQuest* are expected to premier in your time zone:

* **US** — 12am PT / 3am ET
* **Canada** — 12am PT / 3am ET
* **UK** — 8am BST
* **India** — 12:30pm IST
* **Singapore** — 3pm SGT
* **Australia** — 5pm AEST
* **New Zealand** — 7pm NZST

It's worth noting that the above times will change in the weeks ahead.

Indeed, with the clocks going back in the northern hemisphere, as well as forward below the equator, some episodes will be released earlier or later than the aforementioned times. As and when they come into effect, I'll update this section.
