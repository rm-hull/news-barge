Police have issued an appeal for information after a 15-year-old was reported missing overnight.

Officers are asking members of the public to help locate Iveta, who was last seen on Saturday night and is known to have links to the Manchester area.

## **Search Underway for Missing Teenager**

Enquiries are ongoing to trace Iveta, with police urging anyone who may have seen the teenager or knows where she is to come forward.

At the time she was last seen, Iveta was believed to be wearing black leggings, a long-sleeved black top, a black puffer jacket and Nike shoes.

Police have released a photograph of the missing teenager as part of their appeal.

## **Appeal for Information**

The image shared by police shows Iveta with dark hair pulled back and hoop earrings, wearing a dark-coloured jacket over a grey top.

Officers hope the photograph will help members of the public recognise her and assist with efforts to locate her.

## **How to Contact Police**

Anyone who has seen Iveta or has information about where she may be is asked to contact police on 101, quoting reference 148 of October 11, 2026.

Police are urging anyone with information, however small it may seem, to get in touch as enquiries continue.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "Have you seen Iveta? She is 15 years old missing from Halifax since last night. Iveta has links to the Manchester area.

"She was last known to be wearing black leggings, long sleeved black top, black puffer jacket and Nike shoes.

"If you have any information as to where she may be, please call 101 and quote 148 11/10/26 #missing"
