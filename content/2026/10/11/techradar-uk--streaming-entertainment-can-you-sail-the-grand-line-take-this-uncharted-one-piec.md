Since *One Piece* began in the 1990s, its fandom has spread all over the world after being adapted into loads of different types of media. It's the best-selling comic series in terms of volume, amongst other records, and its various adaptations have won awards and broke records too.

So, how well do you know *One Piece*? To figure that out, we've compiled a 30-question* One Piece* quiz that will see if you can earn your sea legs. These questions won't pick your brain about the story: you don't need to know too much about the characters, pirates, events and lore of the Blue Planet.

Instead, we'll be asking you about the franchise itself, and the way *One Piece* has expanded into the real world. The questions are organized by difficulty: we'll start with 10 easy ones, then 10 medium ones, and finally 10 harder ones.
