## What's on at the Milton Rooms in Malton this autumn

Samuel Moore

There’s something for everyone

There’s something to appeal to all tastes to welcome autumn in at the Milton Rooms in Malton.

For music lovers, there is flamenco guitar with Samuel Moore on October 17, blues at the Ryedale Blues Club with Bison Hip on the 29th and an evening with the New York Brass Band on the 30th.

There’s also the final Daytime on the Dancefloor of 2026 on October 24 with a D.I.S.C.O. spectacular from 4pm to 8pm, and a Sunday open mic session on the 25th.

Plus bestselling author Terry Deary appears on October 18 to take the audience through ‘A History of The World in Ten Wars’.

New events are being added to the Milton Rooms programme all the time, so head over to www.themiltonrooms.com to see what’s coming up and to book tickets. And check out what is coming up in 2027 while you are there!

October

Saturday, October 17: Samuel Moore Flamenco Guitarist. The mesmerising music of Samuel Moore, the multi-award winning flamenco guitarist. With his exceptional talent and mastery of flamenco, Samuel brings the heart of Spain to this concert. Whether you are a dedicated flamenco fan or simply looking for a unique cultural experience, this event promises to be unforgettable. 7.30pm. Tickets £15.

Sunday. October 18: Kemps Presents Terry Deary ‘A History of The World in Ten Wars. Join Terry Deary, the number one Sunday Times bestselling author, for a lively and laugh-out-loud exploration of history’s most pivotal conflicts, told only as he can. In his latest book, A History of the World in Ten Wars, Deary takes us on a fast-paced irreverent journey through the battles that shaped our world. Expect surprising insights, unexpected truths and plenty of humour. 2,30pm. Ticket only £10, ticket & book £28.

Saturday, October 24: Daytime on the Dancefloor D.I.S.C.O. Spectacular! The last Daytime on the Dancefloor for 2026 with a D.I.S.C.O special. DJ Murray Mint and his dancers are back for a four-hour disco special from 4pm to 8pm. This event is for over-18s only. Tickets £15.

Sunday, October 25: Open Mic Sessions. Informal music sessions held in the Studio Bar. Open to all-comers – and performers get a free drink from the bar. The last Sunday of every month from 3pm-6pm. Bring your own instrument. PA and mics are provided and tech staff will be on hand. Admission free.

Thursday, October 29: Ryedale Blues Club – Bison Hip. The band are Paul Sloway – vocals; Malcolm Button – drums; Steven Radziwonik – keys; John Gilmour Smith – guitar and Graeme Carswell – bass. Bison Hip were formed by long-time friends during a lockdown Zoom call in 2020. The band are all over 50, with the exception of keyboard player and spring chicken Steven, who clocks in at a youthful 40. By the time they finally got together in early 2021, the blueprint was clear - real musicians, real instruments, no click tracks, culminating in a unique blues rock sound with real life stories. 8pm. Tickets £10.

Friday, October 30: An evening with the New York Brass Band. The New York Brass Band are back at the Milton Rooms for what promises to be another brilliant night. After the huge success of the last three years, this is the fourth year of bringing the party to Malton, Yorkshire-born, New Orleans-inspired and Glastonbury favourites for eight years, they’ve performed for the likes of Liam Gallagher, Kylie Minogue, Ellie Goulding and Jamie Oliver, bringing their infectious energy and huge brass sound wherever they go. 7pm. Tickets £15 from www.newyorkbrassband.co.uk or call Ali on 07813 889085.

November

Friday, November 13: York Latinos. The Latinos return for an unforgettable night of live Cuban and Latin music, bringing the spirit of Havana back to Malton. Inspired by the sounds of traditional Cuba and the golden era of Buena Vista Social Club, York Latinos blend authentic Caribbean rhythms and high-energy performance into a vibrant live experience full of salsa, son and Latin classics. Expect an evening of irresistible music, dancing and feelgood atmosphere. 8pm. Tickets £20.

Sunday, November 15: An evening with Edwina Hayes. One of the finest female singer/songwriters this country has produced in years, Edwina brings together English folk, Americana and the rich Northern singer/songwriter tradition to create a sound that’s truly her own. Highly accomplished, she’s toured with such notable performers as Jools Holland, Van Morrison, Nanci Griffith and Loudon Wainwright III. 7.30pm. Tickets £15.

Friday, November 20: The Marrakesh Express - A tribute to Crosby, Stills & Nash. The Marrakesh Express are a unique live act, recreating an iconic period in music with stunning vocal harmonies, psychedelic guitar and authentic acoustic grooves. All the classics are here. Expect big hits such as Suite Judy Blue Eyes, Our House and Helplessly Hoping, as well as songs from all three men’s original acts – The Byrds, Buffalo Springfield and the Hollies. The band take you back to a time of protest, revolution, dropping out and making great music. 8pm. Tickets £20.

Thursday, November 26: Ryedale Blues Club – Tom Davies & The Bluebirds. The current line-up of Tom Davies & The Bluebirds came together in early 2022 and is based in the southern part of Hampshire. They’ve already been to Ireland to play at the Belfast Empire and the Button Factory in Dublin, to support the campaign to build a statue to celebrate the life and work of Gary Moore. They have continued to consolidate their reputation with further trips to the Ballyshannon Rory Gallagher festival, Tenby Blues festival and the Wickham festival. 8pm. Tickets £10.

Friday, November 27: Hilarity Bites Comedy Club featuring Matt Price, Fran Best and host Kieran Lawless. Matt Price has proved to be consistently funny in comedy venues of all shapes and sizes, all over the world from Penzance to Cambodia. Fran Best is a Chortle 2026 Award Finalist and doctor by day, fast becoming a regular at comedy clubs around the country. Kieran Lawless is an Irish comedian now based in Manchester. He has worked in comedy clubs across Ireland, the UK and Europe. 8pm. Tickets £10.

Saturday, November 28: Pink Floydian – An evening of classic Pink Floyd. Celebrating 50 years of Wish You Were Here. Pink Floydian are an eight-piece tribute band dedicated to paying homage to the greatest progressive rock band of all time, Pink Floyd. Over the years, they have steadily built a reputation as one of the most authentic and captivating Pink Floyd tribute acts in the UK. Their two-and-a-half-hour show spans the full breadth of Pink Floyd’s illustrious career. 7.30pm. Tickets £25.

December

Saturday, December 19: Beatles Complete. Back by popular demand! Beatles Complete are one of the youngest artists to gain residency at the world-famous Cavern Club in Liverpool where they perform every Thursday night. The band made their debut performance in International Beatleweek 2021 at the Cavern and have been performing there regularly ever since. Max (George) and Joe (Paul) met in 2020 with George (John) and Tom (Ringo) joining them in 2021 to complete the line-up. 8pm. Tickets £25.
