North Yorkshire Police are appealing for information to help locate a missing 72-year-old man who was last seen in Skipton.

Anthony Macauley, from Ingleton, was last seen at around 2.10pm on Monday 26 October.

He is described as being around 5ft 9in tall, of medium build, with very short grey hair. He wears glasses.

At the time he was last seen, Anthony is believed to have been wearing a black raincoat, a dark-coloured quarter-zip jumper, blue trousers and trainers.

Police believe he may have travelled out of Skipton by public transport into the surrounding rural areas. They are appealing to anyone who may have seen him since 2pm on Monday, including people who were using public transport, to come forward.

Anyone who sees Anthony or knows where he is now should call 999.

Anyone with other information that could help police find him is asked to call 101 and speak to the police control room.

When sharing information, please quote reference number **12260210779**.

North Yorkshire Police have released CCTV and a photograph of Anthony as part of the appeal.
