Brazilian right-wing Senator Flavio Bolsonaro has followed his imprisoned father's lead by refusing to concede defeat to leftist President Luiz Inacio Lula da ⁠Silva, who won a historic fourth term in Sunday's presidential election.

Bolsonaro, the son of former President Jair Bolsonaro, called into question a late-Sunday decision by a Lula-appointed Supreme Court justice that extended voting by two hours over alleged voter transport irregularities. He said he would ask for the result to be annulled.

"It is impossible to recognize the election results, ⁠given the circumstances under which things unfolded," Bolsonaro told journalists.

Yet the ​scale ⁠of the loss, with Bolsonaro trailing Lula by over 4 million votes, makes it unlikely his efforts to annul the vote will succeed, analysts said. His petition is also undermined by ⁠the fact that his campaign also sought a voting extension due to heavy rains in the conservative-leaning ​south.

Jair Bolsonaro plunged Brazil into a deep democratic crisis after the election in ‌2022, when his supporters stormed government buildings in protest against ‌the result. He later received a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup.
