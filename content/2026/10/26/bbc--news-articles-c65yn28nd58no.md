![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: "Budget set to offer tax reprieve for pensioners".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/624/cpsprodpb/8d83/live/a7ad80a0-d0c0-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

As the lead up to the Budget builds, the Times says the chancellor is set to offer a "tax reprieve for pensioners". The paper says John Healey is expected to protect about one million low-income pensioners from paying income tax by raising their tax-free allowance, adding he has also decided against reducing the amount of pension that can be taken as a tax-free lump sum.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: "PM's betrayal may 'open door' to EU migrants".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/904b/live/4327d190-d0bf-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

Sticking with a domestic political theme, the Daily Express runs a large headline accusing Prime Minister Andy Burnham of "betrayal" after he refused "to rule out a return to freedom of movement between the European Union and the UK". It says the comments have been "savaged by Brexiteers".

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: "Get Brexit redone".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/cbf7/live/b39148d0-d0bf-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

The Metro also looks at Andy Burnham's "pledge to revisit freedom of movement" with the EU, noting it is sparking a "furious backlash" from opponents. It headlines with "Get Brexit redone".

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: "'Red wall' MPs wary as Burnham calls for EU free movement debate".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/040f/live/6ca55ae0-d0da-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Guardian headline says "'Red wall' MPs wary as Burnham calls for EU free movement debate" - reporting the PM risks a rift with politicians in areas which voted in 2016 to leave the EU.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: "'Nothing off table' for Burnham to undo Brexit harm".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/3e5f/live/acb74100-d0cd-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Independent runs a large photo showing a wave driven by Storm Boelo smashing into the Merseyside shore above its main story focusing on the push from the government to "reset EU ties".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: "Meghan dad 'net will widen'".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/624/cpsprodpb/db9a/live/1ebbfa10-d0c0-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Daily Mirror says an investigation into Thomas Markle over the alleged possession of child abuse images in the Philippines could possibly being broadened to the US and Mexico. It says the FBI expected to join the investigation into the 82-year-old father of the Duchess of Sussex, who denies all the allegations made against him.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: "I go to Far East as 12-year-olds are legal there".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/96b0/live/a88b2f10-d0c3-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Sun carries more claims about the letter sent to the Met Police about Thomas Markle from his first wife. Markle has denied all allegations made against him.

![The headline on the front of the Daily Mail reads: "Markle 'abuser of worst kind'".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/480/cpsprodpb/89f7/live/41377570-d0ce-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

As the investigation into Meghan's father continues, the Daily Mail carries quotes from a minister in the Philippines who said he could potentially be a "sex offender of the grossest kind".

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: "Streeting vows UK will 'lead from the front' as billions planned for defence boost".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/52c8/live/931f77c0-d0bf-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

Defence Secretary Wes Streeting's vow that the UK "will 'lead from the front" as defence is set to get a Budget "boost" is carried across the front page of the i Paper.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: "BBC's £400k payout over anti-Semitism".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/e04d/live/de200f50-d0bf-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Daily Telegraph says the BBC has agreed a £400,000 payout to a Jewish documentary maker who claimed she was the victim of anti-Semitism and bullied out of the corporation. The paper says the BBC agreed to pay her compensation – without any admission of liability - after she brought a claim for unfair dismissal. The Telegraph say an internal BBC investigation did not uphold the formal complaint. A BBC spokesman is quoted as saying: "Anti-Semitism in any form is not tolerated at the BBC... We have robust procedures in place to deal with any behaviour that is contrary to our values."

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: "Luxury houses shed €250bn in value as China's shopper tighten their belts".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/fef6/live/651b1280-d0bf-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

The Financial Times headlines on a story about a drop in sales for European luxury goods as demand from Chinese consumers eases, adding that "AI-driven stock wobbles" is also depressing demand.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: "Let's be starvin' you!".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/531d/live/ddac5b60-d0be-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

The Daily Star plays on Dela Smith's famous "Let's be havin' you" half-time rant to Norwich City fans in a piece saying she "reckons bringing back World War Two-style rations will cure the nation's addiction to sugar".
