Left-leading leaders across the Americas welcomed Brazilian President-elect Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva’s win on social media Sunday after he defeated opposition candidate Flávio Bolsonaro.

Lula is a revered figure to Latin America’s left, having governed the country between 2003-2010 when leftists were at the helm in countries across the region. Since returning to power in 2023, he has been a vocal critic of Trump.

Mexico: President Claudia Sheinbaum

Long live democracy, the people of Brazil won. Congratulations, President Lula!

Cuba: President Miguel Díaz-Canel

Dear brother Lula, on behalf of the Cuban government and people, I congratulate you for this historic victory that ensures the continuity of the path of peace, sovereignty and social justice embarked upon by the Brazilian people under your leadership.

Brazil can count on Cuba in the construction of the unity and integration of our Great Homeland, Latin American and the Caribbean.

Bolivia: President Rodrigo Paz

Bolivia and Brazil are neighboring and sister countries, united by a shared history and common challenges. We will continue working together to strengthen our relationship and generate more opportunities for our nations.

Argentina: Former President Cristina Kirchner

Congratulations (...) to you, comrade President Lula, who in your seventh presidential election knew how to awaken your compatriots' passions and their reason to win this battle.

US: Senator Bernie Sanders

Lula has stood up for Brazilian workers & democracy — and will continue to do so in the next four years.
