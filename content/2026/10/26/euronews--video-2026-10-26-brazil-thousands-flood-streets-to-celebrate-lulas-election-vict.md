Fireworks, blaring car horns and chants of “Lula, Lula” filled São Paulo's iconic **Paulista Avenue** as thousands celebrated**President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's victory** in Sunday's presidential runoff.

Supporters **waved Brazilian flags**, danced, sang and embraced one another, some overcome with emotion. The** celebrations stretched into Monday**, while similar scenes unfolded in other cities across Brazil.

The left-wing president won **51.8% of valid votes**, compared with** 48.2% for Senator Flávio Bolsonaro**, securing a** fourth term**with a margin of roughly** 4.4 million votes**, according to the** Superior Electoral Court**.

Lula's victory came after he **overturned a first-round deficit** against his right-wing challenger. Bolsonaro, however, has**refused to accept the outcome** and said he would**seek to have the result annulled**.
