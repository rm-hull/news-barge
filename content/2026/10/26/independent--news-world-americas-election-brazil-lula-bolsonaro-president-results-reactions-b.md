Brazilian right-wing Senator Flavio Bolsonaro has followed his imprisoned father's lead by refusing to concede defeat to leftist President Luiz Inacio Lula da ⁠Silva, who won a historic fourth term in Sunday's presidential election.

Bolsonaro, the son of former President Jair Bolsonaro, called into question a late-Sunday decision by a Lula-appointed Supreme Court justice that extended voting by two hours over alleged voter transport irregularities. He said he would ask for the result to be annulled.

"It is impossible to recognize the election results, ⁠given the circumstances under which things unfolded," Bolsonaro told journalists.

Yet the ​scale ⁠of the loss, with Bolsonaro trailing Lula by over 4 million votes, makes it unlikely his efforts to annul the vote will succeed, analysts said. His petition is also undermined by ⁠the fact that his campaign also sought a voting extension due to heavy rains in the conservative-leaning ​south.

Jair Bolsonaro plunged Brazil into a deep democratic crisis after the election in ‌2022, when his supporters stormed government buildings in protest against ‌the result. He later received a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup.

Meanwhile, world leaders have hailed the victory ​of Lula in Sunday's presidential election runoff.

## Here are some reactions:

**EU Commission President Ursula Von Der Leyen:**

"Democracy and multilateralism have won in Brazil. Europe's partnership with Brazil is thriving. We will remain by your side for the years to come. Together we will keep working closely for free and open trade. A rules-based global order. And strong ambitions for our climate."

**Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi:**

"I look forward to working closely with him to further deepen and expand the multifaceted India-Brazil Strategic Partnership. Together, we will continue to strengthen our cooperation across diverse sectors, advance the shared priorities of the Global South and work towards a more inclusive, equitable and sustainable world."

**South African President Cyril Ramaphosa:**

"This victory will echo beyond the shores of Brazil. It is a victory for the Global South and progressive forces throughout the world."

**French President Emmanuel Macron:**

"France and Brazil have much to build together. In the face of the great challenges of our time, let us strengthen our strategic partnership and keep the friendship between our two countries alive in the service of a more just world."

![Emmanuel Macron is among those who have congratulated Lula](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/26/13/2301645313...jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

**Ukranian President Volodymr Zelensky:**

"We thank Brazil for its unwavering support for Ukraine's sovereignty and territorial integrity. We look forward to expanding our cooperation and congratulate President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva on his remarkable election results."

**Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney:**

"The relationship between Canada and Brazil has flourished under our partnership.

"Let’s seize this opportunity to make it even stronger, together — to create major new trade opportunities, protect the environment, and build greater prosperity for both our peoples."

**Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori:**

"I congratulate President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva ... and salute the Brazilian people for this democratic day."

**Ecuadorean President Daniel Noboa:**

"Our region has common enemies, shared challenges, and much to gain when we work together."

**Chilean President Jose Antonio Kast:**

"I just spoke with the President of Brazil, Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva.

"Chile and Brazil have a lot of shared future ahead, and we will continue working on an agenda that strengthens relations between our countries in economic integration and the fight against organized crime in the region."

**Mexican PresidentClaudia Sheinbaum:**

"Long live democracy, the people of Brazil won."

**Paraguayan President Santiago Pena:**

"Paraguay and Brazil share a common agenda of integration and development. We will continue working together for the well-being of our people."

"I congratulated him on the victory and on the democratic celebration that an electoral process represents."

**Bolivian President Rodrigo Paz:**

"Bolivia and Brazil are neighboring and brother countries, united by a shared history and common challenges.

"We will continue working together to strengthen our relationship and generate more opportunities for our nations."

**Cuban President Miguel Diaz-Canel:**

**Panamanian President Jose Raul Mulino:**

"I congratulate the Brazilian people for this democratic day and especially President Lula for the victory! We will continue working together for our countries."
