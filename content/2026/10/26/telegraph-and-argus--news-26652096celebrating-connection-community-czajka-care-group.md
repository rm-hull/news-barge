For many families, finding the right care for a loved one can feel like one of life’s biggest decisions. At Czajka Care Group, care is about more than providing medical support and other assistance - it’s about creating warm, safe and welcoming places where people truly feel at home.

At Czajka Care Group, the importance of connection, companionship and the spirit of togetherness is something the team strives to create throughout the year across its three homes: Brookfield Care Home and Staveley Birkleas Nursing Home in Nab Wood, near Saltaire, and Currergate Nursing Home in Steeton, between Skipton and Keighley.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21605234.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Czajka Care Group)*

Together, a dedicated team of nurses, carers and support staff provide a wide range of services. These include day care, respite breaks and palliative care at Brookfield Care Home and Currergate Nursing Home, alongside specialist support for younger adults with complex physical disabilities at Staveley Birkleas.

Czajka Care Group’s Managing Director, Konrad Czajka, said: “At our homes, we want residents to have plenty of opportunities to socialise, try new things and enjoy meaningful experiences. That’s why we employ dedicated activities co-ordinators to organise a host of events, celebrations and outings throughout the year.”

This philosophy also extends to the way Czajka Care Group supports its staff, many of whom have built long careers within the organisation. Konrad added: “The long-term relationships our teams develop with residents, and their families are incredibly important. Our focus is always on providing compassionate, respectful and individual care that’s tailored to every resident.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21605236.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Czajka Care Group)*

“Whether supporting older people to live with dignity and independence, or helping younger adults to achieve their goals, Czajka Care Group’s approach is always centred on the individual.”

Visitors are warmly welcomed to tour the homes, meet the dedicated teams and experience the outstanding hospitality at www.clubhousenabwood.co.uk, which is also owned and run by Czajka Care Group. To arrange a visit or find out more visit www.czajka.co.uk or call 01274 599564.
