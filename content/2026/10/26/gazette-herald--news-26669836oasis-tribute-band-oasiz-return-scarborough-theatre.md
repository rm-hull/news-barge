ONE of the UK’s most authentic tribute bands is to return to a North Yorkshire town next month.

Oasiz is coming back to the Stephen Joseph Theatre (SJT) after their sell-out performance last year, performing in the Round on Saturday, November 14.

Oasiz are embarking on their third UK tour and this is a chance to see them in top form performing back in the Round. Their set list will include all the big-hitting career-spanning anthems you'd associate with a greatest hits tour of one of the world's biggest bands.

A spokesperson for the SJT said: "Oasiz are one of the UK’s most authentic tribute bands – in 2023 a short clip of frontman Steve performing as Liam Gallagher went viral on social media with over half a million views. Many fans were convinced the clip was actually Liam himself, which led to the story being covered in the national press as well as a high profile TV appearance later that year.

Oasiz can be seen at the SJT at 7.30pm on Saturday, November 14. Tickets are available from the box office on 01723 370541 and online at www.sjt.uk.com
