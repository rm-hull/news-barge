A driver has been seriously injured following a two-car crash in Pocklington.




It happened at around 7pm on Thursday, October 22 on the A1079 at the junction with The Balk and involved a green BYD Dolphin and a silver Renault Clio, according to Humberside Police.

The Renault and ended up on its roof and the driver was taken to hospital with serious injuries that are not believed to be life-threatening, a force spokesperson said.

Humberside Police is appealing to anyone with information or witnesses, or anyone with dashcam footage, to call 101, quoting log 397 of October 22.
