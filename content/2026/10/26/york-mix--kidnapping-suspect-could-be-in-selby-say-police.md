Police searching for a man wanted for kidnapping say he could be in the Selby or Goole areas.

Detectives in Leeds are appealing for the public’s help in locating Jack Towell, 30, who is wanted on suspicion of assault and neglect as well as kidnapping.

Officers have conducted “extensive enquiries” to try and locate Towell, and are now appealing for the public’s help.

He has connections to East and West Leeds, Pontefract, North Yorkshire and Humberside, namely Goole and Selby.

Anyone with information which may assist in locating Jack Towell is asked to contact Leeds Safeguarding Team by calling 101, quoting reference 13260605910.
