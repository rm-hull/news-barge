Ismail Kartal's side trail their opponents by just three points and headed into the weekend as the division's highest goalscorers, finding the net 22 times in just eight games. They went 2-0 up inside 11 minutes at home to Slavia Prague in the Champions League in midweek but failed to hold on, conceding a 90th-minute equalizer to ensure they remain winless in the league phase table. However, successive 3-0 victories domestically should give them plenty of confidence going into the derby, with top scorer Vedat Muriqi in particularly good form with seven goals in eight league matches.
