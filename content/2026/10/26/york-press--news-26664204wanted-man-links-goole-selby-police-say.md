A man wanted by police on suspicion of kidnapping, assault and neglect has connections to North Yorkshire and to Goole.

West Yorkshire Police issued an appeal to the public for information to help locate Jack Towell, 30.

North Yorkshire Police said he may also have connections to Selby.




A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Officers have conducted extensive enquiries in order to try and locate Towell and are now appealing for the public’s help.

"He has connections to East and West Leeds, Pontefract, North Yorkshire and Humberside, namely Goole.

"Anyone with information which may assist in locating Jack Towell is asked to contact Leeds Safeguarding Team by calling 101 or go online with the force quoting reference number 13260605910.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
