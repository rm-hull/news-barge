## Four killed in two drone attacks in Russia's Belgorod region

A Ukrainian drone attack overnight on the Russian border city of Belgorod ⁠killed three people and injured 13, local authorities said on Sunday, while a separate drone strike later in the ⁠day killed ​another person ⁠and wounded one other in a nearby village.

"Unfortunately, the death toll from last night's attack (in the city of Belgorod) has risen after another man who had been injured has died in hospital. This is a great tragedy for all the city's residents," Belgorod's mayor, Valentin Demidov, wrote on state-backed messaging app MAX.

The overnight attack on Belgorod damaged 30 apartment buildings, 35 cars, four houses, three administrative buildings, eight commercial premises and three social facilities, regional governor Alexander Shuvayev said.

In a separate incident on Sunday morning, one person was killed and another injured when a Ukrainian drone struck a car in the village of Voznesenovka near the town of Shebekino, local authorities said.

## Ukraine strike on industrial zone kills security guard in Russia's Lipetsk

A Ukrainian missile strike on an industrial zone in the city of Yelets in Russia's southwestern Lipetsk region killed a security guard and injured a paramedic, regional governor Igor Artamonov said on Sunday.

Artamonov ​said ⁠the security ‌guard worked at one of the industrial facilities ‌located on the ‌site. The paramedic at the medical centre ⁠was taken to hospital, where she was receiving the necessary treatment, the governor said.

The governor did not identify the facility that was hit in the strike.

Reuters ⁠could not independently ⁠verify the report. There ‌was no ​immediate comment ‌from Ukraine.

## Ukraine says Starlink speed limits lifted after a slowdown that hobbled its drones

Ukraine’s military has regained full use of Starlink satellite links for its drones and other fast-moving equipment, the defense minister said Saturday, ending speed restrictions that Ukrainian officials said had disrupted front-line operations for more than a week.

The restoration comes as U.S. envoys launched a new push to end the war. Russia agreed Wednesday to resume three-way negotiations with Washington and Kyiv next month. On Friday, Kyiv and Moscow also agreed to a localized ceasefire brokered by first lady Melania Trump around Oleshky, a war-ravaged, Russian-occupied town in Ukraine’s southern Kherson region.

The Defense Ministry’s monitoring systems in Ukraine reported stable connections on objects traveling faster than 400 kph (250 mph), Defense Minister Yevhenii Khmara told reporters.
