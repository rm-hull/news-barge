# Biker in his 60s dies in fatal Yorkshire crash as police arrest Audi driver

## A biker in his 60s has died following a crash on a busy road near a Yorkshire village.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened on the A63 Selby bypass near Barlby at around 7am this morning (Oct 26).

The force said the rider of a black motorcycle died at the scene after being involved in a collision with a red Audi saloon.

The road remains closed while police investigate.

The Audi driver, a 31-year-old man, has been arrested in connection with the investigation and is being questioned by police.

![A63 Selby bypass near Barlby](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/26/17/14/A63.png?trim=0,9,0,9&width=640&quality=65)


A statement from the force said: “The rider of the motorcycle, a local man aged in his early 60s, sadly died at the scene. His family have been informed and are being supported by officers.

“The driver of the red Audi, a 31-year-old man from York, has been arrested in connection with the investigation. He is being questioned in police custody.”
