A County Durham man has denied murdering a 51-year-old man in Peterlee.

Kenneth Fishwick is accused of murdering Dean Fishwick at a house on Thames Road shortly before midnight on Wednesday, September 23.

The 51-year-old was found with stab wounds and taken to hospital where he was pronounced dead a short time later.

Kenneth Fishwick, of no fixed address, appeared in court charged with murder at Teesside Crown Court this morning.

The 53-year-old denied the charge during a brief hearing.

Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, told Fishwick he would return to court in January for a hearing ahead of his trial.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21501427.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and CSI remain at the scene in Peterlee

Fishwick was remanded in custody until his trial on April 12, next year.

A 73-year-old woman arrested on suspicion of murder has been bailed, while a 77-year-old man arrested on suspicion of the same offence has been released under investigation.
