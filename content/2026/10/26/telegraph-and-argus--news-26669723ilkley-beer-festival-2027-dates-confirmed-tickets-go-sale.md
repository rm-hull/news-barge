ILKLEY Beer Festival will return to the King’s Hall for two days of celebration and community spirit in February next year.

The event, organised by Ilkley Round Table and supported by Womble Bond Dickinson, will take place on Friday, February 12 and Saturday, February 13, 2027.

It promises a lively mix of drinks, entertainment, and fundraising for good causes.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21630477.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ilkley Beer Festival *(Image: submitted)*

Sam Pearson, beer festival lead at Ilkley Round Table, said: "We’re delighted to be bringing Ilkley Beer Festival back for another year.

"It’s a fantastic opportunity to bring people together, enjoy some great drinks and raise money for causes that make a real difference in our community.

"Last year’s fundraising total was phenomenal, and we’re incredibly grateful to everyone who came along, volunteered or supported the event.

"We’ve kept ticket prices in line with last year, and with early bird tickets available for just £20, there’s no better time to get your tickets booked.

"Whether you’re a real ale enthusiast, prefer a gin or prosecco, or are looking for non-alcoholic options, there’s something for everyone.

"We can’t wait to welcome everyone back to King’s Hall in February."

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21630478.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ilkley Beer Festival *(Image: submitted)*

The festival offers three sessions: Friday evening, Saturday afternoon and Saturday evening.

Guests can choose the time that suits them best.

A broad selection of drinks will be available.

Mr Pearson said: "There’s something for everyone."

In addition to a wide range of beers, the festival will also feature gin, prosecco and non-alcoholic options.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21630476.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ilkley Beer Festival *(Image: submitted)*

Live entertainment and food will also be on offer, ensuring a full and enjoyable experience.

But the festival is about more than just drinks and entertainment.

In 2026, it raised £55,554.45 for local good causes.

This continued Ilkley Round Table’s commitment to supporting the community.

The festival brings together volunteers, businesses, breweries and visitors in a shared effort to make a difference.

Tickets go on sale on Sunday, November 1, 2026. Early bird tickets are priced at £20. Prices will rise in the new year.

Tickets will be available online at ilkleybeerfestival.org.uk.

Organisers are urging visitors to book early to avoid disappointment.
