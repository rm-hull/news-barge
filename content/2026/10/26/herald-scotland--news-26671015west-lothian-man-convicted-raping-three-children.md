**A man has been convicted of serious sexual offences spanning a seven-year period, including the rape of three children, in West Lothian.**

David Smith was found guilty of serious sexual offences, including rape of three males who were aged 7, 10 and 11 between 2013 and 2020.

The 41-year-old man was convicted on Monday, October 26, at the High Court in Livingston.

Officers who led the investigation said that Smith " showed a complete disregard for his victims".

**Read More:**

Detective Inspector Jennifer Priestley said: "Smith showed a complete disregard for his victims, exploiting them for his own sexual gratification.

"I want to acknowledge the strength of the victims who provided information to police in this case. Smith’s conviction is down to them. I hope this sentence brings them and their families some comfort.

“We take all reports of sexual abuse seriously, regardless of the passage of time.

“We remain committed to supporting victims of sexual crime and protecting children from harm and abuse.

“I would urge anyone who is a victim of abuse to speak to police in the knowledge you will be listened to, taken seriously and fully supported.”
