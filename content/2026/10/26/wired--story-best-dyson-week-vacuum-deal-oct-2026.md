Prime Day may be over, but there are still big sale events between now and Black Friday. Case in point: Dyson Week, where you can find several Dyson technology devices (vacuums! hair tools! massive air purifiers!) for a discounted price that you'd usually have to wait for a big sale holiday for. The sale lasts through Halloween, so you'll want to act fast.

If you're in the market for a new Dyson vacuum, one of the best deals is on Dyson's newest, most powerful cordless vacuum: the Dyson V16 Piston Animal. It usually retails for almost a thousand dollars, but it's on sale for $820 right now, and includes the Dyson Floor Dok that not only holds up your V16 vacuum (and it's five attachments), but keeps it charged, too.

You can only find the V16 Piston Animal with Floor Dok deal through Dyson's website. The V16 is on sale on Amazon for $800, but you'll miss out on the Floor Dok stand, which is well worth the extra $20. Take it from someone who tests cordless vacuums for a living—a freestanding charging stand will make your vacuum easier to store and use on the go. It doesn't require any special mounting, either. The V16 Submarine version, which includes a mop-like attachment head, is also on sale.

Looking for a Dyson on sale that's a bit cheaper vacuum than the V16? Below are two of my favorite alternatives:

Both the V8 Cyclone and the V15 Detect Absolute are also on sale. The V15 Detect is more powerful and comes with two vacuum heads, but the V8 Cyclone is great if you're on a tight budget (but still want a Dyson). Both come bundled with the car cleaning kit, which adds on three accessories: a mini soft dusting brush, a stubborn dirt brush, and a quick-release extension hose for reaching deep car corners. I haven't tested the kit (yet), but either way, these vacuums are at a great price for Dyson Week, especially with these accessories added on.
