Harrogate Railway Sports & Social Club has confirmed its annual fireworks display will not go ahead this year.

The club announced the decision on social media, apologising to families and residents who had hoped to attend.

In a statement, the club said:

> “After careful consideration, we have made the difficult decision not to hold our annual fireworks display this year.
>
> “We understand this event has become a much-loved tradition for many families and members of our community, and we sincerely apologise for any disappointment this may cause.”

The announcement did not give a specific reason for the decision.

The club also thanked residents for their support and said it looked forward to welcoming people to future events.

The statement added:

> “We would like to thank everyone for their continued support and understanding.
>
> “We look forward to welcoming you to future events at Harrogate Railway Sports & Social Club.”
