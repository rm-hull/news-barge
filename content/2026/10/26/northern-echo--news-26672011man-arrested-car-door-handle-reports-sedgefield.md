A man has been arrested, and police are tracking a second suspect after 'suspicious activity' involving car door handles was reported in County Durham.

Officers from Durham Police were called to reports of two suspects trying car door handles in Lorimer Close in Sedgefield in the early hours of this morning (October 26).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633980.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police dog who was sent to the scene *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

The dog unit attended and conducted a search of the area, with one man being arrested on suspicion of theft and taken into custody, where he remains.

A second suspect remains outstanding, and an investigation is ongoing.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "This is your reminder now that the nights are drawing in; please make sure your properties are locked up, and your vehicles are fully secured when they are unattended.
