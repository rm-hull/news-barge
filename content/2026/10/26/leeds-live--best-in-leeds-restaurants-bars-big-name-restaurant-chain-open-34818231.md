# A big name restaurant chain will open a new restaurant at The Springs in Leeds

## Work is ongoing ahead of the exciting new restaurant launching

A big name restaurant will soon open at The Springs in Leeds.

The popular retailer park, based near the M1, will see the restaurant open in the near future. Work is going on ahead of the launch of a new branch of Pizza Express.

The team from The Springs have posted about the upcoming launch on social media. They stated: “Something new is cooking at The Springs. The countdown has officially begun, Pizza Express are moving in and we can't wait.

“Stay tuned for more information because things are about to get a whole lot more dough-lightful.” As always, we’ll keep you posted on the exciting new openings coming to Leeds.
