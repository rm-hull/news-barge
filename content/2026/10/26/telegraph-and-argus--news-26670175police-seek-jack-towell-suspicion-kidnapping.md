Police have appealed for the public's help in locating a man wanted on suspicion of kidnapping, assault and neglect.

Officers are trying to trace Jack Towell, 30, and have launched a public appeal after carrying out extensive enquiries to find him.

West Yorkshire Police said Towell is wanted in connection with an investigation into allegations of kidnapping, assault and neglect.

Officers have been conducting enquiries to locate the 30-year-old but are now asking members of the public for information that could help trace him.

## Connections across West Yorkshire

Police said Towell has links to a number of areas, including East Leeds, West Leeds, Pontefract and parts of North Yorkshire, particularly Goole.

Investigators are urging anyone who may have seen him or knows of his whereabouts to come forward.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A WYP spokesperson said: "Police in Leeds are appealing for the public’s help in locating Jack Towell, 30, who is wanted on suspicion of kidnapping, assault and neglect.

"Officers have conducted extensive enquiries in order to try and locate Towell, and are now appealing for the public’s help.

"He has connections to East and West Leeds, Pontefract and North Yorkshire, namely Goole.

"Anyone with information which may assist in locating Jack Towell is asked to contact Leeds Safeguarding Team by calling 101 or go online to our Live Chat facility quoting reference number 13260605910.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111"
