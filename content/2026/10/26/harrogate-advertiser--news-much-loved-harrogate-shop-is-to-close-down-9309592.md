# 'Everything must go' as one of Harrogate's most elegant stores prepares for the end of an ere

## “The sad news is that “closing down sale - everything must go" signs have now appeared”

A much-loved Harrogate shop which has brought style and sophistication to everything it does is to launch a closing down sale tomorrow, Tuesday, October 27.

![End of an era - Circa Vintage Boutique is a unique boutique in Harrogate selling vintage clothing for women, jewellery, accessories, paintings and objet d’art.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/26/12/54/CircaVintagehopClosingDownSale.jpeg?trim=172,0,173,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


It’s been approximately 15 years since Circa Vintage Boutique first opened its doors at 24 Regent Parade in Harrogate.

A unique boutique off Skipton Road selling vintage clothing, jewellery, accessories, paintings and objet d’art, the store’s love of all things elegant and vintage has been an admired characteristic since the beginning.

But the sad news is that “closing down sale - everything must go" signs have now appeared in Circa Vintage Boutique’s stylish shop window at the corner of Westmoreland Street and Regent Parade.

The closing down sale is expected to run for a few weeks.
