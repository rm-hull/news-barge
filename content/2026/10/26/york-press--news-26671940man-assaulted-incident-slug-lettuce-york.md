POLICE have launched an investigation after the alleged assault of a man in a popular York city centre bar.

The incident at Slug and Lettuce, in Low Ousegate, happened on May 30.

North Yorkshire Police said a man was "punched in the face".




Officers have released an image of a man they wish to speak to in connection with the incident.

A force spokesperson said: "We believe the man pictured will have information that could assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC Fiaz Shahpal by calling 101, quoting reference 12260099904.
