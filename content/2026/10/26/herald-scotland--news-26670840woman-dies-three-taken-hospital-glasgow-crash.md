**A woman has died, and three others have been taken to hospital following a crash near Glasgow.**

Emergency services crews were called to Kennish Head Road in Thornliebank on Sunday evening around 7.25pm.

The woman was pronounced dead at the scene, and two women, aged 33 and 18, along with the 20-year-old driver of the black Vauxhall Astra involved in the crash were taken to hospital.

Police Scotland closed the road for nine hours, and officers leading enquiries are appealing for anyone with information to come forward.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "Road policing officers are appealing for information following a fatal crash in Thornliebank.

"Around 7.25pm on Sunday, 25, October, 2026, emergency services were called to Kennishead Road following a crash involving a black Vauxhall Astra.

"A woman was pronounced dead at the scene. Another two women aged 33 and 18 were taken to hospital for treatment, along with the 20-year-old male driver."

Police closed the road for around nine hours.

Sergeant John Houston said: "Our thoughts are with the family of the woman who has died at this difficult time.

“Our enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances, and we are appealing to anyone who saw what happened to get in touch.

“If you were driving in the area please check any dash-cam footage to see if something has been captured that could assist with our investigation.”

Anyone who can help is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 3250 of 25 October, 2026.
