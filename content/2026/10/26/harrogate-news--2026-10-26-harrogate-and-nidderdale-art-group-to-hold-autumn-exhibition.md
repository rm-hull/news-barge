The Harrogate and Nidderdale Art Group will hold its autumn exhibition in Harrogate on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November 2026.

The exhibition will take place from 10am to 5pm on both days at the United Reformed Church on West Park, Harrogate. Entry is free, and visitors are welcome to attend.

Works by local artists will be on display, with many available to buy at affordable prices.

Visitors will be able to browse a range of artwork, including framed paintings, drawings, mounted prints and greetings cards.

## A long history of local art

The Harrogate and Nidderdale Art Group was founded in 1936 by 13 artists who called themselves the Harrogate Group of Artists.

The group has since grown and developed, now boasting more than 60 members of different ages and artistic abilities.

New members are welcome, with the group holding its regular club nights on Thursdays at Harrogate Grammar School.

Further information about the group is available on its website: www.handnart.co.uk.
