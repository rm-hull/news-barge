A member of 1980s UK pop royalty has announced a return to the York stage as he marks 40 years in music.

Marti Pellow is coming to York Barbican in Prospect Street, on September 13, 2027.

The 2027 tour will see Pellow celebrate the milestone with the fans who have been part of his story.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21630254.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Marti Pellow said fans can expect solo favourites and Wet Wet Wet classics *(Image: Contributed)*

From the much-loved songs of Wet Wet Wet to his solo favourites, organisers at the venue said fans can expect a night of non-stop hits, dancing, and a party atmosphere.

When asked what fans can expect, Marti said: "The clue is in the title of the tour, it’s what the fans always sing back to me when the lights go down."

Opening at Nottingham’s Royal Concert Hall on August 28, 2027, the tour takes in dates across England, Wales, and Scotland before culminating in a hometown celebration at Glasgow’s OVO Hydro on October 2.
