**Storm Boelo has forced the historic Mackintosh Tea Rooms in Glasgow to close.**

Charles Rennie Mackintosh and his wife designed the Willow Tearooms, renamed the Mackintosh Tearooms in February this year, and opened them in 1903.

In a notice posted to its front door on Monday, the team said that due to issues following the recent "severe weather", it had been forced to close and hoped to reopen "as soon as possible".

The notice read: "Closed today. We apologise for the inconvenience.

"Due to the recent severe weather, our water supply has been interrupted, and we are unable to open today.

"We are working with the relevant teams to resolve the issue as soon as possible.

"Thank you for understanding."

The Herald reported last month that a Glasgow artist took an 'unexploded vape' to the streets in protest over a vape shop being opened next to the Tea Rooms.

The reported last week that a new visitor centre is planned for Charles Rennie Mackintosh’s Hill House in Helensburgh as part of a £20 million project to conserve the architectural landmark and celebrate the work of Mackintosh and his wife, Margaret Macdonald Mackintosh.

The **National Trust for Scotland** (NTS) has announced plans for the centre through its Mackintosh Illuminated project, which combines conservation work with new visitor experiences, research, learning opportunities and community engagement.
