A crash has been reported on a main road in North Yorkshire which is affecting traffic in the area.




AA Traffic News said it happened on the A63 Hull Road, northbound in Barlby, outside Selby this morning (Monday, October 26).

Emergency services were reported close to the Wishing Well roundabout on the A19 earlier this morning, at the junction with Bawtry Road.

Traffic is said to be coping well, however, there are also roadworks in Station Road where traffic is taking turns to pass which are also causing holdups westbound on Barlby Road.

Delays of 11 minutes and increasing were reported southbound in The Crescent close to Selby town centre.
