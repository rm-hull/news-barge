Police are appealing for witnesses after a two-vehicle crash on a major road in North Yorkshire.

The county's police force said it happened around 5pm on Wednesday, October 21 on the A170 near Nawton involving a grey Toyota Hilux towing a trailer, and a red Fiat 500.

No serious injuries were reported, police said.




Officers from North Yorkshire Police are asking for anyone who saw the collision, witnessed the vehicles beforehand, or has relevant dashcam footage to come forward.

Information can be provided by emailing [lewis.grainge@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:lewis.grainge@northyorkshire.police.uk), or by visiting the North Yorkshire Police website.

Alternatively, calls can be made to police via 101.

Please quote reference 12260207330 when providing information.
