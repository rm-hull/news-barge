The British capital is turning almost a kilometre of **Oxford Street** into a**pedestrian-only zone**,** banning cars, buses, taxis, bicycles and e-scooters**between** Orchard Street**and** Great Portland Street**.

Shoppers took to the traffic-free road as **buses were diverted**. Mayor** Sadiq Khan**visited retailers to promote the changes, intended to make the street** safer and more welcoming** ahead of Christmas.

**Black cab drivers**, however, warn that the restrictions could make access** harder for disabled passengers**.

**Paris** pedestrianised much of**Rue de Rivoli in 2020**, attracting more than** 20,000 cyclists daily**and** reducing nitrogen dioxide levels**, although critics say** congestion has shifted** to nearby roads.

**Brussels** closed 800 metres of**Boulevard Anspach** to traffic in 2015. Some retailers initially reported**sales falling by up to 20%**, but** 85% later supported the scheme** despite criticism of its implementation.

**Oxford Street** now faces a familiar challenge:**attracting more pedestrians** while**keeping shops accessible** and**surrounding traffic moving**.
