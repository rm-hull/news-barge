Amanda Owen, the Yorkshire Shepherdess, is set to visit York for a special evening of stories, questions, and a book signing.

The bestselling author and TV personality will appear at Waterstones in York on Tuesday, October 27, offering fans a chance to engage with her and hear firsthand tales.

The event will kick off with a welcome reception at 6.30pm, where attendees can enjoy complimentary refreshments.

Amanda's talk is scheduled to begin at 7pm, with guests encouraged to be seated by 6.55pm.

Following her talk, there will be a question and answer session, allowing fans to interact directly with the shepherdess.

The evening will conclude with a book signing.

Other titles by Amanda will be available for purchase.

Amanda, a mother of nine, shot to fame with the TV series Our Yorkshire Farm, which attracted over three million viewers in the UK and aired in more than 20 countries.

Her latest series, Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, became the highest-rated show of the year on More4.

Aside from her TV career, Amanda is a bestselling author, with five of her books making the Sunday Times bestsellers list.

Her genuine portrayal of life in the countryside has won her over 800,000 followers on social media.

Christmas Tales from the Farm, Amanda's first book for children, offers a heart-warming collection of real-life stories from her farm, featuring both two-legged and four-legged characters.

In the book, readers can follow the adventures of a lost sheep, special chickens in need of a home, and a runaway reindeer causing mischief on the moors.

The event organisers have issued a late entry notice, requesting attendees to arrive promptly to ensure everyone's enjoyment.

A bookseller will be at the door until 7.10pm, but entry after this point will be at the discretion of Waterstones staff.

Tickets for the event are available for £7 for general admission, and £18 for a book and ticket bundle, which includes a hardback copy of Cosy Bedtime Stories.

For those requiring additional support, the organisers ask to be contacted in advance to ensure a comfortable experience for all attendees.
