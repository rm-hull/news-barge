Bradford’s top 10 curry houses have been named, opening voting for the Telegraph & Argus' latest competition.

Readers can now vote for their favourite finalist, with voting slips appearing in the newspaper until Saturday, November 7. Each voting slip will count as one vote.

The curry house that receives the most votes will be crowned Bradford’s Best Curry House 2026. The winner will also receive a special feature in the Telegraph & Argus.

The newspaper will begin profiling each nominee from tomorrow, October 27, giving readers more information about the businesses competing for the title.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630566.jpg?type=mds-article-620)T&A Best Curry House 2026 *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 10 best curry houses in Bradford

* Akbar’s Restaurant, 1272-1280 Leeds Road, BD3 8LF
* Future Balti, 22 Holroyd Hill, Wibsey, BD6 1PQ
* International Restaurant, 40-42 Manville Terrace, Morley Street, BD7 1BA
* Karachi Restaurant, 15 Neal Street, BD5 0BX
* Kashmiri Aroma, 6 Northgate, Baildon, BD17 6JX
* Kiplings Indian Restaurant, 836 Harrogate Road, Apperley Bridge, BD10 0RA
* Maya’s, 311 Tong Street, BD4 9QS
* My Peshawar, 30 Easby Road, BD7 1QX
* Shimlas, 121-135 Great Horton Road, BD7 1PS
* Silver Jubilee Restaurant, 115-117 Oak Lane, BD9 4QU

## International and Shimlas aiming to win again

The competition has been running for two years, during which the T&A received thousands of votes.

International Restaurant, on Morley Street, won the title in 2024. Shimlas, on Great Horton Road, took the award last year.

Nasir Uddin, regional manager at Shimlas Trade Ltd, said: “We are honoured to receive the Best Curry House 2025 award.”

![The team at Shimlas on Great Horton Road - the winners of the T&A's Best Curryhouse 2025 competition](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20238555.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The team at Shimlas on Great Horton Road - the winners of the T&A's Best Curryhouse 2025 competition *(Image: Newsquest)*

He said: “We firmly believe that such progress is only possible when customers are placed before profits.”

Mr Uddin also thanked customers and the community for supporting Shimlas and voting for the restaurant.

The 2024 winner, Safeer Khan, who runs International, said: “Wow! That's all I can say.”

He said: “It's an amazing feeling to have won best curry house in Bradford.”

Mr Khan added: “It's run by the T&A which is the most relevant news outlet in the region.”
