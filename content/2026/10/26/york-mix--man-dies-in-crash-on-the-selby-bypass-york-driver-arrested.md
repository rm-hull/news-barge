A man died in a crash on the Selby bypass in this morning’s rush hour (Monday)

It happened on the A63 near Barlby at around 7am.

A red Audi saloon car and a black motorcycle collided.

#### Crash location

The rider of the motorbike, a local man aged in his early 60s, died at the scene.

His family have been informed and are being supported by officers.

The driver of the red Audi, a 31-year-old man from York, has been arrested in connection with the investigation.

He is being questioned in police custody.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Diversions remain in place while officers investigate the collision scene and to allow the vehicles to be safely recovered.”

Officers want to hear from anyone who saw what happened, saw either vehicle prior to the crash or have dashcam footage.
