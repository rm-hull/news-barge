A two-bedroom Bradford home is going to auction with a guide price of £25,000-plus.

The property at 21 Tennis Avenue, off Tong Street, will be sold by BTG Eddisons on October 28.

The listing is an end-town house in a cul-de-sac and stands on a corner plot.

## Requiring 'modernisation'

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630441.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Garden *(Image: BTG eddisons)*

BTG Eddisons describes the property as requiring 'modernisation' throughout and is being offered to investors, developers and buyers planning renovation work.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630439.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bedroom within the property *(Image: BTG eddisons)*

The house offers a lounge, kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom.

Gardens extend around the front, side and rear of the home.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630437.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bedroom within the property *(Image: BTG eddisons)*

Auctioneers say the sizeable plot may allow an extension, subject to planning permission.

They also identify the property as a possible family residence after refurbishment or a buy-to-let investment.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630436.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The kitchen within the property *(Image: BTG eddisons)*

## Nearby shops and amenities

The property is located close to a range of amenities and retail outlets, including a nearby Starbucks, while Weaverthorpe Retail Park offers shopping options such as Iceland and Poundstretcher.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630435.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Living room within the property *(Image: BTG eddisons)*

Asda Superstore on Rooley Lane and Morrisons Daily on Broadstone Way are also within easy reach.

The sale will be held as a live-streamed auction from Old Billingsgate in London.

It is an unconditional auction, meaning contracts will be exchanged immediately after the hammer falls.

The winning bidder must pay a minimum payment of £5,000.
