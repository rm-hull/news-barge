A major Bradford road is shut as workers repair railing that has been damaged on a bridge above.

It comes following the effects of Storm Boelo on the district.

New Otley Road appears to have been closed while highway maintenance workers carry out repairs on the pedestrian bridge.

Sections of the railing appear to have been removed or damaged.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630948.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Damaged railings on the pedestrian bridge over New Otley Road *(Image: T&A)*

## Repair work underway

Images from the scene show workers in high-visibility clothing installing replacement metal fencing panels on the bridge.

A highway maintenance vehicle was also parked beneath the structure while crews carried out the work.

## Storm damage suspected

The repair work comes as Bradford continues to deal with the aftermath of Storm Boelo.

Storm Boelo brought strong winds and disruption across the district over the weekend.

A number of fallen trees have already been reported in locations including Bowling Cemetery, Morton Cemetery, Dick Lane and Great Horton Road.

It is not yet clear what caused the damage to the bridge railing.

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted Bradford Council for further information, including whether the damage was caused by severe weather and how long the bridge is expected to remain closed.
