A well-regarded country inn in North Yorkshire is up for sale.

The freehold property, which serves as a gateway to the North Yorkshire Moors, boasts more than an acre of land.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21630663.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of the Cayley Arms at Allerston *(Image: Davey & Co)*

* **Popular Dacre Arms pub in Brandesburton for sale at £735,000**
* **Kirk Deighton's Horse and Hound pub is for sale at £550,000**

The Cayley Arms at Allerton, between Pickering and Scarborough, is being market by Davey & Co.

The property features a restaurant with seating for over 60, a lounge bar, and five premium letting rooms, with the potential for five more.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21630662.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of the Cayley Arms at Allerston. *(Image: Davey & Co)*

The 4-star inn, located on a prominent site on the A170, is being sold for around £950,000.

The inn also offers three-plus rooms of private accommodation and has potential for further development in its grounds, the agents added.

Highly-regarded, TripAdvisor awards the Cayley Arms 4.6 stars out of five, based on 149 reviews, ranking it ninth out of 37 B&B/inns in Pickering.

Google offers a similar 4.6 stars out of five, based on 489 Google reviews.
