# Distinguished Harrogate shop links up with Italian jewellers and iconic Bettys tearooms

## “In collaboration with Italian jewellery brand Fope, renowned fine jewellers Bradleys hosted a very special afternoon tea at Bettys Café Harrogate”

One of Harrogate’s most distinguished shops has linked up with an Italian jewellers for a special afternoon of live music at the town’s most iconic tearooms.

![Renowned fine jewellers Bradleys hosted a very special afternoon tea at Bettys Café Harrogate with an intimate private performance from classical singer Carly Paoli.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/26/16/00/BradleyJewellerOperaBettysLineUp.jpeg?trim=16,0,385,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


In collaboration with Italian jewellery brand Fope, renowned fine jewellers Bradleys hosted a very special afternoon tea at Bettys Café Harrogate with an intimate private performance from classical singer Carly Paoli.

Held in the elegant Imperial Suite at Betty’s Café Tearooms in Harrogate, guests were joined by Harrogate Mayor, Councillor Viv Poskitt and enjoyed afternoon tea whilst listening to Carly perform some of her favourite songs.

“We were delighted to host Carly in Harrogate and to showcase the stunning collection of Fope jewellery pieces,” said Peter Bradley Jr, Sales Director at Bradleys.

“Thank you to Mayor Viv Poskitt for joining us and to all our guests for making it such a memorable event.”

Bradleys The Jewellers was founded in 1970 by Peter Bradley Snr. and Janet Bradley.

The flagship boutique of this family business is located at 6 Prospect Crescent in Harrogate.

A classically trained soprano, Carly Paoli sings an extensive repertoire of opera, classical and classical-crossover pieces.

Guests had the opportunity to view the latest Fope jewellery, including exclusive 18ct gold collections.

Fope, an Italian brand created in the 1920’s is known globally for its innovative Flex’it technology and one of the brands represented by Bradleys in their stores across North Yorkshire.
