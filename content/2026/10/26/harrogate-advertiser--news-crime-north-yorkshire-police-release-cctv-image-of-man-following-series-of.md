# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of man following series of shop thefts in Harrogate area

## If you recognise this person, you should get in contact with the police

North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a number of thefts at shops in the Harrogate area.

![The police have issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a number of thefts at Coop stores on Skipton Road and Grantley Drive, and Sainsbury’s on Kings Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/26/9/21/862752678_1109104054947400_2170622862808419805_n-png.jpeg?trim=12,0,204,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Between 7 September and 10 October, items have been stolen on seven occasions from Coop stores on Skipton Road and Grantley Drive, and Sainsbury’s on Kings Road.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We believe the man in the image will have information that could help our investigation.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org](https://crimestoppers-uk.org)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260201256 when providing any details regarding the incident.
