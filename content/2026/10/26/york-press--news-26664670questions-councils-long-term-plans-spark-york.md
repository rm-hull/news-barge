**Questions over council's long-term plans for Spark:York**

LETTER writer E Hume asks if the council will keep its word over Spark and provide a new site?

The question should be will the City of York Council keep its word and bring this “temporary” abomination to an end?

The council is under no obligation to keep on indulging a favoured enterprise.

When that totally inappropriate development was first approved it was “justified” because it would be gone after three years but the council reneged on this and granted another two years, then another three, then another five.

Moreover, the council cannot provide a new site for Spark at York Central because the council does not own that land. Only the freeholders and developers are able to do that and their plans do not contain any provision for Spark.

The feasibility study produced by Spark’s architects was on a part of York Central which is allocated as landscaped open space on the approved scheme.
