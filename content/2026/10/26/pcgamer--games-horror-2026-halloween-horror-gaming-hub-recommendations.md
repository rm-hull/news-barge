### Staff picks

A selection of lesser-known horror games to check out this Halloween.

![Close up of priest character in Labyrinth of the Demon King and his angry face mask.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/zouHqc9pjxu47qNKVzJKwe-1200-80.jpg)

### Labyrinth of the Demon King

(**Steam**) Gruesome, gnarly, first-person dungeon crawling amid the worst castle in all of Feudal Japan⁠—come for the rancid vibes, stay for the shockingly high melee combat skill ceiling. Our 2025 horror game of the year has only improved since I fell in love with it, adding new weapons and an NG+ mode. —* Ted Litchfield*

![Super Lone Survivor screenshot](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/v6g5cAyyXEWnYH9ytgmpzN-1200-80.jpg)

### Super Lone Survivor

(**Steam**) An unsettling and underplayed indie horror cult classic with shades of Silent Hill: The Room and David Lynch. This 2022 edition adds new features to the 2012 original; I only played and reviewed the original, but the Super version doesn't look like anything but an improvement. —* Tyler Wilde*

![GTFO](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ZTJ3W6mK2VnpiyfnuGRk5C-1200-80.jpg)

### GTFO

(**Steam**) Tense co-op Dead Space where every gun thunders like a cannon and attracts as much attention from the freaks roaming an oppressive space prison. By far the most nerve-racking multiplayer FPS of this type I've played—a nice change if you've played so much Left 4 Dead or Darktide that you can chatter your way through them without actually fearing for your life. —* Wes Fenlon*

![Lure screenshot](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/F5Ff7fqA9fEww2YaMm8FvS-1200-80.jpg)

### Lure

(**Steam**) Horror fishing games are everywhere these days but Lure stands out because it's good, it's free, it'll take you less than five minutes to play it—and I still guarantee it'll scare you. It'll make you laugh, too! All that for the low cost of completely free? I don't think you can say no. —* Christopher Livingston*

### What to watch

Scary, gory, or creepy movies we think you'll like.

![The Exorcist 3 still](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/DXHFWyt72NiG6siqhwry83-1200-80.jpg)

### The Exorcist 3

(**YouTube, Amazon**) I recently learned this sequel, directed by Exorcist novelist William Peter Blatty, was a bit of a cult fave, and it lives up to the reputation. All-time crazy guy actor Brad Dourif (Chucky, Gríma Wormtongue) delivers incredible serial killer monologues while Blatty's haunted Catholic imagery and long takes punctuated by bursts of violence gives the impression that this guy actually met Satan one time and is still pretty messed up by it. —* Wes Fenlon*

![Noroi: The Curse still](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/39CyncufoJ2YNv5s5NP5ZK-1200-80.jpg)

### Noroi: The Curse

(**YouTube, Amazon**) Before directing more mainstream hits like Sadako vs. Kayako, Koji Shiraishi delivered some of my favorite Japanese horror films, including Carved: The Slit-Mouthed Woman and found footage thriller Noroi: The Curse. You can't go wrong with any of them, but Noroi's uncomfortable home video realism and documentary-gone-wrong premise put it right up there with The Blair Witch Project among found footage horror's greats. —* Andrea Shearon*

![Aniara still](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qvkJbey8bkUQ9sbcusCoW5-1200-80.jpg)

### Aniara

(**Amazon**) A spaceship is knocked off course, sending its passengers inexorably toward the depths of space. A spectacularly gloomy Swedish-Danish production; meandering, but visually captivating. —* Tyler Wilde*

![A still from the 1999 film eXistenZ.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/e2KRjLn8RM7Dsmvdx4Voie-1200-80.png)

![Exit 8 movie still](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/2NueUbFSLmFMzKox77AJXe-1200-80.jpg)

### Exit 8

(**AMC+, Amazon**) Resident Evil and Iron Lung got most of the press this year when it comes to horror game adaptations, but don't sleep on Exit 8 (based on indie game The Exit 8). Trapped in a repeating series of subway tunnels, a young man has to figure out the rules so he can reach the only exit. It's tense and spooky, and technically very impressive given that it's shot in a long series of unbroken takes. Most of all, it perfectly captures the feel of playing a horror game. —* Christopher Livingston*
