Called by an **anti-racism coalition**, trade unions and local groups, the demonstration saw protesters carrying placards condemning** racial hatred**.

The rally came three days after a **gunman opened fire** on passers-by in**Trappes and neighbouring La Verrière**, south-west of Paris,** killing 26-year-old amateur MMA fighter Pengo Bossio Wangi**and** injuring five others**.

Among the wounded was a **12-year-old girl** who was shot while walking to a swimming pool and remained in**critical condition**.

Prosecutors are investigating the shootings as a **suspected racist terrorist attack**. The 39-year-old** suspect was arrested** shortly afterwards in Coignières.
