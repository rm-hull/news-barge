*(Image: T&A)*

Storm Boelo has caused havoc across Bradford with several trees coming down.

Here's a selection of photographs revealing the extent of the damage left behind by the weekend's strong winds.

T&A images show trees and branches brought down at Bowling Cemetery, Morton Cemetery and on Dick Lane and Great Horton Road.

## Tree blocks roadway at Bowling Cemetery

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630125.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A fallen tree was among a number of storm-related incidents reported across the Bradford district over the weekend. *(Image: T&A)*

Among the most striking incidents is a large fallen tree at Bowling Cemetery, where debris has been left strewn across a roadway running through the site.

Photographs show substantial branches covering much of the tarmac, forcing vehicles to stop while the obstruction remained in place.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21629667.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Storm damage was also reported at Morton Cemetery, where fallen branches and debris were left scattered across the site. *(Image: Vivienne Hills)*

## Storm damage captured at Morton Cemetery

Further photographs from Morton Cemetery show additional evidence of the severe weather that swept through the region.

Large branches and sections of tree can be seen scattered across the cemetery grounds, with debris spread across paths and grassy areas.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630266.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The splintered trunk shows the force of the winds brought by Storm Boelo. *(Image: T&A)*

## Tree snapped on Dick Lane

Meanwhile, residents have reported a tree being brought down on Dick Lane near Copeland Street.

Photographs from the scene show the tree snapped near its base, with a large section collapsing onto a grass verge and partially blocking the adjacent footpath.

The exposed timber and splintered trunk illustrate the force of the winds that battered the district over the weekend.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21630346.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exposed trunk of a storm-damaged tree on Great Horton Road. *(Image: T&A)*

## Great Horton Road tree brought down by strong winds

Further evidence of the storm's impact could also be seen on Great Horton Road, where a large tree was damaged during the severe weather.

The fallen tree previously caused disruption in the area, although the road has since been cleared and made safe.

## Storm aftermath continues

The latest incidents come as communities across Bradford continue to deal with the effects of Storm Boelo.

Strong winds have been linked to fallen trees, damaged branches and power outages across a number of areas, while clean-up efforts remain ongoing.

The T&A has contacted the relevant authorities for further information about the incidents and the wider response to storm damage across the district.
