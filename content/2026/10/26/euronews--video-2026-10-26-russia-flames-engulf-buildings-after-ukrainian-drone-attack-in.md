Videos posted on Russian social media showed **flames rising from apartment blocks** and from the 25-storey**Galereya Chizhova business centre**, with thick black smoke drifting over neighbouring districts. Other footage showed burning private homes, a damaged commercial building, residential blocks with blown-out windows and** at least ten cars ablaze**in a car park. Residents picked through the wreckage, where** drone engine fragments** were found.

**Alexander Gusev**, the governor, said air defences** brought down 113 drones**over the region, more than 70 of them directly above the city. Russia's defence ministry** claimed 290 across the country**.

Debris also started fires at **several industrial plants**— local reports named the** Voronezhsintezkauchuk rubber works**and the** VASO aircraft factory**— as well as two dacha settlements, a school sports ground, a garage cooperative and an apartment building. The** Novovoronezh nuclear plant**said it was** operating normally**.

Most of the injured were aboard the **Anapa–Moscow train** when debris struck near**Somovo station**, Russian Railways said.** Five passengers were hurt**, two seriously, and** rail traffic through the city was suspended**. Gusev confirmed** three people were killed**.

**Kyiv has not commented**. Independent monitors could not establish whether the explosions beside apartment blocks were** direct hits or falling debris**, a distinction the governor's account leaves open. A strike on Voronezh three days earlier hit the** Baltimor military airfield**.
