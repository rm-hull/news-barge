Two vehicles have crashed in a North Yorkshire village.

Police, firefighters and paramedics were called to the crash in Burn Bridge, south of Harrogate, at about 9.50am on Monday (October 26).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said no one was trapped after the collision in Burn Bridge Lane.

“Crews checked for hotspots and there were no concerns,” a service spokesperson said.

They said the incident was left with police and the ambulance service.
