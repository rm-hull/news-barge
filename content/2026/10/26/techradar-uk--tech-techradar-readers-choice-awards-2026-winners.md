It's been an excellent year for tech releases, with the arrival of everything from new TV panel developments, to groundbreaking drones, to awesome spatial audio speakers, to a new breed of AI-connected screenless fitness wearable.

It's also been a really difficult year in tech, though, due to the component crisis and rising prices across nearly all products. In the middle of that tension, it only makes sense for us to find out which companies navigated the challenges best by asking the community of tech lovers out there — and so here are the first TechRadar Readers' Choice Awards.

The results are in, so below we've listed the TechRadar Readers' Choice Awards 2026 winners winners in every category, all 100% voted by you!

### Phones & tablets

## Phone of the Year

![Apple iPhone 18 Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MMTnuPMtkkmg2vJGFngUdR-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MMTnuPMtkkmg2vJGFngUdR-200-80.jpg.webp) "Apple iPhone 18 Pro")

**Apple iPhone 18 Pro**

It’s no great surprise that readers chose the iPhone 18 Pro as the Phone of the Year. After all, this — along with the iPhone 18 Pro Max — scored five stars in our review, which is a rating we hardly ever give out to phones. But with its excellent camera system — complete with genuinely useful upgrades like a variable aperture and pro controls — plus an exceptionally fast chipset, great battery life, beautiful new Burgundy and Glacier colors, and a superb screen and software, the iPhone 18 Pro excels in just about every area. So its five stars, and Readers' Phone of the Year award, are well deserved.

## Phone Brand of the Year

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qi9H3WJNRojGQmb7J5wfTW-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qi9H3WJNRojGQmb7J5wfTW-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Apple**

Given that the iPhone 18 Pro won Phone of the Year and that Apple’s handsets are well represented among the other Readers’ Choice award winners below, it’s hard to see how any other company could have won Phone Brand of the Year. Apple might not release as many different models as some brands, but it still has a phone for almost everyone — whether you want a high-end flagship, a top-tier gaming phone, a DSLR alternative, something more affordable, or even, this year, a foldable phone (though the Duo was released too late to be eligible for these awards). And not only has Apple covered most of the bases here, but all of its handsets excel and e with genuine upgrades compared to last year’s models.

## Phones Innovation of the Year

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/A9n4D9qoFM5NC7ntdB4UkZ-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/A9n4D9qoFM5NC7ntdB4UkZ-200-80.jpg.webp) "Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8")

**Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8**

Given that the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 was the first passport-shaped foldable to arrive on the scene in Western markets this year, it's easy to see why readers voted it as the Phones Innovation of the Year. This new form factor makes it even more pocketable than a book-style foldable, but still gives you a large screen when it’s unfolded. The smaller cover screen also makes it perfect for one-handed use when you don’t need the large inner display, and coupled with flagship performance and high–quality displays, this is a great all-round phone — not just an innovative one.

## Best Value Phone

![Apple iPhone 17e]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/HonEYmzMj4ppV277Nm28sZ-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/HonEYmzMj4ppV277Nm28sZ-200-80.jpg.webp) "Apple iPhone 17e")

**Apple iPhone 17e**

The iPhone 17e might not be the cheapest phone, but its lower price makes it highly affordable by iPhone standards, and it comes with double the storage of the iPhone 16e without upping the starting price. Plus, with features like MagSafe and an A19 chipset, you’re getting a lot for your money, so it’s no wonder this was voted Best Value Phone. iPhones tend to get updated for many years and hold their value well too, so this isn’t just good value on day one — it should also serve you well for a long time.

## Best Mid-Range Phone

![Apple iPhone 17]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/RhLKcdkFCgjh5ANa4qoJe3-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/RhLKcdkFCgjh5ANa4qoJe3-200-80.jpg.webp) "Apple iPhone 17")

**Apple iPhone 17**

There’s no base iPhone 18 yet, and it’s a testament to just how good the iPhone 17 still is that readers have voted it the Best Mid-Range Phone. Everything that was good about the iPhone 17 at launch still remains so in 2026 — including its powerful A19 chipset, always-on display with ProMotion, and improved cameras. The iPhone 17’s price hasn’t dropped since launch (in fact, thanks to the ongoing RAM crisis, it's actually gone up), but you’re still getting a lot for your money compared to many other mid-range phones.

## Best Folding Phone

![Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8jmXHjBxHxmYpeve3v3Xfc-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8jmXHjBxHxmYpeve3v3Xfc-200-80.jpg.webp) "Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra")

**Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra**

While the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 is the Readers’ Choice Phone Innovation of the Year, it’s the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra that readers think is the overall Best Folding Phone. This isn’t surprising either, as while the form factor here is nothing new, this is Samsung’s most polished, premium, and feature-packed foldable yet — so much so that it got ‘Ultra’ added to the name. For the first time, the foldable screen is essentially flat, with a near-invisible crease, and the Z Fold 8 Ultra also has a bigger battery, more power, and improved cameras compared to its predecessor, while still being slimmer, making for a phone that lives up to its ‘Ultra’ name.

## Best Flip Phone

![Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ji2H78TX7975PTusYucM2g-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ji2H78TX7975PTusYucM2g-200-80.jpg.webp) "Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8")

**Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8**

Samsung is clearly still the king of foldables for now in readers’ eyes, since they’ve also voted the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 8 as the Best Flip Phone. Arguably, this phone didn’t have much competition outside of the Motorola Razr line, but that doesn’t take away from its achievements, including new Horizontal Lock and Fan Cam features, which make it a major upgrade for recording videos. Coupled with a slightly slimmer design than last year, improved software, and a chipset upgrade, it’s no wonder the Z Flip 8 has flipped its way into readers’ hearts.

## Best Camera Phone

![Apple iPhone 18 Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/5YZkC8aeR6u5HBVoMMxQcR-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/5YZkC8aeR6u5HBVoMMxQcR-200-80.jpg.webp) "Apple iPhone 18 Pro")

**Apple iPhone 18 Pro**

The iPhone 18 Pro — along with the iPhone 18 Pro Max — have some serious camera upgrades, so it makes sense that readers would have voted this the Best Camera Phone. You’re getting a trio of 48MP cameras on the back, covering wide, ultra-wide, and telephoto (4x zoom) views, and in a rare move for phones, the wide snapper also has a variable aperture. That feature, coupled with new pro controls, gives you more power than ever to take control of your shots. But the iPhone 18 Pro still shines for point-and-shoot photography, too, and its cameras capture more detail than ever before.

## Best Gaming Phone

![Apple iPhone 18 Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/EQhwNTyv2mFrvKGV888vXR-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/EQhwNTyv2mFrvKGV888vXR-200-80.jpg.webp) "Apple iPhone 18 Pro")

**Apple iPhone 18 Pro**

The iPhone 18 Pro also takes the Readers’ Choice Best Gaming Phone award, and this makes total sense too, given the sheer amount of power on offer here. The phone has a seriously fast A20 Pro chipset paired with improved thermal management — so it can keep cool and maintain its smooth performance even over a lengthy gaming session. On top of that, the App Store simply has a better selection of high-end games than you’ll find over on Android, and they’re usually well optimized for Apple’s hardware.

## Best AI Phone

![Google Pixel 11 Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/LQEtSseEJELajRJMnuo2oi-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/LQEtSseEJELajRJMnuo2oi-200-80.jpg.webp) "Google Pixel 11 Pro")

**Google Pixel 11 Pro**

Between them, Samsung and Apple have swept almost all of these awards — but not quite all of them, as readers voted the Google Pixel 11 Pro as the Best AI Phone. Despite Siri’s recent AI makeover, this makes perfect sense, as Google has spent the last few years improving the Pixel’s AI features and more deeply integrating them into the operating system. The result with the Pixel 11 Pro is a phone with AI everywhere. You can tap into Gemini from any screen, and new features such as Rambler for voice dictation and Magic Capture for photography bring genuinely useful AI-powered upgrades to this phone's core functionality.

### Cameras & drones

## Camera of the Year

![Sony A7R VI]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/fu3ViSBeXY8uXFCgJ6V26X-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/fu3ViSBeXY8uXFCgJ6V26X-200-80.jpg.webp) "Sony A7R VI")

**Sony A7R VI**

From a shortlist that included the Canon EOS R6 Mark III, Sony A7 V, Leica M-EV1 and a trio of five-star DJI products, it was the A7R VI that topped your votes, and it’s easy to see why: Sony gave us highly-detailed images and blazing-fast performance in a single, well-designed model, rounding the package off by debuting its first new battery in almost a decade for class-leading endurance. We rarely use the word ‘perfect’, but for many photographers, the Sony A7R VI mirrorless camera will be just that.

## Camera Brand of the Year

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/t4hEKfEexQqX4h6NQ6U57-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/t4hEKfEexQqX4h6NQ6U57-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Sony**

Readers voted the A7R VI as a double winner, the A7 V as the Best Mid-range Camera, the RX10 V as the Best Compact Camera and the 100mm F2.8 macro as the Best Lens winner — so it’s little wonder that the camera giant wins the Brand of the Year vote too. And the accolade is fully deserved; other leading brands have had a majorly quiet year by comparison, besides perhaps the relentless DJI. Sony’s mirrorless camera lineup is looking strong, and its lens range is formidable. If you can afford its admittedly pricey gear, it’s the ecosystem that has it all.

## Camera Innovation of the Year

![Antigravity A1]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ezT7C3e2Rb7fArJRUNDYPS-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ezT7C3e2Rb7fArJRUNDYPS-200-80.jpg.webp) "Antigravity A1")

**Antigravity A1**

Antigravity was a complete unknown before this year, but the Insta360-seeded company burst onto the scene with the world's first 360-degree drone, the A1. Pipping DJI to the post, the Antigravity A1 is effectively an Insta360 X5 that flies, which means it shoots crisp 8K video of every angle, and when paired with the company’s goggles and motion controller, gives a flight experience that’s both immersive and intuitive. The possibilities for this tech are truly exciting.

## Best Beginner Camera

![Fujifilm X-T30 III]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/wZuqKtaJBtyBQQGj3vXhU7-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/wZuqKtaJBtyBQQGj3vXhU7-200-80.jpg.webp) "Fujifilm X-T30 III")

**Fujifilm X-T30 III**

Love Fujifilm’s retro design but can’t stretch to the pricey X100 VI or X-E5? In steps the portable Fujifilm X-T30 III, fitted with the company’s entry-level 26MP APS-C sensor for sharp stills, 6K video with 10-bit color depth, a tilt screen, EVF and a flash. It’s an excellent entry point to the Fujifilm X-mount for photography-focused users, with very few rivals at its affordable price point, besides the recent OM PEN. The X-T30 III is set to become one of Fujifilm's best sellers.

## Best Mid-range camera

![Sony A7 V]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/66vuR2v7zSvEYkXQRfYM9g-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/66vuR2v7zSvEYkXQRfYM9g-200-80.jpg.webp) "Sony A7 V")

**Sony A7 V**

The Sony A7 IV held onto top spot in our best mirrorless cameras guide for years and had only recently lost its spot to the Nikon Z6 III, only for Sony to wrestle it back with the mark IV’s successor. The Sony A7 V feels like the complete camera; its new processor, ‘partially stacked’ sensor and improved subject tracking autofocus deliver a boost in stills and video performance, even if the A7 V has the same 33MP resolution as its predecessor. The A7 V is a true all-rounder.

## Best Professional Camera

![Sony A7R VI]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/fu3ViSBeXY8uXFCgJ6V26X-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/fu3ViSBeXY8uXFCgJ6V26X-200-80.jpg.webp) "Sony A7R VI")

**Sony A7R VI**

Of course the readers' Camera of the Year also scoops top prize in its individual category. With Canon doing its business last year, and Nikon remaining totally quiet, the A7R VI really stood out. and it's a solid upgrade of the A7R V, with a bump in resolution from 61MP to 66.8MP, but more importantly it’s a quicker stacked sensor that’s ultimately way more versatile. The completely new battery (for class-leading battery life) and Sony’s best autofocus and design yet add further polish to the Sony A7R VI.

## Best Compact and Instant Camera

![Sony RX10 V]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ig86fjqrudbJBUSfJemhCF-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ig86fjqrudbJBUSfJemhCF-200-80.jpg.webp) "Sony RX10 V")

**Sony RX10 V**

We didn't see it coming, but nine years after the now-discontinued RX10 IV, Sony revived its series of bridge cameras, and made the best even better. It ditched the *Cyber-Shot* name and brushed up the Sony RX10 V’s design to feel more*Alpha*, but ultimately the shooting experience from the same 20.1MP 1-inch sensor and 24-600mm F2.8-4 lens combo is entirely familiar, and it's one that many enthusiast wildlife photographers will love. Add the chunkier battery, better autofocus and faster burst shooting, and it’s an even better, albeit pricier, all-in-one camera.

## Best Action and Vlogging Camera

![Insta 360 Luna Ultra]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/VoVmirzAPyvLMxvfXHjRNc-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/VoVmirzAPyvLMxvfXHjRNc-200-80.jpg.webp) "Insta 360 Luna Ultra")

**Insta 360 Luna Ultra**

DJI was busy priming its twin-lens Pocket 4P camera to attempt to change the vlogging camera game, only for Insta360 to steal its thunder with the Luna Ultra, and what an innovative camera it is. Taking the compact, gimbal-mounted camera formula of the Pocket series, the Insta360 Luna Ultra went one step further by adding a 3x telephoto camera, and fitted the device with a clever detachable remote module, which can control the camera remotely and doubles up as a mic. Never has solo vlogging been easier.

## Best Camera Lens

![Sony FE 100mm F2.8 Macro GM OSS]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MMiiXiDz7mSn7LgYcPDcc4-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MMiiXiDz7mSn7LgYcPDcc4-200-80.jpg.webp) "Sony FE 100mm F2.8 Macro GM OSS")

**Sony FE 100mm F2.8 Macro GM OSS**

At 10 years old, Sony’s 90mm macro lens was beginning to look a little dated, and so Sony macro photographers were delighted when the company released an entirely new model with a larger than life 1.4x magnification: the Sony FE 100mm F2.8 Macro GM OSS. Much like the similar Canon 100mm macro lens from 2021, Sony’s doubles up nicely as a portrait lens too, plus it's supported with image stabilization, compatible with teleconverters, and optically it's pretty much flawless.

## Best camera accessory

![Birdfy Bird Bath Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/VpTSLSa8yMTMt62ppitT3e-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/VpTSLSa8yMTMt62ppitT3e-200-80.jpg.webp) "Birdfy Bird Bath Pro")

**Birdfy Bird Bath Pro**

We’ve been reviewing bird feeder cameras for years, but it was the Birdfy Bird Bath Pro that made a splash in 2026 (pun intended). Amid warnings of the part that flat tray bird feeders can play in spreading avian diseases, in stepped this unique bird bath camera, with a more hygienic fountain, and that enabled us to observe a different kind of splashing and preening bird behavior from up close, with the same live feed and app notifications we've come to love from bird feeder cameras. The first generation product needs a little refining, but there's nothing else like it for bird lovers.

## Best drone

![Antigravity A1]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ezT7C3e2Rb7fArJRUNDYPS-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ezT7C3e2Rb7fArJRUNDYPS-200-80.jpg.webp) "Antigravity A1")

**Antigravity A1**

The Antigravity A1 clearly made an impression on readers, picking up its second award here. It’s great to see a company besides DJI entering the drone space, and to do it so well with its first 360–degree drone. The Antigravity A1 shoots 8K video taken from any angle, and delivers an immersive flight experience through its goggles and motion controller. And with firmware updates and new accessories since the product's launch, Antigravity looks like it's just getting started.

### Computing

## Computing Brand of the Year

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qi9H3WJNRojGQmb7J5wfTW-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qi9H3WJNRojGQmb7J5wfTW-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Apple**

Apple shook up the computing world this year with the introduction of its affordable MacBook Neo, which combined a relatively low price tag with a premium design, all without making too many compromises when it comes to performance. It left Windows competitors scrambling to compete, so we ended up with a range of excellent affordable Windows 11 laptops arriving later — which means Apple had perhaps the biggest impact on computing in 2026, and readers clearly felt it.

## Best Computing Innovation

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/xGh4DQqK3HCKaAptk8dCRT-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/xGh4DQqK3HCKaAptk8dCRT-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Nvidia DLSS 4.5**

Out of all the AI upscaling tech that's been helping PC gamers eke out more performance from their rigs, Nvidia's DLSS is by far the most advanced, and its 4.5 release brings better image quality, while Dynamic Multi Frame Generation does a great job of balancing image quality with high frame rates. Nvidia also added 6X Frame Generation, which allows compatible GPUs to reach incredibly high frame rates.

## Best Value Laptop

![Apple MacBook Neo]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/womLd2PCLXN2ft6qBX5jxG-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/womLd2PCLXN2ft6qBX5jxG-200-80.jpg.webp) "Apple MacBook Neo")

**Apple MacBook Neo**

When Apple launched the MacBook Neo earlier this year for $599 / £599 / AU$1,049, it made a huge impact. It was a lower price than many of us were expecting, and no Windows 11 laptop, or Chromebook, that cost similar could compete. Sure, there were compromises to keep the price down, such as a lack of backlit keyboard, slow USB ports and just 8GB of memory, but the overall package offered incredible value for money. So, it was a bit of a shame that Apple increased the price a few months later to $699 / £699, making it less good value. Still, even with the price rise, you won't find a better laptop for the price.

## Best AI Laptop

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/tQrkTP7k3PqRKSc9jUw8MT-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/tQrkTP7k3PqRKSc9jUw8MT-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Apple MacBook Pro 14-inch M5 (2025)**

After a few years where it felt like Apple was being left behind, it's beginning to roll out more of its Apple Intelligence (AI) features, and while it's still early days, the results are promising, with many of the tools featuring Apple's signature polish. Because the company has been including its Neural Engine in its chips for years, it actually has a bit of a head start when it comes to on-device AI, and the latest MacBook Pro is a great example of this.

## Best Gaming Laptop

![Asus ROG Strix Scar 18 (2026)]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/sMWCnFYKCb9gbWxk2KQjoC-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/sMWCnFYKCb9gbWxk2KQjoC-200-80.png.webp) "Asus ROG Strix Scar 18 (2026)")

**Asus ROG Strix Scar 18 (2026)**

Gaming laptops don't come much bigger and brasher than the Asus ROG Strix Scar 18 (2026). It features desktop-grade performance thanks to a mighty RTX 5090 laptop GPU, plus a cutting-edge Intel Core Ultra 9 processor. Its huge 18-inch screen is also fantastic, powered by mini-LED technology, which makes modern games look gorgeous. However, all this power comes with a high price tag.

## Best PC Gaming Handheld

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/nQdAiHs6BFmS9emPLoKhC4-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/nQdAiHs6BFmS9emPLoKhC4-200-80.png.webp) "Image")

**Asus ROG Xbox Ally X20**

While some PC gaming handhelds are going all-in on raw performance with cutting-edge components, the Asus ROG Xbox Ally X20 is more of a refinement of the existing ROG Xbox Ally — but that is actually a bit of a masterstroke, as it means it's cheaper than rivals such as MSI Claw 8 EX AI+. Don't think this is a budget device, however, and the Asus ROG Xbox Ally X20 features some nice improvements such as an OLED screen that looks fantastic, as well as improved buttons and TMR joysticks.

## Best Gaming Keyboard

![Turtle Beach Command Series KB7 TKL]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/XXZsdSz29QSGeKU8rX9GsU-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/XXZsdSz29QSGeKU8rX9GsU-200-80.png.webp) "Turtle Beach Command Series KB7 TKL")

**Turtle Beach Command Series KB7 TKL**

The Turtle Beach Command Series KB7 TKL is an unusual gaming keyboard, because instead of a physical numpad, it has a built-in touchscreen that can give you quick access to macros, apps, and shortcuts that can give you a competitive edge. Thanks to its 8K polling rate, it's fast and responsive, and when you're not playing games, it feels great to type on as well.

## Best Gaming Monitor

![Samsung Odyssey OLED G6 G60SF]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/EqMZqKTqGF53J8s5UCQ5Ha-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/EqMZqKTqGF53J8s5UCQ5Ha-200-80.jpg.webp) "Samsung Odyssey OLED G6 G60SF")

**Samsung Odyssey OLED G6 G60SF**

The Samsung Odyssey OLED G6 G60SF is a brilliant gaming monitor for people who are seriously into their competitive esports, thanks to its incredibly fast 500Hz refresh rate. Matched with ultra-low response times, and you have a monitor that will keep up with even the most frantic of games, and the OLED screen tech makes those games look incredible.

## Best Gaming Mouse

![Logitech G Pro X2 Superstrike]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/2yGprKNGFvTkQfXkcRCRcj-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/2yGprKNGFvTkQfXkcRCRcj-200-80.png.webp) "Logitech G Pro X2 Superstrike")

**Logitech G Pro X2 Superstrike**

The Logitech G Pro X2 Superstrike more than earns its rare five-star rating thanks to its fantastic build quality and lightweight design that makes it feel comfortable no matter what your grip type is. It also features a unique haptic inductive trigger system that lets you fine-tune the mouse buttons to perfectly suit your playstyle.

### Gaming

## Gaming Device of the Year

![Turtle Beach Stealth Pro 2]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/j66iFLJxVK7EoScfdi8myE-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/j66iFLJxVK7EoScfdi8myE-200-80.jpg.webp) "Turtle Beach Stealth Pro 2")

**Turtle Beach Stealth Pro 2**

The new Stealth Pro 2 headset from Turtle Beach is a fantastic premium headset that builds on everything great about its predecessor. ![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/3YvkMTHJHkeAtB6wT6VpRA-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/3YvkMTHJHkeAtB6wT6VpRA-200-80.png.webp) "Image")

**Apple Watch Ultra 4**

It's always tough to push the Apple Watch Ultra off the top spot, and readers once again voted it to give it the top price, and that's because it's such a comprehensive best-in-class beast of a smartwatch. While it doesn't look much different from last year's design from the front, the Ultra 4's redesigned sensor array means it can record heart rate readings up to 60 times more often as older models, plus it (finally!) gets a Readiness score. It remains the smartwatch to beat for another year at least.

## Health & Fitness Brand of the Year

![Garmin]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/SLuEtLxK5cLQAnqLnAaC7j-200-80.jpeg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/SLuEtLxK5cLQAnqLnAaC7j-200-80.jpeg.webp) "Garmin")

**Garmin**

Garmin has had a good year. The hugely popular brand went from strength to strength this year, not only delivering another five-star watch with the Garmin Fenix 9 Pro (among other great adventure- and cycling-focused devices), but *also* delivering the wearable we named the best screenless fitness tracker of 2026 (and there have been quite a few) — the Garmin Cirqa.

## Health and Fitness Innovation of the Year

![Google Fitbit Air]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/kQZMpMVBLvHMSvXG2VCGfn-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/kQZMpMVBLvHMSvXG2VCGfn-200-80.jpg.webp) "Google Fitbit Air")

**Google Fitbit Air**

Despite hot competition (including the Garmin Cirqa), our readers voted the Google Fitbit Air (which came first) the best health tech innovation of 2026. It was quite revolutionary in the fitness tracker field: up until it landed, the screenless space was dominated by the cripplingly expensive Whoop. The Fitbit Air broke this trend and used the power of Google's existing Gemini AI framework to democratize the AI element, delivering it at a lower price, while keeping the basics nice and cheap for under $100 / £90.

## Best Smartwatch

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ZikiYsUWHD8pddyfQhayGJ-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ZikiYsUWHD8pddyfQhayGJ-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Apple Watch Ultra 4**

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 hasn't changed a huge amount since last year (or indeed, since its inception), but it's improving on an already-excellent model with increased battery life and a redesigned health-sensing LED array. This new 'Health Sensing System' takes heart rate readings 60x more often during the day, resulting in what Apple says is the most accurate heart rate detection ever on a wearable. Of course, it's also a fantastic smartwatch with a sumptuous screen and useful features.

## Best Running Watch

![Garmin Fenix 9 Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/V5VRuuqhwb8SW6pZaVPrdm-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/V5VRuuqhwb8SW6pZaVPrdm-200-80.png.webp) "Garmin Fenix 9 Pro")

**Garmin Fenix 9 Pro**

From a technical standpoint, this may be Garmin's finest watch yet, and the one on every serious endurance athlete's Christmas list. The Fenix 9 Pro takes Garmin's excellent Fenix 8 and adds a brighter screen, an all-titanium nano-moulded case, a new green light for improved visibility, better maps, InReach and Solar options, and loads more. We rated it a five-star triumph in our Garmin Fenix 9 Pro review.

## Best Fitness Tracker

![Garmin Cirqa]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Nx5zv2UYqLCxANvmMho7c8-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Nx5zv2UYqLCxANvmMho7c8-200-80.jpg.webp) "Garmin Cirqa")

**Garmin Cirqa**

Although the Fitbit Air came first, we labelled the Garmin Cirqa as our ideal 'Goldilocks' tracker: cheaper than the Whoop, but more feature-rich than the Fitbit. Garmin's tracker lasts for over a week and features a tactile button to start-stop workouts and alarms, after which you get a haptic notification. This is a terrific way to interact with a screenless device and get assurance your workouts are being tracked, rather than opening up the app to manually start an activity.

## Best Smart Ring

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/3mQKxs2cdjtPkKsF9hAf59-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/3mQKxs2cdjtPkKsF9hAf59-200-80.png.webp) "Image")

**Oura Ring 5**

Although there were other smart rings released from the likes of RingConn and Circular this year, the Oura won out again this year simply by virtue of its app's powerful smarts. You do need a membership, but Oura is a premium device, and new health insights along with a redesigned, slimmer ring means it's easy to see why the readers voted it as their pick.

## Best Electric Toothbrush

![Suri Core 1.0]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/oJFBiA3RtUp6kCrhxVCVBb-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/oJFBiA3RtUp6kCrhxVCVBb-200-80.jpg.webp) "Suri Core 1.0")

**Suri Core 1.0**

Suri is the toothbrush company that creates simple sonic toothbrushes with sustainable elements, and it's managed to bring the price down a touch with the Suri Core 1.0, becoming more affordable while retaining its Apple-esque, good-looking design ethos. With the magnetic back attachment and simple dual modes, it's unspectacular, but a free brush head recycling program and a slick look go a long way to making it popular.

## Best Connected Fitness Tech

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/xC7M7qD9hp6P4SuWqrPtcj-200-80.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/xC7M7qD9hp6P4SuWqrPtcj-200-80.webp) "Image")

**Withings BodyScan 2**

Withings' top-flight smart scale is this year's best connected tech entry, which offers so much more than just weight. The retractable handle enables the scale to run currents through the whole body, taking heart rate readings and cardiovascular snapshots in addition to all the usual metrics and a new 'vascular age' metric. Its accurate, reliable, and usable by the whole family with multiple profiles.

### Home tech

## Home Device of the Year

![Philips Hue Essential]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ao75oNtezS8RimWfJnxNiY-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ao75oNtezS8RimWfJnxNiY-200-80.jpg.webp) "Philips Hue Essential")

**Philips Hue Essential**

The Philips Hue Essential series is an affordable entry to smart lighting that doesn’t compromise on quality. Philips Hue Essential bulbs and LED strips are compatible with all devices in the Hue ecosystem, but work equally well on their own, with brightness, color reproduction, and responsiveness that rivals smart lights costing several times the price.

## Home Tech Brand of the Year

![Image]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MBT7uYraMXye9Neh2DdVxJ-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MBT7uYraMXye9Neh2DdVxJ-200-80.jpg.webp) "Image")

**Philips Hue**

It's been an epic year for Philips Hue. At one end of the scale, the brand has made smart lighting more accessible with the budget-friendly Hue Essential series of bulbs and LED strips. At the other end, the new Philips Hue Bridge Pro lets power users connect over 150 lights in a single network and delivers exciting AI features — with lots more on the horizon. Smart lighting has never been so exciting.

## Home Tech Innovation of the Year

![Philips Hue Essential]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ao75oNtezS8RimWfJnxNiY-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ao75oNtezS8RimWfJnxNiY-200-80.jpg.webp) "Philips Hue Essential")

**Philips Hue Essential**

If you like the idea of installing smart lights, but have been put off by the price, the Philips Hue Essential series is for you. This innovative collection of smart lights offers all the best features of the Philips Hue system without the hefty price tag, meaning that it's a game-changer for people put off by the price of whole-home smart lighting in the past.

## Best Home Light Tech

![Philips Hue Essential]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ao75oNtezS8RimWfJnxNiY-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Ao75oNtezS8RimWfJnxNiY-200-80.jpg.webp) "Philips Hue Essential")

**Philips Hue Essential**

Another win for Philips Hue Essential, though this one was inevitable given its other victories here. The Essential light range is affordable and versatile, offering brightness, color, and smart functionality that belies the products' modest price. In side-by-side tests, it’s hard to differentiate between these and their premium counterparts, and they’re fully compatible with automations, scenes, switches and sensors through the Philips Hue app. A perfect entry to smart lighting.

## Best Coffee Machine

![Lavazza Assoluta]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/dbM8NEQnqbZmzxiBAA42iV-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/dbM8NEQnqbZmzxiBAA42iV-200-80.jpg.webp) "Lavazza Assoluta")

**Lavazza Assoluta**

The Lavazza Assoluta takes the guesswork out of making espresso with a clever system that scans the barcode on your coffee beans, then tweaks the machine’s brew settings accordingly. Its built-in milk frother prepares creamy lattes and macchiatos without the need to master a steam wand, and its drinks menu is fully customizable. Want an extra shot in your cappuccino? No problem!

## Best Value Coffee Machine

![Philips Baristina]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/B9GZX3cogdyuExmpFQ4hLD-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/B9GZX3cogdyuExmpFQ4hLD-200-80.jpg.webp) "Philips Baristina")

**Philips Baristina**

The Philips Baristina makes brewing espresso with fresh beans just as simple as using coffee pods. It handles dosing, grinding and extraction for you, and its ingenious design with a removeable portafilter handle makes it a cinch to clean compared to a typical automatic coffee maker. A perfect entry-level espresso machine — and accessibly priced, too.

## Best Fan

![Dyson Hot+Cool HF1]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/PbGYWu7mgEVoJsewzvtBRR-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/PbGYWu7mgEVoJsewzvtBRR-200-80.jpg.webp) "Dyson Hot+Cool HF1")

**Dyson Hot+Cool HF1**

This compact fan will serve you well all year round, offering effective cooling during the summer and efficient heating during the colder months. It’s supplied with a remote, but we were most impressed by the Dyson Hot+Cool HF1's mobile app, which made setting timers and schedules a breeze — and even let us pre-heat a room so it was cozy when we arrived home.
