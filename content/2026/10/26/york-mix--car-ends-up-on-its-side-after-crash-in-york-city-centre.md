Emergency services were called to a crash in York early this morning (Monday).

York firefighters went to the single vehicle collision on **Tower Street** at**12.39am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival the vehicle was on its side, and all persons were out.

#### Tower Street, York

“No action was required by the fire service and the incident was left with the police on scene.”
