Police are appealing for information after a series of cable thefts in North Yorkshire.




The county's force said thousands of pounds worth of the equipment was stolen from Kirk Smeaton, between Selby and Doncaster, on October 16, 18, and 24.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We’re particularly appealing for information about an “old-style” black Range Rover Sport and silver BMW X5, and a large white van all operating in the area together."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference 12260209167.
