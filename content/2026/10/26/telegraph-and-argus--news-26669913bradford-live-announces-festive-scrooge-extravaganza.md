A HIGH-FESTIVE show is set to wow audiences this December.

Scrooge! Cirque Extravaganza will arrive at Bradford Live from December 19 to 21, blending musical theatre, comedy and contemporary circus in a reimagined version of Charles Dickens’ classic A Christmas Carol.

A spokesperson said: "It’s the perfect family Christmas activity, especially if you’re looking for something a bit bigger and bolder than the usual festive fare."

West End performers will join 16 international cirque artists to bring the story to life through aerial acts, acrobatics, fire performances and more.

Richard Gauntlett will lead the 24-strong cast as Ebenezer Scrooge, with Jordan Conway as Charles Dickens, Nicole Clements as Belle, Beccy Lee as Mrs Cratchit, Nathan Paul as Mr Fezziwig and Barry Bloxham as Jacob Marley.

The production also features a unique audience prize draw.

At every performance, every ticket holder is automatically entered into a free £1,000 Lucky Seat prize draw, with one audience member selected during the show to take home the cash.

Tickets are available now via the Bradford Live website.

Scrooge! Cirque Extravaganza is produced by Jon Conway, whose career in family entertainment spans more than four decades.
