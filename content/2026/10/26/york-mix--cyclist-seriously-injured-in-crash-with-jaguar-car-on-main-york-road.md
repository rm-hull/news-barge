A cyclist suffered serious injuries in a crash on a busy York road.

The bike and a Jaguar I-PACE car collided on **Boroughbridge Road** at about 7pm on Saturday.

The cyclist, a man in his 40s, was taken to hospital where he remains.

#### Crash location

The driver of the Jaguar is assisting police with their enquiries.

Boroughbridge Road was closed for several hours to allow emergency services to help the casualty and to allow officers to examine the scene.

It reopened at around 2am on Sunday morning.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant CCTV or dashcam footage, to contact us.”
