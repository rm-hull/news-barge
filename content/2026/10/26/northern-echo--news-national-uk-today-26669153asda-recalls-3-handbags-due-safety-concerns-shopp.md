Asda has issued an urgent product recall over safety concerns.

The supermarket chain is recalling three styles of handbags after they were found to contain excessive levels of phthalates.

These are chemical compounds used to soften plastics, which can pose health risks.

## Asda recalls 3 handbag products over safety concerns

![It was found the bags present a potential health risk because they contain excessive levels of phthalates](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21629446.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It was found the bags present a potential health risk because they contain excessive levels of phthalates *(Image: Kennedy News & Media)*

A spokesperson for Asda said: "At the time of shipment, this product successfully passed all required tests.

"However, subsequent testing has identified that it may contain phthalates at levels exceeding the permitted regulatory limit.

"Certain phthalates, including Di(2-ethylhexyl) phthalate (DEHP), are subject to regulatory restrictions because prolonged exposure above specified limits may pose potential health risks and possibly cause damage to the reproductive system.

"While no safety incidents have been reported, we're taking this action to ensure compliance with applicable regulations and to maintain our commitment to consumer safety.

"The product doesn't meet the requirements of the REACH Regulations.

"If you've purchased this bag, please bring it back to your nearest store where you will be given a full refund.

"You do not need your receipt.

"We're very sorry for any inconvenience caused.

"If you'd like any further information, please contact Asda Customer Relations – 0330 053 0111."

The recall applies to three products:

* Burgundy quilted nylon camera bag (article number 1100258923, barcode 5059206287215)
* Zebra nylon phone bag (article number 1100258936, barcode 5059206286553)
* Brown quilted nylon camera bag (article number 1100264721, barcode 5059206962396).

The bags were sold for between £8 and £12.50.

Customers are advised to return the items to their nearest Asda store for a full refund.

## What is a product recall?

If there is a problem with a food product that means it should not be sold, it may be 'withdrawn' from shelves or 'recalled' when customers are asked to return it.

The Food Standards Agency issues allergy alerts when foods have to be withdrawn or recalled because of missing or incorrect allergy labelling, or if there is any other food allergy risk.
