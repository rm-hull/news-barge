In **Odesa**, firefighters were tackling a blaze at a** private industrial facility**when** Russian forces**struck the same location twice more, according to** Ukraine’s State Emergency Service**. The crews took shelter and escaped injury, but** fire and rescue equipment was damaged**. More than** 50 rescuers**were deployed, and authorities reported** no casualties** in the Odesa attacks.

At a second site in the region, **four garages were damaged**, a fire broke out and** one car was destroyed**.

In **Zaporizhzhia**, the overnight attack damaged** private homes, a hotel, two buses and outbuildings**. Three cars and dry vegetation caught fire, while rescuers searched the area for anyone trapped.

Acting regional military administration head **Mykhailo Semikin** initially reported two injured people, but later raised the toll to**four injured**: two women, aged 22 and 30, and two men, aged 40 and 61.

**Ukraine’s Air Force** said Russia launched**140 Shahed-type attack drones and decoys** overnight, including 70 jet-powered drones. It said air defences**shot down or suppressed 112 drones**, among them 56 jet-powered aircraft, as well as** three Kh-59/69 guided missiles**.

**Emergency crews** remained at the impact sites on Monday morning to**extinguish fires, assess structural damage and restore services**
