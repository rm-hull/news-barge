A Teesside street was closed and cordoned off after a crash left a car flipped on its side last night.

The crash happened on Ingram Road in Middlesbrough shortly after 5pm on Thursday (October 22).

Pictures on social media show police cordoning off the area around the car.

The vehicle had flipped onto its side, between Scalby Road and Burnsall Road.

Police and firefighters dealt with the aftermath of the incident, with residents looking on at the scene.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police confirmed that no one was injured.

A Cleveland Fire Brigade spokesperson said: "Cleveland Fire Brigade were called on October 22 at 5.02pm to an incident at Ingram Road, Berwick Hills, Middlesbrough.

"One fire appliance in attendance from Grangetown.

"Road traffic collision involving one vehicle.

"Crews made the scene safe and the stop call was received at 5:18pm."
