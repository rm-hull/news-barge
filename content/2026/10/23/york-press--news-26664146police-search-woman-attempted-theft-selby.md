Police have released a CCTV image as part of an investigation into an 'attempted theft' at a popular North Yorkshire supermarket.

The incident took place on October 3 at the Tesco store in Portholme Road in Selby.




North Yorkshire Police said the person pictured may have information that could help their enquiries.

North Yorkshire Police said: "Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police, quoting reference 1220194420.
