A thief has struck at supermarkets in a North Yorkshire town seven times.

North Yorkshire Police has issued three photographs of a man officers want to speak to about the thefts in Harrogate between Monday, September 7, and Saturday, October 10.

The force said goods have been stolen on seven occasions from the Coop stores in Skipton Road and Grantley Drive and from the Sainsbury’s in Kings Road.

“We believe the man in the images will have information that could help our investigation,” a police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information which could help police is asked to email [ellie.cooke@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ellie.cooke@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference number 12260201256 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
