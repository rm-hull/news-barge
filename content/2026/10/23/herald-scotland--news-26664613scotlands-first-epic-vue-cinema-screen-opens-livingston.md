Scotland’s first EPIC by Vue cinema screen has opened in Livingston as part of a refurbishment of the venue.

The opening marks the first phase of a multi-stage upgrade at Vue Livingston, with four screens now available following improvements to projection technology and seating.

The new EPIC screen is the cinema’s largest and features high-dynamic-range (HDR) laser projection technology from Barco, alongside Dolby Atmos sound with up to 64 channels.

Vue says the screen can deliver up to six times the brightness of a standard cinema screen, while the sound system is designed to create a more immersive audio experience.

Films currently scheduled for the screen include *Verity*,* Resident Evil*,* Other Mummy*and* Hadestown: The Musical*.

Three other refurbished screens have also reopened, each fitted with Barco laser projection technology intended to improve picture quality and energy efficiency.

All four screens feature new Lux and Ultra Lux seating options. Ultra Lux seats include independently reclining back and leg rests, an extra-wide armrest, a personal table and a beverage cooler.

Vue Livingston is due to close temporarily from November 30 while the final phase of the refurbishment is completed.

The work will include converting the foyer into a Vue Your Way retail area, where customers will be able to scan their tickets and select food and drinks from a self-service section.

Andrew Dykes, general manager of Vue Livingston, said: “Reaching this milestone in our refurbishment means that customers now have the chance to take a first look at some of the exciting features we are bringing to the venue.

“They will be able to test out our luxurious new seating options and watch films on our biggest screen, EPIC, which is the very first to arrive in Scotland.”
