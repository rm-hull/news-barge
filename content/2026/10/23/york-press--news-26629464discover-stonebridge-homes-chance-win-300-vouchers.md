## About the homes

Stonebridge Homes creates thoughtfully designed, premium and spacious homes for modern family living, with a range of two- to five-bedroom properties available across Yorkshire, the North East and the Midlands.

Selected homes are ready to move into now, making this an ideal time to start thinking about where you could be celebrating Christmas.

These are homes designed with gatherings in mind - and there's no better test of that than Christmas.

Anyone who has tried to squeeze 12 people, three courses and a game of charades into a galley kitchen will know that not every home is built for the extended family.

![]()

At the heart of many Stonebridge homes is a generous kitchen-diner, designed for socialising as much as cooking.

It's where the turkey gets basted and the gravy gets stirred.

It's also where everyone somehow ends up standing, glass in hand, long before anyone sits down to eat.

There's room for a big dining table and space to move around, helping the Christmas chef stay part of the celebrations rather than disappearing into the kitchen.

Beyond the kitchen, the natural flow between dining and living spaces means family and friends can spread out comfortably.

There's the sofa for the King's Speech, the floor for present-opening and somewhere quiet to retreat to after one too many Quality Street.

Then there are the practical touches that can make hosting feel easy rather than stressful.

Utility space can take care of muddy wellies, a downstairs cloakroom is handy when guests arrive, and useful storage provides somewhere for the decorations, extra chairs and overnight bags.

And outside, a garden gives everyone a little extra room to breathe between courses - and can really come into its own on a crisp Boxing Day morning.

For buyers hoping to make a move before the festive season, timing matters.

Selected Stonebridge homes are currently ready to move into, while customers reserving an eligible home by October 31, 2026 can receive a £250 M&S Gift Card, subject to availability, eligibility and Stonebridge Homes' full offer terms.

Buyers may also be able to benefit from a 5% Deposit Boost on selected homes and developments, helping make that next move more achievable.

Offers and availability vary by development and plot, so buyers should speak directly to a Stonebridge Homes Sales Adviser to find out which options are available for their chosen home.

In the end, it isn't simply the size of a home that makes it the one everyone wants to visit at Christmas.

The right home simply gives all of those moments room to happen - this Christmas and every one after.
