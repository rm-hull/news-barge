Amanda Owen's son Miles is taking on an increasingly prominent role in the family's farming and renovation work in the Yorkshire Dales.

The eldest of Amanda and Clive Owen's sons has become a familiar face to viewers following the family's adventures at Ravenseat.

![Miles Owen](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21607874.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Miles Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Alongside his mother Amanda, he has been involved in work at Anty John's, the historic farmhouse being restored near Ravenseat.

The ambitious project has involved extensive building work to transform the long-neglected property into a usable family home.

But the responsibilities do not stop at the building site.

Miles is also seen helping his father Clive deal with problems on the farm, including a waterlogged field.

The scenes underline the practical demands of running a remote hill farm, where changing weather conditions and the needs of livestock can quickly alter the day's plans.

Viewers first met Miles as one of the nine Owen children in Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm.

The series followed the family as they balanced school, farming and everyday life in the remote Swaledale countryside.
