Weather experts have updated their forecast for Storm Boelo this weekend – and it is now expected to batter all of York and North Yorkshire.

The Met Office has issued a yellow warning for wind from **midday tomorrow**(Saturday, 24 October) till** 9pm on Sunday**.

Earlier forecasts has suggested only the very north of North Yorkshire would be affected. Now the warning covers the whole county right down to Sheffield.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/storm-boelo-yellow-weather-warning-23-oct-2026.webp)

The Met Office says the storm will bring “impactful winds”.

There will be “widespread gusts” of 55-65mph widely, with these reaching up to 70-80mph in more exposed coastal locations.

“Storm Boelo will bring a very windy and wet spell this weekend with some disruption likely across parts of northern UK,” the Met Office added.

It says:

* Injuries and danger to life from flying debris are possible
* Some damage to buildings and trees, such as tiles blown from roofs and large branches or trees blown over, could happen
* Road, rail, air and ferry services may be affected, with longer journey times and cancellations possible
* Some roads and bridges may close
* Power cuts may occur, with the potential to affect other services, such as mobile phone coverage
* Injuries and danger to life could occur from large waves and beach material being thrown onto sea fronts, coastal roads and properties

There will also be outbreaks of rain on Saturday and possible showers on Sunday.
