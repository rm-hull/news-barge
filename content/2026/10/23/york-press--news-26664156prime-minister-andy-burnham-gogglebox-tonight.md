Prime Minister Andy Burnham is set to feature on a popular TV show tonight (Friday, October 23).

From No. 10 Downing Street, he joins other famous faces on Gogglebox but will sit on the sofa next to the Mayor of Liverpool, Steve Rotheram.

The friends will react to the week's television programmes, and it's all for a good cause as the show's Stand Up To Cancer special is back.

Channel 4 said: “Nothing is off limits as Gogglebox continues to celebrate the simple joy of watching television together.

“They’ll be sharing hilarious, honest and often unexpected reactions to the week’s biggest television moments as well as poignant stories all in aid of Stand Up To Cancer.”

## Andy Burnham receives mixed reaction for Gogglebox stint

There has been some mixed reaction following the announcement that the Prime Minister would be joining the programme for the one-off special.

Channel 4 announced the news on its Instagram, where fans of the show have shared their thoughts on the appointment.

One said: "This is brilliant!!

"Some people will hate it, but it’s refreshing to see a PM being a bit more normal.

"Love it! Ha!"

Someone else said: "I love this!"

This person commented: "Brilliant!

"What an amazing thing to do for Stand up to Cancer!"

One person added: "Because he’s got time for hours of TV whilst the country’s in the state it is!!

"At least he’s made buses cheap 👏👏".

This person commented: "think he should be focused on the mess we call Britain".

Someone else fought back: "there’s people mad about the fact their prime minister is helping raise money for a cancer charity.

"lowest of low."

Antoher said: "Like it or not, it is to raise money for Stand up to Cancer".

So far, the only other celebrities who have been announced for the Gogglebox Stand Up To Cancer special are comedian Joe Lycett and the Sugababes (Siobhan Donaghy, Mutya Buena and Keisha Buchanan).

## How much do you know about Andy Burnham?

Joe Lycett, who will appear with his two aunties Pauline and Margaret, was recently on our screens as he took part in the BBC's Celebrity Traitors, but he was banished last week.

Gogglebox normally shows ordinary families watching TV, but there are often celebrity series too.

In fact, the ordinary families have been so well loved by fans, they get to enjoy some fame too.

Celebrities who have taken part include Martin and Roman Kemp, Nick Grimshaw and his niece Liv, Vernon Kay and Paddy McGuinness, Olivia Attwood and more.

**Gogglebox for Stand Up To Cancer airs tonight on Channel 4 at 9:05pm.**
