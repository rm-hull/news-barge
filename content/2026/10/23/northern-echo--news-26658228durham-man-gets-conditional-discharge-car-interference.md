A County Durham man has been given a conditional discharge after admitting interfering with a car with the intention of committing theft.

Joshua Winter, 24, of no fixed abode in Ludworth, appeared before Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on October 20.

He pleaded guilty to interfering with a Vauxhall Viva, on April 30, at High Pittington.
