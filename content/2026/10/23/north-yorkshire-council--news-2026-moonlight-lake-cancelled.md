Due to the deteriorating weather forecast and increasingly windy conditions, Moonlight on the Lake at Peasholm Park, in Scarborough, has been cancelled on safety grounds.

With gusts forecast to reach unsafe levels, the safety of our visitors, volunteers and staff must remain our highest priority.

We know this news will be disappointing and sincerely apologise for the inconvenience. Unfortunately, it will also not be possible to reschedule the event which was due to be held this evening and tomorrow evening (23 October and 24 October).

All ticket holders will receive an email advising them of the cancellation, along with an automatic refund, which will be processed within the next 10 days.

Thank you for your understanding.
