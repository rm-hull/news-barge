Emily St. John Mandel, best known for her prescient flu pandemic novel *Station Eleven*, joined the New Scientist Book Club to talk about her latest book, the science-fiction novel* Exit Party*. Emily discussed everything from why she is compelled to write about the aftermath of disaster – this latest book takes place in a near-future US, after a civil war – to how hard it was to keep ahead of the US news cycle as she wrote.
