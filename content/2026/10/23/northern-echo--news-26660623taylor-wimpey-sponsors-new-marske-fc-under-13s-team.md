A North East grassroots football club has fresh training and away kits thanks to support from a regional housebuilder.

Taylor Wimpey North Yorkshire has sponsored New Marske Football Club’s Under-13s, providing training kit and away shirts for the 2026/27 season.

The New Marske club aims to bring children, young people and families together through football.

Jason Robinson, chairman of New Marske Football Club, said: "I’m absolutely delighted to welcome Taylor Wimpey as sponsors of our U13s team.

"Support from local businesses like Taylor Wimpey means so much to our club and most importantly to the young players who represent New Marske FC every weekend.

"Sponsorships like this help us provide better opportunities, equipment and experiences for our players, while continuing to build a strong and positive football community."

Mr Robinson said: "On behalf of the club, the coaches, parents and especially the U13s players, I’d like to say a huge thank you to Taylor Wimpey for believing in our club and supporting our young players."

Nicki Hope, Sales & Marketing Director at Taylor Wimpey North Yorkshire, said: "We are so pleased to be sponsoring New Marske Football Club’s training kit and away shirts for the 2026/2027 season."
