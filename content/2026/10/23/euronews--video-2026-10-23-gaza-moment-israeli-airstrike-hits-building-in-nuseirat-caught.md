**Dramatic footage** captured the moment the building was struck, with a**powerful explosion** lighting up the night sky.**Flames spread through the wreckage** as Palestinian residents gathered nearby and**rescue teams rushed to extinguish the fire**.

Other images showed people carrying a wounded person away from the scene. **No deaths were reported** following the strike, although the**extent of the injuries was not immediately clear**.

By Friday morning, residents were **picking through the rubble of destroyed homes**, searching for belongings and assessing the damage. Men and women collected possessions from the ruins, while** bags of food supplies lay scattered** among the debris. Children were seen walking across the roof of a damaged building as families surveyed what remained of their homes.

The aftermath footage showed **piles of broken concrete and shattered structures** across the affected area. The strike left residents confronting**extensive destruction in Nuseirat**, with rescue workers and local families dealing with the immediate consequences of the attack.
