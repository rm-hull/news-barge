A FREIGHT company has applied for a licence to operate in Bradford.

Vectorium Freight Ltd is seeking permission to use Freightroute Ltd on Dealburn Road, Low Moor, as a base for three goods vehicles and three trailers.

The company is currently based at Abbotside Close, Bradford.

Residents and businesses near the proposed operating centre can raise objections if they believe the licence will affect their use or enjoyment of their land.

To do this, they must send written representations to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, within 21 days of the notice, stating their reasons.

A copy must also be sent to Vectorium Freight Ltd.
