Firefighters remain at the scene of an incident in the Bradford district after smoke was seen coming from a power grid box this afternoon.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to the scene on Westgate in Baildon at 2.05pm on Friday following reports of smoke coming from electrical equipment.

Two fire engines from Bingley and Rawdon were sent to the scene and remain in attendance.

The cause of the smoke has not yet been confirmed.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21624570.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Westgate in Baildon *(Image: T&A)*

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “We were called at 14:05 this afternoon following reports of smoke coming from a power grid box.

"Two fire engines from Bingley and Rawdon remain in attendance.

"There is no further information available at this stage."

No injuries have been reported.

More information is expected as the incident develops.
