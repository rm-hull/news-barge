A car has been stolen in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to about the incident in Scarborough on the morning of Sunday, July 26.

The force on Friday (October 23) issued the photograph, saying the man pictured may have information about the theft to help the investigation.

A police spokesperson said the car, a lilac-blue MG3 Series, was stolen from a property in Prospect Road.

They said officers believe the man pictured has a Middlesbrough accent and was drinking in Scarborough centre at the Sun Inn and at Fusion the previous evening (July 25).

Anyone with information is asked to email [laurie.simpson1@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:laurie.simpson1@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260143020 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
