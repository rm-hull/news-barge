People visiting the North East coast this weekend are being warned to stay away from crashing waves as the region braces for Storm Boelo.

Redcar RNLI is urging anyone visiting the coast this weekend to take extra care on beaches, cliffs and sea walls as conditions worsen.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623385.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Crashing waves in Redcar *(Image: RNLI Redcar)*

The Met Office has predicted "impactful" strong winds for Storm Boelo, with weather warnings in place across County Durham and Darlington from 12pm on Saturday (October 24) until 9pm on Sunday (October 25).

Redcar RNLI said: "Stay away from crashing waves and if you think you see anyone in trouble in the water do not hesitate, call 999 and ask for the Coastguard.

"You might just help save a life."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21622684.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Storm Boelo is set to bring another round of turbulent weather to the UK over the weekend *(Image: GETTY)*

The warning covers heavy rain, with gusts of 50-60mph widely and 70-80mph in exposed coastal areas and on high ground.

The region had already been hit by heavy showers on Friday (October 23), with wind speeds set to increase throughout the evening.
