EMERGENCY services have been called to a serious crash on a major road in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place on the A19’s southbound carriageway between Jeater Houses and Knayton, near Thirsk, at 6.44pm on Thursday (October 22).




A car is reported to have crashed with the central reservation, and travelled across to the opposite carriageway before it landed in a ditch.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews from Northallerton and Thirsk had responded to the incident.

A service spokesperson said: “The sole occupier was out on arrival and in care of paramedics, before being taken to hospital via road ambulance. No fire service action was required.”
