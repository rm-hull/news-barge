Set in the villages along the Godavari River, the story follows Raju, a kind-hearted auto driver who falls for Maya, a spirited college student. But their relationship is put to the test by Maya’s father, who values tradition and protection above everything else.

Good news! You can watch *Kinare Kinare* online**from anywhere with a VPN**.

Sumanth Prabhas, best known for *Mem Famous*, plays Raju opposite Nidhi Pradeep, with veteran star Jagapathi Babu playing Maya’s father. The film marks the comeback of yesteryear actress Laila, who plays Jhansi, while comedians Sudarshan and Rajkumar Kasireddy also feature in supporting roles.
