A man has been charged with two counts of rape following an incident at a bus station.

Patrick Rybar, 24, of no fixed address in Leeds, who self-defines as mixed race, appeared at Leeds Magistrates’ Court today.

He has been charged with two counts of rape, sexual assault by penetration, and two counts of sexual assault.

The charges relate to an incident involving a woman at Pudsey Bus Station during Tuesday evening.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21625598.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police at Pudsey Bus station *(Image: T and A staff)*

Rybar was remanded in custody and is due to appear at Leeds Crown Court on November 26.

Enquiries remain ongoing.
