## Azada reflects on her first international competition, representing Azerbaijan and her ambition to become a strong senior gymnast.

For 15-year-old Azada Atakishiyeva, rhythmic gymnastics has become much more than a sport. What began in early childhood grew into a passion that has shaped her life and ambitions. She took part in her first international competition in Georgia at just five years old, marking the beginning of a journey built on dedication and hard work. Representing Azerbaijan abroad and hearing the national anthem at competitions remain among her most meaningful experiences. Now, Azada is working towards her dream of becoming a strong senior gymnast and making her country proud.
