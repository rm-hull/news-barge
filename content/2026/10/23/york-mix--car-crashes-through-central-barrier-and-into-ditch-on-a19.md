Emergency services were called to a serious crash on the A19 in North Yorkshire last night (Thursday).

It was on the southbound carriageway between **Jeater Houses** and**Knayton**, near Thirsk, at** 6.44pm**.

A car had hit the central reservation, travelled across to the opposite carriageway and landed in a ditch.

#### Incident location

Fire crews from Northallerton and Thirsk went to the incident.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The sole occupier was out on arrival and in care of paramedics, before being taken to hospital via road ambulance.

“No fire service action was required.”
