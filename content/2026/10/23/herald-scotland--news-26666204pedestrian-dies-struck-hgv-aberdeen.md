A pedestrian has died after being struck by a HGV in Aberdeen.

Around 10.10am on Friday, October 23, police received a report of a collision involving a male pedestrian and a HGV on the city's Victoria Road.

Emergency services attended and the 58-year-old man was pronounced dead at the scene. His family has been notified.

Road policing officers are appealing for witnesses to come forward.

**READ MORE:**

Sergeant Chris Bain of the North East Road Policing Department said: “My thoughts are with the family and friends of the man who died.

"Our enquiries are ongoing and I’m appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision or who may have dashcam footage to contact police as it may assist in our enquiries.”

Anyone with information is asked to call Police Scotland via 101, quoting incident number 0845 of Friday, 23 October, 2026.
