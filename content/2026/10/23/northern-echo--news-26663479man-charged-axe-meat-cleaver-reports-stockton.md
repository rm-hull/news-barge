A man has been charged after police were called to a person “carrying an axe and a meat cleaver” in Stockton.

Officers attended Stockton High Street on Wednesday (October 21) after reports of a man with a weapon around 3.45pm.

A 42-year-old man was arrested - with police then locating an axe and a meat cleaver, which were seized.

He was questioned and subsequently charged with two counts of possession of a bladed article, as well as three attempted burglaries, a theft from a shop and possession of a Class A drug in relation to separate offences.

Mark Duffie, of no fixed address, is now due to appear at Teesside Magistrates’ Court this morning.
