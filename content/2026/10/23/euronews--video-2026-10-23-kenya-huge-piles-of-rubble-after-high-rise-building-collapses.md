A **multi-storey building under construction collapsed** in**Nairobi’s Eastleigh district** on Friday 23 October, leaving a vast heap of**concrete rubble and twisted debris.** The**Kenya Red Cross** said the structure came down at about**1:40 pm local time** along Yusuf Haji Avenue and dispatched emergency response teams to assist search-and-rescue efforts.

Footage from the Kenyan capital showed the scale of the destruction, with large sections of the unfinished structure **reduced to piles of broken concrete**. An excavator was deployed among the wreckage while Red Cross personnel worked at the site, urging the public to** stay clear so rescuers could operate safely**.

**Several people were feared trapped** beneath the rubble, although authorities had**not yet confirmed casualties, injuries, or the number of missing persons**. The Nairobi County governor’s office noted reports of trapped individuals, while Reuters reported that the** ground floor of the building had been occupied**.

There were **conflicting accounts over the building’s height**, ranging between** 10 and 18 storeys**. No official height or cause of the collapse have been confirmed.

The collapse has once again raised questions about **construction standards** in Kenya, where**similar disasters have repeatedly claimed lives**amid concerns over inadequate** safety checks and enforcement**.
