A CHOIR will perform in a fundraising concert to support a church in Sudan.

Steeton Male Voice Choir will take to the stage at St Peter’s Church in Saltaire on Friday, November 13, at 7.30pm.

The concert aims to raise funds for the Sudanese church amid ongoing conflict in the country.

A spokesperson said: "People have no work, and any food that there is, is hard to find and expensive. We trust that we will be able to help them a little by this concert.

"The choir is on top form with a programme of music to delight all."

Tickets are £10 for adults.
