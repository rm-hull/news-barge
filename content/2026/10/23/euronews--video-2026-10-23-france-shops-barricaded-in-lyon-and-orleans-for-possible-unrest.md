As **Lyon** and**Orléans prepare for a weekend of demonstrations**, shopkeepers are** boarding up windows**, removing valuable stock and** reinforcing entrances**.

In Lyon, workers were filmed on Friday installing **wooden panels and protective fencing** outside**City Hall**, the** Museum of Fine Arts**and the** Chamber of Commerce**. Shops along** Rue de la République**were also being secured, with carpenters cutting boards and covering windows. The precautions reflect** fears of possible violence and property damage**, although no such incidents during the upcoming demonstrations have been confirmed.

In **Orléans**, businesses along the planned protest route are also** taking precautions**ahead of a weekend that will see the city host the** National Rally’s congress**. Nearly** 3,000 police officers and security personnel**will be deployed as between** 20,000 and 30,000 protesters**are expected to gather in opposition to the event, raising concerns over** possible disruption**.
