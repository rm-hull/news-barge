The Rosedale and District Agricultural, Horticultural and Industrial Society will hold its 2026 annual general meeting on November 10.

The meeting will take place at the Updale Reading Room, Rosedale East, at 7pm.

Members will consider previous AGM minutes, matters arising, correspondence, the constitution, the financial report and proposals for the 2027 show.

Those attending will also elect a president and discuss any other business.

The AGM will be followed by the 2027 Show Committee meeting there.
