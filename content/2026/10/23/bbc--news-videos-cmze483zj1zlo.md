# Why is John Travolta's old luxury jet in a small Australian town?

John Travolta's old Boeing 707 luxury jet is being reassembled at the Historical Aircraft Restoration Society Aviation Museum in Albion Park, New South Wales.

Travolta, a Hollywood star and avid pilot, first bought the plane in 1998. So why is it here in a small coastal Australian town?

The BBC's Lana Lam explains everything you need to know.
