# Huge music legend appearing at Club Classics party in Leeds

## It is going to be one huge party

A huge Club Classics party is happening in Leeds.

Project House will be hosting the festivities. It will be happening on Saturday, November 28.

The Club Classics daytime event is dedicated to hands in the air anthems from the 1990s and 2000s. The fun, at the Armley Road venue, will be running between 3pm and 11pm.

Providing the soundtrack for the 4th birthday celebrations, of the popular festivities, are a couple of music heavyweights. We’ll see DJs Allister Whitehead and Jon Pleased Wimmin along with a live vocal performance by Rowetta of Happy Mondays and Hacienda Classical fame.

Completing the line up are Marc Leaf, Gavin Lampitt, Mitch and Bongo Dave on percussion.
