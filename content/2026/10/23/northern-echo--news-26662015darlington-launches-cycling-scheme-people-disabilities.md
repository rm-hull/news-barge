A free cycling programme aimed at helping people with learning and physical disabilities build confidence and independence has been launched in Darlington.

Darlington Council’s Move More team is running the six-week scheme every Monday at Eastbourne Sports Complex.

A spokesperson said: "For the next six weeks, our Move More team is running a free, inclusive cycling programme for people with learning and/or physical disabilities, every Monday at Eastbourne Sports Complex."

"Come along for free, build your independence and learn new skills with support from friendly and experienced experts."

Sessions run for 45 minutes, from 10am to 10.45am or from 10.45am to 11.30am.

The programme is delivered in partnership with Darlington Association on Disability, WHEEL Education, Darlington Cycling Club and British Cycling.
