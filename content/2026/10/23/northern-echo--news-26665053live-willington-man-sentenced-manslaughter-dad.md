* Good afternoon and welcome to the live coverage of the sentencing of Martin Greensmith.
* The 37-year-old was convicted of the manslaughter of his dad, Steven Shevels, following a trial at Teesside Crown Court last month.
* Jurors heard that Greensmith reacted angrily when learning that Mr Shevels was his father after he turned up at the Queen’s Head in Willington on Christmas Eve.
* The fatal push came after 61-year-old Mr Shevels was asked to leave the pub for becoming a nuisance to the Greensmith family.
