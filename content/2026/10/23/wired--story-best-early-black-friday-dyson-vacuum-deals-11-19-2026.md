I’m often asked if Dyson products are really worth the money. As WIRED’s resident vacuum tester, I’ve tried every recent Dyson vacuum—from the newest, most powerful model to the long-favored V15 Detect—and I have a lot of thoughts on which ones you should buy, and at what price. The best time to shop is during sale season, and lucky for you, Dyson's Black Friday deals have already started as of today. If you’re looking to pick up a new vacuum before your family comes over for Thanksgiving, here's the on-sale models I recommend.

## Best Dyson Vacuum Deals Right Now

## The Best Early Black Friday Dyson Stick Vacuum Deals

If you’re going to buy a Dyson vacuum on sale, you might as well go for one of the better ones. The V16 Piston Animal is Dyson’s most powerful stick vacuum, with 315 air-watts of power, some nice quality-of-life improvements in the body design, and an option to add the Submarine 2.0 mop head. I really liked this vacuum for the design upgrades and power level, but the price tag can be hard to stomach. It’s on sale fairly regularly, and there are a few different on-sale combos: The base vacuum is $180 off, bundling it with the Submarine head is $200 off, and the option with the charging Floor Dok is also $200 off. I love the Submarine head, but while I haven’t tested the vacuum with the Floor Dok yet, it’s the accessory I want most—having your cordless vacuum fully charged whenever you want it, and somewhere to store it, is really handy.

If you want a cheaper vacuum that still comes with two cleaner heads, the older Dyson V15 Detect is also already on sale ($599), and it's even bundled with the Car Cleaning Kit, meaning three extra attachments. The V15 was our top Dyson vacuum pick for a long time because it's more affordable than the Gen5Detect and V16, but still packs two cleaner heads (the Digital Motorbar for carpet and Fluffy Optic for hard floors) and has 240 air-watts of suction (almost 100 more than the V10 Konical and other cheaper Dyson vacuums). It's a great all-around vacuum, and this bundle option gets you great extra attachments.

The PencilVac is incredibly handy and compact, and great if you're trying to do quick pickups daily on hard floors. You won't want it if you have any kind of rug or carpet, since it's only designed for hard flooring, but I did find it incredibly easy to grab and use for constant litter trails in my house. It's incredibly lightweight and a great fit for anyone with mobility or strength concerns. If these features speak to you, this is a great time to buy, as it's already on sale.

## The Best Early Black Friday Dyson Wet Floor Cleaner Deal

This wet floor cleaner is often on sale for around $400, so it's not a huge surprise to see this deal ahead of Black Friday. The Dyson Clean+Wash is primarily a wet floor cleaner, but it can do dry cleans, too, and does not use a filter you inevitably need to keep clean. WIRED reviewer Kat Merck noticed no weird smells in three months of use. It's similar to the Dyson WashG1, with a self-propelled design that makes it easy to push around the room, and it does a good job scrubbing floors clean. Keep in mind that it's not great for dry debris, since its just rolling, not sucking.

## The Best Early Black Friday Dyson Robot Vacuum Deal

The Dyson Spot+Scrub Ai came out earlier this year and is one of my favorite robot vacuums from the brand. That's including the newer Dyson R3 Nurovi Spot+Scrub UV I’m currently testing, which hasn't blown me away as a better investment. (It actually failed my corner test completely, which vacuums in that price range should not do.)

The Spot+Scrub Ai is much lower priced, and despite some dubious AI feature claims, it does a good job cleaning autonomously. It’s often discounted around this price, but the $400-off sale is a little better than what I saw during Prime Day.
