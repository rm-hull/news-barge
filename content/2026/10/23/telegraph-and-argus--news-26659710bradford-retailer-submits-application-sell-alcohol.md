A shop has applied for a licence to sell alcohol.

The application, submitted by Taha Said, seeks permission for Mila Shop at 450 Halifax Road, Bradford, to sell alcohol for off-site consumption.

If approved, the licence would permit alcohol sales from 7am to midnight every day.

The application was submitted on October 5, 2026.

The public has the right to object to the application.

Objections must be submitted in writing to the Department of Place Licensing Team, Shearbridge Depot, Shearbridge Road, Bradford, BD7 1PU.

Alternatively, objections can be emailed to [licensing@Bradford.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@Bradford.gov.uk.)

The deadline for objections is November 2, 2026.

The licensing register and record of application are available for inspection at the above office by appointment only.

The application is being processed under the Licensing Act 2003.

Making a false statement in connection with an application under this act is a criminal offence, punishable by an unlimited fine.
