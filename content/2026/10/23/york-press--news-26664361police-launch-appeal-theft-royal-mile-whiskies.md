Police have released a CCTV image of a man they want to speak to following a 'theft' at a whisky shop in York.

The incident took place on Tuesday, October 6 at Royal Mile Whiskies in Coney Street, York.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC1488 Bell by email at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting reference 12260196711.
