A street in the East Riding of Yorkshire is set to close for repairs next month.

A section of Main Street in Sutton-upon-Derwent will be shut to traffic on November 3 from 9am to 5pm.

Yorkshire Water will be conducting repairs on a noisy and loose frame cover, necessitating the closure.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has issued the notice of the temporary traffic regulation order under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

The order will be valid for 18 months, but the works are expected to be completed within the eight-hour window.

The road closure is to ensure the safety of the public during the repair process.

A signed diversion will be in place for those affected by the closure.

The alternative route for traffic will be via Main Street, Sandhill Lane, The Street, Sutton Lane, York Road, Hodsow Lane Roundabout, Hodsow Lane, Back Lane, Melbourne Road, road from Hagg Lane to General Lane, Hagg Lane and Common Lane, Melbourne CP, Allerthorpe CP, Cottingwith CP, Sutton Upon Derwent CP, Barmby Moor CP, Thornton CP, Pocklington Provincial Ward, and Wolds Weighton Ward.

Despite the closure, access will be maintained for emergency vehicles.

For further information, people can contact East Riding of Yorkshire Council's Highways Customer Care on 01482 393939, quoting reference RJH.
