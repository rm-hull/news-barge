STACK venues across the North East are gearing up for a night to remember.

Locations including Sunderland, Newcastle and Middlesbrough are promising live music, themed parties, and a packed line-up of entertainment to see in the new year in style.

At STACK Seaburn in Sunderland, a 10-piece band will headline the night with Ibiza in Symphony, performing classic island anthems such as Rhythm of the Night, Right Here Right Now, Show Me Love, and Better Off Alone.

DJ Mark Ireland will close out the night, keeping the party going into the early hours of 2027.

St James’ STACK will host Ibiza Reborn, another tribute to the world-famous party island, with a high-energy set of electronic dance music and a performance from resident DJ Aaron Robson.

In Newcastle, The Emerald Thieves will perform crowd-pleasers spanning the 60s to the 00s, from Irish sing-alongs and country favourites to indie classics.

DJ Playmoor will also be on hand to keep the music going throughout the night.

STACK Middlesbrough will pay tribute to legendary pop group ABBA with ABBA Fabulous, a live vocal show celebrating some of the Swedish supergroup’s biggest hits.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21624648.jpg?type=mds-article-620)STACK Middlesbrough *(Image: STACK)*

DJ Foster Grant will round out the evening with a mix of 70s and 80s classics.

All venues will open their doors from 7pm, with tickets available online and inclusive of booking fees.

Early bird tickets for Seaburn and Newcastle are priced at £18, with general admission rising to £21 once those sell out.

St James’ STACK early bird tickets are already sold out, but general tickets remain available at £21 each.

Middlesbrough early bird tickets are £13.50, increasing to £16 once the allocation is gone.

Each STACK site will also feature its full range of street food vendors and bars, allowing guests to enjoy a variety of food and drink options throughout the evening.

Chels Morton, marketing manager at STACK for sports and events, said: "Our New Year’s Eve programmes across all of our sites have been designed to be nights to remember.

"We’ve got absolutely brilliant live entertainment and with all of our fantastic food vendors and bars, there’s nowhere better to be see in the new year."
