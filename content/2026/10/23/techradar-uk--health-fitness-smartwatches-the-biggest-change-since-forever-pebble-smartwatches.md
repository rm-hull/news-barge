* **Pebble has announced plug-in support for developers**
* **It should lead to improved app and watch face experiences**
* **Eric Migicovsky calls it 'the biggest change since forever'**

If you own a Pebble smartwatch (or are thinking of getting one), a major update is on the way: plug-in support is coming to the Pebble OS platform, which will mean much more flexibility and variety for apps and watch faces.

As announced by Pebble founder Eric Migicovsky (via Android Authority), the upgrade is "the biggest change to the SDK since... forever". Plug-ins are now available as a developer preview, but there's no timeframe yet for a full rollout.

It essentially means that developers putting together Pebble apps or watch faces will be able to call on pre-packaged add-ons for tasks like checking the weather, controlling music playback, activating smart lights, chatting to AI, and more.

Migicovsky describes them as being similar to Apple Watch complications except "way more flexible and awesome". As developers start to get to grips with plug-ins, look out for their appearance in third-party apps and watch faces.

In the 13 years since Pebble first appeared, it's had its ups and downs, but its return in 2025 was widely welcomed. The introduction of plug-ins is a sign of a platform and ecosystem that's maturing and ready to develop further.

Expect watch faces that are more useful, apps that are more versatile and easier to operate, more consistency between apps, and improved privacy (you'll be able to configure permissions for individual plug-ins as needed).
