POLICE have launched an investigation into a spate of suspected burglary attempts in North Yorkshire.

The reported incidents took place at businesses on the east side of Whitby, North Yorkshire Police said, between 12am and 4am on Sunday (September 20).




A force spokesperson said: “In Church Street, a window was forced open and a quantity of cash was taken from inside.

“Another business premise in Henrietta Street was also broken in to.

“An untidy search was carried out but nothing is believed to have been taken.

“We are wanting to speak with the man in the CCTV image below who may have information to assist us in our investigation.

“Anyone with information regarding the man in the CCTV image or the incidents is asked to email [dan.hardey@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:dan.hardey@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 and ask for Dan Hardey.

“Alternatively, contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or submit an online report via their website.

“Please quote reference 12260184623 when providing information.”
