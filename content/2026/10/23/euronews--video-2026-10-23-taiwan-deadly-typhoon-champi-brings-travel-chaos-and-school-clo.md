In **Keelung**, enormous waves crashed against the shore, while emergency workers cleared fallen branches and debris from streets in the capital.** One person was killed**after being swept away by waves on Liuqiu island on Thursday, while** 94 others were injured**.

**Schools and offices closed**, while** 931 flights were cancelled**, including all 262 domestic services and 669 international flights. At** Taipei's Songshan Airport**, departure counters stood empty and stranded passengers rested beneath screens displaying cancellations.

**Champi** had**weakened** from peak sustained winds of 298 km/h earlier in the week to**155 km/h** as it approached Taiwan. Authorities**evacuated nearly 1,300 people** from vulnerable mountainous areas and urged coastal residents to stay indoors and avoid dangerous roads.

The storm's centre was **forecast to remain offshore** before moving north**towards Japan's southwestern islands**, where one person was injured and nearly 2,000 homes lost power on Ishigaki. The** Philippines also suspended ferry services and flights** in affected areas, while residents of Cagayan province were evacuated as a precaution.
