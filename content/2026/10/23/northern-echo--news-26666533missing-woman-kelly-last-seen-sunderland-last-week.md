Police are appealing for help to find a Sunderland woman who has been missing for a week.

Kelly, 45, was last seen near Kayll Road in Sunderland city centre at around 4am on Friday, October 16.

Northumbria Police said extensive enquiries have been ongoing since she disappeared in an effort to locate her.

Officers say they are growing increasingly concerned for Kelly's welfare and are asking anyone who may have seen her or knows where she is to come forward as soon as possible.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21625969.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kelly *(Image: Northumbria Police)*

Kelly is 5ft 4in tall, of medium build and has shoulder-length blonde hair.

She was last seem wearing a black or navy bomber-style jacket, light blue jeans with a hole in the right knee and a black, long-sleeved jumper.

She was also wearing Nike Air Max 270 trainers with a red or orange heel.

Anyone with information about Kelly’s whereabouts is asked to contact Northumbria Police.

Information can be shared with Northumbria Police through its website's live chat and report forms, via social media, or by calling 101.
