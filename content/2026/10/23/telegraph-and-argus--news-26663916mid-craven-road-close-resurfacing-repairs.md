A road to the west of Skipton that is subject to flooding is set to be closed for resurfacing.

The narrow Nappa Road, off the A682 from Stansfield Farm to Stainton Hall, West Marton, is likely to be closed from Tuesday, October 27 to Friday, October 30.

Work, by North Yorkshire Council, will also include repairs to the road, is scheduled to take place from 7am to 5.30pm.

A seven-mile diversion will be in place.
