## How to redeem Genshin Impact codes

You'll need to reach Adventure Rank 10 to redeem your Genshin livestream codes, but this is relatively easy to achieve if you've just started playing. Once done, there are two methods to claim your codes.

The first is through the game:

* Launch Genshin Impact
* Open the in-game menu
* Select Settings and then Account
* Click Redeem Now and paste a code into the box
* Claim your Primogems from the in-game mailbox

Or you can redeem codes online through the redemption website:

* Navigate to the Genshin Impact code redemption website
* Enter your info and select character region
* Place a code in the box and click redeem
* Grab your rewards via the in-game mailbox
