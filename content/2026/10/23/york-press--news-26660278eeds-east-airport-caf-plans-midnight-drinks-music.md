The café at Leeds East Airport is seeking to extend its opening hours.

The proposed hours are from 9am to 12.30am daily, with alcohol sales until midnight.

The café also seeks to serve late-night refreshments from 11pm to midnight and provide live music from 9am to midnight.

The application has been available for viewing from October 17.

Anyone wishing to comment on the application can contact North Yorkshire Council's licensing team via email at [licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk](mailto:licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk) or by post at Selby Civic Centre, Doncaster Road, Selby, YO8 9FT, by November 13.
