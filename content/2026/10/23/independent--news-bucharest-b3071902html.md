Models in wheelchairs took center stage at a Romanian fashion show promoting inclusion.

Atypic Beauty paired models who use wheelchairs with models who don't to showcase Romanian designer Catalin Botezatu’s creations at Bucharest’s Palace of Parliament for an event aiming to challenge stereotypes and promote equal opportunities for people with disabilities.
