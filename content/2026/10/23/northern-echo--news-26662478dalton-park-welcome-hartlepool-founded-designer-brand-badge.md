A new luxury fashion store is set to open this weekend.

BADGE, an independent North East retailer, will launch at Dalton Park Outlet & Outdoors tomorrow (Saturday, October 24).

Shoppers will enjoy an extra 10 per cent off outlet prices during the opening weekend, along with a live DJ, refreshments, and a first look at the brand’s AW26 collections.

Richard Kaye, centre manager at Dalton Park Outlet & Outdoors, said: "BADGE is a fantastic new addition to Dalton Park and one we know our shoppers will be excited to discover.

"The store will introduce a selection of highly sought-after designer names that have not previously been available here, including Stone Island, C.P. Company, Moose Knuckles and Sandbanks.

"We are looking forward to giving BADGE a warm welcome and inviting shoppers to join us for what promises to be a brilliant opening weekend."

Originally founded in Hartlepool, BADGE has built a loyal following for its curated selection of premium designer menswear, as well as womenswear and kidswear.

The new store will offer designer labels such as Stone Island, C.P. Company, Lacoste, BOSS, Armani, Sandbanks, and Moose Knuckles - all at outlet prices.

Ian Jobling, CEO and founder of BADGE Clothing, said: "We are really looking forward to bringing BADGE to Dalton Park.

"The new store will be packed with the designer names our customers know and love, alongside fresh pieces to discover for the season ahead.

"As a North East brand, it is especially exciting to open at a destination with such a strong regional following."
