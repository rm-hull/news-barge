Comedy and cabaret show Stamptown will return to Edinburgh this winter as part of the city’s Christmas programme.

The show, led by comedian and Netflix performer Zach Zucker, will run at the Palais du Variété in Bristo Square from December 3 to 19.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21499008.jpg?type=mds-article-620)La Clique Noël at the Palais du Variété, Bristo Square. *(Image: Craig Sugden)*

Known for its mix of comedy, clowning and variety acts, Stamptown began at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe a decade ago and has since developed a following for its unpredictable late-night performances.

The show features a rotating cast of comedians and variety performers, with Zucker appearing as his alter ego Jack Tucker, described as an overconfident character whose performances frequently spiral out of control.

Zucker also directs and executive produces the show. Jonny Woolley and Dylan Woodley are executive producers.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21624592.jpg?type=mds-article-620)La Clique Noël at the Palais du Variété, Bristo Square. *(Image: Daniel Boud)*

Previous performers have included Jordan Gray and Mr Cardboard.

Stamptown joins the previously announced La Clique Noël at Bristo Square, adding another late-night option to Edinburgh’s festive entertainment programme.

The show will run from Wednesday to Sunday at 10pm, with doors opening at 9.30pm. Tickets start at £26.50.

Also joining the Bristo Square programme is *The Big Christmas Show*, a family-friendly production combining magic and circus acts from the creators of Fringe show* 1 Hour of Insane Magic*.

The events are produced by Unique Assembly, a partnership between Edinburgh-based companies Unique Events and Assembly Festival. The partnership secured the contract to deliver Edinburgh’s Christmas and Hogmanay celebrations on behalf of the City of Edinburgh Council in 2024, with the agreement running through 2028-29.

The Big Christmas Show will take place on December 5, 6, 12, 13 and 21 to 24 at 2pm, with tickets starting at £16.50.

Edinburgh’s Christmas runs from November 14 to January 3, with tickets for the shows available at edwinterfest.com.
