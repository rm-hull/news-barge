FOOTBALL fans in Bradford can enjoy a free fifth drink every Sunday thanks to a new pub promotion.

The ‘Straight Through Crew’ offer is available at selected Proper Pubs, part of Admiral Taverns, and includes the Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn in Bradford.

Customers who pre-pay for four of the same drink—either draught or cask pints, or soft drinks—will receive a fifth drink free of charge.

A spokesperson for Admiral Taverns said: "Sunday football has a new fixture, with 31 community pubs across Yorkshire & The Humber launching an offer pairing live action with great value at the bar."

The promotion is designed to encourage groups of friends to enjoy Sunday football together in a relaxed, social environment.

Stamp cards will be issued at the bar and marked off with each pint.

Customers are encouraged to drink responsibly and enjoy the offer at their own pace, with no time restrictions on orders.
