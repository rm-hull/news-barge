Elvis Presley and Nirvana mash-up band Elvana is heading to the North East for a Halloween show.

Elvis-Nirvana tribute band Elvana will play Anarchy Brewery in Heaton, Newcastle, on Saturday, October 31.

Fans are encouraged to dress as Elvis, Kurt Cobain or any rock or horror icon.

Formed in 2014, Elvana reworks Nirvana songs through an Elvis impersonator persona in what the band calls a "respectful tribute" to both artists.

The group has performed at Glastonbury and Download Festival.

A special ale named Suspicious Never Minds will be available on the night in both keg and can.

Other acts include New York metal band Car Bomb on Sunday, November 8, followed by Dub Pistols on Friday, November 13.

Thin Lizzy frontman Ricky Warwick and the Fighting Hearts play on November 14, followed by Californian artist Gvllow on November 18.

The following night, Portrayal of Guilt will fuse black, death and nu-metal with crust, screamo, powerviolence and hardcore.

Seventies Canadian metal band Anvil, who influenced Megadeth, Slayer, Anthrax and Metallica, will play on November 20.

New York band Handsome Jack will play boogie, soul and rock 'n' roll on Sunday, November 22, before Canadian five-piece The Commoners play on Wednesday, November 25.

Tribute acts These Smiths and The Sound of Joy Division will play on November 27, followed by UK rock band Kira Mac on November 28.

The Elvana Halloween Special runs from 7pm to 11pm and is open only to people aged 14 and over.

Tickets cost £34.80 and are available through Anarchy Brew Co’s events page.

Anarchy Brewery, known for ales including Wor Beer and Shearer’s 260, hosts alternative acts from around the world.

A spokesperson said: "The venue, which produces some of the region’s best loved ales — such as Wor Beer and Shearer’s 260 — is renowned for attracting some of the best alternative live bands on the global music scene."
