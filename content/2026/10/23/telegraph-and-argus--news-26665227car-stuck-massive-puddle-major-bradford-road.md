A car is stuck in a large puddle that has emerged on a Bradford road.

The T&A has visited Wilmer Road in Heaton, near Lister Park, this afternoon.

Several cars have been spotted struggling around the water.

There has been heavy rainfall throughout the district today.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21624054.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The size of the puddle *(Image: Newsquest)*

## How has it been caused?

The Telegraph & Argus understands the problem has been caused by a drain blockage.

One resident said: "I live locally, it's never happened before, I've lived here for 40 odd years.

"I've had to wade through it, it's about a foot deep'

"It's the drains that are blocked. I can't even go home now."
