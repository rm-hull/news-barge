* **Nacon reveals its officially licensed Xbox controller, the Revolution Pro X – Special Edition**
* **The controller design is inspired by*Hell Is Us* and is now available for £109.99 / $129.90 / €129.99**
* **It combines mechanical fine-tuning for all playstyles and tournament-grade precision**

Nacon has announced the Revolution Pro X – Special Edition, its new officially licensed Xbox wireless controller inspired by Rogue Factor's action-adventure game, *Hell Is Us*.

Revolution Pro X – Special Edition is designed for competitive gaming and combines mechanical tuning and tournament-grade precision, allowing players on PC, Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, Xbox One, and Smart TVs to customize controls to suit their playstyle.

Available now at NaconGaming for £109.99 / $129.90 / €129.99, the jet-black controller features a minimalist whorl design inspired by *Hell Is Us* and features contactless Hall Effect joysticks and instant triggers equipped with micro-switches "without mechanical wear," plus two programmable shortcut buttons on the back.

It also offers four vibration motors, two programmable rear buttons, a 2.4 GHz wireless dongle, and a 3 m detachable braided USB-C cable.

![REVOLUTION PRO X – SPECIAL EDITION | LAUNCH TRAILER - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/xE3Zj_xrydU/maxresdefault.jpg)

"Its control ring lets you adjust the mechanical tension of the joysticks with a single gesture to prioritize either fast movements or precise aiming, depending on the situation," Nacon said.

When connected to a PC, it offers a wired mode with a 1,000 Hz polling rate and matches a claimed latency of 1 ms, prioritizing responsiveness in fast-paced games like first-person shooters.

"With the Revolution Pro X – SE, we wanted to offer a full-featured precision controller at a competitive price,” says Yannick Allaert, director of Nacon's accessories division.
