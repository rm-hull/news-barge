The A1(M) motorway will be closed overnight in parts for maintenance work.

National Highways has announced that the road will be shut between Junction 42 (Selby Fork) and Junction 41 (Holmfield Interchange) to carry out Department for Transport traffic stats site maintenance works.

The closure will be in place on Thursday, 29 October 2026, from 9pm until 5am the following morning.

During this time, the A1(M) southbound carriageway, the southbound entry slip road at Junction 42, and the link road connecting the A1(M) southbound carriageway to the M62 eastbound and westbound carriageways will be closed.

The total length of the closure will be a distance of 8,100 metres.

During the closures, suitably signed alternative routes will be available at all times.

Traffic management will be in force 24 hours a day, with signs indicating the extent of the prohibitions (which will not apply to emergency service vehicles or vehicles being used in connection with the said works, to anyone engaged in the said works, or for winter maintenance or traffic officer purposes).

This order, coming into effect on October 28, 2026, has a maximum duration of 18 months.

If you have any questions or require further information about this notice, you can contact Michael Morgan.
