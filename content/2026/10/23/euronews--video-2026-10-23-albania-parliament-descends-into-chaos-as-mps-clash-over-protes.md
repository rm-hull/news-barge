**MPs pushed and shoved** one another,**tore microphones from desks** and**threw objects** across the chamber.**Opposition members blocked the podium** and banged on desks in an attempt to disrupt proceedings, while other lawmakers continued voting on legislation by raising their cards. Footage showed**overturned chairs** and repeated confrontations between rival politicians as tensions escalated inside the assembly.

The clashes unfolded against the backdrop of **student demonstrations** that have**gripped the Albanian capital for ten consecutive days**. Opposition lawmakers held up photographs of** students reportedly injured by police**during earlier protests, expressing** solidarity with the demonstrators**.

Outside parliament, crowds of students and supporters **gathered behind police cordons**, with some protesters** pulling down security barriers**. Several university professors also joined the demonstration. Protesters shouted that the** state should serve the people** rather than those in power, as the unrest spilled from the streets into the country's political institutions.
