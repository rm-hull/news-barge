A NIGHT of boxing will raise funds for men’s mental health, children’s hospice care, and grassroots sport.

The event, organised by Wibsey Warriors ARLFC, will take place on October 31 at the Cedar Court Hotel in Bradford and will feature more than 22 fighters from boxing clubs across West Yorkshire.

A spokesperson for Wibsey Warriors ARLFC said: "We would love the opportunity to share our story and raise awareness of this exciting event, which is about much more than just a night of boxing.

"We believe this event is a fantastic example of our local community coming together to support children's opportunities in grassroots sport, men's mental health and families who need specialist hospice care."

A percentage of event ticket sales and raffle proceeds will be donated to Andy's Man Club, a men’s suicide prevention charity, and Martin House Children’s Hospice, which supports children with life-shortening conditions and their families across Yorkshire.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/17654231.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A percentage of event ticket sales and raffle proceeds will be donated to Andy's Man Club, a men’s suicide prevention charity *(Image: Newsquest)*

Funds raised will also help the Wibsey Warriors rugby club.

The spokesperson said: "At Wibsey Warriors ARLFC, we believe rugby is about much more than what happens on the pitch.

"It's about bringing people together, building friendships, supporting one another and giving children opportunities to thrive.

"We never want finances to be the reason a child misses out on being part of our rugby family."

Money raised will go towards equipment, team wear, bonding activities, and keeping rugby affordable and accessible.

The event has drawn wide community support, with raffle prizes donated by businesses and supporters.

To build anticipation, a Match-Up Night will be held on October 23 at 6pm at Wibsey Sports Village on Northfield Road.

Attendees will meet the fighters and learn more about the causes being supported.

Anyone wishing to support the event or attend the Match-Up Night can contact Wibsey Warriors ARLFC for more information.
