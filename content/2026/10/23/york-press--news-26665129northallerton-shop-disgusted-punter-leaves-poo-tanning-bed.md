A salon says that it's been left "absolutely disgusted" and "appalled" claiming one of its clients left staff to clean up excrement from walls and a tanning bed.

Equinox Tanning Studio, in Garthway Arcade in Northallerton, issued a statement on social media on Tuesday (October 20), banning the customer.

The tanning studio said: "Customer announcement - to the client who took a poo in bed one today, picked it up and threw it behind the bed, do not bother returning."

The business then said that its staff members had to clean excrement off the walls, bed and plug, adding: "Never did I ever think someone could behave like this in our shop."
