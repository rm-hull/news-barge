## The York cheese shop with a national following that has scooped a top honour

Phillipa Symington, from Clive Owen, congratulates Jordan and Cordelia Hobbs, from Love Cheese which won Food Retailer of the Year.

An independent York businesses has been named Food Retailer of the Year for its passion for artisan produce, innovative customer experiences and exceptional customer service.

Love Cheese, on Gillygate, impressed judges of The Press Food and Drink Awards for its unique approach to food retail, combining outstanding products with memorable tasting experiences and genuine Yorkshire hospitality.

What began as an independent cheesemonger has grown into a thriving destination business attracting locals, tourists and online customers from across the UK, while remaining true to the personal service and welcoming atmosphere that helped build its reputation.

The awards were held on Monday, October 5, at the National Railway Museum as a huge celebration of the talent, innovation and diversity within the area's food and drink sector.

![ Jordan and Cordelia Hobbs, from Love Cheese, centre, celebrating their win with the team from Bar Hashery Bakery.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546316.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Jordan and Cordelia Hobbs, from Love Cheese, centre, celebrating their win with the team from Bar Hashery Bakery.

Judges praised the commitment to quality, with every cheese, wine, beer and accompaniment carefully selected for its flavour, provenance and craftsmanship.

Customers are encouraged to explore products through tastings, recommendations and hands-on experiences that make artisan food approachable and enjoyable.

Innovation also stood out. Love Cheese became widely known for its virtual cheese and wine tastings, which connected tens of thousands of customers across the UK and beyond. The success of those events helped the business expand beyond traditional retail, creating interactive food experiences that introduced artisan cheese to a much wider audience.

The business has continued to build on that momentum with popular in-person tastings, Afternoon Cheese events and collaborations with breweries, distilleries and wine experts. Judges also highlighted the success of its Speakcheesey venues, which have become popular destinations for relaxed cheese and wine dining in York.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546315.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Meet the winners of The Press Food and Drink Awards 2026.

Love Cheese was recognised for championing Yorkshire producers and playing an important role in the city's food, hospitality and tourism sectors. Its commitment to supporting local businesses and creating memorable experiences has earned it a loyal following.

The award comes after the business successfully navigated rising costs, supply chain pressures and the challenge of expanding into a multi-site operation while maintaining the quality and personality customers value.

Looking ahead, Love Cheese plans to expand its programme of tasting events, strengthen partnerships with local producers and continue creating unforgettable food and drink experiences.

Judges said the winner exemplifies the very best of independent retail, combining expertise, innovation and hospitality to create a truly unique customer experience.

![Clive Owen sponsored the award.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546413.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Food Retailer of the Year Award was sponsored by Clive Owen LLP. *(Image: The Press)*

These brand-new awards celebrate the exceptional talent, flavour and creativity that make York and surrounding area one of the UK’s most exciting food and drink destinations.

Food and drink broadcaster Nigel Barden, who champions great taste, provenance and craftsmanship, hosted the event and introduced each category.
