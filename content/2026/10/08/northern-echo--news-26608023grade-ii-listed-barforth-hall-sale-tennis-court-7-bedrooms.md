A Grade II listed country house with a tennis court, orchard gardens and a three-bedroom annexe is on the market in County Durham.

Barforth Hall in Winston, a historic detached property forming part of a larger estate, is being marketed as either a substantial family home or a boutique bed and breakfast thanks to its flexible layout and extensive accommodation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550549.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550518.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550517.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

The main house has four double bedrooms, all en suite, while the annexe adds three more - ideal for multi-generational living, guests or a potential holiday let, subject to permission.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550529.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550526.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

The traditional front door opens into a grand entrance hall with a sweeping staircase.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550544.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550545.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

Period features include wooden sash windows, exposed beams, wood panelling and inglenook fireplaces fitted with multi-fuel stoves.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550535.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550533.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

The country-style kitchen has granite worktops, a double French Butler sink and a cream Aga set in a brick inglenook.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550531.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

There is also a formal dining room, a spacious dual-aspect living room, a second sitting room, study area, garden room and utility space.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550534.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550532.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

The principal bedroom has a walk-in wardrobe and a large en-suite bathroom with a freestanding bath overlooking the gardens.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550525.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: BARFORTH HALL)*

The annexe has its own entrance and an open-plan kitchen, dining and living area with folding doors onto the garden.

A spiral staircase leads to three bedrooms, each with an en-suite bathroom.

Outside, lawns surround the hall, with apple and plum trees, mature planting and sheltered courtyards that catch the sun throughout the day.

A full-sized all-weather tennis court is another standout feature, while a group of outbuildings offers extra space.

The property is up for offers over £795,000.
