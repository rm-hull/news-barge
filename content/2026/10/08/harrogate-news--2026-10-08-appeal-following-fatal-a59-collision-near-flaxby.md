North Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage  
 following a fatal collision that occurred on the A59 near Flaxby earlier on 7 October 2027.

The collision happened at approximately 3.30pm and involved a black  
 Vauxhall Corsa which was travelling towards Harrogate.

Sadly, the female driver of the Corsa was pronounced dead at the scene.  
 Our thoughts are with her loved ones at this very difficult time.

The road remains closed while specialist collision investigators carry  
 out examinations, and it is expected to remain closed for some time.  
 Motorists are advised to avoid the area and use alternative routes  
 where possible.

Police are particularly keen to hear from anyone who saw the collision,  
 or the Corsa in the moments leading up to it, or who has dashcam footage  
 that may have captured something.

Officers are also keen to speak with the driver of a white HGV that was  
 travelling in the opposite direction at the time, as they may have seen  
 something that could help with enquiries.

## How to provide information

If you can help, please contact North Yorkshire Police using one of the following methods:

* **Live Chat:**  
  Available via the North Yorkshire Police website, Monday to Friday,  
  9am to 5pm.
* **Telephone:**  
  If you are unable to use these options, call North Yorkshire Police
