Police are searching for a driver and passenger who fled the scene after a car collided with a parked car.

The incident happened at around 5pm on Tuesday (October 6) on Hastings Close, Thornaby, where a silver Peugeot 207 crashed into a parked car.

During the incident, the Peugeot reportedly overturned.

Cleveland Police are looking for the driver of the vehicle, reported to be a man in his 40s, and the passenger, a teenage girl, who left the scene of the incident.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said: "We received a report that a silver Peugeot 207 had collided with a parked car in Hastings Close, then overturned.

"Enquiries are ongoing to trace the occupants of the vehicle, reported to be a man in his 40s and a teenage girl, who did not remain at the scene.

"Anyone with information is asked to call 101, quoting reference 200476."
