A free family theatre show is coming to Ripon Library this October half-term.

Award-winning York theatre company Next Door But One will perform *The Firework-Maker’s Daughter* on Thursday 29th October at 10.30am.

Based on the children’s story by Philip Pullman, the production follows Lila as she travels across mountains and lakes, meeting talking elephants and magical creatures while learning to follow her dreams.

The performance includes puppetry, Makaton and plenty of opportunities for children to join in.

Ripon is one of six North Yorkshire libraries hosting the production, with performances funded by Arts Council England. The tour will also visit Selby, Tadcaster, Northallerton, Scarborough and Pickering.

North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for culture, arts and housing, Cllr Simon Myers, said:

> “We are delighted to be able to offer families the opportunity to experience high-quality live theatre free of charge in their local library.
>
> “By bringing performances into communities across North Yorkshire, we can help remove barriers such as travel and ticket costs, ensuring more children and families can enjoy the magic of live theatre.
>
> “Libraries are places of discovery, creativity and connection and we hope these performances will inspire children to explore new stories, spark their imagination and create lasting memories.”

Spaces are limited and advance booking is required. Families are encouraged to contact Ripon Library by phone or email as soon as possible to reserve their places.
