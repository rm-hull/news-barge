Emergency services were called in to help rescue an injured person from a York field.

It happened at about **10.37am today** off**Monk Avenue** in Heworth.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York crews were requested by an ambulance crew to assist them with a casualty with a broken ankle in a field.

#### Monk Avenue, York

“Crews assisted in carrying the casualty from the field into the ambulance.”
