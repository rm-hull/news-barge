A popular local cover band is set to return to their home city.

Scapegoat, known for their energetic performances, will play at the White Horse in Bootham on October 24.

The band, which began as a hobby, has become a favourite in North Yorkshire, performing numerous gigs throughout the year.

Their shows are known for their lively atmosphere, with the band often interacting with the crowd.

Entry to the gig is free.

Scapegoat's performance comes as part of their busy schedule, which shows no sign of slowing down.

The band has been praised for providing the same energy and experience as high-priced concerts.

Fans have been encouraged to support the band and enjoy their homecoming performance.
