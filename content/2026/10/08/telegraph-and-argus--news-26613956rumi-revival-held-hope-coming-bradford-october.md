A global celebration of poetry, music and spirituality is coming to Bradford.

Rumi Revival: Held by Hope will take place at St George’s Hall on Monday, October 12, 2026, marking the event’s first appearance in the city.

The night promises a powerful blend of devotional music, poetry, storytelling and a performance by the Whirling Dervishes.

Farkhandah Faraz, organiser and facilitator of the Bradford event, said: "We are delighted to be bringing Rumi Revival to Bradford for the very first time.

"Rumi’s message of hope, love and spiritual connection is as relevant today as it was centuries ago, the evening will speak to people from all backgrounds.

"Bradford is a city rich in culture, faith and creativity, making it the perfect place to host this celebration.

"We hope people leave feeling inspired, uplifted and connected to something greater than themselves."

The event will honour the life and legacy of 13th-century poet Jalal al-Din Rumi, whose works continue to inspire audiences worldwide.

The programme will feature sacred recitation, devotional music, poetry and storytelling, promising an immersive experience.

Performers include Bosnian singer Armin Muzaferija, scholar Ustadh Feraidoon Mojadedi, vocalist Mikhaael Mala, Shaykh Shuayr, The Sufi Ensemble and the Whirling Dervishes of Bosnia.

Tickets are available at [https://www.bradford-theatres.co.uk/whats-on/rumi-revival-held-by-hope.](https://www.bradford-theatres.co.uk/whats-on/rumi-revival-held-by-hope.)
