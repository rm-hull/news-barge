York Theatre Royal are set to co-produce a new actor-musician production of *Sunset Boulevard* in 2027.

Andrew Lloyd Webber’s iconic musical will be reimagined in an intimate new production, opening at York Theatre Royal on **Wednesday 9 June to Saturday 26 June 2027**.

Directed by Lotte Wakeham, artistic director of Octagon Theatre Bolton, the musical will be performed by a company of actor-musicians in a staging that places the music, storytelling and audience closer than ever before.

The production celebrates Andrew Lloyd Webber’s beloved score, including *With One Look*,* As If We Never Said Goodbye*and* Sunset Boulevard*.

The production is the first new professional staging of *Sunset Boulevard* to be created by a theatre in the North of England.

It is a co-production between York Theatre Royal, Octagon Theatre Bolton and Royal & Derngate Northampton. The final performances will be in York.

Silent film star Norma Desmond longs for the spotlight that once made her a legend. When struggling screenwriter Joe Gillis enters her secluded world, hope soon gives way to obsession as their lives become entwined in a tragic search for love, success and belonging.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Director-Lotte-Wakeham-2.webp)

Lotte Wakeham, director said: “*Sunset Boulevard* is a story about ambition, reinvention and the price of chasing the spotlight. Andrew Lloyd Webber’s extraordinary score is full of drama and longing, and staging the show with actor-musicians gives us the chance to make every musical moment feel immediate and alive.

“I’m excited to create a brand-new production which embraces that intimacy, drawing people into Norma and Joe’s world and allowing them to experience this iconic musical from a fresh perspective – literally in ‘close up’.

“It is also a privilege to make the first ever new professional production of Sunset Boulevard created by a theatre in the North of England, alongside our brilliant partners at Royal & Derngate and York Theatre Royal.”

Paul Crewes, chief executive of York Theatre Royal said: “It is very exciting for us to be co-producing the spectacular musical *Sunset Boulevard* by Andrew Lloyd Webber.

“We will be partnering with our friends at Octagon Theatre and Royal & Derngate for the first time to create a reimagined production of this classic story. We look forward to bringing this show to our stage in June 2027.”
