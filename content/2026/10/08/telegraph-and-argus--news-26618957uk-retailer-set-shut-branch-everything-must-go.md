A long-time Bradford shoe shop is set to shut its doors.

Wynsors World of Shoes, which has been located at Avenue Retail Park, off Tong Street, for around 20 years is having a closing down sale.

Signs at the store say “All stock reduced” and “Everything must go”.

Wynsors has more than 45 shops across the UK and sells brands including Adidas, Skechers, Clarks, Timberland, Nike and Crocs.

The move follows a decision by the chain to shut 17 shops across the UK.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21554513.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Signs advertise a closing down sale at the Wynsors store in Tong

## Other Bradford store

Closing-down sales have already begun at several affected branches.

Bradford’s other Wynsors shop, on Thornton Road, is staying open.

Wynsors has not announced when the Tong shop will close.

## What Wynsors have said

In a previous statement, Wynsors Shoes said: “It’s with sadness that we announce that certain Wynsors stores will be closing their doors. We’d like to say a huge thank you to all of our customers who have shopped with us and supported the store over the years. Your support has meant so much to the whole team.”

The Telegraph & Argus has asked Wynsors for comment on the Tong branch’s closure plans.
