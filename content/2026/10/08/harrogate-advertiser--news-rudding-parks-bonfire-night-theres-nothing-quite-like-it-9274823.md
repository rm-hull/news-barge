# Rudding Park's bonfire night: "There's nothing quite like it"

## This year's Rudding Park bonfire on Saturday, November 7 is set to be the biggest yet

A spectacular bonfire night is to return to a leading Harrogate hotel next month.

![Rudding Park bonfire and fireworks](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/08/11/23/RuddingBonfireNight.jpeg?trim=215,0,145,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


This year's extravaganza at Rudding Park on Saturday, November 7 is set to be the biggest yet for the family-owned, award-winning luxury resort and hotel.

The evening will open with a Family Fire & LED Show with walkabout characters roaming the crowd and face painters on hand.

Then the bonfire will be lit and all eyes will turn skyward for a spectacular firework display set to music.

![Independently-owned Rudding Park is located on the edge of Harrogate at Wetherby Road ](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjRjM2RiNjMxLWNkMmUtNDM1ZC04NzA3LTQyOTRiY2RmMGY5MTo5MzdkYjNlNi1lODdkLTQxZmUtOWE3Ni0yYTM2Y2JlZWI4N2M=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


5pm: Gates open.

5.45-6.15pm: Family Fire & LED Show, walkabout characters and face painters.

6.30pm: Bonfire lighting.

7pm: Fireworks.

It was in 1972 that the Mackaness family purchased Rudding Park and began the transformation of 2,000 acres of outstanding countryside into a luxury hotel, spa, golf and conference resort.
