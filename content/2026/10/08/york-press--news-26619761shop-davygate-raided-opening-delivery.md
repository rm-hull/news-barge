## Shop in York targeted in alleged burglary during early-morning delivery

Police have launched an appeal following the alleged burglary of a shop in Davygate, York *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Police have launched an appeal after the alleged burglary of a shop in York city centre.

The incident happened in Davygate just before 8am on September 22.

A suspect is alleged to have 'tailgated' a delivery, stealing from shelves before the shop was due to open to the public.




Officers believe the person pictured may have information that could help the investigation.

Anyone who recognises the individual is asked to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Information can also be reported by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quoting reference 12260191866 or through Crimestoppers anonymously.
