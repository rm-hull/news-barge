This is the moment a balaclava-clad intruder trashed a much-loved community centre overnight.

Birtley Community Centre was forced to close on Wednesday (October 7) after the break-in around 10pm on Tuesday (October 6).

CCTV shows a person wearing a balaclava kicking in a door during the break-in.

A radiator was ripped from the wall and sweet dispensers knocked over. Northumbria Police said "significant damage" was caused and cash stolen.

In a post shared on Facebook, the centre said: “We apologise for any inconvenience and thank everyone for their understanding."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550444.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

The centre later called the break-in “horrific” and said staff and volunteers were working to reopen.

The post read: “Following the horrific events at Birtley Community Centre last night, we are now working hard to get things back up and running and make sure our centre continues to be a safe and welcoming place for everyone.

“We have a new door for our nursery room that needs hanging. We believe we have everything else needed, we just need someone with the skills to fit it for us.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550443.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Birtley Community Centre has been forced to close. *(Image: SOCIAL MEDIA)*

“If you are able to help, please get in touch.

“After a difficult night, any support from our community would mean a lot.”

A Northumbria Police spokesperson said: “Shortly before 7am today (Wednesday 7), we received a report of burglary at a building on Ravensworth Road in the Birtley area of Gateshead.

“The burglary is believed to have taken place sometime around 10pm the previous night.
