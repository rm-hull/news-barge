Chicos Pizzas & Kebabs has announced plans to open in Harrogate.

The takeaway will serve pizzas, burgers, chicken and kebabs from its new shop on Commercial Street.

The address previously housed 'Chico’s Continental' which was formed in 2019 and went into liquidation earlier this year.

Announcing the opening on social media, a spokesperson for Chicos said:

> “The wait is almost over, Harrogate! Get ready for Chicos Pizzas & Kebabs — serving up delicious pizzas, burgers, chicken & kebabs!”

The new Chicos will be open every day, from 12pm 'till late', but an official opening date has not yet been announced.

*Find out more online at [https://chicospizza.uk/.*](https://chicospizza.uk/.*)
