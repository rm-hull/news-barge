TWO vehicles have crashed on a main road in an East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Marton Road in Bridlington at 2.40pm yesterday (Wednesday, October 7).




A service spokesperson said the crash involved a skip wagon and a car.

The spokesperson added: "An elderly female self-released before the arrival of the fire service.

"Small tools were used to make the vehicle safe.

"The incident was left with the police and ambulance service."
