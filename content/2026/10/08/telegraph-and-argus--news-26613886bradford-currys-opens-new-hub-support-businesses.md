Currys Business is investing in new support for SMEs with the launch of a dedicated Business Hub.

Based at its store in Bradford, the hub opened on September 30 and is designed to serve businesses across Bradford and West Yorkshire.

Luke Cowan, business customer manager at Currys Business Bradford, said: "We're delighted with the success of the Bradford Business Hub launch.

"It has been a pleasure to meet such a wide variety of local businesses and hear so many inspiring stories of success from across the region.

"Bradford is home to a thriving and ambitious business community, and we are excited to help organisations continue to develop, grow, and flourish."

The hub offers face-to-face advice from local experts, flexible payment options and tailored tech solutions.

It also includes an introductory offer of five per cent off for new business customers until December 22, 2026.

Full terms and conditions are available on the Currys Business website.
