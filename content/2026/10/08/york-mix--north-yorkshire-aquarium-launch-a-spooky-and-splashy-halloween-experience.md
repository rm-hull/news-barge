A North Yorkshire aquarium is turning into an *Ascarium* this October.

The Sea Witch has returned to Sea Life in Scarborough, and it’s up to you to help the fellow sea creatures by completing magical challenges throughout the aquarium.

Will you be brave enough to step up and help them by solving the spooky and splashy challenges and fill in your activity sheet as you go?

The special autumn event includes photo opportunities, an activity trail, games and activities, and a reward for completing the challenge!

**Ascarium** is at Sea Life Scarborough from**Sunday 4 October to Sunday 1 November**.

The Halloween event is included in admission to Sea Life in Scarborough, and there’s so much to explore while you’re there.

You can look forward to seeing the cheeky colony of Humboldt penguins that reside on Penguin Island and get closer than ever before as you make your way through their walk-through home.

Then dive into an amazing underwater world and take a fascinating journey to the ocean depths as you discover some amazing creatures…

Don’t forget the incredible 200,000l Ocean Tunnel either, you might even come face-to-fin with Blacktip Reef Sharks! Or why not head on a Rainforest Adventure, where you’ll find a world of creepy crawlies, lurking lizards and curious critters.
