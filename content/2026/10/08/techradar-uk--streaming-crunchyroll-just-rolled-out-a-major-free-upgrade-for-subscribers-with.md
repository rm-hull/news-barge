* **Crunchyroll Manga has rolled out to subscribers in the UK and Australia**
* **It's available as part of the Crunchyroll Ultimate plan, or as a separate add-on**
* **The service includes access to hundreds of titles, including some big hits**

Crunchyroll Ultimate subscribers in the UK and Australia just got a major free upgrade as the popular anime streaming service rolls out its manga (Japanese comic book) offering to more English-speaking regions worldwide.

Crunchyroll Manga, which launched in the US in October last year, offers Ultimate members ad-free access to more than 250 localized manga titles from major publishers like J-Novel Club, Square Enix, and Yen Press.

This includes everything from big hits like *The Apothecary Diaries, My Dress-Up Darling, Bocchi the Rock!, and Bungo Stray Dogs* all the way to more niche picks such as the hilariously wordily titled*Rejected by the Hero's Party, a Princess Decided to Live a Quiet Life in the Countryside* or*My Instant Death Ability Is So Overpowered, No One in This Other World Stands a Chance Against Me!*.

Crunchyroll Ultimate costs $17.99 / £9.99 a month (we're waiting for confirmation on which Australia tier it'll be included in). Crunchryoll Manga is also available as a separate add-on for those subscribed to the cheaper Fan or Mega Fan tiers.

## An exciting lineup

There's plenty of good stuff in the lineup to justify that cost, and I'm particularly excited about reading through the two volumes of *Re:Zero — Starting Life in Another World* available on the service, having greatly enjoyed the most recent seasons of the anime adaptation.

There are some noticeable omissions — for example, it's a bit odd not to see *Black Butler* on offer, given that Crunchyroll has the global license for the anime adaptation. But the service promises that more titles are on the way soon.

It's accessible as a standalone mobile app via the Google Play Store on Android and the App Store on iOS, or in your web browser with a dedicated tab on the Crunchyroll website. Key features include the ability to read offline and browse both personalized reading lists and curated manga recommendations.
