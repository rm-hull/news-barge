Harrogate footballer Louie Philpott has signed his first professional contract with Leeds United.

The former St John Fisher pupil has agreed a two-year deal, keeping him at the club’s Thorp Arch academy until 2028.

The left-back progressed into the Under-21s towards the end of last season and scored his first Premier League 2 goal against Manchester United.

A spokesperson for St John Fisher said:

> "Louie follows in a rich tradition of successful footballers to pass through our school.
>
> "Well done Louie! Best wishes from all of us at SJF."

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ac73cc13ba56918f14.jpg)

Louie had already spent six years in Leeds United’s academy, progressing through the Under-16s and earning an appearance for the Under-18s.

A Leeds United spokesperson said:

> “Everyone at Leeds United would like to congratulate Louie on signing a first professional deal."
