Fancy a coffee with a side of alpaca? A new cafe in northern Serbia is inviting visitors to do just that.

The Alpaca Cafe, at the Tiganjica Ethno-Village resort near Stajićevo, north of Belgrade, is home to six adorable alpacas, each with their own unique personality – including Ljubica, the resident grump, and Mona, the largest of the group.

For around €10, visitors can enjoy a drink, spend time with the animals and feed them cafe-approved treats including carrots, lettuce and apples. However, guests must follow a few rules to keep the animals happy, including not approaching them from behind.
