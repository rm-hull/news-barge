A North East logistics firm has hired a regional marketing agency to boost its profile as its e-commerce fulfilment business grows.

TSP Fulfilment appointed Karol Marketing Group to lead its PR and communications after a competitive pitch.

The agency will support TSP Fulfilment’s UK growth plans.

Stefan Lepkowski, managing director of Karol Marketing Group, said: "TSP Fulfilment has built an exceptional operation in the logistics space, and we’re proud to have been chosen to drive their communications.

"Securing this account underlines our expanding presence in the sector and strengthens our footprint here in the North East."

Based in the North East, TSP Fulfilment handles B2B and B2C e-commerce fulfilment for large and mid-sized clients.

It operates more than 400,000 square feet of warehouse space and provides global logistics from start to finish.

The agency’s early work secured media coverage for the Alan Shearer Foundation’s limited-edition hoodie campaign.

The coverage highlighted TSP Fulfilment’s role in running the charity’s e-commerce and logistics operation.

Paul Griffiths, CEO at TSP Fulfilment, said: "Appointing Karol Marketing Group was a natural step for us as we enter our next phase of ambitious growth.

"They understood our business from the outset and what we need, and they share our commitment of collaboration.

"We’ve already seen firsthand what they can deliver and are confident that Karol will be brilliant partners in raising our profile, demonstrating our end-to-end capabilities and reaching our goals."

Karol will raise TSP Fulfilment’s profile as the North East’s largest independent fulfilment partner for fast-growing online brands.
