The body of a man has been found in a North Yorkshire wood.

North Yorkshire Police joined the local coastguard near to Scalby Beck in Scarborough on the morning of Thursday (October 8) following reports that a member of the public had seen a body.

An eye-witness said officers had blocked all paths around the site.




A police spokesperson said: "Officers attended the scene where they sadly found the body of a man.

"There are not believed to be any suspicious circumstances surrounding the man’s death and a report will be prepared for a future inquest into his death."
