A garden centre is transforming into a winter wonderland.

Mirfield Garden Centre, part of the British Garden Centres group, will launch its Christmas department on October 17 and 18 with a weekend of festive fun.

Santa Claus will officially open the Christmas wonderland by cutting the ribbon at 9am on Saturday.

James Hughes, centre manager at Mirfield Garden Centre, said: "Christmas at British Garden Centres is about so much more than decorations; it’s about bringing people together and making the season the best experience for everyone.

"Alongside our expanded Fern Christmas range and a fantastic selection of gifts for all ages, we’re proud to host festive events that families can enjoy year after year.

"Whether you’re shopping for the perfect present, finding inspiration for your home, or making memories, there’s something special for everyone."

The first 50 groups through the doors will receive a golden ticket for a free bauble.

Visitors can enjoy seasonal treats, live festive music and the newly expanded Fern Christmas and Lighting collection, exclusive to British Garden Centres.

Family Card members will receive a 15 per cent discount across the entire Christmas range during the opening weekend.

Bookings are now open for festive dining and Santa experiences.
