A MAN has been charged with murder following a reported fatal collision on the A1(M) in North Yorkshire, police said.

The incident happened at around 6.10pm on Sunday, October 4, on the southbound carriageway near Barton, between Junctions 57 and 56.

North Yorkshire Police said in a statement that emergency services attended the scene, "but sadly a 22-year-old woman from Newcastle was pronounced dead at the scene".




Police said the woman's family continue to be supported by specially trained officers.

A statement from North Yorkshire Police said: "A 31-year-old man from Newcastle who was arrested following a fatal two-vehicle collision on the A1 has been charged with murder.

"He has also been charged with attempted grievous bodily harm (GBH), and dangerous driving.

"A 23-year-old man, who was arrested in connection with the collision, has been released with no further action."

The crash involved a silver Ford Transit and a silver Volkswagen Golf, both travelling southbound, police said.

North Yorkshire Police said it is continuing to appeal for witnesses and information.

A force spokesperson said: "We know there were a number of vehicles travelling on the A1 at the time of the collision, and we would like to thank all those motorists who have already spoken to officers or come forward with information and dashcam footage to assist our investigation.

"However, we are continuing to appeal for witnesses.

"If you saw the collision and have not yet spoken to police, please get in touch.

"Even the smallest detail could prove significant to our enquiries.

"We are also keen to hear from anyone who saw either vehicle in the moments leading up to the collision.

"In particular, we would like to speak to drivers who may have dashcam footage showing either vehicle before the collision, or footage of the collision itself."
