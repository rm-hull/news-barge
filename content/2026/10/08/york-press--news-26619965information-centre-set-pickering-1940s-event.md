## Information centre opens in Pickering ahead of 1940s event

AN Information Centre has been set up ahead of the Pickering 1940s event this week. *(Image: Freelancer)*

AN Information Centre has been set up ahead of the Pickering 1940s event this week.is all set-up and waiting for our 1940s visitors on Smiddy Hill.

The Information Centre is in Smiddy Hill, opposite the green where the monument stands.

A spokesperson for the event said: "Our Programme of Events is on sale here until 4pm and then Friday, Saturday and Sunday 10am to 4pm.

"Programmes will also be on sale from REEDS newsagents on our Market Place all week and at Beck Isle Museum on both Saturday and Sunday."




"With continued thanks to Alba Rose Care Home and Saint Cecilia's Care Group for their sponsorship of the event this year."

Pickering Recreation Club will be offering parking on the cricket field and in the recreation car park across the event at a cost of £10 for a full day, or £5 from 1pm.
