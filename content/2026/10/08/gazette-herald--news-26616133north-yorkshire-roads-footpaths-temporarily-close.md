North Yorkshire Council has announced multiple temporary closures of roads and footpaths due to various works and safety concerns.

Riccal Drive in Helmsley will be closed to vehicles for a week.

The closure will take place between October 12 and December 31, 2026, and is due to necessary roadworks.

Greenland Lane in Kirby Misperton will also be temporarily closed to vehicles due to roadworks.

The closure will take place between October 12, 2026, and February 5, 2027.

Alternative routes will be available for both closures, with signs indicating the new routes.

Pedestrians will also face disruptions, with a public footpath in Ampleforth closed due to an unsafe footbridge.

The closure, starting on October 15, will last for six months, and follows an emergency notice issued earlier.

Similarly, public footpaths in the parishes of Habton and Marishes will be closed due to an unsafe bridge.

The closure, starting on October 14, will also last for six months, following an emergency notice issued on September 23.

All closures will allow access to premises where possible, and signs will indicate the closure periods.

All inquiries regarding the closures can be directed to North Yorkshire Council's Customer Service Centre at 0300 131 2 131.

For inquiries about the Greenland Lane closure, contact United Infrastructure at 07493 983062.

More information can be found on the North Yorkshire Council roadworks map at northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.
