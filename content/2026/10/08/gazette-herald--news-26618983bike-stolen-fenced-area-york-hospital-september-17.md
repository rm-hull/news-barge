A BIKE has been stolen from a fenced area in York Hospital and police are appealing for help.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for help to identify a man after a bike was stolen from York Hospital last month.

They say the incident took place on September 17 after a person had cycled to work and locked her bike inside a fenced compound near the south entrance.
