A LANE along a major road in North Yorkshire has closed following a crash.

Motorists have been warned about increasing traffic delays following a crash on the A1 southbound on Thursday (October 8).




The incident has caused long queues from the A659 Wattle Syke roundabout near Boston Spa to the A1’s junction with the A64, which leads to Tadcaster and York.

At 10.40am, AA Traffic updated road users.

A comment read: “A lane has been closed for the recovery of a vehicle from an earlier accident.”
