Shoppers are being warned to wear gloves and masks when disposing of two recalled Halloween decorations.

The Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) has issued an urgent alert about the potential contamination of the Witch Mini and Pumpkin gonks, both sold by Primark.

Officials say the sand inside the decorations may contain a small amount of asbestos, a banned substance known to pose serious health risks even at low levels of exposure.

An OPSS spokesman said: "The products have been withdrawn from the market and recalled from end users by Primark.

"We recommend owners stop using the product immediately and keep out the reach of children."

Asbestos exposure can lead to life-threatening conditions such as asbestosis, which causes breathing difficulties and can enlarge the heart.

Asbestos-related diseases are believed to cause around 5,000 deaths each year in the UK.

The affected gonks do not comply with the General Product Safety Regulations 2005.

The recalled models are 991174794 (Witch Mini) and 991179148 (Pumpkin), with barcodes 212047125 and 212080261.

Shoppers are being advised to wear gloves and a mask when disposing of the products.

Primark has withdrawn the decorations from sale and issued a recall.

Customers are being told to stop using the products immediately and return them to any Primark store for a full refund.

No proof of purchase is required.

If the product is undamaged, it should be placed in a heavy-duty plastic bag, double taped, clearly labelled, and stored safely out of reach of children until it can be returned.

If the decoration is damaged and the sand filling is exposed, the surface where it was kept should be cleaned with a wet cloth to prevent dust.

Shoppers are then advised to wear gloves and a mask, and place the product, gloves, mask, and cloth in a double bag.
