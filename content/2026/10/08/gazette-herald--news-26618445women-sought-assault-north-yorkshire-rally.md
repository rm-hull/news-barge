TWO woman who were reported to be selling drugs at a North Yorkshire event are being sought after an assault which left the victim unconscious.

The incident took place at the Traction Engine Rally at Scampston Hall near Norton last month.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said the incident occurred at around 10.30pm on Friday, September 4, inside the main tent at the event, near the entrance and exit area where live entertainment and a bar were located.

They said: "It is reported that two females were attempting to sell drugs at the event. When the offer was refused, one of the females picked up a folded chair and struck the victim to the back of the head, causing her to collapse to the ground. A second female, described as being dressed all in black, then punched the victim while she was believed to be unconscious."
