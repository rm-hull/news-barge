A York wedding catering company has said it is "genuinely devastated" as it revealed the business will be entering liquidation.

Dining by Matthew Hyde Ltd, which legally traded as Baba Ganoush – Dining by Matthew Hyde Ltd, will enter liquidation on Friday, October 30, according to a company spokesperson.

They said Baba Ganoush – Dining by Matthew Hyde Ltd ceased trading on Friday, October 2, and has instructed a company to assist with putting the business into liquidation.

Baba Ganoush – Dining by Matthew Hyde Ltd operated for 10 years and undertook catering for weddings, corporates and events.

Social media posts said the company preparing to enter liquidation meant brides no longer had wedding breakfasts to enjoy.

There were also reports that staff had been left out of pocket due to the situation.

The Baba Ganoush – Dining by Matthew Hyde Ltd spokesperson said they would be working with couples, staff, venues and suppliers to make "the necessary arrangements moving forwards".

They told The Press: "We are genuinely devastated for everyone we have been working with – our couples, staff, venues and suppliers.

"Now, we’re working hard with all of them to make the necessary arrangements moving forwards.

"As a family business, Baba Ganoush has been so close to our hearts as we continue to raise our young family.

"We've always aimed for the absolute best for customers, and we find ourselves in a very tough position, as sadly so many hospitality organisations are."

Chris Cox, director at financial and real estate advisory group BTG, said: "BTG is unable to comment at present.

"In the event of a formal appointment, it will take some time to investigate the circumstances and establish the relevant facts before any further comment can be made."

The Press reported in May that Baba Ganoush – Dining by Matthew Hyde Ltd moved from York to the Thorpe Arch Trading Estate.

At the time, the company, previously based in Rose Avenue in York Business Park, said the move to a larger premises would allow it to offer more and better services to a wider area while saving money.

Director Ashley Hyde said in May: "We needed to have more space to broaden our offering. It’s much more economical to be out of York."

The new premises feature a large food preparation area, as well as a dining space and bar area.

"We were spending so much money in York on rent, it became difficult to ensure quality in other areas. We are in a fantastic location, which is reasonably priced, which means we can get the better-quality food," Ms Hyde added.
