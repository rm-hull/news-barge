POLICE have launched an identity appeal after the alleged theft and subsequent use of a bank card in York.

North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to in connection with the incident.




A force spokesperson said: "Please email: [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk), call 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
