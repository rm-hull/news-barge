A woman was knocked unconscious in an attack at a North Yorkshire traction rally.

It happened at the Yorkshire Traction Engine Rally at Scamptson Hall at about 10.30pm on Friday 4 September.

Two women were reportedly selling drugs at the entrance to the main tent which was hosting live music.

#### Scampston Hall

When the offer was refused, one of the women picked up a folded chair and hit the victim on the back of the head, causing her to collapse to the ground.

A second woman, who was dressed all in black, then punched the victim while she was believed to be unconscious.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim received medical treatment at the scene. However, the injury later required further treatment in hospital.

“We are particularly keen to identify and speak with the two females involved in this incident.

“Anyone who witnessed the assault or has information about their identities is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
