Police have released an e-fit image of a man they want to identify following a reported indecent exposure incident in West Yorkshire.

Officers in Calderdale are appealing for information after a man allegedly exposed himself on Tuel Lane in Sowerby Bridge at around 6pm on August 4.

The suspect is described as being in his late 20s, around 5ft 10in tall, of medium build, with short light brown or blonde hair.

An e-fit image has now been issued as part of the investigation, and officers are urging anyone who recognises the man or has information about his identity to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police via the Live Chat facility, quoting crime reference number 13260444035.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
