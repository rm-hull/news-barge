A UK flooring company has ceased trading after 48 years having collapsed into administration.

Ceco (Flooring) Limited provided flooring and tiling consultancy and solutions for commercial and residential projects across the UK and Ireland.

The Carryduff-based company, established in July 1978, employed nine staff and operated as a subsidiary of Headlam Group, one of the UK’s leading floor covering distributors.

## Ceco closes after 48 years as it enters administration

Administrators were appointed to Headlam Group plc and HFD Limited on September 8, 2026.

Efforts were made to find a buyer or investor for Ceco, but no purchaser was found.

As a result, the company has now entered administration and ceased trading.

Ian Leonard and Stuart Irwin of Interpath were appointed joint administrators on September 30.

Mr Leonard said: "Ceco built a strong reputation across Northern Ireland over many years, working with architects, interior designers, universities, and education bodies on a range of prestigious commercial projects.

"It is particularly sad to see a business of this quality reach this point as a consequence of the challenges experienced by the wider Headlam group.

"Our immediate priority is to support the employees affected by this news.

"We will also be engaging with customers and suppliers to fulfil outstanding orders where possible, and will be exploring options for the brand and assets of the business in due course."

Most employees have been made redundant, with a small number retained to assist with the wind-down process.
