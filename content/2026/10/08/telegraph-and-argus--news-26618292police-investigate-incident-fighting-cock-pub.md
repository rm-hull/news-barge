Police are investigating an incident at a Bradford pub.

Officers were called to The Fighting Cock on Preston Place, after a burglary was reported on Wednesday.

Police, including members of the CSI team, attended the scene in the morning.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We received a report of a burglary at 7am yesterday.

"There is no confirmation of anything stolen.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
