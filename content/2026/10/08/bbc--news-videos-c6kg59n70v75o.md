# Watch: The extreme motorsport where women keep winning

Top Fuel drag racing is often described as the fastest sport on the planet. It's the only elite class of motorsport where women frequently beat men to major titles.

In mid-September, Swedish driver Susanne Callin won the European Top Fuel championship for the second year in a row.

So, is Top Fuel drag racing the first gender-equal sport?
