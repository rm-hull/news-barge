A new application has been submitted for a goods vehicle operator's licence in Bradford.

WHMS Wholesalers Ltd, based on Edmund Street, has applied to use Unit 2, Tong Street Business Park, Holme Lane, as an operating centre for five goods vehicles and two trailers.
