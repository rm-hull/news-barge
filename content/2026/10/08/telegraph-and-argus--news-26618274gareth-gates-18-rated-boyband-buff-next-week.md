Gareth Gates will headline a steamy boyband-themed show promising throwback hits next week.

The Pop Idol star, who grew up in Bradford, will take the stage in 18+ rated Boyband in the Buff at St George’s Hall on Thursday, October 15.

He will be joined by a cast of "seriously talented (and seriously ripped) performers" in a high-energy concert celebrating the biggest boyband hits of the 1990s and 2000s.

## What to expect

The promo for the show says: "Get ready to scream like it’s 1999.

"Boyband in the Buff is the ultimate throwback concert with a cheeky twist – starring pop heartthrob Gareth Gates alongside a cast of seriously talented (and seriously ripped) performers.

"If you’ve ever had a boyband crush... this is your night."

The show promises "slick harmonies, killer choreography and undeniable charisma" with songs by Backstreet Boys, NSYNC, Westlife, and Take That.

It’s described as "nostalgia turned all the way up," a high-energy celebration of pop, passion and perfectly sculpted physiques, where the layers drop and the temperature rises.

## How to get tickets

You can visit this link: [https://www.bradford-theatres.co.uk/whats-on/boyband-in-the-buff](https://www.bradford-theatres.co.uk/whats-on/boyband-in-the-buff)

Standard tickets are priced between £35 and £53.

VIP meet and greet tickets, costing £53, include a ticket in the front two rows of the Stalls and the chance to meet Gareth & dancers.

The meet and greet begins at 6pm, with guests asked to arrive at the Box Office by 5.45pm.
