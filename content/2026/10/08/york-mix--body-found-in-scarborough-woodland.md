A body has been found in woodland near Scarborough today (Thursday).

Police and coastguard teams made the discovery during a search close to Scalby Beck.

A number of paths down to the area were blocked to the public while the remains were recovered.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Officers attended the scene where they  
sadly found the body of a man.

“There are not believed to be any suspicious circumstances surrounding the man’s death and a report will be prepared for a future inquest.”
