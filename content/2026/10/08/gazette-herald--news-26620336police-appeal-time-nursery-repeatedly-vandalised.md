Repeated vandalism targeting a nursery’s perimeter fence is causing growing concern for staff, children, and parents.

Sections of fencing between Garden Lane and New Lane in Sherburn in Elmet, belonging to Time Out Nursery, have been damaged multiple times in recent weeks.

North Yorkshire Police said the incidents have resulted in both financial costs and significant inconvenience to the nursery.




A police spokesperson said: "As a vital community asset providing childcare services to local families, incidents of this nature have a considerable impact not only on the physical security of the premises but also on the wellbeing of staff, children and parents who rely upon the facility."

Officers are appealing to the public for information and are keen to hear from anyone who may have witnessed suspicious activity in the area or has relevant mobile phone or doorbell footage.

They are also encouraging residents to remain vigilant and report anything unusual to police immediately.

A spokesperson said: "Neighbourhood policing officers will be carrying out additional patrols in the area and will continue to work closely with local partners to address these incidents and prevent further damage from occurring."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference number 12260197316.

Reports can also be made through the North Yorkshire Police website.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111.

In emergencies or if a crime is in progress, members of the public are advised to dial 999.
