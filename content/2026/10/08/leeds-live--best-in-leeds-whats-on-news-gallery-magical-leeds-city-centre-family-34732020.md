# Magical Leeds city centre family attraction loved by celebs has lots of Halloween fun planned

## We have paid a visit to the Leeds city centre attraction

![](https://video.primis.tech/uploads/cn1/video/users/origin/31466/video_68f8c0e8d8a26009905662/vid6ac62c5f2d8c8415064268.jpg?cbuster=1791387988)

An enchanting Leeds city centre attraction loved by celebs has some magical Halloween fun lined up.

If you’re looking for something to do in Yorkshire this Halloween season, then a trip to The Hole in Wand, based at the Merrion Centre in Leeds, might well be what you’re after. As we’ve previously reported, the magic-themed mini golf attraction has recently held a big bash to celebrate the venue’s first birthday.

Former Emmerdale Gaynor Faye, YouTube sensations Alexis and Liam Blake, and Lindsey Burrow were all in attendance, and the proceedings were hosted by Matt Jameson from Married at First Sight. While, we’ve also seen former Leeds United star-turned-broadcaster, Chris Kamara recently pop in for a visit with his family.

To mark Halloween, the team from The Hole in Wand has some special treats lined up. The team state: “We're asking visitors to spin the wheel on entry and discover whether there'll be a curse that impacts their game, or a treat that enhances it. There are also extra tricks around the course, as well as giveaway treats and a chance to win an overnight stay.

“Perfect for families, friends, groups, and Leeds city centre fans, our Halloween experience is a fun and thrilling but gentle way to celebrate the season without the scares. Expect enchanting surprises, playful challenges and plenty of opportunities to make some magical memories along the way.”

The spooky season fun will run between Saturday, October 10 until Sunday, November 1. You can find out more here.
