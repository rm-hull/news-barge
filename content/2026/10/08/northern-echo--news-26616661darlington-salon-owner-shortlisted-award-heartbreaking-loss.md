A Darlington salon owner says a beauty award nomination has given her a much-needed lift after the sudden loss of her close friend and colleague earlier this year.

Rebecca Valentine-Cooper, owner of Becki C's Nails, Beauty, Aesthetics, has been shortlisted as a finalist in the Northern Echo Best Beauty Salon 2026 awards, a year after winning the title.

The nomination comes after seven difficult months following the sudden death of her close friend and colleague, Emma Hopper.

Becki, who has run the business for 17 years, said beauty therapy was the only career she had ever known.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21551378.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Becki Cooper owner of Becki C Nails. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The 36-year-old said: "This is the only job I've ever had.

"I started this business when I was 17 straight from college straight into beauty.

"My mum and stepdad owned tanning salons so it was the industry I've always grown up in.

"I originally wanted to do performing arts and go abroad and be a rep but my parents advised me to get a trade first that I could fall back on.

"I've done this for 17 years and never once wished that I did anything differently."

Becki said her life was turned upside down when Emma, who had worked alongside her for about five years, died unexpectedly at the end of February.

She said: "Emma who worked with me and had been with me for about five years passed away at the end of February.

"She was there one day and gone the next.

"I've done the past seven months pretty much on my own.

"It's been tough.

"Don't get me wrong, there have been days where I just thought how can I do this?

"I was working 10 hours a day every day to get both of our client bases in."

Emma helped run the salon and was one of Becki’s closest friends.

"Emma was such a high-standard staff member and she was a friend so I wasn't going to find just anyone to replace her because she's irreplaceable," Becki said.

"But I wanted to find someone who had the same kind of respect and appreciation for the clients and the salon and the reputation we've got."

Becki took her time finding staff who could match their standards before welcoming 19-year-olds Ruby and Millie to the team last month.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21551379.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"It took me a long time but last month I found two girls that I've taken on, Ruby and Millie.

"One is a nail tech and the other is a lash tech so they do one aspect of it rather than doing everything myself," she said.

Becki, a mother of two young children, said she was stunned to be shortlisted again after such a difficult year.

She said: "I never in a million years thought that after the year I've had that something like this was possible.

"I almost felt as if I'd let my clients down.

"I felt like I hadn't been able to give them as much time or energy that I usually could because I'd been stretched so thin.

"To get that nomination it genuinely meant the world.

"I'm really happy to have been doing this so long.

"It's a really saturated industry and market but to still be standing this many years on and still have such a massive customer base, it's just a realisation of how well we're doing."

But the nomination is bittersweet for Becki.

She said: "Emma was my right-hand woman.

"We had so many plans to do so many things.

"I can't even put into words how much she's missed.

"She's one in a million.

"I know Emma would be so proud of me.

"She'd be absolutely over the moon that we got nominated again."

The salon is based on Burnside Road in Darlington and offers treatments including aesthetics, spray tans, dermaplaning, lashes and nails.
