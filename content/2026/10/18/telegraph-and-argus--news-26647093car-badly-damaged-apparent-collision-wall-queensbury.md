A VEHICLE has been left badly damaged by what appears to be a collision with a residential wall.

The incident is understood to have taken place last night (Saturday) on Halifax Road, Queensbury.

When the T&A visited the scene around 8am this morning, at least one car with significant damage to its rear end could be seen parked outside a property whose garden wall had been partly destroyed.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21597677.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A garden wall had been partly destroyed *(Image: T&A)*

Commentators have taken to social media to share images of the incident, which show multiple vehicles involved in what appears to be the collision.

A witness who was in Patchett's Mini Mart at the time of the incident said: "I heard a big crash last night around 9.30pm.

"When I heard it, I went straight outside to see what had happened.

"The police and ambulance came down.

"There was a cordon in place until around 11pm."

The circumstances surrounding the incident have not yet been confirmed.

West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been approached for further information.
