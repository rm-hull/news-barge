Sometimes a property offers far more than meets the eye.

At Sycamore Court that comes in the form of a self-contained annexe, a detached garage and grounds of approximately one acre on top of the charming stone-built semi-detached cottage that will be your home.

The cottage’s front door opens into a large hallway, where a stained glass panel adds a touch of whimsy, while stone windowsills – which continue throughout – lend a traditional farmhouse feel.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21585700.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Buyers looking for a home and more besides will find that this delightful rural property in Rowley ticks all the boxes and then some. *(Image: Harrington Brown)*

The lounge is a real showstopper, its apex ceiling and two skylights creating a wonderful sense of space while its wood-burning stove set in a brick-built fireplace and French doors opening onto the patio add character and functionality.

Anyone who loves to cook will appreciate the generous kitchen where Shaker-style cabinetry and metro tiles are joined by an oil-fired Rayburn.

There’s plenty of room for dining too!

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21585699.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Buyers looking for a home and more besides will find that this delightful rural property in Rowley ticks all the boxes and then some. *(Image: Harrington Brown)*

Beyond the kitchen is a further reception room with an exposed stone wall, skylight, French doors and a wood-burning stove.

Completing the accommodation is a traditional-style bathroom plus three bedrooms.

These include the bright dual-aspect principal bedroom and a cosy second bedroom with whitewashed timber joists.

The third bedroom could serve as a dressing room or home office or – subject to obtaining the necessary consents – could become an en-suite.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21585696.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Buyers looking for a home and more besides will find that this delightful rural property in Rowley ticks all the boxes and then some. *(Image: Harrington Brown)*

Outside gravelled driveways provide ample parking to the front and rear, while the rear garden is home to a paved seating area, small ornamental pond and expanse of lawn.

Then there’s the beautifully finished annexe comprising a bedroom and bathroom plus a stylish living room centred around a feature stove which is open plan to a contemporary fitted kitchen with a focal point central island breakfast bar above which is a feature exposed wood truss.

This annexe would be perfect for accommodating guests or extended family or – subject to the relevant requirements – could be used to generate rental income.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21585698.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Buyers looking for a home and more besides will find that this delightful rural property in Rowley ticks all the boxes and then some. *(Image: Harrington Brown)*

Beyond the immediate garden, Sycamore Court’s grounds extend to around one acre, with potential for further development, subject to obtaining the necessary planning permission.

Sycamore Court sits within a small collection of homes in a rural setting at Rowley, near Consett, and is being sold with no onward chain.
