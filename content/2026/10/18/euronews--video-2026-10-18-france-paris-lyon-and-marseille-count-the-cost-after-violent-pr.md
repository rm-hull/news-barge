In **Lyon**, firefighters attended a** blaze**at the city's tourist office, where the façade remained scorched and smoke continued to rise from the building. Nearby shops had been** vandalised**and** looted**, while residents photographed the destruction. Groups of young people joined the** clean-up**effort, helping to restore streets still scarred by the previous day's** violence**.

In **Paris**,** shattered windows**and** damaged storefronts**were visible at the** Les Halles**shopping centre, the** Paris Opera** and several major retailers, including Nike, Sephora and Burger King.

In **Marseille**, clean-up crews hosed down** Rue Saint-Ferréol**, where damaged shops, scattered shoe boxes and** debris**bore witness to the** unrest**. The city had opened an information point for affected businesses, while municipal police patrolled the area.

Across France's three largest cities, municipal workers, shopkeepers and **volunteers** began clearing broken glass and repairing storefronts after Saturday's**nationwide demonstrations** against the**rising cost of living** descended into**riots** and**looting**.
