A person has been taken to hospital after a crash at a junction in Stockton this morning (October 18).

Emergency services were called to the junction of the A135 and Chestnut Road at around 7.30am following reports of a two-vehicle crash.

Officers from Cleveland Police closed the road during the incident and paramedics were seen at the A135 Yarm Road.

The road has since reopened.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that one person was treated at the scene and discharged, and another was taken to hospital for further treatment.

A NEAS spokesperson said, "We received a call at 7.32am this morning (October 18) to a report of a two vehicle road traffic collision at the junction of the A135 and Chestnut Road in Stockton.

"We sent two emergency ambulance crews to the scene, who treated and discharged one patient and conveyed a second to North Tees Hospital for further treatment."
