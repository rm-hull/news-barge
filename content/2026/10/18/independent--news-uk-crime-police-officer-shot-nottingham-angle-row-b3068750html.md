A police officer has been injured after he was shot in Nottingham city centre.

The officer, who is in hospital with a foot wound, had approached two suspects in Angle Row shortly before the incident at 4.50am on Sunday, Nottinghamshire Police said.

The two suspects, a man and a woman, fled the scene and a firearm has been recovered by police.

Assistant Chief Constable Claire Rukas said: “This is a shocking incident and a sobering reminder of the potential dangers faced by officers as they work to protect and serve the public.

“As we investigate what happened and work to apprehend the individuals involved, my thoughts today are with the injured officer, his family and colleagues.

“I also ask that anyone with additional information shares this with us without delay. Even the smallest bit of information could make all the difference to this investigation.”

![A firearm at the scene on Angel Row in Nottingham, after a police officer has been shot in the city centre](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/18/12/01M57CEB58K33GGM2Y0RM7S2MP.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Nottingham East Labour MP Nadia Whittome wrote on X: “My thoughts are with the injured police officer and his family following this truly shocking incident. We all wish him a full and speedy recovery.”

Councillor Neghat Khan, Leader of Nottingham City Council, said: “I am deeply shocked and saddened by the news of this morning’s shooting. My thoughts and full support are with the injured police officer, their family, and their colleagues across Nottinghamshire Police.

​”Nottingham City Council is working tirelessly alongside the police to support the investigation and ensure those responsible are swiftly caught and brought to justice.

​”While incidents of this nature are extremely rare, our commitment to keeping Nottingham safe is absolute. Thanks to the daily bravery of our police and Community Protection Officers, Nottingham remains a safe city, and residents can be assured that every available resource is being deployed.”

![The officer, who is in hospital with a foot wound, had approached two suspects in Angel Row shortly before the incident at 4.50am on Sunday, Nottinghamshire Police said](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/18/13/01M57BGHA9DGPV30Y9WD3RV91P.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The Police Federation of England and Wales also sent its best wishes to the officer.

“This is a stark reminder of the dangers police officers face every day while protecting the public,” it added in a social media post.

“Their courage and commitment must never be taken for granted.”

Anyone with information is urged to call 101 quoting incident 106 of 18 October 2026.
