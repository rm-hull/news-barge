A countryside tea room tucked away on the outskirts of Durham has been praised by visitors for its homemade cakes, generous scones and peaceful surroundings.

The Pump House Tea Room is based at Pump House Cottage in Houghall, a short distance from Durham city centre.

Surrounded by countryside and close to popular walking routes, the café has developed a strong following among visitors looking for somewhere to stop for breakfast, lunch or afternoon refreshments.

It currently holds a Google rating of 4.6 out of five from more than 360 reviews.

Customers have praised everything from its cakes and traditional tea room favourites to its more substantial lunchtime dishes.

The café offers indoor and outdoor seating, making it a stopping point for people exploring the countryside around Houghall and the River Wear.

But it is the food, particularly its homemade baking, which appears to be one of the main attractions.

## What do visitors say about The Pump House Tea Room?

Visitors have praised The Pump House Tea Room for its homemade baking, welcoming atmosphere and substantial meals.

One customer who stopped for coffee and cake described it as a "lovely tea room", after ordering a cappuccino, caramel iced coffee and a slice of lemon cake.

They said the drinks and cake were "all really nice" and expressed an interest in returning to try the hot food.

Another visitor was particularly impressed by the jacket potato.

They described it as "the best jacket potato I've ever had at a café/restaurant", praising the crispy skin and preparation.

Afternoon tea has also attracted positive feedback.

A customer visiting with friends described the experience as "amazing", adding: "The staff are wonderful and it has a lovely atmosphere."

Other visitors have highlighted the tea room's countryside location, with one reviewer recommending it as somewhere to stop after a dog walk.

The customer praised the selection of hot and cold food, along with its "magnificent scones".

The combination of homemade food and nearby countryside walks appears to be one of the venue's main attractions.

## Durham countryside café popular with walkers

The Pump House Tea Room's location is another important part of its appeal.

Houghall is close to woodland and countryside paths, with walking routes leading towards the River Wear and Durham.

The café features on a published Durham walking route, which passes the tea room before continuing towards woodland and the former Houghall colliery area.

That makes it a convenient place to stop for refreshments before or after a walk.

One customer who visited after completing Durham parkrun praised the outdoor terrace and said they were looking forward to returning.

Another reviewer described enjoying a visit after a countryside walk, praising the selection of cakes and scones.

The café also serves savoury dishes including breakfasts, sandwiches and light lunches, with visitors highlighting its friendly staff and relaxed atmosphere.

Although reviews are positive, some customers have reported issues with parking arrangements and restrictions affecting dogs in certain areas.

Visitors planning to bring pets may therefore wish to check the current arrangements before travelling.

For those looking to escape the busiest parts of Durham city centre, The Pump House Tea Room offers a quieter countryside alternative.

Its combination of homemade baking, substantial meals and nearby walking routes has helped it build a loyal following.

The Pump House Tea Room is at Pump House Cottage, Farm Road, Houghall, Durham, DH1 3PJ.

It currently opens from 10am to 4pm, Tuesday to Sunday, and is closed on Mondays.
