A road is set to be closed for 11 days due to drainage works.

Micklethwaite Lane in Bingley will be shut from its junction with Micklethwaite Landings to its junction with Airedale Mills.

The closure will be in place from 8am on October 26 and will remain in force until 5pm on November 5.

The closure could last for up to 18 months if necessary, but pedestrian access will be maintained throughout.

An alternative route will be available for vehicles, taking drivers through the unaffected section of Micklethwaite Lane, Carr Lane, Otley Road, Heights Lane, Parkside, Park Road, Main Street, and Keighley Road.

The City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council announced the temporary closure due to a proposal by B.C Excavations to carry out drainage works in the area.

Vehicles involved in the works will be the only ones allowed on the closed section of the road.

The council intends to make an Order under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 - Section 14.
