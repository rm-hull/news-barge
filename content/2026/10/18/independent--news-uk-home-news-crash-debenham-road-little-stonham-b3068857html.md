Two teenagers have died after a crash between two cars.

Police said officers were called by the East of England ambulance service at 10.15am on Sunday after reports of the crash on Debenham Road, Little Stonham, Suffolk Constabulary said.

The vehicles involved were a red Peugeot 107 and a silver Volkswagen Passat.

A man in his late teens, who was a passenger in the Peugeot, died at the scene.

The driver of the same car, a woman in her late teens, was taken to Addenbrooke’s Hospital where she died from her injuries.

The occupants of the Passat suffered minor injuries.

Police, ambulance, air ambulance and fire crew all attended the scene.

In a statement, the Suffolk force said: “Two people have died following a serious road traffic collision in Little Stonham.

“Police are appealing for witnesses to the collision and would like to hear from motorists driving in the area who may have dash cam footage prior to the incident.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact the Joint Norfolk and Suffolk Serious Collision Investigation Unit, quoting 37/61603/26.
