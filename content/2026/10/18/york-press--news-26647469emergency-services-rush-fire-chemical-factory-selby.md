## Fire at chemical factory in North Yorkshire - emergency services at the scene

Emergency services have been called to a major fire at a chemical factory in Selby *(Image: Supplied)*

EMERGENCY services have rushed to a fire at a chemical factory in North Yorkshire.

The ICL Works, in Canal View, has been engulfed in flames.

A crash has also closed the A1041 Bawtry Road.




Drivers have been advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.

![The fire today comes just over a year after a blaze at the ICL Works on July 31](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/19777318.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The fire today comes just over a year after a blaze at the ICL Works on July 31 *(Image: Supplied)*

It comes just over a year after a fire at the industrial chemical plant on July 31, 2025.
