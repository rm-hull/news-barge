EMERGENCY services have been called to a serious crash in North Yorkshire.

Yorkshire Air Ambulance, North Yorkshire Police, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and paramedics were reported at the scene close to Tadcaster Grammar School on Sunday (October 18).

Images taken appear to show a silver car and white van involved in the crash.




Nearby roads have been affected, including the A64 westbound to York.

Drivers have been warned to expect delays of four minutes.

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
