North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue have been called out to a fire at an industrial building off Bawtry Road in Selby

It is at the ICL Works on Canal View.

There was also an accident on the A1041 Bawtry Road as well, that was closed in both directions at the canal bridge earlier while emergency services were on scene.
