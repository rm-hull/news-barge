Police in County Durham are searching for a man with several links to the region who is wanted in connection with criminal damage and burglary.

Shay Johnson, 22, who has recent links to Wingate and Peterlee, is wanted by Durham Police.

He is wanted in connection with an incident involving criminal damage and burglary.

The force is asking anyone who has seen him to get in touch as soon as possible.

If you have any information about Shay's whereabouts, call Durham Police on 101, quoting reference CRI00693398, or email [Ashley.Hagar@durham.police.uk.](mailto:Ashley.Hagar@durham.police.uk.)

Alternatively, you can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111
