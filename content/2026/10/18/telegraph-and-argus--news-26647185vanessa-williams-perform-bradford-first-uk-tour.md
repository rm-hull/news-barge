Vanessa Williams will perform in Bradford as part of her first full UK tour.

The Broadway and West End star will take to the stage at Bradford Live on February 26, 2027, with a show featuring music from her stage career.

Ms Williams said: "I am delighted to say that, as a part of my 2027 UK Tour, I'll be stopping off at Bradford Live.

"Hope to see you there."

She has starred in productions including Into the Woods, Kiss of the Spider Woman, City of Angles and The Devil Wears Prada.

A spokesperson for Bradford Live said: "Join the legendary Vanessa Williams as she takes to the road with her first full UK concert tour.

"Vanessa will take you on her musical journey, from her 1988 chart debut, through to her latest album, via her iconic stage performances."

Tickets start at £42.95.
