A 69-year-old man has been reported missing from Northumberland.

Stephen was last seen in the Crookham Way area of Cramlington at 11.40am on Sunday (October 18).

An image from a video doorbell shows that Stephen was in the street at that time.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21598458.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stephen, 69, was last seen in Cramlington on Sunday morning. *(Image: NORTHUMBRIA POLICE)*

He is described as a white man, 5ft 9in tall and of slim build.

Northumbria Police said he may have travelled to Newcastle by bus or train.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Stephen, with officers and his family growing increasingly concerned for his welfare."
