An appeal has been launched to trace a man missing from Falkirk who has not been seen for two weeks.

James Booth, 42, was last seen on Sunday, October 4 and may have travelled to the Edinburgh area. It is unknown what he was wearing.

He is described as being around 5ft 3in tall, with short brown hair and blue eyes.

As time passes concerns are growing for his welfare and enquiries are ongoing to trace him.

Officers are appealing for anyone who has seen James or who has information on his whereabouts to contact them.

Anyone with information is asked to contact 101 quoting reference 1734 of 15 October 2026.
