Amanda Owen's daughter Edith has revealed her mum is buying her a car as she prepares to take her driving tests.

The teenager shares the exciting news with her older sister Raven in the latest episode of Channel 4's Our Farm Next Door, which airs on Monday, October 19.

Edith is currently learning to drive and has her theory test coming up, with her practical test set to follow.

The news comes as Raven returns to Ravenseat, where she joins her younger sister mucking out the stables and helping with jobs around the family farm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21590257.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edith and Raven in a stable at Ravenseat *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The sisters are seen catching up in new footage from the programme, with Raven reacting enthusiastically to their conversation.

It is set to be a busy few weeks for Edith, who is also preparing for her exams and the return of the family's cream tea van, which serves walkers passing through Ravenseat on the Coast to Coast route.

Elsewhere in the episode, Amanda joins Sid and Miles on a mission to rescue a poorly pregnant sheep which has become stuck at the bottom of a hill.

Meanwhile, work continues at Anty John's, the farmhouse being restored by the Owen family.

Carpenter Dan and joiner Philip begin laying oak floorboards, while Annas takes on an unusual decorating task involving large quantities of tea, a spray gun and newly plastered walls.

Clive Owen is also kept busy looking after the family's dogs, including new puppies Patch, Rascal and Dora.
