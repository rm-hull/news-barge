This was the scene in the centre of Selby this afternoon.

The former public toilet block in Back Micklegate Car Park went up in flames.

It sent a thick plume of black smoke into the air.

**Video**

#### Fire location

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/fire-selby-toilets-micklegate-18-oct-2026-2.jpg)

The fire service were called at 4.03pm.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished a fire within a derelict outbuilding in a car park.

“Two hose reels used to extinguish. Police also attended the incident.”
