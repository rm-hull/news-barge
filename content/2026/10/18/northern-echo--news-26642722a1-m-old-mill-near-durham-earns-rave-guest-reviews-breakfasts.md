A hotel, pub and restaurant just outside Durham has been praised by visitors for its hearty meals, generous portions and convenient location near the A1(M).

The Old Mill, on Thinford Road at Metal Bridge, Tursdale, is a popular stop-off point for travellers, university visitors and local diners looking for traditional pub food in a relaxed setting.

Situated just off one of the county's busiest routes, the venue combines hotel accommodation with a large restaurant and bar, making it a convenient base for those exploring Durham or passing through the region.

Guests regularly highlight the building's character and welcoming atmosphere.

Many have been left impressed by the size of the car park and the flexibility of being able to arrive for a meal without needing to book ahead.

According to reviews, the extensive menu offers a range of classic pub favourites at reasonable prices, while the generous portion sizes are frequently mentioned as offering good value for money.

One reviewer described the restaurant as a "great stop-off" during a journey, praising both the quality of the food and the friendly service.

Others have highlighted the venue's breakfast offering, with visitors saying the cooked breakfast is a particular highlight for those staying overnight.

The hotel has also proved popular with parents visiting students at Durham University, thanks to its location on the outskirts of the city and its combination of accommodation and dining facilities.

Reviews repeatedly commend staff for being friendly, knowledgeable and attentive, even during busy periods.

Diners say meals are served promptly and that the team works hard to maintain a positive experience when the restaurant is bustling.
