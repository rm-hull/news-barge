Pierre Sage post match interview - Brighton v Crystal Palace

**Crystal Palacemanager Pierre Sage said he felt "shame" at the Eagles' performance during their 5-0 thrashing byBrightonand did not know whether he would be given time to save his job.**

Sage's side conceded three times in the opening 10 minutes as they suffered their worst loss to their rivals since January 1956.

"It's my fault because I am the coach, I gave the motivation. I prepare the situations. We played so bad it's my fault," said Frenchman Sage, who was only appointed Palace manager in the summer.

"It's difficult to summarise because there are a lot of negative points.

"We started really bad. It's completely not normal to concede three goals in this kind of game after nine minutes.

"I prepare the team, that is why I am responsible of what's happened on the pitch. I have a lot of work to do because I have to change this kind of thing."

Asked if he will get time to turn Palace's poor form around, he said: "I don't know."

Under his predecessor Oliver Glasner, Palace enjoyed the most successful phase in the club's history, lifting the FA Cup for the first time in 2025 and then winning the Conference League in May.

But Sage has had a tough start at Selhurst Park since he arrived after guiding Lens to the French Cup and second in Ligue 1 last season.

Palace are 14th in the Premier League with two wins from seven games but their 17 goals conceded are the most in the top flight.

"I just want to apologise to the fans," added Sage. "We were not at the level we expected, and I'm really sorry for that because I know how important it is for them, but we didn't show today that it's really important for us too."

Asked on his emotions after the game, he replied: "Only one feeling - shame. When you play this kind of game you have to show another face. It was our worst face."

Palace, with one win and one loss from their two Europa League matches, play on Thursday with a trip to Turkey to take on Besiktas before returning to Premier League action on Sunday at home to Newcastle.
