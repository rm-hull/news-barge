If you are visiting Scarborough any time soon, you will be spoilt for choice when it comes to food.

From classic fish and chips to cosy pubs and cool bars, the coastal area is a popular spot to visit for foodies all year round.

Feast can be found a short walk away from South Bay beach in Scarborough, making it an ideal option for some drinks and food after a day exploring the coast.

Through the day, the restaurant and bar offers hot drinks while during the evening, you can enjoy some cocktails or a pint of beer.

## What is Feast in Scarborough like?

Whether you need breakfast or something to keep you going through the day, Feast has something for everyone.

Alongside its cocktails and beers, you can enjoy a range of dishes from Feast including different burgers and stone-baked pizzas.

If you want something a bit more homely, you can try the Yorkshire pudding wraps that come with sage and onion stuffing, seasonal vegetables, your choice of meat, and some roast potatoes and gravy on the side.

Feast also has loaded macaroni and pasta dishes as well as dirty fries and nachos.

The restaurant is known for its parmos, which is coated chicken topped with a béchamel sauce, mozzarella, and cheddar cheese.

The parmos come in a range of different styles and are served with salad, chips, and a garlic dip.
