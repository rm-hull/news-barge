## York home ‘full of character and charm’ hits the market – look inside

The four-bedroom house for sale in Sutherland Street, South Bank, York, for £450,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

An "exceptional" York home has hit the market.

The four-bedroom house is for sale in Sutherland Street, South Bank, for £450,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it is "full of original character and charm".

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21581033.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Outside at the four-bedroom house for sale in Sutherland Street, South Bank, York, for £450,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside, an entrance hall leads to the living room with a large bay window, log burner, wood flooring, and original built-in cupboards.

The open-plan family kitchen diner blends traditional style with modern convenience – and features bi-fold doors opening onto the courtyard garden.

Three bedrooms are on the first floor, along with the home’s main bathroom.

The master bedroom is on the top floor and has an en-suite shower room, offering a "luxurious private retreat", Ashtons said.

Outside, the home has a private rear courtyard garden.

"This is a superb and rare four-bedroom home in South Bank, ideal for families seeking a home with style, charm, and character," an Ashtons spokesperson said.
