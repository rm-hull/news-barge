We tried a shop given a one-star food hygiene rating and was surprised at how tasty the homemade cakes were.

Haworth Wholefoods received the rating in January, with inspectors citing the sale of out-of-date products, mould in several rooms, and the absence of a food safety system for the third consecutive year.

T&A reporters decided to sample three of the Main Street shop’s most popular cakes - millionaire shortbread, lemon drizzle, and carrot cake.

## Millionaire shortbread

The millionaire shortbread was the standout favourite.

One reporter said: "It’s got a really good texture and flavour.

"It’s just the right level of moist."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21573035.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The millionaire shortbread stole the show *(Image: T&A)*

Another commented on the texture of the chocolate, saying: "The chocolate was really rich.

"It had a lovely base, as well.

"Not too hard and not too soggy."

## Lemon drizzle

The lemon drizzle cake received mixed feedback.

One reporter said: "It’s got a nice sharp flavour – it’s very tangy."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21573034.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The lemon drizzle was hit-or-miss *(Image: T&A)*

But another reporter found the icing too sweet.

She said: "The bits on the top are too sweet, almost."

## Carrot cake

The carrot cake was generally well received, though the icing divided opinion.

One reporter said: "I like the crunch of the walnut in the icing, which meshes well with the softer texture of the cake."

However, another described the icing as bland and lacking flavour.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21573033.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The carrot cake received mostly positive feedback *(Image: T&A)*

She said: "I'm not massive on the icing.

"It doesn't really taste of much and it's a bit bland.

"I don't think it did anything to add to the overall cake."

Overall, the cakes were well-liked by the team, despite initial concerns about the shop’s hygiene standards.
