Starting from four locations - Dean Clough, Wentworth Woodhouse, Everingham Hall and Mount St John - drivers had up to three hours and 100 miles to photograph churches, places of worship and historic landmarks before finishing at Bowcliffe Hall near Bramham.

The Yorkshire Post photographer James Hardisty went down to capture the best of the action on Saturday:
