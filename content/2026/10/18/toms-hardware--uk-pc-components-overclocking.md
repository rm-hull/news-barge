You don’t need the latest and greatest PC hardware to have fun or even break overclocking world records, as Luumi shows with an ancient Haswell system.

![Nvidia RTX 50-series gaming laptops](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/D7aZSgz5tphrHEQWcDEtPf-840-80.jpg)

A developer has created a fully vibe-coded tool that seems to work to allow some GeForce RTX 50-series laptops to crank their power limits by as much as 28%.

![A product photo of a Thermal Grizzly delidded CPU.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/wPFnMQRgcNRcBmAgKkm2km-840-80.jpg)

Thermal Grizzly offers the halo CPU with the pop topped for an egregious price, yet it almost makes sense on this chip.

![A screenshot from a Trashbench video showing the Arctic WS360 successfully mounted on the operational GeForce RTX 3080 graphics card.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/L4vwQ9775WqR8qVFGquGva-840-80.jpg)

Trashbench decided to take an Arctic WS360 360mm server AIO cooler and strap it directly to a GeForce RTX 3080

![](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Vucagw5oyP53fagn8bEo8k-840-80.jpg)

Premium Overclocker Splave documents the trials and tribulations of overclocking the RTX 5090 Astral from Asus, resulting in a 3DMark world record.

![A photograph of a graphics card being chilled by a portable camping freezer.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/aD9DNrC4dDo2QGznYqtWCY-840-80.jpg)

Using a shunt mod and a freezer, Trashbench achieved a 23% boost in clocks on the smallest Blackwell GPU.

![Intel Arc B580 liquid cooled using automotive coolant](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/hZbWzHT4YhngmgztdRHx43-840-80.jpg)

TrashBench manages to break the 3DMark Time Spy performance record using a water pump and a 50/50 glycol mix.

![GTX 1060 benchmarking world records](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/mw3aNzm3vU6uxbRbjpTSzS-840-80.jpg)

TrashBench dominates 3D Mark Fire Strike GTX 1060 with i5-12600KF world records using a DIY cooler.

![CPU being overclocked with liquid nitrogen](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/FnWjM8H4eciG7sHTvr8CMB-840-80.jpg)

SkatterBencher has broken the GPU frequency world record, previously held by an RTX 4090, with Arrow Lake's built-in iGPU pushed to 4.25 GHz.

![Delidding AMD chips, testing performance differences](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/CSoowq5pskxiFqmkknjDtD-840-80.jpg)

Enthusiasts can expect up to 10% higher performance with a Ryzen 9950X3D using direct-die cooling. Or just run it at stock clocks but much cooler.
