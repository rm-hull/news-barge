New parking and waiting restrictions are set to come into force across parts of County Durham, according to a public notice.

The changes, coming into effect on October 9, will particularly impact Easington, Easington Colliery, and South Hetton.

The council will introduce ‘No Waiting At Any Time’ rules on Bede Street, Glebe Terrace, Tennyson Road and parts of Seaside Lane.

Vehicles will be banned from stopping on the affected sections at any time.

The same restrictions will apply to parts of Milburn Close, Laburnum Crescent, Lauren Court, Jude Street, Hopper Street, Stonelea Court, Thorpe Road and Nursery Gardens.

‘Restricted Waiting’ rules will apply on parts of Seaside Lane from Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.

Loading and unloading will also be restricted on parts of St Nicholas Terrace during those hours.

No-stopping rules will apply at marked entrances on parts of St Nicholas Terrace, Whickham Street and Hall Walks from Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.

‘Restricted Waiting and Loading/Unloading’ rules will apply on St Mary’s Close from Monday to Friday, 8am to 9am and 3pm to 4pm.

At the junctions of Hall Walks and St Mary’s Close, the rule will be ‘No Waiting At Any Time and Restricted Loading/Unloading, Mon-Fri, 8am-9am, 3pm-4pm’.

Some sections of Hall Walks will change from ‘No Waiting At Any Time’ to ‘No Waiting At Any Time and Restricted Loading/Unloading’ during school-run hours.

A loading-only bay will operate on part of Seaside Lane from 8am to 6pm.

The council has published full details of the changes on its website.

The details are also available at Easington Library during normal opening hours.

Anyone challenging the order or any of its provisions must do so within six weeks of September 30.

A challenge must argue that the order exceeds powers under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, as amended, or that its legal requirements were not met.
