# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of person after parcels stolen from property in Harrogate

## North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to after parcels were stolen from a property in Harrogate.

The incident happened at approximately 6.58pm on September 16 when parcels that had been delivered to an address on Woodfield Road were stolen.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We have released an image of a person we would like to identify in connection with our enquiries.

“Please be aware that the image is third-party material and was not captured or produced by North Yorkshire Police.

![The police have issued a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to after parcels were stolen from a property on Woodfield Road in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/crime/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


"As a result, the quality may be limited.

“Despite this, we’re asking members of the public to take a look and let us know if they recognise the person pictured.”

Officers are asking residents and businesses in the area to review any CCTV, doorbell or dash cam footage from around the time of the incident.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively, if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260183427 when providing any details regarding the incident.
