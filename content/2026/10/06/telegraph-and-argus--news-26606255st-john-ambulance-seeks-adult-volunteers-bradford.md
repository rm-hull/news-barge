St John Ambulance is appealing for new adult volunteers in Bradford to help provide first aid support and life-saving skills within the community.

The charity is looking to expand its team of community first aiders, who deliver first aid at events and provide public education to improve community resilience.

St John Ambulance said: "We need people who have a passion for helping others, care about their communities and want to make a difference, supporting them to become safe and more resilient by sharing your first aid skills and knowledge."

Volunteers can expect a full induction and role-specific training, as well as ongoing support during their time with the organisation.

Those who are successful will be invited to attend regular Network nights to experience St John Ambulance.

Anyone interested in volunteering can contact Janet Wormald at [janet.wormald@sja.org.uk.](mailto:janet.wormald@sja.org.uk.)

For more information about St John Ambulance and its volunteer opportunities, visit www.sja.org.uk.
