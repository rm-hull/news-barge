Harry Kane scored twice on his 125th appearance for England - but who else impressed?

**Harry Kane scored twice as England cruised to acomfortable 3-0 winover the Czech Republic in the Nations League at Wembley on Tuesday.**

Here's how I rated the players - and you can see how you scored them below.

**Jordan Pickford**: Will return to Everton knowing his place is now under threat from James Trafford. Some shaky moments.** 6Trent Alexander-Arnold**: Given another opportunity to impress and took it. Ability on the ball perfect for deep-lying opponents like the Czech Republic.** 7Jarell Quansah**: Deployed in his favoured centre-back role and looked comfortable. Slowly becoming a fixture in the squad.** 6Marc Guehi**: But for a couple of lapses, cruised through the game. Started all four of England's matches during the extended international break.** 6Lewis Hall**: Now in a fight with Nico O'Reilly to be England's first-choice left-back. A successful international period for the Newcastle defender.** 7Elliot Anderson**: Goes from strength to strength at international level. Brilliant pass to unlock the Czech Republic for England's second.** 6Myles Lewis-Skelly**: Can be happy with his output on his return to the international scene. Will be unlucky not to be in next squad on this evidence.** 7Eberechi Eze**: Improved after an underwhelming first-half but did not do enough to cause Thomas Tuchel a selection headache after being handed a rare start.** 6Jude Bellingham**: Did not hit the heights of his captivating display in Croatia but still oozed class whenever in possession.** 7Morgan Rogers**: Showed his versatility by starting out on the right and looked comfortable in the role. Provided a real threat.** 7Harry Kane**: Historic night for the England captain becoming the country's joint-highest appearance-maker alongside Peter Shilton with 125 caps. Goes without saying he scored... twice.** 8Subs:James Garner (on 63, Anderson):** More international minutes in his legs.**6Jarrad Branthwaite (on 63, Guehi):** On for his third cap.**6Morgan Gibbs-White (on 73, Hall). 6Rio Ngumoha (on 73, Bellingham). 6Dominic Calvert-Lewin (on 82, Kane). 6Subs not used: James Trafford, Jason Steele, Bukayo Saka, Anthony Gordon, Trevoh Chalobah, Rico Lewis.**
