A Channel 4 show will see media personality Katie Price and MP Suella Braverman handcuffed together 24/7.

Celebrity Handcuffed comes after the success of the civilian version, Handcuffed: Last Pair Standing, which had more than two million views on its first episode and will return for a second series.

Viewers can expect celebrities with different opinions, beliefs and lifestyles to be handcuffed “in the ultimate test of survival”, Channel 4 explains.

## Which celebrities will be handcuffed for Channel 4 show?

The following celebrities will be handcuffed together for the show:

* Katie Price and Rt Hon Suella Braverman MP
* Neil Ruddock and Bimini
* Kerry Katona and Mark-Francis Vandelli
* Lady Victoria Hervey and Georgia Harrison MBE
* DJ Fat Tony and Princess Olga Romanov

Jonathan Ross said: “I loved being part of the civilian series of Handcuffed, and I can't wait to see how the celebrity version pans out.

“I've done my fair share of celebrity shows, but nothing quite like this.

“It's chaotic, it's hilarious, and I'm so excited for viewers to see what happens when you handcuff a group of famous faces together and put them under pressure.

## From glamour model to reality TV star: Who is Katie Price?

“It could of course be a disaster.

“But it could also be the best thing on TV next year!”

Tim Hancock, Channel 4 commissioning editor, said: “Celebrity Handcuffed takes a format audiences couldn’t stop talking about and supercharges it with some truly unexpected pairings.

“Just like the civilian series, the celebrity version will be laugh-out-loud funny, gloriously awkward and full of surprises, and will capture something bigger about modern Britain: even when we seem to disagree on almost everything, we might have more in common than we think.”
