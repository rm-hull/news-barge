A police appeal has been launched after a man allegedly hurled racist and homophobic abuse on a bus in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident took place on the number six bus in the city on September 25.

Officers have launched an appeal to identify a man the force said it would like to speak to in connection with the investigation.




A police spokesperson said: "We would like to speak to him following an incident on the number six bus in York.

"Two members of the public were verbally abused by a man who became aggressive towards them and use racist and homophobic language.

"Please get in touch if you can help identify the man in the image as he may have information that could help our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police and quote reference number 12260189247.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers.
