## Why we recommend it

In his 4.5-star review, TR Editor-at-Large Lance praised the M5-powered MacBook Air as "the best mix of winning design, near-pro-level performance, and battery life you can find in an ultraportable. It's an exquisite, lightweight yet durable laptop that fits perfectly into your work, play, home life, and the Apple ecosystem."

The laptop sailed through his photo editing, video editing, and productivity tests. He even managed gaming. So, despite its tablet-thin stylings, it's capable of some pretty heavy-duty workloads.

## Price context & historical value

This is the cheapest the MacBook Air has been since Apple announced its series of price hikes due to the on-going component cost crisis, which has seen anything with memory and storage increase in price. The last time it was this price was back in October 2025 for Prime Day.

## Should you buy it?

✅ **Buy the Apple MacBook Air 13-inch (M5) if..**.

You regularly travel for work and want a speedy performer that'll tackle most jobs on the move.

❌ **Skip the Apple MacBook Air 13-inch (M5) if...**

You have intensive workloads like 4K video editing where the MacBook Pro is better suited - especially with the M5 Max and M5 Pro chips.
