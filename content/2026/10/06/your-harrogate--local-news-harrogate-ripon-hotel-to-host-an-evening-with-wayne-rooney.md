Former England captain Wayne Rooney will share stories from his football career at Grantley Hall this month.

The hotel near Ripon will welcome the former Manchester United and Everton striker on Thursday 15th October for a dinner and on-stage interview.

Taking place in the Grantley Suite, the evening will give guests an insight into Rooney’s career and some of its standout moments.

Guests will receive a glass of Champagne on arrival at 7pm, followed by a four-course dinner at 7:15pm.

Rooney’s interview is scheduled to begin at 9:30pm.

Tickets, which have now old out, were priced from £375.

The event runs until 10:30pm, with Valeria’s Champagne & Cocktail Bar remaining open afterwards.

The evening is open to guests aged 18 and over, with full payment required when booking.

*For more information, visit the Grantley Hall**website**, contact [events@grantleyhall.co.uk](mailto:events@grantleyhall.co.uk) or call 01765 620070.*
