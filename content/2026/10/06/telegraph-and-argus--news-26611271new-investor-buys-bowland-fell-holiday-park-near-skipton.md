A holiday park is set for new growth under fresh ownership.

Bowland Fell Holiday Park, between Wrigglesworth and Tosside near Skipton, is one of three Yorkshire sites now owned by European asset manager Aermont.

The park is part of Park Holidays UK, which operates 56 sites across England, Wales, and Scotland, and was previously owned by American company Sun Communities.

A spokesman for Park Holidays UK said: "The new investors will create a strong platform for future growth, and the opportunity to welcome even more holiday guests to its parks.

"The spending of visitors to holiday destinations such as Yorkshire benefit many local businesses from shops to restaurants, and help sustain the jobs they provide."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21542236.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bowland Fell Holiday Park is one of three Yorkshire holiday parks acquired by European asset management group Aermont *(Image: UGC)*

The other two Yorkshire parks included in the acquisition are Sand le Mere Holiday Park in Tunstall and Kingfisher Holiday Park in Knaresborough.

Each park offers luxury holiday lodges and caravans for rent or purchase, along with leisure facilities including swimming pools and family entertainment.

Aermont also owns shopping centres, multiplex cinemas and film studios across Europe.

Park Holidays UK has emphasised that the change in ownership will not impact day-to-day operations.

The company employs around 3,000 people during peak season.
