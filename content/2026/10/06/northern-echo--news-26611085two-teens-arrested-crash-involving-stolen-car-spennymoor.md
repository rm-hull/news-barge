Two teenagers have been arrested after a stolen car crashed into another vehicle in Spennymoor.

The collision happened on the High Street shortly after 9.30pm on Sunday (October 4), involving a Seat Ibiza and a Ford Fiesta.

Durham Police said the Ford Fiesta was reportedly stolen from an address elsewhere in Spennymoor prior to the smash.

Two males, aged 19 and 17, were arrested in connection with the incident.

A spokesman for Durham Constabulary said: "Two males have been arrested following a road traffic collision in Spennymoor.

"Police were called to the High Street shortly after 9.30pm on Sunday, October 4, following reports of a collision between a Seat Ibiza and a Ford Fiesta, which was reported to have been taken without the owner’s consent from an address elsewhere in the town."

Enquiries are still ongoing.

Anyone with information should call 101 or contact Durham Police through its website.
