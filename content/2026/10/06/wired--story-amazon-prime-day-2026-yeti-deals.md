Yeti products hardly ever drops in price, so when they do, discounts almost always sell out quickly. There are several rare discounts going right now on items the WIRED Reviews team has personally tested and liked. Find the highlights below, and check here for all the Amazon Prime Day Yeti deals.

## The Cooler Everyone Will Buy Today

I bring the Roadie 32 with me on basically every trip, and I've been eyeing the Roadie 48 because I like the design so much. It's nearly perfect, though the handle can feel a little short if you're navigating rough terrain. But as we expect with Yeti, it keeps ice for days and the lid and latches are all great. The eye-popping lime color on sale will likely disappear quickly.

## A Top Pick, Selling Fast

The Yeti Tundra Haul is our favorite cooler overall. This is the smaller version—and I promise it will sell out. Arguably, you could go with the Roadie 48 if you value portability because this Haul can get heavy, but if you've been hitting the gym and eating your Wheaties it might not be too cumbersome. This cooler is tough and has days-long ice retention. It's a classic for a reason.

## A Multipurpose, Portable Option

I like these little flip-top coolers because they're so versatile. Bring lunch to the beach, bring brews to the tailgate, or bring snacks to the kayak trip. If you don't need a massive capacity, it's a very portable solution that also has a comfortable shoulder strap.

## A Fantastic Little Lunch Bag

It's not in our buying guide yet, but I've tried this lunch bag and I think it's the perfect beach cooler or picnic companion for one or two people. I like the flap closure and I find it comfortable to carry even when it's overpacked. This price is the lowest I've seen.

## The Cup Everyone Likes

Yeti Rambler insulated drinking vessels come in all shapes and sizes, most of which are on sale. I think the 20-ounce version is the most approachable, and at $20, it's a no-brainer if you want an excellent travel mug. Might I also say it would make a good gift?
