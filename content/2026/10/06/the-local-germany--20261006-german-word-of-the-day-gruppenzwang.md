Whether it's joining colleagues for after-work drinks, buying the same trainers as your friends or separating your recycling exactly like the neighbours, social pressure exists everywhere.

**What does* Gruppenzwang*mean?**

*Gruppenzwang* is the standard German word for "peer pressure". Literally translated, it means "group compulsion" or "group constraint".

Grammatically, it is a masculine noun: *der Gruppenzwang*. It’s also a compound noun made up of the words for “group” (* die Gruppe*) and “force” (* der Zwang*).

When the words are joined together, a linking *-n-* appears to produce*Gruppenzwang*.

Once you know *Zwang*, you'll start spotting it in other useful compounds. These might include* Kaufzwang*(compulsive buying),* Sparzwang*(forced budget cutting) and* Handlungszwang* (the pressure to take action).

An added fun fact is that *Zwang* is related to the English word "twinge" – a sudden sharp pain or squeeze – which may be one way to think of the uncomfortable feeling that comes of giving in to peer pressure.

**Why do I need to know* Gruppenzwang*?**

For foreigners living in Germany, *Gruppenzwang* is one of those words that unlocks an entire cultural conversation.

Whether it's in schools, workplaces, politics, sports clubs (*Vereine*) or even debates about recycling, Germans often enjoy a conversation about the pros and cons of social conformity.

But interestingly, the word *Gruppenzwang* is almost always negative.

If you want to talk about the potential benefits of peer pressure – for example when it comes to studying German vocabulary! – then you’re better off reaching for words like *positiver Gruppeneinfluss*(positive group influence) or* Vorbildfunktion* (role-model function).

**Use it like this:**

*Ich wollte eigentlich keinen Nachtisch bestellen, aber es war reiner Gruppenzwang.*

I didn't actually want to order dessert, but it was pure peer pressure.

*Viele Jugendliche stehen in der Schule unter Gruppenzwang.*

Many young people suffer from peer pressure at school.

*Gib nicht dem Gruppenzwang nach.*

Don't give in to peer pressure.

*Wir sollten darauf achten, dass in den Meetings kein Gruppenzwang entsteht.*

We should make sure that peer pressure doesn't develop during meetings.
