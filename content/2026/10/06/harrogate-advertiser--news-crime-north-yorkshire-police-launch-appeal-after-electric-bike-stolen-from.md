# North Yorkshire Police launch appeal after electric bike stolen from property in Knaresborough

## North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after an electric bike was stolen from a property in Knaresborough.

The incident happened in the early hours of Sunday (October 4) when an e-bike was stolen from the communal hallway of a property.

The offender gained entry to the block of flats via Market Place.

The bike that was stolen was a grey Urlife folding electric bike.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after an electric bike was stolen from the communal hallway of a block of flats on Market Place in Knaresborough](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/crime/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


If you have any information about the incident or witnessed anything suspicious in the area, you should contact North Yorkshire Police.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260195337 when providing any details regarding the incident.
