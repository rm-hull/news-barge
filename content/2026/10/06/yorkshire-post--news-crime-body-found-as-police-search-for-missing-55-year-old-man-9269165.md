# Body found as police search for missing 55-year-old man

## Police searching for missing 55-year-old Paul Wilson from Normanby have found the body of a man.

Officers confirmed the discovery was made on Tuesday (Oct 6) during the search for missing Paul Wilson.

Formal identification has not yet taken place, but Paul's family have been informed.

![Stock image.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/crime/smart&trim=&width=620&quality=65.jpg)


Police said their thoughts remain with his family at this difficult time.

The death is not currently being treated as suspicious.

A file will be prepared for the Coroner.
