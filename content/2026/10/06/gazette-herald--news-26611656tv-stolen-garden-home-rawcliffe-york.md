POLICE are investigating an alleged theft from the garden of a York home.

Officers have launched an appeal to identify a man police say they would like to speak to following the suspected theft of a TV from a garden in the Rawcliffe Lane area.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We believe he may have information that could assist our investigation."

Anyone who recognises the man or can help identify him is urged to contact PC Ian Butterfield by emailing [ian.butterfield@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ian.butterfield@northyorkshire.police.uk) and quoting reference number 12260164063.
