Lidl shoppers are praising a new bakery treat online with one customer describing it as "phenomenal" and another saying they are "obsessed", while others are convinced they "need" to try it.

The Milkybar Muffin is proving to be popular among shoppers and is included in the supermarket's fresh bakery section.

In the Bargain Lovers Facebook group, someone shared several pictures of the new muffins, writing: “This milkybar muffin from Lidl is phenomenal

“it’s got milky bar cream inside too!”

## Milkybar Muffin is "phenomenal" new addition to Lidl bakery

The sweet treat is priced at £1.49, but signage in the supermarket tells customers it is a limited offer.

It’s listed in the latest Lidl brochure as a "special guest", with its potential time on shelves unknown.

A customer said: “Sooo nice omg”.

Another said they’re "obsessed" with the new treat: “Omg it really is I'm so obsessed after trying one, can't wait to get another tomoz!!!”

Shoppers who haven’t tried it are excited to see what all the fuss is about, with one saying: “Looks like a must try”.

## Lidl stops selling chocolate bar range after 6 years

The news comes as Lidl has stopped selling a chocolate bar range six years after it launched in UK supermarkets.

Its Fairtrade Way To Go! chocolate bars launched in 2020 and cost £2.69 each, but they aren’t on shelves anymore.

However, Lidl has reassured customers that it still believes in "ethical and sustainable sourcing across our cocoa supply chains".

Lidl told The Sun that although customers can’t shop the range in its stores, it remains available to buy elsewhere.
