A former nurse who left the profession after struggling with stress and anxiety has been shortlisted for a regional beauty award.

Chloe Alderson, who runs Authentic Brows by Chloe in Darlington, has been selected as a finalist in the Northern Echo's Best Beauty Salon Awards - less than a year after launching the business.

The beauty therapist said she was stunned at making the shortlist.

![Chloe of Authentic Brows in Darlington. Photograph:Stuart Boulton/The Northern Echo](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21540838.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chloe of Authentic Brows in Darlington. *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

She said: "I was really shocked. I was laid on a sun lounger in Ibiza and I got a phone call.

"It was amazing.

"I was just shocked, I couldn't believe it.

"I struggle with a lot of self-doubt, so when I got the call it was just amazing.

"I was saying to my partner, 'Do you think someone's joking with me?'"

Chloe previously worked in nursing but left the job after it began affecting her wellbeing and launched her beauty business.

The 27-year-old said: "I started the business just under a year ago.

"It [nursing] was causing me a lot of stress and anxiety and it felt like I was losing myself a little bit.

"I didn't know what path I wanted to go down.

"I didn't have any children or a mortgage or anything.

"I was just at home with my partner and my little dog, so I was in a position where I could take the leap and do something different."

Chloe enrolled on a training course in Barnsley and built her business from scratch.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21540842.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Chloe said she has always loved beauty, despite being encouraged to choose a different career when she was younger.

She said: "I wanted to do beauty but I was advised not to.

"I was always the one doing my friends' makeup and eyebrows when we were younger.

"I was going to do a part-time course alongside nursing, but it was my partner who said to me, 'Just leave and go full in with it and see how you are.'

"You don't get anything without taking chances, do you?"

Chloe has a part-time job alongside running Authentic Brows by Chloe but hopes to go full-time by the end of the year.

She is investing in more training while growing her business.

"I'm just trying to enjoy it because I'm quite impulsive and I like to chase things quickly," she said.

"I actually have a big training course this weekend, so I'm just trying to fund it in the correct way."

Chloe says leaving nursing has transformed her quality of life.

She said: "It's taken a while because I think when you build up anxiety, it doesn't just go away quickly, but I'm finally getting my life back.

"My partner works away so I can kind of control when I'm working.

"It gives me a lot more freedom.

"I'm making a lot more friends through my clients as well, so it's been lovely.

"It's been a breath of fresh air."
