At least one person has died as Ukraine targeted the Moscow region with over 650 drones overnight, believed to be the largest aerial attack on the Russian capital in the last two years.

A major Russian oil facility was among the targets of the attack, with reports of a fire seen at the Volodarsk Linear Production and Dispatch Center near the village of Konstantinovo.

It comes after Ukraine said it had neutralised more than half of Russia’s oil refining capacity with long-range strikes deep into Russian territory, as it seeks to hurt a key revenue stream for Vladimir Putin's war chest.

Kyiv has continued to strike Russian oil infrastructure despite repeated requests to stop doing so from US president Donald Trump, who again blamed Ukraine on Monday for high gas prices in America.

Volodymyr Zelensky has said he is happy to agree to stop striking Russian energy targets, but only if Moscow is willing to reciprocate and work towards ending its invasion.

## Ukraine claims to have taken out more than half of Russia’s oil refining capacity

Ukraine says its long-range strikes have neutralised more than half of Russia’s oil refining capacity, hampering Moscow’s ability to supply its forces during its four-and-a-half-year-old invasion.

The defence ministry said late on Sunday that recent strikes on refineries in cities, including Moscow, Yaroslavl, Ust-Luga, Perm, Saratov and Syzran, had disabled 51 per cent of Russia’s oil refining capacity. The claim could not be independently verified.

## India says it wants to be more involved in ending Ukraine war as Zelensky hails peace plan

India is involved “beyond advocacy” in finding an end to the Russia-Ukraine war, foreign minister S Jaishankar said, after both Vladimir Putin and Volodymyr Zelensky hailed plans proposed by Delhi.

Speaking at the Munich Security Conference in Delhi, Mr Jaishankar said India had held talks with both Moscow and Kyiv in recent months, discussing proposals ranging from protecting Black Sea shipping to halting attacks on energy infrastructure.

India has maintained a neutral stance on the war. It has called for a halt to fighting but has seemed reluctant to directly criticise Russia because of close defence and bilateral ties.

Prime minister Narendra Modi urged Mr Putin to end the war as recently as August, when they met on the sidelines of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit in Kyrgyzstan.

Mr Jaishankar said India was now engaged in conversations with Russia as well as Ukraine about practical steps towards reducing hostilities.
