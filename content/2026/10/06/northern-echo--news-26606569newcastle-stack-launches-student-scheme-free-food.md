A North East entertainment venue is giving hundreds of students free food as it launches a membership scheme offering discounts, prizes and exclusive deals.

St James’ STACK in Newcastle has launched STACK Society, a student-only scheme offering deals all year.

The first 200 students through the doors after 11am tomorrow (October 6) will get a free-food voucher valid at any of the venue’s street-food traders.

Chels Morton, marketing manager for sports and events at STACK, said: "We want to welcome all the new students to the city and those who are returning.

"And what better way to do that than with a range of special events and offers – and the launch of our exciting new STACK Society which will allow them to enjoy incredible offers throughout the year."

Students can register at buytickets.at/stjamesstack/2391688 and spin the STACK wheel for a chance to win free merchandise.

STACK Society members will be entered into a prize draw to win an NUFC stadium tour and a home shirt.

The scheme offers Sunday-to-Thursday deals including two-for-one cocktails and large pizzas from ZZA Pizzeria for £5.

STACK’s street food vendors include Acropolis, Bao Down, Brack Burger, ZZA Pizzeria, Tan Hill Inn and Clucking Oinks.

STACK also hosts daily entertainment and events alongside its food and drink.

Students can sign up for STACK Society at forms.airship.co.uk/forms/1111/stack-society-sign-up-form.
