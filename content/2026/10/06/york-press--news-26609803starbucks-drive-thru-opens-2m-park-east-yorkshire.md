## Global coffee chain opens new drive-thru in £2m East Yorkshire retail site

Artium Construction completed the scheme nearly three weeks ahead of schedule >(Image: Colin Boulter (Neilson Reeves))

A MULTINATIONAL coffee company has opened a new premises as part of a newly completed retail scheme in East Yorkshire.

A Starbucks drive-thru is included in a £2 million development on Bluebird Way in Brough, delivered by Artium Construction nearly three weeks ahead of schedule and includes six retail units, and E.ON electric vehicle chargers.

Every unit in the scheme has been let, Artium said.




Martin Watson, managing director of Artium Construction, said: "We are delighted to have successfully completed this retail park development on behalf of our client.

"The project represents a fantastic example of collaborative delivery, with the whole team working together to overcome the challenges associated with a live, fast-paced development and deliver a high-quality scheme ready for occupation."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21542003.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It is the first Starbucks drive-thru and shop Artium has built *(Image: Colin Boulter (Neilson Reeves))*

Artium said it managed the entire build, from the steel framework to the car park and drive-thru lane.

The company also provided early site access to allow the Starbucks drive-thru to be fitted out while construction continued.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21542004.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Artium has completed its £2m Starbucks drive-thru and retail scheme in Brough, East Yorkshire, nearly three weeks ahead of schedule *(Image: Colin Boulter (Neilson Reeves))*

Mr Watson said: "We said at the ground-breaking that we would deliver Brough with pace and certainty, and handing it over nearly three weeks early is the proof.

"Speed means nothing if the finish isn't right, and the quality here is there for anyone to see.

"The site team and our supply chain have done us proud."

The retail scheme is part of the wider Brough South development.
