This time around, Amazon Prime Day is only two days long, so this is your sign to stop waiting for a better discount. Some of my favorite wearables are on sale, including the latest models and older picks that are hitting their lowest prices yet. Below I’ve rounded up the Prime Day wearable deals I think are worth considering. Every product here has been tested and vetted by WIRED reviewers.

I will be updating this list with the latest Prime Day wearable deals, so be sure to check back regularly. For more information on Prime Day sales, browse our roundups of the Best Prime Day Fitness Deals and Absolute Best Prime Day Deals.

## The Newest Apple Watch Is Already Discounted

Frankly, I’m surprised to see Apple’s latest watch discounted this soon, so I don’t anticipate this deal sticking around for long. The Apple Watch Series 12 is about $50 off—that may not sound like much, but it’s the lowest price I've seen so far. I bet we’ll see similar discounts around Black Friday, but that’s not a guarantee, so if you want the latest Apple Watch, this sale is a good excuse to add to cart.

## The Latest Google Pixel Watch Is $65 Off

Google released its latest Pixel Watch this past summer, and it’s finally on sale. It’s WIRED’s favorite smartwatch for Android users, and it also makes for an excellent fitness and sleep tracker. It’s the second Pixel Watch designed to be repairable, which means you can keep it going for the long haul.

## Save 20 Percent on the Best Value Apple Watch

The Apple Watch SE 3 is already the most affordable Apple Watch, and at $199, it’s an even easier sell. It has practically everything the average iPhone user needs from a smartwatch and fitness tracker, granted you’re not an elite athlete or routinely disappearing off the grid. Note that this is the previous-generation model, and Apple didn’t update the SE this year, so you’ll miss out on newer features like Audio Intelligence. Still, with all the features available in the SE 3, it's a tough deal to beat.

## Save Up to $221 on a Samsung Galaxy Watch

After testing the latest Galaxy Watch, I’m here to tell you that Samsung’s previous models still have plenty of life left in them. The Watch8 is down to $220, which is an $80 discount from its original price and, for a limited time, $160 less than its successor. If you want to spend even less, the Watch7 is just $159, which is $91 off its original price and $221 less than the Watch9. You’ll miss out on the newest health metrics, like Antioxidant Index and Heart Health Score, but you’ll still get a durable, well-built smartwatch. Unless you absolutely need the intricate health tracking, save your money and snag one of these deals.

## A Smartwatch for Just $59

Our budget smartwatch pick is now just $59, which is cheaper than some drop-in fitness classes these days. Don't let the price fool you; the CMF Watch 3 Pro is feature-packed, with wrist-based controls, a built-in mic for voice calls, and a voice assistant. It's also designed with a comprehensive suite of health and fitness metrics, plus dual-band GPS for more accurate route tracking. Plus, it’s got plenty of personality with its retro-futuristic aesthetic.

## A Rare Deal on This Casio

This Casio doesn’t go on sale often, and while a $24 discount may not seem like much, the lowest price I’ve seen it drop to is $185, so it’s a relatively solid deal. It’s editor Julian Chokkattu’s favorite solar-powered smartwatch with a bright LCD screen and durable build.

## RingConn’s Budget Ring Gets Even Cheaper

Yes, there’s a newer RingConn Gen 3, but you don’t necessarily need it. The Gen 2 Air is down to $159, which saves you $40 off its retail price and $190 compared with the latest model. You don’t get the advanced metrics like vascular trends and sleep apnea pattern insights, but you’ll still get a capable entry-level smart ring with up to 10 days of battery life.
