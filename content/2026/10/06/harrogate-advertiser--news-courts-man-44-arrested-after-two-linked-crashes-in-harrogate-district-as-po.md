# Man, 44, arrested after two linked crashes in Harrogate district as police appeal for witnesses

## A man has been arrested following two linked collisions in the Harrogate district involving a scaffolding truck and several parked vehicles.

The first collision occurred at around 8pm on October 1 at the A59/B6451 crossroads near Menwith Hill and involved a scaffolding truck and a silver Kia C'eed.

Officers believe the same Kia was then involved in a second incident about 8.30pm on Old Church Lane in Pateley Bridge, where it reportedly collided with several parked vehicles, causing damage.

The vehicle was later found abandoned.

![A 44-year-old man has been arrested following two linked collisions in the Harrogate district involving a scaffolding truck and several parked vehicles](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/courts/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


A 44-year-old man, from Summerbridge, was arrested shortly afterwards on suspicion of a number of driving offences, possession of a controlled drug and arson with intent to endanger life.

He appeared before magistrates on Saturday, October 3, and has been remanded in custody.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed either incident, or who has dash cam footage that may have captured something.”
