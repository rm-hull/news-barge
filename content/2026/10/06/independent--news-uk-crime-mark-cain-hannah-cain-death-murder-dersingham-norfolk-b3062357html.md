A man has been charged with the murder of a GP who was found dead at her home near the Sandringham Estate, Norfolk Police have said.

Mark Cain, 45, of Fern Hill, Dersingham, was arrested after Dr Hannah Cain’s body was found at her address in Fern Hill in the early hours of October 4.

He has been remanded in custody to appear at Norwich Magistrates’ Court on October 7.

Dr Cain, 45, who was known professionally as Dr Hannah Terry, was listed as a member of the practice team at Grimston Medical Centre.

According to the website of a business she ran called Norfolk Sky Aesthetics, she has been a qualified doctor for more than 15 years, and a GP for eight.

Dr Cain described herself as a “caring and professional doctor” who continued her work as a GP alongside her cosmetics business.

Police say her family have asked for privacy and are being supported by specialist officers.

Michael Devaney from the Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) said: “The CPS has decided to prosecute Mark Cain for the murder of Hannah Cain following a police investigation into an incident at her home in Dersingham.

“We have worked carefully with Norfolk Police during their investigation.

“Our thoughts remain with the family of Hannah Cain at this time.”
