A man is wanted by police in connection with an attack in County Durham.

Durham Police are searching for Jamie Taylor, who also uses the name Byron Taylor and has links to Newton Aycliffe.

It follows an attack that took place on Tuesday, September 29.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: "Have you seen Jamie Taylor, who also uses the name Byron Taylor? The 26-year-old is wanted in connection with an assault that took place on 29 September 2026.

"He has links to Newton Aycliffe."

Anyone with information has been asked to call Durham Police on 101.
