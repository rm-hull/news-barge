Police have released an image of a person they would like to identify after parcels were stolen from an address in Harrogate.

The theft happened on Woodfield Road at around 6.58pm on 16th September, after the parcels had been delivered.

North Yorkshire Police is asking anyone who recognises the person pictured to come forward.

A police spokesperson said:

> “Please be aware that the image is third-party material and was not captured or produced by North Yorkshire Police. As a result, the quality may be limited.
>
> “Despite this, we’re asking members of the public to take a look and let us know if they recognise the person pictured.”

Residents and businesses nearby are also being asked to check CCTV, doorbell and dashcam footage recorded around the time of the theft.

The spokesperson added:

> “Your footage may provide a clearer image or further information that could assist our enquiries.”

*Anyone with information can email [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk) or contact North Yorkshire Police online through ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘An existing case or report’.*

*Anyone unable to use the online options can call 101. Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*

*Please quote reference 12260183427.*
