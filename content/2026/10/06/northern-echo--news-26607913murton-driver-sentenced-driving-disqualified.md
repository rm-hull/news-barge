A County Durham woman who drove a car while banned from the roads has been handed a community order.

Carrie Davies, 43, of Forster Avenue, Murton, admitted driving while disqualified on the B1285 in Seaham on July 11.

Davies was behind the wheel of a Peugeot, despite being banned from holding or obtaining a driving licence.

She appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 2, where she pleaded guilty.

The magistrates made a community order, which will last until October 1, 2027, including a six-month drug rehabilitation requirement under the direction of probation services.
