# Police appeal after cyclist seriously injured in Huddersfield crash

## Police are appealing for witnesses after a cyclist was left seriously injured following a crash in Huddersfield.

The 54-year-old was riding a Carrera bicycle when he was hit by a white Vauxhall Insignia at 5.13pm on Monday.

The rider was taken to hospital with a serious head injury following the crash on Ashbrow Road, close to the junction with Bradford Road.

He remains in hospital in Tuesday, where his condition is said to be serious.

West Yorkshire Police’s Roads Policing Unit is investigating the incident and is appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision or what happened prior to it.

Anyone with footage that may assist this investigation is also asked to make contact.
