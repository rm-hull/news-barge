The LiberNovo Omni is one of the best chairs we've ever tested - our expert clings to his still. So, expect equally great things from LiberNovo's Maxis, an ergonomic office chair built for taller and broader users, which has just got a Prime Day price cut.

**• Best for:** Taller or broader people who sit for long hours and want a chair with a wider frame, deeper recline, and plenty of adjustment.

**• The deal:** The LiberNovo Maxis Ergonomic Office Chair is now $849 (was $1119) at Amazon. In the UK, the Maxis is £833 (was £1086) with voucher for Prime Day.

**• Why it matters:** A 399lb weight capacity, five lockable recline positions from 105° to 160°, an extended-range headrest, adjustable armrests, a flared base, and manual lumbar adjustment.

![LiberNovo Maxis Manual]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8mYHu8uUTVBS5zupaqZPVA-200-80.png.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8mYHu8uUTVBS5zupaqZPVA-200-80.png.webp) "LiberNovo Maxis Manual")

This is the Graphite chair-only configuration, and Amazon lists the price as an exclusive Prime deal. However, there are deals on the electric range too, with footrests.

**In the UK:** now £833 (was £1086)

## Why we recommend it

We haven't tested the Maxis Manual ourselves, but our expert reviewer Collin gave LiberNovo's Omni chair 4.5 stars, so the brand's ergonomic approach has a good record with us.

The Maxis, which comes in manual and electric models, is specifically designed for big and tall body types, with a fit range up to approximately 6ft 7in. The 399lb is relatively high compared to some big-and-tall office chairs, too - many cap out at 350lb.

## Price Context & Historical Value

At $849, this is $270 (24%) below Amazon's $1,119 typical price. That's the lowest price we've seen - before that, it was $939 during September for a brief time. For reference, LiberNovo's Omni dropped from $1,297 to $921.99 at Amazon in March, so the brand does take part in big sales.

## Should you buy it?

**✅ Buy the LiberNovo Maxis Manual if...**

You're tall or broad and want a high-end ergonomic chair with a wide recline range and an adjustable headrest and armrests.

**❌ Skip the LiberNovo Maxis Manual if...**

You're average-sized and don't need the extra width, or you're on a budget.
