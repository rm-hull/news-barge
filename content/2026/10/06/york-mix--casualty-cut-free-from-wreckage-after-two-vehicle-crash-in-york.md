Emergency services have been called to a crash in York this afternoon (Tuesday).

York and Acomb fire crews went to the two-vehicle collision in **Heslington** at**2.40pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews liaised with paramedics and used e-draulic cutting equipment to create space and allow the casualty to get out of the vehicle.

“Crews disconnected the batteries and made the vehicles safe.

“This incident was left with the police.”
