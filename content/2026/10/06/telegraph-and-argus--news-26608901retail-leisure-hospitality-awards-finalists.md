The finalists have been announced for the 2026 Telegraph & Argus Retail, Leisure and Hospitality Awards.

The event will take place on Sunday, November 15 at The Broadway Bradford.

It will celebrate standout businesses and individuals across the district.

The awards will bring together industry leaders, business owners, and special guests for an evening of celebration, entertainment, and networking.

Take a look at the highlights of last year's event here.

## How to get tickets

Tickets are limited, so book early to secure your place at this landmark event by clicking on the ticket line - [https://store.newsquest.co.uk/telegraph-argus-retail-awards-2026-tickets](https://store.newsquest.co.uk/telegraph-argus-retail-awards-2026-tickets)

## Event is 'truly magical'

Ian Ward, manager of The Broadway, said: "The Broadway is all prepared for the festive season and, under the sparkle of its Christmas lights, we're proud to provide the backdrop for an evening dedicated to celebrating the Retail, Leisure & Hospitality sector of Bradford once again.

"There is something truly magical about seeing our business community come together to recognise success, share achievements, and applaud the people whose passion and commitment help our district thrive.

"We can't wait to raise a glass to this year's finalists and winners for what promises to be a fantastic night."
