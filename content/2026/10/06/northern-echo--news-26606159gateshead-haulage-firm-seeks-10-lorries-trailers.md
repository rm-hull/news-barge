A haulage company has applied to expand its operations at a North East trading estate, according to a public notice.

ECM Vehicle Delivery Service Ltd, based in Carlisle, Cumbria, is seeking to increase the number of lorries and trailers it can keep at the North East Wholesale Fruit & Vegetable Market Ltd, Team Valley Trading Estate, Gateshead.

According to the public notice, the company plans to add 10 more vehicles and 10 more trailers to its current licence.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

The notice gives people 21 days to make representations to the Traffic Commissioner if they believe their land use or enjoyment would be affected by the proposed changes.

Representations should be sent to Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, with a copy sent to the applicant at their address at The Airport, Carlisle, Cumbria, CA6 4NW.

A guide to making representations can be found on the government website.
