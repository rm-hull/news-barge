A patient has been taken to hospital after a two-car crash in Darlington at rush-hour.

Emergency services were called to McMullen Road near the A66, shortly before 9am on Tuesday, October 6.

Durham Police and the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) attended.

A NEAS spokesperson said: “We were called to a road traffic collision on McMullen Road in Darlington at 8.56am on October 6.

“We dispatched an ambulance crew who took one patient to Darlington Memorial Hospital.”

The crash caused delays for drivers with the road was partially blocked while the collision was dealt with.

Durham Police confirmed they attended the incident.
