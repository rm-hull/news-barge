A medical manufacturer in Keighley is celebrating 30 years in business.

Medical Supplies and Services International Limited, based at Valley Road Business Park in Keighley, was founded in October 1996 and has grown into a global supplier of anaesthetic vaporisers and related equipment.

The company supports customers in the veterinary and healthcare sectors across the United States, Europe, Asia, and Africa.

John McInnes and Moira McInnes, directors at the company, said: "We are incredibly proud to be celebrating 30 years in business.

"What started as a small Yorkshire company has grown into a respected international supplier of specialist anaesthetic equipment.

"This anniversary is a chance to thank our customers, suppliers and staff, whose support and dedication have helped make this achievement possible."

Equipment made in Keighley is now used by healthcare professionals and veterinary surgeons around the world.

The directors said the company remains focused on the future, with ongoing investment in manufacturing, servicing and support for both healthcare and veterinary markets.

The company will officially mark its 30th anniversary on October 7, 2026.

Medical Supplies and Services International Limited has built its reputation on quality, reliability, and customer service over three decades of growth.
