More than 2,000 vehicles turned heads at a two-day supercar weekender in North Yorkshire.

The CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender took place at The Motorist in Sherburn in Elmet, welcoming thousands of visitors and car enthusiasts to the site.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21537246.jpg?type=mds-article-620)About 2,000 vehicles rolled up for the CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender at The Motorist in Sherburn in Elmet. *(Image: The Motorist)*

The event featured a huge display of high-performance vehicles, from rare classics and modified cars to the latest supercars.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21537247.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A range of high-octane and performance vehicles were on display during the CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender at The Motorist in Sherburn in Elmet. *(Image: The Motorist)*

Ciro Ciampi, founder and CEO of the car culture brand Petrolheadonism, attended the event, while the CTEK team took over the venue for the full two-day celebration with displays and activities throughout the site.

A spokesperson for The Motorist said: "We were absolutely thrilled with how the weekend went.

"We welcomed over 2,000 vehicles across the CTEK weekend, alongside thousands of visitors over the two days.

"There were millions of pounds worth of machinery on site, ranging from brand-new supercars through to rare classics, modified cars and everything in between."

"The event also proved popular online, generating roughly 115,000 views across The Motorist’s social media channels within 24 hours."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21537381.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Motorist's Supercar Day on Saturday, October 3, 2026. *(Image: The Press)*

Supercar enthusiasts enjoyed live product demonstrations from CTEK, a leader in battery care and charging technology.

Experts were on hand to offer advice on maintaining car batteries and charging systems.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21537242.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A range of high-octane and performance vehicles were on display during the CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender at The Motorist in Sherburn in Elmet. *(Image: The Motorist)*

Supercar clubs and owners’ groups had been encouraged to reserve display space in advance, with organisers describing it as a chance to meet fellow enthusiasts, explore the technology behind the vehicles, and enjoy time immersed in automotive culture.

People could also browse The Motorist Store while the Drivers Lounge was open serving breakfast, lunch, and refreshments.

The Motorist regularly hosts automotive events throughout the year, drawing crowds from across the region.

The CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender was free to attend.
