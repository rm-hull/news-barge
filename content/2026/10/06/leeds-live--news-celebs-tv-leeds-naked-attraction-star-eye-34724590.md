# Leeds Naked Attraction star on eye-watering amount she was paid to go on Channel 4 show

## A Leeds actress and model was paid a huge amount to appear on Channel 4's Naked Attraction, after turning down the racy dating show three times

A former Naked Attraction contestant has lifted the lid on whether she received payment for her appearance on the provocative Channel 4 series.

Actress and model Chrissie Wunna became a household name after featuring in series 11 of the Anna Richardson-fronted programme back in 2016, where she took on the role of picker.

This saw the Leeds native select her ideal date based purely on physical attributes - before disrobing completely herself and embarking on a date with her chosen man.

It's safe to say Chrissie's time on the show proved quite unforgettable - from her frank discussions about preferring "really big" penises to her date culminating in somewhat of a shambles.

Speaking recently to Daily Star, Chrissie - a devoted mother-of-two - disclosed exactly what she earned for her television appearance, reports the Daily Star.

"I was a picker and I got an appearance fee, travel, food and hotel and everything paid for whilst you're there, including your date," Chrissie explained. She continued: "You're actually really well looked after the whole entire time.

"You have so many people looking after you. It was a great show to be part of for that. They went out of their way to make sure everyone felt comfortable."

Regarding the substantial sum she pocketed for her stint, Chrissie revealed it totalled £500, insisting she negotiated her fee after rejecting the show three times beforehand. She noted: "I refused to be in the pod, I wanted to be the picker!".

Chrissie also shared that one cheeky moment failed to make the final cut, with the model describing it as her "favourite part" of participating in the show.

Discussing the risqué scene that was cut from broadcast, Chrissie revealed: "My favourite part was this moment that didn't air where all six men in the pods were helicoptering their privates to 'Moves like Jagger' in order to win my heart."

She joked: "It was surreal and the most willies I'd ever seen at one time. We had so much fun, the whole cast and crew were giggling."
