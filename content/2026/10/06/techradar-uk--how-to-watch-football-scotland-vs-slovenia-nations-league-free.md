Scotland remain in contention for promotion to League A of the Nations League at the midway stage of the campaign. Sebastien Pocognoli's men sit third in Group B1, level on points with Slovenia in second place. With Switzerland seemingly on course to finish top, this clash in Glasgow will determine which of Scotland or Slovenia will be in pole position to enter the promotion/relegation play-offs.

These two teams faced off on matchday one, playing out a goalless draw in Ljubljana. Scotland were the better team after a wayward opening 25 minutes and would surely have won the match had Jan Oblak not been in such inspired form between the sticks for Slovenia. Since then, Scotland have lost 3-0 to Switzerland and beaten North Macedonia 2-0.

Pocognoli has made a broadly positive start to his tenure, but the interpretation would change if Scotland were to lose their final match of this international break. A win, on the other hand, would leave them well positioned ahead of meetings with North Macedonia and Switzerland next month.
