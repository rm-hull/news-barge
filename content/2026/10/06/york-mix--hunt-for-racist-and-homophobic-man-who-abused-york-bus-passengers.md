Police investigating an incident on a York bus want to talk to this man.

It happened on the number six bus on Friday 25 September.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Two members of the public were verbally abused by a man who became aggressive towards them and use racist and homophobic language.

“Please get in touch if you can help identify the man in the image as he may have information that could help our investigation.”

If you can help police, you can contact them on the numbers below.
