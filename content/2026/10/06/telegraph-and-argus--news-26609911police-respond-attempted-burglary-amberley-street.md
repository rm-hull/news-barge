Police scrambled to a street in Bradford in the early hours of the morning.

Officers were in Barkerend after a reported attempted burglary on Sunday.

Photographs shared on social media appear to show police vehicles parked on Amberley Street in Barkerend.

West Yorkshire Police said the incident is believed to have happened in the early hours of Sunday.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said the police presence was "in relation to a report of an attempt burglary – which is believed to have occurred in the early hours of 4 October."
