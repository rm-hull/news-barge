I test a slew of devices every year, but it’s rare that a category truly surprises me. I entered the world of digital wall calendars expecting to hate them, and I walked away their biggest fan. If you’ve been curious about getting one for yourself, now is the perfect time: Just about all of my picks are on sale.

If you’re unfamiliar, a digital wall calendar is a physical device that syncs all of your digital calendars onto its screen. No more grabbing your phone to check when the next appointment is or scribbling out plans that fell through. It’s great for couples and families who juggle multiple schedules and want to see them all in one place, on a screen the entire family can access.

But these devices don’t stop there. All my picks also act as an overall family hub, managing chores, meal plans, and more to make life a little easier. It’s the smartest device in my kitchen, though it isn’t a true smart-home device.

Here’s a collection of the best digital wall calendars I’ve tried and their sale prices for Amazon Prime Big Deal Days 2026. You can also check out my guide for more details, and don’t miss all our deal roundups this season.

## The Best On-Sale Digital Calendars

The best deal is on Everblog’s HomeCal, which feels surprisingly similar to the popular Skylight Calendar without requiring a subscription to access every feature. The 21.5-inch device is often discounted from its $400 price tag to around $320, but you can get it for $280 at the time of writing.

I really like that this calendar has all the features I could want without any paywalls. You’ll get meal planning, chores, and to-dos on top of calendars, and you can also have it double as a digital photo frame for free. You can only upload one photo at a time, but a slower experience is well worth not paying a subscription fee.

If you’re looking to spend even less and still avoid a subscription fee, the Apolosign Digital Calendar is a great choice that’s also on sale for Prime Day. It’s available for the cheapest price out of my picks—just $249—with a 15.6-inch screen and options for either Apolosign’s Calendar Mode or Android Mode, which gives you more customization tools.

The Android Mode will allow you to add a photo screensaver so it can also function as a digital photo frame. This view makes it less calendar-focused, however; it feels more like an Android tablet. You’ll get to customize widgets, but it doesn’t sync as smoothly on this mode.

My all-around favorite digital wall calendar comes from Skylight, but my preferred option—the Skylight Calendar 2 with a 15.6-inch screen—has only certain colors on sale. It’s usually available on Skylight’s website for $300, though, so this discount isn’t outrageous. The Skylight Calendar Max, with its massive 27-inch screen, is on sale for a nice discount, but even this price is more than twice Apolosign’s, and some features are paywalled.

Still, it’s a great device, and Skylight’s original product is digital photo frames, so it’s no surprise that the photo feature works best on this one compared to other frames. The interface feels clean on both the device and companion app, syncs fairly seamlessly with my variety of calendars, and updates on its own with new features (and will give me a fun pop-up the next morning telling me what's new). I just wish it didn’t have a paywall and that the smaller, better-priced Calendar 2 had a bigger sale.
