It's Amazon Prime Day, or Big Deal Days as Amazon's taken to calling it, and there are lots of deals on all manner of fitness and wellness kits — but my first port of call was to check the Apple Prime Day deals for any Apple Watch discounts. As luck would have it, there are quite a few in both the US and UK — so now's the time to get yourself a new smartwatch.

In the US, the brand new Apple Watch Series 12 just got its first discount. Right now on Amazon US, the GPS-only Apple Watch Series 12 in 42mm is $349.99 (was $399). Meanwhile, the 46mm, GPS-only Apple Watch Series 12 is available on Amazon for $399.99 (was ~~$449~~).

If that's out of your price range, however, I've got an affordable alternative: the Apple Watch SE 3 now starts at just $199.99 on Amazon (was ~~$249~~) for the 40mm GPS-only model. That's a terrific value pick, and the discount also extends to larger and GPS+Cellular models.

You can check out the full deals below, including the GPS+Cellular versions of both watches, which can be added to a phone's data plan.

In the UK, you can get the Apple Watch Series 11 starting at £259 (was ~~£319~~) on Amazon for its smaller 42mm size, and the 46mm is down to £278.02 (was ~~£349~~), saving you 20%. Those numbers are for the GPS-only models, but the discounts extend across Cellular versions too.

The Apple Watch SE 3 is also on sale in the UK, starting at £199.99 (was ~~£249~~) on Amazon. Check out the deals using the 'UK deals' hotlink or scroll down to see the full list.
