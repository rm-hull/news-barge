Jeremy Clarkson has revealed where he is "dreaming" of opening his next pub.

However, the new venture will not be based in the UK.

The former Top Gear presenter opened his first pub, The Farmer's Dog, in Asthall near Burford in Oxfordshire in August 2024 after purchasing the former Windmill pub.

The pub has since featured in his Amazon Prime series Clarkson's Farm and has attracted visitors from around the world.

## Jeremy Clarkson reveals where he wants to open second pub

Speaking at the Conservative Party conference in Birmingham on Monday (October 6), Clarkson revealed he is looking "quite hard" at the possibility of opening another pub.

The 66-year-old said: "But I'm going to give you all a piece of information right now: it's not going to be in the UK.

"No, I'm not leaving.

"Why would anybody set up a business in this country right now?

"So, you're going to find a country somewhere.

"There will be one where they go, 'Ah, that's a good idea.

"That'll employ people and, you know, that's good for farming and what have you.'

"I'm dreaming of it."

Clarkson also spoke about the challenges facing UK agriculture at the Conservative Party conference, describing the past year as "terrible" for farmers.

The news follows recent fan speculation that Clarkson might open a pub in Symonds Yat East, a village currently up for sale in the Wye Valley.

One user on X (formerly Twitter) suggested: "A beautiful part of the country.

"Maybe someone can scoop up this little piece of heaven?

"It would be a fantastic spot for @JeremyClarkson to run another pub in a popular area."

Clarkson responded with a teasing "Hmmm," prompting excitement among followers.
