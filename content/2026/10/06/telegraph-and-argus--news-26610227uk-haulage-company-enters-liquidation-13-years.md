The company operated a modern fleet of vehicles, offering part or full loads, next-day and same-day delivery, and Saturday services.

Warehousing services were also offered across the UK, with options for short and long-term storage, stock management, and pick-and-pack order fulfilment for local deliveries.

Q Delivery Services also highlighted its commitment to customer service, promising a flexible, reliable, and professional approach.

The award-winning company, based in Widnes and Bedford, had grown to become "one of the United Kingdom’s premier logistics providers".

## Q Delivery Services enters liquidation after 13 years

Now, after 13 years in business, Q Delivery Services has fallen into liquidation.

Richard Cole and Stephen Kenny from KBL Advisory Limited were appointed joint liquidators on September 14, according to The Gazette.

Companies House records show Q Delivery Services’s most recent accounts were overdue, with the last filed for the period ending January 31, 2023.

This latest update comes after the company fell into administration back in 2024.
