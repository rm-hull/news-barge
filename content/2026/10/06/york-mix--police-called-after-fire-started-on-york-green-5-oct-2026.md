Fire crews were to a fire on a York green last night (Monday).

Vegetation was alight on **Leeman Road Millennium Green** off Garfield Terrace at**8.24pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb’s appliance was mobilised to reports of vegetation on fire.

#### Garfield Terrace, York

“On the arrival of crews, the fire had been extinguished, crews inspected the area and left the incident with police.”
