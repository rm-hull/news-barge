Fire crews were called to a blaze at a York recycling plant last night (Monday).

Both Acomb crews and Huntington’s appliance went to the incident at Harewood Whin Waste Transfer Station, on Tinker Lane, Rufforth at 9.41pm.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews used on-site equipment to remove fire and flammable materials from the premises.

#### Harewood Whin

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/york-harewood-whin-landfill-site-rufforth-gsv.webp)

“Two breathing apparatus and two hose reel jets were then used to extinguish the fire and dampen down the surrounding area.

“The incident was left with a responsible person on scene.”

Harewood Whin opened as landfill in the 1980s. According to City of York Council, it “is now used as a sorting and baling depot for recycling, which is then sent to be recycled around the UK and Europe, depending on market demands”.
