## Why we recommend it

The TBT-UD5 can drive two 4K monitors at 60Hz over HDMI on supported systems, or a single display at up to 8K. Its Thunderbolt 4 connection provides up to 40Gbps bandwidth, and the dock can deliver up to 100W to a connected laptop, with 96W certified.

Because the dock is driverless, setting it up with supported Windows and macOS systems should be easy enough.

## Should you buy it?

✅ **Buy Plugable's 13-port Thunderbolt 4 dock if...**

If you frequently move your laptop between your desk and other locations and want to connect everything with one cable. It's particularly useful for a permanent home office setup where your displays and accessories can remain connected to the dock.

❌ **Skip Plugable's 13-port Thunderbolt 4 dock if...**

If you only need a few extra USB ports or a single monitor connection, as a simpler USB-C hub will be cheaper. You should also look elsewhere if you want a Thunderbolt 5 dock as that will offer greater bandwidth.
