## Luxury £1.7m home with wildlife pond and six bedrooms on the market

A six-bedroom detached house set within nearly four acres is on the market for £1.7m.

The house in Roebuck Rise, Breighton, was built in 2023 and is on the market with estate agent Price Independent Estate Agents, Powered by eXp.

The house boasts a wildlife pond and approximately 400 recently planted trees, with deer seen in the garden from time to time.

It boasts around 6,200 sq ft and has five bathrooms, three reception rooms, a double garage, and a room above the garage that could be used as a home office, gym, or cinema room.

The house is set back from the road and has a large entrance hall that is said to set the tone for the rest of the individually designed home.

The open-plan kitchen and living area has a bespoke kitchen with a three-metre island and a boiling water tap.

Expansive glass doors open up the space to the garden.

There is also a separate wine room and a grand reception room with a log burner.

The house also includes underfloor heating, a Sonos audio system, data points, CCTV, an alarm system, and PV battery storage.

The side entrance of the house brings you into the bespoke boot room, which has a built-in dog shower.

Above the garage is approximately 800 sq ft of additional space with its own shower room.

The estate agents said it could be a home office, gym, dance studio, cinema room, or a place for teenagers to have as their own.

Roebuck Rise sits within a small, private group of individually designed homes in the village of Breighton.
