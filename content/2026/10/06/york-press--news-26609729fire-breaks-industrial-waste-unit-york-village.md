A FIRE has broken out at an industrial waste unit in York.




North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews from Huntington and Acomb were called to Tinker Lane in Rufforth at 9.41pm last night (Monday, October 5).

A service spokesperson said: "Crews used onsite equipment to remove fire and flammable materials from the premises.

"Two breathing apparatus and two hose reel jets were then used to extinguish the fire and dampen down the surrounding area.

"The incident was left with a responsible person on scene."
