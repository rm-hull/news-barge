A luxury County Durham hotel has been shortlisted for one of the hospitality industry's most prestigious international awards.

Headlam Hall Hotel & Rural Retreat, near Darlington, is a finalist for Best Rural Hotel at the 2027 Condé Nast Johansens Awards for Excellence.

The family-owned hotel is among a select group of UK and European properties shortlisted in the category.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21545575.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21545576.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

Headlam Hall is a Jacobean country house hotel known for rural breaks that combine traditional charm with modern luxury.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481317.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: DAVE COOIL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481312.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

It has 38 bedrooms, a spa, golf course, walled gardens and several dining options for guests seeking a rural escape.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481319.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: JAKE EASTHAM)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481313.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

Thomas Robinson, director at Headlam Hall Hotel, said: "We're incredibly proud to have been shortlisted for the Condé Nast Johansens Awards for Excellence."

"To be recognised alongside some fantastic hotels from across the UK and Europe means a great deal to the whole team, particularly because so many of our guests have been coming back to Headlam for years."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481311.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

The shortlist comes after continued investment at the rural retreat, including a new terraced spa area and enhanced hydrotherapy pool overlooking the County Durham countryside.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481314.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: DAVE COOIL)*

Seasonal menus use produce from the hotel’s kitchen garden and other ingredients from local suppliers.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481321.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481310.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: JAKE EASTHAM)*

Mr Robinson said the hotel had succeeded by offering an authentic countryside experience while continuing to evolve.

He added: "Headlam has always been about giving people the space and time to enjoy the countryside, while continuing to evolve what we offer."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481320.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

"New additions such as the Paddock Bar and private dining Feed Room give guests different ways to experience the hotel, while keeping the character and sense of place that makes Headlam distinctive."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21481309.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21545617.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: HEADLAM HALL HOTEL)*

The Robinson family bought the Jacobean hall in 1977 after acquiring the surrounding estate for their farming business.
