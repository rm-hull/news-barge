A glowing food review has put a chicken restaurant in the spotlight.

Danny Malin, who hosts the popular Danny v Food YouTube channel, paid a surprise visit to Ronda Rotisserie, on Green Lane in Halifax, and declared its rotisserie chicken the best he’s ever tasted.

## Restaurant workers 'shocked' to see Danny

Carla Hull, manager of Ronda Rotisserie, said: "We didn't even know he was coming in.

"It was quite a shock to see him.

"He was so lovely, and very jokey.

"He immediately put us at ease, because I got quite nervous when he walked in, as you can imagine.

"He told us it was absolutely brilliant, and the best rotisserie chicken he's ever had.

"We've already seen a boost in business, for sure."

## 'Best I've ever had'

Among the items Danny ordered was a half chicken with baked potatoes and a quarter chicken with rice and coleslaw.

He also got a chicken wrap, gravy and an aioli dip before setting up outside to eat the meal.

He praised the food, saying: "The chicken looks amazing.

"And freshly made coleslaw.

"This is what I like.

"This looks like it might have a little bit of their secret sauce in here because I can see some red bits.

Danny went on to praise the seasoning and flavours of the food.

He said: "What they've done, they've put that little bit of seasoning, what they're using with chicken in there.

"It's giving it such a nice flavour.

"That is cooked to absolute perfection.

"Soft, silky in your mouth.

"And then you've got this herby taste with a bit of salt kicking in.

"For chicken, rotisserie chicken, this is up there.

"This is the best I've ever had."

## Chicken and gravy seasoning praised

Danny continued: "That is fantastic.

"Honest to God, that gravy is phenomenal.

"The seasoning on that chicken is next level.

"It is moist. It is beautiful. Best I've had."
