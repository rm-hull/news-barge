A MEXICAN fast-food drive thru could open until the early hours of the morning if new plans are approved.

A new branch of Taco Bell opened at Odsal, in the former Dunkin Donuts unit, in June.

Under its current planning conditions, the branch can only open between 7am and 11pm.

Now the branch’s owners and operators QFM Group and Northgate Fast Food Ltd have applied for permission to change these opening hours to allow it to open an extra three hours - until 2am.

An application to alter these opening times has been submitted to Bradford Council, and says the changes are “to meet demand in a sit-down restaurant and quick service outlet.”

A decision on the application is expected next month.
