A MAIN road in a North Yorkshire town has reopened to traffic.

Welham Road in Norton is now open with a traffic light system in place.

Northern Gas Networks (NGN), the gas distributor for the North of England, is investing in the ageing metal gas network in Norton to ensure the continued safe and reliable supply of gas to the local community for years to come.

Stewart Whitehead, Site Manager for Northern Gas Networks, said: “ We’re grateful for everyone’s patience while we complete this essential upgrade to keep the local gas network safe and reliable for years to come."
