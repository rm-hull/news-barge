Sunderland AFC players will wear commemorative shirts before Sunday’s clash with Leeds United to mark 25 years of the club’s charity, Foundation of Light.

The limited-edition Hummel shirts will be worn during the team’s pre-match warm-up at the Stadium of Light.

Only 500 shirts have been produced.

Fans can buy them at the Beacon of Light reception for £35 for adults or £25 for children, with proceeds supporting the charity’s work across the North East.

After the match, the players’ warm-up shirts will be auctioned to raise more money for the Foundation.

Foundation Matchday caps Founders’ Week, celebrating the charity’s impact on thousands of people and families across the region through education, sport, health and wellbeing.
