Washington has long accused Confucius Institutes of being Chinese propaganda ⁠tools and has taken steps to ​crack down on them.

Hegseth, ‌in a ‌video posted on social media, alleged that Confucius ‌Institutes were operating under disguise at US universities and that the Pentagon had added them to its list of institutions that threaten US national security.

“It turns ‌out that 80 new propaganda cells across 30 universities had popped ​up using different names and different charters, but with the same mission: to steal intellectual property and to infiltrate the minds ⁠of American students,” Hegseth said.

The defense department over ​the ​past two months has “systematically ​dismantled every single one of ​these organizations”, he ‌said.

The Chinese ​embassy ​in Washington did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
