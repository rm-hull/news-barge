A young Scottish mother has died while on holiday in Albania.

Ferne King, from Cardenden in Fife, was found dead in an apartment she had rented in the coastal city of Durres.

The 25-year-old had arrived in the city a few days before for a planned short break with her two-year-old daughter.

Reports in Albania indicate that the mother-of-one may have passed away from a cardiac event.

A statement issued by her family on social media read: "Beloved and cherished mum of Sihana, loving daughter to Heather and the late Alan, fun-loving sister to Saffron, Alistair and Oscar, loving granddaughter to Jo, auntie and a very good friend to many more.

"You will be sorely missed Ferne."

Ms King's family has launched a fundraiser to cover repatriation costs.
