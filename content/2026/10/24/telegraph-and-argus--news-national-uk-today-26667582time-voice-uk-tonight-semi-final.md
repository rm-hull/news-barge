The Voice UK is back tonight (Saturday, October 24) for its semi-final, as several hopefuls look to progress to the final stage of the competition.

Coaches Sir Tom Jones, Willi.i.am, Kelly Rowland, and Tom Fletcher & Danny Jones can pick only one from each of their teams to progress to the climax of the 2026 series.

As a result, there is a lot on the line for the contestants, and here's when you'll be able to tune in to watch it.

## What time is The Voice UK on tonight?

The Voice UK will begin at 8.35pm tonight on ITV1 and last until 10.35pm.

A synopsis for the episode reads: "Emma Willis presents the semi-final of the singing contest, with the remaining acts competing for a place in the final."

The acts are competing to ultimately win a life-changing record contract with music giants Universal Music Group, £50,000 in cash, and a dream holiday to Universal Orlando Resort in Florida.

## The Voice UK changes for 2027

The Voice UK will see a number of changes for its 2027 series, with Alesha Dixon and Stacey Solomon replacing Emma Willis as the presenter.

In a wider shake-up, ITV has also confirmed that Cheryl and rapper Aitch will join the programme as new coaches, taking up seats in the show's iconic red chairs.

Destiny's Child star Kelly Rowland will remain on the coaching panel, alongside Black Eyed Peas frontman Will.I.Am, who has been a coach since the show's launch.

However, long-serving coach Sir Tom Jones won't be returning, alongside McFly’s Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones, who shared a joint chair.

## The history of The Voice UK

The Voice UK first aired in 2012, hosted by Holly Willoughby and Reggie Yates.

Emma Willis took over hosting duties in 2014, and joined for several series by Marvin Humes of boyband JLS.

The show moved from the BBC to ITV in 2017.

Unlike other singing competitions, The Voice UK is known for its blind auditions, where coaches face away from contestants and judge solely on vocal talent.

If impressed, they turn their chairs to offer mentorship.

Other previous coaches on the UK series include Olly Murs, Jennifer Hudson, Kylie Minogue, Rita Ora, Anne Marie and LeAnn Rimes.
