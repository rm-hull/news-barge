The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for York this weekend.

Saturday, October 24, will start off mostly dry and chilly, with temperatures ranging from 6°C to 9°C.

The morning will be the most settled part of the day, with a low chance of rain and occasional brighter intervals.

However, conditions are expected to worsen in the afternoon as thicker clouds bring rain and stronger winds.

Temperatures will rise to 12°C, but the wind will make it feel cooler, with winds that may reach up to 44 mph.

The evening will see heavy rain and strong winds, with some improvement later on.

The rain will become lighter and more showery, with some clearer intervals possible.

Sunday, October 25, will be very windy with frequent showers in the morning.

Temperatures will be around 10°C to 12°C, but will feel more like 5°C to 7°C due to the strong winds.

Winds could reach up to 54 mph, which could make cycling difficult and affect high-sided vehicles.

The afternoon will see a mix of showers and sunny intervals, with winds gradually easing.

Temperatures will reach around 12°C, feeling closer to 7°C in the wind.

The evening will be drier and clearer, with temperatures around 9°C to 10°C.

Winds will steadily decrease, although gusts of around 39 mph are still possible, however, the chance of rain will decrease from around 50 per cent to 10per cent.
