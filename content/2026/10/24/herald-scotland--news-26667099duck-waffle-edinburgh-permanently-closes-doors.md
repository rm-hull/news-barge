A Cuban-themed gastro-diner in Edinburgh has closed its doors.

Duck & Waffle Edinburgh operated for over three-and-a-half years at St James Quarter as the brand's first UK location outside of London.

The shock closure was announced in a statement published on the official Duck & Waffle Edinburgh website.

The statement read: "DUCK & WAFFLE will be closing its doors on October 24th.

"We are incredibly grateful to our guests, our team, and the Edinburgh community for their support throughout our time in the city.

"While our time here has come to an end, we hope to welcome you to our flagship restaurant in London."

The news comes after a Scottish hotel famously linked to a 1963 incident involving a 14-year-old King Charles (then Prince of Wales) also announced its sudden closure.

Management of the Crown Hotel in Stornoway announced on social media and a front-door notice that the business has closed until further notice, with no reopening date currently set.
