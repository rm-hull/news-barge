A man was seriously injured in an attack at a bar.

The man, who is his 20s, suffered serious facial injuries in the incident, which happened at Vibe Bar & Club on Peel Street in Morley, Leeds, at 1am on October 9.

West Yorkshire Police have launched an appeal for information.

A force spokesman said: "Police are investigating a report of a man being assaulted at Vibe Bar & Club on Peel Street, Morley, that happened at 1am on Friday, October, 9.

"The victim, a man in his 20s, was left with serious facial injuries.

"A number of active enquiries reman ongoing into the incident by Leeds CID."

Anyone with information or footage is asked to contact Leeds CID on 101 or via the force’s LiveChat, quoting crime number 13260575883.
