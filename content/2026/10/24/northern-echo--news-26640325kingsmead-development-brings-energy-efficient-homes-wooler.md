In the historic market town of Wooler, a new residential project is demonstrating how energy-efficient construction and regional design can sit comfortably together.

Developed by Wannop Developments, Kingsmead, introduces a thoughtfully planned collection of two, three and four-bedroom homes to this picturesque corner of Northumberland.

The scheme has been created to offer generous living spaces, private landscaped gardens and off-street parking while placing energy efficiency and long-term sustainability at the heart of its design.

Each home at Kingsmead incorporates high-performance insulation and modern energy-saving technology, with energy efficiency considered throughout the design and construction process.

Taking inspiration from the principles of the Future Homes Standard, the homes feature energy-efficient air-source heat pumps and underfloor heating, providing comfortable, low-carbon heating without reliance on traditional gas boilers.

Kingsmead combines contemporary design, comfortable living and energy efficient construction in a desirable countryside setting.

![]()

That commitment to energy-conscious construction reflects a broader building ethos that the Cumbria-based housebuilder is delivering across multiple locations in northern England.

Rather than pursuing large-scale volume building, the firm focuses on smaller, design-led schemes tailored to the distinct character of local market towns and villages.

Further east in the Tyne Valley, the firm has turned its attention to small bespoke builds, including The Paddocks at Humshaugh.

Comprising just three individual properties, The Paddocks combines traditional heritage sandstone facades with insulated concrete construction, wraparound gardens and detached garages.

![]()

Set within the stunning landscape of the North Pennines National Landscape, the company has also developed Viewlands in Allendale, a cluster of five three-bedroom homes featuring dedicated home offices and private gardens.

Both The Paddocks and Viewlands achieve EPC A energy efficiency ratings, incorporating high-performance insulation and modern renewable energy technologies, including air-source heat pumps, underfloor heating and rooftop solar panels. Designed with the principles of the Future Homes Standard in mind, these homes combine high-quality construction with energy-efficient living, helping to reduce environmental impact and household running costs.

Across each development, residents benefit from full fibre broadband connectivity, private driveway parking and ten-year structural warranties.

Comprehensive details on site plans, energy performance and specifications can be explored through the wider Wannop Developments portfolio.

Back in Wooler, the ongoing progress at Kingsmead represents a welcome addition to the local property landscape, creating energy-efficient options for both growing families and downsizers.

Each completed home provides generous outdoor space alongside bright interiors crafted for practical modern living, with energy-efficient features designed to help manage household running costs.

Prospective buyers can learn more about release schedules, site visits and plot choices by speaking directly with the developer.

With its blend of rural charm, thoughtful design and forward-looking energy efficiency, these developments stand as appealing examples of how regional housebuilding can embrace the principles of sustainable modern living.
