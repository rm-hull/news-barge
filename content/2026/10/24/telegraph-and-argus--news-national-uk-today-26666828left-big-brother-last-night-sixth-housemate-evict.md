The sixth housemate evicted from Big Brother 2026 has been revealed.

The hit reality programme returned for a fourth series on ITV last month, following its 2023 reboot.

Friday (October 23) night's episode saw four housemates up for eviction:

* Catrin
* Harry
* Samuel
* Twinks

## Who was the sixth housemate evicted from Big Brother last night?

Following the public vote, hosts AJ Odudu and Will Best revealed Harry was the sixth housemate evicted from Big Brother 2026.

Reflecting on his time in the house, Harry described the experience as “unbelievably intense”.

He explained: "Being in there for six weeks is a mental experience.

"I can't even comprehend how crazy that whole thing was."

## Controversial week on Big Brother

The latest eviction follows a controversial week on Big Brother, with more than 7,000 complaints made to Ofcom about housemate Omar’s behaviour towards Rhianna during Monday's (October 19) episode.

Viewers criticised Omar’s dismissive attitude, including when Rhianna broke up with him.

Big Brother called Rhianna to the Diary Room to check on her wellbeing.

She said: "Sometimes I don’t like the way that he speaks to me and I feel like I’ve let a lot go recently.

"I wouldn’t let a boy speak to me like that outside of here so why am I letting him speak to me like that?"

Domestic abuse charity Refuge issued a statement on Wednesday (October 21) calling out the behaviour and urged broadcasters “not to amplify individuals displaying abusive behaviour”.

The charity said: “Domestic abuse happens every day and thrives in silence. When abusive behaviours, such as coercion and control, are broadcast without meaningful challenge, it sends a dangerous message that abuse has no consequences.

“Refuge is deeply concerned by the behaviours witnessed during the current series of Big Brother.

“Media outlets have a responsibility to prioritise participant welfare and to ensure that harmful behaviour is identified and addressed appropriately. Broadcasters must also actively choose not to amplify individuals displaying abusive behaviour.
