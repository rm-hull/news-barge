An impressive five bedroom Ayrshire home with it's own Harry Potter-inspired sitting room is now on the market for offers over £380,000.

The Manse, in Main Street, Barrhill, also comes with fishing rights on the River Duisk.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611272.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Manse *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

The ground floor provides a welcoming entrance porch to the property. There is a spacious lounge with feature fireplace and wood-burning stove.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611289.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

This home also offers a wonderfully unique Harry Potter-inspired sitting room. Beautifully themed to create a magical and characterful space, the room also features a built-in bar, making it an ideal setting for entertaining, relaxing or enjoying a quiet evening.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611295.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

The large contemporary kitchen with central island and breakfast bar features a glazed door which gives direct access to the rear garden. Accessed directly from the kitchen, the dining room benefits from a south-west facing window providing plenty of natural light.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611296.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

The home offers a useful and practical utility room with built-in shelved cupboard and a range of wall units providing excellent storage. Space and plumbing are provided for a washing machine and tumble dryer, together with a white sink and radiator.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611305.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

Upstairs, there are five well-proportioned bedrooms, including a principal bedroom with en-suite facilities.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611306.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

There is also a family bathroom, separate WC and a versatile home office.

The attic is also partially floored, providing further valuable storage space.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611312.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

Outside, the property enjoys generous garden grounds and a gravelled driveway providing ample off-road parking.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611313.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

There is a selection of outbuildings including a substantial workshop, storage room, summer house and wood store. The property also benefits from solar panels, with electricity generated being exported to the grid, together with an air source heat pump.

![](https://www.ayradvertiser.com/resources/images/21611316.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: A B & A Matthews)*

The gardens and surrounding grounds attract a variety of wildlife, while the rural setting offers wonderfully dark skies.

To find out more about this property, visit A B & A Matthews, Newton Stewart.
