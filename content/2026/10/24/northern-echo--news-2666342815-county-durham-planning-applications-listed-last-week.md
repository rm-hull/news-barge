Durham County Council has received a range of new planning applications recently, including proposals for home extensions, farm conversions, and changes to local amenities. The following is a summary of the latest applications submitted to the council.

* 10 Wilton Court, Newton Aycliffe, DL5 7PU. Proposed first-floor extension, garage conversion, window alterations and new rear bifold doors. Ref. No: DM/26/02807/FPA
* 29 Hedley Terrace, South Hetton, Durham, DH6 2UE. Single storey side and rear wrap-around extension. Ref. No: DM/26/02805/FPA
* Land To The North Of Lowhills Road And West Of Thorpe Road, Littlethorpe. Non-material amendment to allow a phased approach for remediation and validation requirements throughout the development. Ref. No: DM/26/02791/NMA
* 12 Picktree Terrace, Chester-le-Street, DH3 3ST. Construction of rear dormer window. Ref. No: DM/26/02801/CPO
* Woodhey, Bells Folly, Potters Bank, Durham, DH1 3RR. Lime tree (T1) crown reduction, re-pollard and removal of epicormic growth. Ref. No: DM/26/02800/TPO
* Durham C I U Headquarters Ltd, Club Union House, Flass Street, Durham, DH1 4EF. Installation of a non-illuminated aluminium panel. Ref. No: DM/26/02786/AD
* Land West Of Marley Fields, Wheatley Hill, DH6 3BF. Non-material amendments to house types, floor plans, brick choice, and minor footpath changes, plus amendment to Condition 16 to make it a compliance condition. Ref. No: DM/26/02729/NMA
* Tweddle Farm, Fillpoke Lane, Blackhall Rocks, Hartlepool, TS27 4BT. Erection of one detached self-build family rural worker dwelling with associated access, parking, landscaping and ancillary works. Ref. No: DM/26/02740/FPA
* Weather Hill House, Brancepeth, Crook, DL15 9AS. Partially retrospective formalisation and resurfacing of access track. Ref. No: DM/26/02698/FPA
* Wheatside Farm, Bildershaw, Bishop Auckland, DL14 9PL. Class Q conversion of cow byre to a dwelling. Ref. No: DM/26/02607/PN56
* Manor House, Mordon, Stockton On Tees, TS21 2EY. Stables on existing concrete apron. Ref. No: DM/26/02593/FPA
* 1 Balfour Gardens, Consett, DH8 5JL. Erection of two storey extension to south and single storey conservatory to east. Ref. No: DM/26/02537/FPA

**For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo (publicnoticeportal.uk).**
