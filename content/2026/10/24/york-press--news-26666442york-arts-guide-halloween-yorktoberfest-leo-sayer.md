## Music legend brings final UK tour to York venue for emotional night

Leo Sayer: Playing York Barbican on his last tour

**FROM a new female spin on ‘Curious Incident’ to Yorktoberfest in a circus big top, Halloween shows to Leo Sayer’s last tour, Charles Hutchinson finds ways to brighten up darker nights**

What's on - our pick...

*Comedy gig of the week: Troy Hawke, Never Stop, Never Change!,York Barbican, tonight, 7.30pm*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625825.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Troy Hawke: Navigating conversational minefields at York Barbican

TROY Hawke is the internet sensation creation of Michael Patrick "Milo" McCabe, the British television presenter, actor and stand-up character comedian. In Never Stop, Never Change!, angry security guards, barked compliments and crossed wires abound as Hawke navigates various conversational minefields, both online and in real life. Box office: yorkbarbican.co.uk.

*Couple of the week: Stephen Joseph Theatre and Live Theatre, Newcastle, present All At Sea, Stephen Joseph Theatre, Scarborough, until October 31*

MARIA Crocker directs Phil Corbitt and Sarah Belcher in All At Sea, Alison Carr’s new play inspired by the escapades of John and Anne Darwin in the early 2000s. Colin and Deborah are a couple with spiralling debt, a sky-high mortgage and seemingly nowhere to turn. Then Colin comes up with the idea of faking his own death in a canoeing accident in the North Sea for an insurance payout. Box office: 01723 370541 or sjt.uk.com.

*Festival of the week: Jamboree Entertainment presents Yorktoberfest, Clocktower Enclosure, York Racecourse, today,1pm to 5pm & 7pm to 11pm*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625827.jpg?type=mds-article-620)York vocal drag diva Velma Celli with oompah band The Bavarian Strollers in Yorktoberfest’s big top at York Racecourse

FOR the first time, James Cundall’s Jamboree Entertainment is staging Yorktoberfest, York’s traditional Bavarian beer festival, inside a Big Top, launched last weekend by aerial artist Zoe Kulyk twisting and tumbling from the rooftop.

Look out today for outrageous York drag diva Velma Celli’s powerhouse singing and quick-fire critique of the costumes on display, complemented by oompah band The Bavarian Strollers’ traditional drinking songs and disco classics.Tickets: www.ticketsource.co.uk/yorktoberfest and at the entrance, subject to availability.

*Reboot of the week: Melting Pot and Birmingham Rep present The Curious Incident Of The Dog In The Night-Time, Grand Opera House, York, October 27 to 31, 7.30pm plus 2.30pm Wednesday and Saturday matinees*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625830.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alex Keenan’s Chris Boone and her dad, Joe McFadden’s Ed, in Melting Pot and Birmingham Rep’s The Curious Incident Of The Dog In The Night-Time. Picture: Johan Persson

MELTING Pot and Birmingham Rep are touring a gender-swap reboot of Simon Stephens’s inventive adaptation of Mark Haddon’s compassionate novel, in which neurodivergent 15-year-old Chris Boone is now a female role, played by Alex Keenan and matinee alternate Imi Price.

Ned Bennett’s cast features Joe McFadden as dad Ed and Charlie Brooks as absentee mum Judy in the story of Chris becoming both detective and suspect when a neighbour’s dog is discovered killed, drawing her into a mystery that grows darker and more complex with every clue uncovered. Box office: atgtickets.co.uk/york.

*Graveyard smash of the week: Halloween In Concert, York Barbican, October 27, 7.30pm*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625828.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The poster for Halloween In Concert at York Barbican

IN a frighteningly good night of raves from beyond the grave, Halloween In Concert’s cast and band unleash a thrilling crescendo of all things Halloween. Expect big vocals and killer moves as the greatest spooky hits are resurrected, from Monster Mash, Highway To Hell, The Time Warp and Running Up That Hill to Hungry Like The Wolf, Maneater, Bat Out of Hell, She Wolf and Thriller.Box office: yorkbarbican.co.uk.

*Halloween family entertainment of the week: Wharfemede Productions in The Addams Family Musical, Theatre@41, Monkgate, York, October 27 to 31, 7.30pm plus 2.30pm Saturday matinee*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625831.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Abbie Law’s Wednesday Addams in Wharfemede Productions’ Halloween show, The Addams Family Musical

COMPANY co-founder Helen Spencer directs North Yorkshire company Wharfemede Productions in Andrew Lippa, Marshall Brickman and Rick Elice’s devilishly funny, deeply kooky comedy The Addams Family Musical.

When Wednesday Addams falls in love with a “normal” young man and invites his respectable parents to dinner, chaos, secrets and macabre mayhem ensue in this spooky celebration of family, love and embracing the wonderfully weird, replete with sharp comedy, dazzling dance numbers, a witty score and a nine-piece band. Box office: tickets.41monkgate.co.uk.

*Halloween solo show of the week: Christopher Leslie in Jekyll & Hyde: Unbound, York Medical Society, Stonegate, York, October 27 to 30, 7.30pm*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625832.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christopher Leslie in Jekyll & Hyde: Unbound at York Medical Society

YORK writer, performer and Pop Yer Clogs Theatre company member Christopher Leslie presents his haunting one-man adaptation of Robert Louis Stevenson’s “bogey tale”, here re-titled Jekyll & Hyde: Unbound.

Lawyer Gabriel John Utterson, now living a sheltered, haunted life, invites you into the Soho house once occupied by his former client, Doctor Henry Jekyll. Tonight, Utterson will speak at last, looking into the darkness of the human psyche and asking are we really who we appear to be? Box office: 01904 623568 or yorktheatreroyal.co.uk.

*York premiere of the week: York Actors Collective in The Strongbox, York Theatre Royal Studio, October 29, 2.30pm & 7.45pm, October 30, 7.45pm, and October 31, 2pm*

YORK Actors Collective presents Stephanie Jacob’s (CORRECT) study of freedom and control The Strongbox, in which a mother (Beryl Nairn’s Ma) and her daughter (Victoria Delaney’s Kat) keep a homeless woman (Clare Halliday’s Maudie) as a slave in their London home. Power shifts, however, when the daughter becomes distracted by business and the mother falls ill.

"Three women learn how to live and survive by making their own rules, but at what cost?” asks director Angie Millard. “As Ma says, ' If you do and do and do and do to someone, can it be undone?' That’s the question the play explores with honesty and some humour." Box office: 01904 623568 or yorktheatreroyal.co.uk.

*The show must go on...for one last tour: Leo Sayer, Can’t Stop Loving You, York Barbican, October 30, 7.30pm*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21625824.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Leo Sayer: Playing York Barbican on his last tour

AFTER almost 60 years on the road, Leo Sayer, now 78, returns to the UK stage for “what could well be his final full UK tour”. “I love performing for my fans and look forward to doing so for many more years, but this will be the last time I play so many dates across the country. I am not retiring, just slowing the number of live shows down a little,” clarifies Sayer, in a year when he is releasing his autobiography and a career-spanning box set. Cue The Show Must Go On, Thunder In My Heart, When I Need You, You Make Me Feel Like Dancing et al. Meanwhile, Brighton folk-rockers Levellers have sold out October 29’s York Barbican gig. Box office: yorkbarbican.co.uk.
