A car was damaged after overturning in a crash overnight on a busy Bradford road.

A overturned onto its roof in the crash at the Beckside Road and Spencer Road roundabout in Lidget Green.

The exact time of the crash is unknown, but it is believed to have happened late last night or in the early hours of this morning

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police described the crash as a "damage only road traffic collision".
