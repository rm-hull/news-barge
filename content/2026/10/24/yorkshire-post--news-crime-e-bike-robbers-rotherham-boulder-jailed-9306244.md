# Robbers who crashed into boulder after knocking cyclist from e-bike jailed

## One of the men was found by police pretending to be asleep in the boot.

Three men have been jailed for robbery in which a cyclist was knocked off an e-bike by a car.

Police first received reports a man had been robbed of an e-bike on Westfield Road, Brampton, near Rotherham, on July 20, 2025.

The victim reported a red Audi A4 had knocked him off the bike, before a man got out of the vehicle, took his bike and cycled off in the same direction as the car.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said: “Within 20 minutes of the incident, roads policing officers sighted the vehicle and requested it to stop.

“After failing to stop, a short pursuit commenced in which the driver of the Audi drove at high speeds narrowly avoiding collisions with other vehicles before colliding with a boulder close to Manvers Lake.

The driver, Dale Cheatham, and the front seat passenger, Christopher Ogley, made off on foot and were detained nearby by a police dog and handler.

Within the Audi, officers recovered the e-bike and then discovered Sam Kitchen pretending to be asleep in the boot of the vehicle.

“Following the incident, officers recovered extensive CCTV which showed the moments immediately prior to the robbery, as well as the three men trying to fit the bike in the boot of the Audi,” SYP said.

“The footage also showed Kitchen getting in the boot before the car drove off.”

![Pictured left to right: Sam Kitchen; Dale Cheatham and Christopher Ogley](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/23/10/59/Jailed.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The men were charged with robbery, and Cheatham was also charged with driving a motor vehicle dangerously.

They pleaded guilty and appeared at Sheffield Crown Court on Tuesday, October 20.

Cheatham, aged 30, of no fixed abode, was jailed for five years and disqualified from driving for five years and six months.

Ogley, 38, of Furlong Road, Bolton-upon-Dearne, was jailed for three years and nine months.

Kitchen, 29, of Windsor Street, Thurnscoe, was jailed for two years.
