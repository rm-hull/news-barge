Emergency services were called to a motorbike on fire in York last night (Friday).

Fire crews went to the incident on **Rawdon Avenue** at**8.28pm**.

They said the motorbike was “well alight” when they arrived.

#### Incident location

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews used two knapsack sprayers to extinguish and dampen down and the incident was left in the hands of the police.”
