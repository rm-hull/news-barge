Motorists are being urged to take extra care after a large tree branch fell onto a road near Castle Eden amid strong winds from Storm Boleo.

A warning shared on social media said the branch had come down on Castle Eden Road, partially blocking the carriageway on a blind bend.

The post read: "A large tree branch has fallen into the road on a blind bend on Castle Eden Road.

"The branch could cause an accident or damage vehicles, so please take extra care if travelling through the area."

Drivers have been advised to approach the area cautiously and remain alert for debris on the road.

The fallen branch comes as an **amber wind warning covering Scotland and northern England from Saturday evening until Sunday evening** has been issued.

Anyone travelling through the area is urged to slow down and take extra care until the obstruction can be cleared.
