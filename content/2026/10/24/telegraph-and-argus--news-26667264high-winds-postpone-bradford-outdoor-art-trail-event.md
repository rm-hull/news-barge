High winds have forced the postponement of an outdoor art trail in Bradford.

The Take A Way Back exhibition, organised by the Braford Culture Company, was due to take place at Shipley Glen and Baildon Moor over the weekend.

It was scheduled to begin at Bracken Hall on Saturday, but was postponed after Storm Austen hit the South and with Storm Boelo making its presence felt in Bradford.

Braford Culture Company posted on Facebook: "Take A Way Back is postponed until October 31- November 1 due to Storm Austen."

The Met Office issued amber wind warnings for Scotland and parts of Northern Ireland and northern England.

Storm Boelo is expected to bring gusts of 60 to 70mph, with exposed coastal areas possibly reaching 80mph.

The Met Office forecast for Bradford read: "This afternoon will be unsettled as Storm Boelo moves closer.

"It will be very wet as a band of persistent rain moves through from the west, clearing to blustery showers in the evening. Very windy.

"Tonight will remain unsettled and windy.

"It will be cloudy with scattered blustery showers pushing in from the west, more frequent around dawn.

"Tomorrow will remain unsettled but somewhat less windy.

"There will be a mixture of patchy cloud, some sunny spells and one or two blustery showers pushing in from the west."

Take A Way Back is described as part walk, part artwork, and part invitation to wander.

It will now take place from October 31 to November 1.
