Among the cafés, bars and restaurants lining a bustling Teesside High Street is an independent spot bringing something a little different to the table.

Café Nossa, on Yarm High Street, is a Portuguese deli serving pastries, artisan cheeses and cured meats alongside coffee and a brunch menu inspired by Portugal and the wider Iberian region.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21274574.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cafe Nossa, an independent Portuguese deli on Yarm High Street *(Image: Hannah Chapman)*

The independent spot offers somewhere to enjoy breakfast or brunch, stop for coffee and something sweet, or browse a selection of fine wines, cheeses and meats.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623924.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Café Nossa *(Image: MICHAEL LAZENBY)*

The breakfast menu options include toasted banana bread, scrambled eggs with spicy chorizo, berry waffles and poached eggs on toast, mixing familiar favourites with Portuguese flavours.

For those arriving later in the day, the all-day brunch menu features burgers, bagels, omelettes and flatbreads, alongside a tempting selection of sweet pastries, savoury croissants and cakes at the counter.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21274570.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fresh bakes at Cafe Nossa in Yarm *(Image: Hannah Chapman)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623923.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pastries at Café Nossa *(Image: MICHAEL LAZENBY)*

The Portuguese deli side of the business also brings together artisan cheeses, cured meats, freshly baked pastries and other produce, as well as Portuguese wines.

Café Nossa brings the flavours of Portugal to the heart of Yarm, whether customers stay to catch up with friends over brunch and coffee, or take something new to enjoy on the go.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623922.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pastel de nata at Café Nossa *(Image: MICHAEL LAZENBY)*

When The Northern Echo visited the café in August, the café was busy throughout, but staff provided friendly, welcoming service despite the steady stream of customers.

On a quieter morning in Yarm, café Nossa is the perfect place to pause, take in the High Street and watch the day go by.

Café Nossa is at 109 High Street, Yarm, and is open Tuesday to Sunday from 8am to 4pm.
