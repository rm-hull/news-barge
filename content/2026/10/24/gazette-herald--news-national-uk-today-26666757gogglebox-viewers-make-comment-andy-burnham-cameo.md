Prime Minister Andy Burnham’s appearance on Gogglebox for SU2C (Stand Up to Cancer) has sparked a wave of criticism and debate among Channel 4 viewers.

Mr Burnham became the first Prime Minister to appear on Gogglebox on Friday (October 23) night.

He was joined on the Channel 4 show by his close friend and Liverpool City Region Mayor Steve Rotheram.

The pair featured alongside a host of other celebrities, including Stanley Tucci, the Sugababes, and Joe Lycett.

During his time on the show, Mr Burnham watched snippets from various TV programmes, including the BBC’s Celebrity Traitors, Strictly Come Dancing, and Only Connect.

## Gogglebox viewers all make same comment about Andy Burnham appearance

Mr Burnham's appearance on Gogglebox has divided opinion online, with many questioning whether it was appropriate for the country’s leader to take part in a light-hearted TV show during a time of national difficulty.

One viewer, posting on X (formerly Twitter), said: "Our country and economy is failing and our leader has time to go on Gogglebox.

"What the actual fruit is going on."

Another added: "A PRIME MINISTER should NOT be sitting about going on TV shows #Gogglebox especially when country in complete distress and at crisis point in every form of political needs dealt with.

"Your job is running the country, not having a laugh+taking the piss."

## Andy Burnham "not going to apologise" for Gogglebox appearance

The Prime Minister has defended his decision to take part on Gogglebox, saying he was "proud" to support SU2C and would "make no apology" for trying to lead with "a bit of lightness."
