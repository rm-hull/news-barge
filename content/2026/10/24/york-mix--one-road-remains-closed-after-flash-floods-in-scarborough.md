Most roads have reopened after the flash floods in Scarborough.

But Valley Road remains shut, and police are asking people to avoid the area.

Many town roads were left underwater after heavy rain last night.

#### Valley Road, Scarborough

It led police to urge people to stay away. Many cars were stranded in the floodwaters.

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service were called to multiple properties that had been inundated.

This morning, North Yorkshire Police said: “All roads affected by last night’s flooding in Scarborough have now reopened, with the exception of Valley Road, which remains closed.

“Please avoid the Valley Road area where possible and follow all road closure signs and diversions.

“Any members of the public impacted by the flooding who need urgent help can call North Yorkshire Council’s customer services team on 0300 131 2131.”

A yellow weather warning for strong wind is in place covering all of North Yorkshire from midday today (Saturday) till 9pm tomorrow.
