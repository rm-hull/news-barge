# Man left with serious facial injuries after 1am attack inside club

## The man suffered serious facial injuries while at a Morley club

Police are appealing for information after a man was attacked in a Morley club.

The man, in his 20s, has been left with serious facial injuries following the assault at the Vibe Bar & Club on Peel Street at around 1am on Friday, October 9.

West Yorkshire Police says it is appealing for anyone who may have any information that could help the investigation to come forward.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police are investigating a report of a man being assaulted at Vibe Bar & Club on Peel Street, Morley, that happened at 1am on Friday 9 October.

"The victim, a man in his 20s, was left with serious facial injuries.

"A number of active enquiries reman ongoing into the incident by Leeds CID."

Anyone with any information should contact Leeds CID on 101 or the live chat here. You should quote the crime reference number 13260575883 when passing over any information.
