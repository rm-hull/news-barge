Police are investigating after a home's front door was damaged in a York town.

The incident happened in Old Orchard, Haxby, between midnight and 8am on Wednesday (October 21).

North Yorkshire Police said an "unknown person has caused damage to front door on Old Orchard".

The force has urged anyone with information about the damage to get in touch.

Those with information should email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260206883 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
