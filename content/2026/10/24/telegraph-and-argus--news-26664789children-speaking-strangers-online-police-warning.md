CHILDREN are speaking to strangers online.

The warning comes from PCSO Gemma Simpson, who is part of West Yorkshire Police’s new Cyber Champions scheme.

As one of the first three Cyber Champions in the force, she aims to raise cyber crime awareness and promote safer online habits in schools, businesses, and communities.

PCSO Simpson said: "Most children from the age of around seven have a mobile phone that has apps and accounts and they’re not aware of the issues that can happen.

"It’s important to teach children when they’re young.

"When I’ve visited schools, children have often told me they have spoken to strangers online."

Cyber Champions support West Yorkshire Police’s Cyber team by offering advice on online safety, including password protection, email security, and two-factor authentication.

They are also available to help businesses, many of which may not have access to professional cyber protection.

PCSO Simpson said: "Some local businesses can’t afford cyber protection, and some have said they’ve previously been hacked.

"It can happen more than people think."

Emma Davies, cyber protect officer at West Yorkshire Police, believes the scheme is a valuable addition.

She said: "This is a fantastic initiative that provides our officers on the front line with the opportunity to build on their tools."
