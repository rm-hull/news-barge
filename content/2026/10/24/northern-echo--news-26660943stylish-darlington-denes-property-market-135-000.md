THERE’S a lot to be said for buying a home you can simply move into and enjoy, and this house on Hamsterley Street, Darlington – which is presented in lovely condition throughout – is exactly that.

Its neutral decor and contemporary finishes create a bright, welcoming feel, ensuring its new owners will feel at home from day one.

Briefly, its accommodation includes a vestibule that opens into the generous and tastefully decorated lounge with a bay window to the front and a feature fireplace with a living flame gas fire at its heart.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21617861.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Beautifully decorated and maintained, this property in the heart of Darlington’s Denes would make an ideal first home *(Image: Supplied)*

Next to this is the dining room, another good-sized space that could double as a family room and has handy understairs storage.

This flows into the kitchen extension where the modern fitted units which include an electric oven and gas hob are arranged in a galley style configuration.

A useful utility room and WC, with plumbing for a washing machine, completes the ground floor.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21617860.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Beautifully decorated and maintained, this property in the heart of Darlington’s Denes would make an ideal first home *(Image: Supplied)*

Upstairs are the family bathroom with a P-shaped bath with a mains-fed overhead shower and two good-sized bedrooms, including the generous double principal bedroom which sits at the front of the house.

The property has gas central heating and is fully double glazed.

To the front is an enclosed forecourt with established trees and shrubs, while to the rear is a low-maintenance courtyard with artificial grass that has room to create a bijou seating area where you can soak up the sun.

The house is in the Denes area, close to the town centre and a park, and with good road and rail links making it a smart choice for commuters, first-time buyers and those looking to downsize.
