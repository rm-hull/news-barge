## ‘Charming’ apartment tucked away on busy York street for sale – look inside

The three-bedroom property for sale in Blake Street, York, for £395,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)</i>*

A “charming” apartment tucked away on a busy York street has hit the market.

The three-bedroom property is for sale in Blake Street for £395,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it currently operates as a “highly sought-after” short-term let.

The apartment is set over three floors with a ground-floor entrance hallway leading to the first-floor landing.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21590405.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the three-bedroom property for sale in Blake Street, York, for £395,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Two bedrooms and a modern bathroom are on the first floor.

A living room and kitchen are on the second floor.

And the master bedroom, with views over the city centre, is on the third floor.

An Ashtons spokesperson said the sale is a “rare opportunity in such a prestigious location”, adding: “This property is ideal as a city home or an investment opportunity.”

According to the listing, the apartment is leasehold property with 990 years remaining on the lease.

The annual lease insurance is £1,300 but there is no ground rent or service charge.
