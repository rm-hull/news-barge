A tiny village tearoom tucked away in the North York Moors has earned glowing reviews, with visitors saying it is worth going out of their way to experience.

Hawnby Stores and Tea Room is situated in the picturesque village of Hawnby, near Helmsley, and combines a traditional village shop with a café serving homemade food, cakes and locally sourced produce.

Despite its remote countryside location, the business has built an impressive reputation among visitors, earning a Tripadvisor rating of 4.9 out of five.

Customers have praised everything from the freshly prepared meals and friendly service to the peaceful surroundings and warm welcome.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21618439.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hawnby Stores and Tea Room *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

However, it is the enthusiasm of visitors, some of whom have returned several times, that makes the venue stand out.

One visitor stopped at the tearoom with family in August 2026 after a countryside walk.

The group enjoyed homemade leek and potato soup while sitting in the garden.

They praised the setting, service and value for money, describing their experience as excellent.

The reviewer urged others to make a special journey to the venue, saying: "go out of your way to come here."

Another customer visited for the third time in June 2026.

The visitor praised the food and friendly service after stopping for lunch during particularly hot weather.

They thanked the owners and said the experience represented good value for money.

A reviewer visited and described the business as a "Hidden gem in the North Yorkshire countryside!"

The customer praised the cosy interior, unusual menu choices and helpful staff, saying they were looking forward to returning.

Another visitor was similarly impressed after visiting in May.

Writing on Tripadvisor, they described the experience as "simply perfect" and praised the quality of the hot chocolate, professional service and reasonable prices.

The tearoom is run by Sally and Oli, who moved to North Yorkshire after previously working in the tourism industry in the French Alps.

The couple say they wanted to establish a business that celebrated local ingredients while providing a welcoming place for residents and visitors.

Their menu includes breakfasts, lunches, cakes and savoury snacks, with an emphasis on homemade food and Yorkshire produce.

The adjoining village shop stocks everyday essentials alongside locally produced food and drink.

It also sells some of the ingredients and products used in the tearoom, allowing customers to take home items they have enjoyed during their visit.

The rural setting has proved particularly popular with walkers exploring the North York Moors.

Visitors can enjoy refreshments indoors or make use of the garden seating during suitable weather.

The business also welcomes dogs, making it an option for those exploring the surrounding countryside with their pets.

Hawnby Stores and Tea Room is situated on Front Street, Hawnby, near Helmsley, YO62 5QR.
