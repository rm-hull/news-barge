Amanda Owen's daughters are preparing to bring back a much-loved family tradition at Ravenseat Farm in the Yorkshire Dales.

The van has long been associated with Ravenseat, the remote Swaledale farm made famous by Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm.

For years, walkers tackling the Coast to Coast route have been able to enjoy refreshments against the spectacular backdrop of the surrounding hills.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21526875.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edith Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Now the next generation is taking a leading role in preparing the van for its seasonal return.

Despite winding down for several years and the cream tea van not having a place on Ravenseat, it has now returned.

In the programme, Edith is balancing the preparations with her studies and her responsibilities on the family farm.

Her older sister Raven is also back at Ravenseat and helping with the work.

The pair are shown putting the finishing touches on the van ahead of the anticipated arrival of walkers during the warmer months.

Their efforts feature alongside the family's continuing restoration of Anty John's, the historic farmhouse close to Ravenseat.

The tea van has been one of the most recognisable aspects of life at the farm beyond the family's television appearances.
