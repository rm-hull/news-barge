A hidden brunch spot in a garden centre has been praised for its “delicious food” and coffee that is “to die for”.

If you are looking for somewhere a bit different to enjoy a bit of food, you might want to try The Bloom and Brew.

The café is found in the middle of the Spring Green Nurseries in Wakefield and offers visitors a chance to take a break from plant shopping.

The café is described as being “the ideal spot for a casual breakfast or a relaxed lunch”.

## What is The Bloom and Brew like?

The hidden brunch spot was visited by the content creator, The Yorkshireman, who tried a few dishes from the café.

He said: “One of our favourite parts was the matcha lattes in a can.

“The portions were great and we loved the green surroundings that lent well to the garden centre.”

The Bloom and Brew offers a range of “mouth watering dishes that cater to all tastes”.

You can start your day off right by having a full English breakfast, eggs Benedict or a toastie.

If you enjoy a sweeter breakfast, you can enjoy a plate of fluffy pancakes or a granola bowl.

You can also get more substantial meals like burgers and specials that frequently change.

You can also just visit the café for a hot coffee or iced matcha latte and a slice of homemade cake.

### **What do reviews say about The Bloom and Brew in Wakefield?**

The Bloom and Brew has a rating of 3.7 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 67 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “Delicious food & the coffee is to die for.

“We both had a sandwich which came on lovely fresh bread (choice of white or brown).

“The salad in the sandwich was so fresh & crunchy.

“Sandwiches are served with vegetable crisps & balsamic pickled onions (wish I'd asked where they were from as they were amazing!).

“We were sat near the kitchen so saw every meal that came out - wow!

“The portions were huge & all looked so appetising.

“Our visit was made even more pleasant by the lovely friendly staff & the beautiful clean surroundings.

“We'll definitely be back!”

Another person said: “One of the best breakfasts I’ve had & the latte was amazing too!

“Staff was so welcoming & polite - very friendly. Couldn't fault the service.

“Worth the price as you can tell the food is high quality.”
