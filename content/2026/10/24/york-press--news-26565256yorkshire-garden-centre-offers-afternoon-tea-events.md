If you are looking for somewhere to pick up some new plants for your garden or a special gift for someone, a garden centre could be the perfect place to go.

Garden centres not only have plenty of gardening equipment but many now have food halls, lifestyle sections, and even their own cafés.

Dobbies has garden centres across the UK and one can be found right on the edge of the city of York.

Dobbies offers a huge range of products as well as a place to enjoy a relaxing meal and events people can get involved in.

## What is Dobbies Garden Centre in York like?

Dobbies has a variety of plants, bulbs, seeds, and shrubs to help ‘transform’ your garden.

From houseplants and flowering bulbs to strawberries and garden roses, you can find almost anything you might want to decorate your garden with.

You can also find pots, garden tools, and compost to help with creating your perfect garden.

As well as things for your garden, Dobbies has homeware from pots and pans to kitchen appliances and mugs.

If you need a gift for someone, there is a gift section with candles, toys for children, and food hampers with something to suit everyone’s tastes.

Dobbies even has a range of events running throughout the year with festive wreath making workshops and a Santa’s Grotto for Christmas, and a pup-kin patch for you and your dog to enjoy some Halloween themed fun.

After exploring the garden centre, you can go for a meal in the onsite restaurant that serves up hot breakfasts, rolls, and a range of egg dishes.

For lunch, you can enjoy one of the restaurant's soups or pick between sandwiches, toasties, and jacket potatoes.

If you need something a bit more substantial, there are a few larger plates such as fish and chips, beef lasagne, and three cheese macaroni.

If you want to treat yourself, you can book an afternoon tea at the restaurant, which comes with freshly made sandwiches, plain and fruit scones with cream and jam, and a range of cakes.
