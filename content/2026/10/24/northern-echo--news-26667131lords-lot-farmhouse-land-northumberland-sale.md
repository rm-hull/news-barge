A detached farmhouse on land extending to approximately 220 acres is for sale.

The property, called Lords Lot, is located in a rural area around six miles south of Hexham.

Lords Lot Farmhouse is a traditional stone-built Northumbrian farmhouse beneath a slate roof, offering spacious accommodation.

![](https://www.hexham-courant.co.uk/resources/images/21613029.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lords Lot steading *(Image: Galbraith)*

The property comprises four double bedrooms, a family bathroom, two reception rooms, a kitchen, a pantry, and a cellar.

![](https://www.hexham-courant.co.uk/resources/images/21613043.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The farm is in south Northumberland *(Image: Galbraith)*

Estate agents Galbraith Hexham said the farmhouse occupies an elevated position and has "far-reaching views" across the surrounding countryside towards the North Pennines and beyond.

Externally, a generous garden lies to the eastern side of the farmhouse, enclosed by traditional stone walls, with an additional paddock on the western side which "could be incorporated as a back garden."

![](https://www.hexham-courant.co.uk/resources/images/21613045.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The detached farmhouse *(Image: Galbraith)*

Adjoining the farmhouse is a useful stone-built outbuilding which houses the boiler, together with a separate coal and log store.

The farm also boasts an extensive range of traditional and modern farm buildings.

![](https://www.hexham-courant.co.uk/resources/images/21613053.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside a farm building *(Image: Galbraith)*

These include a traditional stone range, a Dutch barn, two large livestock sheds, a lean-to, covered sheep pens, a tin shed, and a polytunnel.

The estate agents said the farm buildings offer "the opportunity for alternative uses, subject to obtaining the relevant consents."

The land at Lords Lot Farm extends to approximately 211.67 acres and comprises a ring-fenced block of predominantly south-facing pasture.

The holding offers productive grazing and mowing ground well suited to livestock enterprises, according to the estate agents.

The soils are described as slowly permeable, seasonally wet acid loamy and clayey soils, supporting permanent pasture across the holding.

The woodland extends to approximately 5.56 acres and comprises mature conifer plantations established as shelter belts, providing natural screening and protection across the holding.

The property is for sale by private treaty as a whole.

Lords Lot is located in a rural setting in South Northumberland, approximately six miles south of the historic market town of Hexham.

The town offers an excellent range of services and amenities, including highly regarded primary and secondary schools, independent and national retailers, leisure and recreational facilities, cafés, restaurants, Hexham Abbey, and Hexham General Hospital.

The town also benefits from a railway station providing regular services to Newcastle upon Tyne and Carlisle.

The A69 trunk road provides transport links, with Newcastle upon Tyne approximately 29 miles to the east and Carlisle approximately 43 miles to the west.

Both cities offer an extensive range of commercial, cultural and leisure amenities.

Newcastle International Airport is approximately 25 miles to the east, providing a comprehensive range of domestic and international flights.

The surrounding countryside is renowned for its rolling landscapes, picturesque villages and exceptional recreational opportunities, including walking, cycling and access to both Hadrian’s Wall and the North Pennines National Landscape.

The property is held freehold and offered for sale subject to a Farm Business Tenancy (FBT) terminating on May 15, 2027.

Lords Lot has a guide price of £1.3 million.
