Plans have been submitted to increase the number of heavy goods vehicles operating from a County Durham transport site.

R and R Civils and Utilities have applied to modify their Goods Vehicle Operator’s Licence to accommodate five more vehicles at their current base in Horden, Peterlee.

The company, located at 1 Station Cottages, Horden, Peterlee, SR8 4HN.

Owners or occupiers of land (including buildings) near the operating centre(s) who believe that their use or enjoyment of that land would be affected, should make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, stating their reasons.

Those who believe that the increase in the number of goods vehicles might affect their property or their use and enjoyment of the land are advised to make written representations.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

These representations should be directed to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, clearly stating the reasons for their objections or concerns.

Anyone making a representation must also send a copy to R and R Civils and Utilities at the specified address.

A government guide on making representations is available online for further details.
