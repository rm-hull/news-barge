# Boy, 15, riding unregistered e-motorbike left in critical condition after crash

## A 15-year-old boy has been left in hospital in a critical condition after the unregistered electronic motorbike he was riding collided with a car.

Police are now appealing for witnesses after the serious collision in Bradford on Thursday at around 5.20pm.

A black Audi S3 and an unregistered electric motorcycle, which the boy was riding, crashed in School Street, in the Holme Wood area.

The boy was rushed to hospital where he was found to have suffered a serious head injury. His condition is described as critical.

The Roads Policing Unit is investigating this matter, and is urging anyone who witnessed the collision, or who saw the movements of either vehicle prior to it, to contact them.

Anyone with footage which may assist this investigation is also asked to make contact.
