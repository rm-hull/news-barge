A fire near Tadcaster bus station is being investigated by police.

Firefighters were called to the incident at **2.11am today** (Saturday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Tadcaster responded to a small fire consisting of footing blocks from harris fencing and cardboard on fire next to an outdoor seating area.

#### Tadcaster bus station

“Crews extinguished the fire using a hose reel jet.

“It is believed the fire was started deliberately and police were informed.”
