## The 2nd Azerbaijan International Investment Forum brought together representatives from more than 70 countries to discuss investment, trade, infrastructure and diversification, with more than $10 billion in contracts and agreements signed.

Geopolitical uncertainty and disrupted supply chains are changing how businesses assess investment opportunities. At the Azerbaijan International Investment Forum, representatives from more than 70 countries discussed trade, infrastructure and long-term partnerships, with contracts and agreements worth more than $10 billion signed during the event.

Speakers highlighted the country’s position between Europe and Asia, while stressing that infrastructure, investor confidence and diversification will be key to future growth. Discussions also covered industrial zones, manufacturing, tourism, real estate and artificial intelligence.
