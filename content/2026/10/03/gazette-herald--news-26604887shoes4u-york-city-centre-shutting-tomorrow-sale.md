A YORK city centre shop is holding a sale after announcing it will be shutting its doors this weekend.

Shoes4U, which is located at 12-14 Spurriergate in York city centre, has revealed that it will be closing down tomorrow (October 4).

The shop, which opened its doors in 2024 after Goldsmiths closed, sells a range of women’s shoes, accessories, and clothing items for both men and women.

The reason for the closure is currently unknown, however it comes as the Coney Street revamp gets underway, with work beginning to improve retail space, add upper-floor accommodation and create new hospitality venues.

Plans for the wider project include creating a new public space called St Martin’s Yard, linking Coney Street to the riverfront as part of the **Coney Street Riverside project**,

Shoes4U will be open until tomorrow evening.
