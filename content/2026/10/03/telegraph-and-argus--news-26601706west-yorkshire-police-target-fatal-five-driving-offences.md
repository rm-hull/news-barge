Police carried out road safety checks at two West Yorkshire locations.

During patrols in Liversedge and Gomersal on October 1, West Yorkshire Police targeted the 'fatal five' driving offences under Operation Trimburg.

## What do police mean by 'fatal five' offences?

The 'fatal five' are failing to wear a seatbelt, speeding, driving under the influence of drink or drugs, using a mobile phone while driving, and driving without due care and attention.

Supported by Kirklees ward councillors and guided by community intelligence, the operation targeted Peep Green Road, Valley Road, Halifax Road, Church Lane, Bradford Road and Leeds Road.

![Google Maps image of one of the areas patrolled.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21529585.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Google Maps image of one of the areas patrolled. *(Image: Google Maps)*

During the evening operation, officers reported a non-compliant registration plate and two vehicles without MOTs on Halifax Road, along with mobile phone use and a vehicle failing to stop on Bradford Road.
