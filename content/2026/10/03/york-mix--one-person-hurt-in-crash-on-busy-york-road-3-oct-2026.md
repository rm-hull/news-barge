One person was hurt in a crash in York today (Saturday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on **Boroughbridge Road** at**11.49am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crew attended a single vehicle road traffic collision.

#### Boroughbridge Road, York

“Crews provided general first aid to one casualty. Incident then left with police.”
