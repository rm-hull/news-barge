A North East coffee chain is cutting hot-drink prices to 2016 levels for one day only.

Siesta Coffee will roll back prices across all five of its stores on October 20 as the business marks its 10th birthday.

The offer will be available at branches in Newton Aycliffe, Spennymoor, Peterlee, Thornaby and Catterick, with customers able to buy their favourite hot drinks at the same prices charged when the company first launched.

Siesta Coffee Club app users will get an extra discount on the reduced prices.

The promotion coincides with a landmark moment for the business, which has grown from a single café in Newton Aycliffe into a regional chain with locations across the North East.

A spokesperson for Siesta Coffee said: "We're taking all our hot drinks back to their 2016 prices as a thank-you to the regulars who've stuck with us since the start.

"It's our way of celebrating with the communities that have supported us over the last decade."

While the discounted drinks will be available across every store, the focal point of the celebrations will be Newton Aycliffe, where the first Siesta Coffee branch opened its doors in 2016.

Staff will cut a birthday cake and invite customers to add messages, photographs and memories to a wall celebrating the company’s journey.

For many regulars, the event offers a chance to revisit the early days of a business that has become a familiar sight on high streets across the region.

The one-day offer gives customers a rare chance to enjoy coffee prices from a decade ago, when costs were lower.

The 2016-price offer runs in every Siesta Coffee store on October 20.
