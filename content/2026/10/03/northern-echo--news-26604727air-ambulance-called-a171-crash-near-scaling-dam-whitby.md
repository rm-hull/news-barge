THE air ambulance was called to a serious crash in North Yorkshire in the early hours of this morning.

An air ambulance from Yorkshire Air Ambulance has responded to a serious crash that took place on the A171 near Scaling Dam, just outside Whitby, just after midnight today (October 3).

At the time of writing, the road is still currently closed and access to Whitby has been diverted down Roxby and Borrowby Road – however drivers have warned of gridlock in the area.

Reports state that recovery agents are currently at the scene.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been contacted for comment.
