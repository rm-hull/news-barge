Police are investigating reports that a lorry hit a bridge on a main East Yorkshire road.

According to reports, an HGV struck the underside of a bridge on the A63 between the junctions of Brough and South Cave.

It happened on the westbound carriageway of the A63 on Monday, 28 September.

#### The A63 between Brough and South Cave

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “Emergency services attended and both the eastbound and westbound carriageways were closed whilst the stability of the bridge was assessed.

“We would appeal for anyone who may have been in the area around the time of the incident specifically the driver of a yellow vehicle who was believed to be at the scene.”

If anyone has dashcam footage or witnessed the collision they are asked to call 101 quoting crime reference 26\*137120
