Renowned author Sir Alexander McCall Smith is set to visit Harrogate for a literature festival.

The celebrated writer, known for his hit series The No. 1 Ladies’ Detective Agency, will appear at the Raworths Harrogate Literature Festival on October 11.

Fans will have the chance to hear him discuss three new books: The Isle of Good Karma, The Big Cats Dance Party, and The Prime of Bertie Pollock.

His latest novel, The Isle of Good Karma, follows Mary Macdonald, a beloved teacher whose life takes an unexpected turn after a surprise windfall.

During the event, Sir Alexander will share the inspiration behind his stories and the themes that have made his work so popular.

The 78-year-old author has delighted millions of readers with his warmth, wit and profound understanding of human nature.

The event takes place at The Crown Hotel at 4.30pm, with tickets costing £20.
