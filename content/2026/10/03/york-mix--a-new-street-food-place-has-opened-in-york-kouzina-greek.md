A Greek street food chain has opened at York Designer Outlet.

Kouzina Greek opened its doors today, Friday 2 October, and is serving up ‘proper Greek and Cypriot street food’ – Gyros wraps, Greek street food boxes, loaded fries, and much more.

This is Kouzina Greek’s first location in York, with other venues in Cannock, Swindon and Alfreton.

The food outlet is ‘inspired by the home recipies of their Mothers and Grandmothers’, and the packed menu includes signature wraps with fillings such as chicken, halloumi, pork, and falafel; Cypriot pittas; salad bowls; loaded fries; and desserts including baklava and Greek orange cake.

Kouzina Greek is open at York Designer Outlet is from Monday to Saturday, 11am to 8pm, and Sundays 11am to 6pm.

Visitors can find Kouzina Greek in the food court next to YO! Sushi.
