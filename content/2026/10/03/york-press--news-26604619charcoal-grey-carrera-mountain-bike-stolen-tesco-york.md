A BIKE with distinctive features has been stolen from a York supermarket.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a bicycle was stolen from Tesco Askham Bar in York on Thursday (October 1).

The theft took place between 8.30am and 4.30pm and saw an adults charcoal grey Carrera mountain bike taken from the cycle racks, near the ATM, outside the store.

A spokesperson for the police said: "The bike has a bottle holder and the rubber ends of the handlebars have completely worn away."

If you have seen this bike for sale or discarded, please contact PC 1864 Marshall by emailing [Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Fiona.Marshall@northyorkshire.police.uk), report it through the website, or call 101.
