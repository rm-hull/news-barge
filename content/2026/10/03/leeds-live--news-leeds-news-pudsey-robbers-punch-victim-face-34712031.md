# Pudsey robbers punch victim in face and flee with wallet and cash in dawn attack

## Do you know these men?

Police are asking for help to find two men they want to speak to in connection with a robbery in Pudsey.

It is said the robbery took place at around 6.20am on Sunday, August 2 and reportedly involved a victim being punched in the face and threatened by two men.

West Yorkshire Police say the men then took the victims wallet and a quantity of cash.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Detectives at Leeds Crime team have issued images of two people they would like to identify following a robbery.

"It happened at about 6.20am on Sunday, 2 August at an address in Pudsey. The victim was punched in the face and threatened by two men. The victim's wallet and a quantity of cash was also taken."

If you can help police with their investigation you should contact officers on 101, or using the LiveChat on the force's website here while quoting the crime reference number 13260438904.

You can also pass information onto Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online here.
