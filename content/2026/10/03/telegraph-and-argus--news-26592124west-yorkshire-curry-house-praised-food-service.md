Known as the curry capital of Britain, Bradford is where you should go if you are looking for a good curry.

With so many restaurants to choose from, you might feel a bit overwhelmed or unsure which curry house to try first.

Found along Market Street just outside of the city, New Manzil Restaurant is one of the highly rated curry houses that could be worth a try.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21516762.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant has a four-course set menu. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is on the menu at New Manzil Restaurant?

The restaurant describes itself as a “tapestry of culinary tales, blending heritage with a modern touch”.

New Manzil Restaurant offers a four-course set meal, which costs £47 per person.

On the menu, you can find a huge range of classic starters from onion bhaji and chicken pakora to different chaats and samosas.

In terms of its main courses, there are lots of grilled speciality dishes like lamb tikka shashlik, lamb chops, and tandoori king prawns.

Of course, for those who love the familiar favourites, you can choose from a range of baltis and old school curries like korma, bhuna, and madras.

If you want to try one of the chef’s specialities, you can opt for keema beghun, deresh gousht or Nepal chicken.

There are lots of different rice dishes, naans, and sundries that you can enjoy alongside your curry of choice.

### **What do reviews say about the New Manzil Restaurant in Bradford?**

New Manzil Restaurant has a rating of 4.5 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 186 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “Absolutely brilliant service from start to finish.

“I ordered a korma, which was beautiful, sweet, and full of flavour, while my partner had the chilli garlic, which was spicy, tasty, and cooked perfectly.

“The staff were friendly, welcoming, and couldn’t do enough for us, making the whole visit feel relaxed and enjoyable.

“Prices were great value, especially for the quality of food and service.

“Highly recommended – we’ll definitely be returning.”

Another person said: “The food was great, full of flavour and really well cooked.

“The staff were lovely too – really chilled, very helpful and happy for us to sit and chat after we’d finished, even though we were the last ones there.

“It made the whole visit feel relaxed and comfortable.”
