A popular retailer in York has announced its reopening date following a huge refurbishment.

HMV on Coney Street in York closed temporarily on Monday 31 August for a ‘glow up’ – with a big transformation happening inside the music, film, and pop culture merch shop.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hmv-coney-street-york.webp)

Now, signs in the window have revealed that the store will reopen on **Friday 23 October at 9am.**

And they’re giving away a ‘free opening day swag bag’ to the first 25 customers to make a purchase on reopening day.

‘We look forward to welcoming you to our new shop!’

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hmv-coney-street-york-3.webp)

The retailer has traded in Coney Street since before 1990, and the major new refurbishment is set to inject a fresh breath of life into the high street staple.
