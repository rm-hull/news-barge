Here are the latest planning applications submitted to Durham County Council, featuring a range of proposals including tree works, home improvements, and school extensions.

Among the applications are plans for tree maintenance at several locations, installation of electric vehicle charging points, and a new SEN space at a local school.

Below is a summary of the most recent applications validated by Durham County Council:

* 10 Meadhope Street, Wolsingham, Bishop Auckland DL13 3EN. Works to three cherry trees, including reducing the height and crown spread by up to 1.5 metres. Ref. No: DM/26/02622/TCA
* Wolsingham School, The Grammar School Building, West End, Wolsingham, Bishop Auckland DL13 3DJ. Various tree works including removal of dead trees, crown lifting, removal of deadwood, and removal of tree stakes to multiple trees on the school grounds. Ref. No: DM/26/02621/TCA
* Kings Head Hotel, Station Road, Lanchester, Durham DH7 0EX. Removal of a large previously fallen limb from a willow tree. Ref. No: DM/26/02619/TCA
* The Old Well Inn, 15 - 21 The Bank, Barnard Castle DL12 8PH. Installation of slim-profile double glazing to 11 existing front windows and two first-floor bay windows. Ref. No: DM/26/02613/LB
* Eastgate Quarry Area, Billingshields Farm and Eastgate Works Site, Bishop Auckland DL13 2LG. Prior notification for a 3D seismic survey within an identified area of interest. Ref. No: DM/26/02629/PNME
* 4 Monks Crescent, Gilesgate, Durham DH1 1HD. Discharge of condition 3 relating to the HMO Management Plan from previous planning permission. Ref. No: DM/26/02610/DRC
* 14 Lee Hill Court, Lanchester, Durham DH7 0QE. Removal of a dead cherry tree and grinding out the stump to allow for reseeding. Ref. No: DM/26/02609/TCA
* Ivesley Lane Farm, Ivesley Lane, Waterhouses, Durham DH7 9HB. Prior notification for a new steel frame farm machinery store and workshop. Ref. No: DM/26/02605/PNA
* Land West Of Aireville Loaning, Burn Easington Village SR8 3TX. Discharge of conditions relating to contaminated land, construction management, and broadband from a previous application. Ref. No: DM/26/02590/DRC
* 16 St Ives Gardens, Leadgate, Consett DH8 7PQ. Crown reduction and reshaping of a sycamore by 2-3 metres and crown reduction of two cherry trees by 2 metres. Ref. No: DM/26/02589/TPO
* St Oswalds Church Of England Infant And Nursery School, Church Street, Durham DH1 3DQ. Single-storey side extension to create new SEN space. Ref. No: DM/26/02579/FPA
* 7 Atherton Street, Durham DH1 4DJ. Discharge of condition 19 regarding bat boxes from a previous planning application. Ref. No: DM/26/02560/DRC
* 87 Braunespath Estate, New Brancepeth, Durham DH7 7JQ. Retention of a freestanding raised deck and gazebo. Ref. No: DM/26/02511/FPA
