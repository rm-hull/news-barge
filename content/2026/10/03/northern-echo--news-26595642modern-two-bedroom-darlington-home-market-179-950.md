For house hunters who prefer the clean lines of a modern new build to the period features and sometimes fussiness of homes from a bygone era, this pretty semi-detached property might perfectly fit the bill – particularly for first-time buyers or a new family looking to settle somewhere of their own.

The current owners purchased the house from new and have, during the time they’ve lived in it, added personality throughout with tasteful decor and styling which really does showcase it to the highest standard.

For added peace of mind, it comes with the remainder of an NHBC guarantee.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21521616.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Light, bright, modern and beautifully maintained, this home is sure to appeal to first-time buyers *(Image: Ann Cordey)*

At the heart of the home is an open-plan kitchen, dining and living space which is well proportioned, light and bright thanks to a window overlooking green space to the front and French doors to the rear.

To one end of the room is a well planned kitchen area with a range of fitted units and integrated appliances including an electric oven and hob with extractor fan, washing machine and fridge/freezer.

Adjoining this is the dining area which can easily accommodate a family-sized table, then the lounge with French doors opening into the garden.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21521621.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Light, bright, modern and beautifully maintained, this home is sure to appeal to first-time buyers *(Image: Ann Cordey)*

A cloakroom completes the ground floor accommodation.

Upstairs are two generous double bedrooms and a family bathroom with a mains fed shower over the bath.

To the front of the property is a paved parking area with a planted flower bed, plus a gate to the side that provides access to the rear garden.

Enclosed by fencing, this has been landscaped to include lawn and a paved patio seating area covered by a fixed timber framed gazebo and a garden shed.

The property is located within Darlington’s West Park development and enjoys a lovely position within a cul-de-sac, looking onto open green space to the front.
