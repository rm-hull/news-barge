If you are looking for an October day out near Darlington, Baydale Farm's pumpkin patch is one of those places that feels tailor-made for autumn.

The farm, located on Coniscliffe Road in Darlington, is welcoming visitors throughout October for a traditional autumn experience, complete with seasonal fun and outdoor adventure.

Pumpkin picking at the farm is available until October 31 on selected Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, with additional dates during the October half-term.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21533746.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Daytime entry is free, with visitors simply paying for the pumpkins they choose.

Prices start at £2, with larger pumpkins costing more.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21533752.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

The farm offers a variety of pumpkins and squashes, suitable for carving, cooking, or autumn decorations.

As the pumpkin patch is part of a working farm, visitors are advised to wear wellies or boots, especially if the weather turns wet.

In the event of heavy rain and muddy conditions, Baydale Farm also has an indoor pumpkin store where visitors can continue to browse and buy.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21533747.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Baydale Farm owner Simon Walton *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Facilities on-site include toilets, and the route from the car park to the patch is flat, though access to the field may depend on weather conditions.

For those looking to extend their visit, the farm also hosts special evening events throughout October.

Its Dusk to Darkness sessions invite visitors to pick pumpkins under festoon lighting as night falls, with entry priced at £10 per car.

Pumpkins are charged separately.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21533745.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

The farm also offers Pumpkins & Pizza evenings, which include homemade stone-baked pizzas, fire pits, marshmallows, live music, and a licensed bar.

These extras make the visit more than just a quick pumpkin pick - it can become a full afternoon or evening out.

For families wanting to make a day of it, nearby food options include the Baydale Beck Inn on Coniscliffe Road, just a short drive from the farm.

Customers call it a "great shout," complimenting the beer-battered haddock that is "cooked perfectly with crisp batter and good chips."

The farm experience remains the highlight for many, offering a simple, affordable way to enjoy the season and make lasting memories.
