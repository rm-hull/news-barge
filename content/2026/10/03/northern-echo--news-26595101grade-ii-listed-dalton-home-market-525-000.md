Moor View is a deceptively spacious Grade II listed property that offers buyers a blend of period features and modern comforts throughout its beautifully presented accommodation.

Entry is into an extensive living room which stretches across the front of the property and is home to whitewashed stone walls, painted beams, windows with both shutters and window seats, and a cast iron stove which serves as a focal point for the room.

![PROPERTY](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21520783.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Moor View offers buyers a wealth of beautifully presented accommodation in a village setting *(Image: GSC Grays)*

Off the living room is a dining room with parquet flooring, a serving hatch to the kitchen and glazed double doors which open into the light-filled conservatory with stone floor which leads, in turn, into the garden.

At the heart of the home is the breakfast kitchen with an informal family dining area alongside.

The kitchen has tiled flooring, a range of fitted units incorporating rolled edge worksurfaces, an integrated Neff oven and dishwasher plus space for a range-style cooker.

Completing the ground floor is a utility room off the kitchen, a shower room with WC and a light-filled garden room with French doors to the rear garden.

![PROPERTY](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21520764.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Moor View offers buyers a wealth of beautifully presented accommodation in a village setting *(Image: GSC Grays)*

Upstairs are a family bathroom and four double bedrooms, including the principal bedroom with an en-suite bathroom.

Moor View’s enclosed rear garden is a true sanctuary that offers a private outdoor space to unwind in.

Enclosed by walled boundaries it includes borders with mature plants, shrubs and hedging, plus a patio perfect for soaking up the sun on.

The property also benefits from two garages and approximately 1.63 acres of land divided into two paddocks which is available by separate negotiation.
