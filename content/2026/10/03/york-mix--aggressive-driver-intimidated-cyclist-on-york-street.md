Police are investigating after a cyclist was intimidated by an aggressive driver on a suburban York street.

It happened at about **5.12pm** on**Wednesday**(30 September) on** Livingstone Street**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for witnesses and information following a public order incident and an allegation of driving without due care and attention on Livingstone Street, York.

#### Livingstone Street, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/livingstone-street-york-gsv.webp)

“It involved a Silver Seat Leon and a cyclist.

“It has been reported the silver vehicle has been driving dangerously, beeping its horn at the cyclist and driving close in an attempt to intimidate the cyclist.

“Officers are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident, saw the vehicle in the area, or may have relevant dashcam, CCTV or mobile phone footage.”
