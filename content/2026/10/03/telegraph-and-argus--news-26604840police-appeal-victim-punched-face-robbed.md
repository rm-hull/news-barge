Police have issued images of two people they want to identify in connection with a robbery.

The alleged incident happened at about 6.20am on August 2, at an address in Pudsey.

West Yorkshire Police said the victim was punched in the face and threatened by two men.

The victim’s wallet and cash were taken.

![WYP have issued images of two people they would like to identify.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21534146.jpg?type=mds-article-620)WYP have issued images of two people they would like to identify.

## WYP issue appeal for info

A spokesperson for WYP said: "Detectives at Leeds Crime Team have issued images of two people they would like to identify following a robbery.

"It happened at about 6.20am on Sunday, 2 August at an address in Pudsey.

"The victim was punched in the face and threatened by two men.

"The victim’s wallet and a quantity of cash was also taken.

"If you can assist this investigation then please contact the team on 101, or by using our LiveChat facility.

"The crime reference is 13260438904.

"Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online."
