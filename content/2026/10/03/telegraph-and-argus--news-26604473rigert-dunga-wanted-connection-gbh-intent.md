Detectives in Bradford are appealing to the public to locate a 22-year-old man wanted in connection with a serious offence.

Rigert Dunga is wanted by West Yorkshire Police in connection with an offence of grievous bodily harm with intent.

Described as 6ft tall and of medium build, Dunga has links to Laisterdyke, Bradford city centre and Headingley.

## What police could say

![Rigert Dunga](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533573.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rigert Dunga *(Image: WYP)*

A spokesperson for WYP said: "Detectives in Bradford are seeking Rigert Dunga, who is wanted in connection with an offence of grievous bodily harm with intent.  
  
"Officers have made numerous enquiries to locate Dunga, 22, and are now appealing to the public to assist.  
  
"He is described as 6ft tall and of medium build.  
  
"He has links to the Laisterdyke area of Bradford, Bradford City Centre and Headingley.  
  
"If you can help to locate him then please contact police in Bradford by using LiveChat, quoting crime reference 13260283660.  
  
"Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online."
