A man was rescued from a lift in York late last night (Friday).

Firefighters went to the incident on **Church Lane** at**11.23pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York crew were mobilised to assist a male out of a property due to lift malfunctions.

#### Church Lane, York

“Crews used crew power to assist male and no further action required.”
