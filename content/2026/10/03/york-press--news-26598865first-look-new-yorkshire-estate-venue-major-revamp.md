A new venue has opened at an historic East Yorkshire estate after a multi-million-pound restoration.

Hotham Hall Estate, south of the village of Hotham, has launched a new venue for board meetings, conferences and celebrations.

The privately owned Georgian estate, set within 117 acres of parkland, is preparing to open the venue's doors with a corporate launch on Wednesday (October 7).

It follows the major restoration by owners Linda and David Kilburn.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21525443.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bar area at Hotham Hall Estate *(Image: Supplied)*

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21525442.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bedroom at Hotham Hall Estate *(Image: Supplied)*

Linda said the work means a company can now take over the estate for an event.

“The ambition is simple,” she said, “to create a destination where businesses genuinely want to bring their people and their guests.

“A leadership team might arrive for a strategy day, meet privately, have lunch overlooking the estate and finish with cocktails and dinner.”

The estate has 13 newly created en-suite bedrooms in the Stable Block.

At the heart of the corporate offering is Hotham Hall's newly restored Georgian Stable, with an event space accommodating up to 120 guests seated or 250 theatre-style for conferences, presentations and workshops.

For larger events, Hotham's grounds offer opportunities for marquees and outdoor events for up to 450 guests.

And for smaller gatherings, the historic hall and its furnished rooms offer a setting for meetings.
