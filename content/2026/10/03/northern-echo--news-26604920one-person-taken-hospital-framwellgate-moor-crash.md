One person has been taken to hospital after a crash in Durham saw a large 999 response in the area.

Emergency services were called to Hutton Way in Framwellgate Moor at 1.48pm today (Saturday, October 3).

One patient was taken to University Hospital of North Durham (UHND) for further treatment.

One ambulance crew attended the scene.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Hutton Way in Durham at 13:48 on Saturday 3 October.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew and one patient was taken to University Hospital of North Durham (UHND) for further treatment."

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Constabulary for more information.
