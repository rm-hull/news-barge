A 15-year-old boy is in critical condition after a collision involving an electric motorcycle and a car in Bradford.

The incident happened early evening on October 1 on School Street in the Holme Wood area and involved a black Audi S3 and an unregistered electric motorcycle.

The teenager, who was riding the motorcycle, was taken to hospital with a serious head injury.

West Yorkshire Police are now appealing for witnesses or anyone with footage of the collision to come forward.

## Full statement from police

In a statement, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a serious injury collision in Bradford.

"It happened at about 5.20pm on Thursday, 1 October in School Street in the Holme Wood area of the city and involved a black Audi S3 and an unregistered electric motorcycle.

"The motorcycle rider, a 15-year-old boy, was taken to hospital where he was found to have suffered a serious head injury. His condition is described as critical.

"The Roads Policing Unit is investigating this matter, and is urging anyone who witnessed the collision, or who saw the movements of either vehicle prior to it, to contact them.

"Anyone with footage which may assist this investigation is also asked to make contact.

"The Roads Policing Unit can be contacted on 101, or via the Live Chat facility on our website. The log reference is 1259 of 1 October."
