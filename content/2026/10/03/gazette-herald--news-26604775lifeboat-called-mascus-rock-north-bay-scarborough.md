TWO women have been rescued after becoming stuck off the coast of a popular North Yorkshire beach.

Scarborough Lifeboat station has issued a reminder to visitors after being called out to help two young women who had become cut off by the rising tide on Mascus Rock in the North Bay.




* **Fire crews called to site of major fire in North Yorkshire Moors**
* **Air ambulance called out to serious crash in rural North Yorkshire - road closed**

The incident took place on Wednesday with Scarborough’s Inshore Lifeboat (ILB) called to assist.

A spokesperson for the service said: “The ILB crew quickly located the casualties, brought them safely aboard, and returned them to shore, where they were met by HM Coastguard.

“If you are planning a walk along the coast, please remember to check tide times before setting out to avoid being cut off by the incoming tide.”
