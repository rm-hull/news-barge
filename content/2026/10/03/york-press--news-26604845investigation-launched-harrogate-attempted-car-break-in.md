AN INVESTIGATION has been launched after an attempted break-in that took place in the early hours of this morning.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after an attempted car break-in in Harrogate that took place today (October 3).




* **Lifeboat called out at popular North Yorkshire beach**
* **Firefighters tackle deliberate fire near North Yorkshire transport hub**

The incident happened at around 4am this morning in Dragon Parade.

Officers are asking anyone who may have seen anything, recorded footage on a mobile phone, or has relevant home CCTV to come forward.

They are also appealing to drivers who were in the area with an active dashcam.

Anyone with information is asked to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260194160 when passing on information.
