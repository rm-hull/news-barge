The third housemate evicted from Big Brother 2026 has been revealed.

ITV's hit reality show returned for a fourth series (after the 2023 reboot) on September 13.

Millie was the first person evicted from Big Brother 2026 on September 18 and Chelsey was the second to be evicted on September 25.

Last night (October 2), the public voted for the person they wanted to be evicted, choosing between Rochelle and Johanne.

## Who was the third housemate evicted from Big Brother last night?

Following the public vote, hosts AJ Odudu and Will Best revealed Johanne was the third housemate evicted from Big Brother 2026.

By the time last night’s show started, AJ and Will said there was almost 1 million votes, and when the voting closed, the number of votes tipped “well over” that.

Reacting to the news of Johanne’s eviction on the official Big Brother Instagram account, one fan said: “One of the most iconic housemates ever”.

Someone else was gutted to see Johanne leave: “Just lost one of the best housemates”.

While radio presenter Dean McCullough said: “one of the best housemates in bb itv history”.

Another said: “Best news ever”.

Someone else commented: “Yay British people made the right decision”.

On leaving the show, Johanne, who is American, said being on the UK show is “not a moment I will ever forget.”

She added: “I do not take it for granted.

“I’m happy to have been a part of British television history.”
