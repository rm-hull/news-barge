North Yorkshire Council has made decisions on planning applications, including home extensions, a garage conversion, and a church sign.

The applications have been approved or refused, with details below.

### Plans for a flat roof extension at The Hollies

A proposal has been made for a flat roof extension to the rear elevation of The Hollies, Common Lane, Warthill, North Yorkshire, YO19 5XW.

The application also covers blocking up a door to the front elevation and a window to the side elevation.

Ref. No: ZE26/00685/HOUSE | Received: August 11, 2026 | Validated: August 11, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Garage conversion proposed at Birches

An application sought permission to convert the garage at Birches, Malton Lane, West Lutton, North Yorkshire, YO17 8TA, into an annex.

Ref. No: ZE26/00679/HOUSE | Received: August 10, 2026 | Validated: August 10, 2026 | Status: Approved

### First floor extension application refused in Warthill

Plans were submitted for the erection of a first-floor extension to an existing single-storey structure in the rear garden of Gower House, Rudcarr Lane, Warthill, YO19 5TL.

Ref. No: ZE26/00543/HOUSE | Received: June 26, 2026 | Validated: July 7, 2026 | Status: Refused

### Sign approved at Church of St Martin

Permission has been approved for the display of one non-illuminated sign alongside the north-west gates at the Church of St Martin, Main Street, Bulmer.

The application relates to the display of the sign at the church site.

Ref. No: ZE26/00496/ADV | Received: June 11, 2026 | Validated: June 11, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Window and external alteration works at Pear Tree Villa

An application covering retrospective works at Pear Tree Villa, High Street, Wrelton, North Yorkshire, YO18 8PG, has been approved.

The works include replacing all existing windows with double-glazed units, moving the existing boiler flue to ground-floor level on the east elevation, removing a gas pipe on the north elevation and repairing the existing outhouse door.

Ref. No: ZE26/00064/LBC | Received: January 26, 2026 | Validated: May 18, 2026 | Status: Approved

### Retrospective householder works approved in Wrelton

A second application covers the same retrospective works at Pear Tree Villa, High Street, Wrelton, North Yorkshire, YO18 8PG.

The proposal includes replacement double-glazed windows, relocation of the boiler flue, removal of the gas pipe and repair of the outhouse door.

Ref. No: ZE25/00878/HOUSE | Received: August 7, 2025 | Validated: May 18, 2026 | Status: Approved
