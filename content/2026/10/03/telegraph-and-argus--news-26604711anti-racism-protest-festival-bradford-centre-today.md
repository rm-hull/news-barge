At noon today (October 3), an anti-racism protest took place in Bradford city centre.

Assembling at City Park at 12pm, community organisers, political activists, charities and campaign groups from across West Yorkshire carried placards, flyers - and in some cases - donned spectacular homemade costumes in aid of the day.

![Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533901.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today

Stand Up to Racism Bradford organised the family-friendly Bradford & District Is For Everyone protest in response to “the increase in racism, hate, division and the rise of the far right” in the UK.

It included a unity march through the city followed by an indoor festival.

## Handmade puppet costumes

![Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533896.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today

Colourful flags littered the skies, and as groups congregated outside City Hall, we got the opportunity to speak with some of the protestors.

A group of Leeds students had taken the trip over to Bradford to take part in the protest, representing the Socialist Students.

![Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533899.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today

One of them told us: "There's a lot more of us [anti-racists] than there are of them [racists].

"I think we have to see what's behind this rise in right-wing attitudes and racist ideas.

"What we have to do [as socialists] is put forward that positive alternative."

![Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533902.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today

Another Leeds student told us: "It's greatly personal to be standing amongst other people fighting for the same cause: smash racism.

"It's important, to me personally, and as a collective, to just show up, to try stand against a growing tide of racism and violence against immigrants, migrants and ultimately people who are not the problem in society."

![Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533904.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today

We also caught up with local homelessness volunteer Matthew.

He works for Bradford Nightstop, a non-profit organisation supporting young people aged 18-25 at risk of homelessness.

![Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533906.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anti-racism protest in Bradford city centre today

Mathew felt it was vital that he showed up to promote "community cohesion" and remind people that "everyone is welcome" in Bradford.

## Here's a video compilation of today's march

The peaceful march then set off at around 12.45pm.

West Yorkshire Police officers followed the crowd from a short distance.

There were no visible signs of any counter-protests, and as the march snaked its way through the streets, passersby stopped to observe the spectacle.

Drums, singing, dancing and chanting continued, with occasional breaks for speeches.

## What police said

In an earlier statement, a spokesperson for WYP said: "Police are aware of an intention by supporters of Stand Up to Racism to march around Bradford city centre from around 12.30pm today.

"There will be a policing presence to allow the supporters to carry out their march safely and minimise any disruption."
