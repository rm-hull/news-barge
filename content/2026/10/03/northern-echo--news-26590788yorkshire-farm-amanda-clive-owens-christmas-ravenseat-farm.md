Amanda and Clive Owen will be seen celebrating Christmas with their family at Ravenseat when Our Farm Next Door returns.

The fourth series was filmed across winter 2025 and spring 2026 and will follow the family through several months of life in Swaledale.

Wise Owl Films, which makes the programme, said life at Ravenseat will continue "as usual" despite the huge amount of work taking place nearby at Anty John's.

The production company said viewers can expect everything "from Christmas Day celebrations to the busy Easter lambing season".

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21514993.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

That means the programme will follow the family from the depths of winter through to one of the busiest periods in the farming calendar.

The festive scenes will unfold against the everyday realities of running Ravenseat.

Wise Owl Films said Amanda, Clive and their family will continue juggling the farm's demands while caring for their large flock of sheep and numerous other animals.

There will also be new arrivals following the death of beloved family dog Chalky.

Away from Ravenseat, Anty John's will continue to dominate the family's workload.

The farmhouse is entering its third year of renovation.

Its plastered walls have finally dried and the focus can move towards painting, flooring and furnishing the interior.

Winter will also bring one of the biggest jobs yet, with the Owens attempting to bring a piped water supply across the moors.
