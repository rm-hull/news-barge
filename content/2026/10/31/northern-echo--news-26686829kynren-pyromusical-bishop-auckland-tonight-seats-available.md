The North East’s largest pyromusical is set to return tonight.

The Kynren Pyromusical will light up the sky from Bishop Auckland tonight (Saturday, October 31) with a 25-minute fireworks display set to music and a promise of "suitably spooky surprises" as the event falls on Halloween.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654292.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Kynren)*

A spokesperson for Kynren said: "There is something quite extraordinary about seeing fireworks on this scale choreographed precisely to music.

"You’re not simply watching a traditional fireworks display.

"The soundtrack and fireworks are designed to work together, so the music becomes as much a part of the experience as what is happening in the sky.

"This year gives us the added bonus of the event actually taking place on Halloween, so we couldn’t resist introducing a few seasonal touches.

"We’re not going to give everything away, but there will be fire pits glowing as darkness falls, plenty of warming food and drink, and a few surprises before the fireworks begin."

The event has become a staple in Kynren’s calendar outside its summer season, drawing crowds of around 8,000 and regularly selling out.

Seats are still available and gates will open at 4.30pm to allow visitors time to enjoy the site before the main event.

Food and drink options include hot dogs, soup, chips, mulled wine, and hot chocolate, with fire pits providing a cosy place to gather.

The spokesperson said: "It has become one of those events that people come back to year after year and we regularly sell every seat.

"The setting makes a huge difference.

"Sitting in the Tribune, looking across the Kynren stage and then seeing the entire night sky come alive in time with the music is difficult to replicate anywhere else.

"Having so many people experiencing that finale together creates a pretty special atmosphere."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654291.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Kynren)*

Standard tickets are priced at £14 for adults and £11 for under-18s.

VIP seating is available at £18 for adults and £15 for under-18s.

Children aged three and under can attend free if seated on an adult’s knee.

The pyromusical will begin at 7pm.

More information and tickets are available at kynren.com.
