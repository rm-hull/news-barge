Bradford is embracing iconic horror characters this Halloween as revellers prepare for a spooky weekend.

Ghostface from Scream, Michael Myers, Jason Voorhees, Freddy Krueger, Art the Clown and Beetlejuice are leading the way as costume-hungry partygoers search for the perfect outfit.

For one Bradford fancy dress shop, the rush has made this Halloween one of the busiest in years.

## Halloween sales provide a boost after a difficult year

Darren Reynolds, 58, owner of Doody's Fancy Dress at Royds Enterprise Park, says demand has surged as people look for an excuse to let their hair down.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21655195.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Doody's Fancy Dress this Halloween *(Image: Doody's Fancy Dress)*

"Halloween has been brilliant," he said.

"I think people are basically a bit fed up and they're wanting to spend and let their hair down a little bit for Halloween.

"We've had a terrible year from April to August, but for Halloween this year, we've done very, very well. It's been a really good one.

"The biggest one we ever had was post-Covid and this one is going to be either very similar or maybe even beating it."

## The city's last fancy dress shop

The business has been operating since 2008 and has spent the last 14 years at its current Bradford location.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21655194.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Doody's Fancy Dress this Halloween *(Image: Doody's Fancy Dress)*

"Over the years, gradually a lot of the shops have closed. I think we're the only one in Bradford now. It's a tough industry," Darren said.

Despite those challenges, Halloween remains the shop's standout season.

"It's the busiest time of year by a long way for fancy dress nowadays," said Darren.

## This year's most popular costumes

When it comes to what's flying off the shelves, horror remains king.

"Ghostface from Scream is probably the out-and-out best seller for us," Darren said.

Adult customers are also snapping up costumes inspired by other iconic big-screen villains.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21626510.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

"Adults will buy things like Freddy Krueger, Michael Myers, Art the Clown, Beetlejuice, Ghostface, all the sort of classic characters," he added.

Not everyone is looking to terrify their friends, though.

"We do get a lot of people coming in saying they just want something funny, or just an everyday costume, like a prisoner outfit or Top Gun, or just something daft really."

Meanwhile, superheroes continue to enjoy enduring popularity, particularly among people taking part in the ever-colourful Otley Run.

With trick-or-treaters, partygoers and pub crawlers all hunting for the perfect outfit, Darren says 2026 is shaping up to be a Halloween to remember for Bradford's last remaining dedicated fancy dress store.
