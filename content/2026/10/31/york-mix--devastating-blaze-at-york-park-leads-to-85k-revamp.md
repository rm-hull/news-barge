An insurance payout following a building fire is set to help fund a revamp to York park worth more than £85,000.

City of York Council plans for Holgate’s West Bank Park include a new pond, refurbished play area, a space for girls and a woodland bike and BMX track.

A council report on the decision stated the four most popular ideas from a public consultation had been chosen for the scheme, with £85,824 set to be spent.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/york-west-bank-park-lodge-fire-31.jpg)

The decision comes after council officials approved the use of the cash which the authority received after a blaze at the former park keeper’s lodge in 2016.

The lodge was devastated in the fire. A campaign to raise the funds to rebuild it eventually came to nothing.

Approval in January 2025 to use the payout, then estimated to provide a budget of around £83,000 for the works, came as the expansion of the park was also backed.

The expansion saw the park take in land previously occupied by the lodge at 14 New Lane.

Labour Holgate councillor Kallum Taylor and Chris Webb, of the Yorkshire Gardens Trust, both welcomed the move at the time as an opportunity to breathe new life into the park.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/west-bank-park-york-1.webp)

Ideas for improvements put forward during a consultation run by the Friends of West Bank Park last year also included new planting, a multi-use play area, a dog park and tea and coffee hut.

Council officials stated three tranches of cash would be given for works to create a new concrete pond, the renewal of the north playground and creation of the Make A Space for Girls zone.

The woodland bike and BMX track is still in its investigation phase, with the Friends of West Bank Park having offered to raise more money to enhance the scheme.
