Crews tackled a deliberate fire in a Selby park last night (Friday).

Firefighters from Selby went to the incident at a recreation ground on Scott Road.

A shopping trolley was alight when they arrived.

#### Scott Road, Selby

The fire was extinguished using a hose reel jet.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Police were requested and attended due to the fire being ignited deliberately.

“The incident was left with police to investigate.”
