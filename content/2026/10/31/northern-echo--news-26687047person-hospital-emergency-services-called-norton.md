A person has been taken to hospital after an incident in Norton.

Emergency services were called to the Mill Street and Summerhouse Square area of the village on Saturday morning (October 31) following an incident.

Pictures taken at the scene show at least three police vehicles - two cars and one van - parked on the roads alongside a large yellow ambulance vehicle.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654558.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and paramedics in the Mill Street area of Norton *(Image: CONTRIBUTOR)*

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that one person has been taken to hospital. The nature of the incident, and the person’s condition, remain unknown.

A NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to an incident at a private address in the Norton area of Stockton at 8.40am on 31 October.

“We dispatched a double crewed ambulance and transported one patient to hospital."
