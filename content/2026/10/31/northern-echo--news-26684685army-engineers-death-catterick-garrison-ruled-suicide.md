The factors and motives involved in the death of an army engineer at a North Yorkshire military base 'may never be known', an inquest has heard.

Salesi Makahokovalu Rabuku, 32, originally from Fiji, was found at Marne Barracks at Catterick Garrison army base on January 20 this year.

At an inquest into his death, held at Northallerton Coroner's Court yesterday (October 30), it was heard that Mr Rabuku moved to the barracks in December 2025.

During the hearing, statements from his colleagues, who described him as a "polite, considerate and kind" man, were read out by assistant coroner for North Yorkshire and York, Sarah Middleton.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/13447603.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

During his time at Marne Barracks, the inquest was told that Mr Rabuku never expressed thoughts of ending his life and never flagged up any mental health issues.

But his cousin, who was also in the same barracks, said he seemed 'subdued' over the Christmas period before his death, but put it down to feeling homesick and not returning to Fiji to see his family.

On the day of the engineer's death, concerns for his wellbeing were flagged by his wife, who lived in Fiji with his two children, to several of his army colleagues.

After checking on him, Mr Rabuku's colleagues found his body and alerted army medics and the police.

The 32-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene.

Summing up the evidence, the assistant coroner noted that it 'may never be known' why the army engineer took his own life.

"On January 20, at Marne Barracks in Catterick Garrison, Salesi Makahokovalu Rabuku was found unresponsive by his army colleagues," said Ms Middleton.

"A short time later, he was pronounced dead. He never expressed any concerns for his mental health and was described as kind, considerate and polite by his commanding officers.

"It may never be known the factors behind why he took the decision to end his own life.

"I offer my condolences to his family."

Mr Rabuku's death was ruled suicide.
