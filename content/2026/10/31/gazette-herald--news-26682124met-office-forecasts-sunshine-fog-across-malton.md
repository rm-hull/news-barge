The Met Office has forecast widespread fog and mist across the weekend in Malton.

Saturday morning will start off particularly misty and foggy, with very light winds allowing the fog to linger, especially in sheltered and low-lying areas.

Rain is extremely unlikely, with probabilities remaining below 5 per cent throughout the morning.

Temperatures will generally remain around 5-7C, although it will feel slightly colder.

Visibility could be significantly reduced in some locations, particularly on rural roads.

Saturday afternoon should bring a gradual improvement in conditions, although fog is expected to linger into the early afternoon before slowly lifting.

As the afternoon progresses, visibility should gradually improve, with overcast skies developing before sunny intervals emerge later.

Temperatures will gradually increase from around 5C at midday towards approximately 10C by late afternoon.

Saturday evening will remain dry and settled, with sunny intervals giving way to clearer skies as daylight fades.

Temperatures will initially be around 11-12C before gradually falling towards approximately 7C later at night.

Although conditions should remain clear for much of the evening, the combination of falling temperatures and light winds could allow fog to redevelop later, particularly in sheltered and low-lying locations.

Sunday morning will begin particularly foggy, with widespread fog expected during the early hours.

Fog is expected to remain widespread around 6am and 7am before gradually lifting into overcast conditions from approximately 8am onwards.

Temperatures will begin around 5C at 6am, gradually increasing towards 9C by late morning.

Sunday afternoon should bring a noticeable improvement, with overcast skies gradually giving way to brighter conditions and occasional sunny intervals.

Temperatures will rise from around 11C at midday towards a maximum of approximately 11C during the early afternoon, before gradually falling towards 8-10C later in the period.

Sunday evening should remain dry and largely settled, with partly cloudy skies gradually becoming clearer as the evening progresses.
