Detectives are seeking information after two officers were assaulted in West Yorkshire.

The incident happened on Wakefield Road in Dewsbury at 12.15pm on Friday, 30 October.

Officers approached a man and a woman regarding possible road-related offences, and were attacked while trying to arrest the man.

Additional officers were called to the scene, and a member of the public also helped.

One officer was taken to the hospital with a broken finger, while the other received cuts and grazes.

Both the man and woman were arrested.

The 35-year-old woman was arrested on suspicion of two offences of assault an emergency worker, obstructing a constable in the execution of their duty and a public order offence.

The 36-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of driving whilst over the prescribed limit, two offences of assault an emergency worker and obstructing a constable in the execution of their duty.

Detectives from Kirklees CID are keen to speak to anyone who was in the area and has footage or witnessed any part of this incident.

Information can be passed to Kirklees CID via Live Chat online or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260617480.
