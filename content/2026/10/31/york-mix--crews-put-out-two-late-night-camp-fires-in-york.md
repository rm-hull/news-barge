Firefighters were called to two abandoned camp fires late last night (Friday).

A crew from Acomb responded to a fire sighted in woodland off **Grange Lane** at**10.48pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “This was found to be a small campfire which had been left unattended.

#### Grange Lane, York

“Crews extinguished using a knapsack sprayer.”

Then, at **11.53pm**, a crew from York responded to a fire sighted in the open at** Osbaldwick**.

The spokesperson said: “This was found to be a small unattended campfire. Crews extinguished using a bucket of water.”
