A woman has been fined after admitting assaulting a police officer while she was on duty in Spennymoor.

Alisha McCreary, of Ashgill, Washington, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 26.

The 24-year-old admitted assaulting the female PC in the execution of her duty at Spennymoor on August 7.

Magistrates fined McCreary £120 and ordered her to pay the officer £50 compensation.

She must also pay £85 towards prosecution costs and a £48 surcharge.

The court took McCreary’s guilty plea into account when dealing with the offence.

A collection order was made, with payments to be deducted from benefits.

A separate charge of common assault of an emergency worker, involving the same police officer and arising from the same incident on August 7, was withdrawn.
