Six vehicles have been involved in a crash on a main road which links York to the East Yorkshire coast and The Wolds.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to the A166 in the village of Fridaythorpe at 1.40pm yesterday afternoon (Friday, October 30).

A service spokesperson said all occupants were out of the vehicles on its arrival and in the care of ambulance staff.

Fire crews made two of the vehicles safe and the scene was left with the police.
