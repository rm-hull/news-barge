An ice cream shop which made headlines for selling black ice creams at Halloween is celebrating a five-star food hygiene rating.

Bethanie’s @ Shakes, in Fountain Shopping Mall on Stockton High Street, was rated very good across the board by a food safety officer.

Stockton Council’s officer praised the shop’s handling of food including preparation, cooking, re-heating, cooling, storage and cleanliness.

The condition of its facilities and building were also rated highly including its layout, ventilation, hand-washing facilities and pest control to enable good food hygiene.

The officer also found a system of checks was in place to make sure food was safe to eat, with “evidence that staff know about food safety, and the food safety officer has confidence that standards will be maintained in future”.

Owner Bethanie Lofthouse said: “We are absolutely thrilled to have received another five-star food hygiene rating. Our team works incredibly hard to maintain the highest standards.

“This rating is a huge testament to their dedication, and we want to say a massive thank you to our staff and our wonderful customers for their ongoing support.”

The shop started selling a black ice cream, billed as a ‘healthier’ Halloween treat, in 2022. She added today: “Our Black Soft Serve will be available Saturday only – so make sure you head down for a spooky Halloween treat!”
