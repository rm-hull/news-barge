A fallen tree has been lifted from a new Darlington home with a gaping hole now leaving the roof open to the elements.

The huge tree came crashing down into the house on Homes by Esh’s Havelock Park estate during the height of Storm Boelo last Sunday (October 25).

The dramatic incident on Antiquary Drive took place just weeks before its new owners were due to move in - but thankfully nobody was injured during the ordeal.

A towering crane moved onto the housing estate earlier this week as contractors got to work on carefully dismantling and removing the tree from the detached home.

And new pictures taken today show that the tree has finally been removed, leaving a gaping hole in the roof of the property.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654904.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The aftermath of a fallen tree crashing into a home in Darlington *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Exposed wooden beams on the roof can be seen, with broken tiles and insulation left hanging from the home, which also has a gap in its red brickwork on the second floor.

The storm also left a neighbouring fence damaged, with roof slates landing on a nearby vehicle and smashing its windscreen.

The full extent of the damage remains unknown until the tree has been completely removed.

Once clearance work is finished, inspections will be carried out to determine the scale of repairs required to the property. It is not known how long this will take.

Neighbours previously told the Echo how new owners had been expected to move into the property next month, with photos showing debris scattered across the site.

Darlington Borough Council has confirmed the fallen tree was not council-owned and stood on land belonging to the developer.
