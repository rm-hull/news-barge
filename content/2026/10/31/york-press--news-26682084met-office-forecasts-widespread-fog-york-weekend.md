The Met Office has forecast widespread fog and mist for York this weekend.

Saturday morning will start with widespread fog and mist, reducing visibility significantly, especially in rural areas.

The fog is expected to persist throughout the morning, with very light winds at around 2 mph, making it unlikely to clear quickly.

Rain is not expected, with probabilities remaining below 5 per cent.

Temperatures will hover around 6C, but it will feel slightly colder.

By the afternoon, the fog should gradually lift, with brighter and sunnier intervals developing.

Temperatures will rise to around 9C to 10C, and the wind will remain light.

The evening will be dry and increasingly clear, with temperatures falling to around 6C to 8C, while the mist may return later in the night.

Sunday morning will begin similarly to Saturday, with widespread fog expected during the early hours.

The fog will gradually lift into overcast conditions from around 8am onwards.

Rain probabilities will remain relatively low, generally between 10 per cent and 20 per cent throughout the morning.

Temperatures will start at around 5C, gradually increasing to 9C by late morning.

The afternoon should bring a noticeable improvement, with overcast skies gradually giving way to brighter conditions and occasional sunny intervals.

Temperatures will rise to a maximum of approximately 12C during the early to mid-afternoon.

Winds will be slightly stronger than on Saturday, generally around 5 mph throughout the afternoon.

The evening will remain largely dry and settled, with partly cloudy skies gradually becoming clearer.

Temperatures will gradually decrease from around 8C at 6pm to approximately 5C by 11pm.

Mist or fog could redevelop in some sheltered locations, particularly towards the early hours of Monday.
