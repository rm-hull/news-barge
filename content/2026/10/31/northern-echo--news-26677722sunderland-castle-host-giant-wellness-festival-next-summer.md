A giant festival is taking over a Sunderland castle next summer for a weekend of health and wellness.

Hylton Castle and its grounds will be home to The North East Health and Wellness Festival, with organisers promising a three-day programme of fitness, yoga, workshops and family activities.

The festival will be held over a weekend in June 2027 at Hylton Castle, with tents popping up full of different activities and organisations for visitors to explore.

North East organisations Active Families North East, Hylton Castle Trust, Community Opportunities and The Wellness Collective are joining forces to create the festival, which they describe as the "region’s ultimate health and wellness celebration."

Organisers say all profits will fund free and affordable wellness activities across the North East.

Colin Burnicle, a trustee at Hylton Castle Trust, said the festival “embodies everything we stand for” and reflects the trust’s commitment to giving back to the surrounding community.

![The North East Health and Wellness Festival organisers outside Hylton Castle](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641524.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hylton Castle and its grounds will be home to a three-day programme of fitness, yoga, workshops and family activities. *(Image: The North East Health and Wellness Festival)*

Mr Burnicle said: "It’s about bringing people together, opening up our historic grounds to the whole community and making wellbeing something everyone can enjoy.

"We can’t wait to welcome visitors to Hylton Castle for what promises to be an unforgettable weekend."

Organisers say each day will target a different audience, starting with workplace wellbeing on Friday, June 4.

Saturday will be an adults-only day of relaxation and self-care, with the closing Sunday dedicated to family and community wellness.

Louise Kennedy, owner of festival sponsor Oculus HR, said: “We supported The Wellness Collective events right from the start because we could see the clear passion and purpose behind them.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641523.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Organisers say all profits will fund free and affordable wellness activities across the North East.

"Events which bring people together, inspire them to prioritise their own wellness and raise vital funds to allow the communities that need it most to access wellness activities again."

Organisers are calling for suppliers, workshop leaders and local businesses to join the festival.

Director of Active Families North East, Kelly Brougham, said: "We really want the very best of what the region has to offer in terms of wellness and health activities, which is why we wanted to open the event out even further to local suppliers, practitioners, and businesses.

“Whether you run yoga classes, offer holistic therapies or have a fresh idea for a family-friendly workshop, we’d love to hear from you.”

Tickets and the full itinerary will be released in the coming months.

People seeking updates should follow The North East Health and Wellness Festival on social media or visit The Wellness Collective's website.
