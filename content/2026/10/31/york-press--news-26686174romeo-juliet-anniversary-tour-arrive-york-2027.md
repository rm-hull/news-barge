A CULT classic is returning to the big screen in a whole new way.

Baz Luhrmann’s Romeo + Juliet, starring Leonardo DiCaprio and Claire Danes, will be shown with a live musical performance at York Barbican on October 21, 2027 as part of a global tour.

The event marks the 30th anniversary of the 1996 film and will feature a hybrid ensemble performing the film’s score and soundtrack in sync with the full movie.

Baz Luhrmann, the film’s director, said: "One of the greatest surprises over the past three decades has been watching Romeo + Juliet find new audiences again and again.

"That’s the enduring magic of Shakespeare and cinema.

"This concert celebrates both, while inviting audiences around the world to experience the film’s emotion, music, and theatricality in a completely new way."

The ensemble will blend rock and orchestral musicians, vocals, electronics, and a DJ to recreate the film’s signature sound.

Floris Douwes, producer and managing director at GEA Live, said: "Romeo + Juliet has influenced generations of filmmakers, musicians, and audiences, and its impact continues to be felt 30 years later.

"Our goal was to create a live experience that honors that legacy while giving audiences a fresh reason to fall in love with the film all over again."
