Bradford is known as the curry capital of Britain thanks to its many amazing Indian restaurants to try out.

However, if you are looking to head a little further out of the city to go somewhere new, you might want to try Syhiba in Wakefield.

The restaurant offers “an amazing selection of authentic Punjabi, Pakistani and Indian cuisine.”

## What is Syhiba in Wakefield like?

Syhiba first opened in Halifax back in 1996 and opened its location in Wakefield in 2017.

On its menu, Syhiba has a huge range of dishes on offer from starters such as samosas, kebabs, and pakora, as well as a mixed starter for those who cannot choose what they fancy.

Syhiba has a lot of chef specialities to choose from, including the shahi korma, chicken tikka makhani, and balti seekh kebab.

The restaurant also offers a lot of familiar favourites from bhuna and madras curries to baltis and dopiaza.

There are gourmet dishes, grill options, and plenty of vegetarian dishes to try out.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21641818.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant has two locations in Yorkshire. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Alongside your curry, you can enjoy a range of rices and naans to soak up any leftover sauce.

If you are trying to treat yourself on a budget, the restaurant also offers an early bird menu on Sundays through to Thursday between 5pm and 8pm.

The early bird menu features an appetiser of poppadoms and a pickle try, a starter of your choice, any main dish, and any sundry for only £18.95 per person.

### What do reviews say about Syhiba in Wakefield?

Syhiba has a rating of 4.8 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 1,085 people leaving reviews after their trip.

One person wrote: “We last visited this restaurant around 2 years ago, we’re not local but made a beeline as we enjoyed the food and service so much we decided to go again.

“Ash remembered us immediately and sat us down and took the time to describe the new dishes on the menu.

“The service we received and food didn't disappoint.

“Ash’s recommendations were just as he described 10/10.”

Another person wrote: “Another exceptional visit to our favourite restaurant.
