One person was killed and six others were injured in a shooting at a Spirit Halloween store in California, with at least three victims fighting for their lives as police search for the person responsible.

The shooting happened Friday around 8:30 p.m. inside the Spirit Halloween at the Solano Town Center shopping mall in Fairfield, about 45 miles northeast of San Francisco.

When emergency crews arrived, they found seven people suffering from gunshot wounds, the Fairfield Police Department said in a statement.

One victim died from their injuries, while the six survivors were taken to nearby hospitals for treatment. At least three of those wounded were listed in critical condition, police said.

Police believe the shooting was a targeted incident involving a suspect inside the store. Detectives are reviewing surveillance footage and other evidence to identify the shooter.

![One person is dead and six others are injured after a shooting inside a Spirit Halloween store in Fairfield, California, on the eve of the holiday](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/10/31/13/2244220369..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Our department extends its deepest condolences to the family and loved ones of the victim who died from their injuries. We also extend our thoughts to the other victims who were injured during this shooting. We are not releasing the identities of any victims at this time,” police said in an update Saturday afternoon.

No arrests have been announced in connection with the shooting.

“We recognize the concern and uncertainty this incident has created throughout our community, particularly with the many Halloween events and celebrations planned for today,” police said. “We understand that an incident of this nature is frightening and deeply unsettling.”

“We encourage everyone to remain vigilant, look out for one another, and report any suspicious activity. The safety of our community remains our priority, and our department remains committed to working alongside our community to ensure everyone can enjoy a safe Halloween,” the department added.

Anyone who witnessed the incident or has information that could help investigators is asked to contact the Fairfield Police Department at 707-428-7600 or email [1030mall@fairfield.ca.gov.](mailto:1030mall@fairfield.ca.gov.)

Meanwhile, across the country in Madison Heights, Michigan, a 21-year-old Detroit man is dead after being shot multiple times during an argument inside a Spirit Halloween store.

The suspected shooter’s wife, Alanna, claims her husband fired in self-defense after being confronted by a man he knew and had a history with as they left the store with their three children, she told ClickOnDetroit.

She said the man reached into his pockets, prompting her husband, a concealed pistol license holder, to open fire.

Police, however, plan to pursue criminal charges.

Spirit Halloween told *The Independent* in a statement: “We are deeply saddened by the tragic incidents that occurred at our Spirit Halloween stores. Our thoughts and prayers are with the families of the victims of these heartbreaking events.”

The seasonal retailer, which typically operates from late summer into early November, added that it is fully cooperating with local law enforcement agencies in both cases.

“Out of respect for those impacted and to support the ongoing investigations, the affected stores will remain closed until further notice,” the statement concluded.
