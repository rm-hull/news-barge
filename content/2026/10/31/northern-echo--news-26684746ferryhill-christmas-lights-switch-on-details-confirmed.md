Ferryhill's Christmas lights will be switched on next month as festive celebrations return to the town following last year's cancellation.

Ferryhill Town Council has unveiled plans for its annual Christmas Fayre and Market, which will take place on Friday, November 27, bringing market stalls, fairground rides, festive entertainment and a visit from Santa.

The event marks the return of the town's Christmas lights switch-on after 2025 celebrations were cancelled due to safety concerns over lamp columns being used to support the displays.

This year, festivities will get under way at 1pm with a Christmas market in the Market Place car park, followed by children's show rides from 2pm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651514.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christmas lights will return to Ferryhill town centre this year after the festive display was cancelled in 2025 due to safety concerns. *(Image: Ferryhill Town Council)*

Pupils from local primary schools will then perform carols in the Town Hall gardens from 4.15pm before the official switch-on at 4.30pm.

A pupil from Dean Bank Primary School has been chosen to switch on the lights alongside Ferryhill Mayor Councillor Curtis Bihari.

Families will also be able to visit Santa's Grotto, enjoy free face painting and children's entertainment, and take part in the Mayor's Charity Tombola, all taking place inside Ferryhill Town Hall between 4.30pm and 6pm.

The town council has also announced a new Best Shop Front Christmas Display competition, with further details set to be released in the coming weeks.

### Event programme

1pm to 6pm – Christmas Market, Market Place car park

2pm to 6pm – Children's rides and catering vehicle, Market Place car park

4.15pm to 4.30pm – Carols by local schoolchildren, Town Hall gardens
