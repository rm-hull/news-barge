A long-standing York MOT test station is up for sale at £435,000.

The freehold business, located in Moorcroft Road, Woodthorpe, York, is being marketed by Barry Crux & Co.

The firm has highlighted the rarity of such businesses coming on the market freehold.

Barry Crux & Co say the vehicle repair and MOT test station has built a strong customer base over the years and has consistently generated significant turnover and profit.

The current owner, who has managed the business for more than 25 years, is planning to retire.

The sale presents an opportunity for a new owner to further expand the business.

Additional financial details will be disclosed to potential buyers after they have viewed the property.

There is also the possibility of redeveloping the site for other purposes, subject to securing the necessary planning permissions.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21652893.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of the MOT Centre. *(Image: Barry Crux & Co)*

The business enjoys a good reputation and a regular clientele, being situated in the small commercial centre of the densely populated residential area of Woodthorpe.

The business has a reception and office area with customer seating, space for two workstations, extra storage, a staff kitchen, and separate toilet facilities.

There are three workshop bays, including two workshop/servicing bays and a central MOT bay.

The workshops are equipped with fully serviced and documented vehicle lifts.

The yard has a separate tyre-fitting shed with a tyre machine and wheel balancer, along with an additional storage shed.

The property also offers 13 parking spaces in the forecourt, with space for three more vehicles in the yard.
