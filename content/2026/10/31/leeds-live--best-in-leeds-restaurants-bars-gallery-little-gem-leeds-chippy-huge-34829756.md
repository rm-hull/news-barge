# 'Little gem' Leeds chippy with 'huge portions' and staff who 'genuinely care about the customers'

## The popular Leeds fish and chip shop has lots of satisifed customers

A Leeds chippy has an army of fans because of its service and portion sizes.

Dotted around Leeds city centre, you will find fish and chip shops waiting for you to indulge in a treat. Based across from Leeds First Direct Arena, at the Merrion Centre, you’ll find Hook and Line.

It boasts a menu of chip shop favourites and deals. On Google, there are an abundance of five-star reviews from customers.

We saw Qabas Mousa Al-Omari post: “Honestly, this is the best fish and chips in Leeds. The food is always fresh, tasty, and cooked perfectly. The guy working there is absolutely amazing — really friendly, welcoming, and provides great service. You can tell he genuinely cares about the customers.”

While Lucy Shanahan added: “Finally a chipper that actually fully cooks its chips. It’s taken me four years in Leeds to find fabulous fish and chips.”. Colette Olive commented: “Super tasty chips and really reasonable price. But the best thing is the service. The guy who works here is so wonderful."

Jan Dunne said: “Generous portions and good choice on the menu - something for everyone.” While Julie Smith stated: “A little gem of a fish and chip shop. Excellent freshly cooked food. Service with a smile and surprisingly good value for a city centre venue.

And Helen Turner said: “The food was delicious and portions were huge.” You can see photos from a recent visit to Hook and Line by scrolling below.
