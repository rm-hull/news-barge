A man has been arrested after a late-night attack in Middlesbrough.

Officers were called to Corporation Road, near Wilson Street, on Saturday (October 31) after reports of an assault and the robbery of a phone just after midnight.

A huge area was taped off by police following the incident, with pictures online showing police tape surrounding the area to the right of Teesside Crown Court, near Russell Street.

Cleveland Police has confirmed that a 35-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of section 18 assault, and he is currently in custody.

A spokesperson said: “At 12.20am on Saturday (October 31) police were called to Corporation Road in Middlesbrough following reports of an assault and robbery of a phone.

“A 35-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of section 18 assault, and he is currently in custody.

“Enquiries are ongoing.”
