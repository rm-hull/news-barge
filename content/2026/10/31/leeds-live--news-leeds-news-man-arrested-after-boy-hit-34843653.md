# Man arrested after boy hit by car in East End Park in Leeds

## The man was arrested on suspicion of driving over the limit

A man has been arrested after a teenage boy was hit by a car in East End Park.

The boy was hit by at the junction of Simmons Court and Easy Road, in Leeds, at 6.24pm yesterday, on Friday, October 30. A 27-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of driving over the limit and dangerous driving.

The boy was rushed to hospital for treatment. West Yorkshire Police has issued a statement.

A force spokesperson said: "A 27-year-old man has been arrested following a collision at the junction of Simmons Court and Easy Road, Leeds. Police were called at 6.24pm yesterday to a report of a collision involving a car and a pedestrian. The pedestrian, a teenage boy, was taken to hospital.

"A 27-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of driving whilst over the prescribed limit and causing serious injury by dangerous driving."
