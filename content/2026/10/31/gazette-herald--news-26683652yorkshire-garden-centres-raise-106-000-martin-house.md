Staff and customers at a chain of Yorkshire garden centres have smashed through the £100,000 fundraising barrier in support of children and families receiving hospice care.

Yorkshire Garden Centres began its partnership with Martin House Children’s Hospice in April 2025 and has since raised more than £106,000 through activities including bake sales, raffles, sporting events and customer donations.

Mark Farnsworth, CEO of Yorkshire Garden Centres, said: "Reaching more than £100,000 is a fantastic milestone and is down to the enthusiasm of our colleagues, customers and charity champions across all seven of our centres."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21650024.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Colleagues at Yorkshire Garden Centres entered two sunflower-themed teams into Martin House’s Dragon Boat Race *(Image: Supplied)*

Martin House supports more than 550 children and young people with life-shortening conditions each year, providing specialist care at its Boston Spa hospice, in hospitals, and in family homes across West, North, and East Yorkshire.

Hannah Finan, partnership manager at Martin House, said: "We’re hugely grateful to everyone at Yorkshire Garden Centres for reaching this incredible milestone."

The partnership will continue until June 2027.
