With temperatures dropping and Christmas on the horizon, shoppers are turning to thermal clothing to stay warm without piling on bulky layers.

M&S is hoping to meet that demand with its Heatgen thermal collection, which includes everything from lightweight long-sleeved tops to fleece leggings designed for colder days.

The retailer also expects to sell more than 10 thermal tops every minute online in December alone.

Interest is already rising, with Google searches for thermal tops up 130% in the last seven days, while searches for M&S thermal leggings have jumped 190%, according to the retailer.

![A woman wearing thermal clothes](https://www.oxfordmail.co.uk/resources/images/21648213.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The M&S collection comes in three warmth levels – Light, Medium and Maximum *(Image: M&S)*

The collection comes in three warmth levels – Light, Medium and Maximum – allowing shoppers to choose layers suited to the weather and what they have planned.

Shoppers can see the full range here.

There are also colourful and printed designs intended to be worn as part of an outfit, rather than hidden underneath it.

M&S expects 40% of its thermal sales this winter to come from coloured and printed styles.
