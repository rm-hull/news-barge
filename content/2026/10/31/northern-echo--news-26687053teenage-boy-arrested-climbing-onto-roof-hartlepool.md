A teenage boy has been arrested after clambering onto the roof of a home and “launching items into the street".

Officers were called to Cornwall Street in Hartlepool this morning (October 31) after reports of a teenager of the roof of a property.

A cordon was erected across the street after items reportedly started being thrown from the roof - with Cleveland Police confirming the boy has since come down.

A 17-year-old has been arrested and taken into police custody.

Sergeant Rutherford said: “I am aware of a number of residents showing concern due to hearing sounds coming from items being thrown by the boy.
