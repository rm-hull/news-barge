A burst water pipe is causing delays in Bradford this evening.

One lane of Sticker Lane, outside Java Joe's coffee shop, has been closed while what appear to be emergency repair works are carried out.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21655371.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sticker Lane this evening. *(Image: T&A)*

When the T&A visited the scene earlier this evening, water could be seen flowing from the ground and across the carriageway, forcing drivers to slow down.

The lane closure also led to traffic backing up onto the nearby roundabout.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21655372.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sticker Lane this evening. *(Image: T&A)*

Reports of the burst pipe first emerged on social media this afternoon, suggesting crews have been working to resolve the issue for several hours.

Motorists are advised to expect delays in the area while repairs continue.
