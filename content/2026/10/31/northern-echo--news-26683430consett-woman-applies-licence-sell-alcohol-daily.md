A County Durham resident has applied for a new premises licence to sell alcohol daily.

Mrs S Kayan has submitted a request to Durham County Council to authorise the sale of alcohol for off-premises consumption at 46 Old Hall Road, Consett.

The proposed licence would allow sales from 7am to 11pm every day.

The application can be viewed at the council offices at Annand House, John Street North, Meadowfield, Durham, by appointment on weekdays between 9.30am and 4.30pm.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Residents or interested parties have until November 13 to submit any comments or objections regarding the application.

These must be made in writing and sent to Durham County Council's Licensing Services at PO Box 274, Stanley, DH8 1HG, or by email to [licensing@durham.gov.uk](mailto:licensing@durham.gov.uk) .

The public notice regarding this application was published on October 29.
