Care home residents in York are challenging members of the community to take them on in an evening of traditional board games.

The event will take place at Care UK’s Chocolate Works care home on Bishopthorpe Road, starting at 4pm on Thursday, November 5, and will feature classic games from the 1950s, 60s, and 70s.

Guests can also enjoy homemade chilli con carne and sparklers.

Ella Bond, deputy home manager, said: "We are delighted to be inviting the local community to join us to mark Bonfire Night with our cosy gaming event and share in an evening of fun, laughter and friendly competition."

The games night is part of Care UK’s national ‘Bored Games’ campaign, which aims to revive the magic of traditional games nights and encourage social connection.

Ms Bond said: "We can’t wait to welcome everyone through our doors and see who will come out on top."

Chocolate Works provides full-time residential, nursing and dementia care.
