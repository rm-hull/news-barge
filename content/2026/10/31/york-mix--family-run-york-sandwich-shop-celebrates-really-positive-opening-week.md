A new family-run sandwich takeaway has opened in Acomb this week – and it’s been going down a treat.

Murphy’s of Acomb specialises in hot breakfast rolls, sandwiches and paninis, and home-made bakes.

Located at 25a Front Street in Acomb, York, Murphy’s is the latest addition to what’s becoming a hub of thriving independent buisnesses.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/788461499_2469245533502031_6549070649376658338_n.jpeg)

Murphy’s is a family-owned business – run by Simon and Beverley Murphy, their son Elliott and daughter-in law Carol. The family previously ran the cafe Hearts of Ampleforth for six of years, before making the move to Acomb to make it easier for their growing family.

Murphy’s of Acomb, which was previously home to The Sandwich Hut, opened this Monday (26 October).

***YorkMix*** spoke to owner Simon to find out more about how the first week has gone.

“It’s been really, really positive,” said Simon. “So far we’ve had no negative feedback.

“We’ve had a lot of customers follow us over from Ampleforth which has been lovely.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/murphys-of-acomb-3.webp)

Some of the most popular menu items for opening week have been a Cadbury Tiffin, Pastrami sandwich (with smoked cheese, tomato, gherkin, mustard and sauerkraut, £6), and Bacon and Sausage breakfast sandwich (£6).

Murphy’s also offers soup and a roll (£3.50), and salads (from £6).

“The community in Acomb have been so welcoming,” said Simon. “We’ve had other businesses come in and support us.

“And we’ve had repeat customers already!”

Comments from some of the first customers to try Murphy’s have been very positive.

“I’ll be back, 10/10,” said one. “Chicken salad was lovely yesterday and others enjoyed their sandwiches too. Great selection and good pricing too,” another wrote.

A third said “those vanilla slices are divine” and another satisfied diner pledged: “‘ll be going back, that’s for sure!”
