The Santa Express film has completed filming in York and on the North Yorkshire Moors Railway.

Filming has finished on *Santa Express The Movie*, a British Christmas family film shot in York and on the North Yorkshire Moors Railway.

Daniel Coll, known for playing DI Frank Blackmore in *Emmerdale*, stars as Bob Browning, a man whose ambition is to play Father Christmas aboard his local steam railway. Coll also directs and produces the film through Picture Point Films.

The cast includes Toyah Willcox, Peter Purves, Victoria Ekanoye, Lisa George, Fiona Sinnott, Olwen May, Andy Dunn, Joe Savino and Richard Ashton.

Peter Purves, known for his work on *Blue Peter*and* Doctor Who*, plays Father Christmas.

The film was written by David C. Thorp and combines a Christmas story with settings in York and the North Yorkshire Moors Railway. The production is now moving into post-production, although no release date has been provided in the press release.

Coll’s other screen credits include *Braveheart*,* The Crown*,* Coronation Street*and* EastEnders*. His theatre work has included* Les Misérables*and the original West End production of* Billy Elliot*.

Further information about the production is available at the film’s official website.
