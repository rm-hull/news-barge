This handsome York building has now gone on sale marketed as a “ready-made investment opportunity”.

Described as a “substantial period townhouse which has been sympathetically renovated”, it’s found on Wenlock Terrace off Fulford Road.

It’s been converted to form six apartments of different sizes, all with sitting tenants, and it’s on the market with York agents Carter Jonas.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/wenlock-terrace-york-townhouse-flats-carter-jonas-2.webp)

The details say: “All properties are all currently tenanted and producing good rental income with scope to increase these further, maximising annual rental yields.”

This is what the flats bring in, per calendar month:

* Basement Flat – £850
* Garden Flat – £800
* Ground Floor Flat – £1,100
* First Floor Flat – £1,100
* Second Floor Flat – £1,100
* Third Floor Flat – £950.

It means the building is now grossing £70,800 per year in rental income.

#### Property location

The agents point out it’s location gives residents “easy access to the city centre, Heslington University, Fulford Golf Club, Fulford School, Ebor Gym, York Designer Outlet, York Marina and river walks”.

To find out more, take a look at the full details on the Carter Jonas website.
