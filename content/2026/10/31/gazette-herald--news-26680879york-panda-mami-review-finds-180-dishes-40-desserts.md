One of the joys of staying in a large hotel abroad is the buffet.

In the last year or two, I have enjoyed some excellent buffets in places as diverse as Rhodes, Tunisia and Turkey.

There’s usually so much choice; you just don’t know what to do.

You think, ‘I’ll have a bit of this and a bit of that’ and even if you just have a spoonful of each thing that takes your fancy, it doesn’t take long to fill your plate.




* **FOOD REVIEW: The Dawnay Arms at Shipton-by-Beningbrough**
* **FOOD REVIEW: The Fox and Hounds, Langthorpe, Boroughbridge**
* **REVIEW: The Anchor Inn at Whixley, south of Boroughbridge**
* **EATING OUT: Panda Mami, Bridge Street, York**

It’s the same with these World Buffet venues in the UK.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645746.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of Panda Mami overlooking the Ouse. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

I recall having a fine lunch at Panda Mami in York some years back and taking my parents to something similar in Leeds.

Scrolling down on Facebook one evening, I read where Panda Mami in York had had a revamp. Panda Mami’s Facebook says it is ‘reborn’- and had increased its range of choices from around 150 items to 180! In addition, are 40 desserts!

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645747.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bustling, if noisy, interior *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

I thought this must be worth trying, especially as it fits the new focus of The Press food reviews at more of the ‘value’ end of the market, no doubt to better reflect the tough times we live in.

This stems from my reviews of carveries being amongst our most well read, according to our editor, leading to much debate from our readers as to which places offer the best carveries.

Let me assure you, future pub carvery visits and other pub visits are on the menu, along with continued restaurant visits.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645798.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Just a few of the many options available *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

But back to Panda Mami in Bridge Street, which overlooks the river in the city centre. It opened in 2018, has seating for 250, and is one of six Panda Mamis scattered around the country.

I called in on Wednesday night around 7.30pm after a function nearby, where I had already eaten.

The place was buzzing and I queued inside for ten minutes to get a table for one.

You pay upfront and the bill was £22.99 for the adult dinner Monday to Thursday, £7.95 for a large merlot, to which 90p was added as an optional surcharge, which I assume goes to the staff. This gave a grand total bill of £31.84.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645832.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The first plate *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The young lass who took the money then took me to my table and several minutes later dropped off the wine.

The venue was large and crowded and very noisy.

The clientele was largely young, both couples and families.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645833.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The sushi looked a little depleted *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

You help yourself to plates and cutlery and off you go.

There is certainly much to find and explore.

The dishes are mainly Asian, with plenty of Indian, Thai, Japanese and Chinese options.

As with the hotel buffets, it was a spoonful here and a spoonful there, when I saw something I fancied.

My first plateful had a variety of ribs, which were very tasty and tender, a couple of chicken pieces, including korma curry, Chinese money bags, battered chicken pieces and roast duck.

It was all good and I still had room for more, so off I went with a fresh plate.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645851.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Even more choice *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

This time I saw trays of sushi, which had certainly been devoured well by earlier diners.

The show cooking looked to have finished but there was a grilled giant prawn available, which I went for and it was good.

More chicken pieces were available, there were curries, naan breads and poppadoms, and a variety of Chinese treats, including beef in a black bean sauce.

There were also green lipped mussels and some more ribs in a sticky sauce.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645852.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Row upon row of choice *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

I filled my plate and again, all was good. You can go back as often as you like.

There were plenty of puddings, along with a chocolate fountain and marshmallows, jelly sweets, but I was absolutely stuffed, settling for a few grapes and a bit of melon for dessert.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645859.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plate 2 *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

It wasn’t long before a young lad took my plates away and all the staff seemed to work their butts off. They certainly are hardworking assets to the restaurant.

Now, Panda Mami might not be the place for date night, a romantic meal, but certainly for a good value night out it is certainly hard to beat.

The quality and flavour of the food is good but like with the hotel buffets I have enjoyed abroad, the best meals are to be found eating a la carte outside the hotel. It might cost a bit more, but I guess you only get what you pay for.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645871.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the 40 or so desserts to choose from *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Such sentiment is reflected online, with the reviews websites giving Panda Mami a decent 4.2 stars out of five.

Google gives this based on 3,953 Google reviews, TripAdvisor gives this based on 1,395 reviews, ranking Panda Mami 89th out of 949 York restaurants.

Google features some mixed reviews, with one noting a lack of vegan options.

TripAdvisor is much more promising.

The latest review awarded five stars out of five and praised the ribs, commenting: “Amazing range of foods. Ribs very tasty. Good value.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21645872.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It's all I had room for for dessert *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Another also awarded five stars and said: “Like the pay on entry idea, you are then free to eat as you please. Good selection and quality of food, regularly topped up. Reasonably priced drinks as well. All good.”

And another also awarded five stars for the ‘real global banquet’, adding: “Value for money. Really a global banquet. Friendly service. Very convenient to reach. Highly recommended.”

Indeed, Panda Mami is open seven days a week, with lunchtimes having a choice of ‘just’ 70 or so items, but for only £15.99 (kids half price).

There are also deals and promotions like bottomless soft drinks for £3.50. Sometimes kids can eat free and Panda Mami recently featured in York Restaurant Week with an all-you-can eat lunch for two for £25.

The restaurant has a detailed website highlighting what on offer, what the prices are for certain times and days and you can easily book online.

So to sum up, Panda Mami is a venue to recommend. It’s not fine dining in elegant surrounds, it’s tasty, flavoursome and honest tucker at a good price, with more choice than you could possibly need.

If you’re not too fussy, go on, give it a go! And make sure you are ravenously hungry!

In the meantime, I look forward to my next hotel buffet in Turkey next week!
