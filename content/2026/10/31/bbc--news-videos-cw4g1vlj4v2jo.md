# CCTV, zip ties and election watchers: How US mail-in votes are secured and counted

Ahead of the US midterm elections on 3 November, President Trump continues to make unsubstantiated claims that mail-in voting leads to widespread fraud.

US states have different voting rules and regulations - but every state allows some postal voting, a method the president says he has used in the past.

BBC Verify visited an election centre in Maryland to get a behind-the-scenes look at how mail-in ballots are processed and counted.
