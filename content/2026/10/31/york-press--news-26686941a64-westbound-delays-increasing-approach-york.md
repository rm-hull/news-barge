Motorists are being warned of delays increasing on a major road in York this morning (Saturday, October 31).




The A64 westbound currently has six minute hold-ups on the approach to the city.

Speeds are limited to just 15 miles per hour on the stretch between Flaxton Moor and the Hopgrove Roundabout, with additional delays after Common Lane.

Traffic is particularly busy on the approach to the junction with the A1237 York Outer Ring Road.

The A64 is a major road for road traffic between York and the North Yorkshire coast.
