A VENUE has applied for a premises licence for alcohol sales and entertainment.

Stevan Shaw is seeking approval from City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council to operate at 93 Bingley Road, BD18 4SB.

The application, submitted on October 21, requests permission to sell alcohol and provide regulated entertainment daily from noon until midnight.

Residents have until November 18 to submit any objections or comments in writing to the council’s licensing team.

Anyone wanting to view the licensing register can do so at Argus Chambers in Bradford, Monday to Thursday from 9am to 5pm, and Friday from 9am to 4.30pm.

False statements could result in an unlimited fine.
