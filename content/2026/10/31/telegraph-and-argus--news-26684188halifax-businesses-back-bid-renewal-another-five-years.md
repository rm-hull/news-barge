BUSINESSES in Halifax have backed a renewed Business Improvement District (BID) for a further five years.

The vote secures more than £2.4 million to be spent on improving the area.

A total of 84.95 per cent of businesses supported the BID in the renewal ballot, with 93.72 per cent in favour by rateable value.

Jason Gregg, chair of Halifax BID, said: "This is a significant moment for Halifax and a clear endorsement of the value of partnership and collaboration.

"Thank you to every business that took part in the renewal process and helped shape the future direction of the BID.

"In today’s challenging economic climate, businesses are looking for tangible support and a strong collective voice.

"The renewed BID will provide both."

The new BID term will begin on April 1, 2027, and will apply to businesses with a rateable value of £12,001 or above.

These businesses will pay a levy based on their rateable value to fund the scheme.

The Halifax BID board, made up of local business representatives, led the renewal campaign with support from pfbb UK.

The BID’s business plan focuses on promoting Halifax, supporting businesses, and improving the town centre.
