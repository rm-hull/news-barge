A man who turned to drugs after returning to the UK when his relationship broke down in Thailand has been banned from driving.

Aaron Clarey refused to give a blood sample on three separate occasions when police stopped him in County Durham.

The 44-year-old was also found driving without insurance during one of the roadside tests, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Syed Ali said Clarey was found in the driver’s seat of a Nissan Juke. “He was found to have cocaine in his system but failed to give a blood sample.”

The court heard Clarey had seven previous convictions for 10 offences, with his last appearance in court in 2003.

Mr Ali said the offences took place in Spennymoor and Bishop Auckland.

Clarey, of Staindrop Road, Cockfield, admitted refusing blood samples for analysis on May 18, May 21 and July 15. He also admitted driving without insurance on July 15.

Elizabeth Aisbitt, mitigating, said Clarey began abusing drugs after his relationship broke down and he returned to the UK.

She said Clarey had already given a blood sample and did not think he needed to give another.

“He is now trying to deal with his issues through his own volition,” she said.
