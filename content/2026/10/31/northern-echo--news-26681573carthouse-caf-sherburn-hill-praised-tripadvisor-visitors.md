A café tucked away inside a historic County Durham antiques and furniture shop has earned near-perfect reviews from visitors, with its homemade food and unusual vintage surroundings.

The Carthouse Café is based at The Old Curio on Front Street in Sherburn Hill, bringing together traditional café dining, antiques and furniture shopping under one roof.

Despite being a relatively recent addition to the former mining village, the café has already attracted positive feedback from customers, holding a rating of 4.8 out of five on Tripadvisor.

Inside, visitors will find chandeliers, William Morris-style wallpaper, antique tills and mismatched vintage seating.

The interior has been designed to evoke the atmosphere of a traditional British tearoom, with tea served in bone china and a mixture of antique and modern furnishings.

However, the food has also become an important part of its appeal.

The menu includes breakfasts, homemade soups, pies, cakes and pastries, alongside coffee and tea.

Recent Tripadvisor reviews have highlighted the café's welcoming atmosphere, food and distinctive surroundings.

One reviewer, who visited in August, described the venue as an "exquisite find" after stopping for a coffee following a parkrun.

The customer had visited on the café's first anniversary and praised its family-run atmosphere and unusual furniture shop.

Another reviewer, who visited with family in March, said the café was particularly welcoming to dogs.

They praised the staff, food and service, adding: "The decor is eclectic and fascinating".

The customer also highlighted the antiques centre upstairs, describing the experience as a treasure hunt.

Another visitor praised the warm surroundings and the service they received.

The reviewer described it as an "Absolutely amazing little cafe", saying they had previously visited the antiques shop and returned specifically to try the food.

The café's appeal is not limited to its menu.

Visitors can combine a meal with browsing antiques and furniture at The Old Curio, which houses independent dealers selling vintage furniture, collectables and decorative items.

The café has also attracted attention for its role in bringing visitors to Sherburn Hill.

In July, Business Durham highlighted how the independent venue was helping generate activity in the village.

The business has also outlined ambitions for seasonal workshops, afternoon tea experiences and potentially restoring an unused garden to provide outdoor café space.

The café is dog-friendly, with treats and a separate menu for four-legged visitors.

The Carthouse Café is situated at The Old Curio, Front Street, Sherburn Hill, DH6 1PA.
