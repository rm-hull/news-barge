## What are the Dreamery Gardens like?

At the Dreamery Gardens, you can find a ‘hidden and rich world of mythology, nature, and creatures both seen and unseen’.

There are plenty of fairy houses to spot along the trails and different things to see during each season.

As well as the gardens, it is well worth visiting the onsite museum that features a collection that ‘goes beyond arcane natural history’ and includes things like rare and preserved specimens, houses, clothing, tools, and other objects.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21643652.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There are lots of fairy houses to spot. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The museum will help teach you about the history of the area and the creatures that could be found there.

If you need something to eat after exploring the woods, you can visit the Dreamery Kitchen where you can enjoy open sandwiches and deli boards.

The café also has cakes, hot drinks, and an afternoon tea with scones and finger sandwiches.
