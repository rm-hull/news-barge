## ‘Beautifully refurbished’ period home hits the market in York – have a look inside

The four-bedroom house for sale in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, York, for £525,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

A “beautifully refurbished” period home has hit the market in York.

The four-bedroom house is for sale in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, for £525,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it is an “immaculate” home that is “ideally placed” near Bishopthorpe Road.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21641573.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the four-bedroom house for sale in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, York, for £525,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

“Attention to detail is evident throughout this beautifully refurbished period home,” a spokesperson for the estate agent said.

Inside, the home has an open plan layout with the living room flowing into the kitchen and dining area.
