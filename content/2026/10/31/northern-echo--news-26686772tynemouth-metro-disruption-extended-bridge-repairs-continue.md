Metro passengers face extended disruption after urgent bridge repairs forced a longer line closure.

The Metro line between Wallsend and Tynemouth will remain closed until Thursday, November 5, after damage was found on a bridge during planned infrastructure inspections.

The closure affects part of the wider Metro shutdown between St James and Monkseaton, which is set to end on Monday, November 2.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654208.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Metro services between Wallsend and Tynemouth remain suspended until Thursday, November 5. *(Image: Nexus)*

Kevin Storey, operations director at Nexus, said: "We need to carry out immediate repair works on this disused bridge at North Shields.

"This will require an extended line closure between Wallsend and Tynemouth until Thursday.

"The issue with the bridge’s stonework was identified during a planned inspection which took place this week.

"The scale of the work means that we must keep this section of the line closed to complete the repairs effectively and as safely as possible.

"Safety is always our top priority.

"We are investigating what may have caused the recent damage, including whether it was impacted by recent storms.

"It is not a quick job to repair this type of damage.

"We need to turn the power off for this section of the system and have scaffolding in place above the tracks.

"We are sorry for the inconvenience caused to our customers.

"We have ensured that a replacement bus service in the affected area will be running until the line re-opens."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654206.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Nexus)*

The bridge, which crosses the Metro line between North Shields and Tynemouth stations, was already closed to the public.

Repairs are underway and the line is expected to reopen on Thursday, November 5.

Metro services on the Yellow Line will continue to run between South Shields and Tynemouth via Whitley Bay, but some journey times may change.

Trains will run every 20 minutes between St James and Wallsend.

A replacement bus service will operate between Wallsend and Tynemouth every 20 minutes during the extended closure.

This service will be very busy.

Passengers travelling from the coast to Newcastle city centre are advised to take the Metro via Benton.

The cause of the bridge damage is still under investigation.
