A CHILDREN'S hospice has received more than £106,000 thanks to a fundraising partnership with Yorkshire Garden Centres.

The money was raised through a variety of activities, including bake sales, raffles, sporting events and customer donations, and will support the ongoing work of Martin House Children’s Hospice.

The partnership began in April 2025 and will continue until June 2027.

Mark Farnsworth, CEO of Yorkshire Garden Centres, said: "Reaching more than £100,000 is a fantastic milestone and is down to the enthusiasm of our colleagues, customers and charity champions across all seven of our centres."

Customers were invited to add 25p to their purchases through the Pennies initiative, while staff took part in personal and team challenges such as the Great North Run, Dry January, and dragon boat racing.

The company also supported Martin House’s 36-hour giving challenge for THE BUILD, raising more than £7,000.

Hannah Finan, partnership manager at Martin House, said: "We’re hugely grateful to everyone at Yorkshire Garden Centres for reaching this incredible milestone.

"More than £106,000 is an amazing achievement and will make a real difference to the children, young people and families who rely on our care."

Martin House supports over 550 children and young people each year across Yorkshire.
