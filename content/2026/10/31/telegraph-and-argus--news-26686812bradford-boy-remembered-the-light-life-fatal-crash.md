Tributes have been paid to a 15-year-old Bradford boy who died weeks after suffering serious injuries in a road traffic collision.

Harley Hartley died on October 28 after being injured in a crash on School Street in Bierley earlier this month.

His mother has now shared a tribute. She said: "I always longed for a boy and after three girls I finally got him, my life felt complete.

"Chunky 9lb 2oz blue eyed boy.

"He would do anything for anyone all you had to do was ask.

"Now I'm left numb, broken hearted, every emotion you could ever feel.

"My son, the light of my life, you knew exactly how to play me to get anything and everything you wanted.

"You will forever be my number one, I got you son always, love Mum."

## Police appeal

Officers were called to School Street at about 5.30pm on October 1 following reports that a black Audi S3 had been involved in a collision with an off-road electric motorcycle.

Harley, who was riding the motorcycle, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

Despite treatment, he later died from those injuries on October 28.

The Major Collision Enquiry Team is continuing to investigate the circumstances of the crash and is appealing for witnesses to come forward.

Police are urging anyone who saw the collision, or the movements of either vehicle before the incident, to contact officers.

Investigators are also keen to hear from anyone who may have dashcam, CCTV or other footage that could assist the inquiry.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101 or via the live chat facility on the West Yorkshire Police website, quoting reference 13260559556.
