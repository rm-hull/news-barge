A beauty salon has been named the best in the district for a second year running.

The Telegraph & Argus launched the competition a few weeks ago and nominations poured in, which were then whittled down to a final 10.

Now our readers have crowned their champion.

## What is the best beauty salon in Bradford?

Shipley's The Perks of Beauty has retained its title.

The salon, located on Commercial Street, first won the award in 2025, just two months after opening.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20054687.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Perks of Beauty, in Commercial Street, Shipley *(Image: UGC)*

## 'We did not expect it at all'

Emma Perkins, who runs the salon alongside her mother Donna and sister Chloe, said: "It's amazing news that we have won.

"I'm over the moon – I feel brilliant."

Ms Perkins admitted that the family did not expect to win for a second time.

She said: "We did not expect it at all, to be honest.

"We've been supporting the other salons and we know how amazing they are.

"I'd like to congratulate all of the other nominees."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20561666.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Donna Perkins (M), with her daughters Emma and Chloe, at The Perks of Beauty *(Image: UGC)*

## Open just over one year

The Perks of Beauty opened in August 2025.

Donna Perkins, a hairdresser with 35 years of experience, leads the hair services, while Emma specialises in nails and Chloe provides massages, facials and a range of beauty treatments.

When they won in 2025, Ms Perkins described the experience as "overwhelming," "surreal," and a "very proud feeling."

## Why has this salon won again?

Central to their success, she believes, is the salon’s warm and welcoming environment.

The family has always strived to create a space where clients feel completely at ease and at home.

Earlier this month, as the salon awaited the results, Ms Perkins said the team felt "incredibly proud, grateful and overwhelmed" to be shortlisted once again.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21547833.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Donna Perkins (L) runs the salon with daughters Emma (M) and Chloe *(Image: Emma Perkins)*

She said: "To have our hard work recognised alongside so many talented people means more than we can say."

The family believes their success is rooted in their commitment to creating a safe and welcoming space for everyone.

Ms Perkins said: "We aim to offer a safe space where everyone is welcome and feels at home."

## How do they plan to celebrate?

Since opening, the salon has grown quickly and developed a loyal customer base.

Reflecting on their journey, the family said they feel fortunate to have achieved so much in such a short time.

Celebrations following the latest win will be relatively low-key, with Ms Perkins saying: "We might open a bottle of wine later."

Looking ahead, she said the team plans to keep doing what has brought them success so far.

She said: "We are going to keep doing what we are doing.

"We are striving for a lot."
