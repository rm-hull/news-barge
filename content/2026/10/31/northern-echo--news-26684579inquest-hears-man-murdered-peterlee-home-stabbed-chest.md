A man "murdered" at his home in Peterlee died after being stabbed in the chest, an inquest has heard.

Dean Fishwick died at the Sunderland Royal Hospital on September 24 following an incident on Thames Road the night before.

An inquest opened into his death at Sunderland Coroners’ Court on Friday (October 30) heard how the 51-year-old had been found with a stab wound to the chest by paramedics.

Ambulance crews carried out CPR on Dean before he was rushed to hospital, where he sadly died the following day.

![Police and CSI at the scene in Peterlee](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21501427.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and CSI at the scene in Peterlee *(Image: Northern Echo)*

Fishwick, 53, has been remanded in custody until his trial on April 12 next year.

Coroner’s officer Alexandra Thomas told the inquest the North East Ambulance Service were called to the home following the incident.

“The North East Ambulance Service attended, calling police [and] reporting that a male had a possible stab wound to the chest,” she said.

His cause of death was established as a stab wound to the chest by pathologist Dr Sam Hoggard.

Senior Coroner David Place said there is an “ongoing police investigation and charges have been brought” and therefore the inquest must be adjourned.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21506379.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes at the scene *(Image: STU BOULTON)*

“I suspend my investigation and adjourn matters to a later date,” he said.

It has now been scheduled for April 23 next year at the same court.

Flowers and tributes were left for Dean following his death last month, with one messaged reading: “You will be sadly missed, love you. Fly high.”

Another said: “You sleep tight me old pal. You will be sadly missed Dean, you will be forever in our thoughts.”
