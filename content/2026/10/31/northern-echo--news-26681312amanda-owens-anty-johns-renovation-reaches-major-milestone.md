Amanda Owen and her family are approaching a significant moment in their years-long restoration of Anty John's farmhouse, with the Yorkshire Dales property finally beginning to take shape as a home.

Their efforts have been documented in Channel 4's Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which is approaching the end of its fourth series.

The final scheduled episode, due to air on Monday, November 2, will see the family reach another important milestone as furniture is moved into the farmhouse.

After years of building work, the development marks a significant step towards making the property suitable for everyday family life.

The restoration has been far from straightforward.

Situated in a remote part of the Yorkshire Dales, Anty John's has presented numerous challenges, with the family battling difficult weather conditions and the practical demands of renovating an old building.

The programme has followed the Owens as they tackle repairs, interior improvements and the installation of essential services.

During the fourth series, Amanda and son Miles have been seen painting walls after the plaster had finally dried.

Other episodes have featured problems with the property's water supply, while the upstairs accommodation has undergone significant changes.

In one unusual development, the family used gallons of tea as part of the decorating process.

The latest programme has also shown the younger children continuing to enjoy life at Ravenseat, while the older siblings balance family responsibilities with their own interests.

For Amanda, the restoration of Anty John's has been closely connected to the family's future.

With nine children growing older, the neighbouring farmhouse offers the possibility of additional living space and different arrangements in the years ahead.

However, the precise long-term plans for the property have not been fully established.

The latest episode will provide viewers with another glimpse of the transformation, as the interior begins to look increasingly like a home.

It also comes at a potentially significant point in the family's television journey.

Series four of Our Farm Next Door is approaching its conclusion, and it remains unclear whether another episode or fifth series will follow.

Although the restoration has been the central focus of the programme, there has been no confirmed announcement establishing whether filming will continue.

*The final scheduled episode airs on More4 at 9pm on Monday, November 2.*
