A Darlington Indian preparing to mark 20 years in town has been shortlisted for The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Bengal Heritage has been serving up a variety of “authentic” Indian cuisine for almost two decades from its home on Gladstone Street.

Manager Faisal Miah said its “brilliant reputation” and loyal customers are the reason the business is still going just as strong as when it opened in 2007.

And the restaurant is now hoping to take the top spot of the Echo’s Best Curry House after being shortlisted for the third year in a row.

Bengal Heritage is run by Faisal but owned by his two uncles, Mohammed Sufi Miah and Mohammed Matab Miah, both of whom have been in charge since day one.

Faisal, 42, says it is a “nice feeling” to be shortlisted for another year.

“It is rewarding but it is hard work,” he said.

“We have built up a brilliant reputation with all our customers, a lot of them are regulars. Without their support, we wouldn’t have been there for so long.

“We get a lot of good feedback, we always get comments about our good service and staff are always polite. Everyone always leaves satisfied.”

Faisal said the restaurant is doing well all year round but expects to be even busier as we enter the festive period: “Our cuisine caters for everyone.

“We get a lot of families and everyone from all ages coming through.”

And when it came to the most popular dish, he was quick to answer: “I think last year I said the chicken tikka balti jalfrezi was the most popular dish.

“But this year we have got a naga jalfrezi, it is like a jalfrezi but with a spicy chilli added.”

The restaurant currently boasts a 4.4 rating on Google, with diners praising the “excellent food and first class service”.

One person said: “Our 'go-to' local curry house. Always lovely food and friendly staff. Never had a bad meal here.”

Bengal Heritage is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Arvelli Indian Restaurant, Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.
