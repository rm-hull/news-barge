The future of Amanda Owen's famous Ravenseat Farm remains a question for viewers as the Yorkshire Shepherdess's nine children grow older and begin carving out their own paths.

For years, audiences watched Amanda and Clive Owen raise their children while managing the demands of life on a working hill farm.

Now, as the family continues appearing in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, attention has increasingly turned towards what the future could hold for Ravenseat.

However, that does not mean they will all choose to pursue careers in agriculture.

Some of the older children have already developed their own interests and responsibilities, while others continue to help with family activities.

Miles has taken on a visible role in the family's farming and renovation work.

During the latest series of Our Farm Next Door, he has worked alongside Amanda and Clive on projects at Anty John's and Ravenseat.

Raven has also returned to Ravenseat during the programme, helping with work around the stables.

Edith has been shown balancing her studies with responsibilities on the farm, including helping prepare the family's seasonal cream tea van.

Meanwhile, the younger children continue to feature in scenes showing the everyday realities of growing up in the Yorkshire Dales.

The restoration of Anty John's has added another dimension to the question of the family's future.

The property, situated near Ravenseat, has been renovated with the family's long-term needs in mind.

It could provide additional space as the children grow older, although the precise long-term arrangements remain a matter for the family.

No verified public succession plan establishes which of the nine children will eventually take over Ravenseat.
