A glittery Christmas decoration has left shoppers divided after some suggested it was 'harmful'.

The 'Fat Burner Injection' Christmas decoration created by the brand Vondels has been spotted in Selfridges stores.

Costing £18, the product is made to look like a needle with a clear cylinder filled with blue sequins and 'fat burner' written along its side.

Titled as part of 'The Influencer Edition' by Vondels, the decoration is on sale alongside a 'wrinkle remover' and 'credit card' ornament.

![](https://www.theargus.co.uk/resources/images/21651040.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can also buy 'anti-wrinkles' decorations. *(Image: Newsquest)*

## Shoppers divided over 'Fat Burner' Christmas decoration

Some have been left unsure by the 'Fat Burner' decoration, calling it "harmful" and "chintzy".

Taking to Reddit, one user said: "I hate it and all of the glamorisation of diet culture.

"Diet culture is so harmful."

As another unsure person wrote, it was "classless as hell".

However, not everyone is opposed to the decoration, with some calling it "harmless novelty" and a second saying it's "actually quite funny".

Novelty Christmas decorations often take inspiration from pop culture, fashion and online trends, with unusual designs appearing on shelves in the run-up to the festive season.

However, the fat burner ornament appears to have struck a nerve, with shoppers disagreeing over whether the design is simply a joke or goes too far.

It is not the first time stores like Selfridges have been seen selling Christmas decorations that have divided shoppers.

Last year, the department store was seen selling an 'Ozempic' decoration costing £28.

In a TikTok clip that gained more than 73,000 views, a shopper showed off a syringe-shaped ornament bearing the label 'Ozempic' along its side.

Other ornaments had the words 'injection' and 'fillers' written across them in glitter.
