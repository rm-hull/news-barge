A major town centre regeneration scheme in the North East has reached a key milestone with construction of seven new retail units now complete.

The new retail units form part of ongoing transformation plans for Eston Precinct, delivered by Redcar & Cleveland Council, and mark a significant step forward in the wider Eston town centre regeneration scheme.

Cllr Lynn Pallister MBE, cabinet member for growth and enterprise, said: "This is a really exciting milestone for Eston.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649731.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An aerial view of the Eston Precinct development *(Image: Supplied)*

"After a great deal of work from everyone involved, we're now approaching the finish line and can start looking ahead to seeing businesses move into the new units.

"We've had strong interest from prospective tenants and some discussions are now at an advanced stage.

"While there is still work taking place behind the scenes, it's encouraging to see the level of interest in the opportunities this development will bring to Eston."

Built by Esh Construction, the new development offers 14,650 square feet of retail space across seven units, alongside newly improved public areas.

The project is expected to help attract new businesses and increase activity in the town centre.

The council is currently in discussions with a number of prospective tenants, with several negotiations at an advanced stage. While announcements about occupiers are expected in due course, enquiries from businesses interested in taking a unit are still welcome.

The council will provide further updates as tenant agreements are finalised and businesses prepare to move in.

In addition to the retail units, the scheme includes new paving, planting, lighting, and improved public spaces designed to create a more attractive and welcoming environment.

Grant Watson, construction director at Esh Construction, said: "Marking the completion of construction at Eston Precinct is a milestone moment for myself and the team at Esh and we are incredibly proud.

"We have worked closely with Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council throughout the scheme, and our trusted partnership has resulted in the smooth delivery of the new precinct."

Esh Construction also delivered a programme of social value initiatives during the build, including STEM activities in schools, support for community groups, volunteering, and on-site experience for apprentices.

The company invested £3,500 in community improvement projects, including £800 towards new outdoor gym equipment at Flatts Lane Country Park.

The development was funded by the UK Government and Tees Valley Combined Authority.
