## North Yorkshire pub/hotel goes on the market for £20,000 a year

The country property is being marketed by Barry Crux and Co of York *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

A pub/hotel in the North York Moors National Park is seeking a new tenant.

The Ugthorpe Lodge Hotel, located on the A171 between Whitby and Guisborough, is available for rent at £20,000 per annum.

The current tenant, who has been operating the business on a partial basis for the past two years, is retiring and will not be renewing their lease.

The property is described as having "Large Well Fitted Ground Floor Trading Spaces", including a main bar with a large wooden-topped bar server, open fire, and separate log burner.

The back bar, which can be used for private functions, has its own dedicated bar server and an attractive vaulted ceiling.

The conservatory area, currently housing a pool table, has flagged flooring and exposed stonework to the walls.

The restaurant/breakfast area, which can accommodate 28/30 covers, is currently not in use but is in a good state of repair.

There are four double-ensuite letting rooms on the first floor, some with views over the surrounding scenery, and space for a fifth room.

The private owner’s/manager’s accommodation includes three bedrooms, a lounge/dining room, kitchen, bathroom, and office space.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21649920.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The hotel interior *(Image: Barry Crux & Co)*

The property also boasts a large grassed beer garden to the front, with a children's play area and views of the moors, as well as a smaller enclosed garden, and parking spaces for up to eight cars.

Sales particulars say the hotel is situated in a location that sees an influx of tourists during the main season and a steady flow of visitors year-round, except for January and February.

The adjoining camping and caravan site, which is operated separately by the landlord, provides a strong source of income.

The Ugthorpe Lodge Hotel is free of tie terms with a private landlord.

The landlord is looking for someone to get the business back up and running full-time for wet, food, and accommodation sales.

The new tenant will have the opportunity to revitalise the business, which has been operating on a limited basis, with no food service and the rooms not being let out, as the current tenant winds down to retirement, the agents added.

The property is being marketed by Barry Crux & Co of York.
