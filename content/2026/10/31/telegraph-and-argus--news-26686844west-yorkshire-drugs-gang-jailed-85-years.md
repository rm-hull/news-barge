An organised crime group has been jailed for running a major drugs operation across West Yorkshire and beyond.

The men were sentenced at Bradford Crown Court on October 30 after a West Yorkshire Police investigation uncovered wholesale cocaine and cannabis supplies across West Yorkshire, with links to Middlesbrough and Blackpool.

Allah Yaar, 35, of Haigh Road, Lindley, Huddersfield, received the longest sentence of 14 years and three months for cocaine and cannabis supply conspiracies and possessing criminal property.

## Bradford man handed suspended sentence

Among those sentenced was Zulfazah Khaliq Hussain, 42, of Washington Street, Bradford.

He was given a 12-month sentence, suspended for 24 months, for transferring criminal property and failing to comply with a Section 49 RIPA notice.

Detective Inspector Kevin Daly of the Force Precision Team said: "This was a huge investigation, and we know the devastating impact the supply and use of class A drugs can have.

"Large scale operations of this nature can drive associated criminality such as violence and exploitation across our neighbourhoods.

"Organised Crime Groups rely on firearms, violence and intimidation to maintain control over local drug markets, causing significant harm to our communities."

## Arrests led to wider investigation

The operation was led by Mohammed Hussain, 33, of Lemington Avenue, Halifax, who ran two distribution hubs in the Holmefield area and on Saville Park Road.

He controlled stock, distribution, transactions and repackaging, and regularly met couriers and customers.

Digital evidence linked Hussain and his associate Zahid Shakoor to the addresses and wider conspiracy.

Hussain was arrested in November 2024 after an exchange led to the seizure of 4kg of cocaine, 20kg of cannabis and £26,970 in cash.

Further searches uncovered two stolen shotguns and industrial-scale drug paraphernalia.

His arrest brought down the wider gang.

The men were sentenced to more than 85 years in total at Bradford Crown Court.

DI Daly said: "Tackling this is a priority, but we cannot do it alone.

"We rely on the support of communities to provide information.

"Every piece of information, no matter how small it may seem, can help us build a clearer picture and take action."

## 11 sentenced at Bradford Crown Court

Eleven people were sentenced following the investigation.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654335.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The ten men sentenced. *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

Zahid Shakoor, 34, of Halifax, was jailed for 13 years, while Muhammed Hussain, 33, of Halifax, was sentenced to 12 years for drugs offences, firearms offences and possessing criminal property.

Muhammed Ahmed Khan, 36, of Middlesbrough, received 10 years, Mohammed Nadeem, 44, of Middlesbrough, was jailed for nine years and six months, and Naveed Iqbal, 36, of Halifax, received eight years and one month.

Mohammed Rana, 57, of Huddersfield, was sentenced to five years for criminal property and fraud offences.

Joseph Ali, 27, of Blackpool, received five years and seven months, Ajmeer Miah, 22, of Sheffield, was jailed for four years.

Jordan Barker, 42, of Thornton-Cleveleys, received four years and three months.
