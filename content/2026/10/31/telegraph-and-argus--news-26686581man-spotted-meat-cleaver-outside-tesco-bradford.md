A court has heard how a homeless drug addict was seen holding a meat cleaver outside a Bradford supermarket.

The moment was captured on CCTV.

When police arrived at the Bradford Peel Centre branch of Tesco on Valley Road on the afternoon of April 21, they arrested Scott North.

The meat cleaver was located nearby.

Cannabis and weighing scales were found in North’s bag when it was searched.

In an interview he admitted having the cannabis but then gave “no comment” answers.

He later admitted possessing a knife in public, possession of cannabis, and failing to surrender to police on a later date.

Bradford Crown Court heard that North, 32, of no fixed abode and who appeared on video link from HMP Leeds, had 27 previous convictions for 38 offences including multiple possession of knives.

In mitigation the court heard that North had been homeless for around ten years and had the meat cleaver “for protection”.

A drug and alcohol addiction was said to underpin his behaviour as he had had a “chaotic” childhood involving being expelled for angry behaviour that led to drug use.

Since being remanded in April he had engaged with the Drug and Alcohol Recovery Team (DART) and had been clean of drugs for four months.

The court heard that he had already served the equivalent of a 12-month sentence.

His Honour Judge Colin Burn handed North a total of nine months’ imprisonment and said he would “almost certainly” be released at some point on Friday.
