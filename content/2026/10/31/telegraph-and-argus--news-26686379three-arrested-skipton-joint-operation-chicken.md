THREE males have been arrested on suspicion of immigration offences following a joint operation in Skipton.

The arrests took place on Thursday after officers from the Craven Neighbourhood Policing Team, working alongside Home Office Immigration Enforcement, visited more than 10 premises as part of Operation Chicken.

PC Sampson, who led the operation, said: "Operations such as this are important to ensure that people are working legally and fairly, while also helping to identify vulnerable individuals who may be at risk of exploitation.

"Working closely with our partners allows us to safeguard communities and tackle criminality wherever it is found."

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said the operation was not solely focused on immigration enforcement and could help identify safeguarding concerns involving vulnerable adults and children, as well as other criminal offences.

The force said it will continue working with partner agencies.
