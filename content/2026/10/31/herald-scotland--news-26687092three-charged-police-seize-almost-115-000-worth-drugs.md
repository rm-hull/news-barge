Three people have been charged after police seized almost £115,000 worth of drugs.

Officers with search warrants went to properties on Babylon Road and Liberty Road in Bellshill, North Lanarkshire, on Friday as part of an intelligence-led operation.

Police said that cocaine was discovered at both addresses as well as cash at Liberty Road.

A 59-year-old man was arrested and charged in connection with alleged drugs offences while a 34-year-old man was arrested and charged in connection with alleged serious and organised crime and drugs offences.

Police said that a 34-year-old woman was arrested and charged in connection with alleged serious and organised crime and alleged proceeds of crime offences.

They will appear at Hamilton Sheriff Court in due course.

Detective Inspector Thomas Carey said: “This is a significant recovery which sends a strong message that the sale and supply of illegal drugs, which cause harm and misery in our communities, will not be tolerated.”

He added: “Serious and organised crime has no place in our society, and we will continue to disrupt activities while working with partner agencies to make our communities safer.

“Anyone with concerns or information about drugs activity or serious organised crime in their area is urged to contact us or Crimestoppers, where anonymity can be maintained.”
