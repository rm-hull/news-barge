**Grimsby Town 5 York City 2**

York City endured a Halloween horror show of a second half to lose 5-2 at Grimsby Town in today’s lunchtime kick-off.

It means they are still waiting to win on the road since their promotion back to the Football League.

Grimsby struck three times in 12 minutes late in the second half to seal the triumph over City.

A lively start to the contest saw the hosts grab a 13th-minute lead as a good team move down the left flank got Cameron McJannet in position to cross for Andy Cook, who headed in.

The visitors replied in the 18th minute and it was a former Grimsby man, Alex Hunt, who netted with a rifled low effort into the bottom corner.

York went in front when Jack Hunt curled in a shot after cutting in from the right side of the area in the 28th minute, but just three minutes later Iwan Morgan levelled the scores again with an effort on the rebound.

Grimsby scored a third in the 75th minute as Morgan delivered to the far post for McJannet to head in.

Josh Andrews added a fourth on the counter-attack three minutes later and Geza David Turi rounded off a fine second half for the hosts with a fifth in the 86th minute.

After the match, York City head coach Scott Lindsey said: “We scored goals, but we can’t concede in the fashion we did today. It was nowhere near good enough.

“We’ve just got to get back to the training ground and work on it. It’s as simple as that, you work harder.

“You only get out of football what you’re prepared to put into it and I’m really disappointed with this performance.

“We were poor and that’s on me, because it didn’t look like us for long spells. You get one of these games every now and then at this level, but we can’t be happy with that.”

He added: “We just got worse as the game went on. We just didn’t look anything like us today.

“You look at the goals, the goals were terrible. Um so yeah, I’m not looking forward to watching them back.”

The result leaves York City in seventh place in League Two, with 20 points after 14 games.
