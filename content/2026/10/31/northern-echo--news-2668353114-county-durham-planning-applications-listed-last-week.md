Durham County Council has received a range of new planning applications, including proposals for home extensions, tree works, and changes to local facilities. The latest submissions feature plans for demolition, amendments to previous permissions, and various tree management works across the county.

Below is a summary of the most recent planning applications submitted to Durham County Council:

* Weardale House, 13 Green Lane, Spennymoor, DL16 6HE. Demolition of existing extensions and construction of a new single-storey side extension with a canopy area. Ref. No: DM/26/02878/FPA
* Britton Hall, Westgate, DL13 1LN. Works to trees as outlined in Arboricultural reports 1 and 2 dated 27th October 2026 under a Section 211 notice. Ref. No: DM/26/02882/TCA
* Lanchester Primary School, Front Street, Lanchester, Durham, DH7 0HU. Tree works including removal of deadwood, felling of several ash, wild cherry, and rowan trees, and monolith/felling of marked broadleaves as per client instruction, under a Section 211 notice. Ref. No: DM/26/02877/TCA
* Brook House, Cotherstone, Barnard Castle, DL12 9QB. Complete removal of a pine tree. Ref. No: DM/26/02880/TPO
* 11 The Green, Hett, Durham, DH6 5LX. Removal of a damaged ash tree branch that has split at the trunk, with the rest of the tree remaining sound. Ref. No: DM/26/02859/TCA
* Land North Of Unit 13, Coundon Industrial Estate, Coundon, DL14 8NR. Non-material amendment to change the wording of conditions 2 and 3, removing the requirement for information to be submitted within 3 months, pursuant to DM/23/01109/FPA. Ref. No: DM/26/02842/NMA
* Garden House, High Street, Witton-le-wear, Bishop Auckland, DL14 0AZ. Felling of a sycamore tree. Ref. No: DM/26/02864/TCA
* Beamish Burn Cottage, Beamishburn Road, Beamish, Stanley, DH9 0RR. Removal of branches overhanging the roof of Coppy Lodge under a Section 211 notice. Ref. No: DM/26/02853/TCA
* Site Of Former Easington Maintenance Depot, To The Rear Of 31 To 37 Peter Lee Cottages, Wheatley Hill, DH6 3RH. Amendment of roof tile colour to plot 7, omission and addition of garages to various plots. Ref. No: DM/26/02831/NMA
* 44 Orchard Drive, Durham, DH1 1LA. Removal of existing rear conservatories and construction of a replacement two-storey rear extension. Ref. No: DM/26/02822/FPA
* Spectrum Leisure Complex, Hunwick Lane, Willington, Crook, DL15 0JA. Prior notification for the demolition of Spectrum Leisure Centre. Ref. No: DM/26/02828/PND

**For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo (publicnoticeportal.uk).**
