Four men were arrested as tensions flared ahead of Bradford City’s derby showdown with Huddersfield Town today.

West Yorkshire Police said an "appropriate policing operation" was in place at Valley Parade to ensure the safety of those attending the fixture.

A 45-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assaulting an emergency worker, while a 23-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of a Section 5 public order offence.

An 18-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of being drunk and disorderly, and a 52-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assaulting a steward.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654875.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rival fans on Manningham Lane. *(Image: T&A)*

The force confirmed the arrests as officers maintained a significant presence around the stadium and in the city centre ahead of kick-off.

## How the day unfolded

Earlier in the day, Bradford City supporters gathered outside pubs and bars on North Parade under the watch of a large police presence.

As kick-off approached, fans made the walk towards Valley Parade, passing the Bradford Arms on Manningham Lane, where Huddersfield Town supporters had gathered after being escorted from Bradford Interchange.

![Bradford City fans gathered on North Parade ahead of kick-off.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654869.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bradford City fans gathered on North Parade ahead of kick-off. *(Image: T&A)*

Rival fans gathered along routes leading to the stadium, with dozens of officers, including mounted police, deployed as part of the operation.

The T&A visited Manningham Lane before kick-off, where police vans lined the road and mounted officers worked to keep the two groups apart.

There were several flashpoints as the supporters passed each other, with pushing and shoving involving some fans and officers.

![There was a large police presence across the city.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654773.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There was a large police presence across the city. *(Image: T&A)*

Projectiles were also thrown between the two sets of supporters.

After Bradford supporters had made their way to Valley Parade, officers escorted Huddersfield fans to the stadium under police supervision.

The derby ended in disappointment for Bradford City, who were beaten 3-0 by their Yorkshire rivals in front of a sold-out Valley Parade crowd.

![Huddersfield fans were escorted to Valley Parade](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Huddersfield fans were escorted to Valley Parade *(Image: T&A)*

City were still in contention at the break but two quick-fire goals after half-time put the game beyond Michael Duff's side before Antoni Sarcevic's red card compounded a miserable afternoon.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “ An appropriate policing operation is in place at Mbanq Valley Parade to ensure the safety of everyone attending.

“A 45-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of assault emergency worker, a 23-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of a Section 5 public order offence.

"An 18-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of drunk and disorderly and a 52-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of assault on a steward.”

The force has not released further details about the incidents leading to the arrests.
