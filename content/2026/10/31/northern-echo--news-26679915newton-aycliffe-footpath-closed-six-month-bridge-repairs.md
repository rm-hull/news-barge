A walking route in Newton Aycliffe is set to remain closed for up to six months while repair work is carried out on a footbridge.

The 3.2-kilometre section of footpath, which includes Footpath No. 30 in Great Aycliffe Parish and Footpath No. 7 in Bradbury & The Isle Parish, was closed under an urgent 21-day Temporary Traffic Regulation Notice (TTRN) on October 7.

Durham County Council has now extended the closure under a Temporary Footpath Closure Order to allow for private repair work on the Low Copelaw Footbridge.

The work, taking place between the junction of Footpath No. 4 in Bradbury and The Isle Parish and the junction of Footpath No. 32 in Great Aycliffe Parish, is expected to last until April 28, 2027.

The closure order, made on October 23, prohibits all vehicular traffic from using the route.

However, alternative routes for pedestrians may be available in the area.

The council has stated that the maximum duration of the order is six months, but on-site signs will indicate if the closure needs to be extended.

The notice is dated October 28
