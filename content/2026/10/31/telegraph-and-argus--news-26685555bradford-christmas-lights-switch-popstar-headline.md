The Broadway has announced who will be switching on Bradford's Christmas lights this year.

The shopping centre's Festive Launch takes place on Saturday, November 14.

Running from 12pm to 6.30pm, the free event will feature live music, activities and appearances by children’s characters.

## Who is the headline act?

Noughties favourite Pixie Lott is the headline act.

It comes after the artist played at Bradford Live as part of the first-ever BBC Radio 1 Anthems Live event nearly a year to the day on Saturday, November 15, 2025.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21652400.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Singer Pixie Lott *(Image: Ian West / PA Wire)*

Ian Ward, general manager at The Broadway, said: "We're delighted to welcome Pixie Lott to Bradford for this year's Festive Launch.

"She's a fantastic addition to what's shaping up to be one of our biggest and best festive events yet.

"Alongside Pixie, we've got a brilliant programme of entertainment throughout the day, from Broadway Voices and live music performances to children's entertainment and festive surprises.

"There's something for everyone and we're looking forward to welcoming families from across the region to celebrate the start of Christmas with us."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21652414.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pixie Lott performing at the Victorius Festival *(Image: Tom Langford / PA Wire)*

## Who else will be performing?

Punjabi Roots and the KPOP Fantasy Tribute Act will be performing as well.

The event is to host the final of Broadway Voices, The Broadway’s year-long singing competition.

The finalists will perform live before the winner is announced to the crowd.

Organisers of the Festive Launch are encouraging visitors to arrive early to make the most of the entertainment and activities on offer.

The event is expected to draw large crowds, with families from across the region invited to take part in the celebrations.

The centre has urged attendees to use public transport where possible due to expected high demand for parking and road closures in the city centre during the event.

## City Park events

After the lights switch-on, the festivities will continue at City Park with live entertainment, living snow globes and a pyrotechnics display.

## Who is Pixie Lott?

Lott achieved commercial success with her debut single *Mama Do (Uh Oh, Uh Oh)* in 2009, which reached number one on the UK Singles Chart.

Her debut album, Turn It Up, was released later that year and produced several hit singles, including *Boys and Girls*,* Cry Me Out*and* Turn It Up*.

The album established her as one of the UK's most successful new pop artists.

In 2011, she released her second album, *Young Foolish Happy*, which featured collaborations with artists such as John Legend and T-Pain.

The album included popular tracks including *All About Tonight*, another UK number-one single.

Alongside her music career, she has also appeared on television and stage.
