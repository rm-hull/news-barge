## Top York restaurant with 'unforgettable' signature dishes up for award

Mirchiz's signature dishes were described as "unforgettable" *(Image: Mirchiz)*

A York Indian restaurant which has received praise for its “unforgettable” signature dishes has been shortlisted for a Best Curry House award.

Mirchiz, in Fishergate, is among the final 10 restaurants selected by readers of The Press, with voting now open to declare the 2026 winner.

Owner Abdul Basith, who has worked in the restaurant industry since his teenage years, said the business was founded with the aim of offering something different to York's curry lovers.




He said: "The market was thriving, but it lacked a modern touch that could elevate the experience without losing authenticity.

"We built our concept around that balance: traditional flavours with a fresh, welcoming atmosphere."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20150155.jpg?type=mds-article-620)"Our signature dishes, whether it's the rich, velvety butter chicken or the slow-cooked, fall-off-the-bone lamb shanks, are truly in a league of their own" *(Image: Mirchiz)*

Abdul said Mirchiz prides itself on combining authentic Indian cuisine with a customer-focused approach.

Despite holding an alcohol licence, the restaurant operates a bring-your-own-bottle policy with no corkage charge.

He said: "It's a simple gesture, but it reflects our ethos, giving guests the freedom to enjoy their meal, their way.”

Mirchiz originally opened in a smaller premises a few doors away from its current location, but growing demand soon prompted a move to a larger restaurant with increased seating capacity and a new kitchen.

Recent renovations have also given the venue a fresh look.

"We have undergone a full change and really gone for the Instagrammable look," Abdul said.

"We're always evolving, whether it's refining our existing dishes or introducing bold new flavours."

Among the restaurant's most popular dishes are its butter chicken and slow-cooked lamb shanks.

![Mirchiz's signature dishes were described as ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20150139.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mirchiz's signature dishes were described as "unforgettable" *(Image: Mirchiz)*

He said: "Our signature dishes, whether it's the rich, velvety butter chicken or the slow-cooked, fall-off-the-bone lamb shanks, are truly in a league of their own.”

The restaurant also caters for a variety of dietary requirements, including vegan and gluten-free diners.

Looking ahead, Mirchiz is developing a new range of slow-cooked, pot-based curries.

"We've perfected the balance of bold flavour and comforting depth, and we genuinely believe no other restaurant delivers the same level of taste," Abdul said.

Mirchiz is open seven days a week from 5.30pm to 10.30pm. On Thursdays and Sundays, diners can enjoy a three-course meal for £15.95.

Reflecting on the restaurant's journey, Abdul said his goal has always been to create a welcoming space centred around great food.

"I started the restaurant to share my love for Indian cuisine and create a space that's warm, welcoming and full of flavour," he said.

"We're proud to offer great-value authentic dishes, generous portions and a BYOB policy that lets guests enjoy their favourite drinks without inflated prices."
