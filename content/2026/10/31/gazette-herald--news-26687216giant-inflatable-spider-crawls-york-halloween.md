A giant inflatable spider has taken over the streets of York this Halloween.

Measuring more than eight feet tall and 12 feet wide, the towering arachnid puppet has been crawling through Little Stonegate, Back Swinegate, Swinegate and Grape Lane this afternoon (Saturday, October 31).

The Press joined crowds in the narrow corner of the rear entrance of Browns department store where families gathered to see the eight-legged creepy-crawly inching its way towards Swinegate.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21654864.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The spider is taking to York city centre streets three times today *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

There are more performances today at 3pm and 4pm and the performance has been put on as part of York Business Improvement District’s (BID) latest Family Friendly Saturday.

The not-for-profit organisation represents and works on behalf of city centre companies and firms, providing services aimed at improving and supporting the area.

Carl Alsop, operations manager at York BID, said: "Our Family Friendly Saturdays have become a fantastic way to bring playful, memorable experiences into York’s public spaces.

"The Giant Inflatable Spider will be a really special Halloween spectacle, giving families another great reason to spend time in the city centre together."

Brought to life by a skilled puppeteer, the spider will bounce, sway, and scuttle through the streets, offering an unforgettable experience and a perfect photo opportunity.

It marks the 13th Family Friendly Saturday organised by York BID, following 12 previous events that have drawn thousands of families to the city centre for free early-evening experiences.
