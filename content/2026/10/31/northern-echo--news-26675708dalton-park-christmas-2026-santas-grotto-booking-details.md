Festive magic is on the way as Dalton Park Outlet & Outdoors launches its Christmas programme.

The County Durham shopping destination is transforming into the North East Pole, with a packed season of family fun including a new Santa’s Grotto, fireworks, markets, and late-night shopping.

The celebrations begin on Saturday, November 7, with a launch event running from 4.30pm to 8.30pm featuring fireworks, live music, and entertainment.

Hits Radio’s Steve and Karen will host the fireworks display, which include roaming characters like The Grinch and Buddy the Elf, a live performance from Sunderland girl band Northern Daughters, and surprise moments throughout the evening.

Dalton Park will offer late-night shopping until 8.30pm during the launch event.

The new Santa’s Grotto, designed as a woodland hideaway, will be open every Saturday and Sunday from November 8 to December 20.

It will run from 10am to 5pm on Saturdays and 11am to 4pm on Sundays.

From December 21 to 23, the Grotto will open daily from 10.30am to 4pm and on Christmas Eve from 10.30am to 3pm.

Visits start from £8 per child and include a meet-and-greet with Santa, his singing reindeer, and a special gift.

Printed photographs are available for an additional £3.

No advance booking is required for standard visits, but relaxed sessions - designed with reduced noise, softer lighting, and fewer visitors - take place each Saturday from 10am to 10.30am and must be booked online.

Dalton Park will also host its Artisan Market on Sunday, November 8, and Festive Market on December 12 and 13.

Shoppers can explore new stores including Crocs, TFG London, Belstaff, French Connection, and BADGE, alongside more than 200 established brands.

Late-night shopping begins on November 27, with stores open until 7pm Monday to Saturday through to December 23.

Richard Kaye, centre manager at Dalton Park Outlet & Outdoors, said: "This will be one of our most exciting festive seasons yet.

"From the brand-new Santa’s Grotto and our beautiful Christmas decorations to the launch event, markets and outstanding new store arrivals, we have created a compelling programme for families, friends and shoppers to enjoy throughout the season."
