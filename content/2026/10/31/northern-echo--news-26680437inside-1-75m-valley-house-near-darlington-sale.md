SOME homes have history and others have style. Valley House in High Coniscliffe has both in abundance.

This exceptional grade II listed property has been meticulously renovated and significantly improved by its current owners over the years to create the iteration that stands today, offering more than 5,000 sq ft of stunning accommodation in around five acres of beautifully landscaped gardens and grounds.

Throughout the renovation great care was taken to preserve the character and integrity of the original building whilst creating elegant living spaces that are both practical and highly efficient.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646326.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Valley House is a stylish blend of historic architecture and contemporary design. *(Image: Frank Knight)*

The result is a seamless blend of historic architecture and contemporary design perfect for a discerning buyer who is looking for space and privacy with more than a touch of grandeur.

Step inside Valley House and its welcoming reception hall leads to a series of beautifully appointed living spaces where the quality of the workmanship is clear at every turn.

At the heart of the home and daily life is the magnificent kitchen, dining and family room which stretches to almost 46 ft and is filled with natural light.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646330.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Valley House is a stylish blend of historic architecture and contemporary design. *(Image: Frank Knight)*

The kitchen itself is a joy to cook in, with bespoke cabinetry, a range of integrated appliances and a substantial central island with breakfast bar seating.

Alongside the kitchen is the dining area with ample space for a large dining table – perfect for those who like entertaining – then a stylish family area with a focal point wood-burning stove and a bay window.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646333.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Valley House is a stylish blend of historic architecture and contemporary design. *(Image: Frank Knight)*

Whether it is a busy weekday breakfast or a dinner party with friends, this is a room built for sharing.  
Certain to take your breath away is the garden room.

This double-height space adjoins the kitchen and is centred around extensive glazing that floods it with natural light and frames delightful views across the gardens and grounds beyond.  
Two sets of double doors open into the gardens and a striking galleried landing with exposed timber beams looks down on the room below.

It is one of the house’s most impressive architectural features and a wonderful marriage of old and new.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646328.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Valley House is a stylish blend of historic architecture and contemporary design. *(Image: Frank Knight)*

Last, but certainly not least on the ground floor is a cloakroom and a study.

On the lower ground floor are a substantial games room ideal for entertaining guests young and old in, plus a laundry room.  
Then there is a staircase that leads to an impressive wine cellar any wine lover is sure to appreciate.

Upstairs are the family bathroom and four bedrooms.  
These include three double bedrooms – one of which has an en-suite shower room – and the principal suite.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646331.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Valley House is a stylish blend of historic architecture and contemporary design. *(Image: Frank Knight)*

A luxurious retreat, this encompasses a generous bedroom with a feature fireplace plus its own dressing room and an en-suite bathroom complete with a period-style fireplace and a striking pale pink ball and claw foot freestanding bath.

Throughout Valley House thoughtful design ensures that period character and modern comfort sit happily side-by-side.  
The gardens and grounds are a particular highlight of the property.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646337.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Valley House is a stylish blend of historic architecture and contemporary design. *(Image: Frank Knight)*

Extending to around five acres in all, they include sweeping lawns, mature specimen trees and established planting, as well as beautifully landscaped terraces for dining outdoors and relaxing in the sun, plus an outdoor cooking area with a pizza oven.

The mature setting offers a real sense of privacy and seclusion, making it easy to forget that the village is right on the doorstep.
