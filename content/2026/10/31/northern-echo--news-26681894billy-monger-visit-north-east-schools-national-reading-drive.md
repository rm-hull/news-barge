An F1 TV star and athlete will visit schools in the North East and North Yorkshire in a national drive to get children reading.

Billy Monger will visit Chopwell Primary School and Emmanuel College in Gateshead on Thursday, November 5, for Macmillan Children’s Books’ Operation Storytime project.

He will discuss Find Your Drive, which draws on his motorsport career and life as a double amputee since the age of 17.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21647757.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The F1 presenter and athlete will show pupils how reading can build confidence and help them shape their futures *(Image: Supplied)*

The book aims to build children’s confidence through reading.

On Friday, November 6, Mr Monger will visit Catterick Garrison, home to the 1st Battalion, Royal Yorkshire Regiment, Royal Lancers, Royal Engineers, Royal Logistics Corps, Royal Regiment of Scotland and Royal Military Police.

He will visit Carnagill, Wavell and Cambrai schools, where many parents serve in the Armed Forces.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21647746.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Billy Monger (GBR) JHR Developments Ginetta Junior *(Image: Supplied)*

The day will feature interactive events, student workshops and a Q&A session for parents, carers and garrison staff.

Leyburn’s Wonky Tree Bookshop will provide books for the event.

The initiative brings author events to forces children facing extra challenges during their school years.

Mr Monger, an F1 TV star and National Year of Reading ambassador, has trained to break the para-triathlon world record and begin training for the 2028 Paralympic Games.

His visit aims to show children how reading can build confidence and help them shape their futures.
