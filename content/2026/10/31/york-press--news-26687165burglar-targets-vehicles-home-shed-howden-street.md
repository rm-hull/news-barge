A burglar has targeted vehicles, a home and a shed in an East Yorkshire town street.

Humberside Police said it happened in Milton Close, Howden, around 11.30am on Thursday October 29.




A force spokesperson said: "An unknown suspect entered a backyard, took a garden hoe and tried the locked summer house doors.

"The suspect then opened an unlocked vehicle, searched it and stole a pocketknife, binoculars and four £1 coins from a tub.

"The suspect left on foot and tried other vehicle door handles in the street."

Humberside Police is requesting that anyone with CCTV or information should contact the force on 101, quoting occurrence 26\*152786.
