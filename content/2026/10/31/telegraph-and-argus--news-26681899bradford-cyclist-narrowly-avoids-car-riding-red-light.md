A driver has spoken of his shock after a cyclist narrowly avoided a potentially fatal collision by riding through a red light at a busy junction.

The incident happened in Bradford where Mayo Avenue and Manchester Road meet.

Gareth Gregory captured the incident on his dashcam while driving with his wife towards Morrisons. The footage shows a cyclist travelling through a red light and crossing in front of his vehicle moments after traffic had begun moving.

Mr Gregory said the incident took place at a junction he already considers a "hotspot" for motorists ignoring traffic signals.

## 'It Could Have Been Fatal'

Describing the moment, Mr Gregory said he was "absolutely shocked" when the cyclist appeared in front of his vehicle at speed.

He said the cyclist entered the junction well after the traffic lights had changed and that he was forced to brake sharply to avoid a serious collision.

Mr Gregory said: "If I hadn't taken evasive action, I'd have hit him head-on.

"It could have killed him and could have caused a major pile-up. I had vehicles behind me and there was a real risk of somebody running into the back of my car."

He added that poor weather conditions made the cyclist more difficult to spot as he approached the junction.

## Veteran Driver Left Stunned

Having spent more than three decades driving throughout Bradford for work, Mr Gregory said he regularly witnesses poor driving standards and dangerous behaviour on the city's roads.

He estimated he drives between 50 and 100 miles a day across the district but described the cyclist's actions as among the most shocking incidents he has encountered.

Mr Gregory said: "I see some terrible things on Bradford's roads, but that one really did shock me. I don't know what was going through his head."

## Dashcam Captures Crucial Evidence

Mr Gregory believes the footage demonstrates the importance of dashcams in recording incidents and providing an accurate account of what happened.

He said that had a collision occurred, the footage would have shown exactly how the incident unfolded.

"The virtues of having a dashcam in Bradford are right there in one little video," he said.

## Calls For Cameras At Junction

Mr Gregory said he believes the main issue at the Mayo Avenue and Manchester Road junction is motorists and cyclists ignoring signals rather than the road layout itself.

He is now calling for red-light enforcement cameras to be installed at the junction, claiming he witnesses people jumping the lights there several times a day.

Mr Gregory said: "The only thing that would solve that junction is cameras.

"There's nothing wrong with the junction. If people stopped at a red light, things like that wouldn't happen."

He added that serious collisions are a regular occurrence at the location and believes stronger enforcement could help improve safety for all road users.

The near miss comes amid ongoing debate about road safety and enforcement as dangerous driving and cycling behaviour continues to be a concern on roads across Bradford.
