Tense scenes unfolded on Manningham Lane ahead of Bradford City's clash with Huddersfield Town.

Dozens of police officers, including mounted units, worked to keep rival supporters apart outside the Bradford Arms pub.

The T&A visited the area as Bradford fans made the walk to Valley Parade.

Bumper-to-bumper police vans and mounted officers were able to keep rival fans apart.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654771.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene outside the Bradford Arms. *(Image: T&A)*

However, there were several flashpoints, with pushing and shoving involving officers and some fans.

Projectiles were also thrown between the two sets of supporters.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Huddersfield fans being escorted to Valley Parade. *(Image: T&A)*

No injuries have been reported.

Police maintained a visible presence across the city in the hours leading up to kick-off.

Officers stationed near Bradford Interchange met Huddersfield supporters arriving by train and escorted them to the Bradford Arms.

After the Bradford fans had made the walk to Valley Parade, officers escorted the Huddersfield fans to Valley Parade.
