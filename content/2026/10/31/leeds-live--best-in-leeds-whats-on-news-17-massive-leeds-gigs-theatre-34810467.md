# 17 massive Leeds gigs, theatre shows and events to look forward to this November

## The Courteeners, Royal Blood and Jungle are some of the big names performing this November in Leeds

Some of music’s biggest names will appear in Leeds this November.

Highlights will include appearances by The Courteeners, Royal Blood and Jungle. While other acts performing are Fontaines DC, The Script and Tinie Tempah.

There’s also many others to look forward to. You can buy tickets for the majority of the shows listed in this article by visiting Ticketmaster and Live Nation.

Big theatre productions to look forward to include Mrs Doubtfire at Leeds Grand Theatre. While, The Grinch will get the festive season kicked off at Leeds Playhouse.

Take a scroll below to see what takes your fancy.

* November 3 - Alien Ant Farm - Leeds Stylus
* November 3 to 7 - Six - Leeds Grand Theatre
* November 6 - The Courteeners - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* November 6 and 7 - Yard Act - Leeds O2 Academy
* November 8 - Myles Smith - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* November 10 - The Script - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* November 10 to 29 - Mrs Doubtfire - Leeds Grand Theatre
* November 13 - Embrace - Leeds O2 Academy
* November 16 - Reverend and The Makers - Leeds Beckett
* November 16 - The Early Years and Sennen - Belgrave Music Hall
* November 18 - Royal Blood - Leeds Beckett
* November 19 to January 16 - The Grinch - Leeds Playhouse
* November 20 - Squeeze - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* November 21 - Jungle - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* November 24 - Fontaines DC - Leeds First Direct Bank Arena
* November 29 - Tinie Tempah - Leeds O2 Academy
* November 30 - Razorlight - Leeds O2 Academy

You can get the latest concert and event news by visiting our Best in Leeds homepage.
