A 15-year-old boy has died following a collision involving an off-road electric motorcycle and a car.

Harley Hartley died on October 28, nearly a month after the crash on School Street in Bierley, Bradford.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were called at around 5.30pm on 1 October to reports a black Audi S3 had been in collision with an off-road electric motorcycle.

"Harley, who was riding the bike, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

"He sadly died from his injuries on 28 October."

Harley’s mother paid tribute to her son, describing him as the "light of my life."

She said: "I always longed for a boy and after three girls I finally got him, my life felt complete.

"Now I’m left numb, broken hearted, every emotion you could ever feel.

"My son, the light of my life, you knew exactly how to play me to get anything and everything you wanted."
