Cobwebs, giant spiders and a dangling skeleton have taken over a Durham petrol station as staff get into the Halloween spirit.

Gulf Bridge End Service Station in Durham has been transformed by the team into a festive stop for drivers and families, with decorations covering windows, doors, and the shop interior.

Glow-in-the-dark footprints guide visitors through the store, while pumpkin lanterns dangle from the ceiling and witches, ghosts, and skeletons peer out from the glass.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21652794.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gulf Bridge End Service Station in Durham has been given a Halloween makeover, with giant spiders, skeletons and pumpkins *(Image: Supplied)*

Tracey Wright, store manager at Gulf Bridge End, said: "Our customers love Halloween, especially kids and families, and we wanted the store to feel fun the moment they pull onto the forecourt.

"Cherie Jones and the rest of the team have had a brilliant time getting creative, and the reaction from customers has been fantastic.

"It's a simple way to make a quick stop feel a bit more special."

Staff are also inviting children from the village to visit the shop in costume.

Ms Wright said: "We encourage the village children to pop in and show us their fancy dress costumers and pick up a few trick or treat sweets."

The in-store theming shows how Gulf sites are using low-cost, high-impact seasonal dressing to boost footfall, build dwell time and give customers a reason to visit beyond fuel.
