There are no prizes for guessing what The Old Chapel was before being converted into a simply stunning home in the heart of the historic village of Bowes.

The clues, if you need them, are not only in the name, but also in its appearance, both inside and out, which celebrates its ecclesiastical past rather than hiding it.

Behind the arched entrance porch the generous hall provides a striking introduction with its flagstone floor and exposed stone and open-tread staircase.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646437.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A stunning interior with more than a nod to its ecclesiastical past makes The Old Chapel a must-see property for house hunters – and then its neighbour of note too! *(Image: GCS GRAYS)*

Even so, it’s the view beyond the property that steals the show, showing off Bowes Castle which sits just over the boundary wall.

Over the years the property has evolved into a wonderfully individual home with original features thoughtfully incorporated throughout the house and made a focal point of within each room.

This includes in the generous living room and dining room, each with flagstone floors and double doors into the garden, and with the living room having the added benefit of a wood-burning stove that adds a cosy glow.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646439.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A stunning interior with more than a nod to its ecclesiastical past makes The Old Chapel a must-see property for house hunters – and then its neighbour of note too! *(Image: GCS GRAYS)*

At the heart of the home, the kitchen and breakfast room stretches to more than 28ft beneath a run of roof windows.

It was designed and hand-built by Anthony Nixon, and includes an old-fashioned larder, timber flooring and a large central island with breakfast bar seating.

A separate butler’s kitchen provides extra preparation space.

Alongside a family bathroom with a contemporary freestanding bath, timber flooring and a feature exposed stone wall are five bedrooms, each with its own individual character.

It is, however, the principal suite that’s truly unforgettable.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646440.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A stunning interior with more than a nod to its ecclesiastical past makes The Old Chapel a must-see property for house hunters – and then its neighbour of note too! *(Image: GCS GRAYS)*

Set over two floors and almost 25ft long, it includes a galleried seating area on a mezzanine level, a spacious bedroom, a dressing room and a stylish en-suite bathroom.

The mature rear garden offers plenty of spaces for entertaining, including a covered seating area and a sunny corner that’s perfect for morning coffee, while to the front there’s a double garage and off-street parking.

And the cherry on the top?

The backdrop of the castle and the surrounding countryside that make it utterly unique, of course.
