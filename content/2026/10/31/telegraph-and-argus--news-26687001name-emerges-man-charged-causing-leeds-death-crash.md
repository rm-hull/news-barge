A 19-year-old man has appeared at court this morning after being charged in connection with a fatal collision in West Yorkshire on Thursday.

Harry Gyamsie-Perah, of Coldcotes Garth, Leeds, who self-defines as black African including black British, has been charged with causing death by dangerous driving, using a motor vehicle on a road without third party insurance, failing to stop after a road accident and driving a motor vehicle otherwise than in accordance with a licence.

He appeared at Leeds Magistrates’ Court this morning where he was remanded in custody to appear at Leeds Crown Court on 27 November,

The incident occurred at 9.52am on Beckett Street, Leeds, when a black Vauxhall Corsa was in collision with a 36-year-old pedestrian who later died in hospital. A pursuit of the Corsa had been authorised a short time before the collision and the Independent Office for Police Conduct are independently investigating the circumstances of that pursuit.

At least two people are understood to have been in the vehicle at the time of the collision and detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team are urging any occupants of the vehicle to do the right thing and come forward to assist with their enquiries.

Detectives are also continuing to appeal for anyone who witnessed this incident or who has any relevant dashcam footage to make contact.

Information can be passed to the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team via Live Chat online or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260615046.
