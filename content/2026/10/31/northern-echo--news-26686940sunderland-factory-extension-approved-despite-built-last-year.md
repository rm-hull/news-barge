A packaging firm has been granted permission to expand its operations.

Shalam Thermoforming (UK) Ltd, based on West Quay Road in Sunderland Enterprise Park, received retrospective approval from Sunderland City Council for a factory extension, which was completed in 2025.

The project is expected to create three new full-time jobs, adding to the company’s existing workforce of 110.

A council decision report stated: "It is considered that, subject to compliance with recommended conditions, the proposed development would be an acceptable form of development at this location and so it is recommended for retrospective approval."

Planners described the extension as "ancillary to the established manufacturing use" and said it would not negatively impact the character or appearance of the area.

They said the extension would be a "subservient addition to the host property."

There were no objections from the council’s highways authority, and planning officers confirmed the development would not cause "unacceptable impacts" related to overshadowing or privacy.

Shalam Thermoforming specialises in thermoforming and blow moulding, producing items such as buckets, containers, closures, caps, and "food to go" containers.

The planning application noted that construction was completed in 2025, with the retrospective application submitted later that same year.

The council’s planning department formally approved the development on October 27, 2026.
