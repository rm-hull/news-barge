AN evening of live music and LED drumming brought a vibrant glow to the city centre.

The Light Leeds hosted Light Up The Light on Wednesday, October 28, as part of Leeds Light Night 2026, drawing hundreds of visitors to the venue for music, entertainment, and interactive performances.

Brian Oakley, centre manager at The Light, said: "It was fantastic to see so many people coming together at The Light for Leeds Light Night.

"We wanted to create an event that was full of energy and gave visitors something they could really get involved in, and the atmosphere throughout the evening was brilliant."

The event featured live entertainment from an LED drummer, who engaged the crowd and encouraged audience participation, and a set from DJ Abbie Pierce.

Visitors were also given 500 free glow sticks to help light up the night.

Mr Oakley said: "The LED drummer was a real highlight.

"His performance was fantastic, and seeing him get the crowd involved and bring people together was great to watch."

The evening formed part of the wider Leeds Light Night programme, which saw the city illuminated with creative installations, performances, and artistic displays in venues and public spaces across Leeds.
