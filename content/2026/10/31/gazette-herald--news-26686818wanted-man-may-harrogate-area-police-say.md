A man who evaded police whilst being treated in hospital is being sought by officers from North Yorkshire.

Ethan Platt, 22, was arrested yesterday (Friday, October 30) on suspicion of breaching a Domestic Violence Protection Order, police said.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said while receiving treatment at hospital he managed to evade officers and left the grounds.




North Yorkshire Police described Ethan Platt as white, around 5ft 11in tall, with a slim build, blue eyes, and brown hair.

A police spokesperson said: "Extensive enquiries and searches are now underway to locate him, and we believe he may be in the Harrogate area.

"Although Ethan is not believed to pose a risk to the wider public, officers are keen to locate him as quickly as possible.

"If you see Ethan, or have any information about his whereabouts, please call 999 immediately quoting North Yorkshire Police reference NYP-30102026-0071."
