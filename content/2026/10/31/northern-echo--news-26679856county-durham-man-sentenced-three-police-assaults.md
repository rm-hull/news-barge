A County Durham man has been handed an 18-month community order after admitting assaulting three police officers.

Robert Stevenson, 31, of Middle Street, Blackhall Colliery, admitted assaulting two police officers in the execution of their duties and indicated a guilty plea to assaulting another officer 'by beating'.

The offences happened at Wheatley Hill on September 24, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Stevenson assaulted a police constable, an inspector and a police community support officer 'by beating'.

The community order runs until April 22, 2028 and includes a probation requirement.

The court record says the order was increased from 12 months to 18 months because the third offence involved an emergency worker.

Stevenson was ordered to pay £50 compensation for each offence.

The court made no order for costs. The court took his guilty pleas into account.
