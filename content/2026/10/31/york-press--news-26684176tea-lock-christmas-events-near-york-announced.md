## Well-known York tearoom unveils Christmas events

Tea By The Lock, at Naburn Lock near York, is getting into the Christmas spirit with festive afternoon teas, workshops and seasonal events *(Image: Supplied)*

A York tearoom is gearing up for Christmas with a programme of festive events, workshops and seasonal treats.

Tea By The Lock, based in the historic 1823 Banqueting Hall at Naburn Lock, is running a series of events in the build-up to Christmas.

The plant-based tearoom will offer festive afternoon teas in its snug or atrium, priced at £39.95.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21650538.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Christmas Fair returns on Sunday, November 29, bringing handcrafted gifts and charity stalls to the historic 1823 Banqueting Hall *(Image: Supplied)*

Its Christmas Fair returns on Sunday, November 29, featuring handcrafted goods and charity stalls, with a few pitches still available.

Wreath-making sessions are also returning, with the £59.95 package including a mini festive afternoon tea and hot chocolate.

The tearoom is also hosting craft and art workshops aimed at giving visitors a chance to relax during the busy festive period.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21650540.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Entertainment at Tea By The Lock *(Image: Supplied)*

They include seasonal card-making sessions with Krafty-Kards Handmade By Helen-Anne, priced at £20, and Hazel Arts workshops using a range of art materials, costing between £15 and £20.

Tea By The Lock is also staging pet-friendly Bonfire Night events on November 5, 6 and 7, with marshmallow toasting, seasonal food and drinks available.

The events run from 6pm to 8pm.

Events can be booked through Eventbrite or directly through Tea By The Lock.
