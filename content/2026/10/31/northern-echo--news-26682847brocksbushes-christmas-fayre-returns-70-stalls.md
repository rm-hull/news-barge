A popular Christmas fair is returning to the North East with more than 70 stalls and four days of live music, demonstrations and festive activities.

Brocksbushes Christmas Fair will run from Thursday, November 12 to Sunday, November 15 at Brocksbushes near Hexham in Northumberland.

The fair will feature a packed programme of expert talks, hands-on workshops, and live music performances, alongside stalls offering artisan food and drink, gifts, jewellery, ceramics, art, and Christmas decorations.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649386.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Brocksbushes Christmas Fair *(Image: Supplied)*

Rose Dickinson from Brocksbushes said: "We really wanted this year's Christmas Fayre to be about more than shopping.

"We work with so many fantastic local suppliers, producers and businesses throughout the year, and the Fayre is a brilliant opportunity to bring all of that local talent together & showcase it to all our visitors."

Visitors will have the chance to meet the people behind the products, gather expert advice, and take part in creative workshops.

Ms Dickinson said: "Visitors can come along and discover some amazing independent businesses, but they can also learn something new, Q&A with a local chef (Daniel Nattrass from the Hadrian's Hotel), learn how to wrap a gift creatively and enjoy fantastic live music."

"Supporting local is incredibly important to us at Brocksbushes and we love being able to give local businesses, makers and performers a platform.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649386.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Brocksbushes Christmas Fair *(Image: Supplied)*

"We hope people will leave not only with their Christmas shopping bags full, but with plenty of new ideas and inspiration for the festive season too."

The faire will include Christmas flower demonstrations and gift-wrapping demonstrations on selected days, helping attendees add a personal touch to their celebrations.

Live music will set the tone throughout the weekend, with performances from Rock Choir, Felling Band, Charlotte Foreman, and Prudhoe Community Band.

A full programme of festive food and drink will also be available.

Seasonal hot dishes will be served by Brocksbushes Bites and Aupa Street Food, while the on-site café will launch a special festive afternoon tea.

The afternoon tea will feature homemade treats with a seasonal twist, all prepared on site.
