A man who strangled a boy was among defendants sentenced recently at York Magistrates' Court.

Stephen Wainwright, 44, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to stealing meat and cheese worth £152.20 from the Co-op in Flaxley Road, Selby.

He also pleaded guilty to stealing washing products worth £89 and worth £100 on separate occasions from B&M in Abbey Walk, Selby, and breaching a criminal behaviour order by entering the Co-op store.

He was jailed for 24 weeks.

Cameron Benson, 19, from Hurns Way, Easingwold, pleaded guilty to strangling a boy and assaulting him.

He was given a 12-month prison sentence suspended for two years on condition he does 12 days’ rehabilitative activities and 100 hours’ unpaid work.

He must pay the boy £300 compensation and was made subject to a two-year restraining order to protect the boy.

Ian Sidebottom, 47, of Denison Road, Pocklington, pleaded guilty to cocaine driving and driving with a cocaine breakdown chemical in his system, committed on the same occasion on the B1224 Elvington Lane.

He was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision and six months’ drug rehabilitation.

He was banned from driving for 28 months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Daniel Kemsley, 30, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to two offences of assaulting a woman.

He was given a 12-month community order with 85 hours’ unpaid work and probation supervision and was ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Bethany Dunn, 41, of Gildercliffe, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to three breaches of a criminal behaviour order by entering shops from which she was banned.

She also pleaded guilty to theft of hair and beauty products worth £94.82 from TK Maxx, theft of meat worth £38.10 from the Co-op store on Newlands Park Drive, theft of wine worth £17 from Sainsbury’s and criminal damage to a police cell.

All the offences took place in Scarborough.

She was given a 16-week prison sentence suspended for 18 months with probation supervision.

She must pay £55 compensation.

Ayden Brough, 34, of Melbourne Street, off Fishergate, York, pleaded guilty to breaching a non-molestation order.

He was given a 12-month community order with 40 hours’ unpaid work and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.
