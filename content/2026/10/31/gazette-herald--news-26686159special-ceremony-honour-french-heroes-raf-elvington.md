A WARTIME connection between France and North Yorkshire will be honoured during a special Remembrance event.

Students and officers from a French Air Force academy will join ceremonies at the Yorkshire Air Museum and in Elvington village on November 8, to remember those who served at RAF Elvington, which hosted two squadrons of French airmen during the Second World War.

The day begins from around 10.15am in Elvington, where a parade of Scouts, Guides, Cubs, and Brownies will gather on the village green and march to the French Memorial.

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Air Museum said: "With all the participants, their families, residents and guests, there will be around 200-300 people proceeding through the village - a gesture of support and friendship that has long been hugely appreciated by former French veterans and their relatives."

This year also marks the 80th anniversary of the formation of the Association des Anciens et Amis des Groupes Lourds, an organisation of veterans and descendants of the French squadrons based at Elvington.

The museum’s Remembrance service will begin at 2pm in the Station Chapel.

It will be led by honorary chaplain Revd Charles ‘Taff’ Morgan MBE RAF (Rtd), who will be assisted by Revd Mike Beresford.
