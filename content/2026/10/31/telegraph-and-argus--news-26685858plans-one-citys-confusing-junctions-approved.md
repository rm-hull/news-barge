It will soon become easier for pedestrians to cross one of Bradford’s most “confusing” junctions.

The junction where Thornton Road meets Listerhills Road and Lower Grattan Road sees traffic coming from four different directions – but there are currently no pedestrian crossing facilities.

One councillor says this currently leaves people “confused” over which way to look when crossing the busy roads.

But at a meeting of the Bradford West Area Committee on Thursday evening, members approved plans to make the junction safer by adding signalised crossing facilities at all four crossing points.

## Back to the drawing board

The decision was made several months after another scheme to make the junction safer was heavily criticised.

Earlier this year, highways officers proposed a range of traffic orders to accompany planned pedestrian crossings.

These included double yellow lines introduced on both sides of a small stretch of Listerhills Road and introducing “no left turn” from the main section of Listerhills Road on to Thornton Road.

At a meeting of the Bradford West Area Committee in July, members said some of these orders could impact local businesses, and urged officers to go back to the drawing board.

## Pedestrian crossing plan

The council’s highways team have since looked at traffic flows around the junction.

At Thursday’s meeting Andrew Smith, Principal Engineer for Traffic and Highways, said officers had decided to introduce a new option following the review.

This would not involve any new traffic orders, but would introduce pedestrian crossing facilities on all four arms of the junction.

If this option was approved, the previous proposals, including prohibiting turns in parts of the junction, would be scrapped.

## Plan is approved

Councillor Carol Thirkill (Lab, Clayton and Fairweather Green) said: “Someone has put a lot of work into these plans.

“The pedestrian crossings are all that is needed. Pedestrians struggle here because of the four-way traffic.”

Councillor Shakeela Lal (Lab, City) said: “This will make traffic flow easier, and pedestrians won’t be as confused knowing which way to look.”

Members voted to approve the plans.

## Thornton Road and changing surrounding areas

This area of Bradford is undergoing some major changes that are likely to bring more pedestrians to this junction.

A new Bradford College engineering building that is attended by hundreds of students recently opened a short distance from the junction.

A planning application was recently submitted to convert a vacant building on the junction into a bakery, and a new burger restaurant called Humbeys will also open in the coming weeks.

A branch of Starbucks opened yards from the junction earlier this year, and this will be followed by the construction of another drive thru restaurant on a neighbouring site.

A new escape room business, Time Trapped, has been built on a nearby car park, and the new Bradford Energy Centre will go live in the coming weeks.

And a planning application to build ground floor shops and an aparthotel building on the Listerhills triangle is currently progressing through the planning system.
