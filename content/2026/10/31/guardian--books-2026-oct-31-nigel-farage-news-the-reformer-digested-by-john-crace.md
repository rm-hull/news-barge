## **FARMING IN CRISIS**

by **Nigel Farage**, agriculture editor

A sensational report that has been hushed up by establishment figures linked to the European Union reveals that migrants are responsible for the failure of this summer’s harvest. The report, which has been leaked to the Reformer by a source who wishes to go by the name of Nigel, reveals that officials have tried to blame the crop failure on climate change. “It’s a total scandal,” said Nigel. “What’s been going on is that immigrants have been bringing their hot weather over to the UK with them in small boats. Global warming is a hoax perpetuated by leftwing politicians who want to encourage unlimited illegal immigration. But there’s no denying the facts. Back in the days when you seldom saw a black or brown face, British summers were much cooler. It’s time for everyone to start telling the truth.”

## **FOREIGNERS INCREASE THE WELFARE BILL**

by **Nigel Farage**, home affairs editor

In one of the country’s biggest surveys of two families, the Reformer can exclusively report that white people much prefer to live next to white people. The families in question say their lives are being made a misery by having a man called Zia Yusuf as their next door neighbour. “It’s got nothing to do with Zia being a brown Muslim,” said Trevor Bloke. “I don’t care what colour or religion he is. What’s intolerable is that he is a deeply unpleasant person who goes out of his way to be rude to everyone. He thinks he owns the whole street. We all just wish he would leave. It’s doing my head in. I’m now on maximum Pip payments for my anxiety and I can no longer work.”

## **HARRY’S GOLDEN BOOTS**

by **Nigel Farage**, football editor

In a stunning triumph for Brexit Britain, England’s Harry Kane has seen off countless other black and foreign footballers to be crowned the world’s best footballer in a glittering Ballon d’Or ceremony in London. Speaking exclusively to Reform, Kane’s best friend, Nigel Farage, said: “It’s about time a white Englishman won this. Harry would never have been able to do this if we hadn’t left the European Union. Just think what might have happened if footballers had freedom of movement. Harry might have ended up playing for some deadbeat team like Bayern Munich. Then he would have remained an unknown. So, really, Harry owes it all to me.”
