A UK-based electrical contractor is set to close after entering liquidation.

Freemantle Projects Ltd, founded in 2020, specialised in electrical installation services and worked with major clients including:

* Heathrow Airport
* British Airways
* Hermes
* Jet2
* American Airlines

The company offered a range of electrical solutions, from consultancy and design to installation, testing, and commissioning.

The company's website added: "We take pride in all of the projects and services we offer, fulfilling our contracts to the highest standard."

## Freemantle Projects Ltd to close after 6 years as it enters liquidation

After six years in business, Freemantle Projects has entered liquidation and will now close.

A resolution to wind up the company was passed on October 22, while Molly Monks of Parker Walsh was appointed liquidator, according to The Gazette.
