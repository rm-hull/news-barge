North Yorkshire Council has received registered planning applications covering new homes, a house extension and work to a sycamore tree.

The proposals are for sites in Beckwithshaw, Harrogate and Embsay.

### Plans to replace dwelling with five new homes in Beckwithshaw

An application has been submitted for the demolition of an existing dwelling and outbuildings at Willow Trees, Howhill Road, Beckwithshaw, North Yorkshire, HG3 1QJ.

The proposal includes the erection of five new homes and associated infrastructure.

Ref. No: 26/06602/FUL | Received: October 8, 2026 | Validated: October 27, 2026 | Status: Registered

### Proposal for single-storey rear extension in Harrogate

An application has been submitted to demolish the existing conservatory and build a new single-storey rear extension at 14 St Georges Avenue, Harrogate, North Yorkshire, HG2 9DP.

Ref. No: 26/06387/FUL | Received: September 29, 2026 | Validated: October 26, 2026 | Status: Registered

### Application to reduce and thin sycamore in Embsay

An application has been submitted for work to a sycamore tree at 32 Main Street, Embsay, North Yorkshire, BD23 6RE.

The proposal is for a 20 per cent crown reduction of new growth and crown thinning.

Ref. No: 26/05643/TCON | Received: September 9, 2026 | Validated: October 26, 2026 | Status: Registered
