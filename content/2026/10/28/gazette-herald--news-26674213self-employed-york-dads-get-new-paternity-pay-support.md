Luke Charters, Labour MP for York Outer, has welcomed the "major victory for dads" after a long campaign for stronger rights.

The Government’s new plans will extend financial support to self-employed fathers and partners who have previously been excluded.

More than 150,000 parents will benefit from an expansion of paternity pay.

Mr Charters said: "I'm absolutely thrilled by this news.

"I've been campaigning for better paternity rights for a long time, and it's brilliant to see a change that will make a real difference to families."

Eligible parents will now receive two weeks of parental pay, currently worth up to £194 per week.

Mr Charters said the change would mean a lot to families who had been unable to afford time off.

He said: "I'm particularly pleased for the dads I've met across York Outer who've told me they couldn't afford to take time off when their children were born.

"Whether you're a self-employed electrician, a builder or running your own business, you shouldn't have to miss out on those first few weeks because of your job."

Mr Charters became the first male MP to take extended parental leave in 2025, following the birth of his second son.
