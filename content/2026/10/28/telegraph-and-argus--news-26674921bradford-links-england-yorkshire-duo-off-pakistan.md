TWO teenage cricketers with Bradford links have been selected for the England Young Lions squad ahead of a tour to Pakistan.

Yorkshire second XI pair Subhaan Ali and Tom Bradley will play three Youth ODIs and a Youth Test at Rawalpindi in December.

The majority of the squad will begin the tour with a training camp in Sri Lanka, which will be held in Colombo from November 19 to 30.

Neil Killeen will step in as head coach for the Young Lions’ winter programme, with the team also set to tour Australia in January, though the squad could change ahead of those Ashes contests.

The Young Lions are of Under-19s age, but Ali already has plenty of senior cricket experience.

He turned out for Bradford club Great Horton Church as a youngster, but of late, he has developed his game at one of the Bradford Premier League’s top clubs, New Farnley.

And now the fast bowler will get the chance to shine in Bradford itself next season, having joined Jer Lane for the 2027 campaign.

Ali, who was the 2025 Player of the Year winner at the MACC Academy, founded by former Yorkshire player Moin Ashraf, will hope to build on his England U19 debut last month.

He took eight wickets in the home series against Pakistan, including 5-59 on his debut at Arundel Castle.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21637941.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Subhaan Ali will play for Jer Lane in the Bradford Premier League next season, but he has a big tour of Pakistan to think about first. *(Image: Allan McKenzie/SWpix.com)*

Bradley was also part of that series and he gets the chance to take part in the return leg out in Pakistan as well.

Bradley’s club cricket team is Elsecar, who are based in Barnsley.

But he has also been helped in his development by the Adil Rashid Cricket Centre, which was built in Thornton and opened in 2022.

The coaching there is led by the Bradford leg-spin icon’s older brother Amar.

He has helped mentor a number of young Yorkshire talents, including Jafer Chohan and Alex Wade.
