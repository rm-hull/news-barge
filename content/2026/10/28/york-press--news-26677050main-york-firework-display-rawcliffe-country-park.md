Thousands of people are due to celebrate Bonfire Night at York’s main firework display.

The bonfire and firework display will take place at Rawcliffe Country Park, near the park and ride site off Shipton Road, on Saturday, November 7.

Former firefighter Bruce Smith, who with Terence Gregg of Acomb Fire Station has organised the event for more than a decade, said it is expected to be the “biggest and best organised official bonfire and firework display in the county and north of the country”.

Gates open at 5pm with the main fireworks display taking place at 7.30pm.

There will be a funfair with food and refreshments at the site.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20124095.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bruce Reid worked for the fire service for more than 30 years before retiring in 2020 *(Image: Supplied)*

Bruce said gates will open at 4pm for young families to enjoy the attractions before the crowds.

There will also be a quieter firework display for children and families from 5.30pm.

“There’s everything for everyone,” Bruce said.

The event will once again raise money for the Fire Fighters Charity.

Bruce, who retired from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service in 2020 after 36 years, said the event raised £42,500 for the charity last year and has raised more than £130,000 since the pandemic.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/17408745.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A firefighter attends to the bonfire at Rawcliffe Country Park during a previous year's event *(Image: Dylan Connell)*

Speaking after last year’s event, Ellie Rocks, director of fundraising at the Fire Fighters Charity, said: “I am so grateful to Bruce, Terrance and the local community for their incredible support of Fire Fighters Charity.

“It is because of events like this that the charity is able to provide vital health and wellbeing support to the fire service community.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20309784.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bruce Reid hands the cheque to the Fire Fighters Charity outside York Minster after last year's firework display *(Image: Rachel Rogers/YorkMix)*

Bruce said this year’s event will be supported by its sponsors Sisk, Murphy and N Jagger Demolition and Earthworks.

Tickets (£12.50 for adults, £7.50 for children and free for under-fives) are available from ticketsource.com/nyfrs-fireworks

## Other firework displays taking place in York and North Yorkshire

Elsewhere, firework displays will take place across York and North Yorkshire more than 400 years after one of the city’s most famous figures Guy Fawkes plotted to blow up the Houses of Parliament.

Selby’s bonfire and firework display takes place from 7pm on Saturday, October 31.

Pickering’s firework display will be held at the Galtres Pickering Showground on Sunday, November 1. Gates open at 3pm with the firework display to start at 7.15pm. Tickets are available from skymagicevents.co.uk

Poppleton’s “bonfire and firework spectacular” takes place at the Poppleton Community Centre from 5pm on Thursday, November 5.

York Acorn Rugby Club will hold its firework display at the club in Thanet Road from 5.30pm on Thursday, November 5.
