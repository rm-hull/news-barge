A drunken man kicked a police officer as he was being led from a police van while being taken into custody.

Officers attending an unrelated incident at a County Durham address on Sunday, August 9, found Kevin David James shirtless and apparently drunk.

He was abusive to one of the officers and so he was handcuffed and taken into custody.

Prosecutor Gabrielle Veitch said James spat four times inside the police van’s holding cage during the journey.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21483945.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Peterlee magistrates were told man slipped being led from police van and kicked officer

At the police facility, as he was being led from the van, James kicked an officer in the right leg.

The 45-year-old defendant, of Johnson Street, Eldon Lane, near Bishop Auckland, admitted criminal damage and assaulting an officer in the course of her duty.

David Malone, for James, said he slipped while being led from the van and struck the officer with an inadvertent backward-kicking motion.

“But he was angry and shouldn’t have behaved as he did.”

Magistrates’ chairman John Marr said he was sure the defendant would be aware that the whole incident was, “a bit of a waste of our time”.

He told the apologetic James: “You were intoxicated and things got out of hand.”

James was fined £200 and was ordered to pay £85 in costs and £50 compensation to the officer he kicked.
