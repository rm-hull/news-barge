Footage released by **Spain’s Military Emergency Unit** shows rescue personnel searching the debris, while Civil Guard officers and firefighters assist at the scene. Around**100 UME personnel were deployed**, with sniffer dogs and specialist units working through twisted metal.

The injured were taken to hospitals in Toledo, where ambulances arrived throughout the morning and police cordoned off entrances. Regional officials said **58 people required hospital treatment**, about** 50 of them seriously**.

**King Felipe VI sent condolences** to the victims and their families, while**Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez expressed his solidarity and support**.

The crash occurred shortly before 9am on the **Madrid–Seville line**, at kilometre 118 between** Mora, Orgaz and Urda**. A** Renfe AVE train carrying 397 passengers struck the rear of a privately operated Iryo train**, which had stopped after a reported signal fault,** knocking two carriages off the tracks**. The Iryo service was carrying** 147 people**, including a baby.

The disaster is **Spain’s second major rail accident this year**, after a** January crash in southern Spain killed 45 people**.
