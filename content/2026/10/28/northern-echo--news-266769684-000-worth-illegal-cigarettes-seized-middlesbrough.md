About £4,000 worth of illegal cigarettes have been seized in Middlesbrough.

Officers from Cleveland Police's Middlesbrough Neighbourhood Policing Team discovered the cigarettes while working in the north of the town.

Police said the cigarettes were being stored and sold illegally, warning that the trade can fuel organised crime and antisocial behaviour.

The cigarettes have now been seized and HM Revenue and Customs has been informed.

The seizure comes as authorities continue to tackle the illicit tobacco trade in and around Middlesbrough.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21562887.jpg?type=mds-article-620)European Supermarket, where Mohammed Tariq Tofiq was caught selling counterfeit tobacco products in October 2025. *(Image: Hartlepool Borough Council)*

Earlier this year, Euro Supermarket on Gresham Road was closed following action by Middlesbrough Trading Standards.

An inspection on January 22 uncovered 560 cigarettes and 42 illegal vapes, before a further 6,240 illicit cigarettes and 1.5kg of hand-rolling tobacco were recovered on February 15.

Teesside Magistrates subsequently granted a three-month closure order against Smart Mini Market (NE Ltd), after hearing details of the premises' previous sales of illicit tobacco products and employment of illegal workers.

The operation was carried out by Redcar and Cleveland Trading Standards and Cleveland Police as part of efforts to disrupt the supply and distribution of illicit tobacco.

Following the latest seizure, anyone with information about the storing or selling of counterfeit cigarettes is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers online or by calling 0800 555 111.
