A Glasgow pub is launching a new Sunday roast especially for dogs, created by a head chef with Michelin-starred kitchen experience.

Anderson’s, located in the space formerly home to the 13th Note on King Street, has teamed up with Barklove, Glasgow’s dedicated festival for dogs and dog lovers, to introduce the Good Dog Roast.

Priced at £8, the dish features slow-roasted beef, sweet potato mash, roasted carrots and fresh greens, finished with dog biscuits and served alongside a fresh bowl of water. Prepared without the seasoning and gravy found on the traditional Sunday menu, the roast offers dogs “their own version of the much-loved British classic”.

The new menu addition has been developed by head chef Inesa Dirginciute, who brings experience from Michelin-starred Angler to the Anderson’s pub’s kitchen. Dirginciute is said to have applied the same care to the Good Dog Roast as to the rest of the Sunday menu.

She said: “Sunday roast is one of those meals that brings everyone round the table, and for a lot of our guests that includes their dog every week.

“We wanted to create something that feels like a traditional roast, made with the same care as everything else coming out of our kitchen, just made for pups. It's been a lot of fun to cook – and we've had no complaints from our taste testers.”

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21640702.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The dog-friendly dish features tender slow-roasted beef, sweet potato mash, roasted carrots and fresh greens *(Image: Supplied)*

To celebrate the launch on Sunday, November 22, Anderson’s and Barklove will host a special event from 12pm until 3pm.

Dog owners are invited to enjoy a traditional Sunday lunch alongside their pets, with "dog-friendly acoustic music" adding to the afternoon's atmosphere.

The Good Dog Roast will then be available every Sunday until the end of 2026.

Founded by Jenn Nimmo-Smith, Barklove launched in 2025 with its first festival at SWG3, returning for a second edition in July this year.

"More than just a day out for dogs", the event brings together the city's passionate pet-loving community for a celebration of everything canine, from independent pet brands and unique shopping experiences to expert-led wellbeing, interactive activities, art and entertainment.

Nimmo-Smith said: “Barklove has always been about bringing people and their dogs together and creating experiences they can enjoy side by side.

“We're delighted to be teaming up with Anderson’s to bring something a little different to Glasgow's Sunday lunch scene. Dogs are such a big part of our lives, so why shouldn't they get to enjoy a Sunday roast too?”

The collaboration comes as Barklove prepares for a series of festive events in Glasgow, including the first Barklove Barket at BAaD on Tuesday, December 8.

Barklove’s flagship festival is also set to return to SWG3 in 2027, with details due to be announced in the coming weeks.
