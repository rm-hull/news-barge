A beauty salon has officially closed its doors ahead of a relocation.

Minnie's Beauty, which was based on Nelson Street in Birstall, announced the closure on social media yesterday (Tuesday).

Owners said it was the last day of trading at the site as they prepared to move to another premises.

## When will the new shop open?

The new shop, based on Low Lane in Birstall, will officially open for business on November 3.

A Facebook post on Tuesday read: “Today we closed the doors to Minnie’s Birstall for the very last time, and I can honestly say my heart is absolutely broken.”

"Seeing the girls getting the last of their things out today and watching a place that has meant so much to us all become empty was one of the hardest things.”

## 'I knew this day would be emotional'

The owner reiterated how the business had been more than a salon and had become an important part of the their life.

It added: “Minnie’s has never just been a salon to me.”

The owner also thanked customers and other people who had supported the business, including those who had attended appointments, recommended the salon or called in for a chat.

The post continued: “To every single person who has ever walked through the doors of Minnie’s, whether you came for an appointment, recommended us, supported our little business or simply popped in for a chat, thank you from the bottom of my heart.

"Watching us all pack up and walk away today absolutely broke me.

"It’s so hard knowing we won’t all be walking through those same doors again, having our little chats, laughing together and doing what we’ve done for so long.

"I knew this day would be emotional, but I don’t think anything could have prepared me for how much it would hurt to say goodbye.

"Sometimes the hardest part of closing a door isn’t leaving the place behind, it’s leaving behind all the little moments and memories that made it feel like home."

The post added: “Minnie’s Birstall might be closing its doors, but the memories, the friendships and the love that filled that salon will stay with me forever.”

The business has not provided further details about the new premises beyond its location on Low Lane.

The T&A has contacted Minnie’s Beauty for further information.
