## Major £13.4m Harrogate town centre transformation gets green light

A £13.4 million scheme to transform Harrogate town centre is set to begin in the new year.

The project, funded through the Government’s Transforming Cities Fund, will focus on Station Parade and aims to improve access to Harrogate railway station for pedestrians, cyclists and bus passengers.

Improvements will also be made to the pedestrian route under the One Arch railway bridge to create a safer and more attractive link to the town centre.

Cllr Malcolm Taylor, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for highways and transport, said: "After almost six years in the pipeline, we welcome the start of this scheme which represents a significant investment in Harrogate town centre.

"We understand that any scheme of this scale can raise questions, particularly around how it will affect trade, so those ongoing discussions are important."

It is hoped that the scheme will be completed in autumn 2028.

Harry Braid, of Harrogate-based property investor Rushbond PLC, said: "As the new owners of The Exchange, we are delighted to see the ambitions of North Yorkshire Council, and the provision of substantial capital to improve the arrival experience and connections between the railway station and Harrogate town centre."

The project will be delivered by NY Highways on behalf of North Yorkshire Council.
