A restaurant that says it has brought 'something truly authentic to Bradford' is up for a top award.

My Peshawar, on Easby Road, near the city centre, is in the running for the Telegraph and Argus’ Best Curry House competition.

Owner Tauseef Malik said: "It's a huge honour for us, especially in a city like Bradford, which is famous for its incredible curry culture.

"There are so many fantastic restaurants here, so to be recognised among the best means a great deal.

![Front of My Peshwari](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634001.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Front of My Peshwari *(Image: Tauseef Malik)*

## Bringing Peshawari flavours to Bradford

"We only opened My Peshawar in 2022, so to receive this recognition in such a short space of time makes us even prouder."

"I'd like to say a massive thank you to everyone who nominated My Peshawar."

The restaurant was founded to bring the flavours of Peshawar to Bradford.

Mr Malik said: "At My Peshawar, we wanted to bring something truly authentic to Bradford.

"Dishes like our Chapli Kebab, Charsi Karahi and Kabuli Pilau are at the heart of what we do."

![Food offered at My Peshwari](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634003.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Food offered at My Peshwari *(Image: Jack Spicer Adams)*

He believes that freshly prepared food, generous portions, and a family-style hospitality have helped the restaurant stand out in a crowded market.

Mr Malik said: "We want every customer to feel like they're experiencing a little piece of Peshawar."

## 'Passion and effort into everything we do'

The nomination, he said, is a reflection of the loyalty and support the restaurant has received from customers since it opened.

![Rashid Khan at My Peshawar](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634006.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rashid Khan at My Peshawar *(Image: Jack Spicer Adams)*

Mr Malik said: "I think it's because our customers can see the passion and effort we put into everything we do.

"Many return regularly and bring their families and friends.

"We always try to maintain the same quality and standard, whether someone is visiting for the first time or the 50th."

![Food offered at My Peshwari](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634002.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Food offered at My Peshwari *(Image: Tauseef Malik)*

Winning the award would be a major achievement for the business, he added.

Mr Malik said: "When we opened My Peshawar in 2022, our goal was simply to serve authentic, quality food and build something the people of Bradford could be proud of.

"To win Bradford's Best Curry House just a few years later would be an incredible achievement."

![Rashid Khan at My Peshawar](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634007.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rashid Khan at My Peshawar *(Image: Jack Spicer Adams)*

## Facing industry challenges

Like many in the hospitality sector, the restaurant has faced challenges due to rising costs for ingredients, energy, and staffing.

Mr Malik said: "It's a constant balancing act between managing our expenses and keeping our prices affordable.

"People want to feel that going out for a meal is worth it."

![Food offered at My Peshwari](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634004.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Food offered at My Peshwari *(Image: Jack Spicer Adams)*

He also stressed the importance of supporting independent restaurants.

Mr Malik said: "Bradford has such a rich food heritage, and independent restaurants play a huge role in that.

"When people support independent restaurants like My Peshawar, they're supporting local families, hardworking teams and businesses that have put their hearts into what they do."
