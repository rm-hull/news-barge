A man has been charged after a suspected drink-driving incident on a Bradford road.

Police arrested the driver in the Wibsey area of Bradford on Saturday, October 24, after a report from a concerned member of the public.

The man was allegedly over the legal alcohol limit and driving without insurance.

A spokesperson for the Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team said: "On the evening of Saturday, October 24, officers from Bradford South NPT Team 3 responded to a report from a concerned member of the public regarding a suspected drink driver in the Wibsey area.

"Officers quickly located the vehicle and the driver subsequently provided a positive roadside breath test and a further positive evidential test whilst in custody.

"The driver was arrested and enquiries established that he was also driving without valid insurance. As a result, the vehicle was seized.

"The male has since been charged with driving whilst over the prescribed limit and driving without insurance and has been bailed to appear before the court.

"This is another example of proactive traffic enforcement helping to keep our roads safe.

"We would also like to thank the member of the public who reported their concerns.

"Never drink and drive. One decision can have life-changing consequences."
