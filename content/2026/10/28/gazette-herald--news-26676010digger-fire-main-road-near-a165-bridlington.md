A digger has caught fire on a main road close to a Yorkshire coastal town.

Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Kingsgate in Bridlington at 3.06pm yesterday afternoon (Tuesday, October 27).

A service spokesperson said staff had extinguished the fire before the crews arrived, and the service cooled the engine with hoses and surveyed the scene with a thermal image camera.
