Malton Museum hosted a successful Regency Weekend earlier this month, raising funds for its development.

In what has been described as ‘the best night out we have had for years’ by an attendee, the weekend consisted of a Country Ball at The Milton Rooms and a Soirée at York House, with music from Ebor Quartet, dance expertise from Lottie at Dance the Past, parlour games, and delicious food.

David and Janet Blakemore travelled from Howden to attend the event, dressed in authentic costume from the Regency period.

David said: "We came last year and enjoyed it so much we wanted to come along again.

"We love everything about the period including the clothes, dancing and social life and we travel all over the country to attend Georgian events."

Lottie Adcock, from Dance the Past, led the dancing for the evening said she had first become interested in the period 10 years ago.

"It is very elegant and very social and really helps to bring people together.

"People often think that it is difficult to do but very easy to pick up. I start by talking people through each dance and then we go from there."

Event organiser Annette Mitchell said: "Thank you to everyone who contributed to Malton Regency Weekend by either attending, buying raffle tickets or helping at the events. We have had such an amazing positive response to the weekend and raised £2261 for Malton Museum development fund."

If you would like to hear about future Regency events, please contact Malton Museum, and they will add you to the mailing list.
