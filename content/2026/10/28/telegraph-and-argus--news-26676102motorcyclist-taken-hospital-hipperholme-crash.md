A major road remains closed after a rush-hour crash involving a van and a motorbike left a rider needing hospital treatment.

Emergency services were called to the incident on Brighouse Road in Hipperholme at around 7.10am on Wednesday morning, with the collision causing disruption for commuters during the busy rush-hour period.

Details about the circumstances leading up to the crash have not yet been released.

However, it is understood the collision involved a motorbike and a van on Brighouse Road.

The condition of the motorcyclist has not yet been confirmed.

## Man taken to hospital

A man riding the motorbike was taken to hospital following the incident.

It is not yet known how serious his injuries are.

Emergency services remained at the scene on Wednesday morning while the aftermath of the collision was dealt with.

## Road remains blocked

Traffic monitoring service Inrix reported that the A644 Halifax Road remained blocked in both directions following the crash.

According to the service, the closure affects the road between Brooke Avenue and Broad Oak Place.

Queueing traffic has built up in both directions as motorists attempt to avoid the area during the morning commute.

Inrix said the disruption was expected to continue until at least 10.15am.

## Traffic building in both directions

Images circulating on social media appear to show a van blocking one side of the carriageway following the collision.

Long queues formed on both approaches to the scene, with drivers reporting significant delays on the route through Hipperholme.

## Investigation ongoing

West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been approached for further information.

Anyone with information about the collision is urged to contact police.

## FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police

A WYP spokesperson said: "This is a report of a road traffic collision on Brighouse Road between a van and motorbike, reported at 7.10am. The man on the bike has been taken to hospital."
