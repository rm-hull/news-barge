PLANS to close the home of a former district council's headquarters have been postponed until next year.

Concerns were raised in September that Ryedale House in Malton was set to close by the end of the year, prompting concerns over the future of key public services based in the building.

However, Cllr Keane Duncan, who represents Norton on North Yorkshire Council, says plans to close Ryedale House have been delayed, with most staff now remaining there until the New Year and weddings continuing until at least September.

It follows concerns raised by Cllr Duncan about a lack of democratic oversight relating to the closure. The council’s leadership has now agreed to involve elected councillors and share a public report.

Under the proposals, some staff are expected to relocate to Stanley Harrison House in Norton and the Kirby Misperton depot - but most face a move to Resolution House in Eastfield, 20 miles away.

So far, just 16 Ryedale House employees have changed their place of work. A further 28 could move in December, but the majority will now remain until further consultation has taken place.

Meanwhile, the council is working with key tenants, including North Yorkshire Police and Jobcentre Plus, to find alternative accommodation.

Cllr Duncan said: “I welcome the fact that the leadership has listened to the concerns I’ve raised and agreed to rightfully give elected councillors a say.

“Closing such an important public building is a major decision, with consequences for staff, local services and the wider community. It requires transparency, consultation and scrutiny.

“This extra time gives an opportunity to examine the proposals properly, ensuring staff are treated fairly and public services are protected here in Ryedale.”

A report detailing the plan is expected to be considered by the Thirsk & Malton Area Committee before going to the council’s Executive in December.
