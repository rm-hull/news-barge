A care home that was previously rated as requiring improvement is celebrating after achieving "Good" ratings across all five areas assessed by inspectors.

The new rating for Cooper House Care Home, on Cooper Lane in Bradford, covered the Safe, Effective, Caring, Responsive and Well-led categories.

The assessment report was published on October 21 following inspections carried out between September 17 and September 25.

## Turnaround recognised by inspectors

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634675.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Staff showcase military memorabilia at Cooper House. *(Image: Cooper House Care Home)*

Inspectors found there were now robust systems in place to keep residents safe, a positive culture among staff and leaders, and effective partnership working with healthcare professionals.

The report also highlighted a positive learning culture within the home and praised the way risks were managed and residents' needs were assessed.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634677.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Bradford care home earned 'Good' ratings in all five CQC categories. *(Image: Cooper House Care Home)*

## Care home celebrates achievement

In a statement, Cooper House said the rating reflected the commitment and compassion of its staff team.

Deputy manager Courtney Kozlow said: "This achievement means the world to us because it reflects the love, compassion, dedication and teamwork that fill our home every single day."

She added: "Above all, it recognises the commitment of our wonderful team to providing high-quality care, ensuring our residents feel safe, valued, respected and truly at home."

The home provides nursing and personal care and can accommodate up to 80 residents.

At the time of the inspection, 74 people were living at the service.

## Residents and relatives praise staff

The CQC report included positive feedback from residents and their families regarding the care provided at the home.

One relative told inspectors: "From the moment [my relative] moved in, the staff have been kind, caring and respectful, and it is clear that they genuinely care about the residents."

Another said: "The staff always have time to chat with my relative and me."

People using the service also described the environment as safe and clean and said staff were responsive to their needs.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21634674.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Veterans event at Cooper House Care Home. *(Image: Cooper House Care Home)*

## Year of milestones

Over the past year, Cooper House said they have achieved a number of notable milestones, including accreditation as a Veteran Friendly Care Home, and the retention of a five-star Environmental Health rating for its kitchen.

## Commitment to continuous improvement

While the inspection identified some areas where documentation and care planning could be strengthened, the CQC noted that issues raised during the assessment were addressed promptly by the provider.

The home said it would continue building on the progress that has been made.

Mrs Kozlow added: "We know that providing excellent care is an ongoing commitment, and we will continue to learn, grow and improve while keeping our residents at the heart of everything we do."
