A moped has burst into flames resulting in a nearby fence being set alight in York.

Firefighters were called to the blaze in Hazel Court shortly before 1.50pm on Wednesday (October 28).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crew from Acomb extinguished the fire.

In a statement, the fire service said: “A crew from Acomb responded to a report of a fire to fence and bushes.

“On arrival this was found to be a moped on fire which had spread to a fence and vegetation.

“Crews extinguished [the fire] using a hose reel jet.”
