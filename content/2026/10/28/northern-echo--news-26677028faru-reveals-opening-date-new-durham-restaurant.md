Award-winning Durham restaurant Faru has announced it will open the doors to its new home this week following its move from the city centre.

The fine dining restaurant shared the news on Instagram, confirming that its new venue at Flass Corner, Durham, will welcome customers for the first time on Thursday, October 29.

In a post on social media, the restaurant said: "We are delighted to announce that our doors will open at Flass Corner this Thursday.

"We can't wait to welcome you into our new space and share what's been in the works."

Bookings opened via the restaurant's website at 5pm on the day of the announcement.

The move marks a new chapter for Faru after it revealed it would leave its former home on Silver Street.

Owners Jake and Laura Siddle said the business had outgrown its original site and wanted a space that would allow it to continue developing its offering.

The new restaurant is based at Flass Corner, and will continue to serve the tasting menu that has helped Faru build a national reputation.

Details shared by the restaurant show guests will be able to choose from four-course and ten-course tasting menus during the opening week, with wine pairings also available.

Opening week will begin with dinner service on Thursday evening, followed by lunch and dinner sittings on Friday and Saturday.

Sunday will feature both lunch and dinner services, before the return of the restaurant's regular Sunday lunch offering from the following week.

The announcement was met with an enthusiastic response from followers, with commenters describing the news as "amazing", offering their congratulations and calling the opening "exciting".

The restaurant noted that its set menu is currently unavailable as it settles into the new venue.

Faru's new home is located at Flass Corner, Durham, DH1 4EF.
