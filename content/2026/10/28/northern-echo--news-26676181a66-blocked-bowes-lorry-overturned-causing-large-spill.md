A lorry flipped and caused a huge spill on the A66 near Bowes early on Wednesday morning.

The road was blocked both ways after the incident just after 6am on Wednesday (October 28), and one lane remains closed eastbound between the A67 and Barnard Castle.

Delays were reported to be building in the area, although traffic was later described as coping well.

Drivers have been urged to check their routes before travelling.

It came just 45 minutes after a lorry crashed and spilled its load on the A1(M) at Bowburn, sparking rush hour chaos.
