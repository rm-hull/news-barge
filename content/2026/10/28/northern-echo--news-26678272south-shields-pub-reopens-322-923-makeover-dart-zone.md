A North East pub is reopening its doors after a huge £322,923 makeover.

The Derby, located on Prince Edward Road in South Shields, is welcoming customers back this week (from Thursday, October 29) after the major investment.

The Derby is part of Proper Pubs, part of Admiral Taverns.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642245.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Derby in 2023 *(Image: Google Maps)*

Gary Thorius has run the pub for 15 years and is known for his strong ties to the community.

He serves as secretary of the local darts league and is a keen player in the pub's pool games.

Mr Thorius said: "I am so excited to re-open the doors of the Derby following the refurbishment and offer our customers even more sporting options.

"With new dartboard features, as well as new sound and visual options for customers, the viewing experience will be even better and I'm looking forward to welcoming everyone back."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642234.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gary Thorius at The Derby *(Image: Admiral Taverns)*

"The pub looks amazing and we have so many exciting events happening to celebrate this week I'd urge people to come and have a look and see all the improvements," he added.

The Derby supports 17 darts teams, four pool teams, and a football team.

Inside, the pub has been fully redecorated with new furniture and upgraded dartboard features to support league competitions.

Outside, improvements include new seating, signage, and exterior decor.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642236.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gary Thorius at The Derby *(Image: Admiral Taverns)*

The reopening will be marked with a series of events starting on October 29 with a grand opening at 6pm.

The launch evening will include live music from Darren Palmer, a fizz reception, and free cocktail sampling.

On October 30, there will be disco and karaoke starting at 7.30pm.

An adults’ Halloween party with a disco is planned for October 31 from 7.30pm.

The week wraps up on November 1 with a sports-focused evening and the launch of men’s darts on Sunday nights.

The Derby will also host regular live music, sports screenings, and entertainment including karaoke, quiz nights, and a games club.

Community bingo will take place on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons at 1pm.

Mr Thorius hopes to collect food donations for the local foodbank and support other causes such as Paws for thoughts, Heal & Toe, and the SURT children’s charity.

He also plans to raise funds for a defibrillator to be installed outside the pub.

Nikki Greenhalgh, operations director for Proper Pubs, said: "It’s a real pleasure to have Gary at the helm of The Derby as it joins our Proper Pubs estate and it’s great to see how well the refurbishment is coming along."

Proper Pubs is part of Admiral Taverns and operates more than 200 pubs across England, Scotland and Wales.

In 2024, Proper Pubs was named Community Pub Operator at the Publican Awards.
