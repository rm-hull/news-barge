**The Renfrew Bridge will be closed for "several months" after flooding during Storm Boelo damaged it.**

The £117m crossing, which links Renfrew with Yoker and Clydebank, opened in May 2025 as the first opening road bridge across the River Clyde.

Renfrewshire Council said it is assessing and repairing serious water damage to the bridge's electrical systems.

Extensive damage in one of the two operating plant rooms means the bridge will remain closed to road traffic for several months, allowing Clyde river traffic to operate normally while crews carry out the necessary repairs.

The incident significantly damaged the electronic systems controlling the hydraulics on the Renfrew side of the bridge.

Renfrewshire Council’s Head of Climate, Public Protection and Roads, Gerard Hannah, said work was underway to assess the full circumstances and restore the bridge's full function.

He said: “We’re very sorry for the inconvenience this will cause all those who use the bridge.

“We have two operating rooms, one on each side of the river, which manage the opening and closing of the bridge. While one side suffered minimal impact from the high-water levels, the plant on the Renfrew side suffered significant damage to the electrical systems required to operate the bridge.

"Urgent work is underway to identify the issues behind the damage and get the bridge back into action as soon as possible. We’ll keep the public updated with further details as the work progresses.”

The council said it is legally obligated to keep the Clyde open to river traffic and, as such, the swing bridge will remain open to allow essential passing traffic.

The exceptional tidal surge on Saturday night saw the Clyde water level reach 6.7 metres at Renfrew, the highest since digital records began in 1998 and more than twice the forecast level.

It is understood that activating the North Renfrew flood management scheme would not have prevented the damage to the bridge, which was designed to protect a different area from where the operating plant room is located.

Temporary measures to open the swing bridge are being put in place today, Wednesday, October 28, to allow scheduled river vessels to pass.
