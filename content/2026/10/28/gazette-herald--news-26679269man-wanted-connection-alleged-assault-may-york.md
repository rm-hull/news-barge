A man wanted in connection with an alleged assault may be in York.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information to trace Marley Ward, 35, who also has links to Selby.

The force said Ward is described as a white British man of average build, around 5ft 10in tall with closely cropped dark brown hair, light brown eyes and “little or no facial hair”.

“If you see Marley Ward, please call 999 immediately,” a police spokesperson said.

They urged anyone with information about Ward’s whereabouts to phone 101 quoting reference number 12260210772.
