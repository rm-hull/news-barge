# Watch: At the scene of Spain's deadly train crash

Dozens of people have died and others have been injured after two high-speed trains collided south of the Spanish capital Madrid.

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez said it was "unacceptable" that Spain had suffered its second fatal rail crash in a year.

The BBC's Guy Hedgecoe reports from the scene of the crash.
