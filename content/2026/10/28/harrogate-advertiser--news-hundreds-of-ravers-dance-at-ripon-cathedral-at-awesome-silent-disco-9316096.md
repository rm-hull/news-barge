# Harrogate superstar DJs wow the crowds at 'brilliant' Silent Disco inside awe-inspiring Ripon Cathedral

## What an utterly brilliant gig at Ripon Cathedral, the crowd were absolutely up for it”

Hundreds of ravers danced to banging tunes from Oasis and Blur spun by superstar Harrogate DJs inside the awe-inspiring, medieval arches and pillars of Ripon Cathedral.

![ DJ Trev played to hundreds of ravers at a Silent disco at historic Ripon Cathedral.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/28/14/02/RiponCathedralDJTrev2026.jpeg?trim=378,0,102,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The Silent Disco at the historic location starred three of Harrogate’s biggest DJs - DJ Trev, Rory Hoy and Andi Aikman of Jimmys nightclub fame .

All three hailed Ripon Cathedral as a “truly incredible venue” and said the crowd response had been superb.

There were two different sessions inside the nave of a building which dates to 1160 and includes the seventh century crypt founded by Saint Wilfrid in 672 AD.

A family-friendly session with wireless headphones and multiple music channels to choose from.

And a 90s-themed Adult-Only Silent Disco featuring three different DJ channels with 90s pop/dance, indie/rock like Oasis and Blur, and house/dance.

DJ Trev said: "What an utterly brilliant gig at Ripon Cathedral.

"The crowd were absolutely up for it.

![Harrogate DJs Andi Aikman, DJ Trev and Rory Hoy at Ripon Cathedral.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/28/14/10/AikmanDJ-TrevRoryHoyRiponCathed2026.jpeg?trim=14,0,156,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


“I played Pop, Metal, Bassline, Power Ballads, House, Indie, Hip Hop and Rave.

"It was awesome to share the stage with Andi Aikman Jimmys and Rory Hoy.

"Really brilliant work from the organisers and the team on the night.”

Forthcoming events at Ripon Cathedral include:

Ripon Cathedral presents The Rozzers, Europe’s most authentic tribute to the music of Sting and The Police on Saturday, October 31 at 7.30pm.

Ripon Cathedral Thursday Lunchtime Concert with Gillian Hart, oboe and cor anglais on Thursday, November 4 at 12.30pm.

A Concert of Remembrance in partnership with the Ripon Community Poppy Project on Friday, November 5 at 7.15pm.
