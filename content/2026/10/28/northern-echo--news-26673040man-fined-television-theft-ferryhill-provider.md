A Ferryhill man has admitted stealing a television worth £250 from a supported living provider.

Shane McIntosh, of Raby Road, Ferryhill, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on October 24 and pleaded guilty to three theft offences.

The 35-year-old admitted stealing the television from Red Supported Living in Ferryhill.

He was fined £120 and ordered to pay £250 compensation and £85 in Crown Prosecution Service costs.

McIntosh also admitted stealing £165 worth of washing pods in two separate thefts.

He stole £117 worth of washing pods from Sainsbury’s Local in Chilton on October 21 and was ordered to pay £117 compensation, with no separate penalty.

He also stole £48 worth of washing pods from B&M in Ferryhill on October 20 and was ordered to pay £48 compensation, again with no separate penalty.
