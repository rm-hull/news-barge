Thieves have targeted a main road in the suburbs of York and made off with a motorcycle.




North Yorkshire Police said a white Honda CBF 125 was stolen from outside a property in Melrosegate between 12pm and 5pm on Friday, October 23.

A force spokesperson said it is appealing for information and urging anyone who may have seen or heard anything in the area during that time to come forward.

They are also asking residents to check any CCTV or dash-cam footage that may have captured the incident or suspicious activity nearby.

Anyone who is able to assist the police should contact constable 1222 David Fulford at [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference 12260208724.
