City of York Council is considering planning applications covering a commercial unit, tree works, changes to homes, access alterations, solar panels, holiday accommodation and signs.

### Plans for a commercial unit at Haleys Terrace

An application has been submitted for the erection of a new commercial unit following the demolition of a garage at land adjacent to 23 Haleys Terrace, York.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01881/FUL | Validated: October 23, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Tree works proposed at Main Street

An application seeks permission to fell three conifer trees and crown reduce one maple tree by about two metres at Nevada, 54 Main Street, Wheldrake, York.

The proposed work would take place in a conservation area.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01875/TCA | Validated: October 20, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Application to reduce protected lime trees

Plans have been submitted to reduce two lime trees at York Associates, Peasholme House, St Saviours Place, York.

The work would provide two metres of clearance from the property, remove lower branches to raise the canopy to three metres, and remove dead or broken branches greater than 5cm in diameter.

The trees are protected by Tree Preservation Order no. CYC287.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01868/TPO | Validated: October 16, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Proposal for an additional storey in Kimberlow Woods Hill

An application has been submitted to enlarge the dwelling at 10 Kimberlow Woods Hill, York, by constructing one additional storey.

The new roof height would be no greater than 3.5 metres above the highest part of the existing roof, under Schedule 2, Part 1, Class AA of the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015, as amended.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01863/PAAA | Validated: October 15, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Access changes planned at Bluebeck Drive

An application seeks permission for alterations to access and the construction of a boundary wall to the side of Verandah Cottages, Bluebeck Drive, York.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01853/FUL | Validated: October 15, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Solar panels proposed at Toremill Close

Plans have been submitted for the installation of solar panels on the east- and west-facing roof slopes of 6 Toremill Close, New Earswick, York.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01819/FUL | Validated: October 20, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Retrospective holiday-let application in Bishopthorpe

An application seeks to change the use of 7 Keble Park North, Bishopthorpe, York, from a dwellinghouse in use class C3 to a short-term holiday let in the sui generis category.

The application is retrospective, meaning permission is being sought after the change has taken place.

It is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01622/FUL | Validated: October 23, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### Hanging signs proposed at High Petergate shop

An application has been submitted for the display of two hanging signs at The Japanese Print Shop, 38 High Petergate, York.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01451/ADV | Validated: August 14, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision

### External signs planned for Japanese Print Shop

A separate application covers external alterations at The Japanese Print Shop, 38 High Petergate, York.

The proposal includes one hanging sign and one fascia sign.

The application is awaiting a decision and is open for comments.

Ref. No: 26/01248/LBC | Validated: October 22, 2026 | Status: Awaiting decision
