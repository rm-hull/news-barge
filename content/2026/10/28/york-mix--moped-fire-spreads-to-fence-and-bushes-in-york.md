Firefighters tackled a blaze near York’s main household waste centre this afternoon (Wednesday).

They were called to Hazel Court in York at 1.48pm.

A moped had caught fire and the flames spread to a fence and bushes.

#### Hazel Court, York

The fire crew, from Acomb, used a hose reel jet to extinguish the blaze.
