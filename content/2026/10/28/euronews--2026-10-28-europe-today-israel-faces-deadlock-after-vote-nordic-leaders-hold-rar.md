## Israel's election has ended in apparent deadlock, with neither Benjamin Netanyahu nor Gadi Eisenkot on track for a majority. Finland's and Norway's leaders have phoned Vladimir Putin as Europe seeks a seat at the table. We also cover Serbia's vote, the upcoming US midterms, and Mbappé's China row.

## On today's show:

**Eylon Levy**, former spokesman for the Israeli government, discusses Israel's election and what comes next, whether this is the beginning of the end of the** Benjamin Netanyahu** era, and the role of Arab-led parties in deciding who becomes prime minister.

**Keith Brannum**, senior adviser on US politics at Greenlane and a Democratic strategist, looks at the upcoming US midterms and whether Democrats really have it in the bag.

**Sasha Vakulina** reports on the rare call Finland's**Alexander Stubb** and Norway's**Jonas Gahr Støre** held with**Vladimir Putin** and whether it brings peace talks any closer.

**Marko Prelec**, senior Western Balkans analyst at the International Crisis Group, discusses whether Serbia is turning a page after the student-led opposition's election upset, and who will become prime minister.

**Ken Early**, Irish sports journalist and broadcaster, assesses the damage to** Kylian Mbappé**'s brand after his joke about** Mao Zedong** cost him sponsorship deals in China.
