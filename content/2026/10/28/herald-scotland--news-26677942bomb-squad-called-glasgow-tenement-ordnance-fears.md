Bomb squad officers were called to a Glasgow tenement after the discovery of a suspicious device.

Police said it received a report of possible ordnance found at a property on Airlie Street in the Hyndland area of the city at around 10am on Wednesday, October 28.

Explosive Ordnance Disposal attended the scene.

The items were assessed and were deemed safe, police said.
