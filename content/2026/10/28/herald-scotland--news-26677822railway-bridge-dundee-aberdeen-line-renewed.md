A £3m project to replace an "ageing" railway bridge in Laurencekirk is due to begin next week.

The bridge carries Blackiemuir Avenue over the Dundee to Aberdeen line and will be replaced with a new structure, which Network Rail said will help "keep the busy route safe and reliable for years to come".

Work is due to start on Monday, November 2, and will continue until the end of March 2027.

Preparations to remove the bridge will get underway between now and January, when the railway will then close between Montrose and Laurencekirk for two weekends.

The existing bridge will be demolished from January 16 to 18, and the new structure installed the following weekend, January 23 to 25.

Blackiemuir Avenue will be closed to vehicles and pedestrians from November 14 until February 23, with signed diversion routes in place.

The signed pedestrian diversion has been designed to avoid the Back Settlement level crossing. As an additional precaution, a crossing steward will also be in place during peak periods, Monday to Friday.

Allison Flanagan, project manager at Network Rail Scotland, said: "Bridges are an essential part of the railway and, like any structure, they need to be looked after and renewed over time. Our job is to plan it carefully, get the work done safely and keep the impact on people as manageable as we can.

“We’ve had really useful conversations with people in Laurencekirk and we’re grateful for the way the community has engaged with us. We’ve taken that feedback onboard in our planning and we’d like to thank everyone for their patience and understanding while we carry out this important work.”

The Blackiemuir Avenue bridge is the first of two railway bridges in Laurencekirk being renewed, with work on the Station Road bridge expected to follow from November 2027.
