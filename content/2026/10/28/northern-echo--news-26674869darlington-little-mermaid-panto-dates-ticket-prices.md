Families in Darlington can dive under the sea with a colourful new panto packed with songs, laughs and traditional mischief.

STEPPS Theatre Group will bring The Little Mermaid to life at Darlington Community Theatre from November 19 to 22.

A spokesman said: "Audiences young and old are invited to dive under the sea for a fun-filled panto brimming with memorable characters, dazzling costumes, lively songs, and plenty of classic panto mischief."

The panto promises the magic, mayhem and warm-hearted storytelling the group is known for.

Laughter and surprises promise fun for all ages.

The four-day run will include evening and matinee shows.

Tickets are priced at £10 and can be purchased at www.stepps.co.uk/tickets.
