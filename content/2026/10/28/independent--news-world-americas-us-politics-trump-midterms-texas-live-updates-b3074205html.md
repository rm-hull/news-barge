President Donald Trump held back-to-back rallies in Texas on Tuesday, where he bizarrely claimed Democrat Senate candidate James Talarico was a vegan and gushed over Republican Ohio Senator Jon Husted’s rear again.

At his first rally in Corpus Christi, Trump mocked Talarico: “You ever see him go to a barbecue and he’s trying to show, ‘I love the meat. Ahhh.’ And then you see him — where is he? He’s behind the tent puking.” Talarico has denied claims that he is vegan.

Trump then stopped in Lubbock, where he couldn’t help but bring up a story about him examining Husted’s body at a previous rally.

”I made the mistake of saying — that I started off by studying his a\*\*,” the president said. “And then I looked at his feet, and they were massive. Then I looked at his hands...he has fingers like sausages.”

Trump was in Texas to drum up support for Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton and other GOP candidates ahead of next week’s midterm elections. Also in Corpus Christi earlier, Trump suggested making Venezuela America’s “51st state.”

Surveys indicate the Republican Party could sustain severe losses on November 3, potentially costing them their congressional majorities.

## Paxton and Talarico are neck-and-neck in Texas polls. How are Republicans in other pro-Trump states doing?

There are several states that have reliably voted for President Donald Trump before — but he may not be able to convince voters of choosing a Republican this time around.

Republican Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton and Democrat James Talarico are neck-and-neck in their Senate race. Talarico gained a 5-point lead over Paxton in a new N+ Univison/YouGov poll, but it’s still a toss-up.

Former Democratic Congressman Sherrod Brown was leading Republican Senator Jon Husted by several points in recent polls, but a new NetChoice/Echelon Insights survey has them tied at 47 percent of voter support.

## Trump announces plan to pull back Biden-era methane regulations

President Donald Trump has announced a plan to pull back Biden-era methane regulations, saying “paying more at the pump is going to come down really fast.”

“I’m pleased to announce another historic action to bring down energy costs and unleash Texas energy,” Trump told supporters at a rally in Texas Tuesday night.

“Under crooked Joe Biden, Democrats crushed the small oil and gas operators...with crippling regulations on methane that caused prices to skyrocket.

“Tonight I’m officially terminating Biden’s expensive and unnecessary methane regulations to save consumers and energy producers $45 billion in costs.”

Trump’s announcement comes as his war in Iran drags on for nearly eight months. The war has disrupted maritime traffic in the Strait of Hormuz, a key passageway for oil tankers. Gas prices have surged as a result.

## Trump continues to float Ted Cruz as potential Supreme Court justice

President Donald Trump has continued to float Republican Texas Senator Ted Cruz as a potential Supreme Court justice.

“The democrats will 100 percent vote for it because they hate the guy and they want to get rid of him,” the president added.

Cruz has a law degree from Harvard. After graduating from law school, Cruz clerked for Supreme Court Chief Justice William H. Rehnquist.
