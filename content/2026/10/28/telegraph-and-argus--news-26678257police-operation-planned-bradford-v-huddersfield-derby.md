West Yorkshire Police have been putting operational plans in place as Bradford City prepare to take on Huddersfield Town in a fiercely-contested local derby.

Last season, there were tense scenes involving fans before the two fixtures between the West Yorkshire rivals., with multiple arrests made.

A large police presence was in evidence before last year's fixture at Valley Parade, with officers located near Bradford Interchange, in Market Street, Darley Street and outside the Bradford Arms in Manningham Lane.

Ahead of Saturday's 12.30pm kick-off at Valley Parade, the T&A has been told that an "appropriate policing operation" will be in place.

In a statement, West Yorkshire Police has confirmed officers would be deployed to help ensure the safety of supporters, staff and residents.

A spokesperson for the force said: “An appropriate policing operation will take place on Saturday at Valley Parade to ensure the safety of everyone attending.

“The force is highly experienced with managing football events and works closely with Bradford City AFC, Huddersfield Town AFC and Bradford Council.”

## Previous derby saw arrests

The fixture has previously required a significant police presence, with officers carrying out a major matchday operation when the two sides met at Valley Parade in September 2025.

Six arrests were made as part of that operation, including two Bradford City supporters and four Huddersfield Town fans.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/19958168.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A large police gathering on Manningham Lane *(Image: Submitted)*

Police also launched enquiries following reports of a disturbance outside a pub on Manningham Lane before the match.

## Large crowds expected

The previous league meeting between the clubs at Valley Parade attracted a record modern-era crowd, with Bradford City running out 3-1 winners in the first league clash between the sides since 2007.

Officers, police vehicles and football intelligence teams were deployed throughout Bradford city centre and around the stadium, with rival supporters kept apart before kick-off.

## Safety plans in place

With another large crowd expected this weekend, police say they are well prepared for the fixture.

West Yorkshire Police regularly works alongside Bradford City, Huddersfield Town and Bradford Council when planning for high-profile matches and managing supporter safety.

Supporters attending the game are being urged to follow the instructions of police and stewards and to report any incidents of anti-social behaviour or disorder.
