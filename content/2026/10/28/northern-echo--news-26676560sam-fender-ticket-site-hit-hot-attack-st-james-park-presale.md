Sam Fender fans were left unable to buy tickets during the presale for his upcoming St James' Park concerts after a "sophisticated bot attack".

Angry fans took to social media to vent their frustration after struggling to secure tickets through Gigantic on Wednesday morning (October 28).

Some had even paid to pre-order the singer's new album to gain access to the presale, only to find themselves unable to buy tickets.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640297.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sam Fender fans were online all morning in hopes to bag some tickets for his Newcastle St Jame's Park dates *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Fender shared a statement on his Instagram story, saying the promoters had told him the ticket site had been targeted in a "sophisticated bot attack".

He added that the pre-sale would now have to be moved to Thursday (October 29) morning to ensure that 'real fans get the tickets'.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640316.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sam Fender's statement on Instagram *(Image: SAM FENDER/ INSTAGRAM)*

He said: "Being told by the promoters that there's been a sophisticated bot attack to the gigantic ticket site which is impacting the St James' Park pre-sale.

"We're going to have to move the Newcastle pre-sale to 9.30am tomorrow to ensure real fans get the tickets."

Fans had reported being kicked out of the website, with some claiming they had managed to get tickets into their baskets before losing access.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640298.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sam Fender fans were online all morning in hopes to bag some tickets for his Newcastle St Jame's Park dates *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

One wrote: "I actually somehow got through and had tickets in my basket for Sam Fender but it’s now kicked me out and page isn’t loading…. @Gigantic sort it out"

Another asked: "Has anyone beat Gigantic yet and managed to fully purchase St James Park Sam Fender tickets?"

One frustrated fan posted: "Sam Fender tickets still not available after 20 minutes of release. What a gigantic failure."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640300.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some managed to get into the queue but it sounds like most were booted back out after a short while *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Another wrote: "Gigantic what is happening? I can't get my Sam Fender tickets! What is this rubbish? All of my friends kicked out! @samfendermusic do something."

A further fan said: "@Gigantic what we doing about Sam Fender St James Park tickets because I have been waiting since 8am for this and it’s crashed…"

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21640299.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sam Fender fans were online all morning in hopes to bag some tickets for his Newcastle St Jame's Park dates *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The disruption comes as the North Shields-born singer prepares for his Proper Massive Stadium Tour in 2027, which will begin with two homecoming shows at Newcastle United's St James' Park on Thursday, June 3, and Saturday, June 5.

The tour will also take in Cardiff, London, Edinburgh and Manchester, alongside a date in Dublin.

The Newcastle concerts mark another return to the stadium for Fender, who played sold-out shows there in 2023 and 2025.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/19570362.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sam Fender has sent fans a cryptic message about a possible tour in 2027. *(Image: Chuff Media)*

His latest run of stadium dates follows the success of his album People Watching, released in 2025, with fans also anticipating his upcoming fourth album.

The album pre-order was used to secure access to the presale, leaving fans who had paid for early access particularly frustrated by the disruption.

General sale tickets for the 2027 stadium tour are scheduled to go on sale at 9.30am on Friday, October 30.

The new artist pre-sale for the Newcastle dates is now scheduled for Thursday, October 29 at 9.30am - leaving fans another 24 hours of anticipation.
