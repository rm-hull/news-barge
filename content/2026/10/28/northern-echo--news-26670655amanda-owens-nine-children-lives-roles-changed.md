Amanda Owen's nine children have grown up in front of millions of television viewers, but their lives today look very different from the early days of Our Yorkshire Farm.

Viewers watched the couple raise their large family at Ravenseat, their remote sheep farm in Swaledale, North Yorkshire.

But eight years after the series began, the children who were once seen helping with everyday farm chores are taking on increasingly important responsibilities.

## Edith and Raven take charge of the cream teas

Two of the Owen daughters are helping bring back a much-loved Ravenseat tradition.

Edith and her older sister Raven are shown putting the finishing touches to the family's cream tea van ahead of the busy summer walking season.

The refreshments were once a popular attraction for walkers visiting the farm, particularly those exploring the Pennine Way.

Their involvement marks another step towards the children taking greater responsibility for activities connected with Ravenseat.

Edith, who appeared as a young child in the original programme, is now helping with a venture that could attract visitors back to the farm.

## Miles takes on demanding farming work

Meanwhile, Miles is continuing to develop his practical farming skills.

In the October 26 episode, he works alongside his father Clive to tackle a waterlogged field at Ravenseat.

The job highlights the less glamorous side of farming in the Yorkshire Dales, where unpredictable weather can create difficult conditions for livestock and agricultural work.

Miles has also played an important role in the family's renovation of Anty John's, a historic farmhouse near Ravenseat.

The project has become a central storyline in Our Farm Next Door.

## Sidney joins the older family members

Sidney, known as Sid, is another of the Owen children taking on new responsibilities.

In the latest episode, he joins Amanda, Clive and Miles at the Tan Hill Inn following a demanding lambing season.

The family travel to the famous Yorkshire Dales pub to celebrate the end of another challenging period on the farm.

The scenes provide a reminder of how much the younger Owen children have grown since viewers first met them.

## Clementine helps care for a newborn lamb

The family's younger children are also continuing to play their part in life at Ravenseat.

Clementine, known as Clemmie, is shown warming a lamb by the fire during the latest episode.

The moment reflects the hands-on upbringing that became one of the defining features of Our Yorkshire Farm.

Lambing season remains one of the busiest periods of the year, with the entire family helping care for new arrivals.

## What are Amanda Owen's other children doing?

Amanda and Clive's nine children are Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Eldest son Reuben has already established a television career of his own, appearing in programmes following his work with machinery and engineering.

The other children continue to pursue different interests while maintaining their connections to the family farm.

The fourth series of Our Farm Next Door also follows the younger children enjoying an Easter egg hunt organised by Amanda.
