A former Royal Marines Officer has been appointed as a wealth planner in York.

Jacob Parsons has joined JM Finn to help meet growing demand for financial planning advice in the region.

Anna Murdock, head of wealth planning, said: "Jacob’s experience is exactly what we needed to help us support the team in York.

"Demand from clients for financial planning advice has continued to grow, so having a Wealth Planner based in the York office was the logical next step."

Mr Parsons will work closely with investment management to deliver integrated financial and investment planning.

Lucy Coutts, investment director and head of the York branch, said: "Having Jacob in the team is a huge boost to our ability to provide top-quality investment and financial advice to our clients.

"Putting advice at the heart of our proposition is key to delivering an enhanced client experience, and we’re delighted that Jacob has chosen us for the next chapter of his career."
