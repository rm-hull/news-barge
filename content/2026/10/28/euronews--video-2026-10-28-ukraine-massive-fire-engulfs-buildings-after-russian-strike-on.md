A **Russian jet-powered drone** struck Kharkiv's Osnovianskyi district late on Tuesday, 27 October,**injuring five people**, including a** four-year-old boy**, and setting residential and industrial buildings ablaze.

The **fires spread across some 2,500 square metres**, engulfing an apartment block, private homes, outbuildings and three industrial premises.** Nearby tower blocks, garages and cars were also damaged**, Ukraine's State Emergency Service said.

Footage shows **firefighters tackling the flames** and rescue workers helping residents to safety as smoke rises over the neighbourhood.

**Three of the five injured were taken to hospital**. Despite the risk of further Russian strikes, emergency crews** brought the blaze under control**, while psychologists assisted those affected.
