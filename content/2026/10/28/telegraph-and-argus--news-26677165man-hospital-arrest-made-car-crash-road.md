A man has been arrested following a crash in the early hours of this morning.

Police attended Woodhall Road in Calverley at 2.48am on Wednesday after reports of a collision involving one vehicle.

Images circulating on social media appear to show a vehicle overturned following the crash.

A person was later taken to hospital following the incident.

## Driver arrested

Following attendance at the scene, officers arrested the driver on suspicion of driving whilst unfit.

An investigation into the circumstances surrounding the collision remains ongoing.

No charges have been announced at this stage.

## One person taken to hospital

Yorkshire Ambulance Service was called to the incident shortly after 3am and dispatched an ambulance to the scene.

One patient was assessed and subsequently taken to hospital for further treatment.

No further information has been released regarding the person's condition.

## What the emergency services said

A YAS spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 3.04am this morning to report a road traffic collision on Woodhall Lane in Calverley.

"An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "At 2.48am this morning officers attended a one vehicle road traffic collision on Woodhall Road.

"The driver was arrested on suspicion of driving whilst unfit.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
