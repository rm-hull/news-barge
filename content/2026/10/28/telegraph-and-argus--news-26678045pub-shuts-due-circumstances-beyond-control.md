A village pub has announced its closure due to "circumstances beyond its control".

Owners of The Registry On The Cobbles, which is based inside The Old Registry on Main Street, Haworth, have taken to social media to share the news.

A Facebook post addressed to friends and customers reads: "It is with a heavy heart that we have to share the news that our bar has closed due to circumstances beyond our control.

"We want to say a huge and heartfelt thank you to every single person who has walked through our doors, supported us, shared a drink, celebrated with us and helped create lots of special memories during the past few months.

"Your custom, friendship and loyalty mean more to us than we can put into words.

"This is not the ending we had hoped for, but we will always be incredibly grateful for the laughter and more importantly the amazing people we have met along the way.

"Thank you for being part of our short journey and for all your support.

"It truly has meant the world to us."

The T&A has reached out to The Registry On The Cobbles for further information.
