A North Yorkshire care home team has raised more than £700 for the Alzheimer’s Society after completing a charity walk in support of people affected by dementia.

Staff from Sowerby House Care Home in Thirsk took part in the Leeds Memory Walk, held at Temple Newsam Estate on Sunday, October 11.

The 6km walk was organised by the Alzheimer’s Society to raise awareness of and funds for people affected by dementia.

Marie Adams, home manager, said: "Taking part in the Leeds Memory Walk was a fantastic opportunity for us to get together with others who know what it’s like to live with, care for, or support someone with dementia.

"It was a chance to raise awareness of this disease that has such a profound impact on those suffering, as well as their family and friends."

The team exceeded their original fundraising target of £175, with donations still open.

Ms Adams said: "In total, eight of us from Sowerby House took part.

"We had a wonderful time and it’s been a real privilege to raise funds and awareness for the Alzheimer’s Society.

"We’d love to raise even more for this worthy cause, and would like to thank everyone who has donated so far."

Sowerby House, part of the We Care Group, provides accommodation for up to 51 residents who need residential or dementia care.

Donations can still be made through the care home’s JustGiving page.
