# Budget 2026: Chancellor announces £100 energy payment for households

## John Healey detailed a Budget of ‘moderation’ which will give households payments towards energy bills but still ‘doesn’t go far enough’, according to critics.

Millions of households will receive £100 to help with soaring energy bills and high street firms will get a tax break as the Chancellor sought to protect British workers and businesses from the economic impact of the Iran war.

The Chancellor said the UK was being battered by a global economic storm, with the Budget watchdog slashing its forecast for gross domestic product growth next year.

Mr Healey hiked taxes on banks and gambling firms to provide “breathing space” he promised for households and high street businesses.

While some analysts in Yorkshire welcomed the Budget giveaways, others echoed the Conservatives in claiming Healey’s initiatives, which also include devolution business rates breaks for local businesses, ‘don’t go far enough’.
