A dog walker has spoken of their frustration after rubbish was once again dumped on a public footpath at a Bradford junction, just weeks after a previous fly-tipping incident at the same location.

The latest pile of waste - including timber, wooden boards and pallets - was discovered on the pavement near the Clayton Road junction and Beckside Road.

## Fresh concerns over repeat dumping

The dog walker said they were dismayed to find more waste blocking part of the footpath so soon after the previous incident.

They said: "We finished work and were going home and all I saw was rubbish out there again."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21640603.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A local dog walker said exposed nails and screws in the waste could pose a risk to pedestrians. *(Image: UGC)*

Photographs supplied to the T&A show a large pile of wood and building materials stacked against a property wall and extending onto the pavement.

The latest reports come less than three weeks after concerns were raised about fly-tipped waste at the same location.

The dog walker said they were concerned about the potential impact on pedestrians using the route.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21640605.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The latest incident comes less than three weeks after fly-tipping was reported at the same location on October 11. *(Image: UGC)*

## 'It's a massive hazard'

The resident said the dumped materials posed a risk to people walking through the area, particularly during school holidays.

"It's a public footpath. It's always been a public footpath," they said.

"People are driving past, school holidays are on, Halloween season is coming up and all the children are out.

"It's a massive hazard there. Imagine someone fell over. You've got screws coming out and nails coming out."

They added that they were especially concerned someone could be injured if the waste remained on the pavement.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21640601.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A pile of timber and building materials left on the pavement near the Clayton Road junction. *(Image: UGC)*

## Calls for stronger action

The dog walker said they had become increasingly frustrated by the repeated appearance of waste at the site.

They said: "Name and shame them. That's what it's gone down to now. We shouldn't be backing down now."

The dog walker said they hoped action could be taken to prevent further incidents and stop the problem recurring.

The T&A has contacted Bradford Council for comment and asked whether the latest incident has been reported, whether an investigation is being carried out and what action, if any, may be taken.
