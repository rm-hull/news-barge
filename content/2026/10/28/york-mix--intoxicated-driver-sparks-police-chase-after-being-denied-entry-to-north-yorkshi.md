Police were called in after a ‘heavily intoxicated’ man was refused entry to an event near Malton – then got into a car and drove away.

It happened last night at about 9.30pm. Officers located the car and signalled for it to stop, but it made off.

In an attempt to get away, it was being driven “erratically” down minor roads.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/malton-alleged-drink-driver-arrested-27-oct-2026-3-1.webp)

Police deployed a stinger, deflating all four tyres and bringing the pursuit to an end.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The driver appeared intoxicated and failed to provide a roadside breath specimen when asked to so. He was arrested and taken into custody, and the vehicle was recovered.

“Once in custody, he provided evidential samples which showed he was nearly three times the legal drink-drive limit.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/malton-alleged-drink-driver-arrested-27-oct-2026-2-1.webp)

“He has since been charged with driving over the prescribed alcohol limit and is due to appear in court.”

They added: “Thank you to the member of the public who contacted us.

“If you suspect someone is driving under the influence of drink or drugs, please call 999 and report it immediately.”
