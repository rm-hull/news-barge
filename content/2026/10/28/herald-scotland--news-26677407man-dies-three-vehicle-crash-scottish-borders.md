**A 43-year-old man has died following a three-vehicle crash in the Scottish Borders.**

Emergency services responded to the incident on Tuesday, which involved a Ford Focus, a Toyota Hilux and a Tesla Model S, on the A6089 Station Road, near Gordon.

The 43-year-old man, who was the driver of the Ford, was pronounced dead at the scene.

The driver of the Toyota, a man, was taken to Borders General Hospital, and the Tesla driver did not require treatment.

**Read More:**

Sergeant Barry Sommerville, of Police Scotland, said: “Our thoughts are with the family and friends of the man who has died at this difficult time.

“Inquiries into the circumstances of the crash are ongoing and I would appeal to anyone who witnessed the crash or has any information that may assist to contact us.

“I would also ask anyone driving in the area at the time with dashcam footage to please review this to see if it has captured anything that could assist our investigation.”

Anyone with information should contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2708 of Tuesday October 27.
