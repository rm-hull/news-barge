**Update: National Highways has confirmed that the westbound carriageway of the A63 has fully reopened this afternoon (Wednesday, October 28) following the earlier crash.**

A crash has closed a major road in East Yorkshire.

National Highways said the A63 westbound is shut between the A15 for Hessle and Hull, and the A1034 for South Cave, which is the junction for a key route to York.

Emergency services are at the scene, the agency said.

Delays of 20 minutes and two miles of congestion are reported in the area.
