A luxury hair salon is celebrating 20 years of business.

Joseph Bespoke, based in Ilkley, has spent two decades serving the community with personalised hairdressing services and has marked the occasion by becoming the town’s exclusive stockist of the Kérastase professional haircare range.

The salon will also celebrate its second anniversary at its South Hawksworth Street premises this December.

Phillip Dawson, who originally established the business, said: “We're incredibly proud to be celebrating 20 years in Ilkley.

“It's a fantastic town to have a business and we've built some wonderful relationships with our clients over the years.

“Many have been coming to us for a very long time and that loyalty means everything.”

The business began as The Loft on Church Street, before rebranding to Joseph Bespoke in 2014 following a merger between Phillip’s salon and that of his husband, Joseph Dawson.

The Ilkley salon is led by Phillip, while Joseph manages the Wakefield branch.

Mr Dawson is joined in Ilkley by business partner Mark McKenzie and a team of experienced stylists.

They offer precision cutting, bespoke colouring, colour correction, specialist treatments, and styling for weddings and special occasions.

A key milestone for the salon is its partnership with Kérastase, the luxury haircare brand founded in Paris in 1964 and known for its science-led approach to hair and scalp health.

Joseph Bespoke is now the only salon in the area to stock the Kérastase range, a partnership that Mr Dawson describes as a “milestone.”

He said:  
“Being selected to stock Kérastase is another milestone for us.

“It's a brand with an exceptional reputation, and we're delighted to be able to offer our clients access to its products, alongside technology that helps us provide an even more individual service.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21638001.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Joseph Bespoke exterior *(Image: Supplied)*

As part of the partnership, the salon now offers complimentary hair and scalp analysis using specialist diagnostic technology.

This service helps clients understand the condition of their hair and scalp and identify the most suitable products and treatments.

The salon’s philosophy has always been to treat every client as an individual.

Mr Dawson said:  
“Our philosophy has always been about recognising that everyone is different.

“We want every client to look and feel their best, but equally importantly, we want them to feel valued and enjoy the whole experience of coming into the salon.”

He also expressed gratitude to the community.

He said:  
“We're very grateful for the support we've received from the Ilkley community over the past 20 years.

“We never take that for granted and we're looking forward to continuing to develop the business, support our team and play an even bigger part in the local community.”
