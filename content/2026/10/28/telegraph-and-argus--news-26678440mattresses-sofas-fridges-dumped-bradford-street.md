A residential street in Bradford was left littered with mattresses, sofas and household appliances after a major fly-tipping incident.

The waste was discovered on Collins Street in Great Horton, where household items had been dumped across the pavement and road.

Bradford Council said officers are carrying out inquiries into a vehicle believed to have brought the rubbish to the site.

## Clean-up operations underway

When the T&A visited the site on October 27, clean-up operations appeared to have commenced.

Bradford Council has since confirmed it is aware of the fly-tipping and that work to clear the waste is underway.

Councillor Ian Walker (Wyke, Reform UK), the council's Executive Member for Neighbourhoods and Communities, said: "The council is aware of the fly tipping on Collins Street.

"Skips were placed there yesterday (October 27), with more arriving today to clear what has been fly tipped on private land."

The council said waste that had spilled onto the road has now been removed.

Cllr Walker said: "The waste on the highway has been removed, and our teams will be checking the area again to remove any final items."

## Investigation ongoing

Cllr Walker confirmed investigations are ongoing as officers work to establish who was responsible for bringing the waste to the site.

The council has identified a vehicle suspected of being involved in the dumping.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21642519.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fly-tipping on Collins Street in Bradford *(Image: T&A)*

The council is urging anyone who may have witnessed dumping activity, or who has information about the vehicle, to come forward as inquiries continue.

Cllr Walker said: "Enforcement action is underway with further investigations ongoing regarding a vehicle that is thought to have been bringing waste.

"Fly-tipping is a criminal offence, and we encourage anyone with information about this incident or any other of this nature to report it via Bradford Council's website or by ringing 01274 431000."
