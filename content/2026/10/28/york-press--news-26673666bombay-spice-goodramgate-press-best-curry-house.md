## Celebrated restaurant in York shortlisted for prestigious Press award

Bombay Spice has received two accolades this year *(Image: Supplied)*

“We seriously cannot believe the love and support we have been getting over the past few years by our wonderful customers”.

These were the words of Bombay Spice manager Mohammad Ali Akbar.

Bombay Spice, in Goodramgate, is one of ten restaurants shortlisted for a Best Curry House Award this year.




It comes after hundreds of readers of The Press nominated their favourite restaurants.

![Bombay Spice, in Goodramgate, is up for a Best Curry House Award 2026](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21636109.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bombay Spice, in Goodramgate, is up for a Best Curry House Award 2026 *(Image: Supplied)*

As reported in The Press, Bombay Spice won Outstanding Achievement for Restaurant of the Year - Yorkshire and secured 3rd place for Outstanding Restaurant of the Year for the 2026 competition.

Speaking about this, Mohammad said: “These prestigious awards recognize our commitment to culinary excellence, and we couldn't be prouder of this incredible milestone.

“We want to take this opportunity to express our deepest gratitude to our amazing staff members for their tireless hard work and dedication.

“Most importantly, a massive thank you to our wonderful customers—your continuous support and loyalty make success like this possible.”

![Popular dishes include chicken karahi, a range of tandoori mixes, biryani and a large selection of vegetarian meals](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21636110.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Popular dishes include chicken karahi, a range of tandoori mixes, biryani and a large selection of vegetarian meals *(Image: Supplied)*

The restaurant’s extensive menu features a la carte, early dining and lunch time special dishes.

Popular dishes include chicken karahi, a range of tandoori mixes, biryani and a large selection of vegetarian meals.

Readers of The Press can vote for Bombay Spice in the Best Curry House Awards 2026 by filling out a voting slip included in editions of the paper.
