A new scheme could help first-time buyers get on the property ladder.

Bellway is hosting dedicated events across Yorkshire from 31 October to 1 November following details of the Government’s Your First Home scheme, which are set to be announced in the Budget on Wednesday 28 October and aim to support first-time buyers with a lower deposit.

The events will run from 11am to 5pm on Saturday and Sunday across several developments, including sites in Beverley, Boroughbridge, and Brough.

Melanie Smith, sales director for Bellway Yorkshire, said: "We understand that the homebuying process can seem daunting, especially for first-time buyers, and our aim is to make it as easy and straightforward as possible."

"We know people will have questions about what it really means for them in practice, so we’ve organised these First-Time Buyer Events as soon as possible after the Budget to guide them through it all."

Visitors will also have the chance to speak with mortgage experts from New Homes Mortgage Helpline.

Under the scheme, eligible buyers could secure a new-build home with a minimum 2.5 per cent deposit, supported by a 20 per cent government-backed equity loan and a 77.5 per cent mortgage from a participating lender.
