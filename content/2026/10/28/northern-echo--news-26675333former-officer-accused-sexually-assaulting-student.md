A ex-police officer is accused of touching a student's bottom and attempting to abuse his position as her tutor to form a sexual relationship with her.

Craig Hamilton, who has since resigned from Cleveland Police, faces a misconduct hearing next month accused of touching the woman's bottom without consent while off duty in Middlesbrough.

He is also accused of seeking to abuse his position as her tutor to form a sexual relationship with her.

A misconduct hearing will take place at Scotswood House in Stockton, on Wednesday, November 11 and Thursday, November 12.

If proven the allegations could amount to gross misconduct.
