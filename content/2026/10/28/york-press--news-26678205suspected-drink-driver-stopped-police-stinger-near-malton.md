## Suspected drink-driver charged after fleeing from police near North Yorkshire town

*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A suspected drink-driver was stopped by a stinger after failing to pull over for police near Malton last night.

North Yorkshire Police received a report at around 9.30pm that a "heavily intoxicated" man had been refused entry to an event before getting into a vehicle and driving away.

Officers quickly located the vehicle and observed it being driven erratically.

The driver attempted to evade police by taking minor roads and failed to stop when signalled by officers.

A stinger device was deployed, deflating all four tyres and safely bringing the pursuit to an end.

The driver appeared intoxicated and failed to provide a roadside breath specimen.

He was arrested and taken into custody, where evidential samples showed he was nearly three times the legal drink-drive limit.

The man has since been charged with driving over the prescribed alcohol limit and is due to appear in court.

A police spokesperson said:

"We are absolutely committed to making North Yorkshire's roads safer by relentlessly pursuing those who put others at risk by driving under the influence of drink or drugs.

"Our officers see first-hand the devastating consequences of these entirely preventable offences and will continue to take robust action against offenders.

"Thank you to the member of the public who contacted us."

Drink and drug driving remains one of the Fatal Five offences that contribute to the most serious collisions on our roads, alongside speeding, careless driving, not wearing a seatbelt and using a mobile phone at the wheel.
