LEEDS railway station is set to be transformed with a £303 million upgrade.

The investment, confirmed in the Budget, includes an additional £200 million in funding to support improvements aimed at easing congestion, improving accessibility, and future-proofing the station for long-term growth.

The upgrades are expected to be completed by 2030.

Heidi Alexander, transport secretary, said: "Leeds station is the gateway to West Yorkshire, and this extra £200 million investment will turn it into a station this city can be proud of: modern, welcoming and built for the people who rely on it every single day.

"The great cities of the north deserve better transport, and that’s exactly what we’re delivering.

"We're paving the way for Northern Powerhouse Rail, forging stronger connections across the region, and backing the infrastructure that creates jobs, builds homes and puts passengers first as we work to set up Great British Railways."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21643188.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Leeds Station arches renovation *(Image: Supplied)*

Key features of the project include a new central overbridge, three additional entrances, expanded concourse space, and an extended Platform 14.

Passenger flow will be improved with nearly 40 per cent more ticket barriers and increased lift capacity.

The new entrances will also improve access to the South Bank, Dark Arches, and Princes Square, supporting regeneration and business growth in central Leeds.

The upgrade paves the way for the Transpennine Route Upgrade, an £11 billion investment to improve rail connectivity and capacity across the North.

Tracy Brabin, mayor of West Yorkshire, said: "Leeds Station is the beating heart of our region's transport network, connecting millions of people to jobs, education and opportunity every year – and it’s only going to get busier.

"This investment will benefit passengers and businesses, help to unlock the capacity we need for future growth and create a gateway to West Yorkshire that reflects our ambition."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21643190.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Leeds Station's new walkway *(Image: Supplied)*

Currently, Leeds station serves more than 27 million passengers annually, with eight out of 10 West Yorkshire rail services starting or ending there.

Passenger numbers are expected to rise by nearly 12 million by 2043.

The station upgrade supports the government’s plan for Northern Powerhouse Rail, which aims to deliver a ‘turn-up-and-go’ network with frequent services between major northern cities.

The Department for Transport said the improvements form part of a broader effort to modernise the UK’s rail system.

A spokesperson said: "From bringing operators back into public ownership to investing new and better stations, fleets and technology, real improvements are underway ahead of the establishment of Great British Railways."
