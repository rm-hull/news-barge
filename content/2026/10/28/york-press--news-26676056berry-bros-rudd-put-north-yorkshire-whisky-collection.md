A limited-edition whisky from a North Yorkshire distillery has been selected for a special bottling by a renowned wine and spirits merchant.

The Spirit of Yorkshire’s 2017 Moscatel Sherry Cask #2231 has been chosen by Berry Bros. & Rudd, Britain’s oldest family-owned wine and spirits merchant, for its 2026 Odyssey collection, The Auld and the Bold.

The collection celebrates the evolving world of whisky, honouring both Scottish heritage and innovative distillers worldwide.




Joe Clark, whisky director at Spirit of Yorkshire Distillery, based in Hunmanby, said: "This release marks the second independent bottling of our whisky from Berry Bros. & Rudd.

"The cask was hand-selected straight from our warehouse by Felix Dear, Spirits Curator at Berry Bros. & Rudd.

"Together, we scoured the warehouse and selected an exceptional nine-year-old ex-Moscatel Sherry cask with notes of peach honey, ginger and a light dusting of chocolate.

"To work with Britain’s oldest wine and spirits merchant for a second momentous release is nothing short of an honour and a privilege."

### 'Not so long ago, a nine-year-old English single malt felt like a distant prospect'

Felix Dear, spirits curator at Berry Bros. & Rudd, described the bottling as a milestone for the distillery and for English whisky more broadly.

He said: "This is our second bottling from Spirit of Yorkshire, and one we’re especially proud to share.

"At nine years old, it marks a real milestone, not just for the distillery, but for English whisky as a whole.

"Not so long ago, a nine-year-old English single malt felt like a distant prospect.

"Now, here we are.

"This whisky spent much of its life in an ex-Moscatel cask, which brings a bright, fragrant character to the fruit-forward house style Spirit of Yorkshire does so well."

Only 318 bottles of The Auld and the Bold, bottled at 57.1 per cent ABV, have been released.

Each 70cl bottle is priced at £125 and is available for purchase online at bbr.com, in the London store, and through selected retail partners.

Berry Bros. & Rudd, established in 1698, is still based at No.3 St James’s Street in London.

The Spirit of Yorkshire distillery produces Filey Bay single malt whisky using 100 per cent homegrown barley, making it one of the few true field-to-bottle distilleries in the UK.
