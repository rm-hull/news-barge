A family-owned restaurant that has served Darlington diners for almost three decades has been nominated in The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

The Star of Bengal, on Duke Street, has once again been shortlisted, having previously made the top ten venues in Darlington and County Durham in 2024.

The venue was first opened in November 1998 by the late Haji Asaddar Miah Choudhury and was previously managed by the late Haji Shabbir Ahmed Chowdhury.

The restaurant first opened on Gladstone Street where it was based for 21 years until 2019, when it moved into a building on Duke Street.

Now owned by Matiur Choudhury, the restaurant has remained a family business across the almost three decades and now boasts a 4.5 star rating on Google Reviews.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633288.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Star of Bengal in Darlington *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The Star of Bengal is a previous Darlington curry chef award, organised by Darlington Borough Council and has also been crowned the northern region curry chef winner.

Diners praise the “fantastic” and “authentic” food - including its “phenomenal” onion bhajis - as well as the "friendly" staff and “lovely” customer service.

One happy diner said: “The food was absolutely amazing and the service was fabulous.

“The staff were super attentive and friendly and there was such a nice atmosphere. The best Indian food in Darlington hands down.”

The Star of Bengal is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Arvelli Indian Restaurant, Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.

Readers will decide the winner in a public vote, and we will be featuring each of the businesses nominated in the next two weeks.

To vote, complete the form in The Northern Echo each day for the next two weeks and return it to: The Northern Echo Best Curry House 2026, Local IQ House, 1 Dalston Road, Carlisle, CA2 5NP.

Votes will be counted soon after voting closes.

The winner will be announced after voting closes on November 7.
