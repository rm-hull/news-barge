Amanda Owen is set to surprise her youngest children with an epic Easter egg hunt at Ravenseat Farm in the latest episode of Our Farm Next Door.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess, who became a household name through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, organises a special Easter trail for the younger members of her family in the remote Swaledale countryside.

And while Easter brings some excitement for the children, it is also one of the busiest periods of the year for the Owen family.

Despite the demanding farming schedule, Amanda finds time to arrange an Easter egg hunt for the younger children.

The youngsters set off on a trail organised by their mum, bringing some family fun to the spectacular Yorkshire Dales setting.

The moment offers a glimpse of the family traditions that viewers came to love during the original Our Yorkshire Farm series.

Since the programme first aired in 2018, audiences have watched the Owen children grow up surrounded by animals, open countryside and the everyday challenges of life on a working hill farm.

While the older children are increasingly taking on responsibilities of their own, the younger members of the family continue to enjoy adventures around Ravenseat.

The latest episode also follows Clementine, affectionately known as Clemmie, as she helps care for a newborn lamb.

She is seen warming the animal beside the fire while other family members tackle the demanding work taking place outside.

Clive and son Miles are busy dealing with a waterlogged field, highlighting the difficulties of farming in the unpredictable Yorkshire Dales weather.

Elsewhere, Amanda's daughters Edith and Raven are preparing for another important development at Ravenseat.

The sisters are putting the finishing touches to the family's cream tea van ahead of the summer walking season.

The refreshments were once a popular attraction for visitors to the farm, and their return marks another chapter in the family's story.

Meanwhile, work continues at Anty John's, the historic farmhouse Amanda and Clive have spent years restoring.

Carpenter Dan is transforming the upstairs space into individual rooms as the ambitious renovation moves forward.

And as the demanding lambing season draws to a close, Amanda sets off on horseback to join Clive and sons Miles and Sidney for a celebratory drink at the famous Tan Hill Inn.

The journey also prompts Amanda to reflect on the history of Anty John's and its former inhabitant, mapmaker Anthony Clarkson.
