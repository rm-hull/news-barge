Police searching for this wanted man say he has links to York and Selby.

Marley Ward, 35, is wanted over an assault.

Marley has connections to the York and Selby areas, and officers are asking members of the public to report any sightings or information about his whereabouts.

He is described as

* white
* around 5ft 10in tall
* of average build
* with closely cropped dark brown hair
* light brown eyes
* and little or no facial hair.

North Yorkshire Police said: “Please share this appeal to help us locate him.”

If you have an immediate sighting, call 999.

If you have any information that could help police locate him, please call 101 and ask for North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference number 12260210772.
