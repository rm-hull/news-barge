A man has appeared in court after heroin worth more than £9 million was seized when police stopped a car on one of Bradford's busiest roads.

Jason Smith appeared before Leeds Magistrates' Court today charged with a count of being concerned in the supply of heroin.

The 24-year-old, of Beech Walk, off Eightlands Road, near Dewsbury railway station, was arrested and charged in relation to an ongoing investigation into drugs offending in Kirklees and Bradford.

Officers stopped a car on Valley Road in Bradford at just before 6pm on Monday, and recovered suspected Class A drugs which were later confirmed to be heroin.

The drugs have been valued as having a street value of just over £9.3 million.

A 44-year-old Dewsbury man was also arrested by officers as part of enquiries, and has now been released under investigation.

Enquiries into the offending remain ongoing by the Kirklees Precision serious organised crime team.

Smith was remanded in custody to next appear before Leeds Crown Court on December 1.
