A popular food reviewer has found the “world’s greatest sandwich.”

Danny Malin, host of YouTube series Danny vs Food and former presenter of Rate My Takeaway, made the bold claim during his recent tour of the United States.

In his latest video, Danny visited Philippe, a Los Angeles sandwich shop that has been serving customers since 1908.

He was joined by special guest Josh Weinstein, a writer and executive producer on seasons four to eight of The Simpsons.

## What was his order and how much did it cost?

The pair ordered beef dip, lamb dip, pastrami dip and turkey dip sandwiches, along with iced teas and lemonade.

The total bill came to $86, or approximately £64.70.

## 'The beef is absolutely beautiful'

Danny began with the beef dip and was immediately impressed.

He said: "The buns have got a nice crust to them.

"What I like about them is that they taste a bit like French stick, but not as hard.

"The dipping softens the inside of the sandwich.

"The beef is absolutely beautiful – full of flavour."

## 'I don't think they are too expensive'

He then moved on to the lamb dip, suggesting it could be improved with a touch of mint sauce.

He said: "I think a little bit of mint sauce on that would elevate it.

"But it is good."

Turning to the turkey sandwich, Danny praised the overall quality and value.

He said: "I think the whole selection of meat is quality.

"These sandwiches start at $14, and I don’t think that’s expensive.

"Sometimes, just the simplest of food, cooked properly, is the best."

## 'I put gravy on my fish'

Danny continued to highlight the appeal of the restaurant’s signature gravy.

He said: "You’ve got that beautiful dip gravy.

"I’m a gravy lover – I put gravy on my fish.

"But it adds a whole different level to the sandwich."

After working his way through the selection, he delivered his final verdict.

He said: "I’ve had beef, turkey and lamb.

"I thought the turkey was phenomenal and the beef was unreal.

"The lamb, I enjoyed because it’s cut straight off the leg.

"But it would have been nice to have some mint sauce.

"I’m going to give this a solid 10.

"I love the place, the food is amazing, the meat is absolutely spot-on.

"If I gave it any less than a 10, I’d be criticising myself."
