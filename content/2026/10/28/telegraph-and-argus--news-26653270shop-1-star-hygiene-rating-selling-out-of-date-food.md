A shop has been found selling out-of-date food and putting customers at risk of cross-contamination.

Vinus Market Bradford Ltd, trading as Vinus Market on Otley Road, was inspected by Bradford Council officers on August 6.

A series of food safety breaches were found and the shop was handed a one-star food hygiene rating as a result.

## Food past its sell-by date

The most serious issue identified was the sale of food past its use-by date.

Officers stated: "Food beyond its use-by date was found for sale on your premises.

"It is an offence to sell or expose for sale food with an expired use by date."

The business was told to take immediate action, with the inspector adding: "You must check your stock daily and dispose of any out-of-date food."

## Risks of cross-contamination highlighted

Inspectors also raised concerns about the handling of ready-to-eat foods, noting that items on display lacked shelf-life dates to indicate when they had been opened.

The business was advised: "You must give a date for discarding on foods which have been opened."

The report highlighted risks of cross-contamination.

The officer said: "You must ensure that cling-film is not left on the floor."

## 'Use separate equipment for raw and ready-to-eat foods'

Staff were told to use separate equipment for raw meat and ready-to-eat foods, including cling film, dispensers, and lining papers.

The inspector wrote: "You must ensure that you use separate cling-film and dispenser for raw meats and ready to eat foods.

"You must ensure that you use separate lining papers for raw meats and ready to eat foods to prevent cross-contamination."

**The T&A reached out to Vinus Market for comment.**
