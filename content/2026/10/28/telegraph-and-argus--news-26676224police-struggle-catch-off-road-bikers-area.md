Police say off-road bikers causing anti-social behaviour along the Spen Valley Greenway are proving difficult to catch because of the route's numerous access points.

Officers from Batley and Spen Neighbourhood Policing Team were called to the Greenway in Heckmondwike on Tuesday evening following reports of a quad bike and off-road motorbike being ridden in the area.

However, despite attending the scene, officers were unable to locate the riders after they reportedly left the area and headed towards Dewsbury.

## Police attend reports of off-road bikes

The incident prompted a response from neighbourhood policing officers after concerns were raised about the vehicles being used on the popular walking and cycling route.

By the time officers arrived, the quad bike and motorbike had already made off from the Greenway.

## 'Difficult to locate offenders'

Police said one of the challenges in tackling the issue is the number of entry and exit points along the Greenway.

A spokesperson for Batley and Spen NPT said: "Although we do encourage everyone to report incidents of this nature, it can prove difficult to locate offenders due to The Greenway having so many access points."

The force said reports from residents remain important, even when offenders are not immediately located.

## Public urged to share intelligence

Officers are now encouraging residents to provide information about those responsible and where off-road bikes may be stored.

The spokesperson said: "We would encourage people to pass any information they have in relation to those involved in these incidents and any locations where off-road bikes are being stored.

"This information can then be acted upon, providing ourselves with the opportunity to seize bikes and quads from offenders."

## Appeal for information

West Yorkshire Police is asking anyone with information about off-road bikes or other anti-social behaviour in the area to get in touch.

Reports can be made via 101 or anonymously through Crimestoppers.

The warning comes amid ongoing concerns about the use of off-road bikes and quad bikes in public spaces, with police continuing efforts to identify riders and seize vehicles being used illegally.
