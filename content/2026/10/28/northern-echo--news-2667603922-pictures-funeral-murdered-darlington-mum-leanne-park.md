Hundreds of mourners gathered to say a heartbreaking final goodbye to 'murdered' Darlington mum Leanne Park on Tuesday.

Friends, family and loved ones packed out Darlington Crematorium to pay their respects yesterday (October 27) to the 44-year-old whose death devastated the community after she was allegedly murdered last month.

Floral tributes surrounded the crematorium as everyone came together to remember Leanne, described as a woman with a “pure heart of gold” who would do “anything for anyone”.

Her children Lewis and Natalia carried their mum’s coffin into the service as LeAnn Rimes’ *How Do I Live?* played.

The scale of the turnout reflected the impact Leanne had on those around her, with mourners gathering outside to follow the service through speakers and screens after the chapel filled.

Leanne was remembered as the “life and soul of the party”, a devoted mum and nana who loved to dance and always made time for her family and friends.

Heartfelt floral arrangements were laid in her memory, including displays featuring Fosters cans, a cigarette, hearts, a butterfly and a horse.

Flowers were also arranged in the shape of the words “friend” and “mam”.

The 44-year-old, who worked at Ventress Hall Care Home, was found dead in a flat above The Greyhound pub on Parkgate on Wednesday, September 23, after emergency crews were called at 9.15pm.

Her death led to police seeking reviews of the premises licences for The Greyhound and the nearby Black Swan. Both pubs subsequently had their alcohol licences revoked.

Andrew Bell, 44, has denied murdering Leanne and is due to stand trial on March 8, 2027. He has been remanded in custody.
