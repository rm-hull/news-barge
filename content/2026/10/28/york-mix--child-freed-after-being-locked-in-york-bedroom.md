Firefighters were called to help a young person who got stuck at home last night (Tuesday).

The Acomb crew went to the incident in **Kingsway West, York**, at** 6.57pm**.

A child was locked in a bedroom.

#### Kingsway West, Acomb, York

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews used mode of entry kit and crew power to gain access and freed them safely.”
