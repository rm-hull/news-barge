A murder mystery set in a seaside hotel is the latest release in a popular cosy crime series.

Glenda Young’s fifth Seaview Hotel book, Music to Die For at the Seaview Hotel, is set in Scarborough and will be released in paperback on Thursday, December 3.

The story follows 1980s pop band The A64 as they reunite for a gig at Scarborough’s Open Air Theatre and stay at the Seaview Hotel, where landlady Helen Dexter finds herself at the centre of another case.

The series was shortlisted for Best New Crime Series at the Harrogate crime festival in 2022, alongside books by Richard Osman and Val McDermid.

Ms Young will be launching her book at the Music Against Child Poverty gig at The Fire Station in her hometown of Sunderland.

Her other books are available to buy in town at Waterstones, Mrs Lofthouse’s Book Emporium, Boyes and Central Tramway.
