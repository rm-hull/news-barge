Thousands of guinea pigs are expected to take part in a nationwide health challenge in Beverley this week.

Nearly 3,000 guinea pigs will be involved in the Big Pig Health Check, which will take place on Friday, October 30, at Bishop Burton College, as part of Guinea Pig Awareness Week.

The challenge aims to deliver 500 five-minute health checks across the UK in a single day.

It comes in response to findings from the Burgess Excel Small Animal Census, which revealed that many owners lack confidence in recognising signs of pain or stress in their pets.

One in three owners do not regularly check their guinea pig’s weight or body condition, and 64 per cent only visit the vet when a problem is suspected.

Suzanne Moyes, deputy managing director and in-house vet at Burgess Pet Care, said: "These findings reinforce why this year's Guinea Pig Awareness Week is focused on encouraging proactive health checks and preventative care.

"Because guinea pigs naturally hide signs of illness, pain and discomfort, owners need to feel confident spotting the subtle changes that can indicate something is wrong."

Organisers are encouraging owners to take a proactive approach to their pets’ health by carrying out regular checks at home.
