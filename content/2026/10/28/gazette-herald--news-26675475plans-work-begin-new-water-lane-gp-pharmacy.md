Plans have been submitted for work to begin on a 'vital' new community pharmacy in York.

York Medical Group said work on a new in-house pharmacy at Water Lane GP Surgery could begin within the next fortnight, subject to approval.

It comes after the surgery, which serves 5,743 patients, announced its three-month closure for major refurbishment works on Monday (October 19).




A spokesperson for the group said: “The work includes renewal of pipes, replacement of some flooring and painting.

“The nature and extent of this work means it is safer for patients and staff for the building to be closed while it is carried out.

“Closing the building should also allow the work to be completed more quickly.

“We are also taking the opportunity during the closure to begin work to accommodate a new community pharmacy within the surgery, planned to open in 2027.

“Following the closure of the previous local pharmacy, we are pleased that this will restore community pharmacy provision locally and give patients convenient access to pharmacy services alongside their GP surgery.”

![“We are also taking the opportunity during the closure to begin work to accommodate a new community pharmacy within the surgery, planned to open in 2027](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638798.jpg?type=mds-article-620)“We are also taking the opportunity during the closure to begin work to accommodate a new community pharmacy within the surgery, planned to open in 2027" *(Image: Google Maps)*

The pharmacy would provide support for minor ailments and would work side-by-side with the wider primary care team.

News of the plans has been welcomed by ward councillor Danny Myers, who joined Cllr Margaret Wells in campaigning for a pharmacy provision in Clifton, following the closure of Boots, in Clifton Green, in 2024.

Cllr Myers said: “I want to thank everyone who signed our petition to restore pharmacy services in Clifton after the old Boots closed.

“We’ve met with the Pharmacist group and remain in close correspondence to support their offer of services in Clifton.

“They are a family business with long-standing experience in the pharmacy sector.

“Based in Leeds, they currently operate one York dispensary.

“We welcome them to Clifton and whilst for many, it has taken some time, we are really pleased to have overturned the initial rejection from the NHS.

“We quickly produced a new needs assessment through our work on the council and submitted a community petition to show the real need for pharmacy services in our community.

“Alongside this new pharmacy premises in an ideal location, we are also excited to see Curious Coffee opening in the old Boots.

“Another independent business at Clifton Green that promises to be a super addition to our local independent offer.”

![Clifton ward councillors Margaret Wells and Danny Myers campaigned to bring back a pharmacy to the area ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/17699948.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Clifton ward councillors Margaret Wells and Danny Myers campaigned to bring back a pharmacy to the area *(Image: City of York Council Labour Group)*

York Central MP Rachael Maskell added: "I'm delighted we are close to reopening this vital service for local residents.

"People in Clifton will welcome the news of work taking place at Water Lane Surgery to accommodate the new pharmacy."

While the Water Lane site remains closed, the surgery’s GPs, clinical staff and patient care co-ordinators will work from other surgeries in York Medical Group.

A spokesperson said: “We plan to maintain our usual number of appointments, with Water Lane appointments provided at other sites, mainly Tower Court and Monkgate.

“We do not anticipate needing additional locum cover as a result of the closure.

"We are sorry for the inconvenience and disruption this temporary closure will cause.

“These improvements are needed, and we are grateful for our patients’ patience while the works take place.”
