Motorists are facing significant delays heading into Glasgow following a breakdown on a busy motorway.

Traffic Scotland said the M77 is partially blocked northbound after Junction 1 at Dumbreck Road due to a broken-down vehicle.

Drivers are facing delays of more than 30 minutes, with significant congestion in the area.

Heavy traffic is queuing on the approach, with congestion stretching back from Junction 4. Drivers are currently experiencing delays of around 33 minutes.
