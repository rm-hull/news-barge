The North East has provided the backdrop for some of the most memorable moments in Bob Mortimer and Paul Whitehouse's popular show, Gone Fishing.

Middlesbrough-born Mortimer has taken Whitehouse fishing in his native region of the North East across several episodes of the Bafta-nominated BBC programme since its 2018 debut, often using the series to revisit his childhood and share memories of his Teesside upbringing.

Most recently, episode 4 of the new series 9 follows the pair as they fish for brown trout at High Force Waterfall, near Barnard Castle, in County Durham.

Mortimer had previously hinted to BBC Radio Tees that the series would return to the North East after the success of the 2020 Christmas Special.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21498178.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mortimer and Whitehouse were spotted filming Gone Fishing at High Force, County Durham in summer. *(Image: SUBMIT)*

The angling comedy duo can be seen fishing in the River Tees as Mortimer leads Whitehouse around High Force, looking at the landscape through the eyes of his childhood self.

He discusses treasured memories of fishing trips with his father, who died when Mortimer was very young, while the pair take in some of County Durham's most beautiful scenery.

The recent episode also includes actor and comedian Reece Shearsmith, who shares a meal with the pair and tells spooky stories about the River Tees.

Mortimer and Whitehouse's most recent visit continues a link between the North East and the pair's travels for Gone Fishing, first filming in the region for the show's 2020 Christmas Special.

The special followed Mortimer as he returned to his childhood roots in Middlesbrough, fishing in the Rivers Tees and Esk with Whitehouse.

The hour-long episode also featured sea fishing off Staithes, North Yorkshire and a visit to Mortimer’s childhood home in Acklam, Middlesbrough.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20073409.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The 2020 Christmas Special followed Mortimer as he returned to his childhood roots in Middlesbrough, fishing in the Rivers Tees and Esk with Whitehouse. *(Image: BBC)*

Mortimer and Whitehouse can be seen travelling on the North Yorkshire Moors steam railway before going north to catch grayling in the River Tees and River Esk.

Another visit has taken the series to fishing spots along the North Tyne in Northumberland.

Mortimer and Whitehouse fly-fished on the North Tyne in search of an English salmon before being joined on the riverbank by former Newcastle United footballer Paul Gascoigne.

The episode formed the 2021 Christmas Special, also featuring manor houses and castles across Northumberland and Cumbria.

Throughout the series, locations across the region have allowed Mortimer to connect with early memories while fishing, and provided an opportunity for North East fans to spot their favourite duo in some landscapes close to home.

The episodes have also introduced some of the North East's most beautiful scenery to viewers across the country.

All episodes of Mortimer & Whitehouse: Gone Fishing are available on BBC iPlayer.
