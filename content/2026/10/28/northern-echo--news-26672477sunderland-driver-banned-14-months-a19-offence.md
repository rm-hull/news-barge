A North East driver has been banned for 14 months after admitting driving with a controlled drug above the legal limit.

Alan Nesbitt, of Santorin Close, Sunderland, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 23.

The court heard that on February 27 he drove a silver Vauxhall Vivaro, on the A19 northbound at Sheraton, County Durham.

A blood test showed benzoylecgonine at more than 200 micrograms - the proportion exceeded the specified limit.

The 50-year-old pleaded guilty on October 23 to the offence.

Magistrates fined him £120 and imposed the obligatory 14-month driving disqualification, taking his guilty plea into account.

He was also ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £48 surcharge.

A collection order was made for deductions from benefit, with the lump sum to be paid in full within 14 days.
