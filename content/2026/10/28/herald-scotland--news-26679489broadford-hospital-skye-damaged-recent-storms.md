Broadford Hospital on the Isle of Skye has experienced water damage as a result of the recent storms.

NHS Highland said that contingency plans are in place "to keep the hospital and its services running while repairs are carried out".

As a result of the damage, some areas of the hospital have been closed off, with alternative areas of the hospital being used in the meantime.

Storm Boelo brought 80mph gusts and travel disruption to much of Scotland this past weekend, shortly after Storm Austen delivered heavy rain to many areas on Friday.

More than 30,000 homes across Scotland experienced power outages on Sunday after Storm Boelo battered the country overnight.

In a statement issued on social media on Wednesday October 24, NHS Highland wrote: Broadford Hospital has experienced water damage as a result of the recent storms.

"Contingency plans are in place to keep the hospital and its services running while repairs are carried out. Some areas have been closed off and we are using alternative areas of the hospital in the meantime. Patient safety remains our priority.

"If you have an appointment today or in the coming days that is affected, the team will contact you directly. If you have not heard from us, please attend as normal.

"Thank you for bearing with us while we get the building back to normal."

Broadford Hospital opened in May 2022 to replace to older facilities like the Dr Mackinnon Memorial Hospital.

It features a 24-hour Accident and Emergency (A&E) and Minor Injuries Unit handling emergencies, bone injuries, eye injuries, and tele-medicine.
