Double yellow lines have been approved at multiple locations across an area in the Bradford district.

The new restrictions will affect roads in Haworth, Oakworth, Oxenhope and Cross Roads, with the legal order now in force following a public consultation earlier this year.

A public consultation period ran from May 25 until June 18.

## New locations revealed by district councillor

Worth Valley district councillor Rebecca Poulsen (Cons) said: "The following locations have now been approved and the legal order has come into force.

"This is following a public consultation period earlier in the year.

"These were as part of the old Worth Valley Ward boundary so don’t include Denholme."

## Which roads are affected?

In Oakworth, the affected locations are Wide Lane, Slaymaker Lane, Hillside Avenue and Manor Park.

In Haworth, the affected locations are Belle Isle, Ebor Lane, Cold Street, Oak Street, Branwell Drive, North View Terrace and Baden Street.

In Oxenhope, the affected location is Hebden Bridge Road and in Cross Roads, it is Vale Mill Lane.

The lining work will now be scheduled with the contractor.
