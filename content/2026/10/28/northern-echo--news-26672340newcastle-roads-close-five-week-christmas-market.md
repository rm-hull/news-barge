Several roads in Newcastle city centre are set to close to traffic for more than five weeks for a Christmas market.

Grey Street, Grainger Street, Eldon Square, and Nelson Street will be shut to vehicles from November 15 until December 23.

The council has warned that vehicles will not be allowed on these roads while the market is in operation, except for delivery and servicing vehicles, which will have access between 9pm and 9.30am.

Emergency vehicles on call will also be granted access.

A council spokesperson said: "The Order is required to facilitate the holding of the Christmas Market Event 2026 (“the Event”) and to reduce the disruption to traffic likely to be caused by the Event.

"The Order is required to allow the Event to be carried out safely on or near the highway and to ensure the health and safety of the general public."

The alternative route for vehicles will be signed on site as required.

Further information can be obtained by emailing [trafficregulationsorder@newcastle.gov.uk](mailto:trafficregulationsorder@newcastle.gov.uk) or calling the council's traffic management section on 0191 2787878.
