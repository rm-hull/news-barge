A NEWLY published football travelogue is shining a spotlight on Bradford's very own Albion Sports.

The Only Way Is Cup – The FA Cup Travelogue, written by Ian McNeilly, follows the author’s journey through all 14 rounds of last season's FA Cup, beginning with Albion Sports’ away fixture against Sheffield FC in the Extra Preliminary Round.

Mr McNeilly said: "The very first tie was Albion Sports away at Sheffield FC.

"All the lads in the game's squad are mentioned in the book, so their little place in the history of the FA Cup has been immortalised in print.

"The book would make a good Christmas present for them, their family members, fans of Albion Sports or just anyone interested in football and/or travel."

The book chronicles 20 matches, featuring 35 teams from the earliest stages of the tournament through to the final at Wembley.

Mr McNeilly describes it as: "Part travelogue, part football and social history,"

He added: "There is a beauty to this unique and historied competition with all its stories – and there’s a beauty to completing your own journey following it.

"Start locally, and explore new characterful grounds, teams, clubhouses, villages, towns and cities.

"The non-league clubs welcome you warmly, and need your hard-earned money much more than the big boys of the Premier League."
