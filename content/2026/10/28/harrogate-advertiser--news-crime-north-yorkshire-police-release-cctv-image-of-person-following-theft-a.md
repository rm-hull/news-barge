# North Yorkshire Police release CCTV image of person following theft at supermarket in Harrogate

## If you recognise this person, you should get in contact with the police

North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to following a theft at a supermarket in Harrogate.

![The police have issued a CCTV image of a person they would like to speak to following a theft at Sainsbury’s, on Leeds Road, in Harrogate](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/28/11/03/868919410_1832023391155681_7548944086551163149_n.jpeg?trim=3,0,397,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The incident happened on September 28 at Sainsbury’s on Leeds Road.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured in the CCTV image, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260193543 when providing any details regarding the incident.
