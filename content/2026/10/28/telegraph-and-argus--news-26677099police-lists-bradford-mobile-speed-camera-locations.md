Here is a list of all the mobile speed enforcement cameras in Bradford currently.

These are portable devices used by police forces and road safety partnerships to monitor vehicle speeds and enforce speed limits.

Unlike fixed speed cameras, which are permanently installed at specific locations, mobile cameras can be moved between sites and are often deployed in areas where speeding has been identified as a safety concern.

## How do the cameras work?

These cameras are usually operated from marked enforcement vans parked at the roadside, although some systems can be used by officers on foot.

They typically use laser or radar technology to measure the speed of approaching or departing vehicles.

When a vehicle is detected travelling above the legal speed limit, the camera records evidence such as the vehicle's speed, registration number, location, date and time.

## How are you told that you were speeding?

Mobile speed cameras are commonly used near schools, accident hotspots, roadworks and rural routes where excessive speed has contributed to collisions.

Their temporary nature means drivers are less able to predict enforcement locations, helping to encourage compliance with speed limits across a wider area.

If a vehicle is recorded exceeding the speed limit, the registered keeper may receive a Notice of Intended Prosecution (NIP) and could face a fixed penalty, a speed awareness course or court action, depending on the severity of the offence.

Supporters argue that mobile cameras improve road safety, while critics sometimes question their effectiveness and placement.
