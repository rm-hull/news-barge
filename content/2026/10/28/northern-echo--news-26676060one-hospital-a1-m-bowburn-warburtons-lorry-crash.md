A HGV driver has been taken to hospital after their lorry crashed onto its side on the A1(M).

The incident happened near Bowburn at 5.14am on Wednesday (October 28), with the road closed to junction 60 at Bradbury.

One person, the HGV driver, was taken to University Hospital of North Durham.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639971.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One person has been taken to hospital after an overturned HGV crash on the A1(M) near Bowburn. *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Pictures shared on social media show the lorry on its side spanning the north and southbound carriageways.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639506.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: submitted)*

Durham Police advised motorists to seek an alternative route while the closure was in place.

Police and recovery vehicles remain on scene, clearing the highway.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "Police were called just after 5.10am today following reports that a lorry had overturned on the A1(M) just after the Bowburn junction.

"The lorry has shed its load across both carriageways, resulting in a full closure of the northbound carriageway between Bowburn and Carville Interchange. The southbound carriageway is restricted to the hard shoulder only.

"Motorists are advised to avoid the area and find an alternative route.

"The driver of the lorry has been taken to hospital for treatment."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639635.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: submitted)*

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on the A1(M) at Bowburn at 5.14am.

"We dispatched a double-crewed ambulance, a clinical team leader and two vehicles from our hazardous area response team.

"One patient was taken to University Hospital of North Durham."

A spokesperson for County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service said: “At 5.12am today (Wednesday), we received a report of an overturned lorry on the A1, between junctions 60 and 61.

“Two crews, from Spennymoor and Durham, attended the scene to assist with the safe extraction of the driver.

“Crews left the area by 5.47am.”
