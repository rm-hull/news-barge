## City of York Conservatives slam 'the bottling budget'

Chancellor John Healey with the famous red ministerial box *(Image: PA)*

City of York Conservatives have slammed what they call the “bottling budget.”

The Conservative Group accuses Chancellor John Healey of ignoring the main issues the country faces, with “an increasingly clueless and directionless government.”

However, they welcome the budget as “the first Labour budget in three years not to have crushing tax rises only because the Chancellor knew he could not get away with it.”

The Conservatives also welcomed the scheme to pay £100 to help 20 million householders with their energy bills.




They also backed moves to take more small business out of business rates, though they believe they do not go far enough.

More funding for Mayors and devolving money and business rates to regions was also backed.

However, York’s Conservatives said the budget should have cut employment taxes, like reversing the National Insurance rise former Chancellor Rachel Reeves introduced in 2024.

Cuts in red tape, regulation and the size of the civil service with the government getting on top of “out of control” spending was also sought.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21641805.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chancellor John Healey with the famous red ministerial box *(Image: PA)*

York’s Conservatives also sought “good news” on council funding, saying York has had an “appalling settlement under Labour.”

There was also “a lack of tangibles” on how Labour would meet its NATO defence spending commitments.

The Conservative Group Leader Cllr Chris Steward said: “UK government debt has recently reached £3 trillion and the average family is paying just under £4,000 of tax purely on paying the interest on that debt.

“These are staggering figures due to an economy which is simply not working due as it should – work is less appealing than ever and more than 20% of working age people are not in work and indeed under Labour 1,000 people have been joining those not working every single day.

“The Chancellor also announced another increase to tax on savings and property earnings – penalising people who want to work hard and do the right thing.”

Cllr Steward continued: “Amidst all the global uncertainty there was more need to act now than ever to cut tax but the chancellor knows he cannot deliver change as his MPS will oppose every penny of welfare reform.

“Rather than moves to get the economy growing, instead we have had to make do with gimmicks like lowering tariffs on gherkins.

“There is no vision from the top of government, they are simply tied in paralysis until global bond market declare time on them.”
