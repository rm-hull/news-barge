Independent traders from across the region will showcase their creations at a Halloween market.

The Spooktacular Halloween Artisan Market is at Teesside Park on Friday, October 30, with trick-or-treating, seasonal treats and handcrafted goods.

The open-air market runs from 10am to 4pm, with independent makers, artists, designers and food producers selling jewellery, Halloween décor, autumn gifts and bakes.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "We are thrilled to be bringing a fantastic mix of independent artisan traders to Teesside Park this Friday.

![A cake](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21632309.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Wares to be found at Teesside Park’s Halloween Artisan Market *(Image: Supplied)*

"Our Halloween Artisan Market is the perfect opportunity for families and visitors to discover incredible regional talent, pick up unique handcrafted gifts, and soak up the autumnal atmosphere.

"We can’t wait to see you all there."

Shoppers can pick up trick-or-treat goodies and cosy homeware while supporting small businesses.

Harriet Thorne, owner of Musko Clothing, said: "We’re thrilled to be part of the Halloween Artisan Market at Teesside Park this Friday.

"Autumn is such a great season for getting outdoors, and we can’t wait to bring our range of sustainable nature-inspired clothing and gifts to local shoppers.

"It is a fantastic opportunity to support independent makers and pick up something truly unique."

Nicola Mortimer-White, owner of Amber and Olive, said: "We cannot wait to be part of the Teesside Artisan Halloween Market this weekend at Teesside Park.

"From our hand-poured, natural plant-based candles and botanical wax melts to our gentle liquid hand and body soaps, we’ve crafted products that bring warmth and wellness into your home as the dark nights start to come in.

"It’s going to be a wonderfully spooky market."

Visitors can also enjoy Teesside Park’s high-street fashion brands, Hollywood Bowl, Showcase Cinema and restaurants including Nando's, Five Guys and Pizza Express.
