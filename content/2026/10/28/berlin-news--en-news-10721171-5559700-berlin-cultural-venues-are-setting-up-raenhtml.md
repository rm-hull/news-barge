In the coming weeks, colorful benches are set to be installed at additional cultural institutions. "Berlin’s cultural institutions stand for a diverse and vibrant urban society and are places of peaceful coexistence and exchange," said Oliver Reese, artistic director of the Berliner Ensemble, according to a press release. "We condemn these attacks on peaceful civil society and want to take a stand against the repeated destruction of this memorial site and anti-queer sentiment."
