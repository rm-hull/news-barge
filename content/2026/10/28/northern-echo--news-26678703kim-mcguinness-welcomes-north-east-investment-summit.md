North East Mayor Kim McGuinness has welcomed plans to bring a major international investment summit to the region.

She said the event would help attract investment and create jobs while putting the North East on the world stage.

The UK Investment Summit will be held in the region in spring 2027, following a request made by Ms McGuinness in a letter to the Chancellor last year.

It is expected to bring global investors and business leaders to the North East.

Ms McGuinness, who also chairs The Great North, said: "This is a confident, outward-looking North of England, with our mayors leading the way on the world stage.

"We believe in our people, our businesses and our communities, and together we are taking that belief to the world.

"We made the case for bringing the UK Investment Summit to the North because we know what we have to offer."

She said the announcement reflects what can be achieved through collective action and government support.

Ms McGuinness said: "These plans show what we can achieve when we come together, backed by a Government that understands our strengths and is investing in our potential."

The summit follows a recent trade mission to India led by The Great North, and is seen as another step towards strengthening international partnerships, boosting tourism, and creating jobs across the region.

Ms McGuinness said: "On our recent mission to India, we took a shared Northern offer to one of the world’s fastest-growing economies.

"Bringing global investors here is the next opportunity to turn those international relationships into jobs, investment and lasting prosperity for our people, while driving growth for the whole of UK plc."

Alongside the summit, the government has announced £7 million in funding for a detailed feasibility study into hosting a future Olympic and Paralympic Games in the North of England.

Ms McGuinness said: "A Great North Olympics and Paralympics would take that ambition even further.
