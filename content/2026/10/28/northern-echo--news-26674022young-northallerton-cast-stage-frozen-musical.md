More than 40 young performers are preparing to bring the magic of Disney’s Frozen the Musical to the stage in North Yorkshire.

Crash Bang Wallop, an award-winning youth theatre company, will present eight performances at The Forum in Northallerton during February half-term, running from February 9 to February 14, 2027.

Dan Brookes, founder and director of Crash Bang Wallop, said: "We are absolutely thrilled to be bringing Disney’s Frozen the Musical to North Yorkshire and are incredibly proud to be one of the first community theatre companies in the area to stage it.

"All our team are volunteers who give up their own time week after week to share their knowledge and passion for performing with young people.

"Already, the work both the leaders and cast members are putting into rehearsals is fantastic to see and audiences are in for an incredible show.

"It’s such a fun and uplifting story with a powerful message of family, friendship and staying true to who you are."

The cast is made up of 43 young people aged 10 to 18 and the production promises the same high standards that Crash Bang Wallop is known for.

Audiences can expect to hear classic songs from the film like Let It Go and Do You Want to Build a Snowman, as well as new numbers written especially for the stage adaptation.

The youth theatre is a charity run entirely by volunteers and aims to provide inclusive, affordable and professional training for anyone who loves to sing, dance and act.

Alice Carr-Smith, musical director, said: "Frozen is a true theatrical spectacle so audiences can expect stunning musical numbers, fantastic performances and plenty of magic.

"It’s a show that children, parents and grandparents will enjoy together.

"With the performances taking place during February half-term and it being one of the most loved Disney stories of recent times, we expect tickets to be in high demand, so I’d encourage people to book as soon as possible to avoid missing out."

Tickets are now on sale through The Forum Northallerton website at forumnorthallerton.org.uk.

With unforgettable songs, beloved characters and a cast of talented young performers, Frozen the Musical promises to be a highlight of the February half-term for families.
