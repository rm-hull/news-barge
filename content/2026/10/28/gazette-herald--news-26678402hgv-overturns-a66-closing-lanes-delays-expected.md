An overturned HGV has closed a major road in North Yorkshire with grain spilling across the carriageway.

National Highways said the A66 is closed eastbound between the A67 at Bowes and the A1(M) Junction 53 at Scotch Corner, with delays expected.

The accident happened shortly before 5am this morning and an operation to clean up the spilled grain began.




A spokesperson for the government-owned company said the HGV had been recovered.

The central reservation barrier was damaged, and National Highways said emergency repairs must take place before the eastbound carriageway can reopen.

On the westbound carriageway, lane two of two is closed because of the barrier damage.

A National Highways spokesperson said road users travelling eastbound are advised to leave the A66 at Bowes, adding that the diversion route is not suitable for heavy goods vehicles.
