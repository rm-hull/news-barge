Emmerdale actor Kevin Mathurin has revealed he suffered a heart attack and heart failure after ignoring warning signs for a week during a period of intense stress.

The television star shared details of his health scare in a social media post marking the first anniversary of the incident, describing how anxiety, grief and work pressures had taken a toll on his wellbeing.

## "I was under a lot of stress and pressure"

Mr Mathurin said the ordeal began in mid-October 2025, following a difficult period in his personal life.

He wrote: "I was under a lot of stress and pressure. External issues were taking over my well being. I was anxious, stressed, always angry and suppressing it all. Not dealing with things head on."

The actor also revealed he was grieving the loss of a close friend to cancer and had been unable to attend the funeral because of work commitments.

He said: "I'd also lost a really good friend to Cancer and was upset that I couldn't make his funeral due to work commitments. It was all too much that week."

## Symptoms ignored for days

Despite experiencing physical symptoms, Mr Mathurin said he continued with his normal routine.

"I felt pains in my ribs and arms all week. But knowing me, I didn't complain, I just carried on filming, working out and driving up and down the country to be with my family," he said.

The actor eventually sought medical advice after being encouraged to book an ECG scan.

He said: "By the time I got home from the hospital my doctor called me and said get straight to A&E."

## Heart attack and heart failure diagnosis

Doctors later discovered a blocked artery that had caused damage to part of his heart.

Mr Mathurin wrote: "Long story short, I had a blocked artery that had damaged part of my heart, making my heartbeat abnormal, causing heart failure and a minor heart attack.

"Within myself I felt fine, but this freaked me and my family out that I didn't pay attention to my body for a whole week."

## Time in hospital

The actor spent time at King's Hospital in London, where he underwent further tests to assess the severity of the condition.

He said: "While staying at King's Hospital, London I was kept under monitor and had an angiogram to determine how serious the blockages were...thankfully I didn't need surgery for stents, just relying on a lot of meds, exercise and a healthy diet to keep my blood pressure, heart rate and cholesterol down."

## A life-changing experience

Mr Mathurin said the experience gave him time to reassess what was most important in his life.

"But while I was in hospital that week I had a lot of time to think, reset, reevaluate my priorities and how I got to this point in my life in the first place," he wrote. "It kick-started a total reboot on my outlook on people, places and things."

The actor said spending time with his family had become a greater priority.

He said: "I want to see my children grow up into wonderful adults and be in a position to guide them when they need me, want to spend quality time with Nikki and be present when I'm with her and my family."

## Praise for NHS staff

Mr Mathurin ended the post by thanking the medical staff who cared for him during his recovery.

"A huge thank you to all the doctors and nurses at King's Hospital and the cardiac physiotherapists at Lewisham Hospital. THANK YOU NHS."

He also shared a message encouraging others to prioritise their wellbeing.

"Fear makes you ill. Shuts you down and stand still. Love your people, love life and keep moving."
