CHANGES could be coming to a York board game venue under plans lodged with the council.

Up A Level York, in Feasegate, would be licensed to serve alcohol from 11am to 22pm every day if City of York Council approves the venue's application.

Operator Rockets Hideout Ltd stated the venue focused on board games and offered a community leisure space.

The application is open for public comments until Monday, November 23.

It comes after the current operators took over the venue in September after its previous owners announced they were selling it.

The previous owners said the decision to sell the space was a difficult one.

They said on Facebook: "This place has been so much more than a business - it’s been a community, a home for gamers, and a space filled with laughter, competition, and connection."

Lee Glenn, of Rockets Hideout, said following the sale they wanted to build on what people already loved about the lounge.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21640575.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Up A Level board game venue *(Image: LDRS)*

The new owner said: "Over the coming months we'll be investing in the venue, introducing some new ideas and events, expanding parts of the gaming and retail offering, and most importantly listening to the people who already call Up A Level their gaming home.

"You'll still see plenty that's familiar, alongside some exciting new additions.

"We're incredibly excited about what we can build together and can't wait to meet more of you."
