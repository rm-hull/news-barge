HISTORIC buses, trains, and transport heritage will take centre stage in Keighley.

On Sunday, November 1, Keighley will host a special after-dark transport event bringing together the Keighley Bus Museum Trust, West Yorkshire Transport Heritage Trust (WYTHT), and the Keighley & Worth Valley Railway (KWVR) for a night-time celebration of the region’s transit heritage.

Centred around the Keighley Bus Museum’s popular Twilight Running Day, the event will see all three sites open to the public with after-dark activities and displays.

A spokesperson for the event said: "There’s a unique magic to travelling on a historic bus or train after dark.

"Uniting Keighley Bus Museum, the West Yorkshire Transport Heritage Trust, and the Keighley & Worth Valley Railway allows us to offer a truly immersive, community-wide celebration of West Yorkshire's rich transport legacy."

The Keighley Bus Museum on Dalton Lane will showcase its collection of vintage double-deckers, single-deckers, and commercial vehicles, with special evening bus runs planned to give visitors the chance to experience classic buses after dark.

The West Yorkshire Transport Heritage Trust site on Parson Street will be open to visitors, offering a look at ongoing restoration projects and preserved vehicles.

At Ingrow Station, the Rail Story site will link heritage rail with vintage road transport, creating a nostalgic atmosphere filled with the sights, sounds, and smells of the past.

A free vintage shuttle bus service will connect all three locations throughout the event, allowing visitors to easily travel between the Keighley Bus Museum, WYTHT, Ingrow Station, and Keighley town centre.

The event runs from 11am to 5pm, with twilight activities beginning in the late afternoon.

Entry to the Keighley Bus Museum is free, though donations are welcome.

Standard fares will apply for KWVR train services.
