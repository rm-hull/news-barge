A woman paedophile whose sexual abuse of a boy led to him repeatedly trying to kill himself has been jailed for nearly 10 years.

Tracy Bancroft, now 64, had sex several times with the teenager when he was underage and didn't want it, said Richard Walters, prosecuting at York Crown Court.

Before she initiated sex on the first occasion, she gave him a rum mixed with juice that he said was “very strong”.

“He had no sexual knowledge and didn’t really understand what was happening to him as he was so young,” said the prosecutor.

“He was frozen and remained in shock for a few days.”

The boy's mother told the court: “This woman has destroyed my family. No-one deserves to feel or live like this, but unfortunately the damage is done.

“I have lost count of the number of times I have had to stop him killing himself.”

Bancroft had shown “absolutely no remorse”, she said.

Mr Walters said the boy kept silent about what Bancroft had done to him for many years and only started to talk about the abuse after police were called out to deal with one of his suicide bids.

It took another year before he was prepared to make a police statement.

His mother said in a personal statement: “He didn’t know how to deal with the horror he had been put through.”

His schoolwork had suffered and he found it difficult to hold down a job for a long period of time.

She said her son would “drink to oblivion”, so when he was out in the evening, she couldn’t sleep and would wait for him to ring at 3am, after which his family would have to tour the streets late at night looking for him and bringing him home.

Mark Foley, defending, said the abuse happened at a time when Bancroft was in an “unhappy and loveless” relationship with an adult male and was drinking heavily.

She had tried to kill herself the day before she was due to stand trial in September 2025.

Mr Foley said: “It could be seen as a cowardly act but it was an act expressing deep shame, her disgust, her regret and her remorse.”

The trial was relisted for September this year and on September 4, Bancroft, of Sycamore Avenue, Filey, pleaded guilty to some of the charges and others were left on file meaning they will appear on her record but not as convictions.

Judge Simon Hickey said there had clearly been grooming and the sexual encounters were planned.

He jailed her for nine years and four months and made her subject to a 15-year sexual harm prevention order banning her from contacting the boy or posting anything about him on social media and banning her from having unsupervised contact with any boy.

She will be on the sex offenders’ register and barred from working with children or vulnerable adults, both for life.

Mr Foley said the suicide bid had caused her to have a stroke.

“She presents as an elderly, vulnerable lady,” he said. “She doesn’t represent a reoffending risk to the public. “

She needed a Zimmer frame to get around and her lack of mobility meant that she was effectively housebound and a recluse, said Mr Foley.
