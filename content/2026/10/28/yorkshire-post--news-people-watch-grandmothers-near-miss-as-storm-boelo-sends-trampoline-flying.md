# Watch grandmother's near miss as Storm Boelo sends trampoline flying over garden wall

## This is the moment a grandmother narrowly escaped being flattened by a trampoline as Storm Boleo sent it tumbling over a garden wall.

Louise Lee, 53, was at her daughter’s home in Redcar on Monday when powerful winds lifted her grandchildren’s trampoline off the ground.

The trampoline flipped over the wall and landed around Louise in the adjoining garden, before another gust sent it tumbling onto a neighbour’s car, damaging the roof.

Louise said she was left "shocked and shaking" by the frightening near miss, having had no idea the winds would be so strong.
