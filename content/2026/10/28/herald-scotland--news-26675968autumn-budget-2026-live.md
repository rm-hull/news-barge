* John Healey delivers his first Budget at about 12.30pm
* Chancellor promises “breathing space for today and fresh hope for the future”
* Gas standing charge to be scrapped
* Other help with energy bills expected, including extending the VAT cut on electricity
* Fuel duty freeze likely
* Tariffs on a range of foods to be cut
* Carers’ allowance cliff edge to be replaced with a taper
* Minimum wage expected to rise by nearly 4%
* Tax raid on banks tipped as the main revenue raiser, with fiscal headroom squeezed by the Iran war
* Defence spending boosted, with a £4.7 billion hole in the investment plan to be filled
