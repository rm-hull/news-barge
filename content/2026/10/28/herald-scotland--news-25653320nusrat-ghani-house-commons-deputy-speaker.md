Nusrat Ghani, the House of Commons’ senior Deputy Speaker, will take charge of the Commons during the UK Budget 2026.

Ghani, elected to the role in 2024, is responsible for maintaining order, calling MPs and ensuring the debate runs fairly.

Who is Nusrat Ghani, the House of Commons deputy speaker? Here is everything you need to know.

## Who is Nusrat Ghani - MP for Sussex Weald and Deputy Speaker?

Nusrat Ghani is the Conservative MP for Sussex Weald and, since 2024, the Chairman of Ways and Means - the most senior Deputy Speaker of the House of Commons.

First elected in 2015, she has held several ministerial roles, including in transport, industry and economic security.

She is also the first Muslim woman to serve as a Deputy Speaker, a historic milestone in UK parliamentary representation.

## What does the Chairman of Ways and Means do?

As Chairman of Ways and Means, Ghani presides over Commons debates when the Speaker is absent and oversees the Committee of the Whole House.

She also chairs the Panel of Chairs, who run bill committees and secondary debates. The role requires strict impartiality, meaning she no longer votes on legislation she chairs.

## Why does the Deputy Speaker, not the Speaker, chair the Budget debate?

Tradition, namely long-standing parliamentary convention, is the reason the Budget debate is chaired by the Chairman of Ways and Means rather than the Speaker, as per the UK Parliament website.

As Deputy Speaker, Ghani is responsible for maintaining order during the Budget statement and ensuring MPs are called fairly. Her role helps protect neutrality and uphold longstanding parliamentary rules.
