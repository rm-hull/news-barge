A North East taxi driver who helped a woman in crisis has been honoured for his "extraordinary kindness".

Newcastle driver Lamin Saho won the North East Acorn Taxi Hero Award after helping a vulnerable passenger who feared returning home.

He stayed with the woman, reassured her and charged her phone so she could contact her teenage son.

Mr Saho said: "I’m incredibly proud to have been named the North East Taxi Hero.

"As a taxi driver, you meet all sorts of people, and sometimes you realise that someone needs a bit more than just a lift from one place to another.

"I’ve always tried to make people feel comfortable and safe in my taxi, and on that particular night I just wanted to make sure my passenger was safe and away from harm’s way.

"I never expected any recognition for it, so this award means a great deal.

"It’s wonderful to see the work taxi drivers do being celebrated, and I’m looking forward to finding out who will be crowned Britain’s Taxi Hero."

The passenger felt safe enough to go home after speaking to her son, while Mr Saho contacted emergency services because he feared for her safety.

Nominators said he “went far beyond his responsibilities as a taxi driver” to keep the woman safe.

Ash Coakley, head of taxi insurance at Acorn Group, said: "Taxi drivers encounter people from every walk of life, often at moments when a little kindness, understanding or reassurance can make an enormous difference.

"Lamin’s story is particularly moving.

"Faced with a passenger in genuine distress, he recognised that she needed more than a journey home.

"He showed patience, compassion and real concern for her safety, taking the time to help her through a very difficult situation.

"It’s an extraordinary example of how taxi drivers look out for the people in their communities, often without expecting anything in return.

"Lamin is hugely deserving of the award, and we’re delighted to recognise what he did."

Acorn Insurance launched the Taxi Hero Awards to honour drivers who show bravery, kindness and dedication to passengers and their communities.

Mr Saho was chosen from more than 1,700 nominees across the UK.

He will receive a £100 Amazon gift card and is in the running for the national title, £1,000 and the inaugural Britain’s Taxi Hero trophy.

The national winner will be announced on November 11. A panel from the taxi and insurance industries judges the awards.
