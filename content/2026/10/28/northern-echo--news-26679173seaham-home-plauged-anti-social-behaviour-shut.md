A County Durham home branded a "hive of anti-social behaviour" has been ordered shut for three months.

A court ordered the home on Marlborough in Seaham be closed after persistent complaints from residents,

Seaham police said the address had become a hub for anti-social behaviour, leaving neighbours facing repeated disruption and distress.

The order means the property must be closed and secured for the next three months.

Anyone who enters or stays inside without the court’s permission commits a criminal offence and could be arrested.

A police spokesperson said: "We understand the impact that ongoing anti-social behaviour can have on a community, and we appreciate those who continued to report incidents, provide information and support our enquiries throughout the process.
