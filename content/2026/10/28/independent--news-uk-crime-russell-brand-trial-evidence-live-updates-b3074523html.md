Russell Brand has told his trial he has no defence witnesses because no one will “touch him with a barge pole” after allegations against him emerged.

The comedian is giving evidence for the first time over sexual assault and rape allegations at Southwark Crown Court on Wednesday.

Brand told the court he had suffered “devastating” consequences to being accused of rape and sexual misconduct by the *Sunday Times*,* Times*, and Channel 4* Dispatches* in September 2023.

His barrister, Ian Winter KC, asked: “Were you able to persuade a single person with relevant evidence to give to come to give evidence on your behalf?”

Brand replied: “I have not.”

The barrister continued: “Would they touch you with a barge pole?”, and Brand replied: “No.”

Brand denies three counts of rape, three counts of sexual assault, and a charge of attempted indecent assault. The trial continues.

## Brand taking long pauses after some prosecution questions

Ms Karmy-Jones asked Brand: “Did you tell Mr Pinheiro you weren’t prepared to leave a situation with a woman without some form of conquest?”

Brand replied: “I don’t remember.”

At times during Brand’s cross-examination, he went entirely silent and stared back at Ms Karmy-Jones for several seconds after she had posed a question.

When asked if it was untrue that he had been “completely abandoned” after first being accused, Brand stayed quiet and Ms Karmy-Jones asked if it is “a difficult question”.

Eventually he responded: “I’m not sure I entirely understand it.”

## Consent 'absolutely non-negotiable', Brand tells court

Russell Brand said that consent is “non-negotiable”.

The prosecutor asked him: “You said that consent was fundamentally necessary and in many ways more important than sex itself, that wasn’t what you thought between 1999 and 2009 was it?”

Brand said: “Consent is absolutely non-negotiable.”

## Brand denies 'goal' of sleeping with 5,000 women

Brand denied having a “goal” of sleeping with 5,000 women.

Prosecutor Ms Karmy-Jones asked: “Is it a goal you may have had?”

Brand replied: “I don’t agree, I don’t recall the conversation you are referring to.”

One of Brand’s former friends, Mark Pinheiro, told the court about the comment being made in 1999.

Ms Karmy-Jones asked: “Have you set yourself a goal of sleeping with 5,000 women?

Brand replied: “No, not a goal.”

## Prosecution begins cross-examination of Brand

Beginning her cross-examination of Russell Brand, prosecutor Riel Karmy-Jones KC, she began by asking: “You blame Dispatches, don’t you?”

After a period of silence, she asked the question again, to which he said: “What for, may I ask?”

When told the prosecutor meant the allegations, Brand said: “Yes.”

Ms Karmy-Jones continued: “You blame the media for these allegations, don’t you?”

After another period of silence, she added: “Is that a difficult question, Mr Brand?”

He responded: “In some ways.”

## Brand says he is innocent as defence finishes questions

Concluding his questions for the defendant, Mr Winter said: “Did you sexually assault her that day?”

“No,” Brand replied.

The defence barrister continued: “Are you guilty or innocent of these allegations?”

“I’m innocent,” he said.

Questioned on whether he was guilty or innocent or the allegations made on the indictment, he said: “I’m innocent.”

## Brand denies accuser told him to stop during sex

Brand said the fan accusing him of raping her at his home in 2009 had not said “no” or told him to stop as they were having sex.

“Did she tell you to stop, or that she didn’t want to proceed in that way?”, said Mr Winter.

Brand replied: “No.”

He told jurors he had not forced her into anal sex, he described her attitude as “coquettishness, coyness”, and he said she had never complained of pain.

Mr Winter asked: “Did she say anything to you?”, and Brand replied “no”, before adding that she had not asked him to stop.

Despite being asked to keep his voice up, Brand soon slipped back into a quieter voice.

Brand told the jury he did not remember what happened immediately after he and the woman had sex, but said watching the TV and “hanging out… sounds right”.

## Brand says he saved accuser's name in his phone as 'T for tart'

Brand saved one of his accuser’s names in his phone along with the letter “T” which stood for “tart”, he told the jury.

He also saved her name with the letters “AP” which stood for “anal play”, he added, following a consensual threesome with her in his dressing room.

He denies anally raping the fan at his home Hampstead, north London, during another sexual encounter in 2009.

## Brand says he does not remember 'picking woman out' of gig audience

Russell Brand said he would “pick out people” at gigs and invite them to the bar.

Questioned about a woman he is accused of raping after meeting her at one of his Hammersmith Apollo stand-up performances, he said he was unable to remember her or the events when interviewed by police.

Brand said he has subsequently been able to rejog his memory of meeting her.

He was asked if he remembered picking her out to go to the bar upstairs, to which Brand said: “I do not.”

Ian Winter KC asked him: “Did you however pick out people in order to invite them to the bar area.”

Brand said: “Yes.”

## I don't remember pinning radio station worker to wall, Brand tells court

Turning to the allegation that he pinned a radio worker to a wall and sexually assaulted her in front of members of his entourage, Brand said he could not remember the incident.

Mr Winter asked if he had “pushed her against a wall, groped her, kissed her sloppily, and behaved in that manner towards her”, and Brand replied: “No.”

He told jurors he sometimes visited the Capital Radio studios, and if there with an entourage it was likely to have included his writing partner Matt Morgan, his agent Nik Linnen, and two hair and make-up artists.
