**A specialist boat will be used to continue searches for two missing men and a missing fishing vessel off the coast of the Isle of Lewis.**

Northern Lighthouse Board vessel NLV Pharos will be deployed to the area from Friday, October 30, 2026 as part of the Marine Accident Investigation Branch's investigation.

Norman Murray, 40, who is known as Tam, and Murdo Morrison, 23, remain unaccounted for and are being treated as missing people.

They are part of the crew of the missing fishing boat Kilda, last known to have been operating close to the Port of Ness in the north of the island on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

The body of Douglas Craigie, who was also part of the crew, was recovered in the area around 2.45am on Wednesday, October 14.

Inspector Neil Taylor said: "Our thoughts remain with everyone affected by this tragic incident.

"We will continue to work with partners, including the MAIB, Maritime and Coastguard Agency and local maritime experts, and are extremely grateful for partners' resources, which are dedicated to this operation.

"It is imperative we get answers for everyone involved and please be assured our efforts are continuing.

"We would urge people not to put themselves at any risk.

"Please do not attempt to carry out searches in areas which put you at risk of harm."

The families continue to ask for their privacy to be respected.

The Marine Accident Investigation Branch (MAIB) previously put an exclusion zone in place for divers in an area between the Butt of Lewis and Port of Ness.

If you have any concerns, or information, please call 101 quoting reference 3575 of 13 October.
