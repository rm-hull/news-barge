Estate agents have revealed their pick for the best neighbourhood in Scotland's capital - with Stockbridge heading their top ten.

The Edinburgh Solicitors Property Centre (ESPC) rated all of the city's districts against a range of criteria, and decided that the upmarket area was their pick of the bunch.

ESPC ranked the capital's districts using a range of measures including property prices, affordability, transport links, access to green space, schools, amenities, community spirit and crime rates.

The Index is an annual assessment of Edinburgh's most desirable places to live, using a scoring system to identify the city's top 10 neighbourhoods and highlight what makes them attractive to homebuyers.

Joining Stockbridge in the top 10 were a diverse range of communities across the capital, including Portobello, Morningside, Leith and Currie, offering options for buyers at every stage of life and budget.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/17840456.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinburgh's neighbourhoods were rated by ESPC *(Image: Getty Images)*

According to the rankings, Stockbridge achieved top marks for transport and walkability, green space, local amenities, safety and community spirit.

Leith emerged as the most affordable neighbourhood in the top 10, with an average selling price of £261,024, around £46,000 below Edinburgh's average of £307,347.

At the other end of the scale, Colinton and Fairmilehead were the most expensive areas featured in the rankings, with an average selling price of £432,514.

Paul Hilton, CEO of ESPC, said: “After 55 years of helping people buy and sell homes, at ESPC we know that when it comes to finding a home, location really is everything.

“Every day, we see where buyers are most keen to live, and our solicitor estate agent member firms know first-hand what makes these neighbourhoods so special.

“Our Index brings that insight together with the latest ESPC property price data, so it reflects not just what makes an area sought-after but how affordable it is.

“Stockbridge is a very worthy winner, and I’m already looking forward to seeing which area takes the top spot next year.”
