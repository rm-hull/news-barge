A teenager was caught with a machete and a baton when he was pulled over by police for dangerous driving.

Kian Casey was arrested while driving his Skoda Octavia through Gainford on the A67 on September 28.

The 19-year-old admitted possessing the machete and baton at Teesside Crown Court.

Casey, of Staithes Lane, Staithes, also admitted dangerous driving and driving without insurance at an earlier hearing.

The teenager pleaded guilty to the weapons charges and a pre-sentence report was ordered.

Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, said: “You have pleaded guilty to two offences of possession of weapons.
