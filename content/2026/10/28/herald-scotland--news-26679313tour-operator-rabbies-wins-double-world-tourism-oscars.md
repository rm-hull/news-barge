An Edinburgh-based small-group tour operator has been voted leading tour operator at the World Travel Awards.

Rabbies, which offers trips across the UK, Ireland and Europe, has been recognised in two categories at the World Travel Awards 2026, winning Europe’s Leading Group Tour Operator and Scotland’s Leading Tour Operator.

The wins come in the company’s very first year of nomination at the awards.

Established in 1993, the World Travel Awards celebrate excellence across global travel, tourism and hospitality, with winners decided by votes from travel industry professionals and the travelling public.

The double win marks another milestone for Rabbie’s, following the company being awarded the King’s Award for Enterprise for International Trade in 2025, recognising its extensive growth in overseas markets. Rabbie’s also holds a B Corp Certification and has been named Best Travel Company for Escorted Group Holidays at the British Travel Awards.

Off the back of these wins, Rabbie's has now been nominated for "World's Leading Group Tour Operator" 2026, putting the company on the global stage alongside some of the world’s best travel companies.

Hazel Rickett, CEO at Rabbie’s, said of the awards: “To win not one but two World Travel Awards in our first year of being nominated is something I’m incredibly proud of. These awards are voted for by the people who travel with us and work alongside us, so they mean a great deal to the whole team.

“Our guides and the team behind the scenes work so hard to give every customer a brilliant experience, and this recognition belongs to them.

"Being named Scotland’s Leading Tour Operator is particularly special, because Scotland is where Rabbie’s started and it will always be home.”
