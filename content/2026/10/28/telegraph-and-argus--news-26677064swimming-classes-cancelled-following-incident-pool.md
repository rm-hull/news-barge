Several Bradford swimming classes were cancelled following an "incident in the pool".

Bowling Pool and Gym, on Flockton Road, informed customers via its Facebook page.

The cancelled sessions included adult lane swim from 6.30am to 8.30am and a Swim for All from 9am to 10am this morning.

The centre, based in East Bowling, apologised to customers for any inconvenience caused.

![Bowling Pool could receive funding for its facilities](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20554007.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bowling Pool *(Image: Newsquest)*

## Pool reopens

A later update on Tuesday confirmed the pool had reopened.

The centre said: "The pool is back open from 12pm for the ladies-only steady swim."

As part of the post, customers were thanked for their patience while the facility dealt with the incident.

## Details remain unclear

The nature of the incident has not yet been disclosed.

The *Telegraph & Argus* has contacted Bradford Council for further information.

## Popular community facility

Bowling Pool and Gym is a council-run leisure facility on Flockton Road, Bradford.

The site includes a 25-metre swimming pool, gym, sauna and accessible changing facilities.

The pool is designed to be accessible for users with impaired mobility, featuring ramp access and a pool lift platform.
