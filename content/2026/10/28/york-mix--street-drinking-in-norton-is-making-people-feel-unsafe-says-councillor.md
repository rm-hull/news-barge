A conservative councillor is calling for tougher action to tackle antisocial street drinking in and around Commercial Street in Norton, following growing concerns from residents and businesses.

Cllr Keane Duncan says he needs the help of residents to push for a Public Spaces Protection Order (PSPO).

They have been reporting issues throughout the day now, with one local woman saying she feels “terrified” as a result.

Cllr Duncan is now seeking the introduction of a PSPO which would give police officers extra powers to tackle alcohol-related antisocial behaviour within a designated area.

To support the case for its introduction in Norton, he is gathering evidence from residents and businesses.

Cllr Duncan said: “Nobody should feel unsafe in Norton. Yet I’m hearing increasingly worrying accounts from residents, particularly women and girls, who feel frightened walking along Commercial Street.

“That’s why I want to see a tougher approach to street drinking, with police given the maximum possible powers to address this growing problem.

“A PSPO would not ban responsible drinking in public, but it would allow police to intervene when alcohol is contributing to nuisance or disorder. Officers can require individuals to stop drinking and surrender their alcohol. Refusing to comply could result in fixed penalty notices or prosecution, with a maximum fine of £1,000.

“But to make the case for these additional powers, I need residents to tell me their experiences. Your evidence will be shared with the council and could make a real difference.”
