A children’s charity is marking 10 years of partnership with a domestic abuse support service.

The Principle Trust has worked with Independent Domestic Abuse Services (IDAS) for a decade to support families across Yorkshire by providing free respite breaks to those who have experienced trauma.

Since 2021, the trust has helped families referred by IDAS teams in Harrogate, York, and Scarborough.

Laura Sykes, service delivery manager at The Principle Trust, said: "For families affected by domestic abuse, a holiday can mean so much more than a few days away.

"It provides an opportunity to step away from everyday pressures, spend positive time together and create happy memories.

"We're proud to work alongside IDAS to help families access these opportunities as they begin to rebuild their lives."

IDAS is Yorkshire’s largest specialist domestic abuse charity, offering refuge accommodation, safety planning and community services for children and young people.

During the 2026 season, 64 per cent of families referred to The Principle Trust were experiencing financial hardship or living on a low income, 45 per cent were living in refuge or safe accommodation following domestic abuse, and a further 45 per cent had additional needs, disabilities or neurodivergence identified within the family.
