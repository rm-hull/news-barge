WEST Yorkshire is among the highest spenders on NHS prescriptions.

A new study by Cured Pharmacy found that the West Yorkshire Integrated Care Board (ICB) spends £292.42 per person on prescriptions, the third-highest rate in England.

The area recorded the largest overall prescription spend in the country at £791,785,012 and dispensed 35.5 items per person—the highest volume nationally.

Tarun Kumar, co-founder and superintendent pharmacist of Cured Pharmacy, said: "What stands out is the enormous variation across England.

"Cambridgeshire and Peterborough spends more than three times per person what North West London does, and that gap is largely driven by the cost of individual prescriptions rather than the volume.

"This could reflect differences in the types of medications being prescribed, such as higher proportions of specialist or newer treatments in certain areas."

Cambridgeshire and Peterborough topped the list at £379.82 per person, while Herefordshire and Worcestershire ranked second at £347.81.

By contrast, North West London recorded the lowest spend at just £115.74 per person.

Integrated Care Boards are NHS organisations responsible for planning health services for their local population.

The figures are based on prescription data from the NHS Business Services Authority and population data from NHS England.
