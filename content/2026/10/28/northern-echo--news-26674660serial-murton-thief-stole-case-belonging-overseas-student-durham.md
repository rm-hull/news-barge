Persistent shoplifter Bailey DiCaprio is back behind bars after breaching a suspended sentence within months of it being imposed.

Bailey DiCaprio was arrested and remanded in custody after being identified as the suspect in thefts in Durham, in September.

The 29-year-old, of Calvert Terrace, Murton, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court on Friday, October 16, and admitted two charges of theft.

They included stealing £120-worth of power banks from Sainsbury’s in Durham on September 21 and theft by finding of a large suitcase containing clothes and an Alienware PC monitor, worth £3,200, belonging to an overseas student in the city, on September 27.

![Police-issue offender mugshot](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21637543.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Prolific thief Bailey DiCaprio's suspended eight-month prison sentence, imposed in early July, was activated in full by magistrates at Newton Aycliffe, earlier this month, for further theft offences committed in Durham in September

Those offences triggered a suspended sentence order imposed at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court, on July 3 this year.

DiCaprio was given an eight-month prison sentence, suspended for 12 months, and ordered to pay £774 compensation to Aldi and Sainsbury’s in Gilesgate, Durham, after striking on May 24 and June 2.

The Aldi offence was an overnight break-in, while the Sainsbury’s offence was a more conventional shop theft.

His eight-month suspended sentence from July was, therefore, activated in full.

Commenting on social media, the officers who have had to deal with DiCaprio’s offending over the years in that part of Durham, said: “This is another positive result for the Sherburn Neighbourhood Policing Team.

“It demonstrates our continued commitment to holding offenders accountable for their actions and protecting our communities."

DiCaprio was previously sentenced to 27 months in prison in July 2020, after admitting the 2018 robbery of a homeless man, in Durham, which a phone and tobacco were taken.
