President Donald Trump and former president Barack Obama continue to push their feud and today both have rallies in different parts of the nation.

The two rallies come after their feud ignited when Obama ripped Trump’s White House renovations.

“He’s got the White House looking like Saddam Hussein opened a Cheesecake Factory,” Obama opined during a recent Ohio rally.

Rubio will be attending “in his capacity as national security adviser,” the official said.

## Federal Reserve leaves interest rate unchanged ahead of the midterms

The Federal Reserve has kept its interest rate unchanged, making the decision days before the midterm elections.

The rate will remain around 3.9 percent as inflation continues to hammer everyday Americans. President Donald Trump has long pushed the board to lower interest rates and has demanded countless times that they make the move.

Wednesday's decision came with little information about The fed’s next move, according to the Associated Press.

“Inflation remains elevated,” the Fed said in a statement. Holding off on an increase will allow “the accumulation of more information” that will help determine what policy the Fed should pursue “to ensure a timely return of inflation” to the central bank’s 2% inflation target, the statement said.

## What is Trump's approval rating?

President Donald Trump’s approval continues to sit below 40 percent, but he saw a slight uptick today.

According to Nate Silver’s poll tracker, Trump had an approval rating of 37.4 on Wednesday, up slightly from his 37 percent on Tuesday. His disapproval on Wednesday sat at 60.2 percent.

Silver’s tracker uses an average of many different polls to create a cumulative results.

Former president Barack Obama is set to campaign in Georgia today as he makes a final push for voters to choose Democrats before Tuesday’s midterm elections.

## Trump has robbed victims of $119M in court-ordered restitution with his pardons of allies

Donald Trump’s pardon wave has erased more than $119 million in restitution owed to crime victims following a historic streak of pardons for white-collar criminals and political allies accused of fraud and corruption.

The list of fines, compiled by CBS News, does not include nearly $120 million that was owed to the Crime Victims Fund and another $171 million in restitution that is jointly owed with co-defendants who did not receive clemency, bringing the total funds that were denied to victims and the government to more than $1.1 billion over the last two years.

Those fines also don’t include $661 million in restitution owed by the former tech CEO who was convicted of defrauding investors, nor the potentially tens of thousands of dollars that would have likely been owed to the government by the more than 1,500 people charged in connection with the January 6 attack. The president issued rioters a blanket pardon on his first day in office.

## Republicans hit the midterm campaign trail

Several high-profile midterm campaign rallies are planned for Wednesday — as Election Day is less than a week away.

President Donald Trump is scheduled to travel to Ohio, where the race for Senate and governor appear to be close.

In the Senate, incumbent Republican Jon Husted is facing a challenge from Democratic former Sen. Sherrod Brown. Trump has made headlines while campaigning for Husted, whose athletic body he has admired even while campaigning for candidates in other states.

In the governor’s race, Republican Vivek Ramaswamy is competing against Democrat Amy Action.

This is Trump’s third trip to Ohio this month.

Meanwhile, Vice President JD Vance will go to North Carolina to campaign for Michael Whatley, who faces a competitive Senate race against Democratic challenger Roy Cooper.
