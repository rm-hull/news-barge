# Wetherby-based Touch Telecom ranked in UK Top 200 fastest-growing businesses

## Wetherby-based Touch Telecom reaches growth milestone

Wetherby-based Touch Telecom has been ranked in the UK Top 200 of the 2026 UK Fast Growth Index, after growing its turnover by 239 per cent in two years.

![Touch Telecom has been named on the 2026 UK Fast Growth Index with a Top 50 place in the North of England](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/ugc/2026/10/28/14/touch-telecom-uk-fast-growth-index-2026-recognised-linkedin_1793195358_11979d6b-67f0c4eb38005e90.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=940&quality=65)


It is also ranked among the fastest-growing businesses in the North of England. It was named North's fastest-growing business communications provider.

Angus Taylor, Director of Touch Telecom, said: "Being featured in the UK Fast Growth Index is a massive milestone for Touch Telecom and to be ranked 14th in the North of England is amazing.

![Touch Telecom Accounts and Admin Office](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/ugc/2026/10/28/14/532114833_18095077075713911_3370141157350580618_n_1793195359_c773bd82-51152aad71df5ab4.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"We founded the business to build a company rooted in local trust, in-house technical expertise and real customer service.  
"Ranking among the fastest-growing firms in the UK proves that our "one point of contact" ethos resonates deeply and this recognition celebrates the dedication of our engineers and support teams who work tirelessly to keep our customers connected."

The 2026 UK Fast Growth Index reveals the 350 fastest-growing independent businesses across the UK's nations and regions.

Together, they have more than doubled in size in just two years, with combined turnover rising from £4.3 billion in 2023 to £10.2 billion in 2025, an increase of 137 per cent.

Professor Dylan Jones-Evans OBE, founder of the UK Fast Growth Index, said: "Touch Telecom is a great example of the extraordinary talent and ambition we see in businesses across every part of the UK.

![Touch Telecom Provisioning Centre](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/ugc/2026/10/28/14/572136617_18104180338713911_6018433572259029270_n__1__1793195359_2d91bdc2-380324a9970dc216.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"Earning a place on this year's Index, alongside firms that have together more than doubled in size in just two years, is a remarkable achievement, and the whole team should be very proud.

"At a time when we hear so much about the challenges facing the UK economy, businesses like Touch Telecom show what is possible when they are given the chance to grow.

"The challenge now is to help more of them become those incredible high-growth businesses that make a massive impact across the country."
