A new British haircare brand in York has launched a child-friendly hair dryer designed specifically for young children.

The AirHeads dryer aims to make hair drying gentler and less stressful for children, offering a gentler, more comfortable experience.

Weighing approximately 250g with its soft silicone styler attached, the dryer is lightweight and easy to manoeuvre around smaller heads, ears and necks.

It features soft silicone attachments instead of traditional hard plastic, including a patented diffuser for curly hair and a smoothing comb for straighter styles.

Holly Casling, one of the founders of AirHeads, said: "Hair drying is an everyday family ritual, yet the products we found were rarely designed with children in mind.

"AirHeads was created to make that experience gentler and more child-led, helping transform hair drying from a task families simply get through into a calmer, more comforting moment together."

Designed for use on children aged 12 months to 12 years with adult supervision, the product prioritises comfort and ease of use.

Rather than taking an adult hair dryer and making it smaller, AirHeads says it has "considered the experience from a child’s point of view – from the temperature and feel of the airflow to the weight of the dryer, its attachments and the way parents use it around little heads".

Key features include gentle heat settings, an LED temperature indicator, and a child-centred design focused on reducing discomfort.

The dryer is intended to make hair drying less overwhelming, especially for children with sensory sensitivities.

Every detail has been developed with the needs of children and parents in mind, turning an everyday task into a calmer experience.

The AirHeads hair dryer is available now at airheadshair.com and will launch in Boots and John Lewis stores at the end of September.
