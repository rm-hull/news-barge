Police are appealing for information after a trailer and approximately 50 bales of hay were stolen from a farm in North Yorkshire.

The theft took place in Camela Lane, Camblesforth in the early hours of yesterday morning (Tuesday, October 27).

North Yorkshire Police said the offenders cut through a gate and fencing to access the property before taking a caged Ifor Williams trailer with a loading ramp and the hay bales.




A police spokesperson said this incident forms part of a pattern of rural crime activity occurring in the area.

Officers are asking anyone who saw suspicious vehicles or people in the area during the early hours of October 27 to come forward.

They are also keen to hear from anyone with CCTV, doorbell, farmyard, or dashcam footage showing vehicle movements on nearby roads.

Anyone with information is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting incident 12260211188.
