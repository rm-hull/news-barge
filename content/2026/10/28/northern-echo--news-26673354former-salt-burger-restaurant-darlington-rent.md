A former swanky Darlington burger joint is looking for a new owner.

The building has now been advertised for rent by estate agents.

Salt built a reputation for its burgers, loaded fries and street food after launching for deliveries and collections in 2020. After Covid-19 restrictions eased, the venue opened fully as a restaurant and continued serving new specials alongside its regular favourites.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21477727.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The outside of Salt on Grange Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

In the listing from Carvers Commercial, the site is described as "an excellent opportunity to secure a prominent retail, restaurant or leisure premises in one of Darlington's most sought-after commercial locations."

The 1,178 sq ft, two-floor building, which is available for £17,000 a year, has also been described as 'prime town centre location' which could be used as a shop, restaurant or café.

The listing added: "Located within Darlington's popular Imperial Quarter, this versatile property benefits from strong footfall, excellent visibility and convenient access to nearby parking facilities. Ideal for ambitious businesses looking to establish or expand their presence in the town centre."

Last month, concern about the business grew after images showed chairs piled on tables, while several tables have been removed from the front of the restaurant.

Owners have not commented on the closure but have not posted on social media since June and have taken down their website.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21477693.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chairs and tables have been removed from Salt, Grange Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21477695.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A few remaining items in Salt, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Some customers said they visited between 5pm and 10pm but found it shut.

Others reacted with disappointment, crediting the restaurant as "the best burger ever" and saying "tell me it isn't so".

![The outside of Salt on Grange Road, Darlington](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21477729.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The outside of Salt on Grange Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

All days and time slots are blocked out on its online delivery service, while adding items to the basket produces an ‘invalid’ message.
