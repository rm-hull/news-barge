A Scots paedophile caught plotting to abuse two boys in a major cross-border police probe has been jailed for 10-and-a-half years.

Andrew McAdam was found to be in regular contact with a fellow sex offender eventually jailed for 20 years for child sex crimes.

This included McAdam while away near Loch Lomond “encouraging” the English-based pervert to abuse a boy he was with.

The 36 year-old first-offender was sentenced at the High Court in Glasgow.

McAdam, of Mayfield, Edinburgh, earlier pleaded to a total of nine charges including plotting with others to commit sexual abuse of two children and the rape of one of them.

Lord Scott told him: “ Some incidents go no further than the agreement to commit a crime before a change of mind or detection but this wicked conspiracy was made real.”

The hearing was told of an emotional statement completed by relatives of one of the youngsters involved prior to the sentencing.

In it, they said: “There is no eloquent way to summarise the emotional and psychological impact of what the perpetrator has done.

“It is life changing. It is a constant struggle. It is utterly devastating.”

McAdam was snared during a probe by Greater Manchester Police in November 2022.

He was linked to indecent photos found on the phone of another pervert.

McAdam's devices were later examined by Police Scotland and it emerged he had “engaged in protracted communications with several paedophiles” including “encouraging one another to sexually abuse children”.

The is also involved the sharing of sick footage.

One person he was in contact with was Connor Cashmore.

The pair discussed a young child.

They communicated via Instagram, Telegram and 1,442 messages on WhatsApp.

Many of the photos and video clips shared “self destructed” after being viewed.

Commenting on the sick content, McAdam stated at one stage: “Nothing is too much for me.”

![Glasgow High Court](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/13398872.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Andrew McAdam was sentenced at the High Court in Glasgow *(Image: Colin Mearns)*

Prosecutor Christopher Jones gave an account of the graphic descriptions of what they discussed.

McAdam told Cashmore: “You are honestly the best.”

This eventually lead the abuse of a boy. McAdam was in Scotland at the time while Cashmore was in England where the child was.

Mr Jones said: “Although McAdam was never present in the same room as (the boy), he participated in the sexual assault remotely by encouraging Cashmore while the abuse was going on.”

McAdam also preyed on another boy himself and recorded it on his phone. He shared footage with Cashmore.

Mr Jones said Cashmore, then 27, pleaded guilty to 16 offences at Worcester Crown Court in October 2024 including the rape and sexual assault of a child.

He was eventually jailed for 20 years the police branding him “a highly dangerous and predatory individual” in what was one of the "worst cases of sexual abuse" they had been involved in.

The court heard of McAdam's contact with the other perverts included more than 100 messages with one of them.

Mr Jones said McAdam was also caught exposing himself on the top deck of a bus in Edinburgh city centre and filming it.

The offences occurred between November 2023 and September 2024.

McAdam will also be supervised for a further three and a half years on his release.
