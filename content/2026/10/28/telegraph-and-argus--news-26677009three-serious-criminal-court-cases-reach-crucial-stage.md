Three serious criminal cases in which multiple people from the Bradford district are on trial for murder, or conspiracy to murder, are reaching a crucial point at two separate court centres.

Mohammed Waqqas Khan, 27, of Folkestone Street, Bradford; Rahees Khan, 42, of no fixed abode but formerly of Granton Street, Bradford; Hanzalah Saladin, 27, of no fixed abode, Bradford; Adam Takolia, 29, of no fixed abode, Bradford; and a fifth man who cannot be named for legal reasons, deny conspiracy to murder Mohammed Zayad Khan and Mohammed Usama Khan on May 11 last year.

In addition, they all deny conspiracy to cause grievous bodily harm to both alleged victims.

Rahees Khan and Alice Dobson, 30, a former Police Community Support Officer (PCSO) of Nether Hall Road, Baildon, both deny perverting the course of justice by accessing confidential material related to the attacks, which Dobson allegedly passed on to Rahees Khan and others.

![The cordons in place after a machete attack in Bradford](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/19443664.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cordons in place after a machete attack in Bradford *(Image: Telegraph & Argus)*

The “prolonged and ferocious” nighttime machete attack resulted in Zayad Khan’s hand being hacked off at the wrist.

Usamah Khan suffered slash wounds to his neck.

The trial began in July but, following almost three weeks of evidence at Bradford Crown Court, His Honour Judge Jonathan Rose discharged the jury following an allegation of jury tampering.

When the trial resumed Judge Rose took the decision to continue without a jury, with him alone deciding on the verdicts.

He is now considering his judgement.

## Shipley murder trial

Also at Bradford Crown Court Beata Walczak and Zbigniew Chlebek are on trial for the murder of Jaroslav “Yanic” Daniszewski, who was found lying in a pool of blood on April 22 last year following an all-day drinking session in Shipley.

The jury heard that 43-year-old Mr Daniszewski suffered 24 separate injuries to his head and face consistent with blunt force trauma and was unlawfully killed.

The weapon used was an iron.

![A police cordon at the scene of Stead Street, Shipley, in April last year](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20795737.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police cordon at the scene of Stead Street, Shipley, in April last year *(Image: Newsquest)*

Walczak, 58, of Barkerend Road, Bradford, and Chlebek, 67, of Stead Street, Shipley, both deny murder as well as an alternative charge of manslaughter.

Six men are on trial at Sheffield Crown Court for their alleged roles in a co-ordinated, pre-planned, and brutal attack that left a 35-year-old father-of-two with catastrophic head and brain injuries.

## Keighley murder trial

Suhail Choudry was assaulted by men wielding metal poles and similar weapons shortly after 3am on February 8 this year on Flasby Street in Keighley.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20533205.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police conducting a murder inquiry in Keighley after the death of Suhail Choudry *(Image: Telegraph & Argus)*

He suffered 52 marks and injuries including multiple skull fractures as well as extensive brain injuries and died in Leeds General Infirmary three days later.

They are Mohammed Aliyaan Khan, 23, of Malsis Road, Keighley; Hakeem Gulzar, 28, of Plover Street, Keighley; Faisal Khan, 26, of Malsis Road, Keighley; Rashid Hussain, 41, of Redcliffe Street, Keighley; Majid Hussain, 42, of no fixed abode, Keighley; and Waseem Khan, 40, of East Avenue, Keighley.

Their trial is ongoing.
