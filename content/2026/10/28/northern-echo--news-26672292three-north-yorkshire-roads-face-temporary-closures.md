Motorists in North Yorkshire are facing a series of temporary road closures as works get underway at several locations.

North Yorkshire Council has issued Temporary Prohibition of Traffic Notices for The Green in Tholthorpe, Oulston to Oak Trees in Oulston, and Station Road in South Otterington.

The Green in Tholthorpe will be closed to traffic from October 26 to November 22, with access to premises allowed.

The closure is due to works, and the maximum duration of this order is until January 26, 2027.

The exact closure period will be indicated by traffic signs, and an alternative route will be marked on site where necessary.

M Group Water, on behalf of Yorkshire Water, can be contacted for enquiries at 07967 841660.

The road from Oulston to Oak Trees in Oulston will be closed from October 28 to March 2, 2027.

This closure is due to the installation of both a replacement foul sewer line and a new surface water sewer.

The maximum duration of this order is until April 27, 2027.

Peter Duffy Ltd is handling this project and can be contacted at 07929 718243 for any questions.

Station Road in South Otterington will be temporarily closed to all vehicles from October 27 to October 30.

Access to premises will be allowed during this time.

The maximum duration of this order is until January 27, 2027.

Morrison Water Services is managing the works, and can be contacted at 0345 1242424 for further information.

All closure periods will be indicated by traffic signs, and local alternative routes will be signed on site where necessary.

For further details on these roadworks, see the North Yorkshire Council website.
