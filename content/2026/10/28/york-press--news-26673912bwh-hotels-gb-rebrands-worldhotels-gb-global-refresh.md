## York-headquartered hotel chain rebrands to reflect global ambitions

The Pavilion Hotel in York, which is part of WorldHotels GB *(Image: Street View)*

York-headquartered BWH Hotels GB is rebranding as WorldHotels GB.

The change is part of a global brand refresh by the international group, which brings more than 4,000 hotels across 100 countries under the WorldHotels masterbrand.

Despite the rebrand, individual hotels will retain their existing brands, signage, and property flags.

Tim Rumney, CEO of WorldHotels GB, said: "Independent hoteliers across Great Britain have faced a challenging few years and we want to help them compete and grow in a changing market.




"Becoming WorldHotels GB is an important step in that ambition, giving us greater scope to reach global audiences and meet the expectations of today's travellers.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21636548.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There are many brands within this global giant. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"Our hotels will retain their existing brands and identities.

"Best Western remains at the heart of our business, but the refreshed brand strategy also gives us greater scope to grow in premium and extended-stay segments, where we see significant opportunity.

"We have ambitious plans to grow our business in Great Britain over the next five years.

"Alongside attracting new hotels, we’ll continue to focus on quality and the guest experience, helping independent hoteliers benefit from the reach of a global group without losing what makes their properties unique."

BWH Hotels GB currently represents more than 200 independent hotels across Great Britain, totalling more than 12,000 rooms.

Nearly 50 hotels are in its 12-month development pipeline, including properties in York, London, and coastal, urban, and rural locations.

So far this year, the group has added 12 hotels, totalling almost 600 rooms.

A further six properties are expected to join the portfolio by the end of 2026.

Premium and upscale properties currently make up around 15 per cent of the GB portfolio.

The group aims to grow its total portfolio by 50 per cent by 2029.

The rebrand also introduces WorldHotels Rewards, a unified loyalty programme that combines Best Western Rewards and WorldHotels Rewards.

The programme will launch globally on January 5, 2027.

Larry Cuculic, president and CEO of WorldHotels, said: "We have a hotel for virtually every travel occasion, but many travelers only know a portion of our portfolio.

"WorldHotels gives us a clearer way to tell our story. By connecting our brands, loyalty program and digital capabilities together under one masterbrand, we're making it easier for travelers to understand who we are today, while enabling our hoteliers to better compete on a global scale and preserving the independence and individuality of our 4,000 hotels."

The new loyalty scheme will feature three streamlined membership tiers and retain existing benefits such as status matching, member rates, and non-expiring points.

Bookings made by Reward customers currently accounts for 27 per cent of total hotel revenue in Britain for WorldHotels GB.
