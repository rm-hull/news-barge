**Johnsons Nurseries has donated 1,300 spring-flowering bulbs to Martin House Children’s Hospice, continuing its support for the facility following an earlier contribution of £1,500 worth of plants.**

The latest donation will help restore and enhance the hospice gardens, with daffodils and crocuses expected to bring additional colour to the grounds in spring 2027.

Martin House, in Boston Spa near Wetherby, provides care and support for babies, children and young people with life-limiting conditions across Yorkshire. Its services include planned stays, symptom management, end-of-life care and bereavement support for families.

Johnsons Nurseries, a family-run business based in Kirk Hammerton, previously donated plants to support the hospice’s £21.9 million redevelopment project, known as The Build.

The redevelopment included new facilities for children and teenagers, alongside a wellbeing centre offering therapy, counselling and wider support for families.

> **Graham Richardson, Group Managing Director at Johnsons Nurseries, said:**
>
> As a Yorkshire family business, supporting organisations within our local community is incredibly important to us. Martin House provides an invaluable service to children and families across the region, so we were delighted to contribute plants to the redevelopment.
>
> It is particularly rewarding to see the planting becoming established and know that these outdoor spaces will continue to be enjoyed by families for many years to come.

Robyn Mountain-Wade, Director of Income Generation at Martin House, said the gardens provided an important space for families to spend time together, relax and reflect.

> **Robyn Mountain-Wade said:**
>
> During the build project, we had to close a lot of our grounds, so we’re extremely grateful to Johnsons Nurseries for helping us restore them with such a generous donation of plants.
>
> We’ve already seen how much families are benefiting from spending time in our gardens since we reopened them, and the difference it makes to stays at the hospice.

Johnsons Nurseries said it also supports other organisations, including Henshaws and York Cares, through plant donations and other initiatives.

More information about Martin House Children’s Hospice is available at martinhouse.org.uk.
