An innovative Ryedale restaurant focussing on the humble British pie, served with mash, says sales are going far better than expected.

Angela Saunders and husband Richard opened the Pickering Pie Emporium a few weeks ago at 6 Market Place, Pickering, in a former Nat West Bank.

The couple, who grew up in Leeds, came to live in Ryedale 22 years ago, with Richard working in sales and Angela having a reike studio.

Angela said: “Richard has had this idea for six years. He was ready for a change. We saw the property come up for let. We thought we would grab the bull by the horns and fulfil this dream we have had.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638542.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pie and mash at the Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

“We wanted to bring something casual, community-led and fun to Pickering, comfort food really. We want people to comein and leave happier than when they arrived.”

“People are made to feel welcome, whether they have two buggies or three dogs.”

In addition to renovating the place, Angela and Richard are displaying local artworks around the venue to make it more beautiful and for the local artists to tell their story.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638543.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Angela and Richard Saunders and the team at The Pickering Pie Emporium. *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

Commission from the sales of artwork goes to the Pickering Community Interest Company.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638555.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Richard and Angela Saunders outside their new eatery before it opened. *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

The Pickering Pie Emporium has five home-made pies on its main menu, plus three gluten free options.

It also works with six suppliers around the UK that make award-winning and “interesting” pies, to offer customers “something curious and interesting.”

Suppliers include Jarvis Pickles of Endinburgh, the well-known Dan’s Pies of Pocklington, The Pie Lady in Hull and Chunk from Devon.

The coffee comes from Three Ridings in Leven.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638556.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The emporium logo *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

Angela explained: “We try and keep it as local as we can.”

And in the three weeks the Pickering Pie Emporium has been open, business has been “unprecedented, absolutely fantastic.”

Angela continued: “We served our 1,100th pie last week. I did not think we expected it would be as popular as it has been. We did 90 pie meals on Monday. We are really busy, people love it.”

Due to public demand, a pork and cider pie has been introduced onto the main menu as it is “crazily popular.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638560.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of the Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

A hot apple pie from Dan’s Pies of Pocklington is also proving a best seller.

Angela said: “It’s divine. It’s a real comfort food.”

Having found a recipe for success, Angela and Richard are looking to offer more live music, having just staged two sell-out jazz nights.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638579.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of The Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

She added the couple are also keen to work with the local community to create other “different things.”

And the locals are most certainly happy, with 15 consecutive 5-star reviews on TripAdvisor.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638588.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A colourful entrance to The Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

Google awards 4.9 stars out of five, based on 19 Google reviews.

The latest TripAdvisor review said: “beautiful food, great service. A must visit.”

It added: “The food was delicious. The pies were some of the best we have ever had. And the staff were lovely, it’s a family run place and you can tell they have poured their heart and soul into it and they are genuinely passionate about what they do. I cannot recommend this place enough.”
