A Teesside gift shop that started as a spare-bedroom hobby is making its biggest move yet.

Pepper Place will open on Stockton High Street on Friday (October 30), in a major move for the independent business.

The shop began as Kraft Station in 2023, operating from the owner's home as a creative outlet created to support mental health.

Crafting became both a way to cope and a new passion before growing into a personalised-gifts business.

Soon, the business had outgrown the spare bedroom, moved into a Stockton shop and later relocated to the Fountain Shopping Mall.

It is now preparing to move to the High Street by the Shambles.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641507.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pepper Place has moved from its old home in Fountain Shopping Mall. *(Image: Google)*

Chris McDonald, MP for Stockton North, welcomed the move.

McDonald said: "Exciting to see Pepper Place-Teesside expanding from the Fountain Shopping Mall and moving onto the High Street by the Shambles.

"It looks like they have some great gifts so I'm sure local people will support them when they open in a couple of days."

As the business grew, so did its range, with a wider selection of handpicked gifts added alongside its personalised items.

Customers can choose from keepsakes, printed designs, collectables, novelty keyrings and the popular Lesser & Pavey Highland Cow collection.

Work has been ongoing to transform the new High Street premises, with customers able to follow along with the progress on social media.

The move follows another major change earlier this year, when Kraft Station became Pepper Place.

The new name was inspired by the owners’ family, adding a personal touch to a business built around gifts celebrating people, memories and milestones.

Stockton is central to the business, with Pepper Place using social media to champion the town and ask followers to share their favourite local places and things.

The new Pepper Place store at 110 High Street will open on Friday, October 30.
