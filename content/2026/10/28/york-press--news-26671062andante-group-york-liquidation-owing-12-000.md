A York company that provided private security activities has gone into voluntary liquidation with debts exceeding £12,000.

Andante Group Ltd of Wigginton Road was incorporated in March 2020 and entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on October 5 with Leeds-based Clarke Business Recovery Solutions appointed liquidator.

Companies House records Anthony Peter Stannard as the sole director.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs details the finances of the liquidated company.




* **Quickfix@home (York) and Quickfix (York) in liquidation**
* **York dental business in liquidation owing more than £400,000**
* **York employment agency liquidated owing more than £31,000**
* **JP Commercial Vehicles registered in York in liquidation**

The report said Andante had estimated total realisable assets of £14,150, including £12,300 cash-in-hand.

There were no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

But HMRC as a secondary preferential creditor – someone paid next – was owed VAT of £26,468.70, leaving a shortfall or deficit of £12,318.70.

In addition, an unsecured creditor – someone paid last – was owed £600.

This gave a deficiency of £12,918.70.

With Andante Group Ltd having issued and called up capital of £1, this gave an overall deficit of £12,919.70.
