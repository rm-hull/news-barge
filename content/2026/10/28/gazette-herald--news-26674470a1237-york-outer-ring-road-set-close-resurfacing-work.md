A section of one of the major roads in York is set to close this week for resurfacing work.

City of York Council said the A1237 Outer Ring Road will close overnight, from 8pm to 5am, between the A19 Shipton Road roundabout and the entrance to Clifton Moor retail park on both Thursday (October 29) and Friday (October 30).

It said the works will be finished with the road reopening to all traffic at 5am on Saturday 31 October, weather permitting and there will be no work at the weekend.




A signed diversion will be in place, using the A19 Shipton Road via Bootham to the A1036 and Malton Road passing Gillygate, Lord Mayors Walk and Heworth Green.

A City of York Council spokesperson said: "Motorists and cyclists may choose to take alternative routes, but due to the capacity the A1237 holds for large vehicles, the council must offer a diversion able to cope with the size and weight that the road usually carries.

"Cyclists or pedestrians wanting to travel in the direction of the closed section of the A1237 can use Manor Lane and a dual use path to Hurricane way to come out at Clifton Moor Gate.

"Bus services will not be affected."

Residents and commuters are asked to plan ahead for these nighttime works, and residents in the immediate area have been written to, the council said.

The Outer Ring Road closure comes at the same time as an existing restriction affecting traffic further round in a clockwise direction.

The Monks Cross Link connecting the shopping park of the same name with the A1237 currently has lane closure and traffic management associated with the building of a new housing development.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21482801.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Works on the Monks Cross Link are scheduled to be completed on November 6 *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

That project of works included the creation of new utilities connections which are being delivered by Redrow Yorkshire in conjunction with the construction of 970 new houses in the area.

As reported by The Press, those works are scheduled to finish on Friday, November 6, with the northern roundabout reopening and two-way access restored.

Motorists had reported spending up to 30 minutes in queues in the area close to Monks Cross shopping park, made more acute on matchdays at the nearby LNER Community Stadium.

Current diversions on that route also take traffic along the A1036 Malton Road back to the Outer Ring Road.
