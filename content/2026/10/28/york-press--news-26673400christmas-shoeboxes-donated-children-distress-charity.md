## Hundreds of Christmas gift boxes donated to children’s charity in York

More than 280 shoeboxes packed with gifts have been donated to the Children in Distress Charity in York *(Image: Supplied)*

More than 280 shoeboxes packed with gifts have been donated to a children’s charity in York.

Children in Distress has received the boxes as part of its annual campaign to send shoeboxes filled with Christmas gifts to Romania.

Simon Errington, CEO of Children in Distress, said: "Our shoebox appeal stretches right back to our foundation as a charity when my father and others set out to help children suffering from HIV and Aids in Romanian orphanages.

"Whilst our work as a charity is much broader today and we have brought our expertise and knowledge from Romania to also help young people and communities here in the UK, we’re proud to continue this annual tradition.

"It’s a vital thread building connections between people here in Yorkshire and Romania."

More than 5,000 filled shoeboxes were distributed in Bucharest and nearby areas last Christmas to children, teenagers and older adults in need.

This year’s collection closes on November 13.
