A person has fallen into a harbour in a North Yorkshire coastal town.

It happened in Whitby yesterday evening (Tuesday, October 27).

The town's RNLI inshore lifeboat was launched at 6pm and when crews arrived the injured person had been pulled from the water by passersby who used a life ring.




Richard Dowson, Whitby RNLI coxswain, said: "They did exactly the right thing using the life ring.

"The water is cold at this time of year and cold-water shock can kick in very quickly."

"We don't advise jumping in to rescue someone as you can then find yourself in difficulty as well.

"In this case, they got the casualty out of the water, and we stood by until the ambulance arrived – well done to all involved."
