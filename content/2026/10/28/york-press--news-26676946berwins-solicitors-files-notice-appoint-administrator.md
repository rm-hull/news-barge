## Well-known North Yorkshire law firm prepares to call in administrators

Members of Berwins’ York team at the opening of its York office in 2022- From left, Jeremy Bristow, Natasha Guest, Paul Berwin, Oliver King and William Kaye. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A well-known North Yorkshire law firm has filed a notice of intention to appoint an administrator.

A filing dated October 26, 2026, is recorded under High Court case number CR-2026-008308 in the name of Berwins Solicitors Limited.

The move comes just weeks after the company celebrated its 40th anniversary with a summer drinks celebration at Cedar Court Harrogate.

Berwins was founded in Harrogate in 1986 by Paul Berwin and has offices in Harrogate and Leeds.

Several years ago, the company also had an office in York.

Danielle Day took over from Paul as managing director in January 2025, following the company’s acquisition by Kent-based Griffin Law Group.

Paul retired from the business in February this year.

Berwins provides a range of legal services to individuals, families and businesses.

Its work covers areas, including property, family matters and services for private clients and commercial organisations.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21640733.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Berwins Salon North event, which takes place in Harrogate. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The company actively supports local events and good causes, most notably as sponsor of the Ted Talk-style Berwins Salon North events.

Its Harrogate office is at 2 North Park Road and Companies House records show Berwins Solicitors Limited was incorporated in April 2009.

Danielle Saunders and Michelle Elizabeth Crowhurst are listed as directors.

The company’s latest published accounts, for the year to June 2025, show Berwins employed 40 people, compared with 47 the year before.

The accounts also showed net assets of around £296,000, down from £400,000 a year earlier.

A notice of intention to appoint an administrator is a preliminary stage of the administration process.

It does not, by itself, mean that administrators have been appointed.

The Press has approached Berwins Solicitors for comment.
