A family-run County Durham salon is in the running for a place among England’s best after winning a regional award just 18 months after opening.

Storey’s Hair and Beauty in Sedgefield won County Durham Hair and Beauty Salon of the Year at the 2026 England Business Awards and will now compete nationally.

Rebecca Storey, salon founder and stylist, said: "When we opened, we wanted to create a really good salon in Sedgefield and build something that our customers would want to come back to.

"We’re still a relatively new business, so winning the County Durham award was a massive moment for us.

"To now have the chance to compete at national level is something we’re incredibly proud of."

Since opening in March 2025, the salon has expanded into beauty and aesthetics, launched wedding styling packages and developed its own hair and beauty products.

Rebecca runs the business with her sister Alice, who leads beauty and aesthetics, and her niece Beau, an apprentice in training.

Hair stylist Sam completes the team.

Ms Storey said: "We’re a family business and our customers have been such a huge part of getting us to where we are.

"A lot of people have supported us from the beginning and it’s been lovely to see the salon grow.

"We’d absolutely love to take the national title, but whatever happens, being recognised in this way means a lot to all of us."

The public can vote in the England Business Awards until November 20.

Supporters can vote for Storey’s Hair and Beauty at the awards website.
