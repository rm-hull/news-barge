25 years on from the original films, North East Harry Potter fans have the chance to step into a world of magic this Halloween as a spellbinding display is gaining attention at the Metrocentre.

Hagrid’s Hut, complete with a pumpkin patch and other iconic props from the Harry Potter films, is now on display in the Metrocentre's Exhibition Square, in Gateshead.

It will remain in the shopping centre until this Sunday (November 1).

Over Halloween weekend, the shopping centre will also offer free wand-making workshops and the chance to meet costumed characters from the franchise.

The immersive experience is part of the shopping centre's extensive half-term programme, offering something magical for families during the school break and over Halloween weekend.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21641198.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The immersive experience is part of the shopping centre's extensive half-term programme, and will last until November 1. *(Image: Metrocentre)*

Helen Atkinson, marketing manager at the Metrocentre, said: "As the days shorten and the nights draw in, Metrocentre offers lots of reasons to get out of the house this Autumn and avoid cabin fever over the half-term holidays.

"From gaming, beauty and circus fun to our free wizardry world event, there’s something for all the family, big and small."

Fans can visit Hagrid’s Hut, explore a whimsical pumpkin patch, and see replica movie props including Mr Weasley’s Flying car, Aragog the giant spider, and Hagrid’s bike.

The display has already attracted excited Harry Potter fans, with one commenter writing on social media: "Amazing! Sad to see no real-life Basilisk moving throughout the malls, that would have made shopping so much more petrifying...for Halloween."

The magic continues throughout the week, with the experience running during normal centre opening hours.

The festivities will peak on Friday, October 30, and Saturday, October 31, when young witches and wizards can take part in free wand-making workshops and meet costumed characters.

Wizarding professors and students will be roaming the centre at set times throughout both days, with wand workshops running from 11am to 4pm on both days, giving children plenty of time to craft their own magical creations.

On Halloween itself, visitors can meet the Dragon Master, who will be making special appearances throughout the day.

The Metrocentre’s wider programme also includes a Nintendo Switch 2 experience, H beauty Carnival 5.0 and Gandey’s K-Pop Dragon Circus, with circus tickets starting at £9.99.
