The boss of a York-based global hotel chain has hit out at today's budget.

Tim Rumney, CEO of WorldHotels GB, says the budget was a 'missed opportunity to give much-needed support to the hospitality sector.

His comments echo that of fellow hotel boss, Simon Cotton of the Harrogate-based Fat Badger Group.

Mr Rumney said: "Once again the Budget is a missed opportunity for independent hotels with no hospitality VAT relief announced.

"Hotels are major employers and anchors of local economies, yet they are being left to absorb rising costs without the tax support that would help them protect jobs and invest into their properties and guest experience.

"Independent hoteliers cannot keep being treated as an afterthought.

"The 3.9% increase in minimum wage rates adds further pressure to hotels already facing rising costs.

"Independent hoteliers want to pay their people fairly, but they cannot absorb continual cost increases without consequences for jobs and growth.

"The Government must ensure its support for workers is matched by action to help the businesses that create these jobs."

His comments come as the company rebranded from the Best Westen brand to reflect its global ambitions.

Mr Rumney added: "Our move to become WorldHotels GB reflects our ambition to strengthen our position in the UK market and help independent hoteliers reach new audiences and compete on a global scale.

"We have the ambition to invest and grow, but the Government needs to create the right conditions for independent hotels to realise their potential and continue contributing to the UK economy."
