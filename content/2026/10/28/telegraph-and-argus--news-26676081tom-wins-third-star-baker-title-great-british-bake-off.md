West Yorkshire baker Tom has won his third Star Baker title after receiving a Paul Hollywood handshake for his pie.

The achievement came during Pastry Week on The Great British Bake Off, when contestants faced three challenges which tested their pastry skills.

### Vietnamese inspired Signature

For the Signature Challenge, bakers had to make eight identical hand-held savoury pies. Tom made a Vietnamese banh mi-inspired pasty filled with pork, ginger and lemongrass.

Tom said: “I’m doing a Vietnamese banh mi inspired pasty, I’ll be using the flavours and the filling of the baguette and be putting it into a pasty instead.”

His recipe was influenced by travels in Thailand, Laos, Cambodia and Vietnam.

As the pastry developed, Tom said: “We’re getting some good layers appearing.”

He added, “Proper pasties them aren’t they, no soggy bottoms here.”

Paul Hollywood said: “I like the consistency and the colour.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21485734.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Judges Paul Hollywood and Nigella Lawson *(Image: Newsquest)*

Nigella Lawson added: “Gorgeous flavours, it’s a really interesting contrast between the flavours and the pastry, I love that.”

Paul said: “It’s beautiful, the spice level is spot on, everything just works together and that pastry is excellent, you’ve got a nice flake on that.”

### 'I'm going to fight for it'

The Technical Challenge involved making a Polish karpatka, consisting of two layers of choux pastry filled with vanilla custard.

Tom said: “I’ve never heard of a karpatka.”

He finished second in the challenge. Paul said: “This is good. I think it needed a bit more colour.”

After judging, Tom said: “I feel like it’s all to play for tomorrow and I would like that star baker, I’m going to fight for it.”

For the Showstopper, contestants had to make one large free-form pie without using tins. The filling had to act as the mould for the pastry.

### Tom scores Hollywood handshake

Tom produced a Mexican-inspired chicken and chorizo pie, seasoned with chipotle and chilli.

Nigella said: “Really fantastic it looks like a piece of Aztec art.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21491129.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Judge Nigella Lawson *(Image: Newsquest)*

The pie also earned Tom one of the competition's most sought-after accolades, a Hollywood handshake.

Paul said: “I love that Tom.”

He added: “It’s the flavour combination and the moisture that you’ve got inside, the thinness of the pastry, the taste of the pastry, it’s delicious. Spot on.”

Tom said: “Can’t believe it. Got a handshake, they both loved it.”

Tom was then awarded his third Star Baker title of the series.

Paul said: “He’s certainly one to watch.”

Tom said: “In terms of the fact that I’m here and I’ve won star baker three times, that is just mad, that is not something I ever thought would actually happen. I’m literally just so happy right now.”
