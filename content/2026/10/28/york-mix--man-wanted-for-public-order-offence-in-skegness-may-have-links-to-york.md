A man who’s alleged to have committed a public order offence in Skegness may have links to York and Scarborough, police say.

This picture of the suspect has been released by Lincolnshire Police.

The offence is reported to have taken place on Roman Bank, Skegness on Saturday (24 October) at around 11.40am.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/skegness-public-order-offence-full.webp)

A Lincolnshire Police spokesperson said: “We would like to speak to the man in the image in connection with an allegation of a public order offence.

“We believe he might be able to assist with our enquiries.”

If you know who he is, or have information to assist officers, you can call police on 101 quoting reference 26000652698.
