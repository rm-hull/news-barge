Rachael Maskell MP has welcomed a new government policy she says will benefit parents in York.

The Labour government has announced that self-employed fathers and partners will now be eligible for two weeks of paternity pay, a change expected to support an additional 150,000 parents.

The new scheme will also extend to adoptive parents, something Ms Maskell has championed in her role as chair of the All-Party Parliamentary Group for Adoption and Permanence.

Ms Maskell, MP for York Central, said: "I am so pleased to see this positive change for parents in York Central.

"My work on the Infants, Parents and Carers Bill has clearly shown how vital it is for all parents, no matter of employment status, to spend time with their newborn to form crucial bonds.

"This Labour Government is giving this opportunity to everyone by putting money back in pockets."

She also highlighted the importance of support for adoptive families.

Ms Maskell said: "Likewise, adoptive parents need crucial time in building new relationships with their new son or daughter.

"This will help more children find their forever family."

Under the new policy, eligible parents will receive paternity pay of up to £194 per week.
