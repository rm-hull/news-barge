A MAJOR emergency response was triggered after a collision on a busy Bradford street.

Police have remained tight-lipped over the incident, which happened on Beckside Road near Lidget Green Post Office earlier this afternoon (Wednesday).

A witness at the scene said: "At first, we thought the post office was getting robbed.

"There was a huge scene and there were loads of police cars.

"They started coming from every angle."

A police cordon was put in place and the road was partially blocked while officers responded.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21642733.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Remnants of police tape at the scene this afternoon *(Image: T&A)*

Images posted on social media show a large emergency presence including ambulances and police vehicles.

Multiple officers could be seen attending the scene.

The Telegraph & Argus contacted West Yorkshire Police for a statement on the incident.

Police declined to provide any details of what had happened, stating that the incident did not meet the terms of a Service Level Agreement imposed by the Force to govern which incidents it provides details on.
