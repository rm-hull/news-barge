Park and Ride buses are set to run more frequently to support Christmas shoppers and reduce city centre traffic.

First Bus has announced an improved timetable for York’s Park and Ride service, delivered in partnership with City of York Council, to help visitors, shoppers, and traders during the busy festive period.

The changes begin Sunday, November 8 and are timed to support the opening of York’s Christmas market on November 12.

Steve Moyle, general manager of First Bus in York, said: "We know how much visitors and local people enjoy the festive season in the city.

"Our investment in more services on the Park and Ride network is designed to help make travel around York easier during this really busy period and reduce car traffic into the city centre, which will benefit everyone."

Buses will now run every 7 to 17 minutes depending on the route, with the schedule designed to meet last year’s passenger demand.

Councillor Kate Ravilious, executive member for transport at City of York Council, said: "Our Park and Ride services are amongst the best in the country, offering a quick and easy way of getting in and out of the city."

All six Park and Ride sites remain open from 5.30am to 10.45pm.
