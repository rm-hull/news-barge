BRADFORD has been named the UK’s top city for gaming connectivity.

The city scored 70.89 out of 100 in the Gaming Connectivity Index, a study commissioned by Currys that considered full-fibre latency, download and upload speeds, and proximity to London server hubs.

With a full-fibre latency of just 12 milliseconds and stable broadband infrastructure, Bradford edged out Manchester and Liverpool to take the top spot.

An expert at Currys said: "You can have gigabit broadband, but if your monitor lacks a high refresh rate or your GPU bottlenecks, you won’t feel that speed.

"Aligning your PC hardware, display, accessories, and network ensures your split-second reactions actually translate into gameplay."

Bradford’s consistent speeds—80 Mbps download and 20 Mbps upload—helped secure its position, despite not being the fastest in the country.

The study found that reliable, low-latency connections are critical for gaming performance, beyond just headline broadband speeds.

Manchester ranked second with a score of 67.95, offering symmetrical speeds up to 10,000 Mbps and the lowest latency in the top five at 9 milliseconds.

Liverpool placed third with 330 Mbps download, 50 Mbps upload, and 11 milliseconds latency.
