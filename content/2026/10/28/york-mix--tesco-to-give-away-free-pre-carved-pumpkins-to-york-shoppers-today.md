Tesco are giving away free pre-carved pumpkins to York shoppers **today, Wednesday 28 October**.

The giveaway will take place at Tesco York Tadcaster Road Extra from 3pm to 5pm.

Shoppers can choose between a classic jack o’ lantern or an Eclipse-inspired design, with the pumpkins professionally carved by Tesco’s very own pumpkin artists-in-residence.

The giveaway is taking place for one afternoon only, with pumpkins available on a first-come, first-served basis and no purchase necessary.

The initiative comes after new research shows that despite pumpkin carving being one of the favourite traditions for almost half the population, just over a third (36%) of Brits say they’d appreciate a little help with pumpkin design. In response to this, Tesco is stepping in to help the UK get Halloween-ready.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sand-In-Your-Eye-9B.webp)

The limited-edition designs showcase the ‘most talkable moments of 2026’ and have been sketched onto pumpkins, taking professional carvers over 50 hours.

The Eclipse-inspired design is of course inspired by this year’s eclipse, with the design featuring a nod to the nation’s inventive viewing methods, including the iconic glasses featuring within the carving.

The pumpkins are first come, first served, so be quick to grab your free pre-carved pumpkin!
