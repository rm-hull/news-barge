Thousands are expected to line Stockton’s Riverside as the town’s much-loved fireworks display returns after five years away.

The free event takes place on Sunday (November 1), with fireworks, live music, a funfair and food and drink planned throughout the day.

With a new viewing location, road and bridge closures, and a park and ride service in operation, here’s everything you need to know before heading along.

### What time does Stockton's fireworks display start?

Entertainment will run from 12pm until 9pm, with the main fireworks display beginning at 6.30pm.

The fireworks will last around 20 minutes and will be accompanied by a modern orchestral soundtrack themed around water.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21025303.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stockton's last riverside fireworks display was in 2021 *(Image: DAWN MCNAMARA PHOTOGRAPHY)*

### Where can I watch the fireworks?

Fireworks will launch from the Teesdale side of the River Tees, with several viewing areas along Riverside Road.

These include the event space next to Riverside Car Park and the bankside off Bishop Street.

For the first time, spectators can also watch from the amphitheatre in the new Waterfront urban park.

### What entertainment will there be?

The celebrations begin long before the fireworks, with a funfair taking over Stockton High Street from 12pm until 9pm.

Rides range from family attractions to high-adrenaline thrills, including the Starflyer, which offers panoramic town views.

Rides cost extra, but the fireworks and all other entertainment are free.

Live music runs in Finkle Square from 12pm to 5pm, with local artists Caitlin Morrow, George Bailey and Annie Lince performing.

The Anywhere Anything Band will then roam the event site from 4.30pm, encouraging crowds to sing and dance ahead of the fireworks.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20906529.jpg?type=mds-article-620)George Bailey will perform *(Image: Supplied)*

### What food and drink will be available?

Visitors will be able to choose from a range of hot and cold food, including burgers, loaded fries, Indian street food and churros.

Hot chocolate will also be available to keep the autumn chill at bay, alongside bar facilities.

Extra food stalls open at Bishop Street Car Park from 12pm and Riverside Car Park from 4pm.

### Is there a park and ride?

A park and ride service will run from 5pm until 8.30pm between Asda on Portrack Lane Industrial Estate and Church Road, opposite the Simon Bailes Peugeot garage.

Arriva will run buses every three to four minutes, with return journeys costing £1 per person.

Nearby residents are urged to walk where possible because heavy traffic is expected. Some car parks will remain open during the day.

### Which roads and bridges will be closed?

Temporary road closures and parking restrictions will affect Stockton town centre, Teesdale, Castlegate Quay and North Shore.

Several bridges will close before and during the display, including the Princess of Wales, Millennium, Infinity and Bishop Street footbridges.

The temporary restrictions are expected to remain in place until November 3, while pedestrian and emergency access will be maintained where it is safe.

Buses will run as normal, although some stops will be temporarily relocated.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21625989.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fireworks at Stockton Riverside, 2017 *(Image: DAWN McNAMARA PHOTOGRAPHY)*

Councillor Nigel Cooke said: "Get ready for an unforgettable experience as Stockton's iconic fireworks display returns this weekend.

"We're delighted to welcome Stockton Fireworks back to the Riverside after five years away.

"Since the event was last held there, the town centre has undergone a remarkable transformation, with the impressive new Waterfront Park offering residents and visitors an exciting new setting from which to enjoy the celebrations."

He added: "As with all big events like this, careful consideration has been given to community safety, and the fireworks will be launched from the Teesdale side of the river, with spectators able to relax and enjoy the display from Riverside Road and, for the first time, the amphitheatre in the urban park.

"There's plenty to enjoy for young and old alike, so wrap up warm and join thousands of spectators this weekend for the unmissable return of Stockton's much-loved fireworks."

Travel details and road-closure maps are available on Stockton Borough Council's website and social media channels.
