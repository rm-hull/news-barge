Thousands of craft enthusiasts are expected in Harrogate next month for the return of Knit + Stitch.

The four-day event will take place at Harrogate Convention Centre from Thursday 19th to Sunday 22nd November.

Formerly known as The Knitting & Stitching Show, it will bring together more than 150 specialist craft suppliers, alongside workshops, live demonstrations and textile art galleries.

Activities will cover knitting, crochet, embroidery, sewing, quilting, dressmaking and felting, with opportunities for both experienced makers and beginners to get involved.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ae1e6fa5e7c76d932e.png)

Visitors will be able to learn new techniques and try different crafts through hands-on workshops and interactive experiences.

There will also be fabrics, yarns, patterns, sewing machines, haberdashery and craft kits available to browse and buy.

Organisers expect the show to attract visitors from across Yorkshire and further afield.

*Tickets and further information are available via the Knit + Stitch**website**.*
