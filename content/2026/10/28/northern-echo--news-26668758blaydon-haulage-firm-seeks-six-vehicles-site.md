A North East haulage firm has applied to increase the number of vehicles it can keep at one of its operating centres.

Premier Truck Hire Ltd, based at Unit 7 and 8 Chainbridge North, Chainbridge Road, Blaydon, has applied to amend its licence.

The application would add six goods vehicles and one trailer to the site.

Residents and businesses close to the site, who believe their property use or enjoyment might be impacted by the change, are invited to submit written objections to the Traffic Commissioner .

Written objections must be submitted within 21 days of the notice, dated October 24.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Residents must also send a copy of their objections to Premier Truck Hire Ltd at its Chainbridge Road address.

Guidance on making objections is available at the government website.

Objections must be sent in writing to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE, within 21 days of publication.
