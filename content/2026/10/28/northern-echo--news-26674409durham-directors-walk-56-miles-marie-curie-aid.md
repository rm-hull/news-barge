Four County Durham company directors are preparing to walk 56 miles in memory of their fathers after raising almost £6,000 for Marie Curie before the challenge has even begun.

Brothers Phil and Michael Pallister, Jason Oakes and Liam Hall, directors at PHS and 0800 Repair, will tackle the two-day Weardale Way trek in memory of their late fathers and to support Marie Curie.

The walk starts in Killhope at 7am on Thursday, October 29, and ends at Durham Cathedral, where the team hopes to cross the finish line together.

Jason Oakes, partnership director, said: "We've been overwhelmed by the support we've received from colleagues, friends, partners and the wider community.

"To be approaching £6,000 before we've even taken our first steps is incredible and shows just how many people have been touched by cancer and want to support the work Marie Curie carries out every day.

"This challenge is about remembering our dads and raising money for a charity that provides vital support to people and families at the most difficult times in their lives.

"We can't thank everyone enough for their generosity."

Fundraising has included a coffee morning and charity day featuring raffles, games, football scratch cards, a dress-down day and refreshments.

The event raised another £750, taking the total to £5,700 before the walk.

Phil Pallister, CEO, said: "The support has been fantastic.

"We know the walk is going to be physically demanding, but seeing the total continue to rise gives us even more motivation to complete the challenge and raise as much money as possible for Marie Curie.

"We're looking forward to getting underway and crossing the finish line together in Durham on Friday."

Colleagues will join the directors for the final stretch into Durham.

Marie Curie supports people with terminal illnesses and their families.

The team hopes to beat its £6,000 target during the challenge and is urging people to donate.

Supporters can donate through the team’s JustGiving page.
