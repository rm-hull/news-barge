## Home in ‘one of York’s most prestigious’ areas hits the market – for £1.45m

The five-bedroom home for sale in Sycamore Place, off Bootham, in York for £1.45 million *(Image: Naish)*

A home has hit the market in “one of York’s most prestigious” areas – for £1.45 million.

The five-bedroom house is for sale in Sycamore Place, off Bootham.

Naish, which is marketing the property, said it is an “impressive family home” which “combines a luxurious finish with a wealth of beautifully preserved period features”.

Inside is an entrance hall leading to the reception rooms.

The central reception room is currently arranged as a dining room with doors opening onto the landscaped rear garden.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21626985.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the five-bedroom home for sale in Sycamore Place, off Bootham, in York for £1.45 million *(Image: Naish)*

To the rear of the house is the kitchen and garden room.

The family room is positioned to the rear of the kitchen and a further set of doors also open onto the garden.

Upstairs, two bedrooms and a snug are on the first floor.
