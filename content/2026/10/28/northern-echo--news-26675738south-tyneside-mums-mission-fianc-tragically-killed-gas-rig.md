A journey that began with heartbreaking loss has brought two South Tyneside friends together with a shared mission to improve physical and mental wellbeing across the North East.

Kristie Graham, a gym owner and fitness coach from South Tyneside, lost her fiancé Robbie Robson in 2022 while he was working on a gas rig off the coast of Qatar.

He was killed by a colleague, leaving behind Ms Graham and their two children, Willow, 14, and Sefa, five.

Ms Graham said: "I couldn’t take in what had happened.

"The grief hit me so hard, and at the same time I was trying to hold things together for our children, Willow and Sefa, who’d just lost their dad.

"Fitness has always been a big part of my life, so I threw myself into training.

"It gave me some structure and kept me going, but on its own it couldn’t touch the grief.

"I was exhausted, I was losing weight, and I was still sinking."

Though she received therapy through the NHS, Ms Graham said it was not enough, citing long waiting lists and overstretched services.

She finally found support through Natalie Wiley, a hypnotherapist, massage therapist, and 9D breathwork facilitator based in South Shields.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639105.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kristie Graham and Natalie Wiley at the Fitfam gym, South Shields *(Image: Provided)*

Ms Wiley said: "Grief can crush people and it was clear that Kristie was trying her best, but needed some additional support.

"We started by working on her breathing and helping her to relax.

"Exercise is brilliant for both body and mind, but when someone is carrying something as heavy as grief, it often needs to go hand in hand with support for what’s happening underneath.

"We look at the root of what someone is going through and work on that."

As Ms Graham started to heal, the pair began discussing how they could use their combined experience to help others.

They launched a community interest company (CIC), Fitfam x Realign, in April.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21639103.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kristie Graham and Natalie Wiley at the Fitfam gym, South Shields *(Image: Provided)*

The CIC brings together physical movement, emotional wellbeing, and nervous system support for individuals, young people, and workplaces.

Services include hypnotherapy, breathwork, emotion regulation, and tailored fitness support.

Ms Wiley said: "Primarily, we want to help people, but there are also major benefits for businesses in supporting their employees to become healthier and happier.

"We can help businesses reduce sickness absence, improve productivity and create a happier working environment."

Ms Graham said the project is also a tribute to Mr Robson.

She said: "Robbie would light up a room when he walked in, and all of this has been inspired by him.

"He loved life, and he’d love knowing that good, positive things have come from him.

"His mantra was always ‘don’t just exist’."

Fitfam x Realign also offers businesses the option to fund treatment and support for individuals who may not otherwise be able to access it.

The pair recently spoke at a South Tyneside Pledge Networking event, where they shared how their combined skills are helping to provide more holistic support in the community.

Councillor Elliot Curry, lead member for economic growth, regeneration and investment, said: "I think people in the borough will remember the heartbreaking story of what happened to Robbie.

"However, it is incredibly inspiring to see something so positive come from such a tragedy.

"I wish Kristie and Natalie every success."
