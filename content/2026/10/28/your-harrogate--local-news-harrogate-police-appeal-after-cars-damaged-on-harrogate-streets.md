Police are appealing for information after several reports of cars being damaged in Harrogate.

The incidents happened around King Edwards Drive and Jesmond Road and are believed to have taken place between 2am and 4am on Monday 26th October.

North Yorkshire Police is asking anyone who witnessed suspicious activity or has information about those responsible to come forward.

Officers are particularly keen to hear from anyone with CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage from the area.

*Anyone who can help is asked to email investigating officer [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting reference 12260210752.*

*Information can also be submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting ‘Tell us about’ and then ‘An existing case or report’.*

*Anyone unable to use online services can call 101. Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*
