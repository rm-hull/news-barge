# Leeds Festival 2027 headliner tips as massive names expected at Bramham Park

## Leeds Festival 2027 will return to Bramham Park over the August bank holiday weekend

Leeds Festival 2027 looks like welcoming some massive names to Bramham Park.

While there’s still yet to be anything official, rumours have started to circulate online about who might be appearing at Leeds Festival and its sister event Reading Festival. Betting firm OLBG’s website has listings for who they expect to appear in 2027.

Leeds Festival 2027 will take place, as usual, over the August bank holiday weekend. The dates for the fun are Thursday, August 26 to Sunday, August 29. You can buy tickets from Ticketmaster.

OLBG states: “Pop star Tate McRae is the name betting experts expect to see at the top of the Reading and Leeds bill next summer. The latest 2027 Reading and Leeds headliner odds make her a 5/4 shot, an implied 44.4 per cent chance, ahead of a chasing pack that mixes pop, indie and rock.

“McRae has fast become one of the biggest pop names around, and a Reading and Leeds headline slot would be a natural next step. The festivals’ audience has always skewed young, and the bookings in recent years have leaned much more heavily on pop than they did a decade ago.”

Here are the other odds listed on OLBG:

* Tate McRae - 5/4
* Sabrina Carpenter - 2/1
* Phoebe Bridgers - 2/1
* The Strokes - 5/2
* Olivia Roridgo - 3/1
* Gorillaz - 3/1
* Fred Again - 4/1
* Billie Eilish - 5/1
* Wolf Alice - 5/1
* Olivia Dean - 6/1

We’ve previously chatted to Melvin Benn, managing director of Festival Republic, which oversees the festivities, with our feature touching on next year and how he, and his team, go about booking headline acts.
