A major acquisition has brought a North Yorkshire manufacturer into the fold of a global industrial group.

Econ Engineering Group, based in Ripon, has been acquired by U.S.-based Alamo Group Inc. in a deal valued at £120 million.

The company, which employs 350, is a leading manufacturer of road maintenance vehicles and equipment including gritters, de-icing vehicles, road repair machinery and multi-purpose truck bodies.

Robert Hureau, chief executive officer of Alamo Group, said: "Econ is a strong addition to Alamo Group and advances our strategy to expand our Industrial Equipment portfolio.

"Its leading market position, differentiated products, strong customer service capabilities, attractive financial profile, and resilient end-markets make Econ a great fit with our portfolio of industrial businesses.

"Econ also gives us a stronger platform in the UK and creates opportunities for long-term growth.

"Beyond the initial synergies we expect from bringing Econ onto Alamo Group's platform, we see significant room to grow the business both within and beyond its core market.

"We want to welcome all the Econ Engineering team members to our family and are excited about our future together."

Founded in 1969 by Bill Lupton, Econ Engineering generated approximately £66 million in net sales and £29 million in adjusted EBITDA for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026.

The company operates a rental fleet of approximately 1,300 of its own vocational trucks and provides parts and service support throughout the UK.

Its primary customers include UK local authorities, government agencies, and related contractors, all operating within road maintenance markets underpinned by statutory infrastructure-maintenance requirements in the UK.

Jonathan Lupton, managing director at Econ Engineering, said: "We are pleased to join Alamo Group and believe the combination provides an excellent foundation for the continued development of Econ.

"Alamo Group understands specialty equipment businesses and the importance of product quality, service and long-term customer relationships."

Following the acquisition, Econ Engineering will become part of Alamo Group’s Industrial Equipment Division.

The purchase was funded through Alamo Group’s revolving credit facility.

Alamo Group, listed on the New York Stock Exchange, manufactures and sells industrial and vegetation management equipment for sectors including infrastructure, public works, agriculture, and land maintenance.

Its portfolio includes vacuum trucks, street sweepers, snow removal equipment, and tractor-mounted mowing tools, as well as aftermarket parts and services.

The company operates two main divisions—Industrial Equipment and Vegetation Management—and employs around 3,800 people across 27 manufacturing facilities in North America, Europe, Canada, Brazil, and Australia.

Econ’s integration into Alamo Group is expected to strengthen the group’s UK presence and support further international growth.
