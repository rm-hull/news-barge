A woman was taken to hospital after a crash on a major road close to a North Yorkshire coastal town.

Cleveland Police said the crash happened on the A171 Whitby Road in Guisborough at 3pm on Wednesday, October 7, near the Waterfall Farm roundabout.




The 58-year-old woman driving the Hyundai suffered injuries to her pelvis, abdomen, and leg, as well as a suspected broken arm.

A force spokesperson said the vehicles involved were a black Ford Ranger travelling eastbound on the A171 towards Guisborough and a grey Hyundai heading in the opposite direction.

The driver of the Ford Ranger was not injured, police said.

Cleveland Police are appealing to anyone who witnessed the crash or who has dashcam footage to contact the force on 101 and quote reference 201077 when passing on information.
