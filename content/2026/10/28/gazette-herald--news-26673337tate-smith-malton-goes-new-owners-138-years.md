A BUSINESS which has been led by six generations of the same family over its 138 year history has changed hands.

Tate-Smith Ltd, which was set up in Malton in 1888, has been taken over by a new company.

The business is now owned and operated by Deckers Hospitality Group Ltd and will continue to trade as Tate-Smith Ltd.

Paul and Cate Tate-Smith are stepping away from the business, however their son, Harry will remain, helping to ensure business continues as usual.

All current staff will also remain in place and customers can expect the same renowned service, strengthened by new investment across the business and access to Deckers’ extensive expertise.

Derventio Wines is now a stand-alone business, run by Alex Danson from its premises on Yorkersgate in Malton.

Deckers is a family-run business based in Rochdale, run by a brother and sister team, Max Brierley and Victoria Cosgrove.

Paul Tate-Smith said: “We feel that Deckers is a great fit for Tate-Smith Ltd, sharing the ethos and ethical values that we have always sought to uphold. We have great confidence that our staff and customers will be in very safe hands going forward.”

Harry said: “I’m incredibly proud to be the sixth generation of our family business and to have the opportunity to help to lead it into an exciting new era under new ownership. It’s a privilege to build on the strong foundations laid by the generations before me, while embracing new ideas, opportunities, and ambitions for the future. I’m looking forward to honouring our heritage while helping shape the next chapter of the business.”

Max Brierley and Victoria Cosgrove said: “We are delighted to be taking on the stewardship of Tate-Smith Ltd, a business with such a proud heritage and strong reputation.

"We recognise the trust that generations of the Tate-Smith family have built with their customers and staff, and our priority is to look after that legacy.

"We look forward to working closely with the team, continuing to provide the service customers know and value, and investing in the business to support its long-term success by building on the strong foundations already in place. We are excited to be part of Tate-Smith’s next chapter.”

Founded in 1888 by Thomas Tate-Smith, who began bottling soft drinks ("aerated waters") from premises behind the Green Man Hotel in Malton and became one of the area's earliest bottlers of Guinness.

The business remained a family-run company across five generations, passing from Thomas to James, then Tom, then Tim, and later Paul.

Despite challenges from both World Wars and economic downturns, the company steadily expanded with a new factory built in 1936.

In 1960, the partnership became a limited Company and the brand name Sundella was introduced and registered as a trade mark.

Also during the 1960s, two old established family businesses, Longbottoms of Driffield and Wilkinson and Son of Malton, were taken over by Tate-Smith.

These years of expansion culminated, in 1969, in the move from Horsemarket Road to a new purpose-built factory at Sundella House, at Sheepfoot Hill, where the company is based today.
