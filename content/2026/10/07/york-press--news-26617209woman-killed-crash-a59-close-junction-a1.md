A WOMAN has died after a crash on a major North Yorkshire road this afternoon.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage after a woman was killed in a crash on the A59, near Flaxby, this afternoon (October 7) - close to the junction with the A1.

As reported by The Press earlier today, it happened at around 3.30pm with a road closure still in place into the evening.

The crash involved a black Vauxhall Corsa travelling towards Harrogate.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Sadly, a woman was pronounced dead at the scene. Our thoughts are with her loved ones at this very difficult time.

"The road remains closed while specialist collision investigators carry out examinations, and it is expected to remain closed for some time.

"Motorists are advised to avoid the area and use alternative routes where possible."

Officers said they are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the crash, saw the Corsa beforehand, or has relevant dashcam footage.

They added that they also trying to trace the driver of a white HGV travelling in the opposite direction at the time, as they may have information that could assist the investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to email [SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Please quote reference 12260197336 when providing information.
