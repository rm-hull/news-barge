A devoted husband and grandad has been remembered as "one of the kindest, gentlest men you could ever meet" following his death in a road collision.

David Reed, 51, from South Hylton, died after a black Mercedes-Benz AMG collided with his bicycle on Nissan Way in Washington at about 1am on Friday, September 11.

Emergency services attended the scene but he was sadly pronounced dead at the scene.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21443068.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Generic view of Nissan Way. *(Image: Google Maps)*

His family have described him as a "devoted husband, much-loved dad and grandad, cherished son, brother and uncle, and a true friend to so many".

In a statement, they said: "He was one of the kindest, gentlest men you could ever meet, with a heart of gold and an infectious laugh that so many of his friends will remember.

"Family was everything to David.

"He adored his grandchildren and would do absolutely anything for the people he loved.

"He was also Sunderland through and through, and his love for SAFC will forever be part of how we remember him.

"He was loved beyond words and will be missed every single day.

"There will forever be a David-shaped hole in our family.

"We love you more than you will ever know, David.

"Until we meet again."

Mr Reed worked in automotive manufacturing.

A 30-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and has been released on police bail pending further enquiries.

Northumbria Police said a full investigation is underway and officers are appealing for witnesses to come forward.

Sergeant Greg Huntley, of Northumbria Police, said: "The thoughts of everyone within Northumbria Police remain firmly with David’s family and friends at this extremely difficult time.

"We have carried out a variety of enquiries since the collision took place on the morning of September 11 and would reiterate that we would like to hear from anybody with information who has not yet contacted us.

"If you have details which could assist us in our enquiries then please get in touch."

Anyone with information or dashcam footage from the moments leading up to the collision is asked to contact Northumbria Police.

Mr Reed’s family also expressed their gratitude to the emergency services and everyone who has supported them since the incident.
