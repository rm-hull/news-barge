Police are appealing for witnesses following a bizarre late-night incident involving a car.

The vehicle, parked on Kirkgate in Shipley, was covered in eggs and flour and left with a cracked windscreen.

The incident happened between 10pm last night and 5am this morning (Wednesday).

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21551000.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kirkgate, Shipley, where the incident took place *(Image: Google Maps)*

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We have received a report of a parked car being damaged in Kirkgate, Shipley, some time between 10pm last night and 5am this morning.

"The windscreen was cracked and the car was extensively covered in eggs and flour across the glass, roof and bodywork."

Anyone who witnessed the incident or who has any information that could assist in identifying those responsible is urged to contact officers quoting crime reference 13260570469.
