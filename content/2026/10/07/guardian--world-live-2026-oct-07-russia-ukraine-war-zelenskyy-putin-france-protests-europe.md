Russia 'pursuing a deliberate strategy of terror,' EU's von der Leyen says

The European Commission president Ursula von der Leyen condemned the latest round of Russian attacks on Ukraine, blaming Moscow for “pursuing a deliberate strategy of terror.”

Ukraine’s agriculture minister said his country would not agree to EU membership if that would entail permanent restrictions on farm subsidies and exports of agricultural products.

The response comes a day after the European Commission proposed a special arrangement that would limit financial support and restrict exports, such as wheat, from Ukraine.

“Transitional periods are a normal part of EU enlargement... Permanent restrictions on access to the single market are an entirely different matter,” Taras Vysotsky said in a statement to AFP.

“Ukraine cannot agree to such an approach.”

The government in Kyiv had not yet received any official proposal, which is due to be discussed by EU leaders at a summit next week, he added.

Russia’s health agency said on Wednesday that it saw “no epidemic risks” following the reported plague death of a laboratory technician in Siberia last week, as Moscow faced mounting calls for transparency.

“More than 90 percent of contacts have completed medical observation. No health disorders associated with infectious pathology were detected,” health watchdog Rospotrebnadzor said in a statement, stressing that “there are no epidemic risks for the population of Irkutsk and the Irkutsk region”.

France's Kagan and Japan's Soai awarded the 2026 Nobel chemistry prize

French chemist Henri Kagan and Japanese chemist Kensō Soai have been awarded the 2026 Nobel prize in chemistry “for the discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.”

Here’s more from the organisers:

“Henri Kagan discovered a new way of manipulating chemical reactions, allowing a greater excess of one of the mirror images to be created than was previously assumed possible. His discovery has been revolutionary for chemists who develop reactions for the manufacture of pharmaceuticals, flavours, scents and new materials.

Kenso Soai designed the first chemical reaction in which only one of the potential mirror images was formed. Other than life itself, no one had ever achieved this feat. The Soai reaction is one of the most spectacular chemical experiments ever conducted.”

At least 11 dead, 34 injured after 'vile' attack on Ukraine on Putin's birthday, Zelenskyy says

Jakub Krupa

Ukraine’s president Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia launched “one fo the most vile strikes” on Ukraine last night, targeting the country’s energy infrastructure overnight.

At least 11 people were killed, and more than 34 injured in a series of attacks on Pryluky, Dnipro, Kremenchuk, Zaporizhzhia, and Kyiv, which Zelenskyy ironically called “a birthday gift for Putin” who turns 74 today.

Rescuers work at a site of an apartment building damaged by a Russian missile strike in the town of Pryluky, Chernihiv region, Ukraine. Photograph: State Emergency Service Of Ukraine/Reuters

“Russian ballistic missiles remain the greatest challenge. And air defense packages for us right now are a vital necessity. That is why every day we are looking for missiles for Patriot systems that can and must actually protect people’s lives here, in Ukrainian skies,” Zelenskyy said.

Ukraine’s foreign minister Andrii Sybiha went further, saying:

“On Putin’s birthday, Russian generals brought him his favorite present: war crimes.”

He added:

“What Russian generals fail to tell Putin is that he is not winning this war and never will. …

I urge all partners to give Putin real presents that will help him finally choose diplomacy over war. Stronger sanctions on his military-industrial complex, including facilities producing ballistic missiles and the Rassvet satellite system, as well as stronger support for Ukraine, including all kinds of air defence and energy assistance ahead of winter.

But his ultimate present will be a dock in The Hague. We are preparing the Special Tribunal for the Crime of Aggression Against Ukraine for practical launch already next year. Putin and his friends should know that accountability is inevitable.”
