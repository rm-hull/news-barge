Dramatic pictures show the scale of a devastating fire which tore through the former Billingham Library.

Huge flames can be seen engulfing the building as thick plumes of black smoke billow into the sky above Billingham town centre on Tuesday evening (October 6).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21547557.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services on the scene of the fire at the former Billingham Library *(Image: SUBMIT)*

Several fire engines and police vehicles can be seen surrounding the former library, while firefighters direct their hoses at the building.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21547561.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services on the scene of the fire at the former Billingham Library *(Image: SUBMIT)*

Cleveland Fire Brigade confirmed it was dealing with a building fire at Queensway and urged people to stay away.

Cleveland Police also attended to support firefighters, with road closures in place at Causeway and Kingsway.

People living close to the scene were advised to keep their doors and windows closed while the incident was ongoing.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21547563.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services on the scene of the fire at the former Billingham Library *(Image: SUBMIT)*

The emergency response continued into Wednesday morning (October 7), although the situation has eased.

Cleveland Fire Brigade said one fire engine remained at the former library as crews carried out damping-down work.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21547565.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services on the scene of the fire at the former Billingham Library *(Image: SUBMIT)*

A Cleveland Fire Brigade spokesperson said: "One fire appliance remains in attendance at the scene of the fire at the former Billingham Library.

"Crews are carrying out damping-down operations, checking the remains of the building and dealing with any remaining hotspots to prevent the fire from reigniting."

The majority of the road closures introduced during the emergency have now been lifted.

A police spokesperson said: "The majority of road closures around Billingham Old Library have now been lifted.

The cause of the fire has not yet been confirmed.
