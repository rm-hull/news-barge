The historic hotel, set in 26 acres of landscaped grounds, attracts visitors seeking spa breaks, weddings and countryside escapes.

Redworth Hall Hotel, near Newton Aycliffe, is a four-star Jacobean country house dating back to 1693.

The Grade II-listed manor combines original period features with modern facilities, including 143 bedrooms, a spa and leisure club, restaurant, event spaces and wedding venues.

The Retreat Spa offers ESPA massages, facials and other treatments designed to help guests unwind.

The leisure club also has a swimming pool, Jacuzzi, sauna and steam room.

Wedding couples can choose from ceremony and reception spaces including the historic Great Hall, with vaulted ceilings and a minstrels' gallery, and the Prince Bishop Suite, with chandeliers and a private bar.

The venue is licensed for weddings and civil partnerships, making it a popular choice for celebrations across the North East.

Food is another major draw; the hotel's 1744 Restaurant focuses on seasonal and locally sourced ingredients, serving dishes ranging from beef and chianti croquettes to miso-baked cod, alongside traditional Sunday roasts, afternoon tea and private dining experiences.

The hotel has a 4.2-star Google rating from almost 3,000 reviews, with guests praising its setting and hospitality.

One recent guest described a weekend in a deluxe spa room as “genuinely special”.

They wrote: "The room was spotlessly clean, the bed was one of the most comfortable I've slept in, and the shower was absolutely brilliant.

“The grounds are stunning, particularly in the sunshine, and gave the whole stay a really special feel.”
