A fire crew came to the rescue when a child got stuck into playtime a little too much.

They were called to a home in **Thorpe Willoughby** near Selby at**6.03pm yesterday** (Tuesday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Selby responded to a child with their fingers accidentally stuck in a wooden toy.

“Crews were able to release the child’s fingers safely using small tools.

“The child had no injuries and the child was left in the care of family members.”
