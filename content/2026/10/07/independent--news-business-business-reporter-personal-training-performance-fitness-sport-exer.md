*Ultimate Performance is a Business Reporter client*

For more than 15 years, Ultimate Performance (U.P.) has been the global benchmark in personal training.

U.P. was founded in 2009 in response to an industry with few consistent standards and scant accountability for what clients could expect in return for their time and money.

Its vision was to give time-poor executives a place where experts would manage every detail of their health and fitness, remove the guesswork and deliver measurable results – while also giving ambitious trainers a genuine career path built around mastering their craft and achieving those results for clients.

This was a model radically different from the status quo – one where trainers were held to professional standards and judged purely on the quality of results they achieved, not on the number of sessions completed or memberships sold.

From a basement gym in East London, Ultimate Performance has grown to become the world’s only global personal training business with private one-to-one personal training facilities across the UK and Europe, North America, the Middle East, Asia and Australia.

More than 30,000 clients have transformed their bodies and their health with U.P. No other fitness business in the world delivers results at this scale with consistency and quality.

Behind these results is the U.P. Method – a system meticulously refined over more than 15 years.

Much like a wealth manager oversees a client’s entire financial life, a dedicated U.P. trainer takes complete ownership of every detail that shapes their health and fitness – managing their training, nutrition, lifestyle and progress so they don’t have to figure it out alone.

Every decision is guided by data. Trainers track more than 50 metrics and biomarkers, so that progress is clear and measurable.

But data alone doesn’t deliver results. The systems of accountability at U.P. play a central role.

Trainers keep their clients accountable and on track, seven days a week – supporting, coaching through challenges and adapting the plan when life gets busy. Behind the trainer, mentors, managers and senior leaders guide and help them stay focused on their clients’ outcomes.

As Sean Murphy, U.P.’s Chief Personal Training Officer, explains: “I don’t think you can underestimate the power of having someone in your corner who is really invested in what you want to achieve versus a book, an app or AI.”

The U.P. Method depends on the people delivering it.

That’s why U.P. runs the most rigorous recruitment process in the industry to select the best candidates from around the world. It’s a six-stage process where only 1 in 100 applicants make the cut.

New trainers complete an intensive education programme to learn the U.P. Method and how to deliver world-class results, followed by more than 200 hours of advanced education, mentoring and training each year.

Unlike a self-employed trainer juggling coaching, marketing and the constant hunt for new business, a U.P. trainer is free to focus entirely on their craft and their clients’ results. And their pay, bonuses and progression depend on the quality of those results, so the trainer is as invested in the goal as the client.

The result is a genuinely personal service where responsibility is shared. The client commits to the process. The trainer provides the expertise, structure and accountability. And U.P. provides the environment, systems and standards to bring it all together.

That is why Ultimate Performance has been the global benchmark in personal training for more than 15 years.
