A man wanted for allegedly failing to attend court over drug dealing offences could be in York.

Noel Abdullah, 20, is also wanted on suspicion of assault and immigration offences, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said Abdullah is believed to be in London but also has links to York, Leeds and Durham.

A police spokesperson said “extensive enquiries” have been ongoing to locate Abdullah.

They said officers are urging anyone with information about Abdullah’s whereabouts to get in touch.

Police said Abdullah is described as a black man, who is known to change his hair from braided to unbraided.

Anyone who sees Abdullah is asked to call 999 and those with information about his whereabouts should call 101.

Information can be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference number 12250044962 when providing any information to police or to Crimestoppers.
