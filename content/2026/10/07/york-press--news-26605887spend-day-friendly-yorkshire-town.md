## How to spend a day in the Yorkshire town named among friendliest to retire to

From cafés to shopping, here is how you can spend some time in Beverley. *(Image: Getty)*

The town of Beverley was recently named as one of the friendliest places to retire to in England.

World Atlas added the town into its list of the seven friendliest towns to retire to thanks to their “warm English welcome” for retirees.

If you have never been to the town before, you might need some guidance on what to do during a visit or if you move there.

Here is how to spend a perfect day in the Yorkshire town of Beverley.

## How to spend a day in Beverley

Any good day is started by going for a coffee so you can pop into Blu Luna.

The café offers lots of hot drinks as well as matchas, smoothies, and whipped coffee.

Blu Luna has a rating of 4.4 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with visitors praising its “delicious coffee and pastries” and the “warm welcome” the staff give you.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21536226.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can start your day at Blu Luna. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

After enjoying your coffee, you might want to head over to the Beverley Minster that has been around for centuries and is described as “one of the most beautiful Gothic buildings in Europe”.

You can either enjoy the minster yourself or book a guided tour to learn more about the area’s history, or pick the roof tour where you will get to take in the views of Beverley from the top of the building.

You can also spend some time shopping, as Beverley has plenty of big-name brands and independent shops to explore.

You can stop off at Flemingate Shopping Centre, which has a range of shops including a Dunelm, H&M, and a Baytree Interiors.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21536228.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Westwood Restaurant featured in the Michelin Guide. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Around the town, you can find shops like Mimi Boutique that sells unique clothes and jewellery or the Yorkshire Soap Company if you are looking for some bath and body items to pamper yourself with.

To end the day, you can go for dinner at The Westwood Restaurant that has featured in the Michelin Guide.

The restaurant has over 1,000 reviews on Tripadvisor with people saying the food is “perfect” and “beautifully presented”.
