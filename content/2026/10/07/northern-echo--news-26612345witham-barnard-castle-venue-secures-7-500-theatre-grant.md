A County Durham arts venue has secured £7,500 to upgrade its main performance space as part of a nationwide funding programme supporting theatres.

The Witham in Barnard Castle is one of 13 venues across England, Scotland and Wales sharing £88,000 from Theatres Trust's Small Grants Programme, backed by The Linbury Trust.

The funding will be used to upgrade the lighting in the venue's main performance area, enhancing the experience for audiences, supporting visiting artists and reducing energy consumption through more efficient LED technology.

The Witham was one of several theatres recognised in the latest round of grants, which support projects aimed at improving a venue's sustainability, accessibility and long-term future.

A spokesperson for The Witham said: “We're delighted to receive this £7,500 grant, which will help us upgrade the lighting in our main performance space.

“These improvements will enhance the experience for audiences, support visiting artists and reduce our energy consumption through more efficient LED technology.

“As an independent community venue, investment like this plays an important role in helping us continue to provide a vibrant programme of arts, culture and events for people across Teesdale and beyond.”

The Small Grants Programme helps not-for-profit theatres carry out practical projects that strengthen their resilience, sustainability, accessibility and audience reach.

Theatres Trust said the latest round of funding reflected the wide variety of theatres benefiting from investment, from small community venues to larger regional theatres.
