Firefighters went to the aid of a child whose fingers had got wedged in a wooden toy.

The alarm was raised after the incident happened on Tuesday, October 6 at 6.03pm in Thorpe Willoughby.

**Recommended reads:**

Firefighters from Selby used small tools to free the child, who did not suffer any injuries and was left in the care of their family.
