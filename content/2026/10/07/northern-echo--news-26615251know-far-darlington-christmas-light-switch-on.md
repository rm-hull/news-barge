It won't be long before Christmas arrives in Darlington and plans have now been revealed for this year's festive lights switch-on.

Darlington Borough Council has confirmed that the town's annual Christmas Lights Switch On will return to Market Square on Sunday, November 15.

The free event is expected to attract thousands of visitors and will feature live entertainment, music, special guests and a fireworks display as the countdown to Christmas begins.

If you're planning on heading into town, here's everything we know so far.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21549322.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Darlington's Christmas lights switch on last year *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

### When will the Christmas lights switch-on take place?

The event will take place in Market Square on Sunday, November 15.

Festivities will get underway from 4pm, with entertainment and live music taking place throughout the afternoon.

The main countdown will take place shortly before 5.30pm when the town's Christmas lights will be switched on.

### Will there be a fireworks display?

Yes.

A celebratory fireworks display will take place immediately after the lights are illuminated, helping officially welcome the festive season to Darlington.

Visitors who are sensitive to loud noises are being advised that ear defenders may be useful during the event, particularly during the fireworks display.

### Who will be appearing at the event?

Darlington Borough Council has confirmed there will be live music, festive entertainment and special guests throughout the afternoon.

However, the full programme and details of this year's special guests have not yet been announced.

Further details are expected to be released closer to the event.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21549323.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Darlington's Christmas lights switch on last year *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

### How much does it cost?

The Christmas Lights Switch On is free to attend.

The event will take place in the heart of the town centre at Market Square.

### Is the event accessible?

Yes.

An accessible viewing area will be available near the stage for wheelchair users and people with physical impairments.

The dedicated area has been designed to allow visitors to enjoy the entertainment and countdown in comfort.

### What facilities will be available?

Public toilets will be available at both the Dolphin Centre and Market Hall, with additional portable toilets also being provided on site.

Cycle parking is available around Market Square and taxi drivers will be able to drop visitors close to the event.

Darlington railway station is around a 10-minute walk from the town centre, while numerous bus services stop nearby.

### What can visitors expect?

According to Darlington Borough Council, visitors can expect an afternoon of "festive fun" featuring live entertainment, music and guest appearances as excitement builds towards the switch-on.

With large crowds expected to descend on the town centre once again, residents are being encouraged to arrive early to secure their spot to enjoy the festivities.
