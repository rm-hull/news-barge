There’s been major rail disruption today after the line between York and Newcastle was blocked.

Services were unable to travel between the two cities due to a person being hit by a train this morning.

The line has now reopened but disruption is expected until mid afternoon.

Earlier, train operator LNER said: “The emergency services are dealing with an incident near the railway between York and Newcastle / Sunderland / Middlesbrough.

“Whilst they conduct their work all lines are closed. As a result, trains running between these stations may be cancelled, delayed or revised.”

Major disruption is expected until 1pm. More here.

#### CrossCountry / Grand Central customer advice

Your ticket can be used at no extra cost on the following services:

* The two CrossCountry services operating before and after your cancelled service
* East Midlands Railway services where required
* LNER services where required
* Northern services where required
* TransPennine Express services where required

If you decide to travel from a different station due to this disruption and need to return to that station later, you will also be able to use your existing ticket to do this at no extra cost

Unfortunately, any seat reservations will not be carried over to another train, but Coach B, H and L are the unreserved coaches on CrossCountry services (where available) so please head to them.

Road transport has been requested to run between York and Newcastle.

One full size coach has been confirmed to head to Newcastle, estimated to depart at 10:30.
