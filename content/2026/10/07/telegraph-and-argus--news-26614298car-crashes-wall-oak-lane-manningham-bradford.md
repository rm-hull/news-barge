Two people were injured and a section of wall was reduced to rubble after a car reportedly crashed into it in Bradford last night.

The crash happened just after 10pm on Tuesday on Oak Lane in Manningham.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 10.19pm last night (October 6) to report a road traffic incident on Oak Lane in Bradford.

"A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including two ambulances and a team leader, and two patients were conveyed to hospital."

Rubble was on the pavement outside a home on Oak Lane this morning.

Telegraph & Argus photographs showed the damage to the wall.

Social media reports claimed the crash involved a car reportedly being pursued by police.

It is not known whether anyone was injured or arrested.

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for further information.
