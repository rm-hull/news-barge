A car was left on its side on the hard shoulder of the A1(M) in North Yorkshire after a crash yesterday afternoon (October 6), which saw firefighters, ambulance and police attend.

Emergency services were called to the southbound carriageway, near Ripon, at around 5.30pm, following reports of a single-vehicle smash.

Firefighters from Bedale, Northallerton and Ripon all attended.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "Bedale, Northallerton and Ripon responded to a single-vehicle road traffic collision with a vehicle stuck on its side, resting on the hard shoulder.
