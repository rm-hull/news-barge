A PERSON has sustained a 'deep cut' after a crash between a car and a scooter in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for witnesses after a crash involving a car and a scooter that took place in Whitby last Thursday evening (October 1).

The incident happened at 7.40pm in Church Street/Green Lane and involved a white Volvo travelling northbound and a Lexmoto scooter heading south.

A spokesperson for the force said: "The driver of the Volvo stopped briefly at the scene before leaving.

"The rider of the scooter sustained a deep cut and other minor injuries."

Police said they are appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact them.

Anyone with information is asked to email [Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk), report through the website/live chat, call 101, pass information to Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260193285 when providing information.
