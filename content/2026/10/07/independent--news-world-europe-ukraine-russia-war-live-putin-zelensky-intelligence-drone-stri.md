## Moscow behind sinking of ship off Bulgaria, Zelensky says investigation shows

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has blamed Moscow for the sinking of a merchant ship in the Black Sea off Bulgaria.

The crew was missing after the vessel sank off Nato member Bulgaria following a drone strike that Prime Minister Rumen Radev branded a gross violation of international law.

President Zelensky said an investigation jointly with Bulgaria's navy showed that Russia was behind the attack ⁠by air and sea drones.

Shipping sources said it appeared to be the first such attack in the waters of Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone.

Radev had said it was unclear whether the drones came from Russia or from Ukraine, the BTA news agency reported.

Bulgaria does not border either country but it holds a strategic position in the ⁠Black Sea, where both Ukraine and Russia have major ports.

## Ukraine shifts to AI robot guns to tackle Russian drones

Ukraine has shifted to using AI-enabled robot guns to intercept and take down Russian drones, said the country’s air force on Monday.

“Robotic turrets are now being installed on bridges” to respond to Russia’s newer jet-powered drones, said air force spokesperson Yuri Ignat.

“There have already been shoot-downs" of the drones, he said, including Russia's bigger and faster model.

The technology uses “optical vision and artificial intelligence to eliminate the human factor and deliver strikes,” Ignat told AFP.
