Police have released CCTV footage of a person they want to speak to following a theft in York.

The incident occurred on July 21, 2026 at Inner Space Station, Hull Road, York.

North Yorkshire Police are urging anyone who recognises the person in the CCTV to come forward, as they may have crucial information to aid the investigation.

**Recommended reads:**

They did not share details of what was stolen, or its value.

Information can be reported online at the North Yorkshire Police website, or those unable to do so can call 101 with reference 12260143246.

Alternatively, Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111, or through their website.
