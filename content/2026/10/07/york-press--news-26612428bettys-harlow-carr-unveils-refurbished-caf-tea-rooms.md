## First look at revamped restaurant in Harrogate after month-long closure

Bettys Café Tea Rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr is now welcoming visitors once again after a month-long refurbishment *(Image: Bettys)*

A tea room in Harrogate has reopened following a month-long refurbishment.

Bettys Café Tea Rooms at RHS Garden Harlow Carr is now welcoming visitors once again with refreshed interiors and a new Bettys To-Go Hatch at the entrance.

The hatch offers visitors the chance to pick up takeaway treats before exploring the gardens.

Graeme Sharp, branch manager at Bettys Harlow Carr, said: "We are delighted to be welcoming people back into our new-look Bettys at RHS Harlow Carr.

"We have a very unique location here on the edge of the RHS Garden, and we hope the changes we have made will enhance our customers' experience of both our Café Tea Rooms and the garden itself."

Table bookings are now available, making it easier for families to plan their visit, particularly later in the afternoon.

Liz Thwaite, head of site at RHS Garden Harlow Carr, said: "Bettys has long been an important part of the visitor experience at RHS Harlow Carr, so we're thrilled to see the new-look Café Tea Rooms unveiled, ready for the autumn season.

"With the garden looking particularly spectacular at this time of year, visitors can enjoy a fantastic day out, taking in the seasonal colour before relaxing and enjoying all that Bettys has to offer."
