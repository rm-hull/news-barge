Leeds United fans recorded one of the highest numbers of arrests in English football last season.

A total of 58 arrests were linked to Leeds supporters during the 2025/26 season, placing the club in the national top 10, according to analysis by 101 Great Goals using Home Office figures.

Jon Fisher, football analyst at 101 Great Goals, said: "Football should be something that brings people together, and the overwhelming majority of supporters attend matches simply to enjoy the game and support their team.

"However, these figures are a reminder that a small number of incidents can have a serious impact on other fans, staff and the wider community.

"Nobody should go to a football match expecting to feel unsafe or caught up in disorder."

Leeds was level with Arsenal on 58 arrests, behind Manchester City, Chelsea, Millwall and Manchester United.

Across all clubs, the Home Office recorded 1,695 arrests during the season.
