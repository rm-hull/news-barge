Knaresborough boutique Sheer Bliss has won a free advertising campaign worth up to £1,000 on Your Harrogate.

The business, owned by Jayne Winn, has been named the winner of a competition giving local businesses the chance to reach more customers.

Your Harrogate’s team will now work with Sheer Bliss to create a campaign tailored to the boutique, helping showcase its offering to thousands of people across the local area.

Based on Knaresborough's High Street, Sheer Bliss specialises in lingerie, swimwear and leisurewear, with a free bra-fitting service.

Your Harrogate's Ricky Durkin visited Sheer Bliss to surprise owner Jayne Winn.

She said:

> "That's amazing, thank you so much! I'm so grateful.
>
> "We are a lingerie and swimwear fitting boutique. We've been here for 15 years and we've got a lovely clientele and all sorts of garments for everyone, from an A cup to a K cup."

The monthly competition invited business owners to share details about their businesses for the chance to win.

It's open to both new businesses looking to build awareness and established names hoping to increase footfall or introduce their products and services to a wider audience.
