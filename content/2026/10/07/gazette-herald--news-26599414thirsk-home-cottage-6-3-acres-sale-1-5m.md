A seven-bedroom property complete with a cottage and outbuilding is on the market for £1,500,000.

The property, Wheel House Farm in Sowerby, Thirsk, has been described as a period barn conversion and has land extending to approximately 6.3 acres.

It also has good road and rail links nearby, according to Lister Haigh, the estate agents selling the property.

The estate agents have said the new owners could use the cottage for "multi-generational living" or to "generate a healthy income".

Features throughout the property include exposed beams, trusses, and brickwork.

The estate agents said there is spacious accommodation throughout and the property is in a private semi-rural location.

There are four bedrooms and two bathrooms in the main house, and three bedrooms and one bathroom in the cottage.

The main house has a reception hall, cloakroom, sitting room, dining room, living kitchen, family room/gym, study, and utility room.

The cottage has a reception hall, living kitchen with sitting and dining areas, and a house bathroom.

There are private south-facing courtyard gardens for both properties, as well as a large parking area.

The barn is currently used for storage but could be put to a variety of uses.

The land and paddocks would be ideal for anyone with equestrian interests.

The property is surrounded by open countryside two miles south of Thirsk, which has a range of amenities including schools, shops, a racecourse, and a train station with direct links to York and London King's Cross.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact Lister Haigh on 01423 427169.
