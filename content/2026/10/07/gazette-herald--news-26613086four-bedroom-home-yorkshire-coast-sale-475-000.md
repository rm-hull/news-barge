A home with panoramic sea views has hit the market on the North Yorkshire coast.

The four-bedroom house is for sale in Killerby Cliff, Cayton Bay, near Scarborough, for £475,000.

Hunters, which is marketing the property, said it offers "amazing sea and castle views".

Inside, two bedrooms are on the ground floor, as well as the open plan living kitchen and family bathroom.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21546457.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The four-bedroom home for sale in Killerby Cliff, Cayton Bay, near Scarborough, for £475,000 *(Image: Hunters/Zoopla)*

Two more bedrooms are on the first floor, with the master bedroom benefitting from floor to ceiling windows and a balcony looking out to the sea.

Outside is a lawned garden, a hot tub and off-street parking.

There is also a converted garage currently used as a games room.

A Hunters spokesperson said: "Being situated in close proximity to the highly sought after village of Cayton you have access to a wealth of local amenities including two convenience stores, post office, popular junior school and secondary school, two public houses and an eating and drinking establishment.

"A little further away is Seamer train station and in close proximity of major employers and industrial estates making it a perfect place for commuting to work."
