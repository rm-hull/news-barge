Jane McDonald is inviting fans to join her for one last week of luxury at sea.

The singer and television presenter, from West Yorkshire, has announced a third and final cruise, Cruise With Jane McDonald 2027, which will set sail from the London Cruise Terminal in Tilbury on September 24, 2027.

The seven-night, round-trip voyage will visit Cobh, Belfast and Cherbourg, with fans promised an experience packed with entertainment and special appearances.

Ms McDonald, of Wakefield, said: "This is not a drill.

"Next year will be the final outing for Cruise with Jane McDonald, so if you want to join me for one last time, please jump onboard and secure your cabin now.

"You all know I love cruising, and I love seeing all of you, so please do join me as we set sail for what will be a very special final fling together at sea."

## Details of the Jane McDonald cruise

Travellers will sail onboard Ambassador Cruise Line’s Ambience and enjoy full-board dining, a Captain’s Gala Dinner, the Baked Alaska Parade, and a Captain’s cocktail party.

Guests will also have access to the ship’s spa, gym, swimming pool, and hot tubs.

Entertainment will be central to the experience, with Ms McDonald performing two headline shows during the cruise.

She will also make special appearances throughout the week, hosting bingo, quizzes, and other events.

Irish country singer Nathan Carter and drag performer Mary Mac are among the special guests expected to join her onboard.

## Who is Jane McDonald?

Ms McDonald first rose to fame in 1998 in the BBC docusoap The Cruise.

Since then, she has sold out venues including the Royal Albert Hall, The London Palladium, and The MGM Grand in Las Vegas.

She has released 11 studio albums, and her latest, Living The Dream, topped the Independent Albums Chart and reached the Top 10 in the Official Albums Chart.

Recorded in Nashville, the album also reached Number Two in the Official Country Chart.

In May, Ms McDonald headlined the main stage at London’s Mighty Hoopla festival, and she recently completed a 20-date UK tour.

She is also known for her work on television, presenting the BAFTA Award-winning Channel 5 series Cruising with Jane McDonald and the recent series Jane McDonald: From Pole to Pole.

Earlier this year, she also fronted a two-part documentary, Jane McDonald in Nashville, which followed her journey to the US to record her latest album.

## How much is the cruise?

Cruise With Jane McDonald is presented by Hello Sailor and Ambassador Cruise Lines.

Prices start from £899 per person, based on two people sharing an inside cabin.

Guests booking during the pre-sale can upgrade to the Ambassador Fare, which includes the top Expedition drinks package, for an additional £149 per person.

This package covers all premium and house brand spirits and wines, premium teas and coffees, unlimited soft drinks, and all gratuities and service charges.

Cabins can be reserved now by signing up at CruiseWithJaneMcDonald.com to access the exclusive online pre-sale, which opens on October 9.

General sale begins at 10am on October 12.

For more information, visit CruiseWithJaneMcDonald.com or call 0808 304 2404.
