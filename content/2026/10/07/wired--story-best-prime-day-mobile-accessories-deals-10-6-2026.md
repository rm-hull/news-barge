Phones are so good these days that there’s little reason to upgrade every year. In fact, the best Android smartphones and iPhones will last up to seven years, if not more. What needs refreshing are accessories. That case is likely breaking, you need more storage, or you want a longer-lasting battery. Perhaps you just want something to personalize your phone to make it stand out in a sea of sameness.

Whatever the reason, Amazon Prime Day is a good time to stock up on accessories to give your phone and other mobile devices a new lease on life. Note that from our research, prices on electronics have gone up across the board, and we aren't seeing deals as good as we've seen in the past (we've pointed those out wherever we can). Still, a sale is a sale, and if you need something, it's a good time to buy it. Here are some of our recommended products that are on sale right now.
