A Shildon salon owner who swapped healthcare for aesthetics says she is “amazed” after her business was shortlisted for a Northern Echo beauty award less than a year after opening.

Buda Beauty, on Church Street in Shildon, has been named a finalist in the Northern Echo Best Beauty Salon Awards, just months after welcoming its first customers in February.

The salon is owned by Nokhu Ishemunyoro, who runs it with colleague Jessica Aldworth.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21546533.jpg?type=mds-article-620)BUDA Beauty, Shildon pictured Jessica Aldworth & Nokhu Ishemunyoro. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Before opening Buda Beauty at 28 Church Street, Nokhu worked in healthcare as a nurse before switching careers to pursue her passion for beauty and aesthetics.

The 42-year-old said: "I used to work in healthcare with other businesses and worked as a nurse years ago.

"But then I came into the beauty industry because I had a passion for it.

"The products that are available and the aesthetic treatments available, that's where the passion fell."

The salon offers facials, skin boosters and corrective aesthetic treatments, focusing on enhancing clients' natural features.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21546534.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Speaking about the nomination, Nokhu said: "It feels amazing.

"I've got Jess working with me as well.

"In terms of what we're doing, we are of the same mind.

"Both of us come from a Christian background in what we believe, so the natural side of things in terms of holistic care really means so much to us.

"We're both so happy about it.

"It means a lot.

"It's been amazing just to be nominated since we haven't been open that long.

"We're just happy to serve the community and bring the best versions out of the men and women in our community."

Nokhu said Buda Beauty aims to help people feel confident rather than dramatically change their appearance.

The recognition is another milestone for the growing Shildon business, which has quickly built a loyal customer base since opening earlier this year.

The salon offers facials, boosters, corrective treatments and more.
