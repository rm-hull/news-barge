A theatre troupe will perform their comedy play featuring four actors playing 20 characters.

The Right Track Theatre group, formed by recent drama school graduates, is set to perform their show, "Meteor, Incoming!" at various venues, including York Mount Leisure Centre Theatre on November 3 and 6.

The play, set in a small-town Comic Con, follows the mishaps of superfan Matt, who is mistaken for famous actor Dick Chase.

A series of scandals and comic misadventures unfold as Matt and his friend Larna try to save Chase's reputation.

The group, who have previously entertained audiences on heritage railways and at the Camden Fringe Festival, aim to revive the farce genre with a modern touch.

The play is directed and co-written by South Milford native Ben Cawood, who is also the artistic director and founder of Right Track Theatre.

"Meteor, Incoming!" will also be performed at Scarborough Old Parcels, Durham Fringe Academy, and South Milford WI Hall.
