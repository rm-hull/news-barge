There were long delays after two people were injured in a major crash on the M62 this morning.

The crash happened between Junctions 22 and 23, prompting a huge emergency response and lengthy delays.

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 6.37am this morning to report a road traffic collision on the M62 between junctions 22 and 23.

"Three ambulances and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and two patients were conveyed to hospital."

National Highways reported there were delays of 40 minutes and four miles of congestion.

In a post on X earlier this morning, National Highways: North-East said: "The M62 in West Yorkshire westbound between Junction 23 and 22 has lanes two and three closed due to a collision.

"Emergency services are on scene.

"Delays are in excess of 40 minutes with four miles of congestion."

It is not known at this point if anyone was injured in the crash or how many vehicles were involved.

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for further details.
