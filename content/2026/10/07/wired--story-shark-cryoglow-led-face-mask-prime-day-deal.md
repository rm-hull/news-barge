## Shark CryoGlow LED Face Mask for $299 ($51 Off)

The Shark CryoGlow is a personal favorite of mine, as it takes the guesswork out of light therapy. Pick your goal—Blemish Repair or Better Aging—and the mask handles the rest, automatically switching between wavelengths and timing each treatment. Blemish Repair combines blue, red, and infrared light in an eight-minute session, and Better Aging uses red and infrared light for six minutes.

After eight weeks of consistent use, Shark recommends switching to the four-minute Skin Sustain mode to maintain the results. It comes with a controller, and I especially love the cooling under-eye pads that can be used during treatment or on their own to alleviate puffiness.

The best part, though, is that we actually noticed a difference. After eight weeks of consistent use, contributor Verity Burns said her perioral dermatitis cleared faster than usual, and her skin looked plumper and brighter. An aesthetician we spoke to also scanned her skin before and after the treatment, and they found less inflammation and hyperpigmentation and an improvement in fine lines.
