Further to the answers already published in response to the question, I can add my own experience.

Advertisement

Between my teens and my early sixties, I suffered occasional migraine attacks. These were preceded by a changing set of symptoms: auras, partial sight loss, difficulty reading, light and sound sensitivity, etc. At one time, I would experience odours for which there was no discernible cause.

Reading around, I found references to these olfactory illusions as a possible precursor of migraine, which was somewhat reassuring.
