A historic pub in York is undergoing a six-figure refurbishment.

The Golden Lion, one of York's oldest pubs, is temporarily closed for an autumn makeover.

Situated in Church Street near the renowned Shambles, the Greene King-owned establishment shut its doors to the public on October 5 to commence the renovations.

The revamp, scheduled to finish later this month, promises to maintain the pub's historic charm while introducing modern-day comforts.

The project will see improvements both inside and out, aimed at enhancing the pub's allure.

A Greene King spokesperson said: "There will be improvements both inside and out to boost the pub’s appeal."

The Golden Lion, a favourite among tourists, families, and sports enthusiasts, will continue to offer a diverse menu catering to various tastes and dietary needs.

Patrons can look forward to enjoying drinks with colleagues, lunch after a city tour, or catching live sports on Sky Sports and TNT Sports.

The pub's famed live music and DJ nights are set to make a comeback.

Its roof terrace, ideal for outdoor drinks and dining, also remains a key feature.

The Golden Lion is actively seeking new, talented people to join its bar and kitchen teams, offering both full- and part-time job opportunities.

Interested individuals can apply directly through Greene King's official channels.

Customers can also pre-book their visits and explore the menu via the Greene King app, ready for when the pub reopens.

Greene King, founded in 1799, is a leading pub company and brewer in the UK, operating around 2,500 pubs, restaurants, and hotels across England, Wales, and Scotland.

With offices in Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk, and Burton on Trent, Staffordshire, the company employs approximately 38,000 people.

It runs three divisions: Managed Pubs, Pub Partners, and Brewing & Brands.

More details about the Golden Lion's reopening and bookings can be found on its official website.

The Golden Lion blends historic charm and modern updates and is set to welcome back its patrons soon, offering a refreshed experience while retaining its beloved character.

As the refurbishment takes place, the pub remains a testament to Greene King's commitment to pouring happiness into the lives of its customers and communities.
