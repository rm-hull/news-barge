A driver suffered arm injuries during a crash at a Darlington junction on Tuesday (October 6).

Emergency services were called to Chesnut Street at the junction with Valley Street in the town at around 5.35pm, following reports of a two-vehicle incident.

Durham Police have confirmed that one of the drivers is believed to have sustained arm injuries.

A spokesperson for the police force said: "Police were called at 5.35pm yesterday (October 6) following a two-vehicle collision on Chesnut Street at the junction with Valley Street, in Darlington.

"One of the drivers is thought to have suffered arm injuries.

"The vehicles have since been recovered."
