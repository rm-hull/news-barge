A technology retailer has opened a Bradford hub to support small and medium-sized businesses across the city and West Yorkshire.

The Currys Business Hub opened at the Bradford store on September 30, offering face-to-face advice, tailored tech solutions and flexible payment options as firms tackle rising costs and complex technology needs.

Luke Cowan, business customer manager at Currys Business Bradford, said: "We're delighted with the success of the Bradford Business Hub launch.

"It has been a pleasure to meet such a wide variety of local businesses and hear so many inspiring stories of success from across the region.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21545422.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Currys' Bradford store *(Image: Supplied)*

"Bradford is home to a thriving and ambitious business community, and we are excited to help organisations continue to develop, grow, and flourish.

"We look forward to building strong local partnerships and supporting businesses on their journey to future success."

The hub is a dedicated contact point for an estimated 16,000 businesses in Bradford and the surrounding area.

Currys is marking the launch with a five per cent discount on all products for new business customers.

The offer runs from September 30, 2026, to December 22, 2026, for new UK business customers aged 18 or over.
