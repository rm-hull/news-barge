# B Corp success for Harrogate Organics Company striving to 'make a positive difference for people and planet'

## A Harrogate company says it is “incredibly proud” to become one of a small handful of businesses in the town to win a globally recognised accreditation.

Now in its 25th year of business, Strive Group Holdings has achieved B Corp, along with a subsidiary firm, Harrogate Organics Company.

The B Corp accreditation recognises businesses that meet the highest levels of social, environmental and ethical performance.

Alistair Grant, Managing Director of Strive Group Holdings, said: "I am incredibly proud that Strive Group Holdings has achieved B Corp certification.

![Alistair Grant, Managing Director of Harrogate's Strive Group Holdings, said: "I am incredibly proud that Strive Group Holdings has achieved B Corp certification. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/people/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


"This certification reinforces our commitment to making thoughtful, transparent and accountable decisions that benefit our people, our clients, our communities and the environment."

Certified B Corporation accreditation is given by B Lab, a global non-profit network founded in 2006 that aims to use business as a force for good.

Following its awards success, Strive Group Holdings says it does not intend to rest on its laurels and wants to continue to set new and high standards.

And, it says, B Corp only marks the beginning of this Harrogate company’s next chapter.

Alistair Grant said: "Achieving B Corp is not a destination; it is a commitment to continually raising the bar, challenging ourselves to do better, and ensuring that growth is achieved responsibly and sustainably.

"As we look to the future, our values of accountability, integrity and respect remain at the heart of everything we do.

"These principles have guided us for the past 25 years and will continue to shape the positive impact we create in the years ahead."

Harrogate Organics Company was acquired by Strive Group Brands in June 2025, having been originally founded in 2000.

Like its parent firm, is based at Freemans Way in Harrogate.

The natural wellbeing brand is renowned for making thoughtfully formulated, nature-inspired products to support everyday wellbeing.

Originally founded in 2001, Strive Group Holdings is a brand, employee and customer experience agency which has pursues a people-first philosophy.

Its headquarters are located at 12a Montpellier Parade in Harrogate.
