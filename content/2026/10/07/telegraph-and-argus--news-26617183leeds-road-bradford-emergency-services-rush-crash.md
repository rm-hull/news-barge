A major Bradford road is shut this evening following a huge crash.

Emergency services have scrambled to Leeds Road to attend to the collision.

Police have cordoned off the lower end of the road just before Bradford city centre.

Metro has confirmed three bus services have been diverted.

> #Bradford - Leeds Road between Shipley Airedale Road and Birksland Street - Police incident. First Bradford Service 72, 608, X6 to divert bothways via Leeds Road, Shipley Airedale Road, Wakefield Road, Bowling Back Lane, Sticker Lane Laisterdyke, Leeds Road.
>
> — metrotravelnews (@MetroTravelNews) October 7, 2026

Social media images show two fire engines inside the cordon and a police van.

It appears that at least two cars were involved in the incident.
