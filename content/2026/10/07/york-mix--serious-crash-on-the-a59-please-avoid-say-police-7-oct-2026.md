Emergency services are dealing with a serious crash on the A59 in North Yorkshire this afternoon (Wednesday).

It’s at Flaxby, and the road is closed in both directions.

North Yorkshire Police posted at 4.25pm: “The road is closed in both directions between the Allerton Park roundabout and the Goldsborough roundabout and will be for some time.

#### Crash location

“Motorists are advised to avoid the area.”
