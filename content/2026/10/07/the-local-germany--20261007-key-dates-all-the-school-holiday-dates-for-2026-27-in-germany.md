Whether you need to secure childcare, you're planning a family holiday or you're wondering when it might be quieter for a staycation, it is handy to know when pupils in Germany are off school.

Germany’s 16 states (*Bundesländer*) are responsible for their school calendar. That means vacations vary depending on the region you're based in.

**READ ALSO: How working parents in Germany manage childcare during the summer holidays**

The main school holiday breaks in Germany include:

Autumn holidays – *Herbstferien*

Christmas holidays – *Weihnachtsferien*

Winter holidays – *Winterferien*

Easter holidays – *Osterferien*

Whitsun – *Pfingstferien*

Summer holidays – *Sommerferien*

**When schools in Germany are closed for the holidays**

We've compiled the dates below for each state in the 2026/27 school year.

Please note there will be further days off depending on the state and individual school, for example teaching-free days. Make sure to check with your school calendar to keep on top of those.

**Other holidays to be aware of:**

Please note some of these are included in school vacation periods. When a holiday falls on a weekend in Germany it is not moved to a week day.

**Nationwide holidays:**

Christmas Day (Erster Weihnachtstag): December 25th, 2026 (Friday)

Boxing Day (Zweiter Weihnachtstag): December 26th, 2026 (Saturday)

New Year's Day (Neujahrstag): January 1st, 2027 (Friday)

Labour Day (Tag der Arbeit): May 1st, 2027 (Saturday)

Good Friday (Karfreitag): March 26th, 2027 (Friday)

Easter Monday (Ostermontag): March 29th, 2027 (Monday)

Labour Day (Tag der Arbeit): May 1st, 2027 (Saturday)

Ascension Day (Christi Himmelfahrt): May 6th, 2027 (Thursday)

Whit Monday (Pfingstmontag): May 17th, 2027 (Monday)

German Unity Day (Tag der Deutschen Einheit): October 3rd, 2027 (Sunday)

**Regional holidays:**

Reformation Day (Reformationstag): October 31st, 2026 (Saturday), in Brandenburg, Bremen, Hamburg, Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, Lower Saxony, Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt, Schleswig-Holstein and Thuringia

All Saints' Day (Allerheiligen): November 1st, 2026 (Sunday), in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, North Rhine-Westphalia, Rhineland-Palatinate and Saarland

Epiphany (Heilige Drei Könige): January 6th, 2027, in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria and Saxony-Anhalt

International Women's Day (Frauentag): March 8th, 2027, in Berlin and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania

Corpus Christi (Fronleichnam): May 27th, 2027, in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, Hesse, North Rhine-Westphalia, Rhineland-Palatinate and Saarland, as well as some districts in Saxony and Thuringia

Assumption Day (Mariä Himmelfahrt): August 15th, 2027 (Sunday), in Saarland and some predominantly Catholic communities in Bavaria

World Children's Day (Weltkindertag): September 20th, 2027, in Thuringia
