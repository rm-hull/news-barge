THREE people were rushed to hospital after a serious crash in West Yorkshire last night.

Emergency services were called to the scene on Bradford Road, Huddersfield, at around 8.30pm on Tuesday.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 8.30pm last night (October 6) to report a road traffic collision on Bradford Road in Huddersfield.

"Two ambulances and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and three patients were conveyed to hospital."

Reports on social media indicated there was a large emergency services presence at the scene.It unknown at this stage how many vehicles were involved in the crash.

The Telegraph & Argus has asked West Yorkshire Police for more details.

West Yorkshire Police carried out road safety checks on Bradford Road on October 1 as part of Operation Trimburg, targeting the "fatal five" driving offences.
