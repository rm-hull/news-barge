A ‘DISHEVELLED’ man is wanted by police in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is searching for 30-year-old Dylan Norman from Scarborough in connection with the theft of a bike in July.

He also failed to attend a police interview.

A spokesperson for the force said: “He is believed to be in Scarborough, and is described as dishevelled in appearance.”

If you see him, call 999, or if you have information as to his current whereabouts, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or report anonymously through independent charity Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or via their website.
