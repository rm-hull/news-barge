Wyvern Academy, proudly part of Consilium Academies, is celebrating its best-ever academic results.

The academy has seen an impressive increase in the number of students achieving a Grade 4 or higher in both English and Maths, representing a significant improvement on the previous year. There has also been strong progress across individual subjects.

“Our best-ever results are something for everyone at Wyvern Academy to celebrate. They reflect the hard work and dedication of our students and staff and show what can be achieved when young people are given both high expectations and a wide range of opportunities to succeed.” - Ms Nadia Robinson, Principal Wyvern Academy.

The results are a reflection of the perseverance and determination of students and staff across Wyvern Academy, and demonstrate the academy’s commitment to ensuring every young person has the opportunity to achieve their full potential.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21518607.jpg?type=mds-article-620)GCSE results day at Wyvern Academy in Darlington. *(Image: Consilium Academies)*

At the heart of life at Wyvern Academy is a strong belief that education extends beyond academic results. The academy provides students with access to enrichment opportunities twice a day, ensuring that young people have regular opportunities to discover new interests, develop practical skills, build confidence and form positive relationships.

Ms Robinson, speaking about the academy’s enrichment offer and its wider impact on students, said:

“At Wyvern Academy, we live our vision every day. We believe that every young person should have access to exceptional opportunities, and that is why enrichment is such an important part of academy life. Our students have access to enrichment opportunities twice a week, giving them the chance to try something new, discover their interests and develop skills beyond the classroom.”

The academy’s enrichment programme includes an impressive range of activities, from a fully equipped gym and Formula One Club to girls’ football, astronomy, cooking, life skills, Minecraft, stitching and mindfulness, alongside many other opportunities.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21518610.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Consilium Academies)*

These activities form an important part of the academy’s commitment to excellence in opportunities, giving students experiences that may not otherwise be available to them and helping them develop skills and interests that can support them both inside and outside the classroom.

“We also understand the importance of building strong relationships between students and staff. When young people feel known, supported and settled, they are in a much stronger position to engage, learn and succeed. We are incredibly proud of how settled our students are and of the positive relationships that have been developed across our academy community,” Ms Robinson added.

The academy believes that the combination of high academic expectations, strong relationships and a rich enrichment programme is helping to create an environment where students can thrive.

From developing sporting interests and practical life skills to exploring science through astronomy, expressing creativity through stitching and cooking, or building confidence through activities such as Formula One and Minecraft, students are encouraged to find something that inspires them.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21518604.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Consilium Academies)*

The GCSE results success provide a significant milestone for Wyvern Academy, while the academy remains focused on continuing to raise aspirations and provide even more opportunities for its students.

If you are looking for a secondary school where your child can achieve, develop their interests and be part of a supportive and settled community, Wyvern Academy welcomes you. The academy is currently offering live tours of the school, giving prospective students and their families the opportunity to see the academy in action, meet staff and students, and experience the opportunities available first-hand.

Families interested in finding out more are encouraged to contact Wyvern Academy directly to arrange a visit and discover what life at the academy could offer their child.

With academic achievement and personal development working hand in hand, Wyvern Academy is continuing to put its vision into practice: providing excellence in opportunities and supporting every young person to reach their full potential.
