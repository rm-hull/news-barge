Armed police have been spotted in a standoff with a man who allegedly attacked fellow officers.

Officers with guns were seen outside a terrace home on Coronation Avenue, in Shildon, where a man in his fifties had barricaded himself inside.

Durham Police said officers were called over concerns for a man’s welfare around 11.20am this morning (October 7), but on arrival a man is believed to have attacked them before locking himself in a house.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550426.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Coronation Avenue in Shildon. *(Image: ANNA BARNES)*

Specialist negotiators were called and the street cordoned off at both ends as police deal with the incident.

Armed officers remain outside the home as of 3.45pm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550428.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Coronation Avenue in Shildon. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550430.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Coronation Avenue in Shildon *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Crowds of concerned and curious residents gathered on neighbouring Fir Tree where police tape blocked the street.

The Northumbria Police dog unit was also called in to help.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550429.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Coronation Avenue in Shildon. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Nearby St John’s Primary School was placed on lockdown by police.

The school said in a statement: “Due to an ongoing incident, the police have advised the children remain in school for their safety and for the safety of parents until the matter is resolved.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21550119.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Coronation Avenue in Shildon. *(Image: ANNA BARNES)*

The school has since confirmed children can be picked up from 3pm.

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “We were called to an incident at a private address in Shildon at 12.19pm on Wednesday.

"We dispatched an ambulance crew, a clinical team leader and a crew from the hazardous area response team. The incident is ongoing.”
