Community sports clubs across Skipton are being encouraged to apply for thousands of pounds in funding through the Cash4Clubs programme.

The scheme is offering £2,000 grants to 225 grassroots sports clubs across the UK and Ireland. Five clubs will also be selected to receive larger grants worth £10,000 each. The funding can be used by clubs looking to improve their facilities, purchase new equipment or invest in their future. Local clubs across Skipton and the surrounding area are being encouraged to consider applying if they could benefit from additional financial support. Applications are now open and will close on 1st January 2027. Further information about eligibility and applications is available through the Cash4Clubs website here.
