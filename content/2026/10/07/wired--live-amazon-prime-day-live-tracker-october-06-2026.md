## Everyone Needs to Hear About This Dish Soap I Bought

I, for one, always enjoy posts about what people actually bought for Prime Day, so I present to you the one thing I myself have bought: dish detergent. Apparently spray-on dish detergent has been around since 2020, but I just became aware of Dawn Powerwash this summer when I saw it on an endcap at the grocery store and bought it on a whim (typically I do grocery pickup to curb my impulse shopping, but this time it paid off!). I am not ashamed to admit that dish spray in a bottle has changed my life. It’s not only a no-mess, only-spray-what-you-need solution for dishes, but you can use it to clean all sorts of things, from clothes to your car. One bottle of Powerwash is $5 at most grocery stores, so not only are you saving $2 a bottle with this deal, it’s being delivered right to your house, so you won’t go to the store and impulse-buy other things. Win-win!
