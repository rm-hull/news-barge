POLICE say they are continuing to investigate an alleged sexual assault in York city centre.

The Coppergate car park was closed and a nearby footpath connecting Piccadilly to the Castle Car Park was cordoned off under police guard on Thursday, October 1 after the reported incident at about 7pm the day before.

North Yorkshire Police said the alleged victim is being supported by specially trained officers.




Detectives are investigating the full circumstances and are asking witnesses to come forward.

In a statement issued on Tuesday, October 6, North Yorkshire Police said its investigation is continuing.

In a separate statement of Thursday, October 1, the force said a man seen in the city centre at the time may have important information.

He is described as white and wearing a pink T-shirt with a black circular logo on the front.

A police spokesperson said the alleged victim cannot recall the exact location of the reported assault.

On October 1, senior investigating officer Matt Wilkinson, of North Yorkshire Police said: “I would urge anyone who was in York city centre at around 7pm yesterday evening and saw or heard anything that could assist our investigation to contact us.

“Even if you think the information you have is insignificant, please get in touch.

"It could prove important to our enquiries."

“If you can help call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference number 12260192537.”
