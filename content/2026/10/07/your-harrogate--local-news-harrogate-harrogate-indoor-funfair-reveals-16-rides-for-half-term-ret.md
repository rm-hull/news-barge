Harrogate Indoor Funfair is returning to the Great Yorkshire Events Centre this October half term with 16 rides.

The event will run from Saturday 24th October until Sunday 1st November, offering families three hours of unlimited rides with a riding wristband.

Ten children’s rides feature in the line-up, alongside fairground favourites including the Waltzer, Miami, Superbob, Twist and Frogs.

There will be two sessions each day, running from 10am until 1pm and 2pm until 5pm.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ac5e6faa89ee70540f.jpg)

Dedicated quiet sessions will also take place at 10am on Saturday 24th October and Sunday 1st November, providing a quieter environment for children and young people who may find a standard session overwhelming.

An Indoor Funfair spokesperson said:

> “Harrogate Indoor Funfair has become a real Yorkshire half-term tradition for us. Although the event is in Harrogate, we get a huge number of families travelling from Leeds, Bradford, Wakefield and across the region.
>
> “One of the biggest attractions is that families know exactly what they're spending before they arrive. A riding wristband gives you three hours of unlimited rides, and because everything is indoors, it doesn't matter what the Yorkshire weather throws at us.
>
> “We're putting together a fantastic line-up for October and we're looking forward to welcoming thousands of families back during half term.”

Online riding wristbands start at £12.99, with spectator tickets available for accompanying adults and other visitors who do not wish to ride.

The venue, at the Great Yorkshire Showground, offers free on-site parking, including priority parking for Blue Badge holders, as well as a café and baby-changing facilities.

*Tickets and the full ride list are available on the Indoor Funfair**website**.*
