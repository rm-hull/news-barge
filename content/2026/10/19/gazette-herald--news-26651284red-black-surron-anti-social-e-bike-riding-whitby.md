THE county's police are looking for witnesses to anti-social e-bike riding in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for information after an e-bike was 'ridden in an anti-social manner' in Whitby last week.

The incident took place around 3pm last Thursday (October 15) in the Helredale Road and Rohilla Close area.

A spokesperson for the force said: "The red and black Surron bike may have also been seen on other days prior to this incident."

Anyone with information, including video doorbell, dashcam, or CCTV footage, is asked to contact PC Bethany Thompson at [bethany.thompson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:bethany.thompson@northyorkshire.police.uk), through the website, or call 101.

Please quote reference 12260203025 when providing information.
