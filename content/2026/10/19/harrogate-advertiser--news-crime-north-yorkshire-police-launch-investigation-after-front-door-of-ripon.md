# North Yorkshire Police launch investigation after front door of Ripon property repeatedly kicked

## Police launch investigation after door of Ripon property repeatedly kicked

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a front door was damaged during a late-night incident in Ripon.

![The police are appealing for information and witnesses after the front door of a property on King Street in Ripon was was repeatedly kicked by an unknown person, causing damage](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/19/8/48/Police-1.jpeg?trim=0,1,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The incident happened at around 11.15pm on Wednesday, October 14, when an unknown person repeatedly kicked the front door of a property on King Street, causing damage.

Officers are asking anyone who witnessed the incident or has information that could assist their enquiries to come forward.

They are particularly keen to hear from anyone who may have recorded the incident on a mobile phone, residents with CCTV or doorbell camera footage, or motorists who were driving through the area at the time with an active dash cam.

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260203041 when providing any details regarding the incident.
