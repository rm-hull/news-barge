A PERSON has been taken to hospital after a crash at a busy junction in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened at the junction of Greengate Lane and Pickering Road in Thornton-le-Dale at 2.30pm on Saturday (October 17).




A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: “We received an emergency call at 2.30pm on Saturday 17 October to report a road traffic collision at the junction of Greengate Lane and Pickering Road, Thornton-le-Dale.

“An ambulance and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital.”
