A long-serving Darlington councillor has resigned from the Conservative Party after 27 years, in order to "speak up freely".

Cllr Gerald Lee, who represents Heighington and Coniscliffe, announced his decision in a letter to residents on Sunday (October 18), seen by The Northern Echo.

Cllr Lee, who has sat on Darlington Borough Council since 1999, said: “For some time, it has become increasingly difficult to put the ward first within the party structure.

“My ability to speak up freely for residents, to challenge where necessary, and to act independently has been constrained in ways I cannot accept.”

However, Darlington Conservative Association said it had been clear “for some time” that the councillor and the local party were moving in different directions.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602914.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cllr Lee pictured on a litter pick in South Park. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Cllr Scott Durham said there were concerns about Cllr Lee's “commitment to the wider team."

Cllr Lee is known for establishing Darlington’s Silent Santa Appeal with his wife, Ruth, and was named Unsung Hero at the 2025 County Durham Together Awards.

The annual campaign, founded in 2017, collects shoeboxes and gift bags for children who might otherwise receive little at Christmas.

Cllr Lee said his values remained conservative, but leaving the party would allow him to represent the ward with “complete independence, honesty and integrity”.

He added: “Rather than allow this to affect my principles and the service I provide, I have chosen to step aside from the party and continue my work without those limitations.”

Darlington Conservative Association chair Cllr Durham said: “Cllr Lee has held a long-standing position on climate change which is at odds with the more pragmatic approach that Darlington Conservatives believe is needed.”

Darlington Conservative Association said it remained focused on campaigning ahead of next year’s elections and offering residents “a positive alternative”.
