Here’s when you can see bestselling York-based author in North Yorkshire.

Harry Whittaker, bestselling author, radio presenter, and improv actor, is taking his latest book on tour around the UK.

Harry will be bringing his new book, *Orlando*, to Waterstones in Coney Street York on**Friday 6 November**. He will also be visiting Kemps Books in Malton on** Saturday 7 November**.

The son of renowned author Lucinda Riley, it was announced that Harry would complete the *Seven Sisters* series on behalf of his mother who passed away in 2021.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/481465360_1202755007873477_3531603806746539102_n.jpg)

*Atlas: The Story of Pa Salt*

*Atlas: The Story of Pa Salt*, co-authored by Harry and Lucinda, was published globally in 2023.* Orlando*, is a step back into the world of Lucinda Riley’s* Seven Sisters*.

*Orlando* marks the beginning of a new chapter in the internationally bestselling series, inviting readers back into the rich and captivating universe that has enchanted millions around the world.

Reader favourite antiquarian bookseller Orlando Forbes – first introduced in *The Shadow Sister* – returns in this dual timeline saga. When Orlando uncovers a hidden cache of manuscripts in an old Harrow rectory, his investigation draws us deeper into the private longings and unresolved fractures of the character’s own life.

Across dual timelines, his search for the truth about a forgotten Victorian author becomes a mirror to his own story, revealing buried betrayals, guarded hearts, and the courage it takes, in any century, to claim your true name.

Expect exciting appearances from beloved Seven Sisters characters, and discover how their lives have been shaped following the events of *Atlas: The Story of Pa Salt*.
