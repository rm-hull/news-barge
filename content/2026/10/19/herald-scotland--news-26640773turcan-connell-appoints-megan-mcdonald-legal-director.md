Law firm Turcan Connell has appointed Megan McDonald as Legal Director in its land and property team, as it looks to expand its presence in Glasgow and the west of Scotland.

Ms McDonald brings experience in the Scottish commercial property market, having worked with national and international law firms.

Her expertise covers investment acquisitions and disposals, property finance, commercial leasing, development and renewable energy projects.

She will work under the leadership of partners Grierson Dunlop and Alice Warne, supporting the team’s continued growth and developing client relationships across the region.

Her appointment comes as Turcan Connell seeks to strengthen its offering to clients in Glasgow and the west of Scotland.

The firm’s land and property team advises a broad range of clients, including landowners, developers, investors, businesses and private clients, across a variety of property matters.

Megan McDonald, Legal Director at Turcan Connell, said: "I’m delighted to be joining Turcan Connell at such an exciting time for the firm.

"I’m looking forward to bringing my breadth of experience in the Scottish real estate market to the team, developing existing and new client relationships, and playing an active part in the continued growth of the Land and Property team, with a particular focus on clients, projects and expansion in the West of Scotland."

Grierson Dunlop, Land and Property Partner at Turcan Connell, said: "We are delighted to welcome Megan to the firm.

"Megan is a highly regarded lawyer with an impressive breadth of experience across the commercial real estate sector.

"Her appointment strengthens our offering in Glasgow and the West of Scotland and further enhances our ability to deliver high-quality advice to clients across a wide range of property matters."
