# 'People are at the heart of award-winning Harrogate company' that leads the way in the North

## “Harrogate firm incredibly proud of major success in Yorkshire awards in an outstanding field”

Harrogate-based Raworths’ Trusts, Wills and Estates (TWE) team has won the coveted Private Client Team of the Yea**r** category at this year’s Yorkshire Legal Awards 2026.

![Yorkshire Legal Awards win for Raworths of Harrogate - Suzi Ruffell (Yorkshire Legal Awards host) with Raworths’ Sam Jenner, Alison Walker, Rachel Tunnicliffe and Ryan Carter. Credit Yorkshire Legal Awards. ](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/19/15/29/Suzi-Ruffell-(Yorkshire-Legal-Awards-host)-with-Raworths-Sam-Jenner-Alison-Walker-Rachel-Tunnicliffe.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The awards, which took place last week at Leeds’s New Dock Hall, are judged by a prestigious independent panel made up of experts from across the Yorkshire legal community.

Commenting on Raworths’ win, judges said: “In an outstanding field that made the decision particularly challenging, Raworths demonstrated the expertise and dedication required to support clients through deeply personal and complex matters.

“Its commitment to client care and contribution to the wider community reflected the very best of private client practice.”

The accolade comes on the back of the 24-strong TWE team, which is one of the largest private client teams in Yorkshire, recently retaining top tier rankings in leading industry guides including the Legal 500 and a Band 1 ranking in the Chambers High Net Worth Guide 2026 for the tenth year in a row.

Rachel Tunnicliffe, Senior Partner and Head of Private Client Services at Raworths said: “We’re incredibly proud to see our Private Client team recognised at the Yorkshire Legal Awards.

“This award reflects the expertise, commitment and collaborative spirit of our whole team, and the exceptional service they provide to businesses, families and individuals across the region and beyond.

“Our people are at the heart of Raworths, and this recognition is a real testament to the culture we have built together – one where talented individuals work collaboratively to support our clients through every stage of their personal and professional lives.”

The Yorkshire Legal Awards, which celebrates its 27th anniversary this year, brings together the region’s legal community to recognise and celebrate the achievements of law firms, in-house teams and individual practitioners.

Raworths was also nominated for Law Firm of the Year (medium); Managing Partner, Simon Morris was shortlisted for Managing Partner of the Year marking his 25th year at Raworths and four years of exceptional leadership at the helm of the firm. Senior Partner and Head of Private Client Services, Rachel Tunnicliffe was also nominated in the Yorkshire Lawyer of the Year category.

The latest awards success further cements Raworths’ established reputation as one of the leading law firms in the North of England, with specialist commercial and private client lawyers working together under one roof.
