AN E-BIKE has been stolen from a York gym.

North Yorkshire Police are searching for information after an e-bike was stolen from outside PureGym on Stirling Road in York

It happened between 7pm and 8.30pm last Wednesday (October 15).




* **Wanted man arrested after 13 day hunt by North Yorkshire Police**
* **Police probe launched into 'anti-social' e-bike riding in North Yorkshire town**

The force said the thief cut a lock to steal the bike.

Officers are appealing for information from anyone who may have witnessed the theft or recorded footage on their phone.

Anyone with information is asked to email [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101, quoting reference 12260203242.
