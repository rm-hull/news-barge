A magical autumn trail has launched at Swinton Bivouac to raise money for Masham CE Primary School.

Created by Friends of Masham School (FOMS), with support from Swinton Estate, the trail invites families to follow woodland clues and discover the magic of the woods.

Trail sheets cost £5 per child and are available from Swinton Bivouac Café. Every child who completes the trail will receive a prize, with entry into a draw to win afternoon tea also included.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad5fe0e60e0b98ae10.png)

All proceeds will go to Friends of Masham Primary School to help fund enriching opportunities for pupils.

Organisers said:

> “Gather your family, wrap up warm, and enjoy a wonderful autumn day out while supporting a great cause.”

The trail runs from 19th October until 31st January 2027, offering families an activity for half term, weekends and the school holidays.

Visitors are advised to check Swinton Bivouac Café’s winter opening hours before travelling.
