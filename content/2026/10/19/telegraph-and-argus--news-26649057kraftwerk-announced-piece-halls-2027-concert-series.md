ELECTRONIC music pioneers Kraftwerk will head to Halifax next summer.

The German group will perform at Halifax’s Piece Hall on Tuesday, June 22, as part of Live at The Piece Hall 2027.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, June 23, via ticketmaster.co.uk.

Nicky Chance-Thompson MBE DL FRSA, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "The Piece Hall is often described as a piece of living history, and now we're getting ready to welcome some genuine history makers to our unique heritage site.

"Fans of any kind of electronic music from pop to techno, house or hip hop owe a huge debt to these trailblazing pioneers, who wrote the blueprint for so many genres.

"Famed for their spectacular immersive shows, with sounds and visuals which still feel like they're at the cutting edge decades later, I can imagine how potent and powerful this performance will be in our stunning setting.

"Simply unmissable."

Kraftwerk are the latest act announced for the 2027 concert series, which will also feature performances by Pete Tong Ibiza Classics, Suki Waterhouse, Rudimental, Bananarama, Slash, Rick Astley, The Saw Doctors, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison.
