*Update: At 10am this morning, the A1/M1 interchange in North Yorkshire was running normally following the earlier delays.*

Drivers are facing delays on a motorway in Yorkshire this morning (Monday, October 19) due to an 'obstruction'.




AA Traffic News is reporting queues close to junction 47 of the M1 and the interchange for the A1 (M).

An obstruction was reported on the M1 southbound from junction 47 (Garforth/Micklefield) to junction 46 (Leeds Colton/Garforth).

Traffic travelling towards Leeds has been held this morning, reports say.
