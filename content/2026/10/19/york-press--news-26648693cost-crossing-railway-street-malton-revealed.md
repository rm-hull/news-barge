THE COST of a new pedestrian crossing in a North Yorkshire town has been revealed.

North Yorkshire Council's Highways have also confirmed that the estimated total cost of the new zebra crossing in Railway Street is £23,000.

Cllr Keane Duncan, who represents Norton, said he has continued to put residents' questions to the council’s highways team about the crossing.

He can report back the laminated beacons are due to be fitted this week (Thursday, October 22), which will complete the work. No road closure is expected to be required.
