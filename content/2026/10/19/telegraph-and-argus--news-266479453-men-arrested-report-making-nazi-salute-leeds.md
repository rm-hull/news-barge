Three men have been arrested after a racially aggravated public order offence was reported in Leeds city centre in the early hours of Sunday.

The incident took place at around 12.47am on Brunswick Terrace, where three men were said to have surrounded an Asian man and directed racist language towards him.

A caller reported that the men had been ejected from the Stick and Twist pub on Merrion Way shortly before the incident and said they had been doing a “Nazi salute”.

Following enquiries, three men aged 25, 26, and 29 were arrested on suspicion of racially aggravated public order and taken into custody.

Officers are appealing for witnesses or anyone with relevant footage to come forward.

The crime reference number is 13260592802.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or through their website.
