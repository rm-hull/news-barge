A crash closed one carriageway of the A1(M) near the York junction this morning (Monday).

The southbound motorway was at a standstill after the collision between junctions 43 (the Hook Moor Interchange) and 44 (York / Tadcaster).

Emergency services went to the scene.

#### Crash location

The road has now reopened and traffic has returned to normal.
