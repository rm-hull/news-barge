## Market returns to landmark North Yorkshire site after 'success of inaugural event'

Little Bird Christmas Artisan Market returns to the spectacular surroundings of Fountains Abbey on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026 *(Image: Contributed)*

An artisan market is returning to North Yorkshire for a weekend of unique gifts and seasonal treats.

The Little Bird Christmas Artisan Market will be held at Fountains Abbey on Saturday, November 28, and Sunday, November 29, 2026.

The market will feature more than 40 independent traders each day in the setting of the North Yorkshire UNESCO World Heritage Site.




Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "We're absolutely delighted to be returning to Fountains Abbey for a second year.

"The response to our inaugural Christmas market exceeded all expectations, and we're excited to welcome visitors back for another unforgettable weekend.

"Every trader has been carefully selected for the quality and originality of their work, creating a luxury shopping experience where every purchase has a story behind it.

"There's something incredibly special about discovering beautifully handcrafted gifts in such a magnificent historic setting, and we're proud to bring together some of the finest independent makers from across the region."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21599708.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Little Bird Christmas Artisan Market returns to the spectacular surroundings of Fountains Abbey on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026 *(Image: Contributed)*

Organisers said the market will offer jewellery, ceramics, homeware, textiles, artwork, fashion accessories, gourmet food and drink, and Christmas gifts.

They added that it follows the success of last year’s inaugural event, which quickly established the market as a highlight of Yorkshire’s festive calendar.

Jason Scully, general manager of Fountains Abbey, said: "We’re delighted to be welcoming Little Bird Made back to Fountains Abbey for a second year.

"Their artisan Christmas market was a fantastic addition to our festive offer last year, and we’re excited to see the team return this Christmas.

"The market provides another wonderful opportunity for people to experience Fountains Abbey during the festive season while supporting independent makers and small businesses.

"We know our visitors will enjoy browsing the wide range of market stalls, and we hope they’ll discover some unique gifts and seasonal treats to take home too."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21599711.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Little Bird Christmas Artisan Market returns to the spectacular surroundings of Fountains Abbey on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026 *(Image: Contributed)*

Among the returning traders is Annie Maguire, owner of A Little Dickie Bird.

Ms Maguire said: "Returning to Fountains Abbey with Little Bird Made was an easy decision for us.

"We are really excited to be trading again at this gorgeous venue for another fabulous Christmas Artisan Market."

Michelle Scott, owner of Clayful Creations, will also return.

Ms Scott said: "Fountains Abbey is truly magical, and to be able to showcase my small business in such an iconic setting again is incredibly special."

Entry to the market is free, but standard admission charges apply for entry to Fountains Abbey.

National Trust and English Heritage members receive free entry to the site.
