A new organisation has launched with an ambition to revive Harrogate’s spa heritage and shape its future as “Britain’s Living Spa”.

Harrogate Spa Society has been established as a Community Interest Company by Harrogate-based designer George Eglese.

It brings together several years of research, creative projects and collaboration across the town, with a focus on water, nature, health, culture, heritage and design.

At the heart of its plans is Harrogate Cure, a cultural programme combining art, music, nature, movement and discovery, with a new season of activities planned throughout 2027.

The Society’s wider work includes Spa Commons, which will explore the restoration and renewed use of Harrogate’s historic spa buildings, mineral springs, gardens and public spaces.

It is also developing Harrogate Wildbelt, a landscape vision connecting the town’s rivers, valleys, woodlands and green corridors.

Early priorities include exploring a new public role for Crescent Gardens Pavilion, researching and mapping Harrogate’s mineral springs, and developing an archive of the town’s spa history and culture.

George Eglese, Founder and Director of Harrogate Spa Society, said:

> “Harrogate was built on an extraordinary spirit of discovery, invention and experimentation.
>
> "Its mineral waters gave rise to an entire culture of health, nature, architecture, entertainment and international exchange.
>
> “Too many of the places and traditions that made Harrogate extraordinary have been neglected, but their potential remains.
>
> "At a time when we’re rethinking our relationship with nature, health and the places we live, historic spa towns have a remarkable opportunity to lead again.
>
> "Harrogate has all the ingredients, and this is an opportunity for the whole town to help shape what comes next.”

The Society’s vision has been informed by visits to historic European spa towns, exploring how their cultures, landscapes and traditions are being adapted for contemporary life.

It is developing relationships with researchers, cultural organisations, environmental groups and other spa towns across Britain and Europe.

Further projects, events and opportunities to get involved will be announced over the coming months.

*People can find out more and sign up for updates at harrogatespasociety.org or email [info@harrogatespasociety.org.](mailto:info@harrogatespasociety.org.)*
