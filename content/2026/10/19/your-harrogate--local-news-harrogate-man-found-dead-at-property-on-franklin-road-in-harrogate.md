A man in his 50s has been found dead at a property on Franklin Road in Harrogate.

Police were contacted at 3pm on Saturday (17th October) following concerns for his safety.

Officers gained entry with help from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and found the man inside.

Specialist emergency services attended after he was identified as having what appeared to be chemical burns.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesman said:

> “At 3pm on Saturday (17 October 2026), we were contacted following concerns for the safety of a man at an address on Franklin Road, Harrogate.
>
> “Officers attended the property and, with the assistance of North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, gained entry. A man in his 50s was sadly found deceased inside.
>
> “The man was quickly identified as having what appeared to be chemical burns. As a precaution, specialist resources from the police, ambulance and fire services attended the scene to assess any potential risks.
>
> "Following an initial assessment, it was established that there was no ongoing risk of harm to emergency service personnel or members of the public.
>
> “The death is being treated as unexplained. Officers are assisting with preparations of a report for the coroner.”

Police have confirmed there was no ongoing risk to the public or emergency service personnel.
