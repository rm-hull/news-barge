Amanda Owen's nine children have grown up in front of television viewers, but their lives are now taking increasingly different directions.

When Our Yorkshire Farm first introduced audiences to the family at Ravenseat in Upper Swaledale, the children were growing up together amid the everyday demands of sheep farming.

Now, in 2026, some have pursued education and careers away from the farm, others have started businesses and several remain closely involved in agricultural life.

The changes have become a major theme of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which follows Amanda and former husband Clive as they restore neighbouring farmhouse Anty John's.

![Amanda, Clive and Kids altogether outside Anty John’s](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21427016.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda, Clive and Kids altogether outside Anty John’s *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Amanda recently admitted that the family's changing circumstances have made it difficult to decide who will eventually live in the renovated property.

Speaking at a behind-the-scenes event in Northallerton on September 30, she said: "With some kids wanting to go to university, some starting their lives elsewhere and others wanting to farm, we're unsure of people's futures."

## Raven Owen

Raven is the eldest of Amanda and Clive's children and has followed an academic and scientific path away from the family's traditional farming life.

She studied biomedical science at York St John University, graduating with first-class honours in 2022, before continuing with postgraduate study.

Her interests have included scientific research and laboratory work.

Despite pursuing opportunities away from Ravenseat, Raven continues to return to the family home.

The fourth series of Our Farm Next Door features her back on the farm, helping with the stables alongside younger sister Edith.

## Reuben Owen

Reuben has become one of the most recognisable members of the Owen family in his own right.

His enthusiasm for machinery was apparent when he was growing up on Our Yorkshire Farm.

He has since developed his own heavy plant and machinery business, taking on practical jobs around the Yorkshire Dales.

His working life has also become the subject of television programmes, including Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales.

Reuben remains involved with his family and has helped with work around Ravenseat and Anty John's.

Amanda recently revealed that he had also come to the rescue of television production crews after they became stranded in difficult conditions.

## Miles Owen

Miles has continued to play an important role in the family's farming and renovation work.

Viewers have watched him take on practical tasks alongside Clive and his brothers.

The latest series of Our Farm Next Door features Miles helping Amanda paint the newly dried plastered walls at Anty John's.

He also joins Amanda and Sid in efforts to rescue a pregnant sheep stranded on a hillside.

His appearances show how the older Owen children are increasingly taking responsibility for demanding work around the farm.

## Edith Owen

Edith has taken charge of one of Ravenseat's best-known traditions.

The family's cream teas, once served by Amanda to walkers passing along the Coast to Coast route, returned in summer 2026.

Edith is now running the operation with help from other family members.

Amanda revealed that she had given Edith a small serving hatch which her daughter had turned into a business.

The latest television series also follows Edith balancing the venture with preparations for important exams.

## Violet Owen

Violet remains part of the Owen family's story, although fewer details about her individual plans have been publicly confirmed than those of some of her siblings.

Like the other children, she grew up helping with the animals and daily responsibilities at Ravenseat.

Amanda has spoken about how her children are increasingly pursuing their own interests, but has not publicly identified a specific long-term career or education plan for every child.

Violet's future plans should therefore not be confused with those of her brothers and sisters.

## Sidney Owen

Sidney, usually known as Sid, is developing his skills on the family farm.

He has appeared alongside Clive and his older brothers during agricultural work and practical projects.

In the fourth series of Our Farm Next Door, Sid takes his tractor test so he can undertake more jobs around Ravenseat.

He is also involved in a sheep rescue alongside Amanda and Miles.

His growing responsibilities show how the younger Owen children are gradually taking on some of the work previously handled by their parents and older siblings.

## Annas Owen

Annas continues to show an interest in farming and caring for animals.

In the third series of Our Farm Next Door, she and younger sister Clemmie were shown hoping to buy their first sheep.

The fourth series features the younger children with a new flock, as well as Annas trying to keep the family's puppies under control.

She also takes on an unusual decorating task at Anty John's, treating newly plastered walls using tea.

It is another example of the children being encouraged to learn practical skills beyond their usual farm responsibilities.

## Clemmie Owen

Clementine, better known as Clemmie, remains closely involved in life at Ravenseat.

She has shown an interest in sheep farming alongside Annas and has also helped with the family's revived cream tea business.

But Amanda recently revealed that Clemmie had been temporarily dismissed from the cream tea van by sister Edith.

The decision followed Clemmie's audible sigh when a customer asked for a photograph.

Amanda joked that her daughter might eventually be given another opportunity to work there.

Clemmie's farming activities and her interactions with her siblings continue to feature in Our Farm Next Door.

## Nancy Owen

Nancy is the youngest of Amanda and Clive's nine children.

Like her siblings, she has grown up helping with the animals and learning about life on the remote farm.

But the fourth series of Our Farm Next Door also reveals an interest away from agriculture.

Nancy is preparing for a ballet exam, with rehearsals taking place at a village hall.

Her interest in ballet offers a contrast with the demanding outdoor work viewers associate with the family.

It also reflects the wider changes taking place as the Owen children grow older and begin exploring their own ambitions.

## What does the future hold for Amanda Owen's children?

Amanda and Clive announced their separation in 2022 but have continued working together and raising their children.

Their shared renovation of Anty John's has become a major focus of the family's latest television programme.

But despite three years of restoration work, Amanda has revealed that no final decision has been made about who will live in the farmhouse.

She told an audience in Northallerton that belongings had been moved into the building, although the children's different ambitions meant the family was still uncertain about its eventual occupants.

For viewers who first encountered the Owens as a family of young children growing up together at Ravenseat, the contrast is striking.

Some are pursuing academic opportunities, others are developing businesses and several remain committed to farming.

But even as their lives take different directions, Ravenseat continues to bring the family together.
