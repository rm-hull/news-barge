A body has been found in the search for a missing man in Bradford.

West Yorkshire Police have confirmed that the body of 82-year-old Barry was found at Low Moor's Royds Hall Dam.

The force said the death is not being treated as suspicious.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers searching for missing Barry, who was reported missing on Saturday, October 10 from Bradford, have sadly found a body.

"At around 9.33am yesterday (Sunday 18 October) a body was found at Royds Hall Dam, Bradford.

"The death is not being treated as suspicious, his family has been informed and continue to be supported by officers at this time."
