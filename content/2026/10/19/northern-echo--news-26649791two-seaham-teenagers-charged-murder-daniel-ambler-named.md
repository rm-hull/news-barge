Two teenagers accused of murdering Daniel Ambler in Seaham can now be named.

Daniel Ambler was hit by a car at the Esso garage on Stockton Road, Seaham, at about 4.50pm on Thursday, January 15.

The 23-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene.

The trial of five people accused of murdering him has started at Teesside Crown Court.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20435374.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Daniel Ambler *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

Ajay Henry, of Melrose Crescent, Seaham, and Tyler Leadbetter, of Elgin Avenue, Seaham, were both 17 when charged with murder.

The teenagers can now be named after a reporting restriction expired when they turned 18.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Esso New Seaham Service Station, SEaham *(Image: Sarah Caldecott (The Northern Echo))*

Barry Anderson, 52, of Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland; Ashton Anderson, 20, of Ryton Crescent, Seaham; and Barry Logan Anderson, 19, of Nursery View, Shotton Bank, are also charged with murder and violent disorder.

Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, said jury selection would take place that afternoon before the trial got under way.

Mourners left floral tributes outside the service station after Mr Ambler’s death.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20431836.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A forensic officer working at the scene on Saturday (January 17). *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

Following Mr Ambler’s death, his family said: “Daniel was a much-loved son and brother, and his death will leave a huge hole in our hearts forever.

“He had a great sense of humour, was popular among friends, and was able to bring laughter to any situation.

“Our family is devastated, but we will remember Daniel for being the kind-hearted soul that he was.”

The trial continues.

The trial is expected to last up to five weeks, with proceedings taking place at Teesside Crown Court.
