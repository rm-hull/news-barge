A ROAD sweeper was involved in a fire on a road close to a busy York shopping park.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said that one of their crews attended a fire in St Nicholas Avenue – the road which leads in, out, and around the York Designer Outlet – at 10.27am this morning (October 19).
