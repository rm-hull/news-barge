Families are being invited to find 18 scarecrows around Knaresborough during the October half-term holiday.

The Knaresborough Scarecrow Trail will run from Saturday 24th October until Sunday 1st November.

Maps will be available for £3 from Bonbon Knaresborough, with participants challenged to track down all 18 scarecrows around the town.

Those who return a completed map will receive a sticker and a sweet treat, as well as entry into a draw to win a cash prize.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad5c909cc782ec209e.png)

Knaresborough Business Collective, which shared details of the event, described the trail as a “fun, affordable day out” for families.

A spokesperson for the group said:

> "This event has been a huge success over the last few years and brought so many people into town and businesses for October half term.
>
> “Thank you to all our amazing sponsors, events like this couldn’t happen without you!”

Families can take part by purchasing a trail map from Bonbon Knaresborough during the event.
