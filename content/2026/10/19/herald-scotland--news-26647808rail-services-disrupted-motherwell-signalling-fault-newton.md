Rail passengers are being warned of disruption after a signalling fault at Newton in Lanarkshire.

Network Rail Scotland said engineers are dealing with a fault affecting the signalling system at Newton. Train services between Cumbernauld and Dalmuir via Motherwell may be cancelled, delayed or revised while the issue is resolved.

Ticket acceptance has also been arranged with JMB Coaches on service 41 between Lanark and Motherwell in both directions to help passengers complete their journeys.

Travellers are being advised to check before they travel and allow extra time for their journey.
