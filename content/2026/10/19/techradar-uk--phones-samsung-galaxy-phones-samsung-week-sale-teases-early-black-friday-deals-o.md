We're barely past the midpoint of October, but some Black Friday sales are already being teased. That's exactly what's happening over at Samsung, where there are a small number of offers on its latest phones, TVs, tablets, smartwatches, and more for Samsung Week.

Some of the best offers are found on the latest Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, if you're looking to upgrade. You can get a guaranteed minimum of £150 off the former when you trade in any other device, while the latter features trade-in discounts of up to £730 with select smartphones.

As for TVs, you have several options, depending on the display type and any extras you're after. For example, there's 20% off when you buy a TV with a soundbar, up to £500 cashback on select TVs, and the option to trade in and upgrade to a 2026 display for £250 less.

Even though Samsung is teasing these as its early Black Friday deals, I doubt that this is everything the manufacturer will offer during the big end-of-year sales. So if there isn't a deal on the tech you're after, it's worth checking back later next month when the full Black Friday sales start.

For now, here are my top picks from Samsung Week, which, despite the name, actually runs all the way through to November 3.

### Samsung Week — quick links

* **Appliances:** up to 20% off
* **Laptops:** up to £400 off
* **Monitors:** claim up to £300 cashback
* **Phones:** guaranteed £150 off with a trade
* **Smartwatches:** get Bupa Well+ for free
* **Tablets:** up to £730 off with a trade-in
* **TVs:** up to 20% off and £500 cashback
