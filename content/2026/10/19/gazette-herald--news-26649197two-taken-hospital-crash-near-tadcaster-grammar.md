TWO people have been taken to hospital after a serious crash close to a grammar school in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened shortly after 12pm on Sunday (October 18), close to Tadcaster Grammar School.

A Yorkshire Air Ambulance helicopter and two ambulances were dispatched to the school.




Images taken near the scene appear to show a silver car and white van involved in the crash.

The incident closed nearby roads, and drivers were warned to avoid the area.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "Tadcaster and Acomb crews attended a two vehicle road traffic collision.

"No persons trapped. Crews assisted with scene safety and general first aid until arrival of ambulance paramedics.

"The incident left with Police and Ambulance."
