# 'Our hearts are broken': Family pay tribute to teenager, 18, who died in Leeds city centre crash

## The heartbroken family of an 18-year-old pedestrian who died following a crash in Leeds city centre have thanked members of the public and emergency services who tried to save his life.

Rowan Carty, who lived in Beeston, Leeds, died after being involved in a crash with a BMW i40 M50 on Park Lane at around 9.12pm on Friday (Oct 16).

He was rushed to hospital for emergency treatment but died from his injuries.

In a statement issued on Monday (Oct 19), Rowan's family said: “We would like to thank members of the public and emergency services for their efforts in trying to save Rowans life.

“Our hearts are broken.

“Please respect our privacy at this difficult time.”

![Rowan Carty](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/19/14/41/rowan_carty.jpeg?trim=79,0,64,0&width=640&quality=65)


West Yorkshire Police are continuing to appeal for witnesses following the fatal crash, which happened near the split for the A58 entry slip road.

The BMW had been travelling away from Leeds city centre when the crash occurred and stopped near the scene.

Police enquiries have also established that a silver Toyota Yaris T-sport was in the area at the time.

Both vehicles have since been recovered by officers.

Two men aged 22 and a 24-year-old man were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in connection with the incident.

All three have since been released on bail pending further enquiries.

Police launched an appeal for any witnesses to come forward.

A spokesperson from West Yorkshire Police said: “Officers from the Major Collision Enquiry Team are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed any part of the incident or saw either of the vehicles being driven in the area in the time leading up to the collision, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage.
