# Watch: Russian saboteur working for Ukraine shares reconnaissance videos

A Russian man secretly working for Ukraine's intelligence services has shared videos of surveillance work he says he has done for Kyiv inside Russia.

The man, who we are calling Ivan, claims to have played a role in Ukrainian drone attacks on strategic bombers and munitions factories on his home soil.

He features in a BBC World Service documentary about the shadowy world of Russian saboteurs.

Ivan was cautious about how much detail he shared, making it impossible to verify the activities he claims to have carried out.
