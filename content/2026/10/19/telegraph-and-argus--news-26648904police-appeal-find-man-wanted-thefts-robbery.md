Police are appealing for help to find a man wanted in connection with a robbery and multiple shop thefts.

Levi Brogden, 29, is described as 6ft tall, of slim build, and has scratches on his neck.

He has strong links across the south Leeds area, is known to frequent the city centre, and is believed to have recently been seen around Bradford city centre.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Can you help us to locate Levi Brogden?We would like to speak to him about a robbery and multiple theft from shop offences – all in Leeds.

"We have made numerous enquiries to find him and are now asking for the public’s assistance to determine his whereabouts.

"He is described as six feet tall and of slim build, with scratches to his neck. He is 29 years old.

"He has strong links across the south Leeds area and is known to frequent the city centre. He is also believed to have recently been seen around Bradford city centre.

"If you can help to locate him, then please contact police in Leeds on 101, or by using the LiveChat facility on our website, quoting crime reference 13260367975."
