**The family of a man who died after being struck by a car on the A92 in Fife say they are “devastated” by his death.**

Daren Baillie, 51, was hit by a red Suzuki Swift at about 11.50pm on Thursday October 15 near Freuchie.

Mr Baillie was pronounced dead at the scene of the incident.

His family released a statement through Police Scotland, saying: “We are devastated by Daren’s tragic death and wish to express our gratitude for the support received.

“Daren was a much-loved husband, dad, son, brother and friend whose sudden passing has left a huge void in the lives of all who knew him.

“He spent 30 years as a PE teacher in Fife and took immense pride in the positive impact he had on his students, with a particular focus on supporting the most vulnerable learners to achieve their very best.

“We would like to thank the emergency services for their efforts and kindly ask for privacy as we mourn the loss of a dearly loved family member.”

Police have said the female driver of the Suzuki was uninjured and their inquiries into the incident are continuing.

Sergeant David Keeney said: “Our thoughts remain with Daren’s family and friends at this difficult time.

“We are continuing to appeal for information and would ask anyone who witnessed the crash or may have dashcam footage which could assist, to contact us as soon as possible.”
