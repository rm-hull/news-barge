## Rage room that allows you to 'vent your anger or pain' is up for sale

The interior of All the Rage *(Image: RightBiz)*

An unusual North Yorkshire business where you can throw paint and smash things up has gone on the market.

All The Rage opened in Spring 2025 in Manor Road, Scarborough, and its sole director is listed as Elizabeth Megan Fletcher.

RightBiz has listed the business as being available for leasehold for £19,995.

The rental is £10,560 or £880 a month.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21600152.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of All the Rage *(Image: RightBiz)*

Profit and turnover figures are available on request.

RightBiz also said: “All the Rage Scarborough is a unique entertainment venue offering both a rage room and a creative paint room experience.

“The rage room gives customers the chance to safely smash a range of breakable items using protective equipment and tools provided on site.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21600142.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can throw paint at All the Rage *(Image: RightBiz)*

“It is popular for birthdays, group activities, couples, work events and anyone looking for a fun way to release some stress.

“The paint room offers a more creative experience, allowing customers to throw, splash and spray paint onto canvases in a relaxed and enjoyable environment. “It is particularly popular with children’s parties, families and groups of friends.”

The description continued: “The business is fully fitted and ready to operate, with dedicated activity rooms, reception space, safety equipment, tools, signage, booking systems and an established online presence.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21600151.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of All the Rage in Scarborough *(Image: RightBiz)*

“All the Rage has built up a strong local reputation and has received around 50 positive Google reviews. The business offers good potential for further growth through corporate events, mobile experiences, school holiday activities, parties and additional packages.”

Among the reviews, one said: “I highly recommend it if you need to vent your anger or pain. The feeling is indescribable. The staff is very nice and friendly.”

Another customer posted: “What can I say.... I don't think I've ever felt so relaxed and calm after something so physical.

"This is definitely more fun and way cheaper than therapy.

"I would recommend this to everyone.

"Def a great way to start the night.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21600143.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of All the Rage *(Image: RightBiz)*

And another also commented: “Such a brilliant day here today for my sister’s birthday.

"Staff were absolutely excellent.

"We paid for extra glasses and a TV to smash up, it was definitely worth it.

"It was fun, stress relieving and really funny.

"Loved it and we will be back! Thanks again.”

All the Rage has no published phone number and it takes bookings by email or through its website, which says the business is currently closed this week.
