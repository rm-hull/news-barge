# Family say 'our hearts are broken' after teen killed by BMW in Leeds

## Rowan Carty, 18, died after he was hit by a BMW on October 16

The 'heartbroken' family of a Leeds pedestrian who died after being hit by a car have thanked members of the public who attempted to save his life.

Rowan Carty, 18, from Beeston, died after he was hit by a BMW i4 M50 on Park Lane, in the city centre, at 9.12pm on Friday, October 16, near the split for the A58 entry slip road. Rowan was taken to hospital for emergency treatment but sadly died from his injuries.

In a statement issued on Monday (October 19), Rowan's family said: "We would like to thank members of the public and emergency services for their efforts in trying to save Rowan's life. Our hearts are broken. Please respect our privacy at this difficult time." The BMW, which had been travelling away from the city centre at the time of the collision, stopped near the scene. West Yorkshire Police have established a silver Toyota Yaris T-sport was present in the area at the time of the collision. Three people have been arrested and both vehicles have been recovered.

A force spokesperson said: "Two 22-year-old men and a 24-year-old man were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in relation to the incident. They have since been bailed pending further enquiries. "Officers from the Major Collision Enquiry Team are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed any part of the incident or saw either of the vehicles being driven in the area in the time leading up to the collision, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage."
