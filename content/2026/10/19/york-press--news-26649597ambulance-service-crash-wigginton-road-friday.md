EMERGENCY services have confirmed that they attended a crash in a main York road on Friday evening.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service have confirmed that two of its vehicle attended the scene of a crash on Wigginton Road on Friday (October 16).

As reported by The Press, traffic was delayed at the time after reports that a delivery van had driven into a tree in between York city centre and the outer ring road.

A spokesperson for the service said: “An ambulance and a rapid response vehicle were dispatched to the scene, but no patients were conveyed to hospital.”
