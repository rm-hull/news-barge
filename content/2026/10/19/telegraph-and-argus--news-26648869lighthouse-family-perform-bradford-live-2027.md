Lighthouse Family are set to take to the stage once again, marking their return with a UK and Ireland tour.

The pop-soul duo will perform at Bradford Live on Saturday, March 20, 2027, as part of a tour celebrating their debut album Ocean Drive and other fan favourites.

Formed in Newcastle in the early 1990s, Lighthouse Family are known for their soulful sound and uplifting melodies.

The duo achieved major success with hits like Lifted, Ocean Drive, and Goodbye Heartbreak.

Their debut album Ocean Drive, released in 1995, reached six-times platinum in the UK.

Their follow-up album Postcards from Heaven matched that success with songs such as High and Raincloud.

In 2019, they released Blue Sky in Your Head, their first studio album in 18 years, which reached number three on the UK album chart.

Priority Live members can access an exclusive venue pre-sale from 10am on Thursday, October 22.

General sale tickets will be available from 9.30am on Friday, October 23.
