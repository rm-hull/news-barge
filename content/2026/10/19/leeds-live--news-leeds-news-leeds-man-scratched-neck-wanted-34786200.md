# Leeds man with 'scratched neck' wanted after robbery and thefts

## Levi Brogden has links to the south Leeds area

A police appeal has been issued to find a man wanted for robbery and theft.

West Yorkshire Police said they are looking to speak with Levi Brogden from Leeds about a robbery and multiple shop theft offences. All these offences took place in Leeds.

Brogden, 29, has strong links to the south Leeds area and is known to frequent the city centre. He is also believed to have been seen recently in Bradford city centre. He is six feet tall and of a slim build with scratches to his neck.

The police said: "Can you help us to locate Levi Brogden? We would like to speak to him about a robbery and multiple theft from shop offences – all in Leeds. We have made numerous enquiries to find him and are now asking for the public’s assistance to determine his whereabouts.

"He is described as six feet tall and of slim build, with scratches to his neck. He is 29 years old. He has strong links across the south Leeds area and is known to frequent the city centre. He is also believed to have recently been seen around Bradford city centre."

If you have any information you can contact the police in Leeds on 101 or by using live chat online here, quoting crime reference number 13260367975. You can also call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
