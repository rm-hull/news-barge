A CAR has been vandalised whilst parked up close to a North Yorkshire coastal village.




The county's police force said a Citroën Berlingo had two tyres slashed whilst parked in Middlewood Lane, Fylingthorpe, near Robin Hood's Bay, overnight from the late evening of Friday, October 16.

The vehicle was parked there due to mechanical issues, a force spokesperson said.

North Yorkshire Police is investigating the reports of criminal damage and anyone with information can contact PC 1223 Colin Irvine via email at [colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting reference 12260193250.
