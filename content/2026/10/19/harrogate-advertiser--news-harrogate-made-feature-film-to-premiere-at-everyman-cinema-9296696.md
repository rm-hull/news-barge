# Everyman cinema to screen Harrogate horror movie whose budget was just £12,000

## "Our first foray into the world of feature film-making will be sure to shock even the most hardened horror fans"

An independent Harrogate film company's new production shot with a budget of just £12k is to receive its world premiere this week in the bright lights of the Everyman cinema.

![The talented team from Harrogate film company Wayside Trinity who made new film A Fate Worse Than Death.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/19/14/29/FateWorseThanDeathFilmTeam.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The 70-minute long horror movie called A Fate Worse Than Death was filmed on location in North Yorkshire and West Yorkshire by Wayside Trinity.

The gripping plot of this feature-length psychological horror film, which used primarily local cast and crew, explores the debilitating effects of grief through a couple struggling to cope with the loss of their child.

As the mother’s physical health begins to deteriorate, with increasingly severe headaches, it becomes apparent that something insidious may be lurking in the darkest corners of their home.

Steve Jones-Robertson, co-writer and co-producer on A Fate Worse Than Death, said "A Fate Worse Than Death promises to be a horror film of different forms, assaulting your comfort, scarring your emotions, and engaging your mind when the credits roll.

"Our first foray into the world of feature film-making will be sure to shock even the most hardened horror fans."

As the name suggests, this award winning independent film and TV production company is based in the Wayside neighbourhood of Harrogate.

In a sign of the fantastic impression Wayside Trinity has already made within the film industry, the premiere at Harrogate’s Everyman cinema this Thursday, October 22 at 7.30pm will see representatives from Screen Yorkshire in attendance.

With a budget of £12,000, the team on A Fate Worse Than Death was tight in numbers but big in talent.

The multi-skilled crew also includes Director - Abraham Fieldsend (director, co-writer, cinematographer and co-producer) and Zachary Fieldsen (co-writer and co-producer).

The cast includes James - Scott Dixon playing James; Portia Lily Crewe, playing Samantha, and Charlotte Henderson playing Jodie.

Not only does Wayside Trinityu write, produce and direct our own features, it also dabbles in all areas of Art and Creative Media.
