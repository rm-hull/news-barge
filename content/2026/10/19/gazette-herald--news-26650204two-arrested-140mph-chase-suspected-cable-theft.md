TWO men have been arrested after a high-speed police chase from North Yorkshire after the suspected theft of high-voltage cables.

North Yorkshire Police have confirmed that it arrested two men after a 140mph motorway police chase last night (October 18).

The incident began at around 11.40pm when North Yorkshire Police received reports of a man wearing a balaclava cutting cables at Scotch Corner services.

Officers quickly identified a black Mini Cooper heading north on the A1(M) and began a pursuit, with the car reaching speeds of more than 140mph before leaving the motorway and continuing onto minor roads in County Durham.

At around 12.15am, the car clipped a police vehicle and spun into a hedgerow where the two men - who were both uninjured - were arrested.

A large quantity of electric cables was found inside the vehicle, which was seized for forensic examination.

A spokesman for North Yorkshire Police said: "Two men were in the car, both wearing balaclavas.

"They were arrested on suspicion of offences including theft, going equipped for theft, dangerous driving, failing to stop, and driving without a licence or insurance."

The suspects, aged 44 and 30, remain in custody whilst the investigation continues.
