A major road in Ayrshire has been closed in both directions following a crash near Kilmarnock.

Traffic Scotland said the A77 just after Bellfield Interchange is currently shut in both directions due to a road traffic collision.

Drivers have been advised to use an alternative route and expect delays while the incident is dealt with.

A spokesperson for Police Scotland said: "The A77 is closed at the Bellfield junction, Kilmarnock, following a two-vehicle crash around 7.35am on Monday, 19 October.  
"Road users are advised to avoid the area."
