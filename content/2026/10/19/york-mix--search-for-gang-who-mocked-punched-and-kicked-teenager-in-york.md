A teenager was hurt after a gang attacked him in York city centre.

It happened between 4.50pm and 5.10pm on Monday 5 October in and around St Helen’s Square.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A group of youths followed a group of three young people, mocking them and prodding them with a fishing net.

“One of the youths punched a 16-year-old victim in the face on more than once occasion, and another youth kicked him in the ribs. The altercation was split up by a member of the public.”

The victim went to hospital with injuries to his face. He was later discharged.

Police have released these descriptions of two of the suspects.

* **suspect 1**: white, male, around 13-14 years old, stocky build, with light brown curly hair, waring rings and a gold necklace
* **suspect 2**: black, male, around 13-14 years old, between 5ft 7ins and 5ft 9ins tall, wearing a white T-shirt.

Officers are asking witnesses or anyone with information to come forward.
