The Royal Bank of Scotland has appointed Robert Begbie as its new executive chair for Scotland, with the banking veteran set to take up the role on January 1, 2027.

The appointment comes as the bank prepares to mark its 300th anniversary next year and aims to strengthen its role in supporting economic growth and investment across Scotland.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21601566.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Robert Begbie. *(Image: Supplied)*

Mr Begbie began his banking career almost 50 years ago, aged 16, at the Royal Bank of Scotland’s Musselburgh branch.

He has since held senior positions within the business, most recently leading NatWest Group’s Commercial & Institutional division through a period the bank described as strong performance and strategic progress.

In his new role, he will continue to advise the business while focusing on relationships with customers, government and other stakeholders across Scotland.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21601567.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Robert Begbie and Isabelle Girolami. *(Image: Supplied)*

He will also work with teams across the bank to support its business plans and raise its profile among its two million retail customers and 110,000 business clients.

Mr Begbie’s appointment forms part of wider leadership changes at NatWest Group. Isabelle Girolami has been named chief executive of Commercial & Institutional and is due to join the Group Executive Committee on January 1, 2027, subject to regulatory approval.

Paul Thwaite, group chief executive of NatWest Group, said: "Scotland has always been central to the story of Royal Bank of Scotland and to NatWest Group today.

"As we approach the bank’s 300th anniversary, Robert’s appointment reflects both the importance of Scotland to our future and our ambition to play an active role in supporting growth across the country.

"Robert brings deep experience, strong relationships, and a personal connection to Scotland that few people can match.

"He has made an outstanding contribution to the bank over many years, and I am pleased he will continue to play such an important role for NatWest Group, our customers, and colleagues."

Robert Begbie said: "Scotland matters a great deal to me and to the bank.

"I started my career with Royal Bank of Scotland in the Musselburgh branch almost five decades ago, so it is a real privilege to take on this role as we look ahead to the bank’s 300th anniversary.

"Scotland has enormous ambition and opportunity.

"Whether it is supporting entrepreneurs and SMEs, helping mid-market firms grow, backing investment in infrastructure and energy, or strengthening communities, Royal Bank of Scotland has an important role to play.

"I am looking forward to working with colleagues, customers, government and partners across Scotland to build on our long-standing contribution and support the next chapter of growth."
