A vehicle has crashed close to road services on a major road linking York and East Yorkshire.




Humberside Fire and Rescue Service said crews were called to Broadhelm Close, just off the A1079 and the Pocklington Services at 10.47pm last night (Sunday, October 18).

A service spokesperson said a woman self-released prior to the arrival of firefighters.

The spokesperson said: "Small tools were used to make the vehicle safe and the incident was left with police."
