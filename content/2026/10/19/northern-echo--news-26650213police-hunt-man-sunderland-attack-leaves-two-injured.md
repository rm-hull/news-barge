Police are hunting a man after a gang attack left two people with broken bones.

The assault took place in Sunderland on Green Terrace at around 1am on Sunday, August 2.

Two men in their 40s were punched multiple times and kicked while on the ground by three men they did not know.

One of the victims sustained a broken jaw and serious facial injuries, while the other suffered broken ribs.

A spokesperson for Northumbria Police said: "Two men, aged 34 and 35, have been arrested on suspicion of grievous bodily harm with intent in connection with the report.

"Both have been released on police bail."

Officers have released images of a man they wish to speak to as part of their investigation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602361.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Northumbria Police)*

Police believe the man was in the area at the time of the assault and may have information that could assist their enquiries.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602362.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Northumbria Police)*

A spokesperson added: "The man, or anyone who knows who he is, should send Northumbria Police a direct message on social media or use the live chat and report forms on the Force’s website.

"For those unable to make contact via those ways, call 101."

Officers are also keen to hear from any witnesses with information about the incident.

Police continue to carry out enquiries, including reviewing CCTV footage, as they work to identify all those involved in the attack.
