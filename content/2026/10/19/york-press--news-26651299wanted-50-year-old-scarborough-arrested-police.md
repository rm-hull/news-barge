A WANTED man has been arrested after an almost two-week search by the county police.

North Yorkshire Police have confirmed that their officers have arrested a Scarborough man with connections to York and Hull after an appeal published just under a fortnight ago.

As reported by The Press on October 5, the force was attempting to locate the 50-year-old on recall to prison and in connection with a domestic assault.

“He has now been arrested. Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal or provided information,” said a spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police.
