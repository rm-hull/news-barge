# Motorist fined after Audi TT caught speeding at 131mph on A64 in Harrogate court case

## Driver fined at Harrogate court after being caught speeding at 131mph

A motorist who was caught driving at almost twice the speed limit on a North Yorkshire dual carriageway has been fined more than £1,500 after appearing at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court.

![A motorist who was caught driving at 131mph on the A64 at Barton-le-Willows, between York and Malton, has been fined more than £1,500 after appearing at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/19/11/20/853145556_2031418507571271_8902438511993731921_n.jpeg?trim=100,12,16,19&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The driver was behind the wheel of an Audi TT travelling at 131mph on the A64 at Barton-le-Willows, between York and Malton, on June 20.

The vehicle was recorded by a North Yorkshire Police safety camera vehicle at a location where the speed limit is 70mph and where there have previously been a significant number of collisions.

The motorist pleaded guilty to speeding at a hearing last week.

At Harrogate Magistrates Court, he was ordered to pay fines and costs totalling £1,551 and given six penalty points.

Following the court case, North Yorkshire Police warned motorists about the dangers of excessive speed.

Richard Fletcher, from North Yorkshire Police, said: “Speed limits are set for a reason and exceeding them is illegal.

“We expect motorists to drive responsibly and resist driving at extreme speeds - the impact of doing so can be devastating.”
