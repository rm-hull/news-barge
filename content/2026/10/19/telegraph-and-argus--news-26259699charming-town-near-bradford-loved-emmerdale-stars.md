If you are looking for a cute spa town to visit to escape from daily life, you might want to try Harrogate.

Described as "charming" and "welcoming", Harrogate is packed with history and culture, meaning there are plenty of things to do during a trip there.

During your trip, you might even spot some famous faces, as many Emmerdale stars love to visit the town or even call Harrogate home.

Whether you want to relax in an independent café or want to walk around beautiful gardens, Harrogate has something for everyone to enjoy.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21151531.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The town is packed with independent shops and lovely restaurants. *(Image: Getty)*

## Why do people love Harrogate?

Harrogate is a hotspot to see lots of the Emmerdale cast, with a few of the actors calling the town their home.

Mark Charnock, who plays Marlon Dingle in the soap, lives in the area in a country house and often shares updates with his fans through social media.

Fans who live in the town often say they bump into the actors while walking around the area.

Charley Webb and Matthew Wolfenden used to live in the town with their three children before their split in 2023.

Aside from seeing the celebrities in the area, you could visit the popular Turkish Baths, where you can spend some time relaxing and enjoying the treatments available.

If you want to spend some time outside, you could visit the RHS Garden Harlow Carr and Valley Gardens to explore the landscaped gardens and play areas available.

For those wanting to get some shopping done, the Montpellier Quarter is packed with independent shops selling things from books to homeware and food products.

The town also has lots of great restaurants to try out, including Paradise Tap & Taco, where you can enjoy burritos and tacos alongside a craft beer.

There are many Italian restaurants to choose from if you are looking for some pizza or pasta dishes, such as Divino, Pranzo, and Stuzzi.
