Police have launched an appeal for witnesses after a man was seriously injured a Bradford street fight with weapons.

Two people have been arrested after a 59-year-old man was injured in an attack on Brompton Road on Saturday evening.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Police are appealing for witnesses after a man was seriously assaulted in Bradford.

"Detectives would like to speak to anyone who has seen or has any information about the incident on Brompton Road on Saturday evening (October 17) .

"Officers were called there just after 9pm after reports that a man had been seriously injured with weapons in a street fight.

"On attending emergency services located a 59-year-old man who had suffered a serious head injury.

"It was established a black car had arrived at the scene and that a man had got out and begun to assault the victim in an affray involving other parties which followed.

"Following enquiries, officers arrested a 38-year-old man on suspicion of attempted murder and a 36-year-old woman on suspicion of section 18 wounding.

"The woman has now been released on police bail, and the victim remains in hospital for treatment to life-threatening injuries.

"Anyone who saw of has footage of the incident is asked to contact Bradford CID on 101 or online at westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat referencing crime number 13260592496.

"It can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111. "

Brompton Road in East Bowling is just off Wakefield Road.
