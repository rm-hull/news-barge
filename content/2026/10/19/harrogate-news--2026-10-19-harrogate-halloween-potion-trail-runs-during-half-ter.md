Families can follow a free Halloween Potion Trail around Harrogate town centre during half-term, from Saturday 24 October to Sunday 1 November 2026.

The trail features 10 locations where participants can find window stickers and collect imaginary potion ingredients. Completed maps can be returned to The Crown Hotel for a chance to win a prize.

Trail maps are available from The Crown Hotel or online at harrogatebid.co.uk/halloween.

Participating venues include Harrogate Theatre, Harrogate Town AFC Shop, Mama Doreen’s and Søstrene Grene.
