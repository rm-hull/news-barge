A crisp, cold night and a sky full of stars provided the backdrop as hundreds of people lit up the streets of Harrogate for the Moonlight Glow Walk, organised by Saint Michael’s Hospice

Wearing bright outfits and glowing accessories, participants walked together to raise vital funds and show their support for the charity

Our photographer Gerard Binks captured the smiles, memories and special moments from this inspiring fundraising event...
