Visitors to Studley Royal Deer Park are being treated to an unforgettable display as the annual rutting season gets into full swing

Echoing roars, dramatic antler clashes and impressive displays of strength are taking place among the park's red deer, set against the iconic backdrop of Fountains Abbey

Our photographer Gerard Binks captured the drama and atmosphere of one of autumn’s most spectacular wildlife events...
