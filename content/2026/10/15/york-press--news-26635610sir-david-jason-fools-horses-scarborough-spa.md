ONE of the UK's most iconic actors is set to visit to the North Yorkshire coast for a special evening of stories and laughter in 2027.

Sir David Jason will appear at Scarborough Spa on Sunday, November 14, as part of his acclaimed show, An Evening with Sir David Jason.

Fans will be treated to a nostalgic journey through his career spanning more than six decades, featuring behind-the-scenes stories, personal memories, and exclusive clips from some of his most beloved roles.

Ross Anderson, sales and marketing manager at Scarborough Spa, said: "We're absolutely delighted to welcome Sir David to the Spa.

"Few performers have had such a lasting impact on British television, and this promises to be a fascinating, funny and heartwarming evening.

"We know audiences from Scarborough, Yorkshire and beyond will be eager to hear Sir David share the stories behind some of the nation's most-loved television moments."

Sir David’s long association with Yorkshire was honoured in 2026 when he was named an Honorary Yorkshireman by the Yorkshire Society.

The award recognised his contributions to the region through classic TV productions such as A Touch of Frost and The Darling Buds of May.

The show will revisit many of the characters that made him a household name, including Del Boy Trotter in Only Fools and Horses, Detective Inspector Jack Frost, Granville in Open All Hours, and roles in Porridge, The Darling Buds of May, and Danger Mouse.

The evening will also explore Sir David’s rise from a young electrician in North London with a passion for amateur dramatics to becoming one of Britain’s most celebrated actors and a true national treasure.

Ross added: "We’re absolutely delighted to welcome Sir David to the Spa.

"Few performers have had such a lasting impact on British television, and this promises to be a fascinating, funny and heartwarming evening.

"We know audiences from Scarborough, Yorkshire and beyond will be eager to hear Sir David share the stories behind some of the nation's most-loved television moments."

Further additions to Scarborough Spa’s 2027 programme, including more high-profile guests, are expected to be announced in the coming months.

Tickets available via the Spa website and box office from Thursday 22nd October 2026 at 10am.
