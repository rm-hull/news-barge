Fashion retailer Peacocks is set to open a new store in Northallerton, bringing a vacant High Street unit back into use and creating new job opportunities.

The shop will open on November 12 in the former Original Factory Shop premises on Northallerton High Street.

Love Northallerton announced the retailer’s arrival on Facebook on October 13.

Love Northallerton said in a Facebook post: “It is exciting to hear that Peacocks is joining our High Street on Thursday 12th November.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21580349.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The unit soon to be Peacocks. *(Image: LOVENORTHALLERTON)*

The post added: “This is wonderful news that a large, empty premises is being utilised which is keeping vacancy rates low for our town.

“Job opportunities are available.”

New signs confirm the opening date and reveal a special offer for shoppers.

The sign announces: “Exciting new store.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21580348.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plans submitted to North Yorkshire Council. *(Image: NORTH YORKSHIRE COUNCIL)*

It adds: “Northallerton opening soon."

The sign also promises that the first 10 customers on opening day will receive a £50 golden ticket to spend in store.

Plans submitted to North Yorkshire Council show proposed new branding and shopfront signs for the prominent town-centre unit.

The unit will be split into two and a separate tenant will be allocated for the second unit.
