Up to 17% of major roads in some parts of England have been flagged as being in poor condition.

In West Yorkshire, Calderdale reported that nine per cent of its A roads and motorways are in poor condition, the highest proportion in the region, according to new figures released by the Department for Transport.

Bradford recorded six per cent, while Kirklees stood at five per cent.

Calderdale has seen a significant rise from four per cent the previous year.

Across the region, Wakefield had just two per cent of its major roads in poor condition, while Sheffield and York reported just one per cent.

Nationally, Brighton & Hove and Nottingham topped the list at 17 and 15 per cent respectively.
