Howe Lane in Kirby Underdale will be closed to through traffic for two days from November 3.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has made an order prohibiting vehicles from travelling along the section between the junction with Uncleby Hill and the junction with Gatehowe Road.

It will start at 8am on November 3 and end at 5pm on November 4.

The closure is needed for proposed telecoms polling works and is intended to allow them to be completed while keeping the public safe.

The council anticipates the works will take two days.

Traffic affected by the order should use Howe Lane, Painsthorpe Lane and Roman Road.

The route is in Kirby Underdale civil parish and the Wolds Weighton ward.

Howe Lane will be closed to all through traffic, but access will remain available for residents, emergency vehicles and pedestrians.

The order will remain in force for no more than 18 months, or until the works are completed, whichever comes first.

Further information is available from Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance, on 01482 393939, quoting reference CB.
