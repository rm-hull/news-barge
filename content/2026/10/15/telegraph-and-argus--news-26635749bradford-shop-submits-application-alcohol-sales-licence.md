A BRADFORD shop is seeking a licence to sell alcohol.

Taha Said has applied for the licence for Mila Shop on Halifax Road, which would allow the shop to sell alcohol from 7am to midnight, seven days a week.

The application was submitted on October 5.

The deadline for any objections to the application is November 2.

These must be submitted in writing to the licensing team at Argus Chambers, Hall Ings, Bradford, BD1 1HX, or via email at [licensing@Bradford.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@Bradford.gov.uk.)

The licensing register and record of application can be inspected at the office by appointment only.

It is an offence to knowingly or recklessly make a false statement in connection with an application under the Licensing Act 2003.

The offence carries a maximum fine on summary conviction, which is unlimited.
