*(Image: Tesco)*

Shoppers can now collect Tesco Clubcard points at 500 online retailers.

Tesco rolled out the expansion on Wednesday, October 14, introducing partner brands across fashion, beauty, home, and travel.

Previously, members of the supermarket loyalty scheme could only collect points through 13 external partners.

The new retailers include Nike, Boden, and Ticketmaster.

Shoppers must access each retailer's online shop through the Clubcard section of the Tesco app or the Clubcard website to collect points.

Tesco delivered the expanded scheme in partnership with loyalty specialist Valuedynamx.

Points rates vary by retailer, with several brands offering more than the standard rate of one point for every pound spent.

For instance, Nike offers up to four points per pound spent, meaning a £100 spend produces 400 points.

Under the scheme, 400 points can become a £4 voucher.

That voucher can also be tripled to £12 when exchanged with seven restaurant Clubcard Reward Partners.

Shama Wilson, Tesco’s Group Loyalty & Membership Director, said: "We are really excited to be giving Clubcard members even more ways to collect points on more brands they already know and love."

She added: "Today marks the biggest expansion of the partner brands where customers can collect Clubcard points, with more than 500 brands now available across fashion, beauty, home, travel and more."

She said: "As we head into Black Friday and Christmas, customers can collect points on spending they are planning to do and turn those points into rewards that matter most to them."
