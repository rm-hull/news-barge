A MISSING teenager who was last seen in a Merseyside village may be in North Yorkshire.

Merseyside Police said the 17-year-old was last seen in Rainhill at around 12.10pm on Wednesday 14 October.

The force said he has links to Harrogate in North Yorkshire.




He is described by the force as being white, six feet tall and slim, with light brown curly hair.

He was wearing a dark blue cap, black Berghaus jacket, black tracksuit pants and black trainers.

Merseyside Police said anyone who sees the teenager should call 999 to ensure he is safe.

Information can be submitted to the Merseyside Police Contact Centre, or by calling 101.
