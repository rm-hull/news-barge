Famous York school St Peter’s has officially launched its new Junior School, welcoming children aged 3 to 11.

As well as classrooms, the Junior School includes laboratories and sporting facilities.

Phil Hardy, head of St Peter’s Junior School, said: “We want every child to arrive each morning excited about the day ahead and leave each afternoon with stories to tell, new ideas to explore and a genuine sense that they belong to something special.”

Music remains a key part of school life, supported by a longstanding partnership with York Minster.

Choristers at St Peter’s continue to receive their education while taking part in musical opportunities through the cathedral choir.

![St Peter’s School launches new Junior School for ages 3-11 in York](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/st-peters-school-launches-new-junior-school-for-1.jpg)

Beyond the classroom, pupils can take part in outdoor learning, educational visits, and residential experiences.

Activities include Forest School sessions, cultural exchanges in Spain, and optional ski trips, all designed to build confidence and broaden horizons.

![St Peter’s School launches new Junior School for ages 3-11 in York](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/st-peters-school-launches-new-junior-school-for-2.jpg)

The school has also invested in outdoor play and learning spaces, recognising the positive impact on children’s wellbeing, creativity, and social development.

The launch coincides with the development of a Centre for Excellence in Teaching and Learning, which will use educational research to inform innovative teaching approaches.
