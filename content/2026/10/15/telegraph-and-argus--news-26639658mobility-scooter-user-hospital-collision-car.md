A mobility scooter user was rushed to hospital after a collision with a car on a busy Bradford road.

Emergency services were scrambled to the scene on Otley Road in Undercliffe on Wednesday night.

Police cordoned off the road while they investigated.

Social media pictures showed the damaged mobility scooter behind the cordon.

The ambulance service confirmed that one person was injured in the incident.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources responded to a collision between a vehicle and a mobility scooter on Otley Road in Undercliffe at 7.24pm yesterday (October 14), and one patient was conveyed to hospital."

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for comment.
