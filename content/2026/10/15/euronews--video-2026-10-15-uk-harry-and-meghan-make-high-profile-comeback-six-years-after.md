**The Duke and Duchess of Sussex**, made their** first official joint public engagement**since returning to the UK in August when they visited charities in** Nottingham** on Thursday, 15 October.

They were **greeted with cheers, hugs** and handshakes as they arrived at the Community Recording Studio, a youth charity in St Ann's that provides access to music, recording facilities and mentoring. There,**they met young people**, watched a drama workshop and listened to a recording session.

The couple applauded the performers, embraced a singer and spoke with well-wishers, while Meghan received several bouquets of flowers as they left.

The visit was the **first of three planned stops in Nottingham**, followed by Himmah Hub, a community organisation tackling food poverty, and Epic Partners, which supports young people through sport, education and mentoring.

The trip marks a **significant public return** for the Duke and Duchess of Sussex,**six years** after stepping back from**royal duties**. Nottingham holds particular significance for the couple, as it was the setting for their first joint public engagement following their** engagement announcement**in 2017. Their visit to the Community Recording Studio highlighted its work using music, performance and sport to help young people develop confidence and practical skills, as the couple seek to** rebuild their public profile in Britain**.
