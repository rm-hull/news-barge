**Demonstrators hurled stones**,** firecrackers and other objects**at riot police, who responded with** tear gas**and** water cannon**.** Fires** broke out in the city centre, engulfing trees and a roadside structure as firefighters worked to contain the flames.

Footage shows **protesters** racing through smoke-filled streets, tearing down**police barriers** and throwing**tear-gas canisters** back at officers. Shops and banks were damaged, some**looted**, while police reported** vandalism**targeting ATMs, cameras, vehicles and fencing. Burnt-out vehicles, smashed windows and wrecked offices were left behind across the** Albanian capital**, where residents and workers began clearing debris as** demonstrations** continued.

What began three days ago as **student protests** over the cost and quality of**higher education** has become a broader challenge to Prime Minister**Edi Rama's government**. Students demanding better university conditions, financial support and job prospects have joined the** Flamingo movement**, which opposes a luxury resort project linked to** Jared Kushner**and is calling for Mr Rama's** resignation**over** corruption allegations**.
