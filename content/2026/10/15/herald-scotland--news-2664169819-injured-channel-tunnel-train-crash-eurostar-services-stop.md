**Nineteen people have been injured in a major collision at the French side of the Eurotunnel, with all Eurostar services between France and the UK completely suspended.**

A statement from the Pas-de-Calais prefect says the incident involved a minibus transporting heavy goods vehicle drivers who were about to board the Shuttle.

**Read More:**

Initial reports indicate 19 people were involved, including the coach driver, the statement continues.

No deaths have been reported, and no lives are considered to be in danger.

It is understood that five people are said to be in critical condition; however, the AFP news agency says these five are likely to be downgraded to "relative emergency" or stable.
