Buchanan Galleries is welcoming six new and returning retail brands as the Glasgow shopping centre expands its offering ahead of the Christmas shopping season.

The arrivals bring more choice in fashion, beauty, accessories and gifting, including Italian leather goods, personalised phone cases, calendars and Scottish-made products.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21187648.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Buchanan Galleries. *(Image: Connor Mollison)*

Bellavera Florence, a brand specialising in Italian leather accessories, has opened a kiosk on the ground floor outside Swarovski. Its range includes handbags, wallets, belts and other accessories in a variety of colours and styles.

AI Vendi has also launched a self-service phone case vending machine on the ground floor opposite Boots. Customers can create personalised cases in under two minutes by uploading images, using AI-generated artwork, adding text or drawing their own designs.

Seasonal retailer Calendar Club has returned with its range of 2027 calendars, planners, diaries, stationery and books. Located next to Smiggle, the shop also stocks merchandise from major Premier League and Scottish Premiership football clubs and popular culture brands.

Fans of Scottish comedy can also visit the official Still Game store, which has returned with merchandise and collectibles including T-shirts, hoodies, hats and the show's official board game.

Heritage Accessories is set to return on Friday, October 16, in a larger ground-floor unit next to Mango Teen and Thomas Sabo. The retailer specialises in Scottish-made winter wear and dog accessories, including hats, gloves, scarves, Harris Tweed products, tartan dog leads and bandanas.

Glasgow-born vegan cosmetics brand Och! Vegan will return on Friday, November 13, with a range of natural and zero-waste products, including soaps, lip balms, deodorants and shampoos. The shop will be located next to John Lewis & Partners.

Kathy Murdoch, centre director at Buchanan Galleries, said: "We’re delighted to welcome this mix of new arrivals and returning favourites, providing guests with even more options to shop across accessories, fashion, beauty, gifting and more.

"With our range of big-name labels to beloved independents and Scottish businesses, guests have plenty of reason to visit Buchanan Galleries to complete their Christmas shopping under one roof."
