Fire crews from Harrogate attended a single-vehicle collision on Bilton Lane, Harrogate, yesterday evening (Wednesday).

The crash happened at 6.12pm when a car left the road and went into a hedge, trapping its occupants inside.

#### Bilton Lane, Harrogate

Using specialist equipment, firefighters moved the vehicle to allow the occupants to open the doors and free themselves.

The ambulance service then took over to carry out precautionary checks on those involved.
