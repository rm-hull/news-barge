According to a DB spokesperson, wherever DB’s house rules apply, the ban on alcohol consumption applies, including the entire station area, platforms, entrances and buildings. In some municipalities, this also includes the station forecourts, which belong to DB. Alcohol can still be purchased in shops within the train station. Drinking alcohol is also still permitted in the station’s restaurants and bars.

The alcohol ban was introduced in response to “suggestions from travellers, DB employees, railway workers from other transport companies, cities, municipalities, and tenants who desire a quieter, cleaner, and safer environment,”. Violations can result in expulsion from the premises, and in case of repeated offences, a ban from the premises can be issued, according to the railway company. The Federal Police can also be deployed to enforce the alcohol ban.

The railway company further stated that the perceived safety of travellers would improve and cleaning costs would decrease. In a survey, around 80% of respondents expressed support for the alcohol ban, the company announced on Thursday.

There is currently no general alcohol ban on Deutsche Bahn trains. A company spokesperson stated at the end of August that there are no plans to introduce one on long-distance trains. Deutsche Bahn itself sells alcoholic beverages in its on-board bistros. Passengers are also permitted to consume their own alcohol on board, “provided this is done within reasonable limits and does not disturb other passengers.”
