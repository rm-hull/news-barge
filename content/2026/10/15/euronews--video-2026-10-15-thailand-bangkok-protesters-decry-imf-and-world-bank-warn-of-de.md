Demonstrators carried **banners**, wore** masks**depicting world leaders and pulled giant** floats**, including a** piggy bank**and an** octopus**symbolising** global capitalism**. They accused the** financial institutions**of making decisions that affect** local communities**without giving residents a** meaningful say**.

**Police blocked**the march near the meeting venue, while brief** scuffles**broke out as protesters tried to hang a** banner**from a** pedestrian bridge**.

Protesters from across Thailand gathered to voice their concerns, **rejecting** the official**forum** as a platform where**ordinary people** would have little**influence**. They used a giant** octopus**to symbolise powerful** economic interests**tightening their grip on local communities and their** resources**.

The march briefly **disrupted traffic** at a busy intersection, but protesters eventually managed to display their**banner** calling for an**end** to**destructive capitalism**.
