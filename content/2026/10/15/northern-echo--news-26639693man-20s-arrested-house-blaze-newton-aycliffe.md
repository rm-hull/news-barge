A man in his 20s has been arrested after a suspected arson attack destroyed homes in Newton Aycliffe.

Two women inside managed to escape with minor injuries and were taken to hospital. They have since been discharged.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21577997.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the fire *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

A 26-year-old man was arrested Thursday (October 15) morning on suspicion of arson with intent to danger life.

He has been taken into custody where he remains.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: "The investigation continues into the circumstances and officers remain at the scene today."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21577927.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the fire *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Neighbouring houses were also damaged and had to be evacuated, although the majority of residents have since been able to return home.

Video taken by a neighbour shows the house engulfed in flames with smoke billowing above.

![Scene of the fire](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584983.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the fire *(Image: SUPPLIED)*

Drone pictures show the homes badly damaged with windows charred and the rooves caved in.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584976.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the fire *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584978.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the fire *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

They said: "It sounded like an explosion going off.

"The whole street was filled with smoke and it happened around 3am this morning."

Another said: "I woke up this morning just after 1am to the sound of screaming.

"The screaming is what woke me up and I came outside and saw the fire and all these police and fire engines here."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21578219.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the fire *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

A large police cordon remains in place today as officers investigate.

"Alternatively, you can call Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
