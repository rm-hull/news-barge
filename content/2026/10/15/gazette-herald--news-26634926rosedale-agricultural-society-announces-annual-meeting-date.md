An agricultural society will hold its annual general meeting next month.

The Rosedale and District Agricultural, Horticultural and Industrial Society is set to meet on November 10 at 7pm.

The meeting will be held in the Updale Reading Room, Rosedale East.

* **For more information about this public notice, click here.**

Attendees will consider minutes from the previous AGM, address any arising matters, and discuss proposals for the 2027 show.

The election of a president will also take place.

The AGM will be succeeded by the first meeting of the Rosedale 2027 Show Committee.

All are welcome to attend.
