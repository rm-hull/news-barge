As the festive season approaches, few places capture the magic of Christmas and New Year quite like Fairmont St Andrews.

Perched on the dramatic coastline overlooking St Andrews Bay, the luxury resort has unveiled an extensive programme of festive experiences designed to bring together families, friends, colleagues and visitors for a season filled with celebration, relaxation and unforgettable memories.

From indulgent afternoon teas and creative wreath-making workshops to family visits with Santa, glamorous party nights, festive stays and spectacular New Year celebrations, Fairmont St Andrews is inviting guests to embrace the very best of the season in a setting that combines Scottish hospitality with festive charm.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21586346.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Santa at Fairmont St Andrews *(Image: Calum Huntington)*

## **Creating Festive Traditions Together**

The festive season is often defined by the moments spent with loved ones, and Fairmont St Andrews has carefully crafted a collection of experiences that encourage guests to slow down, reconnect and celebrate together.

A new addition to the festive programme is the **Festive Wreath Making and Afternoon Tea Experience**, offering guests the opportunity to create their own handcrafted seasonal wreath before settling in for a traditional afternoon tea. Combining creativity with indulgence, the experience is perfect for friends, families, and anyone looking to embrace the festive spirit.

Set against the backdrop of the resort's beautifully decorated interiors, guests can enjoy an afternoon of festive crafting before savouring a selection of seasonal sandwiches, pastries and sweet treats, all thoughtfully prepared by Fairmont St Andrews' culinary team.

Available on selected dates throughout the season, the experience offers a wonderful opportunity to gather loved ones, learn a new skill and create a personalised festive decoration to take home.

**Festive Wreath Making & Afternoon Tea dates:**

· Sunday 29 November

· Friday 11 December

· Saturday 12 December

· Sunday 13 December

Spaces are limited, and advance booking is recommended. Guests can secure their place here.

For those seeking a more traditional celebration, Fairmont's **Festive Afternoon Tea** returns throughout December on Thursdays and Fridays. Guests can enjoy a selection of seasonal sandwiches, freshly baked scones and festive pastries in the elegant surroundings of the resort, with prices from £45 per adult and £25 per child under 12.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21586401.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fairmont St Andrews *(Image: Supplied)*

For an extra touch of festive magic, **Afternoon Tea with Santa** takes place on Saturdays and Sundays throughout December. Alongside the traditional afternoon tea experience, families will have the opportunity to meet Santa, share their Christmas wishes, and capture a treasured keepsake photograph, creating memories to cherish long after the festive season has passed. Afternoon Tea with Santa is priced at £70 per adult and £35 per child under 12/

Book Festive Afternoon Tea or Afternoon Tea with Santa here.

## **Raise a Glass for Festive Brunch with Santa**

Taking place in The Squire Restaurant, this special festive gathering combines a delicious seasonal brunch, live musical entertainment and, most importantly, an opportunity for children to meet Santa himself. As one of the resort's most popular festive experiences, the event offers families the chance to enjoy quality time together while creating magical memories in the lead-up to Christmas.

Throughout the afternoon, families will be invited to see Santa, where children can share their Christmas wishes and experience the excitement and wonder of meeting the man in red. Guests will also have the opportunity to capture a treasured keepsake photograph with Santa beside the beautifully decorated Christmas tree, creating a lasting memory to cherish long after the festive season has passed.

Alongside the magical Santa experience, guests can enjoy a festive bottomless brunch featuring seasonal favourites, festive cocktails, Prosecco, house wine and bottled beer, complemented by live musical entertainment throughout the afternoon.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21586399.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fairmont St Andrews *(Image: Supplied)*

With prices from £115 for the Festive Bottomless Brunch, £85 for the Virgin Brunch and £40 for children under 12, the event offers a memorable festive experience for all ages. Families will have the opportunity to meet Santa, capture a treasured keepsake photograph beside the Christmas tree and enjoy an afternoon filled with festive food, entertainment and seasonal magic. Early booking is recommended, with the event expected to be one of Fairmont St Andrews' most popular family celebrations of the season. Reserve your table.

## **Celebrating with Colleagues and Friends**

For businesses, organisations and groups of friends looking to celebrate in style, Fairmont St Andrews' Festive Party Nights provide the perfect opportunity to gather, reconnect and raise a glass to the season.

Designed to bring people together in a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere, each party night begins with a delicious three-course festive dinner, carefully prepared by the resort's culinary team. Guests can then continue the celebrations on the dance floor as the resident DJ keeps the party going with a mix of classic favourites and festive hits until late.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21586402.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fairmont St Andrews *(Image: Supplied)*

Whether you're rewarding colleagues, entertaining clients or gathering friends for a festive night out, these celebrations offer a hassle-free way to mark the season. Complimentary transport from St Andrews, Guardbridge and Dundee means guests can relax and enjoy the evening from start to finish.

Join a Party Night on 4, 13 or 18 December 2026, with tickets priced at £75 per person, including a three-course dinner, entertainment and return transport. Guests wishing to extend the celebrations can also book an overnight stay from £199 per room, including breakfast.

Groups looking to make the most of their Christmas celebration can take advantage of special booking incentives. Book a table of 10 and only pay for 9, with a complimentary bottle of Champagne Piaff waiting on the table on arrival. For larger groups, booking two or more tables of 10 guests unlocks an exclusive accommodation discount, making it even easier to turn the evening into an overnight festive escape. Spaces are limited, and terms apply.

For larger groups or businesses seeking something more tailored, Fairmont St Andrews also offers private festive party bookings, providing the flexibility to create bespoke celebrations for teams, clients and organisations of all sizes.

Guests are encouraged to book early, with dates expected to sell quickly as Christmas approaches. Reservations and bespoke event enquiries can be made by contacting [SAB.Events@Fairmont.com.](mailto:SAB.Events@Fairmont.com.)

## **Ringing in 2027 in Style**

As Christmas gives way to New Year celebrations, Fairmont St Andrews has planned an unforgettable way to welcome 2027, with a choice of dining experiences designed for couples, families and groups looking to celebrate in spectacular surroundings.

Set against the dramatic backdrop of St Andrews Bay, guests can toast the year gone by while enjoying exceptional dining, live entertainment and the resort's renowned hospitality. Whether seeking an elegant adults-only evening or a family celebration filled with great food and festive atmosphere, Fairmont St Andrews offers a memorable way to mark the occasion.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21586398.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fairmont St Andrews *(Image: Supplied)*

The centrepiece of the New Year's Eve celebrations is the adults-only St Andrews Bay Clubhouse & Grill experience. Guests will be welcomed with arrival drinks and canapés before sitting down to an indulgent five-course dinner at 8.00pm. Carefully selected wines accompany the evening, while live entertainment creates a vibrant atmosphere throughout the celebrations.

Priced at £195 per person, the experience culminates with a glass of Champagne at the bells as guests gather to count down to midnight. With panoramic coastal views and an intimate, sophisticated setting, the Clubhouse & Grill promises an elegant way to welcome the new year.

For families and guests looking to celebrate together, alternative dining experiences will be available across the resort. Guests can enjoy a family-friendly Italian feast in La Cucina, or choose the lively atmosphere of Squire Restaurant, where live food stations and a festive buffet create a relaxed and sociable way to celebrate. These family dining experiences are priced at £95 per adult and £45 for children aged 12 and under.

As midnight approaches, all eyes will turn to the skies above St Andrews Bay for one of the evening's most anticipated moments. A spectacular drone show will illuminate the coastline, providing a breathtaking visual display as guests count down the final moments of 2026.

Combining innovation with festive magic, the display offers a memorable alternative to traditional fireworks and creates a truly unique start to the year ahead.

With exceptional dining, entertainment and unforgettable views, Fairmont St Andrews' New Year's Eve celebrations promise a magical evening of togetherness, reflection and celebration. Book your New Year stay and experience one of the region's most sought-after destinations for welcoming 2027 in style.
