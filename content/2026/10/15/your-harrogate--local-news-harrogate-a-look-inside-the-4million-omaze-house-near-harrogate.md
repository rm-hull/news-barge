A £4million house near Harrogate, complete with its own lake, library and tennis court, is up for grabs in an Omaze prize draw.

The six-bedroom Little Ouseburn property - just a 30 minute drive from Harrogate - offers 8,453 square feet of space, with four bathrooms and a guest cottage.

Inside, an original stone fireplace welcomes visitors into the reception hall, while the drawing room features an ochre sofa, contemporary artwork and a hand-knotted rug.

The kitchen combines an AGA with yellow cabinetry and a farmhouse dining table.

There’s also a separate dining room with a large hearth, plus a library with olive-green shelving and a ladder to reach the higher books.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad0ed3a74184f13caf.png)

Outside, the grounds include woodland, an orchard and a lake with a wooden jetty.

A courtyard provides space for outdoor dining, alongside a pergola and an outdoor lounge.

The draw supports Blood Cancer UK, with a guaranteed £1million donation.

The winner will receive the house mortgage-free, with stamp duty and conveyancing fees covered, furnishings included and £250,000 cash paid in instalments. They can choose to live in it, rent it out or sell it.
