POLICE have issued an image of someone they would like to speak to following an incident in a pub in Sutton-in-Craven.

It is alleged that a phone was stolen from someone while inside the Kings Arms Inn, High Street, at around 7pm on Friday, October 9, and later found on a nearby street smashed.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police (Craven) said: "We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed someone damaging the phone or witnessed the (alleged) theft inside the pub, or who has any information that could assist the investigation."

Anyone with any information is asked to contact police either by email to: [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Information can also be passed to the North Yorkshire Police website; by calling 101, or anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
