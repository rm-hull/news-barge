A community production involving dozens of actors, historic venues and a dramatic Good Friday finale is set to transform a North Yorkshire town next Easter.

Churches in Richmond have joined together to stage a Passion Play titled Something Extraordinary, featuring more than 70 performers and using the town's historic streets as its backdrop.

The performance will conclude with a crucifixion scene in the marketplace on Good Friday morning.

Baroness Angela Harris, patron of the project, said: "It will be an epic event and promises to become as memorable as the York or Wakefield Mystery Plays."

Casting begins on October 18 and organisers encourage anyone interested to get involved through their website.

Volunteers are also needed to make costumes, marshal the event or support through the just giving campaign.
