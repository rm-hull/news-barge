A 40-bedroom Travelodge near Aberdeen Airport is to go under the hammer with a guide price of £1.6 million.

The hotel, on Burnside Drive in Dyce, will be offered at Acuitus’ commercial property auction on October 29.

Recently refurbished, the property occupies a 3.11-acre site with parking for around 100 vehicles. It is let to Travelodge until 2030, with an option to extend the lease by a further 25 years.

The hotel currently generates annual rental income of £224,204 and is located around one mile from Aberdeen Airport, a key transport hub for the North Sea energy sector.

Alexander Auterac of Acuitus said the property offered investors rental income from a nationally recognised hotel operator in a strategic location.

The auction will also feature New Telecom House in Aberdeen city centre.

The 77,940 sq ft office and development site sits beside Aberdeen Railway Station and Union Square Shopping Centre. It has a guide price of £250,000 and currently generates £2,000 in annual rental income.

The auction will be held via livestream on the Acuitus website, with bidders able to participate online, by telephone or by proxy.

Alexander Auterac of Acuitus said: "This opportunity combines the attraction of strong rental income from a nationally recognised hotel chain with the benefit of a strategic location close to Aberdeen Airport, one of the key transport hubs serving the North Sea energy sector."
