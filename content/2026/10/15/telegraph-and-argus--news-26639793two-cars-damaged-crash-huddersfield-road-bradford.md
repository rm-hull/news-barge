TWO cars were damaged in a crash on a Bradford road yesterday.

Emergency crews were called to the crash at the junction of Common Road and Huddersfield Road, Low Mooe, on Wednesday afternoon.

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Services said: "An ambulance responded to a road traffic collision on Huddersfield Road in Bradford yesterday afternoon (October 14).

"No patients were conveyed to hospital."

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for more details.
