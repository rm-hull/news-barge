A TEENAGER is due to appear in court today after two incidents - one which closed a major city centre bridge and another at the city’s hospital.

A 14-year-old girl, who we cannot name for legal reasons, is set to appear in York Magistrates' Court this morning (October 15) in connection with two counts of intentionally or recklessly causing a public nuisance.

She has also been charged with one count of possession of a bladed article in a public place.

This follows an incident reported by The Press last Monday (October 5), where eyewitnesses said **Ouse Bridge** was closed with around eight police vehicles and four fire engines from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service at the scene.

A second incident also took place on the evening of October 13 at York Hospital where emergency service vehicles were spotted at York Hospital.
