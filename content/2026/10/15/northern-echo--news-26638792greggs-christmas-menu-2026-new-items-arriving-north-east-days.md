Greggs fans across the North East will soon be able to get their hands on the bakery chain's Christmas menu.

The fresh update includes the return of its much-loved Festive Bake and several new additions for 2026.

The seasonal line-up arrives in around 200 Greggs branches across the North East on Thursday, October 22, as well as nationwide.

The menu features familiar Christmas flavours alongside new options, from a pigs in blankets-inspired toastie to a hot honey pizza and a selection of seasonal sweet treats.

Here's what's on offer this year.

**Festive Bake**

The Festive Bake returns with a refreshed recipe, once again bringing Christmas dinner flavours together inside a pastry parcel.

The filling includes chicken, smoked ham hock, sweetcure bacon, soft cheese and spiced pork stuffing in a sage and cranberry sauce, wrapped in golden puff pastry with a crunchy crumb topping.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21583813.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Festive Bake *(Image: Greggs)*

**Pigs Under Blankets Toastie**

One of the new additions for 2026 is the Pigs Under Blankets Toastie, which combines smoked sweetcure bacon, pork sausage and Cheddar cheese with cranberry and red onion relish.

The filling is served in sourdough white bread, topped with béchamel sauce and a blend of cheeses.

**Festive Feast Baguette**

The Festive Feast Baguette is also returning to the menu, offering another option for those after a Christmas-inspired lunch.

It joins the Festive Bake as one of the returning savoury choices in this year's seasonal range.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21583812.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Festive Feast Baguette *(Image: Greggs)*

**Festive Flatbread**

For those looking for a lighter option, the Festive Flatbread contains less than 390 calories.

It includes chicken, sweetcure bacon, dried cranberries, pork, sage and onion stuffing, mayonnaise and sage and onion seasoning.

**Pepperoni Pizza with Hot Honey**

Moving away from the traditional Christmas dinner flavours, the new Pepperoni Pizza with Hot Honey adds a spicy-sweet option to the menu.

The focaccia-style pizza is topped with cheese, pizza sauce, jalapeños and red onion, finished with sweet hot honey.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21583814.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pepperoni Pizza with Hot Honey *(Image: Greggs)*

**Millionaire's Doughnut**

The Millionaire's Doughnut is one of the new sweet additions, featuring a caramel filling with salted caramel icing and shortcake pieces on top.

**Double Chocolate Doughnut**

If single chocolate isn't enough, the Double Chocolate Doughnut comes filled and topped with two types of chocolate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21583820.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Double Chocolate Doughnut *(Image: Greggs)*

**White Chocolate Gingerbread Cookie**

The White Chocolate Gingerbread Cookie has several sweet toppings, including white chocolate buttons, chocolate lentils, crystallised ginger and caramel balls.

It is finished with a white chocolate-flavour coating and gold sugar crunch.

**Candy Cane Mint Iced Matcha Latte**

The seasonal drinks range this year includes a Candy Cane Mint Iced Matcha Latte, mixing matcha green tea with mint syrup and peppermint sugar sprinkles.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21583815.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Candy Cane Mint Iced Matcha Latte *(Image: Greggs)*

**Candy Cane Mint Hot Chocolate**

The candy cane mint flavour also features in two chocolate drinks, with iced and hot chocolate versions joining the festive line-up.

**Hazelnut Crunch and Salted Caramel Lattes**

Hazelnut Crunch and Salted Caramel Lattes round off the seasonal drinks selection this year.

Again, these are both available hot or iced.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21583816.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Iced Hazelnut Crunch Latte *(Image: Greggs)*

**Read More:**

The full festive menu will be available in Greggs shops across the North East and nationwide from Thursday, October 22.

Customers can order ahead using Click + Collect through the Greggs app, or have items delivered through Just Eat and Uber Eats.

Customers can also grab a free item after nine qualifying purchases across different food and drink categories.
