Rudding Park is launching an after-dark version of its Yorkshire-themed adventure golf course this Halloween.

The Great Yorkshire Golf Adventure… After Dark will open on Saturday 31st October, with visitors able to tackle its 18 illuminated holes before enjoying pizza, a drink and an after-party.

The launch event will feature a seasonally decorated entrance and surprises around the course, followed by a live DJ in the Masters Suite.

The course brings Yorkshire’s landmarks and legends to life across 18 holes, with the new evening experience giving visitors the chance to explore it after sunset.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ad0e84a5d23e622c77.png)

An event description reads:

> “When the sun goes down, the lights come up… this is God's Own County after dark.”

The Halloween event will begin at 5.30pm with a shotgun start, meaning groups will be allocated different starting holes before making their way around the full course.

From 7pm, players can head to the Masters Suite for the after-party, with a drink from the bar and a pizza of their choice included in the ticket price.

Tickets cost £30 for adults and £20 for children aged 16 and under, with a maximum of four players per group.

The After Dark programme is listed as running from Saturday 31st October 2026 to Saturday 27th March 2027.

Tickets are available through Rudding Park’s events **website**.
