A man has been arrested after police discovered a cannabis farm inside a building on Little Horton Lane.

West Yorkshire Police said officers were called to the property at 12.37pm on Thursday after an energy company reported "suspicious circumstances".

When officers attended, they found a cannabis farm inside the building.

A 45-year-old man was arrested at the scene on suspicion of being concerned in the supply of a Class B controlled drug.

He remains in police custody.

The police activity attracted attention on social media, where photographs showed officers outside the large stone-fronted building.

Photographs appeared to show a large white sheet hanging from an upper-floor window to the pavement below as items were removed from the building.

Other images appeared to show officers loading bags into a police van parked outside.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 12.37pm today officers were called by an energy company to report of suspicious circumstances at a property on Little Horton Lane.

"On arrival officers located a cannabis farm.

"A 45 year old man was arrested from the property on suspicion of being concerned in the supply of a controlled drug, Class B.

"He remains in custody."
