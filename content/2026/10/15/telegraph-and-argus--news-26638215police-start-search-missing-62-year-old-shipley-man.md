Police have started a search for a missing Bradford man.

West Yorkshire Police reported that John, 62, is missing from Shipley.

He is described as a white male, stocky build, grey hair and beard, and speaks with a Scottish accent.

He is believed to be wearing a black jacket and black trousers.

Anyone who has any information call police on 101 and quote log number 0870 14/10/2026.
