Police are concerned for the welfare of a missing 39-year-old Bradford woman.

Stephanie, 39, who has been reported missing, was last seen in the Duckworth Lane area of Bradford yesterday.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We are appealing for the public’s help in reporting any sightings of a woman who has been reported missing in Bradford.

"Stephanie, 39, was last seen in the Duckworth Lane area of Bradford at around 5pm yesterday (Wednesday, October 14).

There are concerns for her welfare and anyone who has seen her since that time is asked to make contact with the police.

"She is described as white, 5ft 9ins tall, with dark, wavy, shoulder-length hair.

"When she was last seen, she was wearing a black Adidas long-sleeved top, a knee-length puffer coat and cream/beige leggings. She was carrying a blue supermarket freezer bag with penguins on it.

"Anyone who believes they have seen Stephanie or who has any information about her movements or whereabouts is asked to contact Bradford CID via Live Chat online or by calling 101, quoting log 1252 of October 14.
