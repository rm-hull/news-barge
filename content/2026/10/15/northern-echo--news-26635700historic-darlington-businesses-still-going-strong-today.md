From traditional butchers and menswear specialists to family-run cafés and restaurants, Darlington is home to several independent businesses that have become institutions in their own right.

While new businesses continue to open across the town, some familiar names have been serving customers for decades, with a handful tracing their roots back more than a century.

Many have survived changing shopping habits, economic downturns and the rise of online retail, remaining firmly at the heart of the community.

As high streets across the UK continue to evolve, these businesses provide a reminder of the independent spirit that has helped shape Darlington for generations.

Together, they represent hundreds of years of trading history and continue to attract loyal customers old and new.

Here are some of the longest-established independent businesses still trading in Darlington today.

## Leggs - since 1911

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21579292.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Leggs has been a fixture on Darlington's high street since 1911, making it one of the town's oldest surviving independent businesses. *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

Among the oldest businesses on the list is Leggs, which first opened its doors in 1911.

More than 100 years later, the fashion retailer remains a fixture on Skinnergate, continuing to offer clothing for a range of occasions.

## Taylor's Butchers - since 1922

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21579274.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Taylor's Butchers has been serving Darlington since 1922 and remains a well-known independent trader on Skinnergate. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Taylor's Butchers has been serving customers for more than a century after starting life as a small market stall in 1922.

It remains a familiar Skinnergate fixture and one of the town’s longest-running independent traders.

## Alderson Butchers - since 1934

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21579251.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alderson Butchers in Cockerton is one of Darlington's longest-established family businesses, having served customers since 1934. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Based in Cockerton Village, Alderson Butchers was established in 1934 and has remained family-run throughout its history.

The traditional butcher prides itself on sourcing locally and providing fresh produce to customers across the area.

## Three Squares Café - more than 80 years

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21579259.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Three Squares Café has been part of Darlington town-centre life for more than 80 years, serving generations of customers. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

One of Darlington's most beloved cafés, Three Squares has been part of town-centre life for more than 80 years.

The Skinnergate business has served generations of customers and recently celebrated record takings despite opening fewer hours than in previous years.

Manager Louise recently described the support received from customers as proof that there is still strong demand for traditional independents in the town centre.

## Glenwood Paint Supplies - more than 50 years

Family-run Glenwood Paint Supplies has been trading in Darlington for more than half a century.

With three locations across the town, the business supplies paints and decorating products to both trade and DIY customers.

## Burnside Carpets - more than 50 years

Another family business to reach the half-century milestone is Burnside Carpets.

Based on High Northgate, the company has been serving customers for more than 50 years, offering flooring solutions and advice from an experienced local team.

## Geoffrey Gillows - since 1966

Geoffrey Gillows established itself as a menswear destination when it opened in 1966.

Now located in the Imperial Quarter on Grange Road, the store also stocks luxury womenswear and remains a prominent independent retailer in the town.

## Eastern Bamboo - since the 1970s

Eastern Bamboo has been serving Chinese cuisine in Darlington since the 1970s.

Located on Northgate, the restaurant has become a familiar name for generations of diners seeking freshly prepared Chinese dishes.

## The Old Yard Tapas Bar - since 1995

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21211376.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The Old Yard Tapas Bar has been welcoming customers since 1995.

The Bondgate venue is known for its Greek and Spanish-inspired dishes and has become a popular destination for food, drinks, live music and sport.

## The Cheese & Wine Shop - since 1999

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20312901.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The Cheese & Wine Shop has been part of Darlington's food scene since 1999.

Now based on Skinnergate, the business specialises in artisan cheeses, fine wines and deli products sourced from local and European producers.
