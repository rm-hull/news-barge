A UK furniture company has closed after 12 years of trading, having entered liquidation.

Furniture Stop UK, which began trading in November 2014, was an online retailer of "modern and classy" furniture.

It held a poor Trustpilot rating of 1.3 out of 5 (from 162 reviews), with customers criticising "extremely poor" service and undelivered items.

## Furniture Stop UK closes after entering liquidation

After 12 years in business, Furniture Stop has entered creditors' voluntary liquidation and closed.

A winding-up notice was approved on October 6, with Nicola Meadows and Mark Newton of AABRS Limited appointed joint liquidators, according to The Gazette.

As a result, the company's website has been shut down completely and is no longer accessible.
