British Transport Police is appealing for witnesses to come forward following a sexual assault on board a train.

Just before 9.30pm on Saturday October 3, a woman sexually assaulted a man as he walked down the carriage on a ScotRail service from Dalmuir to Larkhall.

Detectives are appealing for anyone who was travelling on the train and witnessed the incident, or who may have challenged the woman, to come forward.

**READ MORE:**

Anyone with information is asked to contact British Transport Police by texting 61016 or by calling 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 959 of 3 October.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
