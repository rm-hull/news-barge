A ‘DELIBERATE’ fire has been set close to a building in a busy North Yorkshire town this morning.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service say that both of their fire engines from Malton were called to Norton Road at 6.15am this morning (October 15) after reports of a skip fire.

The blaze – which measured 2 x 2 and was ‘close to a commercial building – was extinguished using a hose reel jet.

“There was no damage to the building and police were informed as this fire was believed to had been deliberate,” said a spokesperson for the service.
