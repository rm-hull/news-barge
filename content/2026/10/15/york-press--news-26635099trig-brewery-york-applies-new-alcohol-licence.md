A brewery has applied for a new premises licence in York.

Trig Brewery Limited submitted the application to the City of York Council on October 8 for Unit 2a Northminster, Harwood Road, Upper Poppleton.

The proposed licence would allow the sale of alcohol from midday to 11pm, seven days a week.

The public can view the application by arranging to visit the City of York Council Licensing Team at Hazel Court, EcoDepot, James Street, York.

The office is open Monday to Friday, 8.30am to 5pm.

Anyone wishing to comment on the application can do so by writing to the City of York Council Licensing Team or by emailing [licensing@york.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@york.gov.uk.)

Comments must be submitted by November 4, within 28 days of the application being made.

It is important to note that making a false statement in relation to a premises licence application is a criminal offence and could result in an unlimited fine.

The council's licensing team can be contacted on 01904 552422 for further information.
