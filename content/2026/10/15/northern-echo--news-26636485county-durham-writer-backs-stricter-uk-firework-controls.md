I am writing in support of the UK Government’s recent proposals to introduce stricter controls on fireworks.

The current consultation includes measures such as restricting the sale of louder fireworks to professional users, banning products that present unacceptable risks, and reviewing classifications to better reflect the dangers posed by different types of fireworks.

These proposals offer significant safety benefits for individuals, families, and communities.

Every year, fireworks are responsible for injuries, burns, property damage, and fire-related incidents.

Reducing public access to the loudest and highest risk products could help prevent avoidable accidents while still allowing people to enjoy celebrations in a responsible manner.

Stronger controls would also help address anti-social behaviour associated with the misuse of fireworks.

Late-night use, setting fireworks off in public spaces, and irresponsible handling can create fear and disruption within neighbourhoods.
