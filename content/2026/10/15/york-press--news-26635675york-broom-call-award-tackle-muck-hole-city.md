**‘York in Broom’: Call for award to tackle 'muck hole' city**

RE: The Press letter 'York could be cleaner’. Mr D A Brown’s letter of October 10, alarmingly entitled ‘York branded “muck hole” over litter and weeds’ highlighted this perennial problem.

Over the decades the cleanliness and tidiness of York’s streets has been an issue with periodic flurries of letters to the Press. The exponential growth in tourism and a city centre dominated by cafes, restaurants and bars will have added to the problem.

It would help if all city centre shops, businesses and ‘eateries’ took care of their own frontage, pavement and surrounds on a regular basis and not just as a ‘flash in the dustpan’.

Amongst businesses showing a good example in times past were Barbican Bookshop in Fossgate and Pickerings’ Bookshop in Shambles where staff swept, cleaned and swilled the pathways and roundabout, each morning before opening.

No doubt some current establishments do the same.

It would be good if more joined them in caring for their bit.

It needs a concerted and consistent effort.

Such practice, if widespread, would surely help the council which appears stretched in this department and also enhance the city’s reputation.

The Press might consider an annual competition and award for the smartest, weed-free and cleanest shop front and ‘walkway’. Call it ‘York in Broom’ if you like.

*Derek Reed,*

*Middlethorpe Drive,*

*York*
