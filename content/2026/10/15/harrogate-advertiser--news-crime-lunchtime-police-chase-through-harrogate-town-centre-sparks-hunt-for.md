# Lunchtime police chase through Harrogate town centre sparks hunt for driver

## Traffic officers chased a vehicle through Harrogate town centre yesterday lunchtime (October 14) before the driver dumped the car outside the Convention Centre

Police chased a driver through Harrogate town centre yesterday afternoon, sparking a search for a suspect who fled on foot.

![Police carried out enquiries at Swinton Court in connection with the pursuit](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/15/14/27/IMG_8228.jpeg?trim=0,0,29,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


The incident began at 1.26pm when traffic officers spotted a Volvo XC40 on Hookstone Road and ordered the driver to pull over.

Instead of stopping, the driver continued into the town centre, forcing police to launch a pursuit

The chase took place across busy town centre streets as lunchtime shoppers and motorists looked on.

It reached an abrupt conclusion outside the Harrogate Convention centre, where the driver suddenly abandoned the Volvo and fled the scene on foot.

The vehicle was recovered by police outside the venue, but the driver managed to escape.

Following the pursuit, armed officers attended Swinton Court in Harlow Hill later that afternoon to carry out investigations in connection with the incident.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police told the *Harrogate Advertiser* that the officers were at the scene "making enquiries into a failed to stop incident in the town earlier in the afternoon."

They added that enquiries remain ongoing to locate the driver.

Police are appealing for anyone who witnessed the chase or saw the Volvo being abandoned yesterday afternoon to come forward.

Information can be submitted online via the 'Tell us about' tab on the force's website, or through their weekday Live Chat portal.

Alternatively, members of the public can call 101. To remain anonymous, contact the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Quote North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260202334 when providing details.
