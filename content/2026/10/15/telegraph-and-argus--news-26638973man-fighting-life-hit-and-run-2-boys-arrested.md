Police have launched an appeal as a man remains in hospital fighting for his life after a hit-and-run in Bradford.

A pedestrian, a man in his 70s, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries after a collision with a motorcycle on Legrams Lane on Wednesday afternoon.

The Suzuki SV 650, which was travelling in the direction of the city centre, was being chased by police at the time.

This was after the rider reportedly failed to stop for officers minutes earlier on Thornton Road.

Two 17-year-old boys have been arrested on suspicion of offences including theft of motor vehicle, failing to stop and causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

## Police appeal

On Thursday, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We are appealing for any witnesses or available footage after a pedestrian was seriously injured in a collision in Bradford yesterday afternoon (Wednesday, October 14).

"At 3.33pm, a motorcycle travelling on Legrams Lane near the junction with Archibald Street was in collision with a pedestrian.

"The Suzuki SV 650 motorcycle, which was travelling in the direction of the city centre, was being pursued by officers at the time of the collision due to the rider having failed to stop for police minutes earlier on Thornton Road.

"The pedestrian, a man in his 70s, was taken to hospital with life-threatening injuries.

"The rider and a passenger on the motorcycle left the scene of the collision.

"Two 17-year-old boys were later arrested on suspicion of offences including theft of motor vehicle, failing to stop and causing serious injury by dangerous driving. "They remain in custody at this time.

"Officers from the Roads Policing Unit are appealing for anyone who witnessed any part of this incident to make contact.

"Members of the public who were either driving or who live or work in the area are also asked to check any dashcams or CCTV to check if they have captured any relevant footage.

"Anyone with information can pass this to the Roads Policing Unit via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat or by calling 101, quoting log 995 of October 14."

On Thursday, a spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources responded to a collision between a motorcycle and a pedestrian at the junction of Summerville Road and Legrams Lane in Bradford yesterday afternoon (October 14) and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
