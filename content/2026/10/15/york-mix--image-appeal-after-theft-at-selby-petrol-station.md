Police investigating a theft in Selby want to identify this man.

It happened at the Jet fuel station on Bawtry Road at around 8.45pm on Tuesday 15 September.

A suspect requested a “substantial amount” to be added to a top-up card and then left the premises without paying for it.

#### Incident location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are releasing the image of a man we would like to speak to following a theft at a petrol station in the Selby area.

“If you recognise the man pictured, please get in touch as he may have information that could assist our investigation.”
