An “honesty” money tin has been stolen from an egg stand at a farm in North Yorkshire, police.

Officers have issued a photograph of a woman they want to speak to about the theft from the farm in Suffield, near Scarborough, on Sunday, October 4.

North Yorkshire Police said the tin — containing money left for the eggs — was stolen at about 12.30pm.

The force said the woman pictured may have information to help its investigation.

Police are urging anyone who recognises the woman or who has information about the theft to email [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Quote reference 12260196077 when passing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
