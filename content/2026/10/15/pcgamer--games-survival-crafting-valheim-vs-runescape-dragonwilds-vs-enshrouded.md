I've seen a million posts all asking the same advice: **"Should I get Valheim, RuneScape Dragonwilds, or Enshrouded first?"** And as someone who can't stop sinking hundreds of hours into mining the same three types of rock, I'm qualified enough to help you decide. I've also recruited my colleague, Chris Livingston, who is always up to in-game riffraff resulting in funny animal encounters or injuries like some sort of bizarre, survival-crafting Jeff Corwin.

### The one for building sickos

**🥇 Enshrouded**   
*🥈Valheim 🥉Dragonwilds*

Enshrouded is the best set of Legos, hands down. It has blocks in all shapes and sizes, tons of furniture, terraforming, NPC villagers, and even tools for adding a sprinkle of decay to buildings.

![RuneScape: Dragonwilds screenshot](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qXjjBLVnbV7pv32EueJkML-1200-80.jpg)

### Excellence in hunting you for sport (raids)

**🥇 Dragonwilds**  
*🥈Valheim*

Valheim raids are a close second (a great way to get resources delivered to your front door) but there's something rad about a dragon boss showing up at your house and carpetbombing you with poison while you're trying to get cozy.

![A large troll in the snow](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/GMqatxWJpKRWMWrUhhXtdC-1200-80.jpg)

### Best 'There and back again' (exploration)

**🥇Valheim**  
*🥈Enshrouded🥉Dragonwilds*

The handcrafted worlds of Enshrouded and Dragonwilds are both awesome, but setting out on a voyage into the procedural world of Valheim is a mysterious thrill each and every time, which makes coming home again all the more satisfying.

![Warrior in a fantasy world](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/5tFa9ArT4UFUugKQ4S73DM-1200-80.jpg)

### Most deserving of a Michelin Star

**🥇Enshrouded**  
*🥈Dragonwilds🥉Valheim*

Enshrouded wins us over with its cooking mechanics—holding your own meaty kebabs over an open flame is a nice touch—but this was a close one. The multi-step process in Dragonwilds is fun to micromanage, while Valheim's system feels the most rewarding.

## Valheim vs Enshrouded vs Dragonwilds: Our picks

![PC Gamer headshot - Andrea Shearon](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qGDcAqeJDHFab8LYduM29E-140-80.png)

I've played Enshrouded since it launched in early access, and it claimed the top spot in my list of survival crafting favorites long before we even had a 1.0 release date. Valheim is a classic, and Dragonwilds has grown on me, but nothing beats Enshrouded's base building or acrobatic combat. I'll never get tired of surprising enemies from the sky with my glider.

![PC Gamer headshot - Christopher Livingston](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/vMPWcamtj9aoVBYFtt2Hp7-140-80.png)

I like Dragonwilds and Enshrouded is really special, but Valheim is easily my favorite—it's not just a great survival game but something I'm sure I'll keep returning to year after year. It's especially great if you think you don't like survival games, because here's the thing: you do.
