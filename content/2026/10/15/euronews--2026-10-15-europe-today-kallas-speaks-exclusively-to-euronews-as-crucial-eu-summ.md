## Kaja Kallas, the EU's top diplomat, speaks exclusively to Maria Tadeo as EU leaders arrive in Brussels for a crucial EU summit on spiralling energy prices, an unfinished seven-year budget, and Donald Trump's pressure on Ukraine. We also interview Abu Abughazaleh, the Jordanian Investment Minister.

## On today's show:

**Shona Murray** reports from the European Council, where EU leaders open a two-day summit on energy prices, the bloc's next seven-year budget and support for Ukraine, and deteriorating relations between the US and Europe.

**Kaja Kallas**, EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, speaks exclusively to our Europe editor** Maria Tadeo** about the summit and Ukraine.

**Beate Meinl-Reisinger**, Austria's Foreign Minister, tells Euronews why enlargement is moving so slowly and sets out her proposal to give candidate countries access to the Single Market as they complete reforms.

**Tareq Abu Ghazaleh**, Jordan's Investment Minister, discusses what he wants from Brussels beyond the EU's roughly €3 billion commitment through 2027, and whether Jordan can become a regional hub while conflict in the Middle East continues.
