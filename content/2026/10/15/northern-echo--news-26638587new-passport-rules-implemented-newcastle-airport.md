Arriving at Newcastle Airport will soon become much easier with the launch of a new, faster system.

A new contactless system, designed to speed up arrivals while maintaining existing border security checks, has begun rolling out at UK airports.

The phased rollout began at East Midlands Airport on October 6, initially for British citizens, with the technology expected to be introduced at all UK airports, including Newcastle, by early 2027.

Testing has shown the contactless system can be up to 25% faster than using a passport on an eGate.

## How the new contactless airport eGates work

Instead of placing a passport on the reader, passengers approach the eGate and have their photograph taken.

The system then compares the live image with verified immigration, travel and passport information.

If the checks are completed successfully, the gate opens automatically, and the passenger can walk through without presenting their passport to the machine.

The Home Office says the facial technology verifies a passenger's identity before the UK's immigration and security checks are completed.

However, there is an important catch for holidaymakers - you must still carry your passport.

If further checks are needed, passengers may be asked to put their passport on the eGate reader or speak to a Border Force officer, just as they do under the current system.

Border Force officers will also continue to monitor the eGates and provide assistance where needed.

## Which airports are getting contactless eGates?

The technology is being introduced gradually rather than all at once.

The airports included in the phased rollout are:

* Birmingham
* Bristol
* Cardiff
* East Midlands
* Edinburgh
* Gatwick
* Glasgow
* Heathrow
* London City
* Luton
* Manchester
* Newcastle
* Stansted

The Home Office expects contactless eGates to be available at all UK airports by early 2027.
