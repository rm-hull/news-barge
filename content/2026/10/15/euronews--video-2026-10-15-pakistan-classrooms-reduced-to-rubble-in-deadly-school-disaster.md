**Rescue teams** rushed to the scene, where**classrooms** were left strewn with**rubble**, school bags, books and** children's shoes**.** Injured pupils**were taken by** ambulance**to** Lady Reading Hospital**, where medical staff treated** survivors**as distressed** relatives**gathered outside. Images from the school show** abandoned belongings**scattered across the floors, offering a stark glimpse of the** devastation**left behind by the** collapse**.

**Peshawar’s police chief** said the roof had been weakened by thebuilding’s**poor structural condition** and the weight of construction work.**Heavy rain** may also have**played a role**, but the exact cause remains under investigation.

Questions have emerged over whether the **tragedy** could have been**prevented**. A** father**who** lost two children**told AFP that pupils had reportedly** warned**the** school administration**earlier that morning that the building was showing** signs of collapse**. His account raises concerns about whether** warning signs**were** overlooked**before the** roof gave way**.

At the hospital in **Peshawar**, ambulances continued to bring in** casualties**as** families**waited for news of their loved ones. The** circumstances**surrounding the collapse and the** reported warnings**have yet to be** independently established**.
