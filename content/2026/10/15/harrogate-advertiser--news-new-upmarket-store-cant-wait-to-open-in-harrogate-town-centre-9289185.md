# Retail brand says 'we can’t wait to open the doors and welcome you into our new store in Harrogate'

## “We can’t wait to open the doors and welcome you into our new store in Harrogate”

A new store is to open in Harrogate town centre across several floors with a reputation for high quality.

![Harrogate is to welcome the opening of a new store by British furniture and interiors brand Neptune.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/15/10/08/NeptuneStoreDrawing.jpeg?trim=128,0,289,0&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


British furniture and interiors brand Neptune will be opening at 47-49 James Street, where ProCook shop used to be.

The upmarket homewares retail brand is well known for good design, exceptional craftsmanship, using natural materials.

The company is currently restoring a terraced building near Princes Street, where furniture, home accessories and design services will be offered across several floors.

The new premises will combine a showroom with a design studio, helping customers plan kitchens and other rooms in their homes.

Neptune revealed some of the details of its plans for Harrogate online recently.

The post said: “We can’t wait to open the doors and welcome you into our new Neptune home in Harrogate.

“Set within a characterful terrace on Princes Street, this beautiful building will be thoughtfully restored to create an inspiring destination.

"Across several floors, you will be able to discover the full Neptune experience, from considered furniture and timeless accessories to our expert design services and our team who will bring it all to life.”

Neptune was founded in 1996 by two friends, John Sims-Hilditch and Giles Redman, Neptune to sell kitchens, furniture, lighting and home accessories.

It now has more than 30 stores across the UK and Europe.

The opening date for its new Harrogate store will be announced soon.
