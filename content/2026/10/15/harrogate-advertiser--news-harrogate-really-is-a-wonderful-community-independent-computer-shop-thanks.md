# 'Harrogate really is a wonderful community': Independent computer shop thanks public after theft appeal goes viral

## An independent computer shop in Harrogate has thanked the local community after receiving an overwhelming show of support following a recent shoplifting incident.

Last week, Phase 4 Computers appealed for information after stock was stolen from its shop, describing the theft as a significant blow for a small, family-run business.

![Phase 4 Computers, an independent computer shop in Harrogate, has appealed for information after a quantity of stock was stolen during a shoplifting incident](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/09/8/16/unnamed-(4).jpeg?trim=0,166,0,166&crop=&width=990&quality=65)


Since sharing details of the incident on social media, the business says its Facebook post has been viewed more than 120,000 times, attracting hundreds of comments, reactions and shares from people across Harrogate and beyond.

The team says they have been deeply touched by the response, with messages of encouragement flooding in from customers, local residents and supporters.

A spokesperson for Phase 4 Computers said: "We certainly didn't expect the response we've had over the last few days.

"Our Facebook post has now received over 120,000 views, hundreds of interactions and an incredible number of shares.

"But more importantly, we've been overwhelmed by the kindness, messages of support and lovely comments from people across Harrogate and beyond.

"As a small, independent, family-run business, it really does mean a lot to us."

The increased attention has also introduced the shop to new customers, with the team revealing that many people were surprised to discover they also stock popular Warhammer products alongside their computer and technology services.

The business confirmed that any relevant information received from members of the public has been passed to North Yorkshire Police, who are now dealing with the matter.

Rather than focusing on the theft itself, the owners said they wanted to use the opportunity to thank everyone who had shared the appeal or offered words of support.

They added: "For now, we just wanted to say a massive thank you to everyone who's supported us, shared our post or simply left a kind word.

"Harrogate really is a wonderful community, and we're proud to be part of it."
