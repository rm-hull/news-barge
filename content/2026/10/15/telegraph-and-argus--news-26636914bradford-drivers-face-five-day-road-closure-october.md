A ROAD in Bradford is set to close for five days due to water works.

Back Apsley Crescent will be shut from the rear of No. 230 Lumb Lane to the rear of No. 252 Lumb Lane.

The closure will start at 8am on October 19 and is expected to be in place until 6pm on October 23.

The City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council said the road will be open to vehicles working on the site.

Pedestrians will have access throughout the closure.

Traffic will be redirected via the unaffected section of Back Apsley Crescent and Lumb Lane.

The council has stated that the closure could be extended for up to 18 months if necessary.

The closure comes under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 - Section 14.
