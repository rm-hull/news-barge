A BRADFORD street will be temporarily closed for water works.

Howard Street, between numbers 14 and 24, will be shut to traffic for three days from October 20.

The closure is for water works.

The road will be closed from 8am on October 20 until 6pm on October 22. However, if needed, the closure could extend for up to 18 months.

During this time, only vehicles involved in the works will be allowed access.

Pedestrian access will not be affected.

Alternative routes for other vehicles include Little Horton Lane, Shester Street, Morley Street, and Lansdowne Place.

The City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council has issued the closure notice under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, Section 14.

The closure notice was published on October 8.
