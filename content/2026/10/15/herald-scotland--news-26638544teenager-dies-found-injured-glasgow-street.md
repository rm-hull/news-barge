A teenager has died following a "disturbance" in Glasgow, police have said.

Around 6.05pm on Wednesday, October 14, police were called to a report of a 17-year-old male found injured following a disturbance in the Ashgill Road area of the city.

Emergency services attended and he was pronounced dead at the scene. His family have been made aware.

A 22-year-old man has been arrested in connection with the incident.

Police said officers remain in the area as enquiries continue to establish the full circumstances.

Chief Inspector Mhairi Crawford said: “Our thoughts are with the family of the man who has died at what is a very difficult time for them.

“I am aware of the impact this kind of incident can have on our communities and would like to reassure everyone that extensive enquiries are ongoing.

“Anyone with concerns can speak to an officer at the scene or contact us via 101.”

Part of Ashgill Road, which is in the Milton area of the city, was closed off to traffic due to the police investigation.
