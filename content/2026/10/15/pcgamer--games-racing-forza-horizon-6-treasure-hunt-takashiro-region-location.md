**🚘** The treasure chest is in east Takashiro at a rest area surrounded by pampas grass, southeast of Soni Circuit.

Autumn is here in Forza Horizon 6's Horizon Meets series and that means a new Treasure Hunt to track down for some season points. These points will let you earn the new seasonal cars that are currently on offer.

This week's chest is in the east of the Takashiro region, at a rest area surrounded by long pampas grass southeast of Soni Circuit, pictured on the map above. To reach this chest, you need to:

* When passing the entrance to Soni Circuit going west to east, turn right down the winding road.
* Take a left down the road into the fields of long pampas grass.
* Keep an eye out for the rest area on your left side close to the pool on the map.

You could also look for the brown spot on the map with the pond in east Takashiro (which marks the long grass) to find it.

Smash the chest to get 100,000 credits, 5,000 XP, and three Season Progress. Also make sure to complete the new photo challenge, too, if you're looking for extra points.
