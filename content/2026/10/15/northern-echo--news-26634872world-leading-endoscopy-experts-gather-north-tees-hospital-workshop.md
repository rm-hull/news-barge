Some of the world's leading endoscopy experts gathered in the North East to demonstrate new procedures and the latest medical technology.

The two-day workshop at University Hospital of North Tees brought together clinicians, trainees and healthcare professionals to examine new techniques, technology and best practice in advanced endoscopy.

Professor Vikramjit Mitra, Dr Iosif Beintaris and Dr John Jacob convened the North Tees Advanced Endotherapy Workshop 2026 on October 8 and 9.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21578059.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Professor Vikramjit Mitra, Dr Iosif Beintaris and Dr John Jacob *(Image: Supplied)*

Professor Mitra said: "Hosting this conference shows the strength of the endoscopy expertise within our team at the University Hospitals Tees.

"We’re proud to be seen as leaders in the field of advanced endoscopy and we’re thrilled to be offering a programme that includes live clinical demonstrations, expert lectures and case-study based discussions."

He also highlighted the event’s focus on digital innovation.

Professor Mitra said: "We also explored the role of artificial intelligence in endoscopy and how we can use the technology to improve patient care.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21578060.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Professor Vikramjit Mitra *(Image: Supplied)*

"We never stand still here – we are always looking for that next procedure that can make a patient’s life better."

Professor Derek Bell opened the event and praised the University Hospitals Tees endoscopy team for its commitment to training and sharing knowledge.

The Royal College of Physicians, UK, and the British Society of Gastroenterology endorsed the workshop, which featured speakers from the UK and abroad, including India.
