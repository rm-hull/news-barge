* Good afternoon, we are covering the sentencing of motorcyclist Gabriel Horsley-Ling after he admitted causing death by dangerous driving.
* The 23-year-old was riding along Yarm Road, Darlington, on February 28, 2024, when he hit an HGV.
* Nineteen-year-old Carla Scown died in the smash near the junction with Barrington Way at about 5.30pm.
* Horsley-Ling, of Thorntree Gardens, Middleton St George, admitted causing death by dangerous driving after crashing his Honda CBF 125 motorbike.
