SIR David Jason is set to return to Yorkshire.

The national treasure will appear in Scarborough in 2027 for a special show at Scarborough Spa Grand Hall on Sunday, November 14.

An Evening with Sir David Jason will see the actor share personal stories, career highlights and behind-the-scenes memories from more than 60 years in television.

Ross Anderson, sales and marketing manager for Scarborough Spa, said: "We're absolutely delighted to welcome Sir David to the Spa.

"Few performers have had such a lasting impact on British television, and this promises to be a fascinating, funny and heartwarming evening.

"We know audiences from Scarborough, Yorkshire and beyond will be eager to hear Sir David share the stories behind some of the nation's most-loved television moments."

Sir David will discuss his life and work, including his rise from a young North London electrician with a love for amateur dramatics to one of Britain’s most celebrated actors.

He will also reflect on earning a knighthood and his long-standing connection to Yorkshire, where he spent much of his career living and filming.

In 2026, he was named an Honorary Yorkshireman by the Yorkshire Society in recognition of his contributions to the region through shows like A Touch of Frost and The Darling Buds of May.

During the evening, fans can expect fresh stories and rare video clips from his most iconic roles, including Del Boy Trotter in Only Fools and Horses, Detective Inspector Jack Frost in A Touch of Frost, and Granville in Open All Hours.

Sir David is known for shaping the landscape of British television comedy and drama by creating characters that continue to entertain new generations.

The show promises to be a highlight of Scarborough Spa’s 2027 programme, with more high-profile events to be announced in the coming months.

Tickets for An Evening with Sir David Jason go on sale at 10am on Thursday, October 22, 2026.

They will be available through the Scarborough Spa website and box office.

Sir David’s appearance is expected to draw audiences from across Yorkshire and beyond, eager to spend an evening with one of Britain’s most beloved entertainers.
