Colourful clogs and playful Jibbitz have arrived at Dalton Park.

The shopping centre is now home to the North East’s first Crocs outlet.

The new Seaham store opened on October 9, and offers a range of clogs, sandals, slippers and accessories for adults and children.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584343.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Crocs)*

Richard Kaye, centre manager at Dalton Park Outlet and Outdoors, said: "As the North East’s first Crocs outlet, the new store gives visitors even more choice when shopping for everyday favourites, seasonal styles and gifts ahead of Christmas – all at great outlet prices."

The shop stocks classic clogs, fleece-lined versions, sandals, slippers and a wide assortment of Jibbitz charms for personalisation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584310.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Crocs)*

To celebrate the launch, shoppers received 20 per cent off throughout the weekend, and those spending £60 or more were given a free pack of five Jibbitz charms.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21584344.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Crocs)*

Videos posted online showed shelves lined with colourful Crocs, as well as Crocs handbags and backpacks and Crocs wellies.
