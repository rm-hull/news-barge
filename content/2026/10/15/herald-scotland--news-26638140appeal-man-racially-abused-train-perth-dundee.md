British Transport Police is appealing for witnesses to come forward after a man was racially abused on a train between Perth and Dundee.

Just before 7pm on Sunday, September 27, a man was on the 5.45pm train from Glasgow Queen Street to Aberdeen train when another man, who had boarded the train at Perth, racially abused him.

Officers are appealing for anyone who may have overheard or witnessed the incident to come forward to support the investigation.

**READ MORE:**

Anyone with information is asked to contact British Transport Police by texting 61016 or by calling 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 2600129303.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
