The heartbroken family of a County Durham biker killed in a crash in South Wales have said he had a 'heart of gold'.

Crook man Wayne Bowron, known as Bow, died following a crash on the A4067 about eight miles north of Swansea at 10.40pm on October 9.

The 52-year-old's family have paid tribute to the loving father, grandfather, uncle, partner and friend.

In a heartbreaking statement, they said: “Wayne (Bow) was a loving father, grandfather, uncle, partner and friend.

"He was only 52 having been taken from us so suddenly.

"Nothing can express the loss that we are currently feeling as a family.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21587321.jpg?type=mds-article-620)52-year-old Wayne Bowron from Crook *(Image: SOUTH WALES POLICE)*

“Wayne was well known throughout Durham and would do anything for anyone.

"Wayne treated everyone like they were part of the family and had a heart of gold

" Wayne loved his motorcycle and the friends he made throughout the motorcycle community. As a family our hearts are broken and there is a hole in all of our hearts that will never be filled.”

An investigation by South Wales Police into the carash is ongoing.
