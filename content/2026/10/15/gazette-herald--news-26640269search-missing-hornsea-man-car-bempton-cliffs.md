**Update: He has been found.**

A CAR belonging to a missing man has been found in a car park at Bempton Cliffs as police continue to search for him.

Humberside Police has launched a search for a missing 60-year-old man from Hornsea whose car has been found close to a East Yorkshire beauty spot.

Christopher – who is described as around 6ft 2 inches tall with grey hair – was last 10.45am this morning (October 15) when he was believed to be wearing a blue jacket.

A spokesperson for the force said: “It was reported he was last seen in the area of The Leys in Hornsea however his car has been located in a car park at Bempton Cliffs.”

If you have seen Christopher, or know where he may be, please contact the police via 101 quoting log 160 of 15 October
