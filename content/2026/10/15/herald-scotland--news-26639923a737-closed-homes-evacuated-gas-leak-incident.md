The A737 has been closed to traffic in both directions due to a gas leak incident.

Police said they received a report of a potential gas leak around 10.45am on Thursday, October 15.

The A737 is currently closed between Beith and Lochwinnoch.

Police and partners are in attendance at the scene.

A number of properties have also been evacuated as a precaution.

Diversions are in place and members of the public are advised to avoid the area.
