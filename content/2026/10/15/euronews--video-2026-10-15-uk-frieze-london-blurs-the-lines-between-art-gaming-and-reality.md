At the fair, **Benjamin Lallier's** * Mother Needham*invites visitors to toss coins and make a wish, while**Yuli Serfaty's** * Who's A Good Girl*explores the relationship between**gender** and**technology**. Greek artist** Theo Triantafyllidis**presents* Feral Metaverse (Spider)*, a giant**climbable sculpture** that doubles as a**multiplayer game**. Up to six visitors can enter a shared** virtual world**, where they must work together to create shapes such as human towers and wheels.

But behind the playful installations, Frieze remains a major **commercial event**. Art critic** Tabish Khan**says a** struggling market**is prompting galleries to favour more** conventional displays**, with** paintings** taking priority over elaborate installations.

Other highlights include Swiss artist **Miriam Cahn's** self-portraits exploring**ageing**, presented by** Galerie Jocelyn Wolff**, and a selection of contemporary** Indian art**from the** Fitzwilliam Museum**. Together, the works reflect a fair balancing** artistic experimentation**with the pressures of a** challenging art market**.
