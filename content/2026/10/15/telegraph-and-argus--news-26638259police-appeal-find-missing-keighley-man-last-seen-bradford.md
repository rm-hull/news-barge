Police have launched an appeal to find a missing 59-year-old Keighley man last seen in Bradford.

Gareth Beck, 59, has been reported missing from Keighley.

West Yorkshire Police said he was last seen in Bradford at 9pm on Tuesday.

He is described as a white male with a slim build, standing 5ft 6in tall, with dark hair and a beard.

Officers are concerned for his welfare.

Anyone who has seen him or knows where he is should call 999, quoting log number 1679 13/10/26.
