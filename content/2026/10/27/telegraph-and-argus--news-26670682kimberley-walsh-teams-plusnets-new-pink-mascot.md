BRADFORD-BORN Kimberley Walsh has been pictured out in London with a giant pink plus sign.

The singer and presenter was spotted alongside Plusso, the new face of value broadband provider Plusnet, during a night out in London last week.

Described as a “giant, fluffy pink plus who loves spotting great deals,” Plusso was revealed by Ms Walsh in a new interview as the new face of value broadband provider Plusnet.

Ms Walsh said: "Who doesn’t love a bargain? "We all have to spend money on broadband, right? "But I prefer to have a little bit left over for other stuff, you know… like shoes. "What do you think of my friend Plusso? "Isn’t he so cute?"

The outing was designed to highlight Plusnet’s message that saving on essentials like broadband can free up money for more enjoyable spending.

Cilesta van Doorn, chief brand and marketing communications officer at Plusnet, said: "Plusso is the newest member of the Plusnet family and there was no one better than fellow Yorkshire favourite Kimberley to show him the bright lights of London.

"We might have a fresh look and a fluffy new face, but what matters hasn’t changed: reliable broadband at a great price."
