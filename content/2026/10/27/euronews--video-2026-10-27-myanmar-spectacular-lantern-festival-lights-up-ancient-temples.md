Families released **paper lanterns** over the centuries-old temples, while devotees travelled from across the country to pray, make offerings and take part in the**three-day celebrations**, held from 25 to 27 October.

For the first time, Bagan hosted an official **Sky Lantern Festival**, organised by the military authorities' tourism department. Around** 1,000 lanterns were released each evening**above the** UNESCO World Heritage site** to attract visitors.

Also known as the **Festival of Lights**, Thadingyut marks the** end of Buddhist Lent**, a three-month period of religious observance during the rainy season.

The festival commemorates the **Buddha's return from the celestial realm** after teaching his mother, traditionally celebrated by**lighting candles and lanterns**. It is also an occasion for** family reunions**and** honouring elders**.
