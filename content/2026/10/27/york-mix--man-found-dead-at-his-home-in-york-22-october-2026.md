A man has been found dead at his home in York.

Stephen Loft, 70, was found last Thursday (22 October).

Police say there are no suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

The coroner is asking for help to locate Mr Loft’s next of kin.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “If you have any information that would help the Coroner to locate any members of Mr Loft’s family then please email [email protected].”
