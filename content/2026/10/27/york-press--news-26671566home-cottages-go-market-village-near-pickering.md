## Home and cottages go to market in North Yorkshire village for £1.35million

The frontage of Mel House at Newton-on-Rawcliffe *(Image: Darren Greenwood)>*

A North Yorkshire Victorian home with multiple cottages has gone on the market for £1.35million.

Leeds-based Ernest Wilson is marketing Mel House, a seven-bedroom home with four cottages at Newton-on-Rawcliffe, five miles north of Pickering.

The home is accessed via a private driveway and has a living space of 4,635 sq ft.

The main residence features six bedrooms, although a games room, which includes a full-size snooker table and darts board, could be converted into a seventh bedroom.

A leisure suite, which includes a pool, hot tub, and sauna, is also included.

A mix of vintage and modern elements are seen throughout the house.

The property also has four holiday cottages, each with residential planning permission.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21633324.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The site of all the properties. *(Image: Ernest Wilson)*

A fifth one is currently being constructed.

It has also been granted outline planning permission for the development of log cabins to the rear of the property.

The four holiday cottages, which have recently been granted residential use, allow buyers to purchase the property on either a commercial or residential basis.

The cottages are made up of two with two bedrooms, and two with three bedrooms.

One of the three-bedroom cottages is fully accessible for disabled guests.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21633323.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of one of the well-appointed homes. *(Image: Ernest Wilson)*

The grounds, which cover 2.1 acres, feature stables, an outdoor games area, a private garden with a large pond, cleaning shed, laundry room, gym, and ample parking.

The solar panels and bio mass boiler generate around £15,000 per year.

The property has "the perfect balance" of historic character and modern convenience.

The recent planning permissions for the holiday cottages further enhance its appeal, providing an exceptional investment opportunity, the agents added.
