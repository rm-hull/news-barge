A new Home Bargains store with a bakery, café, and garden centre will open its doors this week on the spookiest day of the year.

The major retailer is continuing its rapid UK expansion with the new space, which will open in Gateshead on Saturday, October 31, at Sands Industrial Estate on Hexham Road, Swalwell.

A £3million investment has gone into the project, and it will create 75 brand new jobs in the region.

The new 21,313 square foot shop is part of a wider plan to grow the business to 800 nationwide locations, and will follow the new Team Valley store which opened in August.

Richard Duke, store manager of the new branch, said: "Another fantastic store opening in Gateshead.

"It's great to bring on board new colleagues to our business to join the Home Bargains family and to add another exciting shopping dimension to the local community."

The store will sell homewares, health and beauty products, snacks, plus fresh and frozen food.

It will also feature a Home Bargains bakery, café, and garden centre.

Mr Duke said: "I'm really looking forward to welcoming customers new and old.

"Exciting times ahead!"

The discount retailer has more than 650 stores across the UK and serves five million customers each week.

Home Bargains currently operates around 50 stores across the North East, with locations in Northumberland, Tyne and Wear, County Durham, and Teesside.

Most recently, its 40,000 square foot Team Valley site opened in a former Homebase this summer, creating 120 jobs in the area.

The family-run retailer plans to increase its UK workforce from 29,000 to 35,000 and open around 50 stores a year, in an effort to strengthen its presence and offer more convenient low-cost branded shopping options to residents.

The company says its expansion will create jobs, support local economies and offer customers value.

The new Gateshead site marks an important step in those plans, and an exciting new option for residents who love a bargain.
