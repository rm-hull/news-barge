## York care home residents brave storm to raise money for Parkinson's research

Residents from Mulberry Court visited Clifton Explore Library and Reading Cafe to raise money for Parkinson’s UK Research *(Image: Barchester)*

Care home residents recently braved Storm Boelo to support a good cause.

Residents from Mulberry Court visited Clifton Explore Library and Reading Cafe to raise money for Parkinson’s UK Research.

Despite challenging weather, they wrapped up warm and travelled in a convoy to the event, where they enjoyed tea and cakes, with support from Parkinson’s and resident families.

A spokesperson for Mulberry Court said: "We defied storm Boelo, wrapped up well, formed a convoy, and headed to Clifton Explore Library & Reading Cafe to raise money for Parkinson’s UK Research.

"Also, special thanks for the support from Parkinson’s and our lovely resident families, who turned out to support our worthy cause."

The care home, part of Barchester Healthcare, organises community outings to promote engagement and connection among residents, staff, and the wider community.
