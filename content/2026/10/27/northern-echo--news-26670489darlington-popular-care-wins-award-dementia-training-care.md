A care provider in Darlington and County Durham has won an award for improving the lives of people with dementia.

Popular Care, which runs Wilton House in Darlington and Peterlee Care Home in Peterlee, won Training2Care’s Outstanding Dementia Training Award for its staff training and dementia care.

The award recognises organisations committed to professional training and compassionate, personalised care.

Steve Massey, operations manager at Popular Care, said: "We are absolutely thrilled to receive this award, which is a wonderful recognition of the dedication and commitment shown by our colleagues at Wilton House and Peterlee Care Home.

"Providing excellent dementia care requires much more than simply understanding a diagnosis.

"It means getting to know the person, their background, their interests, and the things which make them feel comfortable, happy, and secure.

"Our teams understand how important those personal connections are, and they work incredibly hard to make sure every resident feels valued, respected, and supported.

"This award is a fantastic acknowledgement of their compassion and professionalism, and I am extremely proud of what they achieve."

Both homes care for older people, including those with dementia, helping residents keep their independence and quality of life.

Mr Massey said: "When families entrust us with the care of someone they love, they need to know that person will be treated with kindness, dignity, and understanding.
