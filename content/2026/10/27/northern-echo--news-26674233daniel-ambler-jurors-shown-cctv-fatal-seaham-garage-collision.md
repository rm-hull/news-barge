Jurors have watched harrowing footage of a man being crushed to death against a garage window by a car driven at him.

Daniel Ambler suffered horrific injuries when Ashton Anderson drove his Nissan X-Trail into him during a deadly feud.

Mr Ambler was pronounced dead after the impact at the Esso Asda Express Service Station in Seaham, County Durham, on January 15.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439805.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Daniel Ambler

Detectives pulled together CCTV footage from around the scene after they launched a murder investigation.

The footage showed Barry Anderson, his sons Ashton and Logan, Aja Henry and Tyler Leadbetter hunting down and attacking Mr Ambler and his friend Reece Wilkin.

Jurors at Teesside Crown Court saw Barry Anderson fighting Mr Wilkin before Ashton Anderson drove into a wall outside the Mallard pub.

The 20-year-old climbed out first, followed by Leadbetter and Henry, and joined the attack.

Moments later, the three younger defendants got back into the Nissan and chased Mr Ambler, who had been fighting Logan Anderson.

CCTV from the garage captured Ashton Anderson driving into Mr Ambler as he tried to flee, pinning him against shelving outside the shop front.

Ashton Anderson’s girlfriend, Alexis Prest, said everyone was shocked after the 23-year-old was pinned against the garage shop front and fatally injured.

The teenager told jurors that Anderson drove at Mr Ambler minutes after they had fought in the street.

Service station worker Andrew Nabbs told jurors he saw the Nissan drive at Mr Ambler as he crossed the forecourt.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Esso New Seaham Service Station, in Stockton Road, Seaham, the scene of Daniel Ambler's death

He said Mr Ambler was pinned between the car and shelving outside the garage.

Mr Nabbs described Mr Ambler as ‘bent like a banana’ and said he showed no sign of life.

Forensic scientist Elizabeth Harris told jurors that traces of Mr Ambler’s blood were found in the garage and in multiple drops on the path to the service station.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20437048.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Crime scene investigators at the scene of Daniel Ambler's death

The jury heard the families’ bitter feud began when Barry Anderson was stabbed last August.

Mr Ambler’s brother David and his friend Jed Wilson fought Barry Anderson, 52, who was stabbed during the struggle.

Barry Anderson, 52, of Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland; Ashton Anderson, 20, of Ryton Crescent, Seaham; and Barry Logan Anderson, 19, of Nursery View, Shotton Bank, face charges of murder and violent disorder.

The trial at Teesside Crown Court is expected to last up to five weeks.

Ashton Anderson has pleaded guilty to violent disorder. The jury is expected to decide whether the other four defendants are guilty of the same charge.

The prosecution alleges the attack was coordinated and that the four defendants other than Ashton Anderson intentionally assisted or encouraged him to use unlawful violence against Mr Ambler.
