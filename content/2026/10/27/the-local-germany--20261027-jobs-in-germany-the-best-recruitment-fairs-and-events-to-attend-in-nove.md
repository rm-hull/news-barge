Perhaps you're searching for a new challenge or want to see what kind of roles are available in Germany right now. If so, it may be worth visiting a jobs fair.

We've compiled a list of key events coming up and what you can expect.

**Karrieretag Dresden, November 6th, Dresden**

If you're based in the east of the country, the Karrieretag in Dresden is one event to get in your diary.

A host of employers will be attending, from leading engineering service provider Brunel to e-commerce and energy-saving company Cool Blue.

"Whether you are a skilled professional, a manager, a graduate, a student, a young professional or someone looking to switch fields – this is your opportunity to speak directly with hiring managers," the event organisers say.

Entry is free but you should register in advance. It takes place from 10am-5pm at the Internationales Congress Center.

**Professional2B Messe Köln, November 6th, Cologne**

Students, graduates and other young people can meet potential employers at the Professional2B in Cologne.

The event, happening from 10am to 4pm, focuses on early-career opportunities, including entry-level jobs, internships and trainee programmes. Guests can also network with employers and get advice on starting their careers.

It is free to attend.

**MATCHUP, November 6th, Munich**

Students are being invited to attend an internship hiring event at the Lio office in Munich on Friday, November 6th.

The innovative format sees startups pitch to students, instead of the other way around. Students and recent graduates will also have the opportunity to connect with founders and explore roles.

The event has been organised by the student initiative thinc.de in partnership with venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and five.degrees and Lio.

A previous event held in January in Berlin saw around 250 attendees.

A large recruitment drive is taking place in the north of the country in early November.

Dozens of employers will be in attendance at the Mensa II on Kiel University's campus, from Rheinmetall, Lidl and Aldi to the Bundespolizei.

The event is open from 10am-4pm on Saturday and 11am-5pm on Sunday. It's free but make sure to register.

**Jobmesse Bielefeld, November 7th-8th, Bielefeld**

On the same weekend, recruiters will be out in force at the Stadthalle in Bielefeld, North Rhine-Westphalia.

All day Saturday and Sunday, you'll be able to meet with lots of different employers and potentially seek out your next role.

**Karrieretag Köln, November 10th, Cologne**

Those in the Cologne area who are curious about emerging opportunities should visit the upcoming 'career day'.

Employers represented at the event include the ADAC, the Bundesamt für Verfassungsschutz and Campushunter Media.

The event at the Rheinenergiestadion is free and is scheduled to run from 10am to 5pm. Remember to register in advance.

**Karrieretag Stuttgart, November 11th, Stuttgart**

Those looking for work in south-west Germany can head to the Karrieretag in Stuttgart, which brings together jobseekers and employers from a range of industries.

The event is open to skilled professionals, managers, graduates, students and people considering a career change. Visitors can find out about vacancies, speak to hiring managers and get advice on applications.

It takes place at the Liederhalle from 10am to 5pm. Entry is free, but advance registration is required.

**Karrieretag Frankfurt, November 12th, Frankfurt**

Residents in Frankfurt and the surrounding areas should consider attending this careers fair.

Taking place at the myticket Jahrhunderthalle from 10am to 5pm, the event is open to everyone from experienced professionals to graduates, students and people looking to change careers.

Visitors can speak directly with recruiters, get their CV checked by professionals and attend talks and coaching sessions on job applications and career development.

Entry is free, but you'll need to register for a ticket in advance.

This careers fair is aimed at young people who are considering apprenticeships, higher education or are not quite sure what to do next.

Whether its vocational training, university courses or a gap year, visitors can chat to experts and employers to gain advice.

It is free to attend – but you have to register for a ticket.

**Jobmesse Bochum, November 17th, Bochum**

Everyone – from experienced workers to those seeking a career change – is invited to this jobs fair.

Located in the Ruhrstadion, the event runs throughout the day, bringing together employers and offering opportunities across different industries and qualification levels.

The fair also covers vocational training and education.

Entry is free. You can register for a ticket through the organiser's website.

**Karrieretag München, November 18th, Munich**

Jobseekers in Bavaria will have the chance to meet potential employers at the Karrieretag in Munich.

The event is aimed at a range of people, including skilled professionals, managers, graduates, students and those looking to switch careers. Alongside opportunities to speak directly with recruiters, visitors can get application advice and attend career-related talks.

Expect employers such as the DHL Group and Securitas to have stalls, among others.

It takes place at the Zenith from 10am to 5pm. Entry is free, but visitors should register in advance.

**JOBMEDI Berlin, November 20th-21st, Berlin**

People interested in working in healthcare, nursing or social services can meet possible future employers at this specialist careers fair.

The event, taking place over two days, offers opportunities to explore vacancies, training and further education. It's aimed at everyone, from qualified professionals to those who want to enter the sector for the first time.

The fair is at Messe Berlin and is free to attend.

**Professional2B Stuttgart, November 20th, Stuttgart**

Younger people are invited to this jobs fair in Stuttgart, which connects early-career jobseekers with employers and offers advice.

Employers and institutions at the event include ESB Business School, Hamburg Media School and Edinburgh Napier University.

The fair, at the Kultur- und Kongresszentrum Liederhalle, is free to attend and there's also free popcorn on offer.

**Other useful events:myGwork WorkFair 2026, November 4th, online**

This global virtual career fair is aimed at LGBTQ+ students and recent graduates, and those interested in inclusive employers.

Taking place from 11am to 7pm, it may offer some useful tips, inspiration or networking opportunities.
