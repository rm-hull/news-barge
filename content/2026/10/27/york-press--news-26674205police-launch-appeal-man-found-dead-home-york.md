A MAN has been found dead at his home in York.

North Yorkshire Police have launched an appeal to assist the Coroner’s Office to help locate the next-of-kin of Stephen Loft.

Stephen, 70, was sadly found dead at his home on Thursday (October 22).




A force spokesperson said: “There are no suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

“If you have any information that would help the Coroner to locate any members of Mr Loft's family then please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”
