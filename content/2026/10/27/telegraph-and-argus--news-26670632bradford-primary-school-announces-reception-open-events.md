A PRIMARY school is inviting families to explore its classrooms and meet pupils and staff.

Rainbow Primary Leadership Academy, located on Nelson Street in Bradford, is holding Reception open events on Tuesday, November 10 from 4pm to 5pm and Wednesday, November 11 from 9am to 10am.

Visitors will also be able to tour the school’s modern facilities and speak with pupils and staff.

The school offers a broad curriculum and enrichment opportunities in areas such as the arts, cooking, music and sport.

Rainbow Primary Leadership Academy is part of Star Academies, a UK-wide multi-academy trust that supports schools in areas of social and economic disadvantage.

It joined the trust in July 2021.

For more information, visit www.rainbowprimary.com.
