Good morning. The Trump administration’s assault on the legitimacy of the midterm elections has continued, with the US president announcing a $1m reward for reports of voter fraud, amid several legal actions by the Department of Justice to limit voting.

Donald Trump said the bounty payment was contingent on the arrest and conviction of those responsible. He did not disclose how he would pay for the rewards. Trump’s comments follow his repeated and unfounded claims of widespread voter fraud.

* **What action is the administration trying to take against Washington state?** The administration has asked Washington state to set aside any ballots received from more than 400 people it alleges are registered to vote without US citizenship. The request marks the third time this month that the justice department has brought a direct state challenge even as voting is under way.
* **What is the latest in the battle over California voters?** A US federal appeals court has rejected the Trump administration’s move to block California from counting the ballots of 20,000 registered voters that the justice department claims are non-citizens.
* **Why is Vivek Ramaswamy facing criticism over an advert?** The Republican nominee to be the governor of Ohio ran an ad featuring his opponent’s mother, who said she would not vote for her daughter. As Moira Donegan explains in this excoriating opinion piece, the estrangement between Amy Acton is decades long and stems from the sexual abuse Acton survived as a child.

## **Anthropic files for potentially record-breaking stock market valuation in crucial test for AI industry**

![Anthropic logo.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/bf46a2e3da5948089f4ee10970d19c956789c182/411_0_3333_2668/master/3333.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Anthropic has publicly filed for a stock market listing in what is expected to be one of the largest debuts in history. The company’s float on the US market will serve as a test of the financial viability of the multitrillion-dollar AI boom.

Analysts and investors have valued the San Francisco-based company, founded in 2021, at more than $1.5tn.

* **What are the key takeaways from the investor prospectus?** The document reveals the AI research lab’s financials and the risks – more than 90 pages of them – that might negatively impact its business. Johana Bhuiyan has identified these four key takeaways.
* **What has Anthropic’s model recently been accused of?** Anthropic’s Claude model accessed voter data in the US state of Georgia, the AI company has confirmed, in the latest example of unauthorised behavior by the technology. The AI model managed to bypass a paywall to reach data that is publicly available but is typically accessible only after paying a fee.

## **Lula vows no return to dictatorship in Brazil after election win**

![Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/61f49e5040906378f0ae4214a76205303bb7569f/1322_0_7200_5760/master/7200.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, the Brazilian president, has vowed that dictatorship and fascism will never return to Brazil after winning a historic fourth term in power by defeating Flávio Bolsonaro, his far-right opponent. Bolsonaro, like his father four years ago, has still not conceded defeat to Lula, though his party issued a statement acknowledging the election result.

* **What has happened to defeated Flávio Bolsonaro?** The far-right senator was banned from leaving Brazil and ordered to surrender his passports hours after his dramatic defeat.
