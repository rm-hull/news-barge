Train services between Edinburgh and Dundee are facing disruption after reports of high winds on the Tay Bridge.

ScotRail has warned that services operating between the two cities may be delayed or revised while the situation is monitored.

The Met Office has advised that a yellow warning for wind will be in place across most of Scotland from 3am on Tuesday until 9pm.

The new weather warning follows Storm Boelo, which battered Scotland with 80mph gusts and lead to one fatality after a tree fell on a man in Dumfries and Galloway.

ScotRail said: "Due to a report of high winds on the Tay Bridge, train services between Edinburgh and Dundee will be delayed or revised."

No estimate has yet been given for when normal services will resume.

Travellers are being urged to keep up to date with the latest information through ScotRail's website and social media channels.
