Lidl will open its newest store in York next week.

***YorkMix*** reported last year that the budget retailer was set to open at Clifton Moor Retail Park, in what was Food Warehouse on Hurricane Way.

Now an opening date has finally been revealed. The grand opening will be **Thursday 5 November** from 8am, following a ribbon cutting ceremony with the Mayor of York.

The store has created up to 40 new jobs, with recruitment focused heavily on hiring people who live locally.

Liam Schofield, regional head of property for Lidl GB, commented: “We are thrilled to officially open our fourth Lidl store in York.

“This fantastic new store is a direct response to the local demand for high-quality, affordable groceries, and we couldn’t be prouder to welcome the community inside.

“A huge thank you to everyone who helped us turn these plans into a reality – we can’t wait to start making a real difference to your weekly shop.”

The site at Clifton Moor is the fourth Lidl store in the city, with one on James Street, another on Thanet Road, Acomb, and another at Monk’s Cross.

Since 2017, Lidl has donated almost 380,000 meals to local people in need through its Feed it Back scheme, in partnership with Neighbourly, to local good causes across York.

With the opening of this latest store, Lidl will be able to support even more local charities including Help Brings Hope, Luke’s Larder, The Snack Shack and Your Cafe.

To celebrate the opening next Thursday, early birds will be treated to complimentary food sampling and exclusive deals on the Lidl Plus app.

The new store at Clifton Moor will be open 8am – 10pm Monday to Saturday, and 10am – 4pm on Sunday.
