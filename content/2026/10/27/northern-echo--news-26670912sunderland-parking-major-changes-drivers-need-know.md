Shoppers and visitors are being offered an extra hour of free parking.

Sunderland City Council has updated its parking charges across more than 500 surface spaces as part of a wider refresh aimed at supporting city centre businesses and encouraging people to spend more time in the area.

The scheme gives drivers a free additional hour of parking with every paid session, with a minimum purchase of one hour required to qualify for the offer.

Councillor Bill Blackett, cabinet member for environment, transport, and enforcement, said: "Our city centre already has some of the lowest parking charges around and this is about getting the balance right.

"We continually review our parking charges and we want parking to work for residents and for businesses.

"Making it attractive and convenient for people to park in the city centre to shop, meet friends, have something to eat, all helps support businesses and the jobs they provide."

The changes took effect on Monday, October 26, and apply to all council-managed surface car parks and on-street parking in the city centre.

Ticket machines and parking apps have been updated to reflect the new arrangements.

Covered sites include the Station car park on Holmeside (194 spaces), Tatham Street (94), Gorse Road (54), and Nile Street (49).

Councillor Blackett said: "We'll run the scheme for a year and keep it under review so we can see how well it works for residents, visitors and businesses.

"Visitor numbers are up and rising across the city, we want to keep it like that, and this parking offer can help as a further incentive for residents and visitors."

The council’s 'Free After 3' scheme remains in place at several locations including Gorse Road, Charles Street, West Wear Street, Tatham Street, Nile Street, and Station car parks.

This offer provides free parking after 3pm from Monday to Friday.

While hourly tariffs have increased by 20p, the free extra hour means the cost of a two-hour stay is now lower at many locations.

For example, two hours of parking on Burdon Road has dropped from £3.20 to £1, and at Gorse Road the two-hour rate is now £1.10.

Blue Badge holders continue to park for free and without a time limit in council city-centre off-street car parks.

There are no increases to parking permit charges for residents or businesses in the city centre.

Multi-storey car parks at St Mary’s (480 spaces), Sunniside (653), and Riverside (657) are not affected by the changes.

The scheme has been welcomed by Sunderland Business Investment District (BID), which represents around 850 businesses in the city centre.

Sharon Appleby, chief executive of Sunderland BID, said: "Every time people choose to spend time in our city centre, they're making a real difference."

"Whether it's to shop, grab a coffee, enjoy a meal or attend an event, the support helps local businesses grow, it supports jobs, and it keeps our city centre busy, vibrant and thriving.

"These choices all add up and have a genuine impact on the people and businesses that call Sunderland home."

The new parking arrangements will be in place for one year.
