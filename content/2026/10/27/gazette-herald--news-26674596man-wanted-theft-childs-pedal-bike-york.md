NORTH Yorkshire Police have launched an appeal after a man allegedly stole a child’s bike in York.

The incident happened at 9.15am on Wednesday (October 14) in Huntington Road.




A force spokesperson said: “A male used brute force to remove a child’s pedal bike from a cycle rack.

“Please email: [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) if you recognise the person pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.

“Alternatively, you can call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“Please quote reference 12260202763 when passing on information.”
