A North Yorkshire landmark will host a two-day Christmas market next month, featuring 40 independent stalls.

Fountains Abbey, near Ripon, will host the Little Bird Christmas Artisan Market on Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29.

The market will bring 40 independent makers, artists and food producers to the Unesco World Heritage Site, including returning traders and newcomers.

Visitors can browse unique, handcrafted products in a spectacular historic setting.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21635461.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fountains Abbey, near Ripon *(Image: SUBMIT)*

Stalls will feature handcrafted jewellery, ceramics, homeware, textiles, artwork, fashion accessories, food and drink, gifts and Christmas decorations.

The market offers Christmas gift ideas and a festive day out while supporting independent makers and small businesses.

Jackie Crozier, Managing Director of Little Bird Made, said: “We’re absolutely delighted to be returning to Fountains Abbey for a second year.

"The response to our inaugural Christmas market exceeded all expectations, and we’re excited to welcome visitors back for another unforgettable weekend.

“Every trader has been carefully selected for the quality and originality of their work, creating a luxury shopping experience where every purchase has a story behind it.

"There’s something incredibly special about discovering beautifully handcrafted gifts in such a magnificent historic setting, and we’re proud to bring together some of the finest independent makers from across the region.”

Jason Scully, General Manager of Fountains Abbey, said: “We’re delighted to be welcoming Little Bird Made back to Fountains Abbey for a second year. Their artisan Christmas market was a fantastic addition to our festive offer last year, and we’re excited to see the team return this Christmas.

“The market provides another wonderful opportunity for people to experience Fountains Abbey during the festive season while supporting independent makers and small businesses.

"We know our visitors will enjoy browsing the wide range of market stalls, and we hope they’ll discover some unique gifts and seasonal treats to take home too.”

The market is free, but visitors must pay to enter the abbey site. National Trust and English Heritage members can enter the market for free, while abbey tickets are available through the National Trust website.
