Two men were seriously injured and an arrest was made after a car smashed into a wall following a police chase.

The crash on Huddersfield Road, Low Moor, happened at just before 9pm on Monday night.

Police reported a Volkswagen Passat failed to stop and collided with a wall after a brief pursuit.

Three people were injured, two seriously, and all were taken to hospital.

The driver, a 26-year-old man, was released from hospital and arrested on suspicion of theft of a motor vehicle, failing to stop and dangerous driving.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 8.39pm yesterday (October 26) officers sighted a Volkswagen Passat which failed to stop for police on Huddersfield Road, Bradford.

"After a brief pursuit, the vehicle was in collision with a wall.

"Three people in the vehicle were taken to hospital.

"Two men suffered serious but not life-threatening injuries.

"The driver a 26-year-old man was discharged from hospital and arrested on suspicion of theft of a motor vehicle, failing to stop and dangerous driving.

"He remains in custody this morning.

"Enquiries are ongoing by the Roads Policing Unit."
