A car ended up on its roof in a crash on the A1079 near Pocklington.

It happened at the junction with The Balk at around 7pm on Thursday (22 October).

A green BYD Dolphin and a silver Renault Clio collided at the junction, leading to the Clio coming to a rest on its roof.

#### Crash location

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “Emergency services attended the scene, and the driver of the Clio was taken to hospital for treatment to serious injuries that are not thought to be life threatening.”

Officers are appealing to anyone who was in the area around the time and witnessed the collision, or has any dashcam or CCTV footage, to contact them on their non-emergency number 101 quoting log 397 of 22 October.
