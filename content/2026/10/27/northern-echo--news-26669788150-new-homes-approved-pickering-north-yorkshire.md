Councillors have approved more than 100 new homes for a North Yorkshire town.

Up to 150 homes will be built on land off Firthland Road in Pickering after approval from North Yorkshire Council’s Thirsk and Malton planning committee.

Persimmon’s scheme includes one- to four-bedroom homes and bungalows, with 35 per cent classed as affordable housing.

Graham Whiteford, planning director at Persimmon Yorkshire, said: "We’re pleased that councillors have approved this application for high-quality new homes for local people.

"This will deliver a mix of one- to four-bedroom properties as well as significant funding for local public services and infrastructure.

"We look forward to continuing to work with the Council and the community to bring these plans to fruition."

The homes will meet future home standards and include electric-vehicle charging points, air-source heat pumps and solar panels.

The development will include green spaces, walking routes and a children’s play area.

Persimmon will pay more than £1 million through the Community Infrastructure Levy.
