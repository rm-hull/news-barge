There are delays on rail services between Selby and Leeds this morning (Tuesday).

Due to a points failure, trains have to run at reduced speed on some lines.

Services running through these stations may be delayed by up to ten minutes. Disruption is expected until 9am.

Transpennine Express said: “You should continue to travel as planned, however, your journey may be delayed by ten minutes.

“We are sorry for the delay to your journey today.”

There are also delays on trains going to Newcastle.

Northern trains posted at 7.23am: “There is disruption due to trespassers on the railway at Newcastle.

“Train services running through this station will be cancelled, delayed or revised.”
