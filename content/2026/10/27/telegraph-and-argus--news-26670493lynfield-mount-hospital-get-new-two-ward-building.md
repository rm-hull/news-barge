A major redevelopment is under way at a Bradford hospital.

The work focuses on making improvements to mental health services at Lynfield Mount Hospital.

New modular units will form the heart of a two-ward building under construction, part of a wider scheme led by Bradford District Care NHS Foundation Trust to deliver "recovery-focused" care in a modern setting.

Alex Trigg, deputy director of estates at the trust, said: "This is a really exciting milestone for the project as we’ll quickly see the new ward block take shape on site.

"Due to the nature of modular construction, a lot of the work takes place offsite, helping to minimise disruption to both our services and neighbouring residents by reducing the amount of construction activity required at the hospital."

Modular units will be delivered to the hospital between November 2026 and January 2027, with the majority of deliveries scheduled for weekdays from Monday, November 9, to Friday, November 20, and again from Friday, January 8, to Saturday, January 23.

The only planned weekend delivery is on Saturday, January 23.

Most of the construction will take place offsite, with units arriving around 70 per cent complete and then assembled on hospital grounds.

This approach aims to reduce disruption for patients, staff, and nearby residents, though some temporary traffic measures will be needed while the deliveries are taking place.

Mr Trigg said: "While we appreciate there may be a short period of disruption during these deliveries, this is an important step towards creating a modern facility that will improve the experience of people who use our services.

"We would like to give a huge thanks to everyone who’s working with us during this period and apologise for any inconvenience caused by the temporary changes to traffic arrangements.

"We will continue to work closely with our contractor, McAvoy, to ensure these deliveries are carried out safely and efficiently."

During the delivery periods, temporary traffic lights and parking restrictions may be in place on Heights Lane and Daisy Hill Lane.

Increased traffic and some delays may occur on Heights Lane and Daisy Hill Lane whilst the deliveries are taking place.

Commuters are encouraged to allow extra time for their journey or consider alternative routes where possible.

Latest updates and any changes to the delivery schedule will be posted on the trust’s website HERE.
