We are just three weeks out from the release of Grand Theft Auto 6, which you can tell because it has cleared the release calendar around it like the Chicxulub crater. Ahead of the big day, our friends at Edge magazine visited Rockstar's Edinburgh development HQ to chat with the studio's leaders and put their eyes on GTA 6 *in action*. That's right folks, it's officially happening. This thing is officially almost a videogame you can play.

The result? The 16-page cover story coming in Edge #430, **on sale October 29**. They've really been cooking over there, bringing us a bunch of exclusive new imagery, interviews with Rockstar head honchos, and a special gatefold cover with artwork of Jason and Lucia, about whom I already have very strong feelings.
