Police are appealing for information on the whereabouts of a man wanted in connection with a burglary.

Marc Stephenson, 24, is being sought by officers investigating a burglary in Blackhall.

A spokesman for Durham Constabulary said: "Officers want to speak to him in connection with a burglary in Blackhall.

"The 24-year-old is from Hartlepool but also has links to Wingate.

"If you have any information that could help locate him, call 101."

Anyone with information is urged to contact the force as soon as possible.
