A 'police incident' has blocked the M1 near Lofthouse.

The incident has occurred on the M1 Southbound exit slip road to the M62 westbound at junction 42 near Lofthouse. Traffic has been affected and is currently slow with queues pictured as police block traffic to the exit slip road.

Data traffic site Inrix said: "M1 Southbound exit slip road to the M62 westbound closed, slow traffic due to police incident at J42 M62 J29 (Lofthouse)."

West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been asked for a comment. This is a breaking news story. Scroll below for further details on this. Updates will appear below as they come in.
