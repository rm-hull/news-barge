Students at a County Durham school have taken a step towards their future careers after meeting employers, universities and industry professionals at a dedicated careers fair.

The event, held at King James I Academy, in Bishop Auckland, welcomed representatives from sectors including construction, engineering, healthcare, beauty, fitness, catering, banking, and public services.

Students in Years 7-13 explored a range of opportunities from apprenticeships to university courses.

A spokesman for King James I Academy said: "Careers advice and guidance is an important part of the curriculum at King James, and the Academy employs a dedicated Careers Adviser to make sure that students are able to access regular guidance to ensure that they are on track to achieve their future career goals."

The careers fair also featured information about pathways into the RAF, Fire Service, and Police, as well as opportunities in healthcare and other professional fields.

Students had the chance to speak directly with representatives from both local employers and universities from across the country, giving them valuable insights into a wide range of career possibilities.
