A distillery is celebrating success with two final award nominations.

Spirit of Yorkshire in Hunmanby has reached the finals of the Yorkshire Post Tourism Awards and the Deliciously Yorkshire Taste Awards.

Its Pot Still Coffee Shop is a finalist for Café of the Year, while its Cream Liqueur is shortlisted in the Best Wine and Spirit category.

Jenni Ashwood, marketing director at Spirit of Yorkshire, said: "We're delighted to have been shortlisted for not one, but two awards.

"Both are prestigious awards programmes which recognise and reward the best of Yorkshire's tourism attractions and food and drink producers respectively.

"We're honoured to be included in both shortlists and look forward to the finals next month."

Winners of the Taste Awards will be announced on November 3 at the Pavilions of Harrogate.

Winners of the Yorkshire Post Tourism Awards will be revealed at a gala dinner in Leeds on November 30.
