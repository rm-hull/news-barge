A Spennymoor woman who spent years caring for others in the healthcare sector has launched her own wellness business after deciding it was time to do something for herself.

Jessica Bage is the founder of FORME Body & Wellness, a home-based, semi-mobile massage studio in Spennymoor that aims to provide clients with a relaxing space to focus on their wellbeing.

The business was inspired by Jessica's own experience of balancing a demanding career with the need for self-care.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21622705.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jessica Bage has launched her own business. *(Image: JESSICA BAGE)*

The 32-year-old said: "The inspiration behind FORME came from realising that I needed to do something for me.

"I love learning and challenging myself, and I wanted to explore something new while continuing to work on my own wellbeing.

"I've spent much of my working life caring for other people, and sometimes, when you're so focused on everyone else, you can forget to look after yourself."

Jessica spent six years working as a healthcare assistant in an emergency department while studying for her degree before qualifying as a paramedic.

She now works in brain injury support.

Drawing on her healthcare background and interest in anatomy and physiology, she decided to create a business that combines her knowledge of the body with her passion for wellbeing.

FORME currently offers tailored full-body massage treatments, and Jessica is already working towards expanding her services.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21622703.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: JESSICA BAGE)*

She is studying manual lymphatic drainage and hopes to add the specialist treatment to her menu once qualified.

Jessica said: "I wanted to create something that would allow me to bring together my healthcare background, my passion for learning and my interest in wellbeing.

That's how FORME began."

Launching the business has brought its own challenges, from obtaining qualifications and insurance to creating a website, setting up social media channels and preparing client paperwork.

"There is so much more to setting up a business than people might realise," she said.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21622704.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: JESSICA BAGE)*

"It has been a huge learning curve, but one I'm proud of."

Despite only beginning the process of launching the business at the start of October, Jessica has already started taking bookings and says the support from the community has been overwhelming.

"The support and encouragement from friends, family and members of the public have been genuinely lovely," she said.

"Every message, enquiry and booking has given me another reason to feel excited about what I'm building."

Jessica hopes her business will offer more than just massage treatments.

"For me, FORME is about more than massage. It's a reminder that looking after yourself matters, too."
