# Two boys, aged 12 and 15, missing for three days as police launch urgent appeal

## Police are appealing for help to find two missing boys, aged 12 and 15, who were last seen in West Yorkshire amid concerns for their welfare.

West Yorkshire Police are searching for Yousaf, 15, and Mohammed, 12, who were both last seen in the Liversedge area of Kirklees on Saturday (Oct 24).

Officers have launched enquiries to locate the boys and believe they may have links to the Rochdale area.

Yousaf, who is from Kirklees, is described as being 5ft 11ins tall, of medium build, with short black hair and black facial hair.

![Yousaf, 15, (left) and Mohammed, 12](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/27/12/50/MixCollage-27-Oct-2026-12-54-PM-4767.jpeg?trim=70,0,71,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Mohammed, also from Kirklees, is described as being 4ft 9ins tall, of slim build, with short black hair and glasses.

Police have expressed concerns for the boys' welfare and are urging anyone who may have seen them or knows where they are to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “If you can assist in locating them, then please contact police in Kirklees via our Live Chat facility or by calling 101.

“The log reference is 1454 and 1492 of 24/10.”
