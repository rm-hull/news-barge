An Easingwold-based care team has been named Care Delivery Team of the Year across the Guardian Angel Carers franchise.

Guardian Angel Carers, which covers York and surrounding areas, was recognised for its outstanding care, teamwork, and dedication at a ceremony held on Hayling Island on October 15.

Debbie Burchall, director of Guardian Angel Carers York, said: "We are absolutely delighted to have been named Care Team of the Year across the entire Guardian Angel Carers franchise.

"This is an incredible achievement and something every member of our team should be immensely proud of.

"We have an amazing group of people who genuinely care about the clients they support."

The Easingwold team was praised for its personalised approach and for consistently going above and beyond to support clients and their families.

The award reflects the team's growing reputation for delivering high-quality care in the community and supporting people to remain in their own homes.
