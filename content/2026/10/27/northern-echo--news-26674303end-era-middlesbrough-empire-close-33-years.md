One of Middlesbrough's best-known nightlife venues is set to close its doors after 33 years.

The Middlesbrough Empire will close in its current form on November 17, following a change in ownership, bringing what its team has described as a "very special era" to an end.

The new owners plan to take the building in a different direction, with the existing owners, management team and staff confirming they will no longer be involved in The Empire's future.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/11565449.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Middlesbrough Empire is closing after 33 years *(Image: Paul Robinson)*

For more than three decades, the Empire has drawn thousands to Middlesbrough for club nights, live music and other events.

Announcing the closure, The Middlesbrough Empire said: "After 33 years, we’re incredibly sad to announce that The Middlesbrough Empire will close its doors on 17th November, following a change in ownership.

"With the new ownership comes a new chapter for the building, and the current owners, management team and all existing staff will no longer be part of its future. For those of us who have been part of The Empire, this marks the end of a very special era.

"For 33 years, The Empire has been more than just a venue. It has been a place where friendships have been made, memories created, celebrations shared and countless nights out enjoyed.

"We’ve welcomed thousands of people through our doors, worked alongside some incredible people and been fortunate enough to play a part in the nightlife and entertainment scene of Middlesbrough."

The building first opened in 1899 and has hosted entertainers including Charlie Chaplin, Harry Houdini and Laurel and Hardy before reopening as a multi-floor live music venue and nightclub in 1991.

A string of major names have appeared at the venue, including Kasabian, Courteeners, Blossoms, Jessie Ware, Mel C, Maximo Park, Nothing But Thieves, Myles Smith, Cascada and Paul Oakenfold.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/12723584.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Courteeners are one of several major acts that have appeared over the years. *(Image: Agency)*

In a series of farewell posts on social media, the team highlighted its relationship with Cleveland Police and Middlesbrough Council and thanked staff past and present, promoters, events teams, booking partners and Hinson Security for their support.

The Empire will hold its final weekend of celebrations on November 13 and 14, featuring artists Sugarshack and Mode.

The venue hopes to fill the building with “familiar faces, old friends and people who have helped make The Empire what it is today”, with tickets already running low.

Events scheduled at the Empire after November 17 will instead be moved to alternative venues, with ticket holders advised to check their emails for further information.

Thanking everyone who has supported The Empire over the years, the venue ended its statement: "Thank you for the years of support, the unforgettable nights, the laughter, the dancing and the memories that will stay with us long after the lights go out.

"Let’s make these final nights count. Join us for one last dance. One last weekend. One last chance to go out on a high.

"Thank you for 33 incredible years."
