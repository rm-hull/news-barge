A Scottish childcare technology business that has already signed up more than 700 nurseries is raising fresh capital as it matures from a nursery-search platform into a broader digital service for parents.

Nuuri has secured £550,000 in its second investment round, with the money earmarked for expanding its existing software and developing a consumer app that is expected to launch next year.

The round, which closed on October 1, was backed by SyndicateRoom's Access Fund and Shane Corstorphine, the former chief financial officer of travel technology company Skyscanner.

**Read more:**

Founded by Steven Clarke in 2024, Nuuri aims to simplify the process of finding and enrolling children in nurseries while giving childcare providers software to manage enquiries and fill available places. The business says it has onboarded more than 700 nurseries since entering the UK market in May last year, while its website carries information on more than 19,000 providers.

Nuuri has also reported average month-on-month revenue growth of 34% over the past 12 months, although it has not disclosed its revenues.

Its workforce has increased from three to seven, with the latest funding intended to support further development as Nuuri seeks to expand beyond its original nursery search and enrolment offering. Central to that plan is a consumer app due to launch in the second quarter of 2027.

Nuuri says the app will bring nursery discovery and enrolment together for parents, while also incorporating a financial element intended to offer rewards and incentives to families managing childcare costs. The company has yet to disclose details of how that part of the service will operate.

At the same time, Nuuri plans to continue expanding the software it provides to nursery operators, with the aim of helping them manage enquiries, enrolments and occupancy.

The strategy would effectively place Nuuri on both sides of the childcare search process: providing software to nurseries while building a consumer-facing service through which parents can find and apply for places.

“Closing our second investment round is a major milestone for Nuuri and validation of our rapid trajectory," Mr Clarke said. “With over 700 nurseries on board and robust month-on-month revenue growth, we are proving that both families and providers are hungry for a modern, digital-first approach to early education enrolment.

“Our mission is simple: eliminate stress for parents while driving efficiency for nursery operators.”

The latest fundraising follows an earlier £275,000 investment, meaning Nuuri has now announced £825,000 across its first two funding rounds.

The company has also promoted Yan San to chief technology officer as it steps up development of its technology.

Ms San joined Nuuri as senior founding engineer in October 2025 and was promoted in July. Her previous experience includes roles with care technology businesses Care Sourcer and Lottie.

Graham Schwikkard, chief executive of SyndicateRoom, said Nuuri is looking to build a fully-connected network that the market currently lacks.

“Nuuri is tackling a critical inefficiency in the UK childcare market with a clear, tech-driven solution," he added. "The business has demonstrated exceptional commercial traction, and we are delighted to partner with the team as they scale their technology and transform the landscape for families and providers alike.”
