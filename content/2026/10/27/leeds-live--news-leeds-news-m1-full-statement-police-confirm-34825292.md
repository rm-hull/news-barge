# M1 full statement as 'person falls from height' onto motorway

## Police have blocked an M1 exit slip road to the M62 in Lofthouse

A person has fallen from a height close to a major motorway intersection in Lofthouse.

Paramedics have rushed the person to hospital following an emergency call at 1.18pm today, on Tuesday, October 27. Police have blocked an M1 exit slip road to the M62 westbound at junction 42 and traffic has been affected.

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 1.18pm on Tuesday afternoon to report a person who had fallen from height close to the motorway intersection at Lofthouse. Ambulance resources responded to the incident and one patient has been conveyed to hospital."

Data traffic site Inrix said: "M1 Southbound exit slip road to the M62 westbound closed, slow traffic due to police incident at J42 M62 J29 (Lofthouse)."

West Yorkshire Police have been asked for further comment. The condition of the patient is not known at this stage.
