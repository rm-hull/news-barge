A campaign group is highlighting the need for better mental health and addiction support for the homeless.

People Overcoming Poverty (POP), a group facilitated by York Foodbank, is working on a campaign to improve support for people in the city who are both homeless and dealing with mental health and addiction issues.

Currently, they say there is no dedicated dual-diagnosis service in York, meaning people seeking help are often passed between services and unable to move forward.

This, the group says, leaves people trapped in a cycle of addiction, mental health problems, and homelessness.

In an effort to tackle the issue, the group is hosting a drop-in event on Friday, October 30, in partnership with York in Recovery.

The event is for people who have experienced these issues to share their stories and ideas for better support.

The group will then present their findings to local decision-makers.
