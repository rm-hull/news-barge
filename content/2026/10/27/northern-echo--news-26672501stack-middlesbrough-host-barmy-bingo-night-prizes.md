A night of bingo with a twist is coming to Middlesbrough, with games, music and some unusual prizes up for grabs.

Stack Middlesbrough will host Barmy Bingo on Friday, November 13, combining classic bingo with music, games and prizes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18864118.jpg?type=mds-article-620)STACK Middlesbrough *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Whether guests score a full house or not, there will be plenty of opportunities to win, with players competing in a variety of games for prizes throughout the night.

Stack Middlesbrough said: "Two Little Ducks and a Baker’s Bun – it’s time for fun, as Barmy Bingo turns STACK Middlesbrough into Sunset Strip.

"On Friday November 13 it will be eyes down at the Exchange Square venue for a fun-filled night of wild games and prizes for those with their bingo dabbers to hand."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18864173.jpg?type=mds-article-620)STACK Middlesbrough *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Guests can also grab drinks and food from Stack’s bars and street food traders throughout the evening.

The covered venue can accommodate 650 people indoors and a further 150 outside, with 25 shipping containers housing five street-food traders, three bars, a stage and a large television screen.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18864118.jpg?type=mds-article-620)STACK Middlesbrough *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The Flaming Tattie is Stack's newest food trader, serving loaded baked potatoes finished over a flame.

Its menu includes potatoes topped with double cheesy beans, crispy onions and barbecue drizzle, plus a crispy chicken parmo version with a Teesside twist.

Diners can also build their own, starting with butter or garlic butter before choosing their toppings.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21524881.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Flaming Tattie is now serving loaded jacket potatoes at STACK Middlesbrough. *(Image: STACK Middlesbrough)*

The Flaming Tattie joins a number of other food traders at Stack, including Zza Pizzeria, Brack Burger and Holy Duck.

Doors for Barmy Bingo open at 7pm, with ticket holders asked to arrive by 7.15pm ahead of a 7.30pm start.
