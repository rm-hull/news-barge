A West Yorkshire sex offender who fled the country has been found guilty of multiple rapes dating back more than 20 years.

Muhammed Khalid, 55, was convicted at Leeds Crown Court of eight offences, including five counts of rape, sexual assault, and supplying Class A drugs, following a week-long trial.

The offences took place in Kirklees in the early to mid-2000s against two teenage girls.

Khalid, of Huddersfield, is due to be sentenced on November 24.

## Fled UK while under investigation

He fled the UK in 2020 while under investigation by officers looking into historic sexual offences in the Kirklees area.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/19939383.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Leeds Crown Court)*

After extensive efforts to trace him, police arrested Khalid at Manchester Airport on April 23 this year as he returned to the UK.

He was taken into custody and charged, before appearing before magistrates in Kirklees the following day.

Detective Chief Inspector Phil Davis of Kirklees District Police welcomed the conviction and praised the survivors’ bravery.

DCI Davis said: "We welcome the jury’s verdict today in finding Khalid guilty of very serious offences committed against young and very vulnerable victims more than 20 years ago.

"He committed multiple rapes over a prolonged period against a vulnerable teenage girl, abusing her in a fashion which can only be described as appalling.

"He has also been convicted of supplying Class A drugs to another victim."

He said officers remained committed to the case even after Khalid left the country.

## DCI commends survivors courage

DCI Davis said: "Officers never stopped enquiries to locate Khalid after he fled the country, and arrangements were swiftly put in place with partners to detain him when he arrived back in the UK.

"I hope this case, and the many other convictions we are continuing to see at our courts for non-recent sexual offending, further demonstrate that we will never stop trying to seek justice for victims.

"I want to commend the courage of survivors in this case for speaking with us and disclosing the abuse they suffered.

"We fully appreciate just how difficult it can be for victims of sexual offending to speak with us and can pledge we will do all we can to support them."
