A homeless shoplifter stole food to feed himself while sofa-surfing or sleeping rough, a court heard.

Daniel Mickle helped himself to £114-worth of steaks from Sainsbury’s in Chester-le-Street, on Tuesday, July 21.

Mickle, 35, now of Second Avenue, Chester-le-Street, admitted a single charge of theft when he appeared before magistrates in Peterlee.

David Malone, representing the defendant, said three months ago he had been homeless, either sofa-surfing or sleeping rough.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21632126.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Peterlee magistrates were told defendant 'stole to live' at a time he was homeless and having trouble accessing benefits

Mr Malone said the defendant “stole to live”, “in desperation” while struggling to access benefits.

But Mr Malone told the court that the defendant's situation has improved since his mother took him in.

Magistrates’ chair John Marr told Mickle: “We have looked at your record and can see that quite some time ago you were going through the criminal justice system.

“Almost understandably, if you are homeless and not receiving benefits you need to get by.

“Unfortunately, in your case, you took the Sainsbury’s option.

“Hopefully you can put this down as a lesson.”

The bench gave Mickle a six-month conditional discharge and ordered him to pay £28 in costs and £114 compensation to Sainsbury’s.

Mr Marr wished Mickle “good luck for the future” and said he hopes he can continue to "live properly."
