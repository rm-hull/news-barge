Homicides rose by almost a fifth last year – but the overall number of such cases is still the fourth lowest since records began

According to the latest statistics for 2025-26, 56 victims were recorded, nine more than the 47 victims recorded in 2024-25.

However, this is the fourth-lowest number since comparable records began in 1976, and a decrease of nine compared with a decade ago. It is also a large decrease of 65 fewer than the 121 recorded in 2006-07.

The figures show a sharp divide between the sexes, with men - 41 - making up the majority of victims. In 27 of these cases, the accused was an acquaintance of the victim.

Women accounted for 15 victims, with two-thirds killed by a partner or ex-partner.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/17523643.jpg?type=mds-article-620)All murder cases in Scotland were solved *(Image: PA)*

The greatest reduction in homicide victims over the last 20 years has been among young people aged 16 to 24.

In the five-year period from 2006-07 to 2010-11, there were 113 victims in this age range. This fell to 28 over the latest five-year period, from 2021-22 to 2025-26.

In 2025-26, 81 people were accused of homicide, of whom 93% (75) were male and 7% (six) were female.

All 56 homicide victims recorded in 2025-26 were associated with cases that were solved.

For each of the last 20 years, the most common method of killing was the use of a sharp instrument.

In 2025-26, a sharp instrument was the main method of killing in 52% of cases, accounting for 29 homicide victims.

While most recorded homicide incidents involve one victim and one accused person, some incidents can have multiple victims and/or accused. In 2025-26, there were no incidents involving more than one victim.
