A County Durham filmmaker whose zero-budget horror film became an online sensation has announced a sequel following its "remarkable success".

Dean Midas, the indie filmmaker behind The Stitcher, confirmed The Stitcher 2 is in development after the original short film surpassed 160,000 views in just three weeks.

Mr Midas said: "The response to The Stitcher has been beyond anything we imagined.

"Reaching 160,000 views in three weeks shows what can be achieved right here in the North East using just a smartphone.

"Fans have been asking where the story goes next, so I’m thrilled to confirm that The Stitcher 2 is officially in development for release next year."

Filmed entirely on a mobile phone and produced with no budget, The Stitcher gained attention for its chilling use of historic County Durham locations.
