A 38-year-old man described by his family as having a "beautiful heart and soul" has been named as the victim of a fatal attack in West Yorkshire.

Rahiem Jackson, known as Reema, was killed in New Wortley, Leeds, in what his family called a "senseless" act of violence.

The attack happened on Hedley Chase at around 10.23pm on Saturday, October 24.

## Family tribute

In a tribute, Mr Jackson’s family said: "Rahiem was loved and adored by his sisters and family.

"He had a beautiful heart and beautiful soul; his smile was so infectious, and he lit up the room with his presence.

"Rahiem would have gone above and beyond for anybody he cared for."

They described his death as the result of a "cowardly, cruel and senseless act of vicious violence."

They added: "He wasn’t just a brother; he was a son, an uncle, a father, a partner, a nephew, a cousin, a grandson, brother-in-law and friend and he did not deserve to die the way he did.

"We are heartbroken and in so much pain and have been left inconsolable as a family.

"If anyone has any information to help the police find the persons who murdered Rahiem, please can you come forward."

Mr Jackson was attacked by four people.

## Statement from police

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates of West Yorkshire Police’s Homicide and Major Enquiry Team said: "Rahiem’s murder has devastated his family and we are doing everything we can to identify and bring those responsible to justice.

"We believe four people, armed with weapons, carried out a sustained attack on Rahiem and I continue to urge anyone who may have seen those persons arrive at or flee the scene on Hedley Chase, or who has any footage of them to come forwards."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police on 101 or through the West Yorkshire Police Live Chat, quoting reference 13260606745.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
