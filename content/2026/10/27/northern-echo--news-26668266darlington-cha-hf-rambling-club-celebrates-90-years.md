A Darlington walking club which has survived war, disease outbreaks and a pandemic is marking 90 years of rambling with a 90-mile challenge.

Darlington CHA & HF Rambling Club was formed at a meeting organised by Frank Bowman on October 29, 1936, with its first walk taking place just days later.

On Sunday, November 8, 1936, Bill Sugdon and Harold Sowerby led members from Darlington to Richmond, beginning a tradition which has continued for nine decades.

![Black-and-white photograph of the club’s original members pictured together in 1936](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21628574.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The original members of the club pictured together in its founding year, 1936 *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The club made it through the Second World War, two major foot-and-mouth outbreaks and, most recently, the Covid-19 pandemic.

Today it remains more than 100 members strong and organises full-day walks on Saturdays and Sundays throughout most of the year, as well as some midweek outings.

Its unusual name also provides a link with its past.

![Members of Darlington CHA & HF Rambling Club pictured during a walk in 1957.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21628561.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stepping back in time: Darlington CHA & HF Rambling Club members pictured in 1957 *(Image: DARLINGTON CHA & HF RAMBLING CLUB)*

The club says CHA & HF dates back to its association with the former Countrywide Holidays Association and Holiday Fellowship, the latter of which became HF Holidays.

To celebrate its 90th birthday, members are tackling a 90-mile route linking some of their favourite walking country, completing it in stages over a series of Sunday outings, following the club’s earlier 80th-anniversary 80-mile walk.

The programme takes walkers through places including Leyburn, Redmire, Reeth, Muker and Tan Hill before the final section from Barningham to Barnard Castle on Sunday, November 8.

That final walk falls exactly 90 years after the club's inaugural outing.

The celebrations will also move temporarily from walking boots to the dining table, with an anniversary meal at Bannatyne Hotel on Saturday, November 7.

Walking holidays remain part of club life too, with two HF Holidays trips each year, involving guided walks from country houses around the UK.
