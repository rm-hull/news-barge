# Has Trump gone quiet on Iran because of his Russia deal? - Yorkshire Post Letters

## From: Shirley Brewer, Scarborough.

It would appear that Iran is supplying fuel to Russia by sea and in turn it is going to be sold to the USA.

Is that why Trump, hand in hand with Putin, has decided to leave attacking Iran until the mid-term elections have been declared, to say that the gas price has come down - blaming Zelensky for its increase in the meantime?

And in return for Russia's help in the mid-elections (as the ones before) he has ceased starlink/intel to Ukraine?

Trump keeps harking back to his "stolen 2020 election" as he thought Putin had promised him it and thus can't understand why! If Trump loses these mid-terms, America will be under martial law, as he only wants to emulate his icon Putin and, bless him, Kim Jong Un.

We all know (well most of us) that since 2014 Russia has influenced Europe and the USA via lies (once known as misinformation or propaganda) from mainly RT and Fox News.

Putin is doing a good job of that and I do hope we fight alongside Zelensky for freedom and eventual peace, instead of the armageddon that the far-right governments wish for.

It beggars belief that Putin waged war on "Nazi Ukraine" forgetting that Zelensky is Jewish, when some European countries have turned right-wing and pro-Russia.

We have clowns to the left of us and jokers to the right, stuck in the scary middle!
