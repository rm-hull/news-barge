A torchlit procession through a North East city centre will herald the arrival of a medieval-inspired music group as it celebrates a milestone anniversary.

The Mediaeval Baebes are marking 30 years of atmospheric music and storytelling with a special concert at Newcastle Cathedral on Wednesday, December 9.

The event is part of their 30th Anniversary Tour and promises an evening of ethereal harmonies and seasonal enchantment.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633844.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Mediaeval Baebes *(Image: Rachel Le Fay)*

A torchlit procession will begin at 6.25pm from The Statue of Queen Victoria in The Square.

Families, musicians, and re-enactors are invited to take part by dressing in costume, carrying lanterns, or bringing drums.

They can also simply watch as the procession makes its way to the cathedral for the concert.

Once inside, the Mediaeval Baebes will perform a set blending medieval atmosphere with contemporary storytelling.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633845.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Mediaeval Baebes *(Image: Rachel Le Fay)*

Their music is brought to life with harmonies and historical instruments.

A spokesperson for the group said: "the Baebes invite audiences to celebrate theirjourney with a concert that honours the music that has defined them, while also embracing the nextchapter of their story.

"Expect voices that shimmer and stories that linger.

"Instruments that evoke another age and music thatcarries the audience through winter into light.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633847.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Mediaeval Baebes *(Image: Rob Kelly)*

"Everyone is invited to listen."

The concert will highlight The Spinning Wheel, the group’s latest album.

It features twelve songs inspired by the twelve months of the year, weaving together ancient texts and new melodies.

The album explores themes of customs, romance, nature and the passage of time.

Formed in 1996 by musical director and composer Katharine Blake, the Mediaeval Baebes have released 12 studio albums and toured internationally.

They have performed in cathedrals, castles, and even on the Glastonbury Festival’s Pyramid Stage.

Their career includes a performance at the Royal Albert Hall, a tour with Jools Holland, and an Ivor Novello Award for their performance on the BBC’s The Virgin Queen.

They have also received two Emmy nominations and a Royal Television Society award for their work alongside composer Martin Phipps on ITV’s Victoria.

The anniversary concert will feature a mix of new songs, festive favourites, and pieces from their extensive repertoire.

It promises to be an evening that feels both timeless and fresh, inviting listeners into a world of melody and myth.

For more information and to book tickets, visit www.mediaevalbaebes.co.uk.
