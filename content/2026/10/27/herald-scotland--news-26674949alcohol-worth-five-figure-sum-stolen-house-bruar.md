A police investigation is underway after alcohol worth a five-figure sum was stolen from the House of Bruar.

Police said the break-in at the department store near Blair Atholl took place at around 2am on Tuesday, October 27.

The first suspect was wearing a black jacket, black balaclava, blue denim jeans, white shoes and was wearing gloves.

The second suspect was wearing a white jumper, black trousers, hiking style boots, a black balaclava and gloves.

Officers are currently in attendance while enquiries are carried out, police said.

Detective Constable Mark Chance said: “We are appealing to anyone who was in the area around the time and has any information to come forward and speak to officers.

“We are also particularly keen to speak to anyone who was driving in the area around 2.05am as from enquiries carried out so far, this is when the suspects are believed to have left the scene.

“If you have dash-cam footage from the area around that time, we’d ask you to review this to see if it has captured anything that could assist with our investigation.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 0234 of 27 October, 2026.

Alternatively, information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
