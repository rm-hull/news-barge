Festive cheer is in full swing as a County Durham garden centre launches a packed programme of Christmas events.

East Durham Garden Centre has unveiled a calendar of activities designed to delight visitors of all ages, from magical mornings with Santa to traditional Christmas dinners.

Among the highlights are the centre’s popular Breakfast with Santa events, where children can enjoy a cooked breakfast, meet Santa, take photos, and receive a personal gift.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21636801.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: East Durham Garden Centre)*

There is also a dedicated baby and toddler breakfast with Santa for younger guests.

Families and friends can also indulge in a festive feast at the centre’s Christmas Dinner events.

It features slow-roasted beef with homemade Yorkshire pudding, traditional roast turkey with sage, cranberry and onion stuffing, and delicately cooked haddock béarnaise on a bed of spinach.

For those seeking a vegetarian option, a chestnut and seed roast with cranberry-spiced gravy is also available.

Desserts include classic Christmas pudding with brandy and vanilla sauce, chocolate and mandarin trifle, spiced mince pie cheesecake, and a chocolate cherry dessert with raspberry coulis and whipped Chantilly cream.

Visitors can also enjoy a festive afternoon tea, offering a seasonal twist on the classic tradition.

The tea includes a selection of sweet and savoury treats served with freshly brewed tea or coffee.

All events are designed to capture the spirit of the season and offer guests a memorable way to celebrate in the lead-up to Christmas.

Andrea Smith, centre manager at East Durham Garden Centre, said: "We can't wait to share the magic of the festive season with you at East Durham Garden Centre."

"Join us for an enchanting lineup of Christmas events filled with warmth, community, and holiday cheer.

"From dazzling lights and immersive experiences to unforgettable moments with Santa, our team is dedicated to bringing the wonder of Christmas to life.

"Bring your loved ones and create cherished memories that will last a lifetime."
