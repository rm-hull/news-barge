## Popular supermarket set to welcome first customers to new store in York

Lidl is coming to Clifton Moor on Thursday (November 5) *(Image: Press Association)*

A brand-new Lidl store is opening its doors in York next week.

Located in Clifton Moor, the retailer’s fourth store in the city will officially welcome customers from 8am on Thursday, November 5.

The opening will begin with a ribbon-cutting ceremony featuring the Mayor of York, with early shoppers able to enjoy complimentary food samples and exclusive offers through the Lidl Plus app.




Liam Schofield, regional head of property at Lidl GB, said: "We are thrilled to officially open our fourth Lidl store in York.

"This fantastic new store is a direct response to the local demand for high-quality, affordable groceries, and we couldn’t be prouder to welcome the community inside."

![Inside, customers will find a 1,306-square-metre sales area, an in-store bakery, and the popular 'Middle of Lidl' aisle](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21016822.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside, customers will find a 1,306-square-metre sales area, an in-store bakery, and the popular 'Middle of Lidl' aisle *(Image: Lidl GB)*

He also expressed gratitude to those who helped bring the project to life.

Mr Schofield said: "A huge thank you to everyone who helped us turn these plans into a reality – we can’t wait to start making a real difference to your weekly shop."

The new store has created close to 40 jobs, with a focus on hiring residents from the surrounding area.

Inside, customers will find a 1,306-square-metre sales area, an in-store bakery, and the popular 'Middle of Lidl' aisle.

Lidl has served the York community for more than 15 years, and the new site aims to make shopping more accessible for those in the northern part of the city.

The company’s community support efforts include its Feed it Back scheme, which has donated nearly 380,000 meals to people in need across York since 2017.

Through its partnership with Neighbourly, Lidl supports charities such as Help Brings Hope, Luke’s Larder, The Snack Shack, and Your Cafe.

The new store will open from 8am to 10pm Monday to Saturday, and from 10am to 4pm on Sundays.
