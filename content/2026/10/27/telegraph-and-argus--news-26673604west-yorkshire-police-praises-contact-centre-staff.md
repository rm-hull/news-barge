A CALL handler is being celebrated for her vital work supporting the public and police officers.

Georgina, 27, has worked for nearly three years at the West Yorkshire Police Contact Centre, where she answers 999 and 101 calls, responds to live chat enquiries, and handles online reports.

Marking International Control Room Week, West Yorkshire Police has shared her story to highlight the dedication of control room staff who work around the clock to keep the public safe.

Georgina said: "I wanted to make a difference in people’s lives and saw this role as the opportunity to do so.

"In this job, every day is different, and I’ve learnt so much about the police."

Before joining the contact centre, Georgina worked for five years in hospitality and later in probation.

She had always wanted a career in policing and saw the role as a chance to help others without becoming a police officer.

The role can be demanding, with call handlers often supporting people in distress, gathering critical information, and ensuring officers are dispatched where needed.

Georgina said: "My favourite part of my job is knowing that I have helped a person in need and been there to support them."

She also emphasised the importance of teamwork in the contact centre.

She said: "My team are the main support in this job, and I rely on them every single day.

"There are situations where you need help quickly with a call, and your colleagues around you are the first to help."

Georgina encouraged others considering a career in policing to look at contact centre roles as a starting point.

She said: "Full training is provided, and you don’t need prior experience.

"Before joining, I wanted to work within the force, but I didn’t want to be an officer.

"This job was perfect."

She also reminded the public to use the appropriate contact method when reaching out to police.

While 999 should be reserved for emergencies requiring an immediate response, 101, Live Chat, and online reporting are available for non-emergencies or general advice.

Misusing 999 can delay help reaching people who are in genuine danger, so using the right contact method can make a real difference.

The force thanked Georgina and all contact centre staff for their dedication and hard work.

"Thank you to Georgina and all our Contact Centre staff for the work they do every day!"
