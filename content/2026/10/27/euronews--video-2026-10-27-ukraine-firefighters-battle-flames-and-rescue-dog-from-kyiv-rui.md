Amid smoke and shattered debris in **Kyiv**, firefighters** pulled a frightened dog alive**from a building hit by** Russian missiles**. Its** owner had been killed**. A** second injured dog**was later discovered nearby, with Ukrainian reports saying its** owner had also died**.

The Ukrainian capital came under **two waves of ballistic missiles** overnight into Tuesday, 27 October. A 46-year-old woman was**killed** and**14 people injured**, nine of whom were taken to hospital.

**Students** were among the worst affected after**university accommodation**, campus buildings and medical facilities near Lisova metro station were struck.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia fired **16 ballistic missiles and more than 280 drones** at Ukraine, hitting energy and logistics sites across 11 regions.

In Kyiv, the **Pivdennyi bridge was damaged**,** disrupting metro services** across the Dnipro.

Zelenskyy also **criticised delays in Patriot and NASAMS interceptor deliveries**, warning that each setback left Ukraine exposed to further Russian attacks.
