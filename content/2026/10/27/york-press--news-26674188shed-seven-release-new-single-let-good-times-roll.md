## New single from York indie legends drops and homecoming gig sold out

Shed Seven's new single Let The Good Times Roll has been shared on streaming platforms. *(Image: Tom Oxley)*

Shed Seven have shared the streaming of a new single from their forthcoming album and also announced tickets have just sold out for their biggest headline show to date.

Let The Good Times Roll is the latest offering from the band's eponymously-titled seventh studio album and arrives amid a sold-out UK tour marking the 30th anniversary of their 1996 album A Maximum High.

Shed Seven said the new single shows a different side to the forthcoming album, set for release on January 8, 2027, via Cooking Vinyl.




Lead singer Rick Witter said: "Eyes on the prize, watch silver turn to gold.

"Winning in life, a time to be positive and enjoy the things happening around you.

"Forget the negativity and Let The Good Times Roll."

Shed Seven recently sold-out York’s 14,000-capacity LNER Community Stadium, scheduled for May 29, 2027.

To mark the occasion, they have released a special "York City" card wallet CD edition of the new album, featuring three bonus tracks and unique artwork.

Proceeds will support the York City Foundation.

Shed Seven also plan to use the event to champion emerging talent from their hometown.

A fan zone stage will be set up ahead of the main show, featuring up-and-coming artists from York.

He said: "Our ambition, even as schoolkids, was to one day play our own stadium show.

"What kid who dreams of being in a rock ’n’ roll band doesn’t want to do that?

"Now, almost 40 years later, we’ve reached the point where we thought… it’s now or never.

"And if we were going to do it, it had to be York.

"What will make it even more special is that the stadium is a stone’s throw from where we met, went to school and where we first formed a band.

"We’re coming back to pretty much where it all began to play the biggest show of our career and that is a proper full-circle moment."

![Shed Seven at The Piece Hall Halifax, June 2026](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21249084.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Shed Seven at The Piece Hall Halifax, June 2026 *(Image: Ianthe Photo)*

The band has seen a resurgence in recent years, achieving their first-ever Number 1 album in 2024 with A Matter Of Time.

Just ten months later, they secured a second Number 1 with Liquid Gold, joining the ranks of artists such as The Beatles, Elton John, and David Bowie who have achieved two UK Number 1 albums in a single calendar year.
