**The body of a man has been discovered at a Scottish beach.**

Police said the discovery was made on Tuesday morning when they received a report of a body in the water around 9.45am.

Montrose and Arbroath Coastguard Rescue Teams and an RNLI lifeboat from Montrose attended, along with officers from Police Scotland and medics from the Scottish Ambulance Service.

**Read More:**

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: “Around 9.45am on Tuesday, October 27, we received a report of a body in water at Montrose beach.

“The body of a man has been recovered.

“Inquiries are ongoing.”

A HM Coastguard spokesperson said: “Montrose and Arbroath Coastguard Rescue Teams and an RNLI lifeboat from Montrose attended an incident reported at Montrose at about 10.10am on October 27.”

A Scottish Ambulance Service spokesperson added: “We received a call at 9.52am to attend an incident near Traill Drive, Montrose. A special operations response team (SORT) and an ambulance were dispatched to the scene.”
