**What does* ins Fettnäpfchen treten*mean?**

*Ins Fettnäpfchen treten* literally means “to step into the little grease bowl”.

Figuratively, it means committing a social blunder – saying something tactless or 'putting your foot in it' in other words.

Grammatically, it is an idiomatic verb phrase. *Ins* is a contraction of*in das* and takes the accusative because it describes movement into something.

*Fettnäpfchen* is a neuter compound noun formed from*Fett*(fat or grease) and* Näpfchen*(little bowl).* Treten* is a verb meaning “to step”.

Put them all together and you should get something like *Ich bin ins Fettnäpfchen getreten* (I put my foot in it).

The phrase is linked to the small bowls of animal fat supposedly once kept near the doors of German farmhouses. The grease was apparently used to soften and waterproof leather shoes, but its position on the floor made it a hazard for clumsy visitors.

One misplaced step could send foul-smelling fat across freshly scrubbed floors, leaving the guest embarrassed and the host unimpressed – roughly similar to the reactions you might expect if you congratulate someone on a promotion they didn't get or on passing a test they failed.

In German, if someone never misses an opportunity to say the wrong thing, *er* * lässt kein Fettnäpfchen aus* (literally, he leaves out no little bowl of fat).

Or, if someone makes an especially magnificent blunder, you could say *er* * springt mit Anlauf ins Fettnäpfchen* (literally, he jumps into the little bowl of fat with a running start).

The expression is widely used in everyday conversations, workplaces and media reports about public gaffes.

It’s also particularly handy for foreign residents navigating a new language and unfamiliar cultural practices. After all, putting your foot in it is what you do if you inadvertently wish someone in Germany a happy birthday before the day itself, or use *du* instead of*Sie* at the wrong moment.

More usefully, naming your blunder can help defuse it – possibly with a rueful *Da bin ich wohl ins Fettnäpfchen getreten* (Looks like I’ve just put my foot in it).

**Use it like this:**

*Mit dieser Bemerkung trat der Politiker ins Fettnäpfchen.*

The politician committed a gaffe with that remark.

*Beim Abendessen mit den Schwiegereltern ließ er kein Fettnäpfchen aus.*

At dinner with his in-laws, he made every blunder possible.

*Er sprang mit Anlauf ins Fettnäpfchen, als er ihr zur Schwangerschaft gratulierte.*

He dropped an almighty clanger when he congratulated her on being pregnant.
