Emergency services were called to a crash on the A64 near York last night (Monday).

Fire crews from York, Malton and Huntington went to the collision on the eastbound carriageway at **5.22pm**.

They made the vehicle safe and administered first aid to the casualty before handing over to paramedics at the scene.
