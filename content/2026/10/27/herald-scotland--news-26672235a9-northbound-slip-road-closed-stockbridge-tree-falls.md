A major road north has been blocked in one direction after a tree fell in high winds.

Drivers heading north on the A9 are facing disruption after a fallen tree forced the closure of the northbound on-slip at Stockbridge.

Traffic Scotland said the section of road is currently closed, with a diversion route now in operation for motorists.

Road users are being directed along the A820 towards the southbound on-slip at Keir Roundabout, where they can turn around and rejoin the motorway to continue their journey northbound.

Motorists are being urged to allow extra time for their journeys and follow the signed diversion route while the obstruction is dealt with.

It is not yet known when the slip road will reopen. Drivers are advised to check for the latest travel updates before setting off.
