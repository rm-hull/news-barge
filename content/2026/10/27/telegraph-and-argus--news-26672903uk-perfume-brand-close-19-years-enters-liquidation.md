A luxury perfume brand worn by royalty and stocked at Harrods is closing after 19 years, having collapsed into liquidation.

UK-based Bella Bellissima was founded by Bella Crane in 2007 and has become known for its "unique" fragrances made from "rare, natural and intricately elaborate raw materials".

The perfume brand is stocked in Harrods stores across the UK (London, Lakeside, and Gateshead) and has developed a loyal following that includes royal clientele.

The Bella Bellissima website adds: "Bella Bellissima embraces artisanal production techniques, utilises the natural world’s most treasured ingredients, encapsulated in bespoke glass bottles with thoughtful, reusable packaging.

"The brand has a loyal, global and Royal clientele.

"Each creation has amassed a dedicated following, with the brand often referred to as 'one of the best kept secrets in the world of perfume.'"

## Bella Bellissima to close after 19 years as it enters liquidation

After 19 years in business, Bella Bellissima has now entered liquidation and is set to close.

A resolution was passed on October 21 that House of Unique Parfum Ltd, which operates as Bella Bellissima, be "wound up voluntarily".  
  
Nicholas Simmonds and Chris Newell of Quantuma Advisory Limited were then appointed as joint liquidators, according to The Gazette.

Bella Bellissima has been contacted for comment.

However, Queensway Inns and Common Table Group have now taken over 110 of these former Whitbread sites, saving them from closure.

TG Jones and the British Heart Foundation will also be closing around 150 stores each across the UK.

Wynsors World of Shoes is shutting 17 stores across the UK, with closing down sales already underway at several affected branches.

UK entertainment retailer Zavvi has ceased trading after nearly 20 years, having collapsed into administration for a second time.

Australian stationery retailer Typo is also set to pull out of the UK in 2027, closing all its stores and ending online trading.
