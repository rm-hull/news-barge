Staff and residents at a North East care service raised more than £100 for a breast cancer charity through a day of fundraising and awareness activities.

Cygnet Newham House in Hemlington, Middlesbrough, a 20-bed neuropsychiatric service for women with acquired brain injuries, organised the fundraiser for Breast Cancer Now.

Joanna Yarker, hospital manager, said: "Our event showed what can be achieved when a committed team works together towards a shared goal.

"I am proud of the professionalism, compassion and determination shown by every member of the team."

Support worker and women’s health lead Mshiana Ater organised the event, which included tie-dye T-shirt making, games and a chance to share personal stories and raise awareness.

Ms Yarker said: "The individuals we support are all women, so we thought this charity would be good to raise awareness for.
