**A two-year-old boy was taken to hospital after being attacked by a dog at a property in Glasgow.**

Emergency services were called to Mybster Place at around 9.55am on Monday, October 26.

The child was taken to hospital by ambulance for treatment to injuries.

The dog was later euthanised.

Police say enquiries into the incident remain ongoing.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "Around 9.55am on Monday, 26 October, 2026, we were called to a report of a two-year-old boy being attacked by a dog within a property on Mybster Place in Glasgow.

"The child was taken to hospital by ambulance for treatment to injuries.

"The dog was euthanised.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
