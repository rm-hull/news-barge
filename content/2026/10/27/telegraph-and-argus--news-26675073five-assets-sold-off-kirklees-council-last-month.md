A COUNCIL in West Yorkshire is planning to offload more assets in a bid to bring in £4m this year - including selling a former community centre in Cleckheaton.

Kirklees Council is grappling with a £13.6m overspend in the current financial year, with the acceleration of asset sales one of the measures being taken to address the financial difficulties.

In recent years, the council has earmarked dozens of assets for disposal. Some of the sales have proved controversial – not least the sales of Gomersal’s Red House Museum, the former Almondbury Library and Batley Baths.

This year, the council hopes to make a minimum of £4m from selling off land and buildings and recently identified some more to add to the pipeline. The local authority says this same target will also apply to the three financial years that follow. In 2025/26, it made around £7.5m from sales.

Below are the assets Kirklees Council sold off via auction last month and how much they went for, as listed on the BTG Eddisons website:

Land at Bent Street & Elm Street, Newsome, Huddersfield – £127,000

Federation House, 2a St. Johns Avenue, Newsome – £166,000

Garage site at Knaresborough Drive, Huddersfield – £15,000

Former Garage Site at Nabcroft Lane, Huddersfield – £78,000

Edgerton Cemetery Lodge , Cemetery Road, Edgerton – Sold post auction

At this moment in time, the council has two lots in November’s upcoming auctions. These are set out below, along with the minimum opening bid:

Former Turnsteads Community Centre, Turnsteads Drive, Cleckheaton – £100,000

Land at Stocks Bank Road, Mirfield – £30,000
