Firefighters battled an out-of-control blaze at a business last night.

The emergency service was called at 7pm on Monday to a fire at the Branchfield Industrial Estate on Richardshaw Lane, Stanningley.

The blaze broke out inside a business and involved an industrial burner.

A spokesperson for the West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “Crews attended an out-of-control fire in an industrial burner inside a commercial premises.

“Crews used four breathing apparatus, two hose reels and one aerial appliance to extinguish the fire.”

Specialist fire service resources at the scene included a CLM vehicle, an aerial appliance and aerial support.
