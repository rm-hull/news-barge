# Andy Burnham £192 plan for DWP state pensioners who retired before 2016

## State pensioners on the basic State Pension who retired before April 2016 are expected to receive a weekly pay boost

State pensioners receiving the basic rate who retired before 2016 are in line for £192 weekly payments from the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) come spring. Thanks to the triple lock mechanism, the state pension receives an annual increase each April aligned with inflation, wages, or 2.5% – whichever proves highest.

The Office for National Statistics (ONS) announced on Wednesday that the Consumer Prices Index (CPI) inflation rate stood at 3.5% in September, climbing from 3.1% in August. However, this falls short of the 3.9% wage growth figure that had previously been released.

This means someone receiving the full new state pension would be on track to collect £250.70 weekly from next April – rising from the current £241.30 per week, reports Birmingham Live.

Someone receiving a full basic state pension would collect £192.10 weekly, increasing from £184.90, totalling approximately £9,989 annually.
