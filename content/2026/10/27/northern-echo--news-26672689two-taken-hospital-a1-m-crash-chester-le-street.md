Two people have been rushed to hospital after a four-car crash on the A1(M) in County Durham this morning.

Emergency services were called to the A1(M) between junction 61 Chester-le-Street and 63 Bowburn.

Traffic was temporarily held as crews attended the scene, and delays reached highs of 63 minutes for drivers, as a road was closed.

The traffic has since cleared.

A spokesperson for Durham Constabulary said: "We were called to reports of a four-car collision on the southbound carriageway of the A1(M) near Carrville shortly after 8am this morning (Tuesday, October 27).

"Two people were taken to hospital for treatment.

"The road was blocked while the vehicles were recovered."

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “We were called to a road traffic incident on the A1 southbound in Durham at 8.20am on Tuesday 27 October.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and two patients were assessed and discharged on scene, and two patients were taken to University Hospital of North Durham (UHND) for further treatment.”
