Families affected by the **Hamas-led attacks of 7 October 2023** gathered in Tel Aviv on Tuesday to honour relatives killed that day, observing a**minute's silence** before joining together in song beside a white installation bearing the date “**7.10.23**”.

Holding photographs of their loved ones, participants included **Yona Adam**, whose daughter Mapal was killed at the Nova music festival, and** Einav Zangauker**, mother of former hostage Matan Zangauker.

The commemoration took place as Israelis headed to the polls on **Tuesday, 27 October**, for their** first general election since the attacks**, voting to elect the** 26th Knesset**.

In **Kiryat Arba**, an Israeli settlement near Hebron in the occupied West Bank, voters queued outside polling stations. Far-right National Security Minister** Itamar Ben-Gvir** also cast his ballot in the nationalist-right stronghold.

In **Umm al-Fahm**, an Arab-majority city in northern Israel, election officials prepared ballot papers at al-Aqwas school before voters entered to cast their votes. Similar scenes unfolded at polling stations in** Jerusalem**.

**Exit polls** are expected at**21:00 CET** on Tuesday, although it could**take weeks** before a**new government is formed**.
