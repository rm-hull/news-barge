A hidden chocolate bar in York could be one lucky person's golden ticket to a year of sweet surprises.

York’s Chocolate Story in King's Square is launching a city-wide chocolate hunt from today (Tuesday, October 28) with 100 chocolate bars waiting to be discovered—one of which holds the ultimate prize of a year’s supply of chocolate.

The shop and cafe at the popular York attraction recently underwent a transformation, as reported by The Press, and the owners are taking the news out onto the streets this half-term.




![York's Chocolate Story](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21623196.jpg?type=mds-article-620)York's Chocolate Story unwrapped its new look shop and rebrand in King's Square last Friday (October 23) *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

Stuart Wood, general manager at York’s Chocolate Story, said: "We wanted to celebrate our new look by doing something fun that brings a little extra chocolate magic to the streets of York this half term.

"We’re hiding the chocolate bars across the city, some of which also contain vouchers for extra prizes, including free hot chocolates and Guided Tours, while one lucky finder will win a year’s supply of chocolate to give them 12 months of sweet treats.

"We’ll be sharing some clues on Instagram, but we’re also hoping people will enjoy exploring York, discovering some of its iconic locations and perhaps stumbling across somewhere new along the way."

York's Chocolate Story said the lucky few could pick up a 90g chocolate bar, and more than half contain vouchers for additional prizes, such as 25 free hot chocolates, 25 free Classic Guided Tours, and five teddy bears.

The top prize is a year’s supply of chocolate, delivered as 12 monthly hampers, plus a free Classic Guided Tour of York’s Chocolate Story.

Clues to some of the hiding spots will be shared on York’s Chocolate Story’s Instagram account, @yorkschocstory, while others are left to be found by keen-eyed explorers.

Vouchers found inside the bars can be redeemed in-store at York’s Chocolate Story.

![York's Chocolate Story](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21623198.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Passers-by were met by greeters offering tempting treats outside the shop when the shop re-opened on Friday *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

The chocolate hunt follows the recent unveiling of the attraction’s newly refurbished shop and café.

The upgrades include the first dedicated hot chocolate bar, part of a six-figure investment by operator Continuum Attractions.

The refreshed space celebrates the families who helped establish York as Britain’s "chocolate city" and brings the city’s confectionery heritage to life in a modern setting.

As part of the festivities, teams from York’s Chocolate Story will also be in the city centre on Tuesday, October 27, handing out chocolate samples and sharing additional discounts.

Mr Wood said: "We’re looking forward to spreading a little chocolate happiness and welcoming people into our new-look shop and café to discover more about York’s incredible chocolate story."
