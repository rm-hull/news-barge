## Russian authorities committed crimes against humanity in Ukraine, UN report says

Russian authorities have committed crimes against humanity during their war with Ukraine, a new UN report has found.

Published on Monday, the report found Russia had committed the crimes against humanity of intentionally directing attacks against civilian objects and the crimes against humanity of other inhumane acts with continuous attacks on Ukraine’s energy infrastructure.

“The frequency, intensity and geographic range of systematic waves of attacks with hundreds of drones and missiles at a time have increased over the years and inflicted significant damage to Ukraine’s energy sector,” the report said.

“Last winter, when temperatures dropped to minus 21°C, millions of people in Ukraine faced lengthy outages of heating, electricity, water, and gas, and had to cope with intolerable living conditions.”

It added older people and those with reduced mobility have been particularly badly affected as officials expressed concern about the upcoming winter.

“As the cold season approaches, the attacks have intensified once again, raising serious concerns about the threats to civilians’ lives and health,” said Erik Møse, Chair of the Commission.
