A charity is calling for volunteers to join its new hospital café in York.

Royal Voluntary Service (RVS) is seeking 15 people to help establish a volunteer-led café at Foss Park Hospital, offering refreshments and a friendly face to patients, visitors and staff.

The charity believes volunteering can make a meaningful impact, offering comfort during challenging moments in a healthcare setting.

Chris Gray, Director of Retail at Royal Voluntary Service, said: "We’re really pleased to be bringing Royal Voluntary Service’s retail services to York.

"As well as food and refreshments, our cafes offer patients, visitors and staff a friendly welcome and a chance to stop for a drink, something to eat and a chat during what can sometimes be a difficult or stressful time."

No previous experience is needed, and full training and support will be provided.

Zion Ayokunnu, an RVS café volunteer, said: "What’s unique about the Royal Voluntary Service café is the way we serve people.

"We don’t just give people coffees and snacks, we serve them with a smile, and I find that customers, whether they’re staff, patients or visitors, like to stop for a chat."

Anyone interested in volunteering can contact Marie Adlen on 07773 125137 or visit GoVo.org.
