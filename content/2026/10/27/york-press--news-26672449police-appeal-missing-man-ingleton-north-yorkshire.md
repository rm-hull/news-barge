*Update: In a statement issued this afternoon (Tuesday, October 27), North Yorkshire Police said the 72-year-old man has been found.*

A 72-year-old man is missing from Ingleton and police are appealing for information to help locate him.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "He was last seen in Skipton at 2.10pm on Monday, October 26.

"He is believed to have been wearing a black raincoat, a dark quarter-zip jumper, blue trousers, and trainers.

Police believe he may have travelled out of Skipton by public transport into nearby rural areas.

Anyone who has seen the man since 2pm on Monday is urged to contact police and quote reference number 12260210779.

Sightings should be reported by calling 999.

Other information can be shared by calling 101.
