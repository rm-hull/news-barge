Rail passengers in North Yorkshire are facing delays this morning (Tuesday, October 27) following a points failure.

TransPennine Express said its services between Leeds and Selby are running at reduced speeds.

Delays of up to 10 minutes are expected, the train operator said.

The routes affected are between Leeds and Hull in both directions.

TransPennine Express apologised for any delays and said services could be affected until 9am this morning.

The news comes as services between York and Leeds on a key route are cancelled due to a significant programme of works on the East Coast Main Line.
