York’s Chocolate Story is hiding 100 chocolate bars around York this half term, with one lucky finder winning a year’s supply of chocolate.

Chocolate lovers are being invited to join York’s Chocolate Hunt – as York’s Chocolate Story hides 100 chocolate bars around York for the public to discover from Tuesday 27 October.

One lucky finder will win the ultimate chocolate prize: a year’s supply of chocolate, alongside a free Classic Guided Tour of York’s Chocolate Story.

Each of the 100 hidden bars includes a 90g chocolate bar, with more than half also offering the chance to win an additional prize.

These include 25 free hot chocolate vouchers, 25 free Classic Guided Tour vouchers, five teddy bears and the headline prize of a year’s supply of chocolate and a free tour. The year’s supply will be delivered as 12 hampers, with one arriving each month for a whole year.

The bars will be hidden from Tuesday 27 October, with hiding places ranging from some of York’s best-known landmarks to lesser-known spots around the city, giving residents and half term visitors a fun excuse to explore York in search of a sweet reward.

Clues to some of the hiding places will be teased on York’s Chocolate Story’s Instagram account, while other bars will be waiting to be discovered by eagle-eyed explorers.

Anyone lucky enough to find a voucher can redeem their prize in store at York’s Chocolate Story, in King’s Square.

York’s Chocolate Hunt follows the recent unveiling of York’s Chocolate Story’s new-look shop and café, featuring the attraction’s first dedicated hot chocolate bar as part of a six-figure investment by operator Continuum Attractions.

Stuart Wood, general manager at York’s Chocolate Story, said: “We wanted to celebrate our new look by doing something fun that brings a little extra chocolate magic to the streets of York this half term.

“We’re looking forward to spreading a little chocolate happiness and welcoming people into our new-look shop and café to discover more about York’s incredible chocolate story.”
