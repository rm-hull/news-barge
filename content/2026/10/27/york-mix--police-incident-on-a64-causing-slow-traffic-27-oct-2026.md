A police incident on the A64 is causing slow traffic this evening (Tuesday).

It’s on the westbound carriageway close to Hazelwood Castle.

There are reports of a medical emergency on a Coastliner bus, although this hasn’t been confirmed.

#### Incident location

The AA says: “Delays on A64 Westbound between A162 and Paradise Lane. Average speed 15 mph.”
