PLANS to transform a York building into a new shop have taken a step forward.

An application to install two hanging signs on the former Japanese Print Shop, in High Petergate, for The Old York Cake Shop has been lodged with the council.

Plans from Tony Vickers, also of Goodramgate's The Old York Tea Room, stated the conversion had already received a huge amount of support from the public.

It comes after the applicant and Thomas Vickers, also of T&T The Old York Ltd which operates the Goodramgate venue, announced their plans for the cake shop in July.

The pair said on Facebook the High Petergate store would be dedicated to takeaway cakes and would offer everything from brownies, cheesecakes, pastries and other sweets.

They added the unit was perfect for what they wanted to do.

It also comes after council planning officers approved renovations to the outside of the building ahead of the new store moving in.

Plans proposed repainting the Grade II\*-listed building black and featured gold lettering with the shop's name above the front door and window.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21633867.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An impression showing how The Old York Cake Shop could look *(Image: The Old York Cake Shop)*

Listed building consent for the changes was granted on Wednesday, October 21.

The two wooden hanging signs proposed at the front of the building would also be painted black and bear the store's name in gold letters.

Plans stated: "The proposal retains the historic shopfront fabric and established architectural composition.

"Its restrained black-and-gold treatment, traditional painted lettering, non-illuminated signs and minimal reversible fixings are compatible with the historic commercial character of High Petergate."
