A RENOWNED curry house in York is up for another prestigious award.

29 States, in Tower Street, is up for a Best Curry House in York Award 2026.

The award, hosted annually by The Press, highlights the best restaurants in the region.




It comes just months after 29 States’ owner Jaswant Mehra picked up a Best Restaurant award for Yorkshire at the Northern England Curry Awards.

![“I wanted my restaurant to focus on creating authentic north Indian food – always using the best suppliers locally](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21631676.jpg?type=mds-article-620)“I wanted my restaurant to focus on creating authentic north Indian food – always using the best suppliers locally" *(Image: Newsquest)*

Speaking about this, Jaswant said: “We’re extremely proud and grateful.

"It’s wonderful recognition for our entire team and the hard work that goes into the restaurant every day.

"We hope the award introduces 29 States to even more people, while encouraging our existing customers to continue supporting us."

![29 States has won numerous awards since it opened in Tower Street in 2015](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21631674.jpg?type=mds-article-620)29 States has won numerous awards since it opened in Tower Street in 2015 *(Image: Newsquest)*

29 States joins nine others in the York area selected by readers of The Press in the top 10.

Prior to establishing his York restaurant in 2015, Jaswant worked on RASOI SE – India’s longest-running cooking show.

He combined his culinary excellence with experiences at Radisson Blu in Leeds, Browns in Sheffield and The Ivy in York before launching his own curry house.

Jaswant said: “I completed my training at the Institute of Hotel Management in Pusa, India, in 2000, and have been lucky to work with some wonderful businesses over the years.

“In 2008, I followed my dream of working with renowned restaurants and seven years later I was able to open my own – 29 States – The Indian Kitchen, York.

“I wanted my restaurant to focus on creating authentic north Indian food – always using the best suppliers locally.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21631677.jpg?type=mds-article-620)"I just want to say a big thank you to our guests – without their support, none of this would be possible" *(Image: Newsquest)*

Jaswant’s efforts were recognised, when in 2006, he was honoured with special recognition as an instructor in food production principles by the American Hotel and Lodging Educational Institute.

This was followed by further commendation for his signature dishes for hotel brand Radisson Blu.

Jaswant: “Winning this award would be incredibly special.

“We place being shortlisted down to our consistency and genuine passion for good food and hospitality.

“It gives the team a huge boost and hopefully bring many new faces through our doors.

"Finally, I just want to say a big thank you to our guests – without their support, none of this would be possible."
