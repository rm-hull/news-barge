A globally renowned Swiss chocolate manufacturer is to expand its Scottish footprint with a new store opening.

Lindt & Sprüngli is launching its latest luxurious chocolate shop in the heart of Glasgow’s bustling shopping destination, Buchanan Street.

The new boutique store marks Lindt’s 31st UK chocolate shop and complements its existing stores in Livingston and Gretna.

Opening on November 13, Lindt said the new store "will enchant visitors with indulgent offerings and exclusive features", including its Pick & Mix selection - featuring over 30 different chocolate varieties - and its Chocolate Bar experience.

Faythe King, Retail, D2C and eCommerce Director, commented: “We’re thrilled to be opening our newest Lindt Chocolate Shop in Glasgow – our third in Scotland – to bring the exquisite taste of Lindt to the bustling streets of Buchanan Street, a key location for us.

"Visitors can enjoy a premium chocolate paradise with a feast for all the senses and unique offerings, from discovering more than 30 different Pick & Mix flavours and our fresh Chocolate Bar, to personalised gifts for that extra special touch, only available in store."

**Lindt’s new Chocolate Shop in Glasgow will be open from 9:30am on Friday, November 13.**
