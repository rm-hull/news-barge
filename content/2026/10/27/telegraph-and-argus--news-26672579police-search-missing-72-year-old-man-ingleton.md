POLICE are searching for a 72-year-old man from Ingleton who has been missing since yesterday (Monday) afternoon.

Anthony Macauley was last seen in Skipton at 2.10pm yesterday.

He is described as around 5ft 9ins tall, of medium build, with very short grey hair and wears glasses.

He is believed to be wearing a black rain coat, a dark-coloured quarter-zip jumper, blue trousers and trainers.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: “Anthony may have travelled out of Skipton by public transport into the surrounding rural areas.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21634580.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anthony Macauley, wearing what he is believed to have been wearing when he went missing *(Image: NYP)*

“We are appealing to anyone who may have seen Anthony since 2pm on Monday – including public transport users – to get in touch.”

Anthony or anyone who knows where he is is asked to call police straight away on 999.

“If you have any other information that could help us find him, please call us on 101 to speak to our control room,” added the spokesperson.
