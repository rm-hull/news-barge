A Bishop Auckland beauty salon severely damaged when a vehicle crashed into its shop front has reopened in a new space after a “traumatic few months”.

The Brow Lounge has moved to a new salon and clinic at 122 Newgate Street, opposite Street Pasta, after the incident left its original premises badly damaged.

The vehicle reversed into the salon in the early hours of Monday, August 24, causing structural damage, smashing windows and wrecking the shop front.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21362248.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The aftermath of the building after a car reportedly drove into it *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Durham Constabulary launched an investigation into the incident after officers were called at 12.50am.

The salon initially moved temporarily to a space upstairs at Saks in Bishop Auckland while repairs were carried out.

Now, the business has announced it has moved into its new premises, with owner Anna describing it as “the biggest step yet for The Brow Lounge after a traumatic few months”.

In a post on social media, she said: “Now, I can finally say… we have moved!

“Welcome to our brand-new salon and clinic space at 122 Newgate Street (opposite Street Pasta)”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21362247.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Brow Lounge on Newgate Street, Bishop Auckland *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

She added: “This space has been such a dream to bring to life, Louise and I are so excited to welcome you all through the doors! New transformations to training days and everything in between - this is only the beginning. ”

Thanking customers and supporters, Anna said: “Thank you to every single person who has supported me, booked with us, recommended us, shared our work and believed in The Brow Lounge…

“Here’s to our next chapter… welcome to The Brow Lounge.”
