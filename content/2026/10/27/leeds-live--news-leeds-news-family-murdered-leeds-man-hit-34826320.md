# Family of murdered Leeds man hit out at 'cowardly, cruel and senseless act'

## Rahiem 'Reema' Jackson's family have spoken of the 'vicious violence'

The family of a Leeds man have hit out at the "cowardly, cruel and senseless act" which led to his killing.

Rahiem 'Reema' Jackson, 38, died in hospital after he was attacked with “weapons” outside Don Robins House, a supported living facility managed by St George’s Crypt, on Hedley Chase, New Wortley, at 10.23pm on Saturday, October 24. He later died in hospital. It’s suspected a gang of four were involved in the fatal attack and those responsible are still at large.

In a tribute, his family said: “Rahiem was loved and adored by his sisters and family. He had a beautiful heart and beautiful soul; his smile was so infectious, and he lit up the room with his presence. "Rahiem would have gone above and beyond for anybody he cared for."

They added: “A very cowardly, cruel and senseless act of vicious violence has resulted in our brother’s death. He wasn’t just a brother he was a son, an uncle, a father, a partner, a nephew, a cousin, a grandson, brother in law and friend and he did not deserve to die the way he did.

“We are heartbroken and in so much pain and have been left inconsolable as a family. If anyone has any information to help the police find the persons who murdered Rahiem, please can you come forward.”

Flowers and heartfelt messages were left beside Don Robins House addressed to 'Reema'. They said: "Forever in our hearts until we meet again", "Love you always", and "Thinking of you always, I love you. Forever my big bro".
