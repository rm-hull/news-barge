A new aesthetics clinic has opened its doors in a town centre offering advanced skincare treatments.

AW Aesthetics, owned by practitioner Amy Wilson, is based on Cowpasture Road in Ilkley and promises to bring cutting-edge therapies to the area.

The clinic’s bespoke interior is the result of a £100,000 renovation of the former Ilkley Moor pharmacy, which has transformed the once-vacant premises into a modern treatment centre.

Amy Wilson said: "Opening AW Aesthetics has been a dream I've worked towards for many years.

"From treating clients in my home clinic, to renting a treatment room locally, I've always wanted to create a dedicated space where clients can enjoy advanced aesthetic treatments in a premium environment.

"NeoGen Plasma is an incredible technology and I'm delighted to be able to bring it exclusively to Ilkley."

The clinic is the only provider in the Ilkley area to offer NeoGen Plasma, a non-surgical skin rejuvenation treatment that uses plasma energy to trigger the skin’s natural regeneration process.

The treatment has gained popularity among celebrities and skincare enthusiasts for its ability to stimulate collagen and elastin production.

It is said to help reduce fine lines and wrinkles, improve skin firmness, minimise sagging, fade pigmentation, and refine acne scarring.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21638210.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amy Wilson treating client with NeoGen *(Image: submitted)*

Long-term client Nicola Lazenby said: "I have been getting skincare treatments with Amy for the last five years, from her home clinic through to her time at Beauty and Bloom.

"The new AW Aesthetics clinic feels like a luxury spa experience and reflects the exceptional level of care and professionalism Amy provides.

"She has worked incredibly hard to reach this point and I don't think anyone deserves this success more."

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21638262.jpg?type=mds-article-620)AW Aesthetics clinic has now opened in Ilkley *(Image: submitted)*

To celebrate the clinic’s opening, AW Aesthetics will host a VIP launch evening on Tuesday, November 17, where guests can tour the facilities and learn more about the treatments on offer.

The event will include live NeoGen Plasma demonstrations, expert skincare advice from Amy and Skin Specialist Sophie, and insights from a NeoGen specialist.

Guests will also have the chance to ask questions about treatment suitability and results, and benefit from exclusive launch offers.

Amy added: "We're looking forward to welcoming clients old and new into the clinic and showing them what's possible with modern skin rejuvenation treatments.

"Whether someone is looking to address signs of ageing, improve skin quality or simply learn more about aesthetics, the launch event will be a great opportunity to discover what's available."
