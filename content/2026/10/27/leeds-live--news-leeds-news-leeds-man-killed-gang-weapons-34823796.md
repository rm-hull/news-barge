# Leeds man killed by gang with weapons named and pictured for first time

## Tributes have been written in heartfelt messages

A Leeds man killed by a gang with weapons has been named and pictured for the first time.

West Yorkshire Police have launched a murder investigation after a 38-year-old man died in hospital after he was attacked with “weapons” outside Don Robins House, a supported living facility managed by St George’s Crypt, on Hedley Chase, New Wortley, at 10.23pm on Saturday, October 24. He later died in hospital. It’s suspected a gang of four were involved in the fatal attack and those responsible are still at large.

Flowers and heartfelt messages were left beside Don Robins House addressed to 'Reema'. They said: "Forever in our hearts until we meet again", "Love you always", and "Thinking of you always, I love you. Forever my big bro".

A man known by the name 'Robbin Raheim' has been locally named and pictured online. He also appeared to go by the nickname Reemz Donny on Instagram.

Police were combing the field opposite Don Robins House, which had been taped off with a huge cordon, looking for evidence, on Monday, October 26. Residents on the street spoke about the tragedy and how Hedley Chase is a “nice quiet street” usually free of criminality. Others spoke of feeling “intimidated” by gangs with knives and weapons in the surrounding area.

It’s been suggested the victim was chased all the way from Town Street, down the hill, which leads to Hedley Chase, until they reached Don Robins House where the brutal attack was carried out.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates, of the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team said: “A man has tragically lost his life, and we are supporting his family at this tragic time.

“From initial reports, we understand that four people who were armed with weapons, carried out a sustained attack on the victim. We are working extremely hard to identify those individuals. We have significant resources working to get answers for his family and ensure that those responsible face justice for their actions.

“This was a targeted and isolated attack; we are appealing for any information to who may have been involved to come forward and provide details to us.”

A spokesperson for St George’s Crypt said: “On the evening of Saturday, October 24, an incident spilled over into the grounds of Don Robins House, Hedley Chase, Armley, a property owned and managed by St George’s Crypt.

“The incident sadly resulted in someone’s death. The incident was completely unconnected with the residents, staff, or St George’s Crypt. Our thoughts and prayers are with the family and friends of the person who lost their life.”
