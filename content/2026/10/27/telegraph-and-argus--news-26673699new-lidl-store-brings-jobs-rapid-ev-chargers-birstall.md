A new Lidl supermarket is opening next week.

Located on Bankwood Way in Birstall, the shop will open its doors on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at 8:00am.

The opening marks Lidl’s first store in Birstall and follows a careful planning process to ensure it meets the needs of the community.

Liam Schofield, Regional Head of Property for Lidl GB, said: "We are incredibly excited to join the local community and businesses in Birstall.

"This fantastic new store is a direct response to the local demand for high-quality, affordable groceries, and we couldn’t be prouder to welcome the community inside.

"A huge thank you to everyone who helped us turn these plans into a reality – we can’t wait to start making a real difference to your weekly shop."

A ribbon-cutting ceremony will take place on the morning of the opening, with the Mayor of Kirklees, Councillor Karen Allison, officially welcoming Lidl to the area.

The store features rooftop solar panels that will generate up to 25 per cent of its electricity needs, supporting Lidl’s sustainability goals.

It has also created up to 40 new jobs, with an emphasis on hiring people from the local area.

The new shop will be one of the first to be served by Lidl’s £150 million warehouse in Gildersome.

That facility is due to open early next year and is located just over a mile and a half from the Birstall store.

Lidl said the warehouse and store reflect its ongoing investment in Leeds and the wider region.

Drivers will benefit from a new 12-bay ultra-rapid charging hub for electric vehicles, operated by Source, a joint venture between SSE and TotalEnergies.

The chargers offer speeds of up to 320kW, with ten bays designed for vans and light goods vehicles.

Source hubs feature at least 150kW chargers as standard, allowing drivers to ‘charge and go’ in around 15 minutes based on industry accepted charging session data.

The store has a 1,518m² sales area and includes the UK's No.1 Supermarket Bakery and the popular ‘Middle of Lidl’ aisle.

To celebrate the opening, Lidl will offer complimentary food sampling and exclusive deals through the Lidl Plus app.

Store hours will be 8am to 10pm Monday to Saturday, and 10am to 4pm on Sundays.
