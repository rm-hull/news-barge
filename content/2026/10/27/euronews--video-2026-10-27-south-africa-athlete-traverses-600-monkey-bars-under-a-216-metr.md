South African obstacle course racing world champion **Thomas van Tonder** completed a**world-first crossing beneath the Bloukrans Bridge**, navigating** 600 suspended monkey bars**along more than** 280 metres** of its towering arch.

The athlete finished the **Red Bull Bridge Bars challenge** in**47 minutes and 37 seconds**, suspended beneath the** 216-metre-high bridge**. Setting off at 10:21 with a heart rate of 150 beats per minute, he immediately faced the course's steepest section, a** 45-degree ascent** that forced him to pull himself upwards, bar by bar, while facing backwards.

The **gruelling ascent** pushed Van Tonder's grip beyond its limits, while the descent demanded precise control as gravity pulled him between bars and the tape protecting his fingers wore away. Battling exhaustion, he focused on one bar at a time,**completing all 600 without giving up**.

Thomas van Tonder described the crossing as “one of my **biggest dreams and missions for the last two years**” and said: “** I have finished what I started**.”
