## Hugh Bonneville among household names set to appear at North Yorkshire festival

Hugh Bonneville is part of a list of household names set to appear at Whitby Lit Fest this November *(Image: PA Media)*

TV stars, bestselling authors and household names including Hugh Bonneville, Ann Cleeves, Si King and Joanne Harris will descend on a North Yorkshire town next month.

The famous faces will join for this year's Whitby Lit Fest, which will be held from November 19 to 22.

With more than 50 literary events planned across the town, organisers say this festival is not one to miss.




Mark Williamson, a trustee at Whitby Lit Fest, said: "People see the famous faces, the brilliant authors and the exciting events, but what they don't always see is the extraordinary amount of work and goodwill that goes on behind the scenes.

"Our volunteers really are the heart of the festival.

"They help us welcome visitors, support our authors and audiences, and create the wonderful sense of community that we want Whitby Lit Fest to be known for.

"We are enormously grateful to every single person who gives their time.

"Without them, we simply couldn't deliver a festival of this scale and ambition.

"We’re proud that this is a festival built on local enthusiasm, generosity and a genuine love of books and storytelling."

![Si King will also join for the two-day festival](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21635434.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Si King will also join for the two-day festival *(Image: PA Media)*

Beloved actor Hugh Bonneville - best known for portraying Robert Crawley in Downton Abbey - will be introducing his latest children's book, Rory Sparkes and the Spy Who Loved Tea.

Bonneville will take to the stage at the Whitby Pavilion at 1pm on Saturday 21 November for a special family event packed with storytelling, spies, secret codes and "some extremely suspicious cups of tea", organisers said.

Hugh's latest adventure follows unlikely hero Rory Sparkes, who discovers a series of secret coded messages hidden inside a neighbour's recipe book.

Things become stranger still when Rory’s mum, teachers and seemingly every adult around him develop an obsession with a particularly unpleasant new brand of tea. Could Rory finally have uncovered a genuine spy plot?

Illustrated by Loretta Schauer, the book combines mystery, mischief and humour and is aimed at readers aged seven to 11.

Whitby Lit Fest organisers said: "Bonneville will talk about creating Rory’s world, bring the story to life and take questions from young audience members and their families.

"His addition provides another major name in an already packed Whitby Lit Fest programme spanning crime, fiction, history, poetry, politics, food, film, children’s literature and the stories of the Yorkshire coast."

This year's headline guests also include John Boyne, author of The Boy in the Striped Pyjamas, with his new novel The Weight of Angels.

He will join Sunday Times' bestseller Ann Cleeves, Hairy Biker Si King, bestselling crime writers Elly Griffiths and Mark Billingham, and internationally acclaimed actor Greta Scacchi.

![Trustee Mark Williamson with young volunteer Harriet Kirtlan](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21635476.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Trustee Mark Williamson with young volunteer Harriet Kirtlan *(Image: David Kirtlan)*

Behind the scenes, 34 volunteers, trustees, and patrons are giving an estimated 650 hours of their time to support the event—equivalent to more than 80 full working days.

Speaking about this, Mark Williamson said: "Every hour volunteered is an investment in Whitby's cultural future, and we're incredibly thankful to everyone helping us make it happen."
