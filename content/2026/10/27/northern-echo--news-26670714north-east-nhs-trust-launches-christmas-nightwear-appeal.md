A North East NHS trust is asking for nightwear donations to bring festive comfort to patients spending Christmas in hospital.

South Tyneside and Sunderland NHS Foundation Trust has launched its annual #NightwearBeforeChristmas campaign to collect and distribute gifts to patients at South Tyneside District Hospital and Sunderland Royal Hospital.

Claire Boylan, elder life specialist practitioner with the trust's Delirium and Dementia Outreach Team, said: "Every year we're overwhelmed by the generosity of our communities.

"These gifts provide comfort and dignity for patients who may be spending Christmas in hospital and can make a real difference to their day."

The appeal is for new nighties and pyjamas in all sizes, especially larger sizes and men’s nightwear.

Donors should wrap the items, label them clearly with their contents and size, and take them to a designated drop-off point.

At Sunderland Royal Hospital, donations can be left at the Alexandra Centre on E Floor from 8am to 4pm every day.

At South Tyneside District Hospital, items can be dropped off at the Discharge Lounge in the Deans Wing, opposite the Outpatients Department, from 8am to 8pm Monday to Saturday and from 8am to 6pm on Sundays.

The trust also wants adult dressing gowns, full-shoe slippers, toiletries excluding creams and body butters, quiz books, adult colouring books, pens and pencils.

Ms Boylan said: "Something as simple as a new pair of pyjamas, a dressing gown or a quiz book can help someone feel cared for during what can be a difficult time.

The team also runs the Alexandra Centre, where patients can take part in hobbies, music, games and seasonal celebrations.
