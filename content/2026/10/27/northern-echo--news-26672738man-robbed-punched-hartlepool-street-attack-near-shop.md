A man was punched in the face and robbed during a street attack in Hartlepool.

The attack happened at around 4.45pm on Saturday, October 17, at the junction of Elwick Road and York Road, near a furniture store.

The victim was punched in the face and knocked to the ground, suffering bruising to his eye and leg and swelling to his hip.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21635009.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The attack happened on the corner of Elwick Road and York Road in Hartlepool. *(Image: Google)*

He later reported that his mobile phone and £10 in cash had been stolen.

Cleveland Police are now asking anyone with information about the incident to come forward.

The suspect is described as a white man of slim build, aged around 24, with curly blonde hair.

He is believed to be about 6ft 7in tall and was wearing a grey tracksuit.

Temporary Detective Constable Smith, from Hartlepool CID, said: "Extensive enquiries have been carried out including reviewing CCTV and now we’d be keen to hear from anyone who may have seen what happened.

"We’re particularly keen to speak to anyone who saw a grey Audi stop in the area, with a man getting out of the vehicle before assaulting the victim.

"Anyone who witnessed the incident or has any information, CCTV, dashcam or doorbell footage is asked to contact us on 101, quoting reference number 2508505."
