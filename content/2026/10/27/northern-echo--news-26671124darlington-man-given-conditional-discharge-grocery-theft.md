A Darlington man received a conditional discharge after admitting stealing groceries from a shop in the town.

Graham Marsh, 46, of Sanderson Street, Darlington, pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates' Court to stealing groceries worth £30.45.

The court heard that on August 6 he stole food and confectionery from Tesco Express in Darlington.

The items included a chicken tikka masala with rice, butter chicken meals, chocolate cake bars, Viennese Whirls, prawn crackers, custard cream whirls and pizza potato chips.

Marsh entered his guilty plea on October 23.

Magistrates imposed a six-month conditional discharge and ordered him to pay a £26 victim surcharge.

An application was made for benefit deductions, and a collection order was imposed.

Court records show the offence related to one incident involving goods taken from the store without payment on that date alone.
