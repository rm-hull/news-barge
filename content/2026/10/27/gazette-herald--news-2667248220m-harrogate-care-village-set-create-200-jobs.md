A new £20 million care village is set to bring around 200 jobs to Harrogate.

Springfield Healthcare has begun work on the project at the historic Grove House site on Skipton Road.

The development will include a 76-bed care home, eight eco-friendly town houses, and the restoration of Grove House into luxury care suites.

Graeme Lee, founder of Springfield Healthcare, said: "Breaking ground at Grove House marks a hugely significant moment for Springfield Healthcare and for Harrogate.

The scheme aims to create a vibrant community with intergenerational connections, blending modern care facilities with independent living and heritage conservation.

Springfield Healthcare will also build eight sustainable town houses as part of the Grove House scheme.

The two-bedroom properties are designed for independent living and feature low running costs, private gardens, and dedicated parking.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21634584.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tim Jackson, finance director at Springfield Healthcare, Jenny Delic, HR director at Springfield Healthcare, Graeme Lee, Founder at Springfield Healthcare, Paul Phillips, CEO at Springfield Healthcare *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Grove House, originally built in 1745, will be carefully restored and converted into luxury care suites.

The building once belonged to Victorian inventor and philanthropist Samson Fox and later served as a hospital during the First World War.

Most recently, it housed the headquarters of the Royal Antediluvian Order of Buffaloes.

Mr Lee said: "I am incredibly proud that we are able to re-establish Grove House and its spectacular surroundings as a place with a meaningful future at the heart of the community.

"It is a site with a remarkable history, and we have a real opportunity to respect that heritage while creating something truly special for future generations."

The project also represents a major economic boost for Harrogate.

Springfield Healthcare expects to create around 200 jobs across care, hospitality, catering, management, and support roles.

Paul Philips, chief executive of Springfield Healthcare, said: "We want Grove House to be somewhere people are proud to live, families feel confident in the care their loved ones receive, and the wider community can feel connected to.

"This is also a major investment in Harrogate, creating around 200 jobs and bringing new opportunities for people who want to build a career in care, hospitality, and the wider care sector.

"We are excited to see the vision begin to take shape."

The Grove House development is part of Springfield Healthcare’s ongoing commitment to delivering high-quality care in supportive and inspiring environments.

In October 2024, the company sold six of its Yorkshire care villages to American Healthcare REIT, following a 12-year partnership with BGF.

The Harrogate project marks a new chapter for Springfield as it continues to invest in care facilities across the region, the company added.
