Officials say this video shows escaped inmate stealing a truck

**A manhunt is under way in Tennessee after a convicted murderer serving a life sentence escaped from prison.**

Jacob Allen Morley, 28, was found guilty of the 2022 killing of his co-worker and sentenced in 2025, prison records show.

He escaped on Sunday from the Northwest Correctional Complex in Tiptonville, about 90 miles (145 km) north of Memphis, according to the Tennessee Department of Correction (TDOC).

It said security camera footage confirmed Morley had breached the prison's perimeter fence and escaped in his prison uniform.

Investigators have released security camera video they say shows him stealing a nearby farm's work car.

![Wanted poster for Jacob Morley](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/115e/live/bce08b80-d213-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.png)

Morley was convicted of first-degree murder over the killing of his colleague Cody Birchfield in 2022

Morley has been added to the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation's most wanted list, the TDOC said, and a reward of up to $2,500 (£1,900) is being offered for information leading to his arrest.

Prison staff confirmed he was "unaccounted for" on Sunday night and begun an emergency headcount and search within the prison, officials said on Monday.

"The Tennessee Highway Patrol has deployed aviation assets and state troopers to assist with the search," the TDOC said.

Prison records show his supervision level was listed as "low" and he was not eligible for release until December 2083.

On Tuesday afternoon, investigators said the stolen vehicle had been found in Illinois about 120 miles north of Tiptonville, but Morley was not there.

He was sentenced in June 2025 after being convicted of first-degree murder over the death of his colleague Cody Birchfield in 2022. Birchfield's body was found in a metal box under Morley's home, according to Carter County court records reviewed by the BBC.

Birchfield died "as a result of a gunshot wound to the back of the head", a pathology report cited in court said. Bullet casings matched a gun found in Morley's house.

The pair had developed a friendship while Birchfield was training Morley at the pallet manufacturing company they worked at, Birchfield's sister said in her victim impact statement to the court.

Besides murder, Morley was also convicted of vandalism, tampering with evidence, aggravated burglary, and evading arrest. His request for a new trial was denied in May.

This is not the first time Morley has led law enforcement officials on a manhunt. His 2022 arrest came after a police chase that crossed state lines into North Carolina.

Earlier this month, another prisoner Gregory Redden, 69, who is serving a 15-year sentence for aggravated robbery, escaped from custody but was recaptured shortly after.

Convicted murder Christa Pike survived a botched lethal injection in the state in September, leading to the resignation of TDOC Commissioner Frank Strada.
