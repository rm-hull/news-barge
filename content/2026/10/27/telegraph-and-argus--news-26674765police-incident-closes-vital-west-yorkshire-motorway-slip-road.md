A slip road linking the M1 and M62 in West Yorkshire remains closed while emergency services deal with an incident.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it received an emergency call at 1.18pm on Tuesday reporting that a person had fallen from height close to the motorway intersection.

National Highways says the closure is affecting the southbound exit slip road from the M1 at Junction 42 onto the westbound M62 at Junction 29, near Lofthouse.

Ambulance resources were sent to the scene and one patient was taken to hospital.

In an update, traffic officers said the road remains shut while the incident is dealt with by West Yorkshire Police.

Despite the closure, National Highways say there are currently no significant delays on the approach to the affected junction.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 1.18pm on Tuesday afternoon to report a person who had fallen from height close to the motorway intersection at Lofthouse.

"Ambulance resources responded to the incident and one patient has been conveyed to hospital."

West Yorkshire Police have been contacted for comment.
