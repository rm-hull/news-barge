Several residents have objected to plans to convert a methodist chapel in Skinningrove into a café/bar.

The vacant building in Chapel Street is subject to a change of use planning application recently submitted to Redcar and Cleveland Council.

The proposed hours of operation would be between 9am and 9pm with four full-time staff expected to be employed.

The application described how existing brickwork would be cleaned and other structural elements such as timber doors subject to refurbishment and where necessary redecoration.

Comments received by the local planning authority cited concerns about limited parking and narrow roads potentially being blocked by delivery vehicles.

They said it was a quiet residential area with potential noise in the evening from the venue disturbing the sleep of people living nearby.

There was also no need for another food and drink establishment, as the small village was already well-served by existing local independent businesses.

A concern was also raised about the impact on the local sewerage system from the development.

The application site is in a conservation area, which will be another factor for planning officers to consider.

The council previously gave permission for a small hall associated with the chapel, which is directly next door, to be converted into two new homes.
