Hundreds of new homes for North East first-time buyers have won recognition at a national property awards ceremony.

Bellway’s Falcon Grove and Roman Court developments in Lower Callerton, Newcastle, were highly commended in the Best Development in the North category at the First Time Buyer Readers’ Awards in London.

Emma Chesterton, sales director for Bellway North East, said: "It’s immensely rewarding to be recognised by the First Time Buyer Readers’ Awards for the help we are giving to people looking to take their first step on the property ladder in Newcastle.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21630137.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Falcon Court in Lower Callerton, Newcastle *(Image: Bellway)*

"Roman Court and Falcon Grove form our latest phases of development at Lower Callerton, where we are building a neighbourhood of up to 900 new homes, alongside green open space, a convenience store and allotments."

The two developments offer 235 homes for private sale, more than half aimed at first-time buyers.

Homes range from one-bedroom flats to five-bedroom houses.

The North East developments include smart heating, solar panels and electric vehicle charging points.

Shops, nurseries and schools are all within a mile of the developments.

Falcon Grove will have a Sainsbury’s Local, while Roman Court sits beside new allotments.

Both developments have public transport links to Newcastle city centre, six miles away.

Roman Court has 105 two-, three- and four-bedroom houses.

Falcon Grove has 130 homes, including one- and two-bedroom flats and larger houses.

Ms Chesterton said: "Callerton has been designated as a Neighbourhood Growth Area by Newcastle City Council, and our project here is playing an important role in meeting the demand for new housing in the city, including from first-time buyers."

Three- and four-bedroom homes at Roman Court are available from £299,995.

One- and two-bedroom flats at Falcon Grove start at £154,995, while houses start at £234,995.
