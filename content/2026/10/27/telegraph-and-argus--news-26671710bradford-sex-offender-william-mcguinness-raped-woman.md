A convicted sex offender who raped a woman in her own home has been jailed for more than eight years.

William McGuinness was arrested after the victim told another woman what had happened and she reported it to the police.

Following the incident in the Bradford district last year the victim said what happened had "made her feel dirty".

She described McGuinness as "overpowering" and that she was scared of what he might do.

The court heard that McGuinness may have taken a lot of alcohol at the time of the attack.

McGuinness, 68, of no fixed abode, Bradford, denied the offence.

He also denied going into the woman's home.

He was convicted of rape following a five-day trial at Bradford Crown Court.

The court heard he had previous convictions including for indecent assault in 2014 for which he was jailed for years and added to the sex offender register.

Deputy Circuit Judge Timothy Clayson jailed McGuinness for eight-and-a-half years and made him subject to a 12-year restraining order.
