People in Wetherby are being invited to join residents at a weekly community tea room at Wetherby Manor care home from November.

The tea room will run every Thursday from 9.30am to 12pm, with residents, family members and local people welcome to attend.

Visitors can enjoy complimentary tea, coffee and refreshments in the home’s café and lounge areas, with the sessions intended to give people an opportunity to meet others, catch up with friends and spend time with residents.

> **Caroline Bryon, home manager at Wetherby Manor, said:**
>
> We’re delighted to open our doors and invite people from across Wetherby to join us each week. Whether you’re meeting friends, looking to make new connections or simply fancy a cuppa, everyone is welcome.
>
> Being part of our local community is really important to us, and we hope the Tea Room will become a place where residents and local people can come together and get to know one another.

The sessions will also give families, neighbours and local groups an opportunity to find out more about activities available to residents at the home.

The Community Tea Room will take place every Thursday, from 9.30am to 12pm, at Wetherby Manor, starting in November. Tea, coffee and refreshments are provided free of charge.
