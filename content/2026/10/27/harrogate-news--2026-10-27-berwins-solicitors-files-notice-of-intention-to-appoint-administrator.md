Harrogate law firm Berwins Solicitors has filed a notice of intention to appoint an administrator, according to a court record dated 26 October 2026.

The filing is recorded under High Court case number CR-2026-008308 in the name of Berwins Solicitors Limited.

The development comes less than a month after the firm marked its 40th anniversary with a celebration at Cedar Court Harrogate.

Berwins was established in Harrogate in 1986 and has maintained its connection with the town throughout its four decades in business.

The firm provides legal services to individuals, families and businesses. Its work covers a range of areas, including property, family matters and services for private clients and commercial organisations.

Its Harrogate office is at 2 North Park Road. The firm is regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority and is authorised to provide legal services across the range of reserved legal activities.

Companies House records show that Berwins Solicitors Limited was incorporated in April 2009. The company remains listed as active on the register.

A notice of intention to appoint an administrator is a preliminary step in the administration process. It does not, by itself, mean that administrators have been appointed.

The notice itself has not yet been independently obtained, and the circumstances behind the filing have not been established from the available record.

Berwins Solicitors has been approached for comment.
