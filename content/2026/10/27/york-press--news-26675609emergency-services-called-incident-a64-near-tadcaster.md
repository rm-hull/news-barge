EMERGENCY services have been called to an incident on a major road in North Yorkshire.

The incident is taking place on the A64 Westbound, close to Tadcaster, and is causing slow traffic.

It comes after reports of a medical incident on a Coastliner bus.




The Press has reached out to Yorkshire Ambulance Service for this confirmation.

A spokesperson for AA Traffic said: “Delays of five minutes and increasing on the A64 Westbound, between A162 and Paradise Lane.

“Average speed 20mph.”
