Two men arrested at a £1 million cannabis farm in North Yorkshire are today awaiting sentence.

Aldo Gjeta, 27, and Emanuel Bushi, 28, both of no fixed address, appeared at York Crown Court for a plea and case management hearing.

Both men pleaded guilty through an interpreter to producing cannabis.

Their cases were adjourned until November 27 when they will be sentenced.

Both were arrested in September when North Yorkshire Police found a cannabis growing factory in a disused industrial unit on Standard Way Industrial Estate in Northallerton.

Officers found so many plants in the unit they estimated it could produce enough cannabis to sell for £1 million in street deals.

Both men were remanded in custody following their arrest and were further remanded in custody after their guilty pleas.
