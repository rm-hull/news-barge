AN accessibility advocate has taken up a new role on the board of the Yorkshire Dales National Park Authority.

Craig Grimes, a trustee of National Trails UK and founder of the Yorkshire-based organisation Experience Community, was appointed by the Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs.

Mr Grimes said: "The Yorkshire Dales holds a special place in my heart and has played an important role in my own journey of exploring accessible countryside opportunities.

"I'm looking forward to working with members and officers to help ensure that as many people as possible can experience and enjoy the national park, whatever their circumstances or abilities."

As a long-time campaigner for countryside access, Mr Grimes has focused on supporting disabled people to connect with nature through inclusive activities, specialist equipment, and guidance on accessibility.

His appointment has been welcomed by the authority.

Derek Twine, chair of the Yorkshire Dales National Park Authority, said: "We are delighted to welcome Craig to the authority.

"His personal experience, professional expertise and passion for ensuring everyone can enjoy the benefits of our protected landscapes will bring an important perspective to our work."
