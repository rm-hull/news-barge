A plan to relocate a post office has been abandoned after customers raised concerns about access, parking and public transport links.

The proposal would have moved Thornaby Post Office to 29 Allensway, a site previously occupied by Metcalfe Kime Insurance Services.

A spokesperson for Post Office Ltd said: "We listened to feedback raised during consulation re accessibility.

"Some customers felt it would be harder to reach, particularly for customers travelling by public transport and the lack of a safe crossing point."

They confirmed that the branch will continue to operate from its current site for the time being.

Concerns were also raised about reduced parking, disabled access and the absence of a safe pedestrian crossing.

The decision follows a period of public consultation involving customers and local representatives.

Any future plans to relocate the branch would be subject to a new six-week period of public consultation.
