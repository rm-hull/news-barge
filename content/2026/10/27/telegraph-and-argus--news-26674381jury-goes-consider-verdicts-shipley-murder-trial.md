A jury in a murder trial at Bradford Crown Court has retired to consider its verdicts.

Jaroslav “Yanic” Daniszewski, 43, was found dead on April 22 last year in a flat on Stead Street in Shipley following an all-day drinking session.

He had suffered 24 separate injuries to his head and face consistent with blunt force trauma.

The jury heard that it is accepted that Mr Daniszewski was unlawfully killed, and that the weapon used was an iron.

Beata Walczak, 58, of Barkerend Road, Bradford, and Zbigniew Chlebek, 67, of Stead Street, Shipley, both deny murder as well as an alternative charge of manslaughter.
