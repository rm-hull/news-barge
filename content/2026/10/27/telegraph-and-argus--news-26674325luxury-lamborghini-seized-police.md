A Lamborghini was seized by police after its driver allegedly drove through a red light.

Neighbourhood Policing Team officers stopped the sports car whilst on patrol in Oakwood, Leeds, last week.

Further checks showed the Lamborghini was uninsured.

Police seized the vehicle and reported the driver for prosecution.

Police said the incident was a reminder to obey traffic signals and ensure vehicles are insured before driving.

Officers said proactive patrols would continue under the Vision Zero strategy, which aims to eliminate deaths and serious injuries on West Yorkshire’s roads.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "This incident is a reminder that driving legally and safely is not optional.

"Traffic lights are there to protect all road users, and insurance is a legal requirement for anyone behind the wheel.

"Everyone has a responsibility to follow the rules of the road, to help keep communities and other road users safe.

"Officers continue to conduct proactive patrols in line with the Vision Zero Strategy."
