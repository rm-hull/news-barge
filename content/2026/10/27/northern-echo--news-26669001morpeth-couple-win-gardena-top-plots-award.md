A North East Army veteran whose military career was cut short by a traumatic brain injury sustained in Iraq has won a prestigious national gardening award alongside his wife.

Jean and Rebecca Reno, from Morpeth, were named winners of the GARDENA Top of the Plots Award 2026, which was celebrated at Blenheim Palace.

Their success follows years marked by Army service, injury and support from Armed Forces charity SSAFA.

Jean served in the British Army for 18 years, rising to sergeant with 47 Royal Artillery in air defence.

His career ended after an off-duty car crash in Basra left him with a traumatic brain injury.

Years later, SSAFA helped the couple move from Portsmouth to Northumberland and start again.

On December 17, 2018, 13 years after Jean’s injury, they moved to Morpeth with financial help from SSAFA.

Since then, the couple have used their garden to give back.

They said: "We have never forgotten the help SSAFA gave us when we moved.

"Opening our garden and raising money is our way of saying thank you and helping other members of the Armed Forces community who might need support."

In 2025, Jean and Rebecca opened their garden to visitors for the first time through the National Garden Scheme and raised money for SSAFA.

They raised more than £290 for SSAFA in 2026.

Caroline Pryer, chair of SSAFA Northumberland Branch, said: "Jean and Rebecca’s story is a wonderful example of the lasting difference that a little practical support can make to someone’s life.

"SSAFA was there to help them begin a new chapter, and it is incredibly touching that they have chosen to give something back through their beautiful garden.

"We are delighted to hear of their award-winning success and enormously grateful for their continued generosity.
