**Police have appealed for information on a man believed to have entered the sea in a North Ayrshire seaside town.**

Officers received a report of someone in the water near to Burnlea Road, Largs, at 12.12am on Tuesday.

Emergency services attended and a search operation was launched, however, the unidentified man remains missing.

He is described as a white male in his 20s, tall, of slim build with no facial hair, dressed in all black with a black jacket and hood up.

Two men, who are believed to be potential witnesses, were seen in the area around the time the man went into the water.

The first is described as a white male with ginger hair and beard, wearing a black puffa jacket, blue trousers, and white trainers.

The second is described as a white male wearing a black jacket, blue trousers, dark-coloured trainers with a light-coloured heel and a dark-coloured Trapper hat.

Inspector Martin Fraser, of Police Scotland, said: “Extensive inquiries are ongoing to trace the man seen in the water and establish his identity.

“We are appealing for anyone with information which could assist our investigation, including private CCTV or dashcam footage, to please come forward.

“We would particularly urge the two men who were in the area around midnight to please get in touch as they may have seen the unidentified man or have information that could help us.”

Anyone with information can contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 0026 of Tuesday October 27.
