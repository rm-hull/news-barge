A North Yorkshire pub has closed its doors after serving the community for ten years.

The Buck Inn, Richmond, closed its doors for the final time on Saturday (October 24), after bidding an emotional farewell to its punters.

The pub on Newbiggin, owned by Fiona and Paul Hadden, issued a message to customers on its social media profile.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21635299.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Buck Inn, Richmond, *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

The post read: "Well, the day is here. Ten years of hard work is done.

"Tonight we will close the door for the final time. Thank you to all our lovely customers who have supported us through thick and thin, and let’s not forget Covid.

"This Buck family has been our support network; we have welcomed Leo and have been through so much I can’t begin to say.

"Every member of the team has been wonderful. Particularly, seeing younger members grow and thrive, as well as the mature members gain new skills, has been amazing. Thank you doesn’t seem enough."

Customers branded the closure "devastating" and "hard to deal with".

One person said: "Devastated does not even cut it. Such a sad day. We had some fantastic times with you guys and the Buck family and the locals; not quite sure where we will stay on our February trips to Richmond now."

Another added: "Such sad news, Fiona. My brother and I virtually grew up in the Buck, also my kids and my mum and dad before me. All our special occasions, happy and sad, spent 'over the road'. Praying it’s not closed for long."
