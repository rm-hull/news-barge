The oldest pub and inn on the Isle of Skye listed has gone on the market.

The Stein Inn in Waternish is being offered for sale by Inverness-based property firm ASG Commercial, with a guide price of £1,845,000.

Occupying a waterfront position on the shores of Loch Bay and commanding panoramic views across the water to the Outer Hebrides, the Inn dates back to 1790 and is recognised as one of Skye's most iconic hospitality destinations.

The accommodation comprises five sea-facing en-suite letting bedrooms, together with a public bar, restaurant, snug and a variety of comfortable dining and seating areas.

The Stein Inn attracts visitors from across the world as well as a loyal local clientele and has received recognition in numerous travel guides and international food publications, including the Michelin Guide.

![The Stein Inn dated back to 1790](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21637021.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Stein Inn dates back to 1790 *(Image: ASG Commercial, Inverness)*

The award-winning bar, recognised by CAMRA, offers a selection of over 120 Scottish whiskies alongside local craft beers and premium gins, while the celebrated kitchen showcases the best of Skye's natural larder, with seafood landed daily from Loch Bay opposite, including lobster, crab, langoustines and mussels, complemented by locally sourced produce supplied through long-established relationships with local fishermen and producers.

In addition to the main Inn, the property includes a one-bedroom self-catering apartment. Dedicated staff accommodation is also available and currently comprises two bedrooms within the main building, three caravans and a garden pod.

Throughout the year, The Stein Inn hosts a programme of live music, film screenings, pop-up dining events and private celebrations - reinforcing its role as both a thriving hospitality business and an important community hub.

Externally, guests can relax in the waterfront garden seating areas overlooking Loch Bay, where sunsets, wildlife and the Northern Lights can often be enjoyed.

The listing notes: "The Stein Inn represents an exceptionally rare opportunity to acquire one of Scotland's most historic and celebrated hospitality businesses.

"Combining an iconic waterfront setting, worldwide reputation, outstanding food and drink offering, beautifully refurbished accommodation and an enviable trading history, it is undoubtedly one of the Isle of Skye's finest and most recognisable hospitality destinations."

The business is offered for sale due to the owners' decision to relocate to England for family reasons, ASG Commercial said.
