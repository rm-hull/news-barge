An artisan market will close out its outdoor season with a special Halloween event across North Yorkshire.

Little Bird Made will host its final outdoor market of the year at Knaresborough Castle on Saturday, October 31, from 10am to 3pm, followed by a Harrogate event on Sunday, November 1.

The markets will feature handmade crafts, seasonal goods, and street food from independent businesses across the region.

Jackie Crozier, managing director and founder of Little Bird Made, said: "We’ve had an incredible season at Knaresborough Castle, and ending our outdoor run there on Halloween Saturday feels like a wonderful way to end our 2026 trading season.

"Heading to Harrogate Valley Gardens on Sunday gives us a great momentum shift as we welcome November.

"It is a fantastic opportunity for our visitors to get a head start on early festive shopping while directly supporting Yorkshire’s talented independent traders."

Anne Barningham, owner of Barney’s Farmhouse Foods, added: "We always look forward to trading at Little Bird Made events, and this weekend is particularly special as we move between two fantastic North Yorkshire locations.

"It’s a wonderful time of year for local food producers, and we can’t wait to see both our regular customers and new faces stocking up for the season ahead."
