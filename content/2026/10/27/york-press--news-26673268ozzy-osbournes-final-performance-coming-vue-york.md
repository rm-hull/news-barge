A new music documentary capturing Ozzy Osbourne’s emotional final performance is coming to a cinema in York.

Ozzy & Black Sabbath: Back to the Beginning will open at Vue York from October 28, offering fans a cinematic experience of a historic concert held in Birmingham just 17 days before Osbourne’s death.

The film features the Prince of Darkness reunited with Black Sabbath in a powerful tribute to the band’s legacy and lasting influence.




Rachel Bland, senior screen content manager at Vue, said: "Ozzy Osbourne and Black Sabbath changed the face of rock music forever, inspiring generations of musicians and fans around the world.

"Back to the Beginning captures an incredibly special moment in music history, made even more poignant by Ozzy’s passing just 17 days later.

"From unforgettable live performances to intimate behind-the-scenes moments, this is a fitting tribute to a true music legend.

"We’re delighted to give fans the opportunity to experience this historic farewell on the big screen at Vue, with the incredible picture and sound quality bringing them as close as possible to being there on the day."

The concert took place at Villa Park and included performances by some of rock’s biggest names, including Guns N’ Roses and Metallica, alongside personal reflections from artists influenced by Osbourne and Black Sabbath.

The documentary promises an emotional and electrifying farewell, blending on-stage energy with candid backstage moments.

More information can be found at www.myvue.com.
