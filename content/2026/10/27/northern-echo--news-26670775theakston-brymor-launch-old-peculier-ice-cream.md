A North Yorkshire brewery has teamed up with a neighbouring ice cream maker to turn its famous ale into a new dessert.

Masham businesses Theakston Brewery and Brymor Ice Cream have launched Old Peculier Ice Cream, inspired by Theakston’s iconic ale.

The creamy ice cream blends Old Peculier’s rich, malty flavours with an adult twist.

Simon Theakston, chairman of T&R Theakston, said: "As we look ahead to 200 years of brewing in Masham, we're delighted to be partnering with Brymor on this unique Yorkshire collaboration.

"Old Peculier has long been enjoyed beyond the pint glass, bringing its distinctive character to a wide range of recipes.

"Working with a fellow Masham producer to create an ice cream inspired by our most famous beer felt like a natural fit, and we're looking forward to seeing people enjoy it across the region."

Founded in 1827, Theakston Brewery will mark its 200th anniversary in 2027.

The brewery has long encouraged cooks to use Old Peculier, and the ice cream takes the ale beyond the pint glass once again.

Brymor Ice Cream has been making award-winning ice cream by hand in the Yorkshire Dales with rich local milk for more than 40 years.

Heather Wilson, senior manufacturing manager at Brymor Ice Cream, said: "We’re absolutely delighted to be collaborating with Theakston Brewery to create our new Old Peculier Ice Cream.

"As two Yorkshire businesses with a shared commitment to quality, tradition and a strong sense of local identity, the partnership felt like a natural fit."

The ice cream is now being served at Brymor’s Ice Cream Parlour.

Pubs, bars and hospitality venues across the region can also order it, while selected farm shops, delicatessens and independent retailers in Yorkshire, the North East and the North West will stock it.

"Working with the iconic Theakston brewery, based in our local town, gives us the opportunity to bring together these much-loved products in a very distinctive way."
