Residents are being invited to have their say on plans for a major new mixed-use development at a series of public consultation events.

Housebuilder Persimmon, together with its consultant team, is preparing proposals for the long-awaited East of Otley development and is encouraging local people to view the plans and share their feedback before a planning application is submitted to Leeds City Council.

The first phase of the scheme would deliver around 250 new homes on land to the east of the town centre, alongside extensive landscaping, green spaces and public open space.

A new road, to be delivered by Leeds City Council, is also planned as part of the wider project, with the aim of reducing traffic through Otley town centre.

Future phases will include a further 330 homes, land for employment and business use, and a new two-form entry primary school.

The proposals would see a mix of new homes provided, with 35 per cent to be sold at a discount or transferred to a housing provider.

To give residents an opportunity to learn more about the proposals and ask questions, Persimmon is hosting two public consultation events to be held at Otley Common, Walkergate, Otley, LS21 1HB on Friday, November 6, from 3pm to 7pm and Tuesday, November 10, from 2pm to 6pm.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21634126.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A CGI image of the proposed East of Otley development *(Image: submitted)*

A dedicated consultation website is also due to launch, allowing residents to view the plans and submit comments online.

Local councillors Colin Campbell, Ryk Downes and Sandy Lay are urging residents to take part in the consultation process, describing the proposals as one of the most significant developments Otley has seen in decades.

"Proposals for housing and a relief road to the east of the town have been on the books for over 30 years," said Cllr Campbell.

"It looks like they are finally coming to fruition. The proposed consultation will give residents the chance to influence what form the development takes."

"This is the largest development proposal in Otley this century," said Cllr Ryk Downes.

"It will have a significant impact on the community of Otley and we want to make sure it reflects the views of residents and is not just another large bland housing estate.

"This is why it is very important for everyone who can to go along to the consultation, make sure they understand what is proposed and ensure their opinions are recorded."

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21634125.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A CGI image of the proposed East of Otley development *(Image: submitted)*

Cllr Lay highlighted the importance of the proposed road link between Leeds Road and Pool Road.

"The proposals also include a road link between Leeds and Pool Road," he said.

"We regard this road as vital to help remove east west traffic from the town centre.

"We have already raised this and will be seeking an assurance that the road will help ease the current congestion in Otley.

"This is a once in a lifetime opportunity for us to get this right so we need to make sure everyone who comments stresses its importance."
