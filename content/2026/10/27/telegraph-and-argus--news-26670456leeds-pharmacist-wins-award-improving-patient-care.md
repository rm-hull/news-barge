A PHARMACIST has received national recognition for her dedication to mentoring colleagues and improving patient care.

Heather Smith, consultant pharmacist for older people at West Yorkshire Integrated Care Board, has been named this year’s winner of the Barnett Award, presented by the Royal College of Pharmacy (RCPharm).

The award honours individuals who have made outstanding contributions to the pharmacy profession and is named in memory of Professor Nina Barnett.

Ms Smith said: "It is an absolute honour to receive this award.

"Nina was so inspiring and I learnt so much from her, as did so many colleagues.

"Amongst other things, Nina inspired me to complete a formal coaching qualification.

"Coaching, mentoring and educating others, and then seeing them grow and prosper has brought me so much joy in my career."

She established a peer support group for pharmacy professionals in Leeds more than 10 years ago, helping colleagues develop their skills and confidence through shared learning and discussion of complex cases.

Louise Goldring, RCPharm’s director of education, said: "We’re delighted to present this award to Heather.

"She has made a real difference to pharmacists through the support and guidance she gives to others."

Ms Smith will be invited to receive her award in person at the RCPharm Annual Conference in London on 6 November.
