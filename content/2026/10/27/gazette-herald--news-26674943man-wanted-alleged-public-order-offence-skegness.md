POLICE have launched an appeal following an alleged public order offence.

Lincolnshire Police has released an image of a man, whom officers said they would like to speak to after an incident in Roman Bank, Skegness at around 11.40am on Saturday (October 24).

The man may have links to York and Scarborough, police have said.




A spokesperson for the force said: "We would like to speak to the man in the image in connection with an allegation of a public order offence.

"We believe he might be able to assist with our enquiries."

Anyone with information is asked to call 101 and quote reference 26000652698.
