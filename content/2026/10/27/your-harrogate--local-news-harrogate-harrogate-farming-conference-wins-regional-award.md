The Yorkshire Farming Conference has been named Rural or Farming Event of the Year at the Yorkshire Post Rural Awards.

The Yorkshire Agricultural Society won the award for its 2026 conference, with the second event set to take place in Harrogate next January.

The society also celebrated nominations for alumni of its Goodall Pathway programme and awards for suppliers to Fodder – Great Yorkshire Food Hall.

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Agricultural Society said:

> “We are thrilled to have won, particularly against stiff competition from the other nominees!”

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ae05c365bc4ed17436.png)

They added:

> “It was also wonderful to see so many of our Goodall Pathway alumni nominated in different categories, as well as to celebrate with some of our brilliant suppliers at Fodder - Great Yorkshire Food Hall who took home awards.”

Tickets are now on sale for the second Yorkshire Farming Conference, which takes place at the Great Yorkshire Events Centre on Wednesday 27th January 2027.

*Tickets can be purchased through the Yorkshire Farming Conference**website**.*
