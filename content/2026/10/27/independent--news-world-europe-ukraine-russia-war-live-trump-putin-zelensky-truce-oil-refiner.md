The attack came hours before a temporary ceasefire was due to begin in Russian-occupied Oleshky, in southern Kherson Oblast, to allow humanitarian aid deliveries amid severe shortages of food, water and medicine. The White House says the truce was brokered by first lady Melania Trump.

Separately, a new UN report has found that Moscow has committed “crimes against humanity” in its war in Ukraine.

Russian authorities continuously attacked Ukraine’s energy infrastructure during freezing temperatures last winter, which led to “intolerable” living conditions for Ukrainians, the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on Ukraine said.

It claimed Moscow had also intentionally directed attacks at civilian targets and that it had found “compelling evidence” that Russian authorities had trafficked people to fight from 29 countries, which it said is against international law.

## Russia and Ukraine begin localised ceasefire to allow for humanitarian operation in Oleshky

Russia and Ukraine have started ​a localised ceasefire to allow for a humanitarian operation to begin in the Russian-occupied town of Oleshky, both sides said on Tuesday.

Russia's defence ministry said on Telegram it stopped shelling in the direction of the Ukrainian city of Kherson from 4am Moscow time (0100 GMT) to prepare for the humanitarian operation in Oleshky, just across the Dnipro River from Kherson.

Dmytro Lubinets, Ukraine's ​parliament commissioner ⁠for human rights, confirmed in a Facebook post the ceasefire has started at the same time.

“As head of the Ukrainian negotiating team, I confirm that the Ukrainian side is honouring its commitments," he wrote.

Russia and Ukraine have agreed to a localised ceasefire around the Russian-occupied town of Oleshky in southern ‌Ukraine, an arrangement that will enable aid ‌deliveries and allow evacuations from a frontline town suffering acute shortages of food and medicine.

## Russian attack on Kyiv kills one, injures 10, hits educational institution, metro station
