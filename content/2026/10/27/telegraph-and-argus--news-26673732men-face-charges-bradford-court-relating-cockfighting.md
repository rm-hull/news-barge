Nine men from Bradford and the Midlands are to face trial on charges related to the illegal bloodsport of cockfighting.

The private prosecution on behalf of the RSPCA follows an investigation by Northamptonshire Police into an animal fight in May of 2024.

His Honour Judge Christopher Smith set a four-week trial to begin in April 2029.

One defendant, Mukhtar Ahmed, 53, of Brantwood Road, Bradford, admitted three charges of being present at an animal fight.

However, he cannot be sentenced until after the conclusion of the trial. Judge Smith granted him bail.

The other defendants all pleaded not guilty to all charges.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21636181.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Top row, left to right, Mohammed Hussain, Mukhtar Ahmed, Zafar Ali and Khalid Mahmood Khan. Bottom row, left to right, Daniel Burns, Kabir Zaman, Muhammed Ihtishaam and Adnan Khan *(Image: T&A)*

They are:

* Zafar Ali, 60, of Victoria Street, Burton upon Trent: four charges: keeping premises on Victoria Street to be used for an animal fight, being present at an animal fight, keeping animals to be used in an animal fight, and causing unnecessary suffering to a protected animal.
* Muhammad Ifraz Amin, 38, of Hood Lane North, Great Sankey, Warrington: 21 charges including multiple charges of taking part in an animal fight, and causing an animal fight to take place.
* Daniel L Burns, 49, of Kingfisher Grove, Bradford: five charges, including keeping premises on Kingfisher Grove to be used for an animal fight and keeping animals to be used in an animal fight.
* Mohammed Hussain, 49, of Royal Gardens, Bradford: 17 charges including keeping premises at Royal Gardens to be used in an animal fight, causing an animal fight to take place at Royal Gardens and at Kingfisher Grove, being present at an animal fight, taking part in an animal fight, and keeping/training animals to be used in an animal fight.
* Sadir Hussain, 45, of Empress Road, Accrington: two charges, including the possession of cockfighting spurs.
* Muhammed Ihtishaam, 22, of Leylands Lane, Bradford: three charges including taking part in an animal fight and causing an animal fight to take place.
* Adnan Khan, 40, of Leylands Lane, Bradford: three charges including attempting to make an animal fight take place.
* Khalid Mahmood Khan, 47, of Park View Road, Bradford: one charge of being present at an animal fight.
* Kabir M Zaman, 36, of Girlington Road, Bradford: nine charges including providing information about an animal fight to another with the intention of enabling or encouraging attendance, and being present at animal fights.

Judge Smith adjourned the case for a half-day further case management hearing to be held on September 10, 2027.

The trial, assessed to last four weeks, will begin on April 2, 2029.
