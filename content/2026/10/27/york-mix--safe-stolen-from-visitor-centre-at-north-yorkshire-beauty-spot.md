Thieves have targeted a visitor centre at a North Yorkshire beauty spot.

The burglary happened at Sutton Bank Visitors Centre, between Thirsk and Helmsley, some time between 7pm on Sunday 25 October and 8.30am on Monday 26 October.

Two suspects stole a cash safe from the premises.

#### Sutton Bank Visitors Centre

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are particularly interested in speaking to the men in the picture as we believe they may have information that could aid our investigation.  
“If you recognise the men pictured or were in the area on Sunday evening or during the overnight period and may have seen anything suspicious, including vehicles or people in the area, please get in touch.”

The visitor centre is run by North York Moors National Park. Sutton Bank offers a view which the vet and author James Herriot described as the “finest in England”.
