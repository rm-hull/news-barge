A North East man was given eight penalty points after failing to stop following a crash in County Durham.

Mark Holmwood, of Denton View, Blaydon, Gateshead, appeared before Peterlee Magistrates’ Court on October 23.

The 40-year-old admitted three motoring offences arising from an incident on February 6 on the B6314, New Road, Burnopfield.

The court heard Holmwood used an Audi, registration, without third-party insurance.

He also admitted failing to report an accident involving a Volkswagen within the required time and failing to stop after the collision.

For the offence of failing to stop, magistrates fined him £120, ordered him to pay £350 compensation, £85 prosecution costs and a £48 surcharge.

His driving record was endorsed with eight penalty points.

The other offences were taken into account, with no separate penalty imposed.
