A new head of engineering has been appointed to support commercial growth at a subsea technology firm.

Verlume, which specialises in intelligent subsea power, communications, and control, has named Jay Parle to the role as it continues to expand in the offshore energy sector.

Parle brings more than 20 years of engineering experience across subsea controls, offshore renewables, carbon capture and storage, and nuclear energy.

Robert Heron, operations and engineering director at Verlume, said: "Jay brings an exceptional breadth of engineering experience and a strong track record of delivering innovative technologies.

"He understands what it takes to provide robust, integrated engineering solutions that perform in challenging offshore environments while meeting the operational and technical requirements of customers.

"His experience will be invaluable as we continue to significantly grow our engineering capability and deliver increasingly integrated subsea power solutions across our global markets and customer base."

Throughout his career, Parle has led multidisciplinary engineering teams, developed complex engineering systems from concept through to commissioning, and delivered offshore projects in demanding operating environments.

His expertise covers systems engineering, technology development, and engineering management, along with commercial experience in business development.

At Verlume, he will oversee the continued delivery and development of the company’s subsea power platform and intelligent systems integration globally.

Parle said: "I’m excited to be joining Verlume.

"The company has established itself as a leader in intelligent subsea power, communications and control, with field-proven technology being deployed across multiple offshore sectors.

"I am looking forward to working with the team to build on that momentum and support the successful delivery of commercial projects that provide customers with reliable, integrated solutions for demanding subsea operations."
