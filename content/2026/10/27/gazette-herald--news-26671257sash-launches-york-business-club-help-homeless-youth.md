A new Business Club has been launched by a York charity to help tackle youth homelessness.

Safe and Sound Homes (SASH), which supports young people aged 16 to 25 who are homeless or at risk of homelessness, launched the club to boost support from businesses across York and surrounding areas.

The first event took place on September 29 and was sponsored by City Cruises.

Attendees took part in a River Ouse scavenger hunt using the company’s little blue boats, combining networking with friendly competition.

A spokesperson for SASH said: "We’re delighted to have launched our Business Club and are excited about the opportunities it could create for SASH and the local business community.

"We want the club to be a friendly, informal way for businesses to get to know us, meet other local organisations and discover how they can make a difference to young people in York."

The SASH Business Club offers a space for businesses to network, share ideas, and explore ways to support young people through fundraising, volunteering, or offering professional skills.

Membership costs £350 per year and is open to businesses of all sizes.

The Business Club is said to "help SASH strengthen relationships with local organisations, raise awareness of youth homelessness and develop new opportunities for businesses to support the charity’s work".

The club also aims to help raise awareness of youth homelessness in the region and encourage businesses to take a more active role in supporting young people.

The next Business Club event is scheduled for January 2027.

Businesses can join the club, attend events, and find out more about SASH’s work by visiting the charity’s website at www.sash-uk.org.uk/corporate or emailing [fundraising@sash-uk.org.uk.](mailto:fundraising@sash-uk.org.uk.)

SASH provides safe accommodation and ongoing support to help young people across York achieve stable, independent futures.
