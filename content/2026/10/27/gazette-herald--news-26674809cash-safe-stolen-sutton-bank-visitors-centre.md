Police are appealing for information about two men following an alleged burglary at a North Yorkshire visitor centre.

The incident took place at Sutton Bank Visitors Centre between 7pm on Sunday, October 25 and 8.30am on Monday, October 26, when a cash safe was stolen from the premises, North Yorkshire Police has said.

The force believe the men pictured in a CCTV image may have information that could assist their investigation.




A police spokesperson said: "We are particularly interested in speaking to the men in the picture as we believe they may have information that could aid our investigation.

"If you recognise the men pictured or were in the area on Sunday evening or during the overnight period and may have seen anything suspicious, including vehicles or people in the area, please get in touch."

Anyone with information can contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing Nate Mills at [nate.mills@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nate.mills@northyorkshire.police.uk), or by visiting the force’s website and using the ‘Tell us about’ section to report information about an existing case.

A live chat service is also available on the website, Monday to Friday, from 9am to 5pm.

Those unable to use online options can call 101, quoting reference 12260210530.
