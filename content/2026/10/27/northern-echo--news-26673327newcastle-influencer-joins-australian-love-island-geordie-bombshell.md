From Newcastle to a villa in Fiji, a North East-born influencer is taking her shot at reality TV fame as one of Love Island Australia's newest bombshells.

Ava Toulson, a 24-year-old traffic controller from South Shields who relocated to Perth, Australia, entered the villa on day one of the series while proudly announcing herself as a Geordie.

Ava has built a sizeable social media following with travel and fashion content, amassing over 60,000 followers across her social media pages.

Love Island Australia premiered on Monday, October 26 on Australia's Channel 9 at 8:30pm Australian Eastern Daylight Time (9:30am GMT).

Fourteen contestants are filming at the Love Island USA villa in Fiji this year for the eighth series of the Australian version, which began in 2018.

There is no official way to watch series 8 of Love Island Australia in the UK, but the previous series was released on ITVX after broadcast, so British viewers are crossing their fingers that series 8 will follow.

'Geordie bombshell' Ava moved from Newcastle to Perth before joining the cast. In promotional material, she has been described as swapping "gloomy Newcastle" for some "sweet Perth sunshine."

Ava seems to be keen on stirring up trouble as a bombshell, and said: "I always get what I want," while introducing herself.

"I love Aussie boys," she said, adding: "coming into the villa will be like a little buffet, I can have a bit of everything."

"If I was a girl, I'd definitely be worried."

Love Island Australia is hosted by Sophie Monk, and is not filmed live unlike other versions of the programme.

Contestants will compete for a $50,000 AUD prize and the Love Island trophy.

Ava shared she was looking for a man who can make her laugh, match her energy and remain loyal while in the villa, and shared a story of slashing an ex-partner's tyres after catching him cheating.
