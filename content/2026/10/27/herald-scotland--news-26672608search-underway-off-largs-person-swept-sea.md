A major emergency response has been suspended off the coast of Largs with a person believed to have been swept out to sea.

RNLI lifeboats from Largs and Troon, alongside Coastguard Rescue Teams from Largs and Ardrossan, had been searching the water since just after midnight on Tuesday.

A Coastguard plane and helicopter based at Prestwick were also spotted flying over the area during the night.

However, the search was suspended at around 9am on Tuesday morning pending any new information.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "Around 12.12am on Tuesday, October 27, police received a report of concern for an unidentified person in the water near Burnlea Road, Largs.

"A search and rescue operation was launched, but the person remains missing."

A HM Coastguard spokesperson said: “At around 12.20am on October 27, HM Coastguard received a report of a person believed to have been swept into the sea by large waves.

“An HM Coastguard helicopter and an aeroplane have been sent as well as Coastguard Rescue Teams from Largs and Ardrossan, and RNLI lifeboats from Largs and Troon.

“Police Scotland and the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service have also attended."
