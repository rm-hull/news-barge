If you have a sweet tooth, there’s a new place to visit.

The Old York Cake Shop opened on High Petergate in York yesterday (Sunday).

Found in the former Japanese Print Shop, the interior has been transformed by Tony and Thomas Vickers, who also run the popular Old York Tea Room on Goodramgate.

The shop is dedicated to takeaway cakes and offers everything from brownies, cheesecakes, pastries and other sweets.

Already it is garnering positive reviews. One person said on Facebook: “Wow – huge congratulations on your opening success.

“It all looks absolutely amazing.”

An early customer wrote: “It was so lovely to come in and see you all today after following your progress on social media.

“You guys are fabulous!! And we loved everything we bought.”

It comes as Tony and Thomas have applied to City of York Council for permission to add hanging signs to the outside of The Old York Cake Shop.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/the-old-york-cake-shop-high-petergate-fb-4.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/the-old-york-cake-shop-high-petergate-fb-2.webp)

Council planning officers approved renovations to the exterior ahead of the new store moving into the Grade II\*-listed building.

That success comes in contrast to the controversy when the Old York Tea Room was forced to remove its signage from the front of the café after a complaint was made to City of York Council in 2023.
