A North Yorkshire city business is seeking permission to sell alcohol from its premises.

Shop Sustain Ltd, at Halls of Ripon on Fishergate, has applied for a premises licence to sell alcohol at set times.

The proposed hours are 9.30am to 5pm from Monday to Thursday and 9am to 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

The application was published on Thursday, October 22, and is available to view at North Yorkshire Council’s Civic Centre in Harrogate.

Residents have until Thursday, November 5, to comment on the application.

Send comments by email to [licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk](mailto:licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk) or by post to North Yorkshire Council’s Licensing Team, PO Box 787, HG1 9RW.

Residents must follow the notice’s instructions for their comments to be considered.

The business is at 20 Fishergate.
