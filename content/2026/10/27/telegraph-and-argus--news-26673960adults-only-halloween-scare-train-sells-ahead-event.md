A HERITAGE railway’s adults-only Halloween experience has sold out ahead of the big night.

Keighley and Worth Valley Railway’s The Scare Train will run on Saturday, October 31, promising an immersive night of frights through the Worth Valley.

The event is for ages 18 and over and has attracted strong interest online, with tickets now fully booked.

One social media user said: "This sounds fun," while another wrote: "We need to do this."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21549984.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A previous Scare Train event *(Image: Tom Marshall)*

The experience will begin at Oxenhope Station, with departures at 5.25pm and 6.50pm.

Passengers will board the train for a journey into the night, where live performers will lurk in carriages, at stations and along the tracks, turning the railway into a moving stage of horror.

Carriages, stations and even shadows alongside the line will form part of the immersive attraction.

The entire route will be transformed with the intention of keeping guests on edge.

Tickets were priced at £35 per person and included a Day Rover ticket, allowing visitors to explore the railway before the evening’s main event.
