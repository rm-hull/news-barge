## Drivers warned of long queues in major road near York

Motorists have been warned of lengthy delays along York Road, close to Pocklington *(Image: Contributed)*

DRIVERS have been warned of heavy traffic along a major road close to York.

Motorists have been alerted to queues along the A1079 York Road, close to the B1247 The Balk, which leads to Pocklington.




A spokesperson for AA Traffic said: “Queueing traffic in the construction area.

“Queueing traffic on the A1079 both ways at the B1247 The Balk.”
