Two teenage girls who ambushed a man and a woman at a chip shop face jail.

The 17-year-olds, who cannot be named for legal reasons, pounced on Chloe Rees and Blair McFarlane at Blue Lagoon on Glasgow's Gordon Street on December 16, 2025.

Glasgow Sheriff Court was told that Rees and the first teenager initially had a minor verbal argument.

Prosecutor Lauren Aitchison said: "The first girl repeatedly punched Miss Rees to the head and body and dragged her to the ground.

"While on the ground, the first girl continued to punch Miss Rees to the head and body while others kicked her to the head."

Miss Rees and Mr McFarlane exited the chip shop when the first girl set upon her again by dragging her to the ground.

Miss Aitchison said: "The first girl stamped on her head while others kicked her to the head."

Mr McFarlane kicked one of the people assaulting Miss Rees.

He was then pulled to the ground by another man which caused his head to strike the floor.

Miss Aitchison said: "Mr McFarlane was subjected to repeated kicks and stamps to his head and body by other people including the second girl.

"The second girl repeatedly kicked Mr McFarlane on the body as others kicked and stamped on his head and body."

The attack was seen by off-duty police officers.

The two teenage girls made off from the scene and a warrant for their arrest was granted.

Miss Rees was later treated for a concussion as well as bruising to her right eye. Mr McFarlane did not receive treatment.

The first teenage girl pleaded guilty today to assaulting Miss Rees to her injury and danger of life.

The second girl admitted assaulting Mr McFarlane to his danger of life.

Sentence was deferred pending background reports until next month by Sheriff Owen Mullan who continued the girls' bail meantime.

The sheriff said: "You understand custody will be at the forefront of my mind."
