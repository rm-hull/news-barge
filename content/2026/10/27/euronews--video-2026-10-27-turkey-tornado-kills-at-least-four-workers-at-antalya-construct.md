**Four construction workers were killed** and a fifth injured when a**sudden tornado** struck a building site in**Antalya, southern Turkey**, late on Monday. The violent winds hurled** two accommodation containers**into the air and** brought down a tower crane**.

**Two workers died at the scene and two others in hospital**, according to the governor's office. The** falling crane caused no casualties**.

Governor Hulusi Şahin described the tornado, which struck the **Kepez district**, as**"an exceptionally localised phenomenon"**. No similar weather was reported elsewhere in the city.

The same weather system brought **torrential downpours to Istanbul**, flooding streets in** Pendik and Tuzla**. Passers-by rescued a father and daughter trapped in their car at the submerged Kaynarca underpass, while the** D-100 highway was closed**.

The flooding came just 12 days after **record rainfall around Istanbul on 14 October killed eight people**.
