York police are looking for this man.

They have issued the image as part of an investigation into a bike theft.

It happened on Huntington Road, York, at at 9.15am on Wednesday, 14 October.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A man used brute force to remove a child’s pedal bike from a cycle rack.”
