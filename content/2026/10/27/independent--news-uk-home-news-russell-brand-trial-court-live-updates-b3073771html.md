Russell Brand asked his assistant to get the phone numbers of his female fans for him, she told the court as she gave evidence on Monday.

Helen Berger said the request made her feel “really weird” and she later told the comedian she would not do it again. She told jurors she felt Brand’s bad behaviour was “normalised” and that he was sleeping with women “day and night”.

During her time working for the star between June 2006 and February 2007 she told the court he was always in his underwear when she went to his flat, and recalled his “insane sexual appetite”.

She told Southwark Crown Court she had never witnessed Brand doing anything against the law, but added: “Though I had worked with celebrities before and seen a lot of people normalising bad behaviour, this was on another level.

“I think everyone else normalising and dismissing Russell’s behaviour made me think, I guess, this is just the way he is.”

Brand denies three charges of rape, three counts of sexual assault, and one allegation of indecent assault in alleged attacks against six women between 1999 and 2009. The trial continues.

## Brand’s bad behaviour was ‘normalised’, trial hears

Russell Brand’s bad behaviour was “normalised” and he was sleeping with women “day and night”, his former personal assistant told the court on Monday.

Helen Berger, who worked for the comedian between June 2006 and February 2007, described the role as “one of the hardest jobs I did” and recalled he was always in his underwear when she went to his flat.

“When I went to Russell’s flat he was always in his underwear as he loved walking around in his underwear with no shirt on,” she said in a statement read to Southwark Crown Court by prosecutor Riel Karmy-Jones KC.

## RECAP: Brand 'often met women online using MySpace'

Helen Berger, who worked for the comedian between June 2006 and February 2007 told the court on Monday that women would arrive at his flat during the day and “it was clear they were there for sex”.

“He often met women online and I think he often used MySpace for this,” she said. “He was like a peacock and women always approached him.”

On one occasion she was told to take his mother and girlfriend to the green room during a show because he was having sex in his dressing room, the court heard.

During an early-morning visit to his north London flat she saw a female celebrity asleep in Brand’s bed, Ms Berger added.

## RECAP: 'I saw Russell treat women poorly'

Ms Berger told the court on Monday that during one show, Brand closed a performance by telling female audience members where he would be drinking afterwards.

“Russell would end his show in Edinburgh, ‘If you have a vagina and a heartbeat, I will be in this pub afterwards,’” she said. “And many, many women would show up.”

On another occasion, while filming for MTV, the star asked her to get phone numbers from female fans, which made her feel “really weird” and she later told him she would not do it again, Ms Berger added.

“I did see Russell treat women poorly,” she said. Recalling one moment in which he realised a woman he was speaking to at an afterparty was in a relationship, she added: “He just switched off his charm and moved on to the next woman.”

## Jury will hear evidence later this afternoon

The jury trying the case of comedian and actor Russell Brand will now hear evidence at 2pm on Tuesday.
