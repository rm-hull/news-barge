North Yorkshire Police have issued an update following an appeal for information about a missing person.




The force issued a statement after a 72-year-old man was reported missing from Ingleton yesterday evening (Monday, October 26).

He was last seen in Skipton at 2.10pm on the same day and police said he may have travelled out of the area on public transport.

In a statement issued this afternoon, North Yorkshire Police said the man had been found.
