Marti Pellow has announced a new UK tour to mark 40 years in music – and he’s coming to York.

*No Marti No Party* will bring together the hits from across his career, from the much-loved songs of Wet Wet Wet to his solo favourites.

The tour will see Marti celebrate this milestone with the fans who have been part of his story, and fans can expect a night of non-stop hits, dancing and a party atmosphere.

*Marti Pellow – No Marti No Parti Tour* is coming to York Barbican on**Monday 13 September 2027**.

Tickets will go on sale at 10am on Friday 30 October via the York Barbican website.

When asked what fans can expect, Marti simply replied: “The clue is in the title of the tour, it’s what the fans always sing back to me when the lights go down.”
