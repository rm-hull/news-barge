A popular Darlington restaurant run by two brothers is hoping for a hat-trick in The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Babul’s, on Skinnergate, has once again made the top ten shortlist for the competition, having taken home the title in 2024 and 2025.

The south Asian firm, which opened in the town in 2022 and also has a venue in Barnard Castle, is run by sibling duo Shuhel and Zak Ahmed.

The brothers took the reins of their father Rafique Uddin Babul’s business, Spice Island, which had been in Barnard Castle since 1995, and decided to rebrand.

Since then, Babul’s has gone from strength to strength, now boasting a 4.7 star rating on Google Reviews for its “out of this world” curries and “perfect” atmosphere.

Zak was thrilled to hear Babul’s was back in the running for another year: “Where do I start?

“For the last two competitions we have won, and we are going for a hat-trick, without getting ahead of ourselves!”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633262.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Babul's in Darlington are hoping to scoop a third win in the Echo's Best Curry House competition *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

“It would be great to complete the hat trick, but, again, there are so many Indian restaurants and other curry houses in the area and they have all been running for a long time.

“That suggests they are on the top of their game and just shows that the Darlington curry scene is strong.”

Zak said it was a “great feeling” to be nominated: “It is recognition and it has been the readers. It is hundreds and thousands of readers that are putting their opinion across.”

But, despite the success, Zak admitted that it was "tough times" for the hospitality industry at the moment, saying costs are “through the roof”.

“It is just trying to find ways to be more efficient,” he said. “It is a rough time and let’s just see what the government does to make it easier.

“I just don’t think [the industry] is respected and looked after.”

Babul’s is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Arvelli Indian Restaurant, Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.
