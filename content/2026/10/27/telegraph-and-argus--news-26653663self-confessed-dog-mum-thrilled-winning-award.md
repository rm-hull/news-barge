A talented dog groomer is delighted to have been recognised for her creative scissor work.

Bea Harrison, 31, of East Bierley Dog Groomers, won the Future Fusion Star award at the High Achievement Awards for her Asian Fusion grooming.

She described the accolade as “a really special moment” and a testament to the long hours she has worked to get where she is.

## What does Asian Fusion mean?

Asian Fusion, also known as Asian Freestyle, is a creative dog-grooming style from Japan, South Korea, China and Taiwan.

Instead of following strict breed standards, it focuses on making the dog look like a cartoon character, anime figure, or fluffy stuffed toy.

![Oscar the Cavapoo](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21607296.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Oscar the Cavapoo

Miss Harrison said: "Asian Fusion is all about fluffy, teddy-bear-style cuts, with legs left longer than the body and cute, rounded faces.

"Those proportions create a distinctive look, which can be tailored to suit each dog."

## How did she get into grooming?

Huddersfield-born Miss Harrison started at Pets at Home before earning a Level 3 qualification in dog grooming and salon management.

She later became a grooming tutor, helping others develop their skills in the profession.

But she admits it has not been easy.

![Pickles the Miniature Poodle](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21607297.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pickles the Miniature Poodle

A self-confessed dog mum, she has two “children” — a golden retriever and a miniature poodle — and told the T&A that building her business while learning on the job took great dedication.

Miss Harrison said: "Grooming is much harder than I first expected.

"People sometimes imagine it’s playing with dogs all day, but there’s much more to it.

"You’re assessing behaviour, understanding each dog’s needs and helping them feel confident, comfortable and welcome."

![Niko the Miniature Poodle](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21607298.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Niko the Miniature Poodle

## Her salon celebrates its two-year anniversary next month

Miss Harrison opened her salon, which specialises in poodles, cockapoos and other curly-coated breeds, in November 2024.

Above all else, she is passionate about giving every dog exceptional care.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21608843.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

She said: "I love combining dog care with creativity.

"Every dog has a different personality, and building their trust is just as important to me as the finished haircut.

"It’s incredibly rewarding to help a dog feel more comfortable with grooming and create a style that suits them."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21608845.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You need a steady hand for this job *(Image: T&A)*

## Dreams of opening her own school

Miss Harrison hopes to open a training school for dog groomers.

She said: "As a former grooming tutor, I know how rewarding it is to help someone grow in confidence.

"I’d love to teach them how to create beautiful grooms while making sure every dog feels safe, comfortable and welcome."
