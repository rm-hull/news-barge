In 2022, the Berlin-based writer CJ Hopkins compared the then-current COVID-19 policies to National Socialism. The posts on Twitter (now X) featured a medical face mask emblazoned with a swastika. The image was taken from the cover of his book *The Rise of the New Normal Reich*. Hopkins stated that he intended the post to warn against authoritarian developments during the COVID-19 pandemic. He has now been fined €4,800 by the Tiergarten District Court for these two social media posts.

In January 2024, the Tiergarten District Court initially acquitted Hopkins. The judge at the time saw a clear rejection of National Socialism in the publications. The public prosecutor’s office appealed. In September 2024, the Berlin Court of Appeal overturned the acquittal and found Hopkins guilty of using symbols of unconstitutional organisations in two cases. Therefore, Monday’s hearing focused solely on the sentence.

In determining the sentence, the court took into account in the author’s favour that he had no prior convictions and that the publications were more than four years old. Against him, the fact that the articles were publicly accessible and aimed at a large number of people was considered. Hopkins himself used his closing statement for a scathing critique of the German justice system. He wanted to apologise to the court, the prosecution, and the authorities who, he declared sarcastically, had taught him over the past three years “how things work in Germany.”

When he published the posts in 2022, he didn’t expect to face criminal prosecution. “I assumed that Germany was a democratic state governed by the rule of law,” Hopkins said. He was particularly critical of a house search in connection with another criminal case concerning his book. “I’m just an author, not a terrorist or a criminal.”

Hopkins concluded with unmistakable irony: “I will no longer dare to criticise the German authorities—no matter how totalitarian their behaviour, how blatantly they violate the constitution, or what lies and propaganda they spread.” In addition to the proceedings concerning the two tweets, Hopkins is facing another criminal case related to the distribution of his book.
