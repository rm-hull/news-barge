The ex of a murder-accused man has denied that anybody celebrated when the victim was driven at in an alleged deliberate attack.

Alexis Prest said everyone was shocked when the 23-year-old was fatally injured after being pinned against the garage shop front.

The teenager told Teesside Crown Court that Ashton Anderson drove straight at Mr Ambler minutes after the pair had fought in the street.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20435374.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Daniel Ambler

Ashton Anderson, his father Barry and brother Logan, along with his friends Ajay Henry and Tyler Leadbetter, have been charged with murdering Mr Ambler in January.

A bitter feud between the Anderson and Ambler family erupted last August when Barry Anderson was stabbed, the trial heard.

The five co-accused allegedly attacked Mr Ambler and his friend Reece Wilkin before Mr Ambler was killed when he was hit by a car driven by Ashton Anderson.

Defence barrister Christopher Tehrani KC, representing Ajay Henry, cross-examined Miss Prest about the Nissan X-Trail striking Mr Ambler at Esso New Seaham Service Station.

Mr Tehrani asked whether she heard rear-seat passenger Henry shout at Anderson to stop.

Miss Prest said she was 'frantic' for him to stop, but she had no recollection of anyone shouting anything at the time.

When asked whether anyone in the Nissan was encouraging Anderson to drive onto the garage forecourt, she replied: "No."

Mr Tehrani asked whether anyone in the car celebrated after the Nissan struck Mr Ambler. She replied: "No, they weren't."

Miss Prest told the jury she was last out because the door handle on her side did not work. She accepted that she never looked back as she left.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Esso New Seaham Service Station, Seaham

The teenager said she was 'horrified' by what happened and accepted that everyone else appeared to be in shock.

Jurors saw traces of Mr Ambler's blood in photographs of the damaged shop front and Nissan X-Trail.

Esso worker Andrew Nabbs told the jury he saw the Nissan drive straight at Mr Ambler as he crossed the forecourt.

He said Mr Ambler was pinned between the car and shelves outside the garage.

Mr Nabbs described Mr Ambler as ‘bent like a banana’ and showing no sign of life.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20431836.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A forensic officer working at the scene on Saturday (January 17).

The jury heard the bitter feud between the families began when Barry Anderson was stabbed in August last year.

Mr Ambler’s brother David and his friend Jed Wilson fought Barry Anderson, 52, who was stabbed during the struggle.

Barry Anderson, 52, of Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland; Ashton Anderson, 20, of Ryton Crescent, Seaham; and Barry Logan Anderson, 19, of Nursery View, Shotton Bank, face charges of murder and violent disorder.

Ajay Henry, of Melrose Crescent, Seaham, and Tyler Leadbetter, of Elgin Avenue, Seaham, were both 17 when charged with Daniel Ambler’s murder.

The trial, at Teesside Crown Court, is expected to last up to five weeks.

Ashton Anderson has pleaded guilty to violent disorder, while the jury is expected to decide whether the other four defendants are guilty of that charge.
