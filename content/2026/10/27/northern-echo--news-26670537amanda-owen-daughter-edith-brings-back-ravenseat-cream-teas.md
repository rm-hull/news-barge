A popular tradition at Amanda Owen's famous Yorkshire Dales farm is making a return, with her daughter Edith helping to bring back the cream teas that once attracted visitors from across the country.

Alongside the family's sheep farming activities, visitors were once able to stop at the farm for refreshments, with homemade scones and cream teas becoming a popular attraction.

Now, the family's latest television series, Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, follows preparations to bring the tradition back.

The sisters are preparing for an influx of visitors exploring the Yorkshire Dales.

The return of the cream teas marks another development for the Owen children, who have grown up in front of television audiences.

Edith, who viewers will remember as one of the younger members of the family in the early series of Our Yorkshire Farm, is now taking on more responsibility.

Amanda previously confirmed that the cream teas had returned, with Edith helping to run the operation.

The news attracted interest from fans who remembered visiting Ravenseat during the original television programme.

Visitors have previously praised the homemade refreshments served at the farm, with particular enthusiasm for the scones.

The cream teas became closely associated with Ravenseat's appeal, allowing walkers to stop for refreshments in one of the most remote parts of the Yorkshire Dales.

Ravenseat is situated in Upper Swaledale, close to the Pennine Way, making it a familiar landmark for walkers travelling through the area.

The farm's isolated setting, surrounded by hills and open countryside, helped make it one of the most recognisable locations on Our Yorkshire Farm.

The latest series of Our Farm Next Door shows how the next generation is becoming increasingly involved in the family's activities.

While Edith and Raven prepare the cream tea van, other members of the family continue working with livestock and helping restore the historic Anty John's farmhouse nearby.

The programme also follows Amanda and Clive as they balance the demands of farming with the ambitious renovation project.
