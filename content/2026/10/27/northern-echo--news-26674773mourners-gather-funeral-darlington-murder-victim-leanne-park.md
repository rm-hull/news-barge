Hundreds of mourners gathered in Darlington today to say a heartbreaking final goodbye to ‘murdered’ mum Leanne Park, who would do ‘anything for anyone’.

The 44-year-old, allegedly murdered above the Greyhound Pub in Darlington last month, was remembered as a 'kind-hearted' woman with a 'pure heart of gold' whose life was tragically cut short.

The chapel at Darlington Crematorium overflowed with mourners on Tuesday (October 27) who came together to pay their respects, with a large crowd also forming outside to listen to the service through speakers and watch on screens.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21623691.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Leanne Park, 44, a mum, nana, daughter and friend *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

LeAnn Rimes’ *How Do I Live?* played as mourners entered, with two of Leanne’s children, Lewis and Natalia, carrying their mum’s coffin.

Leanne was remembered as the “life and soul of the party”, a woman who loved to dance, enjoyed life, cared deeply for others and worked tirelessly to provide for her family.

Celebrant Julie Whitehouse told mourners: “We come together to remember Leanne Park, to grieve her passing and to celebrate the beautiful young lady she was.

"Some losses in life are especially hard to bear. And the circumstances surrounding Leanne’s passing have brought a heartbreak that is almost impossible to put into words.

“For Leanne’s family so much has been taken from her, and nothing can make sense of what has happened or ease the pain that they now carry.

“Leanne’s family would give anything for just some more time for her. For the chance to make more memories, such as sharing all those little moments, that means so much, yet, so easily taken for granted. And while nothing can replace the time they should have had with Leanne, the memories they have made together remain, they are part of a life shared, a love that cannot be undone and a bond that will always belong to them.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638009.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mourners gathered today at Darlington Crematorium to remember Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

"Leanne’s life deserves to be remembered so much more than the tragic circumstances in which it ended.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638004.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mourners gathered today at Darlington Crematorium to remember Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The funeral heard how Leanne was the daughter of Karen and Kevin, a mum to Natalia, Lewis and Kai, and also to Leah, whom she thought of as her own.

She was a nana to two grandchildren and played a “very special part in their lives”.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638006.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mourners gathered today at Darlington Crematorium to remember Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Born in Darlington, Leanne grew up in the Firthmoor area and was an only child who was “absolutely adored” by her family.

Even from a young age, she “knew exactly how to get her own way”.

She attended Firthmoor Infant School before moving on to Eastbourne Secondary School, where she was popular and had lots of friends.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638010.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mourners gathered today at Darlington Crematorium to remember Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Mourners heard how she loved sleepovers as a child before discovering a passion for music, parties and raves as a teenager.

She was “always ready to have a good time, whether it was at her friends house, a local rave or out on the town at Route 66.”

Ms Whitehouse said: “Leanne loved to dance, she would sing along to every song, even if she didn’t quite know the words.

“That was Leanne, full of life and enjoying herself - making the most of every moment.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638014.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mourners gathered today at Darlington Crematorium to remember Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

But above all, it was clear that Leanne was devoted to her children.

The chapel heard she was born to be a mother, acting as both mum and dad to give her children everything she could and making every Christmas and birthday special.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638019.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes left at Darlington Crematorium for Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Leanne had worked alongside her mum at the family business, the Railway Hotel, before training in hair and beauty.

In recent years she took on three jobs to make ends meet, working as a cleaner, behind the bar at the Builder’s Arms and at Ventress Hall Care Home, a job that perfectly suited her “kind, caring and loving nature.”

Despite working so hard, she always made time for her family and friends, as well as enjoying her favourite soaps, musicals and music.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638024.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes left at Darlington Crematorium for Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

She loved fashion, handbags and shoes, and was described as a hoarder who never threw anything away.

Supermarket Flowers by Ed Sheeran played alongside a montage of photographs and memories of Leanne with all her loved ones, during the service.

The celebrant said: “Leanne had a wonderful personality, a lovely sense of humour and the most caring and considerate nature. She was incredibly generous and would do anything to anyone.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638017.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes left at Darlington Crematorium for Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

She was remembered as a “real diamond”, “one in a million” and someone who made a lasting impression on everyone she met.

As the service drew to a close, the celebrant spoke of the devastating loss felt by those left behind.

Adding: “The truth is that none of us should be here today to say goodbye. Leanne had so much left to live and so many memories to make.

“The hundreds of messages shared on social media and the staggering amount which has been raised with the Gofundme speaks volumes about the impact that she had on the lives of those around her.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21638015.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mourners gathered today at Darlington Crematorium to remember Leanne Park *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

More than £10,000 was raised by members of the public towards the cost of Leanne's funeral, with her family thanking everyone for their donations, support and outpouring of love.

Floral tributes were laid outside the chapel, with heartfelt displays including Fosters cans, a cigarette, hearts, wreaths, her care uniform, a butterfly and a horse, as well as flowers arranged in the shape of the words ‘friend’ and ‘mam’.

*Castles in the Sky* by Ian Van Dahl, played as Leanne was committed, while her family were left to take a final private moment to say goodbye.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21503234.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Leanne Park, who worked at Ventress Care Home, was found at a flat above The Greyhound pub on Parkgate on Wednesday, September 23. *(Image: FACEBOOK)*

Family and friends were invited to gather at Harrogate Hill Club to celebrate Leanne’s life after the service.

The family asked for donations to go to Family Help Darlington.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21494778.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police, CSI and ambulance crews were called to a property above The Greyhound Darlington pub where a police cordon was put in place around 10pm last night *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Leanne was found dead in a flat above The Greyhound pub on Parkgate after emergency crews were called at 9.15pm on Wednesday, September 23.

Her death led to police seeking reviews of the premises licences for The Greyhound and the Black Swan, also on Parkgate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21517212.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Greyhound and Black Swan in Darlington are to face an urgent licensing meeting after police investigation into the murder of Leanne Park. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*
