More than £10,000 has been raised to support cancer care and education for children.

The money was raised at York Professionals’ Silver Anniversary Ball and will go to York Against Cancer, the organisation’s charity of the year.

A raffle, silent auction, live auction, and themed bingo game all helped boost the final total.

Susie Mortonson, chair of York Professionals, said: "We had a record turnout of over 250 guests to support The York Professionals Silver Anniversary Ball last week as the business community gathered to celebrate the professional services sector in York."

Andy Knowles, chief executive at York Against Cancer, said: "We are absolutely delighted, and enormously grateful to York Professionals and all their members.

"These funds will make a genuine difference to younger people affected by cancer.

"On behalf of everyone at York Against Cancer, our volunteers and the families we support, thank you."

York Against Cancer is an independent charity supporting people in York.
