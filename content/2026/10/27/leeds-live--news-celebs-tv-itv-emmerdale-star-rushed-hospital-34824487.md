# ITV Emmerdale star rushed to hospital after heart attack as actor 'rebuilds'

## ITV Emmerdale actor speaks out about suffering a heart attack and being rushed to hospital in a terrifying health scare

An Emmerdale favourite has opened up about a frightening health emergency that saw him rushed to hospital after suffering a heart attack.

The actor is now concentrating on "rebuilding" and "healing" following the incident, which he confessed "changed my life forever". Kevin Mathurin, 53, who has portrayed Vicar Charles Anderson on the ITV soap since 2000, is a familiar face to viewers, reports The Sun.

Taking to Instagram this week, he shared details of the medical crisis that struck during a period of intense "stress and pressure" in 2025, while also providing an encouraging update on his recovery. His Instagram reel documented the journey, beginning with footage of his arrival at the hospital ward before transitioning to clips of his rehabilitation sessions at the gym.

In an extensive caption, the actor and voiceover artist revealed that his health troubles began in October 2025. He wrote: "I was under a lot of stress and pressure. External issues were taking over my well being. I was anxious, stressed, always angry and suppressing it all.

"Not dealing with things head on. I'd also lost a really good friend to Cancer and was upset that I couldn't make his funeral due to work commitments. It was all too much that week and I felt pains in my ribs and arms all week. But knowing me, I didn't complain, I just carried on filming, working out and driving up and down the country to be with my family".

Kevin, who lives in London with his DJ partner Nikki Beatnik, continued: "It was Nikki who begged me to book an ECG scan. By the time I got home from the hospital my doctor called me and said get straight to A&E. Long story-short I had a blocked artery that had damaged part of my heart, making my heartbeat abnormal, causing heart failure and a minor heart attack. Within myself I felt fine, but this freaked me and my family out that I didn't pay attention to my body for a whole week.

"While staying at King's Hospital, London I was kept under monitor and had an Angiogram to determine how serious the blockages were. Thankfully I didn't need surgery for stents, just relying on a lot of meds, exercise and a healthy diet to keep my blood pressure, heart rate and cholesterol down".

The actor – who has also appeared in the likes of Doctors, Banged Up Abroad and Hooligan Escape – revealed how the frightening ordeal had led him "to think, reset, re-evaluate my priorities".

This triggered a "total reboot" in the father-of-two's perspective on life and a newfound desire to spend "quality time" with his nearest and dearest. Kevin, who previously spoke of how his body was "broken" during a 2021 Emmerdale stunt, went on to express his gratitude towards the NHS and heap praise on the dedicated staff at Lewisham Hospital.
