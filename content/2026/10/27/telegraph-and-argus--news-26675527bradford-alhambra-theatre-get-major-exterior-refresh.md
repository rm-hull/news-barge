Bradford Council has announced that its city centre flagship venue, the Alhambra Theatre, will undergo major external painting and repair works over the coming months.

The historic and much-loved venue plays an important role in the cultural life of Bradford and the region, making a significant contribution to the local economy.

Bradford Council will undertake this programme of works to maintain and improve the building in order to secure sustainability for the future.

The work will start mid-November and is anticipated to take approximately 12–16 weeks.

Scaffolding will be installed around the venue to facilitate the works.

The scope of the project is to assess the render, remove any damaged render and install a modern watertight system; decorate all external elevations; and repair the exterior pea lighting.

Initial plans are to do the work an elevation at a time.

There will be no disruption to customers attending shows, visiting companies or the public during this time.

All shows will go ahead as planned.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council’s Executive Member Neighbourhoods and Communities said: “I’m delighted that work will begin very shortly to refresh this cultural gem in the city centre.

“It’s great news that this historic venue is getting the investment it needs to maintain its position as a key heritage building and international venue.

"It is a beautiful building that is an incredibly important part of our cultural heritage, and it is vital that it is refreshed and can continue to be enjoyed by local residents and visitors to our city.”
