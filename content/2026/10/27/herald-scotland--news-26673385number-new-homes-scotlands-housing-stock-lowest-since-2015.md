The number of new homes in Scotland’s housing stock is at the lowest level for more than a decade, statistics show.

Figures released on Tuesday show 18,028 new homes were added to the country’s housing stock between April 2025 and March of this year – an 8% drop from the previous year and the lowest number since 2014-15, excluding the pandemic.

Housing Secretary Shirley-Anne Somerville said: “We recognise the challenges facing the new build sector, driven by international and domestic economic pressures, and we are using every lever at our disposal to accelerate housebuilding across Scotland.

“Despite this, Scotland continues to lead the rest of the UK in housebuilding relative to our population size.

“Our Affordable Housing Supply Programme is also improving, with approvals up 42% and starts up 34% in the year to June 2026.

“Our new national housing agency, More Homes Scotland, will bring greater focus, co-ordination and ambition to unlock stalled developments and attract investment.

“Backed by a record £926 million budget this year, we remain focused on our target of delivering 110,000 affordable homes by 2032.”
