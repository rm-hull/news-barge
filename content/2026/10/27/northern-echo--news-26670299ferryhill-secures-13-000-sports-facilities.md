Ferryhill Town Council has secured more than £13,000 to improve sports facilities and council services, while investment in football pitches continues to pay off.

The £10,000 will refurbish the bowls green and upgrade football showers at Mainsforth Sports Complex.

The funding includes £5,000 from County Councillors Joe Quinn and Paul Mountford’s Local Network Fund, plus £5,000 from Sport England’s Movement Fund.

Ferryhill Mayor Councillor Curtis Bihari said: "Ferryhill Town Council is extremely grateful to all external funders, who have supported the Town Council to deliver capital projects.

"Hopefully, more grant funding can be secured moving forward, to enable further improvements to be delivered that will benefit the Town and local community."

A further £3,350 from the Local Network Fund will buy a trailer so staff can move equipment and supplies between sites more easily.

The council’s six-year football-pitch maintenance programme at Dean Bank Park and Mainsforth is now in its fourth year.

The council has spent £20,201 improving the pitches this year, including £9,600 from the Football Foundation.

The Football Foundation has awarded £57,598 to the programme in total.

The FA rated the pitches “advanced” at their latest inspection, up from “basic” four years ago.

After regenerating Dean Bank Park Play Area with £90,000 from the Landfill Community Fund, the council’s next priority is King George V Playing Field.

Known locally as the Broom Rec, the site will be regenerated after a recent community consultation, with funding applications to follow.
