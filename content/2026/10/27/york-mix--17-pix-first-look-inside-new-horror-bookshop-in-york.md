A brand-new horror-themed bookshop has opened in York.

And what could be more fitting for the most haunted city in the UK, than a bookshop filled with spooky and frighteningly fun tales.

The highly-anticipated Horrifically Good Books has opened **today, Tuesday 27 October**, at 60 Goodramgate.

It opened its doors from 3pm today for its soft launch, with a queue forming out the door of eager patrons ready to step inside for the first time.

It will then be open from 10am to 8pm this week for Ghost Week, and will then resume its standard opening hours of 10am to 6pm every day.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/horrifically-good-books-goodramgate.webp)

Horrifically Good Books is the second bookshop from the team behind Criminally Good Books, which was founded by Isla Coole in March 2024 and has become a literary staple in the city.

***YorkMix*** spoke to deputy manager Cat Winter to find out more about the opening.

“It feels very surreal,” said Cat. “It’s something that we’ve been working on for so long, and we have put so many nights of work into it, so to be open finally is just a wonderful and wild experience.

“We’ve had a queue outside, since we’ve opened the doors with people waiting to come in and everyone’s been so nice and complimentary. It’s wonderful to see everybody band around the horror community.”

The bookshop is a haven of all things horror across two floors – gothic and classic horror, spooky reads for younger readers and YA horror, folk horror, translated horror, historical horror, haunted houses and ghost literature, weird horror fiction and cosmic horror, literary horror, and small press and indie titles.

“We’ve made sure that we cover all bases, from the people who are new to horror and want an introduction to people who really love books like Splatterpunk and more extreme horror. So there’s certainly something here for you.

“If you want a recommendation and or want to know what’s in the content of the books, you can speak to one of our booksellers. We are so lucky to have such a wonderful team who are experts in their particular categories when it comes to horror.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/horrifically-good-books-goodramgate-4.webp)

The larger premises at 60 Goodramgate means that there will now be room for a bespoke book packing area for UK and International orders at a larger more professional scale – and that the team can launch their subscription service.

The subscription service is expected to start from some point in the new year.

The bookshop is also set to host a series of events, readings, signings and ‘other horrifically good gatherings’ – with the first being an evening with author and illustrator Dom & Ink and bestselling author Juno Dawson, which took place last week.
