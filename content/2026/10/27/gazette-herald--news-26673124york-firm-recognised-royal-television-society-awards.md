A York-based television post-production company has won a top industry award for its work on a children’s comedy series.

Motion Post Production of 24 Swinegate was recognised at the Royal Television Society (RTS) Yorkshire Awards for its contribution to the CBBC series High Hoops.

The company was part of the team awarded the Professional Excellence in Post Production award for its work on the show, produced by Can Can Productions.

The team at Motion Post Production said: "In a world that is becoming more connected than ever, we believe that anything is possible for companies like ours, working in an industry that has historically been London-centric.




"Being based in York has never stopped us from working on ambitious productions for national broadcasters.

"We're incredibly proud to be part of Yorkshire's growing creative industry and to demonstrate that world-class post-production can happen right here in the city."

High Hoops brought together a team of creatives across production, editing, visual effects, sound, and post-production.

Motion Post Production worked closely with the wider team to deliver the finished programme.

The team added: "High Hoops is a brilliant show, made by an incredible team of people.

"We'd like to thank Can Can Productions for believing in us, and all our brilliant extended family of freelancers, whose skill and commitment made this work possible."

Based in York, Motion Post Production specialises in sound post-production, Dolby Vision-certified colour grading, online editing, post-production supervision, and delivery.

The company combines technical expertise with a creative approach to storytelling.

The award highlights the growing strength of Yorkshire’s screen industries and York’s status as a UNESCO City of Media Arts.

It demonstrates that high-level television production is thriving outside London.

Motion Post Production operates as a trading brand of Viridian FX and serves both the film and television sectors.

The company’s recent success underscores the potential for creative careers in York and the wider Yorkshire region, the company added.
