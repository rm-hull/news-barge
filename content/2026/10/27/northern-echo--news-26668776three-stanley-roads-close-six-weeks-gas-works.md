Road closures are being introduced in a County Durham town as gas main replacement works get under way.

The work, which is expected to cause disruption for several weeks, will see three streets in Stanley temporarily shut to all vehicles.

The affected roads are Geoffery Terrace, Gladstone Street and Salisbury Street.

A 44-metre stretch of Geoffery Terrace will close between Park Road and Palmer Street.

An 8-metre stretch of Gladstone Street beside numbers 35 and 41 Park Road will close.

An 8-metre stretch of Salisbury Street beside numbers 117 and 119A Park Road will also close.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Durham County Council said the closures will start at 8am on Monday, October 26, and last six weeks.

Signposted diversions will be in place while the roads are closed.

The closures are being made under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

The council said the closure dates may change, with updates posted on signs at the site.

The work is due to finish on December 7, but residents should watch for updates.
