## Israelis vote today in their first election since 7 October, with Benjamin Netanyahu's future on the line. Washington is reportedly floating Viktor Orbán for UN secretary-general. And we interview EU Climate Commissioner Wopke Hoekstra to ask what Lula's re-election means for Europe.

## On today's show:

**Sasha Vakulina** looks at Israel's election: whether it is essentially a referendum on**Benjamin Netanyahu**, and whether 7 October is still the defining issue. She speaks to Chatham House's** Yossi Mekelberg**.

Our Europe editor **Maria Tadeo** speaks to EU Climate Commissioner**Wopke Hoekstra** about new EU rules on preparing for climate disasters, after a devastating summer of wildfires.

**Sándor Zsiros** joins to discuss the Wall Street Journal report that Washington is floating**Viktor Orbán** for UN secretary-general, and whether he has a real chance.
