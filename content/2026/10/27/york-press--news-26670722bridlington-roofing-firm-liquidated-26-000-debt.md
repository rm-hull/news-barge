An East Coast roofing business has gone into liquidation with debts exceeding £26,000.

Complete Roofing Solutions Yorkshire Ltd of Saltwick Drive, Bridlington, was incorporated in 2022 and went into voluntary liquidation on September 7.

Companies House records the directors as Daniel James Malcolm Bint and James Leed Waddington.

London-based Exigen Ltd were appointed liquidators.

Exigen has produced a liquidator’s report known as a statement of financial affairs outlining the finances of the company.

The report said the Bridlington-based company had no assets and no preferential creditors—those who are paid first.

However, it did owe HMRC as a secondary preference creditor - someone paid next - £1 in PAYE and £722.45 in VAT.

Furthermore, £1 was also owed to Apollo Finance, a security interest held on company assets known as a floating charge.

The report also lists the unsecured claims, which totalled £25,276.59.

These are the claims paid last.

They consisted of £21,176.59 owed to unsecured creditors, £3,600.00 owed to directors and £500 of Corporation Tax owed to HMRC, giving an estimated total deficit of £26,001.04.

With the company having issued and called up capital of £2, this gave a total deficit or shortfall of £26,003.04.
