A woman has been charged with perverting the course of justice as part of the ongoing investigation into the murder of a man who was shot in Eston earlier this year.

Cleveland Police’s Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have charged the 29-year-old woman with the offence connected to the death of Peter Phillips, 47.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21524417.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Peter Philips, 47, died after an incident in Eston, his family have described him as the 'heart and soul' of their family. *(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

Mr Phillips died after being shot once at about 11pm on Saturday, May 23, 2026.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21524416.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes on Ayton Crescent, Eston. *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

The woman is due before Teesside Magistrates’ Court on Thursday, October 1.

Two 39-year-old men have already been charged with murder and remain in custody awaiting further court proceedings.

Police had previously charged a 32-year-old woman with suspected perverting the course of justice.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21524418.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police and CSI on the scene of the incident on Ayton Crescent/Mansfield Road, Eston. *(Image: CHRIS BOOTH)*

The investigation into Mr Phillips’ death continues.

Mr Phillips’ family described him as the “heart and soul” of their family and said they felt they had been “robbed of him”.
