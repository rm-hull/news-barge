Police are investigating a report of the theft of 3 temporary traffic lights that were placed at Hull Road / Water Lane junction in Hemingbrough near Selby.

It happened on the 15th September at around 3am.

A police spokesperson said: “Not only does this sort of theft cause a loss to the company, it also leaves road users at significant risk.”
