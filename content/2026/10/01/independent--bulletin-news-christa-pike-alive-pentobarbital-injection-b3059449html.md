# Chaos in Tennessee as Christa Pike survives two doses of lethal injection

![Christa Pike entering a Knox County Criminal Court in Knoxville, Tennessee](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/30/15/2026-09-30T135639Z_1416470123_RC2PTNAYXSRM_RTRMADP_3_TENNESSEE-EXECUTION.JPG?quality=75&width=1250&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=0%2C8%2C0%2C8&auto=webp)

* Christa Pike, 50, survived an attempted execution by lethal injection in Tennessee after being administered two doses of pentobarbital.
* Pike, convicted of the 1995 murder of fellow student Colleen Slemmer, was set to become the first woman executed in the state in 200 years.
* The execution went ahead late on Wednesday after the US Supreme Court lifted a temporary legal stay in a 6-3 decision.
* Witnesses reported the execution chamber curtain was drawn twice before Pike was taken to hospital, where her lawyers confirmed she was receiving life-saving care.
* Her defence team condemned the state's procedure, citing issues including compromised vein access, degraded lethal drugs, and a lack of emergency medical planning.
