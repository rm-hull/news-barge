# Major new nightclub and music venue opens in Leeds city centre

## The venue enjoyed a huge launch

![Mint XL has launched in Leeds city centre](https://i2-prod.leeds-live.co.uk/incoming/article34699587.ece/ALTERNATES/s810/1_DSC_5051.jpg)

A major new music venue and nightclub has launched in Leeds city centre.

Leeds-based events brand Mint, known for Mint Festival, Mint Warehouse and the former Mint Club, has launched their new venue Mint XL. You will find it in the former PRYZM nightclub site in Woodhouse.

Mint XL is a 2,500-capacity venue built around high-spec production, immersive experiences and forward-thinking event programming. Located within the Merrion Centre, owned and managed by Town Centre Securities (TCS), the venue sits prominently within one of Leeds’ most vibrant and accessible districts, close to the city’s universities, nightlife and key transport links.

Speaking on the launch, the team at Mint said: “Mint XL is a huge step forward for us as a brand. For years we’ve operated just outside the city centre through Mint Warehouse and previously Mint Club, so being able to bring something of this scale directly into the heart of Leeds feels incredibly exciting.”
