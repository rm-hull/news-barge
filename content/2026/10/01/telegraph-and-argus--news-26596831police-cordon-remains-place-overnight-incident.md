A police cordon remains in place this morning after emergency services were called to an incident overnight.

Police were called to Lister Hill in Horsforth on Wednesday with images shared on social media showing a significant police presence in the area.

The nature of the incident has not yet been confirmed.

## Police cordon in place

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21523288.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cordon this morning. *(Image: T&A)*

When the T&A visited the scene on Thursday morning, a cordon remained in place around a property, with officers still present.

A member of the public who visited the scene this morning told the T&A that another cordon was in place outside a nearby block of flats at the junction of Lister Hill and Low Lane.

The T&A has contacted West Yorkshire Police for further information.

The newspaper will provide an update when more details are released.
