A County Durham business has applied for permission to sell alcohol, according to a public notice.

CustomerKnect Limited, based at Unit 6, Fox Cover Enterprise Park, Admiralty Way, Seaham, has submitted a request for a new premises licence to Durham County Council.

The application, which was published on September 28, outlines plans for off-sales only, with proposed hours from 7am to 6pm on weekdays and from 7am to 2pm on Saturdays.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Residents have until October 21, to voice their opinions on the application.

Here is how you can share your thoughts on the matter.

The application can be inspected at Annand House, John Street North, Meadowfield, Durham, DH7 8RS by appointment on weekdays between 9.30am and 4pm.

You can either send a letter to Durham County Council, Licensing, PO Box 274, Stanley, DH8 1GH or email [licensing@durham.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@durham.gov.uk.)
