# A1 crash: Police looking for occupants of this Ford Transit who may have witnessed collision

## Police investigating a serious crash on the A1 which left a woman in critical condition have issued a further image of a van they would like to identify.

The collision happened between a blue VW Polo and a Scania HGV car transporter on the northbound A1, at the junction for the B6474, between Wentbridge and Darrington, shortly before 2.20pm on Monday September 21.

The driver of the Polo, a woman in her teens, suffered life-threatening injuries and remains in a serious condition in hospital.

Officers would like to hear from the driver, occupants, or anyone who can assist in identifying a black van, believed to be a Ford Transit, which was seen entering the northbound A1 from the B6474 a few moments prior to the collision.

![Police investigating a serious collision on the A1 have issued a further image of a van they would like to identify in the hope that the driver could have vital information that could assist the investigation.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/emergency-incident/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


They stress that the van's occupants could hold vital witness information.

PC Rob Wadsworth of the Roads Policing Unit said: "We believe this is a black Ford Transit van which has some distinctive black and possibly orange trim on its back doors and possibly on the rest of the body.

"I want to make it clear that the van's occupants are being treated as potential witnesses to this collision and may have vital information that could assist us in piecing together exactly what happened."
