A man has been charged after a large quantity of cannabis and crack cocaine was found at a property in Bradford following a raid.

Officers from the Bradford East Neighbourhood Policing Team found the drugs at a home on Gladstone Street in Barkerend on September 19.

## Who has been charged?

Faraz Khan, 26, was charged with possession with intent to supply a controlled drug of Class A – heroin, concerned in the supply of a controlled drug of Class B – cannabis, acquire / use / possess criminal property, and possess with intent to supply a controlled drug of Class A - crack cocaine.

He was remanded in custody and appeared at Bradford Magistrates' Court on September 21, where he was released on bail.

His next court appearance is scheduled at Bradford Crown Court on October 23.
