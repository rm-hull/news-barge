A much-loved pub has reopened its doors with a brand new look.

The Fox and Roman in York has welcomed back customers following a refurbishment that brings together countryside charm and updated rustic style, complete with roaring fires, cosy corners and refreshed interiors.

The remodel features a revamped beer garden, a private dining room for up to 18 guests, and a new cocktail station.

The pub has also created five new jobs, covering both front-of-house and kitchen roles.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526184.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The extensive beer garden *(Image: Facebook)*

The closure began on August 24, with the pub, owned by Vintage Inns, reopening on September 19.

Angela Avery, general manager at The Fox and Roman, said: "We’re so pleased to open our doors and welcome guests old and new to see what we’ve been working on at The Fox and Roman.

"It’s the same warm, friendly pub you know and love, just with a fresh feel.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526196.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The plush interior. *(Image: Facebook)*

"Whether you're after a cosy corner to unwind with a drink or a lovely spot to enjoy a hearty meal, we can’t wait to share it with you.

"The great food, service and elegant interior work together beautifully to offer a truly premium respite for tourists and locals alike."

The pub will host games night on Mondays, a pub quiz every Tuesday from 7.30pm, and seasonal craft activities for adults, including pumpkin carving and a monthly flower workshop.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526198.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dining is a key feature at The Fox and Roman *(Image: Facebook)*

Children’s craft sessions will also take place on Mondays from 3pm.

The Fox and Roman remains true to its country pub roots, offering hearty food and a welcoming atmosphere.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526212.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An elegant interior *(Image: Facebook)*

The new menu features slow-cooked pork belly, stone-baked pizzas, and the pub’s signature skewers, alongside classics like steaks, fish and chips, and a home-baked pie of the day.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526229.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Fox and Roman has a country theme *(Image: Facebook)*

A brunch menu is served from 9am to 11.30am on Saturdays and Sundays.

With deep historical ties to the area, the pub was once known simply as The Fox and served as the Fox Hotel, where racehorses were stabled.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526213.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Just look at the fireplace! *(Image: Facebook)*

In 1833, Roman remains were discovered in the car park, and some artefacts from that period are on display inside the pub.

Located near York Racecourse, The Fox and Roman continues to attract racegoers and remains a popular stop for visitors to the city.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21526250.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of the Fox and Roman *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The pub is also dog-friendly, welcoming guests and their four-legged friends before, during or after a walk around York.

The team encourages visitors to check its Facebook page for details of upcoming events and activities.
