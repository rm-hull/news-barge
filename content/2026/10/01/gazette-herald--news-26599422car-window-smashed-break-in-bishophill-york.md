A car window has been smashed during a break-in in York.

The car was broken into in Kyme Street, Bishophill, at about 4.15am on Wednesday (September 30), North Yorkshire Police said.

Police are urging anyone with information about the incident to get in touch.

A police spokesperson said anyone with information should email: [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260191866 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
