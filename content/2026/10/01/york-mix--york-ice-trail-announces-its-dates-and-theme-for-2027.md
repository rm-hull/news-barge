The much-loved York Ice Trail will return for another sparkling weekend this February.

The theme for 2027 has been revealed to be **Legends Unleashed**.

Taking place over the weekend of **6 and 7 February 2027**, the trail will bring legendary stories and characters to life through a spectacular collection of ice sculptures across York.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/07.02.26-Make-It-York-Ice-Trail-2026-AM-9.webp)

Inspired by history and folklore, each sculpture brings a new story to life, inviting you to discover more as you explore the city.

York Ice Trail is a free and family-friendly event.

The ice sculptures, curated by Make It York and partners, are created by Icebox, the UK and Europe’s leading ice sculpture specialist.

Trail maps will be available from 1 February 2027 online and from the Visit York Visitor Information Centre, on Back Swinegate.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/07.02.26-Make-It-York-Ice-Trail-2026-AM-13.webp)

*YorkMix* ice sculpture last year

Sarah Loftus, managing director of Make It York, said: “The York Ice Trail is always a special moment in the city’s winter calendar.

“It’s a brilliant opportunity for families, residents and visitors to explore York, discover incredible ice sculptures and enjoy a little winter magic along the way.

“This year’s legends theme and the new added experiences are set to make it extra special!”

More trail announcements are to come, and keep up to date at the Visit York website.
