Do you recognise this man?

North Yorkshire Police would like to speak to him in connection with a racially aggravated hate crime incident that occurred at around 7pm on Sunday 6 September in York.

The incident took place inside a taxi, where the driver was subjected to repeated racial abuse during the journey. The man was later dropped off outside the Nags Head on Heworth Road.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/829035678_1105826665146790_5703709880733895724_n.jpg)

Please contact North Yorkshire Police if you recognise the man pictured in the CCTV image, as they believe he may have information that could assist their investigation.

Please contact North Yorkshire Police online in the first instance:

* Email: [email protected]
* Online: Visit their website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’
* Live chat: Available via their website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260175133 when providing information.
