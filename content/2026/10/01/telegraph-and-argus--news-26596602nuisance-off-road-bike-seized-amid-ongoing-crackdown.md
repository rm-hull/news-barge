Police have seized a "nuisance" off-road bike as part of ongoing efforts to tackle anti-social riding.

Officers confiscated the bike on Tuesday night after receiving reports of off-road vehicles being ridden around Batley and causing disruption to local residents.

The vehicle was seized under Section 59 of the Police Reform Act, which gives police powers to act against vehicles being used in an anti-social manner.

The latest seizure is part of continued action by police to target nuisance riders, an issue that regularly generates complaints from communities.

A police spokesperson said officers will not hesitate to take action where vehicles are being used in a way that causes "nuisance, annoyance or distress."

## Police statement in full

On social media, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We continue to listen to concerns raised by local residents and will take action where we identify vehicles being used in a manner that causes nuisance, annoyance or distress to our communities.

"Your reports matter. Information from residents helps us identify problem areas and target those responsible.

"If you are experiencing ongoing issues with nuisance off-road bikes, please continue to report them to us."
