These photos were taken in the early hours of this morning (1st October) as crews continued to tackle a significant wood fire, south of Selby.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was called to reports of a fire at the Seras facility on Heck and Pollington Lane, near Great Heck, at 23:41 on Tuesday.

Firefighters remain on scene and are expected to continue operations throughout today. Residents nearby are advised to keep windows and doors closed due to lingering smoke, and motorists should check local road closures before travelling.

The cause is under investigation but is not currently believed to be suspicious.

The fire service thanked the local community for their continued patience and support.
