Parts of St Helens Close and St Helens Road in Pocklington will be closed to through traffic at the end of the month.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council said the closure is needed for ducting works.

The restriction is due to begin at 9.30am on October 26 and end at 3.30pm on October 29.

It will cover part of St Helens Close, from the property boundary of Middlecote and Woldsway to its junction with St Helens Road/Avenue.

Part of St Helens Road will also be closed northbound from its junction with Kilnwick Road, encompassing the properties Clifton and Woldsway.

The closure will apply to all through traffic; however, access will remain available for residents, emergency vehicles and pedestrians.

A signed diversion will be provided via St Helens Avenue, Pocklington TC, Pocklington Provincial Ward at the time of closure.

The council said the road closure is required so the works can be completed while keeping the public safe.

The Order will be made under Section 14(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

It will remain valid for 18 months, although the council anticipates that the works will finish within four days.

The notice says the order will be introduced no sooner than seven days after the date of the notice.

Further information is available from Highways Customer Care on 01482 393939.

People seeking clarification can contact East Riding of Yorkshire Council and ask for Highways Customer Care, referencing: PE.
