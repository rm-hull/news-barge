SWISS singer-songwriter Seraina Telli will perform in Bradford this autumn as part of her UK tour.

She will take the stage at Nightrain on October 9, 2026, as part of a series of UK shows in support of her latest album, GREEN LIVE.

Telli said: "The autumn tour feels like the beginning of the next chapter, one that I'm not writing alone."

Described as "the best rock voice in Switzerland" by St. Galler Tagblatt, Telli is known for her powerful stage presence and emotional connection with the audience.

Her live shows are praised for their energy and spontaneity.

She said: "We've played some of these songs hundreds of times, yet something new keeps happening.

"Those are the moments you can't manufacture.

"They only happen live."

The UK leg of the tour also includes performances in Edinburgh, Newcastle, Sheffield, Nottingham, Milton Keynes, Bridgwater, and London.
