A Darlington logistics company has applied to expand its operations in County Durham by adding a new base for its fleet of lorries and trailers.

Northern Express Logistics Ltd, based in Darlington, is seeking to change its current licence to include an operating centre at NE Parking Services Ltd, Bowburn South Industrial Estate.

The company plans to station three goods vehicles and three trailers at the new location.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

The application was published on September 24, and interested parties have 21 days from that date to make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE.

Neighbours who believe the expansion will impact their use or enjoyment of land nearby are advised to submit their reasons in writing, ensuring a copy is also sent to Northern Express Logistics Ltd at 39 Neasham Road, Darlington, DL1 4AF.

The guide for making representations is available at www.gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.

The new base at Bowburn South Industrial Estate will be an addition to Northern Express Logistics Ltd's existing operations.
