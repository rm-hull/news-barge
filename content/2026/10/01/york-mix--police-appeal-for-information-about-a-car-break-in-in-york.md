Police have put out an appeal for information in relation to a vehicle break-in in York.

The incident happened on Kyme Street, York, at about 4:15am on Wednesday 30 September. A car has been broken into, with the window being smashed.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for if anyone has seen anything; recorded anything on their mobile phone; for any home cameras; if anyone was driving in the area with an active dashcam; and if anyone has any information to give.

If so, please email: [email protected].

Alternatively, you can call North Yorkshire Police on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260191866 when passing on information.
