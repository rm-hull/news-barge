# Exciting new food opening coming to Leeds city centre

## Work is ongoing ahead of the new opening launching in Leeds city centre

An exciting new Leeds city centre opening is coming.

Work is currently ongoing ahead of the launch of Tahin. The Turkey food vendor will soon open in Kirkgate.

It will be based next to The Kitty Cafe in a unit which formerly housed a vape shop. When the business opens, you will find lahmacun wraps and shawarma wraps on the menu.

Elsewhere in Leeds city centre, we have seen a new ornament shop Ghosted open while The Mini Mall has launched inside the Merrion Centre. As always, we’ll keep you updated on the latest new openings in Leeds.
