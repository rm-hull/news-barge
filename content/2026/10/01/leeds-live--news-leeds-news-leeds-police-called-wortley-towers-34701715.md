# Leeds police called to Wortley Towers as woman found wounded - arrest made

## The police were seen at Wortley Towers in Farnley

A man has been arrested after a woman was found wounded in Leeds.

West Yorkshire Police were called to Wortley Towers in Farnley, Leeds on Wednesday, September 30, at around 4.48pm. They had been called to a disturbance, and found a woman there with a wound to her arm.

A 37-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assault. The police investigation is now continuing.

They said: "At about 4.48pm yesterday (Wednesday), police were called to a disturbance in Wortley Towers, Farnley.

"Officers attended and found a woman with a wound to her arm. A 37-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assault and taken into custody. Enquiries are continuing."
