## 'Spectacular' North Yorkshire garden named one of the best to visit in autumn

The Castle Howard Arboretum comes alive during the autumn. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A "spectacular" garden in North Yorkshire has been named one of the best gardens to visit this autumn.

Press Association has listed the top 12 gardens around the UK you can visit to enjoy "autumn colour".

The Castle Howard Arboretum made it onto the list thanks to it being home to more than 7,000 trees from around the world and its red squirrel population.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21517249.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The red squirrel enclosure is a highlight of people's trips to the arboretum. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is the Castle Howard Arboretum like?

On the arboretum, Press Association said: "This is one of North Yorkshire's most spectacular spots for autumn colour, with 120 acres of woodland showcasing bronze oaks, golden beeches, and fiery red Japanese maples from October through to early November.

"Home to more than 7,000 trees from around the world, the arboretum also offers visitors the chance to spot red squirrels as part of one of the UK's most successful conservation programmes.

"There's also a programme of autumn events including fungi foraging walks, a family-friendly fungi festival, and an artists and makers fair."

Described as a "fantastic autumnal day out", the Castle Howard Arboretum offers a peaceful area for visitors to enjoy.

The grounds are filled with thousands of trees, lakes, woodland glades, and wildflower meadows to explore.

You will also want to visit the red squirrel enclosure to see David Attenborough, one of the most famous red squirrels at the arboretum.

There are also lots of events happening at the arboretum, including squirrel talks, the fungi fest, and Pilates classes.
