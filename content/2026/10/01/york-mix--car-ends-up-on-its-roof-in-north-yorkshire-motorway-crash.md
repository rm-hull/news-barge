A car landed on its roof after a crash on the motorway last night (Wednesday).

It happened on the northbound carriageway of the **A1(M)** at**junction 47**, the Allerton Park Interchange.

Fire crews from Knaresborough, Harrogate and Wetherby responded to the collision.

#### Junction 47, A1M

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival crews found one vehicle had left the road and ended up on its roof.

“All occupants were out of the vehicle on arrival of the crews and crews left them in the hands of ambulance crews.”
