From hidden gems serving speciality brews to long-established cafés loved by regulars, Darlington has developed a thriving coffee scene in recent years.

With International Coffee Day taking place today, there is no better excuse to swap instant coffee for a handcrafted latte and support a local business.

Here are six coffee shops that continue to earn praise from customers.

### **Echo 3 Coffee House & Roastery**

Tucked away in Clark's Yard, Echo 3 Coffee House & Roastery has built a reputation among coffee enthusiasts for its expertly prepared drinks and freshly roasted beans.

Customers regularly praise the quality of the coffee, with one describing it as the "best coffee shop in town".

### **Maggie's**

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20293063.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

A staple of Darlington's café scene, Maggie's is known for its friendly atmosphere, homemade food and tempting range of cakes.

Visitors frequently mention the warm welcome and family-run feel, making it a popular spot for a leisurely coffee break.

### **Origins Coffee Lounge**

For those who take their coffee seriously, Origins Coffee Lounge has become a go-to destination.

The Grange Road venue is regularly praised for its drinks, while customers also highlight its relaxed atmosphere and selection of sweet treats.

### **Hatch Luncheonette**

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20253740.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

More than just a coffee shop, Hatch Luncheonette has become a popular destination for brunch, lunch and quality coffee.

Visitors often praise the food and service, with many returning time and again for its laid-back atmosphere.

### **The Sunflower Café**

A favourite with many Darlington residents, The Sunflower Café is known for its traditional café offering.

From hearty breakfasts to freshly brewed coffee, customers regularly single out both the food and staff for praise.

### **Cats Den Coffee House**

Offering something a little different, Cats Den Coffee House combines drinks and cakes with the chance to spend time alongside its resident cats.

Visitors have described it as calm, welcoming and the perfect place to unwind for an hour or two.
