A CONCERT celebrating some of the greatest guitarists in music history is coming to Scarborough.

The Story of Guitar Heroes will take to the stage at Scarborough Spa Theatre on Friday, October 23, as part of its 10th anniversary tour.

The show, described as "the UK’s No.1 guitar anthems concert," spans five decades of music, from 1950s rock and roll to modern legends.

"Every lick is expertly played by highly experienced, gifted musicians" — Time Out

Audiences can expect classics from legends such as Hank Marvin, Eric Clapton, Jimi Hendrix, Brian May, Jimmy Page, Mark Knopfler, David Gilmour, Ritchie Blackmore, Tony Iommi, Van Halen, and Slash.

Tickets are available through the Scarborough Spa box office, by calling 01723 376774, or via the official website at www.scarboroughspa.co.uk.
