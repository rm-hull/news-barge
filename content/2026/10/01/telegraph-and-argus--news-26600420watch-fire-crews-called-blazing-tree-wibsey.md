Firefighters have been called to a blazing tree.

The conifer, near Folly Hall Road, Wibsey, caught fire and was spotted flaming this evening (Thursday).

Photos and video shared on social media showed the conifer burning, with fire crews attending the scene and using a hose to extinguish the flames.

The T&A have reached out to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service for comment.
