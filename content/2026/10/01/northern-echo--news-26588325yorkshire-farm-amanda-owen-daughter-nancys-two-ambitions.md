Amanda Owen's youngest daughter Nancy is set to reveal that she still dreams of combining two very different careers.

Nancy has grown up surrounded by farming at Ravenseat in Swaledale, but her ambitions extend well beyond the sheep and moors.

Details shared ahead of the new series of Our Farm Next Door reveal the youngster still hopes to become both a ballerina and a farmer.

Wise Owl Films, which makes the programme, said: "Youngest daughter Nancy still dreams of becoming a ballerina as well as a farmer."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21511330.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nancy *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The unusual combination is one of several signs that the Owen children are beginning to establish their own identities away from the collective family story.

Nancy is the youngest of Amanda and Clive's nine children.

She was still very young when viewers first got to know the family through Our Yorkshire Farm.

The latest series of Our Farm Next Door will continue to chart her upbringing at Ravenseat while also showing her interests beyond the farm.

Nancy will also remain closely involved in the Anty John's project.

Wise Owl Films has revealed that Amanda and the children will leave the farm for a visit to Beamish Museum.

The trip is designed to give them inspiration for a cupboard bed which the younger girls want joiner Philip to build in their new home.

The historic feature will become another part of the interior as the farmhouse begins to take shape.

The new series will show the plaster finally dry, allowing painting and flooring to begin.

Furniture collected by Amanda will also start appearing in individual rooms.

Towards the end of the series, Nancy and the other girls are expected to take part in one of the project's biggest milestones.

Wise Owl Films said Amanda and the girls will prepare for their "first sleepover at Anty John's".

It will be a major moment for a family which has spent years restoring the property.
