A bank card has been stolen from a family on holiday in a North Yorkshire seaside town.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to about the theft in Scarborough.

The force said the victim’s bank card was stolen while she visited arcades in the town.

A police spokesperson said the woman returned to her hotel and “realised that her bank card [was] missing”.

They said the bank card was then used at a cashpoint by an “unknown male”.

Anyone who recognises the man pictured is asked to call 101 or report information on the North Yorkshire Police website quoting reference number 12260154883.
