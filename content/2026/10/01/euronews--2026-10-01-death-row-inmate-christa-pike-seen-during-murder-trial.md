File footage shows Christa Pike during her murder trial in Knoxville, Tennessee, in 1996 and 2012. Pike was convicted of the 1995 murder of 19-year-old Colleen Slemmer and sentenced to death.

The footage captures Pike in court during proceedings that followed the killing. Additional images show Pike in a Tennessee Department of Correction handout from 2023. Her execution in Tennessee failed on Wednesday after two doses of lethal injection were administered.
