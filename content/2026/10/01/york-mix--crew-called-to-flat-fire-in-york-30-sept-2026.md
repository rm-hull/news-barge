A crew was called after a fire alarm activated in York flats last night (Wednesday).

Firefighters from Easingwold, on standby in the city. went to **Percy’s Lane** at**7.13pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival this was found to be a colander that had accidentally been left on a hob.

#### **Percy’s Lane, York**

“Crews used one breathing apparatus to clear light smoke logging from the flat and gave advice to the occupant.”
