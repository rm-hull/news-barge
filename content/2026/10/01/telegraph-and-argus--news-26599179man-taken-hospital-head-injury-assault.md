A man has been taken to hospital following a reported assault, police have confirmed.

The force were called to Hopwood Lane, Halifax, to reports that a man had been assaulted in Hopwood Lane, Halifax, just after 5.30pm.

A man was taken to hospital after officers found him with a head injury.

Photos posted on social media show a cordon in place and a police vehicle in attendance.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At about 5.35pm yesterday (Wednesday), police received a report of a man who had been assaulted in Hopwood Lane, Halifax.

"Officers attended and found a man with a head injury.

"He was taken to hospital for further treatment.

"Calderdale CID is investigating the incident."

Anyone with information that may assist the investigation is urged to contact the team via Live Chat, quoting crime reference 13260557201.
