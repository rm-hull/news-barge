A person fell into the river after the railings they were leaning on gave way early today (Thursday).

Emergency services were mobilised to the incident at King’s Staith at **2.23am.**

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Water rescue crews were mobilised to a person who had fallen into the river after the railings that they were leaning on gave way.

#### King’s Staith, York

“Prior to the crews getting there a member of the public had thrown the person a life ring and helped them out of the river.

“The person was left in the hands of paramedics and no fire service action was necessary.”
