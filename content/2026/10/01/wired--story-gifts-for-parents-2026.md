Parenting is exhausting. If there's a new parent in your life, whether it's your friend who is the new parent of the group or a family member who just had a baby, there are a lot of ways to show some love. Showing up, checking in on how they're surviving, and bringing something that can improve their life are all great ways to support the new parents in your circle.

There's a lot of parenting and baby gear out there, and not all of it is created equal. Not all of it will suit what the parents in your life might need, either. This guide is organized into a handful of different categories of parenting gadgets, smart home gear, little luxuries, and even food deliveries you can send to their door. All of these are tried and tested by WIRED's reviewers (many of whom are parents) and can confirm the difference it's made in their lives.
