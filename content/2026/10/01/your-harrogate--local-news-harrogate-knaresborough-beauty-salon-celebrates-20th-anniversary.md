A Knaresborough beauty salon is celebrating 20 years in business with a month of anniversary giveaways.

Aimes Health & Beauty was founded by Amy Dodsworth in 2006 and has evolved from its original salon into the beauty boutique it is today.

To mark the milestone, the team will decorate the shop and run a special promotion throughout October, giving clients the chance to win treatments, products and experiences.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6abe47df8f86bd8703b.jpg)

A spokesperson for Aimes said:

> “It’s a milestone we’re incredibly proud of, particularly having survived two decades of changing times, recessions and the challenges of Covid, while continuing to build an excellent reputation within the local community.”

The team has also been looking back through old photographs showing how much the salon has changed over the past two decades.

The spokesperson added:

> “We’ve also been looking back through our old photographs and have some great images showing just how much the salon has changed over the years.”
