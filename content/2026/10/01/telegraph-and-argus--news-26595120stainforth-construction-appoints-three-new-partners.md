A construction firm has strengthened its leadership team with the appointment of three new partners.

Stainforth Construction LLP, headquartered in Shipley, West Yorkshire, has promoted Ben Whitlock, Dan Hayden and Joe Greenwood as part of a planned succession strategy.

The trio, who have a combined 65 years of industry experience and 51 years with Stainforth, will help lead the company through its next phase of growth.

Dean Slingsby, partner at Stainforth Construction, said: "These promotions reflect the hard work, commitment and loyalty that Ben, Dan and Joe have consistently demonstrated throughout their careers at Stainforth.

"They have developed their careers within the business, understand our clients and our way of working, and bring the experience and ambition we need for the next stage of our growth."

The company currently holds an £80 million order book for the next 18 months.

As part of the changes, managing partner Danny Wilson will move into the role of chairman.

Mr Whitlock, Mr Hayden and Mr Greenwood will focus on client relationships, new business opportunities and delivering projects across the UK.

Founded in 1991, Stainforth Construction delivers commercial, industrial, retail, leisure, residential, education and health projects, ranging from £300,000 to more than £15 million in value.
