There is an ongoing police incident in York city centre.

The Coppergate Centre multi-storey car park is currently closed.

Next to the car park there is a police cordon in place at the riverside walkway, leading to Castle Car park.

#### Incident location

There is a police officer and vehicle stationed at each entrance of the riverside walkway alongside the River Foss, with no access to the public.

Piccadilly is currently open as normal for traffic, and Castle Car Park also remains open.

It is unknown yet when the cordon will be lifted.

YorkMix has contacted North Yorkshire Police.

We have no details yet about the incident. We will update when we have more.
