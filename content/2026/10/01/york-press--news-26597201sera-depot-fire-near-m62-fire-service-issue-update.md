## PICTURES: Update on dramatic fire in North Yorkshire which 'could burn for days'

These images captured by North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service this morning (October 1) continue to show the ferocity of the blaze at the Seras depot *(Image: North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service)*

FIRE crews remain at the scene of a significant fire in North Yorkshire which 'could burn for days.'

The county's fire and rescue service are into their third day tackling a blaze at a Seras Energy Limited site in Great Heck, close to the M62.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21523720.jpg?type=mds-article-620)These images captured by North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service this morning continue to show the ferocity of the blaze at the Seras depot *(Image: North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service)*

Over the course of yesterday, the size of the blaze was reduced from 200 metres by 30 metres down to 50 metres by 30 metres, but it was confirmed by North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service that the blaze could burn for some days.

In a statement this morning, the service said crews are expected to continue operations throughout today.

Residents nearby have been advised to keep windows and doors closed because of lingering smoke.

North Yorkshire Council confirmed that Long Lane, and Heck and Pollington Lane are closed until Sunday, October 4, with delays likely in the area.

Motorists have been advised to check local road closures before travelling.
