A McDonald’s restaurant has reopened to customers after a redesign.

The Brighouse branch, owned by Yorkshire franchisee Pritpal Singh, is one of 13 McDonald’s outlets he operates across the region.

Mr Singh said: "I’m really glad we’ve been able to give our Brighouse restaurant a fresh new feel and it’s exciting to welcome everyone back and see the place looking better than ever.

"The dining area feels more contemporary and modern and these changes are set to make things simpler for both customers and staff."

The refurbishment is part of McDonald’s reimaging programme, which aims to improve customer experience through updated layouts and new technology.

The redesigned restaurant now features a more comfortable and relaxed dining area.

It also offers better integration of digital ordering channels, including the McDonald’s app, alongside traditional walk-in service.

The changes are designed to meet consumer demand for greater speed, efficiency and choice.

The improvements are expected to benefit both customers and staff by streamlining the ordering process and making better use of internal space.

The refreshed environment aims to support a more enjoyable and efficient dining experience for all.
