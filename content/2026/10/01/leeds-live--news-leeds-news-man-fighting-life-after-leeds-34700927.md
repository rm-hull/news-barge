# Man fighting for life after Leeds 'disturbance' - police statement in full

## Five people have been arrested

A man is fighting for his life after a "disturbance" in Leeds.

A man in his forties was found in need of emergency medical attention on Lister Hill, in Horsforth, at about 3.32pm yesterday, on Wednesday, September 30. Five people, aged between 44 and 74, have been arrested on suspicion of assault following enquiries into the incident.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "The investigation is being led by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, who would be keen to hear from anyone with dash cam or mobile phone footage of what happened.

"If you can assist then please contact the team via LiveChat, quoting crime reference 13260557050 .Information can also be reported anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555111 or online."
