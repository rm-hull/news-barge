Finalists have been announced for the 2027 Visit York Tourism Awards.

The awards, now in their 29th year and sponsored by LNER, celebrate excellence across tourism, hospitality, and culture in York and the surrounding areas.

The Visit York Tourism Awards 2027 winners will be announced at a spectacular evening of recognition on 11 March 2027 at the historic York Racecourse.

Sarah Loftus, managing director of Make It York, said: "We’re delighted to celebrate the passion, commitment and creativity the finalists bring to York.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527437.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Castle Howard is a finalist in the International Tourism and Large Visotor Attraction categories. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"The Awards are a fantastic opportunity to shine a light on those going the extra mile to create exceptional experiences for visitors and local communities.

"Our partnership with Visit North Yorkshire this year reflects our commitment to celebrating success together and championing tourism not just in the city, but right across the region."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527464.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Puzzling World of Professor Kettlestring is a finalist in the Independent business and the small visitor attraction in York Categories. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The shortlisted finalists represent businesses and individuals who have made outstanding contributions to the visitor economy, which contribute over £2 billion to York’s economy.

Winners across eligible categories will be considered for shortlisting in the national VisitEngland Awards for Excellence 2027.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20393964.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Milner York is a finalist in the Large Hotel of the Year Category *(Image: Pic supplied)*

New for this year, the Awards are also running an additional four categories in partnership with Visit North Yorkshire specifically for hotels, serviced accommodation and attractions in North Yorkshire.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527495.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Guy Fawkes Inn is a finalist in the Pub of the Year and the Serviced Accommodation providers in York categories. *(Image: Pic supplied)*
