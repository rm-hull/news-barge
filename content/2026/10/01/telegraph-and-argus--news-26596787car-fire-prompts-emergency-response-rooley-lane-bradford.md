A car caught fire on a Bradford road yesterday, prompting a response from firefighters.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was called to Rooley Lane at 12:01pm yesterday, Wednesday, September 30, following reports of a vehicle fire.

One crew attended the scene and extinguished the blaze.

Images shared online showed a BMW engulfed in flames as firefighters tackled the blaze.

The fire service is still working to establish the cause of the fire.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "We were called to Rooley Lane at 12.01 yesterday to reports of a vehicle fire.

"One crew attended and extinguished it- no cause recorded as yet."
