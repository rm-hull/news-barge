A car has burst into flames on a road near York.

Firefighters were called to the scene in Foss Field Lane, off Appleton Road near Acaster Malbis, shortly before 2.10pm on Thursday (October 1).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crew extinguished the blaze and left the vehicle’s owner to arrange recovery.

In a statement, the fire service said: “Acomb crew were mobilised to a vehicle on fire.

“Crews used two hose reel jets and one breathing apparatus to extinguish the fire.

“Crews left vehicle with responsible person to arrange recovery.”
