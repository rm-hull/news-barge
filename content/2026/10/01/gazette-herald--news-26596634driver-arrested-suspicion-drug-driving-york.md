A DRIVER has been arrested on suspicion of drug driving following a proactive police operation in York.




The arrest took place in the Fulford area, although North Yorkshire Police did not specify a location or date.

Police said the driver has been released on bail pending the results of toxicology analysis.

PC Dave Ellison of North Yorkshire Police said: "We wish to thank members of the public for providing information which allows us to target drivers causing a danger on the roads."

Anyone with information about drink or drug driving is encouraged to contact their local Neighbourhood Policing Team.
