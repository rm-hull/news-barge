Actor Bradley Whitford received the 2,858th star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in Los Angeles on 30 September 2026, joined by his wife Amy Landecker and former West Wing co‑stars Dulé Hill and Martin Sheen.

Footage shows Whitford posing with the new honour as Hollywood Chamber of Commerce officials and guests look on, then speaking about his family and what the moment means.

The ceremony drew colleagues and fans to the boulevard as Whitford knelt beside the terrazzo marker. He thanked his family for making him feel loved long before luck or fame entered the picture, and said their presence stays with him every day.
