Staff were left unpaid at a Whitby construction company that has gone into liquidation owing almost £200,000, according to a liquidators' report.

P.M Straw (Builders) Ltd was a long-established firm and was established in 2002.

Companies House lists the sole director as Stephen Straw.

The company, which was registered at Bagdale, Whitby, entered voluntary liquidation on August 19.

Westgates Restructuring of Hull was appointed liquidator.

A liquidators’ report known as a statement of affairs outlined the situation.

The report said there were no estimated realisable assets that could be distributed to preferential creditors - those who are paid first.

Among the liabilities, there were seven employees owed £14,004.23 as a preferential creditor.

In addition, HMRC was owed £53,344.74 as a secondary preferential creditor - one who is paid next.

This was made up of £53,343.73 in PAYE and £1 in VAT.

This gave a total deficiency of £67,348.96 with regard to preferential creditors.

In addition, unsecured creditors - those who are paid last - were owed a total of £128,378.28 among them.

This was made up of trade and expense creditors owed £797.89.

In addition, seven employees were owed £117,444.47 between them.

A bank was owed £1 and HMRC was owed a further £10,134.90 in Corporation Tax.

This gave a total deficit with regard to creditors of £195,827.24.

With the company having £103 in issued and called up capital, this gave a total overall outstanding deficit or shortfall of £195,830.24.
