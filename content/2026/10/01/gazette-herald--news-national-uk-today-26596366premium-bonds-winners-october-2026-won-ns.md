Two lucky Premium Bonds holders have each won the £1 million jackpot this month.

National Savings and Investments (NS&I) has announced the October 2026 winners.

Only two lucky winners claimed the top £1 million prize this month, with the first being claimed by a winner from East Sussex with the bond number 331QY484133.

The owner's winning bond was valued at £5,900 and was purchased in May 2018 with an overall holding of £50,000.

## What is a Premium Bond?

The second Premium Bonds winner to claim £1 million this month is from Somerset, with the bond number 681KK770541.

The winner has an overall holding of £16,000, with the winning bond worth  
£11,000, purchased in July 2026.

## **Premium Bond prizes announced in October 2026**

Every month, only two winners take home £1 million, but plenty of other prizes are available, with Bonds also able to win £100,000, £50,000, £25,000, £10,000 and more prizes.

You can check out the big winners for October via the NS&I website here.
