A 91-year-old man has died after a crash in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened at around 5pm on Tuesday, September 29, between Little Edstone and Marton and involved a grey Volvo XC60, which left the road and ended up in a shallow ditch.

Paramedics attended but the driver was pronounced dead at the scene.

A police spokesperson said: "At this stage, it appears that the collision may have resulted from a medical episode.

"We are supporting the man’s family while the investigation continues."

Enquiries remain ongoing.
