# Invitation to 'get ready to sizzle and giggle' at Burlesque Bonanza charity event in Harrogate

## A glamorous Burlesque Bonanza event for charity is to be held in Harrogate this week with the calling card of “bonkers but make it sexy”.

This 18+ cabaret and variety show is the second event of its type this year to be hosted by Harrogate nurse Andy Dennis, renowned for volunteering for Médecins Sans Frontières/Doctors Without Borders (MSF).

Taking place at the Manhattan Club on Friday, October 2 at 6.30pm, this entertaining adult night of the weird and wonderful and outrageous will raise funds for MSF, a charity which provides life-saving, humanitarian medical care around the world.

Tickets are going fast after the success of last February’s Burlesque Bonanza, which raised more than £2,000 for MSF.

![Taking place at the Manhattan Club in Harrogate on Friday, October 2 at 6.30pm, this adult night of sheer pizzazz and outrageous entertainment will raise funds for MSF. (Picture Contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/people/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


Andy Dennis has travelled the world volunteering with life-saving charity Médecins Sans Frontières/Doctors Without Borders (MSF), including during the Ebola Outbreak in West Africa, as well as working in conflict zones in Uganda and South Sudan.

In conjunction with his wife Tracey, he has also accomplished a number of epic charity fundraising challenges in recent years, including walking 1,500 miles from Amsterdam to Barcelona and cycling across America.
