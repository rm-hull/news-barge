A man has been arrested after officers found "several hundred tablets and pills" at his home.

The arrest took place during a proactive patrol in Halifax town centre, where officers stopped a man behaving suspiciously in an area known for drug-related activity.

They recovered Class C drugs and suspected stolen items at the scene.

A further search of the suspect’s home led to the discovery of several hundred tablets and pills.

The man has been released under investigation as enquiries continue.

The tablets and pills seized are undergoing forensic examination.

## Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "We continue to work closely with businesses, residents, and visitors, and encourage anyone with information about crime or anti-social behaviour to report it to us.

"We remain committed to making Halifax a welcoming place for everyone and will continue conducting high-visibility patrols and taking positive action against those involved in criminality."
