# Man, 32, dies after Bradford crash as police appeal for driver to come forward

## A 32-year-old man has died in hospital after a crash involving an off-road motorbike and a vehicle in Bradford.

Officers were called by the ambulance service at around 8.10pm on Monday (Sep 21) following reports of a crash involving a dark-coloured vehicle and an off-road motorbike at the junction of Bolton Road and Leeds Road.

When officers arrived, the vehicle had left the scene and the off-road motorbike had been removed by an unknown person.

The 32-year-old rider of the motorbike was found at the roadside with serious head injuries and was taken to hospital.

Police have confirmed that he died on Wednesday (Sep 30).

![The junction of Bolton Road and Leeds Road.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/news/crime/22&crop=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


Police have launched an investigation are appealing directly to the driver of the dark-coloured vehicle, as well as the person who removed the off-road motorbike, to come forward.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision or what happened after the collision or anyone with Dash-cam or mobile phone footage of the incident is also asked to come forward.

“Information can be given via the Live Chat online or via 101 quoting log 1603 of 21 September.

“Also, you can give information via Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online.”
