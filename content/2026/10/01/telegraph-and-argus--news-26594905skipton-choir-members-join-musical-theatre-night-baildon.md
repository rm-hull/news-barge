A CHOIR brought the magic of the West End to a Yorkshire stage.

The All Together Now Musical Theatre Choir, including members from Skipton, performed at Baildon Methodist Church on Friday, September 25, delivering a night of musical theatre favourites to an audience.

Songs included One Short Day from Wicked and the emotional Tell Me It's Not True.

The concert was led by musical director Chris Kemp and included Skipton choir members.

In addition to the performances, the evening raised funds for charities including Bradford Baby Bank, the Immanuel Project, Our Angels Charity and Support Group, and Homestart Craven.

One audience member said: "I felt uplifted by the whole evening."

A member of the choir said: "We brought the West End and Broadway to Baildon."

Rehearsals for the choir are held in Harden on Wednesday evenings and in Baildon on Friday mornings.
