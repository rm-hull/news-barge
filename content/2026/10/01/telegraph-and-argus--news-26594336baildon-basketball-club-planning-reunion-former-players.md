BAILDON Basketball Club is planning a reunion.

The club, which played at Baildon Recreation Centre, was active for 35 years until 2020.

During their time, they ran a senior men’s team, a junior men’s team and a lady’s team. The teams played in the Bradford and Leeds Basketball Leagues.

Ex-players are hoping to hold a reunion on Thursday, October 29, 2026, and are inviting former members to attend.

For more details about the reunion, former members and interested parties are encouraged to reach out to Tim Beaumont via email at [timbeaumontsemail@gmail.com.](mailto:timbeaumontsemail@gmail.com.)
