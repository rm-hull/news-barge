A new trader has arrived at a popular Middlesbrough food and entertainment hub, expanding its growing collection of independent food vendors.

Street food trader The Flaming Tattie has opened at STACK Middlesbrough in the unit formerly occupied by Bao Down.

The vendor specialises in loaded jacket potatoes, and is already serving customers from its new home in Exchange Square.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18864118.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Announcing the arrival on social media, STACK Middlesbrough said: "She's gorgeous, she's divine and she's absolutely LOADED.

"The Flaming Tattie is our newest trader at STACK and don't mess about when it comes to fresh, loaded, flavourful tatties."

STACK said the new trader would be "a serious upgrade" for its food offering, serving one of the UK's best-loved comfort foods, with a wide variety of toppings including chicken Caesar, chicken parmo and classic cheesy beans.

For those looking for something with a kick, the Pork and Corn Star and Cheesy Chilli Crunch loaded potatoes add a fiery twist, featuring jalapenos and chilli respectively.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21524882.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Flaming Tattie is now serving loaded jacket potatoes at STACK Middlesbrough. *(Image: STACK Middlesbrough)*

Customers can also build their own loaded potato, choosing from nine toppings and adding up to three fillings to create their ideal dish.

Extras include crispy onions, parmesan and fajita tortilla crunch, alongside drizzles such as mayonnaise, sriracha and hot honey.

Coleslaw can also be added for an extra £1.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21524883.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chicken Caesar loaded potato. *(Image: STACK Middlesbrough)*

The loaded potatoes are priced between £6.50 and £9.50, depending on the toppings chosen, while sprinkles and drizzles cost an additional £1.50.

STACK said The Flaming Tattie is also offering a £6 lunch deal between noon and 3pm, Monday to Friday, which includes a build-your-own potato with two toppings.

The Flaming Tattie is now open at STACK Middlesbrough and joins a growing line-up of independent food and drink traders.

The venue hosts regular live music, DJs, family events, themed celebrations and major sporting screenings in a covered, outdoor event space throughout the year.

The complex can accommodate 650 people indoors and a further 150 outside, with 25 shipping containers housing five street-food traders, three bars, a stage and a large television screen.

Since opening in 2024, STACK has become a popular choice for dining, entertainment and social events, attracting visitors from across Teesside and beyond.
