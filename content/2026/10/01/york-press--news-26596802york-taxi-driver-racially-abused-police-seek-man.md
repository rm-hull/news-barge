A taxi driver has been racially abused in York.

North Yorkshire Police has issued a photograph of a man officers want to speak to about the incident at about 7pm on Sunday, September 6.

The force said a man subjected the taxi driver to “repeated racial abuse” during the journey and was dropped off outside the Nags Head in Heworth Road.

A police spokesperson said the man pictured may have information to help the investigation.

“Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured in the CCTV image,” they said.

Anyone who recognises him is asked to email [Benjamin.Portwine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Benjamin.Portwine@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260175133 when providing information.
