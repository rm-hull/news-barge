FIVE businesses have expanded their footprint at Dean Clough.

The growth has taken place at the historic mixed-use estate in Halifax, known for its flexible workspaces.

Among the expanding companies is global equipment management firm EMS, which has increased its office space by 25 per cent to 3,000 sq ft as part of its rapid growth programme.

The Chef Benson-Smith Academy, founded by Michelin-trained chef Matthew Benson-Smith, has also expanded to support higher-level training in hospitality and catering.

Jeremy Hall, chairman and managing director of Dean Clough Ltd, said: "It’s wonderful to see the expansion of so many businesses here.

"The vitality of Dean Clough is driven by the incredible array of diverse, dynamic, aspirant business who make Dean Clough their home.

"We are delighted to have the opportunity to work with so many great companies and individuals."

Sew Different, an international sewing retreat, has increased its space by 50 per cent to 1,500 sq ft to accommodate more workstations, displays, and a new shop area.

Dean Clough is home to around 150 businesses employing more than 3,500 people.

The site offers a mix of Grade A offices, retail, leisure, and cultural amenities, including six galleries and a theatre.
