Sand Lane in Wilberfoss will be closed to through traffic from October 26, 2026, for road works.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council intends to make a traffic order under Section 14(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

The closure will cover Sand Lane from its junction with the A1079 to its junction with Newbridge Lane, Yapham. It will begin on October 26 and is due to continue until November 5.

The works will involve bend strengthening and patching on Sand Lane.

The council said the closure is needed to allow the work to be completed while ensuring public safety.

The order will be valid for 18 months, although the council anticipates that the work will take 11 days.

Sand Lane will be closed to all through traffic during the works.

Access will remain available for residents, emergency vehicles and pedestrians.

Traffic will be diverted along Newbridge Lane, Yapham, Bolton Lane, Wilberfoss, and the A1079 Hull Road. The diversion will be signed when the closure begins.

Further information is available from the council’s Highways Customer Care team on 01482 393939.

The notice reference is CB.
