Part of Thixendale Road will be temporarily closed for fibre network installation.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has announced the closure for safety reasons during the works.

The closure will be in effect from 8am on October 29, 2026, until 5pm on November 2, 2026.

The affected section runs from Millington Glebe Farm to the junction with Church Lane.

The works are part of the installation of the new BDUK fibre network.

The order for the closure is valid for 18 months, but the council anticipates the work will be done within five days.

A diversion route will be clearly signposted at the time of closure, directing traffic via Thixendale Road, Back Street, Main Street, A166 York Road, A166 Garrowby Hill, South Wold, and Roman Road.

This alternative route will affect Thixendale CP, Fridaythorpe CP, and Wolds Weighton Ward.

The road will be closed to all through traffic.

For more information, you can contact the council's Highways Customer Care on 01482 393939, quoting reference CB.
