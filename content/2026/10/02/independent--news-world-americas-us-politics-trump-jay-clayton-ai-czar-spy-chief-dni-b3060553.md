Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton is expected to become US President Donald Trump's primary adviser on artificial intelligence, a role previously referred to by the president as "AI czar," according to reports.

Clayton currently leads 18 US intelligence agencies as the chief spy for the nation and is anticipated to maintain that post.

In response to inquiries regarding Clayton's selection, a White House official told *Reuters*: "Any personnel announcement will be announced directly by the president," adding, "Any reporting until then is baseless speculation."

![Jay Clayton appears before the Senate Intelligence Committee during his confirmation hearing on Capitol Hill July 15, 2026 in Washington, DC](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/19/18/GettyImages-2285609348.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Trump's move to select a new AI czar follows warnings from AI researchers that individuals developing the technology believe it could kill humans within a decade.

Last month, Trump stated he planned to appoint a new "AI czar" and establish an "AI force," though he provided no specific details about either proposal or how they would be carried out.

This marks the second occasion Trump has designated an adviser to handle AI-related decisions across his administration.

Venture capitalist David Sacks held the AI czar role early in Trump's second term prior to leaving the White House. Sacks continues to counsel Trump on AI matters and participated in a Tuesday meeting with the president and sector executives.

The report regarding an AI czar was initially disclosed by *CBS.*
