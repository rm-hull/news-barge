Police are investigating a suspected ram raid in York.

It was at a restaurant on Lindsey Avenue.

The car was abandoned at the scene.

#### Lindsey Avenue, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Officers are investigating a suspected ram raid at a restaurant on Lindsey Avenue in York.

“The report was made at 12.30am this morning (Friday). Officers attended immediately and found a Nissan Juke abandoned at the premises.

“A vehicle which was believed to be travelling in convoy with the Juke was stopped later this morning and two people were arrested by West Yorkshire Police.”

Anyone who witnessed the incident or who has relevant dashcam footage is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference number 12260193338.
