Dogs of all shapes and sizes descended on Raby Castle as the popular Bark in the Park returned for another day of four-legged fun.

The County Durham attraction welcomed pooches and their owners on Sunday, September 27, for the annual celebration dedicated to man's best friend.

Running from 10am until 4pm, the event brought together dog lovers from across the region for a packed programme of activities in the grounds of Raby Castle, Park and Gardens.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21531319.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bark in the Park at Raby Castle *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

One of the highlights was the fun dog show, which gave proud owners the chance to put their pets in the spotlight.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21531326.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bark in the Park at Raby Castle *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Rather than focusing solely on pedigree, the light-hearted competition featured playful categories including Most Like Their Owner and Coolest Canine/Pup.

Dogs and their owners could also watch agility demonstrations and pick up tips and advice during interactive training sessions.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21531332.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bark in the Park at Raby Castle *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A host of independent traders set up stalls around the event, offering everything from gourmet dog treats and toys to leads and outdoor accessories.

There were plenty of opportunities for visitors to explore the wider Raby estate too.

Admission to Bark in the Park included access to the Deer Park and Raby's five-acre Walled Gardens, giving owners the chance to enjoy an autumn walk with their dogs.

The event provided plenty of memorable sights as dogs ranging from tiny pups to much larger breeds explored the grounds and posed for pictures.

Bark in the Park has become a popular fixture in Raby Castle's events calendar, bringing together dogs and their owners for a day celebrating all things canine.
