**What will the weather be like in the North East today?**

According to the Met Office, today will be cloudy and mostly dry to start, rain developing widely later.

**Today:** Partly cloudy to start with the odd spot of rain, mostly over the hills. Cloud builds through the morning with rain moving in from the northwest and turning heavy in places by the late afternoon. Breezy with temperatures around average. Maximum temperature 17 °C.

**Tonight:** A wet evening, with further heavy bursts possible across the region. The rain clears after midnight, with a cool, dry and partly cloudy night to follow. Easing winds. Minimum temperature 4 °C.
