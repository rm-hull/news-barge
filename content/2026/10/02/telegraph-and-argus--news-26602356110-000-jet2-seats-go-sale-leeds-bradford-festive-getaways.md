Jet2 has launched its latest programme, offering travellers a wide selection of city breaks and getaways.

The airline is offering more than 110,000 seats from Leeds Bradford Airport for its Winter 2027/28 Cities and Christmas Markets programme, giving customers and travel agents in Yorkshire access to 11 destinations across Europe.

The programme features five Christmas market routes and six scheduled city routes, with flights operating at least twice weekly during the festive season.

Steve Heapy, CEO of Jet2, said: "Christmas Market and winter city breaks are a firm favourite with our customers, whether they want the traditional festive experience in cities such as Berlin, Cologne, Copenhagen, Tallinn and Vienna or the chance to get away for a seasonal city break. That is why we have put over 110,000 seats on sale from Leeds Bradford Airport, including five fantastic Christmas Markets routes, giving customers in Yorkshire great choice and flexibility when booking. By putting the programme on sale now, customers can get their plans in the diary early and look forward to a fantastic getaway next winter."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21530500.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jet2 has launched its Winter 2027/28 Cities and Christmas Markets programme *(Image: Jet2)*

Destinations in the Winter 27/28 schedule include Barcelona, Budapest, Paris, Rome, Krakow, and Prague, alongside Christmas market routes to Berlin, Cologne, Copenhagen, Tallinn, and Vienna.

Many of the routes are exclusive to Jet2 from Leeds Bradford Airport.

Flights to Barcelona will operate twice a week on Mondays and Fridays.

Berlin, Cologne, and Vienna will also see twice-weekly Christmas market services on the same days.

Tallinn flights will depart on Thursdays and Sundays.

Copenhagen will be served twice weekly on Thursdays and Sundays.

Budapest, Rome, and Krakow will all be served twice weekly on Mondays and Fridays.

Paris will see up to four flights a week on Mondays, Thursdays, Fridays, and Sundays.

Prague flights will operate on Thursdays and Sundays, with extra services on Mondays and Fridays during the festive season.

The expanded winter programme is part of Jet2’s broader seasonal offering, which currently includes Winter 26/27, Summer 27 and Winter 27/28.
