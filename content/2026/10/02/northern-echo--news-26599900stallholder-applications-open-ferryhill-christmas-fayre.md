Ferryhill is gearing up for the festive season as preparations get under way for the town's annual Christmas Fayre and Christmas lights switch-on.

The event will take place on Friday, November 27, from 1pm to 6pm, and will include a Christmas Market in the town centre car park.

A spokesman for Ferryhill Town Council said: "Preparations are currently underway for Ferryhill’s Annual Christmas Fayre and official switch on of the town’s christmas lights."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21527044.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Applications are open for Ferryhill Christmas Fayre *(Image: Supplied)*

Stallholders are encouraged to apply early.

Tombola stalls have already been allocated for this year’s event.

Application forms can be collected from Ferryhill Town Hall or requested by emailing [kyounghusband@ferryhill.gov.uk.](mailto:kyounghusband@ferryhill.gov.uk.)

All completed applications must be returned to the town council by Monday, November 9.

Stalls cost £10 for those providing their own, or £12 for those requiring a council stall.

A spokesman for Ferryhill Town Council said: "Look out for more information about other attractions taking place at Ferryhill’s Christmas Fayre in the coming weeks."
