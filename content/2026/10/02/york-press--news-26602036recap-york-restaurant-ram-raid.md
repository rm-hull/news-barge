## What we know so far

North Yorkshire Police was called to reports of a ram raid of Zeera Spice in Lindsey Avenue, York at around 12.30am on Friday (October 2).

A spokesperson said a Nissan Juke was found abandoned at the premises.

A photo taken from the scene revealed that the car had smashed through the front window.

Police placed a cordon around the scene, awaiting the arrival of North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, who inspected the scene and left it with the police.

A local business owner said the car had been removed from the scene by the time she arrived for work at around 7.30am.

Police have since launched an appeal for more information.

A force spokesperson said: "A vehicle which was believed to be travelling in convoy with the Juke was stopped later this morning and two people were arrested by West Yorkshire Police.

“Anyone who witnessed the incident or who has relevant dashcam footage is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference number 12260193338.”

A resident said he had not heard the incident, but was brought to it after he saw flashing lights through his window.

He said: "I think the car must have pulled up right to the bollards outside and reversed straight in."

Officers and one police van was still at the scene shortly after 11.30am.

Zeera Spice had been boarded up and a pane of smashed glass was seen outside the restaurant entrance.

More smashed glass and debris was seen close to the bins adjacent.

A bin was also damaged.

## Silver vehicle crashes into building

In what is believed to be a separate incident a silver car appears to have crashed into the side of the building at Zeera Spice.

A resident said the incident took place on Sunday (September 27).

The video shows the vehicle's airbags activated.

The driver was not at the scene.

North Yorkshire Police have been approached for comment.
