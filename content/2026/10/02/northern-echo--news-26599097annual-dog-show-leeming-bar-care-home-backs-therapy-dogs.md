Residents, families and their dogs came together at a care home event that raised money for a therapy dog charity.

Leeming Bar Grange Care Home held its annual dog show in September, raising £151 for Jack and Pals Therapy Dogs, which brings companionship and happiness to people in need.

The categories included “Dog Who Most Looks Like Their Owner”, “Most Endearing Eyes” and “Best Sausage Catcher”.

![Care home dog show](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21526307.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Enjoying Leeming Bar Grange Care Home’s annual dog show *(Image: Supplied)*

Kathryn Billett, general manager at Leeming Bar Grange, said: “Residents and the team alike love seeing our wonderful therapy dogs each week.

"Dogs truly are man’s best friend; it was incredible to see everyone’s faces light up as they watched all the dogs showcase their best tricks and adorable personalities."

Resident Alma said she had a special connection to the event.

She said: “I have such a bond with dogs and even used to rescue them.

![Care home dog show](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21526308.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the winners *(Image: Supplied)*

“My own rescue dog came in today to enter the competition and I’m so delighted to announce she won.

The care home said everyone was delighted and thanked the dogs and owners who took part.

Jack and Pals Therapy Dogs provide companionship and happiness to people who need support.

The charity and care home thanked everyone who supported the event.

![Care home dog show](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21526306.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Putting in an appearance at the Leeming Bar Grange Care Home’s annual dog show *(Image: Supplied)*

The home provides residential, dementia and respite care for 60 residents.
