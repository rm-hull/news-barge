AN investigation has been launched into a ram raid at a restaurant in York.

The incident occurred at Zeera Spice – an Indian restaurant in Lindsey Avenue, Holgate, at around 12.30am on Friday (October 2).

North Yorkshire Police found a Nissan Juke abandoned at the premises.




It appeared to have crashed through the restaurant’s front window while reversing, leaving glass strewn across the pavement.

![A Nissan Juke appears to have reversed through the window front ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21531583.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A Nissan Juke appears to have reversed through the window front *(Image: Newsquest)*

Police placed a cordon at the scene while awaiting the arrival of North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service.

An eyewitness said: “I didn’t hear anything, but I saw the flashing lights in my window.

“I came down and saw that the car had smashed through the window.

“It appeared that it had pulled up to the bollards and reversed through.”

![A smashed window pane was seen on the pavement outside the restaurant](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21531580.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A smashed window pane was seen on the pavement outside the restaurant *(Image: Newsquest)*

A local business owner said the vehicle had been removed from the scene before she arrived at work at around 7.30am on Friday morning.

Police said officers had become aware of a separate incident after video footage emerged which appeared to show a silver car that had crashed into the building of Zeera Spice on the night of Sunday (September 27).

An eyewitness said: “I saw the car, which had crashed into the bins area outside the restaurant.

“It had its airbags activated.”

![The airbags of the car had been activated, an eyewitness said](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21531582.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The airbags of the silver car involved in an incident on September 27 had been activated, an eyewitness said *(Image: Newsquest)*

The force is now investigating whether the two incidents are linked.

An appeal has also been launched for further information after a vehicle which was believed to be travelling in convoy with the Juke was stopped on Friday (October 2).

Two people were later arrested by West Yorkshire Police.

Anyone who witnessed or who has relevant dashcam footage is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference number 12260193338.
