# Jamie set for special Starbeck homecoming after 500-mile challenge for St John Ambulance

## Jamie set to be welcomed home after 500-mile challenge

A determined young man from Starbeck is set to receive a special welcome home as he completes an inspirational 500-mile challenge which has raised over £2,500 for St John Ambulance.

![Jamie, pictured with dad Dale, is set for special Starbeck homecoming after inspirational 500-mile challenge for St John Ambulance.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/0&crop=&width=990&quality=65.jpg)


Jamie Thurlow, 23, is due to complete his challenge at 6.50pm on Wednesday 7 October 2026, arriving at St Andrew’s Church Hall car park in Starbeck, just one day before celebrating his 24th birthday.

A spokesperson for St John Ambulance said: “Jamie started his challenge on St John’s Day, Wednesday 24 June 2026, aiming to complete the equivalent of the journey from Starbeck to St John’s Gate in Clerkenwell, London, and back again.

“The challenge was carefully designed around Jamie’s abilities, with the 500 miles completed in manageable stages.

“Alongside riding a tandem bicycle with his dad, Dale Thurlow, Jamie has also been swimming and using a rowing machine at the gym to build up the mileage.”

![Jamie and his dad Dale riding tandem on part of the 500-mile challenge.](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/0&crop=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


Jamie lives with Koolen-de Vries syndrome, learning difficulties, epilepsy and kyphoscoliosis.

Following a bleed on the brain, he also experiences variable muscle tone, making coordination and spatial awareness particularly challenging.

Despite the additional concentration and energy physical activities require, Jamie has approached his challenge with enthusiasm and determination.

Although Jamie has limited speech, he communicates confidently using gestures and his adaptive iPad, allowing his personality and determination to shine through.

Jamie’s relationship with St John Ambulance stretches back many years.

He first joined Starbeck Badgers, later progressed through the Cadets and is now a member of the St John Fellowship, which he attends with the support of his carer.

Throughout his time with St John, Jamie and his family have experienced the organisation’s welcoming, inclusive and supportive environment, and the 500-mile challenge has been their way of giving something back.

Jamie’s family stated: “St John Ambulance has been a huge part of Jamie’s life.

“The support, encouragement and inclusion he has received over the years have meant so much to him and our family.

“This challenge is our way of saying thank you while raising vital funds to help St John Ambulance continue supporting others.

“Every mile of Jamie’s extraordinary challenge represents not just a fundraising achievement, but his resilience, determination and appreciation for an organisation that has played such an important part in his life.”

Now, after months of effort, the final few metres will be particularly special as Jamie arrives home to flags, bunting, friends and supporters ready to celebrate what he has achieved.

Waiting to welcome Jamie home will be members of the Starbeck St John Ambulance Youth and Fellowship, who will line the outside of the church with flags and bunting to celebrate his achievement.

St John Ambulance Area and County team members have also been invited, together with supporters who have followed and encouraged Jamie through the Jamie’s Challenge Facebook page.

Adding to the celebrations, the Knaresborough Town Crier Mark Hunter will be joining the welcome and giving Jamie and his dad, Dale, a suitably big shout as they reach the end of their remarkable journey.

Following Jamie’s arrival, there will be a short presentation inside the church hall, with refreshments and a special cake to mark Jamie’s birthday.
