ENTHUSIASTS are gearing up for the latest supercar event taking place in North Yorkshire this weekend.

The Motorist in Sherburn in Elmet, south of Tadcaster, is hosting its popular Supercar Day, featuring a wide range of high-performance vehicles on Saturday, October 3, 2026.

![Enthusiasts descended on The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet, for its Supercar Day in August 2026. (Image Newsquest)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21523542.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Enthusiasts descended on The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet, for its Supercar Day in August 2026. *(Image: The Press)*

Kicking off at 9am and running until 4pm, car enthusiasts can expect to see brands such as Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Porsche, Aston Martin, and Audi R8 at the event which is being billed as the CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender.

![Enthusiasts descended on The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet, for its Supercar Day in August 2026. (Image Newsquest)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21523543.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A wide range of high-performance vehicles are expected again this weekend, as featured in August at The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet. *(Image: The Press)*

The event will also see experts from CTEK, renowned for its battery care and charging technology, on hand to give live product demonstrations and offer advice on how to best look after your car's battery and charging system.

Event organisers say they are expecting a variety of vehicles to be on display, ranging from modern performance icons and rare classics to limited-production models and heavily-specified examples.

![Enthusiasts descended on The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet, for its Supercar Day in August 2026. (Image Newsquest)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21523544.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet, hosts a variety of events throughout the year. *(Image: The Press)*

A spokesperson said: "Our Supercar events regularly bring some extraordinary machinery through the gates, and this one should be no different.  
  
"Expect everything from modern performance icons and rare classics to limited-production models, heavily specified examples and cars you simply don’t see every day. Part of the fun is never quite knowing what’s going to arrive next.  
  
"Bring the camera. You’re probably going to need it."

Supercar owners are being encouraged to bring their vehicles to join the display.

Whether it’s a modern exotic, an analogue classic, or a more unusual model, all are welcome.

![Enthusiasts descended on The Motorist, Sherburn in Elmet, for its Supercar Day in August 2026. (Image Newsquest)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21523545.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Supercar owners are being encouraged to bring their vehicles to join the display. *(Image: The Press)*

The organisers have also called upon supercar clubs and owners' groups to get in touch early to reserve space.

The CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender is also an opportunity to meet the people behind the vehicles, discover some of the technology keeping them on the road, and enjoy the company of fellow car enthusiasts.

Throughout the day, attendees can explore the CTEK takeover, browse The Motorist Store, and enjoy various happenings across the venue.

The Drivers Lounge will be open throughout, offering breakfast, lunch, coffee, and drinks.

Organisers recommend booking a table in advance due to the expected busy turnout.

The spokesperson said: "The CTEK Charge Your Car Weekender is about more than filling the tarmac.

"It’s a chance to get closer to incredible machinery, meet the people behind the cars, discover some of the technology keeping them on the road and spend a day surrounded by people who are every bit as obsessed with cars as you are."

The event is free to enter, and no general admission booking is required.
