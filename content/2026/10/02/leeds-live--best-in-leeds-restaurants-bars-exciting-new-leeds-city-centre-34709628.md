# Exciting new Leeds city centre burger bar to replace Five Guys

## Work is ongoing ahead of the new Leeds city centre opening

An exciting new burger bar will replace Five Guys in Leeds city centre.

We have previously brought you news of Five Guys, the business itself famous for its burgers, shutting. The fast food giant previously operated out of a unit in Duncan Street.

When we brought you news of Five Guys shutting, in March, a notice read: “We’re sorry, this Five Guys is now closed.

“Visit us in Trinity Leeds.” Boards have gone up which state Urban Burger will soon open in the former Five Guys unit.

You can currently find Urban Burger branches across South Yorkshire.
