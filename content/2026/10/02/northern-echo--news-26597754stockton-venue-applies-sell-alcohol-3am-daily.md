Plans to keep a North East town centre venue open later have been submitted to a council, according to a public notice.

Magnum Properties (NE) Limited, which owns a property on Prince Regent Street, Stockton, has applied to StocktonCouncil to sell alcohol on and off the premises from 10am to 3am every day.

The application also seeks permission to sell alcohol continuously from New Year’s Eve into New Year’s Day.

Alcohol can currently be sold at the venue only from 6pm.

The venue would also be able to offer regulated entertainment and late-night refreshments from 11pm to 3am each day if the application is approved.

Those services would also continue overnight from New Year’s Eve into New Year’s Day.

The proposal would amend the current plan and replace the operating-schedule conditions.

Residents, businesses and responsible authorities can have a say on the application.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Written objections must reach Stockton Council’s Licensing Service by October 26, 2026.

The public can view the application at Dunedin House, Columbia Drive, Thornaby, Stockton-on-Tees, from 9am to 4pm on weekdays, excluding bank holidays.

The documents are also available online at stockton.gov.uk.

Views must be submitted by October 26, to the council at Dunedin House, Columbia Drive, Thornaby, Stockton-on-Tees, TS17 6BJ, or by email to [licensing@stockton.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing@stockton.gov.uk.)
