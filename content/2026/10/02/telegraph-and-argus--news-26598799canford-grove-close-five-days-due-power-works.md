NORTHERN Powergrid works will temporarily close a road in Bradford.

Canford Grove in Allerton will be closed from its junction with Canford Drive to outside No. 3 Canford Grove.

This closure is due to works by Northern PowerGrid.

Pedestrian access will still be available, but no alternative routes for vehicles will be provided.

The closure will start at 9.30am on October 12 and is expected to last until 6pm on October 16.

However, it is stated that the closure could stay in place for up to 18 months if needed.

The City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council is handling the road closure order, which is under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 - Section 14.

Only vehicles used in connection with the Northern PowerGrid works will be allowed through.
