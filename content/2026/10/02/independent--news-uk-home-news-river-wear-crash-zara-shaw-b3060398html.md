The family of a teenager who died after a car plunged into the River Wear have paid tribute to their “beautiful girl”.

Zara Shaw, 16, died after the vehicle went into the water near Wearmouth Bridge, Sunderland, on Tuesday.

Jackson Duke, 17, and Luke Etheridge, 16, also died in the crash.

A 17-year-old boy who was also in the car “reached safety” and sustained non-serious injuries, the force said.

He was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving, and has since been released on bail pending further inquiries.

![Zara Shaw had a ‘zest for life’, her family said](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/02/13/01M3Y8T03E4J2KY6280E3BP55Z.jpg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

On Friday, Zara’s family issued a statement through Northumbria Police, which said: “Zara, you came into this world with a fight, and we know you left it doing the same.

“You had a zest for life and life loved you.

“Created as a companion for your sister, Olivia, who loves you deeply and unconditionally.

“You made us proud from day one, you studied and learnt new skills and made lifetime friends.

“Having fun along the way with your dance videos, infectious laugh and smile, you have always been our beloved daughter, sister, granddaughter, niece, cousin, girlfriend and best friend.”

![Police divers at the scene on the River Wear in Sunderland](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/30/14/31b231d4dd1f8dab02cf3d242738472aY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkwODU5MDI4-2.86024569.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The family said: “To everyone who has been in Zara’s life, we thank you from the bottom of our hearts. You made her what she became.

“At the worst time of our lives we thank each and every one of you for kindness and good wishes. We are humbled and grateful.

“A tragedy has taken you but our memories remain blessed with having you as our daughter.

“Rest safe our beautiful girl, Zara.”

Zara was from Houghton-le-Spring, Tyne and Wear, while Jackson and Luke were from the Sunderland area.

![A car is removed from the River Wear in Sunderland](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/30/15/7bfbfb3bc18818558f2f14c77a777373Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkwODY1Mzg4-2.86025343.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Zara’s family also praised the liaison officers from Northumbria Police and the emergency services called to the scene.

They said: “Thank you for not making our child a number but a child treated with the utmost care and not left alone.

“I know this will impact you all and the hope is you feel supported, as we do.”

Police received reports that a black Audi A3 had careered off the road at Panns Bank before entering the water shortly before 1.30pm on Tuesday.

The force said investigations into what led up to the car entering the water, and to establish who was driving at the time, were ongoing, and no other vehicles are believed to have been involved.

Anyone with information, or CCTV or dashcam footage of the incident, is asked to contact the force quoting reference number NP-20260929-0589.
