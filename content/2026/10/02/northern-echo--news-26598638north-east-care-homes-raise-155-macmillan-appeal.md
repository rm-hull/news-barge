Residents, staff and volunteers from three North East care homes joined forces to raise money at this year's Macmillan Coffee Morning.

Mandale House in Thornaby, Ingleby Care Home in Ingleby Barwick and Hazelgrove Court in Saltburn raised £155 for the nationwide campaign supporting nearly 3.5 million people living with cancer in the UK.

The Hill Care Group homes held the coffee morning at Ingleby Care Home on Lamb Lane, with a tombola, raffle and games to guess the teddy bear's name and the cake's weight.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21525199.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Barry Taylor, 86, a resident at Hazelgrove Court Care Home, Saltburn, enjoys the Macmillan Coffee Morning event at Ingleby Care Home, Ingleby Barwick *(Image: Supplied)*

Sue Dixon, Catherine Sunderland and Jean Simpson from St Francis of Assisi Church Friendship Group joined in with homemade bakes.

Margaret Johnson, 92, of Ingleby Care Home, said: "I was so happy to win guess the name of the teddy.

"I had a teddy just like it once."

Maureen Sellenthin, 85, of Mandale House, said: "I thoroughly enjoyed the games and helping out, as it is for such a great cause."

Barry Taylor, 86, of Hazelgrove Court, said: "It was great going along to Ingleby to help out for this great cause.

"I was delighted we raised £155."

Lindsey Levick, activities coordinator at Ingleby Care Home, organised the event with Sharon Lewis of Hazelgrove Court and Leanne Johnson of Mandale House.

Ms Levick said: "It is great to get together with other care homes within the Hill Care Group to socialise and raise money for a great cause.

"Thank you to all those who helped organise and those who attended and helped raise such a fantastic amount for a charity close to all our hearts."

The annual Macmillan Coffee Morning campaign encourages people across the UK to host gatherings supporting Macmillan Cancer Support.
