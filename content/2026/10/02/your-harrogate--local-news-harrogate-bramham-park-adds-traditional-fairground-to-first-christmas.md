Bramham Park has announced a traditional fairground will be part of its first Christmas market this November.

The estate near Wetherby will welcome visitors on Saturday 14th and Sunday 15th November, with more than 100 artisan traders expected.

The newly announced attractions include a helter skelter, traditional swing boats, a mini swing carousel and a small train.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6abf6028b41c73dd773.jpg)

There will also be a traditional fairground organ and a selection of classic fairground games. Additional charges will apply for the fairground attractions.

Announcing the addition, a spokesperson for Bramham Park said:

> “Visitors can look forward to a Helter skelter, traditional swing boats, a mini swing carousel, a traditional fairground organ, a small train, and a selection of classic fairground games, creating even more festive fun for families to enjoy.”

The Christmas market will feature independent makers selling gifts and decorations in front of the house, with local food stands in the stable yard.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6abf6028028555034e8.jpg)

The Yorkshire Rock Choir will also perform carols outside the house from 12pm until 2pm on the Saturday.

*Early bird booking for the car park at £10 per car until 31st October. 1st November onward £15 per car. Entry to the market is free.  Visit www.Bramhampark.co.uk to find out more.*
