A man has died after being found seriously injured following a disturbance.

Police were called to Lister Hill in Horsforth at about 3.32pm on Wednesday.

The man was found needing emergency medical attention and taken to hospital, where he died on Thursday night, the force has confirmed.

Five people, aged between 44 and 74, were arrested on suspicion of assault. They have been released on bail while enquiries continue.

West Yorkshire Police’s Homicide and Major Enquiry Team is leading the investigation into the circumstances surrounding the disturbance.

Detectives are appealing for information, dashcam footage or mobile phone recordings.

Anyone who can help should contact police through Live Chat, quoting crime reference number 13260557050.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or using its website.

Police said enquiries remain ongoing into this incident at this time.
