A police cordon remains in place this afternoon following a crash between a scooter and a car.

The collision happened in Bradford on Harrogate Road just before midday.

The rider of the scooter, a 30-year-old man, suffered head injuries.

The road is closed between the Leeds Road and Norman Avenue junctions.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21531197.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Harrogate Road *(Image: T&A)*

## T&A visits the scene

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21531195.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Harrogate Road *(Image: T&A)*

The T&A visited the scene this afternoon.

A police officer at the scene said officers did not yet know the direction of travel.

## What the emergency services have said

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said it received the emergency call at 11.39am.

A spokesperson for YAS said: "Two ambulances, a team leader and a doctor were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to Leeds General Infirmary."

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "At 11.48am officers were called by the ambulance service to Harrogate Road to reports a car and scooter were in collision.

"The rider of the scooter, a 30-year-old man, suffered head injuries and has been taken to hospital."
