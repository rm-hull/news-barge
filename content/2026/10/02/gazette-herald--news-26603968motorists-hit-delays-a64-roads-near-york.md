The A64 eastbound near York has been hit with delays throughout the afternoon.

The AA stated that motorists were experiencing 10-minute delays, with an average speed of 10mph at lunchtime.

The heavy traffic has continued throughout the afternoon and has affected the evening rush hour.

Delays have also hit Malton Road near the LNER Community Stadium and the A166 near Stamford Bridge at rush hour.
