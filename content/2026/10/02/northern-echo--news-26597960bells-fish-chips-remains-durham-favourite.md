Fish and chips are woven into the North East’s identity—and few Durham names are better known than Bell’s.

Occupying a prime spot in Durham Market Place, the restaurant has become a fixture of city life, welcoming everyone from students and families to tourists exploring the cathedral city.

With its traditional menu, generous portions and longstanding reputation, Bell's has established itself as one of Durham's most popular destinations for a classic fish supper.

On any given day, customers can be seen filing through its doors for freshly battered fish, chips and mushy peas, either taking a seat in the restaurant or carrying a takeaway out into the historic market square.

Part of Bell's appeal is that it has never tried to reinvent the wheel.

In an era of ever-changing food trends, its focus remains firmly on delivering the dishes people know and love.

The restaurant's popularity is reflected in its strong reputation among visitors.

Bell's holds a 4.5-star rating on TripAdvisor from more than 1,300 reviews, earning recognition as a Travellers' Choice venue.

Among those singing its praises is one reviewer who described their meal as "great traditional fish and chips with friendly service", adding that the restaurant serves "generous portions of well cooked fish" in the heart of Durham city centre.

Another recent customer was equally enthusiastic, writing: "The fish and chips I had were cooked fresh, they were absolutely amazing.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20608044.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: PA MEDIA)*

The fish was beautiful and white and lovely tasting along with the chips and curry sauce." They added that it "could be my all-time favourite".

Its location has also helped drive its success.

A short walk from Durham Cathedral, Durham Castle and the River Wear, Bell’s gives visitors a traditional meal near the city’s historic sights.

Yet while tourists make up a sizeable part of its customer base, locals remain at the heart of the business.

Families return year after year, while childhood trips to Bell’s have become traditions for many Durham residents.

At a time when independent hospitality businesses continue to face rising costs and mounting pressures, Bell's enduring popularity highlights the lasting appeal of quality, consistency and good service.

For many people, Bell's is more than just somewhere to grab fish and chips.

It is a familiar part of Durham's city centre, a local institution that continues to thrive by sticking to what it does best.
