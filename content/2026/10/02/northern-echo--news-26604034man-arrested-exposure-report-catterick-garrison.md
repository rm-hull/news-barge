A man has been arrested following reports of exposure in the Catterick Garrison area, with police investigating whether it could be linked to other similar incidents.

North Yorkshire Police confirmed that a man in his 30s was arrested on Friday (October 2) following a report of exposure on Thursday (October 1) afternoon.

Officers carried out enquiries following the report and arrested the man today on suspicion of exposure.

He remains in police custody while enquiries continue.

A spokesperson for the police force said: "As part of our investigation, we are looking into whether yesterday’s incident could be linked to any similar reports in the area.
