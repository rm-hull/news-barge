A set of rock icons will headline the Piece Hall next summer.

Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators are coming to the Yorkshire venue in 2027.

Featuring Slash alongside vocalist Myles Kennedy, bassist Todd Kerns, drummer Brent Fitz and rhythm guitarist Frank Sidoris, the band has announced their new album Flying Blind and a world tour.

## How to get Slash tickets

They headline Live at The Piece Hall on Tuesday July 6.

Tickets go on general sale at 10am Friday, October 9 via ticketmaster.co.uk.

Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators are the latest artists announced for Live at The Piece Hall 2027 which will also see headliner shows from The Saw Doctors, Kaiser Chiefs, Rick Astley, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison as well as three nights with Simply Red.

Nicky Chance-Thompson MBE DL FRSA, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: “This is going to be another truly incredible night here at The Piece Hall.

"Slash, Miles and the band are globally adored rock icons and we are delighted to be welcoming them and their fans to Halifax.

“Rock music has provided some of the most memorable nights at The Piece Hall in recent years and we know this is going to be another epic evening in our historic courtyard.”
