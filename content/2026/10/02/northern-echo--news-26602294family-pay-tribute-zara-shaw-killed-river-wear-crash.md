The devastated family of a "beautiful" teenager who was tragically killed after an Audi plunged into the River Wear in Sunderland have paid tribute to her.

Zara Shaw, 16, from Newbottle, was among three teenagers who died after a black Audi A3 left the road at Panns Bank, near Wearmouth Bridge, and entered the water shortly before 1.30pm on Tuesday.

Paying tribute to her, her family said in a statement: “Zara, you came into this world with a fight, and we know you left it doing the same. You had a zest for life and life loved you.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522484.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jackson Duke, 17, and Luke Etheridge and Zara Shaw, both aged 16, were all in the car at that time. *(Image: NORTHUMBRIA POLICE)*

“Created as a companion for your sister, Olivia, who loves you deeply and unconditionally. You made us proud from day one, you studied and learnt new skills and made life time friends.

“Having fun along the way with your dance videos, infectious laugh and smile, you have always been our beloved daughter, sister, granddaughter, niece, cousin, girlfriend and best friend.

“To everyone who has been in Zara's life we thank you from the bottom of our hearts. You made her what she became.

“At the worst time of our lives we thank each and every one of you for kindness and good wishes.

“We are humbled and grateful. A tragedy has taken you but our memories remain blessed with having you as our daughter.

“Rest safe our beautiful girl, Zara.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21530426.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Zara Shaw, 16, from Newbottle *(Image: NORTHUMBRIA POLICE)*

The family added: “Lastly, to our Police Liaison Officers and all emergency services. Thank you for not making our child a number but a child treated with the utmost care and not left alone.

“I know this will impact you all and the hope is you feel supported, as we do.

“Forever in our thoughts, Zara. Always Mam, Dad & Olivia.”

Witnesses described seeing large numbers of emergency vehicles on both sides of the river, while RNLI lifeboats searched the water.

A coastguard helicopter and air ambulance were also involved in the operation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522477.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the incident *(Image: NORTH NEWS)*

Following the tragedy, Sunderland College said it was "deeply saddened" and is offering support to staff and students.

A spokesperson said: "We are deeply saddened to hear three students from our Bede Campus tragically lost their lives yesterday afternoon. This is a heartbreaking loss for our college community, and the wider city of Sunderland.

"Everyone at Sunderland College extends our heartfelt thoughts and deepest condolences to their families, friends, and loved ones at this incredibly difficult time.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522479.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the incident *(Image: NORTH NEWS)*

"We also pay tribute to the emergency services and all those who responded at the scene.

"Support is available to any students or our colleagues who may be affected by this tragedy and ask that the privacy of the families of those involved is respected."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21522478.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scene of the incident *(Image: NORTH NEWS)*

Yesterday, (October 1), Northumbria Police confirmed a 17-year-old boy who managed to escape to safety was released on bail after being arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.
