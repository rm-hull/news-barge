A Darlington nail technician says she was left “shocked” after being shortlisted as a finalist in the Northern Echo's Best Beauty Salon Awards.

Katelyn Jenkinson, 24, has worked in the nail industry for eight years and became self-employed two-and-a-half years ago.

After building a loyal client base and regularly filling her diary, she has been named one of the competition’s finalists.

Katelyn said she never planned a career in nails.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21525778.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nail Serenity pictured Katelyn Jenkinson. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

She said: “When I left school, I didn't know what I wanted to do. I just kind of landed in nails.

“I got a job at a nail bar as a side income alongside college, where I was doing Health and Social Care, which was fine but I didn't love it.

“But I loved my job at the nail bar, so I decided to do nail technology and it just stemmed from there.

“I didn't think I would end up loving it so much, but I do.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21525779.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"I can't see myself doing anything else.”

Her passion for nails persuaded her to go it alone, despite fears about losing the security of a regular wage.

“I went self-employed two-and-a-half years ago and I don't regret it at all. In fact, I wish I'd done it sooner,” she said.

“A lot of clients followed me for my nails as well, which I was grateful for.”

Katelyn said she was surprised to be nominated for the Northern Echo award.

She said: “I'm shocked to be nominated.

"I didn't think I would be.

“It feels great that my work is being recognised.

"It feels nice to be nominated.

“I'm always so busy as well.

"I'm always fully booked, which I'm so grateful for, especially after only two-and-a-half years of doing it on my own.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21525782.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"I didn't think I'd be this full.”

She added: “Going self-employed was something I always wanted to do, but you get used to the monthly wage and it's scary to leave that and not know what you're going to get every month.

“But it worked out.

“I was petrified to do it, but I'm glad I did do it.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21525781.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*
