A large police cordon is currently in place on a Bradford road this evening.

Bierley Lane is shut due to an incident on School Street.

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene, a section of the road, including a pedestrian crossing, was cordoned off.

Police officers were also redirecting traffic in the Bierley area.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21533162.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police cordon in place in Bierley *(Image: Newsquest)*

The cordon had been attached to a lamppost.

The road is cordoned from Knights Bridge Walk.

The Telegraph & Argus has approached West Yorkshire Police for a comment.
