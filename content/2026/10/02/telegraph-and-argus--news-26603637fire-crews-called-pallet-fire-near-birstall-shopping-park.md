A blaze involving wooden pallets broke out near a retail park.

Fire crews were called to Gelderd Road, near Birstall Shopping Park, at midday on Friday.

Two crews attended the fire and extinguished it using hose reels and large jets.

There has been no cause established as yet.

Photos and videos posted on social media show fire engines attending a large blaze and plumes of smoke.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to reports of a fire at Gelderd road at 12:00 today.

"This was a 15x5m pallet fire.

"Two crews attended and extinguished the fire using hose reels and large jets.

"No cause recorded as yet."
