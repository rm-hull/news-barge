EMERGENCY services have been called to a vehicle fire in North Yorkshire.

Firefighters from Knaresborough rushed to the scene on the A6055, leading to the A1 in Minskip, at 10.09pm on Thursday (October 1).




A spokesperson from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “On arrival, the vehicle was well alight.”

North Yorkshire Police re-directed traffic away from the scene as fire crews worked to extinguish the blaze.

Traffic is flowing normally this morning.
