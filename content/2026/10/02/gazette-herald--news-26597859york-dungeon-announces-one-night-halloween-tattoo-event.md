A torture chamber tattoo studio is offering a one-night-only experience.

York Dungeon will transform its infamous Torture Room into a tattoo studio on Wednesday, October 21, as part of this year’s Halloween season.

The event celebrates the attraction’s new show, Mother Shipton: The Great Prophetess, and will see six people tattooed with exclusive Dungeon-inspired designs by local tattoo artist Emma Swanton of Swan Tattoos.

Mark Mattinson, Duty Manager at the York Dungeon, said: "We can’t wait to welcome six brave souls to be permanently marked in our Torture Room.

"This truly is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, and Emma Swanton has brought the spirit of Halloween and The York Dungeon to life in her exclusive tattoo designs.

"Plus, what better activity for a tattoo-loving fear fan than doing it for charity?

"Merlin’s Magic Wand does truly inspiring work, and we are thrilled to be showing our support in the spookiest way we know."

The tattoos cost £120 each, including a £90 non-refundable deposit and a £30 donation to York Dungeon’s charity partner, Merlin’s Magic Wand.

The designs include a crystal ball and a plague doctor and can be placed anywhere on either arm.

For more information, visit: [https://www.thedungeons.com/whats-inside/halloween-events/halloween-tattoo-pop-up/](https://www.thedungeons.com/whats-inside/halloween-events/halloween-tattoo-pop-up/)
