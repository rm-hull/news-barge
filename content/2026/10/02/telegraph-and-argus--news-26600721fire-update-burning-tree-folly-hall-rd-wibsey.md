The fire service have issued a comment following a large tree fire in Bradford.

The conifer, near Folly Hall Road, Wibsey, became engulfed in flames on Thursday evening.

Photos and video showed the tree burning as firefighters used a hose.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was called to the scene at 6.22pm.

The cause of the fire had not been determined just yet.

## What the fire service could say

A West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: "We were called at 1822 to Folly Hall Road yesterday to reports of a fire in the open.

"One crew attended and extinguished a fire in a tree using hose reels.

"No cause recorded as yet."
