Police are appealing for information to help find a missing teenager.

Jack, 17, was last seen in the Skipton Road area of Keighley on September 27.

He is described as around 5ft 11ins tall and of slim build.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers are making enquiries to locate him as there are concerns for his welfare."

Anyone with information is urged to contact police in Bradford.
