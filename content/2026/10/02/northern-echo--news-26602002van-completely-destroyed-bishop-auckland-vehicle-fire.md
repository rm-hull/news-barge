A van has been completely destroyed after flames engulfed it during a suspected arson attack in Bishop Auckland.

Emergency services were called to the vehicle fire in the Richardson Avenue area at about 10.30pm on Wednesday, September 30.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21530109.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The van fire in Bishop Auckland *(Image: FACEBOOK/ KARL GUARE)*

One crew from Bishop Auckland Fire Station attended the scene and used a hose to extinguish the blaze.

Firefighters brought the blaze under control and no injuries were reported.

Police have now confirmed that they are treating it as arson.

A spokesperson for County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue said: “At 10.39pm on Wednesday (September 30), we received a report of a van fire between Ramsey Crescent and Richardson Avenue.

"One crew from Bishop Auckland Fire Station attended and used a hose to extinguish the fire.

"Crews left the scene at 11.04pm”
