## Main city centre car park reopens and cordon lifted after alleged sexual assault

Coppergate Centre Car Park reopened this morning (Friday, October 2) *(Image: Newsquest)*

A MAIN city centre car park has reopened after a police incident.

Coppergate Centre Car Park in York was reopened following an alleged sexual assault at around 7pm on Wednesday (September 30).

The cordon has also been removed from the footpath connecting Piccadilly to the Castle Car Park.




It comes after North Yorkshire Police launched an appeal to find a man who may have important information to help the investigation into the sexual assault.

A police spokesperson said: “The victim continues to be supported by specially trained officers while detectives carry out urgent enquiries to establish the full circumstances surrounding what happened.

“As part of our investigation, we are working to identify a white man who was seen in the city centre around the time of the incident.

“He is described as wearing a pink T-shirt with a black circular logo on the front.

“We believe this man may have important information which could assist our investigation and are urging him to come forward and speak to us.”

![Police in Piccadilly, York, on Thursday (October 1) after a reported sexual assault the previous evening](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21527560.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police in Piccadilly, York, on Thursday (October 1) after a reported sexual assault the previous evening *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

Senior Investigating Officer Matt Wilkinson, of North Yorkshire Police, added: ““I would urge anyone who was in York city centre at around 7pm yesterday evening and saw or heard anything that could assist our investigation to contact us.

“Even if you think the information you have is insignificant, please get in touch. It could prove important to our enquiries.

“If you can help call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference number 12260192537.”
