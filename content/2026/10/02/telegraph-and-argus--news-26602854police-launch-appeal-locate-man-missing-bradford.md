Police are appealing to help locate a man reported missing from Bradford.

Thomas, 33, was last seen in the Travelodge in Shipley at about 7.36pm on Thursday, October 1.

West Yorkshire Police are appealing for information to help find him.

A spokesman for the force said: "Can you help our officers to locate Thomas, who has been reported missing from Bradford?

"He is said to be about 5ft 10ins tall and of slim build.

He was last seen wearing a grey tracksuit.

"Members of the public are advised not to approach him and to call 999 if he is seen, quoting log 1322 of 1 October."
