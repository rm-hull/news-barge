A MOPED was stolen from a York address in broad daylight earlier this week.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a moped was stolen from an address in Woodthorpe on Wednesday afternoon.

The theft involved a grey Honda moped being taken from Wharfe Drive on September 30 at about 3pm.

"It was reported that three youths with faces covered, were seen taking a moped from a garden, they were all wearing dark clothing," said a spokesperson for the force.

They added that police recovered the moped 30 minutes later near Acomb Woods.

Anyone who witnessed the theft, saw the moped being taken or ridden in the area, or has CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage, is asked to contact PC Nabiel Galab at [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk), on the website, or by calling 101.

Anonymous reports can be made to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260192236 when providing information.
