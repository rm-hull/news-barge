A firm has applied to expand the number of goods vehicles it operates from an industrial estate near Bishop Auckland.

Twin Scaffold Ltd, based on Furness Close, Bishop Auckland, has applied to add another goods vehicle to its existing licence.

The firm plans to keep the extra vehicle at its operating centre on Coundon Industrial Estate.

The notice of the application was published on September 30.

Anyone who believes the extra vehicle could affect their use of nearby land can write to the Traffic Commissioner.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Representations must be made within 21 days of the notice.

The UK Government website explains how to make a representation.

The Traffic Commissioner will review the application and consider objections from local residents and businesses.

If approved, the application would allow Twin Scaffold Ltd to add another goods vehicle to its fleet.

The application remains open for public comment until October 21.
