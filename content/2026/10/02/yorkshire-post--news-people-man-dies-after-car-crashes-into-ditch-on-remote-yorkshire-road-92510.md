# Man dies after car crashes into ditch on remote Yorkshire road

## An elderly man has died after the car he was driving crashed into a ditch on a rural Yorkshire road.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened at around 5pm on Tuesday (Sep 29) on Malton Road between Little Edstone and Marton, near Kirkbymoorside.

Paramedics treated a 91-year-old man who had been driving a grey Volvo XC6o, but he died at the scene, police said.

The road was closed until around 10pm while the scene was investigated and the vehicle was recovered.

A statement from North Yorkshire Police said: “At this stage, it appears that the collision may have resulted from a medical episode. We are supporting the man’s family while the investigation continues.”

The force has asked anyone who may recall seeing the grey Volvo or anyone who may have relevant information to contact them, by emailing [email protected] or calling 101 and quoting incident number 12260191612.
