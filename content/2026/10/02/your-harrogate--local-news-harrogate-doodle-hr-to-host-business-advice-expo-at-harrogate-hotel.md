Doodle HR is hosting a business advice expo at Harrogate’s Cedar Court Hotel this month.

The Employment Law Expo will take place on Thursday 15th October, from 10am until 2pm, helping businesses understand changes to workplace legislation.

The event will focus on the Employment Rights Act and upcoming reforms, with guidance on how employers can prepare and adapt their working practices.

Guest speakers will discuss employment law changes, alongside developments affecting landlords and pensions.

There will also be exhibitors from local businesses, networking opportunities and tailored advice for organisations of all sizes.

Tickets cost £5 per person, including tea and coffee, with all proceeds from ticket sales donated to the British Thyroid Foundation.

Tickets are available now through Cedar Court’s online event booking **website**.

*Businesses interested in joining the event as an exhibitor can email [admin@doodlehr.co.uk.](mailto:admin@doodlehr.co.uk.)*
