Emergency services were called to a vehicle fire in a North Yorkshire village last night (Thursday).

It happened on the **A6055 at Minskip**, near Boroughbridge, at** 10.09pm last night** (Thursday),

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Knaresborough crew responded to a vehicle fire.

#### A6055 at Minskip

“On arrival the vehicle was well alight. Crews used one breathing apparatus and a hose reel jet to extinguish the fire.

“Police attended to assist with traffic management.”
