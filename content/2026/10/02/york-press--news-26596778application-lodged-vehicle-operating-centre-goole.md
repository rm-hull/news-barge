Steven Carrie, trading as The Maltings Organic Treatment Ltd, has applied to use a Goole site as an operating centre for multiple vehicles and trailers.

The business has applied for a goods vehicle operator’s licence, with the proposed operating centre at Mytum & Selby Goole, Jerry Lane, Goole, DN14 8BJ.

The application covers three vehicles and six trailers at the site.

Owners or occupiers of nearby land, including buildings, who believe their use or enjoyment of the land could be affected may make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner.

Representations must give reasons and be sent within 21 days of the notice to Hillcrest House, Harehills Lane, Leeds, LS9 6NF.

A copy must also be sent to the applicant at Turpin Lane, Common Lane, South Milford, Leeds, LS25 5FP.

A Guide to Making Representations is available from the Traffic Commissioner’s Office.
