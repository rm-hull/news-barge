A York shop could be set for a new look.

Plans lodged with York Council would see the Go Local Extra store in Acomb's Beckfield Lane rebranded as a One Stop.

The application from One Stop Stores stated current signs and branding would be removed and replaced except the Post Office logo which would stay.

One Stop has been owned by Tesco since 2003 and stocks products from the supermarket chain but operates as a separate business.

It had 1,101 company-owned and franchised stores across the country as of August last year and employs more than 10,600 staff.

The business has expanded by acquiring stores and running them directly and by recruiting independent retailers into its franchise network.

Services offered in One Stop stores include Post Offices, National Lottery tickets and parcel collections, along with groceries.

The store on the corner of Beckfield Lane and Ostman Road previously featured Costcutter branding before the installation of Go Local Extra signs.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/19789093.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One Stop has expanded in recent years *(Image: ONESTOP)*

It operated as a Premier Store before that before changing to a Costcutter between 2012 and 2016.

Plans lodged in 2013 to build extensions on the side and back of the shop building to expand the store's area were approved the following year.
