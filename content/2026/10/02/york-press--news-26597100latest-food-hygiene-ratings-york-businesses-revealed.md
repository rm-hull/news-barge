The scores are in — the latest batch of food hygiene ratings for York restaurants, cafes, takeaways and businesses have been revealed.

Environmental health officers from York have been visiting businesses across the city to see if they are meeting hygiene requirements.

Based on guidelines set by the Food Standards Agency, inspectors look to see how clean the facilities are; what food safety management procedures are in place and how hygienic food handling is.

Ratings are then dished out from five to zero — with the higher score the better the result.

### Five — Very Good

Bills Restaurant at 12 Coney Street, York, rated 5 on September 24.

John Roberts Catering at Lords Moor Lane, Strensall, York, rated 5 on September 23.

Towthorpe Vineyard at Manor Farm, Towthorpe Road, York, rated 5 on September 23.

Little Green Rascals Organic Nursery at 312 Tadcaster Road, York, rated 5 on September 17.

Businesses may have been reinspected since then, and this will be reflected in our next round-up.
