# Award-winning luxury Harrogate hotel's special deal gets a special reaction from public

## An award-winning Harrogate hotel says its ‘300 rooms for £300’ promotion has become “one of its most popular experiences of the year.”

The exclusive, highly sought-after seasonal deal offers 300 rooms available for £300 per room on select dates at Rudding Park, the family-owned 300-acre luxury resort on the edge of Harrogate.

Since the Mackaness family bought Rudding Park in 1972 they have transformed the 2,000 acres of outstanding countryside on the edge of Harrogate into an award-winning luxury hotel, spa, golf and conference resort with ongoing investment and redevelopment.

But a business accustomed to success has been delighted by the response to its latest promotion.

![Rudding Park's highly sought-after seasonal deal offers 300 rooms available for £300 per room at the family-owned 300-acre luxury resort on the edge of Harrogate. (Picture contributed)](https://www.harrogateadvertiser.co.uk/news/people/smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65.jpg)


The 300 Club deal includes a night in a Ribston room, dinner, full Yorkshire breakfast and two hours at the award-winning Roof Top Spa.

The dinner offers a choice of restaurant - The Clocktower Brasserie or Horto.

Please note, the price per night is based on two guests sharing a room.

Rudding Park was originally part of the Forest of Knaresborough and still retains some of the ancient oak trees.

As well as being based in a Grade I listed country house, the facilities at Rudding Park include the 18-hole Hawtree Golf Course, which was originally opened in 1995.

The £9.5 million Roof Top Spa and Horto Restaurant opened in 2017, blending modern wellness design with Harrogate's historic spa heritage.

The hotel resort’s message on its popular 300 rooms for £300 offer is simple - get booking before it's too late.
