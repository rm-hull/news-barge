A much-loved bakery has moved to a new unit after closing its high-street shop.

Liv’s Bakes n Cakes shut its Commercial Street shop in Brighouse on August 29.

Owner Liv Dennison blamed personal health problems and tough trading conditions for the closure.

On Thursday, she made two announcements online.

She added that her former shop is now up for let.

## Where is Liv’s Bakes n Cakes now based?

![Liv (far right)](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21275197.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

The business has now moved to a new unit at Project Colt on Elland Bridge.

Announcing the move on social media on October 1, Miss Dennison said: "My new home.

"I’m officially moved in and working from my new unit - thanks to the incredible team at Project Colt that reached out to me about a space they had.

"Jan and his team have welcomed me with open arms and it’s so lovely to be part situated in such a lovely community and charity that do incredible things."

## Collections from the unit or home-bakery only

![Another wonderful creation of Liv's](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21275196.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

She said the new unit is not a walk-in shop and will offer pre-ordered collections only.

Miss Dennison confirmed that all collections will now take place from the Elland Bridge unit or her home in Brighouse.

She also teased an upcoming public event in Elland at the end of the month.

The bakery’s former shop at 25B Commercial Street, Brighouse, is now available to let.
