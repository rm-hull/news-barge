Emergency services have responded to a vehicle, which is reported to have crashed into a building in York.

The incident took place in Lindsey Avenue at 12.44am on Friday (October 2).

North Yorkshire Police cordoned an area of the road off for the arrival of North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service.

The service confirmed that nobody was in the vehicle.

A spokesperson said: “Fire crews inspected the area and gave advice.

“The incident was left with the police.”
