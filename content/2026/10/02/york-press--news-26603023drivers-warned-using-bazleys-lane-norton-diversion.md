MOTORISTS are urged to drive with care while major roadworks are underway in a North Yorkshire town.

A diversion is in place while Northern Gasworks replace pipes in Norton.

Northern Gas Networks are currently replacing pipe works in Norton between the level crossing and Riverside way.

The work is expected to take around 17 weeks.

Access to Welham Road in Norton from the railway crossing will only be for vehicles leaving Norton towards the Stamford Bridge road and St Nicholas Street as the road will be closed one way from Springfield Garth.  
commute or trip into town.

A diversion is in place via Langton, however a number of motorists are continuing to use Bazley's Lane between Welham Road and Langton Road.

Cllr Keane Duncan, who represents Norton on North Yorkshire Council, said: "This route remains open, however there are safety concerns about the speed and volume of traffic. A temporary way, ‘slow’ signage and a 20mph restriction have been put forward as potential steps to improve the situation.

"HGVs should not be using this route and could be prosecuted if they do as the 7.5t restriction remains in force. They should use the official signed diversion via Langton."
