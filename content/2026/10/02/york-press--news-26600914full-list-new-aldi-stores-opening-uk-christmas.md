Nearly 30 new Aldi stores will open across the UK before Christmas.

The supermarket chain has revealed the full list of locations opening or relocating over the next 10 weeks.

Shoppers in communities across England, Scotland and Wales will see 24 new branches open alongside three site relocations.

The expansion will build on the retailer's existing network of 1,092 UK stores.

Giles Hurley, chief executive of Aldi UK and Ireland, said: "Our focus remains on making good food affordable for everyone.

"We are committed to cutting prices wherever possible while investing in long-term agreements with British suppliers to deliver everyday low prices for our customers."

## **Sales growth and price cuts**

The wave of openings follows an annual trading update showing company turnover increased five per cent to a record £19bn for the 12 months to December 31, 2025.

Aldi's share of the UK grocery market stands at an estimated 10.6 per cent.

Consumer groups have ranked the grocer as the UK's cheapest supermarket for five consecutive years.

The company remains the only major supermarket chain where prices are dropping year-on-year, following an investment of £340m to reduce prices on more than 1,000 products this year.
