An evening of music and nostalgia brought smiles to residents and guests at Boroughbridge Manor care home.

Residents clapped and sang along to classic hits performed by local musician Finlay Barrett, including Johnny Cash’s Ring of Fire and Elvis Presley’s Burning Love.

The show ended with a heartfelt rendition of Always on My Mind.

Lynnette Sellers, general manager at Boroughbridge Manor, said: "Our residents and guests had a wonderful day singing along with the wonderful performers.

"We do our best to provide a wide range of activities and entertainment at Boroughbridge Manor as part of the lifestyle enrichment programme for residents, and this was one of our favourites."

The hospitality team at the care home served sweet and savoury treats throughout the evening.

One resident said: "I haven’t heard some of these songs in many years.

"The classics never go out of style."

Boroughbridge Manor is operated by Barchester Healthcare.
