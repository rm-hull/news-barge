Police have released CCTV images of a man they would like to speak with after a woman was reportedly stalked and harassed on two separate train journeys.

The alleged incidents happened just before midnight on Thursday, September 3, and then again at the same time a week later when the woman boarded a train at Bradford Interchange.

## She 'noticed a man acting strangely'

On the first occasion, she reported seeing a man acting strangely.

She says when the train arrived at Bramley station, the man waited for her to leave and then followed her.

A week later, on Thursday, September 10, she reportedly saw the same man on the train again.

Again, she said he waited for her at Bramley and followed her out of the station.

![Detectives would like to speak to the man pictured](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21529394.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Detectives would like to speak to the man pictured

## Man 'chased woman through car park'

Police say when she challenged him about his behaviour, he chased her through the car park and out of the station.

British Transport Police want to speak to the man pictured because he may have information that could help their investigation.

Anyone who recognises him should contact British Transport Police by texting 61016 or calling 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 34 of 11 September.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
