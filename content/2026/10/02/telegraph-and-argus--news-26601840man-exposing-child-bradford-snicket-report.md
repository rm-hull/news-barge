A man reportedly exposed himself to a child in a Bradford snicket.

The incident is said to have happened between St Paul's Road and St Paul's Avenue in Wibsey at around 4pm on Wednesday. It was reported to police shortly before 6pm.

Additional reassurance patrols have been put in place in the area and police officers are liaising with local schools.

## Where is the snicket?

The path connects the two streets through a grassy area, with St Paul's Church on one side and Buttershaw St Paul's Cricket Club on the other.

The red geotag, in the below picture, marks St Paul's Road, not the spot where the alleged incident is said to have happened.

## Police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "A third-party report was received shortly before 6pm on 30 September that a man had exposed himself to a child in a snicket between St Pauls Road and St Pauls Avenue, Wibsey, around two hours earlier.

"This incident is being investigated by the Neighbourhood Policing Team who have also been conducting additional reassurance patrols in the area and liaising with local schools.

"If anyone has seen anyone behaving suspiciously in the area or has any information that could assist the ongoing enquiries into this incident, they are asked to contact Bradford South NPT via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat or by calling 101, quoting reference 13260557205."
