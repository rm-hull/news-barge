Six artists from the Darlington area are joining forces for a wide ranging exhibition.

The exhibition titled Croft Connections will be held in Croft Village Hall on Saturday, October 10, and Sunday, October 11, from 10am to 4pm.

It will feature a selection of paintings, pottery, and prints.

The participating artists are Frances Connolly, David Gale, Petra Lloyd, Kathleen Mooney, Anna Nelson, and Debbie Walker.

Petra Lloyd, a ceramicist and sculptor, creates nature-inspired pieces featuring birds, fish, and animals.

Frances Connolly, a mixed-media artist, is known for her long exhibition history across the region and her use of natural colours.

Anna Nelson is recognised for her linocut prints, while Debbie Walker specialises in abstract landscapes and has exhibited in the village before.

Kathleen Mooney previously exhibited alongside Connolly in a mother-and-daughter show at The Station in Richmond last year.
