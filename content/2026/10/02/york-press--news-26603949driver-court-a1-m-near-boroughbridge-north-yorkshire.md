A MOTORIST who pulled out dangerously into the path two HGVs has been sentenced in court.

North Yorkshire Police has shared a video which shows a driver travelling at more than 90mph on the A1(M) near Boroughbridge in North Yorkshire.

The manoeuvre was captured by a North Yorkshire Police safety camera vehicle at Junction 48 on January 9 and was branded "dreadful driving" by the force.

The driver pleaded guilty to both charges at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on September 30 where they received six penalty points and were ordered to pay more than £850.

Richard Fletcher, from North Yorkshire Police, said: "The consequences of this driving could have been extremely serious.

"Our safety camera vehicles can capture offences like this, ensuring that drivers who put others at risk are held to account for their actions."
