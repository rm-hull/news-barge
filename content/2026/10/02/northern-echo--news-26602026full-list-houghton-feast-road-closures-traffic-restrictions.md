Houghton-le-Spring is preparing for a major festival that will see multiple road closures over the coming days.

The closures support Houghton Feast, a 10-day celebration running from Friday, October 2 to Sunday, October 11, that highlights the town’s heritage, culture, and community spirit.

The festival begins with an official opening ceremony at The Broadway from 6pm on Friday.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20038396.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Carnival Parade at Houghton Feast, 2025 *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Murphy’s Funfairs will also be open to the public from 6pm at Houghton Rectory Playing Field.

To ensure the event runs smoothly, the following traffic restrictions:

No loading or unloading will be permitted on **Friday, October 2**, from 0.01am to 11.59pm on:

* Dairy Lane, from the A1052 to its northern extent
* Newbottle Street, between The Broadway and Station Road
* The Broadway

Full road closures will be in place on **Friday, October 2**, from 4.30pm to 10.30pm on:

* Newbottle Street, between Broadway and Wheler Street
* The Broadway

A diversion will be in place via the northern section of Newbottle Street, A182 Hillside Way, A182 between Lake Interchange and Dairy Lane Interchange, and A1052 Dairy Lane.

On **Monday, October 5**, from 0.01am to 11.59pm, no loading or unloading will be allowed on:

* Wallace Street
* Dixon Road
* Durham Road, between Gillas Lane and its northern extent immediately south of the A690 westbound entry slip road
* A1052 Dairy Lane, between the A690 and Wallace Street
* Lister Close, from Mill Hill for 30 metres
* Rotherhan Close, from Mill Hill for 30 metres
* Mill Hill, from Durham Road to a point 30 metres south of Rotherhan Close
* A690, between the B1284 and A182, including entry and exit slip roads
* Hetton Road, between the A690 westbound entry slip and Burns Avenue South

* Dairy Lane, between the A690 and Wallace Street, and the A1052 and The Broadway
