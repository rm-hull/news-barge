Romesh Ranganathan is one of the UK's most popular comedy personalities and is among the new faces heading to the Scottish Highlands for a new series of Celebrity Traitors.

Known for shows such as The Misadventures of Romesh Ranganathan, Rob & Romesh Vs, The Ranganation, and The Weakest Link, the comedian is one of the biggest faces on British TV.

Throughout much of his career, Romesh has been publicly open about a medical condition that affects his eye.

## What eye condition does Romesh Ranganathan have?

Romesh Ranganathan suffers from a condition affecting his right eye known as ptosis.

The comedian and TV presenter developed the condition after a significant eye infection as a young child, which caused lasting damage and led to the distinctive appearance of his right eye.

Despite common misconceptions, Romesh is not blind in that eye; the condition mainly affects the positioning of his eyelid, not his vision.

Romesh has long discussed his eye condition in a light-hearted manner.

He often jokes about appearing to be permanently winking and has woven the topic into his stand-up routines.

Despite being informed that corrective surgery is possible, Romesh has stated in interviews that he has no intention of pursuing treatment.

He feels his appearance has played a role in shaping his personality, outlook, and comedy career.

Having grown up with the condition, Romesh believes it influenced his life experiences and perhaps contributed to his decision to enter the world of comedy.
