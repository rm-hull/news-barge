A suspected ram raid took place at a restaurant in York.

The incident took place in Lindsey Avenue at around 12.30am this morning on Friday (October 2).

Officers attended immediately, a police spokesperson said, finding a Nissan Juke abandoned at the premises.




They added: “A vehicle which was believed to be travelling in convoy with the Juke was stopped later this morning and two people were arrested by West Yorkshire Police.

“Anyone who witnessed the incident or who has relevant dashcam footage is urged to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101 and quote reference number 12260193338.”

As reported by The Press, North Yorkshire Police cordoned off the road for the arrival of North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service.
