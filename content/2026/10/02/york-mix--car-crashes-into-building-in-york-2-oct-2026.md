A car crashed into a building in York early this morning (Friday).

Emergency services were called to the incident on **Lindsey Avenue** off Boroughbridge Road at**12.44am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Acomb responded to reports of a vehicle that had crashed into a building.

#### Lindsey Avenue, York

“Police had already cordoned off the area on fire service arrival.

“Nobody was in the vehicle.

“Fire crews inspected only and gave advice, the incident was then left with the police.”
