Emirates passengers flying from Newcastle face a major change next month as the airline replaces its newer aircraft with an older model.

The Dubai-based airline has flown its swanky Airbus A350s between Newcastle Airport and the UAE since March, but is now withdrawing the jumbo from the route.

It has been replaced by the Boeing 777-300, which had operated the flight from 2012 until this Spring.

Emirates never confirmed the reason for the change, but it is understood to have been due to lower demand when the conflict broke out in the Middle East.

The change could affect passengers with upcoming bookings, who are being advised to check their seat assignments because of differences in the aircraft's seating configurations.

Those who booked premium economy face an additional blow, as the 777 operating the Newcastle route does not offer that seating option.

Affected passengers will instead be moved into economy or could upgrade to business class.

The A350 proved popular with passengers and aviation enthusiasts who turned out in force to see it arrive for the first time earlier this year.

Emirates has operated flights between Newcastle and Dubai since 2007, initially using an Airbus A330 before switching to Boeing 777s in 2012 as demand grew.

The Emirates A350 was the first aircraft of its type to fly into Newcastle Airport earlier this year.
