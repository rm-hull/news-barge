*(Image: Newsquest)*

A sex offender and a driver who caused a serious injury were among defendants from York who were sentenced recently by magistrates.

Robert Sheard-Smith, 29, of Springfield Way, Heworth, pleaded guilty to three offences of having indecent images of children.

He was given a nine-month prison sentence suspended for 18 months with probation supervision at Hull Magistrates Court.

He was put on the sex offenders’ register and made subject to a sexual harm prevention order controlling his use of social media and contact with children, both for 10 years.

He must pay £85 prosecution costs and a £187 statutory surcharge.

The other cases were heard at York Magistrates Court.

Adam Thackway, 39, of Millfield Avenue, off Hull Road, York, pleaded guilty to stealing items worth £4.25 and £8 on separate occasions from BP Garage, Lawrence Street, York, items worth £15 from the Co-op shop on Tang Hall Lane, items worth £6.25 and to an unknown value on separate occasions from the Co-op in Hull Road, York, stealing items worth £8.38 from the Co-op on East Parade, Heworth and assaulting a police officer.

He was sentenced to 12 weeks’ imprisonment and must pay £100 compensation to the police officer.

Thomas Lang, 34, of Kingsway West, Acomb, pleaded guilty to stealing items worth £69 from a One Stop shop.

He was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision and 120 hours’ unpaid work.

He was banned from the One Stop shop on Acomb Road, York, and ordered to pay £34.60 compensation and £85 prosecution costs.

Stacey Walton, 36, of Barkston Avenue, Chapelfields, pleaded guilty to causing serious injury by careless driving.

She was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision and banned from driving for 12 months.

She was fined £140 and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Daniel Winteringham, 38, of Lady Mill Garth, Clifton, pleaded guilty to sending an offensive or obscene message.

He was given a 10-week prison sentence suspended for 18 months with probation supervision and ordered to pay £100 compensation to a woman.

He was made subject to a two-year restraining order banning him from contacting the woman or going to the premises of a York company.

Richard Cook, 47, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to assaulting a man at McDonald’s in Blake Street, central York, two charges of racial behaviour or words towards police officers, two charges of threatening words or behaviour, assaulting a police officer and criminal damage to a police van.

He was given a nine-month prison sentence suspended for 12 months.
