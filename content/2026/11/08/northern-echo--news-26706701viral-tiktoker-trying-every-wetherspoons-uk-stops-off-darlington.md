A TikToker on a mission to visit every Wetherspoons in the UK shared his verdict on which Darlington branch is best.

Its Ross visited The William Stead and Tanners Hall in Darlington during his nationwide challenge, which involves drinking a pint in every branch in the country.

The Darlington stops mean he has now visited 527 of 803 pubs since February 2025.

At The William Stead, Ross said: “This one's got two floors, and more importantly, they do Staropramen on tap. £4.10, you can't go wrong, cheers.”

The viral creator said that although he thought the William Stead was "bog standard," noting that it was "quite tightly cramped," it had two floors and served Staropramen, which redeemed the Darlington pub in his eyes.

At the second stop on his Darlington trip, The Tanners Hall, Ross praised the atmosphere but ultimately described the pub as "standard."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21681508.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ross visited both Wetherspoons when in Darlington, The William Stead and The Tanners Hall. *(Image: Northern Echo)*

He added: “There's nothing wrong with it, there's nothing amazing about it," and tucked in to his pint of Guinness, successfully marking off number 527 on his list.

The videos prompted a debate among Darlington residents about which Spoons is better, while several people were just pleased to finally see the challenge reach their town.
