A Darlington couple are set to take over a well-known Middlesbrough pub as they look to bring live sport, DJs and entertainment to the venue.

Phil Gamble and partner Jessica, who operate The Hoptimist on Tubwell Row in Darlington, have announced they are the new operators of The Isaac Wilson in Middlesbrough.

The couple said they were “absolutely delighted” to take on the pub, describing it as an exciting new chapter for their family.

They said they are looking forward to meeting customers and becoming part of the community.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21056759.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new owners Phil and Jessica with baby Charlie. *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Phil and Jessica reopened The Hoptimist in June after a six-month closure for major refurbishment work.

The Darlington pub, which temporarily shut in January to allow repairs to issues including its roof and heating, reopened on June 16 under the couple's ownership.

The refurbishment included a new cocktail area and pool table, with the couple hoping to bring back regulars and create a community pub.

They have now revealed plans to bring their experience running pubs and venues in York, Pickering and other locations including The Isaac Wilson.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21681837.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Isaac Wilson pub on Wilson Street in Middlesbrough *(Image: GOOGLE)*

The couple said customers can expect live football and other sporting action, match-day DJs, karaoke, themed events and regular late-night entertainment.

They are also planning food offerings with a new kitchen manager, alongside drinks deals and a welcoming atmosphere.

Announcing the takeover, they said: “It's not just a business venture for us; it's a family adventure!”

Their family includes baby Charlie and their Belgian Malinois dog, Dasher.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21056754.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Hoptimist on Tubwell Row, Darlington *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

They said: “We know building something special takes time, commitment and hard work, but we're ready for the challenge.”

Phil and Jessica said they were looking forward to welcoming existing customers, regulars and people from across Middlesbrough through the doors.

They signed off their announcement: “From The Hoptimist in Darlington to The Isaac Wilson in Middlesbrough, we're excited about what the future holds!”
