Do you know this man?

He is wanted by police after a fraud took place at the Morrisons Daily Post Office in Barwic Parade, Selby.

It happened at 8.04pm on Tuesday 25 September.

#### Morrisons Dail, Barwic Parade, Selby

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The male used a utility payment card to confuse staff and fraudulently obtain a large sum of money.

“Can you help identify the male shown in the two still image taken from CCTV at the Post Office?

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
