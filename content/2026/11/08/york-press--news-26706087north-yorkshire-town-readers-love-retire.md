A market town in North Yorkshire has been named as one of the places Yorkshire locals would love to retire to.

North Yorkshire is packed full of quaint towns and villages that offer a bit of peaceful living away from busy cities.

When asked where the best place to retire to in Yorkshire is, one York Press reader said Leyburn.

## What is Leyburn like?

Found right on the edge of the Yorkshire Dales National Park, the market town of Leyburn is a great place to explore the area from.

The town has a population of around 2,183, according to the 2011 census, but the area attracts lots of tourists.

If you are interested in moving to Leyburn, properties in the area sold for an average of £304,943 over the last year, according to Rightmove.

The majority of properties sold were detached houses that cost around £401,077.

Semi-detached properties sold for an average of £223,857, while terraced properties sold for around £196,040.

The town has plenty of independent businesses to support like Chambers Coffee House that has a 4.6 out of 5 rating on Tripadvisor.

Visitors to the café say it offers a “fantastic full English” as well as “excellent” coffee and toasties for lunch.

You can also visit the Posthorn Tea Room and Café for some classic tearoom treats such as scones served with jam and cream and fruit cake.

Lana’s Sandwich Kitchen is popular for its “fresh handcrafted sandwiches, salads, and jackets” using local ingredients, where you can pick what bread you want and the fillings you fancy.

The Black Swan is described as being “an old-fashioned country pub offering character and history” and is a good option for friends and family to stay in.

The pub has ensuite rooms with a full cooked breakfast to start your day off.

You can also just visit the pub for a drink and enjoy a roast at lunch on Sundays.

Leyburn also has plenty of shops, including a One Stop, a few clothes stores, a chemist, butchers, and bakeries, meaning you do not need to leave the town for your essentials.

The town has its own market that is held every Friday and has a range of stalls selling local produce and household goods.
