Morrisons’ new festive advert reimagines the story of Goldilocks and the Three Bears, with a warm and homely twist.

The 90-second advert will premiere on Monday, November 9, at 9.15pm across major networks including ITV, Channel 4, Five, and Sky.

In the story, Mummy and Daddy Bear prepare to welcome their grown-up son and his girlfriend—Goldilocks—home for Christmas.

Beverley Lumb, marketing and advertising director at Morrisons, said: "Everyone has their own idea of what makes Christmas 'just right' alongside their own family traditions that make the day feel truly magical.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21684324.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The advert is inspired by the classic fairytale. *(Image: Morrisons)*

"As Christmas comes in all shapes and sizes, we pride ourselves on delivering good, honest value, ensuring every piece of festive fare is crafted with the thought, care and quality for everyone gathered around your table."

At the heart of the campaign are Morrisons’ bakers, butchers, fishmongers, and deli teams, who prepare food in-store and help customers find the perfect ingredients for their Christmas meals.

In the advert, Daddy Bear visits Morrisons' Market Street, where he is guided by staff to create the ideal festive spread—including a giant salmon.

A Morrisons fishmonger provides expert cooking instructions to ensure the meal is “just right” for the occasion.

Meanwhile, Mummy Bear works to make the house welcoming, with a few bear-shaped mishaps along the way.

When Baby Bear arrives with Goldilocks, the scene is set for a Christmas that is warm and inviting rather than picture-perfect.

The decorations are mismatched, the tree is crooked, and Goldilocks’ chair is slightly broken.

Despite these imperfections, she is clearly touched by the love and care that has gone into every detail—especially the food.

The advert ends with the campaign line: “Christmas Made Just Right.”

Morrisons is extending the story beyond the screen with a limited-edition cuddly bear plush toy, available in stores.

50p from each sale will be donated to Marie Curie, the charity that supports people living with terminal illness.

The advert was directed by award-winning filmmaker Nick Ball, known for his work with Uber Eats, Tesco, and IKEA, as well as collaborations with stars such as Mariah Carey, Chris Hemsworth, Tina Fey, and Christopher Walken.

The Three Bears are brought to life using animatronics, each with their own personality and charm.

Andrew Long, chief creative officer at Leo UK, said: "There’s a huge amount of craft in bringing a family everyone knows to life and giving each bear their own personality.

"For us at Leo, this is also a great example of Populist Creativity that gets people talking.

"Goldilocks and the Three Bears is a story generations know, but people experience stories very differently today.

"So we’ve built the campaign to work in different ways too: our Christmas TV spot tells the parents’ story, while our social takeover gives Baby Bear his own voice.

"Each generation gets its own way into the story, just as Morrisons helps people make Christmas ‘just right’ for all the different tastes around their table."

The advert aims to highlight the value of a Christmas made with care, no matter how imperfect it might look.
