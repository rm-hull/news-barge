Firefighters were called to a smoke-filled flat yesterday afternoon after an alarm was activated at a residential property.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service was alerted to the incident on Hayburn Road at 1.04pm on Saturday, November 7, following a call from an alarm receiving centre.

Firefighters launched an immediate response to tackle the blaze and prevent it from spreading.

## **Breathing apparatus used**

Two firefighters wearing breathing apparatus entered the property, while crews used a hose reel to bring the fire under control.

The incident was contained by firefighters and no casualties were reported.

## **No injuries reported**

Despite the significant amount of smoke within the flat, nobody was injured in the fire.

The cause of the blaze has not been disclosed.

## **What emergency services have said**

A West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service incident log states: "On arrival crews made incident domestic property, as this was a flat fire with heavy smoke logging in property. Two breathing apparatus and one hose reel used. No casualties reported at the property."
