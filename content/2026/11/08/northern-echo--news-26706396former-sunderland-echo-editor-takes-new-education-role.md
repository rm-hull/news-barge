ROB Lawson OBE is returning to lead Education Partnership North East (EPNE) as its new chair of governors.

The respected media and education leader will begin the role in January 2027, marking a return to the organisation he helped build during his previous eight-year tenure as chair of Sunderland College and later EPNE.

During that period, he oversaw the mergers with Hartlepool Sixth Form College in 2017 and Northumberland College in 2019, forming the current regional group.

Since stepping down from EPNE, Mr Lawson has remained active in the further education sector as a national leader of governance since 2019.

He is currently chair of Hull College and has completed more than 35 external governance reviews over the past five years.

Mr Lawson said: "I am delighted to be returning to EPNE at such an exciting time for the college group.

"I have thoroughly enjoyed my time at Hull College and I'm thrilled be returning to the North East."

Mr Lawson was awarded an OBE in 2021 for services to education.

Mr Lawson also serves as chair of Sunderland Business Partnership and is a former editor of the Sunderland Echo.

He thanked Alison Shaw for her leadership as chair, saying: "Over the past year, Alison has provided stability and strategic oversight, supporting the Board and Executive Team while ensuring the group continues to build on its strong performance and ambitious plans for the future."

EPNE chief executive Toni Rhodes welcomed his return.

Ms Rhodes said: "I am absolutely delighted to welcome Rob back to EPNE as Chair.

"Rob has played such an important role in EPNE’s journey, and I know his insight, challenge, support and passion for the North East will be invaluable as we continue to grow and evolve."

She also thanked Ms Shaw for her leadership during her first year as chief executive.

Ms Rhodes said: "Her leadership has been invaluable during an important year for both me and EPNE."

EPNE was rated outstanding in all areas by Ofsted in 2024 and recently launched its 2025–2030 strategic plan.
