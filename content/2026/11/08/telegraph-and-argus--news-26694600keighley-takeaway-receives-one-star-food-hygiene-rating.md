A TAKEAWAY has been slapped with a one-star food hygiene rating following an inspection earlier this year.

Nice n Spicy, located on Main Street in Crosshills, received the rating after a visit from food hygiene inspectors on August 28.

### 'Generally satisfactory' in two key areas - but 'major improvement necessary' in one

The restaurant, which serves a mix of Indian and Italian cuisine, as well as donner kebabs and burgers, was rated "generally satisfactory" in two areas - hygienic food handling and cleanliness of facilities.

Hygienic food handling includes the preparation, cooking, re-heating, cooling and storage of food.

Cleanliness and condition of facilities and buildings includes having an appropriate layout, ventilation, hand washing facilities and pest control to enable good food hygiene.

However, inspectors found "major improvement" was necessary in the management of food safety, which covers having effective systems to ensure food is safe to eat, staff understanding of food safety, and confidence that standards will be maintained.

### 'The worst takeaway I've ever had'

The restaurant has received mixed reviews from customers online.

One customer said: "I don’t normally leave bad reviews but this was honestly the worst takeaway I’ve ever had.

"Service was ok but food was awful, both me and my husband couldn’t eat it."

### 'I'd say it's the best takeaway around this area'

However, not all feedback has been negative.

Another reviewer said: "If you like a good old fashioned curry this is your place.

"The curry’s are so authentic and spicy and the chapatti are perfectly cooked.

"Portions are large and I’d say it’s the best take away around this area."

The T&A has requested the full inspection report.
