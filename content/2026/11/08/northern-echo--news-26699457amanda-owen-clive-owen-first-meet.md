Amanda and Clive Owen became household names after opening up their lives to television cameras on Our Yorkshire Farm.

The couple first met in 1996, when Amanda was just 21 and working as a contract shepherd.

She had been sent to Ravenseat Farm in Upper Swaledale to collect a ram.

Clive, who was already farming at the property, had been living there since 1989.

![Amanda and Clive Owen](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670937.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda and Clive Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Their meeting marked the beginning of a relationship that would eventually see the couple marry, raise nine children and become two of Britain's best-known television farmers.

She subsequently pursued work in agriculture, eventually arriving at Clive's remote farm.

The property was far from luxurious.

Amanda has previously described encountering a rundown farmhouse, with damp carpets and smoke-stained wallpaper.

Clive was devoted to his sheep, and the couple's early life together revolved around the demanding routines of hill farming.

Despite the challenges, Amanda saw the potential to create a family home at Ravenseat.

The couple went on to have nine children: Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy.

Their unusual family life attracted the attention of television producers, eventually leading to Our Yorkshire Farm, which launched in 2018.

The programme followed the family through lambing seasons, severe winter weather and the everyday challenges of raising nine children on a remote working farm.

However, Amanda and Clive announced their separation in June 2022 after 22 years of marriage.

Despite the end of their relationship, the former couple continued to work together and co-parent their children.

They subsequently reunited professionally for Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which follows the restoration of neighbouring farmhouse Anty John's.

The programme has proved successful enough for Channel 4 to commission two further series, announced on November 2.

Almost three decades after their first meeting, Amanda and Clive remain connected through their children, their farming work and the Yorkshire Dales landscape where their story began.
