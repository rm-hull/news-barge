A family-run café that brings a taste of Greece to the heart of Darlington has become one of the town centre’s most popular places to stop for coffee, breakfast and lunch.

Deli-cious has built a loyal following on High Row since opening in 2021, winning praise for friendly service, homemade food and British-Greek flavours.

Owned by Maria Zaimaki and her family, the café offers something a little different from the usual high street coffee shop.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21674125.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Homemade cheesecakes and sweet treats on display at Deli-cious, one of Darlington town centre's most popular independent cafés. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Alongside breakfast favourites, sandwiches, pastries and freshly baked cakes, customers can enjoy authentic Greek dishes and traditional recipes inspired by the family’s heritage.

The welcoming atmosphere is a key part of what keeps people coming back.

Regulars are greeted with a smile, while first-time visitors quickly discover why the café has become such a popular meeting place for friends, families and colleagues.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21674138.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Indulgent cheesecakes and handcrafted desserts are among the most popular sweet treats served at Deli-cious in Darlington town centre. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

The family's hands-on approach can be felt throughout the business, helping to create a relaxed environment where customers are encouraged to take their time and enjoy their visit.

Whether it's a quick coffee while shopping, a leisurely brunch or a catch-up over lunch, Deli-cious has become a familiar fixture in many people's routines.

Online reviews are filled with praise for the café's food, service and atmosphere.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21674152.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A colourful display of homemade cheesecakes, cupcakes and desserts showcases the popular sweet treats available at Deli-cious in Darlington town centre. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Customers regularly highlight the quality of the coffee, generous portions and selection of homemade cakes, while others have praised traditional Greek dishes such as gyros and beef stifado.

Many reviewers also point to the friendly team and warm welcome as reasons they continue to return.

Its central Darlington location has also helped make it a favourite among shoppers and visitors to the town centre.

Sitting in one of Darlington's busiest spots, the café provides a welcoming escape from the hustle and bustle outside, with comfortable surroundings and a menu that caters for everything from a light snack to a full meal.

Just a few years after opening its doors, Deli-cious has firmly established itself as one of Darlington's standout independent cafés.

Combining family values, authentic Greek hospitality and a passion for good food and coffee, it continues to attract customers looking for something special in the heart of the town.
