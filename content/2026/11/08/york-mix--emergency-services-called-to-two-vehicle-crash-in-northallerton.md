Emergency services were called to a two-vehicle crash in Northallerton yesterday (Saturday).

A fire crew from Northallerton were called to the two-vehicle collision on **Thirsk Road** at**5.27pm**, after reports that one of the vehicles was smoking.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “All persons were out on arrival and there was no signs of fire to either vehicle.

#### Thirsk Road, Northallerton

“Crews made the scene safe.”
