A man locked up for his part in a £2.3m drugs conspiracy has died just months into his sentence.

David Emmerson died on October 22 after being taken to hospital from HMP Kirklevington Grange, near Yarm.

The 49-year-old's cause of death has not been confirmed.

The Prison and Probation Ombudsman confirmed it is investigating.

Emmerson, from Lemington, Newcastle, was jailed for four years and eight months in February after admitting to conspiring to supply cocaine.

He was one of nine members of an organised crime group jailed for a combined total of more than 70 years.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21681652.jpg?type=mds-article-620)David Emmerson, 49 *(Image: NORTHUMBRIA POLICE)*

The investigation uncovered the illicit trade of more than 63 kilos of cocaine with a wholesale value of more than £2.3m, alongside six kilos of heroin and more than 50 cannabis plants.

A total of £44,000 in criminal cash was also seized.

Emmerson acted as a trusted associate of Scott Fawcett and Daniel Chambers, handling cash and the storage of illicit goods as part of a separate conspiracy uncovered during the investigation.

"This was a complex investigation, and I would like to thank officers for their hard work in achieving this result.
