CHILDREN have been invited to help bring the Christmas spirit to an historic Bradford venue.

The Deck the Halls event at Bolling Hall will allow children to create their very own Christmas decoration.

They can take their creation home to hang on their own tree, or leave it with the museum and help decorate the Bolling Hall Christmas tree for everyone to enjoy.

As you get creative, soak up the festive atmosphere with traditional Victorian Christmas music performed by the Crickmore Crewe, filling the historic Hall with the sounds of Christmas past.

Bolling Hall is one of Bradford’s oldest buildings, and has experienced centuries of Christmas celebrations.

The event will be held on November 21 and run from 11am to 3pm.
