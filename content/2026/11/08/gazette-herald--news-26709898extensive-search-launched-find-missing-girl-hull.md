AN extensive search has begun to find a missing seven-year-old girl from Yorkshire.

Dakota was last seen in Noodle Hill Way, near the junction with Wayne Road, in Brandsholme, Hull – close to Beverley – at around 12.30pm today (Sunday, November 8).




She is around four feet tall and was last seen wearing a white-striped playsuit.

She is also believed to be barefoot, Humberside Police has said.

If you see Dakota, please call the force on 101, quoting log 235 08/11/2026.
