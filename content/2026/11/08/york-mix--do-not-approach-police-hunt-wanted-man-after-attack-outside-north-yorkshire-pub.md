Police issued a wanted man alert tonight (Sunday).

Detectives investigating an attack outside a North Yorkshire pub are hunting this man.

He’s Cameron Ramsey, and he’s wanted in connection with a serious assault that took place outside the Bruce Arms in Masham last night (Saturday).

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cameron-ramsey-3.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cameron-ramsey-2.webp)

Ramsey is described as:

* a man in his 30s
* of mixed race
* with bald or very closely shaved hair
* and dark eyebrows.

He was wearing

* a long grey padded coat with a large hood
* dark grey Berghaus tracksuit with a zip-up top and matching tracksuit bottoms
* Black On Cloud trainers.

#### Bruce Arms. Masham

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/bruce-arms-masham-north-yorkshire-gsv.webp)

A police spokesperson said: “If you have seen Cameron Ramsey, or have any information about his whereabouts, please contact North Yorkshire Police immediately.

“Please do not approach Ramsey yourself. Thank you for your help.”

If you have an immediate sighting or believe there is an immediate risk to safety, call 999.

Call 101 with information. Quote reference 12260219596.

Or you can pass information anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through their website.
