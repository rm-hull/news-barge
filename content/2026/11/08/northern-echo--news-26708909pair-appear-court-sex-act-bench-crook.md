A County Durham pair will appear in court accused of carrying out a sex act on a public bench.

Christopher Waite, 29, and Ann Zachart, 43, are both charged with outraging public decency.

The charges allege Zachart, of Arthur Terrace, Stanley, performed the act, while Waite, of Lister Terrace, Wolsingham, allowed it to be carried out on him.

It is said to have happened on March 18 in Crook, in view of members of the public.

The pair will appear at Peterlee Magistrates Court on Monday (November 9) afternoon.
