## Why a staycation trip to East Yorkshire and Hull is the answer for Brits

Willow Pastures Country Park in Hull Road, Skirlaugh, near Hull *(Image: Willow Pastures Country Park)*

Now the nights are drawing in, and the temperatures are beginning to plummet, some Brits may be looking further afield for a holiday at this time of year.

The prospect of heading towards the east coast of Yorkshire might not appeal to everyone at face value but those who venture to that part of the world are in for a pleasant surprise.

Hull itself is a cultural destination which has a lively blend of history, artisan shops and family attractions such as the distinctive Deep aquarium.

Designed by the late Sir Terry Farrell, the architect behind London’s M16 headquarters, the futuristic building is an unmissable feature of the waterfront.

Former industrial warehouses and dock buildings have been converted into trendy bars and intriguing independent shops, while the city’s Old Town has a large choice of quirky and historic pubs including Ye Old Black Boy, dating back to 1729, where punters can pay 50p for Cancer Research, setting a model train into life on a 16 metre track suspended from the ceiling in the bar.

Hull is a city which is proud of its heritage and its links to notable historical figures such as William Wilberforce with a fascinating museum at his place of birth dedicated to his story.

Crowned Britain’s City of Culture in 2017, it is easy to see why the city leaves an impression on visitors beyond the imposing Humber Bridge nearby.

![The Humber Bridge at Hull. ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/4918585.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

Drive 15 minutes north of Hull and you will find Willow Pastures Country Park, a luxury retreat complete with open-plan lodges, private hot tubs, woodburners and convenient parking.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21686479.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Willow Pastures Country Park in Hull Road, Skirlaugh, near Hull *(Image: Willow Pastures Country Park)*

Next to Skirlaugh Garden Centre and Shopping Village, the park is perfect for both couples and families with attentive staff quick to offer support if required, while thoughtful hospitality such as handwritten welcome cards, complimentary prosecco and daily hot tub checks add to the visitor experience.

For families, including those with dogs, Willow Pastures also has a nature trail on site, while a gate at the edge of the site provides easy access to open countryside and walks along a disused railway to explore the East Yorkshire scenery.

A nature-inspired adventure space designed for younger explorers called Little Willows has been added to the park for families taking children on a staycation.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21686480.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Willow Pastures Country Park in Hull Road, Skirlaugh, near Hull *(Image: Willow Pastures Country Park)*

Willow Pastures Country Park owner Emily Cook said: “Everything we do starts with one simple question: ‘How can we make our guests’ holiday even better?’”

“Whether it’s creating Little Willows, launching the Reyt Good Spa Retreat or championing local businesses, our aim is always the same: to create memorable holidays while showcasing everything East Yorkshire has to offer.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21686478.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Willow Pastures Country Park in Hull Road, Skirlaugh, near Hull *(Image: Willow Pastures Country Park)*

Not only does the park have excellent facilities on site with the welcoming Gardeners Country Inn serving tasty pub classics next door, visitors also have easy access to the coast and the seaside town of Hornsea.

For more information on Willow Pastures Country Park, email [reception@willowpastures.co.uk](mailto:reception@willowpastures.co.uk), call 01964 598 303 or visit [https://willowpastures.co.uk/](https://willowpastures.co.uk/)

For those simply wanting to switch off and relax away from the stresses and strains of a fast-paced modern lifestyle, Willow Pastures is ideally suited for those who just want to soak themselves in a hot tub with a complimentary glass of prosecco to unwind.

The options for both exploration and relaxation are on offer, and it is easy to see why many would be tempted to opt for a staycation break, whether that is a hassle-free adventure with children or an accessible couples’ mini-break escape.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21686481.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Willow Pastures Country Park in Hull Road, Skirlaugh, near Hull *(Image: Willow Pastures Country Park)*

A spokesperson for Willow Pastures Country Park added: "Willow Pastures Country Park is an award-winning, family-owned collection of luxury holiday lodges offering peaceful countryside escapes just a short drive from the Yorkshire coast.

"Located in Skirlaugh, the park combines luxury accommodation with genuine Yorkshire hospitality, thoughtful guest experiences and continual innovation.

"Rather than competing on size, the business focuses on creating memorable holidays through exceptional service, personalised touches and experiences that encourage guests to slow down, reconnect and explore East Yorkshire."
