An independent record shop in Darlington has closed its doors for the final time, after 30 years in the town.

Blacketts Records and Entertainment at 75 Skinnergate had its final day trading on Saturday (November 7).

The family-run business broke the news in a Facebook post.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21663226.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Blacketts Records and Entertainment, based at 75 Skinnergate, has confirmed its last opening day will be Friday, November 7. *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

In a statement, they said: "We regret to say we're closing down. Our last opening day will be the 7th November.

"To say thank you for the amazing support from everyone throughout the years we're doing our biggest sale yet with six different sales throughout the week."

The independent retailer described itself as having the most extensive range of old and new music available.

It bought and sold vinyl records, CDs, DVDs and Blu-rays, alongside game consoles and games.

The announcement prompted an outpouring of disappointment from customers, with many taking to social media to share their memories and wish the owners well.

One customer said: "We bought some great records and Blu-rays recently - sad to hear this, bro will also be disappointed."
