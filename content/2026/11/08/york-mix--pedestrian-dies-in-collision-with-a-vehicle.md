A young man has been killed after a collision with a vehicle travelling from Barton to Darlington.

Police say a blue Suzuki Swift was travelling from Barton towards Darlington when it collided with a pedestrian who had been making their way from Darlington and was believed to be going to Catterick Garrison.

The pedestrian, a 23-year-old man, died as a result of the collision.

His family have been informed and are being supported by specially trained officers.

The man was with a friend at the time of the collision, who was not injured.

Investigators are particularly keen to speak to the driver of a vehicle that stopped at the scene at the time of the collision and may have information that could assist the investigation.

They are also appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision, saw either the blue Suzuki Swift or the pedestrians prior to the incident, or has dashcam footage of the area at the time, to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to email the Serious Collision Investigation Unit at [email protected] or call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260219729.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
