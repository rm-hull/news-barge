Police have warned a spate of hoax calls from youths in and around Shildon is diverting officers from real emergencies.

Police say they are aware of a number of false reports being made by a group of youths believed to be frequenting Hackworth Park.

An investigation is now under way to identify those responsible, including efforts to trace the owner of the phone number linked to the calls.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: “Every hoax call diverts officers away from genuine emergencies and people who may be in urgent need of assistance. It wastes valuable police time and resources and can have serious consequences."

Police are also urging parents, guardians and carers to speak to children about the impact of making hoax calls and the consequences of misusing emergency and police services.

The force added that anyone aware of people making false reports should encourage them to stop, warning that such behaviour could lead to police action.
