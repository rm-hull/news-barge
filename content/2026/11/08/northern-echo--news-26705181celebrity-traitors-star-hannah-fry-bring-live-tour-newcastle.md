A Celebrity Traitors star who became a surprise fan favourite on the series is heading to the North East as part of a major UK tour.

Professor Hannah Fry will bring her Come Fry With Me show to Newcastle City Hall next year, promising an "evening of chance, chaos and questionable decisions."

The mathematician, author and broadcaster will visit venues across the UK next Autumn, focusing on subjects like staying cool during a heatwave, the science behind skincare, artificial intelligence and the mathematics of everyday life.

Audiences are promised a show designed to bring complex ideas to life and encourage them to see the world differently.

The Celebrity Traitors star will appear in Newcastle on October 6, 2027, with tickets now available online.

Fry was the first Public Understanding of Mathematics Professor at the University of Cambridge, whose media career first began in stand-up comedy before moving into broadcasting.

She has presented documentaries for BBC, fronted Channel 4's election coverage, and co-hosts the Rest Is Science podcast. Fry has also written several best-selling books, including The Mathematics of Love and Hello World: Being Human in the Age of Algorithms.

The 42-year-old professor recently featured on the second series of Celebrity Traitors, which has caused an explosion in public visibility for her.

Fry mostly flew under the radar outside of academic circles when her casting was announced, with most viewers focusing on entertainment industry giants like Miranda Hart, Richard E Grant or James Acaster.

She quickly won over viewers with her personality, fans praising her intelligence and wit before her departure, gaining over 200,000 fans over the course of the BBC show.

The tour will draw on Fry’s work in television, radio, podcasts, social media and books, where she explains scientific and mathematical subjects to a broad audience.

The tour is also set to coincide with the release of her newest book, No One Knows What They’re Doing, which is expected to explore human fallibility and decision-making.

Tickets for Fry's Newcastle City Hall show are available online.
