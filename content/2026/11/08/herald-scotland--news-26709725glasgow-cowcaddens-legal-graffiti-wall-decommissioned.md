**A pilot for a legal graffiti wall in Glasgow has been decommissioned due to the "suitability" of the location.**

Street artists will have a three-month grace period from the police to keep creating art on the walls in the area, after which it will no longer be considered a risk-free zone for graffiti.

The underpass at Cowcaddens Subway Station became part of Glasgow City Council's legal graffiti wall initiative in 2024.

The walls have hosted full-length murals, tags and even dedications to family members who have passed in recent months.

The wall was introduced as part of the council's pilot scheme for legal graffiti sites, which was developed after councillors approved plans to create designated spaces for street art across the city.

The wider initiative had been under discussion since 2022 and was rolled out in stages, with sites including Custom House Quay, Riverside Museum/Yorkhill and Cowcaddens.

A statement on the Glasgow Legal Walls website announced the news, saying Police Scotland will soon install signage to dissuade artists from spray painting the walls in future.

It reads: "Over the course of the Legal Wall pilot, the Cowcaddens Legal Wall provided opportunities for creative expression and engagement between artists, partner organisations and local communities.

"Following a review of the pilot, a decision has been taken to decommission the Cowcaddens Legal Wall.

"The decision relates specifically to the suitability of the Cowcaddens location and does not diminish the positive contribution made by artists, partners and community stakeholders throughout the project."

It goes on to "thank everyone who contributed to and supported the initiative", while saying that the pilot has provided "valuable learning and experience, which will help inform future discussions and opportunities around street art, creative expression and the development of appropriate creative spaces across Glasgow."

It adds: "The Cowcaddens site will therefore no longer operate as a legal graffiti wall, and artists are asked not to paint at the location.

"Police Scotland have been notified and signage will be displayed at both entrances to the underpasses. A 3 months grace period will be in place until 31 January 2027 while the message is being shared with the Street Art community.

"Legal graffiti opportunities remain available at Custom House Quay and the Partick Underpass, which continue to operate as legal graffiti sites."
