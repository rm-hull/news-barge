Shoppers in York were surprised and delighted to bump into an Oscar winning legend today (Sunday).

Dame Judi Dench popped in to Topping & Company bookshop on Museum Street.

The York-born actor signed copies of her new book, *Is It Too Late to Make a Run for It?*, co-written with Brendan O’Hea.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/judy-dench-signing-toppings-bookshop-2-insta.webp)

Dame Judi was in York to appear at the Theatre Royal, where she was in conversation with Brendan this afternoon

In the sell-out show she shared memories and anecdotes from seven decades of performing with some of the greatest names in theatre and film.

Toppings York said of her visit: “Our bookshop has been graced today with a visit from a York legend – the wonderful Dame Judi Dench herself.  
“Here she is signing a copy of her new book *Is It Too Late To Make A Run For It?* with a couple of suitably starstruck booksellers.  
“Huge thank you Judi for visiting the bookshop.”

There post sparked a lot of love for the star. “No way! Absolute legend!” said one.

Another wrote: “A York legend! Perhaps she’s here to announce Yorkshireman James Norton as Bond (we can hope).”
