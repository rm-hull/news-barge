A story written by a North Yorkshire-based author is taking centre stage as a new theatre production.

Believe in Magic, written by Christina Gabbitas, is being performed at King George’s Hall in Blackburn in a collaboration with Westholme School.

The production tells the story of Peggy and Imtiaz as they journey through time, discovering the rich heritage of their town.

Ms Gabbitas said: "Writing to educate has always been my focus.

"I love researching history, finding those fascinating details and thinking about how a story can help children connect with them."

The show was originally commissioned by Blackburn BID in 2020 and uses an industrial Lambeth rope as a magical means of travel.

The story was praised by actress Jenny Agutter, who called it "The story is terrific, E Nesbit and Lewis Carroll with Narnia thrown in."

Ms Gabbitas worked closely with Westholme’s head of drama and music teacher to develop the stage adaptation, including an original song.

Around 800 children from nearby schools are expected to attend a daytime performance, with an evening show for families.

Ms Gabbitas said: "Seeing a story I created become a theatrical production, with music and young people bringing the characters to life, is a pinch-me moment."
