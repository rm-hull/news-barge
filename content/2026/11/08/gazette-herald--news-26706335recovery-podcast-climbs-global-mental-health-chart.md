A podcast produced in Scarborough that started as a small project has become a global success, reaching audiences in more than 83 countries.

IN RECOVERY, produced by Ark House Rehab, was shortlisted in three categories at the Independent Podcast Awards, including Best Personal Stories Podcast, Best Lifestyle Podcast, and Best Self-Improvement Podcast.

Sam and Richard, the podcast’s hosts, said: "We are absolutely over the moon to have been recognised on such a massive platform alongside the absolute best in independent audio.

"For us, this was never just about winning an award.

"It is about carrying the message of recovery to the people who need it most, whether they are trapped in active addiction, or they are family members trying to understand the illness.

"Being shortlisted for three awards proves that people are listening, the conversation is shifting, and we are successfully breaking the stigma of addiction."

The ceremony coincided with Ark House Rehab’s 34th anniversary.

Björn Roswald, the rehab’s founder, visited the centre during the same week to speak with staff and residents and reflect on its history.

The podcast has reached the top 20 per cent of podcasts globally and climbed to Number 10 in the global Mental Health charts.
