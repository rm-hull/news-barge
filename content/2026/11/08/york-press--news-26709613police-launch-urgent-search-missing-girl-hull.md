## Urgent search launched for missing 10-year-old girl from Yorkshire

f *(Image: Humberside Police)*

An urgent search is under way for a missing 10-year-old girl in Yorkshire.

Humberside Police has launched a extensive search to find Ellie, who was last seen in the Bransholme area of Hull, near Beverley, at around 9.20am on Sunday (November 8).




Ellie is described as white, 4ft 3 inches, with shoulder-length mousey brown hair.

She was last seen wearing pink glasses with love hearts on the arms, a black Gucci cardigan, black top, white trousers, and black trainers with pink soles.

If you've seen Ellie call 101 quoting the log 130 of November 8.
