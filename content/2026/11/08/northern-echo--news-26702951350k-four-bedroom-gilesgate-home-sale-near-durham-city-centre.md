YOU’D be hard pressed to find a home that’s been renovated with more care than this extended four-bedroom link-detached house in Gilesgate.

Its current owners have modernised it throughout to an exacting standard, creating stylish accommodation that’s ready to move straight into – and with Durham City Centre just over a mile away, the location is hard to beat too.

Step inside and a generous entrance hall leads to a cloakroom and utility room.

To the right of the hall is a superb open-plan lounge and dining area which lies at the heart of everyday family life.

![St Josephs Gate, Gilesgate](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675753.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This beautifully renovated home lies just a mile from Durham city centre *(Image: JW WOOD)*

This space is centred around a striking full media wall incorporating a large contemporary fire and extensive storage with lighting which makes an eye-catching focal point.

Bifold doors open into the rear garden while off the room is a dedicated study ideal for working from home.

To the other side of the hall is the kitchen and breakfast room fitted with a contemporary range of granite-topped units and an array of integrated appliances including an induction hob, double oven, microwave, fridge, freezer and dishwasher.

Upstairs are the stylish family bathroom with an elegant freestanding oval bath and a walk-in double shower, plus four bedrooms, including the particularly spacious principal bedroom with fitted wardrobes.

A pull-down ladder on the landing gives access to the loft.

![St Josephs Gate, Gilesgate](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675752.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This beautifully renovated home lies just a mile from Durham city centre *(Image: JW WOOD)*

Outside, a generous driveway provides ample off-road parking and leads to a useful storage area with French doors, lighting and power.

To the rear, the substantial garden has an extensive lawn along with decked and paved patio areas.

St Joseph’s Close is a quiet cul-de-sac within easy walking distance of the shops and amenities in Gilesgate.
