A new apprenticeship scheme is offering hands-on training in home care.

Launched in Redcar and Cleveland, the programme is aimed at school leavers, career changers and neurodivergent individuals who may find classroom learning challenging.

It is the result of a partnership between Caremark Redcar and Cleveland and Vision Training North East.

Michelle Jackson, managing director of Caremark Redcar and Cleveland, said: "This partnership creates a new route for local people to begin a rewarding career in home care.

"Apprentices will have the opportunity to earn, learn and make a practical difference in their community from the outset."

The scheme is designed to be inclusive, focusing on applicants' personal qualities rather than academic background.

Ms Jackson added: "We look at each person’s kindness, reliability and willingness to learn — not whether they fit a particular mould, and we want neurodivergent applicants to not feel excluded and see care as a career where they can belong and thrive."

Apprentices will earn Caremark’s normal care-worker rate from day one, which is above the statutory apprentice minimum wage of £8 per hour.

They will also work towards a recognised qualification, with opportunities for progression into senior care, management, clinical roles, nursing and other areas of health and social care.

Current Carer Chelsea Gibson has ADHD and has worked in care for 16 years.

She said: "Home care suits me perfectly because every day is different.

"You’re meeting different people, visiting different homes, and helping with different situations.

"Having the opportunity to move between visits throughout the day also helps me manage my energy and focus, and at Caremark we use a digital app with which you tick tasks off as you go along.

"As someone who worries about forgetting things, that really helps."

For more information about the apprenticeships, contact Caremark Redcar and Cleveland on 01287 634706 or email [redcar@caremark.co.uk.](mailto:redcar@caremark.co.uk.)
