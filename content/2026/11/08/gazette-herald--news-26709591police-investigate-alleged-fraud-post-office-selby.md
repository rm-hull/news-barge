POLICE are investigating alleged fraud at a post office in North Yorkshire.

The incident happened at Morrisons Daily Post Office in Barwic Parade in Selby at 8.04pm on Tuesday (September 25).




North Yorkshire Police said: “A person used a utility payment card to confuse staff and fraudulently obtain a large sum of money.

“Can you help identify the male shown in the two still image taken from CCTV at the Post Office?

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance:

• Email: [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk)

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote reference 12260185202 when providing information.”
