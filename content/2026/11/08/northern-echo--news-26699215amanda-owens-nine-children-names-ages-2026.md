Amanda Owen's nine children became familiar faces to millions of television viewers when Our Yorkshire Farm first arrived on Channel 5 in 2018.

But eight years later, the children who were once seen helping with lambing, feeding animals and exploring the Yorkshire Dales are growing up and pursuing different interests.

Amanda, 52, and ex-husband Clive, 70, have nine children together, ranging in age from ten to 25.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670643.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda, Clive and some of their children *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Their eldest daughter Raven is now 25, while their youngest child Nancy is ten.

The family continues to feature in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, which follows their lives at Ravenseat and the restoration of neighbouring farmhouse Anty John's.

**According to ages reported in October 2026, the children are:**

* Raven Owen — 25
* Reuben Owen — 23
* Miles Owen — 20
* Edith Owen — 18
* Violet Owen — 16
* Sidney Owen — 15
* Annas Owen — 13
* Clementine Owen — 11
* Nancy Owen — 10

The eldest children have increasingly developed their own interests and responsibilities.

Raven studied biomedical science at York St John University, while Reuben pursued his enthusiasm for machinery and engineering.

Miles has remained closely involved in farming, with his responsibilities at Ravenseat featuring in the family's latest television series.

Edith, meanwhile, has helped revive the family's cream tea tradition, welcoming visitors passing through the Yorkshire Dales.

The younger children continue to feature in the programmes, helping with animals and taking part in everyday life at Ravenseat.

In October, Amanda spoke about raising her children in the countryside and the opportunities available to them beyond television and mobile phones.

She said: "The dynamics are always changing."

The family will remain on television for some time yet.

Channel 4 announced on November 2 that Our Farm Next Door had been renewed for a fifth and sixth series.

The ten new episodes will continue documenting the children's changing lives, alongside work on the historic Anty John's farmhouse.

For viewers who have followed the Owens since 2018, the new programmes offer another opportunity to see how the nine children have grown up.
