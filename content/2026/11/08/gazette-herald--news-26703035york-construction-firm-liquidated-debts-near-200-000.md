A York construction company has gone into liquidation with debts approaching £200,000.

Profix Trades Ltd of Kettlestring Lane was incorporated in 2024 and entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on October 14, with London-based Exigen Group appointed liquidator.

Companies House records Profix Trades had one director – Thomas Hartley.

A liquidator's report known as a Statement of Financial Affairs says Profix Trades Ltd had estimated total realisable assets of £3,600.

The business had no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

However, HMRC was a secondary preferential creditor – someone paid next – and was owed £66,158.42 in VAT and £40,000 in a construction industry levy.

In addition, Profix Trades Ltd had unsecured creditors – those who are paid last – which were owed £91,658.15 in total.

These were made up of £63,779.88 to non-preferential unsecured creditors, an uncertain amount to employees, and £3,600 owed to directors.

In addition, HMRC was owed a further £8,000 in corporation tax, a landlord was owed £1,278.30 and financial lenders were owed £14,999.97.

This gave a total deficit of £194,216.57.

With Profix Trades having issued and called up capital of £200, this left a final estimated deficit of £194,416.57, the statement added.
