## Stunning York townhouse hits the market – have a look inside

Inside the five-bedroom home for sale in Scarcroft Road, York, for £850,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

A stunning York townhouse has hit the market in one of the city’s most sought-after locations.

The five-bedroom Victorian terrace is for sale in Scarcroft Road for £850,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said has a “wealth of original period features throughout” which are “complemented by tasteful modern décor and high-quality fittings”.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21680435.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The five-bedroom home for sale in Scarcroft Road, York, for £850,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody/Zoopla)*

Inside, an elegant entrance hallway leads to two reception rooms, each with high ceilings and.

The kitchen is to the rear of the home overlooking the courtyard garden.

Upstairs, two bedrooms are on the first floor along with the family bathroom.

Three more bedrooms are on the second floor with views of York Minster.
