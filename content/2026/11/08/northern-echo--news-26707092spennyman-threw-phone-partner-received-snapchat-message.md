A Spennymoor thug who made a 'mess' of his partner’s face after she received a Snapchat from a male friend was told he should be 'ashamed' of himself.

Marc Hopper had been drinking with the woman before flying into a jealous rage when they returned home, a court heard.

The 44-year-old left the woman with facial bruising and a lasting distrust of men.

Prosecutor Charlie Thompson said: “She received a Snapchat message from a friend who was male.

“He saw it, picked up her phone and threw it at her and it hits her to the forehead causing bruising to head and eyes which she said lasted some weeks.”

Mr Thompson said dizziness forced the woman to go to hospital, where she told medics she had banged her head on scaffolding.

The woman said in a victim impact statement that the assault had left her traumatised and distrustful of men.

She said: "My life has changed in ways that I never imagined. I have been left with severe anxiety every single day.

"I now struggle with trust and no longer feel safe around others, particularly men."

Hopper, of Wood View, Spennymoor, admitted inflicting actual bodily harm in May last year.

His barrister, Caroline McGurk, said Hopper had spent time on remand after he was arrested for breaching bail and served almost four months in custody.

She added Hopper had recently completed an alcohol treatment programme as part of a community order he received for an earlier offence of violence.

### Judge passes sentence

Recorder Andrew Walker sentenced Hopper to 40 weeks in prison after seeing the extent of the victim's facial injuries.

He said: “While in drink, you became jealous about a message she received on her phone.

“You took it and threw it with force into her face.
