The Met Office has issued a weather warning for fog in York and Selby.

It runs from 6pm today to 11am tomorrow (Monday, 9 November).

The forecasters say the fog could lead “to difficult travel conditions and possible delays”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/met-office-fog-warning-8-nov-2026-map.webp)

People should expect slower journey times with delays to bus and train services possible.

“There is a chance of delays or cancellations to flights,” the Met Office adds.

It advises people to “give yourself the best chance of avoiding delays by checking road conditions if driving, leaving extra journey time, or amending plans if necessary”.
