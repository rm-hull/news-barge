A Bishop Auckland gym is set to expand after securing the former Jewson building next door.

OneGym has announced plans to take over the neighbouring premises, increasing the size of its existing site and adding new training facilities for members.

The gym, which opened in the town in 2012, said the project would create additional space for equipment and classes when work is completed.

Plans include a new group exercise studio, a dedicated Reformer Pilates studio, an expanded free weights area, additional strength equipment, a functional training zone, a boxing area and stretching space.

A training track featuring a tank sled and equipment designed for HYROX-style workouts is also proposed.

The development will also provide additional parking for gym users.

The existing facility is expected to remain open while the work is carried out.

Paul Pearson, director of OneGym, said the expansion represented a significant investment in the company's first site.

He said: "We've built a fantastic community here over the years, and expanding into the building next door gives us the opportunity to introduce more facilities, equipment and training space for our members.

"We're looking forward to welcoming members into the expanded gym in early 2027."

The project forms part of wider growth plans for the fitness operator, which is also preparing to open a new site at Doxford Park in Sunderland.

Members reacting to the announcement on social media largely welcomed the plans.
