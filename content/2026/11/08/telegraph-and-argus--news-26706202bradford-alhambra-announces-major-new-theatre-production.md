A CHILLING psychological thriller is set to take the stage in 2027.

Strangers on a Train will be at Bradford’s Alhambra Theatre from May 5 to 8, starring Emmerdale’s Lewis Cope and EastEnders’ Jonny Labey.

The play is Craig Warner’s stage adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s classic novel, famously adapted for film by Alfred Hitchcock.

David Gilbery, CEO of Bill Kenwright Ltd, said: "We’re delighted to have Lewis and Jonny taking on two of Patricia Highsmith’s most fascinating characters in our new production of Strangers on a Train.

"They are both immensely talented performers, and with the Roxana Silbert directing, alongside designer Robert Innes Hopkins, and the rest of our wonderful creative team, this has all the ingredients for a truly thrilling stage production."

Directed by Roxana Silbert, the cast will be led by Lewis Cope as Charles Bruno, a deeply complex and chilling character, opposite Jonny Labey as Guy Haines.

Cope is known for his role as Nicky Milligan in Emmerdale and his appearance on the 2025 series of Strictly Come Dancing.

Labey is recognised for his performances as Paul Coker in EastEnders and Rex Gallagher in Hollyoaks.

The production is part of a 25-date national tour, with further dates from September to November to be announced.
