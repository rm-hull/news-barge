A new veterans' centre offering mental health, housing and employment support is set to open in the heart of Hartlepool.

It comes after the East Durham Veterans Trust received a £1.5 million funding boost from the Labour Government’s VALOUR programme, which aims to expand support for former service personnel across the UK.

The new centre, set to open at Middleton Grange Shopping Centre, will serve as a permanent base for veterans’ services in the town.

Jonathan Brash, MP for Hartlepool, said: "The nation owes a profound debt of gratitude to the men and women who have served our country.

"That debt doesn’t end when someone takes off their uniform.

"I’m absolutely delighted that the Government has increased its funding commitment to East Durham Veterans Trust from £1 million to £1.5 million.

"This is a significant investment in strengthening support for veterans across our area, including here in Hartlepool."

Mr Brash played a key role in securing the location and negotiating a rent-free arrangement for the premises.

He also welcomed the decision by the previous Labour-led Hartlepool Borough Council to provide free parking for veterans using the centre.

The VALOUR programme, part of the Government’s Defence Investment Plan, is designed to make it easier for veterans and their families to access vital services.

Andy Cammiss, chief executive of East Durham Veterans Trust, said: "We are absolutely delighted that the Government has increased its funding commitment to £1.5 million.

"This additional investment will help us strengthen and expand the support we provide to veterans and their families across the area.

"Establishing a dedicated centre in Hartlepool is an important part of that ambition.

"We want to ensure that no veteran feels they have to navigate the challenges of civilian life alone."

He said the centre’s central location would make it easily accessible to veterans, their families and the wider community.

Calvin Bailey MBE MP, minister for veterans and people, said: "Ahead of Remembrance Sunday, I’m pleased that we can pay tribute to the service and sacrifice of our armed forces community by giving veterans easier access to the support they need, regardless of where they are in the UK.
