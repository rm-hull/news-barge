# 'Stunning' Leeds JD Wetherspoon pub with 'amazing staff' named one the UK's best Spoons

## Lots of customers absolutely love a visit to the Spoons pub

A 'stunning' JD Wetherpoon pub in Leeds has been named one of the UK's best Spoons venues.

Punters at the popular Leeds city centre Spoons absolutely love the venue. Merrion Way’s Stick or Twist, which regularly welcomes those off to Leeds First Direct Bank Arena for a pre-gig drink, has placed seventh in the list of the UK’s best JD Wetherpoon establishments.

The study, overseen by Spin and Shake, included 1.9m reviews on Google. It saw the Leeds venue average 4.35 stars out of 5. The top spot has been taken by Fulham’s Walham Green.

Stick of Twist fans have praised the staff, service, and prices at the Leeds pub. The former building, which housed the venue, was knocked down in 2018 with the pub reopening in 2021.

On Google, posting a review, Leah May said: “I went on Saturday, it was a lovely atmosphere even though it was packed. The staff were amazing and so friendly.”

Tee Miles posted: “Stick or Twist has become my favorite place to eat.” Brian Walmsley said: “The best spoons in Leeds.”

While William Chivers commented: “I must say, service at the stunning Stick or Twist, is as great as ever.”
