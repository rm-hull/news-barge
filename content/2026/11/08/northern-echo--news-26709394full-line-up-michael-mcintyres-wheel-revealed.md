A Strictly Come Dancing star has signed up to a popular BBC One gameshow.

Michael McIntyre’s show The Wheel is returning to screens soon with a new star-studded line-up.

The hit quiz show sees three contestants play for a cash prize using the help of seven celebrities who will guide them when answering each question.

Each of the celebrities is assigned a specialist subject from things such as sport and history to music and science.

## Strictly star signs up for Michael McIntyre’s The Wheel

The first episode of the new series will be broadcast on Saturday, November 14, at 8pm with Michael Ball, Jordan Banjo, Katherine Ryan, Isa Guha, Kate Garraway, Harriet Kemsley, Perri Kiely, and Matt Morsia set to help out the contestants.

The second episode will see Strictly Come Dancing’s Josh Widdicombe taking to The Wheel alongside Italian Bach, Kerry Godliman, Trevor Nelson, Bez and Shaun Ryder, Angela Scanlon, and Alex Scott MBE.
