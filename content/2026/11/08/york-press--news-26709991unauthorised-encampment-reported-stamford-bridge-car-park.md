A parish council has reported an unauthorised encampment at a village car park near York.

Stamford Bridge Parish Council said it had become aware of the arrival of “several caravans” at the Viking Road car park, managed by East Riding of Yorkshire Council this morning (Sunday, November 8).

A representative said: “This has been immediately reported to East Riding of Yorkshire Council by both our parish clerk and the chair.”




The parish council said it was encouraging residents to report the incident to East Riding of Yorkshire Council, and any criminal activity to Humberside Police.

It comes three months after the parish council closed the entrance to the community’s overflow carpark following the establishment of another encampment in Gate Helmsley, with three other instances in the York area reported in August.

Following a public consultation, Haxby Town Council said it would need £25,000 to prevent unauthorised vehicles from entering Ethel Ward Playing Field after it reported it faced a bill of £2,500 to clear up a four-day encampment in July.

The Press has contacted East Riding of Yorkshire Council for comment.
