Police are investigating a car fire near Whitby Abbey.

Firefighters from the town were called to the incident on **Abbey Lane** at**8.50pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Whitby responded to a vehicle which was well alight.

#### Abbey Lane, Whitby

“Crews extinguished and dampened down using two hose reel jets and two breathing apparatus.

“The incident was left with the police.”
