A hotel that once served as a hunting lodge for Henry VIII and later became home to the influential Eden family continues to offer guests a unique blend of history, luxury and relaxation in the heart of County Durham.

Located in the former mining village of West Auckland, The Manor House Hotel and Spa occupies a Grade I listed building whose origins date back more than 600 years.

Formerly known as West Auckland Hall, the property was later used as a brewery before becoming the hotel visitors know today.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680825.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*

Built in the late 15th century on foundations dating back to the early 14th century, the manor retains many of its original architectural features.

Guests are welcomed by exposed wooden beams, uneven floors, sloping ceilings and centuries-old timberwork, all of which help preserve the character and charm of the historic building.

The two-storey property was constructed from local rubble stone with ashlar dressings and stone slate roofing.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680817.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*

Many of the original windows date back to the early 16th century, while parts of the entrance are believed to date from the 18th century.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680820.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*

Today, the hotel combines its rich heritage with modern comforts.

It offers 32 bedrooms, a lounge centred around an original beehive fireplace, a contemporary restaurant and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the building.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680823.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680824.jpg?type=mds-article-620)*(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*

For those looking to unwind, The Manor Spa features a heated indoor swimming pool, sauna, steam room, Jacuzzi and relaxation area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680821.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680822.jpg?type=mds-article-620)*(Image: MANOR HOUSE)*

Guests can also choose from a range of spa treatments and packages designed to provide a relaxing escape from everyday life.

The venue has also established itself as a popular destination for weddings, celebrations, conferences and corporate events, with dedicated facilities available for both private and business functions.

Hotel staff pride themselves on providing a warm welcome, whether guests are visiting for an overnight stay, spa break, wedding celebration or short getaway in County Durham.

The Manor House is also committed to sustainability, with a focus on reducing waste and energy consumption while working alongside partners to minimise environmental impact.

The hotel has also received glowing reviews from guests.
