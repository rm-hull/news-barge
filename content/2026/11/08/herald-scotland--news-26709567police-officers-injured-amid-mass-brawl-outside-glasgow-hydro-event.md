**Three police officers were assaulted and two men arrested after disorder erupted outside Glasgow's OVO Hydro following a bare-knuckle boxing event.**

Footage shared on social media showed large groups of men apparently fighting on Finnieston Quay near the River Clyde on Saturday night, prompting a significant police response.

Police Scotland said three officers were injured while dealing with the disturbance, with two requiring medical treatment.

A 32-year-old man and a 27-year-old man have been arrested and charged with offences including assault, obstructing police and threatening or abusive behaviour. Both are expected to appear at Glasgow Sheriff Court on Monday.

A third man was arrested and issued with a recorded police warning for threatening or abusive behaviour.

Chief Inspector Paul Daley condemned the violence, saying officers had been assaulted while carrying out their duties.

He said: "Several officers were assaulted whilst carrying out their duties during disorder following an event at the Hydro in Glasgow on Saturday night.

"Violence against officers is disgraceful and will not be tolerated. Being assaulted is not part of the job and it's extremely fortunate they were not seriously injured.

"Officers are carrying out extensive enquiries in relation to the disturbance which occurred and anyone found responsible will be brought to justice."

Police have appealed for witnesses and information, asking anyone who can assist enquiries to contact officers via 101 quoting incident 2029 of November 7.

The force said it would continue to work with partner organisations and event organisers to help maintain public safety at major events.

Videos posted online showed dozens of officers and multiple police vehicles outside the Hydro as crowds dispersed following the incident. Other footage appeared to show roads around the nearby Hilton Garden Inn partially blocked, with traffic brought to a standstill as emergency services responded.

According to local reports, the disorder broke out after a Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship event held at the venue on Saturday evening.

Police enquiries remain ongoing.
