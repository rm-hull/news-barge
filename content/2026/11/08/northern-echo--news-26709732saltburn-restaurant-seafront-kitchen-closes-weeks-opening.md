A North East restaurant has made the "heartbreaking" decision to close just weeks after opening.

Seafront Kitchen, at Saltburn House, on Marine Parade, Saltburn, only opened on Sunday, September 20.

Owner Kel Allen confirmed the closure on Facebook, saying all bookings had been cancelled and no more reservations would be taken for the foreseeable future.

Kel said: "I have had to make the heartbreaking decision to close Seafront Kitchen.

"This decision wasn’t taken lightly and has been very difficult. As everyone knows me, I am moving on to something on my own and wouldn’t do this unless there was a serious reason behind it.

"The bookings are all cancelled and will not be taking anymore for the foreseeable.

"I would like to thank Saltburn House and their wonderful staff for all of the support. They have been amazing.

"My new venture begins in January. I’m very excited. Gotta (sic) keep moving."

![Saltburn House](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21440959.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Saltburn House *(Image: The Seafront Kitchen)*

Customers reacted with disappointment and confusion, with some saying they had been looking forward to visiting while others praised the food.

One person commented: "Eh? Hasn't it just opened? I've heard nothing but good things about it."

Another said: "We planned on coming here for date night. Please say you're opening somewhere else."

Others shared their disappointment saying: "Oh no, the food was delicious. What a shame."

Another added: "Wow, we didn’t get to book in and now it’s closed."

Saltburn House later clarified that only Seafront Kitchen had closed and the hotel remains open.

In a Facebook statement, the venue said: "We would just like to clarify that Saltburn House has not closed we are still fully open to guests and customers, and our bingo and live music will still be on each week

"The Seafront Kitchen has ceased its business with us and has closed down.

"Sorry if this has caused any confusion, Saltburn House is still open for business."

Saltburn House later said Seafront Kitchen had “ceased to trade and abandoned their operation” at the venue.
