POLICE have launched an investigation into an alleged theft from a restaurant in a North Yorkshire coastal town.

The incident involved the theft of a cream-coloured CP Company jacket from Abbey Wharfe in Market Place, Whitby at 10.55pm on Saturday (October 10), North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said that the jacket was stolen after it had been left draped over rope at the left side of the bar.




A police spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us.”

Please email [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Please quote reference 12260199783 when providing information.
