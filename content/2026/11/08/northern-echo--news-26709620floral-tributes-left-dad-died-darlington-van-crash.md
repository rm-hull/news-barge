Floral tributes have been left for a dad who died when his van smashed into a tree in Darlington.

The father, who was in his 40s, was pronounced dead after his LDV van hit a tree on Woodland Road shortly after 2am on Friday (November 6).

Police said he was the only person in the van at the time and no other vehicles were involved.

The van was left with serious damage to its front end and wedged up against a tree.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678182.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

Floral tributes have been left at the scene in memory of the driver, who has not yet been named.

One card reads: "To dad, I love you so much. Lots of Love Pop xx".

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21684935.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes left at the scene of the Woodland Road crash *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

Other cards read, "you are in my thoughts", and "you'll be missed".

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21684934.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes left at the scene of the Woodland Road crash *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

The road was cordoned off while crash investigators examined the scene, and the wreckage of the van was removed.

A Durham Police investigation into the crash is ongoing.

![Police presence on Woodland Road following the crash](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678319.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The road was cordoned off by police. *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A force spokesperson said: "Anybody who witnessed the collision, or who has dashcam footage of the van, is asked to contact the Serious Collision Investigation Unit via [sciu@durham.police.uk](mailto:sciu@durham.police.uk) quoting incident number 28 of November 6."

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said previously: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Woodland Road in Darlington at 2.05am on Friday.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene."
