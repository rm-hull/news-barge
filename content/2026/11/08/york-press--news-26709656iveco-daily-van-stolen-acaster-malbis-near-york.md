An Iveco Daily van with significant damage to the passenger side door, including a missing window, has been reported stolen near York.

The alleged theft took place between 1.15pm on November 4 and 4.30am on November 5 from the Appleton Road area of Acaster Malbis.




North Yorkshire Police are appealing for information from anyone who may have seen the vehicle or witnessed suspicious activity during that time.

A police spokesperson said: "The vehicle is a white IVECO DAILY, registration YK03 HWZ, which had significant damage to the passenger side door, including a missing window.

"If you can assist, please call 101 quoting reference 12260217645 or report online using: Home | North Yorkshire Police."
