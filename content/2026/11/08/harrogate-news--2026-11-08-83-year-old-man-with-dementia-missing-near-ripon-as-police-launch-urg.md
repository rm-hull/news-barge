**Now found**

Police are appealing for the public’s help to find an 83-year-old man who has gone missing from the Sawley area near Ripon.

Rodney Green was last seen in Sawley at around 12 noon on Sunday, 8 November 2026. He lives with dementia and may appear confused or disorientated.

Concerns for Rodney’s welfare are growing as time passes, and extensive searches are under way to locate him.

Rodney is described as a White British man with grey hair and a stooped posture. He may be wearing a black woolly hat, a dark-coloured fleece, jogging bottoms and mismatched shoes.

He is known to walk across fields, footpaths and tracks in the local area.

Police searches are being supported by specialist resources, including drones and the National Police Air Service helicopter.

Residents, landowners and businesses in Sawley and the wider Ripon area are being asked to check sheds, outbuildings, garages, barns and other places where Rodney may have sought shelter.

Anyone who sees Rodney is asked to call 999 immediately.

Anyone with information that could help the search is asked to call 101 and select option 1 to speak to the Force Control Room.

Please quote reference **NYP-08112026-0262** when providing information.
