Representatives of Yorkshire Water will face Ripon residents and councillors at a meeting in the town hall this Monday (9th November) evening.

It’s been called over concern about failures in protecting the rivers of the city from sewage pollution.

Yorkshire Water’s Corporate Affairs Manager and one of its River Health Team Managers will join the Full Council meeting, at **6pm in the town hall**, to discuss sewage discharges and plans to improve river health.

Residents can attend, as is usual with all city council meeting.

Ripon’s mayor, Cllr Andrew Williams, is chairing it and explained how it will work.

“Members of the public will be able to speak first and raise their concerns then I will put those concerns directly to Yorkshire Water on their behalf.

There’s a lot of anger not just in Ripon but all over the Yorkshire Water region”
