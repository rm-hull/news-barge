Police are investigating damage done to a Dales hotel.

It happened overnight on Sunday to Monday 1-2 November at the Wensleydale Hotel, Market Place, Middleham.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It is believed to have been caused by a vehicle colliding with the building.
