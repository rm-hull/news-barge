Reports have emerged of a police presence on a residential street in Idle on Saturday evening.

Witnesses reported officers attending Albert avenue at around 6.30pm on November 7, although the nature of the incident remains unclear.

## What has been reported?

A reader contacted the Telegraph & Argus to report that there had been "some kind of incident" on the street.

In photos provided for the T&A, two police cars can be seen heading down the road, whilst another appears to be parked by other vehicles.

No further details about what happened have been established at this stage.

## Police yet to confirm details

West Yorkshire Police has been approached for comment regarding the reported incident.

It is not yet known why officers attended, whether any arrests were made or if any enquiries are currently ongoing.

The Telegraph & Argus will update this story as soon as further information is released by West Yorkshire Police.
