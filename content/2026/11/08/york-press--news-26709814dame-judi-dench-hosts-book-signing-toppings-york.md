Dame Judi Dench has delighted customers during a visit to a York bookshop, where she signed copies of her newest work.

The household name, who grew up York, returned to the city today and was spotted signing copies of her memoir Is It Too Late to Make a Run for It? in Toppings in Museum Street.

The work details Dame Judi’s extraordinary life both on the stage and on screen and has become an instant Sunday Times Bestseller.




After the signing, the 91-year-old is back entertaining those at York Theatre Royal as part of her memoir tour.

There she will be joined by writing partner Brendan O’Hea for a conversation which looks set to unveil details of her life never discussed before.

The six-stop tour began in Richmond, London on October 20 and will make its way to Nottingham, Canterbury and the Theatre Royal Haymarket after York.

Toppings thanked Dame Judi for stopping by at the shop in an Instagram post.

A representative said: “Our bookshop has been graced today with a visit from a York legend - the wonderful Dame Judi Dench herself.

“Huge thank you Judi for visiting the bookshop.”
