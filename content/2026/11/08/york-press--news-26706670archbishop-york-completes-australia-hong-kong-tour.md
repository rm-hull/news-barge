## Archbishop of York takes message of hope during international visits

The Archbishop of York has completed a visit to Australia and Hong Kong to help encourage the sharing of the gospel *(Image: Archbishop of York)*

The Archbishop of York has completed a visit to Australia and Hong Kong.

Archbishop Stephen Cottrell travelled to Perth, Brisbane and Hong Kong to encourage Anglican churches, schools and communities in their mission of sharing the gospel.

His visit began in Western Australia at the invitation of Archbishop Kay Goldsworthy AO, where he preached at the opening Eucharist of the Diocese of Perth's 52nd Synod at St George's Cathedral.

During the Synod, he addressed members, offering greetings from the Church of England and supporting Archbishop Goldsworthy's leadership.

He also preached at St George's Cathedral on the Feast of St Luke, urging worshippers to "recognise their calling to be Christ's hands and feet in the world, sharing God's love, hope and compassion through their daily lives".

In Brisbane, Archbishop Cottrell joined Archbishop Jeremy Greaves in preaching and meeting with clergy from the Anglican Church of Southern Queensland.

He then travelled to Hong Kong for his first official visit to the Province of Hong Kong.

There, he took part in worship services at St John’s Cathedral and All Saints Cathedral, which is marking its 135th year.

He also welcomed the formal approval of a new companion link between the Diocese of Leeds and the Diocese of Hong Kong Island.
