# Peak District: Five teenagers seriously hurt in crash as police release injury update

## Five teenagers have been seriously injured in a car crash in the Peak District.

Derbyshire Constabulary said officers were called to the scene of the single-vehicle collision on the A5270 Old Coalpit Lane, close to the junction of the A515 just after 2am on Saturday.

Three teenage girls and two teenage boys were in the blue Suzuki Swift when it crashed near the village of Chelmorton, Derbyshire, and they were taken to hospital with serious injuries, police said.

Their injuries are not thought to be life-threatening and the teenagers’ parents have all been informed.

Officers are investigating and want to speak to anyone who may have been driving in the area at the time and may have seen the Suzuki before the crash.

Anyone who has information which might help is asked to contact the force by calling 101 and quoting reference 26000688247.
