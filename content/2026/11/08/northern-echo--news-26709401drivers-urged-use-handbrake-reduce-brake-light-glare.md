A new motoring debate is putting a spotlight on the glare from modern brake lights.

Drivers have taken to Reddit to complain about the brightness of LED brake lights, claiming they are just as dazzling as some of today’s powerful headlamps.

Some motorists say the intense glow from these lights can be distracting or uncomfortable when stuck in traffic, especially during dark winter evenings.

The discussion began after one driver urged others to use their handbrake when stationary to avoid blinding those behind them.

Gareth Roberts from LeaseCar.uk said: "It’s easy to see why this topic has generated so much debate online.

"In recent years we’ve seen a lot of discussion about whether modern LED headlights are too bright.

"Drivers often complain about how bright oncoming lights are and how they can leave them dazzled.

"Brake lights can cause a similar problem to drivers behind, but it’s something many motorists may not think about."

While there is no specific penalty for keeping a foot on the brake pedal in traffic, the Highway Code does offer guidance.

Rule 114 states that in stationary queues, drivers should apply the parking brake and remove their foot from the footbrake once following traffic has stopped.

This turns off the rear brake lights and helps reduce glare for the driver behind.

Not all road users agreed with the complaints, pointing out that it was unavoidable in newer vehicles with an auto-hold function.

However, many drivers admitted they leave their foot on the brake.

Mr Roberts said: "The Highway Code recommends you take your foot off the footbrake once following traffic has come to a stop.

"Where it’s safe and practical to do so, it’s a small change that can help reduce glare and make journeys more comfortable for other road users."

With dark commutes set to continue for months, drivers are being reminded to consider the impact their brake lights can have on others.
