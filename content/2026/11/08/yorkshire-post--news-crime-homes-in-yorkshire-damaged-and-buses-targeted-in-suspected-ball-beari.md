# Homes in Yorkshire damaged and buses targeted in suspected ball bearing gun attacks

## Police are investigating

Homes have been damaged and three buses targeted in suspected ball bearing or pellet gun attacks in Wakefield.

Councillor Nadeem Ahmed (Conservative/Wakefield South) said he has seen four photographs of damaged windows - and said ball bearings had been found - following the incidents in Agbrigg late last month.

Police have confirmed they are investigating a number of reports of damage to homes in Agbrigg and Arriva say three buses have been struck by projectiles.

![A ball bearing found after a window was smashed at an address in Agbrigg](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmNmN2ExZGM2LTYxN2QtNDc0MC04MzlhLTQxZDE4NmMyNDE2NTpmOGViY2U5Yy0yYWI3LTRlYTYtOGFhNS1iZTJiYzRhOGRlZTY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=480&quality=65)


West Yorkshire Police are believed to be investigating if the ball bearings were fired from a ball bearing or pellet gun or a catapult.

Coun Ahmed said: "A lady got in touch with me to say she had heard two noises. She went to her window and saw two marks which distinctly looked like a ball bearing or a pellet gun.

"A number of residents contacted me around the same time to say they have damage to windows on their homes.

"It appears Doncaster Road and Agbrigg Road were being targeted with somebody with a pellet gun or BB gun."

![A damaged window at an address in Agbrigg](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjBjNzJjNGQ3LWE4OGUtNDg4Ny1iNjk1LWRkOTU4NmM1MjA1NTozZGQ3ZmU4Mi00YzUxLTQxZmItOGQ5Yy0xZWE0MzQwNTcyMzc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"Everybody who has seen the marks are saying it's a ball bearing or pellet gun which has damaged windows. I have seen four photographs of damaged windows."

A police spokesperson said: "Police have received a number of reports of properties being damaged in and around Doncaster Road and Agbrigg Road on the evenings of Monday October 26 and Tuesday October 27.

"A report was also received of criminal damage caused to a bus travelling on Doncaster Road. This was reported to have taken place shortly before 8pm on Monday October 26 .

"Enquiries are ongoing."

![Damage to a window and a bal bearing at an address in Agbrigg](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmZlNGM0YmE5LTQ5NTctNDcyYS1hYmY2LTg0MmUyY2FlYmM4ZDo0MzU4YmM5ZC1hNTlkLTQzNjQtODIwNS0zNTYyZTYyMjQ3ZTI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=444&quality=65)


A spokesperson for Arriva Yorkshire said: “Three of our buses, including the 148 service, were damaged by projectiles in Wakefield on Tuesday, October 27.

“Several windows were broken and no one was injured.

“We reported the incidents to West Yorkshire Police and are supporting their enquiries. Anyone with information should contact the police.”

Anyone with information about the incidents is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online via Live Chat on our website quoting log 1554 of 26/10.

Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
