## Emmerdale star leads cast of gripping stage thriller coming to York

Lewis Cope *(Image: Grand Opera House York)*

A new psychological thriller is coming to the stage in York next year.

Strangers on a Train, a stage adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s novel, will run at the Grand Opera House in York from Tuesday, February 2, to Saturday, February 7, 2027, as part of a UK tour.

Lewis Cope and Jonny Labey will star as Charles Bruno and Guy Haines, two strangers whose chance meeting leads to a deadly pact.

David Gilbery, CEO, Bill Kenwright Ltd, commented: "We’re delighted to have Lewis and Jonny taking on two of Patricia Highsmith’s most fascinating characters in our new production of Strangers on a Train.

"They are both immensely talented performers, and with Roxana Silbert directing, alongside designer Robert Innes Hopkins, and the rest of our wonderful creative team, this has all the ingredients for a truly thrilling stage production."

Mr Cope is best known for his role as Nicky Milligan in Emmerdale and reached week eleven of the 2025 series of Strictly Come Dancing with partner Katya Jones.

Mr Labey originated the lead role of Scott Hastings in the West End production of Baz Luhrmann’s Strictly Ballroom.

He has also appeared in Hollyoaks and EastEnders.
