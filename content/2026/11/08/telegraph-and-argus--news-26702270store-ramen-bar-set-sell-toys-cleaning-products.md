A Korean-inspired convenience store with a ramen bar will open next month.

Ninety Sixx, at 96 High Street in Wibsey, Bradford, will open on December 1.

The shop is being refurbished ahead of the opening and will sell ramen, iced coffees, Korean-inspired snacks and drinks, sweet treats and everyday essentials.

## Excitement for store

Shop manager Umair Khan said: “The response has been amazing.

"Most people are excited about the ramen bar and iced coffee.

“I thought I would bring something new to the High Street.

“We’ll have the ramen bar area in store and there will be some seating inside for customers to eat.

“People can also take it away or get delivery. Since Covid, grocery stores have been added to delivery platforms.”

## How will the ramen bar work?

Mr Khan also explained how the in-store ramen bar will work.

“Customers will choose a pack of instant ramen and we’ll make it up.

“They can build their ramen however they want, with ingredients such as cheese.

“It’ll be great for teenagers who want a quick meal.

“The shop is Korean inspired as we’ll be selling food from a Korean noodle supplier.”

## 'Ninety Sixx will bring something different'

Mr Khan said his father, who owns the building, is at retirement age, giving him the chance to start his own business.

He said: “I’ve worked in retail all my life, and I’ve always wanted to open my own business.

“I think Ninety Sixx will bring something different to Wibsey.

“We’re on track for opening on December 1. All of the branding and signs have gone up this week.”

## What to expect from the store

Mr Khan recently shared his excitement about the business on social media.

In a Facebook post, Ninety Sixx wrote: “We've been a little quiet over the last few days, but trust me, a lot has been happening behind the scenes.

“We've made some big changes around the store, and here's just a little sneak peek of what's been going on:

* Completely changed the counters and ordered brand-new glass ones
* Added another ramen bar station
* Moved our pendant lights to give the store a fresh new look
* Unboxed our brand-new display fridge
* Installed strip lighting around the store to bring everything together
* And so much more that you'll have to wait and see

“It's been a very busy few days, with so much happening at once.

“But I promise you, the wait will be worth it.

“We're saving the big reveal until the end, and I cannot wait for you to see it.”

“Who’s excited? We are.”
