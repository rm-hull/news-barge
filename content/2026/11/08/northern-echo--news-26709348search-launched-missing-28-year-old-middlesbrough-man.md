A search has been launched for a missing 28-year-old who has not been seen for four days.

Joshua Ayre-Monteiro, known as Josh, was last seen at his home on Scholars Rise, Middlesbrough, around 5.30am on Thursday (November 5).

Cleveland Police officers say they are becoming increasingly concerned for his welfare and are appealing to the public for help in tracing him.

When he was last seen, Josh was wearing black Under Armour tracksuit bottoms, black Asics trainers with a white sole, a dark green and black Regatta jacket, and black fluffy earmuffs.

Police said he was also carrying a carrier bag and had his white Rally bike with him.

Anyone who has seen Josh or who has information about his whereabouts is asked to contact Cleveland Police by calling 101 and quoting reference 223476.
