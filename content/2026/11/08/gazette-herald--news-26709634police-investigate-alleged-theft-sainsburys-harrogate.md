Police has launched an investigation into an alleged theft in a North Yorkshire supermarket.

The incident happened in Sainsburys, in Leeds Road, Harrogate on October 14.




A force spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the people pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.

• Email: PC 1824 Chatland - [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk)

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

“If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

“Please quote reference 12260203274 when providing information.”
