**A man who died in a crash on the A94 has been named.**

The crash occurred between Glamis and Meigle on Thursday, November 5, 2026, around 6.05pm and involved a white Audi A1 and a dark-coloured Ford Focus.

The driver of the Audi was pronounced dead at the scene.

Police Scotland has confirmed that he was Michael Cruickshank, aged 34, from the Alyth area.

His family has asked for privacy and issued the following statement: “Michael will be missed by many. We would like to thank everyone for the messages received so far and now need privacy while we come to terms with our loss.”

The driver of the Ford, a 24-year-old man, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

Sergeant Michael Guild said: “Our thoughts remain with Michael’s family at what is a very difficult time for them.

“Our enquiries to establish the full circumstances are ongoing. Anyone who has not already spoken to officers and can assist is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2728 of Thursday, 5 November, 2026.”
