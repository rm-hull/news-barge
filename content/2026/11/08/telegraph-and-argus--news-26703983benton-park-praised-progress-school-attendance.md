IMPROVED attendance has earned a school national recognition.

Benton Park School has been named among the stronger-performing schools serving comparable communities in the Department for Education’s Similar Schools Attendance Report.

It has also received a series of national FFT School Attendance Awards, recognising its overall attendance, performance compared with similar schools and the scale of improvement achieved.

Nik Skilton, headteacher at Benton Park, said: "We are incredibly proud of the progress being made at Benton Park.

"Improved attendance, stronger examination results and national recognition are all important indicators that the school is moving forward.

"This recognition belongs to our whole school community.

"I would like to thank our staff, pupils, parents and carers for their continued commitment and support."

The awards follow the introduction of the school’s new vision, 'Together we belong, develop character and achieve excellence.'

This work is focused on ensuring that every pupil feels known, valued and part of the school community.

Mr Skilton said the improvement in attendance reflects "a genuine partnership between school and home."

Benton Park has also recorded its strongest exam results in recent years.

The school continues to develop as part of Moorlands Learning Trust.

The school’s focus remains on raising expectations, improving the quality of education and securing better outcomes for pupils.
