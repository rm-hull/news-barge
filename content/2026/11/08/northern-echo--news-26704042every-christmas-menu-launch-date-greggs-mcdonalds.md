The Christmas season has officially landed in the North East.

From Greggs' much-loved Festive Bake to Bella Italia’s Camembert Pizza, high street chains across the region have already begun unveiling their festive menus.

More chains are set to join in the coming weeks, with KFC, Toby Carvery, and McDonald’s among those preparing to launch their seasonal offerings.

Here’s a roundup of what’s already available for North East festive foodies, and they have to look forward to over the coming weeks.

Festive menus already available:

* Costa Coffee: Festive coffees, hot chocolates, and Christmas toasties.

* Pret A Manger: Christmas Lunch Sandwich and festive baguettes.

* PizzaExpress: Christmas Crackler Pizza and Dough Balls in Blankets.

* Burger King: Ultimate Camembert Wagyu and Festive Whopper.

* Bella Italia: Camembert Pizza and Christmas Carbonara.

* Caffè Nero: Festive lattes and Christmas bakery items.

* Subway: Ultimate Festive Sub and Gravy Mayo.

* Starbucks: Christmas drinks and bakery treats.

* TGI Fridays: Triple Stacked Jingle Bell Burger.

* Beefeater: Traditional festive dinners and party menus.

Christmas items coming soon:

* KFC- coming November 9: 'Gravymas' Burger and Festive Loaded Fries.

* Frankie & Benny’s- coming November 10: Festive burgers and desserts.

* Chiquito- coming November 10: Tex-Mex Christmas menu.

* Toby Carvery- coming November 11: Traditional festive roasts with all the trimmings.

* Harvester- coming November 11: Christmas burgers and turkey dinners.

* Greene King- coming November 12: Christmas dinners and festive puddings.

* Miller & Carter- coming November 17: Premium Christmas steak menu.

* ASK Italian- coming November 17: Christmas set menu with seasonal pasta dishes and festive desserts.

* Zizzi- coming November 17: Christmas set menu featuring festive pizzas, pasta dishes and desserts.

* McDonald’s- coming November 20: Festive Stack and seasonal desserts.

Supermarkets have joined the festive food race too, with chains including Marks & Spencer, Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Morrisons, Aldi, Lidl, Waitrose, Co-op and Iceland all unveiling Christmas food ranges, from festive sandwiches and party food to turkey feasts, luxury desserts and seasonal sweet treats.

Whether you're rushing out to grab a Greggs Festive Bake, planning a Christmas coffee run at Starbucks, or waiting for festive burgers from McDonald's, there are plenty of seasonal treats on the way.

With festive menus available now and rolling out through to the second half of November, Christmas food season has officially begun across the North East.
