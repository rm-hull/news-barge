A CARE home has paid tribute to a music legend with a day of celebration.

Tees Grange in Stockton honoured Dolly Parton with a full day of music and dancing, a themed quiz, candy floss, and an American-style feast.

The care home, located on Norton Road, supports adults with complex mental health needs, neuro-disabilities, and physical disabilities, and several of its residents are big fans of the country music star.

Sarah Ibrahim, home manager at Tees Grange, said: "Dolly’s music means a lot to many of the people we support, so we wanted to celebrate her in a way that felt joyful.

"Days like this matter because they give people something to look forward to and the chance to do something they love with the people around them."

The activities hub was transformed into a Dolly Parton-themed space with music, dancing, and decorations.

Residents joined in a Dolly Parton quiz and enjoyed making their own candy floss at a dedicated stand.

The kitchen team prepared a Dolly-themed banquet featuring corn dogs, hamburgers, and other American favourites.
