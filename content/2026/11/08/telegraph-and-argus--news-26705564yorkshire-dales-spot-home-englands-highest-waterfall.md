The Yorkshire Dales is a hugely popular spot with hikers thanks to its great walking trails and many waterfalls to see.

If you have been to the Yorkshire Dales before and do not know where to head next, or if it is your first time ever visiting, you might want to try out a trip to the highest cascading waterfall in England.

Cautley Spout waterfall can be found right on the edge of the Yorkshire Dales National Park and is described as being “a beautiful location” that is ideal for a “lovely walk”.

## What is Cautley Spout waterfall like?

Cautley Spout waterfall can be seen for miles and is found not too far from the town of Sedbergh.

As you walk up to the waterfall, you will be able to see beautiful views of the surrounding countryside.

There are plenty of signs pointing you in the correct direction, making it easy to reach the bottom of the falls.

To reach the top, you have to follow a steep pathway, but it is worth it for the views at the top.

For a short walk, you can park near the Cross Keys Inn.

The inn is looked after by the National Trust and has rooms for those wanting to stay in the area for a little longer.

You can also just pop in for a meal, with light lunches available like pies, Whitby scampi, and Wensleydale tart.

The inn also has a Sunday lunch menu where you can enjoy roast beef served with a Yorkshire pudding, roast potatoes, mash, and seasonal vegetables.

### What do reviews say about Cautley Spout waterfall?

Cautley Spout Waterfall has a rating of 4.7 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with 70 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “Lovely walk and splash in the falls.

“We didn’t walk all the way to the top as we’ve climbed rather a lot this week and the kids wanted a splash: so that’s what we did.

“It was a nice walk over the bridge and following the footpath and then a walk up the smaller falls and a play in them.

“Would absolutely return for more of the same.”

Another person said: “We did a lovely walk from the parking layby through stunning scenery and up towards the waterfall.

“It looked amazing, and was a very peaceful and scenic location.

“The last bit was really steep, but well worth the effort to see the falls at their best - the recent rainfall had made them particularly spectacular. Well worthwhile.”
