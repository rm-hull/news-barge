FIREFIGHTERS have been called to a vehicle fire in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place in Abbey Lane, Whitby at 8:50pm on Saturday (November 7).




Upon the fire crew’s arrival, the vehicle was found to be “well alight”.

A North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished and dampened down using two hose reel jets and two breathing apparatus.

“The incident was left with the Police.”
