A LOGISTICS firm is marking a major milestone with a long-standing industry partnership.

Pink Link, based in Huddersfield, is celebrating 30 years as part of the Palletways palletised freight network, a collaboration that has driven the company’s growth across Yorkshire and beyond.

Over the past three decades, Pink Link has expanded its fleet, workforce and operations, supporting customers and the Palletways network in Huddersfield, Bradford, Halifax and Rochdale.

James Ince, commercial operations manager at Pink Link, said: "Joining Palletways 30 years ago was a key step for our business, and we’ve never looked back.

"Being part of the network has helped us grow, adapt and take on new challenges, but what stands out most is the people.

"We’ve built strong relationships with our teams, customers, suppliers and fellow members over the years, and that sense of collaboration has been a huge part of our success.

"We’re proud of what we’ve achieved since first joining Palletways and look forward to building on it."

In 2025, the company received the Member’s Choice Award in recognition of its dedication and support within the Palletways network.

Since joining the network, Pink Link has weathered major industry shifts, including the move from paper-based processes to digital systems such as archway scanning and Palletways ID.

The partnership has also helped the business navigate challenges like the global financial crises, the Covid-19 pandemic and ongoing driver shortages, by sharing experiences and solutions with other network members.

Looking ahead, Pink Link plans to invest further in its fleet and workforce, with a focus on attracting younger talent to the logistics sector.

The company also aims to develop its palletised freight operations alongside its newer general haulage and international brokerage services.

Warwick Trimble, UK network director at Palletways, said: "Pink Link has been a real success story within the Palletways network, and it has been a pleasure for our team to watch the business continue to go from strength to strength over the past 30 years.

"Pink Link’s growth is a testament to the team’s hard work, and we’re proud to have been part of its journey.

"We look forward to seeing what the next chapter holds."

Pink Link is one of more than 130 independent transport providers in the Palletways UK network.

The Palletways Group operates more than 450 depots and 20 hub operations, delivering services across 24 European countries, including the UK.
