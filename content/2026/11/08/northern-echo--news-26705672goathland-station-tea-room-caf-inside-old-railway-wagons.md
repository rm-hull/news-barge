A quirky North Yorkshire café is offering visitors the chance to enjoy tea and homemade cakes inside historic railway wagons at one of Britain's best-known heritage stations.

Goathland Station Tea Room, in the North York Moors, is unlike most traditional countryside cafés.

Instead of ordinary tables and chairs, customers can take their seats inside original railway goods wagons housed within a converted goods shed.

And while enjoying their refreshments, visitors can watch heritage trains arriving and departing from the platform outside.

The tearoom forms part of Goathland Station on the North Yorkshire Moors Railway, which attracts visitors from across the country.

The station is famous for appearing as Hogsmeade Station in the first Harry Potter film, Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679234.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Goathland Station Tea Room, in the North York Moors *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

It was also used as Aidensfield Station in the long-running ITV drama Heartbeat, giving it a special place in television history.

Today, the station retains much of its traditional appearance, with period features and heritage trains helping recreate the atmosphere of a bygone railway.

The café itself is housed in a converted goods shed, with original wagons forming an unusual dining area.

Its menu includes hot and cold drinks, savoury food, homemade cakes, sweet treats and ice creams.

The railway describes the tearoom as a place where visitors can watch trains coming and going while enjoying refreshments.

For railway enthusiasts, the opportunity to sit inside a historic goods wagon while watching steam trains is an attraction in itself.

Goathland is surrounded by some of the North York Moors National Park's most distinctive scenery, with walking routes connecting the village to neighbouring settlements and attractions.

One popular route is the Rail Trail between Goathland and Grosmont.

The three-and-a-half-mile walk follows a former railway route through the countryside, with visitors able to combine the walk with a heritage train journey.

Another nearby attraction is Mallyan Spout, a waterfall reached by a footpath from the village.

The waterfall drops around 70 feet and has long been a popular destination for walkers visiting the area.

Goathland itself is also known for its traditional buildings, independent shops and surrounding moorland.

Its television connections continue to attract fans of Heartbeat, while the railway station remains a favourite destination for Harry Potter enthusiasts.

The café's opening times follow the North Yorkshire Moors Railway's operating timetable, so visitors should check before travelling.

Goathland Station Tea Room is at Goathland Station, near Whitby, North Yorkshire, YO22 5NF.
