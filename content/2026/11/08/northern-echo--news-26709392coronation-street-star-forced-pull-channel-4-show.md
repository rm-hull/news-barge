A Coronation Street star has had to pull out of a Channel 4 show at the last minute.

It was announced today that Will Mellor would no longer be able to appear on Channel 4’s Sunday Brunch this morning (Sunday, November 8).

It was shared that Will was forced to pull out of the show because of illness.

## Will Mellor forced to pull out of Sunday Brunch at the last minute

In a post on social media, Sunday Brunch said: “Unfortunately, Will Mellor is unable to join us on tomorrow’s show due to illness.

“Wishing you a speedy recovery, Will!

“We look forward to having you back on the show soon.”

Many fans of the show took to the comments to share support for Will with many people wishing him to “get well soon”.

Another person said: “Was looking forward to this - get well soon.”

The actor is known for appearing in the popular ITV soap Coronation Street as Harvey Gaskell but has featured in a number of other TV shows.

Will has starred in many other soaps including Hollyoaks playing Jambo Bolton and EastEnders playing Warren Stamp.

More recently, he has starred in A Woman of Substance as Jack Harte and played Jimmy Spencer in The Teacher.

While Will will not be appearing on the show, Sunday Brunch will still feature Gregory Porter, Philippa Dunne, and Daniel Weyman.

There will also be musical performances from Max Pope and The Djangos.

Sunday Brunch will be broadcast on Channel 4 tomorrow at 10am.

### **What is Sunday Brunch?**

Sunday Brunch is a talk show presented by Tim Lovejoy and Simon Rimmer mixing interviews with celebrity guests and cooking segments.

The show features cooking demonstrations from Simon as well as drink tasting with an expert, a trend round-up section, and a Sunday Brunch playlist that is chosen by the presenters and guests each week.
