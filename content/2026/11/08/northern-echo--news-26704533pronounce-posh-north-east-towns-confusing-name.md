England has lots of towns and villages with some very strange names that only locals will know how to pronounce.

If you are not from the North East, there are quite a few town names that will definitely catch non-locals out.

Ponteland is a town in Northumberland and is home to some of the most expensive houses in the North East thanks to its proximity to the airport and Newcastle.

If you are unsure how to say the town’s name, here is a guide on how to pronounce Ponteland.

## How to pronounce the town of Ponteland

If you are heading to Ponteland any time soon, you might want to know how to say the town’s name to impress the locals.

There are a few different ways people might pronounce Ponteland, so it is very easy to be caught out.

Some people might say it like ponty-land or pont-land.

Others might even pronounce it like pon-tel-und.

However, these are all incorrect.

It is actually said pon-tee-land.

TikTok content creator Liam Lav posted a video about places in the North East that people often mispronounce, with Ponteland making the list.

Many people took to the comments to share their thoughts, with one person saying: “My late Austrian nanna used to say Ponty-land, made me laugh.”

### What is Ponteland like?

The town of Ponteland is quite an expensive place to live, with the average price of properties in the area being £563,906, according to Rightmove.

The majority of properties sold over the last year were detached houses at a cost of around £766,732.

The area has plenty of eateries to spend some time in, like Sip ‘n’ Loaf, where you can enjoy a hot cup of coffee with a slice of homemade cake or an open sourdough sandwich.

For a sit-down meal, you can pop into Fratelli for an “authentic taste of Italian and Sardinian cuisine”.
