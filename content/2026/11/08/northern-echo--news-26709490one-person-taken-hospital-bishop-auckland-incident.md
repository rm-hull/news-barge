A person has been taken to hospital following an incident in Bishop Auckland.

Emergency services including the air ambulance were called shortly after 9am this morning (Sunday, November 8), with the helicopter landing near Bishop Auckland College.

The nature of the incident and seriousness of the person's injuries are not yet known.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) said: “We were called to an incident at a private address in Bishop Auckland shortly after 9am.

“We dispatched two ambulance crews and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS).

“One patient was taken to Darlington Memorial Hospital by road.”

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Police and the Great North Air Ambulance Service for comment.
