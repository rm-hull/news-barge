PERCHED on an elevated spot above the banks of the River Leven, The Wheelhouse is one of those rare homes that manages to be striking and practical in equal measure.

Its bold contemporary architecture is sure to turn heads, but step inside and you’ll find a house that has been designed first and foremost for family life – and with more than 5,000 sq. ft of accommodation arranged over three floors, there’s certainly no shortage of room for everyone.

![The Wheelhouse, Yarm](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675654.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Head turning style and a wonderful location combine to make The Wheelhouse truly special *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

The house has been designed around an impressive central staircase which rises through the very heart of the building.

Greeting you as you come through the entrance porch into the generous hallway, it gives the interior a wonderful sense of scale while linking each floor in a way that feels carefully considered rather than simply grand.

The ground floor offers a generous choice of living space including a lounge, a separate dining room for more formal occasions, a study for anyone who wishes to work from home, a cloakroom and a boot room – a real asset for a family with muddy boots, dogs or both.

The defining room – and one likely to take your breath away – is the sitting room.

![The Wheelhouse, Yarm](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675644.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Head turning style and a wonderful location combine to make The Wheelhouse truly special *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Here the ceiling soars to double height, with a galleried landing looking down from above and full-height windows providing a stunning focal point that draws natural light in and frames the view across the garden.

Exposed brick walls, parquet flooring and a feature fireplace all add a tremendous amount of character.

For all its scale, this room remains a comfortable and inviting space – impressive when you’re entertaining a crowd yet equally suited to everyday family life.

At the heart of the home is the bespoke dining kitchen, accessed via large crittall sliding doors from the sitting room and with a door to the garden.

![The Wheelhouse, Yarm](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675651.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Head turning style and a wonderful location combine to make The Wheelhouse truly special *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Certain to be a natural gathering place for family and friends, it is centred around a large island unit with breakfast bar seating.

Its contemporary detailing sits comfortably within the wider architecture of the house, and there’s plenty of space for serious cooking as well as relaxed informal dining.

The two upper floors are home to six bedrooms in all.

On the first floor are the generously proportioned principal suite which has been designed as a private retreat, with the luxury of separate his and hers dressing rooms, excellent storage and a stylish bathroom.

Also on this floor is a guest bedroom with its own en suite, along with two further bedrooms which share a Jack ‘n’ Jill bathroom – an arrangement that’s ideal for children or teenagers.

![The Wheelhouse, Yarm](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675649.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Head turning style and a wonderful location combine to make The Wheelhouse truly special *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

Upstairs again on the second floor are two more bedrooms, one with an en suite and the other which could easily be turned into a sitting room or games room, making it a superb hideaway for older children, visiting relatives or guests.

The gardens are mainly laid to lawn, with fenced boundaries and lovely views down to and across the River Leven, while an inner courtyard creates a sheltered spot perfect for alfresco dining on summer evenings.

A private driveway provides parking for numerous vehicles, with planning permission for a garage currently being sought from the local authority.

![The Wheelhouse, Yarm](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675655.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Head turning style and a wonderful location combine to make The Wheelhouse truly special *(Image: GSC GRAYS)*

As well as being close to Yarm high street, the location is excellent for commuters, with the A66, A1 and A19 all within easy reach.

Contemporary architecture, generous space and a peaceful riverside setting within easy reach of one of the area’s most attractive market towns combine to offer a home that’s unique – and well worth a closer look.

Buyers should note that the vendor is planning an exclusive development adjacent to The Wheelhouse, for which planning approval is currently being sought, and there is a further, larger development planned at the top of Leven Bank Road.
