Gardeners' World presenter Adam Frost has praised a York firm which he has been working with for more than 30 years.

Adam said he has been using York Handmade bricks, made in Alne, near Easingwold, for projects since the 1990s.

He has been supplied with 3,600 bricks and 1,500 square pavers for a new garden in Lincolnshire.

“I have been using York Handmade bricks on projects since the mid-1990s. They have a magic about them, so to use them on my new garden after all these years is a bit of a dream come true,” Adam said.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21682010.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gardeners' World presenter Adam Frost working on a project with York Handmade bricks *(Image: Supplied)*

He said the bricks are “of the highest quality, and it still blows my mind a little that I first laid them when I was working for the late Geoff Hamilton and I still use them all these years on”.

Adam also said the bricks “really complement our limestone barn”, adding: “They provide warmth and complement our local brick. The garden is set in the countryside, and I wanted it to have a sense of place. I want it to sit comfortably in the landscape, as if it’s been there forever.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21682013.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gardeners' World presenter Adam Frost working on a project with York Handmade bricks *(Image: Supplied)*

"I’ve been very influenced by the work of the poet John Clare (1793–1864), who celebrated the English countryside. The countryside he walked has really inspired the layout of the garden.

"As for planting, I want to create a garden – a bit like my previous one – that is hopefully a little more future-proof, so I’m adding in a wide diversity of plants.

"There are various types of soil here, and I’ve realised that the land at the end of the garden floods a little bit; it’s going to be fascinating.

"We’ve spent the winter putting in a few trees and digging out for walls, terraces and pathways.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21682011.jpg?type=mds-article-620)'I have been using York Handmade bricks on projects since the mid-1990s,' says Gardeners' World presenter Adam Frost *(Image: Supplied)*

A York Handmade spokesperson said it has had an “excellent relationship with Adam” for more than three decades.

“We have supplied bricks to Adam and to his clients ever since then, including to Adam’s gold medal-winning Chelsea Flower Show Garden in 2008,” they said.

“It is an honour and a privilege to provide bricks and pavers for Adam’s stunning new garden.

"It’s been great seeing him lay them on the latest series of the BBC’s Gardeners’ World.

"Everyone who watches the programme knows how passionate he is about his new house and garden. It is wonderful to see our bricks and pavers playing their part in this exciting project.

"These are challenging times for UK brickmakers, so it is very reassuring to maintain strong relationships with leading UK designers and horticulturalists such as Adam, who see the beauty and the value in handmade bricks – and understand how they can help to create the perfect garden.”
