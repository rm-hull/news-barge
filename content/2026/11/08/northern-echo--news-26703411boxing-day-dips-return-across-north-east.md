For many people, Boxing Day means leftovers, football and a walk along the beach. For others, it means pulling on a Santa hat and heading into the North Sea.

Thousands of people are expected to take part in festive dips across the North East on December 26, with events raising money for charities including the RNLI, Northumberland Hospice, Red Sky Foundation, Seaham Coastguard and Teesside Hospice.

Many of the events also form part of the Great North Air Ambulance Service's annual Boxing Day Dip fundraising campaign, allowing participants to raise sponsorship for the charity while taking part in organised events across the region.

Here are the North East’s Boxing Day dips for 2026.

## Sunderland Boxing Day Dip, Seaburn

One of the biggest Boxing Day dips in the region will return to Seaburn, where participants will gather at STACK Seaburn from 10am before taking to the water at 10.30am.

Organised by Red Sky Foundation and Sunderland Lions Club, the event attracts hundreds of participants each year, many opting to take part in fancy dress.

Swimmers who raise more than £50 will receive a free Louie's T-shirt.

Supporting: Red Sky Foundation.

## Seahouses Boxing Day Dip

![Participants wearing festive costumes and fancy dress stand in the sea during a boxing day dip with other swimmers and waves visible in the background.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21676574.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fancy dress, Santa hats and icy waters at a Boxing Day Dip *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Dippers in Northumberland can head to Beach House Hotel and Seahouses Beach, where participants will meet at 10am ahead of a 10.30am plunge.

The event welcomes both wetsuit-wearers and those brave enough to enter the water in festive fancy dress, with organisers encouraging spectators to come along and support from the shore.

Supporting: RNLI and Northumberland Hospice.

## Seaham Boxing Day Dip

A much-loved County Durham tradition is set for a comeback in 2026, with the Seaham Boxing Day Dip returning after several years away.

The event will take place at Seaham Harbour Marina and organisers are planning for up to 250 participants.

Professional water safety cover, first aid provision and support from Seaham Seaside Swimmers will be in place, with participants entering the water at 10am.

Supporting: RNLI and Seaham Coastguard.

## Tynemouth Boxing Day Dip

The annual Tynemouth dip is once again expected to attract large numbers of swimmers and spectators to the coast.

## South Shields Boxing Day Dip

South Shields’ traditional Boxing Day plunge returns this December, giving fundraisers another chance to raise money for charity.

## Hartlepool Boxing Day Dip

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21676672.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A participant carrying a child in a festive outfit takes a selfie while entering the sea during the Hartlepool Round Table Boxing Day Dip at Seaton Carew *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Hartlepool’s annual dip is expected to draw hundreds of participants and spectators.

## Redcar Boxing Day Dip

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21676708.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Participants tackle the waves during the Redcar Boxing Day Dip *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Swimmers seeking a Boxing Day challenge on Teesside can join Redcar’s annual dip.

One event scheduled for Redcar Beach will run from 11am until noon, raising money for Teesside Hospice while offering participants the chance to start the festive weekend with a splash.

Supporting: Teesside Hospice.

## Saltburn Boxing Day Dip

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21676728.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Festive fancy dress is a common sight as hundreds of people brave the cold water on Boxing Day *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Saltburn is again part of the Great North Air Ambulance Service’s Boxing Day Dip campaign.

The North Yorkshire town regularly attracts swimmers willing to brave the winter water.

## Whitby Boxing Day Dip

Whitby’s annual Boxing Day Dip also returns in 2026.

### Also supporting the Great North Air Ambulance Service

Participants in Boxing Day dips at Tynemouth, South Shields, Sunderland, Hartlepool, Redcar, Saltburn and Whitby can also raise money for the Great North Air Ambulance Service through its annual festive fundraising campaign.

Last year, the campaign raised more than £26,000 to support the charity's life-saving work across the North East, North Yorkshire and Cumbria.
