Three violent men and a shoplifting gang were among defendants sentenced at York Magistrates Court recently.

Stephen Hawes, 41, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to using racial words or behaviour and threats to kill two men and threats to kill two police officers and using threatening or abusive words or behaviour, all in York.

He was given a 45-week prison sentence suspended for 18 months with supervision and six months’ alcohol treatment and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Liam Hulme, 32, of Woolstone Avenue, Congleton, pleaded guilty to causing a man actual bodily harm in York.

He was given a six-month prison sentence suspended for 12 months with probation supervision.

He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £154 statutory surcharge.

David Muntean, 19, Marta Cimpeanu, 26, and Yosif Caldaras, 29, all of Stone Close Avenue, Doncaster, each pleaded guilty to theft of items worth £700 from B&M in York.

Muntean and Caldaras were each given a 12-month community order with 80 hours’ unpaid work and each was ordered to pay £90 compensation, £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Cimpeanu was ordered to pay £90 compensation, a £115 fine and £85 prosecution costs.

Adam Stephenson, 44, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to breach of a restraining order and was jailed for 13 weeks.

Joseph Vause, 42, of Low Street, Brotherton, pleaded guilty to two charges of assaulting police and one each of assaulting a man at Byram Social Club, using threatening words or behaviour and assaulting a police officer in Sherburn-in-Elmet.

He was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision and six months’ alcohol treatment and banned from Byram Social Club for 12 months.

He must pay £200 compensation to one police officer, £50 compensation to the other and a £100 fine.

Tyler Porter, 29, of Falsgrave Road, Scarborough, denied a charge of assaulting a man in Scarborough and was convicted at trial.

He pleaded guilty to failure to attend court.

He was given a 16-week prison sentence suspended for 12 months with probation supervision for that and other offences and was ordered to pay £200 compensation.

Donna Ward, 55, admitted failure to comply with a community order and was resentenced for having a plank of wood as an offensive weapon and stealing food worth £131.90 from the Co-op in Tang Hall Lane, £245 from Marks and Spencer, £92.55 from Morrisons in Crichton Avenue, Clifton.

She was jailed for 24 weeks.

Graham Coggins, 47, of North Marine Road, Scarborough, was jailed for 24 weeks for breaching a community order by committing other offences.
