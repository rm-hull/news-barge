France thrash Argentina to reach Nations Championship final

**Nations ChampionshipFrance (14) 45Tries:** Guillard, Mauvaka, Gourgues 2, Gailleton, Duguid, Bielle-Biarrey**Cons:** Gourgues 5**Argentina (10) 17Tries:** Piccardo, Matera**Cons:** Albornoz 2**Pen:** Albornoz**France dispatched Argentina in Paris to book their spot against South Africa in the Nations Championship final at Allianz Stadium next Sunday.**

Knowing pre-game a bonus-point victory at Stade de France would be enough to edge England out of the top spot in the northern hemisphere standings, Les Bleus had to work hard to secure the points required.

Lock Mickael Guillard powered over for the opening score, but Justo Piccardo responded for the visitors before Tomas Albornoz nudged them ahead with a penalty.

Argentina have not been at their best this November, suffering a heavy defeat by Ireland before narrowly beating Italy.

However, as is often the case with the Pumas, the longer they spend together, the more they tend to improve as a campaign progresses.

Felipe Contepomi's side brought the first-half physicality needed to trouble France, who turned to their maul late in the first half to score through Peato Mauvaka and reclaim the lead.

Fly-half Matthieu Jalibert, who missed the win over the world champions through injury, then expertly sent Kalvin Gourgues through a gap to score.

The pivotal moment came when Argentina prop Mayco Vivas was shown a 20-minute red card for making direct head contact at a ruck, with France capitalising on the extra man to score their bonus-point try through Emilien Gailleton.

Hooker Julian Montoya then joined Vivas in the sin-bin after receiving a yellow card and France made their numerical advantage count once again as Tyler Duguid and Gourgues crossed.

Pablo Matera intercepted Louis Bielle-Biarrey's pass to improve the scoreline but Bielle-Biarrey made amends to dot down a brilliant kick into space by Antoine Dupont.

It will now be rematch of last Friday's epic game in Paris, which France narrowly won 21-19 against the world champions.

South Africa booked their spot in the final by picking up a losing bonus point against Ireland.

Scotland will play Argentina on Sunday at Allianz Stadium in the seventh/eighth-place play-off.
