Looking for a family home to rent, with a garden of your own and room to grow? The Linton Collection brings a range of newly refurbished three- and four-bedroom detached homes to Linton-on-Ouse, combining the peaceful North Yorkshire village setting with great access to York, Harrogate and Leeds.

Linton Place and The Paddocks form the latest collection of homes within The Linton Collection. These beautifully designed properties are perfectly suited to families, working professionals, and those seeking a peaceful, community-focused village lifestyle.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21726991.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: The Linton Collection)*

A stone’s throw away from Main Street, Linton Place comprises of 19 four-bedroom detached homes, set on generous sized plots. Each home benefits from a brand-new Howdens kitchen and new high quality flooring throughout.

For those looking for a three-bedroom detached home, The Paddocks boasts exclusivity in a serene setting. Each home features a stylish, brand-new open-plan kitchen and living area, creating the perfect space for entertaining guests or everyday family living.

The Paddocks also benefit from generous sized front and rear gardens, with a selection of homes boasting views of the neighbouring countryside.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21726990.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: The Linton Collection)*

Located in the picturesque riverside village of Linton on Ouse, the homes are just 11 miles northwest of York, 18 miles from Harrogate and within easy reach of York Park & Ride. Linton-on-Ouse is home to the well-regarded Linton-on-Ouse Primary School, alongside nearby pubs, The College Arms and The Linton Lock Inn, and a community hall.

As a professional landlord with 30 years’ experience, renters with Annington can secure their home with complete peace of mind, benefiting from 24/7 friendly customer support.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21726989.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: The Linton Collection)*

Rental prices at Linton Place start from £1,650 per calendar month, with homes at The Paddocks available from £1,800 per calendar month. For a limited time only, Annington is offering renters the opportunity to move into their new home with no deposit, no upfront payment and four weeks deferred rent.
