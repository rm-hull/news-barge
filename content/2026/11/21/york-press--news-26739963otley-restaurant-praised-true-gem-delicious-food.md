## 'True gem' Yorkshire Indian restaurant praised for 'absolutely delicious' meals

Westbourne Spice is loved by locals and visitors. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A “true gem” of a restaurant in West Yorkshire has been praised for its “absolutely delicious” food.

Bradford might be the curry capital of Britain but West Yorkshire’s surrounding towns and villages are also great spots to enjoy some Indian food.

Westbourne Spice in Otley is well worth a visit if you are in the area thanks to its “welcoming ambiance and exceptional service”.

The restaurant is popular with both locals and visitors to the area.

## What is Westbourne Spice like?

Westbourne Spice offers guests an “exquisite blend of traditional Indian, Asian, and Balti flavours” with dishes to suit any visitor.

The restaurant uses traditional cooking methods alongside fresh ingredients to create its dishes.

Westbourne Spice has an open kitchen allowing guests to watch the staff at work and see their meals being prepared.

To start off your experience, you can enjoy a range of appetisers to choose from, including garlic mushrooms, shami kebab, tiger prawns, and samosas.

For main dishes, you can get a mild curry, biryani, or something with a bit more spice like the massaledar chicken.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21732431.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The inside has an elegant but cosy feel. *(Image: Westbourne Spice)*

There are also grilled dishes, a huge range of karahis, and balti dishes to try.

For those who want to stick to what they know, there are lots of familiar favourites as well.

Alongside your main, you can add on a range of vegetable side dishes, different rices, and naans.

Westbourne Spice also offers an early bird menu for £22.50 per person on Mondays through to Wednesdays from 5pm to 9pm.

The menu features four courses including poppadoms, an appetiser, a main dish, and a few accompaniments.
