## The former Brazilian president was taken to hospital with abdominal pain, accompanied by military police.

Former Brazilian president Jair Bolsonaro, who is serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup, was admitted to hospital on Friday with abdominal pain.

According to his doctor, the former president has a "partial obstruction of the small intestine, with adhesions in the abdominal wall", Globo reports *(source in Portuguese)*.

Bolsonaro remains in hospital under observation. Doctors have not ruled out the possibility of further surgery.

The former far-right leader has suffered from chronic abdominal problems since he was stabbed during the 2018 election campaign.

He was escorted to DF Star Hospital Asa Sul, Brasília, the capital of Brazil, by the military police.

### Bolsonaro taken to hospital after falling ill at home

"My father has just been taken back to hospital after suffering severe abdominal pain," wrote senator-elect Carlos Bolsonaro, one of his sons, on X.

According to the Federal District Military Police, the former president, who is under house arrest, felt unwell, prompting a request for a medical assessment. A doctor visited Bolsonaro at home and confirmed that he needed to be taken to hospital.

Bolsonaro was convicted last year of plotting a coup after attempting to remain in power despite losing the 2022 presidential election to left-wing candidate Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva. He has denied the charges, claiming he is the victim of a judicial witch-hunt orchestrated by the left.

The former president has undergone several operations and required repeated hospital treatment for health problems linked to the stabbing he suffered during the 2018 election campaign.

Bolsonaro is serving a 27-year-and-three-month prison sentence under humanitarian house arrest, granted by Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes in March.

"His health is deteriorating day by day," Carlos Bolsonaro said, blaming his father's condition on his lack of mobility and what he described as "psychological torture" inflicted by the state.

Brazil's Supreme Federal Court will rule in December on the constitutionality of a law that reduces Bolsonaro's prison sentence prison, as well as those of others convicted over the coup plot.

Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, Carlos's older brother, lost last month's presidential run-off election to Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva. The day after his defeat, Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes ordered the seizure of his passports as part of a money-laundering investigation.

Flávio Bolsonaro **fled to the United States**, where he refused to concede defeat. On Elon Musk's social media platform X, the former presidential candidate posted a message calling for support for his father.
