## Two Door Cinema Club announce Scarborough Open Air Theatre gig

Two Door Cinema Club *(Image: PAUL JOHN BAYFIELD)*

Two Door Cinema Club will play a special outdoor show next summer at Scarborough Open Air Theatre.

The gig, taking place on Friday, August 6, will see the Northern Irish indie trio perform their debut 2010 album Tourist History in full.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Thursday, November 26, via scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor said: "Two Door Cinema Club are true indie icons – and we are delighted to add them to the line-up for here at Scarborough Open Air Theatre next summer.

"Tourist History remains a stunning piece of work, and this is going to be a very special night as the band plays the album live, in full, alongside many other brilliant songs from their incredible catalogue."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21742422.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Two Door Cinema Club will play a special show next summer at Scarborough Open Air Theatre *(Image: Scarborough Open Air Theatre)*

Support on the night will come from Mercury Prize-nominated band Friendly Fires, with rising singer-songwriter Danny Griffiths opening the show.

Tourist History featured hit singles including What You Know, Something Good Can Work, and Undercover Martyn.

The band has since released four more albums, all reaching the UK top 10, and has become a festival and arena headliner.

This show joins an already packed 2027 line-up at Scarborough Open Air Theatre.

For more information, go to scarboroughopenairtheatre.com
