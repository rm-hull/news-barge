Detectives investigating the stabbing of a local councillor in Yorkshire have charged a man.

Arfan Ali, aged 32, of Thornbury Avenue, Bradford, has been charged with attempted murder, assault, and possession of an offensive weapon in a public place after an incident on Thursday, November 19.

Ali, whose self-defined ethnicity is Asian Pakistani including Asian British, has been remanded into custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court today (November 21).

The charges relate to an incident on Thornbury Avenue at around 11am on November 19.

The 52-year-old man remains in hospital in serious but stable condition.

Councillor Ibrar Ali (Bradford Moor, Independent) was injured in a stabbing incident while supporting council wardens with a community clean-up.

Police were called to the scene at 11.04am following reports of a stabbing.
