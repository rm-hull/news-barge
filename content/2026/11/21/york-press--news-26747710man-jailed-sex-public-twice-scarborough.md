## Man jailed after sex in public twice in one day - and told to keep his trousers on

Marc Cooper pleaded guilty to two charges of outraging public decency by his behaviour on September 14 in Scarborough *(Image: Co-Pilot)*

A man who twice had sex in public in the centre of a North Yorkshire town has been jailed and banned from walking around without wearing pants or trousers.

Marc Cooper pleaded guilty to two charges of outraging public decency by his behaviour on September 14 in Scarborough, Harrogate magistrates heard.

He admitted these offences involved him having sexual intercourse in Waterhouse Lane, an alleyway between St Thomas Street and North Street, and to having sexual intercourse in King Street, both on the same day.

Magistrates said he committed the offences in the presence of other people and after drinking alcohol and taking drugs.

He was also on parole from prison at the time.

Cooper, 31, of no fixed address, was jailed for eight weeks.

Harrogate magistrates also made him subject to a sexual harm prevention order.

For the next five years he is banned from appearing in public without wearing at least one item of clothing below his waist, engaging in explicit sexualised behaviour in public or in the presence of anyone who doesn’t consent to his behaviour, and from making explicit sexual gestures, actions or motions or explicit sexual behaviour towards anyone or in sight of anyone in public.
