Helen Fraser, the actress best remembered as the prison officer audiences loved to dislike in ITV's Bad Girls, has died aged 84.

She played Sylvia “Bodybag” Hollamby in all eight series of the prison drama, which was set at Larkhall and ran from 1999 to 2006.

A death notice said Helen Margaret Handford died at Yaxley House Care Home, in Eye in Suffolk, on November 10 and would be “sadly missed by all who knew her”.

Her funeral will be held on December 4, a notice published by the East Anglian Daily Times said.

Fraser first came to prominence in the 1963 film Billy Liar, appearing opposite Sir Tom Courtenay and Julie Christie.

She met her husband, the late Oscar-winning sound engineer Peter Handford, while working on that film.

The pair also worked together on Gorillas In The Mist in 1988.

![Helen Fraser](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/21/14/49/GettyImages-144606714.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Her film work included Roman Polanski's 1965 film Repulsion and the 1968 film version of Harold Pinter's The Birthday Party.

On television, her credits included The Dick Emery Show, Casualty and The Doctors, with cameos in Rising Damp and The Royle Family.

She twice appeared on Coronation Street – once in 1998 as Magenta Savannah and again in 2013 as Doris Babbage.

Fraser was also known for her stage work and acted opposite Dame Judi Dench in a 1995 production of Absolute Hell at the National Theatre.

She reprised her role as Bodybag in the 2007 West End musical stage adaptation of Bad Girls.
