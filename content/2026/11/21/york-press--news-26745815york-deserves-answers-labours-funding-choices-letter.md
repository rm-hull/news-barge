## York deserves answers over Labour’s funding choices

York's financial problems are not the inevitable consequence of pressures facing councils across the country.

They are also the result of political choices by this Government.

We are told local government grant funding is increasing by 6.3% in real terms nationally.

Luton is expecting a 48% increase in net Government grant funding over the three-year settlement, while York faces a 20% reduction.

Where is the fight from York's Labour administration for a better deal?

It is all very well for the council leader to say she is making representations to Government.

But when she rejects cross-party help and describes the Fair Funding Review that has taken £38 million from York as ‘the right thing to do’, it raises serious questions.

We need York's political leadership to stand together and make a proper case for our city.

On January 30, in The Press, the Deputy Labour leader stated ‘We won’t accept a £20M funding loss’ – but this is what Labour have accepted – and more!

Labour is now exploring Exceptional Financial Support.

If this involves borrowing, it is not free money or a grant.

It is a Government payday loan, with interest and repayments falling on future council budgets.

I am informed by the Council’s finance team, that a £10M loan would result in a £1M annual cost in repayments.

A loan might plug this year's gap, but it could leave the next administration facing an even worse position, with loan repayments on top of an already substantial budget deficit.

So Labour must be honest.

Are they setting the council up for substantially higher council tax increases?

Are they preparing for cuts so severe entire services could disappear?

These are not questions residents should have to wait until after the May election to have answered.

It is Labour's responsibility to explain what this borrowing will mean, how it will be repaid and what its financial plan is for the year after.

There are a few things I would like to get off my chest and where better than the power of the press.

Why do we follow America?

First came Halloween, then baby showers now black Friday (which is the last Friday in November but now it seems to be all November).
