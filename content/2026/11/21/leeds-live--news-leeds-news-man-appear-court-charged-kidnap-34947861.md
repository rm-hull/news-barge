# Man to appear in court charged with kidnap after report of Leeds city centre attempted rape

## Police responded after woman said man tried to rape her

A man will appear in court charged with kidnap following an investigation launched into a report of an attempted rape in Leeds city centre.

Earlier this week, West Yorkshire Police said an investigation had been launched after a woman reported a man tried to rape her at around 3.07am in East Parade on Friday, November 20.

British Transport Police later arrested a man.

A police spokesman said: "At 3.07am today, police received a report of an attempted rape of a woman in East Parade in Leeds city centre. The victim, aged in her twenties, was attacked by a man at the junction with South Parade.

"She is being supported by specially trained officers."

On Saturday, West Yorkshire Police confirmed Andrew Wilks had been charged with kidnap and is due to appear in court on Monday, November 27.

A spokesperson said: "Detectives investigating a report of an attempted rape of a woman in East Parade in Leeds city centre yesterday have charged a man in connection with the incident.

"Andrew Wilks aged 34, of no fixed address has been charged with kidnap. Wilks, whose self-defined ethnicity is white British has been remanded into custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court on Monday 23 November.

"The incident is reported to have happened at the junction of South Parade at around 3am on 20 November. Enquiries remain ongoing by Leeds District Safeguarding Unit."
