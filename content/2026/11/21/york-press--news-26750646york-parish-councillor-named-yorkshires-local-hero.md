A DEDICATED York parish councillor has been deemed a 'local hero' in a regional awards ceremony.

Richard Denton, 75, from Wigginton, has been named Yorkshire's Local Hero in Churchill Living’s 2026 Local Hero Awards, which celebrates the contributions of people over 60 who make a difference in their communities.

The award was presented only days after he was discharged from hospital after undergoing emergency surgery - honouring his decades of service to the community, including fundraising, volunteering, and improving public spaces.

Richard said: "I’m delighted to receive this award and to see the work happening across Wigginton recognised.

"I’ve always believed that being part of a community means getting involved and doing what you can to make a difference.

"Whether that’s helping to look after our open spaces, bringing people together through WiggyFest or giving time to the lunch club in Haxby, it’s about making our local area a better place for everyone.

"I’m very grateful to everyone who nominated me, and to all the volunteers who give their time to support our community."

Since retiring, Richard has become a driving force in Wigginton - serving on the parish council, chairing the committee for open spaces, and helping to raise more than £100,000 for community improvements.

His work includes daily checks of the Churchfield Open Space play area and dog park, maintaining the village flower tubs, and helping care for the community pond.

He is also a founding member and organiser of WiggyFest - Wigginton’s annual music festival - and volunteers every Monday at The Do Drop Inn lunch club in Haxby, supporting older residents who may otherwise feel isolated.

As part of the award, the Churchill Foundation donated £1,000 to the Churchfield Open Space Committee to support its ongoing work.

Spencer J McCarthy, chairman and chief executive of Churchill Living, said: "Following the success of last year’s Local Hero Awards, we were excited to re-launch the initiative this year and recognise another cohort of incredibly worthwhile nominees.

"The Local Hero Awards highlight the incredible contribution older people are making in communities across the country.

"Richard is a wonderful example of the difference one person can make to the place they call home, and his contribution reaches across generations.

"The Local Hero Awards celebrate people like Richard, whose commitment, generosity and community spirit make such a positive difference to those around them.

"We are delighted to recognise his efforts and thank him for everything he does."
