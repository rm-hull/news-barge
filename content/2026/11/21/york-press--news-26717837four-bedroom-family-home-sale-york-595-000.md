## ‘Impressive’ family home hits the market in York – have a look inside

Outside at the four-bedroom semi-detached house for sale in Millfield Avenue, off Hull Road, York, for £595,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

An “impressive” family home has hit the market in York.

The four-bedroom semi-detached house is for sale in Millfield Avenue, off Hull Road, for £595,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it has been “thoughtfully extended” and finished to an “excellent standard throughout”.

An entrance hallway leads to a living room, “providing a relaxed space away from the main hub of the home”, according to Ashtons.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21699586.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the four-bedroom semi-detached house for sale in Millfield Avenue, off Hull Road, York, for £595,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

The estate agent said the open plan kitchen and dining area to the rear is undoubtedly a “highlight”.

The kitchen area opens out to the large garden which is arranged over two levels.
