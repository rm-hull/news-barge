Firefighters were sent to an incident on the motorway in North Yorkshire this afternoon (Saturday).

Crews from Tadcaster, along with an appliance from West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, went to a broken-down vehicle with a suspected fuel leak on the A1(M) between junction 46 and junction 47 at 2.05pm.

On arrival, no fire service action was required. The incident was left in the hands of the highways authority.
