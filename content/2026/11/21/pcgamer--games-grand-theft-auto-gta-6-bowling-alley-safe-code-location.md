The GTA 6 Bowling Alley safe code is rather difficult to find when you undertake the Hit Kingpin Bowling mission in Chapter 2 and rob the place, though you can hit it earlier as just Lucia. This is because the code isn't actually in the bowling alley at all.

First thing's first, the code you want is 0300. Like many other robbery safe codes, this one isn't randomised, and will crack open the safe, netting you about $3,400. Make sure to also steal the unique Gutterland Moreland 850 shotgun from the pool table in the main room.

Now, if you wanted to find the code yourself, it's actually located in Brooks Boatworks, another Kolt & Associates gang hideout in the northwest of Port Gellhorn.

Once inside that robbery:

* Head to the small building on the north east side of the compound.
* Go up the set of stairs into that building.
* Look at the map on the wall next to the whiteboard.

You actually visit the boatworks with the doctor and Dunk during the Slam Dunk mission. Looking at the map on the wall to also get the safe codes for the La Cascara Motel and Brooks Boatworks itself, the other Kolt & Associate robberies in Port Gellhorn.
