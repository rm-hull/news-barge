An East Coast management consultancy has entered administration owing the taxman almost £150,000.

Food Safety Services Ltd of Lightfoots Close, Scarborough, was incorporated eight years ago and entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on October 29.

Companies House lists its sole director as Jamie Alexander Thornton.

BTG Begbies Traynor (Central) LLP of Stockton-on-Tees were appointed liquidator.




The liquidator produced a report known as a statement of affairs outlining the Scarborough company’s finances.

The report said the company had no assets and no preferential creditors – those paid first.

But HMRC was a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next – which was owed £89,624.00.

HMRC was also an unsecured creditor – someone paid last – for debts owed to it of £54,534.00 in corporation tax.

This gave a total deficiency or shortfall of £144,158.00.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £100, this gave an overall total deficiency or shortfall of £144,258.
