US and Israeli officials have urged Donald Trump to resume strikes on Iran, over fears that American military readiness could dissipate after December.

Sources familiar with the matter told *The Financial Times* that the US Central Command is “running out of time, burning money and readiness”.

However, Trump told Axios that he was not “rushing into any decision” and that “we will see what happens after the beginning of the year”.

It comes as Iran's foreign minister Abbas ​Araqchi said Tehran still hoped diplomacy could end its war with the United States.

![US and Israeli officials have urged Donald Trump to restart strikes on Iran](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/23/2305536345..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He told Iranian state media that Tehran was seeking "an agreement that would ensure there was no further war" and wanted guarantees that Washington was serious about negotiations before proceeding further.

The remarks ​came ⁠as diplomatic efforts ‌continued to try to end a conflict that has destabilised the Middle East, disrupted shipping, driven up ‌global energy prices and drawn ‌in countries across the region.

The foreign minister said Iran's red lines included keeping enriched uranium in the country, though he added that he believed "a fair solution" could be reached.

However, on Friday Trump told a press conference: ‘[Iran] want to make a deal, but they don't want to make a deal that's acceptable to me.”

He added that future gas prices were dependent on “how we settle up with Iran,” nearly a year after he started a war that has wreaked havoc on the global economy and upset energy markets by turning the Strait of Hormuz into a war zone.

Earlier this week, Trump said Iran is “looking at gases” and biological weapons, following reports Tehran is plotting a “dirty bomb” in Washington, D.C.

Trump was asked about the possibility of such an attack during an interview with Fox News senior White House correspondent Jacqui Heinrich during his trip to Shenzen, China.

“There is indication that Iran is looking at gases, looking at bio, because we’ve shut them out with the nuclear,” Trump told Heinrich on the sidelines of his Apec summit with Chinese Premier Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

“Nuclear is much harder. And they’re looking at gas and they’re looking at the bio, you know, the biological weapons, not nice, and other things.

“And we’ll see. We’re going to have a new year coming up, we’ll see what happens after January 1 but they’ve been nasty. These are nasty people. We didn’t start this war. This war was started… 51 years ago.”

Asked whether residents of D.C. should panic, the president answered: “I don’t think so, no. Look if it does happen, we wipe them out… Oh, I give you my word. Absolutely.”

Iran’s Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei has also dismissed the reporting in a post on X, calling it “baseless” and a “fabricated media narrative.”
