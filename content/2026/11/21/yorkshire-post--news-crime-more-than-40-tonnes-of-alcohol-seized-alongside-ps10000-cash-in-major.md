# More than 40 tonnes of alcohol seized alongside £10,000 cash in major Sheffield shop raids

## More than 40 tonnes of alcohol, suspected illicit cigarettes and around £10,000 in cash have been seized during a major operation targeting two shops in Sheffield.

On Tuesday (Nov 17), officers from South Yorkshire Police's South West Neighbourhood Policing Team joined HMRC, licensing officers and Trading Standards for a pre-planned operation at two commercial premises in the south west of the city.

During the operation, more than 40 tonnes of alcohol were seized across the two shops, alongside tobacco products and vapes.

Officers also recovered a large bag of illicit cigarettes and several bundles of cash believed to total approximately £10,000.

An amount of alcohol suspected to have been stolen during a reported theft last month was also discovered.

The theft reportedly involved a delivery that was on its way to Ireland.

Sheffield South West Neighbourhood Policing Team Inspector Amy Donaldson said: “Joint-operations and partnership working allows us to explore all possible disruption opportunities for those individuals suspected to be involved in illicit behaviour. This week's activity has showcased this perfectly.

“If you have any information or intelligence around potential illegal activity in your area, please let us know. You can contact us in confidence and where possible we will act. You can contact us online or on 101 or anonymously report information to Crimestoppers.”

Kevin Hubbard, HMRC's Director for Small Business and Individuals, said: “Tax Confident and our online services are there to help businesses understand their responsibilities and we are here to help them get their tax right first time.

“But our compliance teams work with law enforcement partners and will also act against any business that deliberately tries to evade paying the tax that’s due.”
