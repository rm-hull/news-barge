A 32-year-old man has been charged with attemped murder after a councillor was stabbed in Bradford.

Ibrar Ali, 52, was attacked on Thornbury Avenue at around 11am on Thursday 19 November. He remains in hospital in a serious but stable condition.

Police have charged Arfan Ali, 32, of Thornbury Avenue, with attempted murder.

He has also been charged with assault and possession of an offensive weapon in a public place.

![Councillor Ibrar Ali](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/17/11/Councillor-Ibrar-Ali-f6e85886.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A statement from West Yorkshire Police read: “Detectives investigating the stabbing of a local Councillor in Bradford on 19 November have charged a man.

“Arfan Ali aged 32, of Thornbury Avenue has been charged with attempted murder, assault, and possession of an offensive weapon in a public place.

“Ali whose self-defined ethnicity is Asian Pakistani including Asian British has been remanded into custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court today. (21 November)

“The charges relate to an incident on Thornbury Avenue at around 11am on 19 November. The 52-year-old man remains in hospital in serious but stable condition.”
