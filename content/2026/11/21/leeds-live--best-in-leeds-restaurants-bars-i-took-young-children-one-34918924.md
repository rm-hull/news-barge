# 'I took my young children to one of Leeds' fanciest restaurants and discovered it's a brilliant spot for families'

## With a new children's menu serving up proper Italian favourites - San Carlo Leeds might just be the perfect family dining spot

When you think of taking the children out for something to eat, an upmarket Italian restaurant frequented by celebs in the heart of Leeds probably isn't the first place that springs to mind.

In fact, San Carlo is somewhere I'd personally usually save for a date night or a special occasion, but after taking my two children along recently, I couldn't have been more pleasantly surprised.

The popular restaurant on South Parade has recently introduced a brand-new children's menu, alongside its autumn/winter menu for adults, making it an appealing option for families looking for something a little different.

As a mum-of-three, I'm always on the lookout for somewhere that caters for the children without compromising on the food for the grown-ups, and admittedly, we're often left disappointed.

So many children's menus seem to follow the same predictable formula of chicken nuggets, fish fingers and burgers, often leaving little to get excited about.

But San Carlo is doing things a little differently, introducing young diners to authentic Italian flavours with a menu that feels like a proper meal rather than an afterthought.

I visited with two of my children, my seven-year-old son and my almost two-year-old daughter, and from the moment we walked through the door, we were made to feel incredibly welcome, any apprehension quickly left the building.

The new Bambini menu has been designed to bring a taste of Italy to younger guests, with everything from Sicilian arancini and crispy mozzarella fritters to pasta dishes, grilled salmon and mini Milanese.

There's also a 'make your own pizza' option for £6.95, allowing children to choose their own toppings, alongside a selection of desserts including ice cream, cheesecake and fresh fruit salad.

My son was particularly excited about the pizza, although he was slightly disappointed that he didn't actually get to make it himself, as it was more of a choose-your-own-toppings experience.

That said, it was freshly prepared and delicious, and he happily demolished it after starting with the arancini balls, something he would never usually order so it was really nice to see him try something new and actually enjoy it. He finished with strawberry ice cream, which is always a big hit.

My little girl opted for cheesy garlic bread followed by lasagne and ice cream, and I was genuinely impressed by how much she ate. She's not always the easiest person to please when it comes to food, so seeing her clear her plate was a pretty big compliment in itself.

While the children were enjoying their Italian feast, there was plenty for us grown-ups to get excited about too, particularly with the restaurant's new Autumn/Winter menu.

The seasonal offering, which launched in September, celebrates traditional Italian cooking with a selection of dishes using seasonal ingredients, from comforting pasta and risotto to indulgent meat and seafood options.

I started with garlic prawns while my partner opted for mussels in white wine, before we decided to share a tomahawk steak with peppercorn sauce and a side of crispy zucchini fries.

The steak was cooked to perfection and the whole meal was delicious, with generous flavours and that comforting, authentic Italian feel you'd hope for from somewhere like San Carlo.

We finished with traditional tiramisu, washed down with a bottle of Primitivo, which felt like the perfect way to round off the evening, that was just as enjoyable for us as it was the kids.

And while the food certainly made an impression, there was something else that stood out just as much.

San Carlo has long been known as one of Leeds' glamorous dining spots, and its walls are famously decorated with photographs of some of the biggest names from sport and entertainment.

Back in the 1990s and 2000s, the restaurant was something of a celebrity hotspot, attracting famous faces from the worlds of football, music and television.

The framed photographs are still dotted around the restaurant, featuring familiar faces including David Beckham, Liam Gallagher and footballing personalities connected to Leeds. For my football-mad seven-year-old, this was arguably just as exciting as the food.

He loved looking around at all the pictures, spotting familiar faces and asking questions about the people on the walls. It was a lovely little touch that gave him something to get excited about beyond the menu.

The restaurant also deserves a special mention for its staff, who really went above and beyond to make the children feel at home. They were chatting away to them, bringing over colouring sheets and pens, showing my son the photographs and even taking the time to play with my little girl, who is not the biggest fan of sitting still!

Nothing felt forced or like it was too much trouble, and it made such a difference to being able to sit down and enjoy our meal without feeling like we were inconveniencing anyone by bringing children along.

The manager was equally lovely, offering recommendations on the food and wine and taking the time to chat with us. When we mentioned we were planning a trip to Italy next year for a friends wedding, he even took the time to write down some recommendations of places we should visit.

It's those little details that make an experience memorable. The food might be what gets people through the door, but it's the people who make you want to go back.

It's easy to assume somewhere as stylish as San Carlo is best reserved for adults, but our visit completely changed my mind.

The new children's menu offers something a little more exciting than the usual family dining options, while the autumn/winter menu means parents aren't left compromising on their own meal.

And with staff who genuinely seem to enjoy having little diners around, it's a place where families can enjoy a special meal without worrying about whether the children will be welcome.

One of the staff members also mentioned Flying Pizza, another restaurant within the San Carlo group, which is located close to Roundhay Park.

He told us it's another great option for families, with its location making it perfect for combining an afternoon letting the children burn off some energy in the park before heading over for pizza and pasta.

We've not yet tried either Roundhay Park or Flying Pizza, but it's definitely one we've added to our list for a family day out.

So, if you're looking for somewhere to take the family in Leeds that feels a little bit special, I'd definitely say don't rule out San Carlo.

You might go for the Italian food, but it's the warm welcome, thoughtful touches and family-friendly atmosphere that could make it your new favourite.
