A proposal to accommodate 931 asylum seekers at RAF Barnham has prompted a Suffolk village to hold a symbolic independence referendum.

Residents of Barnham began casting their votes on Saturday morning in a Yes/No poll asking whether they support a petition for “crown dependency status”.

The vote is being held at Barnham Village Hall from 10am to 4pm and will carry no legal weight.

Some 400 to 500 people are eligible to take part, according to John Bauer, chairman of Barnham Parish Council.

The result is expected to be announced on Saturday evening.

Mr Bauer previously said the independence referendum has been organised by Barnham Residents' Association.

He said people will vote on whether they support a petition for “crown dependency status” and described this as “a bit like Jersey, Guernsey, Isle of Man whereby we retain loyalty to the crown while still removing ourselves from the Home Office jurisdiction”.

Mr Bauer said the poll will be “Yes for independence, No for keep things as they are”.

He added: “Assuming we have a positive result, we will declare ourselves the bailiwick of Barnham. Not the principality of Piddington. This is going to be the bailiwick of Barnham.”

![Protests outside RAF Barnham in Norfolk](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/10/16/01KZKGQJCSCJ1D63NZP22JN8YS.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Asked what he hopes it will achieve, Mr Bauer said: “It’s to express the concern of the village at the way we’re not being consulted, the treatment by the Home Office, the whole thing, we feel we’re being totally ignored.”

He said the proposal for RAF Barnham, which prompted the referendum, was initially for 1,256 male asylum seekers to be housed at the base, though the figure is now 931.

“I don’t know why they chose that particularly precise figure, but they have done,” Mr Bauer said.

"It makes very little difference, however, when you've only got 584 residents in the village whether it's 1,256 or 931.

“It’s still going to completely swamp the village and change things beyond recognition.”

A final decision on the proposal has not yet been made.

Barnham's poll follows in the footsteps of a similar referendum in Piddington, Oxfordshire, in September, when residents elected to secede from the UK after the Government announced plans to house 1,256 male asylum seekers at a nearby former military base.

Communities Secretary Angela Rayner has since approved full planning permission for MoD Bicester to be used for housing asylum seekers.

Tory leader Kemi Badenoch has shared her support for Barnham residents, saying “the Conservative Party is on your side”.

“We know that the problem of the boats needs to stop and we have got a plan to stop it,” she told the Press Association on Friday.

"We are seeing more and more undocumented young men being put in asylum centres next to villages.

"Piddington, as an example, only has 130 women.

"Putting about 1,300 men right next to that we know is not appropriate and I know that it's not appropriate for Barnham, too.

“But instead of the Government spreading the problem around, it needs to stop the boats.”

A Home Office spokesperson said: "We recognise the strength of feeling in local communities and are listening carefully to the concerns being raised.

"The Government is working on a fairer distribution of asylum accommodation across the country, with over £1 billion saved in costs since the election.

"This includes closing all asylum hotels and moving to alternative accommodation, including former military sites.

“These are designed to be self-contained, with 24/7 security and CCTV, to reduce the impact on local communities.”

The vote in Barnham carries no legal force, but residents will learn the outcome on Saturday evening.
