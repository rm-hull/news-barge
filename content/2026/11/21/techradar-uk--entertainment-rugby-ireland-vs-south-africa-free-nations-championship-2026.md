Andy Farrell's hosts are seeking three home wins in a row, while the Springboks must do without banned head coach Rassie Erasmus after last weekend's defeat.

Ireland ran out comfortable 43-15 victors over Fiji last weekend, the bonus point crucial to keeping pace with France and England at the top of the Northern Hemisphere section.
