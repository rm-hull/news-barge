City of York Council is reviewing its polling districts, places and stations following changes to ward boundaries made by the Local Government Boundary Commission.

A public consultation has opened so that local people can comment on the council’s proposed new arrangements.

The council is checking that voting facilities are reasonable and practical, and that polling places are accessible.

Richard Webb, Chief Executive and Returning Officer, said the council wants to hear views on its proposed new arrangements in response to the Boundary Commission’s changes. He added: “We are also keen to hear any suggestions for improving polling district boundaries or polling places.”

He also said: “In particular, we are interested in feedback from disabled people about access to polling stations and any support that may be needed.”

The consultation starts today (Friday) and runs until Sunday 20 December 2026. Paper copies of the survey, including an Easy Read version, will be available from libraries and West Offices.

People who need materials in other formats can email [email protected]. Full details of the proposals and how to take part are available on the council’s website.
