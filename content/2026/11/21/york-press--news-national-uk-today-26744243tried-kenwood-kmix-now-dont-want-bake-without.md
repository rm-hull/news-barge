From kettles and toasters to microwaves and air fryers, kitchen appliances have become an essential part of everyday life.

For decades, households have relied on kitchen brands promising appliances made to last.

Among the best-known is Kenwood, whose simple, reliable products have been a familiar fixture in kitchens for generations.

Most famous for its range of food mixers, Kenwood has long been a trusted staple when it comes to kitchen gadgets.

Since the late 1940s, the Chef Mixer has found its way into kitchens around the world and remains a popular choice decades later.

Bakers and chefs swear by the accessories,s with mixers often outlasting more modern gadgets.

I grew up using a Kenwood mixer (lovingly nicknamed Kenny) that has lasted since the 1980s with only a few small repairs; it remains in perfect condition to this day.

As a keen baker, I wanted to test out if the modern Kenwood kMix still offers the perfect cakes and bread the trusted brand promises.

So, I put on my apron, gathered the ingredients, preheated the oven and got ready to bake.

## The Kenwood kMix is the kitchen gadget you need on your wish list

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738440.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It made a perfect chocolate cake. (I've never been a tidy icer...) *(Image: Emilia Kettle/Newsquest)*

The Kenwood kMix promises to deliver “great results” with its 1000w motor that ensures “effortless performance” no matter the bake.

I was planning to bake a chocolate cake and a loaf of white bread using a Paul Hollywood recipe.

The kMix is as simple a tool as you could wish for, with an electronic dial that lets you control the speed, a variety of handy tools and a silver mixer bowl that is easy to lock in.

Coming with three interchangeable tools, you get a whisk, a dough tool and the K-Beater, each designed to help make the best batter for what you need.

At the front of the mixer, there is a little pop-out that lets you add on additional tools, like a pasta maker, grater, spiraliser, mincer and more.

The kMix itself is a sturdy piece of equipment that looks stylish and modern, elevating any kitchen to new heights.

Coming in a range of colours, Kenwood offers the kMix in white, black and cream.

Each mixer comes with the classic Kenwood metal mixing bowl, which locks securely into place and features a handy side handle for easy lifting, holding and pouring.

Personally, I could not wish for an easier mixer than the kMix; it takes the fuss out of mixing with its powerful and simple-to-use design.

What would normally take me half an age with a whisk and my questionable arm strength took the kMix a matter of moments, effortlessly turning the butter into a perfectly smooth mixture.

Before I knew it, the chocolate cake batter was mixed, poured into the tin and baked, ready to be finished with beautifully smooth icing whipped up in the Kenwood mixer.

When it came to the bread, the kMix once again did not disappoint; using the handy dough tool, it rolled together the mix, ready for it to be proved.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738438.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The kMix is perfect for bread making. *(Image: Emilia Kettle/Newsquest)*

Within a few hours, I went from a clueless bread baker to someone who had baked a great-looking loaf, all thanks to the kMix.
