Cartel boss El Chapo Isidro has been captured in the Mexican state of Sinaloa with help from the Trump administration.

Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, a leading figure in the “Beltrán Leyva” criminal organization who was on the FBI’s most wanted list, was captured overnight.

His arrest set off a burst of violence that rattled civilians in the city of Guasave, Sinaloa, where the operation took place.

![The FBI had been offering a reward of up to $5 million for information leading to his arrest](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/21/14/05/Screenshot-2026-11-21-at-9-29-40-AM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Videos and photos from Guasave show screaming civilians fleeing gunfire at a baseball game. The same footage and images show burnt cars.

An early Saturday report from the Mexican government said intelligence from the Trump administration helped authorities capture Meza Flores. The report provided few details about how the two countries coordinated.

Mexican authorities said they used “stealth and surprise” to catch him.

They said officials were attacked with guns and explosives from likely cartel operatives. After the attack, the officials entered a home and arrested him, the authorities said.

Meza Flores and others detained in the clash were later evacuated to Mexico City in a Mexican Air Force plane.

The FBI had been offering a reward of up to $5 million for information leading to his arrest, alleging that he and his criminal organization smuggled vast amounts of heroin, methamphetamine, cocaine, and marijuana into the United States for over 20 years.

![Vehicles burn on a highway near Los Mochis, Sinaloa, Mexico, on November 20, 2026, after El Chapo Isidro’s capture](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/21/14/2026-11-21T133748Z_174175452_RC2DSOAMKY38_RTRMADP_3_MEXICO-CHAPOISIDRO.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Fausto Isidro Meza-Flores is the alleged leader of the Meza-Flores Transnational Criminal Organization (TCO), a heavily armed, major drug-trafficking organization,” the agency wrote on his wanted poster.

”The Meza-Flores TCO is allegedly responsible for the possession, distribution, and importation of large quantities of heroin, methamphetamine, cocaine, fentanyl, and marijuana into the United States, and has predominately controlled drug production, transportation, and distribution in several locations throughout Mexico.”

The FBI warned that he was armed and dangerous and should be considered an international flight risk.

Mexico has recently captured or killed a string of high-profile suspected drug traffickers under pressure from Washington.

President Donald Trump has repeatedly threatened to launch military action inside Mexico if Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum's administration doesn't do more to crack down on cartels.
