## The United States announced sanctions against the Sparta II in early May 2022, just over two months after Moscow launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Ukraine has also sanctioned the vessel.

A Russian cargo ship has been struck by drones in the Gulf of Finland, Finnish and Russian authorities have said.

Finnish Defence Forces detected the attack in international waters on Saturday morning, west of Suursaari, according to the country's defence ministry.

The Sparta II vessel is reported to have sustained damage in the incident, with the Finnish Border Guard saying a fire had broken out on board.

The border agency added that the vessel had drifted into Finnish territorial waters in the eastern part of the gulf but that the situation did not pose a danger to other maritime traffic.

"The Finnish Border Guard is leading maritime rescue and environmental damage control operations on the cargo vessel Sparta II, which is currently in Finnish territorial waters on the open sea south of Loviisa city," it said in an update at around 3 pm local time, while confirming that Russian maritime rescue authorities had been contacted.

"Rescue units are on-site. The crew has not been forced to abandon the ship, and no environmental damage has been detected. The Border Guard is prepared to evacuate personnel and assist in firefighting efforts," it continued.

While it was not immediately clear who was behind the strike, Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said the incident was evidence of the Ukraine war "spilling over into our neighboring regions."

"Ukraine has the right to fight back against the aggressor," he added.

Aleksandr Drozdenko, the governor of Russia's Leningrad Oblast, said the vessel had been attacked by two drones as it was making its way to Kaliningrad.

The United States announced sanctions against the Sparta II in early May 2022, just over two months after Moscow launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Ukraine has also sanctioned the vessel.

The Gulf of Finland is a crucial maritime passage for Russia, connecting Saint Petersburg with the wider Baltic Sea.

Finland's defence ministry said a Russian helicopter was also suspected of violating Finnish airspace on Saturday afternoon, off the coast of Loviisa.

“The suspected airspace violation may be related to rescue or monitoring operations involving a cargo ship that sustained damage in the Gulf of Finland. The Finnish Defense Forces and the Border Guard are handling the situation in cooperation with other authorities," Defence Minister Antti Häkkänen was quoted as saying.

It comes after Polish authorities announced that two Russian missiles and a jet-powered drone had crashed in Poland on Friday as allied nations held military exercises in the country, prompting condemnation from European officials.
