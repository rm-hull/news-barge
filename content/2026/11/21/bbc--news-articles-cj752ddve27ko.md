The Finnish Border Guard is leading the operations involving the Sparta II

**A Russian cargo vessel bound for the country's Baltic Kaliningrad territory has been hit by a drone attack in the eastern Gulf of Finland, Russian and Finnish authorities say.**

The ship - the Sparta II - caught on fire after the strike on Saturday, and drifted into Finnish waters. Finnish defence forces said Ukraine was likely behind the attack.

"War is spilling over into our immediate vicinity," Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said, adding: "Ukraine has the right to fight back against the aggressor."

Kaliningrad Governor Alexei Besprozvannykh said two drones were used, according to preliminary information, and that there is "no threat" to their supplies.

The Sparta II was sanctioned by the US in 2022 and Ukraine earlier this year, and is run by a Russian company, according to the Ukrainian and American governments.

The company says on its website that it was established within the structure of Russia's defence ministry in 2011, and since 2015 has been operating within the military construction complex.

On Saturday morning, Finnish defence forces detected the drone attack on the cargo vessel west of Suursaari in the Gulf of Finland, which prompted "temporary aviation and maritime restrictions" over the area.

Shortly after the incident, Finland's defence ministry said a Russian helicopter was suspected of violating the country's airspace, potentially linked to "rescue or tracking operations".

A Russian rescue vessel approached the ship, and was told by the crew that they did not need to be evacuated, Kaliningrad's governor wrote on Telegram, adding: "The crew is currently carrying out the necessary work and plans to continue on its way."

The Finnish Border Guard also said the crew had not been forced to abandon the ship, no environmental damage had been detected, and there was no danger to maritime traffic or people.

Kaliningrad is a Russian exclave that sits between Poland and Lithuania.

Defence Minister Antti Häkkänen said that the Finnish Border Guard are responsible for the investigation into the attack, and are cooperating with other relevant authorities.

Nato has also been informed, the defence ministry said.

The Baltic Sea has become a flashpoint in the Russia-Ukraine war, triggered when Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. Ukraine has recently ramped up its long-range attacks against Russia beyond the front lines.

Earlier this month, a Ukrainian drone strike hit a Russian warship in the Gulf of Finland.
