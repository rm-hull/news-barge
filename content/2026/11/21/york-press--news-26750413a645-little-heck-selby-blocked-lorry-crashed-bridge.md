A ROAD in North Yorkshire is expected to be closed for ‘some time’ after a lorry has crashed into a railway bridge.

North Yorkshire Police has asked motorists to avoid the A645 at Little Heck (near Selby) after a lorry has collided with the railway bridge.

A police spokesperson said: “There are no reports of injuries, but the road is likely to be blocked for some time. Officers are at the scene now to ensure everyone is safe.

“Thank you for your patience and understanding - please consider alternative routes if you're in this area.”

Trains in the area are not thought to be affected by the crash.
