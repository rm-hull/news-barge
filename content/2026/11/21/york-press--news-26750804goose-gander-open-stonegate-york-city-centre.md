## Popular fashion brand to open first northern shop in historic York city centre street

Signs in the window of Goose and Gander in York *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

**A HISTORIC city centre street will be welcoming a new tenant as a popular brand prepares to open its first northern shop in York.**

Goose & Gander, a unisex fashion brand, has taken over 27 Stonegate in what will be the brand’s first location in the north of England.

The building, which has been painted in an eye-catching pastel blue, was previously home to Molly Browns – which has now consolidated its two Stonegate shops into one.

Whilst an opening date is yet to be revealed, the inside of the shop looks to be fully kitted out with a Christmas tree visible in one corner - suggesting that it will be open to the public soon.

First created in 2013 by founders, Maz and Olly, Goose & Gander is an independent British clothing brand creating unisex pieces designed to be worn by everyone.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21747455.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Goose and Gander in York *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

The website says: “We create considered pieces designed to be worn, lived in and loved for years to come. Blending effortless style with quality and comfort.

“Made for the moments that matter, from slow Sundays to spontaneous plans, Goose & Gander is about clothes that fit seamlessly into real life.”

A sign in the window states that the shop will be ‘coming soon’ and another invites customers to scan a QR code to join the mailing list and learn more about ‘exclusive drops’.
