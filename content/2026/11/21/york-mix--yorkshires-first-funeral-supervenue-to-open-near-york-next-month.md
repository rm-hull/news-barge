Yorkshire’s first funeral ‘SuperVenue’ is officially set to open its doors near York next month.

The brand-new **Vale of York Crematorium & Memorial Gardens** will welcome its first services in early December.

Originally scheduled to open on 17 November, the launch was pushed back slightly while teams completed the new building.

Sitting on a 13-acre site off **Arcot Lane near the A64**, close to Sand Hutton and the York Biotech Campus, the new facility aims to offer something completely different for families saying goodbye to loved ones.

Built by independent operator Westerleigh Group, it is billed as one of the most eco-friendly crematoriums in the country.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/york-crematorium-sand-hutton-2-pd.webp)

And the setup allows families to personalise services using immersive tech.

Instead of a traditional backdrop, the entire front wall of the chapel acts as a giant projection canvas.

Coupled with co-ordinated lighting and soundscapes, families can transform the space to reflect their loved one’s passions, from rolling Yorkshire landscapes and floral displays to football stadiums.

Here is what the new site includes:

* **The Chapel:** Seats 112 guests with plenty of standing room, plus external screens for larger numbers.
* **Personal Tributes:** An external welcome screen displays a loved one’s name and photo as mourners arrive.
* **Unrushed Services:** Slots are set at one hour, including entry and exit time, with options to extend.
* **Parking:** Spaces for 127 cars, including dedicated accessible bays.

Site manager Nikki Anderson said she is proud to lead the new team and bring more choice to York and North Yorkshire.

“We had originally expected to open a little earlier but had to postpone due to circumstances beyond our control, which is not unusual with a brand-new development,” she explained.

If you want to see how the technology works for yourself, the team is opening the doors to everyone in the new year. A **community open day** takes place on**Saturday, 9 January, from 10am to 2pm**.

Visitors will be able to walk around the landscaped memorial gardens, test out the chapel’s projection tech, and chat with staff.

“It will be wonderful to meet members of our local communities and for them to get to know us, as we aim to provide a positive presence in the area, as well as the essential support and care when families need it most,” Nikki added.

You can find out more details at their website.
