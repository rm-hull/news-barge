A crew were called to a fire in a home early today (Saturday).

Acomb firefighters went to a “small fire” at the property on **Tudor Road** at**2.51am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The fire was out on arrival and was a result of a small electrical device being placed near to a candle.

#### Tudor Road, York

“Crews used a bucket of water over the item and advice given to the homeowner.”
