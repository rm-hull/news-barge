## The strike mainly targeted civilian infrastructure, Zelenskyy wrote on X. The attack comes after Russia vowed a 'painful' response to Ukraine's strike on the Crimean bridge.

At least two people were killed and dozens injured in a Russian attack on Kryvyi Rih, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's hometown.

A Russian ballistic missile on the city in central Ukraine killed a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman, local authorities wrote. 60 others were injured, including six children.

"A young woman – an emergency medical worker – and a little boy who was just seven years old were killed. His name was Tykhon. He was born in 2019, and, sadly, most of his life was lived during a war like this," Zelenskyy wrote Satuday on X.

"Ordinary homes, offices, and everyday civilian infrastructure in an ordinary city were damaged," he added.

The attack sparked four fires and damaged roughly 25 apartment blocks, a hospital, schools, an industrial facility and businesses and vehicles, Oleksandr Vilkul, the head of the city administration, wrote on Telegram.

Russia also targeted other cities and villages in the Kharkiv, Donetsk, Dnipro, Rivne, Chernihiv, Mykolaiv, and Zaporizhzhia regions, Zelenskyy reported.

In the Sumy region, Russian forces struck a railway station in the city of Krolevets, injuring three people, local media reported.

On its end, Ukraine struck an ammunition depot in Crimea and bridge in Luhansk, local media reported, adding that Ukraine's defence forces also targeted two Russian command posts in the Zaporizhzhia and Donetsk regions.

The ammunition depot was near the settlement of Pervomaiske in occupied Crimea, Ukraine's General Staff wrote on Telegram.

"The shells that were supposed to go to the frontline from there will no longer reach the positions of Ukrainian soldiers."

In respone to Ukraine's strikes on Russia's Crimea bridge last week, Russian President Vladimir Putin vowed a "painful" response after holding a summit with US counterpart Donald Trump and China's Xi Jinping on Thursday.

Zelensky, who met allies in Berlin Thursday, said Putin's attempts to "redraw the world" with Xi and Trump had been a "failure".

"Putin launched this attack in a highly demonstrative manner – following weak meetings in China and the actual failure of his attempt to somehow “redraw” the world," Zelenskyy said on Friday.
