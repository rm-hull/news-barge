The top 30 prettiest villages in Britain have been named – and two are in North Yorkshire.

And one of those is in the top three!

Website Enjoy Travel picks its prettiest British villages every year.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/staithes-north-yorkshire-wk.jpg)

The latest list features a coastal favourite at number 28 – **Staithes**.

“Tucked into a dramatic cleft in the North Yorkshire coast, Staithes is one of those villages that seems to get prettier the further you walk into it,” the website says.

“Narrow lanes wind between charming stone cottages before opening out towards the harbour and the sea.”

It advises readers to stay at the Captain Cook Inn if they visit.

And at number three? It’s the picturesque village of **Thornton-le-Dale** near Pickering.

“Thornton-le-Dale is one of the prettiest villages in the UK, with a babbling beck, stone and thatched-roof cottages and flower-filled gardens creating a wonderfully idyllic scene,” the website says.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/thornton-le-dale-show-summer-safari-2024-4.webp)

“The village is perhaps best known for its famous Beck Isle Cottage. The Grade II-listed building is often thought to be the most frequently photographed home in all of England.

“And it is little surprise given its look and location sitting beside the water beneath a traditional stone bridge.

“There are several quaint coffee shops, independent shops and pubs in and around the village, and plenty of walking and cycling routes to the neighbouring North York Moors.”

And it’s also home to one of the best summer events too – the Thornton-le-Dale Show!
