Detectives investigating a report of an attempted rape of a woman in a Yorkshire city centre have charged a man in connection with the incident.

Andrew Wilks, aged 34, of no fixed address, has been charged with kidnap following an alleged incident in East Parade, just off The Headrow, Leeds, on Friday.

Wilks, whose self-defined ethnicity is white British, has been remanded into custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court on Monday, November 23.

The incident is reported to have happened at the junction of South Parade at around 3am on 20 November.

Enquiries remain ongoing by Leeds District Safeguarding Unit
