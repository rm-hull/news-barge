Four people from the Harrogate district were among those appearing before Harrogate Magistrates’ Court this week.

The cases, heard on November 17 and 19, included two drink-driving offences, driving without insurance and criminal damage at a Harrogate supermarket.

**The following were heard at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on November 17:**

Katarzyna Matusik, 45, of Gallows Hill, Ripon, received a 12-month motoring ban for drink-driving. Matusik was driving a Nissan Note when the offence occurred on Lark Hill Road, Ripon, on August 2. A drink-drive test showed that she had 47mcgs of alcohol in 100ml of breath. The legal alcohol limit is 35mcg. Matusik pleaded guilty to the offence. The 12-month motoring ban would be reduced by 13 weeks if she completed a drink-drive awareness course. She was ordered to pay £85 costs and a £74 surcharge.

Jake Jackson, 21, of Skipton Crescent, Harrogate, was fined £461 and had six points added to his licence for driving without insurance. He was driving a VW Polo on Otley Road, Harrogate, when the offence was detected on September 9 last year. Jackson pleaded guilty to the offence. He was ordered to pay £85 costs.

**The following were heard at North Yorkshire Magistrates’ Court on November 19:**

Sam Bentley-Taffinder, 22, of Ellenthorpe Lane, Ellenthorpe, was made subject to a five-year criminal-behaviour order after she admitted damaging a glass external door at the Asda supermarket in Harrogate. The offence occurred on October 4, 2024, at the Asda store on Bower Road where the defendant damaged the glass on a sliding external door. Bentley-Taffinder pleaded guilty to causing £250 of damage to the door. She was ordered to pay £200 prosecution costs and was fined £45. There was no compensation order for the damage caused to the supermarket door. The criminal-behaviour order stipulates that the defendant must leave any NHS site when asked to do so by staff and must not contact any emergency NHS service except when in a genuine emergency or in need of immediate medical assistance, or if she is making a genuine report of a criminal offence. It also prohibits her from making false reports to the emergency services and threatening to jump off any bridge over a road.

Nathan Jameson, 24, of Harlow Park Road, Harrogate, received an 18-month motoring ban for drink-driving. The offence occurred on Wetherby Road, Harrogate, on October 15. A drink-drive test revealed that he had 74mcg of alcohol in 100ml of breath. The legal alcohol limit is 35mcg. Jameson, who admitted driving over the prescribed alcohol limit, was also fined £415 and ordered to pay £85 costs, along with a statutory surcharge of £166.
