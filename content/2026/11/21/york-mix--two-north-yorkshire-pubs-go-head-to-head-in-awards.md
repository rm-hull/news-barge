Two North Yorkshire pubs owned by the same operator have been shortlisted for Best Pub in upcoming awards.

The Drovers Arms at Skipwith, near York, and The Inn South Stainley, near Harrogate, will go head to head at the Yorkshire Post Tourism awards dinner at New Dock Hall, Leeds, on Monday 30 November.

Both country pubs are operated by Yorkshire-based Mattgray Hospitality.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Image-3-L-R-Graham-Usher-and-Matthew-Rose-Managing-Directors-MATTGRAY-Hospitality-2048x1463.jpg)

It rounds off a strong year for the group, founded by Graham Usher and Matthew Rose.

The Inn South Stainley has been named a Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice 2026, for places in  
the top 10% worldwide on guest reviews. It was also a finalist for Best Yorkshire Pub at the  
Deliciously Yorkshire Taste Awards and shortlisted for Hospitality Business of the Year at the  
Harrogate Business Awards.

The Drovers Arms, which marks 14 years under the pair’s ownership this month, was named  
Visit York Pub of the Year in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025.

Graham Usher, managing director of Mattgray Hospitality, said: “Having both of our pubs  
shortlisted for the same award is something we’re really proud of.

“They’re very different places, one a village pub near York and one a country inn with rooms near Harrogate, but they’re run the same way: good Yorkshire produce, people who care, and a proper welcome for everyone, dogs included.

“Whichever way it goes on the night, this belongs to our teams.”
