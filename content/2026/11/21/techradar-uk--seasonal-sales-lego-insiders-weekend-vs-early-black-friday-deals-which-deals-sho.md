This weekend is Lego Insiders weekend, meaning members of Lego's loyalty program can score bonus points as well as some exclusive discounts. This, plus the looming Black Friday deals, means that there's loads on offer; and this year, I've been genuinely stunned by the volume of record-low deals on some really desirable sets, like this mega 40% discount and record-low deal on Lego Star Wars Jango Fett's Starship (was £59.99 now £35.99) at Amazon.
