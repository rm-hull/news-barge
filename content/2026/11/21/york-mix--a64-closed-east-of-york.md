There was a message from police earlier to say the A64 had been closed.

Officers said: “Thank you for your patience tonight, while we dealt with an incident at Grimston Bar, east of York”.

“The A64 has now reopened in both directions.

“The incident involved concern for the safety of a member of the public. We are ensuring they receive the support they need.”
