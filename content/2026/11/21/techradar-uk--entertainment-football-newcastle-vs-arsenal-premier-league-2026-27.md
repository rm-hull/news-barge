Only three sides have created fewer big chances this season than the Mags, while center-forwards Will Osula and Yoane Wissa have struggled to take the chances even when they've come along. Harvey Barnes has been a rare source of inspiration, the winger having scored four goals in all competitions, while the return to Tyneside of a former captain may lift the atmosphere for this one.

Bruno Guimaraes put a few noses out of joint when he swapped Newcastle for North London in the summer and today's game represents the Brazilian's first trip back to his former club. Arteta's men seek a 10th win in 11 league games to start their title defense and have Martin Odegaard firing on all cylinders after the skipper scored the winner against Hull last time out, then impressed for Norway in the international break. Bukayo Saka remains out but the table-topping Gunners, who have won three of their last four games 1-0, others have a fully fit squad from which to select.
