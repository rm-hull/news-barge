GTA 6 drops you into Chapter 2 desperate for cash, but you'll quickly find a few opportunities to make a quick buck if you don't mind trying to outrun bullets. One of the ways that you can get flush quickly is by visiting **Brooks Boatworks**, a criminal enterprise that's full of pills, cash and explosives. You'll also find a safe in need of a code, which I can help you find.

A word of warning, though: the boatworks is heaving with gun-toting crooks, and getting into a big firefight is just going to bring the cops down on you. Stealth is absolutely a good choice here, but I also recommend going in heavily armed. You can complete the heist just with Lucia, but it's going to be tougher.

You can find Brooks Boatworks in the **north of Port Gellhorn**, just to the right of the larger bridge. When you get near the main gate, guards will start threatening you. Even if you take them out, you're not getting in that way.

Instead, head to the right where there's an unlocked gate. You'll still need to get through the fence to get into the boatworks, but that's simple. Right in front of you when you come through the gate you'll find a big gap in the fence. Just walk through it. This also gives you a decent view of the boatworks. If you want to go in loud, this is a great place to take out some goons with a long-ranged weapon.

Near where you got through the fence, you'll find the smallest building in the compound. This should be your first port of call. Inside you'll find cash, medicine and a unique carbine—**the Clunker**. It's a solid all-rounder. Once you grab your new toy, check out the map on the wall behind it. Not only will you find the code for the boatworks safe, you'll find other safe codes as well.

Looking at the map will save the codes, so now you know **6823 is the magic number**.

You'll find the safe inside the warehouse—it's the largest building, so you can't miss it. There are lots of ways to get inside the warehouse, but I recommend the sneaky route. On the right side of the warehouse, you'll find **a way up onto the roof**. Once you get up, just climb over it and you'll find an opening that lets you drop in from above.

Now you're at the top of the warehouse, but you'll need to go down to find the room with the safe. Head down, but stick to the side you entered from, and you'll find a **room with a pool table and some goons**—this is where you'll find the safe.

Just pop in the code **6823** and grab the cash.

If you're not close to death or being hounded by cops yet, take some time to explore the rest of the compound. There's cash and pills just scattered all over the place, and make sure to grab all the explosive weaponry, especially the **launcher that's just chilling on the top floor**, above the room with the safe.

Once you've cleared the warehouse, you'll find yet another building to the west. There's more to find here, including **another safe**. This one can be broken into without a code, thankfully. Now you can bounce and go on a shopping spree!
