A spa town in North Yorkshire has been named as the second happiest place to live in Great Britain.

Rightmove has released its list of the happiest places to live around Great Britain after surveying over 24,000 people.

Harrogate came in second place after missing out to Richmond upon Thames.

The spa town was recognised by residents as having great access to nature and green spaces as well as access to essential local services.

## What is Harrogate like?

If you love outdoor spaces and exploring beautiful gardens, Harrogate is for you thanks to it being home to the Valley Gardens, the Stray, and the RHS Garden Harlow Carr, which has flowers and plants blooming no matter what time of the year it is.

For a more relaxing day, you can visit the Turkish Baths Harrogate to take in its Victorian interior.

The baths feature heated rooms, cooling facilities, and spots for visitors to relax while enjoying the history around them.

Take a trip to Montpellier Quarter for some shopping, as the streets are packed with galleries, antique dealers, bookshops, and an old sweet shop.

Harrogate is also home to a Betty's Tearoom where you can enjoy a range of breakfast dishes, pastries, sandwiches, and a huge range of cakes from vanilla slices and treacle tart to chocolate eclairs.

If you prefer seafood, FISK is a stylish restaurant offering a selection of fish dishes such as seafood Thai green curry, lobster biryani, pan fried cod, and monkfish Sri Lankan curry.

For a pint, you might want to pop into the Little Ale House serving beers from small breweries with a huge fireplace to relax next to.

Harrogate is also a great place to be to access other parts of Yorkshire including different villages around the Yorkshire Dales.

If you want to live in Harrogate, the average price of a house in the area over the last year was £386,970, according to Rightmove.
