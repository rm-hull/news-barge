## It comes after Russian forces launched a major wave of strikes against Ukraine from Thursday night into Friday morning, killing at least seven people in Kyiv and damaging key bridges across the Dnipro River.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has branded Vladimir Putin a "killer" after Donald Trump referred to the Russian leader as his "friend" earlier this week.

"For us, he is an enemy. And of course, for us, he is a killer. And I think mostly for the people in the world, he is a killer," Zelenskyy told CNN in response to a question about Trump's comment, while also warning that such descriptions could ease pressure on Putin.

Trump called Putin a "friend" as they met on the sidelines of a trilateral meeting with China's Xi Jinping in Shenzhen this week, and he continued to talk up their relationship during a press conference at the White House on Friday.

"I have fantastic relationships with Putin. That's a very good thing. I have fantastic relationships with President Xi. That's a good thing. And our country is safe because of that," he told reporters.

"You're very lucky to have somebody that has a relationship with the heads of the most powerful countries," he went on, adding that both Zelenskyy and Putin wanted to end the war but "don't know how."

Asked whether Putin was a friend of the US, Trump replied: "I can't speak to America."

It comes after Russian forces launched a major wave of strikes against Ukraine from Thursday night into Friday morning, killing at least seven people in Kyiv and damaging key bridges across the Dnipro River.

Zelenskyy said Russia had fired more than 400 attack drones and over 110 missiles during the assault, which he called "one of the largest attacks" of the war so far.

"Absolutely all the targets were ordinary civilian infrastructure – things that literally sustain people’s lives. Energy facilities, logistics – bridges across the Dnipro, railway infrastructure," he wrote in a post on social media.

The attacks followed Putin's warning that there would be "no negotiations" on a partial ceasefire with Ukraine following Ukrainian strikes on his prized Crimea bridge this week, which destroyed a large part of the structure.
