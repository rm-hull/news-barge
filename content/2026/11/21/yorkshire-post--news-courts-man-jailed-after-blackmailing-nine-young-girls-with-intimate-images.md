# Man jailed after blackmailing nine young girls with intimate images and threatening to kill their families

## A Bradford man who blackmailed nine young girls into sending intimate images and money, threatening to expose them online, has been jailed.

Musaib Hussain, 24, of Bridgwater Road, Bradford, was handed an extended 17-year sentence at Bradford Crown Court on Friday (Nov 20), after admitting 18 charges including blackmail, causing a child to engage in sexual activity and making indecent images of children.

West Yorkshire Police said Hussain targeted nine victims, often approaching young girls through social media platforms including TikTok and Snapchat.

He would ask them to send indecent photographs of themselves before threatening to share the images publicly unless they gave him money.

One victim described how Hussain threatened to kill her and her family if she refused to send him further images and videos.

In a victim statement, she said: “This man blackmailed, threatened to kill me and my family if I refused to share indecent images and videos of myself.

![Musaib Hussain](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/21/12/01/MUSAIB-HUSSAIN.jpeg?trim=32,0,228,0&width=640&quality=65)


“I was so scared, so I did what he asked me to do.

“When I found out that my intimate images and videos had been shared on social media my sense of safety was destroyed.

“I felt exposed, humiliated, and unable to control my own life. It has damaged my confidence and my trust in others. I just want my privacy back.”

Hussain was also made subject to a lifelong Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

Detectives believe there may be more victims who have not yet come forward.

Detective Constable Gemma Goodwin, of Bradford's Online Child Abuse Team, said: “Hussain is a dangerous individual who exploited these young women and girls for his own financial and sexual gain.

“I would like to praise the bravery of the victims for coming forward and reporting these crimes to us.

“He exploited nine young girls but we believe there are potentially more victims out there who have yet to come forward and report it to us.

“I hope this case shows that we take these offences extremely seriously and deal with them with the upmost sensitivity. You will be listened to and we will investigate thoroughly.”
