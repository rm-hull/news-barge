A MAIN road linking an East Yorkshire town to the motorway is set to be closed until ‘some time’ next week.

Humberside Police have confirmed that a road closure will be in place from the Tesco Distribution Centre in Tom Pudding Way (A161) in Goole to the M62 junction 36 westbound entry slip road until Monday (November 23).

As reported by The Press this afternoon (November 21), the county police told road users to avoid the area whilst officers were at the scene – with officers later adding the closure will end ‘some time’ on Monday.

A police spokesperson said: “This is in the hands of ERYC and relates to issues caused by fuel spillages unable to be made safe over the weekend.”

They added that to access the M62 westbound, drivers should enter junction 37 at Howden via Airmyn Road and avoid Rawcliffe Road where possible.

Police are yet to confirm any details about the closure, but comments suggest that an overturned tractor was seen on the road around the time that it was closed by officers.
