TWO men and a woman have been arrested in connection with drug crime on the Yorkshire Coast.

Humberside Police says that it has arrested three people after warrants were executed in Bridlington over the past few days.

Officers from the force say that whilst on patrol on Thursday (November 19) spotted a vehicle suspected of being driven without insurance where they stopped a 26-year-old driver was arrested on suspicion of driving while over the limit of a prescribed drug and driving uninsured.

A warrant was executed at an address on St Jude Road linked to the man.

A police spokesperson said: "As a result of this, the 26-year-old man was further arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply class A drugs.

"An 18-year-old woman was also arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply. Both the man and the woman have since been released under investigation."

On Friday (November 20). officers executed two more warrants at addresses on Grasmere Grove and Rhodena Avenue where a 21-year-old man was arrested at Grasmere Grove on suspicion of possession with intent to supply a class B drug.

He remains in police custody.
