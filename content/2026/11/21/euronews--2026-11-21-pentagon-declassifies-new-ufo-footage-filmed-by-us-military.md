The US Department of War published a new batch of declassified files relating to unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) on Thursday, marking the eighth release under a government disclosure programme.

The documents are available on the department's website as part of the Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE).

Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said the department and its partner agencies were already working on the next release, adding that further files would be published on a rolling basis.

The latest release follows previous efforts to make historical records about unexplained phenomena publicly accessible.
