Russell Brand is hoping to appeal his convictions after a jury found him guilty of rape and three counts of sexual assault, it has been reported.

The disgraced former comedian has reportedly instructed lawyers to consider whether he has any grounds to launch an appeal as he begins his 10-year sentence, according to the *Daily Mail*. In the UK, a judge must grant permission and be satisfied there are sufficient grounds for the appeal.

It comes after Brand’s wellness brand Reborn began selling $34.99 (£26) T-shirts emblazoned with the comic’s face on its website, along with the phrase “Free Russell”.

The *Daily Mail* reports Brand has also sacked his barrister Ian Winter KC, who represented him during the rape and sexual assault trial at Southwark Crown Court, after his outburst towards a journalist following the verdict.

Mr Winter confronted *Sunday Times* journalist Rosamund Urwin, whose work prompted police to investigate Brand in 2023, outside court, telling her: “I hope you’re happy”.

![Russell Brand was jailed for ten-and-a-half years (PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/13/f30bf8f74cb86975e2e906049c43d743Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1MTgwNjI0-2.86369922.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

When she responded she was “happy justice had been done” he replied: “Well, it wasn’t. You f\*\*\*\*\* it. Congratulations.”

The KC has since referred himself to the Bar Standards Board for investigation.

Last week, Brand was slammed by politicians after he issued an “appalling” message from behind bars declaring his innocence.

On their Sunday Service podcast, Brand’s wife Laura Gallacher read out a message from her husband in which he claimed: “I am not guilty of the offences I have been jailed for.”

A Downing Street spokesperson said on Monday that prisoners were strictly prohibited from making statements that cause distress to victims and are only allowed to speak to the media in exceptional circumstances.

![Brand's wellness company Reborn is selling 'free Russell' T-shirts for $34.99](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/10/12/56/Screenshot-2026-11-10-at-11-53-21.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Asked about the message, the prime minister’s official spokesperson said: “This is clearly appalling and is being urgently investigated. We expect action to be taken.

“Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims. And speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances. Prisoners cannot get around these rules by having someone else publish their comments.

“We expect action to be taken if the rules have been broken.”

A message from Brand on the Reborn website, which also sells products including methylene blue tincture and adaptogenic mushroom super coffee, boasts that the firm is “my answer to a world that wants you sick, numb, and quiet”.

“They’ve tried to shut me up, shut me down, and squeeze me off every platform – but this is where we fight back,” the message adds. “Every bottle is about spiritual and physical resilience, and it’s your direct way to stand with me and keep this mission alive.”

Brand has been moved from the category B Wandsworth prison in south-west London, to the lower category C HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire – a prison for men convicted of a sex offence.

He has been accused of sexual misconduct and predatory behaviour by six more women, according to a report in *The Times*, and is also facing investigation over two further rape complaints, police have said.
