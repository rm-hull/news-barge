# Wes Streeting calls Putin a 'cold-blooded killer' as he meets Zelensky in Kyiv

UK Defence Secretary Wes Streeting has met with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky following continued Russian attacks on Ukraine on Friday evening and overnight.

Zelensky has justified Ukraine's repeated attacks on a critical bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, describing it as a "fair response" to 109 separate Russian attacks on bridges in Ukraine.

He rejected a claim from Russia that the bridge, which links Russia to the peninsular it illegally annexed from Ukraine, was "exclusively civilian" in use.

Streeting and Zelensky hugged upon meeting and the British defence secretary explained he had come to Kyiv to see what support Ukraine needs for winter.

The BBC's Sarah Rainsford reports from Kyiv.
