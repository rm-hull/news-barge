# Labour and Reform UK private investment deal for DSA ‘superseded' by SYMCA purchase

## A previous agreement between Reform UK and Labour leaders in Doncaster to seek private investment in Doncaster Sheffield Airport has been ‘superseded’, the local Mayor has said.

Labour’s Mayor Ros Jones, who leads the City of Doncaster Council, agreed with former Reform UK leader Councillor Guy Aston to seek private investment in the airport as a condition of his party’s support for approving a £57million loan in November 2025.

Speaking during a November 19 meeting, Coun Aston told the Mayor: “You and I came to an agreement to approve gainshare investment on the basis that we would seek private investment when the first flight goes.”

Now a Reform UK Doncaster backbencher, Coun Aston sought reassurance from Mayor Jones that the agreement would still be honoured once the South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority (SYMCA) completed the freehold purchase of the airport.

![Doncaster Sheffield Airport](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjgzODU0ODYyLWRjYjQtNDA1NS1hYjIzLTZkYTJiMmM3NDg5Yzo1NjVkYmViYS05NjViLTQzNmEtODA4ZS1jZjYzMmVmMThhOTc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


In a brief response, Mayor Jones said: “The agreement that we made last year has been superseded.”

The Doncaster Mayor’s office later said on November 20, 2026, that private investment in Doncaster Sheffield Airport (DSA) would still be sought, but was no longer tied down to the previous agreement.

Journalists first revealed the upcoming deal for SYMCA to purchase DSA days just before it was confirmed by Mayor Jones and Oliver Coppard, the Mayor of South Yorkshire.

Political leaders from across the region approved its side of the purchase deal in a SYMCA Board meeting in October, with Mayor Coppard then revealing the deal was now resting on approval from the current landowner, the Peel Group.

![Reform UK group leader on City of Doncaster Council.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjg0Y2JlMWFiLWI3YzItNDRiMi1hMjJiLTEyN2Y0ZTExY2MwNzpkOGVkNzhmNy0zZDBkLTQ4MzYtOGQ0NC1hZjA4ODFkOTk5MzY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


It is understood that decision by Peel is due in late November 2026.

Details of the purchase deal are limited, but Mayor Coppard has committed to sharing financial details after the agreement is signed.

The move has received support from Reform UK’s national deputy leader, Richard Tice MP, who welcomed the move.

However, Mr Tice also said the airport reopening still requires ‘more private investment’.
