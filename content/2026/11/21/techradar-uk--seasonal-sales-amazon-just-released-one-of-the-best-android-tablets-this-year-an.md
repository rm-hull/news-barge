Amazon's decision to quietly discontinue its FireOS line of tablets bore fruit when it later announced the arrival of a series of AndroidOS-based tablets, the Alexa Tablets 8, 11 and 12 Pro. Having spent the last few weeks with the largest of the three, I've been incredibly pleasantly surprised; even more so when I logged on this morning to find that the very model I tested is on offer already, bringing the base Alexa Tablet 12 Pro with nanomatte coating down to $449.99 (was $549.99) at Amazon US and over at Amazon UK it's £499.99 down to £339.99.  
*•* Shop more Black Friday deals

We're a week out from Black Friday deals proper, but that's not stopping brands like Amazon from bringing us discounts nice and early. Generally speaking, we don't see further price cuts on Amazon's own products and services over the course of sales seasons, so this is a solid option to shop now if you're on the market for a brilliant tablet.

I scored the 12 Pro model a respectable 4-stars in my review, and having quickly tried the other few at an event recently, I'm really impressed as a whole by Amazon's new range of slates, and in their first-ever holiday season, such major discounts bode well for these affordable Android tablets.

My top pick across the board is the fully specced, nanomatte-coated 12 Pro; at its price, it's one of the best-value Android tablets available today.
