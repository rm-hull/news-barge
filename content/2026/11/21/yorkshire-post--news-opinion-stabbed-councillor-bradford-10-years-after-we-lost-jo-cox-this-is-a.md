# Stabbed councillor, Bradford: 10 years after we lost Jo Cox, this is a stark reminder

![Sorely missed: Jo Cox was killed in 2016. That a councillor has been stabbed in Bradford serves as a reminder that more work must be done to protect our public servants. (photo | Yui Mok/PA Wire)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjNjZjNmYTcwLWY2NDYtNDI0NS04ZWFkLTgyMjY5MGI1MWQ3YzplYTk1YWM4MC1hMzIzLTRkYjUtYTQ2ZS01OTRiNWIyNmZkZDE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## That a man has been arrested and charged with attempted murder, after a Bradford councillor was stabbed multiple times whilst out canvassing, serves as a stark reminder of the peril that public servants face as they go about their civic duties.

Coun Ibrar Ali’s attack comes in the tenth year since we lost MP Jo Cox; that will be lost on few, yet whilst West Yorkshire Police has said it has no reason nor information at this stage to suggest the attack was politically motivated, it is no less concerning.

Quite how measures can be put in place that prevent attacks on councillors, MPs and others as they go about their constituency work isn’t clear. The essence of democracy in this country comes from being able to reach at close quarters those who represent us in places of power.

Being able to sit face-to-face with your ward councillor or your elected Member of Parliament, people who know how to solve problems that, on occasion, can vex individuals and communities alike for days, weeks and months, can be a lifeline.

But yet again we find ourselves repeating the words: an attack on one elected representative constitutes an attack upon our very democracy itself; we must not allow those words to become normalised. To lose meaning.

Quite rightly, community leaders have expressed deep concerns for and sympathies with Coun Ali, whose service he should be thanked for. This newspaper joins those expressions of appreciation for him, and for everyone who gives their time, effort and patience to doing public good, and wishes Coun Ali the speediest of full recoveries.
