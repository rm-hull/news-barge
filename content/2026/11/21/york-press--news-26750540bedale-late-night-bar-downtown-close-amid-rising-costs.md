A North Yorkshire town is set to lose one of its late-night drinking spots as a bar announces it will close amid rising costs.

Downtown, based in Market Place, Bedale, has announced it will close following one final weekend of trading.

The bar currently opens on Friday and Saturday nights from 10pm until 2am and has become one of the town’s late-night venues, offering a dance floor, karaoke and pool.

Announcing the closure, the team behind Downtown said increasing operating costs had made it no longer financially viable to keep the venue running for just a few hours each week.

In a statement, Downtown said: “We are unfortunately closing Downtown, effective this Saturday (November 21).

“Increases in operating costs for what effectively is only open seven hours a week have made it impossible to keep running feasibly.

“We would like to thank everyone who’s partied with us and let’s go out on a bang this weekend.”

The venue, at 8 Market Place, also hosts private events and describes itself as “Bedale’s late night boozer”.

Downtown will now welcome customers for a final Saturday night before closing its doors for good.
