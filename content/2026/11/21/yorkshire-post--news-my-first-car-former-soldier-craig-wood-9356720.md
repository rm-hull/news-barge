# My First Car: Former soldier Craig Wood

## Former soldier Craig Wood could not make do with just any vehicle for his first car.

He was just 18 when he suffered serious injuries in a bomb attack in Afghanistan in which he lost both legs and an arm.

But thanks to Motability, the motoring charity, he passed his test first time.

“I had three minors on my test, which wasn’t bad,” said Craig, now 35. He said the car was adapted for him. It was an automatic, and it had a device which engaged forward motion when you pushed it and backwards when you pulled it.

![Clio](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjE4MWY5NTVjLTRhMWYtNGQxZC1hZmRmLTRkNTVkMmM5YTQ2NDowMGFlZTg5Zi05ZTk5LTRhNWItOGMxNy0zZjhhYzYxNDM3NGE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“It was terrific,” said Craig. “I did a lot of miles in it from Yorkshire to the south coast on a regular basis.

“It was remarkably easy to drive. Motability did a great job converting what was essentially a standard Clio automatic.

“I enjoyed driving, but I don’t have a car now. I don’t really need one. If I had a vehicle, I could see myself getting a van. That would be more my style. But in truth I prefer boats to cars.”

Craig, a father of four, lives with his Swiss wife Renata and the children on a boat in Indonesia. He says he rarely travels back to his native Doncaster because the cost would be prohibitive.

![Craig Wood](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmU5ZDhlMjkwLTczZjAtNGYyMi05MjAzLThmZTllMTdiMTA1Yjo2YmZjZTQwZi0wZjMyLTRjZWYtYjdiZS1jYTc3YzlhYTlkZDQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Craig is now a motivational speaker and lives on an Army pension.

He recently took part in a solo voyage sailing from Mexico to Japan, a total of 7,506 nautical miles. It took him 90 days, and he says it was a physical and psychological challenge.

The mission was on a 41ft catamaran, and a video of the expedition is on YouTube. Search for Singlehanded.
