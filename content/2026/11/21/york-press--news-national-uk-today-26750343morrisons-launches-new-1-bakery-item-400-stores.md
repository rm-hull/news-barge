Morrisons has launched a new limited-edition item in its bakery, which will be available until just after Christmas.

The supermarket has added a £1 Bear Paw Cob to the selection, which is a giant, paw-shaped loaf of bread.

It is inspired by Morrisons' Christmas advert this year, which was a festive reimagining of Goldilocks and the Three Bears.

The supermarket added: "Each doughy paw is handcrafted from scratch by Morrisons expert bakers and baked fresh daily at its Market Street bakery counters, ensuring every bite is just right.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21746892.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Bear Paw Cob will be available until December 27 *(Image: Morrisons)*

"Priced at just £1, the novelty bake is designed for tear and share fun, and is paw-fect for pairing alongside festive cheeses, hearty dips, or Christmas buffet spreads."

The Morrisons Bear Paw Cob is available in over 400 bakeries until Sunday, December 27.

## Morrisons crowned Retailer of the Year for 2026

In other consumer news, Morrisons has taken the top prize at the 2026 Quality Food Awards after being crowned Retailer of the Year.

The supermarket finished ahead of Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury’s, Aldi, Lidl, M&S and Waitrose, with the award recognising the retailer with the best overall performance across the competition’s main food and drink categories.

Morrisons collected two Gold awards, 14 Silver medals and 13 Bronze awards for its own-brand products.

The Best Thai Sweet Chilli Crisps and The Best Free From Gluten Chocolate Millionaires Cookies were among the products to receive Gold awards.

Winners at the Quality Food Awards are selected following blind tastings by chefs, food scientists and industry experts, who assess products on taste, ingredients, aroma, packaging and value for money.

Rebecca Haliday, Head of Own Brand at Morrisons, said: “We are delighted to be named Retailer of the Year 2026, along with being awarded a number of accolades at the Quality Food Awards.

“A tremendous amount of care and effort goes into testing and perfecting our products across our ranges, so seeing that dedication recognised by the prestigious QFAs is hugely rewarding - and a testament to everyone who works so hard behind the scenes to bring the very best to our customers.”
