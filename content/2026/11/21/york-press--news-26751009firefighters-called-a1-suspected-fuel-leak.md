FIREFIGHTERS from two different counties were called to an incident on the motorway this afternoon.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service says its crews from Tadcaster, plus an an appliance from West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, were called to the A1 – between junctions 46 and 47 – at 2.05pm on Saturday (November 21).
