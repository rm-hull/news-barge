A former staffer for New Jersey Republican Rep. Jeff Van Drew has been criminally charged for allegedly staging an anti-Trump attack and lying to investigators about it.

Federal prosecutors have accused Natalie Greene and an alleged accomplice Anamaria Moe of staging a violent scene in 2025 to make it appear that three armed men had attacked Greene because of her former work for the congressman. Greene claimed that men restrained and wrote “Trump W\*\*\*\*” on her body.

A federal indictment accuses Greene, 27, of paying a body scarification artist $500 to cut designs into her face, neck, upper chest and shoulder. Scarification involves deliberately cutting the skin to create permanent marks.

Greene then allegedly returned to South Jersey, picked up Moe, 25, and wrote “Trump W\*\*\*\*” across Greene’s stomach and “[Federal Official 1] is racist” on her back, according to the indictment

Greene is charged with making false statements and lying to federal investigators. She was released on a $200,000 unsecured bond with additional conditions after her initial court appearance on Thursday. Moe is charged with conspiracy and making false statements to authorities.

After the alleged hoax attack, Moe then called 911 to report that the pair had been assaulted while walking along a trail.

“They were attacking her. They were like talking about politics and stuff. They were like calling her names,” Moe allegedly told a 911 dispatcher, according to a transcript included in court records.

When officers arrived, Greene allegedly claimed one of the attackers had pointed a gun at her and threatened to shoot her. Police found her in the woods with her shirt pulled over her head and her hands and feet bound with zip ties. The injuries and political messages written on her body appeared to back up her story.

Greene allegedly repeated the fabricated story during interviews with local police and FBI agents.

But investigators later uncovered evidence that they saw shows that the attack had been staged.

A search of Greene’s Maserati turned up zip ties and duct tape, prosecutors said.

Investigators also found that her cellphone had been used to view Reddit communities focused on body modifications and scarification, as well as to obtain directions to the Pennsylvania studio where the procedure took place.

Moe’s cellphone also revealed a search for “zip ties near me” two days before the alleged attack, according to the indictment.

![Greene allegedly paid a Pennsylvania artist $500 to cut permanent designs into her body before staged attack](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/21/21/59/Screenshot-2026-11-21-at-4-55-29-PM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The indictment is the first time Moe has been formally charged in this case. Greene was initially charged in November 2025, when her alleged accomplice was identified only as an unnamed co-conspirator.

Both women face up to five years in federal prison for each count if convicted, as well as a potential $250,000 fine and up to three years of supervised release.

Van Drew's office told the Associated Press in a statement Thursday that it was "deeply saddened" by the incident.

“We hope she's getting the care she needs,” his office said. The office did not immediately clarify whether she was still employed by the congressman at the time of the incident.

Van Drew lost re-election for his seat in New Jersey’s 2nd Congressional District. He was defeated by Democrat Zack Mullock in midterm elections this month.
