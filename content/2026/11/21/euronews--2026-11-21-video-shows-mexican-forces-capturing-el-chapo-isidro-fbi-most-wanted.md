Mexican special forces captured drug kingpin Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as “Chapo Isidro”, during an operation in the northwestern state of Sinaloa on Friday night.

Footage released by Mexico’s Defence Department shows heavily armed soldiers escorting a detainee through a building and into an army vehicle.

Meza Flores was among the FBI’s 10 most-wanted fugitives and is accused of trafficking drugs into the United States and illegally carrying firearms.

Mexican authorities said intelligence provided by the Trump administration helped lead to his capture. The operation was followed by violence in the region, prompting security forces to remain deployed to protect civilians.
