A road is closed after a crash this morning (Saturday).

A lorry has collided with the railway bridge on Broach Road, the A645 at Little Heck near Selby.

It’s left the A645 is closed in both directions.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “There are no reports of injuries, but the road is likely to be blocked for some time.

“Officers are at the scene now to ensure everyone is safe.

“Thank you for your patience and understanding – please consider alternative routes if you’re in this area.”
