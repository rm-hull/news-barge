## Do you know what this is? Mysterious 'UFO' spotted in York city centre

A UFO spotted in York city centre *(Image: Provided)*

A MYSTERIOUS 'UFO' has been spotted hovering in York this morning.

A pair of workmen who were on a job in York city centre on Saturday morning (November 21) have submitted this image to The Press in hopes that readers are able to help them in getting to the bottom of a mystery.

In a photo taken from the bottom of Parliament Street at around 7.20am, one of the men said that he spotted a strange object in the sky in the direction of the Stonebow area.

He said: “I was working with a friend in town this morning and we walked to Greggs for a coffee when we were waiting to get into the site.

“We were stood in that bit outside the Christmas Markets in between Greggs and M&S when I spotted this thing in the sky. It looked like it was stationary, but it couldn’t have just been not moving.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21747781.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another photo of the UFO spotted in York *(Image: Provided)*

“I got my mate to try and take some photos of it, then after we walked away, it sort of veered to the left.”

After sitting down, the pair had Googled it later and found a sighting in April of this year that matched their experience of an orangey red object in the sky.

The man added: “I think there’s definitely a logical explanation behind it, but it’s just weird that in a small city like York that nobody else mentioned seeing it and nobody else was looking around.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21747782.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The eyewitnesses say the object veered to the left *(Image: Provided)*

“We did try searching to see if it was a comet or something else, but nothing came up. I thought someone else might know."
