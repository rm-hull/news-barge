France have a foot in the Nations Championship 2026 final, but as last weekend's results demonstrated, things can shift very quickly in this competition. Fabien Galthie's men enter Round 6 one point clear at the top of the Northern Hemisphere standings, meaning a bonus-point victory over Argentina would guarantee them a shot at the inaugural title. However, they could yet slip to third in the pecking order.

Matthieu Jalibert's return to fitness means we won't be seeing Antoine Dupont in the fly-half role at Stade de France. Les Bleus' inspirational skipper returns to No.9 after putting back-to-back world champions South Africa to the sword with an outstanding display last Friday. Esteban Capilla, Charles Ollivon and Anthony Jelonch also come into the starting XV, while Kalvin Gourgues is expected to take on kicking duties.

Argentina are mathematically out of the running, despite getting the better of Italy last weekend. The 19-14 win, which came courtesy of Bautista Bernasconi, Rodrigo Isgro and Mateo Carreras tries, snapped a six-game losing streak, but what was especially heartening was the way Felipe Contepomi's men held strong despite leading the yellow card count by three to one.

As things stand, the Pumas will contest the fifth-place playoff, at the expense of Australia. However, the Wallabies can still catch them, and they have a far superior points differential to boot.
