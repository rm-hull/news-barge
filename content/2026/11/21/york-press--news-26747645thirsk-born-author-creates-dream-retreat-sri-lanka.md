## Yorkshire author transforms tea plantation into Sri Lanka retreat

The 180° Villa has been created by Thirsk-born author, designer, and yoga teacher Diana de Gunzburg *(Image: Supplied)*

A dream retreat created by a Yorkshire-based author blending hospitality and storytelling has taken shape in Sri Lanka.

The 180° Villa is the creation of Thirsk-born author, designer and yoga teacher Diana de Gunzburg, who transformed a former tea plantation into a guest house and wellness centre just 15 minutes from Galle.

Set on a five-acre estate in Padinnoruwa, the villa is now regarded as one of Sri Lanka’s most sought-after wellness destinations.

Ms de Gunzburg said: "I immediately jumped at it, as I saw and felt how magical it was, and although my finances couldn’t really justify it, I took the huge risk and bought it.

"It has been a labour of love ever since then, but I am proud of how I have turned my dream into a reality."

The three-bedroom property includes an infinity pool, spa facilities, an on-site restaurant, and space for yoga and meditation.

Ms de Gunzburg, co-author of the Moonstone Legacy Quartet with Yorkshireman Tony Wild, said: "I healed physically and emotionally in this stunning setting after my distressing marriage breakdown, and I now want to help others to enjoy the unique quality of my retreat."

The villa also helped inspire the final book in the Moonstone Legacy series.
