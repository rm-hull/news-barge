**DELAYS have now cleared on the railways after a points failure in York.**

Rail travel in and out of York was delayed after a points failure in the area earlier this afternoon (November 21).

“Due to a points failure at York, trains may be delayed in both directions. Engineers are on site investigating the fault,” said a spokesperson for Transpennine Express.

Disruption was expected until 1pm.

The routes affected were as follows:

· Newcastle and Liverpool Lime Street.

· Scarborough and Manchester Victoria.

· Redcar Central and Manchester Airport.

· York and Manchester Piccadilly.

TransPennine Express added that customers should continue to travel as planned, but be aware that their journey may be delayed.
