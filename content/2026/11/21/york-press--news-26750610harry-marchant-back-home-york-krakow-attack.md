## 'He showed true strength to get through' - York man shot in Krakow returns home

Harry Marchant's family shared this photo of him with The Press as they thanked everyone who had supported them. *(Image: Family)*

**A YORK man who was shot in the Krakow terror attack has ‘celebrated’ his 20th birthday by returning home safely to York.**

Harry Marchant, who turned 20 on Wednesday (November 18) and is from Haxby, has now officially made it back to York after travelling across Europe by land.

The York City supporter has been receiving treatment in hospital in the Polish city after being wounded during a shooting in the city’s Main Square on Saturday, November 7.

He was one of 15 people injured in the attack, in which six people were killed, including a British man.

His parents flew to Poland to be at his bedside and later launched a GoFundMe appeal to help meet the significant cost of bringing him home safely via land, as flying was thought to be unfeasible due to his injuries.

As reported by The Press on Tuesday (November 17), he was given the news that he was able to be discharged and could return home via land transport.

In an update, Fran said: “We have wonderful news to share! Harry left Kraków, Poland at 4pm on Wednesday 18th November.

“He travelled with his Mum Fran and Dad Jim all the way through Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium and France, then through the Channel Tunnel and arrived back in York at 1am on Friday 20th November.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21702598.jpg?type=mds-article-620)York teenager Harry Marchant, on the right, pictured with York City FC striker Josh Stones, is said to be 'overwhelmed' by the support in his home city after he was shot in a terror attack in Krakow. *(Image: Family photo)*

“He did amazing and showed true strength to get through the journey.

“We thank everyone so much for supporting us to get Harry home. We are so pleased now he can continue his recovery at home, surrounded and supported by his loving family and friends.

“Thank you - from the bottom of our hearts. We’ll keep you posted on his progress “

The appeal quickly attracted support from friends, relatives, fellow York City fans as well as some of the players, along with complete strangers, including numerous Polish nationals.

Among the contributions was £1,500 from the York City Vice Presidents Association, which was formed in 1980 to support the club, which plays Walsall today (November 21), with other donations going towards training ground improvements, among other projects.

Fran previously said they were overwhelmed by the support which had been "absolutely amazing and taken so much pressure off.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21702193.jpg?type=mds-article-620)People lay flowers in the city center two days after a shooting in Krakow, Poland, Monday, Nov. 9, 2026. (AP Photo/Beata Zawrzel) *(Image: (AP Photo/Beata Zawrzel))*

"He was shot and has been extremely poorly. For me, it is about getting him home," she told The Press last week, paying tribute to the medical team that had been caring for him, from the first responder at the scene.

The Foreign Secretary formally designated the November 7 attack as an act of terrorism, with the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office providing consular support to the family.

The 24-year-old gunman was shot by police and later died in hospital. He was also suspected of killing three members of his own family before the Krakow attack.
