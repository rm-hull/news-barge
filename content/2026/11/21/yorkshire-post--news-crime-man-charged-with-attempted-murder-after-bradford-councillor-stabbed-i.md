# Man charged with attempted murder after Bradford councillor stabbed in street

## A man has been charged with attempted murder after a Bradford councillor was stabbed in a street attack that left him seriously injured.

At around 11am on Thursday (Nov 19), an incident took place on Thornbury Avenue in Bradford, in which a 52-year-old local councillor was stabbed.

The victim remains in hospital in a serious but stable condition.

Arfan Ali, 32, of Thornbury Avenue, Bradford, has been charged with attempted murder, assault and possession of an offensive weapon in a public place.

He has been remanded in custody and is due to appear before Leeds Magistrates' Court on Saturday (Nov 21).
