A longtime superfan of President Donald Trumpis preparing to take down his mountain of MAGA memorabilia after becoming disillusioned with the president he once followed across the country.

Rick Frazier, 70, became a “Front Row Joe” by traveling to nearly every state to attend what he estimates to be more than 100 Trump rallies, often driving hundreds of miles to secure a spot near the front of the crowd.

But after years of defending Trump, the retired Ohio factory worker says he has grown increasingly frustrated with the president's second administration, pointing to his handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files, his pardon of convicted drug trafficker Honduran President Juan Orlando Hernández, and his failure to deliver on promises to clean up Washington.

Now, Frazier is ready to start removing the Trump merchandise that has filled his home for years.

“I haven’t taken down my memorabilia as of today,” he told *The Daily Beast* in an interview published Friday. “But I do feel I will start putting it away. Some of it I actually plan on selling and giving my kids.”

![Retired Ohio factory worker Rick Frazier, 70, is taking down his MAGA memorabilia after turning against President Donald Trump, whom he once followed across the country](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/28/15/1479857010..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“It’s slowly coming down, the kids are going on the wall,” he added.

Frazier’s devout support also included trips to Mar-a-Lago, where he sported a red “NO CRIMES” tie following Trump’s indictment in 2023. He also got a letter from Trump after his daughter died of a drug overdose.

But now, Frazier plans to replace evidence of his Trump years with more personal photos.

“I’m just slowly taking some of it down, but it is good memories to me, and I’ll be slowly putting pictures back up on the wall, but that’s just something I really didn’t have a lot of to begin with. I do have pictures of my family, but there’ll probably be more,” he said.

“I am surrounded by memorabilia,” he added.

Despite his falling out with Trump, Frazier said he does not regret the time or money he spent supporting the president. Instead, he views the collection as a reminder of the people he met and the experiences he had along the way.

“It’s memorabilia to me, memories. I can look at certain things and remember what I did or how I got to be there that day,” he said. “And I don’t look at it like I’m looking at it to a bad memory. ... Memories and experiences, you know? Because I’m not going to live with regret or something. I enjoyed all that. I just didn’t enjoy the outcome.”

![Frazier's loyalty included trips to Mar-a-Lago, wearing a ‘NO CRIMES’ tie in Trump's defense and receiving a personal letter from the president after his daughter's death](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/21/14/1479857537..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

But since returning to the White House, the president's actions have increasingly clashed with the promises that first drew Frazier to the MAGA movement.

Beyond his objections to Trump's handling of the Epstein files and the Hernández pardon, Frazier was frustrated by the president's failure to deliver on his 2015 promise to “drain the swamp” of Washington’s political establishment.

“Honestly, the only bad memories are that I didn’t get what I thought I was going to get back in 2015,” he said.

Frazier has since described Trump as “the king of the swamp,” a stark contrast to the anti-establishment image that initially drew him to the MAGA movement.

Asked what he would tell the president now, Frazier told *The Daily Beast*: “Stop listening to the lobbyists and start listening to the little guy that works hard for you.”
