On the occasion of the World Climate Conference (COP31) in Turkey, the German environmental movement “Fridays for Future” is planning nationwide protests and demonstrations at around 70 locations on Friday.

A rally is planned for this afternoon in front of the Chancellery in Berlin. According to Fridays for Future, a demonstration is also planned for this afternoon in Wittenberge in the Prignitz region.

A group of activists from Fridays for Future projected huge images of the CDU leader onto a Bundestag building last week, accompanied by messages such as ‘Climate summit without Merz’ and ‘We’ll show Merz what the future is’. After he cancelled his trip to the World Climate Conference.

“The fact that Merz isn’t attending the UN summit and climate conference shows once again that he couldn’t care less about the future of young people,” said Fridays for Future. “The Chancellor is abandoning us, but we will never give up on our future.”

In their appeal, the organisers demand, among other things, that Germany and other industrialised nations should spend more money to help developing countries adapt to global warming. The activists also advocate for accelerating the global phase-out of coal, oil, and gas.

The spokeswoman for “Fridays for Future”, Nele Evers, told rbb 88.8 that Germany must take responsibility for phasing out fossil fuels.

At the UN Climate Change Conference in Antalya, Turkey, around 200 countries are discussing global climate protection until November 20th. The focus is on curbing global warming and the consequences of climate change, such as extreme weather.
