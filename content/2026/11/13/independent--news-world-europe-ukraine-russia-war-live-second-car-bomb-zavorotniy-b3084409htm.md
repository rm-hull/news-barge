## What’s next for Ukraine-Russia peace talks as war approaches fifth year?

US envoys met yesterday with Russian president Vladimir Putin in the Kremlin before heading to Ireland for talks with Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelensky as Washington sought new momentum for its peace efforts.

Envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, who is also president Donald Trump's son-in-law, brokered talks this week between delegations from Moscow and Kyiv.

The session in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, brought the first direct discussions between Ukrainian and Russian representatives in nine months as the push for a settlement stalled.

The talks produced no major breakthrough, aside from an agreement to swap a large number of prisoners, with the exact figure still to be announced.

## Sapphire linked to Romanov family fetches nearly £4m at auction

A bracelet containing a sapphire once owned by Russia's doomed Romanov family has fetched more than 4.1 million Swiss francs (£3.7 million) at auction in Geneva.

Sotheby's said that the price vastly exceeded the expected price range of 850,000 to 1.7 million francs (£770,000 to £1.54 million) for the stone, which went under the hammer late on Wednesday.

The buyer was a private collector who was not identified.

The cushion-shaped sapphire weighs 197.80 carats and is a Ceylon stone that likely came from the Mughal Empire in South Asia.

## Ukrainian troops spent month in underground tunnels to launch counterattack

Volodymyr Zelensky had last month hailed the “stunning success” of the operation by the Khartiia corps, launched to prevent Russian troops slipping into the eastern city of Kupyansk.

Now, Kyiv has revealed that its troops spent as long as a month underground, battling through freezing water and sifting through the bodies of dead Russian soldiers that Moscow had built up as a barricade inside the long tunnels - before opening fire on a group of Russian soldiers.
