Firefighters were called to an incident at a commercial building in York today (Friday).

York and Acomb crews, supported by a water bowser from Tadcaster, were sent to a commercial building in Elvington, York, at 9.35am.

Hot embers had travelled from a piece of machinery into the extractor fan and ignited a build-up of sawdust.

#### Elvington, York

Crews used a 9m ladder to reach and inspect the extractor fan. They then removed the build-up and put out the fire using a hose reel jet.
