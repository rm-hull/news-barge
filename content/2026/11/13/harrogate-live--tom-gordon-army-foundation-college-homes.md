Redrow wants to build up to 285 homes beside the Army Foundation College in Harrogate. MP Tom Gordon raised security concerns in the Commons on 12 November.

Plans for up to 285 homes on farmland directly east of the **Army Foundation College** west of Harrogate need “to be thought through very carefully” because of the site’s security, Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon has told the government.

Redrow Homes Yorkshire is seeking outline planning permission for the homes on land west of Oaker Bank, near Penny Pot Lane. Mr Gordon, the Liberal Democrat MP, raised the plans in the House of Commons on Thursday 12 November, and the minister he questioned offered to set up a meeting about Harrogate.

## Overview

* **The plan:** up to 285 homes, outline planning application by Redrow Homes Yorkshire
* **The site:** farmland west of Oaker Bank, near Penny Pot Lane, directly east of the Army Foundation College
* **The concern:** Mr Gordon says the college is a relatively isolated site and that this matters for its security
* **What happens next:** the minister offered Mr Gordon a meeting with the Minister for Veterans and People

## What was said in the Commons

Mr Gordon put his question to Luke Pollard, the Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry. He told the minister that the college is an isolated site west of Harrogate, and pointed out that the minister had already spoken about concerns over the protection of defence sites and the threats they face.

He then asked what steps the Ministry of Defence is taking with other government departments and North Yorkshire Council on the security and protection of places like the college.

In reply, Mr Pollard said Harrogate plays an important role for the armed forces. He offered to set up a meeting between Mr Gordon and the Minister for Veterans and People to discuss Harrogate, and added that communities next to bases across the country care passionately about their security and actively contribute to it.

The full exchange can be watched on Parliament Live, from 11:10:56.

## What the MP said

Mr Gordon said: “The Army Foundation College is a relatively isolated site at the moment, and that matters for its security. Any plan to build hundreds of homes right next to it needs to be thought through very carefully.

“These latest plans come on top of an additional 1000 homes proposed for nearby. There are sites that are far more suitable than this one, which would limit the expansion of the AFC and pose security concerns.”

He added: “The Minister himself recognised that local communities care passionately about the security of these bases, and Harrogate is no different. The armed forces are a crucial part of our community here. I welcome the offer of a meeting, and I’ll be following this process every step of the way.”
