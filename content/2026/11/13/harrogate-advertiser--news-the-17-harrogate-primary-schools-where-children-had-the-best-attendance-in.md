Choosing the right school is an important decision for parents, with attendance playing a key role in helping children make the most of their education

A good attendance record can be a sign of a school where pupils are engaged and supported, with children consistently showing up and taking part

Applications opened last month for children due to start Reception in September 2027, with families having until January 15, 2027 to submit their choices ahead of national offer day on April 16, 2027

With families across Harrogate now considering their primary school options, we have looked back at the latest attendance figures for schools across the town to find out which ones had the highest rates of pupils attending lessons throughout the 2024/25 academic year

Using data from the Department for Education, we have put together a list of the 17 primary schools in Harrogate with the best attendance records...
