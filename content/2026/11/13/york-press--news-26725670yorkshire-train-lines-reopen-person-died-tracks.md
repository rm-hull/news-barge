Train lines in Yorkshire have reopened after a person died on the tracks.

Passengers travelling from Manchester to York faced delays and cancellations on Thursday evening (November 12) after emergency services responded to the incident near Wakefield.

TransPennine Express said all lines have since reopened with services running as normal by Friday morning.

British Transport Police said its officers and paramedics were called to a report of a “casualty on the tracks” near Wakefield Kirkgate shortly after 2.52pm on Thursday.

"Officers attended alongside paramedics but sadly a person was pronounced dead at the scene,” a force spokesperson said.

"The incident is not being treated as suspicious and a file will be prepared for the coroner.”

National Rail said passengers may be eligible for refunds if a train is delayed or cancelled and the passenger chooses not to travel or is unable to complete their journey.

Compensation may be available for passengers who arrive late due to a cancelled or delayed service, the company added.
