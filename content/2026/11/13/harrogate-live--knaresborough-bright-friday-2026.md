The Christmas tree lights go on at about 6.30pm after a lantern parade from the Castle Grounds. Castlegate, the Market Place and Silver Street close to traffic from noon to 10pm.

Knaresborough’s Christmas lights switch-on, Bright Friday, will be held on Friday 27 November. The town council confirmed the date on 11 November.

The evening is free. It ends with the Christmas tree in the Market Place being lit at about 6.30pm, after a lantern parade through the town centre (Knaresborough Town Council).

## Overview

**Bright Friday is on Friday 27 November 2026.** Entertainment starts at 5.15pm in the Market Place. The light parade leaves the war memorial in the Castle Grounds at 6pm, and the tree is lit at about 6.30pm.**Castlegate, the Market Place and Silver Street close to traffic from 12 noon to 10pm.**

## What happens and when

The council’s event poster and parade poster set out the evening:

| Time | What | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.30pm | Lantern making workshop, with pizza and drinks | Gracious Street Methodist Church |
| 5.15pm | Entertainment on the town council stage | Market Place |
| 6pm | Light parade sets off | War memorial, Castle Grounds |
| About 6.30pm | Christmas tree lights switched on | Market Place |

The lantern workshop is free and open to everyone, with donations welcome. The parade poster asks people to bring a lantern or a torch, and a bell to ring if they have one.

The poster lists who is taking part:

* Knaresborough Silver Band
* Harrogate Spa Town Ukes Band
* Knaresborough Choral Society
* a light dance by Orb Community Arts
* carols, mulled wine and Father Christmas

The event is supported by David Skaith, the Mayor of York and North Yorkshire. It is funded by York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority through its Vibrant and Sustainable High Streets Fund, according to both posters.

## Which roads close

The town council has applied to North Yorkshire Council for a temporary road closure order. Its letter to residents and businesses gives these times:

| Where | Closed to traffic |
| --- | --- |
| Castlegate, all of the Market Place and Silver Street | 12 noon to 10pm |
| The Market Place “loop”, from Boots round to Subway | 12 noon to 10pm |
| Market “Street”, from the High Street to Kirkgate | 12 noon to 10pm |
| Access to the Castle Courtyard car park, at the Cross Keys | 5.15pm to 6.30pm |

The letter says the closures do not affect walking access to homes, shops or other premises. The car park entrance shuts because a large crowd, many of them young children, is expected to walk to and from the Castle Top for the parade. Anyone already parked there is asked to stay until the parade has left.

## What it means for you

* **If you drive into Knaresborough that day**, do not park in the Market Place in the morning. The closures start at noon.
* **If you are coming for the parade**, the lantern workshop at 4.30pm leaves time to make a lantern and walk up to the Castle Grounds for 6pm.
* **If you run a business in the Market Place**, deliveries need to be done before noon.

Ripon’s Christmas lights switch-on is on Saturday 14 November. Harrogate is not holding one this year.
