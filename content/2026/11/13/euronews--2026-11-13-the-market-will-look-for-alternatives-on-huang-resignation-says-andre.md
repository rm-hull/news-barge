Nvidia is at the centre of the global artificial intelligence revolution. But the American chip giant is now facing an extraordinary crisis, following the shock resignation of its CEO Jensen Huang amid allegations of an espionage scandal.

The implications could go far beyond one company's leadership, raising serious questions about corporate security, China's intelligence ambitions and America's technological supremacy. To answer all these questions, Stefan Grobe was joined by Andrea Renda, head of research at the Centre for European Policy Studies.
