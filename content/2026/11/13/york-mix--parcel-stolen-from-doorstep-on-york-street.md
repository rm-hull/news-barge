Police are investigating after a parcel was stolen from a York doorstep.

A man took the package from outside a home on **Bootham Terrace** at 4.30pm on Friday, 6 November.

The suspect was wearing a black woolly hat, a royal blue coat, black bottoms and white trainers.

#### Bootham Terrace, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “In the footage we do have, we cannot clearly see his face, due to the way he angles himself.

“Do you have better footage of the suspect, possibly out on the street, or of him speculatively walking up your driveway?”

They ask any witnesses or people with dashcam footage to get in touch.
