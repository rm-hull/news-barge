## Our live feed has now finished.

* GPs, councils and businesses in York and North Yorkshire have been sent scrambling as a global Microsoft outage causes issues across the board.
* The outage has left millions without access to emails and Teams around the world.
* Some online systems at GPs in York and North Yorkshire are down on Friday due to the outage, with patients urged to phone practices instead.
* City of York and North Yorkshire councils have both been hit by the outage and say they are expecting an increase in calls due to the local authorities being unable to access emails.
* North Yorkshire Police was also hit by the outage, and is urging people to phone the force and report non-urgent crimes on its website instead of emailing.
