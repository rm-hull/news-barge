Mama Doreen’s Emporium in Harrogate is getting into the festive spirit with the launch of a brand new Winter Wonderland Afternoon Tea, bringing a taste of Christmas magic to the heart of the town.

The award-winning independent tea room has unveiled its seasonal afternoon tea experience, inviting visitors to enjoy a selection of Christmas-inspired savoury bites and sweet treats throughout the winter months.

The festive menu has been created with the bakery’s signature theatrical style, featuring a mix of traditional Christmas flavours and indulgent homemade treats.

Guests can tuck into savoury delights including a creamy parsnip soup with Bramley apple purée, a honey-glazed Cumberland pig in blanket, a Yorkshire pudding filled with chicken, sage stuffing, cranberry sauce and gravy, as well as a roast beef roll with caramelised onions.

The sweet selection brings even more festive cheer, with highlights including a Christmas stollen slice, a red velvet Yule log, a Black Forest trifle served in a china teacup, a gingerbread cupcake, a handmade mince pie and a chocolate pudding cookie.

The experience also includes unlimited tea or an Americano coffee, making it the perfect winter treat for friends, family gatherings or a festive day out in Harrogate.

Children can also join in the celebrations with their own festive afternoon tea, featuring sandwiches, vegetable sticks and crisps, Christmas treats and a sweetie bag to take home.

The Winter Wonderland Afternoon Tea is available during the festive season, with bookings now open until Thursday, December 31.

The experience is priced at £39.95 for adults and £17.50 for children.

With its colourful décor, creative menus and focus on homemade baking, Mama Doreen Emporium’s latest launch looks set to add an extra sprinkle of Christmas magic to Harrogate’s festive calendar.
