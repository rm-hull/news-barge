Harrogate Brewing Co has won Yorkshire’s top beer award for the second year in a row.

The family brewery’s Kursaal Imperial Stout, Special Edition was named Best Beer and Cider winner at the Deliciously Yorkshire Taste Awards 2026, announced on 4 November.

Now in their 22nd year, the awards are the biggest food and drink awards in the North of England, with hundreds of entries across 26 categories.

The winners were revealed at a black-tie ceremony at Pavilions of Harrogate, after judging in September led by head judge Nigel Barden.

Rosebud Preserves, from near Masham, also won Best Sweet Spread and Preserve with its Yorkshire Rhubarb and Ginger Jam.

## Why the stout won

The awards describe the winning beer as “Rich, dark and deliciously enticing, with notes of bonfire, sticky richness, Christmas cake and chocolate.”

“This is a beer to enjoy next to a roaring fire, and we just loved it!” the write-up adds.

“A very worthy winner!” the organisers said.

The organisers describe a family-run brewery whose beers “celebrate Harrogate’s heritage, landscape and community, with quality and craftsmanship at their heart”.

Last year the brewery won the same category, then called Best Beer, with its Harrogate Best Bitter, described as “complex and intriguing”.

“Following on from Harrogate Best Bitter winning the same accolade last year, we’re thrilled to have made it two years in a row,” the brewery said in its November news.

It said the award was “a real honour” and thanked the organisers.

## Where to drink Harrogate Brewing Co beer

The brewery is run by Joe and Julie Joyce with their daughter Martha, according to last year’s awards.

It brews at the Hookstone Centre on Hookstone Chase, where it runs a taproom, and also has The Old Bell Tavern on Royal Parade.

The taproom has a burger pop-up from God’s Own Grub on Friday 13 November, and pan-Asian street food from Little Somboon Box on Friday 20 and Saturday 21 November, the brewery says.

It also promises “a special new stout on the bar” this month, and a Christmas party on Thursday 17 December with festive food, a quiz and music.

At Royal Parade, a Christmas menu runs from 1 November to 31 January at £30 for two courses or £35 for three, booked in advance.

The brewery says it was also awarded silver in the Mild category of the Champion Beer of Britain last month.
