Police, the councils and GPs have issued updates today after a major Microsoft outage left millions without access to emails and Teams around the world.

The tech giant said it was working with “urgency ” to find a fix for the issue, which has caused widespread disruption since late last night.

A number of key agencies in York and North Yorkshire have been affected. We’ll keep updating this post with the latest.

## North Yorkshire Police

North Yorkshire Police said: “Due to a global Microsoft service issue, some of our systems may be affected today.

“Please visit our website for the latest updates, and thank you for your patience and understanding.

“Non-urgent enquiries can be submitted via our website.

“Our 101 and 999 systems are unaffected. In an emergency, always call 999.”

## North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service

The fire service said: “We are aware of reports of a Microsoft outage affecting some services nationally.

“We would like to reassure our communities that North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service’s emergency call handling and mobilising systems are unaffected.

“We continue to respond to emergency calls and mobilise our fire crews as normal.

“If you need us in an emergency, please continue to call 999.”

## York Hospital

York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust said: “We are experiencing some issues with our IT systems due to the global Microsoft issue.

“Patients should continue to attend for appointments and access urgent care services if needed. Use 999 in an emergency, and otherwise use 111, 111 online, or your local pharmacy.”

## City of York Council

City of York Council said: “We’re currently affected by the worldwide outage to Microsoft Outlook.

“This means we’re expecting more calls than normal which means it’ll take longer to answer enquiries. We’ve put more resource into dealing with calls, but bear with us.

“Still get information about all of our services online.”

## North Yorkshire Council

The council says: “A number of our online services are affected by the current global Microsoft issues and we are awaiting updates.

“If you have an urgent issue, please call us.”

## York Medical Group

The GP group says: “We would like to ensure patients that services are working as normal, and the Microsoft problems are not having an impact on appointments or online tools.

“You can request urgent and routine appointments online as usual, and any booked appointments for today are going ahead as usual (unless you have been directly contacted earlier).

“Our internal emailing system is down, but clinical systems are operational.”

## York College

The college said today: “Important notice: due to ongoing Microsoft service issues, you may experience a delay in hearing back from us. Please be assured that College is open today and all lessons are going ahead as planned. Thank you for your patience and understanding!”

## York St John University

The university said its Microsoft products were affected: “Please note teaching and learning activity on our campuses is taking place as planned.

“Students can log cases via the Student Hub portal, but will not receive email updates while servers are down. Please log into the Student Hub Portal, access your case list, and click on the specific case to see any updates.

“We will keep students and staff updated via the MyYSJ app, Moodle, staff intranet, and social media channels.”

## Clifton Park Hospital

It says: “Access to Outlook and other Microsoft platforms are impacted, which means emails and some online enquiries are currently not being received.

“Clinical services are operating as usual. Please attend as scheduled and follow the instructions you’ve already received.

“If you have questions about your care or procedure, please call your local Ramsay Hospital.”

## Tower Veterinary Group

It said: “Due to a technical issue with Microsoft, we are unable to access emails across our branches until further notice. If you have contacted any of our practices by e-mail, we request that you contact us by telephone for enquiries.”
