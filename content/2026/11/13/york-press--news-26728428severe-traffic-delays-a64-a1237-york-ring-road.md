MOTORISTS have been warned about ‘severe’ delays in two of York’s major roads.

Drivers using the York Ring Road (A1237) are currently experiencing major delays, which are increasing, around the city.

The AA Traffic News has described these delays as ‘severe’ – currently peaking at 13 minutes on the eastbound carriage, close to the exit for Huntington Road, and eight minutes westbound, near the Poppleton roundabout.
