Clean, complete and working toys, games, books and puzzles can be left at the Harrogate, West Harrogate and Ripon tips from 15 to 29 November. They go to families who need them.

Unwanted toys can be dropped off at the tips in Harrogate and Ripon from Sunday 15 November, to be passed on to families for Christmas.

The Reuse Santa appeal takes clean, complete and working toys, games, books and puzzles. Donations are accepted until Sunday 29 November at every household waste recycling centre in North Yorkshire and York, North Yorkshire Council announced on 12 November.

## Overview

**Take clean, complete and working toys, games, books and puzzles to any North Yorkshire tip between 15 and 29 November.** Toys powered by mains electricity are not accepted. The tips are shut on Wednesdays, and you must have registered your vehicle before you go.

## How the appeal works

This is the appeal’s eighth year. It is run by Yorwaste, the waste company, with North Yorkshire Council, City of York Council and local charities and community groups.

More than 5,000 toys were passed on last year, the council said. That is about 80 giant Santa sacks.

The toys are handed out through partners including:

* North Yorkshire Council’s Early Help service
* community groups in the City of York Good Place Network
* Colburn Community Hub
* the British Heart Foundation
* the Daisy Chain Project
* The Recycle Project CIC

Some items may go to charity shops.

Cllr Richard Foster, the council’s executive member for waste services, said: “Christmas can be a time when finances are tight, so the aim of the scheme is to relieve some of the burden.”

He added: “All we ask is that the gifts are in a condition that you would like to receive, and please remember that we can’t accept toys powered by mains electricity.”

## Where to take them in the Harrogate area

Three tips serve Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon:

| Tip | Address |
| --- | --- |
| Harrogate | Wetherby Road, HG3 1DE |
| West Harrogate | Penny Pot Lane, HG3 2SN |
| Ripon | Dallamires Lane, HG4 1TT |

From October to March the tips open from 8.30am to 4pm. They are closed every Wednesday, according to the council’s own site pages.

## What it means for you

* **Check the toy works and nothing is missing** before you bag it. Puzzles need all their pieces and games need their parts.
* **Leave out anything with a mains plug.** The council says it cannot accept toys powered by mains electricity.
* **Avoid Wednesday 18 and Wednesday 25 November**, when the tips are shut. The last day to donate is Sunday 29 November.
* **Register your vehicle first** if you have not used a tip since April. Registration is free and has been compulsory at every North Yorkshire tip since 27 April 2026. It is done on the council’s registration page.
