# DWP Universal Credit, PIP and Attendance Allowance changes for claimants

## Pension Credit and Carer's Allowance are affected too

Recipients of Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Carer's Allowance, Attendance Allowance and Pension Credit may be required to notify the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) about certain changes to their circumstances under the Labour government.

Benefit claimants may need to inform authorities about shifts in their personal situation to prevent overpayments, ensure they receive proper support, or avoid complications with subsequent claims. Both the DWP and HM Revenue and Customs require claimants to maintain current information whenever particular aspects of their lives alter.

Certain changes might boost benefit entitlement, while others could diminish payments or halt support entirely. Citizens Advice cautions that overpayments can occur even when errors are unintentional, and individuals may still face requests to return funds if they've been paid excessive amounts.

Citizens Advice guidance recommends people notify authorities about changes without delay and retain copies of correspondence, emails or digital confirmations wherever accessible, reports Birmingham Live.

### Universal Credit

Universal Credit recipients are expected to notify authorities about changes including:

* moving home
* rent increases or decreases
* changes to working hours or earnings
* starting or stopping work
* relationship changes
* childcare cost changes
* savings rising above benefit thresholds

### Carer's Allowance

Those in receipt of Carer's Allowance may need to declare:

* earnings going above the weekly limit
* changes to working hours
* education or training changes
* the person they care for no longer qualifying
* hospital or care home stays
* stopping caring for at least 35 hours a week

Individuals claiming Personal Independence Payment (PIP) may be required to report:

* changes to their health condition
* mobility improvements or worsening symptoms
* hospital stays
* moving into a care home
* going abroad for a long period

### Attendance Allowance

Those receiving Attendance Allowance may be required to report:

* hospital stays
* moving into a care home funded by the local authority
* changes to care needs
* periods spent abroad

### Pension Credit

Those claiming Pension Credit are expected to report:

* changes to pension income
* savings increases
* someone moving in or out of the household
* housing cost changes
* time spent abroad
