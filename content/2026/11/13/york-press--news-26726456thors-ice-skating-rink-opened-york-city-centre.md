## First ice rink in 16 years opens in York city centre - take a look inside

Our reporter Alice clinging onto the side of the rink *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

**A FESTIVE ice rink - complete with snowfall – has returned to York city centre** Thor's on Ice opened its doors on Thursday (November 12) – bringing the first ice rink to the city centre in over a decade and promising visitors a "full winter wonderland experience" in the heart of the city.**As reported by The Press in July**, Thor's pop-up ice rink - which is the first time that York city centre has had skating since 2010 - has been built on a paved patio at the back of the Art Gallery in the city's Museum Gardens.

Running until January 3, the event is centred around the ice rink which offers 45-minute skating sessions for adults and children – priced at £12-14 for adults, £11-13 for children, and £10-12 for students, service personnel, and senior citizens.

There are two rink-side stalls: one that serves a range of drinks from Lucia (including mulled wine, cocktails, champagne, coffee, and soft drinks) and the other offering Chinese ‘spice bags’ with chicken, halloumi, or vegan chicken, and fruit crumbles.

Above the rink is Lucia’s Après Bar – located on the balcony of the art gallery. It serves the same drinks as the downstairs stall, but in a more luxurious setting with heaters, blankets, and table-side service.

There is also a photobooth (£6 per use) and a marshmallow toasting station, which was not set up and did not have prices available at the time I visited for The Press.

Arriving at around 5.30pm, my housemate and I exploring the surroundings of the ice rink, before getting our skates fitted and handing over our bags for safe keeping ahead of the next session on the ice.

Moments later - and with all the grace of a reversing dump truck without any tires on - I just about made it onto the ice in one piece and with, one hand firmly planted on the side, I did my best to remain upright as I moved around the rink at a pace just as glacial as the weather in York.

![Our reporter Alice clinging onto the side of the rink](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21714199.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Our reporter Alice clinging onto the side of the rink *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Thankfully, assistance came in floral form as one of the ice dancers – who were entertaining patrons in the centre of the rink - came over to see me and offered a few words of advice to give me the confidence to push away from the side.

It would be a stretch to say I was Jayne Torvill by the end of the 45 minute slot, but I did manage a few laps without stopping or touching the side (which I counted as a huge personal win). I also managed to somehow not fall over once.

For the more advanced skaters (like my housemate), there was enough room towards the centre of the ice to twirl, speed, and move freely away from the slower skaters like me.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21714206.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Our reporter trying her best... *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Towards the end of the session, the rink did get busier, but the numbers were kept under control and you never worried about hitting into another person as you did your laps.

The only way to finish the night was with a glass of champagne (£16) in comfort of the Après Bar where, from my vantage point and curled up under a blanket, I watched the next bunch of skaters take to the ice.

Speaking about the opening last month, Amanda Monaghan, director at Thor's tipi, said: "Partnering with Lucia brings another brilliant York experience to Thor's on Ice

"The team shares our enthusiasm for creating places where people can come together and celebrate, and their signature cocktails and festive drinks will add something really special to the rink.

"It’s a wonderful way to bring two York favourites together and make a festive visit to the city even more memorable."
