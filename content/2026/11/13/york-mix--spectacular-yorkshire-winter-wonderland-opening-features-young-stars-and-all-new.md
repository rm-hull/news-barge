Celebrating its milestone 21st season in York, Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland is back with an array of new festive touches and colourful surprises.

The award-winning open-air ice rink, funfair, rink-side café and Santa’s Cabin provides a fun-filled winter experience at Designer Outlet York from today (Friday, 13 November) – and no end of photo opportunities for all the family.

At the Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland lights switch-on last night, 300 invited guests were treated to a spectacular new light display as a backdrop to the ice rink, featuring giant trees edged with ever-changing colourful neon ropes, as well as glowing Christmas bauble trees and dancing circles of light, creating a stunning setting for skating under the stars.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-4.webp)

The illuminations have been designed by the team behind Aglow! at Bishop Auckland, the leading Christmas light trail in the North.

Hosted by ***YorkMix***, the Preview Evening guests were welcomed by costume character Mixie and presenters Victoria Charles and Tim Lichfield.

Led by Town Crier Ben Fry, the assembled guests and 150 competition winners counted down with excitement and applauded ‘Young Star’ and birthday girl six-year-old Matilda Hansell from York as she switched on the 30ft high Christmas tree in the centre of the rink and the magical light display.

Opening the ice rink for 2026 was professional skater Amy Morris, performing a spectacular routine with world-class style.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-13.webp)

Hailing from Manchester, Amy has been dazzling audiences around the world performing with Royal Caribbean Cruise Line, and wowed the crowd of onlookers with her skills and style.

The other ‘Young Stars’ of the evening were skating sisters Ellie and Nora Needham from Sheffield, aged 13 and eight respectively.

Combining grace and feistiness as they sped around the giant rink, they inspired youngsters and adults alike with their easy confidence on the ice.

Both girls have been skating since the age of five, and compete in British Ice-Skating competitions all around the country.

## The North’s largest rink

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-2.webp)

Guests were then invited to go for a glide themselves, enjoy the fun of the fair and explore Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland.

Attracting visitors from far and wide, Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland has become firmly established as one of the top ice rinks in the country, celebrating over one million skaters since its debut in York two decades ago.

At the heart of the event is the North of England’s largest outdoor ice rink at 975 sq m, with its giant Christmas tree as the centrepiece.

#### Booking information

Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland is located at the South Entrance of York Designer Outlet.

Tickets for a 45-minute skating session are priced at £16.45 for adults and £15.45 for children and concessions.

Family packages are available for families of three, four or five. Tickets for parents and toddlers aged 4 and under are priced at £16.45.

Special packages are also available for parties, schools, clubs and youth groups, with discounts applying to groups of 20 or more.

Autism/quiet skating sessions are on Tuesdays at 12.15am until 8 December, and Sundays 10.15am until 13 December, in line with the Designer Outlet’s Autism Friendly Hours.

For those wishing to make the most of the season, a Frequent Skater Pass enables skaters to collect a stamp from the box office over five sessions and enjoy the sixth visit for free.

The rink is flanked by the Chalet café-bar and a spacious heated skate hire marquee, decorated with vintage alpine photographs and posters as well as a cow-bell or two to set the scene.

With this year’s new and spectacular illuminated backdrop, the event lives up to its reputation as what the *Sunday Times* called one of the most ‘fabulously festive and magical’ ice rinks in the country.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-1.webp)

The extra-thick rink utilises the latest ice technology to provide a premium skating experience, and is operated by the most experienced team in the north.

It is open every day except Christmas Day until 3 January, offering eleven 45-minute skating sessions during the day.

## Alpine makeover

New features of the event this year include a re-design of the rink-side café and bar, The Chalet.

Its makeover includes a wooden façade with red-shuttered windows creating an authentic Alpine scene.

Inside, cosy curtained windows and a fireplace, together with sheepskin-covered stools around coffee tables create the perfect place to warm up before or after a skating session, or for non-skaters to relax and watch all the action.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-12.webp)

The fully-licensed Chalet serves an all-day menu of winter-warming food and sweet treats, hot and cold drinks, as well as hot chocolate and seasonal favourites such as mulled wine, mulled cider and prosecco.

As evening draws in, the lighting dims and the vibe eases into an après-skate bar. In front of the fireplace a variety of live music acts will perform on the stage during the seven-week season.

Theme nights include Fizz and Fondue, Acoustic and Aperol, and Charcuterie and Cocktails. Private parties can also be arranged, for skaters and non-skaters alike.

Outside, specially constructed fire pits will offer marshmallows to toast, and the outdoor grill ‘Fed From Fire’ will serve up gourmet bratwurst, Yorkshire Pudding wraps, loaded roast potatoes and hot chocolate.

Individual wooden picnic cabins decorated with festive touches make the ideal spot to enjoy a bite and take in the snow-sprinkled Enchanted Woodland with its illuminated gingerbread men.

## Speedsters and thrill-seekers

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-3.webp)

The funfair offers fun for all ages. For speedsters and thrill-seekers there’s the ever-popular Waltzer, a Miami as well as a ‘twist-and-scream’ Twister.

Making a welcome return is everybody’s favourite ride – the Brooklands Dodgems dating from 1937, a vintage masterpiece from the golden era of fairgrounds.

Younger visitors will be enthralled by three children’s rides, including the Jets, the beautiful vintage Muffin the Mule Autodrome and Toy Set. Plus there’s the Hook-a-Duck game stall offering a prize every time, so everyone goes home happy!

## Santa’s here

Christmas wouldn’t be the same without a visit to Santa and his elves. Families can soak up the festive atmosphere as they follow a sparkling trail of twinkling lights and snow-covered Christmas trees on their way to Santa’s Cabin.

After meeting Santa and receiving a special gift, visitors can also support the annual Toy Appeal, helping bring Christmas magic to local children and families living in need across York and Selby.

And with free parking, Designer Outlet York is the ideal destination for Christmas shopping. From seasonal fashion and sportswear to designer accessories, beauty and homeware, visitors can discover gifts for everyone on their list.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yorkshires-winter-wonderland-ice-rink-opening-york-2026-12.webp)

**Designer Outlet York manager Paul Tyler** said: “Christmas at York Designer Outlet just wouldn’t be the same without Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland.

“For many families, the event has become a cherished fixture in their festive calendar, offering fun outdoor activities for all ages to enjoy.

“We love seeing the joy it brings, and it truly helps spread that festive cheer while people are also able to tick off their Christmas shopping list!

“The ice rink has become a real pillar of the community, bringing together families, couples, and individuals during the festive season to create lasting memories.”

**Jennifer Rojko, event organiser of Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland** added: “The whole team at Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland is excited to be bringing York’s favourite Christmas event back to the Designer Outlet and unveiling our new ideas and festive touches.

“This season will be as colourful as ever, and our new look Chalet with its live music and theme nights will bring a new buzz to the event.

“Not only do we offer great winter exercise and fun for all the family, but with free parking and superb shopping Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland makes a great day – and evening – out!”
