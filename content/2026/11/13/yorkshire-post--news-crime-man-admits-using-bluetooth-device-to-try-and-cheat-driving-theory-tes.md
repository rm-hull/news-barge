# Man admits using Bluetooth device to try and cheat driving theory test

## A man has admitted using a Bluetooth device to try and cheat on his driving theory test.

Miklovan Shahinaj appeared at Bradford and Keighley Magistrates Court on Wednesday, charged with one count of possessing an article for use in fraud.

The case related to an incident at the Bradford Theory Test Centre in April 2025.

Speaking through an Albanian interpreter, Shahinaj pleaded guilty, and he will now be sentenced in March.

![A man has admitted using a Bluetooth device to try and cheat on his driving theory test.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjFhNmE3ZTFmLWQ4MTMtNGQ5MC1iYmIwLThkM2JlZjlhMjVkNDo4Mjk1MDgxZS04MmIxLTQwNzAtOWI3Mi03NjliYmJlYTRiODM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The case is being prosecuted by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency – the Government body responsible for driving tests.

The court heard that Shahinaj, 31 of Radulf Gardens, Liversedge, attended the test centre in Upper Piccadilly on April 7, 2025 to take his theory test.

Magistrates were told that the defendant was seen to be inserting a Bluetooth device into a headset he was using as part of the test “with the intention of someone else assisting with the test.”

Both prosecution and defence agreed that a pre sentence report should be written before Shahinaj is sentenced.

He was bailed until his sentencing date on March 8 2027.
