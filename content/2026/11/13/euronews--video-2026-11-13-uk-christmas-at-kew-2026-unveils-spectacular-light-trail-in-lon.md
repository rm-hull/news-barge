**Over a million twinkling bulbs lit up Kew Gardens** on Thursday 12 November as the botanical landmark previewed its three-kilometre winter trail, showcasing**24 light displays and six global debuts**.

This year's centrepiece is **The Electric Light Symphony**, a spectacular finale outside the Victorian Palm House, where towering** jets of water dance beneath colourful lasers and projections**, creating what organisers describe as the** UK's largest water fountain show**.

Elsewhere, guests can wander through the returning **Fire Garden**, where real flames dance beside glowing sculptures, and the** Star and Snowflake Gobo Walk**, where artificial snow drifts over a lit pathway. The historic** Temperate House** serves as a backdrop for the audiovisual piece *New Life, New Love, New Dawn*, complemented by luminous flora, radiant fungi and kaleidoscopic tunnels.

Among the highlights is **Light Rain** by ITHACA Studio, which transforms**Kew's Turkey oak tree into a shimmering waterfall of lights** suspended from its branches. Other installations include the illuminated Christmas Cathedral, the colourful Bubble Walk and Where Flowers Wait, alongside a traditional fairground with a vintage carousel.

Curators note that the latest edition sets sweeping light installations against the gardens' prized trees and historic structures. **The trail opens on 13 November and continues through to 3 January 2027**, featuring sensory-friendly sessions and over-18s evenings on specific dates. Widely regarded as a flagship London festive event, it draws thousands after sunset every night.
