The Inn South Stainley near Harrogate has won a 2026 Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice award.

The award places the inn among the top 10% of places listed on Tripadvisor worldwide, based on reviews from guests.

Announcing the news, the inn thanked guests who had shared their experiences, from a Sunday roast or a night in the Lodge to a visit with their dog.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/66f116f0194d3.jpg)

The inn is in the village of South Stainley, between Harrogate and Ripon, and offers overnight stays in its Lodge as well as Sunday roasts.

Graham Usher and Matthew Rose have owned the venue since 2021.

The pair said:

> “We're chuffed to bits. That's down to every one of you who's left a few kind words after a Sunday roast, a quiet night in the Lodge or a muddy-pawed visit with the dog.
>
> "And it's down to Shane, Jo and the whole team, who make it happen every day."

First held in 2002, the annual Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice Awards recognise places that consistently earn positive reviews from visitors over a 12-month period.
