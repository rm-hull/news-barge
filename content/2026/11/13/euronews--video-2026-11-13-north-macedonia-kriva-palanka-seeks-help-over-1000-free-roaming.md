Recent footage shows **cattle wandering through traffic, grazing in public parks and moving between houses**, sometimes after dark. Local authorities in the north-eastern municipality of** Kriva Palanka say the animals have caused extensive damage to farmland and municipal property**, while residents fear unexpected encounters with the large livestock.

Mayor Saško Mitovski has **asked the government to declare a 30-day state of crisis**, warning that the situation has steadily deteriorated. According to the mayor, the** number of roaming cattle has risen from around 400 five years ago to more than 1,000 today**.

Many of the animals **previously belonged to a single family**, whose members were detained after allegedly obstructing an attempt by the Food and Veterinary Agency to confiscate the cattle. However, the animals were reportedly** not placed in adequate shelters** following the confiscation and have since continued roaming across the municipality.

Residents describe the situation as **frightening**, saying the** cattle appear unexpectedly**throughout the town. Municipal officials say they** lack the resources and facilities needed to manage such a large herd**. Mayor Mitovski hopes a** temporary state of crisis**would allow local and national authorities to coordinate resources, implement daily measures and find a** lasting solution to the problem**.
