York City travel to Rochdale tomorrow (Saturday) for their Sky Bet League Two fixture, with kick-off at 3pm.

The Minstermen are looking to record their first away victory of the 2026/27 season.

The game takes York back to a ground that holds special memories. Last season they went to Rochdale needing a single point to clinch top spot and promotion, in a winner-takes-all clash in which Rochdale were also title contenders.

Mani Dieseruvwe put the home side ahead late on, but York had one final chance and Josh Stones forced the ball over the line in the 103rd minute to send York back into the Football League. Rochdale went on to win the play-offs and joined York in the EFL.

Rochdale have struggled to get going since returning to League Two, losing four of their first five games. They sit 20th in the table with four wins to their name.

They needed extra time to beat Accrington Stanley in the Emirates FA Cup, and their latest league game was a 4-2 defeat at Port Vale two weeks ago.

York went out of the Jersey Mike’s Trophy after a defeat at Bradford City. Before that, they beat Northampton Town 4-0 in the FA Cup.

In all-time head-to-head meetings, York just edge Rochdale, with 51 wins to Rochdale’s 46.

Tickets for the game are sold out, with 1,800 York fans set to attend.
