Today, Andy Burnham’s been on a trip to COP31 in Turkey and the 9th European Political Community Summit in Ireland. With the latest preview of Alan Milburn’s report into Britain’s nearly one million Neets, under-25s who are not in education, employment or training, the team discuss whether Andy Burnham has done enough to avoid a rebellion against welfare reforms. And, what do his remarks in Dublin ahead of next week’s UK-EU summit reveal about his strategy? Plus, Gary delves into the result of the midterms and what the Republicans might do next.
