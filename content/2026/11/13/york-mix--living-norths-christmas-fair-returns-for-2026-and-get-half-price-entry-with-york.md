Living North’s Yorkshire Christmas Fair is back!

If you’re hoping to make a dent in your Christmas shopping – or looking for a festive treat for yourself – then you don’t want to miss the Living North Christmas Fair at York Racecourse from **Thursday 19 to Sunday 22 November**.

And **YorkMix** have half price entry vouchers for two into the Living North Christmas Fair – meaning you can spend more on those Christmas gifts…

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/image-2700px-h-x-550px-w.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/image-3600px-h-x-550px-w-1.webp)

**YorkMix**‘s Victoria Charles will be broadcasting live from the event on Thursday, so if you see her make sure to say hello!

There will be more than 200 carefully-selected stands, from artisan designer-makers, local food producers and fashion retailers to homewares, gifts and accessories, and of course special Christmas decorations.

There will be a variety of catering on site too as well as demonstrations from renowned chefs.

The line-up for this year includes *GBBO*contestant Freya Cox, Kevin Bonello from The Grand York, Chris Hobson from Kin Café, Pastry Chef & Chocolatier Ben Ellis, Stephanie Moon from All Things Food, and* MasterChef* quarter finalist Owen Diaram.

This year, the event is supporting Yorkshire Children’s Charity, who work across Yorkshire to make things better for children facing challenges caused by ill health, disability or financial hardship.

To find out more about Living North Christmas Fair in Yorkshire, visit their website here.

Full price tickets will be available on the door for all four days priced at £9, but you can gain entry for half price for two people with ***YorkMix***!

#### Christmas Fair Yorkshire 2026

York Racecourse, York YO23 1EX

York Racecourse is accessible from the A1036 Tadcaster Road at the Kanvesmire Road entrance.

AMPLE FREE PARKING is available on the road and directly beyond the main grandstand entrances by following the Living North Car Park signs.
