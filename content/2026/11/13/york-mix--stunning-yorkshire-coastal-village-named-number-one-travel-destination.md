Councillors and businesses have shared their joy and surprise at the small village of Skipsea being named as an on-trend travel destination.

The East Riding coastal village has been named as the place with the fastest growing rise in internet searches for 2027, according to new data.

The study was completed by travel site Booking.com, which has named Skipsea as a stand-out destination in its annual travel predictions.

The East Yorkshire village stands alongside premium destinations such as Christchurch in New Zealand, Bratislava in Slovakia and Pescara in Italy in the research.

UK travellers are increasingly searching for Skipsea in East Yorkshire, with the destination seeing a growth of +218%. This makes it the number one place in the whole of the UK for an increase in searches by holidaymakers.

Ryan Pearson, UK and Ireland regional manager at Booking.com, said: “Our latest travel predictions reveal that Brits are increasingly looking beyond the usual holiday hotspots, with searches for Skipsea in East Yorkshire rising by an impressive 218% year-on-year, making it the top trending domestic destination for UK travellers in 2027.

“With its beautiful coastline, scenic countryside and charming seaside towns, Yorkshire has plenty to offer. This rise in searches highlights a growing interest in discovering lesser-known towns and more unexpected holiday destinations.”

The news that Skipsea is one of the top hotspots has baffled some local people. But many councillors are not at all surprised that online attention is now shifting to the undiscovered and underappreciated beauty of the quiet East Yorkshire coast.

The blend of tradition, natural beauty and peaceful environment means it is a destination that is only going to become more popular as people look to take a break from their busy lives, according to Cllr Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding Council.

“It’s no surprise to see Skipsea growing in popularity,” he said. “With its stunning coastal scenery and access to local attractions, it’s one of many East Riding destinations offering visitors a unique and relaxing experience along our wonderful coastline.

“Whether it’s exploring nearby nature reserves, enjoying traditional seaside towns or taking in the area’s beautiful landscapes and wildlife, this part of the East Riding has a lot to offer.”

## Summer was ‘ridiculously busy’

Victoria Kirby, who runs the Samara Holiday Park in Skipsea, said social media users and TikTok were fuelling interest in the village and its coastline.

Mrs Kirby explained. “The past summer was ridiculously busy,” she said. “I think people come here because it is so quiet.

“There is no phone reception, people come here to log off and I think it boosts their mental health. We have the beach, the countryside – people just come here and reset. They walk into Skipsea, go to the pub and just sit and read next to their caravans.”

A spokesperson for tourist organisation Visit Hull and East Yorkshire said: “We’re pleased to see Skipsea receiving recognition! It does offer visitors a quieter alternative to some of the nearby coastal destinations, while still being within easy reach of everything East Yorkshire has to offer.

“It’s a great base for walking and cycling, with easy access to the coast, the Yorkshire Wolds, nature reserves, and neighbouring towns such as Hornsea, Bridlington and Withernsea.”

The study was conducted through Booking.com searches made by UK travellers between January 1st and August 31st, 2026, compared year-on-year, for stays between December 1st, 2026 and September 30th, 2027.
