# Duo wanted after robbery, assault and thefts in Leeds

## Police are searching for Kris Sanderson and Matthew Wheeldon

A pair of men are wanted after robbery, assault and thefts in Leeds.

Kris Sanderson, 38, is wanted on suspicion of a number of offences including theft from a shop, robbery and assault. Matthew Wheeldon, 35, is wanted on suspicion of more than 20 theft from shop offences and other offences including robbery, assault and theft of motor vehicle.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "A number of enquiries have already been carried out to try and locate Wheeldon and Sanderson and neighbourhood officers are now asking for the public’s assistance in reporting any sightings or other information that could assist them.

"Information can be passed to Leeds West Neighbourhood Policing Team [online] or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260509048 (Wheeldon), or 13260506795 (Sanderson)."
