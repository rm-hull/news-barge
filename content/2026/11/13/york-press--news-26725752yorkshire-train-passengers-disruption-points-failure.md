York train passengers are facing disruption after a points failure resulted in services being cancelled and delayed.

Passengers travelling to stations between Leeds and York via Harrogate are facing disruption due to the issue on Friday morning (November 13).

National Rail said the points failure at Harrogate has since been fixed and all lines have reopened, but disruption is expected until 8.30am.

The company said services between Leeds and York via Harrogate “may still be affected by this disruption”.

“Train services are now able to run between Harrogate and York in both directions,” a National Rail spokesperson said.

They said Northern services between Leeds and York via Micklefield are not affected.

Northern passengers affected by the disruption can travel on the next Northern service to their destination, National Rail said.

The incident comes after lines in West Yorkshire reopened on Thursday evening after a person died on the tracks near Wakefield.

Passengers travelling from Manchester to York faced delays and cancellations following the incident.

TransPennine Express said all lines have since reopened with services running as normal by Friday morning.

British Transport Police said a person was pronounced dead at the scene after its officers were called to the tracks near Wakefield shortly after 2.50pm on Thursday.

"The incident is not being treated as suspicious and a file will be prepared for the coroner,” a force spokesperson said.
