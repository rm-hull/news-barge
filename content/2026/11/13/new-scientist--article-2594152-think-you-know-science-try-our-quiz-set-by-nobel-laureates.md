For *New Scientist’s*70th anniversary, we asked three Nobel laureates to challenge our readers in a quiz about their prize-winning discoveries. The question setters are:**Barry Marshall** at the University of Western Australia, winner of the Nobel prize in physiology or medicine in 2005 for discovering the role of *Helicobacter pylori*bacteria in gastritis and peptic ulcer disease.**Jennifer Doudna**, founder of the Innovative Genomics Institute in Berkeley, California, who won the Nobel prize in chemistry in 2020 for the development of CRISPR gene editing.

**Saul Perlmutter** at the University of California, Berkeley, who won the 2011 Nobel prize in physics for measuring the expansion of the universe through observations of distant supernovae.
