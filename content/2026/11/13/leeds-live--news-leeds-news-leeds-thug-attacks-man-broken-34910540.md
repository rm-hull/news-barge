# Leeds thug attacks man with broken fence after stealing e-bike

## Danny Bucknall carried out a sustained attack on his victim

A Leeds thug attacked a man with a broken fence after stealing his E-bike.

Danny Bucknall, 44, stole the bike on Tong Street, Wortley on Monday, August 31. The victim had left his bike outside of a shop momentarily to buy a drink and on the CCTV screen within the shop, he saw Bucknall approaching his bike and riding away on it.

He chased Bucknall and caught up with him on Bruce Lawn. Bucknall briefly stopped allowing the victim to catch up and try and get his bike back.

However, Bucknall then carried out a sustained attack on the victim by punching and kicking him. The victim fell into a fence and snapped it as a result. Bucknall then picked up a broken piece of wood and used it to hit the victim.

Detective Sergeant Seb Horan, of the Leeds District Crime Team, said: “The level of violence displayed by Bucknall was wholly unacceptable and we welcome the sentence he has received. I hope that this sentence provides some reassurance to the victim as well as the wider community.”

Bucknall, of Saxon Mount, pleaded guilty. Appearing at Leeds Crown Court yesterday, on Thursday, November 12, he was jailed for four years and six months.
