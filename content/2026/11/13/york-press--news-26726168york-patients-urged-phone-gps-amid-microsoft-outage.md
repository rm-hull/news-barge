York patients are being urged to phone GP surgeries as a global Microsoft outage means staff are unable to access online platforms.

Priory Medical Group, which operates eight surgeries in the region, including practices in Fulford, Heworth and Acomb, said it is unable to access its prioryCARE platform on Friday morning (November 13).

“If you have an urgent problem, please call the surgery on 01904 404100 or pop in to one of our reception sites to speak with a member of the team," a group spokesperson said.

"If your query is non-urgent, please consider contacting us another day."

Microsoft said it is seeing “early signs of stabilisation” but has not yet been able to fully restore services after a global outage left millions without access to emails and Teams around the world.

The tech giant said it was working with “urgency” to find a fix for the issue, which has caused widespread disruption since late Thursday, with governments, banks, police forces and health services among those hit.

On its Microsoft 365 status page, the firm said: “We are seeing early signs of stabilisation, but service capacity has not yet returned to normal levels and health remains variable as traffic is carefully reintroduced.”

A UK government spokesperson said: “We are working closely with Microsoft to assess any impact on government services, with robust contingency plans in place to minimise disruption.”

The issue began late on Thursday and users have been unable to access many of its most popular services, with thousands worldwide reporting they were unable to send and receive emails through Outlook.

Teams has also been affected for many, and the tech giant’s Copilot AI assistant and other Microsoft 365 services are also disrupted.

Police forces have said they are unable to receive crime reports online and are urging people to call either 101 or 999 to make any urgent reports.
