A man who carried out an attack on a homeless person that police described as ‘violent in the extreme’ has been jailed.

Christian Watson kicked and stamped on the victim in the horrific attack.

Watson’s co-accused, Fallon Johnson, was given a community order for her part in the incident.

#### Falconers Road, Scarborough

It happened on Falconers Road in Scarborough at around 1.30am on 3 July 2025.

Five minutes after the 51-year-old victim had been sitting down and chatting with Watson and Johnson, they turned on him.

Johnson began the assault by kicking the victim in the face several times. Watson then took over, and at one point, stood on a ledge to get more height and then jumped off onto the victim.

The victim was kicked and stamped on more than 20 times.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/falconers-road-scarborough-gsv.webp)

The attack lasted almost three minutes with Watson going back several times to continue the assault after being pulled away by Johnson.

The victim was rushed to hospital by ambulance for emergency treatment. He suffered a bleed on the brain, several fractures to his face and sternum, along with severe bruising to his face and body.

Police worked closely with the council’s CCTV operators to analyse the footage. They then tracked the defendants as they made their way to a Scarborough hotel.

Officers arrested the pair only hours after the harrowing incident. Both Johnson and Watson were still wearing the blood-stained clothing and footwear worn during the assault.

In his first police interview, Watson tried to blame the victim and justified the attack as self-defence. He claimed the victim had tried to rob Johnson, then the victim threatened him and started pushing him.

Watson was then shown the CCTV footage. It clearly showed the victim had not stood up at any point and had not displayed any intention of violence.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/york-crown-court-summer-2024-2.webp)

Watson, 24, from Abbeydale Grove in Kirkstall, Leeds, pleaded guilty to committing grievous bodily harm with intent.

He was jailed for four years and eight months at York Crown Court today (Friday).

As well as the community order, Johnson, who’s 20 and from Bloomfield Road in Blackpool, must undertake mental health treatment for six months.

Detective Constable Sam Rivers from Scarborough and Ryedale CID, said: “This unprovoked assault was violent in the extreme, and one that could have only been carried out to cause devastating injuries.

“It is one of the most violent assaults I have ever had to view on CCTV.

“Excellent CCTV work and the forensic evidence recovered from the defendants’ clothing and footwear were the backbone of this investigation.

“I am pleased we’ve been able to bring Watson and Johnston to justice for this brutal and sustained attack.

“I hope the victim can now move on with his life following this ordeal.”
