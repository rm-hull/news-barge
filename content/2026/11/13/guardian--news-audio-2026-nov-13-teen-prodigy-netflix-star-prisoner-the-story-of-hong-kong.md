He has been leading protests since he was 14. Joshua Wong has been on the cover of Time magazine, the focus of a Netflix documentary and an icon for Hong Kong’s pro-democracy movement.

Wong has been leading the fight against a Chinese squeeze on freedoms in Hong Kong for the last 15 years. He will now spend more than 16 years in prison.

As **Danny Vincent** explains, the 30-year-old was sentenced last week to a further 10 years in prison, convicted under the island’s draconian national security law.**Helen Pidd** also hears from Joshua’s friend – and fellow movement leader –**Nathan Law** about the prospects for democracy in Hong Kong.
