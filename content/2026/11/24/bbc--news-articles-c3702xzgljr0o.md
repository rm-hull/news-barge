**A man has appeared in court charged with the murder of a missing 20-year-old woman in Dumfries and Galloway.**

Kelsey Hunter was last seen in Castle Douglas at 17:00 on Saturday. A major search is under way in the nearby village of Springholm where she lives.

Daniel McKnight, 21, has appeared on petition in Dumfries Sheriff Court charged with murder and two other charges, including defeating the ends of justice.

He made no plea and was remanded in custody.

![Three officers in white forensic suits next to a police van. They are next to a bus stop and a road leading out to a rural area. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/96ec/live/5b026f40-e82b-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

Police officers in forensic suits are among those in Springholm

Neighbours have said there has been a large police presence in Springholm since Sunday evening.

Dogs, a helicopter and specialist officers have been involved in the search for Kelsey while officers made door-to-door inquiries in the village.

On Tuesday a yellow police tent was seen in the entrance to a field yards from her home and officers in white forensic suits were spotted in the area.

![Map showing Castle Douglas, Springholm and Dumfries](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/0467/live/f2e46190-e805-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.png)

Earlier police said they were doing everything they could to find the missing woman but that it was possible she "may have come to harm".

Det Ch Insp Stephen McGrath said: "Kelsey's family are understandably extremely worried about her and they are being supported by specialist officers at this difficult time.

"We have a dedicated team of officers who are working around the clock to find her and get answers for them.

"While we are keeping an open mind, and continue to hope Kelsey will be found safe and well, we have to consider the possibility that she may have to come to harm."

![A young woman taking a selfie in a mirror, with white corded headphones in her ears. She has shoulder-length red hair. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/320/cpsprodpb/3f52/live/9ea9bde0-e82e-11f1-917e-35f1674fb52c.jpg)

Kelsey Hunter's family are being supported by specialist officers

Det Ch Insp McGrath said they were trying to build up a picture of her life in the days and weeks prior to her being reported missing and appealed for any friends with information to contact them.

"It is absolutely vital that we find Kelsey as soon as possible and I would again ask anyone with information – no matter how small or insignificant it may seem – to contact us," he said.

Police are expected to remain in the area over the coming days.

Local area commander Emma Dodds said: "Our priority remains tracing Kelsey and getting answers for her family about her whereabouts.

"We have a dedicated team, including specialist officers and national resources, following all lines of inquiry."

She added: "I understand this will be concerning for the local community, but please be assured we are doing all we can to find Kelsey.

"We will remain in the area while our investigation continues.

"Anyone with any concerns, or information, can approach these officers – we are here to help."

Kelsey is described as 5ft 4in tall, of slight build with dyed blonde/pink hair. When she was last seen she was wearing a brown jacket and brown boots and was carrying a chequered bag.

Officers have been carrying out door-to-door inquiries and requesting doorbell camera footage from neighbours.

They have appealed for anyone with information about her whereabouts to contact them on 101, external.
