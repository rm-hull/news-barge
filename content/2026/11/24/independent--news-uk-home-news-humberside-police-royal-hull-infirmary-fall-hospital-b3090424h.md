Humberside Police is investigating the death of a man who fell from an 11th-floor window at a hospital in Hull this year.

The force said it had been conducting “extensive” enquiries after a 64-year-old man fell from a block at Hull Royal Infirmary on 24 August.

His death is not being treated as suspicious, but police are urging anyone with information to come forward.

Detective Inspector Lloyd Sharkett said: “Following the report, officers were deployed and a 64-year-old man was sadly found deceased.

“We have a dedicated team conducting extensive enquiries, some of which have been complex and required additional time to complete, and I can confirm at this time we believe his death to be non-suspicious.

“Enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances around the report and the lead-up to the man’s death.”

![Police said they are conducting ‘extensive’ enquiries](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/13/11/af15e825fc03b69ab6aba89b5704bb92Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg0MDIzMzU1-2.21314942.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The man reportedly fell from a tower block where neurology and stroke patients are usually cared for, according to the BBC.

Staff attempted to resuscitate the man, and employees who were working or involved in his care have been offered psychological support, the broadcaster said.

A Care Quality Commission (CQC) spokesperson said: “CQC are in contact with the trust as they look into the circumstances around this incident, so we can understand if there is any regulatory action that needs to be taken to ensure people are receiving safe care.

“CQC’s priority, at all times, is the health and wellbeing of people using health and social care services, and all information we receive informs our monitoring of services and future inspections.”

![Safety at the trust was rated as inadequate in June](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/09/1285429181..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Safety at Hull Royal Infirmary was rated as “inadequate” by the CQC in June, meaning the service is performing badly and the regulator has taken enforcement action against the provider. The hospital was rated overall as “requires improvement”.

An inquest into the man’s death was paused in September to wait for the results of the police investigation.

Police said anyone with information that would help with enquiries is asked to call the non-emergency number 101, quoting log 76 of 24 August.

Royal Hull Infirmary said it was unable to provide comment during a live police investigation.
