## Harrogate MasterChef star wins award for transforming school meals

Owen Diaram from Harrogate won the Customer Experience award from The School Food People for his work at Great Ouseburn Primary School *(Image: Supplied)*

A chef has received national recognition for his work in transforming school meals.

Owen Diaram, a BBC MasterChef quarter-finalist and private chef from Harrogate, won the Customer Experience award from The School Food People for his work at Great Ouseburn Primary School.

He is also known for his appearances at food festivals across Yorkshire.

Mr Diaram said: "When I took on the role, I was cooking for 40 children, and now I regularly cook for 90 to 100 children every day – alone.

"I've more than doubled the take-up of school meals and I ensure the taste, nutrition, and value for every child in school."

The School Food People is a national initiative that promotes high standards in school catering and aims to provide nutritious meals that support children’s learning and development.

Mr Diaram said he hopes to be involved in shaping future changes to school food standards.

New government guidelines set to be introduced in September 2027 will focus on improving nutrition and reducing foods high in fat, salt and sugar.

Alongside his work in education, he runs a private dining business, Cook With Owen, where he prepares meals in clients’ homes using fresh, seasonal ingredients.

Menus start from £80 per person.
