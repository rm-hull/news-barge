## Christmas card contest marks Boroughbridge show home opening

Lancaster Fold in Boroughbridge, a 195-home development by Bellway, will open its first show homes on November 28 *(Image: Supplied)*

The opening of new show homes is bringing festive cheer to the Boroughbridge community.

Lancaster Fold in Boroughbridge, a 195-home development by Bellway, will open its first show homes on November 28.

A Christmas card competition involving nearby Boroughbridge Primary School will help mark the occasion.

Neil Ryder, deputy headteacher at Boroughbridge Primary School, said: "We have some really creative children in the school and the excitement when we announced the competition was huge.

"And I have to say that going through the entries and picking two from each year has been both a great deal of fun and extremely challenging – there really were some great designs."

Visitors to the show homes will be invited to vote for their favourite card, with the winning design to be sent out by Bellway in time for Christmas.

Melanie Smith, sales director at Bellway Yorkshire, said: "Since we started sales back in June, there’s been a lot of interest in Lancaster Fold and a steady stream of sales."

The opening event will include a visit from Santa on the Boroughbridge Lions sled, who will help cut the ribbon and greet children.

For further information on Lancaster Fold, visit www.bellway.co.uk or call 01423 393045.
