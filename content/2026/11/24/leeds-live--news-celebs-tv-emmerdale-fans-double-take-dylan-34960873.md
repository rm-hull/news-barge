# Emmerdale fans double take as Dylan star 'changes overnight' in major transformation

## Emmerdale viewers have been left amazed by the transformation of Fred Kettle, who plays Dylan Penders on the ITV soap

Emmerdale fans were left stunned recently following a notable change in one of the soap's stars. Dylan Penders (Fred Kettle) first appeared on the ITV programme in February 2025 during a compelling episode focusing on April Windsor (Amelia Flanagan).

The character had fled home and found herself living rough on the streets, where Dylan became her protector and taught her survival skills. Following April's return home, Dylan also made his way to the village and began to establish himself in the Dales.

Yet his arrival has been anything but peaceful. Alongside April, Dylan became embroiled in a sinister underworld of narcotics and exploitation, orchestrated by Ray Walters (Joe Absolom) and his mother Celia Daniels (Jaye Griffiths).

The twisted pair coerced April and Dylan into transporting drugs to clear mounting debts, while April also faced disturbing attempts to force her into sexual exploitation.

Thankfully, their nightmare concluded when both Ray and Celia met their demise. In the weeks since, however, Dylan has been struggling with feelings of inadequacy compared to April and appears stuck in limbo, reports the Daily Star.

His unusual conduct has also raised eyebrows, with speculation mounting that he may have returned to drug dealing. Furthermore, this week he deceived April by claiming he needed an early night, only to be spotted sneaking out with a rucksack.

Yet viewers have been left astonished by the physical transformation of Dylan actor Fred Kettle. "Just to say how Dylan has grown in height," remarked one person on an Emmerdale Facebook fan page.

Someone else chimed in: " Not only Dylan, but April too. I know all children become adults, but these two have changed overnight." A third commented: "Definitely turning into a young man now."

Dylan has quickly won over audiences since arriving on screen in early 2025. Speaking about landing the role, actor Fred shared with the Mirror earlier this year: "I am still buzzing. It feels surreal and I can't believe that less than a year ago I was at college. Now I am here on Emmerdale playing Dylan. It's mad and I am loving being in the soap."

Discussing Lisa Riley, who portrays Dylan's mother-figure Mandy Dingle in the programme, Fred enthused: "[Lisa] is such a motherly person, she is very caring and always there for me. Dom Brunt is also good fun to work with. We get on really well."

Heaping praise on Mark Charnock, who plays Marlon Dingle, Fred continued: "[He] was such a mentor to me at the start. He gave me lots of positive advice and he's been a very good role model.'".
