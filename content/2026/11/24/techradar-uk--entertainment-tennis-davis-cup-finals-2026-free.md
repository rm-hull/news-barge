### How does the Davis Cup Finals 2026 work?

Unlike last week's ATP Finals, the Davis Cup Finals is a straight knockout.

Two countries face off in each tie. Each tie comprizes up to three matches, two singles followed by a deciding doubles rubber if the score stands at one victory each after the singles.

The No.2-ranked players do battle first, followed by the No.1 singles. Then the doubles, if required.
