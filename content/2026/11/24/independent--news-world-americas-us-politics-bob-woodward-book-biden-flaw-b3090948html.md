Veteran *Washington Post* journalist Bob Woodward has revealed the worst qualities of American presidents that he’s covered — and gave just two words for Joe Biden.

The 83-year-old, whose reporting helped expose the Watergate scandal, has released a new book, “Secrets: A Reporter's Memoir,” which chronicles his decades covering Washington’s power brokers.

The 592-page book, released Tuesday, names some of the confidential sources Woodward relied on. Most have died, and others gave him permission to identify them. They include Hillary Clinton, the late Senator Lindsey Graham and former CIA Director William Casey.

While promoting the book, Woodward sat down with *Axios* co-founder Mike Allen, who launched into a rapid-fire round, asking him to name the “fatal flaw” of each president he has covered.

Asked about Biden, the longtime reporter said: “I only interviewed him when he was vice president.” Pressed for his fatal flaw, he said: “Being president.”

![The 83-year-old, whose reporting helped expose the Watergate scandal, has released a new book, Secrets: A Reporter's Memoir, which chronicles his decades covering Washington’s power brokers](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/19/19/Screenshot-2026-11-24-at-2-01-45-PM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Woodward, who has written two dozen books, has criticized Biden sharply at times. After Biden's June 2024 debate against Donald Trump, he likened the Democrat's performance to a “political hydrogen bomb” and said calls for him to step aside would be “inevitable.” One month later, Biden announced he would not run for re-election.

The longtime *Washington Post* reporter was more expansive about the other presidents.

He called President Donald Trump a “liar” with “no sense of the obligations he has to the people.” He also said Trump has offered “no justification” for the Iran war.

![President Joe Biden takes a look at his notes during his disastrous presidential debate against Donald Trump in June 2024](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/21/2158930823..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

On Barack Obama, Woodward said he “knew how to keep the shield up.” He recalled Obama jokingly asking whether he had ever thought about becoming CIA director.

Gerald Ford “got it right,” but “it took him a while,” while Ronald Reagan “looks better and better with time.” George H.W. Bush, Woodward said, was the only “one who wouldn't talk to me.”
