A ground stop was issued at Chicago O'Hare International Airport by the Federal Aviation Administration on Tuesday.

The FAA said an equipment outage was to blame but did not offer more information.

Ten days ago, all flights nationwide were grounded for more than four hours due to a technical failure.

Thousands of flights were delayed and canceled because of the FAA outage, which stemmed from a contractor replacing old copper wire with fiber optic cable at a radio facility in El Dorado, Arkansas.
