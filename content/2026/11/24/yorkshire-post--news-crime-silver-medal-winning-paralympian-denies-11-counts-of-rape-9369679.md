# Silver medal-winning Paralympian denies 11 counts of rape

## A wheelchair-basketball player who won a silver medal for Britain at the 2024 Paralympics has denied 11 counts of rape, according to the Crown Prosecution Service (CPS).

Lee Fryer appeared at Leeds Crown Court on Thursday charged with 17 offences, including voyeurism, intentional strangulation, controlling or coercive behaviour in a family or intimate relationship, assault occasioning actual bodily harm, possessing a controlled drug of Class A, and doing an act intended to pervert the course of public justice.

Fryer, 26, from Pontefract, pleaded not guilty to all charges except voyeurism, for which no plea was entered.

He was remanded in custody and his trial will begin at the same court on March 22 2027, according to the CPS.

A spokesperson for British Wheelchair Basketball said: “We are aware of the criminal charges brought against Lee Fryer and recognise the seriousness of the allegations.

“At the point of his arrest, he was removed from the GB programme and all British Wheelchair Basketball activities, pending the outcome of the external investigation.

“As criminal proceedings are ongoing, it would be inappropriate to comment further at this time.”
