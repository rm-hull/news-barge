Outgoing Michigan Representative Haley Stevens is taking some time out at a luxury beach resort after a bruising midterm election season, which saw her ostracized by Democrats in the wake of her shock MAGA endorsement.

Stevens, whose term ends in January, was seen wearing a white baseball hat Monday while enjoying breakfast with her fiancé, Democratic donor Jason Turkish, at a 5-star resort in Hawaii, photos obtained by TMZ showed.

The pair was seemingly getting a head start on their Thanksgiving break while lounging at the lavish hotel. The pictures showed Turkish hunched over the table while wearing a black T-shirt and black baseball cap, while Stevens was in all white.

The 43-year-old Democrat sent shockwaves through the Democratic Party with her eleventh-hour decision to endorse the Trump-selected Republican candidate for Senate, former Rep. Mike Rogers, over Democrat Abdul El-Sayed, who narrowly defeated her in the Michigan primary.

El-Sayed eventually won the seat by a large margin. *The Independent* has contacted Stevens’ office for comment.

![US Representative Haley Stevens was pictured on vacation in Hawaii after her endorsement of Republican Mike Rogers made her a pariah among Democrats](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/16/54/1f284f059d59619c8c5bab375622597e-copy.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Stevens’ sudden drift from Democrats came as tensions grew between her and El-Sayed over their stances on Israel. Stevens had called on El-Sayed to condemn an ad from Rebellion PAC that suggested rising grocery prices were tied to U.S. funding for Israel. Stevens said she felt the ad was anti-Semitic.

While El-Sayed said he did not agree with the contents of the advertisement, he said that Stevens seemed “intent on litigating a primary that ended three months ago” as she continued to press him on the issue.

In a shocking move, Stevens endorsed Rogers, noting, “I don’t belong in Abdul’s campaign.”

“Who we are as Michiganders is not hate, and it is not what we are getting out from our Democratic nominee right now,” she said in the endorsement.

Stevens was swiftly kicked out of the Michigan Democratic Party after the endorsement, which led to some members of the Democratic House caucus calling for her removal over the betrayal.

![Stevens lost the primary to Abdul El-Sayed and later endorsed his opponent, Trump-backed Republican Mike Rogers](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/10/15/51/GettyImages-2304286860-2bc31e5f.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Her Senate bid ruled her out of running for reelection in the House and she has not revealed her next steps for her career. But Stevens said in a statement that she planned to “help lead” the effort to “build a stronger, more welcoming, more courageous Democratic Party.

Her decision to endorse Rogers also cost her more than half of her staff, according to the *Detroit Free Press.*

Some believe that Stevens' decision may have been swayed by her fiancé.

University of Michigan regent Jason Acker said in a since-deleted post that Turkish’s behavior leading up to the election left him “deeply troubled,” according to the Daily Beast. Stevens had reportedly told Acker the night before she endorsed Rogers that she was going to “remain silent.” However, the next morning she issued the endorsement.

Acker also accused Turkish of taking away Stevens’ phone, which he denied to the Daily Beast.
