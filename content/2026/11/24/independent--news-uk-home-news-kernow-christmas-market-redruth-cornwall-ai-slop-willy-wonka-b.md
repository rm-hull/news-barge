A Christmas market will cancel and refund all bookings after facing backlash for not living up to its AI-generated promotional material.

Kernow Christmas Market in Redruth, Cornwall, was advertised on Facebook with posters created by AI, showing a wonderful winter scene complete with festive stalls, bright lights and decorations.

The market was set to be an addition to Cornwall’s Winter Wonderland, based in Pool market hall.

However, many were left underwhelmed when - in reality - they arrived to what appeared to be markets around the sparse warehouse space.

One commenter on Facebook said: “It's like the wonka event all over again”, referring to a failed Willy Wonka experience that took place in Glasgow in 2024 but closed just hours after opening.

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/15/07/Kernow-Christmas-Market.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Another said: “Nothing like promised severely disappointed and no communication to vendors.

In a Facebook post on Sunday, the organisers addressed the criticisms.

It said: “Our original idea was to work alongside Pool Market, helping to increase footfall into their market while using their wonderful location as our winter trading home.

“We have worked as closely and cooperatively as possible within the space, facilities and rules that have been made available to us.

“As this is the first time we have undertaken something like this, we appreciate that there have been a few misunderstandings and perhaps different expectations of what the indoor setup would look and feel like.

“Unfortunately, some aspects of the venue and its layout are outside of our control, but that doesn’t mean we aren’t listening or that we don’t want to make things better.”

One user commented: “Maybe stop using unrealistic AI images to promote standards you cannot uphold!”

Another said: “As soon as I saw the AI adverts, I knew a car crash was coming.”

![Kernow Christmas Market was advertised with AI images](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/15/46/Kernow-Christmas-Market-d5b13170.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Maybe avoid the AI slop of your promotional adverts, it created an unrealistic image of what people where (sic) expecting”, a fellow commenter said.

On Tuesday, the organisers issued another statement, again addressing the criticism. They said that attempts to bring a “little festive magic into Pool Market” had not worked as planned and current bookings will be refunded.

The statement said: “We are gutted, just as much as you all that things didn’t go as planned. The criticism we have faced and the words that have been seen on social media and across the news make us look at the world slightly differently.

“Kindness and compassion goes out of the window so easily to knock something that people don’t care to understand the full story of.

“As a result of this, we are going to cancel and refund all current and future bookings and Kernow Christmas Market will take no further bookings for 2026. Any current bookings will be fully refunded.”
