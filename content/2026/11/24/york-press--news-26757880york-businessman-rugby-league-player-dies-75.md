A well-known York businessman who founded two successful printing companies and played rugby for the city has died aged 75.

George Peter Shelley died peacefully at York Hospital at 7.35pm on Thursday, November 12 after suffering a severe stroke, with his family by his side.

Mr Shelley was the founder of York City Printers and Joshua Barrington, establishing a successful career in the printing industry.

He was also closely associated with York’s sporting community and played at full-back for York RUFC during the late 1970s and early 1980s.

His death notice which can be found here describes him as a much-loved husband to Angela and father to Sam and Jessica.

He was also grandfather to Flora, Bert, Sienna, Archie and Sonny and was described by his family as "a dear friend to many".

A funeral service will be held at St Olave’s Church, Marygate, York, at 11.30am on Thursday, December 3.

It will be followed by a wake at York Sports Club, with the Shelley family inviting everyone who knew him to join them in remembering his life.

The family has requested family flowers only, with those wishing to pay tribute instead invited to make a donation through a memorial page created in his memory at [https://george-shelley.muchloved.com/](https://george-shelley.muchloved.com/)
