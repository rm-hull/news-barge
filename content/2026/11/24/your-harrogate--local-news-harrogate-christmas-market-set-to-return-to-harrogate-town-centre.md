Harrogate Christmas Market will return to Cambridge Street next Friday (4th December).

The market will run across three weekends, with traders selling local and international street food, sweet treats and artisan groceries.

Shoppers will also find handmade crafts, clothing and jewellery.

Stalls will be open from Friday 4th to Sunday 6th December, from Friday 11th to Sunday 13th December and on Friday 18th and Saturday 19th December.

Opening hours are 10am to 5pm on Fridays and Sundays and 9am to 5pm on Saturdays.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b055f452a94b1c9e2d.png)

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said:

> “We're delighted to welcome the Harrogate Christmas Market back to the town centre this December.
>
> “The market will offer a fantastic mix of festive food, gifts and artisan products, creating a vibrant atmosphere for residents and visitors to enjoy while supporting traders and local businesses during the festive season.
>
> “Whether you're looking for a unique Christmas gift, a festive treat or simply a great day out with family and friends, the Christmas Market is the perfect addition to a visit to Harrogate this December.”

Harrogate BID’s Christmas programme also includes farmers’ markets on Thursday 10th and Thursday 17th December, when Yorkshire traders will sell local produce and artisan food.

A Makers Market will take place on Sunday 20th December, featuring work by independent artists and craftspeople.
