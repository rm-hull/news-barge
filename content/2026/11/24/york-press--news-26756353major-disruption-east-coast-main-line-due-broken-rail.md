A broken rail has caused major disruption to trains passing through York station on the East Coast Main Line.

National Rail said the damage is between Peterborough and Huntingdon.

Train operators affected include York-based LNER and Hull Trains.




LNER said a temporary fix is allowing trains to move through the area at 20 miles per hour with the problem being corrected fully tonight.

Train services are being cancelled, delayed by up to 85 minutes, or revised.

These speed restrictions are being enforced to ensure the safety of passengers.

The disruption is expected to continue until at least 2pm today (Tuesday, November 24)

National Rail said LNER customers can use their tickets on Avanti West Coast, CrossCountry, East Midlands Railway, TransPennine Express, and Northern services at no extra cost.

If an LNER customer's train has been cancelled, their ticket will be valid on an alternative LNER service two hours before or after the cancelled service, according to the train operator body.

Passengers are advised to check the latest information on station departure boards and online journey planners before travelling.

Hull Trains and Lumo customers have been advised to continue their journeys as planned, but to expect delays.
