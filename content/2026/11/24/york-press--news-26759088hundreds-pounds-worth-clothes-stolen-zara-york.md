NORTH Yorkshire Police has launched an appeal after hundreds of pounds of clothing was reported stolen from a popular York store.

The force has released CCTV images of three women officers wish to speak with following the alleged theft from Zara, in Spurriergate, on Friday (September 11).

Goods worth £284.65 were stolen, police said.




A spokesperson added: "Please contact us if you recognise the people pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to email [lauren.zagorski@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:lauren.zagorski@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.

Please quote reference 12260206613 when passing on information.
