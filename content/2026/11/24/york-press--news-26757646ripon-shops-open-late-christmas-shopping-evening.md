## Shops to stay open late for festive deals, tastings and book signing

Fiona Trees pictured in front of her delightful display of Jellycats soft toys. *(Image: Tim Flanagan/Rejoicing in Ripon Blog for Ripon BID)*

Independent shops across a North Yorkshire city are staying open late for a special Christmas shopping evening offering festive deals and gift ideas.

The event will take place in Ripon on Thursday, November 26, with businesses across the city centre taking part in the seasonal shopping trail.

From The Castle on North Street to Karma on Kirkgate, shops will remain open into the evening, giving visitors a chance to explore handcrafted goods, fashion, books, toys, and homeware.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21752874.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Jane Aksut, owner of Cafe in the Park at Spa Gardens is hosting an indoor artisan market *(Image: Tim Flanagan/Rejoicing in Ripon Blog for Ripon BID)*

Lilla Bathurst, manager of the Ripon Business Improvement District (BID), said: "It’s beginning to look a lot like Christmas all over our city, which has been celebrating the joyous Yuletide period for more than a 1300 years."

"The holly and the ivy that can be seen at our magnificent cathedral and in the beautiful Spa Gardens, can be enjoyed alongside a centre packed with heritage buildings including three fine museums and other historical attractions, such as the Market Square with its Grade I listed obelisk.

"All add to the magic of a festive shopping trip, while our wide range of hospitality businesses provide visitors and local residents alike with a great choice of food and drink all served with a very warm welcome."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21752875.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jane Robinson, manager at Poundland *(Image: Tim Flanagan/Rejoicing in Ripon Blog for Ripon BID)*

The city is already in the Christmas spirit following its lights switch-on earlier this month, which drew large crowds to the newly redecorated Ripon Town Hall and Market Square.

Many independent retailers will offer special promotions and festive experiences throughout the evening.

This Other Eden on Fishergate will serve a complimentary glass of wine and mince pie, while Eastwick on North Street will treat customers to drinks as they browse.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21752876.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fiona Trees pictured in front of her delightful display of Jellycats soft toys. *(Image: Tim Flanagan/Rejoicing in Ripon Blog for Ripon BID)*

Majestic Wine will remain open until 8pm for Christmas wine tastings, and Saint Michael’s Hospice Shop will offer a glass of mulled wine and a 10 per cent discount on goods they sell.

Poundstretcher, 11 Marshall Way, will also stay open late, offering affordable gifts, decorations, and seasonal treats.

Memorable Moments on North Street, the city's sole stockist of Jellycat soft toys, will also take part, with owner Fiona Trees promising plenty of festive cheer.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21752877.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Almost 40,000 children and young people in North Yorkshire have received free holiday activities and meals through a scheme helping families facing financial pressures. *(Image: Tim Flanagan/Rejoicing in Ripon Blog for Ripon BID)*

She said: "Late night shoppers will be able to buy our design-led greeting cards and gifts, which are perfect for Christmas and other occasions.

"We are also delighted to be Ripon’s sole stockist of Jellycat soft toys, which make delightful presents for children of all ages."

The Portly Pig on Kirkgate, recently awarded Pub of the Year by the Harrogate and Ripon CAMRA branch, will use the evening to support Ripon Dementia Forward through a charity raffle and other fun activities.

Newcomers like Sprout & Crumb on North Street will also join in the celebrations.

Owner Luke Mills said: "Having received such a warm welcome from customers and fellow business owners I decided that I will be opening late on Thursday as enjoy my first taste of Christmas in this lovely city."

The event is designed to support Ripon’s independent retailers and encourage people to shop locally during the Christmas season.
