## Mother spared jail after glassing woman and leaving permanent facial scar

Chelsea Barry attacked the woman with a glass *(Image: Copilot)*

A mother who “glassed” another woman in a nightclub leaving her with a permanent scar has been spared a trip to jail because of her child.

The Recorder of York, Judge Sean Morris, told Chelsea Barry, 28, “you richly deserve to go immediately to prison.”

But, he added: “I have to think about the nine-year-old child that would be left behind.”

Earlier he had told York Crown Court “my hands are tied” because Barry was the sole carer of her child.

He told Barry: “You caused a permanent scar to a woman’s face because you were no doubt in drink and you were in a bad mood.

“Every time she (the other woman) looks in the mirror and sees that, she is going to think of you. You are not going to have anything like that.”

He gave Barry a 16-month prison sentence suspended for two years on condition she does 180 hours’ unpaid work and banned her from Zuzu Lounge nightclub in Selby, where the incident happened, for five years.

He also made a restraining order banning her from contacting the victim for five years.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21751810.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chelsea Barry is banned from the Zuzu Lounge nightclub for five years *(Image: Google Street View)*

In a personal statement, the woman said the incident had left her with a scar and dent in her forehead and since then, she had suffered migraines and headaches.

She had also suffered night-time palsies on one side of her face which had not occurred before the incident.

York Crown Court heard there was no medical evidence about whether the palsy and assault were connected.

The woman said because of the psychological effects of the assault, she had given up her dog grooming business and now worked for the NHS.

Barry, of Meadow View, Sherburn-in-Elmet, was originally charged with wounding the other woman with intent, which she denies.

The prosecution accepted her guilty plea to wounding without intent.

Prosecutor Jennifer Gatland said CCTV footage of the incident from the nightclub was unclear whether Barry had picked up the glass before hitting the other woman on the head with it.

The glass did not break. It caused a 1.5cm cut.

The victim was taken to hospital by her mother, where the wound was cleaned and closed.

Some days later she went to an urgent care service with swelling to the left of the forehead and also went to her GP two weeks after that.

For Barry, Celine Kart said: “She is riddled with remorse.

"The shame she feels as a result of her actions weighs heavily upon her.”

She said Barry was hard-working with a strong work ethic and willing to take on extra responsibilities, she said.
