Dawn of War 4 is almost upon us, and we're conscripting some new recruits. We have five Steam codes for the Commander Edition to give away to our readers in the UK and US, and all you have to do is sign up to PC Gamer and hop on over to the code campaign on our giveaway page.

The Commander Edition includes the base game, the Blood Ravens story prologue, access to a new faction and campaign expansion when they launch, and the official soundtrack.

In October, I played two of the game's campaigns, and I had a ton of grimdark fun. As someone who tends to prefer campaigns and comp stomps to competitive multiplayer, I felt very well taken care of. But if you'd prefer to batter other human players, you can absolutely do that as well.

Dawn of War 4 launches on December 3, but with the Commander Edition you can start playing early, on November 30. Good luck!
