A YORK performance group is rounding off 2026 with its annual festival of Christmas music.

The York Philharmonic Male Voice Choir is set to host a series of 'Christmas Traditions' concerts at The Citadel in, Gillygate from Tuesday, December 1 to December 4, starting at 7.30pm each evening.

Concert-goers can expect an evening filled with carols, festive songs, and readings.




Tickets are priced at £18, with a concession for under 18s at £10.

They can be purchased online at yorkphilchoir.org.uk or on the door.

The concert on Wednesday, December 2, will have a special presence as the Civic Party, including the Lord Mayor, will be in attendance.

The choir, which has been a part of York's festive celebrations for many years, promises a "gloriously tuneful Christmassy evening."

Wine and mince pies will be available both before and at the interval of each concert, adding to the festive atmosphere.

The choir's chairman said: "Christmas wouldn’t be Christmas without music and what better way to mark the proper start to the season than with top quality live music.

"Our own city’s Male Voice choir can provide just that."
