Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, has been indicted by a federal grand jury in Los Angeles on two counts of possessing child sexual abuse material.

Markle, 82, was indicted in federal court on Tuesday, US prosecutors said.

Markle was arrested by FBI agents in Florida earlier this month at the senior care facility where he was living, and had already been in federal court in Tampa in connection with possession of child sexual abuse material in California.

Markle is estranged from his daughter and his son-in-law, Meghan’s husband, Prince Harry. He was initially released by the court in Tampa after making a brief appearance but later jailed after cutting off his ankle monitor, according to the federal authorities.

He is accused of possessing child sexual abuse material in the Philippines, where he previously lived, and keeping illegal images and other material in storage facilities in Los Angeles, involving children under the age of 12.
