Preparations are underway for a new pantomime from the Ebor Players.

Mother Goose will open at Bishopthorpe Village Hall on Monday, November 30, and run until Saturday, December 5.

It promises to be an entertaining family show with traditional pantomime features such as lively songs, colourful costumes, and plenty of laughs.

The plot revolves around Fairy Aurora and Smorg, the wicked troll, in a battle of good versus evil, with the fate of Mother Goose and her beloved Heidi hanging in the balance.

Directed and produced by Philippa Parrish and written by Ben Crocker, the show is sponsored by Martin & Co.

This year, the pantomime is set in Norway, a unique twist for the Ebor Players, who have been performing at Bishopthorpe Village Hall since their debut in 1994.

The evening performances start at 7.30pm, except for the opening night, which begins at 7pm.

A matinee on Saturday at 2pm is already sold out.

The Wednesday and Saturday evening shows are for adults only and will feature cabaret-style seating.

Tickets, ranging from £10 to £13.50, are available from [https://www.ticketsource.com/ebor-players/mother-goose/e-kkqzam](https://www.ticketsource.com/ebor-players/mother-goose/e-kkqzam)
