# Huddersfield rapist, 25, caught the same day thanks to victim’s actions

## A Huddersfield rapist who lured a ‘vulnerable’ teenager to his home was caught the same day thanks to the bravery of his victim.

‘Predatory’ Joel Senior lured the 17-year-old girl to his home in March 2025 before raping her.

The girl was able to flee his house after the attack, before quickly reporting the rape to police.

West Yorkshire Police have now praised the rape survivor after her actions meant Senior was arrested on the same day the offence took place.

The 25-year-old was jailed for 13 years and eight months for rape, after being convicted of the offence at trial in October.

![Joel Senior was described as a 'predatory' sex offender](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/15/30/joel_senior.jpeg?trim=715,6,75,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


DC Eleanor Radley of Kirklees Police, said: “The survivor in this case has shown huge courage in coming forward, and her bravery has resulted in the conviction of a man who we believe to be a dangerous and predatory sex offender.

“He preyed on a particularly vulnerable victim, but her courage in coming forwards so quickly allowed us to arrest Senior on the same day the offence took place.

“We fully appreciate just how difficult it can be to report being a victim of any sexual offence and can pledge we do all we can to support victims.

“Specially trained detectives will always listen, investigate and do all we can to seek justice for you, as well as help you access support you may need.

“Your bravery in coming forward could well help prevent another person becoming a victim of an offence, as well as provide you with the knowledge your attacker has been made to answer for their crimes.”
