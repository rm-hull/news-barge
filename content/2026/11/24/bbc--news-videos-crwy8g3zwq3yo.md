# Watch: Trump pardons turkeys Freedom and Feathers

President Donald Trump continued a long-standing White House holiday tradition, pardoning two turkeys ahead of Thanksgiving Day. This year's turkeys, named Freedom and Feathers, were granted a "complete and total pardon".

The festivities mark the 79th annual National Thanksgiving Turkey Presentation. The event dates back to 1947, when the National Turkey Federation first began presenting turkeys; however, the presidential pardon didn't become a tradition until 1989, under President George H. W. Bush.
