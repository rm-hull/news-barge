![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: 'Huang returns to Nvidia hotseat as board bows to Trump pressure.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/624/cpsprodpb/1ab8/live/bd5148c0-e78a-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO dominates Tuesday's front pages. Less than two weeks after the tech billionaire resigned following claims of an affair with an alleged spy, Huang has returned to the "hotseat" after US President Donald Trump "directly intervened with the AI chipmaker's board of directors", the Guardian reports. "I felt very strongly that he should come back," Trump said, according to the paper.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: 'Trump chips in to secure return of Nvidia boss.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/54fc/live/f9406260-e78c-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

In a play on words, the Times headlines on Trump chipping in "to secure return of Nvidia boss". The US president has claimed credit for "convincing" Nvidia to reinstate Huang as CEO, after he stepped down following claims of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy, the paper reports. The president said the US government "has no reason to believe Jensen's return to Nvidia poses any threat to National Security", according to the Times.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: 'Boomer Huang.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/e0ce/live/53135360-e788-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The Metro describes Huang as the "cheating tech boss" who has "boomeranged back into his job". The 63-year-old co-founder of Nvidia and "fan of black leather jackets", said he was returning "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment" following the fallout, the paper reports. Meanwhile, a row between I'm A Celeb... contestants AJ Odudu and Mariella Frostrup also appears on the front page.

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: 'Huang reinstated as Nvidia chief.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/857f/live/3a06d1d0-e788-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

In what is being described as a "stunning reversal" by Tuesday's papers, the Financial Times also headlines on Huang's return to Nvidia, which sent the AI giant's shares soaring by "35 per cent in early trading", the paper reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: 'Burnham opens door to second referendum.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/3850/live/4cd4beb0-e78a-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

Several newspapers headline on the UK prime minister's mention of a second referendum for the UK to rejoin the European Union. Andy Burnham admitted on Monday it would be "unthinkable" to rejoin the EU without allowing the public a vote, the Daily Telegraph reports. The paper mentions Labour MPs trying to convince the PM to avoid the unfavourable topic.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: 'PM opens door to second EU referendum.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/e3d0/live/66ad42d0-e78a-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The Daily Mail describes Burnham's "plans to cosy up to the EU" as the "last thing our riven, stagnating nation needs". While the PM "opened the door" to another referendum, "Downing Street refused to say how the British people will be consulted" on the plans, the paper reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: 'You should be embarrassed by this bad EU deal.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/c006/live/64201a30-e788-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The Daily Express takes a different tack, headlining on Kemi Badenoch's quotes saying the PM "should be embarrassed by the bad EU deal". During a summit last Friday, Burnham signed agreements with the EU on emissions trading and youth mobility, the paper reports. Elsewhere on the page, British actor Idris Elba is pictured walking with King Charles III, who he says "helped shape my life".

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: 'EasyJet, Ryanair and BA plans among 140 "jammed by Russian warship" off UK coast.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/3f3a/live/7f9fe5b0-e788-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The i Paper reports on dozens of passenger planes as being suspected victims of "jamming" by a "Russian warship sailing within 200 miles of the English coast" earlier this month. Jamming involves powerful radio signals overwhelming a GPS system, the paper explains. Russian authorities suggest the defensive measure is to protect key cities and military infrastructure from Ukrainian drone attacks, according to the paper.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: 'Hopefully I've killed him.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/c434/live/85100a00-e78a-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

And the Daily Mirror leads on what it describes as the "Soham monster death trial" - the trial of a prison inmate accused of murdering convicted killer Ian Huntley. Anthony Russell has denied murdering Huntley, but admits manslaughter.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: 'I won't be Sir Wayne.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/e345/live/2e6d8ab0-e78a-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

Former English footballer Wayne Rooney has said he would turn down a knighthood, according to the Daily Star. Rooney is taking part in the latest series of I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: 'PM: Why The Independent's Christmas campaign will save young homeless sleeping rough.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/f0d5/live/fabb3f60-e788-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

Burnham has said the Independent's Christmas campaign to transform empty buildings into housing for homeless people will "put hope in the hearts" in young people and give them "the chance to build the future they deserve", according to the paper.
