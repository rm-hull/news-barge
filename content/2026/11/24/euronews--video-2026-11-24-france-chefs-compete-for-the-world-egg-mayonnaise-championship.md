A staple of **French brasserie** menus, the dish traditionally consists of halved hard-boiled eggs generously coated in mayonnaise. Contestants brought their own creative interpretations to the competition, carefully balancing flavours, textures and presentation. After tasting the competing dishes, the judges awarded the**2026 title** to**Hugo Riboulet**, chef at** Groot La Tourte**restaurant in** Paris**.

First held in **2018**, the championship was established to celebrate and preserve a classic of** French culinary heritage**while encouraging chefs to reinvent it. Despite the simplicity of its ingredients,** egg mayonnaise** requires precise cooking and a carefully balanced sauce, with enough acidity to offset its richness without masking the flavour of the egg.

Among this year's contestants was **Chris Hanot**, sous-chef at** London's Pavyllon**restaurant, who arrived with an unexpected royal endorsement. According to Hanot,** Prince Harry**had sampled his team's egg mayonnaise at the neighbouring** Bar Antoine**, enjoyed it enough to order a second helping and wished the team luck in the competition.
