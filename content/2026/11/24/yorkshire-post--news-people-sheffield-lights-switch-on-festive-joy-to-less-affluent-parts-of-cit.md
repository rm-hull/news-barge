# Sheffield lights switch on: Festive joy to “less affluent parts of city”

## A ward in east Sheffield will host a free Christmas party and light switch-on, bringing festivities to local communities outside the city centre.

The freeArbourthorne Christmas Event is planned for Friday 4 December atNorthern Avenue shopping parade, with a light switch-on, Christmas carols, Santa and Rudolph the ‘rodeo reindeer’.

Ward councillor Ben Miskell said: “We’re really excited.Last year it had a massive snow globe, it had free food, inflatables. It had Santa there and lots of things happening.

“We are looking forward to bringing back that joy again and bringing people together to switch the Christmas lights on to celebrate Christmas.”

Coun Miskell saidpeople who live in the Park & Arbourthorne ward are “proud and work really hard, but years of cuts from previous governments have really taken their toll.”

“We want children to have just as much opportunity to enjoy their lives as more affluent parts of the city.

“We want them to be able to have access to the joys and magic of Christmas without families having to worry about cost.”

The Labour councillor continued: “As local councillors, we’re focused on uniting the community and Christmas is a great time to do that.”

Reform UK ward councillor Matt Smith said he was pleased to have supported it alongside other councillors for Park & Arbourthorne ward, who together secured funding for the event from Sheffield City Council.

Coun Smith said: “Times are tough, everything is getting more and more expensive and families across the ward and wider city are struggling to make ends meet, so it’s great to have the opportunity to put smiles on faces this Christmas time with this free event.”

The council’s local area ward pot granted £3,216 to the Manor & Castle Development Trust to put on the party.

Coun Miskell added: “Thanks very much to our friends at Manor Castle Development Trust for helping us to make it happen”.
