# Cranswick hails ‘positive’ first half despite major pig industry pressures

## Yorkshire-based meat and poultry firm Cranswick has hailed a “positive” first half despite major issues with oversupply in domestic and global pig production which are currently impacting the market.

Cranswick posted pre-tax profits of £106.4m for the 26 weeks ended 26 September, a dip of 2.6 per cent from the same period a year earlier.

It came alongside revenue of £1.52bn, up 3.9 per cent year-on-year.

The results come after a group of MPs at the start of this week wrote to the government warning that pressures on the pig industry are putting supply chains in financial jeopardy and threatening wider impacts on the UK’s rural economy.

![Meat and poultry firm Cranswick has hailed a “positive” first half](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmM1MWYwM2M1LWUyMzAtNGVjNS1iMTAyLTNkNGY3NDgyZDNmMDo2MjkzYzgwNy0zMmI2LTQzZTAtYjE1ZC05YTk4MDBhY2YzOTE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


In the letter, MPs from the EFRA Committee described a UK pig market as having been “declining since autumn 2025, due to an oversupply of pigs and stagnant demand for pork and bacon”.

MPs also warned of “severe pressure” in the EU pig market due to weaker demand than anticipated.

But speaking on the firm’s results, Adam Couch, Cranswick's chief executive officer, painted a positive picture for the firm.

He said: "We have delivered a positive first half performance, underpinned by volume growth across all product categories and robust demand for our core products.

“This was achieved against the backdrop of well-publicised oversupply in domestic and global pig production, together with broader inflationary pressure on our cost base.

“The consistent delivery of our differentiated long-term growth strategy, with greater integration, diversification and scale, continues to strengthen Cranswick's resilience, mitigating the impact of current market dynamics and supporting continued growth in earnings.”

Elsewhere in its results, Cranswick said its £100m investment in its flagship Hull Pork primary processing site was “progressing well”.

The company added that a £56m further capacity expansion at its Eye Fresh Poultry facility was now also underway.

During the period, the firm submitted a planning application for a new build £220m Fresh Poultry facility, which it said was “progressing as expected”.

Speaking on the scheme, Mr Couch said: “This £220m project will make a meaningful contribution to UK food security and economic growth, creating 1,400 jobs, with further investment and employment generated through the farming supply chain and during the construction phase.”

Cranswick said it had undertaken a capital spend of £61m in the first half, with acceleration anticipated in the second half reflecting the timing of major projects.

The firm also during the period formed a joint venture partnership with The Jolly Hog, a premium sausage and bacon supplier.
