# Teenager from Leeds charged with online harm offences including blackmail after ‘Com networks’ investigation

## A 17-year-old boy has been charged with serious online harm offences after an investigation into “Com networks”.

West Yorkshire Police said the teenager, from Leeds, faces charges of blackmail and participating in the criminal activities of an organised crime group.

He has also been charged with one count of arranging the sexual exploitation of a child aged 13 to 17, one count of inciting a girl aged 13 to 15 to engage in sexual activity, one count of attempting to incite the sexual exploitation of a child aged 13 to 17 and one count of distributing an indecent photograph of a child.

The boy, who cannot be named because of his age, is due to appear at Leeds Youth Court on December 22.

Police said he was charged following an investigation into activity connected to serious online harm, often referred to as Com networks.

The force said their officers had worked with national and international law enforcement agencies to identify and safeguard victims in the investigation.
