**India's 15-year-old batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi hit the most sixes ever recorded in a senior men's innings in blazing 204 from 53 balls for India A against the United Arab Emirates.**

While almost halving the record for the fastest double-century in List A cricket, Sooryavanshi hit 28 sixes and seven fours in the Asia Cup Rising Stars competition - easily breaking Chad Bowes' previous record of 103 balls from 2024.

The teenage opener also broke the record for the fastest century and 150, the latter of which he already held, before being caught at long-off with his side five runs from victory.

India A ultimately passed their target of 250, played against a UAE side that featured a number of internationals but was not their current first-choice side, in 14.2 overs in Doha.

Sooryavanshi now holds the following records in List A cricket, the categorisation given to senior one-day matches:

* **Fastest fifty:** 11 balls v Sri Lanka A, 21 June (previously Kaushalya Weeraratne, 12 balls, 1 November 2005)
* **Fastest hundred:** 28 balls, today (previously Jake Fraser-McGurk, 29 balls, 8 October 2023)
* **Fastest 150:** 40 balls, today (previously himself, 59 balls, 24 December 2025)
* **Fastest 200:** 52 balls, today (previously Chad Bowes, 103 balls, 22 October 2024)

The previous record for the most sixes in senior matches was 26, struck by Tanmay Agarwal in an innings of 366 for Hyderabad against Arunachal Pradesh in India's first-class Ranji Trophy competition in January 2024.

The record in List A cricket was 23 sixes, struck by D'Arcy Short for Queensland in his 257 against Tasmania on 28 September 2018.

Sooryavanshi's record 28 sixes came on both sides of the wicket

Sooryavanshi has rapidly become a sensation in T20 cricket since making his debut in the Indian Premier League in 2025 at the age of 14.

He has hit two hundreds and six fifties in his first two seasons in the competition and has earned 10 caps for India in the shortest format since making his debut against England in July.

The left-hander, who was batting in only his 16th List A innings in Doha and made his second century in the format, is yet to play for India in 50-over cricket.

He put on partnerships of 68 with opening partner Abhishek Sharma, who scored 12, 40 with Prabhsimran Singh, who added just three, and 137 with Rinku Singh, who scored an unbeaten 27.

The India A side, which featured a number of internationals, restricted the UAE to 249-8 in 50 overs, external after their opposition had chosen to bat and swiftly reached 254-3 to win by seven wickets and guarantee their place in the semi-finals.

The Rising Stars competition features A sides from India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Afghanistan alongside squads from the UAE, Nepal and Hong Kong.

India A, who now top Group B, beat Nepal and lost to Pakistan A in their previous two matches in the competition, with Sooryavanshi making scores of 26 and 11.

Pakistan A will retake the top spot if they beat Nepal on Tuesday, with semi-finals on Thursday and Friday to follow before Sunday's final.
