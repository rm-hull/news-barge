## The warning follows a fresh wave of security incidents across Europe in recent weeks, including airspace violations and reported cyber and sabotage attacks.

Britain has warned UK defence firms supplying Ukraine's armed forces over increasing Russian sabotage attempts.

The National Protective Security Authority and the Ministry of Defence issued a joint alert on Tuesday offering "protective security guidance" for organisations involved "in the supply chain providing direct material support" to Ukraine.

"In recent months Russia has increased in attempts to conduct sabotage against European defence related facilities," the statement said.

"This activity is typically characterised by the use of proxy actors employing low-sophistication sabotage methodologies, such as arson," it continued, adding that such activity was intended to "undermine support" for Kyiv.

The alert warned that any organisations involved in military procurement or supplying equipment to Ukraine could be a target.

It also offered a number of steps on how companies could reduce their exposure to "hostile reconnaissance," detect and report suspicious activity and minimise the impact of any attacks.

In a separate statement, Westminster announced that closed-door security briefings were also to be given to defence sector and critical national infrastructure CEOs on Tuesday and Wednesday.

"Both the briefings and the alert will set out actions organisations can take to protect their sites, people and operations amidst the changing threat landscape," it said.

The warnings follow a fresh wave of security incidents across Europe in recent weeks, including airspace violations and reported cyber and sabotage attacks.

UK Security Minister Dan Jarvis said that recent sabotage attempts across the continent demonstrated the "seriousness of the threats we face."

"It’s truly shameful that actors are seeking to disrupt our way of life and that of our allies, especially targeting organisations doing vital work in support of Ukraine," he added.

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham has repeatedly signalled his support for Ukraine as it continues to fight against Russia's ongoing invasion.
