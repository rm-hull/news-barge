A CHRISTMAS market promising festive fun and unique gifts is set to return to a North Yorkshire town.

Harrogate Christmas Market will be held in Cambridge Street across three weekends this December.

Kickstarting on Friday (December 4) to Sunday (December 6) weekend, the market will be welcoming visitors and locals.

Weekend two will run from December 11 to December 13, and weekend three from Friday (December 18) to Saturday (December 19).




Harrogate BID, which is organising the event, said the market will bring together numerous traders, offering everything from local and international street food to artisan pieces, sweet treats, and handmade crafts, jewellery and accessories.

Bethany Allen, operations manager at Harrogate BID, said: "We're delighted to welcome the Harrogate Christmas Market back to the town centre this December.

"The market will offer a fantastic mix of festive food, gifts and artisan products, creating a vibrant atmosphere for residents and visitors to enjoy while supporting traders and local businesses during the festive season.

"Whether you're looking for a unique Christmas gift, a festive treat or simply a great day out with family and friends, the Christmas Market is the perfect addition to a visit to Harrogate this December."

The market is designed to offer a festive shopping experience while boosting footfall and supporting the town’s businesses during the busy Christmas period.

Opening times will be 10am to 5pm on Fridays and Sundays, and 9am to 5pm on Saturdays.

Additional festive events will include farmers’ markets on Thursday, December 10 and Thursday, December 17, featuring Yorkshire traders selling seasonal produce and artisan foods.

A special Makers Market will also take place on Sunday, December 20, showcasing independent artists, makers and craftspeople from across the region.

Further details about the full Christmas programme can be found on the Harrogate BID website.
