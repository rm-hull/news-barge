## Firefighters stage dramatic rescue-from-height exercise in Hornsea

Firefighters have carried out a realistic rescue-from-height during a recent training exercise at Pybus Court in Hornsea *(Image: East Riding of Yorkshire Council)*

Firefighters have carried out a realistic rescue-from-height during a recent training exercise.

The training took place on Saturday, November 21, at Pybus Court in Hornsea, an East Riding of Yorkshire Council-owned building currently under refurbishment.

Crews from Bridlington and Hornsea used the site to practise a simulated rescue from height, including stabilising and transferring a casualty.

Councillor Anne Handley, leader of East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "We’re delighted to work with Humberside Fire and Rescue to enable this vital training exercise while building works on our facilities are under way."

Group Manager Dominic Purchon of Humberside Fire and Rescue said the exercise offered valuable hands-on experience.

He said: "This has been an excellent opportunity for firefighters from Hornsea and Bridlington Fire Stations to work together in a realistic training environment.

"Training exercises like this are an important part of ensuring our firefighters remain prepared to help keep our communities safe."

Wayne Cropp, contracts manager at Hobson and Porter, said the company was proud to support the exercise.

He added: "Supporting Humberside Fire and Rescue Service with this training exercise was a great opportunity to make practical use of the site while the refurbishment works are ongoing."

The Pybus Court refurbishment is set for completion in spring 2027.
