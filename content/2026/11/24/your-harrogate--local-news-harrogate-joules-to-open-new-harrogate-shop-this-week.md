Joules will open its new Harrogate shop on Friday (27th November).

The British lifestyle brand, which previously traded in the town, is returning to James Street.

Joules' previous Harrogate store closed in 2023 as part of a nationwide plan.

On the day of Joules' return, Harrogate Band will perform live, when shoppers can also enjoy hot drinks and sweet treats.

Louise Brown, retail and property director at Joules, said:

> “We're thrilled to be returning to Harrogate.
>
> “It's a wonderful opportunity to reconnect with our loyal customers whilst introducing Joules to a whole new audience.”

*Shoppers can get 20% off when they spend £50 or more from Friday 27th to Sunday 29th November.*
