Taking Out Kitty Litter's lab locations in GTA 6 is a mission in Tyrone's questline, and sees you searching the city for 18 secret labs (after the first one he directs you to). Every lab has a red smoking chimney and a blue and white Spin On This laundry van parked outside.

It'll take a little while, but it's well worth it to get the unique gold SMG at the end. Plus, Tyrone will start buying narcotics from you for the highest price possible. To destroy each lab, simply shoot a smoking pink flask and then run away. **I've also laid these out in the most optimal order**, separated into regions, so you're not driving back and forth across Vice City.

Last note: if you want to unlock map markers for the labs yourself versus relying on these videos, you can threaten cooks in certain labs to potentially reveal them.

## La Perle labs

### The first lab in La Perle trailer park

After you complete some missions for Tyrone (when you meet him with Dre'Quan during Dealer's Choice) he'll direct you to this trailer park in La Perle where you destroy the first Kitty Litter lab and start the hunt for the others. You can see the route to the lab in the video above.

## In the trailer park opposite Zenon's Pawn Shop

You can find this one in the trailer park opposite Zenon's Pawn Shop Ammu-Nation in La Perle. As soon as you go through the main gate, you can see the trailer with the funnel chimney where they're cooking straight ahead. Sneak around the back and break inside to find the lab. Or take out the three guys in front and go in that way.

## Decor District labs

### In the car park of Williams + Whitaker Homeware and Decor

Just across the road from the Mezzasega Espresso and Slinger in the Decor District. Pull off to the right of the road into the Williams + Whitaker Homeware and Decor car park. You'll see a garage with a shutter at the far end with a smoking chimney and the laundry van parked. Open the shutter to find a lone guy in the lab.

## Rockridge labs

### Across the road from Blessed Food Market

This one is behind Peterson's Autoshop in Rockridge, directly south across the road from the Blessed Food Market, under the overpass, on the border between Rockridge and La Perle. Head through the main yard and turn left to spot the laundry van. Break into it, move it forward, and open the shutter behind to find the lab.

## Next door to Xero Gas - Rockridge

Just east of Fleeca Field stadium, next door to Xero Gas - Rockridge. You'll spot the blue and white laundry van parked off to the side of the road behind a wall. Head down that alley and turn right to find a garage door you can open with the lab inside.

### South of Vice City Hospital

On the east side of the condominium building directly south of the Vice City Hospital, by Emilio's Auto Center Gas. Look for the closed road with the laundry van parked at the southern end and some staircases and flats off to the side with the chimney spewing smoke. The lab is through the white door on the bottom floor.

### Near Bump and Grind Coffee

Just down the road to the west of Bump and Grind coffee and Salon Belle, in a block of flats southeast of Fleeca Fields stadium. You can see the laundry van parked outside. The lab is on the upper floor on the left side in apartment 5, where you can see the smoking chimney. Break in via the front door to find the lab.

## Crosstown labs

### Behind Cross My Heart Tattoos

This lab is an easy one to find, around the back of Cross My Heart Tattoos. Head around the right side, turn left, and look to your left for the garage door with the guy sitting down guarding it, with the lab inside.

### Southeast of the Jack of Hearts

This lab is in the block of flats to the southeast of the Jack of Hearts and Urban Legendz, with the laundry van parked out front. Look inside apartment 103 on the first floor on the left side. It's quite hard to get away from this lab blowing up, so shoot it from outside and run away. Also this will likely cause a big shootout in the block, so book it.

### Southwest of your apartment

This lab is to the southwest of your apartment in Chapter 3 and to the east of the Crosstown Mule - City Line marker. Look for an overgrown lot with the laundry van parked in it. The garage with the lab is just behind that.

### South of Rideout Customs

This lab is directly west of Down Town Pawn, just between Rideout Customs and the Vargo's Gas station. Just past the gas station, look to the left where its fuel sign is to spot a laundry van, and further down the track, the garage with the lab and smoking chimney above it.

## Tequesta labs

### West of Vice Seven Condominiums gym

This lab is close to Xero Gas - Tequesta and west of Vice Seven Condominiums gym. When driving east to west past the station, turn right and keep an eye on your right towards the two floored condominiums for the laundry van parked outside. You want the set of staircases to the right of the van. Take the right side one up to the first floor to find the lab door directly ahead.

## VCIA labs

## East from Arrow Gas - VCIA

This lab is in a trailer park directly east down the road from Arrow Gas - VCIA, also west under the bridge from Pawn City and Vice City Firearms. Turn into the trailer park, take the first right and drive a little way around the corner to spot the trailer with the smoking chimney on the left. You can enter via a window around the other side to find the lab.

## Salton labs

### At the southern tip of Salton

This lab is located in the far south of Salton, north of the Pay 'N' Spray - Salton, next door to the Slator Towing Inc robbery. Go around the south side of the derelict block of flats and climb the dumpster to jump up through the open window to find the lab.

### West from Xero Gas - Salton

This lab is west down the road from Xero Gas - Salton. Cross the train tracks, then keep an eye on your right for a carpark with a blue and white laundry van, then turn right into it to spot the building with the smoking chimney on the right. Hop the fence straight ahead and turn right to find one guy guarding the garage with the lab.

## Little Cuba labs

### South of Salon Perez

This lab is easy to find, directly south of Salon Perez and Safia Restaurant in Little Cuba. Look for some bungalows on the left with the laundry van parked outside. It's in the building with the chimney to the left of the one the van is parked in front of. The lab is the second door along.

## Balasero labs

### North of Pay 'N' Spray - Balasero

This lab is north up the road from the Pay 'N' Spray - Balsero. Turn left down the alley with the laundry van parked to find a guy guarding the gate, a couple of guys inside, and the lab in an open garage further back behind them.

### Southeast of the Balasero jewelry stores

This lab is in a trailer park southeast from the Balasero jewelry stores, across the river. If driving from the east and the city, turn left before the bridge, turn right, then turn left and head a little way down to spot the trailer with the funnel chimney and laundry van outside. Sneak around back to get in and find the lab.

### South from Ron's Car Wash and Gas

This lab is south across the river from Ron's Car Wash and Gas. Head over the bridge, across the junction, then keep an eye out to your left to spot where the laundry van is parked. The lab is just in the building beyond the gate with the smoking chimney. The door requires a code which is 1999 (referencing the billboard up to the right).

## So what's the reward?

![GTA 6 Kitty Litter - Tyrone&#039;s SMG](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/xMrK6tinvSiD7yuSHnYejC-1200-80.jpg)

There are three main advantages to destroying all the Kitty Litter labs and completing the Spin On This mission with Tyrone and those are:

* Tyrone gives you his unique W-10 gold smg (this is the sidearm variant). He'll call you and ask you to come to his apartment to get it.
* He'll start buying narcotics from you at the highest price possible.
* He'll give you some narcotic spots to hit in Vice City to steal some drugs.

You'll also get $2,000 a lab, plus $10,000 for the Spin On This mission, meaning $48,000 altogether. Since I did everything, it's unclear to me whether these rewards are for destroying every lab, or the mission specifically that you get after you destroy 15 of them. Either way, if you want all of this, destroy all the labs in the city and complete the mission.
