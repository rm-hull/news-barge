National Rail marked the level crossing disruption cleared at 7.57pm. Northern trains between York and Leeds via Harrogate now have a normal service.

**Update, 8.10pm:** National Rail marked the incident cleared at 7.57pm and now says “Normal service between York and Harrogate”. It says the disruption caused by the level crossing fault “has now ended”.**Update, 7.03pm:** National Rail now expects disruption until 8pm. It said 7pm earlier.

Earlier this evening, Northern trains between York and Harrogate could be cancelled or delayed by up to 25 minutes, National Rail said, after “a fault with the barriers at a level crossing between York and Knaresborough”.

* **What:** All lines reopened earlier in the evening. National Rail now reports a normal service.
* **Where:** Between York and Knaresborough, affecting Northern trains between York and Leeds via Harrogate.
* **When:** Reported at 5.02pm. National Rail marked it cleared at 7.57pm.
