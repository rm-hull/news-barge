Roast & Roam wants to serve coffee, pizza and ice cream from restored horseboxes at the Staveley Arms car park. A council decision is due by 28 December.

Roast & Roam Coffee Co. has asked North Yorkshire Council for permission to serve coffee, stone-baked pizza and ice cream from up to four restored 1970s Rice horsebox trailers on part of the Staveley Arms car park in North Stainley, near Ripon.

## Overview

* **What:** up to four matching 1970s Rice horsebox trailers, with seating under a stretch tent and two free games areas for families.
* **Where:** part of the Staveley Arms car park, North Stainley.
* **Planning:** application 26/05564/FUL with North Yorkshire Council, for a temporary three-year change of use. It is out for consultation now, and the target decision date is**28 December**.
* **Opening:** no date yet. The owners will set one once the council has decided.

The business is run by Drew McGilvray and Jade Overfield, who told Harrogate Live that everything in the plan is subject to planning permission.

## What the trailers will serve

The application covers up to four trailers, each with its own job:

* coffee and hot drinks
* stone-baked pizza
* ice cream
* a fourth position kept for another matching trailer, for example one serving locally sourced game

“They won’t all open at once,” the owners said.

![A cream horsebox trailer with wooden doors parked under a grey tent](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/roast-and-roam-north-stainley-horsebox-trailers-2.webp)

## Opening hours in the application

* Monday to Friday: 7am to 7pm
* Saturday: 7.30am to 5.30pm
* Sunday and bank holidays: 8.30am to 4.30pm
* Closed for four weeks in January

“We’ll trade in the daytime only, with no alcohol and no amplified music,” they said.

## Alongside the pub

The owners said the trailers are meant to sit alongside the Staveley Arms rather than compete with it. “We’re not trying to replace the pub,” Drew McGilvray and Jade Overfield said.

![A cream Land Rover strapped onto a flatbed trailer inside a vehicle deck](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/roast-and-roam-north-stainley-horsebox-trailers-3.webp)

## How to comment

Anyone who wants to support or object to the plan can find it on North Yorkshire Council’s planning search by entering the reference 26/05564/FUL. Comments are accepted while the consultation is open.
