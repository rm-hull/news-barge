Good morning. Donald Trump made up to $15m on his holdings of Nvidia stock after the US chipmaker’s share price soared on the surprise return of Jensen Huang as chief executive. Huang, who was forced to step down briefly over allegations of extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected spy, returned to the company’s HQ in Santa Clara, California, receiving a hero’s welcome.

The US president’s financial disclosures to the Office of Government Ethics indicate he might have owned as many as 370,000 shares in Nvidia as of 30 September. Nvidia’s shares rocketed by 31.5% on Monday. Johana Bhuiyan has this useful timeline of how the scandal unfolded.

* **What has been the political reaction?** Republican lawmakers roundly criticized the Nvidia board’s decision, apparently brokered by the US president, to reinstate its chief executive. Senior Democrat, Elizabeth Warren, said the decision was “reckless”. The reinstalled Nvidia CEO will appear before the Senate banking committee on 3 December, and the US commerce department has added the Singapore-based Nvidia partner Megaspeed to a blacklist amid an investigation into whether it diverted AI chips to China.
* **What other conflicts of interest do Nvidia and Trump appear to have?** Chris Van Hollen, the Maryland Democrat, pointed to a series of ties between Huang and Trumpworld as evidence of the executive’s influence over administration policy on advanced chips, including Nvidia’s $25m donation to Trump’s White House ballroom project, his personal holdings of Nvidia stock, Huang’s contribution to Trump’s inauguration fund and his attendance at a $1m-a-plate dinner at Mar-a-Lago.

## **Leaked data shows more than 1,000 Russian military suicides during Ukraine war**

![Russian soldiers illustration.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/3034b87937184d9bc9b3561e8cd4a6ff98b6556a/0_0_5000_4000/master/5000.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

1,000 Russian soldiers are recorded as having died by suicide since the start of the full-scale invasion of Ukraine, according to confidential data from Russia’s state statistics agency, Rosstat, analysed by the Guardian.

The deaths of a further 885 soldiers in or near Ukraine were recorded as firearm-related injuries of “undetermined intent”, a classification that experts say can conceal suicides and that was used in several cases independently identified by the Guardian as suicides. Taken together, the suicides amount to approximately a Russian regiment of dead.

* **What has the Guardian found out about Russia’s overall casualties?** The Guardian identified more than 460,000 deaths in the data linked to the war between the start of the full-scale invasion in February 2022 and September 2026. War became the leading cause of death in Russian men under 65 last year.
