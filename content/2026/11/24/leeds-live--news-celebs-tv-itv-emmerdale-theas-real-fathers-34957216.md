# ITV Emmerdale Thea's real father's identity 'exposed' as fans say 'surely'

## Emmerdale fans were left reeling after Steve Marchant dropped a bombshell during his dramatic return to the village on Tuesday's episode, revealing he cannot be Thea Ramsden's biological father

Steve Marchant's return to Emmerdale has been nothing short of explosive, with a shocking revelation that has turned everything on its head. During Tuesday's (November 24) episode, available on ITVX, viewers learnt that Steve (Paul Opacic) cannot possibly be Thea's biological father.

The controversial character made his comeback to the village in Monday's (November 24) instalment, reuniting with Kim for the first time in almost 30 years. Long-time viewers will recall the shady entrepreneur featured prominently on the ITV soap from 1996 to 1999, most notably as Kim's husband.

Their marriage came to a catastrophic end when Steve tried to undermine Kim by pinching a horse from a rival. The situation deteriorated when he ran over Kathy Bates (Malandara Burrows) in a hit-and-run incident, abandoning her to die.

He was eventually handed a 10-year stretch for attempted murder after Kim framed him, ensuring he took the fall for crimes they'd committed together.

Following their lengthy separation, he arrived back in the village nonchalantly greeting a gobsmacked Kim with: "Hello, darling. Miss me?" When Kim questioned his surprise appearance, he revealed: " Weirdly, I'm not after an apology - you know, for sending me to prison. I want an explanation. You know full well what I'm talking about. I was contacted by a solicitor. Something about a windfall inheritance.", reports the Daily Star.

Steve expressed his scepticism about the whole situation, struggling to understand why anyone would leave him an inheritance. His investigation soon pointed directly to Kim, leading him to confront her about the solicitor's real motives.

Kim disclosed: "Well, I can tell you that she's not a solicitor. She's a police officer. Detective. Turned up and tried to frame me for my step-daughter's death. That failed, but it's obsessed her. She wants me put away badly. Now she's digging up everything she can to get to me. And that appears to include you."

Kim went on to warn Steve that DS Thea Ramsden, the detective in question, could potentially uncover any "shady dealings" from his history. She then offered him cash on one strict condition - disappear and steer clear of any contact with the officer.

Kim seemed confident that her payment had successfully ensured Steve's permanent departure, but uncertainty crept in when he played back a voice message from Thea stating: "'Hi, it's Thea. 'Look, I hate to do it like this, but... I lied. I'll explain everything if you decide you want to meet, but for now, you need to know - I'm your daughter. 'So text me. Call me. Whatever."

In a dramatic showdown, Steve revealed to his stunned former wife Kim Tate (Claire King) that he cannot father children and never has been able to. Having already pocketed cash from Kim to keep his distance, Steve made it abundantly clear that he understands the significance of what he knows and anticipates this secret will command a much heftier price.

The episode concluded with Kim left reeling from the bombshell, prompting viewers to speculate whether Thea's biological father might already be a resident of the village. Fans flocked to X, formerly known as Twitter, with one writing: "So Thea's dad is Graham? #emmerdale."

Someone else commented: "I still wonder if by some twist Graham is really gonna be Thea's dad. I mean they do act alike. #emmerdale." A third viewer questioned: "Graham has got to be Thea's dad SURELY? ? ? #Emmerdale."

Long-time viewers will recall Graham Foster (Andrew Scarborough), Kim's former husband who was seemingly murdered by Pierce Harris in 2020, but it was confirmed earlier this year during the one-off Corriedale episode that he survived and remained hidden.
