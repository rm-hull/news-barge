**Update: Traffic is now running normally on the M62 following this morning's crash.**

A crash has closed three lanes of a Yorkshire motorway.

National Highways said it happened westbound between junction 30 and junction 29 of the M62 this morning (Tuesday, November 24).

This stretch of the motorway takes in the A642 turn-off for Wakefield and the intersection with the M1.




Traffic had been held, according to AA Traffic News but has now been released.

Motorists are facing delays of over 30 minutes with up to six miles of congestion and average speeds down to five miles per hour.

Delay are being reported all the way back to junction 31 of the M62 for Castleford.
