Nvidia's latest GeForce Game Ready driver, version 621.40, is out now. "This new Game Ready Driver provides the best gaming experience for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War 4, and Assetto Corsa Rally," the notes say, "and the addition of DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering to Where Winds Meet."

Which is kind of burying the lead. This patch is really a hotfix for a hotfix, an update that finally addresses the bug with previous driver packages that had them pointlessly rewriting a log file over and over, quickly adding up to tens of GB every hour and potentially damaging SSDs nobody can afford to replace any more.

Over the weekend Nvidia released a hotfix that supposedly caught the bug, only for users to discover it merely delayed its onset. Rather than setting in immediately after a reboot, it would wait until your PC had been on for a while before shitting the bed. If you're one of the people who leaves your computer running overnight then I hope you stayed on version 621.01, the last safe driver package before the current one.

Forum users have been testing out version 621.40 and it seems to have not only stopped the Nvidia container service from freaking out and rewriting its log, but also caught a bug with custom cursors that would make them turn gray. Which is nice.

I'm tempted to play it safe and stay on my old driver anyway, though I'll update for Dawn of War 4, which comes out on December 3.
