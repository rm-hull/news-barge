Footage filmed on **Tuesday 24 November** reveals the scale of the destruction in**Villavicencio**, southeast of Bogotá, after a car bomb exploded outside the Meta departmental** government headquarters** on Monday night. Collapsed building façades, burnt-out vehicles and streets strewn with shattered glass bear witness to the force of the blast. Several businesses and homes were damaged, including a shop selling religious images located near the cathedral and parish house.

The explosion comes amid a resurgence of violence in **Colombia**, fuelled by rival armed groups competing for control of lucrative illegal economies and trafficking networks, particularly along the** Venezuelan border**.

In **Cúcuta**,** hundreds of residents**, police officers and military personnel joined a candlelit march on** Monday**in solidarity with families affected by a recent attack on the city's police headquarters.** Three people**, including a** child**, were injured and** more than 80 homes**damaged, according to the regional government. Police chaplain** José Rodríguez** joined the procession, blessing a damaged home with holy water.

Authorities suspect the involvement of the **National Liberation Army (ELN)**, although responsibility has yet to be officially established. Figures from** Colombia's Defence Ministry**point to a growing use of** explosive drones**by armed groups, with** 358 attacks**recorded between** January and November 2026**,** 157**of them attributed to the** ELN**.
