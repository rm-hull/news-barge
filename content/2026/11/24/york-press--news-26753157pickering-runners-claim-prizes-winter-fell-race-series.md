*(Image: Newsquest)*

The Kilburn Kanter, a Long Distance Walking Association challenge, took place on a calm day with beautiful autumnal colours.

The hilly 24-mile route around the White Horse packed in 3,000 feet of climb.

Mike Wilson had a great race, finishing in 4 hours 36 minutes, placing him 7th out of 150.

Lucy Saggers and Nicola Thorpe enjoyed completing the event together, finishing in 5:35.

Carol Wilson and Sally Brown power walked their way round in 7:26, well over two hours ahead of the last walkers who crossed the line in 9:49.

The Hardmoors Goathland races took place the same weekend.

New to Pickering, Matt Tarry completed the 28-mile ultra marathon in 6 hours 38 minutes.

Bridget Houlston tackled the Half Marathon, 16 miles and 2,000 feet of climb, with gusto, earning her the trophy for 3rd female with her impressive time of 2:41.

Erin Outram crossed the line in 3:05.

Erin was followed by Mandy Hepton in 3:20 and Chris Adams in 3:29.

Sarah Beal smashed the 10k option, 7.6 miles and nearly 1,000 feet of climb, with her time of 1:20.

Then came Sarah and Malc Hodgson in 1:57 and Graham Robinson in 2:11.

Charlotte Dennison ensured all were back safely, with the last runner back in 3:19.

The latest race in the Winter Fell Race Series, The Saltergate Gallows, took place on Sunday.

At nearly 13 miles with 1,800 feet of steep climbs and technical descents, this is one of the toughest races in the series.

Veteran Dave Smith’s experience, strength and endurance saw him power home in 1 hour 45 minutes, placing him 2nd M55 and 28th out of 138.

He was closely followed by Simon Jones, who was 3rd M55 with his time of 1:46, and Mike Richmond, 1st M50 in 1:47.

Eight seconds later and new to Pickering, Jennifer Thompson stormed across the line in 1:47, earning her the 1st female prize.

Robbie Preston was next in 1:48.

He was followed by an exciting battle for the finish with Kelly Richmond, 2nd female, Johnny Everitt and Tom Pilley all clocking 1:50.

Next came Nick Outram in 1:55, Mike Wilson, 3rd M65 in 2:07, Lucy Saggers, 3rd F55 in 2:09, Ruth Kaftirani in 2:18, Jo Towler, 2nd F50 in 2:20, Lynn Humpleby in 2:30, Nick Cousins in 2:31, Serena Partridge in 2:37, Mandy Hepton in 2:39 and Debbie Rycroft in 2:57.

Pickering Ladies continue their dominance in the series, once again winning gold.

The event was organised by Kim Cavill and supported by many members of PRC in various volunteering roles.
