A Town Investment Plan is being developed for Thirsk to help identify local priorities and support future funding opportunities.

North Yorkshire Council is inviting residents, business owners and visitors to share their views on the town and how you think it could be improved.

There are two drop-in events where you can find out more:

**Thursday 26 November** from 10am to 4pm: Rural Arts,**Old Courthouse**, 4 Westgate, Thirsk YO7 1QS** Saturday 9 January**from 10am to 2pm:** Thirsk Community Library**, Chapel Street, Thirsk YO7 1TH

Or, have your say online at [https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/vision-thirsk](https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/vision-thirsk)

This work is supported by York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority
