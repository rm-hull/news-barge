500 knives were seized and more than 260 people were arrested in London during a crackdown by Metropolitan Police.

The force said some of the capital’s most dangerous offenders were arrested during the operation, which ran from 16 to 23 November.

A total of 266 arrests were made during the week-long crackdown, with 82 of those being brought before the courts as priority suspects.

The Met said one suspect arrested during the operation was charged with possession offences after 175 knives were found during a search of two addresses in east London.

Elsewhere, four men aged between 18 and 27 were charged with possessing offensive weapons after officers stopped a stolen vehicle in south London.

One of the suspects attempted to flee before being detained and was found with a machete, while a hunting knife, zombie-style knife and locking flick knife were also recovered.

![The Met said one suspect arrested during the operation was charged with possession offences after 175 knives were found during a search of two addresses in east London](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/10/2305163430..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Commander James Conway, the Met’s lead for knife crime, said: “Every knife we seize is a weapon that can’t be used to hurt someone.

“That’s why we’re relentlessly targeting knife crime at every level, from those carrying and using them on our streets to those importing and supplying dangerous weapons into the country.”

According to the Met, the organisation’s focus on reducing knife crime has led to a 16.6 per cent reduction in knife-enabled robbery over the last 12 months, while overall knife crime offences are down by 8.1 per cent.

Mr Conway said: “Our work is seeing results. Knife-enabled robbery is down by nearly 17 per cent over the last year and the number of young people admitted to London hospitals following a knife attack has more than halved since 2019.

“We’re using data and intelligence, technology and proactive policing to target offenders and prevent violence. But we’re not complacent.

“We’ll keep doing everything we can to drive down knife crime and keep Londoners safe.”
