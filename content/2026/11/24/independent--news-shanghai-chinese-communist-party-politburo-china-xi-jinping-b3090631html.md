Chinese Communist Party authorities are investigating Shanghai party secretary Chen Jining for suspected violations of party discipline, state media reported Tuesday. The Politburo member is the latest senior official targeted in President Xi Jinping’s long-running anti-corruption campaign.

Chen is under investigation by the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection. The brief announcement provided no further details.

The investigation follows sweeping purges under Xi, who has emphasized greater discipline in the Party. The Chinese leader has sustained an anti-corruption campaign for more than a decade, carrying out purges on a scale not seen since Mao Zedong’s rule.

The post of Shanghai party secretary has traditionally been viewed as a stepping stone to China’s top leadership. The country’s current premier, Li Qiang, previously held the position.

Chen has served as Shanghai party secretary since 2022. He previously held senior positions in Beijing’s municipal government and headed the Ministry of Environmental Protection.
