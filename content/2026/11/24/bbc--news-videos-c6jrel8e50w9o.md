# Nuclear test veterans speak to BBC about search for justice

Nuclear test veterans and their relatives have reacted to the prime minister's announcement of an investigation into the health legacy of Britain's Cold War-era atomic programme.

Andy Burham said a panel will assess the potential impact of testing exercises in the 1950s and 1960s on troops, adding that the process will be treated with the "utmost seriousness".

The BBC heard reaction from veterans and their families as part of Newsnight's 'Nuclear Test Veterans: The Newsnight Special'.

The Ministry of Defence (MoD) has for decades denied it covered up the impact of radiation on troops ordered to carry out nuclear weapons tests.
