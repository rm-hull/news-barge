Plans have gone in to transform one of York’s best-known riverside bars into an “après-ski party venue” – and potentially a second late-night pub under the same roof.

What is now Revolution, next to City Screen on Coney Street, looks set for its next chapter as Barbara’s Bier Haus, a growing chain of Alpine-themed party bars operated by Neos Hospitality.

Promising “après-ski vibes, zero altitude required,” Barbara’s Bier Haus already operates eight venues across the UK, including in Leeds, Newcastle, Cardiff, and Birmingham.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/revolution-coney-street-york.webp)

The brand is known for two-pint steins, beer towers, cocktails, pizzas and hotdogs, alongside live music, karaoke, music bingo, and big-screen sport.

Neos Hospitality Ltd has submitted a planning application to City of York Council for The Old Herald Building on Coney Street, currently home to Revolution.

Interestingly, the detailed drawings submitted with the application also show plans for a second Neos Hospitality brand, Bonnie Rogues Pub.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/barbaras-bier-haus-pd-1.webp)

This may suggest the sprawling building could be split into two distinct venues, or feature a dedicated pub and garden bar area alongside the ski lodge concept.

Bonnie Rogues is billed by the operator as a bold late-night pub concept, with a site recently opening on Call Lane in Leeds.

According to the planning documents, the proposals involve a “significant investment” focused on refurbishing the ground-floor Coney Street frontage and revamping the outdoor customer areas.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/revolution-bar-york.jpg)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/barbaras-bier-haus-pd-2.webp)

Changes to the exterior include:

* **A new-look entrance:** Heritage-blue decoration, timber cladding, and new lighting will be added to the Coney Street entrance, alongside an illuminated yellow projecting sign for Barbara’s Bier Haus.
* **Upgraded riverside seating:** The outdoor area overlooking the River Ouse will get a new timber pergola, replacement dark green and blue retractable awnings, and updated seating.
* **A revamped terrace:** The existing side terrace will be fitted with new decking, fixed seating, planters, and an aluminium pergola with sliding glass doors to provide shelter from the weather.
* **Pub signage:** Traditional timber hanging signs and a neon-effect ‘Garden bar’ sign for Bonnie Rogues are also included in the designs, along with hand-painted murals on the brickwork.

The venue, which sits opposite the rear of St Martin le Grand and overlooks the River Ouse, has a long history in the city.

Built between 1815 and 1820, it famously served as the home for the York Herald and later the Yorkshire Evening Press until the newspaper relocated to Walmgate in 1989. Following a decade of lying empty, it was redeveloped alongside the City Screen Picturehouse in 2000 before becoming Revolution.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/barbaras-bier-haus-pd-3.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/barbaras-bier-haus-pd-4.webp)

The application says the changes are mostly cosmetic and will not alter the building’s historic stonework.

Crucially, the original Yorkshire Herald stone lettering overlooking the River Ouse will remain completely untouched and visible.

“The continued occupation and maintenance of the building will contribute positively to the vitality of Coney Street and the River Ouse frontage,” the heritage statement notes, adding that the improvements will help secure the ongoing commercial viability of the site.
