The fire broke out on **21 November** at the**Fonusa Agung Mulia** factory in**Indonesia’s Banten province**, west of** Jakarta**. Aerial footage filmed on** Monday** shows collapsed roofs and large sections of the complex reduced to ruins.

The death toll has risen to **26**, with** eight workers**still missing. Most victims were recovered from the** second floor**, where rescuers said many had died trying to reach an emergency exit.** More than 600 emergency personnel**have been mobilised, with unstable structures hampering searches across the** 2,000-square-metre**area affected by the blaze. Police are collecting** DNA samples** from relatives to identify the victims.

Preliminary reports suggest an **electrical fault** in an air-conditioning unit may have sparked the fire, although the cause remains unconfirmed.

The company, which produces the popular **Milkita** confectionery brand, has apologised and pledged support for victims’ families. The disaster has renewed concerns over industrial fire safety in**Indonesia**.
