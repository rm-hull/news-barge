## York buffet judged the 'best in Britain'

Plenty of choice at Panda Mami *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A buffet restaurant in York has been named the best in the UK.

Panda Mami on Bridge Street received the title of Best All-You-Can-Eat Buffet Restaurant in the United Kingdom 2026 at the International Restaurant Awards in Dubai.

The award recognises the restaurant group’s commitment to food quality, choice, and customer service.

Abi Zhan, manager of Panda Mami York, said: "To be recognised as the best all-you-can-eat buffet restaurant in the entire UK is an unbelievable achievement for the whole Panda Mami family.

"What makes this year even more special is that we have been travelling across the country and internationally, attending different award ceremonies and seeing the hard work of our teams recognised.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21759010.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Panda Mami York at the Food Awards in Wolverhampton *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"We are incredibly proud of everyone who has contributed to these achievements.

"Our team works hard every day to give our customers the best possible experience, and to see that recognised on such a big stage is truly special."

The Panda Mami team attended the awards ceremony in Dubai on Saturday, November 14.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21758962.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chris Goulding of Panda Mami York *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The restaurant group has enjoyed a string of successes throughout 2026, marking the latest milestone in an extraordinary year for the restaurant group.

Earlier in 2026, Panda Mami was named Best Buffet at the Asian Restaurant Awards in Manchester.

The team also attended The Food Awards England in Wolverhampton in early November, adding another memorable occasion to an already successful year.

Christopher Goulding, assistant manager of Panda Mami York, said: "This is an incredible achievement.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21759041.jpg?type=mds-article-620)More than 40 choices of dessert at Panda Mami York *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

"The team throughout the whole of Panda Mami works incredibly hard to deliver quality, variety, and top-tier service.

"2026 has been a fantastic year for Panda Mami York and all our other branches around the UK.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21759130.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plenty of choice at Panda Mami *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"To have success at the Asian Restaurant Awards in Manchester, The Food Awards England in Wolverhampton, and now the International Restaurant Awards in Dubai is something we are all incredibly proud of.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21759105.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The entrance to Panda Mami *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

"This national trophy belongs just as much to our loyal local customers in York and all over the UK, who support us week in, week out.

"We couldn't have achieved this without our customers and the dedication of our fantastic team.

"Everyone should be incredibly proud."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21759080.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plenty of customers at Panda Mami- even midweek! *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The International Restaurant Awards are judged by an independent panel and consider key areas such as cuisine, service, ambience, consistency, innovation, and overall guest experience.

Recognition at this level highlights the group’s reputation not only within the UK but also on a global stage.

With three major award ceremonies and recognition spanning the UK and beyond, 2026 has undoubtedly been a year to remember for Panda Mami.

Indeed, the restaurant performs well on the reviews websites, gaining 4.2 stars out of five on both Google and TripAdvisor .

It was also praised in a recent review from The Press.
