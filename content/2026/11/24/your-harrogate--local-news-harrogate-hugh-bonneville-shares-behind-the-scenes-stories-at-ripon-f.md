Hugh Bonneville drew a full house at Holy Trinity Church in Ripon on Saturday evening for a theatre festival fundraiser.

The Downton Abbey actor joined Anna Wallace to talk about his latest Rory Sparkes children’s book and share stories from behind the scenes of his acting career.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b0546dd538d05f5ba6.png)

After the talk, fans met Hugh and had their copies signed at an event hosted by The Little Ripon Bookshop.

Festival organisers said:

> “We are so grateful to Hugh for sharing an evening with us and helping theatre to thrive in Ripon.”

The fundraiser was organised in partnership with Cause UK.
