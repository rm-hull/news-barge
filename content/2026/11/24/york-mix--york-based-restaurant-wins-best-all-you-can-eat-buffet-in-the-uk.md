Panda Mami York is celebrating a remarkable year of success.

The York-based restaurant on Bridge Street is part of the independent restaurant group recognised at the International Restaurant Awards in Dubai, where Panda Mami was crowned ‘Best All-You-Can-Eat Buffet Restaurant in the United Kingdom 2026’.

The team travelled to Dubai on Saturday 14 November to attend the prestigious ceremony and celebrate the achievement, marking the latest milestone for the restaurant group.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Inernational-awards-Dubai-1.webp)

The award represents recognition at an international level for a restaurant group rooted in Yorkshire.

The international recognition celebrates Panda Mami’s commitment to food quality, choice and customer service, with an independent judging panel assessing restaurants across six key categories: cuisine, service, ambience, consistency, innovation and overall guest experience.

However, the Dubai award is just the latest success for Panda Mami York, which has enjoyed a hat-trick of award recognition throughout 2026.

Earlier in the year, the restaurant was recognised at the Asian Restaurant Awards in Manchester, taking home the award for Best Buffet.

The team then travelled to Wolverhampton at the beginning of November to attend The Food Awards England, adding another memorable occasion to an already successful year.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/panda-mami-york-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/panda-mami-york-4.webp)

Now, with further recognition on the international stage in Dubai, Panda Mami York and its colleagues across the UK have plenty to celebrate.

Abi Zhan, manager of Panda Mami York, said: “To be recognised as the best all-you-can-eat buffet restaurant in the entire UK is an unbelievable achievement for the whole Panda Mami family.

“What makes this year even more special is that we have been travelling across the country and internationally, attending different award ceremonies and seeing the hard work of our teams recognised.

“We are incredibly proud of everyone who has contributed to these achievements. Our team works hard every day to give our customers the best possible experience, and to see that recognised on such a big stage is truly special.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/panda-mami-york-6.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/panda-mami-york-5.webp)

Christopher Goulding, assistant manager of Panda Mami York, added: “This is an incredible achievement. The team throughout the whole of Panda Mami works incredibly hard to deliver quality, variety and top-tier service.

“2026 has been a fantastic year for Panda Mami York and all our other branches around the UK. To have success at the Asian Restaurant Awards in Manchester, The Food Awards England in Wolverhampton and now the International Restaurant Awards in Dubai is something we are all incredibly proud of.

“This national trophy belongs just as much to our loyal local customers in York and all over the UK, who support us week in, week out.

“We couldn’t have achieved this without our customers and the dedication of our fantastic team. Everyone should be incredibly proud.”
