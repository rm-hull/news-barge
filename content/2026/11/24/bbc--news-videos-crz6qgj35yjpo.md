# Would you let Meta's AI agent run parts of your life?

Mark Zuckerberg's tech company Meta, which runs Facebook and Instagram, is making a big bet on artificial intelligence.

On Wednesday it released Muse, an AI agent which doesn't just respond to questions, but can carry out tasks for you. With Muse, Meta gives the examples of "searching for a cheaper energy deal" or "sorting the school calendar".

The BBC's technology and AI editor Zoe Kleinman set up her own Muse - called Linda - and set it to work running her life for a week ahead of its UK launch.

But how useful was it?
