A police operation to clamp down on uninsured vehicles delivered a more than 20 per cent increase in the number of seized units compared to the last event it carried out.




Humberside Police said officers seized 82 vehicles, including 14 e-scooters, in last week’s Operation Drive Insured.

The total was 15 higher than during the previous intensification week on the same theme, police said.

Police Constable Susan Scott said: “I am really pleased to see these results from the national campaign our officers took part in.

“The public are the ones who are forced to pay the price of uninsured drivers, and the law is clear, it is the driver’s responsibility to ensure they are driving with insurance, whether it is a company vehicle or their own.”
