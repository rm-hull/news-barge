* **Nothing teases Ear (note), new earbuds**
* **They're earbuds that record and transcribe your conversations**
* **Full release scheduled for December 8**

Nothing has begun making some possibly concerning dalliances with AI tech, including its Nothing Ear (3a), which can record and transcribe calls or take 'Audio Snapshots' from voice notes you've been sent, or the Nothing Ear (3), with a Super Mic built into the case.

But until now, said buds have always played actual music too. Now, it sounds as if the company is ditching that aspect of its earbuds.

Nothing has begun teasing the new Ear (note), sharing some details with Bloomberg ahead of a full announcement on December 8. This looks set to be a transcription device, and many of these AI-toting gadgets have been coming to market over the last few years.

According to Bloomberg, both the earbuds and the case will have microphones embedded, and you can use them to record things around you. A companion app will let you access the recordings and their transcripts, all provided courtesy of AI.

A light indicator will show people that you're recording, though that's quite a half-baked measure to address the security concerns people have around this kind of spyware tech (not proving especially popular with those glasses, is it?), although Nothing has promised more ways it'll address security concerns. And we'll likely hear more about those at the full launch event.
