## If approved by member states, new round of EU sanctions will mark the 22nd EU sanctions package since February 2022. US chipmaker Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. After the announcement, the price for Nvidia shares skyrocketed.

## On today's show:

The European Commission has proposed a new round of sanctions against Russia to weaken the war machine and force Moscow into concessions at the negotiating table. We speak about their impact with **Vladyslav Vlasyuk, Presidential Commissioner for Sanctions Policy of Ukraine.** The European Parliament will debate this Tuesday the restoration of the rule of law and fundamental EU values in Hungary. After Viktor Orbán's 16-year rule, we welcome**German Green MEP Daniel Freund** and we ask him if Hungary's new government has already done enough or there is more to be done.

Twenty years after the poisoning of Alexander Litvinenko in London, we speak with his widow **Marina Litvinenko.** And in a stuning turn, just six days after Nvidia's board publicly ruled out bringing Jensen Huang back, the company has reinstated its co-founder as CEO with immediate effect.
