An air fryer offers a convenient way to prepare everything from golden roast potatoes to quick weeknight suppers without relying on a full-size oven. Yet with compact single-basket units, dual-drawer models, and countertop air fryer ovens all competing for your attention, finding the right fit requires careful consideration.

We have rigorously tested dozens of air fryers to identify the models that truly deserve a place in your kitchen. Our evaluations focus on heat distribution, achieving a crisp exterior with a tender interior, intuitive controls, effortless cleaning, and practical capacity without overcrowding. We also assess overall value, recognizing that a higher price point does not automatically translate to superior cooking performance.

Capacity is a key factor if you plan to host family and friends over the festive period. While a dual-basket model allows you to synchronize separate dishes, a smaller fryer is often more practical and efficient for everyday meals. Additionally, for households mindful of energy consumption, an air fryer provides a far more economical alternative to heating a standard oven for smaller portions.

Grounded in our testing findings, our recommendations accommodate a wide spectrum of budgets, kitchen layouts, and culinary habits, ensuring you find a worthwhile investment whether you are preparing quick everyday meals or catering for a crowd.

With most air fryers, the more generous the cooking capacity, the more counter space you'll need to sacrifice. This Ninja model cleverly avoids that issue by stacking its baskets one on top of the other, rather than side-by-side. It's such a smart design, we gave it the prize for Best Air Fryer in the 2024 TechRadar Choice Awards. Each drawer holds 5qts / 4.75L, and you can create even more cooking space by slotting in one of the provided trays. Although do note that the tall-rather-than-wide approach means you won't get able to fit in, say, an uncut pizza. In our review, our tester noted no compromise in performance with this new design approach – the Double Stack delivers the same excellent performance as other Ninja air fryers we've tested. There's also virtually no heat leakage between the two trays, which is good news if you’re dealing with precise foods or don't want to waste energy when only using one basket.

The main down-side here is that the Double Stack is pricier than other dual-basket air fryers, so if you don't need to save on counter space you could get something as good for a lower price. But if you have a small kitchen, it could be well worth the extra investment.
