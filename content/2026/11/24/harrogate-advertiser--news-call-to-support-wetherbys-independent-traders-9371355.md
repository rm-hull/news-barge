Owners of an independent bookshop in Wetherby are urging shoppers to support the town’s individual businesses in the run up to Christmas.

Since opening in 2024, Kemps on the Corner, has worked to earn itself a standing in the community and last year won the town’s Best Dressed Christmas Window competition and supported this year’s Drover’s Day and Wetherby Bandstand events.

Co-Owner Jonathan Wurr said: “We are so pleased to be at the heart of the Wetherby community.

“We believe we are more than just a shop and want to support as much as possible in our local area.”

Jonathan and partner Liz Kemp, who grew up in Bramham and opened the Wetherby shop as an extension of her Malton store, hope local residents will support their high street.

“Our team hope more locals will rediscover the pleasure of shopping independently, supporting their high street, and finding something wonderful just around the corner,” said Jonathan.

“Shopping in one of the amazing independent shops in Wetherby feeds money back into our community.

“We love helping people with their Christmas purchases, whether it is a difficult-to-buy-for grandchild, or a 'has-everything-already' husband.

“Unlike online sellers, friendly and enthusiastic booksellers are always on hand to recommend a brilliant read, a thoughtful gift, or simply chat about books.”

Owner Liz Kemp said they enjoy being part of Wetherby’s thriving town centre.

“It’s a great place to have a bookshop, with a lovely community and fantastic independent businesses.

“We’re looking forward to taking part in the Christmas Window competition again this year and seeing the town looking festive!”
