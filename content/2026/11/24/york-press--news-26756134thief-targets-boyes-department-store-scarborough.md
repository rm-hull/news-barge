A thief has targeted a department store in a North Yorkshire town.




The county's police force said the theft took place at a Boyes in Scarborough on Thursday, October 22 at 9.10am and involved the theft of clothing.

North Yorkshire Police released a CCTV image of a person the force said it would like to speak to, saying that they may have information which could assist its investigation.

In an appeal to the public for assistance, police said anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online or by calling 101, quoting reference 12260208576.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
