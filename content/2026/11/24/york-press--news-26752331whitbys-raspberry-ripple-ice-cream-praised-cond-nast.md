A Yorkshire seaside town has been praised for its “nostalgic” dessert that is a must-try when visiting the area.

Condé Nast Traveller has released its own pudding map highlighting some of the best and most "iconic" desserts around Britain.

While many of the puddings can be enjoyed anywhere across Britain, a few are linked to specific areas where you must try the dessert.

Found along the Yorkshire coastline, Whitby made it onto the list with its famous raspberry ripple ice cream.

## Why you should try a raspberry ripple in Whitby

On the dessert, Condé Nast Traveller said: “Iconic berry-based desserts are British summertime.

“Beloved strawberries and cream; berry pavlova; even a classic Victoria sponge.

“Make mine a nostalgic raspberry ripple ice cream: its seductively rich and creamy vanilla and fruity magenta layers are summer in dessert form and hark back to childhood beach holidays in the UK.

“An authentic raspberry ripple is homemade vanilla ice cream with swirls or ribbons of raspberry purée running through it – hence the “ripple”.

“It’s actually surprisingly easy to make at home with the right ingredients.

“The fun dessert's origins may date back to the 1930s in the USA before its widespread popularity in the UK, though some reports crown a Cornishman, Bernard Longhurst, with naming the delicious raspberry version in a competition on our shores.”

To find the best raspberry ripple according to Condé Nast Traveller, you will have to travel a little further away from the coast to Beacon Farm Ice Cream.

All of the ice cream is made on-site using fresh whole milk, double cream, and a range of other ingredients to create different flavours.

The flavours are constantly changing, meaning you can try something different each time you visit.

Alongside the raspberry ripple that is a Whitby staple, you can also get flavours like vanilla, bubble-gum, cappuccino, salted caramel, and Biscoff.

There are a few sorbets on offer, including mango, lemon, and pineapple.

On the shop, the publication added: “All Beacon’s dairy ice cream is made using fresh whole milk, double cream and natural fruit pastes, for an elite ice cream experience.

“It speaks volumes that they make enough ice cream for over 11,000 cones a day during summer. Whitby’s calling.”
