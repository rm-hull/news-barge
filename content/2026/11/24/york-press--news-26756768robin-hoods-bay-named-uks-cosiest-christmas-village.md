A North Yorkshire beauty spot has been named the UK’s cosiest village for Christmas, topping a list of more than 200 contenders in a study this month.

Robin Hood’s Bay on the Yorkshire coast, known for its historic pubs and winding smugglers’ lanes, claimed the top spot in a study of 211 UK towns and villages.

The study ranked locations using seven criteria, including the number of independent cafés, pubs with real fires, and the frequency of 'cosy' descriptions in visitor reviews.




The village is home to nearly 200 historic buildings within a kilometre of its centre — the highest number among any contender in the UK top 10.

It edged out Alfriston in East Sussex, with a score of 83.6 out of 100, while Polperro in Cornwall placed third.

Other North Yorkshire locations also made the list, including Staithes in ninth place and Hawes, which ranked third among villages in the county itself.

Nearly four in 10 visitor reviews for the village included words associated with warmth and comfort.

One visitor described the atmosphere at The Bay Hotel, located beside the sea wall, saying: "The fire is always on when it’s cold."

The top 10 cosiest villages in the UK according to the study - carried out by hot drinks specialist Liquidline are: -

* Robin Hood’s Bay, North Yorkshire
* Alfriston, East Sussex
* Polperro, Cornwall
* Mousehole, Cornwall
* Culross, Fife
* Cley next the Sea, Norfolk
* Tintern, Monmouthshire
* Beddgelert, Gwynedd
* Staithes, North Yorkshire
* Aberdaron, Gwynedd
