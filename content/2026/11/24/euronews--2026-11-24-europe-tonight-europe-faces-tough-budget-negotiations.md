## To debrief on the EU's passing of its budget, our correspondent Maria Tadeo spoke to Slovak MEP for Progressive Slovakia within the Renew Europe group Lucia Yar on Euronews' new weekly evening show Europe Tonight.

Budget talks are never easy, and this is especially the case when it comes to the European Union. A major clash is underway over the EU’s next seven-year budget, pitting the European Parliament against EU member states and splitting governments over spending cuts and agriculture. Probably the trickiest new fault line is the future financing of Ukraine.

Our correspondent Maria Tadeo spoke to Slovak MEP Lucia Yar, of Progressive Slovakia within the Renew Europe group, on Euronews’ new weekly evening show Europe Tonight. Watch them unpick the issues.
