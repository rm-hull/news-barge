Across Harrogate, parents of children as young as three will already be looking ahead at primary school options for next year

Although the 2026/27 school year has only just begun, applications for a place at a state-funded primary school for 2027/28 are open now

While families have until January to get these in, many schools are already hosting open days and visits for next year’s new starters

With this in mind, we have taken a look at the highest-achieving primary schools in the Harrogate

It is based on the proportion of pupils at each school who completed Year 6 in the most recent school year (currently 2024/25) and achieved the Government’s expected standards in reading, writing, and mathematics

According to the data, the North Yorkshire average is 60 per cent, while the England average is 62 per cent

These are the 19 schools from across Harrogate that came out on top…
