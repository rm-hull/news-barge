US President Donald Trump says he spoke directly with Nvidia's board before the reinstatement of chief executive Jensen Huang, who resigned less than two weeks ago amid a scandal involving an alleged affair with a woman suspected of having links to Chinese intelligence.

In an interview with CNBC on Monday, Trump said he had contacted board members because he considered Nvidia strategically important to the United States.

"I'I found the board to be very, very reasonable," Trump said.

Nvidia announced on Monday that its board had unanimously voted to reinstate Huang as president and CEO, effective immediately.

The decision came after the board initially said it did not intend to bring him back, despite Huang expressing his willingness to return.
