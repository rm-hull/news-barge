A community drama group in Snaith is making its voice heard louder and clearer thanks to new equipment funded by a £2,000 grant.

Snaith & Cowick Drama Group used the funding from the Banks Group’s Banks Community Fund to purchase two radio microphones, two laptops, a smoke machine, and new stage drapes.

The group, which is celebrating its 20th anniversary, stages two productions each year and is currently preparing for its winter pantomime, Rumpelstiltskin, scheduled for January.

Shaun Morrill, a member of Snaith & Cowick Drama Group, said: "The funding we’ve had from Banks has given us a real boost as we build up to pantomime season."

There are more than 40 members, aged 11 to 89, who take on roles both on and off stage, with shows held at Snaith School.

Jamilah Hassan, community relations manager at the Banks Group, said: "The shows that the Snaith & Cowick Drama Group puts on are real highlights in the community calendar and we hope the funding we’ve provided helps to make this year’s Christmas pantomime the best they’ve ever done."

The Banks Group is also behind plans for a major housing development to the west of Eggborough, which would include up to 1,500 new homes.
