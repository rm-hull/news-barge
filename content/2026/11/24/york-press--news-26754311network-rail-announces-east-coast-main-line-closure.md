## Major East Coast Main Line closure to disrupt travel across Yorkshire

Network Rail is warning passengers that major travel disruption is expected as engineering works close part of the East Coast Main Line *(Image: Network Rail)*

Passengers are being warned that major travel disruption is expected as engineering works close part of the East Coast Main Line.

No trains will run between Peterborough or Letchworth Garden City and London on Saturday, November 28, and Sunday, November 29.

Rail replacement buses and coaches will be in operation, and passengers are advised to check their journeys in advance and allow extra time.

Ricky Barsby, head of access integration at Network Rail, said: "We’ve begun to introduce digital in-cab signalling to the East Coast Main Line and we’re getting ready to extend its use to more services.

"We’re sorry to anyone whose journey will be affected."

A spokesperson for the train operators added: "We’re working with Network Rail to help passengers plan their journeys while these important upgrades take place.

"No trains will be able to run on the East Coast Main Line between Peterborough or Letchworth Garden City and London on 28-29 November."

Passengers travelling to and from London from Yorkshire and the North East will face longer journeys.

The engineering works are part of the £1.4 billion East Coast Digital Programme, which will upgrade signalling technology, tracks, and station facilities.

Passengers using rail replacement services are reminded to bring only luggage they can comfortably carry.
