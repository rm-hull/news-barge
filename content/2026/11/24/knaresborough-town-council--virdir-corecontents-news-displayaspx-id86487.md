# Bright Friday 27 November 2026

*Published: 24 November 2026*As Bright Friday approaches, excitement is building for one of Knaresborough's most anticipated community events of the year. Taking place on Friday 27 November, Bright Friday marks the start of the festive season and promises an evening filled with community spirit, entertainment and Christmas celebrations in the heart of the town.**Bright Friday 2026** Residents and visitors are invited to join family and friends for a festive evening celebrating everything that makes Knaresborough special.**Event ProgrammeLantern Workshop  -** Create your own lantern and take part in the colourful parade through the town. The workshop takes place at Gracious Street Methodist Church from 4:30pm.**Live Entertainment -** The Knaresborough Town Council stage in the Market Place will host a programme of live entertainment from 5:15pm.**Lantern Parade -** The parade will set off from the War Memorial in the Castle Grounds at 6:00pm, bringing a colourful procession through the town centre.**Christmas Lights Switch-On -** Gather in the Market Place from approximately 6:30pm as Knaresborough's Christmas lights are officially switched on, illuminating the town for the festive season ahead.**Supporting Our Town Centre -** Bright Friday is a wonderful opportunity to support local businesses, meet friends and neighbours, and enjoy the unique atmosphere of Knaresborough as the town comes together to celebrate the start of Christmas.**Road Closures -** Please note that road closures will be in place in and around the Market Place from 12 noon on Friday 27 November. Residents and visitors are advised to allow extra time for their journeys and follow all event signage and steward directions.**We look forward to welcoming you to Knaresborough for an evening of festive entertainment, community celebration and the official start of the Christmas season. We hope to see you there.**

Bright Friday this year has been
supported by David Skaith, Mayor of York and North Yorkshire and funded by York
and North Yorkshire Combined Authority through the Vibrant and Sustainable High
Streets Fund.
