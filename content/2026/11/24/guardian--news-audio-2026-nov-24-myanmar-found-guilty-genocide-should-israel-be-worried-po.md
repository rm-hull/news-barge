For seven years, **Yasmin Ullah** had been part of the delegation arguing that Myanmar had committed genocide – one of the gravest human rights crimes – against Rohingya Muslims.

The genocide has directly affected Yasmin and her family. Last week she was in court to hear the international court of justice’s landmark verdict against Myanmar. “I was really kind of pinching myself. It felt like we were all able to breathe again,” she tells **Annie Kelly.** The verdict could have far-reaching consequences – especially for Israel, which is also facing a genocide case at the ICJ. The Guardian journalist**Kaamil Ahmed** explains how the case unfolded and what could happen next.
