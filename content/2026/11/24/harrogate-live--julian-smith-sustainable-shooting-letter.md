Sir Julian Smith MP has signed a letter to the Environment Secretary backing sustainable shooting. MPs and peers want a meeting on the future of the sector.

Sir Julian Smith, the MP for Skipton and Ripon, has signed a letter from MPs and peers to the Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs on the importance of sustainable shooting to the countryside, conservation and rural jobs. His seat takes in Ripon and Nidderdale.

The letter raises concerns that further restrictions or licensing requirements could be introduced following the Government’s call for evidence on rearing, releasing and shooting.

## Overview

* **What:** a letter from MPs and peers to the Environment Secretary on sustainable shooting.
* **The concern:** further restrictions or licensing requirements after the Government’s call for evidence on rearing, releasing and shooting.
* **The ask:** that the Secretary of State meets the APPG for Shooting and Conservation to discuss the future of the sector.
* **Local MP:** Sir Julian Smith, Skipton and Ripon, is one of the signatories.

## What Sir Julian said

“Across North Yorkshire, shooting supports jobs, local businesses and the wider rural economy, while also helping to deliver conservation and habitat management work,” he said.

“Those who live and work in our countryside deserve confidence that decisions affecting their livelihoods will be based on evidence and a proper understanding of the benefits that sustainable shooting brings to rural communities. That is why I was pleased to support this letter and call for engagement with those involved in the sector.”

## What the letter asks for

The group has invited the Secretary of State to meet the All-Party Parliamentary Group for Shooting and Conservation, to discuss the future of the sector and to give reassurance to people involved in shooting and conservation work across the country.

Last week Sir Julian asked ministers to put the Visitor Levy bill on hold.
