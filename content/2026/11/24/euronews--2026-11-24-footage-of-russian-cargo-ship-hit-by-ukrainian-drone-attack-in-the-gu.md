A damaged Russian cargo ship hit by a drone attack in the Gulf of Finland has been towed back to Russian waters, according to images released by Russia's Transport Ministry on Monday.

The Sparta II was struck on Saturday, 21 November, while sailing towards the Russian exclave of Kaliningrad. The attack sparked a fire on board and forced the evacuation of its 16 crew members.

Finnish military officials said Ukraine was likely behind the attack, although Kyiv has not publicly claimed responsibility.

The vessel drifted into Finnish waters after the strike, prompting a joint response involving Finnish, Estonian and Russian rescue teams. The fire was brought under control before the ship was towed away.

Russia's Transport Ministry said water was sprayed over the damaged vessel to prevent the fire from reigniting. The ship subsequently returned to the Saint Petersburg area.

The Sparta II has been linked to Russia's military logistics network and is under US and Ukrainian sanctions. Its cargo at the time of the attack has not been publicly confirmed.
