Daisy Mays in Harrogate’s Victoria Shopping Centre has won an advertising campaign worth up to £1,000 on Your Harrogate.

The card and gift shop has been trading since 1981.

Alongside its greetings cards, it sells gift wrap, helium balloons and party supplies for birthdays, weddings and anniversaries.

Our team will now work with Daisy Mays to create a campaign to introduce the shop to more people across the Harrogate district.

Daisy Mays follows Knaresborough lingerie and swimwear boutique Sheer Bliss, which was announced as a winner in September.

There’s two more chance for a local business to win in November and December.

*To enter, business owners can tell us about their business via the Your Harrogate**website**.*
