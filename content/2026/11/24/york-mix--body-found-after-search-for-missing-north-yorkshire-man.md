Emergency teams have discovered a man’s body following an extensive search.

Crews located the body yesterday after launching a major rescue operation to find the missing Knaresborough man the previous evening.

Police say that officers do not believe there are any suspicious circumstances surrounding the death, and they are now preparing a file for the coroner.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Sadly, we can confirm that the body of a man has been found in Saltburn.

“Following an extensive overnight search involving North Yorkshire Police, Cleveland Police and HM Coastguard, a body was located this morning.

“While formal identification has not yet taken place, it is believed to be that of a man who was reported missing from the Knaresborough area yesterday evening.

“His family has been informed.

“Our thoughts remain with them at this extremely difficult time. The death is not being treated as suspicious, and a file will be prepared for the Coroner.”

The multi-agency search effort brought together officers from both North Yorkshire and Cleveland police forces alongside extensive maritime support.

The Whitby Coastguard spokesperson said: “Redcar, Whitby and Staithes Coastguard Rescue Teams, Redcar RNLI inshore and all-weather lifeboats and the Coastguard helicopter attended an incident at Saltburn.”
