North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following an attempted motorcycle theft in Knaresborough.

The incident happened at approximately 8.14pm on Sunday (November 22), when someone attempted to steal a motorcycle from a driveway on Frogmire Close.

Following the incident, the suspects were seen running onto Frogmire Road before making off along the footpath towards the railway line and Stockwell Road.

If you witnessed anything suspicious in the area during this time, or have any CCTV, doorbell, dash cam, or other video footage that may assist the police with their enquiries, you should get in touch.

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260229335 when providing any details regarding the incident.
