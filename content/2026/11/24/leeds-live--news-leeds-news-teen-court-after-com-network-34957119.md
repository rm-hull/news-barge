# Teen in court after Com network probe into 'serious online harm'

## The teenage boy is due in court on December 22

A Leeds teenager is due in court next month charged with several offences following an investigation into activity connected to serious online harm - also known as the Com networks.

The 17-year-old boy, who cannot be named for legal reasons, has been charged with one count of participating in the criminal activities of an organised crime group, one count of blackmail, one count of arrange/facilitate the sexual exploitation of a child aged 13 to 17 years, one count of cause/incite a girl 13 to 15 to engage in sexual activity, one count of attempt to cause/incite the sexual exploitation of a child aged 13 to 17 and one count of distribute an indecent photograph/pseudo-photograph of a child.

He is due to appear at Leeds Youth Court on December 22.
