Biffy Clyro are heading back to the North Yorkshire coast next summer for an unmissable headline show.

The Scottish rock icons will make a welcome return to Scarborough Open Air Theatre on **Wednesday 21 July 2027**, marking their first appearance at the historic coastal venue since 2019.

Scarborough will be their only UK headline show outside of festival appearances in 2027.

Biffy Clyro will be joined on the night by special guests Bleech 9:3.

Tickets will go on sale at 10am this Friday via the Scarborough Open Air Theatre website.

With four UK Number 1 albums to their name Biffy Clyro are one of the UK’s most successful and celebrated rock bands.

Renowned for their explosive live shows, the band have headlined some of the UK’s biggest stages, including TRNSMT and Reading & Leeds Festivals. Their storming set on Glastonbury’s Pyramid Stage was a highlight for many at the 2025 festival, and they were also honoured with The Live Act Award at the 2025 Rolling Stone Awards.

Biffy Clyro kicked off 2026 with their biggest-ever sold-out UK arena tour, before rounding off the summer with their largest headline shows to date at London’s Finsbury Park and Edinburgh’s Royal Highland Showgrounds.

Their success has been recognised with numerous industry accolades, including multiple NME, Kerrang! and Q Awards, three BRIT Award nominations and a prestigious Mercury Prize nomination.

Fans in Scarborough can expect a career-spanning set packed with era-defining anthems.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “Biffy Clyro are one of the biggest and best live bands in the country, so we’re absolutely delighted to be bringing them back to Scarborough.

“It’s been a few years since they last played the venue and it’s a really special addition to our 2027 programme. We know their fans will make this a night to remember, and we can’t wait to welcome them back to the Yorkshire coast.”

Biffy Clyro is the latest artist announced for 2027 at Scarborough OAT which will also see headline shows from Two Door Cinema Club, Mariah Carey, Courteeners, Shania Twain, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison, Simply Red as well as a four show run of the smash hit musical Six.
