**One of Nigeria's best-known monarchs, the Emir of Daura, has been suspended from all official engagements and travel following allegations of sexual assault by a Polish tourist.**

The visitor said Umar Farouk Umar, 92, had grabbed her chest during a tourism encounter and that she felt disgusted by the experience.

The allegation sparked widespread discussion across social media platforms, with many calling for the suspension of the emir and an investigation - calls the Katsina state government has now heeded.

Umar has not commented on the allegations or his suspension.

He is one of several emirs - a traditional role dating back to pre-colonial times - who still wield great cultural and religious influence in northern Nigeria, although they have lost their political power.

Daura, where Umar serves as emir, holds an extremely important position for the Hausa people who dominate northern Nigeria. It is widely regarded as their spiritual homeland and the origin of the Hausa royal lineage, serving as the core of Hausa origin mythology and the legitimate foundation of traditional authority.

Politicians routinely visit his palace for royal prayers and to gain his support.

On her Instagram page, the tourist said she had spent 30 days travelling around Nigeria before visiting the Emir's palace on 14 November.

She posted a video, which has since been deleted, in which a man who appears to be the emir is holding her hands in his lap.

After some moments she was heard saying: "Stop, stop" as the hands reached for her chest.

According to a statement, Katsina Governor Dikko Umaru Radda directed the immediate constitution of a committee to investigate the allegations.

The statement said the state government was taking the allegations seriously and would rely on the committee's findings to determine the appropriate course of action.

It said the committee's findings and recommendations would be fully implemented, stressing that justice would be served.

However, it did not disclose the membership of the investigative committee, its terms of reference or the timeline for submitting its report.

The emir has been on the throne since 2007, succeeding his brother Bashar dan Umaru, who reigned for 41 years.
