Pensioners across Yorkshire are being given advice about their winter fuel payments.

John Paine, a spokesman for the National Pensioners Convention, has issued a briefing note regarding the vital payment.

In July 2024, the UK government restricted the Winter Fuel Payment to pensioners on pension credit or certain means-tested benefits, removing it from about 10 million pensioners in England and Wales.

Following the backlash, the government changed course in June 2025 and reintroduced the payment for all state pension age individuals from winter 2025/2026, while recovering the payment via tax for those with incomes over £35,000.

A spokesperson for the NPC said: "At this time of year people in receipt of a UK State Pension should receive an official letter from the Department for Work & Pensions, quoting their National Insurance number. It will say that you are entitled to be paid the 2026 Winter Fuel Payment.

"It will state that the amount is £200, or if over 80 years old (born before 27 September 1946) the amount will be £300. The letter will say how that amount will be paid to you, usually in the way that you receive you State Pension."

**Payment**

The briefing note added: "The full details of your entitlement and payment will be in the official letter. Most people will receive the relevant amount in November or December. If you do not get a letter – or the money has not been paid into your account by 27 January 2027 – you will need to check the payment method stated.

"You should contact the Department for Work & Pensions. Ring the free telephone number 0800 731 0160 or go online to contact the Winter Fuel Payment Centre."

**Opting out**

The briefing added: "For many pensioners this payment is vital. However, not everyone does need the Winter Fuel Payment.

"If your taxable income (including all pensions which you may receive) is over £35,000 per year then HMRC will take it back in the tax that you pay. Alternatively, from 21 December 2026 you can opt out of receiving future Winter Fuel Payments.

"You can opt in again by contacting the Winter Fuel Payment Centre. To get a payment for winter 2026 to 2027 you need to contact them before 31 March 2027."

**Extra money**

The briefing note for pensioners concluded: "Remember that if you are on a low income there are several ways of getting extra government financial support.

"These include help with heating, housing and other living costs, including Pension Credit or Universal Credit."
