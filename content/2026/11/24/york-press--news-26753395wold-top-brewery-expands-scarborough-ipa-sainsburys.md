A Yorkshire brewery is raising a glass after doubling its presence on supermarket shelves.

Wold Top Brewery’s Scarborough Fair IPA is now stocked in 95 Sainsbury’s stores, up from the previous 45.

The growth follows strong sales in the North East and recent gold medal success at the Indie Beer Awards.

Alex Balchin, brewing director at Wold Top Brewery, said: "Due to the success of Scarborough Fair IPA in the original 45 stores in the North East, Sainsbury’s has doubled our listing to 95 stores and has extended it geographically to stores in the North West too.

"It’s one of our most popular beers, and we’re very pleased that more people will be able to pick it up with their groceries."

Scarborough Fair IPA is gluten-free, vegan-friendly, and brewed with regeneratively farmed barley and water from the farm’s borehole.

The brewery, based at Hunmanby Grange, was founded in 2003.
