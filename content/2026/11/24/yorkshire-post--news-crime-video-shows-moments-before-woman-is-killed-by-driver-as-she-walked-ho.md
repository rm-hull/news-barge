# Video shows moments before woman is killed by driver as she walked home from work

## The family of a woman killed by a speeding driver who ran a red light while on his mobile phone have released footage which shows the moments leading up to her death to raise awareness about dangerous driving.

Ayesha Hussain was walking home from work on Hamm Strasse in Bradford at around 4.30pm on November 4, 2023, when she was hit by a speeding car.

The 21-year-old died after the blue BMW 320D M sport driven by Mohammad Akhtar hit her while speeding through a red light.

Akhtar, 28, of Holme Street, Bradford, was on his mobile phone at the time.

He admitted causing the death of Ayesha by dangerous driving, and was jailed for five years at Bradford Crown Court on November 20. He was also banned from driving for seven years and six months.

A statement issued by her family alongside the video said: “We share these words with heavy hearts. Our beloved Ayesha, words cannot express how deeply we love and miss you.

“You were only three years old when we lost our mother. Despite the hardships you faced growing up without her, you grew into such a kind and loving person.

![Mohammad Akhtar has been jailed for five years after causing death of Ayesha Hussain by dangerous driving](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/9/04/MOHAMMAD-AKHTAR.jpg?trim=383,0,407,6&width=640&quality=65)


“You brought light wherever you went. Your laughter was infectious, and your warmth made everyone feel special. You were generous, compassionate, and always willing to see the good in others.

“Since you left us, we have realised just how much of our joy came from your presence, your smile, and the goodness you shared. We miss you beyond words.

“Your life was far too short, but the mark you left on everyone who knew you will never fade.

“We wish we could tell you how proud we are of you and remind you that what happened was not your fault. You had finished work and were looking forward to coming home, but you never made it. Instead, we were called to your bedside to say goodbye. It was a moment we were never ready for.

![The video shows the moments leading up to the collision which killed Ayesha Hussain](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/9/49/Crash-pic.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“Three agonising years passed as we waited for justice. We have struggled to come to terms with losing you; our hearts still cannot accept that you are gone. We didn’t just lose you; we lost a part of ourselves, too. We are grateful that justice has finally prevailed, but nothing can replace you or the life we shared with you.

“The only comfort we can hold on to is the hope that you are now reunited with our beautiful mother. That belief brings us a small sense of peace amid our grief, knowing you are no longer suffering. We look forward to the next life, when we hope to be reunited with you and make up for the time we have lost.

“Ayesha, you will always be our baby sister, and our love for you will never end.

“We would like to offer our heartfelt thanks to everyone involved in the case, especially Steve Suggitt, Martyn, and our family liaison officer, Daniel. They worked extremely hard to help us get justice and supported us through this difficult process. We are deeply grateful for all they have done.

“We end by thanking Allah for His kindness, care, and love as we endure this unimaginable loss.”

Detective Sergeant Steve Suggitt, from West Yorkshire Police, said: “Ayesha would still be alive today if it wasn’t for the dangerous actions of Akhtar behind the wheel.

“As the footage shows, Akhtar was not paying attention to the road, missed the red light to stop. Ayesha had no chance.

“His wife and child were also in the car at the time.

“Ayesha’s family have shown great courage and bravery throughout the court process, today’s verdict and sentence has been a long time coming.

“Whilst it will never bring Ayesha back, I hope [the] outcome provides them with comfort knowing justice has been served.”
