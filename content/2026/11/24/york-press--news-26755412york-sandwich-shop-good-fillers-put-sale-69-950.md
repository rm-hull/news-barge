A popular York sandwich shop is up for sale after over 20 years.

Good Fillers, located by the side of Haxby Road, is on the market as the current owners wish to focus on other business ventures.

The takeaway, which operates from early morning until 3pm on weekdays and until 1.30pm on Saturdays, serves sandwiches, hot and cold drinks, and some hot food.

Property agents Barry Crux & Co say the shop also has seating for customers who want to eat on-site, a feature not fully exploited by the current owners, offering potential for a new operator to expand.

The business, in a prominent location, has reported steady levels of turnover and profit, with a large customer base and minimal direct competition.

The shop also benefits from a lot of passing trade.

Well regarded, the venue has good reviews online, with Google awarding 4.7 stars out of five based on 225 reviews.

It is the closest sandwich shop to York Hospital, within easy walking distance of York St John University, the city centre, and is surrounded by residential and commercial properties.

Good Fillers, a ground floor lock-up unit, features a serviceable décor, with ample staff working space, an equipped kitchen area, and a wash-up area.

There is also a small external space for parking, which could be repurposed for customer seating pending the appropriate permissions, the agents say.

The shop has been run by the current owner for more than two decades, building a strong repeat customer base.

A new owner would have the opportunity to expand the business further by widening the menu choices, taking on outside catering, and extending the opening hours.

The business is being sold for £69,950 on a leasehold basis.

Barry Crux and Co say prospective buyers will be provided with more detailed accounting information after viewing the property.

They added the sale of Good Fillers presents an opportunity for a new operator to acquire a well-established business with a steady income stream and potential for growth.
