**Two men have been arrested on suspicion of burglary and trespass at the Prince and Princess of Wales's country home on the Sandringham estate, police say.**

Norfolk Police said it received a call just before 18:00 on Monday following a report of suspicious activity at the address.

Police deployed dogs, drones and a helicopter to search the area and arrested two men on the estate, who remain in custody.

The property was unoccupied at the time, police added. Kensington Palace said it did not comment on security matters.

William and Catherine do not live at Anmer Hall full-time, but use the estate as a country retreat during the holidays.

Anmer Hall, a country retreat with 10 bedrooms, was a gift to the couple from the late Queen Elizabeth II following their wedding in 2011.

The red brick Georgian mansion is on the private Sandringham estate in Norfolk and is a short drive from Sandringham House, one of the King's private residences.

The Prince and Princess of Wales's main home is on the Windsor Castle estate in Berkshire.
