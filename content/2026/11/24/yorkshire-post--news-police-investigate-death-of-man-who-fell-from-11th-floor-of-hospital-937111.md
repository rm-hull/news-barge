# Police investigate death of man who fell from 11th floor of hospital

## Police are treating the death of a patient who fell from an 11th floor window of Hull Royal Infirmary as “not suspicious”.

An investigation was launched after the 64-year-old died in the incident on August 24 at the city’s hospital.

An inquest was opened and adjourned into the man’s death ending the conclusion of an investigation by Humberside Police.

![Police are investigating the death of a man who fell from the 11th floor of Hull Royal Infirmary](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmY0ZjlmYmEwLTk4YmUtNDUxNS05Y2NkLTdhM2NjYzI3OGU0Yzo1NmY1NzgxOS1mNjJmLTQ5YTYtYTkwYi05NzA2MmQxOGMxZGU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The force said a team was conducting extensive inquiries into the circumstances surrounding his death.

Detective Inspector Lloyd Sharkett said: “Following the report, officers were deployed and a 64-year-old man was sadly found deceased.

"We have a dedicated team conducting extensive enquiries, some of which have been complex and required additional time to complete, and I can confirm at this time we believe his death to be non-suspicious.”

The 11th floor of the tower block houses neurological and stroke patients. HRI, which is run by Hull University Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, is rated overall as “requires improvement”, with safety judged “inadequate”.

The trust said it was unable to comment due to the ongoing police investigation.
