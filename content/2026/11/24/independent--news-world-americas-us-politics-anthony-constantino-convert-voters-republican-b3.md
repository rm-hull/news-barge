Republican congressman-elect and sometimes rapper Anthony Constantino is proclaiming “I am not gay” while launching a crusade to convert “every gay American” to the GOP.

Constantino — set to succeed Elise Stefanik in New York’s 21st district after beating Democrat Blake Gendebien in the midterm elections — said Monday that one of his “top priorities” in office is “to make every gay American Republican.”

Later that day, the MAGA congressman-elect shared a series of messages he received on social media that apparently proved his approach was working.

One of them read, “you turned me republican [sic]” while others complimented Constantino’s appearance.

“It’s already begun. I want to clarify, I am not Gay but I do want all Gay Americans to become Republican,” Constantino posted Monday evening, along with a screenshot of the messages.

![Republican congressman-elect and sometime rapper Anthony Constantino has clarified that he is ‘not gay’ after launching a crusade to convert ‘every gay American’ to the GOP. He recently dined with Roger Stone (pictured) instead of attending part of his congressional orientation](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/16/2303150362..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“The Democrat Party is making it unsafe to be Gay. If you like Secure Borders, Safety, Low Taxes, Less Regulation, as well as First and Second Amendment Rights then you should be Republican,” he claimed.

But his post was widely criticized on X, with one user calling it “an alarmingly weird thing to post from a politician,” while another simply responded: “What the hell is going on.”

“Elise Stefanik's successor is somehow more unhinged then [sic] her…” another user reacted.

In another unusual video filmed from his car, Constantino demonstrated how he planned to “turn every gay American Republican.” He then broke into song to Stevie Wonder’s *Master Blaster (Jammin’)*. “It’s easy,” he said.

Constantino was admonished last week over his conduct by New York state Republicans.

“Personal attacks, abusive text messages, bullying and intimidation tactics, whether on social media or through private communications, fall below the standard expected of public office,” a letter signed by Liz Joy, the New York GOP State Committee North Country Regional Chairwoman, and five county Republicans, said.

![Constantino posted another unusual video filmed in his car, where he sang along to Stevie Wonder’s Master Blaster (Jammin’) . ‘Everyone wants to know how I’m gonna turn every gay American Republican. It’s easy,’ he said](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/16/43/Screenshot-2026-11-24-at-11-56-18-AM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In response, Constantino hit back in a series of social media posts, including one that said, “Loser Liz Joyless hates Gay Americans and is furious that I am their friend.”

The 44-year-old, an aspiring hip-hop artist who previously released an album titled “Thank You President Trump,” recently made headlines for skipping part of his congressional orientation in Washington, D.C. to have dinner with Roger Stone.

Constantino reasoned it was “more important” to dine with Stone, a longtime adviser and friend to President Donald Trump, than to choose his office space on Capitol Hill, which he left to his staff.

“I let my staff pick the office so I can work on more important things such as eating chicken parmesan with Roger Stone while planning how to defend us from Radical Democrats,” he said.

“Losers in the media can’t understand that it’s possible for me to let my staff pick my office while I plan to save America from Radical Democrats with Roger Stone,” he added.
