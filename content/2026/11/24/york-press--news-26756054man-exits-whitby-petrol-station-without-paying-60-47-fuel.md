A man has driven away from a petrol station in North Yorkshire without paying for fuel.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident happened at Four Lane Ends Garage on Guisborough Road, Whitby at 11.33am on Monday, October 5.




A force spokesperson said a man pulled up to a pump in a black Honda CRV without registration plates, filled the vehicle with £60.47 worth of diesel, and then made off without making a payment.

The force released a CCTV image of a man North Yorkshire Police would like to identify, saying he may have information which could help the force's enquiries.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online or by emailing [david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:david.fulford@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Anyone who cannot use these options can call 101 and quote reference 12260200743, police said.
