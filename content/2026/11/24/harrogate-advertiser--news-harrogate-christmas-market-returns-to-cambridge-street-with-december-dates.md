A Christmas market is to arrive in Harrogate town centre this December, with Harrogate BID confirming the festive event will take place on Cambridge Street across three weekends.

The Harrogate Christmas Market will bring a selection of independent traders, street food vendors and artisan producers to the town centre, offering visitors the chance to browse for Christmas gifts, seasonal treats and handmade goods.

The market will run over three festive weekends:

**Weekend One:** Friday 4 December to Sunday 6 December**Weekend Two:** Friday 11 December to Sunday 13 December**Weekend Three:** Friday 18 December and Saturday 19 December

Organised by Harrogate BID, the event is part of a wider programme of Christmas activities designed to encourage residents and visitors to spend more time in the town centre during the festive season.

The market will feature a mix of traders selling local and international street food, artisan groceries, sweet treats, arts and crafts, clothing, jewellery, accessories and much more.

It comes after recent years saw changes to Harrogate’s Christmas market offering, with organisers and businesses looking for ways to create a festive attraction that works well within the town centre.

Last year, Harrogate hosted a Christmas market event as part of efforts to bring festive footfall into the town.

The event formed part of a wider push to support independent businesses during one of the busiest shopping periods of the year.

The town has also previously hosted Christmas markets on Montpellier Hill, an area which became a popular backdrop for festive stalls and seasonal events.

However, plans for a Montpellier Christmas market were later cancelled, meaning organisers have since explored alternative locations and formats for bringing a festive market experience back to Harrogate.

The move to Cambridge Street is intended to place the market closer to the heart of the town centre, complementing Harrogate’s shops, cafes, restaurants and other Christmas attractions.

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said: “We're delighted to welcome the Harrogate Christmas Market back to the town centre this December."

"The market will offer a fantastic mix of festive food, gifts and artisan products, creating a vibrant atmosphere for residents and visitors to enjoy while supporting traders and local businesses during the festive season."

"Whether you're looking for a unique Christmas gift, a festive treat or simply a great day out with family and friends, the Christmas Market is the perfect addition to a visit to Harrogate this December."

Alongside the Christmas Market weekends, visitors will also be able to enjoy additional festive events organised as part of Harrogate BID’s Christmas programme.

Farmers Markets will take place on Thursday 10 December and Thursday 17 December, showcasing local produce, artisan food and seasonal products from Yorkshire traders.

A special Makers Market will also take place on Sunday 20 December, giving visitors another opportunity to support independent makers, artists and craftspeople.

Harrogate BID said the programme aims to create a welcoming festive atmosphere in the town centre while encouraging people to shop locally and enjoy everything Harrogate has to offer over Christmas.
