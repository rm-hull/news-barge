# Meet Yusef Arjo: The Yorkshire artist selling mini-prints in vending machines

## An artist has launched a trail of vending machines around Leeds city centre, with customers collecting viral £1 mini-prints.

Artist Yusef Arjo moved into a permanent store in the Corn Exchange in Leeds in September 2026.

“I started the brand nearly ten years ago, mainly as just a hobby,” Yusef said. “I was always a lover of drawing and had a background in architecture.”

When he opened Art by Arjo, he positioned a vending machine outside as a “conversation starter.” It sold small prints of different Leeds landmarks for £1, and according to Yusef, was an instant success.

“I've been so surprised how many people want to collect them,” said Yusef. “It's been a bit like Pokémon cards.”

After the prints went viral online, Yusef decided to open different locations across the city. This November, he launched a ‘Mini Print Art Trail’: a 15-minute self-guided trail around Leeds city centre.

![Art by Arjo is a new shop selling hand-drawn prints in the Corn Exchange](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/17/15/30/onecms_e9b271d3-0e0b-4e4b-92a0-be3bbf792a6a.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“I thought it'd be awesome if it was all focused around Leeds and promoting the city,” said Yusef, who hopes the machines will support local tourism.

There are currently four locations: the original machine in Leeds Corn Exchange, two in Kirkgate Market, and one in the Victoria Quarter.

Each machine has a selection of prints, including Elland Road, Leeds Corn Exchange, and Kirkgate Market.

The route is designed to spotlight the city’s most iconic landmarks, including the 19th-century Kirkgate Market, where tourists can stop for lunch at independent restaurants, like Sweet Saeeda.

“We would like to expand it, and the plan eventually would be to try and have a couple more in other parts of the city,” added Yusef.

“People are always giving me good ideas,” he said. “There's always something to do. There's always something to draw.”
