Greek Cypriot and Turkish Cypriot experts have spent two decades searching for Cyprus’ missing persons. They have found more than half the 2,000 missing, bringing closure to families and building trust between communities. But their work is being complicated by time and development as witnesses die, memories fade and construction entombs mass graves.
