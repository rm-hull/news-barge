Migrants from several Central American countries are travelling in a caravan through southern Mexico, hoping to reach the United States despite mounting pressure from US President Donald Trump to curb migration.

The group was filmed walking through Pijijiapan, in the state of Chiapas, on 19 November. Some migrants were carrying a Honduran flag.

Trump has again threatened to impose tariffs on Mexico unless it takes action to stop migrants crossing its territory.
