# Police appeal after woman, 79, robbed of handbag by teen boys in Halifax town centre

## Police have appealed for information after a woman in her 70s was robbed of her handbag by a group of teenage boys in Halifax town centre.

The 79-year-old victim was walking from Halifax bus station to Sainsburys on November 17 when she was attacked.

Calderdale officers said the elderly woman was walking past a group of young males at approximately 7pm when one of them grabbed at her neck and stole her bag.

All the teenage boys then fled in the direction of Winding Road.

DS Baker of the Calderdale District Crime Team, said: “We are appealing for witnesses and footage as we investigate this distressing offence which left the victim unhurt but understandably very shaken.

“We know there were a number of people in the area at the time, and anyone who saw the offence or has any information to help us identify the suspect is asked to come forward.”
