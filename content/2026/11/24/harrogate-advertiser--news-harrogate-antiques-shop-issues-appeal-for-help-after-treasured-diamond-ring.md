An antiques and home interiors shop in Harrogate is appealing to customers for help after a precious diamond ring belonging to its manager was lost during a busy day at the store.

27 West Park Antiques and Home Interiors has asked shoppers to keep an eye out for the ring, which may have accidentally slipped off while staff were serving customers or preparing purchases.

The business believes there is a chance the ring could have unintentionally ended up inside a customer’s carrier bag or become wrapped in tissue paper alongside an item bought from the shop.

The team is especially keen to hear from the customer who bought a red telephone from the shop that day, as there is a chance the ring may have accidentally been placed in the carrier bag or wrapped up with the purchase.

They are also asking anyone else who visited the shop to check through their bags and any items wrapped in tissue paper, just in case the missing ring has made its way home with them.

In a post on social media, it said: “We completely appreciate that this is a long shot, but this ring means a great deal to our Centre Manager, and we would love nothing more than to get it safely back to her.”

They have asked anyone who has found the diamond ring, spotted it, or has any information to contact 27 West Park Antiques and Home Interiors privately.

The team has thanked customers for their kindness and support, adding that they hoped a little community spirit could help bring the ring home.
