Harrogate Shotokan Karate Club won 37 medals to top the table at the JKS England National Championships for the fifth year running.

The club, based at Harrogate High School, took 50 competitors to Grantham, where the championships attracted 700 entries from across England, Scotland and Wales.

Harrogate won 13 gold, 15 silver and nine bronze medals. Former world karate champion Matt Price led the squad alongside assistant coaches Helen Thornham and Anya Holmes-Ellerker.

Luciano Zanuni received the Seb Mitchell Memorial Trophy for the most outstanding fighter. He won gold in the senior male kumite and shared team gold with Jade Stapleton-Smith, Isaac Mitchell and Ethan Chippindale.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b05a4abaf654ecccbf.png)

In the five to seven age group, Lucas Ang won four gold medals. He won the individual kata, kumite and kickmaster events and joined Lennon Lawrence and Odin Ang to win the team kumite. Lennon also took silver in both individual kata and kumite.

Kate Hambleton won the senior female kumite, with clubmate Isobel McGowan taking silver. The pair teamed up with Valerie Jones, who won gold in the female masters kata, to take the senior female team kumite title.

Harrogate’s other gold medallists included Wicktora Kropczyns in the 12–13 years kata and Belle Jones in the senior kyu grade kata. Belle also won the junior female team kumite with Zarana Gilthorpe and Rowan Underwood, while Eleanor Price, Alexandra Ivanics and Mirren Thornham took gold in the 12–13 years team kumite.
