## Yorkshire Museum launches £25 family ticket for dinosaur-themed weekends

Discover the prehistoric at the Yorkshire Museum *(Image: Anthony Chappel Ross)*

Families can enjoy a dinosaur-themed experience at the Yorkshire Museum for just £25, beginning later this month.

The deal, which covers up to two adults and three children, offers a chance to explore prehistoric Yorkshire and learn about Mary Anning's Jurassic discoveries.

Visitors can marvel at giant marine fossils, dig for smaller sea creatures, and interact with dinosaurs through augmented and virtual reality experiences.

The special ticket will be available on the weekends of November 28-29, 2026 and December 5-6, 2026.

The museum is also home to Iron Age artefacts, Roman centurions of Eboracum, and Viking warriors of the North, all included in the ticket price.

A spokesperson for the museum said: "Bring little-sauruses for less with a £25 deal and discover why Mary Anning rocked and the groundbreaking Jurassic discoveries she made."

For more information, terms and conditions, and to book, visit the Yorkshire Museum's website.
