A York man has been banned from every train station and public toilets in the United Kingdom for 10 years under conditions.

Valdir Da Silva, 60, of St Catherine’s Place off Holgate Road, York, pleaded guilty to outraging public decency by his behaviour in a men’s toilets at York Railway Station on July 17.

At York Magistrates Court he was given a conditional discharge for two years and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £26 statutory surcharge.

He was also made subject to a 10-year sexual harm prevention order that banned him from entering any railway station in the UK and from entering any public toilets, both under conditions.

He can only enter a railway station if he immediately buys a ticket and if arriving by train must immediately leave the station either on foot or another train.

He can only enter a public toilet if he immediately uses the facilities and must do so in a cubicle with the door closed and locked.

He was also banned from being in any public area without being fully clothed from the waist downwards.
