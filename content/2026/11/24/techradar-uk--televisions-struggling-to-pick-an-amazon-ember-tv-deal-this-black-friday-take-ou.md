Amazon has a great range of TVs to suit different budgets and preferences and because Amazon usually has great deals during sales season, you can expect to find some big discounts on its TVs. But which of the Amazon Ember TV line-up is right for you? That's what we're here to help you find out.

If you haven't heard of Amazon Ember before, it's the new name for Amazon's Fire TV range. The 2-Series, 4-Series, QLED and Mini-LED models were all previously sold under the Fire TV name. And the QLED and Mini-LED models were known as the Fire TV Omni QLED and Omni Mini-LED. There's also a new addition to the range, the Ember Artline.

It’s not a big range but there’s plenty of choice here. There’s the budget-friendly 2-Series, which is a good option if you want a simple smart TV for everyday viewing. While the Mini-LED is better suited to those who care about picture quality and gaming. Then there's the Artline, which is Amazon's answer to Samsung's The Frame, allowing you to display artwork when you're not watching TV.

Now, you might already have a good idea of which one is right for you. But if you're not sure, take our quick quiz below to find out which Amazon Ember TV best suits your budget, space and viewing habits.
