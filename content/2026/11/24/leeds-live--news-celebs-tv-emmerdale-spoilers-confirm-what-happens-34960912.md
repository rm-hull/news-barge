# Emmerdale spoilers confirm what happens next with Tom as soap icon goes missing

## Emmerdale spoilers have confirmed Tom King's next target after his release from prison

Several dramatic storylines are set to unfold in Emmerdale next week, with Belle Dingle at the heart of the action. Belle (Eden Taylor-Draper) has been grappling with the imminent prison release of her abusive former husband Tom King (James Chase).

She was recently crushed to discover that Tom's job opportunity in Northern Ireland had collapsed because of injuries sustained from Samson Dingle (Sam Hall). Yet, the situation proved more complex than initially thought, as Tom was subsequently seen performing press-ups in his cell, revealing his injuries weren't as severe as he'd suggested.

Tom's release is scheduled for later this week, but what lies ahead for the Dingles? Here's what viewers can expect from the soap's forthcoming episodes, including a devastating disappearance, reports the Daily Star.

Upcoming Emmerdale spoilers have disclosed Tom's next move, and the news isn't positive for Belle. In upcoming scenes, Lydia Dingle (Karen Blick) and Kammy Hadiq (Shebz Miah) continue organising Cain's (Jeff Hordley) party, with Belle's encouragement. However, following a medical appointment, Kammy must step in for a secretly ailing and exhausted Cain.

As the Dingle family relaxes and decorates for Christmas, Tom observes them from a distance, glowering. In a devastating turn of events, Belle's beloved dog, Piper, is confirmed to be missing, prompting an urgent search.

Piper has endured considerable hardship in Emmerdale in recent years. Devoted viewers will recall that Tom deliberately struck her with his vehicle and staged her death back in August 2024, but could he be responsible for her latest disappearance?

The Dingle family are horrified to learn that Sam (James Hooton) struck a bargain with Tom to secure Samson's freedom. Meanwhile, Charles Anderson (Kevin Mathurin) finds himself justifying his decision to assist Tom at the church, where Tom expertly portrays himself as a victim. This prompts Charles to contemplate allowing Tom to undertake voluntary work in the village.

Later, while returning from the hospital, Moira (Natalie J Robb) and Cain are flagged down by Tom regarding an unwell cow, apparently due to polluted water. Moira reluctantly accepts Tom's assistance with the animal, while Cain furiously accuses him of contaminating the water supply.

Tom proceeds to provoke a vulnerable Cain, before harassing Kammy and Sam. When Belle confronts her abusive former partner directly and orders him to stay away, Tom remains ominously unbothered. Kammy is subsequently revealed as Tom's next target.

Elsewhere in the village, Caleb Miligan (William Ash) tries to pre-empt Sadie King (Patsy Kensit) by admitting his affair to his wife, Ruby (Beth Cordingly). He subsequently displays affection towards his wife, driven by remorse.

Sadie refrains from revealing the affair, instead fabricating a story about a professional dispute to protect Ruby's feelings. Caleb then rebuffs Sadie's advances at the depot and attempts to keep the peace when Ruby turns up.

Meanwhile, chaos erupts at the Smokehouse when Sam inadvertently wrecks the kitchen's electrical system. Kev Barton (Chris Coghill) finds himself shouldering the responsibility, while the Sugdens opt to launch a food truck right outside The Woolpack - a decision that infuriates Chas (Lucy Pargeter) and Marlon (Mark Charnock).
