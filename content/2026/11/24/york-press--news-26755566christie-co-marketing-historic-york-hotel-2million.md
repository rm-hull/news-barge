## Historic York hotel on the market for £2million

The interior of the Georgian House Hotel at 25 Bootham, York. *(Image: Christie & Co)*

A historic York hotel is on the market for £2million.

The Georgian House, a Grade II listed building on Bootham, offers a mix of 17th-century character and modern amenities.

This four-star establishment boasts 22 en-suite bedrooms split between the main building and a modern Mews section at the rear, which is about 14 years old.

The B&B, which also includes a bar-restaurant with seating for up to 30 guests, has been described by agents Christie & Co as having a "good turnover and profits".




The sales particulars say the business posted an adjusted net profit of £200,000 up to March 2026.

The property, which is just 400m away from York Minster, is within walking distance of several of York’s most popular tourist attractions, including Betty's Café Tea Rooms and The Shambles.

The business also benefits from two parking spaces and a manager's flat in the basement.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21754813.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of the Georgian House Hotel at 35 Bootham. *(Image: Christie & Co)*

Christie & Co said: "The Mews rooms are equally well appointed and in a more contemporary style and all rooms offer a very good guest experience."

The Mews rooms are more modern in style, providing a contrast to the traditional elements of the main building.

The hotel has been given an energy rating of C.

The agents added the sale of the Georgian House presents an opportunity for a new owner to take over a well-established business in a prime location.
