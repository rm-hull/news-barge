# Exciting new Irish bar with 'proper pints' and live music opens in Leeds city centre

## We have lots of photos inside the major new Leeds city centre opening

Take a peek at a new Irish bar which has opened its doors in Leeds city centre.

Pints of Guinness are now being pulled at the pub. The Galway Girl has opened in Great George Street.

On the venue’s Instagram page, the team are promising ‘proper pints’, live music and big match sports screenings. We have lots of photos of the new opening.
