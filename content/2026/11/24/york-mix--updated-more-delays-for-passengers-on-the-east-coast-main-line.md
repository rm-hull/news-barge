Train services from London to North Yorkshire are currently disrupted due to a broken rail.

Trains running **Northbound** in the Peterborough area may be delayed, altered or cancelled at short notice.

This affects LNER , Grand Central and Hull Trains travelling to the north.

**Additional stops –** Due to the volume of cancellations today, some trains will have additional unplanned stops added at short notice to assist you in completing your journey. This may mean that services are further delayed due to allowing the time for the extra stops.

Network rail are on site and after assessing the damage to the rail, have put a temporary fix in place that will now allow trains to run through the area to a speed restriction of 20mph.

They will complete a full fix through the night.

As services recover, trains running between Peterborough and London may still be cancelled, severely delayed by up to 85 minutes or revised.

Major disruption is expected until 15:15.
