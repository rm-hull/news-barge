# Use Hillsborough Law against Andy Burnham if we rejoin the EU: Yorkshire Post Letters

## Paul Morley, Ribblesdale Estate, Long Preston, Skipton.

Tim McNally, chairman of Piddington Parish Council, has said that he will use Andy Burnham’s own Hillsborough Law to hunt the PM down if a single migrant at the Bicester illegal immigrant site harms a resident of the village. Quite right too because on current form it is pretty much a racing certainty that it will happen.

This made me think about what Burnham is proposing to sign up to with the EU that will in his words make us all better off and get the economy growing.

One only has to do a bit of research to see that the opposite is true with extortionate costs to the taxpayer and many restrictions to align us with EU regulations which we will have no say in.

![Prime Minister Andy Burnham (right) meets attendees during an event at No 10 Downing Street, London, to celebrate England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales being confirmed as the joint hosts of the 2035 Women’s World Cup.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmRlNGU1M2YyLTFmMzktNDI0Yi05Yjc1LTJmY2Q3MDdiMDE1OTpiNDQ1YzcwYy0xZTRjLTRlZTEtODZkNS00YjgyZGJjYmI1M2U=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


It doesn’t take a lot of working out that with all the Eastern European countries wanting to join the EU the bureaucracy in Brussels will need somebody to pay for it as the bloc is drowning in debt.

If we get tied into this with no way out and we end up even poorer and worse off, can the Hillsborough Law not be used to get our PM into the dock to explain under oath what he knew would happen through his actions and why he still went ahead and have him dealt with accordingly?
