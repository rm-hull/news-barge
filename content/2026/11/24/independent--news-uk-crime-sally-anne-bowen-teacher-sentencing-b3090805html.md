A chemistry teacher who indecently assaulted two pupils at an all-boys’ school in the late 1980s will be sentenced next year.

Sally-Anne Bowen, 65, was convicted earlier this year of seven counts of indecent assault.

The former teacher was in her 20s when she had sex with one pupil up to 30 times and kissed a second boy when they were aged about 14 to 15.

Her trial heard that she wore short, tight skirts and flirted in class, with one former pupil comparing her behaviour to “a Sabrina Carpenter video”.

She was due to face sentencing over the offences at Harrow Crown Court on Friday.

![Sally-Anne Bowen will now be sentenced in January](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/15/a8696057263b9d258ddc4eaa1773ec02Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1NjIwMzg2-2.86549069.jpg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

However, the defendant, who was on bail, did not turn up to court on Friday morning and a warrant was issued for her arrest.

She could not be found at her home in Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire, and was eventually located and arrested by police in Hampshire.

Bowen appeared before Harrow Crown Court on Tuesday, where her sentencing hearing was set for 12 January 2027.

She was remanded into custody, a member of court staff said.

Her trial heard that Bowen was in her mid-20s when she taught at Christ’s College in Finchley, north London.

![Chemistry teacher Sally-Anne Bowen arrives at Harrow Crown Court](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/27/15/b541326030f1910d8108295a316ad463Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzg3OTI2ODE2-2.85647899.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

One victim told jurors that he had sex with her “about 20 or 30 times” over several months, regularly visited her home and considered her his girlfriend.

He had fallen in love but kept their relationship secret because Bowen had “drummed it” into him that he must not tell anyone, the court heard.

The second victim described spending lunchtimes with Bowen in a cafe, where he was “goaded” into kissing her by other boys and recalled touching her breasts.

Both victims cannot be identified for legal reasons.

Former pupil Tobias Oliver told jurors Bowen flirted and openly discussed sex in class.

Mr Oliver also recalled her discussing sexual positions from the Kama Sutra during a lesson.

“The topics of conversation in class often strayed away from chemistry and became quite sexual,” he said.

Bowen denied the allegations as “total fabrication” and claimed a medical condition made sex difficult and painful.

She denied flirting, saying: “I didn’t flirt with students, I was just friendly.”
