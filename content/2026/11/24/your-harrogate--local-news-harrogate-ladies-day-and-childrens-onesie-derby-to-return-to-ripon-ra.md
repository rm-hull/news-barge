Ladies' Day and the Children's Onesie Derby are both back on the calendar at Ripon Racecourse for 2027.

Ladies’ Day takes place on Thursday 17th June, with prizes for Best Dressed Lady, Best Hat and Best Dressed Gent.

Children will get their own chance to race to the finish line in the Onesie Derby, part of the Bank Holiday Family Day on Monday 30th August.

Tickets are now on sale for all 16 meetings, with new bundles offering four family days or four evenings of racing for the price of three.

Each bundle gives one person entry to all four chosen meetings in either the Club or Paddock Enclosure.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/66447bcc4ac3a.jpg)

The season opens on Thursday 15th April. A 1930s-themed raceday follows on Saturday 24th April, before Twilight Tracks brings racing and music to Ripon on Friday 7th May.

The first family day takes place on Sunday 16th May, followed by Sunshine Showdown, a Wild West-themed evening at the races, on Wednesday 2nd June.

Scoops & Saddles returns on Monday 2nd August, when families can help choose their favourite flavour at the North Yorkshire Ice Cream Championships.

Other summer fixtures include the Go Racing in Yorkshire Summer Festival on Saturday 17th July and the William Hill Great St Wilfrid sprint handicap on Saturday 14th August.

The season ends with the Season Finale & Harvest Festival on Saturday 25th September.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6a7eb58793dcdc8396a.jpg)

Accompanied under-18s go free at every meeting, and children’s activities are free on family days.

Picnics are welcome in the Paddock and Course Enclosures, and parking is free.

Concession tickets are £5 for the meeting on Thursday 27th May.

*To explore the full calendar and book tickets, packages or bundles, visit www.ripon-races.co.uk.*
