Nvidia employees cheered and applauded as co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang returned to the company's headquarters in Santa Clara, California, on Monday.

Footage posted on X showed staff gathered inside the offices, cheering Huang's name as he made his return.

Huang had stepped down after reports of extramarital affairs, including an alleged relationship with a Chinese businesswoman suspected of having links to Beijing's intelligence services.

He was reinstated just 11 days after announcing his resignation and six days after the company's board of directors had reportedly rejected his offer to return as CEO.
