The Duchess of Sussex’s father Thomas Markle has been formally charged by a grand jury in Los Angeles with two counts of possessing child pornography, court documents show.

According to the filing made in California, Markle knowingly possessed 35mm film negatives that contained child pornography.

The indictment doubles the number of charges that Markle faces in the case. He is accused of posing in photographs with at least one naked underage girl and with other children in various stages of undress.

Markle, 82, was arrested in Florida on 6 November and charged with storing child sexual abuse material in California after FBI agents seized a haul of material from two Los Angeles storage units he had reportedly rented for nearly two decades.

![Markle was recently ruled competent to stand trial (Pinellas County Sheriff’s Office/AP)](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/24/23/c341fe3ce751b57d0e01912f99ca7ddcY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1NjQ4NzY2-2.86458218.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Authorities found items including slides, film negatives and books that all contained suspected child sex abuse material.

Last week, he was deemed psychologically fit to stand trial after being accused of possessing child sex abuse material, including images of himself with naked young girls.

He was accused of “malingering because he is exaggerating a lack of recall or understanding of the charges in order to avoid criminal prosecution” by a medical expert after claims that he had dementia.

Markle is currently in custody in Florida. He had initially been released on conditional bail but was detained just days later after removing his electronic monitoring tag.

A psychiatric assessment was ordered after his arrest at a senior care centre in Flordia.

![Markle was ruled psychologically fit to stand trial last week](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/20/07/download.-2..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

During a 90-minute interview, he reportedly denied the allegations against him and blamed his son for his legal troubles, according to a 15-page report.

“Why I am here? What have I done?” she quoted him as saying. “I don’t have any child porn. I have no pictures of naked kids.”

Thomas Markle Jr told *The Sun* that he reported his father to the police in Philippines and the FBI last month after allegedly finding illegal images in his father’s belongings in Cebu City.

Meghan Markle, who is estranged from her father, said at the time that she had been left “deeply distressed” by the news and had been informed about “troubling and disturbing allegations” by the Metropolitan Police and Royalty and Specialist Protection in 2018.

“I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications’ distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children was for one reason: to protect them.”

She added: “Although the breadth of what has now been uncovered is more sickening than one could ever imagine, my sincerest hope is that justice will be fully served and that the priority remains the safety and protection of innocent children.”
