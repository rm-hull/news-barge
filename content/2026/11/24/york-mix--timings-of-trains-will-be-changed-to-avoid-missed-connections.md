Transpennine Express (TPE) will change its timetable next month after a North Yorkshire MP highlighted problems with ‘ridiculously tight’ connection times.

The change follows complaints that passengers arriving at York from London were being given just a few minutes to transfer onto connecting services to Malton and Scarborough.

The hourly train will depart later than it currently does from mid-December onwards, after Alison Hume, MP for Scarborough and Whitby, missed her connection on several occasions when travelling from London.

After sharing a video highlighting the “ridiculously tight connection times” at York Station for passengers going to Ryedale and the seaside resort, TPE said it would amend the departure time of the service.

From December 13, most TPE services from York to Scarborough will leave at around 13 minutes past the hour instead of 11 minutes past, giving passengers arriving from London extra time to change trains.

TPE said this would give customers arriving at York on LNER’s London service at three minutes past the hour more time to make their connection.

“This is an improvement and will make journeys a fraction easier for passengers, but it’s still going to be tight if, like me, your train from London is delayed,” Ms Hume said.

A spokesperson for the company, which currently operates an hourly service between Scarborough and York, said that the later departure from York would increase the connection time from LNER’s London service from eight to ten minutes, giving customers additional time

George Thomas, TPE’s Strategy and Partnerships Director, said: “We are committed to improving the service we deliver for our customers. A central part of this is listening to feedback and working with colleagues from across the industry to refine our timetable.”

“The change comes into effect from Sunday 13 December as part of the bi-annual industry timetable change.

“It’s a small change that we hope will make journeys to the coast easier.”

​TransPennine Express currently operates one service per hour on the York-Scarborough route via Malton and Seamer, but from December 2028, there will be two additional services in the morning and evening.

​There will be additional services at 6.35am and 5.35pm from York to Scarborough, as well as extra trains from Scarborough to York at 7.30am and 6.30pm.

The company added that it was “constantly working with other rail operators and Network Rail to improve connections”.
