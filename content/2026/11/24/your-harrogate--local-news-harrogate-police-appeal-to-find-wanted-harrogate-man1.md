North Yorkshire Police are asking for help to find 34-year-old Shaun Clarke from Harrogate.

Clarke is wanted in connection with theft and fraud by false representation.

Officers have carried out extensive enquiries and believe he could be in Harrogate or Keighley.

Anyone who sees Clarke should call 999 immediately.

*Anyone with information about his whereabouts should call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference number 12260190702.*

*Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*
