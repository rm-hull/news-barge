The Glebe playground in Killinghall could be made safer and more inclusive under plans to be considered by councillors next week.

The playground is one of nine projects across North Yorkshire that could share more than £5 million in developer funding.

In Ripon, some of the money could help pay for a new car park at Park Street Surgery.

North Yorkshire Council’s executive will consider the proposals on Tuesday 1st December.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b0542ec1848bd9879a.png)

The money comes from the Community Infrastructure Levy, which allows the council to pool contributions from developers.

Money collected in the former Harrogate borough must be spent in that area.

Cllr Mark Crane, deputy leader of North Yorkshire Council, said:

> “The projects identified for this year’s funding programme cover a range of priorities, from transport and healthcare improvements to leisure and community facilities.
>
> “The proposals will be considered by members of the executive next week as we continue to make sure investment is targeted where it is needed most and where it can deliver the greatest long-term value.”
