A coach with Ripon Runners has received the Unsung Hero of the Year award from England Athletics after nearly a decade of supporting young runners.

Helen Cox, the club’s Minis and Juniors Coordinator, received the award at England Athletics’ 2026 Yorkshire and Humberside Regional Volunteer Awards at Oulton Hall in Leeds.

The award follows recognition at Ripon Together’s 2026 St. Wilfrid’s Stars Awards, where Helen received the Special Award for Individual Supporter of Youth.

Ripon Runners also received a Sports and Activity Group award for welcoming people of all ages and abilities.

Helen said: “It was such a lovely surprise to receive these awards, and I’m still feeling very emotional about it.

“Ripon Runners means such a lot to me, and supporting the junior members is so rewarding.

"Of course, it wouldn’t be possible without the fantastic support that I’ve received from club members and volunteers, so I’d like to thank them for all the help that they have given me over the years.”

Alongside coaching, Helen looks after the junior section’s administration and supports the volunteers who help run it.

England Athletics said: “Her inclusive and caring approach has helped create a welcoming environment where children can develop confidence, skills, and enjoyment in athletics.”
