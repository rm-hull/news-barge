# How Nvidia is reacting to Jensen Huang's return as CEO

Nvidia's board unanimously voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO and president on Monday. President Trump has claimed credit for brokering the deal. What is the mood at Nvidia following the announcement? BBC's Lily Jamali reports from Nvidia's headquarters, where she gauges the reaction to Huang's return.
