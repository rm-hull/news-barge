## York couple who ran the London Marathon together celebrate 65 years of marriage

The couple celebrated their 65th wedding anniversary with a favourite meal of sausages, home-made Yorkshire puddings, followed up with a homemade trifle *(Image: Contributed)*

A couple living in York who crossed the 1987 London Marathon finish line side by side have recently celebrated 65 years of marriage together.

Patricia and John Cole were married on November 18, 1961, at Trinity Church Heworth and now live together at Broadway Lodge Residential Care Home in Fulford Road.

The couple also lived in Rawcliffe and after they both retired, said they loved going to the Yorkshire coast at Whitby and Scarborough, with trips to Tenerife part of their enjoyment of spending time abroad.




![John and Pat Cole at their 65th wedding anniversary in York](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21758195.jpg?type=mds-article-620)John and Patricia celebrated their 65th wedding anniversary by opening presents alongside son Rob and daughter Mandy *(Image: Contributed)*

Care home owner Faizal Mohamudbuccus told The Press Patricia and John's children, Rob and Mandy, said they are "so grateful that their parents remain together and it makes them feel at peace knowing they can visit them regularly in a safe and caring environment."

A spokesperson from Broadway Lodge said it is "a pleasure to look after them both," describing John as "always appreciates everything that is done for him" and Patricia as someone who "enjoys the laughter and fun on a daily basis."
