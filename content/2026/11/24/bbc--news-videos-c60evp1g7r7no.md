# 'I'm proud of it': Killer confesses after murdering Ian Huntley

A triple murderer who beat the notorious child-killer Ian Huntley to death in prison has been given a second whole life jail term.

Anthony Russell, 44, repeatedly bludgeoned Huntley, 52, with a metal bar at the high security Frankland Prison near Durham in February, Newcastle Crown Court heard.

In footage released by the Crown Prosecution Service, Russell confesses to planning to kill Huntley and says: "I'm proud of it."

Russell, who was jailed for life with no chance of parole in March 2022 for raping and murdering a pregnant woman and killing a mother and son in the West Midlands, admitted Huntley's murder on the second day of his trial.

Sentencing him for what prosecutors called a "cold-blooded" and planned attack, Mr Justice Hilliard KC said Russell would never be freed from prison or eligible for parole.

Huntley was serving a life sentence at the high security Frankland jail for murdering Holly Wells and Jessica Chapman in Soham, Cambridgeshire, in 2002.
