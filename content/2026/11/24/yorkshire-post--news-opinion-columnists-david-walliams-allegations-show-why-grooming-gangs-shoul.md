# David Walliams allegations show why 'grooming gangs' should be renamed: Yorkshire Post Letters

## Otto Inglis, Crossgates, Fife.

The David Walliams’ affair illustrates the vital importance of accurate and truthful language. He has been accused of ‘grooming’ various teenage girls, although nobody has accused him of sleeping with anyone under the age of consent, and he specifically denies breaking the law.

The main accusation is that Mr Walliams has charmed and seduced teenagers less than half his age.

The same word ‘grooming’ is used in the expression ‘grooming gangs’ in relation to vastly more serious behaviour.

![David Walliams pictured in 2012. Picture: Dave Thompson/Press Association](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQyODRhYWI0LWE5ZDItNDk0MS1iMWQwLTBlNmNjMjY4YzAzNjplMzc0Y2ZhZS03YWE4LTQyODktOTA3Yi0wMWI5MWZjMTMyOTQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The members of many ‘grooming gangs’ in towns and cities throughout the country have been found guilty by criminal courts of drugging, raping and brutalising underage girls and young women selected on the basis that they were not of the same race or religion as the offenders.

Furthermore, in many cases they forced these children and young women into prostitution.

It is time we stopped referring to them as ‘grooming gangs’ and describe them accurately as ‘racist rape gangs’ or, if we are being precise ‘racist child rape and prostitution gangs.’

Once we describe these criminal gangs accurately, it becomes crystal clear why this issue is not some ‘right-wing dog-whistle’ but something which rightly enrages a great many British people.
