Laura Loomer, a far-right activist and staunch ally of Donald Trump, has broken with the president over Nvidia’s decision to bring back Jensen Huang as chief executive.

Nvidia’s board of directors reinstated Huang on Monday, just 11 days after he resigned following reports of multiple affairs, including one with a woman accused of being a Chinese spy. Huang said that he was “honored” to be back.

The decision followed a pressure campaign from Wall Street and from Trump himself. “I’ve been speaking to the board directly and indirectly, and I felt very strongly about it,” the president told CNBC Monday.

“Reinstating Jensen Huang is going to blow back on our national security,” Loomer wrote on X following the news.

“People can like the guy personally,” she added. “But why are we pretending like his relationship with an alleged Chinese spy isn’t the same thing (if not worse) that we all attacked[former California Rep. Eric Swalwell] for?”

![Laura Loomer, a far-right activist, has broken with President Donald Trump over Nvidia’s decision to bring back Jensen Huang as chief executive](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/16/40/GettyImages-2297774940-(1).jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Swalwell, a Democrat who left Congress in April, told FBI investigators he had a physical relationship with a suspected Chinese spy, according to documents released in August by the White House. His attorney said that “he did nothing wrong, and there was no basis to charge him with any offense.”

“Eventually there has to be accountability for jeopardizing U.S. national security,” Loomer continued. “China didn’t just get access to Nvidia chips by accident.”

Alice Huang, the suspected Chinese spy who reportedly had an affair with Jensen Huang, allegedly helped supply Nvidia chips — used to power artificial intelligence — to Chinese customers. U.S. officials are investigating whether chips were smuggled into China in violation of export restrictions.

Jensen Huang, a Taiwanese-born American entrepreneur, has admitted he was “unfaithful” to his wife and has said he is “deeply sorry.”

Trump had been warned of the CEO’s link to a suspected spy in May, but was “not particularly moved,” *The New York Times* reported. He has said that Jensen Huang told him he “had no idea that she was a spy.”

In a subsequent post on X Monday, Loomer wrote: “So much for being China hawks…This will haunt the GOP in my opinion.”

![‘So much for being China hawks…This will haunt the GOP in my opinion,’ Loomer wrote on X](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/20/download.-6..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The following day, she took issue with a report that claimed she had broken with Trump.

“I can disagree with President Trump over Jensen Huang and still support Trump,” she wrote. “The media is so dishonest.”

Numerous Democrats and some Republicans have criticized Nvidia and the president for supporting Huang’s reinstatement. Nvidia’s board has said it is confident, following input from senior U.S. officials, that his return would pose no threat to national security.

Loomer, an Iran war advocate who has described herself as a “proud Islamophobe” and “pro-white nationalist,” has maintained direct access to Trump in his second term, meeting with him in the Oval Office and at Mar-a-Lago and traveling with him aboard Air Force One.

She announced a hiatus from her podcast after the Republican Party’s crushing midterm losses sent her into a posting frenzy in which she declared the American dream “dead” and predicted that a “nuclear holocaust” would ravage the United States.
