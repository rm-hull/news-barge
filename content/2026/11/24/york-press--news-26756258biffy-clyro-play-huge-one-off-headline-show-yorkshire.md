One of the UK’s biggest and most explosive live acts is set to return to Yorkshire for a single headline show.

Scottish rock band Biffy Clyro will play Scarborough’s Open Air Theatre on July 21, 2027, in their first appearance at the venue since 2019.

The Scarborough date is their only UK headline show announced outside of their previously confirmed festival appearances at Tramlines and Truck.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21755784.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Biffy Clyro will play Scarborough Open Air Theatre in July, 2027. *(Image: Uchechi Adeboye)*

Julian Murray, of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, said: "Biffy Clyro are one of the biggest and best live bands in the country, so we're absolutely delighted to be bringing them back to Scarborough.

"It's been a few years since they last played the venue and it's a really special addition to our 2027 programme.

"We know their fans will make this a night to remember, and we can't wait to welcome them back to the Yorkshire coast."

Biffy Clyro will be joined on the night by special guests Bleech 9:3.

The band has four UK Number 1 albums and is known for their dynamic and high-energy live performances.

Their latest album, Futique, was widely praised, with NME calling it "a vulnerable yet life-affirming blast."

Biffy Clyro have headlined major festivals including TRNSMT and Reading & Leeds, and their 2025 Glastonbury Pyramid Stage set was described by Kerrang! magazine as "a masterclass in joy and proffessionalism."

They began 2026 with their biggest-ever sold-out UK arena tour and closed the summer with headline shows at London’s Finsbury Park and Edinburgh’s Royal Highland Showgrounds.

The band has won multiple industry awards, including NME, Kerrang!, and Q Awards, as well as three BRIT Award nominations and a Mercury Prize nomination.

Fans can expect a career-spanning set of anthems in Scarborough.

Supporting act Bleech 9:3 are known for their high-energy performances and distinctive sound, blending heavy riffs and raw vocals.

Biffy Clyro joins a packed 2027 line-up at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, which also includes Two Door Cinema Club, Mariah Carey, Courteeners, Shania Twain, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

The venue will also host a four-show run of the hit musical Six.

Other confirmed dates include Kaiser Chiefs on June 11, Courteeners on June 12, Shania Twain on June 16, Kings of Leon on June 27, and Two Door Cinema Club on August 6.

Tickets go on sale at 10am Friday via scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.
