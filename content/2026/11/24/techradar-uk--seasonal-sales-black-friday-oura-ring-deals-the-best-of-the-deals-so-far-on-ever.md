One of the hottest tickets in the Black Friday tech sales is actually one of the smallest items. Oura Rings, and smart rings in general, are discreet little fitness trackers you wear on your finger to track your sleep, steps, general health and lots more.

Below, we've highlighted the best Oura Ring deals in both the US and the UK. They're on sale at lots of retailers, and a few, such as Walmart in the US and Amazon in both regions, have around $100 / £100 off the Oura Ring 5 in all finishes.

However, there are multiple tiers to those finishes — some cost more than others — so I've included them all below, as well as older devices. The Oura platform also requires an annual subscription of $69.99 / £69.99 too, so I've slipped in a subscription-free alternative too — the Samsung Galaxy Ring.
