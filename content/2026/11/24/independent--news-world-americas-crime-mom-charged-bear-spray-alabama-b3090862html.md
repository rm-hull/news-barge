An Alabama mother has been charged after allegedly spraying her children with bear spray, according to authorities.

A grand jury indicted Christie Lashay Williams on child abuse after her kids told police that she allegedly sprayed them in the face with bear spray, local outlet WBRC reported Thursday, citing court records.

Police in Leeds, less than a 30-minute drive east of Birmingham, said they were called to a suburban block on the afternoon of March 8 following reports of a child running down the street and calling for help.

One witness told authorities that the kid’s face was puffy and their eyes were somewhat red.

![A grand jury indicted Christie Lashay Williams on child abuse after her kids told police that she allegedly sprayed them in the face with bear spray, local media reports](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/24/16/46/Screenshot-2026-11-24-at-11-46-51-AM.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

When the police went to the child’s home, the child and their sibling claimed their mother, Williams, sprayed them with bear spray, and then the two were taken to a hospital.

One of the children later claimed to police that their mother sprayed them in the eyes and mouth.

Williams, then 37, was arrested nearly a month later on April 6, days after the police obtained a warrant for torture/willful abuse of a child, according to WBRC. She subsequently bonded out of the local jail.

Alaska Fish and Game warns that bear spray can inflame the eyes and upper respiratory system. The agency says to “treat bear spray like a firearm,” as the spray comes out at more than 70 mph and “could cause permanent eye damage.”
