To hear President Donald Trump talk about his long-running White House ballroom project is to hear a near-endless stream of potential uses for the gigantic structure being built where the East Wing stood until he ordered it demolished just over a year ago.

It will host future inaugurations. It hides a hospital underneath. It will host a drone-port on the roof as well as snipers — and it’ll be bomb-, bullet- and drone-proof as well, or at least it will, according to the president.

Now, a year after Washington was shocked by the rapid razing of the historic East Wing so Trump’s pet project could rise, the president has revealed yet another purpose for the now reportedly $600 million project: Housing turkeys awaiting executive clemency.

Trump floated the bizarre potential function of his signature building project as he spoke at the annual White House event held to issue pardons to a pair of *Meleagris gallopavo,* also known as the domestic turkey, in a tradition officially dating back to George HW Bush but also practiced by Presidents Reagan and Kennedy before that.

As he introduced this year’s National Thanksgiving Turkeys, a duo of well-fed birds called “Feathers” and “Freedom,” Trump noted that the two avians were just over four months old, having been hatched on July 15, and now weighed a combined 82 points while boasting an approximately 48-inch wingspan.

![President Donald Trump (C) departs with first lady Melania Trump following the 79th annual National Thanksgiving Turkey Presentation on the South Lawn of the White House on November 24, 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/20/2306703736..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He also pointed out that the birds, that arrived in Washington earlier this week, had been cooling their heels at the historic Willard Hotel while awaiting his decision on whether to award them presidential pardons.

“Unfortunately, for 200 years, the White House lacked appropriate accommodation to host such distinguished guests. They've done this for a long time,” he said.

“We've saved a lot of turkeys, but I'm pleased to announce that next year we'll be able to have a special location right over there in that rather small ballroom being built.”

Trump added that next year’s ceremony would be held “inside” the ballroom, which he claimed was “going up rapidly” and is “ahead of schedule.”

The annual ceremony this year took place on the helipad which was constructed on the South Lawn earlier this year to allow Trump and future presidents to make use of the VH-92 Patriot helicopters that were delivered for use as Marine One roughly a decade ago.

Despite nearly 10 years of testing and attempts to modify the aircraft, engineers could not prevent the helicopters’ powerful engines from burning the grass in the spot on the White House’s South Lawn where the older VH-3 Sea King used by previous presidents had landed for decades.

Trump also used Tuesday’s event to take shots at his predecessor, Joe Biden, as he claimed the last two years to have been an “amazing period” for the country.

“We've had an amazing period of time this two years, not even two years, but this has been an amazing period of time. There's never been a better one in terms of living in the United States. The things that we've done have been incredible,” he said.

He also alleged to have “inherited” a “mess” and “the highest prices ever and the worst inflation ever” from Biden despite prices for fuel and most commodities being far higher today than they were on the day he took office nearly two years ago, largely due to his own tariff policies and the war he started against Iran nearly a year ago.
