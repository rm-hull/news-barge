Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce holds its members-only Christmas Business Safari at the Cedar Court Hotel on 14 December, 5.30pm to 8pm. How to book.

Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce will end its 2026 events calendar with a members-only Christmas Business Safari at the Cedar Court Hotel on **Monday 14 December**, from 5.30pm to 8pm.

## Overview

* **What:** the Christmas Business Safari, a structured networking evening.
* **When:** Monday 14 December, 5.30pm to 8pm.
* **Where:** Cedar Court Hotel.
* **Who can go:** registered members of Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce only. Advance booking is essential.
* **Book:** on the Chamber’s events page.

## How the Safari works

Instead of open networking, guests move between tables at regular intervals through the evening, so directors, founders and sole traders keep pitching to new groups of people. The Chamber says the format stops people spending the whole night talking only to contacts they already know.

The evening starts with arrival drinks and informal conversation at 5.30pm, before the table rotations begin. Members are asked to bring plenty of business cards.

The Chamber says the night is also a chance for members to look back on the economic changes of the past year and talk through their outlooks and plans for 2027 with other members.

## ‘Time is a business owner’s most valuable asset’

Phill Holdsworth, President of Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce, said: “The Christmas Business Safari is always a highlight of the Chamber year. We know that time is a business owner’s most valuable asset, and the Safari format is deliberately designed to be energetic, efficient and highly effective.

“It is a fantastic opportunity for our members to rapidly expand their local contact books, discover new regional suppliers and forge collaborative partnerships just before we all break for the festive season.”

## What it means for you

If your business is a Chamber member, book a place through the Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce events page in advance: the evening is open only to registered members, and booking is essential. Earlier this month the Chamber hosted a Bank of England budget briefing at The Crown Hotel.

The Chamber says it has supported local business through campaigning, networking and business briefings for more than 130 years.
