## Christmas classics to fill Malton’s Wesley Centre this December

The Ebor Quartet is set to perform a Christmas concert at the Wesley Centre in Malton *(Image: Supplied)*

The Ebor Quartet is set to perform a Christmas concert in Malton.

The concert, sponsored by Harrowells Solicitors, will be held at the Wesley Centre on Sunday, December 13, from 4pm to 6:30pm.

The ensemble will perform a variety of popular Christmas carols, including The Most Wonderful Time of the Year, Fairytale of New York, and I'm Dreaming of a White Christmas.

The Wesley Centre, a restored heritage venue, will be decorated with winter candles for the evening.

The Ebor Quartet will perform in this atmospheric setting, promising to be a "festive night out in Ryedale".

Tickets are available for purchase and are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.

Seating is on a first-come, first-served basis within an allocated area of the venue.

Doors will open at 4pm.

Latecomers will only be admitted at the interval to protect the acoustic environment.

The event organiser reserves the right to cancel the event if minimum numbers are not met, in which case tickets will be refunded.

Child tickets are available for those aged 3 to 18 years old, and anyone under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

You must email [fundraising@familyfund.org.uk](mailto:fundraising@familyfund.org.uk) as soon as you book your ticket.
