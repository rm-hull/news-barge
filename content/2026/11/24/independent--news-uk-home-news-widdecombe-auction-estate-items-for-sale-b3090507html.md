Items belonging to Ann Widdecombe are to go up for auction next month, providing yet another insight into her colourful life as a veteran politician.

Among the hoard to be sold are a bent spoon, said to be from psychic Uri Geller, with guide price of between £200 and £300, and a trophy from her appearance on BBC quiz show Pointless, with guide price of £30 to £50.

Also included are a framed oil-on-canvas portrait of Ms Widdecombe with her cat, plus ministerial red boxes from her time in the department of employment, Home Office and department of social security.

The items are being auctioned by Devon-based Whittons Auctions over three days from 2 December.

![Joshua Kerry is also accused of murdering Ann Widdecombe (Lucy North/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/02/19/87139b1e1b6fde76ec33c184af0a494dY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkxMDUyNzY5-2.85542805.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Ms Widdecombe, a former Tory MP, was found dead at her home in Haytor, Devon, in July, after she stopped responding to messages before a scheduled interview on Channel 5. Joshua Kerry, 28, has been charged with her murder.

Kerry has also been charged with preparing acts of terrorism, including against Reform UK leader Nigel Farage. A provisional trial date has been set for June 8 next year.

Ms Widdecombe first served as minister in Conservative prime minister John Major's 1992-1997 government before, more recently, working as immigration and justice spokesperson for Reform.

![The Pointless glass trophy presented to Ann Widdecombe which is among the items up for auction](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/11/26/1100096231_PREVIEW.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Outside of politics, she appeared on numerous television shows, including Strictly Come Dancing in 2010 and Celebrity Big Brother in 2018.

Ms Widdecombe’s 176 belongings up for auction contain a mix of personal artefacts, books and wine and spirit collections.

Among the eccentric items are a papier-mâché sculpture of the ex-politician “dressed in a glittery purple and silver dress with bright orange shoes” based on an outfit she wore on Strictly Come Dancing.

![A teaspoon bent in the presence of Ann Widdecombe by Uri Geller in 1998 is also listed](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/11/38/1100096685_PREVIEW.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)
![A papier-mâché sculpture of Ann Widdecombe dressed in a glittery purple and silver dress which is up for auction](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/11/18/1100096890_PREVIEW.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

There is also a hi-vis jacket with her name, a mask of her face and a coaster with the words: “We Will make Britain Great Again”.

Maria Whitton, from Whittons Auctions, said: “As a family of auctioneers for more than 140 years, we approach every estate in the same way.

“It is, however, a privilege to be entrusted with Ann’s estate, and we have genuinely been overwhelmed by the level of interest, from local people to enquiries from around the world. Our aim is simply to present the collection properly and give everyone a fair opportunity to bid.”
