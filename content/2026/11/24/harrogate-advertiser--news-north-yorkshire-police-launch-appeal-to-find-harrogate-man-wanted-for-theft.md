North Yorkshire Police has released an image and launched an appeal to help find a wanted 34-year-old man from Harrogate.

Shaun Clarke is wanted for theft and fraud by false representation.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Extensive enquiries have been ongoing to locate Clarke and we’re now appealing for your help to help find him.”

Officers believe that Clarke could be in Harrogate or Keighley.

If you see Mr Clarke, or have any information about his current whereabouts, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If the sighting is immediate, you should call 999.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260190702 when providing any details regarding his whereabouts.
