Artizan International is asking for help to replace the worn floor at its café on Cambridge Road in Harrogate.

The café opened in 2022 and gives disabled and neurodivergent adults the chance to gain work experience.

Members take orders, make drinks and serve customers alongside the café team.

After four years of daily use, the floor is lifting in places.

The charity says the café’s income won’t cover the £7,000 it needs for a hard-wearing replacement.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b056bb6ae68a741696.png)

Through the Big Give Christmas Challenge, donations made online from midday on Tuesday 1st December to midday on Tuesday 8th December will be doubled until the £3,500 match fund runs out.

The Reed Educational Trust and local supporters have pledged the match funding.

If residents and businesses donate £3,500 during the week, the charity will reach its target.

S, who has trained at the café for two years, said:

> “The Artizan Café is very nice, and I enjoy it here. I work with my friends and colleagues and enjoy making the food and serving it. If you are reading this, make sure you come to this special place”.

Liz Cluderay, CEO of Artizan International, said:

> "Our training café is where so many of our members first find their feet at work.
>
> "They serve Harrogate with real skill and pride every day, and they deserve a space that reflects that. With Big Give doubling every gift, a coffee's worth of support goes twice as far, and together we can reach this target to secure our cafe's future in a single week."

*Anyone wishing to donate can find the**Big Give appeal** online or through the banner on Artizan International’s**website**.*
