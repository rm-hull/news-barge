## York schoolboys cycle 6,900km to help fund Uganda classroom

Matthew Droop and Louis Beck, students at Archbishop Holgate School, are cycling 6,900 kilometres to help build a nursery classroom in Uganda *(Image: Supplied)*

Two teenage boys from York are cycling 6,900 kilometres to help build a nursery classroom in Uganda.

Matthew Droop, 14, and Louis Beck, 13, students at Archbishop Holgate School, began the challenge on April 12 and aim to finish by December 19.

They hope to raise £38,000 to support the construction of a new nursery classroom at Kisole Primary School in Uganda.

Matthew said: "It was fun in the summer, but now it's getting to wintertime it's getting a lot more testing.

"We've still got a long way to go, but hopefully we'll make the deadline on time."

To reach their target, each boy must cycle 95km every week.

Their effort is part of a larger project involving 19 pupils, leaders, and staff from Archbishop Holgate School, who will travel to Uganda in July 2027.

Louis said: "The long rides after school have been tough as the evenings get darker and colder, but it’ll be worth all the effort once we’ve finished.

"We're getting closer every week, and I really hope we reach the finish line in time."

The project is a partnership between Archbishop Holgate School, Abaana Ministries and Kisole Primary School.

Donations can be made at [https://gofund.me/fca9499a3](https://gofund.me/fca9499a3).
