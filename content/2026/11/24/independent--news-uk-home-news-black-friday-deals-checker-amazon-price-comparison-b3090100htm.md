Shoppers have been urged to double check the deals on offer this Black Friday as hundreds of discounts may not be as good as they appear, new Which? research has found.

Only one in five offers over the sales period were genuinely cheaper than at any other point in the 12 months around it, the consumer champion claims, casting doubt over the bargains on offer.

Tracking more than 250,000 daily prices on 756 home, tech, and health products from eight major retailers, Which? found that just 22 per cent hit their lowest price during the four-week Black Friday window last year.

A further four in 10 (44 per cent) only matched prices already available at other times of the year, meaning nearly eight in 10 deals (78 per cent) were the same price or cheaper outside the event.

Of the eight retailers evaluated, Richer Sounds had the lowest proportion of products at their cheapest during the Black Friday window, at just six per cent.

A further 53 per cent matched their cheapest price from another point in the year at the home media store, meaning that 41 per cent of its products on offer were cheaper at another point in the year.

But Amazon finished at the bottom of the combined ranking, with less than half (48 per cent) of its deals matching or beating their lowest price of the year.

By contrast, Argos led the combined ranking, with 86 per cent of its Black Friday deals matching or beating their lowest price of the year.

It was followed by Currys and Boots (both 79 per cent) and John Lewis (74 per cent).

Very sat further down the table on 62 per cent, ahead of Richer Sounds (59 per cent) and AO (54 per cent).

Reena Sewraz, Which? retail editor, said: “Our research shows there are definitely bargains to be had in the Black Friday sales period, but they might be hard to find. Most of us will need a helping hand to separate the real deals from the hype – with the best prices on many products actually appearing at other times of the year.

“If you do have your heart set on a product you've seen in the sales, make sure you check the price history first, so you know you can trust you are getting a good price. You don't need to rush into it, however, as more often than not we've found you'll still be able to get the same deal or a better one towards Christmas and beyond.”

Researchers gave the example of a Dyson Airwrap at Argos, which was on offer for £429.99 during the Black Friday sales period (reduced from £479.99).

It was cheaper on 82 occasions after the main sales period, dropping to £399.99 on 4 December and hitting £349.99 for most of April.

At John Lewis, an LG 48-inch OLED Smart TV was priced at £999 on Black Friday, but shoppers who held out until Christmas Eve would have paid £749 thanks to a price match – saving £250 just weeks later.

Meanwhile, at Amazon, a pair of Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones was labelled a ‘Black Friday deal’ at £399, yet patient buyers could have saved £99 when they dropped to £299.95 in March.

A Richer Sounds spokesperson said: “We're committed to offering excellent value throughout the year but remain confident that our Black Friday promotion represents one of the very best times for customers to shop. Above all, we believe in being open and honest about our pricing, helping customers make informed decisions, a commitment reflected in our Pricing Charter, developed in conjunction with Which?.”

An Amazon spokesperson said: “We offer customers great value all year round by constantly comparing Amazon's prices to make sure that our prices are as low or lower than other retailers, as well as offering savings during events like Black Friday. Our deal savings are based on validated reference prices, and we provide clear pricing information on product pages so customers can shop with confidence.

“Each item shared with us by Which? was cheaper during Black Friday than in the previous 30 days. Our price history feature also shows up to 365 days of pricing data on millions of products, so customers can track exactly how prices have changed before they decide to buy.”

An Argos spokesperson said: “Customers tell us that the most important thing for them is the certainty that they will not see one of our Black Friday Price Guarantee products for less before Christmas.

“Which?’s research confirms that every Black Friday Price Guarantee product delivered on that commitment. We strive to offer great value for our customers throughout the year, with a range of other promotions running alongside our Black Friday deals, giving customers even more opportunities to save.”

An AO spokesperson said: “Black Friday is a month-long promotion when more discounts are available than at any other time of year to help customers spread the cost of Christmas. As these findings show, we also offer great value deals all year round, always backed by our Price Match Promise, so customers can shop with confidence whenever they choose to buy.”

A Boots spokesperson said: “Last year, more than 26,000 products were included in our Black Friday sale and customers enjoyed making genuine savings when shopping at Boots. We want our customers to have time to make informed purchasing decisions, which is why our Black Friday deals run throughout November, supplementing the wide range of promotions we offer throughout the year to help customers save money.”

A Currys spokesperson said: “We’re proud of the value we delivered for customers last Black Friday. We went further for our customers, with thousands of products at their lowest ever Currys price, backed by our Black Friday Price Guarantee. Customers rightly expect Black Friday to deliver genuinely great value, and we’ll continue working hard to earn their trust and give them confidence that they’re getting a great deal at Currys.”

A John Lewis spokesperson said: “We’re proud of our Never Knowingly Undersold customer promise, and regularly check and match our prices against 25 major competitors, so customers get market-leading prices whenever they shop with us. As a result, our prices can change outside of our planned promotions.

“As Which? notes, the majority of our Black Friday deals reflect our very best prices. The event also brings together a unique breadth and volume of offers that can’t be found at any other time of year.”

A spokesperson from The Very Group commented: “We have great deals all year round, and Black Friday is just one of the many moments for customers to take advantage of discounted prices. We regularly review pricing across our range of more than 200,000 products to ensure we remain competitive, and this Black Friday we have unveiled a new price promise to give customers greater confidence that they are getting the best value possible from us.”
