President Donald Trump has claimed Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has “worked it out” with his wife after an affair scandal threatened to destroy his career.

The tech billionaire was forced to resign earlier this month after allegations he had an affair with an executive at Singapore-based tech company Megaspeed, whom U.S. intelligence believed to be a Chinese spy. Subsequent media reports made claims of further affairs with employees, including one with Nvidia’s vice president of health care.

However, following a lobbying campaign by Trump, Nvidia abruptly reversed course and announced Huang’s return Monday, causing its share price to rocket more than 30 percent.

Speaking to *The New York Times*, the president cheered the news before turning his attention to the executive’s wife of 41 years, who has yet to comment publicly on the scandal.

“He’s got a very, very, very good wife, and they’ve worked it out,” Trump told the newspaper. “She’s a fantastic woman.”

![Jensen Huang has ‘worked it out’ with his wife Lori, according to President Donald Trump](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/12/15/2026-11-12T135530Z_806515743_RC2XPNAYGULT_RTRMADP_3_NVIDIA-CEO.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Trump went on to claim he would not have dealt with the matter in the same way, without going into specifics.

“I wouldn’t have handled it the way he handled it,” the president told *The NYT*. “Without that, it would have been a lot easier because that got the attention. You know, sex was involved, and sex sells better than stocks.”

In his initial resignation statement, Huang said he was “deeply sorry” for the hurt he had caused his wife, whom he first met when they were students at Oregon State University and paired together as lab partners in an electrical engineering class.

“Throughout my life, my family has given me love and support,” he wrote. “They deserve my honesty and loyalty. Instead, I let them down and damaged the life we built together. I take full responsibility for what I did and the hurt I caused, and I accept the criticism that comes with it.

“To my wife and family: I love you. I know an apology cannot undo what I have done. I hope our family will heal and that, over time, I can earn back your trust.”

![The Huangs frequently attended high-profile events together, visiting Windsor Castle to meet the British Royal Family in September 2025](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/12/2305156633..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

The couple were married in 1985 and have two children together, who hold senior positions at Nvidia, while Ms Huang is the president of the family's $9 billion charitable foundation.

She has regularly attended high-profile events with her husband, including the state visit of China’s president Xi Jinping to the White House in September this year, and meeting the British royal family at Windsor Castle in 2025.

While there is no suggestion of a split, *Forbes* previously estimated that in the event of a divorce Ms Huang could receive at least $75 billion, placing her in an exclusive tech billionaire ex-wives’ club that also includes Melinda French Gates and MacKenzie Scott.

Trump, whom *The Financial Times* has estimated may have made up to $15 million from Monday’s share price rebound, played a pivotal role in bringing about Huang’s reinstatement and had repeatedly pleaded his case through the media, telling Stephen A Smith recently his friend was “going through hell.”

![The couple have been married for 41 years, having met when they were paired together as lab partners at Oregon State University](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/09/10/2271992036..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

“What he feels most badly about is hurting his wife and family,” the president told Smith, before celebrating his reinstatement with a bizarre meme showing the executive holding a red bra in triumph.

Trump welcomed his return by congratulating Nvidia’s board on making a “correct and proper decision” and declaring: “The Country needs Jensen’s leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy.”

He also dismissed concerns about Huang potentially being exploited through the affair, adding: “The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to Nvidia poses any risk to National Security.

“The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct.

“On a personal note, I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure.”

Huang was warmly received at his company’s Silicon Valley headquarters Monday, promising staff: “I am going to be a better CEO the second time around.”
