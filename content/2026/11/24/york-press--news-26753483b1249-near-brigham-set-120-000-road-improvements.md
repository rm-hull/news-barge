A £120,000 road upgrade is set to begin along the B1249 route.

The scheme will improve the stretch between North Frodingham and Brigham, with work starting in December and expected to take around seven days to complete.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council will upgrade part of Brigham Straight and Church Lane, with repairs to Frodingham Beck bridge planned for January.

Councillor Paul West, cabinet member for environment and transport, said: "Thanks in advance to local residents and drivers for their understanding while we carry out these improvements to the road."

The project will involve resurfacing, structural patching, and strengthening the road edges in preparation for surface dressing.

The section being upgraded runs from Bridge Farm to the junction with Howes Lane.

The improvements are designed to protect the road from potholes and extend its lifespan, with stone chippings to be applied as part of next year’s surface dressing programme.

The work will take place Monday to Friday between 8am and 4.30am.

Affected sections of the road will be closed to through traffic for safety, with a diversion route in place.

Access will be maintained for residents and businesses throughout the works, with the repairs carried out by the council’s own road maintenance team.
