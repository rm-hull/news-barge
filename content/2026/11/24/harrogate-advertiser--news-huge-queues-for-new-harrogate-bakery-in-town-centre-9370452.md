Harrogate can sometimes be a graveyard for big names in the food and drink trade but a new bakery and coffee shop which opened last week is bucking that trend.

The arrival of GAIL’s at 7 James Street has seen big queues of customers building up on a regular basis, especially at lunchtime.

Such has been the interest so far in this upmarket cafe and takeaway once associated with Hampstead and the south that queues have sometimes snaked out the door and into the street at this premium town centre retail address.

“Its great to be in Harrogate as the first GAIL’s in Yorkshire,” said Richard Exton, the bakery’s Head of Neighbourhood Marketing, who hails from Sheffield.

“Although our business now has more then 175 branches, we were only a small working bakery and neighbourhood cafe for years and we still strive to do what the very first GAIL’s did.”

First launched in Hampstead in London in 2005, GAIL’s is renowned for pioneering additive-free sourdough bread and acting as a wider community hub.

The craft bakery serves freshly baked sourdough loaves, pastries (including almond croissants and cinnamon buns), cakes, crustless pies, sourdough sandwiches, salads, breakfast pots, barista coffee, matcha, and iced lattes

The launch of the Harrogate GAIL’s adds another name to an increasingly competitive café scene in the town which boasts a large number of much-loved independents including Bakeri Baltzersen, Café Otto, Caffe Marconi, Pink Door, Seven Districts Coffee, Fi:k, The Italian Connection, Cold Bath Deli and a lot more.

GAIL's Bakery Harrogate is open seven days a week all year-round, except for Christmas Day.

Doors open daily at 6.30am and close at 6pm, approximately.
