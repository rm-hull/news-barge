A search is underway for a man who reportedly went overboard from a Royal Caribbean cruise ship near the Bahamas late Monday night.

The man was aboard the Allure of the Seas when the ship reported him missing near San Salvador Island around 11:30 p.m., according to Bahamian officials and the U.S. Coast Guard, CBS News reports. The ship departed Miami Sunday for an eight-day Thanksgiving cruise through the Caribbean.

The Royal Bahamas Defence Force is leading the search with assistance from the Coast Guard.

“We immediately launched a search and rescue effort and are working with authorities,” a Royal Caribbean spokesperson told *The Independent* Tuesday.

The ship's captain told passengers Monday that the crew had "received a report of a missing guest" earlier that day before determining "that the guest had unfortunately gone overboard," according to two recordings of the announcement obtained by CBS News.

![A man reportedly went overboard from Royal Caribbean’s Allure of the Seas (pictured) Monday, near the Bahamas, during an eight-day Thanksgiving cruise](https://static.independent.co.uk/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/2019/10/18/10/allure-of-the-seas.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Passengers told the outlet that crew members spent several hours calling out the passenger's name over the ship's loudspeaker system before the vessel turned around to search for him.

“Our Care Team is actively providing support and assistance to the guest’s loved ones during this difficult time. For the privacy of the guest and their family, we have no additional details to share,” Royal Caribbean’s statement continued.

A spokesperson for the Royal Bahamas Defence Force confirmed that officials were coordinating with the cruise line.

"We are coordinating with the cruise and will have a vessel on scene," the spokesperson told CBS News.

*The Independent* has contacted the Coast Guard and Royal Bahamas Defence Force for comment.

The incident comes about two years after another passenger fell to her death from a balcony aboard the same ship.

In October 2024, Dulcie White fell from her balcony while sailing on a Taylor Swift-themed cruise aboard the Allure of the Seas. Her body was never recovered.

White's family later alleged in a lawsuit that Royal Caribbean's unlimited drink package encouraged passengers to consume too much alcohol and that crew members ignored warning signs before her fall. The case remains ongoing.
