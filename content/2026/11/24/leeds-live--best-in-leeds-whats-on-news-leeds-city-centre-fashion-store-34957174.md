# Leeds city centre fashion store shuts as special Leeds United shop opens

## There's been some change in Leeds city centre

A Leeds city centre fashion store has closed.

We have seen North South Vintage, in Lands Lane, shut down. The Leeds city centre store stocked a range of retro fashions.

Those we have recently walked down Lands Lane will have likely noticed the empty unit. There is a sign reading ‘to let’.

Elsewhere in Leeds city centre, the popular Burley Banksy has launched a new shop. It stocks dedicated Leeds United memorabilia with lots of Christmas present gift ideas available to be purchased.

The new shop, from the Burley Banksy, opened on Tuesday, November 17 at the Merrion Centre.
