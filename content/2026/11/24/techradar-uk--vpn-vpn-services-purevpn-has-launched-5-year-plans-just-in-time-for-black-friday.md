PureVPN has introduced new five-year plans just in time for Black Friday, becoming one of the few VPNs offering Black Friday VPN deals beyond two years.

Its Basic plan now starts at $1.49 per month, while Plus and Max plans, which add features such as password management and dark web monitoring, are $2.19 and $2.49 per month, respectively.

Five years is far more than most VPNs will ever offer - only FastestVPN's lifetime VPN deal lasts longer at the time of writing. Most VPNs offer two years, and VPNs like NordVPN are only $13 less right now for the far shorter duration.

PureVPN's deal is perfect if you're looking to avoid inflated auto-renewal costs for as long as possible. Just turn off auto-renewal once you've signed up, then, in five years, find a new Black Friday VPN deal that takes your fancy instead.

PureVPN's new five-year plans offer a brilliant way to set up and forget a VPN that protects your whole household. You get 10 simultaneous connections, apps for most platforms, *and* features like password management and dark web monitoring if you choose a more premium plan. Unsure? Try it with its 30-day money-back guarantee.

That said, I'd recommend considering how you'll use it before signing up.

When we recently tested PureVPN, it was surprisingly slow, clocking just 560 Mbps on WireGuard.

This *will* be fine for most things: browsing, gaming, and some streaming, but the fastest VPNs offer speeds around 4x quicker right now if you want reliable 4K streaming, low-ping gaming, or high-speed downloads.

It unblocks all global Netflix libraries, most Disney+ libraries, and many local services such as BBC iPlayer or 7Play. But if you want a VPN for YouTube or Prime Video, I'd again look elsewhere, as it struggled to unblock either.

If you can live with this, then no VPN offers better value - even FastestVPN can't meet PureVPN's performance.

But, if you're more focused on performance, look towards some of this year's best VPNs. NordVPN is $2.99 per month for two years, ExpressVPN is $2.79, and Proton is $2.49, all offering a superior experience if you're willing to sacrifice the 3 additional years.
