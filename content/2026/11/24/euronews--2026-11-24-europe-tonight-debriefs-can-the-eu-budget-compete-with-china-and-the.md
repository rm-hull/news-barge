## Euronews' daily evening show Europe Tonight welcomed Lukas Gutenberg, director of Bertelsmann Stiftung, to discuss issues such as how the EU plans to do more with less, as well as the latest proposal put forward by Antonio Costa, which foresees another round of big cuts.

A major showdown is underway over the European Union’s next seven-year budget, pitting the European Parliament against EU member states while deeply dividing national capitals over proposed spending cuts, digital levies, and agricultural subsidies. As negotiations intensify, governments remain locked in a tug-of-war over how to balance traditional farm aid with new strategic priorities.
