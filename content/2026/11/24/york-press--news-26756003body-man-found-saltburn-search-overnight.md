## Body found in search for missing man

Saltburn Beach Picture *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The body of a man has been found off the Yorkshire coast.

Emergency services launched an “extensive search” for a man who had been reported missing from the Knaresborough area on Sunday evening (November 22).

After a huge 999 response involving multiple Coastguard teams and two police forces, a body was found yesterday morning in Saltburn.




North Yorkshire Police said the death is not being treated as suspicious, and a file will be prepared for the Coroner. His family have been informed.

A spokesperson said yesterday: “Sadly, we can confirm that the body of a man has been found in Saltburn.

“Following an extensive overnight search involving North Yorkshire Police, Cleveland Police and HM Coastguard, a body was located this morning.

“While formal identification has not yet taken place, it is believed to be that of a man who was reported missing from the Knaresborough area yesterday evening.

“His family has been informed.

“Our thoughts remain with them at this extremely difficult time. The death is not being treated as suspicious, and a file will be prepared for the Coroner.”

A Coastguard spokesperson added: “Redcar, Whitby and Staithes Coastguard Rescue Teams, Redcar RNLI inshore and all-weather lifeboats and the Coastguard helicopter attended an incident at Saltburn this morning.”
