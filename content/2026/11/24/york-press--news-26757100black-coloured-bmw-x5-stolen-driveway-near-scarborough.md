Thieves have targeted a village in North Yorkshire and made off with a high value SUV.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing to the public for help to trace a black-coloured BMW X5 stolen from a driveway in Armstrong Way, Cayton, near Scarborough, between 11.30pm and midnight on Friday, November 20.

A force spokesperson said the vehicle's tracking system registered its location to Carr Lane in the village before it was deactivated.




Police urge anyone who was in the area during this time, or who may have seen anything suspicious, to come forward with information.

If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Information can also be passed to Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260228385 when providing information.
