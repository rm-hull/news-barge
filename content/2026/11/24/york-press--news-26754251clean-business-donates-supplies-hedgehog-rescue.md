## York and Harrogate cleaning firm helps hedgehog rescue centre

It’s Clean Ltd recently donated cleaning products to Pepperfield Hedgehog Rescue *(Image: Supplied)*

A commercial cleaning firm operating in York and Harrogate is encouraging others in the industry to support wildlife rescue centres after donating surplus supplies.

It’s Clean Ltd recently donated cleaning products to Pepperfield Hedgehog Rescue in Darlington, where it also operates.

The supplies will help volunteers maintain the high hygiene standards needed to care for sick and injured hedgehogs.

Terence Newton Laheney, owner of Pepperfield Hedgehog Rescue, said: "Caring for rescued hedgehogs requires strict hygiene standards, with volunteers regularly cleaning accommodation and equipment to protect the animals' health and wellbeing.

"It was lovely to be able to talk about our work with the It’s Clean team, and we were extremely grateful to them for donating surplus cleaning supplies."

During a recent visit, the It’s Clean team saw first-hand how vital cleaning products are to the centre’s daily operations, which helps rehabilitate the hedgehogs.

Clary Rigby of It’s Clean Ltd said: "It was a real privilege to visit Pepperfield Hedgehog Rescue and see the fantastic work Terence and the volunteers are doing.

"Terence was keen for us to spread the word to other cleaning companies, and we'd love to encourage businesses across the industry to think about whether they have any spare supplies that could help."
