York has been ranked one of the UK’s best cities for a rainy day out, thanks to its wealth of indoor attractions.

The ranking, compiled by The Co-operative Bank, placed York third out of 30 UK cities based on the number and quality of its indoor activities, including museums, cinemas, escape rooms, bowling alleys and art galleries.

York scored 7.58 out of 10 in the study, with 179 indoor attractions available.

Kat Robinson, Head of Everyday Banking at The Co-operative Bank, said: "A rainy day doesn’t have to be a disappointing day out.

"As the weather turns and sunnier days feel further away, our research shows these cities still offer plenty to enjoy – from museums and galleries to escape rooms, cinemas and bowling alleys."

The city topped the list for museums, with 3.11 per 10,000 people, and 87.7 per cent of its indoor venues are rated four stars or above by visitors.

York Castle Museum was named the city’s most popular indoor attraction, with standard adult entry priced at £18.

Norwich topped the list overall with a score of 8.46, followed by Cambridge at 8.25.

Edinburgh and Brighton rounded out the top five, scoring 6.65 and 5.52 respectively.
