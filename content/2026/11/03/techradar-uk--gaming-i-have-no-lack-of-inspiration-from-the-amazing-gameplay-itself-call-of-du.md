* ***Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4*composer Jason Graves has revealed some inspirations behind the game's soundtrack**
* **He attributes much of it to the gameplay and Infinity Ward's sound team**
* **He confirmed that he didn't reference previous*Modern Warfare*soundtracks, but there is "a little bit of vibe inspiration from the OG*Modern Warfare 2"***

*Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* composer Jason Graves has attributed the gameplay and the Infinity Ward sound team for helping to inspire the game's multiplayer score.

In an interview with TechRadar Gaming, Graves said, "Fortunately, I have no lack of inspiration from the amazing gameplay itself," when asked where he begins when composing a soundtrack for such an iconic franchise.

"The incredible team at Infinity Ward, led by audio director Stephen Miller, was also such a huge creative influence," he continued. "And the music team, led by Jonathan Mayer, were integral in shaping the overall vibe and giving me everything I needed to be as creative as possible."

Graves, who has also composed for *Warzone*,* Dead Space*,* Dead Space 2*, 2013's* Tomb Raider*, and a host of other projects spanning video games and movies, added that it was important that the work was a collaborative effort, as scores can be years in the making.

"I can't stress how important cooperation with other creative human beings makes a difference when it comes to working on a project as big as this," Graves said. "This is the kind of thing that slowly comes together over years of work."

As for finding the basis for a *Modern Warfare*score, Graves explained that he doesn't listen to previous works, but admitted that "there was a little bit of vibe inspiration from the OG* Modern Warfare 2*."

"But nothing too granular or specific," he added, "I'm very intentional about not listening to music from previous projects in a franchise unless specifically directed by the developer. I think that's especially important here because Infinity Ward has been so supportive of trying new things and creating a soundtrack that's every bit as epic and bespoke as the gameplay itself."
