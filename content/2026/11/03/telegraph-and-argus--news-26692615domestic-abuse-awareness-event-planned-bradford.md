A DOMESTIC violence awareness workshop will take place later this year to support women in Bradford.

Organised by Impact Women Network CIC, the event will be held on Friday, November 6, at the Midland Hotel from 11am to 2pm.

The session aims to raise awareness of domestic abuse and will cover topics such as coercive control, trauma bonding, mental health, children’s wellbeing, safety planning, recovery and economic independence.

Funmilola Peters, founder and director of Impact Women Network CIC, said: "Domestic abuse can affect women from every background, but many still suffer in silence because of fear, stigma, cultural pressures or financial dependence.

"Healing Together is about breaking that silence, connecting women with support and reminding our communities that recovery and a life beyond abuse are possible."

The workshop will include talks from experts, a survivor-focused discussion, and a professional panel sharing advice on recognising abuse, accessing support and rebuilding life after domestic violence.

It forms part of a year-long Healing Together project by Impact Women Network, focused on supporting women through awareness, empowerment, wellbeing, and access to services.

The event is open to survivors, professionals, and community groups, and aims to create a "safe and supportive space" for all participants.

More information is available through Impact Women Network CIC.
