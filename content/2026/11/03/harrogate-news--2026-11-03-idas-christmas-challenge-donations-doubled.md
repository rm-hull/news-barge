**IDAS Christmas Challenge:** Independent Domestic Abuse Services (IDAS) is calling on communities across North Yorkshire to support its Christmas fundraising campaign, which aims to raise £20,000 to help survivors escape abuse, recover from trauma and rebuild their lives.

The charity, the largest specialist domestic abuse service in the North of England, is taking part in the Big Give Christmas Challenge. Donations made during the campaign will be doubled until IDAS reaches its £20,000 fundraising target.

The campaign will run from 12pm on Tuesday 1 December until 12pm on Tuesday 8 December 2026. IDAS is encouraging supporters to plan their Christmas giving around the week-long appeal so their donations can have twice the impact.

IDAS provides support to women, children and men affected by domestic abuse and sexual violence across North Yorkshire, Barnsley, Doncaster and Sheffield.

## More than 8,000 people supported during 2025

The charity’s work includes refuge accommodation, community-based support, specialist advice, peer mentoring and recovery services.

During 2025, IDAS:

* Answered 29,500 calls to its free, confidential helpline.
* Managed 20,000 referrals.
* Supported more than 8,000 people in local communities.
* Provided safe accommodation with intensive support to more than 300 families.

To continue providing essential services that fall outside its existing contracts, IDAS needs to raise around £150,000 every year through fundraising and donations.

The charity says this funding helps it respond when survivors need urgent assistance to escape abuse, while also providing opportunities for recovery and support as they rebuild their lives.

## How donations can make a difference

Money raised through the Big Give Christmas Challenge could help recruit and train volunteers to answer more calls to the charity’s helpline.

It could also provide emergency financial assistance to people escaping abuse. This might include paying for a night in a hostel or hotel while someone waits for a refuge place, covering travel costs to safety, or providing food and toiletries for a family’s first nights in refuge accommodation.

Support does not end when a family reaches safety. Fundraising also helps IDAS deliver recovery groups and activities for children, giving survivors opportunities to rebuild their confidence and helping families reconnect after experiencing abuse.

Donations can also help families meet essential costs when they are ready to move on from refuge accommodation and settle into a new home.

## Charity urges supporters to take part

Sarah Hill, CEO of IDAS, said the charity relies on fundraising to provide support that is not covered by its contracts.

> **Sarah Hill, CEO of IDAS:** Every year, we need to raise around £150,000 to provide essential support that isn’t covered by our contracts. This funding can make a life-changing difference, helping someone escape abuse, supporting a family through recovery, or giving children the chance to feel safe again.
>
> The Big Give Christmas Challenge is a fantastic opportunity for our supporters to make their donations go further. We’re asking people to get involved and help us reach our £20,000 target, so we can continue supporting people in our communities when they need us most.

People wishing to support the appeal can visit the IDAS campaign page when it goes live at midday on Tuesday 1 December.

**Donate to the Big Give Christmas Challenge:** [https://bit.ly/biggive-idas](https://bit.ly/biggive-idas)

The campaign will close at midday on Tuesday 8 December 2026, with donations doubled until IDAS reaches its £20,000 target.

More information about the charity and its work is available at idas.org.uk
