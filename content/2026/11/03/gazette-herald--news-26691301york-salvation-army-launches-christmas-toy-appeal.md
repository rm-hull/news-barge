The Salvation Army is calling on the public to help bring Christmas cheer to children in need through its annual Present Appeal.

The York branch is asking for donations of new, unwrapped toys to ensure every child wakes up to a gift on Christmas Day.

Last year, the charity distributed gifts to 920 children in York and fears even greater demand this year due to rising living costs.

Andrew Vertigan, church leader at York Salvation Army, said: "This Christmas, families are wondering how they’ll get through the week.

"Our Christmas Present Appeal is an opportunity for people in our community to embrace the spirit of Christmas and give to families and individuals who have next to nothing."

Volunteers are also needed to help sort and pack donations throughout December.

Mr Vertigan added: "We’ve got people coming to help us out from NFU, National Rail and York University, but would love to have more to help so we can meet the demand."

Donations can be dropped off at Lidgett Gove Methodist Church in Acomb, York.

Gifts for teenagers and babies under two are particularly needed.

The Salvation Army will distribute the toys through schools, social services, and local groups.
