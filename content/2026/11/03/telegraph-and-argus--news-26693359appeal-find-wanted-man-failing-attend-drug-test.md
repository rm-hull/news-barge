Police are appealing for information to help locate a man who failed to attend or remain for the duration of a drugs test.

Christofer Silwa, 39, is believed to have connections in the Batley area.

![Mug shot of wanted man](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21662991.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police are appealing for information to help locate Christofer Silwa *(Image: WYP)*

## Full appeal from police

A spokesperson for WYP said: "Police are appealing for information to help locate Christofer Silwa, aged 39, who is wanted for failing to attend/remain for an initial drug test assessment.

"Silwa is believed to have connections in the Batley area.

"Anyone with information which may assist to locate Christofer Silwa is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or go online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 113260267411.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
