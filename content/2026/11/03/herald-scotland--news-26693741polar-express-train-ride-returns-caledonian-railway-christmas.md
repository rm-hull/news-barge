With the holidays quickly approaching, The Caledonian Railway is celebrating the return of THE POLAR EXPRESS™ Train Ride in Brechin.

This immersive family-oriented experience hosted nearly 1.8 million riders at 58 locations in the US, Canada, UK, and Australia in 2025.

Popular dates and times are expected to fill quickly, but seats are now available for departures beginning 28th November and continuing through 20th December. Tickets are on sale now at Polar Express — Caledonian Railway and guests are encouraged to book soon as this beloved holiday tradition is expected to sell out.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21663512.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Passengers read along with the classic children’s book, The Polar Express, by Chris Van Allsburg *(Image: Supplied)*

The adventure comes to life when the train departs for a round-trip journey to the North Pole led by a cast of talented actors. Set to the sounds of the motion picture soundtrack, passengers will relive the magic of the classic story as they are whisked away on THE POLAR EXPRESS™ for a one-hour trip to meet Santa.

Once on board, the conductor will work his way through the coach and punch golden tickets before hot chocolate and a delicious cookie are served by dancing chefs.

Passengers then read along with the classic children’s book, *The Polar Express*, by Chris Van Allsburg. Santa and his helpers board the train to greet passengers and each guest is given the first gift of Christmas.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21663510.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ticket prices range from £30.95 to £55.95, depending on the date and time of the ride *(Image: Supplied)*

During the trip, characters on each car lead fantastic onboard entertainment – just like in the film! Each guest will take home a keepsake sleigh bell and souvenir golden ticket in addition to the wonderful memories made on this magical trip. Just like the kids in the book, families are encouraged to wear their pajamas for the ride!

The event first arrived at Brechin in 2021 and is now in its 6th season of this magical event at the Caledonian Railway. It has become a tradition for many families across Scotland. There are several locations that host the event all over the world, but riding THE POLAR EXPRESS™ here is unique because we are the closest event to the North Pole! We have very welcoming staff, and all the other amazing opportunities to celebrate Christmas in Brechin.

Exact dates, times, and fares are available by visiting Polar Express — Caledonian Railway. Ticket prices range from £30.95 to £55.95, depending on the date and time of the ride.

Rail Events Inc. and Rail Events Productions are wholly owned subsidiaries of American Heritage Railways, which is the premier operator of heritage railroads in the United States.**About Caledonian Railway Brechin Ltd**

The railway’s story begins in 1848, when a line was built to connect the growing town of Brechin to the wider railway network, supporting its thriving textile and whisky industries.

After more than a century in operation, passenger services came to an end in 1952, with freight continuing until the line eventually closed in 1981.

After years of hard work by volunteers the railway was granted the Light Railway order in 1994 and the first public train ran since British Rail.

32 years later trains continue to run and because of the volunteers hard work and money brought in, the railway has seen large improvements to its facilities including the Brechin canopy being reinstated and new undercover facilities for our rolling stock.

Throughout the rest of the year the Caledonian Railway volunteers run other events such as Diesel Saturdays and steam Sundays, our annual diesel gala for railway enthusiasts, Family orientated events such as Day Out With Thomas and events for the parents of those families like our Sloe Train Gin Tasting experiences.

The volunteers are proud to be one of the worldwide hosts of The Polar Express train ride and thoroughly enjoy welcoming all of the visitors through the doors.
