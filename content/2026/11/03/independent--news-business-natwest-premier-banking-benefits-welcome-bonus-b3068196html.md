From expert financial guidance and exclusive rewards to dedicated support and investment expertise, NatWest Premier is designed to help higher earners, savers and investors make the most of their money and move closer to their financial goals.

For eligible customers considering a new banking relationship, there's currently an additional incentive to switch. New customers who open an eligible NatWest Premier account and meet the offer criteria could receive a £500 welcome bonus. Terms, conditions and restrictions apply, and the offer may be withdrawn at any time.

## **Why choose NatWest Premier?**

Managing finances effectively has never been more important. NatWest Premier combines everyday banking with access to specialist support, financial planning and a range of exclusive benefits designed around customers with more complex financial needs.

All Premier customers receive access to:

* Exclusive 24/7 human support
* A dedicated Premier banking team
* Financial planning guidance (No ongoing advice fees. Product or one-off charges may apply)
* Investment expertise and a range of investments managed by Coutts
* Range of rewards, offers and lifestyle benefits depending on the account selected

Premier banking managers are available from 8am to 8pm, seven days a week, helping customers find banking solutions that match their goals and circumstances.

## **Who is eligible for NatWest Premier?**

To apply for NatWest Premier, customers must be aged 18 or over and be UK residents. They must also meet at least one of the following criteria:

* Have a sole annual income of £100,000 or more paid into a NatWest account
* Have a joint annual income of £120,000 or more paid into a NatWest account
* Hold £100,000 or more in savings or investments with NatWest
* Have a NatWest mortgage of £500,000 or more

Specific product eligibility criteria and fees apply.

## **Get a £500 welcome bonus when switching**

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/30/14/55/GettyImages-1197319130.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Eligible new or existing customers who don’t hold a NatWest Current Account as of 2nd September 2026 and switch their current account from another bank to either a Premier Select or Premier Reward account could receive a £500 welcome bonus.

To qualify, customers must:

* Use the Current Account Switch Service to complete a full switch of their current account that is not with NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland or Ulster Bank to their new NatWest Current Account. This includes closing your current account held elsewhere.
* Pay in at least £15,000 within 90 days of opening the account. This can be made up of multiple payments, such as salary deposits, provided each payment remains in the account for at least 24 hours.
* Log in to the NatWest mobile app within 90 days.

Once all criteria have been satisfied and verified after 90 days, the £500 welcome bonus will be paid within the following 30 days.

The offer is not available to customers who have previously received a switching incentive from the NatWest Group or who held a NatWest current account on 2 September 2026. Terms and restrictions apply.
