A five-bedroom detached house overlooking York’s Knavesmire is on the market for £1,200,000.

The property can be found at Tadcaster Road, in York, and is freehold, spanning 2,633 sq ft, with an EPC rating of D and no onward chain.

Set back from the road, it has driveway parking and a double garage.

Inside, two reception rooms have dual-aspect windows.

An open-plan kitchen and dining room form part of the accommodation.

A utility room, WC and sunroom are also provided.

The sunroom has garden views.

Upstairs, five bedrooms include three with private en-suite shower rooms.

A family bathroom serves the remaining bedrooms.

Outside, enclosed rear gardens face south-west and west.

They include patio areas, lawns and outbuilding storage.

It has access to local amenities, schools, York city centre and the railway station.

York city centre and the railway station are accessible from the property.

Both reception rooms have windows on two sides.

Kitchen and dining areas are arranged as an open-plan space.

Three bedrooms have their own en-suite facilities.

Bathrooms total four, including the three en-suite shower rooms and family bathroom.

Rear gardens are enclosed and include patio areas, lawns and outbuilding storage.

Parking is provided on the driveway, alongside the double garage.
