# Leeds Corn Exchange arrest after man 'threatening public with bottle'

## A man has been arrested on suspicion of multiple offences

An arrest has been made after a man was reported to be "threatening members of the public with a bottle" outside Leeds Corn Exchange.

A man was arrested earlier today, on Tuesday, November 3, on suspicion of multiple offences. He remain in West Yorkshire Police's custody.

A force spokesperson said: "This morning, officers responded to reports of an abusive male outside The Corn Exchange who was threatening members of the public with a bottle and attempting to fight passers-by.

"Officers swiftly attended the scene and arrested the male on suspicion of: Possession of Class A drugs; Public Order offences; Two counts of Assault on an Emergency Worker; The male remains in custody and the investigation is ongoing. Thank you to members of the public who reported the incident promptly."
