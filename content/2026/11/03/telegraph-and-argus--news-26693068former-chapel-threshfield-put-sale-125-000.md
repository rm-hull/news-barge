A FORMER chapel in the Yorkshire Dales with conversion potential has been put up for sale.

Threshfield Primitive Methodist Chapel is being marketed by property agents Dacre, Son & Hartley with a guide price of £125,000.

The stone-built, detached building dates back to 1878 and sits on a corner plot with surrounding grounds.

Ben Hollings, from Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Skipton office, said: "This is a charming building in a prime position in the ever-popular village of Threshfield.

"It’s ripe for conversion into a home or a commercial unit, potentially with two floors, subject to the buyer obtaining the relevant permissions.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21662655.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Threshfield Primitive Methodist Chapel, *(Image: Dacre, Son & Hartley)*

"It also has plenty of character and offers level disabled access, so it’s certain to capture people’s imagination.

"We’re therefore anticipating high levels of interest in this unique opportunity."

For more information, contact Dacre, Son & Hartley’s Skipton office, or visit: dacres.co.uk
