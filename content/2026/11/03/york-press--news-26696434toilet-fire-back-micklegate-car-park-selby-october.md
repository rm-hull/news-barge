A TOILET block has been set on fire in the heart of a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police said that it is appealing for witnesses after a disused toilet block was set alight in what is believed to be a deliberate blaze last month.

The incident took place in Back Micklegate Car Park, Selby, on Sunday, October 18.

A police spokesperson said: “There was a fire in a disused block of public toilets just before 4pm, and it is believed to have been started deliberately.

“We’re particularly appealing for witnesses in the moments leading up to the fire starting who may be able to identify potential suspects.”

If you have any information that could assist the investigation, please contact the force through their website, live chat, or by calling 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.
