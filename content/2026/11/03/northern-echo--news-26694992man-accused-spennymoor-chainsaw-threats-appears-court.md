A man accused of making threats while carrying a chainsaw in Spennymoor is due to appear before a judge.

Matthew O’Dowd, 36, of no fixed abode, appeared at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court this morning (November 3), charged with affray, possessing a knife, blade or pointed article in a public place and criminal damage.

During the brief appearance, the 36-year-old didn't enter any pleas.

He was remanded in custody until his next appearance on December 1, at Durham Crown Court, where he will appear before a judge.

It follows an incident that saw police called to Spennymoor town centre at around 2pm on Sunday (November 1).

They said a man had a chainsaw and was making threats to members of the public.

Armed officers were deployed, and a suspect was arrested and taken into custody.
