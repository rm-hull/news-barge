Housebuilder St. Modwen Homes has entered the Scottish market with four developments planned across the east and west of the country, delivering a total of 268 homes.

The brand, part of Miller Homes Group, is launching its first Scottish sites following the group’s acquisition of St. Modwen Homes in January 2025.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21666059.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Showhome, St. Modwen Homes at Shawfair. *(Image: Supplied)*

In the east, the St. Modwen Homes development at Shawfair in Danderhall, Midlothian, has opened for sales. The site, around nine miles from Edinburgh city centre, will provide 76 three, four and five-bedroom homes.

A further 70 homes are planned at Muiravonside Meadow in Whitecross, Falkirk.

In the west, the company is preparing to launch Huxley Quarter, a 55-home development at Clydesdale Works in Bellshill.

The fourth site, Everley Park in Bargeddie, is scheduled to deliver 67 homes from January 2027.

Together, the developments will provide 268 homes in locations across Midlothian, Falkirk and Lanarkshire.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21666060.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Interior of showhome, St. Modwen Homes at Shawfair. *(Image: Supplied)*

St. Modwen Homes specialises in contemporary family housing and already operates across England and Wales. Its expansion into Scotland is intended to give buyers a wider choice of house styles and specifications.

Miller Homes Group operates across 11 regions in England, Scotland and Wales. Founded in 1934, the housebuilder received a five-star rating from the Home Builders Federation in 2026, its 14th such rating in 15 years.

David McGrath, executive managing director for Scotland and the North at Miller Homes Group, said: "Launching the St. Modwen Homes brand in Scotland is an important milestone for the wider Miller Homes Group.

"These first developments demonstrate how we can use the strength of our established regional platform to provide a wider choice of high-quality homes for our customers, while maintaining the build quality and customer focus for which we are known.

"Shawfair, Muiravonside Meadow, Huxley Quarter and Everley Park will together contribute almost 270 new homes in well-connected locations.

"Prospective home buyers in these areas now have more choice in design and specification, backed by the same experienced teams and consistent standards across the Group."
