A schoolgirl has died after being struck by a bus in North Ayrshire.

Around 1.10pm, on Tuesday, November 3, police received a report of a crash involving a female pedestrian and a bus on Lauchlan Way in Kilwinning.

Emergency services attended, but the 12-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene.

Her family is aware.

There were no reports of any other injuries and enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances, police said.

Lauchlan Way has since reopened to traffic.

Sergeant Stewart Taylor, of Police Scotland, said: “Our thoughts remain with the family and friends of the girl who died at what is an extremely difficult time for them.

“We would ask that anyone with any information contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting reference 1484 of Tuesday, 3 November, 2026.”
