A much-loved rose garden wall in a popular public park was damaged over the weekend.

The incident took place on Saturday, October 31, in Brighouse's Wellholme Park.

It's left community members and volunteers feeling really disappointed.

## Group disappointed

![This was the damage to the wall](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21665942.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This was the damage to the wall *(Image: Wellholme Park)*

Carla Thompson, a trustee for Wellholme Park, said: "We are extremely disappointed by the recent damage to the rose garden wall.

"Our volunteers work hard to help maintain and improve Wellholme Park, while also creating projects and community events that bring people together and make a positive difference.

"It is equally disappointing for the wider community who enjoy and value the park.

"These incidents do not represent Wellholme Park or the overwhelmingly positive impact it has on its visitors."

![This was the damage to the wall](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21665945.jpg?type=mds-article-620)What a picturesque park *(Image: Wellholme Park)*

Wellholme Park spans 32.6 acres and is valued for its green space, mature trees, and walking routes.

It also supports flood alleviation efforts to protect parts of Brighouse.

Carla Thompson said the Friends group would continue to work closely with Calderdale Council to support the park’s development and upkeep.

![This was the damage to the wall](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21665943.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It didn't take long for repairs to begin *(Image: Wellholme Park)*

## What the council said

Councillor Peter Hunt, Calderdale Council’s cabinet member for public services, said: "We are extremely disappointed about the damage caused to the rose garden in Wellholme Park on Saturday night.

"We know how much the park means to local people as a great place to play, relax, exercise, and spend time with loved ones.

"We are assessing the damage and making arrangements to carry out repairs as quickly as possible, in partnership with the Friends of Wellholme Park.

"We are committed to restoring the affected area and ensuring the park remains a welcoming place for everyone to enjoy."
