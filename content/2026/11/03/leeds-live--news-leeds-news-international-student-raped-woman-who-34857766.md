# International student raped woman who feared he would kill her

## Vahora was in the country as a student

An international student put a woman in 'fear of death' before raping her.

Uvesh Vahora, 36, of no fixed abode, was found guilty of raping a woman on Autumn Avenue, Hyde Park, on the night of Thursday, April 9. He has been remanded in custody to be sentenced on Wesdnesday, November 11, following a trial at Leeds Crown Court.

The victim was able to identify the property where the offence took place and Vahora, who was living there at the time, was arrested. The court was told Vahora, whose self-defined ethnicity is Asian Indian, was in the country as a student.

West Yorkshire Police Detective Inspector Tom Crosfill said: “Vahora has subjected the victim to a terrifying experience with her being in genuine fear for her life thinking that he was going to kill her. After disclosing the offence to friends, she contacted police with officers arresting Vahora within around five hours of this offence taking place. He has been held on remand since that time. In the meantime, those working on this investigation have gathered the evidence including victim and witness statements, CCTV and digital lines of enquiry that has led to Vahora’s conviction.

“I understand how incredibly difficult it can be to report rape and other sexual offences and I really hope that this conviction demonstrates how seriously an offence of this nature is treated from the outset. Our message to victims is that you will be listened to, you will be supported and we will do everything possible to bring perpetrators before the courts.”
