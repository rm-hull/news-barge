Harrogate-based robotics and advanced engineering company Synthotech Group has appointed Andy Vine as commercial director as it prepares for further growth in the UK and international utility markets.

The appointment follows a record year for the business in 2025, when turnover increased by 33 per cent to £6.5 million and profits doubled year on year.

Mr Vine will lead Synthotech’s commercial strategy, customer engagement and market development as the company expands the deployment of robotic technologies designed to help utility companies maintain ageing infrastructure more efficiently.

## Synthotech expands its robotics technology

The company’s technologies are designed to reduce disruption, costs and carbon emissions associated with maintaining and upgrading essential infrastructure across water, gas and telecommunications networks.

Growth has been supported by continued investment in research and development, with several technologies moving from development into operational deployment.

These include MicroBORE, a trenchless drilling system developed in collaboration with BT Group and the University of Surrey. The technology is being trialled as part of the UK’s fibre broadband rollout.

Synthotech has also developed a robotic gas service replacement system that allows connections to be renewed from inside existing pipes, avoiding the need for extensive excavation.

Mr Vine brings experience in leadership, account management and project management across the utility sector. He will be responsible for developing customer relationships and identifying opportunities for the company’s technology in existing and new markets.

## New commercial director joins at a key stage

> **Andy Vine, commercial director at Synthotech Group**
>
> I’m joining Synthotech at such an exciting stage in the company’s journey. The business has built a strong reputation for developing innovative technologies that solve real challenges for utility companies, helping them improve network performance while reducing disruption for customers and communities.
>
> What particularly attracted me to Synthotech was the strength of its innovation pipeline and the opportunity to help accelerate the commercial adoption of technologies that can transform the way critical infrastructure is maintained. The business has achieved impressive growth, and I look forward to working with the team to build on that success both in the UK and internationally.

Synthotech works with utility companies, infrastructure operators and academic partners, including the Pipebots team at the University of Sheffield, to accelerate the adoption of robotic inspection, maintenance and repair technologies.

> **Mark Tindley, managing director of Synthotech Group**

> His experience across the utility sector, combined with his leadership and commercial expertise, will be invaluable as we continue to scale the business, strengthen customer relationships and expand into new markets. Andy’s appointment reflects our confidence in the opportunities ahead and our commitment to investing in the team needed to support the next phase of growth.

### About Synthotech Group

Synthotech Group is a Harrogate-based robotics and advanced engineering company developing robotic technologies for the utility and infrastructure sectors.
