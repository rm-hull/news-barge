A UK furniture company has closed down after nine years and entered liquidation.

Rainbows Furniture, established in 2017, was a small "family-run" company which specialised in bespoke, solid-wood kitchen units and furniture and had built a loyal customer base.

The company boasted a 3.6-star rating on Google Reviews, described by customers as "amazing".

## Rainbows Furniture closes after 9 years and enters liquidation

Now, after nine years in business, Rainbows Furniture has closed and entered voluntary liquidation.

Robert Cooksey of Bridgestones Limited was appointed liquidator on October 30, according to The Gazette.

Its website has been taken offline, and customers trying to get in touch by email have received an automated reply confirming the closure.

The message, shared on a public Facebook group, stated: "Please be advised that Rainbows Furniture Ltd has ceased trading and this email account is no longer actively monitored.

"The company is currently in the process of entering creditors voluntary liquidation.

"For any enquiries relating to Rainbows Furniture Ltd, including outstanding orders, accounts, payments or creditor matters, please contact the insolvency practitioner.

"Please note that further emails sent to this Rainbows Furniture Ltd email address will not be monitored or responded to."
