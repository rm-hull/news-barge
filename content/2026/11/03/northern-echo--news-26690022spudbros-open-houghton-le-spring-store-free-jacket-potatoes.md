Fans of the viral jacket potato brand SpudBros will be able to get their hands on a free meal this week as the family-run social media sensation opens a new store in the North East.

The popular business, which has built a huge following online thanks to its loaded jacket potatoes, is launching a new SpudBros Express outlet in Houghton-le-Spring on Tuesday, November 3.

To celebrate the opening, the first 100 customers through the doors from midday will receive a free spud.

Jacob Nelson, co-founder of SpudBros, said: “We're buzzing to be bringing SpudBros to Tyne and Wear and opening the doors in Houghton-le-Spring.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21658634.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jacob and Harley Nelson. *(Image: PROVIDED)*

“The North East has always shown us loads of love online, so getting to bring the full SpudBros experience closer to people here is really exciting for us.

“It's mad seeing how quickly SpudBros Express is growing around the country, but wherever we open, it's still all about serving proper, fully loaded spuds and giving people great value.

“We can't wait to see everyone down there and the first 100 spuds are on us.”

The Mercantile Road site is part of a nationwide expansion bringing 30 roadside SpudBros outlets to EG On The Move forecourts across the UK within three years.

It is one of 21 new locations planned this year as SpudBros expands beyond traditional town and city centres.

SpudBros Express serves freshly prepared jacket potatoes for customers on the move, including the Tram Classic with garlic butter, three-cheese mix, beans, crispy onions and Tram Sauce.

The family business began as a hot potato cart in Preston’s Flag Market in 1955.

Brothers Jacob and Harley Nelson have built the brand into a social media food hit, with more than five million TikTok followers and millions more across YouTube and Instagram.

The Houghton-le-Spring store will open seven days a week, from 11am to 9pm Sunday to Thursday and 11am to 10pm on Friday and Saturday.

Customers can also choose a build-your-own spud or items from the brand's recently launched breakfast selection.
