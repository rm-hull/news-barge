# 'Large quantity of clothing' stolen after ram raid on Leeds shop

## A ‘large quantity of clothing’ was stolen in a Leeds city centre raid when thieves rammed a car into a shop front.

Police said a Kia Ceed deliberately drove into the Belstaff clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds on Monday night, before ‘a number of people’ entered the store and stole goods.

![Belstaff store on Vicar Lane, Leeds.
Photograph by Tony Johnson. 3rd November 2026]([https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/03/15/2026_11_03_15_49_17_712_ASH_483D99071B044F42AD336EB59B1A79F5.JPG?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/03/15/2026_11_03_15_49_17_712_ASH_483D99071B044F42AD336EB59B1A79F5.JPG?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65))


A spokesperson said: “Officers were called at around 8.40pm last night (2 November) to reports that a Kia Ceed had deliberately driven into a clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds along with another Kia Ceed being present at the time.

“It was reported to police that a number of people entered the shop and stole a large quantity of clothing.

“Enquiries remain ongoing by Leeds CID.”
