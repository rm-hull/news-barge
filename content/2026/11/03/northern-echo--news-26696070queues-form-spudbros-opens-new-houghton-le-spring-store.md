Queues snaked out of the door as hungry customers turned out in force for the opening of TikTok-viral potato brand SpudBros' newest store.

Fans of the social media sensation patiently waited to get their hands on a loaded jacket potato as the firm's new outlet opened in Houghton-le-Spring on Tuesday (November 3).

The store opened on Mercantile Road at lunchtime with a red ribbon-cutting and a shower of gold streamers, as excited customers packed into the new branch.

The first 100 customers through the doors from midday received a free spud as part of the opening-day celebrations.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666725.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Two spud super-fans queued outside the shop all night for the opening.

Caroline Harris and Robin Catnatch joined the queue outside the new SpudBros Express in Houghton-le-Spring at 10.45pm on Monday (November 11).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666727.jpg?type=mds-article-620)First customers Caroline Harris and Robin Catnach from Prudhoe. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Caroline said: "We wanted to get here early, and if we'd have come today the earliest we could have got here was probably nearer to 8am and we thought it would be really busy.

"So we set ourselves a challenge to see what time we could get here for.

"At 4pm yesterday I had my wisdom tooth taken out. I went to a drama course and then got straight on the bus, four buses to get here."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666736.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

SpudBros started as a hot-potato cart at Preston's Flag Market in 1955 before brothers Jacob and Harley Nelson turned the family venture into an online sensation.

They now have more than five million TikTok followers and a huge audience on YouTube and Instagram.

Their videos regularly attract millions of views, helping make the baked potato one of the country's biggest food trends.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666722.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Spud Bros opens at Houghton-le-Spring. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Speaking about the new opening, Jacob said: "The North East has always shown us loads of love online, so getting to bring the full SpudBros experience closer to people here is really exciting for us.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666726.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Harley Nelson one of the founders of Spud Bros. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"It's mad seeing how quickly SpudBros Express is growing around the country, but wherever we open, it's still all about serving proper, fully loaded spuds and giving people great value."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666735.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The Houghton-le-Spring branch is part of a partnership with EG On The Move to open 30 SpudBros Express sites at UK forecourts over the next three years.

It is one of 21 sites due to open this year.

The menu includes the popular Tram Classic, topped with garlic butter, a three-cheese blend, baked beans, crispy onions and signature Tram Sauce.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666737.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*
