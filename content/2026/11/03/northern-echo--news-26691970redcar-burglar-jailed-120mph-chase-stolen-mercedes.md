A North East burglar was arrested when police used a stinger to bring his dangerous driving to a halt.

CCTV showed Michael Bishop and an accomplice outside a Middlesbrough house before Bishop stole keys and drove off in a Mercedes E350.

The 23-year-old was later spotted in the stolen car on the A66, sparking a police chase, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Jenny Haigh said the pair burgled the home in the early hours while covering their faces, but Bishop was identified by his jacket and trainers.

She said police spotted the stolen car on the A66 and began a pursuit after officers activated their blue lights.

“The vehicle reached speeds in excess of 120mph in a 40mph limit,” she said. “It was weaving across lanes, causing other vehicles to move out of the way.

“A stinger was activated, but he continued at speed along the A66 before heading south on the A19 despite having two flat tyres.”

Miss Haigh said two police cars boxed Bishop in, but he drove into one before trying to escape on foot.

Police used a drone to locate Bishop, who refused to provide a sample for analysis and was found in possession of Class A and C drugs.

Bishop, of Stable Mews, Redcar, admitted burglary, car theft, dangerous driving, driving while disqualified, possessing Class A and C drugs and failing to provide a specimen for analysis on September 19.

His lawyer, Simon Walker, said Bishop had been recalled to prison on licence after his arrest on September 19.

Judge Richard Clews jailed Bishop for 30 months and banned him from driving for four years and six months.

He said: “Burglary of a house at night when the occupants are inside asleep is always serious. It causes a lot of anxiety and upset.

“It can cause people to feel unsafe in their own home.

“You took the car and drove it dangerously at ridiculous speeds and you were disqualified from driving at the time.”
