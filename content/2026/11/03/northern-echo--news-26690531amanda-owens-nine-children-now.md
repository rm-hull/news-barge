Amanda Owen's nine children are set to return to television after Channel 4 confirmed two more series of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess and former husband Clive Owen became household names through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, which documented their extraordinary lives raising nine children at remote Ravenseat Farm in Swaledale.

Now, with series five and six officially commissioned, viewers will get another opportunity to follow all 11 members of the Owen family.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21340602.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda Owen, Clive Owen and their children *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The broadcaster has revealed that the new episodes will show the older children taking on increasing responsibilities at Ravenseat, while the younger members of the family continue to enjoy life on the farm.

## How many children does Amanda Owen have?

All nine grew up at Ravenseat, where they became familiar to viewers through their appearances on Our Yorkshire Farm.

The children have been involved in farming activities from an early age, helping with animals, machinery and the everyday demands of life in the Yorkshire Dales.

## Raven Owen

Raven is the eldest of Amanda and Clive's nine children.

Viewers watched her grow up at Ravenseat before she left to pursue her education and develop her own life away from the family's day-to-day farming activities.

She has continued to appear in the family's television story, returning to Ravenseat in the fourth series of Our Farm Next Door.

## Reuben Owen

Reuben has established a television career of his own after developing an interest in machinery, engineering and rural contracting.

He has starred in Channel 5's Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales, which follows his work with machinery and rural projects.

His practical skills were a familiar feature of Our Yorkshire Farm, where viewers watched him working with vehicles and equipment from a young age.

## Miles Owen

Miles has continued to take an active role in the family's farming life and the restoration of Anty John's.

He has appeared in Our Farm Next Door helping with the renovation, including painting work as the farmhouse's interior takes shape.

Channel 4 has confirmed that the fifth series will show Miles taking on increasing farming responsibilities alongside his brother Sidney.

## Edith Owen

Edith has grown up in front of the cameras and continues to help with activities at Ravenseat.

The fourth series of Our Farm Next Door followed her balancing education with work around the farm.

She has also helped with the family's cream tea van, which welcomes visitors to Ravenseat.

Channel 4 says the fifth series will feature Edith, Violet and their sisters reopening the van as the summer season gets underway.

## Violet Owen

Violet has frequently appeared helping with animals and other farming activities.

She has grown up surrounded by the responsibilities of life at Ravenseat, with the family's television programmes documenting her involvement in the farm.

She will feature in the new series as the sisters prepare to open the cream tea van.

## Sidney Owen

Sidney, often known as Sid, has developed an interest in the practical side of farming.

He has appeared in Our Farm Next Door helping with tasks around Ravenseat and the renovation project.

Channel 4 has confirmed that series five will follow Sidney and Miles as they take on more farming responsibilities.

## Annas Owen

Annas is among the younger Owen children who continue to feature in Our Farm Next Door.

The fourth-series finale, scheduled for November 2, sees her joining sister Clemmie to sell eggs from the family's chickens to earn extra pocket money.

Like her siblings, she has grown up helping with animals and learning about farm life.

## Clementine Owen

Clementine, known as Clemmie, is another familiar face from the family's television programmes.

The fourth-series finale features Clemmie heading out on horseback with younger sister Nancy for their first ride since winter.

She also joins Annas in selling eggs from the family's chickens.

## Nancy Owen

Nancy is Amanda and Clive's youngest child.

She has grown up on television alongside her eight siblings and continues to feature in the family's adventures at Ravenseat.

The fourth-series finale follows Nancy and Clemmie as they ride across the moors and into a wildflower meadow.

## What will the Owen children do in series five and six?

Channel 4 has confirmed that all 11 members of the Owen family will feature in the two new series.

Series five will pick up after Amanda and her daughters' first sleepover at Anty John's and follow the family from early summer 2026 through to spring 2027.

Edith, Violet and their sisters will reopen the cream tea van at Ravenseat, while Sidney and Miles take on more farming duties.

The programme will also continue documenting the restoration of Anty John's, as the family works to transform the historic farmhouse into a home.

Amanda and Clive, who announced their separation in 2022, have continued working together on the project and co-parenting their children.

The new commission consists of ten hour-long episodes, split into series five and six.

Broadcast dates have not yet been announced.
