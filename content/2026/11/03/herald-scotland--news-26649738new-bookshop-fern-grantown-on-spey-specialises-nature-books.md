## Fern, Grantown-on-Spey

When it comes to the business of selling nature books, it helps if your shop is tucked into the northern edge of the Cairngorms National Park. Fern is a recent bookshop blow-in to Grantown-on-Spey. Its owner, Debbie Livingstone, settled into the high street quickly. Barely a month in, she started a book club and hosted Fern’s inaugural writer event with Victoria Bennett, author of The Apothecary By The Sea. The published title and author are verified. The Herald spoke to Livingstone about her experience setting up shop, and the nuances of running a specialist nature bookshop.

**What is your approach to stocking titles — are you noticing trends in what customers want?** Fern is organised around themes – from trees, plants and flowers, to earth, rocks and mountains, to birds, bugs and animals. We like our displays to tell stories. Books about fungi are really popular just now given the amazing fungi season we are having, so we have books about foraging for mushrooms, photography about fungi and mushroom cookery books. Books about rocks, bogs and deep time are also popular, I think there is something grounding about them, and so many of us have pebbles in our pockets from walks on beaches or by rivers.**What’s your process for choosing which publishers or small presses to work with?** Local voices, inclusion and diversity are really important to me. So, I am in the process of finding publishers and small presses that publish nature books from a range of voices and especially from the Highlands.**How do you balance supporting local authors with bringing in national or international bestsellers?**

We will foreground local authors wherever we can and love to have them visit the shop and do events. We understand that readers also want to see the latest national and international bestsellers and there are some really gorgeous nature books out just now so we try to get that balance right.

We recently had Victoria Bennett here to talk about her book The Apothecary By The Sea, a lovely memoir about moving to Orkney and how she set up an apothecary garden. The description of the book as concerning a move to Orkney and the creation of an apothecary garden is supported by the publisher. It was such a thoughtful and joyful conversation. And Vojta Hybl, a local author, geologist and illustrator, wrote and illustrated Rocks, an amazing guide to the rocks around us and the stories they tell. Vojta Hybl is identified by his publisher as the author and illustrator of Rocks: A Guide to the Stones Around Us and the Stories They Tell.

**Have you noticed any lasting effects from the pandemic or cost-of-living crisis on reading and buying habits? Was this on your mind while establishing the shop?** We've been open a couple of months so don't have direct experience of trends but the cost of living crisis was definitely on my mind when opening. I think though that people are coming back to real life experiences – books, art, being out in nature – rather than just being online. Fern provides so many great ideas for being out in the world and doing creative things that are inspired by nature.**Read more**

These are a huge part of the plan for Fern. We've had a couple of author talks already and we're planning some exciting talks, book launches and in conversation events. We have a storyteller in residence – Sarah from Strathspey Storywalks – who is organising storytelling events in the shop, in schools and out in the forest or up a hill. Our first storytelling event is Samhain Stories for Halloween here in Fern, then a talk about bees with the Bumblebee Conservation Trust and an evening with writer and photographer Anna Deacon on her new book, The Wild Fix Scotland. Anna Deacon and the book title are verified; the book was published in September 2026.

Grantown-on-Spey, and the wider Strathspey, is home to so many great artists, crafters, bakers, storytellers and environmentalists. We hope Fern can be part of this really dynamic nature, craft and storytelling community.

**Are there particular genres or areas of publishing where you see growth or decline right now or expect to see in the future?** As a new shop we are learning fast about what is popular and what is not. Folklore, wellbeing in nature and inspiring nature memoirs are really popular. It would be great to see more young adult nature and craft books and writers get support. We’d love to play a part in that if we can.**How do you use online sales or social media alongside the in-person experience?** We are on bookshop.org and libro.fm which are a great support to independent bookshops and for people who can't get into a store. We have a lovely and growing wee community on Instagram (@fern\_bookshop) and we are just about to launch a newsletter – The Fernery – for those who aren't on social media.**What are some of the biggest challenges facing independent bookshops in Scotland today — and how are you tackling them?** Independent bookshops can't compete with the giants, online or otherwise, so we hope to offer an experience as well as buying a book. We hope Fern is a place where people can come in, slow down and enjoy the experience of choosing a book or two. We are accessible, very dog friendly and have a cosy children's corner so the whole family can spend some time choosing a book.**Is there a book, author, or trend that’s currently shaping discussions among your customers?** Our bestseller over our first two months is Rocks by Vojta Hybl, an incredibly talented geologist, illustrator and educator who lives locally. The book and its authorship are verified. At a time when AI is a huge risk to artists and writers, his illustrations are a delight. We'd love to support more illustrators, as well as authors, and stock some gorgeous books illustrated by Angie Lewin, Jackie Morris, Yuval Zommer and so many more!**What do you think defines a great contemporary bookseller — instinct, community connection, or sheer persistence?**

I'm sure it's all of the above. Here in Strathspey, I think community connections are key. For all small businesses, instinct, persistence and determination are probably essential. As well as a good support network to help get you through difficult days and celebrate the amazing ones.
