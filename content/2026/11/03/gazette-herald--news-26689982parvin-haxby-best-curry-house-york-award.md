A YORK curry house is hoping to add to its accolades with another prestigious award.

The Parvin Indian Restaurant, in York Road, Haxby, has been shortlisted for the Best Curry House in York award after hundreds of readers nominated their favourite spot in the city.

Safiqur Rahman has run The Parvin, at its current site, for more than 18 years.




Speaking about the award, he said: “Having previously won the Press’ Best Curry House in York award in 2017 and 2024, we know firsthand what this recognition can mean for a local business.

“It introduces us to new customers, welcomes back familiar faces and reminds us why we love doing what we do.

“To be nominated once again is an honour in itself.

“We would love nothing more than to bring the award home again, but whatever the outcome, our commitment remains the same: to serve excellent food, provide a warm welcome and continue giving our customers the very best experience possible”

Among The Parvin’s most popular offerings is the Sunday buffet, which costs £15.90 per adult and has attracted customers from across the area over the years.

Safiqur said: “We always pride ourselves on using the freshest & the highest quality ingredients combined with cooking skills & techniques which we have mastered over decades.

“Another main ingredient of our success has been our consistency, whether it’s dining in with us, collecting a takeaway or having it delivered straight to your door we want every experience to be just as enjoyable as the last.”

The Parvin also hosts a monthly charity quiz night.

Its popularity has become so that the restaurant now holds two per month for various causes.

![The Parvin](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20116344.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Parvin *(Image: Supplied)*

Safiqur said: “It’s always a great night for curry and fun with friends or family for great causes.

“It’s our way of giving back to the local and surrounding areas that have helped build our success over many years.”

The dishes are served by The Parvin’s eight full-time staff, along with a number of part-time weekend staff who ensure orders run smoothly.

Aside from its Monday to Saturday service, the restaurant hosts charity quiz nights “once or twice a month” in partnership with local charities and opens its doors to its famous Sunday discount buffets from 3pm till 10pm.

It will also open on Christmas Day and over the New Year.
