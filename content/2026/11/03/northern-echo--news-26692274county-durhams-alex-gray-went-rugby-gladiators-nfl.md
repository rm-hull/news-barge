To millions of TV viewers, Alex Gray is best known as Apollo, one of the towering stars of the BBC's hit reboot of Gladiators.

Long before his Saturday-night fame, Gray's journey began in County Durham and took him from North East rugby to the NFL and television.

It is a career path unlike almost any other.

Born in County Durham in 1991, Gray grew up with ambitions in sport and would soon emerge as one of the region's brightest rugby prospects.

While many young athletes dream of reaching the top of one sport, Gray would eventually make his mark in three very different arenas.

### A rising rugby star

Before television audiences knew him as Apollo, rugby supporters knew him as one of the game's most exciting young talents.

Gray represented England at Under-20 level and helped his country reach the final of the Junior World Championship, showcasing the pace and power that would become hallmarks of his game.

His club career was closely linked to the North East through Newcastle Falcons, where he established himself as a fan favourite and one of the Premiership side's most dangerous attacking players.

At the time, few would have predicted that rugby would be only one chapter of his story.

### Swapping rugby for the NFL

![Alex Gray stands inside a historic stone building with stained-glass windows in the background, looking towards the camera while wearing a dark hooded top.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661688.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alex Gray *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

In 2017, Gray made a decision that surprised many in the sport.

Rather than continue a successful rugby career, he chose to pursue a place in the NFL through the league's International Player Pathway programme.

The move took him thousands of miles from home and into an entirely different sporting world.

American football was unfamiliar territory, but Gray's athleticism quickly caught the attention of coaches.

He went on to spend time with the Atlanta Falcons as he attempted to break into the world's most famous American football competition.

The move cemented Gray's reputation as an athlete willing to risk everything for his ambitions.

### Finding fame as Apollo

Another reinvention followed in 2024 when Gray joined the cast of the BBC's rebooted Gladiators.

Taking on the role of Apollo, he became one of the programme's standout stars as the iconic series returned to television screens and found a whole new generation of fans.

His athletic background made him a natural fit for the competition, while his imposing six-foot-five frame quickly made him one of the most recognisable Gladiators in the arena.

To many viewers, Apollo seemed to appear from nowhere.

In reality, his TV success was built on years of elite competition across two continents.

### County Durham roots remain part of the story

For County Durham, Gray's rise remains a remarkable example of local talent reaching the national stage.

From rugby pitches in the North East to NFL training camps in the United States and prime-time television audiences across the UK, his career has followed an unlikely route.

Yet despite the twists and turns, it all began in the county he still proudly calls home.

Long before the lights, cameras and Gladiators arena, Alex Gray was a County Durham youngster with a passion for sport and a willingness to chase opportunities wherever they might lead.

Few could have imagined that journey would take him all the way to Apollo.
