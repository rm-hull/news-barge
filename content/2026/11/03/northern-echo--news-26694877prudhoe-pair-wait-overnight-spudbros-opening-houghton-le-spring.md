Two devoted SpudBros fans spent the night queuing outside the TikTok-viral chain's new North East outlet to be first through the doors on opening day.

Caroline Harris and Robin Catnatch joined the queue outside the new SpudBros Express in Houghton-le-Spring at 10.45pm on Monday (November 11)

Caroline travelled by bus from Prudhoe, taking four separate services, before settling in for an overnight wait having just had her wisdom tooth out.

The viral business opened on Mercantile Road at lunchtime, with the first 100 customers through the doors given a free spud.

Caroline said: "We wanted to get here early, and if we'd have come today the earliest we could have got here was probably nearer to 8am and we thought it would be really busy.

"So we set ourselves a challenge to see what time we could get here for.

"At 4pm yesterday I had my wisdom tooth taken out. I went to a drama course and then got straight on the bus, four buses to get here."

Jacob one of the 'SpudBros' said: "The North East has always shown us loads of love online, so getting to bring the full SpudBros experience closer to people here is really exciting for us.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21658634.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jacob and Harley Nelson. *(Image: PROVIDED)*

"It's mad seeing how quickly SpudBros Express is growing around the country, but wherever we open, it's still all about serving proper, fully loaded spuds and giving people great value."

The Houghton-le-Spring site forms part of a major partnership with EG On The Move that aims to bring 30 SpudBros Express outlets to forecourts around the country over the next three years.

It is one of 21 locations due to open this year.

Customers visiting the new outlet can choose from a range of toppings, including the brand's 'Tram Classic', which comes topped with garlic butter, a three-cheese mix, baked beans, crispy onions and 'Tram Sauce'.

SpudBros began life as a hot potato cart in Preston's Flag Market in 1955, but has enjoyed a dramatic rise in popularity under brothers Jacob and Harley Nelson.

The pair have turned the family business into a social media sensation, attracting more than five million followers on TikTok as well as millions more across YouTube and Instagram.

The new Houghton-le-Spring branch will open seven days a week, from 11am to 9pm Sunday to Thursday and from 11am to 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays.
