The Berlin Police announced via WhatsApp that Lichtenberg, City West, and the government district are affected by the "extensive closures." The U5 subway line will temporarily not stop at the Bundestag subway station. Waterways and Berlin's airspace are also affected by the closures, according to the announcement.
