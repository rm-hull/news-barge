Detectives are appealing for witnesses after a clothing shop in Leeds city centre was targeted in a ram raid.

Police were called to Vicar Lane at around 8.40pm on November 2 following reports that a Kia Ceed had been deliberately driven into the premises.

A second Kia Ceed was also reported to have been at the scene at the time of the incident.

## **Large quantity of clothing stolen**

Officers were told that a number of people entered the shop after the vehicle struck the building and made off with a large quantity of clothing.

No arrests have yet been announced.

Leeds CID is continuing to investigate the incident and enquiries remain ongoing.

## **Detectives seek information and footage**

Detectives are now urging anyone who witnessed the ram raid, or who may have captured relevant footage, to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police via 101 or through the force's Live Chat service, quoting reference 13260623871.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111.

Anyone who was in the area at the time and has CCTV, dashcam or mobile phone footage is encouraged to contact police.

## **FULL statement from West Yorkshire Police**

A WYP spokesperson said: "Detectives investigating a ram raid at a clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds city centre yesterday (2 November) are appealing for witnesses.

"Officers were called at around 8.40pm last night (2 November) to reports that a Kia Ceed had deliberately driven into a clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds along with another Kia Ceed being present at the time.

"It was reported to police that a number of people entered the shop and stole a large quantity of clothing.

"Enquiries remain ongoing by Leeds CID.

"Anyone who witnessed the incident, has any footage, or can provide information are asked to contact police via 101 or use the Live Chat facility quoting reference 13260623871.

"Information can always be given anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111"
