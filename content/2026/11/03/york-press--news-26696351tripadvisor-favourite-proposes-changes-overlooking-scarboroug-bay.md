A TOP TripAdvisor-rated café in Scarborough has applied to make changes outside the South Bay business.

The Brewed Coffee Shop and Eatery on Museum Terrace wants a new pavement licence from North Yorkshire Council.

Located near the Rotunda Museum, the establishment is rated as one of the top coffee and tea spots in the resort town and has a score of 4.3 out of five on the customer review website.

Claire Brining’s proposal would see eight tables and 16 chairs placed on the pavement outside the business at 5-6 Museum Terrace.

The café is well-rated for its small bites and selection of beverages.

One customer said: “It is great for breakfast and lunch with indoor and outdoor seating overlooking South Bay. The staff are lovely, and the food is amazing.”

Another recent visitor commented: “It was so good for morning coffee and cake that we went back for lunch!

“The hot chocolate was superb, and I highly recommend this. Our friends enjoyed their coffees too. We all chose carrot cake…which was moist and delicious.”

​The deadline for members of the public to comment on the pavement seating plans is Monday, November 9.  
​  
Anyone wishing to make representations regarding the application should give notice in writing to: North Yorkshire Council, Licensing Services, Town Hall, St Nicholas Street, Scarborough, YO11 2HG.  
​  
​The authority can also be contacted by email at [licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.east@northyorks.gov.uk.)  
​
