## Take a look inside £525,000 Victorian terraced home with loft conversion

A Victorian terraced house in York has gone on the market for £525,000.

The four-bedroom property in Nunmill Street has been beautifully refurbished throughout to the highest standard, according to estate agent Hudson Moody.

It has been extended to the rear and into the loft, offering a through living, dining and kitchen space, four bedrooms and two bathrooms.

The home features a bespoke shaker kitchen with built-in appliances and quartz worktops.

It also boasts an open-plan kitchen, dining, and living room.

The first floor houses two double bedrooms and a family bathroom with a claw-foot bath and brass furnishings.

Two additional bedrooms and a shower room can be found in the loft conversion.

The rear courtyard includes additional storage space.

The house is set in a highly sought-after location, with a 15-minute walk to the train station, Rowntree Park, and excellent schools.

It is in the popular Bishopthorpe Road area of the city, near local shops and services and with easy access to the city centre and railway station.

Attention to detail is evident throughout this beautifully refurbished period home.

Upon entering the property, contemporary mosaic tiles create a stylish first impression, before leading through to the beautifully appointed living space.

A light tunnel floods the room with natural light, whilst a central island provides additional storage and a dedicated breakfast seating area.

Viewing is highly recommended to appreciate the beautiful finish and high standard of renovation.
