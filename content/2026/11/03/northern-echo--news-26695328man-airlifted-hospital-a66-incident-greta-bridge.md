A walker was airlifted to hospital after a lorry's blown-out tyre struck his leg on the A66 in County Durham.

The man was walking on a path beside the A66 at Greta Bridge, near the Morrit Arms Hotel, when a lorry suffered a tyre blowout.

Part of the tyre hit the man, causing a serious leg injury.

Police and paramedics rushed to the scene, and the man was airlifted to James Cook Hospital in Middlesbrough.

A Great North Air Ambulance Service (GNAAS) spokesperson said: "Our critical care team was activated at 12.25pm to reports of a road traffic collision on the A66 near Greta Bridge.

"We had two paramedics and a doctor on board our aircraft and they arrived on scene at 12.38pm.

"Our team worked alongside the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) to assess and treat a patient and airlifted them to hospital."

A NEAS spokesperson added: "We dispatched a double-crewed ambulance and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service. One patient was taken by air to James Cook Hospital."

No further details on the man's condition have been confirmed.
