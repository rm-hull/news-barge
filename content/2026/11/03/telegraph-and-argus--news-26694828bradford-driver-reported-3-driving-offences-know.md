A driver has been reported to court after police discovered three separate driving offences.

Officers from Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT) were passing through Paley Road, East Bowling, when they identified a vehicle displaying false registration plates.

The vehicle was stopped and checks carried out by officers revealed the driver was disqualified from driving.

Further enquiries established the driver was also behind the wheel without valid insurance.

Police seized the vehicle at the roadside and the driver has been reported for three offences: using false registration plates, driving while disqualified and driving without insurance.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers from Bradford South NPT Team 3 were passing through Paley Road, Bradford East, when they identified a vehicle displaying false registration plates.

"The vehicle was stopped and checks revealed that the driver was disqualified from driving and was also driving without insurance.

"The vehicle was seized and the driver has been reported for the offences. The matter will now be dealt with through the courts.

"Using false registration plates is a serious offence and can often be linked to wider criminality.

"Bradford South NPT remains committed to proactively targeting offenders and keeping our roads safe."
