# Ram-raiders smash Kia into posh Leeds store and loot designer clothes

## Police are appealing for witnesses

Detectives are appealing for witnesses after a car was deliberately driven into a Leeds city centre clothing shop.

Police were called at around 8.40pm on Monday (November 2) to the Belstaff store on Vicar Lane, in the Victoria Quarter.

A Kia Ceed was driven into the shop while a second Kia Ceed was there at the time. Several people then went inside and stole a large quantity of clothing.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Officers were called at around 8.40pm last night (2 November) to reports that a Kia Ceed had deliberately driven into a clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds along with another Kia Ceed being present at the time.

"It was reported to police that a number of people entered the shop and stole a large quantity of clothing. Enquiries remain ongoing by Leeds CID."

Pictures from the scene showed the shop’s front window smashed and glass and debris across the pavement, with police tape around the store.

Belstaff is a brand known for its expensive coats and jackets.

Anyone who saw what happened, has footage, or has information is asked to contact police on 101 or use the Live Chat facility, quoting reference 13260623871.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
