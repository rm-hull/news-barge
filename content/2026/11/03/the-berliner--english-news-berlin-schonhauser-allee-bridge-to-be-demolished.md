*IMAGO / PEMAX*

Preparations for the new Schönhauser-Allee bridge in Prenzlauer Berg began on Monday. Initially, Dänenstraße will be closed to cars and cyclists. The major construction project will be coordinated from there until 2032. The pedestrian crossing between Dänenstraße and the Schönhauser Allee Arcades will be slightly relocated and secured with a temporary traffic-light system. Because the S-Bahn station is located underneath the bridge and the U-Bahn station above it, the work is extremely complex.

During the construction work on Dänenstraße, pedestrian traffic is only permitted on the sidewalk along the residential buildings. Cars can no longer drive through the street. According to the transport authority, pedestrian traffic on Schönhauser Allee will be maintained in both directions throughout the entire construction period.

Pedestrian walkways will be routed around the construction areas. However, the northern entrance to the Schönhauser Allee U-Bahn station will be temporarily closed. The station will remain accessible via the other entrances.

Traffic for cars, buses, trams and bicycles will continue to be guaranteed in both directions along Schönhauser Allee, according to the transport authority. Following the preparatory work, the gas, water and sewage pipes located in the old bridge will be shut down and relocated.

In March, the Senate in Berlin announced its “Master Plan Bridges 2025-2040.” An estimated €1.84 billion has been allocated for the 175 dilapidated bridges that are to be replaced with new structures within 15 years. A further 125 are to undergo extensive repairs.
