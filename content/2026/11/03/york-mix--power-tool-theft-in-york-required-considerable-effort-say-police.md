Police say a burglary in York, where power tools were stolen, required considerable effort and someone must have seen something

It happened on Bootham between 5pm on Saturday (31 Oct) and 10.30am on Sunday (1 Nov). Entry was forced into a building on the street

The items would have been difficult to remove without attracting attention.

They want to hear from anyone who saw suspicious activity in the area, particularly people carrying items and loading them into a vehicle.

If you have any information that could assist the investigation, including any CCTV, please contact online in the first instance:

• Email the OIC on [email protected]

• Online: Visit our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’.

• Live chat: Available via our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm

If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

Please quote reference 12260215028 when providing information.
