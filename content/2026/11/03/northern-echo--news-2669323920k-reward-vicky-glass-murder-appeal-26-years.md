A £20k reward has been offered in the search for Vicky Glass' murderer - 26 years to the day since her body was found in North Yorkshire.

Victoria “Vicky” Glass was last seen around 4.30am on Union Street in Middlesbrough on September 24, 2000.

Her body was found near Danby, North Yorkshire on November 3, 2000, days after her 21st birthday. Despite years of investigations and witness interviews, no one has been charged or convicted over her death.

Crimestoppers is now urging anyone who may have information to come forward, offering a reward of up to £20,000 for information that leads to the arrest and conviction of those responsible.

A missing-person investigation became a murder inquiry after her body was discovered on the outskirts of Danby.

Crimestoppers said the passage of time may encourage people who were unable to speak out to come forward.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21494236.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Vicky Glass disappeared on September 24, 2000, from Union Street in Middlesbrough. 26 years later, her family continue to search for answers. *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Fleur Brown from Crimestoppers, said: “Vicky’s family have lived without answers for decades. If fear, a relationship or loyalty to someone stopped you speaking at the time, your circumstances may now be different.

“You do not need to give us your name.

"If you have information, even if you are unsure how it fits into the investigation, please tell Crimestoppers what you know. A detail you may think is small could help.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/19995369.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Vicky Glass. *(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

The charity wants to hear from anyone with information about Vicky’s movements around September 24, 2000, or how her body came to be found.

Anyone with information can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or through its website, crimestoppers-uk.org.

To qualify for the reward, information must be given directly to Crimestoppers, not the police.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/19995370.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Vicky Glass. *(Image: FAMILY)*

Anyone calling the charity must request a reward code during the call, while people using the online form must select the ‘Keep in contact’ option and request a reward code in their first message.

Crimestoppers says it guarantees complete anonymity.

Detectives are seeking a lorry driver who gave Vicky money shortly before her disappearance; he was white, aged 34 to 38 at the time, broad-built and had a distinctive mole on his face.

Vicky left personal effects with the driver and agreed to meet him later that day, but detectives have never identified him publicly.

Four people have been arrested in connection with Vicky's murder since 2000, but all were released with no further action.
