Comedian Maisie Adam has paid a visit to her former school in Harrogate.

The former St Aidan's CE High School Head Girl returned to meet staff and students following a performance at Harrogate Theatre in support of Frank’s Fund.

During her visit, Maisie was shown some of the school’s latest developments, including its new library, Sixth Form Lounge and Study Hub.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ae9907f02c67c80ed3.png)

She also surprised the cast of the school’s production of Frozen by joining their lunchtime rehearsal.

A spokesperson for St Aidan’s said:

> “We were delighted to welcome former Head Girl, or Senate Lead as we now call the role, Maisie Adam back to school today.”

They added:

> “Staff and students were thrilled to see Maisie, and it was fantastic to share with her some of the latest developments at school, including our new Library and Sixth Form Lounge and Study Hub.
>
> “She also delighted the cast of Frozen with a visit to their lunchtime rehearsal!
>
> “A huge thank you to Maisie for taking the time to visit us!”

The school described Frank’s Fund as a charity that “holds a special place in the hearts of our school community.”
