## York school in special measures joins academy trust

Carr Infants Head of School Matt Oxley with pupils *(Image: Supplied)*

A YORK school which was placed in special measures earlier this year is joining a growing academy trust.

Carr Infant School in Acomb, York has joined the Excel Learning Trust, which, alongside neighbouring Carr Junior School, brings the total number of schools in the trust to eight.

The school, which educates around 200 children aged three to seven, began working with the trust in January 2026 - following a request for support from its governors during a period of significant change.

Mark Hassack, chief executive officer of Excel Learning Trust, said: "From the moment we began working with Carr Infant School at the start of the year, it was clear that this is a school with real potential and a team determined to realise it.

"That progress owes a great deal to the exceptional leadership of Charlotte Crabtree, chair of governors, Matt Oxley, headteacher, and James McGann, our executive director of education, who have worked tirelessly together for the benefit of every child.

"Just as importantly, the staff at Carr Infants are fully on board with joining the trust, and their commitment has been a joy to see.

"We are delighted, and genuinely excited, to welcome Carr Infant School into the Excel Learning Trust family."

As reported by The Press last month , Ofsted said Carr Infant School remains in special measures following it being rated “inadequate” earlier this year.

However, the inspectors 'renewed energy and drive' and said pupil behaviour has “improved significantly” due to work being done by bosses at the school.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20670706.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Matthew Oxley interim head at Carr Infant School *(Image: Supplied)*

A subsequent Ofsted monitoring visit described progress as "rapid and necessary," noting improvements in behaviour, teaching quality—particularly in reading, writing and mathematics—and support for pupils with special educational needs and disabilities.

At the time, Matt Oxley, head of school at Carr Infant School, welcomed Ofsted's findings but said he recognised there is “more work to do” to improve further.

“Our staff and pupils have worked tirelessly to establish high expectations and embed significant improvements across the school,” the head teacher said - adding that the school was “encouraged by Ofsted’s recognition of our progress in core subjects and behaviour”.

On joining the trust, he added: "Joining Excel Learning Trust is a really positive step for Carr Infant School.

"We have already felt the benefit of working alongside colleagues across the trust, and our staff have been fantastic throughout.

"Being part of a family of schools that shares our ambition for every child is something we are all genuinely proud of, and we cannot wait to see what we achieve together."

Excel, which was previously known as the South Bank Multi Academy Trust, has seven York schools: Carr Junior, Knavesmire, Scarcroft and Woodthorpe Primary Schools, Millthorpe and York High Schools and Inspire Academy.
