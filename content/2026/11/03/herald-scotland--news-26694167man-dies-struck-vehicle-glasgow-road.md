A man has died after being struck by a vehicle in Glasgow.

Around 2.15pm on Monday, November 2, police received a report of collision involving a male pedestrian and black Chevrolet Captiva on Shettleston Road.

Officers attended however the 75-year-old man was pronounced dead at the scene.

Police Scotland said his next of kin have been made aware.

The road was closed to allow for crash investigations to take place and reopened around 9pm.

**READ MORE:**

Enquiries into the full circumstances of the crash remain ongoing, police said.

Sergeant Martin Cunningham said: “My thoughts are with the family and friends of the man who died at this very difficult time.

"Our enquiries are ongoing and I’d like to appeal to anyone who saw what happened to contact us.

“I am also keen to hear from drivers who were in the area at the time and have dashcam footage.

"Anyone with information should contact Police Scotland on 101 quoting reference 1646 of Monday, 2 November, 2026.
