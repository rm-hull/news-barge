ILKLEY Food and Drink Festival is set to return to East Holmes Field on June 12th and 13th, 2027, with organisers outlining plans for their eighth annual celebration beside the River Wharfe.

Building on seven years of celebrations, the 2027 edition will introduce refreshed entertainment line-ups, enhanced production and a series of new family activities scattered throughout the festival grounds.

Michael Johnston, Festival Director, said: “We wouldn’t be able to return year after year out without the wonderful community of festival-goers, foodies, independent traders, local makers, performers and entertainers that make this event so special. Watching the festival develop over the last seven years has been a real privilege, and we're incredibly grateful to everyone who's been part of that journey. We have no plans of stopping anytime soon.”

At the heart of the festival is a commitment to showcasing the vibrant community of local traders, artisans and entrepreneurs that make up Yorkshire’s thriving independent scene, alongside a celebration of international influence, global flavours and performing arts.

Across the event’s cornerstone attractions, including the Street Food Arena, Artisan Markets and Streets of Independent Bars, organisers plan to bring together more than 100 different local businesses, showcasing a handpicked selection of the region’s culinary and creative talents.

Izaak Shuttleworth, Assistant Festival Manager said: "It's important to us that the festival feels exciting and fresh for everyone who visits, whether they've been coming for years or are joining us for the first time.

"Attendee feedback plays a huge part in how we develop the event. That’s helping shape our 2027 plans, from improving production to introducing new activities and partnerships. We’re excited to bring some fresh ideas to Ilkley next summer.”

Four live stages, all included with festival admission, will offer a diverse programme of performances, demonstrations and activities throughout the weekend.

The Sizzling Showdown Arena will return with four interactive shows each day, premised entirely on audience participation. Meanwhile, the Live Entertainers Stage, hosted by Matthew J Magic, will welcome a fresh line-up of magicians, circus performers and family entertainers.

The Cookery Theatre will be led by a new host and compere, former chef consultant Stephanie Moon, who will bring together an eclectic mix of Yorkshire's leading culinary talents for a programme of live demonstrations. Lastly, the Main Stage will showcase a fresh line-up of local bands and up-and-coming performers throughout the festival.

Various cornerstone attractions will feature, including a mobile farm, festival scavenger hunt, foodie colouring books and a traditional fun fair. Organisers are also developing a series of new entertainment experiences and family activities through partnerships with local businesses and performers, with further announcements expected over the coming months.

Bradford Mind will return as the festival’s primary charity partner. Together with its sister events, the Ilkley Food and Drink Festival has helped raise a collective £210,000 for mental health initiatives across the UK, with organisers hoping to build on that milestone heading into 2027.

For more information, Early Bird Tickets, and updates visit: www.ilkleyfoodfestival.com
