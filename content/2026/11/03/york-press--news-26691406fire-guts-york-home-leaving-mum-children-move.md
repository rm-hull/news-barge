A fire has gutted a York home leaving a pregnant mother and her young children having to rebuild their lives.

Melissa Wilding said an electrical fault in the shower of her Bellfarm Avenue home resulted in the blaze which left the three-bedroom house “unhabitable” due to smoke damage.

The 30-year-old, who lived at the council-owned home with her 11-year-old daughter and six-year-old son, said her and her children’s beds and mattresses must now be replaced after being “covered in black smoke”.

She said she has been offered another property in Byland Avenue, the next street over, but the house is unfurnished.

A fundraiser has been launched to raise £1,000 for new items such as beds for the new property.

Melissa said she was at home with her son on the afternoon of Wednesday, October 28, when the fire broke out.

“I was preparing tea in the kitchen and my carbon monoxide alarm was triggered and started going off,” she told The Press. “When I went to investigate at the bottom of the stairs, I could smell gas.

“I said to my family, ‘Everyone get out.’

“When I was standing in the back garden ... I saw smoke coming out the bathroom window.

“I immediately called the fire service and got away from the house.”

## 'I didn’t know if there would be an explosion'

Melissa said she told her neighbours about the fire so they could also evacuate their homes.

“I could smell gas,” she said. “I didn’t know if there would be an explosion.

“A lot of the neighbours came out to help and reassure me.”

Two fire crews attended the scene shortly before 2.30pm and extinguished the fire.

Melissa said the bathroom of the house, which she lived at for seven years, was left “completely destroyed” and the rest of the home was “full of smoke damage”.

The fire also caused one of the water pipes to burst, she said, resulting in the kitchen flooding.

“The upstairs is completely full of smoke damage,” she said. “My kids’ bedrooms and clothes are covered in black smoke.

“… I’m expecting a baby. I had just bought a pram which is now covered in smoke.”

Melissa said the new items she bought for her baby, due in February, also must be replaced due to the smoke damage.

After the blaze, Melissa said she was offered temporary accommodation at the Travelodge at Bilbrough Top but turned it down as the hotel was too far for her children to travel to school. She said she is currently sofa surfing.

Melissa’s brother-in-law has set up a GoFundMe page to raise money for the family. To donate, visit Mark Widdowfield’s page titled: “House fire.”
