Fans of popular North Yorkshire artist Lucy Pittaway have another chance to visit her Newcastle gallery this Christmas after it reopened for the festive season.

The gallery on Blackett Street, Newcastle, reopened on Saturday, October 31, after closing earlier this year.

Announcing the return on Facebook, the business said: “Newcastle—we're Baaa-ck!

“By popular demand, we're reopening our Newcastle gallery for the festive season.

“We will be open from tomorrow through to January.

"A little bit of Lucy is back in the Toon.

“Pop in to see Lucy's latest collections, find something special for Christmas, or just come and say hello.

"We've missed you.”

It is open from 10am to 5pm Monday to Saturday and from 10am to 4pm on Sundays throughout the festive period.

The Blackett Street gallery shut its doors earlier this year, although a reason for the closure was not confirmed.

It followed a difficult period for the business, which also saw galleries in Bowness-on-Windermere and Harrogate close at the start of the year.

In a statement announcing those closures, Ms Pittaway said: “It's with a heavy heart that I share some difficult news from our Lucy Pittaway family.

“Shortly, we will be closing two galleries, Bowness-on-Windermere and Harrogate.

“This has been an incredibly tough decision, influenced by the rising operational costs facing so many UK businesses, including a significant rent increase at our Harrogate location.”

Her galleries in Richmond, Yarm, York and Keswick, plus the Brompton-on-Swale outlet, remain open.

Lucy Pittaway began her art career more than 13 years ago when her twins started school.

Her hobby in a small metal shed grew into a thriving business, as she sold commissioned pieces and prints before opening her first gallery.

Her distinctive paintings of sheep, countryside scenes and family life have won a loyal following across the North East, Yorkshire and beyond.
