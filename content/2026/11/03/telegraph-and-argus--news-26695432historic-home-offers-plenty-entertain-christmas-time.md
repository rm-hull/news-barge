IF YOU have not visited Castle Howard, making your first trip around Christmas time is a real treat.

The stately home, around 15 miles northeast of York, is majestic in itself,

with stunning architecture designed by Sir John Vanbrugh, with significant support from Nicholas Hawksmoor.

It has been in the Howard family for more than 300 years.

At this time of year the historic house and surrounding estate - including Castle Howard Arboretum - has many additional activities to offer visitors. Here are just some of them.

\*Wreath-making workshop

Participants can get into the festive spirit at a Christmas wreath-making workshop held in Castle Howard Garden Centre. Over two hours, they will be able to create their own wreath using natural materials, with guidance every step of the way and no experience required. All materials are provided along with complimentary tea and coffee during the session.

For age 16+, and only ten tickets available per session. It runs from Thursday November 19 to Friday December 11.

\*Artist and Makers Fair

A vibrant Artists & Makers Fair is being held at Castle Howard Arboretum. Spend the day discovering an inspiring showcase of local talent, with a curated selection of artists and artisans exhibiting and selling their work in a relaxed setting.

Visitors can browse these thoughtfully crafted pieces, as well as getting the chance to meet the makers, hear the stories behind their work. Whether you’re searching for a unique gift or simply going along to enjoy a day out, it offers a chance to support independent creatives from across the region.

Artists exhibiting at the fair include Jacqueline Warrington, Anna Cook, Louise Dwyer, Wilf Williams and Nick Kobyluch.

The fair is on Saturday November 28.

\*A Dickens of a Christmas at Castle Howard

Following the success of previous years’ Christmas events based around well-known themes, visitors can this year enjoy Charles Dickens’ classic novels brought to life in the house. The Victorian tales are being recreated in eye-catching Christmas style, leaping off the page with projections, soundscapes, and theatrical surprises.

As the cold nights draw in, visitors can encounter familiar faces from Dickens’ most treasured novels including Ebenezer Scrooge and a ghost or two. Rich in nostalgia, storytelling and Christmas spirit, it’s an immersive journey that celebrates festive magic. It’s a joy for all ages.

This Christmas experience runs from Thursday November 12 to Sunday January 3, 2027

\*Gardens and grounds

If you simply want to get some fresh air and exercise in a picturesque setting, Castle Howard is the perfect place.

Whatever the weather, the landscape is beautiful. At this time of year the woodland on the estate is filled with vibrant autumn colour.

Head out for a walk and you never know what you will come across in the gardens and grounds: there are lakes, temples, follies, woodland and statues. Designed to complement the House, they offer space to wander and explore. There are two lakes, at either side of the house.

There’s plenty of spots to grab a snack and a coffee, and an extra-special restaurant which runs during the Christmas event.

Castle Howard is open daily (excluding the Walled Garden), the gardens are dog-friendly and perfect for year-round visits, except for Christmas Day. The house is open to pre-booked ticket holders during the Christmas event,
