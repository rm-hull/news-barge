AN EXIT RAMP off the M62 remains closed this morning due to ongoing recovery work following a police vehicle fire.

Emergency services were called to M62 junction 28 at late last night (November 2) after a police vehicle caught fire.

West Yorkshire Police said both carriageways were closed while emergency services dealt with the incident.

No-one has been hurt in the incident.

Drivers were urged to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.

This morning (November 3), the M62 westbound exit ramp at junction 28, near the A650 Bradford Road in Tingley, remains closed for recovery work.

The closure began at 11.55pm on November 2 and is due to end at 10am on November 3.

A spokesperson for WYP said: "Emergency services were called to junction 28 of the M62 at about 11.15pm on Monday night after a police vehicle caught fire.

"A full closure of both carriageways of the motorway has been put in place at the junction while the incident is managed.

"No-one has been hurt in the incident, and drivers are urged to avoid the area and seek alternative routes."
