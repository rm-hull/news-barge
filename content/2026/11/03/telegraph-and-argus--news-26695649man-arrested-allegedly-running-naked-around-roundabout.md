A man has been arrested after reportedly running naked around a roundabout during a busy time.

The indecent exposure incident happened at around 6pm on Barkerend Road at the junction with the New Otley Road roundabout in Bradford.

Video shared with the Telegraph & Argus showed that mass traffic disruption was caused.

A man was arrested on suspicion of indecent exposure and assaulting a police officer on duty.

## What have police said?

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Police were called to reports of a man indecently exposing himself in Barkerend Road, Bradford at around 6.01pm yesterday (Monday, November 2)."

The spokesperson said: "A man in his 30s has been arrested on suspicion of indecent exposure and assaulting a police officer on duty."
