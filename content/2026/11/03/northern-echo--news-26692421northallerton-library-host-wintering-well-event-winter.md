A library is set to help residents beat the winter blues.

Northallerton Library will host a Wintering Well event on Saturday, November 14, from 10am to 1pm, offering visitors the chance to connect with community groups and organisations providing support during the colder months.

A spokesperson said: "The event will give visitors the chance to chat to groups and organisations from the town that can offer support and ideas to help people of all ages who struggle during the winter months."

Attendees can take part in a seasonal art activity, either by booking in advance through the library or dropping in on the day.

There will also be a Morris dancing demonstration.

The event is part of North Yorkshire libraries’ wider Wintering Well campaign, which encourages residents to visit the library for support during winter.

The library has created a cosy space to support people who are affected by SAD.
