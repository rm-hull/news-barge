Indie rock favourites Courteeners are heading to North Yorkshire coast next summer – as they celebrate two decades as one of Britain’s most beloved bands.

Known for turning arenas and festival fields into mass singalongs, the Manchester band will headline Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Saturday 12 June, having last played a sold-out show at the venue in 2021.

They will be joined on the night by special guests The Coral and Seb Lowe.

Tickets will go on sale at 10am this Friday via Ticketmaster.

Since releasing their debut album *St. Jude*in 2008, Courteeners have established themselves as one of the biggest bands in Britain. Across their career spanning almost two decades, the Manchester indie rockers have released seven studio albums and produced fan-favourite tracks including* Not Nineteen Forever*,* Are You In Love With A Notion?*,* Bide Your Time, Cavorting, No You Didn’t, No You Don’t*and* Modern Love*.

The band have sold more than one million albums in the UK, with each of their last five albums reaching the Top 5. In 2023, *St Jude* completed an extraordinary journey to the top of the charts, reaching Number One 15 years after its original release.

Courteeners gigs are massive celebrations where songs become a communal experience between band and fans, with their choruses unifying tens of thousands of people. Their return to the Open Air Theatre promises to be another unforgettable night of indie anthems on the Yorkshire coast.

This year marked a major milestone for the band who celebrated their 20th anniversary with the release of *God Bless The Band*, a greatest hits collection spanning all seven studio albums and featuring two brand-new tracks,* The Luckiest Man Alive*and* Plus One Forever*.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “We’re really excited to be welcoming Courteeners back to Scarborough next summer. They were a huge hit when they played to a sold-out crowd here in 2021, so it’s great to have them back and give fans another chance to see them live.

“Courteeners are one of the UK’s most beloved live bands, and we know this will be another very special show. With The Coral and Seb Lowe joining as special guests, we can’t wait to welcome fans from far and wide for what promises to be another fantastic night and a real highlight of our summer season.”
