North East businesses are being urged to tap into the region’s emerging creative talent before Christmas.

Sunderland Creatives Agency, a partnership between the University of Sunderland and creative marketing agency Creo Comms, is offering companies original festive work from student designers, illustrators, animators and other creatives.

Laura Smith from Creo Comms, who helps oversee the agency’s projects, said: "Christmas is a great opportunity for businesses to have a bit of fun with their branding and create something that really catches people’s attention.

"It is also a fantastic opportunity for businesses with a high street presence to really get involved in the celebrations and help make their communities more vibrant.

"By working with our students, businesses can inject fresh ideas into their marketing and displays, while giving young creatives valuable experience of working with real clients. It’s a win-win and something we’d love to see more businesses take advantage of."

Students can create bespoke Christmas cards, window vinyls, social media graphics and videos.

The scheme helps businesses stand out at Christmas while giving students paid experience to launch creative careers.

Sunderland Creatives Agency connects businesses with University of Sunderland students, helping them build their portfolios while giving companies fresh ideas and creative expertise.

Lee Hall, Head of the School of Media & Creative Industries at the University of Sunderland, said: "Opportunities like this are a fantastic way for our students to take the skills they develop at university and apply them to real-world projects.

"Working directly with businesses gives them a valuable opportunity to build their experience, confidence, and portfolio of work, while showing businesses the wealth of creative talent we have here in the North East."

Sunderland Creatives Agency, set up by the University of Sunderland and Creo Comms, offers brand development, design, photography, videography, illustration, social media, public relations and podcast content.

Creo Comms provides professional support for every student project.
