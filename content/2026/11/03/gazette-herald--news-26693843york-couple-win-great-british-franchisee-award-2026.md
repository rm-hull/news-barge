A York couple has won an award for their work helping business leaders make better decisions.

Natalie and Elliot Rich, who run The Alternative Board (TAB) York, have received the Great British Franchisee Award 2026 in recognition of their business growth, client focus, and impact on York’s business community.

Mr Rich said: "Running a business can be rewarding, but it can also be difficult to find the time and space to think things through properly.

"Leaders are often the people everyone else turns to, so having somewhere they can talk openly about challenges, explore ideas and hear a different perspective can make a real difference.

"We want TAB York to be a place where business leaders feel comfortable being honest about what is going well and what isn't, where they can be challenged constructively and leave with greater clarity about what they need to do next."

The couple took over TAB York in 2017 and have since supported more than 90 business owners through peer advisory boards, one-to-one coaching, and strategic business planning.

Their approach encourages members to step back from day-to-day operations and focus on long-term strategy, while also addressing current issues such as AI, rising costs, and wages and taxation in a confidential, supportive environment.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21663764.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TAB Masters of the EU Mags Fuller and Ed Reid, with (centre) Natalie and Elliot Rich, owners of The Alternative Board (TAB) York *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Mrs Rich said: "It is easy to get caught up in the day-to-day and spend all your time reacting to what needs doing.

"Taking a step back to look at the bigger picture, question assumptions and think about where you want the business to be in the next few years is just as important.

"Bringing together people who run different types of businesses is particularly valuable.

"They may face similar challenges but approach them in very different ways, and that shared experience can open up options you might not have considered on your own."

Judges praised the couple’s dedication to client service, their ongoing development of the business, and the collaborative community they have cultivated in York.

The award also recognises their willingness to share knowledge and support the local business community.

Their win comes as they approach 10 years since acquiring TAB York in March 2027.

Looking ahead, they aim to expand the network further and deepen their impact across the city.

Mr Rich said: "York has a diverse and ambitious business community, and we want to keep playing our part in helping it thrive.

"For us, it is about more than running a successful business ourselves.

"It is about helping the people behind other local businesses make better decisions, develop as leaders and build businesses they can be proud of."

For more information, visit thealternativeboard.co.uk/york.
