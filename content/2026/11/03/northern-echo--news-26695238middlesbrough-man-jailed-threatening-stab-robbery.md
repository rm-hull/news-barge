A balaclava-clad thug threatened to stab someone up as he demanded money in a shop raid.

Lee Marshall entered the store on Norfolk Place in Berwick Hils on August 17, threatening to stab someone as told staff to hand over cash.

Teesside Crown Court heard Marshall fled the scene on a black pedal bike.

CCTV footage helped officers track Marshall to a nearby property, where he was found wearing clothing matching the suspect’s description.

Discarded cash was recovered both en route and inside the address, matching the amount stolen from the shop.

Marshall was arrested and later charged with robbery.

The 43-year-old of Ingram Road, Middlesbrough, was sentenced to three years and four months at Teesside Crown Court following the robbery at a shop on Norfolk Place in Berwick Hills on August 17, 2026.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21665597.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lee Marshall *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Detective Constable Itsharm Ramzan of Middlesbrough CID said: "This sentence sends a clear message to anyone considering committing similar offences: robbery and violent crime will not be tolerated in our communities.

"Those who choose to prey on innocent members of the public, or hardworking staff, should expect to be identified, arrested and put before the courts.

"This was a deeply distressing incident which had a significant impact on those involved.

"Incidents of this nature are wholly unacceptable and have no place in our communities.

"The swift actions of the attending officers, witnesses, together with the support provided to the investigation, were instrumental in bringing the offender to justice.

"Let this case serve as a warning to those involved in criminality.
