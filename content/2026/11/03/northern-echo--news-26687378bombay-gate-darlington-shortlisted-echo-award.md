An “amazing” Darlington Indian restaurant headed up by a Masterchef champion has been shortlisted for The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Bombay Gate has been a staple of Skinnergate since owner Syed Ahmed, who previously ran Bombay Café on Duke Street, opened the doors in 2019.

The 46-year-old has already picked up a string of awards for himself and the business, including the UK champion at the International Bangladesh Masterchef competition.

But he’s now set to battle it out on a local scale after being shortlisted for the Echo’s Best Curry House 2026 for the third year in a row.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661325.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Bombay Gate, Darlington. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

“Being nominated is a great thing,” Syed, who is from Darlington, said.

“It is our third timer in the top ten. Customers appreciate what we are doing, it means we haven't lost our level of service and food.

“We get nominated for a lot of awards but if you get nominated for a local award, it means more. We are just doing our best to compete with the other restaurants around the area.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661324.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Syed Ahmed of Bombay Gate, Darlington. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Syed, who runs the restaurant alongside his younger brother Sid, 39, who runs the front of house, said it was great to have such a busy curry scene in Darlington.

“There are a lot of good restaurants here and they are not all the same,” he said.

“It is very busy, we have more than 140 seats now with our intimate dining space upstairs, which we created around a year and a half ago. On the weekend, it is full.”

When it comes to the most popular dish, Syed said the railway lamb curry took the top spot.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661326.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Bombay Gate, Darlington. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

He said: “The railway lamb curry is on par with the tikka masala. That dish was created by myself when I was in Eaglescliffe at the Cinnamon Room.

“I then took it to Bombay Café on Duke Street and then I brought it over to Bombay Gate. A lot of people copy it now with their own version - it is so good that everybody wants to sell it!”

Bombay Gate currently boasts a 4.2 star rating on Google, with customers praising its “classic but brilliant Indian food” and “first class” service.

One diner said: “A ten out of ten restaurant. Everything from the decor to the service and food was nothing short of perfect.”

Bombay Gate is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Arvelli Indian Restaurant, Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.
