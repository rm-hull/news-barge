Jurors in a murder trial heard messages were shared on Snapchat the day after a man was killed at a County Durham petrol station.

Daniel Ambler suffered fatal injuries when Ashton Anderson drove a Nissan X-Trail into him on January 15, Teesside Crown Court heard.

He was pronounced dead after the crash at the Esso Asda Express service station in Seaham.

The jury in the trial of murder-accused Barry Anderson, his sons Ashton and Logan, Aja Henry and Tyler Leadbetter, was shown CCTV footage which prosecutors say shows the five hunting down and attacking Mr Ambler and his friend Reece Wilkin.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20435374.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Daniel Ambler *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

The attack was the culmination of a feud that began when Barry Anderson was stabbed last August.

The trial heard that Mr Ambler’s brother David and his friend Jed Wilson arranged to fight Barry Anderson, who was stabbed in the side during the struggle.

Durham Police intelligence analyst Laura McLaughlin said investigators collected messages and calls between the families.

She said the messages were recovered from the phone of Olivia Mann, Ashton Anderson’s girlfriend.

Prosecutor Matthew Donkin KC took the witness through messages shared over 50 minutes the day after Mr Ambler died.

The messages were shared in a Snapchat group containing Logan Anderson, Tyler Leadbetter, Ashton Anderson and Ajay Henry.

Some people in the group were not in the Nissan X-Trail when the crash happened.

Leadbetter said his fingerprints would be found in the car but denied being caught on CCTV.

Someone else replied that police would be looking at everyone in Deneside, Seaham.

Logan Anderson sent an audio message, but detectives could not download it from the phone.

Ms McLaughlin said other deleted messages were never recovered.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Esso New Seaham Service Station, in Stockton Road, Seaham, the scene of Daniel Ambler's death *(Image: Sarah Caldecott (The Northern Echo))*

Prosecutors say the attack was coordinated and that four defendants helped or encouraged Ashton Anderson to use violence against Mr Ambler.

Witness Alexis Prest told jurors that nobody in the Nissan X-Trail encouraged Ashton Anderson to drive on to the forecourt or celebrated after Mr Ambler was struck.

Barry Anderson, 52, Ashton Anderson, 20, and Barry Logan Anderson, 19, face murder and violent-disorder charges. Their addresses are Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland; Ryton Crescent, Seaham; and Nursery View, Shotton Bank, respectively.

Ajay Henry, of Melrose Crescent, Seaham, and Tyler Leadbetter, of Elgin Avenue, Seaham, were both 17 when charged with murdering Daniel Ambler.

Ashton Anderson has pleaded guilty to violent disorder. The other defendants deny the remaining charges.

The trial at Teesside Crown Court is expected to last up to five weeks.

Earlier in the trial, CCTV showed Ashton Anderson driving into Mr Ambler before the occupants left the Nissan and ran from the service station.

A witness also told the jury he saw a person in dark clothing apparently striking Mr Ambler with an object resembling a crowbar, although he could not confirm every blow made contact.

The trial continues.
