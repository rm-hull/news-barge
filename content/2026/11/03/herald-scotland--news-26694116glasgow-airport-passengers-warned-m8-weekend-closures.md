Passengers travelling to and from Glasgow Airport are being warned to allow extra time for their journeys due to extended weekend closures on the M8 motorway.

Sections of the road are scheduled to close between Saturday, November 7, and Sunday, November 8, as well as between Saturday, November 14, and Sunday, November 15.

Significant delays are expected around the airport, meaning travellers could be caught in traffic as departure times approach.

The first closure will begin at 6pm on Saturday, November 7, and remain in place until 1pm on Sunday, November 8.

During this period, the M8 eastbound will be closed between Junction 24 at Helen Street and Junction 22 at Plantation. The eastbound on-slip road at Helen Street will also be shut.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20567101.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Motorists are warned of delays on the M8 (stock pic) *(Image: Robert Perry)*

On the westbound carriageway, both the main and secondary routes of the M8 will be closed between Junctions 22 and 24. The westbound secondary carriageway on-slip at Junction 21, Carnoustie Street, will also be unavailable to traffic.

The M77 will remain open in both directions throughout the first weekend of works.

A second and more extensive closure is scheduled from 6pm on Saturday, November 14, until 1pm on Sunday, November 15.

During this weekend, all 16 lanes of the M8 will be closed in the work area. The eastbound carriageway will again be shut between Junctions 24 and 22, including the Helen Street eastbound on-slip.

The M77 northbound will also be affected, with a closure in place from Junction 1 at Dumbreck Road, including the Dumbreck on-slip road.

Meanwhile, the M8 westbound will be closed between Junction 20 at the Kingston Bridge and Junction 24 at Helen Street.

The work will involve essential refurbishment of the Cornwall Street and Kirkwood Street footbridges, which span the M8 between Junction 21 (Seaward Street) and Junction 23 (Dumbreck).

Bus journeys to and from the airport will also be affected, with contingency plans put in place.

A Glasgow Airport spokesperson said: "Significant delays are expected across the surrounding road network and diversion routes.

"As such, passengers travelling to and from the airport during this time are advised to plan ahead and allow extra travel time."

The works represent a significant investment by Transport Scotland in improving key active travel assets in Glasgow and will support the long-term safety, reliability and usability of the footbridges, the Scottish Government agency said.

As such, a contingency weekend is provisionally scheduled from 6pm on Saturday, November 21, until 1pm on Sunday, November 22.

The new Citylink 903 service to Glasgow Airport will be unaffected by the M8 closures but will be impacted by the recent Renfrew Bridge closure.

Several other bus routes serving the airport will be diverted during this time.

A Citylink spokesperson said: "The M8 works will not cause our 903 service to divert. However, the recently announced closure of the Renfrew Bridge will require our 903 service between Edinburgh, Blantyre, Glasgow and Glasgow Airport to divert, meaning it will be unable to serve stops at Victoria Park, Scotstoun and Yoker during the bridge closure.

"Our routes between Glasgow and Campbeltown, Oban and Skye, which also serve the airport, will follow a diversion during the M8 works, but all stops will continue to be served as normal. We expect affected services to take longer than usual and would therefore urge customers to allow extra time when planning their journeys during this period."

A spokesperson for First Bus said: "These closures will affect our Service 500 to Glasgow Airport, Service X8 to Silverburn Retail Park, and journeys on both routes to Buchanan Bus Station.

"Our team has worked carefully to minimise disruption on the diversions we have put in place to ensure we continue to serve most stops, and drivers will be advising customers of three alternative stops to use as the bus begins the diverted route.

"It is likely there will be delays and we strongly advise customers to plan in advance for journeys on these dates. Full details of the diversions can be found on our website, and we appreciate the patience and understanding of customers as our drivers manage the changes."
