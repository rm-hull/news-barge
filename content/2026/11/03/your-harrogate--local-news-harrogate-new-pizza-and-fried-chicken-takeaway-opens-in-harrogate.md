A new pizza and fried chicken takeaway has opened in Harrogate.

Chicos Fried Chicken & Pizza, at 4 Commercial Street, is now welcoming customers seven days a week from midday until late.

The takeaway serves pizzas, fried chicken, burgers and more, with the team thanking the Harrogate community for its support following the grand opening.

A spokesperson for Chicos said:

> “We just want to take a moment to say a massive thank you to everyone who came down, supported us and made our grand opening such a fantastic day!
>
> “The turnout was amazing, and we’re so grateful for all the love and support from our customers, families, friends and the Harrogate community.”

They added:

> “We loved seeing so many of you and hope you all enjoyed the food!”

*Customers can order online or by calling 01423 564584.*
