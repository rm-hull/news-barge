Asda's 2026 Christmas sandwich range has arrived in stores across the North East this week, for those looking for a festive lunch.

The upgraded range, including premium mains and festive favourites, launched at more than 60 North East branches, including 9 Asda Express stores, and online yesterday (November 2).

More than one million festive sandwiches were sold last Christmas, and Asda is aiming for another bumper season with its biggest festive lunch range yet.

Four new mains and three new snacks are being introduced to the range this festive season alongside returning sandwiches inspired by traditional festive feasts, Boxing Day leftovers, and "indulgent" seasonal party food.

Asda's new premium launches include Festive Italian Antipasti Wraps and Turkey Deli Sandwiches.

Premium festive sandwiches are available individually or in Asda's £5.50 Exceptional Meal Deal, which includes a premium main, snack and drink.

Standard items are similarly available individually, or in Asda's regular £3.74 meal deal.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21663344.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Asda's Boxing Day Curry Wrap *(Image: Asda)*

A new addition to the standard range is the Boxing Day Curry Wrap, which combines butter chicken, tandoori onions, coriander and mint yoghurt mayonnaise in a tortilla.

Vegan customers have something to look forward to this Christmas too, as the new All The Trimmings Vegan Wrap has launched, containing roasted vegetables, vegan stuffing, sage and onion mayonnaise, crispy onions and cranberry chutney.

Returning favourites include the Boxing Day Sandwich, Christmas Club Sandwich and Christmas Lunch Wrap, all with updated recipes.
