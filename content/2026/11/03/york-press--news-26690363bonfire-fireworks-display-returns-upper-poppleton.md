Poppleton Community Centre is preparing for its 18th annual Bonfire & Firework Spectacular.

The event, held on Guy Fawkes Night, November 5, is a major fundraiser for the community centre.

The event takes place in Main Street, Upper Poppleton, with tickets available at poppletoncentre.org.uk.

Organisers have promised a packed evening with a large bonfire, an expanded fireworks display, food and drink stalls, and activities for families.

The gates open at 5pm, with the bonfire being lit at 6.15pm, followed by the fireworks display at 7.15pm.

The event will conclude at 9pm.

Tickets are priced at £12 for adults and £6 for children aged five to 16, while under-fives can attend for free.

All proceeds from the event will go towards the maintenance and development of the community centre.

Public transport options include the number 10 bus from York city centre and a train from York to Poppleton Station.

For those driving, Poppleton Bar Park & Ride on the A59 will run free shuttle buses from 5pm, with the last bus to the event leaving at 7pm.

The other free car park is at Poppleton Community Football Pavilion in Millfield Lane.

For more information, visit poppletoncentre.org.uk.
