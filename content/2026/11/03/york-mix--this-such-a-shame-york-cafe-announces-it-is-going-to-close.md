A York café has announced it will close in the New Year.

The news was broken on social media today.

Costa Coffee in Haxby will shut on 14 January.

“Speaking on behalf of myself and all my lovely team here it is such a shame that we are coming to a close,” the branch manager said.

They said the staff “will be here smiling and carrying on until our last day” despite jobs being lost.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/costa-coffee-haxby-york-gsv.webp)

“My heart goes out to the wonderful team which I’ve managed for the past year.

“We will miss our wonderful regular customers and friends in the community.”

The decision caused sadness and dismay among residents. One wrote: “This is such a shame.

“A lovely meeting place, but unfortunately the cost of living makes these kind of treats unnecessary and unaffordable in a cost of living crisis.

“The shop’s rent/rates etc will also be high. I’m so sorry for the lovely staff losing their jobs.”

Another said: “That’s very sad. You’ve been brilliant and I’ve enjoyed my visits to Costa.”

And a third wondered if the location was a problem: “What is happening to our village / town? Two empty shops now in that area.

“Quinn’s in the arcade. Post office reduced hours. Morrisons in the village closed

It not like it’s a small rural village. Acomb is thriving – new shops and coffee shops opening up. Is it the cost of the rent?”
