A York start-up has been named a finalist for two prestigious business awards, one national and one regional – after winning another.

The Innate Coach, a people and workplace culture development company, has been shortlisted in the Best New Business category at the SME National Business Awards.

This is in recognition of their approach to leadership and cultural change development plus the growing impact of its Conversations4Change initiative as part of the employer support programme run by York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Since its launch, the Innate Coach has secured two substantial rounds of funding, enabling it to support around 100 leaders and 60 employers across the region.

Co-founded by Andrea Morrison and Dr Helen May in July 2025, The Innate Coach has also been shortlisted for Best Business Startup at The Yorkshire Post Business Awards.

This follows the business winning the One to Watch Award at the University of York’s Enterprise Awards in June.

Andrea, a transformational coach, psychologist and former employment law barrister, said: “We’re incredibly proud to be finalists for these prestigious awards.

“Helen and I created The Innate Coach because we share a belief in people’s innate capacity for insight, resilience and change. We wanted to bring that understanding into the workplace, helping leaders and organisations recognise the difference they can make through improving the quality of their everyday conversations.

“We believe that when people understand more about themselves and others, it changes how they lead, how they relate to one another and, ultimately, the cultures they create.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/L-R_-Dr-Helen-May-and-Andrea-Morrison-co-founders-of-The-Innate-Coach-York.webp)

During its first year, The Innate Coach secured two substantial rounds of funding to provide Conversations4Change through York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority, using national funding from the Department for Work and Pensions’ Get Britain Working Economic Inactivity Trailblazer.

This funding means they can offer a range of free coach and mentoring online training programmes for employers in York and North Yorkshire, three of which are accredited by the Association for Coaching. Helen and Andrea also offer free online microlearning programmes and host two online community events; a relaxed monthly Lunch and Learn, open to any leaders in the region keen to explore inclusive leadership practice, and an informal Monthly Practice Café for leaders to practice coaching and mentoring skills.

It currently has fully-funded places still available on its Coaching for Leaders accredited programme and its Compassionate Leadership micro-learning programme, which is due to launch in early January 2027.

The Innate Coach has already seen its work make a significant impact. Evaluation during the first year, undertaken in partnership with York St John University, showed significant changes in leadership practice, including greater levels of listening, presence and facilitation, impacting team relationships, retention and performance.

Helen and Andrea are both fierce advocates for women in business, and hope that their success will pave the way for more female founders to obtain funding to support the region’s businesses.

The SME National Business Awards winners will be announced on Friday 6 November at a ceremony at the Leonardo Royal Hotel, London Tower Bridge.
