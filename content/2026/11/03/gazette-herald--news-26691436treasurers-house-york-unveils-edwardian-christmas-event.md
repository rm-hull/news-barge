A York townhouse is stepping back in time to host a glamorous Edwardian Christmas.

Treasurer’s House will be transformed into a winter wonderland from November 14 to December 20, inviting visitors to experience the magic of a 1906 debutante ball through its annual Inspired Christmas programme.

This year’s theme centres on the coming-of-age celebration of Nancy Lycett Green, bringing to life the anticipation and excitement of an Edwardian evening through elegant decorations and historical storytelling.

Edward Walker, visitor experience officer, said: "Treasurer’s House has always been a place of stories.

"Frank Green carefully shaped every room to surprise and delight his guests, and this Christmas we're continuing that tradition by inviting visitors to step into Nancy Lycett Green's debutante ball.

"It's a chance to experience the excitement, glamour, and anticipation of a special moment in the house's history, while also celebrating the incredible creativity and dedication of the volunteers who have brought it to life."

Guests will be transported into the preparations for an Edwardian ball, complete with handwritten invitations, dance cards, and rooms dressed for an unforgettable night.

Many of the displays have been carefully handmade by the property's volunteers, with additional contributions from local groups and organisations.

The story is especially fitting for Treasurer's House, once owned by collector and philanthropist Frank Green, who meticulously curated his home to surprise and entertain.

Throughout the house, each room will capture a different stage of the evening, from the early preparations to the arrival of guests.

The immersive experience combines seasonal creativity with historical detail, offering a glimpse into a special moment from the house’s past.

Special events will also take place during the festive season.

Conservation in Action runs every Tuesday from 11am to 1pm, giving visitors the chance to see conservation work up close and speak with the team about how the collection is cared for.

For those who prefer a gentler experience, A Lighter and Quieter Christmas is available every Wednesday from 2pm to 4pm.

During these hours, the house will be decorated as usual with the music turned off, shutters open and lights on, creating a more relaxed environment while retaining all the festive charm.

The on-site café will serve seasonal treats and winter drinks throughout the Christmas period, offering a cosy spot to relax after touring the house.

The National Trust shop nearby will stock Christmas decorations, cards, gifts, and artisan food and drink, as well as a range of National Trust-inspired products.

An Inspired Christmas at Treasurer’s House runs from November 14 to December 20, Saturday to Wednesday, 11am to 4pm (last entry 3.30pm).

No booking is required, and normal admission fees apply.

For more details, visit the National Trust website.
