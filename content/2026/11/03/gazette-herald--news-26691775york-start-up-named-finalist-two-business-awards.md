A York-based start-up is in the running for two business awards.

The Innate Coach, co-founded by Andrea Morrison and Dr Helen May in July 2025, is a workplace culture development company.

It has been named a finalist in the Best New Business category at the SME National Business Awards, and is also shortlisted for Best Business Startup at The Yorkshire Post Business Awards.

Ms Morrison, a transformational coach, psychologist, and former employment law barrister, said: "We’re incredibly proud to be finalists for these prestigious awards.

"Helen and I created The Innate Coach because we share a belief in people's innate capacity for insight, resilience and change.

"We wanted to bring that understanding into the workplace, helping leaders and organisations recognise the difference they can make through improving the quality of their everyday conversations.

"We believe that when people understand more about themselves and others, it changes how they lead, how they relate to one another and, ultimately, the cultures they create."

The shortlisting follows a recent win at the University of York’s Enterprise Awards, where the company took home the One to Watch Award in June.

Dr May, a leadership coach, national change consultant, researcher and educator, said: "For us, being shortlisted for these awards means so much and makes the hard work throughout the last year worth it.

"We believe that it’s time for a fresh approach to leadership and cultural change where people are the priority.

"We recognise the importance of human connection and the difference that compassionate person-centred everyday conversations can make.

"Workplace cultures are a reflection of their people.

"We want to support employers to create working environments where everyone feels heard, valued and included, and where people and businesses can thrive."

The Innate Coach has secured two rounds of funding since its launch, supporting around 100 leaders and 60 employers across the region.

A key part of its offering is Conversations4Change, a range of fully-funded coaching and mentoring programmes delivered through the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.

Evaluation during the first year, conducted with York St John University, showed improvements in leadership practices including listening, presence, and facilitation, which translated into better team relationships, retention, and performance.

Ms Morrison and Dr May are advocates for women in business and hope their success will inspire other female founders to seek funding and support.

The SME National Business Awards will announce winners on November 6 at the Leonardo Royal Hotel, London Tower Bridge, and The Yorkshire Post Business Awards will take place on November 11 at New Dock Hall in Leeds.

More information about The Innate Coach and its programmes can be found at theinnatecoach.mykajabi.com.
