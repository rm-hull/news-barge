## Brussels hosts the Euronews Enlargement Summit as Montenegro, Albania and Ukraine push for EU membership. We speak to the EU ambassadors of Montenegro, Petar Marković, and Ukraine, Taras Kachka. Plus: Republican strategist Matt Klink on how today's US midterms test Donald Trump.

## On today's show:

**Mared Gwyn** reports on today's Euronews Enlargement Summit in Brussels, bringing together EU leaders and the countries hoping to join the bloc.**Petar Marković**, Montenegro's ambassador to the EU, explains why Podgorica believes it is the frontrunner, whether the 2028 target is realistic, and what obstacles remain.** Sasha Vakulina**, Euronews Ukraine correspondent, looks at Kyiv's hopes to open its remaining negotiating clusters and interviews Ukraine's ambassador to the EU,** Taras Kachka**.** Matt Klink**, Republican strategist and president of Klink Campaigns, discusses whether the midterms are a referendum on** Donald Trump**, and what a Democratic win would mean.
