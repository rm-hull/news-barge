* **The next game in the*Elder Scrolls*series is officially titled:*The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel***
* **Bethesda Game Studios has also shared a teaser trailer hinting at the game's location, which appears to be Hammerfell**
* **The studio says, "It’ll be some time before we’re ready to share more" about*Sentinel***

Bethesda Game Studios has finally announced the name of the next game in its long-running *Elder Scrolls*series:* The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel*.

The news comes from a video celebrating the studio's 40th anniversary, which highlighted its many achievements and ended with Bethesda creative director Todd Howard offering a new look at the game.

"Everybody’s always asking about *Elder Scrolls 6,*" said Howard. "And we thought, hey, given the moment, what a perfect time to tell you what* The Elder Scrolls 6* is actually called."

The teaser trailer is short and doesn't showcase gameplay, but the camera pans over sand before a magic-looking scroll emerges. Then the game's title, as fans theorized, "*The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel,"* was revealed.

The trailer description also provides an additional hint to the game, which reads, "The scroll offered only a shimmer, a veil thinly lifted to my eyes. A future lost. A victory undone. Sands of time scattered by fate," which is written in game lore by "Yazar, Moth Priest to High Queen Iradne of Hammerfell (4E 206)."

![The Elder Scrolls VI: Official Name Reveal - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/mFu8t9735IY/maxresdefault.jpg)

*The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel* has been in development for a very long time. Announced all the way back in 2018, today's teaser trailer and name drop are the first new pieces of information we've received about the game since.

In a follow-up post on X, Bethesda seemingly confirmed the game's location is set in Hammerfell, but it will be "some time" before more details
