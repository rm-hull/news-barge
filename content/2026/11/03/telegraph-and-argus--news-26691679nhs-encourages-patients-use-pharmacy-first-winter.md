PEOPLE are being encouraged to visit their pharmacy for quick treatment of common health problems.

The reminder comes during Ask Your Pharmacist Week, which runs from November 2 to 9 and highlights the value of community pharmacy teams.

Dr Faisel Baig, GP and medical director for primary care at NHS North East and Yorkshire, said: "Winter is always a time when we see more people needing NHS help, and your local pharmacy is a great place to start if you have a common health problem.

"Pharmacists are highly trained healthcare professionals who can assess a number of conditions and provide advice and treatment where appropriate, often without you needing to see your GP first.

"So, if you or a family member develop a common winter ailment such as a sore throat, think Pharmacy First."

Pharmacists can assess and treat seven common conditions, including sinusitis, earache, shingles, and uncomplicated urinary tract infections in women aged 16 to 64.

Nailys Straughan, a pharmacist from Amble, shared a recent example.

She said: "I recently carried out a consultation for two members of the same family who had sore throats.

"I was able to assess and treat both patients during the same visit."
