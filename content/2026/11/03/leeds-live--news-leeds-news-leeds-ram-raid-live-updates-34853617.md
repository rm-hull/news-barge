A Leeds store was targeted by crooks in a ram-raid attack on Monday evening.

Police were soon on the scene as the Belstaff store was rammed by a red car before masked intruders entered the Victoria Quarter-based store and made off with items of clothing on Monday evening (November 2). Belstaff is a brand that sells expensive coats and jackets.

Police cordons currently remain in place. It has been reported that two vehicles made off with "designer goods" from the store in Vicar Lane.

### Police appeal for witnesses

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Detectives investigating a ram raid at a clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds city centre yesterday (2 November) are appealing for witnesses.

"Officers were called at around 8.40pm last night (2 November) to reports that a Kia Ceed had deliberately driven into a clothing shop on Vicar Lane in Leeds along with another Kia Ceed being present at the time.

"It was reported to police that a number of people entered the shop and stole a large quantity of clothing. Enquiries remain ongoing by Leeds CID."

Anyone who witnessed the incident, has any footage, or can provide information are asked to contact police via 101 or use the Live Chat facility quoting reference 13260623871.

Information can always be given anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
