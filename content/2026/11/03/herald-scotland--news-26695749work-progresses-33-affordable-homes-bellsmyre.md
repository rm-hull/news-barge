Construction is progressing on the second phase of a major affordable housing regeneration project in Bellsmyre, West Dunbartonshire, with the first of 33 new homes now taking shape.

Led by Caledonia Housing Association (CHA) in partnership with West Dunbartonshire Council, the Bellsmyre Regeneration project aims to deliver up to 138 homes over five years as part of a wider community-led redevelopment.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21663396.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Bellsmyre Regeneration project. *(Image: Supplied)*

The latest phase follows the completion of 27 homes on Whiteford Avenue in January, built by CCG (Scotland).

The new development will comprise a mix of houses and flats for existing Bellsmyre residents, who are relocating from demolished flats or temporary accommodation.

Plans also include new footpaths and cycle routes, alongside improvements to public green spaces to strengthen connections across the community.

The homes are being built using CCG’s modern construction methods and will feature heat pump technology for low-carbon heating and hot water.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21663395.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Bellsmyre Regeneration project. *(Image: Supplied)*

The contractor also plans to support the wider community through site-based employment opportunities and career events with nearby schools.

Phase 2 has received £5.8 million in Scottish Government grant funding and supports the Housing Emergency Action Plan. The project is being delivered through a partnership between CHA and CCG that began in 2018, with completion of the latest phase expected in 2027.

Allan Jones, chairperson of Caledonia Housing Association, said: "The continued regeneration of Bellsmyre is a longstanding commitment for Caledonia and remains a key project within our development programme.

"It is fantastic to see this next phase get underway, delivering new affordable homes for the area and supporting the wider sustainable regeneration of the community."

David Wylie, managing director of CCG (Scotland), said: "It is great to see the first homes take shape at Phase 2, marking another significant milestone in our partnership with Caledonia Housing Association, which began in 2018.

"The association’s decision to appoint CCG directly, combined with their commitment to working collaboratively with us and our valued design team, has helped drive efficiencies at every stage, ultimately enabling us to deliver high-quality sustainable homes on budget and at pace.

This streamlined approach to partnership working is helping to address local housing needs and tackle fuel poverty in the local area, and we look forward to progressing towards completion in 2027."
