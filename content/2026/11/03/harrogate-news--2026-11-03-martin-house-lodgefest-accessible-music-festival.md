Young people with complex needs enjoyed live music, DJ sets and festival entertainment when Martin House Children’s Hospice hosted its annual accessible music festival, LodgeFest.

The hospice grounds in Boston Spa were transformed into a festival site, complete with a tepee, gaming tent, food stalls, glitter face painting and a graffiti wall.

Around 150 people attended, with young people and their families enjoying performances from local bands and DJs.

## Creating an accessible festival experience

LodgeFest is designed to give children and young people with life-shortening conditions the opportunity to enjoy a festival experience in an environment that can accommodate their medical and accessibility needs.

> **Vicki Greensmith, Director of Clinical Services at Martin House:** “LodgeFest is all about giving the young people we support the chance to enjoy the same experiences as other teenagers and young adults – listening to live music, spending time with friends and simply having fun.
>
> For some, attending a mainstream festival can be difficult because of their medical or accessibility needs. At Martin House, we can create that experience in an environment where those needs are supported, so young people and their families can relax and enjoy the day together.

The festival takes its name from the hospice’s teenage and young adult unit, the Lodge. First held in 2017 as part of Martin House’s 30th anniversary celebrations, this year’s event had a video game theme.

The daughters of Jayne Geall, Lily-Rose and Tia have been supported by Martin House.

> **Jayne Geall, Martin House family:** It’s brilliant, for children who can’t get to a normal festival, it means the world. There’s always so much going on – you can see the smiles on people’s faces. It’s the best experience.

## Supporting families across Yorkshire

Martin House supports around 550 children and young people with life-shortening conditions each year, from across West, North and East Yorkshire. It also provides support to their families.

The hospice has been providing specialist palliative care since 1987, offering support at its Boston Spa site, in hospitals and in families’ own homes.

Its services include planned short breaks, symptom management, emergency care, community care and end-of-life care. Bereavement support is also available to families.

There is no charge to families using Martin House’s services. Providing its care costs around £13 million each year, with the majority of funding coming from voluntary donations and fundraising.

This year’s LodgeFest was sponsored by HARIBO UK, which has supported the hospice through a partnership lasting 18 years.

Tabea Powell, Head of Communications at HARIBO UK, said:

> **Tabea Powell, Head of Communications at HARIBO UK:** We’re delighted to support LodgeFest and see first-hand an experience where the young people supported by Martin House are able to make special memories with those closest to them.
>
> Creating moments of happiness for families is something that sits at the heart of both HARIBO and Martin House, and it’s why our partnership has remained so important to us over the past 18 years. We’re incredibly proud to continue supporting the hospice and the children, young people and families it cares for.

Martin House is based in Boston Spa and provides specialist palliative care and support to children and young people with life-shortening conditions across Yorkshire.
