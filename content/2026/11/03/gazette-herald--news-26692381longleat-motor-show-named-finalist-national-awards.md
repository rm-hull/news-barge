A motor show has been named a finalist in the Motoring Spectacle category of the Royal Automobile Club Historic Awards.

The Longleat Motor Show Weekender, held at the Longleat estate in Wiltshire, was selected for its 2026 event, which drew thousands of visitors with historic motoring displays and live rally car demonstrations.

Winners will be announced on November 19, 2026, at the Royal Automobile Club in Pall Mall.

Scott Ashman, head of operations at Longleat, said: "We were delighted to be selected as a finalist; it is testament to the hard work of all involved, feedback from our guests, the team on site, plus every owner, club, visitor and member of the event teams who contributed.

"We are very pleased to be working with Steve and the team to bring the event back in summer 2027."

Longleat partnered with Masters of Motoring, Great British Motor Shows, and the Lombard Rally team to deliver the event.

The next Longleat Motor Show Weekender is set for July 3–4, 2027.

Organisers are inviting expressions of interest from owners and clubs for next year’s gathering, with a potential display to mark the 80th anniversary of Ferrari and 40 years since the launch of the F40.
