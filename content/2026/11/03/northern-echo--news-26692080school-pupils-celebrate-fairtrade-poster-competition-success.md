A chocolate tasting session was held to reward pupils for their creative efforts in a Fairtrade poster competition.

Year 8 students at Stokesley School took part in the challenge, organised by the Stokesley and Great Ayton Fairtrade Group, as part of a wider learning project exploring the challenges faced by producers of Fairtrade goods, the impact of climate change, and the positive effects of Fairtrade support.

Jenny James, a member of the group, said: "We greatly admired the level of engagement that the young students showed and the quality of the artwork they produced.

"We are pleased to arrange for the 28 winning posters to be displayed in our Co-op stores."

The Fairtrade group partnered with teacher Chris Jones to deliver the project.

Mr Jones said: "It’s always a pleasure to work with a community organisation in order to enrich students’ learning within geography."

Co-op stores donated Fairtrade chocolate, cake, bananas and biscuits for the tasting session and as prizes.
