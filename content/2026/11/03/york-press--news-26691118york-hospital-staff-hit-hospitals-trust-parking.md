## 'Atrocious' parking leaves York Hospital staff facing fines

NHS staff at York Hospital have hit out at the Trust, saying they had been "penalised" with parking tickets when there "simply weren't enough spaces available" on site *(Image: Newsquest)*

NHS staff have hit out at the trust that runs York Hospital, describing its parking availability as “atrocious” after some workers were fined for failing to find a space.

It comes after it was revealed that money raised by parking fines across three hospitals in the York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust had risen by more than 50 per cent in just over a year.

The number of available parking spaces at York Hospital has also dropped by 192 bays in three years, owing to the trust's investment in new buildings and facilities to improve patient care.




A trust spokesperson said it recognised that parking at the hospital remained a "challenge" for staff, "particularly in a busy city where demand for parking is high".

One staff member, who did not wish to be named, said: “The situation is atrocious - it’s really bad.

“Thousands of staff work at the hospital and even if only half of us show up for work in our cars, there are nowhere near enough spaces for us.

“We have to resort to paying in full at the multi-storey car park or drive around, sometimes for nearly an hour, to find a space outside the hospital."

In response, a representative for the trust said: “We understand the frustration this can cause and are continuing to look at ways to improve the situation.

“With limited parking available on site, we have permit criteria in place to help ensure spaces are allocated as fairly as possible, taking account of colleagues' circumstances and the needs of our services.”

![The news comes after money raised by the Trust through parking fines had risen by more than 50 per cent from March 2025 to July 2026](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21660382.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The news comes after money raised by the Trust through parking fines had risen by more than 50 per cent from March 2025 to July 2026 *(Image: Newsquest)*

A nurse at York Hospital said that despite holding a parking permit, she had also faced a fine after arriving for her shift to a full car park and being forced to park in a construction-designated spot.

She said: “I arrived at the hospital at 7.05am, yet the car park was already full.

“I spent approximately 20 minutes driving around trying to find a parking space, with no success, and ultimately had little choice but to park in the construction-designated area.

“My shift does not start until 8am, but I already have to arrive at least an hour before my shift simply to give myself a reasonable chance of finding a parking space.

“This has now become the norm for staff, and quite frankly, it is unacceptable."

## 'Staff are being penalised with parking tickets when there simply aren't enough parking spaces available'

She added: “To make matters worse, the trust appears to be planning to introduce parking charges for all staff on lower pay bands, despite the fact that there is already insufficient parking capacity for the existing workforce as well as financial burden.

“How can it be justified to charge staff for parking when the trust cannot even provide enough spaces for everyone?

"What I find particularly frustrating is that staff are being penalised with parking tickets when there simply aren't enough parking spaces available.

"We are expected to arrive early, spend time driving around looking for spaces, and then face the possibility of receiving a fine because we have nowhere else to park."

![The Trust said it remained ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21136674.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Trust said it remained "committed" to doing what it can to make travelling to and from the hospital easier *(Image: Newsquest)*

The trust said it was continuing to work with City of York Council to explore “longer-term solutions” to parking at York Hospital, alongside investment into alternative travel options for staff.

A spokesperson added: “This includes subsidised bus travel, with discounts of up to 50 per cent on fares, improvements to secure cycle parking and support for colleagues who wish to cycle to work.

“We know there is no simple solution, particularly for colleagues who rely on their cars to get to work, but we remain committed to doing what we can to make travelling to and from the hospital easier.”

One hospital worker said: “You shouldn’t have to pay to go to work.

“I love my job, but I spend so much extra time and money getting to and from work – it just adds to the stress.

“I live around 30 minutes away but spend an hour and a half getting to work on the days I choose to take the car.”

On social media, another commenter said: “You have to be on site before 7am in the hope of getting a spot. It’s no good if you start at 9am.

“I feel for the visitors as well, getting charged ridiculous prices for parking.”
