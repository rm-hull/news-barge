The Ministry of Defence (MOD) been rapped after a young Redcar soldier was struck in the head and died during a training exercise when a tank gun turret went into a spin.

Private Jethro Watson-Pickering, 23, from Boosbeck near Redcar, suffered fatal head injuries duringt training exercise iron storm on October 15, 2021.

The soldier served with the 1st Battalion, The Yorkshire Regiment, and was driving a Scimitar tank at the Army’s Salisbury Plain training area in Wiltshire.

His unit was acting as the enemy force for another Army regiment in the exercise.

At about 11.50am, the vehicle was manoeuvring to escape an “attack” when its gun barrel hit a tree.

The impact sent the turret spinning, and a storage bin attached to it struck Private Watson-Pickering’s head.

He died at the scene.

Following a Health and Safety Executive (HSE) probe, the MOD has been issued with a Crown Censure, the most serious sanction available against a Crown body.

The HSE found the Army had no safe system for operating the vehicle during the exercise.

Inspectors found risk assessments had missed the danger of the turret rotating unexpectedly when the gun barrel was hit.

No measures were put in place to stop drivers being struck by parts attached to the turret.

The watchdog also found earlier incidents had not led to proper risk-assessment reviews, while soldiers received inadequate training and instructions.

The HSE said guidance failed to cover the dangers of unintended turret movement and driving with a soldier’s head outside the vehicle.

By accepting the censure, the MOD admitted breaching its duties under Section 2(1) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974.

A Crown Censure is a public reprimand and the strongest action the HSE can take against a government department.

HSE inspector Emma O'Hara said: "Our thoughts are first and foremost with the family and friends of Private Jethro Watson-Pickering, who must live with his loss.

"Just like any other employer, the MOD has a responsibility to take all reasonably practicable steps to control the risks to the safety of its employees.
