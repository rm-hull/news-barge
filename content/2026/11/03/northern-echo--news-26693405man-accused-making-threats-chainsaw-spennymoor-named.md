A man will appear in the dock today accused of making threats while carrying a chainsaw in Spennymoor.

Police were called to the town centre at around 2pm on Sunday (November 1).

They said a man had a chainsaw and was making threats to members of the public.

Armed officers were deployed, and a suspect was arrested and taken into custody.

Matthew O’Dowd, 36, of no fixed abode, has been charged with affray, possessing a knife, blade or pointed article in a public place and criminal damage.

He has been remanded into custody and is expected to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court this morning (November 3).
