The body of a man has been found in a burned-out van on an industrial estate in Renfrewshire.

Police said officers were called to a report of a vehicle fire on Brownsfield Road in Inchinnan at around 2:40pm on Sunday, November 1.

The Scottish Fire & Rescue Service also attended and the fire was extinguished.

A body was found within the vehicle.

Police said enquiries to establish the full circumstances are ongoing.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: “Around 2.40pm on Sunday, 1 November, 2026, police received a report of a vehicle fire on Brownsfield Road, Inchinnan.

"Emergency services attended and the fire was extinguished.

"A body was found within the vehicle and enquiries to establish the full circumstances are ongoing.”
