Darlington Borough Council could spend a further £560,000 progressing plans for new offices and the redevelopment of the former Wilko site in the town centre.

Cabinet members will be asked to approve the money when they meet on Tuesday, November 3.

It would pay for design work and the costs of getting the plans ready for a planning application.

Plans include 50,000 sq ft of new office space on the former Sports Direct site at East Street.

The council also wants permission to demolish the former Wilko building and its car park, with improvements planned for the surrounding area.

The council has already set aside £410,000 to prepare plans for the offices.

However, the project was expanded following talks with Tees Valley Combined Authority about possible funding.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21587075.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Artist's impression of new office site. *(Image: DARLINGTON BOROUGH COUNCIL)*

The combined authority encouraged the council to include the Wilko site in the plans, creating a larger redevelopment project covering both sites.

The new offices are planned close to the £120m government economic campus being built off Brunswick Street.

It is expected to house 1,600 civil servants from nine government departments.

Consultants estimate the office scheme could support 470 full-time jobs, including around 275 for Darlington residents, and add £21m a year to the economy.

A planning application is expected to be submitted by December, with a decision expected by April 2027.

If the money needed to build it is secured and Cabinet agrees, construction could begin towards the end of 2027 and finish in mid-2029.

However, the council says spending the extra £560,000 does not mean the offices will definitely be built.

The project would still need funding and further Cabinet approval before construction could begin.
