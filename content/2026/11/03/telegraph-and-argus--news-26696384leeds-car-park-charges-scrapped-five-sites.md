Parking charges at a number of district car parks are set to be scrapped from next week after the council completed a review and public consultation into the controversial scheme.

Parking charges were introduced at Leeds City Council-owned car parks in Wetherby, Horsforth, Garforth, Rothwell and Guiseley in October 2025 as part of measures to raise revenue and fund maintenance.

The scheme prompted strong opposition from residents, businesses and local councillors, who argued it was damaging town centre trade and forcing motorists onto nearby residential streets.

Following a council review and public consultation launched in July this year, which found widespread opposition to the charges, Leeds City Council has now decided to scrap them.

The charges will be removed from Monday, November 9.

In a message sent to councillors, the authority said: "Further to our recent email and contact regarding the review of district parking charges.

"Please note that a delegated decision has now been taken to revoke all parking charges in the following car parks:

* Wilderness and Station Gardens, Wetherby
* Fink Hill, Horsforth
* Barleyhill Road, Garforth
* Marsh Street, Rothwell
* Netherfield Road, Guiseley

"The charges will be removed at these locations with effect from Monday 9th November, 2026."

The decision means motorists will once again be able to park for free at Guiseley's Netherfield Road car park and Horsforth's Fink Hill car park, alongside the other affected sites.

Reacting to the announcement, former Guiseley and Rawdon Conservative councillor Paul Alderson, who organised a petition opposing the introduction of charges at Netherfield Road, said he was "absolutely delighted".

Posting on Facebook, Mr Alderson said: "I am absolutely delighted that parking charges at Netherfield Road Car Park have officially been scrapped.

"Thank you to everyone who signed my petition, commented on and shared countless posts, and generally supported our campaign to reverse these unfair fees over recent years.

"A huge well done to former councillor colleagues Councillor Alan Lamb and the Leeds Conservative Group for keeping the pressure on and forcing the Labour administration into this common-sense u-turn. A great win for our local area!"

Meanwhile, Guiseley and Rawdon Labour ward councillors Eleanor Thomson, Sonia Leighton and Oliver Edwards welcomed the outcome and thanked residents who took part in the consultation.

In a joint Facebook statement, they said: "Some good news to share with you: charges at Netherfield Road Car Park and some other district car parks across Leeds will be removed from Monday 9th November.

"Thank you to everyone who responded to the consultation; the 200 or so responses to the recent review made it clear that there was a strong preference for removing the charges.

"Thank you for your patience during the last year and especially to those who have chosen to park with consideration for others despite the charges and who have engaged constructively.

"The income that has been raised far outweighs the implementation costs and has helped to support vital Council services.

"We will monitor the ongoing parking pressure points and see what improves in the coming weeks and where issues may still persist."

The councillors also stressed that the changes apply only to district centre car parks and not to parks and countryside parking charges, where income generated is reinvested into local parks and car park improvements.

Leeds City Council has been contacted for comment.
