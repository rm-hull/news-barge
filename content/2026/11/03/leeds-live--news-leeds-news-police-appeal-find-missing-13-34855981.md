Police are appealing for help to find a missing 13-year-old girl who was last seen in Leeds.

Sky was last spotted in the Whinmoor area at around 3pm on Monday, November 2. West Yorkshire Police say there are concerns for her welfare and officers are carrying out enquiries to find her.

She is described as around 4ft 9ins tall, of slim build, with red hair and blue streaks at the front. She was last seen wearing white trainers and a black hoodie.

Anyone who has seen Sky or knows where she might be is being urged to contact police.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Sky, aged 13, is missing from the Leeds area and was last seen in Whinmoor at around 3pm yesterday (2 November).

"She is around 4ft 9ins, of slim build, with red hair with blue streaks in the front and was last seen wearing white trainers and a black hoodie.

"Officers are making enquiries to locate her as there are concerns for her welfare. Anyone who has seen Sky or knows of her whereabouts are asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 or using the Live Chat facility on our website, quoting log number 1809 of 2/11.

Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
