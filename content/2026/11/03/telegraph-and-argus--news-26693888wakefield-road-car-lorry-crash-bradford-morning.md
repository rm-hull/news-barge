A crash involving a car and a lorry caused disruption on a major Bradford road this morning.

The collision on Wakefield Road at around 10am left a grey Vauxhall Corsa with significant damage to both its front and rear.

The hatchback was found wedged against roadside railings, with its front bumper partially detached, and the bonnet buckled.

The rear of the vehicle also sustained extensive impact damage, with the tailgate crushed inward, the rear bumper heavily distorted, and body panels crumpled around the boot area.

![A close-up of the damage on grey car](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21663735.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A close-up of the damage *(Image: T&A)*

A red Joda lorry was seen directly behind the damaged car in the outside lane.

The circumstances leading up to the collision have not yet been confirmed.

![A close-up of the damage on grey car](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21663736.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The railing is also damaged *(Image: T&A)*

There is currently no information on whether anyone was injured or if emergency services attended the scene.

There may be delays in the area as a result of the crash.

West Yorkshire Police has been contacted for a comment.
