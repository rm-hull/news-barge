Police are hunting a man believed to have been carrying a weapon, possibly a crossbow, in the Borders town of Eyemouth.

Officers have appealed for information after a group of males was spotted in possession of the suspected weapon on Monday, November 2.

Police Scotland said it received a report at around 9.40am that a group of males aged between 16 and 30 were in the Renton Terrace area of the town.

The group was later seen on High Street before leaving the area.

Officers attended and carried out a full search of the area, but found no trace of the group or the weapon.

The man believed to have been carrying the weapon is described as white, with short dark hair. He was wearing a T-shirt with orange markings and black trousers.

Inspector Copeland said: “There were no reports of any crossbow being fired or anyone injured, but we are keen to trace this group and establish the exact circumstances.

“A number of enquiries are ongoing, including checking CCTV from in and around the local area to identify those involved.

“We urge anyone with any information that may assist our investigation to come forward.”

Anyone who witnessed the group, or who has dashcam, doorbell or CCTV footage from the area around the time of the sighting, is asked to contact Police Scotland.

Information can be passed to officers by calling 101 and quoting the relevant incident details. Anonymous reports can also be made through the charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
