A man is due in court accused of a string of offences after a hidden camera was found in a North East gym.

Craig Bayliss, 62, of Applewaith Gardens, Skelton, faces seven charges after an investigation was launched on Sunday afternoon.

Officers said they were called to an East Cleveland gym at about 1.40pm after videos were reportedly found on a hidden camera.

Bayliss is charged with two counts of voyeurism, sexual assault and observing a person carrying out a private act.

He also faces charges of possessing an offensive weapon in a private place, possessing a weapon designed to discharge a noxious liquid, gas or electrical incapacitation device, and possessing a class C drug.

He is due to appear at Teesside Magistrates' Court today.

Detective Inspector Jayne Bryan, from Redcar CID, said: “At 1.40pm on Sunday, November 1, police were called following a report of videos being located from a hidden camera.

“Officers attended and an investigation began involving the arrest of a 62-year-old man who has since been charged with seven offences including two counts of voyeurism, sexual assault on a female, possession of an offensive weapon in a private place, possess a weapon for the discharge of a noxious liquid/gas/electrical incapacitation device, possess a class C drug and observe a person doing a private act.

“He has been remanded in custody to appear at Teesside Magistrates Court on Tuesday, November 3.

A 60-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of voyeurism and is currently in police custody for questioning.”
