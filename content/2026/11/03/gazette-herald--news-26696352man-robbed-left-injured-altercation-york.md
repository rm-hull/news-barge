A man has been arrested after another man was reportedly robbed and left injured after an “altercation” in York city centre, police have said.

The man’s mobile phone was allegedly stolen after police were called to St Sampson’s Square at about 3.50am on Saturday, October 24.

Silver Street, near Shambles Market, was cordoned off after the reported incident with traders reporting police in the area when they arrived for work.

North Yorkshire Police said the victim of the alleged robbery, a man in his 20s, required medical treatment following the altercation involving another man.

The force said its suspect in the case is also being investigated for a separate suspected fraud offence after officers reportedly found a stolen bank card in his possession.

“We are keen to speak to anyone who may have witnessed the suspected robbery or recall any suspicious behaviour around that time to come forward,” a police spokesperson said.

“This includes any direct approaches from two men about mobile phones and possibly the offering of controlled substances, and the later realisation that a personal device or bank card were missing.

“One of the suspects is described as black and the other was dressed in dark clothing and wearing a cap.”

They urged anyone with information about the alleged crimes to phone police on 101 or report it anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website. Quote reference number 12260209037 when providing details.

North Yorkshire Police said a 28-year-old man was arrested in connection with the incident on October 24 and was held in custody for questioning before being questioned and remanded by other forces.
