North East musician Sam Fender has landed one of British music’s most coveted gigs - soundtracking this year’s John Lewis Christmas advert.

The singer’s new cover of the Rolling Stones song You Can’t Always Get What You Want features throughout the retailer’s 20th festive campaign, which has become a beloved sign of Christmas season's arrival across the country.

Mercury Prize Award-winner Fender, 32, recorded the song with help from pupils from his former school, John Spence Community High School, marking his dedication to his North East heritage while being one of the world's biggest artists.

Musicians from Easington Colliery Band, who previously worked on Remember My Name from Fender's last album, People Watching, also feature on the cover.

The advert stars Dame Kristin Scott Thomas searching for the right Christmas presents for her grandchildren, joined by Pickles, a vintage Little Snoopy Pull Along Toy voiced by comedian Paul Whitehouse.

After deciding she can do better than giving cash in envelopes, Scott Thomas' character heads out to buy thoughtful gifts before transforming a room in her home into a homemade 'Gran Cave.'

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21663696.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The advert stars Dame Kristin Scott Thomas searching for the right Christmas presents for her grandchildren, joined by Pickles, a vintage Snoopy toy voiced by comedian Paul Whitehouse. *(Image: John Lewis)*

John Lewis said the campaign was created with the cost-of-living crisis in mind.

Items featured in the advert's 'Gran Cave' were either recycled from elsewhere in her house or cost less than £100.

Marketing and loyalty director Rosie Hanley said this Christmas the department store's message was simple: "Don't give up on giving."

Hanley added: "We hope this restores the nation’s gift-giving confidence and encourages customers to rediscover the joy of choosing something truly thoughtful for the people they love.”

Fender’s cover of the Rolling Stones classic was released today (November 3), his first new single since his song with Olivia Dean, Rein Me In, became the longest-running number one in UK chart history.

Fender said it was a "privilege to rework such an iconic song by such a legendary band," adding that his former school was featured on the track to remember his roots "in the spirit of Christmas."

The children’s choir from the original song was re-recorded with the children from John Spence Community High School in North Shields, with a brass section from the Easington Colliery Band.

Fender's involvement came after a chance meeting with his former teacher, Damien Grazzer, head of music at John Spence, over summer this year.

Grazzer told the BBC that the invitation was a “real shock” and an “amazing experience” for the children.

"Just to give something back like that I think is absolutely incredible," he added.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21663681.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fender said it was a "privilege to rework such an iconic song by such a legendary band." *(Image: Melissa Gardner)*

Rolling Stones’ Mick Jagger was "pleased to hear" that the song would be covered for the advert, adding he was a fan of Fender's music and that he had done a "great job with it."

The single release also includes Fender’s 2020 cover of Winter Song, which he has previously performed at Gateshead's Sage with Royal Northern Sinfonia.

The Fender rendition of North East band Lindisfarne's song is being made available physically for the first time, six years after the original cover.

Profits from physical sales through John Lewis will go to Action for Children, which supports people who have grown up in care through the retailer's Building Happier Futures programme.

Sales through Fender’s own store will support Teenage Cancer Trust, which provides specialist nursing care and youth support for young people with cancer.

Fans of the BRIT Award-winner were quick to celebrate the cover online, with many dubbing this year's festive season 'Sam Fender Christmas,' since the advert follows his 2027 tour announcement.

One fan commented on Fender's social media announcement of the single, calling the cover "legendary," and adding "what a way to finish the year."

Another fan praised Fender for his inclusion of his former school, writing online: "Love the fact the choir is on it, class for those kids."

Fender's involvement with John Spence Community High School is the latest example of his commitment to his North East roots, with the 32-year-old consistently using his platform to champion his native region.

Fender regularly highlights the area's industrial heritage, communities and struggles through his music and charity work, and stresses the importance of giving musicians in the region opportunities.

The advert was again created by Saatchi & Saatchi, who have worked on John Lewis' last five Christmas campaigns, and directed by BAFTA-winner Lucy Forbes, who is currently working on HBO's new Harry Potter television series.

It premiered on John Lewis’s social media channels this morning (November 3), ahead of it's TV debut tonight during Coronation Street on ITV, and The Great British Bake Off on Channel 4.
