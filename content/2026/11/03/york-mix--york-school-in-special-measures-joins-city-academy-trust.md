An York school placed into special measures earlier this year has now officially joined a city academy trust.

**Carr Infant School** on Ostman Road in Acomb has joined**Excel Learning Trust**, becoming the eighth school in the trust.

The move also brings the infant school back together with its neighbour, **Carr Junior School**, which is already part of the trust.

In January, an Ofsted inspection downgraded Carr Infants from ‘good’ to ‘special measures‘. The watchdog cited significant turbulence at the school, multiple leadership changes, and gaps in basic teaching across reading, writing, and mathematics.

Inspectors noted that governors had already brought in Excel Learning Trust earlier that month to spark a turnaround. The school said at the time the report “recognises the ‘renewed energy’ within our leadership and the commitment of our staff”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/carr-infant-school-entrance-ostman-road-acomb-york-gsv.webp)

The infant and nursery school, which educates around 200 children aged three to seven, has spent the past year working through a rapid turnaround plan.

A recent Ofsted monitoring visit described the improvements at Carr Infants as “rapid and necessary”.

It said classroom behaviour has improved significantly, curriculum and teaching standards are on the up, and leaders have taken swift action to boost support for children with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND).

Matt Oxley, head of school at Carr Infants, described the trust move as a major step forward for the whole school community.

“Joining Excel Learning Trust is a really positive step for Carr Infant School,” he said. “We have already felt the benefit of working alongside colleagues across the Trust, and our staff have been fantastic throughout.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/primary-school-pupil-work-classroom-istock.jpg)

“Being part of a family of schools that shares our ambition for every child is something we are all genuinely proud of, and we cannot wait to see what we achieve together.”

Leaders say Carr Infant School will keep its distinct character and its central role in the Acomb community, while sharing staff training, resources, and classroom expertise across the trust.

Mark Hassack, chief executive of Excel Learning Trust, said: “From the moment we began working with Carr Infant School at the start of the year, it was clear that this is a school with real potential and a team determined to realise it.

“That progress owes a great deal to the exceptional leadership of Charlotte Crabtree, chair of governors, Matt Oxley, headteacher, and James McGann, our executive director of education, who have worked tirelessly together for the benefit of every child.

“Just as importantly, the staff at Carr Infants are fully on board with joining the Trust, and their commitment has been a joy to see.”

Ofsted will continue regular monitoring visits to Ostman Road as the recovery work continues. Parents can find more details about school admissions and updates directly on the Carr Infant School website.
