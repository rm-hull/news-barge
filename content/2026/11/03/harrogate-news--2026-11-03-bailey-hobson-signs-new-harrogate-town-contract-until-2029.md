Harrogate Town midfielder Bailey Hobson has signed a new contract with the club, keeping him at the EnviroVent Stadium until the end of the 2028/29 season.

The 24-year-old has made an impressive start to his time at Town since arriving in the summer, making 16 appearances and scoring five goals.

Hobson has also captained the side on several occasions, with his performances earning him a nomination for the National League Player of the Month award.

Manager Simon Weaver said the club had been impressed with Hobson’s contribution both on and off the pitch.

> **Simon Weaver, Harrogate Town manager**: We’ve been really impressed with Bailey’s contribution to everything on and off the pitch from day one.
>
> His running power and desire to set new fitness standards for the club was a remarkable start to pre-season and he’s already stamped his authority and personality on the club.
>
> He’s a relentless footballer that has made the team so much stronger and brings a big smile to the training ground every day with his positive approach.
>
> We’ve always tried to have a core in the team that personifies what the club is about, we need good people to drive that culture on and off the pitch and Bailey certainly does that.

Hobson is currently recovering from surgery after suffering a broken metatarsal last month. He is expected to be out of action for around three months.

The club has wished him a speedy recovery and is looking forward to seeing him return to action later this season.
