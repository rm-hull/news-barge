Concern grows for a teenager with links to Bishop Auckland who has been missing for over a week.

Jamie, 16, from Durham, was reportedly last seen in Sunderland city centre on Monday, October 26.

Officers say they are "growing increasingly concerned" for his welfare.

Northumbria Police say that "extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Jamie, with officers growing increasingly concerned for his welfare."

The force added: "He has links to the Bishop Auckland area of County Durham."

He is described as a white boy with light brown hair and was last seen wearing a black Under Armour cap, black North Face coat, and black tracksuit bottoms.

Jamie, or anyone with information on where he may be should send Northumbria Police a direct message on social media or use the live chat or reporting forms on the Force’s website.

For those unable to make contact via those ways, call 101 and quote reference number: 26000484033.
