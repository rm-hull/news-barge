## Yorkshire family tricycle firm records strongest year to date

Jorvik Tricycles, based in Yorkshire, saw more than 35 per cent year-on-year growth in the 2025/2026 financial year *(Image: Jorvik Tricycles)*

An independent mobility tricycle company has reported its strongest financial year to date.

Jorvik Tricycles, based in Yorkshire, saw more than 35 per cent year-on-year growth in the 2025/2026 financial year, driven by expansion in product lines and retail presence.

The company, founded in 2014 by James Walker after his father’s Parkinson’s diagnosis, offers practical, stable and affordable tricycles designed to support independence and mobility.

Mr Walker, founder and managing director, said: "I’m incredibly proud of the financial success we’ve achieved in the last year.

"As an independently run family business, our growth is a testament to the hard work of our team and the quality of the products and service we offer.

"We’ve built the business around addressing a clear gap in the market, and it’s encouraging to see more people recognising the value of what we offer."

Jorvik has continued to invest in its UK showroom network, with new locations opening in Chester and Southampton.

The expanded retail footprint has brought the company’s full range of tricycles and personalised customer service to more customers across the UK.

Mr Walker said the company remains focused on maintaining its roots while reaching new markets.

He said: "Our goal remains continuing to be recognised as the leading brand for electric tricycles in the UK and beyond, while staying true to our Yorkshire roots and the ethos that has shaped the business from day one.

"We plan to expand our ranges, introduce more accessible options and help people across the UK to experience the freedom and joy of cycling."

Jorvik Tricycles was launched from Walker’s living room.

The company’s recent success has been supported by the launch of new models, including the RIDA and VIT, which have helped strengthen its position in the accessible cycling market.
