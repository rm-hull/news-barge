THE biggest Benidorm cast reunion is heading to Bradford Live for a night of stories and live entertainment.

Bradford Live will host the event on March 9, 2027, in celebration of the 20th anniversary of the hit ITV comedy Benidorm, which ran from 2007 to 2018 and followed the lives of holidaymakers and staff at the fictional Solana Hotel.

Fans can expect live performances of songs from the show, behind-the-scenes stories, a quiz to crown the "Brain of Benidorm," and the chance to win original costumes.

Derren Litten, writer and creator of Benidorm, said: "Expect stories you have never heard before, listen to cast members discuss clips you know and love, sing along to your favourite songs from the show, ask the cast the questions you want answering, win prizes available nowhere else and prepare yourself for the ultimate Benidorm fan event!"

He will be joined on stage by cast members including Crissy Rock, Asa Elliott, Hannah Hobley, and Oliver Stokes, with more guests to be announced.

Benidorm opened with an audience of almost six million and reached an audience of almost eight million at its peak.

Tickets go on general sale at 12pm on November 6 via bradfordlive.co.uk.
