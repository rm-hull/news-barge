## Two renowned North Yorkshire groups join forces for special Remembrance concert

Chanticleer Singers

A special concert will take place this weekend bringing together the voices of Chanticleer Singers and the music of Kirkbymoorside Town Brass Band for an evening of music, reflection and remembrance.

Chanticleer Singers, a mixed-voice choir founded in 1975, have built a reputation for performing a wide range of music, from medieval works to contemporary choral pieces.

Based in Malton, the choir performs regularly across North Yorkshire and throughout the UK, with performances ranging from stately homes to cathedrals. Their music has also taken them overseas, including performances in France, the Netherlands and Ireland.

Joining them will be Kirkbymoorside Town Brass Band, which has provided the musical heartbeat of the town for more than 200 years.

The band has performed at countless community occasions, including Armistice Parades, Coronations, weddings, funerals and concerts, as well as representing the town and the North of England in regional and national competitions.

Together, the two ensembles will create a moving evening of music and remembrance, bringing their distinctive sounds together in a celebration of lives remembered and the enduring power of music to unite and offer hope.

The concert will take place on Saturday, November 7, 7.30pm at the James Holt Concert Hall
