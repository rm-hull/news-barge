CREWS attended a two-vehicle crash in North Yorkshire this afternoon (Tuesday).

Malton and Huntington crews attended the collision at 3.02pm near Westow.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "No people were trapped and the two occupants were out of vehicles on arrival of the emergency services.

"Fire and rescue crews carried out general scene safety.

"Ambulance paramedics carried out precautionary checks on both occupants and the incident was left with police awaiting recovery of the vehicles."
