Emergency services have been called to a crash in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place in Deighton Road, Kirk Deighton, near Wetherby at 5.25pm on Monday (November 2).




North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service crews from Tadcaster and Wetherby were called to the scene where a vehicle had crashed with paving.

A service spokesperson confirmed: “On attendance the crews found a vehicle had collided with paving.

“No Fire Service action was required at this incident and it was handed over to police.”
