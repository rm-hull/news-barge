An exhibition of film, sound, and sculpture is set to open in York.

The exhibition features work by two York-based multimedia artists and will be held at the historic Red Tower on Foss Islands Road.

The exhibition will be open for free on November 7, from 6pm to 9pm, and November 8, from 1pm to 4pm.

No Spinoza will display video films that accompany an album of music, while Nick Walters will present his sculptures.

Both artists are interested in the symbolism of found materials and the mystical traditions of the wilderness, promising an immersive, multi-sensory experience.
