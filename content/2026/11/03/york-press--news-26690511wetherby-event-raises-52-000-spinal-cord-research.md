A charity raceday has raised more than £52,000 to support research into spinal cord injuries.

The funds were raised during a special lunch and auction at Wetherby Racecourse's Northern Raceday in support of Spinal Research.

The event was attended by nearly 250 guests, including the family of former jockey Graham Lee, who was paralysed from the neck down following a racing accident at Newcastle nearly three years ago.

His children, Robbie and Amy Lee, spoke at the event about the impact of their father’s injury and the hope that new research could one day restore some function.

Amy said: "Every day is a new battle, but so far we have won each one and live to fight the next, which is something my family is so grateful for."

She described the racing community’s support as amazing.

Wetherby’s Northern Raceday has now been running for 36 years and has raised more than £850,000 for Spinal Research, the UK’s leading charity focused on developing treatments for paralysis.

Suzanne Redding, head of audience and communications at Spinal Research, said: "We are so grateful to Amy and Robbie, to Wetherby Racecourse and to every single person who supported the Northern Raceday."
