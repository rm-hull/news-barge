Independent retailers on Commercial Street in Harrogate have launched their annual Christmas chocolate appeal, collecting treats for children supported by two local charities.

The Commercial Street Retailers Group is asking members of the public to donate chocolate selection boxes, tubs of chocolates and chocolate biscuits as part of its yearly appeal.

This year, donations will be shared between the Woodlands Children’s Ward at Harrogate District Hospital, supported by Harrogate Hospital & Community Charity, and New Beginnings, a peer-led domestic abuse service supporting women and children across North Yorkshire.

## Supporting children in Harrogate

The chocolate collected for the hospital will go to children receiving care on the Woodlands Children’s Ward at Harrogate District Hospital.

New Beginnings provides support for women and their children who have previously experienced domestic abuse, helping them move forward following the immediate crisis.

The retailers are hoping the local community will get behind the appeal and donate some of the chocolate treats that children enjoy at Christmas.

The group said that chocolate selection boxes, tubs of chocolates and chocolate biscuits would all be welcome.
