A serving West Yorkshire Police officer has been jailed for seven years after being convicted of raping a woman more than 20 years ago.

PC Jo Lavender, 45, who was serving in Leeds District before being suspended, was sentenced at York Crown Court on Tuesday after being found guilty of one count of rape following a trial.

The offence took place in 2002, but the victim did not come forward until December 2024.

As part of a Victim Personal Statement, the woman said: "I can say knowing that I was believed gave me a sense of relief and that someone confirmed what had happened to me was wrong did help me."

## **'The offence happened over 20 years ago'**

The victim described the lasting impact the offence had on her life.

She said: "The offence happened over 20 years ago and has had a huge impact on my life. I have felt so low that someone would choose their own self gratification over my wellbeing. This is something that I have struggled to deal with over the years."

The woman also praised those involved in the investigation.

She said: "I would like to express my gratitude to DC 1631 Devonshire and West Yorkshire Police for working so hard to make sure I received the justice for what has lived with me for such a long time."

## **Sexual Harm Prevention Order imposed**

Lavender, who joined West Yorkshire Police as a member of police staff in 2002 before becoming an officer in 2005, was sentenced to seven years in prison.

The court also made him the subject of a Sexual Harm Prevention Order and ordered that he be placed on the Sex Offenders Register indefinitely.

He had been found guilty of rape at York Crown Court on October 19.

## **Police chief condemns 'gross abuse of trust'**

Detective Chief Superintendent Tanya Wilkins, Head of West Yorkshire Police's Professional Standards Directorate, said: "Today’s sentencing reflects the seriousness of PC Lavender’s offending. His actions were a gross abuse of trust and are wholly incompatible with the values and standards expected of those who serve the public.

"I hope that the victim feels that justice has been served. It is her courage and resilience that has helped ensure he has been held accountable for his actions.

"West Yorkshire Police remains committed to tackling violence and sexual violence against women and girls, holding offenders to account, and maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and integrity. There is no place in policing for individuals who commit such offences and misconduct proceedings will now follow."

## **'I would like to express my admiration for the bravery she has shown'**

Following the guilty verdict on October 19, Det Ch Supt Wilkins said: "The victim has lived with the trauma of what PC Lavender did to her for two decades before coming forward to report this offence in 2024.

"I would like to express my admiration for the bravery she has shown. I do not underestimate the courage it takes to report something like this, and even more so knowing that by that time the man responsible was a police officer.

"If anyone has been a victim of rape or any other sexual offence, I would urge them to please report it. Please know that we will listen and we will act, it doesn’t matter who the perpetrator is or where they may work.

"Following this court case, we will now continue with misconduct proceedings to ensure PC Lavender is barred from working in any role in policing in the future."
