A popular fish and chip shop at a County Durham holiday park will continue serving customers right up until Christmas after operators announced an extension to its seasonal opening.

The Witton Whaler at Witton Castle Country Park near Bishop Auckland will give visitors extra time to enjoy a chippy tea before Christmas.

The announcement was shared by the holiday park on social media, where managers confirmed the popular takeaway would then close for a short winter break throughout January and February before reopening in March.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21663979.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Witton Castle Country Park has announced its popular on-site fish and chip shop, the Witton Whaler, will remain open until the week before Christmas. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

The Witton Whaler has become a well-known feature of the park, serving freshly cooked fish and chips along with a range of traditional takeaway favourites.

Based within the grounds of Witton Castle, the chippy attracts not only holidaymakers staying on-site but also visitors from across County Durham looking for a meal in picturesque surroundings.

Announcing the extended opening, Witton Castle Country Park said there was still "plenty of time to enjoy a chippy tea before the festive season".

The update proved popular with customers, who were quick to show their support.

One customer said they were "over the moon" with the news, describing the takeaway as the "best fish and chips around".

Another said they had recently visited and enjoyed their meal in the warmth of the castle after finding the weather too cold to sit outside, describing the food as "lush".

Others welcomed the decision, with comments including "smashing" and "chuffed to bits" and online reviews have praised both the quality of the food and the generous portions on offer.

One TripAdvisor reviewer described the fish and chips as "excellent", while another praised the takeaway as a highlight of a visit to the park.

The extended opening comes as many seasonal visitor attractions begin winding down services for winter, allowing customers to enjoy one last fish and chips outing before Christmas arrives.

After closing for January and February, the Witton Whaler is expected to reopen in March ahead of the new holiday season.
