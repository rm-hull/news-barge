John Lewis' 2026 Christmas advert has divided viewers - here's why.

The advert was released on November 3 and stars Dame Kristin Scott Thomas as Gloria, a grandmother hosting her family in London on Christmas Eve.

When her grandchildren Freddie and Maggie disrupt her study, Gloria brings down Pickles, a vintage pull-along Little Snoopy toy voiced by Paul Whitehouse, to entertain them.

Pickles springs to life and encourages Gloria to do more than give cash, leading her on a tuk-tuk journey to John Lewis.

### Watch the 2026 John Lewis Christmas advert

On Christmas morning, the children are given a key instead of money, unlocking Gloria’s study to reveal a transformed "Gran Cave" filled with lights, disco balls, and a turntable.

Set to a Sam Fender cover of You Can’t Always Get What You Want by The Rolling Stones, the advert aims to capture the magic of Christmas with a personal touch.

The song was recorded with children from John Spence Community High School and a brass section with the Easington Colliery Band.

Proceeds from the single will support the Teenage Cancer Trust and Action for Children.

## **Brits give verdict to John Lewis's 2026 Christmas advert**

Public reaction to the advert has been mixed, with some viewers praising its heartwarming message and others feeling underwhelmed.

One YouTube commenter wrote: "Love the heart and message behind this, it just took a bit of time to get to the point! Worth sticking with though."

Another viewer said: "The sentiment is there but I’m used to crying into my morning coffee with the John Lewis Christmas adverts, not this year."

A third added: "I like the sentiment, but unfortunately was a miss for me again this year."

However, others were more positive.

One fan wrote: "My fave John Lewis ad in a while, wanted to cry for Pickles."

Another said: "Lovely ad, I know my child would love that room.

"And so great that the soundtrack will bring children safety and happiness by supporting Action for Children."
