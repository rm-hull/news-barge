Alan Shearer is urging people across the North East to take part this Christmas as a festive attraction joins his £1million fundraising drive for a new hydrotherapy pool.

Northern Lights Newcastle is backing the Move a Mile challenge with the Alan Shearer Foundation as part of its drive to raise £1 million for the new pool.

People can walk, run, cycle or move a mile in any way, with a collective target of 52,719 miles—one for every seat at St James’ Park.

Alan Shearer, former Newcastle United striker and patron of the foundation, said: "The response to Move a Mile has been brilliant.

"We’ve had people taking part all over the world, but there’s something really special about bringing it back to Newcastle, particularly with St James’ Park just over the road.

"Northern Lights is a fantastic way for families to get involved, enjoy a great night out and support something that means a lot to me."

He added: "I'm looking forward to getting down there, completing my mile alongside some of the Centre’s members and hopefully encouraging thousands more people to do the same.

"We've set ourselves a big challenge with the Million Pound Mission, but we know what a difference a second hydrotherapy pool will make to the people who use the Centre.

"Every mile gets us closer to our target, and every donation helps us get closer to making that pool a reality."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21633277.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Keith Dungait and Alan Shearer *(Image: Move a Mile)*

In a recent example of community effort, Keith Dungait, 50, from Morpeth, completed the challenge in 2,775 steps and raised £2,775 after regaining the ability to take supported steps following a spinal injury.

The Alan Shearer Centre offers free specialist facilities to more than 10,000 members and their families, but demand is straining its only hydrotherapy pool.

A second pool would increase capacity, cut waiting times and give more children and adults with complex disabilities access to essential therapy.

The Northern Lights Newcastle event runs in Leazes Park from November 20 to December 31, 2026.

Visitors can opt to add a £5 donation when booking tickets online, with their mile automatically counted towards the campaign’s target.

QR codes along the trail let visitors log their mile and donate.

A spokesperson for Northern Lights said: "There’s something special about walking the trail with St James’ Park lit up just beyond the trees, and we can’t think of a better place for Alan and the Centre’s members to take on their mile.

"We’ve been proud to work with the Alan Shearer Centre since 2024, and we hope our visitors will help bring the second pool a step closer this Christmas."

The event is giving Centre members and their carers 100 free tickets.

Northern Lights Newcastle has raised more than £100,000 for local causes since it launched at Leazes Park in 2023.

In its first year, it pledged up to £10,000 in matched donations to restore the park’s children’s playground and raised £21,000 in total.

This year, it supports Newcastle Hospitals Charity, whose young patients are creating artwork for the trail, along with the Newcastle United Foundation and North-East SEN Collective.

The 2026 trail, A Christmas Time Machine, features installations inspired by toys, traditions and festive memories, including a giant rainbow Slinky, 65 illuminated Rubik’s Cubes and a kaleidoscope walkthrough.

A second hydrotherapy pool would cut waiting times and make therapy more accessible to children and adults with complex disabilities.

Move a Mile is part of the foundation’s wider Million Pound Mission, launched in April 2026.
