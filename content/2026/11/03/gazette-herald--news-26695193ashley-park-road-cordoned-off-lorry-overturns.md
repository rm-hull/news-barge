A SECTION of a residential street in York has been cornered off after a lorry overturned.

An image of the truck shows it suspended on one side, with barriers preventing pedestrians and vehicles from entering an area of Ashley Park Road, close to its junction with Stockton Lane.




The incident was reported at 2pm on Tuesday (November 3).

The Press has contacted City of York Council's highway team for comment.
