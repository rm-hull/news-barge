COMMUTERS have been warned of disruption to trains travelling to and from areas in North Yorkshire.

National Rail said that a safety inspection of the track between Leeds and Hull, including Selby, means that some lines are now blocked.

Disruption is expected until 4.15pm today (Tuesday, November 3).




A National Rail spokesperson said: “You can travel on the next Northern service to your destination, where available.

“You can also travel on TransPennine Express services at no extra cost between Leeds and Hull.

“Please note that other train operators may also be affected by disruption – please check your whole journey before travelling.

“Road transport has been requested to operate between Micklefield and Selby and between York and Selby via Sherburn-In-Elmet.”

TransPennine Express said that rail passengers can use their tickets on the following services:

· TransPennine Express and CrossCountry services from Leeds to York,

· Northern services from York to Selby, Brough and Hull

· Northern and Hull Trains services from Doncaster to Selby, Brough and Hull

· LNER, CrossCountry and Northern services from Leeds to Doncaster
