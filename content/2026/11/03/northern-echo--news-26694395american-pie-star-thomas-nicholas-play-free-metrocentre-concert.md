American Pie star Thomas Nicholas will delight North East fans with a free live performance this week.

Thomas Nicholas, best known as Kevin Myers in the American Pie films, will bring his band to the Metrocentre in Gateshead this Friday (November 13), as part of his US and European tour.

Despite being widely known for his work as a Hollywood star in projects like American Pie, Rookie of the Year and Stealing Sinatra, 46-year-old Nicholas is also a rock and pop-punk musician.

His 2026 TNB tour has already seen him visit cities across the United States, Germany, France and Spain, with Gateshead's free gig set to mark the award-winning actor's second performance in England.

Local musicians Mitch Laddie and Matt Fairley will support Nicholas' band at the event organised by HMV Live, in partnership with North East tattoo studio Doc Black Ink.

North East fans were shocked at Nicholas' announcement online, with one writing: "Kevin from American Pie coming to Newcastle was not on my bingo card, but let's show the lad some support."

Another commenter said: "You will get a great reception in the 'heed mate! Good folk and we love to dance."

Visitors can attend the performance without paying, but tickets are required and can be picked up online.

Afterwards, Nicholas will appear in HMV for an exclusive poster signing. The first 50 people to book their tickets to his performance will receive a free signed poster.

The star's support acts consist of two North East artists, County Durham blues musician Mitch Laddie and folk artist Matt Fairley.

Shotley-Bridge-born Mitch Laddie was dubbed "one of the best guitarists in the world" by blues musician Walter Trout, and has won awards for his critically acclaimed modern approach to blues, rock, soul, and funk.

Matt Fairley is a North East folk and Americana singer-songwriter based in Newcastle, who describes himself as an "oversharer" and has grown in popularity due to his "raw and authentic" approach to his lyrics.

Nicholas' free performance is one of several events taking place at Metrocentre in the run-up to the centre's festive season, with the shopping centre hosting seasonal attractions, family entertainment and special appearances throughout November and December.

The event will begin at 3pm on Friday, November 13, at the Metrocentre, and is set to last until around 6pm, with tickets available online.
