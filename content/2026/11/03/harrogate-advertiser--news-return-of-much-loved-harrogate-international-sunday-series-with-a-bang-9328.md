The much-loved Harrogate International Sunday Series is back with a bang in the new year, bringing some of the world’s most talented classical musicians to North Yorkshire.

The exciting 2027 Sunday Series – which runs from January to April at the historic Old Swan Hotel – features a collection of outstanding artists who are shaping the future of classical music.

These hugely popular coffee morning recitals have been delighting audiences for more than three decades, offering chamber music fans the opportunity to experience internationally renowned artists on their doorstep.

Following the success of this year’s post-concert talks, Harrogate International Festivals is once again offering audiences the chance to hear directly from three of the musicians as they discuss the stories behind the music.

Sharon Canavar, Chief Executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said: “The Sunday Series has been part of Harrogate’s cultural calendar for more than three decades, bringing some of the world's finest musicians to our town.

“What makes these concerts so special is the opportunity to experience extraordinary musicianship in such an intimate setting and to hear their stories.”

The new season will open on January 24 with violinist Emily Sun, whose compelling interpretations have earned her international plaudits, and pianist Alim Beisembayev – a familiar face after winning First Prize at the prestigious Leeds International Piano Competition in 2021.

This captivating duo will present three remarkable violin sonatas from Beethoven, Strauss and Franck.

February kicks off with Kleio Quartet (February 7).

These acclaimed BBC New Generation Artists are rapidly making a name for themselves as one of the most exciting young string quartets around.

Later in the month, the prodigiously talented Callum McLachlan (February 28) showcases the piano’s extraordinary range in a programme passing through three centuries – from the elegance of Gibbons to the virtuosity of Ravel – concluding with Rachmaninoff’s memorable *Étude-Tableaux*.

The Jess Gillam Trio will make their hotly anticipated Harrogate International Festivals’ debut on March 14, performing music from Jess’s acclaimed new album *Shine* which celebrates the saxophone’s remarkable versatility.

Acclaimed British cellist Maxim Calver and Japanese-born pianist Kumi Matsuo will bring the 2027 Sunday Series to a thrilling close on 4 April 4, with a celebration of some of the most revered classical composers.

*Friends of the Festival tickets are available now.*
