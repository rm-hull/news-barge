A garden centre near York is bringing the magic of Christmas to life with a festive line-up of family-friendly events.

Langlands Garden Centre, on York Road, Shiptonthorpe, has unveiled a programme of seasonal experiences designed to spread joy and create lasting memories.

This year’s schedule includes festive events for all ages and interests, with a little something for pets too.

Fiona Oades, centre manager, said: "We can't wait to share the magic of the festive season with you at Langlands Garden Centre.

"Join us for an enchanting line-up of Christmas events filled with warmth, community, and holiday cheer.

"From dazzling lights and immersive experiences to unforgettable moments with Santa, our team is dedicated to bringing the wonder of Christmas to life.

"Bring your loved ones and create cherished memories that will last a lifetime."

**What can visitors expect?**

Breakfast with Santa invites families to enjoy a cooked breakfast, meet Santa, take photos, and receive a personal gift -a charming alternative to a Santa’s Grotto.

For those who prefer something sweeter, Afternoon Tea with Santa offers a tower of treats, the chance to hand deliver letters to Santa, and a special gift.

Younger children can enjoy the Baby & Toddler Breakfast with Santa, designed especially for their first Christmas.

**Recommended reads:**

There’s even an event for four-legged friends.

Paws and Claus includes breakfast for owners, a festive goody bag for dogs, and a visit from a very special someone.

Langlands Garden Centre is part of the British Garden Centres (BGC) group, the UK’s largest family-owned garden centre chain.

Founded in 1990 by brothers Charles and Robert Stubbs with the opening of Woodthorpe Garden Centre, BGC now operates 80 centres across the UK.

The group has grown rapidly since 2018 through a series of acquisitions.

More than 3,500 people are employed across its garden centres, restaurants, nurseries, and distribution centres.

BGC is owned by the Stubbs family, who also operate Woodthorpe Leisure Park in Lincolnshire.

To book a place at one of this year’s festive events, visit www.britishgardencentres.com/events/?location=langlands-garden-centre.
