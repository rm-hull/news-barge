The much-loved Harrogate Hospital Friends Garden at Harrogate District Hospital has officially reopened following a major refurbishment, providing patients, visitors and hospital staff with an improved outdoor space for relaxation, reflection and connection.

The Friends Garden, which has welcomed visitors since 2001, was officially reopened by Tom Gordon MP, Member of Parliament for Harrogate and Knaresborough, at a special celebration attended by volunteers, supporters and project partners.

The refurbishment has placed accessibility at its heart, including the installation of a new automatic entrance door from the hospital’s main corridor. This will make it easier for wheelchair users and people with mobility difficulties to access the garden independently.

## A space for wellbeing, remembrance and connection

At the heart of the garden stands the Tree of Life, a metal sculpture created by renowned sculptor Steve Blaylock.

The sculpture was developed through a partnership between the Friends of Harrogate Hospital, Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust’s Organ and Tissue Donation Committee, and NHS Blood and Transplant.

It honours the generosity of organ and tissue donors whose gifts have transformed and saved lives.

Visitors will also find several new features designed to encourage wellbeing and connections with nature and other people.

A colourful new Bug Hotel, created by students from Horticap, will provide a home for insects and wildlife. Informative display boards will also help visitors learn more about the natural world.

The garden now features new ‘Happy to Chat’ benches, inspired by an international initiative encouraging kindness and conversation.

Anyone sitting on one of the benches is signalling that they would welcome a friendly chat, helping to tackle loneliness and create opportunities for meaningful connections between people who might otherwise never meet.

## Community support helped deliver the project

Following the ribbon-cutting ceremony, more than 50 guests gathered in Herriots Coffee Lounge to celebrate the reopening and recognise the organisations and individuals whose generosity, expertise and commitment helped make the refurbishment possible.

John Fox, Chair of the Friends of Harrogate Hospital, thanked those who had supported the project.

> **John Fox, Chair of the Friends of Harrogate Hospital:** “We greatly appreciate the help provided by so many companies and organisations and we recognised their support by presenting them with a Membership Certificate of ‘Enterprise Friends’.
>
> We presented Certificates to Morgan Sindall Construction, Moortown Group, Marlborough Brickwork, Metallic Fabrications, G&H Building Services, Steve Blaylock, Horticap, EnviroVent and Harrogate Integrated Facilities (HIF).”

Certificates of Appreciation were also presented to Steven Fry of Morgan Sindall Construction, Carolyn Rothwell, the hospital’s volunteer gardener, Ian Hutchison of Harrogate Integrated Facilities, and accessibility advisers Trevor Chapman, the late Janet Chapman and Hayley Day.

## Garden provides a welcome space for patients and staff

Speaking after the opening, Tom Gordon MP highlighted the important role played by the Friends of Harrogate Hospital and the wider community in supporting local healthcare services.

> **Tom Gordon MP:** “It was an absolute joy to be invited to cut the ribbon and officially open the Friends Garden after its recent renovations. We are so lucky to have such a fantastic local hospital charity, and a local community who all chip in.”

For Steven Fry, Project Manager at Morgan Sindall Construction, working on the project revealed just how important the garden is to the people who use it.

> **Steven Fry, Project Manager at Morgan Sindall Construction:** I didn’t realise how important the garden was to so many people until we closed it to start the works. The number of staff who came up to me saying they were missing their garden and wanted their space back really showed how much it means to them.
>
> For me, it’s about bringing people together. It’s a tranquil space for lots of different reasons, but it also brings out the human aspect of caring, not just among hospital staff, but among the people who come into the hospital too.
>
> The contractors have also taken a real sense of pride from the work because they know how much it means to so many people.

The reopening marks a new chapter for the Friends Garden, which continues to offer a welcoming place for quiet reflection, conversation, remembrance and respite for everyone visiting Harrogate District Hospital.
