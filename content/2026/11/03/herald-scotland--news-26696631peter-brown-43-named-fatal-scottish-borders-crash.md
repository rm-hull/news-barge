**A man described by his family as a “gentle, kind, loving soul” who died in a three-vehicle crash in the Scottish Borders has been named.**

Peter Brown, 43, who was known as Richard, died following the crash on Tuesday October 27 on the A6089 Station Road, Gordon.

Emergency services attended, but he was pronounced dead at the scene.

The crash involved a Ford Focus, Toyota Hilux and Tesla Model S.

Mr Brown’s family paid tribute to him, and thanked Police Scotland for their support.

A statement said: “Richard was a gentle, kind, loving soul who will be deeply missed by his heartbroken parents, wider family and many friends.

“The family wishes to thank the emergency services who attended the scene of the crash and everything that they did to try and save Richard. The caring support of Police Scotland Traffic division has been exceptional.

“The help of motorists who assisted is also greatly appreciated.”

Sergeant Barry Sommerville, Police Scotland, said: “Our thoughts remain with Richard’s family and friends at this difficult time.

“Our inquiries into the circumstances remain ongoing and I would again appeal to anyone who witnessed the crash or has any information that may assist to contact us.”

Those with information have been asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2708 of Tuesday October 27.
