A festive theatre double bill is promising laughter and holiday cheer for audiences of all ages this Christmas season.

Badapple Theatre Company, based in Green Hammerton and currently an Associate Company of York Theatre Royal, is touring two family-friendly productions.

The shows run from November 28 to December 30.

The first show, Santa’s Christmas Cakes, runs from November 28 to December 13 and tells the story of Rascal the elf, who is banned from Santa’s sleigh and must bake cakes instead.

Cinderella In Boots, running from December 11 to 30, puts a spin on the classic Cinderella tale, blending it with the adventures of Puss in Boots.

Kate Bramley, artistic director and writer of both shows, said: "I'm delighted that we have two festive shows on tour this year.

"After our 2024 sold-out tour of Richard Kay's Sleeping Beauty and the Beast, there was a lot of interest in touring another of our signature panto mash-ups.

"But we still wanted to offer a family Christmas show for all ages too.

"So this year it’ll feature baking live on stage on a working oven, as well as all the usual song and puppetry tomfoolery.

"Hopefully plenty of laughs for everyone."

Tickets are available at badappletheatre.co.uk.
