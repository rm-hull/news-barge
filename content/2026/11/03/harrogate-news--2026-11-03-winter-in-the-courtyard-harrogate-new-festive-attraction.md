Winter in the Courtyard Harrogate – The West Park Hotel in Harrogate is creating a new festive attraction this Christmas, transforming its courtyard into a winter-themed destination with Alpine-inspired food, seasonal drinks and live entertainment.

Called **Winter in the Courtyard**, the experience will launch on Saturday 14 November and is intended to provide an additional festive gathering place for residents and visitors during the Christmas period.

The hotel says the idea came about partly because Harrogate’s traditional Christmas Market is not taking place this year, creating an opportunity to introduce something different to the town’s festive offering.

## Live music, DJs and festive food

The courtyard will feature a programme of entertainment, including DJ evenings, acoustic live music and opportunities for people to meet friends and family in a seasonal setting.

The public launch on Saturday 14 November will feature DJ entertainment from Funk My Life from 2pm.

The courtyard will open on selected days throughout November before operating daily during December. Visitors can expect festive food, warming drinks and a relaxed winter atmosphere.

## Creating a new festive gathering place for Harrogate

> **Angelika, Event Manager at The West Park, said:**
>
> At The West Park, we wanted to create something truly special for Harrogate this festive season – a Festive Winter Courtyard that brings people together and captures the warmth, charm and excitement of Christmas.
>
> With the Christmas Market not taking place in Harrogate this year, we saw an opportunity to introduce something a little different to the town’s festive offering. Our aim is to create a welcoming space for both local residents and visitors to enjoy, whether that’s meeting friends and family, enjoying a festive drink, or simply soaking up the atmosphere.
>
> From lively DJ evenings and acoustic live music to festive food, warming drinks and relaxed winter gatherings, we hope there will be something for everyone to enjoy.
>
> Above all, we’d love Winter in the Courtyard to bring a little extra magic to Harrogate this Christmas and, hopefully, become a much-loved part of the town’s festive celebrations for years to come.
