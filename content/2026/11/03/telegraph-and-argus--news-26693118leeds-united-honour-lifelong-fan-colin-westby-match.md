Leeds United has announced plans to pay tribute to a lifelong supporter who died ahead of the club's Premier League match against Manchester United at Elland Road.

The club said it was "heartbroken" to learn of the death of Colin Westby after he suffered a medical emergency on October 18.

It is understood Mr Westby lived in Bradford.

The 68-year-old was taken to hospital, where he was placed into a medically induced coma.

After a period of unresponsiveness, the decision was made to withdraw life support.

## More than five decades of support

A long-standing season ticket holder, Mr Westby had supported Leeds United for more than 50 years and was a familiar face at Elland Road.

Announcing the news on social media, the club said: "Leeds United are heartbroken to learn of the passing of supporter Colin Westby at the age of 68."

## Fans invited to join tribute at Elland Road

Leeds United will honour Mr Westby during their next Premier League fixture against Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday, November 7.

Supporters are being encouraged to take part in a tribute during the 68th minute of the match, marking his age and recognising his decades of support for the Whites.

In its statement, the club said: "The thoughts of everyone at Leeds United are with Colin's family and friends at this sad time."
