A TOWN in York could see security measures added to a popular playing field recently used by Travellers.

Haxby Town Council launched a public consultation at the end of last month to add physical protection at Ethel Ward playing field in Calf Close.

The site encompasses the grass playing fields plus facilities including the sports courts, play area, pavilion, Scout Hut, picnic benches and parking.

Unsanctioned visits over the past few summers had caused anti-social behaviour, distress to residents closest to the approximately 15-acre site and the operation to clean up afterwards proved costly, the council said.




As reported by The Press, in July the council said it faced a bill of more than £2,500 to clear the playing field following an unauthorised four-day Traveller encampment which was given notice to leave by City of York Council on June 29.

A local councillor said professional cleaners were brought in and reported 16 caravans and more than a dozen cars present during the stay.

Families with young children were asked to stay away until the area was sanitised and a clean-up operation began in order ahead of the very popular annual Haxby Town Carnival which centres on Ethel Ward playing field.

The council said in a Facebook post to accompany the public consultation that incidents like this "cannot be allowed to happen again."

Haxby Town Council said the estimated cost of installing the measures to prevent vehicles entering the facility is £25,000.

Options being discussed include fencing, benches, boulders, bicycle racks, and planting.

A poll launched alongside the consultation listing options saw trees, bushes and shrubbery in the lead.

Haxby Town Council is taking ideas and comments for the consultation until Sunday, December 13.
