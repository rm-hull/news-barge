A car has been severely damaged in a collision on a busy Bradford road.

Police were called to reports of the incident on Tong Street this afternoon.

A spokesperson for the force said that officers were alerted at 12.08pm and that there were no injuries.

When the T&A visited the scene at approximately 1.45pm, a white car could be seen parked on the side of the road with its front end badly damaged in the collision.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21665751.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A vehicle suffered extensive damage to its front end in the collision *(Image: T&A)*

Another car appears to have suffered damage to its rear end.

Photos posted on social media show a similar scene, with at least one police vehicle in attendance.
