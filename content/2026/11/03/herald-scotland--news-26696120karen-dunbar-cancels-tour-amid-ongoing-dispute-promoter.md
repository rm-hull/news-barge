**A Glasgow comedian has announced that her upcoming tour shows will be cancelled after her promoter failed to pay her.**

Karen Dunbar took to Instagram to share the news, saying that after doing 39 shows, she'd only been paid for five.

The Chewin' The Fat star apologised to fans who had bought tickets and explained they should be able to get a refund through the promoter.

She said: "I've been doing the tour for the last two months, I've done 39 shows, it would have been 40 but one of the shows was cancelled without me being told and I've been paid for five shows so I can't go on.

"Honestly, I've never been in a situation like this in my life. The stress has been unbelievable and I am so sorry for the people that have bought tickets and will not be coming.

"Hopefully you'll be able to get a refund through the promoter through where you bought them."

She later claimed she would take the promoter to court over the dispute, though she refused to name the organisation.

The tour began in Giffnock back in September and was due to run until November 28, ending in Ayr.

Upcoming shows in Peebles, Greenock and Shotts will go ahead as planned as they are being run by a different company.

Karen added: "Thanks to everybody that supported me. Onward and upward!"
