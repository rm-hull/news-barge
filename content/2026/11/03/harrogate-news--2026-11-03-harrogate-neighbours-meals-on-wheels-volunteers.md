Harrogate MP Tom Gordon has backed a volunteer recruitment drive by Harrogate Neighbours, highlighting how its Meals on Wheels service provides more than just food to people who may be vulnerable or isolated.

Tom Gordon joined volunteers from Harrogate Neighbours’ award-winning Food Angels service during National Meals on Wheels Week, which runs from 2 to 6 November 2026.

During the visit, he heard from Chris Tapping, who has volunteered with the service for almost seven years, about the important role drivers play in supporting people in their own homes.

## More than just a meal

The Food Angels service provides regular meals, but its role extends beyond nutrition. Volunteers also offer valuable human contact and carry out welfare and hydration checks when delivering food.

Regular visits can help identify potential concerns, including cold or unsafe homes, risks of falls and changes in someone’s health or wellbeing. This can help highlight problems before they develop into an emergency.

Chris Tapping encouraged people to consider volunteering and suggested trying a shift to see what the role involves.

> **Chris:**
>
> I can guarantee you will feel so good about it and that you’ve given something back.

Tom Gordon is encouraging people to come forward as volunteers, as well as asking residents to consider whether friends or relatives might benefit from the service.

## Tom Gordon encourages residents to get involved

> **Tom Gordon said:**
>
> It’s more than just a meal, volunteers like Chris at Harrogate Neighbours provide vital wellbeing checks and are a lifeline to some of the most vulnerable.
>
> If you want to make a difference locally, you should absolutely reach out about volunteering.
>
> Take the opportunity to consider whether someone you know might benefit from the service. Good nutrition and hydration could make all the difference to maintaining their health and independence.

National Meals on Wheels Week aims to highlight the contribution made by meal delivery services and the volunteers who help support people in their communities.

Harrogate Neighbours is inviting people interested in volunteering to find out more about the Food Angels service. Residents who are concerned about someone who may benefit from regular meal deliveries can also explore making a referral.

More information about the national campaign is available from the National Association of Care Catering.

Details about volunteering and referrals are available on the Harrogate Neighbours website.
