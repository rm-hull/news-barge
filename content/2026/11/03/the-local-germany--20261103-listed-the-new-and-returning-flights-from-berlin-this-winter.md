While some connections from Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) are disappearing – including several Ryanair flights – other airlines are adding, restoring or expanding their schedules from the German capital this winter.

The additions range from European city breaks and seasonal flights to Lapland to stronger connections with the Middle East and major onward travel hubs.

Here's a look at the new flights you can catch from Berlin this winter.

**New European city routes**

Eurowings launched two new Italian routes from Berlin on November 2nd.

The airline now flies to **Rome four times a week**, on Mondays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays.

It serves **Bologna three times a week**, on Mondays, Fridays and Sundays.

Wizz Air is also expanding its European network from Berlin. The airline began flying to **Vilnius** in Lithuania three times a week on October 26th, and will launch two weekly flights to**Sarajevo** in Bosnia and Herzegovina on December 3rd.

From December 14th, Wizz Air will fly daily to **Milan Malpensa**, marking the airline’s first Italian route from the German capital.

Three weekly flights to **Pristina** in Kosovo will begin on the same day, followed by three weekly services to**Suceava** in Romania from December 17th.

Eurowings is restoring several routes for the winter season.

The airline will resume nonstop flights to **Sarajevo** on December 20th, operating twice a week. This is separate from Wizz Air’s new service to the Bosnian capital.

Eurowings will also continue flying to **Lisbon** and**Porto** in Portugal**and Bilbao** in Spain over the winter, with each route operating twice a week.

For a more temporary seasonal break, the airline will fly to **Edinburgh** in Scotland once or twice a week between December 4th and January 4th.**New flights to Finnish Lapland**

Berlin travellers will have additional options for reaching Finnish Lapland this winter.

AirBaltic is planning to introduce a weekly flight to **Kuusamo** from December 19th.

Eurowings will add its own service to Kuusamo the following day, while also increasing the frequency of its existing flights to **Rovaniemi** and**Kittilä** compared with last winter.**New and returning Middle Eastern routes** Condor began a new daily nonstop service from BER to**Abu Dhabi** on October 15th.

The route operates in partnership with Etihad Airways, allowing passengers to book onward connections through Abu Dhabi to destinations in Asia, Africa and Australia – including Bangkok, Hanoi and Sydney.

**READ ALSO: LISTED - The new direct flights from Germany in Autumn 2026** Eurowings is due to restore its daily service to**Dubai** from November 25th.

The airline will also launch a new direct route to **Baghdad** in Iraq on December 10th. Flights are expected to operate once a week, on Thursdays, with an additional Tuesday service over Christmas and New Year.

A twice-weekly service to **Jeddah** in Saudi Arabia is also scheduled to return from November 12th.**More flights through Doha** Qatar Airways increased its Berlin–**Doha** service from two to three daily return flights on November 1st, with departures in the morning, around midday and in the evening.

Although Doha is not a new destination from Berlin, the extra frequency should give passengers greater flexibility when connecting to destinations across Asia, Africa, Australia and the Middle East.
