A North East cyber security and IT specialist has celebrated a decade in business after transforming from its telecommunications roots into a growing technology firm.

Net-Defence marked its 10-year anniversary with an event at Dakota Newcastle, bringing together customers, partners, and colleagues to reflect on its growth and future ambitions.

The company specialises in managed IT, cyber security, and business resilience services, supporting organisations across the North East and beyond.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21652080.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Debra Cairns, managing director at Net-Defence *(Image: Angela Carrington)*

Debra Cairns, managing director at Net-Defence, said: "Ten years is a significant milestone for Net-Defence, and it was fantastic to be able to celebrate it with so many of the people who have been part of our journey, the evening was really about our customers, partners, and our team.

"We've built the business around long-term relationships and technical knowledge, and we wouldn't be where we are today without the trust and commitment of the people we've worked with over the last decade.

"We're particularly proud of our North East roots and the role we've been able to play in supporting businesses across the region."

The company began with a focus on telecommunications but has since broadened its services in response to the evolving technology landscape.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21652076.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Celebrating the 10th anniversary *(Image: Angela Carrington)*

Over the past decade, the rise of cloud computing, remote working, digital transformation, and increasingly complex supply chains have reshaped how businesses operate and heightened the importance of cyber security.

Ms Cairns said: "The biggest change we've seen is that technology and cyber security are no longer separate conversations from the wider business, they're fundamental to how organisations operate.

"Digital systems underpin everything from customer relationships and supply chains through to finance and day-to-day operations, that makes resilience increasingly important.

"Our own journey has reflected that change, we've continued to develop our expertise so that we can support customers not just with their technology, but with the wider challenges and risks that come with being a digitally connected business."

Net-Defence has invested heavily in technical skills, automation, and talent development, with a strong emphasis on building long-term capability within its workforce.

Its clients span professional services, financial services, manufacturing, and engineering—sectors where cyber security is increasingly intertwined with operational and regulatory considerations.

The company’s growth and evolving service offering have also earned external recognition.

It recently received an industry honour from the Tyne and Wear Business Awards UK and was shortlisted in the Outstanding Security Performance Awards (OSPAs).

As it enters its second decade, Net-Defence is continuing to expand its offerings and invest in future growth.

Part of this strategy includes the launch of The Signal, a free online resource designed to offer practical advice and insights on cyber security and business resilience.

The platform includes content on compliance, regulation, supply chain risk, and proactive defence, as well as guides, self-assessment tools, video content, and events.

Ms Cairns said: "The Signal is one example of how we're looking to take what we've learned over the last ten years and make that knowledge more accessible.
