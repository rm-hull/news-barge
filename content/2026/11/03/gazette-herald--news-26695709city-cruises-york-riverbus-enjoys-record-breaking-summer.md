A riverbus service which offers a traffic-free alternative to getting into York city centre recorded its busiest summer on record.

The York Riverbus, operated by City Cruises, recorded a 12.4 per cent rise in passengers during the 2026 summer season.

The service offers passengers a convenient and scenic way to get from Naburn Lock to York city centre, and saw a 14.7 per cent increase during the peak summer months. 

Chris Pegg, Head of Regional Commercial, Sales & Marketing, City Cruises, said: "We’re incredibly proud to be celebrating a record year for our Riverbus service in York.

"With some fantastic weather over the summer months, we've seen more people choosing to get out on the water and experience the city from a different perspective.

"York continues to be a hugely popular destination for UK breaks, and our Riverbus offers visitors and locals alike a scenic, traffic-free way to explore the city.

"It's been a brilliant season, and we're delighted to play our part in showcasing everything this wonderful city has to offer."

August was the Riverbus’s busiest month on record, with a 24.7 per cent year-on-year increase in passengers.

The upward trend continued into September, with sales up 26.3 per cent compared to September 2025.

The success reflects a wider increase in domestic tourism across the UK, as more people opt for staycations and short breaks due to higher international travel costs.

The Riverbus connects riverside holiday parks and accommodation at Naburn Lock, Acaster Malbis, and Bishopthorpe (The Riverfront York) with King's Staith Landing in the city centre.

This provides holidaymakers and residents with a convenient and scenic alternative to driving into York.

"As more people choose UK breaks and days out closer to home, the service is playing an increasingly important role in supporting the city's visitor economy, helping holidaymakers make the most of their time in one of the UK's most popular holiday cities."

The strong performance of the Riverbus highlights its growing appeal as both a practical transport solution and a relaxing way to enjoy York’s riverside scenery.

Visitors benefit from an easy way to reach the city centre, while residents can enjoy a different view of their own city.

With rising demand for domestic travel and more people exploring the UK, the Riverbus has become an important part of York’s visitor experience.

With another record-breaking summer now complete, the Riverbus has further cemented its reputation as a convenient and enjoyable way to experience York without the need for a car.
