A hired Mercedes was seized by police.

The force said using Saltaire Road as a "race track" was "clearly unacceptable".

Police discovered that the driver was not even insured to drive the vehicle and that it had not been hired in his name.

The driver was reported to court for a number of road traffic offences.

## Full statement from the force

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "The driver of this Mercedes-Benz hire car had already been informed that it was being seized under S.59 Police Reform Act powers for his driving behaviour before we came to learn that it was not even hired in his name.

"Not only that, but he was also uninsured.

"Vehicle seized and driver reported to court for a number of road traffic offences."
