A giant festive wheel is returning to the city centre to kick off the Christmas season.

Bradford’s 22-metre-high BID Wheel will take its place in Centenary Square from Tuesday, November 24, to Wednesday, December 23, lighting up the skyline and offering panoramic views of the city during the holidays.

Alongside the wheel, a traditional Victorian carousel will bring old-fashioned charm and festive fun for families and younger visitors.

Jonny Noble, chief executive of Bradford BID, said: "The Bradford BID Wheel proved hugely popular last year, with so many people coming into the city centre to enjoy the experience and take in the views.

"We’re delighted to be bringing it back for another festive season, this time alongside the Victorian Carousel, giving visitors even more reason to come along.

"We hope the wheel and carousel, funded by our member businesses, will encourage even more people to come and enjoy everything Bradford has to offer, support our local businesses and experience the city at this special time of year."

Both attractions will be open daily from 12pm to 7pm, with tickets priced at £4 per ride.

Children under three can ride for free.
