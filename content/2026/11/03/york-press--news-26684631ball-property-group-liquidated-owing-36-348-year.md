A Harrogate-based developer of building projects has gone into liquidation after little more than a year owing almost £40,000.

Ball Property Group of Bilton Drive, Harrogate, was incorporated in July 2025 and entered voluntary liquidation on October 6, with Leeds-based XL Business Solutions as liquidator.

Companies House records its sole director as Jack Ball.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs outlines the finances behind the liquidation.




The company has assets of £27,000 in the form of a motor vehicle, but Haydock Finance was owed £34,000, leaving a £7,000 deficit.

Ball Property Group had no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

But it did owe HMRC £20,000 as a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next.

The report then outlined the unsecured creditors – those who are paid last.

They included trade and expense creditors owed £6,348.49 between, directors owed £3,000, plus the £7,000 shortfall from above, giving a total of unsecured claims of £16,348.49.

Added to the secondary preferential claims, this gave a total shortfall or deficit of £36,348.49.

With Ball Property Group having £10 in issued and called up capital, this gave a final deficit or shortfall of £36,348.49.
