A man has admitted his role in a stabbing in a North East town in July.

Armed police and paramedics were called to the Viewley Centre shops in Hemlington on Thursday (July 2) where a man was stabbed just before 10am.

Two men, Lee Brannigan and Leon Nicholson, were arrested and charged with attempted murder.

Appearing before Teesside Crown Court on Tuesday (November 3) Lee Brannigan, who denied attempted murder, admitted a charge of wounding with intent.

Addressing Brannigan, Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, said: "You have had the common sense and good judgement to plead guilty to an offence of unlawful wounding."

His co-accused Leon Nicholson admitted attempted murder at Teesside Crown Court on September 18.
