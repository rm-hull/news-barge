Shetland star Alison O’Donnell has revealed she was in floods of tears on her final day of filming – as she promised fans a “memorable exit” for her character Tosh.

One of the original stars of the long-running BBC Scotland crime drama, she has described filming her final scenes as “seismic” and “overwhelming”.

O’Donnell, who predicted it would take her years to come to terms with her departure from Shetland, revealed her farewell story involved specially-built sets, “an incredible stunt artist” and “some extremely emotional digging”.

She admitted she could not fully explain why she had decided to leave the show after 13 years, but suggested it was “a coming together of various components” that persuaded her to take the “leap” by leaving Tosh behind.

O’Donnell sprung a surprise on fans of the show in July when she confirmed she would be bowing out in series 11 of Shetland, reunited for the last time with long-running co-stars Steven Robertson and Lewis Howden, and Ashley Jensen, who joined the cast in the eighth series after the departure of Douglas Henshall’s character, Jimmy Perez.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21665166.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alison O'Donnell and Lewis Howden play DI Alison 'Tosh' McIntosh and PC Billy McCabe in series 11 of Shetland. *(Image: BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson)*

Jensen, who is expected to continue in her role in a 12th series of Shetland, has revealed that the new series will see her team struggling to come to terms with the “enormous hole” left behind by Tosh.

However, it has not been revealed if O’Donnell’s character dies or survives in her final storyline.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21665250.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alison O'Donnell during the filming of her final series playing DI Alison ‘Tosh’ McIntosh in Shetland. *(Image: BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson)*

The new series will see Detective Inspector Alison McIntosh and Detective Inspector Ruth Calder, Jensen's character, investigate the discovery of a badly-decomposed male body in the boot of a car belonging to a respected doctor who left the isles under a cloud of rumours nine years previously.

Guest stars making an appearance include Scottish comedy favourites Gregor Fisher and Gavin Mitchell, as well as This City Is Ours star Kevin Harvey, Holby City and The House of Eliott star Stella Gonet, I Swear star John McLarnon and Sherwood star Christine Bottomley.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21665270.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ashley Jensen and Alison O'Donnell will appear on screen together for the final time in the new series of Shetland. BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson *(Image: BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson)*

The new series, which launches on November 12, sees the relationship between Tosh and Howden’s character, newly demoted Constable Billy McCabe, come under strain again in the wake of the previous case, when it emerged he had accepted bribes from a former sergeant.

The BBC has revealed that Billy’s connection to Tosh proves vital and that he is able to “make amends for his own wrongdoing” in the forthcoming storyline.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21665319.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alison O'Donnell will be playing DI Alison ‘Tosh’ McIntosh for the final time in the new series of Shetland. *(Image: BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson)*

Speaking for the first time about the new series, O’Donnell said: “Billy's place in the department - both in a practical and emotional sense - has shifted quite dramatically and it has knocked everything, and especially Tosh, off kilter.

“There's so much to navigate - her guilt, her discomfort, his shame, his regret. It's an unholy cocktail of awkwardness and tension, which only exposes further the depth of their history, and richness and complexity of their relationship.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21665298.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rab C Nesbitt and Only Child star Gregor Fisher makes a guest appearance in the new series of Shetland. *(Image: BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson)*

“Tosh and Billy have been through a lot together and I can't imagine a world where they would really allow the rot to set in and give up on one another. But this is a really big deal, and it's not immediately obvious how they're going to find their way back to one another.

“However, events in season 11 are going to take them both right to the edge and the silver lining is that this brings about a sense of perspective that helps them to see what's really important.”

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21665356.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This City Is Ours star Kevin Harvey makes a guest appearance in the new series of Shetland. *(Image: BBC/Silverprint Films/Jamie Simpson)*

When O'Donnell's decision to leave the show was announced in July the BBC promised fans that Tosh would get "the fitting farewell she deserves".

O’Donnell said she had filmed some of the “most challenging” scenes of her career for Tosh’s farewell storyline, which she described as “a wild ride”.

She added: “All I can say is there was a huge amount of work involved - the building of special sets, an incredible stunt artist and some extremely deep emotional digging. Hopefully that doesn't give too much away.

“That's the thrilling side of it, but there were also many more thoughtful scenes with all the important people. It's all in there. I think it's a memorable exit.”

Asked how she felt when she was filming her final scenes on the show, O’Donnell said she struggled to take it in.

“It's like trying to look directly at the sun. It's too big and powerful. So, I just got my costume on and went to set and tried to soak it all in and spend time with my favourite people and serve the scenes.

“On my final day, a lot of crew came dressed as Tosh. They were all wearing Fair Isle jumpers and had their hair up in a bun. It was so fun and thoughtful. Our producer Angus (Sharpe) made a speech after we wrapped my final scene but sadly I can't really remember what he said because I was crying. It just came over me.

“It just felt so overwhelming. I think it's going to take me years to fully process it.”

Asked about the impact of Tosh’s exit, Jensen said: “It’s huge and not just for Calder.

“Tosh is such a stalwart of that incident room and she leaves an enormous hole.

“For Calder, it almost confirms her belief that she’s better off not getting too close to people, because ultimately they leave.

"She and Tosh have reached a lovely place in their relationship.

“They’re comfortable with each other, they have a laugh, they wind each other up, but they really get each other. They complement each other at work, so Tosh leaving changes everything. How will Ruth react? Well, she can be quite emotionally dismissive, which sometimes comes across as brutal. Only time will tell.”

Jensen admitted it was “so hard” to bid farewell to O’Donnell.

She added: “Shetland has been such a huge part of Alison’s life, and she absolutely owned it. She was so welcoming when I joined, and we’ve got on brilliantly ever since. I completely understand why she’s leaving, but it’s bittersweet. I’ll miss her terribly.”

Asked why she had decided to leave the show, O'Donnell said: "I'm not even really sure I know how to answer this one! It's not down to one particular thing, but rather a coming together of various components.

"I've been involved with it for 14 years now and I've been so lucky to have been given so many different storylines and challenges, and to have worked with so many extraordinary actors, but at this point it feels like we've probably explored (the character) enough.

"I don't think I would ever have found a moment to step away that didn't feel sad and bittersweet, but something in me said 'leap', so I did."
