TWO PEOPLE have been charged in connection with a historical child sexual abuse investigation.

Brenda Altham and Paul Hinsley, both aged 68 and from Bradford, face multiple counts of counselling the rape of a girl under 16.

The alleged offences took place on the Thorpe Edge estate in Bradford in the late 1970s and involve a single victim.

Both have been bailed and are due to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on December 1.

### Full police statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Detectives investigating reports of non-recent child sexual abuse in Bradford have charged a man and woman.

"Brenda Altham, aged 68 of Bradford has been charged with nine counts of counselling the rape of a girl under the aged of 16.

"Paul Hinsley, aged 68 of Bradford has been charged with four counts of counselling the rape of a girl under the age of 16.

"The charges relate to offences, which are reported to have occurred in the Thorpe Edge area of Bradford in the late 1970s and relate to one victim.

"Both defendants self-defined ethnicity is White British.

"They’ve both been bailed to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on 1 December."
