A man needed medical treatment after an altercation near Shambles Market in York.

Silver Street was cordoned off for several hours as police investigated the incident.

It happened at around 3.50am on Saturday 24 October, but details have just been released.

#### Map location

The victim of the robbery, a man aged in his 20s, required medical treatment following an altercation with another man on St Sampson’s Square.

The 28-year-old suspect was arrested a short time later. He was held in custody for questioning.

He is also being investigated for a separate suspected fraud offence after officers found he had a stolen bank card.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are keen to speak to anyone who may have witnessed the suspected robbery or recall any suspicious behaviour around that time to come forward.

“This includes any direct approaches from two men about mobile phones and possibly the offering of controlled substances, and the later realisation that a personal device or bank card were missing.”

One of the suspects is described as black and the other was dressed in dark clothing and wearing a cap.
