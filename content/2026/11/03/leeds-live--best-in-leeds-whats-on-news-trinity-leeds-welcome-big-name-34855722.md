# Trinity Leeds to welcome big name new opening at shopping centre

## Work is ongoing ahead of the launch

Trinity Leeds is set to welcome a big name new opening.

Work is ongoing ahead of the new launch coming to the city. The popular Joe and The Juice will soon open at Trinity Leeds.

Known for its freshly prepared juices, shakes and specialty coffee, alongside a food menu that includes an array of breakfast bowls and made-to-order sandwiches, the brand is coming to Leeds. Founded in Copenhagen, in 2002, by Kaspar Basse, Joe and The Juice has more than 500 stores worldwide.

The 3,595 sq ft new store, located on Trinity Street, will bring the brand’s distinctive experience to Leeds for the first time. Customers can expect the brand’s signature pink branding, pared-back Scandinavian aesthetics including open-counter layout, along with its high-energy atmosphere.

Steve Foster, centre director at Trinity Leeds, said: “At Trinity Leeds, we’re always looking for new and exciting ways to enhance the experience we offer our visitors, and are thrilled to welcome Joe and The Juice to our line up. Renowned for its vibrant range of freshly prepared smoothies, shakes and juices, the brand is a fantastic addition to our food and drink offering, and we’re confident they will prove popular amongst our guests when they officially open.”

Additionally, the Trinity Leeds Santa Dash is taking place on Sunday, December 6. The festive 5k will see thousands of people dressed at Father Christmas take over Leeds city centre in a spectacular celebration of Christmas spirit, community and fundraising.

Participants of all ages and abilities are invited to run, jog, walk or wheel the route, making it an event that everyone can enjoy together. From young children and grandparents to friendship groups, schools, and work teams, the dash offers the perfect opportunity to make festive memories while soaking up the atmosphere of a city filled with Christmas cheer. As a sea of red Santa suits winds through the streets of Leeds, participants can expect plenty of festive entertainment, a welcoming community atmosphere and the chance to be part of what is set to become a new Christmas tradition for the city.
