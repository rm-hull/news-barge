Global private markets manager CVC Capital Partners has opened a new office in Edinburgh, with 20 employees based at the South Gyle site.

The move forms part of the company’s long-term expansion plans and is intended to strengthen its UK operations while drawing on the city’s established financial services sector.

The Edinburgh office is CVC’s 31st location globally, adding to a network spanning six continents.

CVC manages approximately €239 billion in assets across private equity, credit, secondaries and infrastructure. Its private equity funds are invested in more than 150 businesses worldwide.

Ken Young, chief operating officer at CVC, said: "There is a fantastic pool of talent in Edinburgh, thanks to the city’s history as one of the world’s first global financial centres and as a birthplace of the modern asset management industry.

"There has always been a strong Scottish cohort within CVC’s leadership, so we are particularly proud to be putting down roots here.

"Edinburgh combines a remarkable financial heritage with a thriving, modern professional community.

"We’re starting with a small team, but we see real potential to grow our presence here."

A spokesperson for CVC Capital Partners said: "The new office provides a modern and convenient workspace for collaboration across the CVC network and reflects CVC’s continued investment in its global platform."
