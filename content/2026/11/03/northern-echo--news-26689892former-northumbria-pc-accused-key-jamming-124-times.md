A former police officer allegedly pretended to work by repeatedly jamming his computer keys.

Former Northumbria PC Chris Browne is accused of ‘key jamming’ at least 124 times over an eight-month period, between September 2024 and May 2025.

The alleged key jamming was meant to make him look active on force computer systems.

Browne will face an accelerated misconduct hearing on Tuesday, November 3, where the allegations could amount to gross misconduct and justify his dismissal.

The public hearing will take place at Middle Engine Lane Police Station in Wallsend at 11am.

He is accused of breaching three professional standards: honesty and integrity, duties and responsibilities, and orders and instructions.
