A brand new collectables and toy store with some of the world's most famous characters has opened up in the Metrocentre.

Superpower Toys & Collectibles is now trading from The Crescent following last month's announcement, and is one of two new arrivals at the growing retail space.

The store, which has opened up beside the recently opened Be That Girl kids' spa, is the latest venture from Jonny Dent, who grew up in the late 1990s during the peak of professional wrestling’s popularity.

Superpower Toys & Collectibles opened on Halloween (October 31) and has already received glowing feedback on social media.

Visitors can browse wrestling collectables, movie memorabilia, autographed lines, and much more.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661112.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Superpower Toys *(Image: Metrocentre)*

Mr Dent said: "It’s a real ‘full circle’ moment."

"What started as a side hustle to supplement my income quickly grew into a thriving online business, followed a few years later by the addition of my first retail store in Bishop Auckland.

"All of which is testament to the incredible appetite for collectables, both online and in-store."

He and his brothers spent their childhood visiting Metrocentre to buy action figures with their pocket money.

![Superpower Toys, Metrocentre](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661104.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Metrocentre)*

He now hopes to recreate that sense of excitement for a new generation of fans and collectors.

The store caters to everyone from children seeking the latest toys to adults hoping to rediscover pieces of their youth.

Mr Dent said: "When I spotted The Crescent with its first independents, including trading card games specialist Aqua TCG, I knew I had to go for it.

"Having a store at Metrocentre was always a dream; now it’s a reality, stocking the newest and hottest releases through to ‘holy grail’ finds and everything in between."

![Superpower Toys, Metrocentre](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661107.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Metrocentre)*

Mr Dent is supported by two full-time colleagues who share his passion for the industry.

The Crescent will welcome another new business this Saturday, November 7, with the arrival of SMR Jewellers.

The shop is the vision of Gateshead entrepreneur Sharaz Manzur, who has turned his long-held interest in jewellery and precious metals into a retail venture.

Mr Manzur said: "Opening SMR Jewellers at Metrocentre is something I’ve been working towards for a long time.

"I’ve always had a passion for jewellery, watches, and precious metals, and wanted to create a business that brings all those interests together."

SMR Jewellers will stock new and pre-owned jewellery in silver, gold, and platinum, as well as luxury watches and bullion.

Customers will be able to bring in items for professional testing and valuation, including gold analysis using specialist technology.

They will then have the option to trade unwanted items towards another purchase in-store or receive payment directly into their bank account.

The shop will also offer watch battery replacements, bracelet link removals, and light jewellery repairs.

Mr Manzur said: "The aim is to offer more than just a place to buy jewellery.

"Whether someone is looking for a special gift, treating themselves to something new, or simply wanting to understand the value of jewellery they already own.

"They can feel comfortable coming through our doors, trusting that they will receive an accurate valuation, great service, and a warm welcome, every time."

With these latest additions, only three of the original 10 units at The Crescent remain available.

Ben Cox, Metrocentre asset director, said: "Achieving over 50 per cent occupancy within just months of The Crescent opening speaks volumes about the wealth of independent talent in the North East – and what happens when that talent and ambition are given the right support."
