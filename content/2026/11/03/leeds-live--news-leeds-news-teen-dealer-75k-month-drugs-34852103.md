# Teen dealer with £75k-a-month drugs phone had Canada Goose and Moncler coats

## Addy Wilby has been jailed

A Leeds dealer who kept a Canada Goose and Moncler coat at his home while he peddled Class A drugs has been jailed.

Addy Wilby, 19, was caught by police as he rode a motorcycle in Leeds on August 7 this year. Prosecutor Ciara Church told Leeds Crown Court: "He was not wearing a helmet and the rear number plate had been removed, which drew him to the attention of the police.

"He was arrested and searched and asked if he had anything on him and said, 'A bit of crack.'"

It was found Wilby, of no fixed abode, had more drugs in his pockets, including 26 packs made up of 4.99g of heroin with a street value of £360 and 4.22g of crack cocaine in 31 packs with a street value of £300.

He also had £645.65 in cash and two mobile phones.

Ms Church told the court: "The police had already been investigating a phone number and had found messages advertising cocaine and heroin. One of the phones recovered from he defendant had the IMEI number that matched that of the Ruthless Star drug line. He did not provide the PIN so the phone couldn't be downloaded but the number is attributed to this phone.

"His personal phone was seized and officers could see there was 51 shared contacts. The phone was registered to the address he held in 2014."

The court heard the Ruthless Star drugs line had a number of messages indicating drug dealing, including messages sent advertising the sale of drugs.

Ms Church added: "There were 7,731 messages sent in bulk indicating the dealer is established with a large customer base. The value of the drugs line was estimated to be a monthly amount of £75,000. This was calculated on a number of interactions on the phone, calls and texts. The drug line number was active between the fifth of January 2020 to the 31st of August 2026.

"The defendant was arrested on the seventh of August and the phone was seized. After his arrest, he was released on bail and on that date a customer said they had lost their phone and asked for the drugs line SIM to be given to them."

The court heard Wilby's home was searched and officers found over £3,000 in cash, a Canada Goose coat and a Moncler jacket.

It was said he had previous convictions on his record for offences including possession of class B drugs.

A stand-down report was completed by a member of staff at the Probation Service who told the court: "Employment is available upon release at a bed manufacturing business in Drighlington...He left school at 16 and has undertaken some employment but those roles have not been sustained for a significant period.

"There are reports that he has remained drug-free while in custody and he does not wish to return to drugs upon his release but says he still owes money in relation to a drug debt."

Mitigating, Stephen Uttley told the court Wilby has a gambling addiction and ADHD. He said: "He was 18 at the time [of the offences] and that is significant. In his letter, I think, this is where the mischief is: He has a gambling addiction and as a result has been involved in drug dealing.

"You're aware that what it tells me is that because of his addiction he has been recruited by others to run the line."

His Honour Judge Pema jailed Wilby for 37 months.
