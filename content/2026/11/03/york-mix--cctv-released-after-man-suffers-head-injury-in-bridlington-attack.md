Police want to question these people about an incident in Bridlington which left a man injured.

It’s been released by detectives investigating a serious assault on Flamborough Road in the town on Wednesday, 15 July.

A Humberside Police spokesperson said: “It is reported at around 11pm, a 54-year-old man was assaulted outside a takeaway and sustained a head injury.

#### Flamborough Road, Bridlington

“Since receiving the report, extensive enquiries have been ongoing including speaking to witnesses, and reviewing CCTV from in and around the area leading up to and following the report, which have so far been unsuccessful.”

If you can identify the people in the photograph, you can contact police on 101 quoting crime reference 26\*99023.
