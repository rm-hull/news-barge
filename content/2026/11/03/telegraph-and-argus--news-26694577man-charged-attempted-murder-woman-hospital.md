A man has been charged with attempted murder following a stabbing incident in Bradford.

Police were called to an address on Reevy Crescent, Buttershaw, at around 10.08am on Sunday.

A 40-year-old woman was taken to Leeds General Infirmary with life-threatening injuries.

## Who has been charged?

Jason Jones, 42, of Reevy Crescent, has been charged with attempted murder, two counts of harassment, three counts of theft, threats to kill, intentional strangulation, and assault.

He has also been charged with threatening a 37-year-old woman with possession of an offensive weapon and possession of a knife in a public place.

![Officers at the scene this afternoon](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656635.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Officers at the scene this afternoon *(Image: T&A)*

Jones has been remanded into custody and will appear at Bradford Crown Court on Thursday, December 3.

Police added that the woman, 40, remains in hospital in a serious condition.

## The full statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A man has been charged following reports of a stabbing in Bradford at the weekend.

"Officers were called just after 10am on Sunday (1 November) to an address on Reevy Crescent.

"Jason Jones, 42, of Reevy Crescent, Bradford, has been charged with attempted murder, two counts of harassment, three counts of theft, threats to kill, intentional strangulation and assault on a 40-year-old woman.

![The cordon in place.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656637.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cordon in place. *(Image: T&A)*

"He has also been charged with threatening a 37-year-old woman with possession of an offensive weapon and possession of a knife in a public place.

"Jones has been remanded into custody and will appear at Bradford Crown Court on Thursday 3 December.

"A woman in her 40s remains in hospital in a serious condition.

"Enquires into the incident are ongoing.

"Anyone with information which can assist the investigation is asked to contact via Chat at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat) quoting reference 13260620805."
