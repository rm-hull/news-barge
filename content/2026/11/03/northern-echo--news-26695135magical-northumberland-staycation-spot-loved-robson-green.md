It's that time of year when a break away sounds like a perfect idea - warming up by the fire, drinking hot chocolate and getting lost in a good book or board game inside a cosy cabin.

Part of a family-run farm that was turned into an adults-only glamping retreat has become one of the North East's most sought-after staycation destinations, even featuring on Robson Green's weekend escapes.

Woodland Chase is based at Old Felton Farm, Northumberland, and is just minutes off the A1 despite being at the heart of some of the North East's most stunning natural landscapes.

A short drive from Alnwick and the Northumbrian coast's amenities, the glamping retreat has proven popular through combining calm, natural scenery with modern comforts.

The Campbell family used some of their farmland to create four bespoke accommodations in the Northumberland countryside, featuring a treehouse and three cabins, one of which has a spa.

Each accommodation has been individually handcrafted and furnished with a private wood-fired hot tub and fire pit.

This allows guests to spend time outdoors while enjoying access to facilities they would find in a luxury hotel.

Visitors have described their spaces as "magical," adding that the cabins provide a serene holiday experience away from the stress of everyday life.

One online review wrote: " Worth every penny and I cannot recommend enough.

"I didn't want to leave my little bubble and come home to reality; I will definitely be back!"

The hot tub and fire pit provide space for guests to sit outside, while the sauna offers another way to use the private spa area. Visitors are supplied with wood and kindling during their stay.

The Fieldhouse & Spa is the newest accommodation at Woodland Chase, and has been described as a "sanctuary for mind and soul" by visitors.

The cabin sits beside stunning woodland and overlooks a hayfield, while the spa has its own wood-heated sauna, outdoor shower and cold-dunk bucket made from recycled farm materials.

The rustic vibes extend to the cabins, where there is no TV, although there is Wi-Fi.

The site’s other accommodation includes the Treehouse in the woodland next to the Campbell family farm, horse-themed Saddler’s Cabin, and Crofter’s Cabin, which reflects the family’s farming background.

Crofter’s Cabin is also the only dog-friendly accommodation.

The Northumbria Mysteries and Weekend Escape star Robson Green was spotted at the Treehouse in summer of 2023, later featuring his visit to the Northumberland retreat on the second series (episode 14) of the popular BBC show.

Woodland Chase is adults-only, making it perfect for couples and travellers seeking a child-free countryside break.

One online review said Woodland Chase was "possibly one of the best places I’ve ever had the pleasure of staying in".

They added: "Everything about this place is amazing and my partner and I never ventured out so we could soak up every minute!

"Couldn’t recommend more of a magical UK break for two."

Woodland Chase recently announced their glamping getaways would be taking a short break from 9 November to 1 February 2027, but bookings remain open for after they return next year.

The retreats are among a wider explosion in the North East staycation market, with venues across the region offering a base for visitors who want to explore the county's national parks, walking trails, shops and attractions, or those who wish to stay and relax within the farm’s grounds.

Its rural setting means guests have access to some of the North East's most beautiful scenery while spotting local wildlife like herons.
