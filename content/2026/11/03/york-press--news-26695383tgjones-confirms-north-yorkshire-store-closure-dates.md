Major retailer TGJones has confirmed to the Press the closure dates of branches in North Yorkshire.

The company is closing stores across the country as part of its court-approved Restructuring Plan.

The Malton store is expected to close on January 9 2027.

The Ripon and Scarborough stores are expected to close on January 23 2027.

The impending closures, of which around 150 are expected nationally, follow the closure of the York branch in Coney Street in mid-October.

Branches in Driffield and Beverley closed in August and September respectively.

The Hull branch closed in July and the Bradford branch closed on October 11.

Other branches, such as Leeds and Harrogate, have to date escaped the axe.

The company’s website will also continue.

A spokesperson for TGJones told the Press: “As we work to secure the long-term future of TGJones, we have confirmed that our Malton, Ripon and Scarborough shops will close.

“We know this will be disappointing news for our colleagues, customers and local communities.

“Our focus now is on supporting those affected and continuing to serve customers throughout the closure period.

“We would like to thank all of our colleagues for their commitment and the contribution they have made to TGJones."
