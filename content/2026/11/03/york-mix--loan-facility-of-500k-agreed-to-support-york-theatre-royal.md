York Theatre Royal will be able to access a loan facility of up to £500K to support its work in the city.

The theatre will be able to borrow the money to “develop new work and enable investment to produce an ambitious programme of theatre and deliver long-term benefits to the city”.

It comes following an agreement with City of York Council today (Tuesday).

Executive member for the economy and culture Cllr Pete Kilbane said: “A loan facility has been an available option for some time and does not represent a new funding commitment to the theatre, nor will it incur any costs to the council.”

Chief executive of York Theatre Royal Paul Crewes said: “We are thankful to City of York Council for their strong support of our ambitious but essential work.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/9-The-Penelopiad_Pamela-Raith-Photography.webp)

“In recent years the theatre has progressed to an ambitious in-house producing model, attracting talent and audiences from across the globe.

“We are also proud to be curating and presenting a dance programme of the highest quality, working with world class artists and companies.

“This producing and presenting model is developing the city’s reputation locally, nationally and internationally.

“The theatre also delivers an extensive programme of community engagement projects and events, working in partnership with a wide range of other organisations and charities across the city and region.

“The loan facility will support us in delivering all of this ambitious activity and continuing to offer a world-class cultural offer for residents and visitors to the city.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Paul-Crewes-Credit-Charlie-Kirkpatrick.webp)

Cllr Kilbane added: “York Theatre Royal is an important cultural asset for our city. It enhances the quality of residents’ lives and supports local businesses and employment.

“It also delivers a wide range of activities with people living in York, with schools, charities and other organisations, and it attracts significant numbers of visitors too.

“The council has a really important role to play in supporting the vibrancy and economy of the whole city and we recognise the significant contribution the theatre makes.”

York Theatre Royal has been entertaining the residents of York for more than 280 years.

It says for every £1 spent by the theatre, it returns £2.68 in quantified economic and social benefit to the city and region.

A spokesperson said: “The theatre generates £6.25 million of additional economic activity in the regional economy as well as an additional £3.4 million of health and wellbeing benefits.”
