The West Park Hotel in Harrogate is transforming its courtyard for the festive season.

‘Winter in the Courtyard’ will open on Saturday 14th November, offering Alpine-inspired food, winter drinks and live DJ sessions in a covered and heated outdoor space.

The launch will feature entertainment from Funk My Life from 2pm, with further DJ sessions scheduled for 28th November and 12th December.

The seasonal menu will include currywurst, Flammkuchen, homemade pretzels, tartiflette and Alpine fries.

Blankets will also be available, while Harrogate-based decorative lighting specialists Lights4fun are working with the hotel to create the festive setting.

Further music and seasonal events are planned throughout the festive period.

*Winter in the Courtyard will open Thursday to Sunday during November, before opening daily in December until 9pm.*
