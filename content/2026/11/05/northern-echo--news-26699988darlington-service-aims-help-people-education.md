PEOPLE in Darlington are being encouraged to take the first steps towards achieving their goals.

This is in celebration of Get the Nation Learning Week, which runs from November 2 to 6.

Darlington Borough Council is urging residents to join the hundreds of learners already rediscovering their potential with Learning & Skills.

The service provides a range of free online and face-to-face learning opportunities, from hobby and interest-based classes to essential skills and professional development.

Last year, almost 1,400 learners were supported across more than 200 programmes, with 94 percent rating the provision as good or excellent.

Seventy percent chose to continue their education journey with Learning & Skills Darlington.

Now operating from a new town centre base in the old Northern Echo building, the service aims to attract more learners, particularly those on lower incomes who may have missed out on educational opportunities earlier in life.

Learners can gain new skills and qualifications to help them find work, progress in their careers, or change profession.

Several new courses have been introduced this year and are now open for enrolment, including a work readiness programme and six new pathways to employment courses.

New level 2 apprenticeship training has also been introduced for beverage team members, administrator assistants, and fitted interior installers.

Councillor Chris McEwan, cabinet member for economy, said: "Learning & Skills is much more than an education provider.

"It helps residents gain the skills, qualifications, and confidence they need to access better opportunities, supports employers to build a skilled workforce, and plays a vital role in our local economy.

"Just as importantly, it brings people together and creates a space where everyone can thrive."

People can find out more about the courses available by visiting darlington.gov.uk/learningandskills or calling 01325 405601.
