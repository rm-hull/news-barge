## Foreign investment remains concentrated in relatively few countries, while others struggle to attract funding. At the World Investment Forum in Doha, governments and businesses discussed how more economies could benefit and turn investment into jobs.

The World Investment Forum brought governments, investors and business leaders together in Doha to discuss how countries can attract investment and create jobs. Organised by UN Trade and Development, the event examined why investment reaches some countries more than others and what governments can do to improve their chances.

Discussions covered practical needs, from ports and reliable infrastructure to skilled workers and clear rules. Speakers also addressed the difficulties businesses face when expanding, including rising costs and disrupted supply chains. For smaller countries, working with their neighbours could offer investors access to a larger market.

The forum also examined how public institutions could encourage investment in countries considered riskier. Options included guarantees to cover some potential losses and funding projects alongside other partners. Beyond attracting money, speakers highlighted the importance of creating jobs, supporting local businesses and measuring whether investments improve people’s lives.
