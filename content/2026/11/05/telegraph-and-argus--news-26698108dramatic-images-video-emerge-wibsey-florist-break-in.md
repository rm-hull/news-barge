DRAMATIC images and video have emerged after a Bradford flower shop was broken into last weekend.

The burglary happened in the early hours of Monday, November 2, at Scentiments on St Enoch’s Road, Wibsey.

CCTV footage shared with the Telegraph & Argus shows the suspect approaching the shop, smashing the glass with a large stone and going inside.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669354.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police car parked outside Scentiments flower shop after a break-in *(Image: UGC)*

The owner, who took over the business just a month ago, said: "It’s not been the greatest start to the week.

"We’re only a flower shop, but lo and behold someone has broken in.

"Unfortunately, it’s the world we live in.

"In the grand scheme of things, he caused more damage to me than what he took from the till."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669358.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Broken glass on the ground in the wake of a burglary at Scentiments *(Image: UGC)*

The intruder is believed to have used a knife to force open the till after smashing the glass with a nearby stone.

The owner handed the CCTV footage to police.

He said: "I passed on CCTV footage of the person who broke in."

West Yorkshire Police confirmed the burglary and said the investigation was under way.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669366.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The door at Scentiments which initially had to be boarded up following a break-in *(Image: UGC)*

A police spokesperson said: "Police are investigating a burglary at a business on St Enoch’s Road, Bradford, reported just after midnight on Monday, November 2.

"Enquiries are ongoing.

"Anyone with information is asked to contact police via Live Chat at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat, quoting reference 13260621894."

Shattered glass was left inside and outside the shop, and the front window had to be boarded up before repairs.

The owner said: "It’s unfair but I’m going to have to take it on the chin and make sure it doesn’t happen again.

"I just want to get business back to normal.

"I took over the shop about a month ago.

"The previous owner, who had the shop for 26 years, never had an issue."

Despite the setback, the owner remains determined to move forward and continue serving the community.

Police are urging anyone who saw anything suspicious near the shop at the time to come forward.

Police took fingerprints from the shop as part of the investigation, while the owner is considering additional security measures.
