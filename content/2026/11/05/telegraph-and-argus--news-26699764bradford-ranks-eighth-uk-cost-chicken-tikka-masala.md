BRADFORD is one of the priciest places in the UK to order a takeaway chicken tikka masala.

New research from Buzz Bingo, released to mark Diwali, compared curry prices across 50 of the UK’s most populated areas, using a popular delivery app to calculate the average cost of the dish from 4-star and above takeaways.

Bradford ranked as the eighth most expensive city overall, with an average price of £12.41—well above the national average of £11.28.

Mark Fletcher, head of brand at Buzz Bingo, said: "We all know chicken tikka masala is a national favourite dish.

"Those living in cities like Stoke-on-Trent, Sunderland, Hull, Bristol and Newcastle are paying less than the national average, while residents in places like Southampton and Belfast are paying almost £2 more.

"Here at Buzz Bingo, a curry is one of our popular menu items, so we certainly see how much Brits love a good curry."

Hull was the cheapest city in Yorkshire at £9.77, followed by Huddersfield at £10.04.

York (£11.12), Sheffield (£11.13), and Leeds (£11.84) all hovered close to the national average, but Bradford was the priciest in the region.
