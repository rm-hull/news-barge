A police vehicle has been set on fire amid Bonfire Night disorder in Glasgow.

Officers were called to a report of an injured man on Greenside Street in the Provanmill area of the city at around 7.55pm on Thursday, November 5.

The Scottish Ambulance Service also attended the scene.

Police said that, while in attendance, officers and ambulance crews were targeted with fireworks, resulting in a police vehicle being set alight and seriously damaged.

One of the ambulance staff also sustained a minor injury.

Operation Amberlight specialist resources were deployed, and one man was arrested.

Police said enquiries are ongoing into the incident, with officers maintaining a presence in the area.

Fireworks have also been launched at police amid disorder in the south of Edinburgh. In Oxgangs and around Colinton Mains, bins were knocked over and set on fire in the road.

As part of its Bonfire Night policing operation, Police Scotland called in assistance from forces in England and Wales, as it mounted the biggest deployment of officers since the visit of US President Donald Trump last year.
