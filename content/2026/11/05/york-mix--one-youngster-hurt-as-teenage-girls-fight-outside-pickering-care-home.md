One girl was hurt in a fight which broke out near a Pickering care home.

It happened at around 3pm on the A169 Whitby Road and involved a group of teenage girls fighting.

The victim sustained minor injuries as a result of the incident.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re particularly appealing for information about the member of the public that intervened and the two other members of the public who assisted the victim back to her home address, as they may be able to assist with our investigation.”
