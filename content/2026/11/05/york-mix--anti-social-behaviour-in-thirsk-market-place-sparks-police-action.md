Police are taking action to curb trouble in Thirsk Market Place.

They say they have identified some of the culprits and they could face arrest.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We have received reports of anti-social behaviour in and around Thirsk Market Place, and – working alongside our partners – we’ve identified certain young people that we are in the process of speaking with due to their behaviour.

“Anti-social behaviour may seem harmless to some, but it can have a significant impact on local residents, businesses, and visitors.

“We won’t tolerate people intimidating others, causing criminal damage, drinking underage, littering, riding bikes irresponsibly, or causing alarm, harassment, or distress.”

Police say misbehaving youngsters could face several consequences, including:

* Being stopped and spoken to by police officers.
* Parents or guardians being contacted.
* Receiving formal warnings and intervention measures.
* Arrest and prosecution where offences have been committed.
* A criminal record, which may impact future employment, education, travel, and career opportunities.

The force spokesperson added: “We would encourage parents to speak with their children about their behaviour when out with friends, and remind them to be respectful of the community and those who live and work in the town.”
