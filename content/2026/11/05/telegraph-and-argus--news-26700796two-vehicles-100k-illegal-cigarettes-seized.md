More than 100,000 illicit cigarettes were seized in a crackdown on illegal goods in Bradford.

The enforcement operation was carried out by West Yorkshire Trading Standards, West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, and the police in Bradford on October 29.

Alongside 113,000 illicit cigarettes, 7.3kg of illicit tobacco and illegal vapes were also seized, with an estimated retail value of more than £104,000.

Police also seized two vehicles.

Businesses were advised on firework safety and reminded of the legal requirements for selling age-restricted products.

A West Yorkshire Trading Standards spokesperson said: "This proactive enforcement activity highlights the continued commitment to tackling the sale of illegal and unsafe goods, ensuring community safety, and supporting legitimate businesses."

Consumers with information about illegal trading practices, such as selling unsafe fireworks or age-restricted goods to children, can contact the Citizens Advice Consumer Helpline on 0808 223 1133, and can remain anonymous.
