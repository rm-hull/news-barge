Glasgow's Subway will not operate on two Sundays this month as operator SPT undertakes the next phase of testing for its new signalling, communications and operational systems.

SPT said the planned closures on Sunday November 15, and Sunday November 22 "will allow engineers and operational teams to undertake a programme of comprehensive testing across the network. ensuring the systems and infrastructure delivered through Subway modernisation continue to operate safely and reliably".

The testing programme will also support the continued rollout of Platform Screen Doors (PSDs), which have already been installed at a number of stations.

Glasgow Subway passengers are also being warned that planned closures of the M8 motorway will be in place until 1pm on both Sunday November 15, and Sunday November 22, which may lead to increased traffic and delays on surrounding roads.

SPT Director of Transport Operations Richard Robinson said: “The work taking place during these Sunday closures will allow us to carry out critical testing and validation of key systems that support the operation of the modernised Subway.

"Over recent years, substantial work has taken place behind the scenes to upgrade key infrastructure across the network. We have now reached the stage where we need to carry out extensive testing to ensure the new signalling and communications systems operate safely, reliably and as intended.

"As we near the end of the modernisation programme, this work will allow us to replicate a wide range of operational scenarios and give our teams confidence that the systems and infrastructure supporting the Subway are performing safely and reliably for our customers.

“We appreciate the patience of our passengers while this work takes place. Passenger safety and the smooth operation of the Subway remain our top priorities, and these closures will help ensure both as we complete this important stage of testing."
