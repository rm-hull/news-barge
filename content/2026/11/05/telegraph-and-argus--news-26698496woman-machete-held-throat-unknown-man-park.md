A woman was "threatened with a machete by an unknown man" in a popular Bradford park.

West Yorkshire Police is investigating reports of an attempted robbery at Bowling Park which happened at around 6.18pm on Tuesday, October 27.

A post on a community Facebook group detailed the traumatic incident.

## Dog walker has 'machete held to her throat'

One individual said her sister was walking her dog towards the exit of the park when a man came from behind her and held a machete to her throat demanding her phone.

They added: "She refused to give him it, so he became aggressive towards her, pulling her hair and grabbing her by her arms.

"He then demanded she give him the dog. She also refused.

"At this point, she used the emergency button on her phone to call police.

"She told him instead she would go to get some money for him, so began walking closer to the exit.

"She began running, and luckily the police arrived within minutes.

"They briefly looked in the area, but could not find anyone.

"Please, do not enter Bowling Park alone, this could have ended very differently."

## Police statement

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called to reports of an attempted robbery at Bowling Park, Bradford, at around 6.18pm on Tuesday, October 27.

"A woman was threatened with a machete by an unknown man and officers conducted searches in the area.

"Enquires remain ongoing into the incident. Anyone with information regarding the incident is asked to contact police via [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 13260611731."
