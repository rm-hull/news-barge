North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a fraud incident in Ripon.

The incident happened on the afternoon of Tuesday, September 8 when fraudulent notes were used to purchase an item.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We believe the person in the image will have information that could help our investigation.”

If you can help, you should email [Stacie-Lea.Franks@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Stacie-Lea.Franks@northyorkshire.police.uk)

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260187676 when providing any details regarding the incident.
