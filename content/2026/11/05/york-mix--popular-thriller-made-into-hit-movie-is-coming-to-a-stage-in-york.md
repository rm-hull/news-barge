Popular psychological thriller *The Housemaid* is being adapted for the stage – and it’s coming to York next year.

Based on Freida McFadden’s best-selling novel, *The Housemaid* will make its stage debut in a gripping new production.

It is adapted by acclaimed stage and screen writer Bekah Brunstetter (*The Notebook*), and directed by Matthew Dunster (* 2:22 A Ghost Story; The Hunger Games: On Stage*).

Every house has its secrets. The question is: who holds the keys?

*The Housemaid*will be at the Grand Opera House York from**Tuesday 23 to Saturday 27 November 2027**.

In collaboration with Lionsgate, production company Melting Pot, led by Simon Friend and Hanna Osmolska (*Life of Pi, Paranormal Activity)*, have announced the major new stage adaptation of* The Housemaid*, based on the Lionsgate film of the same name and Freida McFadden’s literary phenomenon.

Lionsgate’s big-screen release of *The Housemaid*was a box office hit, grossing over $400 million worldwide. The live stage production marks the latest step in the studio’s continued expansion of* The Housemaid*franchise, as the sequel* The Housemaid’s Secret* is in production and is set for a global theatrical release on 17 December 2027.

“With the stage play debuting next year and our sequel film in production, the momentum behind *The Housemaid* franchise continues to grow,” said Jenefer Brown, Lionsgate’s president, global products & experiences.

“We’re thrilled to work with Simon, Hanna, Bekah and Matthew to bring this story to the stage in an entirely new way, giving audiences the chance to experience its suspense, twists and turns live.”

Producer, Simon Friend of Melting Pot continues: “Bekah’s adaptation of The Housemaid is exactly what we’d hoped for – a fresh and uniquely theatrical take on this wildly popular story, and made with Matthew Dunster’s vision, it will certainly bring a thrilling new dimension to the franchise.”

Casting details for *The Housemaid* are yet to be announced.

Tickets will go on general sale Friday 6 November at 10am here.
