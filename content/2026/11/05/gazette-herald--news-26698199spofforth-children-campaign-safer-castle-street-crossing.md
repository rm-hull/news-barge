Children carrying handmade posters have joined a demonstration calling for a pelican crossing.

The gathering took place outside Spofforth Village Hall on the morning of Wednesday, November 4, to highlight concerns about heavy traffic in Castle Street and the safety of children walking to school.

Plans are already in place to introduce a 20mph speed limit through the village, but campaigners say a pedestrian crossing is also needed.

Andrew Paraskos, North Yorkshire councillor for Spofforth, Lower Wharfedale and Tockwith, said: "We’ve got children from the Spofforth village school here to raise awareness about a crossing on Castle Street because we get a heck of a lot of traffic down here.

"We’re in the process of a 20mph zone, but we need a crossing on this road."

Councillor Paraskos described Castle Street as a "rat run between Wetherby and Harrogate," noting that Castle Street is currently affected by road closures for utility works and that "the road is currently closed and we’re still seeing a steady stream of vehicles coming through".

Spofforth parish councillor Gill Hewitt, who organised the demonstration, said: "The amount of traffic going through the village is horrendous and children feel unsafe walking to school."
