Shoe shoppers in Selby will have more choice as a relaunched store reopens this weekend.

shoezone is reopening its Selby branch at 16 Market Place on Saturday, November 7, with a wider range of footwear for men, women, and children.

The popular store had been closed for around ten days for the revamp.

Anthony Smith, chief executive of shoezone, said: "It is exciting to be reopening our store in Selby, which will see the launch of new brands for customers to enjoy.

"We hope that our customers like the new variety of products and we look forward to welcoming them back into the store."

The refreshed shop will feature shoezone’s own lines as well as popular brands including Relife, Skechers, and Rieker.

Customers can take advantage of special opening offers, including savings on a selection of styles and other seasonal offers.

Store hours will be 9am to 5.30pm Monday to Saturday, and 10.30am to 4pm on Sunday.
