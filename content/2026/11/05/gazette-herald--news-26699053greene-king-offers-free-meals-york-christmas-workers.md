Christmas cheer is coming early for shift workers who keep the festive season running behind the scenes.

Greene King pubs will offer 30,000 free festive meals nationwide from December 2 to 5 as part of its The Big Christmas Shift campaign, aimed at recognising those who work through Christmas Day to keep essential services running.

The gesture comes as new research highlights the personal sacrifices made by workers in York and across the UK.

A survey commissioned by Greene King found that 60 per cent of Christmas Day workers in York feel people underestimate how many workers keep essential services running on December 25.

The research also revealed that 40 per cent of York respondents have moved Christmas dinner to another day because of work.

Andrew Gallagher, brand and marketing director at Greene King pubs, said: "The magic of Christmas doesn’t just happen.

"Behind so many special moments that the rest of us enjoy, there are people giving up time with their loved ones to make Christmas special for somebody else.

"So, this year, we are giving Santa a little less of the credit and putting the spotlight on Britain’s real Christmas shift workers."

Eligible workers can register or be nominated at [https://www.greeneking.co.uk/christmas/big-christmas-shift](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/christmas/big-christmas-shift).
