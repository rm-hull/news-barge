A shoe company hopes to base a vehicle and trailer at a farm in Malton.

Stuart Marsh Shoes Ltd is applying for a licence to use High Mowthorpe Farm as the operating centre.

The application is for one goods vehicle and one trailer.

The company is based at Hennock Court, Marsh Barton Trading Estate in Exeter.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal at publicnoticeportal.uk/wilts-gazette-and-herald**

Owners or occupiers of land near the proposed operating centre can write to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Leeds.

They must also send a copy to the applicant.

The deadline for feedback is November 25, 2026.
