Here are five Bonfire Night displays taking place across the Bradford district this evening.

Organisers are urging visitors to arrive early, dress warmly and buy tickets in advance to avoid queues.

## Bradford Fireworks Extravaganza at Peel Park

The Bradford Fireworks Extravaganza at Peel Park is one of the district's biggest displays, with gates opening at 4pm.

Visitors can enjoy funfair rides, food stalls and entertainment before the fireworks.

Advance tickets cost around £8, with higher prices at the gate.

Thousands attend each year, making it one of the district's busiest displays.

## Great Horton Park Chapel Cricket Club

Great Horton Park Chapel Cricket Club is hosting its annual bonfire and fireworks display.

The bonfire will be lit at 6.45pm, followed by fireworks at 7.45pm.

Advance tickets cost around £6 for adults and £4 for children.

Family tickets are also available.

Food, drinks and a barbecue will be available throughout the evening.

## Clayton Cricket Club

Clayton Cricket Club is hosting its annual fireworks party.

The event will feature fireworks, refreshments and family entertainment.

Tickets cost £5 to £10, depending on age and whether they are bought in advance.

## The Denholme Junior Football Club

Denholme Junior FC will host its bonfire and fireworks display tonight.

Gates open at 5.30pm, the bonfire is lit at 6.30pm and fireworks start at 7.15pm.

Advance tickets cost £5 for adults and £3 for children, with family tickets also available.

Alongside the fireworks, visitors can enjoy food and refreshments throughout the evening. The annual display is a popular fixture in the local calendar and attracts residents from Denholme, Cullingworth and surrounding villages.

## Legrams Lane

A bonfire and fireworks display will take place at 169 Legrams Lane tonight.

Visitors should check social media for final timings before travelling.

More displays are planned this weekend at Woodlands Cricket Club, Bradford Moor Golf Club and Buttershaw St Paul's Cricket Club.

Each of these events promises a night of family-friendly fun and celebration, lighting up the Bradford skies in a dazzling display of colour and spectacle.
