# Watch: Met Police release Brand interview footage

Comedian and actor Russell Brand has been found guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.

Brand has been remanded in custody and will be sentenced on Monday.

Video released by the Metropolitan Police shows Brand being interviewed by officers in 2023, 2024 and 2025.
