Two roads in the Youlthorpe and Gowthorpe areas are due to close for resurfacing works in December.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council said Kirklands Lane, Youlthorpe and Gowthorpe, and Gowthorpe Lane, Gowthorpe, will be closed to through traffic from 7.30am on December 1 until 4.30pm on December 2.

The closure is planned to allow civil and highway resurfacing work. It will apply along the entire length of both roads.

The council said the order is being made under Section 14(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

The measure is intended to help the work take place while keeping the public safe.

Although the order will remain valid for 18 months, the work is expected to finish within two days.

Access will remain available for residents, emergency vehicles and pedestrians during the closure.

Other through traffic must use the signed diversion via Hatkill Lane, A166 Bridlington Road, A166 Clay Hill and The Balk.

The route passes through Full Sutton, Bishop Wilton and Skirpenbeck civil parishes, within the Wolds Weighton ward.

Drivers can contact the council’s Highways Customer Care team on 01482 393939 for information, quoting reference CB.
