Lynnette Sellers, acting general manager at Boroughbridge Manor, has won a regional award recognising her contribution to care, and will now progress to the national judging round.

Lynnette was named Deputy Manager of the Year for Barchester Healthcare’s North East Division in the organisation’s 2026 Care Awards.

The awards recognise staff who go above and beyond in supporting residents across Barchester’s 267 care homes and private hospitals throughout the UK.

More than 5,000 nominations were received from homes and hospitals across the country, with Lynnette beating hundreds of other nominees to secure the regional title.

The awards cover a range of categories, including Registered Nurse of the Year, Carer of the Year, Dementia Care Champion and Activities Coordinator of the Year.

> **Lynnette Sellers said:**
>
> A huge thank you to my lovely team and residents for their support.

The achievement was celebrated at Boroughbridge Manor, where colleagues and residents organised a party and joined the awards ceremony via livestream.

> **Lynnette Sellers, General Manager, said:**
>
> This is an amazing achievement and the whole home supported me by throwing a party and joining us on live stream at the awards ceremony.

Boroughbridge Manor is operated by Barchester Healthcare and provides residential care, dementia care and respite care.

Barchester Healthcare supports more than 13,500 residents across its 267 care homes and hospitals throughout the UK.
