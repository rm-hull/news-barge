# Katie Price defends Felix Henderson photos as Lee Andrews unfollows her

## Katie Price's estranged husband Lee Andrews has unfollowed her on social media just hours after she defended her photos with OhChat founder Felix Henderson

Katie Price's estranged husband, Lee Andrews, has now unfollowed her on social media, just hours after she delivered a sharp rebuttal to photographs showing her getting close to an AI company executive.

The mum-of-five former Jordan glamour model, who was recently photographed still wearing her wedding ring earlier this week, seemed to be moving forward from Lee when she was pictured snuggling up to OhChat founder Felix Henderson at an awards ceremony.

Felix established the platform where Katie has a 'digital twin' that allows fans to interact with her, though the 48-year-old has maintained he is merely a "work colleague". However, Lee seems less than pleased with this turn of events and has once again unfollowed Katie. He's also removed all traces of the former glamour model from his profile, including their photographs together.

In their place, Lee now displays a collection of AI-manipulated photos at the top of his social media page, reports the Mirror. On Facebook, though, he continues to state that he is "married to the beautiful Katie Price".

This development arrives just weeks after Katie, 48, and her fourth husband, Lee, were reportedly attempting to rekindle their relationship. The pair had separated following his second imprisonment in Dubai within a few months. Katie addressed the images of herself with Felix via her Instagram stories, stating: "Just to clarify, Felix is a work colleague who was representing OhChat at the Glamour Awards.

"Having attended the awards for the last few years, it was a great opportunity for us to finally meet in person following my professional partnership with Oh Chat to create the UK's first digital twin. Our meeting was entirely work-related, and there is absolutely nothing more to it than that." The Sun had reported that Katie was seen "lapping up the attention" from Felix.

Just days prior, Lee had strongly hinted at a reconciliation with Katie, referring to her as his "soulmate" — a post which Katie subsequently reshared on her own Instagram Story. The couple, whose relationship has been under scrutiny ever since they wed just days after meeting, have been estranged since the summer. Katie had previously declared the relationship over, citing mounting frustration over his dishonesty.

Reports emerged that she ended the marriage via WhatsApp upon discovering he had a mere £2.22 in his bank account, despite Lee's earlier claims of having millions stored in cryptocurrency.

Nevertheless, Katie has recently stoked rumours of a potential reconciliation. During Halloween celebrations, she was spotted proudly displaying her wedding ring, its sizeable diamond catching the light as she partied in a barely-there bridal-inspired outfit.

Wearing a bloody barbed wire choker to complete her ensemble, she flashed her wedding ring in Manchester while seated in the front of a car. Previously, both Katie and Lee removed all evidence of each other from their social media accounts, with Katie subsequently making an appearance on Good Morning Britain to provide an update on their marriage.
