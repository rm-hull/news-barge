## Person hit by train south of York - major disruption expected

A person has been hit by a train this morning *(Image: British Transport Police)*

A PERSON has been hit by a train south of York.

The East Coast Mainline between Newark North Gate and Grantham is currently blocked after a person was hit by a train.

The incident was reported at 7.08am today (November 5) and will affect all trains using the country’s major train route including northbound and southbound trains through York.

“The emergency services are dealing with an incident between Newark Northgate and Grantham. Whilst they complete their work, all lines between these stations will be closed.

“As a result, trains running between Doncaster and Grantham may be cancelled, delayed by up to 60 minutes, or revised,” said a spokesperson for National Rail.

The lines have recently reopened, however disruption is expected until midday.
