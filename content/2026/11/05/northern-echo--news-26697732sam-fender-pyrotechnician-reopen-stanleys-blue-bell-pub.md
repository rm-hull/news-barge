After working on major tours with artists including Sam Fender and Take That, a County Durham man is swapping life on the road for life behind the bar as he prepares to reopen a much-loved pub.

Keiran Frame has taken over The Blue Bell in Stanley and has plans to restore it as a traditional community pub.

Keiran has worked in hospitality for years while travelling the country as a pyrotechnician for some of music’s biggest names.

![New owner of The Blue Bell pub, Stanley Keiran Frame Picture: SARAH CALDECOTT](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668926.jpg?type=mds-article-620)New owner of The Blue Bell pub, Keiran Frame. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The 27-year-old said: "I've always been in hospitality.

"I still do work in the industry but I'm normally a pyrotechnician.

"I've toured with Sam Fender and I've just finished a tour with Take That.

"I've got a little one and I wanted to be a little closer to home."

The dad already runs Bellamy's in Consett with a business partner, but The Blue Bell will be his first pub venture alone.

The pub is especially close to his heart as he grew up visiting it and wants to restore it to its former glory.

He said: "I used to go to The Blue Bell when I was young and I just want to try and bring it back to life.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668911.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668908.jpg?type=mds-article-620)*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"I'm local, I understand the area, I understand the people that are around.

"For me, I just want to get it back to its former glory.

"It's exciting times bringing something back to the community.

"We want to be affordable for people and bring the basics back like mince and dumplings.

"We don't want to make it too complicated because people just want a pint and a nice warm meal."

Another element of the pub that Keiran is keen to preserve are the mouse carvings dotted around the building, reminiscent of the famous Mouseman craftsmanship.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668933.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

He said: "It's things like this that make The Blue Bell so special, and we're keen to preserve as much of its original charm as possible."

Keiran announced the plans in a Facebook post, setting out his vision for the pub’s future.

He wrote: "The Blue Bell, Kip Hill – Reopening Soon!

"We've got one simple aim which is to bring The Blue Bell back to the proper traditional pub it once was.

"A good pint, familiar faces, a bit of banter and somewhere everyone feels welcome.

"We know how much this pub has meant to people over the years and we really want to do it justice.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668910.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668928.jpg?type=mds-article-620)*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668923.jpg?type=mds-article-620)*(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"There's plenty of hard work ahead of us but we're excited to get stuck in and hopefully bring back a pub the community can be proud of.

"We'd love to hear your memories of The Blue Bell from back in the day. What would you love to see return?

"Keep an eye on the page for updates as we get closer to reopening."

Hundreds of local residents have welcomed the announcement on social media.

The dad said: "It's been ridiculous. On Facebook we've already got a thousand followers.

"There's been so many people messaging us which we didn't expect, but obviously it's something that people want back."

Keiran has not confirmed a reopening date but hopes to welcome customers in the coming weeks.

He is also moving from Consett to Stanley to be closer to the pub and the community he hopes to serve.

The pub previously reopened as a Turkish restaurant in August 2023 after a five-week renovation, following its closure in 2020.
