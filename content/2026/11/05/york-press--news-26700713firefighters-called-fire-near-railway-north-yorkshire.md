FIREFIGHTERS were called to a fire close to the railway in a North Yorkshire village.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service says its crew from Selby were called to Cridling Stubbs at 5.13pm yesterday (November 4) after a fire by a railway embankment.

“Crews used knapsack sprayers and hose reel jet to extinguish,” said a service spokesperson.
