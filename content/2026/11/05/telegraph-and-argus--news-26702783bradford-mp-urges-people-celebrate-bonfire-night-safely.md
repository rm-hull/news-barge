A BRADFORD MP has called for people to "celebrate safely" following official advice issued ahead of Bonfire Night.

Imran Hussain, MP for Bradford East, has urged the public to act responsibly and consider their neighbours during the celebrations, which are often marked by fireworks and bonfires.

Mr Hussain said: "On Bonfire Night, I urge everyone in Bradford to celebrate safely and respect their neighbours.

"I've long campaigned for tougher firework laws to tackle antisocial behaviour, noise and distress.

"Please attend organised displays where possible and help keep our communities safe."

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service has also issued safety guidance.

In a video posted to social media, Dale Gardiner, district commander for Kirklees, said: "Bonfires can easily get out of control.

"Our advice is leave it to the professionals and attend an organised event.

"Bonfire Night shouldn't end in an emergency."

He reminded the public never to leave bonfires unattended, not to build them close to buildings, and to avoid using flammable liquids.

The service also advised that fireworks should only be purchased from registered sellers and must be CE marked.

People are reminded to stand well back from fireworks, keep children and pets away, and store fireworks and sparklers safely.

Gloves should always be worn when handling sparklers.
