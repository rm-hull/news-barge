A Yorkshire couple's new restaurant - dedicated to the classic British pie - is enjoying “unprecedented” demand just weeks after opening.

Pickering Pie Emporium, opened by Angela and Richard Saunders just weeks ago, has already served its 1,100th pie from a former bank on the Market Place in Pickering, North Yorkshire.

The husband-and-wife duo, originally from Leeds and now living in Ryedale, called the response “absolutely fantastic”.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21664386.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Richard and Angela Saunders, owners of Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

Mrs Saunders said: "Richard has had this idea for six years.

"He was ready for a change.

"We saw the property come up for let.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21664404.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The outside of Pickering Pie Emporium, which is in a former bank building *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

"We thought we would grab the bull by the horns and fulfil this dream we have had.

"It’s been very popular.

"In the first three weeks the Pickering Pie Emporium has been open, business has been unprecedented, absolutely fantastic.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21664430.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Food on offer at Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

"We served our 1,100th pie.

"I did not think we expected it would be as popular as it has been.

“We sold 90 pie meals in one day.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21664412.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The inside of Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

“We are really busy; people love it.”

She believes the restaurant’s early success is down to its welcoming atmosphere and focus on customer experience.

Mrs Saunders, from Crossgates, and her husband, from Adel, told the Telegraph & Argus: “We wanted to bring a warm welcome and a good cuddle to Pickering in the way of comfort food and a relaxed and cosy meeting space.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21664423.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The inside of Pickering Pie Emporium *(Image: Pickering Pie Emporium)*

"I think our key to success so far is making sure every customer leaves happier than when they arrived.

"We treasure them all and treat them as family."

The menu features five homemade pies, three gluten-free options and a rotating selection from six UK suppliers.

The couple aim to source the menu locally where possible while showcasing award-winning and unusual pies from across the UK.

Mrs Saunders said: “We try and keep it as local as we can.”

Customer demand has already shaped the menu, with a pork and cider pie added to the main list.

"It’s divine," she said. "It’s a real comfort food."

Mrs Saunders credits their Yorkshire upbringing and family dining experiences as the inspiration behind the business.

She said: “Our inspiration has come from the places we visited as a family, such as Jinnah restaurant in Seacroft, the Farrier in Cayton and our wonderful friend Tanya’s old place in Malton — number 46 — where she made us feel like we were dining in her home.

"I personally aspire to be like her. What a woman.

“We’ve eaten out a lot as a family. The businesses we return to are based on service for the most part. We revisit spots where we feel valued.”
