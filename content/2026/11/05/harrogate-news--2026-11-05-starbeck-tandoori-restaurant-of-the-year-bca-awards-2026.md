Starbeck Tandoori in Harrogate has been named Restaurant of the Year for the North East and Yorkshire at the 19th Bangladesh Caterers Association (BCA) Awards 2026.

The restaurant received the regional award at a ceremony held on Sunday 1 November at the Park Plaza Westminster Bridge Hotel in London.

Starbeck Tandoori was selected from a shortlist of four restaurants from across the region. The other finalists were Babul’s and Café Spice, both in Darlington, and Vadah in Stockton-on-Tees.

The judging panel assessed restaurants on food quality, service, hospitality, presentation, consistency, innovation and the overall customer experience.

## Recognition for Harrogate restaurant

> **Oli Khan MBE, BCA President, said:**
>
> Congratulations to Starbeck Tandoori on a thoroughly deserved win. Their achievement reflects the talent, commitment and resilience that continue to drive British Bangladeshi hospitality forward.

The awards recognise achievements across the Bangladeshi catering and hospitality sector, which has become an established part of Britain’s economic, social and cultural landscape.

BCA Secretary General Tipu Rahman said the awards provided an opportunity to recognise the achievements of businesses and individuals, celebrate their contribution and encourage the next generation of hospitality professionals and entrepreneurs.

The ceremony was attended by a number of distinguished guests, including His Excellency Mohammed Abdul Muhith, High Commissioner for Bangladesh to the United Kingdom, and several MPs and members of the House of Lords.

The Bangladesh Caterers Association represents the interests of the Bangladeshi catering and hospitality sector in the UK. The association says it represents 12,000 curry houses and takeaways, supporting around 100,000 jobs and approximately £4.2 billion in sector turnover.
