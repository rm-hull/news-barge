## ‘Clearly not working’ - MP renews call for action on York Hospital parking

Luke Charters MP said that parking at York Hospital was one the issues "most frequently raised by residents" *(Image: Supplied)*

A YORK MP has renewed calls for action over parking at York Hospital, saying the current situation is "clearly not working" for patients, visitors and staff.

Luke Charters MP said that parking at the hospital was one of the issues "frequently raised by residents" and has described experiencing difficulties finding a space first-hand.

The York Outer MP’s comments come after it was revealed that money raised by parking fines across three hospitals in the York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust had risen by more than 50 per cent in just over a year.




The number of available parking spaces at York Hospital has also dropped by 192 bays in three years, owing to investment in new buildings and facilities to improve patient care, the trust said.

![The Trust said that it ](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21672442.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Trust said that it "understood the frustration" parking could cause and was exploring "longer-term solutions" with City of York Council, alongside investment into alternative travel options for staff *(Image: Newsquest)*

A spokesperson said: "We recognise the importance of accessible parking for patients and visitors.

"Parking charge notices help prevent inappropriate parking and ensure safe access around our hospitals.

"Income from parking is reinvested in the maintenance and security of our car parks, as well as sustainable travel initiatives, including subsidised bus travel for staff and improved cycling facilities."

Hospital staff have hit out at the trust, describing the parking availability as "atrocious", and say it is the reason why they have received parking fines.

## 'No staff member should have to pay to go to work'

A nurse, who did not wish to be named, said: "What I find particularly frustrating is that staff are being penalised with parking tickets when there simply aren't enough parking spaces available.

"We are expected to arrive early, spend time driving around looking for spaces, and then face the possibility of receiving a fine because we have nowhere else to park."

Another worker claimed she spent £75 on fines in a month.

On social media, one staff member added: "No staff member should have to pay to go to work and the shifts staff work don’t always coincide with public transport."

The Trust said that it "understood the frustration" parking could cause and was exploring "longer-term solutions" with City of York Council, alongside investment into alternative travel options for staff.

A spokesperson added: "This includes subsidised bus travel, with discounts of up to 50 per cent on fares, improvements to secure cycle parking and support for colleagues who wish to cycle to work.

"We know there is no simple solution, particularly for colleagues who rely on their cars to get to work, but we remain committed to doing what we can to make travelling to and from the hospital easier."

## 'We need a proper solution, because the current situation clearly isn't working for patients, visitors or staff'

Mr Charters said: "I'm glad The Press is continuing to campaign on this, because parking at York Hospital is something residents raise with me regularly.

"I've had my own struggles finding somewhere to park at York Hospital, so I know exactly why people are fed up. When you're going in for an appointment or visiting a loved one, the last thing you need is to be driving around looking for a space.

"And for NHS staff, who are turning up early just to find somewhere to park before starting a long shift, it's simply not fair that they're then being hit with fines.

"It's particularly concerning to see the number of spaces going down while the amount being collected in parking charges and fines is going up.

"I'll keep raising this with the Hospital Trust. We need a proper solution, because the current situation clearly isn't working for patients, visitors or staff."

![York Central MP Rachael Maskell called for a new hospital in a better location in the House of Commons](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/16897915.jpg?type=mds-article-620)York Central MP Rachael Maskell called for a new hospital in a better location in the House of Commons *(Image: Pic supplied)*

York Central MP Rachael Maskell has previously called for an end to hospital parking charges, describing them as a "tax on serious illness".

In a statement, she said: "The long-term solution is for a new hospital in a better location, as I raised in the House of Commons last month, co-located with the University of York.

"However, between then and now, we need better public transport for staff and patients to visit the hospital as well as an urgent review of parking.

"I have petitioned about the traffic congestion on Wigginton Road and want to see a solution to stop the daily gridlock of traffic in the neighbourhood and I've met with the Chief Executive to share my and the cities concerns."
