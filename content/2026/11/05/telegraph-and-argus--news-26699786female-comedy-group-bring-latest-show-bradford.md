A BRADFORD venue will host a performance from award-winning comedy girl band Flat & the Curves this Friday.

The trio will bring their show Rosé Tinted - tales of “knee-defying slut-drops and toe-curling one-night stands” to Loading Bay on Duke Street on November 6.

Promotional material from the show says: “Through original music, the comedy girlband pays homage to friendships forged through grape-fuelled revelry.

“Expect a raucous look at life’s disasters and absurdities, delivered with a cheeky wink and ferocious vocals.

“With critical acclaim from The Scotsman, Sunday Express, BroadwayWorld and many more, book now for an unforgettable night of wine-soaked wisdom and glorious fun.”

The venue is a former Marks & Spencer warehouse building that was converted into an arts hub for Bradford’s City of Culture year.

Tickets cost £24, and are available by visiting [https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/flat-the-curves-rose-tinted/](https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/flat-the-curves-rose-tinted/)
