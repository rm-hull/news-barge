A date has been confirmed by Redcar and Cleveland Council for an upcoming by-election.

Residents in the Hutton ward, neighbouring Guisborough, will have the opportunity to elect a new councillor on Thursday, December 10.

In a notice, published on Thursday, November 5, it was confirmed that if the election is contested, the poll will take place in five weeks time.

Applications to register to vote must reach the electoral registration officer by 12 midnight on Tuesday, November 24. Applications can be made online: [https://www.gov.uk/register-to-vote](https://www.gov.uk/register-to-vote)

For those wishing to stand, nomination papers must be delivered to the council’s returning officer, at the Civic Centre on Ridley Street in Redcar on any day from Monday to Friday 10am to 4pm but no later than 4pm on Friday, November 13.

Nomination papers can be obtained from the office of the returning officer – at the same address – during ordinary office hours.

A notice of election, published by the local council, was all but inevitable after the resignation of Liberal Democrat Councillor Graham Jeffery, who previously said he was too ill to attend meetings and then turned up to one in the nick of time to avoid losing his seat.

Mr Jeffery had hoped to secure a third six month exemption from in-person council meetings, but this was rejected in a member vote in October.

He later stood in for Lib Dem group leader Councillor Jemma Joy at a ‘task and finish’ scrutiny committee meeting, thus avoiding failing to attend a single meeting in a six month period with days to spare.

Under legislation, after this time period a councillor loses their status, without an agreed dispensation.

Mr Jeffery was lambasted by fellow members of the council, including being branded a “no show councillor” by one. Posting on Facebook, Cllr Joy said Mr Jeffery’s “decades of service deserve recognition, respect and compassion”.

If Mr Jeffery had waited an extra couple of weeks to resign, there would not be a by-election, due to the upcoming local elections in May 2027.

The Electoral Commission outlines that if a vacancy occurs up to six months before a scheduled council election, which includes the vacant seat, no by-election will take place.

The all-out local elections across Redcar and Cleveland, as well as Middlesbrough and Stockton, will take place on Thursday, May 6, six months from Friday, November 6.

As a result, this council by-election will be the final one in Teesside until the other side of the 2027 locals.
