**Fireworks have been launched at police amid disorder in the south of Edinburgh on Bonfire Night.**

Officers were not injured during the incidents on Thursday evening, which came during a large policing operation across Scotland for November 5.

In Oxgangs and around Colinton Mains, bins were knocked over and set on fire in the road on Thursday evening.

There was a large police presence in the area, as trouble involving groups of young people was reported.

Locals stated that groups of youths were launching fireworks at emergency services as they attempted to deal with the disorder.

One person, who did not want to be named, told the Press Association: “They’re setting fire in the park and shooting fireworks at the police and firemen.”

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: “Around 5.30pm on Thursday, November 5 2026, we received a report of a group of youths creating a blockage in the road on Oxgangs Road North, Edinburgh.

“Officers attended and fireworks were set off in their direction, but no injuries have been reported.

“Several small fires in bins and the park were extinguished by the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

“Officers remain in the area, and inquiries are ongoing to trace those involved.”

In Wester Hailes, the fire service attended a fire in a grass area near houses in Murrayburn Park.

Firefighters were called to the scene of a bonfire shortly after 7pm, with one fire engine called out to extinguish the flames.

As part of its Bonfire Night policing operation, Police Scotland called in assistance from forces in England and Wales, mounting its biggest deployment of officers since the visit of US President Donald Trump last year.

The November 5 celebrations coincide with a major European football clash and a possible protest in Glasgow, and officers from as far afield as Avon and Somerset, Hampshire, Hertfordshire and South Wales will be on the streets of Scotland.
