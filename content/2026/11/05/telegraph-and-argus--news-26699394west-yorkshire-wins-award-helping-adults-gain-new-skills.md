A COMBINED authority has been nationally recognised for its approach to adult learning.

West Yorkshire Combined Authority (WYCA) won the Regional Growth Award at the Get the Nation Learning Awards 2026, held on November 4 at The Royal Society in London.

The award celebrates its work to drive regional growth by integrating adult learning into economic and community development.

Tracy Brabin, Mayor of West Yorkshire, said: "This award is testament to the unique way our colleges, training providers, businesses and employers have all pulled together to make West Yorkshire a region of learning and creativity.

"With over 300,000 enrolments on skills courses, alongside targeted support for those who need it most, we’re ensuring that everyone has the skills they need to succeed."

WYCA was commended for its partnership-led model, which uses devolved powers to align training with job opportunities across the region.

A large proportion of learners are unemployed or from deprived communities, and targeted outreach in Bradford and Kirklees supports those with low or no qualifications.

The impact goes beyond employment.

WYCA’s Out of Court Pathways Pilot, developed with police and justice partners, found that 91 per cent of participants did not reoffend within six months.
