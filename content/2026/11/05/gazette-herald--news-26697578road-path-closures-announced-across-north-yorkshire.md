Multiple road and path closures and a speed restriction have been announced across North Yorkshire.

Highfield Road in Malton will be shut for pedestrian guardrail repairs for a temporary period of two weeks between November 9 and December 31.

A signed alternative route will be available.

High Lane, Beadlam, will also be closed for new connection works from November 9 to November 18.

Forestry operations have prompted the closure of several pathways and bridleways in the Hovingham and Fryton parishes.

The affected areas include Public Footpath 25.49/18/1, Public Bridleways 25.49/17/3 and 25.49/17/2 in Hovingham, and Public Bridleway 25.35/8/3 in Fryton.

The pathways will be shut from November 9 until May 8.

In Birdsall, a temporary 20mph speed restriction will be enforced along Mill Lane due to an uneven road surface.

The restriction will be in place from November 9 for a maximum duration of 18 months.

Temporary closure orders have been issued for these roads and pathways, with the closure periods clearly indicated by traffic signs.

All closures are subject to access to premises.

North Yorkshire Council has advised residents to follow signed alternative routes as appropriate.

For more details on the roadworks and alternative routes, residents can visit the North Yorkshire Council roadworks map online or contact the customer service centre at 0300 131 2 131.

For more details on the High Lane, Beadlam closure, OCU Utility Services Limited can be contacted at 0333 200 0011.

The forestry operation-related closures in Hovingham and Fryton can be queried by contacting Castle Howard's forest manager, Duncan Leckie, on 07816 148003.
