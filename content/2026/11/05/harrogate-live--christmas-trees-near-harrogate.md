## Harrogate

### 1. Christmas Trees Harrogate, Rudfarlington Farm

A family business on a working farm on Wetherby Road, opposite the Kestrel pub, selling trees since 1999. Mike, who runs it, says the farm takes delivery of thousands of trees each season, felled at British sites shortly before they arrive.

It sells Nordmann fir, Fraser fir and Norway spruce, cut from about 3ft to 16ft or pot-grown at 3 to 5ft, and will cut a tree to fit your stand. Trees up to 25ft can be sourced if ordered by the end of September.

The 22-metre tree beside the farm, decorated since 2013, is lit on the first day of sales. The Christmas Barn, in a 200-year-old stone barn, sells decorations, lights and handmade wreaths.

Its delivery list runs from Pannal and Follifoot to Ripley, Bishop Monkton, Wetherby and Harewood. Orders go to [sales@christmastreesharrogate.co.uk.](mailto:sales@christmastreesharrogate.co.uk.)

### 2. Horticap, Otley Road

Horticap is a Harrogate charity that trains adults with learning and physical disabilities in horticulture at Bluecoat Wood Nurseries, and it says it is widely known for its Christmas trees. It sells “non-drop” Nordmann firs from 3ft to 8ft, with taller trees to order and potted trees too.

Trees can be pre-ordered now or chosen on site from early December. Its students make the Christmas wreaths by hand from natural materials, at £32.50 for a 12-inch wreath.

Hopkins Cafe is on the same site.

The prices are from the page Horticap updated on 21 October 2026.

## Spofforth and Wetherby

### 3. Stockeld Park, near Spofforth

The Stockeld estate, on the A661 between Spofforth and Wetherby, says its team grows 500,000 Christmas trees a year, which makes it by far the biggest grower on this list. Trees are sold from a marquee at the park, and the Christmas Tree Shop is separate from the paid attractions, so you need no ticket to buy one.

Visitors with tickets for the Christmas Adventure or Enchanted Illuminations can buy any tree up to 7ft for £39, or get free delivery. It delivers across Yorkshire and beyond, and has a gift shop and cafe on site.

The prices are Stockeld’s 2026 in-person list. Our Stockeld Park Christmas page covers the Christmas Adventure and illuminations.

## Ripon

### 4. Ripon Walled Garden, Palace Road

The walled garden is run by Ripon Community Link, a charity supporting people with learning and physical disabilities, and its members help customers choose a tree, carry it to the car or arrange delivery. It describes its trees as “locally sourced premium grade” and sells potted trees as well.

The Christmas shop, open seven days, stocks handmade wreaths, decorations and gifts made by members, hampers and cakes, and the cafe serves spiced drinks and mince pies. The charity says there will be a children’s Christmas trail, visits from local bands and choirs, and a Santa’s grotto.

The event listing gives 3pm as the closing time on 23 December.

## Getting rid of the tree in January

* **Ripon Community Link** collects real trees from homes on**Friday 8 and Saturday 9 January 2027**. Bookings are open online; the form checks your postcode, and the tree must be outside by 8am on the Friday. The charity recycles the trees.
* **Recycling centres:** North Yorkshire Council takes real trees free at every household waste recycling centre, in the garden waste skip, where they are shredded and composted. Our Christmas bin collections page has the details.
