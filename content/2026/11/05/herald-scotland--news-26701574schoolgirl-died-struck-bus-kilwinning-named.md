A schoolgirl who died following a crash in North Ayrshire has been named as Sophia Aitken from Kilwinning.

Emergency services were called to a crash involving a female pedestrian and a bus on Lauchlan Way in Kilwinning at around 1.10pm on Tuesday, November 3.

The 12-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene. There were no other reported injuries.

The road was closed for crash investigations and reopened at around 7.30pm.

In a statement released through Police Scotland, Sophia’s family have said: “We are devastated at the loss of our darling daughter Sophia.

"She was a ray of sunshine, and was loved by everyone who knew her. A light has gone out, and it will never return. We are comforted by the messages from the community. We would like to ask for privacy to grieve at this difficult time.”

Police said enquiries into the crash are ongoing.
