Above the tents of a displacement camp in **al-Mawasi,** near Khan Yunis,**homemade wind turbines** spin in the sea breeze, providing a lifeline to**families left without electricity**.

The devices were built by **60-year-old Kamal Al-Dabary**, a former engineering student, using scooter motors, discarded water tanks and electrical components salvaged from destroyed homes. The energy generated is stored in batteries.

With **solar panels now costing nearly $5,000** and much of Gaza's electricity infrastructure destroyed, his invention offers**an affordable alternative**.

The turbines produce just enough power to charge phones and run lights, fans and electric razors. But for displaced residents, even this limited supply **makes a difference**.
