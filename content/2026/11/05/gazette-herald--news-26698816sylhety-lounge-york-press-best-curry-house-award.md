A York restaurant serving Bangladeshi and Indian cuisine has been named among the finalists in a competition to find the city's best curry house.

Sylhety Lounge, in Kimberlow Hill Retail Park, York, is one of ten restaurants shortlisted for a Press Best Curry House award 2026.

It comes after hundreds of our readers voted for their favourite spot in the city.




The restaurant describes itself as offering authentic Bangladeshi and Indian cuisine in a relaxed and friendly setting, using fresh ingredients and experienced chefs to create its dishes.

Opening during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, the restaurant's team has worked hard behind the scenes to create a loyal customer base in York.

A representative for the restaurant said: “We started our journey during a very challenging time, with the Covid pandemic beginning shortly after.

“Despite the difficulties, we've worked hard to grow the restaurant and are very grateful for the support and loyalty we've received from our customers over the years.”

“I've always loved the idea of bringing people together through good food, creating a welcoming atmosphere and giving customers an experience they would want to come back for.

“It was about turning a dream into reality and building something I could be proud of. I wanted to offer great-quality food, good service and a place where people feel comfortable and valued.”

The restaurant's signature dish is the Sylhety Lounge Special, a combination of chicken, king prawns and keema rice.

A spokesperson for Sylhety said: “What makes this dish special is the combination of different flavours and textures, bringing together tender chicken and succulent king prawns in one dish.

“It's a dish we're particularly proud of and one that truly represents the character of Sylhety Lounge.”

Since opening, the restaurant has adapted its menu to offer customers more choice.

Today, it employs a team of eight staff and aims to provide a warm atmosphere alongside freshly prepared food and attentive service.

A spokesperson said: “We want our customers to have a memorable experience every time they visit.

“Whether they're coming in with family, friends or for a special occasion, we want them to leave happy, satisfied and eager to visit us again.”

Readers of The Press can vote to crown Sylhety Lounge the winners of the Best Curry House in York 2026 competition by filling out the voting slip included in each edition of The Press.
