**Holyrood’s Presiding Officer has announced plans to give a 20% pay rise to MSPs who chair committees in the Scottish Parliament.**

Stressing that committees “really are at the heart” of Holyrood’s work, Kenneth Gibson said he wants their pay to rise from April 2027.

MSPs currently earn £77,711, but Mr Gibson said the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body (SPCB) will bring forward regulations which, if approved, will increase that to over £93,000 before tax.

The Government's website details that a convener acts as a committee's chair, presiding over meetings, directing scrutiny work and representing the committee in its dealings with ministers, officials and the wider public.

This will mean that from April 1 committee conveners should “receive an additional payment equivalent to 20% of MSP salary”.

Announcing the move – which has the unanimous backing of the SPCB – in a letter to committee conveners, Mr Gibson said the “responsibilities, authority and accountability” of the role warranted this “formal recognition”.

Adding that Holyrood’s committees “really are at the heart of what we, as a Parliament, do”, the Presiding Officer said he would set out plans for further reforms and “strengthening the ties between Holyrood, its committees and communities across Scotland” in the coming months.

Parliament currently has 16 committees. The conveners who will be receiving the pay rise next year, if they remain in those roles, are:

* Stuart McMillan (SNP), Climate Action Committee
* David Linden (SNP), Criminal Justice Committee
* Calum Kerr (SNP), Delegated Powers and Law Reform Committee
* Duncan Massey (Reform), Economy, Tourism and Energy Committee
* Karen Adam (SNP), Education and Gaelic Committee
* Katie Hagmann (SNP), Equalities, Human Rights and Civil Justice Committee
* Patrick Harvie (Scottish Greens), Europe, External Affairs and Culture Committee
* Clare Haughey (SNP), Finance and Public Administration Committee
* Helen McDade (Reform), Health, Care and Sport Committee
* Neil Bibby (Labour), Public Audit Committee
* Paul Sweeney (Labour), Public Petitions Committee
* Bob Doris (SNP), Public Service Reform Committee
* Mark Ruskell (Scottish Greens), Rural Affairs Committee
* Craig Hoy (Scottish Tories), Social Justice, Housing and Local Government Committee
* Jenni Minto (SNP), Standards, Procedures and Public Appointments Committee
* Willie Rennie (LibDems), Transport Committee

The total additional annual cost following the 20% rise would be about £250k a year.
