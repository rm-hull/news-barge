North Yorkshire Police officers have been deployed across the North Yorkshire coast as part of a security operation.

Project Servator officers have been carrying out high-visibility patrols and engaging with the public in key coastal areas this week.

The deployments are intentionally unpredictable and can occur at any time and in any weather, with officers employing a variety of tactics to disrupt criminal and hostile activity.

Police have stressed the importance of community involvement in maintaining security.




* **14 arrested and weapons seized in North Yorkshire**
* **Police seek person after counterfeit money used in Ripon**
* **Major delay and disruption after person hit by train**
* **HGV crashed into house in Coulton Lane, Coulton near Malton**

They have urged the public to remain vigilant and report anything unusual to a police officer, member of staff, or via the North Yorkshire Police website.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21674669.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police on the North Yorkshire Coast as part of Project Servator *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

In emergencies, the public is advised to call 999.

The initiative also involves collaboration with local businesses, transport providers, and local authorities to enhance security, share information, and respond promptly to concerns.

The aim is to ensure the North Yorkshire coast remains safe and welcoming for all.
