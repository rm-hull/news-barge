Spire Harrogate Clinic in Harrogate has received a positive report from the Care Quality Commission (CQC), with inspectors praising its approach to patient care, community partnerships and the compassion shown by staff.

Following an assessment carried out on 28 July 2026, the clinic was rated **Good overall**, achieving Good ratings across all five inspection areas: Safe, Effective, Caring, Responsive and Well-led.

The assessment covered the clinic’s diagnostic imaging and outpatient services, with inspectors highlighting the quality of care provided by colleagues across the clinic.

> **The Care Quality Commission said:**
>
> The service was exceptional at treating people with kindness, empathy and compassion and in how they respected people’s privacy and dignity.

## Community partnerships support inclusive care

A key theme of the inspection was the clinic’s work to make its services accessible and welcoming to people across the local community.

Inspectors recognised its collaboration with community organisations including Disability Action Yorkshire, Dementia Forward Harrogate and Vision Support Harrogate District.

The partnerships have helped the clinic consider the needs of people with a wide range of circumstances and ensure its environment and patient experience are as inclusive as possible.

The report also highlighted the clinic’s personalised approach, noting that staff treated patients as individuals and sought to ensure care and treatment reflected their preferences and needs.

> **One patient told inspectors:**
>
> Outstanding care from everyone today from the minute I walked through the door. Professional attitude shown by everyone which was balanced with kindness and understanding.

## Inspectors praise leadership and safety

The CQC also found that the clinic had robust systems and processes in place to deliver safe, effective and person-centred care.

Inspectors noted that this was supported by the expertise and resources available through the wider Spire Healthcare network.

Leadership was also praised in the report.

> **The Care Quality Commission said:**
>
> Leaders had the skills, knowledge, experience and credibility to lead effectively. They always did so with integrity, openness and honesty.

> **Tim Cook, General Manager at Spire Harrogate Clinic, said:**
>
> We are incredibly proud of this report and how it reflects the dedication, compassion and professionalism of our colleagues and consultants. We believe healthcare should be accessible, inclusive and centred around the needs of every individual, and we’re delighted to have been recognised for the safety and quality of our services.

The full CQC report for Spire Harrogate Clinic is available on the Care Quality Commission website.
