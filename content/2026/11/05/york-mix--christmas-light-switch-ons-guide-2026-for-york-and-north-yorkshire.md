Get into the seasonal spirit with our guide to the Christmas light switch-on and markets happening in York and North Yorkshire.

### The Grand York Christmas light switch-on

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/846073226_1603928701768115_835006151804928771_n.jpg)

The Grand, York will host its annual Christmas light switch-on on Friday 13 November 2026, inviting the local community to come together and celebrate the start of the festive season.

Open to the public and free to attend, the festivities will begin at 5 pm, with the hotel’s Christmas lights set to illuminate at 5:30 pm. Visitors can look forward to an evening of festive entertainment, seasonal treats, and opportunities to support local charitable initiatives.

Adding to the festive atmosphere, Yorkshire Voices will perform a selection of festive favourites throughout the evening.

Visitors will also be able to enjoy a delicious selection of festive food and drink, including loaded doughnuts, warming mulled wine, hot chocolate and comforting crumble, providing the perfect accompaniment to the festivities.

Putting the spirit of giving at the heart of the celebrations, the event will feature a Grand Raffle in support of Cash for Kids, helping to raise vital funds for the charity and support its work in the local community.

* The Grand, York, Station Rise, YO1 6GD
* Fri 13 November
* 5pm

### Minster Mash-Up: Lights switch-on

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/548039885_1204701311698374_7586925680509774477_n.jpg)

Join our worship for young people and families – Minster Mash-Up – and get ready for Advent!

For this special event, families are invited to join us at the lights switch-on for the York Minster Christmas Tree Festival as we prepare to mark the beginning of Advent and await the arrival of baby Jesus.

Families are invited to come at any time during the free 45-minute event, which starts at 10am, and stay for as long or as little as they like. No booking is required.

* York Minster, Deangate, York, YO1 7HH
* Sat 28 November
* 10am

### Norton Christmas Light Switch On

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/DH-2026-poster-2-media.jpg)

Celebrate the start of the festive season at Norton Hive Library & Community Hub’s Christmas Fayre on Sunday 29 November 2026. Doors open at 12 noon, and entry is free.

Enjoy a wonderful range of attractions, including craft stalls, books for sale, tombola, raffle, a white elephant stall, traditional fairground rides and a brass band.

Festive refreshments will include mince pies, cakes, biscuits, sausage rolls, mulled wine, hot chocolate, tea, coffee and soft drinks.

Meet Father Christmas at 4pm before heading to the Norton Town Christmas Lights Switch-On at 4.30pm.

Bring your family and friends and join us for a festive afternoon in the heart of our community!

* Norton Hive Library Car Park, 86 Commercial St, Norton, Malton YO17 9ES
* Sun 29 November
* From 12pm

### Alne Tinsel Tractor Run

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/811994113_122275125416089769_2177028208423587045_n.jpg)

The Tractor Run will take place on Sunday 6th December, following the same route as previous years.

Starting in Alne, the festive convoy will travel through Tollerton, Newton, Linton, Aldwark, Flawith, Myton, Helperby, Easingwold and other surrounding villages.

The event brings a huge number of people into the local area and creates a fantastic festive atmosphere for residents and local businesses alike. We are incredibly proud of how much support the event receives from the community!

* Alne Village Hall, Alne, York YO61 1RT
* Sun 6 December
* From 3pm
