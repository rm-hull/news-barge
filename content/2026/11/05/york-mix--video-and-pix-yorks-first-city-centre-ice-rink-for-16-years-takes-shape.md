York’s first city centre ice rink since 2010 is taking shape.

The rink is being built behind York Art Gallery as it readies to open on Friday 13 November.

It comes with a see-through roof to protect skaters from any rain or snow, while allowing them to still see the skies above.

The rink is being created by Thor’s Tipis and will run till 3 January.

During its run, the team from Lucia’s Restaurant in York will create the Lucia Apres Bar on the art gallery balcony, serving festive cocktails and warming winter drinks.

Foodie treats on offer include an Irish Spice Bag (salt & chilli chicken, thick-cut French chips and seasoned peppers & onions), and crumble pots for dessert.

They are also guaranteeing a snow shower every hour!

Part of the construction on site includes a wooden skate station, where you swap your shoes for ice skates.

Amanda Monaghan, director at Thor’s Tipi, told ***YorkMix*** she was pleased with the build so far : “There’s no ice to skate on yet, but it’s all coming together. I think we’re very pleased with progress so far.

“It’s definitely taken us all by surprise how it looks when you’re on site, and I think it’s going to be good fun.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/THORs-on-ICE-will-have-an-impressive-clear-covering-so-guests-can-enjoy-the-festive-setting-come-rain-or-shine.webp)

“Hopefully, we’ll get plenty of people out there skating – and of course you’ve got the lovely views of the walls and the buildings and the history of York as well.”

Amanda said ticket sales were going well, and if the season is a success, she’ll want to bring it back.

“I one hundred per cent want to be here for a very long time,” she said.
