Harrogate Brewing Co is among the winners of the 2026 Deliciously Yorkshire Taste Awards.

The brewery claimed Best Beer & Cider at a black-tie ceremony at Pavilions of Harrogate this week.

Now in their 22nd year, the awards celebrate Yorkshire’s food and drink businesses, attracting hundreds of entries across 26 categories.

Other winners included Rosebud Preserves, which took Best Sweet Spread & Preserve, while The Angel Inn at Topcliffe received the new Best Yorkshire Pub award.

Skipton-based Coffee Care won Best Wholesaler & Distributor, with founder Rennick Hodgson also receiving the Judy Bell Yorkshire Food Hero award.

The overall Supreme Product Champion award went to Blacker Hall Farm Shop for its homemade steak party pie, chosen as the judging panel’s standout entry of the year.

Judging took place in early September, with chefs, supermarket buyers, writers and other industry experts assessing entries before finalists were announced later that month.

Guests at the ceremony enjoyed a menu featuring shortlisted and winning products.

Headline sponsor Booths also confirmed it will support the awards again in 2027.
