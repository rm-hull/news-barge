## Democrats have seized control of the US Congress in a nationwide rejection of Trump. In Europe, hackers targeted a French blood bank and supermarket fridges, raising fears of a new front in hybrid warfare. Plus: we talk to Reza Pahlavi, one of the main leaders of the opposition to the Tehran regime.

## On today's show:

**Eric Ham**, political analyst and bestselling author in Washington, asks how a weakened** Donald Trump**will react following the US midterm elections in which Democrats took control of both the Senate and the House of Representatives.** Xavier Volatier**, Head of Transatlantic Private Wealth at Banque Transatlantique in New York, explains what the midterms mean for European investors.** Shona Murray**reports from Helsinki on Europe's reaction to the Democratic win and what to expect from the Ukraine talks planned for next week.** Reza Pahlavi**, son of Iran's last Shah, tells** Vincenzo Genovese**why the EU should freeze assets linked to the Tehran regime and show the same resolve it has shown towards** Vladimir Putin**.** Kevin Curran**, professor of cybersecurity at Ulster University, explains how hackers can take over supermarket fridges and blood-bank cooling systems, and what that means for Europe's critical infrastructure.
