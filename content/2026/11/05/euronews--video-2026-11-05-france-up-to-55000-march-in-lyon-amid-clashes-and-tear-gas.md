A slow, noisy column of **tens of thousands** wound through eastern center of Lyon on Thursday, banners and flares at its head, as French unions staged their**fourth nationwide strike day of the autumn** over**pay and public services**.

Authorities counted **30,500 marchers**, while the** CGT union estimated 55,000**. Protesters waved union banners and Palestinian flags as they made their way through the city.

The march followed a **route imposed by local authorities**, who had** banned demonstrations across much of central Lyon** over fears of renewed vandalism and looting. Unions challenged the restrictions in court but lost their appeal.

Tensions escalated near Pont Gallieni, where police said **around 500 black-clad demonstrators** had thrown projectiles and fired fireworks.**Riot officers deployed tear gas**, while** six arrests were reported**.

The disruption extended across the city, with **all metro and funicular services suspended**.
