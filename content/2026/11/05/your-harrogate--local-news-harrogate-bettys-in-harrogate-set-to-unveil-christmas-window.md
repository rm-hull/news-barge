Bettys will unveil its Christmas window in Harrogate tomorrow (Friday 6th November).

The annual reveal takes place from 5pm outside the tea rooms on Parliament Street, with visitors invited to sample festive favourites including mince pies and mulled wine.

An annual raffle will also offer the chance to win Christmas gifts.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aec3eae8c47dd48f92.png)

This year’s display takes inspiration from sending love to friends and family far away, recalling a time when Christmas greetings and gifts travelled by post.

A Bettys spokesperson said:

> “Just as the bonfires go out, the Christmas lights will come on!”

It will feature handcrafted Bettys products designed to be shared and sent to loved ones.

The window reveal has become a festive tradition for local residents, with visitors also travelling from further afield to attend.
