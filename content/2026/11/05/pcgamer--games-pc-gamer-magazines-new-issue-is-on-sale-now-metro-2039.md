![PC Gamer magazine issue 429 Metro 2039](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/6uR2ASAqLXhiXu4rvch8w9-1920-80.jpg)

This month, PC Gamer delivers world-exclusive access to Metro 2039, the hyper-dark new FPS from 4A Games. And we're pleased to say that PC gamers who have been itching for the next great single-player FPS campaign are not going to be disappointed. This is shaping up to be a graphical powerhouse shooter that combines the best parts of each previous Metro game into a gripping, atmospheric FPS that pulls no punches in terms of action and narrative.

![PC Gamer magazine]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/43Fqu3eVcUVAjpsAnqg89B-200-80.jpg.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/43Fqu3eVcUVAjpsAnqg89B-200-80.jpg.webp) "PC Gamer magazine")

There's another awesome feature in this issue of PC Gamer, too. On the long-running World War II RTS series' 20th anniversary, we speak directly to the developers who are currently looking after it and planning for its future, Relic Entertainment. And, with plenty of insider stories from the series past and present, this is a must-read for fans.

![PC Gamer magazine issue 429 Metro 2039](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/GJb7PK7Z2gGmWr6JCmNq9A-2353-80.jpg)

This issue is also loaded with great previews. First up we have our lead BlizzCon 2026 show report, which covers both Diablo 5 as well as the exciting new StarCraft FPS. This is followed by hands-on previews of Masters of Albion, Gundam Rogue Orbit, Guns of Eschaton, Warhammer Blood Bowl, Muramasa: Revenant Blades, and Crown of Hispania, among others.

![PC Gamer magazine issue 429 Metro 2039](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ZH4T8dqdaVwtthwTcPp33B-2353-80.jpg)

Then, in terms of reviews, we pass our official judgement on the adrenaline-inducing Star Wars Galactic Racer, as well as Control Resonant, Endless Legend 2, Nighthawks, Valor Mortis, and Silent Hill: Townfall, among others.

![PC Gamer magazine issue 429 Metro 2039](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/5zZjx8w6uqpyKL96dbjovA-2353-80.jpg)

LEAD PREVIEW

LEAD REVIEW

Our lead review this issue sees PC Gamer buckle up and switch on the afterburner to keep pace with the adrenaline-inducing Star Wars Galactic Racer. And, with a fresh run-based roguelite campaign structure, we find there's plenty to enjoy at breakneck speed, even if the whole racing package is a little rough around the edges.

MOD SPOTLIGHT

Just when you'd think you had seen it all, some talented modder goes and splices slick first-person action-platformer Mirror's Edge into the wet-your-pants-scary horror game, Amnesia: The Bunker. The result is Bunker's Edge, and it is as great/crazy as you imagine.

REINSTALL

The epic story-based role-playing game that forces you, repeatedly, to make genuinely difficult choices gets replayed this issue to see if, ten years later, its unique fantasy world and characters inspired by Norse mythology still hit so hard.

DIARY

This issue of PC Gamer magazine sees us continue our Starfield diary where we attempt to play a deranged corpo serial killer in Starfield. Can Pendleton, whose axe loves to whisper sweet nothings in his ear, appear like a normal corporate suit during the day, while also racking up a huge body count at night? Pick up this issue to find out.
