A County Durham sauna and wild swim, called a “little paradise of nature and luxury”, is attracting interest after a viral influencer said she can't stop coming back to visit.

Elements Sauna, at the TCR Hub in Barnard Castle, is a wood-fired public sauna that offers wild swims in its neighbouring lake, combining heat sessions with cold-water swimming and dips for a relaxing experience without breaking the bank.

The natural spot is popular among North East wild swimmers, with some labelling it "Barnard Castle's best kept secret."

It has attracted fresh attention after content creator Wild Swim Adventures (@sparklyswims) praised it on social media.

Wild Swim Adventures has more than 140,000 followers across her social media pages, where she showcases some of the UK's best natural landscapes to swim in.

She praised the sauna and lake in a video posted yesterday (November 3), while highlighting wild swimming sites across County Durham's stunning scenery, including High Force and Hamsterley Forest.

Her video has introduced her sizeable audience to wild swimming opportunities across the North East, with many commenters keen to visit for themselves.

She said: "I kind of want to gatekeep this but I also wish more people knew about this place," adding: "There are so many reasons I keep coming back here."

Wild Swim Adventures continued to praise the spot's "ridiculously blue lake," and the smells inside the "gorgeous" sauna, which she called "super spacious."

She said Elements was perfect for friends to get together to swim together, adding: "You can even now join a guided sauna session so you can really switch off, relax and enjoy the whole experience."

Visitors can use changing areas, toilets and a cold shower, with sauna sessions including female-only, guided, calm and full-moon events, which start at £15.

A swim without a sauna costs starts at £2, giving swim-focused visitors the choice of a short dip.

Public sessions have no more than eight people to ensure everyone has enough space in the lake, and first-time swimmers are welcomed.

Elements is marketed as a community sauna rather than a spa, which offers guests a chance to experience Finnish saunas alongside a place to unwind from daily stresses.

The surrounding area offers several places to explore after a sauna or swim, Wild Swim Adventures said, highlighting Blackling Falls in Hamsterley Forest, as well as Gibson’s Cave, High Force and Low Force near Bowlees.

One online review described the venue as “Barnard Castle's best kept secret”, while another praised its small groups and peaceful setting.

They wrote: "Tucked away, a little paradise of nature and luxury I cannot wait to go again.

"Wonderful experience for me and a friend. Thank you, just what we needed."

Another had similar praise, writing: "We had a really lovely swim and sauna here, nice small group of like minded individuals appreciating the peace and tranquillity of this hidden, but easily accessible gem. Can't wait to go again soon."

The social media feature has helped bring wider attention to a venue that was already serving loyal North East wild swimmers, as well as the beautiful natural landscapes on offer across the North East.

Elements Sauna previously partnered with the TCR Hub on a wellbeing day featuring mindfulness, gentle movement and relaxation sessions, with tickets supporting the hub's charitable work across Teesdale.

Sessions are held on the grounds, with facilities available for visitors who want to swim, use the sauna or do both.

With waterfalls, woodland walks and wild swimming spots all within easy reach, Elements Sauna is helping to put Teesdale on the map for those seeking outdoor wellbeing experiences in the North East.

More information about sessions and upcoming events can be found on the Elements Sauna website and social media channels.
