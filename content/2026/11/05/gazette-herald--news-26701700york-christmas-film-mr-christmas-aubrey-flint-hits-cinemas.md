*York Christmas film Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint hits cinemas from tomorrow: watch the trailer and spot all the locations in York*

A STAR-studded Christmas film that was filmed in York is coming to cinemas from tomorrow.

Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint was filmed at various locations across York last November and brought famous faces including Richard E Grant and Game of Thrones star John Bradley to the city.

Film fans will have fun spotting the many York locations in the movie.

They can watch the film in cinemas from Friday and on Monday City Screen is hosting a special screening with a bonus Q&A with star John Bradley and film director Jack Spring from 7pm.

A sneak peek of the film has been released in a YouTube trailer which outlines the story and shows John, who plays Aubrey Flint, at several locations in York including walking down Grape Lane and Lower Priory Street in Bishophill and sitting by the Ouse riverbank.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21673871.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Actor John Bradley walking in Bishophill, York, in the new movie Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint. Image: from screenshot from Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21673870.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Actor John Bradley sitting by the Ouse riverside, York, in the new movie Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint. Image: from screenshot from Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint

The feel-good British film which has earned reviews in The Guardian and Radio Times features an ensemble cast boasting a wealth of home-grown talent, including household names such as Traitors favourites Celia Imrie, Karl Pilkington, Nigel Planer and the voice of Jim Broadbent.

The story follows Aubrey Flint, a Scrooge-like man who hates Christmas. After getting into trouble, he undertakes community service at a struggling retirement home.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21673872.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Celia Imrie and Nigel Planer are just two of the British stars in Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint, filmed in York. Image: from screenshot from Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint

He is reluctantly recruited into putting on an am-dram production of A Christmas Carol to help save the home from a property developer.

The cast and crew were seen filming in several locations last year, including Colliergate and Precentor’s Court.

One of the locations was number 5 Colliergate – the former appliance store Blackwell & Denton, which was transformed into The Miniature World Of Wonders for the movie.

The film is released in cinemas from tomorrow, with screenings at Vue and Cineworld, with the special show at City Screen on Monday.

**Watch the trailer: Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint**

In an interview with The Press's Helen Mead, director Jack Spring explained why he filmed the movie in York.

He said: “It’s a lovely place – I loved the years I spent there, and it is the perfect place for a Christmas movie.”

Setting the film in York was seen as positive not only for its appeal to audiences in the UK, but for its success in the US market where, says Jack, “either Royal Britain or Harry Potter Britain” ticks boxes.

Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint has been long in the making. Jack first had the idea in 2018. “I was living for a time in America and would watch BBC news videos – one was a short film about a drama teacher who visited an old people’s home to put on shows, which brought the residents back to life.”

"It's a gorgeous, heartwarming and beautiful film," Jack says.

“In my lifetime I’ve never known the world to be as divided as it is – this film is about the power of love and connection, about being human. It brings with it a powerful message, especially at Christmas.”
