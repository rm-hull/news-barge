North Yorkshire Police are appealing for help to identify a person following a report of fraud.

Officers want to speak to the individual pictured in connection with an incident in Ripon where counterfeit money was used to buy an item on the afternoon of September 8.

North Yorkshire Police believe the person in the image may have information that could assist their enquiries.

A spokesman said: "If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.

"Please quote reference 12260187676 when providing information."

Anyone who cannot use the online options is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Information can also be passed on anonymously to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or online via their website, quoting the same reference number.

The investigation remains ongoing and police are urging anyone with information to come forward.
