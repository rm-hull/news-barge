Lamarre-Condon tells court he looked at Baird’s messages while he was in shower

Lamarre-Condon has told the court he posted a bouquet of flowers on to his story around mid-November 2023 while Baird was in Melbourne.

He says it was to give the impression it was from Baird and wanted to see if another man Baird was seeing would message him and say, “well I’m also dating Jesse”.

Asked if he had gone through Baird’s phone around this time, Lamarre-Condon tells the court he looked at Baird’s messages with that other man while the TV presenter was in the shower.

Baird later called Lamarre-Condon and asked if he went through his phone to block someone:

I denied, denied, denied … within 10 hours of denying it I essentially came clean.

He and Baird went back and forth about the flower messages, he tells the court:

I told Jesse the flowers were sent to my mum by her partner … that wasn’t true.

I then sent a voice not by text message, saying yes I did block [the man] on your phone, that was to try to insinuate they were from you when they weren’t.

I completely owned up to it, I was truthful and honest 10 hours later.

He became emotional as he spoke about his experience working for NSW police. Lamarre-Condon said he experienced bullying and torment at work and told the court of instances of homophobic comments from his superiors.

Lamarre-Condon told the court he poured water into the fuel tank of Baird’s vehicle in what he believed to be a “tit-for-tat”.

He alleged his experiences at work made him feel suicidal and his voice wavered as he spoke of the situation.

Lamarre-Condon said he read messages on Baird’s phone and accessed it while he was in the shower. He also told the court he looked through the device while Baird was sleeping and was chased out of Baird’s home.

That’s where we’ll leave our coverage for today. The trial will continue tomorrow morning.

Lamarre-Condon jokingly asked friend ‘you would help me hide a body right’

Lamarre-Condon has told the court about an occasion when he attended the home of his friend – known to the court under the pseudonym Fiona Rosso.

At the time, he was commencing beginner acting classes and he asked Rosso if she could film with him.

According to Lamarre-Condon, they were rehearsing a “serious play” where the friend’s character wanted to go to the police, while the character Lamarre-Condon was playing did not.

Lamarre-Condon tells the court that he jokingly asked Rosso, “You would help me hide a body right,” and she responded, “Yes of course I would mate.”

It was joking banter in line with the play.

Lamarre-Condon tells the court the “banter” was something officers joke about:

When you have a heavy suitcase, you might joke, ‘shit how many bodies are in here?’

It was not serious and now I would never, ever try to joke about that … if I could go back in time, I would never joke about those things ever again.

Lamarre-Condon afraid he would lose job over medication

Lamarre-Condon tells the court he wanted to start taking medication that belonged to his stepfather, who was prescribed medication for depression and anxiety:

I was very fearful that I would lose my job as a police officer if I was prescribed any medication.

Lamarre-Condon says one of his superiors said that if he “finds out anyone is on medication, that we would not be able to keep our job”.

He tells the court he made a complaint about this.

Lamarre-Condon tells court he could have obtained a gun from youth gang member

Lamarre-Condon says his work in the youth command meant he could have gotten his own gun:

I work with youth gangs. Some of their families are in the Comanchero … I could easily go to these kids that look up to me ‘here’s four grand … go get me a gun’, and have a gun six hours later.

Lamarre-Condon tells court he poured water into Baird’s fuel tank

Shortly after Baird asked Lamarre-Condon to refrain from contacting him in November 2023, Lamarre-Condon says he saw scratches on the rims of his Tesla.

I parked so far away from the curb, I believed it was Jesse who did this.

He says his mother had seen Baird’s car earlier that day.

Lamarre-Condon tells the court:

I know tit-for-tat isn’t right, I did go to the street where Jesse parked his car and did pour water into the fuel tank.

Lamarre-Condon says he experienced ‘bullying, torment’ at work as police officer

Lamarre-Condon tells the jury his experience at work made him feel suicidal:

All the constant pushback, the bullying, torment, being told I can go back to picking up dead bodies … [it] made me feel suicidal. I didn’t want to be at work any more.

His voice begins shaking as he describes how he felt:

As a youth engagement officer, I have a personal relationship with these young offenders.

Me not showing up to work is not like showing up to a general duties [shift] … This is me letting down young people that needed me as a role model, needed me to help them, needed me to be there for them.

It was too much for me and it made me feel like having thoughts to take my life.

Lamarre-Condon tells the court he tried to take his own life, picking up tissues and wiping his face as he gives his evidence.

Lamarre-Condon tells court about a night Baird left key out for him

Lamarre-Condon says he and Baird had a conversation about staying the night at the Paddington home on 17 August 2023 and he agreed to leaving the key in the letterbox.

He tells the court he arrived after 10pm, opened the back door and left a bobby pin in the front door.

Lamarre-Condon tells court about using Baird’s phone

The former officer says in January 2023 he and Baird went to an escape room. Baird then gave Lamarre-Condon access to his phone and asked him to edit a video of them together at the escape room, Lamarre-Condon tells the court.

While editing the video, Instagram notifications began popping up from another man.

Lamarre-Condon says the messages were: “Jesse saying our relationship was somewhat casual.”

He tells the court he then used his own phone to take photos of the messages and sent it to other friends.

Lamarre-Condon says:

I did not block … I did not delete any messages, I did not unfollow anybody at this stage.
