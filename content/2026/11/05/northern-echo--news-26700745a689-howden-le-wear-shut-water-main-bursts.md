Water was sent pouring onto a County Durham street after a large main burst under the road.

Emergency services were called to the A689 at Howden-le-Wear, near the Gulf garage, in the early hours of this morning (Thursday, November 5).

Northumbrian Water crews arrived and isolated the main, but a road closure was put in place “because of the size and location of the burst”.

The road has been shut between Bridgefield Court and Park Terrace, and while residents of Village Gate can access the estate, there is no through-access on the A689.

The water firm said there has been no impact to customer supplies and teams are working on the repair.

A spokesperson said: “Earlier this morning our teams responded to a large burst on a water main on the A689 at Howden-le-Wear near the Gulf garage.

“They arrived quickly and isolated the main, meaning that there is no impact to customer supplies.

“Because of the size and location of the burst, and for the safety of motorists and our teams, a road closure has been put in place between Bridgefield Court and Park Terrace, and diversions have been set up.
