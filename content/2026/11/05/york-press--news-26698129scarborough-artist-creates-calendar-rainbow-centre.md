## Scarborough landmarks brought to life in hand-drawn calendar

The Rainbow Centre is preparing to celebrate its 30th anniversary in 2027 with a special calendar featuring sketches by one of its clients *(Image: The Rainbow Centre)*

A hand-drawn calendar capturing the beauty of Scarborough is helping to raise funds for a good cause.

The Rainbow Centre is preparing to celebrate its 30th anniversary in 2027 with a special calendar featuring sketches by one of its clients, Paul, who has used the centre’s services for many years.

His biro and pencil drawings highlight landmarks and scenes around Scarborough.

James Howard, fundraising officer at The Rainbow Centre, said: "This is a fantastic gesture from Paul.

"When he first approached us about doing this, Paul put a lot of emphasis on how much he wants to give back to The Rainbow Centre after using our services for such a long time.

"We think the drawings are amazing and Paul is very talented.

"It was our pleasure to make this dream come true for him and allow him to get the recognition his work deserves in the shape of a calendar.

"We are sure that the local community will support this by buying Paul’s calendars, and it is a great way for us at The Rainbow Centre to kick off our 30th anniversary celebrations."

Calendars cost £10 and are available at The Rainbow Centre and selected shops around Scarborough.
