Looking where to find that perfect present this Christmas? Here’s our guide to the Christmas markets and fairs in York and North Yorkshire.

York is known for its famous Christmas Market on Parliament Street and St Sampson’s Square but there’s a whole host of festive fairs and markets happening around York and beyond.

To help you to find that special gift for your loved ones, we’ve put them into one handy guide.

## Christmas markets and fairs in York and North Yorkshire 2026

### York Christmas Market

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/york-christmas-market-parliament-street-2026-2.webp)

York’s award-winning Christmas Market is back from 12th November to 21st December 2025, bringing festive cheer to the heart of the city. Alpine chalets will line Parliament Street and St Sampson’s Square, showcasing local crafts, artisan products, unique gifts and delicious seasonal treats. With more than 80% of traders coming from Yorkshire, the Market is a great opportunity to support local businesses and discover the best of the region.

* Parliament St, York
* Thu 12 Nov – Mon 21 Dec
* 10:30am – 7pm

### Malton St Clement’s Festival

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/MaltonMonthlyFoodMarket11March2023milnerCreative-023-2048x1366.webp)

The Malton St Clement’s Festival is set to be a celebration of Malton’s host of craftspeople, the Autumn and St Clement, the patron of blacksmiths.

There’ll be a whole host of activities planned throughout the day including sculpture and art trails, light shows, a variety of musical performances with singing and dancing, and of course, delicious treats from Malton’s eateries. Building on the success of previous years, the 2026 festival will combine beloved community traditions—including live blacksmithing demonstrations, lantern making, storytelling, and the iconic twilight Son et Lumière—with a host of exciting new additions designed to showcase the town’s vibrant independent spirit.

The event is organised and run by the Malton St Clement’s Steering Group which is made up of several representatives of businesses and organisations in Malton.

* Malton Market Place
* Sat 21 Nov
* From 10am
* More info
