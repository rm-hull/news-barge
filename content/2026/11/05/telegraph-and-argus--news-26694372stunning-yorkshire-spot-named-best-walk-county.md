Thanks to its miles of countryside, England is a great place for taking a relaxing walk no matter where you are in the country.

Yorkshire is lucky to have so many incredible spots that are ideal for long hikes like the Yorkshire Dales.

If you are struggling to choose where to head to next, The Telegraph has released a list of the best walks in each county.

For West Yorkshire, Haworth Moor was named the best place for a walk thanks to its literary links.

## What is Haworth Moor like?

On the area, The Telegraph said: “Take a bus from hip Hebden Bridge to hilltop Haworth – where the Brontë sisters lived – and walk back.

“You get literary shivers: from Haworth’s Brontë Museum, you’re soon up on wild, windy Haworth Moor, heading for the ruined farmhouse of Top Withens, possible inspiration for Wuthering Heights.

“Then you get a bit of wild Pennine Way, via heathery uplands and the Walshaw Dean reservoirs.

“Then the world changes completely, as you descend into the enchanted, river-tinkled gorge of Hardcastle Crags.”

Described as the “jewel in the Yorkshire crown”, Haworth Moor is a great place for walking no matter the weather.

There are plenty of spots to stop off at and enjoy a break, such as the Brontë waterfall, which is one of the most popular destinations to visit and is a good place for a picnic.

If you love the Brontë sisters, you will also want to visit places like the ruined farmhouse, which is said to have been the location of the Earnshaw family in Emily Brontë’s book Wuthering Heights.

You can also see the Alcomden Stones that are believed to have been a place of “druid sacrifice and ancient ritual”.

Many spots along the walk offer “stunning” views of the surrounding countryside, making it a peaceful place to enjoy a few hours of calm.

At the end of your walk, you can spend some time exploring Haworth and take a trip to one of the many cosy pubs and cafés found in the village.
