Lanarkshire-based business water retailer Intelligent Water has secured a £3.5 million funding facility to support its next phase of growth.

The finance has been provided by direct lender Beach Point Capital UK and will help the company expand its operations and pursue further opportunities.

Launched in 2021, Intelligent Business Water Ltd, which trades as Intelligent Water, operates in Scotland’s non-household water retail market and serves nearly 4,000 customer supply points.

The company has reached annual turnover of approximately £8 million and aims to increase this to around £18 million over the next five years.

Thomas McDade, director at Intelligent Water, said: "Securing this £3.5 million facility from Beach Point represents an important milestone for Intelligent Water and demonstrates the confidence in both our business model and our growth ambitions.

"Beach Point built a strong understanding of our business and was able to tailor a funding package aligned to our needs.

"This facility gives us significant additional headroom and flexibility to accelerate our growth and pursue further opportunities as they arise.

"We have built a strong platform over the last five years, and this funding gives us the financial capacity to take Intelligent Water through its next phase of growth."

Seth Vaughan, vice president at Beach Point Capital, said: "Intelligent Water has established a strong position in Scotland’s business water market, underpinned by an experienced management team and a compelling growth strategy.

"We are pleased to provide this financing facility to support the company as it continues to expand its customer base and capitalise on the opportunities available in the market."

Adam Kelly, managing director, Debt & Regional Funds, British Business Bank, said: "We're pleased to see Intelligent Water receive this funding, which will support it in its delivery of its growth plans and build on the strong progress it has already made in Scotland's business water market.

"It is with the British Business Bank backing funds such as those managed by Beach Point, that growing businesses like Intelligent Water can increasingly access the finance they need to invest and scale."
