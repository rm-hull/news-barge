A new three-storey teaching block and sports centre extension has been completed at a Newton Aycliffe school.

Greenfield Academy's new education hub includes 19 classrooms, as well as specialist facilities for science, IT, art and design technology.

A sports centre extension has also been completed and the academy will open for community use outside school hours.

The academy will be able to accommodate up to 750 students in the upgraded facilities.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675166.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Representatives from Greenfield Academy, Durham County Council and Esh Construction mark the completion of the academy's new education hub and sports centre extension. *(Image: Graham Brown)*

Construction firm Esh Construction was appointed by Durham County Council to deliver the project, which replaces the academy's previous school building and forms part of wider efforts to modernise the site.

Grant Watson, construction director at Esh Construction, said the project would provide an environment where students can thrive.

He said: "We are incredibly proud to have completed the construction of the new school building and sports centre extension at Greenfield Academy.

"This project has allowed us to bring our expertise in delivering high-quality educational facilities to County Durham, delivering inspiring spaces where students can thrive now and in the future."

The ground floor includes four classrooms and a design and technology department, while the first floor houses 12 classrooms, staff facilities and meeting rooms.

A dedicated science department occupies the second floor, featuring laboratories, science studios and preparation areas.

James Keating, executive headteacher at Greenfield Academy, said pupils were excited to move into the new building.

He said: "We are absolutely delighted to be taking ownership of our new building, and the children are incredibly excited to begin enjoying the fantastic new facilities it provides.

"The academy now has the high-quality learning environment our children deserve, and these facilities will play a vital role in supporting both the school and the wider community for many years to come."

Alongside the new build, Esh has completed refurbishment works to existing parts of the school, including upgrades to music and drama classrooms, the main hall, reception, dining areas and kitchen facilities.

A three-month programme to demolish the former school buildings is set to begin next, before landscaping works are carried out across the site in early 2027.
