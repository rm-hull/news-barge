A NEW girls’ football programme is being launched to boost participation and break down barriers to sport.

The Priestley Academy Trust in Bradford will kick off the initiative as part of its 10th-anniversary celebrations.

The scheme, which starts on November 10, will bring together 72 girls from Years 5 and 6 across the Trust’s six primary schools for a seven-week development programme.

Waqqas Akhtar, a class teacher at Westbourne Primary School, said: "Research has frequently highlighted inequalities in sporting participation among girls, particularly those from underrepresented ethnic minority backgrounds, including South Asian communities.

"In response to Bradford’s diverse population, The Priestley Girls Football Programme aims to break down barriers to organised sport."

The programme will also connect pupils with grassroots teams to encourage continued involvement beyond school.

Representatives from the Football Association will also be invited to celebrate the initiative and support the development of further local pathways for girls in the sport.

A celebratory tournament will take place on January 12, 2027, with all participants receiving medals and winners awarded trophies.

Mathew Atkinson, CEO of The Priestley Academy Trust, said: "As we celebrate ten years of The Priestley Academy Trust, we wanted to launch our anniversary year with an initiative that perfectly encapsulates our commitment to inclusion, opportunity, and holistic development."
