Overnight closures will be in place on the A59 at Kex Gill as North Yorkshire Council work on the tie-ins between the new road and the existing A59.

This is an important milestone in completing the remaining connections and fully opening this section of road between York and Lancashire.

Allow extra time for journeys and follow the signed diversion.

The programme is weather-dependent and dates may need to change at short notice if conditions are unsuitable.

**Friday 6 November:** Church Hill is due to reopen.**Monday 9 November to Friday 13 November:** The A59 will be closed each night from 7pm to 6am between Bolton Abbey roundabout and Menwith Hill crossroads. A signed diversion will be via Ilkley and Otley. Local access will be maintained where possible, but the route available to individual properties will change as work moves between locations. Local residents affected have been sent further details.**Monday 16 November to Thursday 19 November:** Church Hill and Hall Lane junctions with the A59 will be closed during the day for final surfacing. The A59 will remain open to vehicles during this work.**Friday 20 November to Friday 27 November:** The A59 will be closed each night from 7pm to 6am between Bolton Abbey roundabout and Menwith Hill crossroads. A signed diversion will be in place via Ilkley and Otley.
