The previous cap has been suspended until further notice, announced Integration Senator Cancel Kiziltepe (SPD). According to Kiziltepe, the Berlin Refugee Council had objected to the 50-euro cash limit with her administration, arguing that it placed users at a significant disadvantage. The SPD politician said she could see the merit in this argument.
