North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a Range Rover Evoque was stolen from a property in Ripon.

The incident happened at around 7.10pm on Wednesday, November 4 at an address on Knaresborough Road.

If you saw anything suspicious in the area or have CCTV, doorbell or dash cam footage that may help the police with their investigation, you should get in touch.

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260217445 when providing any details regarding the incident.
