REMBERANCE services will take place across the district to honour those who lost their lives in conflict.

The Bradford district will come together on Remembrance Sunday and Armistice Day to pay tribute to the fallen and reflect on the impact of war worldwide.

Bradford’s main service will take place at the Cenotaph on Remembrance Sunday, with participants asked to gather in Centenary Square at 10.15am for a civic parade departing City Hall at 10.30am.

Councillor Chris Herd, Lord Mayor of Bradford, said: "The annual services of Remembrance in our district are always moving events.

"It is important that we continue to remember all those who have lost their lives, have been wounded, have suffered or are still suffering in any way, due to past and present conflicts around the world.

"The services are open to all, and I’d like to invite everyone from across the district to join us at the Cenotaph or to attend any of the services taking place in communities across the district."

Revd Canon Jonathan Triffitt, interim dean of Bradford, will begin the Act of Remembrance with "The Gathering" at 10.55am, followed by a two-minute silence at 11am.

The service will be led by The Venerable Dr Andy Jolley and will include representatives from the Royal British Legion, Bradford Cathedral, and other faith communities.

Wreaths will be laid at the Cenotaph, with arrangements coordinated through the Lord Mayor’s office.

At the end of the service, the Lord-Lieutenant of West Yorkshire, the Lord Mayor, and a representative from the Bradford Central Branch of the Royal British Legion will take the salute during the march past in City Park.

The ceremony will conclude with the traditional Sunset Ceremony.

In Keighley, a parade will depart Russell Street at 9.35am, leading to a service at Keighley Shared Parish Church at 10am.

This will be followed by a procession to the war memorial in Keighley Town Hall Square for an Act of Remembrance at 10.50am.

More than 30 services are scheduled across the Bradford district on Remembrance Sunday.

Details are available on the Bradford Council website.

On Armistice Day, a short service will be held at the Bradford Cenotaph at 11am, attended by the Lord Mayor.

To mark the occasions, the City Hall clock tower will be illuminated in poppy red on the evenings of both Remembrance Sunday and Armistice Day.

The Royal British Legion flag and Union Flags will fly in Bradford city centre, and Union Flags will also fly across the district.

**Roads closed**

A number of roads will be closed for upcoming remembrance services.

The announced road closures will facilitate a series of services across the district.

In Addingham, Church Street and Main Street will be closed from 11.30am to 12.30pm.

In Baildon, roads including Hawksworth Road, Moorgate, Northgate, Browgate, and Baildon Road will be closed from 2pm to 3.30pm.

Bingley will see Main Street, Myrtle Place, Myrtle Grove, Airedale Street, and Thrift Way closed from 10am to 1pm.

Bradford will have closures on Norfolk Gardens, Channing Way, Prince’s Way, Little Horton Lane, Morley Street, Godwin Street, Manchester Road, Thornton Road, Great Horton Road, and Chester Street from 10am to 1pm.

In Cullingworth, Halifax Road will be closed from 10.30am to 11.30am.

Eldwick will have Otley Road and Saltaire Road closed from 10.30am to 11.30am.

Greengates will see Harrogate Road and New Line closed from 10.30am to 11.30am.

In Haworth, Station Road, Bridgehouse Lane, and Brow Road will be closed from 11.30am to 12.30pm.

Ilkley will have Whitton Croft Road, Chantry Drive, Station Road, The Grove, Grove Road, and Bolton Bridge Road closed from 2pm to 5pm.

Keighley will see Russell Street, Cooke Street, Lawkholme Crescent, North Street, Church Street, Bow Street, Cavendish Street, and High Street closed from 9am to 12noon.

In Oakworth, Chapel Lane will be closed from 10.30am to 11.30am.

Queensbury will have Albert Crescent, Sharket Head Close, Albert Road, High Street, Granby Street, West End, and Russell Hall Lane closed from 10am to 1pm.

In Wibsey, Mount Road will be closed from 9.30am to 12.30pm.

Wilsden will see Main Street closed from 10.45am to 11.15am.

The closures will ensure the remembrance services proceed smoothly and safely.
