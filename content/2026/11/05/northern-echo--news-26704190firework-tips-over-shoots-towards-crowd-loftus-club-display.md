A lit firework tipped over and shot towards crowds at a Bonfire Night display.

Families enjoying a display at The Loftus Club were left terrified when the final firecracker came firing towards them.

Video shared on social media shows the rocket burst along an alley leading to the club off West Road, Loftus, where families with young children were standing.

In a statement the club said: “Towards the end of the display, the final firework malfunctioned and tipped over during launch.

“We are deeply sorry this happened and for the distress it caused.

“We worked hard to make sure the display could go ahead. We recognise that fireworks displays carry risks and that once a firework has launched, we cannot control its path.

“Following this incident, we will carefully consider whether to hold future fireworks displays at the club.”

The club added it was “truly devastated” and that a “trained pyrotechnic” set the fireworks off.

The free event, which kicked off at 5.45pm, was billed as a Bonfire Night Party with Minnie Mouse and other children’s characters.
