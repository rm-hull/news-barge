A Harrogate nursery has introduced a new initiative to support children and families connected to the Armed Forces, recognising the challenges that military life can bring.

Partou Tiny Teapots Day Nursery and Pre-school in Harrogate has developed **Little Journeys, Strong Connections** to help families manage experiences including deployment, separation, relocation and changes in routine.

Nursery manager Claire Martin said the setting has a significant number of Armed Forces families within its nursery community.

The initiative was developed around a simple principle of listening to families rather than making assumptions about the support they need.

> **Claire Martin, Nursery Manager**
>
> I have been meeting and having conversations with our Service families to understand their experiences, what we already do well and what we could do differently to support them.

Parents are actively involved in developing the programme, with Service families visiting the nursery to talk to staff about military life, the impact of separation on children and how early years practitioners can provide appropriate support.

The nursery has also assessed staff knowledge and confidence, identifying areas for further training. This includes Service family life, deployment and separation, children’s emotional wellbeing, transitions and maintaining family connections.

Partou Tiny Teapots is also developing a professional partnership with Stephanie, the Service Children’s Champion for North Yorkshire, who has visited the nursery to discuss how she could support the team and its developing programme.

Staff are using books and other resources to help children maintain connections with family members who are away. These include family photographs, drawings and conversations to help children express their feelings.

Another important part of the initiative is encouraging Armed Forces families to build relationships and support networks with one another.

The nursery has created opportunities for families to offer practical help, with posters encouraging simple acts of support such as offering lifts, helping with washing, sharing practical assistance or simply meeting for a cup of tea.

> **Claire Martin, Nursery Manager**
>
> For a family managing a deployment, exercise, relocation or a period of parenting alone, sometimes it is these small acts of connection and understanding from somebody who genuinely ‘gets it’ that can make a difference.

Families are beginning to develop their own informal support networks, with the nursery providing a familiar place where relationships can grow.

Claire said the programme would continue to develop, with the team listening to families, working with specialists, developing staff knowledge and evaluating the impact of its approach.

> **Claire Martin, Nursery Manager**
>
> Ultimately, our ambition is simple: Wherever a child’s journey takes them, we will help them feel connected, understood and that they belong.

Laura Clay, Partou Regional Childcare Director, praised the work of Claire and the nursery team.

> **Laura Clay, Partou Regional Childcare Director**
>
> It is a deeply impressive programme which illustrates how closely aligned the team is to the families and how focussed they are in delivering the best possible nursery experience for them.

She said the benefits had extended beyond the nursery, with friendships and stronger bonds developing between the families themselves.
