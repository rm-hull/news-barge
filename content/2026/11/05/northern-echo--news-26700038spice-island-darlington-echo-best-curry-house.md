A Darlington takeaway that has been serving curries for almost 35 years has been shortlisted for The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Spice Island has been a long-standing feature of Surtees Street since Zitu Ali and his family opened the doors in 1992.

The now-60-year-old, who works as a chef in the venue, said the takeaway is famous in town and continues to attract loyal customers each week.

It is fair to say things have changed quite significantly for the venue since the early 90s, as hungry punters swap walking in for walking to the front door.

But business is still going strong - and Spice Island is now in the running for the Echo’s Best Curry House competition this year.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21671892.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Zitu Ali at Spice Island in Darlington *(Image: CONTRIBUTOR)*

“Business is good,” Zitu, whose son Imran owns the takeaway, said.

“We don’t get the same amount in the shop as we did 35 years ago because most of the people sit at home as they have Uber Eats, Just Eat, and Darlo Eats,” he said.

“Not many people come inside but business is still very good. We are 35 years old but we are doing well - it is the good food and the nice service.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21671893.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Spice Island in Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

“This is the way people see it.”

Dad-of-six Zitu, who has lived in Darlington for almost 40 years, said just some of the most popular dishes include the chicken tikka masala, the mint chicken tikka, and the jalfrezi.

He said it was nice to be shortlisted for the award and be recognised by customers for their food and service. “I hope that is why we have been nominated,” he said.

Spice Island currently boasts a 3.8 star rating on Google, with reviews praising its “second to none food” and “lovely, friendly service”.

One person said: “Excellent food, taste, and quality. Food always arrives with a smile and is hot. Definitely give them a try, I have never been disappointed.”

Spice Island is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Babul’s, in Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.

Readers will decide the winner in a public vote, and we will be featuring each of the businesses nominated in the next two weeks.

Votes will be counted soon after voting closes.
