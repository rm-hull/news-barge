# Watch: 'We've hit the jackpot' - Police uncover suspected heroin hidden under car seat in Sheffield

## A Sheffield police officer declared they had "hit the jackpot" after discovering a hidden stash of suspected Class A drugs during a traffic stop.

Officers from South Yorkshire Police were on mobile patrol on the A57 near Moss Way in Sheffield last week when they spotted a Citroen C3 overtaking another vehicle and continuing at speed.

Police instructed the driver to stop and, after spotting drug-related paraphernalia inside the vehicle, carried out a search.

During the search, an officer discovered a bag of suspected heroin concealed underneath the driver's seat.

![Two men were arrested following the discovery](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/05/11/36/still_36s.jpeg?trim=505,0,695,0&width=640&quality=65)


Reacting to the discovery, the officer said: "I think we've just hit the jackpot under the seat."

Officers also recovered a bag containing hundreds of pounds in cash from the vehicle.

Two men were arrested following the discovery.

A 40-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply Class A drugs, driving while unfit through drink or drugs and driving while disqualified.

A 20-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply Class A drugs and possession of Class B or C drugs.

Both men have since been released on bail pending further enquiries.
