A NURSERY group has become the largest in the UK to earn a top first aid accreditation.

Partou, which operates more than 100 settings across the country including three in Bradford, has achieved Tigerlily Training’s Platinum Accreditation.

The accreditation recognises excellence in child safety and emergency preparedness and is the highest distinction given by Tigerlily.

Karl Cobane, learning and development manager at Partou, said: "We’re thrilled that all Partou nurseries have been awarded Tigerlily’s Platinum Accreditation.

"It’s a testament to our teams and the strong bond we’ve established with Tigerlily in delivering the best possible first aid training."

Earlier this year, Partou named Tigerlily as its approved provider for paediatric first aid training.

Tigerlily Training, established in 2004, trains more than 100,000 learners each year through a network of more than 120 venues.

Partou staff also have access to online refresher training and families have been given the chance to learn basic first aid through sessions led by Tigerlily Training professionals.

Kirsty Jackson, co-managing director at Partou, said: "Congratulations to our UK teams on making Partou the largest day nursery provider to achieve Tigerlily Training Platinum Accreditation across every setting.

"The safety and wellbeing of the children in our care will always be our top priority."
