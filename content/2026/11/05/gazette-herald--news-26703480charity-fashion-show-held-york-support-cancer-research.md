A CHARITY fashion show has been held in support of cancer research and in memory of those affected by the disease.

Yorkshire Cancer Research hosted the event yesterday (November 4) at its new superstore in York, with 15 people from across the region taking to the catwalk in outfits sourced from the charity’s shops.

Each model represented the statistic that every quarter of an hour, someone in Yorkshire is told they have cancer.

As reported by The Press yesterday, the show marked the launch of the charity’s Christmas campaign and featured themed catwalks and speeches celebrating the donors, volunteers, and supporters who help fund research and services across the region.

Christine Talbot, former ITV presenter and breast cancer survivor, hosted the event.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21676486.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Former ITV presenter Christine Talbot, who also has experience of cancer, hosted the evening *(Image: Jonathan Pow)*

Ms Talbot said: "When I was asked to host the Yorkshire Cancer Research fashion show, I was delighted. At this time of year, family and community take on even greater importance, and events like this are a wonderful reminder of the strength and generosity of people across Yorkshire.

"I'm proud to support a charity that is helping bring life-saving breakthroughs to Yorkshire."

The show also highlighted the environmental benefits of shopping second-hand, emphasising that each purchase helps reduce waste while raising money for vital cancer research.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21676485.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Yorkshire coast-based businesswoman Tina Boden and Transformational Stylist Jackie Crawford carefully curated every pre-loved outfit *(Image: Jonathan Pow)*

Those taking part in the show include individuals rebuilding confidence after cancer, people currently undergoing treatment, families marking milestones, and those remembering loved ones.

Yorkshire Cancer Research has invested £99 million in research and services, with more information on their website: www.yorkshirecancerresearch.org.uk
