President Donald Trump has argued that Alaska should hand over votes cast for the independent Senate candidate Dan Sullivan to the Republican incumbent with the same name as the state continues to count its ballots.

Desperate for some good news after losing control of the House and Senate in a midterms wipeout Tuesday, the president took to Truth Social to argue: “The vote totals for the Fake Dan Sullivan, plus the other ‘slug’ that the Dems put on the Ballot, should be immediately given to the REAL Dan Sullivan, and the Election is over – He easily wins. There is no other fair solution!”

In another Senate race where votes are still being counted – Mississippi – Democrats believe they could be on course for at least one last major upset, with candidate Scott Colom in with a chance of unseating GOP incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith in the traditionally deep red southern state.

As the election fallout continues, Vice President JD Vance has reportedly raged in private about Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu dragging the U.S. into the war with Iran that cost them so badly at the polls.

## Defeated Republican claims ‘not even Jesus could have won his midterms race’

New York Republican Rep. Mike Lawler has claimed that not even Jesus Christ could have won re-election in his district after losing his seat in Tuesday’s disaster for the GOP in the midterms.

Lawler, 40, lost his re-election bid to Democrat Cait Conley in the Big Apple’s 17th congressional district, covering the Hudson Valley, picking up 47.8 percent of the vote to her 52.1 percent.

He subsequently told CNN reporter Manu Raju that he “ran as good a campaign as he possibly could” but that “the bottom fell out.”

Recounting their conversation subsequently on air with anchor Anderson Cooper, Raju added: “And when I asked him more about that, he said, ‘Jesus Christ could have run and would have lost.’”
