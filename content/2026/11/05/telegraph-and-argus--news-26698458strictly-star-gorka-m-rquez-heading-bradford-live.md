A FESTIVE dance show packed with glitz, glamour and Christmas sparkle is heading to the stage.

Xmas on the Dancefloor, a new touring production featuring ballroom, Latin, contemporary, and commercial dance, will arrive at Bradford Live on December 6, 2026.

The show promises "This ultimate festive dance spectacular for all ages" and will feature a special guest appearance from Strictly Come Dancing star Gorka Márquez.

Mr Márquez said: "It’s a big show with a lot happening on stage and I’m excited to be a little part of it.

"Perhaps as the cherry on the top."

He confirmed the show will feature classic Christmas songs and joyful routines.

He said: "With it being a Christmas show, I would 100% love to go very golden era, more of those kind of quicksteps and American Smooth, imagine a bit of Gene Kelly... perhaps a good Jive.

"Something really Christmassy and joyful."

The cast will perform to live vocals, bringing festive favourites to life with a mix of dance and song.

Mr Márquez said: "The dances will follow the music and we’re going to be performing to some of the iconic Christmas songs, which will be sung live, Michael Bublé and Mariah Carey – all the hits.

"And there are some Spanish Christmas songs I would love to perform to as well."

The show features a full cast of singers and dancers, including the "Xmas Dolls," a Rockettes-inspired troupe, alongside world-class performers.

The production will feature a soundtrack of festive hits and pop classics, including All I Want for Christmas, Last Christmas, Underneath the Tree, Feliz Navidad, and Carol of the Bells.

A spokesperson said: "Xmas on the Dancefloor is a brand-new Christmas spectacular combining Strictly-style ballroom and Latin with commercial and contemporary dance, live vocals and some of the biggest festive hits of all time."

Mr Márquez added: "There’s plenty of chances to join in and sing along, to get you into the spirit of Christmas.

"It will be a really uplifting show full of glitter, glitz and Christmas magic, and I hope our audiences will leave full of nostalgia and a lot of joy and happiness."

The show opens at Blackpool Opera House on December 5 before beginning its UK tour.
