RUGBY fans could score a free pint during the Nations Championship just by making a correct match prediction.

Admiral Taverns pubs across Yorkshire, including the Crown Hotel and Malt Kiln Inn in Bradford, are taking part in the promotion, which runs until November 29 and is backed by Heineken UK.

Customers can take part by logging into the pub’s guest Wi-Fi and predicting the winner of the next Nations Championship match.

Those who guess correctly will receive a voucher for a free pint, redeemable 30 minutes after the final whistle.

A spokesperson said: "It’s a winning combination: international rugby, a little friendly rivalry and your fantastic local pub."

The offer is part of a wider campaign across 31 Admiral Taverns pubs in Yorkshire.

Vouchers are valid for seven days after the match and are delivered directly to the winner’s email inbox.
