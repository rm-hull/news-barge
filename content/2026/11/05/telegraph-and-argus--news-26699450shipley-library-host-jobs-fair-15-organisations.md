A JOBS and training fair will take place to connect jobseekers with new opportunities.

The free event, organised by the SkillsHouse Employment and Skills Team and the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP), will be held at Shipley Library on Wednesday, November 11, from 10am to 2pm.

Attendees will have the chance to meet employers, explore vacancies, and learn about training options in person.

Councillor Andrew Judson, Bradford Council’s lead member for economy, regeneration and planning, said: "This event will provide specialist advice for anyone looking for support on their next career steps, or information on training.

"The event will welcome all in a supportive environment with the aim of offering expert guidance to really make a difference to people, whatever their individual situation."

Around 15 organisations are expected to take part, offering guidance and support to people at all stages of their employment journey.

Smart Works Yorkshire, which supports unemployed women with interview preparation and confidence building, will be among the organisations in attendance.

The library will also be offering a selection of ties to those in need, who may be attending interviews, and children are welcome.

Attendees can also join the library and borrow books for their children.

No booking is required to attend.
