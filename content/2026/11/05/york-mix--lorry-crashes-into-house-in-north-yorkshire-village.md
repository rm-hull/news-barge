Emergency services are at the scene of a crash in North Yorkshire this morning (Thursday).

A lorry has hit a house.

Fire crews were called to **Coulton Lane, Coulton**, between Easingwold and Helmsley, at** 6.25am**.

#### Coulton Lane

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Helmsley mobilised to an HGV that had crashed on a sharp bend and gone into a residential property.

“This incident is still ongoing.”
