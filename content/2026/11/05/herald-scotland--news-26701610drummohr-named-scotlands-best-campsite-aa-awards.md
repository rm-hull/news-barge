A five-star camping and glamping site near Edinburgh has been named the best campsite in Scotland after winning a prestigious national tourism award.

Drummohr Camping & Glamping Site, near Musselburgh, has been crowned AA Scotland Campsite of the Year 2026/27 in the AA's annual Caravan and Camping Awards, which recognise the UK's leading campsites and caravan parks.

The accolade is the latest in a string of successes for the award-winning site, which has previously been named the UK's top campsite by Camping magazine and Scotland's best campsite by outdoor accommodation booking platform Pitchup.com.

Drummohr has also won Camping Site of the Year at the Scottish Hospitality Awards for the past two years.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21673796.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Glamping pods at Drummohr Camping and Glamping Site *(Image: AA)*

Site manager Angie Purves said the team was "absolutely delighted" to receive the latest honour.

She said: "AA Scotland Campsite of the Year is a very prestigious award in our industry, so we're absolutely delighted to be named the best in the country.

"We're very lucky to be in such a beautiful location close to the coast and just eight miles from Edinburgh, and it's wonderful to see the care we put into the site recognised in this way.

"It is fantastic recognition for the whole team, who work incredibly hard to make every guest's stay special. Our core focus is to create memorable moments for every guest, whatever the weather, through experiences that turn every stay into lasting memories.

"When guests check in, we give them recommendations for attractions and restaurants, as well as information on transport links. We offer that in different languages. I think that kind of attention to detail makes a real difference.

"We welcome people from across the UK and around the world, and it's lovely to see so many of them return year after year."

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21673795.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hot tub at one of the luxury lodges at Drummohr *(Image: AA)*

Open throughout the year, Drummohr is a five-star-rated site accredited by both the AA and VisitScotland. It offers a range of accommodation, including luxury self-catering lodges with hot tubs, glamping pods, en-suite bothies, touring pitches and a self-catering cottage.

The family-friendly attraction is known for its themed wizard, fairy and adventure glamping pods, while facilities also include family bothies, a large playground, a scenic dog-walking route and pet-friendly accommodation options.

The winners of the AA Caravan and Camping Awards 2026/27 were announced during an online ceremony on November 4, coinciding with the publication of the 2027 AA Caravan & Camping Guide. Winning sites are selected by AA inspectors in recognition of outstanding quality standards and excellent customer care.

Drummohr is one of three camping and glamping businesses operated by employee-owned WCF Ltd.

Its sister sites include Longnor Wood in the Peak District and Herding Hill Farm near Hadrian's Wall in Northumberland. Longnor Wood was named AA Heart of England Campsite of the Year in the AA's Caravan and Camping Awards 2024/25.
