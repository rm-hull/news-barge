A worker has died after an incident at a major manufacturing plant in Sunderland.

Emergency services were called to the Liebherr factory on Wellington Lane, in the Deptford area of the city, on Tuesday, October 27 where a worker had suffered serious injuries.

The 52-year-old man was pronounced dead at the scene.

The Crane manufacturer confirmed the man was an employee at the Sunderland site.

In a statement, the company said: "We can confirm that on Tuesday, October 27, a tragic accident occurred at our Liebherr site in Sunderland, resulting in the death of a 52-year-old employee.

"We deeply regret this tragic incident. Our thoughts and sincere sympathies are with the victim's family members and loved ones."

The company added that the circumstances remained unclear and that it was "fully cooperating with the relevant authorities" to provide "all possible assistance to support their work".

A joint investigation is taking place by Northumbria Police and the Health and Safety Executive (HSE).

A police spokesperson said: "On Tuesday, October 27, we received a report that a man had been injured at an industrial premises on Wellington Lane in the Deptford area of Sunderland.

"Emergency services attended and a man sustained serious injuries.
