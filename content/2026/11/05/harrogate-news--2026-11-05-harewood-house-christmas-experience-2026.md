Harewood House will open its 2026 Christmas experience to the public on Friday 6 November, with rooms across the historic house being used for projected displays, installations and stories based on its history.

Called *Magic at the Mansion*, the experience has been created with visual artists Studio McGuire, who have worked with Harewood on previous Christmas installations.

The production uses 16 projectors and 14 screens, with 40 Christmas trees placed around the house and grounds.

Among the displays, the Music Room features projected singing candles, while the State Dining Room includes characters inspired by mythology. In the library, projected conversations between chairs and a lamp refer to people associated with Harewood’s history, including architect Charles Barry and painter JMW Turner.

## Royal Christmas lists on display

The experience also draws on material from Harewood’s archives. Visitors will be able to see handwritten Christmas gift lists belonging to Princess Mary, daughter of King George V and Queen Mary.

The lists offer an insight into royal gift-giving, including both personal presents and more everyday items such as soap, tea cloths and spectacle cases.

Other objects on display include a Cartier owl given to Princess Mary by her mother, Queen Mary.

The house’s volunteers have also made decorations for the event, including more than 100 paper flowers, more than 100 hessian flowers and 100 tassel decorations.

## Elf Village planned for December

A new Elf Village will open on Harewood’s North Lawn during the first weekend of December. The attraction is being developed with Enchantica’s and YurtBar.

Families will be able to visit themed areas including an Elf Post Office, the Wishery and a Tinker Tent, before a Santa School finale and a meeting with Father Christmas.

The Elf Village experience is expected to last almost two hours and includes admission to Harewood House, Gardens and Grounds.

Zoë Hughes, Head of Public Programme at Harewood, said the Christmas programme was intended to give visitors further opportunities to explore stories connected with the house and its collections.

*Magic at the Mansion* opens on Friday 6 November 2026 and runs until Sunday 3 January 2027.

## Visitor information

* **Venue:** Harewood House, Leeds, LS17 9LG
* **Dates:** 6 November 2026 to 3 January 2027
* **Adult tickets:** From £24.50
* **Children’s tickets:** From £10
* **Under-fours and Harewood members:** Free entry

Concessionary tickets are also available, including £3 tickets for people receiving certain benefits. Further details and booking information are available from the Harewood House website.
