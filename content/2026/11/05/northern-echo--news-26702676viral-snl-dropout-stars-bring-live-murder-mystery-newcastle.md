A one-of-a-kind murder mystery show will land in the North East next year on the back of a sold-out run in Los Angeles.

Newcastle's Tyne Theatre will host Denouement next May during the show's first UK tour.

An improvised murder mystery, the show was created by British-American comedian Siobhan Thompson alongside Alex Fernie, known for his work on HBO's Last Week Tonight and the sketch show Key & Peele.

It creates a new detective story from scratch on stage at every performance, with each night featuring a different detective, group of suspects and murder mystery.

Every story is performed for the first and only time.

The Newcastle show will take place on May 30 and the tour includes dates in Bristol, Birmingham, Brighton and London.

While she may not be a household name to mainstream TV audiences, Denouement's creator Siobhan Thompson is a major figure in the online comedy world.

Thompson has also written for comedy shows including Rick and Morty, and Krapopolis.

In Newcastle, she will be joined by performers from comedy hits including Dropout, Smosh and Saturday Night Live.

The cast includes Vic Michaelis, Dhruv Uday Singh, former Saturday Night Live performer John Milhiser and Kimia Behpoornia, who has appeared in Abbott Elementary, Atypical, Lucifer and Hacks.

Tickets are now available through Live Nation.
