Police are appealing for information after an assault during a fight in Pickering.

The incident took place around 3pm on Tuesday, October 27, on the A169 Whitby Road, outside a care home for older people.

It involved a group of teenage girls, and the victim sustained minor injuries.

North Yorkshire Police said: "We’re particularly appealing for information about the member of the public that intervened and the two other members of the public who assisted the victim back to her home address, as they may be able to assist with our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC 1054 Nate Mills by email at [nate.mills@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:nate.mills@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, information can be submitted online via the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting 'Tell us about' and then 'An existing case or report.'

Those unable to use these options can call 101.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
