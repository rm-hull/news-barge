A full calendar of festive events is coming to a North East town this Christmas.

Arts Centre Washington has announced a packed programme of seasonal entertainment, including live performances, family activities, film screenings, and more throughout November and December.

The programme kicks off with the Christmas Craft and Makers Fair on Saturday, November 21, a free event from 10am to 3pm featuring stalls, festive performances, and craft activities.

Matt Blyth, general manager at Arts Centre Washington, said: "We’re delighted to be bringing such a varied Christmas programme to Washington this year.

"From our popular craft fair and fantastic local performers to family shows, live music, classic stories and festive film screenings, there really is something for everyone to enjoy."

The centre will also host Deepest Fears on Thursday, November 26 at 7.30pm, an evening of ghost stories for audiences aged 14 and over.

A Christmas Cabaret follows on Thursday, December 3 at 7pm, featuring music and dance from local performers.

Film fans can enjoy a screening of Last Christmas with the option of afternoon tea on Thursday, December 10 at 1pm.

Younger audiences are invited to The Littlest Elf on Friday, December 11, with performances at 10.30am and 1.30pm.

Music lovers can catch Washington singer-songwriter Callum O’Neil performing Almost A Decade at 7.30pm the same evening, supported by Kayden Turner and Daniel Maple.

The programme concludes on Saturday, December 19 at 1pm, with a screening of The Muppets Christmas Carol and a puppet-making workshop.

For tickets and the full programme, visit sunderlandculture.org.uk/arts-centre-washington.
