# DWP confirms households set to lose £300 after HMRC income check

## The Department for Work and Pensions has confirmed the payments will be recouped by HMRC under the Labour Party government's policy

The Department for Work and Pensions has confirmed that Winter Fuel Allowances will be clawed back by HMRC under the current Labour government's policy. Labour has reinstated DWP Winter Fuel Payments following widespread anger over an eligibility shake-up introduced by former Chancellor Rachel Reeves, reports Birmingham Live.

Gordon Wallis, energy expert at Your NRG, said: "The Winter Fuel Payment can provide valuable support for pensioners facing higher heating bills, but it's important that people understand the income rules before relying on this money.

"Some households may see the payment arrive in their bank account and assume they can put it straight towards their winter energy costs. However, if their individual income is above £35,000, they could end up having to repay the full amount through the tax system.

"It's particularly important for couples to understand that the threshold applies individually, not to their combined household income."

Gordon said: "The most important thing is to understand exactly what support you're entitled to and plan your winter energy spending accordingly. By combining any available financial support with a few practical changes around the home, pensioners could make their heating budgets stretch further and stay more comfortable throughout the colder months."

Those born on or before 27 June 1960 who resided in England or Wales during the qualifying week may be entitled to receive a payment. The qualifying week commences on Monday 21 September 2026. A Winter Fuel Payment amounts to either £200 or £300 per household.

Those aged 80 or above will receive the higher sum. The DWP confirmed: "You will get a letter in October or November explaining what the total amount is and how it will be paid. Your automatic payment will go into the same account as your Pension or other benefits. Payments will be made from mid-November 2026 onwards. Most eligible people will get paid automatically without having to make a claim."

Should your total annual income exceed £35,000, HMRC will reclaim your Winter Fuel Payment via the tax system. The DWP confirmed that HMRC assesses the income of each member of a household on an individual basis.
