Action could soon be coming to two trouble hotspots in York.

Plans for extra police powers to crack down on antisocial behaviour are to be considered for **Acomb Front Street** and**Haxby**.

Both areas are being considered for public space protection orders (PSPO), similar to the one introduced for York city centre.

Here, the council has agreed a new PSPO giving police new powers to deal with groups of people shouting, being aggressive or disruptive or engaging in alcohol-fuelled behaviour likely to cause fear, abuse or confrontation.

They also include additional restrictions covering people pressurising people to give them money, food or other donations.

Liberal Democrat councillor for Westfield Ward, Andrew Waller says he’s pleased that the council executive is moving ahead with the plans for a PSPO in Acomb.

He told ***YorkMix***: “A number of residents have approached me to say it’s deterring them from going to the shops.

“Some elderly residents and families feel intimidated by some of the behaviours over the summer.

“Therefore it’s something that people feel, with so much effort having gone into improving the area, this needs to be tackled as well.”
