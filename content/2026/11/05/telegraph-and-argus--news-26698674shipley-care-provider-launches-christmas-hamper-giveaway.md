A Shipley care provider is set to spread some festive cheer this Christmas by giving away five hampers to families struggling over the holiday period.

Home Care Perfection is asking residents to nominate families for hampers packed with food, treats and gifts.

The Shipley company will choose five families at random and deliver the hampers on Christmas Eve.

Director Sohail Bhatti said: "There are a lot of people struggling out there, a lot of families and we'd like to give back to them.

"It's very tough out there for normal families.

“If it puts a smile on someone's face at Christmas, getting small gifts or chocolates or treats or items to prepare a nice Christmas dinner, it's just giving back to the community basically.”

## How will it work?

Residents can nominate a family through the company’s social media channels or by emailing its administration team.

![Food hampers distributed by Home Care Perfection for a previous giveaway.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/13138102.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Food hampers given out by Home Care Perfection for a previous giveaway.

Nominations must include the family’s name, address and telephone number and will be kept confidential.

The closing date for entries is December 20.

Staff will choose five families on December 21 and deliver the hampers on December 24.

Mr Bhatti said the hampers would be assembled at the company's Shipley offices and would include food for Christmas dinner, presents for children, chocolates and other festive treats.

## Founded after father's care experience

The giveaway marks five years since Home Care Perfection launched in 2021.

The business provides care in people’s homes across Baildon, Shipley and Bingley and is commissioned by Bradford Council in Baildon.

It supports people of all ages, including those with disabilities and terminal illnesses who wish to remain in their own homes.

Mr Bhatti said the business was born out of his own experience trying to find suitable care for his father during the Covid-19 pandemic.

"The reason I started the business was because my father in 2021 was receiving care from various care companies and the standard of care was not very good," he said.

"It was Covid and my other business was shut, so I was sat at home and I thought, this is something that I need to do - to set up something which delivers a better standard of care than what my father was receiving."

## 'She's a fantastic manager'

He said the experience shaped the company’s approach, particularly its communication with families.

Praising manager Lynne Holmes, who joined the company in 2023, Mr Bhatti said: "All these initiatives were put in place by Lynne because that's what I was missing back in 2021 and 2022.

"A care provider would come out and set up the contract and start providing care and I would never hear from them again unless I was complaining about something."

He added: "She's a fantastic manager and she's running the service really, really well."
