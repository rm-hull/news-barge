Plans are well advanced to turn an acclaimed series of novels featuring locations across the North Yorkshire into a major television series.

The Moonstone Legacy Quartet, a sequel to Wilkie Collins’ classic The Moonstone, is co-written by Thirsk-based Tony Wild and Diana de Gunzburg.

The four books focus on the adventures of Lizzie Abercrombie, whose life is shattered when her mother dies in a tragic accident on a full moon.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/north-york-moors-moonstone-legacy-quartet.webp)

Lizzie begins to suspect that the death was no accident and finds evidence pointing towards an ancient family curse tied to the Moonstone, a sacred meteoric Indian diamond stolen by her ancestor George Abercrombie.

Diana said: “Determined to uncover the truth, Lizzie’s investigation takes her from Lizzieland – our fictionalised area nestled in the North York Moors, inspired by real locations like Kepwick, Hawnby, and Brimham Rocks – to the Temple of the Moon in India.”

She added: “Our books are very visual, with stunning locations not just in Yorkshire, but across the world, which is why we believe they would make a wonderful television series.

“Plans for this are well-advanced with a script written, a detailed pitch deck prepared for potential investors and a producer lined up. So, watch this space,” said Diana.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/diana-de-gunzburg-moonstone-legacy-quartet.webp)

Here are some of the key North Yorkshire locations in The Moonstone Legacy Quartet, together with their fictional names, if appropriate, in brackets:

* The Cleveland Way (The Drove Road)
* Brimham Rocks (Grimstone Scar)
* Kepwick Hall Estate (Shalimar)
* The Strid
* Thornborough Henge
* Thirsk, including the Ritz Cinema, (Knowlesby)
* The Owl at Hawnby (The Falcon Inn)

Diana said: “There is no doubt that North Yorkshire boasts some of the most stunning and magical landscapes in the whole of the UK and they make the perfect backdrop to Lizzie’s adventures.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/the-owl-pub-hawnby-north-yorkshire-exterior.webp)

“So, we are excited that they will feature again in a major television series, following in the illustrious footsteps of All Creatures Great And Small, Wuthering Heights and Brideshead Revisited.

“They will be alongside other incredible locations in Spain, India and Sri Lanka, but – speaking as proud Yorkshire people – we know they will hold their own.”
