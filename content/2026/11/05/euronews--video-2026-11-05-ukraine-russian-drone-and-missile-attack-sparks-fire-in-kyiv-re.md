Footage from the scene showed **flames engulfing parts of the complex** as crews from across the region were deployed to reinforce the operation, tackling burning structures and stopping the fire spreading. Ukraine's State Emergency Service said the**blaze had been contained** by morning, though firefighters were still putting out hot spots and dismantling damaged structures. Preliminary information indicated**no fatalities**.

The strike came during a wider overnight assault in which **Russia launched 128 attack drones**, 65 of them jet-powered, and two Kh-35 guided air-launched missiles from the Black Sea, the Ukrainian air force said. Air defences** shot down or suppressed 105 drones**, including 48 jet-powered ones, but** recorded impacts at 18 locations**, with debris falling at nine more. The air force did not report intercepting either missile.

Officials did not identify the facility, in a district holding **warehouses, depots and transport hubs that supply Kyiv**. They gave no further detail on the injured man's condition or the extent of the damage.
