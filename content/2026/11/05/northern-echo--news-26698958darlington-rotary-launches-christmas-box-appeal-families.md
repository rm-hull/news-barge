A growing Christmas campaign is making a difference for care leavers and families in Darlington.

Darlington Rotary’s annual “reverse” Advent box scheme asks people to fill boxes with useful and thoughtful items, rather than receiving gifts themselves.

The scheme supports young people leaving the care system, as well as families who have fled domestic abuse.

Last year, 200 boxes were collected, and organisers are hoping for even more this year.

Rotarian and organiser Nigel Little said: "We started collecting boxes four years ago and the numbers have been steadily rising.

"The idea is that instead of taking items, you put items into a box to help those young people and young families leaving the care system, or who may have very little to their names and little Christmas cheer."

Each box contains around £50 worth of everyday items such as toiletries, cereal, and toilet roll, plus a few festive treats.

This year, the group hopes to collect 250 boxes, with some going to the Family Help organisation, which supports women and children escaping abuse.

Boxes will be distributed through Darlington Borough Council’s Looked After Through Care Team and Family Help.

The scheme is supported by Caroline Weaver at Smurfit WestRock Plc and Marford Design and Print.
