*(Image: Ian West)*Irvine Welsh**has penned a new version of the famous Trainspotting poster for Irish rap group Kneecap's upcoming gig in Glasgow.**

The Belfast group released *Fenian* , their second full LP earlier this year and are playing at the OVO Hydro in December.

In support of the show, they have released a new poster, in which author Welsh has provided a new take on his best-selling work.

Revealing the advert, Kneecap said: "10am tomorrow we're releasing 250 production holds of STANDING tickets for our Dec 9th show at OVO Hydro.

"Be FAST.

"And didn't the legend @IrvineWelsh only go and write a few words for us for a wee poster."

**The poster reads:Choose Life.**

Choose your culture and  
expressing it in your own voice,  
in any way that excites your  
imagination.

Choose respecting the culture of  
others, and helping them to do  
the same.

Choose standing up for the  
oppressed against the controllers.

Choose synths over guns. Dance  
over death marches. Rap and  
rhythm over rancid rhetoric. Beats  
over bullets and bombs.

Choose partying on through  
the whingeing noises of the  
elites and their mouthpieces,  
and the online sneers of soul  
dead basement incels.

Choose the incendiary music  
of uplifting rebellion.

Choose Kneecap.

**- Irvine Welsh**

The Herald reported that Welsh was among the signatories of an open letter to the government in support of Sally Rooney.

The Irish author's books have been removed from sale in the UK due to her support for **Palestine Action** .

The group was proscribed as a terror organisation last year and Ms Rooney said she intended to continue using her royalties to support them.

In a statement to **BBC** News, Rooney said further publication of her work could constitute a "serious criminal offence", so she had agreed with her publisher Faber not to continue supplying her books to UK retailers.
