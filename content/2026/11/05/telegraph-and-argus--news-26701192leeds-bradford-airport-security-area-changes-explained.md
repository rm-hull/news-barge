Passengers travelling through Leeds Bradford Airport are being advised of a number of changes after the launch of a brand-new central security area.

The airport has opened its new Central Search Zone, which is now located directly through the main terminal doors.

The facility currently features three security lanes, with plans already in place to expand to five lanes by December.

## What's changed?

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21673389.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new security area at Leeds Bradford Airport *(Image: LBA)*

As part of the changes, passengers no longer need to remove liquids or electronic devices from their hand luggage when passing through security.

However, all liquids must still be in containers of 100ml or less.

The airport has also confirmed that travellers no longer need to place liquids in clear plastic bags before screening.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21673392.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new security area at Leeds Bradford Airport *(Image: LBA)*

Passengers can leave phones, tablets and laptops inside their bags, helping to speed up the security process.

Before entering the screening area, travellers are asked to remove belts and hats and ensure their pockets are completely empty.

Travellers are also reminded to check in and drop off any hold luggage with their airline before entering the new security area.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21673391.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new security area at Leeds Bradford Airport *(Image: LBA)*

## Statement from LBA

In a post on social media, Leeds Bradford Airport said: "Our fab new security area is straight through the main doors (make sure you’ve checked in and dropped off hold luggage with your airline before going through) and has three lanes which will be opening up to five by December!"

The post added: "All liquids must be under 100ml but you can leave them in your hand luggage. No need for a clear plastic bag either.

"Leave all electronics in your bag and remove belts and hats, and make sure your pockets are empty."
