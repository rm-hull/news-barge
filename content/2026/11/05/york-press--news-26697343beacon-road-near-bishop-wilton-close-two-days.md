A road closure has been announced for a defect repair.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council will close a section of Beacon Road, Bishop Wilton, for two days in December.

The affected stretch begins at the junction with A166 Garrowby Hill and extends 50 metres down.

The closure will start on December 1, 2026, at 8am and end on December 2, 2026, at 6pm.

The council stated the closure is necessary to repair a DF06 highway defect and to rectify the reinstatement, ensuring public safety.

An alternative route for affected traffic will be signposted during the closure.

The diversion includes A166 Garrowby Hill, The Bence and Main Road, Millington, Givendale Hill, Swineridge Lane, Grimthorpe Lane, Givendale Road, and Beacon Road.

The order for the closure is valid for 18 months, but the work is expected to be completed in two days.

Despite the closure to vehicles, access will still be maintained for residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians.

The council encourages those needing more information to contact Highways Customer Care on (01482) 393939, quoting reference: PE.

The closure is a temporary prohibition of through traffic, meaning no vehicles will be allowed to travel through the section of Beacon Road while works are ongoing.

The council has highlighted the importance of the works being carried out in a manner that ensures the safety of the public.
