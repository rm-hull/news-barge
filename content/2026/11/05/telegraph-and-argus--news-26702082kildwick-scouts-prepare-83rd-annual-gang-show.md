KILDWICK and Farnhill scouts are preparing to perform their 83rd annual Gang Show.

1st Kildwick and Farnhill Scout Group will be keeping up with tradition and putting on the much-loved, longest performing Gang Show in the country later this month.

More than 90 Beavers, Cubs, Scouts, Explorer Scouts and leaders will take to the stage at Glusburn Community and Arts Centre to entertain their families, friends and the local community.

In 2023, when the group put on its 80th show, the then chief scout, Bear Grylls, sent a message of congratulations.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21674451.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The 1st KIldwick and Farnhill Scouts are to stage their 83rd Gang Show *(Image: 1st Kildwick and Farnhill Scouts)*

The group was formed in 1940 and has held a gang show every year at various venues in South Craven since 1941 with the exception of 2002, and more recently, in 2020 and 2021 because of the coronavirus pandemic.

There will be two chances for families to catch the 83rd show, featuring sketches, comedy and singing, on Friday, September 20, and Saturday, November 21 at Glusburn Community and Arts Centre.

Doors open at 6.45pm and the show starts at 7.15pm.

Tickets are £9.50 for adults, £7 for children, and family tickets are available.

Book at: www.kandfgangshow.org.uk
