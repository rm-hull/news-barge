North Yorkshire Council is inviting views on planning applications covering homes, businesses, flood protection and an electric vehicle charger.

The applications concern locations across the former Ryedale area and are being publicised under planning and listed building legislation.

At 23 Commercial Street, Norton, application ZE26/00479/FUL seeks permission to change the use of the building to a cafe, meeting rooms and retail space.

Internal changes would support the proposed use.

The application is for Eleanor Dunderdale, trading as Distinctively Dunderdale.

A retrospective application, ZE26/00661/FUL, has been submitted for the Blacksmiths Arms, Main Street, Flaxton.

It covers the replacement of the existing commercial kitchen extraction system, including an inlet fan attached to a window cavity and an outlet fan attached to the outside kitchen wall.

The application is for Mr Nicholas Adams, on behalf of The Blacksmiths Arms (Flaxton) Ltd.

At 25 Bridge Street, Helmsley, application ZE26/00793/FUL proposes converting first-floor storage space into two one-bedroom flats and two studio flats.

It also includes changes to the shopfront, including replacing windows and a door with uPVC, widening the entrance and installing an access ramp.

The application is for Mr Jagadeesh Kumar.

Application ZE26/00802/FUL concerns 3 Danby Mews, Wood Street, Norton. It seeks a change of use from one dwelling to two separate dwellings for Mr Casey, on behalf of Kofis Castles Ltd.

Another application concerns a listed building - ZE26/00748/LBC at Ford House, Brookside, Hovingham, and proposes two 600mm-high demountable flood barriers, one at the front door and one at the rear door.

It also includes non-return valves to waste pipework and the rear foul gulley, lime mortar repairs to masonry, and sealing around cable and service entry points.

The application is for Maria Pridden.

At Ivy Cottage, Main Street, Langton, application ZE26/00803/LBC seeks listed building consent to install an electric vehicle charger on a garage wall.

The application is for Mr Timothy James Foster.

The council has also publicised major application 21/01049/73AM at The Showfield, Pasture Lane, Malton.

It seeks to vary condition 21 and remove condition 22 from planning approval 16/00013/MOUT, dated July 21, 2017, to amend noise mitigation requirements for Linden Homes Ltd.

Comments should be sent to Martin Grainger, Head of Development Management, North Yorkshire Council, Ryedale House, Malton, YO17 7HH.

The notice states that if permission is refused for a householder application and an appeal follows, there will be no further opportunity to comment at appeal stage.

The consultation opened on November 4 and closes on November 25. Representations must be made in writing to the council or sent by email to [planning.rye@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:planning.rye@northyorks.gov.uk.)
