A new independent bookshop with a mission to champion indie publishing is opening in North Yorkshire.

Named The Real Indie Bookshop, it will open in Harrogate on Thursday, November 12.

The shop will stock the latest titles across all genres while providing a platform for independently published authors and small presses.

Christina Gabbitas, the award-winning children’s author, also from Harrogate, said: "A family’s financial circumstances should never determine whether a young person gets to experience the magic of choosing and owning a book."

"I can’t wait to open our doors on November 12 and begin welcoming everyone to the Real Indie Bookshop."

The shop will also run a Pay it Forward scheme, inviting customers and local businesses to buy books or gift vouchers for children and young people who might otherwise be unable to afford them.

Ms Gabbitas said the scheme is about more than just donating a book.

She said: "That freedom to choose matters.

"The aim is to help young people discover reading on their own terms, developing on their interests and experiencing the excitement of finding a story that speaks to them."

The Real Indie Bookshop will open at 8am, with coffee available throughout the day and celebrations continuing into the evening.
