A 20-year-old inmate was found unresponsive in his cell at a County Durham prison, with the cause of his death still unknown.

Luke Sharp, from Middlesbrough, died at HMP Deerbolt, in Barnard Castle, on October 1, this year.

An inquest opening heard held at Crook Coroners Court on November 3 heard that Sharp, who was born in 2005, was found unresponsive inside his cell at the prison.

He was identified by a prison officer to a police officer who attended the scene.

A post-mortem examination was carried out by Dr Bolton but a medical cause of death has yet to be established.

The death appears to be unnatural, the hearing was told.

Further investigations are now required to establish the circumstances surrounding Sharp's death.

A full final post-mortem report and a report from the Prisons and Probation Ombudsman (PPO) are also awaited.

The reports are expected to be ready by mid-April, with the final report anticipated 12 weeks afterwards.

The inquest is due to resume on July 20.
