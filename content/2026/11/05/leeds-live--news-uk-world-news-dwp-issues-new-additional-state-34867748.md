# DWP issues new Additional State Pension rates for claimants over 74

## The Department for Work and Pensions has confirmed Additional State Pension rates will rise by 3.5% from next year for eligible claimants aged 74 and over

The Department for Work and Pensions has announced updated Additional Pension rates for recipients commencing next year.

The Additional State Pension provides supplementary income on top of your basic State Pension, available to men born prior to 6 April 1951 or women born before 6 April 1953 - meaning those aged 74 and above by next spring. Eligible individuals receive the Additional State Pension automatically, provided they haven't opted out of the scheme. The payment is made alongside your basic State Pension.

The DWP has confirmed: "Other state pension and benefit rates covered by my statutory review will be increased by 3.5%, in line with the increase in the consumer prices index in the year to September 2026.

"This includes most working-age benefits and other benefits for people below state pension age; benefits to help with additional needs arising from disability; statutory payments including statutory sick pay and statutory maternity pay; and additional state pension. The pension credit savings credit maximum amount will also increase by 3.5 per cent."

The Additional State Pension doesn't have a standard fixed sum. Your entitlement is determined by the number of years you've contributed National Insurance - alongside your earnings history, reports Birmingham Live.

Whether you receive this also hinges on if you opted out of the scheme or if you boosted your basic State Pension (which was only available between 12 October 2015 and 5 April 2017).

The Additional State Pension comprises three separate schemes. You may have paid into more than one, depending on the length of your working life or whether you decided to enhance your State Pension.

These include the second state pension, state pension top-up, and SERPS. There's no need to take any action to claim the Additional State Pension. If you qualify for an Additional State Pension, it will be automatically included when you claim your State Pension.
