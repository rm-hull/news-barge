A Bradford bakery has reopened following a temporary closure for refurbishment.

Jake's Bakehouse, at 95 Thornton Road, announced a "soft launch" on Tuesday, November 4.

In a social media post, the bakery said: “The wait is over!

"We're delighted to announce that Jakes Bakehouse is officially reopening from today.

“Come down and enjoy our delicious selection of freshly baked goods, pastries, cakes and much more. There's something for everyone.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20894557.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jake's Bakehouse on Thornton Road *(Image: T&A)*

The bakery will initially open from 1pm to 11pm.

## Temporary closure earlier this year

Jake's Bakehouse closed in October to allow renovation work to be carried out at the premises.

The business said at the time that the closure was temporary and thanked customers for their patience while improvements were made.

Jake's Bakehouse has built a loyal following on social media, growing its fame through ongoing food trends.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20894556.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

Its success online has translated into queues at Darley Street Market, where customers regularly flock to the stall for giant pretzels and oversized vanilla slices.

The reopening marks the bakery's return to Thornton Road following the completion of the refurbishment work.

The Thornton Road site first opened in May.
