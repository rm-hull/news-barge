A WORLD champion fighter swapped the brutality of the ring for the tranquillity of a beauty salon.

Gareth "Gaz" Corran, the Lancashire bare-knuckle boxing champion, visited Prity’s salon in Bradford for a facial and IV treatment.

The salon is no stranger to high-profile clients, having previously treated former world heavyweight boxing champion Deontay Wilder.

Mr Corran said: "When you've done people like Deontay Wilder – I've checked the reviews and obviously you're the best lady to come to.

"So I am looking forward to it."

The 2022 world champion, who has held titles in both Lethwei and bare-knuckle boxing, recently secured the inaugural BKFC UK middleweight championship in Manchester after defeating Mac Bygraves in the third round.

His visit to Bradford, however, was motivated by reputation rather than location.

Pritt Farooq, founder of Prity’s salon, said: "It was an honour to have another world champion visit us.

"I was impressed he had looked us up and checked out our reviews.

"All our client reviews are important to us and providing 100% satisfaction to each and every one of them is probably why news is getting out there to people like Gaz Crush to come and try our treatments.

"I am glad he had a good experience."

Mr Corran joins a growing list of sporting figures who are turning to personal grooming to support recovery, relaxation, and overall well-being.

Ms Farooq believes the salon’s reputation for customer care is key to attracting such clients from beyond the Bradford area.

She said: "All our client reviews are important to us and providing 100% satisfaction to each and every one of them is probably why news is getting out there to people like Gaz Crush to come and try our treatments.

"I am glad he had a good experience."

Mr Corran’s visit highlights the growing intersection between professional sport and personal care, as athletes increasingly look for treatments that support both body and mind.

While the fighter is more used to delivering punishing blows in the ring, his time in Bradford was all about relaxation and recovery.

For Prity’s salon, the visit was not only a testament to its reputation but also a reminder that even the toughest competitors need time to recharge.

The salon continues to welcome clients from all walks of life, proving that good service and positive reviews can travel far.
