A curry house was forced to rethink its business model in a bid to remain affordable for customers.

Future Balti, in Wibsey, is one of 10 finalists competing to be named Bradford's Best Curry House 2026 in the Telegraph & Argus competition.

Two years ago, the takeaway launched an additional food business (Deuces) from the same premises, a move owner Faisal Arshad says has helped keep prices affordable.

Mr Arshad said: “Rising costs and economic pressures have resulted in us having to change our overall business model, 'sweat the asset' and offer a secondary cuisine from the same building.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21675801.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the dishes available at Future Balti *(Image: Future Balti)*

“This allows us to keep costs as low as possible for our long-standing customers without compromising on quality.”

## Second final in as many years

The nomination marks the second consecutive year the takeaway has reached the finals of the competition.

Mr Arshad said the recognition was particularly meaningful because it reflected the support of the local community.

He said: “We were thrilled to receive the nomination, as it is the second year we have been finalists.

“This means we are consistent, making what we do worth it.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21675802.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the dishes available at Future Balti *(Image: Future Balti)*

"The dedicated award from customers' nominations is reflective that the people of Wibsey, and neighbouring communities, are appreciative of the food and service we provide.”

## Set menu is 'very reasonably priced'

The business is run by three generations of the same family and has built its reputation on putting customers first, according to Mr Arshad.

He said: “We are a family-run business across three generations, we treat our customers as family. People before profits, as it was last year and it has always been.”

Alongside adapting to rising costs, the takeaway has also introduced set meals designed to help customers save money.

Mr Arshad said: “We offer set meals which can result in big savings, on food which is very reasonably priced.”

![Future Balti](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21675774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Future Balti *(Image: T&A)*

## Family-run business

He said reaching the final was a significant achievement for an independent business competing against larger operators.

He said: “We are underdogs, against some big brands, so well done to everyone nominated.

“Being a finalist is a massive achievement for an independent family-run business our size.”

Mr Arshad added that competitions celebrating local businesses are important at a time when positive stories are often overlooked.

He said: “It is awards like this we need more of, reasons to celebrate the success and joys of business and people within Bradford.”

Readers can now vote for their favourite finalist, with voting slips appearing in the newspaper until Saturday, November 7.

Each voting slip will count as one vote.
