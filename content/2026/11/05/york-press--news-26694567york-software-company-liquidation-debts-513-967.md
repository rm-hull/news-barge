A York-based software company has gone into liquidation owing more than £500,000.

Oak Cloud Software Ltd was incorporated in April 2024 and entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on October 8.

John William Butler of Driffield was appointed administrator.

Companies House says Oak Cloud Software had four directors.

They were Richard Colin Neil Davidson, Marcus Marland, Marie Margaret Melynk and Nigel David Peet.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs outlines the debts accrued.

The report says Oak Cloud Software had realisable assets of £20,255.

However, it had preferential creditors — those who are paid first — which consisted of seven employees owed £4,101 between them, plus a further £706 owed in pensions.

HMRC was also owed £34,092 as a secondary preferential creditor — someone paid next.

In addition, there were unsecured claims totalling £495,208.

These consisted of trade creditors owed £7,241 between them.

HMRC was owed £1.

Seven employees were owed £106,579.

The largest unsecured creditor was a connected creditor, namely The Cricketer Publishing Ltd of Heworth, which was owed £381,387.

This gave a total deficit of £513,853.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £114, this left a final overall deficit of £513,967, according to the statement of affairs.

This is the second York software company to enter liquidation with massive debts recently.

As The Press reported several days ago, Paperless Construction Ltd entered liquidation last month and left a deficit of £594,483.24.

The business was registered at Askham Fields Lane, Askham Bryan, York.
