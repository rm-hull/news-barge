A life raft belonging to a fishing vessel missing off the Isle of Lewis has been recovered off the coast of Shetland.

The raft was recovered after washing up at West Burrafirth on Thursday, November 5, police said.

Earlier this week, police said an examination of two sites identified as part of the ongoing search for the Kilda yielded no results.

The sites, east of the Butt of Lewis, were identified by the Northern Lighthouse Board vessel NLV Pharos.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21581373.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The fishing boat went missing off the coast near Port of Ness on the Isle of Lewis (Alamy/PA)

The Marine Accident Investigation Branch (MAIB) continues to look into the disappearance of the vessel.

The Kilda was last known to have been operating close to the Port of Ness in the north of the Isle of Lewis on Tuesday, October 13.

Norman Murray, 40, who is known as Tam, and Murdo Morrison, 23, from the boat’s crew remain unaccounted for and are being treated as missing people.

The body of their crewmate Douglas Craigie was recovered in the area around 2.45am on Wednesday, October 14.

Inspector Neil Taylor said: "Our thoughts remain with everyone affected by this tragic incident.

"The life raft has been confirmed as belonging to the Kilda and we will continue to work with partners, including the MAIB, Maritime and Coastguard Agency, as well as local maritime experts, to locate the vessel and the two missing men.

"Please be assured we are doing all we can and we are grateful for the support of all of our partners."

Anyone with any information is asked to call 101 quoting reference 3575 of 13 October.
