North Yorkshire artist Lucy Pittaway is recruiting staff for her recently reopened Newcastle gallery, with both full time and part-time roles up for grabs.

The award-winning artist is looking to grow the team at her Blackett Street gallery after it reopened its doors on October 31 for the festive season.

Sales assistants are being recruited for the Newcastle site, with successful applicants set to earn £12.71 an hour plus commission on artwork sales.

Announcing the recruitment drive, the business said: “Newcastle, we're recruiting.

“As we reopen, we're looking to grow our Newcastle team and have opportunities for both part-time and full-time positions.

“We're looking for enthusiastic, customer focused individuals with previous sales experience.

“If you have a passion for people, enjoy working in a creative environment and would love to be part of the Lucy Pittaway brand, we'd love to hear from you.”

The sales assistant role will largely involve working with customers in the gallery, including selling artwork and merchandise and providing customer service.

Successful applicants will also be responsible for processing payments, dealing with phone and email sales enquiries and organising and handling artwork and stock.

Staff may also be asked to support local events and exhibitions.

Previous retail or sales experience is listed as essential, while the business is looking for candidates with strong communication and customer service skills.

Applicants should also be confident handling payments and using a till, have good attention to detail and be able to work both independently and as part of a team.

Both full time and part-time roles are available, with employees working across a seven-day rota including weekdays and weekends.

Alongside the £12.71 hourly rate and commission on artwork sales, staff will receive a discount and the opportunity to work in what the business describes as a “creative, inspiring gallery environment”.

The recruitment drive comes after the Blackett Street gallery reopened on Saturday, October 31, having closed earlier this year.

The business announced last month that the Newcastle gallery would return for the festive season and remain open through to January.

The Newcastle gallery is open from 10am to 5pm from Monday to Saturday and from 10am to 4pm on Sundays throughout the festive period.

Its return followed a period of change for the business, which also saw galleries in Bowness on Windermere and Harrogate close earlier in the year.

Pittaway previously described the decision to close those galleries as “incredibly tough”, with rising operational costs among the reasons given by the business.

Her galleries in Richmond, Yarm, York and Keswick, alongside an outlet at Brompton on Swale, remained open.

Pittaway began her art career more than 13 years ago when her twins started school, with a hobby in a small metal shed developing into a successful business.

She has become known for her distinctive artwork featuring sheep, countryside scenes and family life and has developed a following across the North East, Yorkshire and beyond.

Those interested in joining the Newcastle team are being asked to send a CV and covering letter to [hr@lucypittaway.co.uk.](mailto:hr@lucypittaway.co.uk.)
