The Harrogate Food and Drink Festival is on The Stray on 19 and 20 June 2027, from 10am each day. Dates for a second event at Ripley Castle are still to come.

The Harrogate Food and Drink Festival will be on The Stray on Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 June 2027. The organisers say it will run at two sites next year, with a second stop at Ripley Castle on dates still to be announced.

## Times and where to go

* **Saturday 19 June:** 10am to 8pm
* **Sunday 20 June:** 10am to 7pm
* **Where:** The Stray, Oatlands Drive, HG2 8JT
* **Tickets:** sold through Ticket Tailor

Artisan market stalls may close from 5pm, so go earlier if they are what you are coming for.

## What the organisers promise

The festival’s own page lists:

* international street food traders
* eight independent bars, plus fresh lemonade and juices
* a live cookery theatre all weekend, working with chef Stephanie Moon
* artisan produce markets
* live music and family entertainment

The chefs for 2027 have not been named. Earlier line-ups included The Foundry, Rudding Park and Shear’s Yard, the organisers say. Harrogate Mind is listed as the festival’s charity.

## What it means for you

* **To go,** book through the ticket page, and check the price there before you plan the day.
* **If you prefer Ripley,** wait for the second date. The organisers say only “dates coming soon”.
* **If you run a food or drink business,** the festival’s site has a trader application page.
