Bin collection days will change for some County Durham households over Christmas and New Year.

Durham County Council has confirmed most collections will continue as normal over the festive period.

Changes affect bins normally emptied on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Bins due to be collected on December 25 will instead be emptied on December 28.

Collections scheduled for January 1 will move to January 4.

All collections will take place as normal up to and including December 24.

Most other collection days over Christmas and New Year will remain unchanged.

There will also be a temporary break in the council's large waste collection service over the festive period.

No large, or bulky, waste collections will take place between December 23 and January 4.

Residents can check their individual collection dates by entering their house number and postcode into the 'My Durham' section of Durham County Council's website, the council said.
