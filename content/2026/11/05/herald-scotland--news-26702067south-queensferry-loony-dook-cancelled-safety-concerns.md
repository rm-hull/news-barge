**The annual Loony Dook swim in South Queensferry has been cancelled due to safety concerns.**

For more than four decades, hundreds of people, many dressed in outlandish costumes, have gathered to take the cold water plunge every New Year’s Day.

However, local organiser Tony Pirouet said the event had grown too large to ensure the safety of all swimmers, and would be placed on hiatus.

“Due to its previous success, history is repeating itself,” he wrote in a Facebook post.

“I now honestly feel the dook is becoming a safety issue due to its popularity, and without safety measures being put in place, ie road closures, safety fencing & marshals, I feel it would be totally irresponsible of me, and would feel so bad if there were any injuries to anyone.”

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20362932.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The annual Loony Dook will go on hiatus. *(Image: Agency)*

The tradition began in 1986 when a group of locals attempting to find the perfect Hogmanay hangover cure came up with the idea.

It grew in popularity over the next several decades, ultimately being run by Unique Assembly between 2009 and 2020, before being run by local organisers once again.

Proceeds from the dook have been donated to the RNLI’s Queensferry lifeboat station.

Mr Pirouet added: “Huge thanks go to the wonderful people of Queensferry and the surrounding area who came on the 1st January braving big coats and bigger smiles on yer faces, and the huge amounts of cash that was popped into the collection buckets and online which went to our local lifeboat station.

"I'm sure you'll see me in and around the waters of Queensferry in the future, but a final thank you from your Chief Loony for all your support over the past few years.”

However, it appears that the dook could be back after not too long.

Several hours after the initial announcement on Wednesday, Mr Pirouet said he hoped to bring the dook back in 2028 after discussions with a “mystery man”.

“Loony Dook 27 cancelled, but due to a chat with a mystery man I’m happy to announce Loony Dook 2028 shall return to Queensferry.

“Time and tide is against us to get everything in place in eight weeks unfortunately for Dook 2027, so we shall take a year out."
