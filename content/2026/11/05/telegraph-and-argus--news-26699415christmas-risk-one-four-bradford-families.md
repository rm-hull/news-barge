MORE than a quarter of families in Bradford are considering cancelling Christmas.

New research from charity Family Action reveals that 26 per cent of parents and carers in Bradford are considering cancelling Christmas due to financial pressures, while eight per cent say they cannot afford to buy presents for their children this year.

Sir David Holmes CBE, chief executive of Family Action, said: "Our new research shows the painful reality facing many families this Christmas.

"Parents want so much to give their children joy, but many are being forced to make choices no family should have to make.

"A donated gift can mean far more than a toy – it can mean relief, dignity and a moment of Christmas magic."

The charity is encouraging the public to support its annual Toy Appeal, which helps deliver gifts to families experiencing hardship.

Last year, hundreds of families in Bradford received gifts thanks to generous donations.

One parent said: "The Toy Appeal really helps us out as things are very, very tight this year so we can wrap the gift for him and say it’s from us."

Family Action hopes to reach even more families this year.
