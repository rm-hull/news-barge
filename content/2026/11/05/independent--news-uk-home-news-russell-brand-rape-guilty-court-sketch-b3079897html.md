Russell Brand has been found guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault after a dramatic four-week trial.

The former Hollywood actor and TV star-turned-podcaster, 51, faces years in prison after his conviction for his crimes which date back to the days when he was emerging as one of Britain’s biggest comedians. He was acquitted of two other counts of rape and one attempted indecent assault.

After the verdicts, the Metropolitan Police revealed that he is also under investigation for two more alleged rapes, including that of a a 16-year-old schoolgirl, and warned further victims could still come forward.

The force also released footage of the comedian and actor being questioned by officers after allegations against him first came to light in 2023.

In the gallery of photos below, *The Independent* has gathered the key images of Brand’s trial, from his arrival at court in the typically flamboyant clothing he is known for, to extraordinary court sketches showing him in the dock as his wife watched on.
