A Durham Police dog known for boosting officers' spirits is in the running for a second job - as a pub chain's chief treat taster.

Kali, a cocker spaniel from South Hetton, is competing to become chief doggie treat tester for pub chain Vintage Inns.

It comes after the nationwide chain started selling treats from brand The Canine Menu at some of its pubs, including seven in the North East.

Kali is beaten more than 300 fellow jobseekers already and will go head-to-head with, Oxo, from Usk in Wales, and canine double act Dave and Doug, from Letchworth, in a vote on Vintage Inns' Facebook and Instagram pages.

Kali's owner Sarah, said being a treat tester would "pay her back for all the good work she does" as an OK9 welfare dog.

She said: “Kali is an OK9 welfare dog for Durham Police, so gives her time to making everyone feel better after horrible jobs.

"She is also a Cocker Spaniel, so will sell her soul for any treat going and has been known to go into the odd bait bag while working and borrow the occasional sandwich.
