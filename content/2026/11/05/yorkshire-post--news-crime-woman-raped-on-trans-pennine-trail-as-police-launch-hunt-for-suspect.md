# Woman raped on Trans Pennine Trail as police launch hunt for suspect

## Police have launched an investigation after a woman was raped on a section of the Trans Pennine Trail in Barnsley, with officers appealing for help to trace a suspect.

South Yorkshire Police received a report at around 2.50pm on Wednesday (Nov 4) that a woman had been raped on a section of the Trans Pennine Trail between Mexborough Road and Harlington Road, near Bolton-upon-Dearne.

Officers have launched an investigation and are appealing for witnesses and anyone with information about the attack to come forward.

The suspect is described as being of tall and stocky build and was wearing a dark sweatshirt, black gloves and a black face covering.

He is also believed to have spoken with a southern accent.

Police confirmed that the victim is receiving support and care from officers and medical professionals.

Additional patrols are being carried out in the local area, while officers continue their enquiries.

A police cordon has also been put in place along the Trans Pennine Trail to allow investigators to gather forensic evidence.

Senior Investigating Officer Detective Inspector Helen Hughes said: “We are continuing to work with the victim to ensure that she is receiving the best possible care and support at this traumatic time.
