# New UK fishing rules confirmed by government from November 26

## The UK Government has confirmed new fishing regulations from November 26

The Andy Burnham government has unveiled new fishing regulations in a significant Thursday announcement. The Labour administration has confirmed fresh rules designed to support fish stocks.

Fresh regulations concerning nets and selective fishing gear are being introduced. The new measures are due to be implemented across UK and EU waters to safeguard stocks, according to the Marine Management Organisation, reports Birmingham Live.

The Labour government website stated: "The changes have been made through fishing licence conditions after the UK and EU reached an agreement on a package of technical fisheries measures aimed at rebuilding depleted stocks in the Celtic Sea, English Channel and Irish Sea.

"At the heart of the agreement is a requirement for fishermen to use larger mesh sizes and more selective fishing gear. The licence conditions were brought in on 1 November and come into force from 26 November."

Sean Douglas, Marine Management Organisation's Head of Regulatory Assurance, said: "As a regulator we are looking to support our industry to adapt to these changes. We have published guidance to explain the new requirements in the relevant ICES areas and I would urge all fishermen who fish for these key stocks in those areas to take action now to stay compliant with regulations.

"Modifications to exemptions and conditions have been implemented via secondary legislation, according to the government. Online guidance has been refreshed and sets out (organised by gear sector and region) the measures and exemptions to the landing obligation regulations that are applicable exclusively within UK waters."

The guidance states: "All quota species must now be landed; however, these will no longer be taken into account when enforcing catch composition rules. Catches of quota species are no longer subject to catch composition rules because they can no longer be discarded.

"For example, all cod catches must be landed regardless of the mesh size of a trawl and the 5% bycatch rule in scallop dredging will no longer apply to quota species. Although catch composition rules no longer apply to quota species, minimum mesh sizes set out in technical conservation rules must still be respected."
