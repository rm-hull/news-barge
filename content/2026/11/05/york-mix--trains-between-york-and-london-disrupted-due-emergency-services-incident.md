Rail services between York and London have been disrupted this morning.

It’s due to a person being hit by a train between Newark and Grantham.

Both the northbound and southbound lines were blocked. They have now reopened.

But National Rail says major disruption will continue until midday.

LNER said: “As a result, trains running between Doncaster and Grantham may be cancelled, delayed by up to 60 minutes, or revised.

“Major disruption is expected until 9am.”

## Advice for LNER customers

If you choose to travel today and your LNER train has been cancelled, you will be able to use your existing ticket on another LNER service up to 2 hours before or 2 hours after your cancelled service.

You can read more on the LNER website here.

## Advice for Hull Trains customers

Hull Trains services can divert via an alternative route from Doncaster towards London Kings Cross, however delays can be expected. Trains that divert will not call at Retford and Grantham.

However, due to a failed freight train on the diversionary route, Northbound trains from London Kings Cross towards Hull are unable to run and trains will be delayed until further notice. The 07:18 Hull Trains departure from London Kings Cross to Hull will be delayed at London Kings Cross.

You should continue to travel as planned; however, your journey may be delayed.

There’s more on the National Rail website here.
