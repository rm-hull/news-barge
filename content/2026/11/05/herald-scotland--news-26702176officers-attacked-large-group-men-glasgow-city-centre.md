A police investigation is underway after two officers on patrol were attacked by a 'large group of men' in Glasgow city centre.

Around 9.15pm on Wednesday, November 4, police received a report of a disturbance involving a large group of men on Sauchiehall Street.

Police said two officers on patrol were attacked during the incident, with bottles being thrown and one knocked to the ground.

The officers did not require medical treatment.

**READ MORE:**

The Herald reported how dozens of officers, along with police vans and cars, had surrounded nearby Douglas Street , with one eyewitness saying they had seen “a large group of men” in the area before the police arrived.

The Herald understands that the incident involved a group of Besiktas supporters, who are in the city to attend this evening's UEFA Europa League tie against Celtic at Celtic Park.

Police said enquiries into the incident are ongoing.

Chief Superintendent David Robertson said: "Around 9.15pm on Wednesday, 4 November, 2026, we received a report of a disturbance involving a large group of men on Sauchiehall Street, Glasgow.

"Two officers on patrol were attacked during the incident, with bottles being thrown and one knocked to the ground. They did not require medical treatment.

"This violence is unacceptable and will not be tolerated, being assaulted is not part of the job.

“Our enquiries are ongoing.”
