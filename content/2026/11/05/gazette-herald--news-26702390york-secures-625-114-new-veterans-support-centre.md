City of York Council has revealed how much funding it has secured to set up a centre to strengthen support for veterans and military families across York.

The council has been granted £625,114 through the Government’s VALOUR programme to support a dedicated veterans centre in York, one of just 20 locations nationally to receive this investment.

The new centre was announced this morning, Thursday, November 5, but the detail of the investment and the centre's location was not initially revealed.

The local authority has now confirmed that the VALOUR-recognised centre is scheduled to open in January 2027 at York’s West Offices and will remain in operation until March 2030.

It will serve as a central and accessible hub for veterans, serving personnel, and their families, offering easy access to support and services in one location.

Councillor Claire Douglas, leader of the council and Armed Forces champion, said: "This funding win is absolutely fantastic news for York, our veterans, their families and our wider Armed Forces community.

"It recognises the incredible strength of the partnerships that already exist across our city, bringing together veterans' charities and support organisations, the council, our garrison communities, health services and education providers.

"This new VALOUR veterans centre will build on our strong track record of supporting our Armed Forces community and take that support to the next level, making services even more accessible, better connected and easier to navigate."

In addition to serving as a physical hub, the centre will support outreach work across communities, helping more people access services regardless of where they live in the city.

The programme will take a co-production approach, involving veterans and their families in shaping the services provided.

A co-production champion, hosted by York CVS, will lead this effort to ensure that people with lived experience can help identify unmet needs and influence services.

Over the next three years, the centre is expected to directly support about 1,500 veterans and an additional 3,000 family members, carers, and others connected to York’s Armed Forces community.

The funding will also expand existing support in areas such as housing, wellbeing, employment, skills, carers’ support, and social connection.

Cllr Douglas added: "We are committed to ensuring veterans, serving personnel and their families can access the help and support they need, when they need it.

"An investment like this will strengthen that support for years to come."

VALOUR is a £50 million UK-wide programme designed to improve access to services for veterans and their families, ensuring they can get the right support at the right time.

It is a key commitment within the Government's Veterans Strategy.
