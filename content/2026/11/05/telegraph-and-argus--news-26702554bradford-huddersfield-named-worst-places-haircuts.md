BRADFORD and Huddersfield have been named the worst places for haircuts in Yorkshire and the Humber.

The unflattering title comes from a national study by UK Hair Transplants, which analysed Google reviews for more than 21,000 barber shops across the UK with at least 20 reviews.

Barbers in Bradford and Kirklees, which includes Huddersfield, scored an average of 4.72 stars out of five—just below the UK average of 4.75 and the lowest in Yorkshire and the Humber.

Dr Adam Ali, hair transplant surgeon at UK Hair Transplants, said: "A bad haircut grows out in a few weeks, so Bradford and Huddersfield can live with this one, but your barber matters more than most people think.

"They see the back of your head every few weeks and you never do, so they're often the first person to notice a crown getting thinner or a hairline creeping back."

In Bradford, 18 per cent of barbers rated below 4.5 stars, compared to the UK average of 13.1 per cent.

The most common complaint in negative reviews was customers not receiving the haircut they asked for or coming away with an uneven or badly finished one, with 61 per cent of low-rated comments mentioning this issue.

Other frequent complaints included rude staff, rushed service, and poor value for money.
