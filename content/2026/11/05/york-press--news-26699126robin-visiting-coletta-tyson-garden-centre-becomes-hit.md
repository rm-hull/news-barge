## Robbie the robin becomes unlikely celebrity at Beverley garden centre

Robbie the robin has become a local celebrity after making the Coletta & Tysoin garden centre his home *(Image: Supplied)*

A robin has become a local celebrity after making a Beverley garden centre his home.

The bird, affectionately named Robbie, has made a home at Coletta & Tyson, charming customers as he explores the premises.

The robin's antics, from inspecting Christmas trees to watching over gardening supplies, have been shared on social media, garnering the attention of the local community.

Robbie's story quickly went viral, with the garden centre's Facebook post attracting 25,500 views.

Customers have responded, sharing their own encounters and photos of the bird.

The garden centre, a local, family-owned company for 70 years, has been taken aback by the public's enthusiasm.

A spokesperson for Coletta & Tyson said: "Seeing our community get so involved in what we thought was a simple story has really made our week."

Visitors have even started popping in just to meet Robbie, who continues to spread a little extra magic on their visits.
