Flames engulfed a **huge red banner showing Donald Trump, JD Vance, Benjamin Netanyahu and Steve Witkoff** in Tehran's Enghelab Square on Wednesday, as crowds gathered to mark the**anniversary of the 1979 US embassy hostage crisis**.

The four figures were pictured with torn clothes and apparent injuries. Demonstrators marched with portraits of **Ali Khamenei and his son Mojtaba**, while others displayed caricatures of Trump and Netanyahu, including one depicting the US president as a dog on a leash.

At the former American embassy, crowds chanted **"Death to America" and "Death to Israel"** and stamped on US and Israeli flags.

The rally followed a **Democratic sweep of Congress in the US midterms**, although one participant told AP that the election outcome would** make little difference to American policy towards Iran**.

The annual event commemorates the **1979 hostage crisis**, when** 52 Americans were detained for 444 days**.
