Bradford Bulls have confirmed that Top Notch NW Ltd will continue their partnership with the club for the 2027 season.

As part of the renewed agreement, Top Notch NW Ltd branding will feature on the chest of both the Bulls’ replica and playing shirts, maintaining a prominent presence across the club’s kit for the upcoming campaign.

Top Notch first came on board as main sponsors in 2025 and have been key supporters of the club during their return to Super League.

Bradford Bulls commercial manager Ian Stafford said: “We’re really pleased to be continuing our relationship with Top Notch NW Ltd into the 2027 season.

“They have been fantastic supporters of the Bulls, and we’re delighted that their branding will continue to feature prominently on our replica and playing shirts.

“Partnerships like this are hugely important to the club, and we’re grateful for their continued commitment.

“We look forward to another successful year working together.”

Graham Eastwood from Top Notch NW Ltd added: “We’re delighted to continue our association with Bradford Bulls for the 2027 season.

“The club has a fantastic following and an important place within the local community, and we’re proud to remain involved.

“We look forward to seeing our branding on the Bulls’ playing and replica shirts and wish everyone at the club the very best for the season ahead.”
