A UK delivery company has closed down a site ahead of Christmas.

HelloFresh said it had offered redeployment to its 271 workers in Oxfordshire to the more modern warehouse in Derby.

The firm, which delivers meals to people across the UK, said some people had taken up that offer and made the move up north, but that there remains some redundancies at Banbury's distribution centre.

An exact figure has not been given.

## **Company closes centre**

Having opened 10 years ago, the distribution centre in Banbury officially closed on Saturday, October 31.

Known as The Granary, the 237,000 sq ft facility at Chalker Way became a vital production hub for the company's UK operations.

But a consultation, first revealed in June, has confirmed it will now be closing down, in part due to the "better equipped" site in Derby.

A spokesman for the subscription-based meal provider said: "Following the conclusion of our consultation process, we can confirm that The Granary will close on 31 October 2026.

"The decision was not made lightly, and our priority throughout has been to support affected colleagues as they consider their next steps.

"We have provided a range of support measures, including CV-writing and interview workshops, external job fairs, and opportunities for redeployment within HelloFresh.

"We would like to thank everyone at The Granary for their dedication, professionalism and hard work over the years.

"Their contribution to HelloFresh has been significant, and we wish them every success in the future."

Some 271 people were employed at the Banbury site and have now lost their jobs or been redeployed, HelloFresh said.

An "extensive" review of HelloFresh's operations concluded a single, more technologically advanced site "is the right course of action to ensure the long-term viability and efficiency" of the business.

When it opened 10 years ago, The Granary was a brand new facility with HelloFresh being its first occupant.

HelloFresh said its Derby site, called Windmill, is equipped with technology that "enables greater operational complexity and delivers an expanding menu with flexible ingredient options".

During the pandemic, HelloFresh employed a further 400 people on top of its workforce to help meet the burgeoning demand.

But last year, the German company was forced to make 900 UK job cuts with the closure of the delivery site in Nuneaton.

According to the Guardian, demand for meal kits tumbled as revenue fell by more than 11 per cent during 2025 ahead of the closure.

It was reported in March that the share price has plummeted by 93 per cent since the 2021 boom during Covid lockdowns.

Total orders slumped 12 per cent last year compared with 2024 as the number of meals it delivered tumbled by more than 100 million.

A spokesman previously said: "The site remains operational until October 31, but we are sharing information with our employees as early as possible in the interest of transparency."
