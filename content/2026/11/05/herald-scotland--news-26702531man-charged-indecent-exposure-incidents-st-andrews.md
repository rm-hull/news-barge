A 74-year-old man has been arrested and charged in connection with a series of alleged indecent exposure incidents in St Andrews.

Police said the offences took place mostly in the Kinburn Park and Cockshaugh Park areas of the Fife town between October 2025 and November 2026.

The man is expected to appear before Dundee Sheriff Court on Thursday, November 5.

Detective Sergeant Mark Millar said: “Incidents of this nature can cause a great deal of concern and distress within the local community and will not be tolerated.

“We’d like to thank members of the public for their assistance which was vital during our investigation.

“We’d also urge anyone else who has witnessed this type of criminality to please get in touch with Police Scotland on 101.”
