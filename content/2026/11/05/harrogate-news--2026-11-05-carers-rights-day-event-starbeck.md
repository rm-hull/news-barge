Unpaid carers across the Harrogate area are being invited to a free event in Starbeck this month, bringing together local organisations offering advice, information and support.

Organised by Carers’ Resource, the Carers’ Rights Day event will take place at St Andrews Church, High Street, Starbeck, on Thursday 19 November, from 1pm to 3.30pm.

The event aims to make it easier for unpaid carers to find out about their rights and access support by bringing representatives from a range of organisations together under one roof.

Attending organisations will include Carers’ Resource, Harrogate & District Community Action (HADCA), including its Harrogate Easier Living Project (HELP), Emerging Voices, Alzheimer’s Society, Dementia Forward, the National Autistic Society, Parkinson’s Harrogate and Parent Carers Voice.

Visitors will have the opportunity to speak directly with representatives, ask questions and find out about services that could support both their caring responsibilities and their own health and wellbeing.

> **Heidi Watson, Chief Executive of Carers’ Resource, said:**
>
> Being an unpaid carer can be very rewarding, but it can also be demanding, and it is not always easy to know what support is available or where to turn for advice.
>
> We want unpaid carers to know that their rights matter and that support is available to them. Bringing a range of organisations together under one roof gives carers the opportunity to ask questions, explore their options and make connections that could make a real difference to their lives and their caring role.

The afternoon will include talks from local professionals, an opportunity to meet support organisations and a performance by the Harrogate Wellbeing Choir. Tea, coffee and cake will also be available.

The programme includes Denise Baynton from Disability Action Yorkshire at 1.10pm, followed by Emma Ryan from North Yorkshire Council at 2pm, speaking about the Care Confidence Tool.

The Harrogate Wellbeing Choir will perform at 2.45pm.

Carers’ Rights Day raises awareness of the rights of unpaid carers and helps people understand the information, advice and support available to them.

The event is open to anyone who cares, unpaid, for a family member, friend or someone else who needs support. This includes people who have been caring for years as well as those who are only just beginning to recognise themselves as carers.

People are also welcome to bring the person they care for.

## Carers’ Rights Day event details

* **What:** Carers’ Rights Day – information, advice and support event
* **When:** Thursday 19 November 2026, 1pm–3.30pm (doors open at 12.45pm)
* **Where:** St Andrews Church, High Street, Starbeck, Harrogate, HG2 7JE
* **Cost:** Free

Booking is not essential, but advance registration is encouraged by emailing Amelia at [ahawk@carersresource.org.](mailto:ahawk@carersresource.org.)

Parking is available in the car park next to the church, although spaces are expected to fill quickly. Free on-street parking is available nearby.

Starbeck railway station is a short walk away, while major bus routes into Harrogate stop outside the church.

Carers’ Resource is encouraging unpaid carers from across the Harrogate area to attend, find out about their rights and discover the support available to them.
