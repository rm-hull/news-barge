Energetic entrepreneur Brian Williamson, who received the Lifetime Achievement Award at The Herald and Glasgow Chamber of Commerce’s Inspiring City Awards in September, is featured in this month’s edition.

He speaks to Kristy Dorsey about how he has been drawn back to hands-on business transformation after periods spent investing and serving on boards.

Passive investing, he says, is too much like betting on a horse. The investor can cheer from the sidelines but cannot ride it.

“I’m not a great passive investor because it doesn’t suit my style,” he says. “I think I can stack the odds if I go into a company.”

Meanwhile, I interview Charlie MacGregor, founder and chief executive officer of The Social Hub.

He talks about the “deeply personal milestone” of bringing the model “home” to Glasgow.

A “self-confessed optimist”, Mr MacGregor believes “society’s biggest challenges become far easier to solve when different minds sit in the same room, listen to each other, and share a common table”.
