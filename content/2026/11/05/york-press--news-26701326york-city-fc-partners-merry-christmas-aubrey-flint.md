A NEW Christmas partnership is bringing festive cheer to the big screen and beyond.

York City Football Club has teamed up with Shush Films to celebrate the release of Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint, a heartwarming movie set and filmed in York, which arrives in cinemas tomorrow.

Directed by Jack Spring, the film tells the story of a reclusive model soldier painter who despises Christmas and is sentenced to community service at a struggling retirement home.

There, he becomes involved in a chaotic production of A Christmas Carol that forces him to confront grief, rediscover friendship, and embrace the festive spirit he has long avoided.

Mr Spring said: "It's a beautiful new Christmas movie which is coming out November 6th.

"The film is shot and set in York; I'm delighted to be working with York City Football Club.

"It's a club I've always had a soft spot for after living in York for a couple of years, coming down to Bootham Crescent plenty of times back in the day.

"I look forward to seeing you all soon."

The film features a star-studded cast including John Bradley, Celia Imrie, Richard E. Grant, Adjoa Andoh, Sophia Di Martino, Kiell Smith-Bynoe and Karl Pilkington.

Ed Simpson, media manager at York City FC, said: "It's a real privilege to be working with Jack this winter to recognise the important messages behind the movie, while also showcasing our city on the big screen.

"It's a partnership that we, as a club, are really looking forward to, and we hope to create some exciting opportunities for our supporters over the coming weeks as part of the collaboration.
