A newly upgraded Aldi store has reopened its doors to shoppers today.

The Allerton Road branch in Bradford welcomed shoppers back following a significant upgrade as part of the supermarket's £300 million investment in its existing store estate.

The improvements include updated sections for bakery, health and beauty, fresh food, and produce, along with new energy-efficient technology.

Jonathan Neale, Managing Director of National Real Estate at Aldi UK, said: "We're delighted to be welcoming customers back to these newly upgraded stores in time for Christmas.

"Whether it's creating more space, refreshing key shopping areas or introducing more energy-efficient technology, we're continuing to invest in our stores to make them even better for the communities they serve."

Aldi is also investing £370 million in new stores across the UK in 2026.

A further £900 million investment is planned for 2027, including the opening of 40 new stores.
