THE owner of an historic building has spoken of a 'dark day' after a lorry badly damaged the property.

Nick Burrows, owner of Coulton Mill, Coulton, which dates back hundreds of years, said he was woken just before 6am this morning (Thursday) to the sound of a revving engine and falling masonry.

Nick, who owns Coulton Mill Cyder, said a HGV had crashed into the side of the house and by move was destroying more of the building in the process.

He said: "It is a dark day. I have spent much of the latter half of my life trying to preserve an extraordinarily beautiful building in an area of outstanding natural beauty that I love. I have done this, in large part, for future generations.

"At times like this, I feel crushed

"At times such as this, I wonder if it is best to walk away now.

I have seen the devastation of war and natural disaster, though, and from it springs humanity and hope. This is a small thing in comparison. So I will go on."

A North Yorkshire Police Spokesperson said: "At 6.20am this morning (5 November), we received a report of a HGV colliding with a residential property, causing significant damage to the building. No injuries were sustained and our enquiries into the incident are ongoing."
