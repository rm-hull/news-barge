**Spooktacular Glasgow’s bonfire and fireworks display has been cancelled hours before it was due to take place.**

Organisers announced the cancellation on social media, disappointing families who had planned to attend the Bonfire Night event.

In a statement, organisers said: “A quick but important update for tonight. The bonfire and fireworks display will not take place this evening. We're really sorry to anyone who was looking forward to it. Thanks for understanding, and sorry for any inconvenience caused.”

No reason for the cancellation was provided.

The announcement came on one of the busiest nights of the year, with families across Glasgow making plans to attend organised displays.

The update prompted disappointment among some social media users, many of whom had been looking forward to the event.

Spooktacular, located on South Street in Glasgow, has become a popular seasonal attraction, drawing visitors with family-friendly entertainment, themed attractions and festive activities.

While the display has been cancelled, organisers have not indicated whether other elements will be affected.

Visitors planning to attend are being encouraged to check Spooktacular Glasgow's social media channels for the latest updates.
