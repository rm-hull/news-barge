A controlling thug who made his girlfriend quit her job so she couldn't talk to men ripped out her hair extensions in a burst of rage.

Logan Petch was paranoid about her social life and allowed her to use only Facebook, provided she had female friends, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Petch stopped the woman working in a bar so she could not speak to other men and demanded money from her, the court heard.

Prosecutor Jon Harley said Petch attacked the woman several times, including kicking her in the back and ripping out her hair extensions after finding something he disliked on her phone.

“To enforce his rules, he would regularly take her mobile phone and check her messages or Ring doorbell footage", he said.

In a victim impact statement, the woman said Petch repeatedly called her, threatened to kill her if she got with anyone else and damaged her car.

When the relationship broke down, he bombarded his now-ex with messages.

The 22-year-old was arrested on March 14 after turning up at her home and shouting abuse at her.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21661920.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Logan Petch *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Mr Harley said the relationship began well before Petch started controlling who the woman saw and where she went.

“The relationship was described by the victim as a controlling and violent one,” he said.

“It began well but within a few months his behaviour started to change and arguments would happen.”

Petch, of Kinross Avenue, Middlesbrough, admitted controlling and coercive behaviour between June and December last year.

His solicitor, Albany Kidd, said Petch was embarrassed by his behaviour towards the woman and found it difficult to admit to and discuss it.

Judge Richard Clews jailed Petch for 21 months.
