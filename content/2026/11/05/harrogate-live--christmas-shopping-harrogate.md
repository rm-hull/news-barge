We checked 15 Harrogate area shops, from Imagined Things to The Oldest Sweet Shop, for opening hours and what they sell. Updated November 2026.

Harrogate and its nearby towns have 15 shops worth a Christmas shopping trip in 2026, and this guide lists them with opening hours, addresses and what each one sells. Imagined Things, The Cheeseboard, Bettys and 27 West Park Antiques are the names many people will land on. We checked each entry on 5 November 2026 against the shop’s own site or another named listing, and say where we could not.

We have not shopped at any of these for this guide, and nothing here is a review. Each entry says what the shop says it sells. Shops often change their hours close to Christmas, so check before a special trip.

## Dates for the Christmas shopping diary

**Saturday 14 November: Ripon lights switch-on.** Visit Ripon lists the free afternoon from 1pm to 6pm. Our switch-on page has the detail, including why Harrogate has no switch-on event this year.**Thursday 19 November: late opening at 27 West Park Antiques.** The shop’s events page promises free wine and nibbles from 5.30pm.**Friday 20 to Sunday 22 November: Ripon’s artisan market.** Free, in wooden cabins on the Market Square, from 10am to 7pm on Friday and Saturday and 10am to 4pm on Sunday. The cathedral’s Christmas Food and Gift Fair runs on the Friday and Saturday, £5 entry, and Visit Ripon lists Friday as 9.30am to 4.30pm. Both are on our Ripon market page.**Thursday 26 to Sunday 29 November: Great Yorkshire Christmas Fair.** At the Showground, with tickets, parking and buses on our fair page.**Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November: Fountains Abbey market.** Little Bird Made lists its Christmas market at the abbey on these dates. See our Fountains Abbey Christmas page for the rest of the season.**December weekends: Cambridge Street market.** Zoom Events lists Friday to Sunday on 4 to 6 and 11 to 13 December, then 18 and 19 December. Every date is on our Christmas markets page.**Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December: Valley Gardens and Knaresborough.** Little Bird Made brings makers to Valley Gardens from 10am to 3pm. The same weekend, Knaresborough’s Christmas market fills the town.**Sunday 6 and Wednesday 9 December: Fodder order deadlines.** Fodder closes online poultry orders at 5pm on 6 December and all other Christmas orders at 5pm on 9 December.**Saturday 12 December: ice sculpture trail.** Harrogate BID says eight sculptures will stand around the town centre, with locations to be revealed. Free curling rinks and a giant snow globe are at Victoria Shopping Centre from 10am to 4pm on 5, 6, 12, 13, 19 and 20 December.

## Late nights and gift cards

We found no town-wide late night date for Harrogate, Knaresborough or Ripon in 2026 when we checked on 5 November. In past years the Stray Ferret has reported Knaresborough shops opening late on Thursdays in December, and Ripon Cathedral’s calendar carried a Ripon evening on Thursday 27 November 2025. We will add 2026 dates when they appear.

The Harrogate Gift Card can be spent only at registered businesses in town, not online. Harrogate BID says more than 100 businesses have signed up, from national chains to small independents, and 27 West Park Antiques says it accepts the card.

Victoria Shopping Centre opens from 9am to 6pm Monday to Saturday and 10am to 4.30pm on Sunday, and says individual shops vary. It showed no separate Christmas timetable when we checked.

## Harrogate town centre and Montpellier

### 1. Imagined Things Bookshop, Montpellier Parade

Imagined Things calls itself a proudly independent bookshop and has traded on Montpellier Parade since 2017. Its gift list runs from book subscriptions and bookshop memberships to signed books, stationery, games and personal shopping sessions.

It sells vouchers and book tokens too.

**Open** Mon to Sat 10am to 5pm; Sun 12pm to 4pm**Call** 01423 391301**Address** 21 Montpellier Parade, Harrogate, HG1 2TG**Website** imaginedthings.co.uk**Good for** books, book subscriptions, signed books, stationery

### 2. Fennec and Darwin, Montpellier Mews

Fennec and Darwin describes its range as ladies’ fashion and footwear, with boots a large part of what its site shows. Its site warns that Christmas opening times may vary.

The Visit Harrogate listing gives the Montpellier Mews address and says the shop sometimes opens on Mondays, so email before a Monday trip.

**Open** Tue to Sat 10am to 5pm**Address** 4 Montpellier Mews, Harrogate**Website** fennecanddarwin.co.uk**Good for** women's fashion, footwear

### 3. Bettys, Parliament Street

Bettys on Parliament Street is a cafe tea room with its own shop. It shared the Best Large Company award in the 2025 Christmas shop window competition.

Its site refused our requests, so these shop hours come from a search listing of its Harrogate page and carry no date. Check them before a special trip.

**Open** Sun to Wed 9am to 5.30pm; Thu and Fri 9am to 6pm; Sat 9am to 7pm**Call** 01423 814070**Address** 1 Parliament Street, Harrogate, HG1 2QU**Website** bettys.co.uk**Listing** Our listing**Good for** tea rooms, shop

### 4. Ogden of Harrogate, James Street

Ogden says the family has run its jewellery business since 1893. Its site lists fine jewellery, luxury watches, engagement and wedding rings, and vintage pieces by era, with brands including Georg Jensen, FOPE and Alex Monroe.

It also has a gifts section. The pages we read gave no opening hours, so call before a special trip.

**Call** 01423 504123**Address** 38 James Street, Harrogate, HG1 1RQ**Website** ogdenharrogate.co.uk**Good for** jewellery, watches, vintage pieces

### 5. The Yorkshire Soap Company, James Street

The Yorkshire Soap Company makes gift soaps and bath products, and its site has a Christmas range alongside gift boxes, candles and hampers. It won Best Spirit of Christmas in the 2025 window competition for a festive scene with an animatronic polar bear.

Its Harrogate page shows no hours in the text we could read, so the address and hours here are from the Visit Harrogate listing, which gives no Saturday hours.

**Open** Mon to Fri 9.30am to 5.30pm; Sun 10am to 5pm**Address** 1 James Street, Harrogate**Website** yorkshiresoap.co.uk/pages/harrogate**Good for** gift boxes, soaps, candles, hampers

### 6. The Cheeseboard, Commercial Street

The Cheeseboard was established in 1981 and says it stocks more than 200 speciality cheeses, cut to order. It also sells hampers, gifts and a monthly cheese subscription.

It took Best Innovation in the 2025 window competition for its Twelve Days of Cheesemas display. Its contact page gives the later Thursday to Saturday hours below, but the header on the same page says Monday to Saturday to 4pm, so check before an evening trip.

**Open** Mon to Wed 10am to 4pm; Thu to Sat 10am to 7pm; closed Sun**Call** 01423 508837**Address** 30 Commercial Street, Harrogate, HG1 1TY**Website** thecheeseboard.net**Good for** cheese, hampers, cheese subscriptions

### 7. Toyland Toyshop, Victoria Shopping Centre

Victoria Shopping Centre’s listing describes Toyland as a local toyshop set up to offer good value and a family-friendly atmosphere. It is the only toy shop on this list, and it opens seven days a week.

These are the hours on the centre’s listing, which are shorter than the centre’s own.

**Open** Mon to Fri 9.30am to 5pm; Sat 9.30am to 5.30pm; Sun 10.30am to 4pm**Call** 01423 202025**Address** Victoria Shopping Centre, Station Parade, Harrogate, HG1 1AE**Website** victoriashoppingcentre.com/stores/toyland-toyshop**Good for** toys, children's gifts

## West Park and Cold Bath Road

### 8. 27 West Park Antiques, West Park

27 West Park describes its stock as antiques and home interiors from a group of dealers, across furniture, art, collectables, fashion and vintage vinyl. It won Best Independent in the 2025 window competition and has a new mezzanine floor.

Its late opening is on Thursday 19 November. Its listing gives 10am to 4pm daily, with Monday’s closing time misprinted as 4am.

**Open** daily 10am to 4pm**Call** 01423 369615**Address** 27 West Park, Harrogate, HG1 1BJ**Website** 27westpark.co.uk**Good for** antiques, interiors, vintage vinyl, Harrogate Gift Card

### 9. Weetons, West Park and Leeds Road

Weetons says farmers founded it in Harrogate 20 years ago to sell local produce. It has a food hall on West Park and a flagship on Leeds Road, Pannal, opened in 2024 with a home and garden store and a restaurant.

It was overall winner of the 2025 window competition with a Grinch display. Christmas hampers are on its site, and the pages we read gave no shop hours, so call first: West Park on the number below, Leeds Road on 01423 873102.

**Call** 01423 507100**Address** West Park, Harrogate, HG1 1BJ**Website** weetons.com**Good for** food hall, hampers, home and garden at Pannal

### 10. Illingworths Hardware, Cold Bath Road

Illingworths sells hardware, ironmongery and timber, and offers key cutting and tool sharpening. It suits a practical present.

A local parent’s guest blog on Mumbler called it an Aladdin’s cave, and the Stray Ferret names it among the road’s best-known businesses.

**Open** Mon to Fri 9am to 5pm; Sat 9am to 4.30pm; closed Sun**Call** 01423 506605**Address** 113 Cold Bath Road, Harrogate, HG2 0NU**Website** illingworthshardware.co.uk**Good for** tools, ironmongery, key cutting

## Out of town

### 11. Fodder, Great Yorkshire Showground

Fodder calls itself the Great Yorkshire Food Hall and works with local farmers. Its site says profits go back to the charity behind the Showground, the Yorkshire Agricultural Society.

Christmas gifts, fruit and vegetables are in the shop up to Christmas Day, and hampers and gift cards are online. Note the online order deadlines above.

**Open** daily 9am to 5pm**Call** 01423 546111**Address** Great Yorkshire Showground, Harrogate, HG2 8NZ**Website** fodder.co.uk**Good for** Yorkshire food, hampers, Christmas orders

## Ripon

### 12. The Little Ripon Bookshop, Westgate

The Stray Ferret reported in May that this family-run shop has traded on Westgate since 2009, with a strong children’s section, its own book club and author events. Its site lists book clubs, schools work and storytime.

**Open** Mon to Sat 9am to 5pm**Call** 01765 606689**Address** 12-13 Westgate, Ripon, HG4 2AT**Website** littleriponbookshop.co.uk**Good for** books, children's books, author events

### 13. Karma, Kirkgate

Karma describes itself as an award-winning ethical clothing and gift boutique, selling its own label clothing, gifts and homeware. Its candles are among the gifts on its home page.

It opens seven days a week. Its site prints Tuesday’s closing time as 4.20pm, against 4.30pm on other weekdays.

**Open** Mon, Wed, Thu and Fri 10am to 4.30pm; Tue 10am to 4.20pm; Sat 10am to 5pm; Sun 10am to 4pm**Call** 01765 278100**Address** 13 Kirkgate, Ripon, HG4 1PA**Website** karmaripon.co.uk**Good for** ethical clothing, candles, homeware

## Knaresborough and Nidderdale

### 14. Castlegate Books, Market Place

The Stray Ferret says Castlegate Books began trading in 2008, and it now covers new books including hard-to-find titles, classic fiction, thrillers and children’s books. It also stocks OS maps, cards and National Book Tokens.

Its own site blocked our checks, so the hours are from its National Book Tokens profile. Another listing shows different hours, so call or check first.

**Open** Mon 10am to 4pm, closed 1pm to 1.30pm; Tue to Fri 9am to 4.30pm; Sat 9am to 5pm**Address** Unit 3, 13 Market Place, Knaresborough, HG5 8AL**Website** castlegatebooks.com**Good for** books, OS maps, book tokens

### 15. The Oldest Sweet Shop, Pateley Bridge

The Oldest Sweet Shop says it opened in 1827 and still sells traditional sweets and chocolates from Pateley Bridge High Street. It calls itself the oldest sweet shop in England on one page and in the world on another.

Its site lists sweet jars and gifts. Winter hours apply now, and it shuts on Tuesdays.

**Open** Mon 10am to 5pm; closed Tue; Wed to Sat 10am to 5pm; Sun 11am to 4pm**Call** 01423 712371**Address** 39 High Street, Pateley Bridge, HG3 5JZ**Website** oldestsweetshop.co.uk**Good for** traditional sweets, chocolate, sweet jars

## How we chose

We started from shops people can walk into in Harrogate, Ripon, Knaresborough and Pateley Bridge, using Harrogate BID’s window competition results, the Victoria Shopping Centre listings, the Stray Ferret’s bookshop round-up and the shops’ own sites, and we favoured independents with something to give at Christmas. Hours and stock come from those sources on 5 November 2026, and we say so where another source is used.

Several shops are left out because we could not confirm them on a page of their own, which does not mean they have closed. They include Crown Jewellers, Sophie Likes, Little Alligators and Harrogate Olde Sweet Shop in Harrogate, Upstairs Downstairs and Forbes Gifts in Ripon, and Little Darlings and Dragonfly in Knaresborough. Bigger chains in Victoria Shopping Centre and on James Street sit outside the list.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there late night Christmas shopping in Harrogate?

We found no town-wide late night date for 2026 from Harrogate BID or Victoria Shopping Centre when we checked on 5 November. 27 West Park Antiques has announced its own late opening on Thursday 19 November, with free wine and nibbles from 5.30pm.

### What are Victoria Shopping Centre’s opening hours?

The centre lists 9am to 6pm from Monday to Saturday and 10am to 4.30pm on Sunday, and says individual shops vary. Its hours page showed no separate Christmas timetable on 5 November.

### Which Harrogate shops open on Sundays before Christmas?

Sunday hours are listed for Imagined Things, Bettys, The Yorkshire Soap Company, Toyland, 27 West Park Antiques and Fodder. The Cheeseboard and Illingworths say they close on Sundays. Fennec and Darwin lists Tuesday to Saturday only.
