Police officers have been carrying out "unpredictable" deployments across York.

As part of Project Servator, the operations included vehicle checkpoints.

The initiative aims to disrupt criminal or hostile activities, with the element of surprise being a key tactic.

North Yorkshire Police says deployments can occur at any time and location, making it difficult for those with harmful intentions to act.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21676243.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police at York Station *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Officers were seen interacting with the public and working closely with local businesses, transport operators, and venue staff to share information and enhance security.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21676248.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police making inquiries *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

North Yorkshire Police said if you are in York and see something that does not feel right, to "trust your instincts" and report it.

A spokesperson for the force said: "Your information, no matter how small it may seem, can make a real difference."

People can call 101, or 999 in an emergency.
