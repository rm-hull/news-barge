## Police appeal to identify man after theft at North Yorkshire venue

The man North Yorkshire Police would like to see. *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for help to identify a man following a reported theft.

The incident took place at The Foundry Project in Harrogate in the early hours of Sunday, September 27, when a jacket was stolen.

Officers believe the person pictured may have information that could assist their investigation.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We believe the person in the image will have information that could help our investigation."
