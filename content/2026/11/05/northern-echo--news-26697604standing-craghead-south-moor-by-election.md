Six candidates are hoping to become the new councillor for Craghead and South Moor in an upcoming by-election.

Voters will head to the polls on Thursday, November 19, after former Reform UK councillor Aaron Hillam resigned from Durham County Council.

The official statement of persons nominated shows candidates from Labour, Reform UK, the Conservatives, Liberal Democrats, Green Party and Restore Britain will contest the seat.

All six are listed as having an address in County Durham and have been validly nominated.

The full list of candidates are:

* Victoria Aymard - Liberal Democrat
* Callum Hutchinson - The Green Party
* Dorothy Ann Luckhurst - Conservative Party
* Mark Lee Taylor - Reform UK
* Jean Thomson - Restore Britain
* Les Timbey - Labour Party

## Why is the by-election being held?

Mr Hillam's resignation triggered the contest after he moved away from County Durham.

His departure reduced Reform UK's majority on Durham County Council to just one.

A Reform UK Durham spokesman previously said Mr Hillam was "no longer able to represent his residents to the level he would have liked" after moving away.

The deadline to apply for a postal vote is 5pm on Wednesday, November 4, while applications for proxy votes and free Voter Authority Certificates must be made by 5pm on Wednesday, November 11.

A separate by-election will take place on the same day for the Craghead and South Stanley ward of Stanley Town Council.

Polling day is Thursday, November 19.
