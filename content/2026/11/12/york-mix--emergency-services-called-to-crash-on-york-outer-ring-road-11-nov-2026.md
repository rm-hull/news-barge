Emergency services went to a two-vehicle crash in York last night (Wednesday).

Acomb and Tadcaster fire crews went to the incident on the **A1237 near Copmanthorpe** at**6.51pm**.

All the occupants were out of the vehicles when they arrived.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews isolated the electric vehicle and relocated the second vehicle to a safer position away for the carriageway.

“The incident was left in care of police on scene.”
