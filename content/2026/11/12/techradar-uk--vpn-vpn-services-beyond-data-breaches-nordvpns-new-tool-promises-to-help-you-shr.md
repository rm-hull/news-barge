## What NordVPN's Digital footprint checks

Data leaks grab headlines, particularly after global data breaches jumped 13 percent. But forgotten message boards, unmanaged apps, and location breadcrumbs leak just as much personal information.

"People know about data breaches, but that’s only part of the picture. Your data is also out there because of accounts you forgot about, profiles that were built about you, and everyday browsing. Digital footprint shows you the full extent of your exposure and tells you what to do about it,” said Domininkas Virbickas, product director at NordVPN.

To find that material, the system scans open-source intelligence across the web. It flags lingering profiles you abandoned years ago, highlights exposed geographic data, and maps out what a curious stranger could dig up on you in minutes.

## Does it actually remove my data?

In short, no, it doesn't remove data.

NordVPN is clear that you cannot scrub your past from the internet with a single click. The scanner does not act as an automated takedown service. Instead, it grades your overall exposure, guides you through manual steps to minimize your profile, and monitors whether your trail shrinks or grows over time.

Trimming those public scraps still matters. Fewer public records mean data brokers have less to trade, which cuts your risk of identity theft. It also stops automated scoring systems from misjudging your credit score based on scattered online footprints.

![Inside NordVPN’s new Digital footprint - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/HrMIeLRd4B8/maxresdefault.jpg)

Because the tool focuses on guidance rather than automated removal, the legwork ultimately falls on you. You still need to manually shut down abandoned accounts, adjust privacy settings, and ask data brokers to delete your files. Think of the feature as an audit: it shows you where the leaks are, but you have to do the manual cleanup yourself.

The addition also highlights how standalone VPNs are shifting their priorities. Basic connection encryption is no longer enough to win over subscribers on its own. For existing NordVPN users, the Digital footprint checker adds a helpful diagnostic layer, provided you are willing to spend the time following through on its recommendations.
