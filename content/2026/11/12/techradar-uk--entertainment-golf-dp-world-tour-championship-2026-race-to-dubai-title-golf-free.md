The 2026 DP World Tour season reaches its climax this week as the Earth course at Jumeirah Golf Estates welcomes the 50 leading players on the points list. Spain’s Eugenio Chacarra currently sits top of the standings but with 2,000 points up for grabs in Dubai, several players can still deny him the Race to Dubai title and the $3 million winners’ cheque.

While Chacarra remains in pole position after winning three times on the DP World Tour this season, McIlroy ominously sits in second place after finishing third at last week’s Abu Dhabi Aldar Championship. The six-time major winner knows that a victory in Dubai would see him secure a fifth successive title and eighth overall, which would equal Colin Montgomerie's record.

American Patrick Reed is also in with a strong chance of claiming the season-ending crown. Should he triumph in the UAE, then he’ll need Chacarra to finish third or lower to claim the title. There is also an outside chance that Jon Rahm, who won in Abu Dhabi, could top the rankings, but he’ll need to win and see Chacarra and McIlroy finish well down the leaderboard.

The DP World Tour Championship will also determine who gets their hands on the 10 PGA Tour cards on offer for next year. Chacarra has also secured one place but the likes of Casey Jarvis, Jayden Schaper and Daniel Hillier will want a strong finish in Dubai to secure their place on the lucrative North American tour.
