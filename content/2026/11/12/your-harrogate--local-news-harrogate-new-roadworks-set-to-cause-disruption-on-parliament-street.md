A lane closure on Parliament Street could cause delays in Harrogate town centre.

Yorkshire Water began repair and maintenance work yesterday (Wednesday).

The works are set to remain in place for nine days and are due to finish on Friday 20th November.

The lane closure comes as Yorkshire Water continues separate work on nearby Crescent Road, where a road closure is scheduled to remain in place until Friday 27th November.
