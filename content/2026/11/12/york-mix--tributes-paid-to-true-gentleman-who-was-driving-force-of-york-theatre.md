Tributes are being paid to a powerhouse of the York theatre scene.

Jim Welsman died in York Hospital on Tuesday. He was 87.

A performer and tireless cheerleader of artistic endeavours across the city, Jim was described as “a driving force in support of amateur theatre in York”.

He performed with York Opera in the 1980s. But perhaps his greatest love was musical theatre.

Jim was behind the launch of the York New Musical Festival in 2013, and to celebrate revealed the “six best musicals” in an article for ***YorkMix***.

Among those paying tribute today was Alan Park, chair of Theatre@41 on Monkgate. Jim was a former chair.

Alan said: “Everything we’re able to do today at Theatre@41 is because of Jim Welsman. For over five years he guided Theatre@41, embedding us in the heart of York’s community theatre family.

“After stepping down as chair, he remained one of our most loyal supporters, attending nearly every show.

“As I accompanied him to his seat he would often share advice and stories of 41 Monkgate. I never tired of hearing them. We shall miss him hugely, and remain forever grateful for everything he gave Theatre@41.

“Our love and thoughts are with Alan, Alicia, Stuart and Tara.”

York Musical Theatre Company wrote: “Jim was our chairman from 1999 to 2012 and became a life member of the company in 2006.

“Over many years he gave a huge amount to YMTC, on stage and behind the scenes, and many of our members past and present have fond memories of performing and working alongside him.”

Here are some more tributes.
