The council-run Turkish Baths are forecast £394,000 over budget, mostly on income. The Convention Centre is £109,000 over as the Studio 2 works shut a car park.

Harrogate’s Turkish Baths are forecast to finish the year **£394,000 over budget**, according to North Yorkshire Council’s latest budget report. Most of the gap,**£239,000**, is income the baths are not expected to earn.

The figures are in the second quarter revenue budget report for 2026-27. It goes to the council’s Executive on **Tuesday 17 November**.

## What the report says about the baths

The report describes the Turkish Baths as “an ongoing pressure”. It splits the £394,000 three ways:

| Turkish Baths, 2026-27 forecast | Over budget |
| --- | --- |
| Income below budget | £239,000 |
| Staffing | £81,000 |
| Other running costs | £74,000 |
| **Total**|**£394,000** |

The baths sit in the council’s Sport and Active Wellbeing Operations budget, which is forecast **£486,000** over in total. The Turkish Baths account for most of that. Across all the council’s leisure centres, the net pressure is put at**£92,000**.

This is not the first warning. In August the first quarter report named “underperformance at the Turkish Baths”, where “income targets remain challenging”. The second quarter report is the first to put a figure on it.

The report does not say what the council plans to do about the baths specifically. It says a pricing review across the service has not yet had its full effect.

## Convention Centre: £109,000 over, partly because of Studio 2

Harrogate Convention Centre is forecast **£109,000** over budget. That is slightly better than the £129,000 forecast in the first quarter.

The report gives these reasons:

* **£64,000** less car parking income, because the centre’s second car park is closed for the Studio 2 refurbishment
* **£30,000** in compensation costs for mechanical and electrical failures
* staffing overspends, mainly on casual staff

Savings of **£77,000** on contractors offset part of that. Lettings income is expected to be £27,000 better than budget.

Catering income is forecast **£352,000** below budget. The report says matching savings on food, drink, staff and equipment cancel that out, so it has no effect on the bottom line.

## The other Harrogate lines

* **Knaresborough Castle** is one of the bright spots. The report credits “strong income performance for events and admissions” at the castle, alongside Skipton Town Hall.
* **Weight management in the Harrogate area:** a change in how Public Health grant is shared out has left a**£181,000** gap. The report says the former Harrogate weight management programme is now part of the council’s Healthy You service.
* **Parking:** the county-wide parking budget is forecast**£1.583 million** over. The report blames income the council assumed from “rebalancing” parking tariffs across the county, which has not been approved. That forecast is unchanged since the first quarter.

## The council’s overall position

Across all its services, the council now forecasts a year-end overspend of **£466,000**. That is 0.07% of its £650 million net budget, and a big improvement on the**£10.3 million** overspend forecast at the first quarter.

The report credits savings across directorates and a review of High Needs funding, the money for pupils with special educational needs, after recent government announcements. It warns that children’s and adult social care remain under the most pressure.

## What it means for you

* **Prices and opening at the Turkish Baths are unchanged for now.** The report sets out forecasts, not decisions. Current session prices are on the Turkish Baths website.
* **Parking charges have not changed.** The tariff rebalancing the budget assumed still needs approval before it can happen.
* **You can watch the Executive.** It meets at 11am on Tuesday 17 November in The Grand at County Hall, Northallerton. The agenda and papers are online.
