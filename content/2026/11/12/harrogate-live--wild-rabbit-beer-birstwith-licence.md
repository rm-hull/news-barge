Wild Rabbit Beer has asked to store and sell its fruit lagers online from Oak Tree Cottage in Birstwith, 9am to 9pm. Nobody could drink on site.

Wild Rabbit Beer Company, a new lager brand, has applied for a licence to store its beer and sell it online from a cottage in Birstwith. The application covers Oak Tree Cottage, Birstwith, HG3 3AW.

The company applied to North Yorkshire Council for a new premises licence. Its public notice appeared in the Harrogate Advertiser on 22 October.

## What the licence would allow

The notice asks for:

* sale of alcohol for drinking off the premises only
* from 9am to 9pm, Monday to Sunday
* storage of Wild Rabbit beer, sent straight to customers who order it

“There will be no on-site consumption of alcohol,” the notice says. It is not a shop or a bar. The cottage would act as the store and dispatch point for online orders.

## What Wild Rabbit sells

The brand calls itself “refreshment for everyday adventure”. On its website it describes the beer as “a crisp, clean, British 3.4% lager, alpine-inspired and lifted with fruit”.

There are two flavours, White Peach and Black Cherry. Both are gluten free, the company says. They come in cases of 12 cans of 330ml. The website lists each case at £29.50, with free delivery on orders over £50. Buyers must be 18 or over, and whoever takes in the delivery may be asked for ID.

The website says the beer is “available to buy in 2 weeks” and that the company is “now open to trade orders” from pubs and bars.

## Who is behind it

The company is Wild Rabbit Beer Ltd, registered at Companies House on 19 May 2026. Its registered office is in York. It was first registered as Wild Rabbit Brewing Company Limited.

The brand has signed the television presenter and adventurer Helen Skelton. Its website names her as its “Director of Everyday Adventure”, brand ambassador and investor.

## What it means for you

The deadline for comments on the application was 5 November. The council’s list of open Harrogate-area licence applications does not show it. We could not find a decision on the council’s website.

The council’s licensing team is at [licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk.)
