A new £1.06 million funding allocation to tackle rough sleeping across York and North Yorkshire has been welcomed by Harrogate Homeless Project, although the charity does not expect to receive funding directly from the first phase of the programme.

The funding was approved by the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority cabinet on 6 November as part of the Government’s Rough Sleeping Programme. It will be shared between City of York Council and North Yorkshire Council to provide accommodation and support for people sleeping rough.

Of the £1,062,675 allocated for the first year, City of York Council will receive £484,314 and North Yorkshire Council £578,361.

The programme is intended to offer people sleeping rough a route off the streets by Christmas, with the funding supporting rapid accommodation, help for people experiencing long-term rough sleeping and the development of longer-term housing.

## Harrogate Homeless Project explains why it is unlikely to receive funding

Richard Cooper of Harrogate Homeless Project said the charity was unlikely to benefit directly from this particular allocation because it is intended to create additional accommodation spaces.

He said the project generally operates at occupancy rates of more than 90 per cent, although occupancy is currently a little lower because of renovation work taking place in its properties.

With its existing accommodation already largely occupied, the charity said it was not in a position to provide the additional bed spaces that the funding is designed to support.

Harrogate Homeless Project already receives support from North Yorkshire Council and has also received Government funding through the Ending Homelessness in Communities Fund for new and additional services.

Richard welcomed the latest announcement, saying that Government commitments were more meaningful when supported by funding.

> **Richard Cooper, Harrogate Homeless Project:**
>
> It does though represent a very real ‘putting your money where your mouth is’ moment from the Government. Words and good intentions are welcome. When the words are backed up by hard cash that’s even more welcome and makes a real difference to people’s lives.

## Funding forms part of a longer-term regional plan

The first-year allocation is part of a proposed three-year funding programme, with a total of £4.3 million available up to and including 2028/29, subject to confirmation by the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government.

The Combined Authority has also approved a Homelessness and Rough Sleeping Action Plan, which is due to be submitted to Government in early December. Its stated ambition is to make homelessness and rough sleeping rare, brief and non-recurring across the region.

North Yorkshire Council leader Carl Les said the authority was already investing more than £11 million in temporary accommodation to meet increasing demand, alongside plans for supported housing for people with a history of rough sleeping who need longer-term support.

Government figures estimate that 4,793 people were sleeping rough on a single night in England in autumn 2025.

While Harrogate Homeless Project does not expect to receive funding from this particular allocation, its response highlights the different pressures facing homelessness services across the region, and the importance of ensuring that additional accommodation is available for people who need a route off the streets.
