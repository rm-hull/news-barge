Harrogate Homeless Project has welcomed a new Chair as David Thomas steps down after five years in the role, marking a change in leadership as the charity celebrates its 35th anniversary.

The charity was established in 1991 in response to an increase in rough sleeping in Harrogate’s Pinewoods.

From its beginnings as an eight-bed hostel, Harrogate Homeless Project has grown to provide 43 bedspaces across the town, alongside a day centre which receives approximately 10,000 visits every year.

> **Outgoing Chair David Thomas said:**
>
> Leading an organisation that helps the most vulnerable in our community is a privilege and one that I am sad to leave behind. The team are focussed on providing all-round support to people who have reached the lowest point of their lives. The impact they have is considerable. We are fortunate that our area has this expertise available.

David is handing over the role to Lynne Atkin, who lives locally on Leeds Road and has a background in human resources and charitable work.

Lynne has previously been a trustee at Action for Children and served a term as Vice Chair of the Women’s Business Council.

> **Incoming Chair Lynne Atkin said:**
>
> It’s tough for charities right now with rising demand and rising costs. I have been impressed with how Harrogate Homeless Project makes every pound donated count in their mission to change people’s lives.
>
> It’s great to be a part of the team particularly as we celebrate our 35th anniversary. I look forward to shaping the future of this local charity which reflects and supports the compassion that is the characteristic of our area.

Harrogate Homeless Project is encouraging local people to support its work through donations, attending fundraising events and volunteering.

Further information about the charity and how to get involved is available on the Harrogate Homeless Project website.
