Trains between York and Manchester have been disrupted today after a death on the line.

British Transport say a person has died after officers were called to an area of the line near Wakefield.

While emergency services were working at the scene the line was closed.

It has now reopened but disruption is likely till about 7pm.

National Rail says TransPennine Express services between Manchester Piccadilly and York are among those affected.
