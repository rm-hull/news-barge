Castle Howard has been transformed into a Dickensian world for the festive season.

The stately home’s Charles Dickens-themed event extravaganza opened to visitors **today, Thursday 12 November** running until**Sunday 3 January**.

*A Dickens of Christmas at Castle Howard* draws creative inspiration from the works of the great British novelist, bringing his beloved tales to life through installations, soundscapes and projections, with each room representing a Dickens story.

Take a sneak peek into *A Dickens of Christmas* in our video.

CLW Event Design and imitating the dog have created the displays, with a team of over 50 volunteers supporting the preparations.

Charlotte Lloyd Webber, artistic director at CLW Event Design, said: “It has been a pleasure to imagine the stories of Charles Dickens, the British writer most synonymous with Christmas, inside Castle Howard’s historic interior.

“We’ve had so much fun creating the world of Oliver Twist, Ebenezer Scrooge, and other beloved Dickens characters. The creative teams have been working on the experience since January this year, and we have dressed over 80 trees, used 2000 metres of lights, 30,000 baubles and over 2000 velvet and tartan bows.

“Our teams have worked closely with the Castle Howard curators and are presenting collection items as part of the displays that haven’t been seen by visitors before – that’s very exciting for me, and will be a delight for visitors.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/christmas-at-castle-howard-2026-dickens-pa-5.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/christmas-at-castle-howard-2026-dickens-pa-6.webp)

A Victorian carriage will be on display inside the House for the first time as part of the displays. Castle Howard records show the carriage was made in 1879 for Rosalind, 9th Countess of Carlisle.

Curious objects from the 18th and 19th century, including wax figures, historic clocks, model ships and busts of family members will also be display, some for the first time.

“We try and put on an experience that can be enjoyed by all ages,” said Abbi Ollive, visitor attraction director at Castle Howard. “There’s some spectacular sights that are awe-inspiring and really magical. I think people will really love it, whether you know the work of Charles Dickens or not.

“A lot of people have come out just now saying they love how traditional it is – it’s a really traditional, feel good, warm, fuzzy Christmas experience this year.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/A-Dickens-of-a-Christmas-at-Castle-Howard.-Photo-by-James-Drury-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/A-Dickens-of-a-Christmas-at-Castle-Howard.-Photo-by-James-Drury-4.webp)

Castle Howard’s Stable Courtyard is open to Christmas shoppers from 12 November. It’s hosting a Jellycat Shop, the beloved soft toy brand, open daily during the Christmas period. The Farm Shop has a Christmas food-to-order service of local produce, the Courtyard Café is serving a seasonal winter menu, and the Garden Centre is selling British-grown Christmas trees and wreaths.

“I hope that people take away really happy memories,” added Abbi. “Getting the family together, getting your friends together, and coming to have a festive day out.

“And it’s not just the house! There’s the afternoon teas, grabbing a pizza, going on a wintry walk, popping to the playground if the weather permits, the shopping experience – you can have a whole day out here at Castle Howard.

“So we hope people leave with a lot of festive joy in their hearts.”

Find out more and book your tickets at Castle Howard’s website. *A Dickens of a Christmas at Castle Howard* runs 12 November to 3 January.
