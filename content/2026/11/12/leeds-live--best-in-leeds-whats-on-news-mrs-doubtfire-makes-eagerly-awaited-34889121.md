# Mrs Doubtfire makes eagerly-awaited Leeds Grand Theatre stop with families and Leeds United fans delighted

## Mrs Doubtfire will be staged at Leeds Grand Theatre until Sunday, November 29

Mrs Doubtfire has opened to much excitement at Leeds Grand Theatre with the musical even delivering for fans of the city’s football team.

Having delighted audiences on London’s West End and Broadway, 2026, has seen Mrs Doubtfire embark on its first UK tour. The addictively riotous production is currently enjoying a three-week stint at Leeds Grand Theatre.

Gabriel Vick stepping into the legendary Robin Williams’ body suit and wig in the title role and as alter ego Daniel Hillard. Of course, an unenviable task but one the talented star gives everything to make his own.

For those unfamiliar, Mrs Doubtfire follows the end of a marriage between Daniel and Miranda - the latter played by Kara Lily Hayworth. As well as the impact it has on their children.

Set in San Francisco, Daniel, in a quest to still see his kids, decides to impersonate an aging Scottish nanny…as you do. Mrs Doubtfire is viewed as a rites-of-passage 1990s film for a generation of people so there has been much interest in its presentation in theatres.

The comedy musical delivers no shortage of laughs with a strong heart at its core. Ryan Pidgen and Jairus McClanahan, who play partners Frank and Andre, have great chemistry in the production, which is directed by Jerry Zaks, and constantly delight when they are on-stage.

Them being prominent in arguably the show’s best number, the Donna Summer-inspired, Make Me a Woman - riffing wonderfully with Vick as Daniel transforms to Mrs Doubtfire.

The latter described as looking like a cross between ‘grandma and Shrek’. Although, fittingly given the Leeds stop, there is actually a little something of the Bo' Selecta! about the Mrs Doubtfire mask.

Vick, reprising the lead after helping launch Mrs Doubtfire in the UK back in 2022, brings so much energy and enthusiasm to the titular character. He takes the audience with him on the journey - as unbelievable as it might be. In essence, Vick has the weight of whether the audience goes with the show on his shoulders. Judging by the enthusiasm on Wednesday evening they were with him.

In Daniel’s role, as an aspiring TV star in the musical, Vick produced some hilarious impressions. His Donald Trump raising many laughs. A nice touch coming with his brief portrayal of football and broadcast legend, the unbelievable, Chris Kamara, who represented Leeds United in his playing days.

Cheers came at a further reference to the city’s famous football club. Mrs Doubtfire proclaiming she ‘always watches Leeds on the telly’.

Probably the funniest scene takes shape in the form of a set piece involving a cake as our lead desperately tries to conceal his dual identities. Much laughter rumbled around the historic Leeds city centre venue as the chaos unfolded.

The three Hillard children, led by Aaliya Mai as Lydia, are all afforded an opportunity to express themselves. Song What The Hell gives them their greatest prominence. While much of the emotional core is experienced through Mai’s interactions with Vick.

The production reaches an endearingly farcical conclusion at restaurant La Rosa as the big secret struggles to be contained. Undoubtedly, Mrs Doubtfire has been one of Leeds Grand Theatre’s eagerly-anticipated productions of the year and it is more than worth the wait with it being ideal for those after a little family fun as festive season takes shape in the city.

Musical Mrs Doubtfire is big, bold and brilliant with lots of sentiment. There’s a special experience waiting for you, poppets.
