Find out how to watch England vs Croatia for free from anywhere in the world as two frequent foes meet in Matchday 5 of the Nations League 2026/27.

England have responded in ruthless fashion to a tournament-opening 3-2 defeat by Spain, beating Czechia (twice) and Croatia by an aggregate 12-0. The meat in that sandwich was a 7-0 behind-closed-doors win in Rijeka that served notice of Thomas Tuchel letting off the Three Lions handbrake – a point guarantees a spot in the quarter-finals. Harry Kane scored twice that night and the England captain will surpass Peter Shilton as his country's most-capped male footballer if he plays as expected.

Keep an eye out, too, for Jobe Bellingham, who joins older brother Jude in an England squad for the first time. If they feature, they'll be England's fourth set of post-war brothers after Nathaniel and Trevoh Chalobah, Gary and Phil Neville and Bobby and Jack Charlton.

Croatia have revenge on their minds after that embarrassing October loss. After beginning his second stint in the dugout with a 2-1 win against Czechia, Slaven Bilic has lost three in a row, albeit a 2-1 reverse against world champions Spain last time out was an improved performance post-Rijeka. With 41-year-old captain Luka Modric still anchoring the midfield, the 2018 World Cup runners-up still have quality and in fit-again Josko Gvardiol and Luka Vuskovic a center-back pairing the envy of many European nations.
