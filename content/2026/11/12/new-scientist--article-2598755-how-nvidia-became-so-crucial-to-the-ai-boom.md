You have almost certainly seen the headlines – Jensen Huang, the CEO of Nvidia, has resigned after admitting to an affair with Alice Huang (no relation), who is suspected by US intelligence officials of being a Chinese spy (a claim China dismisses). The story has become so big because Nvidia, and by extension Jensen, are at the heart of the AI revolution. But just why has this one company become so important?

For me, Nvidia will always be a video games company, its logo splashed up at the start of the play sessions of my youth. Its graphics processing units (GPUs) were specifically designed for the intense number-crunching required to create a 3D scene from triangles. Initially, these were a few hundred triangles, then thousands, and now hundreds of thousands as computing power has increased, allowing ever more realistic computer graphics.
