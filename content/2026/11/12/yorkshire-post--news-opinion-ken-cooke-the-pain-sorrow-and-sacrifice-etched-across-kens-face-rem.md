## York’s last surviving Normandy veteran, at 101 years of age, is a beacon of inspiration. Yesterday, at the unveiling of the York Normandy Veterans’ Memorial Bench, on the Soldiers’ Lawn inside York Cemetery, the cameras of this newspaper’s photographers trained on Mr Ken Cooke. Quite why he is not a Sir is a bafflement.

The age–old adage that a picture, or a photograph, paints a thousand words was encapsulated in Mr Cooke’s face. A stirring combination of pain, anguish and loss, as well as a visible concern of his, that is well-documented, that future generations may lose contact with the reasons for his visage, yesterday.

Ken’s youthfully exuberant and most wicked sense of humour makes him a firm favourite with children as he indefatigably tours schools, with a smile on his face and laughter in his heart, to gently urge young people to ward off war. But the sadness, loss and respect that we see in his face: lest we forget.
