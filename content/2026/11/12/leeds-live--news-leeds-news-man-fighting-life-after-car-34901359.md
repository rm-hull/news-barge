# Man fighting for life after car fleeing police hits him in Leeds

## The pedestrian, a man in his 40s, was rushed to hospital

A man is fighting for his life after he was hit by a car fleeing police in Leeds. The man was hit by a silver BMW X1 on Easy Road, in East End Park, at 12.35pm yesterday, on Wednesday, November 11. This was moments after the vehicle failed to stop for West Yorkshire Police.

The pedestrian, a man in his 40s, was rushed to hospital where he is being treated for life-changing and potentially life-threatening injuries. The driver of the BMW failed to stop at the scene of the collision. The car was later recovered by police in an underground car park on Goodman Street in the Hunslet area of the city.

A force spokesperson said: "Officers from Leeds CID would like to speak to anyone who has witnessed any part of this incident, including the vehicle being left in the car park on Goodman Street. Members of the public are also asked to check if they have captured any relevant dashcam or CCTV footage."

Due to prior police contact, a mandatory referral has been made to the Independent Office for Police Conduct. You can call police on 101, quoting 13260641934.
