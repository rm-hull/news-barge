# England player ratings: Who shared the plaudits with history boy Harry Kane?

## England reached the quarter-finals of the Nations League on a night when Harry Kane made history for his country.

But it was not just the Three Lions captain, on the night when he reached a record 126 caps for his country, who impressed in a 2-1 win over Croatia that was not sparkling, but professional.

**Jordan Pickford**– not much to do and no chance with the goal; the battle with James Trafford to be No 1 has not moved forward** 6Trent Alexander-Arnold**– it was his attacking play that got England going; a much-needed performance in his quest to win Thomas Tuchel over** 8Jarell Quansah**– made a big tackle late on and was fouled for the penalty but he will be remembered for his sloppy defending to set up Croatia's equaliser** 5Dan Burn**– beaten too easily as Croatia pushed for a second equaliser** 6Lewis Hall**– did not shine as in the last window, but looks a good bet at left-back** 7Elliot Anderson**– no lack of energy and being the anchorman did not stop him shooting** 6**
