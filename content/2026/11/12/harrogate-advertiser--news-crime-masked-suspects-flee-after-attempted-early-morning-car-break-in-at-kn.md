North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a vehicle was targeted in Knaresborough.

The incident happened at around 2.20am when two people wearing face coverings attempted to gain access to a car on Chestnut Drive.

The suspects were disturbed and made off towards the Boroughbridge Road area.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “While this attempt was unsuccessful, it serves as a timely reminder that vehicle criminals are often looking for quick and easy opportunities.

“Please take a moment to check your own vehicle security today and encourage friends, family and neighbours to do the same.

“A few simple precautions can make a significant difference and help prevent crime before it happens.”

If you saw anything, or have any information including CCTV, doorbell or dash cam footage capturing the incident, you should get in contact with the police.

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260221628 when providing any details regarding the incident.
