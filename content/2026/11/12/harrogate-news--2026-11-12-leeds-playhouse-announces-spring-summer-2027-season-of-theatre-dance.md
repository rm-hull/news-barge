Leeds Playhouse has announced its Spring/Summer 2027 season, bringing together major theatre productions, contemporary dance, comedy, family entertainment and new work from across the UK.

The programme includes five productions from Leeds Playhouse, alongside a wide-ranging schedule of visiting shows across its Quarry Theatre, Courtyard Theatre and Bramall Rock Void spaces.

## Major productions include Shakespeare and Frankenstein

The season’s produced programme includes a new co-production of **Romeo and Juliet**, involving Leeds Playhouse, ETT, Royal Lyceum Theatre and Nottingham Playhouse. The production will be directed by award-winning director Chelsea Walker.

Other productions include **Sirens**, a new play about desire, secrecy and survival written and directed by Tom Wright, and** The Secret Garden**, a contemporary adaptation by Linda Marshall Griffiths which reimagines the classic story through the journey of a young refugee in Leeds.

The programme also features a new adaptation of Mary Shelley’s **Frankenstein**, by Jeff James, exploring creation, humanity and the ethics of artificial intelligence.

The season will also see the return of the cult musical **Little Shop of Horrors**, in a new staging directed by Associate Artistic Director Jamie Sophia Fletcher.

## Comedy and visiting theatre

The visiting programme opens with **The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night-Time**, starring Joe McFadden and Charlie Brooks.

Comedy features prominently, with Russell Kane headlining Chuckl Comedy Club, alongside Henry Paker and a two-night run from Ross Noble.

Rufus Hound and Susie Blake will star in the dark comedy thriller **The Other Side of Murder**, while Agatha Christie’s** The Hollow** arrives following successful tours of Murder on the Orient Express and Death on the Nile.

Opera North will also present **Orpheus**, bringing together Monteverdi’s music and Indian classical music.

## Dance and family entertainment

Dance performances include Phoenix Dance Theatre’s **Sincerely, The Breakfast Club**, the National Youth Dance Company’s** From England With Love**, choreographed by Hofesh Shechter, and Ballet Black’s** Unbound**.

Family audiences will be able to see balletLORENT’s **Hansel & Gretel and the Witch**, contemporary circus production** RPM**by Gorilla Circus, and** Dog Man The Musical**, based on the bestselling books by Dav Pilkey.

## Theatre exploring social issues and local stories

The programme also includes productions exploring social issues, personal histories and questions of identity.

These include the return of **A Thousand Splendid Suns**, adapted from Khaled Hosseini’s novel,** Housemates**, a story of friendship and disability rights, and** Managed Approach** by Open Aire Theatre, exploring Leeds’ first legalised red-light district.

Other productions include **Grimeboy**, written by Casey Bailey and directed by Madeleine Kludje, and** The Nature of Forgetting** by Theatre Re.

The Bramall Rock Void will host a programme of contemporary touring work, including **Son of a Bitch** and**The Entertainers**, alongside Staging the Archive: Black Theatre Makers in Britain, 1900-1948, a festival celebrating overlooked Black theatre-makers.

## A season for the whole city

Leeds Playhouse Artistic Director Tom Wright said the programme was intended to bring together a wide range of work for audiences across the city.

> **Tom Wright, Artistic Director of Leeds Playhouse:**
>
> Our Spring/Summer 2027 season is for the whole of Leeds. You might come for Ross Noble, an Agatha Christie or Little Shop of Horrors, and find a play like Managed Approach on the stage next door, telling a story from this city’s own recent history. We want the Playhouse to be a place where all of that belongs to everyone who lives here. A city without a theatre is a city without a heart, and this season is full of heart.

Tickets for the Spring/Summer 2027 season are on sale now through the Leeds Playhouse website. www.leedsplayhouse.org.uk

Leeds Playhouse is based at Playhouse Square, Quarry Hill, Leeds.
