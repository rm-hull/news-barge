A fully funded agricultural development programme created in memory of farmer Mary Goodall is helping prepare the next generation of farming leaders, with applications now open for its 2027 cohort.

The Goodall Agri-Development Pathway, established through a legacy gift to the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, provides training and development opportunities for people working in farming and related industries across the North of England.

Members of Mary Goodall’s family recently visited the Society’s headquarters at the Great Yorkshire Showground to learn more about the programme and meet those involved in delivering her legacy.

Launched in 2023, the programme has so far supported 22 alumni and has 12 current participants, known as Pathfinders. It is expected to take up to 12 further participants for the 2027 cohort.

## Remembering a lifelong commitment to farming

Mary Goodall was a farmer throughout her life and was described by her family as forward-thinking, determined and welcoming, with many friends in the farming community.

> **Mary Goodall’s family:**
>
> We are delighted that her legacy is supporting young people in agriculture through a programme that reflects her lifelong commitment to farming. The Pathway is an impressive example of how practical development and shared experience can help the sector’s future leaders to thrive.

They added that Mary had been a farmer all her life, making the legacy particularly meaningful to the family.

The gift to the Yorkshire Agricultural Society was made to create opportunities for young people to progress their careers and contribute to the future of agriculture.

## Training and European study trip

The Goodall Agri-Development Pathway consists of eight face-to-face modules, including farm visits, conferences, expert speakers and a European study trip.

Topics covered include teamwork, leadership, media training, business growth and direct sales, sustainable farming and diversification, farm administration and human resources, and lean management.

Participants are given the opportunity to develop practical knowledge, confidence and a wider understanding of the agricultural industry to support their career ambitions.

David Tite, Manager at the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, said:

> **David Tite, Yorkshire Agricultural Society:**
>
> We are incredibly grateful to Mary for her gift which has enabled us to roll out a programme that develops the next generation of farmers through modules and a study trip in Europe. It’s a unique and life changing programme and wonderful to see that her legacy lives on in the industry she loved so much.

Those completing the programme will be recognised at a celebratory awards presentation at the Great Yorkshire Show in 2027.

## Applications open for 2027

The opportunity is open to people aged between 23 and 40 who work in farming or allied industries in the North of England.

The deadline for applications for the next cohort is Friday 20 November 2026. Places are limited, and applicants will be interviewed by a selection panel.

Successful applicants are asked to pay a £250 deposit, which is refundable on completion of the Pathway.

## How to apply

Anyone interested in applying for the Goodall Agri-Development Pathway can contact the Yorkshire Agricultural Society’s Charitable Activities team by email at [education@yas.co.uk.](mailto:education@yas.co.uk.)

Further information is available from the Yorkshire Agricultural Society.
