Gail’s will open its first Yorkshire bakery in Harrogate on Tuesday 17th November.

The bakery has taken over the former Russell & Bromley shop on James Street, where invited guests will get a first look at a preview event on Monday 16th November.

From Tuesday, customers will be able to eat in or take away, with sourdough loaves, pastries, cakes and speciality coffee on the menu.

Speaking about the Harrogate branch, a Gail’s spokesperson said:

> “The opening will bring Gail's craft baking to the community, including creating a number of craft baking, barista, and management roles.”

The bakery also plans to donate surplus baked goods to local charities through its partnership with Neighbourly.

Gail's now boasts 170 venues across the UK.
