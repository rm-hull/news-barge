## Why we recommend it

The Hbada E3 Ultra (Gen 5) takes a more fancy approach to ergonomic seating than a conventional office chair. Its Full-Body Active Tracking System combines a 12-rail linkage, eight-axis synchronized movement, and spring damping to help the chair follow posture changes.

Its three-zone lumbar system features adjustable wings that rotate approximately 80 degrees, along with 60mm of height adjustment and 32mm of tracking movement. This will maintain lower-back contact whether you're working upright or leaning back.

The most unusual feature is without doubt the heated 4D massage system which offers kneading, tapping, and vibration functions. There are three massage intensity settings and three heating levels, with separate controls for each. Heating sessions last five minutes.

The chair also includes 8D armrests, a 4D headrest, adjustable seat depth, and breathable mesh upholstery. The backrest reclines to 150 degrees and locks into four positions.
