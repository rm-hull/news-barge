Homes in council tax bands A to D get £100 from January 2027 via North Yorkshire Council. About 36,000 Harrogate and Knaresborough homes are in those bands.

Households in council tax bands A to D will get a one-off **£100 Household Energy Payment**, and North Yorkshire Council expects to start paying it** from January 2027**. About** 36,000 homes** in the Harrogate and Knaresborough constituency are in those four bands.

The council confirmed its plans on Tuesday. It is still waiting for detailed guidance from the government. It says residents **do not need to contact it** about the payment, and it will tell everyone when more information is available.

## Who qualifies

The government set out the rules in its Autumn Budget explainer on 28 October. A household in England qualifies if it:

* is liable for council tax on the property
* lives there as its main home
* is in council tax band A, B, C or D

Most households that are exempt from council tax will also be included. The government says the full eligibility criteria will be published later.

Some homes will not get it, even in bands A to D:

* second homes
* empty properties
* households where the person living there is not responsible for paying council tax

Up to 20 million households in England are expected to be eligible.

## How you get paid

**If you pay council tax by direct debit**, you do not need to do anything. The £100 goes straight into your bank account.** If you do not pay by direct debit**, the council will invite you to apply. North Yorkshire Council says direct debit payers are likely to be paid more quickly because it already holds their bank details. You can sign up for direct debit on the council’s website.

The government warns that nobody other than your council should contact you about claiming the money. If you are unsure whether a message is genuine, check with the council.

You can check your council tax band on GOV.UK. Bands are based on what a home was worth on 1 April 1991. Band D covers a 1991 value of £68,001 to £88,000, and a band D home in Harrogate pays £2,514.58 in 2026-27.

## How many homes it covers here

Valuation Office Agency figures for 31 March 2026 count **49,600 homes** in the Harrogate and Knaresborough constituency. Of those:

* band A: 6,210
* band B: 10,230
* band C: 12,240
* band D: 7,320

That makes **36,000 homes in bands A to D**, about** 73%** of the total. The VOA’s stock of properties tables give the full breakdown.

That share is lower than the national picture. Across England, **81%** of homes are in bands A to D. Across North Yorkshire it is**75%** (232,380 of 308,570 homes).

In the Skipton and Ripon constituency, which takes in Ripon and Nidderdale, **34,340 of 48,610 homes** are in bands A to D, about**71%**.

These are counts of properties, not of households that will be paid. Second homes and empty homes are included in the VOA figures but are not eligible, so the number of payments will be lower.

## What it means for you

* **Band A to D and paying by direct debit:** nothing to do. Expect the £100 from January 2027.
* **Band A to D and paying another way:** watch for an invitation from North Yorkshire Council, or switch to direct debit now.
* **Band E or above and struggling with bills:** the government is giving councils**£100 million** in total for discretionary support to households that miss out. North Yorkshire Council has not yet published how its share will work.
* **On means-tested benefits:** you may also be eligible for the**£150 Warm Home Discount**, which is a separate scheme. Check eligibility on GOV.UK.
