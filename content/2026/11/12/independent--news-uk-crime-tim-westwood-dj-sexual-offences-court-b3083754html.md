Former DJ Tim Westwood has pleaded not guilty to two additional sex offences dating back to 1991 and 2004.

His latest not guilty pleas mean he now denies a total of 17 charges against nine women between 1983 and 2016.

At Southwark Crown Court on Thursday, the 69-year-old pleaded not guilty to indecently assaulting a teenage girl aged 16 or 17 in Hammersmith and Fulham, west London, in 1991.

The former BBC Radio 1 and Capital Xtra DJ also denied sexually assaulting an 18-year-old woman in Wandsworth, south London, in May 2004.

The DJ, of Fitzrovia, in London’s West End, was charged in October 2025 with offences against seven women, which he denies.

![Tim Westwood is facing a raft of accusations dating back to the 1980s, including rape, sexual assault, and indecent assault](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/12/10/01M77GDMYJVY7FKMHEHY7CW8KF.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He is accused of four indecent assaults in the 1980s in London, as well as three indecent assaults at the BBC in the 1990s.

The defendant is also alleged to have raped a woman in a hotel in 1996.

Westwood is further accused of two indecent assaults and one count of rape from the early 2000s at a London address, and two counts of rape at a London address in the 2010s.

He is also alleged to have committed a sexual assault at a nightclub in Stroud, Gloucestershire, in 2010, and at a London music festival in 2016.

Westwood has entered not guilty pleas to all counts and is due to face trial at Southwark Crown Court in January.
