TransPennine Express (TPE) has given its Class 185 trains a makeover and they should be smelling and looking a lot fresher now.

A total of 16,932 Standard Class seat covers have been cleaned, including the bases and backs, alongside 765 First Class seats.

Teams have also worked their way through 10,750 feet of carpet, equivalent to more than two miles.

TPE’s Class 185 trains run on routes serving Selby, Malton, York, Thirsk and Northallerton.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/transpennine-express-train-pa.jpg)

And the makeover isn’t just happening inside.

The vinyl livery on the outside of the trains has also been renewed, helping to keep the fleet looking its best while protecting the exterior.

More than 24,763 feet, or 4.69 miles of vinyl was used to complete the work.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/transpennine-express-train-york-fl.jpg)

**Paul Staples, Engineering, Safety and Sustainability Director at TPE, said:** “There’s a lot of work that goes on behind the scenes to look after our trains.

“Cleaning and renewing thousands of seats and more than two miles of carpet is a big job, but it all helps make journeys more comfortable for our customers.

“We want people to step onboard and find a train that feels clean, fresh and welcoming.”

The operator recently announced a £1 billion investment in 29 new five-car battery electric trains which will eventually join the TPE rolling stock fleet and provide improved capacity and performance as part of and upgrade programme.

In the meantime, the work on the old Class 185s will help keep the existing trains looking their best.
