## European leaders gather in Dublin for the European Political Community summit after two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi produced a prisoner exchange but no ceasefire. US envoys head to Moscow, then to Zelenskyy. We also hear from the EU justice chief Michael McGrath and look at COP31.

## On today's show:

**Sasha Vakulina** explains what the two days of US-mediated peace talks between Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi achieved,.**Shona Murray** reports on the European Political Community gathering in Dublin, where US envoys**Steve Witkoff** and**Jared Kushner** brief**Volodymyr Zelenskyy** on peace talks progress.**Michael McGrath**, EU Commissioner for Democracy, Justice, the Rule of Law and Consumer Protection, discusses** Angela Skujins**about Brussels' proposed crackdown on harmful e-commerce platforms, and about the fight over Poland's Constitutional Tribunal.** Louise Firth**, marine ecologist at University College Cork, asks whether COP31 in Antalya can deliver for the oceans and coastal communities.

A sneak of peek of our new show *World Conversation,*with**Jane Witherspoon**. It launches tonight at 9:10pm CET, with Thailand's Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister** Sihasak Phuangketkeow**.

MEPs **Benedetta Scuderi**(Greens, Italy) and** Markus Ferber** (Christian Democrat, Germany) clash on *The Ring*'s second-season premiere over whether the EU is heading in the right direction.
