A man was seriously injured in a crash in Scarborough last night (Wednesday).

It happened at around 4.30pm on Scalby Road near the Scalby and Newby Library when the 74-year-old pedestrian was hit by a white Suzuki car.

He was taken to hospital with serious injuries where he remains.

#### Crash location

The driver of the Suzuki, a man aged in his 70s, was arrested in connection with the collision. He has been released while under investigation.  
A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are appealing to anyone who was in the area at the time, anyone who witnessed the collision along, with anyone with dashcam footage of the incident, to get in touch if they have not already spoken to the police.”
