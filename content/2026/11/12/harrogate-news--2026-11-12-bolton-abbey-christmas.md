Bolton Abbey has announced its Christmas programme for 2026, with a new-look Father Christmas’ Village, the return of its popular 12 Days of Christmas trail and a series of festive activities for families.

Located in the heart of the North Yorkshire estate, Father Christmas’ Village will feature bushcraft activities, storytelling, festive crafts and Christmas traditions from around the world.

Children will be able to take part in guided fire-lighting, try reindeer hoopla and toast marshmallows, while visitors can explore traditions from Eastern Europe, Scandinavia, America and Australia.

The experience will also include a visit to Father Christmas in his wooden cabin, where children can have their photograph taken and receive a Bolton Abbey Christmas decoration to take home.

## Father Christmas visits

Visits to Father Christmas begin on 21 November and will run each Saturday and Sunday from 9am to 7.30pm until 20 December. Additional sessions will take place on 21, 22 and 23 December.

Each session lasts approximately 90 minutes, with families able to explore the village at their own pace.

Relaxed visits for children with additional needs will be available on 29 November and 21 December, offering the same activities in a quieter environment.

## Christmas markets and festive trail

Bolton Abbey’s Artisan Winter Markets will take place on 5 and 6 December, and again on 12 and 13 December.

The markets will feature stalls selling gifts and seasonal treats from local makers.

The 12 Days of Christmas trail will also return, running from 28 November 2026 to 3 January 2027.

Families can follow a riverside route from the Cavendish Shop, using clues inspired by the traditional Christmas carol to solve a seasonal puzzle on their trail sheet.

## Festive wreath-making workshop

A half-day wreath-making workshop will take place in the Christmas Village on Friday 4 December, from 9.30am to 12.30pm.

Led by Vanessa Wellock, an RHS Chelsea silver medallist and florist to The Devonshire Arms Hotel, the workshop will give participants the opportunity to create their own festive wreath.

Materials will include a compostable straw ring, foliage and pheasant feathers gathered from the Bolton Abbey Estate, alongside biodegradable decorative materials. No previous experience is required, and the session will include mince pies and music.

> **Moira Smith, Visitor Experience and Marketing Manager at Bolton Abbey, said:**
>
> Christmas at Bolton Abbey is always magical, but this year we’re especially excited to invite families to explore Father Christmas’ Village. From bushcraft activities and storytelling to festive traditions from around the world, the experience is designed to give visitors the freedom to enjoy the village together and create memories at their own pace.
>
> Whether it’s meeting Father Christmas, following the ‘12 Days of Christmas’ trail through Strid Wood or making a festive wreath using materials gathered on the Estate, it’s all about celebrating the season and bringing family and friends together in this unique and beautiful setting.

Bolton Abbey Estate covers around 30,000 acres in the Yorkshire Dales and includes woodland, moorland, valleys and waterfalls. The estate also has a range of visitor facilities, including tearooms, shops and car parking.

Further information about the Christmas programme, including booking details, is available on the Bolton Abbey website.
