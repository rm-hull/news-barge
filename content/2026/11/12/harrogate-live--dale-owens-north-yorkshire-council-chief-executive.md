Councillors vote on 18 November on Dale Owens, now chief executive of Gateshead Council, as Richard Flinton's successor. The post pays up to £217,798.

Dale Owens, the chief executive of Gateshead Council, is being recommended as the next chief executive of North Yorkshire Council. The council that runs Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon services will vote on his appointment at Full Council on **Wednesday 18 November**.

If councillors approve it, he is expected to start in **May 2027**. He would replace Richard Flinton, who announced his retirement in August.

## How he was chosen

The council’s Chief Officers Appointments and Disciplinary Committee interviewed candidates on Monday 9 November. The published minutes show the meeting ran from 9am to 3.15pm, with the public excluded for the interviews.

The committee is now recommending that Full Council approves Mr Owens. The Full Council agenda says the appointment is subject to “the completion of all statutory processes and pre-employment checks”.

The council announced the recommendation on Tuesday.

## Who he is

The council says Mr Owens has worked in North Yorkshire before. His career, as the council describes it:

* assistant director in health and adult services at the former North Yorkshire County Council
* director of integrated health and adult services at Gateshead Council
* chief executive of Gateshead Council since 2024

Council leader Carl Les chaired the appointments committee. He said Mr Owens “demonstrated outstanding leadership qualities, a clear strategic vision and a strong commitment to public service”.

Cllr Les added: “This is a very significant role at a time of acute pressures on local government due to reduced national funding and increased demand for some of our most valued but expensive public services.”

Mr Owens said: “I know firsthand what a special place North Yorkshire is with ambitious communities and strong partnerships.”

## Why the handover waits until May

Richard Flinton will stay until May 2027 to oversee the North Yorkshire local elections as returning officer. The council says Mr Owens is expected to take up the post at that point.

Mr Flinton led the council through the April 2023 merger of eight councils into one, which abolished Harrogate Borough Council. The council credits that reorganisation with more than £69 million in savings so far.

## What the job pays

The council’s 2026-27 pay policy statement sets the chief executive’s salary range at **£199,176 to £217,798**. Those figures are at 2025 pay rates, because no national pay award had been agreed when the policy was written.

The same statement says the highest salary at the council, **£217,798**, is paid to the chief executive. The agenda does not say where in the range Mr Owens would start.

## What it means for you

* **Nothing changes for residents yet.** Richard Flinton remains chief executive until May 2027.
* **The chief executive is the council’s most senior officer.** The post leads the staff who run bins, roads, parking, social care and planning across Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon. Councillors still make the policy decisions.
* **You can take part in the meeting.** Anyone who wants to ask a question or make a statement at Full Council must give notice by**midday on Friday 13 November**, according to the agenda. The meeting starts at 10.30am in the Council Chamber at County Hall, Northallerton, and is streamed live.
