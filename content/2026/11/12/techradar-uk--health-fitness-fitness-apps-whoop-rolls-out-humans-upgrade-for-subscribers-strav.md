* **Whoop Humans is rolling out to subscribers now**
* **It lets you connect directly with family and friends**
* **You get full control over the goals and stats shared**

Whoop is expanding its app offerings to let you connect up with friends and family rather than just focusing on your own fitness: it's called Whoop Humans, and it lets you share your stats, goals, and achievements with a selected network of contacts.

It's similar to what you can do with a Strava account, though Whoop is pushing the privacy angle hard: there are granular controls over what's shared and with whom, the option to keep certain stats private permanently or temporarily, and a promise not to "sell or monetize" any of the data collected.

"In a world that's becoming increasingly artificial, we're building something deeply human," says Whoop CEO and founder Will Ahmed. "This is the most verifiably human network ever launched."

"Every account represents a real person with a verified heartbeat. They have real goals, challenges, and achievements. That's what makes Whoop Humans different, and it’s a big area of investment for us," he added.

## Separate to Whoop Teams

![Whoop Humans app](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/iemE7pih2BBNTKdNAboHUo-1200-80.jpg)

Some of the content you can share includes sleep, recovery, strain, and activity data, as well as achievements. There's a new feature too, called Whoop Moments, which you can use to celebrate photos of workouts or milestones.

According to Whoop, 57% of its members already belong to at least one Whoop Team. This adds another dimension beyond leaderboards and groups though — it's more personal and directly, between you and friends and relatives.

Whoop says it's also pushing back against what modern-day social media platforms have come to be, filled with AI-produced content, bots, and clickbait. The idea is to increase accountability, make fitness more fun and social, and have the opportunity to support other Whoop users when they need it.
