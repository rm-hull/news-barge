Armed police will patrol York Christmas Market and other busy areas in the busy festive season.

The increased visibility of firearms officers “forms part of North Yorkshire Police’s ongoing commitment to keeping communities safe during the festive period,” the force says.

Officers will be carrying out high-visibility patrols at locations including the Designer Outlet and it’s Winter Wonderland attraction, as well as other busy areas across the county.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/armed-police-2026-nyp.webp)

Police say they are there “to provide reassurance, deter criminal activity and help protect the public during one of the busiest times of the year”.

The firearms officers are happy to speak to people while on patrol.

People are asked to remain vigilant. If something does not look right, trust your instincts and report it, the police say.

Chief Inspector Mark Proctor, of North Yorkshire Police’s Firearms Support Unit, said: “The run up to Christmas is a special time of year when towns and city centres become busier with residents and visitors enjoying festive events, markets and shopping.

“Our armed patrols are one of a number of policing tactics used to help keep people safe, prevent crime and provide reassurance in crowded places.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/armed-police-north-yorkshire-christmas-nyp.webp)

“These officers are highly trained and are there to protect the public. Their presence should be seen as a positive measure to help ensure everyone can enjoy the festive period safely.

“We would also encourage people to remain alert and report anything that appears suspicious or out of place.

“By working together and looking out for one another, we can help keep North Yorkshire safe this Christmas.”

Traffic is banned from York city centre during the operation of the Christmas Market under an anti-terror order.

This year the market is closed every Tuesday to allow Blue Badge holders to drive into the city centre.
