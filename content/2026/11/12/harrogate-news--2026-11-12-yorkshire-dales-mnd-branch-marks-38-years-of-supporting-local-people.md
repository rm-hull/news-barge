The Yorkshire Dales branch of the Motor Neurone Disease (MND) Association has been supporting local people affected by the condition since 1988, raising funds, increasing awareness and providing practical help to individuals and their families.

The branch is part of a network of almost 90 volunteer branches across England, Wales and Northern Ireland, helping people affected by MND access support within their communities.

Run by volunteers, the Yorkshire Dales branch uses the money raised through its activities to benefit local people affected by the condition. Some funds also contribute towards research aimed at finding a cure.

Support can include specialist equipment, help with the cost of living and assistance with anything that can improve quality of life for someone living with MND and help them make memories with family and friends.

## Monthly coffee and support group in Pannal

One of the branch’s key activities is its monthly Coffee and Catch Up group, held on the first Tuesday of each month from 2pm to 4pm at Weetons Restaurant on Leeds Road, Pannal.

The informal group is hosted by two experienced volunteers and provides an opportunity for people living with MND, along with their family members and friends, to meet others in similar circumstances.

Those attending can enjoy coffee, cake and conversation, while also having the opportunity to access information about MND and the support available.

The group aims to provide a welcoming environment where people can share experiences, find information and enjoy time together.

## Making every day count

The branch’s focus is on making every day count for anyone affected by MND, through practical support, fundraising and raising awareness of the condition.

Its volunteers say the money raised locally is used to make a direct difference to people in the area, while also supporting research into the disease.

## Contact the Yorkshire Dales MND branch

Anyone affected by MND who would like more information about the branch or its Coffee and Catch Up group can contact Alun Owen by email at [alun.owen@mndassociation.org.](mailto:alun.owen@mndassociation.org.)
