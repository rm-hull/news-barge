The explosion occurred at about 7 am in the Shushary area of the city's Pushkinsky district, on Kolpino Highway, leaving the vehicle badly damaged. A passing bus also had its windows shattered by the blast.

Russian news outlet Fontanka reported that **the victim was a colonel serving as chief navigator of the 6th Air and Air Defence Army**. According to the outlet, an** explosive device attached beneath his vehicle was remotely detonated**as he drove away. Fontanka reported that a** family member nearby had been also hurt**.

Russian authorities have not officially identified the serviceman or confirmed the cause of the explosion. The **Investigative Committee has opened a murder investigation**, with forensic experts examining the scene.

The blast comes more than four years after Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. **Several Russian military officials have since been killed in attacks on Russian territory**, some of which have been attributed to Kyiv, although Ukrainian authorities rarely claim responsibility.** Investigators have not established any connection between Thursday's explosion and the war**.
