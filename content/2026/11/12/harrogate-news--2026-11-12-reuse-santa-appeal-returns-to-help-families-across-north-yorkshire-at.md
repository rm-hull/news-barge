Thousands of children could benefit this Christmas as residents across North Yorkshire are encouraged to donate quality pre-loved toys through the return of the Reuse Santa appeal.

The annual appeal gives clean, complete and working toys, games, books and puzzles a second life by passing them on to families who need them most.

Now in its eighth year, the campaign is run by Yorwaste in partnership with North Yorkshire Council, City of York Council and local charity and community organisations.

Donations will be accepted at all household waste recycling centres across York and North Yorkshire from 15 to 29 November 2026.

Last year, more than 5,000 toys were redistributed through the scheme, equivalent to around 80 giant Santa sacks.

## Donations to support local families

Councillor Richard Foster, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for waste services, said:

> “The Reuse Santa campaign does a fantastic job of encouraging people to donate their pre-loved toys rather than throwing them out.
>
> The donations help to support children, young people and families while keeping usable items in circulation.
>
> Christmas can be a time when finances are tight, so the aim of the scheme is to relieve some of the burden.
>
> All we ask is that the gifts are in a condition that you would like to receive, and please remember that we can’t accept toys powered by mains electricity.”

Donations will be collected and distributed through a network of partners, including North Yorkshire Council’s Early Help service, community groups within the City of York Good Place Network, Colburn Community Hub, the British Heart Foundation, the Daisy Chain Project and The Recycle Project CIC.

Some items may also be passed on to charity shops.

Councillor Jenny Kent, City of York Council’s executive member for environment and climate emergency, said:

> “Reuse Santa is a wonderful example of how a simple act of generosity can make a real difference to families across York and North Yorkshire.
>
> By donating good quality toys, books and games that are no longer needed, we can all help bring joy to local children while reducing waste and giving items a second life.
>
> We know that Christmas can be an expensive time for many families, and this scheme helps support those who may be struggling while encouraging a more sustainable approach to sharing and reusing.
>
> I encourage anyone with clean, complete and working toys to consider donating them and help to make Christmas special for a local child.”

## Where to donate in the Harrogate and Ripon areas

Residents in the Harrogate area can donate at:

**Harrogate Household Waste Recycling Centre** Wetherby Road, Harrogate, HG3 1DE.**West Harrogate Household Waste Recycling Centre**  
 Penny Pot Lane, Harrogate, HG3 2SN.

In Ripon, donations can be taken to:

**Ripon Household Waste Recycling Centre**  
 Dallamires Crescent, Dallamires Lane, Ripon, HG4 1TT.

Knaresborough does not have a permanent household waste recycling centre, so residents can use the facilities in Harrogate or Ripon.

Donations must be clean, complete and in working condition. Toys powered by mains electricity cannot be accepted.

Further details of recycling centres across North Yorkshire and York, including locations and opening times, are available from North Yorkshire Council and City of York Council.

The Reuse Santa appeal runs from 15 to 29 November 2026.
