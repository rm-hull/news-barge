Newmedica Harrogate has marked its second anniversary by continuing its partnership with Harrogate Town's walking football club.

The club’s Community Foundation runs the sessions to give people a chance to stay active, meet others and keep playing football.

The clinic has also sponsored a Harrogate Town matchday and a pitchside banner.

Steve Kirk, operations director and partner at Newmedica Harrogate, joined the players for a recent match and scored twice in his team’s 8-6 win.

He said:

> “Supporting local sport is another way for us to play an active role in the community we serve, and we’re thrilled to continue our partnership with Harrogate Town AFC.”

Lynsey Barraclough, head of the Community Foundation at Harrogate Town AFC, said:

> “Having Steve join us on the pitch at our recent match was great fun, and we can’t thank him enough.
>
> “Team sport plays a vital role in the local community, not only by improving health and wellbeing but by creating an inclusive environment where people can socialise and feel connected, which is exactly what our walking football initiative is designed to do.”

Since opening in November 2024, Newmedica Harrogate has carried out more than 3,372 appointments and treatments.

Patients first seen at the clinic have gone on to have nearly 2,350 cataract operations.

The clinic sees NHS and private patients from across North Yorkshire, including Harrogate, Ripon and Boroughbridge.

Its services include Cataract and Oculoplastic pre-assessments and YAG laser treatment.
