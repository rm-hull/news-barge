# Woman charged with 2023 murder in Bradford to appear in court

## Detectives investigating the death of a man at his home in Bradford in 2023 have charged a woman with his murder.

Said Mohamed, 30, died after an incident at his home address in Sewell Road on June 4, 2023.

Naimo Ibrahim, 36, of Sewell Road has been charged with his murder.

Ibrahim - whose self-defined ethnicity is black African including black British - has been remanded into custody to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on Thursday (Nov 12).
