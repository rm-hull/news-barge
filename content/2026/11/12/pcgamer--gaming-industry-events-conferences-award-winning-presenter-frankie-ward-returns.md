The PC Gaming Show: Most Wanted sponsored by Pot Noodle is right around the corner, airing December 3. Of course, counting down the 20 Most Wanted games is all fun and, er, games, but what makes it truly special are the friends we make along the way. Returning to host the main show is the award-winning Frankie Ward, and our own presenter Midas and FGS presenter Jules Gill will be hosting the pre-show together—what is this, a crossover episode?

Last year, Frankie Ward was Commander Ward, piloting a ship through outer space, while BAFTA-nominee and Baldur's Gate 3 narrator Amelia Tyler was her trusty and not-at-all-evil shipboard AI—she was a disembodied floating head, kind of like Viki from I, Robot. This year, well, you'll have to wait and see who Frankie becomes, I'm not going to ruin the surprise now.

Frankie Ward is a long-time host on the show, and brings charm and passion to every game she talks about. This is her third show of the year, so you're likely familiar with her already, but for those of you who aren't: Frankie is an award-winning presenter with years of experience, as well as plenty of esports knowledge. We're in great hands.

As for the pre-show, that will be headed by Midas and Jules Gill, Future's very own presenters. PC Gamer fans will be used to seeing and hearing from Midas on our videos, but Jules is usually found over on the Future Games Show. They'll be interviewing guests as we get ready to count down the 20 Most Wanted games with you all.

Remember to tune in December 3 via PC Gamer's YouTube, Twitch, or Steam pages, or anywhere that hosts video, really.
