# Sneak peek at Leeds Christmas Market ahead of exciting 2026 festive season launch

## Lots and lots of people are very excited about the return of Leeds Christmas Market

Take a sneak peek at the 2026 edition of Leeds Christmas Market.

As we’ve previously reported, the popular Leeds city centre attraction will soon be back ‘bigger and brighter’ than before. The 2026 run of Leeds Christmas Market will be in City Square.

Leeds Christmas Market will be open between Thursday, November 19 and Wednesday, December 23. A major change has been confirmed for Leeds Christmas Market 2026 - it will have a new operator for 2026 in the form of the team Thor’s tipi.

We are bringing you everything you need to know about Leeds Christmas Market 2026. This includes times, dates and what to expect.

There will be over 40 stalls at the market as well as live entertainment and lots of festive fun. We have seen the stalls being constructed ahead of the Thursday, November 19 launch. You can see what is planned in City Square, and nearby Millennium Square attractions, by taking a scroll below.
