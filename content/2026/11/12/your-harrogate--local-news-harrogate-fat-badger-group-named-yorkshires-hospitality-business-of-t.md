Fat Badger Group has won Hospitality Business of the Year at the 2026 Yorkshire Post Business Awards.

Three’s a Crowd Harrogate was highly commended in the same category.

More than 400 people attended Wednesday night’s ceremony at New Dock Hall in Leeds, where broadcaster Christine Talbot presented the awards.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6af5d06e4ed47c8e113.png)

Fat Badger Group runs the White Hart Hotel and The Fat Badger on Cold Bath Road in Harrogate.

Its other venues in town include The Yorkshire Hotel on Prospect Place and The Pickled Sprout on John Street.

Outside of the Harrogate area, the group operates Guy Fawkes Inn (York), The Fat Badger (York), Clementines Boutique Hotel (York), Black Horse Inn (Kirkby Fleetham) and The Fat Badger and Royal Hotel (Whitby).

Simon Cotton, Managing Director of Fat Badger Group said:

> "I'm over the moon for our business to pick up such a prestigious award as this.
>
> "To be named Best Hospitality Business in Yorkshire is testament to the hard work of all our 500 strong team across our seven venues in North Yorkshire, every one of them who are such amazing hospitality professionals; I'm so proud for all of them in winning this award.
>
> "It also recognises the significant investment we have made into the businesses over the last few years which has been delivering fantastic results in growing the business against the backdrop of difficult times for our industry."
