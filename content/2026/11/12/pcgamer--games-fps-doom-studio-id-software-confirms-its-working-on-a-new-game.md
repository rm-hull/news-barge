Doom developer id Software has confirmed that it's working on a new game, although in phrasing that might strike you as familiar, it'll be "some time" before it's ready to reveal anything.

The new project at id was first announced by Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, who's been making a few posts on X today to mark the looming 25th anniversary of the original Xbox console. "Few studios have shaped the first-person shooter genre as much as id Software," Sharma wrote. "Slaughter Mode comes to Doom: The Dark Ages - Revelations on Monday, and the team is already working on its next game."

"Already" might be stretching it a bit: The studio's previous game, Doom: The Dark Ages, came out well over a year ago, although the Revelations expansion only dropped in July. Regardless, the studio replied to Sharma's post to confirm that yes, a new game is coming.

"We're incredibly excited about our next project, a new game from our teams in Texas and Frankfurt, with game direction once again by Hugo Martin," the studio said. "It'll be some time before we're ready to share more but we can't wait for you to see what we're working on."

If that rings a bell, you may be thinking of The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel, which was given an official subtitle earlier this month—eight years after it was first announced. Despite that passage of time, Bethesda warned that more waiting is in store, using exactly the same phrase as id: "It'll be some time before we're ready to share more."

Of course id Software and Bethesda Softworks are both subsidiaries of ZeniMax Media, which is owned by Xbox, so maybe that shared turn of phrase isn't entirely surprising. It does feel a bit unfortunate, though.

On the other hand, it's nice to have confirmation that id is in fact doing something new. The studio was hit hard by the Xbox layoffs in July, reportedly losing more than 130 employees, raising concerns about its ability to continue functioning as a standalone developer. Studio co-director Marty Stratton said in October that "there was a very deliberate effort [during the layoffs] to make sure that we were incredibly viable coming out of that across everything we do." Stratton also said id is now "working more closely with MachineGames than ever," leading to speculation that the two studios may be collaborating on Wolfenstein 3.
