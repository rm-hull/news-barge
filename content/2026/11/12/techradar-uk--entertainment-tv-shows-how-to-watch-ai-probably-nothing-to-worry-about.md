Whether you're all in on AI or staunchly opposed to it, the new Channel 4 documentary on the topic will be sure to get your attention. Directed by Nick Holt, this two-hour long doc charts the origins and immense rise of artificial intelligence over the last few years, with a focus on the work of Geoffrey Hinton, dubbed the godfather of AI.

His AI model started to learn autonomously, sparking the technological revolution we are now living in, for better or worse. The doc looks at the human impact, as well as the way corporations are buying into the technology, particularly people like Demis Hassabis, Sam Altman, and Elon Musk.

Hinton himself is deeply troubled and concerned by what he created, and it is that irony that frames the narrative of the documentary.
