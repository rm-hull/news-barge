# ‘Dangerous’ thief jailed after targeting homeless woman ‘for what little she owned’

## A ‘dangerous’ thief has been jailed after targeting a vulnerable homeless woman in Scarborough to steal ‘what little items she owned’.

Abubakarr Moiba ,44, targeted the woman who was fleeing domestic abuse, as she sat in a Scarborough town centre doorway in the early hours of June 17.

He approached the woman, made serious threats and tried to steal her mobile phone.

Moiba was caught less than two weeks later on June 28, when he allegedly robbed her for a second time by stealing her AirPods.

The homeless victim has said Moiba made her feel "like a sitting duck with nowhere to go for safety”.

![Abubakarr Moiba ,44, will be deported to Sierra Leone after his sentence.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/12/15/26/download-(11).png?trim=230,0,230,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


In a victim impact statement, she said she moved to the town seeking a fresh start after fleeing abuse and violence in the area where she had previously lived.

She described how the ordeal has left her too frightened to leave her home alone and said she is now a shadow of her former self, constantly looking over her shoulder and fearing repercussions for reporting what had happened to her.

She told the court that Moiba had ruined her life and left her fearful of living in Scarborough. She added: “All I want to do is try to forget what has happened and move on with my life.”

A search of Moiba's home address uncovered a bicycle and black baseball cap matching the ones seen on CCTV. The victim's white AirPods and a black-handled knife were also found.

![Moiba approaching the victim on 17 June before making serious threats towards her](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/12/15/14/download-(12).png?trim=38,0,39,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


While being transported to Scarborough Police Station, Moiba told officers: "I didn't rob anything from her. She owed me money. She gave me the headphones."

At that stage, police hadn’t said what item had been stolen or who from.

Moiba, originally from Sierra Leone but currently of no fixed address, was charged, convicted and sentenced to three years and nine months' imprisonment. He must also pay the victim a surcharge of £228.

He had admitted attempted robbery and was sentenced at York Crown Court on November 10.

He will be deported back to his home country after serving his sentence.

Following sentencing, Detective Constable Alfie Thomlinson, from Scarborough and Ryedale CID who led the investigation, said: “Moiba subjected his victim to a terrifying ordeal attempting to violently prey upon her vulnerabilities as a homeless young woman sleeping on the streets and steal what little items she owned.

"I would like to personally thank the victim of this case for her bravery in standing up to Moiba, who is a violent and dangerous individual by having the courage to give police evidence of this terrifying ordeal that has secured his conviction.”
