The Inn South Stainley, near Harrogate, has been named a Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice 2026 recipient, an award given to establishments ranked among the top 10% worldwide based on guest reviews.

The country inn, situated on Ripon Road at South Stainley, has also been shortlisted for several hospitality awards this year.

It is a finalist in the Best Pub category at the 2026 Yorkshire Post Tourism Awards, with the winners due to be announced in Leeds on 30 November.

The business was also a finalist for Best Yorkshire Pub at the Deliciously Yorkshire Taste Awards and shortlisted for Hospitality Business of the Year at the Harrogate Business Awards.

![](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Dog-in-the-bar-.webp)

> **Graham Usher, Managing Director of MATTGRAY Hospitality, which has owned the inn since 2021, said:**
>
> The Travellers’ Choice means a lot to us because it comes straight from our guests. Every review is someone who’s sat by the fire with their dog, stayed over after Sunday lunch or celebrated something special with us.
>
> To be shortlisted alongside some of Yorkshire’s best pubs as well is a real credit to our General Manager Jo, our Head Chef Shane and the whole team, who put their hearts into this place every day.

The inn has 12 bedrooms, a restaurant, bar and Garden Room. It currently holds a 4.5 rating on Tripadvisor from more than 650 reviews.

The business also works with a number of Yorkshire food and drink producers, including R&J Yorkshire’s Finest, Cryer & Stott and Yorvale ice cream.

## Christmas dining

The inn’s festive dining programme runs from 27 November to 23 December, with four-course menus available at lunchtime and in the evening.

The Garden Room is also available for Christmas parties, with capacity for up to 80 people. The inn has 12 bedrooms for guests wishing to stay overnight.

Further information about The Inn South Stainley is available at theinnsouthstainley.com.
