Nvidia's CEO, Jensen Huang, has resigned as President, CEO, and member of the board, effective immediately. That is the message which has just been posted on the Nvidia newsroom.

He has been at the centre of a big ol' scandal since last week, which threatened his position at the head of one of the biggest companies in the world. It started with a report over his relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset, which moved into him emailing his entire workforce to say sorry that he's had an affair (though didn't name the person), an invite to talk to national security and foreign affairs officials in US Congress next week, and now reports of potentially a second affair, this time with an Nvidia employee, have come to light.

This is what sparked the emergency board meeting last night which has culminated in his resignation from all sectors of the company.

Nvidia CFO, Colette Kress has taken over as interim CEO, while the company searches for a permanent replacement for Jensen, with Harvey C. Jones appointed the first ever chair of Nvidia's board of directors.

"Colette Kress, current Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Nvidia, has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer," reads the statement on the Nvidia newsroom. "Kress will continue to serve as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer while serving as Interim Chief Executive Officer until a permanent successor has been named. The Board is initiating a search for a permanent Chief Executive Officer and will consider internal and external candidates."

“Nvidia’s strength has always come from its people, and together with our leadership team, I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years,” said Colette Kress, Interim CEO and CFO, Nvidia. “We will continue advancing the work that has made NVIDIA a leader in accelerated computing and AI, and execute on the opportunities ahead. Our focus remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders.”

There has so far been no statement from Jensen himself on the matter, but the Nvidia statement thanks him for his service...

"Jensen’s contributions to NVIDIA are profound. NVIDIA is one of the most consequential technology companies in the world. The Board and the company thank Jensen for his immeasurable contributions to the company and the industry."

## Intel is a cautionary tale for Nvidia's board

It's not going to be easy to find a replacement for Jensen Huang, and Intel is a prime example of how jumping between CEOs can lead to stagnation.

Here's Andy on Intel's revolving door of CEOs and how hard it will be to find someone right for Nvidia's top job.

"Whoever leads Nvidia next will step into truly gigantic shoes, and in the face of an AI market that looks increasingly wobbly, they'll need to be the right person for the job. And swapping leaders out on the regular? Well, it's taken its toll on Intel over the years, that's for sure."

## Who is Colette Kress, Nvidia interim CEO?

## Just to note, Jensen's departure from Nvidia doesn't mean good things for PC gaming

Our Andy has written a considered take on what Nvidia and Jensen parting ways, and especially a new CEO coming in, will mean for PC gamers. And the short of it is: nothing good. Donald Trump has spoken up about his buddy Jensen's resignation, stating that there is only one person for the job: former Apple CEO, Tim Cook. ## Who can replace Jensen?

As interim CEO we have, as many predicted (including our Jacob Fox) the current Nvidia chief financial officer, Colette Kress. As a short term measure it makes sense for the board to go straight for the money person who's been talking with investors at earnings calls for 13 years now. That's the way to help calm a volatile market, and one that is likely to be spooked losing one of the main proponents for AI.

But what about the long term appointment? Nvidia's board has reportedly employed an outside agency to aid in identifying candidates for the CEO position, but who would want that job? Here is a brief timeline of events which kicked off last week with a story by the New York times into Jensen's relationship with Alice Huang.

* **October 29** - New York Times report comes out covering the relationship with an alleged foreign intelligence asset.
* **November 5** - Jensen is called to a meeting with various foreign affairs and national security committee leaders in US Congress, set for November 18.
* **November 8** - Jensen sends an email to all Nvidia staff, also published on the company newsroom, admitting to and apologising for an affair. The board also posts a statement on the newsroom saying it was launching an internal investigation.
  + Nvidia share price drops - around 21% since October 28
* **November 9** - Donald Trump speaks to press about Jensen, backing the CEO. "He's an amazing guy. I just hope there's no problem. If there is a problem, we'll let you know about it."
* **November 10** - Law firms chase investors to sign up for class action lawsuits or 'investigations' into the company. We've seen at least five press releases of lawyers offering their services to investors. Nvidia itself hires law firm Wachtell Lipton to lead the internal investigation.
  + The Information reports Alice Huang facilitated some 240,000 AI GPUs, costing around $7.5B, to be accessed by Chinese companies.
  + Axios reports that Howard Lutnick was sent a classified warning memo over the relationship between Jensen and Alice Huang. Noting that Marco Rubio also warned Trump just before they picked up Jensen en route to the China trip
* **November 11** - Reports surface that Jensen had a second affair, with an internal Nvidia employee, Kimberly Powell. An emergency board meeting is convened to determine Jen-Hsun's fate as CEO.
  + Nvidia's share price plumets even further, to lowest value since June 2025.
* **November 12** - Jensen Huang resigns from all positions at Nvidia with CFO Colette Kress taking over the role of interim CEO while the company looks for a successor.
