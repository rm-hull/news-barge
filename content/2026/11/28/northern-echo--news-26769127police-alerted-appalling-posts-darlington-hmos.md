“Appalling” social media posts about HMOs in Darlington could put residents’ lives at risk, the council leader has warned.

Cllr Stephen Harker said some posts were identifying individual properties and making assumptions about the people living inside them.

He said posts brought to his attention had been reported to police, who were taking the issue “very, very seriously”.

The warning came after Green councillor Richard Lawley raised concerns about online posts highlighting houses in multiple occupation (HMOs) around the town at a meeting of Darlington Borough Council on Thursday (November 26).

HMOs are properties where people from more than one household rent rooms and share facilities such as kitchens or bathrooms.

Cllr Lawley said there were “many reasons why people may be unable to afford a whole house or flat of their own” and warned against language which could fuel hatred towards vulnerable people.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21771599.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: LDRS)*

Responding, Cllr Harker said: “With regards to some of the posts, I’ve seen some absolutely appalling posts and it’s worse than just vilification.

“It is potentially putting lives at risk. It is encouraging some people who are filled with hate to go and do something outrageous, and there is absolutely no excuse for doing it.”

Cllr Harker said some posts were identifying properties while making assumptions about their occupants.

He added: “Some of the posts I have seen are listing properties, that people are mistakenly making assumptions about who is living in those properties.”
