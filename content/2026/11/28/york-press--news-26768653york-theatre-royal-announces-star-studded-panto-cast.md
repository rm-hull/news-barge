## York panto cast revealed for Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs

York Theatre Royal has revealed the full lineup for its 2026 Christmas pantomime, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs *(Image: SR Taylor Photography)*

A star-studded panto cast has been announced for this year’s festive season.

York Theatre Royal has revealed the full lineup for its 2026 Christmas pantomime, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, which will run from December 4 to January 3, 2027.

The cast includes children’s TV favourite Richard David-Caine (CBBC’s Horrible Histories), returning dame Robin Simpson, Tommy Carmichael as Muddles, and Christian Mortimer as the Prince.

West End star Jenny Gayner will play the Wicked Queen, with Dean Whatton as Sarge and Marcavia McCarthy in the title role.

Juliet Forster, creative director at York Theatre Royal, said: "We are thrilled to welcome this brilliant group of actors to the York Theatre Royal panto family.

"Audiences are in for a real treat with our talented cast of new and familiar faces excited to entertain audiences throughout the festive period.

"We can’t wait to bring this magical story to life."

Written by Paul Hendy, directed by Ms Forster, and co-produced with Evolution Productions, the show promises lavish costumes, stunning sets, hilarious jokes and dazzling special effects.

Family tickets are available for all performances, with savings of up to £61 when booking four tickets.

More information and booking details can be found at yorktheatreroyal.co.uk.
