A North Yorkshire quarry business has been named and shamed by HMRC after racking up more than £225,000 in unpaid tax.

Low Grange Quarry Limited, based in Melsonby, near Richmond, has appeared on the latest government list of deliberate tax defaulters, published on Thursday (November 26).

HMRC publishes details of people and businesses given financial penalties for deliberately failing to comply with their tax obligations or making deliberate errors in their tax returns.

The business, which specialises in the sale of stone, concrete and quarrying, is registered at Low Grange Quarry, Melsonby, Richmond, DL10 5PN.

According to the figures supplied from HMRC's latest list, the company defaulted on £225,392.10 in tax between November 1, 2017, and October 31, 2023.

It has been handed a financial penalty of £112,696.05.

HMRC publishes details of deliberate tax defaulters following investigations where the relevant conditions are met.
