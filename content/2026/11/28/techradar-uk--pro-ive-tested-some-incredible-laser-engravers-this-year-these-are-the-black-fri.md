## Why I recommend these laser engravers

The laser engraving market has transformed over the last year, with safer, more powerful enclosed models making up most new releases. There’s also been a shift in design towards a more versatile, modular approach, as you’ll see from many of the lasers featured in this Black Friday round-up.

The Creality Falcon T1 gained a full five stars because I was impressed by its modular design. At the time, I wrote: “The concept of the Falcon T1 is impressive: a single machine that replaces five, saving you space and money.” This was something of a new approach.

However, hot on the heels of that release came the next-generation xTool X1, and I commented: “For general cutting, detailed engraving and small-batch production, the X1 is one of the most refined laser engravers I’ve used.”

These two machines are at the upper end of the market. However, for a first serious laser engraver, the Creality Falcon A1 Pro was impressive, especially for mid-sized detailed work. As I wrote in my review: “I was able to get some of the finest, most detailed engravings I’ve seen at this level.”

Interestingly, Bambu Lab has also entered the market this year, with the Bambu Lab R1 ready to shake things up with next-level automation. As I noted in the review: “For small businesses, workshops, model makers and serious crafters, this is a fast, accurate and unusually easy to use CO₂ machine.” This machine also sets the tone for 2027.
