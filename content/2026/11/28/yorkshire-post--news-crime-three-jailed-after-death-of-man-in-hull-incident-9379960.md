# Three jailed after death of man in Hull incident

## Three people have been jailed following an incident in Hull city centre which led to the death of 53-year-old Martin Chadburn.

Lukasz Matusiak, 27, of Needlers Way in Hull, Britney Naylor, 20, of Dibsdane in Hull, and Shaytiya Farrow, 21, of Peter Street in Blackpool, were each found guilty of manslaughter following a seven-week trial at Hull Crown Court, which concluded on Monday, October 19, 2026.

On Friday November 27, all three appeared at Hull Crown Court for sentencing.

Matusiak was sentenced to five years in prison and Naylor and Farrow each received a two-year sentence.

During the sentencing hearing, the court heard submissions from the defence teams about the individual circumstances of each defendant and their involvement in the incident which took place on Sunday April 19 2026.

Britney Naylor’s barrister told the court that she went to aid her sister and had become involved after alleged racial slurs had been made towards the women, although these were not attributed to Martin Chadburn.

Lukasz Matusiak’s barrister told the court that he had intervened because he believed a woman was being assaulted.

At around 3.40am, officers were called to reports that a 53-year-old man had been seriously assaulted following an altercation between a group of people outside Princes Quay, near Carr Lane in Hull city centre.

The man was taken to hospital with a life-threatening head injury where, despite the efforts of medical professionals, he died as a result of his injuries on Tuesday, April 21 2026.

He was later named by his family as Martin Chadburn from Hull.

Immediately after receiving the initial report of the assault, detectives from the Major Crime Team launched an investigation which included speaking to witnesses and identifying CCTV in the area from that day.

CCTV obtained from the area at the time confirmed an altercation had taken place showing a group of people involved in assaulting Martin.

Officers visited a bar on Carr Lane where the group had visited that night and from CCTV, two men were identified, confirmed as Kristjan Suits and Lukasz Matusiak.

As part of their enquiries, officers also identified a taxi used by three women who had left the city centre shortly after the incident.

The taxi driver confirmed the women, who matched descriptions provided to police, had been dropped off at an address in Hull.

During the early hours of the next morning, a phone call was received to police by Bethany Naylor and Shaytiya Farrow who placed themselves at the scene.

The investigation led to five people being arrested and charged in connection with Martin’s death.

Upon conclusion of the trial, Bethany Naylor, 20, of Deerhurst Grove in Hull and Kristjan Suits, 35, of Queens Road in Hull, were found not guilty and therefore acquitted of any crime in connection with the incident.

Senior Investigating Officer, Detective Chief Inspector Leanne Murphy from the Major Crime Team, said: “Today’s sentencing brings this court case to a conclusion, but we know it will never undo the loss Martin’s family have suffered or bring him back.

“Throughout this investigation, we have worked meticulously to establish exactly what happened in the early hours of April 19 which led to Martin losing his life. I know that revisiting those events and hearing the details during the trial will have been incredibly difficult for his family, and I want to recognise the strength they have shown throughout.

“I would like to thank everyone who came forward to assist our investigation, including witnesses and local businesses who provided vital information. I am also grateful to the officers and staff for their commitment to this case, and to our partners in the Crown Prosecution Service for their work in bringing it before the courts.

“Our thoughts remain with Martin’s family and everyone who knew and loved him.”
