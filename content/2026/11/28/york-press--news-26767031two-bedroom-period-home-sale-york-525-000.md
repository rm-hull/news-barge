## ‘Simply stunning’ – have a look inside this period home for sale in York

Inside the two-bedroom house for sale in Bishophill Senior, York, for £525,000 *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

A “simply stunning” period home has hit the market in York.

The two-bedroom house is for sale in Bishophill Senior for £525,000.

Churchills, which is marketing the property, said it is a “superb” home which has an “impressive, glazed extension creating a wonderful connection between the living space and secluded rear courtyard garden”.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21769629.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the two-bedroom house for sale in Bishophill Senior, York, for £525,000 *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

Inside, the home has an entrance hallway leading to a lounge with a log burner.

There is a separate sitting room opening into a kitchen diner.

Upstairs, there is a galleried landing, two double bedrooms and a modern bathroom.
