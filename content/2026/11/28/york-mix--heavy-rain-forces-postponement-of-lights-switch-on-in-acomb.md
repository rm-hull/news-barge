Acomb’s Christmas Lights Switch On event has had to be cancelled today.

It was to take place this afternoon on Front Street.

A spokesperson for Acomb Alive said: “It is with huge disappointment that we have to announce that the light switch in this afternoon is being postponed.

“This decision hasn’t been made lightly but the weather really isn’t in our favour

“Watch this space for a rescheduled event!”
