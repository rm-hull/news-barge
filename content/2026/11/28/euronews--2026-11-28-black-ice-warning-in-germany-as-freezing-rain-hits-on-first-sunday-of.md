## Drivers and pedestrians across Germany are being warned of dangerously icy conditions on Sunday, as early sunshine gives way to snow and freezing rain, bringing a risk of black ice.

Germany is facing hazardous weather conditions from Sunday, with freezing rain threatening to leave roads and pavements coated in black ice. According to the German weather service wetter.com, significant travel disruption is possible overnight and during Monday morning’s rush hour.

Temperatures are expected to fall below freezing across much of Germany overnight into Sunday, potentially dropping to -10°C in the Alps. Rain will move in from the west on Sunday, creating a risk of black ice as it comes into contact with frozen surfaces.

### **Which regions are at risk of black ice?**

Localised black ice could form in the far west and north-west of Germany as early as Sunday morning. By the afternoon, the rain is expected to reach higher ground in western parts of the country.

Overnight into Monday and throughout the morning, the risk of black ice is expected to shift further east, affecting a swathe of Germany stretching from the Baltic Sea through Saxony-Anhalt and Thuringia to Bavaria. Road conditions in Bavaria could be particularly treacherous.

Motorists are advised to allow extra time for their morning commute, while pedestrians and cyclists should be prepared for hazardous icy conditions. Conditions are expected to improve gradually towards midday on Monday.

### **Renewed risk of black ice on Thursday**

The weather is expected to remain largely overcast as the week progresses. Another band of rain will move in from Wednesday, potentially turning to snow in parts of southern Germany.

There is a renewed risk of black ice on Thursday morning, although it is still too early to say which regions will be worst affected.
