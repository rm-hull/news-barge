A hospice fundraiser offering people the chance to write a will has raised nearly £10,000.

Saint Catherine’s Make a Will Week generated £9,525 to support patient care at the hospice, in people’s homes and throughout the community.

The annual campaign, supported by solicitor firms across areas including Scarborough, Whitby, Filey, Malton, Pickering, Bridlington and Driffield, invites people to write or update their will in exchange for a donation to the charity.

Susan Stephenson, fundraising operations coordinator, said: "We are very grateful to all the firms who took part and for those clients who came forward to write or renew their wills and donated towards patient care.

"This money will go towards ensuring that patients and their loved ones receive the best possible care, free of charge, at an incredibly difficult time in their lives.

"A huge thank you to our wonderful supporters at Thorpe & Co, Colin Brown & Kidson, Crombie Wilkinson, Ware & Kay and Pinkney Grunwells.

"We simply could not achieve this without you."

Details of Saint Catherine’s Make a Will Week 2027 will be announced soon.
