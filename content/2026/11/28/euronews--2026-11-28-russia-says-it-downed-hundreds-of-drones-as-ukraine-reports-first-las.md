## Ukraine says a privately developed laser shot down a Russian jet-powered drone for the first time. Meanwhile, Russian strikes continued overnight into Saturday, damaging transport infrastructure in Dnipro and injuring five people across the Dnipropetrovsk region, regional officials say.

Russian air defences shot down hundreds of Ukrainian drones heading towards the Moscow region overnight, Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said on Saturday.

The Moscow mayor said more than 460 drones headed towards the region between late Friday and early Saturday.

His statement came after Ukraine’s state-backed defence group Brave1 said on Friday that Ukraine had used a laser weapon to shoot down a Russian jet-powered drone "for the first time".

Ukrainian presidential adviser Serhiy Beskrestnov published footage earlier this week showing a target crashing in a fireball. He said the Blysk laser system, developed by Carmine Sky, had been protecting a critical infrastructure facility when it shot down a Russian Geran jet drone.

Ukraine and Russia have intensified their drone and missile attacks in recent months as the war continues into its fifth year and the United States seeks talks to end the fighting.

Previously, Ukrainian air defence teams have used machine guns and shoulder-fired missiles to bring down Russian drones.

Brave1 said a private air defence team operated the laser and cautioned that “a first successful interception does not mean the technology is ready for mass deployment”.

Russian strikes continued overnight into Saturday, with more than 20 drone attacks across four districts of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Oleksandr Hanzha, head of the oblast's military administration, said on Telegram.

The attacks damaged transport infrastructure in the city of Dnipro and injured five people across the region, including a 15-year-old boy.

Earlier this week, Russian forces struck residential buildings and an educational institution in Kharkiv, in the country's northeast, killing at least three people in a wider ballistic missile and drone assault that also caused casualties in Kyiv and other regions.
