# ‘Argumentative’ inmate left female prison guard with concussion after attack at HMP Wealstun

## A prisoner who became “argumentative” attacked a female guard at a Yorkshire jail - leaving him with concussion.

Lewis Turner sentenced at Leeds Crown Court after pleading guilty to assault causing actual bodily harm during an incident at HMP Wealstun in Wetherby.

The court heard how he became “argumentative” with the prison guard after she told him his release date and punched a wall.

Prosecutor Robert Galley told the court how the guard then attempted to restrain the 29-year-old and used her radio to call for assistance.

He told the court how Turner punched the woman to the left side of her face, leaving her with a black eye and heavy swelling.

The guard eventually managed to restrain the defendant who then spat at the window of his cell, Mr Galley added.

![Lewis Turner attacked the guard while serving time at HMP Wealstun in Wetherby.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjZjNGIxMjEyLTI1YWQtNGVmNy04MGMxLThjMWYwZTYzZmZjYTpkOGViMzUzNy1jZGZiLTQ0MDMtYWNkZi01MDEyZmU1MDI4YWU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Turner, of Bevre Road, Doncaster, has 14 convictions from 26 previous offences and was serving a sentence for breaching a restraining order at the time.

In a victim impact statement, the court heard how the guard was left with a concussion following the incident, and was “anxious and stressed” about returning to work.

Offering mitigation, defence counsel Lily Wildman said her client suffered from learning difficulties and mental health issues, as well as an alcohol problem.

She told the court how he had already spent a considerable amount of time on remand, but was now being offered stable accommodation at his uncle’s house in Doncaster.

Accepting that treatment would be better than imprisonment, Judge Mushtaq Khokhar handed Turner a community order under three conditions.

He must continue to live with his uncle, undergo a nine-month alcohol treatment requirement and a 12-month mental health treatment requirement.

“I hope you won’t let me down,” Judge Khokhar added. “I’m placing a lot of faith in you and your uncle.”
