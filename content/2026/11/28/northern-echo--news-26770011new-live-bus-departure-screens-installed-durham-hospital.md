Patients and visitors to a County Durham hospital can now check live bus times on new digital departure screens.

The displays at University Hospital of North Durham are at the main entrance and emergency department, and show services leaving in enough time for passengers to reach the nearest stop.

The emergency department screen shows buses due in three minutes or more, while the main entrance display shows services departing in six minutes or more.

Staff can also use the screens to plan journeys to and from the hospital.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21773871.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bus departure display at the hospital's main entrance is one of the new screens. *(Image: Supplied)*

The project was delivered by County Durham and Darlington NHS Foundation Trust and Durham County Council, with funding from North East Mayor Kim McGuinness as part of a regional public transport programme.

The trust said the displays support its Green Plan by making public transport easier to use and encouraging more sustainable journeys.

It said access to live information could help people who rely on buses to attend appointments, get to work or visit loved ones.

Sam Goss, the trust's sustainability lead, said: "These new digital screens are a fantastic addition to University Hospital of North Durham, providing patients, visitors and staff with easy access to real-time bus information.

"For many people, reliable public transport is an important part of accessing healthcare, visiting family members or travelling to work.

"By providing live departure information, we can make journey planning easier and improve the overall experience of coming to and from our hospital."

North East Mayor Kim McGuinness said: "Good public transport is about making everyday life easier.

"Staff going home after a busy shift, people visiting loved ones or patients who are just leaving hospital shouldn’t have to stand waiting for a bus not knowing when it will turn up.
