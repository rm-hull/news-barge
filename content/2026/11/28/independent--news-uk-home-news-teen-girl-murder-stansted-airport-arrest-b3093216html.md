A 48-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of murdering 16-year-old girl who was found dead in east London.

The suspect was detained at Stansted Airport on Friday evening and remains in police custody, according to Scotland Yard.

Officers had been called to the property in Grosvenor Gardens, Newham, at around 3.40pm following reports of concern for the welfare of a teenage girl.

Despite the best efforts of emergency services she was pronounced dead at the scene, the Metropolitan Police said.

Detectives believe the suspect was known to the victim and have said there is no evidence to suggest a wider risk to the public.

![The Met police said there is no evidence of a wider risk to the public](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/19/18/32a297e5d3ee13f60b07c0e5fabdb79aY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzc5Mjk3OTE4-2.18763915.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The girl's family have been informed and are being supported by specialist officers.

Detective Chief Superintendent Jo Lloyd, who leads policing in north-east London, said: “Our thoughts are with this young girl’s family and friends as they face an unimaginable loss.

“The death of a child is a devastating tragedy and specialist officers are supporting her loved ones at this extremely difficult time.

“I understand the shock and concern this will cause locally. I want to reassure residents that specialist detectives are working tirelessly to establish the full circumstances and secure answers for the young girl’s family.

“At this stage, we have no evidence to suggest there is any wider risk to the public. We believe this to be an isolated incident and that the suspect was known to the victim.”

“Neighbourhood officers will remain in the area over the coming days to provide reassurance and support. If you have concerns or information, please speak to them.

“They are there to support both the investigation and the community at what is an incredibly difficult time.”

Anyone who may have information, CCTV, dashcam or mobile phone footage from the area who has not yet spoken to police is asked to contact them on 101, quoting CAD 4208/27NOV.

A post-mortem examination will take place in due course.
