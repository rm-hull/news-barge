A Darlington café known for loyal customers and unique breakfasts and brunches has been shortlisted for The Northern Echo's Best Coffee Shop 2026.

Maggie's Coffee & Kitchen, on Duke Street, is one of 10 finalists in this year's competition, adding to its growing list of awards.

Run by husband-and-wife team Jeanette and Alex MacMurray, the café was named the best café in County Durham at England's Business Awards in March 2025. It was also shortlisted in The Northern Echo's Best Coffee Shop competition in 2024.

The couple hope their quality food, friendly service and community spirit will win them another award.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21766301.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jeanette and Alexander MacMurray of Maggie's Coffee and Kitchen in Duke Street, Darlington *(Image: Scott Akoz)*

Jeanette said: "The food is top-notch, not a brunch-type menu. We have an experienced chef at the helm, our customer service is brilliant, and we're always there for our customers.

"We've been here for eight years now; we've got to know all of our customers.

"We have lots of regulars, some come in just for a coffee, and some come in every day for food.

"It's more than just a coffee shop; it's a place that people can come and talk to us about their lives.

"More than just about the food and drink, it's a hub for the community."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21766300.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Outdoor seating at Maggie's *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The café has been a fixture on Duke Street since Jeanette took over in 2018. Alex joined her two years later, bringing his professional-chef experience to the menu.

The couple have spent decades in catering. They met at Headlam Hall Hotel in 1995, where Jeanette worked in management, and Alex was head chef.

Alex started his own catering business in 2003, with Jeanette joining him in 2007. They later turned their attention to Maggie's.

The café serves freshly prepared breakfasts, lunches and homemade cakes, including signature dishes that set it apart.

The B.E.C. bacon, eggs and cheese is one of Maggie's specialities and a customer favourite.

Jeanette added: "On the menu, we have a unique item, the B.E.C, bacon eggs and cheese in a dish. We've served it for four or five years now, and it's unique to us, and customers love it.

"We also do sunshine eggs, which are popular, and a sweet potato hash.

"We always start our dishes vegetarian, and then people can make additions and add in bacon or other meats. So it gives people the option to have it on its own, vegetarian, or to add meat into it.

"We're famous for the B.E.C though."

![Jeanette and Alexander MacMurray of Maggie's Coffee and Kitchen in Duke Street, Darlington](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21766297.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jeanette and Alexander MacMurray of Maggie's Coffee and Kitchen in Duke Street, Darlington *(Image: Scott Akoz)*

The food built Maggie's reputation, but Jeanette says the people behind the café matter just as much.

She praised the team's dedication and the welcoming atmosphere they have created over the years.

"We're proud of how our staff work together so well. It's just a group of like-minded people who want to do well and enjoy what we do," she said.
