A North East Kibblesworth road will close to traffic while ducting and poles are installed.

Gateshead Council's traffic order covers the entire length of Riding Lane from its junction with Kibblesworth Bank in Kibblesworth.

The order comes into force at 8am on November 30, with the works expected to be completed by 6pm on December 19.

During the works, vehicles will be prohibited from using the road until the work is complete, or at the times and to the extent indicated by traffic signs.

Vehicles associated with the works and emergency service vehicles on duty are exempt from the restriction.

A signed diversion will be in place during the works, and access for residents will be maintained.

The council made the temporary traffic regulation order on November 25.
