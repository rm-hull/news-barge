A North Yorkshire village pub which has been closed for six years is set to reopen with a soft launch on Sunday.

The Whitestonecliffe Inn, in Sutton-under-Whitestonecliffe, near Thirsk, has been bought and renovated ahead of its reopening.

The 17th-century pub has been closed since 2020, with plans for its refurbishment submitted last year.

The proposals included internal and external alterations, replacement windows and doors, a new front porch and double doors at the rear.

Planning permission was granted for alterations to the pub and five additional self-catering holiday units behind it.

The pub was bought in June 2025 after being marketed for £395,000 plus VAT.
