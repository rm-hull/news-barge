TG Jones is closing another North East store as part of a nationwide restructuring plan.

The former WHSmith branch at Middlesbrough’s Cleveland Centre will close on February 27, 2027, following an expected closing-down sale.

It is one of around 150 planned closures aimed at securing the retailer’s future.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21775989.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TG Jones and the associated Post Office in the Cleveland Centre will close in February. *(Image: Google)*

A TG Jones spokesperson said: "As we work to secure the long-term future of TGJones, we have confirmed that our Middlesbrough shop will close.

"We know this will be disappointing news for our colleagues, customers and the local community. Our focus now is on supporting those affected and continuing to serve customers throughout the closure period.

"We would like to thank all of our colleagues for their commitment and the contribution they have made to TGJones."

The announcement follows confirmation at the start of November that the Teesside Park branch will also close, while Redcar's store shut in September and the Darlington branch closes today (November 28).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21530553.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The TG Jones branch in Darlington closes today (November 28). *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The restructuring plan, approved by the High Court in July, includes store closures, debt write-offs and rent cuts.

The company has cited falling sales, rising costs and changing shopping habits as key pressures, and also acknowledged that moving away from the WHSmith brand has impacted performance.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670417.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TG Jones announced the closure of the Teesside Park branch earlier this month. *(Image: CONTRIBUTOR)*

The closure will leave another large Cleveland Centre unit empty after several high-profile departures in recent years.

JD Sports recently moved to the Hill Street Centre, followed by Boots after its Cleveland Centre shop closed.

The former Topshop and Topman units have stood empty since 2021.

The closure has also put the town centre’s Post Office under threat, with Middlesbrough Council now in talks to retain the service.

Middlesbrough Mayor Chris Cooke said: "The Council is in active discussions with the Post Office to maintain a presence in the town centre.

"High streets up and down the country are changing and Middlesbrough is not alone in seeing a decline in traditional retail."

Cooke said the council is launching an Investment Prospectus to “reimagine central Middlesbrough” and build on recent progress.

He added: "That looks to transform the town centre by getting more people living close by and spending their leisure time here, which is why developments like Captain Cook Square and STACK have been such an encouraging success."
