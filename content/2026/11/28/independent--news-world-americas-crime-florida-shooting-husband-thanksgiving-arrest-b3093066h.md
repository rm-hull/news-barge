A Florida woman is accused of killing her husband on Thanksgiving and then waiting in the driveway for the cops to arrive.

Shannon Driggers, 50, of Lakeland, Florida, has been arrested in connection to the killing. She was charged with first-degree murder, according to Fox 13.

Polk County Sheriff’s deputies responded to a home around 8:30 p.m. Thursday, according to the report. They were called after Driggers told 911 dispatchers she shot her husband and was waiting for law enforcement to arrive.

First responders found her waiting outside the home and her husband inside with multiple gunshot wounds.

![Shannon Driggers is accused of shooting her husband dead at a Florida home on Thanksgiving](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/27/22/51/Shannon-Driggers.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Driggers admitted to police that she killed her husband, according to the report.

Police have not released a motive for the alleged murder.

Investigators say the shooting was premeditated and intentional after examining the victim’s wounds, the location of the shell casings and other evidence.

"The suspect called 9-1-1, telling us that she shot her husband. After arriving and investigating, it is clear that she did just that. She murdered her husband on Thanksgiving, intentionally and with premeditation," Polk County Sheriff Grady Judd said.
