A "beloved wife, much loved mum and dearly loved grandma" has died aged 76.

Susan Thompson (née Blackburn) passed away at York Hospital on November 9, aged 76 years.

A death notice published in The Press stated that Susan was the wife of David, mum to Joanne, Julie and James, and grandma to Ashleigh, Thomas, Poppy, Ted and Elliot.

A funeral service will be held at York Crematorium on Monday, December 7 at 11.40am.

Family flowers only have been requested and anyone wishing to make a donation in Susan’s memory is invited to support Macmillan Cancer Support.
