A 21-year-old woman has died following a police chase in Staffordshire.

The woman was travelling in the front passenger seat of a Seat Ibiza, which had been followed by officers before it crashed into another car.

Sadly she was pronounced dead at the scene following the collision on Rugeley Road, in Hednesford, just after 11pm on Friday.

A 23-year-old man from the Walsall area has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving following the tragedy.

Staffordshire Police has referred itself to the police watchdog, the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC), due to the police activity before the incident.

The force said the other driver involved in the crash, who was driving a Vauxhall Astra, is assisting them with their inquiries.

![The accident happened on Rugeley Road in Hednesford, Staffordshire](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/28/14/00/Screenshot-2026-11-28-at-14-30-58.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Two other people were taken to hospital by West Midlands Ambulance Service. Their injuries are not believed to be life threatening or life changing.

The deceased woman's next-of-kin have been informed and are being supported by trained police officers.

Officers are appealing for witnesses to come forward with information about the fatal crash and the road has reopened to traffic after being cordoned off.

A Staffordshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re keen to speak to anyone who was in the area at the time, or those who saw the vehicles in the lead up to the collision.”

Anyone with information should call 101 or visit the force website, quoting incident 893, or email [ciu@staffordshire.police.uk](mailto:ciu@staffordshire.police.uk)
