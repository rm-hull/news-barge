## Police crack down on anti-social behaviour in North Yorkshire town

Police have issued a dispersal authority in part of an East Coast town *(Image: Pic supplied)*

North Yorkshire Police have taken action following anti-social behaviour in Scarborough.

Following reports of youths causing anti-social behaviour around Eastfield shops, they have issued a dispersal authority.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21775335.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A map showing the affected area. *(Image: Pic supplied)*




A police statement issued last night said: "This means that any person found to be acting in, or likely to act in an anti-social manner, will be directed away from the area.

"Failing to abide by this direction to leave, can result in the person being arrested.

"Parents - if you have any young people in the area, please get in touch with them to ensure they are not part of the behaviour and encourage them away from the area."
