The emergency services have been dealing with an incident between York and Leeds stations.

It’s reported that one person was hit by a train near Garforth station earlier this afternoon.

All lines have now been reopened but as a result, trains will be cancelled, delayed by up to 45 minutes or routes revised.

This covers York to Leeds services via Church Fenton and Selby to Leeds too.

This currently affects **Northern, CrossCountry and TransPennine Express** passengers

The route to Leeds from York **via Harrogate** is not affected.
