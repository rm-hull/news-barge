Saturday’s Wales vs Japan live stream promises to be a fascinating contest as both nations will be determined to end the 2026 Nations Championship with a positive result and secure ninth place as the Finals weekend continues at Twickenham.

Wales may have finished fifth in the Northern Hemisphere table but that position does not reflect the positivity of their November performances. Following the nervy win over Japan at the start of the month, Steve Tandy’s side delivered a historic first victory over New Zealand in 73 years and pushed Australia to the limit in a narrow defeat. Now facing Japan for the fifth time in 16 months, they’ll expect to deliver a convincing win to crown a much-improved campaign. Flanker James Botham, so impressive against the All Blacks, returns to the pack with Jac Morgan and Dewi Lake on the bench. In Lake's absence, Dafydd Jenkins will captain, while Ospreys scrum-half Reuben Morgan-Williams gets a first start.

After starting the championships with a win over Italy, it's been all downhill for Japan as they lost their next five games. The Brave Blossoms were competitive in the 28-21 defeat by Wales, but then conceded 24 tries in thumping defeats at the hands of England and Scotland. Basic errors cost Eddie Jones’ side dearly in those games, with poor handling and missed tackles among the many issues. They’ll need to address these problems if they hope to run Wales close and end 2026 with a victory, especially with a World Cup to follow next year.
