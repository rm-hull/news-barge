Police are investigating a burglary at a famous York hotel and bar.

It happened at The Judge’s Lodging on Lendal between 4.30pm and 6.30pm on Tuesday 10 November.

A man entered the staff room and stole some of the personal belongings of the people who work at the hotel.

#### The Judge’s Lodging, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured on CCTV, as we believe that he may have information that will assist our investigation.

“If you witnessed any suspicious behaviour during this timeframe, or have any other information that can help the investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
