# Container of batteries catches fire as crews rush to tackle blaze

## Firefighters have been called out to a waste fire in Leeds involving a container of lithium-ion batteries.

Crews were called out to Knowsthorpe Lane, Cross Green, just after 4pm yesterday (Friday November 27).

Firefighters from Hunslet and Killingbeck fire stations were sent to the scene.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue service said a “large container” with lithium-ion batteries was alight.

Lithium-ion batteries are rechargeable batteries that power devices such as phones, laptops, cordless power tools and electric vehicles.

A spokesperson for the fire service said: “Firefighters from Hunslet and Killingbeck responded to a waste fire.

“One large container with Lithum ion batteries involved in fire. Firefighters used two breathing apparatus, one large jet and a ground monitor to extinguish the fire.

“Incident left with on-site manager.”
