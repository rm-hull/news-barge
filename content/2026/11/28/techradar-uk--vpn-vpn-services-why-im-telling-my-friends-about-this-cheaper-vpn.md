**PrivadoVPN: The cheapest VPN dealJust: $1.11 per month**  
For only $1.11 a month PrivadoVPN offers a VPN service that includes ad blocking, 10 simultaneous connections, a no-logging guarantee, apps for all major devices including tvOS, iOS and Android and all the core functionality you might need including a security kill switch and global streaming service unblocking.

Unlike more expensive products like NordVPN, ExpressVPN or Norton with their multiple subscription tiers, PrivadoVPN has a single price plan. So, there's no confusion once you click through to buy.

If all you’re looking for is a simple, straightforward VPN service with plenty of space for all your family’s devices and no download limits, you really can’t go wrong with PrivadoVPN this Black Friday. It's too cheap not to give it a try.
