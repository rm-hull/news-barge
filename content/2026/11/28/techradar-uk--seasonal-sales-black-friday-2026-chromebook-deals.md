Black Friday has always been a great time to buy a new Chromebook, and this year's sales event has been no different. Even though the day itself is over, I've found some astounding Chromebook deals still live on both side of the Atlantic, such as the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 Chromebook that's just $189, down from $419, and the Lenovo Chromebook 14M8911, which was £349.99 and is now just £159.99 at Amazon.

Chromebooks are much cheaper than MacBooks and Windows 11 laptops, and thanks to the lightweight Chrome OS operating system that they run on, they often outperform similarly-priced budget laptops, making them a great choice for students, kids, or anyone who just wants a cheap but reliable laptop to browse the internet on.

While Chromebooks are usually budget laptops, you can get Chromebook Plus devices which have more powerful hardware for even smoother performance. I've included these here as well, such as the Asus Chromebook Plus CX1505, which was £399.99 and is now £259.99 at Amazon.

Google also recently launched Googlebook laptops, which are essentially high-end Chromebooks, though rather than running Chrome OS, they have a new operating system called Googlebook OS — a combination of Chrome OS and Android. Due to how recently Googlebooks by Asus, Acer, Dell and others have launched, I don't expect any major discounts after Black Friday, but if I do find any, I'll be sure to add them to this page.
