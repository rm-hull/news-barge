A wedding venue has been crowned regional winner in a prestigious industry awards scheme.

Sandburn Hall, near Flaxton, was named Yorkshire & North East Regional Winner in the Wedding Venue (Hotel) category at The Wedding Industry Awards 2027.

The venue now progresses to the UK national final, which will be held in Liverpool on January 20.

Sarah Balding, sales and marketing director at Sandburn Hall, said: "We are absolutely thrilled to have been named the Yorkshire & North East Regional Winner in the Wedding Venue (Hotel) category at The Wedding Industry Awards 2027!"

"Weddings have been at the heart of Sandburn Hall for over 20 years, and it’s a real privilege to be part of such an important day in our couples’ lives. Every wedding is unique, and our wonderful team puts so much care, passion and attention into making each celebration truly special.

"What makes this award even more meaningful is that it’s based on feedback from our couples. Knowing that they’ve taken the time to vote for us and share their experiences means the world. We’re incredibly proud of our team, and this recognition is a testament to their hard work and dedication.

"We’d like to say a huge thank you to every couple who voted for us, and we’re now looking forward to representing Yorkshire at the National Finals in Liverpool in January!"

The award is based on average voter scores, client feedback from the past 12 months, and the opinions of a judging panel made up of more than 160 industry experts.

This year’s competition saw 25,344 votes cast for 1,434 entrants, with 1,157 wedding businesses reaching the regional finals.

Damian Bailey, founder of The Wedding Industry Awards, said: "TWIA is the most rigorous, respected, and coveted awards in the UK wedding industry so the Regional Winners really are at the top of their game.

"The competition is tough, the standards very high indeed, but thanks to 25,344 client votes and our expert judging panel, the Regional Winners have risen to the top in an industry that absolutely demands the best."

With the UK wedding industry valued at around £15 billion a year, the awards are seen as a mark of excellence for suppliers.

Regional winners are being announced at events throughout November, with all going on to compete at the national final in Liverpool.

A full list of regional winners and highly commended finalists is available on The Wedding Industry Awards website.

Sandburn Hall is now preparing to fly the flag for Yorkshire and the North East on the national stage.
