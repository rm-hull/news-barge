Shockingly for a series that just released its ‘sixth’ entry, there are *seven* Grand Theft Auto games that never made it to PC. Seven! And what’s even more incredible is that all seven of them are stone-cold classics, apart from about six of them.

Essentially if it came to a handheld/is the biggest game release of the decade, then Rockstar hasn’t brought it to PC. I’ve ranked all the games we so unjustly missed out on. The good, the bad, and the Toni-Cipriani’s-face-levels-of-ugly. Who’s Toni, you ask, and is he really so hideous? Read on and find out!

## 7. Grand Theft Auto – Game Boy Color (1999)

![A handful of pixels on a tiny, empty street.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/s6eYSGRg5zjgRpxBAVhP6G-1199-80.jpg)

Well, top marks for trying. Unfortunately the city is more lifeless than the set of 28 Years Later. If you somehow manage to find anyone to commit a crime against and then walk anywhere with a wanted rating, you’re instantly arrested with seemingly no way of fighting back or escaping. Driving is borderline tolerable, until you inevitably get stuck on any of the scenery. Still, some of the music goes entertainingly hard. The song the cab drivers are listening to is so intense that Travis Bickle would be recommending a therapist. Anyway, this is awful.

**Why no PC port?** This disaster has been so rightly lost to time that I didn’t even know it existed when I pitched this list feature.

## 6. Grand Theft Auto 2 – Game Boy Color (2000)

![A man who has been killed.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/PHn9DPTXRyRGx8z5jf4Dha-833-80.png)

Last year’s game except now with a populated city. Horrible driving controls are far more forgivable now that I finally have pedestrians to run over. The chiptune music is genuinely great and this time only plays when you’re inside cars (immersion!). I honestly got some borderline-entertaining police chases out of this.

**Why no PC port?** The grimdark PC gaming scene of the new millennium tragically couldn’t allow a soundtrack full of upbeat bangers like this.

## 5. Grand Theft Auto Advance – Game Boy Advance (2004)

![High price for parking around here...](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/saiRzpBLUFsv2wg6pk5iDG-1200-80.jpg)

While the music isn’t as brilliant as GTA 2 for GBC (the new metric I’m judging all videogame soundtracks by) this is an improvement in basically every way. Nicer car handling with a tight handbrake that makes up for the fact that you’re so zoomed in you’ll have to use it every three seconds not to crash into anything. We even get a story this time, laughably crap though it is.

Still, there’s a nagging sense that if Rockstar had truly committed to the GBA they might have made something genuinely excellent. But considering what the competition was attempting on this console at the time it could have been a whole lot worse.

**Why no PC port?** There’s far more pimps than sex workers on the streets, and us PC gamers don’t care for such lopsided bubble economies.

## 4. Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories – PSP, PS2, iOS (2006)

![Who the hell is Vic Vance?](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/KB8fu7x5oFVKX3QKrYv8bN-1200-80.png)

You know you’re in for a rough ride when there’s pop-in in the *opening cutscene.* Lead character Vic ‘boo hoo hoo my brother’s sick’ Vance is also a dud for the ages. When he’s not whining about his sickly sibling (yawn) he’s moaning about how much he hates crime, before unquestioningly smashing up storefronts or slaughtering hundreds for whichever psychopath asks.

You’re helping out wife-beaters and animal-on-animal voyeurs that even Trevor Philips would find a bit much. Get over that and you’ll find a beautiful take on Vice City with an impeccable eighties soundtrack. Still just about worth playing then, though maybe make sure there’s no witnesses or proof that you did.

**Why no PC port?** Rockstar couldn’t find a PR willing to explain why an NPC gleefully watching a video of animals banging is actually a very clever satirical comment on the American dream.

## 3. Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories – PSP, PS2, iOS (2006)

![The ugly protagonist of Liberty City Stories](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/yYfjAxmiMFuHfXjQKiN5uV-1200-80.jpg)

What if Pinocchio had wished to be a real boy, but the blue fairy had turned out to be a talentless hack? You’d end up with Toni Cipriani’s undead nightmare of a face. The only reason he hasn’t turned the gun on his own hideous mug is that the shooting controls in his game are equally as horrid.

But! This first PSP go is still a terrific attempt to squeeze a full fat 3D GTA into a handheld. It’s got sharp writing and characters, along with some of the funniest radio stations in the whole series. Poor shooting’s a shame, but carjacking and pedestrian-pulping is as fun as ever. And if you’re emotionally devastated about the lack of sex workers in GTA6 - yes you are you lying pervert - you can frequently enjoy running over their customers in this. Lovely.

**Why no PC port?** No amount of DLSS 5 could yassify Toni’s face into something acceptable to the human eye.

## 1. Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars – Nintendo DS, PSP, iOS (2009)

![A man with more wrinkles than face claiming she still don&#039;t want his pepperoni.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/U23qHjXoidaKSaxiowvuik-1200-80.png)

Always dismissed top-down old school GTA? I’m confident this DS masterclass can make a convert out of you. Rockstar expertly uses the touchscreen to give old staples a shot in the arm. Unscrewing panels and twisting wires together to hotwire a car under pressure is basically exactly what it feels like to steal a car IRL (before you disagree in the comments, keep in mind I’m *also* wearing a wire).

One mission has you stealing a street racer’s car to sabotage it so it blows up at the start of the race, and you even get to slap a rotten fish on the air conditioner just to really add insult to injury.

![Drug dealing in Chinatown Wars](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Fsb83BcEDSGdbWgqqfsWP3-1200-80.png)

There’s an entire drug-dealing economy in it, which I’m sure Nintendo were delighted by. Blowing up security cameras - with molotov cocktails you make by sloppily pouring gasoline into bottles with the touchscreen, natch - is fun in itself. That it also subtly lowers drug prices because the police are now able to see less crimes is a level of detail I just didn’t expect on DS. More fool me.

Am I going to claim it’s got the best police chases in the entire series? Yes, and with almost a straight face too. You’re encouraged to ram cop cars and force them into collisions, taking them out Burnout-style with an immensely satisfying big red X crossing through their shattered remains. A million times more fun than the usual tedious car hide-and-seek. Plus nothing livens up trying to escape the cops in a stolen ambulance like having to simultaneously press the patient’s heart repeatedly on the touchscreen to defibrillate him back to life. The best of the top-down GTA’s by far, and puts a fair few of the fully 3D ones to shame too.

**Why no PC port?** When I go to Heaven for putting this game at number one, that’s the first thing I’ll be asking God.
