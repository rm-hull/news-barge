**With less than a month to go before Christmas the festivities are ramping up across Yorkshire.**

From twinkling lights and torchlit processions to Snoopy trails and even a crustacean inspired Christmas tree, here's a few of the free events taking place to help keep costs down and excitement levels up.

![Around eight small boats docked in a harbour, decorated with Christmas lights. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/e0b6/live/80846b20-ea6e-11f1-a828-d384c0290f6b.png)

'Ships Ahoy' is one of the light installations at Scarborough Lights

## North Yorkshire

Harrogate is hosting an **Ice Sculpture Trail** on 12 December and two free-to-play**Curling Lanes** every weekend until Christmas, along with street entertainment and the North Pole Post Office.**York Minster's Christmas Tree Festival** features 100 unique trees decorated by local schools, businesses and charities. Entry is free for York residents.

The minster is also running number of **festive services,** tickets are free but must be booked online, including Sankta Lucia on 14 December, which features a traditional Swedish procession and carols.

On the coast, **Scarborough Lights** has returned with illuminations around the town, including dinosaurs on roundabouts and festive ships in the harbour until 20 December.

There is an optional ticketed event at the South Cliff Fire Garden, but the lights on Westborough, Eastborough, The Market Hall, Crescent Gardens are free to see.

**Merry Fishmas:** The Filey Fishtive Tree is made from lobster and crab pots and has been a tradition on Coble Landing since 2017.

The tree lights will be officially switched on during a free celebration on 5 December with a live DJ, food and fireworks at 18:00 GMT.

Cannon Hall will be decked out for a traditional Victorian Christmas

## South Yorkshire

Step into a traditional **Victorian Christmas at Cannon Hall Museum** in Barnsley, where you can wander through the hall and gardens to see the classic decorations, free with a small charge for parking.**Snooping for Snoopy:** You can follow a colourful sculpture trail through Doncaster to find 12 sculptures of Snoopy the dog reimagined by different artists.

There's plenty of time to find them all, as the trail will be open until the 25 January.

There will be free **Christmas crafts and activities** for children every weekend in Sheffield at Cambridge Street Collective and The Cutlery Works, including gingerbread decorating, pom pom snowman making, and a mini disco with the Grinch.**Acrobats, jugglers and aerialists** will be performing live at Meadowhall at 12:00 and 14:15 every Saturday and Sunday, and during Christmas week on the lower level outside Next and Primark.

## West Yorkshire

In Brontë country, you can join **torchlit procession** marching through the streets of Haworth on 5 and 6 December.

Meeting by the Christmas tree at the bottom of Main Street at 16:30, you can join in with carol singing, before torch bearers and marshals lead the march up the hill.

Or, on 19 and 20 December, a live parade will see the **Nativity story brought to life**, with Joseph walking through the town to find room at the inn.** A Living Advent Calendar:**Saltaire's living advent calendar will make a return, with 50 windows opening up the run up to Christmas, with one new window being 'opened' each evening until 24 December.** Darley Street Market**, in Bradford, will host carol singing, choir concerts, brass bands, wreath-making, cheese and wine tasting, festive crafting sessions and visits from Father Christmas.

Same Difference Arts will also present a **free family pantomime** version of Goldilocks and there will be a**living nativity** barn on 21 December with real animals.

If you would rather stay inside in the warm, Leeds City Museum will have an **Elf Trail** for the whole of December, where children can search the museum to find the hiding spots of each elf and claim a sticker.

And Tingley Garden Centre has a **miniature Christmas wonderland,** which it says is the biggest Lemax display in the North.
