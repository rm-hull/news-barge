## Why I recommend them

As one of the machines awarded a Highly Recommended award, the GMKtec EVO-X3 showed what local AI can mean. In the review, I commented: “The EVO-X3 is impressive and shows the full potential of local AI.”

At the same time, this wasn’t the first machine with this potential. After a year of testing an increasing number of these AI-enhanced machines, I was starting to get a flavour of exactly what was possible.

The EVO-X3, along with the newer EVO-X5 Pro, MSI PRO MAX EDGE AI+ and Minisforum MS-S1 Max, are all at the upper end of the AI mini PC market, and these machines really highlight the potential of running local AI models.

Over the last few months, that potential has helped me sort through thousands of images, cataloguing and organising them in an easy-to-archive format. Over the next year, I have further administrative tasks that would have taken me months to complete, and while they aren't quick, I can leave these machines to do the grunt work.

At their present prices, these machines are out of reach for most of us, even with the Black Friday deals. However, there are cheaper options.

The current cost of memory doesn’t help the pricing, but the GEEKOM A9 Max (2026) and VZMORE AX9 Max AI can both handle impressive processing out of the box and give you a taste of the potential.

2027 will inevitably bring another leap in performance for running these local models, but for now, these machines offer some of the best options for performance and potential Black Friday savings.
