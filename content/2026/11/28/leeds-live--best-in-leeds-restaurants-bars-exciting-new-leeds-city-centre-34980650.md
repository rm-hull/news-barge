# Exciting new Leeds city centre coffee shop launches

## The team issued a message to Leeds

Leeds city centre has welcomed an exciting new opening.

We have seen a new coffee shop launch in Lands Lane. The Coffee House is now up-and-running.

The team offered free beverages on opening day. A launch message was shared on social media.

The team stated: “Our Leeds Lands Lane store is now open. You'll find us in the Queens Arcade. Pop in and warm up with a good brew.”
