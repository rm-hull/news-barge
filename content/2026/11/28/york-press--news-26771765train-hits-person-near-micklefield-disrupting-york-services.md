## Person hit by train between York and Leeds - rail services disrupted

Police were called to the scene *(Image: Stock image)*

Some train services to York were affected by an 'incident' near Micklefield in West Yorkshire earlier today.

Emergency services were called to the site, with the 'incident' reported to have been caused by a train hitting a person early this afternoon.

All lines have now been reopened but as a result, some services were cancelled, delayed or rerouted.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776118.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the tweets *(Image: Pic supplied)*

This covered York to Leeds services via Church Fenton and Selby to Leeds.

The Leeds to Harrogate line was unaffected.

The operators affected were Northern, CrossCountry and Transpennine Express.

Services are now said to have returned to normal.
