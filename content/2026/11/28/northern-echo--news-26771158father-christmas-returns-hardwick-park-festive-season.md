Father Christmas is returning to a popular County Durham park this December, giving children the chance to meet Santa and enjoy a range of festive activities.

Hardwick Park, near Sedgefield, will welcome Father Christmas for two weekends in December, with families invited to visit his specially decorated festive grotto.

The event will take place on Saturday, December 5, Sunday, December 6, Saturday, December 12, and Sunday, December 13.

However, tickets for the second weekend have already sold out, with spaces still available for December 5 and 6.

Tickets cost £20 per child, while accompanying adults can attend for free.

### What is involved?

As part of the experience, children will receive a hot chocolate or warm blackcurrant drink while waiting to meet Father Christmas.

They will also have the chance to take part in a festive craft activity and make their own environmentally friendly reindeer food to take home.

Each child will then enjoy a personal visit with Father Christmas in his decorated grotto, where they will receive a gift.

A festive park trail is also included, which families can complete on the day or take away to enjoy later in December.

Durham County Council has encouraged families to book while tickets remain available.

In a Facebook post, the council said: “There is still time left to come and meet Santa at Hardwick Park!

“Tickets are still available for Saturday, December 5 and Sunday, December 6.

“Have a hot chocolate in our yurt, complete a festive craft activity and make your own reindeer food to take home.

“Tickets cost £20 per child with accompanying adults coming free.”

Visits will run between 10am and 4pm, with bookable sessions starting every 30 minutes between 10am and 3.30pm, excluding 12.30pm and 1pm.

Up to six families can attend each session, with visitors asked to report to the Education Room at the park's visitor centre when they arrive.

Families will then be taken to the grotto on a first-come, first-served basis.

The grotto is located at the far end of the car park, just before the dog exercise area.

Visitors do not need to bring a physical ticket but must provide the name used when making their booking.

Tickets can be booked through the Durham County Council events website.
