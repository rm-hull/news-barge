*(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Bishop Auckland has been hailed as the "cultural capital of the North East" by a popular travel influencer after she explored the town's historic palaces, galleries and museums.

Chloe McGuirk, known online as New Girl in Toon, described her day out in the town as "amazing", and added she had "no idea" Bishop Auckland "was hiding so much."

The viral creator's social media career began after she moved from the South to the North East, documenting her exploration of her new home region.

She now has more than 100,000 followers and shares North East guides, covering food, walks, shops, staycations, events and cultural attractions.

In a video shared with her followers, Chloe highlighted the standout attractions from Bishop Auckland, and added: "This has to be the cultural capital of the North East."

After picking up a Discovery Day Pass and climbing Auckland Tower for the "fantastic" views of the County Durham town, the viral travel influencer visited the Spanish Gallery.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21187666.jpg?type=mds-article-620)New Girl in Toon said the Spanish Gallery was her "biggest surprise of the day". *(Image: The Auckland Project)*

The Spanish Gallery is the UK’s first museum dedicated entirely to Spanish art, history, and culture. It spans four floors, with works dating back to the 16th century.

New Girl in Toon called the Spanish Gallery her "biggest surprise of the day", praising its historic paintings, sculptures and religious artworks.

The gallery is inspired by, and home to, works by some of Spain's most celebrated artists, including Francisco de Zurbarán, Diego Velázquest and Bartolomé Esteban Murillo.

Chloe added that the Spanish Gallery has "incredible art" and is "beautifully presented."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18725862.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Auckland Palace is one of the best-preserved Bishop's palaces in Europe.

Auckland Palace was next on the viral creator's itinerary.

Once the principal residence of the Prince Bishops of Durham, Auckland Palace is one of the best-preserved Bishop's palaces in Europe.

Inside the nearly 1,000-year-old palace, Chloe highlighted the "stunning" pink Throne Room, former bishops’ apartments and Long Dining Room.

She also highlighted Francisco de Zurbarán’s paintings, which have been displayed at Auckland Palace for centuries, calling the palace "incredible".

Describing The Faith Museum, the travel vlogger said it "wasn't at all what I expected!"

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21302196.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Faith Museum's 500 Years of Tyndales New Testament Exhibition. *(Image: THE AUCKLAND PROJECT)*

The museum features more than 250 objects from across England, Scotland and Wales, including historic artefacts, personal possessions, national treasures and contemporary artworks.

Its four gallery spaces span two floors, taking visitors from prehistoric Britain to recent history.

Chloe said: "I kind of thought it would be all about religion, but it actually explores 6,000 years of British history through faith and belief, and there's some amazing contemporary art."

Her final stop was the Mining Art Gallery, which celebrates the North East’s coal-mining heritage through more than 400 paintings, drawings and prints about miners and their communities.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/13918439.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One piece of art on display is Durham Big Meeting by Tom McGuinness, painted in 1968.

The Bishop Auckland attraction, which Chloe said was "so moving", features work by renowned regional artists, including Norman Cornish and Tom McGuinness.

She added that she had found a "new favourite artist" during her visit.

The travel vlogger's only regret, she said, was that she had not saved enough time to fully explore visiting Bishop Auckland, adding: "I already need to go back."

With its wealth of historic attractions, impressive art collections and fascinating heritage, it's easy to see why New Girl in Toon praised Bishop Auckland so heavily and dubbed it the "cultural capital of the North East."
