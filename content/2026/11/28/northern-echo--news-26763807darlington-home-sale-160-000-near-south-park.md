Whether you’re taking your first step onto the property ladder or are looking to downsize without giving up your outdoor space, this lovingly maintained three-bedroom semi-detached home in Darlington’s popular South Park area is well worth a closer look.

The house occupies a larger than average corner plot, giving it a lovely sense of space.

A useful porch opens into the entrance hall and from there into the living room – a bright and welcoming space with wooden flooring, a large bay window to the front and a fireplace providing a cosy focal point.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765474.jpg?type=mds-article-620)First-time buyers are sure to fall in love with this stylish Darlington home *(Image: Supplied)*

To the rear is the kitchen diner that stretches to more than 18ft right across the property.

At one end of this is a modern galley-style kitchen with eye-catching green fitted units plus integrated appliances, to the other is a dining area.

French doors from the kitchen open into a sunroom – the ideal spot for relaxing in while you enjoy a morning coffee.

A handy storage room and family bathroom complete the ground floor.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765472.jpg?type=mds-article-620)First-time buyers are sure to fall in love with this stylish Darlington home *(Image: Supplied)*

Upstairs are three bedrooms.

The main and second bedrooms are both generous doubles, while the third would make a lovely nursery, child’s room or home office.

There’s also the added convenience of an upstairs WC.

The property is warmed by gas central heating and has double glazing.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765471.jpg?type=mds-article-620)First-time buyers are sure to fall in love with this stylish Darlington home *(Image: Supplied)*

Outside, there are gardens to the front and side, as well as a rear courtyard that’s easy to look after, plus a driveway that offers off-road parking.

South Park itself is close by and the property is conveniently located for local amenities.

Add to this that it falls into the A band of council tax, and this is a manageable, affordable home with plenty to offer.
