The Christmas period can be quite stressful with all of the shopping and events happening so you might need to take a trip away from your daily life to fully relax.

If you are looking for somewhere in Northumberland to visit over the festive period, you might want to try Blanchland.

The tiny village has a cosy hotel with festive stays available and a quaint tea room found in an old Victorian school.

## Blanchland at Christmas

If you want to visit the quaint Blanchland, you can book a stay at the Lord Crewe Arms.

The hotel features crackling fires and a cosy space to forget about daily life.

The different rooms are found throughout the 12th-century former monastery and garden annexes.

When you get into your room, you will find some treats made by the on-site chef waiting for you.

You can even stay at the hotel over Christmas to get involved with Christmas Eve carols at the Abbey and festive treats in the Crypt Bar and a Christmas Day feast.

The three-day festive break allows you to “switch off and savour a truly traditional country Christmas”.

The hotel’s on-site restaurant has a December menu that brings together “comforting flavours, celebratory favourites and seasonal dishes.”

Reviewers on Tripadvisor have said the hotel’s food is “excellent” and say the rooms are “nice and cosy”.

During your trip, you will also want to stop off at the White Monk Tearoom.

The tea room is found in an old Victorian school that closed in the 1980s and was transformed into a tea room that is “full of character and charm”.

The tea room has a range of light lunches on offer including quiche, pies, and jacket potatoes.

There are also plenty of fresh sandwiches and toasted paninis to choose from.

To help warm you up in the wintery weather, you can have a speciality loose-leaf tea or hot coffee.

If you want to treat yourself, the café also offers an afternoon tea with your choice of sandwiches, any cake or treat, a fruit or cheese scone, and a pot of tea or coffee.

The White Monk Tearoom has a rating of 4.4 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with reviewers praising the “fantastic” menu and “delicious food”.
