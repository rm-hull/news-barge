North Yorkshire fire and rescue responded to cases of reported flooding earlier today.

The call-outs follow heavy rain across York and North Yorkshire this morning before it eased into light rain.

At 1.58pm, the Selby crew was called to Gowthorpe, Selby, to investigate flooding inside a commercial premises.

Crews gave advice to staff on scene but Fire Service action was not required.

Earlier, at 12.47pm, on Sutton Road, Sutton Under Whitestonecliffe near Thirsk, a fire officer was mobilised to investigate levels of rainwater on a busy road due to reports of blocked gullies.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said that after the officer inspection no Fire Service intervention was required.

All details were passed to Highways.

It is unlikely there will be similar call-outs tomorrow.

After a cold night with temperatures down to 3C, Sunday will be sunny with a maximum of nine.
