# Pursuit of net zero commits us to a miserable future: Yorkshire Post Letters

## William Loneskie, Justice Park, Oxton, Lauder, Berwickshire.

J. Shaw (The Yorkshire Post, November 19) thinks that those of us questioning the belief that we can control the climate of the Earth by decarbonising our economy, and do not support "climate scientists" who base their predictions on computer models, are in denial.

I could just as well accuse them of obsession. For the predictions of the well-funded "experts" have proved so wrong so often that most people are no longer scared by them. The key policy to reduce CO2 is net zero but that's now looking like a busted flush.

It was significant that the only leader of the G7 attending the 31st chaotic climate conference in Turkey this month was Prime Minster Burnham (along with 230 staff).

![Prime Minister Andy Burnham delivers his speech to the Cop31 UN Climate Change Conference in Antalya, Turkey. Picture date: Thursday November 12, 2026.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjM1YmZkOTY3LWY1NTYtNGQwNS04MDAxLTdjYjI2NTY4MDU4MTo1ZGE0N2E2Ni0wYzQzLTQ1YTgtOTdhNy0xYmRlNTI5MmEyZGQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Speaking for six minutes to a half empty hall he did so, because, in order to try to stop Labour votes drifting to the Greens, he has to rail against oil, gas, and coal. But we will need oil and gas long into the future.

We need oil and gas for lubricants and to make plastics. We need coal to make cement and quality steel. It makes sense to use our own resources rather than import it from far off often insecure locations. Already the new Jackdaw pipeline is pumping gas ashore just after a few weeks.

The mad net zero scheme made the law of the land by the hapless Theresa May costs billions, makes us less safe, will not influence climate one iota, and undermines British industry. As the Reform Party's new defence spokesman, Wing Commander Alastair Macintyre, said recently in a speech: "Net zero has collapsed Britain's industry and given us the highest electricity prices in Europe". Meanwhile millions are paid to to Drax power station to burn North American wood, not British coal.

Apart from Drax all the big 2GW 365/24 coal-fired power stations like Ferrybridge or Ratcliffe-on-Soar have been closed and replaced them with hundreds of weather-dependent wind farms, and their transmission lines, Chinese battery yards, and fields full of solar panels degrading the countryside. Net zero has given us the Electric Vehicle Mandate; putting reliable hard-working diesel vans, cars, 4x4s and pick-ups off the market in a few years and given carte blanche for Chinese manufacturers to saturate the market in the UK with battery powered cars, free from the tariffs imposed by the EU.

Net zero has virtually banned warming and cheering coal fires, and is coming for gas central heating and wood burners. The loonies of the Climate Change Committee Organisation (yes it is an organisation with its own well paid CEO) intend that we must eat less meat, drive and fly less if at all, and generally make our lives more costly and more miserable. The net zero cult has captured the SNP, the Welsh nationalists, the Liberals and Labour, but for how long for?

Two years ago at COP29, Professor RG Faulkner wrote words which have stood the test of time: "The jetting in of world leaders to the Cop29 climate conference is the greatest example of virtue-signalling hypocrisy of our age. These self-important people believe the world is “boiling”, and that other countries should be implored to cut emissions. Net zero is committing ourselves to a miserable future.”
