**A 48-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of murdering a 16-year-old girl who died in east London on Friday afternoon.**

Emergency services were called to a property in Grosvenor Gardens, East Ham, at about 15:40 BST following reports of concern for the welfare of a teenage girl, the Metropolitan Police said. She died at the scene.

The man was arrested at Stansted Airport on Friday evening and remains in police custody. The Met said it believed it to be an isolated incident and the suspect was known to the victim.

The girl's family have been informed and are being supported by specialist officers.

Det Ch Supt Jo Lloyd, who leads policing in north-east London, said: "Our thoughts are with this young girl's family and friends as they face an unimaginable loss."

She said in a statement: "Neighbourhood officers will remain in the area over the coming days to provide reassurance and support. If you have concerns or information, please speak to them."

Lloyd added officers were keen to hear from anyone who may have information, CCTV, dashcam or mobile phone footage from the area who has not yet spoken to police.
