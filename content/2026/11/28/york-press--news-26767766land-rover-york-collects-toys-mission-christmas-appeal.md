## York car retailer and hotel team up for Christmas toy appeal

Lloyd Land Rover in Ripon and York has teamed up with Rudding Park to support a Christmas appeal *(Image: Lloyd Motor Group)*

A luxury car retailer has teamed up with a hotel to support a Christmas appeal in York.

Lloyd Land Rover Ripon and York and Rudding Park are backing the Cash for Kids Mission Christmas campaign to help children living in poverty across Yorkshire.

The scheme aims to ensure every child wakes up to a gift on Christmas Day.

Mark Redding, head of business at Lloyd Land Rover Ripon, said: "Community is at the heart of everything we do at Lloyd Land Rover, so joining Mission Christmas felt like the natural thing to do.

"We’re proud to be part of an appeal that brings people together to make a real difference for children and young people in Yorkshire, and we’re grateful to Rudding Park for generously donating the prize."

The public is encouraged to donate new, unwrapped toys at Lloyd Land Rover dealerships in Ripon and York.

Those who donate and fill out an entry form will be entered into a prize draw to win a one-night spa break for two at Rudding Park.

Donations will be collected until Wednesday, December 16.

On Thursday, December 17, Lloyd Land Rover vehicles will deliver the toys to the Mission Christmas warehouse to ensure they reach children in time for Christmas Day.
