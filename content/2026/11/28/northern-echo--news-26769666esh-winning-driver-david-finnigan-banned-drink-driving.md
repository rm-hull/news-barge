A County Durham driver has been banned for 29 months after being caught over the alcohol limit.

David Finnigan, 59, of Cypress Park, Esh Winning, pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates' Court on November 23.

The court was told he drove a Ford Focus on Ridding Road in Esh Winning on August 30 with 120 micrograms of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath, above the prescribed limit.

Finnigan was fined £200 and ordered to pay £85 in prosecution costs and an £80 surcharge.

The court took his guilty plea into account and said the fine was imposed as an alternative to a low-level community order.

The register notes magistrates went outside the guidelines because Finnigan was of limited means, and that probation had been consulted.

His driving ban can be reduced by 29 weeks if he completes a course by August 1, 2028.
