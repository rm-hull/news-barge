## WATCH - York Christmas Market 'busy' despite cold and heavy rain

Chris Magee of the Mulled Wine store *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Cold temperatures and heavy rain did not deter shoppers at York Christmas Market today (Saturday).

And neither did the threat of traffic chaos and bus strikes.

Piccadilly and St Sampsons Square were extremely busy this afternoon, though admittedly not as packed as other weeks.

However, stallholders admitted the weather had hit trade, with one saying it had been halved.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776017.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The other side of Parliament Street is also busy *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

James Marston at the Farrahs toffee stall said there were still plenty of people and plenty of people buying despite the cold and the rain.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776031.jpg?type=mds-article-620)James Marston of Farrahs Toffee *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

He said: “It hasn’t put them off. It’s not quite as busy as normal because of the rain but people are still enjoying the festivities.”

Barrie Gittens of the nearby Bowbrook Studios agreed it was “still quite busy with a lot of people getting wet.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776036.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Barrie Gittens of Bowbrook Studios of Wigton, Scotland. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

He added: “So far it’s been a very good show (market), an excellent show.”

The mainly young staff at the nearby Cut & Craft drinks stall were busy serving customers a range of mulled wines and similar drinks.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776037.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The staff at Cut and Craft *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

One said: “It’s just a normal Saturday. We have been busy to be fair. People are still coming even in the rain. They are enjoying the mulled wines because it’s cold. We also have mulled cider and a rose.”

At Tom’s Winter Toddies and Tipples people were queuing for a warming drink.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776047.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Staff at Tom's Winter Toddies and Tipples *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Staff member Elsie Taylor said: “It’s not as busy as last week but it’s still good. People want something to keep them warm.”

Elsie added her best-seller was a cherry Christmas mulled wine made with cherry brandy.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776016.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plenty of people near M&S *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

At the main Mulled Wine outlet Chris Magee said today has been busy but was down.

He said: “It’s terrible for a Saturday because of the weather. Everybody has gone to the pub. Still, there are plenty of people here. It’s about half as busy as last week when the weather was cold and sunny.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776059.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jacob at the Tommy Banks Pie Shop *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Nearby, at the Tommy Banks pie shop the queues were not as long as at other nearby stalls but Tommy’s pies remain the taste of the town.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21776027.jpg?type=mds-article-620)More market crowds *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Staff member Jacob said the rain had made business quieter than last week.

“The pies are still selling but not as much as a normal Saturday.”

Now, you may recall two years ago, Tommy Banks had a tonne of his pies stolen from a van near Ripon.

The 2,500 pies worth £25,000 were eventually found but had to be written off.

Jacob assured: “Tommy’s keeping them safe this year, under lock and key. The steak and stout pie is the best seller. Overall, we are having a good season.”
