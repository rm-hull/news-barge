Overnight closures are planned on the M62 between Junction 22 and Junction 38 beginning next year.

National Highways intends to make a legal order covering the motorway in West Yorkshire, the East Riding of Yorkshire and North Yorkshire.

The proposed restrictions will allow maintenance and improvement works to be carried out safely.

They are expected to operate overnight from 8pm to 6am, during a period starting on January 2 and ending on December 23, 2027.

The order will cover both directions between Junction 22 and Junction 38.

It will also apply to all slip and link roads leading to and from the motorway sections, as well as all refuge areas.

During closures, traffic will be diverted through alternative junctions of the M62, M18, M1, M621, A63, A1(M) and local roads.

Signs will show the diversion routes throughout the area.

The length of each closure will vary according to the work being carried out.

The restrictions will apply only when shown by traffic signs and will not apply to emergency services, works, winter maintenance or traffic officer vehicles.

Drivers can contact National Highways on 0300 123 5000 or at [info@nationalhighways.co.uk](mailto:info@nationalhighways.co.uk) for enquiries about the restrictions.

The notice says the closures will be used as required for the works, rather than applying continuously throughout the stated period.

Enquiries about the order can be made using those contact details.
