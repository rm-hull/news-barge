Overnight closures are planned for a stretch of the M1 for almost a year.

National Highways has announced the closures for maintenance and improvement works between Junction 30 and Junction 48, affecting traffic in West Yorkshire, North Yorkshire, South Yorkshire, and Derbyshire.

The closures will occur overnight between 8pm and 6am from January 2, 2027, until December 23, 2027.

The closures will impact both directions of the M1, including the East Link Road Interchange Roundabout, Austhorpe Interchange Roundabout, all slip and link roads, and all refuge areas along this stretch.

Motorists will be required to follow diversions via other M1 junctions, as well as the M62, M621, A1(M), and local roads.

These diversions will be clearly marked with traffic signs.

The duration of each closure will vary based on the specific work being carried out.

Emergency services, works, winter maintenance, and traffic officer vehicles will be exempt from the route closures.

The closures will only be enforced when indicated by traffic signs.

The effect of the Order will be to impose prohibitions on the following lengths of carriageway: (i) the M1, in both directions, between Junction 30 and Junction 48 (including East Link Road Interchange Roundabout and Austhorpe Interchange Roundabout).
