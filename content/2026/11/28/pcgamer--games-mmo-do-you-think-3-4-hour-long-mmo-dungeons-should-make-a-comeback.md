This is Terminally Online: PC Gamer's very own MMO column. Every other week, I'll be sharing my thoughts on the genre, interviewing fellow MMO-heads like me, taking a deep-dive into mechanics we've all taken for granted, and, occasionally, bringing in guest writers to talk about their MMO of choice.

Dungeons in MMOs have gone through quite an evolution—in ye olden days of Everquest (and WoW Classic, more on that in a second) they were multi-hour sprawling crawls that were sometimes even designed to be done in multiple play sessions.

Nowadays, if you have a dungeon that takes more than 30 minutes, folks start getting the itch—and honestly, it's fair enough. Not everyone has the kind of free time for MMOs anymore, and the daunting prospect of pushing in half a 9-5 workday just to get a max-level dungeon run cleaned up is not appealing to anyone with other responsibilities in their lives.

However, as someone who recently did an all-bosses (plus the Jailbreak escort) Blackrock Depths run, which took *four goddamn hours of my actual life,* I'm not sure on which side of the fence I sit anymore. Ask me a couple of months ago, and I'd say that maybe an hour is pushing it.

But I cannot help the fact that I had an absolutely wonderful time with my fellow dungeon-divers—strangers, all, and yet I felt a certain camaraderie with them, even for the hunter who kept hanging way too far back in the Lycean and pulling extra groups.

I am not a tank, and yet I tanked—and over the course of those four hours turned from apologising profusely for forgetting my Demoralizing Shouts to a field marshal that ordered the whole group into the corner so we wouldn't wipe on the last room.

When we looped back for the Jailbreak quest, me and the three other people still left (our fifth understandably had to go) were yelling at Marshal Windsor, who kept turning and opening doors, ordering us to work through a list of his own petty grudges when we'd already been there for the length of a Lord of the Rings film. We went a little mad.

And there's something sad about the idea that this kind of experience—a sprawling, labyrinthine crawl done in one sitting because everyone involved is too stubborn to come back later—isn't really a thing in modern MMORPGs anymore.

Not only did it endear me to a bunch of group mates I would've otherwise never talked to, it also made me grow attached to BRD as a location. What would have simply been a non-memorable three-boss rinse with a narrow path in a more modern MMO was a real adventure.

What do you think? Am I just nostalgic? Are these types of dungeons deservedly banished to the pit in 2026? Or should MMOs waste our time again? Here's a poll to find out. Also a comments section below, if you'd like.
