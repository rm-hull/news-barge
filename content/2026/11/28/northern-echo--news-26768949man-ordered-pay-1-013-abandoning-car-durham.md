A man has been ordered to pay more than £1,000 for abandoning a car on land in County Durham.

Mario Krupa, 26, of Cadogan Square, Newton Aycliffe, was fined £440 at Peterlee Magistrates' Court on November 25, with the offence proved in his absence.

The court was told he abandoned a Volkswagen Scirocco on land in the open air in County Durham without lawful authority on July 21.
