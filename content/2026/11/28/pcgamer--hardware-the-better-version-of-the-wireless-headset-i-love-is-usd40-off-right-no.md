This is the first generation of this headset, hence the better price. A lightweight, super comfortable, wireless, and reliable headset. Great audio for both gaming and media, and impressive built-in noise cancelling. My only gripe is the non-detachable microphone.

**Sony Inzone H9 price check:** Best Buy $138

The Inzone H9 headset isn't a flashy or particularly popular headset, but it really should be in conversation among the best wireless options. This is a great pair of headphones on discount for Black Friday—a $40 cut at $138.

I had never heard Inzone when I picked up a pair of H6's (the lesser model to the H9) at Best Buy a year ago. It's a newish brand within Sony for gaming peripherals marketed toward both console and PC. I was just desperate for a headset that wouldn't fall apart and the price was right, so I gave them a shot. I was quickly impressed.

The audio quality was seriously good for the price, and they were also the most comfortable headset I've ever owned for two reasons: They're very light for a wireless headset and they don't squeeze my large head.

The H9 set above is better than the H6 in basically every way—better battery life (32 hours), noise cancelling, support for both 2.4 Ghz and Bluetooth connections—and during Black Friday it's only $20 more. So as much as I love my budget-friendly pair, there's little reason not to upgrade.

My only real nitpick about these headsets is the non-detachable mic: I don't use it, so it'd be nice to just ditch it. But if you do use it, know that it's pretty good but prioritizes noise isolation for gaming comms. It does have a convenient feature where flipping it up automatically mutes.
