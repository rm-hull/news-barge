Police are continuing to investigate the night of mayhem which saw a mass brawl break out on a York bridge.

As we reported on Thursday, 15 people have just been sentenced for their part in what officers called a ‘shocking outbreak of violence’ in and around the Slug and Lettuce on Ouse Bridge.

It started on Saturday 26 July 2025 at around 7.45pm.

Dozens of officers were called to the scene, and multiple arrests made on the night and the following weeks.

Enquiries are still continuing.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “From further enquiries throughout the investigation, another image of a man we would be keen to speak to has come to light.

“Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured on CCTV, as we believe that he may have information that will assist our investigation.”
