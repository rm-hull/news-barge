## OpenAI accuses Apple of stealing trade secrets through a former employee, months after Apple has launched a similar lawsuit against the ChatGPT provider.

After a lawsuit from Apple in the summer, OpenAI strikes back with a countersuit, accusing Apple of committing trade secret theft.

The ChatGPT provider alleged that Apple was attempting to steal trade secrets through a former employee while also pressuring shared suppliers to impede OpenAI's entry into the connected devices market.

OpenAI filed the countersuit on Friday in a federal court in San Jose, California.

Apple denounced the filing as "a transparent attempt to distract from the substance" of its own lawsuit it had launched in July against OpenAI and two former Apple employees.

OpenAI's lawyers respond that Apple is seeking "to compete unfairly by any means available."

Both companies have been partnering since ChatGPT was integrated into Apple products in 2024. They are now set to become rivals as OpenAI is developing its first connected device designed for AI with Jony Ive, Apple's renowned former design chief.

## Apple pressures suppliers

OpenAI's filing argues that Apple is pressuring suppliers to identify their projects with OpenAI and the employees assigned to them.

"Suppliers have expressed fear of losing Apple business and have moved or rescheduled meetings with OpenAI to avoid Apple seeing them," the filing says.

No suppliers were named, but the filing says one firm moved OpenAI's production to a night shift, while another dropped planned work with the tech company "because of threats from Apple."

A former Apple employee who worked at OpenAI before returning to the iPhone maker was accused of leaking "dozens of OpenAI documents" about a prototype to senior Apple staff in July "at Apple's request".

OpenAI lawyers challenged an Apple practice encouraging employees to mix work and personal files via their personal iCloud account. A practice, the lawyers say, allowing Apple to later accuse those who leave of theft.

"This case is about OpenAI employees deliberately stealing, accessing, and using Apple's secret and confidential information regarding our unreleased technologies, processes, and products — not about inadvertent retention of Apple information," an Apple spokesperson said.

OpenAI has pledged it wouldn't use information Apple claims to be trade secrets in response to the judge threatening to issue an order barring OpenAI from using it until trial.
