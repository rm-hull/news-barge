If you’re after a VR headset you won’t find better value for money than Meta’s Quest 3S, and that’s doubly true during Black Friday where this tech always sees a healthy discount. Though if you’ve been thinking about finally getting into VR, I expect you’re already well aware of the great immersive software the platforms have to offer — the golf sims, first-person action games, and many more that put you into the heart of the experience.

What you might not know is Meta recently added a new tool to the Quest 3 and 3S’ arsenal: 3D film playback. Now, they were already able to playback stereoscopic video but you had to find your own 3D film files which wasn’t easy. And I’m talking about proper blockbuster 3D films like *Avatar*or* Avengers: Endgame* — some documentaries and other simpler 3D content have been accessible for a while (but again, in a limited capacity).

This was such a major barrier to entry, that I had only ever used my headset to watch a 3D film once, but thanks to Disney+ integration, and Meta’s own updated video platform you can finally buy, rent, or stream 3D blockbusters all from within your headset. It’s super easy, especially if you’re already a Disney subscriber, and it’s a superb excuse to finally pick up the VR headset if you haven’t already.
