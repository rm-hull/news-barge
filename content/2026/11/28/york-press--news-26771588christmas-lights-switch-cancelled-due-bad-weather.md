## Christmas lights switch in York cancelled as rain continues to cause disruption

Acomb's Christmas Lights Switch on has been caancelled due to the weather *(Image: Pixabay)*

A York Christmas lights switch on has been cancelled due to the bad weather.

Acomb Alive was due to host the event today (Saturday, November 28) from 2pm to 4pm on Front Street.

The event had promised plenty of dancing and singing before the big man himself switched on the lights for Christmas 2026.

![Front Street](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21775838.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Front Street area in Acomb, York *(Image: Google Maps)*

However, heavy rain and cold conditions have forced the cancellation of he event.

Reporting the cancellation on its facebook page, Acomb Live said: "It is with huge disappointment that we have to announce that the light switch in this afternoon is being postponed.

"This decision hasn't been made lightly but the weather really isn't in our favour

"Watch this space for a rescheduled event!"

According to the Met Office, heavy rain is set to continue in York until around 5pm on Saturday (November 28), when it should begin to clear.

Sunday, November 29, looks set to be dryer with some sunshine. The maximum temperature is set to be 8C.
