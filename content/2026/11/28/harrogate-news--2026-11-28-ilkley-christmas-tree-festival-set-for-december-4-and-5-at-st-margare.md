**Visitors are invited to enjoy a festive display of decorated Christmas trees created by local community groups, along with music and refreshments, at St Margaret’s Church in Ilkley over the first weekend of December.**

The annual Ilkley Christmas Tree Festival will take place this year on Friday 4 and Saturday 5 December at St Margaret’s Church in Ilkley. The event invites visitors to experience the sparkle of Christmas trees adorned by local community groups, alongside music and refreshments.

Entry to the festival is free, offering an accessible festive occasion for residents and visitors in the region. The event organisers hope the community will come together to appreciate the decorations and seasonal atmosphere.

St Margaret’s Church continues to serve as a central hub for such community gatherings, encouraging people to visit and learn more about their varied activities throughout the year. For further details regarding the festival, services, or opening times of the church and parish hall facilities, the public can visit the church’s website at stmargaretsilkley.org or contact the Parish Administrator via email at [stmargaretschurchpa@gmail.com.](mailto:stmargaretschurchpa@gmail.com.)

The Parish Administrator, Vickie Kemp, noted in a recent communication the hope for many visitors to attend the festival. She manages enquiries during her working days from Tuesday to Friday, 9.15 am to 2.30 pm.

The Christmas Tree Festival represents one of the many opportunities for celebration and community engagement in Ilkley during the festive season.
