An airline that operated long-haul routes to and from the UK has cancelled all flights after filing for bankruptcy.

Norse Atlantic UK Limited operated routes from London Gatwick and Manchester Airports to destinations including:

* Bangkok
* Cape Town
* New York
* Orlando

Now, the company’s Norwegian subsidiary, Norse Atlantic Airways AS, has filed for bankruptcy.

> Norse Atlantic UK Ltd which operated scheduled services from Gatwick to Bangkok, Cape Town, New York, Orlando and Phuket has suspended operations with immediate effect. Further information: [https://t.co/eZ89qX0LkR](https://t.co/eZ89qX0LkR)
>
> — UK Civil Aviation Authority (@UK\_CAA) November 27, 2026

A bankruptcy trustee will now be appointed to manage the process.

Eivind Roald, CEO of Norse Atlantic, said: "The Board of Norse Atlantic Airways AS has made the difficult decision to file for bankruptcy despite extensive efforts from the Company and the Group to find a solution.

"We are deeply sorry for the impact this has on our passengers, employees and partners.

"The Board, management and advisers of the listed entity Norse Atlantic ASA and the UK subsidiary are working to find a solution for the remaining part of the business to continue operations."

## All Norse Atlantic flights cancelled and tickets discontinued

While Norse Atlantic ASA has filed for bankruptcy, it was looking for ways to keep its UK subsidiary operating.

But all Norse flights have now been cancelled and tickets discontinued.

UK Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) spokesperson, Andrew McConnell, said: "We understand that the cancellation of flights is causing concern and disruption for many passengers.

"We urge passengers who were planning to fly with this airline not to go to the airport as all Norse flights are cancelled.

"Norse customers should visit the Norse website or the Civil Aviation Authority’s website for the latest information."

## Advice for Norse passengers

The CAA has issued guidance for affected Norse passengers.

### Booked flight with credit or debit card?

Those who booked directly with Norse using a credit card should check with their card issuer about possible protection under Section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974.

Passengers who paid by debit card are advised to contact their card issuer about making a claim under chargeback rules.

Travellers with insurance that includes Scheduled Airline Failure Insurance (SAFI) should contact their insurer.

### Direct bookings with an airline or travel agent

Those who booked through a travel agent or third party should contact the agent first, as some may have included SAFI in their policies.

Passengers who booked flights only and paid directly by credit card should check with their card issuer for further advice.

### Charter customers

Norse also operated charter flights on behalf of other airlines.

Passengers who believe they may be affected should contact the airline or travel agent that issued their ticket.

Further information for passengers is available on the Norse Atlantic Airways website.
