A motorist who tried to avoid a breath test and a persistent shoplifter were among defendants sentenced recently at York Magistrates Court.

John Sutton, 56, of Flaxley Road, Selby, denied a charge of failure to provide a breath specimen when suspected of drink driving and was convicted at trial.

He was given a 12-month community order with 80 hours’ unpaid work, banned from driving for 18 months and ordered to pay £500 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Steven Ferrol, 57, of Chichester Close, Scalby, pleaded guilty to failure to comply with the requirements of the sex offenders register.

He was jailed for six weeks and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £154 statutory surcharge.

Laura Cowper, 41, of Fossway, off Malton Road, York, pleaded guilty to stealing wine worth £110.50 from Tesco in York.

She was jailed for 12 weeks.

Liam Doherty, 35, of Ethel Jackson Road, Crossgates, Manston, Leeds, pleaded guilty to obstructing police, drink driving on the A64 at Scagglethorpe, driving whilst disqualified and driving without insurance.

He was given a 12-week prison sentence suspended for 18 months with probation supervision on condition he undergoes 12 months’ mental health treatment and six months’ drug rehabilitation.

He was banned from driving for four years and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Alexandru Rostas, 25, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to stealing a ring worth £3,200 from Barbara Cattle jewellers in York.

He was ordered to pay £3,200 compensation and jailed for 12 weeks.

Michaela Allan, 36, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to theft of clothes worth £82 from New Look, theft of food worth £98.25 and £30 on separate trips to a BP petrol station, food worth £120.89, £75.50 and £105 on separate occasions from Tesco, theft worth £57.20 from Co-op, theft of £15 from Tesco, theft of food worth £60 from Heron Foods and assault, possession of amphetamine.

She was jailed for 50 weeks and ordered to pay £643.84 compensation.

Mary Burns, 19, of Thoresby Road, Acomb, pleaded guilty to causing actual bodily harm to a man.

She was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision and 200 hours’ unpaid work.

He was ordered to pay £600 compensation.
