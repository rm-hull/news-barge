## Which UK supermarket does the best pigs in blankets? I tried 7 and ranked them

I tried pigs in blankets from 7 different supermarkets to see which was best *(Image: Newsquest)i>*

When it comes to Christmas, a staple for many households is the pigs in blankets.

A succulent sausage wrapped in crispy bacon can make or break Christmas dinner for many, so getting it right is key.

While you may have your go-to spot to source these delicious treats, have you tried others?

Well, now you don’t have to, as I bought some from an array of UK supermarkets to see who did the best pigs in blankets, and the result might surprise you.

![The pigs in blankets in the oven](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773879.jpg?type=mds-article-620)We cooked all of the pigs in blankets together, and some came out better than others *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 7 UK supermarkets' pigs in blankets ranked worst to best

I will preface this by saying I am no food connoisseur, but I do love a good pig in blanket, so take from that what you will, and how much weight you want to give my opinion.

I will also say that there is no Morrisons local to me, so while it may do a top pig in a blanket, I remain blissfully unaware.

The 7 supermarkets I visited were: Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Asda, Lidl, Aldi, M&S and Waitrose.

After visiting each one and getting the goods, it was time to cook, with everything going in the oven.

Another small note is that to save time, we cooked them all at the same time in the oven, but with some taking slightly less or more time than others, some may come out and taste better if done individually.

When eating them, I did a blind taste test, taking each one without knowing its supermarket, trying and then ranking them.

We did cook a few of each to ensure we got the best taste possible and a decent ranking at the end.

Here is how I would rank UK supermarkets for their pigs in blankets.

![All of the pigs in blankets cooked on baking trays](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773880.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The pigs in blankets were arrange out across two baking trays, with three from each supermarket cooked *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 7. Waitrose

I do not normally shop in Waitrose, and maybe this would be some people’s favourites, but it was not mine.

Waitrose was £5.50 for 12 pigs, so were the most expensive we bought, averaging 45.8p per pig.

But it felt a bit too gristly, and for me lacked a bit of flavour and aftertaste.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the Waitrose packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773942.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Waitrose's pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 6. Aldi

Aldi was next, and I will note that this was our only frozen pigs in blankets on the list, as our Aldi did not have any chilled option.

They were joint cheapest, costing just £1.99 for 12, meaning 16.5p per pig.

Despite this, they were also not among my favourites, tasting plain and lacking anything extra.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the Aldi packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773944.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Aldi's pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 5. Sainsbury’s

Sainsbury’s was next, and is another shop I rarely find myself in.

It was middle of the range pricing, costing £3.50 for 10 pigs, averaging at 35p each.

It was ok, little gristly, but the flavour was fine and it did a solid job, with the bacon a particular highlight.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the Sainsbury's packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773939.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sainsbury's' pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 4. Lidl

The other joint cheapest, as expect, was Lidl, but it was among the nicer options I tasted.

Lidl came in at the same price as Aldi for its chilled pigs in blankets, at £1.99 for 12, and 16.5p per pig.

There were good flavours, with it tasting a bit herby, and the meat was softer and easy to eat, I enjoyed this one.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the Lidl packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773941.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lidl's pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 3. M&S

Beginning the top 3 was M&S, which I assume most people expected to be higher up.

M&S’ pigs in blankets were the second most expensive purchase, costing £4.25 for 10, meaning 42.5p per pig.

And it does feel worth the money, with plenty of pork, nice bacon, and a great combination of flavours and aftertaste.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the M&S packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773943.jpg?type=mds-article-620)M&S' pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 2. Tesco

Tesco is my go-to supermarket as it is the closest, but even in a blind taste test, they were still among the best.

Costing a middle-of-the-range £3.50 per portion of 10, it averaged at 35p per pig.

And the pigs were tasty, crispy bacon around the juicy pork, and a nice aftertaste to finish it off.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the Tesco packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773938.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tesco's pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*

## 1. Asda

Now, this was not what I expected going in, but I have to say Asda does a mean set of pigs in blankets.

It came in at £3.17 for 10, so was actually among the cheapest, at just 31.7p per pig.

Despite this, bacon was smoky, and sausage tasty, and all in all it surprised me, but in the best way possible.

![A pig in blanket on a plate with the Asda packaging above it](https://www.worcesternews.co.uk/resources/images/21773940.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Asda's pigs in blankets *(Image: Newsquest)*
