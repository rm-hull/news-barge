A North Yorkshire pub has won a community award for the second year running.

The Black Swan in Ripon, run by Sara and Kelly Hirst, was named Best Community Pub Operator at the 2026 Craft Union Operator Awards.

The pair were selected from five finalists, having won the same award in 2025.

Craft Union Pub Company said the awards recognise operators from its pubs across the UK for their work with customers and communities.

Judges highlighted the pair's relationships with customers and their efforts to bring people together.

The pub's community initiatives include a weekly walking group that featured on ITV's Lorraine and the founding of Ripon Pride, which has grown into an annual festival supported by the council.

Judges also considered first-hand accounts from guests about the role the Black Swan plays in the community.

Sara and Kelly said: “We are so pleased to have won this award for the second year running.

“Community has always been at the heart of everything we do and we're incredibly proud that people choose to spend their time with us, whether that's supporting local events, attending Ripon Pride, joining our activities or just popping in for a chat.

“This award really belongs to our customers and the wider Ripon community.

“The support they've shown us over the years has helped make the Black Swan what it is today, and we're excited to continue finding new ways to bring people together.”

Frazer Grimbleby, managing director of Craft Union Pub Company, said: “Winning Best Community Pub Operator once again is a tremendous achievement.”

Mr Grimbleby said: “Their dedication to bringing people together, creating meaningful connections and ensuring the Black Swan remains a welcoming pub for everyone makes them outstanding ambassadors for both their community and Craft Union.

“I’m incredibly proud of everything they have achieved.”
