A major emergency response is underway at Garforth station after an incident, causing huge rail delays and cancellations.

Police, fire crews and paramedics were called to the station in east Leeds this afternoon (Saturday, November 28).

Pictures from YappApp show several fire engines, police cars and ambulances outside the station. Firefighters and officers in high-vis could be seen on the tracks.

A CrossCountry train was stopped on the line nearby, and a rail worker was standing on the track beside it.

Local reports say a person was hit by a train at around 12.55pm. The emergency services have not yet confirmed what happened.

Rail operator CrossCountry said its 10.05am Edinburgh to Plymouth service, which was due in at 6.47pm, will now end its journey at Leeds.

It will no longer call at Wakefield Westgate, Sheffield, Chesterfield, Derby, Tamworth High Level, Birmingham New Street, Cheltenham Spa, Bristol Parkway, Bristol Temple Meads, Taunton, Tiverton Parkway, Exeter St Davids, Newton Abbot, Totnes and Plymouth.

In an update to passengers, CrossCountry said: "This is due to the emergency services dealing with an incident."

The line through the area is blocked, and disruption is expected to last until around 4.15pm. Passengers are being advised to check live travel updates before setting off.

British Transport Police has been contacted for comment.
