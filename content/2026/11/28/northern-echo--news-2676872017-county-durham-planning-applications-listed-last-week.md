Durham County Council has received a series of new planning applications, with proposals ranging from tree works and home extensions to changes of use and agricultural developments. Below is a summary of the latest applications submitted to the council.

* 44 Busty Bank, Burnopfield, Newcastle Upon Tyne, NE16 6NG. Proposal to reduce the crown of a Silver Birch by around one third to half of its current height. Ref. No: DM/26/03164/TCA
* Land adjacent to 78 Winford Grove, Wingate, TS28 5DU. Application for a Tree Preservation Order relating to trees near 78 Winford Grove. Ref. No: TPO/448/2026
* 37 Galgate, Barnard Castle, DL12 8EJ. Re-pollarding of one Willow tree. Ref. No: DM/26/03163/TCA
* 11 Eldon Close, Langley Park, Durham, DH7 9FR. Works to a Sycamore tree including a 2m crown reduction, removal of two low branches, 20% crown thinning, and crown lifting to 2.5m for clearance. Ref. No: DM/26/03132/TPO
* Brancepeth Golf Club, Brancepeth, Durham, DH7 8EA. Crown reduction and removal of dead wood from an Oak tree. Ref. No: DM/26/03136/TCA
* Thornley Village Hall, Thornley, Tow Law, Bishop Auckland, DL13 4PF. Felling of a Leylandii tree that has significantly outgrown its location, and an Ash sapling. Ref. No: DM/26/03138/TCA
* Wadley Farm, Fir Tree, Crook, DL15 8EB. Prior notification for the construction of a general purpose agricultural building and livestock shed. Ref. No: DM/26/03135/PNA
* Oak Villa, Vindomora Road, Ebchester, Consett, DH8 0PQ. Reduction of two Pine trees to eaves height. Ref. No: DM/26/03137/TCA
* 2 Kirbys Drive, Bowburn, Durham, DH6 5GA. 30% reduction of a Sycamore tree (T1). Ref. No: DM/26/03146/TPO
* Bowburn Hall Hotel, Bowburn Hall Road, Bowburn, Durham, DH6 5NH. Semi-pollarding of an Ash tree (T1). Ref. No: DM/26/03144/TPO
* Moorhead Cottage, Ramshaw, Bishop Auckland, DL14 0NG. Demolition of existing south porch and construction of a new two-storey extension to the south. Ref. No: DM/26/03063/FPA
* Sea View House, The Green, Easington Village, Peterlee, SR8 3BE. Change of use of land to domestic garden with hardstanding. Ref. No: DM/26/03043/FPA
* 25 Tollgate Road, Hamsterley Mill, Rowlands Gill, NE39 1HF. Erection of a replacement 2m high solid timber fence to the rear garden boundary. Ref. No: DM/26/03035/FPA
* 6 Witton Road, Sacriston, Durham, DH7 6LQ. Construction of a porch to the front elevation and a two-storey rear extension. Ref. No: DM/26/02989/FPA
