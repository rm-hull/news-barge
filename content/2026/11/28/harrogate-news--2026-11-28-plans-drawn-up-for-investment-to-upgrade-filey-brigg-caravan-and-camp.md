**North Yorkshire Council has proposed a £1.6 million upgrade to Filey Brigg Caravan and Camping Park, aiming to enhance facilities, extend the season, and boost local tourism and economy.**

North Yorkshire Council has put forward plans for a significant investment to enhance Filey Brigg Caravan and Camping Park, a popular coastal holiday destination attracting thousands of visitors each year. Situated within Filey Brigg Country Park to the north of Filey town centre, the nine-acre site provides a base for holidays exploring the coastline and nearby countryside.

Currently, the park offers 145 caravan, motorhome or camper pitches alongside 14 grass pitches dedicated to tents and visitor amenities. The proposed improvements, which may require planning permission and are in early stages of development, include the addition of six glamping lodges, new hardstanding pitches, upgraded changing facilities, enhanced Wi-Fi and improvements to the electrical infrastructure.

The overall investment could total up to £1.6 million and aims to modernise the site, attract more visitors and maximise income streams. The upgrades are also expected to support local businesses through increased footfall.

> **North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for finance and property, Cllr Peter Wilkinson, said:**
>
> Filey Brigg is a popular holiday destination for families who come to enjoy the spectacular scenery and everything the Yorkshire coast has to offer.
>
> The proposals being brought forward would explore opportunities to invest in and modernise the holiday park, helping to safeguard its long-term future as a valuable council-owned asset.
>
> If approved, the investment could enhance the facilities, broaden the accommodation on offer and improve the visitor experience 6 which in turn would help the local economy and support local businesses.
>
> The proposals form part of our wider commitment to support North Yorkshire’s coastline, with significant investment in major regeneration and development projects underway in coastal towns including Scarborough and Whitby.
>
> We want Filey Brigg to remain a high-quality visitor destination for years to come, and I look forward to receiving more details on the proposals early next year.

Planned essential infrastructure works will include upgraded electrical systems and improved changing facilities designed to enhance visitor comfort and reduce disruption during poor weather, potentially extending the park’s operating season. The business case for these proposals is due to be presented to the council’s executive in 2027.

The introduction of glamping lodges is expected to generate additional annual revenue, with an anticipated investment recovery period of around six years. The upgraded hardstanding pitches could mitigate weather-related closures and increase occupancy levels.

The council intends to proceed with the plans over the coming months, seeking input from councillors and the local community as the scheme develops. The investment aligns with broader efforts to support tourism and essential services throughout North Yorkshire.
