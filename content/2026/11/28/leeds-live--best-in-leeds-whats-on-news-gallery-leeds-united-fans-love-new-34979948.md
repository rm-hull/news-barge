# Leeds United fans will love this new special city centre opening

## You should be able to find the perfect Christmas gift for the Leeds United fan in your life

Leeds United fans will love this new opening in the city.

Artist Andy McVeigh, better known as Burley Banksy, has offered a look inside his latest pop up shop inside the Merrion Centre. Based near the iconic Jumbo Records, the Burley Banksy’s store stocks lots of Leeds United-themed Christmas gift ideas.

The Burley Banksy is renowned for his distinctive street art celebrating Leeds - its people, places and sporting heritage. Shoppers visiting the new store can browse a wide range of Leeds United gifts, football memorabilia, striking prints, hand-painted canvases and artwork.

Andy, better known as Burley Banksy, said: “It’s brilliant to be bringing the latest version of my shop to the Merrion Centre’s main mall, especially in a fantastic new location right next to Jumbo Records. I’ve been part of the Merrion Centre for a number of years now, and it’s always been a great place to showcase my work and meet people who share my love of Leeds. It’s exciting to give the shop a fresh look and a new home just in time for Christmas.

“I’ve got a great mix of Leeds United-themed gifts, artwork and plenty of quirky bits and pieces that make perfect Secret Santa presents and stocking fillers. There’s something for all budgets, so I’d love people to pop in and have a look.”

Leila Fell, apprentice asset manager at Town Centre Securities PLC, owners of the Merrion Centre, added: “Burley Banksy has become a real favourite with our shoppers over the years, and we’re delighted to see his latest shop take pride of place in a fantastic new location on our main mall. His artwork captures everything people love about Leeds, from the city’s landmarks and culture to our passion for Leeds United.

“With such a brilliant range of distinctive gifts on offer, it’s the perfect place to find something a little different this Christmas. Supporting independent businesses and local talent is incredibly important to us, and we’d encourage everyone to pop in, explore the collection and discover a unique gift this festive season.”

The Burley Banksy pop-up is open between 10am and 5pm every day until Christmas Eve.
