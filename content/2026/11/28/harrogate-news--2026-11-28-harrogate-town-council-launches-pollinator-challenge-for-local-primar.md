**Primary schools across Harrogate are invited to participate in a new environmental education project encouraging pupils to learn about pollinators and biodiversity through practical gardening activities.**

Harrogate Town Council has initiated ‘Harrogate Grows: The Schools Pollinator Challenge’, a town-wide environmental education programme aimed at primary schools within the Harrogate parish. The project seeks to engage children in practical learning about pollinators, biodiversity, gardening, and the care of their local environment during the spring and summer of 2027.

Participating schools will receive a free Pollinator Pack that includes a bee education resource, teaching materials, a participation certificate, and 500 seed balls sufficient to create approximately 25 square metres of pollinator-friendly planting. Schools with limited outdoor space are also encouraged to take part using alternative areas such as beds, planters, or pots.

The planting phase will occur between March and May 2027, after which schools will document pupils’ progress and learning throughout the summer term. Entries detailing their experiences and achievements will be submitted by the end of July, with assessments taking place in August and September.

The initiative places emphasis on participation, environmental learning, creativity, and positive impact rather than purely on horticultural success, to accommodate schools of varying sizes and resources.

The challenge will culminate in a celebration and awards event scheduled for September 2027. Award categories include Pollinator School of the Year, Best Recycled Growing Project, Young Pollinator Champions Award, Wildlife Garden Award, and the Mayor’s Pollinator Award.

> **Paul Forster:**  
> Harrogate is rightly proud of its beautiful parks, gardens and floral heritage. This challenge gives children an opportunity to build on that tradition, learn why pollinators are so important and create something positive for wildlife within their own school grounds. I hope every eligible primary school will consider joining us.
>
> Harrogate Grows has been designed to be straightforward, inclusive and useful to teachers. Schools do not need a large garden or specialist facilities: the emphasis is on children taking part, learning together and understanding the difference that even a small pollinator-friendly space can make.

Primary schools within the Harrogate parish are invited to register their interest by 17 December 2026. Pollinator Packs will be distributed during February and March 2027.

For further details, registration information and to download the information pack, schools can visit the Harrogate Town Council website at [https://harrogatetowncouncil.gov.uk/](https://harrogatetowncouncil.gov.uk/) or contact Events & Culture Lead Paul Forster via [events@harrogatetowncouncil.gov.uk](mailto:events@harrogatetowncouncil.gov.uk) or by telephone at 01423 642542.
