## Police release image after toy stolen from York gift shop

Police would like to speak to this person in connection with an alleged theft from a York shop *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A picture of a young person has been released by police following the theft of a toy in York.

North Yorkshire Police say they are investigating the report of a theft from the Give The Dog A Bone gift shop, Fossgate, York.

The incident is alleged to have happened around lunchtime on November 22, 2026, with a plush toy reported to have been stolen.
