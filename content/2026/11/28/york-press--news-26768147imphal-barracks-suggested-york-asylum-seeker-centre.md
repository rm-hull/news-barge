**Imphal Barracks suggested for York asylum seeker centre**

REGARDING the ex-air force base at Linton on Ouse near York becoming a centre to house asylum seekers, a far more suitable site is available relatively near and far more suitable.

The Imphal Barracks in Fulford Road is due to close by 2031.

It is set in 73 acres and expected to be the site for over 650 new houses.

The site is built to keep intruders out and, I imagine, it could be easily adapted to keep people in.

The Imphal Barracks is near to the York police station. The site has existing water/electric and sewerage services, has its own "lock up" and I imagine it would be a far better bet than Linton on Ouse.

The barracks are to close – just bring the date forward and have a bit of lateral thinking.

*D M Deamer,*

*Penleys Grove Street,*

*Monkgate,*

*York*

**Burnham so much better than the last 5 Tory PMs**

MAY I congratulate Jane White for her excellent letter (November 26, 2026) explaining to Peter Rickaby that his frequent letters to The Press are a perfect example of relentless negativity concerning the present Labour Government in general and Prime Minister Andy Burnham in particular.

May I also censure the letters from TJ Ryder in Acomb as being equally negative.

It is time that both these correspondents faced up to the fact that the next General Election is unlikely to be before the summer of 2029.

Please just get used to that idea gentlemen and stop carping.

I presume that you both favour a government with right wing leanings. If so, do tell us, is there even one Prime Minister from the former Tory Prime Ministers, Cameron through to Sunak, who could do a better job than Andy Burnham?

Some readers may know that I have been a member of the LibDems ever since the party was founded in 1988 and was for 12 years a City of York Councillor including four years as Executive Member (Resources).

So please don’t be surprised that I have found the performance so far of Andy Burnham as our PM really encouraging.

I am now hopeful that this Labour Government will extract the UK from the economic shambles left by the Tories, the very difficult world situation (defence and energy) caused by the madman in the White House, and the economic car crash of Brexit.

*Quentin Macdonald,*

*Manor Farm, Church Lane,*

*Nether Poppleton,*

*York*

... BRAVO Jane White! In her letter of November 26, she has said what I have wanted to say in print for years, but hesitated to do so, for fear of being called woke.

She says that certain people send letters to The Press that are unbalanced and full of negativity.

These people pretend that life is simple, and that problems of governance are simple to solve.

I disagree. Governance at every level is complex, requiring many trade-offs.

When I read about failures in this country and abroad, I ask myself - could I do any better?

I wish that I could say 'Yes', but I know my limitations.

I think that all those writing letters to the papers should first acknowledge their own limitations, and criticise others, if they must, in a temperate way.

*David Martin,*

*Rosedale Avenue,*

*Acomb,*

*York*
