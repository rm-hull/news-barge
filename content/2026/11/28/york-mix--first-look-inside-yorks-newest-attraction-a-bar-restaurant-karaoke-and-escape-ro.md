A new escape room, bar, restaurant and games centre has opened in York.

The Escapologist has officially opened its doors on Swinegate, following their soft launch on Friday 20 November.

It was previously home to the Biltmore Bar & Grill, which closed its doors earlier this year in August.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-29.webp)

Originally built around 1910, 29 Swinegate has previously served as a First World War military hospital and a 500-seat church before it became a bar.

The Escapologist is a Goole-based group, with its two directors based in Thorpe Willoughby, near Selby.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-15.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-20.webp)

York is its fourth site, with its other three venues at the White Rose Centre in Leeds, Xscape in Castleford, and the Metrocentre in Gateshead.

Inside, the steampunk-inspired decor features copper pipes, brass accents, and leather seating, as well as a huge cog wall piece above the bar.

There are currently three escape rooms available to play.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-8.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-12.webp)

Space enthusiasts can take on Mission to Mars, adventure lovers can journey through Hadrig’s Forgotten Forest, while mystery fans can climb aboard The Grand Continental Express.

Each experience is designed and built in-house, and accommodates groups of up to six players. It’s aimed at families, friends, celebrations and team outings.

In addition to the ‘three ever-evolving escape room themes’, there will also be a Valhalla-inspired room and horror-theme room to come.

The Escapologist isn’t just the escape rooms though – there’s a bar, food, and games to enjoy too.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-3.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-6.webp)

There’s EscaPool, an original game that combines crazy golf with pool; interactive darts lanes, which brings instant scoring and video game graphics; and EscaSING, The Escapologist’s private karaoke experience.

Each activity can be booked separately, or booked into a package by a member of staff.

The central bar features ‘smoking cocktails’, classic favourites, fine wines, spirits, and non-alcoholic options. Guests can enjoy a drink before or after their games, or simply pop in for a bev while shopping around the city centre.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-17.webp)

The cocktail menu features classics such as Mojito, Strawberry Daiquiri, and Margaritas, and signatures like the Copper Connection (with raspberry vodka, peach liqueur, and pineapple) and the The Steam Engine (with cherry gin, Chambord, and cranberry syrup).

The extensive food menu has comfort food classics including burgers, loaded fries and nachos, tasty skewers, flatbreads – and sharing boards if you can’t decide!

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-14.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-escapologist-york-13.webp)

The York venue also offers bottomless brunch, with 90 minutes of bottomless selected drinks (Prosecco, wine, draught lager, cider and cocktail options) and main meal of your choice.

The Escapologist also has bookable Mixology classes and party packages.

Escape room bookings start at off-peak pricing of £22. Find out more and book your experience at their website here.
