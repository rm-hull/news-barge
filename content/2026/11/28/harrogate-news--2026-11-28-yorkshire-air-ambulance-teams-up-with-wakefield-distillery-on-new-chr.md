**Forged Spirits and Yorkshire Air Ambulance launch limited-edition ‘Forged and Flight’ gin, with £1.50 from every £39 bottle supporting lifesaving regional air ambulance services.**

Yorkshire Air Ambulance Charity has joined forces with Wakefield-based family distillery Forged Spirits to release a new Yorkshire-made gin, offering a festive gift option that also benefits the charity’s critical services across the region. The limited-edition London Dry Gin, named ‘Forged and Flight’, draws inspiration from the gardens of Nostell Priory, located near the Yorkshire Air Ambulance’s Nostell Air Support Unit.

Handcrafted in Yorkshire, the gin features delicate floral and herbal flavours including lime flower, elderflower blossom and meadowsweet. Priced at £39 per bottle, £1.50 from every sale will be donated directly to Yorkshire Air Ambulance to support its lifesaving work.

The partnership was officially marked with a launch event on 11 November at Forged Spirits’ Art Deco-inspired Distillery Bar at Tileyard North, Wakefield. More than 80 guests attended to sample the gin and enjoy guided tours showcasing the distillery’s traditional small-batch production methods.

> **Gary Ford:**  
> As a Yorkshire business, we’re incredibly proud to be working with Yorkshire Air Ambulance to support a charity that makes such a difference to people across our region. Creating Forged and Flight has been a really special project for us. We wanted to develop something that was genuinely connected to the local area, while giving people another way to support the incredible work of the air ambulance crews.  
> It’s been fantastic to see the gin come to life and celebrate its launch with so many people. We’re delighted to be supporting such a worthwhile cause, and hope people will get behind Forged and Flight in the run-up to Christmas.

Helen Berriman, Corporate Fundraising Manager at Yorkshire Air Ambulance, welcomed the new partnership:

> **Helen Berriman, Corporate Fundraising Manager at Yorkshire Air Ambulance:**  
> We’re absolutely delighted to be working with Gary and the team at Forged Spirits on this fantastic collaboration. Nostell is a special place for Yorkshire Air Ambulance, so it’s lovely to see that connection celebrated through a product that’s been created right here in Wakefield.  
> Support from local businesses makes a real difference to our Charity, and we’re incredibly grateful to Forged Spirits for choosing to support us in such a creative way. We hope people will get behind Forged and Flight this Christmas, knowing that every bottle purchased will help us continue delivering lifesaving care to people across Yorkshire.

Yorkshire Air Ambulance provides emergency response across the county with specialist crews flying from two bases: Nostell near Wakefield and Topcliffe near Thirsk. Operating three helicopters and staffed with highly trained medical teams, the charity delivers critical pre-hospital care to patients in need. It relies entirely on donations and fundraising to maintain operations.

Those interested can purchase the ‘Forged and Flight’ gin for £39 via the Yorkshire Air Ambulance website, with proceeds helping to sustain vital emergency services across Yorkshire this Christmas and throughout the year. For more information, visit www.yaa.org.uk/forged-and-flight-gin.
