Firefighters were called to an incident of flooding in York last night (Friday).

A York crew responded at 9.37pm to reports of flooding inside a property on Bishopthorpe Road.

The electrics had already been isolated before they arrived.

#### Bishopthorpe Road, York

Crews checked the property was safe and gave advice to the responsible person who was present.
