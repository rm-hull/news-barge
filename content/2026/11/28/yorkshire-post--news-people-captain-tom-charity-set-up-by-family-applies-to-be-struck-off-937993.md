# Captain Tom: Charity set up by family applies to be struck off

## A charity set up by the family of renowned pandemic fundraiser Captain Sir Tom Moore has applied to be struck off.

The charity is now known as the 1189808 Foundation, having dropped Sir Tom’s name at the request of his family last year.

An application to have it struck off appeared on the Companies House register on Friday.

The dropping of Sir Tom’s name came after the charities watchdog found there had been “repeated” misconduct and mismanagement on the part of Sir Tom’s daughter and her husband in their running of the foundation.

The report found a “repeated pattern of behaviour” through which Hannah Ingram-Moore and her husband Colin made private gains and which the regulator said would have left people feeling “misled”.

The report found that a £1.4 million book deal and an £18,000 awards ceremony appearance fee were among the financial benefits the Ingram-Moores enjoyed through their family links to the Captain Tom Foundation.

The Ingram-Moores had already been banned from being charity trustees, and Mrs Ingram-Moore was prohibited from being a trustee or holding a senior management role in any charity in England and Wales for 10 years, and Mr Ingram-Moore for eight years.

They both resigned from the foundation.

The millions raised by the late Sir Tom and donated to NHS Charities Together before the foundation was formed were not part of the commission’s inquiry.
