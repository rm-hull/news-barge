The WIRED Reviews team is big on meal kits, especially around Black Friday and Cyber Week, when the last thing any of us wants to do is chop vegetables. In an alternate world, I’m the kind of person who grows her own produce and herbs, meal-preps every Sunday, and buys exclusively organic groceries from my local farmer’s market. But in this world, I live in a Chicago apartment with no counter space, a black thumb, and absolutely no desire to spend my precious free time sourcing ingredients. Without the occasional meal kit, I’d probably funnel my entire paycheck into DoorDash. Sue me.

Fortunately, Black Friday is bringing some fantastic deals on our favorite meal delivery services. They’re convenient, but normally not cheap. CookUnity, Green Chef, and Purple Carrot are all offering up to 50 percent off your first box. I’ve rounded up those deals, plus a few more worth considering, including a steal for parents who could use a little help feeding their babies and toddlers.

For even more non-culinary deals, check out our roundup of the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals.

**Spotlight Deal** For parents and guardians,**Little Spoon is offering 65 percent off** your first order until December 1.

## These Meal Kits Are All Half Off

For flavorful prepared meals like truffled beef stroganoff and Filipino adobo that’s better than takeout, **CookUnity is offering 50 percent off your first order**.

CookUnity is the best-tasting premade-meal delivery service. That’s because CookUnity enlists restaurant chefs to craft recipes and regional cooks to prep meals in commissary kitchens. They’re then delivered in compostable, recyclable packaging, and each meal lasts in the fridge for four to seven days after delivery (modified atmosphere packaging). Former reviewer Matthew Korfhage has taste-tested multiple Indian dishes from the Seattle Kitchen run by chef Gaurav Raj, and he says it’s just as delicious as his local Punjabi restaurant (if not more). That also means menu options and food quality will depend on your location; to date, hubs include New York, Austin, Miami, Atlanta, Los Angeles, Seattle, Chicago, and Toronto. (Check the delivery map to check whether CookUnity services your area.)

For USDA-certified organic meals, **Green Chef is offering 50 percent off your first box and 20 percent off your following orders for two months**. (That’s up to 20 percent off your next eight boxes.)

Green Chef is the healthiest meal delivery service, so much so that they sent an apology note to former reviewer Matthew Korfhage when they had to substitute nonorganic Roma tomatoes. Perishable ingredients, including fresh herbs, produce, and wild-caught fish, almost always arrive fresh, well-organized, and wrapped in mostly recyclable packaging. Green Chef accommodates most diets, with a vegetarian and dairy-free focus, and it offers at least 10 keto options a week. It’s also one of the few services that prepares its gluten-free meals in separate facilities and offers free phone consultations with a registered dietitian.

For new members, **Purple Carrot is offering 50 percent off their first box and free shipping**.

Purple Carrot is WIRED’s favorite plant-based meal kit, offering both premade, oven-ready dishes and meal kits for you to cook yourself. Meals like togarashi gnocchi, shakshuka, and Carolina-style brisket sammies burst with texture and international flavors. For recipes that require some prep, ingredients always come organized in plastic bags. A refreshing Mediterranean rice salad with stuffed grape leaves and oven-roasted radishes, grape tomatoes, and zucchini took tester Molly Higgins about 30 minutes to make. Another meal of squash dumplings required boiling the squash and stuffing the dumplings, which took over an hour, but it also ended up being Molly’s favorite that week.
