Choosing an area to buy your forever home in can be incredibly difficult and can come down to many things such as property prices and public transport availability.

Recently, The Telegraph released a list of the most desirable places to buy a forever home around the UK.

York was named the best place in Yorkshire and the Humber thanks to its “bountiful supply of history and culture”.

However, York Press readers said they would love to buy their forever home in the village of Stillington.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21752858.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Bay Tree Inn is one of the two pubs in the village. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is Stillington like and why do people love it?

If you want to live in Stillington, the average price of a house in the area was £431,250 over the last year, according to Rightmove.

The majority of properties sold were terraced houses and cost around £375,938.

Semi-detached houses sold for an average of £334,167 while detached houses cost around £687,500.

The village can be found on the York to Helmsley road and has a population of 782 people, according to the 2011 census.

The village has two pubs, with both the Bay Tree Inn and The White Bear Inn being highly rated.

The Bay Tree Inn is described as being a “warm, friendly pub” offering a range of ales, wines, spirits, and even hot drinks to keep you warm through the winter.

The pub has a variety of different menus, including small plates and sandwich options for a light lunch as well as classic mains, pizzas, and a Sunday lunch menu.

The Bay Tree Inn has a rating of 4.6 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with over 350 people leaving reviews.

Visitors have praised the pub for its “welcoming atmosphere” and “fabulous” food.

The White Bear Inn is the other pub in the village and offers a wide range of bottled ciders, lagers, and ales alongside its food menu featuring lots of pub classics like battered haddock, lasagna, and steak.
