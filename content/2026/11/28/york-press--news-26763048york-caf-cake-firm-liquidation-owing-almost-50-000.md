## Restructure saves popular York business and jobs following liquidation

Some of the Milliefox delights *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A popular café and cake company in York has gone into liquidation owing almost £60,000.

However, the business continues following a restructure which has saved it and the jobs of staff.

Millie Fox Kitchen Ltd of Chantry Close, York, was incorporated in March 2023, and entered voluntary liquidation on November 10.

Leeds-based Clough Corporate Solutions were appointed liquidator, and it has now published a report into the former company’s finances.

The report, known as a statement of affairs, says Millie Fox Kitchen Ltd had £6,399 of assets available for distribution.




This was made up of £5,706 cash in hand at the accountants and £693 cash at the bank.

The business had no preferential creditors – those paid first – but it did owe HMRC £42,484 as a secondary preferential creditor – someone paid next.

In addition, there were unsecured creditors owed a further £23,302.

This comprised a director’s loan of £12,642 and utility creditors (Business Stream and Yu Energy) owed £10,660.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20511850.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jo Millner *(Image: Pic supplied)*

This gave a total deficit or shortfall of £59,387.

With the business having issued and called-up capital of £1, this gave an overall deficit of £59,388.

Jo Millner is the sole director of Milliefox Kitchen Ltd, which has been replaced by another business Naughty & Nice Events Ltd, continuing to trade as Milliefox

Jo told the Press: “I really don’t think people realise how hard it is at the moment.

I’ve been in hospitality for 38yrs and I’ve never known so many people struggle and close down.

So many people I know are just giving up.”

In addition, Jo has also penned a lengthy article outlining the background of the business, how it was formed and how it evolved over the years, the challenges she and others in hospitality face and what she had to do to save the jobs of her staff.

That is also published online today on The Press website.

The replacement business trades as Milliefox, which can be found at: [https://www.milliefox.co.uk/](https://www.milliefox.co.uk/)
