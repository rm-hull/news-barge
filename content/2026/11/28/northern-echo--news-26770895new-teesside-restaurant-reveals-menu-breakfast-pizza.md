A restaurant has unveiled its long-awaited menu, giving diners a first look at dishes including breakfast pizza, giant meatballs and viral vodka pasta.

Crust K officially opened on Thursday (November 26) at the former Billingham Beck Visitor Centre following months of renovation and menu development.

The family-run venue, created by the team behind Crust Norton and mobile pizzeria Flaming Dough, has transformed the former visitor centre into a new dining destination overlooking the country park.

Now, with the doors open, customers can see what is on offer.

Among the menu items is the restaurant's breakfast pizza, topped with mozzarella, bacon, sausage and egg, putting a spin on the traditional full English.

The breakfast menu also features classic favourites and at lunch and dinner customers can try stone-baked pizzas, fresh pasta and Mediterranean-inspired dishes.

A standout dish is Crust K's giant meatball, while diners can also sample vodka pasta, the dish that has surged in popularity thanks to viral social media recipes.

The menu reveal has already attracted plenty of attention, with customers quick to share positive reviews following the opening.

One diner wrote: “Visited today for lunch. Food lovely, staff very friendly and efficient. Considering opening day all running smoothly. Lovely atmosphere and most of all lovely and warm.”

The opening marks the latest chapter for the business, which has built up a loyal following through Crust Norton and Flaming Dough.
