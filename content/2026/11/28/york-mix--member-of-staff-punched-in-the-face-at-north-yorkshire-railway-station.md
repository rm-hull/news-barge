Police have launched an investigation after a worker was attacked at a North Yorkshire station.

At around 12.55pm on Friday 6 November, a man punched a member of rail staff in the face at Skipton Station.

The victim needed hospital treatment for a cut to his face.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-skipton-railway-station-6-nov-2026-full.webp)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Officers would like to speak to the man pictured, as he may have information that could assist the ongoing investigation.”

Anyone who recognises him is asked to contact British Transport Police by texting 61016 or by calling 0800 40 50 40, quoting reference 387 of 6 November.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
