A Bishop Auckland driver who carried a child unsecured in his car and failed to co-operate with a roadside breath test has appeared in court.

Carl Coulson, of Close House, admitted failing to co-operate with a roadside drug wipe and using a car without a valid test certificate when he appeared at Peterlee Magistrates' Court..

The hearing on November 23 heard a young child was sitting unsecured on the knee of a rear passenger in the Ford Mondeo on Burnie Gardens, Shildon, on Septmber 2.

The manner in which the passenger was carried meant the car's use involved "a danger of injury", according to the charge.

Coulson was fined £80 and given four penalty points for failing, without reasonable excuse, to co-operate with the roadside tests.

He was also ordered to pay £85 in prosecution costs and a £32 surcharge.

No separate penalty was imposed for the passenger offence or for using the car without a valid test certificate.

His driving record was endorsed without points for the passenger offence, and the court took his guilty pleas into account.
