A busy County Durham swimming pool is expected to remain closed for a month due to 'unforeseen circumstances'.

The main swimming pool at Consett Leisure Centre remains out of action after closing earlier this month, and residents are now being told it could be another four weeks before it reopens.

Durham councillor Kenny Hope shared an update on the closure, confirming that the main pool is expected to remain shut for approximately four weeks.

A confirmed reopening date has not yet been announced.

The smaller swimming pool at the leisure centre remains open and available for residents to use.

Swimmers can also access other Thrive swimming pools while the main pool is closed.

In an update, Councillor Hope said: “We wanted to share a further update regarding the main swimming pool at Consett Leisure Centre, which remains closed due to unforeseen circumstances.

“We have now been advised that the main pool is expected to remain closed for approximately four weeks.

“A confirmed reopening date will be shared as soon as it is available, and we will continue to keep residents updated.

“The good news is that the small pool remains open and available for residents to use.

“Residents can also access any of the other Thrive swimming pools during this period.”
