The Los Angeles area was shaken by a magnitude-4.7 earthquake at 9:30 a.m.

The U.S. Geological Survey reported it occurred roughly 3.5 miles west of Manhattan Beach.

There are currently no initial reports of structural damage or injuries, according to the Los Angeles Fire Department.
