Two people have been taken to hospital following a two-car crash which closed a major road.

The road was initially closed in both directions between Brafferton Lane and Ketton Lane while emergency services dealt with the incident.

Three people were treated and discharged at the scene, while two others were taken to Darlington Memorial Hospital for further treatment.

A spokesperson for North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on the A167, Durham Road, Darlington at 12.55pm on November 28.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21775903.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pictures taken at the scene show a Porsche with extensive damage to its rear, with debris scattered across the road. *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

"We dispatched two double crewed ambulances and a clinical team leader.

"Three patients were treated and discharged at the scene and two patients were taken to Darlington Memorial Hospital."

Pictures taken at the scene show a white Porsche with extensive damage to its rear, with debris scattered across the road.

The car can also be seen with its bonnet raised, while a black car nearby appears to have sustained significant damage to its front.

Other pictures show a police car positioned across the road with its blue lights flashing, while traffic queues nearby.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21775843.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Pictures taken at the scene show a Porsche with extensive damage to its rear, with debris scattered across the road. *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

A fire engine was also pictured travelling along the road with its emergency lights on.

One lane was later reopened while officers controlled traffic and waited for the vehicles to be recovered.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21775904.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services were called to the A167 at Coatham Mundeville, near Darlington, on Saturday afternoon (November 28). *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

Both lanes have since reopened.

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Police for further information.
