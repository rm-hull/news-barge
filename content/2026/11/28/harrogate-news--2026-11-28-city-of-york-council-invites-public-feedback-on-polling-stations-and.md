**Public consultation launches on revised polling arrangements in York, focusing on accessibility and effective voting facilities in response to recent ward boundary adjustments.**

City of York Council has begun a public consultation to review polling districts, places, and stations following recent adjustments to ward boundaries made by the Local Government Boundary Commission. The review aims to ensure voting facilities remain practical, accessible, and convenient for residents across York.

The consultation, open from 20 November until 20 December 2026, invites local people to provide their views on the council’s proposed new polling arrangements. Officials are particularly interested in hearing suggestions that could improve polling district boundaries or polling locations.

> **Richard Webb:**
>
> We want to hear your views on our proposed new arrangements in response to the Boundary Commission’s changes.
>
> We are also keen to hear any suggestions for improving polling district boundaries or polling places.
>
> In particular, we are interested in feedback from disabled people about access to polling stations and any support that may be needed.

Residents can find full details of the proposals and participate in the consultation at [https://www.york.gov.uk/PollingReview](https://www.york.gov.uk/PollingReview). Paper copies of the survey, including Easy Read versions, are available at local libraries and West Offices. Requests for materials in other formats can be made by emailing [electoral.services@york.gov.uk.](mailto:electoral.services@york.gov.uk.)

This review follows changes to ward boundaries intended to better reflect the population and improve local representation. The council is emphasising the importance of accessible polling places to encourage inclusive democratic participation.

For further information, the council recommends reviewing the available documents and submitting feedback during the consultation period to help shape the final polling arrangements for upcoming elections.
