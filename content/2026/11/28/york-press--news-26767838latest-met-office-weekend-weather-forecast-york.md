The Met Office forecast for York predicts a cold and wet Saturday with lower rain chances and higher temperatures on Sunday.

Saturday will start off cold, with temperatures between 2°C and 3°C from 6am to 9am, feeling like -1°C due to wind chill.

Rain is expected to start at 40 per cent at 6am, dropping to 20 per cent from 7am to 9am.

Winds will be around 8 mph, with gusts between 17 and 19 mph.

Late morning, from 10am to 12pm, the temperature will hold at 3°C, feeling like -1°C, reaching 0°C around midday.

Rain will gradually increase from 30 to 40 per cent, with winds at 9 to 10 mph, and gusts strengthening to about 24 mph.

In the early afternoon, from 1pm to 3pm, the temperature will be around 3°C, feeling like -1 to 0°C.

Rain will increase from 50 per cent at 1pm, rising sharply to 80 per cent at 2pm and 90 per cent at 3pm.

Winds will be about 10 mph, gusting 25 to 27 mph.

Late afternoon, from 4pm to 5pm, the temperature will be about 4°C, feeling like 0°C.

Rain will be at 90 per cent at 4pm, then at least 95 per cent by 5pm.

Winds will be around 11 mph, gusting as high as 28 mph.

In the evening, from 6pm to 9pm, the temperature will gradually climb from about 4°C to 5°C, feeling like 0°C to 2°C.

Rain will be at least 95 per cent through 8pm, before dropping to 80 per cent at 9pm.

Winds will be around 9 to 11 mph, with gusts peaking at about 29 mph before easing.

Late evening, from 10pm to 11pm, the temperature will climb rapidly to around 6 to 8°C, feeling like 4 to 6°C.

Rain will fall to 70 per cent at 10pm and 60 per cent at 11pm.

Winds will ease to around 7 to 8 mph, although gusts will remain around 20 to 21 mph.

Sunday will be a considerably better day, according to the Met Office's forecast.

After a cool start, temperatures will climb to 8°C, rain probabilities will remain low, and winds will be generally lighter than Saturday.

The early morning will see temperatures around 3°C, rising to 4°C by 9am.

Rain will be below 5 per cent at 6 to 7am, then around 10 per cent at 8 to 9am.

Late morning, from 10am to 12pm, the temperature will rise quickly from 5°C at 10am to around 7°C at midday.

Rain will be only 10 per cent.

Early afternoon, from 1pm to 3pm, the temperature will be around 8°C, with a rain probability of around 20 per cent.

Late afternoon, from 4pm to 5pm, the temperature will be about 7 to 8°C, with rain falling from 20 per cent to 10 per cent.

The evening will see temperatures gradually settling at around 6 to 7°C, with rain only 10 per cent.

Late evening, from 10pm to 11pm, the temperature will be about 6°C, with rain dropping back to below 5 per cent.
