A County Durham resident has been fined £250 over a court order to clear waste from a property boundary.

Agmieszka Humiecki, 47, of Pine Street, Grange Villa, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates' Court on November 25.

The court register records a guilty plea to a complaint to enforce an order requiring the clearance of all waste from the boundary of the property in Pine Street.

The order had been made on June 24, and Durham County Council was listed as the informant.

The complaint was granted and Humiecki was fined £250 and ordered to pay a £100 surcharge to fund victim services.

A collection order was made.
