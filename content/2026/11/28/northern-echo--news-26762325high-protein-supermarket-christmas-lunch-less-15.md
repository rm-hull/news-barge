A popular County Durham food retailer has unveiled its Christmas lunch offering, with customers able to enjoy a traditional festive feast for less than £14.

Family-run G Bolam Foods, based on Salters Lane in Sedgefield, has announced its special Christmas lunch menu.

The supermarket, founded by George Bolam, has been serving North East protein lovers from Salters Lane, Sedgefield since 1989.

The family-run business brings a butcher, bakery, deli, fish counter, fruit and vegetable section, pet food department and restaurant under one roof.

This festive season, it will feature a choice of traditional meats, a selection of classic festive trimmings and a Christmas pudding dessert.

The offering will run from Tuesday, December 8, to Thursday, December 17, giving customers the chance to enjoy a taste of Christmas in the run-up to the big day.

Priced at just £13.99, the meal includes a choice of turkey, pork or gammon, served alongside an extensive selection of traditional accompaniments.

Diners can expect sausage meat, stuffing, mashed potato, carrots and turnip, Brussels sprouts, roast parsnips, roast potatoes and a Yorkshire pudding.

And for those with a sweet tooth, dessert is included in the price, with a traditional Christmas pudding served with brandy sauce to round off the meal.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21650657.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

The announcement is likely to appeal to customers looking for an affordable festive meal, particularly those keen to enjoy a traditional Christmas dinner without the preparation and cooking at home.

G Bolam Foods has built a strong reputation across County Durham and the wider North East for its extensive food offering, traditional butchery and commitment to quality produce.

The business has a long history in the region, with its roots stretching back to founder George Bolam, who began his career as an apprentice butcher in Crook before opening his own shop in Ferryhill.

In the late 1980s, the business moved to its larger Salters Lane site in Sedgefield, where it developed into a multi-faceted food operation incorporating butchery, wholesale, retail, a bakery and a restaurant.

Its latest Christmas lunch announcement adds another festive attraction to the Sedgefield business's offering.

With a choice of three meats, all the traditional trimmings and dessert included, the £13.99 deal offers customers an opportunity to enjoy a classic Christmas dinner at a price that won't break the bank.
