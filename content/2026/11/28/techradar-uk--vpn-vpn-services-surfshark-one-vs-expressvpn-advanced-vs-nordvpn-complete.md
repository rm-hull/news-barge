Black Friday weekend is here, and this year’s VPN deals are as competitive as ever. Surfshark One, ExpressVPN Advanced, and NordVPN Complete all bundle their VPN with a bunch of security extras, and all three are offering discounted prices between $2.29 and $3.99 per month.

Below, we compare features, streaming performance, and pricing to determine the best VPN deal.

**In brief:** Surfshark One is the cheapest option ($2.29/month), combining a fast VPN with antivirus and data breach alerts. NordVPN Complete ($3.99/month) packs in the most extras, including 1TB of cloud storage. Meanwhile, ExpressVPN Advanced ($3.99/month) adds a password manager, travel eSIM, and ExpressAI tools.

## Feature comparison

All three subscription plans include extra tools on top of the VPN itself, but the bundles feel surprisingly different in practice.

Surfshark One is the most familiar security suite: antivirus, data breach alerts via Surfshark Alert, an ad and tracker blocker, and Alternative ID, which generates an alias email and persona. It doesn’t include a password manager.

NordVPN Complete has the most features. Alongside its next-gen antivirus, you get NordPass password manager, a data breach scanner, Dark Web Monitor Pro, Scam Call Protection, and a generous 1TB of encrypted NordLocker cloud storage — the only cloud storage of the three.

ExpressVPN Advanced is arguably the wildcard. There’s no antivirus here, but you do get ExpressKeys (a zero-knowledge password manager), ExpressMailGuard email aliases, a privacy-first AI assistant called ExpressAI, Identity Defender monitoring (US customers only), plus a travel eSIM with three days of data.

## Speed and unblocking

Streaming proved a close race in our most recent tests. All three unblocked most platforms consistently, including BBC iPlayer, ITVX, 9Now, and TVNZ.

ExpressVPN couldn't access Netflix Canada and Prime Video Japan, while NordVPN failed Disney+ in Canada and Netflix Japan, and Surfshark stumbled on Disney+ UK. The latter also had the most trouble with YouTube.

As for speed, Surfshark’s WireGuard proved to be the fastest VPN on local connections at 2,005 Mbps. ExpressVPN’s Lightway Turbo managed 1,810 Mbps, and NordVPN’s NordLynx trailed at 633 Mbps. Connecting to a UK server from the US, NordVPN hit 1,093 Mbps, ahead of ExpressVPN (965 Mbps) and Surfshark (845 Mbps).

## Pricing

Surfshark One is the cheapest of the three Black Friday deals. It runs at $2.29/month for two years plus three extra months. That’s $61.83 billed upfront over 27 months, renewing at $99.99/year.

ExpressVPN Advanced costs more upfront: $3.99/month, billed $111.72 over 28 months (two years plus four free months). The upside is a comparatively gentle renewal of $119.95/year. The 12-month plan plus three free months is $5.99/month ($89.85 for 15 months), and month-to-month is $16.99.

NordVPN Complete matches ExpressVPN’s intro price at $3.99/month — $107.73 for the first 27 months, two years plus three months free. However, it renews at $219.48/year — more than double Surfshark’s renewal. Its one-year plan plus three months matches ExpressVPN’s at $5.99/month ($89.85 for 15 months), with a monthly option at $19.99.

Every plan carries a 30-day money-back guarantee, with Surfshark sweetening things further via its 7-day free trial. On pure value, Surfshark wins convincingly: cheapest upfront and at renewal, with unlimited devices.

## The verdict

On balance, Surfshark One takes it. It’s the cheapest by far at just $2.29/month, offers unlimited devices, and has respectable speeds and streaming. Budget shoppers and beginners should start here.

NordVPN Complete suits power users who want the fullest toolkit: Double VPN, Tor-over-VPN, and 1TB of encrypted storage — but brace yourself for the steep renewal price. ExpressVPN Advanced is the pick for privacy-first users who value its new AI products.

Whichever way you lean, the Black Friday prices make this a solid time to lock in a long-term deal.
