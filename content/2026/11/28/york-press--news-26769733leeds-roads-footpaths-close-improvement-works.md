Multiple roads and footpaths across Leeds are set to be temporarily closed for maintenance and upgrades.

Leeds City Council is implementing several temporary traffic regulation orders, affecting various parts of the city.

Dewsbury Road in Hunslet and Riverside will be closed for bridge refurbishment works from November 30 to December 13, 2026.

The affected area stretches between Apex Way and Jack Lane, with a suspension of the bus lane between Parkfield Street and Jack Lane to accommodate a diversion route.

The road will be closed from 9pm to 5am each day.

Kirkstall Road will also see temporary closures for desilting works.

The bus lanes will be suspended, allowing two running lanes to remain open while the work is carried out between December 1 and December 15, 2026.

The closures will be in effect on weeknights from 10pm to 5am.

Wormald Row will be closed for construction work from November 26, 2026, to March 31, 2027.

The full length of the road from its junction with Albion Street will be affected.

Grange Park Avenue in Roundhay will be closed to lay a water main from December 7, 2026, to January 8, 2027.

This closure will not be in place during the Christmas period from 6pm on December 24, 2026, to 8am on January 4, 2027.

Amberton Mount in Gipton will also be closed and have its speed limit reduced to facilitate the installation of a new water main.

This work will take place from December 7 to December 23, 2026.

Butterley Street will be subject to temporary prohibition of parking and a one-way traffic restriction due to events at the Testbed Event Venue.

These restrictions will be implemented on various dates, including September 25 and 26, October 3, 4, 9, and 13, as well as November 13, 27, and 28, 2026.

Footpaths are also impacted by the new orders.

Definitive Footpath Morley No. 51 will be closed from November 26, 2025, for a maximum of six months for a new site entrance construction for a housing development.

Definitive Footpath Morley 26 will be closed from December 14, 2026, for up to six months due to the creation of a construction access route for Yorkshire Water to install offline storage for sewage during storm events.

There will be no temporary diversion for this closure.

The Skelton Gate Definitive Bridleway will be closed for borehole drilling works from December 7, 2026, to February 19, 2027, on weekdays between 8am and 4.30pm.

A diversion will be available via Pontefract Lane, Beckside Crescent, and a non-definitive bridleway.
