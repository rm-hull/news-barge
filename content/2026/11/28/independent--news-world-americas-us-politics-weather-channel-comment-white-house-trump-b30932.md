There is a 100 percent chance of political drama after a Weather Channel correspondent's White House remark on Thanksgiving sent Republicans into a tizzy and Democrats into a laughing fit.

The controversy began Thursday when correspondent Justin Michaels was reporting on a nor’easter in Washington, D.C., with the White House visible behind him.

“Looking back at the White House, always beautiful to see the White House regardless of the resident of that home,” Michaels said.

Though he did not mention President Donald Trump by name, some conservatives took the comment as a swipe at the president. The clip quickly spread on X, where critics accused the network of bringing politics into a weather report.

“I want his resignation from the Weather Channel. It is offensive to the people that voted for Trump!” one X user fumed over Michaels.

![Weather Channel correspondent Justin Michaels sparked controversy during a Thanksgiving nor’easter report after remarking that the White House is beautiful regardless of who lives there](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/28/17/23/Work-900-px-x-600-px-(2).png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“The Weather Channel should be shut down immediately for this disgraceful comment,” another angry viewer said. “The media is a cancer.”

Michaels first joined The Weather Channel in March 2016 as a freelance correspondent and has since risen to senior national correspondent.

Others threatened to boycott the network, with one writing: “There are lots of weather apps. Yours won't be on my phone anymore.”

Another X user complained: “Well over HALF THE COUNTRY VOTED FOR OUR PRESIDENT,” adding, “I'LL NEVER watch you again.”

The backlash also caught the attention of Katie Miller, wife of White House chief of staff Stephen Miller.

“Legacy media can't even do the weather without bias,” she wrote.

Miller posted the comment around 9 p.m. on Thanksgiving, but it was gone by early Friday morning. She has not said why she took it down.

Conservative radio host Mike Opelka also weighed in, sharing Michaels' remarks and questioning the network.

“Hey, @weatherchannel - wut???” Opelka wrote.

His post attracted more angry responses from Trump supporters, including calls for Michaels to lose his job.

“Is this A-hole, Justin Michaels, still employed at The Weather Channel @weatherchannel ??? This is just unbelievable!!” Pro-Trump influencer Juanita Broaddrick posted Friday.

![Some conservatives interpreted Michaels’ remark about the White House as a subtle dig at President Donald Trump](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/28/17/2026-11-24T200807Z_1487406460_RC2JUOABGWFU_RTRMADP_3_USA-TRUMP.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

But not everyone was worked up about the forecast. Trump's critics had plenty of jokes of their own, defending Michaels and poking fun at the outrage.

“Republicans are such snowflakes,” influencer Jared Schult wrote.

“The majority of America agrees with this weatherman,” another user replied.

Others thought calls to shut down the network were going a bit too far.

“You want to shut down a media channel because of what one of its reporters said?” one user wrote. “Have you ever heard of something called the First Amendment of the U.S. Constitution, which bars federal, state, and local governments from censoring expression or punishing people or an entity for what they say or publish?”

The user continued: “The freedoms of speech and press are enshrined in the US Constitution, which is why the President, his Cabinet members, and MAGA politicians routinely insult, defame, disrespect, lie about, mock, and frequently smear journalists.”

On Friday, Michaels pushed back after an X user called his remark “stupid.”

“In such a polarized society, it's a completely legitimate thing to say. I don't care who lives in that house. It's gorgeous. And we're lucky to have it,” Michaels replied.

Michaels’ D.C. Thanksgiving report was not all about the weather. He also spoke with National Guard members from Tampa, Florida, stationed in the capital for the holiday.

“I wanted to give some of our wonderful National Guardsmen the opportunity to say hi and 'Happy Thanksgiving' to mom and grandma,” Michaels said.

“Despite the cold, wind and rain of the nor’easter, this was easily the highlight of Thanksgiving Day in our nation’s capital,” Michaels captioned a clip of the conversation.

“Thank you for your service on Thanksgiving and every day,” he added.
