**Two historic K6 telephone boxes in Ripon Market Place have been repurposed as a Penny Press souvenir machine and a visitor information point, enhancing the city’s offer to residents and tourists alike.**

Ripon has become home to what is believed to be the world’s first Penny Press machine installed inside a traditional red telephone box, alongside an innovative visitor information point housed in the neighbouring box. These installations open daily beside Ripon’s Town Hall on Market Place, adding a new dimension to the city’s visitor experience.

The two distinctive K6 telephone boxes, designed by Sir Giles Gilbert Scott in 1935, were saved from possible removal when Ripon City Council adopted them in 2024. Their continued presence and repurposing maintain an iconic feature of Ripon’s high street.

The Penny Press, operated by Ripon City Council, offers users four unique Ripon-themed designs to create elongated souvenir coins, providing a tangible reminder of the city. Meanwhile, Ripon BID has refurbished the adjoining telephone box to serve as a visitor information point, stocked with leaflets and posters about forthcoming events. Signage also directs visitors to the Visit Ripon website for further exploration of the city’s attractions.

Adding an interactive element, a talking sign from Ripon-based Audiosigns offers a warm Yorkshire welcome. Its current seasonal message advises visitors on festive events, independent shops, and places to eat and drink in Ripon.

Both telephone boxes are equipped with automatic locking mechanisms and are accessible each day from 8am until 9.30pm, closing shortly after the traditional Hornblower sounds in Market Place.

The project benefited from a collaborative approach, with part-funding from Visit North Yorkshire, North Yorkshire Council’s destination management and marketing service, alongside support from Ripon City Council and Ripon BID. Addisons Electrical contributed by upgrading the electrical installations within the confined spaces of the telephone boxes.

> **Cllr Andrew Williams:**  
> It is wonderful to see these much-loved telephone boxes serving the city in a new way. The Penny Press gives people a small reminder of Ripon to take home, while the BID’s information point helps them discover more of what the city has to offer.  
> This is a lovely example of Ripon City Council, Ripon BID and North Yorkshire Council working hand in hand for tourism. I would like to thank North Yorkshire Council for its contribution and everyone who has helped bring the project together.

> **Lilla Bathurst:**  
> Ripon BID is always striving to enhance and improve the Ripon welcome, and this information point is a lovely example of that. I’m delighted that an unused asset has been brought back into use, and that it now offers visitors a friendly, easy way to find out what Ripon has to offer.

> **John from Janet and John Penny Presses, responsible for the machine installation, noted:**
>
> This is the first time we have installed one of our Penny Press machines inside a red telephone box, and it has been a lovely project to be part of. The setting is full of character, and the four Ripon designs give visitors something special to take home.

Visitors and residents can find both the Penny Press and the visitor information point in the telephone boxes located in Ripon Market Place, adjacent to the Town Hall.

Further details about Ripon’s visitor attractions are available at www.visitripon.co.uk.
