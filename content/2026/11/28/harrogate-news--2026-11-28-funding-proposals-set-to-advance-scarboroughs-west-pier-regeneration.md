**North Yorkshire Council prepares for key decision on £2.4 million additional funding to restore Scarborough’s historic West Pier, improve fishing industry facilities and enhance public spaces as part of a wider harbour revitalisation plan.**

Proposals to breathe new life into Scarborough’s historic West Pier are progressing as councillors prepare to consider a crucial funding package for the project. The regeneration scheme aims to deliver significant investment to restore heritage buildings, improve facilities for the fishing industry, create new commercial opportunities, and enhance public spaces on the pier.

The West Pier has suffered from decades of exposure to harsh marine conditions leaving its listed buildings and structural elements in need of urgent attention to ensure safety and preservation. Final plans are now being developed ahead of a planning application submission, taking steps to safeguard the pier’s historic character while supporting its role as a working port and popular destination.

This project is a key part of North Yorkshire Council’s broader strategy to revitalise Scarborough Harbour and stimulate regeneration throughout the town.

Councillors on the council’s executive are due to consider the final funding proposals on Tuesday, 1 December, when they will be asked to approve an additional £2.4 million investment required to advance the revised scheme. This amount will be combined with previously allocated funds and resources from the ringfenced Scarborough Harbour Reserve.

> **Cllr Mark Crane:**  
> This project has the potential to secure the long-term future and regeneration of Scarborough’s West Pier and we are now reaching a pivotal stage in its development.  
> The pier is one of the town’s most important assets, supporting the fishing industry, local businesses, visitors and the wider economy. The proposals have been re-scoped and re-costed following extensive engagement as we look to protect the unique heritage while providing modern facilities that meet future needs.  
> The final funding package for the scheme will now be carefully considered by councillors next week as we continue to work towards securing the long-term future of this important harbour asset.

The regeneration programme focuses on addressing decades of wear on the pier’s buildings and infrastructure. Planned improvements include repairs and upgrades to historic structures, creation of flood-resilient modern facilities, better public toilets, upgraded pedestrian routes, new retail kiosks, and enhancements to the public realm enabling year-round events and activities.

The scheme has been shaped by engagement with harbour users, businesses, stakeholders, and a public survey conducted earlier this year. Support for the project was strong, with more than 80% of respondents backing restoration and improvement of the pier’s buildings, and over 75% supporting enhancements to the public spaces.

Following stakeholder feedback, earlier plans for a restaurant in Building 1 have been withdrawn. Instead, the revised scheme will feature enhanced fisheries facilities, new bait sheds, and welfare facilities. It also preserves flexibility for future harbour developments such as a potential boat hoist.

Subject to council approval and planning permission, this scheme could represent one of the most significant investments in Scarborough Harbour for decades.

Funding is proposed to be drawn partly from the £20.2 million grant awarded to Scarborough through the Government’s Local Regeneration Fund (formerly the Towns Fund) alongside the council’s own capital budgets. The fund has previously supported projects including upgrades to the West Stand at Scarborough Cricket Club, new facilities at the Construction Skills Village, and the Scarborough Fair arts, heritage, and sporting programme.

The detailed report to the executive committee is available to view or download via the North Yorkshire Council website at: [West Pier.pdf](https://edemocracy.northyorks.gov.uk/documents/s98861/Report%20-%20Update%20on%20the%20Scarborough%20Harbour%20West%20Pier%20Regen%20Scheme%20261201.pdf).

This proposal underlines ongoing efforts to protect an important cultural and economic asset for Scarborough and North Yorkshire, balancing heritage conservation with modern needs for the fishing industry and the local community.
