Amanda Owen's ambitious transformation of a derelict Yorkshire Dales farmhouse has reached another significant stage after years of restoration work.

The property, located across the moors from the family's Ravenseat Farm, has been the focus of a demanding renovation involving Amanda, her former husband Clive, their nine children and local tradespeople.

What began as a project to rescue a dilapidated farmhouse has gradually developed into the creation of a new family home.

Earlier stages of the work concentrated on restoring the building's structure and exterior.

Attention subsequently turned to the interior, including room layouts, plumbing, flooring and the installation of essential services.

During the fourth series, viewers saw the family facing further challenges as poor weather and the demands of working in a remote location complicated progress.

Work on the upper floors marked another important stage, with the installation of floorboards helping the building begin to resemble a home.

The series also explored the family's plans for individual rooms, drawing on traditional features and the history of the property.

By the conclusion of series four in November 2026, Amanda and the girls had enjoyed their first sleepover at Anty John's.

The occasion represented a significant milestone after years of work on the once-derelict building.

However, the renovation story is far from over.

Channel 4 has confirmed that two further series of Our Farm Next Door will follow the family as they continue developing the property.
