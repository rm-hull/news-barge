# Legendary pub reopens with new team looking to exciting future

## Many punters will be happy to see the pub back in business

A legendary pub has reopened with a new team looking to an exciting future.

We previously brought you news of The Fleece, in Otley, closing earlier this year. Now, new operators Lyndsey Acaster and business partner Chris Hall have taken on the historic riverside pub - aiming to give it a sustainable future rooted in the Otley community.

The Fleece reopened on Friday, November 27. This is seven months after it closed its doors.

Lyndsey runs The Old King's Arms in Horsforth and brings her experience of operating an independent community pub to the new venture. Chris has joined her as a business partner.

After spending recent weeks giving the building some much-needed attention, the team have now welcomed customers back. The work has focused on freshening up the pub while keeping its existing character, including its different rooms and fireplaces.

Lyndsey said: “We know The Fleece has a lot of history and means something to people in Otley. We want to look after what makes it special, give it a bit of care and make it somewhere people enjoy coming back to. We're not arriving with all the answers. We want to get to know the town, listen to local people and build something that works for them.”

Although Lyndsey brings experience from The Old King's Arms, she and Chris are clear that The Fleece should have its own identity rather than simply repeating what works in Horsforth. They want it to be somewhere existing regulars feel at home, while welcoming people who might be visiting for the first time. They also hope to build relationships with local musicians, independent businesses and community organisations.
