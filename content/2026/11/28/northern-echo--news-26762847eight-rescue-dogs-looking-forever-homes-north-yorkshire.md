From playful youngsters to quieter characters in need of a little reassurance, rescue dogs across the North East are looking for their forever homes.

Maxi’s Mates Rescue & Rehoming Centre, at Carlin Howe Farm near Guisborough, is home to dozens of dogs hoping for a fresh start, each with their own personality and needs.

## Here are eight of the dogs hoping to find their perfect match.

### Alan

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764318.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alan *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Seven-year-old Alan is a large Labrador-Lurcher described as a “cuddle bunny” once he settles.

Alan loves fetching a ball, then digging a hole for it after racing around, and is not reactive to other dogs on walks.

Alan needs an experienced female owner who is usually at home and has a garden for him to play and sunbathe in.

He is dog-friendly but cannot live with cats or children.

### Benji

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764316.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Benji *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Five-year-old lurcher Benji can be nervous around strangers, but the rescue says he becomes “very friendly and affectionate” once he knows them.

Benji loves toys, being brushed and a good back scratch, then shows his playful side racing around the paddock.

Kennel life is stressful for Benji, so the rescue believes he needs a quiet home and an experienced owner who will take things slowly.

He can live with older children but not other dogs or cats.

### Blade

![Blade](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764314.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Blade *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Blade is under a year old and still has plenty to learn.

The large lurcher is excitable but making good progress with his training, including walking on a lead.

Blade loves charging around the paddocks and playing chase, and needs an experienced owner to continue building his social skills.

Blade can live with adults and older children, but not other dogs or cats.

### Wren

![Wren](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764315.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Wren *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Four-year-old Wren is an American Bulldog-Malinois cross who becomes friendly and cuddly once she trusts someone.

Staff say Wren is walking much better with a Halti, although she remains strong and can be unsure around other dogs.

Wren loves toys and tug-of-war games, and needs a strong, experienced owner to continue her training.

Wren needs a pet-free home with adults or teenagers.

### Tyson

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764319.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tyson *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Three-year-old Akita Tyson is a “big bouncy playful lad” who is always happy to go for a walk.

The rescue calls Tyson a “softie” with plenty of energy who needs an active owner keen on adventure.

He particularly enjoys being outdoors, exploring and having a good sniff.

Tyson can pull on the lead and react to other dogs, but although the rescue says he previously lived with another dog, his current profile lists him as not dog-friendly.

He needs an adult home and cannot live with cats.

### Trigger

![Trigger](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764313.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Trigger *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Seven-year-old Trigger could be the ideal walking or jogging companion.

The Lurcher-Collie cross is fun and loving, and enjoys showing affection to people he knows.

Trigger loves racing around the paddock and going for walks, making him a strong match for an outdoorsy owner.

Trigger needs an adult-only home without other pets, with someone around most of the time.

### Toffee

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764312.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Toffee *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Four-year-old American Bulldog cross Toffee is a big personality seeking a fresh start.

Toffee is a “big bouncy fun loving girl” who loves attention, games, cuddles, treats and belly rubs.

Toffee can pull on walks and does not always know her own strength, so she needs a strong owner in an adult-only home.

Toffee cannot live with other dogs or cats.

### Solo

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21764311.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Solo *(Image: Maxi's Mates)*

Five-year-old greyhound Solo is quiet and laid-back but can be nervous in new situations.

Solo walks well on the lead once settled but sometimes needs reassurance.

Solo also enjoys racing around the paddock and stopping for a good sniff.

The rescue hopes a patient owner can build Solo’s confidence, and he can live with adults or teenagers and possibly similar breeds but not cats.

Anyone interested in adopting one of the dogs can find details through Maxi's Mates.
