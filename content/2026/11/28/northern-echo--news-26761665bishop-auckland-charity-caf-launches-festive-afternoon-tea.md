A busy Bishop Auckland café that helps fund food banks, counselling, and housing support across the region has unveiled its festive afternoon.

The Beehive Coffee Shop and Pantry, on Kingsway, has launched the limited-edition menu, which is available from now until December 19.

The charity-run venue, which opened in 2025 as part of the Angel Trust, has become one of the town's best-known cafes over the past year, attracting hundreds of customers each week while raising money for community projects and support services.

Its latest offering has already caught the eye of food creator 'Kyle V Food', who visited the café and shared a video showcasing the festive spread with his social media followers.

The afternoon tea features a range of Christmas-inspired sandwiches, savouries and sweet treats.

Among the items on the menu are turkey and stuffing sandwiches, bacon, brie and cranberry sandwiches, festive sausage rolls and homemade quiche.

Customers will also be served a Yorkshire pudding filled with pigs in blankets and accompanied by gravy for dipping.

The launch comes as businesses across County Durham begin unveiling their Christmas menus ahead of the festive season, with the Beehive hoping to build on a successful first year in Bishop Auckland.

Earlier this year, the café celebrated its first anniversary after helping support around 1,100 people through the wider work of the Angel Trust.

Profits generated by the business are reinvested into the charity's services, which include food banks, counselling and housing support for vulnerable people.

The festive afternoon tea is available until December 19 and costs £25 per person, with a £5 deposit required.
