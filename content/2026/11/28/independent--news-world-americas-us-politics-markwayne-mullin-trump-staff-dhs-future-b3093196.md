Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin has been labeled an “idiot” by others in the Donald Trump administration and told others he wished he never joined the Cabinet, according to a report.

“He’s telling people he wished he stayed in the Senate,” a Department of Homeland Security source told the *New York Post**.*

Other sources also told the outlet they heard the secretary making similar comments.

Anonymous White House sources also lashed out at Mullin in the report. Rumors have been swirling about Mullin butting heads with chief of staff Stephen Miller, and White House staffers reportedly took bets on when Mullin would leave.

“I wish he had stayed at his plumbing company,” one official told the *Post*. “He’s an idiot. Terrible messenger.”

![Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin has told others he wished he never left Congress, according to a report. His reported comments come after a series of recent missteps](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/28/13/53/GettyImages-2305196332.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Mullin has been under fire in recent weeks after shaky TV appearances and questionable choices. President Donald Trump has said Mullin is doing a “good job,” but reportedly was unhappy with his performance at a recent congressional hearing. There, Mullin tried to defend his trip to an LSU-Texas college football game after the nationwide ground stop for planes.

The Secretary said that if he hadn’t flown to the game, it would have created “confusion” and “distrust” for Americans trying to travel.

Just before the hearing, Miller reportedly put Mullin in a “primetime timeout” for his appearance on Fox News. While speaking to Sean Hannity, Mullin suggested that “SI” – the Trump administration’s preferred term for artificial intelligence – was behind the ground stop, a belief based on “one guy who is very smart with tech.”

Mullin has also been criticized for his handling of Trump’s deportation agenda.

Some have complained that he is too liberal and not pushing the key policy point enough. Mullin was hired after Kristi Noem was fired from the role after a series of blunders and the killings of two Americans during deportation enforcement surges in Minnesota.

Mullin got his wires crossed with Trump when he directed Immigration and Customs Enforcement to end most traffic stops, a decision the president quickly overruled.

![Mullin has been criticized for his response to the nationwide flight ground stop that included him defending his travels to a college football game in the immediate aftermath](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/28/13/58/GettyImages-2305026549.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A DHS spokesperson told the *Post*that reports that Mullin regretted taking the job were “absurd and false.”* The Independent* has reached out to DHS for comment.

However, it’s not the first report in recent days that questioned Mullin’s future. The *Washington Post* reported this week that White House staffers had a betting pool about how long Mullin will remain in the job.

In August, the *Wall Street Journal*reported that Trump’s angst to elevate Mullin from his seat in Congress representing Oklahoma to the Cabinet had been simmering since March. Despite the frustration, insiders told the* Journal* at the time that Trump had no plans to fire Mullin.
