A North Yorkshire care home has brought people together for free Christmas cake decorating.

Guests at Boroughbridge Manor's first class on Wednesday, November 25, met staff, residents and others from the community.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21770949.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Guests decorated Christmas cakes at Boroughbridge Manor's first free class. *(Image: Supplied)*

They had the chance to decorate a Christmas cake, while the home's hospitality team served tea and cakes.

General manager Lynnette Sellers said: “We understand the pressures of the festive season can be difficult and sometimes lonely, and we wanted to make a space for those who wanted to learn a new skill and meet new people within the area.”

The Boroughbridge home is run by Barchester Healthcare and provides residential, dementia and respite care.

For more information, contact the home on 01423 326814 or at [boroughbridgemanor@barchester.com.](mailto:boroughbridgemanor@barchester.com.)
