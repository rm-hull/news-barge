# Tribute to young woman ‘so full of love’ who died just six weeks after becoming mum

## Tributes have been paid to a young woman ‘so full of love’ who died just six weeks after becoming a mum.

Sophie Abram was just 23 when she collapsed in Boothtown on Friday.

Passers-by and paramedics tried to help but she could not be saved and she died in hospital, just two days before she would have turned 24.

Sophie, who was a social worker at Kirklees Council, lived with her partner Jake Ambler in Wheatley and the couple had just welcomed their daughter Rosie into the world.

![Sophie with Jake and Rosie](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmJiODg3MmJmLWM5MDAtNDExNC1iYjE2LTk5ZGJhYWQ5OTlhOTo3ZDJhNjNmMi0wYTg0LTRhODQtYWE4ZC0zN2E1NGQ0Y2EyYzI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"It's hard to describe Sophie to people who have never met her before because whatever I describe Sophie as, she was so much more than that,” said Jake.

"If I tell you Sophie was smart it doesn't do it justice because she was smarter than what I'm telling you. If I told you Sophie was a great mum, well that wouldn't be true because she was in fact the greatest.

"Sophie was so full of love for those closest around her and was so selfless and would constantly think and put people above herself.

"She was caring, kind, intelligent, smart, beautiful and had the most amazing smile that would make my heart melt every time I saw it.”

![Sophie and Rosie](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjc3ZmQ5NTE3LTc3ODAtNDI4MC1iMTAwLWFhMmE0ZjY3ZjA3NTphMDc4YjE2Ny1jNmRiLTQ3NzEtYWNjZC1hNTU2MThjODkwYjQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Jake, 31, said Sophie was very driven and completed a masters degree in Social Work at Huddersfield University before starting work at Kirklees Council.

The couple had been together since 2021 and Jake said it was Sophie’s dream to become a mum.

"We always spoken about both being settled, having good jobs, learning to drive, getting a house together, spending some time together as a couple then starting a family,” he said.

"We did everything we had set out to do and had the six best weeks of both our lives together as a family.

![Sophie and Jake](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjIyYzlmOTI2LTU0YTUtNGM2OS04MzNjLWU5OWY3NmNmNGQ4OTo1YWZmYjA1ZC01YTI2LTQ4MWYtOGI3OS00MmIyMjZkN2NkYjc=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"Sophie always said she wanted to give her children more than she had and this was what drove her to excel everywhere and to be successful in life.

"She also saw how her best friend, India, was with her kids and would always comment on how much of a good mum she was and how she made sure her kids had absolutely everything and were put first every single time.

"Motherhood meant giving our child every single ounce of love Sophie had, it meant working hard to be successful and getting a good job once she left university to ensure our child never had to worry about money and we could provide absolutely everything for them.

"I truly believe Sophie was put on this earth to become a mum - she had 23 years of life shaping and preparing her.

![Sophie after graduating](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjkwNmE5OTliLTdiMjItNDE4Ni1hZGYyLWVmMWFiYzBhYjQzODo2N2IwODUyZi1hYzdkLTQ3YTYtODUxZC04M2ZhZDhkZDg4YmU=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"Everything she had done in life was all leading her to her proudest moment and greatest achievement – giving birth to our little Rosie.

"Even though it was only for six weeks, Sophie made sure Rosie had everything.”

Jake said he wants to thank the members of the public who tried to help Sophie, as well as the police and paramedics.

Some of Sophie’s friends have set up an online fundraiser to contribute towards her funeral and help pay for nursery fees for Rosie.

“Not a moment goes by where I stop hurting or thinking about Sophie, and what should have been for us and our little family,” Jake added.

“If I could say one thing to Sophie it would be I just hope you know how much she was loved.”
