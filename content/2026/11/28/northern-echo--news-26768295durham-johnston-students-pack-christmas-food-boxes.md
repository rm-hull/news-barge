Students from a County Durham school have been helping pack Christmas food boxes for struggling families after hearing that almost a third of children in the North East are living in poverty.

Nine Year 13 house leads from Durham Johnston Comprehensive School spent a morning at Feeding Families' warehouse in Blaydon, where they helped prepare boxes for families across the region.

The visit came ahead of the charity's Big Give appeal, when donations can be doubled between midday on December 1 and midday on December 8, while £100,000 of match funding lasts.

The students were accompanied by senior house teacher Leanne Forbes and saw where donations from their school's Christmas appeal go.

Durham Johnston has run its Christmas appeal across all form groups for ten years, supporting Feeding Families alongside a food bank and a young carers' service.

The house leads are sixth form students who run the school's house system, organising charity and enrichment activities across every year group.

During the warehouse visit, the group heard that 30 per cent of children in the North East were living in poverty, compared with 27 per cent across the UK.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21771384.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Durham Johnston's Year 13 house leads visited Feeding Families' warehouse in Blaydon. *(Image: Supplied)*

The figures were published by Children North East in September 2026 using Department for Work and Pensions data for 2024/25, measured after housing costs, according to Feeding Families.

Ms Forbes said: "Children are at the heart of everything that we do as a school.

"To come in here today and find out that 30% of young children in the North East are living in poverty is a really difficult statistic to hear, and we want to do all we can to try and combat that.

"What I really wanted the house leads to see was what their work in school actually means to the charity, to see the operation that's going on here and all the donations that have come in, and be part of it.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21771386.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A student sorts food at the charity's Blaydon warehouse. *(Image: Supplied)*

"What we want our students to do is dare to belong and to be part of something, and coming here today to help others is a wonderful experience for them."

Feeding Families says it does not hand out food directly or accept self-referrals.

Instead, its boxes reach households through a network of partner organisations that are already working with the people who receive them.

These include schools, domestic abuse services, mental health teams and housing support workers.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21771387.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Students help pack boxes for Feeding Families during the school visit. *(Image: Supplied)*

Some boxes are designed for households that have a kettle but no cooker.

The charity says its network now includes 216 partner organisations across the North East.

At Christmas 2025, it distributed 6,300 festive boxes through 212 partner organisations, reaching more than 12,000 individuals and families.

Across 2025/26, Feeding Families says it supported 83,526 people and distributed 41,763 boxes and packs.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21771388.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Students pack food boxes at Feeding Families' warehouse. *(Image: Supplied)*

Wayne Dobson, the charity's chief executive, said: "The boxes these students packed will go out through the schools, family support workers and community groups we work with, so they reach families those people already know.

"Donations like Durham Johnston's fill the boxes, but how many families we can reach this Christmas depends on what is raised in Big Give week.

"So we're asking people to give between 1st and 8th December, while donations are being doubled."

Under the appeal, a £25 donation made during the matching period would become £50, provided the match funding has not run out.

Donations to the Christmas appeal can be made at feedingfamilies.org.uk/christmas.

The charity says the matching period runs from midday on December 1 until midday on December 8, subject to the £100,000 funding limit.
