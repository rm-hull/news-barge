# Five-Star Skaith: York and North Yorkshire Mayor has some explaining to do

## On the face of it, the £85,000 of tax payers’ money spent on a five-star luxury getaway to India by York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority is tin-eared liberty-taking at a time when public services are being cut left, right and centre.

![Five-Star Skaith: but is this simply the price to pay for devolved decision-making powers and pounds? (photo, PA) Pictured: Mayor David Skaith alongside former Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjgwNzBkZThhLWZmMmMtNDFmOS1iYWYxLTY4NWJiNzI1NmM3ZTozMTYxNWU0Ny1kOGJlLTQ3NTAtOGJhNC00ZjJiNTQ1OWM3MmQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


The Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, David Skaith, cannot be surprised if he is challenged by members of the public about his decision to stay at the five-star luxury Taj chain of hotels, which offer ‘indulgent escapes’ to prospective residents.

Details like the five-star accommodation matter; they stick in people’s minds. Five-Star Skaith. So many of his hard-pressed rate-payers can only dream of an indulgent escape to some of India’s most prestigious hotels, yet here they find themselves paying for someone else.

And, yet, we must remember: this is what devolution brings. It is the manifestation of that which this newspaper has been calling for for years. More money and more power in the hands of local leaders charged with spending it wisely.

It would therefore be churlish of this newspaper to lament the trade mission to India and its intended purpose: to forge trusting relationships with foreign leaders, businesses and investment partners who feel they can place their faith in us.

People like that, themselves, stay in five-star hotels and so in order to be able to spend meaningful time with would-be trade partners, Mr Skaith’s choice makes more sense. The key, now, is to quickly show people that this was money and time well spent, identifying with urgent transparency what the return on that investment looks like and outline when it might arrive.
