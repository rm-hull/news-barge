A Darlington teen with blood cancer has raised more than £8,000 after taking on a gruelling mountain challenge, despite being forced to abandon the final climb in freezing conditions.

Luca Ketteringham, 19, climbed two of the Yorkshire Three Peaks with three friends before worsening weather and temperatures as low as -5C forced them to postpone the final ascent.

The group set off at 4.30am on Saturday, November 21, to climb Pen-y-Ghent, Whernside and Ingleborough for Blood Cancer UK.

But after successfully conquering the first two peaks, the friends decided it was too dangerous to continue to Ingleborough.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765189.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Luca and his friends while doing the challenge *(Image: DARLINGTON COUNCIL)*

Rather than risk their safety, they called off the final climb and are planning to return when conditions improve.

Luca, who works as a lifeguard at Darlington's Dolphin Centre, organised the challenge after being diagnosed with Hodgkins Lymphoma, a type of blood cancer.

He wanted to raise money and awareness for Blood Cancer UK before starting treatment, joined by friends Evan Reeves, Jacob Warwick and Harley Coad.

The fundraising total has passed £8,000, comfortably beating Luca’s original £2,154 target, based on the combined heights of the three peaks.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765187.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Luca and his friends while doing the challenge *(Image: DARLINGTON COUNCIL)*

Luca said: "While we're obviously disappointed not to have completed all three peaks on the day, I'm really proud of what we've managed to achieve so far.

"We had fantastic support from our family and friends throughout the challenge and we're incredibly grateful to everyone who has donated and encouraged us.

"The weather took a turn for the worse, with temperatures as low as -5C.

"There was no point risking our safety to try and complete the final peak when it simply wasn't safe to do so.

"We've come this far and we're determined to finish what we started. We'll be back to take on Ingleborough as soon as conditions allow."
