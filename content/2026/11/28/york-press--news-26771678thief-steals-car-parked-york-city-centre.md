A thief struck at night to steal items from a car in York.

North Yorkshire Police said it was investigating the theft of items from a car in Arthur Street, in the Lawrence Street area of York.




A police spokesperson said: "About 3:30 am on November 26, 2026, an unidentified person has gained entry to a car parked on Arthur Street and stolen various items from within."

Anyone with any information is asked to contact PC 1223 Irvine via email [colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:colin.irvine@northyorkshire.police.uk) quoting Police reference 12260231776.
