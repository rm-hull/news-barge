A Darlington drink-driver has been banned from the road for 23 months.

Adam Hickey, of Sundew Court, pleaded guilty at Peterlee Magistrates' Court on November 23 to driving with excess alcohol.

The 29-year-old drove a red Mercedes A-Class on Haughton Road, Darlington, on August 30 with 103 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath, the court was told.

Hickey was fined £553 and ordered to pay £85 in prosecution costs and a £221 surcharge.

His disqualification can be reduced by 23 weeks if he completes a course by March 14, 2028.

According to the court record, the fine was imposed as an alternative to a low-level community order, taking into account the mitigation provided.

His guilty plea was taken into account when the fine was imposed.
