A dad-to-be burglar was caught mid-break-in after police spotted him with a T-shirt wrapped around his head.

Jayden Defty climbed through a window at Smart Sharp in Spennymoor and picked up a set of keys before police stopped him.

Prosecutor Carlie McArdle said the 19-year-old was inside for only a short time before his arrest.

She said: “He had a black T-shirt wrapped around his face and managed to get into the building through an unsecured window.

“When the officer detained him, he had a set of keys on him which he admitted picking up inside the building.”

The court heard Defty had no previous convictions.

Defty, who has no fixed address, admitted the burglary on August 16.

His solicitor, Rob Hutchinson, said Defty was due to become a father in the new year and wanted to change his life.

He said: “Nothing was stolen and no damage was caused by his actions.

“He accepts full responsibility for what he did.”

Mr Hutchinson said Defty had been open with probation officers and urged magistrates to follow their recommendations.

Sarah Cardew, chairwoman of the bench, said: “We appreciate your honesty and openness, and that’s important.”

“We intend to follow the recommendations in the report.”

Defty received a 12-month community order, including six months of drug rehabilitation and 70 hours of unpaid work.

Defty was also ordered to pay £199 in court costs.
