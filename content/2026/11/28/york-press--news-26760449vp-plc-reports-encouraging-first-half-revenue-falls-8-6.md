A North Yorkshire equipment rental group has reported an encouraging start to the financial year despite challenging market conditions.

Harrogate-based Vp plc announced its interim results for the six months to September 30, 2026, with chief executive Alice Woodwark highlighting "good momentum" in targeted infrastructure sectors and improvements following internal restructuring.

Revenue for the period fell by 8.6 per cent to £172.2 million, compared to £188.4 million in the same period last year.

Adjusted profit dropped by 2.3 per cent to £16.9 million, while statutory profit before tax was down 3.6 per cent at £10.6 million.

Ms Woodwark said: "Vp delivered an encouraging first half performance, demonstrating good momentum in target infrastructure markets and the benefit of a number of ‘self-help’ actions, aligned to the Vp strategy.

"Revenue was broadly flat half on half, before the impact of a planned reduction in Brandon Hire Station trading volumes following the restructuring of that business to focus on serving strategic Vp customers.

"Core infrastructure markets, including water and transmission, are in growth, offsetting ongoing challenges in UK general construction and housebuilding."

She added that the company has seen "clear quarter-on-quarter profit improvement" and expects the second half of the year to outperform the same period in 2026.

The group's international businesses also performed well during the first half of 2027.

Brandon Hire Station returned to profit in the second quarter following its restructure, which Ms Woodwark said has "strengthened the foundations of the group," alongside a new operating model.

Disciplined capital allocation and strong cash generation have helped reduce net debt.

Vp plc has introduced a new customer-focused operating structure and launched Vp Water, reflecting its specialism in that sector.

The group remains confident of meeting full-year market expectations and anticipates stronger performance in the second half of the year.
