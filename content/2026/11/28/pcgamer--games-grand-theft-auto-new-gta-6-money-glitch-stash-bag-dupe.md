Grand Theft Auto 6 money glitches were inevitable, but until now the supposed "glitches" weren't really glitches at all. The most popular methods involve looting a location and forcing the loot to respawn, which is less of a glitch and more of a quirk of how the game works.

The new GTA 6 money glitch is clearly *not* how the game should work. Reddit user MichaelOrSum was the first person I saw who found a way to make up to $1M in a few minutes. There's no swapping characters or forcing loot to respawn required here: You just need access to one of GTA 6's fence locations if you're in chapter 1 or 2 (there's a way around it in chapter 3).

Most people recommend finding a winched vehicle and stealing an ATM to pull this glitch off, but you can also just visit the location of the old GTA 6 infinite money glitch. The goal is to get your hands on a stash bag with some kind of stolen goods in it.

The next part will sound fake, but, trust me, it works. I gave it a shot myself and walked away with $30,000 in under a minute.

Here are the exact steps for the new GTA 6 money glitch:

1. Pick up a stash bag, preferably one with high-value stolen goods, like marked bills
2. Move your character away any nearby cars and crouch
3. Open your weapon wheel, swap over to items, and drop your stash bag
4. Without moving, rotate your camera until you can pick your stash bag back up
5. Repeat this until the money value stops increasing, which will depend on what's in your stash bag
6. Sell the stash bag at a fence or store it in your car and tell your partner to go sell it (this works in chapter 3 even if you haven't unlocked a fence yet)

For whatever reason, when your character picks the stash bag up while crouched the money value doubles. I've never carried around stolen drugs, but I'm pretty sure this isn't how it's supposed to work.

People in the original Reddit thread report that sometimes you can't pick the bag up. The solution to that is to swap to your other character and have them perform the money glitch. Otherwise, you'll need to start over by reloading a save or finding another stash bag. Apparently, marked bills (which cap out at $320K) are less prone to this than other stolen goods.

Reddit user angryPirate12 adds that keeping a glitched stash bag on your shoulder while in a stolen van and registering it at a Pay 'n' Spray will fill all 10 storage slots with stash bags of the same value. "I have over $10 million in my vans in less than 30 mins," they said.

I will note that most of GTA 6's story is about doing jobs for money, so filling up your bank account might ruin the fun of watching the numbers go up as Jason and Lucia's crimes escalate. Once you're pretty deep into the later chapters, money isn't much of an issue anyway, and there isn't a whole lot to spend it on. So, money glitch *responsibly*, is all I'm saying.
