The M18 is set for overnight closures for nearly a year due to maintenance.

National Highways will close the Thurcroft to Langham section, which includes Wadworth and North Ings roundabouts, between 8pm and 6am.

This will affect not only the main carriageway but also all slip and link roads, as well as refuge areas.

The closures begin on January 2, 2027, and are expected to last until December 23, 2027.

Traffic will be diverted via other M18 junctions and local roads, with signs directing the way.

Emergency services, works, winter maintenance, and traffic officer vehicles are exempt from the closure.

The exact duration of each closure during the period will depend on the specific maintenance tasks being performed.

The order for this closure was made under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

The notice states the prohibitions will only be enforced as indicated by traffic signs.

The measures come into force on January 1, 2027.

For more information, the public can contact the National Highways Customer Contact Centre on 0300 123 5000 or email [info@nationalhighways.co.uk.](mailto:info@nationalhighways.co.uk.)
