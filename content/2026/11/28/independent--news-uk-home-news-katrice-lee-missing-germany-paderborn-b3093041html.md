Investigators probing the disappearance of a toddler who vanished on her second birthday 45 years ago have urged anyone who has remained silent to finally come forward.

Katrice Lee disappeared on November 28 1981 while on a shopping trip with her family at a supermarket near the British Army base in Paderborn, Germany, where her father Richard Lee was serving.

Saturday marks her 47th birthday, but her family are still waiting for answers.

The Defence Serious Crime Command senior investigating officer said: “Katrice’s family deserve to know what happened to her.

“If you know something, but have convinced yourself it does not matter, it does.

“And if you have been waiting for the right moment to speak, that moment is now.

“Forty-five years should not mean the end of our search for answers.

![Age progression photo first issued 30/04/13 by the Ministry of Defence, of Katrice Lee, as she may have look aged 38-years-old.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/20/01M8F5RZVM7K1907V59M8WAJ9X.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“It should be the reason that someone finally finds the courage to come forward.”

In May 2024, Katrice’s father Mr Lee, a former warrant officer, tried to hand back his military medals at Downing Street in protest against what he labelled police failings in the investigation.

He said he was “not going away” until he received answers from the Ministry of Defence.

Mr Lee believes his daughter was abducted and has requested an independent or public inquiry into the handling of his daughter’s case.

Speaking in 2024, Mr Lee said: “I haven’t got answers yet.

“The MoD and the Royal Military Police know they’ve failed my daughter’s case since 1981.

“With what’s going on today, when everybody is aware of the blood contamination scandal, they’re aware of the Hillsborough disaster, it’s rather sad that all these institutions are more concerned about their reputation than just being open, honest and telling the truth.”

Natasha Walker, the older sister of Katrice Lee, previously said living without her sibling was “the hardest thing to do”.

Katrice was last seen wearing a blue duffle coat with a green fur hood, a green and blue pinafore dress, red wellies and a white blouse with a blue collar and cuffs.

Anyone with information can contact the Defence Serious Crime Command on 0800 616 818 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously via 0800 555 111.
