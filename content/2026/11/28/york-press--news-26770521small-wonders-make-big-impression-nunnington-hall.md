A Christmas tree filled with miniature treasures is capturing imaginations at a festive exhibition.

The tree, titled Little Footsteps, Curious Eyes, is part of Nunnington Hall’s Nature’s Noel: A Community Christmas and has been created by artists from Malton’s AHH Studio Collective in collaboration with Sophie Roberts of York’s West Plum Studio.

The design tells an imagined journey of discovery through Nunnington Hall, brought to life with tiny objects and hand-crafted decorations.

Serena Partridge, an artist at AHH Studio Collective, said: "Nunnington Hall is full of things to spark a child’s curiosity and imagination.

"Inspired by this sense of discovery, we created a tree filled with miniature objects and unexpected treasures.

"Little knitted socks represent the footsteps of imaginary explorers, while the decorations reflect things they might notice, discover or dream up along the way, like flowers, art and bees."

All decorations are made from natural, recycled and sustainable materials and incorporate scraps and offcuts from both studios.

The display also highlights creative techniques such as crochet, weaving and embroidery, which are taught in workshops at both studios.

Nature’s Noel is open to visitors at Nunnington Hall from Thursday to Sunday until December 20.

AHH Studio Collective has also been awarded a grant from the National Lottery Community Fund – Awards for All.

The funding will support new evening workshops for adults and a Saturday art club for young people.

More information about the studios and their programmes can be found at www.ahhstudiocollective.co.uk and www.westplumstudio.co.uk.
