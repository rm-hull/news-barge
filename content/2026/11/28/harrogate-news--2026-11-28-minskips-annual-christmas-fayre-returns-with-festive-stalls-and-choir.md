**The popular event in Minskip Village Hall on 5 December promises handmade gifts, refreshments and a free choir concert to support the local church.**

The village of Minskip, near Boroughbridge, will hold its popular Christmas Gift Fayre on Saturday 5 December from 1.00pm to 4.00pm, with free entry to the event hosted at Minskip Village Hall.

The fayre will showcase a wide range of stalls offering home-made Christmas gifts and festive items. Organiser Debbie Hargreaves said the event aims to support St John’s Church in the village, which has benefitted from proceeds of previous fairs.

> **Debbie Hargreaves:**
>
> We have been absolutely delighted with the reaction to our previous Fayres, which have raised much-needed money for St John’s, our lovely little village church.  
> Christmas can be a financial burden to many, especially this year. So, we are keeping prices as low as possible.  
> Once again, we have rallied all the Minskip crafters, makers and bakers to create a superb array of items which we can sell, including Christmas wreaths, table decorations, tree decorations, biscuits, plants, pre-loved items, refreshments, cakes and biscuits, plus a tombola.

Following the fayre, from 4.00pm there will be a free concert by the Boroughbridge Love Pop Choir in St John’s Church, providing a seasonal conclusion to the afternoon.

> **Debbie Hargreaves:**  
> We were approached by Boroughbridge Love Pop Choir a couple of years ago. Could they do a concert in Church for us after the fair? Absolutely yes, they could. The concert was free to all and was a perfect end to a brilliant afternoon. The acoustics in church of the choir belting out many well-known Christmas hits was truly fantastic. This is now the third year they have joined us at our Fayre and they are brilliant.

The event offers local residents and visitors an opportunity for festive shopping at affordable prices, with all proceeds helping support the village church.
