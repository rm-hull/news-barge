### What is the UK Championship 2026 snooker format?

After four rounds of qualifying, the 16 qualifiers and 16 seeds will meet in the last 32 over the best of 11 frames. That's first to six.

Each round will be over the best of 11 frames until the final, which will be played over two sessions comprizing up to 19 frames. That's first to 10.
