A 21-year-old woman has been killed after the car she was travelling in crashed following a police pursuit.

Staffordshire police said it had reported itself to the police watchdog, the Independent Office for Police Conduct, after the Seat Ibiza was involved in a collision with a Vauxhall Astra. The woman was a front seat passenger in the Seat.

The force said she had died at the scene in Hednesford in the West Midlands shortly after 11pm on Friday. Both cars were being driven by members of the public.

The Seat had been involved in a police pursuit shortly before the crash on the Rugeley Road, a single-carriageway A-road that runs to the east of Hednesford.

A 23-year-old man from the nearby Walsall area has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

The woman’s next-of-kin have been informed. Staffordshire police said the family were being supported by specially trained officers.

West Midlands ambulance service took two people to hospital. Their injuries are not believed to be life-threatening or life-changing.
