For anyone who’s dreaming of the good life – whether that entails keeping horses, growing your own fruit and veg or simply having room to do your own thing – Hett Mill Cottage could be just the ticket.

This detached four-bedroom home sits in a secluded spot close to the village of Hett, with 2.78 acres of gardens, paddock and grazing land all within a ring fence, giving it a decidedly rural feel despite the fact that it’s only around six-and-a-half miles from Durham City.

An added bonus is that the A1(M) is only around four miles away, too, making the property perfectly placed for those who commute to other parts of the region and beyond.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765525.jpg?type=mds-article-620)With a beck running along its boundary, a wealth of stylish accommodation and enough room to keep horses, Hett Mill Cottage is a perfect rural retreat *(Image: Supplied)*

The house has been tastefully decorated and well maintained by the current owners.

Its front door opens into an inviting entrance hall with a stone floor, while an everyday side entrance leads directly into the kitchen, which has a real country cottage feel thanks, in part, to an AGA, Belfast sink and country-style units in contrasting muted shades.

There are two living rooms, each with a multi-fuel stove to ensure cosy winter evenings, plus a further large reception room that could be used as either an office or dining room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765521.jpg?type=mds-article-620)With a beck running along its boundary, a wealth of stylish accommodation and enough room to keep horses, Hett Mill Cottage is a perfect rural retreat *(Image: Supplied)*

Upstairs is a modern family bathroom with a roll top bath and a rainfall shower, plus four well-proportioned double bedrooms, all finished in a bright neutral style.

These include the master bedroom whose large windows enjoy uninterrupted views over the garden, paddock and beck.

The house has oil-fired central heating.

A large lawned garden lies to the front of the property, with a granite gravel perimeter encircling the home.

To the rear is a large parking area overlooking the lawned garden and paddock, with the peaceful Tursdale Beck running along the boundary.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21765527.jpg?type=mds-article-620)With a beck running along its boundary, a wealth of stylish accommodation and enough room to keep horses, Hett Mill Cottage is a perfect rural retreat *(Image: Supplied)*

There’s also a detached double garage and a large general-purpose building with three block-built stables – or the makings of a sizeable workshop.

The land includes established grassland, pockets of woodland, and a newly planted orchard of heritage fruit trees.

There are also excellent bridleway connections for off-road hacking.

Additional land is available by separate negotiation.
