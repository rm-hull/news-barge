Drivers could face overnight closures on the A1 in North East during 2027, according to a public notice.

National Highways intends to make an order covering both directions of the A1 between junctions 65 and 80, Newcastle/Gateshead Western Bypass, so maintenance and improvement work can be carried out safely.

The works are expected to take place between 8pm and 6am during a period from January 2 to December 23, 2027.

Slip roads and link roads leading to and from the affected carriageway, as well as adjacent lay-bys, are also covered by the proposed restrictions.

The notice says each prohibition will vary in duration depending on the work and will apply only when indicated by traffic signs.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

During closures, traffic will be diverted via alternative A1 junctions, the A19 and local roads, with signs showing the diversions.

Emergency services, works and winter maintenance vehicles are exempt from the prohibitions.

The proposed order is due to come into force on January 1, 2027, and expire on December 23, 2027.

For enquiries, drivers can contact the National Highways customer contact centre on 0300 123 5000 or email [info@nationalhighways.co.uk.](mailto:info@nationalhighways.co.uk.)
