# Philippines takes the crown in first Eurovision Song Contest Asia

The Philippines has won Asia's first ever Eurovision Song Contest with 19-year-old singer Carmelle and her women's empowerment anthem, Sobra-Sobra.

She received 211 points in total to win what was a nail biter to the end.

Despite its success with fans, the future of Asia's Eurovision depends on a huge commitment from broadcasters and sponsors.

The BBC's South East Asia correspondent Jonathan Head reports from Bangkok.
