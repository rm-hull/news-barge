Naturally, this will show us a younger version of the character, who is undercover as an MI6 operative in Germany, going by the name of Martha Schmitt (and played by Ann Kelly).

There'll be six episodes to enjoy here as Martha/Carolyn finds her work compromised when she becomes embroiled in a love triangle.
