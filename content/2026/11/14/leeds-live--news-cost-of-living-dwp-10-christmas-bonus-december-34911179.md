# DWP £10 Christmas Bonus in December - who qualifies for payment

## The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has explained who will qualify for the annual £10 Christmas Bonus one-off payment

The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has clarified who will be eligible for a one-off additional payment next month.

Every year, the DWP issues a Christmas bonus to pensioners and individuals receiving particular benefits. Originally launched in 1972, the bonus has remained at £10 ever since.

To qualify for the payment, you must be present or "ordinarily resident" in the UK, Channel Islands, Isle of Man or Gibraltar during the "qualifying week", which occurs in the first week of December.

If you haven't claimed your State Pension and don't qualify for any of the other eligible benefits, you won't receive a Christmas Bonus. The Christmas Bonus is a one-off payment of £10, reports the Mirror.

It won't affect any other benefits you're receiving. All benefits, pensions and allowances are usually paid directly into an account, such as your bank account, and may show as 'DWP XB' on your bank statement.

You don't need to apply for the Christmas Bonus - it should be paid automatically. If you think you're entitled to it but haven't received it by January 1, contact the Jobcentre Plus office managing your payments or get in touch with the Pension Service.
