## Severe delays as police incident continues to partly block Yorkshire motorway

A police incident continues to blosk the M62 *(Image: Denise Bradley)*

A police incident is blocking part of the M62 motorway in Yorkshire.

The westbound of the M62 is closed from Junction 37, the A614 Howden turnoff, until junction 36, the A614 turnoff for Rawcliffe Road, Goole.




* **Delays warning on major North Yorkshire road for over a week**
* **York road closures announced for Acomb and Poppleton**
* **Restrictions expected to end on landslip-hit Yorkshire road**

The closure was reported at 1.14pm this afternoon and is still ongoing.

AA Roadwatch says the incident has caused severe delays of 19 minutes between Junction 39 (A63 North cave) and Junction 36, with vehicles travelling at an average of 5mph.

No details have been given, as yet, of the incident.
