## York Christmas Market - happy visitors unfazed by crowds on first Saturday

Milia Taylor (right) and her colleagues have been busy selling mulled wine and cider *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The centre of York was packed on Saturday for the first weekend of York Christmas Market.

The annual event, organised by Maker it York, features almost 70 traders in stalls and chalets.

It began on Wednesday November 11 in Parliament Street and nearby St Sampson’s Square and runs for over five weeks until Monday December 21.

Some years have seen complaints the event has become so popular, it can be too crowded. But whilst Parliament Street was pack on Saturday, especially close to M&S, all the visitors we spoke to, were happy and not complaining.

The Press tried speaking to hungry visitors enjoying The Irish Spice Bags in St Sampson’s Square but several told us they did not want to comment as they were enjoying their food.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718478.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Milia Taylor (right) and her colleagues have been busy selling mulled wine and cider *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Further away, however. Janey and Mary Schofield from Wakefield told us were liking their first York Christmas Market.

Janet said: “It’s very nice. We have got mulled wine and are going to have a march around.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718494.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Susan Calder says her Artifactually stall brings glitz and glitter. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

“It’s not too crowded, it’s okay, it’s not too bad. It (crowds) adds to the atmosphere.”

The pair had also just eaten.

Janet added: “We have had Crumbles, that was really lovely, apple and blackberry crumble.”

Paula Boag from Greenock, Scotland, was visiting with friends for a few days, on their first visit to the market.

She said: “I think the market is lovely, all the stalls, all the ornaments. It’s just the atmosphere and the hot toddy.”

Kara and Delta Moss from Chorley had also just arrived for their first York Christmas but agreed it was nice.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718450.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plenty of people are enjoying York's Christmas market *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Delta said: “There’s a lot of options. There’s a nice atmosphere. It’s not too busier. I thought it would be busy.”

Kara said they aimed to “spend a lot of money- as much as we can” on mainly food and little Christmas gifts.

Traders were also happy with the first few days, though they agreed it was early days.

Milia Taylor of Mulled Wine, Cider and Chocolate caravan said: “We are having a good Christmas. It’s been quite busy this morning. It’s still the first weekend, so it’s gearing up for the season.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718489.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jane Mower of Raisthorpe Distillery says 'so far so good' with trade this year *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Claire Harrison of Paper Starlights said: “It’s been good. I think it’s (trade) the same as last year. It’s the nice weather and the good vibes that have brought such trade.”

“We always have a stall here and employ local people.”

Jane Mower of the Raisthorpe Distillery, near Malton, said: “So far so good, but it’s early days. It’s been busy for us so far. I think last year was busier but today it was busy earlier.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718495.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Paula Boag has come down from Scotland with friends for their first York Christmas market. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The distiller has been attending the Christmas market for a dozen years.

Jane said: “I am looking forward to another bumper season,” adding her best sellers were the salted caramel chocolate brownie vodka and the sloe gin.

Susan Calder of Artifactually has been in business for 16 years. Her Surrey-based company which blows glass and makes baubles counts Harrods among its customers.

Susan, who is originally from Australia, has been attending the market for ten years.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718459.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Many people are enjoying fine weather as well as a wide range of options. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

She told the Press: “I had a customer here this morning who had come up from Lincoln especially to see us.

“I am looking forward to a good market. We are here until the 21st hoping to sell 15,000 baubles. We are one of the favourite stalls at the Christmas market. We bring the bling and glitter. We look like Christmas.”

York Christmas Market will run from Wednesday to Monday weekly, closing at 7pm except on its last day on Monday, December 21 when it shuts at 5pm.
