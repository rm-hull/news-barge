## Christmas magic event at Murton Park to support bereaved York children

Christmas at Murton Park - the York attraction is hosting a special event for bereaved children in York

*Murton Park will host a free Christmas event for bereaved children, reports MAXINE GORDON*

BEREAVED children and their families will be offered a morning of festive magic, fun and reflection at a special Christmas event near York.

The unique event, taking place at Murton Park on Saturday, November 28, has been created specifically for children who have lost a loved one and the adults supporting them.

Organised by celebrant Fiona Brown in partnership with Talking About Loss and Murton Park, the gathering aims to give families a chance to enjoy Christmas activities while gently remembering someone special who is no longer with them.

A total of 64 free children's places are available, with organisers hoping to reach families who may be finding the festive season particularly difficult.

Fiona, who works closely with bereaved families throughout the year, said Christmas can be one of the hardest times for children coping with loss.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21710894.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Christmas Bereavement Event at Murton Park will help bereaved children enjoy the festive season while remembering loved ones. Image: Your Ceremony

"As a local celebrant, I am delighted to once again be involved with this very special Christmas event for bereaved children and their families," she said.

"Working alongside Talking About Loss and Murton Park, we're creating a morning filled with Christmas magic, laughter and activities, with a gentle opportunity for children and families to remember someone special along the way."

Families will arrive from 9am and begin their experience with a magical train ride before walking through the North Pole to meet Santa and his elves.

Children will then be able to choose a free gift from the Toy Shop and take part in a range of festive activities, including creating reindeer food for Christmas Eve and making a wooden tealight decoration in memory of a loved one.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21710893.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bereaved York children will light tealight candles in a special event at Murton Park

The morning will also include the Mother Christmas Show, featuring songs, laughter and opportunities for children to join in the festive fun.

The event will finish with a candle-lighting ceremony around The Wish Tree, led by Fiona in her role as Mother Christmas.

She said: "Through my work with bereaved families, I know how difficult Christmas can be for children who are missing someone they love.

"That's why this event means so much to me. It's about creating a little space where children can enjoy being children, have some fun, make new memories and know that it's perfectly okay to remember someone special."

Unlike Murton Park's public festive attractions, the event is a private session created exclusively for bereaved children and their supporting adults.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/18816635.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christmas at Murton Park near York

Fiona hopes raising awareness of the event will help reach families who may not know such support is available.

She added: "Christmas can be a particularly difficult time when someone special is missing, and if this event can bring a little happiness, create some lovely memories and help children feel less alone, then it will have achieved exactly what we hoped for."

Places must be booked in advance. Children's tickets are free and adult tickets cost £12. To receive the private booking link, email [fiona.brown@yorkshirebarn.co.uk.](mailto:fiona.brown@yorkshirebarn.co.uk.)
