## Highly recommended pub in one of the friendliest villages in England

The Three Tuns in Heddon-on-the-Wall has been praised for its food and drink offerings *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A pub found in one of the friendliest villages in England has been praised for its “delicious food” and “good range of drinks”.

While the North East has plenty of welcoming towns and villages to visit, Heddon-on-the-Wall was recently named one of the friendliest in England by World Atlas.

If you want to visit the area to experience its friendliness, there are a few places you can stop off at, including the Three Tuns.

## What is the Three Tuns like?

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21714213.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The pub often has events on. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The Three Tuns offers its guests a “warm welcome” and is the ideal place to stop off to relax while exploring Hadrian’s Wall.

Throughout the year, the pub hosts a huge range of events from parties and live music events to quizzes.

The pub also offers food, including a Sunday roast at the weekend to help warm you up during the winter.

The Three Tuns often has a special cheesecake on offer for something sweet after your roast, with flavours ranging from Mars Bar-inspired cheesecakes to mint-flavoured ones.

There are also sandwiches and pies for those who need a lunchtime boost.

## What do reviews say about the Three Tuns in Heddon-on-the-Wall?

The Three Tuns has a rating of 4.5 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with 37 people leaving reviews after their visit.

One person said: “Stopped off at this pub by chance when walking the Hadrian’s Wall path.

“It was perfectly located along the path and was just the break we needed to keep us motivated.

“Staff were friendly, food was delicious, and there was a good range of drinks.

“All very affordable too.

“Would definitely recommend this as a resting point to anyone doing the walk.”

Another person said: “We stopped at Three Tuns at Heddon-on-the-Wall for lunch.

“We had a hearty meal of vegetable soup and a sandwich.

“There are several sandwiches to choose from.

“Phil, the publican, was very welcoming.

“The soup was delicious and I chose the BBQ Pork sandwich, which was scrumptious.

“The place was very clean, including the toilets, and I recommend this place for a great bite to eat.”

Other visitors have said they have never been disappointed by the food and enjoy the “generous portions”.
