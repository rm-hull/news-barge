The Friends Garden at Harrogate District Hospital has reopened with an automatic door for wheelchair users, a bug hotel and benches for anyone who would like a chat.

The garden at the heart of Harrogate District Hospital has reopened after a refurbishment. It now has an automatic door from the main corridor, so wheelchair users can get in on their own.

The Friends Garden has been open to patients, visitors and staff since 2001. Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust announced the reopening on 3 November. Tom Gordon, the MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough, cut the ribbon.

## Overview

**The Friends Garden at Harrogate District Hospital is open again to anyone visiting the hospital.** The refurbishment added step-free access through an automatic door, a bug hotel and “Happy to Chat” benches, alongside the Tree of Life sculpture honouring organ and tissue donors.

## What is new in the garden

The trust says the refurbishment was designed with accessibility at its heart. The new features include:

* an automatic entrance door from the hospital’s main corridor
* a bug hotel made by students from Horticap, with display boards about the wildlife it is meant to attract
* “Happy to Chat” benches: sitting on one signals that you would welcome a conversation

The benches come from an international scheme to tackle loneliness. The idea is that people who would never otherwise meet can start talking.

## The Tree of Life

The centrepiece is a metal sculpture, the Tree of Life, by the sculptor Steve Blaylock. It honours organ and tissue donors.

It was developed by the Friends of Harrogate Hospital, the trust’s Organ and Tissue Donation Committee and NHS Blood and Transplant.

## Who helped

Local companies gave their help to the project, and the Friends of Harrogate Hospital thanked them at the opening. More than 50 guests went to a celebration in the hospital’s Herriots Coffee Lounge after the ribbon was cut.

John Fox, chair of the Friends, said: “We greatly appreciate the help provided by so many companies and organisations.”

The Friends gave “Enterprise Friends” certificates to:

* Morgan Sindall Construction
* Moortown Group
* Marlborough Brickwork
* Metallic Fabrications
* G&H Building Services
* Steve Blaylock
* Horticap
* EnviroVent
* Harrogate Integrated Facilities

Mr Gordon said: “We are so lucky to have such a fantastic local hospital charity, and a local community who all chip in.”

Steven Fry, the project manager at Morgan Sindall Construction, said staff kept telling him they missed the garden while it was shut. “I didn’t realise how important the garden was to so many people until we closed it to start the works,” he said.

## What it means for you

If you are visiting someone at Harrogate District Hospital, or waiting for an appointment, the garden is a quiet place to sit outside. You no longer need help with a heavy door to reach it in a wheelchair.

If you would rather not sit alone, the “Happy to Chat” benches are there for that.
