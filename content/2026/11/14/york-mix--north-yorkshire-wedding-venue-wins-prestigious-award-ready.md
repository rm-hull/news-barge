A North Yorkshire wedding venue has won a prestigious regional award

Camblesforth Hall & Grange, near Selby, has been awarded Highly Commended for Best Historic Wedding Venue across Yorkshire and the North East at The Wedding Industry Awards 2027.

The awards recognise outstanding wedding businesses, with regional winners and highly commended finalists selected through a combination of votes and feedback from recently married couples and an independent panel of industry experts.

Yorkshire couple Naomi and Byron transformed the derelict grade 1 listed historic country house and its surrounding buildings into the thriving wedding venue it is today.

They took ownership of Camblesforth Hall & Grange in 2020, the hall and its buildings – whicg date back to around 1690 – had fallen into serious disrepair.

The ambitious restoration project has transformed the neglected buildings and grounds into a beautiful exclusive hire wedding venue, that today welcomes couples from across the country to celebrate their weddings in the lovingly restored barns, historic house and picturesque gardens.

“When we first took on Camblesforth Hall & Grange, we knew we had an enormous project ahead of us, but I don’t think either of us could have imagined quite how much our lives would change over the following six years,” said Naomi.

“We’ve poured so much of ourselves into restoring the Hall and creating the wedding venue we always envisioned, all whilst raising our family. We welcomed our little boy last October, so it’s certainly been a busy year juggling a baby, a seven-year-old and a wedding venue!

“It hasn’t always been easy, but we’re incredibly proud of what we’ve achieved together. To  
see a place that was once in such a sad state of disrepair now filled with love, laughter and  
celebrations is something that will never get old.”

The Wedding Industry Awards are among the UK’s most respected wedding industry accolades.

For Naomi and Byron, the fact that the award is based significantly on feedback from couples who have celebrated their weddings at Camblesforth Hall & Grange makes the achievement even more meaningful.

Naomi continued, “We are absolutely ecstatic to have been awarded Highly Commended. To be recognised alongside so many fantastic venues is an incredible honour.

“We couldn’t have achieved any of this without our wonderful team and the lovely couples  
who put their faith in us. This award really is for all of them.”
