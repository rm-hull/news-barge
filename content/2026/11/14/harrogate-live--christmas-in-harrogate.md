Harrogate's Christmas: Sleeping Beauty opens 27 November and the Cambridge Street market 4 December, plus grottos and ice skating. Updated November 2026.

**Christmas in Harrogate 2026 starts in earnest when Harrogate Theatre’s pantomime, Sleeping Beauty, opens to the public at 7pm on Friday 27 November, and the Christmas market on Cambridge Street follows on Friday 4 December.** Knaresborough’s Christmas Market and Little Bird Made’s market in Valley Gardens share the weekend of 5 and 6 December. Every Christmas page we keep for Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon is linked below.

## Overview

* **Friday 27 November:** Sleeping Beauty, first public show at Harrogate Theatre, 7pm, from £20. The same evening Knaresborough lights its tree at Bright Friday, about 6.30pm.
* **Friday 4 December:** Harrogate Christmas Market opens on Cambridge Street, the first of three weekends to 19 December. Free.
* **Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December:** Knaresborough Christmas Market, 10am to 5pm, fireworks 4.30pm on the Sunday; Little Bird Made in Valley Gardens, 10am to 3pm; the town centre Santa’s Grotto opens at Victoria Shopping Centre, £10 a child.
* **Thursday 17 December to Sunday 3 January:** Harrogate Ice Pavilion in Valley Gardens, closed on Christmas Day.

Last checked 14 November 2026.

## The three dates to know

**Sleeping Beauty at Harrogate Theatre, 27 November to 17 January.** The opening night and the final show, 4pm on Sunday 17 January, are the two cheapest performances, from £20; the rest start at £23. The relaxed performance is at 11am on Sunday 13 December (Harrogate Theatre). Cast, access dates and prices are on our Harrogate pantomime page.**Harrogate Christmas Market on Cambridge Street, from 4 December.** Friday 4 to Sunday 6, Friday 11 to Sunday 13, and Friday 18 to Saturday 19 December. Fridays 10am to 5pm, Saturdays 9am to 5pm, Sundays 10am to 5pm, free entry (Zoom Events).**Knaresborough Christmas Market, 5 and 6 December.** 10am to 5pm both days, free park and ride from GSPK on Manse Lane, and a ten-minute firework finale over the viaduct at 4.30pm on the Sunday (Knaresborough Christmas Market). Bring cash; the Banking Hub opens 11am to 4pm on the Sunday.

## Christmas markets in Harrogate and nearby

* Harrogate Christmas market: Cambridge Street on three weekends from 4 December, and Valley Gardens on 5 and 6 December.
* Knaresborough Christmas Market: 5 and 6 December, with the full timetable, trains and coach drop-off.
* Ripon Christmas market: Market Square, Friday 20 to Sunday 22 November.
* Great Yorkshire Christmas Fair: indoors at the Showground, 26 to 29 November, tickets from £5.

## Christmas lights in Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon

* Christmas lights switch-ons: Ripon’s lights go on at 5pm on Saturday 14 November; Harrogate’s BID-funded lights come on from late November.
* Knaresborough Bright Friday: lantern parade at 6pm on Friday 27 November, tree lit at about 6.30pm, and the road closures.
* Glow at RHS Harlow Carr: the illuminated trail on selected evenings from 26 November to 30 December, pre-booked only.

## Christmas events in Harrogate for families

* Santa’s grotto in Harrogate: Victoria Shopping Centre from 5 December at £10 a child, and Mother Shipton’s in Knaresborough from 28 November.
* Santa’s Polar Kingdom: the Great Yorkshire Showground, 11 to 13 and 16 to 23 December, entry from £22.99.
* Ice rinks near Harrogate: the Harrogate Ice Pavilion in Valley Gardens from 17 December, and six more within reach.
* Stockeld Park Christmas: the Christmas Adventure from 21 November to Christmas Eve, lights and skating to 3 January.
* Christmas at Fountains Abbey: 28 November to 3 January, with A Christmas Carol at Fountains Hall at weekends.
* Harrogate pantomime: Sleeping Beauty, 27 November to 17 January.

Free at Victoria Shopping Centre: curling rinks and a giant snow globe on 5, 6, 12, 13, 19 and 20 December, 10am to 4pm, just turn up. On Saturday 12 December eight ice sculptures go up across the town centre, with locations still to be announced (Harrogate BID).

## Carols and Christmas concerts

* Carol services and Christmas concerts: Ripon Cathedral’s Nine Lessons and Carols on 23 and 24 December at 5.30pm, the Royal Hall concerts on 12 and 19 December, and more.
