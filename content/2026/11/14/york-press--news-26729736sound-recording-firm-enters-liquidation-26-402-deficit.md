A sound recording and music publishing business has entered liquidation with debts topping £26,000.

Inner Sound Audio Ltd of Clifton Moor Business Village was incorporated in 2013 and entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on October 23 2026.

Business Guardians of Chorley were appointed liquidators.

Companies House lists Innersound’s directors as Joseph William Graves, Julian Paul Graves and Samuel Patrick Graves.

* **York construction firm liquidated with debts near £200,000**
* **AG Construction and Developments Ltd of York in liquidation**

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs has outlined the company’s finances.

It says Innersound Audio had assets of £3,600 available for distribution, which was cash in the bank.

It had no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

However, HMRC was owed £3,000 as a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next.

In addition, there were unsecured creditors- those paid last- who were owed £27,000 between them.

These consisted of trade and expense creditors, namely Escrick Park Estate, which was owed £13,000.

There were consumer creditors owed £10,000 and HMRC was owed a further £4,000.

This gave a shortfall of £26,400.

With the company having issued and called up capital of £2, this gave an overall shortfall or deficiency of £26,402.
