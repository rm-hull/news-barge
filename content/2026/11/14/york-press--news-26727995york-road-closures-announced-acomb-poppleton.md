Multiple temporary traffic restrictions and road closures are set to take place across York.

The City of York Council has announced multiple temporary traffic restrictions and road closures due to various works across the city.

Upon the recommendation of the Chief Constable of North Yorkshire Police, the council has made an Anti-Terrorism Temporary Traffic Regulation Order (ATTRO) for the York Christmas Market.

This will restrict vehicles from certain roads, which started on November 12, to December 21, to ensure the event runs safely.

Restrictions will be in place from 10.30am to 7.30pm, Wednesday to Monday, and from 10.30am to 5pm on Tuesdays.

All on-street parking at St Sampson’s Square will be suspended from 8am to 10.30am each day during this period.

The York (Public Right of Way 55/197/10 – Walmgate Stray) Traffic Order 2022 will continue to be in force, prohibiting pedestrians from this area until May 11, 2028, while unsafe bridge works are undertaken.

In Acomb, The Green will be closed for highway maintenance from November 16 to November 20.

An alternative route will be provided, and access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained.

In Poppleton, Common Croft Lane will be closed for telecoms works from November 16 to November 18.

An alternative route will be signed, while access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained.

The council has assured that these temporary orders will not prohibit works or emergency access, nor will they prevent access to premises, provided that access is not obstructed by ongoing works or safety concerns.

Traffic signs and barriers will indicate the extent of the prohibitions and restrictions.

Residents are advised to be aware of these changes and plan their travel accordingly.
