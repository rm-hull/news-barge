A burglary at a York property under renovation has prompted a police appeal for witnesses and information.

North Yorkshire Police said the incident took place on Thursday, November 12, between midnight and 4am in the Bootham area, near North Parade.

Entry was forced into the vacant premises and copper piping along with cable drums were stolen.

A police spokesperson said: "Did you or anyone who you live with, hear or see anything around the above time and date?

"Do you have any CCTV coverage capturing the burglary, or anything happening on the street before or after?

"Do you have a vehicle with a dash-cam parked in the area?"

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260222774.

Officers have also issued security advice to residents in the area.

The spokesperson said: "Ensure that all doors are locked overnight, and windows are locked or on a latch.

"Remove any valuables from sight and it is advisable to keep house/cars key, wallets/purses, phones/laptops/tablets in the room where you sleep.

"Consider fitting a security light to the side of your property."

North Yorkshire Police reassured the public that the area remains safe, but encouraged residents to remain vigilant and take simple steps to secure their properties.

The force also offers property marking and crime prevention advice through its neighbourhood policing teams.
