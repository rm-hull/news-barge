## Here’s why armed police have been out in force in York

Armed police at York Christmas Market North Yorkshire Police 2 *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)>*

Armed police will be on patrol as festive crowds gather in York and across North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police say high-visibility armed officers will be present at busy locations including the York Christmas Market, York Designer Outlet, and Winter Wonderland as part of efforts to keep the public safe during the festive season.

Chief Inspector Mark Procter of the Firearms Support Unit said: "The run up to Christmas is a special time of the year when towns and city centres become busier with residents and visitors enjoying festive events, markets, and shopping.

"Our armed patrols are one of a number of policing tactics used to help keep people safe, prevent crime, and provide reassurance in crowded places."

He said: "These officers are highly trained and are there to protect the public. Their presence should be seen as a positive measure to help ensure everyone can enjoy the festive period safely."

The public is being urged to stay alert and report anything suspicious.

Chief Insp Procter said: "By working together and looking out for one another, we can help keep North Yorkshire safe this Christmas."

North Yorkshire Police reminded residents that in an emergency, they should call 999, and for non-emergencies, dial 101 or contact the force online.
