# Andy Burnham urged to introduce 'scrappage' scheme for polluting cars

## Andy Burnham has been urged to bring in a national 'scrappage' scheme for the most polluting vehicles in the UK

Andy Burnham has been called upon to introduce a "scrappage" scheme targeting the most polluting vehicles across the UK. The vehicle scrappage scheme was a Labour government initiative launched in the 2009 Autumn Budget, designed to encourage British drivers to buy new, environmentally-friendly cars or vans while scrapping older, more polluting vehicles they'd owned for over 12 months, reports Birmingham Live.

The programme was extended in September 2009 and again in February 2010, before concluding at the end of March 2010.
