## 'Delicious' restaurant in Yorkshire market town readers would love to retire in

The Sandpiper Inn offers guests "absolutely delicious" food. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A restaurant with "absolutely delicious" food has been praised for its cheese soufflé and sea bass dishes.

Leyburn was recently mentioned by readers as being one of the places they would love to retire to.

It is easy to see why with spots like the Sandpiper Inn being found in the market town.

The restaurant offers guests a "unique dining experience in a traditional atmosphere".

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21696372.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant also has two rooms. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is the Sandpiper Inn like?

The Sandpiper Inn has been looked after by Jonathan and Janine for over 25 years and offers dishes made using local ingredients.

Over the years, the restaurant has been featured in the Michelin Guide and in the Gastro Pub Top 100.

The Sandpiper Inn features a bar with a cosy log fire and a seating area where you can enjoy a spot of lunch or dinner.

The restaurant has a range of dishes on offer from starters like ham hock terrine and a warm goat’s cheese salad to Sandpiper fishcakes.

The mains consist of things like pan-fried chicken on a pea and herb risotto, slow-cooked lamb, and steak and chips.

You can enjoy a few sides alongside your main such as seasonal vegetables and dauphinoise potatoes.

The desserts on offer include affogato, sticky toffee pudding, and an iced lemon parfait with English strawberries.

If you want to extend your stay in the area, the Sandpiper Inn has two rooms featuring 17th-century features such as exposed beams and old fireplaces.
