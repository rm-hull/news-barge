The Met Office has forecast a cold, dry, and sunny Saturday, with a cloudier, wetter, and windier Sunday for York.

On Saturday, November 14, the morning will be very cold, with temperatures around freezing, feeling like -1 to -2°C.

The skies will be mostly clear, with almost no chance of rain.

Winds will be light at about 3 mph, with occasional gusts up to 11 mph.

Despite the calm conditions, it will feel below freezing throughout the morning.

The afternoon will remain dry with sunshine, and temperatures will gradually increase, reaching around 7°C by 5pm.

Winds will pick up slightly to about 7 mph, with gusts reaching 18 mph.

The chance of rain remains below five per cent throughout the afternoon.

Saturday evening will stay clear and dry, with temperatures initially around 8°C, dropping to about 3°C by 11pm.

Wind speeds will generally remain between 7 and 8 mph, with gusts of around 17-18 mph.

On Sunday, November 15, the morning will start cold and cloudy, with temperatures around 2°C.

The chance of rain will increase as the morning progresses, with light rain expected to develop around 8am.

Temperatures will gradually rise to about 4°C, although it will feel closer to -1 to 2°C due to the breeze.

Winds will remain fairly steady at 6-7 mph, with gusts of 16-18 mph.

Sunday afternoon will be milder, with temperatures rising from about 4°C at midday to around 8°C by 3-5pm.

There may be a temporary improvement in conditions around lunchtime and early afternoon, but further light rain and showers are forecast from about 3pm onwards.

Winds will be relatively light at 5-6 mph, although gusts could reach 19 mph towards the end of the afternoon.

Sunday evening will begin unsettled, with light showers expected around 6-7pm.

Temperatures will fall from about 7°C at 6pm to around 4°C by 11pm.

Wind speeds will increase from about 7 mph at 6pm to 10 mph by 10-11pm, with gusts potentially reaching 30 mph by 9-10pm.
