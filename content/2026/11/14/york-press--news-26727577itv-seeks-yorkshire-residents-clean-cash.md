Josie Gibson is seeking Yorkshire residents for ITV's How To Clean Up For Cash.

The presenter is hunting for people to nominate friends, partners, or family members whose possessions are cluttering up their homes.

The show offers a surprise declutter, helping people reclaim their space.

It's not just about tidying up; the show is interested in homes filled with vintage, unusual, and collectable items.

These can include costume jewellery, old toys, musical instruments, cameras, war memorabilia, gold, silver, watches, and old technology.

Yorkshire could harbour its own hidden treasures, like Sheffield silverware, Yorkshire pottery, woollen mill memorabilia, and Leeds United mementos.

The show provides an opportunity to discover the value of these items, which could be more than expected.

With Josie and her team's help, the unsuspecting owners will have their possessions carefully packed up before receiving an offer for the lot.

They will then face the decision of whether to keep their belongings or sell for cash.

The team is keen to hear from people of all backgrounds, living in various types of homes across Yorkshire.

Applications are open until December 12, 2026.

Applicants must be aged 18 or over, and to nominate someone, visit ITV's Be on TV page.
