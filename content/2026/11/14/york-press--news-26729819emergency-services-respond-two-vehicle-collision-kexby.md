A major road south east of York suffered delays due to a two-vehicle collision this morning.

Firecrews from York responded to a two vehicle road traffic collision with all persons out on arrival and in the hands of paramedics.

The call-out to the A1079 at Kexby was at 9.58am.




* **Delays warning on major North Yorkshire road for over a week**
* **York road closures announced for Acomb and Poppleton**
* **Restrictions expected to end on landslip-hit Yorkshire road**

Crews removed the vehicles from the carriageway to enable road to be reopened prior to the arrival of police.

The incident left with police and crew power used only for scene safety.
