The Eastfield Neighbourhood Police Team are supporting the Yorwaste and NYC ‘Reuse Santa Collection’ scheme this year.

They’ve been reaching out to the local schools and organisations to assist in sourcing unwanted and preloved toys and books to ensure they are put to good use and bring joy and happiness to children that may otherwise go without.

They say if anyone has items that fit this description, then please could they donate them to their local recycle centre or drop them off at the locations specified in the poster.
