## York Christmas Market - major new products launched to celebrate its start

Jack and Stephen with the two new gins *(Image: Matthew Kitchen Photography)i>*

York Gin is launching not one but two new festive gins to celebrate the start of York Christmas Market.

York Gin Sugar Plum is inspired by the fairy in the Christmassy Nutcracker Suite.

Made with Yorkshire plums and festive spices including ginger, fig and allspice, York Gin bosses say it is a ‘real taste of Christmas’.

And, for the first time, the company has launched a sloe gin - York Sloe Gin - a sweeter liqueur than the normal range.

* **York Gin notes a 2025 full of spirit and confidence in 2026**

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718057.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Izzy of York Gin with the two new gins *(Image: Matthew Kitchen Photography)*

It is made in collaboration with sloe gin experts SloeMotion distillery based near the Castle Howard Estate - with a suitably festive-looking label.

Natalie Hall, York Gin director said: “York is such a Christmassy city, with our famous market, ancient streets and beautiful independent shops. We always come near the top of national surveys for the most festive cities.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718056.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jack and Stephen with the two new gins *(Image: Matthew Kitchen Photography)*

“So this year we’ve pushed the boat out to create two limited-edition Christmas drinks that we are very proud of and know our customers will adore.”

York Gin Sugar Plum was developed in the company’s boutique York Distillery which also houses the city’s only Gin School, as well as gin-tasting experiences and cocktail-making parties.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718051.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Puss N Mew with the two gins *(Image: Matthew Kitchen Photography)*

The second in York Gin’s ‘Distiller’s Cut’ series, only around 400 bottles have been made. It makes a ‘fabulously festive’ G&T.

York Sloe Gin meanwhile is a traditional gin liqueur - a dark red colour and a Christmas favourite for many. The company says it can be enjoyed with tonic, in a festive cocktail with Champagne or Prosecco or neat.

Natalie added: “We’re really looking forward to a great Christmas alongside our brilliant fellow local businesses. York really is the place to enjoy the festive season with such a wonderful atmosphere, amazing shops - and the best places to eat, drink and be merry!”
