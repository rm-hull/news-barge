A series of road closures and diversions have been announced across Leeds.

Leeds City Council has announced a series of temporary road closures and diversions across the city starting in November.

These closures are due to various works, including gas mains replacement, water service replacement, new connection works, and the installation of new ducts and cables.

In Armley, Armley Grange View will be closed 24 hours a day from November 17 to November 24 for new connection works.

There will be no available diversion, but essential access to premises and for emergency vehicles will be maintained.

In Chapel Allerton, Gledhow Park Avenue will be closed from November 20 to November 27 for Yorkshire Water service replacement works.

A diversion will be in place via Roxholme Grove, Harehills Lane, King George Avenue, and Gledhow Park Drive.

Moor Lane in Harewood will be closed from November 24 for the installation of new connection ducts and cables.

A diversionary route will be available via Main Street, Lumby Lane, and Harewood Avenue.

Fearnville Terrace will be closed from November 16 for the installation of a new water main.

The closures will be in place fully between 8am to 5pm, with one-way northbound closures from 5pm to 8am.

Holbeck Lane will have a temporary parking bay suspension from November 22 for one day between 9.30am and 3.30pm.

Stairfoot Lane in Alwoodley will be closed from November 23 to December 4 for the installation and supply of a new water meter.

A diversionary route will be available via King Lane, Eccup Lane, Church Lane, and Back Church Lane.

Eaton Hill in Cookridge will be closed from November 25 to December 1 for new connection works.

No diversionary route will be available as it is a dead-end street.

Abbey Road in Kirkstall will have a right turn ban from Abbey Walk onto Abbey Road from November 16 to November 21 between 8pm and 6am for footway works.

A diversionary route will be available via Abbey Road, Savins Mill Lane and Bridge Road, returning to Abbey Road.

Luxor View in Harehills will be closed from November 16 to November 27 for lead renewal works.

A diversionary route will be available via Harehills Lane, Luxor Avenue, and Karnac Road.

Northgates in Wetherby will be closed from November 24 to November 30 for the excavation and installation of ducts and cables.

No diversionary route will be available.

Parking bays on Cavendish Street and Abbey Street will be temporarily closed from November 23, 2026 to August 1, 2027 for cladding replacement works.

Moorland Crescent, Fieldhouse Close, and Fieldhouse Drive will have temporary closures for gas main replacement works as follows:

* Moorland Crescent at its junction with Scott Hall Road: November 23 to December 4, 2026
* Fieldhouse Close at its junction with Scott Hall Road: November 30 to December 11, 2026
* Fieldhouse Drive at its junction with Scott Hall Road: January 4 to January 15, 2027. Diversionary routes will be available.

Taxi bays on York Street will be temporarily suspended from November 23 to November 26.

They will be open outside of these hours.

All works are expected to be completed by their respective deadlines, but the orders will remain in force for a maximum period of 18 months or until the works are completed, if earlier.

Essential access to premises and for emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout all closures.

Communications regarding these notices should be addressed to [streetworks.coordin@leeds.gov.uk.](mailto:streetworks.coordin@leeds.gov.uk.)
