Firefighters from Easingwold and Acomb responded to a fire involving a biomass boiler last night (Friday).

Crews used a hose reel jet to extinguish the fire and small tools to clear the feed pipes of woodchip debris, following the 7.52pm call-out.

They also used a thermal imaging camera to check for hotspots.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue said: "The cause of the fire was due to a defective mechanical belt which overheated and caused the woodchip to ignite."

Earlier, at 6.52pm, a crew from Knaresborough responded to a chimney fire in North Deighton.

They used small tools to dislodge a blockage in the flue and allowed the fire to burn out. They advised the occupant not to use the fire until the chimney had been swept.
