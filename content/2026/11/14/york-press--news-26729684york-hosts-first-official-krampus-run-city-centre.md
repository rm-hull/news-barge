## Mythical horned creatures wow crowds in York city centre

Another Krampus thrills the crowds *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Visitors to York have been having a monster of a day wanting their photos taken with horned mythical creatures of Alpine folklore.

York’s first-ever official Krampus Run took place today (Saturday), bringing the dark legend of Krampus to life in the city centre.

It follows a visit the legendary figures made to the city last year and in 2024.

The Krampus, a fearsome, horned being from Alpine myth, is said to punish naughty children during the festive season, in contrast to the gift-giving St Nicholas.

![Jeanette Mann of Malton poses with here 'scary' friend](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718633.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jeanette Mann of Malton poses with her 'scary' friend *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

The procession began at the Black Swan in Peasholme Green at 10.30am and followed a route through the city centre.

There were pauses at Villians Rum at 11am and Valhalla at noon, before entering Minster Yard and returning to the starting point by 3pm.

The Krampus Run featured participants dressed in elaborate, hand-crafted costumes including carved wooden masks, imposing horns, and heavy furs.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718635.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Saint Nicholas arrives to see his Krampus *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

It follows on from similar events in Whitby, which will return from Friday December 4 until Sunday December 6, bringing three days of folklore, music and festive mischief to the Yorkshire coast.

The Press caught up with the Krampus by Valhalla in the centre of York where people of all ages were keen to have their photos taken with the creatures.

Among them, Elisabeth Bradbury of Huddersfield said: “They were absolutely brilliant. There’s just the right amount of scariness and fun. It really does add to the ambience of York.

“I came here especially to see them after seeing them in Whitby. I am having a loverly day.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718650.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another Krampus has another fan. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Jeanette Mann of Malton was with her friend Janet Reid, also from Malton, had come to the city for the Christmas Market.

Jeanette said: “I think they are amazing. They are unique. I like them.”

Jane agreed: “They are amazing. It’s the outfits and everything, brilliant!”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718655.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Children also loved the Krampus *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Kate Ashworth from Wetherby said: “They are great. They are also terrifying.”

Olivia and Joan Smith from Manchester said they have come to York just to see the Krampus.

The pair said: “It’s just so funny to see them. They Are something different.”

A couple of friends from York, who did not give their names, said they had wanted to see them in York after seeing them in Whitby.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718634.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another Krampus thrills the crowds *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

They added: “They were very friendly and had great costumes.”

This year’s Whitby Krampus Run is known as Zwölf, as it is the 12th such annual Krampus Run.

It includes an opening event at Whitby Brewery, Saturday's Krampus parade and Thee Krampus Ball at The Royal Hotel.

The Whitby Krampus Run also incorporates local legends, including the Whitby Wyrm, a dragon-like creature associated with the headland near Whitby Abbey.
