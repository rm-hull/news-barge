It was the first York Krampus Run today. It is the dark magic of Alpine folklore, bringing an ancient European tradition straight into the heart of Yorkshire. Krampus—the legendary horn-headed creature of Alpine myth—comes looking for the naughty.

Armed with rusted chains, heavy clanging bells, and birch switches, a procession of Krampusse rattled through York’s medieval streets to warn mischief-makers to mend their ways before the holidays. It was the first York Krampus Run today.

Big crowds followed them through the city.
