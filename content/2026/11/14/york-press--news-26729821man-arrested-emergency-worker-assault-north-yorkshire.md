A man has been arrested after an aggravated burglary in North Yorkshire.

Emergency services were called to an address on Farndale Gardens in Lingdale on Friday (November 13) after a man suffered injuries to his hands in an incident.

The man then attended a shop on Boosbeck High Street just after 8pm, where ambulance crews were called to the scene.

Cleveland Police has confirmed that a man has been arrested on suspicion of aggravated burglary and assault on an emergency worker.

He remains in police custody and an investigation is now ongoing.

A force spokesperson said: “Following an incident at an address on Farndale Gardens in Lingdale, a man suffered injuries to his hands.

“He then attended a shop on Boosbeck High St around 8:10pm, where ambulance service colleagues were called.

"A man aged in his fifties was arrested on suspicion of aggravated burglary and assault on an emergency worker.

"He remains in police custody at this time."

Enquiries are continuing.
