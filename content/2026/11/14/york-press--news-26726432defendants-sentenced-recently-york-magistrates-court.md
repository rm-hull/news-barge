A violent shoplifter and a man who attacked a woman were among defendants sentenced recently at York Magistrates Court.  
Gareth Gowlett, 45, of Burdyke Avenue, Clifton, pleaded guilty to failure to comply with a 12-month community order and was jailed for 24 weeks.  
The order had been imposed after he pleaded guilty to burgling Vapes and Phones in Gillygate, York city centre, possessing cocaine, and stealing Lego worth £64.99 from B&M stores.  
Ryan Turner, 20, of Falsgrave Crescent, Clifton, pleaded guilty to breaching a restraining order.  
He was given a two-year community order with a rehabilitative programme, supervision and 180 hours’ unpaid work and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Tom Keedwell, 33, of St Stephens Road, Acomb, pleaded guilty to driving whilst disqualified in York.  
He was fined £769, ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £308 statutory surcharge and given six penalty points.

Jody Miller, 43, of Westfield Place, Acomb, pleaded guilty to obstructing police.  
He was given a 12-month community order with supervision and six months’ alcohol treatment.  
He was ordered to pay the police officer £50 compensation.  
Adam Humpherson, 40, of no fixed address, denied theft of £10 of beer from Sainsbury’s and failure to attend court but was convicted of both at trial.  
He pleaded guilty to assaulting a woman in Tesco’s on Low Ousegate and two failures to attend court.  
He was jailed for 24 weeks.

Adam Clarkson, 30, pleaded guilty to assaulting a woman twice.  
He was jailed for 12 weeks and made subject to a three-month restraining order.

Shaun Robinson, 30, of no fixed address, pleaded guilty to stealing a bicycle in Whitby and failure to attend court.  
He was given a 12-month community order with six months’ alcohol rehabilitative treatment and probation supervision and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.  
Joshua Price, 37, of Scalby Gate, Falsgrave Road, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to possessing a metal chair leg as an offensive weapon in public, carrying a knife in public, harassment of a woman, criminal damage, possessing cannabis, disorderly behaviour, and failure to attend court.  
He was given a 24-week prison sentence suspended for 18 months with probation supervision.  
He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £154 statutory surcharge.

Billy Johnson, 19, of Friargate, Scarborough, denied a charge of carrying a knife in public in Scarborough and was convicted at trial.  
He was jailed for 24 weeks as part of a 36-week sentence including other offences.
