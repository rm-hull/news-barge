## Have a look inside this ‘attractive’ period home on the market in York

The three-bedroom Victorian mid terrace for sale in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, York, for £425,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

An "attractive" period home has hit the market in York.

The three-bedroom Victorian mid terrace in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, is for sale for £425,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it has been "thoughtfully modernised while retaining its traditional features", resulting in the home combining "period charm, practical living space and a highly desirable York location".

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21695171.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the three-bedroom Victorian mid terrace for sale in Nunmill Street, off Scarcroft Road, York, for £425,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside, the property opens into a tiled entrance hallway leading through to the main living room.

The living room has a bay window, original wood floorboards, a cast iron fireplace, and a decorative crown moulding and ceiling rose.

Next door is the dining room with the kitchen located at the rear.

The kitchen opens into a private courtyard garden – a "pleasant setting for outdoor dining and entertaining", according to Ashtons.

Upstairs, the main bedroom has original wood flooring and a feature fireplace.

The second bedroom includes a cast iron fireplace, painted wooden floorboards and fitted wardrobes.

Also on the first floor is the home's main bathroom with a walk-in shower and a freestanding bath.

On the top floor is the converted attic room which Ashtons said provides "useful additional space for a study, hobbies or occasional guests".
