## York protesters join national campaign to stop Rosebank oil field

Protestors with their Stop Rosebank banners *(Image: Provided)*

**PROTESTERS have gathered in York as part of a national campaign against a controversial new oil development.**

Campaigners took to the streets of York city centre on Friday (November 13) as part of a nationwide day of action to stop the development of Rosebank Oil Field.

Demonstrators targeted MP offices from Aberdeen to Exeter, calling on politicians to oppose the North Sea oil development which they say, if approved, would produce over 250 million tonnes of CO2e - equivalent to around 70% of UK annual emissions

In York, the protest was held outside West Offices with Rachael Maskell, MP for York Central, attending the event to show her support.

Rachael said: "I joined the protest today to make the case that Rosebank must not go ahead.

“I cannot support further North Sea extraction when the consequences are established as to the harm that this will cause with a rise in global temperature, fuelling the climate crisis, just short of 30,000 heat-related deaths by the end of the century, and will not create the jobs a more sustainable energy solution would.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21716314.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rachael with a Stop Rosebank banner *(Image: Provided)*

“I have voiced my deep concerns in Parliament this week in a debate on Rosebank, and told the Minister that Government must not proceed with drilling.

“Above all, Rosebank will have to import oil to the global market, so the public will not gain from oil extraction; rather, the multinational oil and gas companies will make significant profits. I will continue to campaign with colleagues on this."

She was joined by other environmental activists, who held up signs calling for people to ‘just say no’ to the development and others stating that it should be stopped.

Rosie Latchford, a York campaigner for Stop Rosebank said: "This past summer, the UK suffered record-breaking heatwaves and wildfires - all fuelled by fossil fuels. Now, as winter sets in, millions are worrying about staying warm this winter due to soaring oil and gas prices.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21716316.jpg?type=mds-article-620)York Green Party at the protest *(Image: Provided)*

“Rosebank just makes all of this worse. It will make no meaningful difference to UK energy security, will do nothing to lower household energy bills, and the vast climate pollution it generates will exacerbate the extreme weather we are already seeing.

"All it will do is give even more money to Big Oil, who’ve already made billions at our expense.

“Andy Burnham promised to stand with ordinary people, not corporate profiteers. The only way to lower bills and secure a safe climate for generations to come is to break free from the fossil fuel rollercoaster. Labour must step up and stop Rosebank."
