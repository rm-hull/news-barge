A charity golf day is returning once again to raise funds for good causes.

Organised by York Vikings Rotary Club, the event will take place on Thursday, May 7, 2027, at York Golf Club.

Last year’s tournament raised £10,000 for charity, with 44 teams and 176 golfers taking part.

Andrew Lindsay, organiser of the event, said: "We would love to hear from anyone who would like to work together with us to make the York Vikings Rotary Charity Golf Day an even greater success, especially for the good causes we support."

The Rotary club is now seeking sponsors, as long-term sponsor SAY has relocated to the Midlands.

Mr Lindsay said this change offers "a superb opportunity for another local business to put themselves in the frame and take over the main sponsor role".

He also encouraged businesses and teams to get in touch soon to secure their place.
