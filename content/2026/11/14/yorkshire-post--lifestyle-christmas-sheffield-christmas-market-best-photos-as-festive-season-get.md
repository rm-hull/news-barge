The city’s Christmas market once again has a whole host of stalls, traders and attractions for the whole family to enjoy.

As well as the new and returning traders, the market this year also boasts a brand new ice rink, snow slide and Alpine bar.

The market is spread across Fargate, Pinstone Street, Barker’s Pool and The Moor with various food offerings and stalls.

The market is open from 10am to 6pm every day until Christmas Eve, while the four bars are open from 10am to 10pm, with the main bar situated in The Peace Gardens.
