## Giant nature murals by York artist Ben Walgate unveiled at top attraction

One of the new murals at York's NRM - photo by Nicky Clark from Press Camera Club

*York's National Railway Museum unveils giant nature murals, reports MAXINE GORDON*

Visitors to York's National Railway Museum can enjoy a new display of murals created by city artist Ben Walgate.

Among the first people to see the new mural 'trail' which features giant-sized images of a badger and fox set in nature with a train crossing a viaduct was Nicky Clark from the Press Camera Club.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21714089.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the new murals at York's NRM - photo by Nicky Clark from Press Camera Club

Nicky shared some of her photos taken during her visit on the Press Camera Club's Facebook site.

She posted: "The new nature mural at the National Railway Museum in York is finally finished, and I just had to go see this amazing artwork in person!

"A huge thank you to Eric and Jack, who kindly took me for a ride on the Miniature Railway today and made it possible for me to enjoy and share this experience with you all.

If you are in York, it is definitely worth a visit!"

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21714091.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the new murals at York's NRM - photo by Nicky Clark from Press Camera Club

Artist Ben Walgate posted more photos and a video of him creating the works on his Facebook and Instagram pages.

And he posted a poignant message about why the work was extra special.

"My nature mural at the National Railway Museum in York is finally finished!

"There’s a bit of irony in this one. Trains and graffiti have a long history together, so being invited into the Railway Museum as a graffiti artist to paint something for train lovers feels pretty special.

"This wasn’t an easy commission to get through. During the time I was painting it, I lost and said goodbye to my best mate, Rew.

"Getting back to the wall and finding my focus was hard, but I’m proud I’ve seen it through. A fellow graffiti artist and a huge part of my life, he was in my thoughts throughout.

"A massive thank you to the Railway Museum for having me back for a second time and trusting me with another piece. It means a lot.

"This one will always mean a little more."
