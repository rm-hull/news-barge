Temporary traffic lights are causing delays on the A59 west of the city in both directions due to telecoms work.

The disruption is between the A1(M) Junction 47 (Allerton Park) and Hopperton Street.

The works began on November 11 and is expected to end at 6pm on Friday November 20.
