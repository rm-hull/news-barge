Firefighters from Harrogate were reported to a picnic table, which was well alight in a children’s play park.

At 21:15, they used a hose reel jet to extinguish the fire and dampen down the area in Starbeck. They also used a triple extension ladder and a thermal imaging camera to ensure no fire had spread to the guttering of a nearby commercial property due to flying embers from the picnic table.

The cause of the fire was believed deliberate and details were passed to Police.
