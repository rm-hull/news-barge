*(Image: Pickering Running Club)*

With two big local events to choose from, a large number of Pickering runners went charging through the countryside, several racing in club colours for the first time.

On Sunday, the annual and much-loved six-mile Dalby Dash took place in Dalby Forest to raise funds for Help for Heroes.

After two minutes' silence, the runners vied for king and queen of the mountain as they slogged up the two-mile climb of House Dale.

Then came the fun part, careering back down Heck Dale and on to the finish.

Richard Robinson had a cracking run, finishing in 38 minutes 50 seconds, placing him first M50, 13th overall and just missing out on first local by 10 seconds.

Paul Simpson was over two minutes faster than last year and third local with his time of 40.59, followed by Tim Foxton in 41.37.

Ruth Kaftirani was third F40 and 2nd local female with her time of 39.02, and Paul Robinson's time of 50.01 earned him silver in the M70 category.

Six seconds later, Suzy Hodgson flew across the line in 51.07, followed by Jo Paveley in 57.42, earning her the third F65 spot, and Lisa Adams in 65.01.

The second race in the East Yorkshire Cross Country League also took place on Sunday.

The 6.5 mile course was based in the rolling countryside near Welton.

Conditions were good for running, although the views over the Humber were obscured by mist.

First back for Pickering was the consistently speedy Simon Jones in 46 minutes 4 seconds, placing him first M55 and 39th overall.

Kelly Richmond ran an excellent race, finishing in 46.12, placing her third in the female category.

Hot on her heels was Mike Richmond in 46.26.

Kirsty Brown achieved a course personal best and 10th female with her time of 50.50.

Next came Dave Owuor in 53.40, then Paul Smith in 55.34, 2nd M65, and Karen Farmer-Scott, third F60, in 58.01.

They were followed by Rich Johnson in 58.22, Jo Towler in 58.25, Serena Partridge in 62.05, and Jenny Richards, who was third F65 with her time of 65.29.

Jenny was closely followed by Lynsey Hatfield in 65.32, Lydia Adrian in 67.04, Nina Overtoom in 69.00, Rebecca Bruce in 73.37, Mike Punchard in 78.20 and Dean Wise in 84.16.

Nicola Wise ran with Donna Reuth, who crossed the finish line together in 84.22.
