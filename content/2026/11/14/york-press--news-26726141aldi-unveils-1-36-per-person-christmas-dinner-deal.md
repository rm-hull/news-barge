Aldi has announced a budget-friendly Christmas dinner offer at a cost of £1.36 per person.

The full festive feast, available from Aldi's UK stores, including York, includes essentials such as a British Whole Fresh Turkey, priced at £2.95 per kg, and vegetables reduced to 8p each as part of Aldi’s Festive Super Six promotion.

Aldi aims to feed a family of eight for £10.85, offering items such as Yorkshire puddings, sage and onion stuffing mix, and gravy granules.

Aldi’s British Small Whole Fresh Turkey (3kg, £8.85, £2.95 per kg) is billed as the centrepiece of the Christmas meal.

Other items in the offer include Nature’s Pick White Potatoes (2kg), Brussels Sprouts (500g), Carrots (1kg), Parsnips (500g), Swede, and Red or White Cabbages, all priced at 8p each.

Aldi has confirmed that its usual Super Six promotions will see the price reduction funded by the retailer, ensuring British growers and suppliers won’t be affected.

The Festive Super Six and Value Christmas Dinner range will be available in stores from December 19 until December 24.
