## Fire started deliberately at Yorkshire play park

A fire has been started deliberately at a play park in Starbeck *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

A picnic table was believed to have been set on fire in a North Yorkshire children's play park.

The incident was reported in Starbeck, Harrogate, at 9.15pm and prompted multiple calls to firefighters, who arrived from Harrogate to find the table "well alight."




* **Firefighters called to fan fire in Elvington, York**
* **Police appeal after burglary at Bootham vacant building**
* **Severe traffic delays on A64 and A1237 York Ring Road**
* **York road closures announced for Acomb and Poppleton**

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "They used a hose reel jet to extinguish the fire and dampen down the area.

"They also used a triple extension ladder and a thermal imaging camera to ensure no fire had spread to the guttering of a nearby commercial property due to flying embers from the picnic table."
