## New art exhibition captures beauty of wildlife

Robert with a Giv Geese painting in his gallery *(Image: Robert Fuller)*

A new art exhibition is capturing the beauty of British wildlife in winter.

Hosted by Yorkshire wildlife artist and filmmaker Robert E Fuller, the display opens at his gallery in Thixendale, North Yorkshire, on November 14.

It features paintings of foxes diving into snowdrifts, barn owls hunting over frozen fields, and hares sheltering in blizzards, alongside the films that inspired the artwork.

Visitors can also watch live wildlife cameras showing owls preparing their nests for spring.

Robert said: "I wanted to capture how beautiful it is when the world freezes over and the landscape turns white.

"My films show how even in the most extreme conditions, animals just get on with it.

"They even seem to have the energy to be quite playful – I’ve watched hares boxing in blizzards."

Known for his appearances on BBC1’s The One Show, Mr Fuller uses hidden cameras around his Thixendale garden to observe wildlife.

Among the featured films is a documentary about swan cygnets breaking through ice on an East Yorkshire lake.

Other footage shows garden birds feeding on frosted berries.

Robert also travels internationally to film and paint wildlife in their natural habitats.

The exhibition includes scenes of penguins in the Antarctic, captured during one of his recent expeditions.

The exhibition runs until January 10, and is open from 9.30am to 4.30pm on weekdays and from 10.30am to 4.30pm on weekends.
