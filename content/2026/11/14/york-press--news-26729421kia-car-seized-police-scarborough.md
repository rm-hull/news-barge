A CAR has been seized after police stopped a vehicle in North Yorkshire.

The county's police say the incident took place in Falsgrave Road in Scarborough on Friday (November 13) when a Kia was stopped.

Police said the driver was found to be "driving otherwise than in accordance with a driving licence" and the vehicle was subsequently seized.

The driver was reported for the offences.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "This might be a good reminder to check your driving licence photocard.

"Generally, you need to renew your photocard every 10 years up to the age of 70.

"Ensure you have the correct details including your address."

People with information can contact Crimestoppers.
