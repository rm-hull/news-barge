A York charity is raising funds through an upcoming Advent raffle.

Kyra, which aids women in challenging situations, including domestic abuse and poor mental health, is running the raffle with prizes donated by local businesses.

All proceeds go towards supporting Kyra's initiatives and work.

The charity is said to have been "blown away" by the local business community's support, now boasting 35 prizes.

The raffle aims to ensure women and children can access vital support, community activities and opportunities throughout the year ahead.

Kyra hopes to reach as many people as possible before the draw.

For more information about the raffle, visit: www.goodhub.com/go/kyraadventraffle
