North Yorkshire Police say high-visibility armed officers will be present at busy locations including the York Christmas Market, York Designer Outlet, and Winter Wonderland during the festive season. It is part of efforts to keep the public safe.

Chief Inspector Mark Procter of the Firearms Support Unit said: “The run up to Christmas is a special time of the year when towns and city centres become busier with residents and visitors enjoying festive events, markets, and shopping.

“Our armed patrols are one of a number of policing tactics used to help keep people safe, prevent crime, and provide reassurance in crowded places.”

He said: “These officers are highly trained and are there to protect the public. Their presence should be seen as a positive measure to help ensure everyone can enjoy the festive period safely.”

The public is being urged to stay alert and report anything suspicious.
