![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Times reads: Brand called me 'the child', I was only 16](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/2e7b/live/c0fc9780-e079-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

The Sunday Times leads on its interview with a woman who alleges that she was raped by Russell Brand when she was 16. Mia Latter was one of six women who made allegations against Brand in 2023 as part of a Times, Sunday Times and Channel 4's Dispatches investigation. She was known under the pseudonym 'Alice'. She has now waived her right to anonymity and is campaigning for a staggered age of consent. Brand denied the allegations at the time. None of the women in the 2023 media investigation were complainants in his recent criminal trial, which culminated in his sentencing on Monday to 10 and a half years for one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: Brand move to soft jail](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/52c6/live/1175aa10-e077-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Sun on Sunday meanwhile reports that Brand has been moved to a "cushy jail for his own safety". It is "flabbergasting" that the disgraced comedian has been transferred to the "soft" HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire, an ex-governor tells the paper. The Ministry of Justice told the paper it does not comment on individual prisoners. The Sun's front page is also one of several to splash on remarks made by Wayne Rooney ahead of his entry to the I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! jungle. "I lost three stone after quitting booze," is the Sun's summary, "and Coleen loves it".

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Mirror reads: Wayne: Coleen, booze and me](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/c11f/live/54427e90-e077-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

"Footie legend: Marriage in a much better place after going teetotal," is the Sunday Mirror's take. The former footballing star tells the paper that quitting alcohol six months ago has helped him mentally as well as physically.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: Wayne: I'm a roo man](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/16c9/live/ff870430-e075-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

Never shy of a pun, the Daily Star's headline today reads: "I'm a roo man." The paper reports the ex-England footballer is a favourite to win I'm a Celebrity... once he lands in Australia to film the long-running competition programme.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Express reads: Shock analysis reveals Burnham plan to rejoin EU would leave every household in Britain... £6,500 poorer](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/51bf/live/b72a4700-e076-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

Without missing out on Rooney's forthcoming "jungle battle", the Sunday Express gives the rest of its front page to what it calls "Andy Burnham's plot to rejoin the EU". Ahead of a UK-EU summit next week, the paper reports on "shock analysis" which it says models the possible impact of rejoining the bloc on UK households within 10 years. The study has not yet been published. Burnham has previously said he believes a "win-win" deal can be agreed with the EU, which could be "more like the Brexit that people thought they were voting for" in 2016.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Telegraph reads: Taxpayers funding pro-EU university courses](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/177d/live/3b02a610-e079-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

A range of different stories lead the Sunday Telegraph. Its top story is the Conservatives accusing Labour of pushing "pro-EU propaganda" into universities by rejoining the Erasmus Plus programme. The paper reports the move will mean universities can apply for funding to conduct courses which "promote European citizenship". A Labour spokesman said: "Teaching students about EU institutions and how they work is not the same as promoting a pro-EU viewpoint." In other news, it covers Wales beating the All Blacks for the first time in 73 years, and the revelation that Friday's US airspace shutdown was caused by "an upgrade to remove copper wire".

![The headline on the front page of the Mail on Sunday reads: Robots really are taking over](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/58f1/live/bf16f980-e077-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

The Mail on Sunday focuses on other news. It reports that a "No 10 speechwriter was caught using artificial intelligence to help craft a keynote address". According to the Mail, the individual was seen using ChatGPT by a fellow passenger.
