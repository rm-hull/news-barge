## Early bird tickets go on sale for major York charity beer festival

The Great Hall Keg Bar *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Early bird tickets are now on sale for the fifth Merchant Adventurers' Charity Beer Festival, priced at just £10.

The event will return to Merchant Adventurers’ Hall on July 2 and 3, 2027, offering a celebration of beer, live music, and community spirit in the heart of the city.

Lee Grabham, festival founder and co-founder of Brew York, said: "We’re proud that this event has become a firm favourite for locals and visitors wanting to enjoy our stunning Hall while sampling a cracking selection of beers from the best breweries in Yorkshire and beyond.

"We urge attendees to make the most of the early bird offer to secure a ticket for their favourite session."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718152.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Enjoying the garden at the Merchant Adventurer's Charity Beer Festival *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The festival will feature a 30-foot cask bar and 40 keg pumps, serving a wide range of beers from Yorkshire and further afield.

Attendees can choose from four sessions: Friday, July 2, from 12pm to 4.30pm and 5.30pm to 10pm, and Saturday, July 3, at the same times.

Each ticket includes entry to the chosen session, a festival programme, and a festival glass.

Professor Sarah Brown, governor of the Merchant Adventurers, said: "Now one of the key events in our calendar, we are looking forward to celebrating the fifth year and welcoming back many of our regulars!

"The festival is the perfect opportunity to spend a relaxed afternoon or evening in beautiful surroundings while sampling some of the best beers in Yorkshire.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21718154.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cask Bar Friday evening *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"The festival also supports our education programme, which provides a range of opportunities for young people in the city."

Live music will be provided throughout the event on the YO1 Radio stage, featuring performances from local artists.

On Friday evening, a special tasting session will be hosted by Mr Grabham and Jamie Hawksworth of Elvington Brewery.

The pair will share their personal festival picks and discuss the stories behind two of the region’s most successful breweries.

Early bird tickets are available now for £10.

Festival updates and announcements can be found by following @merchant.adventurers\_beer\_fest on Instagram.
