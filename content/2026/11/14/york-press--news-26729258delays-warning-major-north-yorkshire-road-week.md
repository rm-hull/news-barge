## Delays warning on major road near York

Drivers are being warned to expect delays *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

MOTORISTS have been warned to expect delays on a major road near York.

Temporary traffic lights are causing delays on the A59 west of the city in both directions due to telecoms work.




* **York road closures announced for Acomb and Poppleton**
* **Restrictions expected to end on landslip-hit Yorkshire road**

The disruption is between the A1(M) Junction 47 (Allerton Park) and Hopperton Street.

AA Roadwatch was reporting queuing traffic earlier today (Saturday).

The works began on November 11 and is expected to end at 6pm on Friday November 20.
