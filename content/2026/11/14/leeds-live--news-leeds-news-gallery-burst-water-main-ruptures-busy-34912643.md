# Burst water main ruptures on busy road sending huge fountain into air

## Burst water main caused congestion and traffic issues in Leeds

A burst water main sent fountains of water high into the air on a Leeds road earlier today (Saturday, November 14).

The huge water fountain was reported in Hunslet this morning and led to traffic congestion for motorists. The traffic congestion is ongoing, according to travel monitors.

The burst water main has been sending water high into the air on Thwaite Gate. The surface of the road was left ruptured by the force of the water.

The leak caused a massive spray across the road. Drivers have been navigating their round around the water while a section of the road has been closed.
