Standing desks alone may not be a panacea for reducing health risks from sitting all day, but throwing a walking pad underneath and getting some steps in while attending to brainless tasks certainly can’t hurt. If your New Year’s resolution has anything to do with health, you might want to consider grabbing a standing desk now, while many of our WIRED-tested and recommended brands are on sale. A walking pad we recommend is also discounted ahead of Black Friday, if you want to complete your setup.

Be sure to also check out our main Best Black Friday Deals, as well as early deals on headphones, TVs, and hot tech at Best Buy.
