A man is fighting for his life and four people have been arrested after a three-car crash on the A1.

Emergency services were called to the southbound carriageway near between Kingston Park and Gosforth Park on Sunday (November 8) after a crash just before 10pm.

The incident involved a Toyota Prius, a BMW Series 3 and a Volkswagen Golf, which collided just before the junction for Kingston Park.

The driver of the Toyota Prius - a man in his 50s - was taken to the Royal Victoria Infirmary (RVI) with life-threatening injuries. Another person also remains in hospital.

Northumbria Police has confirmed four people have been arrested in connection with the incident and remain in custody.

Officers have launched an investigation and are appealing for witnesses or anyone with CCTV to come forward.

The force would like to hear from anyone who may have seen the BMW and the Volkswagen Golf in the hours before the crash.

The road was closed for around ten hours before it reopened to drivers this morning, sparking severe traffic during rush hour.
