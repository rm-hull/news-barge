Plans for a major hotel and golf resort in the Angus countryside have been submitted to Angus Council.

The proposed Brae of Angus development would cover 310 acres at the Shank of Omachie, north and east of Wellbank, and include a five-star hotel, an 18-hole championship golf course, leisure and wellness facilities, visitor accommodation and housing.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21691952.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The proposed Brae of Angus development. *(Image: Supplied)*

Led by Angus Land Company Ltd, the scheme is the subject of a Planning Permission in Principle application.

The masterplan includes a 150-bedroom hotel with spa, golf academy and performance centre, lodges and townhouses, a country club, conference facilities and staff training provision. A range of residential properties, from two-bedroom cottages to larger family homes, is also proposed.

Global hospitality brand Hyatt is backing the hotel, while sports and events group IMG is supporting the golf offering. Both arrangements are subject to planning consent and contract.

The project follows a pre-application consultation programme, including two public events at Wellbank Village Hall. Its developers say the scheme aims to establish a destination that will attract visitors, support local businesses and create employment and training opportunities.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21691954.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The proposed Brae of Angus development. *(Image: Supplied)*

Development is expected to take place in phases, with the golf course planned for an early stage to allow the landscape to mature before the hotel opens.

Housing would be delivered in line with Angus Council approvals, with proceeds from property sales helping to fund the wider resort.

The plans also include extensive landscaping and tree planting, alongside walking and cycling routes intended to support biodiversity and active travel.

Natalie Calderbank, director of Angus Land Company, said: "We are delighted to be submitting our proposals for the Brae of Angus in this Planning Permission in Principle (PPiP) submission.

"The scheme represents a new and exciting vision for the area and its community, with a well-thought-out and sustainably constructed blend of amenities that support the broader initiatives of the region.

"Brae of Angus is a truly multi-faceted scheme that delivers for the growing demand across Scotland for high-quality hospitality, golf and leisure experiences whilst promoting the best of the region's heritage, provenance and produce.

"With multi-generation accommodation, all-season golf provision, and best-in-class leisure and wellness facilities, the proposed development strengthens the region’s tourism offer and complements its established reputation as a leading golf destination.

"We believe Brae of Angus is a unique opportunity to create a destination within Angus, knitting this community into the East Coast golf belt of Scotland and creating a place to live, play and stay.

"The residential element is designed to meet local housing demand with a range of offerings from two-bedroom cottages to large family homes – all playing an important role in supporting delivery of the wider destination, with proceeds reinvested to help deliver the hotel, golf and broader facilities.

"We would like to thank everyone who took the time to engage with the proposals during the consultation process; your feedback has been warmly welcomed, and we look forward to continuing that dialogue as Angus Council considers the application."
