This rare view of Raby Castle - a side of the Raby Estates that is closed off to visitors – shows its remarkable history up close.

BUSINESSiQ Editor Mike Hughes was given an escorted trip on to the ROOF of the castle, following in the footsteps of thousands of soldiers and staff over more than 600 years.

These photographs show the immaculately restored rooftops, battlements and towers that dominate the skyline here and draw hundreds of thousands of visitors every year.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21688739.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Exploring the rooftops of Raby Castle *(Image: Mike Hughes)*

Next year it will form the backdrop to the 2027 BUSINESSiQ Awards taking place just a few yards away in the shadow of the castle at the Riding School and Dutch Barn buildings on Thursday, April 29.

There will be food and entertainment in and around The Dutch Barn before and after the awards, which will be held in The Riding School—an 18th-century architectural gem.

As Sophie Brown, sales and events manager at Raby Castle, Park and Gardens told us: “The BUSINESSiQ Awards are a brilliant opportunity to celebrate the achievements of businesses from across the region and everyone’s hard work to support and champion the North East. To be the location for the awards next year is going to be a momentous occasion for Raby and we’re looking forward to welcoming all the nominees.

"We’ve seen an incredible amount of interest in our corporate events and have welcomed a fantastic array of brands, companies and organisations over the last few years.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21688744.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Exploring the rooftops of Raby Castle *(Image: Mike Hughes)*

“We have something unique to offer everyone, whether you’re a family visiting to experience the new trails in The Plotters’ Forest, enjoying a guided tour round Raby Castle with our archives team or taking part in one of our garden tours or themed floristry workshops. We also offer wellbeing events, cooking masterclasses, estate tours, stargazing experiences, crafting workshops and so much more.”

Built in the 14th century by the powerful Neville family, was home to Cecily Neville, mother of two kings of England, it was also the scene of the plotting of the Rising of the North and a Parliamentary stronghold during the Civil War.

Much of the exterior with medieval towers, terrace and embattled walls dates back to the 14th century, while the interior represents centuries of developments by the Vane family, from the Georgian period, to the Victorian, to the present day and Lord and Lady Barnard.

Mike Hughes said: "This was a day I will never forget, and we sare so grateful to the Raby Estates staff for being so generous with their time and letting us see such an astonishing side of the castle.

"I can't wait for April when we can welcome hundreds of guests to the BUSINESSiQ Awards and let them soak up the history we have seen up close."
