North Yorkshire Police has issued an appeal for information and witnesses after a vehicle was stolen from a property in Harrogate.

The car was stolen between 4.30pm and 7.30pm on November 1 from Crag Lane in Kettlesing.

If you have any information that could help the police with their investigation, you should get in touch.

If you can help, you should email [Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Dom.Tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk)

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260215326 when providing any details regarding the incident.
