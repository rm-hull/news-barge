A driver accused of murdering a 23-year-old man offered to plead guilty to manslaughter before his trial began, jurors heard.

Ashton Anderson smashed his 4x4 into Daniel Ambler, pinning him against metal shelving at a garage forecourt.

Mr Ambler was pronounced dead after the crash at the Esso Asda Express station in Seaham.

Barry Anderson, 52, his sons Ashton, 20, and Logan, 19, and 18-year-olds Tyler Leadbetter and Ajay Henry deny murder.

Ashton Anderson wanted to “scare” Mr Ambler as a feud between their families turned violent, Teesside Crown Court heard.

## ‘I didn’t intend to hit Daniel’

Prosecutor Matthew Donkin KC questioned Anderson about whether he meant to seriously injure Mr Ambler.

Ashton Anderson replied: "No."

Mr Donkin said: “You have told the jury that you have accepted that you are guilty of manslaughter.” Ashton replied: “Yes.”

Mr Donkin continued: “You killed him by driving into him, didn’t you?” Ashton replied: “Yes.”

Mr Donkin asked: “If you drive a car into somebody, what’s going to happen to them?”

Ashton said: "They could be injured or killed."

Mr Donkin asked: "Why did you drive into him?"

Ashton replied: “I didn’t intend to hit Daniel with the car.”

Earlier, Ashton’s barrister Imran Shafi KC questioned him about the fatal crash and his state of mind.

Anderson said he heard Mr Ambler shout, ‘see what f\*\*\*\*\*\* happens’, after Logan Anderson assaulted him moments earlier, the court heard.

The 20-year-old said he got back into the 4x4 and followed Mr Ambler to “scare him” away from the garage where his cousin worked.

The defendant admitted misjudging his speed and driving faster than he realised onto the forecourt.

Logan Anderson has admitted affray after a feud between the Anderson and Ambler families turned violent.

Barry Anderson declined to give evidence in his defence.

The attack followed a feud that began when Barry Anderson was stabbed in August.

Barry Anderson, 52, of Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland; Ashton Anderson, 20, of Ryton Crescent, Seaham; and Logan Anderson, 19, of Nursery View, Shotton Bank, face murder and violent-disorder charges.

Ajay Henry, of Melrose Crescent, Seaham, and Tyler Leadbetter, of Elgin Avenue, Seaham, were charged with Daniel Ambler’s murder.

The day after Mr Ambler died, Snapchat messages warned that police would be looking at everyone in Deneside, Seaham.

The court heard that Mr Ambler's brother David and his friend Jed Wilson had arranged to fight Barry Anderson, who was stabbed in the side during the struggle.
