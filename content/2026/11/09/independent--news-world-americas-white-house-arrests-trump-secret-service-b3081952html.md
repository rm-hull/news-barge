Two people were arrested near the White House by the Secret Service on Monday after one person jumped a barricade at Lafayette Park in front of the presidential compound while another person drove a car onto the sidewalk.

President Donald Trump was home at the time of both incidents, prompting a brief White House lockdown and road closures

Shortly before 10 a.m., a vehicle struck the sidewalk surrounding Lafayette Park, according to a statement from a Secret Service spokesperson.

![Members of the U.S. Secret Service Crime Scene Search Unit inspect a car that crashed at Lafayette Square](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/09/17/download.-1..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"The driver was combative with law enforcement, but was quickly placed under arrest," the statement noted.

"Around the same time, a second individual jumped a barricade near the northwest corner of Lafayette Park. That individual was also quickly placed under arrest."

The Secret Service is currently investigating both incidents and it remains unclear if they are connected.
