Fresh air, a great pub, meeting new people and supporting a local business – what more could anyone want?

For Matt Kendall, it was the perfect combination, but he couldn’t find what he was looking for after trying multiple groups.

These groups were often fast paced, started in remote locations and left little time to socialise at the end.

“I felt there was a gap in the market where the emphasis is as much on the talking as the walking,” said Matt.

So Matt decided to create his own group, bringing together everything he felt was missing, including a pub stop so people could stop and chat.

After his first posts in a local Facebook group received an overwhelmingly positive response, he created his own page Walks to the Pub, a community designed to help people meet new people, explore York and build connections along the way.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/walks-to-the-pub-york.webp)

On 13 March 2025, the group took its first walk, exploring a route through York’s famous snickelways before finishing at Three Tuns pub in Coppergate for a well-deserved drink.

Recalling the first meeting, Matt says, “it was dark, cold and raining, but 18 people still turned up, it was a fantastic evening and there was definitely an appetite for it.”

Despite the wet weather, it marked the beginning of a group that has continued to grow.

Since the first walk, Matt has organised 42 walks that have kept attracting more and more people.

The walks typically take place twice a month, with a shorter two-mile route on a Thursday evening and a longer Saturday afternoon walk.

The Saturday walks often cover more than five miles, with a pub lunch and a second pub stop afterwards, making it a full-day experience.

“These walks now regularly get between 30 and 50 people per walk, while the events are even bigger, with over 100 people attending,” said Matt.

## Making connections

Previously, the group’s events have included a Christmas party, a first-anniversary celebration, and a late-summer social, giving members more time together than on regular walks.

While the walks offer a chance to discover new corners of York and support local businesses, Matt believes the biggest achievement is the social side.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/walks-to-the-pub-york-3.webp)

“For me, the biggest benefit of Walks to the Pub is the opportunity for people to make genuine connections,”

“People can come on their own, discover parts of York they have never visited, meet new people and form friendships that continue beyond the walks, we have even had multiple couples meet through the walks.”

The group attracts people from different backgrounds and at different stages of life, making it a perfect place to meet others.

Matt explains that members range from those who have just moved to York and want to build a new social circle to those going through big life changes, including separation or their children leaving home.

For these members, *Walks to the Pub* offers a reason to get out of the house or discover York’s hidden gems they might not know about otherwise.

Matt also chooses routes accessible by public transport so people can join without a car, and keeps them dog-friendly, giving everyone options.

When talking about the group’s future, Matt said: “Looking ahead, our aim is to continue doing what we do best: bringing people together, exploring York and supporting local, independent businesses.

“We now have a fantastic group of regular members, but we are always keen to welcome new faces. Ultimately, we hope that Walks to the Pub will continue to make a positive difference to people’s lives, one walk – and one pub – at a time!”
