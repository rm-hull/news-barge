Ripon's shops stay open until 8pm on Thursday 26 November for late night shopping, with 12 businesses signed up so far. A gnome hunt runs from 1 December.

Ripon’s Christmas late night shopping evening will be on **Thursday 26
November**, Ripon BID has announced. Its event listing runs from** 9am to 8pm**,
and entry is free
(Visit Ripon).

Twelve businesses had signed up by 9 November. The BID is still asking others to join, so the list will grow (Ripon BID, 5 November). It says shops will offer festive treats and “exclusive one-night-only deals”.

## A gnome hunt from 1 December

The BID is also recruiting shops for a **Gnome for Christmas Hunt**, a family trail
running from **1 December to 1 January**
(Ripon BID).

Handmade wooden gnomes will be hidden in businesses across the city. Each comes with a secret letter, and families collect the letters to crack a festive password.

The gnomes are made by **Jennyruth Workshops**, a charity that provides meaningful
work and skills development for adults with learning disabilities. Businesses have until **15
November** to ask for a gnome, and the BID says spaces are limited.

The BID has not yet said where families pick up the trail or whether there is a prize. We will add that when it is published.

## What it means for you

* **Thursday 26 November, until 8pm:** a weekday evening to shop in Ripon, with deals in the shops listed above. Check a shop’s own page before you go, because not every business has published its late hours.
* **The weekend before:** Ripon’s artisan Christmas market fills the Market Square from 20 to 22 November. Our Ripon Christmas market page has the times.
* **From 1 December:** the gnome hunt gives children a reason to walk round the city’s independent shops.

More festive shopping across the district is on our Christmas shopping page.
