North Yorkshire Police has issued two CCTV images of two people they would like to speak to following a theft at a supermarket in Harrogate.

The incident happened on October 14 at Sainsbury’s on Leeds Road.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “Please contact us if you recognise the people pictured in the CCTV image, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”

If you can help, you should email [ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ed.chatland@northyorkshire.police.uk)

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260203274 when providing any details regarding the incident.
