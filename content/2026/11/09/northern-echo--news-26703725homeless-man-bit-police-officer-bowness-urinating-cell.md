A homeless man sank his teeth into an officer's leg and tried to bite another before urinating in a police cell.

Jamie Robertson planned to sleep in the yard of The Fizzy Tarté in Bowness until the owner disturbed him.

The 29-year-old became racially abusive before police were called, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Rebecca Rowlands said: “He became angry with the owner and said ‘it’s you fault I’m homeless. Go back to you own country’.

“Police were called and they located the defendant. During his arrest, he has bitten one officer on the leg and attempted to bite another one but only managed to bite their trouser leg.

“He was taken to the police station, where he urinated on the cell wall, wiped blood across it and spat on the door.”

Miss Rowlands said cleaning the cell cost Cumbria Police £50.

Robertson, of no fixed abode, admitted assaulting an emergency worker, criminal damage, assault by beating and racially aggravated harassment on October 2.

His solicitor, Georgia Snowdon, said Robertson was suffering a psychotic episode while struggling with the death of his mother, who died in front of him.

She added: “His actions and behaviour come as a result of his homeless state.

“There was a verbal altercation between him and the man, but he was in a psychotic state at the time.”

Miss Snowdon urged the bench to follow Robertson’s pre-sentence report, saying he needed mental-health support.

Bench chairman David Johnson gave Robertson a two-year community order, ordered him to pay £150 compensation and fined him £80.
