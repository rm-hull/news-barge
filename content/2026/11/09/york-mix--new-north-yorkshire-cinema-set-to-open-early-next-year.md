Ripon’s new three-screen cinema set to open its doors February/March 2027.

Housed in a 10,000 sq ft premises on Fishergate, currently undergoing major refurbishment, it will be showcasing the latest film releases, independent cinema, event cinema and much more.

The countdown is on for the eagerly awaited opening, and film lovers across Ripon and the surrounding areas are invited to be part of Ripon Cinema’s journey from the very start.

Memberships and gift vouchers are now available to purchase via www.riponcinema.com, where visitors can also enjoy a preview of what’s to come.

John Tate, who with his business partner John Hewitt is behind the return of big screen entertainment to the city told Tim Flanagan of the Rejoicing in Ripon Blog: “We had originally intended to have a pre-Christmas opening, but as is often the case with large reconfiguration and refurbishment works of this nature, there have been a couple of issues that we needed to resolve.”

He added: “We are now on schedule for an opening in early 2027 and are looking forward to welcoming filmgoers from Ripon and neighbouring towns and villages, to come and experience the wow factor of watching the latest releases and screenings from the National Theatre and Royal Opera House in the sumptuous surroundings that we are creating in Fishergate.”
