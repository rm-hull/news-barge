Families will be able to meet festive favourites, enjoy live entertainment and visit a Christmas street food market this month, as Teesside Park has announced its festive celebration line-up.

A range of seasonal activities will launch at the popular Stockton retail park on Friday, November 20, with a packed evening of performances, character appearances and family fun from 4pm to 7pm.

On Primark Terrace, children will be able to meet roaming characters like the Huggable Snowman, the Snow Sisters inspired by Frozen, and the park's own take on the Grinch, its mischievous Mean Green One.

Urban Kaos will then lead children from in and around Teesside to the Primark Terrace for a number of dance performances, while The Cleveland Police Community Brass Band play Christmas favourites outside the Hello building.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679252.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Teesside music group The Funk Collective will perform at the November launch event. *(Image: The Funk Collective)*

The Globe Community Choir, the schools’ Big Sing and Teesside music group The Funk Collective will also perform three thirty-minute sets between the brass band and Urban Kaos' sessions.

An Arriva bus outside Sports Direct will provide children an opportunity to meet Santa Claus, make reindeer dust and have their faces painted.

The Hello building will host additional activities from Stagecoach, including Christmas colouring, 3D cardboard bus crafts and festive giveaways.

The launch event also marks the opening of Teesside Park’s Christmas street food market, which will run until January 3.

Visitors will be able to enjoy seasonal treats such as Yorkshire pudding wraps, Dutch pancakes, churros, Christmas toasties and brownies.

Catherine Furlong, centre director at Teesside Park, said the launch was a "lovely opportunity" for residents to come together ahead of Christmas.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679254.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Teesside Park’s Christmas street food market will open on November 20 and run until January 3. *(Image: Dave Charnley)*

She added: "We've got a brilliant evening planned to kick off Christmas at Teesside Park, with lots for families to enjoy, from meeting some familiar festive characters to watching live performances and enjoying the Christmas street food market.

"Christmas is always a fantastic time of year at Teesside Park, but this year feels particularly special as we celebrate our 35th anniversary."

In December, the Little Bird Made artisan markets will return on December 3, 4, 10, 11, 17 and 18 in the run-up to Christmas, offering unique gifts from more than 30 independent artists and small businesses.

With Santa, festive characters, live music and seasonal food all on offer, Teesside Park is hoping to give families a taste of Christmas as thousands of shoppers prepare for the festive season.

More information about Teesside Park's festive season can be found on its website.
