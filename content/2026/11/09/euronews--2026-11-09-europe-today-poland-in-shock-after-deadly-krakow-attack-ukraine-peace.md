## Poland is in shock after this weekend's deadly shooting in Krakow's Main Market Square, which Polish PM Donald Tusk called an act of terrorism. The shooter died this morning. Also on the show: new Ukraine peace talks in Abu Dhabi, the race to succeed Lagarde and the COP31 climate summit.

## On today's show:

**Jacek Siewiera**, Advisor to the European Commission's PreparEU initiative, asks whether the Krakow shooting that killed six people was a preventable security failure and whether Europe has a blind spot on lone attackers.** Maria Tadeo**, Euronews Europe Editor, reports from the European Council on the finance ministers' meeting and the search for** Christine Lagarde**'s successor at the European Central Bank.** Sasha Vakulina**breaks down the upcoming Ukraine peace talks in Abu Dhabi, what is on the agenda and whether Europeans will be in the room.** Maria Tadeo**sits down with** Fatih Birol**, Executive Director of the International Energy Agency, as COP31 opens in Antalya, Turkey. The full interview airs on her new programme, *On the Record*, tomorrow night at 9:10pm.**Chloé Mikolajczak**, social and environmental activist, asks whether climate activism has failed and what would count as success at COP31.
