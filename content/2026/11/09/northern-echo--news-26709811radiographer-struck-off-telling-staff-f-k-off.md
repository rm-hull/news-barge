*(Image: PA MEDIA)*A radiographer who lied about being sacked by the County Durham NHS trust went on a sweary rant telling medical bosses to "f\*\*\* off".

Kyle Harris, was dismissed for fross misconduct in 2014, then worked at the Gateshead NHS Foundation Trust before again being dismissed.

He had applied to be restored on the New Zealand Medical Radiation Technologists board's register to work as a radiographer and carry out MRIs, but hid his previous disciplinary action.

He has now been struck off by a Healthcare Practitioners Tribunal.

He was found to have sent a string of offensive emails to staff dealing with his application, including wishing an earthquake would swallow their office, calling them “d\*\*\* heads”, and using the F-word.

Harris worked as a Band 5 radiographer at County Durham and Darlington NHS Foundation Trust from June 2012 until he was dismissed for gross misconduct in June 2014.

He appealed against the decision but was unsuccessful.

He later joined Gateshead NHS Foundation Trust in August 2017, as a radiographer in the MRI department.

He was dismissed on August 15, 2023, following a disciplinary hearing for gross misconduct.

In May 2023, Harris had contacted the New Zealand Medical Radiation Technologists Board to apply to rejoin its register as a radiographer, with MRI as an additional scope of practice.

As part of the application process, he was required to declare whether he had been subject to disciplinary proceedings.

But on September 11, 2023, he signed a form confirming he had not faced any disciplinary proceedings since he was last registered.

The tribunal found Harris deliberately withheld information to conceal the previous disciplinary proceedings from the New Zealand regulator.

The hearing also examined his correspondence with the Medical Sciences Secretariat, which provides regulatory services for the New Zealand board.

The panel heard there had been delays and oversights in processing his application, including staff asking him to resend documents he had already provided.

After being told on January 26, 2024, that fees were non-refundable, Harris emailed staff saying: “you guys really are a bunch of d\*\*\* heads”.

On January 29, he sent another email to the board's registrar beginning: “Hey douchebag.”

He continued: “It takes literally a week to get a reply and it's some pillock who asks for something I have already provided. S\*\*\*\* behaviour, s\*\*\*\* service from s\*\*\*\* people at nzmrt (sic).”

He went on to write: “It's nice that your inclusive, but obviously you aren't mentally competent to understand f\*\*\* off. So maybe ask your carer to explain. Stop messaging me. I hope the earthquake shallows your office with you in it.”

On March 1, 2024, he sent another sweary email to the organisation's chief executive.

The panel said he had shown no insight or evidence of remediation.

A Healthcare Practitioners Tribunal Committee hearing which ended last month was told Harris was cautioned in 2015 after posting sexually explicit comments about colleagues on social media and behaving inappropriately towards colleagues.

It said there remained a real risk of repetition and that his conduct could potentially cause harm to colleagues and service users.

The panel concluded striking off was the only appropriate. punishment.
