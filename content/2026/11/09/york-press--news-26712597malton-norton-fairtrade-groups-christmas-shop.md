Malton and Norton’s Fairtrade Group will host its annual Christmas shop this weekend.

The event will feature Fairtrade Christmas cards, decorations, crafts, chocolates, and clothes.

Helen and Steve Morgan, Janice Gwilliam, Janice Clark, April Cunningham, Jo Paveley, and Martine Clark showcasing items for sale.

Fairtrade refreshments will be available, and entry is free.

Visitors can also browse a range of local crafts, an artist and Ryedale Environment Group.

The event will be held on Saturday, November 14, at the Friends’ Meeting House in Greengate, Malton, running from 10am to 3pm.
