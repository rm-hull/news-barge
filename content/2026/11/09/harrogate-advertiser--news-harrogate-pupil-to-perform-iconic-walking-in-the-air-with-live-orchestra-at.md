A pupil from Duchy College in Harrogate will take centre stage at the York Barbican this Christmas, performing *Walking In The Air* accompanied by a live orchestra.

Charlotte successfully auditioned for the role, which sees her perform the well-known song from The Snowman and forms part of an award-winning national tour, featuring a screening of the classic 1982 film, accompanied by a live orchestra.

“When I found out that I’d got the part, I was really, really shocked,” Charlotte said.

“I thought I’d just give it a go for the experience, so finding out I’d been successful was amazing.”

Charlotte has played key roles in school nativities and is regularly involved in Duchy College Prep’s curricular and extra-curricular performing arts offering.

“This school is amazing for opportunities,” Charlotte said.

“There’s always a chance to do something, and if you get the opportunity to do something big, the school will always support you.

“That support makes you feel more confident and willing to give things a go,” she said.

“This is an incredible achievement,” Head of Duchy College Prep, Mr James Savile, said.

“Charlotte is an outstanding member of our community and we are exceptionally proud of her.

“Duchy College Prep has always had a strong reputation for performing arts, with our specialist music and drama teachers giving opportunities for children of all ages to gain confidence and develop key skills.

“From our pre-school nativity to our annual Year 6 production and our excellent results at local competitive festivals, we will continue to support all pupils as they pursue opportunities on stage.

The Snowman Tour will take place in 13 other venues across the country, with soloists performing in venues in Birmingham, Liverpool and the iconic Durham Cathedral.
