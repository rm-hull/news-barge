A Hollywood star from one of the biggest teen comedies of the 1990s is heading to the North East for a one-off night of film, live music and nostalgia.

Thomas Ian Nicholas, who played Kevin Myers in the American Pie franchise, will appear at KU Stockton on Friday, November 13, for an American Pie Emo Night.

The event will include a special screening of the original 1999 film, a live audience Q&A, a VIP meet and greet, an acoustic set, a full band performance and an American Pie-themed emo club night.

Mr Nicholas said: "American Pie has been part of people's lives for more than 25 years now, and it's crazy how much love there still is for those films.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690311.jpg?type=mds-article-620)American Pie star Thomas Ian Nicholas heads to KU Stockton for a film screening, Q&A, live music, emo club night and VIP packages for over-18s *(Image: Supplied)*

"I get to meet fans all over the world who grew up watching them, and that's always a lot of fun.

"But what makes these nights really special is bringing everything together.

"We get to watch the movie, share some stories, have a few laughs and then turn the place into a rock show.

"I've played some incredible places over the years, and I can't wait to bring the band to Stockton.

"If you grew up on American Pie, pop-punk and those classic 90s and 00s soundtracks, this is your kind of night.

"Come hang out with us."

The event will take place across KU and The Social Room, promising a full celebration of the films, music and culture that defined a generation.

American Pie became a global box office hit and cultural phenomenon, launching a successful franchise and a cast of memorable characters.

Mr Nicholas starred alongside Jason Biggs, Seann William Scott, Alyson Hannigan, Chris Klein and Tara Reid, helping to cement the film's cult status.

Fans will have the rare chance to watch the movie alongside one of its stars, hear behind-the-scenes stories and enjoy a night of live music.

As frontman of the Thomas Nicholas Band, Mr Nicholas has performed more than 1,500 shows across 15 countries over the past 15 years.

Their seventh studio album, produced by Taylor Carroll of Lit, features collaborations with Bowling For Soup, Less Than Jake, The Early November and Ayron Jones.

Their track My Generation appeared on the American Reunion soundtrack.

A spokesperson for KU Stockton said: "This is exactly the kind of night we love bringing to Stockton.

"American Pie is one of those films that practically everyone of a certain generation knows, and having one of its original stars here in person is something pretty special.

"It's not every Friday night you get to watch a Hollywood classic with one of its stars, ask him questions about the films, see his band live and then party to the music you grew up with.

"We're expecting a brilliant atmosphere across KU and The Social Room.

"Whether you're a massive American Pie fan, love your pop-punk and emo anthems or just fancy a completely different Friday night out, this is going to be a lot of fun.

"Events like this don't come around very often in Stockton, and we'd encourage people to grab their tickets and come and be part of it."

The night will begin at 7.30pm with a VIP experience including a meet and greet and acoustic performance, before doors open to the public at 8pm.

The film screening starts at 8.30pm, followed by a live Q&A at 10pm.

At 10.30pm, the Thomas Nicholas Band will take to the stage, followed by the American Pie Emo Clubnight.
