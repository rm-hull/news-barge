A person has been hit by a train between Peterborough and Huntingdon.

Whilst the emergency services responded to this incident, all lines were closed but LNER say trains are now running again.

However, many are being delayed, cancelled in full or revised to only complete part of their journey.

It affects all North Yorkshire train operators who serve London Kings Cross. That’s LNER at York, Northallerton and Harrogate. Hull Trains at Howden and Selby and Grand Central at Thirsk and Northallerton.

*If you’ve been affected by what happened on the network today, there is always someone to talk to. You can contact the Samaritans on 116 123 from any phone for a confidential chat.*
