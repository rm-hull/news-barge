On the beaches of Ngor, a traditional Lebou fishing village on Dakar's northern coast, a new generation of **young surfers is discovering the thrill of riding Atlantic waves**.

At Anda Surf, local volunteers help neighbourhood children learn to balance, stand on their boards and build confidence in the water. Alongside paid lessons, the school **offers free surfing sessions to local children every Sunday**.

Although the sport is gaining popularity along Senegal's coastline, **expensive equipment, limited resources and the cost of competing abroad remain major obstacles**. Boards are often shared, and many young surfers lack suitable clothing.

The initiative comes as **Senegal hosts the Youth Olympic Games**, the** first Olympic sporting event on African soil**. Surfing is among ten non-medal engagement sports, offering a platform for young athletes hoping to make their mark.
