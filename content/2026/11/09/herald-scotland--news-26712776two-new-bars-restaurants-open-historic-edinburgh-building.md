Two ‘major’ new bars and restaurants are set to open in Edinburgh next year.

Signature Group has today announced plans to launch Ember & Fellows in the city’s Old Town in early 2027, "breathing new life into a historic site" and creating more than 90 jobs.

The Forrest Road building, close to the National Museum of Scotland and Greyfriars Bobby, was originally built between 1872 and 1874 as a meeting hall for the Independent Order of Oddfellows.

Offering two “completely different” experiences under one roof, Ember will be a traditional high-end Scottish pub, with a menu built around seasonal produce and charcoal cooking, while Fellows will be a sports bar and entertainment space.

**Read more:**

Ember & Fellows will be the first entirely new hospitality launch by Signature Group since the opening of McLarens on the Corner in Edinburgh’s Morningside in 2020.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21691687.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nic Wood, director, Signature Group *(Image: Supplied)*

Nic Wood, director at Signature Group, said: “2027 is set to be a really exciting year for Signature as we gear up to launch our first two major new bars and restaurants in the capital for over six years, creating over 90 hospitality jobs.

“We didn’t intend to take such a long break, but recent government policies have made expansion increasingly challenging.

“When this opportunity came along, though, we knew we could put our own signature on the building with fresh concepts, a new design and something Scotland hasn’t seen before.

“That’s always been our USP. We like to do things a little differently and give guests something they won’t find anywhere else.

“Forrest Road offers the perfect location to open Ember & Fellows, right in the heart of Edinburgh’s Old Town. Expect two separate hospitality venues with their own distinct personality, all under one roof, day and night.”

Signature Group operates more than 20 bars, restaurants and hotels across Edinburgh and other parts of Scotland.

Its venues in the city include McLarens on the Corner in Morningside, Copper Blossom on George Street and Badger & Co on Castle Street, as well as The Rutland, The Huxley, Kyloe and Understory on Rutland Place.

The group is now recruiting for a range of roles at Ember & Fellows ahead of the planned opening in early 2027.
