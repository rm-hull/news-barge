Leeds Station Gateway has officially opened following a £46.1 million transformation designed to improve access, safety and facilities for passengers using Leeds City Station.

The Leeds Station Sustainable Travel Gateway was delivered by Leeds City Council on behalf of Network Rail, in partnership with the West Yorkshire Combined Authority.

The project has transformed the station’s main entrance, creating a more accessible environment for pedestrians, cyclists, taxi users and public transport passengers.

The work was carried out alongside essential maintenance to the station’s underground supporting structure. Enabling works began in 2022, with the main engineering works starting in May 2023.

Combining the two projects was intended to provide better value for money and reduce the long-term disruption that would have been caused by completing the schemes separately.

Improvements to New Station Street and the pavement along Bishopgate Street opened in recent weeks. On Monday, 9 November, the remaining safety hoardings and barriers were removed following testing of the passenger lifts connecting the two streets.

The steps between Bishopgate Street and New Station Street have also opened, alongside a new taxi rank on Bishopgate Street. Hackney carriages can now pick up passengers there as well as at the existing location on Princes Square, behind the station.

## New facilities at Leeds Station Gateway

The project includes two passenger lifts providing step-free access between New Station Street and the taxi rank on Bishopgate Street, alongside improvements to pedestrian areas and public spaces.

## Further £200m investment for Leeds railway station

The recent government budget confirmed a further £200 million investment to transform Leeds City Station, on top of the £100 million already committed.

The additional funding is intended to provide more space, improved infrastructure, better passenger facilities and improved accessibility. It will also help reduce congestion and increase capacity for the almost 12 million additional passengers expected to use the station by 2043.

The wider transformation of Leeds City Station will support the first phase of Northern Powerhouse Rail, which aims to improve rail services between major Northern cities.

It will also complement the Transpennine Route Upgrade, an £11 billion programme intended to increase passenger capacity by 30 per cent and electrify key routes across the North.

## What the improvements mean for Harrogate passengers

The improvements will also be relevant to rail passengers travelling from Harrogate to Leeds, many of whom use the city as an interchange for onward journeys across the country.

Better accessibility and passenger facilities at Leeds Station could make connections easier for people travelling from Harrogate and the surrounding area. The wider investment programme also has implications for the future capacity of the rail network serving the region.

However, Leeds City Council’s announcement does not identify any specific improvements to services on the Harrogate line as part of the Station Gateway project.
