# Drone delivers food to chimpanzee cut off by flooding

A Florida chimpanzee sanctuary that provides lifelong care for animals formerly used in research laboratories, the pet trade and the entertainment industry was hit by flooding that left roads impassable. Staff used a drone to deliver food to Kaleb, a 38-year-old chimpanzee cut off from access. The non-profit facility said despite floodwaters damaging roads, buildings and equipment, staff closely monitored and cared for the animals.
