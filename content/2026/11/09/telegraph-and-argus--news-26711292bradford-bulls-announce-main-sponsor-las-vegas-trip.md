Bradford Bulls have announced Nathan Hadfield Utilities as a new kit sponsor for the 2027 season.

As part of the partnership, Nathan Hadfield Utilities branding will feature on the neckline of both the Bulls' replica and playing kits for the 2027 campaign.

It sees the local business expanding their support of the club following their previous involvement as a box holder in the Southbank Stand.

The partnership will also see Nathan Hadfield Utilities take on the role of main sponsor of the Bulls' Las Vegas trip, including the training wear range, which will be available to purchase in the coming months.

The collection will form part of the club's wider retail offering, with NHU branding featuring prominently across the range.

Based in West Yorkshire, Nathan Hadfield Utilities (NHU) is a growing civil engineering and utilities company delivering specialist infrastructure solutions across the UK.

Their expertise includes trenching and cabling, high and low-voltage installations, utility infrastructure, reinstatement works and electric vehicle charging infrastructure.

Bradford Bulls commercial manager Ian Stafford said:

"We're absolutely delighted to welcome Nathan Hadfield Utilities as a new kit sponsor for the 2027 season.

"Nathan and the team have already shown their commitment to the Bulls through their support as a box holder in the Southbank Stand, so it's fantastic to see that relationship develop further.

"To now have NHU branding on the neckline of our playing and replica kits, alongside their support as main sponsor of our Las Vegas trip, including the training wear range, is a fantastic step forward for both parties.

"It's always pleasing to see businesses who are already involved with the club choose to increase their investment and we're incredibly grateful to Nathan for his continued backing.

"We look forward to developing the partnership further throughout 2027."

Nathan Hadfield added: "We're really excited to be taking our relationship with Bradford Bulls to the next level.

"Having initially supported the club through our box in the Southbank Stand, becoming a kit sponsor for 2027 felt like a natural progression.

"The Bulls are a fantastic club with a huge history and a passionate supporter base, and we're proud to be increasing our involvement.

"Having our branding featured on the playing and replica kits is something we're really looking forward to.

"And becoming the Main Sponsor of the Las Vegas training wear range is another exciting opportunity.

"As a growing West Yorkshire business, supporting organisations and initiatives within our local community is important to us.

"We're looking forward to seeing the range launched and wish everyone at the Bulls every success for the 2027 season."
