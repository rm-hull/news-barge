Police are appealing for information after a car was damaged at a North Yorkshire holiday park.

The incident happened at Blue Dolphin Holiday Park, near Filey, between 10.30pm on Saturday, November 7, and 9am on Sunday, November 8.

During this time, the front and rear windscreen wipers of a vehicle were snapped off.

RECOMMENDED READING:

"We’re particularly appealing to anyone who saw this incident happen, or saw any suspicious behaviour during this timeframe."

Officers are urging anyone with information that could help the investigation to get in touch.

People can contact the force online by emailing Alastair Graham-Merrett at [alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:alastair.graham-merrett@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, information can be submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting 'Tell us about' and then choosing 'An existing case or report.'

A live chat service is also available on the website from Monday to Friday, between 9am and 5pm.

Those unable to use the online options can call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Anyone wishing to remain anonymous can contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260219787 when providing information.
