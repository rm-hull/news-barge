A police officer narrowly avoided serious injury after her leg was trapped in a car door when a vehicle sped off.

The incident occurred at about 4.20pm on Saturday, November 7, when Middlesbrough Proactive Team officers attempted to stop a silver Skoda Octavia on Dales Park Road in Hemlington.

The officer was taken to hospital and later discharged.

A 25-year-old man was arrested the same day on suspicion of failing to stop, dangerous driving, and possession with intent to supply cannabis.

He has been released on bail while enquiries continue.

A police spokesman said: "Officers are now appealing to locate the Skoda, which remains outstanding."
