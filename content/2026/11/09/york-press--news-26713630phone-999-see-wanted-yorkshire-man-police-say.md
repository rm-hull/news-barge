Police are urging anyone who sees a wanted North Yorkshire man to dial 999.

James Davey, 51, of Scarborough, is wanted after allegedly failing to appear in court on Monday, October 26.

North Yorkshire Police said “extensive” inquiries are ongoing to locate Davey who is believed to be in the Scarborough area.

The force is asking anyone with information about Davey, described as a white man with dark hair, to come forward.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us," a police spokesperson said.

Those with information which could help the investigation are asked to phone 101 or report it via North Yorkshire Police’s website.

Information can be reported anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Quote reference 12260210649 when passing on information to police or to Crimestoppers.
