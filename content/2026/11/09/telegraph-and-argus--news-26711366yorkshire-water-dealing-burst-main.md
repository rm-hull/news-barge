A burst water main has caused flooding and major disruption.

The incident happened in Yeadon, on the stretch between East View Road and Grange Avenue.

When the T&A visited the scene this morning (Monday), standing water could be seen covering much of the carriageway, with the affected section closed to traffic while repair work was carried out.

Yorkshire Water confirmed that teams are working on site to repair the main and manage the flooding.

## Full Yorkshire Water statement

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Water said: "We’re currently on site in Yeadon repairing a burst main, which has unfortunately caused localised flooding.

"We are also beginning to see reports of low water pressure or loss of supply for some properties."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21688977.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floodwater has affected a major stretch of road in Yeadon *(Image: T&A)*

## Motroist advised to avoid the area

The company said it was working closely with Leeds City Council to help clear blocked drains and reduce surface water on the road.

The spokesperson said: "We’re working with the council to unblock the road gullies as quickly as possible so that the standing water can begin to drain away, reducing the flooding and providing better access for our teams to complete the necessary repair.

"We're also looking at how we can bring properties back onto supply as soon as possible."

Motorists were advised to avoid the area, with the road remaining closed for safety reasons.

The spokesperson said: "To keep our colleagues and road users safe, the road is currently closed.

"We apologise for the disruption and would like to assure customers that we're working as quickly as we can."
