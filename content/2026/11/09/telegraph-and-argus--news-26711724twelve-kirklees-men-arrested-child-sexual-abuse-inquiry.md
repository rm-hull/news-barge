Twelve men from Kirklees have been arrested as part of an investigation into non-recent child sexual abuse.

Most of the men were arrested at addresses in Batley from September onwards, while one arrest took place in the West Midlands.

All 12 men have been interviewed and released on bail.

The alleged offences, including rape, sexual assault and human trafficking, mainly took place in Kirklees between 2006 and 2009, when the victim was a teenager.

The victim contacted police in 2023, leading to a large-scale investigation by Kirklees district.

Detective Chief Inspector Phil Davis, of Kirklees District, said: "Child sexual abuse and exploitation is an abhorrent crime and significant strides have been made in recent years to bring persons responsible for committing these offences in years and decades past to justice.

"This recent operation follows a report made by a victim who came forward as an adult to report being the victim of serious sexual offences over a number of years previously.

"A complex investigation by specialist officers has resulted in 12 men being arrested from September onwards."

He added: “Tackling child abuse is not something any one agency can do in isolation; we work closely with local authorities, other organisations and charities to support victims, bring the perpetrators to justice and make our communities safer."
