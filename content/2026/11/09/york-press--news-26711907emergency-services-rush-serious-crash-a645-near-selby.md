EMERGENCY services are currently at the scene of a serious crash in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police said officers were dealing with an incident on the A645 in the Camblesforth area, near Selby.




A police spokesperson said: “The road is closed between the A1041 and Brigg Lane while emergency services attend the scene.

“Motorists are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes. Please allow extra time for your journey.”

The section of road affected has been closed by police.
