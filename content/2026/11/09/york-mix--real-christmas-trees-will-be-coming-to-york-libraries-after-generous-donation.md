Four Explore centres will be sparkling a little brighter this Christmas thanks to a generous donation from local business Haxby & Wigginton Christmas Trees.

The family-run business has donated four real Christmas trees for York Explore, Clifton Explore, Acomb Explore and Tang Hall Explore, helping create festive spaces for library visitors throughout the Christmas season.

The donation has been matched by the generosity of Explore’s community. Following a recent appeal, community members donated more than £400 towards Christmas decorations and lights, helping transform the libraries and Reading Cafés into welcoming seasonal spaces.

Jenny Layfield, chief executive of Explore York Libraries and Archives, said: “We’re incredibly grateful to Ian and everyone at Haxby & Wigginton Christmas Trees for this generous donation.

“For the first time this year, we’ll have real Christmas trees in our Explore Centres, something that has only been possible thanks to the support of local businesses and our communities.

“Alongside donations towards decorations and lights, these trees will help create welcoming and festive spaces for people across York to enjoy throughout the Christmas season.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Group_With_Sign-2048x1365.jpg)

Ian from Haxby & Wigginton Christmas Trees said: “We’re really pleased to be able to support Explore this Christmas. It’s great to know the trees will be enjoyed by so many people across York, and we’re looking forward to seeing them on display in the libraries.”

Haxby & Wigginton Christmas Trees will be holding its Christmas tree weekends on 14-15 November and 21-22 November at Westfield Lane, Wigginton, offering a wide range of locally grown Christmas trees.

Visitors can see the donated trees in place from mid-November and enjoy them alongside Explore’s programme of Christmas fairs and seasonal events.
