MARIAH Carey is set to bring her European tour to Scarborough next summer.

The five-time Grammy Award winner will take the stage at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Saturday, July 3, for one of only four UK dates on her European Firefly Summer Festivals Tour.

The show will feature British R&B group Eternal as special guests.




![Mariah will be joined by special guests Eternal](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21687186.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mariah will be joined by special guests Eternal *(Image: Scarborough Open Air Theatre)*

Julian Murray, of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, said: "2027 just keeps getting better and better.

"We have announced some huge names for next year already and adding Mariah Carey to that mix, along with Eternal, is fantastic.

"This will be an incredible show from the best-selling female artist of all time.

"What's not to love about that?"

Mariah Carey, who has sold more than 200 million albums worldwide, is the best-selling female artist of all time.

She has achieved 19 Billboard Hot 100 Number One singles, 18 of which she wrote herself.

Earlier this year, she released her 16th studio album, Here For It All, which debuted in the Top 10 on the Billboard Hot 200 chart.

The album maintained her 35-year streak of landing new projects in the chart’s top 10 and also reached Number One on Billboard’s Top Album Sales, Top Current Album Sales, Top R&B Albums, and Independent Albums charts.

Best known to many as the Queen of Christmas, her 1994 classic All I Want For Christmas Is You remains one of the longest-charting singles in any genre, spending 65 weeks on Billboard’s Hot 100.

Outside of music, her memoir The Meaning of Mariah Carey debuted at Number One on the New York Times Best Seller list.

Eternal, the special guests for the evening, rose to fame in the 1990s with their chart-topping soul and R&B hits.

The group has sold more than 10 million records worldwide, with their debut album Always & Forever reaching Number Two on the UK Albums Chart and earning four-times platinum status.

In 1997, they topped the UK Singles Chart with I Wanna Be the Only One, which also won them a MOBO Award for Best Single.

Mariah Carey is the latest addition to Scarborough Open Air Theatre's 2027 line-up, which already includes headline acts such as Courteeners, Shania Twain, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

Tickets for the Scarborough date go on general sale at 9am on Friday via ticketmaster.co.uk.

For more information, visit scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.
