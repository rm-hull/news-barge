A theatre that has been at the heart of a city's cultural scene for more than 50 years is set to close next year.

The University of Bradford says Theatre in the Mill will close permanently in March 2027 amid mounting financial pressures across higher education.

The 100-seat theatre opened on campus in November 1976 and became a hub for artists, students and community groups.

Announcing the closure, the university said it had taken the decision "reluctantly" but needed to focus resources where they have "the greatest impact."

## Statement from university

A university spokesperson said: "The university has reluctantly taken the decision to close Theatre in the Mill from March 2027.

"Like many in the higher education sector, Bradford faces significant financial pressures.

"We have a responsibility to focus our resources where they can have the greatest impact on students, teaching and research.

"Theatre in the Mill has played an important role in the university's cultural and creative life for 50 years, supporting students, artists, audiences and community partners."

## Theatre assessed on ability to 'deliver value'

The statement added: "We must assess all university activities and facilities against their long-term sustainability and their ability to deliver value for the university.

"These assessments are necessary in a challenging operating environment.

"We recognise that this decision will be disappointing for many people who care deeply about Theatre in the Mill.

"We remain committed to working with partners across the city to support culture, creativity and opportunity for future generations."
