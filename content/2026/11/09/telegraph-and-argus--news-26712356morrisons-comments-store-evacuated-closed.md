Morrisons has commented after one of its stores was evacuated and closed.

The Bradford-based supermarket chain's Keighley store sustained a suspected arson this past weekend.

The incident occurred at Morrisons on Worth Way, just after 4pm on Saturday.

Three crews were called to the blaze, which West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said was already out when they arrived.

It is believed to have been started deliberately.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21686937.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Morrisons on Worth Way, Keighley *(Image: Google Maps)*

## What Morrisons has said

Morrisons says, due to the swift action of its security team, everyone got out safely and no one was hurt.

A spokesperson for the supermarket chain said: "Following a fire on Saturday afternoon, our Keighley store was evacuated and closed.

"Thankfully, due to the swift action of the security team and our colleagues, everyone got out safely and no one was hurt.

"We would like to extend our huge thanks to the police and fire crews for their rapid response during the afternoon, and we are continuing to support the police as they investigate the incident

"We were able to reopen as planned on Sunday."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21690608.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another image inside the store *(Image: UGC)*

## What the fire service said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 16.08 on 7th November to Morrisons in Keighley to reports of a fire.

"Three crews attended but on arrival the fire was extinguished.

"This has been recorded as a suspected deliberate ignition."
