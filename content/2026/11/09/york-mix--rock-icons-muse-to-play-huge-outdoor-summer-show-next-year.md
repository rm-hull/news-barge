Roundhay Festival is returning next year – and today announces its first headliner.

The newest highlight of Leeds’s summer cultural calendar had its debut earlier this year, with headliners including Lewis Capaldi, Pitbull, and Kesha.

The outdoor festival is returning for 2027 – with first headliner rock icons MUSE to play Roundhay Park on **Sunday 27 June 2027**.

They will be joined by guests Nothing But Thieves. The full line-up is to be announced.

Tickets go on general sale on Friday 13 November at 10am at the Roundhay Festival website.

Since forming in Teignmouth, Devon in 1994, MUSE have built a reputation as one of the most ambitious live bands in the world, known for vast, boundary-pushing productions and a catalogue of huge rock anthems.

Next summer they bring that spectacle to Roundhay Park, joining a rich music legacy that was revived this year by the inaugural Roundhay Festival.

Epic tracks from “Plug In Baby” and “Time Is Running Out” to “Supermassive Black Hole” and “Uprising” have made MUSE global superstars and one of the UK’s most recognisable international artists of the past two decades.

In June 2026 they released their tenth studio album, ‘The Wow! Signal’, which went to Number 1 on the UK Official Albums Chart, their eighth consecutive chart-topper.

Their performance at Roundhay Festival will be MUSE’s first time back in Leeds in a decade.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/NBT-May-2026-2-Steve-Gullick.png)

Since forming in 2012, Nothing But Thieves have grown from a local Southend band into a global headline act, amassing a fiercely dedicated international fanbase, billions of streams, 1.2 million album sales and multiple gold and platinum records around the world.

With four UK Top 10 albums, including their first UK Number One with 2023’s Dead Club City, and thousands of shows worldwide, the band have cemented their arena status with landmark sold-out performances at Wembley Arena.

Jim King, CEO of AEG Presents UK & European Festivals, said “Following the success of our first year at Roundhay Park, we’re delighted to be returning to Leeds with one of the most incredible live bands in the world.

“Muse’s legendary outdoor performances and unrivalled production values continue to set the benchmark for live music, and we’re thrilled to welcome them to Roundhay for what promises to be a truly spectacular event.”
