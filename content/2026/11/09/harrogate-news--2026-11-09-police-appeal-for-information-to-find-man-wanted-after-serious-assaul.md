**North Yorkshire Police are appealing for information to locate a 34-year-old man wanted in connection with a serious assault outside the Bruce Arms in Masham.**

Cameron Ramsey, 34, is wanted following the incident, which took place on Saturday 7 November 2026.

Police have issued a description of Ramsey and are asking anyone who has seen him, or who has information about his whereabouts, to contact them immediately.

## Description of Cameron Ramsey

Ramsey is described as:

* A man in his 30s
* Mixed race
* Bald or with very closely shaved hair
* Dark eyebrows
* Wearing a long grey padded coat with a large hood
* Wearing a dark grey Berghaus tracksuit, consisting of a zip-up top and matching tracksuit bottoms
* Wearing black On Cloud trainers

## How to contact police

Anyone who has seen Cameron Ramsey or has information about his whereabouts is asked to call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference number **12260219596**.

If you see him at the time of a sighting, or believe there is an immediate risk to safety, call 999.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through the Crimestoppers website.

Police have advised the public not to approach Ramsey themselves.
