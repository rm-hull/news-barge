Edinburgh Food & Drink Academy has partnered with Swiss knife manufacturer Victorinox in a deal to provide students with professional equipment and specialist training.

The partnership will see the academy’s kitchens equipped with Victorinox knives, alongside dedicated masterclasses designed to develop students’ knife skills and cooking techniques.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21691280.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinburgh Food & Drink Academy Head Teacher, Aisha Elshani with CEO Lucinda Bruce-Gardyne. *(Image: Supplied)*

It will launch with an EFDA x Victorinox Knife Skills and Cooking Techniques class on Saturday, November 28. Participants will learn to use professional knives safely and effectively, with practical sessions covering how to joint a chicken, chop like a chef and fillet fish.

Students on the academy’s 12-week Diploma in Professional Cookery programme, as well as those attending selected masterclasses, will benefit from the upgraded equipment and training.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21397920.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinburgh Food & Drink Academy. *(Image: Ian Georgeson)*EFDA relaunched earlier this year, having operated for 17 years as the Edinburgh New Town Cookery School.**Read more**

Aisha Elshani, head teacher at the EFDA, said: "Whether you’re a professional chef, training to become one, or an avid home cook, the knives you use and the techniques implemented make a difference to every aspect of the dish.

"By equipping people with the understanding of different techniques, what knife to use for what dish or ingredient, and how to safely use sharp equipment in the kitchen, we can ensure uniform prep, consistent cooking times and an overall smoother, safer experience in the kitchen.

"Victorinox knives are highly regarded internationally as the ultimate chef’s companion.

"I’ve personally used their knives throughout my career and in my home kitchen, so I’m particularly delighted to be partnering with the team and introducing emerging chefs to their premium Swiss craftsmanship."

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21691253.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lucinda Bruce-Gardyne, Executive Chair, Edinburgh Food & Drink Academy. *(Image: Ian Georgeson)*

Philipp Steiner, head of strategic partnerships and sponsorships at Victorinox, said: "At Victorinox, we are proud to support professionals and aspiring chefs around the world with reliable tools for their craft.

"Our partnership with Edinburgh Food & Drink Academy is an opportunity to contribute to culinary education and help students build confidence in the kitchen through hands-on learning and practice.

"We are delighted to work with an institution that shares our commitment to quality, craftsmanship and lifelong learning."
