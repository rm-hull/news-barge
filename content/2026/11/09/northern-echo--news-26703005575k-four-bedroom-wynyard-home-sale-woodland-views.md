FAMILIES looking for space, style and a setting to match will find all this and much more at this four-bedroom detached home which not only offers buyers a wealth of accommodation but also occupies a prime position on Harebell Close in Wynyard, directly facing woodland.

Briefly, the accommodation includes a generous entrance hall off which is a cloakroom, a versatile front-facing reception room that could be used as a family room, playroom or formal sitting room, and a study perfect for home working.

The real showstopper, though, is the superb open-plan kitchen, dining room and lounge area at the heart of the home.

![Harebell Close, Wynyard](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675814.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There's plenty to appeal to buyers in this stylish Wynyard home, including a showstopping open-plan kitchen, dining room and lounge *(Image: IGOMOVE)*

This stretches right across the rear of the house, with the stylish kitchen being home to a generous central island with breakfast bar seating, plenty of modern fitted units, integrated appliances, a drinking water tap and a water softener.

Flowing seamlessly off the kitchen are the dining and lounge areas, where extensive glazing and two sets of French doors overlook the garden, filling the room with natural light.

A contemporary fireplace adds a luxurious touch.

Alongside the kitchen is a utility room that opens to the garden and an inner hallway leading directly into the double garage.

![Harebell Close, Wynyard](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675816.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There's plenty to appeal to buyers in this stylish Wynyard home, including a showstopping open-plan kitchen, dining room and lounge *(Image: IGOMOVE)*

Upstairs are four bedrooms: the principal bedroom with an extensive range of fitted wardrobes and a luxurious en suite bathroom, a second bedroom with an en suite shower room and two bedrooms cleverly linked by an inner passageway with built-in storage that leads to a shared Jack and Jill bathroom.

Tech-savvy buyers will appreciate the whole-home WiFi system, with CAT6A cabling, to each downstairs room and the two main bedrooms.

Outside, the south-facing rear garden is mainly laid to lawn with a paved patio ideal for soaking up the sun adjoining the house.

![Harebell Close, Wynyard](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675818.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There's plenty to appeal to buyers in this stylish Wynyard home, including a showstopping open-plan kitchen, dining room and lounge *(Image: IGOMOVE)*

To the front is a substantial driveway that leads to a double garage.

Add that leafy woodland outlook and sought-after Wynyard address, and it is a home it is easy to fall in love with.
