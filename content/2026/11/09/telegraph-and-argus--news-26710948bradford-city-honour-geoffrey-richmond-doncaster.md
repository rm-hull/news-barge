THE Bantams are in the middle of a three-week break from league action, but when they do return, it will be a poignant occasion, with a tribute planned for Geoffrey Richmond.

The former City chairman took charge in 1994 and bankrolled two promotions, including the memorable ascent to the Premier League in 1999.

The Bantams spent two seasons under Richmond’s stewardship in the top-flight, but they were relegated in 2001 and have yet to return.

As for present-day City, their next League One match is at home to Doncaster on Saturday, November 21.

And to mark the occasion, a minute’s applause will be held ahead of kick-off in honour of Richmond.
