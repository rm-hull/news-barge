Emergency services were called to a car crash in a North Yorkshire village last night (Sunday).

They went to the incident in **Bolton Percy**, between York and Tadcaster, at** 9.34pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews from Tadcaster responded to a single vehicle road traffic collision with reports of the driver being trapped.

#### Bolton Percy

“On arrival the driver managed to self extricate from the vehicle and was left in the care of paramedics. Crews isolated the vehicle.”
