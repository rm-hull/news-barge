## British rock giants announce huge festival show near York

Rock icons Muse will headline Roundhay Festival at Roundhay Park in Leeds on Sunday, June 27, 2027, joined by Nothing But Thieves. *(Image: Submitted)*

British rock giants will headline a major UK music festival near York in 2027.

Muse has been announced as the first headliner for American Express presents Roundhay Festival at Roundhay Park, Leeds, on Sunday, June 27, 2027.

A special line-up of guests including Nothing But Thieves will join the GRAMMY Award-winning group with further acts yet to be announced.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21692569.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nothing But Thieves will be joining Muse at Roundhay Festival in Leeds in June 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

Jim King, CEO of AEG Presents UK & European Festivals, said: "Following the success of our first year at Roundhay Park, we’re delighted to be returning to Leeds with one of the most incredible live bands in the world.

"Muse’s legendary outdoor performances and unrivalled production values continue to set the benchmark for live music, and we’re thrilled to welcome them to Roundhay for what promises to be a truly spectacular event."

Muse, formed in Teignmouth, Devon in 1994, are known for their epic live shows and a catalogue of arena-filling rock anthems.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21692438.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Muse will headline Roundhay Festival in June 27, 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

Their return to Leeds marks the band’s first appearance in the city in a decade.

Earlier in their career, Muse played smaller venues in Leeds, including the Town & Country Club in 1999, the Duchess of York in 2000, and the Leeds University Union Refectory in 2001.

They have also headlined Leeds Festival multiple times.

The band released their 10th studio album, The Wow! Signal, in June 2026.

The album went straight to Number 1 on the UK Official Albums Chart and was described by NME as their 'best album in 20 years'.

Their 2026 world tour included stops in North America and Europe, with upcoming shows in Manchester and London.

Nothing But Thieves, who will support Muse at Roundhay Park, have risen from their beginnings in Southend to become a global headline act.

The band achieved their first UK Number One album with Dead Club City in 2023. Their latest album Stray Dogs was released in September.

The Roundhay Festival debuted in July 2026, bringing major artists like Pitbull and Lewis Capaldi to the city.

It aims to build on Roundhay Park’s legacy as a venue for historic live performances by artists such as Madonna, The Rolling Stones, Michael Jackson, and Genesis.

Tickets will go on general sale on Friday, November 13, at 10am via the festival website www.roundhayfestival.com.

An American Express pre-sale will begin on Monday, November 9, followed by artist and AEG pre-sales on November 11 and 12 respectively.

The full line-up for the 2027 edition of the festival will be announced in the coming months.
