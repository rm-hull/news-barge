A man in his 20s has appeared in court accused of attempted murder after a 'stab attack' in County Durham.

Travis Lally, 21, of Sandgate, Shotton Colliery, appeared in the dock at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates' Count this morning (November 9) charged with attempted murder.

During the brief appearance, the 21-year-old did not enter a plea.

He was remanded in custody until his next appearance on December 7, at Durham Crown Court, where he will appear before a judge.

Police said a woman in her thirties suffered injuries to her arm, head, chest and back.

The woman was taken to hospital for treatment, and her injuries are not thought to be life-threatening.

Pictures show armed police outside a house, while the air ambulance landed near the Greenhills Centre.

![A picture taken at the scene shows armed police outside a house in Wheatley Hill , with several police vehicles also in attendance](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689532.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A picture taken at the scene shows armed police outside a house in Wheatley Hill , with several police vehicles also in attendance *(Image: JONATHAN OSBORNE)*

One witness described the village as being “at a standstill”.

A North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) spokesperson said: “We were called to an incident at a private address in Wheatley Hill, County Durham on Friday, November 6 shortly before 3pm.

“We dispatched one ambulance crew and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service.

“One patient was taken to hospital by road for further treatment.”

A spokesperson for the Great North Air Ambulance Service added: “Our critical care team was activated at 3.08pm to an incident in Wheatley Hill, County Durham.

“We had a paramedic and doctor on board our aircraft and they arrived on scene at 3.18pm.
