Starting school is one of the biggest milestones in a child’s life, and The Northern Echo is celebrating the occasion with a special keepsake packed with pictures of new starters from across County Durham and Darlington.

The Northern Echo’s **First Class 2026** supplement will be published on**Tuesday, November 10**, featuring photographs of children taking those exciting first steps into school life.

From Darlington and Bishop Auckland to Newton Aycliffe, Crook, Barnard Castle and communities across County Durham, youngsters have been getting used to their classrooms, making new friends and beginning a journey that families will remember for years to come.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689028.jpg?type=mds-article-620)First Class pull-out in your Northern Echo *(Image: Northern Echo)*

Our First Class supplement has been created as a lasting souvenir for parents, grandparents and other family members, capturing a special moment that can all too quickly pass.

For the children themselves, it will also provide something to look back on in years to come, remembering classmates and the start of their school days.

There are **28 schools** included in the list of participating schools provided for the supplement, with children from primary, infant and preparatory schools taking part.
