A row has broken out after York’s council leader accused the opposition Liberal Democrats of spreading “fake news and complete rubbish”.

Labour’s Claire Douglas has written to the chair of the York Lib Dems making the claim.

But the Lib Dems leader, Cllr Nigel Ayre, has hit back, saying it’s “a sad and pathetic attempt by Labour to manufacture a row”.

The dispute centres on Lib Dem leaflets discussing ward boundary changes and school catchment areas.

A review by the independent Local Government Boundary Commission for England has resulted in significant changes to York’s ward boundaries and the creation of a number of new wards, from May 2027.

The outcome “has prompted York Liberal Democrats to falsely claim the new boundaries will affect school catchment areas, and even house prices”, the Labour Group says.

## ‘Struggle with the truth’

Cllr Douglas said: “It’s really important this misinformation is formally challenged; it’s fake news and complete rubbish.

“We know York Liberal Democrats often struggle when it comes to being truthful with the public but this is a particularly bad example.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/york-council-elections-2023-claire-douglas-speech.jpg)

“Senior council officers have confirmed in writing to me that school catchment areas remain completely unaffected by ward boundary changes so there’s not much more to be said.

“York Liberal Democrat Party needs to stop the scaremongering and start to prioritise honesty in how it communicates with the public. We all know how much misinformation there is out there at the moment and we don’t need local political parties adding to it.

“It’s always important and especially as we approach an election that the public can trust the information they receive from political parties. We will challenge and expose each and every example we see where this isn’t the case”.

## ‘Wild accusations’

Cllr Nigel Ayre, leader of the York Liberal Democrat Group, described the allegations as “a sad and pathetic attempt by Labour to manufacture a row and distract residents from the reality of what is happening to our council”.

He said: “Months after the boundary review was published, Labour are suddenly lashing out with wild accusations against councillors who were cleared of any wrongdoing by the council.

“What’s more, the independent boundary commission considered the evidence submitted by our councillors and local residents, agreeing entirely with us on this matter.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/york-councillor-nigel-ayre-citadel-joe-cooper.jpg)

“Let’s be clear about what our councillors actually said. They raised a perfectly legitimate question about the potential longer-term implications of the boundary changes for school catchment areas.

“They did not claim that catchment areas would automatically change overnight.

“Indeed, Labour’s own submission to the boundary commission referred to school catchment areas no fewer than 18 times when arguing how communities across York should be represented.

“Apparently, catchment areas are important evidence when Labour wants to make an argument, but suddenly become completely irrelevant when somebody else raises them.

“Instead of addressing the substance of those concerns, Labour have chosen to launch personal attacks and throw around accusations of dishonesty.”
