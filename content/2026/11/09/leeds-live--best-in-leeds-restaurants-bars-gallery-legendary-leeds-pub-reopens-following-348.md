# Legendary Leeds pub reopens following a major makeover costing £230,000

A legendary Leeds pub is back in business again following a major makeover.

The Mustard Pot, in Chapel Allerton, has enjoyed an exciting refurbishment. The popular reopening as a ‘Grandstand’ sports pub, following an investment of £230,000.

The refurbishment included the installation of market-leading technology, including multiple massive screens, a state-of-the-art soundsystem and flexible tiered seating.

The beer garden has also been refurbished with new TV screens added. The Mustard Pot has also been decorated with local sports memorabilia.

The pub will show all key sporting fixtures and events - including football, rugby, cricket, tennis, F1, darts and horse racing. The new Grandstand concept has been introduced after industry research revealed that 8.5m consumers have watched sports games at the pub, and 86 per cent of sports fans say that they watch games at sports pubs due to a livelier atmosphere, better screens, sound, and connecting with friends and family.

Marstons operations manager, Zoe Eastwood, said: “We will have some amazing new technology, food and drink offers and events, and we want as many local people to be featured on our walls as possible. We can’t wait to welcome all our regulars and some new faces in, to see the new and improved the Mustard Pot."

The Mustard Pot reopened on Wednesday, November 4.
