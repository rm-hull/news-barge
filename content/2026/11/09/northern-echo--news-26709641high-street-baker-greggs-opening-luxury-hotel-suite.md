High-street baker Greggs is opening a luxury hotel suite with a “ring for a sausage roll” concierge service.

The permanent suite will open at five-star Hotel Gotham in Newcastle on Thursday, November 12, in Greggs’ midnight-blue and gold colours.

Guests will be able to use a dedicated rotary phone to request freshly baked Greggs favourites delivered directly to the room on a silver platter.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21685053.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The nation's favourite bakery has teamed up with Hotel Gotham Newcastle to launch a permanent suite opening from Thursday, November 12. *(Image: SEAN ELLIOT/ GREGGS/ PA)*

Those staying in the first 96 days will receive limited-edition merchandise to take home, including bespoke dressing gowns, slippers and eye masks, in “homage to the 96 layers of Greggs’ iconic puff pastry”.

The ‘Greggs Suite’ will sleep two guests and will be available to book year-round.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21685054.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The nation's favourite bakery has teamed up with Hotel Gotham Newcastle to launch a permanent suite opening from Thursday, November 12. *(Image: SEAN ELLIOT/ GREGGS/ PA)*

A share of each stay’s proceeds will go to the Greggs Foundation to support local communities and schools through its Feeding Brighter Futures programme.

Greggs said the collaboration brought “an unexpected twist on top-tier hospitality”, combining Hotel Gotham’s elegance with Greggs’ high-street character.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21685052.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Custom Greggs branded velvet dressing gowns, slippers and eye masks. *(Image: SEAN ELLIOT/ GREGGS/ PA)*

Zoe Harris, customer director at Greggs, said: “We love finding unexpected ways to bring joy to our customers and partnering with Hotel Gotham has allowed us to create something that’s both luxurious and delightfully Greggs.

“The suite offers a truly unique experience that could only happen in our hometown of Newcastle. We can’t wait to welcome guests through the doors and hear what they think.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21685056.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The nation's favourite bakery has teamed up with Hotel Gotham Newcastle to launch a permanent suite opening from Thursday, November 12. *(Image: SEAN ELLIOT/ GREGGS/ PA)*

Hotel Gotham’s general manager Chris Thompson said: “Hotel Gotham Newcastle was created to celebrate the bold spirit of this incredible city, so a collaboration with one of Newcastle’s most recognisable brands feels like a natural fit.

“The Greggs Suite brings together our signature Art Deco glamour with Greggs’ unmistakable sense of fun, creating a stay that feels uniquely Newcastle.

“We’re delighted to welcome it as part of the next chapter of Hotel Gotham Newcastle.”

Reservations open on the Hotel Gotham website on Monday.

Greggs is also offering two people the chance to win an overnight stay through a giveaway on its app every three months for the next two years.
