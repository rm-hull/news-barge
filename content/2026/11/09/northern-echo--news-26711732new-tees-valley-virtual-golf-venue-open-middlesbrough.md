A new indoor golf venue has opened in Middlesbrough, offering players the chance to tee off on some of the world’s best courses.

Tees Valley Virtual Golf opened on Tuesday, November 3, on Mandale Business Park, giving golfers the chance to practise their swing, play virtual rounds with friends or try their hand at the sport.

The venue has a private, single-bay TrackMan simulator with a 5.5m by 3.5m hitting screen described as the largest in the North - creating a more immersive experience for players.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689692.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tees Valley Virtual Golf has moved into Unit 15 on Cannon Street, Middlesbrough *(Image: Google)*

With just one bay, groups have the space to themselves during their session, whatever the weather outside.

TrackMan technology allows experienced golfers to work on their game, while newcomers can learn to play and groups of friends can take on virtual courses together.

Sessions can be booked for between one and six hours, costing £20 an hour before 5pm on weekdays and £25 from 5pm and at weekends.

The price covers one player, with each additional guest costing £5 an hour and up to four people allowed in the bay.

The bay is available from 6am to 11pm every day, giving golfers the option of an early session before work or a later round in the evening.

Monthly memberships are also available for regular players, ranging from Bronze at £33.25 a month for two hours to Founders at £125 a month for seven hours.

Keen members can also enter competitions including the Tees Valley Virtual Golf Open and the four majors, while corporate members have access to a corporate league.

The venue hopes to offer something for seasoned golfers, beginners and friends looking for a different way to play in Middlesbrough.
