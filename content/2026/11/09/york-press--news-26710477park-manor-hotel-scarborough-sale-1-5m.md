## North Yorkshire hotel up for £1.5m after 35 years in same family

Park Manor Hotel in Scarborough is on the market for offers over £1.5million after 35 years in the same family. *(Image: Colliers)*

A long-established hotel in North Yorkshire has gone up for sale after more than 35 years of family ownership.

Park Manor Hotel in Scarborough is on the market for offers over £1.5 million, as the owners look to pursue new business ventures.

The 43-bedroom hotel, on Northstead Manor Drive, overlooks the popular Peasholm Park and is close to many of the seaside town’s main attractions.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21687258.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Park Manor Hotel in Scarborough is on the market for offers over £1.5million after 35 years in the same family. *(Image: Colliers)*

Helen Marsden, owner of Park Manor Hotel, said: "Park Manor has been in our family for over three decades and has been a hugely rewarding business to own and operate.

"We’ve continually invested in the property and built a loyal customer base, and the hotel is now very well positioned for the future.

"The decision to sell is simply to allow our family to move on to other opportunities, and we hope a new owner will enjoy taking the business forward."

The sale is being managed by the Hotels team at Colliers.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21687554.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Park Manor Hotel overlooks Peasholm Park, pictured. *(Image: (Image: Tripadvisor))*

Robert Smithson, director at Colliers, said: "Park Manor is a hotel with a strong reputation in Scarborough and a loyal customer base built up over many years.

"It offers excellent public areas, leisure facilities and a proven trading history, all in a location that continues to attract visitors year-round.

"It’s a rare opportunity to acquire a family-owned hotel of this scale in such a well-established part of the town."

The hotel features a large reception, a conservatory-style lounge and bar seating around 70 guests, a games room, and a formal dining room with space for 70 diners.

It also includes the Manor Room, which can host private events for up to 36 people, and leisure facilities such as a swimming pool, jacuzzi, steam room, and changing rooms.

These amenities are used by both hotel guests and local members, supporting the hotel’s events business, which includes weddings, private functions, and funeral teas.

Park Manor Hotel has seen steady financial performance, with net turnover reaching £941,762 for the year ending March 2025.

Scarborough remains one of the UK’s most popular seaside towns, drawing visitors to its beaches, historic harbour, Victorian esplanade, and entertainment venues such as the Scarborough Open Air Theatre, which can hold up to 8,000 people.

The hotel’s location supports strong seasonal demand, particularly during major events, and attracts guests exploring the wider North Yorkshire coast and destinations such as Whitby, Filey, Bridlington, Pickering, Helmsley, and the North York Moors.

Mr Smithson said: "It’s a rare opportunity to acquire a family-owned hotel of this scale in such a well-established part of the town."
