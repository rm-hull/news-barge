Shoppers will have the chance to enjoy an evening of festive browsing, food and drink as businesses across Northallerton prepare to stay open later for a special Christmas shopping event.

A host of retailers, cafés, bars and independent businesses are set to take part in Northallerton's late-night shopping evening on Friday (November 13), giving residents extra time to pick up gifts, enjoy seasonal refreshments and support local businesses in the run-up to Christmas.

The event, promoted by Love Northallerton, will run from 5pm until 8pm, transforming the town centre into a festive destination after the usual close of business.

Participating businesses are expected to remain open until 8pm, allowing shoppers to browse at a more relaxed pace and make the most of the town's mix of national names and independent retailers.

Among those taking part is Waterstones Northallerton, which has confirmed it will be open until 8pm and offering festive refreshments throughout the evening.

Barkers Northallerton has also confirmed it will remain open until 8pm.

Organisers are encouraging people to make a night of it, whether they're searching for Christmas presents, enjoying dinner and drinks in town or simply soaking up the seasonal atmosphere.

In a post on social media, Love Northallerton said: "Fancy a little extra Christmas magic? Join us on Friday 13th November for a festive evening of late-night shopping in Northallerton.

"Whether you're ticking off your Christmas shopping list, searching for that perfect gift or simply fancy a festive evening out, there's plenty to discover.

"From independent shops and fantastic local businesses to great food, drink and a little Christmas sparkle, it's the perfect excuse to get out, explore and support local."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21688755.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Barkers department store in Northallerton is among the businesses taking part in the town's late-night Christmas shopping event. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

The initiative comes as town centres across the region launch festive events aimed at encouraging residents to shop local in the run-up to Christmas.

Visitors can expect a mixture of shopping, refreshments and evening hospitality, with businesses across the town inviting customers through their doors for an extended trading session ahead of the busy festive season.

### Businesses taking part

The businesses currently advertised for the late-night shopping event are:
