## Ninja PREPi: 30-second review

The Ninja PREPi food processor has power that belies its looks. The 5-in-1 model I tested comes with three different blades for five different food processing options. The included blades include: a chopping/puree blade, a crinkle cut disc, and a reversible slide/shred disc. There is also a six-in-one model that includes a dicing disc, or you can purchase the dicing disc separately if you prefer.

The Ninja PREPi comes with two prep bowls and lids, a five-cup size and a seven-cup size. This allows you to do a big food prep ahead of time and then pop it in the fridge until you need it, without much in the way of extra dishes to wash.

This machine handled all of the tasks we threw at it with ease, including slicing a large bag of potatoes into thin, uniform slices in no time. The one exception was an attempted smoothie, but after all, this isn't a blender.

Like most food processors, the Ninja PREPi has a lot of parts that will need to be taken apart and cleaned after use. Luckily, they can all be cleaned on the top rack of your dishwasher (except for the motor which wouldn't ever need more than a swipe with a damp cloth anyway.)

## Ninja PREPi: design and features

* **Three multi-use blades**
* **Three-part food pusher**
* **Two bowls with lids for prep and storage**

The Ninja PREPi's clever design makes it pretty easy to use. Choose either the larger (seven-cup) or smaller (five-cup) prep bowl, place the spindle inside, and then choose your blade. The blade that sits at the bottom of the bowl is for both chopping and pureeing.

![Ninja PREPi chop/puree blade](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/hc2HQ8Q5CiKqupwae7VXpE-1200-80.jpg)

There are also two disc-style blades that sit at the top of the bowl. One disc is for crinkle cutting, the other disc is reversible and can be used for slicing or shredding.

![Ninja PREPi disc blades](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/FdczzrJqQx2FmurJvuGxdL-1200-80.jpg)

Once you've installed your blade of choice, click the feed chute lid into the bowl and then slide the motor pod into its spot on top of the lid. Turn the dial to your desired mode and press start. It's pretty easy and intuitive to use.

![Ninja PREPi lid assembly](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Vte3MnsYEH2oapxbYM75Lb-1200-80.jpg)
![Ninja PREPi lid assembled](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/pwhwgtC6oSvi9KatgZgy3j-1200-80.jpg)

![Ninja PREPi lid assembled](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/pwhwgtC6oSvi9KatgZgy3j-2560-80.jpg)

The feed chute lid has a three-part pusher so that you can put in anything from a carrot to a potato and it will be held in place upright for proper slicing or shredding.

Cleanup is pretty easy, just take all of the parts apart. Put the motor pod aside, it doesn't contact food and won't need to be cleaned at all unless you splash something on it, in which case, just wipe it with a very slightly dampened cloth. All of the other parts can be washed on the top rack of the dishwasher (skip the heat dry cycle) or hand-washed very carefully. The blades are sharp!

I couldn't figure out a perfectly neat way to nest and store the Ninja PREPi. I tried a couple of different ways to try and minimize storage space, with the blades all safely enclosed inside the lidded prep bowls.

## Buy it if

**You don't want to dread taking out your food processor** It's easy to put together, use, take apart, and clean. Just pop all the parts in the dishwasher when you're done. I never thought I'd describe a powerful food processor as 'cute' but the Ninja PREPi is just that.**You do food prep** Do you like to prep large batches of food ahead of time? Ninja PREPi makes it easy, since its two prep bowls have lids and double as storage bowls. For example, you could chop up a bunch of veggies in one go and use them for days in your salads, stir-frys, curries, and soups.**You like to entertain**

You could easily whip up a party-sized batch of salsa, hummus, or other favorite dip and put it right out in either the five-cup or seven-cup prep bowl. Or put the lid on and bring it to a potluck.

## Don't buy it if

**You want a blender/processor combo** This a food processor, full stop. It does offer a lot of flexibility with multi-purpose blades that cover chopping, pureeing, slicing, shredding, and crinkle cuts. Plus the prep bowls that serve as storage. But it's not a blender.**You don't have the space** While it's not overly large, you must have some space designated for the Ninja PREPi motor pod, feed chute lid, three blades, and two large prep bowls.**You don't want to spend the money**

You can certainly find cheaper food processors from other brands you know and love. You may have to give up some features, but for basic food processing, you don't have to spend a lot.
