**UPDATE: A spokesperson for Humberside Police said: "Following our earlier appeals, we are pleased to confirm that the missing 10-year-old and the missing 7-year-old from Hull have both been located."We would like to thank everyone who assisted in the search, shared our appeals and called in with sightings."**

AN extensive search has begun to find a missing seven-year-old girl from Yorkshire.

She was last seen in Noodle Hill Way, near the junction with Wayne Road, in Brandsholme, Hull – close to Beverley – at around 12.30pm today (Sunday, November 8).

She is around four feet tall and was last seen wearing a white-striped playsuit.

She is also believed to be barefoot, Humberside Police has said.

If you see her, please call the force on 101, quoting log 235 08/11/2026.
