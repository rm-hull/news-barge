A former hotel is set to be transformed into a new pizza restaurant following approval for a change of use.

The George Hotel in Malton will become the new Da Elia pizza restaurant, offering takeaway and delivery services, along with a lounge bar and outdoor garden facilities.

A spokesperson for Pete Midgley Design said: "Great news, Pete Midgley Design is proud to announce that the change of use for the former George hotel in Malton has been granted approval to become the new Da Elia pizza restaurant."

"The restaurant will also include takeaway and delivery options , a lounge bar and outdoor garden facilities continuing their fantastic work and quality from the Castlegate premises .

"Wishing the award winning Da Elia restaurant every success , bringing a new venture to the town of Malton .

"The pictures are proposed images of the conversion of the George Hotel."

Da Elia was recently shortlisted in the Best Restaurant/Food Business category in the UK Small Business Awards.

Originally set up as a food van the business moved to Castlegate in April 2024.
