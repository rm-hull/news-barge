A driver who fatally rammed his 4x4 into a 23-year-old man, impaling him on metal shelving outside a garage, told a court he only wanted to scare him.

Ashton Anderson faces murder charges after driving a Nissan X-Trail into Daniel Ambler on January 15, causing fatal neck injuries.

Mr Ambler was pronounced dead after the crash at the Esso Asda Express station in Seaham.

Barry Anderson, 52, and his sons Ashton, 20, and Logan, 19, deny murder, as do 18-year-olds Tyler Leadbetter and Ajay Henry.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20435374.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Daniel Ambler *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

Logan Anderson has admitted affray after a feud between the Anderson and Ambler families turned violent.

Barry Anderson declined to give evidence, while his 20-year-old son told jurors there had been several incidents between his family and the victim's.

Ashton’s barrister, Imran Shafi KC, questioned him about the fatal incident on the garage forecourt and his state of mind.

Anderson said he heard Mr Ambler shout, ‘see what f\*\*\*\*\*\* happens’, after Logan Anderson assaulted him moments earlier, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 20-year-old said he got back into the 4x4 and followed Mr Ambler to "scare him" away from the garage, where his cousin worked.

The defendant admitted he had misjudged his speed and was travelling faster than he realised as he drove on to the forecourt.

Asked how he felt after hitting Mr Ambler, Ashton said he had been shaken by what happened.

The barrister asked: “How do you feel knowing that you killed Daniel?” Anderson replied: “I feel terrible.”

Jurors saw CCTV footage prosecutors say shows the five murder defendants hunting down and attacking Mr Ambler and his friend Reece Wilkin.

The attack followed a feud that began when Barry Anderson was stabbed last August.

Barry Anderson, 52, of Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland; Ashton Anderson, 20, of Ryton Crescent, Seaham; and Barry Logan Anderson, 19, of Nursery View, Shotton Bank, face murder and violent-disorder charges.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Esso New Seaham Service Station, in Stockton Road, Seaham, the scene of Daniel Ambler's death *(Image: Sarah Caldecott (The Northern Echo))*

Ajay Henry, of Melrose Crescent, Seaham, and Tyler Leadbetter, of Elgin Avenue, Seaham, were both 17 when charged with Daniel Ambler’s murder.

Ashton Anderson has admitted violent disorder; all other charges are denied.

The day after Mr Ambler died, messages in a Snapchat group warned that police would be looking at everyone in Deneside, Seaham.

The trial at Teesside Crown Court is expected to last up to five weeks.

The court heard that Mr Ambler's brother David and his friend Jed Wilson had arranged to fight Barry Anderson, who was stabbed in the side during the struggle.

Messages and threats continued between members of both families in the months before Mr Ambler's death, including one telling Logan Anderson on January 5 that Daniel wanted to "sort things out like men".

A witness also told jurors he saw a person in dark clothing apparently striking Mr Ambler with an object resembling a crowbar, although he could not confirm every blow made contact.
