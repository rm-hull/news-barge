The PlayStation Store is now joining in on the Black Friday fun with discounts of up to 75% on thousands of PS5 and PS4 games. I've searched through the sale to pick out a small selection of my favorites, including record-low prices on new releases, Game of the Year contenders, and highly-rated hits for super-cheap that I'd strongly recommend if you haven't tried them yet.

For starters, there's IO Interactive's fantastic spy thriller *007 First Light*, which does an incredible job of combining the linear action of the* Uncharted*series with the reactive and expansive sandboxes of the* Hitman* games.

I also have to mention *Control Resonant*, which has blown me away with its world design and creativity. The action is a little rough around the edges, but I can look past that when every other moment I'm wowed by some mind-bending new environment or ingenious set piece that establishes it as one of the most unique games this year.
