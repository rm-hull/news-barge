A ‘spectacular’ nine-bed mansion complete with its own bar, gym and almost four acres of land has gone on the market for £3.95 million.

Four Acres, at Hartford Bridge, near Morpeth in Northumberland, overlooks Plessey Woods and combines contemporary architecture with extensive entertainment and leisure space, estate agents say.

The substantial family home is being marketed by Bradley Hall.

Set behind electric gates and approached along a sweeping private driveway, the property features Burlington Lakeland stone, cedar cladding and large expanses of glass.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690155.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

Inside, the home has dramatic double and triple-height spaces, a bespoke staircase and extensive glazing designed to make the most of views across the gardens and surrounding woodland.

At the heart of the property is a large open-plan kitchen and breakfast room, centred around a contemporary island and fitted with integrated appliances.

Adjoining living and dining areas offer further space for entertaining, while one of the home's standout features is a dedicated entertainment room and private bar.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690156.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

There is also a substantial home gym with a shower room, as well as a utility room, boot room and plant room.

The house has nine bedrooms, including a principal suite with a dressing room, en-suite bathroom and balcony access.

The dressing room includes extensive fitted storage and a central island, while bathrooms have granite and marble finishes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690158.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

A substantial family room on the upper floor could be used as a cinema, games room or additional lounge.

The property also features underfloor heating across its ground and first floors, air conditioning in the principal bedrooms, smart lighting and a professionally installed network supporting high-speed fibre broadband.

Outside, the main house opens onto a large south-facing paved terrace, while a landscaped sunken seating area is arranged around a central fire feature.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690159.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690160.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge*(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

A annex-cum-garage extends to around 1,478 sq ft and includes bedrooms, a sitting room and bathroom facilities.

Bradley Hall says the additional accommodation could potentially be used by guests or extended family, subject to any necessary consents.

Lauren Gill, branch manager at Bradley Hall's Durham office, said: “Four Acres is a truly distinctive property, combining contemporary architecture and exceptional interiors with the privacy and tranquillity of its woodland setting.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690153.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*

“The scale of the home, its bespoke entertaining spaces and extensive grounds make it an impressive offering for buyers seeking a substantial family residence in the North East, with the added appeal of being surrounded by nature while remaining within easy reach of the region’s key towns and cities.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690154.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four Acres, Hartford Bridge *(Image: BRADLEY HALL)*
