FIREFIGHTERS have been called to a motorbike blaze in York which is believed to have been started deliberately.

The incident took place in Holgate Road at 7.45pm on Sunday (November 8).




North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from York found the motorbike “well alight” upon arrival.

The cause, the service said, is believed to have been deliberate.
