A McDonald's restaurant has been granted permission to open from 6.30am every day after a planning officer approved extended operating hours.

The restaurant and takeaway on Coutances Way, Burley-in-Wharfedale, will now be able to begin serving customers from 6.30am seven days a week, instead of 8am, while retaining its existing midnight closing time.

Applicant Mr Pritpal Singh applied to Bradford Council to vary a condition attached to the site's planning permission, allowing earlier opening hours. The application was determined under delegated powers by a planning officer rather than councillors.

The site opened as a McDonald's on October 2, 2024, and was previously occupied by restaurant businesses including Kashmiri Aroma and, before that, Little Chef.

During a public consultation, the council received four responses. Three supported the proposal, saying the earlier opening time was reasonable, could create employment opportunities and would improve breakfast options in the area. One objection was submitted, raising concerns about litter, traffic congestion and possible impacts on nearby residents.

However, planning officers concluded the proposal would not result in unacceptable harm. A noise assessment submitted with the application found sound from kitchen equipment operating between 6.30am and 7am would remain below recorded background noise levels in the area.

Officers also noted that any increase in vehicle movements was unlikely to be noticeable given the restaurant's location alongside the busy A65 Coutances Way, a major commuter route. Existing lighting arrangements will remain unchanged, and the council's Environmental Health team raised no objections.

Approving the application, officers said the revised hours would enable the restaurant to extend its breakfast service while having only a limited impact on neighbouring properties.

In their decision report, they concluded: "The proposed hours extension is a modest change and one that can be achieved without resulting in a loss of amenity to adjoining occupiers."

Adding: "The proposal will result in a wider choice for customers and could potentially result in the creation of additional jobs, although this is not expressly stated in the application, both of which are welcomed."
