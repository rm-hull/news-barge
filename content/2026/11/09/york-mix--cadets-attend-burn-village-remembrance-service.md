For the fourth year running, cadets from the Combined Cadet Force at the Read School, Drax joined residents and visitors in Burn on Sunday November 8 to mark Remembrance.

The cadets, accompanied by Flight Lieutenant Annette Oliver, OC RAF Section and family members, laid the wreath honouring the Royal Canadian Air Force 431 ‘Iroquois’ (The Snowbirds) Squadron at the service which was led by Martin Pascoe on behalf of Burn Methodist Chapel.

Wreaths were also laid and memorial crosses left for RAF 578 Squadron and others by members of Burn Chapel, Burn Parish Council, the families of former RAF veterans and servicemen and women.

Burn, just south of Selby, is home to Burn airfield which saw members of the Royal Air Force and Royal Canadian Air Force fly out on bomber missions over enemy territory during the Second World War.

Burn Parish Council chair, Alice Houlden, said: “Burn village thanks the Read School Combined Cadet Force and families for joining us at the Remembrance observations once again.

“Their presence adds a poignant sense of continuity and respect for those who gave so much.”
