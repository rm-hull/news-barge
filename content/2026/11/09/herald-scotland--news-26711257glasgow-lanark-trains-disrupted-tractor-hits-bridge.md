Rail services travelling into Glasgow have been disrupted after a tractor collided with a bridge.

Scotrail said that trains running between Glasgow and Lanark are being cancelled, delayed or revised after the incident this morning.

Network Rail confirmed it had received reports of a tractor hitting the bridge, which carries the railway over a road in the area.

A response unit has been dispatched to assess the damage and determine whether it is safe for trains to continue running.

Passengers have been warned to expect disruption while inspections are carried out.

ScotRail said its Glasgow Central to Lanark services are affected by the incident, with some trains cancelled and others subject to delays or alterations.

However, rail officials said services on the West Coast Main Line are continuing to operate normally.
