Christmas lights switch-on season has arrived, with festive events taking place across the North East throughout November.

From major celebrations in Newcastle, Sunderland and Middlesbrough to community events in villages and market towns, many switch-ons will feature fireworks, live entertainment, festive markets, Santa appearances and family activities.

Here's our area-by-area guide to the confirmed events announced so far.

### Teesside

November 21- Redcar Lights Switch-On, Redcar High Street.

Event runs from 12pm to 5.30pm and includes fairground rides, an inflatable curling rink, walkabout characters, a giant snow globe, Santa visits, festive crafts, street food and live entertainment.

November 22- Middlesbrough Christmas Lights Switch-On, Centre Square.

Event begins at 5.30pm, with festive entertainment and fireworks planned.

November 29- Yarm Christmas Lights Switch-On, Yarm High Street.

Timings are yet to be confirmed.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20208464.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

### Darlington and South Durham

November 15- Darlington Christmas Lights Switch-On, Market Square.

Event begins at 4pm, with the switch-on just after 5.30pm. Free entertainment, live music, and special guest appearances are planned.

November 16 and November 17-Darlington Sensory Christmas Lights Event, town centre.

Event begins at 4pm, with the switch on at 5.30pm. Christmas lights sessions for children and adults with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), offering a quieter festive experience away from the crowds.

November 27-Newton Aycliffe Christmas Lights Switch-On, Aycliffe Town Centre.

Event runs 4pm to 7.30pm, featuring a Christmas Indoor Market, Festive Street Food Market and appearances from Santa.

November 27- Ferryhill Annual Christmas Fayre, Market and Christmas Lights Switch-On, Ferryhill town centre.

Festivities begin at 1pm, with the switch-on at 4:30pm

November 27, times not yet confirmed:

* Barnard Castle Christmas Lights Switch-On, Market Place.
* Chilton Christmas Lights Switch-On, Chilton town centre.

November 28- Middleton-in-Teesdale Christmas Lights Switch-On, Market Place. Event begins approximately 4.20pm.

November 28-Bowes Christmas Lights Switch-On, Bowes village centre. Event begins at 6pm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651514.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Ferryhill Town Council)*

### Durham City and North Durham

November 20- South Causey Christmas Lights Switch-On, South Causey Inn, Stanley.

Event begins 6pm, with live music, appearances from Santa and festive snacks.

November 28- Lanchester Christmas Lights Switch-On, Village Green, Lanchester.

Event begins at 5.30pm, with live music.

November 28-Spennymoor Christmas Festival and Lights Switch-On, Spennymoor town centre.

Event begins at 6pm.

November 29- Durham Christmas Sparkle and Lights Switch-On, Market Place, Durham City.

Festivities begin at 5pm, alongside live music, street performers and seasonal attractions.

Former Durham City police inspector Dave Clarke and City of Durham Parish Clerk Adam Shanley will turn on the lights alongside Durham Mayor, Councillor Amanda Hopgood.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/16101294.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Newsquest)*

### Sunderland and East Durham

November 19- Sunderland Christmas Lights Switch-On, Keel Square, Sunderland city centre.

Event runs from 6pm to 8pm, with live entertainment, family activities and appearances from the Sunderland Empire pantomime cast.

November 27- Seaham Christmas Lights Switch-On, Terrace Green, Seaham seafront.

Festivities run from 5.30pm to 8pm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20156987.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Dalton Park)*

### Tyne and Wear, Tyneside and Northumberland

November 12- Newcastle Christmas Lights Switch-On, Grey's Monument, Newcastle city centre.

Newcastle's city-centre switch-on includes live entertainment around Grey's Monument from 6pm before the Christmas lights are switched on at 7pm.

November 14- Morpeth Christmas Lights Switch-On, Morpeth town centre.

Event begins at 5pm.

November 15- Whitley Bay Christmas Lights, Spanish City Plaza, Whitley Bay.

Event begins at 5pm.

November 20- Hexham Christmas Lights Switch-On, Hexham town centre.

Timings are still to be confirmed.

November 21- Blyth Christmas Lights Switch-On, Market Place, Blyth.

Event runs from 10am to 6pm.

November 22- Amble Christmas Lights Switch-On, Amble town centre.

Festivities run from 5pm to 7pm.

November 27- Prudhoe Christmas Lights Switch-On, Prudhoe town centre.

Timings are still to be confirmed.

November 28- Bedlington Christmas Lights Switch-On, Front Street, Bedlington.

Christmas entertainment is expected in the town centre from 5pm before Bedlington's festive lights are illuminated.

November 28- Rothbury Christmas Lights Switch-On, Rothbury village centre.

Timings are still to be confirmed.

November 28- Bamburgh Christmas Lights Switch-On, Bamburgh village centre.

Event begins 6.15pm.

As Christmas approaches and festive lights begin to twinkle across town squares, village greens and city centres, communities across the North East are preparing to come together for one of the most magical traditions of the year.

Whether you're hoping to catch a spectacular fireworks display, meet Santa, browse festive markets or simply enjoy the countdown to the big switch-on, there's no shortage of Christmas cheer on offer.

Be sure to wrap up warm, grab a hot chocolate and check organisers' websites or social media pages before travelling, as timings and activities can occasionally change at short notice.
