Work has started on a 75-home development in Erskine, including 18 affordable apartments designed for older residents.

Named Blyth Gardens, the Cala Homes (West) development will comprise 57 private properties and 18 affordable homes, following final planning approval and the conclusion of a Section 75 Agreement with Renfrewshire Council.

The private homes will include three-bedroom terraced properties, three- and four-bedroom townhouses, and larger four- and five-bedroom detached houses.

The affordable apartments will provide elderly amenity housing, intended to help older residents live independently with access to appropriate support.

The development is next to Cala’s existing Gilchrist Gardens neighbourhood, where around 40 homes have been delivered each year over the past five years.

Plans also include wildlife-friendly features such as hedgehog-friendly fencing and invertebrate bricks to support bees and other insects. Green space will connect the site with nearby Sandieland Woodland, and a children’s play area is planned.

Located on the eastern edge of Erskine, the development has access to the Erskine Bridge and the M8, while Glasgow Airport is around five miles away.

Groundworks are now underway, with the first residents expected to move in from summer 2027.

Graham McNeil, land director at Cala Homes (West), said: "Securing full consent and starting work at Blyth Gardens is a significant milestone, and one that reflects our continued commitment to delivering much-needed homes in Erskine.

"We have built a strong presence here through Gilchrist Gardens, and we are pleased to be expanding on that with a development that offers genuine variety, from starter homes through to larger family properties.

"Importantly, the affordable homes at Blyth Gardens include specialist amenity apartments designed specifically for older residents.

"These homes directly support Renfrewshire Council's Strategic Housing Investment Plan, which prioritises affordable, energy-efficient housing tailored to the needs of specific groups.

"For older people in particular, having access to accommodation that enables independent living with appropriate support in place makes a real difference."
