A craft brewery that recently shut its Halifax town centre venue has revealed the reason behind the closure.

Vocation Brewery ceased operations on Alexandra Street on Friday alongside its bar on Devonshire Street in Sheffield.

Its flagship bar in Hebden Bridge remains open.

## Closures will enable the brewery to focus on sales

The company has said that the closures were part of a "carefully considered decision" to reallocate resources and investment toward expanding its core beer business across the UK.

A spokesperson for Vocation Brewery said: "The closures have been a difficult, carefully considered decision and will enable us to concentrate resources and investment on building on Vocation’s momentum during the past year.

"We’ve seen double-digit growth in sales volumes across pubs and bars nationwide, while also increasing our market share in shops and supermarkets, from approximately 3.8 per cent to 4.3 per cent.

"Focusing our efforts on these areas is a natural next step in driving long-term, scalable growth."

## Plans to expand core beer range

The brewery says its growth strategy focuses on expanding the premium core beer range it offers and responding to evolving consumer preferences through ongoing product innovation.

Recent investments include additional brewery tanks and a pilot facility to speed up the development and testing of new beers.

The spokesman continued: "We are incredibly grateful to our colleagues for their role in making these bars such popular venues, and for the contribution they’ve made to Vocation’s journey from a small brewery in an old chicken shed in the Yorkshire hills above Hebden Bridge to a nationally recognised beer brand.

"We also appreciate the valued role that pubs and bars play within local communities, and we are incredibly grateful to our customers and the communities in Sheffield and Halifax for the support they have shown us over the years."

The company is working with affected staff to support them through the transition.

## When and where was the brewery founded?

Vocation was founded in 2015 by John Hickling.

The business began in a former chicken shed near Hebden Bridge and has since expanded its operations, producing more than 10 million pints of beer annually for sale across the UK and overseas.

The brewery produces a range of beers, including IPAs, pale ales, stouts and barrel-aged varieties.

Its products are sold through pubs, bars, supermarkets and other retailers across the country.
