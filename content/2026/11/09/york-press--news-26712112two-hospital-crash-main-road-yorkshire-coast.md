Two people were taken to hospital after a crash on a main road on the North Yorkshire coast, police have said.

Officers are appealing for information about the collision on the A170 near Hutton Buscel on Wednesday, October 28.

Police said a silver Ford Fiesta and a white Skoda Fabia were involved in the crash at about 5pm.

A police spokesperson said two people were taken to hospital for treatment.

They urged anyone with information about the crash to contact North Yorkshire Police.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us,” the police spokesperson said.

Anyone with information is asked to email [nick.simpson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nick.simpson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or phone 101.

Crimestoppers can be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.
