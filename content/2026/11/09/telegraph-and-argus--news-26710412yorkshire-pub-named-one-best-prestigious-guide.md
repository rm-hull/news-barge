A ‘wonderful’ pub found next to the North York Moors National Park has been named as one of the best in the UK.

Every year, the Good Food Guide releases a list of the 100 best pubs across the UK in partnership with Timothy Taylor’s.

The Abbey Inn in Byland made it onto the list and has been described by the guide as having "seriously appealing modern pub food in a dreamy setting".

## What is The Abbey Inn like?

The Abbey Inn can be found in a historic building that has been "transformed into a cosy, relaxed country pub" that overlooks the ruins of Byland Abbey.

The guide said the inside of the pub is "pure, cosy magic" with "flickering fires" making it a great spot for the autumn.

Food at the pub is made using ingredients from the Abbey Inn’s nearby family farm and garden as well as independent suppliers in the local area.

You can enjoy a range of dishes from simple sausage and mash to confit Whitby Bay trout and BBQ fallow deer haunch.

If you visit on a Sunday, you can enjoy a roast choosing between beef, pork belly, a potato and mushroom wellington, and hoggett.

All of the roasts come with seasonal vegetables, roast potatoes, a Yorkshire pudding, and gravy with sides of broccoli and cauliflower cheese and braised cabbage available to add onto the side.

In terms of the drinks on offer, there are a range of options such as pints of real ale to seasonal cocktails and an extensive wine list.
