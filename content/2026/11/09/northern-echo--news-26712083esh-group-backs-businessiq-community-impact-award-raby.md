One of the North East’s leading businesses is backing the BUSINESSiQ Awards at Raby Castle.

Esh Group will be presenting the award for Community Impact at Raby, with Darush Dodds, Director of Corporate Affairs & Social Value telling us: “At Esh, we believe that successful businesses have a responsibility to create opportunities, strengthen communities and leave a positive legacy.

"That's why supporting the BUSINESSiQ Awards was a natural fit for us, particularly the Community Impact Award."

There are now only two categories left for sponsors to snap up for April 29, where the castle will provide a breathtaking backdrop to the event taking place just a few yards away at the Riding School and Dutch Barn buildings.

There will be food and entertainment in and around the Barn before and after the awards, which will be held in The Riding School—an 18th-century architectural gem.

Darush said: “The award we are supporting recognises organisations that go beyond commercial success and make a genuine difference to people's lives. As a business that invests heavily in education, employability, community wellbeing and social mobility across the North East, we're passionate about celebrating those who are helping their communities thrive.

“The Community Impact Award shines a spotlight on the individuals and organisations creating lasting change, and we think those stories deserve recognition.”

Darush hopes the awards will help more people to speak out for the region and celebrate its success.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21090152.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Darush Dodds at a BUSINESSiQ Impact event at NETPark *(Image: Chris BOOTH)*

“The North East has a long-standing reputation for innovation, resilience and collaboration, but we're often too modest about shouting about our achievements.

“Celebrating success inspires confidence, encourages investment and showcases the incredible talent we have across the region. It also provides role models for the next generation of entrepreneurs, apprentices, innovators and business leaders.

“Events like the BUSINESSiQ Awards help tell the positive story of the North East, demonstrating that our businesses are not only succeeding commercially but also contributing to stronger communities and a more prosperous regional economy.

“There is a real sense of momentum across the North East. We're seeing significant investment in infrastructure, green technology, advanced manufacturing and digital, creating new opportunities for businesses and communities alike.

“While there are still challenges around skills, productivity and economic inactivity, the region has a clear vision and a strong track record of collaboration between business, education and the public sector.

“What's particularly encouraging is the focus on inclusive growth, ensuring that investment translates into opportunities for local people. If we continue to work together and invest in developing our talent pipeline, the North East has every reason to be optimistic about the future.”

Esh itself will play a key part in that regional future, with keystone projects and investments planned.

Darush said: “We're continuing to deliver major projects across the North East, including some of the region's most significant infrastructure, housing and regeneration schemes. Alongside this, we're investing heavily in our social value programmes, helping thousands of young people engage with core and growth sectors, and providing skills and employment opportunities through initiatives such as Building My Skills and Get into STEM.

“We're also seeing increasing demand from clients who want projects to deliver wider social and economic benefits for local communities, something that aligns perfectly with Esh's values and long-standing approach. Looking ahead, we have a strong pipeline of work, exciting new partnerships and a number of major project milestones to celebrate, so there is certainly plenty of positive news still to come.”

Sophie Brown, sales and events manager at Raby Castle, Park and Gardens told us: “The BUSINESSiQ Awards are a brilliant opportunity to celebrate the achievements of businesses from across the region and everyone’s hard work to support and champion the North East. To be the location for the awards next year is going to be a momentous occasion for Raby and we’re looking forward to welcoming all the nominees.

"We’ve seen an incredible amount of interest in our corporate events and have welcomed a fantastic array of brands, companies and organisations over the last few years.

“We have something unique to offer everyone, whether you’re a family visiting to experience the new trails in The Plotters’ Forest, enjoying a guided tour round Raby Castle with our archives team or taking part in one of our garden tours or themed floristry workshops. We also offer wellbeing events, cooking masterclasses, estate tours, stargazing experiences, crafting workshops and so much more.”
