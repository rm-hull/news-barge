FORMER Emmerdale star and Strictly Come Dancing contestant Lewis Cope will star in Strangers on a Train at Bradford's Alhambra Theatre.

Bill Kenwright Ltd has announced that Lewis and Jonny Labey will lead the cast for Craig Warner’s stage adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s celebrated novel, famously brought to the screen by Alfred Hitchcock.

Lewis (pictured) will play chilling, psychologically complex Charles Bruno, with Jonny Labey as Guy Haines, the unsuspecting stranger drawn into his deadly game.

Charles Bruno has an idea: the perfect crime. Two murders. Two strangers. No motive. No connection. No consequences. Guy Haines dismisses it as a joke. But Bruno isn’t joking. As his obsession spirals out of control, Guy finds himself trapped in a nightmare of guilt, manipulation and fear.

Lewis Cope’s stage work includes Agatha Christie’s Witness for the Prosecution and The Legend of Sleepy Hollow. He played Nicky Milligan in Emmerdale, receiving a Rising Star nomination at the 2023 National Television Awards, and he appeared on Strictly Come Dancing in 2025, making it to week 11 with professional partner Katya Jones.

Jonny's theatre credits include Baz Luhrmann’s Strictly Ballroom. He played Rex Gallagher in Hollyoaks and Paul Coker in EastEnders.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21689417.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jonny Labey plays Guy Haines in the thriller *(Image: Bradford Theatres)*

David Gilbery, CEO, Bill Kenwright Ltd said: “We’re delighted to have Lewis and Jonny taking on two of Patricia Highsmith’s most fascinating characters in our new production of Strangers on a Train. They are both immensely talented performers, and with the Roxana Silbert directing, alongside designer Robert Innes Hopkins, and the rest of our wonderful creative team, this has all the ingredients for a truly thrilling stage production.”

Strangers on a Train is at the Alhambra Theatre from May 5-8. Call (01274) 432000 or visit bradford-theatres.co.uk
