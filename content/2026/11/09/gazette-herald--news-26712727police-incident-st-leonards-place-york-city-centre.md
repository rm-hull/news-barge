**POLICE have been called to York city centre following an incident.**

One North Yorkshire Police car and two unmarked vehicles are currently parked outside York Theatre Royal in St Leonard’s Place this afternoon (November 9).

Around seven officers were spotted in the area moving from Duncombe Place to the nearby city centre road.

An eyewitness confirmed that there is no cordon in place and both the road itself and nearby bus stops are still in operation, despite the police presence at the scene.
