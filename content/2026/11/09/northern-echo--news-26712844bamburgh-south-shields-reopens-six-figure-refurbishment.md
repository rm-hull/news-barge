A popular pub overlooking one of South Tyneside's best-known stretches of coastline has reopened following a six-figure refurbishment.

The Bamburgh, on Bamburgh Avenue, has unveiled a revamped interior and upgraded outdoor space.

Owners Greene King hope the improvements will help attract both local customers and visitors ahead of the busy festive season.

Overlooking The Leas and close to the finish line of the Great North Run, The Bamburgh is a familiar sight to thousands of runners and spectators every year.

As participants complete the final stages of the world's biggest half marathon and make their way towards the South Shields seafront finish, many pass the pub along the route and head in afterwards for a drink.

The seafront pub has reopened with upgraded lighting, signage, and outdoor furniture to help visitors enjoy the seafront views in comfort.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21691525.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The reopening comes just ahead of the critical festive season, a time when pubs and restaurants across the North East are gearing up to attract customers for Christmas parties, family gatherings and end-of-year celebrations. *(Image: Greene King)*

Inside, the bar and dining areas have been refreshed with new décor, flooring, and furnishings.

Kyle Flannigan, general manager at The Bamburgh, said the revamp will provide customers a "brilliant spot" to enjoy the coastline, adding that it was "great to be open again."

He added: "The Bamburgh is a great community pub and we are here for the everyday and special occasions, and with the Christmas countdown on, we are so pleased with our new look."

It’s also known for its weekly pub quiz and live sports, now shown on improved TV and audio systems.
