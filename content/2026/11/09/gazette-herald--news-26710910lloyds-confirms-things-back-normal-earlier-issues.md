Thousands of Lloyds Bank users are experiencing problems with online banking.

More than 4,200 issues were reported on Downdetector as of 9.20am on Monday, November 9, with 72 per cent of complaints related to the Lloyds Bank app.

One user said: "Down yet again, happening far too often these days."

Another said their banking issues had disrupted their day, adding: "Our shopping delivery down, now the bank, something bizarre happening."

Some customers have also complained about being unable to access Halifax banking apps on Downdetector, which is part of the wider Lloyds Banking Group.

## Lloyds confirms "things are back to normal"

Lloyds previously said it was aware of the issue affecting internet banking and the mobile app, and was working to fix it.

The banking group has now confirmed everything is back to normal.

A Lloyds spokesperson said: "We're sorry customers had trouble logging in to our app earlier this morning, but can confirm things are back to normal.”

## What is Downdetector?

Downdetector is an online website that allows users of popular services, including X, Instagram, Facebook, Virgin Media and more, to report if they are having trouble with the services.

The site only reports an incident when the number of problem reports is significantly higher than the typical volume for that time of day.

Describing itself on its website, Downdetector is “where people go when services don’t work.”

The website monitors disruptions to various key services including the internet, web hosting platforms, banks, social media and more.

You can see the full methodology and learn more about how Downdetector collects status information and detects problems via its website.

In short, users can get involved by submitting any issue reports on the platform.

Problem indicators are also collected from social media and other factors on the web, Downdetector explained.

These reports are then validated and analysed in real-time.
