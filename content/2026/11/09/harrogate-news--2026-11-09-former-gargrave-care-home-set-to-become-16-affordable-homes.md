**A former care home in Gargrave is set to be redeveloped into 16 affordable homes, with preparatory work now beginning ahead of the building’s planned demolition.**

North Yorkshire Council is progressing plans to transform the former Neville House care home, on Neville Crescent, which closed in 2023.

The preparatory work is starting this week and will include establishing a secure working area and removing asbestos from the buildings. The work is expected to be completed before the end of the year.

Demolition is scheduled for early 2027, with both the preparatory works and demolition being managed by NY Highways.

## Plans for 16 affordable homes

Once the site has been cleared, the council intends to redevelop it, subject to planning permission, to provide a mix of one, two and three-bedroom flats, houses and bungalows.

The proposed homes would be available through a combination of social rent and shared ownership, with the aim of meeting local housing needs and helping residents remain in their communities.

The scheme forms part of North Yorkshire Council’s wider commitment to deliver 500 new council homes by 2029 through its Housing Revenue Account.

## Housing waiting lists across North Yorkshire

North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for housing, Cllr Simon Myers, said the development represented an important step towards increasing the supply of affordable homes.

> **Cllr Simon Myers, North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for housing**
>
> Providing more affordable housing is one of our biggest challenges and schemes such as this represent an important step in delivering the homes people need.
>
> With more than 7,200 people on housing waiting lists across North Yorkshire, increasing the supply of genuinely affordable homes remains a priority to ensure local people and families can access housing in the communities where they want to live.
>
> We are taking a range of approaches to achieve this, including working with housing providers through the York and North Yorkshire Strategic Housing Partnership, and a joint delivery programme with a nationally renowned housing developer to help accelerate the construction of homes on council-owned sites.
>
> We are also the only council in the country to use funding generated through the second homes council tax premium exclusively for housing projects such as delivering more affordable homes.
>
> The redevelopment of the former Neville House site is an example of the progress we are making, as we look to transform a redundant building into a modern, high-quality development that we hope will provide much-needed homes for social rent and deliver genuinely affordable housing for local residents.

## Funding for the development

The proposed scheme is being supported by funding from North Yorkshire Council’s Housing Revenue Account and the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority’s Brownfield Housing Fund.

The council is also exploring additional external funding opportunities to support delivery of the project.

The Gargrave development is one of several affordable housing projects being progressed by the authority.

Last month, construction began on the first affordable homes to be funded through the council’s second homes council tax premium. North Yorkshire Council is supporting Broadacres Housing Association to build 26 affordable homes across two sites in Hunmanby, near Filey, and Shipton by Beningbrough, near York.

The Neville House redevelopment remains subject to planning permission.
