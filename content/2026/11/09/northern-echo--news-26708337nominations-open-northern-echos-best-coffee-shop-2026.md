The search is on to find the best coffee shop County Durham and Darlington, and readers are being invited to nominate their favourites.

Whether it's your go-to spot for a morning cappuccino or your favourite independent, we're putting the spotlight on the region's thriving café culture as nominations open for our Best Coffee Shop competition

The Northern Echo editor Gavin Foster said: "The Best Coffee Shop competition gives us the chance to celebrate some of the best coffee shops County Durham and Darlington have to offer.

"Too often we tell stories of coffee shops closing and the struggles the industry faces, but this is our chance to support those hospitality businesses."

Delicious on Darlington's High Row will hope to make it a hat-trick, having claimed the top spot in 2024 and 2025.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682946.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Delicious, Darlington *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Owned Maria Zaimaki said of last year's win: "It's nice to be recognised by our customers for all of the staff's hard work.

"This is the greatest Christmas gift, and it comes at such a busy time for us."

Nominations open on today (Monday, November 9) and close on Sunday (November 15).

Readers can nominate any coffee shop or café they believe stands above the rest, whether it's for the quality of the coffee, exceptional customer service, delicious food or the atmosphere that keeps them coming back.
