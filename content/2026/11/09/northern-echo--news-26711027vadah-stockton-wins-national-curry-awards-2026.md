An award-winning Indian restaurant hailed for being the “best curry house on Teesside” has scooped another gong.

Vadah, which has been a feature on the Riverside in Stockton since 2014, has been named restaurant of the year at the National Curry Awards 2026.

The venue, which has built an impressive reputation with award wins and glowing reviews, took home the trophy at a glitzy ceremony at the House of Lords in London on October 19.

Owner Ahmed Siddique said it was a “fantastic moment” for the restaurant, which has now scooped the award for three years in a row.

“Winning Restaurant of the Year North East for the third time means so much to everyone at Vadah,” he said.

“It’s a wonderful recognition of the hard work our team puts in every day and the support we receive from our customers and local community.

“Collecting the award at the House of Lords made the occasion especially memorable, and it motivates us to keep delivering the best possible experience for everyone who visits Vadah.

“We’re proud to represent the North East.”

Vadah currently boasts a 4.4 star rating on Google and is praised by diners for its “absolutely beautiful” food and “second to none” service.

One person said: “This was our first ever visit and wow. The atmosphere, service, staff and food were outstanding. They go above and beyond.
