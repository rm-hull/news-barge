Two people have been taken to hospital after a multi-vehicle crash on the A1.

Emergency services were called to the southbound carriageway at Gosforth Park on Sunday (November 8) after a crash just before 10pm.

The road was closed by police for around ten hours before it reopened to drivers this morning, sparking severe traffic during rush hour.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that two people have been taken to the Royal Victoria Infirmary, in Newcastle, by road.

Their conditions - and the details regarding the crash - remain unknown at this stage.

A NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on the A1 near Kingston Park at 9.56pm on Sunday, November 8.
