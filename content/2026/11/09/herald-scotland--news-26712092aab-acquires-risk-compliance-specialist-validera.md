Professional services firm AAB has acquired specialist risk and compliance provider Validera, bringing 28 new experts into the business.

The deal will strengthen AAB’s governance, risk and compliance offering, adding Validera’s expertise in internal audit, governance, risk and assurance.

Validera works with private and not-for-profit organisations to improve governance, manage risk and optimise operations.

The acquisition will give Validera’s clients access to AAB’s wider range of services, including environmental, social and governance (ESG), audit, tax and people advisory support.

It will also allow Validera to expand its internal audit services.

The deal follows AAB’s appointment of Jason Davies as governance, risk and compliance partner earlier this year.

Emma Lancaster, chief executive at AAB, said: "This acquisition represents another important step in the growth of our Governance, Risk & Compliance offering. Validera has built a strong reputation as a trusted and independent partner, with an approach that closely aligns with our own values.

"Together, we'll continue to deliver the qualities that clients value most: objective assurance, deep sector expertise and the flexibility to focus on the issues that matter most to their organisation.

"Validera's client-centric approach makes them a natural fit to join our team."

Lee Glover, director at Validera, said: "What makes Validera different is our commitment to delivering independent, credible assurance alongside a flexible, client-focused approach.

"By joining AAB, we can build on those strengths, giving clients access to a wider range of specialist services while continuing to provide the high-quality internal audit, governance, risk and compliance support they value.

"We're excited about the opportunities this creates for both our clients and our people."

AAB provides audit and accounting, tax, payroll and HR, outsourcing and advisory services to clients internationally.

The firm now employs 2,000 people across the UK and Ireland and is continuing to invest in specialist services, regional expertise and talent.
