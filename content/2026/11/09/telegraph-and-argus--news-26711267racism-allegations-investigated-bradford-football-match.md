An investigation is under way after allegations of racism emerged following a semi-professional football match involving a Bradford team.

The controversy relates to a Northern Counties East Football League fixture involving Route One Rovers, who play at Myra Shay, off Barkerend Road.

Screenshots circulating on social media appear to show highly inappropriate, racist, ableist and sexually explicit remarks exchanged in a WhatsApp group reportedly involving players from opposition side Kinsley Boys FC, from near Wakefield.

In a statement released on social media, Kinsley Boys FC said it was aware of allegations concerning comments made within a private players' WhatsApp group following the fixture.

On social media, Route One Rovers said: “Racism has absolutely no place in football. No excuses. No tolerance.

"Those responsible must be held accountable, and the relevant authorities must take firm action. Silence is not an option. Enough is enough.”

## Incident referred to authorities

The club's statement came after screenshots of messages allegedly sent following a recent match were shared online.

Kinsley Boys FC said its management committee had no prior knowledge of the messages and only became aware of the issue after they began circulating on social media.

Kinsley Boys said it takes allegations of racism, discrimination and inappropriate behaviour “extremely seriously” and has launched an internal investigation to establish the facts and identify any individuals responsible for inappropriate conduct.

The club added it would cooperate fully with any enquiries carried out by the Football Association and the Northern Counties East Football League (NCEL).

In a statement, the NCEL said: "The Northern Counties East Football League can confirm that it has received a formal complaint concerning alleged discriminatory comments circulated via a private messaging group involving individuals connected with a member club.

"The allegations are extremely serious and include claims of racism, religious discrimination, disability discrimination and abusive conduct.

"The matter has been formally recorded and referred to the appropriate authorities for investigation."

## Kinsley Boys FC statement

A Kinsley Boys FC spokesperson said: “Whilst this process is ongoing, we believe it would be inappropriate to comment on individual players or specific allegations until all the facts have been established.

"Kinsley Boys FC does not condone racism, discrimination or abusive behaviour in any form.

"Any necessary action will be taken following a fair and thorough investigation.

"We ask that everyone allows the appropriate processes to take place and refrains from speculation or targeting individuals.”

The NCEL added: "The NCEL is committed to ensuring that football is inclusive, respectful and welcoming to all.

"Discrimination of any kind has no place in our game and will not be tolerated.

"As the matter is now subject to investigation, the league will be making no further comment at this stage."
