Pupils at St Cuthbert's CE Primary in Pateley Bridge quizzed Sir Julian Smith MP on public transport, rural services and the Queen. Updated November 2026.

Pupils at St Cuthbert’s CE Primary School in Pateley Bridge used a visit from their MP to raise public transport links and access to services in rural communities, alongside questions about his job and whether he had ever met the Queen.

Sir Julian Smith, the MP for Skipton and Ripon, met pupils and staff at the Nidderdale school and took part in a question-and-answer session, his office said in a release on Monday 9 November.

## What the children asked

According to the MP’s office, the children asked about his role as a Member of Parliament: what he considers his proudest achievement in Parliament, whether he had ever met the Queen and what a typical working week looks like for him. The release does not give his answers.

Pupils also brought up issues that matter to them closer to home, including public transport links and access to services in rural communities. Pateley Bridge is in Nidderdale, about 14 miles north-west of Harrogate.

## What the MP said

“I had a really enjoyable visit to St Cuthbert’s and was grateful for such a warm welcome from the pupils and staff,” Sir Julian said.

“The children asked some brilliant questions about my job as an MP. We talked about Parliament, local transport and services.

“I was impressed by how engaged and interested the pupils were in what happens both locally and nationally.”

It is the second time in a week that the MP has been in Pateley Bridge: on 2 November he opened the expanded Pateley Bridge Dental Practice on Low Wath Road.

Sir Julian’s constituency office publishes his news and contact details at juliansmith.org.uk.
