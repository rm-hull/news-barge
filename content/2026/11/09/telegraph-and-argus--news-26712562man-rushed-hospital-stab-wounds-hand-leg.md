A man in his 30s has been taken to hospital with serious injuries after a stabbing incident.

It happened on Howley Mill Lane, Batley, at around 6.50pm on Sunday evening.

The victim suffered stab wounds to his hand and leg.

His injuries are described as serious but not believed to be life-threatening.

Kirklees CID is investigating the incident.

Anyone who witnessed the incident or has information is asked to contact Kirklees Police on 101, quoting crime reference 13260636097.

Enquiries remain ongoing.

## What police could say

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Emergency services were called to reports that a man had been stabbed in Howley Mill Lane, Batley, at around 6.50pm last night (8 November).

"A man in his 30s was taken to hospital with serious stab wounds to his hand and leg.

"His injuries are thought to be serious but not thought to be life threatening.

"Enquiries are ongoing by Kirklees CID.

"Anyone who saw or has information about the incident is asked to contact Kirklees Police on 101 or online to our Live Chat facility referencing police crime number 13260636097.

"Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111."
