North Yorkshire Police has issued a CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following a serious assault at a pub in Masham.

Cameron Ramsey, 34, is wanted in connection with a serious assault that took place outside the Bruce Arms in Masham on Saturday (November 7).

Ramsey is described as a mixed-race man in his 30s, with a bald or closely shaved head and dark eyebrows.

At the time of the incident, he was wearing a long grey padded coat with a large hood, a dark grey Berghaus tracksuit and black On Cloud trainers.

If you have seen Cameron Ramsey, or have any information about his whereabouts, you should get in contact with the police.

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If the sighting is immediate, you should call 999.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260219596 when providing any details regarding his whereabouts.
