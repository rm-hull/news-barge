*IMAGO / Anadolu Agency*

The 22-year-old man climbed the pole on the Platz der Republik at around 11:30 on Sunday. He took down the German flag and threw it to the ground. In its place, he hoisted a Palestinian flag. Two other men, aged 20 and 32, stood at the base of the pole and assisted him.

This morning, the group “Peacefully Against Genocide” claimed responsibility. In an online statement, they said it was about “civil society solidarity with the people of Palestine.”

According to police, they secured the scene and arrested the two people assisting the climb. Meanwhile, the 22-year-old remained suspended at the top of the mast, secured by a climbing harness. He climbed down on his own around 12:30 pm.

Police officers, with the help of the fire department, removed the Palestinian flag. Because the flagpole’s tensioning system was damaged, the national flag could not be rehung immediately, according to police.

The State Security Police are investigating on suspicion of defaming the state and its symbols, suspicion of malicious damage to property, violation of the Berlin Assembly Freedom Act, and suspicion of trespassing.

The 22-year-old climber was temporarily taken into police custody, as was a supporter. The state security service is to transfer the 22-year-old. The 20-year-old supporter was later released from police custody and issued a ban from the area. The third person, a 32-year-old, only received a ban from the area.

Bundestag President Julia Klöckner (CDU) demanded: “Our German Bundestag needs more protection!” She stated that she will not accept “that something like this is even possible at the German Bundestag.” It must be ensured that the parliament “does not degenerate into a backdrop for activism of any kind.” The immediate surroundings of the Reichstag building are also relevant for the protection of the parliament.
