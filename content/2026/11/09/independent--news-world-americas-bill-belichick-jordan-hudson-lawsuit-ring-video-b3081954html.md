The owners of a Massachusetts home are being sued over Ring camera footage that allegedly outed the relationship between former Miss Maine contestant Jordon Hudson and football coach Bill Belichick.

The legal action, filed Friday, November 6, in Suffolk County Superior Court, comes shortly after news surfaced that Hudson, 25, and Belichick, 74, ended their relationship late last year. The two remain close friends and business partners, as reported by *People*.

According to court documents obtained by *People*, the complaint alleges that homeowners Elise McDevitt and Craig Nolan unlawfully leaked Ring camera footage of the six-time Super Bowl-winning coach leaving a Winthrop, Massachusetts, property shirtless in June 2023. The images later became the central topic of an episode of the podcast “Pablo Torre Finds Out.”

The relationship between Hudson and the University of North Carolina football coach was completely private at the time of the four-night stay, the filing states, asserting that their romance was “outed to the general public as a direct result of [McDevitt and Nolan] leaking this personal and private information.”

The lawsuit details an exchange between Hudson and the property owners that began over parking and guest concerns. McDevitt allegedly sent Hudson images of the shirtless coach, texting: “I’m a huge Cowboys fan, but don’t live under a rock! Wishing you the best! Please don’t forget to leave the parking pass.”

![Former Miss Maine contestant Jordan Hudson is suing the owners of a Massachusetts home over Ring camera footage that allegedly outed her relationship to football coach Bill Belichick](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/31/13/2274224104..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Hudson allegedly attempted to defuse the exchange, replying: “To be honest – I don’t quite understand your message. Regardless, I hear you…no parking…”

To that, McDevitt allegedly responded: “It’s clear who is on the video…I’ll leave it at that.”

When Hudson requested that the recording be destroyed, McDevitt allegedly replied: “We will delete it from Ring, but as you know, unfortunately, videos are never totally deleted.”

![The legal action comes shortly after news that Hudson and the 74-year-old coach ended their relationship late last year](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/31/14/2198457470..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The filing notes that Hudson warned the couple of potential legal steps, prompting McDevitt to text back: “If you decide to take any legal action, we will respond and the discovery process would presumably not be welcomed by the person in the video.”

The lawsuit alleges the dissemination of the footage caused “unwanted publicity and scrutiny” regarding a relationship the pair “had intended to remain private.”

McDevitt and Nolan later appeared on Torre’s podcast to address the stay and the surrounding attention, though they denied leaking the 2023 images.

![Hudson is asking the court for damages and a strict order preventing the hosts from sharing any further personal information with third parties](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/09/18/2247401017..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Hudson is asking the court for damages, attorney fees, and an injunction barring McDevitt and Nolan “from disclosing or disseminating any personal and/or private information related to Ms. Hudson to any third party.”

The filing adds, “Further, it is believed that [McDevitt and Nolan] possess additional private videos and/or other private information pertaining to Ms. Hudson, which [McDevitt and Nolan] have an admitted financial incentive to exploit.”

Public attention on the former couple intensified following a viral April 2025 appearance on “CBS Sunday Morning,” in which Hudson declined to discuss how they met, as well as reports that she was temporarily barred from North Carolina athletic facilities during Belichick's initial season with the program.

Belichick recently declined to answer questions about their past relationship during a team press conference.
