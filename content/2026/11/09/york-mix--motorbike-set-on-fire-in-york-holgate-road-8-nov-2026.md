A motorbike was set on fire in York last night (Sunday).

Firefighters from York went to the incident on **Holgate Road** at**7.45pm**.

They found the bike well alight.

#### Holgate Road, York

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A hose reel jet was used to extinguish. The cause is believed to be deliberate.”

It’s the second similar incident in just over two weeks.

On Friday, 23 October, firefighters put out a motorbike on fire on **Rawdon Avenue**, and called in the police.
