Amanda Owen's Ravenseat Farm has attracted interest from visitors for years after becoming famous through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm.

The remote Swaledale property is known for its spectacular Yorkshire Dales setting, its working sheep farm and the family's cream tea tradition.

The farm remains a private family property, meaning visitors should not assume that the farmhouse, farmyard or surrounding private land is open to the public.

However, the family's cream tea business has returned, with Amanda's daughter Edith taking on an important role in its revival.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670820.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edith and Raven Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The tradition originally developed as the Owens served refreshments to walkers passing through Upper Swaledale.

The cream teas became a familiar part of the family's story, offering walkers an opportunity to enjoy refreshments in the Dales.

After the tradition was suspended, Edith helped bring it back through a small serving hatch and cream tea van.

Speaking at an event in Northallerton, Amanda explained how the business had developed.

She said: "It's an empire built on cups of tea really, where any good business starts."

Amanda also explained that Edith had taken on responsibility for the venture with help from her siblings.

The cream tea business has featured in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, with the children preparing to welcome visitors during the summer.

Channel 4's announcement of two further series confirmed that Edith, Violet and their sisters would continue to be involved in opening the cream tea van.

## Where is Ravenseat Farm?

Ravenseat is situated in Upper Swaledale, around three-and-a-half miles west of Keld, within the Yorkshire Dales National Park.

The surrounding countryside is known for its remote landscape, walking routes and traditional stone buildings.

Visitors can explore the area using public rights of way, but should respect the working farm, livestock and private property.

## Is the cream tea van currently open?

Although the family has confirmed the return of the cream tea tradition, the cream tea van has closed for the year now.

Anyone planning a journey specifically for refreshments should check the family's latest official announcements before travelling.

The new series of Our Farm Next Door will continue to document the family's life at Ravenseat, including the cream tea venture.
