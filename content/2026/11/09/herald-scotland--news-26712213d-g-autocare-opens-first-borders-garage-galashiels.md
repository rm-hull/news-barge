D&G Autocare has opened its first garage in the Scottish Borders, taking over the former ATS site in Galashiels.

The new branch on Huddersfield Road is the company’s 21st in Scotland and brings its UK network to 22 garages.

It is the business’s first location in the Borders, extending its reach from its existing Scottish sites and its Carlisle branch, which opened in August 2024.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21690395.jpg?type=mds-article-620)D&G Autocare has taken over the former ATS site on Huddersfield Road. *(Image: Supplied)*

The Galashiels garage is managed by Hawick resident Richard Bell, who has experience working with major car dealerships and previously worked at D&G’s Musselburgh branch between 2022 and 2024.

Located beside Galashiels retail park and close to major retailers including Tesco, the garage will provide Class 4 MOTs, servicing, tyres, brakes, wheel alignment and general repairs.

It will also have a Class 7 MOT testing station, which the company says is the only one in Galashiels. The nearest alternative is in Selkirk.

Richard Bell, branch manager, said: "We are absolutely thrilled to be opening our 21st branch in Scotland, and establishing our very first presence in the Scottish Borders, my local area.

"The old ATS site is a prime location in Galashiels, making it highly accessible for local drivers.

"Taking over such a prominent site gives us the perfect platform to deliver the reliable, honest, and affordable autocare services that D&G is famous for.

"I see myself coming on board as Manager as a fantastic win for the area, as my roots are 100% local. I am fully committed to excellent customer care and building our client base in the coming months.

"We look forward to welcoming customers to Galashiels and keeping the Borders moving safely as we approach the winter months."

The branch is open six days a week, from 8am to 5.30pm Monday to Thursday, 8am to 5pm on Fridays and 8am to 12.30pm on Saturdays.

D&G Autocare is a family-owned business with branches across Scotland, including Fife, Edinburgh, the Lothians, Lanarkshire, Stirlingshire, Perthshire, Tayside and Glasgow.
