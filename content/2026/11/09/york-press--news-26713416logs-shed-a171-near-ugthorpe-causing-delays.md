A lorry shed its load of logs on the A171 near Ugthorpe, causing delays.

The incident happened on Monday, November 9, and led to logs spilling across the road.

RECOMMENDED READING:

A police spokesperson said: "We are currently on the A171 outside of the Ugthorpe area.

"There is a lorry which has shed it's load across the road.

"Please expect delays in this area whilst the clear-up is completed."
