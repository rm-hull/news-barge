# Asda Leeds HQ evacuated as Army called in to deal with 'suspicious package'

## Asda House has been evacuated after a 'suspicious package' was discovered

The army has been called in to deal with a suspicious package sent to Asda headquarters in Leeds.

The device was discovered and police were alerted at 11.16am on Monday (November 9) morning. A deemed "suspicious packaged" has been sent to Asda House in Great Wilson Street.

The building has been "evacuated as a precaution and a cordon is in place" and motorists are being urged to avoid the area. The incident is "ongoing" while emergency services deal with the situation. Find everything about the live breaking news incident here.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Motorists and members of the public are advised to avoid the Great Wilson Street area of Leeds city centre while emergency services deal with an ongoing incident.

"At 11.16am this morning, police received reports of a suspicious package having been sent to Asda House, Great Wilson Street, Leeds. Emergency services are currently at the scene and an Army EOD team has been called to attend.

"The building has been evacuated as a precaution and a cordon is in place. Motorists are asked to find alternative routes due to nearby road closures."
