## Popular York chippy for sale as owner plans career change

Copmanthorpe Chippy is on the market *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A popular York village fish and chip shop has gone up for sale as its owner prepares for a change of career.

Copmanthorpe Chippy, in Church Street, Copmanthorpe, has been put on the market as a leasehold business for £35,000.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21687931.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Copmanthorpe Chippy is on the market. *(Image: Ernest Wilson)*

The long-established takeaway is being sold through business agent Ernest Wilson, which describes it as a "superbly well-presented and highly regarded" fish and chip shop.

The family-run business is being sold because the owner plans to return to full-time education, allowing them to pursue a new career.

Copmanthorpe Chippy serves the village to the south-west of York and also draws customers from a wider area, with the A64 linking York and Tadcaster nearby.

![Copmanthorpe Chippy is on the market.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21687930.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The owner of Copmanthorpe Chippy plans to return to full-time education. *(Image: Ernest Wilson)*

According to the sales particulars, the chippy has been established for many years and is the only fish and chip shop in the village.

It has recently undergone a "thorough makeover", with the owners focusing on high-quality food and friendly service.

The agent says the business benefits from "excellent weekly takings" and low overheads, with rent of £192 per week and a new 10-year lease.

It is also suggested that there is scope for a new owner to grow the business further by making greater use of online ordering and food delivery apps.

The ground-floor premises are situated in a parade of shops with parking nearby and include an outside seating area for customers.

Inside is a customer ordering counter with a three-pan Hopkins electronic frying range, along with a drinks display chiller and ice cream maker.

There is also an equipped food preparation room, pot-wash area and staff toilet.

To the rear, an outbuilding provides a wet-preparation area, potato store and additional dry and chilled storage.

As the takeaway is currently family-run, the sales particulars say a buyer would not be required to take on existing staff.

The business is being marketed under reference 591294 for £35,000 leasehold.
