A man has been arrested following a reported late-night break-in at a fish and chip shop.

Police were called to reports of an incident at Salty's Fish and Chips on St Helena Road, Wibsey, Bradford, at around 1.18am on Sunday.

A 47-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of burglary.

## What have police said?

![Salty's Fish and Chips](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21690982.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Salty's Fish and Chips *(Image: Newsquest)*

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called to reports of a burglary at a business premises on St Helena Road, Bradford at around 1.18am on Sunday 8 November.

"A 47-year-old man has been arrested and enquiries remain ongoing."

The fish and chip shop has confirmed to the T&A that it is open today.
