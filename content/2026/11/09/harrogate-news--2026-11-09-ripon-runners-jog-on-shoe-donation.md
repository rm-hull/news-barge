**Ripon Runners has donated running shoes to the Jog On initiative, which collects unwanted trainers for reuse and aims to prevent one million pairs from going to landfill.**

The North Yorkshire running club organised a collection among its members, with 54 pairs of shoes donated as part of the initiative.

Jog On was founded in the UK by chief executive Tony Piedade. It works with logistics and distribution partners to collect, sort, inspect and distribute running shoes to people in need, with destinations in around 15 locations worldwide.

The organisation says many unwanted running shoes still have plenty of life left in them. Its work aims to reduce waste while helping people who lack suitable footwear.

Jog On announced in August 2026 that it had processed 60 tonnes of shoes.

## Ripon Runners members support shoe collection

Sarah Beldon, ladies’ captain at Ripon Runners, organised the collection after learning about the initiative.

> **Sarah Beldon, Ladies’ Captain at Ripon Runners**
>
> I was inspired by reading about Jog On and realised that lots of us at the club have unwanted running shoes ready to pass on to others. So, I decided to co-ordinate a collection at the club and was delighted with the response – which resulted in 54 pairs of shoes being donated. I’d like to offer a huge ‘thank you’ to all my club colleagues who got involved.

The response was sufficiently positive that the club is considering repeating the collection in the future.

> **Sarah Beldon**
>
> I think we could hopefully repeat the process again in a couple of years’ time, and I’m sure that Jog On would be happy to receive more trainers to pass on to those that need them – both in the UK and abroad.

## About Ripon Runners

Ripon Runners was founded in 1985 by local milkman Cameron Weatherburn. The club welcomes runners of all ages, genders and abilities, and also has a dedicated junior section.

Based at Ripon Rugby Club, it meets on Tuesday and Thursday evenings throughout the year, alongside regular weekend runs and social events.

The club also organises the Jolly Holly Jog, Ripon 10 Mile and Ripon Ultra races, which are open to members and non-members.

Further information about the club is available at www.riponrunners.org.uk.
