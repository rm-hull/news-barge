# Leeds city centre sealed off after bus ends up on pavement

## A police cordon is in place

A bus appears to have mounted the pavement in Leeds city centre, with police and ambulances at the scene.

Emergency services were called to the street outside The Light on Monday evening (November 9). A large cordon has been put in place.

Pictures from the scene show a First Bus electric single-decker standing on the pavement right next to the building’s frontage. It is close to the Coffeeboy café and the Turtle Bay restaurant.

Police tape has been wrapped around the bus, and yellow barriers have been put up beside it.

Two emergency ambulances and an NHS rapid response vehicle were parked along the road.

At least two marked West Yorkshire Police cars were also at the scene. Officers in high-vis jackets stood guard at the cordon.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were called to reports of a collision involving a bus on The Headrow in Leeds at around 4.37pm today (9 November). It is reported that the bus had mounted the kerb and collided with a building.

"No serious injuries have been reported. Merrion Street will be closed to allow for the safe recovery of the vehicle. Motorists are advised to avoid the area and use an alternative route."
