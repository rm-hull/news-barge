A MAN was rescued from the roof of his home after climbing up to escape a late-night blaze.

Fire crews were called to Woodhall Road in Thornbury at 10.33pm last night (Sunday), where a bedroom fire involving a mattress had broken out.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "One male occupant had climbed onto the roof to escape the fire.

"An aerial was requested and he was rescued using this aerial.

"The occupant was given a precautionary check up by ambulance.

Crews used two hose reel jets, four sets of breathing apparatus, and a positive pressure ventilation fan to tackle the blaze.

The fire service confirmed that no smoke detectors were fitted in the property.

A fire investigation officer was called to the scene to determine the cause of the fire, which has not yet been established.
