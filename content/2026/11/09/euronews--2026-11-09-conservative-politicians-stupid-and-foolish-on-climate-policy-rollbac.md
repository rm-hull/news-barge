Chloé Mikolajczak, social and environmental activist, believes the world is missing "political willingness to act" on climate policies ahead of COP31 in Turkey.

She denies accusations of climate activists being "out of touch" as she warns Europe is "the fastest warming continent in the planet". The last 12 years have been the warmest on record, according to a newly published report from the World Meteorological Organization. She believes conservative politicians are being "very stupid and foolish" on their recent decisions to roll back climate friendly policies.
