York City reached the second round of the FA Cup for only the third time since 2010 after a 4-0 thrashing of Northampton Town on Saturday.

Ollie Pearce scored after ten seconds – the third quickest goal in FA Cup history – to set City on their way. He doubled his tally after 11 minutes.

Alex Hunt and Josh Stones also found themselves on the scoresheet, making it a very comfortable afternoon for York City.

So now the question is: who can the Minstermen get in the draw tonight (Monday)?

Hosted by Jules Breach, the draw will be made at 6.45pm by Karen Carney and Joel Ward and will be available to watch on TNT Sports 1, HBO Max, and the TNT Sports YouTube channel.

York City are number 36 in the draw. Here are some other key numbers to watch out for.

**1 – Dover Athletic**. Dover are two leagues below York City so it is one of the easier matches in the draw. Many York City fans want to play Dover as it gives the Minstermen the best chances of making the third round and a potential meeting with a Premier League giant.** 15 – Barnsley**. The Tykes are 23rd in League One after 12 matches, they are a well followed club which would be a great game for York City. Barnsley knocked York out of the FA Cup last year and played Liverpool at Anfield in the following round.** 34 – Leicester City**. Ten years ago the Foxes were champions of England, now after back-to-back relegations they find themselves 10th in League One. Speaking to many York fans, playing Leicester City away is the best possible fixture.** 38 – Wigan Athletic**. The Minstermen will want revenge after being knocked out of the FA Cup three years ago by them, who went on to play Manchester United in the next round.
