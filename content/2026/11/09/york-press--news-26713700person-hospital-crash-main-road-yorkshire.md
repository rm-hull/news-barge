A person has been taken to hospital after a crash on a main road in North Yorkshire.

An air ambulance, police and firefighters were called to the single-vehicle collision on the A645 in Camblesforth, near Selby, at around 10.50am on Monday (November 9).

The A645 was closed between the A1041 and Brigg Lane with police urging drivers to avoid the area. The road has since reopened.

Yorkshire Ambulance Service said one person was taken to Doncaster Royal Infirmary by road ambulance after the crash.

An ambulance service spokesperson said: "An ambulance, a team leader and an air ambulance were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed by road to Doncaster Royal Infirmary."

Firefighters helped pull one person from the vehicle after the crash and assisted with first aid until paramedics arrived, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

A fire service spokesperson said: "Crew from Selby and York were mobilised to a single-vehicle road traffic collision.

"Crews assisted with rescuing one casualty from the vehicle and assisted with first aid until ambulance arrived on scene."
