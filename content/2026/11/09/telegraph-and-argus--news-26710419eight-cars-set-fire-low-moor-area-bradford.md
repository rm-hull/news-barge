Eight cars have been set ablaze in a suspected arson attack.

Fire crews were called to Huddersfield Road and Woodside Road, Low Moor, just after 11pm on Friday, November 6, to reports of a vehicle on fire.

However, on arrival, crews discovered eight cars alight.

The incident, which was tackled with hose reels and a large jet, has been recorded as suspected arson.

A spokesman for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called to Huddersfield Road/Woodside Road at 23.04 on 6/11/26 to reports of a vehicle on fire.

"Two crews attended and found eight cars alight, which they used hose reels and a large jet to extinguish.

"This has been recorded as suspected deliberate ignition and passed to police."

West Yorkshire Police have been approached for comment.
