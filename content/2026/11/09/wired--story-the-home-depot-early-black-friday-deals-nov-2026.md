## Early Black Friday Deals on Refrigerators

No kitchen is complete without a fridge of some kind. Most fridges will last for years, but unexpected problems can force your hand; a friend of mine had a drawer in her fridge break, and she couldn’t order a replacement, so she faced either living with a missing drawer for the rest of the fridge’s life or replacing it early. She chose the latter, and I look at her fridge with envy every time I’m over since it’s such a beautiful appliance.

Whatever the reason for replacing one, you can get up to $500 off Home Depot’s fridges right now, including an LG smart fridge we found to be both beautiful and reliable and the gorgeous four-door Samsung smart fridge my friend has.

Fridges aren’t the only appliance on sale, either. The Home Depot has up to $1,250 off select appliances, and includes free delivery on any appliance purchases over $398.

## Early Black Friday Deals on Floor Care

I love a good vacuum, and the right robot vacuum or stick vacuum can change your life and your home—being WIRED’s resident vacuum tester has certainly improved mine!—and some of my favorite vacuums are on sale over at Home Depot during its Early Black Friday deals.

If you’re looking for a great robot vacuum without settling for something mediocre just to get a good price, the Roborock Qrevo Curv is on sale for almost $1,000 off. It is an older model, but Roborock’s vacuums are fantastic, and even the older Curv should still leave your home spic and span. Looking for a more classic vacuum? The Home Depot has a variety of stick (and upright, if you don’t mind a cord) vacuums on sale, including the Dyson V8 Cyclone.
