Sheer Bliss on Knaresborough High Street has won the Body Confidence and Shaping Award at the Stars Best Shop Awards, the lingerie trade's national shop prizes.

Sheer Bliss, the lingerie shop on Knaresborough High Street, has won a national award for helping women feel confident in what they wear.

The shop took the **MiracleSuit Body Confidence/Shaping Award** at the Stars Best
Shop Awards 2026. The organisers published the winners on Friday 6 November
(Stars Best Shop Awards).
The awards carry the name of Underlines, the lingerie trade magazine, and
the organisers say shops from across the UK and Ireland were represented. The Body Confidence prize was presented
by Chris and Katie Eve of Patricia Eve.

Sheer Bliss was on the shortlist the organisers published in August (finalists, 2026).

## Who else won

Sheer Bliss was the only winner from the Harrogate district. Other prizes on the night included:

* **Best Newcomer:** Lincoln Bra Lady
* **Etailer of the Year:** Naughty Knickers
* **Department Store:** Smith Bradbeer
* **Personal Touch:** The Fitting Room
* **Boutique Style:** Hidden Agenda

## About the shop

Sheer Bliss is at **14 High Street**, directly opposite the bus station at the top
of the street, according to its
own website. It sells lingerie,
swimwear, nightwear and hosiery, and offers a free fitting service. Its ranges
include:

* bridal lingerie
* mastectomy bras
* sports and nursing bras
* first bras for teenagers

The shop says it fits back sizes from 28 to 44 inches and cup sizes from A to I.

Its listed opening hours are **10am to 4pm, Monday to Saturday**, closed on
Sundays. It also offers one-to-one and private appointments, and its phone number
is 01423 869096.
