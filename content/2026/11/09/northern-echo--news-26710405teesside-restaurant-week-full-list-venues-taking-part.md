Parmos, steak, and tapas are on the menu for diners this week as Teesside Restaurant Week returns.

Foodies will be able to enjoy some of the region’s best restaurants for less from today (November 9) as the popular initiative returns.

The event, run by James Watson of hospitality business consultancy Over Ice, takes place four times a year with a string of the region’s best known food spots once again taking part.

Some of the venues offer two courses for just £20 and others three for £25 - with the new Brasserie at Acklam Hall and The Scruffy Duck in Norton joining the roster.

The restaurant week gives diners a chance to try some of the best offerings on Teesside at a snippet of the price, with steak, tapas, chicken parmesan, and curries all up for grabs.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20994220.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Vadah in Stockton *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

He said both venues had “consistently led the way” across the last three events.

He said: “Looking ahead to the next Teesside Restaurant Week, which kicks off today, we're delighted to welcome The Scruffy Duck in Norton to the event for the very first time.

“It's also fantastic to see the return of popular venues including G & T House, Whinstone View, The Pickled Parson and The Sporting Lodge, all getting behind the event once again.

“We're equally grateful for the continued support of our regular participants, with The Glass House, The Tree Bridge, Copperstone, Chapters Deli and The Keys all returning to offer diners another opportunity to enjoy some fantastic food at great value.

“The event's popularity is also reaching further afield, with The Fox and Tree and Stapylton Steakhouse in Seaham joining the line-up, giving food lovers even more choice and new places to discover.

"It's this fantastic mix of loyal supporters and new participants that makes Teesside Restaurant Week so special.
