A man in his 20s has been charged with attempted murder after a woman was 'stabbed' at a County Durham home.

Police said a woman in her thirties suffered injuries to her arm, head, chest and back.

Travis Lally, 21, of Sandgate, Shotton Colliery, has since been charged with attempted murder and remanded in custody. He will appear at court today.

A spokesperson for Durham Police said: “Police were called by the ambulance service just before 3pm on Friday (November 6) following reports that a woman had been stabbed at a property in Alexandra Terrace.

“Officers attended on an immediate response and found a woman in her thirties with injuries to her arm, head, chest and back.

“She was taken to hospital for treatment. Her injuries are not thought to be life-threatening.

“A 21-year-old man was arrested by officers and taken into police custody.

“He has since been charged with attempted murder and remanded in custody to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court today (November 9).”

Pictures show armed police outside a house, while the air ambulance landed near the Greenhills Centre.

One witness described the village as being “at a standstill”.

A North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) spokesperson said: “We were called to an incident at a private address in Wheatley Hill, County Durham on Friday, November 6 shortly before 3pm.

“We dispatched one ambulance crew and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service.

“One patient was taken to hospital by road for further treatment.”

A spokesperson for the Great North Air Ambulance Service added: “Our critical care team was activated at 3.08pm to an incident in Wheatley Hill, County Durham.
