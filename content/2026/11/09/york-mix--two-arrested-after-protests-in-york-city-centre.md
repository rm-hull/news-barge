Two people were arrested during the protests in York city centre on Saturday.

As ***YorkMix*** reported, one group organised a “Let The Flags Of Britain Be Flown” gathering in St Helen’s Square.

A counter protest, billed as “Stop the Far Right, No To Racism” gathered in the same place.

Separated by a significant police presence, the two demonstrations chanted at each other.

Today, a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “North Yorkshire Police was aware of planned protest activity and an associated counter-protest in St Helen’s Square, York, on Saturday 7 November.

“A proportionate policing operation was planned and put in place to ensure the safety of those attending and the wider community, while upholding the lawful rights of people to peaceful protest.

“The events passed largely peacefully and safely.

“Two arrests were made in connection with separate public order-related incidents. No injuries were reported.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-flags-protest-7-nov-2026-5.webp)

“We would like to thank those present and the wider York community for their cooperation with officers throughout the policing operation.”

During the demonstration, a Stand Up To Racism protester called Jane told ***YorkMix*** why she was there.

“I was involved with things like Rock Against Racism and Women’s Rights, and in my sort of naivety, I thought that once we’d done that decades ago that things would get better,” she said.

“And for a while, things did get better. But now with the rise of the far right here, with what’s happened in America, it feels to me like yet again we have to stand up and say what we believe in.

“I’m really worried about the rise of the far right here. It’s all very well as laughing at people like Farage, but behind Farage, there’s a whole phalanx of people who will turn out, and I think it’s time for all of us to stand up.”
