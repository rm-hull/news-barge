**Two Harrogate men have launched a 60km walking challenge during November to raise funds for suicide prevention charity Andy’s Man Club and encourage more men to talk openly about their mental health.**

George Carroll, Head PGA Professional at Harrogate Golf Club, and Chip ’n’ Chat co-founder Andy Wilson are each walking 60km throughout the month.

The distance is intended to represent the 60 men who, according to the organisers, die by suicide globally every hour.

Chip ’n’ Chat was established by Carroll and Wilson as a Harrogate-based men’s wellbeing initiative, using golf and informal social meetups to encourage men to connect and talk about mental health.

## Community walk planned for Harrogate

As part of the November challenge, the pair are organising an open group walk around Harrogate on Friday 13 November. Anyone is welcome to join them for a walk and a conversation.

During the walk, Carroll and Wilson will carry their golf bags on their backs as a symbol of the invisible weight people can carry when dealing with mental health difficulties.

The challenge will also include an 18-hole round at Harrogate Golf Club on 23 November, with the pair completing the remaining kilometres individually on foot during the month.

## Raising money for Andy’s Man Club

The challenge will culminate in the next official Chip ’n’ Chat meetup at Harrogate Golf Club on 29 November.

The event will welcome local men who want to get outdoors, try golf and meet others in a relaxed and supportive environment. No previous golf experience is required, with coaching provided.

> **George Carroll, Head Professional at Harrogate Golf Club and co-founder of Chip ’n’ Chat**
>
> As Head Pro here in Harrogate, I see how easily men can bottle things up. I’m taking on this challenge to show guys that it’s okay not to be okay, that it’s okay to talk, and that people like us are here and ready to listen. Everyone’s situation is different, but opening up has made a huge difference in my own life, and that’s why getting behind this cause means so much to me.

Andy Wilson, co-founder of Chip ’n’ Chat, said Andy’s Man Club had played an important role in his own recovery.

> **Andy Wilson, co-founder of Chip ’n’ Chat**
>
> Andy’s Man Club was key in my recovery from a breakdown in my mental health nearly 10 years ago, and it’s a group I still attend and facilitate at today. We’re taking on this 60km challenge to show everyone that whatever you’re going through, you don’t have to carry it alone. There are groups out there that can help, and we want to get men talking and point them toward life-changing support.

The pair are aiming to raise at least £500 for Andy’s Man Club, which provides free weekly peer-to-peer support groups for men across the UK.

Anyone interested in joining the Harrogate group walk on Friday 13 November, or sponsoring the challenge, can contact Chip ’n’ Chat through its Instagram account, @chipnchatmensclub.
