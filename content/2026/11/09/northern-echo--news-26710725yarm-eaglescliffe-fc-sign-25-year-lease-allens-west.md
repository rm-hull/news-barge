A North East football club is closing in on a permanent home after securing a 25-year lease.

Yarm & Eaglescliffe FC signed the lease for the Allens West site in Eaglescliffe, marking a major step towards establishing a permanent base in the community.

The lease, agreed with SPARK Education Trust, will allow the club to develop the site into a home ground.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21687922.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Concept picture of the Allens West grounds. *(Image: Yarm & Eaglescliffe FC)*

Glenn Kyle, club founder and director of football, said: "What we have achieved so far as a club in under ten years is amazing.

"What we’ve wanted all along is a home in our local community. We’re a big step closer to that now.

"This will allow us to grow as a club and provide a pathway for young players into senior-level football.

"We’ve had an amazing few years on the pitch and we want to now match that off the pitch. We want to continue to be a club that the people of Yarm and Eaglescliffe can be proud of."

The club has also applied for planning permission to upgrade Allens West's facilities.

Plans include refurbishing the pavilion with better changing rooms, a clubhouse and new toilets, while upgrading the main pitch to meet non-league standards.

Glenn said the lease marks a major step in a journey that began with one training session at Preston Park in 2017.

Since then, Yarm & Eaglescliffe FC has risen through the non-league divisions and now competes in the Ebac Northern League Division One, groundsharing with Billingham Town.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21687923.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Club founders Luke Allen (left) and Glenn Kyle (right). *(Image: Yarm & Eaglescliffe FC)*

Stephen Jackson, first-team manager, said: "When I first joined the club back in 2023, it was clear the club was ambitious and wanted its own ground.

"We’ve had success on the pitch and having our own home will strengthen us further and allow the club to grow.

"There’s still a lot of work to do, but it’s an exciting time to be part of the club."

The announcement comes after a standout season for the club, capped by three major honours in just six days in early May.

Yarm & Eaglescliffe FC lifted the North Riding Senior Cup and Northern League Division Two League Cup before securing promotion with a play-off victory.

The club now hopes to build on its success on the pitch by establishing a permanent home of its own.

The school will continue to use Allens West during term time, with the club using the site on weekday evenings, at weekends and during school holidays.

The agreement will also see the football club take responsibility for the management and maintenance of the site.

The club’s long-term ambition is to create a home of its own, where local players can progress, supporters can come together and the club can continue to grow.
