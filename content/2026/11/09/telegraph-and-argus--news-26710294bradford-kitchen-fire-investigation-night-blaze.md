A kitchen fire that broke out at night is under investigation.

Firefighters were called to Farfield Crescent in Bradford at 9.02pm yesterday (Sunday).

Crews from Spen Valley and Bradford responded to the emergency, bringing the fire under control using a hose reel and two sets of breathing apparatus.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) said: "Reports of a house fire and kitchen involved.

"One hose reel and two breathing apparatus were used to extinguish the fire.

"A positive pressure ventilation fan was used to clear the smoke."

A fire investigation officer has been assigned to determine the cause of the incident.

During the response, crews discovered that although smoke alarms were installed in the property, they were not operational at the time of the fire.

The spokesperson added: "Smoke detection fitted but not working.

"New detection fitted by fire crews."

No injuries were reported as a result of the fire.

The investigation into what caused the blaze is ongoing.
