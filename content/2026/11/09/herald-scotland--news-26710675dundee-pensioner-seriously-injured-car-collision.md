A pensioner has been seriously injured after being struck by a car in Dundee.

The 79-year-old was taken to hospital following the collision, which happened on Strathmartine Road near the junction with Kingsway on Friday evening.

Police Scotland said officers were called to the scene at around 5.25pm on November 6 following reports of a crash involving a grey car and a female pedestrian.

The woman was taken to Ninewells Hospital for treatment to what police described as serious injuries. No other injuries were reported.

The road was closed for a number of hours while emergency services dealt with the incident and investigations were carried out.

Officers are now appealing for information as they work to establish the full circumstances surrounding the crash.

Constable Darren Derrick said: "Enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances and we are appealing for anyone who was in the area around the time of the crash to contact us.

"We'd also ask any motorists with dash-cam footage, which could assist our enquiries, to come forward."

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident 2749 of Friday, November 6, 2026.
