A husband and wife who have called Darlington home for 15 years have taken the plunge and opened their first shop together, hoping to offer shoppers good-quality clothes without the hefty price tag.

Daniel and Agnieszka Maj opened Agnes Second Hand Darlington at 4 Stonebridge on Monday, October 26, selling a range of affordable, pre-loved clothing.

Originally from Poland, the couple have lived in Darlington for around 15 years and have two daughters, aged seven and 12.

Daniel and Agnieszka have been together for 27 years and said opening the shop marks an exciting new chapter for their family.

Daniel previously ran a small painting and decorating business, while Agnieszka has worked in a kitchen, but the pair decided they wanted to try something completely new together.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680773.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Agnes Second Hand Darlington at 4 Stonebridge *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The idea for Agnes Second Hand came from their belief that shoppers are increasingly looking for clothes which offer good quality without costing too much.

Daniel said: “Sometimes when you buy new clothes now, you pay more money, but the quality is not always good. Older clothes can be really good quality.

“Everything is expensive now and I think people are looking more at second-hand clothes. We want things to be cheaper but still good quality. That is what we want for people.”

The couple source their pre-loved clothing through suppliers and hope to continue expanding the selection available as the business grows.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Agnes Second Hand Darlington at 4 Stonebridge *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Daniel said their priority is to offer customers a combination of quality and affordable prices.

He said: “We buy from suppliers and all the time we are looking for better suppliers. We want quality and we want good prices.

“We want people to be happy. We want everything to be good for people and for the prices not to be too high.”

Having only opened their doors on October 26, Daniel and Agnieszka are now hoping to spread the word about their new venture and encourage more people to discover the shop.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680777.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Agnes Second Hand Darlington at 4 Stonebridge *(Image: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

Daniel said: “It is difficult when you start because nobody knows what it is or where the shop is.

“A lot of people ask where we are and what the shop is because it is something new. That is the problem when you start a business, but we are trying.”

For the couple, the shop represents another chapter in the life they have built together since moving to Darlington from Poland 15 years ago.

“Now we wanted something new. This is the first time we have done something like this, and we are trying to make the business good.”
