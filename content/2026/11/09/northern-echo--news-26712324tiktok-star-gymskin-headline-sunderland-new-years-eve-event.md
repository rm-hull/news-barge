A viral streamer and social media star has been confirmed as a headline act for Sunderland’s New Year’s Eve celebrations.

With more than 1.4 million TikTok followers, Gymskin has become a household name through his popular livestreams and online content.

He will headline the afternoon entertainment in Keel Square on December 31, as part of a programme of family entertainment, live music and festive fun.

Councillor Ciera Hudspith, Sunderland City Council Cabinet Member for Culture, Tourism and Heritage, said: "We're committed to making Sunderland's New Year's Eve event a celebration that brings people together, and Gymskin is a fantastic addition to this year's line-up.

"He's one of the most talked-about names on social media and has a huge following among younger audiences, so we're excited to welcome him to the city.

"Alongside our family activities, live entertainment, Gok Wan's headline performance and two spectacular fireworks displays, we're looking forward to ending the year in style and giving residents and visitors another great reason to visit Sunderland city centre."

The celebrations will continue into the evening, with DJ and broadcaster Gok Wan taking to the stage ahead of the city’s midnight fireworks display.

Two fireworks displays are planned for the night, with an early show at 5.30pm and the main event at midnight.

This year’s event will also feature fairground rides, festive light installations and food traders serving seasonal treats and warming drinks.

Gymskin’s appearance comes at the end of a standout year in which he is credited with bringing Madonna’s 1985 hit Into the Groove back into the UK charts this year.

His signature dance to the song went viral, and he celebrated by getting Madonna to join in his dance on a livestream.

The full entertainment programme for Sunderland’s New Year’s Eve celebration is still taking shape, with more acts and details expected to be announced in the coming weeks.
