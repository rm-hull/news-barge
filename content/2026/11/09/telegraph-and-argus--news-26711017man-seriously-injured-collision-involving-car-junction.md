A man has been seriously injured in a late-night collision.

Emergency services scrambled to an incident involving a car and a pedestrian at the junction of St Mary's Garden and St Mary's Avenue in Wyke.

The collision occurred at around 10.45pm on Saturday.

A 30-year-old man was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to reports of a collision between a car and a pedestrian on the junction of St Mary’s Garden and St Mary’s Avenue in Wyke, Bradford at around 10.45pm on Saturday (7 November).

"A 30-year-old man was taken to hospital with serious injuries."

Enquiries remain ongoing by Bradford CID.
