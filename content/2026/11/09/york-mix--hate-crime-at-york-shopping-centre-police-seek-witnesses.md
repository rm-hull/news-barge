Police are investigating a hate crime at a York shopping centre.

It happened at the Designer Outlet at 2pm on Friday, 30 October.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It involved two people in a Fiat 500 car shouting abuse towards another person in a different vehicle.

#### Designer Outlet York

“We’re particularly keen for any witnesses who may have seen what happened to please get in touch.”

If you can help police, you can contact them on the numbers below.
