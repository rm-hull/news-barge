Push for Pudsey has begun for Children in Need

**The BBC Look North big red sofa is being pushed across Yorkshire this week, as presenter Amy Garcia is joined by Lindsey Burrow for this year's Children in Need charity challenge.**

Lindsey is no stranger to fundraising as she has previously run several marathons in memory of her late husband, Leeds rugby league legend Rob Burrow.

Last year, she joined Amy to raise tens of thousands of pounds by doing a three-legged walk for Children in Need.

As the pair set off on their 80-mile (129km) trek through the region, where will the team be, and when can you see them?

Lindsey and Amy will start in North Yorkshire on Monday, setting off from Ripon marketplace at 08:30 GMT.

Speaking at the start line, the pair said they were raring to go and wanted to "do Yorkshire proud".

They will walk from Ripon to Bishop Monkton, then Burton Leonard and Ripley, where they will stop for lunch.

They hope to reach Harrogate in time for a live broadcast for Look North at 18:30 before they head to Starbeck and finish in Knaresborough at about 20:00.

## How can I donate?

![A crord of people near Ripon Cenotaph, including Amy Garcia who is about to push a trolley with a sofa on it. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/3b81/live/365071c0-dc1d-11f1-8244-051a160372a2.jpg)

A crowd gathered for the start of the challenge in Ripon Marketplace

You can donate any amount you like, as little as £1 can make a difference.

£30 pays for a new cot mattress for a family living in temporary accommodation, ensuring their baby has a safe place to sleep.

£50 pays for one to one play therapy for a child with a life limiting illness, helping them to cope with their condition.

£100 will pay for one to one therapy for a child who has been sexually exploited, helping them express their feelings and rebuild their life.
