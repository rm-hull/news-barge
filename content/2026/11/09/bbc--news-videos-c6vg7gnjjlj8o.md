# Watch: Moment judge sentences Russell Brand in court

Russell Brand has been sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and three counts of sexual assault.

Passing sentence at Southwark Crown Court on Monday, Mr Justice Bennathan said Brand was a "sexual predator" who told a "series of lies" and had displayed "staggering arrogance".
