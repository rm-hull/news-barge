A man allegedly persuaded a member of the public in York to give him £500 after claiming he needed 'emergency cash'.

North Yorkshire Police in York are investigating the incident and believe it is linked to other reports involving a man requesting 'emergency cash' because he cannot access his bank account.

**Recommended reads:**

A police spokesman said: "Do you have any information which would assist North Yorkshire Police in identifying this male and help keep your community safe?"

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21691529.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police want to identify this person following an incident in which a man allegedly got handed £500 after claiming he needed 'emergency cash'. *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Anyone with information is asked to call 101 quoting reference 12260209076.

Any information can also be passed to Crimestoppers or via the North Yorkshire Police website.
