A “selfish” rapist who launched a sickening sex attack on a teenage girl has been jailed for almost a decade.

Connor Quinn prayed on the vulnerabilities of a young girl when he raped her after giving her alcohol.

The victim disclosed the horrific ordeal to a friend and a report was made to police - with Quinn, now 31, being arrested shortly after.

He was questioned in connection with the incident but denied any wrongdoing - and Quinn, of Ulverston Gardens in Gateshead, was charged with one count of rape.

After a five-day trial at Newcastle Crown Court, on February 25, Quinn was found guilty of the offence, which happened in Gateshead.

He appeared in the same court on Friday (November 6) where he was jailed for nine years.

He will also spend a further five years on extended licence after release, and was placed on the Sex Offenders Register for life.

He was also given an indefinite restraining and Sexual Harm Prevention Orders (SHPO).

Speaking after the sentencing, Detective Constable Claire Ashurst, from Northumbria Police’s safeguarding department, said: “Connor Quinn has preyed on the vulnerabilities of someone significantly younger than him for his own selfish desires.

“As a result, the victim-survivor was subjected to an appalling and life-changing ordeal which she had to re-live throughout this investigation and trial.
