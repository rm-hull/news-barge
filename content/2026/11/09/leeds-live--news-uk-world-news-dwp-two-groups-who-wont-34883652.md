# DWP on two groups who won't get Winter Fuel Payment unless they ask

## The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has confirmed two groups of pensioners who won't get the £300 Winter Fuel Payment unless they ask

The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has identified two categories of pensioners who will need to actively apply for the £300 Winter Fuel Payment rather than receiving it automatically. To be eligible, you must have been born on or before 27 June 1960 and ordinarily reside in England, Wales or Northern Ireland.

The vast majority of eligible recipients will receive the Winter Fuel Payment without needing to do anything. However, you will need to submit a claim if either of the following circumstances applies to you: you have never previously received the Winter Fuel Payment OR you have deferred your State Pension since your last Winter Fuel Payment.

Claims can be submitted by post from 21 September 2026, and by telephone from 19 October 2026. The final deadline to submit a claim for winter 2026 to 2027 is 31 March 2027.

Eligible recipients can expect to receive a letter in October or November confirming their Winter Fuel Payment amount. If no letter arrives but you believe you qualify, check whether you need to make a claim.

The sum awarded is determined by your date of birth and your personal circumstances during the 'qualifying week' of 21 to 27 September 2026. Any payment received will have no impact on your existing benefits, reports Birmingham Live.

You do not need to claim if you already receive any of the following:.

* - State Pension.
* - Pension Credit.
* - Universal Credit.
* - Attendance Allowance.
* - Personal Independence Payment (PIP).
* - Carer's Allowance.
* - Disability Living Allowance (DLA).
* - income-related Employment and Support Allowance (ESA).
* - awards from the War Pensions Scheme.
* - Industrial Injuries Disablement Benefit.
* - Incapacity Benefit.
* - Industrial Death Benefit.

Regarding telephone claims, the DWP states: "Call the Winter Fuel Payment Centre to claim by phone.. Before you call, you'll need your National Insurance number, the date you were married or entered into a civil partnership (if appropriate) and your bank or building society details.

"Payments cannot be made into a National Savings and Investments (NSandI) account unless you already get other benefits paid into the account. You'll also need to say whether during the qualifying week of 21 to 27 September 2026 you were in hospital getting free in-patient treatment or in a residential care home or Ilford Park Resettlement Home or in prison."
