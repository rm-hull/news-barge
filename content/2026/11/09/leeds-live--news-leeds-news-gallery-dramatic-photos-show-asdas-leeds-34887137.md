# Dramatic photos show Asda's Leeds city centre base evacuated as 'suspicious package' found

## Emergency services have been at the scene during Monday afternoon

Dramatic photos capture what has been a concerning day at Asda’s Leeds city centre base.

We have been bringing you details of the ongoing incident. It has seen the headquarters of Asda, in Great Wilson Street, evacuated.

This followed a report of a 'suspicious package' at the scene. Workers at the huge building congregated on an area of grassland on the road opposite Asda House in Leeds city centre.

Emergency services have attended the incident and have been at the scene for several hours. Earlier today, West Yorkshire Police issued a statement.

They stated: "Motorists are advised to avoid parts of Leeds city centre for the coming hours due to the ongoing incident in Great Wilson Street.

"Emergency services are still at the scene and it is anticipated that road closures will remain in place throughout this evening's rush hour causing disruption to traffic. An Army EOD team has conducted a controlled explosion to a suspicious package and further investigation is ongoing. Nobody has been harmed as a result of this morning's incident and there is no wider threat to the public.

"Roads affected include: Victoria Road, Meadow Lane, Neville Street, Bridge End and Great Wilson Street. We appreciate your patience during the road closure.”
