A York GP surgery has called in the police after being hit by late night car meetings on its property.

Elvington Medical Practice, on York Road, Elvington, today (Monday) shared “with sadness” news “of antisocial behaviour taking place in our car park overnight”.

“We have had reports of cars gathering overnight and throughout the night,” the practice said.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/elvington-medical-practice-york-fb.webp)

“Due to the concern this has raised for individuals’ wellbeing, and the condition that we have found the car park to be in, we have had no choice but to report this to the police.”

Officers have told them that they will be monitoring the car park over the coming nights.

The surgery added: “If you are aware of any groups of people who may be utilising our car park for gatherings, please help us by sharing with them that this is not acceptable behaviour.”

Another business owner and resident revealed they had experienced similar problems.

“We also get groups of cars parked on the forecourt outside Rebecca’s Kitchen most nights too blaring music out and other antisocial behaviour.

“We have been out and confronted numerous groups in the middle of the night and I’m starting to fear for our own safety too.

“I feel it could be the same groups.”
