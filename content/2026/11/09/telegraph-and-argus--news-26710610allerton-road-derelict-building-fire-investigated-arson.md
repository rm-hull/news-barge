A fire in the attic of a derelict building is being investigated as arson.

Fire crews were called to reports of a derelict property blaze on Allerton Road at 3pm on Saturday, November 7.

It is currently being investigated as a suspected arson attack.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: ""We were called to Allerton Road at 15:00 on the 7th to reports of a fire at a derelict property.

"Two crews and an aerial ladder attended and extinguished a fire on the first floor/attic space of the property.

"Fire Investigation are looking into this as suspected deliberate ignition."
