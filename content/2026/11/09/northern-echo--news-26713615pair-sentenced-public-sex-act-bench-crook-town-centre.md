Members of the public were “shocked” and “appalled” after a woman performed a sex act on a man on a public bench in broad daylight in a County Durham town centre, a court heard.

Peterlee magistrates were told that, although the act was under clothing, anyone nearby would have known what Ann Louise Zachart and Christopher Waite were doing on the bench in Hope Street, Crook, on Sunday, March 18.

An “absolutely disgusted” witness filmed them as evidence, but the footage later found its way onto social media, causing humiliation for both of the participants.

Prosecutor James Gallagher said the witness confronted them and told them to “move away”.

The witness did not know how long the act had been going on or whether anyone else had confronted them.

![Court snatch offender pix.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692662.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ann Zachart, right, committed a sex act on Christopher Waite on a public bench in Crook town centre in broad daylight on Sunday March 18 *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

Police were informed, and Zachart and Waite were arrested and charged with outraging public decency.

Appearing separately at Peterlee Magistrates’ Court today (Monday, November 9) 43-year-old Zachart, of Arthur Terrace, Stanley Crook, and Waite, 29, of Lister Terrace, Wolsingham, pleaded guilty at their first hearing in the case.

Josh Peacock, for Zachart, said it was clearly “not very discreet”, but not everyone would have seen the act as it was under clothing.

He said Zachart, who was of previous good character, was, “very upset at what she had done” and had regretted it every day since.

![Snatch court pic](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692663.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ann Louise Zachart performed a sex act on co-accused in Crook town centre, on Sunday March 18 *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

The court heard that Zachart had little memory of the incident but admitted it at the first opportunity.

Amrit Jandoo, representing Waite, said he had also pleaded guilty at the earliest opportunity.

Mr Jandoo said the defendant, who is on the autistic spectrum, was caught up in the “intimate encounter”, “absorbed” in what was happening and “didn’t appreciate other people were going about their business” in the town centre that day.

“It was an intimate act and shouldn’t have been carried out on the high street.

“I doubt very much the witness did it (filmed the act) out of malice, but it made it onto social media, and he’s been the subject of public attention in his local area.”

![Court snatch offender pic](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692670.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Public sex act in Crook on March 18 left participant Christopher Waite £365 out of pocket at court today (Mon Nov 9) *(Image: The Northern Echo)*

“He's genuinely embarrassed at placing him and his family in such a predicament.”

The offence put Waite in breach of a community order imposed last April for sexual activity with a woman. He had been ordered to complete 150 hours of unpaid work of which he has completed 85 hours, having spent six months on remand.

Magistrates’ chairman Keith Richardson told Zachart it was “always disappointing” when someone of previous good character loses their good name, particularly at the age of 43.

She was given a six-month conditional discharge and ordered her to pay £111 in costs and statutory surcharge.

Mr Richardson told her: “I think you realise how stupid you were.”

He said due to Waite’s previous offending he would be punished more harshly, with a total bill of £365 in fines, costs and surcharge.

Mr Richardson told Waite: “What a ridiculous situation you got yourself into.

“Because of your previous history we have treated this more seriously than with your co-accused.”
