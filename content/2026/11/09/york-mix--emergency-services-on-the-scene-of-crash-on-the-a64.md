Emergency services are on the scene of a crash on the A64 tonight (Monday).

It’s near Potter Brompton between Ganton and Sherburn.

North Yorkshire Police said at 7.39pm: “We are currently at the scene of a road traffic collision at Potter Brompton on the A64.

#### Crash location

“The road was blocked, but we are now allowing traffic through as a single carriageway under traffic control of the officers at the scene.

“Please prepare for delays in the area whilst we await the recovery of the vehicles involved.”
