A new filling station and convenience store can be built on a vacant site on one of Bradford’s busiest roads.

Earlier this year an application to demolish the former Mercedes Benz dealership on Thornton Road was approved by Bradford Council.

Applicants EG on the Move said the demolition would make way for a “two-phase redevelopment” of the site – which lies within an Employment Zone.

Shortly after, the company, which owns and operates forecourt sites across the country, submitted a planning application for the first phase of that development.

The application was for the construction of a petrol filling station, a linked 477 square metre “kiosk”, associated parking including four EV charging bays and landscaping.

The development would create 20 jobs, and access to the site would be via Brownroyd Street.

Planners have now approved the application, saying: “Whilst a petrol filling station is not a traditional industrial or business use, it would provide a valuable service to the wider Employment Zone and surrounding area.

“The development would bring a vacant brownfield site back into productive use, support local economic activity and generate employment opportunities, whilst preserving the overall function and purpose of the Employment Zone.

“Whilst the redevelopment would alter the appearance of the site, it would introduce a modern and well-designed development that would sit comfortably within its surroundings.

“The landscaping proposals would further enhance the site's appearance through the introduction of native hedgerows, trees, species-rich grassland and ornamental planting, whilst retaining existing boundary trees.

"These measures would soften the development, improve visual amenity and provide a greener and more attractive setting.”
