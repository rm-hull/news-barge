Edinburgh Zoo’s capybara triplets have been named Tattie, Tunnock and Toddy following a public vote.

The Royal Zoological Society of Scotland (RZSS), which runs the zoo, announced the winning names, with Tunnock and Toddy given to the two males and Tattie to their sister.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21691394.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Capybara triplets. *(Image: RZSS)*The names beat other shortlisted suggestions inspired by the Scottish Isles and the sitcom* Still Game*.

Born to parents Luna and Rodney, the three pups were first spotted on October 28 and quickly attracted attention on social media.

They follow in the footsteps of Bru, a capybara born at the zoo in May who was the first of his kind to be born in Scotland in 18 years.

Their arrival also renewed interest in their father Rodney, whose much-publicised romantic escapades have included a photograph of him sharing a pool with Luna’s sister, Cali.

RZSS is a conservation charity that also manages Highland Wildlife Park. It works to protect wildlife and connect people with the natural world, using its animal parks to help visitors learn about conservation and the threats facing species.

The organisation was also recognised for its work on inclusivity, winning two awards at the Scottish Thistle Awards 2026.
