During the winter months, working at a computer can be uncomfortable, especially if suffer from cold hands like I do. The Afenau heated mouse pad offers a simple solution, warming the surface beneath your keyboard and mouse while doubling as a full-size desk mat.

With three temperature settings, a waterproof surface, and a built-in digital display, it's designed to make long sessions at your desk far more comfortable.

**Best For:** Home workers, students, gamers, and anyone who spends hours at a computer in a chilly room.**The Deal:** The Afenau 80 x 33cm heated mouse pad costs £17.59 at Amazon. It offers three heat settings, a waterproof PU leather surface, and automatic shut-off.

This heated mouse pad features an 80 x 33cm waterproof polyurethane surface, three temperature settings from 40°C to 50°C, digital display, rapid heating, non-slip base, automatic shut-off, and sitting reminders.

## Why we recommend it

With heating bills remaining a concern during the cost of living crisis, the Afenau heated mouse pad offers a way to keep your hands toasty warm without turning up the heating. Measuring 80 x 33cm, it provides enough space for a keyboard and mouse, with heating elements that cover 95% of its surface.

There are three selectable temperatures: 40°C, 45°C, and 50°C and the digital display in the upper-left corner shows the selected temperature, which the heating system can reach in 10 seconds.

The polyurethane surface is waterproof and resistant to everyday spills and scratches, with a non-slip underside to keep everything in place. At 550g, the mat can be rolled up for storage when you're not using it.

There's a visual reminder designed to encourage you to take a break after an hour of sitting and automatic shut-off offers another safeguard.

## Price context & historical value

At £17.59, the Afenau heated mouse pad is an inexpensive way to add warmth to your desk without investing in a heater. Its combo of adjustable temperatures, waterproof construction, and digital controls provide several features beyond those of a conventional desk mat.

This is the cheapest price we've seen it at, so if you want to stay warm while working this winter, I'd definitely recommend it.

## Should you buy it?

✅ **Buy the Afenau heated mouse pad if...**

If you work in a chilly room or suffer from cold hands, this is a great solution. The large dimensions, three heat settings, and wipe-clean finish make it a practical desk accessory.

❌ **Skip the Afenau heated mouse pad if...**

If your workspace is already warm or you need something that heats the entire room. No manufacturer warranty is specified either.
