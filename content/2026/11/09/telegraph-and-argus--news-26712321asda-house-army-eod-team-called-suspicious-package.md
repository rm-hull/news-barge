An Army EOD team has been called Asda's headquarters in Leeds after police received reports of a suspicious package this morning.

West Yorkshire Police said the Asda House building on Great Wilson Street was evacuated as a precaution and a cordon was put in place.

Police received the call at around 11.16am.

Emergency services are at the scene, with road closures affecting motorists.

Police have asked motorists and members of the public to avoid the Great Wilson Street area and find alternative routes.

The public are still being urged to avoid the area because of the ongoing incident.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21690607.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A Google Maps view of Asda House. *(Image: Google Maps)*

## What police have said

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Motorists and members of the public are advised to avoid the Great Wilson Street area of Leeds city centre while emergency services deal with an ongoing incident.

"At 11.16am this morning, police received reports of a suspicious package having been sent to Asda House, Great Wilson Street, Leeds.

"Emergency services are currently at the scene and an Army EOD team has been called to attend.

"The building has been evacuated as a precaution and a cordon is in place.

"Motorists are asked to find alternative routes due to nearby road closures."

The spokesperson added: "Ongoing incident in Leeds city centre.

"Members of the public are being asked to avoid the Great Wilson Street area."
