**A bomb disposal team has been called to Lanarkshire after what is believed to be possible ordnance was discovered near a premises in Larkhall.**

Police were alerted to the item on Broomhill Road at around 9.05am on Monday.

Police have established a cordon, with Broomhill Road and Broomhill Drive closed while emergency services deal with the incident.

Access to Larkhall Academy and the nearby leisure centre has been blocked off as emergency services continue to deal with the incident.

Staff have been evacuated from a nearby premises as a precaution, and the public has been urged to avoid the area.

Larkhall Academy has been closed as a result of this morning's alert, South Lanarkshire Council has confirmed.

No pupils were at the school today because of a scheduled in-service day.

However, after the alert, staff at the school were sent home.

A council spokesperson said: “There is an in-service day at Larkhall Academy today, so the school was closed to pupils. Following this morning's alert, school staff were sent home, and the school was closed.”

Specialist Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) officers have attended and are assessing the item.

Police Scotland said the incident remains ongoing.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "Around 9.05am on Monday, 9 November, 2026 we received a report of possible historic ordnance found near to a premises in Broomhill Road, Larkhall.

"Broomhill Road and Broomhill Drive are closed and a cordon is in place.

"Staff have been evacuated from the premises as a precaution and people are advised to avoid the area.

"Explosive Ordnance Disposal will attend to assess the item."
