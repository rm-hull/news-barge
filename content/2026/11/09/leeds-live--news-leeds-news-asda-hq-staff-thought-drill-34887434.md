# Asda HQ staff 'thought it was a drill' until told to get out immediately over suspicious package

## "We didn’t know what the situation was"

Staff at Asda’s Leeds HQ had no idea what was happening until they were told to get out immediately.

Workers at Asda House, on Great Wilson Street, were evacuated today (Monday, November 9) after a suspicious package was sent to the building. An Army bomb disposal team later carried out a controlled explosion.

One worker, who was waiting to pick up their car, said: "The alarm didn’t go off at the beginning. Someone came round our desks and told us to get out immediately. We thought it was a drill, to begin with."

Another worker, who didn’t want to be named, said: "We trust in the process that everything will be good. We got instructions to evacuate and we heard the emergency fire alarm. Generally, we are trained to respond to these emergency situations. We didn’t know what the situation was."

Staff gathered on an area of grass on the road opposite the supermarket giant’s headquarters as police put a cordon around the building and closed surrounding roads.

West Yorkshire Police said officers were called at 11.16am after reports of a suspicious package having been sent to Asda House. The building was evacuated as a precaution and an Army Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) team was called.

Police, ambulance and fire crews were at the scene throughout the afternoon, along with Army personnel and bomb disposal vans.

In an update, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "An Army EOD team have conducted a controlled explosion to a suspicious package and further investigation is ongoing.

"Nobody has been harmed as a result of this morning’s incident and there is no wider threat to the public."

Motorists have been warned to avoid parts of the city centre, with road closures expected to remain in place throughout the evening rush hour. Roads affected include Victoria Road, Meadow Lane, Neville Street, Bridge End and Great Wilson Street.

Traffic monitoring service Inrix reported Great Wilson Street was closed from Asda House to Crown Point Road, with heavy congestion on Neville Street and Victoria Road.
