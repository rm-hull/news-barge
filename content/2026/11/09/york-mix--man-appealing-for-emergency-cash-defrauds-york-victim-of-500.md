A man has defrauded a member of the public of £500 in York.

Police have now issued these pictures of a man they want to talk to about the crime.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Do you have any information which would assist police in identifying this male and help keep your community safe?

“This is similar to other reports to North Yorkshire Police from members of the public in the York area about a male asking for ’emergency cash’ due to not being able to access his bank account.”
