Maya Jama has announced she is stepping down as host of ITV’s Love Island after three years on the job.

The 32-year-old presenter took over hosting duties from Laura Whitmore in October 2022 and fronted nine series of the popular dating show during her time in the role.

In a heartfelt statement shared on social media, Ms Jama said: "What an incredible era and what an unforgettable time I’ve had, it’s been far too much fun to put into words or a video..but as all good things must come to an end, it’s my time to step down as the host of my beloved Love Island.

"Firstly, I want to thank all of the people who rooted so hard for me to host the show in the first place."

She also expressed her gratitude to fans, writing: "To the fans of the show, thank you for every supportive message and every single time you’ve tuned in during my time as host, I’m forever grateful to you all.

"The show really does belong to the people who watch it and embrace it year after year."

Reflecting on her journey, Ms Jama said: "I started hosting the show aged 28 and during the 9 incredible series met the most wonderful humans along the way.

"I’ve loved every slow-mo walk, fire pit and live show.

"I’ll forever be grateful for the experience."

Although she has not shared details of her next steps, she hinted at new opportunities ahead.

She said: "But I’ve always believed that to truly grow, you have to take a leap every now and then, and now is my time to throw myself into something new."

ITV has yet to announce her replacement.

A spokesperson for the broadcaster told The Sun: "Maya has been a fantastic and much-loved host on Love Island – we thank her for everything she has brought to the show over the last three years and wish her every continued success."

She went on to donate her potential winnings from the show to charity.

Action for Children confirmed she donated £83,500 to support their work.
