POLICE were to a suspected incident in a North Yorkshire at the weekend.

Officers attended the scene at the Premier store in Commercial Street, Norton.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "At around 2.27pm on Saturday 7 November, we received a report of a suspected shop theft on Commercial Street in Norton. No arrests have been made. Our enquiries remain ongoing."
