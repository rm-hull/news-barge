In Dakar, Senegal, professional surfers Ibra Samb and Cherif Fall offer free lessons to local children, sharing skills they first learned on scraps of plywood. As the city hosts the Youth Olympic Games, surfers hope for greater visibility and support for their growing sport, which faces hurdles including limited equipment, costly travel and ocean pollution.
