A man has appeared in court charged with sexual assault and voyeurism after a hidden camera was found in a North East gym.

Craig Bayliss stood in the dock at Teesside Magistrates’ Court last Tuesday (November 3) charged with a string of offences.

It follows an incident at an East Cleveland gym at around 1.40pm on November 1 when officers were called to reports of videos being found on a hidden camera.

The 62-year-old was arrested and charged with two counts of voyeurism, sexual assault and observing a person carrying out a private act.

He was also charged with three counts of possessing an offensive weapon in a private place, possessing a weapon designed to discharge a noxious liquid, gas or electrical incapacitation device, and possessing a class C drug.

Bayliss indicated a guilty plea to one count of possessing an offensive weapon in a private place and possessing a weapon designed to discharge a noxious liquid.

He indicated a not guilty plea for two other counts of possessing an offensive weapon in a private place and entered no pleas to all other charges.

The defendant, of Applewaith Gardens, Skelton, was released on conditional bail and will next appear at Teesside Crown Court on December 2.

Cleveland Police also previously arrested a 60-year-old man on suspicion of voyeurism in connection with the investigation.
