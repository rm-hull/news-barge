A £100 million hotel development has completed a £350 million regeneration project in Edinburgh’s Haymarket district.

The 351-room Hyatt Centric hotel marks the final phase of M&G’s transformation of the area, backed by its £136 billion With-Profits Fund.

Designed by Foster + Partners, the hotel is expected to open in 2028. The development is estimated to create up to 450 construction jobs and 110 permanent roles once operational.

The hotel is next to Haymarket Station and close to the Edinburgh International Conference Centre. It will include social spaces, a restaurant and bar intended to attract both guests and members of the public, alongside contemporary guest facilities.

The wider Haymarket development has created a mixed-use district that is now home to businesses including Baillie Gifford, Deloitte, Dentons, Shoosmiths and Shepherd & Wedderburn.

Martin Towns, head of global real estate at M&G Real Estate, said: “This investment completes a major new district for Edinburgh, combining high-quality design, a strong operating partner and an exceptional location.

“M&G is a significant investor in Scotland and projects like this show how we deploy capital into high conviction real assets that support the long-term strength of both the city and our portfolio, while adding momentum to the Haymarket area and one of the UK’s most popular visitor destinations.”

Ciaran Mulligan, chief investment officer at M&G Life, said: “Haymarket is a powerful example of how long-term savings can be put to work in the real economy, helping to shape communities, support jobs and create long-term value for customers.

“Our research shows people are more engaged with their pensions when they can see the impact their savings are having, and investments like this bring that connection to life.

“By supporting regeneration and job creation, investments such as Haymarket can help build momentum in the local economy, contributing to the area's transformation into a thriving destination while supporting long-term growth and opportunity.”

Anna Corkill, regional vice president development at Hyatt, said: “Hyatt Centric Haymarket Edinburgh reflects Hyatt’s strong confidence in the UK as a growth market, particularly in internationally recognised regional cities with distinct cultural identities and year-round demand.

“The Hyatt Centric brand continues to grow rapidly within Hyatt’s Classics portfolio, with standout locations including Madrid, Milan, Venice and Istanbul, and we are excited that the brand is contributing to the area’s ongoing regeneration while creating a vibrant new destination for visitors and the local community alike.”
