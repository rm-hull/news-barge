# Live blog: ASDA HQ evacuated after 'suspicious package' found as bomb squad on scene

## ASDA’s headquarters on Great Wilson Street in Leeds has been evacuated this afternoon, with several police vehicles spotted in the area.

An eyewitness said staff had been sent home and were not being allowed to enter the car park to retrieve their cars.

West Yorkshire Police has urged the public to avoid the area. You can read their full statement further down on this page.

ASDA has been contacted for more information.
