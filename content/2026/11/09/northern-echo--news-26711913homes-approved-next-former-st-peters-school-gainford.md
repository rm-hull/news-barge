Plans for more homes next to the site of a long-abandoned former County Durham school have been given the go-ahead.

Durham County Council has granted permission in principle for up to six homes on land next to the former St Peter's School site, off Main Road in Gainford.

It is part of the wider redevelopment of the former school site, with permission previously granted for a total of 48 homes.

Council planners said the additional six properties, to be built by Kebbell Development, would be a "logical and proportional extension" of that development.

The land earmarked for the latest homes covers around 0.23 hectares and sits alongside the A67, on the eastern edge of the village. It has not previously been developed.

![It was knocked down in 2024 after becoming a vandalism hotspot.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20113342.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It was knocked down in 2024 after becoming a vandalism hotspot. *(Image: Tom Banks)*

One objection was submitted, arguing that building on the site would push development further into the countryside and that new homes should instead be restricted to previously developed land.

Planning officers acknowledged that building on the greenfield site would cause some harm to the surrounding landscape.

However, they said the benefits of providing six homes outweighed that harm.

Final design, layout and access plans will need further approval before the homes can be built.

## From orphanage to decades of dereliction

The neighbouring St Peter's building had stood derelict for decades before being demolished in 2024.
