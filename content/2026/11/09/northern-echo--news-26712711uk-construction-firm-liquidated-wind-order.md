A construction firm that says it worked on The Shard has been ordered to be wound up.

Facade Group Ltd, which says it is headquartered in Cardiff, was subject to a High Court winding-up order on November 4, according to a notice in The Gazette.

The official receiver was appointed as liquidator on the same date, the notice says.

A separate notice shows that HM Revenue and Customs presented the petition to wind up the company on September 16.

A winding-up order places a company into compulsory liquidation, with a liquidator responsible for dealing with its assets and creditors.

![Construction firm for 'UK's most recognised buildings' to be liquidated](https://www.newsshopper.co.uk/resources/images/21688520.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Construction firm for 'UK's most recognised buildings' to be liquidated *(Image: Kennedy News & Media)*

Facade Group says on its website that it has provided services at The Shard, Chelsea Bridge and Canary Wharf.

It also describes work at Fulham FC's Craven Cottage, including painting over existing logos on the Putney stand and repainting the roof ahead of new sponsorship branding.

The firm's website lists glass polishing, maintenance, water testing, sound testing and rope access among its services.

It says its team has more than 70 years of combined experience and that the business works across the UK.

Other projects listed on the website include water testing in central London and Manchester, and abseiling work for a client in London.

The company was incorporated in March 2021, according to the original report.

The Gazette gives its registered office as being in Aberkenfig, Bridgend.
