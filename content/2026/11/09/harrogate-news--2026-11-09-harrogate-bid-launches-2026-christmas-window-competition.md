**Harrogate Business Improvement District (BID) has opened entries for its 2026 Christmas Window Competition, inviting businesses across the town centre to create festive displays.**

The competition is organised by Harrogate BID, which is encouraging local businesses to decorate their windows and help create a festive atmosphere for residents and visitors.

This year, Harrogate BID is partnering with the Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce to recognise businesses taking part and celebrate the effort that goes into their Christmas displays.

## Four categories for local businesses

Businesses can enter one of four categories:

* Shops
* Pubs and restaurants
* Hotels
* Professional services

Displays will be judged against three criteria: illumination, innovation and the spirit of Christmas.

Judging is scheduled to take place on Thursday 3 December 2026, starting at 4pm. Winners will be announced at a dedicated awards evening on Thursday 10 December.

As well as category winners and an overall competition champion, the judges will present a number of special discretionary awards.

## Competition celebrates Harrogate businesses

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said:

> **Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID**
>
> The Christmas Window Competition has become a much-loved part of Harrogate’s festive celebrations, and we’re delighted to launch it once again for 2026.
>
> Every year we’re incredibly impressed by the creativity and effort that local businesses put into their displays. The competition not only helps create a magical atmosphere throughout the town centre, but also encourages residents and visitors to explore Harrogate and support local businesses during the festive season.
>
> We’re pleased to be partnering with the Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce this year and look forward to seeing businesses across all sectors bring festive cheer to the town.

The competition forms part of Harrogate BID’s wider Christmas programme, which aims to create a welcoming and vibrant town centre while supporting local businesses during the festive trading period.

## How to enter

Entries are now open. Businesses wishing to take part can complete the online entry form on the Harrogate BID website.

For further information, businesses can contact Harrogate BID by emailing [info@harrogatebid.co.uk.](mailto:info@harrogatebid.co.uk.)

*The competition is being run by Harrogate BID, with the Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce partnering for the 2026 event.*
