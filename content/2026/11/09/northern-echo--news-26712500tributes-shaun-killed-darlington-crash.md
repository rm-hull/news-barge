The partner of a dad killed in a Darlington crash has vowed to "take care of our little girl" in a heart breaking message left at the scene.

'Shaun', who was in his 40s, was pronounced dead after his van hit a tree on Woodland Road near the junction with West Crescent, shortly after 2am on Friday (November 6).

Police said he was the only person in the van at the time and no other vehicles were involved.

Floral tributes have now been left at the scene by his devastated loved ones. His partner has written a note to her "prawn" vowing to take care of their little girl.

It reads: "My prawn.

"Love you so much, I can't believe you're gone. I will take care of our little girl. Until we meet again. Forever yours."

![A floral tribute at the scene on Woodland Road, Darlington](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690961.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A floral tribute at the scene on Woodland Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The van driver has not yet been officially named but has been referred to in messages as 'Shaun'.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690962.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Flowers laid on Woodland Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690974.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police at the scene of the fatal crash on Friday (November 6)*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690972.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police cordon was in place after the fatal crash on Friday (November 6)*(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

After the crash, the road was cordoned off by police while crash investigators examined the scene, and the van was recovered.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21690973.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A road closed sign was also in place after the fatal crash on Friday (November 6) *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The crash caused widespread disruption, with nearby roads closed, bus services diverted and pedestrians prevented from entering the cordoned-off area.

A Durham Police investigation into the crash is ongoing.

A spokesperson for the North East Ambulance Service said previously: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Woodland Road in Darlington at 2.05am on Friday.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene."
