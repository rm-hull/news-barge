Police are urgently searching for a man wanted in connection with a serious assault.

Cameron Ramsey, 34, is being sought by North Yorkshire Police following an incident that took place outside the Bruce Arms in Masham on Saturday (November 7).

Ramsey is described as mixed race, in his 30s, with bald or very closely shaved hair and dark eyebrows.




He was last seen wearing a long grey padded coat with a large hood, a dark grey Berghaus tracksuit, and black On Cloud trainers.

![Police have warned members of the public not to approach Ramsey](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21686941.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police have warned members of the public not to approach Ramsey *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "If you have seen Cameron Ramsey, or have any information about his whereabouts, please contact North Yorkshire Police immediately.

"Please do not approach Ramsey yourself."

Anyone who has seen him or knows of his whereabouts is urged to call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260219596.
