Ripon Grammar School has been ranked as the highest-performing secondary school in North Yorkshire in the latest Department for Education GCSE performance tables.

Provisional figures for the 2025/26 academic year place the Ripon school first among the county’s 41 secondary schools, with an Attainment 8 score of 69.9.

The school recorded a higher score than Skipton Girls’ High School, which achieved 67.5, and Ermysted’s Grammar School in Skipton, which recorded 64.3.

The Department for Education’s Attainment 8 measure assesses pupils’ average achievement across eight GCSE subjects, including English and mathematics alongside a range of other approved qualifications.

A spokesperson for Ripon Grammar School said: “It’s a brilliant achievement and a testament to the hard work, dedication and ambition of our students, alongside the commitment and expertise of our teaching staff.

“Congratulations to everyone who makes Ripon Grammar School such a special place to learn and achieve.”

Attainment 8 scores provide an indication of overall GCSE attainment, but do not measure the progress pupils make during their time at school or reflect the full breadth of a school’s educational provision.

The figures are provisional and remain subject to revision by the Department for Education before the final performance tables are published.
