Fourteen workers at Molson Group's Darlington depot have been made redundant after the machinery dealer collapsed.

Administrators from PWC UK confirmed 14 employees at Red Barnes Way in Darlington have lost their jobs.

PWC said the firm had faced challenging trading conditions and weaker demand in the UK construction market.

The business employed 345 people across the UK before it entered administration.

Ross Connock, Tom Crookham and Toby Banfield of PWC were appointed joint administrators of Molson Group Limited and its trading subsidiaries, Molson Equipment Services, Capital Equipment Finance, Aggregate Processing Solutions and Molson Scotland.

Mr Connock said: “It is regrettable that Molson Group's UK businesses have entered administration and that this has resulted in a significant number of redundancies.

“We recognise how difficult this will be for those affected and their families.

“Our immediate priority is to support affected employees through the redundancy process, while working with other stakeholders to undertake an orderly wind-down of the UK businesses and maximise value from the companies' assets.”

Administrators said a range of options had been explored in an effort to rescue the company, including a sale of the UK operations and assets.

However, no buyer was able to complete a deal before the business ran out of time, meaning the UK arm will now be wound down.

The company's interests in the United States have been preserved through a separate transaction.

Shares in Molson's American operations have been transferred to Fieldsec Hastings Limited, alongside a small number of UK employees and all US staff.

Mr Connock added: “The sale of the US businesses means those operations can continue outside the UK Group, preserving the business and employment there.”

A notice on the company's website now confirms the appointment of joint administrators as efforts continue to wind down its UK operations.
