A record number of wreaths were officially laid during Wetherby Remembrance Sunday tribute to the fallen of world conflicts.

President of the Wetherby and District Branch of the Royal British Legion, Edna Hilditch, said the town’s event was well supported by the local community.

“We had 42 wreath layers this year, a record amount, so we had to form groups to ensure they were laid in good time to march to St James Church for the 11.30 Remembrance Service.

“The parade was again well supported by the local community.

“Fortunately the weather was kind. We have been very lucky over the years to have had fine weather.”

The parade of community groups and dignitaries, led by Wetherby Silver Band, marched from the Town Hall to the Bridge Foot war memorial.

Following the laying of wreaths, service and two minutes silence, the parade moved on to St James’ Church for a service of remembrance.

A spokesperson for Wetherby Air Cadets, which took part in the parade, said the event was a poignant reminder of the importance of taking part.

“We were proud to be at the Remembrance Sunday parade.

“The cadets conducted themselves brilliantly and helped us all remember that young men and women not much older left Wetherby years ago, to protect our way of life. “
