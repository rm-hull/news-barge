Emergency services were called out after youths were seen setting off fireworks dangerously in York.

Firefighters went to the incident on **Willow Bank**, Huntington at** 8.18pm yesterday** (Sunday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “A crew from Huntington responded to a report of youths setting off fireworks dangerously, causing them to explode on grass.

#### Willow Bank, York

“On arrival the youths had dispersed and no fire was located. Crews liaised with the police.”
