North East families sending Christmas presents across the UK and overseas are being urged to post early after Evri announced its final Christmas posting dates.

The deadlines apply to customers using Evri’s hundreds of ParcelShops and lockers across the North East, with the company issuing advice for smooth Christmas deliveries.

Standard parcels sent through a ParcelShop or locker must be posted by 11am on December 19.

Next day parcels must be sent by 11am on December 21, while courier collections must be booked by December 18.

David Saenz, chief commercial officer at Evri, said staff would be working throughout the festive season to "keep parcels moving and help deliver Christmas" for customers.

Mr Saenz added: "Christmas is all about connecting with the people who matter to us, and for millions of people that means sending presents to loved ones across the country.

"We know how important it is for those gifts to arrive in time for Christmas, so our advice is simple: send early wherever you can and make a note of the last-send date for the service you’re using."

Evri is encouraging customers to plan ahead and take note of the deadlines for the service they are using.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20377751.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Evri has confirmed its last-send dates for this festive season to help ensure gifts arrive in time for Christmas. *(Image: Evri/PA Wire)*

Evri urged North East residents sending gifts overseas to check the deadline for their destination.

Parcels to Australia, Canada and the USA must be sent by December 4, while those bound for France, Germany, Spain and Ireland must be sent by December 10.

A full list of international destinations and final posting dates is available on the company’s website.

Evri’s will operate up to and including Christmas Eve.

There will be no deliveries on Christmas Day, Boxing Day or New Year’s Day.

Normal deliveries will resume after the holidays.

Families are being urged to avoid the stress of last-minute posting as the Christmas sending season gets under way. Planning ahead and following Evri’s advice can help customers see their gifts arrive safely and on time.
