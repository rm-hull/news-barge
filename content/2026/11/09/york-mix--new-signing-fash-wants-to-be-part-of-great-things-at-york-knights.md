York Knights have signed free agent forward Brad Fash on a two year deal thanks to support from the squad builder fund.

Fash joins York after his contract from Hull FC expired. He was at the club since he was a youth player.

He also spent time on loan with Toronto Wolfpack and Leigh Centurions.

Fash made his debut for Hull against Castleford in July 2015 and went on to play more than 200 games for the side, earning a testimony in 2025.

During his time with Hull, he was named a member of the England Knights squad that played Papau New Guinea in 2018.

Joining as a loose forward, he brings a wealth of Super League experience to the Knights ahead of their second season in the top division.

Looking to build on a tenth-place finish from last year, Knights head coach Mark Applegarth said “I’m really excited to see him in a York shirt.

“I think he’ll bring a lot of passion, and he’ll do a lot of the dirty work for us. I think he’ll be great for us moving forward.”

Fash was equally enthusiastic. “I know the club and team are capable of achieving great things and I want to be a part of that,” he said.
