* **Rockstar Games announces Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival for November 21**
* **The special live concert will feature music from artists in the game and on the official soundtrack**
* **A pre-sale lottery for tickets will be available on November 11**

Rockstar Games has announced Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival, a special live concert scheduled for after *GTA 6*'s launch and featuring music from the game.

The Festival is set for November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami and will feature a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack across two stages.

Those performances include: Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, and Sofaygo; Cardi B; Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso; Justice (DJ set); Metro Boomin; Nick León B2B DJ Python; Prospa B2B Skream; Rauw & Friends; Suero; Tainy; The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set).

![Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/zNLj5RTYMuXUScMSerW4uP-1200-80.jpg)

Rockstar has confirmed that a pre-sale lottery will be available for a chance to purchase tickets, beginning on Wednesday, November 11.

Tickets are limited to two per pre-sale lottery code, and the lottery closes at 4PM ET / 9PM GMT on Tuesday, November 10.

Fans can sign up for the GTA 6: The Festival lottery here, with entries limited to a maximum of one entry per person. Entries must also be from people aged 17+.

"Winners will be drawn at random from eligible entries and contacted by SMS with a code on Tuesday, November 10 by 7 P.M. ET," Rockstar said. "Odds of being selected will depend on the number of eligible entries received."
