The highlight of the week is the "Berlin Freedom Conference" on Tuesday at the Colosseum movie theater in Prenzlauer Berg. Experts will discuss freedom, democracy and the theme "Change is possible." Among them is Putin critic and former world chess champion Garry Kasparov. Prior to that, on Monday, the Bundestag will address women's rights and democratic culture. Bundestag President Julia Klöckner (CDU) will also participate in the "Freedom and Security Women’s Summit." Also expected to attend are female opposition politicians and human rights activists from countries such as Belarus, Iran, Afghanistan and Cuba.
