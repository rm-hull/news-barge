US president Donald Trump suggested that a peace deal could involve reshaping Ukraine.

“I think we’re gonna have it done, and I think it’s gonna happen and they’re gonna end up with a little bit different shape Ukraine,” Trump said in an interview with CBS’s *60 Minutes.*

Asked whether Russian president Vladimir Putin was deliberately prolonging negotiations, Trump replied: “Oh, I don’t think so.”

Meanwhile, according to data leaked from Russia’s Kremlin statistics agency, at least 390,000 Russians have been killed in the Ukraine war.

Two separate hacker groups, Silent Crown and Belarus Cyber Partisans, claimed responsibility for cyberattacks on the Kremlin’s ‘Rosstat’ statistics agency where they alleged to steal data on Russia’s economy, demographics and wartime losses, according to the *Moscow* * Times.*

Silent Crow claimed its analysis showed that 450,100 Russians had been killed in the war with Ukraine, while the Belarus Cyber Partisans released a lower figure of 393,881 killed, the paper reported.

It comes after Russia said on Sunday that it had struck three cargo vessels and two electricity substations that it claimed were supporting Ukraine, ahead of a US-brokered trilateral meeting this week aimed at ending the war.

## UK military chief issues stark warning over Russian threat

Britain is falling behind its European allies in confronting the “very real” threat posed by Russia, the head of the UK military has warned.

Air Chief Marshal Sir Richard Knighton told the *FT* that the UK needs to “catch up with our European allies” who, he said, are more awake to the Kremlin’s mounting appetite for risk.

“This is the most dangerous period I’ve known in my adult life,” the chief of the defence staff, 57, said, adding: “That threat - principally from Russia, but more broadly globally - is rising.”

Sir Richard assessed that Moscow was increasingly tolerant to risk and told the newspaper that the term “hybrid activity” no longer captured the scale of Russia’s alleged disruptions.

“We’re talking about targeted assassinations, we’re talking about sabotage, we’re talking about cyber attacks,” he said. “These are very real, real things [by] the Russian state we have proof of in Europe and to some extent in the UK.”

## At least 390,000 Russians killed in conflict - report

At least 390,000 Russians have been killed in conflict, according to data leaked from Russia’s Kremlin statistics agency.

Two separate hacker groups, Silent Crown and Belarus Cyber Partisans, claimed responsibility for cyberattacks on the Kremlin’s ‘Rosstat’ statistics agency where they alleged to steal data on Russia's economy, demographics and wartime losses, according to the *Moscow Times.*

Silent Crow claimed that 450,100 Russians had been killed in the war with Ukraine, while the Belarus Cyber Partisans released a lower figure of 393,881 killed, the paper reported.

## Russia hits Ukrainian power substation and container ship

The Russian defence ministry said its forces hit energy infrastructure used by the Ukrainian military.

A power substation in Ukraine's Zhytomyr region, ‌which supplied ​electricity ‌to Ukraine's ⁠defence industry, was targeted, the ministry said.

Moscow also hit a container ship delivering weapons and military equipment to Ukraine south-west of the Black Sea port city of Odesa, the defence ministry said.

## Russia likely behind bomb hoaxes that grounded flights, say European intelligence

European security officials are investigating whether the series of bomb threats against European airlines and news organisations originated in Russia.

Sky said it received a security threat amid a wide range of hoax threats disrupting passenger flights across Europe this week.

Newsrooms in Ireland and Bulgaria also received emailed bomb threats on Friday from a purported “Mikhail Andropov”, who claimed responsibility for the hoax threats seen across the continent this week.
