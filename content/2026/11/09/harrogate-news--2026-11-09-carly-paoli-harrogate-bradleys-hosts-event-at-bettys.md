**Harrogate jewellers Bradleys hosted a special afternoon tea at Bettys Café Tearooms, featuring a private performance by renowned classical singer Carly Paoli.**

The event, held in collaboration with Italian jewellery brand Fope, took place in the Imperial Suite at Bettys Café Tearooms in Harrogate.

Guests enjoyed afternoon tea while listening to Carly Paoli perform a selection of her favourite songs.

A classically trained soprano, Paoli has an extensive repertoire spanning opera, classical music and classical crossover.

The event was also attended by Harrogate Mayor, Councillor Viv Poskitt.

Alongside the musical performance, guests had the opportunity to view the latest Fope jewellery collections, including pieces from its exclusive 18-carat gold ranges.

Fope is an Italian jewellery brand established in the 1920s, internationally recognised for its Flex’it technology. The brand is represented by Bradleys in its stores across North Yorkshire.

> **Peter Bradley Jr, Sales Director at Bradleys**
>
> We were delighted to host Carly in Harrogate and to showcase the stunning collection of Fope jewellery pieces. Thank you to Mayor Viv Poskitt for joining us and to all our guests for making it such a memorable event.

The afternoon brought together music, jewellery and hospitality in the surroundings of one of Harrogate’s best-known venues.
