A historic pub in a North Yorkshire coastal town is set to re-open today after more than three years.

The Jolly Sailors will welcome customers along St Ann’s Staith, a short stroll from Whitby Harbour, today (Saturday, November 7) at 11am.

The pub had been lying vacant since the previous managers shut up shop on July 1, 2023.

Built around 130 years ago, the traditional pub features an open fire and cobbled outdoor area, overlooking the harbour, with magnificent views to Whitby Abbey.




The pub previously served food from first-floor dining rooms and includes owner’s accommodation on the second floor.

![The pub closed its doors on July 1, 2023 but will reopen today under new management](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21594975.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The pub closed its doors on July 1, 2023 but will reopen today under new management *(Image: Google Maps)*

A representative for Sam Smiths Brewery welcomed the opening and said: “These are not just pubs. They are inns with stories to tell, places where good beer, good food and good hospitality have been part of life for generations.”

The re-opening of The Jolly Sailors follows the reopening of The Railway Inn in Spofforth, between Harrogate and Wetherby, and The Trafalgar Bay in Nunnery Lane, York, with The York Arms in High Petergate, York, set to open in January.

It comes after the brewery stepped up its campaign to see its empty pubs – some of which had remained vacant for years – reopened under new management.

It is understood that from today, The Jolly Sailors will open from 11am till 11pm Monday to Saturday, and 12pm till 10.30pm on Sunday.
