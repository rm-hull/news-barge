YORK City Ladies have found their newest fan after Maisie Adam was spotted wearing their shirt at her latest show.

Maisie, 32, recently performed her live stand-up tour show “Whatsherface” at York’s Grand Opera House.

Born in Pannal, North Yorkshire, she was keen to show her support for the area by wearing a York City Ladies shirt during the performance.

It was a “no brainer” for Maisie, who even has her eye on a York City Christmas Jumper.

“Rachel Rudman from the club reached out via our podcast Big Kick Energy asking if I'd like a shirt, and told me how York City Ladies were getting on,” Maisie told The Press.

“I'm always keen to rep not only the north, but women's football, so it was a no brainer.

“The kit’s gorgeous, but the York City Christmas Jumper is unbeatable – big thanks to Rachel and the club.

“Good luck to YCLFC for the rest of the season.”

Maisie grew up in Harrogate, and the comedian has been active since 2016 with shows all over the UK.

She has appeared on television shows such as 8 Out Of 10 Cats, Would I Lie To You and Taskmaster.

Her performances have seen Maisie win the Amused Moose National New Comic in 2018 and the So You Think You’re Funny award in 2017, and also appeared in three Soccer Aids.
