A Bradford restaurant has been given a one-star food hygiene rating.

Ariya African Restaurant & Bar, 118a Sunbridge Road, received the low score on September 30.

The city centre food establishment prides itself on "Bringing Lagos to Bradford".

Urgent improvement was deemed necessary for food safety management.

Edward Ogunkoya told the T&A that he has since addressed all the issues outlined by the council and submitted an application for a revisit.

Reviews on Google are mixed with some saying: ""Very good and enjoyable environment experience spice with afro music and foods."

Others were less complimentary and said: "While waiting for the food, the cold almost finished us. There was no heating on. We had to call the attention of one of the chefs, who turned on a heater, but it wasn’t effective."

Another said: "I have tried the food before and I loved it! The customer service however was terrible!"

A more positive review read: "I recently ordered for isiewu from this restaurant and my goodness! The portion was large, the meat was succulent with the perfect amount of spice. I really enjoyed it."

The T&A has requested the full report from Bradford Council.
