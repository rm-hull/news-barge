A woman has been taken to hospital after being attacked outside a Middlesbrough takeaway.

Police and paramedics were called to Palladium Shops in Grove Hill just after 3.30am on Monday (November 9) after a woman was assaulted.

The 51-year-old was left with a head wound after the incident outside a property on Eastbourne Road.

Cleveland Police said her injuries are not life threatening.

Officers taped off a section of the shops in front of Peking Box and A1 Cutz.

A police van remained stationed beside the cordon this morning.

Pictures show a wheelchair lying on the ground beside the red shutters of the takeaway, while the door to a nearby property remained open.
