A six-bed MO has been approved despite objections from police and residents.

Hartlepool Borough Council’s planning department approved the change of use for 35 Mulgrave Road, Hartlepool, on November 5.

Hershenson Estates Ltd plan to turn the home into six-bed home with en-suite rooms, a shared kitchen and dining area.

During the consultation, two members of the public objected, raising concerns about noise, the loss of a family home, and the growing concentration of HMOs in the area.

Cleveland Police also raised concerns, but council planners said the objection was based on "existing crime, disorder and vulnerability issues within the wider Victoria ward area rather than evidence demonstrating that the proposed six-person HMO would itself materially worsen those conditions."

**Read More:**

A supporting report from the council concluded there would not be a "significant adverse impact" on the character of the area, neighbouring properties, highway safety, parking, or crime levels.

Planners also emphasised that the HMO would be limited to six residents.

The scheme was ultimately deemed acceptable, and the application was approved despite the concerns raised during the consultation process.
