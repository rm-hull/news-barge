A village pub's new owner has spoken of his delight after crowds packed through the doors on reopening day.

The Farmers Arms in Scorton, near Catterick, North Yorkshire welcomed customers back on Wednesday almost ten months after the pub closed.

New owner Shaun Corrigan, 35, from Gateshead, said turnout had far exceeded expectations, with locals eager to see the pub reopen.

The team had thanked residents for their support and said they were looking forward to welcoming people in for a quick pint.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670568.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside The Farmers Arms in Scorton as the village pub reopens its doors to customers under new management. *(Image: Shaun Corrigan)*

Shaun said: "It was absolutely crazy. There were so many more people than we thought there would be.

"It was amazing to see everyone laughing and catching up. A lot of them hadn't seen each other since the pub closed as there aren't many others, and so to witness reunions was awesome."

He added that even the pub's previous owners attended the reopening to see customers back through the doors.

The 35-year-old said: "The pub has been closed since January. I'm unsure why it closed, but the previous owners were at the opening and seemed really happy it was back up and running."

The reopening marked the end of a lengthy process after Mr Corrigan took on the business.

Despite having been keen to get the doors open as quickly as possible, he admitted he underestimated just how much work was involved.

He said: "I've been wanting to get it open for four months now, but this is my first brewery, and I didn't realise just how much goes into setting them up.

"The next step is to get menus sorted. The kitchen should open a week on Monday (November 16), and all I'll say is it's proper pub classics.

"Next year we are planning a complete refurb to freshen up the pub, but it's important to us that we don't lose that happy, community feel.

"We're absolutely thrilled to open the doors of The Farmers Arms again and meet everyone in the village and become part of a great community.
