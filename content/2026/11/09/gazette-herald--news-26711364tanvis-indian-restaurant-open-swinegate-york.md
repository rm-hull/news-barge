A POPULAR family-run Indian restaurant is opening its second location in the heart of York city centre.

Tanvi’s Indian Cuisine will open its doors in 26 Swinegate in York next Thursday, (November 19), bringing its blend of North and South Indian food to a new audience.

The restaurant, which first opened its doors in Wakefield, is named after the owners’ daughters, Tanya and Anvi, and is known for its emphasis on family, hospitality and authentic flavours.

A spokesperson for Tanvi’s said: "Independent, family-run restaurant Tanvi’s Indian Cuisine will open a new location at 26 Swinegate on Thursday 19 November, bringing authentic North and South Indian food and familiar British Indian favourites to York city centre.

"Its York premises, formerly The Indian Lounge, will reopen under Tanvi’s ownership, with a dedicated management team and highly experienced chefs trained in India."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21689012.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A thali tray *(Image: Provided)*

The menu, which hopes to bring together traditional cooking and British favourites, will feature regional curries, tandoori dishes, biryanis, and Indo-Chinese options.

Among the most popular dishes in Wakefield are Tanvi’s Mixed Sizzler, Dragon Chicken, Hyderabad Lamb Curry, however there are also wraps, thali trays, indulgent desserts, and milkshakes.

Customers also enjoy the Ian and Amanda Special, which is named after two of the Wakefield restaurant's original customers who were the first to try a complex curry that was created for them by the chef.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21689014.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the starters *(Image: Provided)*

The spokesperson added: "It's one of our most intricate curries, made with 21 ingredients, each roasted individually before being ground together to create a beautifully rich, deep-brown sauce."

The dish has since become a firm favourite, particularly among our British customers, and remains one of our most requested curries!

The original Wakefield restaurant has earned a five-star food hygiene rating and a 4.9-star rating on Google from more than 1,000 reviews.

For more information, visit Tanvi's restaurant at [https://tanvis.co.uk/](https://tanvis.co.uk/)
