Emergency services are on the scene of a serious crash today (Monday).

A section of the A645 at Camblesforth, south of Selby, has been shut.

It’s closed between the A1041 and Brigg Lane

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Motorists are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes. Please allow extra time for your journey.”
