Muse have been announced as the first headline act for the 2027 Roundhay Festival, which will return to Roundhay Park in Leeds next summer.

The multi-million-selling band will headline on Sunday, 27 June 2027, with support from Nothing But Thieves and other artists still to be announced.

The announcement follows the inaugural American Express presents Roundhay Festival, which took place over three days in July 2026. The event was organised by AEG Presents in collaboration with Leeds City Council.

Planning is now well under way for next year’s festival, which will again take place in the surroundings of Roundhay Park.

Roundhay Park has a long history as a concert venue, having previously hosted major performers including Madonna, Bruce Springsteen, Robbie Williams, Genesis, Ed Sheeran, U2 and the Rolling Stones.

Leeds City Council said bringing live music back to the park was an opportunity to showcase the city to new audiences. Leeds has a cultural calendar that includes events such as Light Night, Leeds West Indian Carnival and Leeds International Film Festival.

The inaugural festival also provided a boost for the local economy, using local suppliers and staff while generating spending on accommodation, food and other entertainment by visitors travelling to the city.

The commitment of AEG Presents and the council to making the event sustainable and environmentally friendly was recently recognised with a commended rating through a certification programme run by A Greener Future.

## Leeds City Council welcomes Muse announcement

> **Councillor James Lewis, leader of Leeds City Council, said:**
>
> This festival is a great example of how concerts and other cultural events and celebrations can act as a hugely positive showcase for Leeds.
>
> Bringing the music world’s top names to our city and giving them the opportunity to play in the breathtaking setting of Roundhay Park allows us to connect with new national and international audiences, while at the same time supporting the local economy.
>
> It also gives fans, from Leeds and beyond, the chance to make their own magical memories, just like the crowds who flocked to the park in years gone by for shows by Madonna, U2 and many more.
>
> Today’s headliner announcement means the countdown is well and truly on to another special occasion for Roundhay Park and the city of Leeds.
>
> Local residents and businesses can also be assured that the council, alongside AEG Presents, will be working hard to keep them informed about road closures and the other traffic and travel arrangements that are required for the safe and smooth operation of an event on this scale.

> **Jim King, CEO of AEG Presents UK & European Festivals, said:**
>
> Following the success of our first year at Roundhay Park, we’re delighted to be returning to Leeds with one of the most incredible live bands in the world.
>
> Muse’s legendary outdoor performances and unrivalled production values continue to set the benchmark for live music, and we’re thrilled to welcome them to Roundhay for what promises to be a truly spectacular event.

Muse are no strangers to performing in Leeds, having played at venues including the Town & Country Club and the Duchess of York during their early years.

The band have also headlined Leeds Festival three times, with their most recent headline appearance taking place in 2017.

## Tickets for Muse at Roundhay Festival 2027

Tickets for Muse’s performance at American Express presents Roundhay Festival will go on general sale at 10am on Friday, 13 November 2026.

Further information is available from the Roundhay Festival website.

Artists who appeared at this year’s festival included Pitbull, Lewis Capaldi, Alfie Boe, Tinie Tempah and Conan Gray.

Further details of the multi-day line-up for 2027 are expected to be announced shortly.
