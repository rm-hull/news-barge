There is only one week left to see the 20th edition of Aesthetica Art Prize at York Art Gallery.

The international art exhibition will close on **Sunday 15 November**, and showcases talent from contemporary artists around the globe working across a range of mediums including digital media arts.

The shortlisted artists are competing for the Grand prize of £10,000 – and there’s also an Emerging Artist prize, which several of the artists qualify for, of £1,000.

This year the Prize’s short-listed artists present strong environmental themes, relationships to nature both past and present, as well as health and wellbeing in the modern age. Some works mesh a combination of these themes together portraying the link between people and the natural world through powerful imagery.

Several works look at identity, race and cultural memory in the community, whilst others make a poignant comment on mental health in the modern world.
