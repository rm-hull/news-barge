A SUPERMARKET has revealed it was behind mysterious bear sightings as part of a festive advertising campaign.

Morrisons confirmed the sightings in Leeds were a teaser for its 2026 Christmas advert, featuring a bear character.

The sightings began with giant paw prints in Roundhay Park, followed by doorbell footage of a bear-like figure nearby, and CCTV of a furry character at a bus stop.

All three were part of the campaign introducing the bear ahead of the advert’s launch.

Beverley Lumb, marketing and advertising director at Morrisons, said: "Everyone has their own idea of what makes Christmas 'just right' alongside their own family traditions that make the day feel truly magical.

"As Christmas comes in all shapes and sizes, we pride ourselves on delivering good, honest value, ensuring every piece of festive fare is crafted with the thought, care and quality for everyone gathered around your table."

The new 90-second advert reimagines the Goldilocks and the Three Bears story.
