Reports of a hate incident at York Designer Outlet have sparked a police appeal for information.

The incident happened at about 2pm on Friday, October 30, in the car park of McArthur Glen Designer Outlet, York.

North Yorkshire Police said it involved two people in a Fiat 500 who allegedly shouted abuse towards another person in a separate vehicle.




Officers are now appealing for witnesses who may have seen what happened to come forward.

A spokesperson for the force said: "We're particularly appealing to any witnesses who may have seen what happened to please get in touch.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us."

Anyone with information can email **[james.wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:james.wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk)**, report information via the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting 'Tell us about' and then 'An existing case or report', or use the live chat service available Monday to Friday between 9am and 5pm.

Those unable to use these methods can call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Please quote reference **12260213645** when providing information.
