More than 400 runners, joggers and walkers took part in the latest Lister Park parkrun in Bradford, with James Habergham leading the field home.

A total of 426 finishers completed the 5k event on Saturday, November 7.

James Habergham, competing in the SM25-29 category, was first across the line in an impressive 17 minutes 46 seconds.

Andy Tanner was close behind, taking second place in 17:58, while Martin Fillingham, of Saltaire Striders, finished third in 18:15.

Richard Haydock, of Northowram Pumas Running Club, was fourth in 18:21, with Baildon Runners' Lee Kaznowski fifth in 18:22.

Northowram Pumas also supplied the sixth-placed finisher, Paul Wilson, who recorded 18:32.

There were some impressive performances from younger runners. Alex Croft, of Holmfirth Harriers AC, finished eighth in 19:11 in the JM15-17 category, while Bradford Airedale AC's Lewin Rajan, competing in JM11-14, took 13th place in 19:51.

The first female finisher was Taliska Faulkner, of Queensbury RC. She finished 22nd overall in an excellent 20:37.

Helena Croft, of Holmfirth Harriers AC, was the second woman home in 20:58, while junior Lerryn O'Neill, of Ilkley Harriers AC, produced an eye-catching performance to finish third female in 22:19 while competing in the JW10 category.

The event featured a strong representation from local running clubs including Saltaire Striders, Northowram Pumas, Baildon Runners, Eccleshill Road Runners, Ilkley Harriers, Bingley Harriers and AC, Keighley & Craven AC and Queensbury RC.

The weekly event takes participants on three laps through one of Bradford's best-known parks, passing Cartwright Hall, the Mughal Water Gardens and boating lake, with a number of climbs and descents along the way.

The results demonstrated the inclusivity of parkrun, with participants ranging across numerous age categories. The oldest categories represented included VM80-84, while several youngsters competed in the JM10 and JW10 categories.

At the other end of the field, the final finisher was Karen Dutson-Bromley, of Trail Snails Bingley, who completed the course in 1:08:16.

The Lister Park parkrun has previously recorded an average attendance of around 415, with 516 people taking part at an event in September.

Organisers have also announced plans for an additional Lister Park parkrun on Christmas Day, although there will be no New Year's Day event
