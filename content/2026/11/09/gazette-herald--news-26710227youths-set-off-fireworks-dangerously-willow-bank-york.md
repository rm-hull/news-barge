FIRE crews have been called to reports of youths setting off fireworks "dangerously" in York.

The incident took place in Willow Bank at 8.18pm on Sunday (November 8).




North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: “A crew from Huntington responded to a report of youths setting off fireworks dangerously, causing them to explode on grass.

“On arrival the youths had dispersed and no fire was located.

“Crews liaised with the Police.”
