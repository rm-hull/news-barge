**'Three cheers for the BBC: Why it's worth very penny'**

LIKE recent Press correspondents Rose Berl (November 4) and Bernadette Oxberry (November 5), I appreciate ‘Auntie Beeb’.

My own favourite channels are BBC Radio 3, where the presenters know what they’re talking about, and BBC Radio 4.

My TV preferences are the three main BBC channels; 1, 2 and 4.

The BBC does attract ‘flak’ and criticism, occasionally with justification but the corporation must be without parallel.

It’s regularly accused of bias, one way or another or neither way or in between!

Some fine (and brave) journalism, many subjects and most genres covered, entertainment…some of it good, appealing to a wide range of preferences and interests - what’s not to appreciate?

Compare and contrast the equivalent in other nations, having first overcome the language barrier! Incomparable for news coverage, generally trustworthy, to its detractors I’d politely say ‘think again‘, even allowing for repeats and excessive sport!

With regard to the licence fee, cheap at the price, equivalent to a four-course meal for four in a posh restaurant, I’m told.

Two and a half cheers for the BBC and as I’m struggling to master the half cheer, I’ll settle for three!

*Derek Reed.*
