A supermarket has been targeted in a suspected arson attack.

The incident occurred at Morrisons on Worth Way, Keighley, just after 4pm on Saturday, November 8.

Three fire crews were called to the blaze, which West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said was already out when they arrived.

It is believed to have been started deliberately.

Images posted to social media show police and fire vehicles parked outside the shop.

A spokesman for the service said: "We were called at 16.08 on 7th November to Morrisons in Keighley to reports of a fire.

"Three crews attended but on arrival the fire was extinguished.

"This has been recorded as a suspected deliberate ignition."

The T&A has reached out to Morrisons for comment.
