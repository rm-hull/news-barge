As part of the National Vaccination Action Week from November 9 to 15, not only family doctors’ offices but also medical practices in other specialties and Berlin’s public health departments are participating by offering vaccination counseling and checking vaccination status, according to the Senate Department for Science, Health and Care. This is the first time the awareness week is being held nationwide.
