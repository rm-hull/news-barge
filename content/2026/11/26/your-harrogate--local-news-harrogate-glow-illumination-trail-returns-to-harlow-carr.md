Glow returns to RHS Garden Harlow Carr in Harrogate tonight (Thursday) for its tenth anniversary.

For the first time, visitors will be able to follow the Winter Walk after dark as part of the trail.

Elsewhere in the garden, they’ll find giant illuminated dandelions, columns of light and a neon tree beside the Queen Mother’s Lake.

The Bubble Man will also be back each evening, blowing giant bubbles that catch the light as they float through the garden.

Glow runs on selected evenings until Wednesday 30th December.

*Entry is by timed ticket and visitors must book in advance through the RHS**website**.*

*Off-peak adult tickets start at £15.25, or £9.50 for RHS members.*
