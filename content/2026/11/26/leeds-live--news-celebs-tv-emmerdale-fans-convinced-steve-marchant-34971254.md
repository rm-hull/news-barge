# Emmerdale fans convinced Steve Marchant will return after unexpected twist

## Emmerdale fans are convinced Steve Marchant, played by Paul Opacic, will return to the ITV soap after Kim Tate and Graham Foster left his fate unclear in a dramatic Christmas episode

Steve Marchant's comeback to Emmerdale appears to have been fleeting. The dodgy businessman, portrayed by Paul Opacic, originally graced the ITV soap between 1996 and 1999, notably as Kim's (Claire King) husband during that period.

His surprise reappearance in the Yorkshire village occurred earlier this week after Kim had convinced her long-lost daughter, DS Thea Ramsden, that Steve was her biological dad. However, Steve subsequently revealed to Kim that this couldn't be true, as he is infertile.

It has now been established that Graham Foster, who returned from the dead earlier this year, is actually her father.

During the most recent episode of the ITV soap, Thea had organised to meet Steve, who has allowed her to think he is her father, at The Woolpack, and Kim was incensed to see the two of them strolling through Main Street together. He had been discussing his intentions to remain over the Christmas period, reports the Daily Star.
