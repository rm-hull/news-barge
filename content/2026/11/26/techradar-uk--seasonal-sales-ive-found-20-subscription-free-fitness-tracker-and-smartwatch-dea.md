There's a problem no-one's talking about when it comes to the best Black Friday fitness deals, and it's about subscriptions. A lot of fitness trackers have subscriptions now, to be paid on a monthly or annual basis.

Some of these, like Google Fitbit or Garmin, provide optional features while you can use the core features for free. Others, such as Whoop and Oura, require an ongoing subscription before you can use the tracker at all. Refuse to renew or pay, and your tracker becomes an inert lump of plastic, metal and sensors.

• Browse all today's best Black Friday deals in the US

This is important, because on Black Friday, you need to know what you're buying. You might think you're getting a fantastic deal on a Whoop band (and there are good deals around) only to realise it needs a 12-month subscription to be renewed annually. Or you might buy one of the best Black Friday Fitbit deals expecting to use the new Google Health Coach, only to find it locked behind a paywall. If you know that going in, and you're going to get a lot of value out of the subscription, great! But if you're looking for a subscription-free tracker, it can be a real sting in the tail.

On this page you'll find Black Friday deals on a range of smartwatches, fitness trackers and health wearables that don't require a subscription to be used. Some of them have no optional features at all and everything's included in the base price, while others have such a good core suite of features that the optional features are truly that — optional — rather than a poorly-implemented paywall that makes you feel like you've only bought half a tracker.

I've included both UK and US deals below. Check out the offers to find the right wearable for you — and pick it up safe in the knowledge you aren't going to get sidelined by subscription fees.
