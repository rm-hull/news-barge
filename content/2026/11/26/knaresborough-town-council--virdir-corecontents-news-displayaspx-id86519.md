# See anti-social behaviour? Please report it.

*Published: 26 November 2026*

Everyone deserves to feel safe in our community. If you witness anti-social behaviour, please don't assume someone else has reported it. By reporting incidents, you can help the relevant authorities identify issues, target resources, and take appropriate action.

Whether it's vandalism, nuisance behaviour, littering, fly-tipping, or other activities affecting our town, your report can make a real difference.

Together, we can help keep Knaresborough a welcoming and enjoyable place for everyone. Thank you for playing your part.
