**North Yorkshire Council proposes relocating services from Ryedale House to Stanley Harrison House, aiming to create a modern, accessible customer hub in Malton and achieve significant cost savings.**

North Yorkshire Council has revealed proposals to relocate services currently housed at Ryedale House to Stanley Harrison House in Malton. The initiative aims to establish a new customer hub in a more central and accessible location within the town, enhancing user experience while generating considerable savings.

Ryedale House, which has accommodated various council functions including the customer hub, registrars service, wedding ceremonies, committee meetings, and workspace for back-office staff, is described as ageing and underused. The building reportedly receives about 13 face-to-face customer enquiries daily, with desk occupancy sometimes falling to 35%, highlighting underutilisation.

Council officials note the building’s deteriorating condition and the associated high running costs, estimated at around £290,000 per year alongside additional maintenance expenses. By shifting services to Stanley Harrison House, the council expects to save approximately £250,000 annually and reduce long-term maintenance liabilities by millions of pounds.

Stanley Harrison House already hosts Citizens Advice, and bringing council services alongside may foster greater collaboration with partner organisations, including the Department of Work and Pensions and North Yorkshire Police. The customer hub’s relocation would maintain local access to key services such as birth, death, and marriage registrations.

The proposals are part of a broader strategy to optimise council-owned properties and support regeneration efforts, including the potential redevelopment of the Ryedale House site. Following the acquisition of Resolution House earlier in the year as part of estate modernisation plans in Scarborough and Malton, the council is progressing its efforts to reshape service delivery and improve workplace environments.

> **North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for property and finance, Cllr Peter Wilkinson, emphasised the goal of sustaining a strong town centre presence while creating more efficient facilities:**
>
> This is about creating better facilities for residents and staff while ensuring we continue to have a strong presence in the heart of Malton.
>
> Ryedale House has served the area well for many years, but the building is now in poor condition. It is becoming increasingly expensive to run, is significantly underused and would require substantial investment in the future.
>
> We have looked at a range of options and believe this represents the best way to deliver services in Malton for the future. By relocating front-line services to Stanley Harrison House, we have an opportunity to create a modern hub in a more central location that will make it easier for residents.

> It will also make better use of taxpayers’ money by reducing running costs and supporting the regeneration of the Ryedale House site. This is not about reducing our presence in Malton – it is about creating a sustainable base that keeps services in the heart of the town and supports our ambition for a joined-up, one front door approach to public services.

The proposals are at an early stage and scheduled for discussion at the Thirsk and Malton area committee meeting on 4 December, with further consideration by the council’s executive planned for early next year. Ongoing engagement with staff and partners continues as part of the plans to maintain coordinated delivery of public services in Malton.

The full report for the upcoming committee meeting is accessible online via the North Yorkshire Council website.
