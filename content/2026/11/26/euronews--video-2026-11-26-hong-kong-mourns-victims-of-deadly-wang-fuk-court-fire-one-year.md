Bereaved relatives and former residents gathered near the Wang Fuk Court apartment complex in Hong Kong, China, on Thursday, 26 November 2026, to remember the 168 people killed in a devastating fire a year earlier.

Mourners laid flowers, prayed and embraced one another beneath the charred residential towers. Others observed a minute's silence at around 2:50 pm, marking the time the blaze broke out and engulfed seven high-rise buildings.

The Hong Kong government did not organise an official memorial ceremony. An investigation report released in October identified faults in maintenance work and shortcomings in regulatory oversight.

Legal proceedings against companies and individuals involved in the maintenance project are ongoing. The complex remains deserted.
