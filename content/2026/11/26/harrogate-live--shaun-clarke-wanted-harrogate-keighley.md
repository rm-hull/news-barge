North Yorkshire Police want to find Shaun Clarke, 34, wanted for theft and fraud by false representation. He could be in Harrogate or Keighley. Quote 12260190702.

North Yorkshire Police are asking for help to find a wanted man who they believe could be in Harrogate or Keighley.

The force says Shaun Clarke, 34, is wanted for theft and fraud by false representation. It published the appeal on 23 November.

## Overview

* **Who:** Shaun Clarke, aged 34, wanted for theft and fraud by false representation.
* **Where he may be:** Harrogate or Keighley, the force believes. Keighley is about 17 miles south west of Harrogate.
* **Reference to quote:** 12260190702.

## What the force says

The force says “extensive enquiries” have been carried out to find him, and it is now asking the public for help.

Being wanted for an offence is not a finding of guilt.

## What it means for you

If you see him or know where he is, North Yorkshire Police ask you to quote reference 12260190702 and:

* call 101
* or call the independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111, or use its online form

If you see him right now, call 999.
