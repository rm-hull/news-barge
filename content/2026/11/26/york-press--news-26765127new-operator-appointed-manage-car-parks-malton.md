A new parking operator has been appointed to manage car parks across Malton.

National Parking Control Group Ltd will now oversee car park management on behalf of the Fitzwilliam Malton Estate and Visit Malton CIC.

The estate has confirmed that it will continue to offer two hours of free parking at the Market Place, Livestock Market (Shambles), car parks and now at Navigation Wharf off Yorkersgate.

A spokesperson for Fitzwilliam Malton Estate said: "Two hours free parking at these locations is provided and underwritten by the Fitzwilliam (Malton) Estate, working with Visit Malton, to support local business in Malton and to facilitate ease of access to businesses and services in town for visitors and customers."

A permit scheme will also be reintroduced at peripheral sites, giving businesses and employees access to managed parking spaces.

A spokesperson said: "An email inviting previous customers to re-apply for a permit has been issued with fees frozen at previous rates."

Management by National Parking Control will begin on December 8, 2026.

New signage will be installed across Malton in the coming weeks.

The Market Place car park is owned by the Fitzwilliam Malton Estate, which wholly funded and delivered the re-surfacing and re-lining of the Market Place to improve the town centre and visitor experience.
