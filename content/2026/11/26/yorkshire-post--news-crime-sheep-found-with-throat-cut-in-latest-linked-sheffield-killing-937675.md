# Sheep found with throat cut in latest ‘linked’ Sheffield killing

## A sheep has been found with its throat cut in the latest incident involving the death of one of the animals in Sheffield.

South Yorkshire Police said a farmer in Mosborough went to check on his livestock on November 18 and found that one had been stolen, while another had been killed.

The incident follows a nearby farmer reporting having 17 sheep stolen.

Officers believe the incidents are linked.

Earlier this month, several sheep were also killed after disappearing from a field off the A57 Manchester Road in Hollow Meadows, Sheffield.

The animals were taken from the field then killed and cut up using ‘professional methods’ with offal, skin and limbs left behind, police said.

Describing the most recent incident, a South Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “On 18 November, it is reported that a farmer checked his sheep in a field on Plumbley Hall Road and one had been stolen and another killed, by having its throat cut.

In a nearby field, another farmer reported having 17 sheep stolen and officers suspect the incidents are linked.

We are urging anyone with information or who may have witnessed anything suspicious in the area, have dashcam footage of parked vehicles to get in touch.”
