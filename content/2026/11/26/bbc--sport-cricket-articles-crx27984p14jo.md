Rishabh Pant scored 128 in the first Test at Wellington

**India wicketkeeper Rishabh Pant made a surprise appearance for a club team in Christchurch under the false name 'Stan Stan'.**

Pant, 29, played for the Open House Swingers against The Chosen Ones and smashed 54 from 21 balls in a 16-run win.

His appearance came a day before India's second Test against New Zealand, which begins at the Hagley Oval at 22:00 GMT on Thursday.

Open House player Mohammad Safi told the New Zealand Herald that he had seen Pant on an electric bike in the city and approached him, saying that his team were a player short.

Safi also said that Pant was not immediately recognised by many on either side, and he turned up in a purple tracksuit and had to borrow kit.

New Zealand captain Tom Latham said Pant's appearance was "brilliant" and showed his "real character", while India bowling coach and former South Africa quick Morne Morkel said he "nearly fell off my chair" when he saw the footage that circulated on social media.

The first Test in Wellington ended in a high-scoring draw, where Pant scored 128 in India's only innings of 720-9.

Pant is regarded as one of the world's most entertaining cricketers, and has scored 3,853 runs in his 53 Tests at an average just shy of 45.

Of those to have scored more than 2,000 Test runs for India, only Virender Sehwag and Kapil Dev have a better strike-rate than Pant's 74.43.
