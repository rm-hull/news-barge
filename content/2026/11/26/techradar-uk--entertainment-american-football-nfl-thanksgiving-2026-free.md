The NFL schedulers have nailed the brief for Thanksgiving 2026, with a three-act festival of football that should perfectly complement the rhythms of your day. We're starting big, with an all-NFC North shootout between the Bears and Lions. Then it's over to the Eagles and Cowboys to ensure you get your post-meal nap in before tempers start fraying, just in time for the grudge match between the Chiefs and Bills.

A Thanksgiving Day showdown between the Lions and Bears is a fitting way to determine the cream of the best division in the NFL. Both teams are tied on eight wins and two defeats, but Detroit has the edge, having won each of its two previous divisional games. Chicago, meanwhile, has lost to both the Vikings and the Packers.

The NFC East, by contrast, is one of the *worst*divisions in the league, yet the Eagles and Cowboys are both playing catchup. While Philadelphia isn't* mathematically*out of the running for a playoff spot, it's purely a matter of time, while Dallas could soon join Nick Sirianni's men on the scrap heap. Lose this, and the NFL gives the Cowboys an* 8%* chance of making the cut.

Once that's out of the way, it's time for a fixture that's taken on a life of its own. Patrick Mahomes and Josh Allen are two of the NFL's biggest stars, and while Allen has had the Chiefs' number in regular-season meetings, Mahomes is the Bills' kryptonite in the playoffs. He's shattered their Super Bowl dream four times over the past six years and, five years on, "13 Seconds" continues to haunt Buffalo.
