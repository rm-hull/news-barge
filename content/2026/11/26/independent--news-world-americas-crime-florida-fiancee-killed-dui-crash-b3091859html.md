A bride-to-be has been killed in a crash caused by a suspect who was allegedly drunk driving and had been released from jail just days earlier in relation to a similar offense.

Leanna Blaize Albertson-Czerwinski, a 27-year-old from Tarpon Springs, Florida, was killed after 30-year-old Diego Arreola rear-ended his Nissan Altima into her Tesla on the Courtney Campbell Causeway late last Friday night, according to police.

The two cars drifted across the center median into traffic, where Albertson-Czerwinski’s Tesla was hit by a man in his mid-20s driving a Dodge Ram. Arreola’s Nissan was also struck by a man in his mid-20s driving a Nissan Pathfinder.

Arreola and the Dodge driver both suffered from non-life-threatening injuries, and the other Nissan driver was unharmed — the same could not be said for Albertson-Czerwinski.

Now, the fiancée’s family is speaking out.

![Leanna Blaize Albertson-Czerwinski, a 27-year-old from Tarpon Springs, Florida, was killed after 30-year-old Diego Arreola rear-ended his Nissan Altima into her Tesla on the Courtney Campbell Causeway late last Friday night, according to police](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/26/1/45/Screenshot-2026-11-25-at-8-04-59-PM.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"It's just the flaw in the justice system. And there's many of them, but this is one that I want to point out — is the DUIs," Albertson-Czerwinski’s fiancé, Tanner McElwee, told Fox 13 News. "You know what you're doing when you get behind the wheel of a car, and you can't walk, let alone drive; you know what the risks are. That's murder."

Tampa Police said in a press release that Arreola had slurred speech and a strong smell of alcohol. Investigators also allegedly found an open beer container inside his Nissan.

Arreola had allegedly admitted to first responders that he had been drinking, and his blood alcohol concentration was 0.286, more than three times over the legal limit, WFLA reported, citing court documents.

Court records also showed that Arreola had a history of drinking behind the wheel.

On June 11, Arreola was convicted of driving under the influence and refusing to submit to testing, according to a motion for pretrial detention filed Sunday. He was sentenced to 12 months of probation and his license was also suspended for 12 months.

After Arreola allegedly violated the terms of his probation, a warrant was filed on October 20, WFLA reported. He was held in the Pinellas County Jail until November 16 and then granted supervisory release.

![Arreola was convicted of driving under the influence in June and then allegedly violated the terms of his probation, sending him to jail, according to a local report. He was released on November 16](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/26/1/21/Screenshot-2026-11-25-at-8-08-24-PM.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Albertson-Czerwinski was set to tie the knot with McElwee in April on Honeymoon Island, a state park on Florida’s coast, after a life of overcoming her struggles.

When she was a teenager, she was diagnosed with a chronic pain condition and was reliant on a wheelchair for years. But she learned how to walk again and became a thrill-seeker, riding dirt bikes and skydiving.

"She was completely wheelchair bound, overcame that, the lowest point of her life. I've seen her overcome everything," her sister, Kelsie Ellingsworth, told Fox 13 News.

A GoFundMe page created by Ellingsworth asking for donations to help with funeral arrangements and other costs said her sister “had finally made it to the life she dreamed of.

“She deserved so much more time to live it.”

Arreola has been charged with DUI Manslaughter, DUI with Property Damage or Personal Injury, and Violation of Driver License Restrictions in connection with last Friday’s crash.
