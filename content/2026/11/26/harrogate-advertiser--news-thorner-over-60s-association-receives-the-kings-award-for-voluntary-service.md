Thorner Over 60’s Association have been awarded The King’s Award for Voluntary Service for 2026.

The group is one of only 250 charities, social enterprises and voluntary groups to receive the prestigious award, equivalent to an MBE, this year.

Bev Cussans, Chair of Thorner Over 60’s Association said: “This is a huge accolade for our volunteers, building on the hard work of previous members.

“I wish to thank all our volunteers including the committee for their dedication and incredible input, and to our members for all their loyal support.

“Our work together has enabled us to be recognised for this award.

“We are both proud and delighted to receive this honour.

“Thorner is an extremely friendly and community-minded village and this award is a real boost for us all.”

Based at The Bungalow, in the centre of Thorner, near Wetherby, the Association offers a varied programme primarily for older people of the area.

Founded in 1962, the registered charity acts as a social bug and offers exercise and language classes, a fortnightly lunch-club with home-cooked meals.

A spokesperson for the King’s Award said: “On Thursday mornings, to coincide with the visiting Post Office, there is a busy and friendly coffee morning and everyone is welcome whatever their age.

“There is also a book exchange and a discussion group.

“In addition, they have a regular programme of events, talks and outings and occasional workshops for IT issues, which includes individual help with mobile-phones and tablets as well as laptops.”

The Bungalow acts as a base for other village groups to meet and opens its doors to the whole village on special occasions, such has May Day Bank Holiday.

The Over 60’s, which has 30 volunteers and over 80 members, works with other organisations in the village and is a part of Thorner Net Zero Partnership which aims to improve sustainability.

The Award spokesperson added: “In the past few years, the Association has improved its energy efficiently markedly at The Bungalow, with better insulation, new windows and doors, and solar panels on the roof and plans to install a heat pump soon.

“Their work, along with others from across the UK, reminds us of all the ways fantastic volunteers are contributing to their local communities and working to make life better for those around them.”

The King’s Award for Voluntary Service aims to recognise outstanding work by local volunteer groups to support their communities.

It was created in 2002 to celebrate Her Late Majesty The Queen’s Golden Jubilee.

Representatives of Thorner Over 60’s Association will receive the award crystal and certificate from Professor Adeeba Malik CBE HM Lord-Lieutenant of West Yorkshire in the coming months.

In addition, two volunteers from Thorner Over 60’s Association will attend a Royal Garden Party at Buckingham Palace in May or June 2027, along with other recipients of this year’s award.
