Wetherby has been included in a series of improvements aimed at making West Yorkshire’s bus stations and stops safer and more accessible, which are set to be introduced over the next year.

Improvements to the region’s Weaver Network, including an additional changing places toilet, new signage, CCTV and defibrillators will take place across 15 bus stations in the region, while more than 100 individual bus stops will see accessibility improvements.

The changes, involving more than £2.7 million of investment, were approved by the Combined Authority, and are expected to be delivered by the end of next year.

One changing places toilet will be installed at Leeds City Bus Station, allowing access to facilities for those with extra physical needs.

Tactile signs for blind and partially sighted passengers will be installed in a further five bus stations from 2027, while 25 new and replacement CCTV cameras will be installed.

Around 104 individual bus stops will see improvements, such as improved footways, altered kerbs and tactile paving for passengers with disabilities.

The changes were voted through by the West Yorkshire Combined Authority’s Investment Board today (Thursday 26 November).

Mayor of West Yorkshire Tracy Brabin said: “From our biggest bus station to our smallest bus stop, our region is getting ready for public control of our bus network – and this work ensures as many passengers as possible can feel the benefits of our Weaver Network.

“These schemes go hand-in-hand with the work we have done to purchase five depots and 193 new electric buses for phase one of our network, which we will roll out next spring.

“Making our bus stations safer and easier to use, as well as improving access to our bus stops will help us to create a better-connected West Yorkshire that works for all.”

Under bus franchising, West Yorkshire Combined Authority will own the majority of buses and depots for the larger contracts, while setting routes, fares and service standards for all franchised services.

Operators will be contracted to run services on its behalf.
