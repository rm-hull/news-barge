Authorities scrambled to a bomb threat at the Royal Navy’s headquarters in Portsmouth on Thursday.

The threat was made against HMS Excellent in a telephone call.

However, searches by police and Ministry of Defence personnel identified no threat at the base.

An investigation into the incident is ongoing.

As well as being the Royal Navy HQ, the base is also home to the navy’s Defence Diving School and the Phoenix Damage Repair Instructional Unit.

A Royal Navy spokesperson told *The Independent*: “The security incident at HMS Excellent has now concluded following a search Police and Ministry of Defence Personnel.

“The base remains fully operational and investigations into the incident are continuing.”

Earlier, the Royal Navy had asked peopel to stay away from the area.

Sky News reported that a cordon was put in place at the site, which is located on Whale Island in the city.

It comes after thousands of bomb hoaxs against schools and airports earlier this month.
