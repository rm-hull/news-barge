## York city centre shop - which stood empty for a year - is under offer

Low Petergate *(Image: Newsquest)*

**A YORK city centre shop in a prime location is under offer – more than a year after its previous tenants have left.**

Moda in Pelle, a women’s shoe brand founded in Leeds, closed its doors last November after forfeiture notices appeared in the window of their Low Petergate shop.

According to estate agents Garness Jones, the property - which rents for £42,000 per year - is currently under offer.

The shoe brand closed their shop last autumn with notices, dated November 13, 2025, stating that bailiffs acting on behalf of the landlord have entered the premises – adding that “peaceable re-possession of the property has been secured.”

Moda in Pelle was given seven days to organise collection of the items inside the property, however shoes appeared in the window of the shop until around early December.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20234223.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Forfeiture notices were posted to the door *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Since then, the brand has reopened a concession partnership inside Browns in Davygate

It also has four other local stores inside Browns department stores in Northallerton and Helmsley, within Barkers in Beverley, and a stand-alone shop in Harrogate.

![Moda in Pelle is set to open soon in Browns in Davygate](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20497419.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Moda in Pelle has now opened inside Browns in Davygate *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

Nicholas Brown, owner of Browns, said: "We're supporting the redevelopment of Davygate along with Breitling, Tag Heuer, Mappin & Webb and Betty’s café.

"We are entering a difficult year for the high street, but we're starting off in a positive direction - you have got to invest to keep up with modern consumer demands and this concession partnership is the largest one in Britain."
