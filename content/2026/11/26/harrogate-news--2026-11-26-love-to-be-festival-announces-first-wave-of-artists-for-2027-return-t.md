**The renowned house music event will take place on 4 September 2027, with early line-up including Hannah Wants, Sandy Rivera and K-Klass, alongside a new collaboration with Café Mambo Ibiza.**

The popular Love to be… Festival is gearing up for its fourth consecutive year at The Stray in Harrogate, with organisers revealing the first phase of their 2027 line-up. Taking place on Saturday 4 September, the event promises to maintain its reputation as a highlight in the UK house music calendar.

Leading the early artist announcement are Birmingham-born DJ and producer Hannah Wants, known globally for her energetic sets that have taken her to major festivals such as Creamfields and Tomorrowland. She will be joined by the internationally acclaimed producer Sandy Rivera, a key figure behind influential house releases on labels like Defected and Subliminal.

The line-up also features legendary groups and performers including K-Klass, Allister Whitehead, and festival favourites Trimtone, Stuart Pilling, and Aimee. Additionally, Juanito Chanclas, Sinclair and Scott Harris are set to perform, supported by a new collaboration with Café Mambo Ibiza on the Music Factory Stage—a nod to Love to be…’s origins in Sheffield.

This year, for the first time, the festival will introduce The Future stage, dedicated to showcasing emerging talent in the house music scene. Further headline artists, guest performers, and a VIP stage takeover by Funky Love are expected to be announced in the months ahead.

Set within the iconic parkland of The Stray, the festival transforms the space into a vibrant festival ground complete with entertainers, fairground attractions, street food vendors, premium bars and chill-out areas. State-of-the-art sound and production aim to enhance the immersive experience for attendees throughout the day, which runs from midday to 11pm.

Tickets for the event are now on sale via Skiddle, priced from £51.50 plus booking fees. The festival has continued to sell out in previous years, attracting house music fans from across Yorkshire and beyond.

Fully embracing its thirty-plus years at the forefront of the UK’s dance music culture, Love to be… was co-founded in Sheffield in 1994. It has a longstanding history of hosting influential house music pioneers and continues to unite generations of clubbers with a focus on quality and community.

Further details on the full line-up and festival features will be released early next year. For tickets and updates, visit the official festival website or the Skiddle event page.

For those interested, Love to be… Festival 2027 takes place Saturday 4 September 2027, from 12pm to 11pm at The Stray, Harrogate, HG2 8JR. Tickets are available at [https://www.skiddle.com/festivals/love-to-be-festival/](https://www.skiddle.com/festivals/love-to-be-festival/).
