British interiors brand Neptune will open a showroom on Princes Street in Harrogate next year.

The two-storey showroom at 7–11 Princes Street will be home to Neptune’s first Kitchen Lab in the UK - and it's due to open on Thursday 11th February 2027.

Customers will be able to explore the layout of their kitchen before it is built and work with designers to choose cabinets, colours and finishes.

The 3,520 sq ft showroom will also sell gifts and home accessories, including lighting, tableware and textiles.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6acf2822f16764cb018.jpg)

It will feature living room, dining room and bedroom displays as part of Neptune’s whole-home design service.

The Harrogate opening follows the launch of a Neptune concession at Fenwick in Newcastle in October.

Michael Hope, Neptune’s regional head of retail, said:

> “Following on from our Fenwick concession that opened earlier this year in October, Neptune Harrogate is the next step in our Northern expansion journey, bringing to life our promise to invest in this key region.
>
> "Home to our new Kitchen Lab concept, the showroom will give customers a chance to experience Neptune in a fresh way.”

Neptune also plans to hold events at the showroom with local businesses throughout the year.
