Japanese and international visitors gathered in Kyoto, Japan, on Thursday, 26 November 2026, as the city's autumn foliage reached its peak.

Maple leaves in shades of red, orange and gold framed rivers, gardens and historic buildings across the former imperial capital.

At the Yusai-tei Gallery in Arashiyama, reflections of colourful trees created striking displays, while tourists photographed the seasonal scenery in Maruyama Park and near Togetsukyo Bridge.

Known as 'koyo' in Japan, the autumn foliage offers a different experience from the famous spring cherry blossoms.
