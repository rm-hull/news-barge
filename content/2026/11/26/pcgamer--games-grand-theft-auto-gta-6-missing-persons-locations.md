The **GTA 6 Missing Persons**case is available as early as Chapter 1, but you'll need access to both Lucia and Jason to wrap up the side quest in Chapter 2. Your stint as a fledgling detective begins in an abandoned warehouse near Mo's shop in Gellhorn, though there are two ways to flag the objective on your map and start the hunt for Gellhorn's serial killer.** If you begin in Chapter 1**when you can only play as Jason, head straight to the abandoned warehouse to begin. This is how I began the quest the first time, after following a pack of coyotes through a small opening on the side of the abandoned warehouse. This route gives you an extra text conversation where Jason sends Lucia creepy photos from the warehouse.** Begin in Chapter 2** by heading to the Starlet Motel lobby in Gellhorn and inspecting the missing persons posters. Afterward, head to the abandoned warehouse as a couple or go alone, and squeeze through the crawl space.

## Enter the abandoned warehouse trophy room

After crawling inside the warehouse, walk to the far end and turn right to enter the glowing red room. Go a little farther to find the, uh, "trophies," and inspect all three lockers, along with the missing persons posters on the wall. This is where you begin your hunt for Gellhorn's three missing tourists.

![GTA 6 - Map of Port Gellhorn highlighting](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/2HuHkQ53pJM9stLXxY7mdn-1200-80.jpg)

* The images are saved as "Creepy Photos" in your phone gallery
* Refer to the pictures to find three locations around Gellhorn
* Your phone reminds you to look around when you're close to one of the spots
* Call the Kelly County Tip Line after every discovery

I've got all three locations highlighted below, but if you want to solve it yourself, now is the time to turn back.

### All three missing persons locations

![GTA 6 - Map of Port Gellhorn highlighting](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/nFY5U9kqny7QqhZqKoMjmn-1200-80.jpg)

* **Patrick Murray is in an abandoned trailer park** just north of a water tower. It's the northernmost location in the Missing Persons case, and it's impossible to miss if you head west from Hank's Waffles toward the beach.
* **Susanne Keeble is in the junkyard**, across the street from a drive-in theatre and beside the Pay 'n' Spray off of Gellhorn Ave.
* **Donald Miller is in a Borrachero Sound inlet**, southeast of the hangar at Wild Hog Point. Look for a submerged car when you're in the area.

## How to find Patrick Murray

The first missing person in northwest Gellhorn is in an abandoned trailer—the water tower's shadow is your giveaway. Run along the fence and enter the blue building from the back. There's an awfully suspicious hole in the floor, so pull the remaining floorboards back to discover what's left of Patrick Murray.

Call the Kelly County Tip Line after you find Patrick's remains and leave a message. No one will answer the phone, for now.

## How to find Susanne Keeble

The second missing person is in the junkyard, inside the trunk of an old pink DeVanity. The junkyard is right beside Pay 'n' Spray off Gellhorn Ave, across the street from the drive-in theatre. You'll spot Susanne Keeble's pink car with the PRTYGRL license plate while facing the drive-in.

After finding a second body, call the tip line again to finally speak to an investigator. They're thrilled to hear there's renewed interest in the case, and ask that you keep in contact should you make any further discoveries.

## How to find Donald Miller

The third missing person is in a U-shaped inlet off the Borrachero Sound. You can find the smaller body of water by traveling southeast from the Wild Hog Point hangar. The view of Wild Hog Point from the tree in the creepy photo is your giveaway, so start looking for a submerged car when you find the right perspective.

After finding a third body, the investigator from the tip line asks to meet in the abandoned warehouse. And no, it's not just you, it's a pretty shady proposition.

## Find the Port Gellhorn Serial Killer

![GTA 6 - Missing Persons serial killer](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qswgtd7pL3JHhaWGBTxV6e-1200-80.jpg)

When you're ready for the meeting, consider whether you want to go with your partner or go alone. Both seem to give me the same boost to Jason and Lucia's relationship status, elevating it to Hot Together (4/4) status for the first time in Chapter 2. It had dropped all the way down to Partners in Crime (2/4) just before this, so the Missing Persons quest was a much-appreciated bump.

You can also get the serial killer's glasses from his body if you choose to continue as a couple. They're beyond creepy, so I wouldn't blame you if you left them there, but aside from that, there's no cash reward. You will have to do it, though, if you're going for a 100% GTA 6 run.

### Return to the abandoned warehouse trophy room alone

As either Jason or Lucia, return to the abandoned warehouse trophy room and meet Alvan West—the man you spoke to on the Kelly County Tips Line. He's got a 'surprise' waiting for you in the fourth locker, and a fight ensues after checking it out.

Going alone means there's no one there to save the passed out Jason or Lucia, and the serial killer takes them to a cave in Lummox County. The attack automatically triggers a character swap, leaving you to investigate your partner's whereabouts after receiving suspicious texts.

* Head to the waypoint marking the mine in Lummox County
* Enter the mine and follow the lights to free your partner
* Alvan will attack the couple with an axe a few times
* Chase him to the dead end and shoot him to stop his next lethal attack

### Return to the abandoned warehouse trophy room together

Enter the abandoned warehouse trophy room just like before while your partner waits outside. When they sense something is off, they'll run inside to save you just as you pass out. After Alvan is dead, inspect his body—you can take his glasses as a souvenir. That's freak behavior, but I'm not judging. We gotta get that 100% cosmetic collection somehow.
