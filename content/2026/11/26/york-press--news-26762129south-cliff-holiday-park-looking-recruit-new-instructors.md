Young people are being encouraged to apply for a seasonal job that promises adventure and professional development.

South Cliff Holiday Park in Bridlington is recruiting casual high ropes instructors for the summer 2027 season at The Edge: Aerial Adventure, a high and low ropes course run by East Riding of Yorkshire Council.

No prior experience is needed, as full training will be provided.

Councillor Nick Coultish, cabinet member for culture, leisure and tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "The Edge: Aerial Adventure is one of our most exciting attractions that we have in the East Riding.

"We hope to see lots of applications from young people looking to spend the summer working in a fun, outdoor environment while gaining recognised qualifications and practical experience."

Instructors will work towards a European Ropes Course Association (ERCA) High Ropes Instructor certification and an Emergency First Aid qualification.

Leah, an instructor at The Edge, said: "I didn’t have any experience when I started, so I was a bit nervous at first, but you get loads of training, and everyone’s really supportive.

"It’s such a good summer job because you’re outside, working with a great group of people and having a laugh while you work."

For more information, visit: www.edgeaerialadventure.co.uk
