A MAIN road in a North Yorkshire town is closed today (Thursday).

Langley Drive in Norton is shut both ways from Langton Road to the B1248 Beverley Road.

The road is closed due to work by Yorkshire Water.

The company is carrying out work on the sewer.

It follows the flooding in Langley Drive last January which saw residents experiencethe 'worst' flooding issues in over 50 years.

Areas of Langley Drive and Hambleton Road saw water coming up through the drains following heavy rain due to Storm Chandra.

Meanwhile, temporary traffic lights are in place due to water main work on the B1248 Scarborough Road.
