A new free support programme for people with long-term health conditions is launching in York.

Nuffield Hospital's Long Term Conditions programme, introduced by rehabilitation specialist Claire Hanchard, aims to provide additional support to those struggling with their health, well-being, or daily functioning.

The charity-run programme, tailored for individuals with cardiovascular, respiratory, and musculoskeletal conditions, among others, will also support individuals with Long COVID symptoms such as long-term fatigue, pain, or breathlessness.

The initiative is especially beneficial for those who face barriers accessing other services due to low confidence, social factors, or limited resources.

Participants will receive one-to-one support from a trained rehabilitation specialist, alongside personalised guidance to manage symptoms and boost confidence.

The programme also offers safe, supervised physical activities, and education on lifestyle, pacing, energy management, and long-term self-care.

Ms Hanchard said: "Our goal is to help people feel more in control of and better able to manage their everyday life."

The programme operates within community settings where there is no Nuffield gym facility, ensuring accessibility for those most in need.

The exact location for York is yet to be confirmed, however, Ms Hanchard plans to provide further details once the venue is finalised.
