## Chart-topping rock duo announce Scarborough Open Air Theatre show

Royal Blood will play Scarborough Open Air Theatre on July 15, 2027. *(Image: Pixabay)*

Another major name has joined Scarborough’s blockbuster summer line-up, with a chart-topping rock duo heading for the Yorkshire coast.

Royal Blood will take to the stage at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on July 15, 2027, as part of a UK tour supporting their new album, *Dead Company*.

Julian Murray, of Cuffe and Taylor, said: "We’ve been itching to announce this incredible show for some time.

"Bringing three of the most explosive rock acts in the UK right now together on the same night is going to be something else.

"Royal Blood remain on top of their game, their new album is among their best work ever.

"We know fans will absolutely love the fact we are bringing them here to the Yorkshire coast, alongside the brilliant SOFT PLAY and Bad Nerves."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21766218.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Royal Blood will play Scarborough Open Air Theatre on July 15, 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

Royal Blood, made up of Mike Kerr and Ben Thatcher, will be joined by special guests SOFT PLAY and Bad Nerves for the show.

The band have toured internationally and built a dedicated fanbase, with more than 2.8 billion streams across all platforms.

Their latest album, Dead Company, follows 2023’s Back to the Water Below, which became their fourth UK Number One.

The new record has been praised for returning to the band’s heavier, early sound while showcasing their growth and experience.

SOFT PLAY, formerly known as SLAVES, have made a name for themselves in the UK punk scene with their high-energy performances and irreverent style.

They have previously toured with major acts including Foo Fighters, The Prodigy, and Idles.

Bad Nerves, known for their fast-paced, distorted pop-punk sound, have earned praise from rock icons like Green Day’s Billie Joe Armstrong and Pearl Jam’s Stone Gossard.

The Scarborough date is part of a wider UK tour that includes stops in Leeds, Manchester, Brighton, Cardiff, London, and Ludlow.

Other artists set to perform at Scarborough Open Air Theatre in 2027 include Biffy Clyro, Two Door Cinema Club, Mariah Carey, Courteeners, Shania Twain, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

The venue will also host a four-show run of the hit musical Six.

Tickets for Royal Blood’s Scarborough show go on general sale at 10am on Friday, December 4 via scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.

More information about the full line-up and ticketing can be found at scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.
