York has been named one of the most festive cities in the country.

Ranked eighth in England and Scotland and the most festive in Yorkshire and the Humber, York scored 61.3 out of 100 in the LNER Festive Town Index, which compared 46 towns and cities across England and Scotland.

The index considered how people in each region celebrate Christmas (based on a survey of 2,000 adults), Christmas lights switch-on dates, festive event listings, seasonal travel searches, and the number of cosy pubs with real fires.

David Flesher, commercial director at LNER, said: "Christmas means something a little different in every community across the LNER route.

"Whether it’s what belongs on the plate or the traditions we cherish, every region, town or city has its own customs and festive favourites.

"This year, we wanted to celebrate with a guide to the places where this special season really comes alive.

"Travelling by train means leaving the winter motorway traffic jams behind, relaxing from the moment you step onboard, and arriving right in the centre of the best festive towns and cities."

York ranked first in Yorkshire and the Humber for four out of five festive measures and was the sixth most searched-for Christmas destination in England and Scotland.
