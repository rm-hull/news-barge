Polling analyst Nate Silver has predicted that President Donald Trump “absolutely” could be impeached over the sale of Nvidia’s AI chips to China.

As the scandal of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s alleged affair with a Chinese spy unfolded last week, the House Foreign Affairs Committee released more eyebrow-raising information. The Trump administration approved shipments of nearly 900,000 AI chips made by Nvidia and AMD to alleged Chinese military-backed companies.

After Democrats crushed Republicans in the midterms earlier this month, they have vowed to hold the Trump administration accountable. But will they try to kick Trump out of office over his ties to Nvidia? Silver said there’s definitely a chance.

“Do you know what the prediction markets say for what's the chance that Trump will be impeached and removed from office before his term ends?” Silver asked his co-hosts on the *Still Counting* podcast.

“Are they saying it’s like 25 percent? I have not looked,” Galen Druke guessed.

![’If [Trump’s] doing s*** to like profit off selling advanced chips to China, abso-f***ing-lutely he could be removed — and he should be,’ the polling analyst said](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/21/15/GettyImages-2305643148.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Silver said Druke was “uncannily accurate,” adding that he thinks it’s “entirely reasonable” that there’s a 25 percent chance the House adopts articles of impeachment against Trump and the Senate votes to convict him.

But their co-host, Clare Malone, disagreed that Trump — who was impeached by the House and acquitted by the Senate twice during his first term — would be removed from office in his second term, saying the betting markets are “addled.”

“If he is aiding and abetting, he could absolutely get removed by the Senate,” Silver continued. “If his approval keeps going down at the rate it is, he’s going to be at whatever 29 percent by the end of next year. And if he’s doing s\*\*\* to like profit off selling advanced chips to China, abso-f\*\*\* ing-lutely he could be removed — and he should be.”

Huang admitted to being unfaithful to his wife on November 8 after *The New York Times* reported that he had formed a close relationship with Alice Huang, a Chinese executive at a Singapore-based data center whom U.S. intelligence suspected of feeding information to Beijing.

When the Nvidia scandal broke, Trump said he was “amazed” by the news “because I view [Huang] as a very high quality person. I've known him a long time.”

![Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO on November 12, causing Nvidia’s stock to plummet. The company’s board announced Monday that it unanimously agreed to reinstate him, which Trump admitted he had a hand in](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/21/14/GettyImages-2301328054.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Huang stepped down as CEO on Nov. 12, causing Nvidia’s stock to plummet. After shareholders and Huang’s allies in Silicon Valley, including Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, called for the Nvidia founder to be brought back at the helm, the company’s board announced Monday that it unanimously agreed to reinstate him.

It was reported that Trump had a hand in Huang’s return, with three people familiar with the matter telling CNBC that Huang had not broken national security laws.

“I convinced them,” Trump admitted to CNBC. “We believe he’s fine. He’s absolutely fine.”

Trump made up to $15 million on Nvidia shares Monday as the company’s stock price spiked over the news of Huang’s return, the *Financial Times* reported.

Trump, who raked in about $2.2 billion last year, has denied profiting off the presidency, telling *60 Minutes* correspondent Ross Douthat earlier this month, “I devote almost no time to my net worth.”
