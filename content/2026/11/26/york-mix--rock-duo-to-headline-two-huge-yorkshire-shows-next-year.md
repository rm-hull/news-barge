Royal Blood have announced a series of large outdoor shows across the UK for summer 2027 – including two dates in Yorkshire.

The rock duo will headline Leeds Millennium Square on **9 July 2027** and Scarborough Open Air Theatre on**15 July 2027**.

The tour is in support of their critically acclaimed new studio album *Dead Company*, which was released earlier this month via Warner Records.

Joining them are special guests, the electrifying UK punk duo SOFT PLAY (formerly SLAVES) and Essex-formed garage-punk quintet Bad Nerves.

The Brighton based duo – Mike Kerr and Ben Thatcher – teased fans throughout the summer with various intimate live shows, including their talk-of-the-town surprise appearance at Reading Festival in August.

*Dead Company*marks the follow-up to 2023’s* Back to the Water Below* – their fourth full-length studio record – which debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart, their fourth consecutive number one album.

As with *Back to the Water Below*,* Dead Company* was self-produced in Brighton.

Royal Blood have established themselves as one of the most ‘important’ rock bands of their generation. Having toured the world multiple times, they have played sold-out headline shows and major festivals to audiences across the globe, and their music has amassed more than 2.8 billion streams across all platforms to date.
