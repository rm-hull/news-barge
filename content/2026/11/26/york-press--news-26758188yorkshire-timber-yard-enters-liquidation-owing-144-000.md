A North Yorkshire timber yard has gone into administration with debts of £144,000.

MHK Timber and Veneers was registered at 7 Princes Square, Harrogate, and was incorporated in July 2012.

It entered a creditors liquidation on November 10, with Leeds-based Opus Restructuring LLP appointed liquidator.

Companies House records its sole director as Michael Howard Kramer.

A liquidator’s report, known as a statement of affairs into the finances of the business, said it had no estimated realisable assets.

There were no preferential creditors—those paid first.

But HMRC was owed £100,000 as a secondary preference creditor—someone paid next.

In addition, there were unsecured creditors—those paid last—who were owed £43,900 between them.

This comprised three trade and expense creditors owed £23,900 between them and a bank/financial institution owed £20,000.

This gave an estimated total deficit of £143,900.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £100, this gave a total shortfall or deficit of £144,000, the report added.
