Laura Loomer, a far-right activist and close ally of President Donald Trump, lashed out at New York City's mayor in a racist rant after he postponed the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade because of a major storm.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced Wednesday that the city's 100th parade would be postponed for the first time ever because of severe weather forecast for Thanksgiving Day. Winds of up to 60 mph are expected in parts of the city as well as moderate to heavy rain.

“New York, we’ve got a storm headed our way this Thursday,” the democratic socialist mayor wrote in an X post. “So we’re making a change to keep everyone safe: the 100th annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will now take place on Saturday, November 28.”

Loomer, who has described herself as a “proud Islamophobe” and “pro-white nationalist,” weighed in on X. “Muslim mayor destroys 100 years of tradition,” she wrote.

Her post drew a flurry of replies, most of them critical.

![Far-right activist Laura Loomer lashed out at New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani after he announced that the Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade would be postponed due to severe weather](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/16/34/GettyImages-2306341958.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“This is a safety concern. He doesn't control the weather, dumb\*\*\*,” wrote one user, while another chimed in: “It's 50 MPH wind gusts and torrential rain, you psycho.”

“Don't be ridiculous,” wrote a third user. “He's the mayor but the decision wasn't made just by him. The NYPD recommended postponing because 60 mph winds and massive balloons don't really go together…”

Others said Loomer's rhetoric is the kind that helped Democrats win big in the midterm elections, when they reclaimed control of both the House and the Senate.

“You don’t seem to understand that voters don’t want influencers like you to have a base with [the] government which is why the midterms were so bad for Republicans,” one X user wrote. “Happy thanksgiving.”

Loomer has maintained direct access to Trump in his second term, meeting with him in the Oval Office and at Mar-a-Lago and traveling with him aboard Air Force One. She’s taken aim at Mamdani before, urging Vice President JD Vance not to shake hands with him at a 9/11 memorial in September.

She announced a hiatus from her podcast after the Republican Party’s crushing midterm losses sent her into a posting frenzy in which she declared the American dream “dead” and predicted that a “nuclear holocaust” would ravage the United States.

![Loomer has maintained direct access to Trump in his second term, meeting with him in the Oval Office and at Mar-a-Lago and traveling with him aboard Air Force One](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/20/download.-6..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is a New York tradition that draws millions of spectators along its route through Manhattan and millions more across the nation on television. It features giant character balloons, elaborate floats and marching bands.

Macy's first staged the parade in 1924, and it has been held nearly every year since. The only suspension came during World War II due to wartime shortages of rubber and helium.

In 1971, high winds and heavy rain forced organizers to ground all of the parade's large balloons.

This year’s postponement disappointed some New Yorkers and out-of-town visitors who had planned their holiday around the parade. Others said they understood the decision, given the forecast.
