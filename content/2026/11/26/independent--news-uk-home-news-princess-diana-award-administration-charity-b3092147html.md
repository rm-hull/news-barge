Prince William has expressed his sadness after a charity set up in tribute to his mother Diana, Princess of Wales, has been placed into administration.

The Diana Award, which marked its 27th anniversary this year, was founded in memory of Diana, who was killed in a Paris car crash in 1997.

The charity was launched by the former UK prime minister Gordon Brown in 1999 and aimed to honour people who worked to improve the lives of others.

The Diana Award said on its website it was announcing the news with “heavy hearts”, but independent advice had made clear it could no longer continue to meet its obligations.

The Prince of Wales is a joint patron of the charity with his brother the Duke of Sussex, but the pair have attended separate Diana Awards events in recent years amid their ongoing rift.

![Prince Harry and William both made a number of appearances for The Diana Award](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/12/2234152895..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Prince William has expressed his sadness at the news that the Diana Award has gone into administration, as he thanked “everyone whose dedication and hard work helped” encourage thousands of young people “over so many years”.

William said in a statement: “The Diana Award has recognised and encouraged thousands of young people who are changing the world for the better.

“I want to thank everyone whose dedication and hard work helped make that possible over so many years. W”

It is understood that he is said to be very sorry about the development but believes the legacy of Diana, Princess of Wales continues in many forms.

The charity was inspired by her belief that young people have the power to change the world, and its awards recognised young people for their social action and humanitarian work.

![Prince William has expressed his sadness at the news that the Diana Award has gone into administration, as he thanked ‘everyone whose dedication and hard work helped’ encourage thousands of young people ‘over so many years’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2024/03/15/08/2024-03-14T195945Z_1913007822_RC2TL6AJV6EE_RTRMADP_3_BRITAIN-ROYALS-WILLIAM.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In a statement, the charity wrote: “Twenty-seven years ago, the Diana Awards were created to honour Diana, Princess of Wales and her belief that young people had the power to change their community, their country and the world.

“We are proud to have shone a spotlight on a generation of young people. Over 50,000 young people have received the prestigious Diana Award and the charity has worked with thousands more through programmes to power the impact created by these inspirational young leaders on the lives of those around them.

“It is with heavy hearts that we announce The Diana Award charity has been placed into Administration, after independent advice made clear that the charity can no longer continue to meet its obligations.

“This is not the outcome any of us wanted. We thank everyone who has supported this charity over the years and, above all, the young people whose achievements have always been at the heart of it.

“Their achievements matter, and nothing about today's decision changes that.”
