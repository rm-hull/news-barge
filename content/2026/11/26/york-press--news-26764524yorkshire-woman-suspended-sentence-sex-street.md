*(Image: NQ)*A woman had sex twice in broad daylight in a popular Yorkshire town before flashing teenage boys in a McDonald’s**.**

Vanessa Wrigglesworth, 38, of Scarborough, was handed a 16-week suspended sentence after admitting to outraging public decency, exposure causing alarm and distress, and sexual touching of a minor.

The Press was told by the Crown Prosecution Service that she was also handed a probation requirement and a six-month alcohol treatment requirement at York Magistrates' Court on Wednesday, November 25.

The offences took place in Scarborough on September 14.

Deputy district judge Walid Khalil, speaking at York Magistrates’ Court, said: "On two separate occasions in a public place you had sexual intercourse.

"That was witnessed by a number of individuals.

"It is fair to say that behaviour – you will be the first to recognise – is unacceptable, it is appalling. The offences were committed while you were clearly intoxicated."

The court heard Wrigglesworth was first seen having sex with a man in the town centre at around 6.45pm.

Kathryn Walters, prosecuting, said the pair were later seen at a disused garage before Wrigglesworth entered a McDonald’s, where she exposed her breasts to a group of teenage boys and kissed a 14-year-old.

Lee-Anne Robins-Hicks, defending, said Wrigglesworth was "remorseful" and "acknowledges her feelings of shame".

She told the court: "She was drinking and had consumed alcohol but has no memory of acting that way, although she takes full responsibility for having done so.

"She was using alcohol as a crutch at this time, and was experiencing alcoholic black-outs as a result."

Ms Robins-Hicks explained that Wrigglesworth had turned to alcohol following the breakdown of her marriage and the loss of her job.

She later became homeless.

The court heard that Wrigglesworth had been sober for seven weeks and was "genuinely optimistic" for the future.

In addition to the suspended sentence, she was ordered to complete alcohol treatment and pay £272 in fines and costs.
