Unveiled on **26 November 2026**, the** tenth edition**of** The Gingerbread City**brings together** 50 architectural creations**, from intricately crafted replicas of famous landmarks to futuristic skyscrapers. Among the highlights is a miniature** Royal Albert Hall**, painstakingly recreated by architect** Zak Wharton** using computer-aided design to capture the building's distinctive symmetry. Gingerbread bridges cross rivers of blue sugar, marshmallow monsters roam snowy landscapes and Father Christmas stretches between towering buildings.

Organised by the **Museum of Architecture**, this year's exhibition takes**"Future Forward"** as its theme, challenging architects to imagine how buildings might adapt to**climate change** and improve people's wellbeing. A futuristic skyscraper named**SpireHive** explores innovations such as**living algae** and**self-healing concrete**. The exhibition also features a partnership with homelessness charity** St Mungo's**, for which former** Great British Bake Off**contestant** Carol Pui Man Li**has created** five structures**inspired by the experiences of people who have faced homelessness. Celebrating its** tenth anniversary**,** The Gingerbread City**opens to the public at** Space House**in** Holborn**on** 28 November**and runs until** 3 January 2027**.
