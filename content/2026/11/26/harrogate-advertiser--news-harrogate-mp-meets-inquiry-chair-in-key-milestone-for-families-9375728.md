Harrogate’s MP says a “key milestone” has been reached after meeting the chair of a long-awaited public inquiry into mental health care failings at Tees, Esk and Wear Valleys NHS Foundation Trust.

Tom Gordon, Liberal Democrat MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough, said the discussions with Judge Potter about the inquiry's terms of reference was a major step on the road to getting answers for the families and patients who have been badly let down.

“For too long, people across Harrogate and Knaresborough have told me about failings in the mental health care they or their loved ones received,” said Mr Gordon.

“They have shown real courage in coming forward, and they deserve answers.

“We had a constructive discussion about the terms of reference.

“I was clear they need to be broad enough to capture the full range of experiences my constituents have shared with me, so that no family is left out.”

It was after his election in July 2024, that the Harrogate MP began pushing for a statutory inquiry into TEWV, which provides mental health services to people in Harrogate and Knaresborough, after hearing growing numbers of distressing accounts from people in his constituency failed by the trust, including families who had tragically lost loved ones while under its care.

He used his first question at Prime Minister's Questions to add his voice to the calls for a statutory inquiry, and has since chased multiple ministers to get a Chair appointed and the inquiry up and running

After the meeting, Mr Gordon said he was reassured by Judge Potter and, in particular, his openness to hearing from people who aren't "core participants" to the inquiry.

Core participant status gives families and former patients an enhanced role in the process, allowing them to view evidence not available to the general public.

Harrogate MP To Gordon said: "I used my very first question at PMQs to back the call for this inquiry.

“Since then I've chased minister after minister to get a chair appointed and the inquiry moving.

“Now the focus shifts to making sure it works for the people it is meant to serve.

"I was clear in my meeting with Judge Potter that the inquiry needs to be broad enough to capture the full range of experiences my constituents have shared with me, so that no family is left out.”

The statutory nature of the public inquiry into mental health care failings at Tees, Esk and Wear Valleys NHS Foundation Trust gives it full legal powers, requiring evidence to be given under oath and the Trust to provide legal documentation.

The inquiry was launched in December 2025 and formally began on September 21, 2026.

Tom Gordon MP is asking anyone who has in any way been affected by care at TEWV to make their voice heard.
