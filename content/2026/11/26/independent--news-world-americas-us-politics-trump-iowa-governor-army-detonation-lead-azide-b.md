President Trump has issued an ominous warning to the incoming Iowa governor following the detonation of an Army stockpile in the Hawkeye State.

​The near-5-ton stockpile of lead azide was destroyed Wednesday despite concerns from environmentalists about the potential impact of the blast. Trump described the operation as “PERFECT” in a series of posts uploaded to Truth Social.

​“Rob Sands, (sic) the new future Governor of Iowa, is obviously unhappy that the Obama created (2009!) mess was so perfectly handled by the U.S. Army through a flawless detonation, that he’s now complaining about the lead up to the event,” Trump wrote. “He hurt and delayed the process. Was absolutely no help. Just another politician.

​“He should rejoice at this great success, not nitpick and issue phony complaints on ‘transparency.’ I will remember!”

​Sand, who swept to victory as part of a wider “blue tsunami” in the midterm elections, has repeatedly criticized the handling of the planned explosion in Iowa. In a post on X, he described the lead-up to the blast as “chaotic and unclear.”

![The detonation of the near-5-ton stockpile was carried out on Wednesday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/15/2306835116..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

​Trump’s reference to 2009 seemingly refers to a decision by former President Obama’s administration to consolidate the nation’s lead azide stockpile at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown.

ATF Director Robert Cekada told reporters that some of the lead azide moved to the facility was already over 60 years old.

​An environmental assessment conducted in 2004 and viewed by KCRG states that the existing stockpile in Middletown had been degrading for 40 years and that the Department of Defense already had concerns about the build-up.

​Trump continued hitting out at his critics on Truth Social, even taking aim at news outlets that reported on the detonation.

![Journalists prepare to document events while the Army detonates several tons of highly-volatile, degraded lead azide on the grounds of the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/16/2306840926..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

​“Fake News CNN was hoping for a bad outcome in Iowa,” Trump claimed. “Their pre-coverage was inaccurate and deplorable.

​“When a PERFECT detonation was completed and there for all to see, they went CRAZY with anger,” he added. “They want our Country to lose. ‘Another win for Trump!’”

​In another post, Trump wrote: “Great job by the Army, and others, in Iowa!!!”

​The explosion was carried out at 9 a.m., with a visible smoke plume appearing at the site.

​Major General Eric Shirley from the U.S. Army Sustainment Command described the operation to reporters on Wednesday as “exceeding expectations.”

​“I believe absolutely that we reduced as close to zero as is absolutely humanly possible the risk to the health and the safety of the people of Middletown and the extended communities throughout Iowa,” he said.

![Iowa Governor-elect Rob Sand (pictured) has repeatedly criticized the handling of the detonation](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/19/download.-1..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

​The plan to detonate the stockpile proved controversial, though, on both sides of the political aisle, drawing critics from environmental campaigners to former MAGA loyalist Marjorie Taylor Greene.

​Greene described the planned detonation as a “dirty bomb” and warned of a potential “Chernobyl event,” while campaigners filed lawsuits seeking to stop the explosion.

​Middletown is located mere miles from the Mississippi River and is close to the Illinois and Missouri state lines.

Lead azide is classified as being “probably carcinogenic to humans,” by the National Center for Biotechnology Information, leading to concerns that the dust plume from the blast could contaminate the surrounding environment.​
