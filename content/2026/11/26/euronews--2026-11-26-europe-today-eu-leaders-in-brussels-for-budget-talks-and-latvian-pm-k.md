## The EU’s 27 heads of state are meeting in Brussels for a European Council summit, which some are forecasting to be a long-term budget bust-up like no other. We speak with the Latvian prime minister Andris Kulbergs.

## On today’s show

EU leaders arrive in Brussels for what is expected to be a long discussion over the EU's seven year budget. They will have to decide the bloc's priorities for the next seven years, from defence and competitiveness to agriculture and regional funding.

But there is another urgent question hanging over the negotiations: how to keep Ukraine financially afloat in 2027, with Kyiv facing a massive funding shortfall and Russia showing no sign of backing down. We speak with **Latvian prime minister Andris Kulbergs** minutes before he sits with other leaders.**MEP Michał Szczerba** will also give us his take on how to fund Europe's own defence while keeping Ukraine in the fight against Russia.
