A company has applied to add a new operating centre for a goods vehicle in Pocklington.

ON Services (Yorkshire) Limited, based in Hull, is seeking to modify its existing Goods Vehicle Operator's Licence to include Little Grange Farm, Pocklington, as a new operating centre for one goods vehicle.

The company has specified that no trailers will be kept at this site.

The proposed change has been officially notified, and now, people who own or occupy land near the proposed operating centre have the opportunity to raise objections.

They need to write to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, stating their reasons, within 21 days of the notice.

A copy of their objections should also be sent to the applicant.

A guide on how to make representations is available on the government website at gov.uk/object-hgv-operator-licence.
