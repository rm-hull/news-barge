A hair and beauty salon in Harrogate has scooped two national awards after impressing judges at one of the country’s leading hairdressing competitions.

Tracy Roche and Jenny Hullah, from Kingsgrove Hair and Beauty, travelled to London on Sunday, November 15, to attend the Salon Awards 2026 Grand Final at the Leonardo Royal London Tower Bridge.

The salon was shortlisted in three categories at the prestigious awards, which are judged by an independent panel of respected industry experts.

Entrants are required to submit detailed written entries alongside photographic evidence showcasing their work.

The salon enjoyed a successful evening, taking home the award for Best Salon for Extensions, Wigs or Replacement, while salon owner Tracy Roche was also named Best Extension, Wig or Replacement Artist.

The awards recognise excellence, creativity and innovation within the hairdressing industry, with winners announced across regional categories during a ceremony hosted this year by Michael Douglas of MD Hair.

Speaking after the awards, Tracy Roche said the recognition reflected the team's commitment to providing specialist services for clients seeking hair extensions and hair loss solutions.

She said: "Winning these two awards is a wonderful recognition of the passion, dedication and specialist knowledge that goes into what we do every day.

“We couldn't be prouder of our team and what we have achieved over this past year."

Kingsgrove has developed a reputation for its expertise in hair extensions, wigs and hair replacement services, with the team placing a strong emphasis on education and ongoing professional development.

Rather than simply fitting extensions, the salon offers clients personalised consultations, bespoke colour matching, a choice of application techniques and advice designed to protect the health of their natural hair.

The team also works with specialist partners to provide hair loss solutions for clients experiencing thinning hair or medical hair loss.

One of Kingsgrove's most distinctive services is its exclusive Yorkshire partnership offering Gold Class Naturally Curly Hair Extensions.

The specialist service has attracted clients from across the UK, with visitors travelling from Manchester, Liverpool, Newcastle, the South of England and throughout Yorkshire to benefit from the salon's expertise.

The double award success marks another milestone for the Harrogate business, highlighting its growing reputation as one of the country's leading specialists in hair extensions and hair replacement.
