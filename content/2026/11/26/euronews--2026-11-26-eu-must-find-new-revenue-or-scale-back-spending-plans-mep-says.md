EU leaders are meeting in Brussels to discuss the bloc’s next seven-year budget. European Council President António Costa hopes to bring them closer to a deal by the end of the year, but governments are divided over how much to spend and who should pay. Spending priorities include supporting farmers, addressing the high cost of housing and energy, and increased defence spending, particularly for Ukraine.

Portuguese Socialist MEP Carla Tavares, one of the European Parliament’s budget negotiators, told Euronews the size of the budget remains Parliament’s biggest concern. She welcomed progress on new sources of EU revenue, including the possibility of a levy on big tech firms. She said she was open to other ways of raising the money and warned that without new revenue, the EU would have to scale back its plans.
