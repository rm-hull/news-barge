A Ripon charity has begun its Christmas fundraising campaign, with festive trees, handmade wreaths, gifts and visits from Santa among the attractions.

Ripon Walled Garden is transforming its site into a winter wonderland for the festive season, with money raised helping Ripon Community Link support adults and young people with learning disabilities.

The first delivery of Christmas trees arrived this week, with staff, volunteers, members of the charity, local businesses, military veterans and helpers from Ripon Fire Service and Lightwater Quarries joining in to help prepare the site.

Alongside its popular Christmas trees, the garden is selling handmade wreaths, decorations, gifts, food and drinks.

Its café and shop will also be serving festive treats and preparing hampers filled with homemade produce, including apple juice, jams and chutneys made using fruit from its Victorian orchard.

Santa Claus will be visiting on November 28, December 5, 6 and 12. Children will be able to meet him, post their letters in his special post room and enjoy free Christmas activities.

Victoria Ashley, chief executive of Ripon Community Link, said the festive period was an important time for fundraising.

She said: “Last year, our high-quality, premium, yet affordable, Christmas trees were so popular that we had to replenish stock.

“All of the money we raise will go towards supporting the delivery and development of our services for adults with learning disabilities.

“We are fundraising at present to kit out a new classroom so that we can help more people who will benefit from our services.”

The charity will also hold its annual Christmas celebration at Holy Trinity Church on Wednesday, December 16, at 10am.

Members, volunteers, parents, staff and the wider community are invited to attend the morning, which will include carols, readings and a performance by the charity’s own music group.

Ripon Walled Garden is also offering a Christmas tree collection and recycling service on January 8 and 9, with every penny raised going towards its work.
