A nuisance driver has had their vehicle seized following allegations of months of anti-social behaviour in a Yorkshire village, police said.

North Yorkshire Police said the driver of a white-coloured Audi was stopped by officers and reported for driving without due care and attention yesterday morning (Wednesday, November 25) in Hutton-le-Hole and the vehicle was seized.

It comes after months of reports of alleged repeated incidents involving a driver who regularly sounded their horn while passing through the village around 6am, police said.




A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Working together with the local community, officers tracked down the details for the vehicle and attempted to contact the registered keeper by phone and letter.

"Unfortunately for the driver, officers from the Neighbourhood Policing Team asked colleagues from the Roads Policing Team to assist whilst they were on patrol.

"Witnessing the conduct of the driver this morning he was promptly stopped by officers and reported for driving without due care and attention."
