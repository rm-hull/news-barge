# Girl filmed doing star jumps on level crossing near Leeds

## CCTV footage shows a teenage girl jumping on the tracks as two teenage boys linger on the railway

This is the moment a teenage girl risked her life by performing star jumps on a level crossing while a friend paused to snap a photograph.

Disturbing CCTV footage reveals the girl leaping up and down on the crossing as two teenage boys loiter on the railway line. One of the boys even halted to take a picture rather than crossing the tracks safely.

The footage was recorded at Methley North level crossing, near Leeds, West Yorkshire, in September. Network Rail has contacted local schools following concerns that youngsters are endangering their lives by messing about on the railway.

The rail operator has also expressed worries about young people playing 'chicken' with trains, vaulting over barriers and pausing on crossings to pose for photographs. A total of 462 safety incidents were logged at level crossings nationwide in 2025/26 - an increase of more than a fifth compared with eight years previously.

On Network Rail's North & East route alone, 72 incidents involving children and young people were documented - up from 59 in 2017/18. Darren Robertson-Wilson, level crossing manager for Network Rail's North & East route, stated: "Level crossings are safe when people follow the instructions, remain alert and cross without stopping, but the consequences of distraction or deliberate misuse can be devastating.

"The behaviour captured at Methley North shows why this campaign is so important. Stopping on a crossing to take a photograph or treating the railway as somewhere to play puts lives at risk.

"We work with schools and communities to make young people aware of the risks and help them make safer choices. There are no second chances on the railway."

Network Rail highlighted that individuals using mobile phones and wearing headphones while crossing railway lines were becoming an increasing worry. British Transport Police Chief Inspector Joe Gorman stated: "Level crossings are safe when used correctly, but they can become incredibly dangerous in an instant if people ignore warnings, take risks or become distracted.

"We continue to see incidents involving young people at crossings, whether distracted by mobile phone use, peer pressure or deliberate misuse, but what may seem like harmless behaviour can have devastating consequences. That's why campaigns like this are so important, so that young people are educated about the dangers of the railway and they can make safer decisions."
