A York man has been jailed after chasing two men down the street with a knife.

Joshua Tyssen, 37, chased the men down Thoresby Road on 15 December, 2024 in what was believed to be a drug-related incident, York Crown Court heard.

His jailing prompted another stark warning by York’s chief judge Sean Morris about the scourge of knife crime in the city and the wider county.

Prosecutor Kelly Clarke said the incident was witnessed by several residents who “looked out of their windows” to see a man brandishing a knife and chasing two individuals.

Tyssen, a drug addict of no fixed address, was charged with affray and carrying a blade and admitted the offences.

#### Incident location

He appeared for sentence yesterday (Wednesday, November 25) after being remanded in custody.

Ms Clarke said that Tyssen’s record – which comprised 73 offences – included a previous conviction for carrying a knife, as well as assault causing actual bodily harm, assaulting a police officer, shoplifting and drug possession.

Defence barrister Zarreen Cheetham-Alam said that a crack-cocaine addiction had “taken over (Tyssen’s) life”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/york-crown-court-may-2026-ldrs.webp)

She said he had been caught up in an “endless cycle of homelessness and drug addiction”.

Judge Sean Morris said the knife chase was yet another example of someone “running amok in the streets with a knife”.

“It can easily go badly wrong,” he added.

“Knife crime has got to stop. Men and boys who go out in North Yorkshire with knives need to know they are running the risk of an immediate prison sentence.”

Tyssen was jailed for 11 months for the affray and six months consecutive for carrying a knife.

He will be released partway through the total 17-month jail sentence and serve the remainder of his sentence on prison licence.
