**Local hairdresser and new business owner Robyn Mackin has been recognised internationally, winning Bronze in the Blonde Specialist and Hair Extensionist categories at the 2026 Beauty Full Awards.**

Robyn Mackin, a Harrogate-based hairdresser, has marked a significant milestone in her career and business by winning two Bronze awards at the 2026 Beauty Full Awards. The awards, which have a global reach, recognised Robyn in both the Blonde Specialist of the Year and Hair Extensionist of the Year categories.

Robyn has built her career over approximately five years, developing her skills and clientele before making the decision to go independent. She launched her own business, Robyn Mackin Hairdresser, four months ago, renting a chair at Graceful Luxe salon on Princes Square, Harrogate, where she works alongside former colleague Grace Patrick.

Speaking about the move into self-employment, Robyn explained that she was motivated by a desire to challenge herself and gain more control over her career direction. “Having spent years gaining experience and developing my own clientele, I felt ready to take that leap,” she said.

Robyn specialises in balayage, blondes, dimensional colour, and hair extensions, with a particular passion for tape extensions. She enjoys creating personalised looks tailored to her clients’ complexions and lifestyles, favouring low-maintenance, natural results. She notes a growing local trend in Harrogate towards lived-in blondes and natural tones, with clients prioritising healthy, glossy hair with dimension.

The Beauty Full Awards process involved submitting entries for the two categories. As a global competition, Robyn’s work was assessed alongside hair professionals worldwide. She described receiving Bronze in both categories so early into running her own business as “an incredible feeling” and a significant boost to her confidence.

Since the announcement, Robyn has received a warm response from her clients, who have expressed their congratulations and support. She views the awards as recognition of her dedication and passion and motivation to continue expanding her skills and business.

Looking ahead to 2027, Robyn aims to undertake further training in hair extensions and is planning to study for an Assessor qualification at Kleek Academy. She also hopes to transition into education within the industry to mentor and inspire emerging hairdressers.

New clients interested in booking an appointment with Robyn can do so through her Fresha online booking system or via Instagram @robynmackin.hair. The salon is located at 6 Princes Square, Harrogate, North Yorkshire.
