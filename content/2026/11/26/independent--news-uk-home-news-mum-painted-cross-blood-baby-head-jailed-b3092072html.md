A mother who painted a cult symbol in blood on her three-month-old baby’s head hours before he died with cocaine in his system has been jailed for two years.

Joanna Lea had behaved “erratically” throughout the day of her infant son Allister’s death in August 2022, driving on a flat tyre while under the influence of cocaine. Police said she had left the baby in her car alone for a long period, ignoring his cries and failing to feed him.

She later painted an inverted cross on his head in blood and shared the images before calling her mother saying: “Mum, Allister’s dead”.

Emergency services raced to her home in Padiham, Lancashire, in the early hours of 9 August to find her mother giving the three-month-old CPR. He was rushed to hospital, where he was sadly pronounced dead.

A post-mortem examination found the presence of cocaine and pregabalin in Allister’s body, which had been ingested through Lea’s breast milk. Police said Lea was “aware of the risks” of taking drugs while breast-feeding and showed “little remorse” for her actions.

![Joanna Lea was sentenced to two years in prison](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/11/55/joanne-lea-watermarked.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A blood test also showed Lea had taken cocaine on the day before his death and was 24 times over the drug drive limit.

Police said Lea told a “series of lies” throughout their investigation, including claiming Allister's milk could have been tampered with and blaming a friend after a bag of cocaine was found at her house.

The 36-year-old, of Massey Croft, Whitworth, pleaded guilty to child cruelty and was sentenced at Preston Crown Court on Monday to two years in prison. She was not sentenced for causing Allister's death, as the cause could not be ascertained.

Detective constable Keely Powell of Lancashire Police said: “This has been a very distressing case and tragically resulted in Allister losing his life at only three months old.

“Lea was aware of the risks of taking drugs whilst breast-feeding and continued to do so, passing these drugs onto Allister.

![Baby Allister died at just three months old](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/11/33/screenshot_20220826-150038.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"She then went onto co-sleep whilst under the influence of drugs, again knowing the significant risk this causes.

"Lea showed little remorse for her actions. She tried to blame others and consistently lied at every stage of our investigation."We will continue to work hard with our partners to protect children like baby Allister from harm, to educate and support parents, but ultimately to protect and safeguard children, to be their voice and to prevent tragic cases such as this.”
