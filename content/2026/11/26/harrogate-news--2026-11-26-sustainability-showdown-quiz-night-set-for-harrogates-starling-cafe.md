**A lively, gameshow-style quiz event promoting environmental awareness will take place on 9th December, encouraging community engagement through fun and prizes.**

Harrogate residents are invited to join a unique quiz night with an environmental twist at the Starling Cafe on Wednesday 9th December. The event, titled the “Sustainability Showdown,” promises a fun and fast-paced evening styled after popular TV gameshows.

Organisers from Zero Carbon Harrogate, including Climate Action Coordinator Sunita Soundur, have collaborated with Ria Hughes, a Climate Action Officer from Community First Yorkshire’s Village Green project, to deliver the event. Funding support comes from the National Lottery’s Community Fund and York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority’s Vibrant and Sustainable High Streets Fund.

The quiz night will feature quirky rounds and interactive elements such as buzzers and paddle boards, offering an engaging way for participants to learn about sustainability topics. Everyone taking part will receive prizes, with special awards for winners and runners-up.

Sunita Soundur described the gathering as an opportunity for locals to have a laugh and enjoy a different kind of quiz night, stating it will be “fun and interactive” with something for all attendees.

The event aims to bring together the community in an entertaining atmosphere while raising awareness about environmental issues affecting the region. It will take place at the Starling Cafe, a local venue supportive of community events.

Tickets and further event details are available through the official event page online.
