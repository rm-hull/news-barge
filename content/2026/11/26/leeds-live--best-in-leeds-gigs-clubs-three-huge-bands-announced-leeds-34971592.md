# Three huge bands announced for Leeds outdoor summer 2027 show

## Fans will absolutely love the headline booking

Three huge bands will be performing in Leeds city centre as part of a newly-announced show.

We will see Royal Blood headline Millennium Square next summer. The Brighton duo will take to the stage at the Leeds city centre venue in support of their new album Dead Company.

They will perform on Friday, July 9. You can buy tickets from Ticketmaster.

Lots of fans will be desperate to see Royal Blood - who are Leeds Festival favourites. They will be supported, on their return to the city, by Soft Play and Bad Nerves.
