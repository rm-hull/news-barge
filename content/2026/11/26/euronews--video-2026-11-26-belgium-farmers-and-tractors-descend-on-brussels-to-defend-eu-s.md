Rolling into **Brussels’ European quarter** alongside a convoy of tractors, protesters marched past the**European Parliament** with banners demanding that agricultural funding be protected, one machine bearing a stark placard reading**"Agriculture for sale, €0"**. Chanting slogans against looming cuts, demonstrators echoed warnings from farming unions that subsidies could drop by** 20%**under the** EU’s**next long-term budget, tightening the squeeze on already strained rural incomes — a plight underscored by** Lucas Bierlaire**of the** Walloon Farmers Federation**, who warned that many growers were already struggling to make a decent living.

The rally coincided with a **two-day summit** in which**EU leaders** are locked in fractious talks over the bloc’s**2028-2034** budget, divided over the**European Commission’s** proposed**€2 trillion** spending plan that would substantially erode agricultural support in real terms.**Arnaud Rousseau**, head of** France’s**largest farming union, the** FNSEA**, urged Brussels to show greater ambition for European agriculture despite fiscal pressures, ahead of a follow-up summit in** mid-December** aimed at sealing a deal before the year’s end.
