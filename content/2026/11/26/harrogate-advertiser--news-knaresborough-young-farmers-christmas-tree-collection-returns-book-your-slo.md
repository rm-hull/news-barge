Christmas is a few weeks away but Knaresborough Young Farmers are urging people to book their post festive tree collection.

The group, which caters for 10 to 28-year-olds, has been running the service to gather and chip real trees for three years and has raised over £11,000 for charities.

Spokesperson Nicky Villars said: “Knaresborough Young Farmers Christmas Tree Collection is the largest Young Farmers tree collection in the country.

“We go out with pickups and trailers to bring the trees to a central point where they are loaded into a big trailer and taken to a local farm to be chipped and used as animal bedding.

“We have good support from the local farming community to help us achieve this.”

The collection also helps to fund the club’s activities in the region and rest of the country.

“Our meetings cover a wide range of activities and include practice for competitions, visits to local rural businesses, talks and fun activities with the slip and slide being a favourite,” added Nicky.

“It is great to see the members embracing the activities and competitions and gaining confidence as they learn.

“The club takes part in the National programme of competitions with qualifying rounds at district, county and northern area levels.

“This has led to some of our members having successes at a national level.

“The competitions range from stock judging, Tug of War, shooting, flower arranging, public speaking, cookery, sports and novelty ones too.”

The tree collection will run on Saturday, January 9 around Harrogate, Pannal and Starbeck.

Then on Sunday January 10, the group will collect around Farnham, Ferrensby, Follifoot, Goldsborough, Kirk Deighton, Lingerfield, Little Ribston, North Deighton, Scotton and Spofforth.

The cost is £7.50 per tree, with funds going to Mamillan Cancer Support, Yorkshire Air Ambulance and Saint Michael’s Hospice.
