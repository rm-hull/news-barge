The hugely popular North Pole Post Office has opened its doors in Harrogate bringing not just festive messages but also a sprinkling of festive spectacle.

Located at the Harrogate International Festivals office on Cheltenham Parade, the town’s branch of the North Pole Post Office is now open with the all-important post box – where children and adults alike can post their letters for free to Father Christmas - until December 24.

Funded by Harrogate BID and produced by Harrogate International Festivals, with support from AV Matrix, the North Pole Post Office is free for everyone and has become a hugely popular feature of the town’s festive celebrations with thousands of letters received each year.

Jason Maxwell, Chief Executive of Harrogate BID, said: “Christmas is a really special time of year in Harrogate, and this is a great way of bringing families together and encouraging more people to spend some time in the town centre.”

Visitors to the North Pole Post Office can post their letter to Father Christmas and then scan a QR code to download a special letter and message from Father Christmas here

They can also enjoy fairy lights, snow flurries and this year there’s also a sleigh to add even more festive magic.

Sharon Canavar, Chief Executive at Harrogate International Festivals said: “Harrogate’s North Pole Post Office has become a much-loved fixture in the town’s festive calendar.

“We know from speaking to parents that this starts the countdown to the big day for a lot of families, which means a great deal.”

The North Pole Post Office Harrogate Branch, 32 Cheltenham Parade, Harrogate, is open until noon on Thursday, December 24.
