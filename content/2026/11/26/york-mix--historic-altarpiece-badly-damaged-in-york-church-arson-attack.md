A man has been arrested after an arson attack badly damaged an historic altarpiece in a York church.

As ***YorkMix*** reported yesterday, firefighters from York and Acomb went to reports of smoke coming from the Holy Trinity Church on Micklegate at 12.12pm.

The fire inside was already out when they arrived.

#### Holy Trinity Church, Micklegate

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-fire-holy-trinity-church-goodramgate-25-nov-2026-3.webp)

Substantial damage has been caused to a large and listed historical altarpiece within the church.

Fire crews made sure the building was safe and informed police that they believed the fires had been started deliberately.

Police enquiries aided by council CCTV operators led to the arrest of a 42-year-old local man on Blake Street just after 2pm.

He remains in custody for questioning.
