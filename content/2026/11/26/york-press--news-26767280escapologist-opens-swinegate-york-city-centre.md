## New £1million entertainment venue opens in historic building in heart of York

The Escapologist has opened in Swinegate *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A NEW venue offering food, drinks, and interactive games has opened in a historic building in the heart of York city centre.

The Escapologist opened its doors in Swinegate last Friday (November 20).

Located in the former Biltmore bar and Grill in Swinegate, the building has undergone a £1 million transformation with a steampunk inspired bar, alongside a range of different activities aimed at people of all ages.

Downstairs, the venue also offers a full bar with cocktails and food menu with a mixture of ‘lite bites’, loaded flatbreads, burgers, skewers, and sharing boards.

Joe Penney, Marketing Executive, said that he believed the venue would be popular among people finishing work for the day and looking for something different to do.

He said: “We currently have four venues for the Escapologist across the north of England, but this is our first outside a shopping centre and will be our new flagship location for the brand.

“Being here in the heart of York is something that we hope locals will enjoy, but will also appeal to tourists visiting the city for a day trip. We’re hoping that we can build up a lot of repeat guests who can come by to experience all we have to offer.

“We’ve also noticed that there’s a lot of curiosity among people about what’s happened to the Biltmore, so we’d love to welcome them in to see what we’ve done.”

There are several activities in the downstairs area, including interactive lane darts, a photobooth, and a private karaoke room – with bookings available online or in person at the bar.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770075.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The bar has a full cocktail menu *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Joe added: “We’ve found most people tend to book an hour on the darts as it’s just one booking with up to six people per lane, so it doesn't end up being too expensive. We’ve got an automated system, so people can get prompts or use sensors to guide them on how to throw.

“It adds up all the scores, so there’s no need to be a maths wiz. People tend to enjoy it more as they have a few drinks, like in a regular pub, and the location by the bar just means a group can dip in and out.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770078.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A York Minister themed prop as part of the EscaPOOL *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

On the upper floor, visitors can take part in a nine-hole game of escaPOOL, a game which is a cross between crazy golf and pool, or challenge one of the escape rooms.

There are currently three escape room experiences are currently available, with plans for two more – a Valhalla themed puzzle and a horror room – set to be developed going forward, involving a number of different puzzle solving techniques from locks to magnetic elements.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770084.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A look inside Hadrig's Forgotten Forest *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Each room is described as ‘medium difficulty’ – something which allows it to cater to people of ages - with Hadrig's Forgotten Forest, a game which caters to lovers of fantasy and all things wizards, expected to be the most popular among families.

The two other rooms include a space adventure to find a cure for a deadly disease, Mission to Mars, and The Grand Continental Express, which takes visitors on an Agatha Christie style adventure into the depths of Egypt.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770079.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Grand Continental Express room *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Joe said: “Our York rooms are the biggest so far and they’re all built in-house by our head of operations Cam, who has put in so much attention to detail to what’s happening here. We also plan to decorate them for Christmas.

“Our Grand Continental Express room has been a particular favourite here in York so far as York has such a great history of the railways. What we really love about the city is that it’s so beautiful and has such a strong sense of identity.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770082.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The escape pod from the Mars room *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Bookings for the Escapologist are now open on their website.
