US envoy Jared Kushner has been accused of being Vladimir Putin’s spokesman after he lauded the Russian president as a “grand master of politics” in an interview that will spark alarm in Ukraine and Europe.

Mr Kushner, who has spearheaded US efforts to strike a peace deal between Moscow and Kyiv, praised the Russian president for not using a nuclear weapon and claimed Europe had “no plan” to end a war Putin started in February 2022.

Trump’s son-in-law, along with special envoy Steve Witkoff, have drawn criticism in Europe for consistently refusing to condemn Russia’s aggression and appearing to side with Putin on several key issues. Trump last week called Putin “my friend” at a trilateral summit with Xi Jinping.

“Going into the meetings, President Putin is very organised. People can like what he’s done, they can not like what he’s done, but he’s definitely a grand master of politics,” Kushner said during a podcast interview with Mathias Döpfner, the new chief executive of the *Telegraph*’s parent company Axel Springer.

![Kushner met with Putin earlier this month](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/12/13/2304238218..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Analysts accused Kushner of once again repeating Kremlin talking points in the interview and failing to recognise Ukraine’s perspective.

“Kushner speaks as if he’s Putin’s spokesman rather than a mediator or a US envoy,” Maria Popova, director of the Jean Monnet Centre Montreal who focusses on the Russia-Ukraine war, said on X.

“He praises Putin, denies Russia is an aggressor, and speaks of Russia’s conquest and occupation of Ukraine as ‘peace.’”

During the interview with Döpfner, who has previously expressed support for the Trump administration, Kushner also claimed that Europe has “no plan” to end the war in Ukraine. European officials recently attended negotiations for the first time since the conflict began and insisted that the continent must play a role in deciding a final peace deal.

“What we should do is focus on the post-war period, which again, I think makes sense for us because you want to get to a peace that’s going to last,” Kushner said, in comments which echoed Putin’s previous rejection of talks on an immediate ceasefire.

The Trump administration has consistently played softball with Putin, attempting to coax him towards peace with financial incentives while heaping pressure on Kyiv and Europe to make concessions to Russia, despite Moscow’s responsibility for launching the war.

![The US envoy praised him as a ‘grand master of politics’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/21/2293176513..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The envoy added: “We’ve asked the Europeans or the Ukrainians if they have a better idea on how to get there, but nobody has presented us with one so far.”

Mr Kushner said he pushed the Russian president “very hard” on Europe’s desire for a ceasefire before discussions on postwar security but that he “doesn’t seem that interested”.

“I think once we’re able to define that post-war relationship, I think the issue of territory will become much easier to resolve, because right now there’s no way to bridge that last issue,” he added.

In his glowing praise of the Russian president, Mr Kushner continued: “He’s a historic figure. He’s always very, very well-prepared. He’s very thoughtful. He takes a lot of notes when we’re speaking, and then he transmits very specifically.

“It’s very clear he has a very deep understanding of the issues. He has some very strong perspectives on them.”

He added: “My assessment is that he does not only want peace, he wants to try and change the relationship with America, and he does want to try and see if there’s maybe a way to start a new beginning.”
