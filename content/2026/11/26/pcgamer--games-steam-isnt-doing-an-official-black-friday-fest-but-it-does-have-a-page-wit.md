Steam isn't running an official Black Friday fest—it says so right here in this update, "Steam isn't running an official Black Friday fest"—but plenty of game developers have marked their stuff down to take advantage of the big sale season, and so Valve is helping them all out a bit by "dressing up" its Discounts and Events page in the appropriate seasonal garb.

Is there a little bit of *wink wink, nudge nudge* to that 'not a Black Friday fest' business? Hey, maybe.

![Steam Black Friday 2026 Discounts and Events page](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/XgrthmufKHAtxiyPs3SSR3-1200-80.png)

In fair play, though, Steam traditionally hasn't run Black Friday sales, because its Autumn Sale was always happening at the same time. The realignment of seasonal sales in 2025 changed that, however, leaving a hole that was filled by the first-ever Steam Black Friday Sale last year.

Valve has other priorities this year, though, which could explain the absence (*wink wink, nudge nudge*) of an official Black Friday event on Steam in 2026. In September, Valve announced changes to Steam's Discounts and Events page to make it prettier and more personalized, and it said in today's update that Black Friday is "the perfect time to share the updates we've been working on to make the Discounts & Events page a better experience altogether."

![Discounts & Events Page Update - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/EsvP2WXeTa8/maxresdefault.jpg)

With that all cleared up, I'll say that the Black Friday non-fest page is definitely an eye-catcher, and it will be interesting to see how Valve puts this section of Steam to work in the future. If it's treated like an 'almost a Steam sale' that never really ends, I have to think that developers and gamers alike will benefit—at least until we all get used to it and Valve has to figure out some new way to tweak the formula.
