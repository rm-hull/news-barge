He has become an icon of the European left. A great political survivor, Spain’s Pedro Sánchez has been in power for eight years while – across the continent – the right has been on the march.

The Socialist party leader has championed immigration, stood up to Donald Trump and been a notable voice for Palestinians in Gaza.

But on Sunday, Sánchez might be replaced by a conservative government in league with the far right.

Madrid correspondent **Sam Jones** and European community affairs correspondent**Ashifa Kassam** report on a pivotal Spanish election, and the gamble that led Sánchez to call it.

Where will this leave the left in Spain, asks **Nosheen Iqbal**, and across Europe too?
