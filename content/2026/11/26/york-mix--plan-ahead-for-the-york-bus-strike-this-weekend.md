To help residents plan ahead for upcoming industrial action on some of York’s bus routes, City of York Council has prepared travel advice

|  |
| --- |
| The strike will affect First York bus services on Saturday 28 & Sunday 29 November. Most local routes will still run but with revised timetables and earlier finishes, around 7pm. Full information on First services can be found here: firstbus.co.uk/yorkindustrialaction  The only **service which will not run** at all is the 10/10A/10L between York and Stamford Bridge. Alternative travel options include Grimston Bar Park and Ride, or East Yorkshire services X45, X46 and X47. Passengers for Stamford Bridge should use service 747. |

The most flexible ticket to swap between operators or to be able to catch the first bus that arrives is an **All York ticket**.

This is valid across all six of York’s operators and can be bought on board.

An adult day ticket costs £6 and a family day ticket costs £9.60.

For full info, visit [https://www.itravelyork.info/fares-passes/york](https://www.itravelyork.info/fares-passes/york)

**Evening travel:** For travel home from town past 7pm, three Park and Ride sites can be reached into the evening by using different bus services:**Askham Bar:** to return to Askham Bar after 7pm, the Coastliner [https://www.transdevbus.co.uk/](https://www.transdevbus.co.uk/) runs later into the evening and stops on Tadcaster Road close to the Park and Ride site.

Please get off at the York College stop and make sure you’re back at your vehicle by 11pm as normal site closing times apply

**Designer Outlet** will be served by Arriva’s 415 later in the evening but again please ensure you’re back at your vehicle by 11pm as normal site closing times apply.

Arriva will also be providing double decker vehicles to increase the number of people each service can carry. [https://www.arrivabus.co.uk/services/AYK\_SE\_415](https://www.arrivabus.co.uk/services/AYK\_SE\_415)

**Grimston Bar Park and Ride** can be reached after 7pm as East Yorkshire’s X47 eastbound services will divert into Grimston Bar for drop off only, so that you can collect your car. The last bus to reach Grimston Bar before the site closes is the X47 which leaves from York Railway Station at 10.20pm on Saturday evening and 7.21pm on Sunday evening: [https://www.eastyorkshirebuses.co.uk/services/EY/X45?date=2026-11-28&direction=inbound](https://www.eastyorkshirebuses.co.uk/services/EY/X45?date=2026-11-28&direction=inbound).

If you will be returning to Askham Bar, Designer Outlet or Grimston Bar after 7pm please either buy single tickets on each service or All York Day or All York Family tickets.

On Saturday and Sunday a shuttle service will be available for residents in Nether Poppleton to link to Poppleton Bar P&R.

**A spokesperson at City of York Council said:** “This weekend promises to be a very busy one for York and we know a lot of residents will need to get to work or appointments, visit family, go shopping or simply enjoy our city.

“We’d encourage everyone to plan ahead and ensure you’re aware of changes to York’s usual bus services.

“Continuing to catch the bus really will help keep everyone moving and reduce congestion.”

Visitors and tourists are encouraged to use the Park and Ride or train services. York can be reached quickly by train from Leeds, Selby, Hull, Scarborough, Thirsk, Northallerton and more; full details are available via [https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/)
