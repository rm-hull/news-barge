# Watch: Man and dog narrowly escape powerful king tide in Oregon

Footage from Cannon Beach, Oregon, shows a man and a dog being swept away after a powerful wave surged ashore during unusually high "king tides".

Both the man and his dog escaped unharmed, the police department said.

Authorities have urged people to stay away from beaches as record-breaking tides, large waves and coastal flooding affect parts of the US west coast.
