Tesco is seeking permission to sell alcohol from a new site in Pocklington.

The supermarket has applied for a premises licence to allow alcohol sales for consumption off-site from 6am to midnight at the new store, on land off the Balk.

* **For more information about this public notice, click here.**
* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal at publicnoticeportal.uk/york-press**

The application also includes provision for late-night refreshments from 11pm to midnight.

Residents can view the licensing application at East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s Public Protection Department at County Hall, Beverley, during normal office hours.

Further details are also available on the council's website.
