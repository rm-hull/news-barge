**Residents are urged to prepare for altered bus services in York on 28 and 29 November, with a dedicated webpage and alternative travel options provided by the council.**

City of York Council has issued guidance to help residents plan their journeys around an upcoming bus strike affecting First York services over the weekend of 28 and 29 November. Most local routes will operate with revised timetables and earlier finishes, around 7pm, but service 10/10A/10L between York and Stamford Bridge will not run at all during the strike.

The council has set up a dedicated webpage to provide travel information: www.york.gov.uk/YorkByBus. Detailed updates on First York services can be found at firstbus.co.uk/yorkindustrialaction.

Passengers travelling to Stamford Bridge are advised to use service 747, as alternative travel options include Grimston Bar Park and Ride and East Yorkshire services X45, X46, and X47.

For flexibility across operators during the strike, the council recommends the All York ticket, valid on all six York bus operators. Adult day tickets cost £6, and family day tickets £9.60, which can be purchased on board buses. Full fare information is available at www.itravelyork.info/fares-passes/york.

Evening travel options after 7pm are limited but available via three Park and Ride sites:

* Askham Bar can be reached using the Coastliner service, which runs later into the evening.
* Designer Outlet is served after 7pm by Arriva’s 415 service, operating with double decker vehicles to accommodate passengers.
* Grimston Bar Park and Ride can be accessed via East Yorkshire’s X47 service, which diverts for drop-off only.

Passengers using these sites after 7pm are advised to purchase appropriate tickets for each leg of their journey, including single or All York Day tickets.

Additionally, a shuttle service will operate on Saturday and Sunday for residents of Nether Poppleton to connect with Poppleton Bar Park and Ride.

> **A City of York Council spokesperson said:**  
> This weekend promises to be a very busy one for York and we know a lot of residents will need to get to work or appointments, visit family, go shopping or simply enjoy our city. We’d encourage everyone to plan ahead and ensure you’re aware of changes to York’s usual bus services. Continuing to catch the bus really will help keep everyone moving and reduce congestion.

Visitors and tourists are encouraged to use Park and Ride or rail services; York has direct train connections to Leeds, Selby, Hull, Scarborough, Thirsk, Northallerton, and more. Train travel information is available at nationalrail.co.uk.
