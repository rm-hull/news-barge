A 36-year-old man has been charged with burglary and assault after a disturbance on Ashfield Terrace in Harrogate.

Officers were called in the early hours of Wednesday 25 November after reports that a man had thrown a Christmas tree through a window, smashing the glass.

North Yorkshire Police said the man then allegedly entered the property, assaulted one of the occupants and threatened others inside.

He is also accused of stealing a jacket, wallet, handbag, mobile phone, keys and cash, and of kicking an upstairs bedroom door off its hinges before leaving.

Officers arrested the man at his home. Police said they recovered all the stolen items and returned them to the victims.

The man appeared at York Magistrates’ Court today (Thursday 26th November) and was remanded in custody until his next appearance at York Crown Court on Monday 21st December.
