## The 77-year-old actor is accused by actress Charlotte Arnould, then 22, of raping her twice in August 2018. The appeal court confirmed he will stand trial for one of the alleged rapes.

Another trial is looming for Gérard Depardieu. On Thursday 26 November, the Paris court of appeal confirmed that he is being sent before a criminal court over rapes reported by actor Charlotte Arnould.

The court on Thursday ordered a dismissal for the allegations against the actor relating to 13 August 2018, but he has indeed been sent for trial over the events of 7 August.

"The courts have only half proved him right", said one of Gérard Depardieu’s lawyers, David-Olivier Kaminski, on BFMTV on Thursday. "As soon as we receive the ruling, we will examine the possibility of an appeal to the Court of Cassation", he added.

According to Carine Durrieu-Diebolt, who represents Charlotte Arnould, the decision is a "relief" for her client. "It is a first, which promises a historic trial", she said, welcoming the decision in front of the press.

Hoping to avoid a new court date, the 77-year-old actor had appealed against the indictment order issued in August 2025, after five years of investigation. In mid-September, his lawyers, Delphine Meillet and David-Olivier Kaminski, had torn apart several aspects of that order. His defence particularly disputed the rape charge, insisting there was no evidence of sexual penetration and denying the modus operandi described by the prosecution, arguing that there had been "no criminal intent".

## "State of shock"

On 7 and 13 August 2018, Charlotte Arnould, then 22, went to the actor’s Paris townhouse, which she had known since childhood, to rehearse a theatre text informally. She says she was subjected to several digital penetrations by the actor, then aged 69.

After five years of investigations, a judge ordered that Gérard Depardieu should be tried before a departmental criminal court for rapes committed by "moral constraint resulting from a state of shock that prevented Charlotte Arnould from protesting and running away". The judge notably found that at "no point" did Gérard Depardieu "make sure of the voluntary and explicit consent" of this young anorexic woman, "abusing her vulnerability".

## Growing number of complaints against the actor

Beyond Charlotte Arnould, accusations and complaints have been piling up against Gérard Depardieu for the past eight years. He is currently at the centre of investigations in Paris, following a complaint filed in May 2025 by an actor.

This complainant accuses him of gang rape, with the alleged complicity of writer Michel Houellebecq, during the shooting of the film "Thalasso" in 2018 in France.

The actor has already faced trial once. In May 2025, he was convicted of sexual assaults on two women during the filming of the movie "Les Volets verts" in 2021. Although he had lodged an appeal, he withdrew it on 13 November, offering "deep and sincere apologies to those who may have suffered from it".
