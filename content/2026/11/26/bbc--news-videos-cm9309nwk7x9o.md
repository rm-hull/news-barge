# Zelensky questions 'strange' Trump-Putin friendship in BBC interview

Volodymyr Zelensky has voiced his frustration with the United States and questioned Donald Trump's relationship with Vladimir Putin in an interview with the BBC.

In a picture posted to Truth Social on Saturday, where Trump and Putin embrace, the US president described his Russian counterpart as "my friend".

"Of course it's up to the President of the United States, but... it's strange," Zelensky told the BBC's Sarah Rainsford at his office in central Kyiv.

"He [Putin] is an aggressor, and a killer…He can't be a friend. Just cannot be."
