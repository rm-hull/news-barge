Crews from Selby and York were called to a kitchen fire at a home in Osgodby last night (Wednesday), at around 5.50pm.

Firefighters used two sets of breathing apparatus and a hose reel jet to put the fire out.

They then used a thermal imaging camera to check that there were no hotspots.

#### Osgodby, North Yorkshire

Crews gave advice to the responsible person at the scene.
