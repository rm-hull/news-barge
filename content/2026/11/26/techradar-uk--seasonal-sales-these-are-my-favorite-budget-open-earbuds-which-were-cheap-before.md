If you've been considering using Black Friday to get into the wildly popular open earbuds category, you'll want to avoid buying anything too cheap. In a few weeks, you'll have buyers' remorse and the buds will have a new home in a landfill site. That's why I feel the need to recommend the Baseus Bowie MC2; they're the best affordable pair of buds I've tested, and I used them for months... and they're now even cheaper in the sales.

In the US, the MC2 are now $45.99 at Amazon (was $79.99), saving you 43%, while in the UK the MC2 sell for £49.99 at Amazon (was £79.99), making for a 38% discount.

At those prices, you can skip all of the bargain-bin clipping buds and try this pair, which has served me well for ages. And if you're just getting into the form factor, banish the thought of the $/£100+ options: these are all you need.

When I was commissioned last week to write about my favorite open earbuds of 2026 ahead of the Black Friday sales, the Baseus Bowie MC2 were one of the three models I mentioned, and I said then what I said now: they're the best budget models.

I wrote about how a discount wasn't important, since they were already cheap, but I wasn't expecting them to be reduced by over a third. That's a big percentage saving on affordable tech, and so their selling point just became even more valid.
