Ripon Grammar School student Alistair Broadwith has been selected for England’s under-18 hockey squad.

The 16-year-old midfielder from Bedale earned his place after five days of trials across two camps, having spent the past two years in the England under-16 squad.

Alistair will now attend monthly training camps and represent England in matches and tournaments against other European nations.

He said:

> “I felt very excited and grateful when I found out.
>
> “I love competing, and my strengths as a player are my individual skill and vision on the pitch.
>
> “I’m really looking forward to the training and matches, particularly playing against other countries. My ambition is to play for England and Great Britain when I’m older.”

Alistair started playing at Thirsk Hockey Club when he was six.

He now trains once a week with Leeds Hockey Club’s men’s first team, plays for the side at weekends and does his own strength and conditioning work.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b084d8dbc53388580b.png)

He joined Ripon Grammar School’s lower sixth in September and said the school had helped him fit his studies around training and matches.

Adam Milner, the school’s director of sport, said:

> “Alistair’s selection is a hugely significant achievement and reflects the tremendous commitment and hard work he has put into his hockey, largely outside school.
>
> “To secure a place in the England U18 squad after spending two years representing his country at U16 level is a fantastic accomplishment.
>
> "Credit must also go to his previous school, Cundall Manor, for the part they have played in his development.
>
> “We are incredibly proud of him and look forward to following his progress.”
