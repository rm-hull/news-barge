## York Christmas cinema with falling snow sells 4,000 tickets as shows sell out

Impression of how the Enchanted Christmas Cinema coming to York might look. Image supplied

*York Christmas cinema nears sell-out as 4,000 tickets sold, reports MAXINE GORDON*

A magical Christmas cinema where snow falls before every screening is on course to sell out after around 4,000 tickets were snapped up within weeks of going on sale.

The Enchanted Christmas Cinema will transform Yorkshire Museum's historic and rarely visited Tempest Anderson Hall into a festive woodland wonderland with some screenings already sold out.

Taking place from December 21 to 23, the attraction will convert the museum's rarely visited Tempest Anderson Hall into a cosy forest-themed cinema where snow falls before every screening.

Visitors will step into a winter woodland complete with real trees before settling down to watch some of the nation's favourite Christmas films: Home Alone, Elf, Love Actually, The Holiday and The Muppet Christmas Carol.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21764955.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Impression of how the Enchanted Christmas Cinema coming to York might look. Image supplied

Guests can enjoy mulled wine, hot chocolate, popcorn and festive treats from the venue's bar before taking their seats for one of the screenings.

Demand has already proved strong, with around 4,000 tickets snapped up and several showings selling out.

Dominic Davies, co-founder of Enchanted Christmas Cinema, said: "We are thrilled at the response to our Enchanted Christmas Cinema. We knew when we saw the venue it would be the perfect setting for a cosy Christmas movie and knew we could add some extra magic to kick-start people's Christmas season."

At the time of writing, all screenings for Home Alone, Love Actually and The Holiday were sold out, with film fans invited to join a wait list.

A spokesperson for the venue said: "10 of 15 shows have now sold out! There are 350 tickets left for the remaining shows."

The auditorium which will be turned into a winter wonderland dates back more than 100 years and is named after Victorian York explorer and surgeon Tempest Anderson. It is normally closed to most museum visitors.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21764956.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tempest Anderson Hall at Yorkshire Museum which will be transformed into the Enchanted Christmas Cinema. Image: Sophia Cheng Jayme Elkins

For the festive event, organisers are creating an atmospheric Christmas setting featuring woodland scenery, seasonal decorations and a selection of festive food and drink.

The event has been designed to appeal to both families and adults looking to get into the Christmas spirit, with screenings running throughout the day from 12pm until 8pm.

Organisers say the combination of classic festive films and an unusual historic venue has helped attract audiences keen to enjoy a different kind of cinema experience.

The Enchanted Christmas Cinema is just one of many festive attractions in Museum Gardens this year: others include Thor's Tipi, serving food and drinks, and Thor's On Ice, an ice-rink located just behind the City Art Gallery.
