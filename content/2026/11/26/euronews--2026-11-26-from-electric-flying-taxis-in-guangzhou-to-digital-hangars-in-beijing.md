## China is testing passenger flying taxis in Guangzhou and digitising aircraft maintenance in Beijing, as it pushes new technology into every stage of air travel.

"When we became the first to obtain an airworthiness certificate, everyone could see a clear goal," says Wang Zhao, Co-Chief Operating Officer, EHang. CGTN's Yang Zhao rode a flying taxi in Guangzhou. It has no control stick, so passengers cannot take over, and follows pre-programmed routes.

In Beijing, AMECO, a joint venture between Air China and Germany's Lufthansa, electronically tags every tool in its hangar. "If a tool is left on the aircraft, it creates a serious safety hazard for the flight," says Hou Bo, Tool and Equipment Engineer.
