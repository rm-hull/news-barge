Grabbing a Truffade Thrax in GTA 6 isn't too hard, but if you want Andrew Node's supercharged version, the true fastest car around, you'll need to complete a little collectible hunt. Before you start, it's worth noting that this special Truffade Thrax costs 70k to register when you steal it, though you can also sell it to Randy for 20k (not that I would recommend this as it's a unique car).

The first step is scanning ten No Flusher toilet graffiti stamps around Vice Beach, the east portion of Vice City, with your WAiNK It app. Each symbol is basically a little blue toilet. After that, you'll have to head to a secret lockup, and then you're on for stealing the car.

## 1. Southeast of Nola - Ocean Beach

This first one is southeast of Nola - Ocean beach on the pavement on the west side of the road.

## 2. East of Podium Sports - Ocean Beach

The second is east of Podium Sports - Ocean Beach, on the south side pavement of the road leading out onto the grass towards the beach.

## 3. Near the bridge leading to Waxman Island

This one is at the east end of the bridge leading from Vice Beach to Waxman Island, on the southern pavement. Also to the west of LTD Gas - Ocean Beach

## 4. East of Aural Expert

This fourth one is east of Aural Expert and north of Venus Fitness, on the pavement at the side of the junction.

## 5. Out front of Banana Hole Main Bar

This one is on the pavement directly in front of Banana Hole Main Bar, east down the road from the previous one above.

## 6. At the southern end of the park

This sixth one is on the sidewalk on the far west of the road running along the bottom of the huge park in Vice Beach.

## 7. West of Podium Sports - Ocean Beach

This seventh is west of Podium Sports - Ocean Beach, down the road you can see with the roundabout at the end on the map.

## 8. East of Shorefront - Ocean Beach bank

The eighth is east down the road from Shorefront - Ocean Beach bank and south of Easy Electronics, on the pavement on the corner.

## 9. North of Marshal's Jewelry

This ninth is north of Marshal's jewelry and northeast of Endzone - Washington Beach, at the east end of the big carpark bordering the beach.

## 10. At Oldby Island junction

On the right side of the northern road at the Oldby Island junction, directly opposite Ron Gas - Washington Beach.

## Accessing the secret hideout

![GTA 6 Truffade Thrax - Code](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8ToTqSpgqBjQjRMnN9BaMn-1200-80.jpg)

After you've found all ten, Cal's conspiracy theory chat (which unlocks after the first) will go dark, before you get a separate message a little later giving you their hideout location. Head there, read the note to the right of the garage door, and then flip it around to get the code for the lock:

* 8554013

Inside you'll have a bunch of evidence pointing to Andrew Node's house on Starfish island, so examine it all and then you're ready to steal the car.

## How to steal Andrew Node's Truffade Thrax

First off, Starfish Island is a gated community with security, so it's better to get a boat there. Head west from the hideout and steal a boat on the piers, then drive west across the water to the jetty of Andrew Node's house—it's the one on the southern side towards the centre, that looks like a backwards L.

Dock, head around the left side of the house to find a white garage on the right you can open up with Andrew Node's Truffade Thrax inside. You'll have to drive it off the island, but after that you can head to Randy and register it for 70k. Just make sure you have the garage space and the money before you steal the car.
