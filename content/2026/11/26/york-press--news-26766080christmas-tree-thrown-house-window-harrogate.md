A man has been charged after allegedly throwing a Christmas tree through a window during a reported burglary in North Yorkshire, police said.

The county's force said the incident took place in the early hours of yesterday (Wednesday, November 25), at a property on Ashfield Terrace, Harrogate.

It was reported by police that a man threw a Christmas tree through the window, entered the property, and assaulted one of the occupants.




The man is also alleged to have threatened others inside the home and to have stolen a jacket, wallet, handbag, mobile phone, keys, and a quantity of cash, police said.

Before leaving, the suspect allegedly went upstairs and kicked a bedroom door off its hinges, causing further damage.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Officers attended the scene, identified the suspect, and arrested a 36-year-old man at his property on suspicion of robbery, assault occasioning actual bodily harm, and criminal damage.

"All stolen items were recovered and returned to the victims.

"Following an interview and further enquiries, the man was charged with burglary and assault."

The man appeared before York Magistrates’ Court this morning and was remanded in custody.

He is due to appear at York Crown Court on Monday, December 21.
