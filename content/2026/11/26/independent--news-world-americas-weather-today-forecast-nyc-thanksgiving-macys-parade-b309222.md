Foul weather has disrupted Thanksgiving Day for much of the U.S., with air travel disrupted, warnings issued for those on the roads, and the iconic Macy’s parade postponed.

Macy's said in a statement that it was postponing the event until Saturday in an abundance of caution after consulting with city officials.

The strengthening storm had raised worries about how the annual parade's massive balloons would fare in the wind-swept canyons between Manhattan's skyscrapers.

Meanwhile, strong winds, heavy rain and flooding are affecting both coasts.

The National Weather Service said the storm was expected to hamper air travel at eastern U.S. airports Thanksgiving Day into Friday morning, with reduced visibility, greater turbulence, strong winds and aircraft icing.

![A biker pedals down a flooded bike path caused by high tides in Mill Valley, California](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/13/California_Extreme_Weather_736_7.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Wind gusts were expected to reach 50 mph (80 kph) or higher along the Interstate 95 corridor.

The weather service posted wind advisories from northern Georgia to most of the mid-Atlantic to southern New England.

Gov. Mikie Sherrill declared a state of emergency for all of New Jersey on Wednesday night.

Winter storm warnings and watches were also posted for parts of Missouri and Illinois.

Up to 13 inches (33 centimeters) of snow was expected around St. Louis by Thursday afternoon.

![Flooding, caused by high tides, extends across a bike path, road and onto Tamalpais High School football field](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/13/California_Extreme_Weather_89434.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

On the West Coast, king tides continued surging, with coastal flood warnings issued from Southern California to the San Francisco area to the Oregon and Washington coasts.

King tides occur when the gravitational pulls of the moon and sun align to raise water levels.

Their effects this year are being amplified by the ongoing super El Niño, which has warmed the ocean, leading to an increased volume of seawater.

![King tides and high surf batter the California coast](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/23/Extreme_Weather_California_91558.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A video showed a massive wave tearing down a portion of the pier in the city of Pacifica, California, Wednesday and slamming it into the ocean.

Officials had closed the L-shaped pier in the summer after finding cracks in the structure.

North of San Francisco, Marin County officials issued an evacuation warning for residents of beachfront properties in Stinson Beach, saying large waves expected through the afternoon could send water and debris into roads and homes.
