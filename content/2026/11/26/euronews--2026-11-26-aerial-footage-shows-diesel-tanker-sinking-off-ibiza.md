Spanish authorities are assessing the risk of marine pollution after a tanker carrying around 7,000 tonnes of diesel capsized off the coast of Ibiza in the Mediterranean.

The Maltese-flagged vessel, named 'Med Emre', was travelling from Algeciras in southern Spain to Barcelona when it suffered an accident in the early hours of Thursday, 26 November.

All 17 crew members were rescued, but one later died in hospital. Six others reportedly suffered minor injuries, including hypothermia.

The vessel was located overturned in the sea, with its hull facing upwards. Authorities have not confirmed a fuel spill, although monitoring operations are continuing.
