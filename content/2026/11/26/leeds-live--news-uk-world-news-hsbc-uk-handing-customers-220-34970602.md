# HSBC UK handing customers £220 payment along with supermarket perk worth £40

## HSBC has confirmed an 'upfront' offer that could see eligible switching

HSBC has confirmed an "upfront" bank-switching incentive featuring instant payments that could arrive in customers' accounts before the festive period. The "rarely offered" deal also incorporates a £40 reward for those who shop at M&S, Sainsbury's, Tesco and Waitrose.

The team at BBC and ITV personality Martin Lewis' Money Saving Expert (MSE) revealed that the HSBC Bank Account is accessible to a broad range of Britons, with those switching eligible for the cash reward. MSE said: "FREE £220 cash and £40 voucher from a bank which rarely offers fee-free bank switch bonuses. Big upfront bonus we remember on a non-high-earner account."

Securing the £220 before Christmas is entirely feasible, provided you apply straight away and satisfy the necessary qualifying criteria. With 37 days remaining until Christmas Day (December 25), and HSBC UK's switching terms stipulating that the bonus arrives within 60 days of fulfilling all conditions, a pre-December 25 payment is attainable if you act promptly, though it cannot be guaranteed, reports Birmingham Live.

To be eligible, you'll need to open a bank account, which can be completed via HSBC's mobile banking app or through their website.
