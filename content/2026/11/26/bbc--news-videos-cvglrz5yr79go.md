# Watch: BBC confronts German man suspected of supplying migrant gangs with small boats

A German boat dealer is suspected of being a key supplier to migrant smugglers operating in the English Channel, the BBC can reveal.

Nearly a third of small-boat crossings to the UK, in the first four months of 2026, allegedly involved boats or engines from MaReBoote, a small firm in western Germany, according to files we have seen.

The managing director, Marvin Reuter, is contesting extradition to France, where he could face smuggling, criminal conspiracy and involuntary manslaughter charges.

His equipment is suspected of being linked to two fatal accidents, in which four men died.

We confronted the 35-year-old at his business, but he would not answer our questions. He said he was reporting us to the police.
