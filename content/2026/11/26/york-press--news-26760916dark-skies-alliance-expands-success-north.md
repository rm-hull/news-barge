## North York Moors dark skies initiative to expand across England

Milky Way in the skies near Hawnby *(Image: Steve Bell)*

A dark skies initiative that began in northern England is set to expand nationwide following its success.

The Northern England Dark Skies Alliance (NEDSA), which brings together all 13 National Parks and National Landscapes across the North, has demonstrated the value of collaboration in tackling light pollution.

At a conference in York last week, NEDSA agreed to broaden the alliance to include all protected landscapes across England.

Mike Hawtin, Dark Skies Officer for the North York Moors National Park and founder of the Alliance, said: "The conference underlined just how far we have come since we first began the Alliance.

"Now we have a wealth of resources and contacts, including researchers, lighting manufacturers and installers, and conservation specialists sharing their knowledge.

"By extending this collaboration to include all of England’s Protected Landscapes we can build on a momentum which is placing the quality of the night higher up on society’s and policy makers’ conservation radar."

Mr Hawtin said protecting dark skies supports both wildlife and human wellbeing.

He added: "By broadening out the collaboration nationally, we can drive forward the message that by having the right light at night we can strike a compromise between human needs and wildlife's."
