A magical woodland walk is lighting up the season as families enjoy the return of the Lotherton Christmas Experience.

The festive event, located just outside Leeds, opened on Saturday, November 21, and has already delighted visitors with its reimagined 12 Days of Christmas trail through Captain’s Wood, complete with glowing lights, interactive displays and sensory play points.

The immersive trail offers playful stops including slides, giant flower ladies, swan rides and glowing gold rings.

Megan Allen, founder of the Rural Travel Guide, said: "We had a wonderful time at the Lotherton Christmas Experience.

"The woodland trail was our highlight.

"My son, Ezra, has autism and ADHD, so anything interactive and sensory is wonderful for him.

"He loved the bright colours, all the lights and particularly the five gold rings where he could walk through the steam."

Rosie Heard, curator of the Christmas Experience, said: "Hearing the woods ring with laughter on opening weekend has been absolutely magical.

"The woodland walk has been a much-loved part of Christmas at Lotherton for years, and seeing families step into the reimagined story for the very first time was a real joy."

The Christmas Experience runs until Sunday, January 3, 2027, with Santa in residence until December 24.
