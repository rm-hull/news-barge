The Bravado Banshee is another one of the cars you'll need to track down for Randy in GTA 6, but unlike the Pegassi Infernus, this one doesn't involve anything as dodgy as robbing a police lockup.

You can find this car inside the Sunrise Premium Autos showroom at the north tip of Southside, southwest of Pay 'N' Spray - Salton or east across the road from Ron Gas - Southside. This region is to the northeast of Hamlet.

There are two showrooms here, so make sure you have the right one, though the other does contain Pfister Growlers, in case you haven't gotten that car for Randy yet, either. Head inside to find the car—if you've played previous GTAs, you'll recognise it as a convertible with a white stripe down the middle. Hop inside, use the key cloner, and drive it through the glass.

This might damage it a bit, but you can always repair it at Ron Gas nearby, or at the gas station near Randy's shop. Just remember to drive fast, and if the tracker starts to beep, speed up to stop it pinging your location to the cops. Delivering this one gets you 6,500k and a 5k condition bonus as usual if it's in good nick.
