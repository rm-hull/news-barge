CROWDS are expected at a community Christmas event and lights switch-on this weekend.

The Bishopthorpe Road Traders Association has announced it will be hosting a festive bazaar, taking place on Sunday (November 29) from 11am to 4pm in the Bishopthorpe Road Shopper’s Car Park.

The free event, which will bring more than 50 traders to the street, will conclude with a Christmas lights switch-on at 4pm.

A spokesperson for the Bishopthorpe Road Traders Association said: "We are thrilled with the response to our bazaar series this year.

"These events have created a genuine feeling of community coming together.

"The final bazaar, capped off with our much-anticipated Christmas Lights Switch-On, is the perfect way to kick off the holiday season for everyone on Bishopthorpe Road."

This will be the fifth and final market of the year, following a successful series of events that have brought residents and visitors together to celebrate and support independent businesses in York and South Bank.

As with previous events, the Christmas Bazaar will showcase more than 50 stalls featuring handmade crafts, unique gifts, seasonal foods, and work from local artists and makers.

There will also be festive live entertainment, with performances and activities for people of all ages.

Organisers say the event will be the "biggest yet" and that they want to create a festive gathering point for the community, drawing Christmas shoppers and supporters from wider York and beyond.

The Bishopthorpe Road Traders Association is a community-led group dedicated to promoting and supporting businesses along Bishopthorpe Road, which - through events like the bazaar - hope to 'strengthen the connection between businesses and residents while encouraging visitors to explore the area'.

A spokesperson said: "This series of bazaars has not only provided a platform for local makers and traders to showcase their work but has also strengthened the bonds between residents and businesses along Bishopthorpe Road."

The final bazaar is expected to draw large crowds and organisers encourage visitors to arrive early to enjoy the full day of activities.

The event is organised by the Bishopthorpe Road Traders Association and is free to attend.

More details can be found on the @bishyroad Instagram page with previews of the traders shared.
