Proton has faced a lot of scrutiny in recent weeks, with many wondering whether it’s time to look elsewhere.

If the headlines don’t deter you, it’s a great time to sign up to the Swiss provider. It’s running one of the best Black Friday VPN deals available — you can get Proton VPN Plus for $2.49 a month for two years, saving $24 in total.

Whether Proton’s recent track record bothers you or not, there are three other Black Friday VPN deals worth considering before you decide.

**NordVPN** gives you faster speeds and stronger streaming performance for just $0.50 more a month.**Surfshark** includes extra security tools and unlimited device connections for a lower price. And**Windscribe’s** unchanging price means you can avoid Proton’s steep renewal price hikes.

Personally, I'd go with Surfshark, and here's why:

## Surfshark: Lower price, more features

Today, Surfshark announced that it was dropping its Black Friday price even further. You can now get Surfshark One for $2.29 a month, down from the usual $2.79.

Initially, the company had dropped Surfshark One to the same price as its Starter plan, which meant you got extras — including antivirus, Scam Protection, and dark web breach monitoring — essentially for free.

But with the latest drop in price, it's now cheaper than Proton VPN Plus’s monthly rate, while giving you unlimited simultaneous connections (versus Proton’s 10) and faster WireGuard speeds in our recent head-to-head testing.

## Windscribe: No annual price hikes

Black Friday is always a great time to get a deal, but you have to keep an eye out for automatic renewal price hikes. With many plans running for two years, forgetting to cancel before the price jumps is pretty common.

Windscribe fixes that by locking in one set price of $29 for a year, every year.

That slightly undercuts Proton VPN Plus’s two-year rate while ensuring you won’t get hit with costly renewal spikes — unlike Proton, which roughly doubles in price after your first term.

## The verdict

Despite the recent controversies, there’s still lots to like about Proton. Its VPN is one of the best we’ve tested, and it has a solid track record on protecting users' privacy. It’s also one of the best Black Friday deals available. Still, it’s not the only deal out there, and it’s worth deciding what you value most and exploring all your options before making a long-term commitment.
