## Traffic builds near road closure in York after damage caused by burst water main

St Helen's Road was closed this morning (Thursday, November 26) as engineers worked on the burst mains *(Image: Cllr Stephen Fenton)*

A burst water main has caused flooding to a York street and closed a junction with a main road in and out of the city.




![A road closure in York](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21766287.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The junction of Tadcaster Road and St Helen's Road was closed this morning (Thursday, November 26) *(Image: Cllr Stephen Fenton)*

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Huntington was called to St Helen's Road, off Tadcaster Road, at 6.29pm last night (Wednesday, November 25).

A service spokesperson said pumps were used by its crews to divert the water away from properties.

Yorkshire Water engineers were called to the scene by the service, it confirmed.

A spokesperson for the water company said: “Last night, our colleagues repaired a burst main on St Helens Road.

"Unfortunately, the burst has caused significant consequential damage.

"We are meeting with the York City Council this afternoon to assess the situation and devise a plan and timeline for the reinstatement and eventual reopening of the road.

"We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone for their patience.”

Dringhouses and Woodthorpe ward councillor Stephen Fenton went to the scene last night.

He said: "The volume and the force of the water escaping was quite intense.

"A few people got in touch and some residents who usually park their vehicles outside their houses were unable to get in.

"Most were reassured that the Fire Service and Yorkshire Water engineers were on site."

Later that evening, AA Traffic News reported the road was closed both ways from the A1036 Tadcaster Road to Dringhouses Primary School.

Traffic is running slowly in Tadcaster Road at the junction with St Helen's Road which remains closed this morning.
