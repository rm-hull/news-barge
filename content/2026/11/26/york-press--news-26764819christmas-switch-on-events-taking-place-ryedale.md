Christmas lights will be switched on in towns across Ryedale.

Helmsley will welcome Santa in his sleigh, along with performances from the Helmsley Arts Centre Singers and Swinton and District Excelsior Band.

The festivities, organised by Helmsley Town Council, will begin in the Market Place at 4.30pm on Friday, November 27.

Pickering's switch-on event will take place in the Market Place on Saturday, November 28.

The festivities will take place from 2pm to 5.30pm, with the lights being turned on at 4.15pm.

Norton will switch on their Christmas lights on Sunday, November 29.
