Powerful waves combined with king tides destroyed part of the municipal pier in Pacifica, California, US, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

Heavy surf battered beachfront homes and flooded stretches of the coastline. The 350-metre pier, located south of San Francisco, had been closed since the summer after cracks were discovered in its structure.

The National Weather Service issued coastal flood and high surf warnings, with breaking waves expected to reach six to nine metres.

In Marin County, authorities temporarily urged residents of beachfront properties in Stinson Beach to prepare to evacuate. King tides, the highest tides of the year, occur when the gravitational forces of the sun and moon align.

This year, their impact is being amplified by warmer ocean waters associated with El Niño.
