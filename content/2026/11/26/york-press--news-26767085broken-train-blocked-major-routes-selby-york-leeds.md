A ‘RESCUE train is being prepared’ after a broken down train has blocked a major route in North Yorkshire.

Rail travel in and out of Selby station has been blocked today after a train has broken down in the area - with a points failure in Leeds causing further issues in the area.

The incident was reported at 4.20pm on Thursday (November 26) and will affect trains running between Leeds / York and Selby.

“Trains running between Leeds / York and Selby may be held until the lines are clear or revised to terminate and start back from a different station on their route,” said Network Rail spokesperson.

They added that services on the following routes will be affected by this disruption:

* Between Halifax and Hull - These services will run between Halifax and Leeds only
* Between York and Hull/Bridlington - These services will run between Hull and Bridlington only
* Between Liverpool Lime Street to Hull - No trains can run between Leeds and Selby.

Disruption is expected until 7pm today.

The spokesperson added that Northern train ticket restrictions have been lifted in the affected areas, including Advance and Peak restrictions, during this disruption.

In Leeds, a point failure means that some lines are blocked and trains may be delayed by up to 20 minutes or revised.
