Oatlands Infant School in Harrogate has been named one of eight National Inclusion Champions by the Inclusion Quality Mark (IQM).

The annual award recognises schools that make inclusion part of everyday school life and share their approach with others.

It follows the school’s recent achievement of IQM Flagship Status.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b0845913eaffeacfe9.png)

Headteacher Christopher Harrison said:

> “We want our children running through the school gates on a Monday morning, skipping through the corridors, and singing on the playground.
>
> "Feeling included is a key foundation in building mental health and wellbeing, and we recognise that happy staff teach better, and happy children learn more.”

Oatlands is part of Yorkshire Causeway Schools Trust. Jane Goodwin, the trust’s chief executive, said:

> “This remarkable achievement is a testament to the dedication, compassion and professionalism of everyone at Oatlands Infant School.
>
> "Being named one of only eight National Inclusion Champions in the country demonstrates what can be achieved when inclusion is placed at the heart of everything we do.”
