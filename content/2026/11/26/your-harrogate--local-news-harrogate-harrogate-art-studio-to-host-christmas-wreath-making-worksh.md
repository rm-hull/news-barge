PAINT! Yorkshire is hosting three Christmas wreath-making workshops at its Harrogate studio on the first weekend in December.

The team at 24 Regent Parade will guide guests through making their own wreath and decorating it with ribbon, ready to take home and hang on their front door.

The first workshop takes place on Saturday 5th December from 6pm to 8.30pm.

Two more follow on Sunday 6th December, from 2pm to 4.30pm and from 6pm to 8.30pm.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b08151b4f5f95c1b20.png)

Each session costs £45 per person, including all materials, a welcome drink and a mince pie.

No previous experience is needed, and under-16s are welcome with a parent or legal guardian.

*Places must be booked in advance**here**. Doors open 15 minutes before each session.*
