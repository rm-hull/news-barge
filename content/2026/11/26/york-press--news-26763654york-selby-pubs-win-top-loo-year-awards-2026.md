## Three York and North Yorkshire pubs win top ratings in national toilet awards

Toilets in The Giant Bellflower in Gowthorpe, Selby *(Image: Provided)*

SEVERAL pubs in York and North Yorkshire have been recognised for having some of the best toilets in the country.

Two Wetherspoon pubs - The Postern Gate on Piccadilly in York and The Giant Bellflower on Gowthorpe in Selby - have both received a platinum plus rating in the 2026 Loo of the Year Awards.

The Punch Bowl on Blossom Street in York was awarded a platinum rating.




Maddison Crompton-Page, manager of The Postern Gate, said: "We are delighted with the grading.

"Staff at the pub ensure the toilets are in excellent condition at all times and it is great that this has been recognised by the inspectors."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21765224.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sinks in The Giant Bellflower in Gowthorpe, Selby *(Image: Provided)*

The Loo of the Year Awards aim to highlight and improve ‘away from home’ toilet standards across the UK.

Inspectors make unannounced visits and grade toilets as bronze, silver, gold, platinum, platinum plus, or diamond - with ungraded toilets deemed unacceptable.

Facilities are judged on decor and maintenance, cleanliness, accessibility, hand-washing and drying equipment, and overall management.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21765223.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The ladies loos in The Postern Gate in Piccadilly, York *(Image: Provided)*

Becky Wall, managing director of the Loo of the Year Awards 2026, said: "The toilets at the three pubs have been independently assessed against the Loo of the Year Awards’ 101 criteria, recognising the standard of the facilities, their cleanliness, maintenance, and overall presentation.

"The pubs thoroughly deserve their gradings."
