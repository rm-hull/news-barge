# Police find weapons and £10,000 in early morning raids in Yorkshire

## Police have seized £10,000 in cash and weapons following a raid in the early hours of the morning in Leeds.

Raids took place at two addresses in Talbot Gardens and Harehills Lane at 6am on Wednesday (Nov 25).

Five people have been arrested on suspicion of drug supply and production, West Yorkshire Police said.

![Police seized cash, weapons, gold jewellery and prepared dealer bags](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/25/17/31/R-AIDS.jpeg?trim=46,3,86,0&width=640&quality=65)


Three men aged 54, 46, and 35, and two women aged 44 and 26 have been taken into custody.

Police found a machete, a pickaxe handle, a knuckle duster, and four kilos of gold jewellery.

Prepared dealer bags, 20 grams of ketamine, a drugs price list and eight phones were also seized in the raids.

![Amongst the weapons seized was a machete and a knuckle duster](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/25/17/32/R-AIDS1.jpeg?trim=62,0,70,3&width=640&quality=65)


Detective Inspector Heather Shearer, of the Yorkshire and Humber Regional Intelligence Unit said: “The Yorkshire and Humber Regional Organised Unit remains committed to taking down those who involve themselves in the organised supply of drugs.

“The supply of drugs fuels further criminality and violence in our communities and brings misery to the lives of individual users and their families.

“I hope today’s arrests will provide some reassurance to the public.”
