## Simply the best

![The LG C6 OLED TV with Elemental on screen, showing Ember by a colorful vase. This shot shows the C6&#039;s vibrant, refined colors which also look accurate](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/WyLMr8MuGdoXdZ2BxPBy3K-1200-80.jpg)

The LG C6 was my personal pick for the best TV this year, and it won TV of the Year in this year's TechRadar Choice Awards. It's not a flashy, flagship OLED or a new RGB mini-LED powerhouse, but what the LG C6 does, it does *incredibly* well.

As I said in my LG C6 review, the C6 is a notable upgrade over its predecessor, the LG C5. When I compared the two side-by-side, the C6 showed better color accuracy, even revealing that the C5 we had in our testing room had a green tint when watching scenes in *The Batman*, whereas the C6 didn't have this issue.

With a new Alpha a11 Gen 3 AI Processor, the same used in the flagship LG G6 OLED, the C6 feels faster when navigating the webOS smart TV interface (which is one of the best on the market in my opinion). It also gets a nice brightness boost, hitting 1,427 nits peak HDR brightness to the C5's 1,180 nits.

This means pictures on screen have more impact, with punchier HDR highlights that look great in movies like *Elemental*. The gorgeous colors of this movie, like Ember's flame or Wade's water, looked vibrant and dazzling on the C6. Elsewhere, high-contrast movies like* The Batman*and* Dark City* showed how powerful the C6's contrast was.

The C6 is also the best gaming TV on the market in our opinion, with four HDMI 2.1 ports all supporting 4K 165Hz, AMD FreeSync and Nvidia G-Sync VRR, ALLM and Dolby Vision gaming. A 9.2ms input lag time means performance is razor-sharp, and combined with the superb picture quality it means gaming is a blast on the C6. If you need a TV upgrade for *GTA 6*, this is a terrific option.
