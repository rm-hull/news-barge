**The dealerships are collecting new, unwrapped toys to support Cash for Kids Mission Christmas, aiming to help disadvantaged children across Yorkshire enjoy festive presents.**

Lloyd Land Rover’s Ripon and York dealerships have teamed up with Rudding Park to support this year’s Cash for Kids Mission Christmas appeal, in an effort to bring festive gifts to children and young people living in poverty across Yorkshire.

The initiative encourages clients and the local community to donate new, unwrapped toys that will be distributed to children who might otherwise wake up without presents on Christmas Day. Donations are being accepted at the Lloyd Land Rover showrooms in Ripon, located on Hutton Bank, and in York, on Lysander Close, until Wednesday 16 December.

Mission Christmas, organised by charity Cash for Kids, aims to tackle the challenges faced by families struggling with the rising cost of living, ensuring children throughout the region can experience the joy of Christmas regardless of their circumstances.

As an incentive, anyone donating a toy and completing an entry form will be entered into a prize draw to win a one-night Dinner, Bed and Breakfast Sleeping Beauty Spa Break for two guests at Rudding Park, which includes complimentary spa access and a meal.

On Thursday 17 December, Lloyd Land Rover will collect donations from both showrooms and deliver the toys directly to the official Mission Christmas warehouse, expediting their distribution to local children in time for Christmas Day.

> **Mark Redding, Head of Business at Lloyd Land Rover Ripon, said:**  
> Community is at the heart of everything we do at Lloyd Land Rover, so joining Mission Christmas felt like the natural thing to do.  
> We’re proud to be part of an appeal that brings people together to make a real difference for children and young people in Yorkshire, and we’re grateful to Rudding Park for generously donating the prize.

For more information and to find the nearest donation point, visit the Lloyd Motor Group website at www.lloydmotorgroup.com/News/Lloyd-Carlisle–Kelso–Ripon-and-York-Join-Mission-Christmas/2698
