# Woman groomed and raped by multiple men in Rotherham as a teenager, jury told

## A jury has heard a tearful woman describe how she was groomed and raped by a number of men in Rotherham when she was teenager, saying: “I thought it was normal.”

Sheffield Crown Court was shown a video interview with the woman, who is now in her 30s, as three men – Basharat Dad, Nasser Dad and Reza Tavakoli – went on trial in relation to the grooming and sexual exploitation of her and another girl 20 years ago.

In the video played to the jury on Wednesday, the woman wiped away tears as she described to officers how she believed she loved Basharat Dad, despite him forcing her into sex with him and other men.

She said: “He must have known what he was doing, but I didn’t see it.

“I thought it was normal.”

Basharat Dad, 42, denies 13 counts of rape, four offences of trafficking within the UK for sexual exploitation, and one of false imprisonment.

![Sheffield Crown Court](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/13/55/20261002_162316(1).jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


His brother Nasser Dad, 46, denies two counts of rape; and Tavakoli, 55, denies one charge of rape and one of false imprisonment.

Opening the case for the prosecution, Matthew Cobbe told jurors: “It is the prosecution case that Basharat Dad coerced and manipulated (the first complainant), causing her to believe that she was in a relationship with him whereas, in truth, he was doing no more than taking advantage of her, using her for himself, and passing her off to others as he chose.”

Mr Cobbe said the woman met Basharat Dad – known as Bash – through a friend in Rotherham in about 2004.

Her and her friends “were invited to a fireworks shop by Bash and others where they drank alcohol, played cards, including strip poker, and had sex”.

The prosecutor said: “Following this occasion Bash called her repeatedly, gave her attention and made her believe that she was in a relationship with him.

“She believed that she loved him and she believed that he loved her.

“That was precisely what he wanted her to believe.

“He had been grooming her.”

Mr Cobbe said: “The fact that he was grooming her and he had exploited her was made clear by the sex he expected her to have with others.

“She did not enjoy being passed around, but that is what he compelled her to do.”

Mr Cobbe described multiple occasions when the woman was raped, starting when she was about 14 or 15 years old.

He outlined one time when Bash left her in a house in Sheffield after she was made to perform oral sex on one of his friends.

The prosecutor said she was raped in the house by an older man after another tried to make her take drugs in a “crack pipe”.

He said the woman recalls men passing cash between them and it was “clear to her that the older male had paid for the privilege of raping her”.

Mr Cobbe said that, on another occasion, Basharat Dad drove her to a secluded spot where she had to perform oral sex on another man who told her: “Bash says you’ll sort me out.”

He told the jury of five men and seven women: “Being caused to have sex with other males had, as Bash said, become a feature of that exploitative relationship.

“She recalled being taken to the upstairs of the takeaway (owned by the Dad family) on several occasions where she was expected to have sex with several of Bash’s friends.”

Mr Cobbe said: “Reza Tavakoli was a beneficiary, as he would see it, of Basharat Dad’s grooming.

“He forced himself on (the first complainant) when Basharat presented him with that opportunity.”

Mr Cobbe said Nasser Dad “groomed and manipulated” the second complainant in the trial in the same way.

He said: “He was twice her age, and used her as he saw fit.”

The girl was about 14 when it began, he said.

The prosecutor said: “Basharat Dad was a beneficiary of older brother Nasser Dad’s grooming of (the second complainant).”

Mr Cobbe said the defendants have all responded with “flat denials” to the allegations.

In another video interview with the first complainant played to the jury, she described the incident in Sheffield when she says she was raped by an older man in Sheffield after she was offered a crack pipe.

She said she felt “violated”.

Asked why she thought the men were exchanging money, the woman said: “I’d been sold-out.”

And she said: “I think I was there for that reason – to be prostituted out.”

The trial continues.
