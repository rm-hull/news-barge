A local distillery has announced plans for an exclusive virtual whisky tasting.

Spirit of Yorkshire Distillery will host the online Merry Sherry Virtual Tasting on December 18, featuring all seven editions of its Filey Bay Sherry Cask Reserve whisky.

Joe Clark, whisky director at Spirit of Yorkshire, said: "Our lockdown virtual tastings were really successful, and recently people have been asking us to repeat the at-home experience.

"We’re really looking forward to what promises to be a fun and informative evening."

Participants will receive a tasting pack containing seven 5cl miniatures, with tickets priced at £50.

The event will be co-hosted by Mr Clark and brand ambassador Amy Teasdale, who will guide guests through each dram and share the story behind the series.

This year marks the distillery’s 10th anniversary, and it was named one of the world’s top 50 spirits producers by the International Wine and Spirit Competition (IWSC) in 2025.
