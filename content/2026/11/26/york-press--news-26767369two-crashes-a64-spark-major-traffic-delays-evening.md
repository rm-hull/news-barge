A MAIN road in North Yorkshire is partially closed following a crash.

The A64 is shut westbound with traffic moving very slowly eastbound on the between Malton Lane and Steelmoor Lane.

Delays of 13 minutes are currently being reported with traffic moving at around 10 miles an hour.

There is also a crash on the further west near the Askham Road roundabout on the outskirts of York.

Severe delays of 25 minutes are being reported near the A1079 Hull Road (Grimston Bar Interchange) and A1237 (Copmanthorpe roundabout), with average speeds ten mph.
