Politics students at Ripon Grammar School put questions to Sir Julian Smith MP, who also met Headmaster Jonathan Webb to talk about funding and staffing costs.

Politics students at Ripon Grammar School have put their questions directly to Sir Julian Smith, the MP for Skipton and Ripon, during a visit to the school.

The students asked about current affairs, Parliament, the role of MPs and issues affecting North Yorkshire. Sir Julian also met the Headmaster, Jonathan Webb, to hear about the school’s successes and the pressures it faces, including funding, staffing costs and its long-term plans.

## Overview

* **Who:** Sir Julian Smith, Conservative MP for Skipton and Ripon, and politics students at Ripon Grammar School.
* **What:** a question and answer session, followed by a meeting with Headmaster Jonathan Webb.
* **Topics raised by students:** current affairs, Parliament, the role of MPs and North Yorkshire issues.
* **Topics with the Headmaster:** the school’s successes, funding pressures, staffing costs and the school’s long-term plans.

## What Sir Julian said

“It was fantastic to be back at Ripon Grammar School to take part in such an engaging discussion with students,” he said. “Their questions were thoughtful, well-informed and covered a wide range of important issues.

“I was also grateful for the opportunity to meet with Jonathan Webb to discuss the challenges facing schools, particularly around funding and rising costs, as well as the excellent work taking place at Ripon Grammar School to support pupils’ success.

“I look forward to following the school’s continued success over the coming period.”
