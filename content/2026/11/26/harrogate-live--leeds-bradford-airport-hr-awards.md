Leeds Bradford Airport's HR team won two Yorkshire Excellence in HR Awards, for talent acquisition and employer brand and for organisational change.

The HR team at Leeds Bradford Airport has won two categories at the Yorkshire Excellence in HR Awards, which recognise excellence, innovation and best practice in human resources across the county.

The team was named winner of Best Talent Acquisition & Employer Brand Initiative and of Excellence in Transformational Change through Organisational Development.

## Overview

* **Who:** the HR team at Leeds Bradford Airport (LBA).
* **What:** two wins at the Yorkshire Excellence in HR Awards.
* **Categories:** Best Talent Acquisition & Employer Brand Initiative; Excellence in Transformational Change through Organisational Development.
* **Why it matters:** the airport says the awards recognise HR’s part in its recruitment, growth and organisational change.

![Leeds Bradford Airport's HR team holding their Best Talent Acquisition and Employer Brand Initiative award in front of the awards sponsor board](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/leeds-bradford-airport-hr-awards-2.webp)

## What the airport says the awards recognise

According to the airport, the awards reflect the team’s work on talent acquisition, organisational development and business transformation, and the part HR has played in supporting LBA’s growth, recruitment ambitions and organisational change.

The airport says the double win reflects the team’s success in attracting and developing staff, strengthening the employer brand and supporting colleagues through “a period of significant change and development across the business”. It adds that HR worked closely with teams across the airport on recruitment, retention, development and growth.

LBA employs hundreds of people across operational, commercial and corporate roles, the airport says.

![Leeds Bradford Airport's HR team holding the Excellence in Transformational Change through Organisational Development award](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/leeds-bradford-airport-hr-awards-3.webp)

## ‘Congratulations team LBA’

Lindsay Beresford, HR Director at Leeds Bradford Airport, said: “I am so proud of this wonderful team who are so talented and passionate about what they do. Transformation and growth are exciting but challenging, and this team is more than up for it.

“These awards are recognition of the incredible commitment, professionalism and care our HR colleagues bring to work every day. They have played a vital role in supporting our people, attracting fantastic new talent and helping shape the future of Leeds Bradford Airport. Congratulations team LBA, you really deserve this.”
