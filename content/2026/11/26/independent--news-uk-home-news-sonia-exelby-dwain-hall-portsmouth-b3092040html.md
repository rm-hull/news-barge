The family of a British musician who was lured to Florida to be killed has said they are 'extremely relieved' to have got 'justice' after her murderer was sentenced to life in prison.

Sonia Exelby's family have returned back in the UK after a week-long trial that found Dwain Hall, 54, guilty of kidnapping and first-degree murder.

Members of the 32-year-old's family have now spoken out, saying they were 'truly blown away with the support' they had received during Hall's trial.

The trial found that Ms Exelby had been groomed on a fetish website by 'sick' predator Hall and lured to the US where he stabbed her four times to death.

Ms Exelby, who had a history of self-harm and had previously attempted suicide, was reported missing from her home on October 12 last year, by which time police established she had left the country.

![Dwain Hall of Ocala, who has been sentenced to life in prison in the US for the kidnap and first-degree murder of 32-year-old Sonia Exelby from Portsmouth](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/09/01M7X0YV945RD9CXHSNNHFS4QQ.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Her body was found by US investigators in Marion County, Florida, on October 17 in a shallow grave after Hall stabbed her to death to act out his 'sadistic pleasure'.

Hall, who was seen smiling and laughing with his defence team during the trial, was traced by police through the attempted use of the victim’s credit card by the roadside assistance company he owned.

A week-long trial at the Marion County Judicial Centre, Florida, concluded on Friday when jurors returned unanimous guilty verdicts after less than an hour of deliberation.

Hall was given two consecutive life sentences by a Marion County Florida judge.

Ms Exelby's 'terrified' siblings attended the trial after a GoFundMe to help towards their flights and travel costs fetched around £10,000.

Gemma Isaj was among the family witnesses - alongside sister Sara Exelby and Ms Exelby's fiancé Stevie Hunt - called during the trial.

The sisters were emotional as they gave evidence during the harrowing trial but said they can at least take comfort that warped Hall will spend the rest of his life in jail.

Ms Isaj has now said that the siblings arrived back in the UK on Tuesday and are 'exhausted' after their ordeal but pleased justice was served on Hall.

She said: “We are all feeling extremely overwhelmed by what has happened and trying to take a bit of time to process.

“We are extremely relieved to have got justice for Sonia and are so overwhelmingly grateful for the kindness and warmth we were met with by everyone in Florida. We just need time, we are all physically, mentally and emotionally exhausted.”

Ms Exelby's brother Luke Wilton said: “Reliving such an intense year in such a short time frame was so incredibly overwhelming, but being (at the court) with our loved ones felt so essential and wouldn't have been possible without the support that all you guys have provided.

“We're so grateful for every donation and positive inspirational comment we've received. The Floridian state took our breath away with the effort and personalism that each person on the case displayed.

“Not to mention the continued support from our countrymen, our English authorities that have been with us the whole way. We've been truly blown away with the support from all corners and simply cannot ask for more.

“The support we've received recently has entered our hearts and given us some much needed support in this terrifying time. So once again, thank you all, for any and everything. We're so deeply grateful. Much love.”
