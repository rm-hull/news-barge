**The college prepares to welcome students in January to its newly completed campus, designed to enhance skills development and career readiness.**

Harrogate College has officially taken possession of its new £22 million campus located at Hornbeam Park. The handover marks the completion of a major construction project intended to provide an improved learning environment for students across the region.

The college’s principal, Danny Wild, described the occasion as an important milestone for both the institution and the wider educational landscape in North Yorkshire.

> **Danny Wild, Principal of Harrogate College, said:**
>
> This is an important milestone for Harrogate College, marking the start of an exciting chapter for the college and for education and skills in the region.
>
> Our new facilities will provide students with an outstanding environment in which to learn, develop their skills and prepare for successful careers, while enabling us to strengthen our relationships with local employers and deliver industry-relevant training to meet the needs of the future workforce.
>
> We’re now preparing the campus ahead of welcoming students in January and look forward to seeing them settle into their surroundings and make the most of everything the learning environment has to offer.

This is an important milestone for Harrogate College, marking the start of an exciting chapter for the college and for education and skills in the region.

He emphasised that the modern facilities are designed to offer students an outstanding space to learn, develop skills, and prepare for successful careers. Mr Wild added that the new campus would also enhance the college’s ability to work closely with local employers and deliver training that meets the future workforce’s demands.

The college is currently making final preparations ahead of welcoming students in January. Mr Wild expressed anticipation for students to settle into their new surroundings and fully utilise the benefits offered by the upgraded campus.

Harrogate College forms part of the Luminate Education Group, which includes several other institutions such as Leeds City College and Leeds Conservatoire. The handover event was attended by key college representatives, members of the college’s Board of Governors, and executives from Luminate Education Group, alongside representatives from construction partner Caddick Construction.

The new campus at Hornbeam Park represents a significant investment in the region’s education infrastructure, aiming to better equip students with the skills needed to thrive in evolving industries within North Yorkshire and beyond.
