A major explosion rocked an oil facility in eastern Bangkok on Thursday, sending flames and thick black smoke high above the Thai capital.

The blast occurred at the Bangchak oil depot, operated by Thai energy company Bangchak Corporation.

Bang Na district authorities urged people in the surrounding area to evacuate immediately as a precaution.

The Bangkok city authority later said the fire had been extinguished less than an hour after the explosion, which was reported at around 3pm local time.

No casualties were immediately reported.

The cause of the explosion and the extent of any damage remain unclear.
