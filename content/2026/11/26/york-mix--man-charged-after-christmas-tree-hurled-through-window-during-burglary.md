A man has been arrested after allegedly throwing a Christmas tree through a window.

In the early hours yesterday (Wednesday), police were called to a disturbance on Ashfield Terrace in Harrogate.

It was reported that a man had thrown a Christmas tree through the window of a house, smashing the glass.

#### Ashfield Terrace, Harrogate

The man then allegedly entered the address, assaulted one of the occupants and threatened others who were inside at the time.

He is also accused of stealing a jacket, wallet, handbag, mobile phone, keys and a quantity of cash. Before leaving, he went upstairs and kicked a bedroom door off its hinges, causing further damage.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Officers quickly attended the scene and, after establishing the circumstances, entered the suspect’s property and arrested the 36-year-old man on suspicion of robbery, assault occasioning actual bodily harm and criminal damage.

“All the stolen items were subsequently recovered by officers and returned to the victims.”

The suspect appeared before York Magistrates’ Court this morning.

He was remanded in custody until his next appearance at York Crown Court on Monday 21 December.
