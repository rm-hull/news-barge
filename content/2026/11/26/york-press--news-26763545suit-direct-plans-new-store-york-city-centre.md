A recognised UK high street brand could be set to occupy a former opticians and hearing store in York city centre.

BMB Clothing Limited, trading as Suit Direct, has applied to City of York Council for consent to display advertising and display signs on the front of 35 Coney Street.

Suit Direct, which has more than 30 stores across the UK high street and outlet centres, as well as a worldwide shipping website, specialises in modern formalwear for all occasions.




The stores stock a variety of brands, including Ted Baker, Marc Darcy, Limehaus, Alexandre of England, and Racing Green, according to its website.

As reported by The Press at the time, European eyewear and audiology brand Acuitis closed its doors in January citing that "the current economic climate has not been favourable" in a notice to customers displayed in the window.

The opticians and hearing centre shut after nearly three and a half years in the city centre.

City of York Council is expected to make a decision on the application by Friday, January 8.
