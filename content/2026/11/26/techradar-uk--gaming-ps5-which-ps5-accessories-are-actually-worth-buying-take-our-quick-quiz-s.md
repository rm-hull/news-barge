There are often plenty of Black Friday deals on gaming kit, especially for PS5 accessories, but none of that matters at all if you don't know what to buy. Don't fret: that's where we come in.

When you browse online for PS5 accessories, there are usually two kinds of tech that come up: controllers and headsets. There are hundreds of both of these, and so it's hard to decide what to buy.

And what about the world beyond controllers or headsets? These aren't given as much love, even from Sony's own website or big retailers, but there are still a few useful pieces of kit that certain users might find handy. I'm talking things like storage expansions, racing wheels and on-the-go options.

If a lightbulb has just gone off above your head, and you've found some inspiration: cool it, you haven't even gotten to the quiz yet! To help you decide which direction to take your research, I've prepared a short quiz that should point you towards a certain item. Each of these picks comes from a TechRadar buying guide, rated either as the top pick or our favorite budget alternative (with some exceptions, for products in guides that aren't structured in our typical formatting).

Five simple questions is all that stands between you, and a renewed Black Friday hunt:
