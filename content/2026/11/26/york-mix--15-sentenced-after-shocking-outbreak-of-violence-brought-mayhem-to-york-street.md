Fifteen men have been sentenced after a mass brawl saw pub staff attacked and doors smashed on a York street packed with horrified families.

The mayhem was sparked around the Slug And Lettuce on Ouse Bridge around 7.45pm on Saturday 26 July 2025.

Huge numbers of police were sent to the incident, which happened after the York Races Music Showcase.

All the 15 men sentenced at Leeds Crown Court yesterday (Wednesday) were from the South Yorkshire area.

Their sentences included suspended jail terms, fines and bans from York city centre for five years. You can see all of them below.

The violence began when two members of the group, Corey Hall and Thomas Salt, attacked people in the Slug and Lettuce bar without provocation.

Door staff were escorting Hall from the bar when they were set upon by about 12 members of Hall’s group who followed them outside.

The gang targeted “multiple innocent members of the public,” police said, along with staff at the venue.

## Multiple injuries

Door staff managed to get back inside and shut the doors to protect themselves and customers from the violence. Multiple members of the team suffered injuries during the disorder, including facial injuries, cuts, bruises and head injuries.

But the gang forced their way back inside, pushing the door the wrong way and smashing it off its hinges.

Other members of the group who had stayed in the Slug and Lettuce then joined in the violence, assaulting members of the public and staff.

Staff from other venues went to assist their colleagues.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/police-incident-ouse-bridge.webp)

After police arrived, they were able to get the group back outside of the venue.

Violence then broke out on Low Ousegate.

As Hall tried to leave the scene, he was challenged by door staff and taken to the floor. Kevin Hubbuck, Matthew Gee, Luke Gee and Corey Hall were arrested for their violent response.

Following his arrest, Hubbuck was found to have been seriously injured and was taken to hospital. His injuries transpired to be a bruised and swollen eye, broken ribs and punctured lungs.

The other defendants were arrested after being identified by CCTV appeals following the incident.

## ‘People distressed and fearful’

**Assistant Chief Constable Ben Moseley from North Yorkshire Police said:** “Firstly, I would like to thank everyone who contacted us following the release of our CCTV appeals.

“The information provided by members of the public was instrumental in helping us identify a number of those responsible.

“This shocking outbreak of violence took place on a key street in York city centre, in full view of members of the public, including children and families.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/low-ousegate-york-disturbance-26-july-2025.webp)

“The impact on those who witnessed it was clear, with people left distressed and fearful as the disorder unfolded around them.

“Some individuals were forced to put themselves at risk to protect others from harm, while parents had to quickly move their children away from the violence.

“No one should have to experience scenes like this while enjoying a day or night out in our city.

“York remains a safe city, and today’s sentencing sends a clear message that this type of violence has no place on our streets.

“We will continue to work tirelessly to identify offenders, bring them before the courts, and ensure those who threaten the safety of our communities are held accountable.”
