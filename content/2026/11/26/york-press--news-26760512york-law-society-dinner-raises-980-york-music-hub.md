The Yorkshire Law Society Annual Dinner took place at Merchant Taylors' Hall.

Guests gathered for the annual event on 20 November 2026, which brought together professionals from various sectors.

The evening began with a drinks reception, providing an opportunity for attendees to socialise and have professional photographs taken, which were distributed to guests free of charge.

Among the distinguished guests were members of York's Civic Party, including the Lord Mayor and Sheriff of York, along with the President-Elect of the York Medical Society.

His Honour Judge Anthony Dunne represented the judiciary.

Sally Hobbs CBE, Master of the Company of Merchant Taylors, was the guest speaker.

The dinner, prepared by Bradshaws of York, featured a four-course meal concluded with cheese and port.

Yorkshire Law Society President Duncan Morter acknowledged the contributors to the event, highlighting the importance of personal relationships in the profession.

Sally Hobbs CBE delivered an address linking York's medieval guilds' history with the modern professional world.

The evening also supported the President's charity, York Music Hub, raising £980 through the raffle.

Sponsors Barclays, Hazlewoods Accountants, and JM Finn Wealth Experts were acknowledged for their support.

The event was seen as a testament to the importance of such gatherings.
