North Korean leader Kim Jong Un has called for expanding the country's nuclear arsenal after inspecting facilities involved in producing nuclear materials and developing weapons, state media reported on Thursday.

Kim visited the sites on Wednesday, including two nuclear material production factories and a nuclear weapons research institute. Their locations have not been disclosed.

During the visit, Kim called for the wider use of tactical nuclear weapons across different military operations, describing the expansion of the country's nuclear capabilities as an important and urgent task.

North Korea's state news agency KCNA said the country was systematically increasing its capacity to produce nuclear materials to strengthen its nuclear arsenal.

The visit comes amid growing international concern about Pyongyang's nuclear programme.
