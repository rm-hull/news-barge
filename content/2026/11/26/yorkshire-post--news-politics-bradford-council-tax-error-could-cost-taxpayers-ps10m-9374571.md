# MP calls for special audit after Bradford council tax error could cost taxpayers £10m

## A Tory MP has called for external auditors to examine an error by Bradford Council, which could cost the taxpayer more than £10m.

An internal report has revealed that council tax premiums imposed on owners of long empty properties between 2019 and 2024 were illegal, and need to be paid back.

Bradford Council’s current Reform leader Stephen Place has called for Labour councillor Imran Khan, the previous deputy leader, to resign over his part in the matter.

But the Labour group says the policy was introduced “in good faith” – and it was up to officers at the time to make sure it was implemented properly.

Keighley and Ilkley MP Robbie Moore has said the local authority should call in independent experts to assess the books.

He said: “This shocking story will only erode the already low trust my constituents have in Bradford Council.

“I believe the only way to build that trust back up and improve transparency is if external auditors are called in and carry out a forensic, independent financial investigation of how the council are making decisions and handling public funds.”

A Bradford Council spokesperson said: "While it is disappointing to discover a historic issue of this nature, it is important that we are open and transparent about it, take responsibility and put matters right.

"As soon as the issue was identified, we acted quickly to review the position and develop a clear and fair approach to correcting it.

"The vast majority of council tax payers across the district are completely unaffected. Those who are affected will be contacted directly by the council tax team over the coming weeks and do not need to contact the council.”

As a public body, the council’s budget is scrutinised each year by external auditors.
