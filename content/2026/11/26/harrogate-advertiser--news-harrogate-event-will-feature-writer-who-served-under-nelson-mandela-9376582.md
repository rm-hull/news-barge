A politician turned writer who once served under Nelson Mandela is to visit Harrogate for an author's event which will showcase an acclaimed work of investigative reporting.

Taking place on Friday, December 4 at 7pm at the Friends Meeting House, Queen Parade in Harrogate, the event will see Andrew Feinstein discussing his latest book Making a Killing which traces the deals, shadowy or brazen, connecting London, Washington, and European capitals to the violence and suffering in Yemen and Gaza.

This former ANC MP who served under South African President Nelson Mandela, will be interviewed by Dr. Suesanne Samara, with an opportunity for the audience to ask questions afterwards.

The book, which is co-written by Ruth Rohde and Jack Cinamon and published by Verso Books , argues that the profits of war are corrupting putatively democratic governments in a nightmare of spiralling violence, as money feeds the wars that justify the arms sales.

Doors open at 6.30pm and the event will run from 7pm to 9pm.

Currently Executive Director of the non-profit Shadow World Investigations, Andrew Feinstein's previous book, The Shadow World: Inside the Global Arms Trade was made into an award-winning documentary.

In 2024, he stood as an independent candidate against Keir Starmer to highlight the government's "complicity in the genocide in Gaza".

The event will include a by a book signing by the author.
