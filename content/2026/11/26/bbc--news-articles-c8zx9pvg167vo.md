**The Princess of Wales has been in the Scottish Borders to see the work of an arts organisation that is helping to support projects across the UK.**

It was her first engagement since two men were charged with trying to steal cufflinks and a clock from the royal couple's country home in Norfolk.

The Hugo Burge Foundation (HBF) which has three bases in the region - one of them on the Marchmont Estate near Duns - carries the name of the internet entrepreneur and philanthropist who died in 2023 aged 51.

The princess - who is known as the Duchess of Rothesay while in Scotland - got a glimpse of the work being carried out by the group.

She was welcomed by Lucy Brown, HBF chief executive, and learned more about its work supporting creativity and craftsmanship.

She also spent time talking to head gardener Toby Loveday and creative director James Fox during her time in the Borders.

The HBF offers grants, prizes, residencies and studio spaces to artists and craftspeople and provides funding to arts organisations and community groups.

It also funds major public art projects with the goal of "permanently enriching Britain's cultural landscape".

Established in 2022, last year it distributed more than £400,000 of grant funding to individuals, communities, organisations and projects.

![The Princess of Wales in the middle of a group of three other people walking across a lawn in a very well-tended garden](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/fabf/live/c4c40230-e9ad-11f1-859a-37130cd3a1d7.jpg)

The princess walked around the gardens on a cold but dry day

Across its three locations, it offers a range of workspaces including a community pottery studio, a state-of-the-art silversmithing workshop, a printmaking studio, and a traditional chairmaking workshop.

It also safeguards a nationally important collection of modern British art.

On its main campus it offers funded residencies throughout the year and cares for a walled garden.

In 2026, it opened Rhymer's Works, a new creative hub in the town of Earlston that offers subsidised studios and community spaces.
