A safari park in Wiltshire is celebrating the birth of its first baby hippopotamus in more than four decades.

The calf, whose gender is not yet known, was born to a mother named Matylda at Longleat last week, as part of the European breeding programme.

Matylda made the journey to the safari park from the Czech Republic in summer 2025, travelling alongside her sister Manon.

They were joined by Hodor, who is the father to the newborn calf, and his mother Lola, who came from Whipsnade Zoo near Dunstable in Bedfordshire.

Darren Beasley, head of animal operations at Longleat, said keepers were “over the moon” to have hippos back at the safari park.

“Hippos are under threat and to be able to contribute to the conservation breeding programme is a massive achievement,” he added.

![The baby hippo, whose gender is not yet known, was born to at Longleat in Wiltshire last week](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/12/01M8BVF6F2S5JEWKRGKCP6CJQH.jpg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Hippos carry their young for eight months before giving birth.

They give birth in and out of water, distancing themselves from the others in their group, which is known as a herd or pod.

Keepers noticed Matylda behaving this way and separated her from the other hippos.

The birth itself took a few hours.

“She is a new mum but is doing well. We will be monitoring them all closely as the first few weeks are a critical time for any newborn animal,” Mr Beasley said.

“Our focus is now on enabling mum and calf to bond and grow over the winter.

![Hippos carry their young for eight months before giving birth](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/12/01M8BVFA9YHAAWTSSY607WD5HF.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Guests may see them as the calf is following Matylda around outside the House and, of course, our other hippos are also in and out of the paddocks and Half Mile Pond.”

He added that the calf was a “special Christmas gift” and came as Longleat celebrated 60 years since opening as the first drive-through safari outside of Africa.

An adult hippo can weigh up to 4.5 tonnes and eat around 40kg of grass and plants each day.

On land, hippos can reach speeds of about 19mph, but only over short distances.

Longleat was previously home to two hippos called Sonia and Spot, and both had calves in the early 1980s.

Sonia arrived at the park in 1976 and died in April 2023, aged almost 50.

Spot died in early 2025, just before her 50th birthday.
