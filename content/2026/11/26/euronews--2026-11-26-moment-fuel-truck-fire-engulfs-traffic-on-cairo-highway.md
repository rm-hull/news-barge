At least 31 people have been killed and 21 injured after a crash involving a fuel tanker sparked a massive fire on a major highway surrounding Cairo on Wednesday night, Egyptian authorities said.

The fire broke out on the Ring Road in Waraq, a densely populated neighbourhood in Giza, part of the Greater Cairo area. Huge flames rose into the night sky as vehicles and people passed the burning area.

According to the Health Ministry, more than two dozen cars and motorcycles were destroyed in the blaze.

The incident is the latest deadly road accident in Egypt, where thousands of people lose their lives in traffic crashes every year.
