Staff at Harrogate's Poundland face an uncertain Christmas after the retailer went into administration yesterday (Wednesday).

Katy Heffron, who manages the store in the Victoria Shopping Centre, said her team had already faced weeks of uncertainty and now had to consider the possibility of losing their jobs so close to Christmas.

She said:

> “As you can imagine, this is a stressful and unsettling time for our staff, who now face the possibility of losing their jobs so close to Christmas.
>
> “Whilst we try to carry on and continue to hope for a positive outcome, we kindly ask our customers to be respectful and thoughtful when making comments or asking questions in-store or online.”

Poundland entered administration on Wednesday 25th November, putting around 10,000 jobs at risk across the UK and Ireland.

Administrators from AlixPartners are looking for buyers for all or part of the business.

Katy thanked customers who had checked in on the Harrogate team and praised her colleagues for the way they had carried on working.

She added:

> “The staff have already been going through a difficult few weeks of uncertainty.
>
> "Despite this, they have continued to show up every day and do an amazing job.
>
> "We want to say a massive thank you and well done to the entire team—you have all been amazing!”
