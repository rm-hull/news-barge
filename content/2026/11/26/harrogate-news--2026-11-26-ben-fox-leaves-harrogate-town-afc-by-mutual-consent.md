**Harrogate Town AFC has confirmed that midfielder Ben Fox has departed the club after making 34 appearances since joining in February 2025.**

Harrogate Town AFC announced on Thursday that midfielder Ben Fox has left the club by mutual consent. Fox, who signed for the club in February 2025, made a total of 34 appearances for Harrogate Town during his tenure.

The club expressed their best wishes for Fox in his future endeavours. No further details regarding the terms of his departure or potential next steps have been disclosed at this time.

The news was confirmed by Harrogate Town AFC’s Commercial Manager, Hal Boxhall, in a statement released at 5pm on 26 November 2026.
