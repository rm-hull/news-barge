With the news that UK energy bills are set to rise by an average of 26% in January 2027, you may well be looking for ways to soften the bill and cut down on your electricity costs — and we've picked out some tech that can help.

DIY energy is now proving more popular than ever, with plug-in solar kits now legal in the UK, and a growing number of gadgets and appliances available to help you convert whatever sunshine you get into free energy.

There are also home batteries that you can use to stock up on electricity when costs are cheap, and tap into during peak times.
