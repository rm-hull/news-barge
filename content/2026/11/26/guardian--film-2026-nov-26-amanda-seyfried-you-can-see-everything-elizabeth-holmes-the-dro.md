Amanda Seyfried says that the script and footage of You Can See Everything was kept secret even from her until the last possible moment, and that despite being asked not to reveal her involvement in the project she “told a lot of people”.

Seyfried was speaking on the Jimmy Fallon Show on Tuesday, after the film’s extraordinary commercial success in North America, where it is already the third most successful documentary in box office history. Having already played You Can See Everything’s central figure, Theranos fraudster Elizabeth Holmes, in the dramatised TV series The Dropout, Seyfried was called in by directors Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim to stand in for Holmes after the latter started her prison term in 2023.

Seyfried said that, after being introduced by fellow actor Emma Stone, Fielder “just reached out and said, ‘Hey, I’d love to, like, pick your brain about, you know, your experience playing Elizabeth Holmes in The Dropout … We talked for like an hour on FaceTime about Elizabeth Holmes, and he told me that he had lived with her for a month before she went to prison.”

She added: “And then he said, ‘Would you be comfortable watching the footage if we came to your house and recorded you for the documentary? And I was like, ‘Yeah, that’s fine.’ A couple weeks later, I was watching the footage with four cameras in my apartment. And I was like, ‘How did I get here?’”

Saying that she “just jumped in kind of last minute”, Seyfried said that while she had access to all the research footage she had accrued for The Dropout, the script and material shot for You Can See Everything was a different proposition. “I couldn’t watch the footage until I would get to set, and I didn’t know what the script would be until I got to set. Or, you know, it’d be like five-factor authentication [for an] app where I could maybe read it the night before.”

Fallon asked Seyfried about a specific sequence in You Can See Everything where she had dinner with Fielder and Holmes’ partner Billy Evans, which she said caused her to question herself. “It was the Twilight Zone because I was in this man’s house with her children. And I was like, ‘Did I get too close?’ How did I get into this house with these reptiles and these children, and Nathan and Lance and all the cameras? … it was meta at a point.”

Joking that “I’m the most indiscreet person I’ve ever met in my life,” Seyfried said that she found it difficult keeping her involvement in the film secret for over a year. “I told a lot of people! But I guess I could trust them all. One person tweeted something and Nathan was like, ‘Hey. What is this?’ And I was like, ‘I don’t know!’”

She added: “I guess I was a perfect replacement for the purpose of trying to understand [Holmes] more.”
