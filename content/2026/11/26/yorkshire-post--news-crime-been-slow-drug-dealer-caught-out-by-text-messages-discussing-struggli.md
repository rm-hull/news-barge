# ‘Been slow’ - Drug dealer caught out by text messages discussing struggling sales

## A drug dealer who was caught discussing slow sales on a mobile phone has been jailed for almost four years.

Lee Poole was arrested by officers from Cleveland Police on August 16 and found with cocaine and crack cocaine. A search of his backpack also uncovered two axes and a mobile phone.

On the device, the 41-year-old was updating others with how his drug dealing wasn’t going so well, as well as storing a list of people who owed him money.

Poole, of Badmington Close in Darlington, sent one text message which said: “I’m on graft lad just selling stone…been slow today’.”

He was charged and remanded to Teesside Magistrates Court, and was later released on bail until an appearance at Teesside Crown Court, to which he failed to attend.

A warrant was issued for an arrest and he was located the same day.

![Lee Poole, of Badmington Close in Darlington, had his criminality uncovered after his phone was seized by police.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/25/10/16/Poole.png?trim=76,0,143,0&width=640&quality=65)


On November 12, he appeared at the court where he was sentenced to 45 months behind bars after admitting two counts of possession with intent to supply crack cocaine and cocaine and two counts of possession of an axe.

A statement from Humberside Police said: "We are committed to targeting those who cause misery dealing drugs in our communities and disrupting their activity that will hopefully lead to them being put behind bars.

“I encourage anyone with information on drug supply in their neighbourhood to come forward so we can act.
