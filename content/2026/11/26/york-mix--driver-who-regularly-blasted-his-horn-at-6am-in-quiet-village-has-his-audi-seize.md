Residents of one of North Yorkshire’s most picturesque villages have been plagued by an anti-social driver.

He would drive through Hutton-le-Hole near Pickering sounding his horn at 6am.

Residents got together and reported the behaviour to police.

#### Hutton-le-Hole

Officers tracked down the details for the vehicle and attempted to contact the registered keeper, including by phone and letter.  
Hearing nothing back, neighbourhood police called in the roads policing team.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Unfortunately for the driver, asked colleagues from the Roads Policing Team to assist whilst on patrol.

“Witnessing the conduct of the driver yesterday morning (Wednesday), he was promptly stopped by officers and reported for driving without due care and attention.

“In relation to driving in a manner which was likely to cause alarm, distress and annoyance, the white Audi was seized under section 59 of the Road Traffic Act.”
