**North Yorkshire Police seek public assistance in locating 34-year-old Shaun Clarke, wanted for theft and fraud by false representation.**

North Yorkshire Police have issued an appeal to the public for help in locating Shaun Clarke, a 34-year-old man wanted for theft and fraud by false representation. Clarke, who was born on 14 August 1992, is believed to be in the Harrogate or Keighley areas.

Extensive enquiries to find Clarke have been ongoing. The police are now asking anyone who has seen him, or who has information about his current whereabouts, to come forward.

Members of the public with information can contact North Yorkshire Police by calling 101. For those wishing to remain anonymous, information can also be passed to the independent charity Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or via their website.

In the event of an immediate sighting of Clarke, the police urge people to dial 999 straight away. It is important to quote the reference number 12260190702 when providing any information to assist the authorities.

This appeal forms part of the police’s ongoing efforts to ensure community safety in Harrogate and surrounding areas.
