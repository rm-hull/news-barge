**A sports reporter defended by Jude Bellingham when another journalist tried to interrupt her interview says it has "added a whole new layer to the love people have for Jude".**

BBC reporter Elizabeth Conway had asked the Real Madrid midfielder to talk her through a second-half goal that helped the team towards a 5-1 win over PSV Eindhoven on Tuesday.

When the second reporter, who was male, began speaking over her follow-up question, Bellingham told him: "Sorry, she's got one more".

The clip of the moment - which has since gone viral - has prompted an outpouring of support online for the Stourbridge 23-year-old, with people praising him as "respectful" and "well mannered".

Elizabeth Conway reports on Real Madrid for BBC Sport

Conway, from Solihull, had been standing in a "mixed zone" at the Bernabeu following the Champions League game, where reporters wait by a barrier to interview players.

"It is utter carnage," she told BBC Radio WM.

"Obviously as an English reporter at Real Madrid, getting Jude is exactly what we want, it's gold dust.

"You've got elbows you've got microphones, you've got cameras, you've got massive tall men reaching over you.

"I'm one of very, very, few female reporters and one of very, very, few English reporters there, so trying to make a stand for yourself and get in a good position is so important."

She said being interrupted was par for the course, adding the environment could be "intimidating", but that "it was so nice" when the player stepped in.

"It's shown him yet again being respectful, polite, and really respecting me as a journalist," she added.

Bellingham's actions led to thousands of online comments on the BBC's website and social media pages.

"A role model for young men," said one.

"In just a few words he shows how to make room for women and make them feel heard."

"Mom and dad did a great job," added another.

Manager Jose Mourinho described the former Blues player as "without any doubt, the best midfielder in the world" following the game, which saw his team maintain their 100% record in the Champions League.
