Work on rebuilding Harrogate College’s new £22 million campus at Hornbeam Park has been completed with the official handover taking place this week.

The milestone marks an exciting new chapter for the college whose original building dates back to the ICI Fibres Estate in the 1950s before the college’s relocation to Hornbeam Park in 1985.

Harrogate College’s new campus, which supported by £20m of Department for Education funding, is furnished with state-of-the-art equipment to meet North Yorkshire’s priority skills needs.

Danny Wild, Principal of Harrogate College, said: “This is an important milestone for Harrogate College, marking the start of an exciting chapter for the college and for education and skills in the region.

“Our new facilities will provide students with an outstanding environment in which to learn, develop their skills and prepare for successful careers, while enabling us to strengthen our relationships with local employers and deliver industry-relevant training to meet the needs of the future workforce.”

The official handover of the £22m new campus representatives from Harrogate College and senior leaders from Luminate Education Group joined by members of the college’s Board of Governors and representatives from construction partner Caddick Construction.

From January, students will be able to access the new facilities which will provide an enhanced learning environment, supporting the development of skills and future career opportunities across the region

Danny Wild said: “We are now preparing the campus ahead of welcoming students in January and look forward to seeing them settle into their surroundings and make the most of everything the learning environment has to offer.”

Harrogate College is a part of Luminate Education Group, which also includes Leeds City College, Leeds Sixth Form College, Pudsey Sixth Form College, Keighley College, University Centre Leeds and Leeds Conservatoire.

The college’s redevelopment was finally given the go-ahead in May 2025 after a failed legal challenge against it by a local company.
