KEY bus routes will continue to operate in York this weekend, despite planned strike action across the city.

First Bus has announced it will run a reduced service on Saturday (November 28) and Sunday (November 29), after workers have voted to walk out over the weekend in a pay dispute.

The industrial action will come after retailers launched Black Friday deals - and will coincide with one of the busiest weekends for York Christmas Market and the start of the the UK Snooker Championships at York Barbican.

Unite said bus services across the city, including vital Park and Ride routes, will be “severely impacted” by the industrial action, which comes as drivers demand pay parity with Yorkshire Coastliner colleagues.

A spokesperson at City of York Council said; “This weekend promises to be a very busy one for York and we know a lot of residents will need to get to work or appointments, visit family, go shopping or simply enjoy our city.

"We'd encourage everyone to plan ahead and ensure you're aware of changes to York's usual bus services. Continuing to catch the bus really will help keep everyone moving and reduce congestion.”

First have said that York's Park and Ride centres will be open until 11pm and will operate its usual weekend timetable, although services will be limited to 8am to 7pm.

Connexions will operate the Number 3 Park and Ride service from Askham Bar and the 7 to the Designer Outlet will be served by Arriva's 415.

The only bus service which will not run at all is the 10/10A/10L between York and Stamford Bridge, however a shuttle service be available for residents in Nether Poppleton to link to Poppleton Bar P&R - which will run its normal route under a reduced timetable.

For Grimston Bar Park and Ride, people should use East Yorkshire services X45, X46 and X47; passengers for Stamford Bridge should use service 747.

York Pullman will provide replacement buses for the Number 4 and university services into York city centre and the train station.

Several services will run on a reduced frequency, including the 1 - which will operate between 9am and 7pm - and services 5, 5A, 6, and 11A which will also run less frequently and will operate from 8am to 7pm.

First Bus advises customers to buy an 'All-York' multi-operator ticket during the industrial action, which is valid on all bus services in the city.

They added that customers should check whether a service is running before they buy tickets as refunds will not be offered, aside from customers with pre-purchased tickets who can get new tickets or refund once details are revealed next week.

Steve Moyle, general manager of First Bus in York, said: "Our message is clear: 'Get to the Park and Ride and we will get you into the city.'"

"Our team is doing all we can to keep our customers moving, but there will inevitably be some disruption and inconvenience.

"We apologise in advance to all our customers and appreciate their patience when travelling with us over the coming weekend."

Full details of the revised services are available on the First Bus website under the 'Industrial action in York' section.
