The remarkable Badapple Theatre Company is hitting the road this Christmas to deliver high quality festive fun to villages around Harrogate and Ripon.

The small but award-winning Green Hammerton-based rural touring theatre group which was founded in 1998, is touring with not one show but two.

Santa’s Christmas Cakes is a new family Christmas comedy for aged five to 95 written by the company’s Harrogate-based founder and artistic director Kate Bramley.

Running from November 28 to December 13, Santa’s Christmas Cakes follows the naughty elf Rascal who misbehaved at Santa‘s Grotto and is now banned from taking the sleigh ride on the longest night to deliver presents.

Instead, she has to stay in Santa‘s kitchen to make the special Christmas cakes for Santa and his helpers to eat when they return.

She asks her woodland friends to help but they only make things worse and with time running out, will Rascal and her friends be able to save the day?

Coming to venues at Marton cum Grafton, Beckwithshaw and Wath near Ripon, the show promises a festive mix of songs, puppets and delightful comic cooking tomfoolery.

Running from December 11 to December 30, Cinderella In Boots is also written by Kate Bramley and sees the title character dreaming of a life where she can make her own way, free from her endless work in the kitchen.

But the magical Puss In Boots has other ideas as he quests for the perfect partner for his reclusive master the Marquis de Salad.

Cinderella In Boots will be performed at venues in Green Hammerton, Bishop Monkton and Hampsthwaite.

For tickets for both shows, call 01423 331304.
