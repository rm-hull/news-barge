A road in Full Sutton will be closed for four days for fibre installation works.

Moor Lane will be shut for four days from December 15, 2026, as work begins on installing ducting and chambers for the new BDUK fibre network.

The East Riding of Yorkshire Council has issued a temporary prohibition of through traffic order, which will come into effect at 8am on December 15, 2026 and will last until 5pm on December 18, 2026.

The closure will stretch 280 metres west from its junction with the Moor Cottage access road.

The works are being carried out on behalf of Quickline Communications, and the council says they are necessary to ensure the safety of the public.

Residents, emergency vehicles, and pedestrians will still be able to access the road.

However, all other vehicles will need to follow an alternative route.

This will take drivers along Moor Lane, Newbridge Lane, Ling Lane, Station Road, Highfield Lane, and Hatkill Lane, covering Catton, Stamford Bridge, Full Sutton, Bishop Wilton, Fangfoss, Pocklington Provincial Ward, and Wolds Weighton Ward.

The order will remain in force for up to 18 months or until the work is finished, whichever comes first.

The order is expected to last only four days, but it allows for flexibility if unforeseen circumstances arise.

More information can be obtained from Streetscene Services, Highways Maintenance, on 01482 393939, quoting reference CB.
