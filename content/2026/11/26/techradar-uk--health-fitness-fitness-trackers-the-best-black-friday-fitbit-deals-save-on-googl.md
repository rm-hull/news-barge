The Black Friday Fitbit deals for 2027 don't have many discounts on this year's trending tracker, the Google Fitbit Air. I have seen, however, some terrific deals on older Fitbits and Google Pixel Watches — which, like Fitbit, uses Google Health and Fitbit architecture.
