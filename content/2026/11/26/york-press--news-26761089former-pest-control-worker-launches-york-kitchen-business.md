A former pest control worker has launched a new business giving old kitchens a fresh lease of life.

Jamie Storey, 30, from Haxby, spent four and a half years dealing with unwanted guests in people’s homes before deciding to pursue his dream of running his own company.

He now runs Kitchen Makeovers in York, offering a less disruptive and more affordable way to transform kitchens by updating doors, drawer fronts, worktops, handles, and fittings.

Mr Storey said: "I've seen some weird and wonderful things in pest control – there were definitely some interesting days.

"I've never been someone who wants to sit behind a desk.

"I love being out in the van and meeting people.

"The difference now is that instead of turning up to deal with a problem, I'm helping people create a space they'll love spending time in."

Mr Storey visits customers to discuss their ideas and help them choose from more than 800 colour and style options.

He stays as the main point of contact throughout the project, which he oversees from start to finish.

Most kitchen makeovers are completed in just two to three days and cost around half the price of a full kitchen refit.

He said: "A kitchen is a big investment and I'd never be comfortable putting pressure on somebody in their own home.

"I'd hate that if it was me.

"I'd much rather have a proper chat, show people what's possible and give them time to decide.

"Being friendly and offering good service goes a long way."

With AI-generated adverts and reviews making it harder to know who to trust online, Mr Storey believes being a familiar local face matters more than ever.

He said: "People make all sorts online nowadays and even with reviews you sometimes question whether they're genuine.

"I'm from here, I'm the person who comes to your home and I'm the person you speak to all the way through.

"I think that means a lot."

Originally from Acaster Malbis, Mr Storey went to school in Tadcaster and played football in Copmanthorpe before moving to Haxby five years ago.

He is a lifelong Leeds United fan, a passion that began at the age of eight when he saw goalkeeper Paul Robinson while out working with his dad.

Now several projects in, he said he is enjoying his new venture and the chance to help people enjoy their homes.

He said: "The best part for me is taking a kitchen that's been in for 20 years and making it look really fantastic.

"Seeing how happy people are to be in that space again, that's what it's all about."

Kitchen Makeovers York covers Haxby, Acomb, Bishopthorpe, and surrounding villages.
