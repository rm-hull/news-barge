A new app from Aktion Mensch is designed to make volunteer opportunities more visible. In the future, it will also show where support is needed in Berlin.

The app called "Engagier dich" (English: "Get involved") now also displays opportunities from the Mitte Volunteer Agency, according to the Mitte District Office.

The app is designed to be accessible and offers an overview of various volunteer opportunities through a mobile search feature. "Many people want to get involved but don’t know where to start," the press release states. With the app, the first step toward volunteering is "literally in your pocket." The app is available for free in various app stores. As a potential future feature, there are plans to notify users locally about new opportunities.
