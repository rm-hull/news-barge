The postponement of the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has put a dampener on the holidays for many Americans hoping to enjoy the 102 year-old tradition on Thursday as usual.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani confirmed the 100th parade would be delayed on Wednesday night, in a bid to keep attendees safe as the Big Apple braces for a major storm.

The parade has been rescheduled to Saturday instead of Thanksgiving Day. However, some fans argued moving the day will make the event feel less special, while visitors who had specifically planned to be in the city Thursday said they will end up missing it altogether.

​“If they’re doing it on Saturday, is it really Thanksgiving? I don’t feel like it’s really Thanksgiving,” a boy who lives near the parade told CBS News. “No, I just feel like it’s another day.

​“I feel like they could have the parade. Maybe they could keep the balloons somewhere else.”

![The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been rescheduled to Saturday due to weather conditions in New York City. This picture shows workers deflating a parade balloon ahead of Saturday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/08/2306915612..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The Grimble family were among the many visitors who had traveled just to see the annual extravaganza, with seven members reuniting in New York City for the occasion.

“We were ready to be head-to-toe in rain gear,” Lindsey Grimble-Adams told MEAWW. “It’s a big deal. It’s the 100th. How could they shut it down?”

​Others highlighted that moving the parade to another day has caused expensive disruption for youth bands who planned to attend or participate.

​Dan Davis, the mayor of Manvel in Texas, responded to Mamdani’s announcement by appealing for help for students from Pearland High School.

​“The @PearlandHighSch is in NYC for the parade and trying to figure out a way to stay until Saturday as they were supposed to leave Friday…..500 students that have worked so hard for this,” he wrote. “Can you help them out!?!?”​

NBC’s decision to air an encore presentation of the 2025 parade Thursday in lieu of the actual event didn’t go down well on socia media either.

“Dear NBC,” wrote SkipperDoodle on X. “No one wants to watch a repeat of a parade. Even if it is Macys. Ever. Signed, Murica”.

![New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani has said that he is 'very disappointed' that he will be unable to attend the rescheduled parade](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/11/2306909950..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​Some critics have even tried to blame Mamdani himself for the postponement, with Abe Greenwald - the editor of conservative publication *Commentary* - claiming that the mayor has “killed” the tradition.

​“Gotta hand it to Mamdani,” Greenwald wrote on X. “The guy killed the New York City Thanksgiving Parade for the first time in 100 years, and everyone is walking around believing that it has to do with rain.

“Out of an abundance of anti-Americanism, Zohran Mamdani has canceled the Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City.”

​Mamdani says he will miss Saturday’s parade due to surgery on his right leg after he tore his Achilles tendon during a soccer match. He told reporters that he is “very disappointed about it.”

Speaking about the parade’s iconic balloons, he quipped: “It’s the only inflation we support here in New York City.”

![The parade was postponed due to a storm expected to bring 60 mph winds to the Big Apple](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/11/2306914431..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Although the event has never been postponed in its history, adverse weather conditions have disrupted the parade in the past. In 1997, a Cat in the Hat balloon collided with a lamppost amid high winds, injuring four people.

The event was cancelled entirely from 1942 until 1944 due to the Second World War.

​Others have made light of the postponement. Anthony Blackwood, a marketing and communications manager at Marvel Comics, used a viral meme depicting actress Sarah Jessica Parker to express his disappointment at the parade’s postponement.

​“My actual reaction hearing the news about the Thanksgiving Day Parade,” Blackwood captioned a video of Parker covering her face with her hand.

​Another social media user responded to the news with a clip from *The Devil Wears Prada* in which Miranda Priestly, the ruthless magazine editor played by Meryl Streep, demands to be flown back to New York from Miami despite a hurricane grounding flights out of the city.
