Cuts to the EU's Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) could threaten farmers' livelihoods, food security and the future of European farming, according to Francie Gorman, president of the Irish Farmers' Association (IFA).

EU leaders meet in Brussels on Thursday to discuss the bloc's long-term budget. Farmers' groups say the current proposals "have not improved from an agricultural point of view".

Gorman also told Euronews that rising production costs and EU regulation remain a "huge issue" for farmers.
