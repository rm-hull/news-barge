**Alistair Broadwith from Bedale has earned selection for the England U18 hockey squad after impressing at national trials, continuing his progression from the U16 level.**

A talented young hockey player from North Yorkshire has been named in the England Under-18 squad after impressing selectors during recent trials. Sixteen-year-old Alistair Broadwith, a lower sixth form student at Ripon Grammar School, secured a place on the national team following two years representing England at Under-16 level.

Originally from Bedale, Alistair has played hockey from the age of six, beginning at Thirsk Hockey Club before progressing through the ranks. Currently a midfielder for Leeds Hockey Club’s men’s first team, he attended two nationwide trial camps over five days where he competed against some of the country’s top young hockey talents.

His selection means that he will now participate in monthly training camps and represent England in international matches and tournaments against leading European hockey nations.

> **Alistair said of his achievement:**
>
> I felt very excited and grateful when I found out.
>
> I love competing, and my strengths as a player are my individual skill and vision on the pitch.
>
> I’m really looking forward to the training and matches, particularly playing against other countries. My ambition is to play for England and Great Britain when I’m older.

Alongside his international duties, he plays regularly for Leeds Hockey Club’s men’s first team, training once a week and completing additional strength and conditioning work independently. Balancing his sporting commitments with academic studies has helped him develop strong time-management skills.

Despite joining Ripon Grammar School only at the start of the academic year, Alistair has found the school supportive and flexible in helping him manage his schedule.

> **Adam Milner, Director of Sport at Ripon Grammar School, praised the young athlete’s dedication:**
>
> Alistair’s selection is a hugely significant achievement and reflects the tremendous commitment and hard work he has put into his hockey, largely outside school.
>
> To secure a place in the England U18 squad after spending two years representing his country at U16 level is a fantastic accomplishment. Credit must also go to his previous school, Cundall Manor, for the part they have played in his development.
>
> We are incredibly proud of him and look forward to following his progress.
>
> Alistair’s journey highlights the opportunities for young sportspeople in North Yorkshire to reach national teams through dedication and strong club and school support. His progress will be followed closely by the local community as he continues to represent England at an international level.
