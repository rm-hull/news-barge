# ‘Appalling’ Kirklees sex offender jailed after admitting offences against boys under 13

## An ‘appalling’ Kirklees sex offender who admitted multiple offences against two boys under 13 has been jailed.

Deacon Taylor was sentenced for engaging in sexual activity in the presence of a child, sexual touching and causing children to engage in sexual activity.

The 31-year-old, of no fixed address, committed 11 of the offences in Kirklees between 2022 and 2026 against two young boys who were both under 13.

Taylor was also sentenced for indecent exposure offences, and four separate sexual offences which took place on a third child victim in South Yorkshire between 2022 and 2024.

Those offences were the attempted rape of a child and three counts of sexual touching.

![Deacon Taylor was jailed for 14 years.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/26/13/45/deacon_taylor.jpeg?trim=126,6,664,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Taylor’s offending emerged in March this year when one of this victims came forward.

He was sentenced at Sheffield Crown Court on Nov 20 and jailed for 14 years for the 15 child sex offences.

DC Hannah Sammon of the Kirklees Child Safeguarding Team, who investigated the Kirklees offences, said: “Deacon Taylor subjected the survivors in this case to appalling sexual abuse and without doubt presents a real danger to children.

“It is through the bravery of one of those survivors coming forward that his awful offending in West Yorkshire began to be revealed, and I want to praise the courage of all victims in this case.

“By speaking with our team and South Yorkshire Police colleagues they have played a huge role in putting this man behind bars.”
