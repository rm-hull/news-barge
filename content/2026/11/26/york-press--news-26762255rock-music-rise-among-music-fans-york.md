## Rock music enjoys resurgence as York fans embrace the genre

Rock music is on the rise once again across Yorkshire and the Humber *(Image: Supplied)*

Rock music is on the rise once again in York, with a new generation of fans and bands fueling its return.

A recent survey shows that 68 per cent of adults in Yorkshire and the Humber are interested in the genre, spanning classic and modern styles.

Grunge band DeadBlondeStars commissioned the poll to gauge public sentiment across the region.

Matt Simons, a member of the band, said: "As one of a new generation of modern rock bands taking root in the UK, we see progress being made by new rock bands from different pockets of the UK, and we have a growing fan base of younger fans backing us here in South Yorkshire.

"There are things to be done to better support a new generation of bands, but from our research I’d regard Yorkshire and Humber as a vibrant new home for rock and roll – and this is certainly something to celebrate."

Classic rock is the most popular subgenre in the region, followed by indie rock, metal and commercial rock-pop.

The research also reveals a resurgence in physical music formats, with 29 per cent of adults now listening to CDs—more than three times the number who prefer vinyl.

DeadBlondeStars will release their new album Devotion on November 27.
