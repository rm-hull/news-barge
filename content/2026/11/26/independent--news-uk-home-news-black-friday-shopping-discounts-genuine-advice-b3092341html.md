Shoppers hunting for a new TV or coffee machine this Black Friday could get a better deal by waiting, according to new research that has revealed the best time of year to buy certain products.

Analysis by Which? found that while vacuum cleaners and tablets are likely to hit their lowest prices during the sales, other big-ticket items are cheaper later in the year.

The consumer champion tracked the lowest monthly average prices across 10 popular product categories at Argos, Currys, John Lewis and Very over two years to see how deals during the four-week Black Friday window (14 November to 12 December) stacked up against the rest of the calendar.

Coffee machines fared the worst for Black Friday bargain hunters, with more than half (55 per cent) cheaper outside the sales period. Researchers found December was the best month to buy overall.

Televisions also proved a poor bet during the November rush, with just over half (51 per cent) found for less at other points in the year.

Those looking to upgrade their set would be better off waiting until spring or summer, with April to July offering the lowest prices as retailers clear stock ahead of new model launches.

Smartwatches and air fryers were more of a mixed picture, with 47 per cent and 41 per cent respectively available for less outside the sales window – meaning shoppers will need to do their research to verify a genuine discount.

However, the study also confirmed the products that are more likely to hit their lowest price in November.

Vacuum cleaners topped the list of best Black Friday buys, with fewer than one in 10 (nine per cent) cheaper at other times of the year.

Tablets followed closely behind, with just 14 per cent found for less outside the sales period, though researchers noted those deal prices are likely to be repeated.

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/26/16/2026-11-24T132357Z_1435879838_RC2CUOA48HGZ_RTRMADP_3_HOLIDAYSHOPPING-RETAIL-BLACK-FRIDAY-BRITAIN.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Shoppers looking for electric toothbrushes and flossers (17 per cent cheaper outside the sales) or hair care appliances (22 per cent) were also likely to find the year’s lowest prices in November.

Reena Sewraz, Which? retail editor, said: “Black Friday can feel like a minefield, with so many deals on offer that it can be difficult to know whether you're really getting the best price or whether it's the right time to buy.

“Our analysis found that Black Friday can be a good time to invest in products such as vacuum cleaners, tablets, electric toothbrushes and hair care appliances. However, if you're looking for a new TV or washing machine, you could get a better deal by waiting until the spring or summer.

“It's always worth tracking prices to make sure you truly are getting a good deal.”

To ensure they are getting a genuine saving, Which? advises shoppers to set up retailer wish lists in advance, sign up for newsletters for extra reductions, and use price-tracking tools before buying.
