## Why we recommend Keeper

Keeper's Personal plan lets you store unlimited passwords and passkeys and synchronize them across all your devices. You can access your encrypted vault through mobile apps, web apps, and browser extensions, with biometric authentication available on compatible mobile devices.

The built-in password generator creates strong, random passwords, while KeeperFill automatically enters your credentials when you visit websites or use apps.

The Family plan extends protection to five users, each with their own private vault. It also includes 10GB of secure file storage and lets family members share records and folders while managing access permissions.

For smaller businesses, Keeper's Business Starter plan provides encrypted individual vaults, shared team folders, and user activity reporting. Every team member also receives a free Family plan.

Optional add-ons include BreachWatch, which alerts you if compromised passwords matching yours appear on the dark web.

In his 4.5-star Keeper review, our expert Mike said it "has industry-leading security with a strict zero-knowledge policy to keep your company’s passwords safe. For managing sensitive information across various teams and roles, Keeper is hard to beat."
