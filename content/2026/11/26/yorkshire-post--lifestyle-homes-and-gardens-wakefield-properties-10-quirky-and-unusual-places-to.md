# Wakefield properties: 10 quirky and unusual places to stay in the Yorkshire city from a converted water tower to a narrowboat

## There are apparently quite a few unique and quirky places to stay across Wakefield if you are looking to come back with stories.

Whether you are in the mood to spend the night in a converted water tower, on an island within the world’s first nature reserve, or even in one of the UK’s most famous ‘haunted’ houses, we have compiled a list for you to peruse.

The Wakefield district is quickly becoming known as the country’s Capital of Sculpture and is also home to some of Yorkshire’s most unusual places to stay.

There are historic landmarks and waterside hideaways to luxury rural retreats and quirky conversions, each unique accommodation provides a memorable foundation for exploring everything the district has to offer.

## **10 quirky places to stay in WakefieldA luxurious converted water tower**

Name: Flockton Water Tower

Location: Bar Lane, Flockton, Wakefield, WF4 4JH.

The converted water tower offers vast 360-degree countryside views and sleeps up to eight guests. This unique retreat features floor-to-ceiling windows, ensuite bedrooms and space to relax and watch the TV. It has been featured on Derelict Rescue on Discovery+ and is close to some of the district’s major attractions, including Yorkshire Sculpture Park.

**A cosy, dog-friendly narrowboat**

Name: Lazy Days Narrow Boat

Location: Stanley Ferry

For a waterside escape with a twist, guests can spend the night aboard the Lazy Days Narrowboat at Stanley Ferry. It is moored along the tranquil Aire and Calder Navigation. It is dog-friendly and sleeps up to four guests while offering a cosy retreat surrounded by scenic canal views and countryside walks.

**A farmhouse countryside escape**

Name: The Farmhouse, Farmer Copleys

Location: Ravensknowle Farm, Pontefract Road, Pontefract, WF7 5AF.

For a countryside escape with plenty of character, book a stay at The Farmhouse at Farmer Copleys. It is set on a working farm and was recently renovated. It sleeps up to eight guests across three spacious bedrooms and features a private hot tub, bar, party room and outdoor entertaining space. It is a dog-friendly accommodation and ideal for family getaways, celebrations or group breaks.

![Outside of the narrowboat.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/25/16/25/NLYP-25-11-2026-Outside-of-the-narrowboat-YORupload.jpg?trim=99,63,146,55&width=640&quality=65)


**A house ‘haunted’ by Europe’s most violent poltergeist**

Name: 30 East Drive

Location: Pontefract

You can spend the night at 30 East Drive, a property famous for its alleged paranormal activity. It is said to be home to the infamous ‘Black Monk of Pontefract’ and has attracted ghost hunters and thrill-seekers from around the world and even inspired the horror film When the Lights Went Out.

**The World’s First Nature Reserve**

Name: Waterton Park Hotel

Location: Walton, Wakefield, WF2 6PW.

You can enjoy a stay at the hotel where the historic Walton Hall sits on its own island surrounded by a picturesque lake. Set within Waterton Park, widely known as the world’s first nature reserve, the retreat combines Georgian grandeur with luxury spa facilities, outdoor hot tubs and lakeside views. It is also an 18-minute drive from Wakefield city centre for access to a range of attractions.

**A Shepherd’s Hut beside a working farm**

Name: The Lambing Shed

Location: Ryhill Retreat.

For a romantic couple’s countryside retreat, The Lambing Shed, a bespoke shepherd’s hut nestled beside a working dairy farm in rural West Yorkshire, blends the charm of glamping with modern comforts. It features a cosy wood burner, ensuite shower room, fire pit and pizza oven, making it the idyllic setting for a relaxing getaway for two. The quirky accommodation has beautiful countryside views and thoughtful extras available from the owners. It also offers a memorable stay with a touch of luxury. You can unwind the next day with a walk around Anglers Country Park, or Wintersett Reservoir which is just a short drive away.

**A yoga retreat in a converted barn**

Name: Hall Farm Barns

Location: Badsworth, Pontefract

For a restorative escape, unwind at Hall Farm Barns, where converted farm buildings offer a peaceful stay in the Yorkshire countryside. Guests can combine their overnight break with yoga and pilates sessions in the on-site wellness studio, designed for relaxation and reflection. With opportunities to enjoy meditation, hot stone and Swedish massages, alongside characterful accommodation in lovingly restored former farm buildings, it is the ideal getaway for those looking to slow down and recharge.

**A group getaway in a historic Manor House**

Name: Hillthorpe Manor

Location: Wentbridge

This unique place makes for a memorable group trip and offers the flexibility to book the East Wing, West Wing or the entire manor house. It is set within spacious grounds and guests can enjoy grand living spaces, private gardens, terraces and a hot tub. This makes it ideal for family celebrations, reunions and special occasions. The East Wing provides an elegant five-bedroom retreat, while the larger West Wing features spacious entertaining areas and self-contained flat accommodation. For larger groups, the full manor can accommodate guests across 13 bedrooms, with multiple kitchens, lounges and outdoor spaces, creating a unique countryside escape with room for everyone.

**A picture-perfect cottage**

Name: Went Cottage

Location: Wentbridge, Pontefract.

For a stay steeped in history, Went Cottage offers a charming escape in the picturesque village of Wentbridge. It dates back to the 17th century and it is a restored cottage that combines original character features with modern comforts, including a roll-top bath, cosy living spaces and a private courtyard garden. It sleeps up to four guests and welcomes dogs.

**Glamping amongst the horses**

Name: Ryhill Retreat Glamping Pod

Location: Ryhill Retreat

This stay is perfect for a cosy countryside escape; the accommodation is on a family-run horse stud near Wakefield city centre. It is surrounded by rolling views and walking routes and is a unique retreat for wildlife spotting, stargazing and unwinding in a private jacuzzi hot tub. Guests can also meet the resident animals, making it an ideal rural getaway for couples looking to relax and reconnect with nature.
