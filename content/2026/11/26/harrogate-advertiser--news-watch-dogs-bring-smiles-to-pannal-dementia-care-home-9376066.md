There were smiles all round at a Pannal dementia care home when some four-legged visitors dropped in for a visit.

The dogs from Harrogate-based Waggy’s Wheels called in at Vida Grange, where residents enjoyed spending time with the four-legged visitors, stroking them and sharing stories about their own dogs and their love of animals.”

Waggy’s Wheels founder Sarah Verity said: “We loved meeting and chatting to the residents and seeing the pups so happy and loved.

“A huge thank you to all the wonderful residents and staff at Vida Grange for making us all feel so welcome. Your smiles, stories and kindness made our visit extra special.”

Sarah set up Waggy’s Wheels in March, offering a dog bus service which picks up dogs from their homes and takes them out for supervised group adventures, play and exercise.

The service covers Harrogate, Knaresborough, Wetherby and surrounding areas.

The team said it was looking forward to seeing the residents again.
