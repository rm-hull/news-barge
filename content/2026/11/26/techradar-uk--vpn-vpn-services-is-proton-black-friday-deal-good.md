It's worth making clear that Proton's ongoing reputational crisis isn't about the privacy and security of its products. The company still offers one of the best suites to fully de-Google your digital life, with its flagship products — Proton Mail and Proton VPN — still performing great in TechRadar's independent tests.

So, if you are not bothered by the recent headlines so much, now is certainly the most convenient moment to subscribe to Proton — whether you are looking to sign up for one of the best VPN services, or if a full privacy-first workspace is what you are after.

## Is it worth signing up?

The short answer is, it's up to you and your needs.

If you're looking for one provider that can get you a privacy-first alternative to up to 11 digital tools — Proton even has a privacy-first chatbot right now, Lumo — Proton Unlimited is what you should be getting, assuming you can look past the current controversy around the company and its CEO.

All the apps offer a free tier, but premium is what you want if you're planning to leave your Gmail or Outlook account behind for good. And, frankly, the chance to get all this for $1 for your first month is hard to pass up.

Things may be a bit different if what you need is just one secure and reliable virtual private network (VPN). The Proton VPN Plus Black Friday deal is certainly one not to be sniffed at — you can see why in our full Proton VPN review — but it won't be difficult to find a suitable alternative if you'd rather not spend money on a company that doesn't convince you fully.
