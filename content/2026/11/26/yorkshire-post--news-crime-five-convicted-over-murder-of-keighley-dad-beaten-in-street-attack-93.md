# Five convicted over murder of Keighley dad beaten in brutal street attack

## Four men have been convicted of murdering a Keighley father-of-two who was beaten with metal and wooden bars in a ‘calculated, violent attack.’

Suhail Choudry was left with catastrophic skull and brain injuries after he was beaten in the street by an armed gang in the early hours of February 8 this year.

The 35-year-old died in hospital three days after the brutal attack.

Five men were convicted over Mr Choudry’s death at Sheffield Crown Court today.

Mohammed Aliyaan Khan, 24, Rashid Hussain, 41, Faisal Khan, 26, and Majid Hussain, 42, were all found guilty of murder, violent disorder and possession of an offensive weapon.

Waseem Islam, 40, was found guilty of manslaughter.

![Suhail Choudry was attacked in the early hours of February 8 this year.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/26/17/19/suhail_choudry_2_crop_1.jpeg?trim=146,4,417,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Mr Choudry’s family said the convictions following a two-month trial showed that ‘no one is above the law’.

In a statement, they said: "We welcome these convictions and although nothing will change the fact that our life’s have been changed forever from the loss of Suhail, we hope this serves as a reminder that people’s actions have consequences, and no one is above the law.

![(L-r) Faisal Khan, Majid Hussain, Waseem Islam, Mohammed Aliyaan Khan and Rashid Hussain were all convicted over Mr Choudry's death.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/26/17/51/Screenshot-2026-11-26-at-17-48-48.png?trim=0,422,0,422&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


“As a family we are devastated from the loss of Suhail and the whole ordeal of the investigation, the prolonged trial and hearing the detailed evidence of Suhail’s injuries.

"Having to listen to the planning, the violence and the actions of others has inflicted further trauma on us, that we will never forget.”

A sixth man, Hakeem Gulzar, 28, was found guilty of assisting an offender.

All were remanded into custody to be sentenced at a later date.

Detective Superintendent Matt Holdsworth, from West Yorkshire Police, said: “This was a calculated and violent attack that had been planned on Suhail Choudry.

“These group of men stopped at nothing with their intention to cause significant harm to Suhail.

"This was a senseless loss of life that has had a devastating impact on the victim's family, friends and the wider community.

“Our thoughts remain with the victim's loved ones, and we hope this outcome brings them some comfort as they continue to come to terms with their loss."
