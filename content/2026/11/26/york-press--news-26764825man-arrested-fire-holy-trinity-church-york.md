## Man, 42, arrested after fires cause substantial damage inside historic York church

Holy Trinity Church in Micklegate. *(Image: Newsquest)*

A MAN has been arrested after fires were allegedly started inside a historic York church, causing substantial damage to a listed altarpiece.

The 42-year-old local man was detained in the city centre just over two hours after firefighters were called to Holy Trinity Church in Micklegate.

![Holy Trinity Church on Micklegate, York.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21766692.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Holy Trinity Church, Micklegate, York. *(Image: Nadia Jefferson-Brown)*

Emergency services were called following reports of fires inside Holy Trinity Church in Micklegate at about noon yesterday (Wednesday, November 25).

North Yorkshire Police said enquiries, assisted by council CCTV operators, led to the arrest of the 42-year-old on Blake Street shortly after 2pm.

![Police attended York city centre on Wednesday afternoon where officers arrested a 42-year-old local man on Blake Street.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21763551.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police attended York city centre on Wednesday afternoon where officers arrested a 42-year-old local man on Blake Street. *(Image: Newsquest)*

A spokesperson said the suspect remains in custody for questioning.

"The fires were already out when firefighters attended the scene at 12.12pm," added the spokesperson. "They made sure the building was safe and informed our officers that they believed the fires had been started deliberately.

"Substantial damage has been caused to a large and listed historical altarpiece within the church."

Fire crews from York and Acomb rushed to the scene after smoke was reported coming from the building, and checked the church was safe.

No injuries were reported at the time, but North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service described the cause as "deliberate".

Firefighters used positive pressure ventilation, a thermal imaging camera and knapsack sprayers before the incident was passed over to police.

![Plain-clothed police officers arrested someone in York city centre on Wednesday afternoon.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21763571.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This unmarked police car was seen on Davygate in York shortly before the arrest on Wednesday afternoon. *(Image: Newsquest)*

An eyewitness said an unmarked police car had initially pulled up on Davygate before a police van appeared shortly afterwards on Blake Street.

Holy Trinity Church stands in the heart of Micklegate's historic quarter.

Police are continuing their investigation and are appealing for anyone with information or video footage which could help to come forward.

People can email [Alicia.Hoyle@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alicia.Hoyle@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call North Yorkshire Police on 101 and ask for Alicia Hoyle.

Information can also be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through its online reporting service.
