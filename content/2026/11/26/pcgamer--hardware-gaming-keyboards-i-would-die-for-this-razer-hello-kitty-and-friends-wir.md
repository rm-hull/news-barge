A ridiculously adorable keyboard with an equally amazing matching wrist rest. You can enjoy a great discount on this keyboard as well as the purple and black Kuromi version which is currently 33% off at Walmart.

**Price Check:** Amazon $98.11

Black Friday is almost upon us, and you know what that means: I get to scour the internet for the cutest and best Black Friday deals. I have no prejudice when it comes to cute peripherals, but I do have a preference: Hello Kitty & Friends.

I am self-aware enough to understand that Hello Kitty & Friends has an unearthly grasp on my wallet. It's simple: I see something vaguely Kitty-related, and I get that thing. I have, however, been holding off on the Razer Ornata V3 Hello Kitty & Friends wired Gaming keyboard, because I already have a perfectly functioning keyboard, and it's usually $119. But with this Black Friday deal on Walmart, it's now down to $98.11. $20 in savings may be just enough to change my mind. Although, I could also just be looking for any viable excuse to buy this; see, very self-aware of me.

* **We're curating all the Black Friday PC gaming deals right here**

It's not just Hello Kitty & Friends, though, there's also a Kuromi version of Razer's Ornata V3 wired keyboard. Its colour-scheme is purple and black, it's got an even better deal on Walmart down to $78.88 (was $119), and it's just as cute as the pink version. This is also part of a whole Kuromi Collection that includes a Kraken wireless headset, a DeathAdder mouse and Goliathus mousepad, and the Leviathan V2 soundbar. We actually reviewed the whole set and gave it an impressive 86, with our reviewer Hope Corrigan saying it's "some of Razer's best bang for your buck kit," and also how cute the whole set is.

For the keyboard specifically, our review details how it's satisfying to use "with good actuation and fast responsive keys." Yes, the Hello Kitty design usually puts it around $50 extra over the plain version, but this discount certainly knocks that difference down a peg or two.

If you've been looking for a reason to trade out your keyboard for a pink or purple cute alternative then this is a great deal to take advantage of. Plus, who could say no to Hello Kitty & Friends? Clearly not me.
