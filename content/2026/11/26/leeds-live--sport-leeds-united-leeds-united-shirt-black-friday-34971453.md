**Leeds United’s 26/27 Home Shirt** is designed in classic club colours, with horizontal blue and yellow stripes set against a clean white base. This is said to mark the 400th anniversary of the Leeds town charter and honour ‘the city’s enduring football culture’.

The jersey also has blue and yellow piping on the collar and cuffs and a bold ‘LEEDS’ sign-off on the back neck, while the club crest sits proudly on the left chest. Red Bull sponsor branding returns on the front, along with the adidas logo on the right chest and thick, blue three-stripes across each shoulder.

The **Kitbag Black Friday sale** comes as Leeds prepare to host newly-promoted Coventry City in their next Premier League fixture on Saturday (November 28). Daniel Farke’s side head into the game after stunning Chelsea with 4-1 at Stamford Bridge last week, while Frank Lampard’s men secured three points in their last outing with a 3-2 win against Crystal Palace.

Leeds vs Coventry will not be broadcast in the UK as kick-off is scheduled for 3pm, but last-minute tickets may still be available at **Seat Unique**, which offers official match tickets with VIP and hospitality packages at Elland Road.
