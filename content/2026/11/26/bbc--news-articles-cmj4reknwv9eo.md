![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: “Revealed: six-day streak 'smashes' the global temperature record”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/624/cpsprodpb/7442/live/0f42b770-e91d-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Guardian leads with reports the world has experienced six consecutive days of temperatures 2C warmer than pre-industrial levels, for what it says is the first time. The paper says the "record hot streak" is likely to feed into growing scientific concerns that global heating may be accelerating.

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: “Record energy bills will hit millions in UK after 26% hike”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/75fc/live/22d29c20-e91c-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"Record energy bills will hit millions in UK after 26% hike" says the i Paper, which suggests people in the larger homes could pay £870 more a year from January. The paper says the annual increase, which averages at £443 a year, will "dwarf" the £100 payment the government is set to give 20 million households in January. The government has said councils have a £100m discretionary fund to support vulnerable households which fell outside of the main scheme.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: “MoD prepares veterans for war”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/9082/live/bc29f3c0-e91f-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Ministry of Defence has told Britain's military reservists to prepare to be called up for war, according to the Daily Telegraph. It reports letters have been sent to about 100,000 strategic reservists "for the first time since the Cold War", requesting updated contact details and information regarding reservists' jobs and skills. The paper says the move comes amid "increasing threats from Russia".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: “Intruders climbed up drainpipe”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/0801/live/22dabd00-e91d-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Daily Mirror reveals more details from the alleged burglary at the Prince and Princess of Wales's country home earlier this week. The paper says intruders "climbed up" a drainpipe to break in to Anmer Hall, where it alleges they attempted to steal cufflinks and a clock. Celebrity chef Gordon Ramsay also makes an appearance on the front page, following his announcement as the new host of Top Gear. "Gord speed" reads the headline.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: “2 illegals 'in Wills' dressing room'”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/1416/live/170a9950-e91d-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The two men charged with trespassing at the Sandringham estate have appeared in court, the Sun reports, naming the accused as Mirgen Doku, 34, and Arilen Ndoci, 32. The two have not entered pleas. Elsewhere, it features the phrase "Gordon bennet!" in a reference to Ramsay's new gig.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: “Top Gear's ram raid”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/3326/live/e9352160-e91e-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

"Top Gear's ram raid" declares the Daily Star, alongside a photo of the Stig handing a set of car keys to "petrolhead Gordon Ramsey". Elsewhere, the paper marks Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor's death.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: “BBC faces inquiry over 'antisemitic workplace'”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/0d26/live/1bf8c3c0-e91c-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The BBC will face an inquiry over antisemitism in the workplace, the Times reports. It says the formal investigation will be announced on Thursday, led by Labour peer Lord Mann. The paper adds that the broadcaster has previously denied allegations of institutional antisemitism, and said in a statement on Wednesday it was determined to ensure staff were supported and did not suffer discrimination.

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: “Global teach stocks swing wildly after investors question faith in AI trade”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/9ea6/live/16688260-e91c-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Financial Times considers the state of global tech stocks in their main story, following a "volatile" day of trading on Wednesday. The paper says the mood is "extremely skittish" among investors, with growing fears over whether the AI boom can survive higher interest rates and political backlash after the US midterm elections.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: “Over 70s will have to pass an eye test to keep driving”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/50b8/live/3eda8df0-e91d-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"Over 70s will have to pass an eye test to keep driving" reads the front page of the Daily Mail. The "major road safety shake-up" will be announced within weeks, the paper says, and will require those 70 and older to take an eye test every three years.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: “Cabinet rift over Miliband's election stunt with Brand”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/960c/live/c1f4b800-e91d-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

Labour MP Douglas Alexander has told the Independent that there was a "Cabinet rift" over then-Labour leader Ed Miliband's interview with Russell Brand in 2015. The Scottish politician says he warned the foreign secretary against the election "stunt", but was ignored. Miliband has said he "deeply regrets" the Brand interview.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: “Kemi: You must say sorry for debasing your office”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/25ef/live/115d4f80-e91c-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch is pictured on the front page of the Daily Express, following her demand that Andy Burnham apologise for allowing influencers to film around Downing Street. The prime minister says he will not apologise for engaging with TikTokers and influencers, as young people "are looking to these people for their information."

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: “Possibly the craziest Tube strike EVER”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/895c/live/08e6de20-e91c-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"Possibly the craziest Tube strike EVER" is the headline of the Metro, with Rail, Maritime and Transport (RMT) union members set to walk out for 24 hours from midday on Friday. The paper says "millions" of travellers will face "days of chaos" due to the strike, which it notes will hit amid the Black Friday sales. The RMT said it was striking as it was opposed to proposals surrounding a four-day working week.
