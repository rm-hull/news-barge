A popular pub that had to close suddenly due to a water leak today has reopened – much to the relief of its customers.

The Postern Gate on Piccadilly shut when water came down from the ceiling this morning.

Managers were forced to evacuate the Wetherspoon pub and close the doors.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-postern-gate-york-exterior-nov-2026.webp)

Signs were put up saying, “Due to unforeseen circumstances, we have had to close temporarily.

“Our nearest Wetherspoon is the Punch Bowl, Blossom Street, York.”

This afternoon, a Wetherspoon spokesperson said: “The pub is currently closed, there was a burst pipe in the Travel Lodge above us.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-postern-gate-york-closed-sign-25-nov-2026.webp)

“We are waiting for the water to stop before we can get emergency contractors here to check that we are safe to reopen.

“I suspect that this will take a few hours, although we are unsure at this time.”

In fact, it reopened shortly before 5.30pm. When told the pub was back open by ***YorkMix***, a group of people waiting nearby said, “Thank god – let’s go.”

The Postern Gate closed for a week in August as it underwent a refurb.
