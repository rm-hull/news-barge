## A botched attempt to rob an armoured vehicle set off the day-long standoff, during which negotiators had already secured the release of 10 captives.

Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella said all the hostages held inside a supermarket in the Colombian city of Cartagena had been rescued and one hostage-taker killed, after security forces raided the building early on Wednesday.

Several explosions were heard before security forces entered the building in the Caribbean port city, AFP reported. A police helicopter had been seen hovering low overhead shortly before the assault.

About 20 people were believed to still be inside when the raid began. Officials said armed men had initially held around 30 people, but 10 were freed after hours of negotiations, among them several elderly women.

De la Espriella travelled to Cartagena while the crisis unfolded and met security commanders at the scene.

Authorities said the siege began after a failed attempt to rob an armoured vehicle.
