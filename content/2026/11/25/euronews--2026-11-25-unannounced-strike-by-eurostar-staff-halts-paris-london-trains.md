## The rail operator has advised customers to postpone their journeys and has warned that more disruption on other routes is possible.

Several Eurostar trains due to travel between Paris and London have been cancelled because of strike action by Paris-based staff.

The operator said it was informed of the industrial action overnight. Train drivers and managers are among the staff on strike.

A spokesperson for Eurostar said: "Regrettably, Eurostar customers travelling between Paris and London today are being impacted by industrial action which the company was notified of overnight."

The statement continued: "Unfortunately, this is causing some cancellations to services. Other routes are not directly affected but may see some knock-on impact."

At least six trains have been cancelled, including one that originated in Brussels. Another five have been subject to delays.

One passenger travelling from Paris Gare du Nord to London St Pancras International told Euronews that they were initially told their delay was due to an "unexpected number of travellers". This was later corrected in an email informing them of the unannounced strike action.

The email reads: "Due to unannounced strike action by some of our staff, we expect your train to be delayed by about 90 minutes," and advised passengers with onward connections to speak to staff.

The operator has told customers whose trains have been cancelled that they can rebook for another date or receive a full refund.

Eurostar also said discussions with unions were ongoing.
