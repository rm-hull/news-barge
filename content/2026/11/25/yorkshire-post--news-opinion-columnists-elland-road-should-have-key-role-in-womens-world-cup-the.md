# Elland Road should have key role in Women's World Cup: The Yorkshire Post says

## For the first time since 1966, England – and Yorkshire – has a football world cup on the horizon.

While Sheffield Wednesday’s Hillsborough ground was one of the host nations for the famous men’s tournament of 1966, Leeds United’s Elland Road is one of the proposed venues for the 2035 Women’s World Cup which is due to be played in stadiums across the United Kingdom.

The final host cities, stadiums and training sites will be confirmed by FIFA in due course but it would be a major disappointment if Elland Road, which is the process of being expanded to a 53,000 capacity and was included in the successful bid proposal as a candidate city, is not amongst them.

That is particularly because there are no other Yorkshire stadia in the running (despite Sheffield United’s Bramall Lane previously successfully hosting England women’s games, including their victory in the Euro 2022 semi-final on the way to winning the tournament).

The event will be a simply huge opportunity for the women’s game in this country – not just in the summer of 2035 but also all the years surrounding it.

Organisers believe around 4.5 million fans will attend games, while it is claimed the global audience could be 3.5 billion. It comes at an important time for the game in this country amidst debate about falling attendances at Women’s Super League matches.

With Andy Burnham setting an aspiration to get a million more girls in sport by the time the UK hosts the Women’s World Cup in 2035, it is vital Yorkshire hosts matches to inspire the next generation.
