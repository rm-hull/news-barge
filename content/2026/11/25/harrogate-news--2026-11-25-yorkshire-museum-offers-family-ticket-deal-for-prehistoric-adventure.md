**Families can explore dinosaurs, ancient history and interactive exhibits at the Yorkshire Museum with a specially priced family ticket available on selected weekends.**

The Yorkshire Museum is offering a special family ticket deal across two weekend periods this late autumn, allowing visitors to explore prehistoric Yorkshire and the region’s rich ancient history at a reduced price.

Available on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November, as well as Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December 2026, the ticket admits up to two adults and three children for £25. Families can immerse themselves in the museum’s dinosaur-themed exhibits, including giant marine fossils and smaller prehistoric sea creatures, alongside interactive augmented and virtual reality experiences designed for all ages.

Visitors can pick up a trail sheet to guide their prehistoric adventure, delving into significant discoveries such as those made by the pioneering fossil hunter Mary Anning. Beyond the dinosaur displays, the museum showcases artefacts from the Iron Age, Roman Eboracum, and Viking eras, offering a broad journey through Yorkshire’s archaeological heritage.

The Yorkshire Museum, located in York’s botanical gardens and housing notable collections from the Roman, Viking, Medieval and earlier periods, opens Wednesday to Sunday from 10am until 5pm. Seasonal variations to opening times may apply.

Those interested can find more details, book tickets, and review terms and conditions on the Yorkshire Museum’s official website.

This offer provides an engaging opportunity for families in Harrogate and the North Yorkshire area to enjoy cultural and educational experiences close to home during the festive season.
