Emergency teams pulled **one survivor** alive from the ruins of a collapsed**six-storey** building in**Karachi** as rescuers raced to reach**around 15 workers** feared trapped beneath vast piles of concrete and twisted metal. Using heavy machinery and carving**three access routes** into the wreckage, crews from**Pakistan’s** main emergency service,**Rescue 1122**, carried injured victims away on stretchers to** Medical Centre**, though officials cautioned that the exact number of missing remained unconfirmed.

The unfinished structure gave way while still under construction, leaving **at least three people** dead and**14 injured**, according to** Rescue 1122**, following initial reports of** between 10 and 14**hurt. As authorities investigate the cause of the disaster, the collapse has renewed concerns over lax building standards and weak safety enforcement in** Karachi**and across** Pakistan**.
