## More than 80 boxes containing Christmas ornaments and decorations were found, with an estimated value of €50,000.

The shipping container that washed ashore near Tsarevo, on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast, on 2 November has been opened.

The operation was carried out with the authorisation of the Military Prosecutor's Office in Sofia and in the presence of investigative authorities and customs officials.

More than 80 boxes containing Christmas ornaments and decorations were found, with an estimated total value of €50,000.

"We are currently conducting an inspection of the container's contents," said Atanas Yankov, a member of the military police.

The goods belong to a Romanian company. "The merchant who placed the order will subsequently arrange for the shipment to be transported to Romania," Yankov added.

"There is nothing hazardous here, and I want to put everyone's minds at ease. This is simply a commercial shipment that, unfortunately, was caught up in military action," said Diko Dikov, regional governor of Burgas.

## Nine ships struck in 24 days off Bulgaria and Romania

The container is believed to have been part of the cargo aboard the container ship Contship Jet, which sank off the coast of Shabla following an attack by naval drones.

A string of such incidents off the coasts of NATO members Romania and Bulgaria this month has led some alliance members to wonder if Russian President Vladimir Putin is seeking to test their support for Ukraine in its fight against Moscow's full-scale invasion.

Russia has not taken responsibility for the recent attacks, but suspicion has fallen on Moscow.

Last week, a Liberian-flagged gas tanker sank in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone in the Black Sea, according to the Defence Ministry, the latest in the series of incidents affecting shipping there.

Over 24 days in October, nine cargo ships and tankers were struck by aerial or naval drones near the Bulgarian and Romanian coast
