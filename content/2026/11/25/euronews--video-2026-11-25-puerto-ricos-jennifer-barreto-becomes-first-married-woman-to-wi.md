Puerto Rico's Jennifer Barreto was crowned Miss Universe 2026 on Tuesday evening, winning the 75th edition of the pageant in San Juan.

The 27-year-old received the crown from Miss Universe 2025 Fatima Bosch, as contestants and spectators celebrated.

Barreto becomes the first married woman and mother to win the title since organisers lifted restrictions on contestants who had married or given birth three years ago.

The victory brings Puerto Rico's total to six Miss Universe titles.
