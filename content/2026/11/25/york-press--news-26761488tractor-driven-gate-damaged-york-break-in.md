*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A tractor was driven into a gate and badly damaged during a break-in in the early hours at the weekend.

The attempted theft took place at about 2am on Sunday, November 22, at the junction of Knavesmire Road and Tadcaster Road in York after a site was broken into.

North Yorkshire Police said both the gate and the tractor sustained "significant damage".

**Recommended reads:**

A police spokesperson said: "We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone may have witnessed the incident or anyone who has any dashcam or CCTV footage of the area during this time."

Anyone with information is asked to contact police online, quoting reference number 12260229133.

Information can also be shared by calling 101 or emailing [maddi.feeney@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:maddi.feeney@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Those wishing to remain anonymous can contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
