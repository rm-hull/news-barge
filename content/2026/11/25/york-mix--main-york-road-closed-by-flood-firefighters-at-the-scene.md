A main York road is closed tonight due to flooding.

A burst water main has left a stretch of St Helen’s Road in Dringhouses underwater.

It’s shut from its junction with Tadcaster Road down to Dringhouses Sports Club.

Firefighters are working to pump water clear from the street close to the club.

Some residents in the area have no water.

One posted on Facebook: “Oh wow now I know why my water’s gone off.”

It was reported to Yorkshire Water earlier this evening. Their engineers are at the scene.

The scale of the burst means work will be going on for many hours.

Yorkshire Water said: “We’re aware of a burst water main in YO24. If you’re in the area, you may experience no water or low water pressure.

“There’s no need to call us – we’re working on it now. Thanks for your patience.”
