The winner of this year’s *The Great British Sewing Bee* has teamed up with St Leonard’s Hospice for a sewing-themed event in January.

Anna Pownall, who also works for the Hospice, will star in ‘An Afternoon with Anna’ on **Friday 22 January 2027** at The Milner Hotel in York.

The event, which is the first of its kind for Anna, will give fans the opportunity to hear Anna speak about her time on the show, see her creations from the series up close and get the chance to ask their questions in person.

Tickets also include a welcome drink, a two-course meal and a gift bag.

All proceeds raised from the event will be donated to the Hospice, which provides care and support to people in and around York who are affected by terminal illness and bereavement.

Anna was crowned champion of *The Great British Sewing Bee* earlier this year, after she blew the judges away all through the series.

Speaking ahead of the event, Anna said: “I’m really excited to visit The Milner in January. I love being part of the Retail team at St Leonard’s and I’m so passionate about the work the Hospice does.

“Having the opportunity to talk about my time on the show and showcase my designs while raising money for our services is incredible.

“There’s so much that I want to share, and I’m really looking forward to answering people’s questions. I hope it’ll be a really inspiring and enjoyable way to spend an afternoon.”

Channon Barlow, head of fundraising at St Leonard’s Hospice, said: “We’re so grateful to Anna for donating her time for this event.

“We know that *The Great British Sewing Bee* has a huge following, and people were blown away by Anna’s performance and designs on the show. It’s such a special opportunity to be able to find out more about her experience in this kind of forum.

“As a charity, 75% of our funding comes from our community in York and the surrounding area. It’s because of events like this that St Leonard’s can continue providing specialist palliative and end-of-life care without charge.

“We’re so proud of all that Anna’s achieved, and we really think this is an event that people won’t want to miss.”
