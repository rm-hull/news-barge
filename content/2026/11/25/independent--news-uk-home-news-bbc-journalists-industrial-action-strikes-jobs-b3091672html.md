Journalists at the BBC have voted in favour of industrial action amid ongoing disputes surrounding job security and workload pressures.

Members of the National Union of Journalists across England, Scotland, Wales and international bureaux overwhelmingly supported both strikes and and other forms of industrial action.

A separate ballot conducted in Northern Ireland saw 84.21 per cent back strike action, while 90.35 per cent favoured non-strike industrial action.

The result gives the NUJ a legal mandate over the next 12 months to take industrial action.

The union said this remains a last resort and no decision to take action has yet been made.
