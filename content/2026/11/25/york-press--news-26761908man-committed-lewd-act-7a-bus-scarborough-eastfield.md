## Bald pervert spotted 'carrying out lewd act' on bus in North Yorkshire

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a man was reported to have 'committed a lewd act' on a bus in Scarborough earlier this month *(Image: Copilot)*

A BALD man has been spotted 'carrying out a lewd act' whilst travelling on a bus.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a man was reported to have 'committed a lewd act' on a bus in Scarborough earlier this month.

The incident took place on the 7A bus travelling towards Eastfield at around 12pm last Wednesday (November 18), with a member of public reporting the incident.

The suspect is described as a white man aged between 30 and 40 years old.

He is believed to be bald, with facial stubble, and was wearing green trainers, black trousers, a green hoodie, and a black coat.

A police spokesperson said: "We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed this incident or anyone who may have seen the male before or after the incident."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [Olivia.Harrison@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Olivia.Harrison@northyorkshire.police.uk), reporting the incident through the website, calling 101, or contacting Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260226523 when passing on information.
