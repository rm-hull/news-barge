Police have released CCTV footage of a person they want to speak to following an alleged theft in York.

The incident happened at Sainsbury's on Bootham on October 15, 2026.

North Yorkshire Police are urging the public to help identify the person, who could provide information to help to their investigation.

**Recommended reads:**

Anyone with information is asked to contact PC Joanne Bell via email, referencing 12260203612.

Alternatively, details can be submitted through the North Yorkshire Police website, or by calling 101.

Anonymous tips can be given to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
