Seagate's Expansion Desktop Hard Drive is a great pick for buyers who need a lot of storage for a cost-effective price. Backed by one of the biggest names in storage, this one comes with a two-year manufacturer's guarantee and Seagate's Rescue Data Recovery Services, plus a fresh new design compared with earlier models.

Now, Amazon has dropped the price of the 8TB hard drive to £217 (was £272) for Black Friday, with other discounts on the 4TB model, which is £145 (was £158), and the 20TB HDD, now priced at £522 (was £666).

The drive promises 120MB/s read speeds via a handy USB-C connection, but it's slightly more limited by its Micro-B USB connection on the hard drive's end, so it's ideal for home users who need capacity, not speed.

![Seagate 8TB Expansion Desktop Hard Drive ]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ENtEvzFqpT3gNWfXARGXRB-200-80.avif.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ENtEvzFqpT3gNWfXARGXRB-200-80.avif.webp) "Seagate 8TB Expansion Desktop Hard Drive ")

A portable external hard drive with a 3.5" 4TB/8TB/16TB/20TB/24TB hard disk drive within. It connects to your device via an 80cm USB-C cable, and offers data read speeds of up to 120MB/s.

## Why we recommend it

It might not be the quickest external storage available (SSDs are far quicker), but this HDD-based external drive is a great pick for buyers looking to keep costs low without sacrificing on huge capacity. Plus, USB-C connectivity means it should be pretty widely compatible, too.

## Price Context & Historical Value

Storage costs have risen sharply in recent months, and even HDDs remain in demand for their affordable capacity. Seagate's 4TB model is right on the bottom end of similar competitors on Amazon, and bigger savings on higher capacities are even more attractive.
