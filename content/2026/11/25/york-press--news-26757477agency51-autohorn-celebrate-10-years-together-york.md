A long-standing partnership is being celebrated by two York businesses.

Digital agency Agency51 and Autohorn Fleet Services are marking 10 years of collaboration, when Agency51 took on marketing for Flexed, Autohorn’s short-term leasing brand.

Since then, the partnership has grown to include new website builds, SEO, PPC, social media, email marketing, PR and paid social advertising.

Hannah Pittman, director of marketing at Agency51, said: "It’s fantastic to celebrate ten years of working with another successful York business.

"What makes the relationship particularly special is that we share a commitment to York, its businesses and the wider community."

Autohorn has supported the York community for more than 30 years.

Mark Williams, brand strategist at Autohorn, said: "Whether it’s fundraising, local partnerships or helping someone get to work in the morning, it’s important to us that we’re a real part of our community, and our collaboration with Agency51 is part of that. It goes beyond just growing the business."
