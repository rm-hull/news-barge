EU leaders are meeting this week to discuss the bloc’s next seven-year budget. The EU wants to spend more on defence and support for Ukraine, but governments are divided over how much to spend and who should pay. Brussels is pushing for EU-level taxes, but that risks opening new tensions with businesses, national governments and even Donald Trump.

Sean Bray, vice president of global tax policy at the Tax Foundation, told *Europe Today* that a deal is more likely in December. He also warned that one proposal to help pay for the budget, an EU-wide tax on digital services, could hurt economic growth.
