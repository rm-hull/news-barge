Niels Fuglsang, a Danish Socialists and Democrats MEP, says democratic accountability is at “stake” ahead of a European Court of Justice ruling on the European Commission’s failure to advance plans to phase out the use of cages for farm animals.

In 2019, 1.4 million Europeans called for an end to the practice of keeping farm animals in cages through a European Citizens’ Initiative. The European Commission pledged to take action by 2023, but no legislative proposal has yet been put forward.

Several MEPs, including Fuglsang, subsequently took the Commission to court. In a ruling published on Thursday, the European Court of Justice found that the Commission had “failed to follow up on its intention to put an end to the farming of certain species and categories of animals”.

According to Fuglsang, agricultural interests are among those “blocking progress” on the issue.
