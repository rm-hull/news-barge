Several hundred protesters gathered outside the European Parliament in Strasbourg on Tuesday to oppose a controversial bill on pesticides.

Demonstrators say the proposed legislation could weaken existing safeguards, allowing unlimited authorisations for certain pesticides and extending the periods during which banned products can remain on the market.

Protesters, including campaigners, farmers, scientists and cancer victims, called on MEPs to protect public health and reject the changes.

The bill has raised concerns over the potential impact of pesticide exposure on human health and the environment.
