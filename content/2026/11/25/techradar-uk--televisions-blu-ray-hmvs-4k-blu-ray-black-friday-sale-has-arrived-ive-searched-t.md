Good news, 4K Blu-rays fans — HMV is having a huge 20% off sale on 4K Blu-rays! Simply use code HIDEF20 at checkout. There are hundreds of discs to choose from including some classic titles and some of 2026's biggest releases.

As TechRadar's 4K Blu-ray reviewer, I've picked 12 of my favourite discs from the sale, all of which I've personally tested as part of the Blu-ray Bounty, our monthly column where we test the latest 4K Blu-ray releases.

If you're looking for discs that showcase a TV's contrast, then ***Fight Club*** , ***Dark City*** and ***Project Hail Mary*** are three excellent examples, with plenty of high contrast scenes and deep black levels which look great on the best OLED TVs.

4K Blu-ray does wonders for classic movies and three of my favourite examples are ***Double Indemnity, Some Like It Hot*** and ***3:10 to Yuma (1957)***, with all three discs getting new life on 4K.

If you're looking for discs bursting with colour, then you can't go wrong with ***The Super Mario Galaxy Movie*** and ***Supergirl***. Both these discs are fantastic Dolby Atmos discs too, which will sound immersive and powerful on your home theatre system.
