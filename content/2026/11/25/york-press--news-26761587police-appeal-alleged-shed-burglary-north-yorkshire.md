A person was captured on CCTV allegedly breaking into a shed and stealing some of the contents in a North Yorkshire village.

Police are now appealing for help to identify a man following the alleged shed burglary on Main Street in Burton Salmon, Selby.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "It happened at about 7.55am on November 5, 2026, and involved a male breaking into a shed and stealing some of the contents.
