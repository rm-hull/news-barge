Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled a years-long legal battle over their French winery, according to a joint statement from the former Hollywood couple.

“We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further,” representatives for Jolie and Pitt said in a statement. “Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us.”

Both sides have moved to dismiss claims against each other. The terms of the agreement were not made public.

The couple had bought a controlling interest in Château Miraval in 2008, and married there in 2014.

Jolie initiated divorce proceedings in 2016, and the couple was declared legally single in 2019, although their divorce settlement continued to play out until 2024. Jolie was cleared to sell her share in 2021 for $164m (£125.06m) before their wider financial settlement was finalized.

Pitt filed the lawsuit against Jolie in February 2022, after the actor sold her stake in the French winery to the Stoli Group, a spirits maker controlled by oligarch Yuri Shefler. In the suit, Pitt said Jolie had broken their agreement not to sell their interests in the winery without the other’s consent after she sold her stake to the Russian businessman.

In October 2022, Jolie filed a counter lawsuit against Pitt, which for the first time described alleged abusive behavior that led to their divorce. The actor’s attorneys said negotiations for Jolie to sell her share in the winery to Pitt broke down over his demand that she sign “a nondisclosure agreement that would have contractually prohibited her from speaking outside of court about Pitt’s physical and emotional abuse of her and their children”. Pitt denied the accusations.
