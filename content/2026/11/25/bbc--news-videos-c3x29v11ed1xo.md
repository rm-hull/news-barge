# What we know about Trump's Thanksgiving tariffs

BBC business reporter Jonathan Josephs breaks down President Trump's latest tariffs, announced on the eve of Thanksgiving, which will affect some of the United States' biggest trading partners including China and the European Union.

The measures, set to come into effect in January 2027, come after the Supreme Court ruled Trump's April 2025 'Liberation Day' tariffs illegal.

So what is the administration's alternative plan, and what could it mean for American businesses and consumers?
