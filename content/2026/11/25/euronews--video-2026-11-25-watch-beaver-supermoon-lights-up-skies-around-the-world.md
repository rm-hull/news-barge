The Beaver Supermoon illuminated skies over Rome and Dubai on Tuesday evening. In Rome, the full moon rose above the Italian capital’s historic landmarks, as tourists gathered on the Gianicolo terrace to take photos.

In Dubai, the moon appeared above the city’s towering skyscrapers, with its reflection visible across the illuminated downtown skyline.

The supermoon occurs when the full moon coincides with its closest point to Earth in its elliptical orbit, making it appear larger and brighter than usual.
