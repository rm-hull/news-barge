## Why we recommend it

Scoring 4.5 stars in our review, Airalo proved to be cheaper than most international roaming plans, and we loved the wide coverage and simple setup.

As noted, the service covers more than 200 destinations with its local, regional, and global eSIM packages. That’s a great start, and you get more flexibility too: you can choose plans for specific countries and wider regions and opt for different validity periods that suit your travel plans.

You can do all of your setup through the Airalo app, which keeps things simple - and it also means you can set up your roaming before you travel, rather than scrambling to sort it out when you arrive.

Having tested all the top servies, we rated the provider the best eSIM when it came to global plan availability and coverage.
