Heading into winter can make it tough to make ends meet for some, with the extra expenses of social events, travel and often a longer time between December and January paydays.

That’s without even considering spending more on gifts, food or sale events like Black Friday – even someone who budgets strongly can sometimes feel the need for extra cash at this time of year.

With more and more Brits taking on side hustles or second jobs, it’s perhaps refreshing to see that there are a few jobs out there which are still in need of more staff and can sometimes be done at times that suit your schedule.

![More and more Britons are taking on side hustles or second jobs](https://static.independent.co.uk/2021/01/07/11/iStock-1213470238.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Christmas doesn't seem to be getting any cheaper, with the cost of buying presents and filling the fridge for the big day continuing to add up. So, a little extra cash can go a long way, and taking on a side job can be a handy way to top up your bank balance before the festive season,” said Katie Emerton, recruitment expert at CV-Library.

“From walking dogs and delivering takeaways to supporting small businesses with day-to-day admin as a virtual assistant, there are plenty of flexible ways to earn some extra money without giving up your day job. The rise of gig-style work means you can find something that not only brings in a bit of extra cash, but also fits around your lifestyle, rather than the other way around.”

CV-Library research identified 10 of the best-paying roles that offer the potential for flexibility and work-life balance, with their own job data yielding the average salary that can be expected in the UK and the average number of vacancies advertised across the wider market over the past 12 months.

Here are the five, ranked by expected hourly income:

* Online tutor: £28.28 average hourly wage (55,087 job postings)
* Virtual assistant: £23.14 (178,000)
* Freelance writer or copywriter: £17.68 (5,544)
* Mystery shopper: £17.22 (4,518)
* Brand ambassador: £16.49 (3,419)

Naturally, some of these might be more suited to some people than others. An online tutor requires not only specialist subject knowledge, but also the patience to deliver it to others remotely. And while a mystery shopper might sound like a fun task, you’ll naturally need to be available while those shops or stores are open, rather than it being an exclusively evening activity.

CV-Library pointed out that for those confident speaking to people, brand ambassador work could be worth exploring – which might involve promoting products, handing out samples or representing companies at events. Temporary opportunities for that kind of work are often available during busy shopping periods... which likely suits upcoming Black Friday and Christmas spells.

For the rest of the top 10, they are a mix of seasonal and constant requirements, jobs you can set up alone and those you need to be hired for.
