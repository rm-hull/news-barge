A retirement community in Knaresborough has celebrated its first anniversary with an afternoon of jazz, food and warm company.

Riverside Gardens in Wetherby Road, operated by Adlington Retirement Living, welcomed residents and prospective homeowners to the event, which included live entertainment from the Chris White Jazz Duo, a drinks reception and a celebratory meal.

Jean, 92, who moved in earlier this year, said: "The whole event was so lovely.

"We are so well looked after here.

"I really appreciate the variety of social activities available to us and, being on my own, I value the opportunities to join in."

Marilyn, 78, who relocated to Riverside Gardens with her husband Brian just two months ago, said: "Initially we were reluctant to move from our home of 42 years, where we had been so happy.

"We have been very pleasantly surprised at just how quickly Riverside Gardens has become home."

Gillian Ward-Brown, general manager at Riverside Gardens, said: "It's wonderful to celebrate our first birthday and look back on everything that's happened over the past 12 months.

"We've had some lovely moments together over the past year, and our birthday celebration is a chance to bring everybody together and look forward to many more."
