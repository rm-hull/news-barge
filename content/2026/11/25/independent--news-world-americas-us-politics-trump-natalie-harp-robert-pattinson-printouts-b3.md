There was only one woman up to the special 007 mission President Donald Trump had assigned. And her name’s Harp…Natalie Harp.

In the middle of a sit-down media interview in the Oval Office, the president needed his executive assistant, dubbed the “human printer,” to bring him hard copies of reports about Robert Pattinson, confirmed to play the next iteration of James Bond.

Trump said he wished Pattinson luck in the new coveted role after months of speculation, before instructing Harp to print off news articles about the British star.

“He looks nice. You know, I wish him luck,” Trump told *The New York Post.*

The president then reportedly called to Harp through the open door of the Oval Office and asked her, “Is James Bond conservative? Natalie.”

![President Donald Trump needed his executive assistant, dubbed the ‘human printer,’ to bring him hard copies of news articles about Robert Pattinson, confirmed to play the next iteration of James Bond](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/06/09/2298782614..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Harp then came “rushing” in from her desk outside the Oval Office with the printouts and gave her assessment.

“He’s been good,” she told Trump.

Trump reportedly responded, “I always like people that like me,” and proceeded to flip through “clips about him and Pattinson,” according to the outlet.

“That’s cute. He says nice things,” Trump said of of the *The* * Batman*and* Twilight* star.

It was not immediately clear what evidence Trump has that Pattinson likes him or what “nice things” the actor has said about the president.

*The Post* also asked the president about unsolicited advice he gave the actor in 2012 on Twitter about his former relationship with Kristen Stewart amid a cheating scandal.

“Robert Pattinson should not take back Kristen Stewart. She cheated on him like a dog & will do it again--just watch. He can do much better!” Trump posted in October 2012 before he was president, along with a slew of other tweets about the relationship.

![It was not immediately clear what evidence Trump has that Pattinson likes him or what ‘nice things’ the actor has said about the president](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/17/20/HXQR9HKWsAADsY6.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“This was a long time ago,” Trump reflected on the tweets to the *Post* after reading them aloud.

The actor ignored the attention at the time, but eventually responded to the posts five years later — though his answer was unclear.

“When he said that, it didn't really mean anything. But I guess now I'm sort of thinking, like, ‘Well, I guess that is related to me.’ But how does that fit in with all the other things going on in my life?” Pattsinon said in 2017. “And sometimes you think, ‘Can I use this in my acting? Or should I be putting it away.’ It's kind of interesting, I guess. I don't know — this could be why people get annoyed with me.”

The Oval Office interview was the last White House engagement the president had on his Tuesday schedule before jetting off to Mar-a-Lago for Thanksgiving, with Harp in tow.

She was pictured boarding Air Force One alongside Secretary of State Marco Rubio.

*The Independent* previously reported how Harp appears to have taken control of the president’s personal mobile phone and is deciding who can and cannot speak to him.

Harp’s apparent involvement comes at a time when the young White House aide appears to be exerting more and more control over the president’s consumption of news and information.
