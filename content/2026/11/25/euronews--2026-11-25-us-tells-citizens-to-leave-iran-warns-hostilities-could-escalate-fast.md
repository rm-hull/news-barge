**Euronews Persian**

## The US Virtual Embassy in Iran said Tehran, Yemen's Houthis and other aligned groups may target US interests and citizens in the region, including companies and institutions. The alert followed Iranian attacks on US assets in Bahrain and Kuwait and a Houthi strike on Riyadh that injured 11 people.

Washington warned citizens across the Middle East to exercise heightened vigilance after Iran attacked US assets in Bahrain and Kuwait and Yemen's Houthi forces struck a tower in Riyadh's financial district, wounding 11 people.

The US Virtual Embassy in Iran issued a security alert on Wednesday citing the attacks and warning that hostilities could escalate rapidly.

It said Iran, the Houthis and other groups aligned with Tehran may target US interests and citizens in the region, including companies and institutions.

"Do not travel to Iran for any reason. United States citizens should leave Iran immediately," the alert said.

A Houthi attack on Tuesday struck a tower in Riyadh's financial district, wounding 11 people, Saudi Arabia's Civil Defence said.

Bahrain's Interior Ministry said Tehran's attacks on Saturday wounded two people and caused a fire at a civilian industrial facility.

US citizens in the region were urged to monitor airline and airport operations and prepare for possible flight cancellations and airspace closures.

The alert also warned that rules for crossing Iran's land borders and entry conditions in destination countries may change at short notice.

Citizens were advised to avoid demonstrations, large gatherings and areas with a heavy police presence, and to stay alert in places associated with the US.

In the event of air attacks, the alert said citizens should keep away from doors, windows and debris, keep their mobile phones charged and follow instructions from local authorities.

It directed citizens to register with the US State Department's information system to receive security updates.

The US and Iran have had no diplomatic relations since 1980. Switzerland acts as the protecting power for US interests in Tehran, and the alert named the US interests section at the Swiss embassy there as a point of contact for citizens needing assistance.
