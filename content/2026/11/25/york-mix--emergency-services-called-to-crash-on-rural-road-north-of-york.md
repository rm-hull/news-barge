Emergency services were called to a crash north of York yesterday evening (Tuesday).

The single-vehicle collision happened on **Goose Track Lane**, near** Sheriff Hutton**at** 5.44pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “All occupants were out on arrival and no fire service action was required.

#### Goose Track Lane

“The incident was left in the hands of the police.”
