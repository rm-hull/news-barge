A person was taken to hospital after a fire at a property in a North Yorkshire village last night (Tuesday).

The incident happened at 8.35pm in Northfield, Barlby, involving a pan fire and heavy smoke.

**Recommended reads:**

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: "Two crews from Selby responded to reports of smoke issuing from floorboards in a residential property.

"This was found to be smoke from a pan fire in a neighbouring property.

"Crews gained entry to the neighbouring property and led one person to safety who was then taken to hospital for checks.

"The fire was out on arrival and crews cleared the property of heavy smoke logging using a positive pressure ventilation fan."
