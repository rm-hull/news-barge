# Emmerdale's Charles star Kevin Mathurin on heart attack and his famous partner

## Kevin Mathurin, who plays Charles Anderson in Emmerdale is loved-up with his partner

Kevin Mathurin has won over Emmerdale fans with his portrayal of Charles Anderson - but what's the story behind the man himself?

Since joining the beloved ITV soap in 2020 as the village vicar, Kevin has quickly captured viewers' hearts with his compelling performance. Throughout his tenure, Charles has been at the centre of numerous gripping storylines.

Whether it's his ill-fated relationship with Manpreet Sharma (Rebecca Sarker) or the tragic loss of his son Ethan (Emile John), life in the village has been anything but quiet for the character, reports the Mirror.

Off-screen, however, Kevin's life bears little resemblance to his dramatic Dales persona. So who is the woman in his life? And which blockbuster film featured him? Here's what we've discovered about Kevin Mathurin.

### Kevin's previous profession

Before establishing himself as an actor, Kevin carved out an impressive career in dance. Speaking on ITV's Lorraine, he recalled: "When I left college in 1991, my journey started more on the dance career, so I kept going down that route."

He also shared a memorable experience performing alongside legendary singer Diana Ross at New York's Madison Square Garden. Kevin recounted: "We had a moment where, she likes to swap the running order of the show, so we would come on thinking we were doing a dance routine, and one time she decided 'Ok I got a friend here in the audience.' And this friend starts walking up on stage and it's Luther Vandross. They sing a duet of endless love and I'm sitting there like, I can't believe this is happening.".

Beyond his role in Emmerdale, Kevin appeared in the DC blockbuster Justice League and its reworked version, Zack Snyder's Justice League - both hitting cinemas in 2017 and 2021 respectively. In these films, the soap star took on the part of a sketch witness.. The superhero epic features legendary characters including Batman and Wonder Woman, with Kevin sharing screen time with the likes of Ben Affleck, Henry Cavill and Gal Gadot.

### Kevin's famous other half

Kevin has been together with internationally renowned DJ Nikki Beatnik since 2011 - she's collaborated with chart-topping artists such as Kelis, Jay-Z and Kanye West. Much like Kevin, Nikki operates within the entertainment industry, building her career as both a producer and DJ.

Speaking to The Standard in 2016 about her work, she said: "I've met some cool, interesting people. " She recalled: "One night Spike Jones hung out with me in the booth, but I didn't say a word to him because I was just too busy DJing.

"He said: "Another time, we went out with Tom Jones and was like, 'This is crazy'. I did parties for Alicia Keys a few times, and she'd have people like Stevie Wonder coming.". The couple share a son named Harley. When Harley turned eight in September 2022, his father marked the occasion with a birthday tribute on Instagram.

### Kevin on suffering a heart attack

Back in October, Kevin revealed he had suffered a heart attack and was rushed to hospital. He took to social media to candidly explain to his followers that he had been "suppressing" a lot of things in life and he ended up going to A&E on doctor's orders.

Kevin wrote on Instagram: "I was under a lot of stress and pressure. External issues were taking over my well-being. I was anxious, stressed, always angry and suppressing it all. Not dealing with things head on.

"I'd also lost a really good friend to cancer and was upset that I couldn't make his funeral due to work commitments. "He said: "It was all too much that week and I felt pains in my ribs and arms all week.

"But knowing me, I didn't complain, I just carried on filming, working out and driving up and down the country to be with my family. It was Nikki who begged me to book an ECG scan. By the time I got home from the hospital my doctor called me and said get straight to AandE."

He added: "Long story-short I had a blocked artery that had damaged part of my heart, making my heartbeat abnormal, causing heart failure and a minor heart attack. Within myself I felt fine, but this freaked me and my family out that I didn't pay attention to my body for a whole week."

Kevin then revealed that while at King's Hospital he was kept under monitor and had an Angiogram to determine how serious the blockages were. Fortunately, he didn't need surgery and said he was relying on "a lot of meds, exercise and a healthy diet to keep my blood pressure, heart rate and cholesterol down".

He continued: "But while I was in hospital that week I had a lot of time to think, reset, reevaluate my priorities and how I got to this point in my life in the first place. It kick-started a total reboot on my outlook on people, places and things."

He said: "I want to see my children grow up into wonderful adults and be in a position to guide them when they need me. I want to spend quality time with Nikki and be present when I'm with her and my family."

Kevin finished off the post and said: "I want to see my friends and let them know how much i value their company and time. I want to be a reliable work colleague and have gratitude for what this profession has given me and be open-minded for whatever is around the corner."
