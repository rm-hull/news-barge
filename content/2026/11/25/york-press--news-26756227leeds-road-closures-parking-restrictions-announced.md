Leeds City Council has announced several temporary road closures and parking restrictions across the city.

These measures are aimed at facilitating essential works, including the installation of new water meters, gas main replacement, and bridge refurbishment.

Eaton Hill in Cookridge will be closed from Wednesday, November 25, to Tuesday, December 1, for works on a new connection.

The closure will be in effect 24 hours a day.

Northgates in Wetherby will be closed for excavation and installation of ducts and cables from Tuesday, November 24, to Monday, November 30.

The closure will be in effect from 8am to 6pm on weekdays and 24 hours on Saturdays and Sundays.

Cavendish Street and Abbey Street in Little London and Woodhouse will have parking restrictions due to cladding replacement works.

The restrictions came into effect from Monday, November 23, and will last until Sunday, August 1, 2027.

Moorland Crescent, Fieldhouse Close, and Fieldhouse Drive will be closed at different times between Monday, November 23, and Friday, January 15, 2027, for gas main replacement works.

Dewsbury Road will be closed for bridge refurbishment works from Monday, November 30, to Sunday, December 13.

The closure will be in effect from 9pm to 5am daily.

Kirkstall Road will have bus lane suspensions for desilting works.

The suspension will be in effect from Tuesday, December 1, to Tuesday, December 15, on weeknights only from 10pm to 5am.

Morley Footpath No. 51 will be closed for the construction of a new site entrance for a housing development.

The closure will be in effect 24 hours a day from Thursday, November 26, for a maximum period of six months.

Leeds City Council has also introduced a city-wide order to amend waiting restrictions and on-street parking places within the Beeston and Holbeck Ward.

The changes include no waiting, loading, or unloading at any time on sections of Northern Street and Whitehall Road, and on-street pay-by-phone parking on Spence Lane.

The council has urged residents and motorists to plan their journeys accordingly and to follow the diversionary routes provided where applicable.

Essential access to and from premises and for emergency vehicles will be maintained throughout the duration of the works.

All enquiries regarding these notices should be directed to the respective contacts provided by the council.

The council has stated that these measures are necessary to ensure public safety and the smooth execution of the planned works.
