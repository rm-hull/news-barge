A mother has paid tribute to her “beautiful boy” after he was found seriously injured in a hospital car park.

Reece Massie, aged 21 and from Dundee, was discovered in a car park at Ninewells Hospital in Dundee at about 3.45am on Sunday.

He was taken to the hospital but he was pronounced dead.

Investigations revealed he had been injured in Arbroath a short time earlier.

![Police said the 21-year-old was found with serious injuries near Ninewells Hospital in Dundee at 3.45am on Sunday, but investigations have revealed he was injured in Arbroath](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/08/01M83QXCQNE9VDZBNV90XDMHSD.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In an emotional statement released through Police Scotland, his mother Leanne Boath said: “My beautiful boy Reece, aged just 21 was tragically taken from me.

“Reece was my whole life, and I have been left heartbroken.

“I do not know how I will navigate through this heartbreak and loss. I will never understand why this happened.

“I’m utterly broken. Sleep tight, my boy, until I see you again.

“Our family wish to be left alone to come to terms with and grieve the loss of our wonderful boy Reece.”

Police confirmed on Monday that a 25-year-old man has been arrested and charged in connection with Mr Massie’s death.

He appeared at Forfar Sheriff Court on Tuesday.

Detective Inspector Mike Ness said: “Our thoughts remain with the family of Reece.

“They are being supported by specialist officers from Police Scotland and they have asked for privacy at what is a very difficult time for them.”
