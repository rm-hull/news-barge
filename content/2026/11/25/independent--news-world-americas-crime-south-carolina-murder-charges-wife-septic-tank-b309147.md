A South Carolina man whose wife was reported missing in 2020 has been charged with her murder after her remains were found in a septic tank on his father’s property.

Harry Capers III, 42, of Hollywood, South Carolina, has been charged with murder, desecration of human remains and possession of a weapon during a violent crime, relating to the disappearance and death of his wife, Adrean Capers, Charleston County Sheriff’s Office said.

Adrean Capers, a 31-year-old mom of three, was first reported missing to the North Charleston Police Department in March 2020.

In May 2025, authorities determined she most likely went missing from the EconoLodge motel in Summerville, and gave the case to the Summerville Police Department.

While investigating, authorities in Summerville determined that she was most likely killed in the jurisdiction of the Charleston County Sheriff’s Office, and gave the case to that department in April of this year.

![Harry Capers III has been charged with the 2020 murder of his wife, Adrean Capers](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/25/13/16/Harry-Capers-III.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Detectives received a warrant to search the property owned by Harry Capers’ father in Hollywood, about 20 miles west of Charleston, which they executed on June 11, 2026.

Deputies utilized a cadaver K-9 during their search and the dog led them to a septic tank on the property where Adrean Capers’ body was found.

The Charleston County Coroner’s Office confirmed her identity through dental records and tattoos she had that remained intact.

Adrean Capers’ family remembered her as a loving and involved mother of three.

“She was a very loving daughter. Very kind, generous, sweetest girl I know,” her mother, Kemmie Beaton, told WCIV.

Her sister, Kay Beaton, 27, said Adrean’s youngest child was just two months old when she disappeared. Beaton said she knew something was wrong because she was missing milestones in her child’s life.

![Adrean Capers was positively identified by authorities through her dental records and tattoos](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/25/13/57/Adrean-Capers.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“She didn’t see her walk her first steps. She missed all of that,” Kay said, adding that it was unlike her to be away from her children for so long. “She was a motive and an active one.”

Since his wife disappeared six years ago, authorities said Harry Capers had moved several times, living in Texas, California, Florida and most recently Georgia.

He was taken into custody in Gwinnett County, Georgia, on fraud and theft charges and extradited Monday to Charleston County, South Carolina, to face murder charges.

Authorities said there are also fraud warrants out against him with the Berkeley County Sheriff’s Office and a kidnapping warrant out against him with the Summerville Police Department.

"Cases like these require patience, determination, and teamwork, and this outcome reflects the commitment of everyone involved. While nothing can erase the pain this family has endured, we hope this development brings them some measure of closure and reassurance that their loved one was never forgotten. The Charleston County Sheriff's Office remains committed to seeking justice for victims and their families, no matter how much time has passed,” Charleston County Sheriff Carl Ritchie said in a statement.

Capers was taken into custody and booked at the Sheriff Al Cannon Detention Center.
