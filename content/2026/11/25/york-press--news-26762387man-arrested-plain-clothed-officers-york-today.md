## Man 'arrested by plain-clothed officers' in York

A man was arrested in York by plain-clothed officers. *(Image: Newsquest)*

A man has been "arrested by plain-clothed officers" in York this afternoon.

An unmarked police car and a police van went to the scene on Davygate at about 2pm today (Wednesday, November 25).
