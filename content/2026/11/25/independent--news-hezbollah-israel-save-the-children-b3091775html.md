An orphanage in southern Lebanon’s village of Tebnine offers refuge to girls who have lost one or both parents. Damaged in the 2024 war between Israel and Hezbollah, it still needs repairs as Israeli strikes continue despite a ceasefire. The girls attend school and share meals, while caretakers provide emotional support. Daily routines offer some stability as Save the Children works to complete repairs.
