## How to Build Custom CRM Analytics Apps with AI

With Alaio Vibecode, teams can create applications that retrieve relevant Bitrix24 records, apply company-specific logic, and present results in a format suited to a particular decision.

They can build entirely new applications or adapt ready-made solutions. An app can be designed around a particular calculation, workflow, combination of data sources, or business process.

Building a custom app starts with a simple description of what you need. Alaio Vibecode works with AI coding agents such as Claude Code, Cursor, and OpenAI Codex to turn your instructions into an application that connects to your Bitrix24 workspace and works with your business data.

For the sales team using its own scoring model, an initial instruction might look like this:

Build a CRM analytics dashboard that ranks open opportunities by our scoring model. Use account type, days since the last activity, deal amount, and engagement history. Show the score breakdown and let employees open the corresponding CRM records.

The team would still define its calculation rules. For example, it might assign 40% of the score to engagement, 30% to account fit, and 30% to closing potential, with explicit criteria for each component. The application could calculate scores and present relevant deals in a ranked table.

## How to Test an AI-Built CRM Scoring App

Before using a custom scoring app with your team, try a decision replay test using historical CRM data:

1. **Select three past deals:** One that closed successfully, one that was lost, and one that stalled.
2. **Recreate the original situation:** Use only information available at the time, not details learned afterward.
3. **Run the scoring rules:** Check how the app would have ranked those opportunities.
4. **Evaluate the results:** Would the rankings have helped your team prioritize more effectively?

The goal isn't just accurate calculations. It's a scoring model that supports better decisions. Use this initial check to identify questionable assumptions, then test the model against a larger, representative set of records.

After testing, the team could adjust weights, introduce filters, or change how results appear. An analytical idea that once required a dedicated development project becomes easier to prototype and evaluate.

## Adding External Context with Deal Radar

A CRM shows what happened with an account, but sales representatives may also need information about recent developments outside it: a product announcement, expanded hiring, or new funding. Such signals can influence the questions worth asking in the next conversation.

**Deal Radar** combines CRM records with external research. It retrieves a deal and its associated company from Bitrix24, searches for relevant external signals, and generates a briefing with source references and an explanation of why the account may be worth contacting now.

Sources still need checking, and an announcement doesn't automatically make an opportunity more valuable. But Deal Radar assembles context employees would otherwise research manually, illustrating how internal data, external information, and AI-generated interpretation can support a specific business decision.

## When the Most Useful Dashboard Isn't a Dashboard

Consider a marketing department coordinating campaigns, content, and design approvals through Bitrix24 Tasks and Projects. Its manager may be less interested in reviewing every assignment than in identifying which tasks need attention first.

A task-monitoring application built with Alaio Vibecode illustrates this approach. **Stale Task Monitor** calculates a risk score using overdue days, time elapsed since the planned start date, and task priority. It displays the highest-risk tasks in a ranked list, with an AI-generated explanation and suggested next step.

The approach adapts to different workflows. Marketing teams might prioritize overdue campaign approvals; operations departments could focus on assignments affecting upcoming deliveries. Each organization defines what urgency means.

For a manager facing dozens of assignments, five prioritized tasks may be more useful than a dashboard covering every project. **The best analytical interface isn't necessarily the one with the most visualizations, but the one that helps someone make the next decision.**

Placement matters too. Apps built with Alaio Vibecode can be configured to appear directly in Bitrix24 CRM records, the main menu, or Tasks and Projects. A task-monitoring app, for example, can open from the project environment rather than a separate reporting section.

## How to Build Your First AI Analytics App

Start with a recurring analytical question that requires specific data or business rules: perhaps a weekly report assembled manually or a prioritization method employees follow but haven't automated.

Before creating an application, define five things:

1. **The decision:** What should users understand or do after seeing the result?
2. **The data:** Which CRM records, tasks, fields, dates, or statuses are required?
3. **The logic:** How should values be calculated, compared, or prioritized?
4. **The output:** Would a dashboard, table, ranked list, or conversational answer work best?
5. **The interaction:** Should users filter results, investigate records, or take further action?

Compare two instructions. "Build a project dashboard" leaves the AI coding agent guessing what matters. A more useful description would be:

Show overdue tasks in the marketing department, grouped by responsible employee, with upcoming deadlines and a prioritized list of assignments requiring attention.

The second instruction identifies the department, relevant records, and expected result. Once the application works, users can refine its rules, filters, or interface through further natural-language instructions.

Teams can also adapt ready-made solutions by changing scoring rules, activity thresholds, or other details.

Whatever the starting point, **validate the results against actual business records**. Check calculations, filters, and data permissions. Alaio Vibecode supports read-only API keys for reporting applications and authorization options based on signed-in employees' Bitrix24 permissions.** Security** is an important part of the Alaio Vibecode environment. Businesses can build custom apps around data stored in Bitrix24 without moving their CRM records into separate databases. Apps connect to Bitrix24 through permission-controlled access, so teams can limit which information each app can retrieve. For additional protection, apps can run on private Black Hole servers that are not directly accessible from the public internet.

Finally, involve the people who will use the app. Their feedback can reveal missing filters, questionable calculations, or opportunities to make results more useful. AI-assisted development makes iteration easier, but testing remains essential.
