## Picture-perfect North Yorkshire village named the third most beautiful in the UK

The village is home to a famous cottage. *(Image: Getty)*

A “wonderfully idyllic” village in North Yorkshire has been named one of the most beautiful in the UK.

Enjoy Travel has revealed a list of the 30 most beautiful villages found across the UK.

The list features spots offering “scenic” seaside strolls as well as “peaceful countryside escapes” and areas packed with history to explore.

The village of Thornton-le-Dale made it onto the list and was named the third most beautiful village.

## What is Thornton-le-Dale like?

On the village, Enjoy Travel said: “Thornton-le-Dale is one of the prettiest villages in the UK, with a babbling beck, stone and thatched-roof cottages and flower-filled gardens creating a wonderfully idyllic scene.

“The village is perhaps best known for its famous Beck Isle Cottage.

“The Grade II-listed building is often thought to be the most frequently photographed home in all of England.

“And it is little surprise given its look and location sitting beside the water beneath a traditional stone bridge.

“There are several quaint coffee shops, independent shops and pubs in and around the village, and plenty of walking and cycling routes to the neighbouring North York Moors.”

Thornton-le-Dale can be found right on the edge of the North York Moors National Park, making it a great spot for hikers.

The village itself is great for walks, as you can take a look at the thatched cottages and the famous Beck Isle Cottage while taking a stroll along the beck.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21750921.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The village has some great food spots to test out. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The village has plenty of places to visit for a meal, from quaint cafés to restaurants for a sit-down meal.

For a coffee and spot of lunch, you can try Lavenders Tea Rooms for a slice of cake or a full afternoon tea.

If you need something savoury, the tearoom also offers light meals made in-house using “only the best ingredients”.

For an evening meal, you might want to try Brandysnap Bistro, which has a near-perfect Tripadvisor score of 4.9 out of 5.

Reviewers praise the restaurant for its “excellent” food and “very professional service”.
