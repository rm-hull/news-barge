Kemi Badenoch has called out Nigel Farage’s apparent hypocrisy for launching a personal attack on her husband Hamish despite objecting to his own family being dragged into politics.

The Reform UK leader attacked Mr Badenoch at a press conference on Brexit on Tuesday where he claimed that the Tory leader was surrounding herself with former Remainers and was not serious about Brexit.

But in a video responding to his attack, Ms Badenoch reminded everyone that Mr Farage had objected to his own family being pulled into politics.

When he returned from one of his many trips to the US in July, he confronted Sky News at the airport for going to a house he was registered as living in and talking to his daughter.

At the time he said that he had “never been angrier in my life” and warned: “You tell your bosses, you harass my family anymore and there will be serious consequences.”

![Farage attacked Kemi Badenoch's husband at a press conference on Brexit](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/20/b1306a1ac52ab54eaa19770148c0fc4eY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1NjM3MjY0-2.86590717.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Calling out Mr Farage’s apparent hypocrisy, Ms Badenoch said: “It turns out, he can’t take it but he likes to dish it.”

She said: “My husband voted Remain like 16 million people in this country and I voted Leave.

“I know my own mind, I don’t need anyone telling me what to think. Certainly not Nigel Farage.

“I don’t think politicians' families are fair game and Nigel Farage doesn’t believe that either. So why is he doing this? It is because he has now realised that he is never going to be prime minister.

“As he slides into irrelevance, the attacks on me get more and more personal.”

Ms Badenoch also attacked Mr Farage for suggesting that his fellow Reform MP Sarah Pochin was drunk when she said Reform is “misogynist”, describing the attack as “appalling.”

![Conservative party leader Kemi Badenoch and her husband Hamish at the Tory party conference](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/06/19/2299054353..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

She suggested: “Rather than attacking my husband - who is quite lovely by the way - he should sort out the problems on his own side.”

She added: “Reform can never unite this country. Attacking people for how they voted is not the way to go. What I am doing is bringing the country together.”

Turning her fire on the prime minister, she said: “All of this is happening because Andy Burnham is reopening the Brexit debate. All it is doing is causing division and distraction.”

The row has broken out as Mr Farage will join his former Brexit allies Arron Banks and Andy Wigmore to launch Keep Britain Out to campaign to keep the UK out of the EU.

Already, Ms Badenoch’s mentor Lord Michael Gove has launched a Tory focussed pro-Brexit campaign group Stay Out in echoes of the Brexiteer split between Vote Leave and Leave.EU a decade ago.

*The Independent* revealed that the rejoin campaign grouping has also begun with Get In!

Responding to Ms Badenoch at a Brexit campaign launch press conference, Mr Farage said there was “no comparison” between Mr Badenoch and his daughter.

He said: “Hamish Badnoch is a public figure. He has stood for council. He has been a councillor. He was acted publicly in the Remain campaign. Has talked about it, been interviewed about it.

“He is the leader of His Majesty's opposition's chief adviser. He's given up his job to advise politically. Kemi's angry. Well, I mentioned it, but she's often angry. I mean, she's good at that.”
