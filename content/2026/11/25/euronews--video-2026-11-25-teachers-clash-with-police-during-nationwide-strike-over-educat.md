Thousands of teachers took to the streets of Santiago on Tuesday during a nationwide strike against proposed education budget cuts by President José Antonio Kast’s government.

Demonstrators marched past the presidential palace, La Moneda, demanding better funding for public schools and universities. Some protesters threw stones at police vehicles, while officers used water cannon to disperse the crowd.

Teachers say deteriorating school conditions, broken desks and unreliable internet are making public education increasingly precarious.

The proposed 2027 budget includes cuts to several university and education programmes.
