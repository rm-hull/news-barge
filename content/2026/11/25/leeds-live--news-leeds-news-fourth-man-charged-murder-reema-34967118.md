# Fourth man charged with murder of 'Reema' after weapons attack in Leeds

## Zaeem Phillip, 22, charged with the murder of Raheim Jackson, known as Reema

A fourth man has been charged with murdering Raheim Jackson, who was attacked with weapons in New Wortley.

Zaeem Phillip, 22, of Park Mill Drive, Armley, appeared at Leeds Magistrates' Court today (Wednesday, November 25) charged with the murder of Mr Jackson, who was known as Reema.

He was remanded to appear at Leeds Crown Court on Friday (November 27).

Mr Jackson, 38, was pronounced dead a short time after he was attacked outside a property on Hedley Chase, New Wortley, on the evening of October 24.

Police received a report at 10.23pm that a man was being attacked with weapons. Emergency services went to the scene but could not save him.

West Yorkshire Police said four people have now been charged with murder. Two others have been arrested and bailed pending further enquiries.

Earlier this month, Darnell Anderson-Browne, 29, of Holtdale Place, Leeds, became the third man to be charged in the investigation.

Detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team are still investigating.

Speaking after an arrest earlier in the investigation, Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates, from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, said: "Since this attack, we have been carrying out a thorough investigation that has already seen us charge two men and arrest two others who were subsequently bailed.

"Last night's arrest is another step forward in ensuring we get Mr Jackson's family the answers they need. We would still urge the public to come forward with any information that may be helpful to our investigation."

Anyone with information that can help the investigation is asked to contact police on 101 or use Live Chat on the West Yorkshire Police website, quoting reference 13260606745. Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
