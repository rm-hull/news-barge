A York company has gone into liquidation with debts topping £50,000.

YHG Property Maintenance was registered in Jasmine Close, New Earswick, and was incorporated in April 2023.

Companies House lists its sole director as John Robert Patrick.

The company entered liquidation on November 9, with Leeds-based Lewis Business Recovery and Insolvency appointed administrators.

A report known as a statement of financial affairs says the business had estimated assets of £6,000 available for redistribution to creditors.

The company had no preferential creditors – those who are paid first – but HMRC was owed £31,000 in VAT and PAYE as a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next.

In addition, there were unsecured creditors owed a total of £31,512.34.

This was made up of a trade and expense creditor owed £2,675.79, a bank/financial institution owed £18,836.55 and a director’s loan account owed £10,000.

This gave an estimated deficiency or shortfall of £56,512.32.

With the business having issued or called up capital of £2, this gave a total deficit of £56,512.32.
