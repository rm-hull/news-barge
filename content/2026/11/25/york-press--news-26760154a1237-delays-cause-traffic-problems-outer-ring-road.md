Drivers are facing delays on the outer ring road this morning due to delays on the A1237.

Traffic is moving at an average speed of 15mph with the AA reporting delays of six minutes on the A1237 Eastbound between the A1237 and Great North Way.

Congestion is heavier than usual, notably on the A1237 near Knapton and near the junction with the A59.

Traffic is also heavier than usual on the A64 eastbound between the junctions with the A659 and A1237 due to roadworks on the centre lane.
