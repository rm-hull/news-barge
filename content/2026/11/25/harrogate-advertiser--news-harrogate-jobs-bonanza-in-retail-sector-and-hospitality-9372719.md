Harrogate Jobcentre says it is ready to help as the town experiences a recruitment bonanza in its key retail and food and drink sectors this Christmas.

Employers across the district are actively recruiting seasonal staff, with businesses including Marks & Spencer, Tesco, Card Factory, Hotel Chocolat, Crew Clothing and Everyman Cinema advertising opportunities ahead of the festive season.

Harrogate Jobcentre is also reporting a surge of recruitment in hotels, cafés, restaurants, visitor attractions and leisure venues, reflecting strong seasonal demand across Harrogate and the surrounding area.

Harrogate Jobcentre says it is continuing to work closely with local employers and partners to help residents access the flurry of opportunities.

Its efforts have been boosted by the launch last month of the new Harrogate Youth Hub.

Delivered in partnership with Harrogate Town AFC, the new hub provides dedicated support for young people aged 16 to 24, looking to move into employment, training or further learning opportunities.

Michael Dales, Employer Partnership Manager for North Yorkshire and DWP spokesperson, said: "There are some fantastic opportunities available across Harrogate, particularly as employers begin their recruitment for the busy Christmas period.

“We're seeing strong demand across retail, hospitality and customer service, creating a wide range of opportunities for people looking to start work, gain experience or earn extra income in the run-up to Christmas.

"The opening of the Harrogate Youth Hub has been fantastic news for young people across the district.

"We know that every young person has different ambitions, talents and aspirations, and the Hub has been designed to provide a welcoming and supportive environment where they can access the help they need to move forward.”

Harrogate’s job figures area traditionally better than the national trends.

The most recent official claimant count figures from the DWP or September 2026 show 1,835 people claiming unemployment-related benefits in Harrogate, including 325 aged 18 to 24 and 475 aged 50 and over.

The new Harrogate Youth Hub aims to provide a welcoming and accessible space for young people aged 16 to 24, whether they are in work, looking for work, in education, training, or considering their next steps.

Bringing together DWP Work Coaches and local partner organisations, the Hub offers tailored support to help young people build confidence, develop skills and access opportunities.

Michael Dales said: “With Christmas recruitment now underway and employers continuing to advertise across a range of sectors, Harrogate Jobcentre is encouraging both jobseekers and businesses to take advantage of the opportunities available across the district and make the most of the busy festive recruitment period.”

Alongside seasonal recruitment, Jobcentre teams are encouraging employers to make use of the Government's Youth Guarantee and Youth Jobs Grant initiatives to help attract and develop young talent.

The new grant offers eligible employers up to £3,000 when recruiting qualifying young people, helping businesses invest in future talent while creating valuable employment opportunities for young people in the Harrogate district.
