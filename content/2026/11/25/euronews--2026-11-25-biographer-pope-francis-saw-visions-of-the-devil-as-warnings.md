The late Pope Francis claimed he saw the devil three times during his lifetime, in extracts from his private diary quoted in a new book. "He looked at me with flaming eyes... more intense than the previous one. They were like flashes of fire," he wrote.

In the diary, compiled between 1992 and 2024, he also describes visions of hell and reveals a deeply troubled spiritual life behind the public image of a joyful, reform-minded pontiff.

Austen Ivereigh, a Catholic commentator and biographer of Pope Francis, told Euronews that Francis understood the encounters as warnings. He said they tended to come at times of strain, when Francis felt tempted to rely on his own strength and saw the experiences as a reminder to put his trust in God.
