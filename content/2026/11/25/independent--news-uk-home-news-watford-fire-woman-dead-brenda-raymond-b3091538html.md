An 80-year-old woman who was set on fire as she returned home to her flat has died after spending more than two weeks fighting for her life.

Brenda Raymond suffered catastrophic injuries in the attack at a block of flats in Caractacus Cottage View, Watford, around lunchtime on November 9.

She was taken to hospital for treatment where she remained in a critical condition until she died earlier this morning (Wednesday 25 November).

Ms Raymond was attacked in a first-floor communal area of the building, with detectives believing an accelerant was used.

Evidence at the scene also suggests a flammable liquid was poured through the letterbox of another property in the area, the force added.

Detective Inspector Adam Bridges, from the Major Crime Unit, said: “Our thoughts remain with Brenda’s family at this incredibly sad time.

“Her death is now being treated as murder and we continue to work hard to understand the circumstances of what happened to her.

“While an arrest has previously been made, we are still urgently appealing for anyone with information, no matter how insignificant it may seem, to come forward.

“Even the smallest piece of information could prove vital in helping us identify those responsible.

“If you know anything at all, I urge you to contact police immediately. Please do not assume that someone else has already reported what you know.

“Someone knows who carried out this attack. If that person is you, or if you know who is responsible, now is the time to do the right thing and come forward. Brenda’s family deserve answers, and we are determined to get them.”

![Brenda was found alight in a communal area of a block of flats at Caractacus Cottage View](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/15/01M7DC67Q4DE4SG4K1MV20R0PC.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Detectives investigating the incident are appealing for potential witnesses to come forward with information.

Officers want to speak to anyone who noticed suspicious behaviour near Caractacus Cottage View between 10am and 2pm on Monday 9 November 2026. This includes sightings of individuals wearing face coverings, balaclavas or masks, or anyone fleeing towards nearby footpaths or woodland.

Anyone holding dashcam, CCTV or doorbell camera video footage recorded during these hours is urged to check their devices.

Police also want to hear about people who bought flammable liquids around this date, individuals with unexplained burn marks on their skin or clothing, or witnesses who heard screams, shouting or commotion immediately before emergency services arrived.

Crimestoppers are offering a reward of up to £20,000 for information which leads to the arrest and conviction of those responsible. Further information is available on the Crimestoppers website.
