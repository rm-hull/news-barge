Rescue crews were searching for six people on board a small plane that crashed Tuesday afternoon off the coast of Mississippi.

Crews have so far found three life jackets, a life raft and the cabin of the plane. The plane’s cabin was floating on the water, Juan Ros, a US Coast Guard spokesperson, said.

The plane, a Cessna 210, went down around 3.30pm in the Gulf of Mexico between Pascagoula, Mississippi, and Horn Island, which is part of the Gulf Islands national seashore. There were four adults and two boys, ages eight and 10, on board, authorities said.

The Cessna was en route from New Orleans to Fort Meyers, Florida, according to the flight tracking website FlightAware.

According to the Coast Guard, the plane was descending at a rate of 10,000ft per minute when contact was lost.

The Federal Aviation Administration and the National Transportation Safety Board, which will investigate the crash, said on Tuesday they were aware of it.
