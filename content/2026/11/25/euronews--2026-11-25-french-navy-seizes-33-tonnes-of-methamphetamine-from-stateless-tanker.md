## A boarding team moved in after weeks of surveillance, backed by a helicopter, a Falcon 50 jet and a US Coast Guard patrol plane.

The French navy seized more than 3.3 metric tons of methamphetamine from a tanker sailing without a flag in the Pacific Ocean, the French army said.

The vessel was intercepted on Friday, November 20, following several weeks of surveillance, according to AP.

The operation was coordinated from Maritime information centers in French Polynesia and New Caledonia along with with other international partners.

A boarding team from a French frigate took control of the vessel. Air support came from the frigate's helicopter, a French military Falcon 50 and a maritime patrol plane from the US Coast Guard.
