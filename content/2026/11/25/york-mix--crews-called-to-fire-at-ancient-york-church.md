Firefighters were called to a fire at an ancient York church this afternoon (Wednesday).

Crews from York and Acomb went to the Holy Trinity Church on Micklegate at 12.12pm.

Smoke had been spotted coming out of the building.

Officers at the scene said the fire was inside the church. No one was hurt.

An investigation into the cause is now getting underway.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The fire was out on arrival of crews.

“Crews used positive pressure ventilation, thermal imaging camera and knapsack sprayers.

“The incident was left in the hands of police. Cause believed deliberate.”

Originally founded as Micklegate Priory, a Benedictine monastery, in 1089, the church dates from the 1100s, with additions in the 13th and 14th centuries.

It includes stained glass of national importance.
