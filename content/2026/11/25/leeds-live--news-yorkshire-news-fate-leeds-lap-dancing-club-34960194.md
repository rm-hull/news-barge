# Leeds lap dancing club learns fate after CCTV caught dancer 'simulating sex act'

## The decision was made following a further inspection

A lap dancing club has been permitted to continue operating after its new management tackled violations of its licence conditions.

Liberte, on York Place, has been granted a renewed premises licence by Leeds City Council following an inspection which confirmed procedures were now being adhered to.

The council said the venue had closed after its previous operator, TRL Promotions, ceased trading in April this year.

On November 6 it was reopened by Tokyo Industries Ultimate Ltd, which already operates the Whiskey Down lap dancing club on Crown Street.

A licensing hearing heard that CCTV footage captured in private booths on November 13 was reviewed during a subsequent visit by the council and police.

A report to the hearing said: "At approximately 03.04 hours, a performer was observed making physical contact with a customer and simulating a sexual act.

"At approximately 03.09 hours, a second performer in a separate booth was observed touching a customer and gyrating whilst maintaining physical contact."

Issues were flagged concerning performer conduct, CCTV monitoring, record keeping and the provision of panic alarms.

However, the hearing at Leeds Civic Hall was informed that the council withdrew its objections to renewal and transfer of the licence after a further inspection.

Marcus Bentley, Tokyo Industries operations manager, said: "We expect some teething issues. We expect to fix those issues. We had a meeting yesterday with licensing. All our records were up to date."

Council enforcement officer Jane Wood confirmed she would maintain ongoing oversight of performers' behaviour.

She said: "I went back and I was happy to see everything was in order. We have agreed that I will go back out and continue to assess the CCTV."

Back in 2013, the council brought in stricter regulations governing the number of city centre lap dancing venues and where they could be situated.

Under licensing requirements, strip clubs are obliged to apply for the renewal of their sexual entertainment venue licences annually.

The licence transfer and renewal application was approved by councillors at a hearing held at Leeds Civic Hall.
