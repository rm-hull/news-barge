A shoplifter who targeted the same shop in York 15 times in three months for small amounts has been given a suspended prison sentence.

On each occasion, Conal Hawes, 23, stole goods worth no more than £15 from the Jorvik Service Station on Lawrence Street, York.

On one day, August 17, he raided the shop three times and got away with items, mostly alcohol, worth a total of £22.

During the 15 raids from June 2 to August 30 he stole items worth a total of £144.94.

Then on October 6, he raided a Savers store in York and got away with items worth £84.92, bringing the total stolen in all 16 thefts to £229.86.

Conal Hawes, 23, of James Street, central York, didn’t turn up to his sentencing hearing at York Magistrates Court and a warrant was issued for his arrest.

Within hours, police arrested him in Humberside and sent him in custody to Hull Magistrates Court where he was given an eight-week prison sentence suspended for 12 months.

He pleaded guilty to 16 thefts and one failure to attend court.
