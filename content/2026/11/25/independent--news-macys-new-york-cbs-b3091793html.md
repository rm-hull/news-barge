The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed as powerful winds are expected to bear down on New York during the annual event, the company said Wednesday.

In a statement to CBS, Macy’s said the parade would now be held on Saturday, citing conversations with the city and concerns over the safety of participants and spectators.
