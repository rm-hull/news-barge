# The CCTV that helped convict a Sydney policeman of double murder

A former Sydney policeman, Beau Lamarre, has been found guilty of murdering his ex-partner, Jesse Baird, and Baird's new partner Luke Davies, in a trial that shocked Australia.

More than 14,000 hours of CCTV footage was seized by police as evidence. The BBC's Helen Livingstone explains the key moments captured on camera that helped convict Lamarre of double murder.
