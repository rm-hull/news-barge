![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: “PIP benefits 'not fit for purpose' - but review offers no answers to Britain's soaring welfare bill”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/56e8/live/d4341a30-e853-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

Many of the papers on Wednesday are leading on the findings of a government-commissioned review into personal independence payment (Pip). The i Paper says that, though "long-awaited", the review "offers no answers to Britain's soaring welfare bill". According to the paper, Disability Minister Sir Stephen Timms has said the "unfit" system "requires a fundamental reset". It also notes that Pip is forecast to cost around £40bn a year by the end of the decade.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: “Benefits bill to rise under plan”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/301d/live/2ad30520-e856-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

"Benefits bill to rise under plan" declares the Times, likewise noting that the report is "under fire" for failing to identify any savings. The paper says it largely contradicts a separate review by Alan Milburn, the former Labour health secretary, which recommended cutting cash benefits for under-25s in favour of giving them employment support. The government has said it will consider the Timms review alongside Milburn's recommendations, with its final proposals set out early next year.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: “Labour: Make it easier to claim benefits”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/1ac1/live/da44af20-e853-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The Daily Telegraph says Labour MPs are urging Prime Minister Andy Burnham "to make it easier" to claim sickness and disability benefits. The paper says he is expected to decide which elements of the two reviews to accept early next year, but will face a "rift" within his party on how to tackle the "mounting welfare bill". It adds that political opponents are warning that the system is being abused by people "claiming cash for low-level conditions such as ADHD and anxiety".

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: “Wills 'burglar' an asylum sneaker”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/34f5/live/67f6d590-e855-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The Sun reports claims that the two men arrested last night for allegedly breaking into the Norfolk home of the Prince and Princess of Wales are "Albanian nationals", and that one is an "illegal migrant who previously claimed asylum". "Break-in at Royals' retreat", it says.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: “Kate & Wills in security scare!”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/2ef3/live/e032c7f0-e853-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"Kate & Wills in security scare!" is the Daily Star's headline, referring to the arrests. The paper says King Charles visited Anmer Hall following the incident.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: “Wills & Kate's shocking security scare”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/d7b9/live/14599c70-e854-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Daily Mirror says the "shocking security scare" has "sparked concern", with a former royal protection chief telling the paper that he expects a "full review". It notes that the property was unoccupied when the alleged incident occurred.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: “Met police is 'still racist, misogynistic and homophobic'”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/c1e8/live/4e72e4a0-e856-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Independent focuses on a new review into the Metropolitan Police, which said the institution is "still racist, misogynistic and homophobic". The paper says the independent report was carried out by Dr Gillian Fairfield, and comes just three years after a "damning report into Britain's largest force".

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: “Met still 'institutionally racist, misogynistic and homophobic'”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/624/cpsprodpb/6c88/live/01ec8250-e854-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The same headline leads the Guardian, which adds that the 389-page review was commissioned by the Mayor of London, Sadiq Khan. It found that "fundamental systemic issues" and "institutional defensiveness" still exist in the Met, which it said has struggled to "acknowledge racism as a structural problem". The paper says the Met's commissioner, Mark Rowley, accepted the review's recommendations but said he refused to be drawn into "what I think is a politicised debate about certain labels".

![The headline on the front page of the Financial Times reads: “MoD bought fake explosives for Kyiv from part-time American policeman”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/ffcc/live/edb46c30-e853-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The Financial Times reports that the Ministry of Defence bought tonnes of fake explosives for Ukraine in a deal worth "millions" spanning several years. The paper says a formerly bankrupt part-time American policeman won contracts to sell the MoD C4 explosives, but an investigation has found that a key ingredient was missing from his product. The findings expose "serious weaknesses in the UK's fast-tracked defence procurement", the paper says. Jeremy Pocklington, the MoD's permanent secretary, told the House of Commons defence select committee he would look into the matter and write to MPs.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: “TikTok PM who demeans No. 10”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/c87e/live/33ab5780-e854-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"TikTok PM who demeans No. 10" declares the Daily Mail. The paper says Burnham has been accused of "gross disrespect" to Downing Street, after he invited influencers to No 10. The Prime Minister's official spokesman told the paper the video in question was "clearly not acceptable" and argued that content creators were "responsible for what they choose to post".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: “Brexit reset turns Britain into a 'vassal state'”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/7c67/live/f3d5ca00-e853-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The Daily Express carries a warning from Reform UK leader Nigel Farage, who says the prime minister's Brexit reset "might backfire on Labour at the ballot box". Farage has told the paper that Burnham's Brussels deal "turns the UK into a 'vassal state' of the EU", insisting that voters "do not back a return to the bloc". Burnham has previously said new agreements with the EU are "pragmatic" and that it would be "unthinkable" for the UK to rejoin the bloc without a referendum.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: “A billion scam calls blocked”.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/7d08/live/fa095040-e853-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"A billion scam calls blocked" reads the Metro, which reports that 1.3 billion scam calls and text have been blocked from Britons in just 12 months. It says that industrial-scale scam centres now accounts for 70% of cons in the UK.

Many of the papers react to the government's review of the main disability benefit in England and Wales - the personal independence payment (Pip). The Times says the welfare bill would rise under the plan, external - which it says will make claims easier. The i Paper newspaper notes that Pip is forecast to cost about £40bn a year, external by the end of the decade.

The Daily Telegraph's editorial believes the report "reflects ministers' priorities, external rather than those of the hard-pressed taxpayer". The Daily Mirror's commentary calls on the government, external to use the review as an opportunity to "restore humanity, dignity and compassion to the system".

The Daily Express highlights a warning from the Unite union that support to help with rising energy bills from January "won't touch the sides"., external The campaign group SilverVoices has told the paper that Andy Burnham has "failed to recognise the lethal consequences of unheated homes on older people's health". Ministers say the financial help on offer will give millions of people "breathing space with energy bills".

The Guardian highlights the Mayor of London's response to a review that has found the Metropolitan Police is still "institutionally racist, misogynistic and homophobic"., external Lord Khan calls on the commissioner of the force to "provide Londoners with the police service they deserve". But Sir Mark Rowley said the review had "recognised the scale and pace of change" in the Met.

The Sun reports claims that the two men arrested last night for allegedly breaking into the Norfolk home of the Prince and Princess of Wales are "Albanian nationals", external, and that one is an "illegal migrant who previously claimed asylum". The paper says the King was staying at Sandringham House, just 3 miles (4.8km) away, at the time of the suspected break in.

"TikTok PM who demeans Number 10" is the headline on the front of the Daily Mail. It says Andy Burnham has been accused of "pandering to social media influencers", external, who were allowed to visit and film in Downing Street. A spokesperson for Burnham defended the reception for content creators, saying they reach millions of people.

"Britain in virgin territory", declares the Times, in its coverage of a study that suggests young people are having less sex., external The paper highlights "major shifts in British society" - including increased use of smartphones and higher rates of anxiety and depression. "Is sex getting a bit old?", asks the Guardian.
