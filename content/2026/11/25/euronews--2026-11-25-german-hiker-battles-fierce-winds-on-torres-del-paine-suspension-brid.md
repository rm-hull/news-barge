## Violent gusts of wind set a suspension bridge swaying in Chile's Torres del Paine National Park. A fellow hiker captures the moment the German tourist struggled to keep his balance.

What started as a hiking adventure turned into a nerve-racking experience for German tourist Henry Thomas. During his seven-day tour on the O Circuit in Torres del Paine National Park in the south of **Chile**, he was caught in a fierce storm while on a suspension bridge, as reported by the New York Post.

According to the official park administration (CONAF), the bridges in this area stand at heights of more than 20 metres. The famous 52-metre-long Las Guardas Bridge spans a gorge that is about 45 metres deep.

Powerful gusts of wind suddenly caused the narrow pedestrian bridge to sway violently. Thomas, who was carrying a rucksack weighing around 18 kilograms, had to brace himself against the bridge’s movement so as not to lose his balance.

His girlfriend had safely crossed the bridge just a few moments earlier. For Thomas, however, the crossing became a real challenge.

## Video shows struggle against the gusts of wind

Footage of the incident shows the **hiker** taking a wide stance on the swaying structure. He clings to the side wire ropes with both hands while the strong gusts buffet the bridge.

The video was recorded by fellow hiker Martin Esteban Gonzalez Lopez. He had met the couple during the trek and captured the dangerous moment with his camera.

Thomas tried to keep his body position as stable as possible and to withstand the bridge’s movement and only when the strongest gusts subsided did the situation seem to ease.

Torres del Paine National Park is one of the best-known hiking regions in South America.

At the same time, the area in Patagonia is known for its changeable and sometimes extreme weather conditions. Particularly strong winds can make hikes more difficult and become an additional danger on exposed trails and bridges.
