## EU and Western Balkans leaders are meeting in Brussels today to discuss enlargement, regional cooperation and how to strengthen security and defence.

## On today’s show

**Montenegro’s President Jakov Milatović wants his country to sign an EU accession treaty in the first half of 2027 and join the bloc in 2028.** We ask whether he's secured a firm commitment from EU leaders and whether judicial reforms, the fight against corruption and organised crime, and unresolved disputes with Croatia could delay Montenegro’s bid.

The interview comes as leaders gather for the **EU-Western Balkans summit in Brussels.** European leaders are expected to reaffirm the EU’s commitment to bringing the region into the bloc and urge Western Balkan countries to speed up reforms.

We also look at **Hungarian Prime Minister Péter Magyar’s visit to western Ukraine**, where he hailed a "fresh start" in relations and discussed closer trade and transport links with President Volodymyr Zelensky.

At COP31, the EU refused to back a deal that left out fossil fuels. Yet European governments are signing gas contracts that could keep them reliant on fossil fuels for decades. **We ask Szymon Kardaś, co-author of a new report from the European Council on Foreign Relations**, what this means for the EU’s climate goals.

And in newly released diaries, the late Pope Francis describes three encounters with the Devil and a spiritual crisis in 2021 and 2022. **His biographer Austen Ivereigh joins us to discuss how to interpret those accounts** and what they reveal about Francis’s private life.

In the headlines, Palestinian officials say **Israeli air strikes killed seven people in Gaza**, including the head of Hamas’s internal security service. And Germany’s Federal Criminal Police Office says the country recorded its** highest number of domestic violence victims** in 2025.
