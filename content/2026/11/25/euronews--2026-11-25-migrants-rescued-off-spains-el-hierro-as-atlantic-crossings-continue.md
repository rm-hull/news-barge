Spanish rescuers have assisted groups of migrants arriving by boat at La Restinga, on the island of El Hierro, in the Canary Islands.

Spain's Maritime Rescue Service, Salvamento Marítimo, escorted the boats to port, where Red Cross teams helped those arriving ashore.

El Hierro, home to around 11,400 people, has become a major entry point for migrants attempting to reach Europe from West Africa.

Local authorities declared a state of emergency in November after thousands of people arrived in a short period, putting pressure on the island's reception facilities, healthcare system and other public services.
