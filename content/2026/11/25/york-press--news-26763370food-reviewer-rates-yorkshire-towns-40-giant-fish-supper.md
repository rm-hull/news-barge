A fish and chips feast has caught the attention of a popular YouTube reviewer.

Danny Malin, host of the YouTube channel Danny v Food, visited Catch 55 in Scarborough to try the restaurant’s colossal £40 fish supper, which features a giant cod fillet, chips, mushy peas, bread and butter, curry sauce, and gravy.

Although he didn’t take on the restaurant’s food challenge, Danny wanted to see whether the meal lived up to its reputation as one of the largest in the country.

He said: "Apparently, it's one of the biggest bits of fish in the UK.

"There's about four or five fish and chip shops now all claiming to have the biggest piece of fish.

"There's only so big you can get the fish, right? And so much you can fry it."

## Beef dripping praise

Staff at the restaurant told him the giant meal must be pre-ordered and costs £40.

When the dish arrived, Danny was impressed by its size, describing it as "a whopper" before turning his attention to the chips and sides.

He was particularly taken with the chips.

Danny said: "They're cooked in beef dripping.

"That's why they're so crispy and crunchy.

"I love the fact that some of them are like chips, some are like roast tats."

## Sauces win his approval

Danny also praised the inclusion of bread and butter, calling it a traditional touch that’s becoming increasingly rare.

He described the curry sauce as "a naughty little number" and said it was "fruity, tangy" and "absolutely delicious."

The gravy also earned his approval.

He said: "That's a beautiful fish and chip shop gravy.

"It's exactly how it should be."

## How did he rate the fish?

When he finally turned to the fish, Danny was equally impressed.

He said: "What a piece of fish that is.

"That looks absolutely class.

"It's cooked well.

"The batter is cooked really well.

"The fish looks like it's cooked to perfection."

Despite the size of the fillet, he said it remained moist and flaky.

He said: "It's really hard to cook these big bits of fish.

"The cod is cooked to perfection."

## What did he rate the meal?

After finishing the meal, Danny concluded that it offered good value for money and could easily serve multiple people.

He said: "I think this will definitely feed a family of four.

"I think it's an absolute steal."

The reviewer awarded the meal a perfect score.

He said: "The fish and chips is absolutely fantastic.

"If you've not been, come down, try it out.

"For me guys, it's a solid 10 without a shadow of a doubt."
