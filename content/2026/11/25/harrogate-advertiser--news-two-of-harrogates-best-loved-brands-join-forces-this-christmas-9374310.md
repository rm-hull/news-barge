Two iconic Harrogate brands are joining forces for the first time to showcase their pride in their town with a distinctive gift collection for the Christmas season.

Slingsby Gin and Mama Doreen’s Emporium have created a special collection of Christmas bundles and seasonal treats, pairing Slingsby’s spirits with Mama Doreen’s much-loved sweet creations.

This new partnership will celebrate the best of Harrogate, showcasing the quality of local producers and creating a unique collection for the Christmas season.

From gin-inspired confectionery and festive bakes to carefully selected local products, each bundle offers different combinations of Slingsby Gin flavours and specially chosen treats for a memorable festive gift.

Chris Williams, Director of Slingsby Gin, said: “Slingsby has strong roots in Harrogate, and we're proud to be part of a town with such a wealth of independent businesses and talented local producers.

“Our collaboration with Mama Doreen’s brings together two Harrogate brands with a shared appreciation for quality, craftsmanship and the pleasure of sharing good food and drink with friends and family.”

Mama Doreen’s brings a selection of its festive favourites to the collaboration, including Gooseberry Gin-flavoured Fudge, Slingsby Chocolate Marmalade Fudge, Slingsby Rhubarb Gin and Ginger Jam, Slingsby Marmalade Cake and Slingsby Blackberry Gin Mince Pies.

Returning Slingsby favourites also feature, including Yorkshire Rhubarb Gin Flavoured Milk Chocolate and Marmalade Gin Flavoured Dark Chocolate, created in collaboration with York-based artisan chocolatier Choc Affair, alongside marmalade made with local producer Bracken Hill Farm, whose marmalade is also used in Slingsby Marmalade Gin.

Jessica Wyatt, owner and founder of Mama Doreen’s Emporium, said: “Christmas is one of our favourite times of year at Mama Doreen’s, when the shop really comes to life with our festive displays and seasonal treats.

“Working with Slingsby has given us the opportunity to build on that with a collection of gin-inspired creations that bring something a little different to the season.”
