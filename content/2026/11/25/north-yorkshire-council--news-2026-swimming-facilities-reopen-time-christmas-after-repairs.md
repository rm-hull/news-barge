Swimming facilities in Richmond are set to reopen in time for Christmas following the completion of major repair and refurbishment works.

The pools at Active North Yorkshire Richmond closed in April last year after part of the ceiling collapsed.

Since then, we have invested £1.65 million to secure the future of the facility, following overwhelming public support for keeping the pools open.

The work has included replacing the ceiling, carrying out extensive mechanical and electrical repairs, upgrading the centre’s heating system, and installing new lighting around the pool and in the café.

The project is now in its final stages, with safety checks and water testing underway ahead of the reopening on Monday, 7 December.

Executive member for leisure, Cllr Simon Myers, said: “This is fantastic news for Richmond and comes just in time for Christmas.

“Residents made it clear from the start how much these pools mean to them, so we’re delighted to be reopening the doors after such a significant investment in the facility.

“This has been a very complex project, and like many major building schemes, there have been some unexpected issues along the way, including the discovery of sealed asbestos in the roof space.

“However, rather than simply dealing with the repairs, we have been adaptable and took the opportunity to make further improvements to the centre, including upgraded lighting and heating systems, helping to create a better experience for customers.”

Councillors approved the repair scheme in October 2025. More than half of those surveyed said they used the site at least once a week, while 91 per cent said it was either “very important” or “moderately important” that the pools were saved.

Final preparations are now underway, with the Active North Yorkshire team filling and testing the pools, carrying out a deep clean, updating timetables and completing refresher training for staff ahead of reopening.

The café is expected to reopen shortly afterwards, early next year, once the final finishing touches have been completed, while children’s Learn to Swim lessons will return in January next year, with bookings opening from December 7.

The programme has been managed by the council and has involved a number of consultants and contractors, including North Yorkshire Highways and Align.

To mark the reopening, customers will be able to take advantage of a special welcome-back offer, allowing them to join now and pay nothing until January.

Customers can also claim one free swimming session for up to four people, valid until the end of January, giving both new and returning visitors the chance to enjoy the improved facilities.

A free gym and health suite session will also be available until the end of January.
