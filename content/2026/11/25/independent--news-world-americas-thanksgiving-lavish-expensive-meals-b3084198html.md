Thanksgiving dinner doesn't have to break the bank — but for those who want to splurge (or show off) a bit for friends and family, there are some interesting and pricey options out there for an elevated table.

While grocery chains like Meijer and Aldi are offering Thanksgiving meal deals for around 10 people for less than $40, those looking to splurge can spend hundreds, or even thousands, on their holiday spread.

From a $270 turkey-shaped cake, to a bird dubbed the “Rolls-Royce of Turkeys,” *The Independent* has rounded up some of the most luxurious Thanksgiving dishes and dining experiences money can buy.

## Mains

Last year, Forbes nicknamed the KellyBronze Turkey the “Rolls-Royce of turkeys” - and its price tag certainly reflects that. A 32-pound bird can cost as much as $500, about $14 to $15 per pound, while smaller, 14-pound turkeys start at $207.

![Dubbed the ‘Rolls-Royce of turkeys,’ KellyBronze’s free-range, certified organic birds can cost up to $500 and come pre-brined with autumn flavors like apple, lemon, garlic and rosemary](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/19/01/KellyBronze-Thanksgiving-turkey.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The hefty price comes down to how the birds are raised and prepared. KellyBronze turkeys take more than six months to raise because they’re a slower-growing heritage breed. Once the birds are slaughtered, they’re dry-plucked and hung for at least a week to develop their flavor and crispier skin. The birds are raised in Crozet, Virginia, using traditional methods that originated in Essex, England.

Even the smaller options aren't cheap. The company recommends its 10-pound Thanksgiving Whole Turkey, priced at $157.50 and intended to feed 10 to 12 people.

For comparison, Aldi's $39 Thanksgiving bundle feeds 10 and includes a 16-pound turkey, stuffing, sweet potatoes, rolls, cranberry sauce, vegetables and pumpkin pie ingredients.

Another upscale option is the Willie Bird Fresh Free-Range Turkey, sold through Williams-Sonoma. A 12- to 14-pound bird costs $149.95, while a 16- to 18-pound turkey goes for $199.95. The Northern California-raised birds are free-range and certified organic. They're also pre-brined with a mix of autumn flavors, including apple, lemon, garlic and rosemary.

## Side dishes

Even the most ordinary of Thanksgiving sides can get an expensive makeover. Think fresh black truffles, imported Gruyère and other ingredients that can turn a basic potato dish into a luxury item.

London-based food supplier Fine Food Specialist has a recipe for Autumn Black Truffle Dauphinoise, an upscale take on scalloped potatoes. The dish layers thinly sliced potatoes with garlic-infused cream, butter, black truffle butter and fresh black truffle shavings. The fresh truffles are the biggest expense. Marky’s sells Italian Burgundy truffles online for about $50 per ounce, with a 1.3-ounce truffle costing $65.

For something smoky and cheesy, food smoker company Bradley Smoker offers a Smoked Potato Fennel Gratin. The recipe combines Yukon Gold potatoes with caramelized fennel, onions, heavy cream and melted Gruyère before smoking everything over applewood. Imported Gruyère is the priciest ingredient, and typically costs $15 to $31 per pound at U.S. retailers, with wholesale blocks averaging about $17.56 per pound, depending on the brand and aging, according to Wilson Cheese and Restaurant Depot.

## Desserts

Thanksgiving desserts aren't being left out of the luxury treatment.

Celebrity baker Duff Goldman is selling the Lady Showbird Turkey Cake through Goldbelly for $270. Made at his Charm City Cakes bakery, the nearly 5-pound cake features pumpkin layers, vanilla buttercream and tart cranberry filling, all decorated to look like a dressed-up Thanksgiving turkey.

It comes with a silver serving platter, feeds 10 to 12 people and ships frozen, ready to thaw before serving.

![Celebrity baker Duff Goldman is selling a nearly 5-pound, turkey-shaped Thanksgiving cake featuring pumpkin, vanilla buttercream and cranberry filling for $270 through Goldbelly](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/19/46/Turkey_Cake_Packaging_2_1x1.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Pie lovers can also spend a pretty penny on dessert. New York baker Michelle Kogan is charging $150 for a 12-inch Thanksgiving pie at her Queens bakery, Mishie's Sunnyside. The elaborate creation features a Cinnamon Toast Crunch crust, apple custard filling swirled with apple cider caramel, gold leaf and sour cream rosettes. *New York Magazine* named it the best pie to order this Thanksgiving.

Kogan said she created the pie to get people excited about the traditional dessert again, arguing that the price reflects the labor, ingredients and costs involved.

“If you really break down the numbers, it’s not that crazy,” she told the outlet.

## Get it catered

For those who would rather skip the cooking altogether, a fancy Thanksgiving dinner can cost your family hundreds, if not thousands, of dollars.

At Flagler Steakhouse in Palm Beach, Florida, diners can enjoy a three-course Thanksgiving meal for $245 per person. The menu includes holiday staples such as Butter-Basted Tom Turkey and Pumpkin Pie, alongside steakhouse favorites like Surf & Turf and Bone-in Filet.

The Breakers, also in Palm Beach, is hosting a Thanksgiving buffet in its grand Ponce de Leon Ballroom for $295 per person. Guests can fill their plates with an extensive holiday spread surrounded by festive harvest decorations.

Prefer to host at home? A full Thanksgiving dinner from Williams-Sonoma can run well over $1,000 for 13 to 14 people.

But even those prices are modest compared with what some celebrity families reportedly spend. The Kardashian-Jenner family Thanksgiving celebrations could cost anywhere from $150,000 to $500,000 - with particularly elaborate gatherings hosted by Kim K or Kris Jenner potentially topping $1 million, event and brand strategist Stacy Jones told *The Mirror* last year.

She pointed out the costs go well beyond food and decorations. Catering, staff, entertainment and production all add up, while beauty teams, photographers, videographers and social media services alone could tack on another $25,000 to $75,000 or more.
