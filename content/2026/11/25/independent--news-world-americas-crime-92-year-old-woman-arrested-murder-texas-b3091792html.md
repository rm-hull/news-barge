More than 50 years after a five-year-old Texas boy fell into a coma and died, police have arrested his 92-year-old mother and charged her with his murder.

Harriet Evelyn Hall was indicted Saturday by a grand jury in Bexar County, Texas, on a murder charge in the death of her son, Ronald Hall, on November 25, 1969, according to court records obtained by News 4 San Antonio.

The indictment alleges that Harriet Hall killed the child by striking him with her hand and a stick, tying his hands and feet and withholding food and nutrition. The case is classified as family violence, according to the report.

Police in Universal City, about 15 miles northeast of San Antonio, told KENS5 that Harriet Hall abused her son for four years before he fell into a coma and died.

The boy’s father was reportedly stationed at Randolph Air Force Base and the family was living in Universal City’s Aviation District.

![Harriet Hall was arrested and charged with the murder of her son, Ronald Hall, in 1969, after the case went unsolved for nearly six decades](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/25/21/59/Harriet-Hall-arrest.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Ronald Hall’s brother, James Randall Hall, is also reportedly listed as a victim in the case. He has provided information to authorities in the years since. It was not clear why it took so long to indict Harriet Hall.

Investigators said that Harriet Hall provided varying accounts of what had happened to her son, including that he was hurt in a fall. However, other times she claimed she could not remember what happened to him.

At the time of his death, Harriet Hall was living in the San Antonio area. However, she later moved to Waco, about 180 miles north, where she was arrested Tuesday, according to KENS5.

The 92-year-old remains in custody Wednesday with her bail set at $500,000, according to the *San Antonio Express-News.*

*The Independent* has contacted Universal City police and the Bexar County Sheriff’s Office for more information on the case.
