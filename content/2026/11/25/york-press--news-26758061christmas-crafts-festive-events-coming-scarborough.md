From behind-the-scenes tours to festive arts and crafts, Scarborough has a packed schedule of events to enjoy this December.

### Lunchtime Talk: Entertainment – Fun, Culture and Spectacle

Discover how 400 years of seaside fun shaped Scarborough’s identity at this engaging lunchtime talk.

The Collections Team will explore the 'Dive In' exhibition and share stories from the town’s vibrant past.

Tickets are £3 for Annual Pass Holders and £8 for non-holders. December 1, 12–12.30pm at Scarborough Art Gallery.

### Christmas at Woodend – Art Club

Enjoy a fun, festive morning of creativity at Scarborough Art Gallery. All materials are provided, and the session is free. December 21, 10am to 12noon.

### Festive Donkey Trail

Hunt for the festive donkey hiding across the Rotunda Museum, Scarborough Art Gallery and Woodend Gallery.

Complete the trail, collect your sticker and uncover the Christmas clue.

Runs until December 31 during venue opening times.

For further information about other events, visit the museum's website.
