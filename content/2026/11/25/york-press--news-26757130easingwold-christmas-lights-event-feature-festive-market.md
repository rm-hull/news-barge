## Easingwold council welcomes festive market for Christmas lights switch-on

Dutch Pancakes *(Image: Little Bird Made)*

Easingwold is set to shine with a festive evening of lights, markets, and community spirit.

The town’s annual Christmas Light Switch-On will take place on Saturday, November 28, from 3pm to 8pm in the Market Place.

The event will feature the St Leonards Hospice ‘Light Up A Life’ service and the Little Bird Made Artisan Market.

Organised in partnership with Easingwold Town Council, the evening promises entertainment, shopping, and a chance to support small businesses.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "Our evening market for the Easingwold Light Switch-On is always one of the highlights of our winter calendar.

"There is nothing quite like seeing the Market Place lit up for Christmas while the community comes together to support local independent traders."

Visitors can browse stalls from Yorkshire makers and bakers offering handmade gifts, seasonal treats, and street food.

Jane Bentley, of Easingwold Town Council, said: "The Christmas Light Switch-On is a cherished tradition in Easingwold, and having the Little Bird Artisan Market alongside the festivities makes the evening even more special."

The final Little Bird Made Artisan Market of 2026 will return for its final visit of 2026 on Saturday, December 12, from 9am to 2pm.
