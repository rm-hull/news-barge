# Moment Chinese space mission lifts off with first Pakistan astronaut aboard

China launched its Shenzhou-24 space mission on Wednesday to bring three astronauts to its Tiangong space station.

It was a landmark moment as astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali became the first foreign national to board the Tiangong space station.

The mission is expected to last for several days before he returns to Earth.
