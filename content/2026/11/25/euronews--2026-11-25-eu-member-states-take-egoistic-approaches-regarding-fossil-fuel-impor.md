Szymon Kardaś, an energy expert at the European Council on Foreign Relations (ECFR), says the EU lacks “clear targets for phasing out fossil fuels”, whether indicative or legally binding.

He argues that stronger energy cooperation is essential if the European Union is to become fossil fuel-free, warning that member states tend to act “individually” and “egoistically” when addressing immediate challenges.

Kardaś calls for “more coordination” and a “collective approach” to accelerate the phase-out of fossil fuels across the bloc.
