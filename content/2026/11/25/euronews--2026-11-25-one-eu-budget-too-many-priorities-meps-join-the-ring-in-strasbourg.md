## In this week's edition of our show, MEPs Marcos Ros (S&D) and Ondřej Kolář (EPP) debate the future of the EU's budget. Is a deal in sight?

The European Commission is proposing a new €45 billion loan to Ukraine as the country continues to face Russia’s war. But who ultimately bears the cost and how will the money be financed?

With the EU debating the scale and conditions of its long-term commitment, the proposal raises difficult questions about European solidarity, financial responsibility and the path towards peace.

At the same time, the stakes for the EU budget have risen sharply as the German chancellor Friedrich Merz tied the deal to approving the €45 billion loan for Kyiv.

EU lawmakers Marcos Ros (S&D) from Spain and Ondřej Kolář (EPP) from Czechia, discuss whether the EU can continue its aid to Ukraine without cutting funds from other areas such as the EU's cohesion policy.

Is a deal on the EU budget in sight? And how will the EU continue its support to Ukraine?
