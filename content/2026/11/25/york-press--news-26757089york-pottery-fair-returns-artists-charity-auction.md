## Renowned potters to showcase their work at York Christmas fair

Christmas lights by Nadine Blakemore *(Image: York Potters Fair)*

Renowned potters will showcase their creations at York Potters Fair.

The anticipated York Potters Fair will unfold at Southlands Methodist Church on December 5th, from 10am to 4pm.

Sponsored by the Northern Potters Association, the event is a well-established feature of York’s Christmas celebrations.

Among those exhibiting are Lyn and Gerry Grant from Fangfoss Pottery, noted for their 50 years of crafting expertise, and Sarah Schiewe, who is recognised for her creative ceramic Easter trail in York and Haxby.

The fair will also welcome newcomers in the pottery scene, offering them an opportunity to present their work.

Held at Southlands Church, a key artistic hub in York since 2018, the event is not just a showcase of pottery talent but also a fundraiser.

It seeks to gather funds for local Methodist Churches and Refugee Action York through a silent auction, where potters donate their work.

The York Potters Fair is a free-entry event, opening its doors to all, including fans of the Great Pottery Throwdown, which has sparked a rise in pottery enthusiasts.

A spokesperson for the event said: "Potters love coming together and showing their work so we hope you will come and see us."

The fair promises an engaging day out for both pottery enthusiasts and community supporters, set against the backdrop of York’s festive atmosphere.
