A long-standing North Yorkshire petrol station has been sold as part of a family’s retirement plans.

Vale Motors in Pickering was sold by Paul and Julie Ibbotson through business property adviser Christie & Co after decades of ownership.

The site includes a busy forecourt, convenience store, workshop and drive-in car wash, and has now been acquired by Selby-based Jos. Richardson & Son Ltd, a family-run fuel and convenience retailer with an established network across Yorkshire and the Humber.

Alex Ibbotson, son of the former owners, said: "We are delighted to announce the successful completion of the sale of the Vale Motors petrol station, marking the end of a long chapter in our ownership.

"We would like to extend our sincere thanks to our loyal customers, dedicated staff and trusted suppliers for their support over the years.

"We are confident that the business will continue to thrive under Joseph and his team, and it will remain an important service for local residents and motorists.

"We wish the new owners every success for the future and thank everyone who has been part of the station's journey."

Located on the A170/A169 route, the forecourt serves residents, commuters and visitors travelling through the North York Moors National Park and surrounding areas.

Vale Motors recorded annual fuel volumes of more than two million litres, and the accompanying store sells a range of confectionery, car care, soft drinks, coffee and sandwiches.

Joseph Richardson, managing director of Jos. Richardson & Son Ltd, said: "We chose the site because of its strategic fit with our existing network.

"We already operate Middleton Service Station in Pickering and our New-to-Industry development, Eden Service Station in Malton, which opened in spring 2025.

"Vale Motors is a strong addition to our growing North Yorkshire portfolio and further strengthens our presence in the area.

"We hope to improve the shop over the coming months and look forward to making other additional improvements across the site."

The sales process was managed by Andrew Birnie, Director - Retail & Leisure at Christie & Co.

Mr Birnie said: "I am really pleased to have sold Vale Motors on behalf of our clients, the Ibbotson family, who for the senior members can now enjoy a well-earned retirement.

"It was a pleasure to act on behalf of the vendors, and I would like to thank both parties for their cooperation throughout the transaction.

"We wish Joseph and his team every success with the business going forward.

"This sale further demonstrates continued demand for established forecourt businesses across the North of England, particularly those offering multiple revenue streams including fuel, convenience retail, car wash facilities and those with scope for improvement."

The purchase price was not disclosed, but the freehold was marketed at £1.8 million.
