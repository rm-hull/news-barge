A murderer, who claimed to be disabled, was filmed walking as he and his daughter moved his victim’s body in his wheelchair.

John Anslow, 65, of Stour Street, West Bromwich, was jailed for life with a minimum term of 26 years at Worcester Crown Court on Wednesday after he was found guilty in September of strangling 36-year-old mother Chereiss Bailey at his home.

His daughter Hayley-Marie Ferguson, a 42-year-old mother of five from Heronville Road, West Bromwich, was jailed for six years for assisting an offender and preventing a lawful burial.

Video footage released by West Midlands Police following their sentencing showed Anslow using a wheelchair as he boarded a bus on 27 September last year after meeting his victim in the Great Bridge area of the West Midlands.

![Handout screengrab from CCTV issued by West Midlands Police of 65-year-old John Anslow and his daughter Hayley-Marie Ferguson using his wheelchair to transport the body of murder victim Chereiss Bailey](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/14/01M89FC9ZAFNAYF929NK1NA2WM.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Further images showed him being pushed along the street near his home by his victim later the same day.

Anslow then killed Ms Bailey, who suffered a broken jaw and other serious injuries, and recruited his daughter to help him dispose of evidence and the victim’s body.

CCTV cameras captured the defendants walking around 600 metres with Ms Bailey’s body in Anslow’s wheelchair in the early hours of 29 September, as they made their way to a canal where they dumped her body.

Addressing Anslow’s supposed disabilities and his daughter’s use of a crutch during a sentencing hearing on Wednesday, Judge Simon Drew KC said of the male defendant: “I have had no medical evidence placed before me as to your mobility issues or why you need to use a wheelchair.”

![36-year-old Chereiss Bailey whose body was found in a canal off Ryders Green Road, West Bromwich, on October 7 last year](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/08/01M88T3CENN9YN2T4HJ804CK57.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The judge said Ferguson was someone who claimed to have difficulty walking, having used a crutch during her trial, while “bitterly complaining about the pain you were suffering”.

Although he accepted that Ferguson does have a history of back problems, including spinal surgery, the judge said she had much greater strength and range of movement than she was prepared to admit, as evidenced by footage of her striding across the lobby of the court building during her trial.

He said of Anslow in his absence after he refused to attend court: “It is clear that whatever disabilities you led people to believe you have, you are in fact a strong fit and able man who was more than capable of attacking Chereiss and inflicting the brutal injuries she sustained.

![John Anslow using a wheelchair to board a bus on 27 September last year, hours before the murder of Chereiss Bailey](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/15/01M89DYY13HEWK08V7N9FR9F8K.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Hayley Ferguson, you accompanied your father as he pushed Chereiss’s body in the wheelchair.

“You had abandoned your crutch, you were walking freely and you had to run to keep up with him.

“You too are clearly much stronger and fitter than you like to give the impression, and like your father’s wheelchair your crutch appears to be largely a theatrical prop.”

![Hayley-Marie Ferguson was jailed for six years for assisting an offender and preventing a lawful burial](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/08/01M88T3CVFQ2RJP9YEB4PFTMC3.jpg?quality=75&width=640&trim=0%2C0%2C0%2C0&auto=webp)

The judge said of the site where the body was dumped: “There the two of you had to navigate the steps down to the canalside, which must have been difficult and taken considerable physical strength given that you were manhandling a dead body in a wheelchair.

“The two of you then pushed Chereiss’ body into the canal.

“By that time, her body had been stripped naked and one of the paving slabs that you Hayley Ferguson had brought with you the day before was tied to her with a length of washing line.

“When the two of you callously deposited her body in the canal, you were not only preventing her lawful burial, but also deliberately seeking to prevent any investigation into her disappearance and murder.”

After dumping the body, the judge said Anslow had climbed a flight of steps and sat back down in his wheelchair “resuming the pretence that you were immobile and a threat to no one”.
