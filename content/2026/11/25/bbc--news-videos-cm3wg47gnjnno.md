# Why are bills going up?

Energy bills will rise by 26% for millions of households in January, the highest rate in nearly four years, after a surge in gas prices sparked by the Iran war.

Isaac, a student at Durham University, said he had no choice but to keep the heating in his student house on 'constantly' due to poor insulation, and he was not looking forward to the price increase.

In the Autumn budget, the government announced a one-off payment of £100 for households in council bands A to D, 'half a weekly shop' according to Isaac.

BBC correspondent Colletta Smith explains the January increase.
