Local artists have raised nearly £5k for Safe and Sound Homes (SASH) through a year of creative events, exhibitions and fundraising initiatives.

Sharon McDonagh and DJ Sola have raised £4,696 for SASH, continuing a commitment that has raised almost £45,000 for the York charity over the past three years.

RARE Collective, founded by Sharon and DJ Sola, has spent the past year bringing together artists, musicians and the wider community to raise vital funds for SASH, which supports young people aged 16–25 who are homeless or at risk of homelessness across York and surrounding areas.

For Sharon, supporting SASH has been a personal commitment for several years. Her previous projects include *Rise of the Vandals*, which raised more than £21,700 in 2024, and* Vandalfest* in 2025, which generated over £18,100.

Both events transformed a disused office block in central York into a vibrant street art exhibition, shining a light on hidden youth homelessness while raising significant funds for the charity.

Now, through RARE Collective, Sharon and Sola are continuing that work, combining visual art, music and creative experiences to champion independent artists and support community causes.

The fundraising year began in March with solo exhibitions at WET York featuring Nicolas Dixon, Alison Jagger and Matt Sewell, with 10% of artwork sales donated to SASH. This was followed by a group exhibition at The Crooked Tap in conjunction with Acombfest, featuring artists from across the UK.

In July, RARE was invited by Art of Protest to curate the RARE Paint Jam at Acombfest, held in the grounds of the Carlton Tavern. Artists created a collection of mural boards which later became the centrepiece of *The Board Meeting*, a one-day festival featuring a live art auction and the boards being sold via a silent auction on RARE’s website.

Other initiatives included the launch of the RARE Colouring Book and a further group exhibition at City Screen York.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/acombfest-york-street-art-festival-2026-2.webp)

Sharon McDonagh, founder of RARE Collective and SASH’s Creative Charity Champion, said: “We’re incredibly proud to have raised another £4,696 for SASH.

“Supporting the charity has been close to my heart for several years, and setting up RARE Collective with Sola has given us a new platform to bring artists and the community together while continuing to make a difference.

“For me, there’s a real connection between street art and the work SASH does. Graffiti culture has historically given a voice to people who might otherwise feel overlooked or marginalised. I believe everyone deserves to be seen, heard and given opportunities, and that’s something that sits at the heart of both RARE and our support for SASH.

“A huge thank you to every artist, venue, collaborator and supporter who has helped make this possible. It’s been a fantastic year, and we’re looking forward to what we can achieve together in the future.”

Aimee, fundraising lead at SASH, said: “We’re hugely grateful to Sharon, Sola and everyone who has supported RARE Collective. It’s fantastic to see the creative community coming together to support SASH, and their continued commitment means so much to us.

“Every pound raised helps us continue our work with young people facing homelessness, while the events also help raise awareness of an issue that is often hidden.”
