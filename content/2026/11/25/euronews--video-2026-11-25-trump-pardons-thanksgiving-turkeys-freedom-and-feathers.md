US President Donald Trump pardoned two Thanksgiving turkeys, Freedom and Feathers, during the annual ceremony at the White House on Tuesday.

Trump was joined by First Lady Melania Trump and other officials as he declared the birds would be spared from becoming part of the holiday feast.

Only Freedom appeared at the ceremony on the South Lawn, while Feathers ran into the bushes.

Trump later pardoned both turkeys, which will spend the rest of their lives at Texas A&M University, where they are expected to become part of the university’s Thanksgiving tradition.
