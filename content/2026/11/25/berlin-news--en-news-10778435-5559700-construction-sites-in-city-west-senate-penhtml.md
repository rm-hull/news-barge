The area around Breitscheidplatz is one of the city’s most important hubs, attracting several million visitors annually and home to a wide variety of stores, Giffey said. Approximately 500 individual businesses will be affected by the construction work and the associated restrictions and disruptions over the next five years. According to the Senate Chancellery, extensive construction work will take place in City West through 2031. Construction will take place next to Breitscheidplatz, along Kurfürstendamm and Tauentzienstraße, as well as on surrounding streets such as Kantstraße and Lietzenburger Straße. According to Gaebler, Breitscheidplatz is being remodeled and the subway tunnel ceilings are being renovated. During this time, local businesses will be significantly impacted, particularly in terms of their visibility and accessibility.
