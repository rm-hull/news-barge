**Residents and visitors are invited to enjoy festive food, gifts and crafts at the Harrogate Christmas Market, running across three weekends in December on Cambridge Street, as part of the town’s wider seasonal celebrations.**

Harrogate is set to welcome back its popular Christmas Market on Cambridge Street this December, inviting both residents and visitors to soak up the festive atmosphere and explore a variety of stalls offering food, gifts and artisan products. The market will operate across three weekends, making it a significant feature of the town centre’s seasonal activities.

Taking place on Friday 4th, Saturday 5th and Sunday 6th December; Friday 11th, Saturday 12th and Sunday 13th December; and concluding on Friday 18th and Saturday 19th December, the market will be open from 10am to 5pm on Fridays and Sundays, and 9am to 5pm on Saturdays.

Visitors will find a carefully curated selection of traders showcasing an array of local and international street food, artisan groceries, handmade arts and crafts, clothing, jewellery and accessories. The market aims to provide an opportunity for Christmas shopping that supports independent businesses while offering unique gifts, stocking fillers and festive treats.

> **Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, commented:**
>
> We’re delighted to welcome the Harrogate Christmas Market back to the town centre this December. The market will offer a fantastic mix of festive food, gifts and artisan products, creating a vibrant atmosphere for residents and visitors to enjoy while supporting traders and local businesses during the festive season. Whether you’re looking for a unique Christmas gift, a festive treat or simply a great day out with family and friends, the Christmas Market is the perfect addition to a visit to Harrogate this December.

The Christmas Market is part of Harrogate BID’s wider programme to enhance the festive experience in the town centre, encouraging shoppers to explore the area and support local businesses.

In addition to the Christmas Market weekends, there will be other festive market events including Farmers Markets on Thursday 10th and 17th December, featuring local produce and seasonal goods from Yorkshire traders. A special Makers Market on Sunday 20th December will showcase the work of independent makers, artists and craftspeople, providing further opportunities to purchase distinctive gifts.

For more details about the Christmas events in Harrogate, residents and visitors can visit the Harrogate BID website or follow their social media channels.
