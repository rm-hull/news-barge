## Why we recommend it

There's something refreshing about seeing a modern piece of computer hardware that isn't another anonymous slab of black or gray plastic. The translucent Bondi Blue back of the Triple Aero ’98 Pro Max is a nostalgic nod to the colorful computer designs of the 1990s.

It's far more than a retro novelty, though, as it adds three 16-inch Full HD+ IPS displays above your laptop. The 16:10 aspect ratio provides extra vertical workspace, and the adjustable aluminum stand lets you set a preferred viewing height and angle.

The extender accommodates laptops measuring between 12 and 18.5 inches and offers three viewing modes, including a weird, but handy triangular arrangement for meetings (see below).

A single USB-C cable handles video on compatible laptops, with USB and HDMI available as an alternative. The included 65W power adapter runs all three screens and charges your laptop at up to 35W.

There's also a fun Easter egg hidden on the Aura Displays website for anyone who wants to travel back to the late '90s. Visit any page on aura-displays.com and enter the classic Konami code (↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A) using your keyboard. If you're on a phone, swipe in the same directions and then tap twice. The entire site will instantly gain a retro look.
