Multiple roads and footpaths across York are set to be temporarily closed for maintenance and utility works.

Lowick, Woodthorpe, will be shut between its junction with Grassholme and the boundary of property No 6 for telecoms work.

The closure is set to start at 8am on Monday, November 30, and end at 6pm on Monday, December 7.

An alternative route will not be signed.

In Haxby, The Village will be closed between the boundaries of property Nos 96 and 104 for water mains work, starting at 7pm on Monday, November 30, until 6am on December 1.

A signed diversion will be in place via York Road, Haxby Road, A1237, Wigginton Road, and Mill Lane.

Askham Fields Lane, Askham Bryan, will also be closed for water mains work between its junction with Main Street and the boundary of property No 8.

The closure will run from 8am on Wednesday, December 2, to 6pm on Thursday, December 17.

A signed diversion will be in place via Main Street, Askham Bryan Lane, and A1237.

Bankside Close, Poppleton, will be closed for telecoms work between the boundaries of property Nos 16 and 18.

The road will be shut from 9.30am to 3.30pm each day from Wednesday, December 2, until Thursday, December 3.

There will be no signed alternative route.

Birch Park, Huntington, will be closed for telecoms work between the entrance to the Royal Mail Delivery Office and a point 28 metres south-west of the entrance.

The closure will be in effect from 7pm on Wednesday, December 2, to 7am the next day, and again from 7pm on Tuesday, December 8, to 7am the next day.

There will be no signed alternative route.

A footpath from next to 16 Melcombe Avenue to the end of Osmington Gardens, Strensall, will be closed for telecoms work.

The closure will be in place from 8.30am to 4pm each day from Monday, November 30, to Wednesday, December 2.

A signed alternative route will be in place via Toby Court and the footpath from Osmington Gardens to Toby Court.

All the temporary orders will not stop works or emergency access, and access to properties will be allowed unless stopped by ongoing works or safety issues.

Signs and barriers will show the extent of the closures.

For more information, contact [highway.regulation@york.gov.uk.](mailto:highway.regulation@york.gov.uk.)
