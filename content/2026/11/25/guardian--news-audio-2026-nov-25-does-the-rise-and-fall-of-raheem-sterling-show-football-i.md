# Does the rise and fall of Raheem Sterling show football is broken? – The Latest

Raheem Sterling has been handed a 12-month jail term, suspended for 18 months, for reckless driving and possession of nitrous oxide. It marks a remarkable fall from grace for the former England and Manchester City player, who was awarded an MBE in 2021. Sterling started playing professional football at 16, and his sentencing has raised questions about whether there are sufficient protections for younger players.
