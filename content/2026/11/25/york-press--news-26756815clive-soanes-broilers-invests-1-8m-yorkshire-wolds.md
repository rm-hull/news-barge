## Poultry business invests £1.8m in East Yorkshire expansion

Clive Soanes Broilers has invested £1.8 million in new rearing sheds to support growing demand *(Image: Supplied)*

A poultry business in East Yorkshire has invested £1.8 million in new rearing sheds to support growing demand.

Clive Soanes Broilers, based in Middleton on the Wolds, has built two modern poultry sheds with space for 65,000 birds.

The sheds include advanced heat exchangers and ventilation controls designed to improve bird welfare and energy efficiency.

Claire Wright, managing director of Clive Soanes Broilers, said: "This is a fantastic investment for the future of our poultry production.

"We've placed a real emphasis on incorporating technology that supports bird welfare while also improving energy efficiency and reducing our environmental impact.

"The Eco Unit heat exchangers are a particularly exciting addition, helping us create a more consistent environment for the birds while reducing heating costs and emissions."

The new facilities use Fancom One environmental control systems, which monitor and manage ventilation and climate.

Specialist contractors involved in the project included Morspan, JF McKenna, Connop & Son Ltd, and RE Thompson Ltd.

Clive Soanes Broilers is a sister company of Soanes Poultry and rears more than six million chickens each year across its Yorkshire Wolds farms.

The company said the investment reflects its ongoing commitment to sustainability, animal welfare, and efficient poultry production.
