**Jane Giffould**
  
*Halstead, Essex, UK*

Some years ago, my friend would sometimes take his dog, Delilah, up in his Auster plane – she loved it. If I was over at the air club, sometimes she would sit with me while I had a cuppa. She ignored most aircraft, even the other Auster. Then when she heard “her” Auster on its final approach or even just as it started to descend, she would sit up and look up in its direction until it had landed.

She had flown with me, too, but I don’t think she did the same with my plane. However, once I had landed and done my post-flight checks, I would give a low, quiet whistle. She would look up at my right-hand jacket pocket, which, by dog law, contained biscuits!
