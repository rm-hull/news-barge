# Man dies after touching live electricity cable in Yorkshire

## A man has died after touching power lines in Yorkshire in what police have described as an ‘unexplained death’.

Police were called to South Moor Lane in Knottingley just before 2pm on Sunday (Nov 22) to a report of concern for a safety of a man.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Sadly, the man, aged 58, was pronounced dead at the scene. The death is currently being treated as unexplained.

“Enquiries are ongoing, initial enquiries suggest that the man might have made contact with an exposed live cable in the field nearby.”

A Northern Powergrid spokesperson said: “On November 22, Northern Powergrid was called by the emergency services to attend a location in West Yorkshire following reports of a person being seriously injured after contacting a live electrical conductor.

“Our engineers found that the lines in question had been the subject of an attempted metal theft.

“We are very sad to learn that the injuries proved fatal and must stress that we do not know why the injured person was in the vicinity of the damaged power lines.

“The police are handling the situation. The network has been made safe and supplies are restored.

“We ask anyone who sees any sign of damage to power lines to keep their distance and report it immediately to us on 105.”
