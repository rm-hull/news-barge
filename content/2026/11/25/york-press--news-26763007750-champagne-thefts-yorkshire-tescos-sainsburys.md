A man who is alleged to have stolen more than £750 in champagne from two Yorkshire supermarkets has been arrested and remanded in custody.

North Yorkshire Police said the alleged thefts took place on Wednesday, November 18 from Tesco in Shipton Road, Harrogate then the Railway Road Sainsbury's in the same town.

Police said two suspected thefts from the first supermarket took place within minutes of each other, where it is alleged on each occasion the man entered the store, selected several bottles before concealing them in a shopping bag and left without paying.




A force spokesperson said: "Later the same afternoon, at around 2.30pm, the suspect allegedly entered Sainsbury's in Railway Road and selected four bottles before attempting to leave the store without paying.

"A member of store security detained the man and contacted us."

A vehicle linked to the man was searched and a substantial amount of alcohol was recovered from its boot, police added.

The spokesperson said: "During our investigation into that offence, we also carried out further checks which revealed he was wanted on a warrant by a court in Worcestershire.

"The man in his 30s from the Stoke area was arrested and charged with theft, the wanted offence, and is alleged to have been driving without valid insurance and driving without a full licence."

The man has been remanded in custody to appear before magistrates, police said.
