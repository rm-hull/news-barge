Welcome Spaces across Leeds are now open and all set to provide warm, safe places where people can gather for free over winter.

Leeds City Council is also reminding residents of the support available during the colder months, all of which can be accessed at a dedicated webpage.

The page has links through to finance support for people struggling with utility bills and debt, how to stay healthy over winter and how to access food support.

As in previous years, the council works closely with voluntary, community and social enterprise organisations to provide a network of Welcome Spaces across Leeds throughout the winter ensuring places that are safe, warm and welcoming are available free of charge and available to anyone in Leeds.

Welcome Spaces are already available through the council’s Community Hubs and libraries, including Wetherby and Boston Spa, with more spaces delivered by third and faith sector organisations due to go live.

The Leeds Winter Coat Appeal run by Wear and Share is also working to collect and redistribute great quality, second-hand winter coats to people across Leeds for free, helping to save money and reduce waste.

During last year’s appeal over 8,000 items of winter clothing were given out to families for free.

For full details about the winter coat appeal, including a list of collection and donation points and how to get involved, visit Leeds Winter Coat Appeal – Wear and Share.

Coun Asghar Khan, Leeds City Council’s executive member for housing and hubs, and Coun Emma Flint, executive member for equality, adults and health, stated: “Winter and the festive season are wonderful in many ways, but we know that they can be challenging times for many people, whether that is because of health issues, difficulty with paying bills, or any other reason.
