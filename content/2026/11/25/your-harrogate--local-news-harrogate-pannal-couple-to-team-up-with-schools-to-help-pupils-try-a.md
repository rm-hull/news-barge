A couple who run a workshop in Pannal are keen to hear from schools whose pupils would benefit from learning a trade.

Rachel and Robert Bullock run Core Trade Skills at Thirkill Park on Thirkill Drive, where young people can try their hand at carpentry, plumbing, basic electrics and dry stone walling.

Robert has more than 30 years’ experience in plumbing and construction, while Rachel has a background in working with children.

The couple hope spending time in the workshop will help pupils who struggle in the classroom build confidence and explore options for college, an apprenticeship or a job.

Rachel and Robert said:

> “We believe there are young people who are capable of so much more than their current circumstances might suggest.
>
> "Not every young person learns in the same way, and not every young person finds their confidence in a classroom.
>
> “Sometimes it takes a practical challenge, the opportunity to use their hands, learn a new skill and achieve something they didn't think they could do, to change how they see themselves and their future.”

Figures from the Office for National Statistics show an estimated 981,000 people aged 16 to 24 in the UK were out of education, employment or training between April and June 2026. That equates to 13% of the age group.

*Schools, colleges and other organisations supporting young people can contact Core Trade Skills on 07921 020302.*
