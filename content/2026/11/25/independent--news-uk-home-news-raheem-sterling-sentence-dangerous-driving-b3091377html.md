Former England footballer Raheem Sterling has avoided jail after admitting to dangerous driving in his Lamborghini while inhaling from a balloon on a busy motorway during rush hour, before crashing into a metal gate.

The 31-year-old showed no emotion as he was sentenced to 12 months’ imprisonment, suspended for 18 months, at Basingstoke Magistrates’ Court for dangerous driving and possessing nitrous oxide on May 28.

He was also handed a 24-month driving ban and ordered to undertake 100 hours of unpaid work.

A motorist who contacted police expressed that he was “shocked” by what he saw, while another witness noted she had "genuine concerns the driver might kill someone", previous court proceedings heard.

Body-worn video recorded during Sterling’s arrest at 9.15am showed him inside the crashed vehicle holding two gas canisters on his lap, with an officer saying: "You can put that down, mate."

![Sterling was found with multiple cannisters of nitrous oxide](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/11/01M89893DJFB2403FXZG0Q1G23.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Sterling pleaded guilty in September to driving a Lamborghini Urus dangerously across the M25, the M3, the A327 and Minley Road in Surrey.

He further admitted to possessing six canisters of class C drug nitrous oxide for wrongful inhalation, alongside a charge of failing to provide a specimen.

During the September hearing at Basingstoke Magistrates’ Court, prosecutor Simon Jones informed the court that multiple emergency calls were made between 7.45am and 8.52am regarding the erratic driving of a black sports car.

Witness Christopher Jennings alerted police at 7.49am after travelling beside Sterling’s vehicle on the M25, with Mr Jones noting "he was shocked by what he saw", adding that "he sighted the defendant inhaling from the balloon".

Fiona Jennings, driving along the southbound M3 at roughly 8.45am, described the black Lamborghini’s driving as "absolutely horrendous" and "swerving in and out of lanes", the court heard.

"She had genuine concerns the driver might kill someone," Mr Jones said.

She initially believed an airbag had been deployed before realising it was an inflated white balloon with its neck in the driver's mouth.

Dashcam footage provided by the Crown Prosecution Service showed the car changing lanes repeatedly before striking a concrete barrier on the right side and driving onwards.

Sterling exited junction 4a of the M3, completed a loop via the A327, and lost control shortly after passing a roundabout.

![Camera footage shows the winger swerving across all three lanes](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/11/01M89895G6PF0QCQXSZWP5C33D.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The vehicle veered off the road and "must have collided with either the sign or metal gate", causing "substantial" damage to the car and all four wheels, rendering it inoperable, the hearing was told.

After officers brought Sterling to Basingstoke police investigation centre, he told custody personnel "he had a problem with nitrous oxide", according to Mr Jones.

While he completed an impairment test, Sterling requested legal counsel before deciding whether to supply a blood sample, Mr Jones said.

In the police sergeant’s view, "he was deliberately failing to provide a clear answer".

When interviewed later alongside his solicitor, Sterling admitted he had been driving but gave no comment regarding his manner of driving.

Defending, Jason Bartfeld KC acknowledged it was a "top category case" owing to prolonged driving and "at times" severe impairment over brief intervals.

However, the defence disputed that vehicle damage was severe and highlighted "confusion" surrounding the failure to supply a specimen.

Sterling scored 20 goals across 82 England appearances, helping the national side reach the 2018 World Cup semi-finals and the Euro 2020 final.

Born in Kingston, Jamaica, Sterling transferred to Manchester City from Liverpool in 2015, winning four Premier League titles, five League Cups and the 2019 FA Cup. He joined Chelsea in 2022 before spending the 2024-25 season on loan at Arsenal.

Following his departure from Stamford Bridge, Sterling moved to Dutch side Feyenoord on a short-term contract lasting until the end of the season. He is currently without a club.

Sterling received an MBE in the 2021 Queen’s Birthday Honours for services to racial equality in sport, following years of leading anti-racism initiatives and speaking out against discrimination. The honour came amid public debate surrounding fans who booed players taking the knee prior to matches.
