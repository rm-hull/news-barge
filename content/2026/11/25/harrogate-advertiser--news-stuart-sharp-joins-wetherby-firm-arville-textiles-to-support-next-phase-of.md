Wetherby based manufacturer, Arville Textiles has appointed Stuart Sharp as Group Chief Finance Officer, further strengthening the Group's leadership team as the business continues to invest in innovation, growth and operational excellence.

Stuart joins Arville with extensive senior finance and commercial leadership experience across the manufacturing and technology sectors.

A CIMA-qualified finance professional, he has built a strong track record of supporting business growth, leading organisational change and providing strategic financial leadership.

The appointment comes as Arville continues to invest in its people, capabilities and infrastructure to support customers and drive future growth.

Established in 1951 and headquartered in Wetherby, Arville Textiles is a specialist technical textiles manufacturer serving customers across defence, aerospace, automotive, food processing and industrial markets.

The business combines textile design, weaving, coating and fabrication to develop engineered fabric solutions for demanding applications.

Steve Gill, Chief Executive Officer of Arville, commented: "Stuart's appointment further strengthens our leadership team and brings significant financial, commercial and strategic expertise to the business.

"His experience supporting ambitious organisations through growth and transformation will be invaluable as we continue to invest in innovation, manufacturing capability and customer service. We are delighted to welcome Stuart to Arville."

Stuart Sharp added: "I'm excited to be joining Arville at such an important stage in its journey.

"The business has an outstanding reputation for technical excellence, innovation and customer partnership, and I look forward to working with colleagues across the Group to help support its continued growth and long-term ambitions."

As Arville continues to strengthen its position as a leading technical textiles manufacturer, Stuart's addition to the executive team will help ensure the business remains well placed to deliver sustainable growth, invest in future opportunities and create long-term value for customers, employees and stakeholders.
