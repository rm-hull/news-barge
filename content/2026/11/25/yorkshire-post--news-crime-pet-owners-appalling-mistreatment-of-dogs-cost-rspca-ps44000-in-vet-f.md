# Pet owners ‘appalling’ mistreatment of dogs cost RSPCA £44,000 in vet fees

## Two men whose ‘appalling’ mistreatment of four starving dogs cost the RSPCA £44,000 in kennelling and veterinary treatment have been sentenced.

Kyle and Tyler Williams kept four dogs at a house in Rotherham with no food or water and ‘terrible’ conditions.

The starving dogs, named Luna, Bear, Bronx and Kiesha, had cuts, wounds, conjunctivitis, fleas, cracked skin and hairs growing on the inside of their eyelids.

When the RSPCA visited the pair in March, the house had a strong smell or urine and faeces, and it became clear the animals needed urgent vet treatment.

The two men failed to get help for the animals, however, leading dog legislation officers to seize the pets and take them away for immediate treatment.

![The four dogs were found starving and mistreated.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/17/57/926525540_2260851431441705_5664416985760263380_n.jpeg?trim=0,2,18,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


The pair were told they were responsible for ‘prolonged failure to seek treatment’ for the dogs, along with ‘appalling conditions’ the dogs were kept in.

Magistrates described the £44,000 amount required for kennelling and vet treatment as ‘eye-watering’.

The pair pleaded guilty in October, and were given lifetime bans of having any animal, given 18-month community orders with 125 hours of unpaid work and ordered to pay £360 each in fees.

A destruction order was granted by the judge for Bronx as he is a banned breed dog and cannot be rehomed.

South Yorkshire Police said Kiesha, a husky cross, has since been rehomed and is enjoying her new life, pain and suffering free and loved by a family.
