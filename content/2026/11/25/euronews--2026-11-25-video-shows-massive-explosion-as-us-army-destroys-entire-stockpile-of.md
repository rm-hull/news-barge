A massive explosion sent smoke into the sky as the US Army destroyed its entire stockpile of lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown on Wednesday.

Around five tonnes of the degraded explosive, used in munition detonators, were detonated in a controlled blast. The operation raised concerns over potential health and environmental effects.

Officials said barriers, ballistic gels and foams were used to contain the blast and limit the spread of lead dust. Residents nearby had been advised to prepare for possible evacuation.
