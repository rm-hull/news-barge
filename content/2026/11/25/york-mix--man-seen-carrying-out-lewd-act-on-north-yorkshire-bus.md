Police are investigating a report of outraging public decency on a North Yorksh3ire bus.

It happened on the 7A Scarborough bus travelling towards Eastfied at about midday last Wednesday, 18 November.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Concerns were raised after a member of the public reported seeing a man carrying out a lewd act while on the bus.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed this incident or anyone who may have seen the male before or after the incident.”

The suspect is described as

* a white man
* aged around 30 to 40-years-old
* bald
* with facial stubble.

He was wearing green trainers, black trousers, green hoodie and a black coat over the top.
