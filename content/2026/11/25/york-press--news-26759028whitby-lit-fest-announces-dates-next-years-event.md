## Whitby Lit Fest doubles ticket sales in record-breaking second year

Ann Cleeves interviewed by Alison Graham *(Image: @thelensartistic)*

A literary festival has doubled its ticket sales in just its second year.

Whitby Lit Fest sold 5,628 tickets across 57 events held at seven venues throughout the town, attracting audiences of all ages.

Organisers have now announced that the festival will return from November 18 to 21 in 2027.

Lois Kirtlan, chair of Whitby Lit Fest, said: "We have been absolutely blown away by the response to this year's festival.

"To have almost doubled our ticket sales in just our second year is an extraordinary achievement and a testament to the enthusiasm and generosity of everyone who has got behind us.

"There is an incredible sense of community behind this festival.

"So many people have given their time, energy, and expertise to make it happen, and we are immensely proud of what we have achieved together."

This year's festival featured a sell-out event with crime writer Ann Cleeves, who spoke to an audience of 600, while television personality Si King drew nearly 500 attendees.

Other notable guests included Hugh Bonneville, Corinne Bailey Rae, Joanne Harris, John Boyne, Mark Billingham, and Louise Minchin.

Festival merchandise also proved popular, with branded beanie hats selling out and helping raise additional funds.
