A Yorkshire-based law firm has made two senior hires to strengthen its private client and conveyancing teams.

Ramsdens Solicitors has welcomed senior associates Keil Walsh and Liam Douglas to its York office.

Mr Walsh joins from Clarion with more than 10 years’ experience in private client work, advising high-net-worth clients, business owners, and blended families on non-contentious matters.

Mr Douglas, a conveyancing specialist, has experience with freehold, leasehold, new-build, and shared ownership transactions, and brings expertise in advising corporate clients on complex buy-to-let matters.

Lindsey Frith, joint CEO of Ramsdens, said: "Continuing the growth of our York office is a strategic priority for the firm, and Keil and Liam are excellent additions to the team.

"Private client and property are key growth areas for us in the region, and their specialist expertise and commitment to delivering meaningful client service will be highly valued by both existing and new clients."
