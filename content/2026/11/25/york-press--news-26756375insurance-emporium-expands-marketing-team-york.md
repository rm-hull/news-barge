A York-based insurance company has made three new hires as part of a strategy to boost its marketing and brand growth.

The Insurance Emporium has appointed Tom Butler as marketing manager, alongside Ben Godbold as senior digital marketing specialist and Jaimini Karsan as organic social media and paid media specialist.

Mr Butler said: "In an increasingly competitive market, I am delighted to be moving into this role at a pivotal time for the business.

"I look forward to beginning 2027 with a strategic plan to strengthen The Insurance Emporium’s brand position with a stronger emphasis on utilising digital channels, supported by traditional media, and direct customer engagement."

Maryline Jardin, chief commercial officer at The Insurance Emporium, said: "Tom is an experienced and passionate marketing leader whose career spans several industries, including financial services."

For further information on The Insurance Emporium’s award-winning insurance, visit: [https://www.theinsuranceemporium.co.uk](https://www.theinsuranceemporium.co.uk)

The Insurance Emporium has also celebrated 39 employee milestone anniversaries this year.
