# DWP confirms new Pension Credit rate for couples from April 2027

## The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has confirmed the Pension Credit rate for couples will rise

The Pension Credit rate for couples is set to increase to £377.40 from April, as confirmed by the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP). The new rates were announced following the Autumn Budget delivered by Labour Party Chancellor John Healey at the end of October.

Announcing the updated figures, Pat McFadden, Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, stated: "I am pleased to announce that the basic and full rate of the new State Pensions will be increased by 3.9%, in line with the increase in average weekly earnings in the year to May-July 2026, reports Birmingham Live.

"This delivers on our triple lock commitment throughout this Parliament to increase these rates in line with the highest of growth in prices, growth in earnings or 2.5%. From April, the full annual rate of the new State Pension will increase by around £490. The full annual rate of the basic State Pension will increase by around £375."

"The standard minimum guarantee in Pension Credit will also increase by 3.9% in line with the increase in average weekly earnings. From April, it will be £247.30 a week for a single pensioner and £377.40 a week for a couple, ensuring the incomes of the poorest pensioners are protected."

A partner refers to your husband, wife or civil partner - provided you reside together - or somebody you cohabit with as a couple, without being married or in a civil partnership.

You'll require the following details about yourself and your partner if applicable: National Insurance number, particulars of any income, savings and investments you hold and details of your income, savings and investments on the date you wish to backdate your claim to (typically 3 months previously or the date you reached State Pension age).

You'll also need your bank account information. Depending on your application method, you may additionally be requested to provide your bank or building society name, sort code and account number.
