An incoming storm threatens to keep the giant character balloons out of this year's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City.

Heavy winds and rain are expected to hit the city on Thursday and the inflatables, which have flown between Manhattan's skyscrapers for decades, could be grounded if conditions become too windy.

## What could ground the balloons?

City rules have long stated that large balloons can't fly if sustained winds exceed 23 mph (37 kph) or gusts exceed 34 mph (55 kph).

The restrictions were put in place after a mishap in 1997, when a balloon careened into a lamppost near Central Park and critically injured a woman.

![Joe Cool and Woodstock by Peanuts Worldwide seen during 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade - Balloonfest at MetLife Stadium on November 07, 2026 in East Rutherford, New Jersey](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/14/41/GettyImages-2304297655.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Local officials have long been wary of strong winds knocking the balloons off course and into spectators along the parade route.

## What weather is expected for this year's parade?

Brian Hurley, a meteorologist at the National Weather Service, said the parade crowd should be ready for a wet and windy day.

But for the balloons, he said, the big headache could be the wind gusts. Sustained winds will range from 15 to 25 mph (24 to 40 kph), Hurley said.

Gusts of 30 to 40 mph (48 to 64 kph) are expected in the morning, and they could grow stronger in the late afternoon.

The parade kicks off at 8:30 a.m.

Hurley also noted that gusts could feel more concentrated because Manhattan's tall buildings often create a wind tunnel effect.

## When will a decision be made?

Whether the balloons take to the sky this year will be a decision made on the morning of the parade, Macy's said in a statement, as officials keep tabs on wind gauges along the route.

The New York Police Department has previously described it as a game-day decision.

Officials have added that the balloons can be steered off the route and deflated on a side street if weather conditions get bad enough that handlers can't control them.

In past years, balloons have flown a bit lower than usual because of weather.

Each inflatable character has a distinct risk profile that includes its size and weight, and an optimal altitude for wind speeds.

In an interview with *WABC*, New York City Emergency Management Commissioner Christina Farrell said one option being considered is moving the parade start time earlier due to the “challenging” forecast.

![PAC-MAN balloon floats down the street during the annual Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City on November 27, 2025](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/14/15/GettyImages-2248126441.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"You may remember, there was some lightning and storms coming through on July 4th. It's been that kind of year. The fireworks were moved up a little bit. That is one option. If the weather is coming in a little later, can we start things a little earlier?" Farrell said.

## Have the balloons been grounded before?

Balloons were grounded due to weather in 1971.

“Our team is closely monitoring the weather forecast with our City and agency partners, and as always, the safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority,” Macy's said in a statement.
