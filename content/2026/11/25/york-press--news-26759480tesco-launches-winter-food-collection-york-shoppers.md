Tesco customers in York are being encouraged to make a difference in their communities this winter.

Shoppers across the UK are invited to take part in Tesco’s 14th annual Winter Food Collection to support families facing hunger and hardship.

The collection runs in Express stores from November 23 to 28, and in larger stores from November 26 to 28.

Ashwin Prasad, Tesco UK CEO, said: "With more families expected to need help with essentials this winter, along with our partners, Felix and Trussell, we are encouraging customers to donate whatever they can to our Winter Food Collection.

"Food banks and frontline charities need our help more than ever before.

"Whether it’s in your local store through donating to the Winter Food Collection, rounding up at the till, or donating online via our Donr link, even the smallest donation can make a real difference to someone else’s Christmas."

Last year’s collection resulted in 1.48 million meals being donated.

Donations will be distributed by The Trussell Trust and The Felix Project.

Pre-packed donation bags, which typically cost between £2 and £4, will be available in stores.

These bags contain long-life items such as tinned fish, tinned vegetables and pulses, pasta, grains and rice, and UHT milk.
