# Controversial bus gates suspended for Christmas after recent traffic chaos

## A controversial bus gate in Sheffield has been temporarily suspended following traffic chaos in recent weeks.

Over the last two weeks, there has been bumper-to-bumper traffic throughout much of the week stemming from Charter Row and Furnival Gate.

Business owner Bruce Payne, who runs The Market Chippy on The Moor, said shoppers faced severe delays leaving city centre car parks, with gridlock in all directions.

The council has acknowledged traffic woes in the city and has “temporarily paused” the controversial Furnival Gate bus gate for the foreseeable future while it investigates - while claiming it always intended to do this but is “moving the schedule forward.”

The bus gate - which fines any cars that pass through it and diverts them down Matilda Way - was introduced while works continue to redevelop nearby Pinstone Street, and around three years after a similar scheme was introduced on Arundel Gate.

![Cars at a standstill in Sheffield City Centre on Saturday, November 21.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/21/19/52/Untitled-Design-(64).jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


A statement released on Thursday (Nov 24) said: “The council has been closely monitoring road conditions due to the temporary traffic management changes that are part of the works on Charter Row, increased traffic levels over recent weeks and other ongoing construction in the city centre.”

This refers to the ongoing installation of a bike lane on Charter Row - where the owner of Atkinsons department store, Nick Atkinson, says trade has now fallen due to the roadworks.

The statement added: “ With planned remedial works on Pinstone Street that will require the pausing of the bus gates on Furnival Gate, the decision has been made to bring this measure forward.”

![A controversial bus gate on Furnival Gate will be suspended for the Christmas period after two weekends of bumper-to-bumper traffic.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2025/09/01/12/35/20250901_130127.jpeg?trim=106,516,157,198&width=640&quality=65)


The Furnival Gate and Pinstone Street bus gates will be suspended from Friday, November 27.

Meanwhile, traffic will also be reallowed on currently-closed Young Street, between Moore Street and South Lane, “as part of efforts to ease congestion around Moore Street roundabout.”

## ‘Temporary’

The statement says the suspension is “temporary” and that the “bus gates will be instated as soon as possible.”

Councillor Alexi Dimond, chair of transport, regeneration and climate committee, acknowledged “concerns” raised over congestion in recent weeks.

He said: “We’ve been closely monitoring traffic since the Furnival Gate bus gates became operational, and we’ve listened to the concerns raised about congestion in this part of the city centre.

“With planned remedial works on Pinstone Street that will require the bus gates to be suspended, we’re taking the opportunity to move the bus gate suspension forward to cover the Christmas period.

“This gives us an opportunity to understand the impact the change makes to traffic, what further measures, such as phasing of the work on Charter Row, could help and carry out the planned remedial works on Pinstone Street.

“I want to be clear that this is a temporary measure. The bus gates will return and remain part of our wider plans to improve public transport journey times and make it easier and safer to walk, wheel and cycle through the city centre.”
