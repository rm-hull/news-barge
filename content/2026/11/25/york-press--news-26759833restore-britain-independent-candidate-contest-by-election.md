## Restore Britain and independent candidate to contest by-election

A North Yorkshire polling station. Photo: NYC.

VOTERS in a North Yorkshire town will head to the polls next month after two candidates were confirmed for a council by-election.

A contest will be held in Whitby’s Stakesby Ward on December 17, with residents choosing who will fill one of several vacancies created during a turbulent period for Whitby Town Council.

The election will be contested by Dr Janet Broad, standing for Restore Britain, and Carolyn Ann Lavagna, according to the official statement of persons nominated. Polling will take place between 7am and 10pm at Marton Court.

The by-election was triggered by a vacancy in Stakesby Ward and will be administered by North Yorkshire Council. Nominations have now closed and the two candidates will face voters in the run-up to Christmas.

The election forms part of the continuing fallout from months of upheaval at Whitby Town Council, which saw five councillors resign in October.

Those departures followed a prolonged governance dispute that culminated in an extraordinary council meeting and the publication of findings from an independent investigation into the conduct of two former councillors.

The council subsequently approved a series of measures intended to protect staff, including restricting access to council premises and authorising legal advice regarding possible future action.

The Stakesby by-election will be the first time voters have been asked to fill one of the vacancies created during that period.

The successful candidate will join the council at a time when members are continuing efforts to rebuild stability and restore public confidence following months of controversy.

Residents in Stakesby Ward can vote on December 17, with polling stations open throughout the day until 10pm.
