## Why we recommend it

Apple's MacBook Neo is around half the price of a MacBook Air, its next-cheapest model, so it offers an excellent affordable entry point to the company's laptop lineup. It feels premium, delivers excellent performance from the A18 Pro chip (derived from the iPhone 16 Pro) and lasts up to 16 hours on a full charge.

## Price Context & Historical Value

Apple had launched this model at £599, but sweeping price hikes added £100 to that bill thanks to ongoing supply chain constraints and rising hardware prices. You can knock up to £80 of that price hike off with this Black Friday deal.
