There was a happy ending for an antiques shop in Harrogate after a public appeal to help find a treasured diamond ring was met with an incredible response from the community.

Earlier this week, 27 West Park Antiques and Home Interiors issued an appeal after Centre Manager Lisa realised her much-loved diamond ring had disappeared during a busy day at the shop.

Staff feared it may have accidentally been wrapped in tissue paper or placed inside a customer's shopping bag, prompting them to ask shoppers to check their purchases.

The story was later featured by the *Harrogate Advertiser*, helping spread the appeal even further.

Now, the team has confirmed the ring has been found – and the discovery came much closer to home than anyone expected.

In an update shared on social media, the shop thanked everyone who rallied behind the search.

They said: "The response to our lost diamond ring post has been absolutely incredible.

“The number of people who saw it, shared it, sent kind messages and helped spread the word has been overwhelming.

“Even our local newspaper picked up the story - thank you Harrogate Advertiser.”

The breakthrough came thanks to Hafaz, who went above and beyond searching the shop and retracing Lisa's steps.

To everyone's amazement, the ring was eventually discovered underneath one of the shop's four-poster bed displays.

It is believed Lisa's hands became so cold that the ring slipped off without her noticing while she was working.

The team said they were ‘delighted’ to have their treasured piece of jewellery back after days of worry.

They also expressed heartfelt thanks to everyone who shared the original appeal, checked shopping bags, sent supportive messages or simply wished them luck.

The post added: “A huge thank you again to Hafaz for not giving up.

“Sometimes it's the little things that remind us just how lovely people can be.”

What began as a worrying search for a sentimental piece of jewellery has ended with a happy resolution and a lasting reminder of the kindness shown by the Harrogate community.
