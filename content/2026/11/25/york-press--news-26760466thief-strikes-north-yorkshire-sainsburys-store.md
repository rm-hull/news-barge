A thief has struck at a supermarket in a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police said the alleged theft happened on October 25 at Sainsbury's, Cold Bath Road, Harrogate.

The force has released CCTV footage of a person they want to speak to following the incident.

**Recommended reads:**

Police are urging anyone who recognises the man in the footage to get in touch, as he may have information that could help the enquiry.

People can reach out by email, online, or through live chat.

For those unable to use these options, the police can be contacted on 101 or via Crimestoppers.

The reference number for the case is 12260210299.
