## Pocklington lash artist wins beauty award three years after launch

Emily Jayne, who runs Bronzed & Beautiful by Emily Jayne from her home in Pocklington, took home the title at the Beauty & Skincare Awards. *(Image: Emily Jayne)*

A beauty business owner has been named Lash Artist of the Year at an awards ceremony.

Emily Jayne, who runs Bronzed & Beautiful by Emily Jayne from her home in Pocklington, took home the title at the Beauty & Skincare Awards.

The win comes just three years after launching her business.

Ms Jayne said: "To say I’m overwhelmed and proud would be an understatement.

"Building a business independently, working from a small one-to-one home studio, and growing a loyal client base has taken an enormous amount of hard work, dedication and support from the wonderful clients who have been part of the journey.

"I entered the awards after encouragement from my clients, never really believing I would take home the top award.

"To now be recognised as Lash Artist of the Year is an incredibly special milestone."

The award win came on the same day as a client event hosted by Ms Jayne, making the moment even more meaningful.

She said: "What made it even more special was that I was actually hosting an event with my lash clients that day.

"The event was something I’d organised to bring together an amazing group of women, some of whom had never met before, to celebrate them, the friendships formed through my little business, and the wonderful community we’ve built.

"To then find myself celebrating such a huge achievement surrounded by the very women who have supported me throughout my journey was incredibly special."

Ms Jayne launched Bronzed & Beautiful by Emily Jayne, starting out by offering eyelash treatments in her spare bedroom and spray tans in her kitchen while training.

After 18 months of professional training with Nouveau Lashes, she converted her garage into a purpose-built beauty studio.

Ms Jayne said: "I’ve built the business entirely independently over the last three years.

"I’m a one-woman business, which means every aspect of it, from the treatments and client relationships to the marketing, finances, training and day-to-day running, is down to me."

Last year, she placed in the Top 10 at the same awards for Spray Tan Artist of the Year and Best Salon Décor.

The beauty business had been a long-held dream, dating back to when she was 15.

Ms Jayne said: "When I turned 32, I decided it was time to stop imagining it or accepting it would never happen and actually do something about it.

"I took out a £10,000 loan through a government enterprise scheme, spent hours putting together a business plan and took the leap.

"My original vision was much bigger, but I’ve learned that success doesn’t necessarily have to look like a chain of salons.

"I think the relationships I’ve built with my clients are one of the biggest achievements of all."
