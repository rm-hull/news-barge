## It’s the time of year we’re all dreaming of escaping to the sun

The evenings are dark and the days are cloudy with precious little sunshine so many of us Brits are dreaming of escaping to sunnier climes, like the swallow or the swift.

The good news for people who want to escape the miserable British autumn and winter weather is that hotel group Hilton is offering big discounts on hotels in locations where sunshine is pretty much guaranteed.

We all know that sunlight in the right amounts can offer lots of physical and mental health benefits which is one of the reasons why so many Brits flock to warm locations in December, January and February.

With this in mind, holidaymakers booking hotels with Hilton can potentially save hundreds of pounds on trips to Nicosia, in Cyprus; Tangier and Rabat, in Morocco, near the Atlantic coast; the Seychelles, in the Indian Ocean, and Cape, or Cabo, Verde which is made up of a number of islands, not unlike the Canaries, situated in the Atlantic off the coast of west Africa.
