Police have released a CCTV image following a theft at Sainsbury’s on Cold Bath Road in Harrogate.

The theft took place on Sunday 25th October. North Yorkshire Police would like to speak to the person pictured as they may have information that could help the investigation.

Anyone who recognises the person can email PC Joanne Bell at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk) or visit the North Yorkshire Police website and select ‘Tell us about’, then ‘An existing case or report’.

You can also call 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or through its website.

*Please quote reference 12260210299 when providing information.*
