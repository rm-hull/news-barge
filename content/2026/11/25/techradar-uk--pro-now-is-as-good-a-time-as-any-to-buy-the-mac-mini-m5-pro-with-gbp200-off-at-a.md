The Apple Mac mini has long been one of our favourite mini PCs - when we tested it out, we even wondered if it was the best Mac out there. And one of the best models is the Mac mini M5 Pro, currently £1,499 at Amazon (that's a £200 discount for Black Friday).

Its M5 Pro chip comes with no fewer than 15 CPU cores and 16 GPU cores, plus 24GB of memory, so it's ideal for above-average and heavy workloads.

Built-in Neural Accelerators in the GPU are designed to boost on-device AI tasks for up to 4x performance versus the M4 Pro. 2.5Gb Ethernet, Thunderbolt 5 (up to 8K display support) and USB 4 keep you connected, too.

![Apple Mac mini M5 Pro]([https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/GisWeSdtjXSZMQcKTRgfyQ-200-80.avif.webp](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/GisWeSdtjXSZMQcKTRgfyQ-200-80.avif.webp) "Apple Mac mini M5 Pro")

Apple's small form factor desktop, equipped with the high-performance M5 Pro chip, is now available with a £200 saving. Ideal for demanding AI workloads or editing, it's well connected with 2.5Gb Ethernet, Thunderbolt 5 and USB 4.

## Why we recommend it

Whether you want a desktop suited to 4K video editing, photo processing or software development, the Mac mini fits the bill. Its M5 Pro chip delivers 4x performance versus last year's M4 Pro, with more CPU/GPU cores and impressive efficiency.

## Price Context & Historical Value

Apple launched this specific configuration at £1,699, and that price has remained flat ever since. Amazon's £200 discount offers a considerable 12% saving, so if you're going to buy one, this is about as good as we expect it to get.
