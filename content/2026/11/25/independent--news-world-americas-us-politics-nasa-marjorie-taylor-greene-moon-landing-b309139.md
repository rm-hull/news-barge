The head of NASA has ridiculed former MAGA congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene after she denied the space agency went to the Moon.

The Georgia Republican’s latest online spat began after she raged at President Donald Trump and Pete Hegseth in all-caps on X, over the planned detonation of dangerously degraded lead azide at the Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown, Iowa, accusing them of setting off a “dirty bomb.”

She then took issue with NASA after the space agency outlined how it would be assisting the U.S. Army with the controlled explosion Wednesday.

The X post explained NASA has deployed three research aircraft and is using its space-based Tropospheric Emissions: Monitoring of Pollution (TEMPO) instrument to gather environmental and atmospheric data in the aftermath of the explosion “to inform the broader federal response.”

![Former Republican congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene has been rebuked by the head of NASA after saying the agency never went to the Moon](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/05/19/1483082301..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Greene replied: “You didn’t even go to the Moon and everyone is supposed to feel better now because you are monitoring the Chernobyl event that the U.S. government is doing tomorrow in America. Oh. Ok.”

The former MAGA loyalist is well-known for sharing baseless conspiracy theories, having suggested in 2018 that forest fires in California were started by “Jewish space lasers”.

Her post was immediately amended with a fact-check on X, which plainly stated “NASA went to the Moon 10 times and put humans on the Moon 6 times,” offering four links to sources providing further information.

NASA administrator Jared Isaacman then responded to Greene directly, writing: “Respectfully, your post departs so significantly from reality on a matter of public concern that I feel compelled to respond.

“In challenging circumstances such as this, you want the nation’s best on the field. That is precisely why you call on NASA, putting our airborne and space-based sensors and some of the brightest scientific minds in the country to work.”

![Isaacman told Greene that reaching the Moon in 1969 ‘remains one of America’s crowning achievements, acknowledged even by our greatest adversary at the time’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/30/13/03/GettyImages-51098550.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Even if it’s hyperbole, it is irresponsible to characterize this as a ‘Chernobyl event’ when there is no reactor and there are no fission products,” he continued. “This is a controlled detonation of aging explosive material and is being handled in the most responsible way possible.”

“And we did go to the Moon. To suggest otherwise is an insult to the hundreds of thousands of talented Americans who achieved the near impossible on July 20, 1969, and then did it five more times, because we are NASA and we could.

“It remains one of America’s crowning achievements, acknowledged even by our greatest adversary at the time. It is disheartening that this needs to be explained to a former member of Congress.”

Greene could not leave it there, replying: “Go ahead and defend the U.S. government blowing up lead azide, which is a neurotoxin, all you want.

“This is the most outrageous thing happening in our country right now the day before Thanksgiving. I’m not downplaying it. I’m doing everything I can to expose it. You’re living inside the government bubble, which is not reality.”

![Administrator of NASA Jared Isaacman took the Republican to task on X](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/13/2305270691..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In spite of her concerns, the blast is scheduled to take place at around 9 a.m. local time Wednesday and will destroy nearly five tons of the chemical, held in 68 barrels, without which the partially-crystalized lead azide could explode at any time, particularly if exposed to friction or cold temperatures.

“Inaction is no longer an option,” said Maj. Gen. Eric Shirley, who is leading the operation to safely blow up the aging explosive, which represents the country’s entire stockpile.

Greene resigned from Congress in January this year after falling out with Trump, who she once passionately supported, over his handling of the Jeffrey Epstein scandal and other issues.

She has continued to attack him from the sidelines ever since and been nicknamed “Marjorie Traitor Brown” by the president in retaliation.
