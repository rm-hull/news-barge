Firefighters were called to a kitchen incident in York last night (Tuesday).

A crew from York went to a home on Tudor Road at 7.46pm after an air fryer had been left on a hob that had been accidentally turned on.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The air fryer had been removed from the property before crews arrival.
