Harrogate hair salon Kingsgrove has won two regional titles at the 2026 Salon Awards.

Kingsgrove was named Best Salon for Extensions, Wigs or Replacement, while the salon’s Tracy Roche won Best Extension, Wig or Replacement Artist 2026.

Tracy and colleague Jenny Hullah attended the grand final at the Leonardo Royal London Tower Bridge on Sunday 15th November.

Kingsgrove was a finalist in three categories.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b07004c6dd42ea2ed6.png)

The team advises clients experiencing hair loss and works with specialist partners to help them find a suitable option.

For clients having extensions fitted, the salon offers bespoke colour work to match their natural hair.

A spokesperson for Kingsgrove said:

> “Winning these two awards is a wonderful recognition of the passion, dedication and specialist knowledge that goes into what we do every day.
>
> "We couldn't be prouder of our team and what we have achieved over this past year.”

Kingsgrove says it is the only salon in Yorkshire to offer Gold Class naturally curly hair extensions, with clients travelling from as far away as the south of England for fittings.
