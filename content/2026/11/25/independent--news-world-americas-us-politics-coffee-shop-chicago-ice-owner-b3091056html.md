Staff members at a Chicago coffee shop have walked out on the job after accusations that an agent for Immigration and Customs Enforcement was the co-owner of the popular community spot.

A sign that read “permanently closed” hung on the door of Tasa Coffee Roasters in the Humboldt Park neighborhood Tuesday, a photo obtained by CBS News Chicago showed. The storefront had remnants of eggs that were seemingly thrown at the windows and what appeared to be a sign workers had written that was now torn off the glass.

Local nonprofit news site Block Club Chicago had shared a photo of the workers’ sign on Monday before it was taken down. It read, “After finding out from Reddit, that there are allegations Pierre Marquez is an ICE agent, we the baristas and roasters have closed for the day.

“We do not stand beside, condone, support, or accept anyone or any org associated with ICE. As the baristas, we are discussing what steps will be necessary for us. We intend to do our best as employees to be as honest and transparent as we can be.”

![A sign that read ‘permanently closed’ hung on the door of Tasa Coffee Roasters in the Humboldt Park neighborhood Tuesday, after workers walked out on the job over accusations that an agent for Immigration and Customs Enforcement was the co-owner of the popular community spot](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/22/50/Screenshot-2026-11-24-at-5-41-59-PM.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A recent post in a subreddit for the Humboldt Park community claimed that the ICE List, an unofficial database about ICE activity, listed Pierre Marquez as an agent.

“They have their ‘Hands Off Chicago’ sign and act like they're for the community, all while her husband is ripping families apart,” the Reddit user who made the post wrote.

Jackie Marquez opened Tasa Coffee Roasters with her husband Pierre Marquez in 2022, according to local reports.

“We're proud to show that two first-generation Latinos were able to create a space by Latinos, and not just for Latinos, for everyone," Pierre Marquez said in an October 2025 interview with CBS News Chicago.

![A recent Reddit post claimed that Pierre Marquez, who reportedly opened Tasa Coffee Roasters with his wife, Jackie Marquez, in 2022, was an ICE agent](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/22/34/GettyImages-2245411179.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

But Jackie Marquez seemed to distance her husband from the coffee shop in a post on Instagram Stories, calling herself the “sole owner.”

“I personally disagree with current immigration policy, and I believe in civil dialogue and mutual respect, even when we disagree,” the post read. “I ask that my employees not be targeted or held responsible for matters outside their control. My commitment remains firmly with my employees, my customers, and my community.”

*The Independent* has reached out to Tasa Coffee Roasters for comment.

The accusations against Pierre Marquez outraged the community, with customers and elected officials speaking out.

"I started coming here at 10 a.m. every single morning, Monday through Friday…It's Latina-owned. I'm Latina," former customer Alex Rodriguez told WLS. "It's just really disappointing to see that sometimes your own people will turn their backs on you."

The Northwest Side Progressive Coalition, which includes Democratic Congresswoman Delia C. Ramirez and other local politicians, said it was “no longer supporting, promoting, or partnering” with the coffee shop following the accusations against Pierre Marquez.

“As our immigrant neighbors are living with the fear that someone they love could be taken into detention, we reject any and all businesses that profit from or enable ICE’s terror.”

![A spokesperson for ICE declined to confirm whether Pierre Marquez worked for the agency or in another role at the Department of Homeland Security, telling The Independent , ‘We don’t confirm or deny officer identities’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/22/20/GettyImages-2234145111.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

It’s been nearly two years since President Donald Trump took office and kicked off his massive deportation campaign. Stories of federal immigration agents fatally shooting U.S. citizens and detaining a five-year-old boy in Minnesota earlier this year sparked huge public outcry. Many Americans find ICE’s tactics too aggressive and have taken to the streets of major cities, including Chicago, to protest the agency.

The Northwest Side Progressive Coalition mentioned Operation Midway Blitz, an ICE operation targeting illegal immigrants in Chicago that started in September 2025, in Monday’s statement, saying the “terror” created by it “kept families home, and neighborhood businesses lost customers they depended on.”

A spokesperson for ICE declined to confirm whether Pierre Marquez worked for the agency or in another role at the Department of Homeland Security.

“We don’t confirm or deny officer identities. Doxxing our officers puts their lives and their families in serious danger. Our law enforcement officers are on the front lines arresting terrorists, gang members, murderers, pedophiles, and rapists. Now, thanks to the malicious rhetoric of sanctuary politicians, they are under constant threat from violent agitators,” the spokesperson said.

ICE agents are “experiencing coordinated campaigns of violence against them and facing more than a 1,600% increase in assaults against them, a 3,300% increase in vehicular attacks, and an 8,000% increase in death threats,” the spokesperson said, adding, “Attempting to publicize their identities puts their lives and the lives of their families at serious risk.”

*The Independent* has not verified the agency’s figures.
