## Newly refurbished York pub forced to close

A sign in the window of The Postern Gate *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A POPULAR York city centre pub has reportedly closed its doors today after a leak.

The Postern Gate in Piccadilly, York, has shut its doors on Wednesday (November 25) after reports of a leak from the ceiling.

The pub, which has a patio backing on to the River Foss, takes its name from the medieval tower called Fishergate Postern – which was previously on the banks of the river - and is just around the corner from Fishergate Bar.

Luke Brewer, who was drinking in the pub earlier this afternoon, said that the closure took place shortly after 1.30pm today.

He said: “It didn’t look great. Water was coming down from the ceiling near the bar – the staff basically said we needed to finish up our drinks and leave.

“I saw others in the pub had food and I think a few people were given time to finish up their food. I’m not sure what I’ll do for the rest of day, but I thought I’d get in touch to make sure people don’t go there expecting service.”

A spokesperson for Wetherspoons said: "The pub is currently closed, there was a burst pipe in the Travelodge above us. We are waiting for the water to stop before we can get emergency contractors here to check that we are safe to re-open.

"I suspect that this will take a few hours, although we are unsure at this time."
