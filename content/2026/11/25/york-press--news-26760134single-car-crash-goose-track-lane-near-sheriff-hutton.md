Emergency services were called out to a single-car crash north of York on Tuesday evening.

Fire crews from York and Malton went to the scene on Goose Track Lane, near Sheriff Hutton, where they found all occupants already out of the vehicle when they arrived.

**Recommended reads:**

The single-vehicle incident happened at 5.44pm.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service said: "The accident was left in the hands of the police."
