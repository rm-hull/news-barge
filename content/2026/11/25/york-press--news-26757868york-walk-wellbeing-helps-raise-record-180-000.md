York’s hospitality community has helped raise a record £180,000 through the UK-wide Walk for Wellbeing.

Funds raised will support Hospitality Action, a charity that assists hospitality workers and their families during times of need.

The York leg of the event began at The Milner York Hotel and followed a scenic 20km route along the River Ouse to Bishopthorpe and back.

Kai Ikulayo, chair of Hospitality Association York and event host, said: "It was a fantastic experience to see so many colleagues, friends, and industry collaborators come together for this year's Walk for Wellbeing in York.

"Raising funds to support those who have dedicated their careers to the hospitality industry was rewarding in itself."

The charity walk began in 2020 and has since raised more than £680,000 nationwide, helping workers with debt relief, mental health support, and counselling.

Walk for Wellbeing will return from September 24 to October 10, 2027.
