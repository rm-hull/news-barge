A charity founded in Harrogate is looking for walkers to climb Malawi’s highest mountain in 2027.

Open Arms Malawi, which began in Harrogate 26 years ago, has opened registration for its next Mount Mulanje Challenge.

The trip runs from 29th June to 6th July and includes a safari in Majete Wildlife Reserve.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b06b27922ce642a798.png)

Eight women from Harrogate were among the 12 people who took on the challenge in 2025.

Between them, they raised £32,500 to help orphaned and vulnerable children in Malawi grow up within their own families.

Before the climb, the group visited nursery schools and met some of the families supported by the charity.

They then spent three days trekking to Sapitwa Peak, staying overnight in mountain huts on their way to the 3,002-metre summit.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b06b27922ce642a798.png)

Suzie Thomas of Open Arms Malawi said:

> “The Mount Mulanje Challenge was an incredible experience.
>
> "Not only did participants achieve something remarkable by reaching the summit, but they also gained a deeper understanding of life in Malawi and the work we do alongside children and families.
>
> “Many people describe the trip as life-changing.
>
> "The combination of visiting our projects, meeting local families and taking on such a significant challenge creates memories that stay with you long after you return home while also forging lasting friendships."

She added:

> “We're excited to be launching the 2027 challenge and would love to hear from anyone who is looking for an adventure with real purpose.”

Walkers can sign up without previous trekking experience, although they should have a good level of fitness and be prepared to train.

*Places are limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Anyone interested can call Open Arms Malawi on 01423 530129 or email [hello@openarmsmalawi.org.](mailto:hello@openarmsmalawi.org.)*
