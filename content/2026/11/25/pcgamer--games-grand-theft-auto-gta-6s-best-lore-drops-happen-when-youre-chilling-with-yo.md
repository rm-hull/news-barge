![Jason and Lucia holding hands in Grand Theft Auto 6.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MybRDJeCE4mrHokyi4PpA5-1920-80.png)

My real relationship be damned, this last week has been all about playing cupid in Grand Theft Auto 6. I've become utterly invested in keeping Jason and Lucia in love, which means a lot of my time in the game has been spent doing things with my partner in tow. Hitting the gym, inviting them out for a late-night bite, robbing a few jewellery stores. You know, couple things. Things couples do.

And, as people tend to do when they're spending a lot of time together, Jason and Lucia talk. A lot. And you know what? Some of the best nuggets of information about their lives—both their time spent together before the events of the game, and their own individual lives before meeting one another—are tucked away in these interactions that fill the game's quieter moments.

![Relaxing in GTA 6](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qXq3E8NKHzC2dgtb2ddH6D-1200-80.jpg)

Sometimes the interactions are simply fluffy, or funny. Lucia poking fun at Jason for his deep voice and monosyllabic responses, or them reminiscing about little moments that happened in their relationship before the whole "Lucia ghosting Jason and ending up in jail" thing. Sometimes it's Lucia getting a spam call and giving the bot (and eventual human) on the other end of the phone an absolute earful.

But then, occasionally, beyond all the comedic conversations and flavour dialogue that give these characters so much life, the game'll drop some proper good lore. I'll do my best not to spoil stuff here, but for almost every complaint I've seen on social media about why certain protagonists did certain things, there's an answer tucked away in these interactions. The kind of things you'd really want to know about these characters? Your best chance of finding it all out is when you bring them together.

I've really enjoyed having lore drops unfold in mundane conversations while walking around, driving, or eating a burger, rather than hamfisted into the main story. They feel like natural breathing moments for the couple to recount the past, or get into the stuff that was happening in the time that they were apart. You know, the exact kind of times actual couples and friends would find the time to dig into these things in real life, too.

![Grand Theft Auto 6 - Jason driving while Lucia looks out at Vice City from the passenger](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qssUN2VdpiBrvmcfvqWGdK-1200-80.jpg)

That doesn't mean you have to keep them together all the time. Some of their exclusive missions also provide a great bit of insight into their backstories—like Jason's side stuff with ex-army pal Bret Vogel, or the missions that have Lucia in some all-too-real situations with her mother.

I've played plenty of the game flying solo as both protagonists too, and you'll occasionally get some of this nattering just by hanging around in your abode together. But I can't recommend enough that you find plenty of opportunities to bring them together out in the open world. Even if it's just for 15 minutes here and there, you'll uncover a whole lot about who they were before we first got to know them as the player. And as someone who has really enjoyed learning just about everything about these two as I can, it's been one of my favourite parts of the game so far.
