A key member of a Harrogate charity has spoken about his own experiences of sleeping rough as the charity launches a hard-hitting campaign to ensure that nobody is homeless this Christmas.

Caius is the Day Centre Chef at Harrogate Homeless Project, an independent charity which was founded in 1992 to support people out of homelessness.

After spending 20 years living and working abroad, Caius returned to the UK only to find himself facing a series of circumstances beyond his control that left him homeless and sleeping in a tent on the outskirts of Harrogate.

Through autumn and winter, he endured freezing temperatures, isolation and uncertainty, often sitting in a launderette simply to keep warm.

Today, thanks to the support he received from Harrogate Homeless Project, Caius has rebuilt his life.

He now has a home, a job and a future, and works every day supporting others facing many of the challenges he once experienced himself.

Speaking as part of the appeal, Caius said: "I never imagined I would be homeless.

“The hardest part wasn't the cold, it was feeling invisible.

“I thought no one cared, then I found Harrogate Homeless Project.

“They gave me the first hot meal I'd had in weeks, practical support and kindness when I needed it most.

“I was lucky to get the support I needed. Not everyone does."

Caius’s life story is the focus of a festive campaign by Harrogate Homeless Project which features a hard-hitting new poster.

The poster highlights the stark reality that homelessness can be life-threatening.

Every year, 1,720 people die while homeless in the UK, while the average age of death for a long-term rough sleeper is around 45 years old.

Through accommodation, practical support, warm meals, advice and ongoing encouragement, Harrogate Homeless Project helps people rebuild their lives and move towards a more stable future.

The charity supports individuals facing a wide range of challenges, including bereavement, relationship breakdown, poor health, addiction, financial hardship and job loss.

This Christmas, the charity is encouraging local residents, businesses and community groups to donate and help provide safety, support and hope to people experiencing homelessness,

Donations can help fund everything from essential items and practical support to specialist services, accommodation and long-term recovery support.

Richard Cooper, CEO at Harrogate Homeless Project said: "Despite Harrogate's reputation as an affluent area, homelessness remains a very real issue within our community.

“Every day we support people who have nowhere else to turn.

“This Christmas, we're asking local people to help us ensure that nobody facing homelessness feels forgotten or alone.

“Every donation, no matter the size, can make a meaningful difference*.*"

Harrogate Homeless Project provides people experiencing homelessness with a safe place to stay and the support they need to build confidence and move towards independent living.
