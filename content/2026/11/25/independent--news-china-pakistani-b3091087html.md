China on Wednesday launched the Shenzhou 24 spacecraft with three astronauts, sending the first foreign crew member to its space station.

Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, is flying alongside Chinese astronauts Tang Hongbo, 51, and Wu Yi, 39 after the craft blasted off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China. The launch marked the first joint flight involving Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China’s manned space program.
