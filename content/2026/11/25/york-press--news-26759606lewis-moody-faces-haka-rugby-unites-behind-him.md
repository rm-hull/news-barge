## Powerful Haka moment shows what makes rugby so special

I have loved Rugby Union since I was a young boy.

I love the game itself, but I also love the camaraderie and friendships that it creates away from the pitch.

Saturday’s game at Twickenham delivered another iconic rugby moment, as England’s former captain, Lewis Moody, once again stepped onto the pitch to face the legendary Haka.

However, Lewis is now facing the biggest battle of his life as he fights Motor Neurone Disease.

The rugby community showed on Saturday that we stand firmly alongside Lewis, his family, and his friends during a time when they need all the support they can get.

We have already witnessed the devastating impact this cruel disease has had on rugby great Doddie Weir and Rugby League legend Rob Burrow.

Saturday was another reminder of what makes rugby so special.

It is more than just a sport. It is a family that comes together in its greatest moments and stands united through its toughest challenges.

## A sad end to a wonderful theme

What a sad end to such a beautiful idea.

Started by an idea by Tracey Ostle turning this badly neglected riverside walk into a wonderful spellbinding walk down the side of the River Foss.

An enchanting walk for children and adults alike.

The Foss Fairy Trail was a credit to Tracey, and the hard work she put into maintaining the Trail over the years, despite the vandalism by these feckless individuals, hell bent on causing mayhem and destruction.

Now with her team of helpers falling by the wayside, probably because of the thankless task in trying to maintain the walk and with Tracey being the last person left standing, she is now turning off the lights on the fairy trail experiment.

But what will replace it, a bleak path, or perhaps a grim trail.

Thank you Tracey from myself and on behalf of all the others who you enchanted with your efforts.
