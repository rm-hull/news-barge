## Join Lilly Douse in the Czech Republic as she visits royal castles, explores its historic capital, and tries her hand at traditional Bohemian glassmaking.

The Czech Republic is home to around 2,000 castles, palaces, and ruins. Recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage site, the city’s historic centre is home to **Prague Castle**, one of the world's largest castle complexes.

Here, Lilly meets Miloslava Nevolova from the **Prague Tourism Board** to learn about the castle’s history. Known as the ‘City of a Hundred Spires’, Prague is famous for its medieval and Gothic architecture.

They then visit Old Town Square before visiting the Mucha Foundation, which celebrates the work of renowned Czech artist Alphonse Mucha.

During her time in the city, Lilly stays at the **W Prague**, housed in the carefully restored former Grand Hotel Evropa on Wenceslas Square.

At **Myšák**, a historic bakery and café, Lilly discovers the country's pastry traditions and enjoys some traditional Czech pastries.

To burn off the sweets, Lilly climbs **Petřín Tower**, one of Prague's most recognisable landmarks. Inspired by the Eiffel Tower, it offers panoramic views across the city's rooftops, church spires and historic skyline.

Outside Prague, Lilly travels into the Bohemian countryside to explore **Křivoklát Castle**, one of the Czech Republic's oldest and most important royal castles.

Her journey finishes with a hands-on introduction to traditional Bohemian glassmaking. At **Rückl Glassworks**, Lilly learns how the crystal is made, meets the craftspeople and tries her hand at glassmaking.
