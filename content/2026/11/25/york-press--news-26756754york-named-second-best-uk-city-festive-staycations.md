York has been named the second-best city for a festive staycation.

Research by Joules found the city offers excellent options for dining, attractions, and affordability.

York's appeal lies in its density of attractions, boasting 705 highly rated sites, the highest per capita of all cities analysed.

Alongside these, the city also has 522 four-star-plus restaurants, offering ample spots for a cosy meal after a day of exploring.

For those seeking a quieter escape, York offers 21 parks, providing a peaceful retreat from the festive hustle and bustle.

The affordability of York also contributes to its high ranking, with average December hotel prices at just £80, making it one of the most budget-friendly options in the top 10.

York’s high search interest for Christmas-related events also highlights its popularity as a festive destination.

The overall score of 7.59 out of 10 placed York just behind Bath, which took the top spot with a score of 7.62.

Other cities in the top five included Edinburgh, Chester, and Nottingham.

This recognition reinforces York's status as a prime spot for those looking to enjoy the festive season.

The full guide can be found at [https://www.joules.com/blog/the-best-uk-cities-for-a-festive-staycation](https://www.joules.com/blog/the-best-uk-cities-for-a-festive-staycation).
