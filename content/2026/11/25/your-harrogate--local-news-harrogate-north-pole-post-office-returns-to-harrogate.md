Children can once again post their letters to Father Christmas on Harrogate’s Cheltenham Parade.

Harrogate International Festivals has reopened its North Pole Post Office at 32 Cheltenham Parade, where families will find fairy lights, festive music and even snow flurries as they drop off their letters.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b0690c062734b6f4d4.png)

Viv Poskitt, The Mayor of Harrogate, joined Mark Askew from The Ivy Harrogate, Jason Maxwell from Harrogate BID and Sharon Canavar from Harrogate International Festivals to officially launch this year’s post office.

A spokesperson for Harrogate BID said:

> "With twinkling fairy lights, festive music and even snow flurries, it’s the perfect place to share those all-important Christmas wishes."

*Families can also download a letter from Father Christmas by scanning the QR code at the post box or visiting the Harrogate International Festivals**website**.*
