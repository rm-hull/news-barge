One person was taken to hospital after a fire in a North Yorkshire village last night (Tuesday)

Two crews from Selby responded to reports of smoke coming up through the floorboards of a home in Northfield, Barlby, at 8.35pm.

The smoke was traced to a pan fire in a neighbouring property.

#### Northfield, Barlby

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews gained entry to the neighbouring property and led one person to safety who was then taken to hospital for checks.

“The fire was out on arrival and crews cleared the property of heavy smoke logging using a positive pressure ventilation fan.”
