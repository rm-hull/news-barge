A suspected stolen mobility scooter has been recovered by police in York.

Officers believe the scooter, found by a local York neighbourhood team, may have been stolen and are appealing for the owner to come forward.

**Recommended reads:**

Proof of purchase or other documentation will be required to reclaim the scooter.  
If this is your scooter, call police on 101 and quote reference number 12260230644.
