## Sewing Bee winner Anna to share show secrets at special York event

Winner of The Great British Sewing Bee 2026, Anna Pownall, will share her stories from her time on the BBC hit show at a special event in January *(Image: BBC One)*

THE winner of BBC One’s The Great British Sewing Bee 2026 is set to share stories from her time on the hit television show at a special fundraising event in York.

Anna Pownall, who also works for St Leonard’s Hospice, will headline An Afternoon with Anna at The Milner Hotel on Friday, January 22, 2027.

The event will give fans the chance to hear Anna discuss her Sewing Bee journey, view some of her creations from the series up close and ask questions about her experience.




Tickets include a welcome drink, a two-course meal and a gift bag, with all proceeds going to St Leonard’s Hospice.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21466728.jpg?type=mds-article-620)"Having the opportunity to talk about my time on the show and showcase my designs while raising money for our services is incredible" *(Image: St Leonard's Hospice)*

The York charity provides care and support for people affected by terminal illness and bereavement across the city and surrounding area.

Speaking ahead of the event, Anna said: “I’m really excited to visit The Milner in January.

"I love being part of the Retail team at St Leonard’s and I’m so passionate about the work the Hospice does.

"Having the opportunity to talk about my time on the show and showcase my designs while raising money for our services is incredible.

“There’s so much that I want to share, and I’m really looking forward to answering people’s questions. I hope it’ll be a really inspiring and enjoyable way to spend an afternoon.”

Channon Barlow, Head of Fundraising at St Leonard’s Hospice, said: “We’re so grateful to Anna for donating her time for this event.

“We know that The Great British Sewing Bee has a huge following, and people were blown away by Anna’s performance and designs on the show. It’s such a special opportunity to be able to find out more about her experience in this kind of forum.

“As a charity, 75 per cent of our funding comes from our community in York and the surrounding area. It’s because of events like this that St Leonard’s can continue providing specialist palliative and end-of-life care without charge.

“We’re so proud of all that Anna’s achieved, and we really think this is an event that people won’t want to miss.”

An Afternoon with Anna takes place at The Milner, Station Road, York, on Friday, January 22, 2027.

Tickets cost £60 and are available via the St Leonard’s Hospice website: [https://stleonardshospice.org.uk/anna/](https://stleonardshospice.org.uk/anna/) .
