EMERGENCY services have been called to reports of a fire in a York city centre building.

The incident took place in Micklegate at 12.12pm today (Wednesday, November 25).

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said it believed the cause of the fire to have been deliberate.




A service spokesperson said: “York and Acomb crews were mobilised to reports of a fire in a building in Micklegate.

“Crews used positive pressure ventilation, thermal imaging camera and knapsack sprayers.

“The incident was left in the hands of police.

“The cause is believed to have been deliberate.”
