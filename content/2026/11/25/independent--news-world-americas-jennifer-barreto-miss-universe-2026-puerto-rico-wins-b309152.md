Jennifer Barreto made history late Tuesday by becoming the first mother and first married contestant to win Miss Universe, sparking widespread, joyful celebration across her mountain hometown in Puerto Rico.

Located in western Puerto Rico's lush mountains, San Sebastián is famed for karst rock formations that earned its local residents the long-standing nickname "the cucumbers."

"It was a day for the history books!" exclaimed one energetic municipal worker at the mayor’s office, which was brimming with local excitement.

The community of roughly 39,000 residents could hardly hold back its enthusiasm, with "La Barreto," lingering on everyone's lips shortly after her triumph.

![Miss Universe 2026 Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/15/2306829617..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"So excited!" said María Rivera as she handled orders Wednesday morning at El Guayabal bakery. "We were sure she was going to win."

Barreto, 27, is a mother to one daughter and holds master’s degrees in both clinical social work and research psychology. She works as a prevention specialist for the U.S. Department of Defense, creating strategies to reduce harmful behaviors.

"Her journey is a powerful reminder that motherhood, marriage and personal ambition are not mutually exclusive, and that a woman’s potential should never be defined by the stage of life she is in," Ronald Day, CEO of the Miss Universe Organization, said in a statement.

Pageant policy opened to married women and mothers in 2023. The contest has lost appeal in several nations, where critics contend that it objectifies women and reinforces unrealistic physical standards.

Barreto became the sixth Puerto Rican woman to secure the Miss Universe crown, ending a 20-year wait for the island.

Her hometown previously gained fame for "Pepino Power," a volunteer initiative organized to restore electricity after Hurricane Maria struck Puerto Rico in 2017 as a Category 4 storm.

![Miss Puerto Rico Jennifer Barreto, left, next to Miss Colombia María Alejandra López](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/15/download..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Puerto Rico currently ranks third in total Miss Universe victories, behind the United States with nine titles and Venezuela with seven.

Spectators cheered loudly at Puerto Rico’s José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum every time Barreto advanced through the final rounds of the event.

She brought her hands to her face in shock when named Miss Universe 2026, defeating Miss Dominican Republic and Miss Venezuela, before joyfully dancing on stage to ABBA’s "Dancing Queen."

Across the U.S. territory, thousands of Puerto Ricans shared in the dance and celebration.

"It’s a truly spectacular achievement," San Sebastián Mayor Eladio Cardona Quiles told T*he Associated Press*. "All the most beautiful women in the universe are there, and she managed to stand out. I’m happy she represented ‘los pepinianos.’"

Cardona attended the pageant in person, while local fans gathered in San Sebastián’s public plaza to watch on a large screen before forming a late-night caravan, honking their horns to celebrate Barreto’s victory.
