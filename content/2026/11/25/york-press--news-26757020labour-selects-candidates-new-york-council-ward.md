York Labour Party has selected its candidates for the new Fulford and Fishergate ward ahead of the next council elections.

The party has chosen Faizal Mohamudbuccus to stand alongside current councillors Kate Ravilious and Conrad Whitcroft.

Mr Mohamudbuccus, who runs Broadway Lodge Residential Home in Fulford, could become York’s first Muslim councillor if elected.

In 2023, he helped set up and run York Mosque Community Kitchen, which has provided free lunches and social connection to hundreds of people from all walks of life over the past few years.

Mr Mohamudbuccus said: "Caring for people and trying to make the place where we live better for everyone is a huge motivation for me.

"It’s a great honour to be selected as a candidate for my home ward of Fulford and Fishergate.

"Having been born in York and lived in the city all my life, I have seen how York has developed over the years.

"I hope that if elected, I’ll be able to put my experience, enterprise and compassion to the service of local residents and the great city of York."

Councillor Whitcroft paid tribute to outgoing councillor Sarah Wilson and welcomed Mr Mohamudbuccus to the team.

He said: "It has been a pleasure to serve alongside Sarah, and I look forward to working with Faizal and Cllr Ravilious on behalf of Fulford and Fishergate residents.

"York is a great place, and this Labour Administration has created a trustworthy and stable Council that represents the whole city, one that people can trust."

Councillor Ravilious said she was "thrilled" to be standing for re-election.

She said: "York Labour councillors come from all walks of life and the diversity of our candidates is a huge strength, helping us to really understand the needs of all residents.

"I’m delighted that residents in Fulford and Fishergate will now have the opportunity to elect York’s first Muslim councillor."

Councillor Wilson, who is stepping down, thanked residents and colleagues for their support during her time in office.

She said: "It has been a great honour to represent Fishergate Ward since Cllr Whitcroft and I were first elected in May 2023."
