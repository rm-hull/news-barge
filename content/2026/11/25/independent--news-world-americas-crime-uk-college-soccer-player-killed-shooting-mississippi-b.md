A British student playing college soccer in the U.S. was shot dead outside of a popular taco shop in Mississippi, authorities said.

Danny Luna, 22, was arrested Tuesday and charged with murder in the death of Krissh Mahatma, an 18-year-old from London, England, who played soccer at Copiah-Lincoln Community College in Wesson, about 45 miles south of Jackson, WLBT reported.

Mahatma was shot around 3:39 a.m. Sunday outside of Green Ghost Tacos, a popular taco restaurant in downtown Jackson. The restaurant was closed at the time of the shooting.

He was taken in a private vehicle to a nearby hospital, where he died, Hinds County Coroner Jeramiah Howard said.

Mahatma and a group of people had been at the restaurant before the shooting, which allegedly unfolded in a parking lot across the street as the group was leaving, sources told WLBT.

![Krissh Mahatma, a freshman soccer player at Copiah-Lincoln Community College in Mississippi, was shot and killed outside a taco restaurant over the weekend](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/25/15/20/Krissh-Mahatma.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Witnesses said that Mahatma’s group noticed an intoxicated man as they were leaving the restaurant and offered to give him a ride home. That offer turned into an argument that eventually became violent, leading to the man pulling out a gun and firing at the group, the witnesses told the *Dark Horse Press.*

Authorities have not shared a motive for the shooting.

“Copiah-Lincoln Community College is mourning the loss of freshman soccer player Krissh Mahatma,” Dr. Dewayne Middleton, the college’s president, said in a statement. “Krissh will be deeply missed by his teammates and the entire Co-Lin family. We ask for your thoughts and prayers for his family, friends, and all who loved him.”

Capitol Police Chief Mark Miller said Luna’s arrest was a crucial step toward justice while acknowledging the heartbreaking loss suffered by Mahatma’s loved ones.

“Our thoughts and prayers remain with the victim’s family and loved ones during this difficult time,” Miller said.

Police said their investigation remains ongoing, and that they would provide additional information when it becomes available.

![Authorities arrested Danny Luna on Tuesday in connection with the fatal shooting](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/25/15/37/Danny-Luna.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Green Ghost Tacos, the restaurant near where the shooting unfolded, shared their condolences with Mahatma’s loved ones — and reiterated in a statement that they were closed at the time of the shooting.

“The safety and well-being of our patrons, employees, and surrounding community remain a top priority. We are committed to cooperating fully with law enforcement as they investigate the circumstances surrounding this tragic incident,” the restaurant said in a statement. “Out of respect for the grieving family and the investigation, we will refrain from speculating or commenting further on the details at this time.”

The restaurant added: “Above all, we recognize that a family has suffered an unimaginable loss. We ask that the community join us in extending compassion, prayers, and support to those affected during this incredibly difficult time."

Mahatma wore No. 11 and played forward for Co-Lin’s soccer team, according to the Dark Horse Press.

The soccer team announced his signing in February, posting a graphic along with the message: “Welcome to the Wolfpack, Krissh.”

Before coming to Mississippi, Mahatma played for Watford’s “A” team in England. The Football Association’s records list him as having three goals across 17 appearances during the 2024-2025 season.
