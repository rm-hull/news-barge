A classic Hershey's Milk Chocolate bar will cost Southern California fans just 5 cents today, matching the price the candy carried when it went on sale in 1900.

The discount is a one-day promotion from Hershey's, timed to the theatrical release of "Hershey," a film about company founder Milton Hershey. According to Hershey's, the 5-cent offer runs while supplies last through select retailers and experiences.

Walmart is one of them. Shoppers can buy a bar for 5 cents through online pickup orders placed on Walmart.com starting at 6 a.m. Wednesday. A Walmart account is required, and each person is limited to five bars.

![Southern California Hershey fans will be able to buy a classic Hershey's Milk Chocolate bar for 5 cents today](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/19/13/45/GettyImages-452235886.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Customers in Walmart stores have another route: buy a bar, then submit the receipt through the Ibotta app to receive cash back, Hershey's said. The offer cannot be used for home shipping. Amazon Grocery is also taking part, offering 5-cent bars through same-day delivery in qualifying areas from 6 a.m.

The company said customers must use a promotional code, to be posted on Hershey's social media channels, to receive free shipping. Through Amazon, the limit is two bars per person, while supplies last. Taxes and delivery fees may apply.

Hershey's Chocolate World locations in Hershey, Pennsylvania, and Times Square in New York will sell the bars for 5 cents plus tax, capped at 10 per person. Alongside the price cut, Hershey's has released a limited-edition collection of vintage-inspired packaging drawing on elements from the brand's history.

The film stars Finn Wittrock as Milton Hershey and Alexandra Daddario as his wife, Kitty Hershey, and is directed by Mark Waters. Angel Studios will release the PG-rated film in theaters on Wednesday.
