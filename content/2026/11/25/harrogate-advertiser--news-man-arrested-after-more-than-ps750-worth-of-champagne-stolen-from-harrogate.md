A suspected thief has been remanded in custody after stealing more than £750 worth of Champagne from a number of shops in Harrogate.

North Yorkshire Police has arrested a man in his 30s following reports of luxury French fizz being taken from stores on Skipton Road and Railway Road last week.

The first incident took place at Tesco on Skipton Road on Wednesday afternoon.

Police say the suspect entered the store, selected several bottles of Champagne, hid them inside a shopping bag and left without paying.

Officers said the man allegedly returned to the store just minutes later and repeated the theft before leaving again without making payment.

Later that afternoon, at around 2.30pm, the suspect is alleged to have entered Sainsbury’s on Railway Road, where he selected four bottles of Champagne and attempted to leave the shop without paying.

A member of the store’s security team detained the man and contacted police.

Officers attended the scene and searched a vehicle linked to the suspect.

During the search, a substantial quantity of alcohol was recovered from the boot.

As part of their investigation, police also carried out further checks and discovered the man was wanted on a warrant issued by a court in Worcestershire.

The suspect, who is from the Stoke area, was arrested and charged with theft, the outstanding warrant offence, driving without valid insurance and driving without a full licence.

He has since been remanded in custody and is due to appear before magistrates.
