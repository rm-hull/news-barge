Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents have arrested an illegal immigrant days after he was pictured claiming a $1 million lottery prize.

The Massachusetts State Lottery shared a photo of Ariel Antonio Velasquez-Morales holding a giant check in his hands on Facebook last Saturday.

Velasquez-Morales was all smiles after winning a huge lot from the $2,000,000 50X Cashword game in East Boston. The lottery said it was his first time playing the $10 scratch-off and that he matched an impressive 11 words.

On Tuesday, Velasquez-Morales was arrested by ICE agents during operations in East Boston, the agency’s local field office said on X, alongside the same photo of the lotto winner holding his $1 million check.

“Looks like his luck ran out,” ICE Assistant Director of Public Affairs Micah Bock said in a press release about the arrest Wednesday.

ICE Boston said that Velasquez-Morales was from El Salvador and that he illegally entered the U.S. in May 2013. Nearly a year later, an immigration judge ordered his deportation in April 2014, according to the field office.

Velasquez-Morales attempted to flee arrest on Tuesday, Fox News reported. Three sources from ICE told the outlet that he ran when agents tried to take him into custody, but that he was quickly arrested.

In a follow-up post Wednesday, ICE Boston celebrated Velasquez-Morales’ arrest, writing, “Welcome to the ERO [Enforcement and Removal Operations] Boston lottery! Where illegal aliens and sanctuary politicians are out of luck, and the law-abiding citizens of New England are always the winners!”

Velasquez-Morales had chosen the cash option for his lottery prize, receiving a one-time lump sum of $650,000 before taxes, ICE said in Wednesday’s press release.

An official from the Massachusetts State Lottery told *The Independent* that anyone 18 years or older who is physically present in the state can buy lottery tickets at retail locations and win prizes. It’s unclear what will happen to Velasquez-Morales’ money now that he is detained.

![Velasquez-Morales will remain in ICE custody until he is removed from the U.S., according to the agency](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/17/48/GettyImages-2258656470.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Velasquez-Morales will remain in ICE custody until he is removed from the U.S., according to the agency.

“Criminal illegal aliens are not welcome in the United States and will be removed as quickly as possible,” the press release read. “Under President Trump and Secretary Mullin’s leadership, ICE is delivering on its mission to enforce our nation’s immigration laws, restore accountability and protect American communities.”

Earlier this month, ICE announced that it had broken the record for the highest number of immigrant arrests and deportations in agency history in the 2026 fiscal year with more than 491,000 arrests and more than 483,000 deportations.

“Thanks to President Trump’s leadership, ICE has been unleashed to target and remove illegal aliens so that we can save American lives,” Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin has said.

But many Americans find ICE’s tactics too aggressive, with the fatal shootings of two U.S. citizens by federal immigration agents last January causing a huge wave of public outcry.

President Donald Trump even admitted after a crushing GOP midterm defeat that his massive deportation agenda was “too tough.”
