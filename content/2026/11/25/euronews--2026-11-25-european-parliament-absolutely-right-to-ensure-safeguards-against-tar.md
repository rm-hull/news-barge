Irish Renew MEP speaks to Stefan Grobe on the upcoming EU budget negotiations.

He warns against being "dramatic" considering the new trading relationshp with Donald Trump's Washington. He believes the European Parliament was "absolutely right" to ensure safeguards in the EU-US tarriff deal, and that the US administration "appears to have learned nothing" from the Republican party's defeat in their midterm elections earlier this month.

The US administration is trying to reinstate the so-called "Liberation Day" tarriffs imposed by Donald Trump, and then cast as illegal by the US Supreme Court. Andrews says that "we need clarification", and that Europeans have to "sit and wait" to learn what, if anything, the new proposed tariff measures mean for the transatlantic trading relationship.

He also reiterates the fact that the Parliament added further "safeguard agreements" such that the EU would be able to "safeguard industry at all costs."
