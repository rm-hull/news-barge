A new Harrogate store has revealed the date for its opening in the middle of town - and it is only a matter of weeks.

Work is well under way on new Harrogate branch of Neptune, which was founded in Wiltshire in 1996 by two friends, John Sims-Hilditch and Giles Redman.

Located at 47-49 James Street, near Princes Street, where ProCook shop was formerly, Neptune is renowned for high quality furniture and kitchens - and never using MDF or chipboard.

Co-founder John Sims-Hilditch said the business still kept faith with its philosophy of crafting simple, well-made pieces for the home, guided by a belief that everyday living deserves better design.

“We believe good design should last a lifetime,” he said.

“It's why we choose natural materials that develop character with age and why we back our furniture and kitchen collections with a lifetime guarantee.

"We never use MDF or chipboard, instead choosing solid timber and natural materials that can be maintained, refinished, and enjoyed for years as they develop character over time.”

After 30 years of making houses into homes, its new location is currently a flurry of activity as it refurbishes its Harrogate address to ensure its shows off its own quality and skills when it opens.

"We're looking forward to opening our doors early next year,” said John Sims-Hilditch.

“We believe the best homes are built slowly, lived in fully and enjoyed for generations.”

Neptune is also legendary for extending the life of its furniture by carefully restoring ex-display and photoshoot pieces at itsWiltshire workshop before they are returned to use.
