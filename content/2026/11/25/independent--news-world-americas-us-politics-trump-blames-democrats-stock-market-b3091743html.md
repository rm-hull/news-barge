President Donald Trump took credit Monday for the stock market rebound following his intervention to get disgraced Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reinstated, hailing it a “great day” for the country.

Two days later — the same day that the Trump administration unveiled a wave of new tariffs on China and Europe — the president is blaming the Democrats for the stock market dip.

“Stocks are down sharply because of the November Third Election results,” Trump posted Wednesday on Truth Social. “I wasn’t on the Ballot, but if I was, the results, just like all three times before, would have been much different, and the Stock Market, and your 401-k’s, would be BOOMING.”

“I told you so!!!” he gloated, in contrast to his celebratory tone earlier in the week after he personally waded into the leadership crisis at Nvidia and helped restore Huang as CEO of the billionaire AI-chip-making company.

Nvidia’s stock suffered after Huang stepped down as CEO on November 12 following a confession that he had been unfaithful to his wife of more than 40 years.

![President Donald Trump took credit Monday for the stock market rebound after his intervention to get disgraced Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reinstated, but was blaming Democrats for a slump two days later](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/25/17/50/GettyImages-2301532592.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“I watched that and, you know, I better get involved,” Trump reacted Monday on CNBC. “If I didn’t get involved, you’d probably have a very sad Nvidia for a long time to come.”

After Huang was reinstated, Nvidia’s stock soared 31 percent as Wall Street enjoyed its best day since April 2025.

“I believe my intervention was very important,” the president said. He explained that he held off from interfering previously but decided to speak to Nvidia’s board “directly and indirectly” over the weekend.

“And I felt more strongly as I saw what was happening with the company, because that was to me a very important part of our country,” he said. “When the stock starts dropping 40, 50 percent, it was a lot easier for me to do it yesterday.”

Trump acknowledged the situation could change.

“I’ve seen some pretty good comebacks, but I tell you, what you have to have for a comeback really is longevity,” he told CNBC. “So, you know, we can’t call it a comeback one day, but I think you’re going to see some tremendous numbers out of this company.”

![Trump said, ‘I told you so!!!’ after celebrating the stock market rebound on Monday. He personally waded into the leadership crisis at Nvidia and helped restore Jensen Huang as CEO of the billionaire AI-chip-making company](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/24/16/40/GettyImages-2297774940-(1).jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

At the end of the call with the outlet, Trump celebrated “a great day for the market” and “for the country” but his tune changed Wednesday – a pattern that did not go unnoticed by his critics on social media.

“Stocks go up: Trump did it,” the progressive *MediasTouch* outlet posted on X. “Stocks go down: Democrats did it.”

“Didn't he take credit for the stocks going up on Monday?” another left-leaning account quipped.

“Maybe stocks are down because the U.S. economy has staked the future of itself and all of humanity on a bunch of greedy maladapted tech bros who can’t keep it in their pants,” another X user posted.

On November 4, the day after Trump and the Republicans suffered sweeping losses in the midterm elections, he posted that the stock market had “hit another Record High” on Truth Social. “Your 401(k)s are through the roof. Record Setting All!” he bragged.

But the next day, he said the stock market “suffered one of the Worst Days in History based on the results of the Election.”

“It took so much work to get us to a level that no one has ever seen before, and then this. Your 401(k)s have, therefore, dropped. Remember, it wasn’t Republicans, it was Dumocrats,” he posted.
