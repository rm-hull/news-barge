# Machete, knuckle duster and 4kg of gold seized in Leeds dawn drugs raids

## Five people have been arrested

Five people have been arrested in Leeds drugs raids that uncovered a machete, knuckle duster and four kilos of gold.

Officers from the Yorkshire and Humber Regional Organised Crime Unit carried out warrants at two addresses in Talbot Gardens and Harehills Lane at around 6am today (Wednesday, November 25).

Three men, aged 54, 46 and 35, and two women, aged 44 and 26, were arrested on suspicion of drug supply and production. They remain in custody while enquiries continue.

Police seized about £10,000 in cash during the searches. They also found weapons, including a machete, a pickaxe handle and a knuckle duster, along with four kilos of gold jewellery.

Officers also recovered prepared dealer bags, 20 grams of ketamine, a drugs price list and eight phones.

Detective Inspector Heather Shearer, of the Yorkshire and Humber Regional Intelligence Unit, said: "The Yorkshire and Humber Regional Organised Unit remains committed to taking down those who involve themselves in the organised supply of drugs.

"The supply of drugs fuels further criminality and violence in our communities and brings misery to the lives of individual users and their families. I hope today's arrests will provide some reassurance to the public."
