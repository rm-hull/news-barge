A man has been arrested after the serious crash last night (Wednesday) which left a cyclist in hospital.

As ***YorkMix*** reported, the junction of **James Street** and**Lawrence Street** was closed following the collision at around**6.14pm**.

It involved a Land Rover and cycle ridden by a man aged 20.

#### Crash location

He was taken to hospital with serious injuries. The road was later reopened.

Today (Thursday), North Yorkshire Police gave this update: “The driver of a vehicle believed to be involved was arrested later on Wednesday evening on suspicion of failing to stop following a collision and causing serious injury by careless driving.

“He has been released while under investigation while enquiries continue.

“Officers continue to appeal for witnesses and dashcam footage – please get in touch if you can help.”
