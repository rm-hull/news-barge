# Man faces multiple charges including causing death by careless driving

## A man faces multiple charges in connection with an investigation into a fatal collision in Hull.

Police responded to reports that a pedestrian who was hit by a car on Saturday, October 31, 2026 at around 5.30pm.

Tenerif Constantin, 21, of Abbey Street, Hull has been charged with causing death by careless driving whilst over the specified limit of controlled drug, causing death by careless driving, causing death by careless driving whilst unlicensed, driving otherwise than in accordance with a licence and failing to stop after a road traffic collision.

He appeared at Hull Magistrates Court on Tuesday November 17, where he was remanded into custody.

He is next due to appear at Hull Crown Court on Friday, December 18, 2026, police said.
