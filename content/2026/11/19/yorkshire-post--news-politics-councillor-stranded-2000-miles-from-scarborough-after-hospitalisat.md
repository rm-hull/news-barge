# Councillor stranded 2,000 miles from Scarborough after hospitalisation

## A councillor stranded 2,000 miles from home has said politics can ‘take the back seat’ after being admitted to hospital in Tenerife.

Rich Maw, a councillor in Scarborough, has had his trip to the Canary Islands extended indefinitely after being diagnosed with a blood clot and told he cannot take a flight home until doctors give him the all-clear.

Coun Maw flew out to the Spanish island on November 8th and had intended to return this week in order to attend today’s full meeting of North Yorkshire Council.

Instead, the independent representative is receiving treatment for deep vein thrombosis off the coast of West Africa, accompanied by his wife, Claire.

![Coun Rich Maw And Claire In Hospital In Tenerife.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjI5NmI1ZTk4LThiNjMtNGMyYy1iOGFmLWY1ZGY5ZTE1OTU2OToyYjdkNDczNy1hYzhiLTQ3ZTgtODAwNS1lNTgzZDFlZjlkYTE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“Claire and I were so looking forward to our holiday in Tenerife, but unfortunately, things haven’t quite gone to plan,” he told the Local Democracy Reporting Service (LDRS).

“There’s no confirmed date for getting home yet, as the priority has to be making sure I’m well enough and safe to travel.”

Coun Maw praised the excellent care he had received from Spanish medical staff and said he was “incredibly grateful to everyone looking after me”.

He added: “I’m in a private hospital and on top of dealing with my current health issues, I’m also having to navigate travel insurance, medical authorisations, claims and all the associated paperwork.

![Coun Rich Maw In Tenerife Hospital.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjBhMWRmMzc5LWVjYjktNDQzNC04ZmY4LTE3ZTFiMmVjMzU0OToyOTMwNTFkNC00OGY1LTQwZDUtODA0My1jMGMxMWNmMWJhMWQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“It has certainly been an education. Anyone who thinks dealing with insurance companies when you’re ill and away from home is straightforward might be in for a shock. And whilst the underwriters are conducting their checks, an almighty added amount of stress for the patient!”

Coun Maw added that the experience had reinforced his belief in the importance of the NHS and “its founding principle that healthcare should be free at the point of use, available to everyone according to need, not their ability to pay or the strength of their insurance policy”.

The representative, who sits on North Yorkshire Council and Scarborough Town Council, thanked residents for “genuinely overwhelming” well wishes.

However, he added that “for now, politics can take a back seat.

“I’m concentrating on getting home and getting better, and there’ll be plenty to get stuck into when I’m back!”
