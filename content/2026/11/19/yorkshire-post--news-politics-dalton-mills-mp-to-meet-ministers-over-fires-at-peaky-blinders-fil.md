# Dalton Mills: MP to meet ministers over fires at Peaky Blinders filming site

## Keighley and Ilkley MP Robbie Moore is set to meet with ministers to discuss how to protect the iconic Dalton Mills from repeated fires and arson.

The Peaky Blinders filming site in Keighley caught ablaze again on Tuesday night, with West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue treating the incident as arson.

This was at least the sixth major fire at Dalton Mills in the last five years, with a much-larger blaze in September destroying its roof.

Mr Moore referred the suspended sentence of McKenzie Mottram to the Attorney General, describing it as “absolutely pathetic”.

![Credit: Melody Pugh](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2025/07/27/14/17/dalton-mills.jpg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)


The Tory MP said he was raising the continued fires with ministers from Angela Rayner’s Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government “as the status quote cannot continue”.

He also hit out at Bradford Council accusing them of refusing “to secure the Dalton Mills site properly”.

“Just weeks after the last fire at Dalton Mills, there has been another entirely preventable fire at the site as a vehicle has been set alight,” he said.

![A fire at Dalton Mills in Keighley, West Yorkshire on 12 September 2026.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/09/13/13/45/Red-And-Black-Modern-Tips-Trick-YouTube-Thumbnail-(1600-x-1080-px)-(1600-x-1080-px)-(1600-x-1080-px).jpg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)


“I am relieved that there were no injuries, and want to thank West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue for their continued brave service.

“Bradford Council's inaction of making the site secure, as has been consistently requested, is putting my constituents at risk, and they must act now.

“I continue to demand they secure the Dalton Mills site properly and prevent this from happening again.

![The original March 2022 fire which destroyed Dalton Mills](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjgwNDJmZTc2LWEyNDYtNDI5Ni05Y2QzLTE3MTE0NDQ0MjAxYjpiMjJkNWNhYy1kNzRiLTQzYjAtOTU0ZS1lYzUyZGZiMmE2ZDQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“I am in contact with local police and fire officials to understand more details about this particular incident, and hope the police will ensure that those responsible are caught quickly.

“Our heritage is being lost and lives are being put at risk as a result of Bradford Councils inaction.”

The council has been pushing for the site to be moved into private ownership for several years.

Coun Andrew Judson, deputy leader of Bradford Council, said: “We share the concerns of local residents and businesses whenever incidents occur at Dalton Mills and are grateful to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service for their response.

“It is important to be clear that Dalton Mills is not owned by Bradford Council.

“The site is currently held by the Crown Estate through the legal process of escheat, which creates significant constraints on the council's ability to intervene directly or undertake works on the site.

“The council continues to work with the Crown Estate and partner agencies to explore all available options to improve safety and security.

“While we understand local frustrations, it is incorrect to suggest that the Council has simply refused to act.

“The site's complex ownership status places legal limitations on the powers available to the Council, and we will continue to pursue all lawful and practical measures to address concerns about the site.”
