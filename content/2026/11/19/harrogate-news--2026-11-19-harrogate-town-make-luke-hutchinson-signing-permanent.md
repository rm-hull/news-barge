**Harrogate Town have completed the permanent signing of Luke Hutchinson from Bolton Wanderers for an undisclosed fee, following an impressive start to his loan spell at the club.**

The 24-year-old has made 14 appearances for Town so far this season and has signed a long-term deal.

## Hutchinson commits to Harrogate Town

Town Head of Recruitment Lloyd Kerry said the club were pleased to secure Hutchinson on a permanent basis after his performances since arriving.

> **Lloyd Kerry, Head of Recruitment at Harrogate Town:** We are really pleased to get Luke signed on a permanent deal. He has put in some impressive performances since joining us and has fitted in really well with the group.

Kerry also highlighted Hutchinson confidence, his understanding of the way the team wants to play and his potential to develop further at the club.

Hutchinson said he was delighted to extend his stay at Harrogate, having quickly settled into the club after arriving on loan.

> **Luke Hutchinson:** I am absolutely delighted to extend my stay here. Since coming here on loan, it felt like a perfect place to play and enjoy my football, so I am glad that I am here long term.

He also praised his teammates and goalkeeping coach Phil Priestley for helping him settle into the group.

## From Bolton to Harrogate

Hutchinson joined Bolton Wanderers at the age of eight and signed his first professional contract in 2021.

Bolton extended his contract in the summer of 2025 after he had impressed during loan spells at several clubs, including Crawley Town, who were playing in League One at the time.

He then spent the 2025/26 season on loan at Altrincham, making 44 appearances for the club.

His performances earned him both the Players Player of the Year and Young Player of the Year awards at Altrincham.

Hutchinson now makes his move to Harrogate Town permanent after an encouraging start to his time with the club.
