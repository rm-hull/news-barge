## Imamoglu's office has released a copy of his business management degree from Istanbul University, awarded in 1995.

A Turkish court ruled on Thursday that jailed Istanbul mayor Ekrem Imamoglu's university diploma was invalid, preventing him from running in the 2028 presidential election, according to media reports.

Imamoglu, considered one of the strongest opposition challengers to President Recep Tayyip Erdogan, was arrested in March 2025 on corruption charges that his party says are politically motivated.

Turkish law requires presidential candidates to hold a valid university degree.

The State Council's decision to reject his appeal over his diploma is the latest legal setback for Imamoglu, a member of the opposition Republican People's Party (CHP). He was elected Istanbul mayor in 2019, defeating Erdogan's preferred candidate.

The CHP later nominated Imamoglu as its presidential candidate.

## **Arrest sparks widespread protests**

His arrest on 19 March 2025 on multiple corruption charges triggered widespread protests. Critics saw the move as an attempt to remove one of Erdogan's main political rivals.

A day before his arrest, Istanbul University cancelled Imamoglu's degree, claiming it had been obtained fraudulently.

Police raided his home hours later and detained him. He was subsequently jailed on the same day the CHP officially named him its presidential candidate.

## Imamoglu defends his university degree

The diploma case went to trial in September 2025. Imamoglu told the court he had been admitted to a university in the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC), which is recognised only by Turkey, when he was 18.

He said he later transferred to Istanbul University to complete his degree, following the rules set by Turkey's Council of Higher Education (YOK).

Imamoglu defended the transfer, saying he had followed the university's application process.

"I saw the announcement for a horizontal transfer to Istanbul University, met the requirements, applied and was accepted, studied and graduated," he said.
