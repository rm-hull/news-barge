# 'You can be believed and understood': Joanne Young speaks after verdict

A woman who was drugged by her husband and secretly advertised for sex on the internet has said if people "recognised their own experience" after this trial, "you're not alone and there are people who will believe you".

Joanne Young, who has waived her right to anonymity, spoke outside of Winchester Court and encouraged people to speak out against violence against women and girls.

Four men have been found guilty of sexually abusing Young in the first trial of its kind in the UK.

Philip Young, 50, already pleaded guilty to multiple charges of drugging and raping his wife over a 13-year period at their home in Swindon, Wiltshire.

Norman Macksoni, 48, Richard Wilkins, 62, Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32, and Mohammed Hassan, 38, were found guilty of multiple sexual offences by a jury at Winchester Crown Court. Wilkins and Macksoni were each found guilty of one count of rape.
