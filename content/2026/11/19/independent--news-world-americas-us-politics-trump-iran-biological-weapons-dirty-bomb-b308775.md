President Donald Trump has said Iran is “looking at gases” and biological weapons, following reports Tehran is plotting a “dirty bomb” in Washington, D.C.

U.S. intelligence experts expressed concern earlier this week about the prospect of Iran targeting the capital in revenge for Trump’s airstrikes in the Middle East, although there was no agreement over whether an imminent threat existed.

Trump was asked about the possibility of such an attack during an interview with Fox News senior White House correspondent Jacqui Heinrich during his trip to Shenzen, China.

“There is indication that Iran is looking at gases, looking at bio, because we’ve shut them out with the nuclear,” Trump told Heinrich on the sidelines of his Apec summit with Chinese Premier Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

“Nuclear is much harder. And they’re looking at gas and they’re looking at the bio, you know, the biological weapons, not nice, and other things.

![President Donald Trump discusses the potential terror threat posed by Iran in an interview with Fox News correspondent Jacqui Heinrich on the sidelines of the Apec summit in China](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/9/27/Trump-Fox.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

“And we’ll see. We’re going to have a new year coming up, we’ll see what happens after January 1 but they’ve been nasty. These are nasty people. We didn’t start this war. This war was started… 51 years ago.”

Asked whether residents of D.C. should panic, the president answered: “I don’t think so, no. Look if it does happen, we wipe them out… Oh, I give you my word. Absolutely.”

Earlier this week, Trump addressed CNN’s original reporting on the bomb during his visit to the Philippines, saying that if it proved accurate, Iran “will pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid before. I hope it’s not [true]. But we take it very seriously because they happen to be crazy.”

Secretary of State Marco Rubio was also asked about it in Manila and took a different tack, disparaging the seven anonymous intelligence officials cited.

“Let me tell you guys something,” he told the assembled press corps. “Some of your intelligence sources are liars. Others are liars and criminals.

“They’re liars because they’re making up [stuff]. They’re arguing they have access to something they don’t and because they have an email address in the government, they somehow have credibility with you.

“So number one, you have a bunch of liars out there that pitch stuff to people. I’m not talking about that reporting specifically, just in general.

![Trump met with Chinese Premier Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Shenzen but said he would not ask either man for help ending his stalemate with Tehran](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/09/2305276336..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

“As far that specific report is concerned, the United States is always on the lookout for anyone who tries to do harm to our country and to our people. And we have threat streams every day, and the majority of them are not credible.”

Iran Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei has also dismissed the reporting in a post on X, calling it “baseless” and a “fabricated media narrative.”

Elsewhere in Trump’s interview with Fox, the president said he would not be asking Xi and Putin for help in ending his stand-off with Iran, commenting: “We don’t need any help. They want to make a deal. Frankly, they’ve treated us very well. They haven’t fired. They haven’t put ships in the water.”

Interestingly, he also called the memorandum of understanding Vice President JD Vance agreed with negotiators from Tehran earlier this year “a waste of time,” an assessment his deputy might well feel chagrined by.

On other subjects, the president agreed with Rubio that the recent run of tech outages that have also alarmed the American public this week were not connected, said he had discussed the Jensen Huang “tragedy” with Xi and that he respected Democrats following their triumph at this month’s midterms.

On Putin’s invasion of Ukraine, Trump said the Russian and his Ukrainian counterpart Volodymyr Zelensky “have got to get along,” again insisted it would never have happened if he had been in office at the time and concluded: “It doesn’t affect us. In fact, I guess you could say… we make a lot of money.”
