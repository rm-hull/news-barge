## The US president reportedly called into a meeting of Kyiv's European allies from Air Force One, hours after talks with Putin and Xi, and said Moscow was ready to negotiate a peace deal.

US President Donald Trump has “sharply questioned” European countries over their continued financial support for Ukraine during the country’s conflict with Russia.

The president unexpectedly phoned into a meeting of Ukraine’s European allies on Thursday, according to the Politico publication, which heard from four European officials who were briefed on the discussion.

He asked the allies why they were continuing to spend billions on military support for Kyiv.

The call came mere hours after Trump wrapped up a meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin and his Chinese counterpart, President Xi Jinping, in Shenzhen, China.

The US president is said to have called Berlin from his private aircraft, Air Force One, and insisted that Putin was serious about future peace negotiations about Ukraine.

Three of the European officials, who have been granted anonymity, said that Trump was strongly sceptical about the possibility of further aid being provided to Ukraine by the Coalition of the Willing, an alliance of 34 countries – plus Ukraine – who have pledged strengthened support for Ukraine against Russian aggression.

One of the officials said Trump bemoaned the fact that a lot of people are dying in the conflict, and a lot of money is being “wasted” on the nearly five-year-long war. That official also confirmed that the US leader believes Putin will cooperate with peace negotiations with Ukraine.
