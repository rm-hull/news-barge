# Leeds man wins £1.5m jackpot months before becoming a dad

## A Leeds couple's plans for parenthood have been transformed after a £1.5m win gave them the chance to buy their first home

A Leeds man has won £1.5m in a competition, transforming his family's future just months before welcoming his first child. Jensen Barnes, 25, from Leeds, won the life-changing cash prize alongside his girlfriend of three years, Jess, 25, who is expecting their first baby together in February 2027.

The couple, who currently rent a two-bedroom terraced house in Leeds, are now hoping to get on the property ladder and give their son the future they had always dreamed of.

Jensen won the £1.5m cash alternative after entering Elite Competitions' biggest-ever competition, which offered a £2m coastal mansion in Lytham St Annes as the main prize.

The marketing manager was given the choice of taking the property or the cash, opting for the £1.5m instead. He and his partner Jess were sitting at home watching the live draw when they realised they had won.

Recalling the moment, Jensen said: “We put the livestream on the telly, I didn’t believe it at first, really surreal. As the numbers were coming out, I was going through my tickets and just watched my numbers line up.

“I kept pacing up and down, rang the family group chat and no one believed us. It was a very weird experience and it didn’t feel real. Like it was all a weird fever dream.”

The win means the couple can now look towards buying their first home, something they had discussed but had not yet started saving for. Jensen explained how their current rented home compares to the property he won.

He said: “Our living room is probably smaller than the kitchen in the house we won, we have a leaky ceiling with a bowl under it, two bedrooms. We have lived there just short of two years.

“We were planning on moving anyway, but this opens up a lot more doors. We have to do a bit of exploring now, finding out what we want as we have the money now.”

Jess said they had always hoped to own their own home but had planned to start saving after her maternity leave.

She said: “We spoke about owning our own home but we hadn’t started saving yet, we were going to start after maternity leave. The house prices in Leeds are quite expensive but we can afford it now.”

Jensen had previously been spending around £50 a month on the lottery without winning before switching to Elite Competitions' £50-a-month Elite Club subscription in October.

Remarkably, he landed the £1.5m jackpot in his very first month as a member. The money will also allow the couple to enjoy experiences together as a family, including a big holiday, as well as helping Jess' younger sister buy a car after passing her driving test.

But the biggest difference will be the opportunities it gives them as they prepare to become parents. Jess said the win means she will not have to rush back to work after having their baby and that they will be able to spend more time together as a family.

She said: “We can give our child a better life now. We can be more present parents and be very present in his life. I won’t have to go straight back to work. I can be there, we can enrich his life, take him around the world. He can have anything he wants.”

Jensen said the financial security had lifted a huge weight from their shoulders, particularly as they had been worried about managing financially during maternity leave.

He said: “We are able to give him the tools and ability to do anything, which we weren’t sure we could do before. We were stressing that we wouldn’t be able to survive through maternity because my salary and maternity pay isn’t the best, but we don’t have to worry now.

“I am really grateful, I was working really hard to increase my salary because I was already incredibly stressed about being able to afford things and ensuring my little boy is going to have the best life. But this changes everything, he is going to have the best life and we are going to have a beautiful home.”

Alex Beckett, founder and CEO of Elite Competitions, said he was delighted to see what the win would mean for the couple.

He said: “It is really special to see what a win like this can mean to a young family. Jensen and Jess have so much to look forward to, with their little boy on the way, and knowing that this has given them greater financial security and the opportunity to spend more time together as a family is brilliant.”
