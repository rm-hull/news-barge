## Top York and 'Great British Menu' chef to open restaurant in town near York

Some of the cooking planned for Filos *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A new neighbourhood restaurant influenced by the Mediterranean is set to open near York, blending flavour with friendship.

Filos, meaning 'friend' in Greek, is the creation of chef Ahmed Abdalla and Banos Pavlou.

The pair are long-time friends who bonded over food and shared meals, and the restaurant is set to open in Ryedale Court, Haxby, in early 2027.

Mr Abdalla said: "We have been working on Filos for some time now.

"The idea started one evening over a meal.

"Ban has a wealth of operational experience and knowledge; our skills complement each other’s.

"Filos feels like a natural fit.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738043.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ahmed Abdalla and Banos Pavlou to open Filos, a neighbourhood restaurant in early 2027 *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"It encompasses the friendship and the food that brought us together, and I am excited to share that hospitality with others.

"As for the location, York has helped shape me into the person and the chef I am today, so I knew I wanted it to be in or around the city."

The menu will be influenced by the Mediterranean, with dishes built around open-fire cooking.

Many ingredients will be aged, cured, or smoked in-house, and the dishes will combine Mediterranean influences with Yorkshire produce and sensibilities.

Mr Abdalla, who previously led the kitchen at Legacy at The Grand in York and returned as a wildcard for the final of BBC’s Great British Menu, said: "There are lots of incredible restaurants in York already, but I am excited to bring something a little different to the scene.

"For chefs, York and Yorkshire is a dream.

"We are so lucky to have such high-quality produce on our doorstep, grown, caught, and reared locally.

"That gives me so much to work with in the kitchen, whether it’s a beautiful piece of fish from the East Coast, some local grass-fed beef, award-winning strawberries or foraged chanterelles."

Mr Pavlou, who will lead front of house, the bar, and the wider running of the business alongside Abdalla, brings around 17 years of hospitality experience to the venture.

He has managed well-known York venues, including Vudu Lounge, Bobo's Cocktail Bar, and Dum Dum Tiki Bar.

For Mr Pavlou, Filos is also a personal project, influenced by his Greek Cypriot heritage and the culture of shared meals and family hospitality.

He said: "York has a brilliant community, and we are really looking forward to adding to that.

"Enjoying this style food with family and friends is a huge part of our culture.

"It is how we build and maintain our community.

"We take our time to enjoy lunches and dinners together that use quality, humble ingredients, cooked well."

He added: "Me and Ahmed have had a shared love of food & hospitality which has meant that we can start this exciting new chapter as business partners with the same vision."
