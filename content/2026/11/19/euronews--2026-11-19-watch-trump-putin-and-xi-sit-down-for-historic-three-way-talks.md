The leaders of the United States, China and Russia have sat down for their first-ever trilateral talks in Shenzhen, in a meeting with major implications for international relations.

US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin met at the Kylin Villa on Thursday, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit.

The three presidents are expected to discuss Ukraine, the conflict involving Iran, nuclear weapons, trade and wider international security.
