York has been named the top Christmas market in Yorkshire and fifth best in the UK.

The ranking comes from a 2026 index by Locals Insider, which placed York’s St Nicholas Fair ahead of Leeds’ market in 16th place.

York scored 71.5 out of 100 in the nationwide study, rising three places from eighth in 2025.

Martin Danemaq, a spokesperson for Locals Insider, said: "York proves you don't need to be a big city to put on a world-class market.

"The chalets on Parliament Street, with the medieval streets and the Minster all around, offer that traditional, historic feel that visitors travel for – and it's good value too."

St Nicholas Fair received one of the highest marks for setting and full marks for accessibility, with strong value-for-money ratings.

Leeds, the only other Yorkshire market in the top 18, was praised for affordability.

Mr Danemaq said: "Breaking into the top five is a brilliant result for York, and for anyone in Yorkshire looking for an affordable festive day out, Leeds is also well worth a visit."

Manchester was named the UK’s best Christmas market for 2026, scoring 86.8, followed by London’s Winter Wonderland and Bath.

Edinburgh placed fourth in the rankings.
