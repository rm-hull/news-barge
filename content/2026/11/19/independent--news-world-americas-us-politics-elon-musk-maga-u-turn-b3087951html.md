Wearing a black baseball cap emblazoned with the words “Make America Great Again,” Elon Musk stood before the crowd in Butler, Pennsylvania, in October 2024 and proclaimed: “I am not just MAGA, I’m dark MAGA.”

Later, Musk stood behind Donald Trump on stage, jumping with joy in what would later become his signature “dark MAGA” baseball cap.

He repeated the sentiment at Donald Trump’s campaign rally at Madison Square Garden later that month. “I’m not just MAGA. I am dark, gothic MAGA,” Musk announced to the crowd.

Months later, after helping propel Trump to the White House by splurging at least $250 million towards his campaign, Musk sat among the president’s Cabinet officials wearing a “TRUMP WAS RIGHT ABOUT EVERYTHING” cap. On other occasions, he appeared in the Oval Office wearing the hat, of which he had several styles.

Now, he is trying to pretend it all never happened.

![At the same rally, Musk stood behind Donald Trump on stage, jumping with joy in his MAGA hat.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/13/01/GettyImages-2176089209.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In a remarkable U-turn, and after blowing nearly $500 million in the midterm elections on a failed bid to back Republicans, Musk boldly claimed that he is “not even sure what MAGA means.”

“I'm not MAGA,” he said Wednesday during an interview from China, where he’s accompanying the president on his trip to Asia. “I’m not sure what MAGA means, but if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that.”

People aren’t buying it.

The world’s richest man has been eviscerated online, as Americans are keen to remind him of how he personally contributed to the MAGA movement on the 2024 campaign trail and at the White House, where he wreaked havoc at the helm of the now-defunct Department of Government Efficiency.

“This is the guy who spent nearly a billion dollars on the movement, called opposing it an existential threat, and worked inside the Trump administration,” one user commented on X. “Sorry dude, you're not rebranding now.”

“Musk trying to rebrand himself as ‘not MAGA’ after midterms wipeout is the most transparent thing I’ve ever seen in my life,” another user posted. “Not gonna happen @elonmusk. You sink with this ship.”

![Musk’s millions helped propel Trump to the White House where he headed up the Department of Government Efficiency. He sat among the president’s Cabinet officials wearing a ‘TRUMP WAS RIGHT ABOUT EVERYTHING’ cap](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/13/52/GettyImages-2206211823.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Elon literally jumped around onstage wearing a MAGA hat,” said another user. “Imagine spending hundreds of millions electing Trump just to pretend you don't know what his movement stands for.”

“I guarantee you he thinks that our memories are not that long and he can rewrite history,” added another X user. “WRONG WRONG WRONG, Elon. It's all on video.”

Musk also reflected that he felt “foolish” after plunging nearly half a billion dollars into failed Republican candidates after the party suffered major losses in the midterm elections. “Probably if I could rewind the clock, I would have spent less money,” Musk said Wednesday at *The New York Times* DealBook Summit.

Despite Musks stunning dumping of MAGA and trying to proclaim he was never a central figure in it, Democrats say that it won’t spare him from investigations when the party takes control of Congress next year.

“What’s truly foolish is that our laws let him do it,” Democratic Michigan senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed reacted, referring to Musk’s campaign spending.

“You are still getting a subpoena,” added Arizona Sen. Ruben Gallego.

California Rep. Robert Garcia, the ranking Democrat on the House Oversight Committee, warned after the election result that upcoming probes will center on the billionaire’s role in terminating federal employees in early 2025 during his overseeing of the Department of Government Efficiency under Trump.

In February 2025, Musk made a surprise appearance at the Conservative Political Action Conference, where he wielded a chainsaw around the stage while wearing his “dark MAGA” baseball cap and sunglasses. Many interpreted the chainsaw as a metaphor for DOGE’s efforts to slash the federal budget.

“I think there's a lot of interest, not just from our committee, but from committees across the Congress to speak to Mr. Musk,” Garcia said recently.

Trump indicated he was weighing pre-emptive pardons for his close aides and others around him should the Democrats pursue investigations, *Axios* reported ahead of the midterm elections.

“It’s talk. But if it is talk, we’ll have to protect them,” Trump said recently on the *Pod Force One* podcast.

Trump and Musk had a spectacular falling out a few months into Trump’s second term that saw Musk booted out of the White House. Musk added fuel to the fire when he posted, “@realDonaldTrump is in the Epstein files. That is the real reason they have not been made public. Have a nice day, DJT!”

Less than two days later, he quietly deleted the posts and later said he went “too far.”

In the months that followed, Trump and Musk appeared together in public a handful of times to signal their bitter feud was over. In November 2025, the two attended a White House Dinner. In January, this year, Musk praised Trump for a raid in Venezuela that captured leader Nicolás Maduro. Just this week, Musk was in China with the president.

The tech billionaire has invested nearly $700 billion in MAGA with little to show after the midterms and wants to “rewind the clock” - but Americans won’t let him rewrite his dark MAGA chapter.
