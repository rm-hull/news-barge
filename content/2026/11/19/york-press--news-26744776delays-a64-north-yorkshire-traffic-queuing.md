Delays are being reported on a main road in North Yorkshire.

The AA says there is queuing traffic on the A64 Westbound between A169 (Old Malton roundabout) and Barnygate Lane.

Delays of nine minutes are being reported and increasing. The average speed is ten mph.

The A64 is also partially blocked due to emergency repairs both ways from B1248 York Road (Musley Bank) to Greets House Road.

One motorist said it had taken them about 45mins to get through the roadworks.
