Two Afghan migrants who took turns to subject a 14-year-old girl to a “sickening” sex attack on a train are to be deported, the Government has said.

Baryalai Rahimi, 25, and Mirebadurahman Sadat, 29, were convicted of attacking the girl on a London-bound train after approaching her at Sturry station near Canterbury, Kent, on 5 February.

Both men arrived in the UK by small boat in 2023, the Press Association understands.

A jury at Inner London Crown Court found Rahimi, an asylum seeker, guilty of rape on Wednesday, along with sexual assault and three counts of sexual activity with a child.

Sadat, who has leave to remain, was found guilty of assault by penetration, sexual activity with a child and two counts of causing a person to engage in sexual activity.

A Home Office spokesperson said on Thursday: “Our thoughts are with the victim of this sickening crime. The perpetrators have now been rightly convicted, and justice has been delivered by the courts.

![Mirebadurahman Sadat was convicted on Wednesday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/17/01M7QVSYMPG6QRKAZ0ZK54KHHC.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Once sentencing has taken place, we will ensure deportation action is pursued. We will do everything in our power to remove the vile criminals off British soil.

“Over 11,700 foreign criminals, including murderers and rapists, have been removed since the last election, up 41% on the previous two-year period.”

British Transport Police said Rahimi forced himself upon the girl once the pair were on board the train, sexually assaulting her before taking her into one of the train’s toilets and raping her.

Rahimi then left the girl with Sadat, who too subjected her to a sustained sexual assault. The girl repeatedly told the men “no” throughout the attack and was ignored, police said. She had also told them she was just 14.

When the train arrived at London Bridge, the men followed the girl onto another train. Rahimi got off at St Pancras and Sadat at Finsbury Park.

The girl spoke to police at Peterborough station and told officers about the attack. Police used her account, along with CCTV footage from the trains, to identify Rahimi and Sadat.

The two friends were arrested in west London on 10 February.

Rahimi, of Southall, west London, and Sadat, of Cricklewood, north-west London, will be sentenced at Inner London Crown Court on 18 December.
