Rarely-seen photographs of well-known historic landmarks in Harrogate and Knaresborough will feature in a new set of calendars this Christmas season.

North Yorkshire Council’s library staff in Harrogate and Knaresborough have worked with the archives team to choose the vintage images.

The Harrogate calendar will feature images of well-known buildings including the old Harrogate Town Hall, which is now the Mercer Gallery, and the interior of the former Winter Gardens. There are also images of shopfronts and a roller-skating rink in the Royal Hall Gardens.

The Knaresborough edition includes views spanning town centre streets, including the Market Place and main High Street.

There are also photographs of the castle, once a stronghold of medieval kings, and the River Nidd.

North Yorkshire Council’s executive member for culture and arts, Coun Simon Myers, whose responsibilities include libraries, said: “This is the third year of producing calendars in these libraries, which have proved incredibly popular and are sure to spark conversations about local history.

“There is something special about seeing familiar landmarks from years gone by, giving us a chance to reflect on how our towns have changed over time.”

For the past two years, the libraries have produced calendars, with money raised from the sales being reinvested to support the service.

Last year, the calendars quickly sold out at both Harrogate and Knaresborough libraries, raising £750.

A third calendar this year featuring images of Selby has proved so popular that it has already sold out.

This year’s are on sale at Knaresborough and Harrogate libraries for £6 and are available while stocks last.
