Starting at around 6:15 this morning, 47 protesters glued themselves to the road surface at six intersections in the Wedding district, as the Berlin police initially reported via their X account. A police spokesperson confirmed that the action was a protest directed against Rheinmetall.

According to the police spokesperson, emergency services have removed all the protesters from the road. A few protesters still remain at the site, continuing their demonstration against the arms factory. Traffic is flowing freely again, and the BSR has removed the graffiti from the roadway.

Rheinmetall operates a factory on Scheringstrasse. Recently, activists have repeatedly blocked access to the site. The site has belonged to Rheinmetall Waffe Munition GmbH since July 2025. According to the company, the plant manufactures shell casings for artillery ammunition and components for drone warheads. Previously, the factory, then called Pierburg, produced automotive parts.

The activist group Peacefully Against Genocide has been organising protests of this kind for months at the factory in Wedding and recently claimed responsibility for hoisting a Palestinian flag outside the Bundestag building.
