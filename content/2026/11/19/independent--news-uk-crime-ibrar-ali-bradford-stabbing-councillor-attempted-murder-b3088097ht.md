A 32-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder after a councillor was stabbed while our door-knocking in Bradford.

Ibrar Ali, 52, was attacked at about 11am on Thursday.

The independent councillor was named in Parliament by local government minister Baroness Taylor of Stevenage, who raised the case in the House of Lords.

She said: “I am afraid I have to mention a very tragic incident that’s occurred in Bradford… where an independent councillor who was out canvassing has been stabbed.

“I would want to send the wishes, that I’m sure noble Lords would agree, of the whole House to councillor Ibrar Ali for a speedy recovery, and our thoughts are with all of those in Bradford who are affected by this awful incident.”

![Police at the scene following Thursday's stabbing](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/17/01M7TC6SH6T52VMZS6M8E0VCM0.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

West Yorkshire Police said there was nothing to suggest the incident was politically motivated, although the investigation was at an early stage.

Officers were called just after 11am to reports that a 52-year-old man, a serving Bradford councillor, had been found seriously injured on Manse Street.

Police said: “The man is believed to have first been injured on Thornbury Avenue, before being driven to Manse Street where emergency services attended.

“He was taken to hospital where his condition is described as serious but not life-threatening.”

The 32-year-old suspect fled to the roof of a property on Thornbury Avenue.

He was brought down some time later and arrested on suspicion of attempted murder. He remains in custody.

Police cordons were in place on Manse Street, Leeds Road and Thornbury Avenue while inquiries are carried out.

Adam, who works in the area and did not want to give his surname, said: “I know the councillor on a personal level and it’s shocking.

“I just know he’s a very kind, warm-hearted man whose goal is to serve, his life is to help out everyone.

“He just wants everyone to succeed. He definitely didn’t deserve what’s happened. The world’s not a nice place.”

Chris Herd, the Lord Mayor of Bradford, said: “Sadly, a Bradford councillor was seriously assaulted this morning.

“He is currently receiving treatment in hospital and we wish him a full and speedy recovery. Our thoughts are with him, his family and his friends at this difficult time. It is deeply distressing that this incident has occurred.

“Councillors work tirelessly on behalf of residents across the district and should be able to carry out their duties safely.

![The area has been cordoned off as police carry out their investigation](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/17/01M7TC6PPWTGZMN10TXQN8BE88.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“We will continue to work closely with our colleagues at West Yorkshire Police as they investigate this matter and seek to bring those responsible to justice, and would like to thank those who provided aid and support at the time.”

Bradford East MP Imran Hussain MP said: “This horrific attack is deeply disturbing. My thoughts and prayers are with councillor Ibrar Ali and his family.

“No elected politician should feel under threat or fear for their safety while carrying out their duties and serving their community.

“I am receiving regular updates from the police as they deal with this extremely serious incident, and I understand a suspect is in police custody.”

Later in the Commons, minister Jim McMahon described the attack as “horrific” and added: “Our thoughts are with (Mr Ali), his family, councillors in Bradford and of course our Members of Parliament too.

“Our thoughts are very much with you all at this time.”
