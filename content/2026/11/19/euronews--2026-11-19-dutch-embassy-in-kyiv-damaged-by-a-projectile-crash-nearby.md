## A projectile struck the area behind the Dutch embassy in Ukraine on Thursday, causing minor damage to the building; no casualties were reported.

Dutch Minister of Foreign Affairs Tom Berendsen reported damage to the Netherlands' embassy in Kyiv this Thursday. A projectile struck the area behind the embassy, causing minor damage to the building.

"Our colleagues are unharmed, although there has been minor damage to the building," Berendsen said. "We are investigating exactly what happened. Our priority now is the well-being of our staff."

While there was no confirmation on the Dutch side, Ukraine’s Minister for Foreign Affairs, Andrii Sybiha, has said on Telegram that it was a Russian attack.

"Russian attacks on Kyiv have once again damaged a diplomatic mission. This time, the building housing the Dutch Embassy in Ukraine was hit," Sybiha said.

"We call on the international community to strongly condemn Russian attacks on foreign diplomats and express our firm support for our Dutch colleagues."

The Kyiv Independent says the embassy's windows were shattered and that cars and a security post were damaged.

About 40 people work at the Dutch embassy in Kyiv, including Dutch and local employees; none of them was injured during the attack.

## 'We must support Ukraine', Dutch Defence Secretary

"It's not the first time there have been impacts at an embassy," Dutch Defence Secretary Derk Boswijk told Dutch public broadcaster NOS.

"It is additional confirmation that we must support Ukraine. I see above all an encouragement to continue to do that," he told NOS.

The impact on the embassy comes two days after a Ukrainian attack on the Crimean Bridge was carried out, allegedly using Dutch missiles and drones.

Russian President Vladimir Putin, speaking at the APEC summit in China, called for retaliation after Ukraine's attack on the Crimean bridge.

The Dutch-based arms manufacturer Destinus, which allegedly provided the weapons for the bridge attack, was previously mentioned as a "legitimate" target by Former Russian President Dmitry Medvedev because it regularly supplies weapons to Ukraine.

## Several attacks on Kyiv

Kyiv has suffered massive drone strikes on the same day, injuring four people, according to the city's mayor, Vitali Klitschko.

Several districts of the Ukrainian capital were hit by drone strikes and drone debris, including the district where the Dutch embassy is located.
