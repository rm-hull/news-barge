Ryanair has announced four new routes from Leeds Bradford Airport for summer 2027, giving Yorkshire travellers more options for European getaways.

The low-cost airline revealed on Thursday that it will launch twice-weekly services to Bari in Italy, Budapest in Hungary, Corfu in Greece and Tirana in Albania from March 29, 2027.

The new routes have gone on sale more than a year in advance, allowing customers to book early and secure lower fares.

Ryanair said the additions will provide passengers across Yorkshire with a wider choice of destinations, ranging from Mediterranean beach resorts to city-break locations.

Bari, on Italy’s Adriatic coast, is known for its historic old town, waterfront and southern Italian culture.

Budapest attracts visitors with its architecture, thermal baths and nightlife, while Corfu remains a popular destination for beaches, clear waters and traditional Greek cuisine.

Meanwhile, Albania’s capital, Tirana, offers colourful streets, a growing tourism scene and access to some of the country’s most dramatic landscapes.

All four routes will operate twice a week from Leeds Bradford Airport.

Jade Kirwan, Ryanair’s director of communications, said: “Although we have not yet launched our full Summer 2027 schedule, Ryanair is giving our UK customers in the North a sneak peek at what new routes we will be offering with the announcement of these four exciting new Summer 2027 routes from Leeds Bradford to Bari, Budapest, Corfu and Tirana.

“Each of these new Ryanair routes will operate twice per week from 29 March 2027, giving our Yorkshire customers even more low-fare choice and regular connections for their Summer 2027 getaways.

“Tickets are available to book via Ryanair’s app and website now.”

The airline said the routes form part of its expanding programme from Leeds Bradford Airport and are designed to meet demand for affordable flights from the region.
