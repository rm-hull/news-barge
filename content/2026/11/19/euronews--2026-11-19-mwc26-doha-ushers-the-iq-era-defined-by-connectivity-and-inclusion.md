## FOCUS heads to GSMA’s MWC26 in Doha, where AI readiness, cloud computing and the road to 6G networks take centre stage. Running alongside the ITU’s Plenipotentiary Conference for the first time, the event brings together 194 member states to advance global connectivity and digital transformation.

MWC26 returned to Doha with a clear message: while the opportunity to power up connectivity through AI is immense, millions across the MENA region still remain offline. GSMA Director General, Vivek Badrinath said at the opening ceremony, “Progress only counts if everyone shares in it.” UNDP shares the sentiment, with Abeer Shakweer sharing her insights with Euronews. The buzz over how AI will transform industries was palpable across the MWC event floor. Euronews speaks to SandboxAQ CEO Jack Hidary about what’s making him excited in the region. And we explore the world of financial inclusion with Ooredoo Fintech CEO, Mirko Giacco.
