Florida is set to execute murderer Thomas Mitchell Overton Thursday, 35 years after the horrific killing of an eight-months pregnant woman and her husband.

Overton, 71, is scheduled to receive a three-drug injection at 6 p.m. at Florida State Prison in Raiford after he was convicted of the 1991 murders of married couple Michael and Susan “Missy” MacIvor.

The pilot and schoolteacher, aged 30 and 29 respectively, were expecting their first child at the time. Their unborn baby did not survive the brutal attack.

The couple’s killer was convicted on two counts of first-degree murder in 1999 based on DNA retrieved from the MacIvors’ home in Tavernier in the Florida Keys but has continued to maintain his innocence, insisting the evidence was planted by corrupt police officers and his attorneys have submitted an emergency application for a stay to the U.S. Supreme Court.

His execution will be the 20th time the state has administered the death penalty this year.

![Thomas Mitchell Overton is set to be executed in Florida Thursday over the 1991 murder of husband and wife Michael and Susan ‘Missy’ MacIvor and their unborn infant son](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/19/05/Executions_38286.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

According to court documents cited by *USA Today*, the career criminal had been working at an Amoco gas station near the MacIvors’ house in the early 1990s where he first encountered Missy and became obsessed with her.

Prosecutors believe Overton discovered where she lived from a check she used to pay for gas. He later reportedly told a cellmate he had staked out the residence over the course of several weeks before eventually striking in the early hours of August 22, 1991, a stormy and humid night.

Wearing a “Ninja-type suit,” according to the documents, Overton cut the phone lines and climbed into the property through a second-floor window, hiding out in a nursery the couple had just finished painting light blue ready for the baby boy they were expecting.

They planned to name the child Kyle Patrick and, according to Michael’s sister, Sharon MacIvor, hoped to have at least three more.

The records state that Overton initially attacked Michael MacIvor as he was returning to bed with a glass of milk, knocking him unconscious with a pipe before overpowering the screaming Missy, whom he tied her up with a clothesline, belt and neck tie before raping.

He went on to fatally strangle both Michael and Missy, binding their heads with masking tape, with the unborn child dying in the womb shortly after his mother stopped breathing.

Despite Sharon MacIvor telling investigators about Missy having had an uncomfortable encounter with a creepy gas station attendant she believed was a “predator” who “looked at her like prey,” the crime languished unsolved for a further five years.

![The MacIvors were expecting their first son at the time of Overton’s gruesome home invasion attack](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/19/12/02/Missy-and-Mike-MacIvor.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Overton was arrested on an unrelated burglary charge in October 1996, after which he attempted to take his own life. The blood lost in the attempt was then used to provide a DNA match with semen recovered from the MacIvor house.

At trial, Overton protested his innocence, claimed he had been working at the gas station at the time of the killings but that timesheets proving as much had been lost. He also claimed he was being persecuted by police because he had had a three-month affair with a deputy whose husband was a detective.

A jury did not believe him and he was found guilty of murdering the MacIvors and their unborn son, as well as sexual battery and burglary, with the judge sentencing him to death in March 1999.

Remembering her tragic brother and sister-in-law, Sharon MacIvor told *USA Today*: “They were a delightful, wholesome couple who had their whole lives ahead of them. This guy was just an animal.”

She said her parents had passed away in quick succession in the years following Overton’s sentencing and commented: “I feel like their lives were shortened by the grief. They were never the same.

“My mother and father would both want to be here for this day and I feel it’s important for their sake that I'm present to see this through.”
