Morrisons has beaten some of Britain's biggest supermarkets to be crowned Retailer of the Year at the 2026 Quality Food Awards, with 29 of its own-brand products also recognised by judges.

The supermarket came out ahead of rivals including Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury’s, Aldi, Lidl, M&S and Waitrose to claim the top title, which recognises the retailer with the strongest overall performance across the awards' main food and drink categories.

Morrisons secured two Gold awards, 14 Silver medals and 13 Bronze awards for its own-brand range.

Among the Gold winners were The Best Thai Sweet Chilli Crisps and The Best Free From Gluten Chocolate Millionaires Cookies.

The awards also recognised a range of products across the supermarket's fresh food, bakery and deli aisles, with Silver medals going to The Best Beer Battered Cod, the Deli Ultimate Sausage Roll, The Best Parmigiano Reggiano and Morrisons Free From Mac & Cheese Bites.

Sweet treats also featured, with Peach Melba Ice Cream Bars picking up a Silver award.

Among the Bronze winners were The Best West Country Butter, The Best Avocados, Deli Grazing Houmous and The Best All Butter Brioche Bread & Butter Pudding.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21736763.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Morrisons had 29 own-brand products also winning medals *(Image: Morrisons)*

The Quality Food Awards are judged through blind tastings by a panel of chefs, food scientists and industry experts. Products are assessed on factors including taste, ingredients, aroma, packaging and value for money.

The Retailer of the Year title is awarded to the supermarket that achieves the strongest overall performance across the competition's main food and drink categories.

Rebecca Haliday, Head of Own Brand at Morrisons, said: “We are delighted to be named Retailer of the Year 2026, along with being awarded a number of accolades at the Quality Food Awards.

“A tremendous amount of care and effort goes into testing and perfecting our products across our ranges, so seeing that dedication recognised by the prestigious QFAs is hugely rewarding - and a testament to everyone who works so hard behind the scenes to bring the very best to our customers.”

The award-winning products are available in Morrisons stores and online, alongside the supermarket's wider range of groceries.
