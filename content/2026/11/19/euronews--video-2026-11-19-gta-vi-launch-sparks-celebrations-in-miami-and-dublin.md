Screens displayed images from the game, while mock crime scene tape added to the atmosphere. The excitement was palpable among players eager to explore **the latest instalment of Rockstar Games' crime series**. Some players told AFP they** planned to skip college for two days to play**, declaring that nothing else mattered for the next 48 hours.

In Miami, United States, the launch inspired a **crossover with the NBA's Miami Heat, whose arena was transformed into Vice City**, the fictional metropolis featured in** GTA VI** and inspired by Miami itself. Fans queued for hours before rushing into the arena shop to purchase limited-edition jerseys and shorts bearing the game's branding.

The release follows the **extraordinary success of GTA V, launched in 2013**, which became one of the most commercially successful entertainment products ever. The celebrations underline the** franchise's cultural reach, extending well beyond gaming into sport, fashion and popular culture**.
