From rising interest rates to high inflation, it’s a tough time for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) right now. And as founders look to scale over the next 12 months, they’ll be even more keen to find solutions that make their money work harder.

That’s where generative AI comes in. Increasingly, businesses across Europe are turning to these digital platforms to drive growth and increase productivity, as evidenced by new data released today, which reveals that the total number of products listed by European sellers on global B2B online marketplace Alibaba.com has risen by 78 per cent to 1.28 million from January to November 2025 to January to November 2026.

Used in the right way, AI can help small businesses overcome financial and operational barriers, freeing up time and resources to innovate and grow. According to a recent report by Alibaba.com and Small Business Britain, SMEs have noticed the benefits of this technology. They have noted more efficient workflows and lower business costs, and how these platforms help free up time to innovate and come up with new ideas.

## **Beating the benchmark: Agentic AI**

Conversely, fewer businesses are adopting agentic AI – a lesser-known type of artificial intelligence that can manage tasks for you. In fact, just 44 per cent of small businesses surveyed by Alibaba.com said they had heard of it, and only five per cent said they were using it regularly.

And yet, there is a clear appetite to learn, with 68 per cent of UK entrepreneurs wanting to find out more about what agentic AI can do, from researching options and comparing information to identifying potential solutions and helping move a task towards completion.

Most AI tools have become more accessible, but the quality of outputs can vary, especially if you rely on free, general-purpose agents. This is illustrated by a 107-task benchmark evaluation by Alibaba.com in September 2026.

It showed that its agentic AI platform for businesses, Accio, completed a range of e-commerce tasks, such as spotting payment fraud, calculating landed costs and booking multi-carrier shipping routes, at more than 50 per cent lower estimated cost than other providers.

## **From Ask to Task**

Unlike generative AI tools that simply respond to individual prompts, Accio’s AI agents for e-commerce can carry out multi-stage, autonomous tasks, including comparing supplier options and tracking pricing trends, reducing the repetitive, time-consuming work that takes up small business owners’ days. It brings research, sourcing, and daily e-commerce operations into one AI workspace for small businesses.

Entrepreneurs and business owners can see how other SMEs are putting agentic AI to work at Alibaba.com’s CoCreate Europe 2026, its annual commerce summit for product-driven businesses, which showcases the creativity and entrepreneurial ambition of Europe's next generation of innovators.

To mark the kick-off of this year’s event at the Excel Centre in London today, Alibaba.com has given free access to ‘Accio Sourcing Expert’ to SMEs and founders across Europe as they take their first steps towards becoming more agentic AI-ready. Accio Sourcing Expert is a key part of Alibaba.com’s new ‘European SME Startup Pack’, which is specifically designed for small and medium-sized businesses, consisting of core sourcing capabilities and design features.

## **Innovation Inspiration**

CoCreate Europe demonstrates how access to AI-powered tools and global sourcing opportunities can help businesses turn promising product ideas into commercially viable ventures. And for the first time this year, Alibaba.com is introducing the ‘One Person Company (‘OPC’) Market at CoCreate, which brings together 30 founder-led European brands to show what it takes to turn an idea into a business.

Across beauty, fashion, homeware, food and outdoor products, visitors can meet the people behind the brands, experience their products and discover how they were developed.

Another highlight will be the live final of CoCreate Pitch, a global competition powered by Accio, spotlighting the next generation of product-based innovation in the UK and Europe more widely.

The occasion will see 20 finalists pitch their product ideas live to a star-studded judging panel competing for prize packages worth up to $200,000. Last year, 16-year-old Harrison Nott, founder of CoolTowel, was crowned CoCreate Pitch 2025’s Europe grand winner. Since winning the competition last year, Harrison has used his $200,000 prize money as fuel for expansion of his business, scaling up operations and building out his support team, including logistics and customer service.

More winning entrepreneurs will be announced at this year’s event, giving them the financial tools and confidence to grow. And next year, with the right agentic AI tools allowing you to do more with less, it could even be you embracing your innovative side.
