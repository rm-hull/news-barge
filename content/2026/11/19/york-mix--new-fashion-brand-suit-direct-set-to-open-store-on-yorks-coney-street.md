A fashion brand is planning to open a new shop on Coney Street in York.

Plans have gone in to transform the former Acuitis Opticians shop at number 35.

It had closed by January 2026, blaming “the current economic climate”, after three years trading on the street.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/suit-direct-shop-basingstoke-insta-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/suit-direct-shop-basingstoke-insta-1.webp)

Now menswear company Suit Direct wants to open a branch in the unit.

It stocks a range of men’s suits, accessories and casualwear from brands including Marc Darcy and Antique Rogue

Suit Direct also has a partnership with fashion brand Ted Baker. It launched an exclusive Ted Baker casualwear collection in May.

Earlier this year the retailer, owned by the Leeds-based Baird Group, announced plans to open ten more stores in major retail destination “tier-one cities”.

Suit Direct said it was targeting smaller units below 2,500 sq ft for its new stores.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/acuitis-shop-coney-street-york-outside.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/suit-direct-coney-street-fascia-pd.webp)

The York opening is part of the business’s turnaround strategy.

Fashion business bible Drapers reported: “[Baird Group chief financial officer Tim] Murphy said the retailer’s improving product offer and upgraded store fits had helped rebuild landlord confidence following Baird Group’s 2020 company voluntary arrangement (CVA), which saw rent reductions for 29 stores, alongside 18 store closures and 264 redundancies.”

Online trade accounts for around 20% of Suit Direct sales, with stores representing the remaining 80%.

A planning application submitted to City of York Council asks for permission to create a new store fascia and sign at 35 Coney Street.
