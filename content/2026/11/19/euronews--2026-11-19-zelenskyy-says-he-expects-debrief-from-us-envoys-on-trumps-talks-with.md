## The US envoys told Kyiv they would give a debrief on the talks being held by Trump in China during meetings in Berlin on Thursday, Zelenskyy said.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Wednesday that he expects US envoys to brief him on the talks taking place in China, as Donald Trump prepares to meet China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin.

Trump has said he plans to raise limits on nuclear weapons and an end to the war in Ukraine in his historic trilateral meeting with Xi and Putin at the APEC summit in Shenzhen.

Speaking in his nightly video address, Zelenskyy said, “We and the Europeans have a clear, shared position: issues concerning Ukraine must be decided with Ukraine’s participation, and issues concerning Europe must be decided with Europe’s participation."

The Ukrainian leader said the US envoys had told Kyiv they would give a debrief on the talks being held by Trump in China during meetings in Berlin on Thursday. Ukraine will also hear from other partners currently in China, he added.

Germany, France and Britain are co-hosting talks in Berlin that begin only hours after Trump, Putin and Xi wrap up their landmark trilateral summit in Shenzhen.

More than 30 European leaders are due to attend the Berlin meeting, along with Zelenskyy and US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff, amid fears that Trump, Putin and Xi could make decisions on Ukraine without Kyiv and Europe.

## **War grinds on amid Kyiv's strained ties with Washington**

Trump has often boasted of his rapport with Putin, while relations between Washington and Kyiv appear to have deteriorated recently.

Trump has rejected new sanctions on Russia, and Ukraine has faced disruptions in satellite support for its drones during the war.

But for the first time since February, Russian, Ukrainian and US negotiators held talks on ending the full-scale war in Abu Dhabi earlier this month, with security guarantees topping the agenda.

The two-day session followed previous negotiations early this year that failed to produce any visible progress toward ending Europe’s most devastating conflict since World War II.

Meanwhile, fighting is continuing, despite the Trump administration's renewed diplomatic efforts to end the nearly five-year conflict.

Russian drones attacked Kharkiv Oblast on the evening of Wednesday, hitting an apartment building, Ukrainian media reported, while Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko reported that drone debris fell in the Pecherskyi district of the city on Wednesday afternoon, causing fire.

No injuries were reported, as emergency services were dispatched to the scene.

Earlier, Ukraine struck two oil refineries in southern Russia, something Zelenskyy has praised and described as the "effectiveness of Ukrainian-made weapons."

A strike partly damaged the Kerch Bridge, a strategic crossing linking Russia to the occupied Crimean Peninsula.
