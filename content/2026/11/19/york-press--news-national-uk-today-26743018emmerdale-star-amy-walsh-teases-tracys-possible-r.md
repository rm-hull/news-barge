An Emmerdale star has teased a "very exciting" return to the soap.

Amy Walsh, who plays Tracy Metcalfe (now Robinson), first joined the ITV series in 2014 and became a fan favourite during her time in the village.

The character left the show in March in emotional scenes, departing with her young daughter Frankie in what appeared to be a permanent exit.

However, Ms Walsh has since hinted at a possible return.

In a recent Instagram story, she shared a short clip of herself walking through the soap’s offices, writing: "Was very exciting being back in the building today for some meetings re Tracy’s return."

One of her most significant storylines involved the death of her husband, Nate (played by Jurell Carter) in 2024.

Nate was revealed to have been murdered by John Sugden (Oliver Farnworth), with his body thrown in a lake.

Ahead of his exit earlier this year, Tracy revealed to sister Vanessa Woodfield that the calls she had been having were with Nate’s mum.

Cara, who returned to the village for Nate’s funeral, offered Tracy a fresh start away from Emmerdale, complete with a new job and home.

Tracy’s final episode saw her packing the last of her belongings and telling Vanessa that she needed to leave the village because it held too many painful memories.

She said a heartfelt goodbye to Cain, Nate’s father and Frankie’s grandfather, before driving away to begin her new life with Cara.

The exit storyline was written to coincide with Ms Walsh’s maternity leave, following the birth of her second child with husband Toby-Alexander Smith in March.

She shared a black-and-white photo of her new-born daughter’s feet in a floral baby grow, writing: "This time last week I was heading into established labour.

"We’ve been in the most magical bubble ever since."

The couple are also parents to their daughter Bonnie, who was born in 2022.
