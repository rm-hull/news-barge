# Yorkshire train station revealed as one of busiest outside London

## A city centre train station in Yorkshire was one of the busiest in the UK outside London last year, new data has revealed.

The Office of Rail and Road (ORR) released new annual statistics which showed the busiest and quietest railway stations in Britain.

The station had 28 million entries and exits between April 2025 and March 2026, making it busier than the main stations in Manchester, Liverpool, Edinburgh and Glasgow.

Only Birmingham New Street had more entries and exits in the same period, with 38.8 million.

![The new entrance at Leeds City Station is officially open](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/09/9/36/TCF-station-gateway-main.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The news came as Leeds station finally opened the new steps outside its entrance this month, which were transformed in a £46m project.

London Liverpool Street, meanwhile, retained its title of Britain’s busiest railway station for the fourth year in a row.

London Liverpool Street first replaced Waterloo as the country’s busiest station in the year to the end of March 2023 and has retained this title ever since.

It has now became the first station in Britain in history to surpass 100 million entries and exits in a single year, the ORR said.

![Reddish South was the quietest train station in the country.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2024/12/06/11/56/3-Reddish-South-Greater-Manchester.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Reddish South, in Greater Manchester, was the least used British station of all those that were open that year and saw just 94 entries and exits.

A total of 1,829 million passenger rail journeys were made between April last year and March 2026, an increase of 6% from the previous year, the ORR said.
