## The heads of state of China, Russia and the US are expected to discuss denuclearization and the Iran war, but not Ukraine. We speak with Kremlin critic Bill Browder. The Coalition of the Willing will meet in Berlin, we talk about it with Ignacy Niemczycki, Poland Deputy Foreign Minister.

## On today's show:

Three powerful world leaders, Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump are set to meet for a historic trilateral at the APEC summit in the Chinese city of Shenzhen today. **Investor and Kremlin critic Bill Browder** and Euronews' Russian edition editor**Serguei Doubine** help us understand the consequences of their meeting.

Just hours after the summit, Trump's envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff will be in Berlin for a meeting of the “Coalition of the Willing” with senior officials from Ukraine and its top European allies as well as NATO and the EU.
