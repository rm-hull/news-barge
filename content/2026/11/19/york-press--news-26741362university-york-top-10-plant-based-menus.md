York has been ranked among the top 10 universities for promoting sustainable, plant-based food choices at catered events.

The University of York secured eighth place in the new Russell Group Menu Leaderboard, developed by Forward Food.

The ranking evaluated how effectively universities use menu design and behavioural 'nudges' to encourage diners to choose plant-based meals.

Jo Randall, senior programme manager at Forward Food, said: "Universities serve thousands of meals every day and have a real opportunity to lead the way in making sustainable, plant-based choices easier and more appealing.

"We know students are demanding more sustainable choices, and our research shows there is already brilliant practice at some UK universities, but plant-based dishes are still too often presented as the exception rather than the norm.

"The reality is that plant-based food can be delicious and appealing for all diners and put less pressure on animals and the planet."

Forward Food, now in its tenth year in the UK, has trained more than 500 chefs in plant-based cooking and supports the transition to nearly one million plant-based meals annually.

The leaderboard focused on catered-event menus, such as those for graduations and Christmas parties, which are consistently published online and widely available.
