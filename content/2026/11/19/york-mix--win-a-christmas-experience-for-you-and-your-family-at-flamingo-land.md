We’ve teamed up with Flamingo Land to bring you a fantastically festive competition for you and your family.

This magical prize includes the Christmas Passport to meet Santa in his grotto, bake cookies with Mrs Claus and take part in all the festive activities at Flamingo Land’s Christmas Experience!

Wrap up warm and enjoy a family day out with a visit to the zoo, home to an incredible range of animals from around the world. Winter is a great time to explore at a more relaxed pace, see your favourite animals and enjoy some fresh air together.

Whether you’re visiting with younger children, spending the day with family or simply looking for something different to do, Flamingo Land Zoo makes a great winter day out.

Join Santa’s elves and visit our magical festive land, on these dates: Saturday 5th & Sunday 6th December, Saturday 12th & Sunday 13th December, Saturday 19th to Thursday 24th December.

Christmas Experience Days

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/christmas-at-flamingo-land-logo-2.jpg)

Christmas Light Switch On – Join Peter Rabbit around our Christmas Tree in Muddy Duck Square for the ceremonial switching on of our festive lights to the sound of Christmas Carols!

Show starts at 4:30pm with the big switch on at 4:45pm!

Elf Hunt – Follow the sparkle and find our cheeky elves hidden around the park!

Solve the clues, crack the code and claim your **magical Christmas prize!** Reindeer Feed – Create a little Christmas magic to take home! Visit our**Reindeer Pick & Mix** and choose the perfect festive treats to leave out for Santa’s reindeer on Christmas Eve.

Letter to Santa – Christmas wouldn’t be complete without writing to Santa! Create your **Christmas wish list**, pop your letter into our special post box and send your Christmas wishes all the way to the** North Pole!**

Santa’s Grotto – The most magical Christmas experience of all! Step inside Santa’s enchanting Grotto, meet the big man himself and settle in for a magical Christmas story. A special festive memory for the whole family!

15 children per session, please note groups will be mixed.

Santa’s Magical Toy Shop – Step inside **Santa’s magical Toy Shop** and choose your very own Christmas present! With lots of wonderful gifts to choose from, which one will you take home?

The Decoration Station – Get creative at our **Christmas Decoration Station!** Choose your festive decoration, add your own magical touches and create a special Christmas keepsake to**take home and treasure.**

Wishing Well – Close your eyes, make a Christmas wish and drop it into our magical Wishing Well. Who knows? With a little Christmas magic, your special wish might just come true…

Gingerbread Making – Step inside **Mrs Claus’s magical kitchen** and create your very own gingerbread friend in our fun, interactive gingerbread-making show. Sprinkle, decorate and bring your festive creation to life!

To be in with a chance of winning, simply answer the question on the entry form below.
