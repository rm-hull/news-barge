A much-loved York restaurant has won a national award.

The Asian Curry Awards 2026 were held at Grosvenor House in London’s Mayfair on Sunday.

It’s a prestigious affair, with the Mayor of London giving a key speech at a previous ceremony.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-everest-ghurka-restaurant-asian-curry-awards-web.webp)

And among the winners at the 2026 event was York’s very own **Everest Gurkha Nepalese Restaurant**!

The Fossgate venue took the national title Nepalese Restaurant of the Year.

Afterwards the team posted on social media: “We did it! We are absolutely overwhelmed and so deeply grateful to each and every one of you.

“From the bottom of our hearts — thank you.

“To be voted Best Restaurant in York means more than any of us can say. It isn’t just an award – it’s your trust, your kindness, and your love for what we do that has brought us here.

“This belongs as much to you as it does to us.

“Thank you for choosing us, for sharing your special moments with us, and for making our little place feel like home. We promise to keep cooking with all our hearts, for you, always.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/everest-gurkha-nepalese-restaurant-fossgate-york-gsv.webp)

The long-running restaurant has a 4.6/5 rating on TripAdvisor, and won the website’s Travellers’ Choice award this year.

Reviews include: “Wow! If in York, go to this restaurant”, and “Feels like home”.
