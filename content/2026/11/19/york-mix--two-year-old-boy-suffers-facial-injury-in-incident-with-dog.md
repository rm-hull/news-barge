Police are investigating an incident with a dog which left a two-year-old child in hospital.

It happened at around 2pm on Saturday (14 November) on Skipwith Common in Selby.

The dog, described as a wire-haired terrier, was being walked by a group of three men and two women on Common Road near the car park.

#### Skipwith Common

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said the incident “involved a dog and a two-year-old child who sustained an injury to his face which required medical treatment at hospital.

“We are appealing for the owner of the dog to come forward along with anyone who witnessed the incident including members of the group.”

The men and women with the dog were white and aged between mid-twenties and mid-thirties.
