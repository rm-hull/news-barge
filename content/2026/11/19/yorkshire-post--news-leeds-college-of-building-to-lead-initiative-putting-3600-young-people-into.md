# Leeds College of Building to lead initiative putting 3,600 young people into paid employment

## Leeds College of Building (LCB) is leading a major government initiative which could see up to 3,600 young people across West Yorkshire move from long-term unemployment into paid work.

The college is the only further education institution among 12 organisations selected nationally to deliver the government’s Jobs Guarantee, which will provide eligible young people aged 18 to 24 with six months of paid employment, training and ongoing support.

Nikki Davis, principal and CEO at LCB, said: "For more than 60 years, this college has given West Yorkshire's young people a trade and a future. Now we can do that for thousands more who have been shut out of work for too long.
