A broken down train is causing problems for passengers at York Station this morning (Thursday).

The LNER service became stuck across points before reaching the platform.

A passenger at the station told ***YorkMix*** most trains are now being delayed or cancelled.

LNER said: “A fault on a train in the York area means that some lines are blocked.

“As a result, trains running through this area may be delayed by up to 40 minutes or revised.”

It says disruption is expected until 1pm.
