**A woman who allegedly inspired the stalker character Martha in the hit Netflix drama Baby Reindeer is to appear at Harrow Crown Court on charges of racially aggravated harassment without violence.**

Fiona Harvey, 61, is accused of sending several emails and three voicemails including antisemitic material to lawyer Mark Lewis, between 4 September and 9 October 2025.

During a hearing at Highbury Corner Magistrates' Court on Thursday, the court heard that Lewis initially believed Harvey was a potential client.

Harvey of Camden, north London, did not indicate a plea, but elected for a Crown Court trial.

The charge is an either-way offence, which means it can be heard in either a magistrates' court or Crown Court.

She will next appear at Harrow Crown Court for a pre-trial preparation hearing on 17 December.

Harvey has previously identified herself as the woman on whom Martha is based, after the release of Richard Gadd's Emmy Award-winning series in 2024.

She is suing the streamer £128m for defamation, negligence and privacy violations, arguing that that Netflix told "brutal lies" about her to over 50 million viewers around the world.

Netflix denies the claim, which will be heard in front of a jury on May 18, 2027, at the US District Court for the Central District of California.
