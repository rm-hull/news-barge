**Christmas has arrived at York Castle Museum, with its recreated Victorian street transformed for the festive season and a programme of performances, music and family activities running until 3 January 2027.**

The museum says its popular Kirkgate Victorian Street has been decorated to recreate the atmosphere of Christmas Eve, with a grand Christmas tree, twinkling garlands and festive shop windows.

Visitors can explore the snow-dusted street, see traditional Victorian Christmas decorations and, on selected occasions, meet Father Christmas in his traditional green attire.

## Christmas traditions through the centuries

The seasonal displays explore Christmas traditions across different periods, including seventeenth and eighteenth-century feasts presented in historic dining rooms.

Visitors can also browse festive displays in the Victorian shop fronts and see how Christmas has been celebrated across different generations.

Scrooge also returns for one-man performances of Charles Dickens classic seasonal story, A Christmas Carol, taking place throughout November and December.

## Music from different generations

Music is another feature of this year celebration. Traditional folk music and carol singers will bring familiar Christmas songs to Kirkgate.

The Swinging Sixties Gallery will feature Christmas music from the decade, while recreated kitchen displays will receive a festive makeover featuring memorable Christmas favourites from the 1980s.

## Special events and craft workshops

Additional events include Edwyrdian Tales, an evening of traditional Victorian ghost stories, alongside guided craft workshops.

Visitors will be able to make their own Christmas wreaths and greeting cards to take home.

Carla Starkey, Immersive Interpretation Producer at York Castle Museum, said the team wanted to recreate the excitement of the night before Christmas, bringing together the gifts, food and traditions of Victorian England.

> **Carla Starkey, Immersive Interpretation Producer at York Castle Museum:** She said the museum was particularly excited about the new decorations, performances and festive details in the Kirkgate shop windows, which give visitors a glimpse of preparations taking place behind the doors of the recreated street.

## Dates and visitor information

Christmas at York Castle Museum runs from 14 November 2026 until 3 January 2027.

General admission tickets and information about special events, workshops, times and prices are available from the York Castle Museum website.

The museum is normally open from 11am to 5pm on Mondays and 10am to 5pm from Tuesday to Sunday. Seasonal opening hours may apply.
