A luxury retreat has been named the UK’s best wellness destination.

Yorkshire Spa Retreat in Nawton, North Yorkshire, claimed the Wellness Experience of the Year title at the 21st annual Hoseasons Diamond Awards, held at the Celtic Manor Resort in Newport, South Wales.

The awards, presented by Hoseasons Group Chief Commercial Officer Simon Altham and comedian Tom Allen, recognise the UK holiday parks, lodge resorts and boating locations that guests rated highest for satisfaction throughout the 2026 season.

A spokesman for Yorkshire Spa Retreat said: "We are very proud of this award.

"Our team always goes above and beyond to make sure our guests have a fantastic break when they stay with us, so it’s brilliant to see that hard work recognised in this way."

Yorkshire Spa Retreat, set in more than 11 acres of Yorkshire parkland, offers a tranquil escape in the heart of the Yorkshire Moors.

Known for its luxurious lodges and spacious holiday homes, the retreat is designed to offer the highest level of comfort and relaxation.

Each lodge is equipped with modern amenities, outdoor entertainment areas and Kamado Joe BBQ and ovens, with outdoor kitchens and high-specification hot tubs at selected lodges.

The new Wellness Experience of the Year award is a national honour for 2026, making Yorkshire Spa Retreat its first-ever recipient.

The retreat also retained its title as the Lodge Escape Small Park destination in the North of England.

Ryan Day, account director at Hoseasons, said: "The team at Yorkshire Spa Retreat are richly deserving winners.

"These awards recognise the very best in UK holiday accommodation, so it's a fantastic achievement and one they should be very proud of.

"They always go the extra mile to delight our guests, something which is clearly reflected in their excellent feedback scores.

"We are grateful for their continuing partnership and wish them all the very best for another great year in 2027."

Hoseasons is one of the UK’s leading self-catering accommodation providers, offering more than 29,000 options across coastal and countryside locations.

Part of the Awaze group, Hoseasons welcomes more than 2.4 million holidaymakers and more than 121,000 pets each year.

The company’s Magnificent collection, which includes Yorkshire Spa Retreat, features locations that prioritise luxury, outstanding service, and innovation.

Awaze is Europe’s leading managed vacation rental and holiday resort business and is home to well-known brands including Hoseasons, Cottages.com, and Novasol.

The company delivers holiday experiences to more than six million people annually.

Yorkshire Spa Retreat’s latest success highlights the ongoing popularity of wellness-focused travel and the evolving preferences of British holidaymakers, who are increasingly seeking short, restorative getaways alongside traditional week-long breaks.

The retreat’s focus on relaxation and comfort has clearly resonated with guests, earning it national recognition and reinforcing its reputation as a standout destination in the UK’s growing wellness tourism sector.
