## York Disability Week returns with free arts and culture events

Stephen Lee Hodgkins' letterpress printing press, which will be used during the free Disabled Myths & Legends workshops at York Explore *(Image: York Explore)*

A week-long celebration is set to shine a light on disability culture and creativity.

York Disability Week will run from November 28 to December 5, with Explore York Libraries and Archives hosting a programme of free events to mark the occasion.

The programme is part of a city-wide effort to honour the United Nations International Day of Persons with Disabilities, and will feature the annual Eleanor Worthington Prize exhibition, as well as two printmaking workshops themed around disabled myths and legends.

Sadhbh Dunne, Access and Engagement Archivist at Explore York Archives, said: "Printing has always been a democratising medium.

"By sharing the written word, it allowed ideas, experiences and perspectives to reach audiences that traditional histories have often overlooked."

The workshops, led by artists Stephen Lee Hodgkins, Imogen Kate, Kerry Fox and Alfie Fox, will explore York’s historical print collections, including the York Mercury and materials relating to the Delittle printing business, through hands-on, creative activities.

Mr Lee Hodgkins said: "Our printmaking workshops will reimagine some everyday disabled stories through a myths and legends lens."

The events are part of Explore's Disability Arts programme, which has run for three years and supported disabled artists in creating new work inspired by York’s archives.
