At least 11 people were injured in a Ukrainian attack on the Russian city of Kursk on Thursday, according to regional Governor Alexander Khinshtein.

The daytime strikes damaged an apartment building, private homes and several public facilities, including a regional court, a central library, a medical college and the facade of a trauma centre, the governor said.

Khinshtein said the injuries were caused by falling debris and blast waves from weapons that he did not identify. He described the victims' injuries as minor.
