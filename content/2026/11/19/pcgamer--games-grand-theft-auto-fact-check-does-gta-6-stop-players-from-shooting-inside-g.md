As players explore Leonida for the first time, misleading claims about Grand Theft Auto 6 have begun circulating on social media as evidence that Rockstar has toned down the series' violence. The two claims I've seen most often are along the lines of:

* "GTA 6 prevents players from shooting inside of a gay nightclub"
* "GTA 6 makes planes bounce off of buildings to avoid 9/11 references"

Both of these claims are misleading half-truths.

## Does GTA 6 prevent players from shooting inside of a gay nightclub?

**⛔ No, because there is no gay or LGBTQ nightclub in GTA 6.**![Grand Theft Auto 6 nightclub](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/MukEFn4U4SvDfoXt7t2CrJ-1200-80.png)** It is true that you cannot use firearms inside of GTA 6 clubs, such as Nine1nine and The Vault**. Grand Theft Auto 5 had the same restriction. But none of the clubs in GTA 6 are specifically presented as gay or LGBTQ clubs.

It is reasonable to speculate that Rockstar would want to avoid in-game scenarios that closely resemble the 2016 Pulse nightclub shooting in Florida—the state Leonida parodies—but the studio has not stated as much.

## Do GTA 6 planes just bounce off of buildings?

**⛔ No, unless you've turned on a setting which reduces vehicle damage.** Clips of Grand Theft Auto 6 airplanes smacking into tall buildings without taking any apparent damage have been used on social media to claim that Rockstar is intentionally preventing players from replicating 9/11-like crashes, or that the vehicle physics are shoddy.**The clips of ultra-strong planes aren't fake, but are achieved by turning on "Reduced Vehicle Damage" in the gameplay settings.**

In the menu, Rockstar warns players that turning the setting on will "significantly change the balance and experience of gameplay."

If you fly into a building without reduced vehicle damage, your plane will explode. It doesn't damage the building, but there's nothing to indicate that Rockstar has manipulated vehicle physics to avoid recalling terrorism.
