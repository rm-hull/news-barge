The Reuse Santa appeal is asking residents across North Yorkshire and York to donate quality pre-loved toys so they can be passed on to families who need them most.

**How to donate**  
When: 15th – 29th November  
Where: all household waste recycling centres in York and North Yorkshire  
What: clean, complete and working toys, games, books and puzzles  
What cannot be accepted: toys powered by mains electricity  
Please donate gifts in a condition you would like to receive yourself.

**About the appeal**  
Now in its eighth year, Reuse Santa is run by Yorwaste in partnership with North Yorkshire Council, City of York Council and local charity and community partners. Last year, more than 5,000 toys, the equivalent of around 80 giant Santa sacks, were redistributed.

Donations are collected and distributed through partners including North Yorkshire Council’s Early Help service, community groups within the City of York Good Place Network, Colburn Community Hub, the British Heart Foundation, the Daisy Chain Project and The Recycle Project CIC. Some items may be passed to charity shops.

**Find your nearest recycling centre**  
Details of recycling centres across North Yorkshire and York can be found at www.northyorks.gov.uk/hwrc and www.york.gov.uk/WasteAndRecycling.
