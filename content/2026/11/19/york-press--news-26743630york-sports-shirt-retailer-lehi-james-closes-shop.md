## Independent York shop closes after business rates more than tripled

The empty store today *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

The owner of an independent York shop has cited a raft of reasons for its closure, adding it is becoming a myth that York is a hub of independents.

Lehi James sold football, rugby and other sports shirts as well as women’s beauty products.

The business was situated on the corner of Little Stonegate and Back Swinegate, where Devil’s Elbow Antiques used to be.

The shop opened three years ago, featuring a grand launch involving the civic party.

Over the years, the independent business became highly regarded, earning 4.9 stars out of five on Google based on 20 reviews.

But owner James Humphrey, 34, has now revealed a list of factors behind the store’s closure as he focusses on online sales.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21737445.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The store exterior before the closure *(Image: Pic supplied)*

He told the Press: “Our contract was finishing and when doing the math for extending it did not add up.”

In his time in York James says his business rates have “more than tripled.”

Heating and insurance costs have risen and car parking charges are also up.

Furthermore, retail sales in York have dropped, as confirmed by York BID sales figures, and many retail venues are closing.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21737461.jpg?type=mds-article-620)James Humphrey in the store three years ago *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Landlords also seek 10/15 year contracts.

James added: “Love all the people who came Into the shop and spoke to me and all the customers but we are still online and we will be still getting some great, shirts in.”

“York needs to decide what it wants to be because for me the mantra of York being a hub for Independent shopping is becoming a real myth.”
