# Discrimination claims by McDonald's workers can go ahead, court says

A former McDonald's worker who says a manager failed to protect her when she was being sexualised by customers, has called on the firm to "change the way they treat younger employees".

Hope, who worked at a branch in the West Midlands in 2023, also told BBC News she saw older colleagues eyeing up 16- and 17-year-old workers, and talking about wanting to sleep with them when they turned 18.

McDonald's said: "Ensuring a safe, respectful and inclusive working environment across our restaurants and offices remains a fundamental priority."

BBC correspondent Noor Nanji looks at the latest developments.
