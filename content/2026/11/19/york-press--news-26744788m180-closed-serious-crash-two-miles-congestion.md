A serious crash involving a car and a heavy goods vehicle has closed a motorway in Yorkshire.

The M180 motorway is closed westbound between junction three, Midmoor Interchange, for Scunthorpe and the M181, and junction two, Woodhouse Interchange, for Goole, Epworth and Crowle via the A161.

Humberside Police said an investigation will be required, and the motorway will be closed for a "protracted period."

The eastbound stretch re-opened just after 4pm.

National Highways is reporting that Yorkshire Ambulance, Humberside Fire and Rescue Service and its own officers are at the scene.
