The appeal is organised by Chantry Rotary Club in Wakefield and the Community Awareness Programme (CAP) on Market Street. It is supported annually by the Wakefield Express with the finished hampers being distributed to those in most need by the Children First organisation through its centres in Hemsworth, Castleford, Normanton, Featherstone and Wakefield.

Hampers are also distributed through some local schools where the need is identified.

Scores of people have already donated hampers, so we are well on the way to finalising the packing of hundreds of hampers to bring us up to that target of 600. You can see from the image with this article that the CAP Warehouse is overflowing with the donated hampers.

Chantry Rotary Club volunteers and friends will be packing the rest in the next few days.

Next week will see the distribution completed to the Children First centres so that the hampers will be starting to reach those families who need them most.

Once again, to Express readers and all of those who have contributed, a big thank you for your support and assistance in this truly wonderful exercise in offering community help to those in most need.
