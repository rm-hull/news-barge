North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a motorcycle was stolen in Knaresborough.

The incident happened between 8.30pm on Monday 16 and 1.15pm on Tuesday 17 November when a black and grey Benelli BN125 motorcycle was stolen from Castle Yard.

Following initial enquiries by officers, the motorcycle was eventually found and recovered.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have seen anything suspicious in the area during this period or who may have relevant CCTV, dash cam or doorbell footage.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260225869 when providing any details regarding the incident.
