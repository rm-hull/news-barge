## Claudia Lawrence CCTV breakthrough exposes new clue in disappearance

Claudia Lawrence was last seen or heard from on Wednesday (March 18), 2009 *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

FRESH forensic analysis of CCTV footage captured near missing York woman Claudia Lawrence's home has revealed “previously unseen” details about a person who was in the area on the night she disappeared.

The images feature in the ‘This Much Is True Crime’ podcast hosted by criminologist Professor David Wilson and TV producer Martin Frizell.

They focus on one individual seen in CCTV footage walking along Heworth Place which leads to an alleyway at the back of Claudia’s home in Heworth Road at 7.15pm on Wednesday, March 18, 2009.




The same person is believed to have been seen on CCTV in the area the next morning.

![Fresh forensic analysis has unveiled 'previously unseen' details about a person spotted near Claudia's home on March 18](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739223.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fresh forensic analysis has unveiled 'previously unseen' details about a person spotted near Claudia's home on March 18 *(Image: This Much is True Crime)*

Chef Claudia, then 35, was reported missing on Friday (March 20) after she failed to show for her 6am shift the previous morning at the University of York and a planned drink with a friend at The Nag’s Head, a pub next to her home.

Detectives suspect she was murdered, but neither her body, nor any indication of her being alive have ever been found.

Her house keys, mobile phone, hair straighteners and rucksack have never been recovered.

Forensic experts have digitally enhanced compressed CCTV footage recorded on the evening of March 18 and the following morning.

The technology used was not available at the time Claudia disappeared, Professor David Wilson said.

He added that from the analysis the person seen walking down Heworth Place on March 18 is believed to be a man.

While specialists have been unable to determine the person’s height because of the quality of the compressed footage, they have identified previously unseen features including what appears to be a receding grey hairline or bald head.

The same person was seen just over a minute later walking back towards the junction of Heworth Place and Heworth Road, carrying what appeared to be a rucksack over their shoulder.

Police footage shows that they come to an abrupt stop in the middle of Heworth Place as a person crosses the road ahead – something described as “suspicious” by Professor Wilson.

In the podcast documentary, host Martin Frizell said: “And then he is picked up again [on CCTV] in this very same location at 5 o’clock in the morning the next morning.”

![Tom McDermott (pictured with Claudia's mum Joan), who has spent years examining Claudia's disappearance with his Answers for Claudia podcast, said the new analysis had revealed important details that were not previously known.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739726.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tom McDermott (pictured with Claudia's mum Joan), who has spent years examining Claudia's disappearance with his Answers for Claudia podcast, said the new analysis had revealed important details that were not previously known *(Image: Tom McDermott)*

Claudia’s mum Joan Lawrence, now 83, said she hoped the new images could generate fresh leads.

She said: “It would be good to get more information about the height and build of this man.

“I’m particularly interested in the morning CCTV footage because it’s earlier, daylight, and someone may have seen this person on the way to work.”

York-based researcher Tom McDermott, who has spent years examining Claudia's disappearance with his Answers for Claudia podcast, said the new analysis had revealed important details that were not previously known.

![Tom McDermott appeared on the This Much is True Crime podcast to speak about the findings](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739177.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tom McDermott appeared on the This Much is True Crime podcast to speak about the findings *(Image: This Much Is True Crime)*

He said: “Professor Wilson reached out to me to join him on his podcast with Martin about six weeks ago.

“The independent research is significant because the person was shown to be male, with receding hair or bald, and was seen on March 18 and during the early hours of March 19.

"This is one of the most important findings in the five to six years I have been focused on the missing case.

"It provides further descriptions and could be a vital piece of information.”

## 'At the heart of this is Joan'

He added: “At the heart of this is Joan, who is still desperately searching for any trace of her daughter.

“We need to keep Claudia’s name in the public domain.”

North Yorkshire Police said the man in the footage has yet to be identified.

A force spokesperson said: “We continue to urge anyone with information that could support our enquiries to come forward.

“The investigation into the disappearance and suspected murder of Claudia Lawrence has encompassed numerous lines of enquiry over many years, and has undergone extensive independent review.

“Despite the passage of time, we remain committed to finding the answers Claudia’s family and friends deserve.”
