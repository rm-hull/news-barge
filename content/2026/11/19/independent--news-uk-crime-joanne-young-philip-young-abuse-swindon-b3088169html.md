A former Tory councillor “delighted in fooling his drugged wife” who he raped and abused along with strangers he recruited on the internet to have sex with her in a “betrayal of her trust” which lasted 13 years.

Philip Young advertised his former spouse Joanne Young on websites including Craigslist, Fab Swingers and Grindr as a willing participant in what was portrayed as a “cuckolding” scenario.

Cuckolding is where a man is invited to have sex with the wife as the husband watched on.

In reality, however, Young was actually “stage-managing” a “rape fantasy” where men would silently enter his wife’s bedroom, perform intimate acts on her or have sex with her without her knowledge then quickly leave before they could be discovered, the trial of four men at Winchester Crown Court was told.

The business consultant then posted hundreds of images of his wife being abused on the internet.

To enable this, he would drug his wife with a spiked nightcap he gave her before bed, blindfolded and tied her hands to the bed and told the men that she wanted “anonymity” and did not want to engage with them.

The court heard that Young would target evenings after he and his wife had been out for an evening with a tight-knit group of friends when she would have been drinking during the night to reduce the chance of her waking up.

Describing the betrayal of his wife, prosecutor Sarah Jones KC told the jury: “Mr Young had advertised her on Craigslist, Grindr and swinging websites – he sought strangers to come and sleep with his wife – any age, any race – but as close to Swindon as possible so that when the circumstances were right they could be seized upon immediately.

“He presented it as a kink for both of them but there was no information from Joanne herself to support this and the photos he posted of her to advertise his offer showed her inanimate and tied to the bed blindfolded.

“The nightcap they shared it appeared would be used to plunge her into a state where anything could be done to her by whoever answered and she would be unaware or unable to remember.”

Ms Jones said that video footage recorded by Young of defendant Norman Macksoni having sex with his wife showed Young’s pleasure at his wife’s deception.

She said that Young can be seen asking her: “Are you enjoying that baby?”

“As she agrees he chuckles. It would appear he is delighted with the effect of his system for fooling a drugged woman – by speaking closely in her ear he is making her believe she is having relations with her husband,” Ms Jones said.

And the court heard that it was the dangers of the ploy and its potential consequences which further excited Young.

When he made arrangements with defendant Mohammed Hassan to have unprotected sex with his wife, Young wrote to him: “The thought of even a slight risk is a massive thrill for us.”

Ms Jones said this was a sign of “the absolute determination of the defendants to ignore the risk to her”.

But it was after the night Hassan came round to Young’s house in Swindon, Wiltshire, in February 2023 with the intention of having sex with Mrs Young that Young had to refine his scheming to prevent his deception of his wife from unravelling.

For during Hassan’s visit, blindfolded Mrs Young felt a hand on her arm preventing her from undoing the restraints while another person was performing a sex act on her causing her to panic and pull her blindfold off to see a person ducking down at the end of her bed on the instruction of her husband.

The following morning, Mrs Young, “shocked and confused”, confronted her husband as she had begun to realise that he had “poisoned their marriage”.

Ms Jones said that Young embarked on a mission of “exceptional gas-lighting by presenting himself as penitent” and tried to assure her that the man in her bedroom had been “simply invited to watch” and it had been a one-off.

Both Macksoni and Hassan claimed in court they had been manipulated by Young’s deceit and denied the offences they were accused of during the trial.

But Young’s lies and 13-year series of abuse of his wife were exposed when Wiltshire Police followed up a tip-off that three extreme pornography images featuring bestiality had been uploaded by him on to the Kik messaging app.

When officers turned up at his door on July 4 2024, a search of his phone and other devices revealed numerous images of his wife naked and being abused without her consent.

And it was only at this point did Mrs Young start to realise the extent of her husband’s betrayal.

Ms Jones said: “What she had considered to be already a gross invasion, a betrayal of her trust was far, far more invasive and shocking.”

Police went on to discover that Young had taken 480 intimate images and videos of his wife on 200 occasions and posted images online on at least 500 occasions, with the vast majority of these taken at the family home.

The prosecutor added that the revelations about her husband also brought back memories, warning signs from many years earlier, as Mrs Young recalled an occasion when Young asked her during sex: “Wouldn’t you like it if somebody else was here?”

The court heard that Mrs Young said: “No” and later told police: “I was scared. I was really, really scared.”

The court heard that Young hid his offending not only from his wife but their close group of friends, who described the couple as having a “dream life”, with one friend saying he was always on his phone and described him as “loud and arrogant but that this is what we liked about him”.

James Foster, specialist Crown Prosecution Service prosecutor, said that he feared Young’s crimes against his wife would only have continued if the police had not found the extreme pornographic images he had been viewing.

He said: “Whether he would have continued had he not been discovered given the evidence that we’ve been looking at and the fact that this dates back to 2010, the evidence suggests that probably so.”
