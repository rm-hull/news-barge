A 25-year-old father has died after reportedly being crushed beneath a heavy glass panel while components for a new padel court were being unloaded.

Callum Williams, from Sheffield, was taken to hospital after the incident at an industrial site in Retford, Nottinghamshire, on 11 September, but died the following day.

Emergency services were called to an industrial unit on Aurillac Way shortly after 10am, after Mr Williams became trapped beneath the glass pane as materials for a new padel venue were being unloaded from a shipping container.

He was taken by ambulance to Northern General Hospital, where he later died.

Nottinghamshire Police and the Health and Safety Executive are continuing to investigate the incident.

Mr Williams, known as Cal to his family and friends, leaves behind his long-term partner Claire and their four-year-old son, Vinnie. The couple had been together for around a decade.

![Mr Williams, known as Cal to his family and friends, leaves behind his long-term partner Claire and their four-year-old son, Vinnie](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/15/35/Jam_Press_JMP937139.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=0%2C6%2C418%2C0&auto=webp)

A fundraiser organised by friends and relatives described him as a devoted partner and father, saying his young son had been his “absolute world”.

“With the heaviest of hearts, we are asking for your support following the heartbreaking and unexpected loss of Cal,” the organisers said.

“Cal was a loving father, devoted partner, cherished son, brother and a deeply valued member of his family.

“He was an extremely hard-working man who gave so much of himself to the people he loved.”

The appeal added that the family had lost someone irreplaceable and that the money raised would help ease some of the financial pressure facing Claire and Vinnie.

The GoFundMe appeal had raised more than £1,000 towards its £26,000 target at the time of writing.

## Third death involving padel court components in a year

Mr Williams is the third person known to have died in the UK while handling padel court components since September 2025.

Yasir Ali, 34, and Muhammad Zafar, 33, died on 6 September 2025 while unloading glass panels from a lorry near Hitchin Town Football Club in Hertfordshire.

A third man suffered minor injuries in that incident, according to Hertfordshire Police.

Padel House Co has since opened at the Retford site where Mr Williams was injured.

![A padel court in Battersea, London](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/15/2300239053..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

A spokesperson for the company told specialist publication *The Padel Paper* that his death had been devastating and that its thoughts remained with his family, friends and everyone affected.

The company said it understood and supported efforts to raise awareness of the risks involved in handling and unloading materials during padel court construction.

“We sincerely hope that raising awareness can help prevent another family from experiencing such a terrible loss,” the spokesperson said.

Padel House Co has been contacted for further comment.

The circumstances surrounding Mr Williams's death remain under investigation by Nottinghamshire Police and the Health and Safety Executive.
