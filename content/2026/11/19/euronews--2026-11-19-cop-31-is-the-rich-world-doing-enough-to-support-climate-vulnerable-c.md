## Euronews' weekly evening show Europe Tonight welcomes Sandrine Dixson-Declève, Honorary President of the Club of Rome, to discuss whether COP31 can be called a success, as well as whether the rich world is handing out crumbs to poorer countries grappling with the impacts of climate change.

COP31 negotiators from more than 200 countries are racing in Turkey to reach a contentious deal ahead of Friday's deadline, but tensions have emerged over climate financing and the phase-out of fossil fuels.

Earlier this week, the EU's climate commissioner, Wopke Hoekstra, told Euronews that Europeans do not want to focus on money, but on implementation.
