Soap fans will notice a change to the TV schedule for Emmerdale and Coronation Street next week.

While ITV viewers will be able to watch the usual amount of episodes without missing any, some will air at different times.

Here’s what we know about the soap schedule changes and why Emmerdale and Corrie will air slightly differently in the coming days.

## Emmerdale and Corrie to see TV schedule change next week

On Tuesday, November 24, Emmerdale and Coronation Street follow The Martin Lewis Money Show: Live and will air before I’m A Celebrity…Get Me Out Of Here!

Martin Lewis’ show airs from 7.15pm until 8.15pm, with Emmerdale to start at 8.15pm, 15 minutes later than its usual timeslot.

It will air for its usual 30 minutes until 8.45pm, meaning Corrie will start then, which is 15 minutes later than usual.

I’m A Celebrity starts at 9.15pm, meaning Corrie fans will still get to watch a 30-minute episode.

The rest of the week will run smoothly for the soaps with no other schedule changes planned.

## Emmerdale star Amy Walsh teases Tracy's possible return

An Emmerdale star has teased a “very exciting” return to the soap.

Amy Walsh, who plays Tracy Metcalfe (now Robinson), first joined the ITV series in 2014 and became a fan favourite during her time in the village.

The character left the show in March in emotional scenes, departing with her young daughter Frankie in what appeared to be a permanent exit.

However, Ms Walsh has since hinted at a possible return.

In a recent Instagram story, she shared a short clip of herself walking through the soap’s offices, writing: “Was very exciting being back in the building today for some meetings re Tracy’s return.”
