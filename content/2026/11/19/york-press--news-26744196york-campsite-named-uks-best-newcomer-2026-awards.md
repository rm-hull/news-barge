## North Yorkshire campsite named UK’s Best Newcomer in 2026 awards

Holly Tree Nook *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A newcomer near York has made a big impression after being named among the UK’s best camping destinations.

Sites across York and North Yorkshire took home a string of honours at the 2026 Campsites.co.uk Camping and Glamping Awards, which celebrate the UK’s most outstanding camping, glamping, and touring sites.

North Yorkshire’s Holly Tree Nook was named Best Newcomer and was also runner-up in the Best Adult Only Site (North East England) category.

The adult-only site, located just 10 minutes from York, offers guests a quiet countryside retreat with convenient access to the city.

* **Flaxton and Ravensdale camps earn place in national top 150**

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738323.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Flaxton Meadows *(Image: Pic supplied)*

It features a complimentary evening sauna, free WiFi, and is within walking distance of a café and two pubs.

A park and ride service is also just a 15 minute walk away, providing easy transport links.

The site boasts a 4.94-star rating, with 98 per cent of guests saying they would recommend it.

Reviews include glowing comments such as "Beautiful little campsite" and "Excellent is not a good enough description."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738343.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Glamping Pods at Dwell on the Moor *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Martin Smith, founder of Campsites.co.uk, said: "All of these sites have earned their place through hard work and a commitment to getting the details right, treating guests to a special stay time after time.

"My congratulations to all of our winners, and thank you for giving us all so many reasons to get outdoors."

Other York-area winners included Flaxton Meadows, which was named Best Adult Only Site (North East England).

The site offers nature trails, hedgehog houses, and bird boxes, alongside freshly made pizzas and a bar offering both alcoholic and non alcoholic drinks, with sporting events shown on TV.

Hillside Caravan Park in Knayton took the title of Best Caravan Park (North East England), while Catgill Farm Camping and Glamping in Bolton Abbey was named Best Campsite in the region.

Howgill Lodge, also in Bolton Abbey, was runner-up in the same category.

Black Bull Caravan Park in Pickering and Vale Of Pickering Caravan Park in Allerston were both runners-up for Best Caravan Park.

Westgate Carr Farm Caravan and Motorhome Touring Park in Pickering was runner-up for Best Adult Only Site, and St Helens In The Park in Scarborough was runner-up for Best Family Campsite.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738322.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Aerial view of the pitches at Holly Tree Nook *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The Campsites.co.uk awards recognise excellence across a range of categories, including Best Campsite, Best Glamping Site, Most Unique Site, Greener Site Award, and Best Budget Friendly Campsite.

National winners for 2026 included Nantcol Waterfalls in Gwynedd (Best Campsite), Tregarton Park in Cornwall (Best Caravan Park), and Carrock Glamping Pods in Cumbria (Best Glamping Site).

Other top titles went to Cosmos Camping in Carmarthenshire (Best Adult Only Site), Beech Estate Campsite in East Sussex (Most Unique Site), and Tehidy Holiday Park in Cornwall (Greener Site Award).
