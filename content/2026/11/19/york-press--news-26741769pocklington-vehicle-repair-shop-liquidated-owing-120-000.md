A highly-rated Pocklington car repair company has been liquidated with debts topping £120,000.

R&B Automotive of Blenheim Road, Pocklington Industrial Estate was incorporated in 2023 and went into a creditors voluntary liquidation on November 2.

Companies House records its directors as Thomas Ashley Birch and Lewis George Rawding.

The liquidators were Clark Business Recovery of Leeds, who produced a report known as a statement of affairs outlining R&B’s financial situation.




* **Opus Recruitment Ltd of Pocklington in voluntary liquidation**
* **JP Commercial Vehicles registered in York in liquidation**
* **Harrogate sandwich shop enters liquidation owing £42,883**
* **AG Construction and Developments Ltd of York in liquidation**

The report says the business had no preferential creditors – someone who is paid first.

However, HMRC was owed £19,272.57 as a secondary preferential creditor – someone paid next.

The report also reveals unsecured creditors – those paid last – who were owed more than £100,000 between them.

They consisted of eight trade and expense creditors owed £45,230.34 between them.

In addition, two banks/financial institutions owed £40,537.44 between them.

A landlord was owed £10,000 and HMRC was owed a further £8,545.23 in corporation tax.

This gave a total shortfall or deficit of £123,585.58.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £100, this gave a total shortfall or deficit of £123,685.58, the statement of affairs added.

Earlier in August, the two directors posted: "After many amazing years, we have made the incredibly difficult decision to close R&B Automotive.

"This has not been an easy decision for us to make, and we would like to take this opportunity to thank each and every one of our customers for your continued support, loyalty and custom over the years.

"We are truly grateful for everyone who has supported our business and been part of our journey."

Customers have reacted with sadness at the closure.

One said: "Your absolutely joking. You was the only place I would take my car who I trusted, absolutely gutted, all the best for the future guys thank you."

Another added: "Absolutely gutted to read this. It’s so hard to find a trustworthy mechanic. You have been super to rely on."

Such support is confirmed by online reviews, with Google awarding it 4.8 stars out of five, based on 26 Google reviews.

The latest Google review said: "Called in on a Saturday for a aircon regas, was extremely helpful and friendly and happy to fix as a walk in. Would recommend."

Another said: "Know a good and friendly workplace when I see it. I gave them a car most places would be scared to touch, they’re honest about what they can do and what they can’t. I’ll keep coming back."
