Robert Jenrick has been mocked for criticising Margaret Thatcher’s legacy, despite having admitted that he named one of his daughters after the former prime minister.

The Reform UK MP said Thatcher had left a “deep scar” on post-industrial communities, adding she was a “dated” reference.

In response, Labour MP Luke Charters posted: “This you Rob?” on a screenshot of an article which reveals that Mr Jenrick’s daughter’s middle name is Thatcher, alongside a post of his interview with *The Sun*, in which he criticises the Iron Lady.

At a Conservative Party conference in 2024, the former Tory MP said his daughter was born the same year that Thatcher died.

He told the audience that the name was “a good way of reminding her of a good prime minister”, as well as being a tribute to “strong women”.

![Robert Jenrick has been mocked for criticising Margaret Thatcher despite admitting to naming daughter after her](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/12/14/01M783Z7W0REVM599JAFJ689HH.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

However, in an interview on Wednesday, Mr Jenrick said that Thatcher’s policies led to "a loss of pride and identity" in communities across the country, adding they “never fully recovered”.

He also criticised Kemi Banedoch’s praise of the former prime minister, saying Thatcher is a “dated” reference to young people.

![Margaret Thatcher served as prime minister of the UK from 1979 to 1990](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/09/29/17/31/margaret-thatcher-aa924a5b.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Mr Jenrick has also faced mockery this week over an “AI slop” parody video of hotly-anticipated game Grand Theft Auto VI, in which a character intending to be the Reform MP tackles crime and works to reduce the cost of living.

The party’s spokesperson for the economy shared the video on social media on Thursday evening, as Britons eagerly waited to play the new video game, released at midnight, 13 years after the previous instalment in the blockbuster series.

In Reform’s parody of the GTA 6 trailer, called “Grand Theft Jenrick”, a virtual Mr Jenrick, wearing jeans, a white t-shirt and a plaid overshirt, is presented as the protagonist of the new game.

“I didn’t leave my last party on the best of terms”, he says in the video about his defection from the Conservatives, before adding it was the “best thing I’ve ever done”.

![The Robert Jenrick character in his GTA parody](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/8/19/Screenshot-2026-11-19-at-08-44-29.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The video shows Mr Jenrick tasked with cutting the cost of living, controlling migration and tracking down fare dodgers.

On X, Canning Town Conservatives said: “From running to be the PM, to creating AI slop of yourself. Thank goodness Kemi won.”

Political commentator Luke Robert Black said: “He’s now doing a Trump and making AI slop of himself.”
