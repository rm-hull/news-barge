Berlin is facing heightened security measures and traffic disruptions for several days. On Thursday, German Chancellor Friedrich Merz (CDU), together with French President Emmanuel Macron and British Prime Minister Andy Burnham, will host a meeting of the “Coalition of the Willing” in the capital.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy is expected to arrive on Thursday. According to the German Police Union (GdP), 3,000 additional police officers will be deployed, partly due to concurrent Gen Z protests.

Security authorities use levels 1 to 3 to determine safety measures, with level 1 being the highest standard threat level. The classification is based on the perceived risk of an attack. President Zelenskyy falls under an exceptional level 0 classification.

Security authorities are taking special measures: sealed manhole covers, explosive-detection dogs, airspace monitoring, water police on the Spree River, snipers on roofs, a close security-strategic escort for the state guest, and comprehensive road closures.

The U5 line will temporarily not stop at the Bundestag station. Boat trips in the government district are also not possible. Furthermore, Berlin airspace is closed, according to police. The arrival and departure of state guests will cause further disruptions, they said.
