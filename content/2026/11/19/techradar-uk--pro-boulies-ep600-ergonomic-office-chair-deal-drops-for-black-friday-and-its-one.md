## Why we recommend the Boulies EP600

Our office furniture editor Steve rated the Boulies EP600 4 stars in his in-depth review, where he called it "one of those office chairs that does everything most people need (and a few things they didn't know they wanted)."

Spending two weeks using the chair as his daily driver for 10 to 12-hour shifts, he found it comfortable, with the lumbar support and breathable upholstery as particular highlights. It's so good, he's continued to use it now a month after he began testing it.

The EP600’s 4D adaptive lumbar support is designed to respond to changes in your posture, and the chair has 6D armrests that are packed with movement options. It’s also got adjustable seat depth to accommodate different body shapes.

## Price context and historical value

The EP600 occupies an interesting position in the market. At its usual $489.99 price, it may struggle to compete with some established designs.

However, its combination of adaptive lumbar support, extensive armrest adjustment and Black Friday $80 discount makes it a more appealing proposition.

That combination of lower price and superb adjustment makes the EP600 a strong contender if you want a chair that handles both work and relaxation, and it’s particularly effective if you regularly switch between different sitting positions throughout the day.

The big draw here is the adjustability, so make sure you’ll actually use all of those features before you invest.

## Should you buy it?
