A beloved Channel 4 Yorkshire drama starring Brenda Blethyn has been given a major update.

After it was confirmed earlier this year that Channel 4’s A Woman of Substance would be getting a second series, it has now been announced that filming has started for it.

The show is based on the novel written by Barbara Taylor Bradford, with the first series being broadcast in March of this year.

The drama proved to be popular with viewers, with the second series announced shortly after it aired.

## Filming for A Woman of Substance has started

Set in Yorkshire, A Woman of Substance is based around Emma Harte, who is described as being a “housemaid-turned-mogul daughter, mother, lover, fighter and a 20th century feminist icon”.

The show is set across two different timelines with Emma’s character being played by both Brenda Blethyn and Jessica Reynolds.

Both of the actors have been confirmed to be returning for the second series.

The synopsis for the show reads: “Ambition, betrayal and burning revenge spurred the formidable Emma Harte to the top.

“But success comes at a cost...

“Adaptation of Barbara Taylor Bradford’s classic bestseller, with Brenda Blethyn.”

Channel 4 has also confirmed there will be a few new characters being introduced to the show.

Sherwood’s Oliver Huntingdon is set to play Wilson, someone from Emma’s past, and Under Salt Marsh’s Dino Fetscher will be playing Paul, a suitor who is set to “capture Emma’s attention at an unexpected moment”.

Freddie Dennis, who starred in Queen Charlotte, will be playing Arthur, a figure who will become intertwined with Emma’s life.

Alongside Brenda and Jessica, several other cast members will also be returning to the show, including Mobland’s Emmett J Scanlan and Slow Horses’ stars Lenny Rush and Niall Wright.
