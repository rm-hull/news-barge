## Independent makers set to hold festive Christmas market in Malton

The Little Bird Made Christmas Artisan Market is set to bring festive cheer and unique gifts to shoppers in Malton *(Image: Supplied)*

An artisan market is set to bring festive cheer and unique gifts to shoppers in Malton.

Taking place in the area's historic Market Place, the Little Bird Made Christmas Artisan Market will run from 10am to 3pm and will feature a curated selection of independent makers, bakers, and producers.

Visitors can browse a variety of handcrafted goods, including ceramics, artwork, festive food, preserves, and more.

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: "Christmas artisan markets bring real magic to our market towns and high streets, offering festive shoppers something genuinely unique that can’t be found anywhere else.

"Shopping small and supporting independent creators this Christmas keeps our local economies thriving, as well as celebrating the charm and community spirit that make North Yorkshire towns like Malton so special at this time of year."

The market promises a festive atmosphere and an opportunity to support small businesses while finding one-of-a-kind gifts.

Charlotte Goldie, owner of Priory Vodka, said: "We are very excited to be trading at Little Bird’s Malton Market.

"It is an opportunity for us to share our passion for creating quality Yorkshire spirits with the local community."

For more information on Little Bird Artisan Markets, or to book a stall at an event, email [info@littlebirdmade.co.uk.](mailto:info@littlebirdmade.co.uk.)
