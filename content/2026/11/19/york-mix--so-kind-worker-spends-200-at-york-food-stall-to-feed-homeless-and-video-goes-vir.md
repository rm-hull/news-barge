A video capturing a customer spending £200 at a York Christmas Market food stall to buy hot food for his team and the city’s homeless has been viewed around two million times in 24 hours.

Simon Long, owner of Shambles Kitchen, was serving at his Scandi sausage stall at York Christmas Market yesterday morning (Wednesday) when the man placed an order for 20 sausages for his workers.

When Mr Long offered a discount to charge for just 18, the customer – called Ali – asked to round the total up to £200, with the money left over to pay for meals for rough sleepers.

The gesture has prompted an outpouring of praise online, and Ali even returned to the stall this morning to pay for a second batch of food for the homeless, which Shambles Kitchen has matched.

Simon, who is in his 12th year trading at the York Christmas Market, had only just opened the stall on Parliament Street when the exchange took place.

“I don’t really like it when you see influencers doing nice things just for the camera,” he told ***YorkMix***.

“But it wasn’t like that at all. He came up to the stall and we’d only just opened. We didn’t actually have that many sausages on the barbecue.

“And he asked for 20 sausages for his team, and then I said I’d only charge him for 18. Then he asked if we could round it up all the way to £200, saying ‘Just give them to the homeless, please’.”

## Let people know

After starting the order, Simon paused to ask if he could record the man repeating his request so he could share the moment on social media.

“I feel like my pride was saying don’t video this – then I thought, ‘Oh no, I need to let people know about this guy’.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/shambles-kitchen-york-christmas-market-sausages.webp)

“He’s so kind, and he definitely didn’t do it for the camera. There was no one around, so it was a genuine act of kindness.”

The customer, who is not on social media himself, identified himself as Ali. He works for Derby-based construction firm GF Tomlinson.

Ali had no idea how widely the clip would be shared until younger members of his team told him he had become “Insta famous” and “TikTok famous”.

Since being posted, the footage has racked up around 1.5 million views on Instagram alone, alongside hundreds of thousands more on Facebook.

“It’s been overwhelmingly positive,” Mr Long commented. “People are just saying this is a good reminder of humanity’s best bits.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-christmas-market-2026-first-day-simon-long-york-sausage-company-ldrs.webp)

He added that while a small number of online commenters initially assumed the interaction was staged for views, the context of the footage quickly set the record straight.

With the extra funds paid yesterday and the matched donations from this morning, Shambles Kitchen is now working to ensure the food reaches those who need it most in the city centre.

Mr Long said his team can take food out to homeless people in the evenings once the stall closes, and is urging members of the public to spread the word.

“If anyone passes by a homeless person, just tell them to come to the stall, and we’ll give them a free sausage,” he said.

“It’s been quite a tough year for a lot of people. So just to be reminded that there are very nice, kind, generous people out there is definitely a good timely reminder with Christmas.”
