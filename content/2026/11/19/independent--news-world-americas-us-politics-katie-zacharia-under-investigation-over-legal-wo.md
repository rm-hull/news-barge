The State Bar of California has opened an investigation into former White House press secretary Katie Zacharia, who is accused of practicing law in the state without a license.

Zacharia, who lasted only five days as Donald Trump’s chief spokesperson, had previously claimed that she served as general counsel for a nonprofit organization in the state despite never passing the bar exam there or having any legal credentials to practice law anywhere in the U.S., let alone California.

After Trump tapped Zacharia as White House press secretary, several legal advocacy groups filed complaints with California’s bar following media reports that detailed her lack of legal experience.

In its letter to nonpartisan nonprofit group Campaign for Accountability, the state bar indicated that an investigation is underway, and the group’s complaint has been forwarded to an investigator and legal advisor.

“Zacharia may have thought she was just playing a lawyer on TV, but by repeatedly holding herself out as an attorney on news programs, she was pretending to have authority she lacks,” Campaign for Accountability executive director Michelle Kuppersmith said in a statement Thursday.

![The State Bar of California is investigating former White House press secretary Katie Zacharia following complaints that she practiced law in the state without a license](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/17/08/2299465387..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“These are baseless allegations,” a spokesperson for Zacharia told *The Independent.*

“This complaint is politically motivated and a clear waste of the State Bar’s time and resources,” the person added. “We are confident it will be dismissed with no finding of wrongdoing.”

Zacharia had repeatedly referred to herself as a lawyer throughout her media career, and Trump hailed Zacharia as an “attorney” in his Truth Social post announcing her appointment to the highly-scrutinized role.

After graduating from Pepperdine University’s Caruso School of Law in 2011, Zacharia worked for Chinese firm Zhong Lun, where she has claimed to have worked as an “outside counsel” to the World Bank and the International Finance Corporation. “It was an interesting and exciting experience,” she told *The Buck Sexton Show* last year.

From January 2024 to March 2025, she served as general counsel with Fix California, a right-wing nonprofit group founded by Richard Grenell, who previously served as acting director of national intelligence during the first Trump administration.

Fix California is “committed to pursuing long-term solutions that advance conservative ideas and causes throughout the state,” according to its website.

The group reported $557,089 in revenue in 2024, according to tax filings. It does not appear Zacharia was paid a salary, but she received $27,367 for her “in person and fundraising solicitation,” filings show.

The State Bar of California operates under the Supreme Court of California as the chief licensing organization for attorneys in the state. Zacharia’s name is not listed with the California bar, nor does she appear on the state’s list of attorneys under her maiden name.

![Zacharia, center, alongside Trump aide Natalie Harp, left, and Trump campaign adviser Danielle Alvarez, right, lasted only five days in the role of White House press secretary after media outlets raised critical questions about her legal career](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/15/10/2300304613..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Following media scrutiny into her legal credentials, Zacharia’s LinkedIn profile appears to have been updated to change her role with Fix California to “senior advisor.”

Zacharia’s profile also states that she was an “associate” on the “international practice team” at Zhong Lun Law Firm. The international firm has more than 400 partners and “2,200 professionals” in “17 locations throughout major financial centers in China as well as Hong Kong, Tokyo and New York,” according to its website.

“I practiced international transaction law, with all the appropriate credentials,” Zacharia said in a statement to *The Independent* last month.

Following her work with Zhong Lun and Fix California, Zacharia does not appear to have engaged in any other legal work. She began working as a commentator for right-wing media outlet Newsmax in 2024 and then as a commentator with Fox News the following year.

She also worked as a deputy assistant secretary for public affairs with the Department of Homeland Security from February to March of this year.

Since June, she has served as a senior communications adviser with the president’s social media platform Truth Social and its parent company Trump Media & Technology group before Trump brought her over to the White House.

Under California rules prohibiting the unauthorized practice of law, anyone found guilty of the misdemeanor of practicing law or falsely holding themselves out as a lawyer can face up to one year in a county jail and a fine of up to $1,000.

“The White House press secretary is the voice of the administration, we should be able to trust what he or she says,” Norm Eisen, a former White House ethics lawyer and co-founder of Democracy Defenders Fund, among groups that filed a complaint with the State Bar of California.

“How can we trust someone who appears to have held themselves out to be one thing when they are not?” he added. “No one is above the law, which is why we’re calling on the state bar and the attorney general to thoroughly investigate these serious allegations.”
