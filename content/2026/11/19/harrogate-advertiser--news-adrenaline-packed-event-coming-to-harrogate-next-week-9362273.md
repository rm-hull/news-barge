Harrogate fans of adrenaline-packed adventures in the great outdoors can look forward to the return of the spectacular Banff Mountain Film Festival World Tour next week.

On the bill on the big screen at the Royal Hall will be six stunning films featuring extreme journeys and, intriguing characters engaged in extreme running climbing, kayaking, mountain biking and a lot more.

Taking place on Wednesday, November 25, this regular feast of real-life short films shot in the great outdoors around the globe has become a Harrogate favourite for nearly 20 years.

Tour director Nell Teasdale said: “Whether you’re a seasoned adventurer or simply love great storytelling, this year’s films offer something for everyone.

“This is your chance to immerse yourself in a night of gripping adventure – up on the big screen.”

The UK and Ireland tour is back for its 17th year, and will visit more than 50 locations in 2026.

The films are chosen from hundreds of entries into the Banff Mountain Film and Book Festival, which is held every November in the Canadian Rocky Mountains and celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2025.

The breath-taking documentaries will be introduced by a compère, and each screening will see a free prize giveaway to win outdoorsy goodies from the tour’s partners.

For this visit by Banff Mountain Film Festival, Harrogate audiences will be treated to a selection from the Blue Film Programme.

Among the film highlights will be:

A Baffin Vacation: Love on Ice (26 mins) – An expedition into the wild and remote Arctic Circle.

Best Day Ever (46 mins) – The Audience Choice Award winner featuring deep powder skiing.

Cold Calls: Japan (6 mins) – Fast-paced action and exploration in Japan.

Flow Vision (8 mins) – Dynamic cinematography showcasing motion and terrain.
