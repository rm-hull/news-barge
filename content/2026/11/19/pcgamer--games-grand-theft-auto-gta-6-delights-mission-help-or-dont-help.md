You'll face a choice in Chapter 2 of GTA 6, when you're ready to help Vicky rob her boss, Frankie, at the Delights strip club in Port Gellhorn. Frankie asks you an important question and you've only got a couple seconds to respond:

"Can you dance? Yes or no, dude?" Frankie has caught you (as Jason) coming in the back door of the club as you and Lucia prepare to rob it. It's ladies night and one of the male strippers is out of commission, but Mario, the lead stripper, gets an idea when he meets Jason. Frankie has the same idea, and asks if Jason knows how to dance.

Yes, they are asking Jason if he can fill in as a male stripper for the show.

* **Help**: If you help, Jason becomes a male stripper for the night, and yeah, it's hilarious. You dance, you strip, you grind on ladies in the crowd (including Lucia) while wearing a teeny tiny white thong, and it's just a whole lot of silly fun. And you still get to rob the place!
* **Don't help**: If you don't help, Mario walks away and the strip show is cancelled. But Frankie tries, by himself, to get the ladies worked up, performing a cringe-worthy (but funny) show on stage. Jason quickly interrupts it by putting a bullet into the ceiling and announcing that it's a robbery. You still get to rob the place.

Personally, both versions are worth seeing, but Jason playing a stripper, and Lucia's utter delight at seeing him play a stripper, is way more fun.
