Russian President Vladimir Putin has described his meeting with US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping as an "entirely open and candid" exchange, saying the leaders discussed a range of international security issues.

The talks covered the war in Ukraine, nuclear risks, maritime security and the role of international institutions, according to Putin.

He said he hoped the meeting would lead to further discussions and potentially agreements.
