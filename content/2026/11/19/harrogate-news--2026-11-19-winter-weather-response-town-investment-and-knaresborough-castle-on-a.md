**North Yorkshire councillors will discuss a review of the council’s response to the January 2025 winter weather, progress on investment plans for Harrogate and surrounding towns, and the future maintenance of Knaresborough Castle when they meet next week.**

The Harrogate and Knaresborough Area Committee will also hear from local MP Tom Gordon, receive an update on the council’s local services and consider reports relating to a petition about Viaduct Terrace and active travel schemes.

The meeting takes place at Harrogate Civic Centre on Thursday 26 November 2026, starting at 10am.

## What will councillors be discussing?

### Review of the January 2025 winter weather

Councillors will receive a report looking back at the winter weather event which began on 4 January 2025 and how the council responded.

The review has been prepared by the council’s Highways and Transportation service. It will look at the response to the event and consider what needs to be taken into account when preparing for similar weather in the future.

The meeting papers also include separate assessments looking at the equality and environmental implications of the review.

### Tom Gordon MP to attend

Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon is due to attend the committee meeting to give councillors an update and take part in a discussion.

The agenda does not specify the subjects he will cover.

### Investment plans for Harrogate, Knaresborough and Boroughbridge

The committee will receive an update on Town Investment Plans covering Harrogate, Knaresborough and Boroughbridge.

The report, prepared by the council’s Community Development service, will bring councillors up to date on progress with the plans and the projects and programmes being developed for the three towns.

### Knaresborough Castle maintenance

Councillors will hear an update from North Yorkshire Council and Historic England about work following a petition concerning the maintenance of Knaresborough Castle.

The petition was considered by the committee at its June 2026 meeting. This latest update will cover the action taken since then.

The update will be given verbally by the council’s Assistant Director for Property Procurement and Commercial, alongside Historic England.

### Update on local council services

A report from the council’s Localities Service will cover projects and programmes being delivered in the area and progress made since the committee’s previous report.

The Localities Service works with communities and local organisations on issues affecting their areas. The report is intended to give councillors an overview of the work taking place across Harrogate and the surrounding communities.

### Viaduct Terrace petition

A written report concerning a petition about Viaduct Terrace will be included in the papers circulated to councillors.

The agenda lists this as a report for information only. It does not provide further details about the petition or set out any specific decision for councillors to make at this meeting.

### Active travel schemes

Councillors will also receive an update on active travel schemes. These are projects intended to make it easier and safer for people to walk, wheel or cycle.

The update is included among the supporting papers for the committee’s work programme.

### Other business

The committee will also review its work programme, which records issues raised at previous meetings, work undertaken since then and matters that may need further attention.

## Can the public attend?

The meeting will take place in the Council Chamber at Harrogate Civic Centre, St Luke’s Avenue, Harrogate, HG1 2AE, and members of the public can attend.

Residents wishing to ask a question or make a statement must give notice to the council’s Democratic Services team by midday on Monday 23 November. Speakers are limited to three minutes.

There will be no livestream of the meeting. However, North Yorkshire Council says a recording will be uploaded to its website after the meeting.
