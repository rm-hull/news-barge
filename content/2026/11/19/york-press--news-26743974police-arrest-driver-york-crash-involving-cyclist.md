A MAN has been arrested following a crash which has left 20-year-old man with serious injuries in York.

The incident took place on the junction of James Street and Lawrence Street at around 6.14pm on Wednesday (November 18).

It involved a cyclist and a vehicle.




North Yorkshire Police said: “The driver of a vehicle believed to be involved, was arrested later on Wednesday evening on suspicion of failing to stop following a collision and causing serious injury by careless driving.

“He has been released while under investigation while enquiries continue.

“Officers continue to appeal for witnesses and dashcam footage - please get in touch if you can help.”

Please email [SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 if you believe you can help.
