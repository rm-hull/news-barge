# I'm A Celeb Ricky Wilson's real name, TV director dad and Leeds United connection

## Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson is currently appearing on I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!

Ricky Wilson is amongst the celebrities currently battling it out in the Australian jungle on the latest series of I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!. The singer joined 10 other well-known personalities who ventured into the wilderness earlier this week.

Upon arriving at Brisbane Airport last week, Ricky playfully said that he was involved in a "top secret TV show that I can't talk about." He continued: "My flight was cancelled but there are lots of trials. I am here to do a top secret TV show I can't talk about. Are my bandmates not here? I thought I was meeting them here. I am not a great liar am I?

"I have never been camping. I've been to festivals playing them, never camping." While Ricky embarks on his jungle adventure, here's what you should know about the musician's background, including his connection to Leeds United, reports the Daily Star.

### Ricky Wilson's early life and career

Born Charles Richard Wilson in Keighley, West Yorkshire, Ricky is the son of Glynne Wilson and television producer Geoff Wilson, who directed the 1970s game show It's a Knockout. The pair crossed paths while working in the television industry.

Prior to achieving stardom, Ricky was educated at Leeds Grammar School following Ghyll Royd primary school, before going on to Leeds Metropolitan University where he obtained his degree in Graphic Arts and Design. Following his master's qualification, Ricky spent a year teaching at Leeds College of Art and Design, until his band's breakthrough moment arrived.

Ricky initially formed the band Runston Parva in 1996, which later evolved into the Kaiser Chiefs in 2000. The group achieved multiple UK chart successes, with tracks such as I Predict a Riot and Ruby. I Predict a Riot holds particular significance in Leeds, as it's famously played following every Leeds United home fixture at Elland Road - the club Ricky supports.

In 2006, the Kaiser Chiefs frontman suffered injuries in a hit-and-run incident while using a pedestrian crossing. "I wanted to get home to watch Phone Booth, which was on at 10pm. I was crossing the road; it was a green man. I walked past one car that had stopped, and when I got halfway across, a car came out of nowhere," Ricky previously told NME.

"You know how they say you see it coming in really slow motion? Well, it did. I jumped in the air, and it clipped my shins and flipped me over, and I hit the windscreen."

Shortly after the accident, the Kaiser Chiefs posted an update on their website, which stated: "He would like all of you to know he's OK. He's the luckiest man alive, because he is still alive. It sounds daft, but a 'Ricky trademark jump' has probably saved his life. The jump meant he was flipped over the top of the car, hitting the windscreen, rather than getting trapped under it."

### Who is Ricky Wilson's wife?

Ricky is married to celebrity stylist and costume designer Grace Zito, whose impressive portfolio spans numerous TV productions, commercials and music videos. The pair first crossed paths on the set of ITV's hit competition The Voice, where Ricky served as a coach, while Grace worked her magic behind the scenes as a stylist.

Ricky got down on one knee in 2018, however the road to the altar proved far from straightforward. Originally intending to tie the knot in 2020, the couple were forced to delay their big day no fewer than four times due to the COVID-19 pandemic. They finally said their vows in June 2021, and the following year welcomed their twins, Knox and Rocco, into the world.

Ricky's "good friend" Leigh Francis made his jungle entrance via helicopter on Wednesday (November 18) night. Before joining the show, Leigh revealed: "I thought to myself, if I don't do it this year, my mate Ricky's doing it, and I'm sat at home watching it on the sofa. I'll be really envious watching a slow-mo of him laughing and having a great time. So, that's why I'm here."
