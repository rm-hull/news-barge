President Trump’s warm reception of Russian leader Vladimir Putin in China has been branded an “embarrassment” for America.

​Trump met with President Putin at an APEC summit in Shenzhen, on Thursday - almost five years after Russia launched its war against Ukraine.

​Upon entering the room, Trump paused, pointed at Putin and jokingly asked “Who is he?” before breaking out into a smile.

​As he walked over to his Russian counterpart, the grinning Trump answered his own question: “He’s my friend!”

​The commander-in-chief’s warm reception of a man shunned by much of the international community has been slammed by politicians, journalists and political commentators in the United States and abroad.

![President Donald Trump gave Russia’ Vladimir Putin a very warm reception ahead of the trilateral meeting](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/8/51/bfeff330c7434a519bd3cf7322ba4555-ap.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

​Crime writer Don Winslow uploaded a fiery post on X, shredding Trump.

​“Man responsible for the invasion of Ukraine is ‘my friend,’” Winslow wrote. “Man responsible for the Russian army raping and slaughtering women and children in Ukraine is ‘my friend.’

​“Man responsible for the Russian army kidnapping 20,000 Ukrainian children is ‘my friend.’”

​Ben Meiselas, the co-founder of news outlet *Meidas Touch*, was more blunt: “WTF WTF WTF.”

​Dan Shapiro, the former U.S. ambassador to Israel, said: “Trump greeting Putin with such warmth is beyond nauseating.”

​Former U.S. Ambassador to Russia Michael McFaul noted that many in the United States do not view Putin favorably.

​“In calling Putin his ‘friend,’ Trump is radically out of step with American society,” McFaul wrote.

![On the same day as their one-on-one meeting, Putin and Trump met with Chinese President Xi Jinping](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/08/2305842980..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Matthew Sussex, an assistant professor in strategic studies, wrote on X: “What an embarrassment for America to have to witness this.”

​The U.S. president attended the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit as part of his Asia tour, which has also included a visit to the Philippines.

​Trump’s meeting with Putin lasted for around 40 minutes, with Reuters reporter Trevor Hunnicutt confirming that the meeting was held in a small room within the wider conference center.

​“No editorial presence is being allowed in with the video camera,” Hunnicutt wrote on X. “Russian officials cited parity and small room size.”

![Putin was condemned by much of the international community following his invasion of Ukraine](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/09/2305843089..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​Trump has long praised Putin, previously stating that he has a “great relationship” with the Russian president whom he considers a “genius.”

​Kremlin foreign policy aide Yuri Ushakov told *Reuters* that Trump and Putin had discussed Ukraine in their meeting and that the Russian president’s mood was “simply excellent” after it concluded.

Later on Thursday, Trump met with Xi and Putin in a trilateral meeting.

Ahead of the APEC summit, Trump said that he wanted to discuss his “favorite subject: Ukraine” with the two presidents, along with the Iran war and efforts to reduce nuclear arsenals.
