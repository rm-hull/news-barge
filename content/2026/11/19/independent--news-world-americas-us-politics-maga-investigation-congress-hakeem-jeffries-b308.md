House Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries vowed to investigate “everyone” involved in the “MAGA extremist movement” when Democrats retake control of Congress in January.

Jeffries, a New York Democrat, made the remarks during a Wednesday interview with *MS NOW*, just hours after his colleagues elected him in a closed door meeting as their nominee for House Speaker, putting him on course to become the first Black lawmaker to hold the position.

“We've got to make meaningful, dramatic, decisive and bold change,” Jeffries, who has served as House minority leader since 2023, told host Jen Psaki. He said top priorities for Democrats — who took back the House and Senate in the midterm elections — include affordability and accountability.

“I think that everyone who has been part of this MAGA extremist movement that has unleashed all of this carnage and damage on the American people should be prepared to answer questions as it relates to what has happened,” he said.

It was unclear whom he was referring to, and he declined to name names, saying that would be up to the House Judiciary and Oversight committees, both set to be chaired by Democrats.

![House Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries said that Democrats are prepared to investigate 'everyone who has been part of this MAGA extremist movement'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/17/52/Screenshot-2026-11-19-at-12-15-52-PM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“We've made clear that if folks out there have been stealing from the American people, short-changing the American people or screwing over the American people, then in the new Congress, we’re gonna hold the crooks accountable,” the lawmaker said, adding, “We gotta follow the facts, we gotta apply the law.”

His message is consistent with what congressional Democrats have been signaling for weeks: that they intend to cast a wide net in investigating the Trump administration and its allies.

On Wednesday, Rep. Robert Garcia, the Oversight Committee’s top Democrat, wrote letters to a dozen administration officials and others in Trump’s orbit, requesting that they preserve documents, signaling an investigation could be forthcoming. Among those who received letters were President Donald Trump’s eldest sons, Vice President JD Vance and Attorney General Todd Blanche.

In an apparent bid to thwart Democrats, the White House has reportedly instructed federal officials to obtain permission before testifying on Capitol Hill. Blanche has said the Justice Department will cooperate with Congress but has insisted “we will not back down” from Trump’s agenda.

Democrats have also signaled an eagerness to pursue the impeachment of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. Some high-ranking members have also suggested impeaching Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.

![Jeffries, a New York Democrat, was reportedly nominated to serve as House speaker in a 251-11 vote that took place behind closed doors Wednesday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/17/2305758000..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Jeffries, who was reportedly nominated to serve as House speaker in a 251-11 vote, also outlined “three broad areas” he wants to focus on when the new Congress is seated next year.

He said these are: “ending the [Iran] war, stopping the Trump tariffs and reversing the damage that’s been done as it relates to the health care of the American people, and that’s got to include reversing the Medicaid cuts and also extending the Affordable Care Act tax credits.”

Psaki predicted that the House would pass legislation on these issues and “dare” Trump to veto it.

“That would be correct,” said Jeffries, who will face a separate roll call to become speaker in January.

On the heels of the midterms, the Republican president expressed a willingness to work with the opposition on certain issues, such as immigration, though he also said he would veto “insane” legislation.
