**A councillor has been seriously injured after being stabbed while undertaking his duties in Bradford.**

Bradford Moor councillor Ibrar Ali was attacked at about 11:04 GMT on Manse Street, according to the deputy leader of Your Bradford Independent Group, councillor Ishtiaq Ahmed.

West Yorkshire Police said a 32-year-old man had been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder and was being held in custody.

The force added there was "no information to suggest that this is a politically motivated incident at this time".

Ali is believed to have been injured on Thornbury Avenue, before being driven to the nearby Manse Street where emergency services attended, according to police.

A force spokesperson previously said the suspect had climbed on to the roof of a property on Thornbury Avenue following the incident and was later brought down.

![A smiling man in a suit with a grey beard and bald head](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/9659/live/906518e0-e42a-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

Councillor Ibrar Ali was stabbed while walking around Bradford Moor

Ahmed said Ali was undergoing emergency surgery at Leeds General Infirmary after being stabbed in the leg. He said Ali was in a stable condition but had lost a lot of blood.

"My thoughts are with him and his family and loved ones. It's an extremely distressing time," said Ahmed.

"We shouldn't have to fear for our personal safety because we have chosen to serve the public.

"There should be no normalisation of violence in our democratic life."

Responding to the incident in his Bradford East constituency, Labour MP Imran Hussain described the incident as "horrific" and "deeply disturbing".

"My thoughts and prayers are with councillor Ibrar Ali and his family," said Hussain.

"No elected politician should feel under threat or fear for their safety while carrying out their duties and serving their community."

Ch Supt Richard Padwell urged anyone with information to contact police.

"This is a serious incident where a local councillor has been stabbed in the street and our thoughts are with him, his family and colleagues at this difficult time," he said.

"Whilst the investigation is in the early stages, there is nothing to suggest that this incident is politically motivated at this time."

Ali is a community development manager at charity The Leap.

He has worked with community organisations Bradford Moor Pass and was one of the founders of Leeds Road Festival.

He was first elected as a councillor in the May local election, replacing the previous Labour representatives.
