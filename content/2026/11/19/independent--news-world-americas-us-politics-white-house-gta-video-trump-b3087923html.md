The White House was blasted for posting a montage of President Donald Trump in the style of Grand Theft Auto, with online users labeling it “cringe” and “a little stale.”

The 30-second clip was posted by the official White House X account Wednesday, a day before the worldwide launch of GTA VI, one of the most hotly anticipated video games in years. It was captioned “WELCOME TO MAGA CITY XLVII.”

It opens with a graphic that states “The golden age” and “47” in the same font as the popular action-adventure game.

“Less than two years ago, the American people gave us a mandate to save America,” a voiceover of Trump says in the video. “And in 19 months, I've delivered on every single promise I've made.”

Then begins a montage of the 80-year-old president walking alongside fighter jets, riding in the presidential limo and speaking to a crowd of supporters. The song “Money for Nothing” by rock band Dire Straits blares in the background.

![The White House was blasted for posting a GTA-style montage of President Donald Trump, hours before the long-awaited video game was released](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/12/38/Screenshot-2026-11-19-at-7-35-58-AM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The clip garnered 700,000 views and over a thousand comments, many of which expressed little enthusiasm for the spoof.

“So Cringe,” wrote one user, while another chimed in: “This s\*\*\* ain’t hitting anymore.”

“These just aren’t serious people and it’s getting a little stale,” added a third commenter.

The Trump administration has a long track record of leaning into pop culture in its messaging. Earlier this year, it promoted a Tetris-style “Build the wall” game. Last year, the administration shared a Studio Ghibli-style illustration of a detained migrant — capitalizing on a viral social media trend — and posted a video likening ICE agents to Spider Man.

Other users begged the White House to focus on voter concerns.

“We just want healthcare, affordable groceries-gas-oil……..etc. But go off with your middle school bulls\*\*\* tweets,” wrote one.

![Grand Theft Auto VI) is one of the most hotly anticipated video games in years and made its debut Thursday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/13/2026-11-19T093141Z_1928277512_RC2VQOABLLWO_RTRMADP_3_GAMING-GTA-VI-THAILAND.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Delusional,” added another commenter, who highlighted the president’s low approval rating. “There is no Golden Age.”

The post came on the heels of the midterm elections, in which Democrats romped to victory in a string of red states, reclaiming the House and the Senate, amid voter frustration over the Iran war, the cost of living and other issues.

Fox News, the president’s preferred channel, has also touched on GTA 6 this weekend, casting it as a culture war issue.

“I’m gonna push back a little,” Fox & Friends co-host Rachel Campos-Duffy said during a segment on the game, in which players find themselves in a criminal underworld. “I’m not thinking that this is a good thing for our culture.” She then wondered aloud whether one of the female characters was “a hooker.”

The game launches worldwide on Thursday, with players able to access it from midnight local time.
