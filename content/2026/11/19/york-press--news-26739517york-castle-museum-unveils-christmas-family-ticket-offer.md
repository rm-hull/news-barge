York Castle Museum has announced a special weekend ticket deal following the first snowfall.

The museum is offering a family ticket for £45, valid for unlimited visits throughout the Christmas holiday.

The deal is available for two days only, Saturday, November 21 and Sunday, November 22, 2026, and covers up to two adults and three children.

Visitors will have the chance to experience 'The Night Before Christmas' in the Victorian street of Kirkgate, which is frozen in time on a Victorian Christmas Eve.

On Saturday, November 21, Victorian carol singers will perform traditional tunes throughout the day.

On Sunday 22 November, visitors may bump into a jolly fellow dressed in green and telling stories, a Victorian Father Christmas!

Throughout the museum, there are interactive trails, creative activities, fun interactions and play opportunities for all ages.

The ticket is valid for 12 months, meaning unlimited revisits throughout the Christmas holiday.

Dressed shopfronts are filled with Victorian Christmas goodies, and snow on the cobbles creates the perfect seasonal atmosphere.

The ticket deal offers families the opportunity to make festive memories together before the December rush.
