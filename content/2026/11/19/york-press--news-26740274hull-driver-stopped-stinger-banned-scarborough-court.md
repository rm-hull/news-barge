## Drink-driver stopped by police stinger banned for nearly 4 years

The arrest of Max Harrison *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A drink-driver stopped by a police stinger has been banned from driving for nearly four years.

Max Harrison, 31, of Woodhall Street, Hull, pleaded guilty to drink driving on Amotherby Lane, Amotherby, when more than twice the limit on October 27.

He was ordered to pay a total of £730 consisting of a £461 fine, £85 prosecution costs and a £184 statutory surcharge.

He was also banned from driving for 46 months.

Scarborough Magistrates’ Court heard that when he took a breath test, the reading was 86 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21642158.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police give Max Harrison a breath test - which revealed he was drink driving *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

The legal limit is 35 microgrammes.

Magistrates gave him the chance to reduce his ban for 46 weeks by taking an approved drink driving rehabilitation course.
