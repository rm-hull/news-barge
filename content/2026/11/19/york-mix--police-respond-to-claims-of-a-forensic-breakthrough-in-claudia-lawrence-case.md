North Yorkshire Police have responded to reports of a breakthrough in the Claudia Lawrence case.

Imaging specialists say they have uncovered new physical details about a mystery man caught on CCTV near the Heworth home of missing York chef Claudia, according to reports by The Mirror.

The enhanced analysis suggests that two separate clips released by detectives in 2015 – filmed on the evening Claudia vanished in March 2009 and the following morning – feature the same individual, who has a receding hairline of grey hair or may be bald.

Claudia, who worked at the University of York’s Goodricke College, was 35 when she disappeared from her Heworth Road home nearly 18 years ago.

Five weeks later, North Yorkshire Police announced that the case was being treated as a suspected murder.

The fresh forensic work was commissioned by criminologist Professor David Wilson and broadcaster Martin Frizell for their podcast, *This Much is True Crime*.

Using publicly available footage, two independent analysts examined clips recorded outside nearby student flats at 7.15pm on 18 March 2009 and 5.07am on 19 March.

In the evening footage, the man walks towards an alleyway behind Claudia’s house before returning over a minute later with his hood up, carrying a rucksack and stepping into the shadows to avoid a passing pedestrian.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/york-chef-claudia-lawrence.webp)

Prof Wilson said the experts believe both CCTV images involve the same man.

He told *The Mirror:* “We are now very close to identifying ‘the man with something to hide’ and would urge North Yorkshire Police to subject the original CCTV footage – which only they possess – to new, improved forensic analysis.

“The CCTV that exists from the night of the 18th and the morning of the 19th clearly shows a man – and it has now been established the same man – hiding something.”

He added that the case could still be solved without a crime scene or body if that suspect is identified.

Prof Wilson told *The Mirror:* “Cold cases often become hot again when people feel, for whatever reason, that they can no longer remain silent about what they know.”

Claudia’s mother, Joan Lawrence, 83, is now calling on North Yorkshire Police to run the raw CCTV files through modern forensic enhancement tools.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/joan-lawrence-mother-claudia-judge-rinders-crime-stories.jpg)

Ms Lawrence told *The Mirror:* “This could be a critical piece of information and could jog someone’s memory.

“The police need to delve into this more. It’s soul destroying that the police haven’t enhanced the CCTV. It’s heartbreaking. It’s so odd that someone was hanging about Claudia’s house.”

She added: “If anyone knows who this is please come forward because having no answers consumes my every waking hour.”

Mr Frizell told *The Mirror* that the podcast team will hand over all their findings to detectives.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The investigation into the disappearance and suspected murder of Claudia Lawrence has encompassed numerous lines of enquiry over many years and has undergone extensive independent review.

“The man in this footage has not yet been identified, and we continue to urge anyone with information that could support our enquiries to come forward.

“Despite the passage of time, we remain committed to finding the answers Claudia’s family and friends deserve.”
