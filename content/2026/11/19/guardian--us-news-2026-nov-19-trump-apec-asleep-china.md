Donald Trump was, again, accused of falling asleep during a televised event, this time during the speech of the Chinese president, Xi Jinping, at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (Apec) summit in China.

The US president appeared to close his eyes for roughly 10 seconds on Thursday. As his eyes closed, Trump’s head tilted downward, and his hands remained clasped in front of him.

Several prominent Trump critics on social media alleged that the US president was asleep, including Republicans Against Trump, California governor Gavin Newsom’s press office and conservative lawyer George Conway.

White House officials denied the allegations, claiming the 80-year-old US president was looking down at papers in front of him.

“Fake News! President Trump was looking at papers in front of him while the speaker made remarks. You can clearly see his eyes move back and forth,” Steven Cheung, the White House director of communications, wrote on X in response to a Newsweek article.

“This is why the people despise media so much. They constantly lie,” Cheung added.

Trump often faces accusations of falling asleep during televised events such as Oval Office press conferences, cabinet meetings and sporting events.

![The president attending a sporting at an arena.](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/b761406ed9aff21d719985260a563826e908fef0/0_0_3934_2506/master/3934.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Occasionally, while listening to other people speak, the US president will close his eyes for several seconds, appearing as though he is falling asleep. Despite footage of the instances going viral, the White House and Trump allies have insisted the US president does not fall asleep during meetings.

Administration officials have excused Trump’s apparent drowsiness as blinking or an example of the president listening intently. They consistently claim Trump “doesn’t sleep”.

Marco Rubio, the US secretary of state, testified to lawmakers earlier this year that he had “never seen” Trump fall asleep.

“On the contrary, the guy doesn’t sleep, which is a big problem because he calls me at two in the morning,” Rubio said in June.

![An older man apparently asleep next to a yonger man](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/60e5e6e60c1669a87db200d2bc3c06851693e7e6/0_0_3000_2000/master/3000.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

Last year, JD Vance said that Trump hardly ever sleeps, even while traveling overnight.

“If we go on a 20-hour trip somewhere, he doesn’t sleep the entire time. And of course, if he’s not sleeping, if he’s working, he expects everybody else to be working too,” Vance told Fox News.

Trump became the oldest US president ever inaugurated when he was sworn in nearly two years ago. Despite his age, Trump often claims he is in near-perfect health, often bragging about his “perfect” cognitive test results and insisting he has a lot of energy.
