## York café to give away 1,000 free coffees on opening day

Federal prepares to open a café in Coney Street, York, on Friday, December 4 >(Image: Federal)>

A new brunch café will celebrate its arrival in York by giving away 1,000 free coffees on opening day.

Federal will open on Coney Street on Friday, December 4, and plans to hand out complimentary espresso drinks, lattes, flat whites, and cappuccinos from 7.30am.

This will be the brand’s largest café to date and its second in Yorkshire, following last year's opening in Leeds.




Claudio Ribeiro, founder of Federal, said: "Giving away 1,000 coffees is our way of saying hello to York.

"We’ve found a building with real character on Coney Street, and we want it to feel like somewhere people can make part of their day, whether they’re popping in for a coffee or settling in for brunch with friends.

"We have been meticulous in our vision for York and I’m extremely proud of the whole team who have worked so hard to create a really stunning space.

"We are hugely excited to welcome our first customers through the doors at the end of November and become part of York’s thriving independent food scene."

![A serving suggestion from Federal brunch cafe](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738718.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Federal York will open on Coney Street with a giveaway of 1,000 coffees *(Image: Federal)*

The menu will include Federal’s signature dishes such as French toast, steak and eggs, halloumi and shrooms, alongside rotating specials and seasonal food and drink options.

The café will serve a range of drinks, including coffee and teas, fresh juices, beer, and cocktails.

As part of its launch, Federal has partnered with York’s Chocolate Story to create a special drink for the opening month: Emily’s Banana Bread Hot Choc.

Lucky customers will also have the chance to win exclusive chocolates from York’s Chocolate Story during opening weekend.

The café is housed in a Grade II-listed building and features seating across two floors plus a large outdoor area.

Founded in Manchester in 2014, Federal is inspired by the neighbourhood cafés of Australia and New Zealand and is known for its "sunny side service," speciality coffee, and quality brunch offering.
