A major UK care provider has collapsed into liquidation and is set to close after 28 years of operation.

Better Healthcare Services, established in 1998, delivered home, hospital, and care home support, including live-in and complex care.

It also offered temporary and permanent staffing for hospitals and care services in areas such as mental health, elderly care, and learning disabilities.

Better Healthcare Services employed up to 500 people and described itself as "one of the fastest-growing care providers in the UK."

The company had offices across East Anglia, South East England, and London,

## Better Healthcare Services enters liquidation after 28 years

After 28 years, Better Healthcare Services has now entered voluntary liquidation and is set to close.

A winding-up order was approved on November 12, according to The Gazette, while Edward Robert Bines and Janet Elizabeth Burt of Kroll Advisory Ltd were appointed joint liquidators.

The company’s website has since been taken offline.
