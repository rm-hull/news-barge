If you're one of the countless millions contributing to the collective endangering of global productivity by enjoying Grand Theft Auto 6 at launch, you might have paused in the middle of blatantly disregarding traffic laws to wonder: *Wait a second, am I hearing "Weird Al" Yankovic hosting this pretend radio station?*

And yes, you are: In a Bluesky post earlier today, the beloved comedic musician and polka maestro confirmed that he offered his voice for DJ Al, who hosts '80s music station Vice City FM alongside Swedish actress, model, and singer Brigitte Nielsen. Their contributions were then double-confirmed by the full GTA 6 credits, which Rockstar published once launch time hit.

> I’m the host of GTA 6’s “non-stop ‘80s” radio channel Vice City FM. I also wrote some in-game commercials, and I’m the voice of the Burger Shot ads as well! As a long-time fan of the Grand Theft Auto franchise, I couldn’t be more excited about this. Okay, gotta get back to playing the game!
>
> — @alyankovic.bsky.social (@alyankovic.bsky.social.bsky.social) 2026-11-19T17:06:55.045Z

"I’m the host of GTA 6’s 'non-stop ‘80s' radio channel Vice City FM. I also wrote some in-game commercials, and I’m the voice of the Burger Shot ads as well!" Yankovic said. "As a long-time fan of the Grand Theft Auto franchise, I couldn’t be more excited about this. Okay, gotta get back to playing the game!"

Somewhere out there, Weird Al is securing the love of his virtual latina wife, and I think that's beautiful.

Yankovic is just one of countless recognizable names you'll find if you scroll down the GTA 6 credits. Among the core cast, SNL alumnus Bobby Moynihan voices Cal, Jason's bumbling, conspiracy-brained buddy. The pair's criminal boss man, Brian Heder, is performed by legendary Stephen Root—who you'll likely recognize as Bill Dauterive on King of the Hill, Milton in Office Space, and all kinds of magnificent weirdos across the Coen brothers filmography.

![GTA 6 character Brian Heder](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/KAVCkMQ6m9p7Ayo6NQ3BdD-1200-80.jpg)

But it's the radio talent where GTA 6's credits get particularly wild. It's a cavalcade of international music icons, comedians, and film figures. While driving around GTA 6, you might hear Lana Del Rey hosting Back Country Radio, or Latin music phenom Bad Bunny on Cocoteo FM. On Dirty South Classics, there's Trina, Trick Daddy, and Gucci Mane. Pop station Flash FM is helmed by Robyn and Alex Consani—the first transgender model to win Model of the Year at the British Fashion Awards and celebrated TikTok goofball. *Keith Richards is in there.* I didn't even know he was still capable of speech.

On talk radio, you can hear Ted Lasso's Jason Sudeikis, Abbot Elementary's Janelle James, and John Leguizamo of being John Leguizamo fame. Stavros Halkias is on there doing a Conservative talk radio bit. But maybe the most compelling inclusion, for me, is that Kaleidoscope FM is hosted by *Jeremy Irons.* The Academy Award-winning actor. You were in Kafka, Jeremy. How'd you end up in cyber-Florida?
