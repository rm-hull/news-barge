A new shop is to open on a prestigious Harrogate street as a well-known modern British fashion brand becomes the latest well-known business to be captured by the lure of the town.

Refurbishment is ongoing at Joules at 18 James Street, which was formerly occupied by outdoor retailer Trespass.

Founded in 1989 by Tom Joule, the family-run brand hopes to ‘redefine British classics’ offering high quality takes on traditional British clothing with clothes for men, women, and children.

Already the new Harrogate branch is sporting the brand’s signature bright yellow ochre, with blue and grey features.

This is not the first time that Joules has had a shop in Harrogate.

In fact, this famous national name in clothing formerly had one in the same street.

Located at number 29 James Street, that one closed in 2023 as part of a nationwide closure plan.

Joules currently has more than 60 shops in the UK in total, the majority of which are in listed buildings in historic town settings.

Its new Harrogate store is expected to open its doors at the end of next week.
