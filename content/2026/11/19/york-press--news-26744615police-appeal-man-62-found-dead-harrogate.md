A 62-year-old man has been found dead in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is assisting the Coroner’s Office to locate the next of kin of Stuart Slater, who lived in Harrogate.

He sadly passed away on Wednesday (November 18).




A police spokesperson said: “There are no suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.

“If you have any information that would help the Coroner to locate any members of Mr Slater's family then please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”
