Two History Hit presenters have spent time at Ripon Workhouse Museum & Garden to find out what life was like for those who came through its doors.

Filmed in the museum’s restored rooms, *Could You Survive a Victorian Workhouse?* follows Luke Tomes as he seeks shelter for two nights and Katie Kennedy, known online as The History Gossip, as she takes on the role of a resident pauper.

Luke is admitted to the casual ward, where people without a settled home could stay for a short time in return for work.

The Tramp Major takes his clothes and sends him to break rocks. Luke is given gruel to eat and ends up in the cells after he is caught in the Master and Matron’s quarters.

Katie follows a resident’s routine of work, exercise, prayer and school.

She collects vegetables from the workhouse garden and meets the Workhouse Guardian, who interviews her before she is allowed to stay.

Ripon Museums said:

> “We were delighted to welcome Luke and Katie to Ripon Workhouse.
>
> "Their visit brings the museum’s stories to life in a fresh, informative and fun way, while showing how differently people could experience the Workhouse.
>
> "We hope the episode puts Ripon in the spotlight and introduces the museum to a new audience.”

The episode is available now on History Hit’s YouTube channel.

The final open day of the season at Ripon Museums is Sunday 22 November. All three museums reopen on Saturday 6 February 2027.
