The National Centre for Early Music have launched their Young Composers Award for 2027.

The announcement was made on on BBC Radio 3’s Early Music Show.

For the 2027 instalment, young composers will be working with The Marian Consort, renowned for their innovative programming, thrilling performances and groundbreaking recordings of early and new music.

Composers are invited to write a new piece, between three and four minutes long, for the eight voices of The Marian Consort, inspired by the music of Portuguese composers of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

Stylistically, these works are notable for refined counterpoint, sometimes contrasted with striking moments of homophony, and expressive text-setting, full of chromaticism, dissonant suspensions and gestural figuration.

Composers should take inspiration from aspects of this music and reframe it through the lens of our own times. Details of the full composition brief can be found here.

Composers selected for the final will be invited to a collaborative workshop day in at the National Centre for Early Music in York on Thursday 29 April 2027. The shortlist of entries will be presented by The Marian Consort and their Artistic Director Rory McCleery, in a workshop led by composer Christopher Fox.

This will be followed by a public performance of all the selected compositions as well as repertoire chosen by The Marian Consort in an evening concert the same day. The winning compositions will be announced at the end of the performance.

The concert and announcement of winners of the 2027 NCEM Young Composers Award will also be livestreamed on YouTube and the NCEM website.

The winning works will be premiered by The Marian Consort in a public performance as part of the Brighton Early Music Festival on 8 October 2027, which will be recorded for broadcast on BBC Radio 3’s The Early Music Show and BBC Sounds.

This major national annual award is open to young composers up to the age of 25, who are residents in the UK, and is divided into two categories: 18 years and under and 19 to 25 years.

Dr Delma Tomlin MBE, NCEM director said: “The Young Composers Award is one of the highlights of our musical calendar and every year we are amazed at the calibre of work we receive from the enormous pool of young talent.

“The award provides an invaluable boost to the composers’ future careers, giving them the chance not only to hear their work performed by world class artists, but also receive mentoring and advice.

“This year we are delighted to be working with The Marian Consort and of course, our partners, the fabulous team at BBC Radio 3.”

Rory McCleery, artistic director of The Marian Consort said: “Working with living composers, particularly those at the early stages of their careers or who haven’t previously had the opportunity to write for vocal ensemble, is, alongside our championing of lesser-known early music repertoires, one of the cornerstones of our activity, so it’s wonderful to bring these two strands together in this project.

“We’re looking forward to working with Christopher and to exploring all the fantastic ideas the composers bring to their pieces!”

Entrants should register online by Wednesday 3 February 2027 to take part in the competition. Entry is open to all UK residents aged up to 25 at the time. The deadline for submission of scores on Wednesday 3 March 2027.

Shortlisted candidates will be invited to attend the Award Day in York on Thursday 29 April 2027. NCEM will meet reasonable travel and accommodation costs from within the UK.
