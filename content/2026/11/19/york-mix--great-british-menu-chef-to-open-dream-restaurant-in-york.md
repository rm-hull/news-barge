A new Mediterranean-inspired restaurant is set to open in Haxby next year.

The new independent neighboured restaurant Filos, meaning ‘friend’ in Greek, will open in Ryedale Court in Haxby, York, in early 2027.

Fittingly, the restaurant is founded by two good friends. Ahmed Abdalla met Banos Pavlou not long after moving from London to York – before long, he was often sat around the Pavlou family table, enjoying Mediterranean meals and barbecues.

Now after a decade of friendship and shared meals, the pair are opening a restaurant of their own.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Ahmed-Banos-%EF%BF%BD-3.webp)

Some of the menu that will be served is influenced by the Mediterranean, with dishes built around open-fire cooking.

Visitors to Filos can expect ingredients that are aged, cured, smoked in-house and turned slowly over flame, with inspiration from across the Mediterranean with a Yorkshire twist.

“We have been working on Filos for some time now. The idea started one evening over a meal,” said Abdalla.

“Ban has a wealth of operational experience and knowledge; our skills complement each other’s.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Filos-%EF%BF%BD-Menu-Preview-2.webp)

“Filos feels like a natural fit. It encompasses the friendship and the food that brought us together, and I am excited to share that hospitality with others.

“As for the location, York has helped shape me into the person and the chef I am today, so I knew I wanted it to be in or around the city.”

Abdalla previously led the kitchen at Legacy, the fine-dining restaurant at The Grand, York, and returned as a wildcard for the final of BBC’s Great British Menu.

“There are lots of incredible restaurants in York already, but I am excited to bring something a little different to the scene,” said Abdalla.

“For chefs, York and Yorkshire is a dream. We are so lucky to have such high-quality produce on our doorstep, grown, caught, and reared locally.

“That gives me so much to work with in the kitchen, whether it’s a beautiful piece of fish from the East Coast, some local grass-fed beef, award-winning strawberries or foraged chanterelles.”

Banos Pavlou, general manager and co-founder of Filos, brings around 17 years of hospitality experience to the venture.

He built his career running family-owned venues across York, managing some of the city’s well-known bars, including Vudu Lounge, Bobo’s Cocktail Bar and Dum Dum Tiki Bar.

At Filos, he will lead front of house, the bar and the wider running of the business alongside Abdalla.

For Pavlou, this project is also personal with his family being Greek Cypriot he has drawn on that heritage to bring a warm, family led hospitality feel to Haxby.

“York has a brilliant community, and we are really looking forward to adding to that,” said Pavlou.

“Filos is close to home for me. Enjoying this style of food with family and friends is a huge part of our culture. It is how we build and maintain our community. We take our time to enjoy lunches and dinners together that use quality, humble ingredients, cooked well.”

“Me and Ahmed have had a shared love of food and hospitality which has meant that we can start this exciting new chapter as business partners with the same vision.”

More details about Filos will follow over the coming months.
