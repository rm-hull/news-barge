# 28 magical Leeds Christmas Market launch photos as festive fun returns for 2026

## We've been at the special launch of Leeds Christmas Market 2026

Leeds Christmas Market’s 2026 edition is spreading lots of festive cheer across the city.

We saw this year’s market launch on Thursday, November 19. The popular Leeds city centre attraction is back ‘bigger and brighter’ than before - it has a new operator for 2026 in the form of the team behind Thor’s Tipi.

Leeds Christmas Market 2026 is based in City Square. Leeds Christmas Market will run through to Wednesday, December 23. We have been bringing you everything you need to know about Leeds Christmas Market 2026. This includes times, dates and what to expect.

In City Square, you will find 40 stalls forming Leeds Christmas Market as well as live entertainment and lots of festive fun - we have previously brought you a sneak peek of everything coming together. Alongside the market, you will find lots of family fun dotted around Leeds city centre.

This includes the return of the ice rink Ice Cube in Millennium Square. You can take a scroll below to see the magical first day Leeds Christmas Market 2026 photos.
