French women will symbolically start working for free from 10:51 am on November 19, according to calculations by feminist newsletter 'Les Glorieuses'.

The annual campaign uses the gender pay gap to estimate when women effectively stop being paid compared with men for the rest of the year. It does not mean women literally stop receiving their salaries.

Rebecca Amsellem, economist and founder of Les Glorieuses, says the initiative has been highlighting the issue for a decade.

According to the organisation, women in France earn 21.8% less than men across all sectors and working arrangements.

One reason is that women are more likely to work part-time to look after children or elderly relatives.
