## Two protesters threw what appeared to be ashes at EU Health Commissioner during a cancer summit in Brussels, prompting a strong condemnation from von der Leyen.

EU Health Commissioner Olivér Várhelyi was targeted by protesters at a cancer summit in Brussels on Thursday, as campaigners voiced opposition to proposed changes to EU pesticide regulations.

Várhelyi was about to deliver a speech at the European Cancer Summit on the EU's efforts to tackle the disease when two protesters climbed onto the stage and threw a grey, dusty substance at him.

"These are the ashes of the people you will kill with the pesticides," one of the protesters can be heard saying in the video.

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen strongly condemned the incident.

"This behaviour is unacceptable in a free and democratic society. Everyone is free to express their opinions including on EU policies. But there can be no place for physical attacks and violence," she said in a statement.

In a press release, French activist group Les Soulèvements de la Terre said the protest was organised by four organisations and that four people were arrested.

A separate demonstration was also held outside the summit venue, where farmers and cancer patients attempted to join protesters but were pushed back by Belgian police, according to the group.

The protest comes amid criticism of the European Commission's proposed changes to EU pesticide regulations, part of its so-called "omnibus X" package.

The package aims to simplify existing rules and reduce administrative burdens, but environmental and health campaigners warn that the proposed changes could weaken protections for public health and the environment.
