One of the first things GTA 6 players searched for was strip club locations—genuinely, data doesn't lie. So, as a responsible guide writer, I loaded up the game and proceeded to spend my initial hours beelining around Leonida's hotspots. There are a few different strip clubs in Leonida, each with their own price range and different activities to try (that sounds sordid, but I'm also talking about, like, pool tables).

There are three strip clubs in the game I've found, plus a load of other night spots, like the Rusty Anchor close to where Jason starts, that have drinking and other activities, too.

## NINE1NINE

![GTA 6 strip club locations - Nine1Nine](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/vzvn7dXkGGJHZyUSNdGAsQ-1200-80.png)

* **Activities**: Drinking, club dancing, private dances
* **Prices**: $300 for a private dance, $50 tip
* **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐

This fancy strip club in downtown Vice City is the classiest option you have available, but also the priciest. It's quite easy to get here, even from Leonida Keys where Jason starts—just drive along route 1 towards Hamlet, merge onto route 97, and head north into Downtown. The clubs are just to the right of the giant crossroads route interchange in west Vice City. NINE1NINE itself is next door to The Vault club. This is the best option if you have a lot of money to burn, but also it feels a bit *too* fancy.

If you want a private dance, same as with previous GTA games, you just need to greet the dancer in question and then use the interaction icon to pay for it. Since this club is fancier, you can also get a private dance by interacting with the person by the entrance to the private rooms.

![GTA 6 strip club locations - Nine1Nine](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/h9MevpH3XRutQPqnK8ALxP-1200-80.jpg)

I appreciated NINE1NINE's lively club atmosphere, but I also ran out of cash very fast. Maybe one to try later in the game when you're a lot richer.

## Delights

![GTA 6 strip club locations - Delights](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/dB37GM7P37isWjuZf74LqQ-1200-80.png)

* **Activities**: Drinking, private dances, stage dances, pool
* **Prices**: $40 for a private dance, $10 tip, $5 stage tip
* **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Delights in Port Gellhorn is your cheapest option and it's a bit more rough and ready relative to NINE1NINE. It's also a long drive if you're just starting the game and want to visit the club in the northwest of the map. You'll want to drive to Hamlet via route 1 from the Keys, as with NINE1NINE, but then head northwest on route 82 towards Capri, then 103 merging onto the 404, past the airport towards Port Gellhorn. Bank a right when you reach the town, and the big Delights billboard to find it a little way further, on your left.

![GTA 6 strip club locations - Delights](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/xyT7o82UvMtNTUfcmHoNxP-1200-80.jpg)

This club has stage dancing, unlike NINE1NINE, pool tables, but also cheaper private dances, tips, and drinks. Hilariously, some of the dancers also already know Jason and will actually make comments about him. I went here early in the game and one of the dancers mentioned Jason and Lucia, so I guess he must be a regular. In general, this club has a lot more character than the others, it's reasonably priced, also you can play pool—four stars. I also witnessed an old woman on a mobility scooter mugging someone outside, which gave added entertainment value.
