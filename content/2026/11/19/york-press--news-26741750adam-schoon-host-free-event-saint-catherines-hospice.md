Following the success of last month's auction valuation event, Hansons Auctioneers will return to Saint Catherine's Hospice.

The free event will be held on Friday, November 27, from 10am to 12pm in the hospice's education area.

Adam Schoon, known for his appearances on the Antiques Roadshow, will be the valuer at the event.

Attendees are invited to bring a variety of items for valuation, including gold, silver, jewellery, watches, textiles, antiques, and collectables.

No appointment is necessary, with visitors welcome to drop in between 10am and 12pm.

Items deemed suitable for auction can be consigned on the day, with Hansons providing full guidance on estimates, costs, and potential prices.

For those with more than 25 items or objects too large to bring, Hansons also offers free home visit valuations.

Those interested in more information about Hansons can email [enquiries@hansonsharrogate.co.uk](mailto:enquiries@hansonsharrogate.co.uk) or call 01423 368189.
