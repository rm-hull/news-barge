Knaresborough Young Farmers Club is taking bookings for its January 2027 Christmas tree collection.

Members will collect trees in Harrogate on Saturday 9th January, followed by Knaresborough and the surrounding villages on Sunday 10th January.

The villages listed on the booking form include Farnham, Ferrensby, Follifoot, Goldsborough, Kirk Deighton, Lingerfield, Little Ribston, North Deighton, Scotton and Spofforth.

Residents can book through the club’s online Christmas tree collection form.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/693a6de658b47.jpg)

They’ll need to give their address, contact details and the number of trees they’d like collected.

The club will send confirmation and payment instructions by email.
