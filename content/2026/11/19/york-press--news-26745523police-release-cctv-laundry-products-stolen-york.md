*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Police have released CCTV images as part of a theft investigation.

The incident took place in York at Home Bargains, where laundry products were reported stolen at 3.30pm on October 10.




A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We’ve issued this CCTV of a person we would like to speak to following a theft in York.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance."

The force can be reached via their website or by calling 101.

Please quote reference 12260199466 when providing information.
