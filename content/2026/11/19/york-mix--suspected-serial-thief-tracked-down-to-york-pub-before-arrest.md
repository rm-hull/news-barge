A man has been arrested by police investigating a series of shop thefts in York.

At 10.51pm on Tuesday (17 November), police received a report of a theft at the Sainsbury’s store on Bootham.

Staff identified a man who police suspected of being involved in a number of previous thefts.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sainsburys-local-bootham-york-2024-gsv.webp)

The suspect is alleged to have picked up a quantity of alcohol before leaving the store without paying.

He was later located by CCTV operators at the Museum Street Tavern.

Officers went to the area and made enquiries before subsequently locating the suspect as he cycled towards Union Terrace.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Further investigation found that the man is also suspected of entering the Sainsbury’s store on Burton Stone Lane on Friday 6 October and stealing a number of items.

“He is further suspected of returning to the store on three separate occasions over the following days and stealing additional stock.

“Following enquiries, a 31-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of theft from a shop. He remains in police custody while our investigation continues.”
