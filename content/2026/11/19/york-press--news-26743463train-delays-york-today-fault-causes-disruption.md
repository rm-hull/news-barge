COMMUTERS have been warned of severe delays due to a fault on a train in York.

TransPennine Express said that train services running to and from York may be cancelled or delayed this morning (Thursday, November 19) until 12pm.




A spokesperson for the train operator said: “Due to a fault with a train in the York area, trains are being delayed in both directions.

“While work is being carried out to resolve the issue, the following routes may be delayed or cancelled in both directions:

* Manchester Airport and Redcar Central.
* Manchester Victoria and Scarborough.
* Liverpool Lime Street and Newcastle.
* Manchester Piccadilly and York.

“You should continue to travel as planned; however, your journey may be delayed.

“We are sorry for the delay to your journey today.”

One person, whose train to York from Mirfield has been delayed by almost an hour, told The Press: “Unfortunately train delays have meant that I am late for work and have been delayed getting into York by an hour.

“We have been stuck outside of the station for over 40 minutes, with little communication or updates on the service.

“We’ve finally been told an LNER train has broken down nearby and that there is massive congestion getting into York.”

Customers can use tickets at no extra cost on the LNER services between York, Newcastle and Edinburgh in both directions.
