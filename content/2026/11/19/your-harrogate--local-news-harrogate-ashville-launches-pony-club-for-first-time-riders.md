Ashville Prep School in Harrogate has launched a Pony Club for pupils aged five to 11.

The club is aimed at beginners and combines sessions at school with visits to Follifoot Park Riding Centre.

Pupils will learn how to groom and handle ponies, look after a stable and care for riding equipment. They’ll also have the chance to ride.

Suzy Styles, a Classics and Latin teacher at Ashville Senior School, leads the club and has more than 25 years’ experience with horses.

The programme follows British Horse Society accredited learning, and each pupil will receive an activity book to record their progress.

Rhiannon Wilkinson, Head of Ashville, said:

> “We’re thrilled to add this new club to the extraordinary number of co-curricular activities and sports we already offer.
>
> "As well as increased self-confidence and fitness, riding offers young people a sense of responsibility as well as a discipline that serves them well for life.
>
> “We look forward to our younger pupils experiencing the benefits of spending time with horses and making new equine friends.
>
> "Hopefully they will follow in the successful footprints, and hoofprints, of our senior school riders in the years to come.”

The launch follows two top-five finishes for Ashville Senior School riders at the Horse of the Year Show at the NEC in Birmingham.

Christina Cotton rode a double clear on Jet Set des Champs, known as Pepper, to finish fourth in the Foxhunter class.

Poppy Pollard finished fifth on her debut at the show, riding her cob Fair View Barney Bear, known as Charlie, in the Search for a Star working pony class.

She’s now aiming to qualify for the 2027 season.
