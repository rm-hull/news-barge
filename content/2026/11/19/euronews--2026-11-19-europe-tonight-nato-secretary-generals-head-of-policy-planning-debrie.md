## Euronews' evening show Europe Tonight welcomed Fabrice Pothier, head of policy planning in the private office of the Secretary General of NATO, he also oversees the review of current and future strategic policy issues facing NATO.

US President Donald Trump, Russian President Putin and Chinese leader Xi Jinping issued what they describe as a ‘joint declaration on peace’ after historical trilateral talks in China, saying nuclear wars must never be fought. The Russian President also says he has invited European envoys to Moscow to clarify their position ahead of peace negotiations.

To discuss the matter Euronews' evening show Europe Tonight welcomed Fabrice Pothier, head of policy planning in the private office of the Secretary General of NATO, he also oversees the review of current and future strategic policy issues facing NATO.
