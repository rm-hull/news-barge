# A66 fatal crash: Further charges in connection with activity prior

## Further charges have been made in connection with activity prior to the fatal A66 crash in August, police said.

Two men, aged 21 and 45, were arrested on Tuesday November 17.

A 16-year-old boy was arrested on Wednesday November 18, police said.

All three were taken to police custody for questioning and have since been charged.

The teenager has been charged with conspiracy to cause criminal damage, criminal damage intentionally/recklessly endangering life and burglary with intent to cause grievous bodily harm.

The 21-year-old has been charged with conspiracy to cause criminal damage, conspiracy to steal, criminal damage intentionally/recklessly endangering life and burglary with intent to cause grievous bodily harm.

The 45-year-old has been charged with burglary with intent to cause grievous bodily harm.

Since then, they have all been remanded in custody to appear at Teesside Magistrates Court on Thursday November 19.

The charges relate to two vehicles – a Volvo and Land Rover – suspected of being used to destroy or to cause damage to houses and vehicles in Middlesbrough including a property on Ruskin Avenue on Saturday August 22.

Another man, aged 19, was charged on Wednesday November 11 and has been remanded in custody.

His next appearance will be at Teesside Crown Court on Thursday December 10.
