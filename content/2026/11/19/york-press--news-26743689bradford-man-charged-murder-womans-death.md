## Name of man charged with murder after death of woman and another seriously injured

CSI at the address on Carr Bottom Road. *(Image: T&A)*

A man has been named and charged with murder following the death of a woman in Yorkshire.

Police was called to an address on Carr Bottom Road, Bankfoot, at about 1.40am on Tuesday after allegations that a man had seriously assaulted two women, as reported by our sister title The Telegraph & Argus.

Emergency services found two injured women, aged 46 and 20.

The older woman was pronounced dead at the scene.

![Floral tributes have been left](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21732895.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes *(Image: Newsquest)*

The 20-year-old was taken to hospital where in a critical condition.

A man was later arrested elsewhere in Bradford.

## Man charged

Bilal Ajmal, 26, of Carr Bottom Road, Bankfoot, has now been charged with the murder of Tahira Hussain, 46, and the attempted murder of a 20-year-old woman.

He is due to appear before Bradford Magistrates' Court this morning.

Police say the 26-year-old self defines as Asian Pakistani including Asian British.

![Police van](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21729147.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police van at the scene *(Image: Newsquest)*

## Update on investigation

Enquiries remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team into the incident.

The 20-year-old woman continues to be treated in hospital after suffering serious injuries.

West Yorkshire Police said specialist officers were supporting the victim's family.

Detective Chief Inspector Michael Cox, who is leading the investigation, said: "We would like to speak to anyone who has information or footage which could assist the investigation or who saw suspicious behaviour on Carr Bottom Road at about 1.40am."

Anyone with information, CCTV, dashcam or mobile phone footage that could assist the investigation is asked to contact police quoting the crime reference 13260652937.
