Ofsted has praised a Harrogate nursery for the support it gives children as they settle in.

Partou Playaway Day Nursery and Pre-school, just off Skipton Road, met safeguarding requirements and achieved the ‘expected standard’ in all six areas assessed, including inclusion, curriculum and teaching, and children’s welfare and wellbeing.

The inspector wrote:

> “Leaders and staff have embedded a culture of inclusion across all aspects of the nursery.
>
> "They recognise that barriers to children's learning can take a variety of forms and emerge at different times.”

The report highlighted the care staff take when planning changes for children with special educational needs and/or disabilities.

It also praised the way staff and families share information at handovers, helping children settle quickly, including those who have only recently joined.

The inspector wrote:

> “All staff know each child well.
>
> "This helps children to feel emotionally secure.”

Nursery manager Joanna Waller said:

> “We are absolutely delighted with our Ofsted report and so pleased that it reflects what makes Playaway so special.
>
> "It captures the warm, welcoming and inclusive environment we strive to create every day, where children and families feel valued, supported and truly part of our nursery family.”

Sam Sleeman-Boss, Partou’s Head of Early Years, said:

> “I am particularly pleased to see the culture of inclusion highlighted with tailored support for children who speak English as an additional language deemed to be particularly effective.”
