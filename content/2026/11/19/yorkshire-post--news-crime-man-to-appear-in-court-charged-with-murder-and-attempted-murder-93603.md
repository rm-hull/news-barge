# Man to appear in court charged with murder and attempted murder

## A man will appear before magistrates on Thursday (Nov 19) charged with murder and attempted murder.

Bilal Ajmal, 26, of Carr Bottom Road , Bradford, will appear before the morning session of Bradford Magistrates' Court charged with the murder of Tahira Hussain, 46.

The 26-year-old, who self defines as Asian Pakistani including Asian British, will also appear charged with the attempted murder of a 20-year-old woman.

Enquiries remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team into an incident at a house in Carr Bottom Road, Little Horton, on Tuesday November 17 in which both women were attacked.

The 20-year-old woman continues to be treated in hospital after suffering serious injuries.
