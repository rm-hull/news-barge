## Four people taken to hospital after crash closes A-road

Four people have been taken to hospital after a crash in North Yorkshire. *(Image: Public submission)*

Four people were taken to hospital after a “serious” crash closed the A66 in North Yorkshire overnight.

Emergency services were called to the crash near Melsonby on Wednesday evening (November 18).

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) said it received an emergency call at 6.33pm and sent four ambulances and four rapid response vehicles to the scene.

A spokesperson said: “We received an emergency call at 6.33pm yesterday to a road traffic collision on the A66.

“A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including four ambulances and four rapid response vehicles, and four patients were conveyed to hospital.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21738041.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Four people were taken to hospital *(Image: GOOGLE MAPS)*

North Yorkshire Police described the crash as a "serious road traffic collision" and confirmed the carriageway was closed between Melsonby and the Gilling West junction.

Drivers faced delays of around 30 minutes on the approach to the crash, with around two miles of congestion reported.

The road remained closed while investigations were carried out before reopening at around 5am this morning (Thursday, November 19).
