The North Yorkshire coast is set to welcome a royal visit next summer.

Global musical phenomenon SIX are embarking on their first-ever outdoor tour – as part of its 10th anniversary celebrations, including four performances at **Scarborough Open Air Theatre** from**Friday 4 to Sunday 6 June 2027**.

From Tudor Queens to Pop Princesses, the six wives of Henry VIII take to the mic to remix 500 years of historical heartbreak into an electrifying 80-minute celebration of 21st-century girl power.

Tickets will go on sale at 12pm on Thursday 26 November. An exclusive 24-hour presale is available from 12pm Wednesday 25 November by signing up here.

Casting is yet to be announced but it will feature a powerhouse cast of all-star Queens drawn from SIX productions around the world, together with a big band of 16 musicians.

Presented by Kenny Wax and Cuffe and Taylor, SIX OUTDOOR will also visit Lincoln Castle, Cardiff Castle, Delamere Forest, Westonbirt Arboretum, Bedford Park, Margate Dreamland, Blenheim Palace and Henry VIII’s favourite royal residence Hampton Court Palace.

Peter Taylor, co-founder of Cuffe & Taylor, added: “We’re thrilled to be taking SIX on its first-ever outdoor tour. Pairing this phenomenal show with some of the UK’s most stunning historic and natural settings, plus a 16-piece band, makes for an unmissable summer night out.

“This is exactly what Scarborough Open Air Theatre was built for back in the 1930s, so we are thrilled to be kicking off this run of dates in this incredible venue.

“Expect a history lesson with SIX but above all else, prepare for a fast-paced pop concert because this is a musical like no other.”
