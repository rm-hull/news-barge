Clean, working toys, games, books and puzzles can be left at the Wetherby Road, Penny Pot Lane and Ripon tips until 29 November for families who need them.

Unwanted toys can be dropped off at the Harrogate district’s three tips until
**Sunday 29 November**, to be given to local children this Christmas.

The Reuse Santa appeal is in its eighth year. It is run by Yorwaste with North
Yorkshire Council, City of York Council and local charities. Last year more than
**5,000 toys** were passed on, which the council puts at around 80 giant Santa
sacks (North Yorkshire Council, 12 November).

## Where to take them

Donations are taken at every household waste recycling centre in North Yorkshire and York from 15 to 29 November. In the Harrogate district that means:

| Tip | Address | Winter hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harrogate | Wetherby Road, HG3 1DE | 8.30am to 4pm |
| West Harrogate | Penny Pot Lane, HG3 2SN | 8.30am to 4pm |
| Ripon | Dallamires Crescent, off Dallamires Lane, HG4 1TT | 8.30am to 4pm |

All three are **closed on Wednesdays**, so the last Wednesday of the appeal,
25 November, is no use. Our Harrogate tip opening times
page has the full detail for each site.

## What they will take

The council asks for toys, games, books and puzzles that are clean, complete and working. It cannot accept toys powered by mains electricity.

Cllr Richard Foster, the council’s executive member for waste services, said: “All we ask is that the gifts are in a condition that you would like to receive.”

He added: “Christmas can be a time when finances are tight, so the aim of the scheme is to relieve some of the burden.”

## Where the toys go

The toys are shared out through the council’s Early Help service and partners including:

* the British Heart Foundation
* the Daisy Chain Project
* The Recycle Project CIC
* Colburn Community Hub
* community groups in the City of York Good Place Network

Some items may be passed to charity shops, the council said.

## What it means for you

If you are clearing a cupboard before Christmas, a working board game or a complete jigsaw can go to a family instead of a skip. Take it to one of the three tips before the appeal closes on 29 November, and avoid a Wednesday.
