**A family have said they are "completely devastated", after their 23-year-old daughter was killed in a crash in Leeds.**

Jade Langton and Cameron Bell, from York, had been travelling along Gelderd Road when a blue BMW 123D, that had failed to stop for police, crashed into them at 00:44 GMT on Sunday.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed Cameron, 22, died at the scene and his passenger, Jade, died in hospital shortly after.

In a statement, Jade's family said: "Whilst she always loved a bit of drama, Jade did not deserve to have her life cut short in this way."

They added: "We are completely devastated by the loss of our beautiful Jade. We are struggling to come to terms with what has happened and the reality that she has been taken from us far too soon.

"Jade had a big personality and loved being with people, chatting, giggling, going to live music, traveling and other adventures. She left an impact wherever she was."

The family also shared their "deepest condolences" with Cameron's family and friends, and thanked emergency services who "did everything they could to help".

"There is a big hole in our family and she is already hugely missed," the statement read.

"The kindness, support and condolences we have received from family, friends and the wider community is appreciated.

"As we try to navigate this unimaginable loss, we respectfully ask people to be mindful about what they share and comment on social media. We kindly ask that our family's privacy is respected while we grieve together and try to come to terms with losing our Jade."

Leigh Ross, from Harrogate, appeared on Monday at Leeds Magistrates' Court, in connection with the crash.

The 39-year-old, of Prospect Place, has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while uninsured, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while disqualified and two counts of causing death having taken a vehicle without consent.

He is due to appear at Leeds Crown Court on 17 December.
