## York fashion students impress with innovative designs at awards

A centuries-old York livery company is supporting creative talent through a new award.

The York Company of Cordwainers recently presented its Rising Talent Award at the annual St Crispin’s Feast.

The award recognises outstanding students from York College who have shown exceptional creativity and skill as part of their fashion and design studies.

Master of the Corwainers, Joy Cullwick, presented the award to first-year Fashion Design and Garment Technology student Carrie Slater.

One of her innovative designs, on the theme of "Transitions," was a reversible butterfly dress inspired by York Museum Gardens.

Her collection also included a dress with discreet zips to provide easier access for medical treatments such as chemotherapy and dialysis.

Highly Commended was third-year student Lauren Harper, whose portfolio featured a festival-inspired outfit of leather hot pants and top, showcasing her creativity and skill in working with leather.

The garments were displayed at Bedern Hall, giving guests an opportunity to see the talent emerging from York College.

The Cordwainers thanked tutor Lynn Wood and York College for their commitment to supporting and developing young designers.

Congratulating both students, the company said it looked forward to following their future creative journeys and seeing their talents continue to flourish.
