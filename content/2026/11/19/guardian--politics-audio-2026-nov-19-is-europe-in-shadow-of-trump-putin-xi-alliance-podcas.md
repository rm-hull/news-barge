# Is Europe now in the shadow of a Trump, Putin and Xi alliance? – podcast

Andy Burnham is meeting western leaders in Berlin and Brussels this week, to discuss Ukraine and closer ties to the EU. Meanwhile, Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping have met at the Apec summit in China. Is this the start of a global shift of power away from Europe, or was it just a hyped-up photo opportunity?
