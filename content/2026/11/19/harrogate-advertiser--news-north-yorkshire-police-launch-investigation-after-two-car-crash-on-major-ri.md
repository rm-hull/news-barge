North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following a collision involving two cars on a major road in Ripon.

The incident happened at around 6.20pm on Monday (November 16) on the B6265 Boroughbridge Road near Bridge Hewick.

It involved a red Vauxhall car travelling towards Ripon and a silver Volkswagen Passat car travelling in the opposite direction.

Occupants from both vehicles were taken to hospital by ambulance after the collision and have since been discharged following treatment.

The road was closed for five hours while officers investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dash cam footage, to contact us.”

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260225456 when providing any details regarding the incident.
