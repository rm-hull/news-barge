Leeds council leaders have expressed solidarity after a colleague in Bradford was seriously injured in a stabbing.

Bradford Moor councillor Ibrar Ali was taken to hospital following the incident on Manse Street in the city just after 11am, it is understood.

West Yorkshire Police said a 32-year-old man had since been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder.

A suspect reportedly climbed onto the roof of a property on Thornbury Avenue following the incident.

Leeds City Council leader James Lewis said: “I was shocked and deeply saddened to hear of the attack on Bradford councillor Ibrar Ali.

“Our thoughts are with him, his family, friends and colleagues at this very difficult time, and we wish him a full and speedy recovery.

“Councillors of all parties play a vital role in serving their communities and should be able to carry out their duties without fear of violence or intimidation.

“We stand in solidarity with our colleagues in Bradford and thank the emergency services for their response.”

Police confirmed a man aged 52 had been taken to hospital with serious injuries.

Crime scene cordons were in place on Manse Street, Leeds Road and Thornbury Avenue earlier and armed officers were deployed.

A police spokesperson said: “At 11.04am officers were called to Manse Street, Bradford to reports a man had been stabbed.

“The man aged 52 has been taken to hospital with serious injuries.

“Several scenes are in place on Manse Street, Leeds Road and Thornbury Avenue.

“A man aged 32, has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder. Enquiries are ongoing by Bradford CID.”

Chief Superintendent Richard Padwell, Bradford district commander, appealed for witnesses to come forward.

He said: "This is a serious incident where a local councillor has been stabbed in the street and our thoughts are with him, his family and colleagues at this difficult time.

“Whilst the investigation is in the early stages, there is nothing to suggest that this incident is politically motivated at this time.

“We understand this will no doubt cause some concern in the community. Local officers are in the area and we are continuing to liaise with our partners and communities affected."

Any one with information was urged to contact police via 101, quoting crime reference 13260657899.
