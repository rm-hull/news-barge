# Trump greets 'my friend' Putin as pair shake hands

Donald Trump has called Vladimir Putin his "friend" ahead of a meeting with China's President Xi in Shenzhen.

The pair were seen sharing a friendly handshake, with the US president saying they have a "great relationship".

According to Putin's top foreign policy aide Yuri Ushakov, the war in Ukraine was the main topic of the meeting, which lasted 35 to 40 minutes.

The two leaders also discussed US-Russia bilateral relations.
