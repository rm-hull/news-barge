A 20-year-old man has been taken to hospital following a serious crash in York.

The incident took place on the junction of James Street and Lawrence Street at around 6.14pm on Wednesday (November 18).

It involved a cyclist and a Land Rover, North Yorkshire Police confirmed.




A spokesperson said: “The cyclist, a 20-year-old man, sustained several injuries following the collision and is currently receiving medical treatment in hospital.

“The road remains closed while we continue to investigate the scene.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the Land Rover before or immediately after the incident or anyone who has any relevant dashcam or doorbell footage.

“If you can help, please contact us using one of the following methods:

• Email: [SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk)

• Online: Visit our website, select 'Tell us about', then choose 'An existing case or report'.

• Live Chat: Available on our website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

“Please quote reference 12260226802 when providing information.”
