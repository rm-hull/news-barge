A market town in Yorkshire has been named one of the happiest places to live in Great Britain.

Rightmove has released its annual list of the happiest places to live around Great Britain with Skipton making it into the top 10.

Skipton came in seventh place with residents saying, "they feel proud to live there".

Skipton’s high rank is also thanks to its great access to nature and green spaces and public transport links.

## What is Skipton like?

Skipton has a great mix of green spaces as well as lots of shops, restaurants, and cafés.

If you need to get out into some fresh air, you can visit Aireville Park, Middletown Recreation Ground, and Skipton Castle Woods.

Skipton is also close to the Yorkshire Dales meaning it is very easy to take a trip to the national park for a long hike.

If you love history, it is worth visiting Skipton Castle to enjoy the building’s impressive interiors and learn more about the town’s past.

The town is also home to a famous market that runs for four days a week with a range of independent stalls selling different homemade gifts, art, and food.

The high street is also packed with both independent and national shops that are worth a visit.

After a long day walking around the town, you might want to stop off at Phoebe’s for a meal.

The casual restaurant offers brunch and dinner with a range of dishes on offer from French toast and homemade granola bowls to harissa chicken and mushroom rigatoni.

If you need a relaxing coffee spot, you can visit the Clubhouse for a coffee and a slice of cake.

The café has a rating of 4.8 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with over 40 people leaving reviews saying the coffee is "excellent".

If you want to live in Skipton, the average price of a property in the area over the last year was £301,616, according to Rightmove.

The majority of properties sold in the area were terraced houses for around £210,726.

Semi-detached properties sold for an average of £346,392 while detached properties went for around £636,591.
