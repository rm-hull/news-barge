According to the police, starting today there will be "extensive road closures" throughout the government district. Subway service will also be affected: The U5 will not stop at the Bundestag subway station at certain times.

File photo

Due to international conferences attended by numerous heads of state and government, people in Berlin should expect traffic disruptions.

Boat tours in the government district are also not possible. In addition, Berlin’s airspace is closed, according to a police statement. The arrival and departure of state guests will cause further disruptions, officials said. This also applies to the access roads to the capital’s BER airport in Schönefeld. According to the Brandenburg police, significant traffic disruptions are expected in some areas. They advised air travelers to pay attention to traffic updates and, if necessary, to allow extra time for their trip to Berlin-Brandenburg Airport.

Chancellor Friedrich Merz (CDU) has invited members of the “Coalition of the Willing” - a group of Ukraine supporters - to a meeting. On Friday, a major German-Ukrainian economic forum will begin in Berlin-Mitte. According to the Traffic Information Center, further disruptions may occur in the City West area around Budapester Straße due to the 72nd annual session of the NATO Parliamentary Assembly. The police are providing security for the international conferences, which include participants at risk, with approximately 2,500 additional officers today alone.
