Deciding whether to kill or spare Arnold in GTA 6 is the culmination of the Hey Arnold mission in Chapter 1, the 7th after you encounter Mandy during Pipework.

Essentially, Arnold, Brian's business partner, has gone dark, so you're sent to rough him up. Arnold and his cronies will do a runner, hopping into a vehicle and speeding off. You'll need to chase them down and do a bit of high-speed shooting. This will lead to an on-foot chase, at the end of which you'll corner him on a pier, where he'll explain he wants to leave the criminal life behind and disappear. There are two choices and consequences:

* **Kill Arnold**: Gun him down and you'll get a Hardened Criminal rating in your Criminal Profile. This is essentially GTA 6's morality system, based around how often you kill people. Obviously, killing Brian will contribute significantly towards the negative.
* **Spare Arnold**: Let him go and this will give you a Restrained rating, contributing less negatively to your Criminal Profile. Brian will also show up in a mission later if you let him live, so this seems like the best choice of the two if you want extra content.

Funnily enough, if you choose neither and just wait, Arnold will leave, though this will result in the same outcome as if you decided to spare him. Besides this, there are no downsides to sparing him or advantages gained through killing him.
