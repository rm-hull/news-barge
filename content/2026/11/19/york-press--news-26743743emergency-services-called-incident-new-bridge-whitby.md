Emergency service have been called to an incident along a North Yorkshire bridge.

The incident took place on New Bridge in Whitby on Wednesday (November 18).

Coastguard teams from Whitby and Staithes, along with North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, Yorkshire Ambulance Service, North Yorkshire Police, and Whitby RNLI, were called to the area.

A spokesman for Whitby Coastguard Rescue Team said: "As part of the response, our team deployed Coastguard Rope Rescue Technicians to assist at the scene.

"We’re pleased to say the incident concluded with a positive outcome.

"Incidents like this show just how important it is for our emergency services to work together.

"Each agency brings its own specialist skills, training and experience, and last night was a great example of everyone working together to achieve the best possible outcome."

No further details about the nature of the incident have been released out of respect for those involved.
