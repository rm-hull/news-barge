A woman who reportedly inspired the Netflix drama *Baby Reindeer* is accused of harassing a lawyer with voicemails making derogatory comments about Jewish people.

Fiona Harvey, 61, is charged with racially aggravated harassment without violence against Mark Lewis from 4 September to 9 October last year.

In one of several voicemails left in a day, she referenced “Jewish media” and claimed she “has a lot of Jewish stalkers”, the prosecution told Highbury Corner Magistrates’ Court on Thursday.

Harvey, of Camden, north London, did not indicate pleas and elected her case be tried at a crown court.

Prosecutor Neda Naeijnoori said the defendant first contacted Mr Lewis around 14 years ago by calling and emailing his workplace, leading him to believe she was a potential client.

In September last year, Harvey allegedly contacted him about him representing a specific client.

![Fiona Harvey, 61, is charged with racially aggravated harassment without violence against Mark Lewis from 4 September to 9 October last year.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2024/05/09/21/TAizE3BG-720.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“He responded stating that he could not discuss his clients (and) stating he did not represent that individual,” the prosecutor said.

The following month, on 9 October, Harvey allegedly left a voicemail with her phone number saying she “requires that he calls her urgently”.

In other comments left on voicemail, “she references Jewish media and says that she has a lot of Jewish stalkers”, the prosecutor said.

That same day, she allegedly left a message “making derogatory comments about Jewish males and Jewish people”.

In a third voicemail sent that day, Harvey allegedly “went on a tangent of bigotry comments about him and the Jewish faith”.

Mr Lewis answered a fourth call and told her he was ending their conversation, Ms Naeijnoori said.

Asked if she elects a crown court trial, Harvey, wearing a large herringbone trench coat and grey tracksuit bottoms, said: “Yes, yes, absolutely.”

She remains on conditional bail and will next appear at Harrow Crown Court on 17 December.
