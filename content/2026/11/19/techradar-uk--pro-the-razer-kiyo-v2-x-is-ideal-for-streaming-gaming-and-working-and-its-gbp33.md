## Why you should buy the Razer Kiyo V2 X

The Kiyo V2 X features a 3.7MP sensor that captures 1440p video at a smooth 60fps. It has autofocus, and its wide-angle lens gives you loads of flexibility if you want to frame yourself or show more of your surroundings. Automatic low-light adjustment helps ensure good picture quality in dimmer environments.

Razer’s camera has automatic and manual face tracking, digital zoom, and a built-in microphone - so you’ll be ready to stream or join video calls right out of the box. Its adjustable mounting system attaches to different monitor types and offers 80 degrees of rotation.

## Should you buy it?

**✅ Buy the Razer Kiyo V2 X if…** you want an affordable webcam for streaming, gaming and video calls, with autofocus, 60fps capture and straightforward USB connectivity.**❌ Skip it if…** you need professional-grade image quality, 4K recording or a dedicated microphone for high-quality audio production.
