# TV rugby pundit cleared of sexually assaulting woman in cricket club toilet

## A former England rugby league player and TV pundit has been found not guilty of sexually assaulting a woman by putting her hand up her skirt in a cricket club toilet.

Danika Priim had been accused of reaching up the woman’s dress twice and grabbing her underwear at Horsforth Cricket Club in July 2022.

Two of the complainant’s friends said they also witnessed the alleged incident, with one saying Priim laughed and said “wahey” before walking out of the toilet.

On Thursday a jury of seven women and five men cleared her of sexual assault after deliberating for just more than two hours.

Priim, and her family and friends in the public gallery, sobbed as the verdict was announced.

During a three-day trial at Leeds Crown Court, the complainant told the jury she had washed her hands, put on some lipstick and was talking to two of her friends when Priim “walked straight up to me and put her hand up my skirt and grabbed hold of my pants and my private parts”.

The woman said: “I said: ‘What the f\*\*\* are you doing?’

“Then she did it again, grinning at me, and walked off.”

Two of the complainant’s friends told the jury they saw Priim put her hand up the woman’s skirt.

One of them said she exchanged text messages with her friend the following day in which she encouraged the woman to report what had happened, saying she would support her.

Priim told the jury she was never in the toilets with the three women and the sexual assault “categorically did not happen”.

Asked why she thought three women would have “got together to make completely false allegations”, Priim said: “It’s the biggest question I have – it’s the why.”

She told the court: “I’m also a very happy heterosexual woman. I’m with a man. I live with a man.”

Priim, 42, of Keighley, West Yorkshire, played for Bradford Bulls, Leeds Rhinos and represented England, including at the 2017 World Cup.

She retired from the game in 2021 and she told the jury she has appeared on BBC, Channel4 and Sky Sports as a pundit, reporter and commentator.
