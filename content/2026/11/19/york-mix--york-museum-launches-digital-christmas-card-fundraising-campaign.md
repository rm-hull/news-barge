York Castle Museum are bringing Christmas cheer in more ways this year, with a new fundraising campaign.

Christmas cards have become a staple of seasonal celebrations across the world after being popularised in Victorian England. York Castle Museum holds a large collection of 19th century Christmas cards in the collection; from cute and cheerful to downright strange and peculiar.

So the museum have launched a new Christmas card fundraising campaign designed to support access to the museum for more visitors across the festive period.

This year, York Castle Museum is bringing a 21st century twist by producing their own digital Christmas card campaign. The card campaign has two functions, spread Christmas cheer, whilst also supporting a York charity.

This Christmas, York Castle Museum are piloting a series of Relaxed Christmas Visits, designed to support visitors with additional needs or those looking for a quieter experience.

All donations made through the card campaign are helping York Castle Museum to fund accessible opening hours and give the gift of magical Christmas memories to even more people this year.

Dawn Clements, head of development at York Museums Trust said, “We are really excited to bring this card campaign to life this year and connect with other businesses in the area to spread a little festive joy and encourage support of the vital work we are doing here at York Museums Trust.

“This card acts as a fundraising tool to help support our charitable missions of caring for historic collections and spaces to protect Yorkshire’s heritage for the future, but also to make these spaces accessible to as many people as possible.

“We are inviting organisations across York and beyond to take that familiar Christmas tradition of sending cards and give it a new purpose.”

Businesses that support the campaign will receive a digital supporter graphic to share with clients, colleagues and followers.

Every donation in lieu will support Relaxed Christmas Visits at York Castle Museum and help develop more accessible and welcoming museum experiences for the future.

For more information, head to York Castle Museum’s website.
