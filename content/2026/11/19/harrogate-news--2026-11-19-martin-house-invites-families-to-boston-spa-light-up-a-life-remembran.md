People from across Boston Spa, Wetherby and surrounding areas are being invited to join Martin House Children’s Hospice for its Light up a Life event this December.

The service will take place at **St Mary’s Church, High Street, Boston Spa, on Tuesday 1 December at 7pm.**

Light up a Life gives families the opportunity to come together at the end of the year to remember children who have died. The event is also open to anyone wishing to remember a loved one.

> **Saarah Hamayun, Spiritual Care Practitioner at Martin House, said:**
>
> At the darkest and also busiest time of year, Light up a Life gives people the space and time to remember their child, but also provides a shared moment of remembrance, where we can reflect on and celebrate their lives.

The service will include music, readings, poems and the lighting of candles in remembrance of loved ones. Refreshments will be available afterwards.

**The event is free to attend and there is no need to book.**

Martin House provides specialist palliative care for children and young people with life-shortening conditions across West, North and East Yorkshire. Its services include respite stays, symptom control, emergency care, end-of-life care and support following a death.

The hospice also provides bereavement support to families, including those whose child did not have the opportunity to use its services.

Alongside the church service, Martin House has an online book of dedication where people can leave a message and upload a photograph of their loved one.

It is available at www.martinhouse.org.uk/lightupalife.

For further information about the event, contact Saarah Hamayun at [shamayun@martinhouse.org.uk.](mailto:shamayun@martinhouse.org.uk.)
