# The Yorkshire rail route revealed as one of the busiest in the UK

## A Yorkshire rail route has been named as one of the most-used in Britain outside of London.

The Office of Rail and Road (ORR) released new annual statistics which showed the busiest stations and most common journeys nationwide.

The journey between Leeds and York was one of the most completed outside of the capital, according to The ORR.

Their data for 'flows' (journeys in either direction between two places) placed Leeds to York as the ninth busiest in the UK, outside of London.

The journey in either direction was completed 1.3 million times between April 2025 and March 2026.

Journeys between Leeds and York are run by Northern Rail, Transpennine Express and LNER and typically take between 20 and 30 minutes.

Routes from Birmingham New Street dominated the list outside of London, with Edinburgh to Glasgow and Manchester Piccadilly to Manchester Airport also featuring.

The ORR said the busiest 'flow' in Britain was between London Liverpool Street and Tottenham Court Road, which was completed 8.2 million times.

London Liverpool Street also retained its title of Britain’s busiest railway station for the fourth year in a row.

![Passengers wait on the concourse at Liverpool Street station in London](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/11/cs-b4a00fd1-f2fb-4188-808b-efad16231978.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Leeds station, which recently revealed its £46m renovation of the area outside its entrance, was the 2nd busiest outside of London, with 28 million entries and exits between April 2025 and March 2026.

A total of 1,829 million passenger rail journeys were made between April last year and March 2026, an increase of 6% from the previous year, the ORR said.
