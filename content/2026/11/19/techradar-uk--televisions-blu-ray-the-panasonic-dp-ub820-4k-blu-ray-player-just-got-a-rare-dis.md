## A true workhorse

![A Panasonic DP-UB820 4K Blu-ray player, with its disc tray open, and copy of The Batman on 4K Blu-ray disc in the tray](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/H5bwamFwsi6bthiUr3JEFT-1200-80.jpg)

The reason the Panasonic DP-UB820 sits at the top of our best 4K Blu-ray player list, and has done for years, is not only because of its fantastic performance, features and value for money, but because of its longevity.

When I worked at an AV retailer before joining TechRadar, the UB820 was the player we used every day to display content on the display TVs — and it never showed signs of giving up the ghost, despite running eight hours a day, seven days a week.

The UB820 is our reference 4K Blu-ray player here at TechRadar as well, used to play discs when testing the best TVs, and to test the latest 4K releases as part of our Blu-ray Bounty feature.

When I first reviewed the UB820 in 2023, I knew it would get five stars, as there is quite simply no better value 4K Blu-ray player on the market.

The UB820 supports both Dolby Vision and HDR10+ HDR formats, Dolby Atmos and DTS:X formats, and up to 7.1 channels of audio via an analogue output, and can play back a host of different physical media formats.

Its picture reproduction is superb, rendering crisp textures, vibrant colors, rich contrast and even demonstrating great upscaling of standard Blu-ray and DVD.
