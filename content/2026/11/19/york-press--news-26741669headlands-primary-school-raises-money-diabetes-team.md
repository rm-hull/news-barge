## York school rallies behind six-year-old with type one diabetes

Headlands Primary School has raised more than £680 in support of a hospital team caring for six-year-old Ayda with type one diabetes *(Image: YORK AND SCARBOROUGH TEACHING HOSPITALS NHS FOUNDATION)*

A school has raised more than £680 in support of a hospital team caring for a young pupil with type one diabetes.

Six-year-old Ayda, who was diagnosed earlier this year, and her family raised £681.43 for York Hospital’s Paediatric Diabetes Team to thank them for their care and support.

Headlands Primary School marked World Diabetes Day by encouraging pupils and staff to wear blue instead of their usual uniform.

Ayda’s siblings, Ella and George, also organised a bake sale after school with cakes donated by Totally Caked.

Ayda’s mother, Laura, said: "When Ayda was diagnosed, we knew very little about type 1 diabetes and suddenly had so much to learn.

"The team at York Hospital have been amazing, and we wanted to give something back to the people who have supported us so much."

The combined fundraising efforts raised the money for York & Scarborough Hospitals Charity, which supports the Paediatric Diabetes Team.

Becky White, Community Fundraiser at York & Scarborough Hospitals Charity, said: "It’s been wonderful to see Ayda’s family and school community come together to support the team that has been there for them since her diagnosis.

"We’re incredibly grateful to everyone who took part, donated, or supported the fundraising."
