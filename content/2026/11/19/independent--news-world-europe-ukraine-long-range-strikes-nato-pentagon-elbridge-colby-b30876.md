The Pentagon’s chief strategist walked out of a Nato meeting in Brussels after rebuking Ukraine over its long-range strikes inside Russia, according to a report*.* The*Telegraph* reported that Elbridge Colby, the US under secretary of defence for policy, told the Ukrainian Defence Contact Group (UDCG) on Tuesday that Kyiv’s deep-strike campaign lacked military value and had failed to blunt Russian advances on the front line.

Mr Colby reportedly walked out of the room immediately after delivering his remarks, departing before Ukrainian defence minister Yevhenii Khmara could present a classified battlefield briefing.

The incident has sparked concerns regarding Washington’s stance toward Ukraine, with German sources telling the newspaper that Mr Colby’s behaviour suggests that the Trump administration has, in effect, sided with the Kremlin against Europe.

![Mr Colby walked out after insisting that Kyiv's deep-strike campaign has little military value](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/21/2026-11-17T161332Z_340965551_RC2RPOACHYAU_RTRMADP_3_UKRAINE-CRISIS-NATO.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The rebuke follows an intensified campaign of long-range strikes targeting Russian arms factories and oil infrastructure, where Ukrainian drones and missiles have struck 28 major Russian oil refineries in more than 200 confirmed attacks this year.

Mr Colby’s remarks have prompted sharp pushback from British and European ministers, who view strikes on Russian oil refineries and weapons factories – alongside this week’s Ukrainian raid that collapsed a section of the Kerch Bridge – as vital in weakening Vladimir Putin’s war effort.

Defence secretary Wes Streeting defended Kyiv’s strategy following Tuesday’s talks, stating that the strikes “helped to degrade Putin’s war machine, buying time and saving lives,” while German defence minister Boris Pistorius dismissed Mr Colby’s assessment as “stupid”.

German officials also pointed to the perceived contradiction in Washington’s stance given the US military’s own reliance on long-range strikes in its campaign against Iran.

![German defence minister Boris Pistorius dismissed Mr Colby's assessment as 'stupid'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/21/2305162112..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A second source confirmed Mr Colby left as soon as media departed, leaving US ambassador to Nato Matthew Whitaker to represent Washington for the remainder of the session.

Mr Colby subsequently addressed reporters in the atrium of Nato headquarters at around 5pm local time before departing for the airport to catch a flight.

The diplomatic friction comes ahead of a major summit of the “coalition of the willing” in Berlin on Thursday, which prime minister Andy Burnham will co-chair alongside his French and German counterparts.

Around 40 leaders – joined by Donald Trump’s peace envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner – are due to discuss long-term security guarantees and post-war reconstruction plans for Ukraine.
