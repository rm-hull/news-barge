# Attempted murder of Bradford councillor: Everything we know so far

## Police have given a new update on the Bradford stabbing of independent councillor Ibrar Ali, who was attacked while door-knocking.

Coun Ali, 52, was attacked and sustained serious injuries on Manse Street, off Leeds Road, at around 11am.

As West Yorkshire Police issued new information about the incident, here is everything we know about the stabbing so far.

## **What happened in the Bradford stabbing?**

Police were called to Manse Street after 11am on November 19, after reports the man, thought to be independent Bradford councillor, Ibrar Ali, was stabbed.

The alleged attacker, a 32-year-old man who cannot yet be identified, fled to the roof of a nearby property on Thornsbury Avenue, ending up in a stand-off with police.

![Emergency services at the scene after a man was arrested on suspicion of attempted murder following the stabbing of a councillor while out door-knocking in Bradford](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/17/04/01M7TC6PPWTGZMN10TXQN8BE88.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He was eventually brought down and arrested on suspicion of attempted murder, and remains in police custody.

West Yorkshire Police later confirmed the stabbing took place on Thornbury Avenue, and that the victim was then driven to Manse Street, where emergency services attended.

Police also added in a new update that they did not have any information to suggest the attack was politically motivated.

## **What is Coun Ali’s condition?**

Fellow Bradford councillor for the Lib Dems, Riaz Ahmed, confirmed to The Yorkshire Post that it was Coun Ali who had been attacked.

Lord mayor of Bradford Coun Chris Herd said Coun Ali was “currently receiving treatment in hospital”.

![Coun Ibrar Ali sustained serious injuries after being stabbed while door-knocking in Bradford.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/16/25/Screenshot-2026-11-19-at-16-19-26.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=544&quality=65)


West Yorkshire Police said the victim’s condition was “serious but not life-threatening”.

It has been reported that Asif Khan, a friend of Ali, said the councillor was taken to Leeds General Infirmary with stab wounds to his arm and leg.

Mr Khan reportedly said Coun Ali was “awake and talking”.

## What have other political figures in Bradford said?

Bradford East MP Imran Hussain MP said: “This horrific attack is deeply disturbing. My thoughts and prayers are with councillor Ibrar Ali and his family.

“No elected politician should feel under threat or fear for their safety while carrying out their duties and serving their community.

“I am receiving regular updates from the police as they deal with this extremely serious incident, and I understand a suspect is in police custody.”

Chris Herd, the Lord Mayor of Bradford, said: “Sadly, a Bradford councillor was seriously assaulted this morning.

“He is currently receiving treatment in hospital and we wish him a full and speedy recovery.

“Our thoughts are with him, his family and his friends at this difficult time.

“It is deeply distressing that this incident has occurred.

“Councillors work tirelessly on behalf of residents across the district and should be able to carry out their duties safely.

“We will continue to work closely with our colleagues at West Yorkshire Police as they investigate this matter and seek to bring those responsible to justice, and would like to thank those who provided aid and support at the time.”
