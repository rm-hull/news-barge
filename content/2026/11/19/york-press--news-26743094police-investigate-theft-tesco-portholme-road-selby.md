Police have issued an appeal to help identify a person they would like to speak to following a reported shop theft in North Yorkshire.

The alleged theft took place at Tesco in Portholme Road, Selby on October 30, 2026.

Officers have released an image of a person they believe may have information that could assist with their ongoing enquiries.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We would like to speak to this person as they may have information that could assist with our enquiries."

Anyone who recognises the individual, or has information that could help identify them, is asked to contact police.

Information can be provided by emailing [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk), reporting online via the North Yorkshire Police website, using the force's live chat service, calling 101, or contacting Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference number 1226021361 when providing information.
