A winter ball in Harrogate has raised nearly £10,000 for North Yorkshire charity Dementia Forward.

Around 180 people gathered at the DoubleTree by Hilton Harrogate Majestic Hotel & Spa on Friday 6th November for an evening inspired by *The Chronicles of Narnia*.

The money will help Dementia Forward support more than 5,500 families affected by dementia across North Yorkshire.

The charity began its work in the Harrogate district.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afedc5a1d8a25d2a1c.png)

Billy Bowness, who has appeared in *Nobody’s Fool*and* Silent Witness*, hosted the ball after recently becoming an ambassador for the charity.

Billy said:

> “I am delighted to be an ambassador for Dementia Forward, as having witnessed, first-hand, with my father’s diagnosis and rapid decline, the devastating effects of dementia, not just on the person living with the disease but those closest to them, I wanted to do something to help.”

Walker Foster Solicitors and Country Court Care sponsored the ball, with an anonymous sponsor supporting the drinks reception.

Local people and businesses donated auction and raffle prizes.

Debby Lennox, Head of Community Operations at Dementia Forward, said:

> “We’re absolutely delighted that so many people attended the Winter Ball and want to say a huge thank you to every single person who has supported the event.”

She added:

> “With your support, we can continue to reach more families, develop our services and ensure that nobody has to face dementia alone.”

Next year’s Winter Ball will take place on Friday 12th November 2027.
