## The US defence secretary, under fire for his role in the Iran war and illegal bombings in the Caribbean and Pacific, has announced a military pact with Venezuela. His next stop is Honduras.

United States Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth landed in Ecuador on Thursday as part of his South American tour, after brief visits to Colombia and Venezuela, where he pledged to eliminate and defeat narcoterrorists.

Ecuador's president, Daniel Noboa, is ideologically aligned with the Trump administration and has driven a growing militarisation of the country's cities, in line with other conservative populist leaders in the region.

Hegseth's trip to Latin America comes after an attempt by United States lawmakers, including a Republican representative, to remove him from office, prompted by the war against Iran and by air strikes without legal backing on several vessels suspected of drug trafficking over the past year, causing the deaths of more than 200 people.

The Defence Secretary will later make a brief visit to Honduras, according to the country's press office, where he will meet at a nearby air base with Nasry Asfura, another leader allied with Trump. This is the Pentagon chief's first official visit to South America, which has included a meeting with Venezuela's interim president, Delcy Rodríguez.

As part of his trip to Venezuela, Hegseth announced that he would increase military cooperation with the post-Maduro regime and promised to hunt down drug traffickers.
