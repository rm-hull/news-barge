## Six classic Christmas films returning to the big screen in York

Home Alone *(Image: supplied)*

Festive favourites are returning to the big screen in York this winter.

Vue York is bringing a selection of classic Christmas films back to cinemas, with highlights including Arthur Christmas, Home Alone, Elf, The Holiday, It’s a Wonderful Life, and Miracle on 34th Street.

Vue is also offering gift cards this Christmas, which can be used to purchase tickets, snacks, and drinks at any Vue location in the UK.

Dean Cross, senior screen content manager at Vue UK and Ireland, said: "There’s something really special about watching your favourite Christmas films on the big screen, whether it’s a family tradition or a chance to rediscover a much-loved classic.

"This year’s Back on the Big Screen line-up features some fantastic festive favourites, including three films celebrating major anniversaries.

"We’re delighted to be giving audiences the chance to experience these magical stories all over again, just as they were meant to be seen."

Arthur Christmas, celebrating its 15th anniversary, will be screened from November 20.

The animated film tells the story of Santa’s youngest son as he embarks on a mission to deliver a forgotten present before Christmas morning.

Home Alone returns from November 27, inviting audiences to relive the adventures of eight-year-old Kevin McCallister as he defends his home from two bungling burglars.

Also returning on the same date is Elf, starring Will Ferrell as Buddy, a human raised by elves who travels to New York City in search of his real father.

Vue is marking Elf’s return with exclusive Christmas-themed merchandise, including a premium cup inspired by Buddy the Elf.

The Holiday, celebrating its 20th anniversary, will be shown from December 4.

The romantic comedy stars Cameron Diaz, Kate Winslet, Jude Law, and Jack Black, and follows two women who swap homes over the festive season.

A restored version of Frank Capra’s It’s a Wonderful Life will be screened from December 11.

The 4K restoration marks the film’s 80th anniversary, bringing the story of George Bailey and his journey of self-discovery back to cinemas in stunning detail.

Rounding off the line-up is the 1947 classic Miracle on 34th Street, which will be shown from December 25.

The film tells the story of a department store Santa who claims to be the real Father Christmas.

Guests can also join the Club Vue loyalty scheme, earning a stamp for each screening booked through the Club Vue app.

After collecting 10 stamps, members receive a free Vue ticket.

Screenings begin on various dates throughout November and December, with Arthur Christmas starting on November 20, Home Alone and Elf from November 27, The Holiday from December 4, It’s a Wonderful Life from December 11, and Miracle on 34th Street from December 25.

Tickets can be booked through the Club Vue app or at www.myvue.com.
