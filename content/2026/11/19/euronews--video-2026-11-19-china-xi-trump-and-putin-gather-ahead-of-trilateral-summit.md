The **three presidents stood together for the official group shot** ahead of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) forum’s opening session. The high-level assembly drew leaders from across the Pacific, featuring Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, Canada’s Mark Carney, Australia’s Anthony Albanese and South Korean President Lee Jae Myung.

Taking the podium, **Xi Jinping made a plea for closer Asia-Pacific ties** in an era of sweeping global upheaval and rapid technological breakthroughs. He challenged member nations to prioritise innovation, openness and deeper integration, framing the goal as a shared regional community rooted in solidarity and inclusive development.

All eyes, however, were fixed on **Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin, who are slated to join Xi for separate trilateral talks**. The rare sight of the Chinese, American and Russian presidents sharing a stage at the opening ceremony threw into sharp relief the diplomatic significance of the** Shenzhen meeting**.
