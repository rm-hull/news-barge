# Sweet treat Leeds city centre café shuts

## You can feast on some wonderful food at the former Leeds city centre business

A Leeds city centre café has shut down.

Those who have walked down Merrion Street may have spotted the closure. Nesso has sadly ceased operating from a unit there.

Nesso launched a Leeds city centre site, after building a loyal following at its Morley base, in 2025. It was known for its creative twist on Italian café culture with lots of sweet treats on the menu.

Notices have now gone up in the window of the now vacant Merrion Street unit. The sign reads: “Fitted café bar 1,500 sq ft to let.”

As we understand it, the Morley branch is unaffected following the Leeds city centre closure.
