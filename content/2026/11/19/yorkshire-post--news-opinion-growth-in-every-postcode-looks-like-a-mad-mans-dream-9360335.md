# Growth in every postcode looks like a mad man's dream

![Prime Minister Andy Burnham departs 10 Downing Street, London, to attend Prime Minister's Questions at the Houses of Parliament.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjViOGM4MGU4LWMzNzctNGJhMC04MTZhLTdiMmY1MWE4M2M2ODpiZjU0YTUyOS0yNzZlLTQwZGItYWEzNC1lZWJkZGZjNjY3Y2Q=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## With Christmas fast-approaching and inclement weather incoming, that household energy bills are forecast to rise angrily in the new year is unwelcome news. Gas and electricity bills, according to a leading consultancy, are predicted to rise by more than a quarter; a typical household, consuming gas and electricity, is going to be a further £500 out of pocket next year, should it consume the same amount of the aforementioned fuels.

That is on top of an already eye-watering, pocket-punishing rise in recent months, thanks in no small measure to the whims of Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump, two men whose behaviour belies the status of their office.

Those fuels are throttled by price cap checks and balances; spare a thought, then, for those rural homes reliant upon kerosene for heat, who receive no help from any price capping. They have seen prices leap from around 55-pence per litre to more than double that. It doesn’t help, either, that petrol and diesel at the pump has hit Champagne prices. £2 a litre for on-the-road diesel is extortionate. Little wonder, then, that one of Yorkshire’s marquee companies, Jet2, is reluctantly cutting flights to holiday destinations this winter. It is a price-conscious firm that prides itself on getting people away on holidays they deserve, and can afford, but even its executives are struggling to make the sums add up.

The challenge for this Government is significant: the money that might have been spent on festive treats by so many people will be held back for warmth and basics. Trips out, including commuting, will be thought about twice. Growth in every postcode will, with each passing day, begin to look more and more like a fantasy dreamed up by a mad man who is out of touch with reality, not a Prime Minister, as Andy Burnham says he is, committed to improving people’s lives.
