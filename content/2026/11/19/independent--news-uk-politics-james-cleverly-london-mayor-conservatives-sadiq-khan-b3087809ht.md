Sir James Cleverly has been selected as the Conservative candidate for the next London mayoral election.

The Braintree MP and former cabinet member won in the first round of ballots, gaining 86 per cent of the vote from Conservative Party members.

He beat Westminster councillor Festus Akinbusoye, communications professional Alex Challoner, and former parliamentary candidate Sara Gezdari to win the nomination.

Sir James also confirmed that he would not be seeking re-election as an MP, declaring he was “all in” to fight to become mayor.

The presence of Sir James on the ballot for a role the Tories have not held since Boris Johnson stepped down in 2016 suggests the party is preparing for a serious contest.

The veteran Tory served as foreign secretary and home secretary in the last Conservative government, before running against Kemi Badenoch for the party leadership in 2024.

The next election for London mayor will take place in 2028 and Sir James said that incumbent Lord Sadiq Khan is “letting London down”.

“People are leaving London, or not choosing to come here in the first place, businesses, job creators, families leaving London, and by the time of the election in 2028, Khan will have been mayor for 12 years, and those two things are related,” he said.

![James Cleverly poses with his wife Susannah after he was declared the Conservative candidate for Mayor of London](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/10/2305859054..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“The problems London faces are the symptoms. Khan is the cause.

“When I ask Londoners, do you feel safe walking home at night? The answer is no.

“It’s shameful, it’s a failure, and it’s not just crime.

“We have had the worst period of house building since the Second World War and only the highest-earning Londoners can afford the cheapest houses in London.”

Sir James’ selection also means that London is set to become a battleground over Brexit.

Lord Khan said in September that Andy Burnham must start work immediately on rejoining the EU, urging the prime minister to do the “heavy lifting” on returning to the bloc as soon as possible.

However, Sir James told *The Independent* in October that any push to rejoin the EU would damage London’s interests.

He also said that London needs to reverse a brain drain that is seeing talent flee because of the cost of living and made the extraordinary claim that he would be able to cut prices of new houses in the capital by £100,000 if he is elected mayor.

“I want to make it cheaper for people to get on the housing ladder,” he said.

“The accumulated cost of bureaucracy, taxes, and various other things adds about £100,000 per property onto new build properties in London. That’s £100,000 that doesn't go into the bricks or mortar. It doesn’t go into the windows and doors. It just gets soaked up by tax and bureaucracy.”

Tory leader Kemi Badenoch congratulated Sir James.

Posting on social media, she said: “Under Sadiq Khan, our capital has been in decline and Khan and Labour are to blame.

“It’s time for change. It’s time to build a city we’re proud of again. It’s time for James Cleverly.”

Lord Khan was elected to serve his third term as mayor in 2024, with 43.8 per cent of the vote - ahead of the Conservatives’ Susan Hall with 32.7.
