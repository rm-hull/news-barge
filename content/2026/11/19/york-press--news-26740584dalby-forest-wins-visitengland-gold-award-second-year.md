Dalby Forest has been awarded a Gold Accolade by VisitEngland for its outstanding visitor experience.

Located in the North York Moors, the forest earned the honour for the second consecutive year, with only three sites in Yorkshire receiving the award.

The accolade follows a thorough assessment in June, which praised Dalby Forest for its wide range of outdoor activities and family attractions.

Andy Widdowson, head of recreation and visitor experience at Forestry England, said: "We are very proud and delighted that Dalby Forest has received the Gold Award from VisitEngland for the second year running.

"It is a true reflection of how hard our team works to ensure every visitor has the best time possible while they are with us.

"We thank our business partners at Go Ape, cycle hire, catering, accommodation and other activities for their contributions and sharing our values of excellent service."

The assessment highlighted walking, cycling and mountain biking trails, wildlife watching, tree climbing, paintballing, and art installations as part of the visitor offering.

The forest also features a Dark Sky Discovery Site for stargazing, accessible all-terrain mobility scooters, and regular events such as parkrun.

Recreation ranger Tasmin and visitor experience manager Ian collected the award on November 10.
