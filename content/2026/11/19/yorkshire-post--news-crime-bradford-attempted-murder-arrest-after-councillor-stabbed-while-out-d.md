# Bradford councillor stabbed ‘while out door knocking’ in ‘attempted murder’

## A man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder after an independent councillor was stabbed while out door knocking in Bradford.

Police were called to Manse Street, just off Leeds Road, after 11am on Thursday, after independent Bradford councillor Ibrar Ali was stabbed.

The alleged attacker, a 32-year-old man, fled to the roof of a nearby property before being brought down after a stand-off with police.

![Coun Ibrar Ali sustained serious injuries after being stabbed while door-knocking in Bradford.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/16/25/Screenshot-2026-11-19-at-16-19-26.png?trim=99,2,67,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


He was then arrested on suspicion of attempted murder and remains in custody, West Yorkshire Police said.

Fellow Councillor Riaz Ahmed (Bradford Moor, Lib Dems) confirmed to The Yorkshire Post it was Coun Ali who had been attacked.

Coun Ahmed said: “My thoughts are with Councillor Ibrar, and I sincerely wish him a speedy and full recovery.

“As an elected councillor, I find incidents of this nature deeply disturbing. No councillor, elected representative or member of our community should have to fear for their personal safety while serving the public.

![Scene on Thornbury Avenue, Bradford, where a councillor was stabbed.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/16/09/onecms_d6459ea6-3bc9-42cc-862c-1da6194bf191-(1).jpeg?trim=0,7,0,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


“Violence and intimidation have no place in our society or in local democracy.”

It is understood Coun Ali had been out door knocking at the time.

West Yorkshire Police said there was nothing to suggest the incident was politically motivated, although the investigation was at an early stage.

Local government minister Baroness Taylor of Stevenage named Mr Ali in the House of Lords.

She said: “I am afraid I have to mention a very tragic incident that’s occurred in Bradford… where an independent councillor who was out canvassing has been stabbed.

“I would want to send the wishes, that I’m sure noble Lords would agree, of the whole House to councillor Ibrar Ali for a speedy recovery, and our thoughts are with all of those in *Bradford* who are affected by this awful incident.”

Officers remain at the scene in Manse Street and are also in nearby Leeds Road and Thornbury Avenue and detectives are investigating.
