Police are appealing for information after plumbing materials and scrap metal were reported stolen in York.

The alleged theft took place on October 15 at 2.48pm.

Officers have released CCTV images of a person they believe could help with their investigation.




A police spokesperson said: "We are asking anyone who recognises the individual pictured, witnessed the incident, or has any information that could help the investigation to get in touch.

"We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who may have CCTV, doorbell, or dashcam footage from the area, witnessed any suspicious activity, or has information that may help identify the person shown in the images.

"Please do not publicly speculate on the identity of the person pictured.

"Anyone with information is asked to email [Keilee.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Keilee.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk), report online, or use the live chat service on the North Yorkshire Police website.

"Those unable to use online services can call 101.

"Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111."
