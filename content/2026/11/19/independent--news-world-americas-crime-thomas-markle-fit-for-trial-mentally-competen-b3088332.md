Thomas Markle, the 82-year-old estranged father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, was found to be mentally competent by an expert to face charges of keeping child sexual abuse material in storage, a judge said on Thursday.

Markle was arrested in Florida on Nov. 6 and charged with storing child sexual abuse material in California.

Markle's defense lawyer will not contest the findings of Dr. Amie Gerodimos. The exam was ordered after his arrest at a senior care center in Lakeland, Florida, on Nov. 6.

During a 90-minute interview, Markle denied the allegations against him and blamed his son for his legal troubles, according to Gerodimos' 15-page report.

“Why I am here? What have I done?” she quoted him as saying. “I don’t have any child porn. I have no pictures of naked kids.”

Markle was arrested in Lakeland, Florida, after the seizure of approximately 78 items from storage units in the Los Angeles area, including slides, film negatives and books that contained suspected child sexual abuse images, the FBI said in a court filing.

He was released after his arrest at a senior care center but was ordered back to jail four days later, on Nov. 10, when authorities accused him of cutting off his electronic ankle monitor. A judge also ordered him to be examined by a mental health professional.

“Mr. Markle is accused of possessing child sexual abuse material, including photographs depicting himself with minor girls,” First Assistant United States Attorney Bill Essayli said after Markle’s arrest.

“These allegations are extremely serious, and we will prosecute Mr. Markle to the fullest extent of federal law.”

Markle suffered a stroke earlier this year while living in the Philippines and had his left leg amputated, forced him to return to the U.S. and settle in Florida.

Meghan’s half brother allegedly found child sexual abuse images while clearing out their father’s home in the Philippines, The Sun reported. That led police to search two storage units in California that Markle had rented since 2008.

Markle is under investigation in the Philippines for possession and intent to distribute images. On Monday, the Women and Children Protection Center, a branch of the Philippine National Police, filed a formal criminal complaint against Markle, police spokesperson Brig. Gen. Allen Rae Co said.

The complaint was filed with the prosecutor’s office after an investigation into digital devices found at Markle’s former home in Cebu City, per the police statement. The prosecutor’s office will assess the case for possible charges.

Markle did not attend his daughter Meghan’s wedding to Prince Harry at Windsor Castle in 2018. Meghan, who starred in the U.S. television drama Suits, said at the time that he needed to tend to his health.
