North Yorkshire has been named Yorkshire's sweet tooth capital, with the highest concentration of sweet shops in the region.

According to new research by Forever Dental and Skin, the county boasts 27 sweet shops — equivalent to 4.22 per 100,000 residents — more than two and a half times the UK average of 1.47.

This gives North Yorkshire the 24th highest concentration of sweet shops of any council area in the UK.

York follows with 3.35 sweet shops per 100,000 people, while East Riding of Yorkshire has 3.33.

Dr Murray Pratt, principal dentist and clinical director at Forever Dental and Skin, said: "Most of us get through more sweets than usual over Christmas, so it's worth thinking about when you eat them, not just which ones you pick.

"The best time to have them is with a meal or just after one.

"Every time sugar reaches your teeth, the bacteria in plaque turn it into acid, so a bag of pick and mix that's picked at all afternoon does a lot more harm than the same bag finished after dinner."

Nationally, Blackpool tops the list with 8.36 sweet shops per 100,000 residents, followed by Gwynedd at 8.31 and East Lindsey at 8.18.
