## York photographer seeks 40 women over 40 for special portrait campaign

Edina, the owner of Photography by Edina, a photography studio based in York *(Image: Photography by Edina)*

A York photographer is seeking 40 women aged 40 and over for a new campaign.

Edina White, the owner of Photography by Edina, is launching the "40 OVER 40" campaign to celebrate the confidence, beauty, strength, and stories of women from York and the surrounding areas.

The project, which aims to challenge societal norms that often prioritise youth, is offering each selected woman a complimentary professional portrait session at Edina's York studio.

They will also receive a free printed portrait chosen by Edina herself.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21713498.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edina White with her daughter *(Image: Photography by Edina)*

Ms White said: "I believe women over 40 are so often overlooked, particularly when it comes to beauty, visibility, and representation.

"Society places so much emphasis on youth, yet women in their 40s, 50s, 60s, 70s, and beyond have so much to celebrate."

The campaign will culminate in a special art exhibition showcasing all 40 portraits, highlighting the individuality and stories of the participants.

The campaign is not about trying to appear younger, but rather about demonstrating that beauty, confidence, and self-worth are ageless.

Ms White added: "Women spend so much of their lives looking after everyone else.

"They become mothers, partners, carers, and professionals, and somewhere along the way, many stop putting themselves first.

"I want to remind them that they deserve to be seen, celebrated, and photographed, exactly as they are today."

The search is currently on for women aged 40 and over who would like to participate in this campaign.

The campaign is welcoming women in their 40s, 50s, 60s, 70s, and beyond.
