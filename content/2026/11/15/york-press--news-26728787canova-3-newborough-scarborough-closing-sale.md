## North Yorkshire business to close its doors after festive sale

Signs in the window of Canova in Scarborough *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A NORTH Yorkshire business has announced that it will be closing its doors for good later this year.

Canova - a fashion accessory, wallet, and handbag shop located at 3 Newborough in Scarborough – has said that it will be shutting its doors for good after a festive sale.

In a post to Facebook, the shop announced an initial closure date of October 16 this year – with 50 per cent off all stock – however signs in the window suggest that it will be back for a last hurrah.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21717198.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Canova in Scarborough will be closing its doors after a clearance sale *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

New signs spotted in the shop window state that it will be open from Monday (November 16) until Christmas for a ‘final clearance sale’.

The shop offers a range of different bags from suitcases to smaller bags and purses.
