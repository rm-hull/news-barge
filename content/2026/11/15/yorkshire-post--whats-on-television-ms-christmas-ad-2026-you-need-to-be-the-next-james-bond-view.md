# M&S Christmas Ad 2026: ‘You need to be the next James Bond’ - Viewers react to Happy Valley actor James Norton in M&S Christmas advert

## Happy Valley actor James Norton’s presence in the new M&S Christmas advert has evoked a strong response from the public - many of whom believe he should be the next James Bond.

James Norton, known for his roles in Happy Valley, Grantchester, War and Peace, Mr Jones, House of Guinness and House of the Dragon, stars as a disguised Santa in the new M&S Christmas advert.

Amandaland’s Lucy Punch stars as Mrs Claus in the advert called The Home of Christmas and Santa goes undercover to experience Christmas without being recognised.

James’s performance has elicited a positive response from viewers, many of whom are eager for him to be the next James Bond.
