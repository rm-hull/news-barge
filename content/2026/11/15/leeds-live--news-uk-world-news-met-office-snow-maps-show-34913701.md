# Met Office snow maps show 18 areas facing Arctic blast on Monday

## The Met Office has forecast snow to hit England, Scotland, and Wales on Monday as temperatures plunge across the UK

The Met Office has issued a snow warning for England, Scotland and Wales on Monday, with temperatures set to nosedive across the nation.

Weather charts from the forecaster suggest snowfall could begin before 5am. The prediction follows warnings from meteorologists that polar vortex winds have shifted direction, potentially unleashing a severe Arctic cold snap on Britain this winter.

Met Office mapping indicates snow will arrive in areas of the Scottish Highlands, southern Scotland including Lanarkshire, and sections of northern England. The early hours snowfall is expected across Lancashire, Durham, Cumbria and parts of North Yorkshire, according to the charts, reports the Express.
