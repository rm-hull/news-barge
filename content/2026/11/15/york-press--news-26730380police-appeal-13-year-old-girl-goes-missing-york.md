A 13-year-old girl from York has gone missing, North Yorkshire Ppolice have reported.

The teenager, named Arianna, was last seen at her home on the morning of November 14.

Police believe she has travelled to the Manchester area and may be in the company of another teenager from the Scunthorpe area.

Arianna is described as white, 5ft 11in tall, with short blonde hair and nose piercings.

She was last seen wearing a black and grey checked shirt, charcoal-coloured baggy jeans, a dark-coloured hoody, a black leather jacket, black shoes, and possibly carrying a black rucksack.

The police urge anyone who has seen Arianna in York, on public transport, or in the Manchester or Scunthorpe areas, to contact them immediately.

Those with an immediate sighting are asked to call 999.

Anyone with other information is asked to call 101, quoting reference 12260224146.
