A new housing development offering 151 energy-efficient homes is set to launch in North Yorkshire during early 2027.

Manor Farm, located on Chapel Street in Hambleton, near Selby, is being developed by David Wilson Homes and will feature a mix of three, four and five-bedroom properties.

The development will include a play area and public open space as part of the plans to build a well-connected and green community.

Paul Hogan, sales director at David Wilson Homes Yorkshire East, said: "We are looking forward to launching Manor Farm early next year.

"It is an exciting prospect for homebuyers to own a property in the idyllic village of Hambleton.

"We are expecting a high volume of interest in the homes at Manor Farm, so we encourage prospective buyers to register their interest on the website before the development launches."

The site is less than six miles from Selby and offers convenient access to York, Leeds and nearby towns and cities.

David Wilson Homes says Hambleton is particularly suited to buyers seeking a village lifestyle with good transport links and amenities nearby.

The village itself offers a number of local amenities, eateries and well-regarded schools, with options for outdoor activity, hikes and areas for children to play and explore.

The homes will include energy-saving features such as photovoltaic panels, electric vehicle charging points, and argon-filled double glazing.

David Wilson Homes has received a five-star rating in the Home Builders Federation Customer Satisfaction Survey for 17 consecutive years.

The rating, the company says, reflects the developer’s ongoing commitment to quality and customer service.

Anyone interested in the new development can register their interest online through the David Wilson Homes Manor Farm website.

Further information about other developments in the county is also available at www.dwh.co.uk.
