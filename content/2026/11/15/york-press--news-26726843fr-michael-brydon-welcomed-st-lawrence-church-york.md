More than 230 people filled the pews in York for the licensing of Fr Michael Brydon as priest-in-charge.

He was licensed at St Lawrence Parish Church during a service of Choral Evensong by the Bishop of Beverley, Stephen Race.

The congregation included the Lord Mayor of York, Councillor Margaret Wells, her Consort Trevor Gant, and the Bishop of Selby, Flora Winfield, who preached the sermon.

Fr Brydon said: "I am so privileged to be serving in a church with a committed, loving and dynamic congregation in this beautiful City of York."

Fr Brydon previously served as Chaplain to the Bishop of Sodor and Man and Priest-in-Charge of St Matthew’s in Douglas, Isle of Man.

A spokesman for St Lawrence Parish Church said: "We are delighted to welcome Fr Brydon to St Lawrence and are grateful to everyone who joined us for this service."
