Firefighters dealt with two fire in York and North Yorkshire earlier today (SUN), one of which was believed to have been caused deliberately by youths.

The first call-out was at 9.34AM involving the Tadcaster crew crewing to Sherburn-in-Elmet.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue spokesperson said: "Tadcaster crew responded to reports of a small fire in a park, on fire service arrival crews found a small fire consisting of cardboard smoldering, crews ensured it was fully extinguished.

"Cause believed to be deliberately set by youths."

Shortly after, at 10am, a crew from Acomb responded to reports of a small fire.

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue said: "On arrival crews found a fire consisting of shrubbery and aerosols, crews extinguished using one knapsack sprayer."
