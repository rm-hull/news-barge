Dan Shrimpton, chair of the board of trustees at Joseph Rowntree Theatre, has been awarded an accolade at the inaugural Great British Theatre Awards.

He won the Shining Star award, in a joint win with the Leith Theatre Trust.

The award was in recognition of ‘his leadership as Chair of York’s Joseph Rowntree Theatre, overseeing significant building improvements and strengthening fundraising to secure the theatre’s future while remaining true to its original purpose.’

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/886770055_1131463282862699_4059336861114036388_n.jpg)

The Great British Theatre Awards, presented by the Theatres Trust in their 50th anniversary year, had a night of celebration at Central Hall Westminster in London on Monday 2 November.

The new awards programme is ‘dedicated to the theatre buildings and spaces where extraordinary performances come to life and communities gather to connect.’

The awards ceremony was hosted by theatre icon Marisha Wallace, and was filled with organisations and individuals who make an ‘outstanding contribution to the UK’s cultural life’.

Acclaimed actor and theatre advocate Julie Hesmondhalgh was also presented with the Great British Theatres Lifetime Achievement Award.

Dan told ***YorkMix*** what this award means to him and the team at Joseph Rowntree Theatre. “I am really chuffed. The theatre’s gone through a number of developments and work, which I’ve led on, so it’s been nice to get the recognition that the work’s been noted.

“It’s also nice to actually be able to put the theatre on a a national stage. Most people won’t even know it exists in the country, so it’s nice to be able to talk about it at an event and explain what the theatre is and and why it exists.”

The Joseph Rowntree Theatre have recently launched their ‘Light Up The Future‘ campaign, which aims to raise £100,000 to replace some of their technical equipment such as new radio mics, new lighting control board, and new stage lights.

The theatre also regularly has fundraising shows and events.
