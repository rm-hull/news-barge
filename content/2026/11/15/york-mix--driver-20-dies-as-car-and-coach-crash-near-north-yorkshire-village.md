A 20-year-old man has died after a car and a coach crashed in North Yorkshire.

It happened at around 6.45pm yesterday (Saturday) on the A19 near the village of Thirkleby south of Thirsk.

A silver Vauxhall Corsa travelling south collided with a coach travelling in the opposite direction.

#### Crash location

The driver of the Corsa, a 20-year-old man, died in the collision. His passenger, another man in his 20s suffered serious injuries and was taken to hospital.

There were no serious injuries reported by anyone on the coach.  
The road was closed to allow emergency services to work at the scene, for collision investigators to examine the scene and recovery of the vehicles.

Police remain at the scene pending recovery of the coach.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are appealing to anyone who was travelling in either direction and may have seen either vehicle prior to the collision, witnessed the collision, or has any dashcam footage of the vehicles before or of the collision, to get in touch.”
