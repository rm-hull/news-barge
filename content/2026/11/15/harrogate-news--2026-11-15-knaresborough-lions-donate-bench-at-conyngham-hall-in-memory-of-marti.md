The Knaresborough Lions Club has donated a bench at Conyngham Hall in memory of Martin Berry, a long-serving member of the club and one of the organisers of the Great Knaresborough Bed Race.

The bench has been installed on the riverbank, close to where teams competing in the annual Bed Race emerge from the water after crossing the River Nidd.

Martin Berry joined the Lions in 2010 and remained a member until his death in 2019. He was fully committed to the Bed Race and also competed in the event during the 1970s and 1980s.

> **Lisa Swiers said:**
>
> We are delighted to remember Lion Martin in this way and it is a perfect donation for a man who was such a stalwart both of the Lions and of Bed Race itself.

Lisa Swiers arranged for the bench to be obtained from North Yorkshire Council.

### Getting involved with Knaresborough Lions

Knaresborough Lions is a volunteer-run charity which organises community events including the Great Knaresborough Bed Race and Beer Festival.

Anyone interested in getting involved can find out more through the Knaresborough Lions website or the Bed Race Facebook pages.
