Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is plotting his return to the AI chipmaking company with his allies, a new report has revealed.

It’s only been a few days since Huang stepped down from the $4 trillion tech firm he founded after he fessed up to an affair with a suspected Chinese spy — and now some say he wants back in.

Huang has had discussions with confidantes about possibly returning as Nvidia’s chief executive, multiple people familiar with the matter told the *Financial Times*.* The Independent* has reached out to Nvidia for comment.

One ally said they were convening a “war room” of tech executives to get Huang back at the helm and would try to get support from the White House.

President Donald Trump acted shocked when news broke of Huang’s romantic ties to Alice Huang, a Chinese executive who U.S. intelligence believed was sharing information with Beijing.

![Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is plotting his return to the AI chipmaking company with his allies, a new report has revealed](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/15/20/54/GettyImages-2301014104.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

*“* I view him as a very high quality person. I've known him a long time,” Trump told reporters earlier this week.

The president insisted he had no idea about the U.S. intelligence on Alice Huang when he visited China in May, but the *New York Times* reported in late October that Trump was briefed on the findings about a year ago and chose not to take action.

Nvidia announced last Sunday, the day that Huang admitted the affair to employees, that it would be conducting an investigation following reporting that Huang “has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort.”

Huang resigned Thursday and was replaced by Colette Kress, Nvidia’s executive vice president and chief financial officer, until a permanent replacement is found.

![One ally said they were convening a 'war room' of tech executives to get Huang back at the helm and would try to get support from the White House](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/15/21/35/GettyImages-2298607332.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

But it’s not just a couple of allies calling for Huang to return.

An online petition to reinstate Huang as Nvidia’s CEO has garnered more than 100,000 signatures, including around 60,000 from shareholders, 6,000 from current Nvidia employees and 1,000 from former employees, according to reports.

Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, Replit CEO Amjad Masad and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney were among the tech bros publicly backing the effort.

“Everyone that matters in tech outside the frontier labs loves Jensen,” the Huang ally who spoke to the *Financial Times* claimed.

![While supposed efforts by tech executives to reinstate Huang as Nvidia CEO remain mostly under wraps, an online petition for Huang’s return has garnered more than 100,000 signatures](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/15/21/22/GettyImages-2300710246.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

But it might not be enough to bring Huang back, especially considering new reports that he also had affairs with two Nvidia employees.

Huang had an affair with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of health care who reported to him and is also married, five people who worked at the company told *The New York Times*.

Huang also had an intimate relationship with a second Nvidia employee, Reuters reported, citing two people familiar with the matter.

“Having an affair with a subordinate is a fireable offence,” one person involved in the push for Huang’s return admitted when speaking with the *Financial Times*.
