Arianna, aged 13 from York, was last seen at her home in York on the morning of Saturday 14 November and has been out of contact since. Officers are growing concerned for her safety and welfare.

North Yorkshire Police believe Arianna has travelled to the Manchester area and may be in the company of another teenager from the Scunthorpe area.

She is described as white, 5ft 11in tall with short blonde hair, nose piercings and was last seen wearing a black and grey checked shirt, charcoal coloured baggy jeans, a dark-coloured hoody, black leather jacket, black shoes and possibly carrying a black rucksack.

They are appealing to anyone who has seen Arianna in York, on public transport and in the Manchester or Scunthorpe area, to get in touch.

If you have an immediate sighting of her, or know where she is now, please call us on 999.
