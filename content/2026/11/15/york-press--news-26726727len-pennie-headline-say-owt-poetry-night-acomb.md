The spoken word will be taking centre stage in Acomb this December.

Scottish poet Len Pennie will headline Say Owt’s final poetry event of the year at Rise, Bluebird Bakery, on December 5.

Ms Pennie is known for her 'Scots Word of the Day' videos on Instagram and TikTok, where she shares the Scots language with a global audience of more than 655,000 followers.

Henry Raby, Say Owt's artistic director, said: "We’re always excited to bring new artists to our city.

"We’ve been following Len’s career for years, and this will be her first time performing in York, so it’s going to be a real treat."

Supporting her on the night will be York-based performer Sylvia Marie, who appears under the name No Dry Eyes Poetry.

Ms Pennie has published two collections, Poyums and Poyums Annaw, both of which became Sunday Times bestsellers.

Her debut collection was named Scots Book of the Year in 2024 and won the British Book Awards Discover Book of the Year in 2025.

Ms Pennie’s poetry blends humour with honesty and often explores themes such as mental health and domestic violence.

The event is part of York Disability Week, with doors opening at 7.30pm and the show beginning at 8.30pm.
