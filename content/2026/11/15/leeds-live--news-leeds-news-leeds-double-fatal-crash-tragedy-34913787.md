# Leeds double fatal crash tragedy as two killed in horror collision after police give chase

## West Yorkshire Police said two people died and that five people have been arrested

Two people have tragically died in crash involving a car being pursued by police in Leeds. Four people, including three teenagers, have been arrested.

West Yorkshire Police said that at 12.44am this morning a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a police vehicle in Gelderd Road, Leeds. The Force said that a pursuit began and the BMW crashed into a white Volkswagen Scirocco which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, was sadly pronounced dead at the scene, and a passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman from York, died at hospital a short time later.

A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis in relation to the incident and remains in custody.

Three other occupants of the BMW, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, were taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life threatening. They have each been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

A police scene and road closure remain in place in Gelderd Road, and drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.

West Yorkshire Police said a referral has been made to the Independent Office for Police Conduct which has declared an independent investigation.

Anyone who witnessed the collision or saw either vehicle in the time leading up to it, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online quoting reference 13260649342.
