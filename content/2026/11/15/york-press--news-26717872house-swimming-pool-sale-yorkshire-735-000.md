## Inside ‘fabulous’ family home for sale in Yorkshire town (with its own swimming pool)

Harmony House, for sale in Leeds Road, Selby, for £735,000 *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

An impressive family home has hit the market in North Yorkshire – and it even has its own swimming pool.

The four-bedroom property, called Harmony House, is for sale in Leeds Road, Selby, for £735,000.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21699662.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Harmony House, for sale in Leeds Road, Selby, for £735,000 *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

Churchills, which is marketing the property, said it is a "fabulous and substantial family sized home".

Inside, an entrance hallway leads to the first bedroom, a lounge and the kitchen/dining area.

The swimming pool and spa area, including a sauna and steam room, is also on the ground floor.
