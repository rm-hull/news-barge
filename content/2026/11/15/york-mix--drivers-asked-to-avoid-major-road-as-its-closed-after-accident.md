A major North Yorkshire road, near Thirsk, remains closed today (Sunday 15th November) following a crash between a bus and a car, which happened before 7pm last night.

The air ambulance, plus fire crews from Easingwold, Northallerton and Ripon were called out to the scene on the A19 at Hutton Sessay between Easingwold and Northallerton.

The road is currently closed both ways between Thirkleby turning and The Avenue, that is the turn-offs to Bagby and Hutton Sessay.
