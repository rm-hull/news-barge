In reality, Zinedine Zidane's men simply need to avoid defeat in Bordeaux to ensure passage to the quarter-finals. A key to getting the job done will be Kylian Mbappé's injury status. The captain, talisman and all-time top scorer for Les Bleus, Mbappé missed the game in Milan due to a niggling hamstring, but returns today.

It was his goal that decided the previous tie between these sides in a rotten campaign for the Crescent-Stars. Head coach Vincenzo Montella's sacking halfway through the group stage certainly didn't help matters, with new boss Sergen Yalçın unable to turn the tide. They're destined to play League B football next time, but a win today will certainly earn them some national pride.
