## North Yorkshire villages left without water

Yorkshire Water says the problem has now been fixed *(Image: Yorkshire Water)</i>*

Several villages in North Yorkshire went without water for several hours today due to a burst water main.

The water main was said to have burst near the roundabout where the A19 meets the A163, near Selby.

This led to reduced water pressure in large parts of Barlby and parts of Riccall and in some cases leaving no water whatsoever.

The North Duffield Village Forum said the burst water main is “flooding the roundabout and there is a lorry parked at this point with hazards on.”

Yorkshire Water has posted online said online it it was aware of the issue and was working on the issue.

At about 11.30am today (November 15) the company told The Press that all properties are back on supply now and have been for a few hours.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “We’re currently repairing a burst main on the A163, which temporarily resulted in loss of supply or no water pressure for some properties in the area this morning.

“To keep our colleagues and road users safe, we are working under two-way traffic lights. We apologise for the disruption and thank everyone in the area for their patience.”
