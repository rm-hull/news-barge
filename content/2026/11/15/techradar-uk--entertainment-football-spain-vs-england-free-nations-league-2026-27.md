Luis de la Fuente's world champions look unbeatable right now. La Roja made it five Nations League wins from five on Thursday night with a routine 2-0 victory over Czechia to extend their unbeaten run to 43 games. Spain didn't even need Lamine Yamal in that one, but the teenage winger should be back with top spot on the line after scoring in the 3-2 win at Wembley that kicked off the tournament. Mikel Oyarzabal is also in line to return.

England have done all they can since that opening defeat, winning four from four thanks to a scrappy 2-1 against Croatia on Thursday. Morgan Rogers and Harry Kane, on the night he became the Three Lions' most-capped men's player, got the goals but Jarell Quansah's error for the Vatreni's strike may result in fit-again Ezri Konsa returning to Thomas Tuchel's back four. Myles Lewis-Skelly is also available after a ban.

If England gain some revenge for the Euro 2024 final defeat they'll top the group, anything else it's Spain who will get a home second leg in the quarter-finals.
