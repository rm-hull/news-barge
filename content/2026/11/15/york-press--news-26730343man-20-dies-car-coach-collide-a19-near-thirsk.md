## 20-year-old man dies in crash in North Yorkshire

Sad news as North Yorkshire Police issue an update on last night's collision on the A19. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A 20-year-old man has died following a collision between his car and a coach in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police has just now issued an appeal for information about the collision, which happened on the A19 (York Road) between Thirsk and Easingwold on Saturday evening.

A police statement said: "We are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage following a fatal collision south of Thirsk on Saturday 14 November.

"It happened at around 6.45pm near the village of Thirkleby when a silver Vauxhall Corsa travelling south and a coach, travelling in the opposite direction, collided.

"Sadly, the driver of the Corsa, a 20-year-old man died in the collision. His passenger, another man in his 20s suffered serious injuries and was taken to hospital."

The statement continued: "We are appealing to anyone who was travelling in either direction and may have seen either vehicle prior to the collision, witnessed the collision, or has any dashcam footage of the vehicles before or of the collision, to get in touch."

Anyone with any information is asked to email [SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk)

Or call 101 and pass information for incident number 12260224121

North Yorkshire Police added: "The road was closed to allow emergency services to work at the scene, for collision investigators to examine the scene and recovery of the vehicles. At the time of writing, emergency services remain at the scene pending recovery of the coach.

"There were no serious injuries reported by any occupants of the coach."
