## Businesses across Yorkshire asked to share opinions in economic survey

Businesses across Yorkshire are being urged to take part in a survey tracking local economic conditions *(Image: Supplied)*

Businesses across the region are being urged to take part in a survey tracking local economic conditions.

The quarterly economic survey aims to paint a clearer picture of the challenges and opportunities businesses face.

It offers companies, no matter their size or sector, a chance to share experiences about trading conditions.

The survey also highlights pressing issues affecting businesses, from recruitment and rising costs to investment, sales, and confidence.

The responses help build a comprehensive snapshot of business performance and identify where more support or action might be needed.

Business leaders are being encouraged to invest a small amount of time in completing the survey, as the collective responses provide crucial insights into the health of the regional economy.

A high number of participants is particularly significant, as the more businesses that respond, the more representative the findings become, ensuring the experiences of companies across the area are properly reflected.

Quarterly economic surveys can also help identify changing business sentiment and emerging pressures.

Participating gives businesses a chance to make their voices heard and ensures their issues are recognised.

Whether a business is expanding, recruiting, investing, or facing tough trading conditions, its experience is important.

Local employers are therefore being urged to fill out the latest survey.

Taking part is not just an opportunity to report on individual business performance, but to contribute to a broader understanding of the local economy and the challenges businesses face.

Businesses can complete the quarterly business survey online here: [https://chamber-survey.co.uk/wny/business-survey/](https://chamber-survey.co.uk/wny/business-survey/)
