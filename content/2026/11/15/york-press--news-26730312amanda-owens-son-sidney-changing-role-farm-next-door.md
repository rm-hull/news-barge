Amanda Owen's son Sidney is among the family members whose changing roles are set to feature in the continuing story of Our Farm Next Door.

The Owen children became familiar faces to millions of television viewers through Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed their upbringing at Ravenseat in the Yorkshire Dales.

From helping with animals to dealing with the challenges of rural life, the children regularly played a part in the everyday running of the farm.

Now, as the family grows older, their responsibilities are changing.

Sidney, also known as Sid, is among the children expected to take on more farming duties as the Channel 4 programme continues, and was recently renewed for another two series.

In series four of the show, Sid was seen taking his tractor test.

The development reflects a broader shift in the family's television story, with the next generation becoming increasingly involved in decisions and practical work.

For viewers who remember the children as youngsters, watching them develop their own skills has become an important part of the programme's appeal.

Life at Ravenseat has always involved more than the picturesque landscapes associated with the Yorkshire Dales.

The farm's remote location, changing weather and demands of livestock farming mean that practical work forms a major part of everyday life.

The family's television appearances have frequently shown how the children learn by taking part in jobs around the farm.

Those experiences are becoming increasingly relevant as the older siblings move towards independent adult lives.

The latest chapter of Our Farm Next Door also follows the restoration of Anty John's, another property in Swaledale.

That project has brought new challenges for the family alongside their continuing farming responsibilities.

With two further series commissioned, viewers will have another opportunity to see how Sidney and his siblings contribute to the family's changing future, which includes a storyline in the last series, which saw Sidney receive his tractor licence.
