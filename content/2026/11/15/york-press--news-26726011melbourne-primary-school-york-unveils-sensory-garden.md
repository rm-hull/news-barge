## York primary school unveils new sensory garden to inspire young pupils

Melbourne Primary School, part of the Wonder Learning Partnership, has launched its new sensory garden for pupils *(Image: GARETH BUDDO)*

A new sensory garden in York is helping pupils learn through nature and experience outdoors.

Melbourne Primary School, part of the Wonder Learning Partnership, has launched its new sensory garden for pupils as part of its “Grow, Explore, Achieve” ethos, which promotes hands-on, outdoor learning.

The garden was created with the support of the Melbourne Allstars, a community football team made up of dads and residents from nearby villages who raise money for local projects with the money raised from subs and donations.

Louis Taylor, a member of the team, said: "This year we had the pleasure of helping Melbourne Primary School realise their aim of creating a sensory garden for the children.

"It's a lovely sunny space and we are so happy that the children will benefit, and all from our fun-filled playing of the beautiful game.

"Thanks also need to go to my employer, NHBC, who have matched the team's fundraising in recent years."

Headteacher Vicky Burdett said the garden reflects the school’s commitment to practical, outdoor education.

Ms Burdett said: "Our ethos has developed to encompass hands-on learning through our natural landscape.

"The development of the sensory garden is inspiring our children every day as they connect the concepts we teach using practical applications.

"We are very fortunate to the support from our parents, carers and community and thank them for their generosity in making our sensory garden plan a reality."

Pupils are already enjoying the new space.

A Year 4 pupil said: "I like the way it represents the five senses and the peace it brings."

The school has expanded its outdoor learning facilities, adding a polytunnel, hosting Forest School days, and building an outdoor classroom shelter to support more lessons outside.

These initiatives form part of a wider strategy to use nature and the surrounding countryside as a foundation for learning and wellbeing.
