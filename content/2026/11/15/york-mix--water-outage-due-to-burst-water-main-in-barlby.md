There are reports that large parts of Barlby, in the YO8 area and Ricall, those with YO19 postcodes, are currently without water due to water main burst on the A163.

Yorkshire Water claim they area are of the issue which may mean some people have no water or low pressure.

The advice is to not contact them as they’re currently working to resolve the matter.
