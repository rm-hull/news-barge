A York businesswoman is launching a new gifting company that sells only products made by small, independent Yorkshire makers – with every order planting a tree.

Thoughtfully Yorkshire, founded by Helen Quinn from Acomb, creates themed gift boxes that mix handmade food and crafts, from fudge to candles and reed diffusers. Every box comes with a Meet the Maker card telling the story of the person who made what’s inside.

Thoughtfully Yorkshire will open with a Meet the Makers launch at DoubleTree by Hilton York on Saturday 30 January 2027.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Helen-with-products.jpeg)

Helen, who was born in Leeds and has run market stalls across North Yorkshire, said the idea came from the people she met along the way.

“At every market I’d meet incredibly talented makers who just weren’t getting seen,” she said.

“A wet weekend and a cancelled market could wipe out their month. I wanted to build something that gives them a proper shop window, and gives people a gift with a real Yorkshire story behind it instead of another quick high-street buy.”

The range includes The Harrogate Spa, The Dales Weekend, The Yorkshire Welcome for new homeowners, With Thanks from Yorkshire, The Lavender Fields and The Yorkshire Larder, plus a build-your-own option.

A Mother’s Day box will be the first seasonal release in 2027. Businesses can also order boxes for client and staff gifts.

The makers on board so far include Sweetly Indulgent, a Yorkshire fudge maker; Scents of York, which makes candles and reed diffusers; Salt + Steam, which makes bath salts, facial steamers and bath oils; Soap Out of the Garden, which makes handmade soaps and balms; and Viscount Folly, which makes tissue box covers featuring York landmarks.

The boxes also feature Yorkshire Rapeseed Oil and Charlie & Ivy’s, made by Breckenholme on its family farm in the Yorkshire Wolds.

The business has partnered with Ecologi to plant a tree for every order, and its events throughout the year will support UK nature projects.

The public launch takes place on Saturday 30 January 2027, 1pm to 5pm, at DoubleTree by Hilton York.

Tickets are £6.13, redeemable against any order on the day, and everyone attending is entered into a free prize draw to win £50 of maker goods.

Anyone who orders on the day receives a free Thoughtfully Yorkshire calendar and greetings card set.
