# Call Lane bar attack leaves victim with serious face wound

## Image released by police of man they would like to identify

Police investigating an assault in Leeds have issued an image of a man they would like to identify.

Detectives from Leeds CID would like to speak to him following an attack in licensed premises in Call Lane on Saturday, September 12, which left the victim with a serious facial injury.

West Yorkshire Police said: "Enquiries have been made to identify this man and now officers are asking for the public’s assistance in providing his name."
