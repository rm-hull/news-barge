Find out how to watch Belgium vs Italy for free from anywhere in the world as the Red Devils host the in-form Azzurri in Brussels with a spot in the Nations League quarter-finals on the line.

The conundrum for Belgium is simple – win. Three points guarantees progress to the quarter-finals (and may even be enough to top the group) but anything less and they need favors from pointless Turkey against France. The Red Devils came from behind on Thursday to beat the Turks 2-1 thanks to two goals in three first-half minutes from Kevin De Bruyne and Charles De Ketelaere, but Mark van Bommel knows his side must improve after goalkeeper Sanne Lammens made two fine late saves to secure the win.

You'd have got a good price on Italy topping Group A1 at this stage after Roberto Mancini began his second coming in the Azzurri dugout with a 2-0 opening loss at home to the Belgians. The Euro 2020-winning coach's side are on the up, though, and their 3-1 come-from-behind victory over France at San Siro on Thursday proved it after Gianluigi Donnarumma's early howler. Pio Esposito's brace took his international tally to nine goals in just 14 caps and the Inter attacker will again lead the line as Italy need a point to qualify and a win to top the group. Lose, though, and they also need help from Turkey.
