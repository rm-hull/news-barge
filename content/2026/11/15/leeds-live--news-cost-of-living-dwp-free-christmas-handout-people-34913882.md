# DWP free Christmas handout to people on 24 benefits - full list

## The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) is automatically paying a tax-free £10 Christmas Bonus in December 2026

The Department for Work and Pensions ( DWP ) is set to hand out a free Christmas payment to households throughout the UK in December for those claiming certain benefits.

The one-off, tax-free payment of £10 is awarded to individuals receiving one of 24 qualifying benefits, including the State Pension, Carer's Allowance, Personal Independence Payments and Pension Credit. First introduced by the UK Government in 1972, the £10 payment has been distributed to eligible claimants ever since, yet has never been uprated in line with inflation.

This means those who qualify for the bonus in December 2026 will receive the same sum as recipients have done over the past 50 years. The £10 bonus will be deposited automatically into bank accounts by the DWP for those receiving qualifying benefits, meaning households are not required to submit a claim.

The tax-free £10 Christmas Bonus will be issued to eligible recipients by the DWP during the 'qualifying week' next month, reports the Express. Typically, the DWP releases the payment during the first full week of December, ensuring those who qualify receive the bonus well ahead of Christmas.

According to DWP criteria, you will be entitled to the Christmas Bonus if you are in receipt of at least one of the following 24 benefits during the 'qualifying week', which is ordinarily the first full week of December:

* Adult Disability Payment
* Armed Forces Independence Payment
* Attendance Allowance
* Carer’s Allowance
* Carer Support Payment
* Child Disability Payment
* Constant Attendance Allowance (paid under Industrial Injuries or War Pensions schemes)
* Contribution-based Employment and Support Allowance (once the main phase of the benefit is entered after the first 13 weeks of claim)
* Disability Living Allowance
* Incapacity Benefit at the long-term rate
* Industrial Death Benefit (for widows or widowers)
* Mobility Supplement
* Pension Age Disability Payment
* Pension Credit - the guarantee element
* Personal Independence Payment (PIP)
* Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance (SADLA)
* Severe Disablement Allowance (transitionally protected)
* State Pension (including Graduated Retirement Benefit)
* Unemployability Supplement or Allowance (paid under Industrial Injuries or War Pensions schemes)
* War Disablement Pension at State Pension age
* War Widow’s Pension
* Widowed Mother’s Allowance
* Widowed Parent’s Allowance
* Widow’s Pension

To qualify for the payment, you must also be present, or 'ordinarily resident', in the UK, Channel Islands, Isle of Man or Gibraltar during the qualifying week.

If you are part of a married couple, in a civil partnership, or cohabiting as though you are, and you both receive one of the qualifying benefits, you will each receive a Christmas Bonus payment from the DWP. If your partner does not receive one of the qualifying benefits, they may still be entitled to the payment if both of the following apply:

* you’re both over State Pension age by the end of the qualifying week
* your partner or civil partner was also present (or ‘ordinarily resident’) in the UK, Channel Islands, Isle of Man, Gibraltar, European Economic Area (EEA) country or Switzerland during the qualifying week

You must also be entitled to an increase of a qualifying benefit for your partner or civil partner, or the only qualifying benefit you're getting is Pension Credit. The DWP stated: "If you have not claimed your State Pension and are not entitled to one of the other qualifying benefits you will not get a Christmas Bonus.

"You do not need to claim the Christmas Bonus - you should get it automatically. If you think you should get it, but have not by 1 January, contact the Jobcentre Plus office that deals with your payments or the Pension Service."

The Christmas bonus will typically be deposited into the same bank account as your pension or other benefits, and will display the payment reference 'DWP XB' on your bank statement.
