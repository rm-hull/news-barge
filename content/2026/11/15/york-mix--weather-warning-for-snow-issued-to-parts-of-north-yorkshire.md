A Yellow level weather alert for snow has been issue by the Met Office.

In North Yorkshire it covers areas west of the A1.

They say snow may cause disruption on high ground during Tuesday morning and the warning runs from 00:01 – 12:00 on Tuesday (November 17th)

Currently Hawes, Leyburn, Skipton, Settle, Masham and Pateley Bridge are all covered by the warning.

**This is the Met Office statement in full:**

“A band of rain is expected to head northwards across the UK through Monday night and Tuesday morning.

“As it encounters colder air, it is likely to turn to snow for a time, mainly affecting locations at 100 m altitude or higher. Any snow falling below this level will more likely be temporary and quickly melt.

“Where snow does accumulate, **depths of 1-5 cm** are likely; some locations**above 300-400 m could see 5-15 cm.**

“This may cause travel disruption on higher level routes, particularly in combination with a strong easterly wind which could also cause some localised drifting.

“Some accretion of ice onto infrastructure such as power lines is also a possibility. Snow will gradually turn back to rain both from the south and through the morning on Tuesday.”
