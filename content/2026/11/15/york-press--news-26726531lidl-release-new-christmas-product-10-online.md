Lidl will be launching a new advent calendar for £10 with items inside valued at £90.

The supermarket chain has announced plans to release its first-ever beauty calendar, filled with 24 products from its Cien and Deluxe beauty and self-care ranges.

In a statement, Lidl said: "Launching for the first time this festive season, Lidl’s debut Beauty Advent Calendar features 24 carefully curated items, all wrapped up in an intricately designed calendar inspired by a festive Lidl store.

"Featuring mischievous elves, snowmen in the bakery, snowball fights, and hidden Easter eggs to discover, shoppers will have the opportunity to bring home a Lidl magic this Christmas."

![](https://www.southwalesargus.co.uk/resources/images/21705432.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lidl's first-ever beauty advent calendar features 24 items *(Image: Lidl)*

## What will be included in the Lidl beauty advent calendar?

The calendar includes the Cien LED Gua Sha, worth £7.99, which offers a facial massage with adjustable Electronic Muscle Stimulation (EMS) levels to help firm and revitalise skin.

Other items include the Cien Facial Cleanser (£3.29), Cien Q10 Intense Serum (£2.85), Cien Sheet Mask (£0.99), and Cien Q10 Intense Day and Night Creams (£1.79 each).

The calendar also features fragrances such as Suddenly Femelle (£3.75) and the Deluxe Hair & Body Perfume Mist (£3.99).

Other products include the Deluxe Aromatic Hand Wash (£2.99), Deluxe Black Candle (£3.15), and a set of Deluxe Brazilian Shampoo and Conditioner (£3.99 each).

There’s also a Deluxe Brazilian Body Wash, priced at £3.99.

Some items are exclusive to the calendar and not available in stores, including a Double-Headed Jade Facial Roller, Folding Hair Brush With Mirror, and Wide Angle Eyelash Curler.

Behind one of the 24 doors, shoppers will also find a £20 voucher to spend on Lidl’s wider Cien beauty range.

## When will the Lidl beauty advent calendar release?

This limited-edition calendar will be sold on a first-come, first-served basis for £10 from 10am on Thursday, November 19, on the Lidl website.

The calendar measures 95cm by 35cm by 52cm and weighs over 12kg.

## Lidl launches new Deluxe Beauty collection

The news of the Lidl beauty advent calendar follows a recent announcement from the supermarket that it has launched a Deluxe Beauty collection.

In a statement, Lidl shared: "Beauty-savvy shoppers should have their baskets at the ready as Lidl’s new selection of beauty products land in store from 13th November, available while stocks last.

"These cream of the crop beauty bargains start at just £2.99 and come at the perfect time, with searches for skincare steadily rising over the past month."
