## It's Christmas! Well almost - look who was photographed in York

Noddy Holder in The Shambles, York. *(Image: Suzan Holder)*

It wouldn't be Christmas without Slade and Noddy Holder.

And look who has been photographed in The Shambles but the great man himself.

His wife Suzan has posted on social media: "Noddy Holder found a quiet moment to do some early Christmas shopping on The Shambles in York.

She added: "He’s more scared of being trapped in crowded stores once THAT song starts playing than he is of the haunted city’s famous ghosts."

Suzan is an author and journalist, who writes a column for Cheshire Life.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21720340.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Suzan Holder *(Image: Suzan Holder)*

She married the now 80-year-old Slade singer in April 2004, some 15 years after first meeting him. They have a son together named Django. Suzan is Noddy's second wife. He has two daughters, Jessica and Charisse, to his first wife Jessica Russell. They were wed from 1976 to 1984.

Wallsall-born Neville John Holder was the lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist of the English rock band Slade, one of the UK's most successful acts of the 1970s. He left the band in 1992, and diversified into television and radio work, notably starring in the ITV comedy-drama series The Grimleys.

The famous Slade single "Merry Christmas Everbody" was firest released in 1973 and became the Christmas Number One that year. It has featured in the charts often since then.

Fans responded with glee to seeing the photo, with many saying Noddy looked 'dapper' and is a 'legend.'

One said he was "the ghost of Christmas Past", and reflecting on the longevity of Slade's longtime Christmas classic, wife Suzan replied: "and Christmas Present AND Christmas Future !"
