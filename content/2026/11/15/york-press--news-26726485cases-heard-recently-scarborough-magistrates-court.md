Three violent men and a drug driver were among defendants sentenced recently at Scarborough Magistrates Court.

George Haylett, 27, of Blueberry Way, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to using threatening or abusive words or behaviour.  
He was given a prison sentence of 17 weeks and two days suspended for 18 months with probation supervision and on condition he wears an alcohol abstinence tag for 120 hours.  
He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Paul Owens, 45, of Overdale, Eastfield, pleaded guilty to cocaine driving and driving with a cocaine breakdown chemical in his body, committed on the same occasion.  
He was banned from driving for three years, fined £120 and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £48 statutory surcharge.  
Christopher Pashby, 44, of St Sepulchre Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to causing grievous bodily harm.  
He was given a 12-month community order with 40 hours’ unpaid work and ordered to pay £100 compensation, £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.  
Ben Petruska, 32, of St Sepulchre Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to criminal damage to a police van and assaulting a police officer.  
He was given a 10-week prison sentence suspended for 12 months with supervision.  
He was ordered to pay £50 compensation to the police officer.
