## Why is I'm a Celebrity not airing today?

In short, the answer is a very simple one, with ITV broadcasting the Nations League fixture between Spain and England today instead of *I'm a Celebrity*.

Kick-off is at 7.45pm GMT, with coverage set to overlap with the usual 9pm slot that *I’m a Celebrity* traditionally occupies.

So why not simply move the football or *I’m a Celebrity* over to ITV2? Well, both are must-see TV, and the delay will allow viewers to catch both events live.

The schedule interruption has seen ITV opt to move *I’m a Celebrity* to Monday evening, with an hour-and-35-minute episode airing from 9pm until 10.35pm.
