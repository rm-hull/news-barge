Two people from York have been killed in a crash in Leeds.

It happened in the early hours of this morning (Sunday).

At 12.44am a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a West Yorkshire Police vehicle in Gelderd Road.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crash-gelderd-road-leeds-15-nov-2026-pa-1.webp)

Police gave chase. During the pursuit, the BMW collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, was sadly pronounced dead at the scene.

A passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman from York, was sadly pronounced dead at hospital a short time later.

A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis in relation to the incident and remains in custody.

#### Crash location

Three other occupants of the BMW, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, were taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life threatening.

They have each been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A scene and road closure remain in place in Gelderd Road, and drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.

“A referral has been made to the Independent Office for Police Conduct which has declared an independent investigation.”

The junction where the crash took place is surrounded by several car dealerships.

Pictures shared on social media overnight appeared to show an area around a segment of Gelderd Road cordoned off, with a fire engine and several police cars and ambulance vehicles in attendance.

The pictures also appeared to show a dark car a short distance away from a very badly damaged white car just outside used car dealership Players Cars on Gelderd Road.

Gelderd Road remains closed and drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.

Anyone who witnessed the collision or saw either vehicle in the time leading up to it, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260649342.
