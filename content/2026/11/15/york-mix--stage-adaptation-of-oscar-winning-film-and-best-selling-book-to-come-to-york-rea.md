The first UK tour of the Royal Shakespeare Company and Neal Street’s acclaimed stage production of *Hamnet* has been announced – and it’s coming to York.

Based on Maggie O’Farrell’s best-selling novel, now also an Oscar-winning film, *Hamnet* has been adapted for the stage by award-winning playwright Lolita Chakrabarti.

This powerful story is a love letter to passion, birth, grief and the magic of nature and the production will play at Grand Opera House York from **Tuesday 1 to Saturday 5 February 2028**.

Directed by former RSC deputy and acting artistic director Erica Whyman and featuring set design by Tom Piper, *Hamnet* premiered at the Swan Theatre in Stratford-upon-Avon, breaking box office records before transferring to the West End and opening in 2023 to the biggest advance at the time recorded in the Garrick Theatre’s history.

Now audiences across the UK can experience this critically acclaimed play which pulls back the curtain on the story of the greatest writer in the English language and the woman who was the constant presence and purpose of his life.

Warwickshire, 1582. Agnes Hathaway, a natural healer, meets the Latin tutor, William Shakespeare. Drawn together by powerful but hidden impulses, they create a life together and make a family.

When the plague steals 11-year-old Hamnet from his loving parents, they must each confront their loss alone. And yet, out of the greatest suffering, something of extraordinary wonder is born.

Maggie O’Farrell said: “It’s wonderful to know that the RSC/Neal Street production of *Hamnet* is embarking on a tour of the UK. The production goes from strength to strength, and it’s lovely that more people will get to see it.”

Lolita Chakrabati said: “I am so thrilled that *Hamnet* is returning to the stage here in Britain. I was very aware of the enormous responsibility of making these historical figures relatable and to make them live again.

“This is a story about family – an eleven year old boy and the people around him. It has been an absolute privilege to imagine Shakespeare himself, his wife, mother, father, sister, daughters and his only son. I am very excited to bring this tale of love, loss and rebirth to so many cities in the UK.”
