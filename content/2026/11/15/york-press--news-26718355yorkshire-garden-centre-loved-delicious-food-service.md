Many people love taking a trip to a garden centre whether that is to pick up some new flowers to decorate their garden with or to enjoy the onsite café.

Those living in Yorkshire are lucky to have a few garden centres to choose from when searching for plants and gardening equipment.

The Thirsk Garden Centre not only has plenty of gardening equipment but also a cosy coffee shop that offers "delicious food".

## What is Thirsk Garden Centre like?

Thirsk Garden Centre has plenty of outdoor plants and gardening equipment to help you create your ideal garden.

If you fancy growing your own fruit and vegetables, you can pick up a huge variety of seeds to set up your own allotment.

You can find bird feeders and feed to help attract more wildlife to your garden.

If you need to create somewhere to relax in your garden, the garden centre also has a range of furniture to help you build a peaceful space.

To brighten up your indoor spaces, Thirsk Garden Centre also offers lots of houseplants to suit any home.

After exploring the garden centre, you can visit the coffee shop to relax and enjoy a light lunch.

The coffee shop offers different hot drinks as well as breakfasts like eggs Benedict and pancakes with a choice of toppings.

For lunch, you can grab a range of sandwiches, toasties, pies, quiches, and even pizzas if you need something more filling.
