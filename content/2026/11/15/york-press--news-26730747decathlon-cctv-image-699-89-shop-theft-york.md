Police have released a CCTV image as part of a shop theft investigation.

The incident took place at the Decathlon store in Monks Cross, York, on Thursday, October 1, and involved the theft of goods worth £699.89.

Officers are appealing for information and want to speak to the man pictured, as he may have information that could assist their investigation.

Anyone who recognises him is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Information can also be provided via the force’s website by selecting 'Tell us about,' then 'An existing case or report.'

Alternatively, call 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Those providing information are asked to quote reference number 12260196560.
