* The road remains closed, as reported by AA Roadwatch just before 7am today (Sun). There are no reports of any injuries from the incident, which was reported just before 7pm on Saturday night.

The road remained closed both ways at 3.50pm between the Thirkleby turning and The Avenue due to police crash investigation work.

The road remained closed at 2.30pm.

A 20-year-old man has died following a collision between his car and a coach in North Yorkshire.

Anyone with any information is asked to email [SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:SCIU@northyorkshire.police.uk)

Or call 101 and pass information for incident number 12260224121

North Yorkshire Police added: "The road was closed to allow emergency services to work at the scene, for collision investigators to examine the scene and recovery of the vehicles. At the time of writing, emergency services remain at the scene pending recovery of the coach.

"There were no serious injuries reported by any occupants of the coach."

The road still remains closed. The Press has approached North Yorkshire Police for an update.

The road remains closed to traffic as of 10.30am.

In the comments below the North Yorkshire Police Facebook post, one person said: “We just passed it and air ambo landed on the road and there’s so many police cars, fire engines and ambulances I hope they are all ok.”

North Yorkshire Police posted on social media last night: “Emergency services are at the scene of a serious collision on the A19 (York Road) near Thirkleby in the Thirsk area.

“The road is currently closed between the turn-offs to Bagby and Hutton Sessay.

“Motorists are asked to avoid the area and find an alternative route.

“The road is expected to remain closed for some time.

“Thank you for your

and patience while emergency services work at the scene.”

North Yorkshire Police gave no details of any injuries.

The air ambulance, plus fire crews from Easingwold, Northallerton and Ripon were called out to the scene on the A19 at Hutton Sessay between Easingwold and Northallerton at 6.49pm.

The crash, between a bus and a car, took place shortly before 7pm on Saturday night on the A19 between Thirsk and Easingwold at Hutton Sessay.

The Press will be giving further updates when we can.
