For the next six weeks, we will hear his voice rasping “It’s Christmas!” in almost every store.

And now Noddy Holder himself has been in some of those York stores to do some festive shopping.

Noddy, lead singer of Slade, creators of the annual favourite *Merry Xmas Everybody*, was photographed on Shambles by his wife Suzan.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/noddy-holder-shambles-insta-full.webp)

Suzan shared it today on her Instagram feed, with the words: “Noddy Holder found a quiet moment to do some early Christmas shopping on The Shambles in York.

“(He’s more scared of being trapped in crowded stores once THAT song starts playing than he is of the haunted city’s famous ghosts).”

The post got a lot of love for Noddy, now 80. “Such a fabulous photo Noddy – have already started playing it in York Designer Dress Agency,” said the team from the Fossgate shop.

Another said: “My daughter served him breakfast at the hotel! She knows I’m a fan!”

A third wrote: “My first Christmas single as a 8 year old. Will always be my favourite. Must have been very early to catch The Shambles like that – us locals don’t go anywhere near come November.”

And another added: “If Noddy is so scared, then come 1 December, all he has to do is shout those immortal words: “ITS CHRISTMAS! ” If that don’t frighten the ghosts, then nothing will…”
