A California man who killed a father of five showed zero remorse at his sentencing hearing, telling the victim’s family, “I’d do it a million times.”

William Carney, 39, was fatally shot at a gas station in Fresno in October 2023, and weeks later, Damien Murphy was arrested for his murder.

Murphy was sentenced to 32 years in prison Thursday after pleading no contest to voluntary manslaughter in September to avoid a first-degree murder conviction and a longer sentence, according to local reports.

"I hope every second of your 32 years, you think about William and his family and the damage you've caused," said the judge presiding over the hearing, according to local outlet KFSN.

![Damien Murphy, a California man who killed a father of five, has shown no remorse at his sentencing hearing, telling the victim’s family, ‘I’d do it a million times’](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/15/22/41/3b82568a-0a88-40fc-b174-825fa78d0457-DamienMurphy.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The judge then gave Murphy a chance to address the court, but he refused to apologize to Carney’s family.

"I would apologize, but I'm not going to apologize for protecting my family. I'd do it a million times," he said.

It’s unclear what exactly led to the fatal shooting, but it was supposedly linked to a confrontation between the victim and Murphy’s family, local outlet KMPH reported in November 2023.

"I hope you change your mind and have some remorse for your actions, and don't be disrespectful to the people you murdered and hurt their families," the judge said in response to Murphy’s chilling confession.

![William Carney, 39, was fatally shot at a gas station in Fresno in October 2023, and weeks later, Murphy was arrested for his murder](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/15/22/43/e9b773c5-114e-451d-b8c6-549675d8ddfc-WilliamCarney.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Heartbroken members of Carney’s family spoke at Thursday’s hearing about his death, which they said happened less than a month before his 40th birthday.

"It was like all my dreams died at once," Carney’s daughter told the court. "My dad won't ever see me on prom day. He can never walk me down the aisle on my wedding day and cry, seeing how much I've grown.”

Carney’s mother said her life “simply no longer holds the happiness it once had. The joy that used to come naturally has been replaced by grief."

"I can't hate you because I know you're suffering more than he is, and that brings me peace of mind," one of Carney’s childhood friends told Murphy.
