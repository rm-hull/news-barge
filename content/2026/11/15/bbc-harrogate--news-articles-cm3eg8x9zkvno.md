Four arrested after two killed in Leeds crash

**Two people have been killed after their car was hit by a vehicle fleeing from police in Leeds.**

West Yorkshire Police said it happened at 00:44 GMT, when a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for officers in Gelderd Road and then crashed into a white Volkswagen Scirocco.

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man, died at the scene. His passenger, a woman, 23, died later in hospital. Both were from York.

The occupants of the BMW, a 39-year-old man from Harrogate, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, have been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

The 39-year-old is also being held on suspicion of driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis, the force confirmed.

The teenagers in the BMW were taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life-threatening.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said a referral had been made to the Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC), which confirmed it had started an investigation.

Officers are appealing for anyone who witnessed the crash to get in touch

![A white car which has been hit by a car in Leeds](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/9f61/live/0bb78600-e0e7-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.png)

The crash happened in the early hours of Sunday morning

A spokesperson for the IOPC said it had launched an independent investigation at about 02:30 and sent investigators to the crash scene to "begin gathering evidence".

The spokesperson said that inquiries were "at a very early stage".

A police scene and road closure remain in place in Gelderd Road, and drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes. A steady stream of cars is being directed by officers to turn around at the cordon.

The area is made up of business units and car dealerships and was very quiet on this cold Sunday morning, with very few people about.

A dark car can be seen a short distance away from a very badly damaged white Volkswagen just outside a used car dealership.

Anyone who saw the vehicles before or during the crash and may have information or footage is urged to contact police.
