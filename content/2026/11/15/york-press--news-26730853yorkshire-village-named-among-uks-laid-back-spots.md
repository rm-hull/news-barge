## Yorkshire Dales village named one of the most-laid back places in the UK

The village is ideal for exploring the rest of the Yorkshire Dales. *(Image: Getty)*

If you enjoy living life slowly away from busy cities, you might be thinking of moving out to one of the UK’s many tiny towns and villages.

World Atlas has released a list of the 11 most laid-back towns and villages across the UK.

The list features coastal spots for a bit of relaxation and countryside escapes where you can enjoy long hikes.

Found in the Yorkshire Dales, the village of Horton-in-Ribblesdale made it onto the list thanks to it being a great base for walking.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21706682.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The village has a few amenities including two pubs. *(Image: Getty)*

## What is Horton-in-Ribblesdale like?

On the village, World Atlas said: “Sitting near the famed Yorkshire Three Peaks of Yorkshire Dales National Park, Horton-in-Ribblesdale is a base for hill walking in the North of England.

“Traditionally, the town marks the starting point of the Three Peaks Walk that provides a combined 1,585 metres, or 5,200 feet, of ascent while traversing the mountains of Pen-y-ghent, Ingleborough and Whernside.

“A less strenuous walk into the wilderness can be found along the Hull Pot path, which leads up the western side of Pen-y-ghent and reveals a massive collapsed cavern.

“When the itinerary calls for refreshment and refueling, the Blindbeck Tea Room is a good place to stop for a bite.

“And just down the road from Blindbeck, The Rowe House is a country house with a small campsite for tents, as well as en suite rooms.”

If you want to move to Horton-in-Ribblesdale, the average price of a house in the area was £360,000 over the last year, according to Rightmove.

The majority of properties sold were detached houses selling for an average of £565,000.

Semi-detached properties sold for around £155,000.

There are plenty of walking and mountain biking routes you can complete that will take you to places like Crummackdale, Clapham, and Ingleborough.

If you need to warm up somewhere, you can visit the Blindbeck Tea Room, which has a rating of 4.4 out of five on Tripadvisor.

Visitors love the cake and coffee on offer at the tea room, with many saying they receive a “very warm welcome” from the staff.

The village has two pubs, The Crown and The Golden Lion, that are ideal for a post-hike pint.
