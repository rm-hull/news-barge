Firefighters rescued four people who were stuck in a York lift last night (Sat).

A crew from York was called out to a building at 10.23pm.




* **Air ambulance and others respond to A19 North Yorkshire RTC**
* **Emergency services respond to two-vehicle collision in Kexby**
* **M62 westbound closed near Goole and Howden due to incident**

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue posted today (Sunday)): "All persons were released using a mechanism in the lift room."

No further details, included where the lift was, were given.
