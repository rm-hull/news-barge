## Police watchdog probe after 2 people die in serious crash

The scene of the fatal crash on Gelderd Road, Leeds *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis / PA)*

The police watchdog has launched an investigation after two people from York died in a crash.

An IOPC investigation is now underway after the pair from York died in Morley in Leeds not far from IKEA in the early hours of Sunday (November 15).

A chase began at 12.44am after a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a police vehicle on Gelderd Road in Leeds, West Yorkshire Police said.

The BMW then collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21719820.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene of the fatal crash on Gelderd Road, Leeds *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis / PA)*

The Volkswagen’s 22-year-old driver from York died at the scene, while its 23-year-old female passenger from York died in hospital shortly afterwards, the force said.

A 39-year-old man from Harrogate was arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving over the prescribed cannabis limit. He remains in custody.

The BMW’s other three occupants—boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl—were taken to hospital with non-life-threatening injuries.

All three were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

West Yorkshire Police has referred itself to the Independent Office for Police Conduct, which has declared an independent investigation.

A spokesperson for the IOPC said: “We have started an independent investigation after a fatal road traffic collision in Leeds following a pursuit involving West Yorkshire Police.”

They added: “After we were notified by West Yorkshire Police, we declared an independent investigation at around 2.30am and sent investigators to the scene and police post-incident procedures to begin gathering evidence.

“Our inquiries are at a very early stage.”

In a full statement, a spokesperson for Greater Manchester Police said: "Officers were responding to a theft of a motor vehicle from Wilson Avenue in Swinton at 10.33am yesterday morning (November 14) .

"Through the help of NPAS the offending vehicle was sighted on M60 and located by Officer from our Tactical Aid Unit and the offending vehicle failed to stop.

"Officers pursued the vehicle on to the M62 working alongside officers from West Yorkshire Police.

"The offending vehicle then collided with GMP TAU vehicles, between Junction 26 and Junction 27 on the M62, causing damage to them.

"No serious injuries have been recorded and a man aged 18 was arrested on suspicion of theft of a motor vehicle at 12;30pm."

The junction where the crash took place is surrounded by several car dealerships.

Pictures shared on social media overnight appeared to show part of Gelderd Road cordoned off, with a fire engine, police cars and ambulances at the scene.

The pictures also appeared to show a dark car near a badly damaged white car outside the Players Cars dealership on Gelderd Road.

A cordon remained in place on Sunday morning, with several officers inspecting the damaged cars.

Gelderd Road remains closed, with drivers urged to avoid the area and find another route.

Anyone with information or dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage is urged to contact police on 101 or online, quoting reference 13260649342.

Police are also appealing for witnesses who saw either vehicle in the time leading up to the collision.
