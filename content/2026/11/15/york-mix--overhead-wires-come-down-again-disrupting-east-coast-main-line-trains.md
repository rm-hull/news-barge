There are long delays between **Newcastle** and**Edinburgh** due to damage to the overhead electrical wires.

LNER says: “We **strongly advise you to avoid travelling north of Newcastle today.”** This also affects CrossCountry No trains will run direct between these stations for the rest of the day, and you may be heavily delayed and/or experience severe overcrowding on any remaining available services.

A limited rail replacement bus service is running between **Newcastle** and**Edinburgh.** Staff have confirmed the damage to the overhead wires at Drem Junction, and that lines in both directions are shut.**Advice for customers who have already commenced their rail journey**

* **If you are on board an LNER service** affected by this disruption, your Train Manager will keep you updated with information about the incident and the impact on your journey today.
* **If you are currently waiting at a station**, please listen out for station announcements or speak to a member of station staff.
* **If you have commenced your journey, and you’re unable to complete your journey** between Newcastle and Edinburgh today, or disruption causes you to miss your last connection, you can arrange alternate transport or accommodation and claim the cost back from LNER via the customer services team.**You can use your ticket on the following train companies at no extra cost:**

* **Avanti West Coast** between London Euston, Manchester, Glasgow Central and Edinburgh.
* **TransPennine Express** between Manchester, Leeds, York and Newcastle.
* **ScotRail** between Glasgow Central and Edinburgh Waverley.
* **Northern** between Carlisle and Newcastle.
