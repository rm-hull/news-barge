*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A major North Yorkshire road remains closed today (Sunday) following a collision between a bus and a car, which took place shortly before 7pm last night.

The air ambulance, plus fire crews from Easingwold, Northallerton and Ripon were called out to the scene on the A19 at Hutton Sessay between Easingwold and Northallerton at 6.49pm.

AA Roadwatch said at 6.51am today: “Road closed due to serious accident on A19 Both ways between Thirkleby turning and The Avenue.”

North Yorkshire Police posted on social media last night: “Emergency services are at the scene of a serious collision on the A19 (York Road) near Thirkleby in the Thirsk area.




* **York Christmas market - armed police to patrol crowds**
* **Emergency services respond to two-vehicle collision in Kexby**
* **M62 westbound closed near Goole and Howden due to incident**

“The road is currently closed between the turn-offs to Bagby and Hutton Sessay.

“Motorists are asked to avoid the area and find an alternative route.

“The road is expected to remain closed for some time.

“Thank you for your understanding and patience while emergency services work at the scene.”

North Yorkshire Police gave no details of any injuries.

However, in the comments below on their Facebook post, one person said: “We just passed it and air ambo landed on the road and there’s so many police cars, fire engines and ambulances I hope they are all ok.”

Another person said: “There was a coach in the field with people walking around everywhere round the field. Plus all emergency services and air ambulance.”
