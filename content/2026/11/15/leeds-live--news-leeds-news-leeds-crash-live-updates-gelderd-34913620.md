A major road in Leeds has been shut after two people lost their lives in a crash.

The collision came after a BMW crashed into a white VW Scirocco while police were in pursuit of the BMW. The crash happened in Gelderd Road at 12.44am on Sunday morning (November 15).

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, was sadly pronounced dead at the scene, and a passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman from York, died at hospital a short time later.

A62 Gelderd Road is still shut in both directions. INRIX says: "A62 Gelderd Road in both directions closed due to accident from The Boulevard to A58 Whitehall Road. Note change of location. Sensors show that the closure has shortened."

### Double fatal

West Yorkshire Police:

> We are appealing for witnesses following a road traffic collision in Leeds which has tragically resulted in the deaths of two people.At 12.44am this morning a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a police vehicle in Gelderd Road.A pursuit was commenced and the BMW was involved in a collision with a white Volkswagen Scirocco which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, was sadly pronounced deceased at the scene, and a passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman from York, was sadly pronounced deceased at hospital a short time later.A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis in relation to the incident and remains in custody.Three other occupants of the BMW, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, were taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life threatening. They have each been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.A scene and road closure remain in place in Gelderd Road, and drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.A referral has been made to the Independent Office for Police Conduct which has declared an independent investigation.Anyone who witnessed the collision or saw either vehicle in the time leading up to it, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 13260649342.
