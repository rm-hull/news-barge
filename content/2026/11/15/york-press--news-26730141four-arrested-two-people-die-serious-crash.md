## York man and woman die in crash - 4 arrested

A police cordon near to the scene on Gelderd Road, Leeds *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis/PA)*

A MAN and a woman from York have died in a serious crash following a police pursuit.

The crash happened at 12.44am this morning (November 15) in Gelderd Road, Leeds , not far from the IKEA store after a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a police vehicle, prompting a pursuit.

The BMW then collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

A 22-year-old man from York, who was driving the Scirocco, died at the scene.

A 23-year-old woman, also from York and a passenger in the vehicle, was taken to hospital but later died from her injuries.

A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21719819.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A recovery vehicle lifting a car at the scene on Gelderd Road, Leeds, where two people have died and four people have been arrested after a BMW that was being pursued by police collided with a Volkswagen in the early hours of Sunday morning, West *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis/PA)*

He remains in custody.

Three other occupants of the BMW, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, were taken to hospital with injuries not considered life-threatening.

They have also been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

West Yorkshire Police have referred the incident to the Independent Office for Police Conduct, which has declared an independent investigation.

Officers are now appealing for witnesses and information.

A police spokesman said: "Anyone who witnessed the collision or saw either vehicle in the time leading up to it, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online via the Live Chat facility on our website quoting reference 13260649342."

A road closure remains in place in Gelderd Road.

Drivers are advised to avoid the area and seek alternative routes.
