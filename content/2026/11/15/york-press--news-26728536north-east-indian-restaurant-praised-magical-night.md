## 'Unique' and 'magical' experience that ends at 'sublime' Indian restaurant

A curry will be waiting for you at the end of your train ride. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A “unique” experience in the North East has been praised for offering a “magical night out”.

If you fancy enjoying some Indian food but want a bit of a different experience from your usual restaurant, you might want to try the Curry Train.

The Valley has a few restaurants around the North East but is best known for its Curry Train ride.

## What is the Curry Train like?

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21714884.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Valley is highly recommended. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The Valley’s Curry Train takes passengers from Newcastle Central Station to the Valley restaurant in Corbridge.

The train runs along the Tyne Valley line and makes a few stops at other stations during the journey.

On the train, you can enjoy the views of the countryside while relaxing with a drink.

Your order is taken on the train and will be ready for your arrival in Corbridge.

You can choose any starter and main course, which is served with naan, rice, and the chef’s choice of vegetables.

The dinner is followed by a dessert and coffee with chocolates.

The menu is packed with traditional curries, from dhansak and vindaloo to karai and dopiaza.

There are also plenty of vegetarian specials and side dishes to make sure everyone is catered for.

To make the evening more special, you might want to go for one of the chef’s recommendations like the salmon bhaja or beef adrok.

### What do reviews say about the Curry Train?

The Valley has a rating of 4.1 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with over 500 people leaving reviews after their trip.

One person said: “We did the curry train from Newcastle out here as a group of 9 with mixed ages including children.

“We got met at the train station and everything explained to us as well as being given a menu to begin looking at our options.

“We ordered once on the train and a small amount of drinks were offered on the train too.

“We arrived at the restaurant and our food was quickly ready.

“It was delicious and really varied.

“The staff were quick with advice and suggestions of if asked.

“They were also very attentive and kind.

“It was a birthday celebration and they made it a lovely occasion singing happy birthday etc.

“Really beautiful curries and puddings.

“Would absolutely recommend and a fun and delicious evening out.”

Another person said: “Possibly our favourite Indian restaurant in the country, the setting is sublime and the curry train concept unique.

“The menu is extremely original and the beef dishes we've sampled the best we've ever had.

“Fabulous setting with every detail thought through.

“The service is beyond compare and staff so diligent and professional.

“Always a magical night out and we visit often.”
