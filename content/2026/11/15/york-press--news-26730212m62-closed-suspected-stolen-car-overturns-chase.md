## Motorway in Yorkshire closed after stolen car overturns during police chase

A suspected stolen car overturned during a pursuit on the M62 in West Yorkshire on Saturday *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A motorway was closed after a police chase ended in a dramatic crash.

A suspected stolen car overturned during a pursuit on the M62 in West Yorkshire on Saturday, prompting the closure of the eastbound carriageway between the Chain Bar junction 26 near Cleckheaton and junction 27 near Birstall.

The incident reportedly began in Swinton, Salford, around 10.30am, when Greater Manchester Police (GMP) responded to reports of a vehicle theft on Wilson Avenue.

A GMP spokesman said: "The driver failed to stop for officers, prompting a pursuit which continued onto the M62, with assistance from West Yorkshire Police.

"Between junctions 26 and 27 of the M62, the vehicle collided with GMP tactical aid unit vehicles, causing damage to the police vehicles."

One person was taken to hospital with injuries not thought to be life-changing.

An 18-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of stealing a motor vehicle and remains in custody.

The M62 eastbound was closed to allow emergency services to recover the vehicle and clear the scene, causing significant delays and congestion in the area.

National Highways said the closures were lifted shortly after 3.30pm, although delays of up to 30 minutes remained as traffic began to ease.

The westbound carriageway remained open throughout the incident, though the eastbound side saw queues of up to three miles and delays of around 25 minutes during the closure.

A National Police Air Service helicopter helped track the vehicle from the M60 before the chase continued onto the M62, involving both forces.
