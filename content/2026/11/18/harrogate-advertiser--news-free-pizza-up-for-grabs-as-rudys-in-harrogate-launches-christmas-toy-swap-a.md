A popular pizzeria in Harrogate is inviting customers to take part in its festive toy swap for the first time this Christmas, giving them the chance to enjoy a free pizza while helping to spread some seasonal cheer to children in need.

Rudy’s Pizza Napoletana, located on John Street, is joining the brand’s much-loved festive toy swap initiative in partnership with Cash for Kids.

The Harrogate restaurant, which opened earlier this year, will be taking part in the nationwide campaign for the first time, with customers invited to get involved from Monday 7 till Thursday 10 December, between 12pm and 5pm.

Guests are invited to bring along a new, unopened toy worth at least £10 and, in return, they can swap it for a free pizza of their choice.

The festive scheme aims to bring a little extra Christmas magic to local families who may otherwise struggle during the festive period, while rewarding customers for their generosity with one of Rudy’s signature Neapolitan pizzas.

Since launching the initiative in 2024, Rudy’s has collected more than 10,000 toys, helping to make Christmas brighter for children and families across the country.

This year, the team hopes to build on that success and collect even more gifts.

Neal Bates, from Rudy’s Pizza Napoletana, said: “We’re excited to bring back our festive toy swap with Cash for Kids this Christmas.

“Last year our guests were incredibly generous and we’d love to see even more people get involved this time around.

“It’s such a simple idea, but one that can make a huge difference to children who might otherwise go without.

“We love that we can bring people together over a brilliant pizza while spreading a little festive cheer and giving something back to the local communities where Rudy’s has found a home.”

Alongside the return of its toy swap, Rudy’s is also celebrating the festive season with a limited-edition Christmas menu featuring three new pizzas.

Available from November 23, the seasonal menu combines traditional festive flavours with Rudy’s famous Neapolitan-style pizza-making.

The new creations include **Zucca alla Capuano**,** Gamberetti**and** Campagnola**, each offering a different seasonal twist for diners looking to try something new over the Christmas period.
