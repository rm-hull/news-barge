An 80-year-old lollipop man who has become a much-loved figure in the Harrogate community is hanging up his stop sign after 11 years of dedicated service.

Bob O’Neill has been a familiar face at Richard Taylor Church of England Primary School, helping pupils safely cross the road each day on their journey to and from school.

He will carry out his final crossing patrol duty on Friday, November 27, bringing to an end a much-loved chapter in the school’s history.

Throughout his time at the school, Bob has become a cherished member of the community, known for his friendly smile, cheerful greetings and genuine care for the children he helps each day.

Despite reaching the age of 80, Bob has continued to carry out his role with enthusiasm and dedication, providing a reassuring presence for pupils, parents and staff whatever the weather.

Many children have known Bob throughout their time at Richard Taylor Church of England Primary School, with his daily conversations and warm personality making him much more than a crossing patrol officer.

He has become a familiar and valued part of school life.

A spokesperson for Richard Taylor Church of England Primary School said: “Bob has been a constant and reassuring presence for our children and families for more than a decade.

“His commitment to keeping our pupils safe, whatever the weather, has been truly remarkable.

“He is hugely respected and appreciated by staff, parents and pupils alike and will be greatly missed.

“We would like to thank him for his years of dedicated service and wish him a long, happy and well-earned retirement.”

As Bob prepares to enjoy his retirement, pupils, families and staff at Richard Taylor Church of England Primary School have shared their thanks for the important role he has played over the years.

After more than a decade of helping children safely cross the road, Bob leaves behind many happy memories and a lasting legacy as one of the school’s most recognisable and respected faces.
