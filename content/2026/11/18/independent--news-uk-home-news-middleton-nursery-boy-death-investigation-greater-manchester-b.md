A three-year-old boy has died after an incident at a nursery in Greater Manchester.

Emergency services were called to Cosy Toes Nursery in Middleton at around 4pm on 2 November to reports of concern for a child. The boy was rushed to hospital, but died three days later on 5 November, Greater Manchester Police said.

An investigation has now been launched into the “tragic” circumstances surrounding his death “in line with usual procedure” when a child dies in a care setting, the force confirmed.

![Police were called to Cosy Toes Nursery in Middleton on 2 November](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/13/01/Cosy-Toes-Nursery-in-Mainway-East.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

It added no arrests have been made at this time.

In a statement, Greater Manchester Police said: “An investigation has been opened following the tragic death of a three-year-old boy in Middleton.

“Shortly before 4pm on Monday 2 November 2026, we were called to reports of a concern for a child at Cosy Toes Nursery in Mainway East. We attended alongside other emergency services and a three-year-old boy was rushed to hospital.

“Sadly, the boy passed away on Thursday 5 November. His family is being supported by specialist officers during this incredibly difficult time.

“As the circumstances leading up to the boy’s death occurred in a care setting, and in line with usual procedure, a police investigation has been opened. No arrests have been made at this time.”

Detective Chief Inspector Claire Brennan said: "This is an incredibly tragic development in a deeply upsetting incident and our thoughts remain with the boy's family.

"We are in the very early stages of our involvement and are carefully considering events and the evidence available to us. For now, we ask that the family's privacy is respected as they begin to navigate the unimaginable loss of their little boy."

Cosy Toes Nurseries said: “We are all heartbroken to hear of the tragic news of the child’s passing. He was a wonderful and beautiful child, and our thoughts and prayers are all with his family at this terrible time.

“We will of course give our fullest possible assistance in every way possible to the enquiries that must rightly follow this tragic event."

Anyone with information that could help officers is being asked to report online or call 0161 856 3635.

Details can also be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or by filling out a form on their website.
