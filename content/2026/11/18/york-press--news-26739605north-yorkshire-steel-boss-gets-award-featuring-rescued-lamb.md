## Rescued Ron the Ram features in award to lifesaving North Yorkshire businessman

Ron the Ram trophy *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A managing director in North Yorkshire has been honoured with a personalised award featuring a lamb he rescued and hand-reared.

Roy Fishwick, managing director of Thirsk-based Cleveland Steel & Tubes (CST), received the surprise tribute at a construction industry sustainability event for his work championing the circular economy and reusing structural steel in building projects.

The award features Ron the Ram, an orphaned lamb Mr Fishwick raised earlier this year at his homestead.

Thanks to Roy’s care, Ron has flourished – and become a popular fixture at CST’s head office in North Yorkshire, where he regularly visits. Ron has also become a cult figure online, thanks to Roy posting updates about him on LinkedIn.

Roy Fishwick said: "It was an honour to receive an award from my peers – and deeply touching that they personalised it in this way."

Presented by the Alliance for Sustainable Building Products (ASBP), the award recognises Mr Fishwick's long-standing efforts to promote the reuse of steel.

The ASBP is a cross-sector, not-for-profit organisation dedicated to accelerating the transition to a healthy, low-carbon, and circular built environment by promoting sustainable building products.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21732197.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Roy Fishwick receives his Ron the Ram trophy *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Simon Corbey and Katherine Adams from ASBP presented the award to Mr Fishwick during the Building Resilience Through Reuse conference at the British Library Knowledge Centre in London.

Mr Fishwick has worked with the ASBP for more than a decade and is a leading advocate for steel reuse.

He chairs the British Constructional Steelwork Association’s (BCSA) sustainability committee and has contributed to several Innovate UK projects focused on steel reuse and decarbonisation.

He also supported the Steel Construction Institute in developing standards and protocols for steel reuse, helping position the UK as a leader in this area.

As managing director of CST for more than 33 years, Mr Fishwick has helped establish the company as the UK’s largest steel stockholder and largest supplier of reused steel.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21732198.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Roy Fishwick with Ron the Ram at work *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Reused steel offers up to 97 per cent lower embodied carbon than new steel and is often more cost-effective.

Based in Dalton, CST supplies reused steel to major construction contractors and has contributed to iconic British projects including the 2012 Olympic Stadium, Wimbledon, and the London Eye.

The company has also expanded into supplying reused steel beams for residential projects such as self-builds and extensions.

Outside of work, Mr Fishwick runs a smallholding in the Yorkshire Dales, where he keeps livestock including sheep and chickens.

Ron the Ram remains in good health, and Mr Fishwick plans to breed from him in the future.
