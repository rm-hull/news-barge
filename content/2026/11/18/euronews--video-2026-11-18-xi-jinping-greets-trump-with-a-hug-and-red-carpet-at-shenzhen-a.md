Chinese President Xi Jinping greeted Donald Trump at Shenzhen airport on Wednesday as the US president arrived for the APEC summit.

After stepping off Air Force One, Trump saluted on a red carpet lined with people waving flags and pom-poms, before the two leaders shook hands and hugged.

Trump, Xi and Russian President Vladimir Putin are due to meet on the sidelines of the summit on Thursday.

He flew in from the Philippines, where he had attended a regional summit and met leaders from India, Japan and Australia.
