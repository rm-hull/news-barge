A Swedish council chief has been fired after starring in several pornographic films released while he was in the role, according to a report.

Lars Eriksson appeared in and produced multiple films since 2018, the latest of which were released in 2025 and 2026 while he served as the head of the Jokkmokk municipality, Swedish outlet SVT Nyheter said. His previous activities in the adult film industry sparked fears of a security risk.

“It is extremely important that a senior executive cannot be subjected to blackmail,” municipal councillor Roland Boman told the outlet.

Eriksson, formerly the Social Democratic member of the Swedish parliament for Västmanland, was fired in October before a formal agreement for his departure was agreed earlier this month.

Eriksson insisted he has not been involved in the pornographic industry since 2022, before he entered local politics. He argued he had no control over when the films were published, and that he did not operate the X account, which was instead run by the film’s distributor.

“Given that this is a legal, albeit taboo, activity that ended more than two years before I became eligible for the municipal service, I find it difficult to see any impact,” the politician in his mid-50s said in response to the allegations.

Eriksson also denies that the termination of his employment is as a result of the SVT’s reporting on his pornographic films.

“We have been having these discussions for a certain time, which you yourself have noticed, and it has nothing to do with your reporting,” he told the outlet.

![Jokkmokk is a municipality in Swedish Lapland (file photo)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2025/10/23/15/41/iStock-2167457067.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

His pornographic films were marketed on an X account using the pseudonym ‘Hans’ until October this year, local media reported.

Experts said the emergence of pornographic material could have left Eriksson vulnerable to blackmail, a claim he denies.

Administrative law professor Olle Lundin, of Uppsala University, pointed out Jokkmokk’s importance for hydropower and Swedish army activity.

“The municipality as such is of great interest to foreign powers, by which I mean primarily Russia,” he said. “Having such a vulnerability factor in a municipality of heavy security policy interest is not optimal, but something that the municipality would need to plug and clean up.”

Boman said Eriksson has “involved in meetings with the Armed Forces”, and that even though sensitive material was not discussed, “parts we do not consider serious may be important pieces of the puzzle for foreign powers”.

Eriksson argued that by openly answering the questions posed to him by SVT that blackmail is rendered “pointless”.

The politician will be dismissed with seven months’ severance pay, reportedly totalling 594,104 Swedish Krona (£44,452).
