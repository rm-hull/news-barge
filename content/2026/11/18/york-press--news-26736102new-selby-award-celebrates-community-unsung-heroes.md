A new award scheme is set to shine a light on unsung heroes who help bring people together.

Keir Mather MP has launched the Community Connector award to recognise individuals and groups who offer an outstanding contribution to their local area to prevent and reduce loneliness and social isolation.

Mr Mather said: "I want to raise awareness of loneliness and social isolation in our area.

"That means shining a light on the serious problems we face, but I also want to recognise the amazing people, community groups, and organisations in our area who already do such great work in bringing people together and building resilience."

Nominations can be sent by email to [keir.mather.mp@parliament.uk](mailto:keir.mather.mp@parliament.uk) and should include the nominee's details and postal address.

David Skaith, mayor of York and North Yorkshire, said: "People need to feel a sense of belonging and connection to their local area, the best way to do that is by creating spaces where people can come together."

Research by the New Economics Foundation estimates that chronic loneliness costs UK businesses around £2.5 billion each year.

Evidence shows that proactive community interventions to boost social connection and participation can reduce loneliness and improve quality of life.
