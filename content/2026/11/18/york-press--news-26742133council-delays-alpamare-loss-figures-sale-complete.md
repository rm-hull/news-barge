## Answers demanded over 'Alpamare losses' ahead of sale

QUESTIONS have been raised about how much taxpayers have lost on Scarborough\'s Alpamare

QUESTIONS have been raised about how much taxpayers have lost on Scarborough's Alpamare waterpark amid continuing negotiations to sell the attraction to a new owner.

Tom Seston, Reform councillor for Eastfield, asked when elected members and the public would be told the full financial cost of what he described as the "Alpamare debacle".

The exchange comes as the authority has taken temporary control of the North Bay attraction while it works to complete the sale to a preferred purchaser.

"Can the council provide an update on the timescale in which members and the public will be able to find out exactly how much money we've lost on the Alpamare debacle?" he asked at a full meeting of the authority on Wednesday, November 18.

Responding, the council's executive member for finance and resources, Cllr Peter Wilkinson, said the information would not be released until the sale process had been completed.

He told councillors: "The proposed disposal of the Alpamare site has not yet been completed, and as previously advised to members, this is commercially sensitive.

"However, the council will provide a transparent account of the financial position once this transaction has been completed."

According to a recent council update, operational management of Alpamare transferred from Flamingo Land to the council on November 1.

The authority said it will act as a caretaker for the site until it can be handed over to its new owner.

The council recently confirmed that a preferred bidder had been identified following a competitive marketing exercise, although the identity of the purchaser has not been disclosed.

Councillors have previously been told that the buyer intends to carry out refurbishment and energy-efficiency improvements before reopening the attraction, with the aim of welcoming visitors back during the first quarter of 2027.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/17969797.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: NORTH YORKSHIRE COUNCIL)*

The future of Alpamare has remained the subject of intense scrutiny since the collapse of its original developer.

The attraction opened in 2016 with the support of a £9 million loan from the former Scarborough Borough Council.

After developer Benchmark Leisure Ltd entered administration in 2023, the council took possession of the site and subsequently wrote off most of the loan.

The waterpark later reopened under a management agreement with Flamingo Land, but that arrangement ended at the end of October.
