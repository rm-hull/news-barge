The M8 is open again following the closure of a stretch of the road after a double-decker bus caught fire during rush hour.

The fire occurred on the westbound carriageway of the motorway in Livingston between junctions two and three before 5pm on Tuesday afternoon.

Emergency services closed the motorway in both directions as firefighters tackled the blaze.

The Scottish Fire Service said two fire engines attended the scene just before 5pm. Crews extinguished the blaze and the last appliance left the scene just before 9pm. There were no reports of injuries, police officers have confirmed.

Police Scotland has since said the motorway has reopened, posting on social media: "The M8 has re-opened following a report of a bus on fire, around 4.55pm on Tuesday, 17 November, 2026.

"There were no reports of any injuries and the fire is not believed to be wilful.”

Motorists faced long delays during the incident with drivers diverting off the motorway onto nearby roads.

Traffic officers gradually reopened the road and the westbound carriageway had reopened by late on Tuesday evening, Traffic Scotland said.
