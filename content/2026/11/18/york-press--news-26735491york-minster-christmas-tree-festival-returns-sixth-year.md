A cathedral will be transformed this winter as more than 100 Christmas trees fill its historic interior.

York Minster will welcome visitors to its sixth annual Christmas Tree Festival, which will see the medieval building adorned with trees throughout the Chapter House, Nave and Lady Chapel.

The festival begins on November 28 and runs until January 5, with entry included in a standard sightseeing ticket.

Reverend Timothy Goode, Canon for Congregational Discipleship and Nurture at York Minster, said: "The Christmas Tree Festival has become a much-loved part of the York Minster year, and we are delighted to be making it bigger than ever before.

"For centuries, York Minster has stood at the heart of city life, and the festival is a wonderful opportunity to continue that tradition by bringing people together in the spirit of Christmas."

Visitors will be able to enjoy trees decorated in themes such as The Muppets Christmas Carol and The Nutcracker.

Other displays will honour loved ones or raise awareness of causes including homelessness and mental health.

Four prayer trees will also be on display, inviting visitors to write messages of hope and reflection for the season.

The festival launches with the 'Great Christmas Tree Switch-on Minster Mash-Up' at 10am on November 28.
