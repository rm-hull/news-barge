# Man in his 60s fighting for life after serious assault outside mosque as suspect arrested

## A man in his 60s has been left fighting for his life following a serious assault outside a mosque in Halifax.

Shortly before 5pm on Tuesday (Nov 17), police officers came across an injured man at the junction of Gibbet Street and Queens Road in Halifax.

The man was taken to hospital, where his injuries were described as potentially life-threatening.

Police have linked the assault to an earlier incident of criminal damage at a shop on Gibbet Street, which was reported shortly after 4pm.

A number of windows had been smashed and damage caused to a door at the premises.

Officers attending the criminal damage incident subsequently discovered the injured man.

![The junction of Gibbet Street and Queens Road](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/18/12/35/The-junction-of-Gibbet-Street-and-Queens-Road.png?trim=0,2,19,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


A 35-year-old man was arrested near Calderdale College later that evening on suspicion of several offences, including wounding with intent, criminal damage, threats to commit criminal damage and possession of a Class B drug.

He was also arrested on suspicion of a racially aggravated public order offence relating to language allegedly directed at a police officer during his arrest.

Detectives believe the victim and suspect are known to each other and have stressed that there is no evidence to suggest the assault was motivated by race or religion.

Detective Inspector Richard Thornton said: “This is a very serious incident which has left a man with significant injuries.

“While our enquiries are in their relatively early stages, our understanding is that the victim and suspect are known to each other.

“We understand that there may also be some concerns in the wider community given that this incident has taken place outside a mosque but I would like to reassure people that there is nothing to suggest that this assault was in any way religiously or racially-motivated.”

Detectives in Calderdale are appealing for witnesses as their investigation continues.

Neighbourhood policing officers will also be carrying out reassurance patrols in the area.
