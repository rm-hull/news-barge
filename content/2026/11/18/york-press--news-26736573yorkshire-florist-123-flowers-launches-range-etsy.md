## Yorkshire florist becomes first UK online brand to launch on global marketplace

Yorkshire-based florist 123 Flowers has expanded its digital presence by joining the global marketplace Etsy *(Image: 123 Flowers)*

A Yorkshire-based florist has expanded its digital presence by joining a global marketplace.

123 Flowers, the first online UK flower brand to launch on Etsy, aims to reach a broader audience with its range of fresh flowers and gift arrangements.

The company's offerings, including hand-tied bouquets and signature hatbox arrangements, are now available to Etsy customers seeking unique and high-quality gifts.

The florist, known for its blend of creativity and convenience, provides fast and free UK delivery on all bouquets and a seven-day freshness guarantee.

123 Flowers is also recognised for its sustainable practices, using minimal and compostable packaging for all products, and sourcing flowers from Fairtrade farms and local growers.

This approach supports fair treatment for the farmers and workers who cultivate them.

Alex Biggate, Head of Brand at 123 Flowers, expressed enthusiasm about the move, saying: "Joining Etsy is an exciting next step for 123 Flowers, helping us reach more people who appreciate thoughtful gifts and beautiful flowers.

"Our hand-tied bouquets and signature hatbox arrangements are a natural fit for customers looking for something special, whether they’re celebrating a big milestone or simply making someone’s day."

The launch was timed ahead of the busy Christmas gifting period, offering shoppers another option to discover unique and thoughtful gifts.

123 Flowers offers a fresh take on festive gifting, making it easy to send something special to friends and family over the Christmas period.

The brand's products are now available to shop via Etsy at etsy.com/uk/shop/123FlowersUK.
