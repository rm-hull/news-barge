## The party leadership will submit the proposal to the political assembly, where it is expected to secure approval. SNS could make a comeback, provided it meets certain conditions.

The European People’s Party (EPP) is expected to suspend the membership of Serbia’s Serbian Progressive Party (SNS) at its next political assembly, scheduled for December.

The EPP presidency, headed by Germany’s Manfred Weber, is set to propose the suspension following an internal scrutiny process launched more than a year ago, EPP sources told Euronews.

The centre-right political family has long been on a collision course with the party of former Serbian president Aleksandar Vučić over its ties to Russia and its violent crackdown on protests.

Frustration within the EPP has grown in recent months, culminating in renewed calls for action following the granting of state honoursand the presence of senior Serbian officials at the funeral of Bosnian Serb commander Ratko Mladić.

A convicted war criminal known as the “Butcher of Bosnia”, Mladić died in a UN detention facility in The Hague in late August, aged 84.

The political assembly, comprising delegates from EPP-affiliated parties, must endorse the presidency’s proposal at its meeting in Brussels on 7–8 December. The number of delegates allocated to each party is determined by its performance in the most recent European elections, ensuring that national parties are represented in proportion to their electoral strength.

“An overwhelming majority of the EPP is in favour of the suspension,” a member of the presidency told Euronews, considering the outcome all but certain.

The presidency has been considering the move for several months but wanted to wait for the outcome of the Serbian elections before putting it forward.

SNS, which had dominated Serbia’s political landscape until this year, lost October’s parliamentary election to the Student List and is set to enter opposition for the first time in nearly 14 years.

If suspended, the party would have one year to meet certain conditions established by the assembly to be reinstated, an EPP official told Euronews.

SNS has been an EPP associate member since 2016. The party was contacted for comment but had not responded by the time of publication.
