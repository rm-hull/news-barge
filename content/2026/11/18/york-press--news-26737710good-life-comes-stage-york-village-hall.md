## York theatre group brings comedy classic The Good Life to stage

The Stockton Foresters will perform The Good Life at Stockton on the Forest Village Hall *(Image: Chloe Rowan)*

A classic sitcom is coming to life on stage this November in York.

The Stockton Foresters, one of York’s longest-running amateur dramatic groups, will perform The Good Life at Stockton on the Forest Village Hall from November 26 to 28.

Directed by Susie Young, the production is based on Jeremy Sams’ stage adaptation of the beloved TV series by John Esmonde and Bob Larbey.

Moira Tait, chair of the Stockton Foresters, said: "It has been a challenge to stage this great comedy classic, as the original script called for a revolving stage.

"However, our incredible backstage and tech teams have come up with a great design featuring both houses.

"Their magic doesn’t stop at set design, either – they have created the most realistic-looking goat, with Geraldine stealing the odd scene or two."

The group has introduced a Saturday matinee in response to audience requests.

Ms Young said: "Several of our loyal audience members have asked us about the possibility of a matinee, and we are looking forward to trialling it.

"With the darker evenings, many people might prefer to attend in the afternoon."

Evening performances will take place on Thursday and Friday, with the Saturday matinee beginning at 2pm.

Tickets are priced at £10 and are available via the Joseph Rowntree Theatre website or from The Village Stores in Stockton on the Forest (cash only).
