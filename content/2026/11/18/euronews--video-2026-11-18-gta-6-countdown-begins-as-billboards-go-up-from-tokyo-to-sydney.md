Billboards advertising Grand Theft Auto VI appeared across Melbourne and Sydney, Australia, on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, as gamers worldwide counted down to its long-awaited release.

The latest instalment in the blockbuster franchise is set to launch on Thursday, 13 years after GTA V. In Tokyo, fans shared their excitement, with some looking forward to exploring the story and eventually playing online with friends.

Meanwhile, internet providers in New Zealand are preparing for a surge in downloads. Telecommunications company Chorus expects around 35 petabytes of additional data traffic as players download the 212-gigabyte game.

Network strategy manager Kurt Rodgers said the company had planned for the increased demand and did not expect any disruptions. GTA VI follows two protagonists, Lucia and Jason, as they navigate a fictional world inspired by Florida.
