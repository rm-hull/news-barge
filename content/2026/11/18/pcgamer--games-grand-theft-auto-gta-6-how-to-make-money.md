There's a lot of ways to make money fast in GTA 6, from robbing jewelry stores, pawn shops, and banks to boosting cars, winning races, and even mugging strangers. But some jobs are more lucrative than others, and sometimes the payoff of a big heist isn't worth the time, prep work, and violence it takes to complete them. If you need some cash fast and easy, here are some ideas that are mostly low risk, high reward. Heck, a couple of them aren't even illegal!

## 1. Rob other criminals

![GTA 6 money - Jason on a bike](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/AfT7Ty88gKDsUub2xbssGD-1200-80.jpg)

* **Take**: $1,000-$15,000

Here's the best bit of advice you're going to get for making money in GTA 6: steal from your fellow thieves.

As you're out and about in the world, you'll often see a crime in progress: a bank robbery taking place, a jewelry store heist happening, an armored car being jacked, a drug deal down at the docks, or sometimes just a speeding vehicle filled with criminals fleeing from pursuing cops.

Hit those criminals. Hard and fast. And then take their shit*.* Especially in Chapter 2 and Chapter 3, another criminal's sack of bills or jewels or drugs is worth lots more than the take you'll get from hitting most robbery locations yourself. If you see crooks fleeing with duffel bags over their backs, drop 'em. (You can do it non-lethally if you like, but drop 'em.)

Then grab their bags and beat cheeks because the heat that was on them will quickly be on you. Some robbery crews will have multiple bags of goods, so best to travel as a couple, Lucia and Jason, if only to be able to carry off *all* that re-stolen loot and cash it in with a fence. You don't want to leave a sack of cash or jewelry behind, right? And if you act quickly enough, you'll be able to make a clean getaway.

When you're out exploring Leonida, keep your ears open at all times. Hear sirens? Alarms? Gunshots? Screams? Cars speeding or crashing? Don't hesitate. Take out the other crew and grab the loot for yourself. It's usually a great payday.

## 2. Steal cars

![GTA 6 money - Jason in a car](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/an5qCscgc9CwmyWVCooJ2D-1200-80.jpg)

* **Take**: $1,000 - $20,000, depending on make/model/fence

Okay, a pretty obvious one, but once you hit Chapter 2 and meet a mechanic, Moses, who gives you a slim jim and will buy stolen cars, it's probably the best way to turn a quick profit for relatively little risk. A decent car (Moses is looking for older model, low profile cars) will usually net you between one and a few thousand dollars, which is better than most smash-and-grab jobs you'll be doing at that stage and way easier to pull off. This is because Moses pays well and the fence you have in Chapter 2 does not.

Keep up to date with the most important stories and the best deals, as picked by the PC Gamer team.

The only drawback to Moses is that once he buys a stolen car, you can't sell him another for a bit. There's a big plus, though: he will often tell you precisely where the cars he's looking for are located. Randy, your car fence starting in Chapter 3, will pay way more because he's after higher end, much flashier cars.

## 3. Demolition Derby at Ambrosia Raceway

![GTA 6 money - Mud people](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/zvDuuxmLCYLeFkh6tej3HD-1200-80.jpg)

* **Take**: $3,800 - $6,300 per win

There are lots of races with nice payouts in GTA 6, but what if, like me, you're a terrible driver? The demolition races at Ambrosia Raceway are just the thing for those of you who tend to crash a lot anyway. Win on the oval track and you'll make over three grand, and it's double the take on the figure-eight track. Plus, you can repeat the races as many times as you like. Easy money.

The secret is, you come in first by default when all the other cars have been destroyed. If you can't beat 'em, just ram 'em, flip 'em, and destroy 'em. You don't even need to use your own wheels, just rent one from the track for a few hundred, spend a couple minutes slamming your front fender into everyone you see, and pocket the winnings long before you've done the eight laps.

## 4. Hunting

![GTA 6 money - Man with a dead alligator](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/RhvWHU4evhfwzJZHvrByED-1200-80.jpg)

* **Take**: From hundreds to thousands, depending on the kill

The hunting payouts aren't huge at first, but as you start taking on hunting missions for your old military buddy Bret in Lummox County in Chapter 2 (these missions are for Jason only), he'll request you hunt creatures that he'll pay more and more for. Like Red Dead Redemption 2, there are legendary animals in GTA 6, and that means money.

You can take down the animals from Bret's lists or just find your own prey out in the world. Just today I was exploring, spotted a large boar in the woods, grabbed my rifle from my truck, and shot it: Bret paid me $2,500 and even came to pick up the carcass himself, saving me a trip. For smaller trophies, just park outside and step into Bret's building, and he'll auto-buy the dead critters right out of your trunk. Good deal.

## 5. Robbing stores with back doors

![GTA 6 money - Jason and Lucia doing a robbery](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/cJzBTfpfhiEthMEfnToL5D-1200-80.jpg)

* **Take**: $6,000-$15,000

Your entire criminal strategy will change in Chapter 3 when you get a tool called the snap gun. This lets you pick door locks, which means bypassing alarms. From then on, make sure the places you want to rob have a back door: it's the key to turning a quick smash-and-grab into a proper heist.

Coming in the back of the store is *the* way to do crime: you get inside without anyone (usually) immediately noticing, can quickly and quietly subdue any security or employees hanging around in the rear of the store, get quick access to the security footage (destroy it) and the alarm system (disable it) and can locate the safe before anyone knows a heist is going down.

With alarms off and your partner doing crowd control, you can take the time to make sure you're getting every penny out of the place. Smash every jewelry display case. Threaten someone for the safe code or find it written down somewhere in the office. Empty all the cash drawers, even take people's wallets and the drugs from first-aid kits. Do it right and you can make a clean getaway, avoiding the police pursuit altogether. Beautiful.

## 6. Pilot Pete's drops

![GTA 6 money - A plane flying over the keys](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/2GkGGcCWCUK4SmBWAmWEHD-1200-80.jpg)

* **Take**: $10,000-$20,000

You'll meet a pilot in Chapter 3 and he'll start offering you regular work in Chapter 4. Get in your flying practice, though: these are time-sensitive jobs.

Pete will direct you to his plane and you'll use it to distribute cocaine around Leonida by dropping packages to buyers waiting below. You'll have multiple targets to hit across the map, and a time-limit: those buyers won't hang around forever, waiting. (I guess they have something more important to do than acquiring huge bricks of coke?) Fly smart and plot your route efficiently and you'll make a nice chunk of change from each run.

## Other money-makers

![GTA 6 bank - A guy in a car with cash and a gun](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/WupBLNoLkWTWcyn8GQ3JbW-1200-80.jpg)

There are, naturally, lots of other ways to make money, but most come with more risk, more time, or less of a haul.

* **Mugging**: You can just stick a gun in someone's face, but it's never gonna get you more than a couple hundred bucks.
* **Breaking into car trunks**: I've opened a lot of car trunks and it's not a bad place to find drugs or cash… but usually not much.
* **Searching rooftops**: Feels like just about every time I get onto a roof, I find a lockbox with some cash in it. Just not a lot.
* **Diving for sunken treasure**: It's mildly lucrative and extremely enjoyable, but it is extremely time consuming.
* **The coastline**: I've found a number of washed up boats with bricks of cocaine in them, but no major scores.
* **Illegal operations**: Robbing a gang stronghold pays a ton, usually in narcotics, but be prepared for a major gunfight or a lengthy and risky stealth mission.
* **Pawn shops**: If you can get into the safe, there's usually a good amount. But those pawn shop clerks are just dying for a chance to shoot someone, and they won't go down without a fight.
* **Bank jobs and Payday Loans**: Lots of cash, but lots of security and so many opportunities for someone to panic and hit the alarm.
* **Towable ATMs**: Modify a truck to add a winch, and you can yank ATMs right out of the wall. The downside? You've gotta smash the hell out of it or use explosives to get it open, then break the individual cash containers. That's all very noisy and time-consuming business, drawing cops galore.
