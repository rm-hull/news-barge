A MAJOR road on the outskirts of York is currently closed after a crash.

Stockton Lane is closed after an accident that took place outside Deans Garden Centre, at the junction of the Holtby Lane on Wednesday morning (November 18).

The accident was first reported at around 10.30am with Coastliner adding that they are missing the stops for Deans Garden Centre, Kings Moor Road, Stockton Hall, Stockton Primary School, Stockton Village Hall, Barr Lane, Sandy Lane, Station House, and Hazlebush Farm.
