At least seven people were killed and nine wounded in an alleged Saudi airstrike on a sponge factory in Hodeida, Yemen, on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, according to the Houthis.

The group blamed Saudi Arabia for the attack and warned that the death toll could rise. The kingdom had not commented on the allegations.

Footage released by the Houthi-run Al-Masirah television channel showed thick plumes of black smoke rising from the factory before an explosion sent debris flying into the air.

Aerial images showed large fires burning across the site as firefighters battled to extinguish the flames.

The footage and claims have not been independently verified.
