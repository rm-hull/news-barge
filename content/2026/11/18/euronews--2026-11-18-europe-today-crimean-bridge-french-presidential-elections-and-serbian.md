## Ukrainian forces used drones and missiles to strike the road and railway sections of the Crimean Bridge. France presidential election race is already taking shape and we speak with Willy Nyeko, Emergency Coordinator in Gaza for the UN World Food Programme.

## On today's show:

We explore with our Ukraine correspondent **Sasha Vakulina** the impact of themajor attack on the Crimean Bridge, partially destroying one of Russia's most heavily defended infrastructure sites. We speak with**Roman Kyslyi, Chief AI Officer of Ukraine**, over the role drones and Artificial Intelligence have had in targeting the Crimean bridge

Our correspondent **Angela Skujins** interviews**Norwegian Foreign Affairs minister Espen Barth Eide** over the importance of Oslo having a seat at the Ukraine-Russia peace talks table.

We go over the consequences of the dramatic four-day crisis the Dutch government has just survived and we ask **MEP Raquel Garcia Hermida-Van der Walle** if it will have a permanent impact on the centre-right coalition in power.

We also speak to **Willy Nyeko, Emergency Coordinator in Gaza for the UN World Food Programme** as the Gaza's humanitarian crisis remains devastating more than a month into the ceasefire.
