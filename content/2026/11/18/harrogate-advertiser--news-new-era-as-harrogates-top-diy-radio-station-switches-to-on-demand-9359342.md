The co-founder of Harrogate's leading independent radio station says he is looking forward to the launch of a new era as the "Netflix of local radio."

It was at midnight on Yorkshire Day in 2019 when founders Andrew Backhouse, Stewart Thornton and Allan Smyth, three friends well known in the town’s DIY music scene, first launched Harrogate Community Radio.

Since then this unfunded, underdog radio station with a ‘no adverts' philosophy has live streamed thousands of locally-generated shows, from all types of music to a wide range of spoken word broadcasts.

From January 1, 2027, Harrogate Community Radio will stop broadcasting live and become an on-demand station.

“We've been on air for seven years now,” said Andrew Backhouse, who is also the Station Manager of Harrogate Community Radio.

“When Allan, Scooby and I started Harrogate Community Radio, a live, linear stream felt like the heart of a radio station.

“Seven years on, the way people listen has changed.

“Most people now find our shows on Mixcloud after they've gone out or catch up with our shows while on the bus, or cooking in the kitchen, or late at night when the house is quiet.

“Think of the new way we present the station it as the Netflix of local radio: the shows you enjoy, ready whenever you are.”

Offering a platform for diverse voices and perspectives in the Harrogate area, this inclusive radio station has always been self-funded and reliant entirely on public donations.

The small team behind HCR has always looked on Harrogate as a very musical town and has aimed to bring the community together through the many different voices showcased on the station.

It’s been a tough decision to end live broadcasts on New Year’s Day, said Andrew Backhouse, but the positives outweigh the negatives of the situation.

“Without a 24-hour schedule to fill, people can make the shows they want, when they're ready to make them,” he said.

”Plus, there are years of free jazz, reggae, drum and bass, house, spoken word and plenty of undiscovered gems for listeners to work through.

"Some shows may wind down and new ones may start.

”We will keep everyone posted as we work out the details.

“In the meantime, our shows are already waiting for you online.”
