A couple hoping to breathe new life into a North Yorkshire village following the closure of its pub have revealed more details of their plans for a new food and drink venue.

Drew McGilvray and Jade Overfield are behind Roast and Roam Coffee Co, which they hope to open in the car park of the Staveley Arms in North Stainley, near Ripon, next spring.

The couple want to create a family-friendly outdoor destination combining speciality coffee, stone-baked pizzas and ice cream, with vintage vehicles, outdoor seating and locally sourced Yorkshire produce.

A planning application has been submitted to North Yorkshire Council for up to four vintage-style food trailers in the pub car park.

Drew, who runs a nationwide industrial cleaning company, said the venture was intended to provide the village with a community asset it had lacked since the pub closed in January 2025.

He said: “Our aim first and foremost is to bring life back to a village which has lost its soul since the departure of the pub.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/drew-mcgilvray-and-jade-overfield.webp)

“Unfortunately, due to the size of the pub and the shift in culture towards multi-avenue food vendor sites, we believe this will offer something unique, crafted and in keeping with the village aesthetics to put North Stainley squarely back on the map of the Ripon landscape.”

The couple have already travelled to France to collect a vintage Land Rover Series 2A, which will form part of the venue.

Finding a cream-coloured 109-inch model in good condition in the UK had proved challenging, the couple said.

The business will initially concentrate on coffee, with a grab-and-go breakfast and lunch menu featuring locally sourced ingredients.

Pizza and ice cream outlets are expected to follow during spring and summer 2027.

## ‘Not been done before’

Drew said: “Our plan is to marry coffee, pizza and ice cream in a way which we believe hasn’t been done before.

“This concept is centred around creating an outdoor venue which can be enjoyed by all ages, pulling together vintage vehicles, modern culture and the surrounding nature.”

“We are knitting together multiple different local suppliers to ensure most of what we sell comes from good old Yorkshire.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/staveley-arms-north-stainley-gsv.webp)

He added that all three units were already under construction, but the couple wanted to make sure they got the initial offering right before expanding.

The project is Jade’s first business venture, although she has years of experience working as a barista.

She also has a master’s degree in risk management from Durham University and previously worked for an MP in Leeds before deciding to pursue a different career.

Drew described Roast and Roam as a first step into the hospitality sector.

He said: “This venture is a toe dip into the hospitality sector and also to provide the village with a community asset it has lacked since the pub closure.”

“I’m confident our offering will be a unique, popular spot on the North Yorkshire tourist landscape, offering families somewhere to escape to with little outlay.”

The planning application proposes picnic benches beneath stretch-tent canopies, with heaters and sheepskins to allow the venue to operate throughout the year.

Free outdoor games, including giant Jenga and Connect 4, would also be available.

The scheme is expected to create three full-time and seven part-time jobs, with local recruitment prioritised.

The proposals are intended as a first step towards eventually reopening the Staveley Arms itself in a different format.

The pub’s owner, Lightwater Leisure Limited, has backed the scheme, saying it could help establish a sustainable customer base before the building is brought back into use.

The proposals are separate from plans for around 145 new homes on land elsewhere in North Stainley, which could also help support the long-term viability of the pub.

Drew and Jade hope to launch Roast and Roam in February or March 2027, subject to planning permission.
