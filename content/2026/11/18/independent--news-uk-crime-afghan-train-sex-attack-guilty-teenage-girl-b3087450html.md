Two Afghan nationals have been convicted at Inner London Crown Court for subjecting a 14-year-old girl to a “heinous and prolonged” sex attack on a train.

A jury found Baryalai Rahimi, 25, guilty of rape on Wednesday, along with sexual assault and three counts of sexual activity with a child.

His co-defendant Mirebadurahman Sadat, 29, was convicted of assault by penetration, sexual activity with a child and two counts of causing a person to engage in sexual activity.

The British Transport Police (BTP), which led the investigation, said the two friends approached the girl at Sturry station, near Canterbury in Kent, on 5 February. All three then boarded a London-bound train that afternoon.

Police said Rahimi forced himself on the girl and sexually assaulted her once the train was under way, before taking her into one of the train’s toilets and raping her.

Rahimi then handed the girl over to Sadat, and CCTV cameras on the train captured him walking down the carriage. Sadat also subjected the girl to a sustained sexual assault.

![Mirebadurahman Sadat was also convicted on Wednesday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/17/01M7QVSYMPG6QRKAZ0ZK54KHHC.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The girl repeatedly told the men “No” throughout the attack and was ignored, police said. She had also told them she was just 14.

Footage released by BTP shows Rahimi walking through the carriage in a black puffer jacket and black trousers.

Sadat can be seen in the same footage walking up and down the carriage in a black leather jacket and blue jeans, turning once to look over his shoulder.

When the train reached London Bridge, the men followed the girl onto another train. Rahimi then got off at St Pancras and Sadat at Finsbury Park.

The girl spoke to police at Peterborough station and told officers what had happened to her. Police used her account together with CCTV footage from the trains to identify Rahimi and Sadat.

The pair were arrested in west London on 10 February.

Footage from the arrests shows the two men handcuffed and sat in a police car, with Rahimi stroking his chin.

BTP Detective Constable Gemma Littlewood said: “Baryalai Rahimi and Mirebadurahman Sadat have caused irreparable harm to a child.

“They deliberately targeted her travelling alone, then conspired together so Rahimi could rape her and Sadat could sexually assault her.

![The two men were convicted at Inner London Crown Court (John Stillwell/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2024/08/09/15/e4b8a8feb6dab2f84f8c2002be6b33c0Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzIzMjk5MjIy-2.11713238.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“They knew her age, they knew she was vulnerable, and they heard her say no, but none of that stopped them from subjecting her to a heinous and prolonged attack.

“Today, the spotlight is rightly on these two despicable men, however I want to recognise the exceptional courage shown by the victim in reporting what they did to her.

“Her testimony enabled us to identify, track down and convict two dangerous predators, ensuring they can no longer pose a threat to young girls. We are unwavering in our commitment to tackling sexual violence against women and girls.

“If you report a sexual offence to us, you will be supported and listened to, and we will do everything in our power to secure justice for you. To report something to us, text 61016, or call 999 in an emergency.”

Rahimi, of Southall, west London, and Sadat, of Cricklewood, north-west London, will return to Inner London Crown Court for sentencing on 18 December.
