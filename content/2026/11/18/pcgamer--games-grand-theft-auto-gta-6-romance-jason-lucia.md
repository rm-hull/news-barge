In GTA 6 you've got a partner in crime, and it's quite a bit different than it was when you were pulling heists with Michael, Franklin, and Trevor in the last game. Jason and Lucia aren't just criminal associates, they're a couple, and their romantic relationship is a big part of the story.

Jason and Lucia can grow closer or more distant, depending on your actions, behavior, and choices. That goes for everything from story missions to random criminal acts to everyday interactions and activities. Here's how Jason and Lucia's romance works in GTA 6.

### Relationship levels

![Grand Theft Auto 6 launch gallery: An overhead shot of Jason and Lucia holding handguns while driving in a convertible.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/q5MfnxFC8y7WrZGptZueLF-1200-80.jpg)

There are four levels to Jason and Lucia's relationship that can change depending on how you play and the choices you make, one being the lowest and four being the highest. Here are the levels:

* Running on empty (1/4)
* Partners in Crime (2/4)
* Crazy in Love (3/4)
* Hot Together (4/4)

When you begin playing as a couple in Chapter 1, your relationship is already a strong one, though it'll still take a while to get it up to 4/4, if that's your goal. Certain actions, activities, and choices will raise your relationship score, and you'll know you've done it when you hear a chime and see a +♥️ appear on the right side of your screen.

### What improves your relationship?

![Grand Theft Auto 6 - Jason and Lucia holding each other on a balcony](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/LKBnVpRq3heeVoNhrBEiUK-1200-80.jpg)

Here are just a few actions that will add a ♥️ to your score, and there are plenty more:

* Holding hands
* Going to bed at the same time
* Hanging out on the couch together
* Instigating a conversation
* Greeting your partner when you get together
* Giving your partner a kiss or a hug (a prompt with a heart)
* Buying and handing food or drinks to your partner
* Going out (Choose 'Go Out' when you're both at home)
* Getting your hair done together
* Doing joint activities like minigolf or scuba diving
* Dancing at a club together
* Buying clothes accessories for each other (both will need to be together at the store)
* Complimenting their new clothes/hair styles
* Doing joint crimes successfully
* Depositing money in your Buck Me savings account
* Responding to texts
* Checking in via texts
* Sending selfies
* Opening the car door for each other
* Saying goodnight via text if you're not at home
* Parachuting out of a plane together
* Sex\*![Grand Theft Auto 6 launch gallery: Jason and Lucia rob a bank while covering their faces with bandanas.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/Kwv64ampTr6jcqMUaQVBJF-1200-80.jpg)**\*Note: You can't initiate sex whenever you want, but there are multiple times in GTA 6 when a sex scene is available to you, signified by double hearts over your home icon on the map or with certain limited-time onscreen prompts.**

What leads to a sex scene? In my playthrough, twice these scenes happened while I was showering as one of the characters and the other asked if they could join me (and you can decide to let them or not). Another was after I reached a major milestone by contributing money to our savings account (hey, financial security is a major turn-on in real life, too!) and there was also a sex scene after a sizable heist. All of these happened while I was at a 4/4 "Hot Together" relationship status.

### What hurts your relationship

![Grand Theft Auto 6 launch gallery: Lucia fires an assault rifle from the passenger seat of a convertible as Jason drives through an firefight between other criminals and the police.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/X6Ve3dfqNuxrhGd6n4oeJF-1200-80.jpg)

You can also lose hearts from your relationship. This didn't happen often in my playthrough, but here's the ones I experienced:

* Failing a joint mission
* Ignoring your partner for a while
* Occasional mission choices your partner doesn't agree with
* Hitting them with your car 🙁

## A good relationship requires playing both roles

According to Rockstar, if you want a strong relationship in GTA 6, you have to play both sides of that relationship. That means not just playing as Jason and doing nice things for Lucia, but also playing as Lucia and treating Jason well.

For my playthrough, where I reached the end of the game with a 4/4 "Hot Together" status, my stats show that I played nearly the exact same amount of time as Jason as I did Lucia.

### Solo time

![Grand Theft Auto 6 launch gallery: Lucia holding a handgun as a police officer approaches her from behind.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/yTKHqSdfMgjayUTSfEEiKF-1200-80.jpg)

Don't worry, this couple isn't joined at the hip. Both Lucia and Jason have missions, storylines, and activities they can (and are sometimes required to) complete alone. You can play plenty of GTA 6 as just a single character running around without the other, just remember to occasionally spend some time with your partner, respond to their texts, and switch to the other character from time to time.

But trust me, you'll want to spend lots of time together. Their relationship is fun to take part in, from both sides. Plus, doing crimes as a couple really pays off. It's helpful to have an extra set of hands to point guns at people, do crowd control during robberies, shoot out a cop's tires while driving, and carry off more loot than you could handle if you were rolling solo.

### Effects of a strong or weak relationship

![Grand Theft Auto 6 - Jason driving while Lucia looks out at Vice City from the passenger](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/qssUN2VdpiBrvmcfvqWGdK-1200-80.jpg)

I'll be honest: I don't know how the game is different if you have a 4/4 relationship or a 1/4 relationship, because I've only played through the story once at this point, and I kept my relationship as strong as possible for nearly the entire game. We'll know more about the effects of a weak relationship as other players experiment with their relationship status, and we'll update this guide with what we find out.
