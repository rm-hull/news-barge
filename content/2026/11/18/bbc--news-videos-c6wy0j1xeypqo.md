# Emma Barnett pays tribute to 'decent journalism' in final Today episode

Radio 4 presenter Emma Barnett has hosted her final episode of BBC Radio 4's Today programme ahead of joining a new BBC show next year.

She used her final moments on the programme to praise "decent journalism and journalists" - citing those at the Times, Sunday Times and Guardian who worked on investigations into Russell Brand and David Walliams respectively.

"All power to the storytellers," Barnett said, while also paying tribute to those who have trusted the media with "some of the hardest but also most important stories to tell".

The presenter will move to what she has called a "bold" new show set to launch in 2027 across BBC Sounds, BBC iPlayer and YouTube.
