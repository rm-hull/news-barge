**Deer Shed Festival has announced the first acts for its 2027 edition, with Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Antony Szmierek and English Teacher among the headliners.**

The independent North Yorkshire festival will return to Baldersby Park, near Topcliffe, from 23 to 25 July 2027.

The first wave of artists also includes This Is The Kit, Arab Strap, Charlotte Adigéry & Bolis Pupul, Maruja and Adult DVD, alongside Shovel Dance Collective, Pigeon, Native James and Prewn.

Further names announced include Bab L’Bluz, Lauren Auder, Spanish Horses, Marla Kether, Wyatt and HØST.

## First headliners announced

New Zealand rock band Unknown Mortal Orchestra will make their Deer Shed debut. Led by singer, guitarist and multi-instrumentalist Ruban Nielson, the band blend psychedelic rock, funk, soul and lo-fi pop. They have released seven albums and performed at festivals including Glastonbury, Coachella and SXSW.

Manchester poet, spoken-word performer and musician Antony Szmierek will return to Deer Shed after appearing in 2025. His combination of spoken word and dance music has earned him recognition, including being named BBC 6 Music’s Artist of the Year in 2023. His debut album arrived in 2025.

Completing the headline acts are Leeds-based indie-rock band English Teacher. The Mercury Prize-winning and BRIT Award-nominated group previously performed at Deer Shed in 2023 and will return for their first headline appearance at the festival.

## Returning favourites and new acts

This Is The Kit, the indie folk-rock project fronted by Kate Stables, will return to the festival. Their seventh album, *Ghost Coat*, is due for release in January.

Scottish indie-rock band Arab Strap will make a return ten years after their last Deer Shed performance. The group, formed by Aidan Moffat and Malcolm Middleton, recently marked their 30th anniversary with the release of their ninth studio album, *Half-Told Tales*.

Belgian electronic duo Charlotte Adigéry & Bolis Pupul will bring their electro-pop sound to Baldersby Park, while Manchester-based four-piece Maruja will also return.

Leeds band Adult DVD are back after their 2025 appearance, alongside Shovel Dance Collective, Margate-based psychedelic group Pigeon, alternative-rap artist Native James and indie-rock band Prewn.

## Festival theme and activities

The festival has also revealed its 2027 theme, ‘The Network’. Organisers say the site will become one vast hidden network, inviting festival goers to connect, build and work together to complete it over the weekend.

Alongside the music programme, Deer Shed is expected to bring back activities including wild swimming, off-grid saunas, live-action wrestling, skateboard masterclasses, tree climbing, fireside storytelling, cinema, theatre and literary and performance arts.

The announcement follows this summer’s festival, which featured Sleaford Mods, Say She She, Everything Everything, The Twilight Sad, Fat Dog and Getdown Services. Comedy performers included Stewart Lee and Adam Buxton.

Organisers say more than 60% of tickets for the 2027 event have already been sold.

> **Megan Evans, Director of Deer Shed, said:**
>
> Bringing Unknown Mortal Orchestra to Yorkshire is something we’ve wanted to do for a long time. We’re also proud to welcome first-time festival headliners and returning Deer Shed favourites English Teacher and Antony Szmierek.
>
> Across this first wave you’ll find returning friends of the festival, alongside the introduction of newcomers and rising favourites. The first wave is always a labour of love; it’s been at the heart of Deer Shed’s booking to never compromise, and to build a line-up that reflects our values: championing progress, introducing music fans to their new favourite band, and giving a platform to artists who are first-time headliners or headliners of the future.
>
> This year, our Deer Shed community has shown up in force, with over 60% of our tickets are already gone. That is a remarkable show of trust, and a testament to the loyal audience we’ve built together.
>
> With plenty more yet to announce, we’re gearing up for the best Deer Shed yet. We can’t wait to welcome you to Baldersby Park next summer.

Deer Shed Festival takes place at Baldersby Park, Topcliffe, from 23 to 25 July 2027. Tickets are on sale now.

For more information and tickets, visit deershedfestival.com.

## Listen to the artists

With a varied line-up spanning psychedelic rock, indie, electronic music and experimental sounds, here is a taste of some of the artists announced for Deer Shed Festival 2027.

### Unknown Mortal Orchestra

Led by New Zealand musician Ruban Nielson, Unknown Mortal Orchestra blend psychedelic rock, funk, soul and lo-fi pop. Known for their distinctive guitar sounds and melodic songwriting, the band have built an international following and performed at major festivals including Glastonbury and Coachella.

### English Teacher

The Leeds-based four-piece won the 2024 Mercury Prize for their debut album, *This Could Be Texas*. Their music combines inventive guitar arrangements, unconventional song structures and sharp, often surreal lyrics. They return to Deer Shed after appearing in 2023, this time taking on a headline slot.

### Charlotte Adigéry & Bolis Pupul

The Belgian electronic duo combine infectious electro-pop, dance beats and playful songwriting. Their music also explores subjects including identity, race and contemporary culture, with their acclaimed album *Topical Dancer* bringing them wider international attention.

### Maruja

The Manchester-based band bring together post-punk, jazz, experimental rock and spoken-word influences. Their music is often intense and politically charged, with saxophone playing a prominent role in their sound. Their live performances have helped establish them as one of the more distinctive acts on the current British alternative music scene.
