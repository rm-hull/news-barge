Six people were taken to hospital with minor injuries after a drilling operation at a landfill in Los Angeles County triggered a sudden release of gas and decomposing waste.

The incident happened on Monday at the Chiquita Canyon Landfill in Castaic, where crews were carrying out routine drilling around 46 metres below ground.

Workers struck naturally occurring landfill gas produced by decomposing rubbish, causing it to escape through the drilling hole. The gas carried small pieces of decomposing waste into the air, creating a large cloud, according to fire department officials.

The city of Santa Clarita urged the public to avoid the area and advised some nearby residents to be prepared to evacuate.
