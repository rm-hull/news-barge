York City FC Foundation is set to hold a sale of old York City match programmes.

The event will take place ahead of York City's game against Walsall on Saturday.

Programmes from different decades will be available for 50p or £1 each.

The sale will be held at the foundation's gazebo outside the front of the stadium, with all profits from the sale going towards supporting York City FC Foundation.

The sale is part of the foundation's 'Charity Partner Focus Fixture' initiative.
