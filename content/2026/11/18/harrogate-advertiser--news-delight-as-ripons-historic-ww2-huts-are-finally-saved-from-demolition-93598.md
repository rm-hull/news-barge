Campaigners say they are delighted that their long battle to save historic Second World War huts at a former military camp in Ripon credited with “saving lives and winning the war” has at last paid off.

For the past three years Ripon Military Heritage Trust has been campaigning to save some of the huts at Deverell Barracks in Ripon.

During the Second World War, the huts housed Ripon’s School of Military Engineering which played critical role in the Allied victory over the Nazis.

Demolition of the last surviving intact, 1939 wooden-hutted Militia Camp in the country had been due to begin early in the New Year.

But a delighted Ripon Military Heritage Trust says that Homes England, which now owns the site, has agreed to dismantle rather than demolish two of these huts and give them to the Trust instead.

Guy Wilson, Chairman of RMHT, said: “We are most grateful to Homes England for giving us this opportunity to preserve these unique buildings.

“Now the work begins to store them safely and work with planners, politicians and the community to find an appropriate site on which to rebuild them to tell Ripon’s fascinating military history.

“What better buildings in which to inspire young people with an interest in science and technology than those in which, during the Second World War, allied engineers learnt the skills that saved lives and win us the war.”

The huts chosen to be saved, the Lecture hut and one of the two Instruction huts, are the only known examples of their type.

They are the huts in which British and allied army engineers were trained during the Second World War.

Homes England is giving RMHT the opportunity to dismantle and retain the four Cold War metal “Twynham huts” on the barracks site before demolition begins in the New Year.

RMHT has also been allowed to select and retain moveable items ranging from beds and cupboards to a cell door and an air raid siren to facilitate future interpretation of the barracks.

Tom Baker, Senior Planning & Enabling Manager at Homes England, said: “We recognise Ripon Military Heritage Trust's ambition to create an educational facility that reflects the history of the site and are pleased to have been able to contribute to that effort.

“As part of our planning for the redevelopment of Deverell Barracks, we have worked with the RMHT to make a range of original materials available for reuse, including former military buildings, furniture and documents.”

Ripon Military Heritage Trust (RMHT) is a charitable incorporated organisation (registered charity no. 1203294) founded in 2023 to research, conserve, interpret and provide access to the military heritage of Ripon and its surroundings.

It is run by unpaid volunteer trustees. Its chairman, Guy Wilson, is a former Master of the Royal Armouries.
