A Burger King outlet can operate its drive-thru service until 5am after permission was granted by the council.

The fast food chain applied to extend the hours customers can order from their cars at Kirkstall Retail Park.

The company was already permitted to run its indoor restaurant and drive-thru from 7am-midnight.

Leeds City Council granted a late night refreshment licence for food orders between 11am-5am.

Burger King also needed a variation to its planning permission to run the Savins Mill Way drive-thru between those hours.

A council report said there were concerns over noise and increased traffic if later opening was allowed.

But it said: “The restaurant occupies a commercial town-centre location adjoining Savins Mill Way and the A65.

“It is surrounded by retail units and a petrol filling station, with no residential properties in close proximity.”

The opening hours of the interior area, near retailers Matalan, Boots and B&M and Morrisons, would not be extended, Burger King said.

There were also concerns over potential anti-social behaviour and cars queuing up overnight.

The report said: “From 11pm, however, the internal restaurant would close and sales would be restricted to the drive-thru.”

The existing premises licence required the monitoring of outside areas and customers being asked to leave quietly.

Burger King was given permission to build the drive-thru near busy Kirkstall Road in 2024.

A council plans panel originally voted to reject the scheme after road safety concerns were raised.

It was eventually allowed that October after planning officers said the traffic impact was not serious enough to refuse permission.

The application to vary the planning permission said conditions were in place to prevent anti-social behaviour, including CCTV and an incident log.

Burger King’s planning report said: “Notices will also be displayed within the restaurant and premises to request that patrons respect the needs of local residents and leave the area quietly, which will be overseen by restaurant staff.”
