Drivers are facing delays on a major road into and out of York due to works.




AA Traffic News is reporting queues on the A1079 between Feoffee Common Lane and Carr Lane, between Barmby Moor and Wilberfoss, first reported just before 7.30am.

Roadworks are being carried out by Yorkshire Water Services and temporary traffic lights are operating in the area.

According to the traffic network system, the works are scheduled to run until December 23.

The A1079 is a principal road linking York with the Wolds and the East Riding of Yorkshire.
