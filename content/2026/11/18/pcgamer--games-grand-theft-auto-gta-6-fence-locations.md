Visiting a fence to sell your stolen stuff is one of the fastest ways to earn money in GTA 6. After you make a clean getaway (you did get away clean, right?) you can stash your goods with one of several fences to get cash back for your crimes. Just make sure you pay attention to who pays best for what types of goods and whether you're going to get paid immediately or a while after dropoff.

## How to fence stolen goods

Bring your stolen goods to any fence location at any hour of the day to trade them in for cash. Hover over each fence's name to see what they pay highest for between narcotics, jewelry, and marked bills. Some you can walk up to and get paid immediately, others pay later after you leave goods in their drop box, and eventually you'll unlock the ability to have stolen goods picked up from your garage concierge style.

### Drop boxes

* Brian's Lockup
* Donnie's drop box at his trailer
* Dunk's drop box in his car trunk

### Immediate sale

* Tyrone's apartment
* Yasmani's shop

### Garage pickup

* After doing two Payday Loan jobs for Yasmani you can leave stolen goods in one of your garages to have them picked up, but you do pay an extra fee for the convenience

### Send your partner

* You can hand your stolen goods to Jason or Lucia (whichever you're not controlling at the moment) and tell them to fence the goods for you. They'll leave and handle it for you, though you'll get a bit less money than you would have if you turned in the goods yourself.
