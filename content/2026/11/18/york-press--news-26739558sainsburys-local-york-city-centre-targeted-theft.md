A thief has struck at a national supermarket chain in York.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened at the Sainsbury's Local in Davygate on Wednesday, October 7.




Police have released CCTV images of a man the force said it wants to speak to, saying he may have information that will assist its investigation.

Anyone who recognises the person is asked to contact PC1488 Bell by email at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Information can also be provided by calling 101 or contacting Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

The reference number for the case is 12260197526.
