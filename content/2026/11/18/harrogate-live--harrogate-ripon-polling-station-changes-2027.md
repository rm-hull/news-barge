Parts of 15 Harrogate wards and five Ripon and Littlethorpe areas get a new polling station for the May 2027 elections. Knaresborough is unchanged.

Voters in parts of Harrogate and Ripon will cast their ballot somewhere new at the North Yorkshire Council elections in May 2027. Knaresborough’s polling stations are not changing.

The moves are in the final recommendations of the council’s interim review of polling districts and polling places. The Standards and Governance Committee will be asked to approve them on Tuesday 24 November.

## Why polling stations are moving

The review follows the Local Government Boundary Commission’s redrawing of North Yorkshire Council’s divisions. The boundary changes split parishes in new ways, so 14 parishes get new warding from 1 April 2027. They include Harrogate, Knaresborough, Ripon, Killinghall, Littlethorpe and Haverah Park with Beckwithshaw, according to the committee report.

Polling districts have to follow those new lines. In many places two old districts are merged, and everyone in the new district votes at one building. Some venues the council used before are also no longer available, the report says.

The council received 102 responses to its consultation, which ran from 3 August to 12 October. Most were broadly in favour, according to the report. A revised electoral register based on the new districts is due on 1 December.

St George’s Dance Club drops out as a polling station altogether. Its voters are split between St Mark’s Church Hall, Oatlands Community Centre and Harlow Community Centre.

Two areas just outside the town move too:

* **Killinghall’s urban ward**, which is in the new Jennyfield division: voters who used Killinghall Village Hall or St Wilfred’s Church Hall will vote at Jennyfield Styan Community Centre.
* **Beckwithshaw’s Harlow Hill Grange ward:** voters will go to Harlow Community Centre instead of Beckwithshaw Village Hall.

One Jennyfield resident told the consultation it was “good to have one central place”, pointing to the parking at the centre and across the road.

## Knaresborough

Knaresborough’s parish wards are being redrawn, but every polling place in the Knaresborough East and Knaresborough West divisions stays the same, according to the schedule.

## What it means for you

* **Check your poll card.** It will name your polling station before the May 2027 election. You can vote only at the one it names.
* **If the new venue is harder for you to reach,** you can apply to vote by post or by proxy through North Yorkshire Council’s elections and voting pages.
