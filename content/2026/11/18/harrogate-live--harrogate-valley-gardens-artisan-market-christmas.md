Little Bird Made's artisan market fills Harrogate's Valley Gardens on 5 and 6 December, 10am to 3pm. Entry is free and dogs are welcome on both days.

The Harrogate Valley Gardens Artisan Market Christmas Weekend is on **Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December**, from 10am to 3pm on both days. Entry is free, and dogs are welcome.

The market is run by Little Bird Made, and every stall belongs to an independent maker selling handmade gifts, art and festive food.

## Overview

* **When:** Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December, 10am to 3pm each day.
* **Where:** Valley Gardens, Harrogate.
* **Cost:** free entry.
* **Dogs:** the event is dog-friendly.

## What is on the stalls

Jackie Crozier, managing director of Little Bird Made, said: “Visitors can expect a wonderfully festive atmosphere filled with talented independent artisan makers, delicious street food, and seasonal treats. It’s the perfect opportunity to pick up unique Christmas gifts while taking a winter stroll to explore the stunning Valley Gardens.”

![Customers pointing at chocolate-topped bakes on the Sweet Tooth Bakery stall while a stallholder in blue gloves serves them](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/harrogate-valley-gardens-artisan-market-christmas-2.webp)

Asked which stall people should not miss, Little Bird Made said: “It is impossible to pick just one! Every stall is run by an amazing, talented independent artisan offering unique, handmade gifts, art, and festive treats. Make sure you take your time and explore them all: you never know what hidden gems you will find.”
