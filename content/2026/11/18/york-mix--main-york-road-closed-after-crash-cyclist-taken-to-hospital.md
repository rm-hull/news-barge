A York road is closed tonight (Wednesday) after a crash between a Land Rover and a bicycle.

It happened at the junction of **James Street** and**Lawrence Street** at around**6.14pm**.

North Yorkshire Police said at 7.43pm: “The cyclist, a 20-year-old man, sustained several injuries following the collision and is currently receiving medical treatment in hospital.

#### Map location

“The road remains closed while we continue to investigate the scene.

“We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have witnessed the collision, anyone who may have seen the Land Rover before or immediately after the incident or anyone who has any relevant dashcam or doorbell footage.”
