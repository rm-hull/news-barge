**Euronews Persian**

## Army commander-in-chief Amir Hatami said Iranian forces would strike the US “along with its supporters” wherever they were based, as CENTCOM confirmed the USS Theodore Roosevelt had joined two other carriers in the region.

Iran's army commander-in-chief Amir Hatami warned on Wednesday that if Iran were attacked again, its forces would deliver a "more crushing, harsher response, without geographical limitation," threatening to strike the US and its allies regardless of where they were based.

"If the enemy once again takes action, it will without doubt be dealt with more crushingly, more harshly and without geographical limitation, along with its supporters," Hatami said.

He said he hoped the other side would not "miscalculate," warning that if it did, "it will receive a lesson that will be a warning to everyone."

Hatami said the US had brought a new aircraft carrier into the region and that Iranian ground, naval, air and air defence forces were monitoring military movements and continuously raising their readiness.

He said the necessary preparations to counter possible attacks had been put in place under orders issued to senior commanders.

US Central Command (CENTCOM) has confirmed the arrival of the USS Theodore Roosevelt in the region, bringing the number of US carriers operating there to three.

## Iran claims drone downed

The Iranian army's public relations department said its forces had destroyed a US MQ-9 drone east of the Strait of Hormuz using domestically produced air defence systems deployed in the south-east of the country and operated through an integrated air defence network.

The claim has not been independently verified and Washington has not commented.

Yadollah Javani, political deputy of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), claimed Tehran's forces could now strike or intercept advanced drones in areas including the Strait of Hormuz and Qeshm Island, citing advances in Iranian missile and electronic warfare capabilities.

On Tuesday, the US carried out air strikes on positions on Qeshm Island, following Iranian reports of attacks on US military installations in the region.

There have also been reports of attacks on commercial vessels in the Strait of Hormuz.

Iran has warned that any further attack would draw a military response not confined to targets within Iranian borders.

The US has said it will protect its forces and its allies' interests in the region.
