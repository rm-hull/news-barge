A care home is set to host a community event aimed at raising awareness and offering support.

Thistle Hill Care Centre in Knaresborough will hold the event on Thursday, November 26, starting at 10am, led by Sarah Welburn from TENA and Essity.

The session will focus on breaking the stigma around continence issues and offer a supportive environment for open conversation.

Pratheesh Thankachan, general manager at Thistle Hill Care Centre, said: "The event will be a fantastic opportunity to bring people together and raise awareness of an issue that affects many individuals and families.

"Continence can often be a difficult topic to discuss, but through events like this we can help break down barriers, provide valuable information and reassure people that support is available.

"We are extremely grateful to Sarah and the team from TENA and Essity for sharing their expertise with our home and the wider community."

The care centre has built a strong reputation for supporting both residents and the wider community through regular events and educational activities.

Members of the public are invited to attend the continence awareness event, which will combine expert advice with a welcoming atmosphere to encourage open discussion and confidence in seeking help.

Thistle Hill Care Centre is operated by Barchester Healthcare, one of the UK’s largest care providers.

The centre offers residential, nursing, and dementia care for 93 residents, including both respite and long-term stays.
