## Bootham and St Wilfrid’s claim victory at York schools chess contest

St Wilfrid's Kings team won the primary school category *(Image: Supplied)*

Players from Bootham School and St Wilfrid’s have been crowned champions at the York inter-school chess competition.

The sixth annual York inter-schools chess tournament took place at Bootham School, with 22 teams from across York and North Yorkshire taking part.

Bootham’s Quakers team, made up of day pupils, retained the secondary school title, while St Wilfrid’s Kings team won the primary school category.

Ben Rich, organiser of the event, said: "School chess in York is going from strength to strength, with four junior teams now competing in the Yorkshire under-18s, two junior teams in the York Evening League and our first York form junior winning his Candidate Master title in the past year.

"I was particularly pleased to see how many girls were playing, and for a team largely comprising female players to win the Primary competition was particularly exciting as we work hard to encourage girls to play in the city."
