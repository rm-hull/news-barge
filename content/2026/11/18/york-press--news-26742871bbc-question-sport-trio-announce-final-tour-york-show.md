## Famous sporting and TV trio announce farewell tour and show in York

The Final Showdown Farewell Tour will be the very last chance for fans to see the legendary British institution of Sue Barker, Phil Tuffnell and Matt Dawson before they call time on their quizzing. *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

A much-loved TV quiz trio are hitting the road one last time, with an appearance in York.

Sue Barker, Matt Dawson, and Phil Tufnell, English sporting heroes and icons of BBC TV show A Question of Sport will bring their Final Showdown farewell tour to York Barbican in Paragon Street next September, giving fans a final chance to see them live on stage.

The tour, which begins in Oxford on September 11 and finishes with a grand finale at London’s Theatre Royal Drury Lane on September 26, will visit cities including Bristol, Nottingham, Manchester, Edinburgh and Brighton.




Sue Barker said: "We always said we would know when it was time to call full time, and a farewell tour with the audiences who have been so loyal to us feels like the right way to do it."

The stage show will feature classic and new quiz rounds, plenty of laughter, and behind-the-scenes stories from their careers that never made it to air.

Audience members will also have the chance to take part in the action from their seats.

![Matt, Dawson, Sue Barker and Phil Tufnell](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21736456.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The trio have been popular off the courts and fields as the stars of TV quiz show A Question of Sport *(Image: Contributed)*

Men's Rugby World Cup winner Matt Dawson said: "Phil and I have been going head to head for years, so it feels right to do one last round in front of a live audience.

"We have had some great times together, and I can’t wait to get out on the road with these two one more time."

Each night will also include a rotating cast of special guest sporting superstars, with those line-ups to be announced closer to the dates.

Mr Tufnell said: "It’s been a cracking run and I’ve loved every minute of it.

"It wouldn’t feel right to finish without getting out and seeing everyone one last time, so come along, have a laugh and help us go out in style."

The much-loved trio spent 13 years together on screen, with Sue Barker at the helm as host, and Dawson and Tufnell as rival team captains.

Fans have continued to enjoy their chemistry and humour through live tours and theatre shows.

Tickets go on sale on Friday 20 November via MyTicket.co.uk, with the York Barbican event scheduled for Wednesday, September 15, 2027.
