**The Prince and Princess of Wales have met India's prime minister as they ended their three-day visit to the country.**

Narendra Modi thanked Prince William for bringing his Earthshot prize to the country during the couple's first international tour since 2022.

It comes a day after the royal couple gave out £1m prizes to winners of the environmental award, which was founded by the prince and remains one of his biggest personal projects.

Catherine also visited a maternity hospital, where she comforted a grieving mother who lost a baby after giving birth to premature twin girls.

On Wednesday, William and Catherine travelled from Mumbai to capital New Delhi for the final leg of their trip.

During a meeting at Modi's private residence, a Kensington Palace spokesperson said the royals and Modi discussed the "strength and importance of the UK–India bilateral relationship, reflecting on the deep and enduring ties between the two countries and the opportunities to strengthen their partnership in the years ahead".

The prince and princess also spoke "warmly" of their time in the country, thanking the prime minister and the people for their welcome, and saying how much they enjoyed returning to India, a decade after their last visit.

Modi called it a "pleasure" to meet them, and said he looks forward to "further strengthening" the UK–India partnership for the "shared prosperity of the people of our nations and the wider world".

When Prince William announced earlier this year that The Earthshot Prize ceremony was going to be held in India for the first time, he highlighted India's leadership in climate innovation, its biodiversity and its massive youth population as the key reasons for choosing the country.

The royal spokesperson said that the meeting with Modi touched on the success of the ceremony in Mumbai, and India's "growing leadership in environmental innovation".

The event, founded by Prince William in 2020, was hosted this year by Karan Johar, presenter of The Traitors India and one of the country's most well-known filmmakers.

The couple last met the Indian prime minister on their official visit to India in 2016.

Ahead of their meeting with Modi on Wednesday, William met with film stars during a visit to a film production company, while Catherine visited a maternity hospital, where she met grieving mother Jyoti Karande.

Karande, whose other child remains in intensive care, is receiving treatment in the post-partum ward at Nowrosjee Wadia Maternity Hospital.

Catherine told the 29-year-old: "Stay strong. Well done. You've clearly been very, very brave."

William and Catherine's first official overseas trip in four years was short, but allowed them to combine personal projects - like Earthshot - with diplomacy.

As the future king and queen, they are viewed as a valuable diplomatic asset by the UK government.

And with Catherine recovering well and back on international duties following her cancer treatment, visits of a similar kind can be expected to fill their calendar.
