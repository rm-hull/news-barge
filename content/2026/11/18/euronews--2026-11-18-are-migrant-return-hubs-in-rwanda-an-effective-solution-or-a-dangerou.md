## In this edition of our debate show, MEPs Lena Düpont (EPP) and Robert Biedroń (S&D) go face-to-face over the EU's migration policy.

This week, five EU countries announced the first-ever return hub for migrants in Rwanda. Denmark, Austria, Germany, the Netherlands and Greece unveiled the pilot project during the Vienna Migration Conference.

While supporters argue that a credible migration system needs effective returns and better control of irregular arrivals, critics, however, warn that outsourcing migration management could weaken access to asylum and leave vulnerable people without legal protection.

In this edition of *The Ring* from the European Parliament in Brussels, anchor Maria Tadeo is joined by German MEP Lena Düpont from the European People's Party (EPP) and her Polish counterpart Robert Biedroń from the Socialists & Democrats (S&D).

Is Europe striking the right balance between security and human dignity, or moving towards a more restrictive approach? Are return hubs part of the solution?
