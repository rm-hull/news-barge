A California couple’s proposal went off the deep end when a man popped the question on the beach, only to watch a massive wave sweep his engagement ring out to sea.

Jace Harrick was proposing to his girlfriend, Zoey Mattinson, Sunday during a sunset outing at a beach in San Clemente, just south of Los Angeles, when the wave swept through the couple, taking them by surprise.

Video captured by Harrick's family showed the engagement ring still inside its box before the sudden surge of water washed it away.

“Water came and hit us literally like waist-high, so powerful out of nowhere. He's always worrying about me all the time, so he tries to pick me up, and then there goes the ring,” Mattinson recalled to ABC7.

“I'm still kind of like holding on to him and then he said the ring and I was like, huh?” she continued. “And it was like your stomach just drops. I'm like, there is no way.”

![Jace Harrick and Zoey Mattinson are engaged, but still searching for the ring swept away by a wave during their sunset proposal Sunday on a San Clemente beach](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/18/01/Zoey-Mattinson-and-Jace-Harrick.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The proposal quickly turned into a frantic search effort as family, friends and strangers joined the couple in scouring the beach and nearby water for the missing ring. But despite the group effort, the ring remains lost at sea.

Mattinson said she caught a brief glimpse of the ring before it disappeared, and was impressed by her fiancé’s pick.

“It was so beautiful,” she told ABC7. “That's literally my dream. Like, it is so, it's so beautiful. So at least I got to see it for a second. Blinded me a little bit.”

Despite the disappointing turn of events, the couple has not let the missing ring overshadow their engagement and said the mishap fits their whirlwind romance after meeting just six weeks ago.

![The couple, who met just six weeks ago, says the mishap fits their whirlwind romance](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/18/10/112585681-16302637-The_couple_got_engaged_after_six_weeks_of_knowing_each_other_onl-a-2_179501767655-3d04392a.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“When people say love at first sight, when you know, you know, that was so me and Jace, so having this happen to our ring is like a whole other crazy addition to our crazy, perfect love story,” Mattinson said.

Harrick is already looking into getting a replacement, but the couple hasn't given up on finding the original.

“He was, like, ‘it was the greatest moment of my life but also the worst moment of my whole entire life.’ I think he's just really sad cause he just wanted to, like, put a ring on my finger,” Mattinson said.

A diver plans to search for the ring once ocean conditions improve, Mattinson said.
