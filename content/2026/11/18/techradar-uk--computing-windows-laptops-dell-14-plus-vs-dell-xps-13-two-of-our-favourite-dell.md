This week's early Black Friday sale at the Dell Store has quite a few excellent discounts today. In fact, the official retailer's sale includes everything from sub-£500 entry-level machines to high-end Alienware gaming laptops.

Two deals have really caught my eye in particular, however: the Dell 14 Plus for £899 (was £1,099), and the Dell XPS 13 for £1252.52 (was £1565).

These are two of our favourite Dell laptops here at TechRadar, so they immediately stood out as highlights in the wider Dell sale. For those who don't already know, the 14 Plus and XPS 13 are excellent laptops that are absolutely perfect for both casual and professional use.

Which one should you go for? Well, honestly, you can't go wrong with either since they're both lightweight, powerful, and well-designed laptops. Of the two, the 14 Plus is the more sensible value option, which you'd guess looking at the price. It lacks the verve of the XPS 13, but you do get excellent bang for the buck *and* more ports, which is very useful.

The XPS 13, on the other hand, is the clear choice if you want something a little special. Its all-aluminium design competes with the very best offerings from other premium brands, and it's extremely thin and lightweight for such a powerful machine. Other highlights include the lovely keyboard and display, which is fantastic to work on, despite this being a slightly smaller 13-inch laptop.
