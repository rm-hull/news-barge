Thursday 19 November is International Men’s Day and The Spa Surgery at Mowbray Square Medical Centre in Harrogate is encouraging men to look after their health, and to register with a GP if they haven’t already.

Men are less likely than women to book a check-up, and many wait until a problem gets worse before they speak to anyone.

Around 1 in 8 men in the UK will get prostate cancer, and high blood pressure often has no symptoms at all. A quick check now can pick up problems early, when they’re easier to treat.

“Sometimes it feels hard to speak up when something’s bothering you, and you just want to get on with it, hoping it will go away,” said Mike Drew, Practice Manager at The Spa Surgery.

"We really encourage our patients to contact us if they have any concerns, so we can make sure you get the support you need, whatever it looks like.

"If you don’t have anything worrying you right now, we encourage you to book in for an NHS Health Check with one of our healthcare assistants, as this helps us identify issues early and proactively provide you with the care you need.”

**Know when to speak to your GP**

NHS Health Check: if you’re aged 40 to 74, you can have a free NHS Health Check to spot early signs of heart disease, stroke and diabetes.

Blood pressure: high blood pressure rarely has symptoms, so the only way to know is to get it checked.

Prostate: speak to your GP if you notice changes when you pee, or if you’re at higher risk, such as being over 50, being Black, or having a family history of prostate cancer.

**Don’t forget your mental health** Looking after your mind matters just as much as your body. Andy’s Man Club Harrogate runs free, peer-to-peer talking groups for men aged 18 and over, every Monday at 7pm (except bank holidays) at Harrogate College, Hornbeam Park Avenue. There’s no need to book and no pressure to speak. If you need urgent help, call Samaritans free on 116 123, or 999 if a life is at risk.**Not registered with a GP?**
