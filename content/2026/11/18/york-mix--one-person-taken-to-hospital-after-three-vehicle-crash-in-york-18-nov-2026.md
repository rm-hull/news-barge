One person has been taken to hospital after a three vehicle crash at a York junction.

Police, fire and ambulance crews were called to the incident on Stockton Lane at its junction with Holtby Lane.

One car ended up in a field next to the road.

#### Crash location

Fire crews from York and Acomb responded to reports of people trapped in the wreckage.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “On arrival, no persons were trapped and casualties were out of their vehicles. Crews assisted with scene safety and isolating the batteries on the vehicles prior to recovery.;

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “Three ambulances, a team leader and a specialist paramedic (critical care) were dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to York Hospital.”
