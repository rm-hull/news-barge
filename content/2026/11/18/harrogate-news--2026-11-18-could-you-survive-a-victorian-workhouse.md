**Two presenters have experienced very different sides of Victorian workhouse life in a new History Hit programme filmed at Ripon Workhouse Museum.**

*Could You Survive a Victorian Workhouse?* takes Luke Tomes and Katie Kennedy, known online as The History Gossip, inside the museum to explore what life was like for people who found themselves within its walls.

Luke is processed as a vagrant by the Tramp Major. His own clothes are taken from him before he is sent to break rocks as hard labour and given a meal of gruel. After being caught in the Master and Matron’s quarters, he is thrown into the cells and sent away after two nights.

Katie experiences a different route through the Workhouse, following the life of a resident pauper. The programme contrasts her experience with that of vagrants, who could receive short-term shelter in a separate casual ward, often in return for labour.

She follows a demanding routine of work, exercise, prayer and school, is interviewed by the Workhouse Guardian and collects vegetables from the garden. Unlike Luke, she is allowed to remain as a resident.

The programme also explores daily routines, food, sanitation, punishments and the treatment of residents. It looks at what happened to those who died in the Workhouse, whether there was any hope of escape and how the institution eventually came to an end.

Filmed at Ripon Workhouse Museum & Garden, the special features the museum’s restored spaces and exhibitions.

> **A spokesperson for Ripon Museums said:**
>
> We were delighted to welcome Luke and Katie to Ripon Workhouse. Their visit brings the museum’s stories to life in a fresh, informative and fun way, while showing how differently people could experience the Workhouse. We hope the episode puts Ripon in the spotlight and introduces the museum to a new audience.
