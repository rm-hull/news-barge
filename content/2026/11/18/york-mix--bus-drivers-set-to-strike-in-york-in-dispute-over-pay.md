Drivers for First Bus in York are planning to strike next week.

Members of the Unite union who work at the company’s York depot will walk out on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November.

It follows their rejection of First’s pay offer.

First Bus is preparing to operate a limited network, including Park and Ride, on strike days and will provide further information nearer the time.

The company’s service delivery director Megan Hope-Rushforth said: “’We are planning to operate as many services as possible and will give customers as much notice as we can to allow them to plan journeys and limit disruption and inconvenience during the festive period.”

She added: “We are very disappointed and saddened at the decision to go ahead with strikes.

“Industrial action will be damaging to everyone and cause severe disruption to the daily lives of our customers who rely on the bus to get to work, education and for appointments as well as the thousands of people that want to enjoy the city in the lead up to Christmas.”

## ‘Numerous offers’

The company says it has submitted two pay offers to Unite members.

The first covered a period of 12 months and equated to a 4.5% increase. The second offer covered 24 months and equated to an 8.3% increase over that time.

Ms Hope-Rushforth said both were well above current inflation and backdated to April this year.

“We have met repeatedly with Unite representatives over the past eight months and made numerous offers in our attempts to find an agreement,” she said.

“We believe this reflects the vital role our drivers play while ensuring we can continue to provide reliable services for the thousands of people in York who depend on us every day.”

She added: “We fully recognise the strength of feeling of colleagues in York and remain committed to working constructively with Union representatives to reach a fair and sustainable agreement.

**“** Our door remains open to continue discussions and avoid unnecessary industrial action.”

We have asked the Unite union for its response and will update if we hear back.
