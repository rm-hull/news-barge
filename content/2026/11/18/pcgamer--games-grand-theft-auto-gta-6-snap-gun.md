Traditional lockpicking gets an upgrade in GTA 6 with the introduction of the **snap gun**—a handy little device that makes picking locks faster and quieter. It's perfect for those scenarios where you don't want Jason and Lucia to set off alarms by kicking open locked doors and making demands. Instead, the snap gun offers a stealthier way through locked doors.

Unfortunately, it's locked behind story progression like the slim jim and key cloner, so you can't make those earlier missions easier. If you don't mind a small spoiler, here's how to get the snap gun in GTA 6.

## How to get a snap gun

![GTA 6 - snap gun minigame on locked door](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/7uv273REHiZSFTgBmsev5S-1200-80.jpg)
![GTA 6 - snap gun minigame on locked door](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/oQiEJEidwoH3scUjkcPF9S-1200-80.jpg)
![GTA 6 - snap gun minigame on locked door](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/7uv273REHiZSFTgBmsev5S-2560-80.jpg)
![GTA 6 - snap gun minigame on locked door](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/oQiEJEidwoH3scUjkcPF9S-2560-80.jpg)

* **You get the snap gun from Yasmani** in Chapter 3 after completing the jewelry heist in the Dubious Provenance mission.
* Approach a locked door with the tool equipped, and the "Use snap gun" option will appear.
* Use the left analogue stick to find the lock's sweet spot, then press the right trigger until the meter fills. Voilà, you're in.
* While the snap gun circumvents alarms, the alarm itself is still active. It may still be set off by kicking open other doors, breaking display cases, or by a store employee.

Using the snap gun on an establishment's back door is a great way to get access to the office to completely disable the alarm altogether.
