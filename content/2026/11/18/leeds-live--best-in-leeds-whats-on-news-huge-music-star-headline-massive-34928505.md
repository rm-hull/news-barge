# Huge music star to headline massive summer 2027 Harewood House show

## This is going to be a big party

A huge music star will headline a special outdoor summer show at Harewood House.

In 2027, we will see two-time BRIT Award winner Becky Hill perform at Harewood House. The star will be bringing a night of live dance music to the historic estate.

Becky Hill, a Leeds Festival favourite, will take to the stage on Thursday, June 10. Tickets go on-sale at 10am on Friday, November 27 - You can buy them through SeeTickets and Skiddle.

The Harewood House concert is being brought to the estate by promoter Iconic Concerts and will see a capacity of 8,000 fans take over the ground’s impressive landscape. There will also be street food vendors and bars serving cocktails and premium drinks.

Iconic Concerts managing director, Liam Hardy, said: “We’re hugely excited to be bringing Becky Hill to Harewood House. There’s something special about seeing an artist with Becky’s energy and voice performing in an open-air setting, especially somewhere as beautiful as Harewood.

“It’s going to be a brilliant summer night for live music in Yorkshire, and we look forward to revealing more details soon about what fans can expect on the night.”
