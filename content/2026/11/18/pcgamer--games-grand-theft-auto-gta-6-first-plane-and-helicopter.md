If you've just started GTA 6 you might be wondering where the nearest airplane and helicopter are located to your starting point. There's a huge new world to explore, and the best way to do it is by air. But first, you've got to find a plane or chopper to steal.

Luckily, there's a reliable spot to snag your first few aircraft close to your starting safehouse, and you can probably steal them without raising a single alarm.

## Where to find your first airplane

You'll drive about 2.5 miles southwest from Jason's house in Key Lento. Start the trip by driving **southwest through Key Lento**, then** west through Abismo Key**, and finally** west and then north to Southwind**.

You'll be **taking Sunbelt Highway (Route 1) the whole way** and you'll know you're close after you**cross two long bridges**. Southwind is a small district located next to the Mile Zero Air Station: GTA 6's military base.

(Yes, the military base has planes and helicopters too, but it's a little trickier getting your hands on them so check out our GTA 6 military base guide before you attempt it.)

The highway will make a bend to the north as you enter Southwind. On your right you'll see a concrete barrier alongside the road, and where it ends is where you want to **turn right into the parking lot**, at the sign for SBW (Sherwin Boatworks) and Chicki's Tiki Bar.

![Plane location in GTA 6](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/m7UUXDruAGqPvKFhjRKSGM-1200-80.jpg)

If you're **facing the water (east), on your right (to the south) is a small hangar** with (typically) a few**Mammoth Dodo seaplanes** parked inside and around it, and often a couple more moored off the dock. Seaplanes aren't the fastest planes in the game, but they're perfect for early exploration because they can land on both water and land, and they require almost no room to get airborne.

There's never anyone hanging around here, either, so you won't draw any attention if you help yourself to one of these planes. Just drive one into the water and take off, or steal one from the dock.

## Where to find your first helicopter

Follow the same instructions to Southwind as above, but instead of looking to the south, **look straight east from the entrance to the parking lot**. There's an open gate leading out to a dock where a** Buckingham Maverick helicopter** is sitting.

![Plane location in GTA 6](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/KJxApM6ZSj3BjW4WLBWrEM-1200-80.jpg)

Just ignore the no trespassing sign, run over to the dock, and hop into it. Now the skies are yours.
