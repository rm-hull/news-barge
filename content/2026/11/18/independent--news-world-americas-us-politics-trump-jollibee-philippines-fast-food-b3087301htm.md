President Trump was treated to a late-night delivery of fried chicken by Philippine officials as part of his visit to the Southeast Asian country.

​Trump’s team received the delivery on Tuesday, after arriving in Manila as part of a tour that includes visits to the Philippines and China.

​Opening the bilateral meeting with Trump the following day, President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. revealed more about his gift: a “taste of Manila” in the form of a Jollibee delivery.

​“Since you’re strapped for time, I thought a taste of Manila might be good,” Marcos said. “So last night, I asked my staff to have some Jollibee delivered as a late-night snack for you and your delegation.”

​Founded in 1978, the hugely popular chain is known for its Chickenjoy dishes - a hand-breaded and deep-fried meal often partnered with gravy.

![President Trump's delegation to the Philippines was gifted with a delivery from Jollibee](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/15/2305146337..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

​As Marcos spoke, Trump smiled and nodded but did not comment on the delivery.

Then, the Philippine president went on to say that the chain is so successful that it is challenging the U.S. fast food giant.

​“Jollibee is a very popular chain and one of their main products is fried chicken,” Marcos began. “And we are very proud to say that we have surpassed KFC in the United States in terms of popularity.

“So we thought that’ll give you a taste of Manila, and I hope some of your staff was (sic) also able to partake.”

​Trump merely responded: “Oh wow, fantastic.”

![Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr touted Jollibee's popularity during the meeting with Trump](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/15/2026-11-18T073410Z_412425699_RC23QOAR04OX_RTRMADP_3_PHILIPPINES-USA-TRUMP.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​The U.S. president has been vocal about his love for fast food, even serving customers at a McDonald’s restaurant during his 2024 presidential campaign.

​In 2016, he explained to CNN’s Anderson Cooper why he enjoys McDonald’s in particular.

​“It’s great stuff,” he said. “One bad hamburger, you can destroy McDonald’s. One bad hamburger and you take Wendy’s and all these other places, and they’re out of business.

​“I like cleanliness, and I think you’re better off going there than maybe some place that you have no idea where the food is coming from.”

​In October 2025, Republican National Committee Chair Joe Gruters recalled that Trump created his own combination of McDonald’s burgers during the 2024 campaign.​

![Jollibee, which is known for its chickenjoy meals, was described by Marcos as a ‘taste of Manila’](https://static.independent.co.uk/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/2018/10/18/10/jollibee-01.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“He had a Filet-O-Fish, a Quarter Pounder, a Big Mac, and I think he combined two of them,” he said.

​Although McDonald’s and KFC both have more restaurants in the U.S. than Jollibee, the Philippine chain has promoted its food as the best fried chicken in the United States for a number of years. For three consecutive years, Jollibee has topped “readers’ choice” polls carried out by USA Today.

​Although Trump has yet to publicly offer his thoughts on the chain, he did take to Truth Social to give his view on Marcos as the Philippine leader.

“President Marcos is a powerful leader, and a man of high integrity and substance,” he wrote. “It was an Honor to be with him, and his beautiful wife and son.”
