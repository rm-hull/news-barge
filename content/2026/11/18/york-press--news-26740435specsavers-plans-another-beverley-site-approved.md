A national chain of opticians has moved one step closer to opening an additional branch in a Yorkshire town.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has approved plans submitted by Specsavers to carry out works on a Grade II listed building in Beverley.

Council planning officers gave the green light for internal and external changes to Unit 3 at 117-119 Walkergate.




Specsavers are eye health and hearing specialists and have a store in Toll Gavel in the town.

The property in Walkergate, built around 1730, had been altered in the past for commercial use and has a modern shopfront on the ground floor, while retaining its historic architectural features, the council said.

The proposed works include internal alterations, new external signage, and the replacement of a skylight and air conditioning condenser units.

According to a council document, these proposed changes are "minimal in nature" and do not raise concerns as they largely replicate existing features.

The internal alterations are also not expected to raise any issues due to the age and modern nature of the extension.

The building, situated in the Markets Area of the Beverley Conservation Area, is an integral part of the historic core of the town.

According to the council's conservation officer, the proposed works are unlikely to harm the significance of the listed building.

The officer stated that the works would have no material effect on the historic or architectural character of the principal listed building, and its setting would remain unchanged.

"The building's positive contribution to the character and appearance of this part of the Beverley Conservation Area would therefore be preserved," the officer added.

The application also received no objections from the Beverley and District Civic Society and Beverley Town Council.
