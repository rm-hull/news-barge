Wayne Rooney says he has “loads” of regrets.

The 41-year-old football legend is currently competing on I’m a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! and opened up about his past struggles during a recent episode.

When asked about his biggest regret, Mr Rooney said: "Loads if I’m being honest... in terms of both mistakes made off the pitch and regrets when you drink alcohol then you’re missing out on time with your kids and stuff.

"That’s why now, the last six months, I haven’t drank.

"I think me clean, me with the kids is probably the happiest place I’ve ever been."

He said he is grateful that his family has stayed together.

Mr Rooney, who shares four children with wife Coleen, admitted that his alcohol use became problematic in his early 20s.

He said: "I struggled, I turned to alcohol when I was in my early 20s.

"I didn’t wanna be around people so I’d drink in the house for a couple of days.

"I struggled with the pressure of it, yeah."

He described one incident that became a turning point.

He said: "I’d be lying on a pool table in my house and I remember Kai walked in and found me asleep and then it was a wake up call, it was like, what are you doing?"

Mr Rooney said his drinking once lasted for two days straight.

He added: "Then I’d get up, go and train for the rest of the week and then I’d score two goals on the Saturday, so you think it’s okay."
