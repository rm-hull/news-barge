## Pub landlord spends £5,000 on Christmas display to cheer up locals

Christian Ogley, Licensee of Blind Jacks pub in Knaresborough, North Yorkshire, stands in front of the pub with a beautiful Christmas light display. November 16, 2026. // The historic and picturesque market town of Knaresborough in North Yorkshire has *(Image: Charley Atkins / SWNS)*

A Christmas-mad landlord has spent £5,000 on a stunning DIY festive display on the front of his pub - all to cheer up his town's locals.

Christian Ogley, 34, says this year has been particularly tough for residents in Knaresborough, with the cost of living crisis.

In an attempt to spread some Christmas cheer he spent thousands of pounds on materials and equipment and with help from seven staff and regulars, made the "classy" display on the front of his pub, Blind Jack's.

They have spent the past week building and installing the design, which includes a 5-metre-tall tree as its centrepiece, surrounded by white lights imitating falling snow and red ribbons.

The landlord said: "It has been a turbulent year for everyone in the area and across the UK to say the least, so I just wanted to do something nice to make people smile.

"So far it has seemed to have done that - we have had many compliments.

"To start with it was just a bit of fun, but as I have been installing it so many people have been wanting to talk to me about it and have been posting it on social media.

"It has given them something positive to talk about and see.

"At times it has been hard to complete anything as so many people want to chat and take pictures in front.

"Hopefully it can become a bit of a tradition and something locals can look forward to."

Christian was inspired after visiting New York City and Ireland over the past two years and seeing the effort landlords put into their displays.

And decided that he would do the same at his watering hole, something which he says isn't too common to see in the UK.

He put on a smaller display last year but decided to go big this year and started designing the mega Christmas project in January by sketching out initial ideas on the back of beer mats.

The dad-of-two studied graphic design at university, which he says "helped him bring" his ideas to life.

He began installing the display last Monday, November 9, using a cherry picker and the help of seven other staff members, friends, and family.

This included building the huge Christmas tree, which is made from wood and tarpaulin.

It also has red present lights around the windows with DIY red ribbons underneath.

Altogether, it has over 10,000 lights and costs around £2.50 a day to run from 12am to 12pm, according to Christian.

Including all the equipment hire, lights, ornaments, and running costs, he says it will have cost him £5,000.

He said: "It is surprisingly cheaper than I thought to keep the lights on.

"It is great, really, as it means I can keep them on for longer.

"The lights mean so much to me and the community, I'm trying to keep them glowing for as long as I can each day."

Christian says he still isn't finished yet, as he needs to install two seven-foot nutcrackers.

He bought the pub in 2017 alongside his 33-year-old wife Alice Ogley.

Last year it was crowned the Best Beer Pub in the UK at the Great British Pub Awards.

The historic and picturesque market town of Knaresborough has served as a filming location for several productions, including the Christmas comedy film Tinsel Town starring Kiefer Sutherland, Rebel Wilson, and Danny Dyer.
