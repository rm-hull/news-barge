According to the German Weather Service (DWD), heavy cloud cover on Wednesday will bring scattered showers. Brief thunderstorms are also possible.

File photo

The weather in Berlin and Brandenburg will remain rather unpleasant for the time being.

In addition, there will be gusts of wind in some areas, which may become stormy at times in the afternoon. According to the forecast, severe gusts are also possible near thunderstorms. An official warning for gusts of wind is in effect from 1 p.m. to 9 p.m. Temperatures will range between 10 and 13 degrees Celsius. The wind will ease noticeably overnight, the DWD added. Rain will be only scattered. Temperatures will drop to between 4 and 8 degrees Celsius.

Thursday is also expected to remain heavily cloudy with occasional rain. According to the DWD, temperatures will reach 6 degrees Celsius in Prignitz and 11 degrees in Lower Lusatia. A high of 9 degrees is forecast for Berlin. Overnight, rain will continue in some areas of southern Brandenburg. However, the clouds are currently clearing in northern Brandenburg. According to the forecast, lows will range from 6 degrees in the rain down to minus 1 degree in northern Brandenburg. There, freezing rain may also lead to slippery conditions. Friday will show a different side: it will be partly sunny to cloudy.
