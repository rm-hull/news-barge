The first wave of live acts for the 2027 edition of a North Yorkshire event often called “Harrogate fans’ favourite music festival” has been revealed.

The big names for next summer’s Deer Shed Festival include the three headliners - Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Antony Szmierek and English Teacher.

Running from July 23-25, 2027, this independent, highly civilised music, arts, and science event, Deer Shed Festival has grown organically over the last 16 years, mostly through word-of-mouth.

Created in 2010 by husband-and-wife team Oliver and Kate Webster-Jones, Deer Shed Festival also shows exquisite taste in programming each year at its beautiful site at Baldersby Park near Thirsk.

Making their Deer Shed debut in 2027 will be New Zealand rock band Unknown Mortal Orchestra**.**

The brainchild of singer, guitarist and multi-instrumentalist Ruban Nielson, the band’s psychedelic rock, funk, soul and lo-fi pop has earned widespread critical praise from the offset,

Megan Evans, Director of Deer Shed Festival, said: ”Bringing Unknown Mortal Orchestra to Yorkshire is something we’ve wanted to do for a long time. We’re also proud to welcome first-time festival headliners and returning Deer Shed favourites English Teacher and Antony Szmierek.

“The first wave is always a labour of love; it’s been at the heart of Deer Shed’s booking to never compromise, and to build a line-up that reflects our values: championing progress, introducing music fans to their new favourite band, and giving a platform to artists who are first-time headliners or headliners of the future.”

Next year’s event will also include This is the Kitt, Arab Strap, Charlotte Adigéry and Bolis Pupul, Maruja, Adult Dvd, Shovel Dance Collective, Pigeon, Native James, Prewn, Bab l’bluz, Lauren Auder, Spanish Horses with much more to come!

The announcement follows the 2026 edition of Deer Shed, which saw the family-owned festival welcoming an array of acts including Sleaford Mods, Say She She, Everything Everything, The Twilight Sad, Fat Dog, Getdown Services and Madra Salach; while comedy legends Stewart Lee and Adam Buxton had the 10,000 capacity Baldersby Park site in stitches.

Word is that tier 2 tickets are still available but hurry, more than 60% of all adult tickets have been sold for 2027.
