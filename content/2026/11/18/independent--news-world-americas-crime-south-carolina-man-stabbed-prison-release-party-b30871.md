A South Carolina man is accused of fatally stabbing another man at a party just two days after being released from federal prison.

Cedrick Cunningham, 50, was charged Monday with murder and possession of a weapon during a violent crime in connection to the death of Andre Smith, 42, according to the Richland County Sheriff's Office.

Smith was found dead inside a car Saturday in Hopkins, South Carolina, with a large cut on his chest, WIS News 10 reports.

Cunningham and Smith, who knew each other, and allegedly fought before Cunningham stabbed him Saturday. Smith drove away but crashed into a ditch a short distance away, where deputies found his car still running, WLTX reports. It’s unclear what the men were fighting over before the alleged murder.

Cunningham is being held at the Alvin S. Glenn Detention Center.

![Cedrick Cunningham, 50, allegedly stabbed 42-year-old Andre Smith to death Saturday during a party for Cunningham's release from federal prison on a weapons charge](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/18/13/22/Work-900-px-x-600-px-(4).png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Cunningham's reported release from federal prison comes after a 2019 conviction on a weapons charge, for which he was sentenced to 10 years in prison, according to federal court records reviewed by WIS News 10.

Other documents from the case show that Cunningham's previous drug convictions qualified him for classification as an armed career criminal under the federal Armed Career Criminal Act. The federal law imposes enhanced penalties for certain people convicted of possessing firearms after multiple prior convictions for violent felonies or serious drug offenses.

A judge denied Cunningham's request for compassionate release in 2023, the documents also reportedly show.

Further details about the circumstances surrounding Smith's death have not been released.
