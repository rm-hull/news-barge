Baroness Benjamin has joined a host of high-profile names backing *The Independent*’s Christmas campaign,

The actor and children’s TV presenter threw her support behind the Project Nest appeal, saying every contribution will “help to change a young person’s life” and provide them with “hope, opportunity, and a fresh start”.

This Christmas, *The Independent* is partnering with young people’s charity EveryYouth on Project Nest, a campaign which aims to give young people a safe place to call home while transforming dilapidated properties blighting UK city centres.

We are aiming to raise £200,000 to refurbish three properties in Fife, Sheffield, and Basingstoke into purpose-built accommodation to help young people launch their futures.

![Baroness Floella Benjamin has thrown her support behind the campaign](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/13/1239389101..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

It has already been backed by prime minister Andy Burnham and actor Ruby Wax.

Baroness Benjamin said: “I truly believe Project Nest is a brilliant initiative that turns empty, forgotten properties into safe, welcoming homes where young people experiencing homelessness can work or train, save towards independence, get support and find stability. It’s about so much more than providing a roof over someone’s head – it’s about giving them hope, opportunity and a fresh start.”

She added: “Giving gives so much satisfaction and is amazingly rewarding, knowing that every contribution will help change a young person’s life and will show what's possible when we all come together. Childhood lasts a lifetime so please join me in supporting this wonderful appeal and donate whatever you can.”

The appeal is also being supported by *Silo* star Remmie Milner, who urged people to donate to the campaign. She said: “Our towns and cities are full of empty buildings, while young people are facing homelessness.

“Project Nest is about bridging the gap – taking properties that have fallen into ruin and transforming them into comfortable homes where young people can start rebuilding their lives. It’s a simple but powerful idea: rebuild houses and rebuild lives at the same time.

“The solutions to homelessness are all around us; we just need to bring things together. And Project Nest is positively doing that – giving young people hope and a future, breathing new life into neglected buildings, and creating stronger communities in the process.

“Please support Project Nest in whatever way you can, whether by donating or spreading the word. Every single penny raised will make a difference and help us reach young people in need sooner.”

![Silo star Remmie Milner called Project Nest an 'incredible' initiative](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/13/2181798072..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Married at First Sight UK star Davide Anica also called Project Nest an “incredible initiative” and said it will help give young people the “stability” they need to move forward.

“Every young person deserves a safe place to call home, and the chance to build a future filled with hope and opportunity,” he said. “It is heartbreaking to think that so many young people have experienced poverty, trauma and homelessness before they have even had the chance to begin their adult lives.

“Project Nest is an incredible initiative that will do so much more than provide a roof over someone's head. It will give young people stability, care and the support they need to move forward.

“I would urge everyone who can to support *The Independent*'s Christmas appeal and EveryYouth. Every donation will help turn neglected buildings into homes and give young people the opportunity to rebuild their lives.”
