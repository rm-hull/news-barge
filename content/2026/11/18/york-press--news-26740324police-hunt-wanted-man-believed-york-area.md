POLICE are hunting a wanted man who officers believe is in York.

Callum Ellerby, 28, is wanted on recall to prison after breaching the terms of his licence, North Yorkshire Police said.

The force said he is believed to be in the York area, but “still believed to be in the city but could also be in its surrounding areas.”

Police described him as a white man of a medium build with brown eyes and short dark brown hair. Ellerby is 5ft 9in.

A police spokesperson said “extensive enquiries are ongoing” to find him.

They urged anyone who sees Ellerby to call 999 and those with information about his whereabouts to dial 101 or report it via North Yorkshire Police’s website.

Information can be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via its website.

Please quote reference 12260221657 when passing on information.
