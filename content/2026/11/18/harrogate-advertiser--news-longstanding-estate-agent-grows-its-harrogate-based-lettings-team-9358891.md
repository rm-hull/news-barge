Independent estate agent, Dacre, Son & Hartley, is expanding its lettings and property management division in North Yorkshire after appointing Emma Blaker as Senior Lettings Manager at its Harrogate area offices.

Emma has spent the last two-and-a-half years working for a national estate agent in Harrogate.

In her new role, Emma will oversee the growth of Dacre, Son & Hartley’s rentals portfolio throughout the Harrogate area, Wetherby, Knaresborough, Ripon, Pateley Bridge and all their surrounding communities.

Dacre, Son & Hartley has more than doubled the number of rental homes it manages across Yorkshire in the past 12 months, at a time when many landlords are seeking expert guidance on increasingly complex legislation.

Emma said: “I’ve lived locally for almost 30 years, so I know the area very well and completely understand the nuances of the rental market in this part of North Yorkshire.

“Demand remains exceptionally strong across all property types, from studio and one-bedroom apartments to larger family homes.

"The region continues to attract a diverse tenant demographic, including personnel associated with Menwith Hill, local families, as well as professionals relocating to the area for work.

“With changes in legislation, including the recently introduced Renters’ Rights Act this year, many landlords are looking for extra support and reassurance when managing their properties.

"Having an experienced lettings agent by their side can help them navigate the evolving regulatory landscape with confidence and ensure they remain compliant.

"With a strong local reputation, a fantastic team, and a client-focused approach, it felt like the perfect time for me to join Dacre, Son & Hartley.

"I’m excited about the opportunities ahead and look forward to supporting landlords and tenants across Harrogate and North Yorkshire.”

Patrick McCutcheon, Head of Residential at Dacre, Son & Hartley, said: “Our lettings and property management division is growing month on month.

"We are therefore thrilled to welcome Emma as we continue growing our rentals portfolio across Yorkshire.

"She brings a wealth of experience to the role and knows the local area extremely well, which all makes her a great addition to the team.

"Next week will see another high-profile appointment within this sector of the business as we continue our trajectory of growth.”

Dacre, Son & Hartley’s lettings division offers landlords both a fully managed service and a tenant-finding package and continues to expand its rental portfolio across Yorkshire.

Founded more than 200 years ago, Dacre, Son & Hartley has 17 offices across North and West Yorkshire and it also owns the Lister Haigh estate agency brand.
