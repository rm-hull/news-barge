## Best Black Friday iPhone Deals

The iPhone Air impressed us with its super-svelte form factor when it came out last year. A lovely 6.5-inch OLED screen and excellent performance, courtesy of Apple’s A19 Pro chipset, are wrapped up in a body that’s just 5.6 millimeters thick and only weighs 165 grams. Battery life isn’t stellar, and the main camera is a lone 48-MP shooter, but it’s *so thin*. Discounts on iPhones can be hard to find, so if you’ve had your eye on the iPhone Air, this deal is worth grabbing.

They may be last year's Apple flagships, but Best Buy still has some stock. They are currently on offer for $100 less than usual, which is a rare deal. These powerful iPhones promise fantastic battery life, great performance, with nice thermal management, and bright 120-Hz screens. You also get a versatile triple-camera system, with some of the best video recording capabilities on a phone.

## Best Black Friday Google Pixel Phone Deals

The most powerful Pixels in Google's range are the 11 Pro and 11 Pro XL, and they're on sale at the deepest discount since they launched this summer. Classy designs, bright, colorful screens, and powerfully versatile camera systems, make these the best Android phones you can buy right now. The cameras are the best Google offers, with faster low light performance and powerful telephoto lenses to zoom in from afar. Google’s AI features offer proactive assistance, more conversational ability, and handy photography features like Magic Capture to automatically capture photos and videos for you. Pixels are also the only major Android phones with full Qi2 support, including magnets for easy mounting.

While it’s not my favorite folding phone, this is a polished update to Google’s foldable line, offering some genuine improvements, including impressive durability with an IP68 water and dust resistance rating. You get two lovely, sharp OLED displays: an 8-inch main screen and a 6.5-inch cover screen. It misses out on the better cameras in the other Pro models, but the same smart AI features are present and correct. This is a return to the lowest price we've seen on the device.

The regular Pixel 11 isn't far behind the Pro models, with the same Tensor G6 chipset, smart features, and Qi2 25W support. But it comes in more fun colors, including the bright pink Hibiscus I tested. You can also get Pistachio (warm green), Frost (pale lavender), or Obsidian (black). This phone is the perfect size, and the sweet spot in Google’s range for most folks, at least until the cheaper A series variant comes along.

## Best Black Friday Samsung Phone Deals

Our top pick of the best folding phones pulls all of Samsung’s experience together into a fresh form factor that feels *just right*. The 5.5-inch cover screen is usable, and the 7.7-inch inner screen is perfect for opening two apps side by side. This is also a powerful phone with a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and has plenty of stamina thanks to the 4,800-mAh silicon-carbon battery. This $400 discount brings it to the lowest price we’ve seen so far.

If you prefer a compact flip phone, the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip8 is calling. Samsung’s eighth-generation clamshell is wonderfully refined, slimmer than ever, and offers a 4.1-inch AMOLED cover screen with a 6.9-inch display inside. Performance and battery life are excellent. The camera will be enough for most folks, and there’s a healthy dose of AI. Best of all, this discount deletes the annual $100 price bump and knocks another $125 off. It has never been cheaper.

Samsung’s most powerful phone is the pick for professionals. It combines excellent performance, great battery life, and a reliable quad-camera system. The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra has a special Privacy Display to keep folks from peeking over your shoulder at what’s on your screen. The Ultra is also the only Samsung phone with the integrated S Pen stylus.

## Consider Black Friday Carrier Deals

While it usually makes more sense to buy your phone outright and then shop around for service, several carriers are offering discounted phones or extras such as earbuds and smartwatches if you sign up for new contracts with them. Popular budget carrier Mint Mobile is offering some solid discounts; Verizon has some interesting bundle deals; you’ll find deep discounts as incentives to switch to T-Mobile, and there are lots of trade-in deals at AT&T. Just make sure you crunch the numbers to understand how much you’ll pay over the course of the contract.
