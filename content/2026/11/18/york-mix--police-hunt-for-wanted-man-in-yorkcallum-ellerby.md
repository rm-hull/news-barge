Police are hunting for this wanted man from York.

He is Callum Ellerby, 28, and he’s wanted on recall to prison after he breached the terms of his licence.

Police say Ellerby, who is from York, is still believed to be in the city but could also be in its surrounding areas.

He is described as a white man of a medium build and is 5ft 9in. Ellerby has brown eyes and short dark brown hair.  
A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate Ellerby and we are now appealing for your help to find him.

“If you have an immediate sighting of him, please call 999.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online.”
