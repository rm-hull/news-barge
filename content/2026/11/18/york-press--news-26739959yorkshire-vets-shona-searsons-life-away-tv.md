The Yorkshire Vet is a hugely popular docuseries that follows a few different vets and the work they do with pets and farm animals around the county.

If you are an avid viewer of the Channel 5 series, you will have gotten to know the vets who appear in the show.

Much of the show takes place at Donaldson’s Vets in Huddersfield and follows Matt Smith, David Melleney, Rohin Aojula, and Shona Searson through their workdays.

However, you might want to know what Shona’s life is like away from the cameras.

## Shona Searson’s life away from the Yorkshire Vet

Before joining the Yorkshire Vet, Shona graduated with a Bachelor of Veterinary Science from the University of Liverpool in 2012.

She then went on to become the clinical director of Donaldson’s Vet.

Outside of her work on the Yorkshire Vet, Shona lives with her Royal Marine husband named James.

The pair got married in a ceremony held outside with all of their pets and livestock involved.

Shona’s wedding was actually filmed for a special Channel 5 documentary called My Big Yorkshire Wedding.

The night before her wedding, Shona was called out to help a pregnant cow give birth.

Despite the interruption to her wedding preparation, Shona was able to put on her wedding dress and walk down the aisle.

After the ceremony, Shona said: “It's just been absolutely brilliant.

“Everything's worked out really well.

"The weather's held out. We can see the view.

“The animals were involved. Yeah, I'm just... I'm really happy.”

Outside of this documentary, Shona rarely shares insights into her life but will sometimes post photos of her with James on her social media.

### How can I watch the Yorkshire Vet?

If you have missed any episodes of the Yorkshire Vet or just want to rewatch the show, you can find all of the episodes for series one through to the recent series 24 on Channel 5’s streaming service My5.
