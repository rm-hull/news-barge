The boy died in hospital three days after the incident at Cosy Toes Nursery in Middleton, police said

**An investigation has been launched into the death of a three-year-old boy following an incident at a nursery in Greater Manchester, police said.**

The boy became critically ill at Cosy Toes Nursery in Middleton shortly before 16:00 GMT on Monday 2 November.

He was rushed to hospital, where he died three days later.

Greater Manchester Police said his family were being supported by specialist officers.

Det Ch Insp Claire Brennan said: "This is an incredibly tragic development in a deeply upsetting incident and our thoughts remain with the boy's family."

Police said an investigation had been launched in line with standard procedure following a child's death in a care setting.

No arrests have been made at this time.

## 'Wonderful boy'

A spokeswoman for Cosy Toes said they were "all heartbroken" by the "wonderful and beautiful" boy's death.

"Our thoughts and prayers are all with his family at this terrible time," she said.

"We will of course give our fullest possible assistance in every way possible to the enquiries that must rightly follow this tragic event."

Anyone with information about the boy's death is being urged to contact police.

Brennan said: "We are in the very early stages of our involvement and are carefully considering events and the evidence available to us.

"For now, we ask that the family's privacy is respected as they begin to navigate the unimaginable loss of their little boy."

Ofsted said it had taken the decision to suspend the nursery's registration in the wake of the incident, meaning the nursery will be closed for six weeks as investigations are carried out.

A spokesperson said: "Our thoughts are with the family of the young boy and everyone affected by this deeply distressing and tragic incident.

"We have worked closely with the police, and we have suspended the nursery's registration. We will continue to support the police and other agencies in their enquiries. We are unable to comment further while the police investigation is ongoing."
