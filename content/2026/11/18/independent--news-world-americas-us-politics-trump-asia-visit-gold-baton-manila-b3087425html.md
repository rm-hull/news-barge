President Donald Trump has another gripe to add to his long list of grievances — he wasn’t given a gold baton for making an appearance in Manila during his Asia trip.

Trump is known for his love of gold — just look at the Oval Office — and his love of awards and gifts, like his $400 million Qatari jet.

He has repeatedly moaned about not getting a Nobel Peace Prize, despite starting a war with Iran, and even said he’d give himself the Medal of Honor if he could, despite never making it into the military after a timely case of bone spurs during the Vietnam War draft.

Now, Trump has complained to Filipino President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. that he didn’t appoint him field marshal or give him a gold baton at the Malacañang Palace, as Gen. Douglas MacArthur was awarded during his visit in 1936.

“Of course, it was right here, this very palace, that President [Manuel L.] Quezon bestowed Douglas MacArthur with a gold baton and the rank of field marshal in the Philippine Army. I’d like to have you do that to me if you don’t mind,” Trump said Wednesday to laughter from other attendees. “I’d love that. I mean, why the hell shouldn’t I get that?”

![Donald Trump complained to Filipino President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. that he didn’t appoint him field marshal or give him a gold baton at the Malacañang Palace, as General Douglas MacArthur was awarded during his visit in 1936, during his stop in Manila Wednesday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/17/00/GettyImages-2305149540.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

While Trump left Manila without the gold baton he was eyeing, he did not leave empty-handed — or rather, he did not leave hungry.

As Trump, a known McDonald’s lover, arrived in the Philippines Tuesday, Marcos Jr. greeted him with fried chicken from his country’s beloved fast food chain, Jollibee, to give him what the Filipino president called a “taste of Manila.”

“Jollibee is a very, very popular chain,” Marcos Jr. said. “And one of their main products is fried chicken. And we are very proud to say that we have surpassed KFC in the United States in terms of popularity.”

“Oh, wow. Fantastic,” Trump said in response.

![Trump arrived in the Chinese city of Shenzhen later Wednesday before his trilateral meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin to end his Asia trip](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/17/50/GettyImages-2305167096.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Trump arrived in the Chinese city of Shenzhen later Wednesday on the backdrop of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

In a rare move for the Chinese president, Xi welcomed Trump on the tarmac with a red carpet, cheerleaders and fighter jets. Trump put on a similar show for Xi when the Chinese leader visited Washington in September.

The president will end his 48-hour trip to Asia with a trilateral meeting Thursday with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. The leaders are expected to discuss tariffs, nuclear arms control, the war in Ukraine and restrictions on AI chips.
