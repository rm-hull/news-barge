David Beckham’s company is suing a beauty firm after his “luxury” branded fragrances were allegedly found for sale in discount stores, including Poundland, and a petrol station bargain bin.

DB Ventures has filed a claim in New York Supreme Court against German beauty group Coty, which makes and distributes a range of Beckham-branded fragrances and toiletries, seeking at least $41 million (about £31 million) in damages for breach of its licensing agreement.

The claim argues that Beckham’s “world-renowned global brand is synonymous with excellence and refinement,” and “David Beckham products are a symbol of everyday luxury.”

“Imagine, then, the shock and dismay experienced when DB Ventures discovered that David Beckham products were being offered for sale in a gas station,” the legal document says.

“How could this possibly have happened? Desperation and greed.”

Lawyers for DB Ventures allege that Beckham-branded products “ended up in the bargain bin inside a gas station” and at Poundland, “allowing the product to be sold for dollar store prices and associating the David Beckham brand with cheap, discount products.”

Coty has asked a judge to dismiss the lawsuit, which was filed in April.

The company – which distributes the Classic and Instinct lines and the eaux de toilette Intimately and Exquisite Leather – argues that New York courts have no jurisdiction in the case, and says DB Ventures is using the claim to “manufacture its early exit” from its licensing deal and hand over the Beckham contracts to another firm, Interparfums.

Coty and DB Ventures did not immediately respond to requests for comment on Tuesday.

Beckham, the former soccer star for Manchester United and Real Madrid and captain of the England men’s national team, parlayed success on the pitch into a lucrative business career that includes co-ownership of soccer team Inter Miami. He was knighted last year by King Charles III, becoming Sir David.

The Sunday Times Rich List earlier this year estimated the wealth of Beckham and his wife, Spice Girl-turned-fashion designer Victoria “Posh Spice” Beckham, at almost £1.2 billion.
