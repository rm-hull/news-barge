## Students praised after restoring memorial at Yorkshire Air Museum

Students have helped restore a historic war memorial at the Yorkshire Air Museum in Elvington *(Image: Ad Astra)*

Students have helped restore a historic war memorial at the Yorkshire Air Museum.

The memorial, located in Elvington, honours French airmen who served at RAF Elvington during the Second World War and was restored by students from York’s Ad Astra Alternative Learning Provision.

The project offered the students a chance to develop practical skills while contributing to a piece of living history.

A spokesperson for Ad Astra said: "We are incredibly proud of our students and the work they have put into restoring this memorial.

"This wasn't simply a painting and decorating project; it was an opportunity to contribute to preserving an important part of history.

"A special thank you to Becky from Spice Interior Design, whose dedication as one of our tutors has been instrumental in guiding and encouraging our students throughout the project."

The work was supported by Becky from Spice Interior Design and Laura from JW Myers Memorials, who completed the delicate gilding.
