Some of Scarborough’s Christmas lights could become a permanent feature of the town centre under plans being explored by town councillors.

This year’s festive display has been a success, according to town councillors, with members now looking at how lighting could be expanded and improved in future years.

Scarborough Town Council’s Assets and Infrastructure Committee heard that around £18,500 had been spent on new infrastructure for the scheme, less than originally expected, while a further £6,600 was spent on purchasing new lights.

At a meeting on Tuesday, November 17, the town clerk reported that the lights were now up and working, with the exception of issues affecting part of Aberdeen Walk after scaffolding was erected around lighting equipment.

Cllr Sarah Mason, who chairs the committee, said the council still had decorative lighting available and wanted to keep building on this year’s display.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/scarborough-christmas-lights-2026-anttoni-numminen-ldrs-1.webp)

“We’ve got a lot of braids left over,” she told colleagues.

Councillors discussed using some of the remaining lighting stock in additional locations across the town, including trees and public spaces that currently receive little festive decoration.

The committee also revisited a longer-term ambition that has been discussed several times this year: whether some decorative lights could remain in place throughout the year rather than only appearing during the Christmas season.

Cllr Mason suggested future investment should focus not only on seasonal displays but also on creating a brighter town centre all year round.

“We need to get on with that and have a project moving forward.”

Members highlighted locations including Alma Square, Pavilion House and other parts of the town centre as possible areas for future improvements.

The committee heard that additional electrical infrastructure may be needed to support future displays, with the installation of new power supplies potentially costing around £5,000 per location.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/scarborough-christmas-lights-2026-anttoni-numminen-ldrs-2.webp)

Councillors said the town council had budgeted £70,000 for Christmas lights and argued that some of that funding could be used to create longer-term infrastructure that would support future displays.

Cllr Mason said: “At least we’re trying as Scarborough Town Council to make it brighter.”

The assistant clerk told the meeting she was compiling suggestions from councillors, residents and businesses ahead of planning for next year’s displays.
