A share of a large funding boost has been awarded to support hands-on sustainability efforts in York.

More than £6,000 has been granted to Repair Cafés by enfinium, a UK energy-from-waste operator, to help expand volunteer-led repair services across the region.

The funding will support Repair Café Tang York, along with others in Yorkshire, to help residents repair household items rather than discard them.

Karl Smyth, director of corporate affairs and strategy at enfinium, said: "Repair Cafés are a great example of how local communities can make a real difference in reducing waste.

"By helping people to repair everyday items rather than replace them, these cafés save residents money, extend the life of household goods and bring people together to share practical skills."

Since launching its Repair Café Support Fund in March 2024, enfinium has provided more than £55,000 to local Repair Cafés, supporting the repair and reuse of thousands of items that might otherwise end up in landfill.

Spanner Spencer, from Slawit Repair Café, said: "Enfinium’s support allows us to help more people get their things repaired, free of charge, instead of throwing them away.

"We’re really grateful for the support, it’ll make a real difference to our work in the community."
