An 18-year-old apprentice has set the world record for the most backflips - by more than doubling the target.

Austin Beasty smashed the previous Guinness World Record of 1,234 backflips to complete a staggering 2,470 backflips in under 20 hours.

The teenager powered through the previous world record in just eight hours at Wolds Gymnastics Club in Driffield, on Sunday (November 15).




Austin, an engineering apprentice, also more than tripled his fundraising target for the Yorkshire Air Ambulance in memory of his late brother Jaygo.

Jaygo tragically lost his life in a road traffic collision near Middleton-on-the-Wolds four years ago, aged just 17.

The previous record for the most backwards somersaults in 24 hours was held by Mathias Stavdal in Moss, Norway, on June 15, 2025.

Now Austin, from Market Weighton in Yorkshire, officially broke the world record of 1,234 at 2.07pm on Saturday, November 14.

Refusing to stop there, he dug deep and pushed his body to the limit to complete an astonishing 2,470 backflips at 1.49am on Sunday, November 15.

Austin said: "When the clock hit 2:07pm and the record broke, the noise in the gym was unreal - I knew Jaygo was right there with me.

"My legs were burning and every muscle was aching, but having my family, my coach Harry, and the whole community cheering me on carried me through.

"To more than double the record and triple the money raised for Yorkshire Air Ambulance is beyond anything I could have dreamed of.

"Breaking the record means all the world to me, just to prove to myself and everyone else that you can do anything if you put your mind to it.

"You can achieve the impossible if you refuse to give up."

Watching from the sidelines throughout the gruelling marathon was Austin’s father, Dominic Beasty.

Dominic said: “From being absolutely tiny, Austin’s always been into anything that involves adrenaline, motocross, gymnastics and flipping.

"We’re immensely proud of him for achieving the world record, words can’t put it into perspective.

"He’s proven it to himself, he’s done it for his brother Jaygo and we’re all so proud that he’s done it for Yorkshire Air Ambulance.”

Austin has raised £4,636 of his £1,000 target for the Yorkshire Air Ambulance charity.
