# 'The calibre of talent is incredible': Top Yorkshire hairdressers compete for British Hairdressing Awards 2026

## Top Yorkshire hairdressers are set to go head to head as finalists in the prestigious British Hairdressing Awards 2026, which recognise the work and creativity of stylists and salons across the UK.

In the North Eastern Hairdresser of the Year category, the finalists are: Ross Charles of Ross Charles Hairdressing in York; Jenny Hainsworth and Kirstie Bowdin-Emsley of Era Harrogate; Emma Simmons of Salon 54 in Thirsk; Kirby Blythe of Hair by Kirby Blythe at Messingham near Scunthorpe; Claire Morley and Stephen Chapman, Contemporary Salons in Guisborough, Stokesley and Harrogate; and Jonathan Turner and Sophie Lenton, Hooker & Young, Newcastle area.

Olivia Brady from Allertons (Leeds/Harrogate) is a finalist for Newcomer of the Year, and Rick Roberts from Rick Roberts Salon (Beverley) is a finalist for Trend Image of the Year.

The awards, which will take place on Monday, November 23, at the JW Marriott Grosvenor House London, recognise outstanding creativity, technical excellence and artistic vision.

![Ross Charles of Ross Charles Hairdressing in York. Picture by David Charles.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmI0Yzk2MmNkLTRhMzktNDA2Yy05MTAyLTRkZTc3NjliYTQ0YzoxYzMyOTVlYi00YzJhLTRjMDYtYWJhNC1kNjBlZjM0ODU3MmE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Previous Yorkshire winners of the overall British Hairdresser of the Year award include Robert Eaton of Russell Eaton salons in Leeds and Barnsley in 2019 and 2021, Barnsley-born Andrew Barton in 2006, and Mark Hill of Kirk Ella, Hull, who won in 2000 and 2003.

Ross Charles says: "To be named a finalist at the British Hairdressing Awards is an incredible honour. The calibre of talent is incredible, which makes this recognition even more special. This will be the fifth time finalising and I am so proud of this collection.

“A huge thank you to my amazing clients who inspire me every day and have helped build the reputation that has brought me here. Congratulations to all the finalists.”
