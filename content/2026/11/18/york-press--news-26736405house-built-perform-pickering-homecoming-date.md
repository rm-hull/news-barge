A rock band is set for a two-night homecoming event.

This House We Built will perform at Pickering Memorial Hall on Friday, November 20 and Saturday, November 21.

The band will headline the Saturday night show, following performances by Dead Zebras, Fireheart, and Black Lakes.

On the Friday night, Different State, Those Made Broken, Wailing Banshee, and Thieves of Liberty will play.

There is also an after party at The Rose Inn, Pickering, Sunday, 22 at 2pm, which will feature lead singer Rhiannon and guitarist Joe from the band Kira Mac.

The band will also be releasing their debut album on limited edition LP which will be available over the weekend.

For more information go to www.thishousewebuilt.co.uk/shop
