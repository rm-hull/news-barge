A fraudster struck at a coastal North Yorkshire supermarket - and now police are looking for more information.

North Yorkshire Police has released a CCTV image of a person its officers want to speak to following fraud at a supermarket in Scarborough.

The incident took place at Tesco on Castle Road on October 10.
