Tesco customers are being encouraged to help bring joy to children in need by donating new toys in stores.

The supermarket’s annual Gift a Toy appeal is running until December 20, across large and selected Express stores across the UK, including those in York.

Customers can donate new, unwrapped toys purchased in store, which will be distributed to children in need by local charities and community groups.

Claire De Silva, head of communities at Tesco, said: "Throughout the year our stores play a huge role in supporting their local communities.

"The annual toy donation campaign is a wonderful way to make a difference at Christmas, and that’s all down to the hard work and generosity of our customers and colleagues.

"It's great to see stores building on their local connections to support charities making a difference locally to ensure that every child experiences the magic of Christmas."

Charities set to benefit from the scheme include Ty Hafan Children’s Hospice, Amelia’s Rainbow, and Caister Community Larder, as well as children’s hospital wards, foodbanks, and The Salvation Army.

Each Tesco store has selected its own charity partners based on relationships built through ongoing community support.

More than 800 large Tesco stores across the UK have set up dedicated donation points for customers to drop off toys.

Last year, more than 100,000 toys were donated through the scheme.

Tesco also runs other community programmes such as Free Fruit and Veg for Schools, which has delivered more than 20 million portions of fresh produce to children.
