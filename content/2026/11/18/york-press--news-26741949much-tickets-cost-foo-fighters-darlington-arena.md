Ticket prices for the Foo Fighters' historic Darlington Arena show have been confirmed.

The American band will visit Darlington on September 14, 2027, as part of the European leg of their Take Cover tour.

It will be their first performance in Darlington and their first North East appearance for more than 12 years.

Music lovers from York can hop on a train to Darlington which takes around 30 minutes.

More than 31,000 tickets will be on sale for the concert, being billed as the town's biggest ever music event.

The first presale opened at 10am this morning (Wednesday, November 18) via Ticketmaster and Gigs and Tours, with ticket prices for the gig now confirmed.

The cheapest tickets cost £62.50.

Some restricted view seats are going for £111.90, with side and front-facing seats priced at £156.70.

Standing tickets are available for £111.90, with "best seat experience" tickets costing £300 each.

Fees are included with all ticket orders, with a maximum of 6 tickets per purchase.

The Foo Fighters will come to Darlington after two shows at Wembley Stadium before playing Edinburgh’s Scottish Gas Murrayfield Stadium.

Dave Grohl will lead Foo Fighters in Darlington alongside Nate Mendel, Chris Shiflett, Pat Smear, Rami Jaffee and Ilan Rubin.

The set is expected to include hits such as Everlong, Best of You, Times Like These and The Pretender, with support from Grammy-winning artist St. Vincent and Leeds band Yard Act.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21721229.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Foo Fighters will play Darlington Arena on September 14, 2027, as part of the European leg of their Take Cover tour. *(Image: PR)*

Eligible fans who missed out on today's presale can still access the past bookers’ presale opens on November 19, with information being sent directly to their emails.

The past bookers’ presale is open to customers who previously bought tickets through participating promoters.

Fans who miss out on Foo Fighters presale tickets altogether can join the general sale on Friday, November 20, at 10am, subject to availability.

The concert was announced after Foo Fighters’ logo and the message “Monday 9am GMT” appeared on Darlington’s Clock Tower on November 12.

A similar projection appeared at Wembley Stadium before the European tour was confirmed.

The arena and organisers are expected to release more details before September.
