Chinese President Xi Jinping and his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen, China, on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, ahead of a planned trilateral summit with US President Donald Trump.

The meeting took place on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) gathering, with discussions expected to cover energy, finance and foreign policy, including Ukraine and North Korea.

The talks came a day before the three leaders were due to meet to address global security and relations between major powers.

Speaking at the APEC gathering, Putin called for dialogue rather than ultimatums and said he expected Thursday's summit to be 'highly useful'.
