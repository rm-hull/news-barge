# Inside the old gas pipeline where Ukraine is fighting an underground war with Russia

For the past seven months Ukrainian forces have been living and fighting Russian troops in an old Soviet gas pipeline in the Kharkiv region.

The BBC is the first international broadcaster to enter the pipeline where this subterranean battle is taking place.

With a diameter of just over a metre there's enough room only to crawl. But it is relatively safe from the drones that now saturate the skies above the front line – and which are making it nigh-on impossible for either side to advance.

The BBC's defence correspondent Jonathan Beale reports from near Kupyansk, where he went underground with the 13th Khartiia Operational Brigade.
