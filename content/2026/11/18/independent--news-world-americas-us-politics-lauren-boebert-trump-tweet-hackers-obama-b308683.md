Outgoing Republican Representative Lauren Boebert of Colorado drew widespread criticism on X on Tuesday after resurfacing a 12-year-old post from President Donald Trump that called on hackers to target former President Barack Obama’s college records and birth certificate.

Boebert quote-posted Trump’s September 6, 2014, message — in which he wrote, “Attention all hackers: You are hacking everything else so please hack Obama's college records (destroyed?) and check 'place of birth'” — and captioned it with only a face-with-hand-over-mouth emoji and a skull emoji.

The ousted MAGA hardliner seemed to think it was a good time to make a joke as massive tech outages wreaked havoc on Americans in the past few days, which included an air travel debacle over the weekend and several major banks having tech issues on Monday.

The Tuesday post quickly backfired in the replies, where users heavily criticized the congresswoman following her recent midterm election defeat. Many commenters mocked the post as a desperate attempt to stay relevant by recycling decade-old material, while others suggested she no longer needed to placate Trump or the MAGA movement after losing her seat.

Several users pointed to the post as an example of why voters rejected her in the midterms, urging her to focus on her remaining time in Congress rather than posting emojis. Others turned the joke back on Boebert by questioning her own educational background — she obtained her GED shortly before taking office — or suggesting hackers look into Trump’s academic transcripts from the Wharton School instead.

![Outgoing Republican Representative Lauren Boebert of Colorado was mocked on X on Tuesday after resurfacing a 12-year-old post from President Donald Trump that urged hackers to obtain former President Barack Obama’s college records](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/14/16/54/GettyImages-2264752004.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The online backlash comes just days after Boebert lost her re-election bid in Colorado’s 4th Congressional District to Democratic challenger Eileen Laubacher, a retired U.S. Navy rear admiral and former National Security Council member. The race was called on Friday, marking a major upset in a heavily Republican district that Trump carried by 18 percentage points in 2024.

Laubacher, a former Republican who switched parties after retiring from the military in 2024, ran as a moderate emphasizing constituent service.

“It was about people who felt that they were not being heard and not being served,” Laubacher told *The Washington Post*.

Laubacher significantly outpaced Boebert in fundraising, bringing in more than $13 million during the campaign cycle compared to Boebert’s $1.2 million, the newspaper reported.

Earlier in the week, while Laubacher attended new-member orientation on Capitol Hill, Boebert attributed her loss to outside spending, telling reporters that “there was a lot of money dumped on my head” and describing the financial disadvantage as “gross.” Boebert also told Scripps News that she did not plan to run for office again in 2028.

![The backlash comes just days after Boebert lost her heavily Republican district to Democratic challenger Eileen Laubacher, a former Navy rear admiral who outpaced her in fundraising by more than $11 million](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/13/12/2303887029..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Boebert, who was first elected in 2020 and built a national profile as a fierce supporter of the MAGA movement, had switched to the 4th District in 2024 after narrowly winning re-election in Colorado’s 3rd District two years prior.

Throughout her three terms in Washington, her tenure was frequently marked by public controversies, including being escorted out of a 2023 theater performance of *Beetlejuice*in Denver — an incident that featured heavily in Democratic attack ads during the recent campaign. Colorado-based Republican strategist Dennis Lennox told* The Washington Post* that Boebert’s time in office was characterized by “six years of largely unforced errors and scandals and endless controversies.”

![Critics responded by questioning the three-term lawmaker’s own educational background and suggesting hackers look into Trump’s Wharton School transcripts instead](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/01/download..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Her relationship with Trump also became strained in 2025 after she joined three other House Republicans in pushing for a vote to release additional files tied to Jeffrey Epstein and later campaigned for Representative Thomas Massie of Kentucky. Though Trump criticized her earlier in the year, he ultimately endorsed her campaign in late October.

Following Friday’s race call, Boebert posted a separate farewell video on X reflecting on her political rise from her former restaurant in Rifle, Colorado, writing that “God’s got this” and telling supporters to “Stay Tuned!” That post was similarly met with mockery online, with commenters referencing her past scandals to question her religious messaging.
