I've been secretly playing Grand Theft Auto 6 for the past several weeks and my suffering is finally at an end.

Not that I suffered from playing GTA 6! I had a blast, actually, and as I laid out in my not-a-review this morning, I really like the game. In fact, after putting in about 100 hours, I'm ready for 100 more, though I desperately wish the game were on PC.

You know, the way it should be. The way it's *meant* to be.

No, my suffering is that for the past two weeks I haven't been able to *talk*about GTA 6 with anyone, so I'm positively bursting to let a few things fly. With that in mind, here's my invitation to you:**Ask me anything!** In the comments below, ask me whatever burning questions you might have about GTA 6. I'm gonna be at my desk all day (and most of the night, probably), so I'll answer them as promptly as I can.

I don't mean ask stuff like "how does it end" or anything that could be considered a major spoiler, but stuff about how Jason and Lucia's relationship works, the activities, the characters, the world, the systems, and honestly, any other questions you might have about the game. I'll be checking the comments section for the next few days and on Friday we'll (probably) turn your questions and my answers into a story for the site.
