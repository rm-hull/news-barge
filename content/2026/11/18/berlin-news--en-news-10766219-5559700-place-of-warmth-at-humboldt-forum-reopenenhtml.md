From Wednesday through Sunday, between 2 and 6 p.m., homeless people and those affected by poverty can come here for a light meal, read, play board games and access low-threshold counseling. The opening ceremony will take place in the early afternoon. The "Place of Warmth" (German: Ort der Wärme) will be open on the days mentioned through April 11.
