Consumers are being urged to “shop small” and support their local traders in the coming weeks, ahead of the annual weekend day backing those traders who provide a hub of community and retail-lovers alike.

Small Business Saturday is on 5 December this year and aims to shine a spotlight on the independent local firms that are the backbone of the economy for a 14th year.

From cafes, to hairdressers and independent retailers, small businesses enhance peoples’ daily lives as well as support the financial well-being of many millions.

The campaign aims to get people to spend at some of the “much-loved” 5.7m small businesses across the country by “spending and shopping small”.

Organisers describe it as a year-round national movement rather than a one day event, that can make a big difference to communities and the economy. American Express founded and is sponsoring the campaign, which the organisers said has reached millions of people.

They added it is estimated that billions of pounds have been spent with small businesses as a result of the campaign’s thirteen years of operation.

A similar campaign has been running in the United States since 2010, and was originally an effort to help the American economy recover from the global financial crisis.

The organisers have put together a new video advert - viewable above - to promote the event, which follows a young shopper on a festive journey to showcase how rewarding supporting Small Business Saturday can be.

The shopper visits Cream Dream, a Ukrainian cafe in Covent Garden run by Lisa Tataryna, before moving to Mud House, a community pottery studio in Brighton run by Rosalind Poerwantoro.

Next, the woman then heads to award-winning hair salon Simon Webster Hair, run by Simon and Sophie Webster.

The advert concludes with the pair arriving at a festive meal at Shambles, an independent Italian restaurant in Teddington run by siblings Massimo and Margherita Langella. There they are welcomed by Maurice Press, a talented visually impaired pianist.

Michelle Ovens CBE, director of Small Business Saturday UK, said: “The launch of our festive advert is reminder of how much small businesses contribute to our everyday lives.

“They sit at the heart of our communities, bringing our high streets to life, creating jobs and offering unique experiences.

“This Small Business Saturday, we’re urging the public to celebrate their favourite small businesses, discover new ones and show their support during one of the most important trading periods of the year.”

Dan Edelman, general manager, UK Merchant Services at American Express, added: “As the founder and Principal Supporter of Small Business Saturday, American Express is proud to help shine a spotlight on the incredible independent businesses that make our high streets so special.

“This advert is a celebration of these businesses and those that run them, and a timely reminder of the difference we can all make by choosing to shop small.”
