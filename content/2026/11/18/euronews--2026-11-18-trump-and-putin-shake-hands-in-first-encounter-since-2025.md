US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin shook hands on Wednesday for the first time since August 2025, as they prepared for landmark talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping.

Trump was welcomed by Xi with a lavish airport ceremony in Shenzhen, including a red carpet, military honour guard and cheering crowds.

The three leaders are expected to meet on Thursday to discuss a range of major international issues, including the war in Ukraine, the conflict in Iran, nuclear proliferation, trade tensions and artificial intelligence.
