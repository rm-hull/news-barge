## GP surgery serving 10,000 patients given new CQC rating

A GP practice in Scarborough has received a new CQC rating (File image). *(Image: Jonathan Pow/[jp@jonathanpow.com](mailto:jp@jonathanpow.com))*

A GP practice serving almost 10,000 patients near Scarborough has retained an overall Good rating from the health watchdog following its latest inspection.

The Care Quality Commission (CQC) rated Ayton and Snainton Medical Practice as Good overall, with Good ratings awarded across all five inspection categories: safe, effective, caring, responsive and well-led.

Inspectors carried out the assessment between 29 October and 3 November and covered the practice’s main site in West Ayton and its branch surgery in Snainton.

The practice provides GP services to approximately 9,819 patients and also operates a branch surgery in Thornton-le-Dale.

Inspectors praised the practice’s culture, staffing and patient care, finding that people were treated with kindness and compassion and that staff felt supported in their roles. The watchdog said managers investigated incidents thoroughly, maintained a good learning culture and ensured facilities were clean, safe and well maintained.

The report stated: “People were treated with kindness and compassion. Staff protected their privacy and dignity.”

Inspectors also found that patients were involved in decisions about their care and that the practice worked to reduce health inequalities while ensuring services remained accessible.

Feedback from patients was largely positive.

The CQC reviewed responses from patients, NHS survey data, feedback from the practice’s Patient Participation Group and information from Healthwatch, with no major concerns identified.

However, inspectors did identify some areas where improvements were needed.

The report notes that clinical searches found a small number of cases where medicines guidance issued by the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) was not being fully followed.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21734611.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ayton And Snainton Medical Practice, Scarborough. Google Maps

Inspectors also said certain governance and monitoring arrangements could be strengthened.

The watchdog said: “Care was not always based on latest evidence and good practice.”

Inspectors added that the practice took immediate action when the issues were highlighted.

In the well-led section of the report, inspectors found leaders and staff shared a culture based on learning, trust and continuous improvement, but said some governance processes required further review to strengthen oversight and monitoring arrangements.

Despite those observations, the practice retained Good ratings in every inspection category and overall.
