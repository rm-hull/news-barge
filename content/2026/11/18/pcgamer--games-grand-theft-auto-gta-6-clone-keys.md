Vice City's glow-up in GTA 6 ushers in a new era of smart car tech, bringing keyless ignition to the series. That's great for car owners, but a pain for our criminal duo since you can't hotwire a keyless car. Luckily, that doesn't mean you can't steal a car with high-tech security. Modern problems call for modern crimes, and that's where the **key cloner** comes in.

## How to get a key cloner

![GTA 6 key cloner in use](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/YDUDXzPPjWk8DjVR9FLqGY-1200-80.jpg)

* **You get the key cloner in Chapter 3 during the "Steal the Zorruso for Randy" mission.** It's pretty far into GTA 6, but the convenience of stealing keyless cars is worth the wait.
* To unlock a keyless car, stand next to the vehicle's door, press the interact button, and select "Clone Key." Use the control stick to find the sweet spot to speed up the process.
* The key cloner unlocks and starts keyless cars, making it a serious upgrade over the slim jim while also introducing a new problem: trackers.
* The Waink It app on your phone can identify keyless cars when you scan them, so you'll know what you're up against.

## Beware of trackers when stealing keyless cars

![GTA 6 key cloner in use](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/2xyMxoqYUvcuupSthFHDaZ-1200-80.png)

**Standard trackers** on stolen cars aren't much of a problem—they just forbid you from making modifications at a mod shop unless you register the car as your own.**Advanced trackers**, however, are a whole other beast. They relay your exact location to the cops, but you can outrun the signal if you're fast enough. Keep your foot on the gas, and the cops won't be able to track you until you slow down. Apparently, the solution to beat high-tech surveillance is still a good ol' fashioned pedal-to-the-metal joyride.
