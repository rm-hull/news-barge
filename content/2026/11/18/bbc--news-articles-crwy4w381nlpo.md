Harry Styles will compete with Cher for the weekend's most flamboyant outfit

**Harry Styles will make his Glastonbury festival debut next year as one of the event's headliners, alongside Sam Fender and Billie Eilish - with Cher among the other stars on the bill.**

The veteran US pop icon will play the coveted Sunday afternoon "legends" slot, which is often considered a fourth headline set. It will be her only UK festival appearance of the year.

Other acts on the line-up include Sombr, Sienna Spiro, Lily Allen, The Streets, Chase & Status, Kacey Musgraves, Primal Scream and breakout pop star Adéla.

Tickets for the festival sold out in just 42 minutes last month, with demand particularly high after the event took a year off in 2026.

![Cher on stage](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/de8c/live/414e67b0-e2c8-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

Cher is bound to draw a huge crowd to hear hits like Turn Back Time, Believe and I Got You Babe

Electro pioneers Kraftwerk will make their Glastonbury debut with a headline set at West Holts - the festival's third-largest stage.

The event will also welcome experimental Canadian band Angine de Poitrine, who have drawn attention for their prog-rock sound and polka-dotted stage costumes; rap legends De La Soul and Mike D; and pop provocateur FKA Twigs.

"We are so happy to be coming back after our fallow year and, as we head into winter after that peaceful period, we wanted to give you an early musical taster of what's to come at Glastonbury 2027 with this spread of incredible artists," said organiser Emily Eavis.

"We can't wait to see you down here on Worthy Farm in June for some much-needed good times!"

The gates will open at Worthy Farm in Somerset to 200,000 fans on Wednesday 23 June, with music on the main stages kicking off on Friday 25 June.

Tickets prices are at an all-time high of £408, including a £5 booking fee per ticket.

![Sam Fender on stage](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/9cf8/live/6466eec0-e2c8-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

Festival organiser Emily Eavis watched Sam Fender's set from the side of the stage in 2022

Sam Fender will headline the first night, in what will be his second appearance at the festival.

He last played in 2022, immediately before Billie Eilish, and fought back tears as thousands of fans chanted along to hits like Seventeen Going Under and Hypersonic Missiles.

"Me and the boys have never been here before," he said. "We've never been here as punters, never played here before and we're doing that all in one day, which is the most crazy experience."

He will return as one of the UK's biggest rock stars, having won the Mercury Prize for his third album, People Watching, in 2025. Earlier this year, his duet with Olivia Dean - Rein Me In - became the UK's longest-running number one single of all time.

Fender is expected to release a new album ahead of his Glastonbury slot, which will land in the middle of his catchily-titled Proper Massive Stadium Tour.

![Billie Eilish plays Glastonbury in 2022](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/704a/live/231b6310-e2c8-11f1-b8cb-ad3732a9717a.jpg)

Billie Eilish was the youngest solo act to headline Glastonbury when she topped the bill in 2022

Harry Styles will take the Pyramid Stage crown on the Saturday.

It comes amid acclaim for his euphoric and athletic Together Together tour, which largely focuses on the understated dance anthems of his fourth album Kiss All The Time. Disco Occasionally.

The pop star has previously been spotted partying at the festival's late-night dance arenas, despite attempting to go incognito in tracksuits and sunglasses.

In 2022, he was rumoured to be making a guest appearance during Eilish's set after his music was played during an early-morning soundcheck - but the duet never materialised.

Instead, Eilish gave a universally well-received performance, described as "imperious [and] hugely engaging" by the Guardian,, external and "proof that the future is here" by the NME, external.

At the time, she was 20 years old - making her the youngest-ever solo act to headline Glastonbury.

Aged between 24 and 32, Styles, Fender and Eilish represent the festival's youngest set of Pyramid Stage headliners since 1997.

Cher, meanwhile, will be the oldest ever act to play the legend slot - strutting through the gates of Worthy Farm at the age of 81.

The diva's appearance will come eight years after her last UK tour, and 63 years into a career that began with her ex-husband Sonny Bono, and recording sessions with Phil Spector's legendary Wrecking Crew band.

She emerged as a solo artist in the 1970s, scoring three US number ones, before switching into disco mode with Take Me Home.

In the 1980s, she became an award-winning actress and scored chart hits with FM rock staples like Turn Back Time and Just Like Jesse James.

Then in 1998, she revitalised her career with club anthem Believe, which helped to popularise the use of autotune in dance music.

Inducted to the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2024, she is about to release the second volume of her memoir.

She has previously talked about retiring from live performance - but it seems the lure of Glastonbury was too tempting to turn down.

Cher will follow previous bookings in the crowd-pleasing legends slot including Sir Rod Stewart, Shania Twain, Diana Ross and Kylie Minogue.

More details about the 2027 line-up will be revealed in the New Year. Eavis has previously dismissed rumours that Madonna and Oasis will feature on the bill.
