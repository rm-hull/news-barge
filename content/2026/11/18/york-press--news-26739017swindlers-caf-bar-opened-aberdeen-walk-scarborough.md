## 'We want to be part of the community' - venue opens in coastal North Yorkshire town

Ben who owns Swindlers *(Image: Provided)*

A NEW bar which hopes to ‘a bit more life into the evenings’ by the coast has opened its doors in a coastal North Yorkshire town.

Swindlers, a new café and bar located in Aberdeen Walk, Scarborough, is celebrating a successful first month in business after first opening its doors on October 16.

Owned by local entrepreneur Ben Stonehouse, the new venue offers coffee, cocktails, and has recently launched a full food menu - with hopes of filling what Ben sees as a gap in Scarborough’s hospitality scene.

He said: "The public response has been great, just to see something opening up rather than another place shutting down.

"We want to become a local meeting spot for people - a place for people to meet, catch up with old friends, and feel welcome."

Swindlers plans offering quiz nights, cheese and wine evenings, and cocktail-making sessions – with food set to be offered in the evenings after Christmas.

Ben said: "We’re aiming to do something a bit different for Scarborough. There aren’t many places offering this kind of experience, especially for a crowd that aren’t either really young or older.

"The evening scene in Scarborough has declined, and we’re hoping to help turn that around."

Ben has also been involved in other ventures, including the quirky helicopter stays at Wild Encounters, but says Swindlers is “about giving back to the town” and providing employment.

![Swindlers in Scarborough hopes to give back to the community](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21731492.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Swindlers in Scarborough hopes to give back to the community *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

He said: "This isn’t about personal gain. It’s about creating jobs and doing something positive for Scarborough."

“We want people to be able to come in for a coffee during the day, stay for a drink in the evening, and feel just as comfortable throughout."

The venue includes a basement area that is currently being renovated for private dining, but also has space for games, relaxed social events, and a ‘bit of mischief’.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21731493.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The private dining area in Swindlers, Scarborough *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

Ben added said: "It’s about building something that lasts. We’ve had a lot of support so far and we’re really grateful for that."

"Scarborough is full of independent shops and venues. It’s a great place, and we want to help bring a bit more life into the evenings, especially outside of the main tourist season."

"We’ve got a lot planned for next year, including live music, more food options, and a regular calendar of events. It’s still early days, but we’re excited to see how it grows.

"We want to be part of the community and contribute to the town’s recovery and growth. It’s about more than just business – it’s about helping to shape Scarborough’s future."
