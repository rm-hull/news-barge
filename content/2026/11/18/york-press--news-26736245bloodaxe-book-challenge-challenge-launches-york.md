Two time-travelling penguins have helped launch York’s 2027 Bloodaxe Book Challenge.

Pablo and Splash, the penguin duo from Sheena Dempsey’s graphic novel Pablo & Splash: Viking Voyage, are at the heart of this year’s Bloodaxe Book Challenge in York.

The annual programme is a partnership between the JORVIK Viking Festival and Explore York Libraries, encouraging children across the city to read and discover new books.

Sheena Dempsey said: "Getting children into reading can take many forms, and graphic novels are a fantastic way of making reading fun for young people.

"As my latest book features Pablo and Splash travelling back to the Viking age, I’m thrilled to be working with the team from JORVIK Viking Festival to share the story with children around York.

"Our penguin friends would love the reward for completing the initiative – a ticket to JORVIK Viking Centre where everyone gets to visit the Vikings in the 10th century."

Ms Dempsey visited St Oswald’s CE Primary School in Fulford to read from her book and lead a Viking-themed drawing workshop.

Erin Tattersall of York Archaeology, co-ordinator of the challenge, said: "We're delighted to welcome Sheena as the latest in a long line of fantastic children's authors who have supported the challenge over the years."

Children who read four library books between November 28 and January 21 will earn a bookmark, stickers, a certificate, and a free ticket to the JORVIK Viking Centre.

The challenge is named after Erik Bloodaxe, the Viking king who once ruled York.
