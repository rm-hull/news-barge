## Period home in ‘sought after’ York location hits the market – have a look inside

Inside the three-bedroom semi-detached house for sale in Park Lane, off Acomb Road, York, for £475,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

A period home has hit the market in a "sought after" York location.

The three-bedroom semi-detached house is for sale in Park Lane, off Acomb Road, for £475,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said it is a "much-loved" home which is "well placed for access to York city centre" with "good transport links".

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21699524.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the three-bedroom semi-detached house for sale in Park Lane, off Acomb Road, York, for £475,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside, a modern kitchen is on the ground floor of the home.

Also on the ground floor is the dining room and lounge with a log burner.

Upstairs are two double bedrooms and a third bedroom "ideal for being used as a nursery/office", according to Ashtons.

The home's main bathroom is also on the first floor.
