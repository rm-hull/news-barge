Commercial property searches are surging across Yorkshire, with Selby, Ripon, Scarborough, York and Bridlington among the fastest-growing hotspots.

The data, compiled by Hiscox from Google search data, shows a 24 per cent year-on-year increase in commercial property interest across Yorkshire and the Humber, with Selby recording a 265 per cent rise.

Ripon saw a 51 per cent increase, followed closely by Scarborough and York at 49 per cent each, and Bridlington at 30 per cent.

York also ranked third in Yorkshire for total search volume, with 17,480 searches, while Scarborough placed sixth with 3,990.

Nick Thornhill, director and partnerships director at Hiscox, said: "Physical locations continue to offer important advantages for businesses, whether that's attracting customers, building communities, strengthening brand presence or supporting collaboration among teams.

"Our research suggests businesses are taking a broad view of where those opportunities might exist.

"Interest is growing across a breadth of locations, indicating that businesses are considering a wider range of towns and cities when planning for growth."

The analysis reflects a broader trend of businesses seeking growth, despite ongoing economic pressures.

Matthew Hayes, managing director of business solutions firm Champions, advised business owners to use a structured approach when selecting a site.

He said: "Small businesses should assess a location through a structured scoring process rather than relying purely on instinct.

"A simple matrix can be useful.

"Business owners should list the factors that matter most to them, score each location out of 10 and give each factor a weighting based on importance.

"Those factors might include cost, customer access, staff commute, parking, public transport, local demographics, nearby competitors, brand perception, room for growth, and lease flexibility."

He also recommended visiting potential locations at different times of day to assess real-world conditions.
