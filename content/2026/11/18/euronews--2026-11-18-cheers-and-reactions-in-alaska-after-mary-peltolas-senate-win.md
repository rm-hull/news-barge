Democrat Mary Peltola has defeated two-term Republican Senator Dan Sullivan in Alaska, completing a political comeback in a state where President Donald Trump has won by wide margins.

The result makes Alaska the last Senate race to be decided in the 2026 US midterm elections and gives Democrats a 56-44 majority in the chamber, after flipping nine Republican-held seats.

Peltola's victory was welcomed by supporters across Alaska, particularly members of Native communities, who say she understands the difficulties of living in remote villages, including the high cost of living.
