A man has been found guilty of manslaughter following a fatal stabbing during a fracas outside a tenpin bowling alley in Croydon, south London, after celebrating Valentine’s Day.

Ellis Anim-Annor, 29, claimed he was acting in self-defence and to protect his girlfriend when he fatally injured Lorik Abazi, 22, in the early hours of 15 February.

After an Old Bailey jury deliberated for almost 12 hours, Anim-Annor, from Bromley, south London, was found not guilty of murder but guilty of manslaughter.

Anim-Annor was also acquitted of wounding a second man with intent, with the jury convicting him of the lesser alternative offence of unlawful wounding by a majority of 10 to one.

The court had previously heard how the defendant and his girlfriend, Ines Bernado, had been celebrating Valentine’s Day at Tenpin Croydon Bowling Alley where Mr Abazi, his partner and friends had also gone.

Prosecutor Anthony Orchard KC had said there was no trouble inside the venue with both groups leaving at around 1am, before the defendant went to a nearby drive-through McDonald’s restaurant.

![Lorik Abazi was stabbed to death in the early hours of 15 February](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/12/01M7QB329X3M3EXPCKBMAH3RRG.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He subsequently returned to the area and engaged in a confrontation with Mr Abazi’s party outside the bowling alley in the Valley Retail Park car park, it was alleged.

Mr Orchard told jurors: “The reason behind the confrontation is not entirely clear. There is some suggestion one of Lorik’s group had earlier remarked on Ms Bernado’s appearance.

“The confrontation escalated into a fight. The defendant had armed himself with a knife. During the fight he used it to stab Lorik Abazi in the heart. Two other members of Lorik Abazi’s group also suffered slash or stab injuries.”

Emergency services were called at 1.19am and Mr Abazi was treated at the scene before being taken to Croydon University Hospital, where he died at 2.01am.

The other injured men were treated at the scene and taken to St George’s Hospital in Tooting, south London.

Giving evidence, Anim-Annor claimed he had acted in self-defence although he accepted he had the knife hidden in his pocket when he got out of his car. He told jurors he wanted to put on a “performance” for his partner and afterwards threw the weapon out of his car window.

Following the verdict, Judge Rebecca Trowler KC ordered a report on the defendant’s dangerousness and adjourned sentencing until 5 February. Anim-Annor was remanded into custody.

Detective Chief Inspector Alex Gammampila, of the Metropolitan Police, highlighted the tragic consequences of carrying knives after the verdict was read out.

He said: “I want to pay tribute to Lorik’s partner and his family for their support as we investigated this tragic death, as well as their dignity during the trial process.

![Anim-Annor was found guilty of manslaughter by an Old Bailey jury](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/01/16/2173673457..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Like so many of the cases my team deals with, this is another incident that shows the danger of knives on London’s streets.

“The Met is committed to tackling knife crime, which we know is a real concern to the community in Croydon, where this incident took place, and London more widely.

“Lorik’s death could so easily have been prevented had Ellis Anim-Annor not armed himself with a knife that night. I would urge anyone who, like this defendant, thinks little of carrying knives in public to think carefully about their decision. It can only end in tragedy.”

Mr Abazi’s family thanked police and the Crown Prosecution Service for their “hard work, kindness and sensitivity in handling this tragedy”.

They said in a statement: “We are, however, disappointed with the outcome, but we respect the jury’s verdict and look forward to hearing a sentence that reflects the seriousness of the crime, and that acknowledges that we have lost our son, Lorik, for no obvious reason.

“We are, and continue to be, devastated beyond words and our family is broken.”
