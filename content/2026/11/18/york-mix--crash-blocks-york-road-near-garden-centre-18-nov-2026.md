A road has been reopened after a serious crash in York.

Stockton Lane, near Dean’s Garden Centre, was shut after the collision.

The collision was near to the junction with Holtby Lane.

#### Crash location

Witnesses said there were multiple ambulances at the scene, along with fire engines and the police.

One person posted on Facebook: “Avoid Holtby Lane / Stockton on the Forest near Deans Garden Centre.

“Looks like a very bad accident on the Holtby lane junction. Nine ambulances, fire engine and police. Traffic being diverted.”

The Coastliner bus service was diverted for a while. It is now back to its normal timetable.
