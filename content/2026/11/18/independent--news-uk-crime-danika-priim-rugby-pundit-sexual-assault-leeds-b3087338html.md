A former England rugby league player and TV pundit accused of sexually assaulting a woman in a cricket club toilet has told a jury that it “categorically did not happen”.

Earlier this week, a woman told Leeds Crown Court that Danika Priim twice reached up her dress and grabbed her underwear at Horsforth Cricket Club on 22 July 2022.

Two of the complainant’s friends told the court they also witnessed the incident, but, on Wednesday, Priim denied ever being in the toilets with the three women.

When it was put to Priim by prosecutor Chloe Hudson that either she was lying or the complainant and her two friends were lying, Priim told the jury: “It categorically did not happen.”

Priim agreed that she was not suggesting that it was an accident, or the woman had invited her to touch her, or it was a joke.

![Priim says the alleged sexual assault 'categorically did not happen'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/16/2153842647..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

She told the jury she was never in the toilets with the complainant and “I did not touch (her) in the toilets at all”.

Ms Hudson said to her: “What you must be saying is that these three got together to make completely false allegations about you.”

Priim responded: “It’s the biggest question I have – it’s the why.”

The prosecutor asked the defendant about how the complainant demonstrated to the jury how she was grabbed.

Priim said: “She was lying. I did not do that. I’m also a very happy heterosexual woman. I’m with a man. I live with a man.”

The prosecutor said: “The truth is that you sexually assaulted (the woman), didn’t you?”

Priim stated: “The truth is that it never happened.”

Ms Hudson asked Priim whether she thought the complainant and her two friends had colluded in a “witch hunt against you”.

Priim, who was wearing a chunky light brown cardigan and a cream-and-black scarf while stood in the witness box, replied: “I believe they have.”

On Tuesday, the complainant described how Priim “walked straight up to me and put her hand up my skirt and grabbed hold of my pants and my private parts”.

The woman said: “I said: ‘What the f\*\*\* are you doing?’ Then she did it again, grinning at me, and walked off.”

One of the complainant’s friends told the jury how she witnessed Priim put her hand up the woman’s skirt.

She told the court: “I recall seeing her remove her hand, laughed about it and then repeated it. She said ‘wahey’.”

Giving evidence on Wednesday, Priim said she only went to the toilet once during her time at the cricket club, and produced a photograph she took of her and two friends in there, to help the jury with timings.

![Priim is a former England rugby league player](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/16/877302162..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

She said she drove to the venue and was drinking soft drinks until later when she had one pint.

Asked about the police getting involved following the allegation, she said: “It absolutely broke me.”

Priim, 42, of Keighley, West Yorkshire, played for Bradford Bulls, Leeds Rhinos and represented England, including at the 2017 World Cup.

She retired from the game in 2021 and she told the jury she has appeared on BBC, Channel4 and Sky Sports as a pundit, reporter and commentator.

Priim denies one count of sexual assault.

The jurors were sent out to consider their verdict on Wednesday afternoon.
