Becky Hill will headline an open-air concert at Harewood House next year.

The two-time BRIT Award winner will perform in the grounds of the estate near Harrogate on Thursday 10th June 2027, with space for up to 8,000 fans.

Hill topped the UK singles chart with Oliver Heldens on ‘Gecko (Overdrive)’.

Her other hits include ‘Remember’ with David Guetta, ‘Wish You Well’ with Sigala and ‘Disconnect’ with Chase & Status.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/663dd5f45fc6c.jpg)

She has previously played sold-out shows at Leeds Arena and Scarborough Open Air Theatre and performed on the Chevron Stage at Leeds Festival in 2025.

Liam Hardy, managing director of Iconic Concerts, said:

> “We’re hugely excited to be bringing Becky Hill to Harewood House.
>
> "There’s something special about seeing an artist with Becky’s energy and voice performing in an open-air setting, especially somewhere as beautiful as Harewood.
>
> “It’s going to be a brilliant summer night for live music in Yorkshire, and we look forward to revealing more details soon about what fans can expect on the night.”

Street food traders and bars will be on site. Organisers will announce the support acts in the coming months.

*Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday 27 November 2026 through Skiddle and See Tickets.*
