I'm modding GTA 5 every day in a quest to either transform it into a PC version of GTA 6 or break it entirely. Come back tomorrow to find out what new horrible thing I've done to Rockstar's baby, or check out what I got up to earlier.

Well, it's almost time. The big day is upon us: Grand Theft Auto 6 is here.

I mean, not *here*. We don't have it. But over in consoleland my understanding is the masses are—as we speak—building some sort of life-partnership with their new husband/wife. Also, doing crimes. Also, shooting kneecaps.

Which means it's almost time to end this journey. I think we can all agree: my hyper-modded version of GTA 5 is, if anything, much better than whatever nonsense Rockstar has people doing over in Vice City. Does Vice City have Crazy Taxi? Does Vice City have roving bands of cougars? Does Vice City have, uh, *banking*? I think not.

And since my quest is so clearly, definitively complete, it's time to say goodbye. Almost. We'll actually properly say goodbye tomorrow, but for now, I have installed Farewell Journey – A Goodbye to GTA 5, which bills itself as "One last day in Los Santos before moving on to GTA 6".

It's mostly a series of goodbyes between Franklin and pals before he boards a plane, for some reason, to Vice City. Or it should be. With the state my copy of GTA 5 is in, it all gets a bit… harder to parse. Please enjoy, and I will see you back here tomorrow for our own final farewell.
