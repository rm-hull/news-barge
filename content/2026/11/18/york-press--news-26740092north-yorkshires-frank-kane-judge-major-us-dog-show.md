A renowned dog judge from North Yorkshire is set to take centre stage at a major international canine event.

Frank Kane will judge the Sporting Group and Miscellaneous Group at the 26th annual American Kennel Club (AKC) National Championship, presented by Royal Canin, in Orlando, Florida.

The event will be broadcast nationally in the United States on ABC on Sunday, December 27.

Mr Kane said: "I have been very fortunate to judge for three years at the AKC shows in Florida, one of the glittering prizes in the canine calendar.

"This year I feel honored to be judging the Sporting Group, where my interest in dogs was founded, and look forward to judging the Miscellaneous breed classes which show the increasing diversity in breeds from around the world.

"I am looking forward with great anticipation to a memorable experience."

Mr Kane’s involvement in the dog world began in 1962 with his first English Cocker Spaniel.

Since then, he has shown Sealyham Terriers and American Cocker Spaniels, producing 13 UK champions, including three group winners.

He has judged dog breeds since 1974 and is now approved to judge 150 breeds across all groups.

His judging credentials include Best in Show at Crufts in 2012 and two appearances at the Westminster Kennel Club Dog Show in the US.

He has also judged Best in Show at the Santa Barbara Kennel Club Dog Show and other major shows.

Beyond the show ring, Mr Kane has served on the Board of the Kennel Club, chaired the Breed Standards Committee for 15 years, and led the Health Committee for seven years.

He was instrumental in introducing seminars on conformation and movement and has delivered many breed-specific seminars.

A published author, Mr Kane has written three books, including Judging the Gundog Breeds, which has been used in many countries as the core for educating judges.

He is also a contributing journalist and has been part of the Crufts television commentary team for 20 years.

A spokesperson for the American Kennel Club said the AKC National Championship is one of the world’s premier canine events and the largest dog show in North America.

The event brings together exceptional dogs, skilled competitors, and passionate enthusiasts for a celebration of canine excellence.

This year’s show features more than 9,000 entries across a wide range of competitions, highlighting the versatility, athleticism, and skill of dogs.

Events include the AKC Agility Invitational, AKC Obedience Classic, and AKC Fast CAT Invitational, as well as junior competitions in conformation, agility, obedience, and rally.

The AKC National Championship presented by Royal Canin is said to be "one of the world’s premier canine events and the largest dog show in North America".
