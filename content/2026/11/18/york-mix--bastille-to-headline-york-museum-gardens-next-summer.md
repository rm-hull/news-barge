Chart-topping, Brit Award-winning pop titans Bastille have been announced to headline York Museum Gardens next summer.

They are the latest headliner revealed for next year’s Live at York Museum Gardens concert series, and will play on **Friday 2 July 2027**.

Bastille will be joining the previously announced fellow headliners Olly Murs and the Human League in York next summer.

Founded in 2010, the quartet’s debut album, *Bad Blood*, which features the international smash hit ‘Pompeii’, entered the charts at no. 1 and propelled Bastille to become the biggest-selling global breakthrough act of the year, with over a billion streams in 2013 alone.

Two more acclaimed albums and countless hit singles followed, including 2018’s massive collaboration with Marshmello, ‘Happier’, which topped charts around the world and spent a record breaking year at no. 1 on Billboard’s Hot Dance/Electronic list.

Their latest full length release, 2022’s magnificent, sci-fi inspired concept album *Give Me The Future*, has been hailed by many as Bastille’s finest work and landed the band their third no. 1 record.

Returning for a fourth season, this summer’s Live at York Museum Gardens series saw sold-out performances from the likes of Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark, Self Esteem and Super Furry Animals.

There was also standup from comedy headliners Russell Howard, Ross Noble and Joanne McNally and many more as part of sister event York Comedy Festival.

Live at York Museum Gardens’ independent promoters, Leeds based Futuresound Group and York Museums Trust are set to reveal more of this newly extended collaboration and deliver two weekends of amazing outdoor summer concerts.
