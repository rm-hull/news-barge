## Creativity is becoming part of Qatar’s economic vision. Beyond galleries and exhibition spaces, Qatar is investing in infrastructures designed to nurture creatives so that their concepts can turn into creation.

A love for art is part of Qatar’s culture. Across the country, it’s hard to miss the bold architecture and public art. But more than what meets the eye lies a growing ecosystem designed to turn ideas into opportunity and creativity into enterprise. In this episode of Qatar in Motion, Laila Humairah steps inside Design Doha Biennial and the Doha Design District, creative hubs and incubation spaces to meet the people building Qatar’s new creative economy.
