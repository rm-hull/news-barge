The family of Jade Langton have spoken of their “unimaginable loss” after her death in a car crash in Leeds.

Jade, 23, from York, died when the white Volkswagen Scirocco she was in was hit by a blue BMW 123D on Gelderd Road in the early hours of Sunday (15 November).

The VW was driven by Cameron Bell, also from York.

Cameron, 22, died at the scene. A man has appeared in court charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving.

Today, Emma Langton, Jade’s devastated mum, shared this tribute to her daughter, a former pupil at All Saints RC School in York.

“We are completely devastated by the loss of our beautiful Jade.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/jade-langton-york.webp)

“We are struggling to come to terms with what has happened and the reality that she has  
been taken from us far too soon.

“Jade had a big personality and loved being with people, chatting, giggling, going to live music, travelling and other adventures. She left an impact wherever she was.

“Whilst she always loved a bit of drama, Jade did not deserve to have her life cut short in  
this way.

“There is a big hole in our family and she is already hugely missed.

“Thank you to the emergency services and everyone who did everything they could to help.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crash-gelderd-road-leeds-15-nov-2026-pa-4.webp)

“The kindness, support and condolences we have received from family, friends and the  
wider community is appreciated.

“We share our deepest condolences to Cam’s family and friends too.

“As we try to navigate this unimaginable loss, we respectfully ask people to be mindful  
about what they share and comment on social media.

“We kindly ask that our family’s privacy is respected while we grieve together and try to  
come to terms with losing our Jade.”

Friends have also paid tribute to Jade.

Skye Hagan posted on Facebook: “Sadly on Sunday 15th of November at 12:44am I lost my best friend Jade Langton.

“She was the most caring loving and craziest person I have ever met. She never failed to cheer me up.

“I spent every day with her for nearly the past tw years and now I’m never going to get a single second with her again and that has broken me. I honestly can not believe this has happened.

“I love you endlessly Jade and I never said that enough. I will miss you forever my beautiful angel. Rest easy.”

Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, has appeared in court.

He is charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while uninsured, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle whilst disqualified and two counts of causing death having taken a vehicle without consent.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police Major Collision Enquiry Team are continuing to appeal for any witnesses to any part of the incident.

Specialist family liaison officers are supporting the families of the two people who died. The families have requested that people respect their privacy at what continues to be a very difficult time for them

Anyone with information about the incident, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online via the Live Chat facility on our website quoting reference 13260649342.
