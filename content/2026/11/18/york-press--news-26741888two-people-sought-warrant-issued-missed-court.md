A WARRANT has been issued for two people with links across the UK.

North Yorkshire Police are searching for James Williams, 46, and Vicki McWilliams, 43, after they failed to attend court.

They have connections in Salisbury, North Yorkshire and Scotland - and both speak with a Scottish accent.

A police spokesperson said: "They are thought to be in Salisbury, possibly in a homeless shelter."

If you see them, please call North Yorkshire Police on 999 or for any information, contact 101. Information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers.
