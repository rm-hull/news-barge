A Clarkson’s Farm star has hinted that she is filming a farming spin-off series.

Earlier this year, Harriet Cowan revealed that she had decided to make a move to her own farm.

The former nurse opened up about how she had split from her partner, James, who she had been with for eight years.

Harriet has now been dropping hints that she is filming her own series away from Clarkson’s Farm.

## Clarkson’s Farm’s Harriet Cowan filming spin-off series

In a TikTok video Harriet posted sharing parts of her daily life on the farm, one fan spotted a cameraman in the corner of the video.

Harriet responded to a comment about the cameraman with a side-eye emoji.

A source told The Sun: “Harriet’s in the beginning stages of filming a really exciting project.

“She’s been through a lot of change recently and she’s looking forward to putting her all into making her new farm a big success.

“The venture has nothing to do with Prime Video – and Harriet has been very much enjoying getting to know a different team.

“Harriet loved every minute of her time working on Clarkson’s Farm, it completely changed her life and opened her up to so many opportunities social media wise.”

Harriet first starred on Clarkson’s Farm in its fourth series that was broadcast in 2025.

While Harriet has not always been on the show, fans quickly warmed to her.

In April, she revealed she had given up her job as a nurse to be able to take on more opportunities.

Harriet spoke on the Fed by Farmers podcast about this decision.

She said: “The stress of doing social media, being a nurse, a farmer and doing brand deals was too much.

“In black and white I don’t need the money.

“I was working all week and I’d be earning 34k a year but I just did not need that, that was nothing you can earn in the social media game, I look at some things and think it’s crazy.
