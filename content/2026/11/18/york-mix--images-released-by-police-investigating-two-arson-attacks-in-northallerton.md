Police investigating two suspected arson incidents in Northallerton have released CCTV images of a man they would like to speak to.

They say the man pictured may have important information that could assist the investigation.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Although the quality is limited, the images show a man wearing distinctive clothing and carrying what appears to be a large backpack.

“The man is thought to be the same person in all the images, and is believed to have changed his clothing.”

At about 11.44pm on Monday (16 November), firefighters were called to a residential property on the High Street near to All Saints Church following reports of a fire at that address.

The occupant was alerted by the fire alarm and was able to get to safety unharmed.

At 2.44am the next morning, fire crews attended a further fire at the rear of business premises on the High Steet.

The spokesperson added: “Increased police patrols continue, to provide reassurance to residents and the local business community.

“We are also appealing for witnesses and any CCTV, doorbell, and dashcam footage that might capture the incidents or people involved.”

If you recognise the man pictured, or can help in any way, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If you would prefer to remain anonymous, contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or make an online report.

Please quote reference number 12260225561 when providing details.
