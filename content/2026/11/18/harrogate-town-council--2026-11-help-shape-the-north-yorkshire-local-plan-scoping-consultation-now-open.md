Residents of Harrogate are invited to take part in North Yorkshire Council’s scoping consultation on its new Local Plan. It is a chance to comment on what the plan should contain and how the council should involve you as it is developed over the next few years.

The North Yorkshire Local Plan will guide development growth across the county up to 2045, outside of the two national parks. It will set out where new development should take place, which areas should be protected from development, and the planning policies used to decide planning and other development applications.

The plan is at the earliest stage of preparation, so getting involved now means you can help shape it and the future of our local community. This is the first consultation under the new national plan-making system, which came into force in March 2026. Feedback from last year’s ‘First Conversation’ consultation remains important and will feed into the development of the plan.

The scoping consultation launched on 26th October and closes on 7th December. To find out more and take part, visit www.northyorks.gov.uk/localplan.
