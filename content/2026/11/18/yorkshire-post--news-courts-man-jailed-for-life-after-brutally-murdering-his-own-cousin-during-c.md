# Man jailed for life after brutally murdering his own cousin during camping trip

## A Rotherham man who brutally beat his own cousin to death during a camping trip before lying to police about what happened has been jailed for life.

Wayne White, 54, was sentenced at Sheffield Crown Court on Wednesday (Nov 18) after being found guilty of murdering his cousin, Garry Claydon, 54.

He must serve a minimum of 18 years in prison before being eligible for parole.

In the early hours of Sunday (Jun 1), 2025, White subjected Garry to a vicious beating while the pair were camping in Rotherham.

Following the fatal attack, White called the ambulance service and claimed his cousin had been assaulted by an unknown person during the night.

He told emergency services that Garry had said he had been beaten up after leaving their camping spot.

![Wayne White](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/18/14/08/waynewhitebwv.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=448&quality=65)


When police officers arrived at the scene, White repeated the same account, denying any involvement in his cousin's injuries.

White was initially arrested and later released under investigation while detectives carried out extensive enquiries.

A pathological examination subsequently confirmed that Garry's death had been caused by an assault, and White was rearrested on suspicion of murder.

During his seven-day trial at Sheffield Crown Court, jurors heard that extensive CCTV footage covering the area where the pair had been camping showed no evidence of Garry leaving during the night, contradicting White's account.

The court also heard that White had attacked his cousin just two nights earlier, on May 30, fracturing his eye socket.

White was found guilty of murder on Tuesday (Nov 17) and sentenced the following day.

Following the sentencing, Garry's family paid tribute to him and thanked those involved in bringing his killer to justice.

In a statement, they said: “As a family, today marks the end of nearly 18 months since Garry was murdered and we continue to try and come to terms with our loss.

“We are extremely grateful to everyone who has supported us through the investigation and trial. Our thanks go to all those involved in the police investigation and to the prosecution team. We would also like to thank the jury for their careful consideration of this case.

“Garry had a difficult life, but no one deserves to have their life taken away by another person. He has been deprived of his opportunity to turn his life around and that is incredibly sad.”
