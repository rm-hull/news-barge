## 'I struggle with simple tasks' - victim speaks after attacker jailed for nine years

Martin Longstaff, aged 51, of Holywell Lane, Castleford was sentenced yesterday (17 November) at Leeds Crown Court after he was found guilty of five charges. *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

A WOMAN has shared her struggle with simple tasks after the man who assaulted her was jailed for almost a decade.

Martin Longstaff, 51, of Holywell Lane, has been jailed for nine years and placed on the Sex Offender’s Register for life being convicted of sexual assault and non-fatal strangulation, among other offences.

The offences came to light following an assault in Castleford in April that meant the victim required medical attention.

Longstaff was found guilty at Leeds Crown Court on Tuesday (November 17) of sexual assault by digital penetration, non-fatal strangulation, two counts of actual bodily harm, and controlling and coercive behaviour.

He was also given an indefinite restraining order against his victim, who said the abuse continues to have a lasting impact on her.

She said: "Since the attacks, I suffer with bad nightmares which keep me awake and I keep re-living what happened to me.

"I have two broken fingers on both hands, and I struggle with simple tasks like fastening buttons and several broken ribs which are still healing and cause me pain daily."

Detective Constable Adele Bossons, of Wakefield’s Adult Safeguarding Unit, praised the victim’s bravery.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21735873.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Martin's full mug shot *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

She said: "We welcome the sentence given in this case and hope it provides the victim with some reassurance and a degree of peace as she moves forward with her life.

"I want to recognise the courage and bravery the victim has shown, not only in seeking help but also in supporting the investigation.

"Coming forward and speaking about abuse can be incredibly difficult, and her strength throughout this process should be recognised."

"We will always take reports of abuse seriously, thoroughly investigate allegations and do everything within our power to bring offenders to justice."
