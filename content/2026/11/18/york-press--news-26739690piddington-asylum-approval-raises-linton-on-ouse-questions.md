A GOVERNMENT decision to approve asylum accommodation at a former military site near Piddington has raised fresh questions about similar proposals for Linton-on-Ouse.

Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government, Angela Rayner, granted permission to use the former MoD Bicester site in Oxfordshire as a location to house more than 1,200 male asylum seekers.

It was confirmed that it had been approved through a fast-tracking process known as an Urgent Crown Development (UCD) procedure - for projects of national importance on Thursday (November 12).




Sir Alec Shelbrooke the Tory MP for Wetherby and Easingwold said the decision through the UCD procedure highlighted the need for locals in Linton-on-Ouse to have their concerns “fully considered”.

![Sir Alec Shelbrooke MP said the decision through the UCD procedure highlighted the need for locals in Linton-on-Ouse to have their concerns “fully considered”](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21732681.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sir Alec Shelbrooke MP said the decision through the UCD procedure highlighted the need for locals in Linton-on-Ouse to have their concerns “fully considered” *(Image: Supplied)*

He said: “Whilst the circumstances surrounding Piddington may differ, the principle remains the same: local residents must have their concerns properly heard and taken seriously.

"This is particularly important given the proximity of the proposed site at the former RAF Linton-on-Ouse to the village playground, school and nursery."

It follows Sir Alec’s requests for Border Security and Asylum Minister Anna Turley to visit Linton-on-Ouse and meet residents and campaigners.

In a statement, he said: "On Monday, I asked the Minister for Border Security and Asylum to visit Linton-on-Ouse, meet with local residents and the Action Group, and see the site for herself.

"I welcome her agreement to meet with me and look forward to working with Minister Turley to ensure that the views and concerns of the local community are properly heard and fully considered before any decisions are made."

![Minister for Border Security and Asylum, Anna Turley, has been asked to visit Linton-on-Ouse, meet with local residents and the Action Group, and see the site for herself](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21514370.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Minister for Border Security and Asylum, Anna Turley, has been asked to visit Linton-on-Ouse, meet with local residents and the Action Group, and see the site for herself *(Image: Newsquest - Abby Backhouse)*

Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon also called for greater transparency around the government's plans.

He said: "Whether it's Bicester or Linton, communities are asking the same basic questions. Is the site suitable? What will it cost? Who pays? They deserve answers.

"This is a really emotive issue. We need to put aside the political point scoring, support the sensible, cross-party amendments that have been put forward this week and cut through the noise.

"The government can choose to back these amendments, be honest with communities in Linton and Piddington and put transparency into law."

Cllr Malcolm Taylor, who represents Huby and Tollerton at North Yorkshire Council said the authority was monitoring developments at Piddington but stressed that no planning announcement had been made regarding the Linton-on-Ouse proposal.

Cllr Taylor said: "I have received a letter from Anna Turley MP, the immigration and asylum minister, stating that the feasibility study at Linton-on-Ouse is continuing and no decision has been made.

"We continue to work closely with the Linton-on-Ouse Action Group, the Parish Council and the community."
