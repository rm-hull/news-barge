# Gamers react as New Zealand first to see Grand Theft Auto 6

Grand Theft Auto 6 has now officially been released in New Zealand and players have had the chance to take their first look at the game.

POPE NZL and Hayden 'Prawln' Tresch, both New Zealand streamers and among the first to download the newest version in the series, praised its soundtrack and graphics.

It will now launch in successive countries at midnight local time with those who had preinstalled it able from then to download it to their consoles.

Its launch had been delayed twice and comes 13 years after GTA V.
