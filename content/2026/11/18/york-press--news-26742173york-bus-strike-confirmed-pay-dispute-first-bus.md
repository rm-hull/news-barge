YORK bus passengers face disruption later this month after First Bus confirmed plans for industrial action.

Industrial action is set to take place on Saturday (November 28) and Sunday (November 29), following a dispute over pay with First Bus.

The walkout will affect services in York.




First Bus has confirmed that a "limited network" will operate during the strike, including Park and Ride.

Megan Hope-Rushforth, service delivery director at First Bus, said: "We are very disappointed and saddened at the decision to go ahead with strikes.

"Industrial action will be damaging to everyone and cause severe disruption to the daily lives of our customers who rely on the bus to get to work, education and for appointments as well as the thousands of people that want to enjoy the city in the lead up to Christmas."

The strike follows the rejection of two pay offers by Unite members at the York depot.

The first offer proposed a 4.5 per cent increase over 12 months, while the second offered 8.3 per cent over 24 months.

Both offers would have been backdated to April, First Bus said.

Megan said: "We are planning to operate as many services as possible and will give customers as much notice as we can to allow them to plan journeys and limit disruption and inconvenience during the festive period."

She said First Bus had met "repeatedly" with Unite representatives over the past eight months in an effort to reach an agreement.

Megan said: "We fully recognise the strength of feeling of colleagues in York and remain committed to working constructively with Union representatives to reach a fair and sustainable agreement.

"Our door remains open to continue discussions and avoid unnecessary industrial action."

First Bus will provide further information to help customers plan journeys.
