The Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman has issued a report following its investigation of a complaint about City of York Council. The complaint was about Education & Children’s Services. The Ombudsman found fault by the Council which impacted the family and upheld the complaint.

City of York Council has agreed to take action which the Ombudsman regards as providing a satisfactory remedy for the complaint.

The Council must now consider the report and tell the Ombudsman within three months (or such longer period as the Ombudsman may agree) what it proposes to do.

Copies of the report will be available for public inspection during normal office hours at West Offices, Station Rise, York, YO1 6GA for three weeks starting on **Thursday 19 November.**
