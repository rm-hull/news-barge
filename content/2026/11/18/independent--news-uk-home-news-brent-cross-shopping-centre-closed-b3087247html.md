A shopping centre is currently closed after a man was rushed to hospital following a ‘health and safety incident’.

Emergency services responded to reports of an incident at Brent Cross Shopping Centre, Hendon at around 5.09am on Wednesday morning.

A paramedic treated a man at the scene as more paramedics and ambulance crew provided further medical assistance.

The man was taken to hospital for further treatment. Further details around the circumstances of the incident are not yet clear.

Councillor Alex Prager said Brent Cross Shopping Centre is closed due to a “health and safety incident involving an electrical item”. He added: “The incident is not security-related, and there is no wider threat to public safety”.

A London Ambulance Service spokesperson said: “We were called at 5:09am today (18 November) to reports of an incident at Brent Cross Shopping Centre, Hendon, NW4.

“We sent an ambulance crew, paramedics in fast response cars, an incident response officer and an advanced paramedic.

“The first paramedic arrived in less than seven minutes.

“We treated a patient at the scene and took him to a major trauma centre as a priority.”

Brent Cross Shopping Centre has said it will remain temporarily closed until further notice due to “unforeseen circumstances”.

A spokesperson shared on social media: “Important update: Brent Cross is temporarily closed until further notice.

“We apologise for any inconvenience and will share further information as soon as possible. Thank you for your understanding.”
