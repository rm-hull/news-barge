## Why get this deal?

Until the Garmin Fenix 9 Pro came along, we regarded the Garmin Fenix 8 (and its Pro version) as the ultimate in adventure watches: a rugged, do-anything trail companion. We tested its heart rate reading accuracy, GPS accuracy, battery life and myriad other features to ensure it lived up to expectations.

I particularly like the LED torch — now standard on most premium Garmin devices — and training tools such as Hill and Endurance scores. As someone who lives in a very hilly area, I have enjoyed seeing my hill score increase (and occasionally decrease) during my regular road-running training.

However, its GPS capabilities and advanced navigation tools are where the Fenix 8 really shines, helping hikers, mountain bikers, trail runners and orienteers navigate the great outdoors, contact emergency services, monitor their health, and lots more.
