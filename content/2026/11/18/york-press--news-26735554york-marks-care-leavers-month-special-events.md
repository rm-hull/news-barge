York is celebrating the achievements and potential of its care leavers this month.

City of York Council is marking Care Leavers’ Month throughout November with a programme of events organised by its Pathway and Speak Up Teams.

The theme this year is ‘Leading the Way: From Experience to Empowerment.’

All activities are designed to bring people together, raise awareness and celebrate the progress and contributions of care-experienced young people.

Councillor Bob Webb, executive member for children, young people and education, said: "Young care leavers stay within our wider York family and Care Leavers’ Month gives us a wonderful opportunity to celebrate them, their talents and potential, as well as the positive contribution they make to our local communities."

The events recognise care leavers aged 16 to 25 who have spent time in the care of the local authority.

A special information session will be held to help others learn more about the team’s work and ways to support care leavers.

As part of the celebration, York’s Bar Walls will be lit up in blue from November 16 to 23.

This forms part of the National Light Up for care leavers campaign.
