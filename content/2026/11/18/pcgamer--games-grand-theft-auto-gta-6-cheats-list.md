GTA 6 cheats are expected to be a huge part of the game. Saying you're going to use cheats in Grand Theft Auto is like saying water is wet—supplementing your cash, arsenal of weapons, or doing other fun stuff like turning off gravity is a core part of these games.

Unfortunately, this time **you need to complete the main story**—all 91 GTA 6 story missions —before you can access cheats and view their effects. After that, you'll have to complete the listed challenge to unlock each cheat command, though this does take into account what you've already done.

We loaded a save to test if cheat commands work earlier in the game and they don't. Our list also doesn't include some challenges, since we completed these while playing the game and so never got to see them.

## GTA 6 cheats list

First thing to note: **if you use a cheat, you will not be able to save your game** from that point on. That means you can't use cheats to progress through side missions or challenges, because with cheats on you'll have to quit your game without saving it.

Once unlocked, you can enter a cheat in GTA 6 if you:

* Press ↑ on the D-pad to open your phone
* Hold L1 and press R1
* Input the listed command
