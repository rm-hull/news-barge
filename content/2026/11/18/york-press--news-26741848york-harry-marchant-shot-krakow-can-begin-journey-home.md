A York man shot in the Krakow terror attack has received the “greatest news” for his 20th birthday after being told he can finally leave hospital and begin his journey home.

Harry Marchant, from Haxby, has been receiving treatment in hospital in the Polish city after being wounded during the gunman's rampage in the city’s Main Square on Saturday, November 7.

The York City supporter, who turns 20 today, Wednesday, November 18, was one of 15 people injured in the attack, in which six people were killed, including a British man.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21734973.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Harry Marchant pictured with York City striker Josh Stones

His parents flew to Poland to be at his bedside and later launched a GoFundMe appeal to help meet the significant cost of bringing him home safely via land, as flying was thought to be unfeasible due to his injuries.

At the time, Harry's mum Fran Talbot said they thought trains would be the best and most comfortable option available to the them, but it was a huge logistical - and costly - challenge on top of the ordeal Harry had gone through.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21734974.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Flowers and messages laid out on the main square two days after a shooting in Krakow, Poland, Monday, Nov. 9, 2026. (AP Photo/Beata Zawrzel) Image: Beata Zawrzel

"He was shot and has been extremely poorly. For me, it is about getting him home," she told The Press, paying tribute to the medical team that had been caring for him, from the first responder at the scene.

In an update, Fran said: “Thank you to everyone who has donated to Harry’s fund. We are so grateful for all your help.

“As most of you know it’s Harry’s 20th birthday on November 18.

“He has been told the greatest news he can be discharged from hospital.

“We will look to start our journey to get him back to the UK via ambulance and we have only been able to do this by the kind donations. So thank you everyone.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21702030.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Harry Marchant has been told he can leave hospital in Poland where he has been treated since being shot in the Krakow terror attack on November 7.

The appeal quickly attracted support from friends, relatives, fellow York City fans as well as some of the players, along with complete strangers, including numerous Polish nationals.

Among the contributions was £1,500 from the York City Vice Presidents Association, which was formed in 1980 to support the club, which plays Walsall this Saturday, with other donations going towards training ground improvements, among other projects.

Fran previously said they were overwhelmed by the support which had been "absolutely amazing and taken so much pressure off.”

The Foreign Secretary formally designated the November 7 attack as an act of terrorism, with the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office providing consular support to the family.

The 24-year-old gunman was shot by police and later died in hospital. He was also suspected of killing three members of his own family before the Krakow attack.
