Jenny has served as a councillor since the formation of Harrogate Town Council and was one of its inaugural members. She also served as Deputy Mayor during the 2025/26 civic year.

The Council would like to thank Jenny for her service and contribution during the important early years of the new Town Council and wishes her and her family well for the future.

The resulting vacancy is expected to be filled through the ordinary Town Council elections scheduled to take place in May 2027.
