New footage shared by a Ukrainian Telegram channel on Wednesday appears to show extensive damage to the Kerch Bridge, a strategic crossing linking Russia to the occupied Crimean Peninsula.

Ukrainian drones and missiles struck the bridge on Tuesday.

The Russian-installed governor of Crimea, Sergei Aksyonov, said road traffic across the bridge had been halted, while rail services resumed by Tuesday afternoon.

The Kerch Bridge is a key route for military and civilian supplies to Crimea and carries major strategic and symbolic importance for Moscow, which annexed the peninsula in 2014.
