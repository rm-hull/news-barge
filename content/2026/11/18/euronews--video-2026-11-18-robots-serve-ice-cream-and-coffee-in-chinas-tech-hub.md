Humanoid robots are serving ice cream and coffee in Shenzhen, China, as developers work to introduce artificial intelligence into everyday tasks.

At a park refreshment stand, one robot prepares ice cream while another makes coffee, attracting curious visitors who film them in action. Both are powered by AI, although staff still need to refill supplies and empty coffee grounds.

Chinese companies are also using data collected from workers wearing cameras and sensor gloves to train robots. These recordings help the machines learn tasks such as handling parcels and stocking shelves, as well as adapting to different environments.

Although they still struggle with complex household chores, robots are increasingly taking on repetitive work. Shenzhen's manufacturing network allows developers to test and refine their technology quickly.
