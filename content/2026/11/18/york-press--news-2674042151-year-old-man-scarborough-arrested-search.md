**A WANTED man has been arrested after a search by the county's police.**

A 51-year-old man who was wanted after failing to appeal in court in Scarborough has now been arrested after an appeal by North Yorkshire Police published on Monday, November 9.

As reported by The Press, the man is described as a white man with dark hair.

His name was also reported at the time, but has since been removed by North Yorkshire Police.

A police spokesperson said: “He has now been arrested and brought into custody. Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal.”
