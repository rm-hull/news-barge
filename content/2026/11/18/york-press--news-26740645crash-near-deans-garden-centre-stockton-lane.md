## Fire crews assisted at the scene

Crews from York and Acomb crew say they responded to a three vehicle road traffic collision with reports of persons trapped on Stockton Lane at 10.04am today.

A spokesperson said: "On arrival, no persons were trapped and casualties were out of their vehicles. Crews assisted with scene safety and isolating the batteries on the vehicles prior to recovery. Incident left with Police and Ambulance."

Thank you to our commenters for confirming that the accident happened further down the road, not outside Deans Garden Centre. We have received various different pieces of information on this and the story has now been updated accordingly.

## Traffic unaffected

The crash took place on the road between Stockton on the Forest and York, outside Deans Garden Centre. Traffic on the A64 and other nearby roads has not been affected by the closure.

## Buses diverted

Coastliner have said that their buses - the 840 and 843 to/from Leeds, Scarborough, Whitby, and Malton - are being diverted in both directions until the crash clears.

They will be missing the stops for Deans Garden Centre, Kings Moor Road, Stockton Hall, Stockton Primary School, Stockton Village Hall, Barr Lane, Sandy Lane, Station House, and Hazlebush Farm.

![The Coastliner services are between Scarborough and Leeds](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/11162132.jpg?type=mds-article-620) The Coastliner services are between Scarborough and Leeds

## What do we know so far?

As reported by The Press already, Stockton Lane is currently closed after an accident that took place outside Deans Garden Centre, at the junction of the Holtby Lane on Wednesday morning (November 18).

It was first reported at around 10.30am today, with buses in the area diverting around the closure.

Outside of this, traffic is flowing well in the area.

An eyewitness stated that the crash seemed to involve multiple vehicles.
