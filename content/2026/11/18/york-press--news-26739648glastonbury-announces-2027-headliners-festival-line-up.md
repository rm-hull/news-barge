Glastonbury Festival has announced its three headliners for the 2027 event.

Sam Fender, Harry Styles and Billie Eilish will all take to the famous Pyramid Stage during the weekend in June.

It comes as Cher was confirmed to be taking the coveted legends slot that Shania Twain and the late Dolly Parton have previously graced.

After a break in 2026, the event will return next year, June 23-27, at Worthy Farm in Pilton, Somerset.

## Glastonbury confirms headliners and lineup for 2027

Styles, Fender and Eilish will be joined by acts Sombr, Sienna Spiro, Lily Allen, The Streets, Chase & Status, Kacey Musgraves, Primal Scream and Adéla.

Fender will headline the first night, marking his second time at Glastonbury after last playing in 2022.

'As It Was' singer Styles will perform on Saturday before Eilish closes Glastonbury on the Sunday night.

Discussing the 2027 festival, organiser Emily Eavis said: "We are so happy to be coming back after our fallow year and, as we head into winter after that peaceful period, we wanted to give you an early musical taster of what's to come at Glastonbury 2027 with this spread of incredible artists.

"We can't wait to see you down here on Worthy Farm in June for some much-needed good times!"

Glastonbury will kick off on Wednesday 23 June, with music starting on Friday 25 June.
