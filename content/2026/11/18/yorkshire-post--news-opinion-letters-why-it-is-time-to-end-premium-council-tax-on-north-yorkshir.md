# Why it is time to end premium council tax on North Yorkshire holiday homes in places like Whitby: Yorkshire Post Letters

## Paul Growdon, Rotherham.

There is and quite rightly so, unrest regarding the Premium (double) Council Tax imposed by North Yorkshire Council on holiday homes regardless of their suitability as much-needed housing for families wanting to stay local to their roots. The expectation was that the owners of holiday homes would sell up thus resolving the housing problem.

We have a flat in the Whitehall Landing development in Whitby, one of 154. Several, including ours, are up for sale and in up to two years we have not had so much as a cheeky offer despite the “affordable” price.

This highlights the fact that these flats are unsuitable for any other occupation than short term stays (3-5 days).

![A seagull soars across Whitby seafront. Photo: Marisa Cashill](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjA3NDIxZDA1LTQ1ODYtNDg0OC05OGM2LTc5MmIyMDA3YjkzNzo2MGU0YTUyNC1kMWU3LTRhNjktYTQ5Ni0yMWYzZGY5ZDY5MmU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


They are not “chocolate box” cottages in picturesque villages to which a Range Rover shows up twice a year. They are the holiday homes of ordinary people who have invested and continue to regularly invest in Whitby.

So come on NYC, do the right thing and remove the punishing and unfair Premium Council Tax on these and similar flats.
