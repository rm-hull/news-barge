A FORMER student at has made a York university’s history by becoming the institution’s oldest-ever graduate today.

Robin Gallagher, 85, received his Doctor of Philosophy degree at a graduation ceremony held in York Minster on Wednesday (November 18).

Graduating as part of the university’s largest-ever graduation cohort, Robin said it was “never too late” to take on a new challenge.




His journey at York St John began with a master’s degree in 2017.

After its completion, a 79-year-old Robin started a part-time PhD, which explored how the inheritance of family wealth and property can shape family relationships.

He said: “My journey has shown me that it is never too late to pursue an ambition or take on a new challenge.

"I would encourage anyone considering further study to have confidence in themselves, make connections with other students and make the most of the support and guidance available.

“Above all, embrace lifelong learning and never stop believing in what you can achieve."

Robin left school at 16 to train as a chartered accountant.

A successful career in finance followed – but as he entered his retirement years, Robin didn’t wind down, instead enrolling on a Creative Writing course at Kirklees College aged 73.

Fast-forward 12 years, Robin’s work has inspired a Gothic family drama set in the Conwy Valley – and his doctoral research, which drew on the 85-year-old’s experience and treasured childhood memories in North Wales.

His achievement comes as more than 3,200 students graduate over five days of ceremonies, representing the largest graduating cohort in York St John University's history.

Professor Karen Bryan OBE, Vice Chancellor and Chief Executive of York St John University, said: “Graduation is a wonderful opportunity to celebrate our students’ achievements, recognise their hard work and reflect on the friendships and experiences that have shaped their time at York St John University.

“As they embark on their next chapter, I hope they take with them the knowledge, skills and confidence to embrace the exciting opportunities ahead, knowing they will always be valued members of our university community."
