Harrogate Theatre has revealed that Cinderella will be its pantomime for Christmas 2027.

The show will run from Wednesday 24th November 2027 to Sunday 16th January 2028, following this year’s production of Sleeping Beauty.

The story follows Cinderella, whose wicked stepmother and mischievous sisters are determined to keep her at home when an invitation to the palace ball arrives.

With help from her Fairy Godmother and a pair of glass slippers, she makes it to the ball, but she must be home before the clock strikes midnight.

A spokesperson for Harrogate Theatre said:

> "Packed with sparkle, laughter and festive fun, Harrogate Theatre’s magical family pantomime is back and we can’t wait to welcome you to the ball!"

*Priority booking for Harrogate Theatre members opens today (Wednesday 18th November), with tickets going on general sale on Wednesday 25th November.*
