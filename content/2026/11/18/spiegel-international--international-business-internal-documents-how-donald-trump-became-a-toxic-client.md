Donald Trump's request arrives in early December 2016. It’s a few weeks after his election victory, and he needs cash. A lot of it. And some of the money is to come from Frankfurt in Germany – from Deutsche Bank. From the same institution that has repeatedly helped him out in the preceding years.

Trump wants to increase an existing loan by $35 million. He wants access to a reserve of $50 million set aside for a luxury hotel in the heart of Washington. The US president-elect also asks for the terms on three other loans to be extended.
