A criminal investigation has been opened into Julio Iglesias by a Spanish High Court judge, court records have revealed.

The 83-year-old singer-songwriter has been accused of sexual offences, assault and degrading treatment towards two former employees. He has denied the allegations.

International human rights organisation Women's Link Worldwide filed the complaint on behalf of two women who worked in Mr Igelsias’ Caribbean residences for 10 months back in 2021 following an investigation by US broadcaster *Univision*and Spanish outlet* elDiario.es.*

![Mr Iglesias previously said the allegations were ‘absolutely false’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/14/2026-11-18T133509Z_1989449661_RC2DQOAG2ILH_RTRMADP_3_PEOPLE-JULIO-IGLESIAS.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

On 16 January, Mr Iglesias, who was born in Madrid, described the accusations in an Instagram post as “absolutely false” that have caused him “great sadness”.

“I deny having abused, coerced or disrespected any woman,” he wrote at the time.

Investigating judge Antonio Piña ‌said the complaint contained sufficient preliminary ‌evidence to justify opening criminal proceedings.

The judge added that the investigation would cover parts of the complaint relating to potential crimes against sexual freedom, as well as assault and degrading treatment, for conduct alleged to have taken place after October 5, 2021.

Piña rejected portions of the complaint relating to human trafficking and labour-rights crimes.

![The two complainants will testify next year](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/14/1230928374..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Prosecutors earlier this year argued that ​Spanish courts lacked jurisdiction because the alleged offences ‌occurred abroad and the complainants ⁠did not reside in Spain.

The women subsequently filed a direct complaint with the High Court, which has now agreed to investigate the allegations. Piña ordered the two ‌complainants to testify in ​person on 3 February 2027.

Mr Iglesias has sold more than 300 million records during his career and is known for songs including ‘Soy un Truhán, Soy un Señor’, ‘Begin the Beguine’, ‘De Niña a Mujer’ and ‘Me Va, Me Va’.

He is also the father of pop star Enrique Iglesias.
