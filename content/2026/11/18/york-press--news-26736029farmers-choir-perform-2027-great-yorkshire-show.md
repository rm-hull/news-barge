## Great Yorkshire Show reveals headline Main Ring act for 2027

The Hawkstone Farmers Choir will be the Main Ring act at the 2027 Great Yorkshire Show *(Image: Britain's Got Talent)*

A choir of farmers made famous through TV show Britain's Got Talent is set to make history at next year’s Great Yorkshire Show.

The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir will become the first to perform in the Main Ring in the show’s 189-year history.

Performances will take place twice daily from Tuesday, July 13 to Friday, July 16.

Show director Rachel Coates said: "We’re always looking for ways to make a visit to the Great Yorkshire Show even better, and so we have made a few forward-thinking changes this year, which we are excited to be announcing as we reveal our Main Ring attraction for 2027.

"The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir is doing a fantastic job of reaching the public about farming, and so it’s a perfect fit for the Show, which celebrates and shares the important story of agriculture to thousands of people every year."

The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir, made up of farmers and agricultural workers from across the UK, are the first choir to win Britain’s Got Talent.

They are set to release their debut album, This Is Home, on Friday, November 20.

Tickets for the show went on sale today through a new digital ordering system aimed at improving the customer experience.

Tickets must be purchased in advance, with visitor numbers capped at 150,000 over the four days.

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Agricultural Society said the new online system is more sustainable, allowing tickets and membership cards to be stored on Apple and Google wallets.

A new Family of Four ticket option has also been introduced.

Earlier entry is another change for 2027.

The showground will open at 7.45am each day, giving visitors more time to enjoy show ring competitions, many of which start at 8am.

Planning for next year’s event is already underway, with organisers promising a showcase of the country’s best livestock, the latest agricultural machinery, entertainment, and food.

The show has sold out a week in advance every year since moving to advance-only ticket sales in 2021.

Tickets for 2027 are priced at £38 for adults, £13.50 for children, and £93 for a family of five.

The new Family of Four ticket (two adults and two children) costs £91.28.

Membership of the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, which includes entry to all four days, costs £95 for a single pass and £155 for joint membership.

Entry remains free for under-fives, though a ticket must still be booked online in advance.

Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

The age ranges for society membership have also been updated, with Junior Membership now covering ages five to 16 and Intermediate Membership for those aged 17 to 25.

Tickets are available to purchase via greatyorkshireshow.co.uk/ticket-information.
