A York pub has won an award following a makeover costing six figures.

The Roman Bath on St Sampson’s Square in York took the honour to recognise its new-look unveiled last year.

The pub was named Best Investment at the 2026 Craft Union Operator Awards.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/roman-bath-york-exterior.webp)

The annual awards celebrate the best pub operators across Craft Union Pub Company’s estate of more than 600 pubs. The Best Investment award goes to operators who have delivered and embraced a newly refurbished pub.

When plans were unveiled to update the Roman Bath, it caused controversy. Long-running landlord Liam Fagan’s lease wasn’t renewed and staff and customers feared it was being turned into a sports bar.

Hundreds of people signed a petition asking to keep the pub as it was.

The investment went ahead and the Roman Bath reopened in February 2025. The award rewards the team for “embracing the changes and turning the pub into a thriving all-day destination”.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/roman-bath-york-team.webp)

The pub is operated by Tom Lakin and sits above the remains of a Roman bathhouse dating back nearly 2,000 years.

While the venue was trading as the Mail Coach in the early 20th century, builders uncovered the bathhouse during redevelopment works.

Rather than removing it, the find was preserved, and the venue was renamed The Roman Bath in 1930.

Visitors can still explore the ancient bathhouse, including its hot, warm and cold bathing rooms, as well as Roman artefacts linked to the city’s military past.

## Live music

The refurbishment, carried out in early 2025, included a redesigned layout, upgraded sports viewing facilities and enhancements that have helped the pub continue its weekly live music programme.

Craft Union has also refurbished and reopened the venue’s five hotel rooms, which welcomed guests again from February 2026.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/roman-bath-york-reopens-after-makeover-4.webp)

Judges praised Tom for his his leadership, organisation and entrepreneurial approach.

Tom said: “The team and I are absolutely delighted to receive this award.

“The investment gave us a fantastic opportunity to enhance everything people already loved about The Roman Bath while making sure we could make the most of what is such a unique venue.”

Frazer Grimbleby, managing director of Craft Union Pub Company, said: “The Roman Bath is a fantastic example of how investment can help a great pub reach its full potential.”

Seven Craft Union pubs from across Yorkshire were recognised at this year’s awards, accounting for more than half of the 12 awards presented.

The Craft Union Operator Awards are held annually to celebrate pub operators who go above and beyond for their customers, teams and communities across the UK.
