## 'We are completely devastated' - family's heartbreaking tribute to Jade Langton

Emma Langton with her much-loved daughter Jade who was killed in a crash in Gelderd Road in Leeds. <i>(Image: Emma Langton)

Jade Langton's family has spoken of their “unimaginable loss” and the huge hole her death has left in their lives following a fatal crash.

Jade, aged 23, and Cameron Bell, 22, who were both from York, were killed in the early hours of Sunday, November 15, after a BMW 123D hit the white Volkswagen Scirocco they were travelling in Gelderd Road in Leeds.

Mr Bell was driving the Volkswagen, and died at the scene while his passenger Ms Langton died from her injuries in hospital shortly afterwards.

News of their deaths has led to an outpouring of grief from loved ones.

Jade's mum Emma Langton shared this family statement with The Press today, Wednesday: "We are completely devastated by the loss of our beautiful Jade.

"We are struggling to come to terms with what has happened and the reality that she has been taken from us far too soon."

Tributes have also been paid in a special Book of Condolences created by The Press, as well as on social media.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21730121.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A photo of Jade Langton, whose family has spoken of their 'unimaginable loss' following her death in the early hours of Sunday, November 15. *(Image: Family/ Facebook)*

Mrs Langton added: "Jade had a big personality and loved being with people, chatting, giggling, going to live music, traveling and other adventures. She left an impact wherever she was.

"Whilst she always loved a bit of drama, Jade did not deserve to have her life cut short in this way.

"There is a big hole in our family and she is already hugely missed.

"Thank you to the emergency services and everyone who did everything they could to help.

"The kindness, support and condolences we have received from family, friends and the wider community is appreciated.

"We share our deepest condolences to Cam’s family and friends too.

"As we try to navigate this unimaginable loss, we respectfully ask people to be mindful about what they share and comment on social media.

"We kindly ask that our family's privacy is respected while we grieve together and try to come to terms with losing our Jade.

"If anyone had anything negative to say, please - just scroll by.

"Love you more Jade xx"

West Yorkshire Police would like to hear from anyone who witnessed or has footage of the incident via 101 or the Live Chat facility on our website quoting reference 13260649342.

The Press Book of Condolences for Jade and Cameron is inviting friends, family and well-wishers to post their tributes.  
  
Jade's sister Keelie Langton was the first to share her own heartfelt tribute, writing: "Jade was always such a funny girl, always tuned a bad into a good with her presence. Her laughter was contagious - I could never have asked for a better sister in my life.

"I will miss you for the rest of my life. Fly high my beautiful angel."

She described Cameron as "an amazing kind caring boy".

"They so perfect for each other. I will miss our drives out and tea at mine with us all. "

A friend, Fallon Wardell said Jade was such a big part of her childhood.

"We did everything together growing up. We were inseparable, fought like siblings and laughed like idiots," she wrote.

"You were such a big part of my childhood, and I’ll always treasure the memories we made. Although life took us in different directions as we got older, you’ll always hold a special place in my heart. I wish we’d had more time, but I’ll forever be grateful for the childhood memories we shared. You’ll always be part of my story. Rest peacefully."

Richard Watson said Jade would never be forgotten: "Jade, I am proud to say I was your friend, but for too short a time. I will always remember your laughter and your cheeky face. RIP."

Karl Watson wrote: "I was glad to be a part of your life and it was an honour to have had you as part of mine. We had many good memories and times together. You will be never forgotten."

Another wrote: "You were an amazing person and a good friend... You made me laugh when I cried and smile when down. You’ve been taken far too soon. Keep smiling and laughing like you made me. Thank you for everything."

Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place in Harrogate, was charged in connection with the collision and appeared before Leeds Magistrates' Court on Monday, November 16.

He is charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while uninsured, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle whilst disqualified and two counts of causing death having taken a vehicle without consent.

Ross is alleged to have caused the deaths of Mr Bell and Ms Langton while fleeing from police in a blue BMW 123D, reaching speeds of 80mph on two roads and doing a U-turn, before crashing into another vehicle, the court heard.
