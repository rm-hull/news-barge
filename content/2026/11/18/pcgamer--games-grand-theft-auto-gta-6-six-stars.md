Achieving six stars in GTA 6's new wanted system is the most notoriety you can get in the game, and killing enough cops and soldiers to get there will likely have a big impact on your criminal profile—which is effectively this game's version of Red Dead Redemption 2's karma system.

As with the previous games, the highest star levels are reserved for trespassing in an airport and stealing a passenger jet, or heading to a military base and hijacking an APC. And, as always, five stars will see a tank dispatched.

But you can only get to six stars if you cause mass destruction and loss of life. The only way we were able to get a six star wanted level in GTA 6 was by stealing a tank from a military base and blowing up other tanks and vehicles.

Just keep this in mind:

* At six stars, you'll no longer be able to lose heat or wanted level.

You can run but you can't hide because once you're at six stars, the law will never stop hunting you. This means your only way out is to die, essentially. It's also worth considering that killing all of those cops could impact your criminal profile quite severely.
