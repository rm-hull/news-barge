Seven Peruvian pelicans were released back into the wild near Lima, Peru, on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, after around a month of rehabilitation.

The birds returned to their natural habitat on a beach near Los Pantanos de Villa Wildlife Refuge, marking the opening of the country's first state-run rehabilitation centre for marine wildlife affected by El Niño.

Established by Peru's wildlife service SERFOR and Universidad Científica del Sur, the centre can care for more than 100 marine and coastal animals at a time. Staff provide veterinary treatment and supervised feeding before the animals are ready for release. Two Humboldt penguins remain at the facility.

The centre opens as warmer ocean waters linked to El Niño disrupt food supplies, leaving seabirds weakened along the Peruvian coast.
