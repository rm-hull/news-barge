Harrogate Town are delighted to announce the permanent signing of Luke Hutchinson on an undisclosed fee from Bolton Wanderers.

The 24-year-old has made 14 appearances so far this season and has signed a long-term deal with the club after an impressive start.

said Town Head of Recruitment Lloyd Kerry said:

> "We’re really pleased to get Luke signed on a permanent deal,
>
> “Luke has put in some impressive performances since joining us, and he has fit in really well with the group.
>
> "He plays with confidence, has a really good understanding of how we want to play as a team and has plenty of potential to grow with us.”

Luke was also quick to express his delight at making his initial loan move to the Marcy Fitness Stadium permanent.

He said:

> “I’m absolutely delighted to extend my stay here.
>
> "Since coming here on loan, it felt like a perfect place to play and enjoy my football, so I’m glad that I’m here long term.
>
> “All of my teammates have been brilliant with me since I came in, Phil [Priestley, Town's goalkeeping coach] has also been really helpful too, so it’s made it really easy to settle into the group.”

Hutchinson joined Bolton at the age of eight and went on to sign his first pro deal in 2021. Luke would see his stay at the Wanderers extended in the summer of 2025, after impressing on loan at a couple of different clubs, including Crawley Town, who were in League One at the time.

He would then spend the 2025/26 season out on loan at Altrincham, where he made 44 appearances for the side and would go onto to win the club’s players' player and young player of the year awards.
