Bob O’Neill is retiring after 11 years as the lollipop man at Richard Taylor CE Primary School near Harrogate.

The 80-year-old is hanging up his stop sign after today (Friday) when he helps pupils cross the road for the final time.

Many pupils have known Bob throughout their time at the school.

His daily greetings and chats with families have made him a familiar face at the gates each morning and afternoon.

A spokesperson for Richard Taylor CE Primary School said:

> “Bob has been a constant and reassuring presence for our children and families for more than a decade.
>
> "His commitment to keeping our pupils safe, whatever the weather, has been truly remarkable."

They added:

> "He is hugely respected and appreciated by staff, parents and pupils alike and will be greatly missed.
>
> "We would like to thank him for his years of dedicated service and wish him a long, happy and well-earned retirement.”
