A Democrat has won the Alaska Senate race, completing her party’s sweeping victory in the midterm elections.

Mary Peltola defeated not one, but two Dan Sullivans in the closely watched contest, the ninth Senate seat the Democrats flipped.

​The Republican nominee and incumbent Senator Dan S. Sullivan was battling both Peltola and two other Republicans, including one named Dan J. Sullivan who later appeared on the ballot without a party affiliation.

​During the two week vote count, Peltola maintained a steady lead over now-outgoing Senator Sullivan.

![Mary Peltola has officially won the US Senate election in Alaska](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/08/2303441769..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​In a statement released on November 17 announcing her victory, Peltola spoke about a theme that had formed the bedrock of her campaign: the cost of living.

​“Over the course of this campaign I’ve spoken with elders in tears about empty fish racks; families struggling to find housing they can afford; and parents in the Bush faced with the impossible choice of heating their homes or putting food on the table,” she said.

“Today we take a monumental step forward in addressing those issues and putting Alaska first.”

Alaska’s ranked-choice voting system allows voters to name candidates in the order of their preference. Secondary choices are used to determine a winner if no candidate crosses the 50 percent threshold needed to win the election.

![Peltola defeated the incumbent US Senator Dan S. Sullivan (pictured)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/04/18/2303390870..?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=97%2C0%2C97%2C0&auto=webp)

Voting data analysed by the BBC showed that over a third of Dan J. Sullivan’s voters left their second choice blank, with Peltola being their second most popular choice.

Peltola received almost double the second-choice votes as the now-outgoing Senator Sullivan.

However, Republicans, including President Trump, have long claimed that having two Dan Sullivans on the ballot could confuse voters.

“You need about a 190 IQ to figure out the ballot,” Trump previously told Kansas City host Pete Mundo on KCMO 95.7 FM. “I mean, you talk about voter fraud.

“Nobody’s ever seen voter fraud like this.”

![Some Republicans said that having two Dan Sullivans (Dan J Sullivan pictured here) could confuse voters](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/05/31/17/35/DanSullivan-copy.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The Trump administration filed a lawsuit seeking to block Alaska from declaring a winner in the election if now-outgoing Senator Sullivan did not win and votes for Dan J. Sullivan could have changed the outcome. The lawsuit was dismissed by U.S. District Court Judge Sharon Gleason in a 20-page order.

The lawsuit came months after a separate dispute connected to Dan J. Sullivan, which ended in June 2026 when the Alaska Supreme Court put the candidate back on the ballot after he was briefly removed by the Alaska Division of Elections.

Sources claimed to *The New York Times* that Peltola’s campaign had sought out men named Dan Sullivan and hoped to convince them to run in the election, in an effort to dilute the Republican incumbent’s vote share.
