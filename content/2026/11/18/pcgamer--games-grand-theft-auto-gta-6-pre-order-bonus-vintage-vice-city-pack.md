The Vintage Vice City Pack is a pre-order bonus for everyone who purchased GTA 6 early, even if you didn't opt for the more expensive Ultimate Edition. It includes the '55 Vapid Stanier car, some 80s outfits and hair styles, plus a tropical palm tree weapon pattern similar to Tommy Verceti's iconic shirt.

You can view the pre-order bonus in the main menu store that unlocks after the "Coming Home" mission (number 11 in Chapter 1). You'll need to:

* Open the main menu.
* Select the store option.
* Cycle down past GTA+ to see the Vintage Vice City Pack.

As it notes in the entries, **you can't claim these until Chapter 3** in the story, though it does give you details for how to grab each.

When you do reach that point:

* **55' Vapid Stanier**: You'll notice a new task in the Exploration section of the map index, directing you to head to Ocean View Hotel to collect the '55 Vapid Stanier & Garage, where it's located.
* **Outfits and hairstyles**: After you collect the car, you can find the outfits in your wardrobe, and the hairstyles in the Special menu section of any salon.
* **Weapon pattern**: Also once you've collected the car, you can apply this pattern in the Cosmetics/Wraps section of the modify menu at any Ammu-Nation store.

With that, you'll have collected all of the pre-order bonuses.
