# Police investigate suspected targeted arson and gunfire attacks at Sheffield home

## Police have launched an investigation after a house in Sheffield was targeted in two separate incidents involving a suspected firearms discharge and an arson attack.

At around 8.52pm on Tuesday (Nov 17), officers were alerted by South Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service to a reported arson at a property on Westfield Avenue in Hackenthorpe.

Emergency services attended and extinguished the blaze, which caused significant damage to the property.

No injuries were reported.

While carrying out house-to-house enquiries in the surrounding area, officers were made aware of a separate reported firearms discharge at the same address.

Following further investigations, police believe the firearms incident took place on Thursday (Nov 5).

No injuries were reported in connection with the suspected shooting.

![Police at the scene following the arson attack at Westfield Avenue, Hackenthorpe, Sheffield. Photo: Iconic Media](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/18/15/52/WhatsApp-Image-2026-11-18-at-14-54-20.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Detectives believe both incidents were targeted attacks and are appealing for information from anyone who may have witnessed suspicious activity in the area.

A police cordon remains in place on Westfield Avenue while enquiries continue, with an increased police presence expected in the surrounding streets.

Sheffield Superintendent Sarah Gilmour said: “We understand that news of these incidents will cause concern amongst the local community, particularly residents of Westfield Avenue and the surrounding streets.

“Two very serious incidents occurred at the same property, and following our enquiries we believe both incidents were targeted attacks.

“This could have been much more severe and as our enquiries continue, we have a team of detectives working at pace to understand the circumstances that led to both incidents, but we also need information and help from our communities.

“We'd like to hear from anyone in the local area who may have seen anything or have any information relating to the reported firearms discharge, which we believe took place on 5 November, or from the arson incident yesterday evening (17 November).

“If you know anything at all about either of these incidents, I urge you to come forward and report it to us.”
