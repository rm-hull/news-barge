Harrogate events company Impulse Decisions has raised £5,241 for Saint Michael’s Hospice at its ninth Harrogate Business Golf Day.

The event brought 92 people from the local business community to Pannal Golf Club, where 23 teams competed for the trophy and a £1,000 prize to donate to a charity of their choice.

Moore Quantity Surveying won the trophy and chose to give the £1,000 to Sue Ryder Manorlands Hospice in Keighley.

Sam Moore, from Moore Quantity Surveying, said:

> “We had a brilliant day at the Harrogate Business Golf Day.
>
> "It was a great opportunity to enjoy some golf, meet plenty of people and have a bit of healthy competition along the way.
>
> "Impulse Decisions put on a fantastic event, and coming away with the trophy was the perfect way to finish the day.
>
> "We’ll definitely be back next year to defend it!”

Players also took on Liv Jackson in a ‘beat the pro’ challenge before joining host Pete Emmett for dinner.

Guests bid for prizes in silent and live auctions to raise money for Saint Michael’s.

Tom Hesketh, Director of Hospitality Sales at Impulse Decisions, said:

> “A huge thank you to everyone who joined us - it was fantastic to see so many local businesses come together for a day of golf, networking, hospitality, and ultimately giving back.”

He added:

> “We love running this annual event and next year will be our tenth year so we’ve exciting plans!”

**Impulse Decisions** has helped charities around the world raise more than £13 million over the past decade through auctions, fundraising events and its work with clients.
