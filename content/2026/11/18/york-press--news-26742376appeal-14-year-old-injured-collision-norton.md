A 14-year-old boy was taken to hospital after a collision involving a moped and a bicycle in Norton.

The incident happened on Parliament Street at 3pm on Thursday, November 12.

The teenager, who was riding the bicycle, suffered a minor ankle injury that required hospital treatment.

The moped rider is described as a man in his 50s.

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage to help with their investigation.

A police spokesperson said: "We are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have seen the collision, anyone who may have seen the moped prior to the incident or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online in the first instance.

People can email Alyssa Upton at [Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Alyssa.Upton@northyorkshire.police.uk) or visit the force’s website and select 'Tell us about,' then choose 'An existing case or report.'

A live chat service is also available on the website, Monday to Friday, from 9am to 5pm.

Those unable to use these options can call 101.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
