A UK construction machinery giant, noted to be the country's largest firm of this nature, has collapsed into administration with over 200 redundancies reported.

Molson Group, headquartered in Avonmouth, Bristol, employs almost 350 people across sites in England and Scotland.

The construction machinery giant supplied equipment to contractors and plant-hire businesses across the UK, with its nationwide operation covering southern England.

The business operated from 16 locations around the UK and employed more than 95 service engineers who supported contractors and plant-hire companies.

Molson Group had established itself as one of Europe's leading distributors of construction equipment from major global brands including Kobelco, Hyundai, Sennebogen, Bell Equipment, Thwaites and Merlo.

The company had a workforce of 345 across its various UK sites, with 203 redundancies reported following the administration.

Founder Robin Powell reportedly broke the news to staff on Friday morning during what was described as an "emotional" team meeting.

The group's five businesses are Molson Group, Molson Equipment Services, Capital Equipment Finance, Aggregate Processing Solutions and Molson Scotland.

Administrators have been appointed across all five businesses.

Ross Connock, Tom Crookham and Vesela Romagnoli have been appointed as joint administrators and will seek to sell the remaining machinery and parts stock while collecting outstanding debts.

Administrators blamed weakened demand in the UK and uncertain trading conditions for the company's difficulties.

The scale of the financial problems facing the firm was highlighted in its latest accounts.

For the year to September 2024, Molson Group reported turnover of £333 million.

Operating profit fell sharply from £15.9 million to £1.9 million, while the business recorded a pre-tax loss of £10.3 million, compared with a pre-tax profit of £8.1 million the previous year.

It is not the first time the business has faced insolvency, as Molson Holdings, as the company was previously known, entered administration in November 2007.

The business was subsequently rebuilt as Molson Group, with Jonathan Wilson and Mr Powell as co-founders.

The company's history stretches back to 1996, when it was founded by Mr Powell and Mr Wilson.

While the UK businesses have entered administration, Molson Group also has significant operations in the United States.

Its American operations will continue trading after the group's shares in the US businesses were sold to a newly incorporated company.

Mr Connock, of PwC, said: “It is regrettable that Molson Group’s UK businesses have entered administration and that this has resulted in a significant number of redundancies.

“The sale of the US businesses means those operations can continue outside the UK Group, preserving the business and employment there.”
