Level 42 are set to perform a special one-off concert in Scarborough.

The jazz-funk band will appear at Scarborough Spa’s Grand Hall on Sunday, December 6.

The group, led by frontman and bassist Mark King, has sold more than 30 million albums worldwide and enjoyed 18 UK Top 40 singles including Lessons in Love, Something About You, and Running in the Family.

Mr King’s distinctive slap-bass style and the vocals and keyboards of founding member Mike Lindup have defined the band’s sound for more than 40 years.

Level 42’s discography includes 14 studio albums, seven live albums, the Sirens EP, and six compilations.

They have previously sold out Wembley Arena for 21 nights.

Their unique mix of jazz-funk, pop, and musicianship continues to set the benchmark for the genre.

Special guests on the night will be Uncle Frank, known for their energetic live shows and soulful reggae sound.

Tickets are now extremely limited.

Fans are encouraged to book soon to avoid disappointment.

Platinum Lounge upgrades are also available for the event.

For tickets and more information, visit www.scarboroughspa.co.uk or contact the Scarborough Spa box office on 01723 376774.
