**Harrogate Town have signed Bolton Wanderers goalkeeper Luke Hutchinson for an undisclosed fee on a "long-term" deal.**

The 24-year-old joined the National League side on loan in September and has made 14 appearances in all competitions.

He joined Bolton's academy at the age of eight and made one appearance for the first team in the EFL Trophy in 2024.

"He plays with confidence, has a really good understanding of how we want to play as a team and has plenty of potential to grow with us," Harrogate's head of recruitment Lloyd Kerry told the club website., external
