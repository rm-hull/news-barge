Police in the Philippines have asked prosecutors to charge Thomas Markle Sr with possession of and intent to distribute child sexual abuse material, according to reports.

Graeme Elmido, Cebu province prosecutor, has told the BBC police filed documents requesting the Duchess of Sussex’s father be charged on Monday. Last month, Philippines police were granted a warrant to examine alleged child sex abuse images.

The 82 year-old was previously arrested and charged in the US with possession of child sexual abuse material, in a separate matter, following the seizure of approximately 78 items held in storage in the Los Angeles areas.

Among them were slides, film negatives and books that contained suspected child sexual abuse images. Markle has previously denied the allegations.

![Thomas Markle Sr faces a number of serious allegations](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/11/07/2026-11-11T050834Z_1901315574_RC27LOAZIMJ0_RTRMADP_3_BRITAIN-ROYALS-MEGHAN.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The US has an extradition treaty with the Philippines, which means Markle could face trial there if charged, and could face between 10 and 12 years behind bars if found guilty of accessing child abuse images.

Markle has admitted to paying for sex in the southeast Asian country but denies ever possessing a hoard of such material.

Earlier this month, he was taken into custody after cutting an electronic monitoring device off his ankle, the US Department of Justice said.

When he was first detained, he was taken by ambulance from a senior care center in Lakeland.

Hours later, a judge allowed his release on condition that he be electronically monitored, which typically means wearing an ankle tether. The judge also ordered a mental competency exam and a report by November 20.

First assistant US attorney Bill Essayli said: “Release conditions are court orders, not suggestions.”

![The Duchess of Sussex has said she was ‘deeply distressed’ at the accusations against her father](https://static.independent.co.uk/2023/05/01/09/meghan2.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Approving his return to custody following a hearing in Tampa, Florida, Judge Griffin said in her written ruling: “I found that the defendant violated the conditions of his pretrial release and that, based on the lack of suitable housing, no condition or combination of conditions would reasonably assure the safety of any other person or the community and the defendant’s appearance in court.

“Accordingly, for the reasons stated at the hearing, the defendant’s pretrial release is revoked, and he is detained pending the resolution of this case.”

Court documents show Markle has surrendered his US passport and tourist visa for the Philippines.

His daughter Meghan Markle, who moved back to the UK with the Duke of Sussex in August, said she was “deeply distressed” at the accusations against her father. She has been estranged from him since the run-up to her 2018 wedding.
