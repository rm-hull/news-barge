A Year 7 pupil at Harrogate High School has competed alongside his sister at the Commonwealth Karate Championship.

Jack, who represented JKS England, was six when his sister inspired him to join Harrogate Shotokan Karate Club.

He has since won medals in both kumite (sparring) and kata (patterns).

His performance at a competition earlier this year earned him an invitation to England squad trials at Harrogate High School, where both he and his sister secured places.

In August, just before starting secondary school, Jack earned his black belt.

The Commonwealth Karate Championship was his biggest competition to date, although he finished outside the medals.

Jack’s parents said:

> “We are incredibly proud of Jack and his sister for their achievements.
>
> "We hope they keep up the hard work and determination, not just in karate but in all aspects of their lives.”

Zoe Price, who owns Harrogate Shotokan Karate Club, said:

> “Our karate club has been proudly running in Harrogate for over 50 years, and we have been based at the Harrogate High School site throughout this time.
>
> "We are incredibly proud of all our students and love seeing their dedication, commitment and progress.”
