An ambitious new tour that celebrates the music of Billy Joel is coming to York next year.

Singer-songwriter and pianist Elio Pace and his band are taking their award-winning *Billy Joel Songbook* to a whole new level, launching an extraordinary 10-year project celebrating all of Billy Joel’s albums on their landmark anniversaries.

The journey will begin with *Elio Pace’s Billy Joel Songbook: Celebrating 50 Years of The Stranger*, as the acclaimed live show hits the road next autumn visiting 17 theatres across the UK – including in York.

The show will play at York Barbican on **5 October 2027**.

Tickets for York will go on sale at 11am this Friday via the York Barbican website.

Over the next decade, Elio and his band plan to take audiences on a journey through the extraordinary catalogue of one of the world’s most celebrated singer-songwriters, performing each celebrated album in full as it reaches a landmark anniversary.

From the records that launched his career to the multi-million-selling releases that produced some of the most enduring songs in popular music, each tour will shine a spotlight on a different chapter of Joel’s remarkable musical legacy.

“I am beyond thrilled to be embarking on this unprecedented 10-year project, performing these incredible albums live on stage in some of the most prestigious theatres across the country,” said Elio.

“There are 40th, 50th and 60th anniversaries that all fall so beautifully over the next decade, and I can’t wait to share the genius of Billy Joel and bring these albums to life on stage.”

The opening tour, *Elio Pace’s Billy Joel Songbook: Celebrating 50 Years of The Stranger*, marks the golden anniversary of Joel’s iconic breakthrough 1977 masterpiece.

Widely regarded as one of the greatest albums of all time, *The Stranger*will be performed in its entirety, featuring timeless classics including* Just The Way You Are, Movin’ Out (Anthony’s Song), She’s Always A Woman, Only The Good Die Young*and* Vienna*.

At every show audiences will be invited to submit requests for their favourite Billy Joel songs, which will be selected at random each night.

Elio added: “What makes this project even more exciting is the element of surprise. We’ll be inviting audiences to request songs from across Billy’s catalogue, then choosing them live on the night, proper drawn-from-a-hat requests! It’ll definitely keep us on our toes, but that’s all part of the fun.”

The decade-long celebration will span a remarkable series of landmark anniversaries, with each year bringing a new album into the spotlight.
