A STOLEN motorbike has been recovered - however the county police are looking to get to the bottom of the theft.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a black and grey Benelli BN125 motorcycle was stolen in Knaresborough.

The theft took place in Castle Yard between 8.30pm on Monday, November 16, and 1.15pm on Tuesday, November 17.

A police spokesperson said: "Following initial enquiries, officers recovered the motorcycle."

Officers added that they are particularly interested in speaking to anyone who may have who may have seen anything suspicious in the area during this period or who may have relevant CCTV, dashcam or doorbell footage.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Dean Barrett at North Yorkshire Police by emailing [dean.barrett@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:dean.barrett@northyorkshire.police.uk), submitting information through the website, or by calling 101.

Please quote reference 12260225869 when providing information
