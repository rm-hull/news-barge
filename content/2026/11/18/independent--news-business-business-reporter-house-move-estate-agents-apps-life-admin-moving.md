*Street Group is a Business Reporter client*

Most of life has moved onto a phone. Banking, shopping, even booking a doctor’s appointment takes a few taps. But moving home still runs much as it did decades ago: chasing updates by phone, signing paperwork in an office and waiting weeks for news on a sale. Sitting in the middle of this are estate and letting agents, juggling higher client expectations with outdated technology, rising compliance and squeezed margins, often without the capacity to grow.

Street.co.uk exists to change that. Built in Manchester by a team of more than 220, Street is rebuilding the home-moving process from the ground up.

The aim is simple: a home move that feels as joined up as the rest of modern life. Every party in the transaction sees what’s happening without chasing, while technology takes care of the admin behind the scenes. That leaves estate agents free to do what no software can: advise, negotiate and build relationships with their clients.

![Any time, anywhere: A seller can check the progress of their sale whenever and wherever they want to](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/11/51/Street-Group_Body-Image-1.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

## **One platform for the whole move**

Most agencies still run on a patchwork of systems. Sales sits in one place, lettings in another, accounts and marketing somewhere else, and they rarely talk to each other. Every gap between those systems means a delay for the client and more admin for the agent. Street.co.uk brings sales, lettings, client accounting and property management into one platform. Estate agents on Street are able to offer their customers free dedicated apps for sellers, buyers, landlords and tenants. A seller can check the progress of their sale at 11pm on a Tuesday. A buyer can give feedback and submit offers on their terms. A tenant can report a repair and track it without picking up the phone.

This modern, mobile-first experience suddenly becomes a huge USP for vendors choosing an agent over their competitors. As everything sits in one place, the data is clean and connected. Every enquiry, viewing, offer and tenancy lives in the same record. And because Street ships over 50 new features every month, agencies aren’t left waiting years for an upgrade. That connected foundation is what makes the next step possible.

## **Street Cortex: AI that does the work**

The phrase “AI-powered” now appears on almost every piece of software, and much of it amounts to little more than a chatbot. Street Cortex is different. It’s an agentic AI platform that sits on top of Street, letting estate agencies build and orchestrate their own AI agents and set them to work inside the software.

Those agents don’t just answer questions. They take action. A Street Cortex agent can pick up an enquiry at 9pm, check whether the buyer is already registered, offer viewing slots and book the appointment when they reply.

Another can work through hundreds of potential tenants in a morning, match each one to new property stock, send a personalised email and update the record when they respond. Others chase compliance documents, write local area content or even manage lettings maintenance end-to-end.

Street Cortex Agents can start the moment an enquiry lands or run in bulk across tens of thousands of records, drawing on each agency’s own procedures and the latest legislation. The estate agent stays in control throughout. Any action can require human approval before it happens, and every agent can be tested on real data before it goes live.

The result is capacity. Across the industry, 40 per cent of enquiries never get followed up. With Street Cortex, every enquiry gets a response, a follow-up and action, while the team spends its time on valuations, viewings and the conversations that win instructions.

## **Proof in the numbers**

The increased capacity Street.co.uk gives estate agencies is crucial because UK agency fees remain among the lowest in the developed world, so every agency is being asked to do more with less for each instruction.

Estate agents running on Street are already showing what’s possible. They are outpacing their competitors, growing 14 per cent faster and 24 per cent more likely to complete a sale on average. Faster growth means more instructions without a matching rise in headcount, and home movers who walk away with a story worth recommending.

That’s what happens when the busywork disappears and the whole move runs through one connected platform.

## **Expectations will continue to evolve**

The next generation of home movers will expect to manage their move the way they manage everything else: from their phone, on their schedule. The agencies that can offer that, backed by AI that handles the work behind the scenes, will set the standard for everyone else.

Street is building the platform that lets estate agents deliver that standard, and shaping what the future of estate agency looks like.
