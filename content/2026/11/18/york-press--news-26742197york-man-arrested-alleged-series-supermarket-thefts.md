A MAN has been arrested after targeting supermarkets across York.

North Yorkshire Police says its officers have arrested a 31-year-old man has been arrested following a series of shop thefts that took place over several weeks this autumn.

The arrest was finally made after a theft was reported at the Sainsbury's store on Bootham at 10.51pm on Tuesday (November 17)

A police spokesperson said: "Staff identified a man suspected of being involved in a number of previous thefts.

"The suspect is alleged to have picked up a quantity of alcohol before leaving the store without paying. He was later located by CCTV operators at the Museum Street Tavern."

He was then located after cycling towards Union Terrace.

Further investigation found that the man is suspected of stealing from Sainsbury's store on Burton Stone Lane on October 6.

He is suspected of returning to the same store on three separate occasions in the following days to steal more items.

The man was arrested on suspicion of theft from a shop and remains in police custody whilst the investigation continues.
