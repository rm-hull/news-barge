A tiny whimper from deep underground led rescuers to a missing Harrogate dog who had been trapped in a badger sett for almost a week.

Enzo, a black Cocker Spaniel, disappeared from the Sweet Briar area of Jennyfields on Monday, November 9, sparking a major search involving volunteers, drones, tracking dogs and members of the local community.

Despite days of searching, putting up posters and checking CCTV and Ring doorbell footage, there had not been a single confirmed sighting of him.

But on Sunday (November 15), with Enzo approaching his seventh night missing, volunteers from search organisation K9 SAR decided to go back to the beginning and reassess the search.

New information had come to light which led the team to a specific area, where an eagle-eyed volunteer spotted Enzo's yellow and black harness tangled in brambles.

A further search nearby revealed what appeared to be an uninhabited badger sett.

Enzo's owner was immediately contacted and rushed to the scene, calling his name.

At first there was no response, but after a little while a small whimper could be heard coming from deep underground.

Enzo was alive.

With time running out, the fire service was called immediately. Crews from Harrogate and Ripon arrived quickly to carry out the delicate rescue.

Enzo's owner's friends and family also brought a drain camera, which proved invaluable in helping establish where he was inside the underground structure.

After almost seven days missing, Enzo was finally brought safely back above ground and reunited with his owner.

Footage of the reunion shows him back in his owner's arms, appearing to be doing well after his ordeal.

The rescue followed an enormous community effort, with residents and volunteers searching on foot, drones covering difficult-to-reach areas and people checking their CCTV and Ring doorbell footage for any sign of him.

Tracking dogs were also brought in on Friday, November 13, identifying an area of interest around Grantley Drive and the surrounding streets.

Residents in Grantley Drive, Lincoln Drive, Harewood Road and Lindrick Way were subsequently asked to check their cameras again.

Two potential sightings had been reported during the search, but neither could be confirmed as Enzo.

Sharon Ainley, from K9 SAR, said the lack of any confirmed sightings eventually led the team to question whether they had been looking in the wrong places.

She said: "After a week of searches, drone flights, postering and the community checking their CCTV/Ring doorbell with absolutely no sign of Enzo, local or anywhere - not one sighting - we realised something was wrong.

"We had to be missing something, so this morning we went back to the beginning and started again."

K9 SAR, which has been operating since 2022, works across Yorkshire, supporting searches for missing dogs through local teams and experienced search coordinators.

Following Enzo's rescue, the organisation thanked the fire crews from Harrogate and Ripon for their swift response and for being "so kind and gentle with Enzo".

It also thanked Mike for bringing the drain camera, as well as the volunteers and residents who searched, put up posters, checked cameras and shared appeals on social media.

Sharon said: "For seven days, people looked for a dog they didn't know simply because somebody else's dog needed to come home."
