An oil tanker has erupted in a huge inferno in the Strait of Hormuz, leaving one crew member missing and 11 injured.

Dramatic footage shows the tanker engulfed in towering flames throwing up a thick column of black smoke over the waterway, which remains blockaded by Iranian and US forces.

The crew had abandoned the Panama-flagged Tara liquefied petroleum gas on Tuesday after the vessel was struck by a projectile while sailing through the strait, security sources said.

There has been a surge in tanker attacks this month in the Strait of Hormuz, which remains blocked by Iran in response to the US-Israeli war against it.

![A tanker erupts in flames in the Strait of Hormuz](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/09/2026-11-17T171352Z_450277371_RC2SPOAB868L_RTRMADP_3_IRAN-CRISIS-TANKER.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The attack also came after the US launched fresh strikes on Iran’s Qeshm Island, with military officials saying they targeted a missile launch site in retaliation for Iran's targeting of US personnel and assets. Iranian media said no infrastructure was damaged.

The back-and-forth attacks were a stark reminder of the major hurdles hindering diplomatic efforts to end a war that has destabilised the Middle East and significantly pushed up global energy prices.

United Arab Emirates shipping company ADNOC L&S, said on Wednesday that most of the crew had been evacuated but that one person was missing, without naming the vessel.

![One person is missing following the attack](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/16/2026-11-17T163423Z_135826967_RC2SPOAEF0LI_RTRMADP_3_IRAN-CRISIS-TANKER.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Oman's emergency response authorities said they had rescued 24 crew members from the Tara around 2 nautical miles off the Musandam Peninsula after it caught fire. The search is ongoing for a missing person and efforts to put out the fire are still under way.

The ship had also been attacked on Monday, as confirmed by India’s external affairs ministry, which said the shipping attacks are “deeply worrisome and pose a serious threat to the safety of civilian seafarers”.

British navy-linked agency UKMTO said a ship had been disabled by a first attack on Monday, before the second attack caused a fire on board. It did not name the ship in its statement.

![The US military said it struck a Revolutionary Guards missile launch site on Qeshm Island](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/09/2026-11-18T071442Z_913088351_RC27QOA8QSRZ_RTRMADP_3_IRAN-CRISIS.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Launched by the US and Israel on 28 February - before Iranian forces immediately blockaded the strait - the war has threatened energy exports from Gulf countries as shipping remains disrupted in the vital waterway. The strait carried one fifth of the world's oil and gas supplies before the conflict.

Elsewhere, Tehran said it had struck US drone and missile installations at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem base. It was the first Iranian attack on the oil-rich country since September.

Neighbouring Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian aerial attacks for the third time this week.

It accused Tehran of maintaining a "systematic hostile approach" of targeting the country, which hosts the US Navy's Fifth Fleet.

The Saudi-led coalition fighting the Iran-aligned Houthis in Yemen said it destroyed a cruise missile launched by the militia towards the Saudi capital of Riyadh.
