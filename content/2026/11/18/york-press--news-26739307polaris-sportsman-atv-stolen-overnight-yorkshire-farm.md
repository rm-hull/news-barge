Thieves have targeted a farm in East Yorkshire and made off with agricultural equipment.




Humberside Police said an ATV/Quad Bike was stolen from a farm in the Blacktoft area near Goole overnight between Wednesday, November 11 and Thursday, November 12.

A force spokesperson said offenders entered the farmland, approached an outbuilding, and cut off the bolt and lock before stealing a dark green Polaris Sportsman 570 ATV.

The vehicle was carrying a 70-registration plate and had a red and white slug pellet machine attached, the spokesperson added.

Anyone with information or who sees a similar ATV abandoned or parked nearby is asked to contact Humberside Police, quoting reference number 26000159866.
