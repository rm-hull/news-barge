# BBC correspondents discuss historic Trump, Xi and Putin meeting

Three powerful world leaders - Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin - are set to meet on the sidelines of the Apec summit in Shenzhen.

The BBC's China and North America correspondents, Laura Bicker and Nada Tawfik, and Russia editor, Steve Rosenberg, explain everything you need to know about the historic trilateral meeting.
