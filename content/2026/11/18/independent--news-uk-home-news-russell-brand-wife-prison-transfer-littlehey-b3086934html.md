Russell Brand could be transferred to another prison after he issued an “appalling” message from his cell protesting his innocence.

Prison officials are understood to have decided to move the rapist after meeting on Tuesday to discuss whether he needed to be placed in a higher security jail, sources told *The Times*.

It is unknown where Brand will be transferred to, but he had previously been assessed for HMP Wakefield, which is home to some of Britain’s most notorious serial killers, murderers and rapists.

However, the prison, where disgraced Lostprophets singer Ian Watkins was stabbed to death, was deemed to be too dangerous for Brand, the publication said.

Brand was jailed for 10-and-a-half years earlier this month for raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others.

![Russell Brand with his wife Laura Gallacher, who shared a message from her jailed husband (James Manning/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/08/ffbc77f3e24dc114517a30983e106d15Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1MDc2MzY0-2.86369927.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In a statement shared on YouTube by his wife, Laura Gallacher, on Sunday, the former Hollywood star claimed: “I am not guilty of the offences I have been jailed for.”

A Downing Street spokesman condemned the “appalling” message and said the matter was being urgently investigated because prisoners are strictly prohibited from making statements that cause distress to victims.

Brand was already transferred to the lower-security Category C prison, HMP Littlehey, on Friday from HMP Wandsworth.

His move was first reported by *The Sun*, which said concerns for Brand’s safety and him being deemed a low escape risk could have prompted his transfer. The prison is for men who cannot be trusted in an open prison but are judged unlikely to try to abscond.

A prison source told the newspaper that Brand’s calls will now be listened to by staff and that the rapist will face far greater scrutiny if he is moved to a higher-security prison.

Investigators will reportedly determine whether Brand’s message was an attempt to influence accusers whose allegations are currently being investigated by police.

Brand’s message said: “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support.

“As you know, I am not guilty for the offences I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.

“The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do.

“I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey, little as in small, and hey as in hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders’ prison in Europe.

“The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind, and they are dedicated.

“And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”

![Russell Brand was moved to HMP Littlehey on Friday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/18/b00c31c2661c81ef5081438fe226e3dbY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk0OTM3NDAx-2.1364645.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He added: “I don’t know which other men here are not guilty, and the ones that are, they are people that Christ died to save, like you and me. Let us all love, pray for, and forgive one another as God forgave us.”

Former prison governor John Podmore told the BBC: “We cannot stop him speaking to his wife. We cannot stop his wife relaying his thoughts, his views and his comments.

"Those views are normally being conveyed to his followers on his channel and we can’t do anything about that.

“But I’ve been listening to TV and radio in the past 24 hours and most other people have done that, and it’s enormously disrespectful for the victims.

“So yes, let’s have a look at the rules. Let’s have rules that pertain to the way social media works, but the prison service lives in an analogue age and it’s not about to change any time soon, I’m afraid. But if the government wants to do something, [it] should do it.”

The prime minister’s spokesman added: “Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims. And speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances. Prisoners cannot get around these rules by having someone else publish their comments.”

Brand is now facing a string of fresh sexual assault and rape allegations, including one made by a 16-year-old schoolgirl.

Under the sentence he received on Monday, he is expected to serve two-thirds of his prison sentence before being released on licence.
