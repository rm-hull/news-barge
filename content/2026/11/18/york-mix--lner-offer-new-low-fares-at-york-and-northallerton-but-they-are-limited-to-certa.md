LNER, the East Coast Main Line rail operator, is offering a one-way ticket to or from London for £14 (York) and £15 (Northallerton).

It follows the success of a similar trial on the Leeds to London route.

These tickets are for early-bird and night-owl services.

*Travel Period:*
*from 16th November until 16th December 2026 inclusive, with limited availability over the Christmas travel period.*

*Availability will continue from 4th January 2027.*These tickets can be booked on the following trains only:* 04:40 York to London King’s Cross*

*22:03 London King’s Cross to York*

*20:09 Northallerton to London King’s Cross*

*22:03 London King’s Cross to Northallerton*

Monday-Thursday Only

These are some of the cheapest fares available on the main line to London for years.

They are roughly the same as British Rail charged on the old Nightrider overnight train which ran in the early 1980s
