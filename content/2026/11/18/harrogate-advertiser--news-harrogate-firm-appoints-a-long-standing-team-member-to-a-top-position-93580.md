A Harrogate law firm has appointed a long-standing team member to a top position.

Harrogate-based LCF Residential, the specialist conveyancing division of leading law firm LCF Law, has appointed director Lynn Wilkinson as its new head of residential.

Lynn has been with the firm for ten years and brings extensive experience, in-depth technical knowledge and a strong understanding of the firm’s clients and operations to the role.

She will lead LCF Residential’s team of 18 people, based across the firm’s four offices in Ilkley, Harrogate, Bradford and Leeds.

“I am delighted to be promoted to head of residential,” said Lynn.

“I have very high standards and want to ensure that every client receives the same personal, proactive service, regardless of the value or complexity of their property transaction.

“Buying or selling a home can be one of the most stressful experiences people go through, and I genuinely enjoy the client contact and personal nature of the work.

“It is particularly rewarding when clients tell us that we have taken that stress away.

CF Law is an award-winning full-service law firm, which operates regionally, nationally and internationally and has more than 145 people, including 24 partners, across offices in Leeds, Bradford, Ilkley and Harrogate LCF Law, whose office is based at The Exchange on Station Parade.

In addition to advising homebuyers and property sellers, Lynn Wilkinson has particular expertise in agricultural and rural property transactions, including smallholdings and land, as well as dealing with technical complexities such as water supplies.

She is also a valued source of technical support for the wider team, regularly drawing on her experience to help colleagues navigate more challenging and complex property transactions.

Lynn said: “What makes LCF Residential different is that clients are never just a file number.

“They have a dedicated lawyer who knows their transaction, understands their circumstances and is available to answer their questions.

“We also have an incredibly knowledgeable team of qualified solicitors, who understand the local market and can deal with the most complex property matters.

“My priority is to build on the excellent foundations already in place, support the team and continue delivering the high standards of service our clients expect.”

LCF Residential works closely with homebuyers, sellers, estate agents and mortgage lenders across the UK and handles a significant number of high-value property transactions.

It also benefits from a substantial amount of repeat business and referrals.

Ragan Montgomery, managing partner at LCF Law, added: “Lynn is an excellent example of the talent and experience we have within our business.

“She understands our culture, our clients and, importantly, what makes LCF Residential different.

“Her technical knowledge, leadership qualities and commitment to exceptional client service make her the ideal person to lead the team.”
