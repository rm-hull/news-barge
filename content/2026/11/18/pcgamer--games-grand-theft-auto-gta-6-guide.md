## GTA 6 guides

![GTA 6 bank - A guy in a car with cash and a gun](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/WupBLNoLkWTWcyn8GQ3JbW-2560-80.jpg)

* **Criminal profiles**: This is the GTA 6 equivalent to RDR2's Honor System. This is what you need to know to keep Jason and Lucia's reputations in alignment, whether you want to be by-the-book or totally unhinged on the criminal scale.
* **How to make money**: This is what it's all about, baby. Here's all the most efficient ways to make cash and some ways that are a little less efficient but still fun.

![GTA 6 protagonists Jason and Lucia.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/SQTxCj2iTGEqc2BjeqrxsW-2560-80.jpg)

* **Save the game**: You've got 15 save slots to work with, here's how.
* **Open a bank account**: You need one to start selling cars and keep your cash safe.
* **Get safe codes**: Here's how to find the codes to the cash during normal robberies.
* **Get a parachute**: Find this to take a nice glide around Leonida or escape your airplane theft gone wrong.
* **Get six stars**: Why's the dial go up to 11? I don't know, but here's how to do it.

![Jason stealing a car](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/UdvPTt9yoAQQC52GZNGfXK-2560-80.jpg)

* **Steal cars**: Here's all the basics on the name o' the game.
* **Get a slim jim**: You need this to break into cars without an elbow ouchie.
* **Clone keys**: Do this so you don't have to keep hotwiring your lifts.
* **Get a snap gun**: This helps you break into all sorts of locks, not just cars.
* **Refuel and repair cars**: Freshen them up for resale or your own garage.

![GTA 6 Leonida Keys screenshot](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/9pDAzpntNnfZrmZwFU6oBo-2560-80.jpg)

* **Military base**: Everybody just wants to steal a tank. Good news: It's not a long drive from the game's starting zone to the barbed wire pantry of government goodies.
* **Fence locations**: You've got loot and they've got buyers. These are all the dealers willing to give you cash for your ill-gotten goods.
