Shop staff wrestled an imitation firearm from a would-be robber during a dramatic confrontation.

The attempted robbery took place on Mill Lane in Shiney Row at 11.45am on Monday, when a man entered a shop carrying what appeared to be a firearm.

A second man, who was outside the store, also fled. Police have have released an image of a man they want to speak to in connection with the incident.

A spokesman for Northumbria Police said: "We want to speak with him following a report of an attempted robbery at a premises on Mill Lane at 11.45am on Monday, in the Shiney Row area of Houghton-le-Spring.

"This man was in the area at the time and may have information which could assist our enquiries."

During the incident, a member of staff managed to disarm the suspect before he escaped.

Both men were last seen heading towards South View.

Nothing was stolen during the attempted robbery and no one was injured.
