# Stalker with 'fixation' on Co-op worker smashed 10 fridges with hammer

## Stephen Reith has been sentenced

A stalker wrote letters to a shop worker he had become "fixated" with during a terrifying campaign.

Stephen Reith wrote a number of letters to the woman, who worked at his local Co-Op store in Swarcliffe and his campaign of terror came to a head when he used a hammer to smash a number of fridges, causing thousands of pounds worth of damage.

Leeds Crown Court heard on Tuesday, November 3 that the woman would hide when Reith would enter the shop in the hope he would leave.

Prosecutor Vincent Blake-Barnard said: "On the fifth of April, early in the morning he entered the store carrying a hammer and bought orange juice. Later, at 9.30am, he entered the store and started shouting for the woman. In fear, she walked around the store followed by the defendant, who had a hammer in his pocket. She was not aware of this.

"She left the shop floor and hid in the back only to be followed by the defendant who waited for around three minutes knocking on the door causing her clear distress. A manager spoke with him and he said he wanted to apologise for the letter and he was told to leave. He was said to be intoxicated at the time.

"She returned to the till and he returned four more times and on each occasion she left the till to hide until he left."

The court heard that Reith, of no fixed abode, entered the store in the early hours of April 13 and then later that same day, once again armed with a hammer.

Mr Blake-Barnard said: "He proceeded to use it to attack 10 fridge units, smashing them...A staff member asked him what he was doing and he said, 'What the f\*\*\* are you going to do about it?' He walked calmly from the store. At 13.42, the police arrived at his home address. The door had blood on it and there were concerns about his safety so they entered and found a hammer with blood on it on a unit.

"He was not there and they later found him outside his address shouting in the street and holding a mug."

Reith was then arrested. The court heard he caused £43,000 in damage to the fridges. Stock inside also had to be thrown away at an additional cost, and the shop was closed that day and the following day, costing £24,000

In a victim impact statement, the woman Reith admitted stalking said the incident has made her "hypervigilant" when working and her manager has since moved her to another store.

Her Honour Judge Rayner told the court: "She said she is constantly on edge and the idea of serving him made her uncomfortable because by then she was aware of the letter he had left. She was unnerved by what happened."

It was said Reith had previously stalked an ex-partner by sending her 160 messages.

Mitigating, Andrea Parnham told the court Reith would like support with his alcohol use and can "clearly see the benefits of being sober" and added: "There is a number of triggers to alcohol consumption from the end of his relationship and seeing his children."

Judge Rayner said Reith had served the equivalent of a 21-month sentence while on remand at HMP Leeds and said of the victim: "You appeared to have somewhat of a fixation or interest in her which was unrequited...She became very nervous to go to work and the thought of serving you was causing her great concern and she had to move stores."

Reith was made the subject of a two-year community order during which he must complete a 12-month alcohol treatment requirement and will be subject to an exclusion zone around the store and a trail monitoring requirement to ensure his probation officer can see where he is spending his time.

He will also have to complete a Probation requirement.
