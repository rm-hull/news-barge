The recording shows people gathered outside a former school building, anxiously discussing the threat of further attacks. "Misha, is it coming again?" a woman asks, before a warning crackles over a police radio: **"Guided aerial bombs heading towards Komyshuvakha! Take shelter!"** Officers urgently direct people down the stairs into an underground refuge. Moments later, the camera records a**violent explosion**.

The strike **killed at least 37 people**, according to Ukraine's national police,** including the officer whose bodycam captured the attack**. The shelter, located inside a former school building, was hit by a** Russian FAB-3000 aerial bomb**, a powerful weapon weighing approximately three tonnes.

Rescue workers continued searching through the wreckage for victims as the scale of the destruction became clear. The footage provides a harrowing record of the final warnings and frantic efforts to reach safety before the building was struck, offering a **rare first-hand perspective** on the deadly bombardment.
