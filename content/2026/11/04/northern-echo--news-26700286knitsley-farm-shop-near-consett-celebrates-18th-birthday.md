A much-loved County Durham farm shop has celebrated its 18th birthday after growing from a “small but brave idea” into a multi-award-winning rural business.

Knitsley Farm Shop, near Consett, marked the milestone today (Wednesday, November 4), with its owners thanking the generations of staff and customers who have supported the business since it first opened in 2008.

The family-run shop and Granary Café at East Knitsley Grange Farm is known for home-reared produce, breakfasts and locally sourced food.

But the family behind the business said the journey to its 18th birthday had not always been easy, with challenges including the pandemic, rising costs, health issues and building problems.

Marking the anniversary, the team said: “We have no idea where to start, but we are so incredibly grateful to each and every customer who has stepped foot in our door, to every staff member from our very first day in 2008 to today.

“The past 18 years simply couldn’t have been possible without you guys showing up each and every day.

“We think 18 years is a pretty big milestone.”

The family said what started as a relatively small idea had developed into something they were immensely proud of.

They added: “What started as a small but brave idea has transformed into something so special, and it’s not stopping there.

“It’s not been the easiest of 18 years, a global pandemic where we thought that was the end of Knitsley, family health issues, building challenges, the daily dilemmas that every business faces and of course huge rising costs.

“But throughout it all, we’ve kept our heads down and worked hard to keep everything going.”

The business has built a strong local following, with the Granary Café serving breakfasts and lunches alongside the farm shop.

Its own home-reared produce remains at the heart of the business, with the family describing that as the “little original dream” which helped turn Knitsley Farm Shop into the multi-award-winning business it is today.

Its popularity has previously seen customers praise its breakfasts, meat and other farm-shop produce, with the rural venue attracting visitors from Consett and the surrounding area.

The 18th anniversary is also a particularly personal milestone for the family's next generation, who reflected on having grown up alongside the business.

They said: “On a personal note, growing up around the business since I was one year old has been incredible, tough at times with such hardworking parents but I could not be prouder of every single person involved.

“Our team is just amazing and you guys (our customers) are delightful to work with.

“We are so proud to serve the quality and our own home-reared produce - that little original dream that’s pushed on to make Knitsley the multi-award-winning farm shop and cafe that it is today.”

The family also thanked its team, local businesses, loyal regulars, new customers and those who have recommended the farm shop to others.

Looking towards the next chapter after 18 years in business, they added: “Here’s to whatever the future may bring… are we ready?”

In 2023, Knitsley was highly commended in the Countryside Alliance's Local Food Awards after a public vote that attracted more than 40,000 votes.
