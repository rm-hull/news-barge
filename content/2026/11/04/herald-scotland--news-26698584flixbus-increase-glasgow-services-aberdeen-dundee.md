FlixBus is expanding its Scottish network, increasing services between Glasgow, Aberdeen and Dundee and introducing two new destinations.

From November 23, the coach operator will increase daily departures between Glasgow and Aberdeen, and Glasgow and Dundee, bringing the total to 20 in each direction on both routes. The services operate in partnership with McGill’s Buses.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669919.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Flix buses. *(Image: Supplied)*

The company will also launch its new 095 route between Aberdeen and Edinburgh, with stops in Kirkcaldy and Glenrothes. Both towns will join the FlixBus network for the first time, with each set to receive up to 16 daily departures.

The expansion follows the introduction of additional cross-border services between Scotland and England in August, as well as the opening of a FlixBus shop at Edinburgh Bus Station.

FlixBus, which marked its fifth anniversary this year, now serves more than 100 destinations across the UK.

Shamil Maindiratta, managing director of FlixBus UK, said: "As part of our long-term strategy for growth in Scotland, we’re offering more services from Glasgow to Dundee and Aberdeen, as well as new destinations in Kirkcaldy and Glenrothes, with our trusted partner McGill’s.

"Together, we’re making affordable, reliable and sustainable long-distance travel available to even more residents and visitors to Scotland, with journeys for less than a fiver."
