A resident was left terrified and shaken up after four yobs tried to break into their home in broad daylight.

The incident took place just after 3pm on Beckfield Road in Bingley on Tuesday afternoon.

West Yorkshire Police is investigating the reported attempted burglary.

## What does the CCTV show?

Footage shows two men dressed in black attempting to gain access to the property via the back door.

One of them appears to be holding pliers.

The men are then joined by two others, who begin to try and force their way into the home.

The incident left the victim "absolutely terrified and shaken up".

## What police have said

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called to reports of an attempted burglary at a residential address on Beckfield Road in the Bingley area.

"The incident occurred at around 3.08pm yesterday (Tuesday 3 November)."

Enquiries remain ongoing.
