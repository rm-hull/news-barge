A man crushed into metal shelving when he was hit by a car on a garage forecourt suffered a fatal cut to his neck, a jury heard.

Daniel Ambler also suffered a fracture to his spine which could also have proved fatal, a pathologist told a murder trial.

The 23-year-old died after Ashton Anderson drove a Nissan X-Trail into him on January 15, Teesside Crown Court heard.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20435374.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Daniel Ambler *(Image: DURHAM POLICE)*

He was pronounced dead after the crash at the Esso Asda Express service station in Seaham.

Pathologist Dr Louise Mulcahy said Mr Ambler suffered multiple cuts and bruises when he was pinned against shelving racks.

She said blows from a hammer or crowbar could have caused his head injuries.

Prosecutor Matthew Donkin KC took Dr Mulcahy through her post-mortem findings.

Dr Mulcahy said: “Mr Ambler’s death was caused by traumatic injuries he sustained to his neck.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20439774.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Esso New Seaham Service Station, in Stockton Road, Seaham, the scene of Daniel Ambler's death *(Image: Sarah Caldecott (The Northern Echo))*

She confirmed that he had suffered cuts to his jugular vein and carotid artery.

“That would have resulted in immediate bleeding or haemorrhage,” she said.

Dr Mulcahy said the spinal injury could cause “almost immediate death”.

Asked whether it was the shelving that could have caused the neck injuries, she replied: “It is, yes.”

The doctor also confirmed that Mr Ambler had cocaine, cannabis and alcohol in his system when he died.

The jury was shown CCTV footage prosecutors say shows the five murder accused hunting down and attacking Mr Ambler and his friend Reece Wilkin.

The attack was the culmination of a feud which began after Barry Anderson was stabbed last August.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20437048.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Crime scene investigators at the scene *(Image: Andy Futers)*

The court heard that Daniel Ambler’s brother David and his friend Jed Wilson arranged to fight Anderson, who was stabbed in the side during the struggle.

Barry Anderson, 52, of Canon Cockin Street, Sunderland, Ashton Anderson, 20, of Ryton Crescent, Seaham, and Barry Logan Anderson, 19, of Nursery View, Shotton Bank, face charges of murder and violent disorder.

Ajay Henry, of Melrose Crescent, Seaham, and Tyler Leadbetter, of Elgin Avenue, Seaham, were both 17 when charged with murdering Daniel Ambler.

Ashton Anderson has pleaded guilty to violent disorder, while all other charges are denied.

The day after Mr Ambler died, messages shared in a Snapchat group included a warning that police would be looking at everyone in Deneside, Seaham.

The trial is expected to last up to five weeks at Teesside Crown Court.
