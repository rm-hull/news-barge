# Man hit by bus in Leeds city centre as 999 crews descend

## A 60-year-old was hit by a bus on Vicar Lane

A man was hit by a bus in Leeds city centre.

The 60-year-old was hit by the vehicle on Vicar Lane at 10.24am today, on Wednesday, November 4. He was rushed to hospital for treatment. There was a road closure in place for about 45 minutes.

A Yorkshire Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We received an emergency call at 10.24am this morning (Wednesday, November 4) to report a collision between a bus and a pedestrian on Vicar Lane in Leeds. An ambulance was dispatched to the scene and one patient was conveyed to hospital."
