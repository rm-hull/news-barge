A FORMER water treatment works is set to be transformed into a hub for industry and job creation.

Located in North Bierley, the West Yorkshire site has been sold by Keyland Developments Ltd to Bond It Group, a UK manufacturer of sealants, adhesives, and building chemicals.

Keyland, the property trading arm of Kelda Group, secured planning permission for around 130,000 sq ft of industrial development prior to the sale.

Matthew Turnbull, strategic land lead at Keyland Developments Ltd, said: "The sale of North Bierley to Bond It Group marks another significant milestone in the regeneration of this former Yorkshire Water site.

"Following the successful delivery of planning permission, we are pleased to complete a transaction that will see the site brought forward for employment use and deliver long-term economic benefits for the area."

The site, located near the Chain Bar junction of the M62 and M606, forms part of a wider employment area with potential to deliver in excess of 500,000 sq ft of industrial and employment space.

The first phase of the site’s transformation secured permission in 2016 and was later developed into Interchange 26.

Keyland said the North Bierley project reflects its strategy of regenerating brownfield land across the north of England through planning and development.
