A MUCH-LOVED Yorkshire nightclub has closed its doors after more than 40 decades.

Spiders – an alternative and rock music venue located in Cleveland Street, just outside Hull city centre – has announced yesterday (November 4) that it is closing with immediate effect.

First opening in 1979, the club was independently owned and spread over two floors, with three main rooms adorned with gothic style decorations including metal spider webs creating dividers between the main dancefloors and seating areas.

They offered a range of music styles from metal and punk, to rock, 80s, Motown, and alternative with a promise to ‘never play cheesy music’.

In a statement, the owners said: “We are sad to inform you that due to personal and external situations, Spiders Club has closed, it will be put up for sale on the open market, including the freehold; as they say, Lock Stock and Barrel.

“We know closing the club will come as a great shock to many of you, as the club is financially sound and doing well, but there comes a time, after 46 years of loving our club and what it gave to the people of Kingston upon Hull, that now, is the right time to hopefully pass it on to others, who will carry it forward with the same enthusiasm, idealism and integrity, that we started so many years ago.

“We appreciate that many of you will be upset by the club closing, but please remember, as the saying goes, time and tide wait for no man or woman, and for us, being upset, doesn't even come close.

“To all our loyal and respected customers. Take care and thanks for being a part of our journey.”

The bar is yet to be listed listed for sale, although several commenters have shared their upset and spoke about the idea of turning it into a community venue.
