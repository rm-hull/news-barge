## What you get for £130 at one of York’s leading fine-dining restaurants

Culinary director Kevin Bonello is leading a new seasonal concept at Legacy restaurant at The Grand, York. *(Image: Submitted)*

A chef is foraging wild ingredients by hand for a bold new seasonal tasting menu at a leading York fine-dining restaurant.

Culinary director Kevin Bonello is leading the new concept at Legacy restaurant at The Grand in York, which launches today, Wednesday, November 4.

The menu, named Three Seasons, will change throughout the year and is inspired by Yorkshire’s natural larder and the people who produce its ingredients.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21667246.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Culinary director Kevin Bonello is leading a new seasonal concept at Legacy restaurant at The Grand, York. *(Image: Submitted)*

As part of the line-up of events, Legacy will offer an eight-course tasting menu for £110 per person and a 10-course option for £130.

The restaurant will also host monthly Legacy Chef’s Table events, with the next one taking place on Friday, November 20.

Guests will be welcomed with champagne and canapés before enjoying a five-course menu curated by Mr Bonello. Tickets are priced at £150 per person.

![Culinary director Kevin Bonello is leading a new seasonal concept at Legacy restaurant at The Grand, York.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21667247.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant will host new events including a monthly Legacy Chef’s Table. *(Image: Submitted)*

Mr Bonello said: "After two and a half years, we've given the entire dining experience at Legacy a complete overhaul, and we can't wait to share it with our guests.

"I've spent the best part of my life obsessing over food and the stories behind it, and this new concept is that love story brought to life.

"I've travelled all over the world throughout my career but found the inspiration I was looking for in my very own village.

"There have been months of preparation behind the scenes, and as a team, we're so excited to finally bring it all to the table here in York."

The menu will be shaped by what is available locally, including ingredients foraged by Mr Bonello himself.

It also features bespoke hand-carved utensils crafted by the chef.

![Culinary director Kevin Bonello is leading a new seasonal concept at Legacy restaurant at The Grand, York.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21667267.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chef Kevin Bonello is overseeing the menu, named Three Seasons, which will change throughout the year. *(Image: Submitted)*

Each course begins with a single ingredient and is built around produce from Yorkshire farmers, producers, and fishers.

The current season, Wild & Ferment, celebrates game and wild food with earthy, rich and full-bodied flavours.

Dishes include venison and game birds paired with foraged blackberries, kale, and hispi cabbage.

In March, the menu will shift to Forage & Root, highlighting ingredients from northern forests and hedgerows.

This will be followed in July by Fish & Harvest, celebrating the waters surrounding the British Isles with sustainably sourced fish and seafood, prepared through light, fresh cooking.

The restaurant’s interiors have also been redesigned to reflect the Three Seasons theme.

Former staff member and artist Lily Roberts has painted custom murals for the space, and a cheese trolley crafted by York woodworker Ollie Abell will feature as part of the service.

A wall of fermentation cupboards has also been installed.

Mr Bonello previously held a Michelin star at de Mondion in Malta before joining The Grand in 2023.

A new Legacy Lunch menu will launch on Friday, November 13, offering three courses for £60 or four for £80, available every Friday and Saturday.
