## York school partnership aims to change perceptions of dyslexia

St Peter’s School has teamed up with the global charity Made By Dyslexia to host an event promoting the strengths of Dyslexic Thinking *(Image: Made By Dyslexia)*

A new partnership is aiming to change perceptions of dyslexia in York.

St Peter’s School has teamed up with the global charity Made By Dyslexia to host an event promoting the strengths of Dyslexic Thinking and raising awareness of the potential of dyslexic individuals.

The event will feature screenings of the charity’s short animation What is dyslexia?, featuring the voices of Jeremy Irons and Liv Tyler, and will include talks from Dame Maggie Aderin, space scientist and broadcaster, and Kate Griggs, founder of Made By Dyslexia.

Head master Jeremy Walker said: "At St Peter’s, we believe every child has unique strengths and talents that deserve to be recognised and nurtured.

"Our partnership with Made By Dyslexia reflects our commitment to helping young people understand themselves, celebrate the ways they think differently and develop the confidence to thrive."

Dame Maggie will share how her own Dyslexic Thinking skills have helped her navigate challenges and succeed in her career in science.

A panel discussion will also address the value of Dyslexic Thinking in the workplace, with speakers from HSBC and Randstad Enterprise.

The event supports Made By Dyslexia’s mission to shift the narrative around dyslexia from a focus on challenges to recognising strengths such as creativity, communication and big-picture thinking.
