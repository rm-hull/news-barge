The Bowes Museum's late-night event returns on November 21, 2026 for an atmospheric evening of art, creativity and culture. Visitors can choose from creative workshops inspired by the museum's three current exhibitions, expert talks on the autumnal night sky, and entertainment throughout the event. Stay for the whole evening experience or simply soak up the late-night atmosphere while dining and shopping in the museum.

The evening combines free and ticketed creative activities. Visitors can tailor their evening from the programme of free events included with general admission, or book onto ticketed activities for a more hands-on experience.

## Premium experiences

Brush & Bubbles is a creative painting and dining experience in the picture galley, led by artist Betty Barnet Brown. Visitors will explore a selection of striking portraits featuring flamboyant hats, hairstyles and headpieces, using these as inspiration for artwork of their own. Betty will offer advice on colour and technique, helping painters of all abilities grow in confidence while they enjoy a grazing board and a glass of fizz, or non-alcoholic alternative.

Inspired by the themes and styles explored in Impressions of Light: Masterpieces from MuMa Le Havre and The Bowes Museum, art lovers can create their own picturesque scene in Framing the Landscape led by artist Anna Selway. Visitors will experiment with expressive brushstrokes and a loose painting style to capture atmospheric scenes with a sense of movement through colour and light.

## Activities included with general admission:

### Creative workshops

In drop-in Artist Workshop sessions led by artist Jane Young, visitors can create a beautiful souvenir: a hanging decoration created using origami techniques and lace inspired by the Lace and Legacy: Treasures from the Blackborne Lace Collection.

Sketching Prompts invites visitors to pick up a sketchbook and pencil from Norman Cornish: A Life in Sketchbooks and in the style of the master, follow prompts around the galleries creating an artistic record of the evening to take home.

### Explore the night sky

Look up to the stars with the Learning Team in a Beyond the Bowes Tour exploring planets, stars and the night sky. For an experience that is truly out of this world, step into the Wonder Dome planetarium for an immersive exploration of space, with remarkable views and insights from astronomy experts.

### Music and sound

Throughout the night, poet and performer Lizzie Lovejoy will present a series of pop-up spoken-word pieces around the museum, culminating in a specially curated finale inspired by the event. The silent disco will be open throughout the evening in the Jubilee Room, for music lovers to dance the night away.

### See the collection in a new light

In My Museum in Five Objects, the museum's executive director Hannah Fox reveals five favourite items from the collection, explaining why they mean so much and how they reflect the museum's story.

Explore the newly opened Lace & Legacy: Fashion Treasures from The Blackborne Collection with Fashion & Textiles curator, Rachel Whitworth in the Fashion and Textiles Cube. Go behind the scenes with rare access to the museum's vaults, or to witness preservation in action in the Conservation Studio and learn how the museum protects treasures from the collection.

Two Silver Swan performances offer a rare opportunity to see the beloved 18th century automaton in action in the atmospheric setting of the museum after dark.

## Eat, drink and shop

Café Bowes will serve an evening menu, offering visitors the opportunity to dine in the museum after hours, booking is essential. Drinks are also available throughout the night from the Picture Gallery Bar.

Tables for Café Bowes can be reserved by calling +44 (0)1833 690 606 or emailing [events@thebowesmuseum.org.uk.](mailto:events@thebowesmuseum.org.uk.)
