To call Berlin’s housing market difficult would probably be an understatement. Affordable housing is scarce, and finding a flat can feel like searching for a needle in a haystack.

For many, online property portals that help them navigate the market are their last hope. But the companies behind these sites often exploit that hope. The Berlin Consumer Advice Centre (Verbraucherzentrale Berlin, VZB) is currently scrutinising the online portal mietsofort.de – and issuing a strong warning about hidden costs.

### One-Off Fee to Access Listings on Other Property Portals

“Looked for a flat, got a bill instead,” the VZB writes in an Instagram post. It explains why anyone using mietsofort.de is unlikely to find an available flat but may instead receive a €79 bill.

Anyone who finds a suitable flat on the criticised online portal and wants to contact the advertiser must first register. “Shortly afterwards, a €79 bill arrives, with just five days to pay,” the VZB writes.

The housing platform lists the fee, somewhat hidden away, under the “Prices” section. It even advertises the fact that users are not signing up for a monthly subscription but are instead purchasing a software package. This package is said to include market data and a rental price check, an application assistant that creates a personalised application letter tailored to the listing, and various checklists.

### Many Flats Have Already Been Advertised for Weeks

However, according to the VZB, all users get access to other property portals where the flats were originally listed. Mietsofort.de does not feature any listings of its own.

A spot check by the VZB found that many of the flats had already been advertised on the original portals for weeks or even months. “Given Berlin’s housing market, it is highly doubtful that the flat is still available,” the Consumer Advice Centre writes. According to the VZB, the provider is merely exploiting people’s desperation.
