## 'Outstanding' Michelin star restaurant named one of the best in the world

Mýse has earned global recognition for its elegant Yorkshire cooking. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A “fantastic” restaurant in Yorkshire where “every detail is wonderful” has been named one of the best in the world.

La Liste has revealed its list of the best restaurants in the world, with many spots being found right on our doorstep.

Found in the picturesque village of Hovingham, Mýse made it onto the list with a score of 86.5 out of 100.

Mýse “celebrates the ancient North Yorkshire terroir surrounding” it and offers an “elegant spin” on Yorkshire cooking.

## What is Mýse like?

The restaurant is named after the Anglo-Saxon word for eating at the table and offers dishes using ingredients and flavours from the local area.

Mýse has both a lunch and evening tasting menu with dishes ranging from cured old sow with wild fennel pollen and braised ox cheek in Yorkshire pudding batter to duck bone broth and crab custard served with preserved wild mushrooms, alliums and oxidised wine.

Towards the end of your meal, there are a few sweet dishes, including simple strawberries and cream and candied raspberries as well as a flamed quince cake with malted barley and crushed frozen apples with lemon balm.

The lunch tasting menu costs £115 per person, while the evening menu is a little more expensive at £175 per person.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659273.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant offers a lunch and evening tasting menu. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

You can also add a drink pairing to your experience with a non-alcoholic pairing, a wine pairing, and a prestige pairing, all being available.

If you want to extend your stay in the area, Mýse also has six guest rooms that blend “charm with classic elegance and comfort”.
