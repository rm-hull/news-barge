Bin collection days will change for some Darlington households over Christmas and New Year.

Darlington Borough Council has confirmed revised collection dates for both household waste and recycling over the festive period.

Most collections will continue as normal, but people whose bins would usually be emptied on Christmas Day or New Year's Day will see changes.

In a leaflet sent to residents, the council asked residents to make sure bins are out for collection by 7am, as collection times themselves may also change over the festive period.

Bins should be brought back in as soon as possible after they have been emptied.

## Key dates

Collections due on Friday, December 25 will instead take place on Monday, December 28.

Those normally due on Friday, January 1 will be collected a day later, on Saturday, January 2.

Collections scheduled for Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, will take place as normal.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20991442.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

Services will also run as usual from Tuesday, December 29 to Thursday, December 31, with all other collection days remaining unchanged.

Recycling collections, including food waste, will continue to take place weekly, while general household waste collections remain fortnightly.

The changes are:

* Thursday, December 24: No change
* Friday, December 25: Collection moved to Monday, December 28
* Tuesday, December 29 to Thursday, December 31: No change
* Friday, January 1: Collection moved to Saturday, January 2

All other collection days will remain the same.
