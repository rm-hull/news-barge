**A Scottish high school has been closed, and all pupils have been sent home after a "suspicious package" was reported on the campus.** The council confirmed it evacuated the school as a precaution this morning, in line with emergency procedures, after a suspicious package was reported.**Read More: Elgin Academy to reopen after suspicious envelope incident**

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (SFRS) has confirmed crews are on scene but said it is a "police matter".

Three fire units were sent to Elgin Academy, along with two special fire service appliances.

A Moray Council spokesperson said: “We can confirm that Elgin Academy was evacuated as a precaution this morning in line with emergency procedures after a suspicious package was reported.

"Pupils were sent home and school transport was arranged. We thank the pupils and staff for their professionalism and calm approach to the evacuation. More details will follow once emergency services have completed their enquiries and parents will receive further updates directly.”

Police Scotland has confirmed that the package was a "suspicious envelope".

Local Area Commander for Moray Chief Inspector Andy Meikleham said: "Around 11.05 am on Wednesday, 4 November, 2026, we received a report of a suspicious envelope sent to a school on Morriston Road, Elgin.

"The school is being closed as a precaution and emergency services are in attendance.

"Enquiries are ongoing."

The Press and Journal has reported that an “urgent” message was sent to pupils’ parents, stating: “There has been an issue in one of the toilets and the school is being closed for the day.

“All pupils are being sent home immediately.”
