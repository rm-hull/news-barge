York St John University alumni have returned to York Business School to share their career experiences with the next generation of marketing professionals.

Alessandra Betancourt Gallo, Grace Whittaker and Grace Thomas met Level 4, 5 and 6 Marketing students in October to discuss university opportunities, internships, placements and the transition into graduate employment.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21644946.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: York St John University)*

The graduates also shared advice on building professional networks and developing the skills needed to succeed in the marketing industry.

Organised by marketing course leader Emma Johnson, the session gave students first-hand insights into the opportunities and challenges of starting a career in marketing.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21644941.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: York St John University)*

The event highlighted the important role alumni play in supporting current students and helping them prepare for life after graduation.
