It is a game which has captured the nation's attention, inspiring countless theories around kitchen tables and office desks.

And now people are being given the chance to have their very own Traitors adventure by hiring an entire Scottish hostel for some TV-themed skulduggery.

Hostelling Scotland is giving groups the chance to create their own version of the game with its new 'Turret Experience', available through its Rentahostel scheme.

Groups can take over selected youth hostels for their exclusive use between November 2026 and March 2027, with some able to cater for groups of 50 guests or more.

The package includes cloaks for designated Traitors, tartan shawls, shields, chalkboards, picture frames, a Traitors board game and a Claudia Winkleman-inspired wig, giving gamers the props to organise their own night of secret roles, round-table discussions and banishments.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669252.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Groups of guests can play Traitors for a weekend *(Image: Hostelling Scotland)*

The latest series of Celebrity Traitors, which saw Faithfuls Romesh Ranganathan and Sebastian Croft emerge victorious, attracted an average audience of 11.23 million viewers, making it the most-watched episode of the celebrity spin-off to date.

Filmed at Ardross Castle in the Scottish Highlands, the series has once again brought the country's dramatic landscapes to a huge national audience.

Margo Paterson, CEO of Hostelling Scotland, said: "It's been fantastic to see audiences across the country captivated by the Scottish Highlands through The Traitors, and the incredible locations that have provided the backdrop to the series.

"We wanted to give people the chance to experience that for themselves, with our own take on the show.

"Hiring an entire castle isn't realistic for most groups, but through Rentahostel it's possible to enjoy the same dramatic Highland setting without the huge price tag."

She added: "It's a chance to bring friends and family together somewhere special, with the Turret Package adding a little Traitors-inspired drama to the weekend."

Available locations include Rowardennan Lodge on the eastern shores of Loch Lomond, Braemar, Cairngorm Lodge, Ratagan on the banks of Loch Duich, and Torridon on the NC500.

The properties sleep between 37 and 69 guests, offering options for everything from smaller groups to larger gatherings.

Two-night private stays start at £1,180 at Torridon Youth Hostel, which sleeps up to 53 guests. Other options include Braemar for up to 40 people, Rowardennan Lodge for up to 57, Ratagan for up to 37, and Cairngorm Lodge for up to 69.

For smaller groups, a partial Rentahostel booking may be available, and people are urged to contact Hostelling Scotland's reservations team directly to discuss specific requirements.

The Turret Package is available on request when booking selected Rentahostel properties for exclusive stays between November 2026 and March 2027.
