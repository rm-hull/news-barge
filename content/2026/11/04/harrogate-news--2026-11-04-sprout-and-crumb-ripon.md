Sprout & Crumb Ripon is a new food outlet on North Street, bringing chef Luke Mills’ extensive experience from Michelin-starred kitchens and established restaurants to the city’s independent food scene.

A new food outlet has opened on North Street in Ripon, with chef Luke Mills bringing extensive experience from Michelin-starred kitchens and established restaurants to the city’s independent food scene.

Called Sprout & Crumb, the business is based at 85 North Street and focuses on wholesome, nutritious takeaway food, using locally sourced ingredients and offering a menu that can evolve in response to customer feedback.

## Experienced chef behind the new venture

Luke Mills has developed his culinary skills working alongside Michelin-starred chefs, including Herbert Berger at Lombard Street in London and Tessa Bramley at the Old Vicarage in Sheffield.

He also spent seven years as a menu development chef at Filmore & Union, followed by two years working at the Light Brigade Restaurant in Woollahra, New South Wales, Australia.

Luke said customers would play an important role in shaping the menu, with their feedback helping him develop dishes and introduce new choices.

![Freshly prepared salads, sandwiches and food on display at Sprout & Crumb in Ripon](https://harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/sprout-2.webp)
> **Luke Mills**
>
> For me, the most important ingredients are the customers, because their feedback helps me to create customised dishes which add to an ever-evolving choice, rather than just offering a set menu.
>
> I’m open to suggestions and if I get asked to introduce a new dish to meet a particular taste, I will give it a go.

## Locally sourced ingredients

Sprout & Crumb aims to provide wholesome and nutritious takeaway food, making use of locally sourced products and working with other independent businesses.

Among the suppliers are sweet treats made by Leah Wright at The Wright Cake Bakery in Northallerton, alongside artisan filter coffee from Lonton, roasted in Teesdale.

Luke runs the business with the support of his wife Lucy, who also runs the Sprouted Kitchen in Bedale and L Bar coffee shop at Leeming Bar. The couple have two sons, Drew and Jax.

## A warm welcome from Ripon

Luke and his family have been impressed by the welcome they have received since arriving in Ripon. They see the city as having considerable potential, with a strong community and independent business scene.

> **Luke Mills**
>
> We have been welcomed with open arms by local residents, visitors and other independent business owners and it is clear that there is a real community feel here and a buzz on the streets.

That welcome has also been reflected by neighbouring businesses. Sarita McDermott, owner of Realitea Indian tearoom and bistro, which is next door to Sprout & Crumb, welcomed the new arrival.

> **Sarita McDermott, owner of Realitea**
>
> We’re delighted to welcome this lovely new addition to our local community — a lunchtime deli serving up fresh, healthy salads and tasty options to brighten up your lunch break.
>
> Why not pop in, say hello and explore what they have to offer? Remember shopping totally locally really is the way to go.
>
> We encourage everyone to keep supporting all our wonderful independent businesses. Help Ripon be vibrant, and our high street to thrive.

## Adding to Ripon’s food and drink choice

Ripon BID manager Lilla Bathurst said the arrival of Sprout & Crumb would add to the choice available to residents and visitors.

> **Lilla Bathurst, Ripon BID manager**
>
> Luke brings a wealth of experience to Ripon and the skills he has learnt in award-winning kitchens adds to the choice that the city’s food and drink sector offers visitors and local residents alike.

## Opening hours

Sprout & Crumb is open at 85 North Street, Ripon, from 11am to 3pm on Tuesdays and 10am to 3pm from Wednesday to Saturday.
