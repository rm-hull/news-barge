Rail passengers are facing delays and cancellations this morning following an operational incident in the Cambuslang area.

ScotRail said services travelling through the area are currently subject to delays, alterations and cancellations while the issue is dealt with.

The disruption is affecting a number of routes across the west of Scotland, including services between Dalmuir and Larkhall, Dalmuir and Motherwell via Whifflet, Dalmuir and Cumbernauld, and trains running between Lanark and Glasgow Central.

Commuters have been warned that journeys may take longer than usual and that some services may not run as scheduled.
