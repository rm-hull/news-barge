A NEW scheme is aiming to improve safety and sustainability on match days.

Bradford Council is introducing reusable polycarbonate cups for bars on North Parade, a busy area for Bradford City Football Club fans on home match days.

The cups are designed to replace single-use plastic glasses and reduce the risk of broken glass on the streets.

Councillor Ian Walker, portfolio holder for neighbourhoods and communities, said: "This scheme is a simple step which will both improve safety on the public realm and help pubs and bars on North Parade be more sustainable as these cups are reusable.

"We want to support these businesses which are often extremely busy on home match days."

Bradford Council’s licensing department developed the scheme, funded by £3,000 from Gainshare Capacity Funding via the West Yorkshire Combined Authority.

The funding includes a strand to support the District’s nighttime economy, improve the public realm and support the hospitality industry.

A total of 10,200 pint-sized cups will be distributed among venues on North Parade.

Each cup is white, branded with the council’s crest, and carries the message: "Bradford Council Licensing Service. Support the team. Respect the City.

"Making North Parade welcoming for everyone," along with "North Parade BD1" on the reverse side.
