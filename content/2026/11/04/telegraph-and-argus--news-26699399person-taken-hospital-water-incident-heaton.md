A person was taken to hospital after an incident in the water.

The incident happened near Long Lane in Heaton yesterday evening (Tuesday).

An ambulance responded and transported the individual to hospital.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "An ambulance responded to an incident involving a person in the water near Long Lane in Heaton yesterday evening (3 November).

"One patient was conveyed to hospital."

The T&A has reached out to West Yorkshire Police for further comment.
