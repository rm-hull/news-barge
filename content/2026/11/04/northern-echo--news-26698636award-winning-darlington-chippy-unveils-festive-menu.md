Halloween has barely ended, but a Darlington fish and chip shop is already serving up Christmas.

Award-winning Cockerton Fisheries, on Woodland Road in Cockerton, has unveiled a festive menu packed with seasonal favourites, giving customers the chance to tuck into pigs in blankets, stuffing and cranberry sauce weeks before December arrives.

The chippy declared on social media that the "festive season has officially arrived" after putting away its Halloween decorations.

The centrepiece of the new menu is The Big Festive Box, priced at £7.50, which includes pigs in blankets, stuffing, chips, cranberry sauce and a can of pop.

Customers after something a little smaller can choose The Cozy Combo for £5, made up of chips, stuffing and gravy, while Pigs & Gravy costs £4.50 and comes with pigs in blankets and a regular tub of gravy.

The business invited customers to head down to Woodland Road for their "first taste of Christmas" and asked followers which festive offering they would be trying first.

The early festive launch is the latest move from a business that has become one of Darlington's best-known takeaways.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670083.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cockerton Fisheries has unveiled its Christmas menu weeks before December, offering customers festive-themed chip shop treats from its Woodland Road premises. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

Last year, Cockerton Fisheries was crowned The Northern Echo's Best Chippy for a second consecutive year, and owner Samantha Todd said the recognition had helped boost business despite difficult trading conditions across the fish and chip industry.

She said rising fish prices had created challenges for many independent chip shops, but praised the support shown by loyal customers.

The family-run shop has also expanded beyond its traditional takeaway offering in recent years, branching out into catering for weddings and large events. Samantha previously revealed the business had delivered dozens of portions of fish and chips to wedding parties across the region.

Meanwhile, daughter Poppy Wilson has helped strengthen the shop's online presence by taking charge of social media and marketing.

Now, with Christmas food appearing on the menu before Bonfire Night celebrations are even out of the way, Cockerton Fisheries is hoping customers are ready to embrace the festive spirit early.

For anyone already counting down the days until Christmas dinner, the Woodland Road chippy may have the perfect answer.
