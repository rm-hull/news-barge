Tucked away in a hidden Darlington courtyard is a café that Star Wars fans love.

The independent business has built a loyal following by blending artisan coffee, homemade bakes and a passion for a galaxy far, far away.

Echo 3 Coffee House & Roastery, located in Clark's Yard between Skinnergate and High Row, was recently highlighted by Northern Echo readers as one of the best in the North East.

It has earned praise for its freshly roasted coffee, friendly atmosphere and unique Star Wars theme.

The independent business was founded by husband-and-wife team Kris and Helen Eland, whose journey into coffee began more than 11,000 miles from County Durham.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21664662.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Echo 3 Coffee House & Roastery in Darlington has become a popular destination for coffee lovers thanks to its artisan roasts, homemade bakes and distinctive Star Wars theme. *(Image: ECHO3)*

The couple spent 18 months living in Wellington, New Zealand, a city renowned for its thriving café culture.

During their time across the pond, Kris trained as a barista and developed a passion for speciality coffee.

When they returned to Darlington, they felt the town lacked the coffee scene they had come to enjoy overseas and decided to create something themselves.

Echo 3 opened its doors in 2018 and has since established itself as one of Darlington's most distinctive independent businesses.

Its name is a nod to the Star Wars universe, inspired by the couple's love of The Empire Strikes Back, and that influence is immediately apparent to customers stepping inside.

The café's walls are lined with memorabilia, from Stormtrooper helmets and collectables to themed decorations and artwork, creating an atmosphere that stands apart from the average coffee shop.

Visitors are greeted by the aroma of freshly roasted coffee beans, with roasting taking place on site.

The process allows the team to maintain quality while offering customers a range of carefully crafted drinks.

Alongside classic coffees including cappuccinos, lattes and americanos, Echo 3 has earned a reputation for its homemade sweet treats, with cakes, brownies, traybakes and scones regularly tempting customers from behind the counter.

The café's coffee has won praise from visitors, who regularly highlight the smooth flavour of its house-roasted beans and the care that goes into every cup.

But it is not just the coffee that has helped Echo 3 attract a dedicated customer base.

Over the years, the business has become known for its annual Star Wars Day celebrations on May 4, when fans queue along Clark's Yard to join themed festivities.

The events have become a fixture in the café's calendar, raising money for charity while bringing together customers and Star Wars enthusiasts from across the region.

Its combination of quality coffee, homemade food and community spirit has helped establish Echo 3 as a favourite among Darlington residents and visitors alike.

For those seeking an alternative to the high street chains, the café offers something a little different, combining speciality coffee culture with personality and independent charm.

Whether customers arrive for a flat white, a slice of cake or simply to admire the collection of Star Wars memorabilia, Echo 3 has become a destination in its own right and one that many coffee lovers say is worth seeking out.
