A Harrogate homeless charity has linked up with one of the region’s top country estates to offer the perfect way to kick off the festive season with your loved ones.

As the Charity of the Year partner for Swinton Estate, Harrogate Homeless Project has joined together to host two festive fundraising events: a Christmas Santa Dash and a Festive Afternoon Tea, both taking place at Swinton Estate.

Every pound raised will help Harrogate Homeless Project provide vital support during the winter months, including emergency accommodation, warm meals, essential supplies and personalised support, helping people build brighter, more stable futures.

**Sunday, November 22, 11am-2pm: Santa Dash at Swinton Bivouac**

The 2.5k off-road Santa Dash is a fun, family-friendly event that will bring together supporters of all ages.

Participants can run or walk through the scenic grounds, with tickets from £5 and well-behaved furry friends welcome to join the adventure.

**Special Promotional Offer:** There is a 25% off all ticket prices for bookings made up to November 10.

Simply include the **SANTADASH25** promotional code when checking out to claim the discount.**Friday, December 4: Festive Afternoon Tea** Guests can enjoy a festive Afternoon Tea at Swinton Estat**e**, featuring a delicious selection of sandwiches, savouries, freshly baked scones and seasonal treats, accompanied by live music and a warm, cosy atmosphere.

Tickets are £59.50 per person or £200 for a table of four, and include entry to Luminescence, Swinton's enchanting Winter Light Trail, where guests can explore a magical mile-long illuminated wonderland from 4pm.

Founded in 1991, Harrogate Homeless Project provides:

An alternative to addiction.

43 rooms across Harrogate.

Mental and physical health support .

Relationship Counselling.

A day centre offering food and support.

A 9 strong client support team.
