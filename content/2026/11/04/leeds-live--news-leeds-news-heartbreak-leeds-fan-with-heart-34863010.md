# Heartbreak as Leeds fan 'with heart of gold' dies on way to Man Utd clash

## Michael Wright was 'funny, loveable and he had a heart of gold'

A lifelong Leeds United fan sadly died on his way to the club's most recent Premier League clash with arch-rivals Manchester United.

Beloved dad Michael Kenneth Wright, 80, from Malton, was a season ticket holder at the club since the early 60s, from the early days of the Don Revie golden-era. The retired butcher, painter and decorator, was heading from Leeds Train Station to catch his bus to Elland Road with a group of friends when he collapsed.

Two off-duty nurses were travelling past in a taxi when he collapsed and started CPR on him before he was sent to Leeds General Infirmary. He never regained consciousness and his life support machine was turned off the following day, on Monday, October 19.

Devastated daughter-in-law Melanie Wright said: "He was so funny, loveable and he had a heart of gold. Sitting in Leeds General and waiting for his final breath was heartbreaking. I had his Leeds scarf wrapped round our hands for comfort."

Michael leaves behind children Gary and Amanda. The father-of-two's friends and loved ones shared tributes online.

Paul Jennison said: "Top man played football at Westlers for him. Liked our chats when met him going to Leeds matches. Known him for 45 years. Never know anyone have a bad word about him. RIP Mick MOT."

Steve Willie said: "Absolute gentleman R.I.P Mick. Billy, Jack, Norman, Peter, Gary and the rest will have a drink for you." Keith Davies said: "Top man Mick. Worked with him at Westlers Beef Pack. Leeds through and through. Sorry to hear this."

Paul Cooke said: "Worked with Mick at Westlers Beef Pack. Top bloke. RIP Mick." Elizabeth Wattrus added: "He was a lovely man."

His funeral service will be held at The East Riding Crematorium, Octon, on Thursday, November 12, at 12.30pm. There will be a Leeds United theme and the family have requested no flowers.

The game ended in a 1-1 draw.
