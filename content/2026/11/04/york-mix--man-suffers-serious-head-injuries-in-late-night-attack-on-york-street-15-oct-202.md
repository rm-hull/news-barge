A young man had to be treated for serious injuries in hospital after being attacked in York.

It happened on **Blake Street** at around**4am** on**Thursday 15 October**.

The victim, a local man aged in his early 20s, suffered serious head and facial injuries which required hospital treatment.

#### Blake Street, York

Now police investigating the assault have shared these images of two people they want to talk to.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are urging people to come forward if you recognise either man or have any information that could help us to find them.

“We also want to hear from any witnesses to the incident or anyone with relevant video footage.”
