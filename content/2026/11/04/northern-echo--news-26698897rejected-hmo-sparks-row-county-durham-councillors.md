A row has broken out between two senior County Durham councillors with one accused of "misrepresenting" the other's comments after plans for a HMO were thrown out.

Plans for 20A Front Street in Annfield Plain would have seen a five-bed HMO created upstairs, while a former takeaway on the ground floor would have become a tanning salon.

Council planning officers had recommended approval, but a planning committee meeting held on Thursday (October 29) saw the plans thrown out.

In the aftermath, deputy council leader Darren Grimes accused Liberal Democrat opposition leader Amanda Hopgood of arguing that Annfield Plain "hasn't got enough HMOs", but Cllr Hopgood hit back, saying: "He has misrepresented me."

Cllr Grimes had asked for the application to be decided by councillors rather than officers because of concerns including the impact on the community, parking, highway safety and neighbouring residents.

Nine objections were submitted, including from Cllr Grimes, however, he did not attend the meeting.

Explaining his concerns, Cllr Grimes said: "I called it in on the grounds they were unwilling to go into detail on who would be moving into the property.

"It wasn't just myself that raised concerns about it.

"It would have ramifications on the wider area.

"I didn't think it should be a continuation of a migrant dumping ground and that is me jumping to conclusions, but I think that is fair because we don't get told anything."

Durham Police had also raised concerns about the fact the future occupants were unknown and said that, if the property were used for asylum relocation, there could be concerns about anti-social behaviour and hate-related crime.

However, council officers stressed that a decision could not be based on assumptions about who might live there.

![Deputy Council leader Darren Grimes.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21628675.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Deputy Council leader Darren Grimes. *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

## 'He has misrepresented me'

Following the committee decision, Cllr Grimes wrote on Facebook that Cllr Hopgood had argued Annfield Plain "hasn't got enough HMOs", contrasting the area with Durham city.

He added that the rejection showed Reform UK was "standing up for our communities".

Cllr Hopgood disputes his account, saying: "He has misrepresented me.

"I did not say there wasn't enough HMOs in Annfield Plain, I said that 3.5 per cent is not excessively high as we have 99 per cent HMO coverage in parts of Durham city."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670460.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cllr Grimes' post 'misrepresented' Lib Dem leader Amanda Hopgood, she said. *(Image: FACEBOOK)*

Cllr Hopgood pointed out that she voted against the application.

"There wasn't anybody there to speak against it, nobody came," she said.

"The irony is that I didn't vote for the application either.

"He is making all this fuss as if I didn't vote against it."
