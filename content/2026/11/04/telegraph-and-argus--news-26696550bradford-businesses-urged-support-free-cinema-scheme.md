A CINEMA scheme is aiming to double the number of free tickets it can give away this Christmas.

Pictureville Presents, launched during Bradford 2025, supports community groups across Bradford who face barriers to visiting the cinema, including financial hardship, social isolation, or mental health challenges.

The museum is calling on Bradford businesses to support the scheme and help expand its reach.

Last Christmas, 555 free tickets were distributed through 21 community groups, including MIND, Age UK Bradford, WomenZone, and Horton Housing.

Sally Folkard, head of screen and cultural engagement at the National Science and Media Museum, said: "Pictureville Presents showed us that the value of a cinema visit can go far beyond the ticket itself.

"For some people, it was a rare treat they would not normally be able to afford.

"For others, it meant getting out of the house, spending time with other people, building confidence or simply feeling welcome somewhere new."

A £5,000 sponsorship would double the number of free tickets available for Christmas 2026, offering even more people across the district the chance to enjoy a festive trip to the cinema.

Businesses interested in supporting the scheme are encouraged to contact [cinemaevents@scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk.](mailto:cinemaevents@scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk.)
