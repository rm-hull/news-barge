Footage shows demonstrators lying on the pavement, with groups of officers lifting them and carrying them to a waiting police van, while others continued holding placards. Police said **25 people had been detained** on suspicion of**supporting a proscribed organisation** under the Terrorism Act, with further arrests expected.

The legal challenge was brought by Palestine Action co-founder Huda Ammori, who is seeking to **overturn the group's designation as a terrorist organisation**. The court heard that it was the first of 98 proscribed groups** banned solely for causing serious property damage**, rather than advocating violence against people.** More than 4,000 arrests**have been made under the ban, including 325 outside the US embassy on 31 October.** Charges linked to the prohibition could be affected** if the appeal succeeds.
