Police have issued an appeal to help trace a missing man last seen in an inner-city area of Bradford.

Ludovit, 58, was last seen at 1.45pm on Otley Road in the Barkerend area on November 3.

West Yorkshire Police said: "We are appealing for the public's help in reporting any sightings of 58-year-old Ludovit who has been reported missing from Bradford.

"We are concerned for his welfare and would like to hear from anyone who has seen him or who has any information that could assist in locating him."

He is described as bald and wears black glasses.

It is believed he was last seen wearing a black coat, black sweatshirt, white shirt and a cap.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police quoting log 0771 of November 3.
