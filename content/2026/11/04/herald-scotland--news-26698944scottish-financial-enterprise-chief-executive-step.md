A chief executive is stepping down after nearly seven years in the role.

Sandy Begbie CBE, who has led Scottish Financial Enterprise (SFE) since October 2020, will leave the financial services body by next summer’s annual general meeting.

During his time as CEO, Mr Begbie led SFE through a period of growth, including delivery of Scotland’s Global Investment Summit last October.

He previously held senior roles at Tesco Bank, Aegon, Standard Life, Scottish Power, and Royal Bank of Scotland.

The SFE board has begun the search for a new chief executive, with Mr Begbie staying on to oversee major events in the first half of next year.

Mr Begbie said: "It has been an honour and a privilege to be CEO of Scottish Financial Enterprise for over six years.

"I was asked by the Board to lead the organisation into its next stage of development, modernise the way it operated and ensure SFE became one of the most influential trade bodies in Scotland and I am personally delighted with what we have delivered over the last six years.

"My decision has not been taken lightly but I feel, after what will be almost seven years, that it is time to pass over the opportunity to someone new, who can bring a fresh perspective and take SFE forward into its next stage of development.

"I will always look back and consider my time at SFE as a highlight in my career and I look forward to watching the organisation continuing to go from strength to strength."

Sue Dawe OBE, said: "Sandy has been central to the success of SFE over the past six years, both in leading our organisation and in championing our industry with government, in the media and overseas.

"Personally, I have thoroughly enjoyed working with Sandy during my time as Chair and am very grateful for all he has done."
