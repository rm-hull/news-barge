## Thief strikes in York suburb

Hybrid Riverside 920 cycle reported stolen in York *(Image: Supplied)*

North Yorkshire Police has received a report that a Hybrid Riverside 920 cycle in grey was stolen from outside a property in Manor Drive South, York.

This occurred between 10pm on October 31 and 1am the following day in Holgate.

North Yorkshire Police says anyone with any CCTV or information to assist the investigating officer, should call 101 and quote crime reference number - **12260215763."**
