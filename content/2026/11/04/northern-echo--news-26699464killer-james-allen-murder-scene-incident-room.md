The shocking story of a cruel killer who murdered two people on a three-day rampage is set to air in a Channel 5 show tonight.

James Allen savagely attacked 81-year-old Colin Dunford in his Middlesbrough home before travelling to Whitby and killing Julie Davison across three days in 2012.

The killer broke almost every bone in the “kind and generous” retired steelworker’s face when he unleashed a sustained and violent assault on April 22.

Three days later and the then-36-year-old hacked and slashed Julie Davison’s throat when he stabbed her 31 times to the head and neck.

![James Allen](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/2236319.jpg?type=mds-article-620)PURE EVIL: James Allen

Now, the story of how Allen carried out his robbery killing spree is set to feature in the third series of Channel 5’s Murder Scene: The Incident Room tonight (November 4).

A brief description of the episode, the sixth in the current series, states: “Detectives pursue a killer after 81-year-old Colin Dunford is murdered in his living room.”

Colin, who was last seen on the evening of April 22, was found dead the following day with head injuries at his Leven Street home.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/1985346.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Colin Dunford

Allen was trusted by the pensioner, and used his friendship to gain access to his home. At the time, he was lying low at a friend's house after being accused of a serious crime.

Allen repeatedly kicked and stamped on the pensioner before ransacking his home, eventually trying but failing to use Mr Dunford's bank card at a local cash machine.

Colin’s facial injuries were so severe that he would have probably died rapidly because his airway was blocked - and death was almost inevitable.

The alarm was raised when Colin failed to turn up at the Samuelson’s Workingmen’s Club for two nights in a row.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/7089816.jpg?type=mds-article-620)MURDERS: James Allen, from Middlesbrough was jailed for life with a minimum of 37 years

Police forced their way into his home and found his body.

By this time, Allen had disposed of his bloody clothes and cycled 30 miles to Whitby and then on to Scarborough, where he sold a stolen gold ring.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/1985236.jpg?type=mds-article-620)VICTIM: Julie Davison

A day later he was seen hanging around the communal hallway of Julie’s Whitby home, where police believed he talked his way in before launching an attack.

Investigators linked the two killings and a major manhunt was launched.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/1985233.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police at the scene of the murder of Julie Davison in Church Square, in Whitby, North Yorkshire

Allen had walked out of Ms Davison's flat wearing some of her clothes and with her laptop, travelling by bus to Malton where he bought food on his way to Leeds.

The manhunt ended on April 29 when he was spotted by an off-duty police officer.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/2236336.jpg?type=mds-article-620)NO EMOTION: James Allen on the Scarborough to Leeds Coastliner after carrying out the two killings

Allen was jailed for 37 years after a jury spent three-and-a-half hours deliberating and stayed silent when the unanimous verdict was read out.

He had numerous previous convictions, including an eight-year sentence for grievous bodily harm with intent and five years concurrent for burglary.

He had also been sentenced to 11 months in a young offenders’ institution for affray, 12 months for two offences of assault, and 18 months for possession of an offensive weapon.

He will not be eligible for parole until he has served 37 years.

*Murder Scene: The Incident Room airs tonight on Channel 5.*
