A man in his 20s has been taken to hospital after being stabbed in Grangetown.

Police were called by paramedics who found a man injured on Essex Street around 1.45pm today (Wednesday, November 4).

The man, 28, has been taken to hospital with a stab wound, Cleveland Police said.

His injuries are not believed to be life-threatening at this time.

A spokesperson from Cleveland Police said: “Enquiries are ongoing to establish the circumstances of what happened, and to identify those responsible.”

A cordon has been erected in the area and an investigation is ongoing.

A spokesperson from The Great North Air Ambulance said: "Our critical care team was activated at 1.48pm to an incident in Grangetown, Middlesbrough.

"We had two doctors and a paramedic on board our aircraft and they arrived on scene at 1.57pm.

"Our team worked alongside the North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) to assess and treat a patient.

"The patient was taken to hospital by a NEAS road crew, accompanied by our team."

Anyone who may have information is asked to contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting reference number 220840 or by contacting Crimestoppers online at crimestoppers-uk.org or by calling 0800 555 111.

The Northern Echo has contacted the North East Ambulance Service for more information.
