Two men have been charged after tensions flared ahead of Bradford City’s derby day defeat to Huddersfield Town on Saturday.

West Yorkshire Police said an “appropriate policing operation” was in place at Valley Parade to protect fans attending the fixture on Saturday, October 31.

A 45-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assaulting an emergency worker, while a 23-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of a Section 5 public order offence.

## Names of duo charged

![Rival fans on Manningham Lane.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654885.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

Today, police revealed that Jacob Bryan, 23, of Alexandra Street, has been charged with a public order offence and possession of a Class A drug.

He has been bailed to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on December 11.

Joseph Bryan, 45, of East Byland, Illingworth, Halifax, has been charged with a public order offence.

He has been bailed to appear at Bradford Magistrates’ Court on December 2.

An 18-year-old man was also arrested on suspicion of being drunk and disorderly, and a 52-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of assaulting a steward.

![Bradford City fans gathered on North Parade ahead of kick-off.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21654869.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

## How the day unfolded

As reported on the day, Bradford City supporters gathered outside pubs and bars on North Parade under the watch of a large police presence.

As kick-off approached, fans walked to Valley Parade past the Bradford Arms on Manningham Lane, where Huddersfield supporters had gathered after being escorted from Bradford Interchange.

Bradford City fans gathered on North Parade before kick-off.

Rival fans gathered along routes to the stadium as dozens of officers, including mounted police, were deployed.

Police vans lined Manningham Lane before kick-off as mounted officers kept the two groups apart.

There were several flashpoints as the supporters passed each other, with pushing and shoving involving some fans and officers.

Police mounted a major operation across the city.

Supporters from both sides also threw projectiles at each other.

After Bradford supporters reached Valley Parade, officers escorted Huddersfield fans to the stadium.

Bradford City lost the derby 3-0 to their Yorkshire rivals in front of a sold-out Valley Parade.

Huddersfield fans were escorted to Valley Parade

City were still in the game at half-time, but two quick-fire goals put it beyond Michael Duff’s side before Antoni Sarcevic’s red card made matters worse.

## Police statement on Saturday

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “An appropriate policing operation is in place at Mbanq Valley Parade to ensure the safety of everyone attending.

“A 45-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of assault emergency worker, a 23-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of a Section 5 public order offence.

"An 18-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of drunk and disorderly and a 52-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of assault on a steward.”
