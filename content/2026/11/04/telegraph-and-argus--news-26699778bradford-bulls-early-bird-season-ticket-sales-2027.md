Bradford Bulls have confirmed that more than 2,000 season tickets were sold during the Early Bird period.

The club say they are confident of reaching sales in excess of 3,000 by the start of the 2027 season, while they have confirmed they are adapting to the concerns of some fans.

This season's Early Bird period ran from September 21 to October 31.

Meanwhile, it lasted from October 15 to December 24 ahead of last season's return to Super League, when it was split into Red (slightly cheaper) and Amber periods.

The late start to sales then was due to Bradford waiting to find out whether they would be in Super League or not.

After announcing this season's figures, Bulls said: "We would like to place on record our sincere thanks to every supporter who has purchased or renewed their season ticket.

"Your continued loyalty, commitment and backing means a huge amount to us.

"We know that not every supporter was in a position to renew or purchase during the Early Bird window, and we've been listening carefully to the feedback received.

"Two recurring themes have been fixture scheduling and affordability.

"With that in mind, we're pleased to announce that we will soon be unveiling a new flexible payment option through Klarna.

"This will allow supporters to spread the cost of their season tickets over a number of months."

Further details, including how to take advantage of this option, will be communicated in due course.

Bulls will also be introducing Flexi Passes for the 2027 season, offering supporters a more flexible way to enjoy the action at Odsal.

These will go on sale once the 2027 fixture schedule has been confirmed and communicated to the club, with it still uncertain whether London Broncos will enter as Super League's 15th club or not.

Although the Early Bird period has now closed, season tickets remain on sale and continue to offer significant savings compared to purchasing tickets on a match-by-match basis.
