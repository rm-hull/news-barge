A North East England-founded accommodation business has made its first move into Scotland with the opening of a new design-led student property in the heart of Glasgow.

Cohabit8 began operating the newly completed 49-studio property on St Vincent Street this summer, marking an important milestone for the new company as it begins to establish its presence beyond the North East.

Cohabit8 said the Glasgow property reflects its approach to modern student and co-living accommodation, with a focus on creating well-designed, professionally managed places where residents can feel comfortable, supported and connected.

CEO Dan Salmon said: “Glasgow is a brilliant city, particularly for students, and it felt like a natural place for Cohabit8 to take its first step into Scotland.

“What’s particularly exciting for us is the property itself. This is a brand-new, design-led development and a chance for us to create the kind of experience we think modern student accommodation should offer from the outset.

“Newcastle will always be an important part of our story, but Glasgow represents a significant next step for Cohabit8 and our ambition to take this approach into more cities across the UK.”

The Glasgow opening forms part of Cohabit8’s plans to establish a presence in more UK cities, with the company exploring further opportunities across student accommodation and the wider residential market.

Mr Salmon added: “For us, growth isn’t simply about being in more places. It’s about finding the right properties and partners where we can create somewhere people genuinely enjoy living.

“Glasgow is an exciting opportunity to put that approach into practice in a new city and build on it as Cohabit8 develops across the UK.”
