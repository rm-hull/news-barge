Seven guns and live ammunition have been stolen after thieves broke into a locked gun safe at a Darlington home.

Police said they are "concerned" about the potential risk if the firearms fall into the wrong hands.

The incident happened at around 4am yesterday morning (November 3) in the Pateley Moor Crescent area.

Suspects forced entry to a house and stole shotguns, rifles and live ammunition that were stored inside a gun safe.

A silver Nissan Navara was stolen, but has since been found by police.

In total, five shotguns were stolen, and two bolt-action rifles, along with live shotgun ammunition.

Detective Inspector Clare Lambert, from Darlington CID, said: “The guns were all lawfully held, licensed, and stored inside a locked gun safe, which had been broken into and all the guns removed.

“Enquiries are currently ongoing to trace the offenders, and we are actively pursuing a number of leads in an attempt to apprehend those responsible and recover the stolen items.

“We are concerned about the potential risks posed by these firearms falling into the wrong hands, and are appealing for anyone with any information to come forward.
