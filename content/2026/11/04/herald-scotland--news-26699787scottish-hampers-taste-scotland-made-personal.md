Family-run since 1999, Scottish Hampers creates food and drink gifts hand-packed in Scotland which are not only perfect for Christmas but also birthdays, thank-yous and other special occasions throughout the year.

You can choose a ready-made hamper or build your own, each with a complimentary personalised gift message.

Scottish Hampers is a family-run gifting business established in 1999. Hand-packed in Scotland, its wonderful packages are also perfect for corporate gifting, with UK delivery available.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21671523.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Spirit of Christmas Collection *(Image: Supplied)*

Presented in a wicker basket, this festive collection combines Scottish shortbread, Dundee cake, mince pies, chocolates and traditional sweets with savoury biscuits, pâtés, preserves, tea and coffee. Hand-packed in Scotland, with a complimentary personalised gift message.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21671526.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Glenfiddich 12 Year Old Whisky & Scottish Luxury Hamper *(Image: Supplied)*

A full 70cl bottle of Glenfiddich 12 Year Old single malt Scotch whisky, paired with shortbread, Dundee cake, Scottish tablet, preserves and savoury treats. Hand-packed in Scotland for birthdays, Christmas and milestone celebrations.
