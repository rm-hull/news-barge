## Who are the most significant candidates that won a Senate seat?

Significant winners that won a Senate seat:

 

* Democrat James Talarico in Texas: The first Democrat Senator to represent Texas in more than three decades.

## Democrats Tlaib, Jeffries and Ocasio-Cortez win re-election to House

Prominent Democratic figures Rashida Tlaib, Hakeem Jeffries and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez have been re-elected to the House of Representatives.

Tlaib represents the state of Michigan, while Jeffries and Ocasio-Cortez both represent the state of New York.

Why are they prominent?

Jeffries is currently the Democratic leader in the House and could become the next Speaker if his party wins a majority, Ocasio-Cortez has previously said she would weigh out the decision to run for President in 2028, and Tlaib made history as the first Palestinian-American woman, as well as one of the first two Muslim women, to serve in Congress.

All 435 seats in the House of Representatives are up for election, of which a 218 majority is needed to gain control of the chamber.

## What you need to know

Voters across the US are heading to the polls to decide the balance of power in Congress for US President Donald Trump's final two years in office.

Around a third of the Senate's 100 seats are up for grabs, with 51 needed for a party to hold a majority. The Republicans currently hold 53 to the Democrats 45, while two are with independents.

Eyes will be on six key Senate races in Alaska, Iowa, Kansas, Maine, Ohio and Texas. In the House of Representatives, 218 seats are needed to gain control of the chamber, the exact number held by the Republicans.
