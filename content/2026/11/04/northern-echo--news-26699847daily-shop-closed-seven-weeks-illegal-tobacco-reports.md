A Darlington shop has been shut down following a trading standards sting after reports it was selling illegal cigarettes.

Peterlee Magistrates’ Court granted a seven-week premises closure order for the Daily Shop, at 98 North Road, last week.

The council said the order followed reports from the public of suspected criminal activity linked to the sale of illegal cigarettes and tobacco, as well as a failed test purchase.

An inspection also uncovered counterfeit products at the premises, according to the council.

The Daily Shop will remain closed for seven weeks.

The court also granted a three-month extension to an existing closure order at Vape City, on Bondgate, which was shut down in July.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21671677.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The courts also granted a three-month extension to the closure order already in place for Vape City on Bondgate. *(Image: DARLINGTON BOROUGH COUNCIL)*

Darlington Borough Council said it takes the illegal sale of cigarettes, vapes and other tobacco products “very seriously”.

A council spokesperson said: “This type of criminal activity is often linked with anti-social behaviour and nuisance, helps fund organised crime and goes hand-in-hand with drug dealing, child exploitation and money laundering.

“It also harms public health by encouraging smoking and targeting young people with cheap products.

“Our focus is on using all available legal powers to tackle illegal tobacco sales, protect legitimate businesses and keep our communities safe.”

The council said its work includes test purchases and inspections, seizures, closure orders and prosecutions.

Residents can report suspected illegal tobacco sales and other illegal activity to [tradingstandards@darlington.gov.uk.](mailto:tradingstandards@darlington.gov.uk.)
