A cabaret-style evening of big ideas and bold conversation is returning to Harrogate this November.

Raworth's Salon North returns to the Crown Hotel on November 12, promising to challenge assumptions and ignite curiosity.

The event features three 30-minute talks designed for the audience to catch up with friends and soak up the atmosphere.

Simon Morris, managing partner at Raworths, said: "Salon North is a brilliant night out.

"It’s entertaining, thought-provoking and you always leave with a new perspective on life and feeling a little bit more enlightened."

Organised by Harrogate International Festivals, the November edition will explore topics including perception, consciousness, danger in the arts, and the enduring fascination with ancient Egypt.

Dr Daisy Dixon, a prize-winning philosopher, will examine why certain artworks shock or unsettle audiences.

Professor Ben Alderson-Day will discuss the science of hallucinations, sleep paralysis, and the feeling of a mysterious presence.

Dr Campbell Price will offer insights into the world of ancient Egyptian gods, tombs, and treasures.

Sharon Canavar, chief executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said: "Salon is all about making interesting and sometimes complex ideas accessible in a relaxed and welcoming setting."

Salon North was named one of GQ magazine’s 'Top 100 Things to do in the World.'
