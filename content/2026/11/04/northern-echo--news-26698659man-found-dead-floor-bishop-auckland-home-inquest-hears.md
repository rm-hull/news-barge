A pensioner was found dead on his living-room floor after neighbours had not seen him for 'several' days.

Brian Morris, 83, was found lying on the living room floor at his home on Pasture Row, Eldon on September 26.

An inquest was opened into the Shildon-born man's deathon Tuesday (November 3) and heard neighbours had not seen Mr Morris for several days before he was found.

He was formally identified through dental records.

A post-mortem examination was carried out by pathologist Dr Henry at Newcastle's Freeman Hospital mortuary but his cause of death was "unascertained".

The inquest was told the available pathology was limited, and Dr Henry would be asked to attend a further hearing to help establish how Mr Morris died.

Area Coroner Crispin Oliver adjourned the inquest until December 10 at 2pm.
