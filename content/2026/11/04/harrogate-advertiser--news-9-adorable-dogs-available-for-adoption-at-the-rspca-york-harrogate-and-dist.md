We take a look at nine dogs that are currently looking for their forever home at the RSPCA York, Harrogate and District branch…

Newt is a two-year-old Large Crossbreed who is a sweet but shy boy that came to the centre via an inspector. Newt was rescued from appalling conditions after he was found living in a squalid property along with a few other dogs. He has come such a long way from the traumatised dog that arrived in the van. The staff have even had to teach him how to play as he was even scared of toys when he first arrived. Newt will need understanding adopters who will give him the time and patience he needs to settle in to a home. He loves to lean in to his people and get cuddles which helps him feel secure. | RSPCA

Dave is a five-year-old Greyhound who came to the centre after his racing days came to an end. Dave is a sweet and elegant ex-racing greyhound ready to trade the track for a cosy retirement. Whilst he can run fast when he gets the zoomies, he is much better known as a professional 18-hour-a-day couch potato. He is gentle and affectionate and looking for a calm, patient family to show him what home life is all about. One of Dave's most favourite things in the world is sharing a sofa with his people getting lots of TLC. | RSPCA

Snoopy is a six-year-old Sharpei Cross who is a sweet boy that came to the centre via a dog warden as an unclaimed stray. Snoopy is a lovely boy who has settled well at the centre after being a little scared initially. He has quickly come out of his shell and has proved to be a lovely natured boy. Snoopy walks well on the lead and is very well behaved when out and about. Snoopy loves his food and cuddles. He can also be a little independent and take himself off for some time out when he feels like some quiet time. Snoopy is a great little dog who will make a fantastic addition to the family. | RSPCA

Sandy is a four-year-old Staffy Cross Collie who is a really sweet natured girl that came to the centre via inspectors after she was locked in a room and left on her own with another dog for two days straight with no water. Sandy is exceptionally friendly with everyone she meets. She is a very active girl who will need equally active adopters who will take her on lots of exciting walks and adventures. Sandy is a very happy go lucky kind of girl who loves to live life to the max. | RSPCA

Diesel is a one-year-old Staffordshire Bull Terrier who is a smashing boy that came to the centre via an inspector after he was found living in terrible conditions. Diesel is such a brave little dog and despite his rough start in life, his resilience shines through and his determination to live life to the fullest is very clear to see. Diesel loves his people and is now learning what it feels like to be safe and loved and will not let his past define him. Diesel is an extremely active lad and loves to live life to the max. He is a very loyal dog who will love you with his whole heart. | RSPCA

Mufasa is a 13-year-old German Shepherd who is a kind, gentle soul with a heart of gold. Mufasa has been in RSPCA care for a long time now due to an ongoing investigation. Court proceedings have now concluded and Mufasa can now find his very own forever home. He is a distinguished senior gentleman looking for a quiet place to call his forever home. He may have a little grey on his muzzle and a slower pace in his step, but his heart is as big as ever. He may not have lots of years left to give you, but he promises to give you every single ounce of the love he has been saving up. He is as loyal as they come and will be your best friend and loyal companion if you give him that chance. | RSPCA

Emmet is a six-year-old Greyhound who came to the centre as his racing days are over and he is now ready to find his very own family. Due to spending all his life in racing kennels, Emmet can be a little nervous in new and unfamiliar situations and surroundings. He is now getting used to life outside of racing and learning that his new life is full of love and fun times. He can be a little shy when meeting people for the first time but once he knows he is safe with you, his goofy, affectionate side comes right out. He will love you with his whole big heart and be your best friend. | RSPCA

Kaizer is a 15-month-old German Shepherd who is a very nervous boy that has had a difficult start to life. Rescued from appalling conditions, he arrived at the centre extremely thin, dirty and in generally poor condition. He has clearly not had the easiest beginning, and now it is time for him to discover just how wonderful life can be. Kaizer will need very special adopters who are patient, understanding and willing to give him the time and reassurance he needs to build his confidence. His new family will play an important role in helping him overcome his insecurities, learn to feel safe and settle into a home environment. Most importantly, they will need to show him the joys of life and what it truly means to be part of a loving family. | RSPCA

**2**/ 3

Teddy is a two-year-old Border Collie Cross who came into the care of the RSPCA following an inspector's visit, having been found living in terrible conditions. He was painfully thin, his coat was filthy and he had completely shut down, understandably unsure of the world around him. But with time, patience and plenty of love and TLC, Teddy has slowly blossomed. His beautiful personality has begun to shine through, and now this special boy is ready to take his next big step – finding a loving forever home of his own. | RSPCA
