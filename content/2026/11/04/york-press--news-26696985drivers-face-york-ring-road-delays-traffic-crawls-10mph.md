## Delays on York ring road as traffic crawls at 10mph

Traffic is building on the York ring road *(Image: Contributed)*

UPDATE: Traffic has returned to usual after this morning's rush-hour congestion.

Motorists were experiencing delays on the York ring road this morning.

Traffic was slow on the A1237 York ring road.

**Recommended reads:**

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21667982.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Traffic is slow on the York ring road this morning. *(Image: Newsquest)*

Delays were reportedly six minutes and increasing eastbound between the A1237 and Haxby Road, with an average speed of 10mph.

On the A59, westbound between Wheatlands Grove and A1237, there were also delays with traffic moving at an average speed of 10 mph.

Meanwhile, Main Street, heading northbound between the A19 and A19 Fulford Road, was causing hold-ups with average speeds of 15 mph and estimated delays of 11 minutes and delays.
