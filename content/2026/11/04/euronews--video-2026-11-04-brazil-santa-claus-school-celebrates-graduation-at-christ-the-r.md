Christmas came early to Rio de Janeiro on Tuesday, when **55 graduates of the Escola de Papai Noel do Brasil** gathered beneath Christ the Redeemer to mark the end of their training. Dressed in red suits and white beards, the**new Santas** sang carols as they rode the Corcovado mountain railway to the statue, then climbed the final steps for a ceremony that included a**blessing and the presentation of diplomas**.

The **free course**, open to men aged 50 and over with naturally white or grey hair and beards, runs for a month of Tuesday afternoon classes covering** acting, improvisation, diction, posture, make-up, costume and beard care, plus Brazilian Sign Language**. It was founded in 1993 by the actors Limachem Cherem and Conrado Freitas, with the costume designer Fátima Tavares Cherem, in the basement of Cherem's house in Vila Isabel.** More than 1,000 Santas have passed through the school in 33 years**, along with about 300 Noeletes, its female equivalent. Many graduates are** retirees earning extra income** at shopping centres, hospitals and parties; some, such as the lorry driver Marinezio Teixeira de Oliveira, come back for a second certificate.
