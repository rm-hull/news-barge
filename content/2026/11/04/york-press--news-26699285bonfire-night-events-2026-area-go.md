Here are some of the best bonfire night events in North Yorkshire.

Monk Park Farm Bonfire Night

Monk Park Farm in Thirsk is hosting a bonfire and low-bang fireworks night on November 5.

The event will feature a bonfire, a low-bang firework display set to Disney music, carnival games, marshmallow toasting, street food, and an animal barn.

There will also be an on-site bar for adults.

Sandburn Hall Bonfire Night

Sandburn Hall in York is hosting a bonfire night event on November 4.

The event will include a firework display set to music, food and drink vendors, fire performers, and amusements.

The gates will open at 5pm and the fireworks will start at 7pm.

Sky Magic Driffield

Sky Magic Driffield will host a professional firework display on November 5.

The event will also feature live entertainment, a traditional funfair, food and drink stalls, a licensed bar, and free face painting for kids.
