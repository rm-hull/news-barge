A CHARITY shop is offering festive bargains to help raise funds for a good cause.

Age UK’s Bingley shop is encouraging shoppers to choose sustainable gifts and pre-loved treasures this Christmas, with each purchase helping to support vital services for older people.

The shop stocks a range of festive items including toys, books, homeware, decorations, and partywear, all aimed at bringing joy to loved ones while supporting those experiencing loneliness during the holidays.

A spokesperson for Age UK said: "Every item purchased from Age UK shops helps fund vital services, including the Charity’s Telephone Friendship Service, free and confidential Advice Line, and The Silver Line Helpline, which offer friendship, support and advice to those who are struggling during the festive season and beyond."

The charity is also appealing for donations of quality, unwanted items, and reminds supporters that donations can be boosted by 25 per cent at no extra cost by signing up to Gift Aid.

Shopping at Age UK stores not only supports the charity’s work but also promotes sustainability by giving pre-loved items a new lease of life, reducing waste during the festive season.

Age UK operates more than 270 shops across England and Wales, with additional gift options available through its eBay store.
