JD Wetherspoon has said it plans to open up to 25 bars over the year ahead as it reported a jump in first-quarter sales - including a new bar in Edinburgh.

The budget pub chain reported an 8.2% rise in like-for-like sales in the 14 weeks to November 1, with bar sales leading the way with a 9.4% jump and food up 6.8%.

But sales growth eased back in the most recent five weeks, to 7.5% against the 8.6% surge seen in the first nine weeks of the quarter.

The new pub in Edinburgh, called The William Chambers, will take over the spot vacated by Revolution.

Wetherspoon bought up the property on Chambers Street last year after the cocktail venue suddenly shut in summer 2024.

According to the budget pub chain's website, the new venue will open its doors to the public on Tuesday, January 26.

Licensing board papers indicate that the capacity of the pub will have a capacity for 485 persons, who will be located across the basement and ground floors.

The papers say the venue will be ‘completely refigured’ to create a ‘traditional JD Wetherspoon style of operation’.

Otherwise, the building – once part of the Edinburgh Dental Hospital and School – will remain unmodified.

The new venue sits immediately next to Edinburgh Sheriff Court and Edinburgh Justice of the Peace Court.
