A CHURCH has created a display of more than 1,000 knitted poppies.

Allerton Congregational Church has installed the tribute for Remembrance Day and will keep it on display throughout November.

The poppies were knitted by congregation members, who have been working on the project throughout the year.

A Remembrance Day service will be held on Sunday, November 8, led by Mr Kershaw.

The service will begin at 10.30am, with the final hymn sung outside on the terrace.

All are welcome to attend, and refreshments will be served in the hall afterwards.

The display is located at the front of the church on Allerton Road.
