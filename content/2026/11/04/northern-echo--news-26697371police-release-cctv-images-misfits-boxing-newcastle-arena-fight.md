New images have been released of eight people police want to speak to after a fight broke out during a boxing event.

Police were called to the Misfits Boxing event at the Utilita Arena in Newcastle on Saturday, September 12 to reports of an altercation involving a group of people attending the event.

It sparked widespread attention after videos from inside the arena went viral on social media, showing security staff attempting to intervene.

Seven people were initially arrested.

Officers have now released pictures of several people they want to speak to.

A Northumbria Police spokesperson said: "They were in the area at the time and could have information which may assist officers in their investigation."

Alternatively, people can call 101 and quote reference number 26000448543.
