Nearly a year after the establishment of the special police unit “Ferrum”, shots are still being fired regularly in Berlin. The unit will therefore continue operating “until further notice”, a police spokesperson said in response to an enquiry. The *Tagesspiegel* had previously reported on the decision.

“Ferrum” (Latin for “iron”) was established at the State Criminal Police Office (LKA) on November 13, 2025, and was intended to operate for just a few months. Given the high level of gun violence in the capital, the unit is still considered. According to police, the unit has around 110 officers. Additional personnel are brought in for operations such as searches.

By October, more than 720 criminal investigations had been opened since “Ferrum” was established, according to police. Of these, 483 cases had direct or indirect links to investigations involving firearms-related crime.

### More Than 300 Searches and Nearly 290 Arrests

According to the figures, police have arrested almost 290 people in connection with these investigations, while more than 5,200 have temporarily had their freedom of movement restricted. A total of 80 arrest warrants have been issued so far, police said. Of the suspects, 51 are currently being held in pre-trial detention.

Police have so far carried out more than 300 searches, inspected 4,120 vehicles and checked 886 establishments. According to police, 73 functional firearms and around 3,400 rounds of live ammunition have been seized.

Police believe the investigations so far have helped to “shed light on criminal structures, identify and detain suspects. Law enforcement pressure on those involved has also increased significantly,” police said. Extortion is a major focus of Ferrum’s work.

### Gun Violence in Berlin Has Escalated Dramatically

The Berlin Public Prosecutor’s Office has a dedicated investigative unit, “Telum” (Latin for “offensive weapon”), which handles these cases. It was established in response to the creation of the police unit to strengthen efforts to combat organised crime.

Gun violence in Berlin has escalated dramatically in recent years. According to police figures, a total of 585 offences involving firearms were recorded in the first half of 2026. These included 324 threats involving firearms and 261 incidents in which shots were fired.

In 2025, police recorded 1,119 cases involving firearms. These also include threats in which no shots were fired. According to the authorities, this represented an increase of 68 percent compared with 2024.
