Autumn has arrived across County Durham, bringing with it golden woodland, crisp riverside paths and some of the region's most spectacular scenery.

For those looking to make the most of the season, few locations offer a more striking backdrop than Lumley Castle.

Rising above the River Wear on the outskirts of Chester-le-Street, the historic castle is surrounded by mature trees that burst into shades of amber, copper and gold each autumn, creating a picture-perfect setting for a seasonal stroll.

The landmark, which dates back to the 14th century, is one of County Durham's most recognisable buildings and provides a focal point for walkers exploring the network of paths around the castle grounds and nearby riverside.

As temperatures cool and leaves begin to fall, the area takes on a distinctly autumnal feel.

Tree-lined routes alongside the River Wear offer peaceful walks through colourful woodland, while regular glimpses of the castle through the foliage provide some of the best photo opportunities in the county.

The changing season transforms the landscape around the castle, with carpets of fallen leaves covering the ground and reflections of autumn colours shimmering on the water below.

Walkers can enjoy gentle routes suitable for families, couples and dog owners, with much of the surrounding terrain proving accessible and easy to navigate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21666779.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Autumn decorations bring seasonal warmth to the historic interior of Lumley Castle *(Image: Lumley Castle and Phil Reilly Media)*

The nearby riverside setting also attracts a variety of wildlife, with birds frequently spotted along the banks and squirrels busy preparing for winter among the trees.

For photography enthusiasts, autumn is arguably the best time to visit.

The contrast between Lumley Castle's historic stone walls and the vibrant colours of the surrounding woodland creates dramatic scenes from almost every angle.

Built in 1388 by Sir Ralph Lumley, the castle has stood watch over the Wear Valley for more than 600 years.

Today it serves as a luxury hotel, restaurant and events venue while remaining one of the North East's most iconic landmarks.

Visitors looking to warm up after a walk can also take advantage of an autumn offer currently running in the castle's Library Bar.

Guests can enjoy a hot drink alongside a slice of cake or a freshly baked scone for £6 between 10am and 2pm each day, making for a cosy way to round off a seasonal visit.

Whether you're hoping to capture autumn colours on camera, enjoy a leisurely riverside walk or simply spend an afternoon outdoors, Lumley Castle provides a reminder of why this is one of the most beautiful times of year to explore County Durham.
