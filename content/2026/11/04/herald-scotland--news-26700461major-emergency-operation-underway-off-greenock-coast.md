Emergency services launched a major search off Greenock on Wednesday evening after concerns were raised about a potential person in the water.

The search involved Helensburgh RNLI Lifeboat, Greenock and Largs Coastguard Rescue Teams, a Coastguard helicopter and four vessels, who responded to a broadcast from HM Coastguard.

CalMac confirmed that the MV Argyll was diverted to assist HM Coastguard, with services operated by the vessel disrupted as a result.

Police and other emergency services were seen patrolling the area from Greenock's Battery Park to Cove Road, heading towards the pierhead.

A large area was also cordoned off by officers.

In a statement issued late on Wednesday evening, HM Coastguard confirmed that the search had been suspended, after no person was found.

A spokesperson for HM Coastguard told The Herald: "A search has been conducted this evening off Greenock for a potential person in the water.

"This has involved Helensburgh RNLI Lifeboat, Greenock and Largs Coastguard Rescue Teams, a Coastguard helicopter and 4 vessels who responded to a broadcast from HM Coastguard.

"The search has now been suspended with no person found in the water."
