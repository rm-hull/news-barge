A woman has admitted perverting the course of justice by disposing of a firearm after a murder investigation was launched.

Peter Phillips was shot dead at about 11pm on Saturday, May 23, on Ayton Crescent in Eston, Middlesbrough.

Abigail Beatty admitted the charge at Teesside Crown Court.

Tony Gaffney and Jonathan Spencer are charged with murder, while Rachael Britton is charged with perverting the course of justice over the shooting of the 47-year-old.

The three defendants will stand trial on January 25 next year.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20976304.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police on Ayton Crescent *(Image: Chris Booth/The Northern Echo)*

The court ordered a pre-sentence report for Beatty, of Bevanlee Road, South Bank, before she is sentenced in the new year.

Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, said: “You have pleaded guilty to a serious offence.

“Your sentence will not take place until the conclusion of the trial of the other defendants.

“In the meantime you will be remanded in custody.”

Gaffney, 39, of Ambrose Road, and Spencer, 39, of Normanby Road, both in Middlesbrough, were remanded in custody.

Britton, of Simpson Close, South Bank, denied the charge of perverting the course of justice. The 33-year-old is accused of disposing of a firearm, a motorbike and a helmet.

The murder charge against Annelise McQuilling was dropped in August after prosecutors offered no evidence against her.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20976303.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Floral tributes on Ayton Crescent, Eston *(Image: Stuart Boulton/The Northern Echo)*

Peter Phillips’s family paid tribute to him as the “heart and soul” of their family after his death.

They said: "Peter had a big family who all loved him, he was the heart and soul of our family. We are devastated at his loss and feel we have been robbed of him.

Emergency services were called to Ayton Crescent on Saturday, but Peter was pronounced dead at the scene.

Floral tributes were left at the scene. One read: "You will always be with me," while another said: "I will love you forever."

Earlier in the investigation, five people were arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender and bailed pending further enquiries.
