A "deeply loved" grandad died after suffering fatal head and chest injuries in a "traumatic" crash with a tractor, an inquest heard.

Andrew Lowther died when the van he was a passenger in crashed with a tractor in Crane Lane Row, Hamsterley, on September 23.

Andrew, from the Butterknowle area, was pronounce dead at the scene.

An inquest was opened into the 60-year-old's death at Crook Coroners Court on Tuesday (November 3).

Coroners' officer Stephanie Clough told the brief hearing that a post-mortem examination had been carried out at the RVI in Newcastle and pathologist Dr Sam Hoggard concluded Andrew's cause of death was head and chest injuries.

The inquest heard Andrew was identified to officers by a friend at the scene and that an investigation remains ongoing.

Area Coroner Crispin Oliver described the death as "traumatic" and adjourned the inquest for a brief hearing on February 15 while police produce a crash investigation report.

They said: “It is with the heaviest of hearts that the family of Andrew Lowther would like to let friends, family and loved ones, both near and far, know of the tragic loss of Andrew.

“There are simply no words to describe the pain of losing someone so deeply loved. Andrew was a devoted husband, dad, grandad, son, brother, uncle and friend, and he meant so much to so many people.

“Our hearts are broken, and we are trying to come to terms with the devastating loss of such a special person. Andrew leaves behind so many precious memories that we will carry with us forever.

“As a family, we would like to say a heartfelt thank you to everyone who has reached out to us with messages, calls, flowers, cards and kind words. Every single message and gesture of support has meant so much to us during this heartbreaking time.
