A new independent cafe and bistro has opened in High Harrogate.

LeLi’s Deli Bar/Bistro is now serving customers at 23 Regent Parade, offering breakfast, hot and cold sandwiches, coffee and cakes to eat in or take away.

The kitchen is led by Sicilian chef Leonardo, who brings 38 years of experience as a head chef.

He previously worked at the Deli Bar Cafe, which occupied the same premises.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aeb4d6975653e0d3ca.png)

The new business opened on Friday (30th October) under new ownership.

The opening brings the premises back into use following the closure of the Deli Bar Cafe in September.

The former cafe had served the neighbourhood since 2006.
