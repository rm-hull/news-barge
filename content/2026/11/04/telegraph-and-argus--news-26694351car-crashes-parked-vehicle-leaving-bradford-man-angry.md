A Bradford man has been left angered after a car smashed into his wife's vehicle - leaving it badly damaged.

The incident took place on Carr Bottom Road in Wibsey at around 7.55am on Monday morning.

Gareth Talbot told the T&A the vehicle hit his wife’s car, reversed and then drove away.

West Yorkshire Police is investigating the incident.

## 'We're not happy'

Mr Talbot said: "It's not on.

"The car headlight is broken and the bumper is cracked.

"We're not happy. We do not have the money to spend fixing our car."

Mr Talbot said he fears his partner’s insurance costs will rise because she is a new driver making a claim.

He added that the damage could cost up to £600 to repair.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "It was a damage-only crash on Carr Bottom Road reported at 2.50pm."
