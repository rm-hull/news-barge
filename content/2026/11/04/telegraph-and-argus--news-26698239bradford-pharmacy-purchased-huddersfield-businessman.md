A Bradford pharmacy has been sold to a first-time buyer.

Rockwell Pharmacy Plus Health operates from the ground floor of the Rockwell Medical Centre in Thorpe Edge, around three miles north of the city centre.

The pharmacy dispenses an average of 4,602 items per month and offers services including Pharmacy First, New Medicine Service, contraceptive services, flu and COVID vaccinations, and weight management.

The sale was managed by specialist business property adviser Christie & Co.

Tom Young, senior business agent – pharmacy at Christie & Co, said: "The sale of Rockwell Pharmacy Plus Health demonstrates the strength of demand for quality pharmacy businesses across West Yorkshire.

"Following extensive interest from the market, we received multiple offers at or above the guide price.

"It has been a pleasure to help Pharmacy Plus Health with their strategic business planning, and I wish Fazal every success with the business moving forward."

The pharmacy was previously owned by Pharmacy Plus Health, which operates 30 regional pharmacies.

It was sold as part of a strategic divestment.

Rockwell Pharmacy Plus Health has now been purchased by Fazal Abbas of Pharmcare Plus Ltd, a Huddersfield-based first-time buyer.

The sale price has not been disclosed.
