Humberside Police have announced temporary road closures in Goole town centre for the Remembrance Sunday parade.

Pasture Road, around the Charter Club assembly area, and Boothferry Road, near the Cenotaph Memorial Gardens, will be closed between 10.15am and 11.45am on Sunday, 8th November.

The streets will be reopened in stages by East Riding Council once the parade has passed.

Residents have been warned about temporary "no parking" rules along the parade route, with vehicles needing to be moved before 10.15am to avoid being towed or ticketed.

Motorists are advised to avoid the town centre during these times and use Airmyn Road to bypass the disruptions.

A police spokesperson said: "Thank you for your cooperation and patience as we support our community in honouring Remembrance Sunday safely."

The closures are to ensure the safety of those taking part and attending the service.
