Firefighters were called to a North Yorkshire property after reports of water leaking through a ceiling.

A crew from Easingwold arrived at the scene in Market Place, Easingwold, at 8.35pm yesterday, Tuesday, November 3, and isolated the water, offering advice to the homeowner.

Elsewhere, a series of incidents kept fire crews occupied, with a smouldering bin on James Street, Harrogate, extinguished by a hose reel jet at 5.45pm.

The fire, caused by a discarded cigarette, was quickly put out by the Harrogate team.

Around the same time, a bin in Station Road, Selby, was reported to be on fire.

However, upon arrival, the Selby crew found the fire was already out, and no further action was required.

In York, the Acomb crew was called to Ascot Way at 6.11pm to assist a carer in gaining access to a property. They helped obtain the key from a key box.

Later, at 6.24pm, the same crew attended a controlled burn in an alleyway on Compton Street.

Due to the location, they requested for it to be extinguished.

Danby crews assisted paramedics in safely lifting a patient onto an ambulance at 3.17am on West Lane, Danby.
