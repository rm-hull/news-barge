*Jaja Finance is a Business Reporter client*

Major consumer brands want stronger customer loyalty, and a credit card is one of the most effective ways to build it. Today’s customers expect clarity, control and rewards that work for them, delivered as smoothly as every other digital service in their lives.

But credit has a reputation for complexity. Jaja Finance (“Jaja”) aims to design that complexity out, making credit simple, fair, and easier to understand and manage.

Jaja issues its own-brand credit card, Vanta, and delivers co-branded cards for leading UK brands, including Asda. The company is building AI-enabled consumer credit and believes clarity, technology and the right incentives sit at the heart of modern credit.

Jaja has a mission to make credit clearer, easier to manage and safer to use, whether you’re a cardholder or a brand looking to launch a card of your own. As an AI-enabled consumer-finance platform, Jaja combines its regulated lending capability with proprietary credit, data and decisioning expertise to build and manage innovative credit products for customers and partners.

Technology sits at the centre of this because Jaja combines modern technology, data and agile product development to launch new propositions quickly, adapt to changing customer needs and scale through strategic partnerships. Data-driven personalisation, real-time decision-making and tailored customer journeys, with responsible lending built into each, mean that credit products are shaped around how customers actually behave and what they need.

For cardholders, that shows up in the app. Spending Insights breaks down where their money is going, budgeting tools help them plan ahead and AI-enabled tools support customer service, helping customers get faster answers while allowing colleagues to focus on more complex needs. It’s the kind of experience most high-street lenders don’t yet offer.

Rewards are another area where Jaja does things differently. Many financial products do best when customers spend more. Jaja takes a different view. Programmes that reward spending alone always encourage more of it, whereas rewarding more financially responsible behaviour changes the incentive and, over time, leads to healthier credit use. Launch to Rewards and Asda Rewards put that principle into practice, offering personalised rewards aligned to customers’ financial goals and using rewards as a tool for alignment, not just promotion.

Jaja sees no conflict between doing right by customers and running a profitable business. Disciplined credit risk management, intelligent use of data and a long-term approach to customer relationships underpin its focus on sustainable growth, while partner brands benefit from being associated with a responsible lender.

That matters to the brands Jaja works with. More brands now see financial products as a way to strengthen customer relationships and open up new revenue, but few want to build the capability or take on the regulatory complexity themselves. Jaja brings together regulated lending, credit and data expertise, technology and strategic partnerships to deliver the proposition, while the card carries the partner’s name.

Over the next five years, Jaja expects people to demand more from credit: fairer terms, clearer information, wider access and real help with their finances. AI and real-time personalisation are how lenders will meet those expectations, and Jaja plans to be one of the companies setting the pace.
