British comedian Ricky Gervais is bringing his new stand-up show to York and Leeds as part of a worldwide tour.

The comedian will take to the stage in York on Monday, February 15, followed by a date at Leeds first direct bank arena on June 24, 2027, at 7pm, with doors opening at 6pm.

The shows are part of a major international run for the comedian, whose tour began in September 2026 and is scheduled to continue around the world until the end of 2027.

Gervais, who is known for creating and starring in The Office, Extras, and After Life, continues to be one of the most commercially successful live performers in comedy.

The show, titled Legend, is Gervais's "most personal" yet, according to the comedian himself.

He promises a night of his typical sharp, confrontational stand-up.

The show follows his previous specials Humanity, Supernature, Armageddon, and Mortality.

Netflix has already acquired Legend, making it Gervais's fifth stand-up special for the streaming platform.

The special is expected to be filmed at the conclusion of the tour.

Both Armageddon and Mortality have won Golden Globes, underlining the international reach of his recent stand-up work.

The newly-announced European leg includes arena dates in Antwerp, Barcelona, Lisbon, Vienna, Paris, Zagreb, Amsterdam, Zurich, and Stockholm, with further UK and North American dates expected to be added.

Tickets for the York Barbican show went on sale on Friday, October 30 and are available through the venue's website.

Tickets for the Leeds performance are available through AXS, the venue's official ticketing partner.

Standard age restrictions apply, and anyone under 14 must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or over.
