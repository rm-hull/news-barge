The North Yorkshire Green Party has set out its priorities for Harrogate and the wider area, highlighting the cost of living, affordable housing, support for local businesses and funding for public services.

Arnold Warneken, chair of the North Yorkshire Green Party, outlined the party’s position, calling for greater fairness for households, businesses and local communities.

## North Yorkshire Green Party calls for action on cost of living

The party said work should provide people with a decent standard of living, rather than simply enough to survive.

It said people should be able to afford their homes, essential bills and opportunities for family life, leisure and rest.

The Green Party called for action on housing, energy and transport costs, including better support for rural public transport.

## Affordable housing and protection for tenants

On housing, the party said it wanted genuinely affordable, energy-efficient homes, with appropriate infrastructure and protection against excessive rent increases.

It said landlords should receive fair returns, but tenants should not face exploitation.

The party also supports exploring government-backed low or zero-deposit mortgages for first-time buyers with proven rental payment histories, subject to affordability safeguards.

It said housing development should meet community needs while protecting valuable green spaces.

## Support for Harrogate’s independent businesses

The North Yorkshire Green Party described independent shops, hospitality businesses and local employers as essential to Harrogate’s prosperity.

It called for fairer business rates, effective taxation of large multinational corporations and support for smaller businesses facing rising energy and employment costs.

The party also said major infrastructure projects should properly consider their impact on local businesses.

## North Yorkshire Council funding and local services

The party called on central government to provide North Yorkshire Council with fair, sustainable funding that reflects the costs of delivering services across a large rural county.

It said social care, education, healthcare and support for vulnerable residents should be protected.

The Green Party also called for transparency, accountability and value for public money, rather than councils continually relying on higher council tax or service reductions.

## Green Party’s priorities for the area

> **Arnold Warneken, Chair of the North Yorkshire Green Party, said:**
>
> We believe government’s role is to create opportunities for people and communities to prosper, while ensuring those who cannot support themselves receive the care and dignity they deserve.

The party said prosperity, fairness and environmental responsibility must work together.

It added that government should be judged not simply by what it spends, but by the lives it improves.
