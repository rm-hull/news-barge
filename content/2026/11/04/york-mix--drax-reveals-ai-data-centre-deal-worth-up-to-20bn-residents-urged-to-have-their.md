North Yorkshire power generation firm Drax has struck a partnership deal with Brookfield and AI business Radiant to develop a data centre at its power station.

Global asset management company Brookfield said it will initially invest £2.5 billion in the project through its joint venture with Qatar’s Qai, but said total investments could ultimately exceed £20 billion.

Today, Drax launched a consultation with local people over the development.

Drax first announced proposals to repurpose existing infrastructure at its power station to develop a data centre to power artificial intelligence late last year.

The power business has now confirmed it had struck the agreement with Brookfield and Radiant to launch the first phase of the data centre project.

The group initially hopes to have a roughly 100MW (megawatt) centre operating from the first quarter of 2028, depending on planning and regulatory approvals.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/drax-ai-centre-plan.webp)

It wants to ramp this capacity up to 500MW between 2028 and 2031, and to develop further in the following years.

The data centre would be located just off the A645 at the south end of the power station site.

## ‘Protect hundreds of jobs’

In a statement, Drax said: “Data centres are critical national infrastructure, providing the AI capabilities needed to discover new medicines, advance scientific research and support essential public services, and Drax Power Station has the land, power and infrastructure that can support the UK’s ambition to become a leader in AI.”

**Drax Group chief executive Will Gardiner** said: “We are proud to be partnering with leading AI infrastructure providers on this project which can help secure the long-term future of the site and protect hundreds of jobs, maintain a critical source of this country’s energy security, support northern growth and enable us to continue delivering for our local communities.

“This country has a unique opportunity to bring together AI leadership, energy infrastructure and industrial renewal to drive national growth.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/phone-chatgpt.webp)

“If constructed in full, this project could become one of the largest data centres in the UK, attracting billions in inward investment, supporting the North’s reindustrialisation and strengthening sovereign AI capability.”

The draft masterplan shows the proposals for the data centre, which includes:

* A data hall houses the advanced computer equipment and cooling equipment
* A plant building, which houses electrical equipment
* A logistics/office building with supporting infrastructure.

The tallest structure would be about 27m high, less than a quarter the height of the cooling towers.

**Richard Gwilliam, future of Drax Power Station director**, said: “We are now calling on local residents and businesses to help shape our plans.

“The public consultation is an opportunity to find out more about our proposals, ask questions and share feedback before we submit our planning application. We encourage everyone with an interest to take part.

“The project would bring new investment, jobs and supply chain opportunities to support Selby’s economic future. It would also lay the foundations for further data centre projects, helping to secure the power station’s future and protect existing jobs across Selby.

#### Drax location

“AI has enormous potential to improve our lives, from accelerating medical research to improving public services and helping businesses become more productive. Drax is well placed to support this growth.”
