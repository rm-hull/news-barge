A convicted thief has been banned from entering several stores under a Criminal Behaviour Order.

Tyrone Letremy, 42, was sentenced on Wednesday at Leeds Magistrates’ Court for multiple theft from shop offences and has now been handed a three-year CBO covering the Kirklees area.

The order prohibits him from entering any Co-op or Aldi stores, including their car parks, within Kirklees.

He is also banned from the B&M store and car park at Blakeridge Mill shopping and Boots in Dewsbury’s Princess of Wales precinct.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Failing to comply with the terms of the order is considered a criminal offence for which Tyrone Letremy could receive a fine or even a prison sentence."
