A York hotel will be hosting its annual Christmas light switch-on next week, and is inviting the community to come along and celebrate the start of the festive season.

All are welcome at the free event held at the Grand Hotel, on Station Rise, from 5pm on **Friday 13 November**. The lights are set to go on at 5.30pm.

There’s also festive entertainment, seasonal treats and opportunities to support good causes.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-grand-york-christmas-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-grand-york-christmas.webp)

Adding to the festive atmosphere, Yorkshire Voices will perform a selection of festive favourites throughout the evening.

Visitors will also be able to enjoy a selection of festive food and drink, including loaded doughnuts, warming mulled wine, hot chocolate and crumble.

And putting the spirit of giving at the heart of the celebrations, the event will feature a Grand Raffle in support of Cash for Kids, helping to raise vital funds for the charity and support its work in the local community.

The Grand, York will also act as a designated Christmas gift drop-off point for the charity, giving visitors the opportunity to donate presents to those who may need a little extra support this Christmas. Visitors can pick up a gift tag at the event and purchase a present to donate or bring along a gift they have already bought.

The Christmas light switch-on offers York residents and visitors the chance to celebrate the start of the Christmas season and support a meaningful cause.
