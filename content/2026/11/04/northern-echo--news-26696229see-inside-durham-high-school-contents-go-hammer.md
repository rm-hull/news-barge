Members of the public were given one final look inside Durham High School today as the former school's furniture, equipment and memorabilia went under the hammer.

A video from the open day shows rows of items up for grabs, including ping pong tables, a gazebo and a piano, as prospective buyers browsed the contents of the historic site.

The online auction features hundreds of lots from across the school, ranging from classroom furniture and IT equipment to science apparatus, catering facilities, theatre props and musical instruments.

![One of the items on offer.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21667057.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the items on offer. *(Image: SANDERSON WEATHERALL)*

Among the items listed for sale are Samsung 85-inch smart TVs, Promethean interactive boards, Dell desktop computers, sports equipment, nursery furniture, catering machinery and grounds maintenance equipment.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21667058.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the items on offer. *(Image: SANDERSON WEATHERALL)*

Some of the most valuable lots include musical instruments, such as a K.Kawai GX2 Baby Grand Piano, Yamaha Clavinova digital pianos, keyboards and a range of brass, string and percussion instruments.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21667036.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A picture from inside the open day for the Durham High School auction. *(Image: PROVIDED)*

The auction also includes artefacts linked to the school's long history, including a Holy Bible inscribed in 1925 and wooden altars dating from 1921 and 1989.

The Northern Echo understands that some parents of former pupils are also attempting to collect leftover books and school uniforms with the aim of donating them to a school in Africa, ensuring some of the school's resources continue to benefit young people after the site's closure.

Potential buyers were given the chance to view the lots in person at Durham High School's Farewell Hall site on South Road.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21133366.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The last day at Durham High School. *(Image: STUART BOULTON)*

Online bidding is due to close at 11am on Wednesday, November 4.

The sale marks another significant moment in the winding down of Durham High School, which announced earlier this year that it would close after more than 140 years of education in the city.

In July, owners Galaxy Global Education confirmed the school would shut at the end of the summer term, leaving 281 pupils and staff facing an uncertain future.

At the time, school leaders described the decision as a "terribly sad chapter" in the institution's history.

In a statement to parents, the school said: "Today, we write the last chapter in this incredible history.

"It is a terribly sad chapter as our owners are closing the school at the end of this term.

"To the 281 current pupils, we are deeply sorry you will not have the chance to reach the end of your journey in this truly special school."

The closure followed weeks of uncertainty for families, many of whom had begun seeking alternative school places amid fears the school would not reopen after the summer holidays.

Galaxy Global Education blamed falling pupil numbers, rising employment costs and VAT on private school fees, saying the school was "no longer financially sustainable".

Days after the closure was announced, it also emerged that headteacher Michelle Hill had been the subject of an internal misconduct investigation in the weeks before the decision to shut the school was revealed.
