One person has been taken to hospital after a crash on a North Yorkshire road.

Emergency services, including the air ambulance, were called to Longwood Bank near Richmond on Wednesday morning (November 4).

One person was taken to James Cook Hospital in Middlesbrough for further treatment.

A spokesperson from Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: “We received an emergency call at 10.16am on Wednesday morning to report a collision on Longwood Bank, Richmond.

“An ambulance, team leader and an air ambulance (YAA) were dispatched to the scene, and one patient was conveyed to James Cook Hospital by road.”

North Yorkshire Police and the Yorkshire Air Ambulance have been contacted for more information.
