There was no escape for a wanted man in York this week.

Magistrates had issued a warrant for his arrest on 6 October after he failed to attend a court hearing. This was in connection with the theft of goods from the local Morrisons store on 22 May.

Following extensive efforts to track him down, the Tadcaster-based Neighbourhood Support Unit (NSU) finally caught up with him at lunchtime on Monday (2 November) when they spotted him riding a bicycle in the Acomb area of the city.

The suspect ditched the bike before heading into a housing estate.

Officers said: “He must have thought he had got away after jumping over garden fences and hiding in a small gap between a house and a shed at the rear of the property.”

Then the NSU drone pilot launched the ‘eye in the sky’.

The drone initially detected a heat source in the garden and then maintained visual contact on the suspect while the other officers closed in to make the arrest.

The suspect was remanded into custody and will appear at court in due course.

Police added: “This is another example of the effective use of drone technology along with our relentless determination to tackle all levels of crime including shoplifting.”
