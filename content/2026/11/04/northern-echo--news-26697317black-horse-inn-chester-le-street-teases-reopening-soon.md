A reopening date is edging closer for a Chester-Le-Street pub as its new owners reveal progress on a refurbishment.

The team behind The Black Horse Inn say they are hoping to welcome customers through the doors "very soon" after weeks of renovation work.

Mother and daughter Natalie and Jessica Young are restoring the pub while preserving the traditional character that made it a local favourite.

They said: "We're hoping to be open very soon, and we honestly can't wait to welcome you all through the doors.

"We want to get things right and give The Black Horse the fresh start it deserves."

The pair revealed a host of improvements have already been completed inside the pub.

New flooring has been laid behind the bar, fresh lighting installed and walls repainted throughout. The owners have also removed the venue's graffiti-covered chalkboards, fitted new blinds and are preparing for new carpets to be laid this week.

A former dartboard area has been removed to make way for a DJ booth.

The owners say improvements for the outdoor spaces are planned for next year.
