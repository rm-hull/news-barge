Residents had gathered for their **weekly humanitarian aid distribution** when at least nine**FAB-3000 bombs**, each weighing around three tonnes, hit the site. Footage shows a** huge crater**, debris scattered across the area and bodies being recovered from the wreckage.** Four police officers and two rescue workers**were among those killed, while** around 50 people remain missing**. Volunteers joined the search despite repeated** disruptions caused by Russian drones**.

At a hospital in **Zaporizhzhia**, doctors treated four survivors for** burns, fractures and shrapnel wounds**. Among them was a 22-year-old woman with** burns covering around 81% of her body** and a rescue worker who underwent surgery for a broken jaw and arm injuries.

President Volodymyr Zelensky described the attack as **"one of the largest Russian mass killings of this war"**, while regional authorities declared** three days of mourning**.
