## A blue wave has swept the US in the midterm elections, with Democrats winning the Senate and heading for a big House majority as votes are still being counted on the West Coast. We bring live analysis from Philadelphia and Brussels, plus Poland’s crisis, Ukraine talks and the EU enlargement race.

## On today's show:

**Boyd Wagner**, elections expert and former Euronews head of polling, gives the latest from Philadelphia after a night following the US midterm results.** Penny Naas**, Senior Vice President and Brussels Office Director of the German Marshall Fund, discusses whether this is the end of Trumpism, and what it means for 2028.** Vera Jourová**, former EU Justice Commissioner, assesses Poland's constitutional crisis and whether judicial independence can be restored without another confrontation.** Shona Murray**, Euronews NATO correspondent, previews this week's talks with US envoy** Steve Witkoff**, EU countries, the World Bank and NATO on ending the war in Ukraine.** Angela Skujins** brings the highlights of yesterday's Euronews Enlargement Summit and what to expect from this week's Commission report.
