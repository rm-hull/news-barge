A drug dealer caught with a cocaine filled Kinder egg down his trousers has been jailed for more than two years.

Connor Lawlor, 20, was sentenced to 26 months in prison after police found 15 wraps of crack cocaine and around £600 in cash during a search on Bishopton Lane, Stockton.

He admitted possession with intent to supply crack cocaine and possession of criminal property.

Cleveland Police were on proactive patrols in the Newtown area on August 14 when they spotted Lawlor standing near bushes alongside a vehicle they suspected had cloned plates.

Police said Lawlor fidgeted with his trousers as officers approached, prompting them to search him.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21669061.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Body-worn camera footage released by Cleveland Police shows officers recovering a Kinder egg containing wraps of crack cocaine during the arrest of Connor Lawlor in Stockton. *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Officers discovered a Kinder egg concealed down his trouser leg containing 15 wraps of crack cocaine. Around £600 in cash was also recovered.

Police arrested Lawlor and charged him with possessing crack cocaine with intent to supply it and possessing criminal property.

He later pleaded guilty to both offences and appeared at Teesside Crown Court on Tuesday, November 3, where he was jailed for 26 months.

Following the sentencing, PC Foster of Cleveland Police said: "Lawlor thought he was above the law. Despite his previous offending, he continued to be involved in criminality and was caught dealing crack cocaine.
