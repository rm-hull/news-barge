A limited-edition lager has been launched to mark 10 years of distilling at the Spirit of Yorkshire Distillery.

Created in collaboration with Wold Top Brewery, the Filey Bay 10th Anniversary Lager celebrates a decade of whisky-making at the Hunmanby distillery.

Jenni Ashwood, marketing director at Spirit of Yorkshire Distillery, said: "Long-term fans of Filey Bay will already know that we’ve collaborated with Wold Top in plenty of different ways over the years, from using their beer casks to mature our whisky to releasing our own barrel-aged beers.

"However, this is the first time we’ve produced a standalone lager together, and it’s delicious.

"It’s the perfect way to celebrate a decade of distilling whisky in Yorkshire."

The 4.2 per cent ABV lager is brewed from regeneratively farmed barley grown on the distillery’s family farm.

It is available exclusively online at spiritofyorkshire.com and from the distillery shop in Hunmanby, priced at £30 for a 12-pack of 330ml cans.
