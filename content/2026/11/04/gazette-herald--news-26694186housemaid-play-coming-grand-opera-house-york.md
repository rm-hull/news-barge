A stage adaptation of the blockbuster psychological thriller The Housemaid will make its world premiere in York.

Based on Freida McFadden’s bestselling novel, the play will run at the Grand Opera House from November 23 to 27, 2027, as part of its UK tour.

The Housemaid has been adapted for the stage by Bekah Brunstetter, a Tony Award nominee known for The Notebook, and will be directed by Matthew Dunster, whose credits include 2:22 A Ghost Story.

Jenefer Brown, president of global products and experiences at Lionsgate, said: "With the stage play debuting next year and our sequel film in production, the momentum behind The Housemaid franchise continues to grow.

"We’re thrilled to work with Simon, Hanna, Bekah and Matthew to bring this story to the stage in an entirely new way, giving audiences the chance to experience its suspense, twists and turns live."

Producer Simon Friend said the adaptation offers "a fresh and uniquely theatrical take" on the story, promising a "thrilling new dimension to the franchise".

The original novel was released in 2022 and became a viral hit, leading to two sequels and a successful film adaptation released in December 2025, which grossed nearly £300 million worldwide.
