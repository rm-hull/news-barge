Severe delays of ten minutes have been reported on the A19 in North Yorkshire.

Traffic reported to have an average speed of 10mp south of York when travelling northbound.

The delays, reported by AA Roadwatch, are connected to temporary traffic signals that have been erected due to electrical work on the A19 at Deighton Grove.

Both directions are affected by the signals.
