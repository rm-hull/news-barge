York Racecourse has closed its 2026 flat season with record prize money on its final cards, and much of the money behind a year like that carries a betting brand's name. For Ryedale, where York is the nearest major course, the Knavesmire's results matter as much as anything run closer to home.

The final two days of York's season on October 9 and 10 carried more than £660,000 in prize money, a record for the meeting, with three races worth six figures. The course was hoping to edge ahead of last year's attendance, with head of marketing James Brennan pointing to 280,000 racegoers as the mark to beat.
