More funding to progress a major town centre development, including new offices and changes to the former Wilko site, has been backed by Darlington council leaders.

Darlington Borough Council's cabinet approved a further £560,000 for the plans on Tuesday, November 3.

The money will pay for design work and other costs needed to take the scheme through the planning process.

Plans include 50,000 sq ft of new office space on the former Sports Direct site at East Street and have now been widened to include the former Wilko building and car park, where proposals could include demolition, parking and improvements to the surrounding area.

However, this approval does not mean the development will definitely be built, with further funding and another cabinet decision needed before construction can begin.

Deputy council leader Chris McEwan said discussions had been taking place with Tees Valley Combined Authority since June over possible grant funding.

"The discussions have identified an opportunity to access complementary funding via the Mayoral Transport Fund, but require the proposal to be widened to include the Wilko site," he said.

"The funding requested will deliver this subject to the formal planning process."

The council had already set aside £410,000 to prepare a planning application for the new offices.

The development is planned close to the £120m government economic campus being built off Brunswick Street, which is expected to house 1,600 civil servants.

Consultants estimate the office scheme could support 470 full-time jobs, including around 275 for Darlington residents, and add £21m a year to the economy.

A planning application is expected to be submitted by December, with a decision targeted for April 2027.

If further funding and approval are secured, construction could begin towards the end of 2027 and finish in mid-2029.
