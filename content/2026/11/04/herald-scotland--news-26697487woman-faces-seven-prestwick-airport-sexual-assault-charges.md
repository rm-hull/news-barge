A woman who allegedly assaulted teenagers and other males while working at Glasgow Prestwick Airport's warehouse has appeared in court.

Denise Dela Cruz faced numerous charges of sexually assaulting males as young as 17 during their work, appearing for a brief hearing at Ayr Sheriff Court last week.

The 41-year-old is alleged to have sexually assaulted a warehouse operative then aged 17 to 18 at Transit House at Prestwick Airport in the course of his employment.

It's alleged that she placed her hand on his groin and genital area and squeezed or held his privates on or between May 1, 2025 and January 6, 2026.

She faces similar charges involving five other male complainers.

A further sexual assault claim, on or between August 1 and August 31 last year at Prestwick Airport, involves similar alleged conduct.

The case was continued without plea when she appeared represented by defence solicitor Alan Watt on Monday, October 26.

Cruz, of Symington, was ordained to appear again at a later date.
