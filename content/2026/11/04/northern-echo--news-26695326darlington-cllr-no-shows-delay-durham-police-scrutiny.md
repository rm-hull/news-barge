A councillor has warned children's safety is at risk after councillors failed to turn up for a "very important" meeting about police failings.

Councillors could be heard saying "you're joking" as Police and Crime Panel chair Cllr Robert Potts confirmed a meeting on Monday (November 2) would have to be postponed because there were not enough members present for it to legally go ahead.

Nine members were needed for the meeting, which is made up of councillors from Durham and Darlington Councils, both part of Durham Constabulary's patch, to go ahead.

Eight panel members attended, including County Durham councillors and independent representatives - one County Durham councillor had sent apologies.

But none of the three Darlington members, Conservative group leader Cllr Jonathan Dulston, Labour’s Cllr James McGill and independent Cllr Kevin Nicholson, attended.

The three no-show councillors did not respond to a request for comment.

The panel had been due to question Police and Crime Commissioner Joy Allen over Durham Constabulary's response to a May inspection report which said the force requires improvement in how it investigates abuse, neglect and exploitation.

Cllr Potts said: "Unless we have these meetings then we can't ensure the safety of the children in Darlington or Durham."

![Councillor Robert Potts.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/19406173.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Councillor Robert Potts. *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

A report prepared for the meeting said the inspection identified four areas for improvement and Durham Constabulary accepted the findings, introducing improvements.

Joy Allen was originally due to update councillors in September, but that meeting was postponed for the same reason, with no representitives from Darlington attending.

Ms Allen was due to discuss progress made since the inquiry, including better oversight, targeting offenders and improving specialist safeguarding services.

Cllr Potts said: "If this was something minor that we were looking at, you could sit there and think, you know what, we'll have a look at the agenda, maybe agree at the end of the agenda to turn around and say, 'look, we all agree with this', but this isn't something minor.

"We're talking about His Majesty's Inspector of Constabulary's National Child Protection inspection.

"Durham Constabulary were heavily criticised in this report.

"We're talking about children being sexually abused and for us not to be able to progress this a second time in a row is obviously very, very disappointing to say the least."

Cllr Potts said he did not want to criticise the missing members without knowing the circumstances.

He said: "I don't know why they haven't turned up and I don't want to be critical if something's happened and they're in an accident on a road or whatever, but to me this is a very, very important issue that we're looking at and to miss it twice in a row is disappointing.

"I'd like to hope that the next time we call this meeting those people are in attendance."

The next meeting is due to be held in Darlington in December.
