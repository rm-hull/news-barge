AN East Yorkshire construction installation business has gone into liquidation owing more than £12,000.

Brick Slip Fitters Ltd of York Road, Shiptonthorpe, was incorporated in 2020 and entered liquidation on October 14, with London-based Exigen Group appointed liquidator.

Companies House records Wayne Williamson as Brick Slip Fitters Ltd’s only director.

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs says the business had estimated assets of £3,007.61 available for distribution.




* **Ball Property Group liquidated owing £36,348 after a year**
* **York building company liquidated with debts over £60,000**
* **AG Construction and Developments Ltd of York in liquidation**
* **Quickfix@home (York) and Quickfix (York) in liquidation**

When it came to creditors, the business had no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

However, HMRC was owed £10,848.16 in VAT as a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next.

In addition, the company owed a further £4,200 to unsecured creditors – those who are paid last.

£1,200 was owed to consumer creditors and £3,000 was owed to its director.

This gave an estimated deficiency of £12,040.45.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £1, this left an overall deficit or shortfall of £12,041.45.
