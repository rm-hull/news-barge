Copenhagen, Denmark’s capital, is known for its colourful architecture, rich cultural heritage and relaxed way of life. It’s a city where historic landmarks and modern Scandinavian design sit together.

One of the best ways to discover the city is with a local guide. In this episode of Grand Voyager, Lilly joins Therese from Copenhagen by Mie to explore the Danish capital, learning about its history, traditions and distinctive character.

Art is an important part of Danish culture. At the National Gallery of Denmark, Lilly explores the country’s artistic heritage, discovering works from Danish masters alongside contemporary art.

Cycling is one of the most popular ways to get around in Copenhagen, with dedicated cycle lanes and a city built around two wheels. To experience this for herself, Lilly joins a cycling tour before visiting the Happiness Museum. Here, she meets the team to discover why Denmark is often associated with happiness and how the Danish concept of hygge influences everyday life.

Copenhagen is also known for its contemporary architecture and waterfront neighbourhoods. In the district of Nordhavn, Lilly checks into Hotel Frihavnen, a boutique hotel surrounded by modern buildings, independent cafés and harbour views.

Food is at the heart of Danish culture, with a strong emphasis on tradition, quality and local ingredients. At Epicurus, Lilly discovers a creative approach to Nordic cuisine, where seasonal ingredients meet French culinary techniques. The restaurant also combines its dining experience with an intimate jazz atmosphere.

To finish her journey, Lilly visits Tivoli Gardens, which first opened its gates in 1843. With its landscaped gardens, amusement rides and lively atmosphere, it remains one of Copenhagen’s best-known attractions.

With its rich cultural heritage, celebrated food scene, cycling culture and distinctive approach to wellbeing, Copenhagen offers visitors a fascinating mix of experiences and plenty to discover.
