Activewear brand TALA is to open its first store outside London at Edinburgh’s St James Quarter on November 21.

The new shop will be the company’s third UK location, following the launch of stores on Carnaby Street and at Westfield London.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21475043.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TALA. *(Image: Supplied)*

The Edinburgh store will stock TALA’s full product range and offer what the brand describes as an immersive retail experience, including personalised customer service.

Founded in 2019 by entrepreneur Grace Beverley, TALA is a female-led activewear brand focused on sustainability and inclusivity.

The company raised £5 million in 2024 to support international growth, with investment from Pembroke VCT, Venrex and Active Partners.

Its first physical store opened at 3-4 Carnaby Street in May 2025, followed by a second location at Westfield London in November 2025.

Beverley founded TALA after identifying a gap in the market for sustainable activewear. She is also the founder of fitness technology business Shreddy and The Productivity Method, and has built a global audience of more than three million people.

Sally Hughes-Daniels, chief executive of TALA, said: "We’re proud to announce that TALA is opening its third store outside of London.

"The success of our Carnaby and Westfield White City stores has proven our retail model and it’s now time to build on this success.

"Expanding beyond London has long been in the works, but for our first step outside the capital it was so important that we found a location that felt a natural fit for TALA, and Edinburgh ticks every box.

"We already know we have a great TALA community in Edinburgh, and the city’s strong outdoor and active culture makes it the perfect place for us to put down roots.

"Bringing TALA to women all around the UK is one of our key goals, and we’re looking forward to Edinburgh marking the start of that IRL journey.

"We can’t wait to meet more of our community in person and become part of this historic city."
