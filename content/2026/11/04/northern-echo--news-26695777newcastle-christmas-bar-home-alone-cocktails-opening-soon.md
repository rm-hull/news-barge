A pop-up bar is promising mulled wine, movie-themed cocktails, and giant nutcracker soldiers.

Final preparations are under way for the launch of Christmas at Grey Street in Newcastle, which will open on Thursday, November 12 - the same night as Newcastle’s Christmas lights switch-on.

The bar, created by transforming the former Leila’s Lily’s venue, will open exclusively for the festive season.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21586064.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Malhotra Group)*

The Malhotra Group plc, which owns the venue, said the opulent, glamorous venue is the ideal place to kick start Christmas celebrations.

From 4pm to 8pm on launch night, guests will be greeted by two stilt-walking nutcracker soldiers and offered a complimentary glass of mulled wine on arrival.

Inside, the green and gold-themed venue will feature a main bar and a Christmas movie room, where visitors can watch subtitled festive films and enjoy a complimentary mini bag of popcorn.

The themed cocktails are expected to be the main attraction, with each one inspired by a Christmas film, dessert, or classic cocktail with a seasonal twist.

Highlights include To Me, You Are Perfect - a vodka-based strawberry martini inspired by Love Actually and The Filthy Marg - a Home Alone-themed margarita.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21586063.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Malhotra Group)*

All Christmas cocktails are priced at £9 each, with a festive happy hour from 5pm to 7pm offering two for £15.

Classic cocktails are available for £8 each, or two for £14 during happy hour.

The venue will also feature a self-service Guinness machine and a range of Hawkstone products, including cider, vodka, gin, and beer.

A charitable element has been included, with £1 from every hot chocolate and Aperol Spritz sold going to the venue’s 2026 charity partner, Children’s Cancer North.

Live music will be featured on Friday, November 13, with a programme of live performances and DJ sets throughout December.

The venue will close at 6pm on Christmas Eve.

Christmas at Grey Street will be open Thursday to Sunday in November, and daily from noon in December.

It will operate on a walk-in basis only, with no reservations accepted.
