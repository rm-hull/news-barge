A CAFÉ in the heart of a York town has revealed it will be closing its doors this winter.

Costa Coffee in Haxby has announced that it will be shutting on January 14, 2027, after an announcement made by staff members on social media.

In a post made to Facebook, a manager from the store said: “Speaking on behalf of myself and all my lovely team here, it is such a shame that we are coming to a close.
