A family-run Indian restaurant in Darlington that “gives its everything” to customers has made the top ten of The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Arvelli Indian Restaurant, on Northgate, first opened in November 2021 and is still going strong with owner Raju Ahmed at the helm.

The 30-year-old, who is from Darlington, said the restaurant prides itself on providing both quality food and service, adding that it is about “giving your everything” to customers.

And now, Arvelli is hoping to get the votes of Northern Echo readers after making the shortlist for our Best Curry House 2026 competition.

“It is 100 per cent nice to be nominated,” Raju said.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21667215.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raju Ahmed and Arisha Ahmed *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

“It is not all about winning - at least we are doing something good. It is about the service as well as all about the food.

“You have to give your everything to customers. It is about quality, you can buy food everywhere but you don’t find good quality food and service everywhere.

“We use fresh products, everything is made from scratch. We try to do everything better and we are always looking to improve.”

Raju said Arvelli’s has plenty of loyal customers, drawing in punters from not only Darlington but the likes of Yarm and Richmond.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21667207.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the highly-rated restaurant *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*

He said the most popular dish at the moment is the lamb shank, balti, and thali tandoori, which he described as “Indian tapas”.

With a 4.8 star rating on Google, Arvelli has been praised for offering “proper authentic south Asian cuisine” as well as its “delightful service and ambiance”.

One diner said: “Can’t recommend Arvelli enough! The food is consistently amazing, good quality and delicious. The staff are very friendly and polite.

“A regular for us and wouldn’t go anywhere else.”

Arvelli is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Babul’s, in Darlington, and The Indian Cottage, Crook.

Readers will decide the winner in a public vote, and we will be featuring each of the businesses nominated in the next two weeks.

To vote, complete the form in The Northern Echo each day for the next two weeks and return it to: The Northern Echo Best Curry House 2026, Local IQ House, 1 Dalston Road, Carlisle, CA2 5NP.

Votes will be counted soon after voting closes.
