# Yorkshire wildlife photographer captures birds stocking up for the winter

## A wildlife fan has captured pictures of birds searching for food ahead of winter.

He says of his pictures taken on October 27: "I was keen to photograph birds who were busy feeding as the weather starts to become colder.

"The colourful jays especially put on a great show stocking up with food ready for winter. I really love the bright iridescent blue on their wings.

![Robin bird searching for food at a nature reserve in Leeds. Credit: Martin Goff](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjMwNDJlYWI3LTA4NmQtNDc0ZS05YTVhLTJlMTBiNTk2MWUyNDphYzRkYTgyNS0yZmYwLTQ5MTgtYTkwZi0yZDkzN2YwYjBhMzM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"The smaller birds were also out in force and I can never resist photographing a blue tit or a robin, such colourful birds, common maybe but so beautiful.

"Catching birds taking off or in flight is always such a pleasure as the resulting photos give you glimpses of what your eye normally can't discern.

"The frozen wings especially make stunning patterns instead of a blur as they whizz past."
