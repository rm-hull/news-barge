A leading chartered surveyors in Scotland is stepping up its expansion in England with the opening of a new office and an enhanced focus on London's commercial and residential property markets.

The move marks a significant new chapter in the firm's English growth strategy, bringing together its established London team with additional senior expertise in commercial agency and residential valuations.

Based at 12 Bolton Street, Mayfair, the new office will be home to commercial agency partner Adam Honeyman, who has relocated from Glasgow to develop Shepherd's commercial agency offering in London, and Michael-John Henretty, who returns to the firm to lead its residential valuation services in the capital.

The expansion builds on the foundations established by existing London partners Tom Higgins and Krishan Patel, who have played a central role in developing Shepherd's English operation and its commercial property management and consultancy services.

Established in 1880, Shepherd has built a formidable reputation north of the border. Its continued investment in London reflects the firm's ambition to establish a similarly strong position in the English property market, offering clients access to a broader range of specialist services through its expanding national network.

Ian Fergusson, Senior Partner at Shepherd Chartered Surveyors, said: “Shepherd has built a strong foundation in England thanks to the work of Tom Higgins, Krishan Patel and the wider team.

“Our continued investment in London, bringing together their experience with the expertise of Adam and Michael-John, reflects the increasing demand we are seeing from clients operating across London and the wider property market.

“Our ambition is to build on those foundations and establish an even stronger presence in one of the UK's most important property markets.”

The firm is also exploring opportunities to expand its team further as it develops its presence in the capital.
