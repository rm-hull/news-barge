In business, relationships matter. Whether the aim is to entertain valued clients, reward colleagues, bring a team together or support a charitable cause, the right setting can make all the difference.

At Trump International Golf Links on Aberdeenshire’s spectacular north-east coast, companies can combine championship golf with first-class hospitality, tailored events and facilities designed to make an impression.

Set against the backdrop of the Great Dunes of Scotland, the resort is home to two championship courses, the Old Course and New Course, offering an impressive choice for corporate golf outings.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21668669.jpg?type=mds-article-620)As well as golf, the resort also offers a range of team-building activities, including clay pigeon shooting, archery, mini Highland Games, estate Segway tours, whisky tasting, corporate golf coaching and barbecue or hog-roast experiences *(Image: Jacob Sjoman)*

Its corporate and charity golf day packages are designed to take the pressure out of organising an event, with an experienced team working with organisers to create an occasion suited to their requirements.

For groups of 20 or more golfers, the package includes 18 holes on either the Old or New Course, or the opportunity to take on The Greatest 36. Tournament administration and live scoring are provided, alongside tea, coffee and bacon rolls on arrival, a welcome tote bag, nearest-the-pin and longest-drive competitions, and a two-course meal at The Dunes Restaurant.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21668663.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The dining room is set for a banquet at the Trump Aberdeen hotel on the Menie Estate *(Image: Ashley Morrison)*

Guests also have full use of the practice facilities for the day.

There is plenty of scope to make an event more distinctive. Options include a pre-golf clinic with a PGA professional, engraved welcome gifts, dual-branded merchandise, a Beat the Pro competition and drams on the 18th hole.

Private room hire, tailored menus, executive VIP travel and bed-and-breakfast accommodation can also be arranged, while groups seeking a more exclusive experience can enquire about private use of MacLeod House & Lodge.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21668684.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Stunning MacLeod House is available to for guests who desire the height of Highland hospitality *(Image: Michal Wachucik)*

Golf is only part of the proposition. The resort also offers a range of team-building activities, including clay pigeon shooting, archery, mini Highland Games, estate Segway tours, whisky tasting, corporate golf coaching and barbecue or hog-roast experiences. Such additions provide opportunities to bring colleagues together and give guests something beyond the fairways to remember.

For businesses looking to establish a longer-term relationship with the resort, corporate membership offers a different kind of opportunity.

Available in Classic and Premium tiers, it is positioned as more than simply access to a golf course: a platform for client entertainment, networking and rewarding employees and executives.

Corporate members benefit from access to both championship courses, priority tee times, concierge-style service, practice facilities, private dining and hospitality, and exclusive member events. It is an opportunity to bring business relationships into a setting where conversation can continue beyond the boardroom.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21668665.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The resort can also accommodate business gatherings away from the golf course *(Image: Supplied)*

The resort can also accommodate business gatherings away from the golf course, with drawing-room lounges and meeting spaces, day delegate rates, land rental opportunities and private butler service.

Whether planning a meeting, a corporate retreat or a full programme of golf and hospitality, organisers can explore arrangements tailored to their event.

From a single charity day to an ongoing corporate membership, Trump International Golf Links, Scotland, offers businesses a distinctive setting in which to entertain, connect and reward.
