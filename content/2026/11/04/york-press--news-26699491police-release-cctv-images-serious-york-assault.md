## Two men wanted after 'assault' in York street

One of the men the police are seeking *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

POLICE have released CCTV images of two men they want to speak to following a serious assault in York.

The incident happened in Blake Street at around 4am on Thursday, October 15, 2026.

The victim, a local man in his early 20s, suffered serious head and facial injuries and required hospital treatment.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671004.jpg?type=mds-article-620)One of the men the police are seeking *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

North Yorkshire Police are urging anyone who recognises the men, witnessed the incident, or has relevant video footage to come forward.

Information can be emailed to [Sophie.Riley@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Sophie.Riley@northyorkshire.police.uk), or provided by calling 101, quoting reference 12260202663.
