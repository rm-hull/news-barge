Harrogate School of Swimming will become Ashville College’s exclusive swimming partner from early 2027.

The partnership will see lessons introduced at Ashville Sports Centre, creating more spaces and new lesson times for swimmers across the Harrogate district.

The venue will operate alongside the swimming school’s existing locations, with Willow Tree Primary School remaining the home of its baby swimming programme.

The team at Harrogate School of Swimming said:

> “This is an exciting new chapter for us. Ashville Sports Centre will become a fantastic new venue for our lessons, giving swimmers of all ages and abilities across the Harrogate District even more opportunities to learn, grow and enjoy the water.”

The additional venue will provide opportunities for children, families and adults, including swimmers progressing through the school’s lesson stages.

The team added:

> “We look forward to working closely together to inspire even more people to fall in love with swimming and develop skills that will last a lifetime.”

Timetables and sign-up details will be announced over the coming weeks.
