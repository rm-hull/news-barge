**The Democrats wrestled control of the Senate and House of Representatives from Donald Trump’s Republican Party overnight on Tuesday, clinched a resounding victory in the US midterm elections.**

As of 5.30 am in Scotland, the party had flipped Senate seats in Ohio, Kansas, Texas, Iowa, North Carolina and South Carolina, all states handily won by Mr Trump in November 2024.

The Democrats also defeated longtime Republican senator Susan Collins in Maine and retained seats in Georgia, Minnesota and New Hampshire.

That means the Democrats have secured at least 53 seats in the upper chamber, even as results in Democratic-leaning states like Michigan and Alaska are yet to be confirmed.

Similarly, in the House, the party overcame gerrymandering attempts in several states to flip control of the lower chamber to the Democrats for the first time since 2022.

![Trump, Talarico to hold dueling rallies in Texas with Senate control at stake](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21639425.jpg?type=mds-article-620)James Talarico will become the first Democratic senator from Texas in 38 years. *(Image: John Oliva/Caller-Times, USA TODAY Network via Reuters Co)*

NBC News has projected that the party could pick up more than 40 districts en route to a 75-seat majority over the Republicans, who struggled to defend Mr Trump’s tariffs, as well as high living costs and the war in Iran.

The rout included historic wins in Kansas, where a Democrat senator had not been elected in 94 years, as well as in Texas, where seminary student James Talarico became the first Democrat to be elected senator since 1988.

Reacting to the results, Mr Trump posted on Truth Social: “I wasn’t on the Ballot and if I was, we would have won IN A LANDSLIDE!”

However, Georgia senator Jon Ossoff, considered a potential frontrunner for the Democratic presidential nomination in 2028, told supporters that the vote was a clear repudiation of Mr Trump’s character and policies.

He said: “In sweeping and decisive victories the American people have rebuked the depravity, corruption, and incompetence of the failed lame duck president Donald J Trump.

![Videos taken inside Atlanta airport used to blame shutdown on Democrats](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20676927.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Georgia senator Jon Ossoff was re-elected. *(Image: Jack Gruber, USA TODAY)*

“And if, at times these last two years, amidst the ceaseless onslaught of brutality and indecency, you doubted whether our republic would survive, let tonight’s results give you courage that the first light of dawn is on the horizon.”

Mr Trump also hit out at Texas governor Greg Abbott, who lost his election to Democrat Gina Hinojosa.

“If Greg Abbott, the Governor of Texas, hadn’t become a RINO in the last six months of his Political Life, he wouldn’t have lost,” Mr Trump posted.

“He went back to his original formula, and it wasn’t pretty.”

A key aide to Mr Trump similarly blamed the result on poor candidates. According to Politico, political consultant Alex Bruesewitz defended the president, stating:

“His endorsement has cleared the field for them in their primaries and oftentimes in general elections.

“Most of them have never had to fight in a tough race, so they forgot the basics of campaigning and communicating in a digital era.”

The Democrat victory places the party in charge of the United States’ legislative branch of government, allowing them to propose bills, set budgets, and scrutinise the president and his administration.

However, Mr Trump will retain veto power, in line with the Constitution.
