Former Prime Minister Rishi Sunak has opened a home care provider's new office in Northallerton as the business expands into Darlington, South Durham and Yarm.

The family-run company says the move will allow more people across the region to receive care in their own homes, including dementia care, personal care, companionship and 24-hour live-in support.

Bluebird Care Northallerton, Thirsk & Catterick has been supporting residents across Hambleton, Richmond and surrounding areas since 2013 and currently cares for more than 100 people.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21658615.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: PROVIDED)*

The business recently took over the neighbouring Darlington, South Durham and Yarm territory, significantly increasing its coverage across the North East and North Yorkshire.

Richmond and Northallerton MP Rishi Sunak opened the company's new headquarters at Thurston House, Omega Business Village, in Northallerton.

During the visit, Mr Sunak toured the new office and met members of the management team, led by registered manager Lauren Smith.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21658618.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: PROVIDED)*

Discussions focused on the importance of recruiting and retaining skilled care workers to meet growing demand for home care services.

Rishi Sunak said: "It was a pleasure to visit Bluebird Care's new office in Northallerton and congratulate Lauren and her team on this expansion.

"Being able to receive care in the comfort of your own home makes an enormous difference to people's independence and quality of life, while also providing reassurance to their families.

"Bluebird Care has been supporting people in our communities for over a decade, and it is wonderful to see the business growing. I wish the whole team every success as they bring their services to more people across the region."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21658619.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Rishi Sunak at Northallerton Bluebird Care Office. *(Image: PROVIDED)*

The new premises provide upgraded facilities for staff, including improved parking and a more central location, and will serve as the operational base for both territories.

As part of the expansion, Bluebird Care will also open a community hub at Amity House on Coniscliffe Road in Darlington town centre later this month. The hub will provide a local point of contact for residents seeking advice and information about care at home.

The growth plans are also expected to create new jobs, with the company currently recruiting care assistants and support staff across Darlington, South Durham and Yarm.

Lauren Smith, who joined Bluebird Care as a carer aged 18 before progressing through the organisation and becoming registered manager at 23, said the expansion would help more people remain in familiar surroundings while receiving support.

She said: "Taking on Darlington, South Durham and Yarm means we can now support many more families who want their loved ones to stay in the home they know and love.

"Our new office gives us improved facilities to better support our colleagues, which matters more than ever as we continue to grow. Many of our care professionals have been with us for over a decade, and we look forward to welcoming new colleagues."

Bluebird Care has offered live-in care since 2015 and says its services are rated "Good" across all areas by the Care Quality Commission.
