Glasgow Prestwick Airport is in talks to establish a new cargo route with Nanjing Lukou International Airport in China next year as it continues to expand its direct connections with East Asia.

The plans were discussed during a meeting in Nanjing between Prestwick's Chief Executive Officer, Jules Matteoni, Regional Director, Colin Dai, Vice Governor of the People’s Government of Jiangsu Province, Ma Shiguang, and local officials.

During the meeting the Provincial Government confirmed its backing for a new route between Nanjing and Prestwick.

Mr Matteoni said: “China is an increasingly important part of our cargo network, and a direct link with Nanjing would strengthen the options available to businesses moving goods between the United Kingdom and one of China’s largest provincial economies.

“We are working with our partners in Jiangsu to establish a route that can support long term demand, strengthen trade links, and create economic opportunities at both ends of the connection.”

Discussions will now continue on the commercial and operational requirements for the proposed service, with further details on the operator and launch schedule to be announced in the coming months.

Mr Dai said: “Every new connection into China creates another opportunity for exporters to reach customers and supply chains more directly.

“Jiangsu has a substantial manufacturing base, and we see a clear opportunity to build stronger cargo flows in both directions while giving businesses in Scotland and across the United Kingdom greater access to the Chinese market.”

Prestwick already hosts over twenty weekly scheduled services to China, with direct links to Ürümqi (URC), Guangzhou (CAN), Chengdu (CTU), and Shanghai (PVG).

The proposed route would build on Prestwick's growing cargo connections with China and its position as a freight and e-commerce gateway serving the United Kingdom and northwest Europe.
