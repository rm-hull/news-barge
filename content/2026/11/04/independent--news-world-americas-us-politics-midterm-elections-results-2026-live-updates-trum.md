President Donald Trump is already threatening to use veto powers after Democrats won back full control of Congress in a stunning midterms election victory.

Democrats are projected to take back control of both the House and the Senate after a series of major wins dubbed a “Blue Tsunami”.

In one of several significant upset wins, Democrat James Talarico won his Texas senate race by defeating Trump-backed attorney general Ken Paxton and breaking the party’s 38 year losing streak in the Lone Star state.

Elsewhere Amy Acton was elected governor in Ohio despite a controversial attack ad campaign against her using her own mother, and potential presidential hopeful Senator Jon Ossoff won his race in Georgia.

Midterm election results are still pouring in after millions of voters went to the polls to give their verdict on the second Trump presidency.

Trump’s initial response to what is turning out to be a disastrous night for the Republicans was to blame the candidates themselves, before posting a picture of a MAGA-style red hat with ‘VETO ‘ written on it.

## Slurring Trump makes stunning admission after devastating night

President Donald Trump acknowledged Democrats’ success after Republicans suffered bruising midterm defeats, telling Fox News: “The Democrats did a good job.”

“We had tremendous rallies,” Trump said, talking about the “great excitement” he saw from supporters on the campaign trail. “We had about 30 of them.

“There were 1000s and 1000s of people outside, let alone inside. Didn't get inside, and they watched for big, you know, screens on the outside of the buildings. They were excited. There was great excitement.

“So I'm a little bit surprised. I thought that I could transfer those people into candidates, but maybe in certain cases, you know, don't forget we had a lot of candidates that did win also.”

“Today is a great day for America,” they said. “The American people have spoken and made clear that they want to see an end to the high costs, endless chaos, and constant corruption from Republicans in Washington.

“We worked tirelessly to elect a Democratic majority so the American people have a united team of leaders who will fight each and every day to tackle the issues that matter most to them, their pocketbooks, and their families.

“Senate Democrats have just delivered the greatest midterm performance in modern political history when just a year and a half ago, no one thought it was possible.

## Trump is Texas toast as ‘Talafreako’ trounces Paxton

In a historic victory that will rock Texas politics and pave the way for a Democratic majority in the U.S. Senate, Texas voters have elected Democratic candidate James Talarico, becoming the first Democrat in the state to be elected to the upper chamber of Congress in nearly 40 years.

Talarico is projected to defeat the state’s Republican attorney general Ken Paxton, who was endorsed by President Donald Trump despite investigations, a fraud indictment, an impeachment and endless scandals that were an albatross on his campaign.

Yet the president has repeatedly urged voters to get behind Paxton, who defeated Republican incumbent John Cornyn during the state’s Republican primary, and even traveled to the state to campaign alongside him on October 7 and again on October 19.
