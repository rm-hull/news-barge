Police have received reports of anti-social behaviour in and around Thirsk Market Place.

Officers say they have identified a number of young people believed to be involved and are currently speaking with them about their actions.

A police spokesperson said: "Anti-social behaviour may seem harmless to some, but it can have a significant impact on local residents, businesses, and visitors.

"We won't tolerate people intimidating others, causing criminal damage, drinking underage, littering, riding bikes irresponsibly, or causing alarm, harassment, or distress."

Police have warned that those involved could face serious consequences, including being stopped and spoken to by officers, having parents or guardians contacted, receiving formal warnings, or facing arrest and prosecution if offences have been committed.

A criminal record could also affect future employment, education, travel, and career opportunities.

Officers are encouraging parents to talk to their children about their behaviour when out with friends and to remind them to be respectful of the community and those who live and work in the town.

Anyone who witnesses anti-social behaviour is urged to report it by calling 101 or using the North Yorkshire Police website.

In an emergency or if a crime is in progress, always call 999.
