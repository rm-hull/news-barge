A Bonfire Night dispersal order has been put in place in a North East town after reported antisocial behaviour, including fireworks being let of improperly.

The order begins in the Guisborough area at 3pm today (Wednesday, November 4) and gives officers the power to move large groups on, and anyone returning to the area within 48 hours can be arrested.

Acting Sergeant Brad Passmore, from Guisborough and East Cleveland Neighbourhood Policing Team, said: “We have received reports of antisocial behaviour with large groups causing a nuisance, and the improper use of fireworks around the Westgate Park and Woodhouse estates.

“This order will help us to move on large groups causing a nuisance from the area, and those who do return to cause further problems can then be arrested. We won’t tolerate groups causing antisocial behaviour for our local communities.”

The order will be in place for 48 hours, the maximum length of time allowed, although further orders can be put in place if necessary.

The area covered by the dispersal order Hutton Lane, Rectory Lane, Bow Street, Redcar Road, Wilton Lane, the A171, Middlesbrough Road then back to Hutton Lane.
