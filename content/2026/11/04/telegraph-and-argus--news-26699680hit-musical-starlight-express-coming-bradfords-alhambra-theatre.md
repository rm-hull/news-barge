START your engines! Smash hit show Starlight Express is coming to Bradford’s Alhambra Theatre.

The critically acclaimed new production of Lord Andrew Lloyd Webber’s much-loved musical will roll into Bradford next year as part of its highly anticipated world tour.

The beloved musical made a triumphant return to London when the new production opened to widespread critical acclaim at Troubadour Wembley Park Theatre in June 2024.

It went on to win multiple awards, including an Olivier Award and a record-breaking seven WhatsOnStage Awards, and became London’s longest running revival of an Andrew Lloyd Webber musical in 20 years.

Now the thrilling new production has been redesigned for theatres across the UK and worldwide, bringing all the spectacle and high-speed energy of the London production on tour.

Seen by more than 30 million people across the world, Starlight Express is an electrifying experience for all ages.

Performing on roller skates, the cast whizzes across the stage performing some of musical theatre’s most loved songs, including AC/DC, Make Up My Heart, Light at the End of the Tunnel and Starlight Express.

As a child’s train set magically comes to life and the engines race to become the fastest in the world, Rusty the steam train has little hope of winning until he is inspired by the legend of the ‘Starlight Express’.

What unfolds is a tale of rival trains, with shy Rusty and his sweetheart Pearl, a rail carriage, at its heart.

The show started life as a bedtime story for Andrew Lloyd Webber’s children - and became one of the world’s longest-running musicals.

Described as “the fastest show on earth”, the spectacular family show is performed entirely on roller-skates, with ‘human trains’ moving to high energy song and dance numbers.

Starlight Express has music by Andrew Lloyd Webber and lyrics by Richard Stilgoe, with Arlene Phillips as creative dramaturg for the production.

\*Starlight Express is at the Alhambra Theatre from September 29 to October 9, 2027.
