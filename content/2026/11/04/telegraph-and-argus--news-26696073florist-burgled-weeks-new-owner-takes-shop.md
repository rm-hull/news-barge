The owner of a flower shop has spoken of their devastation following a late-night break-in.

Scentiments on St Enoch’s Road in Wibsey, Bradford, was reportedly burgled in the early hours of Monday, November 2.

The incident is now under investigation by police.

The shop’s new owner, who recently took over the business, described the impact of the burglary and the damage caused.

## 'It’s the world we live in'

The owner said: "It’s not been the greatest start to the week.

"We’re only a flower shop, but lo and behold someone has broken in.

"Unfortunately, it’s the world we live in.

"In the grand scheme of things, he caused more damage to me than what he took from the till.

"The police have come and they have taken fingerprints."

## CCTV shows man in act

CCTV appears to show a man smashing a window to break into the shop.

The owner believes a knife was used to force open the till and a nearby stone to smash the glass.

He said: "I passed on CCTV footage of the person who broke in."

## What next for owner?

The owner cleaned up the mess and repaired the broken window, which had initially been boarded up.

He is now considering extra security measures.

The owner said: "It’s unfair but I’m going to have to take it on the chin and make sure it doesn’t happen again.

"I just want to get business back to normal."

## Shop been going for 26 years

They added: "I took over the shop about a month ago.

"The previous owner, who had the shop for 26 years, never had an issue."

The former owner marked the shop’s 26th anniversary in August, thanking customers for their loyalty and wishing its new owner success.

She said: "It has truly been a privilege to be part of this journey and to have had the opportunity to meet so many lovely customers and colleagues along the way.

“I hope you will continue to support Scentiments for many more years to come under its new ownership, and wish the business every continued success for the future.”

## Full police statement

West Yorkshire Police confirmed the burglary and said enquiries were ongoing.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Police are investigating a burglary at a business on St Enoch’s Road, Bradford, reported just after midnight on Monday, November 2.

"Enquiries are ongoing.

"Anyone with information is asked to contact police via Live Chat at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat, quoting reference 13260621894."
