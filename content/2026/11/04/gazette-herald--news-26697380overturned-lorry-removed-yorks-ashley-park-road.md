A lorry that overturned in York has been removed from the scene.

The incident happened on Ashley Park Road at the junction with Stockton Lane, close to Christ Church, on Tuesday afternoon, November 3.

As reported by The Press, a Vac-Ex lorry tipped over onto the pavement and an area of grass.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21666612.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A lorry that toppled over on Ashley Park Road, at the junction with Stockton Lane in Heworth, York, has been removed. *(Image: Joe Fenwick)*It is not yet known what caused the vehicle to topple or whether any damage was caused but Vac.Ex.co.uk confirmed that the company was carrying out an investigation.**Recommended reads:**

Removal vehicles were on site at about 5pm yesterday to carry out the careful operation of righting the lorry and removing it from the scene.

Local resident, Garry Hornby, earlier described coming across the dramatic scene as he was heading to the shops.

![A lorry that toppled over on Ashley Park Road, at the junction with Stockton Lane in Heworth, York, has been removed.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21666615.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Diversions were put in place and barriers were put up around the fallen lorry on Ashley Park Road in York. *(Image: Joe Fenwick)*

Mr Hornby said the road was cordoned off at about 2.30pm when he saw the lorry overturned.

He said he felt it would have caused disruption for people doing the school run.

He said: "My wife was driving so I took a photo and quickly uploaded it to the Heworth Community Facebook group to advise people it was closed off. It's a massive lorry.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21668432.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A lorry that toppled over on Ashley Park Road, at the junction with Stockton Lane in Heworth, York, has been removed. *(Image: Oscar Duncan)*

"Hopefully the driver got out ok. I don't know how it occurred. Hopefully they get the road reopened soon."

Dave Branney, operations director at Vac.Ex.co.uk, confirmed that the driver had not been injured in the incident, and said: "We are aware of the incident. We are conducting our own investigation."

Vac-Ex is one of the UK's longest-established vacuum excavation specialists, providing services for utilities, rail, highways, and construction projects.

Their vehicles are designed to safely expose buried services and reduce the risk of utility strikes.

Pictures from the scene show the lorry tipped over onto its side on the grass verge of the road, with barriers placed around the vehicle.

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Police for further information.
