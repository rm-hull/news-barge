MAGA bro influencers are completely spiraling as President Donald Trump was crushed in Tuesday night’s midterm elections.

Democrats are poised to take control of the House and the Senate, despite Trump spending all of last month traveling across the country to support Republican candidates.

As election returns came in, Trump’s staunch base painted a grim picture of the state of the GOP and what the last two years of his presidency will look like.

“What is happening...Democrats are winning EVERYTHING!!! They are flipping seats we didn’t even know were in play…how is this even possible?!!!!!” Matt Van Swol wrote on X.

“We will not surrender the United States of America to Marxism,” Jack Posobiec, senior editor of the conservative news outlet Human Events, posted. There were no credible candidates in the midterms who identified as communists.

![MAGA bro influencers have spiraled as President Donald Trump was crushed in Tuesday night’s midterm elections, with one writing on X, ‘They are flipping seats we didn’t even know were in play…how is this even possible?!!!!!’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/04/6/03/Trump.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“We have much work to do to save our country,” Posobiec continued. “We must make the focus the homeland. And put our trust in God.”

Tim Pool, host of *The Culture War Podcast*, wrote, “This isn't a blue tsunami. This is a blue great mud flood. S\*\*\*.”

“This is what happens, RINO [Republican In Name Only] Senators, when you stab President Trump and We the People in the back by not passing the SAVE America Act,” Bo Loudon posted.

Multiple members of Trump’s base blamed Senate Republicans for failing to pass the SAVE America Act, which would require proof of U.S. citizenship when they register to vote. Non-citizens are already not allowed to register to vote, and it is extremely rare for non-citizens to vote in federal elections.

“They’re going to be ruthless. This is going to be two years of pure hell,” Joey Mannarino posted.

![Dejected: Supporters at a midterm watch party for Republican Senate candidate, former U.S. Rep. Mike Rogers, in the key swing state of Michigan. Progressive Democrat Abdul El-Sayed beat Rogers to become the first Muslim senator in U.S history](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/04/10/34/GettyImages-2303420966.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)
![Democrats saw massive wins Tuesday night in key Senate races, including James Talarico in Texas, Josh Turek in Iowa and Adam Hamilton in Kansas](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/04/6/25/GettyImages-2303426921.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Sean Davis, CEO and co-founder of conservative outlet The Federalist, called on Senate Republican leaders to resign promptly.

“Senate GOP leadership should all resign tomorrow. Utter incompetence all around. Lazy, incompetent, and completely brain-dead,” Davis wrote.

Democrats saw massive wins Tuesday night in key Senate races, including James Talarico in Texas, Roy Cooper in North Carolina, Annie Andrews in South Carolina, Josh Turek in Iowa, Adam Hamilton in Kansas and Sherrod Brown in Ohio.

Trump has been coping with his election losses by shifting the blame off himself after becoming the face of Republicans’ campaigns and telling his base to pretend he was on the ballot.

“I wasn’t on the ballot, and if I was, we would have won IN A LANDSLIDE!” he wrote on Truth Social.
