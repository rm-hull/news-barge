A film about fostering has been nominated for a Royal Television Society Award.

The Run, which tells the story of a young boy whose life is transformed by a new foster family, was developed with input from care-experienced young people in York.

Members of York's Children in Care Council, known as Show Me I Matter, worked with the film team to ensure its messages were authentic and reflective of real experiences.

Councillor Bob Webb, executive member for children, young people, and education at City of York Council, said: "I’m so pleased that this important film has been nominated for a TV award.

"My thanks go to the young people in our Children in Care Council, who helped to ensure that the experiences and feelings of Tom, the lead character, were authentic."

The film premiered in October 2025 and forms part of a nationwide campaign involving more than 90 local authority fostering services, project managed by CAN Digital.

Rachel Brown, the film’s project director, said: "This whole project grew out of the constant struggle to recruit foster carers, when you have limited resources."

The winner will be announced at an awards ceremony on December 2.

A list of shortlisted entries is available at [https://rts.org.uk/award/rts-midlands-awards-2026-nominees](https://rts.org.uk/award/rts-midlands-awards-2026-nominees).
