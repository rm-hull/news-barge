Several drivers have been caught speeding in a police crackdown on two 30mph roads where serious crashes have taken place over the years.

Officers from the Shipley neighbourhood policing team carried out speed checks on Cottingley Moor Road on October 23.

Three drivers were caught exceeding the 30mph limit, reaching speeds of 37mph, 38mph and 44mph.

An additional traffic offence was issued to a driver who did not have a valid MOT.

Two more speeding offences were recorded on Manywells Brow in Cullingworth, where drivers reached 38mph and 40mph.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "On October 23, Shipley NPT officers carried out speed enforcement on Cottingley Moor Road outside the primary school, between 7.40pm and 9pm.

"The officers then moved to Manywells Brow, Cullingworth, between 9.20pm and 9.45pm.

"Further enforcement operations are planned for the area."
