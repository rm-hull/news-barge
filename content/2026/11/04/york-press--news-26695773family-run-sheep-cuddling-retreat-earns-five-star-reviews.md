## Five-star sheep cuddling retreat launched from family home in Yorkshire

Sammy with one of her Valais Blacknose sheep *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A family business where you can cuddle sheep is earning five-star reviews following its launch over the summer.

Sammy Johnson-Roberts runs The Cuddly Sheep Retreat from her home in the East Coast village of Speeton, overlooking Filey Bay.

Sammy has worked with animals for more than 20 years, including at Yorkshire Wildlife Park, where she progressed to Team Leader of Primates and Presentations.

Now, she lives with wife Jo, their two children Harley and Oscar, plus a large collection of animals.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21666318.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sammy with two of her best friends *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Sammy came up with the idea for The Cuddly Sheep Retreat while worrying about how to return to work and support the family following the birth of Oscar.

She was cuddling two of her Valais Blacknose Sheep whilst watching dolphins in Filey Bay when an owl flew past.

That “lightbulb moment” made Sammy realise how lucky she was and it made her want to share it with others.

She told the Press: “I wanted to create somewhere people could escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life, slow down for a little while and experience that same wonderful feeling I had sitting on the hillside.”

Sammy has six sheep, each with their own personalities and are seen as part of the family.

A variety of experiences are offered for different ages and occasions.

The main Sheep Cuddling Experience is £45 per person and lasts about an hour.

Sammy said: “These are private sessions where visitors can spend quality time meeting, feeding and cuddling our wonderful sheep, getting to know their personalities and learning a little about them along the way.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21666343.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The flock of Valais Blacknose sheep *(Image: Pic supplied)*

“We also offer our Couples Sunset Experience at £150 per couple. This is a private, romantic experience with sheep cuddles, a picnic, some bubbly and the opportunity to watch the sun setting over our beautiful surroundings.”

There are also seasonal events with the sheep.

For example, in December, there will be Father Christmas, gingerbread decorating in Mrs Claus’s kitchen, and an elves area promising a magical experience for £35 per child (with one adult).

This follows a Halloween-themed experience with the sheep.

Sammy continued: “We’re also introducing children’s birthday parties and have lots of exciting plans for 2027, including further seasonal experiences.”

Business has been “a whirlwind” since opening, with an “absolutely wonderful” response from customers of all ages.

Social media and word of mouth have also “been fantastic for us” with the couple “overwhelmed by the lovely feedback and reviews we’ve received.”

Sammy said: “Running a small business alongside family life and a young toddler certainly isn’t always easy.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21666357.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Two of the cutest sheep around *(Image: Pic supplied)*

It’s a lot of hard work, often with very long days, but seeing people leave with huge smiles on their faces makes it all worthwhile.

“I think people are increasingly looking for something a little different, something personal and memorable, and we’ve been fortunate enough to create an experience that seems to resonate with people.”

Sammy wants people to know how much love goes into the business, with wife Jo helping behind the scenes and daughter Harley also getting involved.

She said: “What started as me sitting on a hillside cuddling two sheep has grown into something the whole family has become involved in.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21666317.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Are these the cutest sheep around? *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Sammy added: “Our sheep’s happiness and welfare will always come first.

They’re our pets, and we love them dearly.

Being able to share their wonderful characters with other people is such a privilege.

“I’d also love to encourage people to support small, independent businesses.

"There’s often an awful lot of hard work, sacrifice and determination going on behind the scenes, but it’s incredibly rewarding when people choose to support something you’ve created from scratch.”

For details and to book, go to: www.thecuddlysheepretreat.co.uk
