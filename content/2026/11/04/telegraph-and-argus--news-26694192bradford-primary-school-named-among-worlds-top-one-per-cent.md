A PRIMARY school has been named among the top one per cent of schools in the world for its approach to play.

Cavendish Primary School, part of Exceed Academies Trust in Bradford, received the OPAL Platinum Award from Outdoor Play and Learning (OPAL), recognising its commitment to creating high-quality play opportunities for pupils.

Jonathan Nixon, headteacher at Cavendish Primary School, said: "We are absolutely delighted to achieve the OPAL Platinum Award.

"This is a fantastic achievement for our whole school community and reflects the enormous commitment of our staff, children, and families."

Cavendish Primary has been recognised for making play an important part of every child's school experience.

Mr Nixon said: "At Cavendish, we want children to enjoy coming to school and to have opportunities to develop confidence, independence, and positive relationships alongside their academic learning.

"Play is an incredibly important part of childhood, and we are proud of the environment and experiences we have created for our pupils."

The Platinum Award will be formally presented on Friday, November 6, with pupils set to enjoy a special celebratory playtime.

Michael Follett, founder and director of OPAL CIC, congratulated the school on its achievement.
