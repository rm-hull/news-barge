Alan Carr, Susie McCabe and Mike Wozniak are among the first acts announced for the 2027 Glasgow International Comedy Festival.

The festival will return from March 10 to 28, with performances across venues throughout the city, ranging from major theatres to smaller stages.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669089.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Comedians, Karen Dunbar, Christopher Macarthur-Boyd , GICF Festival Director Krista MacDonald and comedians Kim Blythe and Rosco McClelland at The King’s Theatre in Glasgow. *(Image: Elaine Livingstone)*

The open-access event will feature stand-up, improv, musical comedy, live podcasts and family-friendly shows, with comedy cabaret trio Fascinating A’ida also among the names confirmed.

Carr will perform three sold-out shows at the King’s Theatre with his latest show, *Have I Said Too Much?*, which promises stories of oversharing, including tales from the Traitors turret.

Wozniak, known for his appearances on *Taskmaster*and* Man Down*, will bring his show* The Bench*to the festival. Fascinating A’ida will also perform their new cabaret show,* Bawdacious!*, at the King’s Theatre.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669106.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alan Carr. *(Image: Supplied)*Scottish acts will feature prominently in the programme. Rosco McClelland will perform his critically acclaimed show* The Bones*at the Citizens Theatre, while Kim Blythe returns to the same venue with* Puzzle* following a sold-out run last year.

Other announced highlights include Mark Black’s musical comedy show *Horrible Family*, Chris Forbes’ festive Fringe hit* Father Christmas*at Oran Mor, and the improv adventure* Tartan Tabletop*, aimed at Dungeons & Dragons fans.

McCabe, following a successful Edinburgh Fringe run, will perform her new show *Myth Informed* at the King’s Theatre over two nights. Connor Burns is also set for two headline performances at the SEC Armadillo.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669107.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Susie McCabe. *(Image: Supplied)*International performers include Los Angeles comedian Morgan Jay, who will bring his debut UK arena tour,* La Dolce Vita*, to the festival.

Kiell Smith-Bynoe, known for *Ghosts*and* Taskmaster*, will perform his improv show* Kool Story Bro* alongside a surprise celebrity guest.

The 2026 festival featured a record 636 shows across more than 40 venues, attracting more than 62,000 people.

Krista MacDonald, festival director, said: "Glasgow’s comedy scene continues to grow from strength to strength and we take great pride in having the opportunity to platform some of this country’s most talented comedians at Glasgow International Comedy Festival every year.

"2027’s programme is shaping up to be filled with big hitter headline shows, rising stars and, as usual, an abundance of homegrown talent.

"We can’t wait to reveal more of this year’s programme."

Bailie Annette Christie, chair of Glasgow Life, said: "Comedy is an important part of Glasgow’s cultural life, and the International Comedy Festival gives audiences the chance to enjoy an incredible range of talent in venues across the city.

"The first shows announced for 2027 offer a taste of what’s to come, and we’re looking forward to another brilliant month of comedy in Glasgow."

Registrations for acts and venues wishing to take part in the 2027 festival are open until November 20.
