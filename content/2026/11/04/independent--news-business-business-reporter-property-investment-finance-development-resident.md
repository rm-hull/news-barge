*Select Property is a Business Reporter client*

Manchester has established itself as one of Europe’s most talked-about property markets, attracting growing interest from domestic and international investors alike. With strong economic fundamentals, an evolving city centre and continued demand for high-quality urban living, its appeal is clear.

But as the market matures, the conversation is moving beyond growth potential alone. Today, investors want confidence that their investment will be delivered to a high standard, professionally managed and positioned to generate long-term value.

In an increasingly competitive property landscape, opportunity is only part of the equation. The real differentiator is how that opportunity is delivered.

## **Confidence comes from taking responsibility**

Investors have more choice, more information and more opportunities to compare developments than ever before. While this creates greater transparency, it can also introduce uncertainty. Headline growth forecasts and attractive locations are important, but they do not tell the whole story.

A development’s long-term performance depends on a combination of factors: the strength of its location, the quality of its design and finish, the amenities it offers and, crucially, the experience of living there.

Confidence comes from looking at the entire investment process, rather than treating development, delivery and management as separate considerations. A clear understanding of the market and attention to detail at every stage can help investors make informed decisions and create a stronger foundation for sustainable returns.

## **People-first living creates lasting value**

The strongest residential investments recognise that property is fundamentally about people. Residents are not simply occupying apartments; they are choosing a home, a lifestyle and a place to build their lives.

This is why the quality of the wider living experience matters. Well-considered amenities, thoughtfully designed spaces, high standards of management and a genuine focus on resident needs can all contribute to a development that people are proud to call home.

When residents feel well served, they are more likely to stay longer, helping to support occupancy, reduce turnover and create a more consistent rental proposition. For investors, these factors can contribute to stronger income performance and long-term asset value. For residents, they mean a better place to live.

A people-first approach is therefore more than a philosophy. It is a commercially important part of creating resilient residential investments, and one that is at the heart of everything Select Property does.

## **Looking beyond Manchester: Birmingham’s opportunity**

While Manchester continues to attract significant investor interest, Birmingham is emerging as a compelling market for long-term growth. The city is undergoing a similar phase of urban transformation, albeit one that is at an earlier stage than Manchester. This presents investors with an opportunity to enter the market early and benefit from Birmingham’s ongoing development and future growth potential.

In particular, the Snow Hill and Colmore Row districts present an opportunity to address the undersupply of premium residential apartments in established central locations. For investors, this combination of location, demand and limited high-quality supply warrants closer attention.

Both cities also feature prominently in the UK’s residential growth outlook. Savills has forecast capital value growth of 24.6 per cent for Manchester and 22.2 per cent for Birmingham over its relevant forecast period.

## **Building confidence for the long term**

As investor expectations evolve, success in residential property will depend on more than identifying the next growth market. It will require a commitment to quality, professional management and a clear understanding of what makes people want to live in a place.

Our perspective is simple: confidence is built through delivery. By taking responsibility across the investment process and putting residents at the centre of the experience, we believe it is possible, and commercially beneficial, to create developments that work for both investors and the people who call them home.

Manchester and Birmingham offer exciting opportunities. The priority is to turn that potential into places, experiences and investments that stand the test of time.
