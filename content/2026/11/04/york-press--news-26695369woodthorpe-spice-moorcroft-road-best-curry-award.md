## Bangladeshi restaurant in York up for Press Best Curry House award

Woodthorpe Spice is up for a Best Curry House in York award *(Image: Woodthorpe Spice)*

A long-established restaurant, which has served the York community for more than 20 years, is up for an award.

Woodthorpe Spice, in Moorcroft Road, has been named a finalist in the Best Curry House in York 2026 award.

The restaurant, inspired by the new owner’s Bangladeshi heritage, boasts a new menu and interior.




It was taken over by three business partners in December 2025.

![The restaurant was taken over by three business partners in December 2025](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21665361.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant was taken over by three business partners in December 2025 *(Image: Woodthorpe Spice)*

Speaking about this, a representative for Woodthorpe Spice said: “Our inspiration is rooted in our Bangladeshi heritage and culture.

“We wanted to create a place where people could enjoy classic, authentic Indian and Bangladeshi curry house dishes while experiencing the flavours, traditions and hospitality that are so close to our hearts.

“As three business partners, we each bring our own individual ideas, strengths and experience to the restaurant.

“We work together closely, both front of house and behind the scenes, to ensure the business runs smoothly and that our shared values remain at its core.

“Keeping our roots and culture at the heart of everything we do is incredibly important to us.”

One of the restaurant’s most popular dishes is the Woodthorpe Special – a dish inspired by the culinary traditions of Sylhet – a city in Bangladesh.

A team member said: “The dish features a special in-house sauce, carefully combined with a variety of ingredients and spices to create a unique and memorable flavour.

“It is a dish that we are particularly proud of, as it reflects our heritage and the culinary traditions that inspire us.”

![“Keeping our roots and culture at the heart of everything we do is incredibly important to us](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21665362.jpg?type=mds-article-620)“Keeping our roots and culture at the heart of everything we do is incredibly important to us" *(Image: Woodthorpe Spice)*

They added: “One of the most distinctive features of our restaurant is our signature graffiti wall, which represents the Bengal tiger and celebrates our cultural heritage.

“It is a meaningful feature that reflects our roots while giving the restaurant its own unique identity.

“We want customers to enjoy not only the flavours of our food but also the atmosphere, surroundings and experience that make a visit to Woodthorpe Spice memorable.”

Woodthorpe Spice is run by six members of staff.

A representative said: “We believe that a strong team is at the heart of a successful restaurant, and we are very proud of the people who help make Woodthorpe Spice what it is.

“We are open seven days a week, from 5 pm to 10 pm, including Christmas and bank holidays.

“We are incredibly proud of our journey so far and look forward to continuing to welcome customers through our doors.”
