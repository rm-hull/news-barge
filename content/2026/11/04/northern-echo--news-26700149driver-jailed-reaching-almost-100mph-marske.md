A Mercedes driver went through red lights and hit kerbs on flat tyres as he led police on a 100mph chase before crashing into a field and trying to run away.

Jack Naisbett, of Woodhay Avenue, Middlesbrough, lost control of a silver Mercedes-Benz after officers used a stinger in an attempt to bring him to a stop.

Teesside Crown Court heard that, shortly after 10pm on Friday, June 12, police received reports raising concerns about a driver on High Street in Redcar.

Naisbett was spotted in the Mercedes by CCTV operators and officers attempted to stop him after he got into the vehicle.

But he drove off at speed with people nearby before continuing to exceed the speed limit, drive on the wrong side of the road, and pass through numerous red lights.

Officers deployed a stinger which deflated both of the car's front tyres.

Despite this, Naisbett continued driving, reaching speeds of almost 100mph and hitting kerbs while people were on the pavement.

A short time later, he lost control of the Mercedes at a junction, crashed through a fence and ended up in a field.

Naisbett then fled on foot before being caught and arrested following a foot chase.

The 31-year-old appeared at Teesside Crown Court on Wednesday (November 4), where he was sentenced to 42 months in prison after pleading guilty to dangerous driving, failing to stop when required by a police officer and driving without a licence or insurance.

Sergeant Chris Lindsey, from Cleveland Police's Matrix team, said: "Naisbett's actions that night showed no regard for other drivers and pedestrians who were at huge risk of being seriously injured, thankfully that didn't happen.

"Officers tried numerous times to bring him to a stop, even deploying the stinger and he continued to travel along the road at almost 100mph – it was a very dangerous situation.
