Police are investigating an arson attack in a Selby car park.

***YorkMix*** reported that the former public toilets went up in flames at the Back Micklegate Car Park, Selby, on Sunday 18 October.

It sent a large plume of thick smoke over the town.

**Video**

Firefighters were called at 4.03pm and used two hose reel jets to put it out.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for information about a fire that occurred in Back Micklegate Car Park, Selby, on Sunday 18 October.

“There was a fire in a disused block of public toilets just before 4pm, and it is believed to have been started deliberately.

“We’re particularly appealing for witnesses in the moments leading up to the fire starting who may be able to identify potential suspects.”
