A new Christmas film that was filmed in York is having its UK premiere in cinemas this Friday.

And there will be a fair few York locations to spot!

It was this time last year that TV and film stars were seen in York city centre for new Christmas movie *Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint* – and now audiences can finally see it on the big screen.

The film features Game Of Thrones star John Bradley as the titular Aubrey Flint, with *Celebrity Traitors* favourites Celia Imrie and Richard E Grant, Adjoa Andoh, Kiell Smith-Bynoe, Karl Pilkington, Nigel Planer and the voice of Jim Broadbent.

After a snow globe shopping catastrophe lands him in hot water, Aubrey Flint is forced into community service at a struggling retirement home.

Under threat from an evil property developer, Ellen rallies the residents and ropes Aubrey into a gloriously chaotic production of ‘A Christmas Carol’ in the hope of saving the site.

From the streets of York to the makeshift stage, Aubrey not only discovers the power of the local community but also rediscovers the magic of the season.

The cast and crew were seen filming in several locations last year, including Colliergate and Precentor’s Court.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Merry-Christmas-Aubrey-Flint-Exclusively-In-Cinemas-From-November6-Signature-Entertainment.webp)

One of the locations was number 5 Colliergate – which has been vacant since appliance store Blackwell & Denton shut in 2022, but is now set to become a shop and bar.

Owned by York Conservation Trust, the Grade II listed building dates from the 1500s, and had been transformed into The Miniature World Of Wonders for the scenes.

Two more York locations that can be spotted in the trailer is Bradley as Aubrey Flint walking down Grape Lane and Lower Priory Street.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/merry-christmas-aubrey-flint-trailer-2048x890.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/merry-christmas-aubrey-flint-trailer-2-2048x884.webp)

*Merry Christmas Aubrey Flint* is produced by SUMS Film & Media in association with Ripon-based Shush Films, directed by Jack Spring and written by Chris Boyle-McQuarry.

Its cinema release date is this **Friday, 6 November**. It has a showing on opening day at Cineworld York at 1:45pm – which you can book your tickets here to see York on the big screen.

You can also read a review of the film in the Radio Times and The Guardian, the former stating that ‘York deserves a credit for its inviting alleyways and flickering lights’.
