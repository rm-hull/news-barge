*(Image: Getty Images)*

A tent box was stolen from a vehicle in the early hours of the morning.

The theft took place at about 4.20am on Sunday, November 1 on New Lane in Burton Salmon, Selby.

North Yorkshire Police say the tent box was attached to a vehicle at the time.

It follows a spate of thefts earlier in the year when police urged people to secure tent boxes properly to vehicles and fit tracking devices.

Officers are now appealing for information and urging anyone who may have seen or heard anything suspicious in the area to come forward.

A police spokesperson said: "Officers are carrying out enquiries and are asking anyone who may have witnessed suspicious activity in the area, seen the tent box being removed, or who may have CCTV, doorbell, dashcam, or mobile phone footage that could assist, to come forward.

"Information can also be passed online via the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting "Tell us about" > "An existing case or report".

"Live Chat is available via the North Yorkshire Police website Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

"If you are unable to use the online options, call 101."

PC319 Nabiel Galab, the officer in charge of the investigation, can be contacted by email at [nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:nabiel.galab@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Information can also be provided anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through their website.
