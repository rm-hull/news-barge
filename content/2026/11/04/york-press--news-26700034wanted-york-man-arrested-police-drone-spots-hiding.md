A wanted man in York was captured after a drone spotted him hiding.

The man was wanted for failing to attend a court hearing on October 6, related to the theft of goods from a Morrisons store on May 22.

After spotting him on a bicycle in the Acomb area of the city, officers from the Tadcaster-based Neighbourhood Support Unit (NSU) set up a search in the area on Monday, November 2.

The man abandoned his bike and ran into a housing estate, jumping over fences and hiding in a small gap between a house and a shed.

The police drone pilot then launched their drone, which detected a heat source in the garden and maintained visual contact on the suspect while officers moved in to arrest him.

The man has been remanded into custody and will appear in court at a later date.

A police spokesman said: "This is another great example of the effective use of drone technology by North Yorkshire Police, along with our relentless determination to tackle all levels of crime, including shoplifting."
