A taste of Italy has arrived at a Bradford district market.

Pure Dough Pizza & Deli has opened at Keighley Market, serving a range of freshly made pizzas, deli sandwiches, coffee and sweet treats.

The business, run by Roy Luigi Edmondson and his team, originally operated from a converted horse trailer earlier this year before moving into units 16 and 17 at the market.

Mr Edmondson said: "Opening something more permanent was always something I have thought of doing, along with a deli offering both quality pizza and sandwiches.

"Living locally, the market community has always appealed to me.

"We pride ourselves on creating a warm, friendly atmosphere paired with great quality, freshly prepared food.

"We have had a fantastic local response and are already making lots of new friends along the way."

The menu features freshly baked schiacciata, a Tuscan bread used for Italian deli sandwiches, and in-house pizza Romano sold by the slice.

Deli options include classic fillings like Bologna and Calabria, as well as vegetarian choices such as Campagnia.

The pizza selection changes daily, offering customers new flavours with each visit, from mortadella, stracciatella cheese and pistachio pesto to prosciutto and fig.

Alongside the food, the deli serves coffee made with beans from Baildon-based roasters Casa, as well as tea, hot chocolate, and Italian pastries like cannoli and lobster tails.

A seating area is available for customers who wish to dine in.

The business operates from 9am to 4pm, Monday to Saturday, closing at 3pm on Tuesdays.

Councillor Ian Walker, executive member for neighbourhoods and communities at Bradford Council, said: "It’s fantastic to see a local business like Pure Dough Pizza & Deli making its home at Keighley Market.

"Roy and his team have brought something special to the market, combining quality, freshly made food with a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

"Independent businesses like this play an important role in keeping our markets vibrant and giving people more reasons to visit Keighley town centre.

"The response from customers has already been really positive, and I’m delighted to welcome Pure Dough Pizza & Deli to our market community and wish them every success for the future."

Pure Dough Pizza & Deli is located next to Adam’s Café.
