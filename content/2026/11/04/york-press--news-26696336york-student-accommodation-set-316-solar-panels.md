MORE than 300 solar panels could be installed on the roof of a York building under new plans.

An application lodged with City of York Council would see 316 solar panels installed on the roofs of three blocks at the Frederick House student accommodation complex, in Fulford Road.

Plans from Black Architecture Ltd lodged on behalf of accommodation operator Student Roost stated the panels could generate around 145kWp.

They added it could save an estimated £10,039 a year in energy costs.

The plans would see the panels installed on the roofs of the accommodation blocks B, C and D opposite Fulford Road Police Station.

Student accommodation blocks were built at the site after a planning application was approved in early 2020.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21667143.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Plans for the roof-mounted solar panels on Blocks B, C and D of Frederick House, in Fulford Road *(Image: Black Architecture Ltd)*

The complex covers an area of one hectare and accommodates 365 students across a mix of blocks and town houses.

The development was sold to Student Roost, owned by the firm Brookfield, in November 2020.
