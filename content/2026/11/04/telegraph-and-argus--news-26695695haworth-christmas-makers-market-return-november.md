A festive makers market is set to return to Haworth this November.

Taking place at the Old School Room on Church Street, the Haworth & Yorkshire Creatives Christmas Makers Market will run from 10am to 4pm on Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29.

The free-entry event will showcase artwork, gifts, jewellery and homeware from independent artists and makers across Yorkshire.

Visitors can expect a range of unusual items, from guitars made from recycled cigar boxes to botanical art and bespoke jewellery.

The market is curated by Rosalia Ferrara, founder of Haworth & Yorkshire Creatives and the Brontë-inspired brand Brontë Bitch.

She said: "The market champions independent creativity and gives visitors the opportunity to meet the people behind the products while supporting small businesses."

Returning for its final appearance of the year is the Featured Artist Stage, which highlights a selection of artists and their individual styles, inspirations, and creative journeys.

Among those taking part is Leeds-based Sarah Thornton, whose colourful artwork blends her love of nature, music, and dance.

Bingley artist Karen Harman will exhibit on Saturday, showcasing her commissioned pet portraits.

Nicola of Nicolour Illustration & Design will appear on Sunday, offering storybook-style illustrations.

Queensbury artist Carol Spencer will feature her detailed coloured-pencil portraits of pets and wildlife.

Ms Spencer began her pet portrait journey during the 2020 lockdown after posting a drawing of her dog Bella online.

Her work has since reached customers as far as New Zealand.

Carolyn Edwards, a professional artist of more than 25 years, will display portraiture and pop-culture-inspired artwork.

Philippa of Withens Art creates contemporary pieces inspired by Top Withens and the surrounding moorland.

Visitors will also be able to browse a curated collection of independent makers offering goods not found on the high street.

These include botanical art and terrariums from Black Crafts by CK, Brontë-inspired gifts from Brontë Bitch, and guitars made from recycled cigar boxes by Chisel Monkey.

Other featured makers include Eco Beez with recycled birdhouses and bee camps, Flawed Maud’s silversmith jewellery, and Nerfertum Skincare & Aromatherapy’s organic products.

The market will also feature handmade accessories, natural skincare, and sustainable creations.
