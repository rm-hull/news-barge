Edinburgh property management firm Trinity Factoring Services Limited has marked its 50th anniversary with the purchase of a new headquarters at Canal Court.

The company, which manages around 15,000 properties across Scotland, has acquired the entire office space on Craiglockhart Avenue to bring its Edinburgh team together under one roof.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669247.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Canal Court office space on Craiglockhart Avenue, Edinburgh. *(Image: Supplied)*

Supported by a Building Transition Loan from Bank of Scotland, the move will allow Trinity to consolidate its two existing Edinburgh offices into a single, larger base and create room for future expansion.

The Canal Court premises comprise two identical buildings with a combined floor space of 11,000 sq ft, situated beside the Union Canal.

One building will accommodate Trinity’s 50 office-based employees, while the second is occupied by charity Children’s Hospices Across Scotland (CHAS), which will remain as a long-term tenant.

The company also employs 25 field staff and operates an office in Rosemount, Aberdeen, supporting its property portfolio in the north and north-east of Scotland.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669245.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alasdair Seale, Managing Director at Trinity Factoring Services Limited. *(Image: Supplied)*

Trinity plans to let its existing Edinburgh offices to generate additional income.

The business began with managing director Alasdair Seale renting out lock-up garages at the age of 15. The venture developed into property management, lettings and insurance services, with the company remaining family-run. Seale’s son is now its operations manager, alongside a chief executive.

Alasdair Seale, managing director of Trinity Factoring Services Limited, said:"Reaching 50 years is something we’re incredibly proud of, particularly when we look at how the business has developed from where it started.

"But we’re equally focused on what comes next.

"Bringing our Edinburgh team together will make a big difference to how we work, while giving us the space to continue growing and creating opportunities for people to build careers within our business."

Ross Penman, relationship manager at Bank of Scotland, said: "Trinity has built an impressive business over almost five decades, combining steady growth with a clear commitment to its people and customers.

"The purchase of Canal Court gives the team a long-term base that can support its future ambitions, while the building’s energy efficiency made it a strong fit for our Building Transition Loan.

"We’re pleased to support Alasdair and the team as they invest in the next chapter of the business."
