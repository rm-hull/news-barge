Aldi will reopen its Dunston store ahead of Christmas after a major refurbishment.

The Ellison Road supermarket will reopen on November 19 following a temporary closure for upgrades.

It is one of eight Aldi stores across the UK currently undergoing improvements as part of the retailer’s £300 million investment programme, which they say will modernise its existing stores.

Jonathan Neale, managing director of national real estate at Aldi UK, said: "We’re delighted to be welcoming customers back to these newly upgraded stores in time for Christmas.

"Whether it’s creating more space, refreshing key shopping areas or introducing more energy-efficient technology, we’re continuing to invest in our stores to make them even better for the communities they serve."

The Dunston branch, along with seven other UK stores, will reopen with new improvements just in time for Christmas shopping season.
