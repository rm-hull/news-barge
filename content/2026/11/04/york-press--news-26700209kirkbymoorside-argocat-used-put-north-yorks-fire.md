North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue were called out to tackle a forest fire in Wykeham Forest earlier today.

Both crews from Scarborough alongside Kirkbymoorside's crew were called out at 8.08am.

The Kirkbymoorside Argocat- a highly specialised amphibious all-terrain vehicle was also mobilised.

Crews used wildfire tools, a hose reel jet, high pressure lance and a thermal imagining camera to extinguish the fire and monitor the area for any hotspots.
