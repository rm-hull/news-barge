A robotics company has appointed a new commercial director to drive its next phase of growth.

Harrogate-based Synthotech Group has welcomed Andy Vine to the role as it targets further expansion in UK and international utility markets.

Mr Vine said: "I'm joining Synthotech at such an exciting stage in the company's journey.

"The business has built a strong reputation for developing innovative technologies that solve real challenges for utility companies, helping them improve network performance while reducing disruption for customers and communities."

His appointment follows a record year in which the company saw turnover rise 33 per cent to £6.5 million, with profits doubling year-on-year.

Mark Tindley, managing director of Synthotech Group, said: "Andy's experience across the utility sector, combined with his leadership and commercial expertise, will be invaluable as we continue to scale the business, strengthen customer relationships and expand into new markets."

Synthotech develops robotic technologies for utility infrastructure.
