Isle of Lewis music festival HebCelt has been shortlisted for three awards at this year’s National Outdoor Events Association (NOEA) Awards.

The festival is in contention for Regional Event of the Year and Best Accessibility and Inclusion Initiative, while its operations co-ordinator, Carol Ferguson, has been nominated for Best Unsung Heroine.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669723.jpg?type=mds-article-620)HebCelt festival. *(Image: Supplied)*

The nominations recognise the festival’s contribution to the islands, its efforts to improve accessibility and inclusion, and the work of the people behind the event.

Other Scottish nominees in this year’s awards include Tiree Festival and the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games, which featured the King’s Baton during its visit to HebCelt last summer.

Winners will be announced at a ceremony in Bath on November 25.

Carol Ferguson, festival operations co-ordinator, said: "These three nominations mean so much to us.

"We are absolutely delighted and incredibly proud of everyone who makes our festival what it is.

"HebCelt is so much more than three days of music – it is a celebration of our islands, our culture, our community and the people who make it all happen, so to make the Regional shortlist is incredibly special.

"Being nominated for Best Accessibility and Inclusion Initiative is particularly poignant for us and is an area that is incredibly close to our hearts."

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21669722.jpg?type=mds-article-620)HebCelt festival. *(Image: Supplied)*

Ms Ferguson added: "We are continually striving to make HebCelt more accessible and inclusive across every aspect of the festival, from our programming and volunteering opportunities to the experience of our audience.

"For me personally, to be nominated has come as such an amazing surprise.

"But we simply couldn’t do what we do without all the incredible people who give their time, energy and passion to our festival and, of course, our amazing audience."

A spokesperson for the National Outdoor Events Association said: "Together with categories recognising safety, innovation, education and unsung individuals, the 2026 shortlist puts the spotlight on an industry whose achievements reach from the international stage to the heart of local life."
