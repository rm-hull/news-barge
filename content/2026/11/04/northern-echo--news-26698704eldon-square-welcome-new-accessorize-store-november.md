A popular British fashion retailer is set to open a new North East store ahead of the festive season.

Accessorize will officially launch its fourth North East store at Newcastle’s Eldon Square next Friday (November 13), marking the occasion with a range of exclusive treats for shoppers.

The store will offer the brand’s full range of accessories, gifting items and winter collections, including bags, jewellery, knitwear, scarves and festive gifts.

It will also feature changing rooms and two dedicated piercing spaces, including a private studio for a more personal experience.

The new 5,167 sq ft unit will be located on St Andrew’s Way, in the Upper Mall between Vodafone and KIKO Milano.

Vicki Baker, retail director at Accessorize and Monsoon, said the brand had put a "lot of thought into making this new space feel special," with the new piercing studios and store layout.

Baker added that it was a great time open and "be part of Newcastle's shopping community" ahead of Christmas.

The first 50 Eldon Square customers will receive a complimentary goody bag with purchases of £35 or more

Customers can also enjoy free piercings with selected jewellery purchases, with free gift embroidery available throughout the opening weekend (November 13 to 15), offering shoppers the chance to personalise their Christmas presents.

Baker said: "We're so excited to open our new store at Eldon Square and welcome Newcastle shoppers through our doors.

"Accessorize has always been about those little things that make a big difference, whether it's finding a bag you love, adding a little something special to your everyday or picking out the perfect gift for a loved one.

"We've been bringing fresh energy to the brand, from our collections to the way our stores look and feel."

Accessorize is known for its colourful and creative approach to style, offering everything from jewellery and handbags to hair accessories and seasonal gifts.

The new store is Accessorize’s fourth in the North East, joining existing locations in Durham’s Prince Bishops, Newcastle Airport and the Metrocentre.

Their Eldon Square site will sit at the heart of one of the North East's most popular shopping centres, joining more than 100 other retailers ahead of the busy Christmas period.

Helen Cowie, centre director at Eldon Square, said: "We're really looking forward to Accessorize unveiling its new store at Eldon Square ahead of the festive season.

"The refreshed space will offer even more choice for our visitors, while a fantastic line-up of opening-weekend activities, including goody bags, complimentary piercings and free embroidery, will make it an unmissable visit."

Cowie added: "Eldon Square is the perfect destination to find everything you need for Christmas."

Opening-day offers will be available while stocks last.
