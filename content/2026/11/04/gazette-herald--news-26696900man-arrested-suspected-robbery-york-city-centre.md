A man has been arrested following a suspected robbery in York city centre.

The incident took place in St Sampson's Square at about 3.50am on Saturday, October 24, and left the alleged victim needing medical treatment.

The 28-year-old suspect was arrested shortly afterwards and was held in custody for questioning before being remanded by other forces.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said the man was also being investigated for a separate suspected fraud offence after officers found a stolen bank card in his possession.

**Recommended reads:**

"We are appealing for witnesses and information following a reported robbery in York city centre at around 3.50am.

"The victim of the robbery, a man aged in his 20s, required medical treatment following an altercation with another man on St Sampson's Square."

Police are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident or noticed suspicious behaviour nearby.

This includes reports of two men approaching people about mobile phones, possibly offering controlled substances, and the later discovery of missing devices or bank cards.

One of the suspects is described as black, while the other was wearing dark clothing and a cap.  
  
Anyone who can help the investigation in any way is urged to call North Yorkshire Police on 101 or contact Crimestoppers anonymously via their website or by calling 0800 555 111.
