Two suspected burglars were chased down and arrested after allegedly being caught breaking into an empty County Durham home.

Officers were patrolling Stanley in the early hours of Saturday (October 31), when they were alerted to an ongoing burglary in South Moor, where two men had reportedly been seen breaking into an unoccupied house.

Durham Roads and Armed Policing officers rushed to the scene and disturbed the suspects in the act.

The pair attempted to flee but were chased by officers before being caught and arrested on suspicion of burglary.

The men, aged 24 and 26, allegedly gave false details to police but were later identified.

Police said checks then revealed one of the men was wanted by Police Scotland, while the other was a registered sex offender who was wanted on recall to prison.

Both men were taken into custody.

A spokesperson from Durham Police said: “Top tip – you really can’t outrun a motorbike.”

An investigation into the incident remains ongoing.
