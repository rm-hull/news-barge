North Yorkshire Police have issued a CCTV image of a person they wish to speak to regarding a theft in York city centre.

The incident took place at Tesco on Goodramgate on October 13.

Police are urging anyone who recognises the individual captured on CCTV to come forward, as they may have information crucial to the investigation.

PC1488 Bell can be contacted via email at [joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:joanne.bell@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
