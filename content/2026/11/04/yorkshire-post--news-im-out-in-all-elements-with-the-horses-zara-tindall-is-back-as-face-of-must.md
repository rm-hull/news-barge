# 'I'm out in all elements with the horses': Zara Tindall is back as face of Musto Country clothing AW26 range

## Zara Tindall is back as ambassador for British countywear brand Musto in a renewed partnership marking the launch of Musto Country’s campaign Back Where We Belong.

Zara has spent much of her life in the saddle, competing at the highest level of the sport, including at the Olympic Games and World Equestrian Games.

“I've worn Musto for many years, first introduced to them through sailing as a child,” she says.

“I've partnered with Musto for nearly 20 years, across many different collections, including my own. Their commitment to innovation, quality and performance hasn't changed in that time.

![Zara Tindall wears: Prices across the Musto collection range from £25 for beanies to £300 for outerwear, with a selection of mid-layers completing the collection.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJhMTNmNGE0LTg1MTMtNGY1MS1hMGI2LTg0OTZhYjVmNmM1NDo1MzZlZTdmMy01ZGU1LTRiNWMtYTk0MC03NTUwOTYxNDVhMzA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“I'm out in all elements with the horses and various family activities, it's so important for me and my team to be warm and dry, which Musto does so well.”

The Back Where We Belong campaign was shot against the backdrop of the British countryside, created to evoke the quiet pace and timeless beauty of rural life while showcasing technical performance, functionality and considered design.

Hero pieces featured include Musto’s Burnham and Fenland coats, alongside the Suffolk Riding Coat. At musto.com.
