# Raheim Jackson murder investigation latest as police issue major update

## Leeds police issue major Raheim Jackson murder update

Murder detectives have detained a 39-year-old man following the death of Raheim Jackson in Leeds last month.

Raheim 'Reema' Jackson died in Hedley Chase on Saturday, October 24. Three men have since been arrested.

In an update on Wednesday, officers say they have arrested a 39-year-old man on suspicion of murder and he remains in police custody. A 26-year-old man arrested on suspicion of murder on Sunday has been released under investigation.

Michael Gyedu, of Rathmell Road, Leeds, has been charged with murder and appeared at Leeds Magistrates' Court on Monday. Gyedu was arrested in London by the Met Police on October 30.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesman said: "Detectives investigating the murder of Raheim Jackson on Hedley Chase in Leeds on Saturday (24 October) have arrested a further man.

"A 39-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of murder and remains in police custody. A 26-year-old man arrested on suspicion of murder on Sunday has been released under investigation.

"Enquiries into Raheim’s death remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team. Anyone with information which can assist the investigation is asked to contact us via 101 or use the Live Chat options here, quoting reference 13260606745

"Information can always be given anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 11 or via online."
