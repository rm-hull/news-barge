*Inspired York Christmas Show returns to York Cemetery Chapel, reports MAXINE GORDON*

SHOPPERS looking for unique Christmas gifts and festive inspiration are being invited to a special showcase of local creativity as Inspired York celebrates the 20th anniversary of its annual Christmas show.

The popular event will take place at the York Cemetery Chapel on November 7 and 8, bringing together a collection of artists and makers from across the region.

Jeweller Jo Bagshaw, one of the group's founding members, said: “I can't believe it's our 20th year of our show. We are always surprised at how quickly our event comes round each year.”

This year's exhibition will feature returning artist Wilf Williams, who is known for creating hand-carved wooden birds, unique wooden objects and bespoke furniture.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671213.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work by Wilf Williams

Visitors will also be able to meet two guest artists. Textile artist Galina Titova will showcase her printed silk scarves, while mixed media artist Jackie Lunn will display handmade books and felt pictures.

Alongside the guest exhibitors, the show's core group of artists and makers will be presenting a wide range of handcrafted work. Adi French will exhibit original paintings, while Petra Bradley will display purses and bags made from vintage fabrics.

Printmaker Sally Clarke will bring her atmospheric collagraph prints, and leather artisan Naomi Wells Smith will showcase handcrafted bags, wallets and keyrings.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671220.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Galina Titora scarf

Ceramicist Andrea Cundell will present decorative vases and tea holders, while jeweller Richard Whitelegg will display collections of silver and gold jewellery.

Jo Bagshaw will also be showing and selling her contemporary silver jewellery designs.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671216.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jo Bagshaw jewellery

Organisers say the event offers residents the chance to buy original artwork directly from its creators, meet local artists and support independent businesses in the run-up to Christmas.

For more information, visit Inspired York on Facebook.
