## York’s newest grocery market announces opening date

MAE is set to open a brand-new market space in Holgate Road *(Image: MAE)*

A new grocery store promising a "Yorkshire Dales meets M&S" shopping experience is set to open in York this month.

MAE Market is opening in Holgate Road on Saturday (November 21).

It comes just over a year since mother-daughter duo Laura Mae Bowes and Joanne opened the doors to MAE café, just a few doors down, on August 16, 2025.




The business owners are currently renovating the former CAMEO Engraving shop, which moved to new premises in Clifton Moor in March.

The grocery market will sell fresh, organic food, including quality meats, along with home essentials.

MAE’s meals, a new food service, where people can grab a full meal freshly prepared by the company’s inhouse chef will also be introduced from the shop.

Laura said: "We have had so much success with the café that we decided to build on our brand.

"The shop will sell fresh, organic supplies, along with essentials, and we hope to introduce MAE's meals - a service prepared by our wonderful in-house chef.

"We want these meals to a provide convenient, healthy alternative, which can be purchased and ordered online."

“The overall vibe will by Yorkshire Dales meets M&S – and we’re so excited.”

Announcing the opening date on social media, the MAE team said that the grocery store would bring together all your kitchen essentials in one place.

A statement read: “From fresh produce, quality meats, household essentials, cheese to breads, MAE meals, juices, coffee and carefully to make everyday shopping feel a little more special.

“Thoughtful, seasonal and beautifully sourced; whether you’re picking up dinner, stocking the fridge or simply calling in for something good.”

MAE got the keys to the former CAMEO Engraving shop in September and has spent months renovating the space.

You can find out more by heading to MAE’s Instagram – meetatmae.
