A car that obstructed the pavement near a pedestrian crossing has been issued with a traffic offence notice.

Officers from Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team spotted the vehicle while on patrol in the Smithy Hill and High Street area of Wibsey.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers from Bradford South NPT Team 3 were on patrol in the Smithy Hill / High Street area of Wibsey when they observed a vehicle parked in a manner that obstructed the footpath and was within the confines of a pedestrian crossing.

"The vehicle was issued with a traffic offence notice.

"Parking irresponsibly can create dangers for pedestrians, particularly children, older people and those with mobility issues.

"It can also reduce visibility for road users and increase the risk of collisions near crossings.

"Please park considerately and legally to help keep our roads and pavements safe for everyone."
