# Leeds businesses including The Swine Bistro and Alfonsos rally to support The Rotisserie Club after PETA stunt on opening week

## Leeds-based businesses including Jasper and The Swine at Bistro have rallied in support of newly launched The Rotisserie Club after a PETA stunt outside the business on opening day.

Owners of the Leeds restaurant were left “shaken” as PETA activists staged a grotesque protest outside their newly launched chicken rotisserie on Wellington Street in Leeds.

On Tuesday November 3, activists set up a rotisserie grill on the pavement behind the restaurant.

Dressed in a chef outfit, PETA campaigns manager Reuben Skeats pretended to spit-roast blood-smeared dolls while interacting with shocked passersby.

“We have families, we are not the people to fight against,” said Luigi Ragaglia, who opened Rotisserie Club alongside Norman Tafadzwa Dungwa in late October.

![Dressed in a chef outfit, PETA campaigns manager Reuben Skeats pretended to spit-roast blood-smeared dolls while interacting with shocked passersby.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/03/15/46/DSC_9757.JPG?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He said the protesters were “still free to be out there,” but added: “it isn't helping anybody; you're just killing another small business.”

“We are a very, very small business,” he said. “It is me and Norman, and we have a couple of staff.”

“We are a local independent,” he said. “We make coffees for people who work in the office and we make food for people who work in the office.”

“I'm really concerned that they picked us on basically our first week of business,” said Luigi, “I'm shaken. I had no idea that they were going to come out here and protest.”

The protest challenged the slaughter of chickens, which according to PETA, typically takes place at six weeks old.

“Whether they have feathers or fingers, babies don't belong on a rotisserie – or on our plates,” said PETA vice president Elisa Allen. “PETA urges anyone horrified by the thought of roasting a child will extend that same compassion to other animals too.”

Video footage of the stunt was posted across social media and attracted hundreds of comments.

Leeds Steak and Cocktail Bar Jasper Leeds said “this is not right”.

“If they want to protest, why don’t they go to KFC?”, the comment reads.

Alfonsos Deli said: “Disgusting behaviour, stop targeting innocent people that are trying to make a living.”

The Swine Bistro also commented on the stunt and said: “PETA currently targeting small businesses who don’t have the clout of the big chains like McDonald’s...They are choosing to target small businesses like ours in an extremely difficult time for hospitality.”

The Rotisserie Club menu includes chicken-themed wraps, salad bowls, and wings, but does offer vegetarian alternatives.

“A new opening obviously draws a lot of attention,” said Mr Skeats. “It’s the perfect time to remind people that a new chicken restaurant isn’t happy for everyone.

“We saw plenty of shocked faces, double takes, and people stopping to see what was going on in Leeds today.”

“Our message to the business would be to opt for vegan food,” he added.
