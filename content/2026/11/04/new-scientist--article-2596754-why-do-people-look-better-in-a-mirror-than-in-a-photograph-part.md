**Kevin Wooding**
  
*Via Facebook*

Faces aren’t symmetrical and you become accustomed to the unique proportions of your mirrored impression over many years. A photograph shows your face without mirroring, and it can be unfamiliar and surprising with the two sides of your face swapped.

Advertisement

Of course, you could always spend a lot of time looking at photographs of yourself to get used to the non-reflected image of your face. Have fun with this.

**Burcu Akten**
  
*Via Facebook*

I know the answer in my own case. I have a better relationship with myself than I do with others. I’m worried about how others see me, but I’m not worried about how I see myself. So, when my photo is taken, I tense up and look uncomfortable and distressed under the gaze of others. In the mirror, I am kind to myself. So, I see a confident and happy person in the mirror, not a scared one.
