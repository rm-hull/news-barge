An "exquisite" restaurant in Yorkshire has been said to have the best Sunday roast in the whole of the UK.

Enjoy Travel has released a list of the top 25 Sunday roasts in the UK, with a spot in West Yorkshire crowned the best.

The Hawthorn can be found in the quaint village of Haworth and offers a cosy place to enjoy a roast each week.

## What is The Hawthorn like?

On the Sunday roast, Enjoy Travel said: "Attention to detail is really one of the key factors that turns a great roast into the best roast – and that's exactly the case at The Hawthorn.

"Found in the small West Yorkshire village of Haworth, the home of the Brontë sisters, The Hawthorn has been delighting diners with its award-winning Sunday roast for years.

"In fact, the Sunday roasts are so well-known and delicious at The Hawthorn that it is not uncommon to see Sundays fully booked up to six months in advance!

"The preparation for the roast begins on Wednesday, with the Head and Sous Chefs carefully preparing the gravy – proof that days' worth of care and attention go into every bite that you have."

The Hawthorn offers a "relaxed environment" for you to enjoy a meal with friends and family.

The restaurant offers seasonal dishes using ingredients sourced from the Yorkshire Dales, the Lake District, and Hartlepool.

For its Sunday roast, you can choose from a huge range of meat options such as beef, smoked pancetta, chicken schnitzel, shoulder of lamb, and chateaubriand.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21663817.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The restaurant offers a cosy spot for food. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

The roasts are served with lots of vegetables, a Yorkshire pudding, gravy, wild garlic roasted potatoes, and cauliflower cheese.

You can also add on some pigs in blankets on the side for an extra £6.

To finish off your meal, you can enjoy a dessert with a range of dishes available from sticky toffee pudding and ice cream to a simple affogato.
