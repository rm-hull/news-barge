A Spitfire replica will be kept out of Durham Market Place for a second Remembrance Sunday because of safety concerns and growing crowds.

Durham County Council will display the aircraft in Millennium Place again this year, rather than return it to the Market Place.

The reproduction Spitfire had been a familiar sight in Durham Market Place for Remembrance events and Battle of Britain anniversaries before safety recommendations moved it in 2025.

With thousands expected for Remembrance Sunday on November 8, the council says the aircraft will remain in Millennium Place because of crowd-safety and insurance concerns.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21668577.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Spitfire in Durham during Remembrance Sunday in 2025 *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Gemma Ptak, head of housing, communities and leisure at Durham County Council, said: "We understand why some people would like to see the reproduction Spitfire located in the Market Place.

"However, the decision to site it in Millennium Place in 2025, and again this year, reflects recommendations from the multi-agency Safety Advisory Group, which includes representatives from our Highways team and the emergency services, as well as insurance considerations associated with the Market Place."

The Spitfire remains a symbol of Britain’s wartime history and the courage and sacrifice of RAF pilots during the Battle of Britain in 1940.

But growing crowds at Durham’s Remembrance Sunday parade have created challenges for organisers in the historic Market Place.

Ms Ptak said: "In recent years, Durham City's Remembrance Parade has grown in popularity and now attracts thousands of people, with particularly large numbers gathering in the Market Place.

"After exploring several alternative locations, Millennium Place was considered the most appropriate place for the Spitfire.

"This approach is supported by City of Durham Parish Council, which organises the Remembrance Day Parade."

The aircraft was first moved for Remembrance Sunday in November 2025, leaving its former Market Place position.

The council says the Spitfire will remain a prominent part of the city’s commemorations despite staying away from the Market Place.

Millennium Place is near the Gala Theatre, a short walk from the Market Place and on the Remembrance Parade route.

The council also highlighted last year’s arrangements, when thousands viewed the aircraft at its new location.

Ms Ptak added: "Millennium Place is only a few minutes' walk from the Market Place and forms part of the Remembrance Parade route.

"Last year thousands of people visited the Spitfire there, and we received positive feedback from members of the public.

"Our priority is to support a Remembrance Sunday event that allows people to come together to pay their respects, while ensuring the necessary safety and operational arrangements are in place."
