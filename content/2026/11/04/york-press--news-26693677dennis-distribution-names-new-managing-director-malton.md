A new managing director has been appointed to drive growth and efficiency for a Malton-based business.

Daniel Vasey has taken up the role at Dennis Distribution LLP, a temperature-controlled logistics specialist.

He brings more than 25 years of industry experience, including more than 19 years at Bowker Group, where he was general manager for UK transport.

Mr Vasey said: "I’m looking forward to building on the strong foundations of the business.

"By working closely with the team, making the best use of our fleet and embracing technology where it can make a real difference, I want to help put Dennis Distribution in a strong position for sustainable growth."

Mr Vasey will support senior partner Katie Dennis and commercial manager Andrew Revely in shaping the company’s direction and aligning daily operations with long-term growth plans.

Katie Dennis said: "Daniel brings a wealth of experience in the logistics industry, along with a strong understanding of how to run efficient, customer-focused transport operations."
