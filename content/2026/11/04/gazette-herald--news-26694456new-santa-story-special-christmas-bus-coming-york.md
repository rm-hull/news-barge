Santa is trading his reindeer for a Christmas bus in a new immersive experience heading to York.

The Santa Story Special, an interactive 90-minute adventure for families, will take place on a CitySightseeing York double-decker bus, reimagined as a festive storytelling stage.

Created by immersive theatre company Enchanticas, the experience promises live characters, music, games, and surprises as families journey through the city onboard the Christmas bus.

Suzie Vaughan, founder and creative director of Enchanticas, said: "We’re so excited to bring Story Specials to CitySightseeing.

"There’s something incredibly magical about taking children on a real journey while a story unfolds around them.

"We want children to feel as though they’ve stepped right inside the adventure – meeting characters, joining in, and never quite knowing what might happen next."

Eight shows will run over two days, with departures at 9.30am, 11.30am, 1.30pm, and 3.30pm from Exhibition Square on December 12 and 13.

Victoria Giannini, tourism manager at CitySightseeing York, said: "Everyone wants to create memories that will last a lifetime, especially for little ones at Christmas – and that’s exactly what we’re trying to achieve with these new interactive Story Specials."

Tickets cost £35 per person and are available at [https://www.yorkcitysightseeing.com/christmas](https://www.yorkcitysightseeing.com/christmas).
