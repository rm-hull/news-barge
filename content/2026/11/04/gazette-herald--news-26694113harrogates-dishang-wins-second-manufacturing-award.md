A Harrogate-based clothing manufacturer has won a top industry award for the second year in a row.

Dishang Group was named State-of-the-Art Manufacturer at the 2026 Professional Clothing Industry Association Worldwide (PCIAW) Awards, held in London.

The award recognises the company’s continued investment in industrial technology, operational agility, and sustainable production methods across its global network.

Richard Watts, managing director of Dishang (UK), said: "Winning this award for the second consecutive year is a tremendous achievement and recognition of the investment Dishang continues to make in its people, technology and manufacturing capabilities.

"This award is a testament to the hard work and commitment of our colleagues across the world, and we'd also like to thank our customers for their continued trust and partnership."

From its UK design headquarters at The Chapel in Harrogate, Dishang (UK) serves as the technical and commercial link between British and European clothing brands and the group’s wider manufacturing operations.

The UK team oversees everything from trend research and fabric sourcing to garment technology and full production runs.

Dishang Group operates more than 90 wholly owned manufacturing sites and works with 200 production partners worldwide, delivering end-to-end design and garment solutions across menswear, womenswear, activewear, tailoring, and workwear.

The company’s latest win follows significant progress since its first award in 2025.

Over the past year, Dishang expanded its Smart Digital Manufacturing Initiative, which integrates machine learning, robotic handling, and automated data monitoring into daily factory operations.

An AI-driven energy platform is now in use at 80 per cent of its manufacturing sites, helping to reduce per-unit energy consumption by 12 per cent compared to 2024.

The company also uses high-speed 3D digital sampling, AI-driven design tools, and textile-to-textile recycling technology to reduce its environmental impact and meet growing demand for certified, sustainable supply chains.
