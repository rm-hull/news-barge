Patients have praised a hospital for its outstanding care, facilities and personal approach.

Bishop Auckland Hospital, which now specialises in planned operations such as hip and knee replacements, was awarded accredited surgical hub status by NHS England earlier this year.

The hospital performed more than 3,600 operations in 2025/26, supported by specialist surgical teams and dedicated rehabilitation facilities.

Graham, a patient who returned for his second knee replacement, said: "I would choose to come to Bishop for my surgery every time.

"The service here has been absolutely brilliant.

"I had my left knee done here last year, so when I was told I needed my other knee replaced.

"I wanted to come back to Bishop Auckland Hospital without a doubt."

Another patient, Hazel, who recently underwent a knee replacement, said: "It's a very professional service they operate here.

"The staff are lovely.

"It's always been my first choice to come here.

"It's such a calm and relaxing environment on this ward, you wouldn't actually think you're in a hospital."

Suzanne Taylor, ward manager at Bishop Auckland Hospital, said: "It's fantastic for our teams to hear such positive feedback from patients.

"We know that having an operation can be worrying, so we want everyone who comes here to feel comfortable, supported and confident in the care they receive."

"We care for patients having a wide range of procedures, including hip and knee replacements, plastic surgery, and pain management treatments."

She added: "Some patients can go home the same day, while others need a little more time to recover.

"Having our dedicated physiotherapy and rehabilitation facilities here means we can give people the support they need to get moving again and return home safely.

"We're really proud of the care we provide at Bishop Auckland Hospital, and it's lovely to know that patients value it too."

Bishop Auckland Hospital is one of six accredited surgical hubs in the region.
