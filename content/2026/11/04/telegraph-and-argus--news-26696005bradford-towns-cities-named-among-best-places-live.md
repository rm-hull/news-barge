A lifestyle website has named multiple Bradford locations among eight Yorkshire towns and cities as the best places to live in the UK.

Muddy Stilettos, a women’s lifestyle platform, included Slaithwaite, Saltaire, Beverley, Dore, York, Harrogate, Ilkley, and Hebden Bridge in its 2027 'Top 260 Best Places to Live' guide, which features towns and villages across England, Scotland, and Wales.

Hero Brown, founder and editor-in-chief of Muddy Stilettos, said: "When people decide to move, they’re searching for more than just a house – they want a home, a lifestyle that works for them and their family, and if they’re strategic, a long-term investment too.

"Our 2027 'Top 260 Best Places To Live' guide will give them that extra reassurance they need to make their decision easier in these difficult economic times."

The guide offers readers insights on property, schools, culture, shopping, restaurants, and commuting, with each location independently selected by Muddy Stilettos’ editorial team.
