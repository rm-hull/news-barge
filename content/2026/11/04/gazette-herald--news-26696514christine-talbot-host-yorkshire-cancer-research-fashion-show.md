A CHARITY fashion show which will shine a spotlight on people affected by cancer in Yorkshire is set to take place today.

Yorkshire Cancer Research is set to launch its Christmas campaign at the event at its new superstore in York, with 15 people from across the region set to model outfits sourced entirely from the charity’s shops.

Each model represents the statistic that every 15 minutes, someone in Yorkshire is told they have cancer.

Christine Talbot, former ITV presenter and host of the event, said that she was delighted to host the show and support the charity after her own battle against a fast-growing type of breast cancer.

She said: "When I was told I had cancer in 2012, I was live on TV every night and very much in the public eye. The support I received from colleagues and the public was overwhelming.

"Cancer is not something you would ever wish on anyone, but it showed me a whole new level of kindness I didn't know existed."

The show will feature themed catwalks, personal speeches, and will recognise stock donors, volunteers, and supporters whose generosity helps fund research breakthroughs in Yorkshire.

More than four million items have been sold through Yorkshire Cancer Research shops since the beginning of 2026, helping to fund research and services.

Those taking part in the show include individuals rebuilding confidence after cancer, people currently undergoing treatment, families marking milestones, and those remembering loved ones.

Among the models are mother and daughter Lynn and Jemma Hall from Doncaster - who will walk together following Lynn’s cancer diagnosis - and married couple Claire Charlton and Mohan Nagaraj.

The event will also highlight the variety of pre-loved fashion available at the charity’s shops.

Tony Graham, director of retail at Yorkshire Cancer Research, said: "Funds raised through the charity's shops play a vital role in funding the talented researchers and experts working to find new and better ways to prevent, detect, and treat cancer in Yorkshire."

Every outfit on the catwalk will be curated by businesswoman Tina Boden and image consultant Jackie Crawford.

Tina said: "For more than a decade, I’ve had the privilege of supporting the work of Yorkshire Cancer Research in a variety of ways.

What first inspired me to get involved was the charity’s commitment to putting the needs of people in our region at the heart of everything it does.

"Raising awareness of the impact cancer research has on people and families across Yorkshire is so important, and the Yorkshire Cancer Research fashion show was a truly magical way to have those conversations while supporting life-saving research."

Jackie added: "As a Transformation stylist, I help people embrace positive change by using colour and personal styling to build confidence and self-esteem.

"This project has allowed me to flex my creative muscles and style charity supporters using pre-loved clothes from Yorkshire Cancer Research shops.

"As a champion of sustainable fashion, I love the idea of the new adventures that pre-loved clothes will enjoy with their new owners. It has been a truly special opportunity to help such a wonderful group of people feel confident and empowered after some of the most challenging times of their lives."
