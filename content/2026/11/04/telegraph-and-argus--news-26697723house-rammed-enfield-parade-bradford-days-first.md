Another residential property has been rammed just days after a very similar incident on an adjoining street.

West Yorkshire Police were called at 8.13pm on Tuesday, November 3 after a vehicle crashed into a house on Enfield Parade, Wibsey.

It follows our weekend report of a similar incident on a nearby street.

## What have police said?

![damaged front of residential property](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669274.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This scene on Enfield Parade today (November 4). *(Image: T&A)*

Police recorded a criminal-damage offence. No injuries were reported.

Enquiries into the incident remain ongoing, the force said.

Police urged witnesses to call 101, quoting log 1592 of November 3.

![The scene on Sunday morning](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656252.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Sunday morning *(Image: T&A)*

People on social media have speculated that the attackers targeted the wrong house first, leading to the second ramming.

![damaged front of residential property](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669273.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This scene on Enfield Parade today (November 4). *(Image: T&A)*

In the first incident, people living on Enfield Drive said they heard “two loud bangs” at around 10pm on October 31.

Locals told the T&A they believed a vehicle had deliberately hit the property, home to an elderly woman who lived alone.

![The scene on Sunday morning](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656250.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Sunday morning *(Image: T&A)*

One resident said: “I heard a couple of bangs last night after 10. I think a car has gone into the house deliberately.

“It’s a little old lady that lives by herself.

“It was horrible. I looked out of the window and didn’t see anything, I just heard two bangs.

“I’ve heard metal and I just thought it was some lads on the road.

“I thought it was a van because it was two very loud bangs.”

Residents speculated the incident may have been a case of mistaken identity, although these claims have not been confirmed by police.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669048.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A map showinge proximity of Enfield Drive and Enfield Parade *(Image: Google Maps)*

Another resident said: “I hope she’s OK. It’s just horrible. What was the need?”

Locals also said the woman who lives there was taken to hospital after the incident.

The T&A could not independently verify the claim, and her condition is unknown.

![damaged front of residential property](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21669275.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This scene on Enfield Parade today (November 4). *(Image: T&A)*

## The T&A visit the scene

When the T&A visited on Sunday morning, two police vans were at the scene and crime scene investigators were working there.

Detectives and CSI officers examined the area outside the house and spoke to residents.

The T&A contacted West Yorkshire Police for comment.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "At 8.13pm yesterday (3 November) officers were called to reports that a vehicle had crashed into a house on Enfield Parade, Bradford.

"No injuries have been reported.

"A crime of criminal damage has been recorded, and enquiries are ongoing by Bradford CID.

"Anyone who witnessed the incident is asked to call police on 101 quoting log 1592 of 3 November."
