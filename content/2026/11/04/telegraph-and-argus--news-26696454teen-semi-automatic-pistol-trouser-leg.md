A teenager was caught with a prohibited semi-automatic pistol when police officers detained him on a large estate.

Bradford Crown Court heard that Rehaan Mohammed, who had no previous convictions, was just 18 at the time and had been subjected to violence and intimidation from others in the lead up to his offending in March.

## How police found the pistol

Prosecutor Antonia Adie told the court that police officers investigating a vehicle theft had approached a Seat Ibiza with tinted windows and found Mohammed and a teenage co-accused sat in the rear.

Mohammed was avoiding eye contact and shaking and tried to resist arrest during the incident on Wansford Close in Holme Wood.

Ms Adie said when he was searched officers found the 9mm semi-automatic pistol down his trouser leg.

Mohammed, now 19 and of no fixed abode, pleaded guilty at an earlier court hearing to possession of a prohibited firearm.

His co-accused Noah Weldemichael, now 18, of Hinton Road, London, had admitted being in possession of a machete and his case was remitted back to the youth court because he was only 17 at the time of the offence.

## 'Genuinely remorseful'

Barrister Chloe Fairley, for Mohammed, said there was an element of him possessing the firearm through “a degree of intimidation” .

“It is clear from his letter and indeed from the tenor of the pre-sentence report that he is genuinely remorseful for his actions,” she added.

“He genuinely regrets his involvement and he has a genuine desire never to be in this position again.”

## Locked up for 5 years

Recorder Patrick Palmer sentenced Mohammed to five years detention in a young offenders institution.

He said the weapon had been a blank-firing handgun which had been adapted so it could fire.

“You, I am sure, are fully aware that this is a serious offence,” the judge told Mohammed.

He said he accepted that the teenager was taking steps to sort himself out and hopefully this would be his first and last custodial sentence.
