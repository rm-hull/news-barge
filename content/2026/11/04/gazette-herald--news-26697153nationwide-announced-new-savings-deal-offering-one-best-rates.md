Nationwide has launched a new savings bond offering one of the best fixed rates on the market.

The building society's latest Member Exclusive Bond offers 5.3 per cent AER/gross per year (fixed) over 30 months, with returns reserved for existing members.

Exclusively available to Nationwide members, the bond underlines the society’s focus on rewarding customer loyalty.

A customer depositing £10,000 would earn £1,381.92 in interest over the full term.

This offer comes alongside new research from Nationwide, which found that 85 per cent of banking customers believe existing customers should be rewarded at least as much as new ones.

Despite this, 59 per cent of those surveyed feel that better savings rates are only given to new customers.

The perception is strongest among younger customers, with 72 per cent of 25 to 34-year-olds believing new customers are favoured, compared to 52 per cent of over-55s.

Nearly three-quarters (73 per cent) of savers said they have felt overlooked despite their loyalty.

Richard Stocker, head of savings at Nationwide, which is headquartered in Swindon, said: "Our research shows people want loyalty to be recognised, and too many feel the best deals today are reserved for new customers.

"We don’t think being a loyal customer should mean watching the best deals go to someone else.

"That’s what being a mutual is all about – we’re owned by our members rather than shareholders, so we can focus on delivering value back to them.

"Whether that’s through exclusive products like our new Member Exclusive Bond or the £100 Fairer Share payments we’ve paid for four years running, we want our members to see a real benefit from being part of Nationwide."

Nationwide returned £1.8 billion in value to its members last year, including £1.4 billion from above-average rates and incentives.

Anna Bowes, personal finance expert at The Private Office, said: "It’s good to see a provider rewarding loyalty rather than paying the best rates to new customers only.

"We know that not enough savers regularly switch providers, so having the chance to earn a market-leading rate as a loyal customer is refreshing, especially from a high street brand."

Nationwide’s research also revealed that nearly two-thirds (63 per cent) of respondents would find a savings account exclusively available to existing customers or members appealing, rising to 71 per cent among current savers.

More than a third (35 per cent) said that better savings rates would make them feel most valued by their bank or building society.
