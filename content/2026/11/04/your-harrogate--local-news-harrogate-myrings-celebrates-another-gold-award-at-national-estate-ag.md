Harrogate estate agent Myrings is celebrating another gold award at this year’s EA Masters.

The family business has again been recognised in the Best Estate Agent Guide, with the team thanking customers for their continued support.

Myrings was founded nearly 30 years ago and said its original values remain central to the business.

A spokesperson for Myrings said:

> “When we started our family business nearly 30 years ago, we wanted to build something centred on people, shared values and a passion for doing the right thing. That’s still what drives us today.”

The company also paid tribute to its staff and the clients who continue to recommend its services.

The spokesperson added:

> “To our brilliant team, thank you for the care, energy and hard work you put into every client’s move.
>
> "And to everyone who chooses Myrings, recommends us and keeps coming back, thank you for your trust.”
