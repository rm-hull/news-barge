Amanda Owen's life in the Yorkshire Dales is set to remain on television after Channel 4 confirmed two more series following the Yorkshire Shepherdess and her family.

The broadcaster has commissioned a fifth and sixth series of Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, documenting the Owen family's continued restoration of Anty John's farmhouse in Swaledale.

But viewers may be wondering how the family's new property differs from Ravenseat, the remote farm that first made Amanda and her nine children famous.

The two properties have become central to the family's television story, with Anty John's providing the focus of their renovation project while everyday farming life continues at Ravenseat.

## What is Ravenseat Farm?

It became famous through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, which first aired in 2018 and documented the family's unusual life in one of the most isolated parts of the Yorkshire Dales.

Viewers followed the family through lambing seasons, harsh winters and the everyday challenges of running a traditional hill farm.

The Owen children became familiar faces as they helped with the animals and learned the practical skills needed for rural life.

Ravenseat continues to feature prominently in Our Farm Next Door, with the family maintaining their farming activities while restoring another property nearby.

## What is Anty John's?

Anty John's is a historic farmhouse close to Ravenseat which had fallen into disrepair before the Owen family began restoring it.

The project became the central focus of Our Farm Next Door when the programme launched in 2024.

Amanda and Clive have worked alongside their children and local tradespeople to transform the building into a family home.

The renovation has involved extensive repairs and improvements, with the programme documenting the challenges of restoring an old property in a remote rural location.

By the fourth series, work had progressed to the interior, with the family tackling painting, flooring and the creation of individual rooms.

## Has Amanda Owen moved into Anty John's?

The fourth-series finale, scheduled for November 2, features a major milestone as Amanda and her daughters prepare for their first sleepover at Anty John's.

According to the programme synopsis, it is the first time anyone has slept at the farmhouse in many decades.

The family also moves furniture collected by Amanda over the years into the building as the interior begins to take shape.

However, the first overnight stay does not necessarily mean Amanda and her children have permanently moved into Anty John's.

Channel 4 has confirmed that the restoration will continue in series five and six, as the family works to transform the property from a house into a home.

Are Amanda and Clive Owen still together?

Amanda and Clive announced their separation in 2022 after more than two decades together.

Despite their separation, they have continued working together and co-parenting their nine children.

Both feature in Our Farm Next Door, with the renovation project providing a shared focus alongside their farming responsibilities.

## What happens next at Ravenseat and Anty John's?

Channel 4 has commissioned ten more episodes of Our Farm Next Door, divided into a fifth and sixth series.

Series five will follow the family from early summer 2026 through to spring 2027.

While work continues at Anty John's, the programme will also document the family's activities at Ravenseat.

Edith, Violet and their sisters will reopen the cream tea van, while Sidney and Miles take on increasing farming duties.

Amanda will also investigate the history of Anty John's and former owner Anthony Clarkson, a 19th-century Swaledale mapmaker.

The broadcaster says all 11 members of the Owen family will feature in the new episodes.

The programme's renewal means viewers will continue to follow the family's lives across both properties as their plans for Anty John's progress.

Broadcast dates for series five and six have not yet been announced.
