Have you seen any of these men?

These images are taken from CCTV camera footage and may show suspects or witnesses West Yorkshire Police officers wish to speak to.

Each individual is wanted in connection with a crime, and police believe identifying them could help progress ongoing inquiries.

Police warn the public not to approach anyone shown in the images and to contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 instead.

## Why are they being sought?

Person 1 (top left) is sought in connection with an alleged burglary in Bradford City on October 31, 2026. Ref: BD9152.

Person 2 (top right) is sought in connection with an alleged burglary in Bradford City on October 31, 2026. Ref: BD9153.

Person 3 (bottom left) is sought in connection with an alleged theft of a vehicle in Bradford East on October 22, 2026. Ref: BD9151.

Person 4 (bottom right) is sought in connection with an alleged serious offence in Bradford South on October 28, 2026. Ref: BD9150.

## What the police said

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Our CCTV gallery of people who have been caught on camera – do you recognise anyone?

“The identities of those shown are sought in connection with enquiries into the described alleged offences.

“Images may be of both potential suspects or witnesses.

“Members of the public should not approach anyone who they believe to be displayed in the images but should contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.”
