TWO bands are teaming up for a night of 90s nostalgia.

Kula Shaker and Reef will perform in Halifax as part of the Live at The Piece Hall series, taking to the stage on Saturday, August 21.

The show will celebrate the 30th anniversary of their breakthrough albums, with both bands performing K and Glow in full.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "Who wouldn’t want a time machine to take them back to the heady days of Britpop, and this show is the next best thing.

"With two classic albums in full from two major artists I feel this gig will have a real festival energy.

"We got a taster of Gary from REEF in action at Britpop Classical this year, so we can’t wait to welcome him back with his full band.

"Add to that the unique sound of Kula Shaker who also have the full original line up and this show has the makings of a really special event so get it in your calendar now."

Other artists confirmed for the 2027 Live at The Piece Hall series include Courteeners, Basement Jaxx, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kraftwerk, Pete Tong Ibiza Classics, Rudimental, Bananarama, and Simply Red.
