A dad has scooped a £4 million mansion in North Yorkshire - and said the last thing he won was a dead goldfish.

Malcolm Disson, 58, now owns a Grade II-listed six-bedroom home in Little Ouseburn, near Linton-on-Ouse, with its own lake, tennis court and two-bedroom cottage.

The lucky father-of-two, from Newbury, has won the Omaze Million Pound House Draw plus £250,000 in cash.

Malcolm said the only thing he can remember winning before was a goldfish at Hampstead Heath fair when he was 12.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21681522.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Malcolm Disson at his new £4m North Yorkshire home *(Image: Omaze / Mark Field Photography)*![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21681524.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The six-bedroom home has its own lake, tennis court and separate two-bedroom cottage*(Image: Omaze / Mark Field Photography)*

He won it by bouncing ping pong balls into goldfish bowls but it was already dead when he opened the bag.

Two weeks ago, Malcolm won £8 on a lottery and said he thought his "luck was finally starting to turn".

Malcolm's new home comes mortgage-free, with all stamp duty and legal fees covered, as well as £155,000 worth of furnishings.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21681523.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Malcolm Disson at his new £4m North Yorkshire home *(Image: Omaze / Mark Field Photography)*![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21681527.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tennis court at the £4 million Omaze Million Pound House won by Malcolm Disson*(Image: Omaze / Mark Field Photography)*

His winning entry cost £30 as part of his Omaze subscription.

Malcolm said: "I certainly didn't expect to go all the way from a dead goldfish to a £4 million mansion with its own lake full of fish that are alive and well!"

Malcolm, dad to Lilly, 22, and Daisy, 18, said the win has "transformed my daughters’ futures", adding: "It’s a wonderful feeling to know they’re going to be secure no matter what.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21681525.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the £4 million Grade II listed six-bedroom home won by Malcolm Disson *(Image: Omaze / Mark Field Photography)*

"It's taken any pressure off. I can think about relaxing a bit more and perhaps retiring earlier, but I love what I do and I don't think I'll ever stop working."

Malcolm, who grew up in London, said he had never been to Yorkshire before visiting his new home.

He has since enjoyed visiting York and is looking forward to trying the local pubs.

"What a way to visit for the first time, coming to your own £4 million house!" he said. "I can't wait to try the local pubs, and beers.

"I had tennis lessons as a kid and played quite a bit around 40 years ago, so I'll have to dust off my backhand and work on my serve.

"I can't believe I've got a potting shed and a library too - I haven't had much time to read, but I'll make time now. That’s what this win will give me, more time to spend with family and loved ones."

Malcolm co-founded the British Shop, a specialist fine art and antiques packing and international shipping company based near Heathrow, in 2004.

"I spend my working life shipping fine art and antiques for people with incredible houses," he said. "Never in a million years did I think I'd end up with a house fit for one of my clients, it feels very surreal."
