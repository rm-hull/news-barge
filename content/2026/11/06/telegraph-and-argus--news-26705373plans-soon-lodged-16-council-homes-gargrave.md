PLANS to build council housing on a former care home site will soon be lodged with the planning authority.

An exhibition of the plans for the former site of the Neville House Care Home in Neville Crescent, in Gargrave, near Skipton, took place in the village earlier this year, heard Wednesday's meeting of Gargrave Parish Council.

The scheme for 16 homes, including flats, bungalows and houses, was largely well received but with comments that the homes be prioritised for local people.

It is expected all the homes will be available for social rent, which is government subsidised, or shared ownership.

Councillor Simon Myers, who represents Mid Craven on North Yorkshire Council, said: "The planning application for the new council homes in the village should be going in soon, and I think there will be for the first letting a local connection requirement, we do know that there is a need.

"I think generally the plans were well received, the only negative comments I heard about was that there was not a priority for local people, I think we may have achieved that and I am keen to see this crack on."

The application will be considered by North Yorkshire Council.

The care home closed three years ago with the remaining six residents moved to Ashfield Care Home in Skipton.
