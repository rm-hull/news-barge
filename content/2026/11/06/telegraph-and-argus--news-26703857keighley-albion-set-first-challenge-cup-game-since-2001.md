KEIGHLEY Albion look set to play in the Challenge Cup for the first time in 25 years.

But their premature announcement has left the RFL looking a little red-faced.

The sport’s governing body have still not revealed much, if anything, about the upcoming 2027 season.

London Broncos look set to be named as Super League’s 15th and final team, but there has been no confirmation as yet.

According to Love Rugby League, the Championship may be split into two conferences of 10 teams, which would include the return of Featherstone Rovers to the sport.

The likely make-up of those conferences would see all the Yorkshire sides, including Keighley Cougars, grouped together, along with outliers Newcastle Thunder.

Meanwhile, Cougars’ neighbours appear to have let the cat out of the bag.

There have been reports that the Challenge Cup will also look a little different this year, with possible staggered entry for Super League teams.

But before the big boys enter the tournament, community clubs get the chance to shine.

No details had been confirmed by the RFL about next year’s tournament, from dates to the identity of teams entering.

But Albion, who won the National Community Rugby League Yorkshire A title in 2026, gave the game away this week.

In a now-deleted Facebook post, they said the draw for round one would take place on Monday, November 30.

On top of that, they also said those games would be played on the weekend of January 16–17.

Assuming Albion do take their place in next season’s Challenge Cup, and those dates are correct, they will be playing their first match in the competition for over a quarter of a century.

Their last match in the tournament came in December 2001, where they were knocked out with a 44–10 defeat to Eastmoor in the first round.

Their last win came in the 2000 Challenge Cup, where they beat Simms Cross 17–10 in their opener before losing to Stanley.

That year saw Bradford win the trophy for the first time since 1949.
