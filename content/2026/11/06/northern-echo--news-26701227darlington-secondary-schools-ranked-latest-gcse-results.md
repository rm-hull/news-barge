The performance of secondary schools across Darlington in the latest GCSE results has been revealed in new Department for Education figures.

The provisional figures for the 2025/26 academic year show how schools across the borough performed using the Government's Attainment 8 measure, which tracks pupils' average achievement across eight GCSE qualification slots.

Carmel College emerged as Darlington's highest-performing mainstream secondary school, recording an Attainment 8 score of 52.7.

![A GCSE exam](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21271273.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A GCSE exam *(Image: Ben Birchall/PA WIRE)*

The school finished ahead of Hummersknott Academy, which achieved 50.7, while Polam Hall School completed the top three with a score of 45.9.

The latest figures mean Carmel College and Hummersknott Academy were the only two mainstream secondary schools in Darlington to record Attainment 8 scores above 50.

Hurworth School finished fourth in the borough's rankings, recording 42.4, narrowly ahead of Haughton Academy, which achieved 42.3.

St Aidan's Church of England Academy followed with an Attainment 8 score of 41.6, while Wyvern Academy recorded 41.3.

Longfield Academy completed the rankings with an Attainment 8 score of 39.8.

The figures also reveal differences in the proportion of pupils achieving grade 5 or above in both English and mathematics.

Hummersknott Academy recorded the highest proportion among the eight schools, with 58.1 per cent of pupils reaching the benchmark.

Carmel College followed with 54 per cent, while Haughton Academy achieved 47.2 per cent.

At St Aidan's Church of England Academy, 41.3 per cent of pupils achieved grade 5 or above in English and maths, compared with 40.4 per cent at Polam Hall School.

Hurworth School recorded 36.1 per cent, Longfield Academy achieved 33.5 per cent, and Wyvern Academy recorded 29.3 per cent.

Attainment 8 measures pupils' achievement across eight qualification slots, including English, mathematics, three English Baccalaureate subjects and three further approved qualifications.

A higher score indicates stronger average attainment in the qualifications included in the measure.

However, the figures do not directly measure how much progress pupils have made during secondary school, nor do they provide a complete assessment of the quality of education at an individual school.

The Department for Education's data also includes special schools and independent special schools, which have been excluded from the main rankings because of differences in their educational provision and pupil intakes.

The results are provisional and remain subject to revision.

* 1 Carmel College 52.7
* 2 Hummersknott Academy 50.7
* 3 Polam Hall School 45.9
* 4 Hurworth School 42.4
* 5 Haughton Academy 42.3
* 6 St Aidan's Church of England Academy 41.6
* 7 Wyvern Academy 41.3
* 8 Longfield Academy 39.8

*\*All data is correct as of November 5, 2026, and is available in the latest Department for Education figures. Just because these 30 schools rank highest doesn't mean there are no worst-ranked schools, because numerous other indicators measure school performance. This list is purely based on the Government's Attainment 8 measure*
