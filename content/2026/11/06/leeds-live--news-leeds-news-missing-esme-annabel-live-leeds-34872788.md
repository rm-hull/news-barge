A search has been launched for missing girls from Leeds.

Police are scouring the city for the missing girls - one aged 12 and the other 15. Officers say it is suspected that the pair are together.

West Yorkshire Police posted: "Have you seen Esme and Annabel? Both are missing from Leeds and suspected to be together. They were reported missing yesterday (Thursday 5 November), and enquiries have been ongoing to locate them since.

"Esme is 12 years old, and described as white, approximately 5ft 3ins, of a slim build with brown eyes and mousey brown long wavy hair with slight pink through it. She is known to wear vest tops, jogging bottoms and a beige basketball type jacket.

"Annabel is 15 years old, she is described as white, 5ft 8ins, of a slim build with long blonde hair. She has pierced ears and is believed to be wearing grey jogging bottoms, Adidas campus shoes and a reversible jacket which could be black or navy.

"Anyone who has seen them is asked to contact police on 101 or online here. For Esme, please reference log 2060 of 5/11, and for Annabel use reference 2166 of 5/11."
