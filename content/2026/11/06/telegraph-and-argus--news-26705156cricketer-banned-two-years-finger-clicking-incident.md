Former Bradford League cricketer Brian Devine has been banned for two years after an on-field deception sparked global headlines.

Brian Devine, nicknamed "Clicky Ponting," was caught clicking his fingers at first slip during a match for Saltburn Cricket Club’s second XI, in an attempt to convince the umpire that the batsman had edged the ball.

The incident took place in the North Yorkshire and South Durham Cricket League, which launched an investigation into the matter.

Teesside Live reports that Devine has been banned from all forms of cricket for two years.

Saltburn Cricket Club was also fined £300, while its second XI was relegated to the third division and will start next season with a 60-point deduction.

In a statement reported on gazettelive.co.uk, Saltburn Cricket Club said: "The club fully participated in the disciplinary proceedings and accept the decision of the appeal following the original decision from the league.

"We are now focusing on preparations for the 2027 season."

Devine has played for Saltburn for several years, but during a brief spell away from the club, he captained Pudsey St Lawrence to the Bradford Premier League Second XI Premier Division title in 2023.

The nickname “Clicky Ponting” refers to former Australia captain Ricky Ponting.

Saltburn had previously suspended Devine and conceded a cup tie against Marton while the North Yorkshire and South Durham Cricket League investigated the incident.
