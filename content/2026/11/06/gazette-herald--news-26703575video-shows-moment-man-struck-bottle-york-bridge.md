This is the moment a man was struck with a glass bottle on a York bridge.

David Shaffi, 40, hit the man twice on the head with a glass bottle then struck him in the chest on Ouse Bridge on April 25, 2025.

Shaffi proceeded to throw the glass bottle in the direction of the man after the attack.

York Crown Court heard Shaffi launched the attack in response to the man assaulting him the previous evening.

Shaffi, of Station Road, Castleford, was later charged and pleaded guilty to Section 20 wounding.

Judge Simon jailed Shaffi for 18 months suspended for two years at York Crown Court on Thursday (November 5).

He also ordered that Shaffi completes an offending behaviour course.
