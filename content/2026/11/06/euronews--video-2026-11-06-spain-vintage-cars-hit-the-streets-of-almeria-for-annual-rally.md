Among the highlights was a **rare 1905 Vermorel** belonging to local collector Pepe Moreno, a vehicle normally displayed in a museum in nearby El Ejido.

A **century-old Franklin** also drew attention, alongside an elegant**early 1950s Chevrolet**, a** late 1920s Nash**,** vintage Rolls-Royce and Mercedes-Benz models**, and a** DeLorean DMC-12**, the futuristic sports car immortalised by the Back to the Future films.

Spectators watched as owners started their historic engines and drove through the city centre, offering a glimpse of motoring history spanning several generations. For the owners, the rally is as much about sharing their passion as preserving these remarkable machines.

Running from **5 to 9 November**, the rally brings together enthusiasts from across Spain for a** journey through the landscapes and villages of Almería province**, including stops in Enix, Terque and Alsodux. The route also takes participants to** Fort Bravo**, the famous Western film sets in the Tabernas desert,** combining automotive heritage with a tour of southern Spain's cinematic scenery**.
