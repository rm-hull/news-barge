A Harrogate-based engineering consultancy has gone into liquidation with debts topping £70,000.

Yorkshire Consulting Engineers was incorporated in 2012 and entered a creditors voluntary liquidation on October 21.

Leeds-based Clough Corporate Solutions were appointed liquidators.

Companies House records the directors of the engineering consultancy as John Charles Grainger, Andrew John Kirkham and Robert James Gill.




* **Horsebox Window Centre Limited of Harrogate in liquidation**
* **Red Kite Recruitment Group of Harrogate in liquidation**
* **FK Building Ltd of Harrogate in liquidation owning over £3m**
* **Zelst Ltd of Harrogate in liquidation owing over £400,000**

A report known as a statement of affairs outlined the finances of the company, reporting it had realisable assets of £10,565.

The report said Yorkshire Consulting Engineers had no preference creditors – those who are paid first.

HMRC was a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next – which was owed £9,924, leaving a surplus of £641 for distribution to unsecured creditors.

There were unsecured creditors – those who are paid last – owed a total of £71,137.

These were made up of a bank owed £20,811.

A credit card company was owed £17,747.

HMRC was owed £13,136.

A lease company was owed £9,555.

An insurance company was owed £7,706.

Three trade creditors were owed £702 between them.

An accountant was owed £480.

This left an estimated deficiency of £70,489.

With the company having issued and called up capital of £420, this left a final deficit or deficiency of £70,916.
