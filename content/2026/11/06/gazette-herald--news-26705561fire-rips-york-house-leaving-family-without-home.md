A fire has ripped through a York home leaving a family without accommodation.

Portia Baker told The Press her four sons, aged 14–19, and the family dog were at home in Constantine Avenue, Tang Hall, when the blaze broke out on Thursday night (November 5).

She said “everyone is fine” but the fire has destroyed the entire first floor and the smoke damage has left the council-owned property uninhabitable.

An investigation has been launched into the cause of the blaze after fire crews were called to the scene at about 8.10pm. A fire investigation officer attended the home on Friday morning to start the probe.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21679136.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A fire investigation officer at the house in Constantine Avenue, Tang Hall, on Friday morning (November 6) *(Image: Dylan Connell)*

Portia, 40, who has lived in Constantine Avenue for 14 years, said City of York Council is working to find her alternative accommodation while her house is repaired.

Her friend Becky Smith has set up a fundraising page to raise money for the family.

Becky said Portia and her children have been left with “absolutely nothing but the clothes on their backs” after the blaze.

Portia, who works as a carer, said she was on shift when the fire broke out.

“I got a phone call [from her son] while I was at work saying, ‘Mum, the house is on fire’,” she said. “When the [fire service] did come, they said the fire had been going on for a lot longer than [the family thought].

“One of my boys was in bed asleep so the boys had to run upstairs and wake him up.

“I don’t know how it started – all I can think of is an electrical fault or a charger.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21679120.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The home in Constantine Avenue, Tang Hall, which has been destroyed after a fire on Thursday night (November 5) *(Image: Dylan Connell)*

Portia said the fire has destroyed the home’s bedrooms on the first floor.

She said the worst of the damage appeared to be in her own bedroom at the front of the house, adding that her clothes and Christmas presents she bought have all been destroyed.

“It’s all gone,” she said. “[The fire service] said my bedroom is not salvageable whatsoever ... There’s not one thing left in it.”

She praised the community in Constantine Avenue for rallying around the family to help after the fire.

“The street came together and was united while I was at work until I could get here,” she said. “They made sure all the boys were safe.

"I couldn’t fault this street at all. Everyone has been so lovely and caring, and making sure that we’re OK.”

Portia thanked people who have donated to help the family, saying: “I really appreciate it.”

To donate to the fundraiser, visit Becky Smith’s GoFundMe page titled: “Help support Portia and her family.”
