Around **300,000 energy-efficient LED lights** brought the famous shopping street to life, with more than**30 angel-shaped decorations returning for their 11th year**. Presenter Harriet Fisher** switched on the display at 6.30 pm**, coinciding with Britain's** Bonfire Night celebrations**.

With **traffic banned for the evening for the first time since 2019**, crowds filled the** pedestrianised street** to enjoy live bands, a Christmas choir, LED dancers, stilt walkers and playful Hamleys elves.

This year's decorations feature glowing words including "Joy", "Smile" and "Hope", as well as illuminated hearts, stars and **new installations in Princes Arcade**.

Nearby, visitors to St James's Market could also support **food-rescue charity Felix through the returning Wishing Tree**, where a tap triggers a**£5 donation**.
