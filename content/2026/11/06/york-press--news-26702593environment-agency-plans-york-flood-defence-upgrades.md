The Environment Agency has announced plans to upgrade flood defences in York.

The improvements will take place in Blue Beck, between the A19 and Shipton Road.

The work will involve removing existing debris screens and providing better access and fencing.

The agency has stated that these works, due to their scale, are considered low risk to the environment.

They further noted that the areas affected by the improvement works are not ecologically sensitive or designated sites.

An ecological constraint note was conducted at the site to identify and develop mitigation for any species present in the area.

All necessary mitigation has been integrated into an Environmental Action Plan, which details steps to manage any environmental risks.

The Environment Agency has announced that they do not intend to prepare an environmental statement regarding the proposed improvement works, citing the lack of significant environmental effects.

The public is invited to submit any representations regarding the likely environmental impact of the proposed improvement works.

These should be made in writing to the Environment Agency Office at Quarry House, Leeds, LS2 7UE.

The deadline for submissions is December 5, 2026.
