Graham + Sibbald has strengthened its leadership team in Scotland with two senior appointments in Glasgow and Aberdeen.

Douglas Wood joins the chartered surveyor and property consultancy as senior director in its capital markets team, based in Glasgow, while Andrew Michel takes up the role of director in the building surveying division in Aberdeen.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21679223.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Graham + Sibbald team. *(Image: Supplied)*

Wood brings more than 20 years of experience in investment transactions and asset management, having previously worked with Avison Young. His expertise includes acquisitions, disposals, development funding and value-add strategies for institutional investors, REITs, private equity firms and property companies.

He will focus on the central belt, covering Glasgow, Edinburgh and surrounding areas, with support from colleagues in Edinburgh, Birmingham and London.

Michel joins with more than 18 years of experience in building surveying and project management. Most recently, he worked with CBRE/Turner & Townsend and has managed a range of complex property and construction projects, providing technical and strategic advice to clients.

Douglas Wood, senior director in the capital markets team, said: "It is great to be joining Graham + Sibbald at such an exciting point in their growth journey. The business has ambitious growth plans and a strong reputation across Scotland and it is growing as it continues to expand South of the Border. I look forward to working with colleagues across the UK to continue to take the Capital Markets team to the next level."

Andrew Michel, director in the building surveying division, said: "I am delighted to be joining Graham + Sibbald at such an exciting time for the business. The firm's strong reputation, client-focused approach and commitment to delivering high-quality professional services made this a fantastic opportunity. I look forward to working with colleagues across the business to support our clients, contribute to the continued growth of the Aberdeen office, and help deliver successful outcomes across the built environment sector."

Founded in Dundee in 1959, Graham + Sibbald operates 24 offices across the UK and employs more than 400 professionals.
