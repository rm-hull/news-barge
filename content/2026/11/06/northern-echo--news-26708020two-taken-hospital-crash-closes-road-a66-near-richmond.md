Two people have been taken to hospital after a crash on the A66 in North Yorkshire.

Two people were taken to Darlington Memorial Hospital for further treatment.

A spokesperson from the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: “We received an emergency call at 4.51pm on Friday afternoon (November 6) to report a collision on the A66 near Scotch Corner.

“A number of ambulance resources were dispatched to the scene, including three ambulances and two air ambulances.

“Two patients have been conveyed to Darlington Memorial Hospital, and some resources are still on scene.”

The road remains closed in both directions with traffic building in the area.

North Yorkshire Police have advised drivers to find alternative routes while they work at the scene.

A force spokesperson said: “We are currently dealing with a road traffic collision on the A66 near Melsonby and Scotch Corner.

“The road has been closed while we attend the incident, causing a build up of traffic.
