A footpath will be closed for reconstruction in Newton Hall.

The County Council of Durham has announced the temporary closure of an 80-metre stretch of footpath linking Featherstone to Cotherstone Road, due to begin on Monday, November 9.

The work is expected to last 19 days, with the path reopening on Saturday, November 28.

The closure affects the length of footpath extending westwards and northwards from beside 21/23 Cotherstone Road to the garages off 21 Featherstone Road.

The reconstruction work will be carried out under a temporary footpath closure order made under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

Pedestrians will be diverted via alternative footpath links between Cotherstone and Featherstone Road to the north and south of the closed section.

The order allows for the closure to last up to six months if necessary, and any changes to the schedule will be indicated on site.
