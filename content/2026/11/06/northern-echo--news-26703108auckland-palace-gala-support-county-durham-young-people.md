An exclusive gala concert will take place next week, promising an evening of classical music, charity, and elegance.

The event will be held at St Peter’s Chapel in Auckland Palace on Thursday, November 12, in support of the High Sheriff's Youth Awards.

Hosted by The Auckland Project on behalf of the High Sheriff of Durham, Dr Arnab Basu MBE DL, the concert will raise funds for grassroots organisations supporting young people across the North East.

Dr Basu said: "I'm very grateful to The Auckland Project for hosting this concert and helping us raise funds for the High Sheriff's Youth Awards.

"During my year so far as High Sheriff, I've already had the opportunity to visit some remarkable organisations working with young people, often in communities facing considerable challenges.

"I've seen first-hand the difference that relatively modest amounts of funding can make to the work they do.

"The concert should be a very enjoyable evening in a wonderful setting, and I hope people will come along, support the event and help us to support more community organisations next year."

The evening will feature music by Mozart, Tchaikovsky, and Handel, with performances by the Durham University Chamber Choir and a range of soloists and ensembles.

Highlights include Purcell’s Trumpet Sonata in D Major, Schubert’s An Sylvia, and Tchaikovsky’s Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy.

The first half will conclude with Handel’s The Trumpet Shall Sound from Messiah.

A charity auction and raffle will take place during the interval, offering prizes such as a private flight over Northumberland, cricket tickets, a wine-tasting experience, and a bottle of wine signed by the Prime Minister.

Tickets are priced at £30 per person and include wine and canapés during the interval.

VIP tickets have already sold out.

To book tickets or for more information, visit aucklandproject.org/event/high-sheriff-durham-fundraising-concert/.
