Three males were arrested after fireworks were reportedly thrown at a police station on Bonfire Night.

The incident happened at Manningham Police Station on Lilycroft Road in Bradford at around 6.30pm on Thursday.

West Yorkshire Police has launched an investigation.

## What police could say

A spokesperson for the force said: "Officers were called to reports of youths throwing fireworks at Manningham Police station on Lilycroft Road, Bradford at around 6.30pm last night (5 November).

"Police attended and three males were arrested for firework related offences.

"Enquiries are currently ongoing into the incident.

"We would urge anyone with information to contact police using the Live Chat options at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting reference 1430 of 05/11."
