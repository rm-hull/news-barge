Police have issued Bradford residents with advice on parking and access.

Mostyn Grove is a residential cul-de-sac in Wibsey, described by West Yorkshire Police as a "narrow residential road".

On November 4, PCSOs delivered letters to households on the street, where parking is limited and vehicle use is rising.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Mostyn Grove is a narrow residential road with limited on-street parking.

"As the number of vehicles regularly using the area has increased, parking has become increasingly challenging.

"Some households have multiple vehicles and receive regular visitors, contributing to congestion at busy times."

Drivers often park partly on the pavement to let traffic pass, but this can block pedestrians using pushchairs, wheelchairs or mobility aids.

Poor parking could also block emergency services, refuse lorries and delivery drivers.
