Bonfire Night celebrations are set to continue across the district this weekend.

Thousands are expected to attend displays across the Bradford district, with a mix of traditional bonfires, fireworks, and fire-themed spectacles taking place on Friday and Saturday.

The T&A has rounded up just five of the displays you can attend over the next two days.

### Clayton Rugby Club

Clayton Rugby Club will kick off the celebrations tonight (Friday) with an event running from 6pm to 10pm.

The evening will feature a large bonfire, fireworks, funfair rides, food stalls, and a fully stocked bar.

Admission is £5 in advance or £8 on the gate for adults, while children can attend for free.

### Ilkley Cricket Club

Ilkley Cricket Club is offering a free Bonfire Night event tonight, starting at 5pm.

The bonfire will be lit at 6.45pm, followed by fireworks at 7.30pm.

Pie and peas will be available from the kitchen.

### Bradford Moor Golf Club

On Saturday, November 7, Bradford Moor Golf Club will host a Bonfire Party Night from 6pm.

The event will include a traditional bonfire, fireworks, live music, DJs, and food vendors.

Tickets start from £3 and go up to £12 depending on age and package.

### Eldwick Recreation Ground

Eldwick Recreation Ground will host one of the district’s most popular community events on Saturday in support of local charities and the Yorkshire Air Ambulance.

The Eldwick Bonfire and Fireworks Display will feature a musical fireworks show and refreshments.

Tickets are priced at £6 for adults, £2 for accompanied children, and £15 for a family ticket.

### Woodlands Cricket Club

Also on Saturday, Woodlands Cricket Club in Oakenshaw will hold its annual Bonfire and Firework Night.

Gates open at 5.30pm, with the bonfire lighting at 6pm and fireworks at 7.30pm.

Live music will follow from 8.30pm.

Tickets range from £3 to £8.
