Around **100 demonstrators entered the site in the Sloterdijk district**, although organisers claimed several hundred had taken part. Activists used ladders to scale the perimeter fence and cut an opening to allow others inside.** Some climbed a construction crane, displaying a banner reading "Fuck AI"**, while others waved Palestinian flags and held signs accusing Microsoft of supporting genocide.

Police surrounded the protesters before the mayor ordered the demonstration to end. **Riot officers subsequently intervened**, with** several arrests reported**.

The protest was organised by **Dutch activist collective Geef Tegengas**, alongside a separate mobilisation by environmental campaign group** Fossielvrij NL**. Campaigners oppose the development over its** expected electricity consumption**, its impact on housing plans and concerns about Microsoft's activities.

The project, **developed by Pure Data Centres for Microsoft**, involves three towers approximately 85 metres high, with an** investment exceeding $1 billion**. Protesters argue that the facilities could consume as much electricity as all Amsterdam households combined, a claim that has not been independently established.

The action formed **part of an international campaign against large-scale data centres**, with demonstrations planned across several European countries. Both Pure Data Centres and Microsoft said they respected peaceful protest, while urging demonstrators to avoid unlawful actions.
