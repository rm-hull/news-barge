Lead casting for multi-award-winning musical *Chicago* has been announced.

And the name on everybody’s lips is gonna be…Lauren Oakley, as the *Strictly Come Dancing*star has been announced as Roxie Hart in the new tour of* Chicago* coming to York next year.

It will play at the Grand Opera House York from **Monday 31 May to Saturday 5 June 2027**.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/885265241_18415640599084133_3003577272695445729_n-scaled.jpg)

*Chicago*will be Lauren Oakley’s musical theatre debut, and alongside her are two-time Olivier Award nominee Adam Garcia as Billy Flynn and* The X Factor* winner Sam Bailey as Mama Morton.

They are joined by Charlotte Gooch as Velma Kelly, Jonathan Dryden Taylor as Amos Hart and R. Leonard as Mary Sunshine.

The company is completed by Bethany Adamson, Kofi Aidoo-Appiah, Gabby Antrobus, Jamie Armour, Talia Duff, Harvey Fawcett, Marie Finlayson, Sergio Giacomelli, Sam Holden, Aoife Kenny, Harrison Langham, Oliver Loades, Will Markendale, Gabrielle Parker, and Tanith Williams.

Set amidst the razzle-dazzle decadence of the 1920s, *Chicago* is the story of Roxie Hart, a housewife and nightclub dancer who murders her on-the-side lover after he threatens to walk out on her.

Desperate to avoid conviction, she dupes the public, the media and her rival cellmate, Velma Kelly, by hiring Chicago’s slickest criminal lawyer to transform her malicious crime into a barrage of sensational headlines, the likes of which might just as easily be ripped from today’s tabloids.

Created by the musical theatre talents of John Kander, Fred Ebb and legendary choreographer Bob Fosse, *Chicago*‘s sexy, sassy score includes the show-stopping songs ‘Razzle Dazzle’, ‘Cell Block Tango’, and ‘All That Jazz’.

Winner of six Tony Awards, two Olivier Awards and a Grammy, *Chicago* is the longest running American musical in Broadway and West End history.

Tickets for *Chicago* at the Grand Opera House York are available starting from £18 here.
