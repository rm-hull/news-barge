Reform UK MSP Graham Simpson has dismissed suggestions he is considering defecting to the Scottish Liberal Democrats, describing the claims as “rubbish”.

It comes as the SNP has demanded that Scottish Lib Dem leader Alex Cole-Hamilton rule out accepting Reform politicians into his party amid speculation at Holyrood over Mr Simpson's political future.

Responding to the claims, Mr Simpson told The Herald: “This story is rubbish.”

The SNP has told The Herald it heard from an "external stakeholder" that Mr Cole-Hamilton had informed them a meeting had taken place between him and Mr Simpson.

The Lib Dems have denied that such a meeting took place.

A move to the Liberal Democrats would mark Mr Simpson's second political defection in little more than a year, having quit the Scottish Conservatives to join Nigel Farage's Reform UK in August 2025.

A Scottish Lib Dem spokesperson also flatly rejected the suggestion, saying: “This is nonsense.”

A senior Lib Dem source poured cold water on the prospect of Mr Simpson joining the party, saying: “We don’t think Graham has much in common with us.”

Mr Simpson recently joined Mr Cole-Hamilton on a cross-party parliamentary delegation to Taiwan.

The SNP has seized on speculation that the Reform MSP could be preparing to switch parties, calling on Mr Cole-Hamilton to confirm whether any discussions have taken place.

It follows the decision by former Conservative MSP Maurice Golden, who had been working as an adviser to Reform UK at Holyrood, to join the Liberal Democrats.

Mr Golden is understood to be a potential Lib Dem candidate at the next general election.

SNP MSP Alex Kerr claimed the party's willingness to accept Mr Golden raised questions about whether other politicians associated with Nigel Farage's party could follow.

He said: “Reform defectors have already been welcomed into the Liberal Democrats and now this – it’s time Alex Cole-Hamilton spilled the truth on what on earth is going on in the Liberal Democrats?

“People who voted Liberal Democrat were not voting to see Reform UK MSPs offered safe harbour in their ranks – it’s illiberal and undemocratic.

“Rumours are swirling around Holyrood that Alex Cole-Hamilton has held shadowy talks with Graham Simpson to woo him from Nigel Farage’s party into the Liberal Democrats – it’s time Alex Cole-Hamilton came out the shadows and ruled this out.”

He added: “Under the SNP, Nigel Farage’s Reform UK will be offered no quarter or influence – it is time the Liberal Democrats followed suit.”
