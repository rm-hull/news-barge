Major engineering works on the West Highland Line are due to begin this weekend, with more than 4km of track set to be renewed as part of a programme to improve reliability.

The nine-day project will involve work at several locations along the route, including near Arisaig, Corrour, Arrochar and Tarbet station, and the Gleann Mama Viaduct.

Engineers will also carry out improvements in the Loch nan Uamh and Beasdale tunnels, refurbish Fassfern Bridge near Spean Bridge and remove a redundant bridge near Loch Eil.

Vegetation clearance between Fort William and Arisaig is also planned to reduce the risk of disruption.

The work will affect services between Helensburgh, Fort William and Mallaig. Buses will replace trains between Crianlarich and Fort William and Mallaig from Monday, November 9, to Tuesday, November 17.

Gerry McQuade, capital delivery director at Network Rail Scotland, said: "This weekend marks the start of a significant programme of work across the West Highland Line, with our teams ready to deliver improvements at a number of locations along the route.

"By bringing this work together over nine days, we can renew track, improve drainage and carry out essential maintenance that will help make the railway more reliable and resilient for passengers.

"We know there’s never a good time to close the railway, and we’d like to thank passengers and local communities for their patience while we complete these important improvements."

Mark Ilderton, ScotRail service delivery director, said: "We know these works will mean changes to journeys for customers travelling on the West Highland Line, and we want to thank everyone for their patience while this important investment takes place.

"The improvements being delivered by Network Rail will help make the railway more reliable and resilient, helping to protect this vital route for the communities, businesses, and visitors who depend on it.

"We’ll be doing everything we can to keep people moving during the works with a robust rail replacement plan in place.

"This includes buses replacing trains between Crianlarich and Fort William and Mallaig from Monday until Tuesday, 17 November.

"Customers should plan ahead and check their journey before travelling."
