A CARE service has been rated outstanding following a recent inspection.

Dawn to Dusk Care Services Ltd in Halifax, which supports people in their own homes, was inspected by the Care Quality Commission (CQC) in October as part of CQC’s continual checks on the safety and quality of health services.

The service was rated outstanding overall, with top marks for being caring and well-led, and rated good for being responsive, effective and safe.

Victoria Marsden, CQC deputy director of operations for adult social care in the north region, said: "When we inspected Dawn to Dusk, we found an exceptional service where staff and leaders worked hard to provide the best possible care to people living in their own homes.

"Feedback from people was overwhelmingly positive due to kind and caring staff who understood their needs.

"Staff thrived on providing highly personalised support to people that went above and beyond to ensure they felt genuinely cared for."

Inspectors found a strong person-centred culture, with leaders ensuring there were always enough qualified and skilled staff.

They also praised the service’s commitment to continuous improvement and its investment in team wellbeing.

The full CQC report will be published on the regulator’s website in the coming days.
