Pupils at Ashville College's Prep School in Harrogate have raised £941.50 for Martin House Children's Hospice.

The fundraiser included a pyjama day and a movie night.

Pupils wore their pyjamas to school and Marty Bear joined in with the ‘Wake Up, Shake Up’ session.

The school said:

> “A huge thank you to everyone who supported the event.
>
> "Your generosity will help support the vital care and services Martin House provides to local children and their families.”

Martin House cares for children and young people with life-shortening conditions across North, West and East Yorkshire and offers bereavement support to families.

The hospice relies almost entirely on donations and fundraising to provide its care free of charge.
