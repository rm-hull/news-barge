A PHOTOGRAPHY exhibition capturing the stories of a city will go on display in the heart of Bradford this November.

A City in Frames: Bradford, organised by Odyssey Media House CIC and supported by Arts Council England, will run at Darley Street Market from November 6 to 20, 2026.

The exhibition features photographs and personal stories from residents, gathered through free mobile photography workshops, guided walks, and storytelling sessions.

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council's Executive Member for Neighbourhoods and Communities, said: "Bradford is a district full of stories, and this exhibition offers a fantastic opportunity for residents to share their experiences and perspectives through photography.

"It's wonderful to see local creativity showcased at Darley Street Market, a venue that is becoming an important space for culture, community and connection in the heart of the city."

The project worked with young people across Bradford East, Bradford West, and Bradford South, helping them develop new skills while capturing authentic perspectives of their communities.

Oluwadamilola Adetomiwa, founder and executive director of Odyssey Media House CIC, said: "A City in Frames is about giving people the opportunity to tell their own stories and see their communities differently.

"Every photograph represents a perspective, a memory or an experience that deserves to be celebrated."
