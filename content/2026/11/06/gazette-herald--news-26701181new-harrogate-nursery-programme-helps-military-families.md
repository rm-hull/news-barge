A nursery has launched a new programme to better support children from Armed Forces families.

Partou Tiny Teapots Day Nursery and Pre-school in Harrogate has launched the Little Journeys, Strong Connections programme.

The nursery team has identified areas for professional development and is beginning targeted training on topics such as deployment, separation, emotional wellbeing, and maintaining family connections.

Claire Martin, nursery manager, said: "I have been meeting and having conversations with our Service families to understand their experiences, what we already do well and what we could do differently to support them.

"Parents are also becoming actively involved in developing the project.

"Service parents are coming into nursery to speak directly with the team about what military family life can be like, how periods of separation can affect children and the wider family, and the things early years practitioners can do that genuinely help."

The nursery also plans to continue working with specialists and evolving the programme.

Laura Clay, Partou regional childcare director, said: "It is a deeply impressive programme which illustrates how closely aligned the team is to the families and how focused they are on delivering the best possible nursery experience for them."
