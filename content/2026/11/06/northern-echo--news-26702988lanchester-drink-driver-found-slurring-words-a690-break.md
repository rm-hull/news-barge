A boozy driver blew more than three times the legal limit after police found him slurring his words in his broken-down car.

Stuart Wood admitted drinking before driving but said he thought he was under the legal limit.

At his trial, Wood, 40, said he drank more while sitting in his broken-down car on the A690 near Pelton.

Prosecutor Rebbecca Rowlands told Newton Aycliffe Magistrates Court that Wood had 128 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath when police tested him - more than three times the legal limit of 35.

She said: “When speaking to the male, they (police) noticed he was unstable on his feet, slurring his words and smelling of alcohol.

“He told the officers that he had a drink but thought he was under the limit for driving.”

Wood, of Fenhall Park, Lanchester, was convicted of drink driving after his arrest on February 4.

His solicitor, Jaxon Taylor, said Wood accepted the magistrates’ verdict but maintained he had been drinking after his Toyota Yaris broke down.

“There was evidence of empty cans of alcohol in the car,” he said.

“He was unable to say how long he had been on the roadside and the magistrates were reluctant to accept that he had drunk as much as he had in that time.”

Mr Taylor said Wood was likely to lose his job because of the conviction.

Bench chairman David Thompson gave Wood a 12-month community order and ordered him to complete 100 hours of unpaid work.

Wood was banned from driving for 29 months and ordered to pay £764 in court costs.

Mr Thompson said: “If you drive while you are disqualified, you will commit a serious offence and you could find yourself in custody.”
