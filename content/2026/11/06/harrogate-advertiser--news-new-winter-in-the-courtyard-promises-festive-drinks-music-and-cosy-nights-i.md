A new festive experience is coming to Harrogate this winter, as The West Park Hotel transforms its courtyard into a cosy seasonal destination for friends, families and visitors to enjoy.

Winter in the Courtyard will launch this November at the hotel on Cold Bath Road, creating a winter retreat complete with twinkling lights, outdoor heating, festive drinks and a menu of seasonal small plates.

The courtyard will be given a festive makeover, offering guests the chance to enjoy the outdoors while staying warm and comfortable — making it an ideal spot for a winter catch-up, after-work drinks or a festive celebration.

Alongside the food and drink offering, visitors can enjoy live music sessions from Funk My Life, bringing DJs, music and a lively atmosphere to the courtyard throughout the season.

A spokesperson at The West Park Hotel said: “Whether you’re meeting friends for a festive catch-up or making an evening of it, we’ll have a cosy corner waiting for you.”

Winter in the Courtyard opens on Saturday, November 14.

The launch adds to Harrogate’s growing list of festive attractions, giving locals and visitors another reason to enjoy the town during the winter months.
