Scottish events business 21CC Group has acquired Edinburgh-based drone display company SkyShows as part of a £2.1 million investment plan to expand its capabilities across the UK.

The acquisition will support plans to quadruple the group’s drone fleet and deliver aerial displays featuring up to 1,800 drones.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21677234.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Clive Coombes OBE, Founder of SkyShows with 21CC Group team members, Emma Nimmo and Gordon Rough. *(Image: Sandy Young)*

The investment is expected to make SkyShows Scotland’s largest drone display operator and strengthen its position in the UK market.

21CC Group plans to integrate drone displays into its wider events portfolio, which includes creative design, production management, audiovisual services, lighting, sound, lasers, fireworks and special effects.

Founded in 2000, the group has delivered projects across major sporting events, national celebrations and festivals. Its previous work includes the UCI Cycling World Championships, Royal Highland Show, BBC Live at Edinburgh Castle, BBC Proms and V&A Dundee.

Geoff Crow, managing director of 21CC Group, said: "We’re always looking for ways to grow and bring something new to our clients.

"Drone technology is evolving rapidly, and we see huge potential in what it can bring to live events, from corporate activations and large-scale public celebrations, to festivals, bespoke brand experiences and private celebrations.

"We want to be at the forefront of that growth, creating bigger, bolder and more memorable experiences for audiences, and this acquisition will enable us to do just that.

"Bringing SkyShows into the group, investing in new technology, creating opportunities for our people to develop new skills and bringing specialist expertise into the sector is all part of that.

"It's about strengthening what we can deliver here in Scotland, whilst continuing to take that expertise to events across the UK and beyond."

Clive Coombes OBE, founder of SkyShows, said: "Having pioneered drone light shows, where we were the first UK company to hold official permission to fly drone swarms, we are incredibly proud of the reputation for high-quality innovation we’ve built over the last five years.

"Joining 21CC Group is an exciting next chapter for SkyShows.

"It gives us the perfect platform to build on everything we’ve achieved so far, opening doors to new audiences and providing the infrastructure, support and investment to take on new and innovative drone displays at a vastly increased scale.

"This is a truly positive new chapter for SkyShows, and I am delighted we are becoming part of this outstanding sector-leading brand."
