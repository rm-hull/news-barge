A new emergency bleed control kit has been donated to help save lives in Hartlepool.

The John McNab emergency bleed control kit was donated by Utilita's Hartlepool Energy Hub to the Hartlepool Ambulance Charity (HAC), whose volunteer medics provide vital support in the town centre every weekend.

Kristine Gorman, hub supervisor at Utilita Energy Hub Hartlepool, said: "As a community hub, we're always looking for ways to support organisations that make a real difference locally.

"Hartlepool Ambulance Charity does incredible work to help keep people safe, and we're proud to donate this bleed kit to support their volunteers.

"We hope it never has to be used, but having the right equipment available could make all the difference in an emergency."

The kit will be formally handed over at 2pm on Friday, November 6, at the Utilita Energy Hub in York Road.

It contains specialist equipment to treat catastrophic bleeding, including a tourniquet, clotting bandage, and wound dressings.

A spokesperson for Hartlepool Ambulance Charity said: "Our volunteers regularly respond to a wide range of medical incidents in the town centre, so having access to emergency bleed control equipment is extremely important.

"We're incredibly grateful to Utilita for this donation and for recognising the role our team plays in the local community."

Over the past four years, Utilita's Hartlepool Energy Hub has become a valued community resource, offering free energy and household budgeting advice while supporting a range of local charities and community initiatives.
