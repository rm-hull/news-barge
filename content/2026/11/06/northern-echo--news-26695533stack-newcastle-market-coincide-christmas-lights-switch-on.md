A festive night market is set to light up the city with shopping, food and entertainment.

STACK Newcastle will host the event on Thursday, November 19, featuring more than 60 unique sellers and a packed evening of seasonal fun.

The Night Market UK, which previously staged a successful evening at the venue earlier this year, will return with vintage sellers, specialised crafters and independent traders from across the region offering a range of unique products.

Chels Morton, marketing manager at STACK for sport and events, said: "For anyone looking for gifts that are really different, then this is definitely something they won’t want to miss.

"There’ll be dozens of artisan creators bringing an exciting range of items, covering everything from jewellery to candles, from handmade cards to vintage clothing.

"It’s also a great way to support some really exciting independent businesses and find things that you’re unlikely to find anywhere else."

The market will run from 5.30pm to 10.30pm, coinciding with Newcastle’s Christmas lights switch-on.

Entry costs £3.50 in advance or £4 on the night, with the option to book arrival time slots.

Tickets are available at events.humanitix.com/the-night-market-uk-stack-newcastle-christmas-special/tickets.

A live DJ will keep the energy high throughout the evening, and visitors can also enjoy a special art installation by artist and technologist Ross Blair, commissioned by The Night Market UK to celebrate Newcastle.

The installation will be displayed on STACK’s giant screens during the event.

All food and drink outlets at Stack Newcastle will be open for the occasion, offering a wide selection of street food from The Flaming Tattie, Dinithi, Acropolis and Dylan’s Philly Cheese Steaks.

Other long-standing favourites such as Bao Down, Brack Burgers, Zza Pizzeria and Pattersons fried chicken will also be serving customers through the night.

The event promises a lively and festive atmosphere, blending Christmas shopping with music, art and food in the heart of the city.
