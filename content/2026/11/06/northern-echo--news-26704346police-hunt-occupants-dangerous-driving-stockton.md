Police are hunting the occupants of a car seized following reports of dangerous driving in Stockton, with a senior officer warning the incident could have had 'serious consequences'.

Officers were called following several reports of a yellow Dacia being driven dangerously around Scurfield Road in Hardwick at approximately 6.30pm.

The vehicle was quickly located and seized by police, but those believed to have been inside the car have yet to be traced.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21677925.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Scurfield Road in Hardwick, Stockton *(Image: GOOGLE)*

Chief Superintendent Paul Richardson warned that families and young children had been in the area at the time, raising fears over what could have happened.

He said: “We’ve received several reports this evening regarding a yellow Dacia driving in a dangerous manner around Scurfield Road in Hardwick, Stockton.

“Our officers quickly located and seized the car and are in the process of tracing those who were in the vehicle at the time.

“Lots of people, including families and young children, were in the area at the time so this could have been a serious incident. Thankfully, nobody was hurt.”

Police have now appealed for information as they work to identify and trace those involved.

Although the vehicle has been recovered, officers have not confirmed who was driving it or how many people were inside.

Chief Superintendent Richardson urged anyone who witnessed the incident or has information about those responsible to contact police.

Anyone with information should call 101, quoting reference 221816.
