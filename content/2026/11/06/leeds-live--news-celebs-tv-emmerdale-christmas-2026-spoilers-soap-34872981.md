# Emmerdale Christmas 2026 spoilers as soap legend confesses 'when I read it my jaw fell'

## One of Emmerdale's most famous families will be at the centre of the action

Emmerdale fans have been offered a hint of what to expect this Christmas from one of the soap’s legends.

Lisa Riley, 50, is known to millions of Emmerdale fans as Mandy Dingle, has provided a tease of what is coming for Christmas 2026 for the Dingle clan. The star, who first joined Emmerdale back in 1995, informed us that she has recently been filming festive scenes.

When asked if we can expect a dramatic Christmas for the Dingles, Lisa replied: “Yeah, it is. There’s a massive clip doing the rounds from the red carpet (at the Pride of Britain Awards) and it is true.

“The Christmas Day episode - when I read it my jaw fell. When we shot it, my jaw fell.

“It is so true. I played it as Mandy (when finding out). It is all about the jaw drop - not the mic drop.”

We chatted to Lisa as she provided her support for a major new cancer care centre opening in Leeds in the new year. The soap great is supporting the launch of the new world-leading cancer centre - its ambition being to revolutionise lung cancer care in the UK.

Lung cancer is the UK's biggest cancer killer, claiming more lives than any other cancer. Nearly 40,000 people in England are diagnosed each year.

The pioneering new centre called GenesisCare, which will launch in Century Way, Thorpe Park, in January, aims to confirm whether a patient is likely to have lung cancer or not within just seven days.

That is three weeks sooner than NHS England's target of 28 days and will make a huge difference to patient outcomes. There will be the first ever lung cancer rapid response pathway - aiming to ensure patients are provided with a diagnosis and treatment plan in the fastest and most efficient timeframe possible.

Lisa is supporting the need to get a lung cancer diagnosis in the quickest timeframe possible after losing both her grandma Pat and her close friend and former Emmerdale co-star Leah Bracknell, who played Zoe Tate, to the disease.

Lisa explained: “It is on our doorstep. I can’t shout loud enough. I’m so loyal to the north. Having this on our doorstep is incredible. For me, with my gene pool, I’ve got to be a realist. I’ve got to be honest with myself, and my family, because cancer has hit both sides of my family that there’s probably a big, maybe 75 per cent, chance I’m going to be using that centre in my lifetime.

“For it to open in January, that’s why I jumped onboard to help. This one, in particular, was a learning curve for me. I feel I can use my platform because there are so many stats I didn’t know.

“For example, I did lose my beloved grandma Pat to lung cancer but I always put it down to things like smoking. But, it is not true. The amount of people that I spoke to and asked ‘what’s the biggest killer?’.

“Everybody tends to say breast cancer. But, it is not, it is lung cancer.” Lisa, who sadly lost her mum Cath to breast cancer, added: “This campaign is so dear to my heart. Because of my gran. If I’m unaware, and I like to think I’m quite on-the-ball, I feel that we need to spread the word and say ‘you have to look for the warning signs’.

“The rapid response pathway that they are doing at GenesisCare is so brilliant. It is all about time. The time for diagnosis, that can be all sped up. We are truly, truly blessed having it here in Leeds.”
