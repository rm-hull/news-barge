A GROUP of people including a 12-year-old child was racially abused in a York city centre street.

North Yorkshire Police has released a picture of a man that its officers would like to speak to in connection with a report about racial abuse in Blossom Street last month.

It happened on October 13.

A police spokesperson said: “A group of people, including a 12-year-old child, were subjected to racist verbal abuse.

“Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured as we believe that he may have information that will assist our investigation.”

Anyone with information is asked to email [James.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:James.Wilson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101; alternatively, you can contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.
