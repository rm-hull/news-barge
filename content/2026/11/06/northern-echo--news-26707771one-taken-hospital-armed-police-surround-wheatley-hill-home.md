One person was taken to hospital after armed police surrounded a house in a County Durham village.

Emergency services including the air ambulance descended on Wheatley Hill Front Street shortly before 3pm on Friday, November 6, with the air ambulance also called to the scene.

Pictures show armed police outside a house, while the air ambulance landed near the Greenhills Centre.

One witness described the village as being "at a standstill".

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson said: "We were called to an incident at a private address in Wheatley Hill, County Durham on Friday, November 6 shortly before 3pm.

"We dispatched one ambulance crew and were supported by Great North Air Ambulance Service.

"One patient was taken to hospital by road for further treatment."

Durham Police and the Great North Air Ambulance Service have been contacted for comment.
