A van was left severely damaged after smashing into a tree in Darlington.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) confirmed it was called at 2.05am, and sent two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene.

Pictures show a dark grey van wedged against a tree with extensive damage to its front.

It is not known if anyone was injured.

Bus services were disrupted, hospital patients were warned to allow extra travelling time, and the nearby Pasta Shop was forced to shut for the day.

The road has since reopened.

## When did the crash happen?

A spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Woodland Road in Darlington at 2.05am on Friday, November 6.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene."

Durham Police confirmed the road had reopened and thanked everyone for their patience.

The force has been contacted for an update.

The Pasta Shop in Darlington confirmed it will remain closed on Friday as staff were unable to access the premises.

The business said: "Unfortunately, due to a serious incident in the area this morning, the police have closed the surrounding roads, and there is currently no access to our shop.

"Sadly, we can't even reach the premises ourselves, so The Pasta Shop will remain closed today.

"Our thoughts are with everyone affected by this morning's incident and we sincerely hope everyone is safe."

## Are buses affected by the crash?

Arriva confirmed its buses were unable to travel along Woodland Road.
