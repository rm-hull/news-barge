Ripon’s big bonfire and fireworks display will take place tonight, Friday in front of a crowd of thousands of people - with the support of Specsavers Ripon.

Taking place at Ripon Racecourse, the annual event is organised and managed by volunteers from Ripon Rowels Rotary Club.

For the second years running, Specsavers Ripon, which is locally owned and run, will be the lead sponsor,

The Specsavers team has contributed £600 to help fund the event, with ticket proceeds going to a selection of local charities and community initiatives.

The Specsavers team will be there tonight, handing out tote bags filled with items to care for your eyes and ears.

Annette Duffy, director at Specsavers Ripon, says: ‘We’re delighted to support one of the Rotary Club’s beloved events.

“It’s something the whole community looks forward to every year, and knowing that the money raised on the night will support a selection of charities throughout Ripon and the surrounding communities makes it such a wonderful initiative to be part of.

“We hope everyone has a magical night and enjoys everything the evening has to offer.”

Gates will open at Ripon Racecourse at 5.30pm with the bonfire lit at 7pm and the fireworks display at 7.30pm.
