DRAFT proposals for a footpath linking Gargrave with its railway station have been drawn up, heard a meeting of the parish council.

But the possible long-awaited solution to people having to walk in the road is "horrifically" expensive, the meeting was told.

The parish council has long called for an extension of the footpath from Church Street right up to both the entrance and exit of the station.

The footpath currently finishes just short of the entrance and people getting off on the northbound, platform two, have to walk in the road and over the narrow bridge.

Cllr Simon Myers, who represents Mid-Craven on North Yorkshire Council, told yesterday's (Wednesday) meeting that the draft proposal was very expensive because it involved traffic lights.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21676492.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gargrave Station *(Image: Lesley Tate)*

The creation of a footpath on the narrow bridge would reduce the bridge to single lane, which would need lights, he told the meeting.

"The estimate is horrific, because of the narrow bridge they will have to put lights in, and that makes it very expensive. I am going to ask the officers to go to the Combined Authority to apply for funding for a detailed feasibility study looking at potential ways of solving the issue.

"I am very happy if the traffic lights are the only solution, and will push for it, but I want to be very sure that this is the only way of solving the safety issue for people going to the station, " he said.
