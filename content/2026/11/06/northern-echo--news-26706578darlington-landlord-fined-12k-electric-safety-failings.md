A private landlord has been slapped with a £12,500 penalty after failing to comply with electrical safety requirements at a rented property in Darlington.

An investigation by Darlington Borough Council’s private sector housing team found the landlord had breached the Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector Regulations 2020.

The regulations, which have applied to all existing tenancies since April 2021, require landlords to ensure electrical installations in rented properties are inspected and tested by a qualified and competent person at least every five years.

Any necessary remedial work must also be completed within the required timescales.

Following its investigation, the council concluded the landlord had failed to meet the legal obligations and imposed a £12,500 financial penalty in line with its enforcement policy.

The council said it remained committed to raising standards in the private rented sector, supporting responsible landlords and taking action when housing and safety requirements were not met.

Councillor Jim Garner, cabinet member for stronger communities, said: “Tenants have the right to live in safe homes, and electrical safety checks are vital in protecting them from harm.
