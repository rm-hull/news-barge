A LORRY has hit power lines on a main road near a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police have asked the public to avoid the A63 at Hull Road near Hemingbrough, Selby, after a crash involving a heavy goods vehicle and power lines.




* **Major Yorkshire road reopens in both directions after crash**

A spokesperson for the force said: “The road is currently closed while utilities work to make the area safe again. Please avoid the area and find an alternative route.

“Thank you for your understanding and patience.”
