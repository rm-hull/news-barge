People across the North East will honour servicemen and women killed in conflict this weekend as services, parades and wreath-laying ceremonies take place across the region.

Remembrance Sunday events will take place across the North East on November 8, followed by Armistice Day events on November 11.

Most communities will also hold a two-minute silence at 11am.

Here is a guide to Remembrance events across the North East.

### Darlington and South Durham

**Darlington**’s service starts at Holy Trinity Church at 10am, followed by a parade to the Cenotaph at Darlington Memorial Hospital. The act of remembrance and silence begin at 11am.

A second ceremony takes place at West Cemetery War Graves at 2.30pm.

The November 11 silence at High Row begins at 11am.

In **Bishop Auckland**, the parade assembles outside the Newgate Centre at 10.30am before an 11am service at the Market Place War Memorial and a service at St Anne’s Church.** Newton Aycliffe**’s parade leaves Barnard Armoury at 10.40am.

Its 11am service at St Clare’s Church includes the Last Post, Reveille, silence and wreath laying. A church service takes place afterwards before a march along Stephenson Way.

**Spennymoor**’s town hall service starts at 10am, followed by a parade assembling at 10.45am and remembrance at the Market Place War Memorial at 11am.

Its November 11 service starts at 10.50am at the War Memorial, beside the Grand Electric Hall.

**Ferryhill’** s parade assembles at Market Place Car Park at 9.30am and leaves for St Luke’s Church at 9.45am.

The service begins at 10am, followed by remembrance at the Town Hall Cenotaph at 11am and wreath laying at Memorial Cottage at 12.30pm.

Its November 11 service starts at 10.40am at the Town Hall Cenotaph.

**Barnard Castle**’s service starts at St Mary’s Parish Church at 10.58am, followed by a silence and a ceremony at The Bowes Museum at about 11.50am.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20176635.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: ANDY FUTERS)*

### Durham City and North Durham

In **Durham City**, a replica Spitfire will be displayed at Millennium Place from 9am to 2pm on November 8.

Durham Cathedral’s memorial service starts at 10.15am, followed by the 11am silence and the annual parade through the city. Durham University Brass Band will also take part.

**Consett**’s service begins at the Cenotaph at 10.55am, with silence and wreath laying at 11am.** Stanley**’s services start at St Margaret’s Church at 9am, St Peter’s Church at 10am, and the village war memorial and St Andrew’s Church at 10.45am.

Beacon lighting will be held on Stanley's Front Street at 4pm.

Services are also planned at **South Moor** Memorial Park and**Craghead** Cenotaph at 10.40am.**Greencroft** will hold services at Loud Bank and The Avenue at 10.45am.**Tanfield Lea**’s service starts at Tanfield Lea Club at 12.30pm, while Annfield Plain Park will hold a service at 2pm.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18747712.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Sarah Caldecott)*### Sunderland**Sunderland**’s city centre parade begins on Burdon Road at 10.35am, with the service and silence at 11am.** Castletown**’s service starts at Castledown Methodist Church at 10.30am, followed by wreath-laying at the cemetery.

The service at **Southwick** War Memorial begins at 10.45am.

Services at several other war memorials, including Hetton-le-Hole, Houghton-le-Spring, Ryhope, Silksworth, South Hylton, Fatfield and Washington, begin at 11am.

The November 11 silence on Burdon Road starts at 11am.

### Newcastle and North Tyneside

**Newcastle**’s military parade leaves the Civic Centre at 10.30am for the City War Memorial, where the service and silence start at 11am.** North Tyneside**services start at 11am at war memorials in North Shields, Tynemouth, Wallsend, Longbenton, Seaton Burn and Shiremoor.** Whitley Bay**’s ceremony begins at 3pm from the Cenotaph on The Links.** Killingworth**’s November 11 service starts at 11am at the Post-1945 Jigsaw Memorial in White Swan Centre.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20160800.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Getty)*### Gateshead**Gateshead** events include a 10.45am service at Gateshead Cenotaph and 9am events at St Patrick’s Church, High Spen, and Holy Trinity Church, Swalwell.

St Paul’s Church, **Winlato** n, holds a service at 9.45am, followed by a 10.45am event at the War Memorial.

St Cuthbert's Church, **Marley Hill** begins at 10.15am.

St Barnabas Church, **Rowlands Gill** will start at 10.45am, with a 12pm service at the War Memorial.

In **Whickham**, spectators gather at 10.15am before the memorial service at The Square at 10.45am.

Holy Cross Church, **Ryton** will host their event at 10am, then 10.50am at the War Memorial on Station Bank.

Other Gateshead services include a parade from Birtley Community Centre to the Birtley Cenotaph, where a remembrance service begins at 10.45am.

In **Dunston**, a service starts at St Nicholas Church at 10am before moving to the War Memorial for an 11am ceremony.** Felling**'s service begins at 11am in the Garden of Remembrance, while Blaydon holds a 10.15am service at St Cuthbert's Church before a noon ceremony at the town's War Memorial.

Further ceremonies take place at nearby town war memorials, including those in Kibblesworth and Greenside at 10.45am.

Chopwell Cenotaph and Clara Vale War Memorial services start at 11am.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21543852.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: Steve Brock)*

### Teesside

In **Stockton**, a parade will march along the High Street before a service at the Stockton Cenotaph from 10.45am.

In **Thornab** y, a parade starts at 10.25am before a 10.40am service at Acklam Road Cenotaph.

In **Billingham**, the parade leaves The Salutation at 10.30am for a 10.45am ceremony at Station Road Cenotaph.

In **Yarm**, a service starts at Yarm Parish Church at 9.45am before moving to the town war memorial.** Egglescliffe**'s service starts at St John the Baptist Church at 10am before a procession to the Egglescliffe War Memorial at 10.45am.

In **Ingleby Barwick**, a service starts at St Francis of Assisi Church at 10am before an 11am Act of Remembrance at the Memorial Stone.

Remembrance services will take place at the Redcar Cenotaph, Guisborough War Memorial, Loftus War Memorial, Saltburn War Memorial, Skelton War Memorial and King George's Square War Memorial in South Bank, all beginning between 10:35 and 10:50.

Earlier services include one at Moorsholm War Memorial at 9am and one at Easington War Memorial at 11am, after a church service at All Saints Church.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/13185472.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: PA)*Later ceremonies are planned at Carlin How War Memorial at 11.45am, Skinningrove War Memorial at 12.15pm, Lazenby War Memorial at about 12pm and North Skelton War Memorial at 1pm.**Lingdale** War Memorial’s service starts at 1.50pm, Boosbeck Cemetery War Memorial’s at 2.30pm and St Thomas Church in New Marske’s at 3pm.

Normanby and Teesville will hold their events on Saturday 7 November.

**Normanby**'s event will begin at the Bowling Club at 10.30am, to parade at 10.50am to the War Memorial at Smiths Dock Park for its service and wreath laying ceremony.** Read more**

Residents are encouraged to arrive early, particularly at larger events in town and city centres, as roads may be closed for parades and ceremonies.

Whether attending a major civic service or a local gathering at a village war memorial, communities across the North East will come together to remember those who gave their lives in service and to pay tribute to veterans past and present.
