Ferryhill Town Council has launched a competition for local traders to compete for the title of 'Best Festive Shop Window Display for 2026'.

The winner will receive a £100 voucher and a commemorative trophy.

The competition will be judged by the council's events committee, with the winner being announced on December 17, 2026.

Entry is free for all traders.

Anyone interested in taking part in the competition should send their full name, the name and address of their shop, an email address, and two clear photographs of their festive window display to [kyounghusband@ferryhill.gov.uk.](mailto:kyounghusband@ferryhill.gov.uk.)

The deadline is Friday, December 11, 2026.

For more information about the competition, or for those unable to email their entry, contact Karen Younghusband at Ferryhill Town Council on 01740 652157.
