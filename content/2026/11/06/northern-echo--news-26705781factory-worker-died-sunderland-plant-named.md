A factory worker who died following an incident at a North East manufacturing plant has been named.

Anthony Ian Clementson, 52, died in October after suffering serious injuries at the Liebherr factory on Deptford Terrace, Sunderland.

An inquest was opened into his death by Assistant coroner Laura Hopkinson at Sunderland Coroner's Court, on Wednesday, November 4.

The inquest was told that Mr Clementson, who was born on April 27, 1974, was pronounced dead on October 27.

Ms Hopkinson adjourned the case for a full inquest due to take place at 10am on March 17, 2027, at City Hall, Sunderland.

The Northern Echo previously reported how emergency services were called to the Liebherr site after a worker suffered serious injuries.

The 52-year-old was pronounced dead at the scene.

Liebherr later confirmed the man was an employee at the Sunderland plant and described the incident as a "tragic accident".

In a statement issued after the incident, the company said: "We deeply regret this tragic incident. Our thoughts and sincere sympathies are with the victim's family members and loved ones."

The firm said it was fully cooperating with the relevant authorities, while a joint investigation by Northumbria Police and the Health and Safety Executive remains ongoing.

A Northumbria Police spokesperson previously said officers were working alongside the HSE to establish the circumstances surrounding the incident.
