A PROPERTY management company has been shortlisted for three industry awards.

Watson, based in Leeds, has seen team members reach the finals of the 2026 Property Management Awards, which recognise excellence across the residential leasehold sector.

Amy Bishoprick, managing director at Watson, said: "To have three colleagues shortlisted across such different categories is something we are incredibly proud of.

"Kath, Megan and Ian each bring distinct strengths to Watson, but what they all share is a genuine commitment to doing the right thing for our clients and residents."

Senior property manager Kath Simpson is a finalist for Regional Property Manager of the Year, while property manager Megan Moore is nominated for New Property Manager of the Year.

Business development director Ian Omant is shortlisted for Business Development Manager of the Year.

Headquartered in Leeds, Watson manages developments in York, Manchester, Newcastle, Nottingham, and Sheffield.

The awards ceremony will take place on Thursday, November 19.
