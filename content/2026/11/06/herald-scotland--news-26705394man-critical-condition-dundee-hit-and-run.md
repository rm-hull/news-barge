A man has been left in a critical condition after being struck by a car in a hit-and-run incident in Dundee.

The 54-year-old was taken to hospital following the incident on Dundee's Arran Drive on Thursday night.

Police say the vehicle involved was a dark-coloured hatchback.

Officers have no further information about the car and are appealing to anyone with information to contact them.

Arran Drive remains closed to traffic while police continue their enquiries at the scene.

The incident happened at around 7.50pm. Officers are continuing to examine public and private CCTV footage from the area.

It is possible the vehicle sustained damage in the collision before making off towards the South Road area, where it was last seen.

Detective Inspector Kevin Mollison said: "A man is in a critical condition and we are working to establish the full circumstances and identify who is responsible.

"I am appealing to anyone who was in Arran Drive or the surrounding area to get in touch with us with any information. If you have doorbell cameras or dash-cams, please check your footage. You may have captured images which could assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 3417 of Thursday, 5 November 2026. Alternatively, information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
