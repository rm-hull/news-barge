A MAN has been arrested on suspicion of arson following a vehicle fire.

Police and fire crews were called to the incident on Carlisle Street, Manningham, just before midnight yesterday (Thursday).

When the T&A visited the scene on Friday morning, the remnants of a burnt-out white vehicle, debris and a large number of rugs could be seen.

### What did emergency services say?

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were alerted to an incident by the fire service on Carlisle Street, Bradford at around 11.48pm last night (Thursday 5 November).

"A 44-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of arson.

"Enquires remain ongoing."

![Here's the aftermath of the fire this morning](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21678853.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Friday morning *(Image: T&A)*

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 22.43 to Carlisle Street to reports of a van fire which had spread to the ground floor of a building.

"Five crews used breathing apparatus, a large jet and hose reels to extinguish this."
