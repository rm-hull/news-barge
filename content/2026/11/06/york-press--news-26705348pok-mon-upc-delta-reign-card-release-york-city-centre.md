## Huge queue spotted in York city centre - here's why

Queues stretched down Goodramgate *(Image: Staff)*

GROUPS of people were spotted queuing overnight in York city centre for the release of a new trading card set.

Queues stretched down Goodramgate this morning (November 6) with punters hoping to get their hands on a range of new Pokémon cards – the Mega Evolution: Delta Reign expansion sets and the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection (UPC) Day and Night collectors set.

The release comes as part of a resurgence for the Japanese game franchise in their 30th anniversary year.

As well as the much-anticipated cards, the 30th Celebration Ditto Premium Collection & Figure Collections (Mew & Mewtwo) were also released today along with a match foil promo card and a large ‘showcase’

However, not all shops in York chose to have their customers queue with independently owned trading card game shop, Rocket’s Hideout in Micklegate, stating that they would not placing the items on general sale and would instead wait to ensure they went to regular customers.

In a post on Facebook, they said: “We don't feel that allowing these to go to whoever can queue the longest is the fairest way to do it.

“I’ve had over 50 people contact me who potentially have never shopped with us and people offering to camp out all night if we have them.

“125+ people signed up and only 40ish of these customers have visited over the past 3 weeks. I realise there will be some that don't find this fair, but with such limited availability we won't be able to please everyone.

“Apologies in advance, wouldn't want people to waste their time travelling to us if you are just after a UPC tomorrow.”
