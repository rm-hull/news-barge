NO child should wake up without a gift on Christmas Day.

That is the message behind The Salvation Army’s annual Christmas Present Appeal in Keighley, which is now underway and aims to bring festive cheer to families in need.

The charity is asking the public to donate new, unwrapped toys and gifts for children who might otherwise go without this Christmas.

Last year, the Keighley branch distributed more than 1,500 toys to around 500 children, provided toys to community centres, and provided support to Ilkley Foodbank.

More than 175 families also received a family food parcel.

Penny Southgate, Keighley Salvation Army community centre manager, said: "This Christmas, families are wondering how they'll get through the week.

"Our Christmas Present Appeal is an opportunity for people in our community to embrace the spirit of Christmas and give to families and individuals who have next to nothing."

Donations can be dropped off at Sainsbury’s, Asda (from November 1), or the Keighley Salvation Army church on High Street.

The deadline for donations is December 5.

Suitable gifts include educational toys, baby clothes, books, dolls, action figures, sports equipment, toiletries, clothing, and gift vouchers for older children.

The Salvation Army will distribute the gifts through local groups, schools and social services.

Ms Southgate said: "The charity is always overwhelmed by the generosity of the public.

"We hope the good people of Keighley can help us again to make sure every child wakes up on Christmas Day with a smile on their face."

Donations can also be made online via the Keighley Salvation Army JustGiving page.
