Dramatic pictures show the aftermath of a serious crash which saw a van smash into a tree as the road remains closed.

A large cordon has been erected following a crash on the A68 Woodland Road, in the early hours of Friday morning.

The images show the black van has sustained severe damage to the front of the vehicle after colliding with a tree.

Police officers can be seen standing guard at a cordon stretching along Woodland Road, with the route closed in both directions.

The crash has sparked widespread disruption, with nearby roads also shut, bus services diverted and pedestrians prevented from entering the cordoned-off area.

Durham Police has released a statement confirming that Woodland Road is closed between Greenbank Road and Stanhope Road North, with Outram Street, Maude Street and West Crescent also affected.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678317.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A crash has happened on Woodland Road, Darlington, this morning (November 6) *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The closure extends to pedestrians, with officers warning that the road is expected to remain shut for several hours while enquiries are carried out.

In an updated statement, a Durham Police spokesperson said: "Emergency services are currently dealing with a serious collision on Woodland Road in Darlington.

"The road is closed between the junctions of Greenbank Road and Stanhope Road North. Other roads affected in the closure are Outram Street, Maude Street, and West Crescent.

"The closure also includes pedestrian access.

"The road is expected to be closed for several hours while police carry out enquiries at the scene - members of the public are advised to find an alternative route."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679568.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police on Woodland Road, Darlington *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that it attended the scene.

An NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Woodland Road in Darlington at 2.05am on Friday 6th November 2026.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene."

Arriva has confirmed that buses are unable to travel along Woodland Road until further notice, with passengers advised to use alternative stops at Cockerton when travelling towards Darlington town centre.

Darlington Memorial Hospital has also issued a reminder about the road closure, with patients advised to allow additional time for their journeys.

Businesses in the surrounding area have also been affected by the ongoing police operation.

The Pasta Shop Darlington took the decision to close this morning.

A statement from the business read: "Unfortunately, due to a serious incident in the area this morning, the police have closed the surrounding roads, and there is currently no access to our shop.

"Sadly, we can't even reach the premises ourselves, so The Pasta Shop will remain closed today, Friday 6th November.

"We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this may cause and really appreciate your understanding. Unfortunately, the situation is completely beyond our control.

"Our thoughts are with everyone affected by this morning's incident and we sincerely hope everyone is safe."

The AA previously issued an alert describing the incident as a serious accident, with Woodland Road closed in both directions.

There is currently no confirmed time for when the road will reopen.

It is not yet known if anyone has been injured in the crash.
