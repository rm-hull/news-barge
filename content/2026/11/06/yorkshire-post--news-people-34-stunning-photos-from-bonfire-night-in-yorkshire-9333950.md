Fireworks lit up the sky, while a huge bonfire cast a warm glow over all who attended the event at the Leeds pub on Thursday (November 5) night. People young and old wrapped up warm and enjoyed the spectacular display.

The Yorkshire Evening Post sent a photographer down to capture the highlights from the night. You can see 34 of our stunning pictures in the gallery below.

It includes pictures of the crowds, captured enjoying both the huge bonfire and the spectacular fireworks display.

Did you go to Kirkstall Bridge Inn on Bonfire Night, can you spot yourself in the gallery?
