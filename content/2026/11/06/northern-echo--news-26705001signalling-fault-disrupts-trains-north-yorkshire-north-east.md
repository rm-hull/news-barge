Rail passengers travelling through North Yorkshire and Teesside are facing disruption this morning after a signalling fault blocked lines between Northallerton and Yarm.

National Rail said the fault is affecting services between Northallerton and Sunderland and between Northallerton and Redcar Central, with trains being cancelled, delayed by up to 30 minutes or revised.

The issue was first reported at 9.12am on Friday, November 6, and disruption is expected to continue until around 11.15am.

Train operators affected include Grand Central and TransPennine Express.

Grand Central services between London King's Cross and Sunderland, along with TransPennine Express trains running between Manchester Airport and Redcar Central, are being impacted by the disruption.

National Rail said all lines between Northallerton and Yarm were blocked because of the signalling fault.

Passengers travelling to or from Yarm have been advised to travel via Eaglescliffe using Northern services between Darlington and Redcar Central.

TransPennine Express customers can also use their tickets at no extra cost on alternative services, including Northern trains between Darlington and Redcar Central, TransPennine Express services between Darlington and Manchester, and LNER and CrossCountry services between Darlington and York.

Rail passengers whose trains are cancelled can travel on alternative services operated by participating train companies up to two hours before or two hours after their original departure time.

Customers who decide not to travel, or who are unable to complete their journey because of cancellations, are entitled to a full refund from their original point of sale.

National Rail has advised passengers to check their journey before travelling and warned that delays and cancellations could continue until late morning while engineers work to resolve the problem.
