The countdown to Christmas in York is on – with just one week to go until the city’s Christmas Market returns.

From handcrafted treasures and thoughtful gifts to tempting Yorkshire treats, the traders at York Christmas Market 2026 are getting ready for opening day.

Soon, Parliament Street and St Sampson’s Square will be transformed by the Market’s iconic Alpine Chalets, packed with unique finds, delicious festive flavours and plenty of inspiration for Christmas shopping.

York Christmas Market returns from **12 November to 21 December 2026**, opening Wednesday to Monday each week.

This year’s line-up brings together new faces and familiar favourites, with talented makers and independent businesses showcasing the best of what Yorkshire has to offer.

Organised by Make It York, York Christmas Market is a celebration of local creativity, craftsmanship and community spirit, with 85% of traders based in Yorkshire and 100% of food traders representing the region.
