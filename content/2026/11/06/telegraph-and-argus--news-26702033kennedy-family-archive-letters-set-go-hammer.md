AN archive of letters linked to the Kennedy family is set to go under the hammer.

Expected to fetch between £2,000 and £3,000, the collection will be sold during Tennants Auctioneers’ Books, Maps & Manuscripts Sale on November 25.

The archive features correspondence from Rose Kennedy, mother of US president John F Kennedy and senator Robert F Kennedy, both of whom were assassinated.

A spokesman for Tennants Auctioneers said: "The archive is an intriguing and previously unknown collection of documents relating to one of the most important American political families.

"Most of the letters are to her English friend and travel partner Marie Bruce.

"particularly those written soon after the deaths of her son and husband."

Other highlights in the sale include a collection of 16th and 17th century historical and heraldic manuscripts linked to Sir Henry St George and Sir Richard St George, estimated at £300–£500.

There is also a rare copy of Prelude by modernist writer Katherine Mansfield, expected to fetch £800–£1,200.

A 1935 edition of Andreas Vesalius’s anatomical work Bruxellensis Icones Anatomicae is valued at £1,500–£2,000.
