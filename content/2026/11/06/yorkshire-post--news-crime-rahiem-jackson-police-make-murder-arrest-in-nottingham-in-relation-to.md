# Rahiem Jackson: Police make murder arrest in Nottingham over fatal Yorkshire attack

## Police investigating a murder in Yorkshire last month have arrested a man in Nottingham.

The 28-year-old man was arrested in the East Midlands city on Thursday (Nov 5) in connection with the death of Rahiem Jackson in Hedley Chase, New Wortley, on October 24.

A 39-year-old man, who was arrested on suspicion of murder on Wednesday (Nov 4), has been bailed pending further enquiries.

Mr Jackson died after he was attacked on Hedley Chase in New Wortley on October 24, West Yorkshire Police had previously said.

A 26-year-old man - Michael Gyedu, of Rathmell Road, whose self-defined ethnicity is listed as 'Any Other Black' - has been charged with murder appeared at Leeds Magistrates Court on November 2.

In a tribute, released last week, Raheim’s family said: “Rahiem was loved and adored by his sisters and family. He had a beautiful heart and beautiful soul; his smile was so infectious, and he lit up the room with his presence.

![Rahiem Jackson has been named as the man killed in Leeds](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/27/17/14/Leeds-murder-victim-named.png?trim=100,0,100,0&width=640&quality=65)


“Rahiem would have gone above and beyond for anybody he cared for."

They added: “He wasn’t just a brother he was a son, an uncle, a father, a partner, a nephew, a cousin, a grandson, brother in law and friend and he did not deserve to die the way he did.

“We are heartbroken and in so much pain and have been left inconsolable as a family. If anyone has any information to help the police find the persons who murdered Rahiem, please can you come forward.”
