Armed police were called to a town centre after reports of a man with a weapon.

Officers were called to George Square in Halifax shortly after 2.05pm on Friday, November 6, following reports of a man holding what was thought to be an offensive weapon from an upper window in a nearby building.

A spokesperson for the force said: "A spokesperson for the force said: "Police were called to George Square, Halifax, shortly after 2.05pm this afternoon (6 November) following reports of a man holding what was thought to be an offensive weapon from an upper window in a nearby building.

"The area was cordoned off and officers gained entry to the building.

"Further enquiries established it was a false call with good intent."
