MORE than 50 artists will feature in this year’s Richmond Rotary Charity Art Exhibition.

Well-known North Yorkshire artist Lucy Pittaway is among those supporting this year’s event and will attend the opening night at The Station in Richmond, where she will take part in a Q&A session and meet guests.

The exhibition will run from Friday, November 6, to November 17, raising funds for Rotary causes, The Station and Motor Neurone Disease charities.

Organised by the Rotary Club of Richmond, the exhibition will showcase a wide variety of original artwork across different mediums, all available for purchase.

Dr Richard Gibson from Richmond Rotary said: "This exhibition has always been about community.

"It gives local artists the chance to exhibit their work, attracts visitors to Richmond, and raises important funds for charities.

"We are delighted that Lucy Pittaway is supporting the event and helping us shine a spotlight on the incredible artistic talent we have in our area."

Ms Pittaway said: "I am delighted to support Richmond Rotary’s Charity Art Exhibition.

"It is a wonderful opportunity to celebrate the creativity we have here in North Yorkshire while helping to raise money for causes that have a real impact on people’s lives.

"Events like this bring communities together and provide a fantastic platform for local artists to showcase their work."

Organisers hope the exhibition will encourage people of all ages to engage with the arts, and students and aspiring artists are especially being encouraged to attend.

A raffle will also take place during the event, featuring a framed Lucy Pittaway artwork valued at more than £250 as one of the prizes.

The exhibition opens with a preview evening at The Station on November 6 from 6pm.

Tickets for the preview night cost £7 and include a glass of wine and nibbles.
