## New Christmas journey to Santa's Grotto launches in Harrogate

Weetons of Harrogate has launched The Weetons Christmas Journey to Santa’s Grotto *(Image: Weetons)*

A new Christmas experience is giving families the chance to embark on a magical festive adventure.

Weetons of Harrogate has launched The Weetons Christmas Journey to Santa’s Grotto, a new family event at its Leeds Road store running from December 5 to 24.

The experience includes interactive activities, storytelling, and a special Christmas bear for each child to adopt and take home.

Rebecca Germaine, Food Services Manager at Weetons, said: "We wanted to create something that goes beyond the traditional visit to Santa.

"Christmas is all about those little moments of magic that children remember, and we've put so much thought into making every part of the journey feel special.

"It's an experience families can enjoy together, with something meaningful for children to take home and treasure long after Christmas."

The journey begins at Elf Check-In before moving on to Mrs Claus’ Christmas Table, where children can decorate a festive cookie and enjoy a mini hot chocolate.

Each child then chooses a Christmas bear before entering Santa’s Grotto for a story and a personalised adoption certificate.

The experience costs £40 per child and includes all activities, the bear, and entry for up to two adults.

Weetons is also hosting wreath-making workshops and its popular Breakfast with Santa.
