College staff battled through a gruelling coastal walk to raise money for Darlington Mind.

Darlington College volunteers walked 17 miles from Saltburn to Sandsend to raise money for Darlington Mind, one of the college’s two chosen charities.

The staff who took part were Emma Davis, Becky Winnard, Amy McMillan-Tait, Mick O’Reilly, Clare Cox, Andy Wears, Mel Gibson, Louise Snowdon, Carrie Morrell, Ashlea Harding, Ian Venis, Phil Robson, Fiona Ellgood, Jonny Brown and Becca Lister.

A charity padel tournament and bake sale took the total raised to £3,662.

A spokesman for Darlington Mind said: “College staff got stuck into all sorts of fundraising events and we are very grateful to everyone who took part, organised an activity, or made a donation.

“We are an independent local Mind, so the money they’ve raised will stay in our area, supporting people with their mental health.”

Darlington College’s head of marketing and engagement Claire Hankey said: “Our amazing staff are to be congratulated for supporting a fantastic local charity.
