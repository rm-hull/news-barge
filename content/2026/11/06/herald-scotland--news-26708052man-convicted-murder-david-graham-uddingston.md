A convicted killer has been found guilty of the murder of a father in a brutal machete attack.

Liam Kippen struck David Graham before the 38 year-old victim's wife went to his aid and screamed for help for her dying husband.

The 31 year-old lied that he only hit David – a relative stranger to him – because he was apparently armed and feared he was going to be assaulted.

After the attack in Uddingston, Lanarkshire, Kippen fled the scene, the weapon was dumped in a river before he spent the night in a hotel enjoying a beer.

He was today found guilty of the October 6, 2024, murder following a trial at the High Court in Glasgow.

Jurors then learned Kippen had previously been jailed for the culpable homicide of a man in 2016.

Kippen faces life behind bars when he is sentenced next month.

![Glasgow High Court, aka the High Court of Justiciary. Photograph by Colin Mearns 11 November 2024](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/19141757.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Liam Kippen found guilty of murder following a trial at the High Court in Glasgow *(Image: Colin Mearns)*

David had been out with his wife Donna Graham that afternoon – including having a meal together before tragedy struck.

They had then planned to pick up some laundry before David believed he had left a door open at the home of his late father, so he went there instead.

It was on getting to Uddingston that he was fatally attacked.

Donna – a law firm admin worker – told jurors she got a call from her husband's number, but all she initially heard was “a lot of noise” leaving her “confused”.

She believed there was also mention of “call the police”.

Donna immediately went to where she thought David would be and soon heard shouting.

The witness then recalled seeing her husband running and someone “chasing” him.

She initially thought the other person – Kippen - had a golf club. David was struck by the killer.

Jurors were shown a short clip that Donna managed to capture of Kippen moments after the attack.

She then went on to frantically bang on doors yelling: “Please help me phone an ambulance, help me.”

David sadly never recovered in what she agreed was “the worst day of her life”.

Kippen gave evidence and told how he had been outside the home of a man who David and his family had seemingly some previous “difficulties” with.

The convicted thug claimed he was “scared” and his “adrenaline” was going as David apparently came running towards the property.

It was moments later Kippen said his friend gave him a weapon. The killer further claimed he effectively had nowhere to run and feared he was the one going to “end up dead”.

Tony Graham KC, defending, put to him: “Was it your intention to hurt him?”

Kippen: “I did not want any of this. I was defending myself. I did not think this would happen.”

He claimed it was only later that he discovered on the news that David had died.

Prosecutor Leanne McQuillan later stated to Kippen that the victim did not have a knife and was running away when he was struck.

Lord Harrower today told Kippen: “What started as an ordinary day for David Graham and his wife ended in tragedy leaving an incalcuable void in the lives of his family.”

Co-accused Hugh McBean, 30, and James Cairney, 43 – neither faced the murder charge – were found guilty today of attempting to defeat the ends of justice by assisting Kippen after the attack.

They were also remanded in custody and will return with Kippen next month.
