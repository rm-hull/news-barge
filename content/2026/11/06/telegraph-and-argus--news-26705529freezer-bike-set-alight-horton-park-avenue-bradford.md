A bike and freezer were on fire in Bradford on Bonfire Night.

This comes after a spate of incidents that occurred over the course of the night.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a fire broke out in the open on Horton Park Avenue at 9pm.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "There was a fire in the open on Horton Park Avenue at 9pm.

"One crew attended waste, including a bike and a freezer, which were on fire, and extinguished it using a hose reel."
