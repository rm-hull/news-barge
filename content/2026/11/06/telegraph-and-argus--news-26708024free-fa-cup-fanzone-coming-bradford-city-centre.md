Fans are being invited to kick off their matchday in style at an FA Cup FanZone in the heart of the city.

The free event will take place at Darley Street Market in Bradford ahead of Bradford City AFC’s Emirates FA Cup First Round tie against Marine AFC.

Running from 12pm to 2.30pm on Saturday, November 7, the FanZone will offer pre-match entertainment and activities for supporters of all ages in the market’s courtyard.

### 'We're pleased to be hosting this special FA Cup edition'

Councillor Ian Walker, Bradford Council's Executive Member for Neighbourhoods and Communities, said: "Our FanZones have become a popular feature of Bradford City's home league matchdays, bringing supporters into the city centre and helping showcase everything Darley Street Market has to offer.

"We're pleased to be hosting this special FA Cup edition, creating a great atmosphere ahead of what promises to be an exciting cup tie at Valley Parade.

"We hope visitors will take the opportunity to browse our fantastic independent traders, enjoy the entertainment and sample the food and drink available in the Street Kitchen."

### Face painting, garden games and penalty shoot-out challenges

The FanZone for this match builds on the successful programme of matchday events held at Darley Street Market during Bradford City AFC's home league fixtures which began at the end of last season.

Aimed at building cup-tie excitement in the city centre, the event will feature outdoor bars from Molson Coors and Northern Monk, who will be serving their Bantams lager, Take Me Home.

Fans can enjoy free face painting, garden games, football highlights on big screens, and a penalty shoot-out challenge with club mascot Billy Bantam.

### Live entertainment to include music and breakdancing

Inside the Street Kitchen, visitors can take part in Mario Kart 8 Deluxe and FC27 gaming, while exploring a variety of food and drink options before and after the match.

Live entertainment will include music in the courtyard and a performance by singer Cornell Daley in the Street Kitchen from 12pm to 2pm.

Tranquil Productions will return with their breakdancing showcases, scheduled for 1.45pm in the courtyard and 2.15pm in the Street Kitchen.

The FanZone is open to both home and away supporters.
