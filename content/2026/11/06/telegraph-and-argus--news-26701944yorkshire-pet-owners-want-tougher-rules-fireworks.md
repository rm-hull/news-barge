PET owners are calling for quieter fireworks and stricter regulations to protect animals during the fireworks season.

New research from Animal Friends Pet Insurance highlights growing concern in Yorkshire and the Humber, where 62 per cent of pet owners are worried about the impact of fireworks on their animals.

Jen Hipwell, head of veterinary services at Animal Friends Pet Insurance, said: "For many pets and owners, fireworks are no longer confined to Bonfire Night.

"Displays can start around Halloween and continue right through to New Year, creating weeks of uncertainty for animals that are sensitive to loud noises and disruption."

Despite 61 per cent of owners in the region feeling prepared for the season, only 10 per cent have a detailed plan to support their pets.

Nearly a quarter (23 per cent) admit they have no plan and do not intend to make one.

A majority of owners (82 per cent) believe more should be done to improve safety for pets, wildlife and livestock.
