A farming conference will return to Yorkshire in 2027 with a focus on collaboration.

The 2027 Yorkshire Farming Conference, held at the Great Yorkshire Events Centre, aims to inspire fresh thinking in agriculture by showcasing successful examples of farmers working together to reach new markets.

This year’s programme includes a session on direct selling and farmer collaboration, chaired by Pasture for Life regional manager Rob Bunn.

James Newhouse, a founding member of The Farmers’ Larder, said: "The Yorkshire Farming Conference is a great event to meet farmers doing amazing things from all over our amazing county.

"Getting together to share stories over a brew is what it’s all about.

"I can’t wait to attend and share our story, but more importantly to listen to all the other good things going on and maybe steal a few good ideas."

Mr Newhouse farms a small hill farm in the Dales with his wife Amy and their two children, focusing on nature-friendly livestock and breeding hardy Belted Galloway cattle and Swaledale and Herdwick sheep.

He was recently named the 2026 Farm of the Future Award winner by the Royal Agricultural Society of England.

The conference is organised by the Yorkshire Agricultural Society.

It will feature Mr Newhouse, fellow Farmers’ Larder suppliers Holly Story and Alan Robinson, and Andy Swinscoe, owner of The Courtyard Dairy.

The Farmers’ Larder is a collective of six Yorkshire Dales family farms that stock and manage a frozen meat vending machine at The Courtyard Dairy in Settle.

Mr Swinscoe, who runs the specialist cheesemonger with his wife Kathy, said: "What a great opportunity to drive forward the discussion in connecting the future of food and farming.

"The Conference is going to be a fabulous event to bring some of the top minds from across the food and farming spectrum together."

The 2026 conference drew more than 700 farmers and agricultural workers.

More than 60 businesses and organisations supported the sold-out trade show.

David Tite, manager of the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, said: "I’m really excited about the positivity the Conference will bring.

"This is an event for farmers to hear from farmers, learn from successes, share common ground in the challenges and feel the weight of support and energy this community has by working together."

Tickets for the 2027 Yorkshire Farming Conference, taking place on Wednesday, January 27, are now available at yorkshirefarmingconference.co.uk.
