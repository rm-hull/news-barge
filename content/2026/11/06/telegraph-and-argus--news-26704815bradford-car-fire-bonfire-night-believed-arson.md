A car fire on a major Bradford road has been recorded as suspected deliberate ignition by the fire service.

The Bolton Road incident was reported to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) at 11.21pm last night (November 5).

One crew put out the fire.

The fire service believes the blaze was started deliberately.

## What the fire service could say

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "We were called to reports of a car on fire on Bolton Road at 23.21 yesterday.

"One crew attended and extinguished this.

"It’s been recorded as suspected deliberate ignition."

Bolton Road runs through Bradford, linking the city centre with Undercliffe, Eccleshill and areas further north-east.

West Yorkshire Police have been contacted for a comment.
