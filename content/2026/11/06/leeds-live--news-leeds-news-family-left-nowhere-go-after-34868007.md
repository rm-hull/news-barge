A couple have been left with "nowhere to go" after paying a £750 deposit to rent a home before being blocked by the owners of the property.

Chantelle and her partner, both from Gloucester, have spent seven years on the road living in an RV. However, her partner was recently diagnosed with fibromyalgia, meaning the pair are looking to settle down.

As she was looking for somewhere to live, Chantelle spotted a bungalow for rent in Leeds on Gumtree. The property, in Swillington, was perfect for the couple, who struggle with health problems.

They went to visit, meeting a wife and husband who said they owned the property, and paid a £750 deposit. It was agreed that Chantelle and her partner would move in on October 23, and spent a great deal of time talking at the house during a visit.

Chantelle sold the couple's RV ahead of them moving in, but communication soon started to break down. She received a message asking if it she was the one renting the property, which she thought was odd, and then found herself blocked on all platforms.

She said she has been left feeling "violated and vulnerable as a result", and with both Chantelle and her partner suffering from health issues, this situation has only made things worse. Chantelle said: "We trusted someone and they violated us. We can't trust anyone anymore there could be the most genuine people out there and were still not trust them because of these selfish people."

She added: "We have no where to turn to, no where to go and we're just broken."

Chantelle's partner did follow up with some research, and found the house actually belonged to Leeds City Council and not the couple who had shown them around. A spokesperson for Leeds City Council said: "We would advise anyone who believes they, or someone they know, may have been a victim of housing fraud involving a Leeds City Council property to contact us by phone on 0800 188 4000 or 0113 376 0410, or by email at [tenancyfraud@leeds.gov.uk](mailto:tenancyfraud@leeds.gov.uk), so our officers can investigate further.

"We will investigate all reports of tenancy fraud and take action against offenders. People who have made money from tenancy fraud will also be made to pay this back if they are convicted."

Chantelle also saw the same property listed on Gumtree after she had been blocked although this has since been taken down. Gumtree said the company had taken action against the listing.

A Gumtree spokesperson said: "We take reports of fraudulent activity extremely seriously and do not tolerate fraudulent listings on our platform. Following a review of the account in question, we have taken immediate action to block it.

"We would urge anyone affected to contact our customer support team if they have not already done so, so we can provide appropriate assistance. We will also support any investigation by the relevant authorities through our dedicated law enforcement channels.

"We encourage anyone looking to rent a property to carry out appropriate checks on both the property and landlord before making any payment, and to follow the latest guidance available through the Gumtree Safety & Advice Hub and government and local authority websites."

Finally, Chantelle has also been in contact with the police who are now investigating what happened. West Yorkshire Police said: "Officers were contacted to report a fraud related incident on the 18 October. Enquiries are currently ongoing."
