Pauline Izat has been appointed permanent head of health at University Health & Social Care North Lanarkshire, after serving in the role on an interim basis.

She takes over following the retirement of Maggs Thomson.

Ms Izat began her career as a nurse with NHS Lanarkshire and has worked across acute and community healthcare settings.

Her experience includes emergency care, specialist nursing, team leadership and management, including responsibility for addiction services in North and South Lanarkshire.

Before becoming interim head of health, she was general manager of children’s health services.

Pauline Izat, head of health at University Health & Social Care North Lanarkshire, said: "It’s a real privilege to take on this role permanently, particularly after having the opportunity to work with so many fantastic colleagues during my interim appointment.

"Throughout my career, I’ve seen the difference that committed, compassionate staff make to people’s lives.

"I’m looking forward to working with colleagues and partners to keep improving services for the people of North Lanarkshire."
