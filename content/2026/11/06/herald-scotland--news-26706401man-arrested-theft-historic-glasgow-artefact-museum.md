A 71- year-old man has been arrested in connection with the theft of a historic Glasgow artefact from one of the city's museums.

On Thursday, October 23, 2025, police received a report of the theft of the Lipton Cup from a display case at the city's Riverside Museum.

Police said the theft happened between 5pm on Wednesday, October 22, and 10am on Thursday, October 23.

On Thursday, November 5, the 71-year-old man was arrested in connection with the theft.

The man was later released without charge and police enquiries remain ongoing, police said.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/20865720.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lipton Cup *(Image: Glasgow Life/PA)*

The cup was given to Glasgow merchant Thomas Lipton in New York in 1930 after his fifth attempt to win the America’s Cup, a global prize in yacht racing.

Following his death, it was given to the city of Glasgow.

The cup, which is reportedly worth nearly £500,000, has been circulated as a stolen artefact via Interpol and other industry stolen property registers.

It is described as gold, with two handles, a detachable domed lid, set atop a silver square plinth base and has an enamelled red, white and blue shield and gold ropework.
