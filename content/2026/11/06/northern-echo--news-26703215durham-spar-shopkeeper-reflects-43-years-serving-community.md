A shopkeeper has reflected on 43 years of retail service as Spar marks its 70th anniversary in the UK.

Ram Sundavadra, who runs the Spar Belmont store in the Carrville area of Durham with his wife Raju, has worked at the same site since 1983 and has become a familiar face in the community.

The couple have become known not only for their store but for their community spirit and support over the years.

Mr Sundavadra said: "Running a shop is a way of life and it is still enjoyable to serve local people in this part of Durham, which we have done for four decades.

"It seems unbelievable to think it has been that length of time, and well over half the period Spar brand has been in the UK."

Mrs Sundavadra has largely stepped back from the business after suffering a stroke in 2021 but continues to help where she can.

She was awarded a Special Recognition award in 2024 from the Federation of Independent Retailers for her work during the pandemic, which included organising food parcel deliveries across the Durham area.

Despite changes in the retail industry and numerous accolades, the Sundavadras have remained focused on customer service, value, and a personal touch.

Mr Sundavadra said: "Spar was up and coming with the ‘8 till late’ change to opening hours, and we started to get fresh food three days a week from our wholesale partner James Hall & Co. Ltd.

"This was a big positive as it meant I no longer needed to go to the market every day to purchase fresh produce.

"Fresh food deliveries progressed to become six days a week, and we used to have our own deli where we would cook our own meats."

He described running the shop as "a way of life" and said the couple has served "generations of the same family" over the years.

Spar UK is celebrating 70 years since the brand arrived from the Netherlands in 1956.

James Hall & Co. Ltd, a fifth-generation family business, supplies independent Spar retailers and company-owned stores across the North of England.
