A van fire spread to the ground floor of a building on Bonfire Night.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed crews attended a van and building blaze on Carlisle Street, Manningham, late on November 5.

## What the T&A saw

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene on Friday morning, a burnt-out white vehicle, debris outside and a large number of rugs could be seen.

## What the fire service could say

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 22.43 to Carlisle Street to reports of a van fire which had spread to the ground floor of a building.
