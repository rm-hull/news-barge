A family-run butcher and bakery in the Yorkshire Dales has made a surprise appearance in Tesco's Christmas advert for 2026.

J W Cockett and Son, which has been serving customers in Hawes for more than 170 years, features in a blink-and-you'll-miss-it moment in the supermarket giant's festive campaign.

The fifth-generation family business, based on Main Street in the Wensleydale market town, is among the locations to appear in the advert.

The three-minute film, titled The Wrong Address, stars comedian Roisin Conaty and actress Anna Maxwell Martin in a festive story about friendship, kindness and an unexpected Christmas road trip.

And eagle-eyed viewers watching the advert may be able to spot the historic Yorkshire Dales butcher's shop.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680362.jpg?type=mds-article-620)J W Cockett and Son, which has been serving customers in Hawes for more than 170 years appears in the Tesco 2026 TV Christmas advert *(Image: TESCO)*

The inclusion puts a business which has been part of Hawes since 1854 in front of a national audience as Tesco launches its Christmas advertising campaign.

J W Cockett and Son remains one of the town's long-standing independent businesses, combining a traditional butchery and bakery under one roof.

Now run by the fifth generation of the Cockett family, the shop is particularly well known for its pork pies, sausages, pastries and traditional fruitcake.

Its appearance in the festive advert comes as Tesco tells the story of two former school friends who unexpectedly reunite after a Christmas delivery goes wrong.

Roisin Conaty plays Polly, a Tesco delivery driver who discovers that Lizzy, played by Anna Maxwell Martin, has accidentally ordered her family's Christmas groceries to her old address.

With Lizzy now living hundreds of miles away, Polly offers to help get the delivery closer to its intended destination.

The advert is accompanied by The Police's hit Every Little Thing She Does Is Magic.

Speaking about the campaign, Roisin Conaty said: "I love that this story centres on female friendship. It starts in a way many people can relate to, all stress and mayhem, but shows how one small act of kindness can bring people back together after years apart.

"It's lovely to show Polly and Lizzie reuniting and celebrating kindness and grace. Also, the silliness we can only feel with old friends alongside the magic of Christmas."

For J W Cockett and Son, the appearance is another interesting chapter in a history stretching back to the Victorian era.

Founded in 1854, the shop has continued trading through generations of the same family and remains a popular stop for residents, tourists and walkers exploring the Yorkshire Dales.

Its traditional pork pies have earned particular praise from visitors, while the bakery also produces bread, sausage rolls, cakes, pastries and its signature Cocketts fruitcake.

The business prides itself on using local suppliers wherever possible, reflecting its position in the heart of the farming communities of Wensleydale.

Customer reviews have previously described its pork pies as "legendary", with some visitors saying they travel considerable distances to buy them.
