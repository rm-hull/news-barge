A County Durham road was left covered in water after a burst pipe sent water gushing through the carriageway.

The pipe is understood to have burst at about 4pm today (Friday, November 6), in Sunnybrow, close to the school and St John’s Gardens.

Pictures from the scene show water bubbling up through the road and gushing across the carriageway as Northumbrian Water crews attend.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682364.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The pipe is understood to have burst at around 4pm on Friday, November 6, in Sunnybrow, close to the school and St John’s Gardens. *(Image: Paul Willis)*

Several Northumbrian Water vans can be seen parked nearby with amber warning lights flashing as water continues to cover part of the road.

Vehicles were still making their way through the affected stretch, sending huge sprays of water into the air as they passed.

![The pipe is understood to have burst at around 4pm on Friday, November 6, in Sunnybrow, close to the school and St John’s Gardens.](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682366.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The pipe is understood to have burst at around 4pm on Friday, November 6, in Sunnybrow, close to the school and St John’s Gardens. *(Image: Paul Willis)*

One dramatic photograph shows a car ploughing through the water, creating a large wave of spray across the road.

Other pictures show water rising from the road surface and flowing along the carriageway, while Northumbrian Water vans gathered nearby.

Northumbrian Water has been contacted by The Northern Echo for more information.
