More than £590,000 has been awarded to nine community organisations across Argyll through the first round of a funding scheme run by SSEN Transmission.

The Argyll and Kintyre 275kV Local Fund has distributed £592,770.85 as part of a wider £3.2 million scheme supporting communities affected by electricity transmission infrastructure.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21682202.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Snowdrop Argyll has been awarded £220,000 to reduce isolation and improve wellbeing through community-led activities, outreach, peer support and welcoming spaces.  *(Image: Supplied)*

The grants will support projects covering health and wellbeing, community facilities, environmental initiatives and youth development.

Among the largest awards, Snowdrop Argyll Ltd received £220,000 for outreach, peer support and community activities.

Mid Argyll Youth Development Services (MAYDS) was awarded £125,000 to complete its WheelPark project, while Clachan Village Hall (Kintyre) SCIO received £100,000 to develop its community hub and emergency centre.

The GRAB Trust received £60,000 for environmental work, including marine clean-ups and skills workshops.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21682203.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Clachan Village Hall (Kintyre) SCIO received £100,000 to support its development as a community hub.  *(Image: Supplied)*

Other funding recipients include Ford Village Hall Committee, which will use its grant to upgrade kitchen facilities to catering standard, allowing it to provide hot food during emergencies.

The fund is intended to benefit communities hosting infrastructure associated with the Argyll and Kintyre 275kV Reinforcement Project, which aims to strengthen the region’s electricity transmission network.

Linsay Chalmers, community investment manager for SSEN Transmission’s community benefit funds in Argyll, said: "We’re delighted to announce the first awards from our Argyll and Kintyre Community 275kV Local Fund, with more than £590,000 going to nine organisations delivering projects across the area."

Clachan Village Hall trustee Malcolm Ward said: "We are extremely grateful to SSEN Transmission and the Argyll and Kintyre 275kV Large Projects Fund for this generous £100,000 award.

"The funding will help provide catering and accessible toilet facilities, allowing Clachan Village Hall to become a fully functional community hub for local people."

Ford Village Hall Committee secretary Steve Walker said: "This grant to upgrade the kitchen facilities to catering standard will provide the community with a kitchen that can supply hot food to local people in times of crisis, adding to our resilience abilities."

Fiona Kalache, manager at MAYDS, said: "This Wheel Park will be a great asset for the local community, and in particular children and young people.

"We thank all those who have supported and dedicated time to this development over the last several years including funders, local contractors, and the wider community."

Karen McCurry, centre manager for Snowdrop Argyll, said: "For Snowdrop Argyll, this funding means so much more than financial support.

"It gives us the stability and opportunity to strengthen the relationships and community connections that have been at the heart of our work for 30 years."
