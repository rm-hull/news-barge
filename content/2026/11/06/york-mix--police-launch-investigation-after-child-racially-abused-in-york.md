Police want to trace this man after an incident on a main York road.

They want to speak to him after a group of people, including a 12-year-old child, were subjected to racist verbal abuse in Blossom Street.

It happened on Tuesday 13 October.

#### Blossom Street, York

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the person pictured as we believe that he may have information that will assist our investigation.”
