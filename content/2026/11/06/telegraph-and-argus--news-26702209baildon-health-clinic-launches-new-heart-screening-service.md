A HEALTH clinic has launched a new heart screening service.

Based in Baildon, Bradford, BloodWorks Health and Wellbeing Ltd officially launched the service on October 31.

It is the first of its kind in the UK to combine advanced blood screening with specialist-reviewed 12-lead ECG assessments in a community setting.

Joseph Kean, director and SRO of BloodWorks, said: "For years, I've been involved in delivering specialist clinics that help people understand their health through blood screening.

"We've been able to identify cardiovascular risk factors through blood analysis, but we've always recognised that blood results alone cannot provide the complete picture.

"This partnership allows us to take a significant step forward, combining the biochemical information we already provide with specialist cardiac assessment."

Developed in partnership with CardioLogic Diagnostics, the Advanced Heart Health Screening service includes 32 blood markers and a resting ECG reviewed by a cardiac specialist.

It also incorporates onsite health measurements.

The service aims to make preventative care more accessible and help individuals understand their heart health before symptoms appear.

BloodWorks Health and Wellbeing Ltd was established in April 2025 and is led by professionals from clinical, public health, fitness and social science backgrounds.
