## The 7th World Forum on Intercultural Dialogue in Baku brought together cultural figures, religious leaders and international representatives to explore how art, faith and dialogue can bridge divisions, challenge prejudice and promote peace and mutual understanding.

Culture has long played a role in connecting communities across borders, offering opportunities for exchange even when political differences remain. From artistic expression to religious traditions, shared experiences can open conversations that might otherwise be difficult.

At the 7th World Forum on Intercultural Dialogue, discussions focused on the role of the arts in challenging stereotypes and the importance of interfaith understanding. Speakers highlighted peace, human dignity and respect for different beliefs, while personal encounters demonstrated how a single conversation can change perspectives. The discussions also reflected Azerbaijan's position at the crossroads of different cultures and traditions.
