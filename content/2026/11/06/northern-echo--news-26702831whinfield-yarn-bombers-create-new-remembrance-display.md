A group of talented knitters have transformed a Darlington neighbourhood with a poignant display ahead of Remembrance Sunday.

The Whinfield Yarn Bombers have once again decorated the area with their handmade creations, this time producing a Remembrance display honouring those who lost their lives in conflict.

The community group is made up entirely of volunteers and has become well known for bringing colourful creations to Whinfield throughout the year.

Darlington councillors Jamie Bartch and Andy Keir praised the latest display, describing it as a "beautiful and thoughtful tribute".

In a joint post on Facebook, they said: "The Whinfield Yarn Bombers have once again done an incredible job with their Remembrance display.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675583.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Yarnbombing creations made by The Whinfield Yarn Bombers. *(Image: WHINFIELD NEWS)*

"A truly beautiful and thoughtful tribute, reflecting the time, care and talent that has gone into creating it.

"A huge thank you to everyone involved. It’s a wonderful addition to the community and a fitting way to honour those who made the ultimate sacrifice.

"Lest We Forget."

The Whinfield Yarn Bombers regularly decorate parts of the neighbourhood to mark different seasons, celebrations and community events.

Previous creations have included displays for Easter, Christmas, Remembrance and the summer holidays, with railings, post boxes and bollards among the locations given a knitted makeover.

Earlier this year, the group welcomed the summer with colourful creations featuring mermaids, birds and fish.

The volunteers have continued producing their displays despite being targeted by vandals in September 2024, when two creations were stolen and another was ripped from a bollard.

The incident did not stop the knitters, who have continued to create new displays for the community.

Their work has previously been praised by residents for helping to brighten up the Whinfield area.
