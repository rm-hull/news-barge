Glasgow’s Citizens Theatre has been named Great British Theatre 2026 at the inaugural Great British Theatres Awards, also taking home the Great Renovation Award.

The double win recognises the theatre’s transformation following a major refurbishment and its reopening in August 2025 after seven years of closure.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21487208.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Citizens Theatre *(Image: Newsquest)*

The original Victorian auditorium remains at the heart of the refurbished building, which now includes upgraded facilities and new spaces for artists, audiences and the local community.

Judges praised the redevelopment for respecting the theatre’s heritage while preparing it for the future.

Joshua McTaggart, chief executive of the Theatres Trust, said: "What the Citizens really demonstrated is bringing past, present, and future together – really thinking about where the building has come from and also navigating some of the challenges that presents, while also really thinking about what a theatre of the future needs to be, while being really confidently rooted in its local area.

"The return of a reinvigorated Citz is not just a boost for the Gorbals neighbourhood or the city of Glasgow, but for the nationwide theatre community."
