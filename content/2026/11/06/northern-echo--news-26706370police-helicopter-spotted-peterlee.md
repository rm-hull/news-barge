A police helicopter was seen hovering over County Durham yesterday afternoon (November 5).

Emergency services have now confirmed the nature of the incident.

Those living in the Peterlee and Horden areas reported hearing the helicopter overhead at around 3.50pm.

The National Police Air Service (NPAS) has confirmed that the incident was related to the search of a suspect car.

NPAS helped Durham Police try to track down the vehicle.

A spokesperson for the National Police Air Service said: "At 3.51pm, on Thursday, November 5, a police helicopter crew from NPAS Newcastle was deployed to Peterlee to assist Durham Constabulary in a search for a vehicle.

"Total flying time on the task was 20 minutes."
