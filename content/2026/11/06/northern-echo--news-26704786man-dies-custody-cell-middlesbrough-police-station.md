A man has died in custody after being found unresponsive at Middlesbrough Police Station.

An investigation has been launched following the death of a 31-year-old man at the Bridge Street West station on Thursday morning (November 5).

The detainee - who was arrested at 10pm the night before - had been found unresponsive by staff just before 6am and was sadly pronounced dead by paramedics at 7.01am.

The Northern Echo understands that people in custody were transferred out of the station during the ordeal and sent to Durham Police’s custody cells.

Usual custody arrangements in Middlesbrough are now in place.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) launched an independent investigation at 7.07am and has already sent investigators to the custody suite.

The watchdog said the man was checked into custody after being arrested by Cleveland Police just before 10pm on Wednesday and was put on half hourly checks by staff.

The man's family has been informed.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: “Sadly, we can confirm that a detainee has died while in custody at Middlesbrough Police Station.

"The circumstances surrounding this death are currently being established, and it would be inappropriate to comment further at this time. The family of the deceased have been informed.

"As is standard practice, we have referred this incident to the IOPC, which is conducting an independent investigation."

An IOPC spokesperson said: “The IOPC is independently investigating the death of a 31-year-old man in police custody in Middlesbrough.

"Initial enquiries indicate the man was checked into custody after being arrested by Cleveland Police just before 10pm on Wednesday and was put on half hourly checks by custody detention staff.
