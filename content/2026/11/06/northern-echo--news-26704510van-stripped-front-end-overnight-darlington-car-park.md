A Northern Echo van was stripped of its front end in Darlington overnight.

The vehicle had the full bonnet section taken off it.

It's believed that the incident took place on Thursday (November 5) night or in the early hours of Friday (November 6).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678096.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A van with its front end missing in St Augustine's car park, Darlington *(Image: SUBMIT)*

In images, the vehicle can be seen with the bumper on the floor and no front bonnet section, while wiring can be seen hanging from the front of the van in the Darlington car park.

The front of the vehicle can't be seen in the car park in the images.

The Northern Echo has contacted Durham Police for comment.
