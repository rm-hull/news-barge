WORK is underway to replace the 'Tin footbridge' over the Leeds and Liverpool canal in Skipton.

The footbridge over the Leeds and Liverpool canal will be closed for several weeks up until December 18.

The canal beneath the bridge, which links Sackville Street and Lower Union Street, will also be closed while the work is taking place.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21681397.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The work to replace the Tin Bridge is set to take several weeks *(Image: Keith Huggins)*

Part of Cavendish Street car park will be closed during the work to accommodate a site compound, and there will be two full-day road closures on Saturday, November 21, when the footbridge is removed; and again on Saturday, November 28, when the new bridge is put into place.

A crane will be used on both occasions.

North Yorkshire Council says the bridge has reached the end of its life and has allocated around £500,000 for its replacement.

The old bridge, which it is believed was opened towards the end of 1912, will be removed in sections and transported by pontoon—a flat-bottomed boat—along the canal.

The new section will be delivered to the town's Cavendish Street Car Park and hauled into place using cranes and the canal pontoon.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21681376.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work is underway on a replacement for the Tin Bridge over the canal in Skipton *(Image: Tim Forman)*

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Council said: “The existing bridge will be removed on Saturday, November 21 between 6am and 11pm, and the new bridge will be installed on Saturday November 28, between 6am and 9pm."

“To safely remove and install the bridge, a crane will need to be positioned at the junction of Union Terrace and Union Wharf, immediately west of the bridge."

“This will require two full road closures.”

The spokesperson added: “Pedestrian access to properties within the affected area will be maintained via a temporary route through the private ginnel on Whitfield Court.”
