A UK snack subscription company has collapsed into administration after four years in business.

SnackVerse, founded in 2022, delivers monthly boxes of snacks from different countries to subscribers.

Customers can choose from three box sizes, starting at £7.99 for a box with at least five snacks.

A standard box with 10 or more snacks and a drink costs £13.99, while a premium box with 20 or more snacks and multiple drinks is priced at £23.99.

The company has more than 35,000 Instagram followers and has been promoted by several food influencers who reviewed its products.

## SnackVerse caught breaking advertising rules

Earlier this year, the brand came under scrutiny for breaking advertising rules.

In May, the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) ruled that SnackVerse had made "misleading claims" about previous snack boxes on its website.

A customer complained that they had not received boxes themed around the countries advertised, and the company was unable to provide evidence that other subscribers had received them.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21673430.jpg?type=mds-article-620)SnackVerse has come under scrutiny for breaking advertising rules. *(Image: Kennedy News & Media)*

On October 9, the ASA found that SnackVerse had also breached promotional marketing rules.

The company’s website featured a "50 per cent off" offer with a countdown timer, which restarted once it expired.

The ASA said it contacted the company but received no assurance that the website would be changed.

Details of the breach have been published on the ASA website and will remain there until Snackverse removes or amends the claims.

As of November 5, the countdown timer was still visible on the SnackVerse website.

## SnackVerse collapses into administration after 4 years

Now, after four years in business, SnackVerse has collapsed into administration, leaving the company's future unclear.

Kieran Bourne of Cromwell & Co Insolvency Practitioners was appointed administrator on November 2, according to The Gazette.

SnackVerse Limited and Cromwell & Co Insolvency Practitioners have been contacted for comment.
