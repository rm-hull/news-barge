The Piece Hall in Halifax is preparing for its Christmas celebrations, with *Emmerdale* actor Dominic Brunt set to switch on the venue’s festive lights on Friday 13 November.

Brunt, who plays Paddy Kirk in the ITV soap, will illuminate the 30-foot Christmas tree and switch on the Christmas sign at 4.30pm, on the opening day of the festive markets.

He will be joined by local basketball team Yorkshire Dragons and The Sing Space Choir, who will perform Christmas songs.

The free-entry Christmas markets will run from Friday to Sunday each week until 20 December, with around 50 Yorkshire-focused traders selling artisan gifts, jewellery, homeware and seasonal food.

Last year’s markets attracted around 276,000 visitors, and thousands more are expected to visit the historic courtyard in the run-up to Christmas.

The programme will include a traditional Victorian carousel, a market bar and live performances from local singers, community choirs and brass bands. The bandstand is sponsored by Heart.

Some of the latest bands from The Piece Hall Academy Artist Development Programme will also perform during the festive season.

Children’s craft activities and a Christmas trail will take place every Saturday and Sunday in December before Christmas.

The celebrations will conclude with a community event on Christmas Eve, featuring performances from Halifax Minster Choir at midday, Queensbury Brass Band at 2pm and vintage soloist Francesca Rosado at 4pm.

There will also be a pantomime in The Caygill Rooms on Wednesday 23 December. Produced by SD Theatre, *Goldilocks and the Three Bears* will feature songs, comedy and quick costume changes, with performances at 2pm and 5pm. It is suitable for children aged three and over.

> **Nicky Chance Thompson, Chief Executive of The Piece Hall Trust, said:** We are incredibly proud to be attracting so many visitors to Calderdale this Christmas while also bringing our local community together in this magnificent, historic setting at the most magical time of the year!
>
> Our traditional festive experience, complete with beautiful tree, vintage carousel, and live music, alongside the showcasing of our incredible independent shops and local market traders, creates a truly unique festive destination.
>
> A visit to The Piece Hall is a chance for everyone to make wonderful winter memories while discovering truly special gifts you won’t find on the high street, supporting small businesses and knowing their present choices are helping to preserve the past by supporting this unique heritage site.

The Piece Hall’s shops and boutiques will be open seven days a week, while its food and drink outlets will offer seasonal menus.

These include festive hot chocolate, Christmas cake with Wensleydale cheese and turkey, Brie and cranberry toasties at The Deli. The Astronomer Diner will serve seasonal drinks and food, including mulled wine, gingerbread lattes and turkey and stuffing sandwiches.

The Piece Hall originally opened in 1779 as a trading centre for Yorkshire’s handwoven woollen cloth. It is the only surviving Georgian cloth hall in the world and reopened following its transformation in August 2017.
