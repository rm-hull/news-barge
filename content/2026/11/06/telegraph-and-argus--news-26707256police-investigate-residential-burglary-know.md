Police are investigating a burglary that took place at a residential property in Bradford.

Officers were called to reports of an incident at a home on Chippendale Rise, Allerton, shortly after 8pm last night (Thursday).

The residnetial street sits roughly half way between the better-known roads of Rhodesway and Allerton Road.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to reports of a burglary at a residential address on Chippendale Rise, Bradford at around 8.02pm yesterday (Thursday 5 November).

"Enquires remain ongoing."

Police are urging anyone with information to contact them via [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/LiveChat) quoting reference 13260630394.
