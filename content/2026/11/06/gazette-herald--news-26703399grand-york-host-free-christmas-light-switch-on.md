The Grand, York is set to host its annual Christmas light switch-on.

The event is inviting the local community to celebrate the start of the festive season.

Open to the public and free to attend, the festivities will begin at 5pm on Friday, November 13, 2026, with the hotel's Christmas lights set to illuminate at 5.30pm.

Visitors can look forward to an evening of festive entertainment, seasonal treats, and opportunities to support local charitable initiatives.

Adding to the festive atmosphere, Yorkshire Voices will perform a selection of festive favourites throughout the evening.

Visitors will also be able to enjoy a selection of festive food and drink, including doughnuts, mulled wine, hot chocolate, and crumble.

The event will feature a Grand Raffle in support of Cash for Kids, helping to raise funds for the charity.

The Grand, York will also act as a designated Christmas gift drop-off point for the charity, giving visitors the opportunity to donate presents to those who may need a little extra support this Christmas.

Visitors can pick up a gift tag at the event and purchase a present to donate or bring along a gift they have already bought.
