The **676-square-metre display** featured images of**Elon Musk and Jeff Bezos** alongside the slogan**"Big Tech Bros suck our water, electricity, data"**. Drone footage showed campaigners spreading the enormous banner across a field beside a facility being developed by** US company STACK Infrastructure**.** Greenpeace is calling for a nationwide moratorium on new mega data centres** until binding regulations are introduced to protect energy supplies, water resources and the public interest.

The environmental organisation argues that the **rapid expansion of data centres is placing increasing pressure on local infrastructure**. According to Greenpeace, the two facilities planned in Beringen could together** consume around one and a half times as much electricity annually as the entire canton of Schaffhausen**, while requiring substantial amounts of water for cooling.

The group also questions how waste heat will be reused and who will ultimately benefit from the developments. The demonstration formed **part of an international campaign against the unchecked expansion of large-scale data centres**, with similar actions taking place across Europe. Greenpeace is urging authorities to** prioritise environmental protection and public needs over the commercial interests of technology companies**.
