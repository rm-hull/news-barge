WEST Yorkshire ranks among the highest in England for prescription spending.

According to a new study by Cured Pharmacy, NHS West Yorkshire Integrated Care Board (ICB) spent £292.42 per person on prescriptions.

The total cost reached £791,785,012 for its population of 2,707,693.

This placed it third nationally, behind Cambridgeshire and Peterborough (£379.82 per person) and Herefordshire and Worcestershire (£347.81 per person).

North West London recorded the lowest prescription spending at just £115.74 per person.

The study found West Yorkshire also had the highest number of prescription items per person at 35.5.

This suggests a clear link between volume and overall cost.

Tarun Kumar, co-founder and superintendent pharmacist of Cured Pharmacy, said: "What stands out is the enormous variation across England.

"Cambridgeshire and Peterborough spends more than three times per person what North West London does, and that gap is largely driven by the cost of individual prescriptions rather than the volume.

"This could reflect differences in the types of medications being prescribed, such as higher proportions of specialist or newer treatments in certain areas."

Integrated Care Boards are NHS organisations responsible for planning health services for their local population.
