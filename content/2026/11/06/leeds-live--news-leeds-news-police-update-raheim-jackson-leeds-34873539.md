# Police update on Raheim Jackson Leeds murder probe as man bailed

## Fourth Raheim Jackson murder arrest made by West Yorkshire Police

Leeds murder detectives investigating the death of Raheim Jackson have made a fourth arrest.

Mr Jackson, known as 'Reema', died in Leeds following "an attack with weapons" on October 24. Officers arrested a 28-year-old man in Nottingham on Thursday, November 5. He was arrested on suspicion of murder and remains in police custody.

A 39-year-old man, who was arrested on suspicion of murder on Wednesday (November 4), has been bailed pending further enquiries, the force say.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "Detectives investigating the murder of Raheim Jackson on Hedley Chase in Leeds on 24 October have arrested a further man. The 28-year-old man was arrested in Nottingham yesterday on suspicion of murder and remains in police custody.

"A 39-year-old man, who was arrested on suspicion of murder on Wednesday (November 4), has been bailed pending further enquiries. Enquiries into Raheim’s death remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team. Anyone with information which can assist the investigation is asked to contact us via 101 or use the Live Chat options here, quoting reference 13260606745

"Information also be given anonymously via the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 11 or online."
