Work has started to repair a burst water main that's closed a major County Durham road, with contractors expecting the stretch to remain closed over the weekend.

This morning (Friday, November 6), Northumbrian Water confirmed that the A689 at Howden-le-Wear is likely to remain shut throughout the weekend after teams discovered the extent of the damage beneath the road.

Contractors have been seen working at the site of the burst water main, with diggers and excavation trucks seen parked on the A689.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679643.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work starts on the burst water main at Howden-le-Wear, County Durham *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

Road closed signs and pavement closure signs have been seen on the road, while new pipework can be seen stacked up on the path.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679638.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work starts on the burst water main at Howden-le-Wear, County Durham *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

Digging work is expected to continue over the weekend, but Northumbria Water still hasn't confirmed how long residents can expect the road to be closed.

The water firm initially hoped to carry out repairs following the burst near the Gulf garage in the early hours of Thursday morning (November 5).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679637.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work starts on the burst water main at Howden-le-Wear, County Durham *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

However, after spending the day assessing the damage, engineers warned that the repair could now take several days to complete.

The A689 remains closed between Bridgefield Court and Park Terrace, with drivers facing diversions and disruption over the coming days.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679632.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work starts on the burst water main at Howden-le-Wear, County Durham *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

A spokesperson for Northumbrian Water said: “Our teams are still working on site following a burst on a water main at the A689 in Howden-le-Wear, County Durham.

“They have been assessing the damage throughout the day, and have discovered that this is an incredibly difficult repair, which we anticipate may take a few days to complete.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679630.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work starts on the burst water main at Howden-le-Wear, County Durham *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

“For the safety of road users and our teams, this means that the road closure between Bridgefield Court and Park Terrace will remain in place and diversions are likely to continue over the weekend.”

The burst sent water pouring onto the road, prompting an emergency response and the closure of the busy route.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679640.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Work starts on the burst water main at Howden-le-Wear, County Durham *(Image: PAUL WILLIS)*

Crews were able to isolate the damaged main, preventing any disruption to customers' water supplies.

However, the size and location of the burst mean the road cannot safely reopen while repairs continue.

Drivers are being urged to plan alternative routes and allow extra time for their journeys.

Northumbrian Water added: “Customer water supplies are not affected, and residents of Village Gate can still access the estate, but there is no through-access on the A689.

“Please allow for additional time when passing through this area, or plan any routes accordingly.

“We would like to apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience.”
