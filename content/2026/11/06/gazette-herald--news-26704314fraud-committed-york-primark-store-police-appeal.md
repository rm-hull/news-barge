North Yorkshire Police is investigating a fraud that took place at a national retailer in a York shopping park.

The county's force said it happened at Primark in Monks Cross Shopping Centre on two days last month, Thursday, October and Friday, October 9.

CCTV images have been released by the police of a man the force said it would like to speak to, saying he may have information which could assist the officer's investigation.

Police are urging any members of the public with information to call 101 with the reference 12260198840 or email PC Tyson 55 at [dom.tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:dom.tyson@northyorkshire.police.uk.)
