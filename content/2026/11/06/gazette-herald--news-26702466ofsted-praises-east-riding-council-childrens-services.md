Children’s services have been rated “Good” across the board following a recent Ofsted inspection.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council received the rating for its children’s services earlier this year, with inspectors praising improvements in support for children at home, in care, and those transitioning to independence.

Ofsted highlighted a strong culture of listening to children and involving them in service development and decision-making.

Councillor Victoria Aitken, cabinet member for children, families and education, said: "I’m delighted that the inspection recognises the work and progress that has taken place over the last few years to ensure we deliver good services.

"Our priority has been to ensure that children, young people and families receive the right support, at the right time, in the right place."

Inspectors found that social workers visit children regularly, understand their needs, and that support is well-coordinated with families and professionals.

Children in care were described as well looked after, most able to remain with carers into adulthood, making educational progress, and supported in developing independence skills.

Ofsted also noted improvements since the previous inspection, including stronger management oversight, better partnership working, and a continued focus on relational practice.

Leaders were praised for their clear understanding of areas needing further development.
