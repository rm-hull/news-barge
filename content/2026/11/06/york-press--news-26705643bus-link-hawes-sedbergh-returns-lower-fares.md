A community-run bus service is returning to help residents of two Yorkshire Dales towns stay connected, enjoy days out and support local businesses.

The Little White Bus is relaunching its popular Hawes Hopper service under a new name, The Dales Neighbour, providing a link between Hawes and Sedbergh.

The service aims to make it easier for people to visit friends, enjoy a change of scenery and access shops and cafés in the neighbouring town, particularly those who may otherwise struggle to make the journey.

Return fares have also been reduced from £10 to £7.50 to make the trips more affordable.

Three journeys are planned before Christmas, on Tuesdays November 17, December 1 and December 15, with organisers hoping the service will continue into next year.

The initiative has been welcomed by Councillor Jill McMullon, chair of Hawes and High Abbotside Parish Council.

She said: “A partnership with another town is an excellent idea, particularly in these difficult times – the more that we help each other, the better.”

The Little White Bus says the relaunched service is intended to strengthen the longstanding links between the two communities, giving residents an opportunity to swap their usual shopping destinations, meet friends and support businesses in both towns.

A spokesperson said: “We're thrilled to bring this much-requested service back under its new name, The Dales Neighbour.

“Hawes and Sedbergh have always enjoyed a strong neighbourly connection, this service is all about making it easy for people to visit, shop, walk and spend time in each other's communities.

“With lower fares and three journeys planned before Christmas, we hope to welcome both familiar faces and new passengers on board.”

The organisation hopes the service will also provide opportunities for socialising, with the journeys themselves offering passengers a chance to catch up with friends and neighbours.

The driving team is particularly looking forward to welcoming passengers back.

The spokesperson added: “Our drivers really enjoy hearing a chatty bus. We look forward to seeing people from Hawes and Sedbergh on the next three dates and then continuing next year.”

The Dales Neighbour will operate on selected Tuesdays, with the three pre-Christmas journeys providing an opportunity for residents to enjoy festive shopping, a café visit or simply a day out in the neighbouring town.

The relaunch comes as efforts continue to strengthen community transport across North Yorkshire. Earlier this year, councillors called for greater support for volunteer-led schemes, which are regarded as a lifeline in rural areas with limited bus services.

Although a proposal to establish a dedicated working group was not adopted, councillors agreed to raise the issue with York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority as it prepares to take over responsibility for funding and delivering community transport from April 2027.

Tickets can be booked through the excursions page at www.littlewhitebus.co.uk.
