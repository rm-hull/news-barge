ASPIRING authors are being offered a chance to learn about publishing from one of Yorkshire’s leading crime writers.

Bradford-based crime writer Liz Mistry will host The Publishing Roadshow – What's Right for You? at the Shipley Business Enterprise Hub inside Shipley Library on Saturday, December 5, 2026, starting at 10am.

The free event will guide writers through traditional, hybrid, and self-publishing options, with a focus on practical advice and real-world experience.

Councillor Andrew Judson, Bradford Council’s lead member for economy, regeneration and planning, said: "This event shows the variety of support and courses offered through Shipley’s Enterprise Library.

"It was established to offer support to people at all stages of their careers, in many different fields and the growing number of events being held is doing just that."

Attendees will receive guidance on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing, covering formatting, editing, cover design, and both eBook and paperback production.

Ms Mistry will also discuss common pitfalls and alternative publishing platforms.

She is the author of 18 crime novels—nine traditionally published and nine self-published—and is known for supporting and mentoring fellow writers.

The event is part of the Shipley Business Enterprise Hub’s expanding programme, which offers workshops for entrepreneurs, creatives, and residents.

Places are limited and advance booking is recommended.
