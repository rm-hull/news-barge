Knaresborough’s annual Christmas Market returns on Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December, bringing almost 80 stalls, festive entertainment and a firework finale to the town centre.

The traditional market will be centred around the Christmas tree in Knaresborough’s historic Market Place, with stalls selling seasonal goods, decorations, cards, gifts, home décor, jewellery, accessories, artisan products and locally sourced produce.

The market opens at 10am on Saturday 5 December. Visitors will also be able to enjoy mulled wine and performances from local dancers, brass bands and choirs, alongside the town’s independent shops.

## More festive events across the town

There will be plenty to explore beyond the Market Place, including Knaresborough Castle Keep and grounds, the Tudor Courthouse Museum and Knaresborough Heritage Centre.

Visitors can also see the Christmas Tree Festival at St John’s Parish Church or book tickets for the Christmas Experience at Mother Shipton’s Cave. Smaller indoor craft fairs will also be taking place around the town.

The Knaresborough Banking Hub on Kirkgate will open from 11am to 4pm on Sunday 6 December to provide visitors with access to cash.

> **Bill Taylor, Market Chair, said:**
>
> Last year’s market was a huge success and we were thrilled with the feedback from both traders and visitors. The 2026 market promises to be even bigger and there has been a really high demand from traders.
>
> Whilst a lot of our visitor favourites will be returning again this year, we’ve worked hard to attract some new stallholders too, including original Arts & Crafts, high quality hand crafted goods, products of local and regional excellence and seasonal stalls.
>
> We are also keen that the market benefits the town as a whole. Members of our volunteer committee are currently liaising with other local organisations and businesses to ensure that we are promoting the full range of festive events taking place in Knaresborough over the market weekend.

## Supporting local organisations

The market is organised by volunteers from Knaresborough Rotary Club, Knaresborough Lions and Knaresborough & District Chamber.

The event also provides financial support to Hearing Dogs for Deaf People, St John Ambulance, Knaresborough Museum Association and Claro Enterprises.

This year’s market is supported by Mayor of York and North Yorkshire David Skaith and part-funded by York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority through Eventure Knaresborough, via the Vibrant and Sustainable High Streets Fund.

> **Charlotte Gale, committee member, said:**
>
> We have a fantastic range of local independent shops, galleries, cafes and restaurants in town, not just in the market square, but on the surrounding streets and High Street.
>
> We hope that visitors to the market will also take the time to explore everything which Knaresborough has to offer. The businesses always make such an effort to decorate their windows for Christmas, which gives a wonderful festive feeling in town on the market weekend.
>
> Be sure to check out the Chrismouse advent display in the window of Buttercup Cafe on Butter Lane too.

## Firework finale and travel arrangements

The market weekend will close at 4.30pm on Sunday 6 December with a firework display against the backdrop of Knaresborough Viaduct and the Nidd Gorge. Organisers say the display is best viewed from a viewpoint overlooking the river.

The town centre will be largely pedestrianised during the event. Knaresborough railway and bus stations are within walking distance of the market.

A free Park and Ride service will operate throughout the weekend from the GSPK car park on Manse Lane (HG5 8LF) to Chapel Street, close to the Market Place. Buses will run from 8am to 5.30pm.

Organisers have also advised dog owners to consider whether their pets would be comfortable at the event. Although Knaresborough is a dog-friendly town, the market is expected to be very busy.

Further information, including entertainment timings, is available from the Knaresborough Christmas Market website.
