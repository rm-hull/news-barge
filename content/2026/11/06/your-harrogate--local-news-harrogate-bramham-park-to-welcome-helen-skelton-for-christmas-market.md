TV presenter Helen Skelton will join families at Bramham Park’s first Christmas market next week.

Helen will help visitors bake and decorate Christmas biscuits on board the Dr. Oetker Baking Bus when the market starts on Saturday 14th November.

The bus will stay at Bramham Park on Sunday 15th November, when families can decorate their own festive bakes.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6aabff60bda6d39b9e4.jpg)

More than 100 artisan traders will set up stalls in front of the house over the weekend, selling Christmas gifts, decorations, food and drink.

Bramham Park owners Nick and Rachel Lane Fox said:

> “We’re delighted to welcome Helen and Dr. Oetker to our family home”.

The 150-strong Yorkshire Rock Choir will sing seasonal songs from 1pm to 2pm on Saturday.

Michael Bublé tribute act Seth as Bublé will perform from 2pm to 3pm on Sunday.

The Mos Eisley Misfits are also due to appear on Sunday, weather permitting.

Bramham Park will sell traditional Christmas decorations at its own stall.

*Visitors who spend more than £20 there will receive a voucher code for 20% off Thursday and Friday car parking, admission and membership for the Bramham International Horse Trials in June 2027. Tickets bought on the day will cost £15 per vehicle, regardless of passenger numbers. The baking activities and face painting are free.*
