Six people have been arrested after police were attacked with fireworks and bricks in "shocking scenes" on Bonfire Night linked to "misinformation".

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680249.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene in Meadow Well, North Tyneside, today (November 6), the day after violence erupted last night when emergency services were attacked with fireworks, bricks and missiles during Bonfire Night mayhem *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

It was understood a protest was raised about fears a building would be used as a house of multi-occupancy (HMO), but that was not correct, and it was going to be a supermarket.

A group launched fireworks at the building and deliberately rammed it with a car, polic said.

When emergency services arrived, they were subject to attacks.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678064.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Riot police form a barrier as tensions escalate on the Meadow Well estate in North Shields, North Tyneside on Thursday (November 5) *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Several police officers were injured in the incident, while five police cars and three fire engines were also damaged.

Two men, aged 25 and 27, a woman aged 27, and three teenage boys have been arrested on suspicion of violent disorder and are currently in police custody.

Chief Superintendent Daniel Whyte, of Northumbria Police, said: “The shocking scenes we witnessed last night in the Meadow Well were completely unacceptable.

"Our officers and firefighters were met with violence, including fireworks and bricks being thrown at them and their vehicles.

"I want to recognise their commitment and professionalism and the ongoing support we have received from our partners.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678050.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Riot police form a barrier as tensions escalate on the Meadow Well estate in North Shields, North Tyneside on Thursday (November 5) *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

“We believe the disorder relates to misinformation that the building is going to be used as a House of Multi-occupancy (HMO), which is not accurate.

"The site is planned for use as a supermarket with flats above for private rent.

“We are sharing this information to stop speculation in the community.

“Quite simply, the disorder, violence and damage which has occurred will not be tolerated.

"Our response has been swift and robust with a full investigation ongoing to identify those responsible.

"We have already arrested six people in connection with what happened – but we haven't stopped there.

![Riot police form a barrier as tensions escalate on the Meadow Well estate in North Shields, North Tyneside on Thursday (November 5)](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678057.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Riot police form a barrier as tensions escalate on the Meadow Well estate in North Shields, North Tyneside on Thursday (November 5) *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

"Anyone involved should be expecting a knock on the door.

“We also recognise the overwhelming number of people in the community will condemn this type of behaviour, so would ask for their help at this time.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21680250.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene in Meadow Well, North Tyneside, today (November 6), the day after violence erupted last night when emergency services were attacked with fireworks, bricks and missiles during Bonfire Night mayhem *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

"Anyone who has information about what happened or footage from last night is encouraged to share this with us.

"We also know there will be concern in the community in relation to what happened and we will have an increased presence in the area to offer reassurance.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678053.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Riot police form a barrier as tensions escalate on the Meadow Well estate in North Shields, North Tyneside on Thursday (November 5) *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

"We would encourage people to speak to our officers."

Deputy Chief Fire Officer, Lynsey McVay from Tyne and Wear Fire and Rescue Service added: “Anti-social behaviour and criminality of this nature have no place in our communities. The actions witnessed last night put members of the public and emergency responders at risk, damaged property and disrupted the efforts of crews responding to protect the community."

Meanwhile, political leaders have condemned what happened in Meadow Well last night.

North Tyneside’s Elected Mayor, Karen Clark, said: “What happened in Meadow Well last night was appalling and completely unacceptable. There is no excuse for this kind of violence and disorder, particularly when emergency services are being attacked simply for doing their jobs."

Police and Crime Commissioner Susan Dungworth added: “The abhorrent scenes of anti-social behaviour and violence in Meadow Well last night are totally unacceptable and really show the harm and unwelcome tensions misinformation can cause.
