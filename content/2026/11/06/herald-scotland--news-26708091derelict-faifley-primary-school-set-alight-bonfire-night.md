A police investigation is underway after a derelict school in West Dunbartonshire was deliberately set ablaze on Bonfire Night.

Police and emergency crews were alerted to the old Edinbarnet Primary School building in Faifley at around 7.35pm on Thursday evening.

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service mobilised three fire appliances to the scene. Crews left the scene after extinguishing the blaze at 9:14pm.

There were no reported injuries.

![Edinbarnet Primary School pre demolition](https://www.clydebankpost.co.uk/resources/images/20074010.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Edinbarnet Primary School pre demolition *(Image: NQ)*

Police said the fire is being treated as wilful, with enquiries ongoing to establish the full circumstances.

The derelict school building has previously attracted vandals while it awaits its completed demolition.

A spokesperson for Police Scotland said: “Around 7.35pm on Thursday, 5 November, 2026 we were called to a report of a fire at a derelict building on Faifley Road, Clydebank.

“Emergency services attended, and the fire was extinguished by the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

“There were no reported injuries.

“The incident is being treated as wilful and enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances.”
