BRADFORD has been named one of the top 10 UK places most likely to wake up to snow on Christmas morning.

New research from Lapland Famille, based on 47 years of weather data, reveals that Bradford has recorded snow on the ground at 9am on Christmas Day four times since 1979—specifically in 1981, 1996, 2009, and 2010.

This places the city joint eighth in the UK rankings.

Alex Dyer, managing director at Lapland Famille, said: "Waking up to snow on Christmas Day is the picture on every card, but in most of the UK it has become very rare.

"None of the places we looked at has had it since 2010, so if your children have their hearts set on a snowy Christmas, it's worth looking further north.

"In Lapland, snow at Christmas is about as reliable as weather gets."

The research compared 67 UK towns and cities using Copernicus weather data.

Aberdeen topped the list with five snowy Christmas mornings since 1979, while others such as Bournemouth and Truro have not seen any during that period.

The last widespread white Christmas in the UK was in 2010, when the Met Office recorded snow lying at 83 per cent of its weather stations.
