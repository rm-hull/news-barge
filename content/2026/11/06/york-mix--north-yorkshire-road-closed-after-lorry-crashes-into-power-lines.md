A road is closed this afternoon after a lorry crashed into power lines.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said at 2.21pm: “Motorists and pedestrians are asked to avoid Hull Road (A63) in Hemingbrough near Selby following a collision involving a heavy goods vehicle and power lines.

“The road is currently closed while utilities work to make the area safe again.

#### Map location

“Please avoid the area and find an alternative route.

“Thank you for your understanding and patience.”
