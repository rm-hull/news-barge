MG Motor UK has been named Best Value Manufacturer 2027 at the Parkers Awards, recognising its affordability and continued sales growth.

The award highlights the brand’s approach of combining competitive prices with generous specifications, practicality and a range of powertrains, including petrol, Hybrid+, plug-in hybrid and fully electric models.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21679427.jpg?type=mds-article-620)MG Motor UK has been named Best Value Manufacturer 2027 at the Parkers Awards. *(Image: Supplied)*

The recognition comes during a record-breaking year for the manufacturer. MG registered 7,837 vehicles in October, making it the UK’s fourth best-selling car brand for the month and marking its seventh consecutive month of record sales.

Year-to-date registrations have reached 86,767, up 25% on the same period last year. The figure means MG has already exceeded its previous annual sales record, with two months of 2026 remaining.

The Parkers Awards have been relaunched for 2027 with a focus on UK car buyers. The new Best Value Manufacturer category recognises brands that offer value across the ownership experience, rather than simply low purchase prices.

Founded in England in 1924, MG now operates in 34 European markets through a network of more than 1,300 dealer partners. The manufacturer recorded more than 300,000 annual sales across the UK and Europe in 2025 and earlier this year celebrated delivery of its one-millionth vehicle in the European market.

David Allison, director of product and planning at MG Motor UK, said: "Customers want to feel they have spent their money well, every time they get behind the wheel.

"And this is MG’s commitment, with generous space, equipment, and technology—at great value prices—reflected across our entire model range."

Tom Webster, head of content at Parkers, said: "MG offers much more than a low purchase price.

"The value it provides comes in many forms, with elements such as the amount of equipment, space, and practicality all playing a role.

"To take the MG S9 as an example, it offers more space for seven than its main rivals, has a premium feel, and a simple and well-equipped line up.

"Value and simplicity in one package."
