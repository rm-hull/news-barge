A new café-bar has officially opened in Durham city centre, transforming the former Cosy Club site into what owners hope will become a new community hub.

Remo Lounge welcomed its first customers on Saturday, November 2, at The Riverwalk, taking over the venue previously occupied by sister brand Cosy Club.

Run by café-bar group Loungers, the venue will serve food and drinks from breakfast through to late evening, including brunch, evening meals, cocktails and coffee.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21677404.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: UNP MARK BICKERDIKE)*

Managers hope to make the popular riverside site a relaxed space for families, students, shoppers and community groups.

Conor Roberts, General Manager of Remo Lounge, said: "If the building looks familiar, that's because we were previously Cosy Club Durham, our sister brand.

"While the setting may be the same, Remo Lounge brings a completely new feel and a fresh experience to enjoy.

"We're thrilled to be opening our doors to the people of Durham and becoming a place where friends, families, neighbours and shoppers can come together over great food and drink in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere.

"We want Remo Lounge to be a real hub for the community, supporting local groups, hosting meet-ups and creating a space where everyone feels at home."

The all-day menu ranges from brunch and toasties to tapas, salads, burgers, tacos, noodles and fried chicken.

Separate vegan and gluten-free menus are also available.

Weekly promotions include Cheeky Mondays, when customers ordering a burger, main meal or salad get a free drink or pudding, and Tapas Tuesdays, offering three tapas dishes and a drink for £13.95 from 5pm.

Cocktail Club runs daily from 5pm until close, while a weekday lunch deal offers soup and half a cheese toastie for £6.95.

Families can use games, books, colouring activities, highchairs, baby-changing facilities and a dedicated children’s menu.

In a message to residents following the launch, the team said: "Durham, your new home from home has arrived.

"But what exactly is a Lounge? Well, it's so much more than somewhere to grab a bite to eat.

"It's somewhere to feel at home, catch up with friends, make new ones, enjoy fantastic food and drink, or simply take a little time out of your day."

The team invited clubs, community groups and coffee mornings to get in touch and use the venue.

"We believe great communities are built around bringing people together, and we'd love Remo to become a little hub for Durham," the statement added.
