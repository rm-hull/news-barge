IF YOU’RE talking comfort food, sticky toffee pudding ticks every box.

The moist sponge cake, containing finely chopped dates, covered with toffee sauce, warms your soul. Many enjoy it with custard or vanilla ice cream, but it is delicious on its own.

In traditional recipes the sponge is fairly dense and moist and is sometimes lightly flavoured with nuts or spices such as cloves. The toffee sauce is usually made from double cream and different dark sugars.

Sticky toffee pudding is popular across the UK, where its origin is disputed, but believed to be the Lake District. A relatively recent invention compared to traditional Victorian or ancient British puddings, it was popularised in the 1970s by hoteliers and chefs Francis Coulson and Robert Lee, who served it at the Sharrow Bay Country House hotel on Ullswater.

It’s still a popular winter warmer. I tested eight varieties to see whether any stood out from the crowd.

\*Sticky Toffee Puddings by Sainsbury’s, 220g (2x110g), £2.80

Dark brown in appearance, these puddings had a very generous amount of tasty sauce. The pudding itself had a moist, spongy texture with a good taste - I could detect dates and the toffee flavour really came through.

Perhaps because of the sauce, this was a very sticky pudding, perhaps beating the others to the ‘stickiest of all’ title.

Pros: texture, taste.

Cons: none.

Mark: 4/5

\*M&S Food All Butter Sticky Toffee Pudding, 230g, (2x115g), £3.30

An appetising dark sponge, although the sauce was quite thin in consistency. Texture-wise, it was soft and moist; the sauce had a good toffee taste. In the pudding I could detect the taste of dates above all else. This one had useful, clear warning strips for possible allergies on the film lid. A bit more expensive than the others.

Pros: texture (sponge), taste.

Cons: price.

Mark: 3/5

\*The Bakery at Asda Sticky Toffee Pudding, 210g (2x105g), £2.64

The pudding looked fine, and the sponge was nice and moist, but there wasn’t much sauce. It also didn’t offer much of a taste - it vaguely reminded me of a Jamaica ginger cake, even though it doesn’t contain ginger. Even the sauce was lacking in flavour.

Pros: Texture (sponge).

Cons: syrup, taste.

Mark: 2/5

\*The Dessert Menu Sticky Toffee Pudding, Aldi, 220g (2x110g), £1.99

An enticing toffee smell drifted from this dark brown pudding with a generous helping of sauce. The sponge was moist with a rich date flavour, while the golden toffee sauce was smooth and tasty. Yummy, yummy, yummy and a great price too. Perfect comfort food.

Pros: texture, taste, price.

Cons: none.

Mark: 5/5

\*Aunty’s Sticky Toffee Steamed Pudding, 2x95g, £1.50 (£1.25 with Nectar card), bought at Sainsbury’s

This one was not chilled. This one didn’t look like the others - the sponge was light in colour and, flecked with small pieces of date, it looked more like spotted dick. It was quite claggy in texture and there wasn’t much sauce. I quite liked the taste, although it wasn’t of toffee, more a plain sponge with chewy bits of date. This was the cheapest, but it wasn’t what I would expect of a sticky toffee pudding. Cheaper, but smaller than some others.

Pros: price.

Cons: consistency, taste.

Mark: 1/5

\*Tesco Sticky Toffee Puddings, 220gg, (2x110g), £2.80

These looked appetising and had a slight smell of toffee on opening. The sponge was a little rubbery in consistency and there wasn’t a great deal of sauce. It was sweet, but there wasn’t much flavour to speak of.

Pros: presentation.

Cons: taste.

Mark: 2/5

\*Morrisons Sticky Toffee Puddings, 220g, (2x100g), £2.60

These were a little uneven with sauce along one side only but the taste made up for that. The sponge puddings were soft and moist, and the sauce was delicious, offering a rich toffee taste - I would definitely curl up in front of the TV with this one on chilly autumn nights.

Pros: consistency, taste.

Cons: presentation.

Mark: 4/5

\*Mr Kipling Sticky Toffee Puddings, 2x95g, £1.50 (Bought at Morrisons)

This one was very dark in appearance and looked more like a chocolate pudding - I had to check the packaging to make sure I had bought the right one. The sponge had a light, moist consistency, but it was very sweet and tasted quite smoky, like treacle toffee. It could have done with a little more sauce too. Cheaper, but smaller than some others.

Pros: consistency.

Cons: appearance, taste.

Mark: 2/5
