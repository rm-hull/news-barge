Three Stockton gym members have rescued the club where they trained after stepping in to take over the business following its closure.

Paul Williams, Dominique Lawton and Phillip Edwards will reopen the former Elite gym in Preston Farm as Engage Fitness this month, ensuring the facility can continue serving members after its future was thrown into doubt.

The trio were regulars at the gym before becoming its new owners and said they felt compelled to act when the previous business closed.

Rather than see the club disappear, they joined forces to take on the site themselves, with the aim of preserving not only the gym but also the community that had grown around it.

In a joint statement, the directors said: "When you find somewhere you feel comfortable training, it becomes part of your life.

You get to know the coaches, you build friendships and you become part of a group of people who encourage each other.

"We knew the closure meant people could lose more than their gym.

They could lose a routine, a support network and somewhere they felt they belonged. That mattered to us because we were part of it too."

The venue, at Vanguard Court on Preston Farm Industrial Estate, will reopen under the Engage Fitness name in November, with familiar faces returning to help lead its next chapter.

Well-known coach Donna Pankhurst will be part of the coaching team, while former coach Joe Atkinson is returning as club manager.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678623.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The training area inside Engage Fitness, the Stockton gym being reopened by three former members after the closure of the previous business. Picture: Engage Fitness *(Image: Engage Fitness)*

The new owners say protecting the gym was only the first step, with plans to invest in the club and build on the foundations already in place.

They hope to grow membership, improve facilities and equipment over time, and create a sustainable future for a gym they believe plays an important role in the lives of many local people.

The directors said: "We want existing members to feel they're coming home, and new members to feel welcome from the moment they walk through the door.

"We want to build a successful gym, and we believe that starts with looking after the people inside it.

"We also want members to see the gym moving forward. Our vision is to keep reinvesting, listen to what people need and make thoughtful improvements as we grow.

"We want to protect what people already love about this place while giving them even more reasons to be part of its future."

Engage Fitness will hold an open morning tomorrow (Saturday, November 7), to mark the reopening.

The event will begin at 8am, with visitors invited to meet the team, look around the facilities and enjoy a coffee.

Two 45-minute HIIT classes will also be held at 8.30am and 10.30am for anyone wanting to experience the gym's training sessions first-hand.

For the three new owners, the takeover represents far more than a business venture.

They added: "The classes matter. The coaching matters. But so does someone knowing your name, noticing your progress and encouraging you when you're having a difficult day.

"That is what we want Engage Fitness to stand for: a place where people can get stronger, grow in confidence and feel part of something."
