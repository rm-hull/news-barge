Fire ripped through the bedroom of a York house last night (Thursday).

Three fire crews were called to the blaze on **Constantine Avenue** at**8.10pm**.

They found the bedroom well alight.

#### Constantine Avenue, York

They put out the flames but the bedroom was destroyed.

The family, including a mother, her boys and their dogs, were safely out of the property and no one was hurt.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said the cause was still being investigated.

Crews were due to return to the scene this morning.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/house-fire-constantine-avenue-york-gofundme.webp)

A fundraiser has now been set up to help the affected family by friend Becki Smith.

You can donate here.

She writes: “As you all will know the Portia Baker family home was devastated by fire last last night, 5th November, whilst she was at work.

“Thank god everyone is out and safe. However this situation has now left Portia and her boys with absolutely nothing but the clothes on their backs. Her whole life was in her home.

“I wanted to start this page in order for people whom love and care for Portia and her children to be able to help and support her through this tough time (those who know, know she’s already been through enough in life) to replace anything that she needs.

“Portia is the most kindest , caring selfless human , a fantastic mum , friend and grafter please give what you can big or small.”
