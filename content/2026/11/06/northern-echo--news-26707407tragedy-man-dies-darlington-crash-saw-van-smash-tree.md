A man has died in a crash which saw his van smash into a tree in Darlington in the early hours of this morning (November 6).

Police were called at around 2am after an LDV van collided with a tree on Woodland Road, near the junction with West Crescent.

Emergency services attended, but the driver, a man in his forties, who was the sole occupant of the vehicle, was pronounced dead at the scene.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678182.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police presence on Woodland Road following the crash *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

No other vehicles were involved in the incident.

The road was closed for several hours while investigators worked at the scene, but has since reopened.

Dramatic pictures show severe damage to the vehicle and a large cordon erected on the A68 Woodland Road.

Durham Police are investigating the crash and have asked anyone with information to come forward.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21678319.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Police presence on Woodland Road following the crash *(Image: NORTHERN ECHO)*

A force spokesperson said: "Anybody who witnessed the collision, or who has dashcam footage of the van, is asked to contact the Serious Collision Investigation Unit via [sciu@durham.police.uk](mailto:sciu@durham.police.uk) quoting incident number 28 of November 6."

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) also attended the incident.

An NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to a road traffic incident on Woodland Road in Darlington at 2.05am on Friday 6th November 2026.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews and a clinical team leader to the scene."

As reported the crash sparked widespread disruption, with nearby roads closed, bus services diverted and pedestrians prevented from entering the cordoned-off area.
