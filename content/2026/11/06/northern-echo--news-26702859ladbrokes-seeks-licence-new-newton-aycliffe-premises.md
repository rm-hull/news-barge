A betting shop is seeking to relocate its Newton Aycliffe shop.

Ladbrokes Betting & Gaming Limited has applied for a new premises licence for its shop at 62 Beveridge Way, Newton Aycliffe, under the Gambling Act 2005.

If approved, the company will surrender its current licence at 41 Beveridge Way once the new premises are ready to operate.

This means the number of betting shops in the area will remain unchanged.

The application has been submitted to Durham County Council's Environment, Health & Consumer Protection department.

Details of the application can be accessed through the licensing authority.

Written representations about the application are welcomed from individuals living close to the premises, those with potentially affected business interests, or representatives of the aforementioned groups.

These must be submitted by November 25, 2026.
