Police mounted extra Bonfire Night patrols to tackle anti-social behaviour, seizing a quad bike in the operation.

Keighley neighbourhood officers were backed by community mediators, youth workers and West Yorkshire Police Specials.

A force spokesperson said: "This evening, officers from Keighley Neighbourhood Policing Team have been carrying out increased patrols to tackle anti-social behaviour as bonfire night got into full swing.

"Utilising a mixture of uniformed and plain-clothed officers, we were supported by some amazing volunteer members of the community, including community mediators, local youth services, and West Yorkshire Police Specials."

While most people celebrated safely, police received numerous reports of fireworks being used irresponsibly, including some being directed at officers and vehicles.

No damage was reported and no officers were hurt.

Several people were dealt with other firework and anti-social behaviour offences.

Plain-clothes officers seized a quad bike after spotting it being ridden on Keighley’s Bracken Bank estate.

The quad bike had no number plates and was uninsured.

A police spokesperson added: "Unfortunately for the two males, who looked rather cosy sharing its single seat, the quad bike was found to be lacking in any form of number plates or insurance, and soon found itself on the back of our recovery truck."
