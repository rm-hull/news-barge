Three people have been charged in connection with alleged offences under the Terrorism Act following a protest in support of Palestine Action in Edinburgh.

The demonstration took place on Saturday, October 31, outside the US consulate on the city's Regent Terrace.

It coincided with a similar protest taking place outside the US embassy in London.

Police Scotland said that on Friday, two women and a man were charged in connection with alleged terrorism offences relating to supporting a proscribed organisation.

The force said: “A report will be submitted to the Procurator Fiscal.

“Extensive enquiries remain ongoing to identify people seen wearing T-shirts and holding banners showing support for a proscribed organisation.

“Evidence is being reviewed and further action will be taken as appropriate.”
