An eye clinic in Harrogate says it has carried out nearly 2,350 cataract operations since opening in November 2024.

Newmedica Harrogate reports that it has delivered more than 3,372 appointments and treatments during its first two years of operation, supporting NHS and private patients from across North Yorkshire.

As well as cataract treatment, the clinic provides services including pre-assessments, glaucoma treatment, oculoplastic procedures and YAG laser treatment.

Steve Kirk, Operations Director and Partner at Newmedica Harrogate, said the clinic had focused on making specialist eye care more accessible to people across Harrogate, Ripon and the surrounding area.

> **Steve Kirk, Operations Director and Partner at Newmedica Harrogate, said:**
>
> Celebrating two years in Harrogate is a tremendous moment for our team. From the outset, our focus has always been to support as many people as possible with their eye health, making high-quality ophthalmology care more accessible to communities across Harrogate, Ripon and the wider North Yorkshire area.

## Support for Harrogate Town

The clinic has also supported local community activities, including a partnership with Harrogate Town AFC.

Newmedica has sponsored a matchday and pitch-side advertising banner, as well as providing support for the club’s walking football initiative through sponsorship of players’ bibs.

The initiative, run through Harrogate Town’s Community Foundation, provides an opportunity for people to remain active and social through football.

Steve Kirk recently joined the group for a match, scoring twice in an 8-6 victory.

> **Lynsey Barraclough, Head of Community Foundation at Harrogate Town AFC, said:**
>
> We’re extremely grateful to the team at Newmedica Harrogate and for their continued support throughout this partnership. Having Steve join us on the pitch at our recent match was great fun, and we can’t thank him enough.

Newmedica Harrogate is owned and run by consultant ophthalmologists Aldrin Khan, Kamran Khan, David Dunleavy and David Richards, alongside Operations Director Steve Kirk.
