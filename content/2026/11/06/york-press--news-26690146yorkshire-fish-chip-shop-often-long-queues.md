If you are craving fish and chips, you might think you need to take a long trip to the coast to grab some.

However, there are many incredible fish and chip shops dotted around North Yorkshire that bring the coast to you.

The White Horse Café is a traditional fish and chip shop found in the heart of Thirsk that has been serving the community since 1982.

If you are desperate for a taste of the seaside and are near Thirsk, this might be the perfect spot for you.

![The exterior of a cafe](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21658544.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can also dine in at The White Horse Café *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is the White Horse Café like?

The Almond family have been running the White Horse Café for decades, and the shop often has “queuing customers snaking out of the doors” on busy weekends.

The fish and chip shop’s menu has a range of classic options such as fish and chips, fishcakes, and scampi.

For those who do not like fish, there are plenty of alternatives like sausage, spam fritter, and chicken bites.

There are also lots of traditional sides such as bread rolls, pickled onions, and pickled eggs.

You can also add a few sauces to your meal, like curry sauce, gravy, tartare sauce or just some vinegar.

You can also enjoy a meal in the restaurant area if you prefer to sit down.

## What do reviews say about the White Horse Café?
