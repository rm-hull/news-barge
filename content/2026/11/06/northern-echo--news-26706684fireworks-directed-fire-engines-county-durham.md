Fireworks were directed at fire engines responding to incidents across County Durham on Bonfire Night.

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service said firefighters were targeted while attending incidents during one of the busiest periods for emergency services.

Thankfully, no firefighters were injured

A spokesperson for the service said: “Sadly, there were incidents where fireworks were directed at our fire appliances, although thankfully no firefighters were injured.

"Bonfire Night is always a busy period for the emergency services, and our Fire Control team once again worked side-by-side with police colleagues to help keep our crews and communities safe.

“This close working relationship meant vital information could be shared quickly as incidents developed, helping us to coordinate our response effectively.

“A huge thank you to everyone who worked through Bonfire Night, and to our blue light partners for their continued support. And a thank you to the vast majority of our communities who enjoyed a safe and respectful Bonfire Night.”

Fire crews on Teesside also reported being attacked as they faced a 70 per cent surge in Bonfire Night incidents.
