An award-winning South Asian restaurant is preparing to open the doors to its long-awaited Durham city venue, with bookings open for the Christmas period.

Babul's is putting the finishing touches to its new restaurant at Unit 17, The Riverwalk, with sites already in Darlington and Barnard Castle.

The restaurant has been in the works since early 2025 and bosses had previously hoped to welcome customers this summer.

However, after what creative director Zak Ahmed described as a "tough" journey involving a number of setbacks, the finish line is now in sight.

While Babul's has not yet announced an official opening date, customers can make bookings from November 27 onwards, including for the busy festive period.

Mr Ahmed said: "Everyone's excited. Obviously, it's been quite a stressful time getting everything ready, so the excitement hasn't kicked in yet.

"But because it's now at that stage where it's décor, finishing touches and ordering the last bits of equipment, it's starting to come together.

"There's a light at the end of the tunnel."

Babul's branding has already appeared at the venue as preparations continue ahead of its opening.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21674175.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The venue has been in the works since early 2025. *(Image: BABULS)*

However, the long-awaited venue has not been without its challenges.

Mr Ahmed said: "It's been tough. It's been hard and we've faced several problems, but we're overcoming them and we want to get it right.

"That's why we're not announcing a date just yet because we want to be sure that we hit the target that we've set ourselves.

"We should have been open months ago, but we're opening at the busiest time of year, so it's going to be interesting."

Customers can now book tables for dates from November 27 through the Babul's website, with the official opening date expected to be announced soon.
