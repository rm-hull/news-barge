Retailers have revealed their toy picks for Christmas 2026, with everything from LEGO's latest botanical creations to Pokémon collectibles and a new-generation kids' tablet featuring on festive shopping lists - with huge buys at Amazon, Argos and a host of independent shops.

Amazon's annual Toys We Love 2026 selection highlights the toys, games and gadgets it expects to be popular with families this Christmas, including familiar favourites, trending collectibles and new launches.

Among the products making the cut are a LEGO Botanicals Rocking Plants set priced at £13.49, Mattel's UNO Liar's card game at £10.03 and a Pokémon Mega Moonlit Tin featuring Mega Gengar ex, costing £42.38.

For children who love building and collecting, there is also a Mattel Brick Shop Hot Wheels Lamborghini Miura, although this comes with a much bigger £119.99 price tag.

Other picks include a Star Wars Grogu Action Buddy, Disney's Minnie Mouse Feature Doll and a Playmobil Mars Research Rocket developed around the European Space Agency theme.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21681900.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Star Wars Action Buddy Grogu 25-cm Animatronic, Interactive Plush, Action Figure, The Mandalorian *(Image: Amazon)*

Amazon customers can browse the full Toys We Love 2026 selection at www.amazon.co.uk/toyswelove2026.

The retailer has also sent out its Kids Toys Book and made it available to download, bringing together a wider range of toys and games to help shoppers find gifts for children this Christmas.

Amazon says Prime members can enjoy fast, free delivery on eligible products, which could also come in handy for those leaving their festive shopping until the last minute.

## Argos reveals Black Friday toy deals ahead of Christmas 2026

Argos is also getting into the festive shopping spirit, launching what it describes as its biggest-ever Black Friday event, with a month of deals across toys, technology, homeware, gaming, beauty and appliances.

The retailer is offering discounts on major brands including LEGO, Dyson, Shark, GHD, Garmin and Samsung, with shoppers able to browse deals online and in stores.

Among the toy offers are discounts on popular children's favourites, including Bluey, Paw Patrol, VTech and Little People.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682110.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fat Brain Toys Air Toobz *(Image: Argos)*

## Ms Rachel launches £11.99 Yoto Christmas gift for children

Parents looking for Christmas presents for younger children could add a new Ms Rachel music release to their shopping lists this year.

The popular children's entertainer has teamed up with interactive audio platform Yoto to bring her debut studio album, I'm So Happy, to its library from November 12.

The Yoto Card is available to pre-order now for £11.99 and features all 23 tracks from the album, including favourites such as I Love a Rainbow, Big Feelings, Hop Little Bunnies and Icky Sticky Bubblegum, alongside classic nursery rhymes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682204.jpg?type=mds-article-620)I'm So Happy, the debut studio album from Rachel and Aron Accurso, will arrive in Yoto’s library from 12th November. *(Image: Yoto)*

Ms Rachel and Mr Aron said: “Music is at the heart of our show and we’re so happy (jump, clap, spin!) that children can choose and play the songs independently with their Yoto players!”

The album reached number one on Billboard's Kid Albums chart following its release. Ms Rachel's YouTube channel has more than 21.5 million subscribers and nearly 18 billion views.

The card will also be available in discounted bundles from November 12:

* **I'm So Happy + Yoto Mini 4th Generation – £91.78**, saving 10%.
* **I'm So Happy + Yoto Player 4th Generation – £118.78**, saving 10%.
* **I'm So Happy + Yoto Mini 4th Generation + Adventure Jacket – £109.77**, saving 10%.
* **I'm So Happy + Yoto Player 4th Generation + Adventure Jacket – £141.27**, saving 10%.

The card works with all Yoto Players, including the fourth-generation models, as well as the free Yoto app.

It can be pre-ordered from Yoto.
