Tankind founder Coral Glover has handed over her Harrogate tanning and beauty salon to a new owner.

Coral put the Knaresborough Road salon up for sale in September 2025.

It features a reception and waiting area, a nail station, five tanning booths and a beauty treatment room.

Coral said:

> “I began Tankind 10 years ago with the vision of creating an innovative tanning and beauty salon - and I have achieved that and more.
>
> “I’d like to thank all my fantastic clients, many of whom have supported the salon from its opening.”

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/68b93341a32eb.jpg)

New owner Hannah Bairstow, who lives locally and has a background in sales and business development, said she had always wanted to run her own business.

Hannah said:

> “I'd like to say a huge thank you to Coral for everything she's put into building the business over the years and for her support throughout the handover.
>
> “I'm looking forward to putting my own stamp on Tankind, building on its success and creating a fresh, welcoming and friendly salon for both existing and new customers.”

In a message to customers, Hannah added:

> “I'll be spending plenty of time in the salon, so please pop in, say hello and introduce yourself!”
