*IMAGO / Mike Schmidt*

Chancellor Friedrich Merz has cancelled his trip to this year’s COP31 climate conference in Turkey due to “other commitments”. His decision has sparked outrage among climate activists across Germany.

A group of activists from Fridays for Future projected huge images of the CDU leader onto a Bundestag building, accompanied by messages such as ‘Climate summit without Merz’ and ‘We’ll show Merz what the future is’.

“The fact that Merz isn’t attending the UN summit and climate conference shows once again that he couldn’t care less about the future of young people,” said Fridays for Future. “The Chancellor is abandoning us, but we will never give up on our future.” The initiative announced further protests on November 13 at more than 70 locations across Germany.

Luisa Neubauer of the climate movement had already called the absence of the CDU leader an “absolute taboo violation” the day before, especially in a year with record heat and numerous other climate disasters in Europe.

A government spokesman in Berlin did not give any specific reasons for Merz’s cancellation, saying only that there were “always different interests and different scheduling conflicts”.
