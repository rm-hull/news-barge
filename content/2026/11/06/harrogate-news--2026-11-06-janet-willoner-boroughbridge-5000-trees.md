A grandmother from Boroughbridge who has grown 5,000 native trees from seed will feature on BBC Two’s Gardeners’ World tonight (Friday 6 November).

Janet Willoner began growing trees in her back garden in 2019 after watching her grandchildren take part in the school climate strikes.

She taught herself how to raise native trees from seed, setting herself a target of growing 5,000 young trees ready for planting. She has now reached that goal.

Janet has donated the trees to Yorkshire-based rewilding organisation Make It Wild, which has planted them across its nature reserves. Planting began beside ancient woodland at Summerbridge.

Make It Wild said Janet’s efforts had made a significant contribution to its woodland creation work and wanted to publicly recognise her achievement.

> **Chris Neave, co-founder of Make It Wild, said:**
>
> Janet is an amazing lady and worthy of all the praise and awards she has received for her diligent work growing trees from seed over many years. We have been honoured to take her tree seedlings and plant them in our nature reserves. Her efforts have made a huge difference to our woodland creation. We are immensely grateful. Her approach perfectly aligns with Make it Wild’s aims to protect and enhance the UK’s biodiversity.

Janet and her work will be featured on *Gardeners’ World* at 8pm tonight on BBC Two.
