Police are investigating a series of burglaries, including in villages near Selby and Thirsk.

They say homes have been broken into in Cliffe near Selby and Cowesby near Thirsk.

The Mill Hill Lane area of Harrogate and Hill Top Lane area of Pannal have also been hit.

The burglaries have taken place during the late afternoon or early evening between Saturday 17 October and Wednesday 4 November when the suspects have broken into homes and stolen jewellery and watches.

Inspector Greg Davies of the Neighbourhood Policing Team, said: “We are carrying out a full and thorough investigation into this series of burglaries, and our communities can expect to see increased patrols in the areas targeted.

“While responsibility for these crimes always lie with the offenders, we are encouraging residents to review their security and take as many crime prevention measures as possible.”

#### North Yorkshire Police security advice

• Consider installing motion sensor CCTV as this can be linked directly to a mobile device. Use an accredited burglar alarm with upper-floor sensors, mounted high, and put up signage.

• Keep high-value jewellery out of view and/or in a wall or floor mounted, fire-rated safe.

• Please consider taking photos of your property and possessions, or mark valuables for your own records and for insurance purposes which would make them more easily identifiable if they were ever stolen.

• Secure all windows and doors on every level, not just the ground floor. Consider sash stops and key-operated locks.

Police are also appealing to anyone who has seen any suspicious people, vehicles or activity in these areas to get in touch.

“No matter how insignificant you think the information may be, if it looked unusual for your neighbourhood, please trust your instincts and report it.”
