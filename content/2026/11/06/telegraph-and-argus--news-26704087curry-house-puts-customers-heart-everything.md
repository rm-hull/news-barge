A curry house that puts its customers at the heart of "everything" has been named one of the best in Bradford.

Silver Grill, based on Oak Lane, Manningham, has been shortlisted for the T&A's Best Curry House Awards 2026 following nominations and votes from the public.

The restaurant, known for its Indian and South Asian cuisine, now hopes to take home the overall title.

## 'We wanted to build more than just a restaurant'

Mohammad Ilyas, owner of Silver Grill, said: "We are absolutely delighted and proud to have been shortlisted for this award.

"To know that our customers and members of the public have recognised us and voted for us is a real honour."

Mr Ilyas said the restaurant’s success is built on quality food, friendly service, and strong relationships with customers.

He said: "We wanted to build more than just a restaurant; we wanted to create a business rooted in the local community, where customers feel valued, families feel welcome and every meal is prepared with care and pride."

## 'We understand that a great curry is about much more than ingredients on the plate'

He added that hospitality is about more than just food.

Mr Ilyas said: "What makes Silver Grill special is our commitment to quality, consistency and customer satisfaction.

"We understand that a great curry is about much more than the ingredients on the plate.

"It is about flavour, freshness, attention to detail, generous hospitality and the experience customers take away with them."

## 'Our customers are at the heart of everything we do'

Silver Grill has thanked its customers for their support, saying the recognition has given staff a boost as they continue working to maintain high standards.

Mr Ilyas said: "Our customers are at the heart of everything we do.

"We are proud to serve a diverse community, welcoming regular customers who have supported us over the years, families enjoying meals together, groups of friends celebrating special occasions and new customers discovering Silver Grill for the first time."

## 'We would like to thank every customer who has supported us'

He said the award is a reflection of community support.

Mr Ilyas said: "Being named among the top 10 is not just a recognition of our business; it is a reflection of the support we receive from the community.

"We would like to thank every customer who has supported us and helped make this achievement possible."
