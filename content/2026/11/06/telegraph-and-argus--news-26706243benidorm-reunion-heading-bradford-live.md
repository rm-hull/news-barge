THE cast of hit ITV comedy Benidorm will be reunited at Bradford Life to celebrate the 20th anniversary of the show.

Benidorm the Reunion, on March 9, 2027, will feature songs from the show sung live and behind-the-scenes stories.

There will also be a Brain of Benidorm quiz, with prizes including original costumes from the series.

The show will be hosted by writer, creator and director of Benidorm, Derren Litten, who will be joined on stage by actors including Crissy Rock, Asa Elliott, Hannah Hobley and Oliver Stokes, who played some of the show's best loved characters, with more to be announced.

Says Derren: “Expect stories you've never heard before, listen to cast members discuss clips you know and love, sing along to your favourite songs from the show, ask the cast the questions you want answering, win prizes available nowhere else and prepare yourself for the ultimate Benidorm fan event!"

Benidorm ran for 10 series from 2007-2018. Featuring stories of the guests and staff at the Solana Hotel, the show was a hit with viewers, reaching an audience of almost eight million at its peak.

The show won many accolades including Best Comedy Programme at the National Television Awards and was nominated for a BAFTA. A stage version of the show, Benidorm Live! toured the UK in 2018/19.

For tickets for Benidorm the Reunion at Bradford Live visit www.bradfordlive.co.uk
