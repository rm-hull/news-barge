Two alpacas have brought joy to care home residents during a special visit in Darlington.

Nora and Glitz, from Anvil Alpacas Durham, visited Wilton House, where they spent time with residents and staff.

Emma Hardy, home manager at Wilton House, said: "Nora and Glitz were absolutely adorable and everyone fell in love with them straight away.

"It was lovely to see the smiles on our residents' faces as they met the alpacas.

"They were so gentle and friendly, and there was plenty of laughter and excitement throughout the visit."

Animal visits are known to encourage interaction, trigger memories and offer companionship — especially for older individuals who may have kept pets or enjoyed spending time with animals earlier in life.

Ms Hardy added: "Activities like this are so important because they bring something a little different into the home, encourage conversation and create special memories for our residents.

"We're always looking for new ways to make life enjoyable and engaging for the people who live here, and this visit was certainly a huge success.

"A big thank you to Anvil Alpacas Durham for bringing Nora and Glitz to see us.

"They were very welcome guests, and we'd love to see them again."
