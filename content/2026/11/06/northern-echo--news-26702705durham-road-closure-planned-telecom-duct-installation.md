A road in Durham will be temporarily closed for two to three weeks for works.

Crossgate will be closed for 60 metres between numbers 4 and 11 for telecom duct installation work.

The closure will begin on Monday, November 9, 2026, with no alternative route for vehicles.

Emergency services will still be able to access the area.

The County Council of Durham issued the order under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, and it could last up to 18 months.

The council has warned that alternative dates might be necessary, with signs on site displaying the updates.
