# ITV Emmerdale and Coronation Street pulled off air tonight in huge schedule shake-up

## Emmerdale and Coronation Street have both been pulled off air tonight in an ITV schedule shake-up

Emmerdale and Coronation Street have both been axed from tonight's schedule in an ITV programming reshuffle. The popular soaps have endured numerous interruptions in recent weeks due to sporting fixtures and one-off programmes forcing alterations to their typical time slots.

Typically, Emmerdale broadcasts on weeknights at 8pm, with Corrie following at 8.30pm. But Friday (November 6) will instead feature ITV's coverage of the Nations Champions as Ireland face Argentina, meaning viewers must wait until Monday (November 9) for fresh episodes.

Though fans face a three-day gap, next week promises plenty of explosive storylines. Emmerdale will witness the Dingle clan fractured after Cain Dingle (Jeff Hordley) orchestrates an assault on Tom King (James Chase) in prison by Samson Dingle (Sam Hall), leading Belle Dingle (Eden Taylor-Draper) to take the challenging step of facing her abusive former partner.

Meanwhile, Kim Tate (Claire King) will openly recognise Thea Ramsden (Chelsea Edge) as her daughter, though Joe harbours doubts about the arrival.

Kev Barton (Chris Coghill) secures an unexpected position working alongside Robert Sugden (Ryan Hawley), leaving Nicola King (Nicola Wheeler) distressed, while Sadie King (Patsy Kensit) persists in scheming against Caleb Miligan (Will Ash) and his spouse Ruby Miligan (Beth Cordingly), reports the Daily Star.

Additionally, Robert gains a welcome financial injection for his venture, though his joy leaves Gabby Thomas (Rosie Bentham) devastated.

Over in Weatherfield, an upcoming Corrie instalment will centre on Debbie Webster (Sue Devaney) and her mates as they orchestrate a birthday surprise for Carl Webster (Jonathan Howard), while Kit Green (Jacob Roberts) collapses during his cancer fight and subsequently learns that Bethany Platt (Lucy Fallon) is expecting.

Elsewhere, Carla Connor-Swain (Alison King) contemplates putting Underworld up for sale, Brody Michaelis (Ryan Mulvey) makes threats to reveal Idris Nazir's (Junade Khan) manipulation, and Mary Taylor (Patti Clare) is devastated following the loss of her position at Preston's Petals.

Additionally, Shona Platt (Julia Goulding) gets a cryptic threatening telephone call regarding Jodie Ramsey (Olivia Frances Brown).

This follows the announcement that Owen Armstrong (Ian Puleston-Davies) is set to return to our screens in January.

The character was last spotted in Weatherfield back in April 2015 when he declared his departure after his romance with Anna Windass crumbled and his business went under, partly due to Pat Phelan's interference.

He relocated to Portugal before eventually making Scotland his home.

Since his exit from Coronation Street, actor Ian has appeared in numerous prominent dramas, taken the director's chair for feature films, and maintained his thriving writing career.

Speaking about his comeback, Ian remarked: "I can't wait to pick up where I left off 10 years ago! It's brilliant to be back and to get stuck into a cracking storyline. Owen's back and he's still angry!".
