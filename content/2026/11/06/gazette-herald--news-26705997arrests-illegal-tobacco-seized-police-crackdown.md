Seventeen people were arrested and £75,000 worth of illegal tobacco and cigarettes were seized during a crackdown on the criminal use of high street businesses in North Yorkshire.

Police visited 73 premises across the county and found 19 people working illegally.

A total of 158 people were spoken to and 17 people were arrested for immigration offences, North Yorkshire Police said.

Officers also targeted businesses selling illegal tobacco, with £24,000 worth of the prohibited products seized from a premises in King Edward Drive, Harrogate.

The force said it carried out action as part of Operation Machinize, an ongoing National Crime Agency-coordinated project to tackle money laundering and the criminal use of cash-intensive high street businesses.

Over the past two months, North Yorkshire Police said it worked with teams across the county to identify offenders, safeguard vulnerable people and disrupt criminality linked to organised immigration crime.

Chief Superintendent Nikki Shimwell, of North Yorkshire Police, said the operation aims to identify the criminal networks.

"Criminal networks often seek to profit through a range of offences, including illegal working, exploitation, money laundering and the sale of illicit goods," Ch Supt Shimwell said.

"By working closely with Trading Standards, immigration enforcement, licensing, fire safety and other partner agencies, we can take coordinated action against those involved while protecting vulnerable people and supporting legitimate businesses."
