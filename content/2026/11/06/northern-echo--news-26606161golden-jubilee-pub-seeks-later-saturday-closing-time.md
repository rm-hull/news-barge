A North East pub is seeking permission to stay open later on Saturdays, according to a public notice;.

The Golden Jubilee in Healaugh Park, Yarm, has applied to Stockton Council for an extension of opening hours.

Presently, the pub closes at 10pm on Saturdays, but it is seeking to extend this to 11pm.

The pub has said this extension would only apply to Saturdays.

The application for a change in the premises licence was published on October 3.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

Members of the public have until October 19 to submit their written opinions on the matter.

These should be sent to The Licensing Service, Stockton Council, Dunedin House, Columbia Drive, Thornaby, Stockton-on-Tees, TS17 6BJ.

People can view the record of the application in person at the Licensing Office between 9am and 4pm, Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays.

The application can also be viewed online.
