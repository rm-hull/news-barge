## Our live feed has now finished.

* A large fire started last night (Thursday, November 5) in an industrial estate in Osbaldwick.
* It measured 150 square metres, according to North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service
* Smoke was seen this morning issuing from the site operated by skip hire and waste transfer company Martins of York.

Thank you for following us today.

A large fire which started last night (Thursday, November 5) in an industrial estate in Osbaldwick, and which measured 150 square metres, according to North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service, has seen smoke issuing from the site operated by skip hire and waste transfer company Martins of York.

The fire service said this morning that the fire has been left under supervision to burn out.

![Smoke was seen issuing close to the large pile of aggregate behind the Martins of York yard this morning (background)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678014.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Smoke was seen issuing close to the large pile of aggregate behind the Martins of York yard this morning (background) *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the incident has been left with responsible persons on site to allow the fire to burn out under supervision.

We're waiting to hear back from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue service and will update the blog when we know more.

A member of staff at a neighbouring steel fabricators said he was aware of the smell as he arrived for work around 6am.

![Operations on Osbaldwick Industrial Estate appear to be carrying on as normal this morning](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678017.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Operations on Osbaldwick Industrial Estate appear to be carrying on as normal this morning *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

An odour, probably from the smoke, is discernable on the estate and was noticeable along the A1079 Hull Road this morning.

Operations and activity on Osbaldwick Industrial Estate appear to be carrying on as normal, and traffic is running freely on Murton Lane outside the estate.

From Outgang Lane, smoke can be seen drifting across a large pile of aggregate in the direction of Osbaldwick.

The fire was said to be on land operated by Martins of York, a waste transfer and skip hire company, in Osbaldwick Industrial Estate.

An employee told The Press that the fire consisted of wood.

The site is surrounded by hard-core and soil, he said.

A colleague of his has been at the site since 3am, he added, and said the fire service is no longer at the site.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Tadcaster was called to Outgang Lane in Osbaldwick at 9.34pm yesterday.

The service went to a large fire measuring 50 metres by 30 metres made up of waste materials.

Outgang Lane is off Murton Way, very close to the Osbaldwick Link Road and the A1079 in the east of the city.

In a statement issued around 7am this morning, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the incident was still ongoing.
