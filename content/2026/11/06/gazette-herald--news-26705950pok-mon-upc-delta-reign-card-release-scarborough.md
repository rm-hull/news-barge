QUEUES of people were spotted in Scarborough town centre today for the release of new collectable Pokémon cards.

Outside of Toyland Toyshop – an independently owned toyshop in Newborough – lines of punters waited to get their hands on the newly released packs.

The release comes as part of a resurgence for the Japanese game franchise in their 30th anniversary year, which has seen lines of people waiting across the country.

As well as the much-anticipated cards, the 30th Celebration Ditto Premium Collection & Figure Collections (Mew & Mewtwo) were also released today along with a match foil promo card and a large ‘showcase’

However, the shop stated on Facebook earlier this week that they are enacting a new policy for all those buying Pokémon cards.

The post reads: “In an effort to combat the growing number of scalpers buying Pokémon cards for the sole purpose of reselling, Toyland Toyshop will be enforcing the following policy:

“All Pokémon items will be opened upon purchase. Items wrapped in plastic will have the plastic removed. Boxed items will be opened and the flap torn off.”
