## York mourns ‘Wonder Wyman’ Jules Wyman after death at 53

Jules 'Wonder Wyman' Wyman who has died aged 53

*Tributes to York confidence coach Jules 'Wonder Wyman', by MAXINE GORDON*

Moving tributes are being paid to an inspirational York woman whose mission was to help others believe in themselves.

Pioneering 'confidence coach' Jules Wyman, known to many as Wonder Wyman, died from cancer on October 13 aged 53.

Through her life coaching business Positive Belief, Jules spent her career encouraging people of all ages to realise their potential and face challenges with self-belief.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671786.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jules 'Wonder Wyman' Wyman - York 'Confidence Coach'

Her brother Kev Wyman led the tributes to his younger sibling. "Her goal was to change people for the positive.

"Her motto was: 'positive belief' and it was all about being positive."

Kev said she maintained this attitude in dealing with her illness.

"When she came to tell me she had cancer she told me to be positive. And she said no matter what happens we have to stay positive."

Jules' funeral was taking place today at York Crematorium followed by a celebration of her life.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671790.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jules Wyman who has died aged 53

Kev said: "She wanted a positive celebration of her life and a party afterwards."

Friends have been sharing their special memories of Jules on social media following the announcement of Jules' death, and Kev said they had been a comfort to him and their mother Patricia.

"It's so warming and nice to read – it's a great comfort."

With her tongue-in-cheek tag of Wonder Wyman, Jules – who had struggled with self esteem in her own life – dedicated her time to helping others believe in themselves, develop confidence and realise their potential.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671782.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jules Wyman as a superhero

Following a first career in theatre stage management, she set up her business, Positive Belief, working as a life coach, motivational speaker and trainer.

She worked with people of all ages, and had a special programme for children, delivering her popular How To Be A Superhero workshop to schools across York. The programme encouraged children to build resilience, self-belief and ambition.

Away from her professional life, Jules embraced adventure with the same enthusiasm she brought to her coaching. Whether hiking in Peru, cycling across Cambodia, abseiling from the Humber Bridge or even fire-walking, she lived by the positive, fearless values she encouraged in others.

She took part in York's Dragon Boat Race in aid of women's organisation Kyra for several years.

Her achievements brought widespread recognition. She was a finalist in The Press Business Awards on several occasions, earning nominations including Women in Enterprise and Best Business and Education Link, and in 2009 she reached the final of Britain’s Next Top Coach competition.

She also appeared on BBC Breakfast.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671789.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jules Wyman on Breakfast TV talking about happiness

Friend and fellow York coach Lisa Clifford said: "Jules was a bold, brave and intelligent woman who was never afraid to put herself out there.

"She had a wonderful sense of humour, a sharp mind and a presence that made her memorable.

"She lived life in her own way, with the courage to speak up, take chances and be unapologetically herself.

"I was privileged to walk alongside her during the final chapter of her life, and I will always remember her spirit, her strength and the laughter she brought into the world."

Close friend Ellie Hudson was reading the eulogy at Jules' funeral today.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21671783.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jules Wyman with her friend Ellie Hudson

She said Jules would be remembered for leaving a lasting impression on people through her work.

"Laughter, dancing, energy, inspiring, and fun were just some of the terms people have used to describe her.

"So many people said her energy, smile, and laughter were infectious – as was her talent at encouraging positivity.

"She never failed to make an impression."
