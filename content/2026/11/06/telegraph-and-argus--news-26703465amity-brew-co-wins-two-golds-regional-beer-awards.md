A Bradford brewery has won gold at a major regional awards competition.

Amity Brew Co, which operates its brewery at Albion Mills, Greengates, won two golds at the Indie Beer Awards North East, held at Brew York Beer Hall in York.

The competition, organised by the Society of Independent Brewers (SIBA), celebrates the best independent breweries across the North East and Yorkshire.

Amity Brew Co’s Festoon won gold in the Keg Premium Lager (4.5% to 6.4%) category, while Waterpistol won gold in the Keg Pale Ale (4.5% to 5.5%) category.

Other West Yorkshire breweries also enjoyed success at the awards.

Leeds-based Northern Monk, which began in Greengates, picked up silver in the Keg Alcohol Free category for AF Faith and in the Keg Pale Ale (4.5% to 5.5%) category for Faith.

Northern Monk’s A Little Faith took bronze in the Keg Session Pale Ale (up to 4.4%) category.

Salt Beer Factory in Saltaire won gold for IKAT in the Keg Double, Triple and Imperial (7.5% and over) category, and silver for Jute in the Keg Session Pale Ale (up to 4.4%) category.

Ilkley Brewery won silver for Jet Lag in the Keg Specialty IPA category and bronze for Slake in the Keg Premium Lager (4.5% to 6.4%) category.

Craven Brew Co, based in Cross Hills, won bronze for Craven Wheat in the Keg Belgian and Continental Style Beer category.

The awards cover entries from across South Yorkshire, West Yorkshire, Humberside, North Yorkshire, Cleveland, Durham, Tyne & Wear and Northumberland.

Anneli Baxter, chair of the Indie Beer Awards, said: "Our Overall Champion category sees all of the category winners across various beer styles battle it out to be named the 'best of the best' and this year huge congratulations are due to Little Critters.

"Their Buffalo Charge was the standout beer of the competition with judges, from what was a hugely impressive final round.

"Massive well done to the Little Critters team and to all of this year's medalists and category champions.

"I'd also like to say a huge thank you to the Brew York team for helping run such a fantastic awards judging and for giving beer drinkers in York the chance to taste all of this year's winners in their superb Beer Hall.

All category champions will now go through to the national finals at BeerX in Liverpool in March.

Amity Brew Co began during the 2020 lockdown and opened to the public in April 2021.
