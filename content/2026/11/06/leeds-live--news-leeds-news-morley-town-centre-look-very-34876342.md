# Morley town centre to look very different with £1.1m makeover about to begin

## Construction work is set to begin next week

Construction work on a £1.1m town centre transformation project in Morley is set to begin next week.

Leeds City Council said construction company CR Reynolds Ltd has been appointed to begin construction on the Morley town centre transformation scheme from November 9. Work is set to conclude in March 2027, and will see work in the Queen Street pedestrianised area and Commercial Street.

The council said this work would aim at making the town more attractive, accessible, and welcoming. It includes additional seating and planting, improved accessibility, and creating a better environment to promote investment and increase footfall.

Councillor James Lewis, the leader of Leeds City Council, said: "The start of the public realm work in Morley town centre marks the culmination of years of planning and public consultation to deliver an improved town centre experience for all.

"The planned changes to the pedestrian area on Queen Street and the highway works on Commercial Street will not only make the area more visually appealing but will also have practical benefits in terms of accessibility and safety.

"This work is only the start of the planned improvements for the town, with three further schemes along Station Road, Queen Street North and Town Square scheduled to be delivered over the next six months."

Morley town centre is set to remain open throughout the work, meaning cafés, restaurants, pubs, and shops will continue to operate. Chair of the Morley Town Deal, Gerald Jennings, said: "The Morley Town Deal set out with the aspiration to create education and career opportunities, improve the town centre experience and build economic growth.

"These public realm improvements, together with the work to rejuvenate the Town Hall and deliver a new learning and skills centre, continue to deliver on that aspiration and help Morley be one of the best places in Leeds to live, learn, work, trade and visit.

"While there may be some temporary disruption during the work, I encourage everyone to continue to use the town centre as normal and support Morley’s incredible range of local businesses in the usual way."

The work has also been phased to minimise disruption, and clear signs and communications will keep people updated on the progress of the development.
