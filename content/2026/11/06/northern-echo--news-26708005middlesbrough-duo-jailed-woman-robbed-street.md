A man and woman have each been jailed for 40 months after their victim was dragged to the ground and robbed in a North East street.

Callum Brown, 27, and Kelsey Fuller, 35, both from Middlesbrough, targeted the woman on Burlam Road in Middlesbrough on the night of Sunday, August 9.

The victim and a friend were walking to a nearby shop on Acklam Road when they were approached by Brown and Fuller, who were known to the victim.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682411.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Callum Brown, 27 *(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

While Fuller held the victim’s friend back, Brown grabbed the strap of the victim’s cross-body bag, swinging the victim around and onto the ground, where the victim hit their head on the concrete.

While the victim was on the ground, Brown raised his foot above the victim's head, causing the victim to fear that Brown would stamp on the victim.

Brown then pulled the handbag from the victim and fled.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21682410.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kelsey Fuller, 35 *(Image: CLEVELAND POLICE)*

The stolen items included a £70 Diesel bag, a mobile phone, medical documents and £60 in cash.

Brown and Fuller were both handed 40-month prison sentences for robbery.

A ten-year restraining order was also imposed to protect the victim from the pair.

DC Lewis, of Middlesbrough CID, said: “This was a particularly nasty robbery, with the victim being targeted by people she knows.

“Thankfully, the pair are now behind bars where they will no longer pose a risk to others.”
