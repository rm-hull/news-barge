A fitness entrepreneur is helping to train the next generation of fitness professionals in Darlington.

Victoria McFaull, owner of VIXI Fitness, has joined Darlington College as a tutor on its Level 3 sports massage course.

Ms McFaull retrained as an adult learner to become a personal trainer and now works with clients who have a range of health conditions, including respiratory disease.

She said: "We see a range of students at the night classes.

"Some are continuing their journey in education, and some are returning after a break from learning.

"I really enjoy supporting the students, passing on my experience and giving back."

Ms McFaull began her career in fitness after being encouraged to become an instructor at a gym in Billingham.

She later worked for a major fitness chain before launching her own business.

She completed her qualifications over a year while working full-time and raising two children as a single mother.

Claire Hankey, head of marketing and engagement at Darlington College, said: "Bringing current industry experience directly into the classroom is incredibly valuable for our learners.

"Victoria is a positive role model who can inspire both young people and adults and show them what a successful career in the sector can look like."

Ms McFaull is based at Business Central, a workspace owned by Darlington Borough Council and managed by the North East BIC.

Nicole Johnstone, centre manager at Darlington Workspace, said: "We love having Victoria as part of our Darlington Workspace family.

"We are delighted to support her latest journey with Darlington College and VIXI Fitness."

Ms McFaull has built a broad portfolio of massage qualifications, including sports massage, trigger point massage, lymphatic massage, and massage during pregnancy.

More information can be found at www.vixiwellbeing.co.uk or www.darlington.ac.uk.
