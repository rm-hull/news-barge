Childline is marking 40 years of supporting children and young people across the UK.

Rebecca Wilcox, deputy president of Childline and daughter of the charity’s founder Dame Esther Rantzen, visited the service’s Yorkshire base to mark the anniversary.

The visit was one of Ms Wilcox’s first official engagements since Dame Esther’s death in September.

Ms Wilcox said: "I’m so proud of the service, and I wish my mum were here today.

"I feel that she is here.

"She lives on through Childline and the work they do every day, and I’m so grateful to everyone who supports us."

Childline’s Leeds base has delivered nearly 280,000 counselling sessions since it opened in 1997.

The team handled five per cent of all Childline sessions last year.

York-based parenting influencer Kelly Medina Enos also attended the event.

Ms Medina Enos said: "I’ve definitely learned a lot today, all about Childline being more of a friend and a companion to children, rather than something that feels a bit official and scary - they’re just on the end of the phone to talk about anything you need to talk about."

The service is available to all young people until their 19th birthday by calling 0800 1111 or visiting www.childline.org.uk.
