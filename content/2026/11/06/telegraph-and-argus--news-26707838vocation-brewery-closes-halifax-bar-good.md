A POPULAR independent brewery has closed one of its bars in West Yorkshire for good.

Vocation announced the permanent closure of its Halifax bar on Alexandra Street on November 6.

The decision marks a shift in focus for the brewery, which was founded in 2015 by John Hickling.

Whilst its flagship Hebden Bridge bar will remain open, Vocation's Halifax and Sheffield premises are now closed.

### 'To everyone who's been part of this bar's story - cheers'

In a statement shared on social media, a spokesman for Vocation said: "To the many who have visited, shared a beer with us and supported Vocation Halifax over the years, thank you.

"After much careful thought, we've made the difficult decision to permanently close the bar from Friday 6th November.

"We're putting more of our focus into our brewery, expanding our core range of beers and making Vocation easier to find in pubs, bars, bottle shops and supermarkets across Yorkshire, the UK and beyond.

"If you're after the full Vocation bar experience, you can still visit our flagship bar in Hebden Bridge.

"It remains a big part of Vocation, with plenty more bold beers to come.

"To everyone who's been part of this bar's story, raised a glass with us and made it what it is, cheers.

"It really has meant a lot."

### 'These continue to be really tough times for local businesses'

One regular, who wished to remain anonymous, said: "It’s very sad news it’s closing.

"I went there a few times and always had a nice time - good food and drink.

"It always seemed to be busy.

"My thoughts are with all the staff.

"These continue to be really tough times for local businesses.”

The T&A has reached out to Vocation for further comment.
