Each month, inspectors from the Food Standards Agency visit numerous establishments across the Harrogate district.

Businesses are given a food hygiene rating from 0 to 5 based on the standards found during their inspection.

The rating is based on several areas, including how food is handled, the condition of the premises and how effectively food safety is managed.

A score of 5 means the business has very good hygiene standards, while a rating of 0 means urgent improvements are needed.

In October, 13 restaurants, takeaways, cafes and pubs in the Harrogate district were visited by inspectors...
