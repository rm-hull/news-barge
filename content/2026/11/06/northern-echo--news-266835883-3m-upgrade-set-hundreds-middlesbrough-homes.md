Hundreds of households in a North East town will benefit from a £3.3 million home-improvement programme.

The work is part of Thirteen’s £63 million regional investment programme and will upgrade 463 homes on Middlesbrough’s Saltersgill estate.

Thirteen appointed Esh Construction to install new windows and external doors through its Capital Delivery Framework.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21649876.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Thirteen's £3.3m Saltersgill home improvement scheme will upgrade 463 Middlesbrough homes with new windows and doors, boosting security and energy efficiency *(Image: Dave Charnley)*

Rubie Dixon-Lee, investment area manager at Thirteen, said: "We're committed to investing in our homes and communities so our customers can live in homes that are safe, secure and comfortable.

"The new windows and doors will make a real difference for hundreds of households in Saltersgill, helping to keep homes warmer, more secure and more energy efficient.

"It's great to see the second phase of work completed and the next stage now underway."

The project is being delivered in phases, with phase two complete and phase three underway.

A total of 164 properties have already been upgraded, with work now due to continue on a further 442 homes.

Thirteen said the work will make its homes safer, more comfortable and energy efficient, while improving their appearance and condition.

The project is due to finish by March 2027.

The programme is improving thousands of homes across the North East and Yorkshire this year.

Thirteen is a housing association that owns and manages more than 36,000 homes for around 100,000 customers across the North East, Yorkshire and the Humber.

In March 2025, Thirteen secured the highest possible C1/G1/V1 ratings following an inspection by the Regulator of Social Housing.
