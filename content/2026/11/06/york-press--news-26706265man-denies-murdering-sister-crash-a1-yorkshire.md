A man has denied murdering his sister after a fatal crash on the A1(M) in North Yorkshire.

Florin Chiriac, 31, pleaded not guilty to murdering Elena Chiriac, 22, between Junction 57 and Junction 56 near Barton on October 4.

Florin Chiriac, of Hampstead Road, Newcastle, also denied Section 18 wounding with intent and driving a vehicle dangerously at Leeds Crown Court on Friday (November 6).

Another man, Cristi Chiriac, 25, of Hampstead Road, Newcastle, was charged with murder and pleaded not guilty to the offence on Friday.

Both men are due to stand trial at Leeds Crown Court from March 22.

They will next attend court for a case management hearing on December 11.

Elena Chiriac, originally from Romania, died of head injuries after the crash on the A1(M), her inquest opening heard.

She was pronounced dead at the scene after a silver Ford Transit and a silver Volkswagen Golf collided shortly after 6pm on October 4.

Coroner Mark Armitage gave Elena Chiriac’s cause of death as traumatic head injuries.

North Yorkshire Police said a 52-year-old woman from Newcastle was arrested on suspicion of encouraging or assisting the commission of an assault offence but was later released on bail pending further inquiries.
