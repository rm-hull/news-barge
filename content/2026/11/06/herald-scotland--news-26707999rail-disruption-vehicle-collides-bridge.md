Rail services are being disrupted after a vehicle struck a bridge.

The incident took place on the line between Perth and Dunkeld & Birnam on Friday evening.

ScotRail said that, as a result of the incident, services have to run at a reduced speed through the area.

The disruption is affecting services between Glasgow Queen Street/Edinburgh Waverley and Inverness.

Disruption is expected to last until 8pm.
