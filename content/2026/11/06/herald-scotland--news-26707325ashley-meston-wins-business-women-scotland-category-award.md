The chief executive of Scottish-headquartered ESP Group has been named a category winner at the Business Women Scotland Top 100 Awards 2026.

Ashley Meston received the Manufacturing, Engineering and Product Design award at a ceremony held at voco Grand Central Glasgow on October 28.

She joined ESP Group nearly 20 years ago as a customer service adviser, became a member of its board in 2024 and was appointed chief executive in 2025.

During her career with the business, she has led major projects for clients including Network Rail, Transport for London and London Councils.

ESP Group provides customer experience and support services, back-office processing, smartcard production and fulfilment, and technology solutions for organisations across transport, local government and the public sector.

The awards, now in their 12th year, recognise women contributing to Scotland’s economy across sectors including technology, manufacturing, professional services, healthcare, retail, design, charity and education.

Karen Greenshields of GAP Group was named overall winner, while Professor Jillian MacBryde of the University of Strathclyde received the Lifetime Achievement Award.

ESP Group has more than 30 years of experience supporting businesses and their customers.

Ashley Meston, chief executive of ESP Group, said: "Being included in the BWS Top 100 was a real honour, so to go on to be named one of this year's ten category winners makes the recognition even more special.

"I have been with ESP Group for nearly 20 years, having joined as a Customer Service Advisor and I now have the privilege of leading the business.

"My experience across different roles has given me a real appreciation of the people, resilience and partnerships that sit behind a successful business.

"I am incredibly proud of what we have built at ESP and the progress we are continuing to make.

"While this is a personal recognition, it means a great deal to have the contribution of our wider team recognised too.

"Congratulations to Karen, Professor MacBryde and all of this year's winners and thank you to Business Women Scotland for continuing to champion and celebrate women in business across Scotland."
