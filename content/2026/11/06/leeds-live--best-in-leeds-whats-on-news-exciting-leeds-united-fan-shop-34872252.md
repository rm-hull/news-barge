# Exciting Leeds United fan shop to launch at popular shopping centre

## You might find the ideal Christmas present for the Leeds United supporter in your life

Leeds United fans will be delighted to know a new fan shop is launching in the city.

The popular Burley Banksy is launching a new store in Leeds. It will be dedicated to Leeds United with lots of memorabilia and Christmas present ideas available to be purchased.

The new shop will open on Tuesday, November 17 at the Merrion Centre. Previewing the opening, the Burley Banksy Facebook page posted: “My new shop in the Merrion Centre open for Christmas on Tuesday, November 17 and open every day (except Christmas Day and Boxing day) until New Year’s Eve.

“I'm next door to Jumbo Records on the ground floor. I'm officially licensed now by Leeds United so a percentage of sales goes to the club. Hope to see you.”
