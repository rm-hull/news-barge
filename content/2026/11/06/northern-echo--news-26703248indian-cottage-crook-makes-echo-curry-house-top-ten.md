A town’s oldest curry house has been shortlisted for The Northern Echo's Best Curry House 2026.

The Indian Cottage, on Church Street in Crook, has been serving generations of curry lovers in the County Durham town for 34 years.

Owner Dabir Uddin said his customers are fiercely loyal - with those who once came to the venue as children now returning with families of their own.

The site is praised for its “absolutely first class” food that is “probably the best in the county” to some punters, with quality that is “second to none”.

And now, for the second year in a row, The Indian Cottage has been named in the shortlist for the Echo’s Best Curry House 2026 competition.

Some of its most popular dishes include the chicken cottage murgi masala and chicken tikka naga, as well as the chicken tikka zindabad, which is a curry with cheese.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21676146.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dabir Uddin at The Indian Cottage in Crook *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The venue currently boasts a 4.4 rating on Google Reviews, with praise for its “simply sensational” food offering.

One person said: “I have been buying curries from here since it opened in 1992 and the quality of service, customer care and attention to detail has never wavered.

“Always worth a try and you will not be disappointed.”

The Indian Cottage is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including Bengal Lounge, Newton Aycliffe, Babul’s, in Darlington, and Spice Island, Darlington.
