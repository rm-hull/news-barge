Police are asking for the public’s help to identify this man.

It’s in connection with an investigation into a crime at a fashion store in York last month.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We would like to speak with this person regarding a fraud which occurred at Primark, Monks Cross Shopping Centre, on Thursday 8th of October and Friday 9th of October.”
