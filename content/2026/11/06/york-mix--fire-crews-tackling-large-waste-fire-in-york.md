Fire crews are tackling a large waste fire in York this morning (Friday).

A crew from Tadcaster was called to a large unattended fire on **Outgang Lane, Osbaldwick** at**9.34pm**.

This morning North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The crew confirmed a large fire measuring approximately 50m by 30m consisting of waste materials.

#### Outgang Lane, York

“This incident is still ongoing.”
