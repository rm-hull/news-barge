PUBS are raising a glass in thanks to military personnel.

Thirty-one venues across Yorkshire, including two in Bradford, are offering a free drink to serving members and veterans this Remembrance Sunday, November 8.

The offer is available to those who show a military card, with drinks valued up to £6.

Participating Bradford pubs include the Crown Hotel and the Malt Kiln Inn, both part of Proper Pubs.

A spokesperson for Admiral Taverns, which operates Proper Pubs, said: "It’s a small gesture of thanks to those who have given so much.

"A moment to reflect, a chance to say thank you and a place for the community to come together."

At 11am, all participating pubs will pause service to observe the two-minute silence.

Nearby venues also include the Cavendish in Keighley, and several pubs in Leeds such as the Black Lion Hotel, Broadway, and the Butterbowl Hotel.
