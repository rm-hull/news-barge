ACTOR and musician Alan Fletcher took time out from his UK-wide tour to visit Settle Falconry in the Yorkshire Dales.

Alan, best known for playing Dr Karl Kennedy in the Australian soap Neighbours, was in the area appearing with his band at Settle Victoria Hall.

The long-serving actor had clocked up 31 years on on Neighbours when the popular soap finished at the end of last year.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21678082.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Actor Alan Fletcher spent an afternoon with Settle Falconry *(Image: Settle Falconry)*

He is also a keen wildlife photographer, and bird of prey enthusiast, so a visit to the award-winning Settle Falconry, based at Elaine's Tearooms, Austwick, was a must.

Kieran Bathers and David Edwards, who own and run the falconry centre, said it was a pleasure to spend the afternoon with Alan, his wife, Jennifer Hansen, who sings and plays the keyboard, and other bandmates.

![](https://www.cravenherald.co.uk/resources/images/21678084.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kieran Bathers and David Edwards, with Alan Fletcher and Jennifer Hansen, centre *(Image: Settle Falconry)*

“We were pleased to arrange this as a break during his UK band tour.

"We’ve even got a keepsake Ramsay Street sign and presented some little falconry keepsakes to take back home to Australia,” they said.
