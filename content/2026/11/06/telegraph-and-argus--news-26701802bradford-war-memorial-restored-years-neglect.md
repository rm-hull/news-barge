A NEGLECTED war memorial has been restored thanks to the efforts of a veteran and community volunteers.

The Tong War Memorial, located in the Bradford village of Tong, had become overgrown and obscured by bushes and weeds over time.

It stands as a tribute to those who lost their lives in the First World War, many of whom were sons, husbands, and fathers from the village who never returned home.

Terry Sanson, a veteran and SSAFA branch secretary, led the restoration project.

Mr Sanson said: "These men gave their lives more than a century ago, but their sacrifice should never be forgotten.

"It was important to us that their memorial should once again be a place where people could come to remember them with pride and respect.

"I’m incredibly grateful to everyone who gave their time and support.

"This has been a real community effort, and seeing the memorial restored and so many people come together for the rededication made it all worthwhile."

With the help of local volunteers and council support, the site has been transformed.

Overgrown vegetation was cleared, the stonework cleaned, and fresh gravel laid to return the memorial to a place of dignity and reflection.

A rededication ceremony was held to mark the restoration, attended by residents, civic dignitaries, and family members of the fallen.

Those present included the Lord Mayor and parliamentary representatives.

The service featured an Act of Remembrance, prayers, a blessing, the Last Post, two minutes’ silence, and the National Anthem.

Mr Sanson also thanked Reverend Ruth Yeoman from Baildon Parish, the volunteers, and everyone who contributed to the project and the rededication service.

He said: "The restored memorial stands as a testament not only to the sacrifices of Tong’s fallen, but to the determination of a community to ensure their names and stories are never forgotten."

Further planting and improvements are planned for the site.

Donations to support the ongoing work are welcomed and can be sent to Mr Sanson at [terry.sanson@volunteers.ssafa.org.uk.](mailto:terry.sanson@volunteers.ssafa.org.uk.)

SSAFA volunteers have supported the Armed Forces community—both serving and retired personnel, as well as their families—since 1885.
