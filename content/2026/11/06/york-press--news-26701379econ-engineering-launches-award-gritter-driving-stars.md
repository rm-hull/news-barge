## Ripon firm launches award to find next generation of gritter drivers

Econ Engineering, based in Ripon, has launched the Rising Star category as part of its 2026 Grit & Glory Awards *(Image: Econ Engineering)*

A new award celebrating the UK’s future winter service heroes is shining a spotlight on the next generation of gritter drivers.

Econ Engineering, based in Ripon, has launched the Rising Star category as part of its 2026 Grit & Glory Awards, recognising standout newcomers in the winter maintenance sector.

The awards honour gritter drivers across the UK, with one overall winner named Gritter Driver of the Year.

Steve Sinnott, sales director at Econ Engineering, said: "The inaugural Grit & Glory Awards was a huge success, with hundreds of nominations from talented individuals throughout the country.

"In 2026, we will again celebrate the experienced drivers who keep their community’s roads clear and safe through our established regional categories.

"The new Rising Star award will sit alongside these awards, giving the next generation a visible route into a career they can be proud of."

Last year’s title went to Gary Lawson of Durham County Council.

Jonathan said the new category aims to address a national HGV driver shortage and the low proportion of drivers aged under 24.

Econ, the UK’s leading manufacturer of gritters and snow ploughs, supplies 85 per cent of the winter maintenance vehicles used by councils and contractors.
