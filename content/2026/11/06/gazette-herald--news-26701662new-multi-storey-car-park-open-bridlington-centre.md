A £15.5m multi-storey car park is set to open in Bridlington.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council leader, Councillor Anne Handley, will lead the official ribbon-cutting ceremony on Monday, 9 November, at 3pm.

Local council representatives, community leaders, and project partners will also attend.

The 426-space car park, located between Manor Street and Hilderthorpe Road, aims to reduce traffic congestion, boost local business, and support the town centre's regeneration.

With the increase in parking bays, the facility significantly enhances parking capacity for shoppers, workers, and visitors.

Its prime location offers easy pedestrian access to Manor Street, Hilderthorpe Road, and Bridlington's town centre.

The building also includes 31 dedicated accessible bays for convenient entry and exit.

The new structure, inspired by ocean waves, boasts distinctive twisted fins, adding a modern touch to Bridlington's skyline.

The first hour of parking will be free of charge.

The project is part of a broader strategy to revitalise the town centre, aiming to increase footfall and provide year-round support to local businesses.
