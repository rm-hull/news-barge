A WOMAN suffered smoke inhalation after a kitchen fire.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service attended the incident on Stonegate Road in Eccleshill at 2.24pm today (Friday).

Two breathing apparatuses, one hose reel and positive pressure ventilation were used.

An ambulance was requested for the woman at the scene, who was reportedly suffering from smoke inhalation.

### What did emergency services say?

A spokesperson for the fire service said: "Domestic property fire - kitchen involved.

"Two breathing apparatuses, one hose reel and positive pressure ventilation used.

"Ambulance requested for one female suffering smoke inhalation."

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "Ambulance resources attended a residential fire incident in the Idle area of Bradford on Friday afternoon."
