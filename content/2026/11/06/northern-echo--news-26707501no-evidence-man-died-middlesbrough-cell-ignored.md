There is no evidence to suggest a man who died in a police custody cell was “being ignored” by staff, a watchdog has said.

An investigation was launched on Thursday (November 5) after a 31-year-old man died in a cell at Middlesbrough Police Station.

The detainee - who was arrested at 10pm the night before - had been found unresponsive by staff just before 6am and was sadly pronounced dead by paramedics at 7.01am.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) has since launched a probe into the incident, saying regular checks were carried out on the man by custody staff.

The watchdog this afternoon said it is aware of a video circulating on social media that claims the man was mistreated and “ignored”.

IOPC director of engagement Emily Barry said there is no evidence to suggest this is accurate and urged otters not to speculate while the investigation continues.

She said: “This was a tragic incident in which a man has sadly died and our thoughts are with his family and loved ones.

“Cleveland Police referred this matter to us, and as he was in the custody of police when he died, it is important there is a thorough and independent investigation.

“We will examine all relevant matters including the interaction staff and officers had with the man when he was detained and what happened while at the custody suite.

“We are aware of a video circulating on social media which includes claims about mistreatment and help requested by the man being ignored.

“At this early stage, we have no evidence to suggest this is accurate.

“We urge people to not engage in speculation and spread disinformation while we continue to investigate and verify the facts.

“It’s important to remember how distressing it can be for family members at this difficult time.

“We will of course seek to obtain evidence from anyone who was present and who believes they may hold relevant information.”

IOPC investigators have attended the custody suite to begin reviewing evidence by obtaining CCTV footage and initial accounts from the officers and staff involved.

The Northern Echo understands that people in custody were transferred out of the station during the ordeal and sent to Durham Police’s custody cells.

Usual custody arrangements in Middlesbrough are now in place.

The IOPC investigation has been ongoing since 7.07am yesterday and the man's family has been informed.

A Cleveland Police spokesperson said: “Sadly, we can confirm that a detainee has died while in custody at Middlesbrough Police Station.

"The circumstances surrounding this death are currently being established, and it would be inappropriate to comment further at this time. The family of the deceased have been informed.

"As is standard practice, we have referred this incident to the IOPC, which is conducting an independent investigation."
