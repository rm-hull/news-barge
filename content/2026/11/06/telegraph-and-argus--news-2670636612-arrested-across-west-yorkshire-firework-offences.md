12 people were arrested over firework-related offences on Bonfire Night, it has been revealed.

West Yorkshire Police said the majority of these were over illegal possession or the throwing of fireworks.

The force highlighted an incident in Bradford where three youths were arrested.

This was after reportedly throwing fireworks at a police station.

The incident happened at Manningham Police Station on Lilycroft Road in Bradford at around 6.30pm on Thursday.

Police will continue reassurance patrols across the weekend.

## What police have said

A spokesperson for the force said: "Police carried out a number of reassurance patrols across West Yorkshire on Thursday as part of our planned activity during the Halloween and Bonfire night period.

"Officers did attend a small number of firework related incidents, mainly in the Kirklees and Bradford areas.

"A total of 12 Bonfire Night related arrests were made across the force.

"Reassurance patrols will continue into the weekend and we hope people attending fireworks related displays and activities do so enjoyably and safely."

## Other incidents on Bonfire Night in Bradford

There were several other incidents across Bradford on Bonfire Night.

A van fire spread to the ground floor of a building.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service confirmed crews attended Carlisle Street, Manningham, late on November 5.

![debris from fire, burned out van](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21678853.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The aftermath of a fire on Friday morning *(Image: T&A)*

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene on Friday morning, a burnt-out white vehicle, debris outside and a large number of rugs could be seen.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "We were called at 22.43 to Carlisle Street to reports of a van fire which had spread to the ground floor of a building.

"Five crews used breathing apparatus, a large jet and hose reels to extinguish this.

"No cause recorded as yet."

A car fire was recorded as a "suspected deliberate ignition" by the fire service.

The Bolton Road incident was reported to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service (WYFRS) at 11.21pm. One crew put out the fire.

A bike and freezer were seen on fire after a blaze broke out in the open on Horton Park Avenue at 9pm.
