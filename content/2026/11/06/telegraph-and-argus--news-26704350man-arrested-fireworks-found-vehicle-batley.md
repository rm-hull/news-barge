A man has been arrested on suspicion of burglary after a large quantity of fireworks were found in a vehicle's boot.

Late on Bonfire Night, West Yorkshire Police said officers found the items in a vehicle on Burnsall Road, Batley.

The discovery followed a report of a burglary at a business in Heckmondwike.

The investigation is ongoing.

## The lowdown from police

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "A bright idea? Not quite…

"Officers made quite a discovery this evening after stopping a vehicle on Burnsall Road in Batley.

"A search of the vehicle uncovered a large quantity of fireworks in the boot after a report of a burglary at a business in Heckmondwike.

"A 38-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of burglary and remains in custody.

"Enquiries are currently ongoing."
