### Young, British and Tradwife FAQs

### What is a tradwife?

Short for ‘traditional wife’, a tradwife is a woman who embraces conventional gender roles, typically focusing on homemaking, raising children and supporting her husband.

The movement has gained a huge following on social media, particularly TikTok's #tradwife videos.

Influencers share content celebrating domestic life. However, it has also sparked debate about feminism, women’s independence and the expectations placed on wives.

Critics argue that the trend can romanticise female dependence on men, reinforce outdated gender stereotypes.

Popular tradwife TikTok accounts include @onwildway, @stayzontopp and @tradwifelyss.
