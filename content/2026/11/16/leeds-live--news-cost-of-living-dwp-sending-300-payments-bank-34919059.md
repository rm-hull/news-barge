# DWP sending £300 payments into bank accounts from today

## Some will get £200, and others will be handed another £100 on top of that

State pensioners will receive between £150 and £300 winter fuel payments from this week onwards, the DWP says.

Every state pensioner will be paid at least £150 in Winter Fuel Payment cash between November 2026 and January 31, 2027, regardless of their income. Those earning more than £35,000 will have to repay it to HMRC, while those aged over 80 will see theirs bumped to £200- £300, depending on whether they live alone or as a state-pensioner couple.

According to the DWP, this payment will be made from 'mid-November' onwards, which would be this week. The DWP's An Introduction to Winter Fuel Payment (Easy Read) guide says: "Payments will be made from mid-November 2026 onwards. Most eligible people will get paid automatically without having to make a claim."

The Winter Fuel Payment provides state pensioners with up to £300 in support for energy bills. For state pensioners aged under 80, the payment is £200 (or £100 for a state pensioner under 80 who lives with another state pensioner under 80). Then, for a state pensioner aged over 80, the payment is increased to £300 (or £150 if they live with another state pensioner over 80).

These payments are made regardless of income, though those earning over £35,000 in a single tax year will have to repay the winter fuel payment through their HMRC tax code or via self-assessment.

On top of this, Chancellor John Healey announced a new £100 Household Energy Payment in the Budget last month, aimed at helping households with spiralling energy bills. Current forecasts predict that the Ofgem price cap could rise by as much as £465 in January, according to EDF Energy, which would represent a staggering 87.7% year-on-year price rise for gas alone.

As well as extending the VAT cut on electricity and scrapping the daily standing charges for gas, the Chancellor announced the £100 Household Energy Payment, which will be paid to those living in households in council tax bands A to D.

It means that state pensioners living in a house in council tax bands A, B, C or D will be able to get the £100 Household Energy Payment on top of their existing Winter Fuel Payment.
