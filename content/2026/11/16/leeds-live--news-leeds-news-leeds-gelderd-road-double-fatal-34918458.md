# Leeds Gelderd Road double fatal crash - everything we know so far

## A young man and woman lost their lives in a double fatal crash

A young man and woman lost their lives in a double fatal crash in Gelderd Road, Leeds. A man has been arrested and charged in connection with the tragic incident.

* West Yorkshire Police said that at 12.44am this morning a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a police vehicle in Gelderd Road. The Force said that a pursuit began and the BMW crashed into a white Volkswagen Scirocco which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.
* The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, was sadly pronounced dead at the scene, and a passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman from York, died at hospital a short time later.
* Leigh Ross, of Prospect Place, has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving
* Ross has also been charged with two counts of causing death by driving while disqualified; two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed/uninsured, failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required by a constable and aggravated vehicle taking. He has been remanded in custody ahead of his court appearance.
* Two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl who were in the BMW, were arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving but were released on bail. The teenagers received treatment to injuries that were not considered life-threatening after being taken to hospital.
* Officers from West Yorkshire Police Major Collision Enquiry Team are continuing to appeal for any witnesses to any part of the incident. Specialist family liaison officers are supporting the families of the two people who died.
