## Motorists warned of lengthy delays on major road into York

Motorists have been warned of heavy traffic along the A59 after reports of a broken-down lorry *(Image: Submitted)*

MOTORISTS have been warned of heavy traffic after reports of a broken-down lorry at a main junction in York.

The incident is currently ongoing with lengthy delays along the A59 Roman Road, close to the roundabout leading to York’s Outer Ring Road.
