A pet food company near Tadcaster has been named Manufacturer of the Year.

The Innocent Pet Care Company, based at Thorp Arch, won the title in the under £10 million category at the 2026 Pet Industry Federation (PIF) Awards.

The award recognises its expertise in air-dried food production for dogs and cats.

Chloe Heaton, founder of The Innocent Pet Care Company, said: "We’re incredibly proud to have won this award.

"Since 2013, we’ve invested in developing our own bespoke air-drying process and building the manufacturing expertise behind it.

"It’s an exciting category within pet food, and we’re proud to not only manufacture for our own brands, but to work with many other businesses who want to bring this growing category into their own ranges.

"Everything we do comes down to the people behind the products.

"This award is a real recognition of the whole team’s hard work, commitment and passion, and we couldn’t have achieved it without them."

The company operates under its own brands, The Innocent Hound and The Innocent Cat, and produces for more than 20 other pet food brands in the UK and internationally.

Founded in 2013, the business has built its reputation on a bespoke air-drying process that preserves nutrients by gently removing moisture over several days rather than hours.

Its products are stocked by independent retailers and available direct to consumers.

The company runs two factories, where it oversees the entire manufacturing process in-house, from sourcing ingredients to production, packing and dispatch.

Its focus on using British meats supports the UK farming sector and reinforces its commitment to British manufacturing.

The award comes as the company marks 10 consecutive years of SALSA (Safe and Local Supplier Approval) accreditation, demonstrating its compliance with rigorous food safety and production standards.

The win recognises the collective efforts of the people behind the business, with colleagues across every department contributing to its continued growth and success.

The company continues to develop its own brands while championing the air-dried pet food category more broadly.

Its signature air-drying method, which can take up to seven days, ensures the final product is nutrient-rich, ready to serve, and does not require refrigeration or freezing.

Handcrafted in small batches, the company says it's products reflect its values of natural ingredients, sustainability, and pet wellbeing.

The Innocent Pet Care Company has also expanded its reach through private label manufacturing and international exports.
