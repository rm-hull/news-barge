## York taproom celebrates first anniversary with brand-new lunch menu

Northern Monk has celebrated its first anniversary with the launch of its new lunchtime menu *(Image: Newsquest)*

A popular York craft beer venue is celebrating a successful first year in the city with a new lunch menu, special cocktails and plans for its first-ever Christmas menu.

Award-winning independent brewery Northern Monk says its Refectory York taproom on Little Stonegate has become a favourite among locals and visitors since opening last year.

The venue has served more than 12,700 pints of its flagship Faith pale ale and more than 9,500 pints of A Little Faith during its first 12 months, alongside welcoming around 2,500 diners.




To mark the milestone, food partner Slice Culture is expanding its offering with a new range of New York-style loaded sandwiches available weekdays from noon until 5pm.

The lunchtime menu includes a Southern Fried Chicken Caesar baguette and a Roast Pepper Halloumi baguette, adding to the venue's popular pizza offering.

Pizza remains at the heart of the menu, with Slice Culture's hand-stretched 12-inch pizzas continuing to be served throughout the day. Lunchtime deals include a pizza and soft drink for £10, or £12 with a pint of Great Northern lager or A Little Faith.

The celebrations come as Refectory York prepares to take part in the winter edition of York Cocktail Week from November 6 to 15. Visitors will be able to sample a Spicy Pineapple Margarita, blending pineapple tequila and jalapeño syrup, for £6.

Housed in a historic townhouse in the city centre, the venue has also expanded its dining options with a dedicated upstairs dining room, alongside a ground-floor snug and a tasting room used for craft beer masterclasses.

Northern Monk said York had long been on its radar due to the city's established pub culture and growing craft beer scene.

The brewery is also preparing to unveil its first Christmas menu, with bookings now open for festive gatherings in the venue's dining and private spaces.

Russell Bisset, co-founder of Northern Monk, said: "We've had an incredible year soaking up the history and heritage of this amazing city whilst providing the people of York and its visitors from around the world with the freshest beer brewed less than an hour's drive away in Leeds.

"The perfect partner to a cold pint, we couldn't be more thrilled to have welcomed Slice Culture and their taproom pizzas to Refectory York this year.

"They're taking the North by storm at the moment and we're excited to keep working together by bringing the very freshest of Northern beer and food under one roof, in a space designed to celebrate community, culture, and everything we love about the North.

"We're putting the finishing touches to our very first Refectory York Christmas Menu at the moment and we've already got some exciting plans for next year too. But until then, come by for a Birthday beer!"
