The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for snow across some parts of the Harrogate district, which could lead to some disruption.

The warning covers most of the north of England and parts of Scotland and is in place from midnight tonight till 11am on Tuesday, November 17.

Some roads and railways are likely to be affected, with longer journey times by road, bus and train.

There is also a small chance of power outages or degradation of mobile phone services.

A spokesperson at Met Office said: “A band of rain is expected to head northwards across the United Kingdom during Monday night and Tuesday morning.

“As it encounters colder air across the region, it is likely to turn to snow for a time, mainly affecting locations at 100m to 200m altitude or higher.

“Any snow falling below this level will likely be temporary and quickly melt.

“Where snow does accumulate, depths of 1cm to 5cm are likely, while some locations above 300m and 400 m could see 5cm to 15cm.”
