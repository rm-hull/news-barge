*(Image: Ian West/PA Wire)*

Robert Pattinson has been announced as the next actor to play James Bond.

The 40-year-old was revealed in a series of Instagram posts on the official franchise account.

It comes after a lengthy casting process in which several names were rumoured to be in the running for the role.

This included the likes of Slow Horses star Jack Lowden, Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor and Nicholas Hoult.

Pattinson succeeds Daniel Craig in the role, who last played the character in 2021's No Time to Die.

In a statement reported by Variety, Pattinson said: "James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime.

"I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me.

"To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement.

"I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team."

Denis Villeneuve is set to direct the next James Bond film and discussed Pattinson's casting.

He shared: "Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey.

"We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007.

"I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond.

"He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin."

## Who is Robert Pattinson?

Robert Pattinson is an English actor who began appearing in TV and film roles in 2004.

He is best known for playing Edward Cullen in The Twilight Saga films, Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire and Bruce Wayne/Batman in 2022's The Batman.

Other major films he has appeared in include Tenet, Mickey 17, The Lighthouse and Die My Love.

In 2026, Pattinson has featured in Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, The Drama and Primetime, and he will also appear in Dune: Part Three as Scytale.
