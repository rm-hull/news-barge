Sustain, the health food, wine and coffee shop at Halls of Ripon, has applied to sell alcohol until 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays. Comments closed 5 November.

Sustain, the health food and coffee shop inside Halls of Ripon on Fishergate, has applied to North Yorkshire Council for a premises licence to sell alcohol. It wants to sell until 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

The applicant is Shop Sustain Ltd, at Halls of Ripon, 20 Fishergate, Ripon, HG4 1DY. Its public notice appeared in the Harrogate Advertiser on 22 October.

## What the licence would allow

The notice covers the sale by retail of alcohol only, at these times:

* 9.30am to 5pm, Monday to Thursday
* 9am to 10pm, Friday and Saturday

The notice lists no Sunday hours. It does not ask for live music, recorded music or late night refreshment.

## What Sustain is

Sustain describes itself on its own website as “a new health food and coffee shop” at Halls of Ripon, with “a curated selection of health foods and organic wines”. The site also says it is hiring.

It opened in April, according to Ripon BID, the city’s business improvement district. Its owner is Charlotte Baldwin. “Our aim is help people to eat well, drink well and feel well,” she told the BID’s blog in May.

The company behind it, Shop Sustain Limited, was registered at Companies House on 11 August 2025.

## Where it is

Sustain is at the Marshall Way entrance to Halls of Ripon, at the back of the Fishergate building. Its terrace has a 42 foot deep medieval well, found during building work and now under a glass cover with lights, the BID reported in September.

Halls of Ripon calls itself “the home of independent shopping in Ripon” and has about 20 independent businesses under one roof, its website says. The BID describes it as the city’s only department store. The building had a £400,000 refurbishment by Ripon firm Sterne Properties Limited. The Halls name came back to Fishergate after several years as Wrens, the BID said in August.

## What it means for you

The deadline for comments was 5 November. The application is not on the council’s list of open Harrogate-area licence applications, and we could not find a decision on the council’s website.

The council’s licensing team can be reached at [licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk.](mailto:licensing.central@northyorks.gov.uk.)
