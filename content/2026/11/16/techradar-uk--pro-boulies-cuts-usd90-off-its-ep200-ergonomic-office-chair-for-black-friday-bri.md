## Why we recommend it

Boulies' EP200 focuses on the features that matter most when you're sitting at a desk for long periods of time. Its adjustable lumbar support helps you tailor the chair to your lower back, while the adjustable headrest and armrests offer more flexibility than a basic office chair.

The breathable mesh construction is great if you find padded chairs uncomfortable during warm weather. It also has a multi-position recline, allowing you to change your sitting position throughout the day.

In his four-and-a-half-star review, our expert Steve said the chair was "Affordable, comfortable, and ergonomic".

A year-on after reviewing it, he said: "This is still my daily driver. Unless I'm testing out another model, this is the chair I use all-day at work, which I think speaks volumes. I haven't felt any fatigue when using it, although ten hours is about my limit before I need a break. It's a firm sit. If you suffer back pains or aches, this is ideal — it may sound counter-intuitive, but a soft seat office chair is the worst choice for chronic lower back pain, since it won't support the lower back or spine."

## Price context & historical value

Boulies has reduced the EP200 from its regular price of $309.99 to $219.99, a saving of $90, or 29%. This is one of the cheapest prices we've seen it sell for.

British buyers can snap up the chair for £209.99 down from £279.99.

## Should you buy it?

✅ **Buy the Boulies EP200 chair if...**

If you work from home, spend long periods at a desk, and want adjustable lumbar support, breathable mesh, and reclining functionality without paying more than $300.

❌ **Skip the Boulies EP200 chair if...**

If you need a wider seat, an integrated footrest, or more extensive adjustments. The EP500 offers those features, while the OP300 adds forward seat tilt, although both cost more.
