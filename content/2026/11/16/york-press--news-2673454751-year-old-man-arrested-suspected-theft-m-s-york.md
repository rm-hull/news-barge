A MAN has been arrested after an ‘incident’ in York city centre this afternoon.

North Yorkshire Police says that its officers have arrested a 51-year-old man this afternoon (November 16) after a ‘suspected’ shop theft in a York city centre supermarket.

As reported by The Press, vans were spotted parked up with their blue lights on in Pavement close to York Gin in York city centre this afternoon.
