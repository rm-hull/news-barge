A huge fire erupted outside New Jersey’s MetLife Stadium Sunday afternoon, shortly after the New York Jets recorded their fifth consecutive defeat of the season.

Black smoke was seen billowing from the site near Lot A and the Amex gate of the stadium at around 5.50 p.m., which subsequently drifted into the arena itself and across its vacant field.

Eyewitness video posted to social media meanwhile showed flames soaring from the tent outside. Local network WABC said the fire was visible for miles around.

Approximately 82,500 people had been in attendance to see the Jets lose 31-12 to the Buffalo Bills in the AFC East. Most had left the venue by the time the blaze broke out inside a post-game tent, an hour and 40 minutes after the final whistle had been blown.

![Eyewitness video of the fire that erupted outside of the MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, Sunday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/11/52/metlifefire.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

New Jersey State Police subsequently told Fox5 New York that firefighters from the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority, Moonachie and Carlstadt had been dispatched to tackle the second-alarm blaze as soon as it was reported.

The fire crews eventually succeeded in bringing the flames under control, reporting that no injuries had arisen during the incident.

New Jersey State Police Sergeant Charles Marchan said that nobody had been inside of the tent when it went up and that only its charred metal frame now remains.

“Upon arrival, troopers observed visible flames,” Marchan told *OutKick*. “At the time of the fire, the tent was unoccupied. NJSEA, Moonachie and Carlstadt fire personnel arrived on scene and extinguished the fire.

“No damage was reported to any additional structures, and no injuries were reported on scene. The cause of the fire is currently under investigation. No additional information is available at this time.”

![MetLife Stadium in New Jersey hosts major sporting events for tens of thousands of fans](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/15/11/2271366774..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

An NJ Transit spokesperson told *The New York Post* the episode had had no adverse impact on local train services, despite its close proximity to a nearby station.

ESPN reporter Rich Cimini, who was still inside the stadium when the blaze occurred, wrote on X: “Just announced in an empty MetLife Stadium: There’s a ‘structure fire’ near the adjacent train station. People told not to go near the east plaza of the stadium (train station side). There is smoke billowing in the stadium.”

“Situation has been resolved,” he said in an update shortly afterwards.

Given that no one was harmed, Fox Sports pundit Ralph Vacchiano branded the flaming structure a “metaphor” for the Jets’ poor season, which has seen them fall 2-8 so far. Sunday’s defeat to the Bills saw their starting quarterback Geno Smith forced off with an ankle injury that could keep him on the sidelines.
