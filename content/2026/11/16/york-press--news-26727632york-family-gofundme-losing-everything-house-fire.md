## 'We're lucky we got out alive' - York family's plea after fire destroys home

The washing machine, which was damaged in the fire *(Image: Provided)*

**A YOUNG family has been left without any belongings in the run-up to Christmas after a fire destroyed their home.**

Rebecca Darlington and her partner Lewis Quinn are appealing for donations to rebuild their lives after an electrical fault with their tumble dryer is thought to have sparked the fire on the morning of Sunday, November 8.

The family, who did not have insurance, has lost all of their possessions in the blaze, which has destroyed their kitchen and caused the rest of the house to become fire damaged.

To donate to Rebecca's GoFundMe, please visit [https://www.gofundme.com/f/please-help-our-family-after-losing-everything-to-a-fire](https://www.gofundme.com/f/please-help-our-family-after-losing-everything-to-a-fire)

They are currently living in a City of York Council hostel whilst waiting to move into a new home - something they have described as "the only good thing to come from this situation".

Rebecca, 23, who lived at the council-owned flat with her two daughters - who are aged three and 11 months - said she was making breakfast for her children and had just put a load of laundry into the tumble dryer when the fire broke out.

“I had just given my eldest daughter her breakfast, but my youngest likes it warm. They were having Weetabix, so I put her food in the microwave,” she told The Press.

“As it was heating up, I put the washing into the dryer, turned it on, and pretty much straight away, it started sparking and flaming.

“I was stood right next to it. I just screamed for my partner, who was asleep at the time, but he shot out of bed, we got the kids, and we ran.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21715800.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The washing machine, which was damaged in the fire *(Image: Provided)*

She said that the family, who were clothed only in their pyjamas and slippers at the time, had to sit in Rebecca’s car to stay warm while emergency services were called.

She added: “The fire brigade showed up before the smoke alarm even went off in the flat. It shouldn’t have taken so long for the alarm to go off.

“I’m so lucky that the smoke didn’t harm my children – it was so thick and black; the whole flat was filled in about 60 seconds.

“We’re so blessed that we were awake when it happened, otherwise that situation would have been so much worse. You can replace everything, but you can’t replace a life.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21715802.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Damage along the doorframe *(Image: Provided)*

Whilst everyone was able to leave the house safely, the smoke damage has meant that the young family have lost everything that was in their home – including food, clothes, toys, and presents intended to make Christmas special.

Rebecca said: “We've literally just put all the Christmas decorations up and we have to leave them now, and we've just spent £700 on a brand-new mattress.

“We're lucky that we all got out of it alive and unharmed, but it's still not great that we've lost everything – that includes our Christmas presents and it's my baby’s first birthday on December 2, so it’s everything for her.

“We've spent quite a lot of money on that and we've just redecorated it as well to try and make it feel a bit more like home because my partner was moving in with me.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21715803.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Damage to the floor *(Image: Provided)*

“We went away to a caravan last week, so all of the money we had was spent because we’d stocked up and we didn’t expect something like this to happen.

“The only thing that we have taken out, which we probably weren’t meant to do, is sentimental stuff like photo albums because we can just store them away in a box.”

The family are currently living in temporary accommodation, but will soon move into a new home in Burnholme after being rehoused by City of York Council.

She added: “We've been offered a two-bedroom new build house and it's beautiful, so the fact that we’ll be in our forever home is probably the only good thing to come from this situation. The lady supporting us at the council has been fantastic.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21715804.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The kitchen floor *(Image: Provided)*

“However, as it stands, we've got nothing to put in it, so we're relying on the GoFundMe to buy everything including appliances, clothes, and even food day to day.

“We’re getting support from family, but given when it’s happened in the month, none of us have been paid so the little bit of money that they've got, they’re trying to use to help us, but also handle their daily lives too.

“It’s not only put me and Lewis in a sticky situation, but it’s also put all the people that know us in a sticky situation because they've got to kind of standby without being able to do anything, because they've got nothing either.

“We've gone from being a family that can afford everything we need to how it is now. I wouldn’t wish this on our worst enemy.”
