Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon has called for regional health targets, warning that national figures can mask poorer outcomes in Yorkshire and the Humber.

The Liberal Democrat MP raised the issue in Parliament with Layla Moran, chair of the Health and Social Care Select Committee, as the committee presented its final report on obesity.

The report drew on work by Diabetes UK and Yorkshire Cancer Research.

Mr Gordon called for progress to be measured in each region so MPs could see whether the gap between the North and South was closing.

Ms Moran agreed that services varied across the country and said tackling health inequalities should be a “core mission” of government health policy.

Tom Gordon said:

> “North-South health inequalities are widening and we won't stand for it.
>
> "We have to make sure progress is equal across our regions.
>
> “The work of regional charities like Yorkshire Cancer Research is vital in making sure we have equal progress across the country.
>
> "I'm proud to have worked with them and see their work making a big impact.
>
> “Focused regional sub-targets are a vital part of that puzzle, making sure the numbers aren't obscured by a bias towards the South East.”
