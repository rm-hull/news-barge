Isaac the cat, missing since a Brooklyn building collapse killed eight people, has been found alive inside his owner's apartment.

Allie Taylor, director of the Mayor’s Office of Animal Welfare, said her office worked with the veterinary nonprofit Flatbush Cats to locate Isaac and reunite him with his young owner, 13-year-old Zariah Coleman, following Wednesday’s tragedy, which killed eight people, injured 18 others and forced the evacuation of several nearby buildings.

Zariah and her family hadn't been allowed in since the collapse, which tore off the side of her building and left a hole where her bedroom wall had been.

Workers found Isaac on Sunday in a cabinet under the kitchen sink of Zariah’s apartment after hearing a soft meow, the *New York* * Times* reported.

Isaac had gone four days without food or water and was taken to Flatbush Cats' veterinary clinic for a checkup.

![Zariah Coleman and her mother Sheena Coleman pose for a photo in New York on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, after being reunited with their cat, Issac](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/16/45/AP26320136265850.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Zariah, who had been waiting outside, hugged the cat’s cage after workers carried him out of the building.

The reunion was confirmed by New York City officials on Sunday.

“I had it in my heart they would find Issac,” Zariah told the Times. “I knew they would.”

Zariah had spoken tearfully about her missing cat at a news conference on Friday, telling reporters: “I just really hope they come and get our cat. He’s hiding from them, and I think he’s scared.”

Mayor Zohran Mamdani thanked Taylor's office, the city's Buildings Department and the nonprofit for the happy outcome.

“Isaac is now receiving the veterinary care he needs,” Mamdani said. “We’re grateful to everyone who helped bring them back together.”
