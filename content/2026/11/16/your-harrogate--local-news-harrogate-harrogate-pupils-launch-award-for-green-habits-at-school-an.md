Pupils at St Peter’s C of E Primary School in Harrogate have launched an award to recognise the ways their classmates care for the environment.

The school’s Climate Crew designed and runs the Creation Care Award with support from Rachel Patefield, the school’s climate education lead.

Children can earn the award for cutting energy use, reducing waste or caring for plants.

They are also encouraged to share examples of what they do at home.

Year 1’s Kingfishers and Year 4’s Starlings were the first classes to receive the award. Kingfishers changed the way they recycle in class and made their own recycled paper. Starlings were recognised for looking after their plants and classroom.

Year 2 pupils Ned and Juliet said:

> “Climate Crew is special because we look after nature and the earth.
>
> "We pick up litter; we care for animals and make good choices.”

Rachel Patefield said:

> “It has been wonderful to see the children taking genuine pride in their actions and sharing practical ideas with one another.
>
> "The Creation Care Award shows them that small everyday changes at school and at home really do add up.”

Headteacher Paul Griffiths said:

> “The Creation Care Award goes right to the heart of our school vision to Love, Learn and Shine - showing care for our environment, learning how our everyday choices make a difference, and giving our children the confidence to lead the way.
>
> "Seeing their enthusiasm spread across the whole school, and beyond the school gates to their families, is wonderful.”

The Climate Crew plans to present more awards throughout the academic year.
