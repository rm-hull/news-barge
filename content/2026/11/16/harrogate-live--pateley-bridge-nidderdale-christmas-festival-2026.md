The Nidderdale Christmas Festival runs in Pateley Bridge from 4 to 6 December, with a lantern parade, late night shopping, markets and a treasure trail.

The Nidderdale Christmas Festival will fill Pateley Bridge from **Friday 4 to
Sunday 6 December**. It opens with a lantern parade up the High Street, with
Santa riding a tractor, and then Santa switches on the lights of the Christmas
tree festival at St Cuthbert’s at 6pm.

The Dickensian-themed weekend is organised by Nidderdale Plus, the town’s community charity, which has published the full programme (Nidderdale Plus).

## Overview

* **When:** Friday 4 December from 4pm, Saturday 5 December from 10am, Sunday 6 December from 10am.
* **Where:** the High Street, King Street, the Auction Mart, the Memorial Hall and St Cuthbert’s on the hill.
* **The big moment:** the lantern parade, 5.30pm to 6pm on the Friday, then Santa’s switch-on at St Cuthbert’s at 6pm.
* **Cost:** walking round, the parade and the markets have no listed charge. Some events are ticketed or paid (see below).

## Friday 4 December: parade and late shopping

The lantern parade leaves the **Auction Mart at 5.30pm**. It goes up the High
Street, turns left onto Church Street and climbs to the Makers Yard on King
Street, then finishes at **St Cuthbert’s Church** at the top of the hill at
6pm. Nidderdale Community Choir sings along the way.

At 6pm Santa switches on the lights to open the church’s Christmas tree festival, and gives out gifts to children.

Also on the Friday evening:

| Time | What | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4pm to 8pm | Farmers market and craft fair | Pateley Auction Mart |
| 4pm to 8pm | Artisan workshops and makers market | King Street Workshops |
| 4.30pm to 7.30pm | 12 Days of Christmas treasure trail | Prize at the Old Sweet Shop |
| 5pm to 8pm | Late night shopping | High Street |
| 5.15pm to 7pm | Summerbridge and Dacre Band | Dales Market Corner, then the Pet Shop |
| 6pm to 7pm | Nidderdale Community Choir | Around the High Street |
| 7.30pm to 9pm | Carols by candlelight concert (tickets on sale from 20 November) | Pateley Theatre |
| 7.30pm to 9.30pm | A Taste of Yorkshire Christmas cookery evening (ticketed) | Memorial Hall |

The cookery evening is led by Steph Moon and Sandy Docherty.

A **community transport** bus runs continuously from**5pm to 8pm** from
the bus stop on King Street. It takes people round the main venues, including
the Auction Mart, and up the steep hill to the church.

## Saturday 5 December

* **10am to 5pm:** the children’s treasure trail. Work out the phrase and collect a treat at the Sweet Shop.
* **10am to 5pm:** artisan market and workshops at the Makers Yard, King Street.
* **10.30am to 3.30pm:** the Workhouse Museum opens specially, with its own children’s trail. Adults £5, under-16s free.
* **11am to 12pm:** meet Santa at the Paper People.
* **11am to 12pm:** Pateley Players perform “A Christmas Carol” in the street, with audience joining in. Meet at the bottom of the High Street.
* **11am to 3pm:** free drop-in cookie decorating with Yorkshire Born & Bread.
* **1pm to 4pm:** live Christmas music on the High Street.

The Summerbridge and Dacre Silver Band plays on the High Street at 10am.

## Sunday 6 December

* **10am to 5pm:** the artisan market at the Makers Yard.
* **10am to 2pm:** the treasure trail, for its last day.
* **10.30am to 3.30pm:** the Workhouse Museum special opening again.
* **11am to 12pm:** a second street performance of “A Christmas Carol”.
* **11.30am to 1.30pm:** Pateley Long Sword Dancers around the High Street (to be confirmed).
* **2pm to 4pm:** a family disco at the Memorial Hall. Children £2, parents free, with tickets from Nidderdale Plus or the Spar.

The weekend ends at **4.30pm** with carols by candlelight at St Cuthbert’s,
followed by the winners of the tree, photo and lantern competitions and the
raffle.

## What it means for you

* **Go on the Friday evening** if you want the parade, the switch-on and the shops open late in one trip. Be at the Auction Mart before 5.30pm.
* **Use the community transport shuttle** from King Street if the hill to St Cuthbert’s is too much.
* **Book ahead** for the Friday carol concert and the cookery evening. The concert tickets go on sale on 20 November.
* **Saturday is the best day for children**: Santa, the treasure trail, cookie decorating and the street theatre.

Nidderdale Plus has published a map of the venues and a list of traders on its festival page.
