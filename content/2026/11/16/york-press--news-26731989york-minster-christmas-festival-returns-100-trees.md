## York Minster set to unveil biggest-ever Christmas Festival with more than 100 trees

York Minster is preparing to welcome visitors to its biggest-ever Christmas Tree Festival *(Image: Emily Horner)*

YORK Minster is preparing to welcome visitors to its biggest-ever Christmas Tree Festival, with more than 100 festive trees set to fill the historic cathedral.

Returning for its sixth year, the popular event will transform the Chapter House, Nave and Lady Chapel with an array of decorated Christmas trees created by local schools, charities and businesses.

Organisers say the festival, which has become a much-loved part of York's festive calendar, will be larger than ever before when it opens with the Great Christmas Tree Switch-On Minster Mash-Up at 10am on Saturday (November 28).




Reverend Timothy Goode, Canon for Congregational Discipleship and Nurture at York Minster, said: "The Christmas Tree Festival has become a much-loved part of the York Minster year, and we are delighted to be making it bigger than ever before.

"For centuries, York Minster has stood at the heart of city life, and the festival is a wonderful opportunity to continue that tradition by bringing people together in the spirit of Christmas."

The trees have all been supplied by York Christmas Trees, a grower based less than three-and-a-half miles from the Minster which was recently named Christmas Tree Grower of the Year 2026 by the British Christmas Tree Growers Association.

Each tree will have its own theme, with displays inspired by The Muppets Christmas Carol and The Nutcracker, alongside tributes to loved ones and installations highlighting issues such as homelessness, mental health and medical conditions.

Visitors will also be able to leave messages and prayers on four dedicated prayer trees located within the Minster.

The family-focused service marks the start of Advent, with visitors then invited to explore the festival.

Emily from York Mumbler said: "This is the fourth time we've taken part in York Minster's Christmas Tree Festival, and we love it more each year.

"The Mumbler team looks forward to choosing a theme and coming together to decorate our tree in such a magnificent setting. The festival is a wonderful celebration of community, creativity, and the start of the festive season in York."

The Christmas Tree Festival runs from Saturday, November 28, until Tuesday, January 5 during normal sightseeing hours.

Entry is included with a York Minster sightseeing ticket, which remains valid for a full calendar year. Adult tickets also include free entry for up to four children aged 17 and under.

Further details about Christmas events at York Minster can be found on the cathedral's website.
