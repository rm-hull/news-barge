A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been arrested after a road traffic collision in Leeds that claimed the lives of two young people in the early hours of Sunday morning.

The incident happened at around 12.44am on Sunday (November 15) on Gelderd Road, after officers attempted to stop a blue BMW 123D.

West Yorkshire Police said the BMW failed to stop, prompting a pursuit before the vehicle was involved in a collision with a white Volkswagen Scirocco, which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, was pronounced dead at the scene.

A 23-year-old woman from York, who was a passenger in the same vehicle, was taken to hospital but sadly died a short time later.

Following the collision, officers arrested a 39-year-old man from Harrogate on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis.

He remains in police custody.

Three other occupants of the BMW – two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl – were taken to hospital with injuries that police said were not believed to be life-threatening.

All three have also been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

A police cordon remains in place on Gelderd Road while investigations continue, and motorists have been advised to avoid the area and use alternative routes.

A spokesperson at West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone who witnessed the collision or saw either vehicle in the time leading up to it, particularly anyone with relevant dash cam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to get in contact.”

If you can help, you should call West Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the West Yorkshire Police reference number 13260649342 when providing any details regarding the incident.
