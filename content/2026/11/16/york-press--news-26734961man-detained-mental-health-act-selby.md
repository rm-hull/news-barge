A MAN has been detained under the Mental Health Act after reports of suspicious behaviour.

North Yorkshire Police says it received a number of reports overnight of a man entering private driveways, trying door handles, and "acting unusually" in Selby

Officers said they later found the man and determined he was 'experiencing a mental health crisis'.

A police spokesperson said: "The male has since been detained under the Mental Health Act and is currently receiving appropriate care and treatment from healthcare professionals.

"We would like to reassure the community that there is no ongoing threat linked to these incidents.

"We would also ask people to be mindful that the individual involved is vulnerable and entitled to privacy while receiving medical treatment."

Officers thanked the public for reporting their concerns, which helped officers respond appropriately and ensure the individual received the support he needed.

They added that any suspicious behaviour or concerns for someone's welfare, should be reported to 101 or 999 in an emergency.
