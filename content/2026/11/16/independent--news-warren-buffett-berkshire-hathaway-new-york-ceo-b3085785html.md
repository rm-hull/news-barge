Warren Buffett is accelerating his plans to give away his entire wealth.

The billionaire investor pledged Monday to donate about $5.5 billion worth of Berkshire Hathaway shares to his family's foundations — an estimated value more than four times as large as recent years' Thanksgiving stock gifts.

The goal, he wrote Monday, is to grow the grants each year as he donates all his shares by 2034 to four charitable foundations associated with his three children. He reiterated his faith in his children Monday, writing that they "have given thoughtfully to worthy causes for decades” and that he trusts “their abilities and their fidelity.”

In brief comments, Buffett wrote that kindness “compounds” as he reflected on the “costless benefits” he and his longtime business partner received from their teachers. His good fortune, he said, “has come in the form of people who were kind to me and expected nothing in return.”

“This Thanksgiving, thank one of the selfless people in your own life,” Buffett wrote. “Better yet, be a benefactor for someone else.”

Buffett donated 9,225,000 Class B shares of Berkshire Hathaway stock to the foundations — 3,690,000 shares to The Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation and 1,845,000 shares each to The Sherwood Foundation, The Howard G. Buffett Foundation and NoVo Foundation. Berkshire Hathaway Class B shares opened trading Monday at $594 a share.

This year's Thanksgiving stock gift is Buffett's first since stepping down as the chairman and CEO of the $1 trillion conglomerate he built. He wrote Monday that his confidence in Berkshire Hathaway is “as strong as ever.”
