A man has been charged with causing death by dangerous driving after a collision between a car being pursued by police and another vehicle left two people from York dead.

A chase began at 12.44am on Sunday after a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for a police vehicle in Gelderd Road, Leeds, West Yorkshire Police said.

The BMW then collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco, which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crash-gelderd-road-leeds-15-nov-2026-pa-8.webp)

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, died at the scene and a passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman, also from York, was pronounced dead in hospital a short time later, the force said.

On Monday, officers said Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving.

Ross has also been charged with two counts of causing death by driving while disqualified, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed or uninsured, failing to stop for police, and aggravated vehicle taking.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crash-gelderd-road-leeds-15-nov-2026-pa-6.webp)

He is due to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court on Monday.

The force said two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl, who were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in relation the incident, have now been released on bail pending further inquiries.

They were taken to hospital for treatment to injuries that were not considered life threatening.

Officers are continuing to appeal for witnesses to any part of the incident and specialist family liaison officers are supporting the families of the two people who died.

West Yorkshire Police has referred itself to the Independent Office for Police Conduct, which has declared an independent investigation.

A spokesperson for the IOPC said on Sunday: “We have started an independent investigation after a fatal road traffic collision in Leeds following a pursuit involving West Yorkshire Police.”

They added: “After we were notified by West Yorkshire Police we declared an independent investigation at around 2.30am and sent investigators to the scene and police post incident procedure to begin gathering evidence.

“Our inquiries are at a very early stage.”

The junction where the crash took place is surrounded by several car dealerships.
