## Scarborough’s Alpamare returns to council control once again

Alpamare Water Park In Scarborough. Nyc

THE running of Scarborough’s Alpamare waterpark has been taken over by North Yorkshire Council while it works to complete the sale of the attraction to a new owner.

The authority revealed that operational management of the site transferred from Flamingo Land on November 1, creating an interim period in which the council is responsible for maintaining the facility until it can be formally handed over to a preferred purchaser.

Cllr Peter Wilkinson, the council’s executive member for finance and resources, said: “The council has put arrangements in place to maintain the site in an operational condition during this interim period and facilitate a smooth handover to the purchaser.”

It follows the council’s announcement in September that a preferred bidder had been identified following a competitive marketing process for the North Bay attraction.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21472741.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alpamare. Courtesy Stripe Leisure Consulting

The waterpark continued to operate under Flamingo Land until October 31, but the council is now effectively acting as caretaker while legal due diligence and contract negotiations continue with the prospective buyer.

The authority said a sale remains the preferred option because it would transfer future liabilities and investment responsibilities to the private sector.

Earlier this year, council officers said the preferred bidder intended to carry out improvement works before reopening the attraction during the first quarter of 2027.

The sale would bring an end to years of uncertainty surrounding Alpamare.

The attraction opened in 2016 with the help of a £9 million loan from the former Scarborough Borough Council, but its original developer, Benchmark Leisure Ltd, entered administration in 2023, leaving the council to take possession of the site and write off most of the loan.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21472740.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alpamare Interior. Courtesy Stripe Leisure Consulting

The waterpark reopened in 2024 after Flamingo Land agreed to operate the facility under lease arrangements, but that agreement expired at the end of October.

The identity of the preferred purchaser has not yet been made public and the council says further details will be released once contracts have been exchanged and the sale has been completed.
