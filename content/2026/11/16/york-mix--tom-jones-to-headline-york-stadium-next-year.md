Welsh music icon Tom Jones is heading to the *Green, Green Grass of* York next year – in a huge gig at York’s LNER Community Stadium.

The global superstar will become the first music artist to headline the community stadium in Huntington on **Friday 28 May 2027**, and is the second to be announced following York icons Shed Seven.

He will be joined on the night by special guests Level 42, Heather Small MBE and Stone Foundation.

Tickets go on sale at 10am this Friday via Ticketmaster.

The stadium gig will form part of his *Come Gather Round World Tour*.

Tom Jones was last seen in York in 2015, as part of the Music Showcase at York Races, as his York gig in 2018 was cancelled due to poor weather.

From the South Wales Valleys to stages around the world, Tom Jones has enjoyed a career spanning more than six decades, establishing himself as one of the most successful and enduring artists of all time. having sold more than 100 million records worldwide.

The Welsh icon will bring his *Come Gather Round World Tour*to York performing songs from across his extraordinary career. The set will feature tracks from his 2021 Number 1 album* Surrounded By Time,*alongside fan favourites including* It’s Not Unusual*,* What’s New Pussycat?*,* Delilah*and* Green, Green Grass of Home*.

Fans can expect an unforgettable evening of music, delivered with the charisma, energy and powerhouse vocals that have made him a global star for generations.

The concerts at the LNER are presented by live music promoters Cuffe & Taylor.

Promoter Julian Murray said: “Coming just weeks after we announced what is now a sell out show with Shed Seven at the LNER, we are delighted to be adding a second date here with the legendary Tom Jones.

“This will be a fabulous concert with an icon so what better way to kickstart live music at this incredible venue.”
