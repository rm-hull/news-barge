A gang of teenagers subjected a man to racial abuse, hit him with a bottle and stole his bike on a York street corner.

It happened at about **6am** on**Friday**(13 November) at the junction of** Cromer Street**and** Crombie Avenue**.

A group of six to eight young people assaulted the man and racially abused him before taking his bicycle, police say.

#### Incident location

The victim was hit on the back of the head with a glass bottle, but wasn’t seriously hurt.

Police say the group were mainly boys plus one girl. They were aged about 14 or 15 years old, and three of them were on bikes.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the robbery, knows those involved, or has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage that may have captured the incident or the group leaving the area.”
