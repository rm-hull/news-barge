A SERIOUS collision involving a motorbike and a car in Ryedale features on tonight's Traffic Cops TV programme.

The series returns to TV screens tonight at 8pm on Channel 5, featuring North Yorkshire Police and their specialist Roads Policing Unit.

The latest episode will follow officers as they respond to a serious collision involving a motorcycle and a car in Ryedale.

Other incidents include a vehicle crashing into a fence post and an emergency response.

Officers will also be seen searching for the driver of a red Volkswagen Polo after reports that a motorist was threatened with a hammer in the Ripon area.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: " From serious collisions and dangerous driving to wanted offenders and proactive patrols, see what it's really like policing more than 6,000 miles of roads across North Yorkshire."
