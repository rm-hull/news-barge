A group of European countries aims to set up a “migrant hub” in Rwanda where rejected migrants could be sent from next year, the Austrian leader said Monday.

The European Union has worked for years to tighten migration rules, push down the number of asylum-seekers coming to the 27-nation bloc and increase deportations of those who aren’t entitled to stay. Sending people to centers outside Europe has emerged as a central plank of that effort. Austria, Denmark, Germany, Greece and the Netherlands have spearheaded negotiations.

Austrian Chancellor Christian Stocker said the aim is for the first center to be set up in Rwanda next year.
