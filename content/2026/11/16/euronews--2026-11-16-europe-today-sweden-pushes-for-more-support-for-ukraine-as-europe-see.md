## EU foreign ministers meet in Brussels to discuss peace talks on Ukraine. We speak to Sweden’s Foreign Minister Maria Malmer Stenergard and explore the wave of cyberattacks targeting Europe with EU cybersecurity chief Juhan Lepassaar.

## On today's show:

We speak with **Maria Malmer Stenergard, Sweden's Foreign Affairs minister,** as her country pushes for more military assistance, particularly air defence, tougher sanctions against Moscow and the use of frozen Russian assets to help finance Ukraine.**Shona Murray** interviewed**Latvia's Prime Minister Andris Kulbergs** during the Informal European Summit held in Dublin last week as he said Vladimir Putin is playing tactical games and that Europe needs to show greater strength and confidence.

We debrief the escalating wave of cyberattacks Europe is facing with **Juhan Lepassaar, Executive Director of the EU Agency for Cybersecurity, ENISA.** Behind many of these incidents are increasingly sophisticated criminal and state-backed hackers, with Russia and other hostile actors exploiting Europe's digital vulnerabilities.

And as Ukraine is facing a new political test, we speak with Czech **MEP Danuše Nerudová of the European People’s Party Europe's** over who is prepared to pay for Kyiv's defence and how far the EU should go in confronting Moscow.
