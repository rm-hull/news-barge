**A long-serving trustee of Yorkshire children’s cancer charity Candlelighters has completed a trek to Everest Base Camp, taking the charity’s elephant mascot Champ along for the journey.**

Mike Richards, Vice Chair of Candlelighters, travelled to the Himalayas in October, reaching Everest Base Camp at an altitude of 5,364 metres (17,598 feet).

His trek was one of several fundraising and awareness activities undertaken by the charity’s trustees, whose voluntary contribution was recognised during Trustees’ Week, which ran from 9 to 13 November.

Candlelighters currently has 12 trustees who have collectively given 109 years of service to the charity. Their backgrounds include people with personal experience of childhood cancer, family members, medical and clinical specialists, and those with professional and business expertise.

Richards has served as a medical trustee for 27 years. He is a Consultant Paediatric Haematologist at Leeds Children’s Hospital, where he cares for children and young people with blood disorders including haemophilia, sickle cell disease and thalassaemia.

In September, he also took part in a 50km walk around Leeds to mark Candlelighters’ 50th anniversary. Organised by fellow trustee Julie White, the walk included trustees, NHS staff and Candlelighters employees.

The team began at Seacroft Hospital, where the charity was first formed, and visited locations connected with its history. The walk raised more than £1,000 for the charity.

Richards said his 27 years with Candlelighters had been a rewarding part of his working life.

> **Mike Richards, Vice Chair of Candlelighters, said:**
>
> It has been one of the privileges of my working career to be part of the Candlelighters team over many years. From small beginnings, Candlelighters has developed into a highly efficient and respected organisation that remains wholly aligned to its objectives putting children with cancer and their families at its heart. My small contribution has brought me great satisfaction and quite a few laughs along the way!
>
> The Everest trek was a moving experience, I realised just how amazing the world is and also appreciated how hard life is for young people in less well-developed countries. Champ was a source of conversation amongst many walkers, I was so pleased he waited for me when we arrived at Basecamp!

Candlelighters supports children, young people and their families following a childhood cancer diagnosis. More than 150 children are diagnosed with cancer in Yorkshire each year.

The charity provides emotional, practical and financial support, including talking therapies, grants, holidays and accommodation. It also funds research into childhood cancer and has invested more than £9.7 million in research projects over the past 25 years.

The charity was founded in 1976 and receives no government funding, relying on donations from individuals, communities, businesses and foundations.

> **Emily Wragg, Chief Executive of Candlelighters, said:**
>
> Trustees have been at the heart of Candlelighters throughout our 50-year history, and we simply wouldn’t be the charity we are today without people volunteering their time, skills and experience to help guide us. Our Trustees care passionately about Candlelighters and the families we support, and that commitment extends far beyond the boardroom.
>
> Mike’s fantastic achievement in reaching Everest Base Camp with Champ is just one example of the extraordinary enthusiasm our Trustees bring to the charity. We are enormously grateful to all our Trustees, past and present, and incredibly proud to have them as part of the Candlelighters family.

More information about Candlelighters and its work is available on the charity’s website.
