A car has been reported stolen after a break-in at a flat in York.

The incident happened at around 4.10am on Thursday (November 5), in Melrosegate in York, near the Alishaan takeaway, North Yorkshire Police confirmed.




A spokesperson said: "A suspect entered the flat and took the victim’s car.

"The vehicle has since been recovered, and enquiries remain ongoing.

"We are particularly keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident, saw anything suspicious in the area at the time, or has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage which may assist our investigation."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference 12260217765.
