Tom Gordon has questioned the security implications of a proposed development of up to 285 homes beside the Army Foundation College in Harrogate. Redrow says it has kept the Ministry of Defence fully informed about its plans.\*\*

Tom Gordon, Liberal Democrat MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough, has raised concerns in Parliament about plans to build up to 285 homes next to the Army Foundation College (AFC).

Redrow Homes Yorkshire is seeking outline planning permission for homes on farmland west of Oaker Bank, near Penny Pot Lane, directly east of the college.

Speaking in the House of Commons on 12 November, Mr Gordon questioned Luke Pollard, the Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry, about what steps were being taken to protect defence sites such as the AFC.

Mr Gordon described the college as a relatively isolated site west of Harrogate and said that its location was important to its security.

He also pointed to proposals for a further 1,000 homes nearby, arguing that the cumulative impact of development in the area needed to be considered.

The Minister said Harrogate played an important role for the armed forces and offered to arrange a meeting between Mr Gordon and the Minister for Veterans and People to discuss the town.

> **Tom Gordon MP, Liberal Democrat MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough, said:**
>
> The Army Foundation College is a relatively isolated site at the moment, and that matters for its security. Any plan to build hundreds of homes right next to it needs to be thought through very carefully. These latest plans come on top of an additional 1000 homes proposed for nearby. There are sites that are far more suitable than this one, which would limit the expansion of the AFC and pose security concerns.
>
> The Minister himself recognised that local communities care passionately about the security of these bases, and Harrogate is no different. The armed forces are a crucial part of our community here. I welcome the offer of a meeting, and I’ll be following this process every step of the way.

## Redrow says MoD has been kept informed

In response to questions from The Harrogate Informer, Redrow said it had kept the Ministry of Defence fully informed about the proposed development, including the masterplan.

> **Traci Moore, land and planning director for Redrow in Yorkshire, said:**
>
> As the landowner, we have kept the MoD fully informed about the proposed development, including the masterplan. We will continue to talk to the MoD about the plans, which will bring much-needed new homes, including 40% affordable homes, to the area, along with investment in local infrastructure and community services.

Redrow also said the proposed site occupies only a small part of a much larger Ministry of Defence landholding around the Army Foundation College.

The company said the main vehicle access would be from the east, away from the college, and that existing mature trees along the site boundary would be retained as a physical buffer.

Redrow added that national and local planning policy does not prevent housing development near sites such as the college, provided the relevant planning considerations are addressed.

The company’s statement confirms that the MoD has been kept informed and that discussions will continue. It does not, however, clarify whether the Ministry has raised any objections or expressed specific security concerns about the proposals.

Mr Gordon has welcomed the offer of a meeting with the Minister for Veterans and People and said he would continue to follow the proposals.

The application is for outline planning permission for up to 285 homes.
