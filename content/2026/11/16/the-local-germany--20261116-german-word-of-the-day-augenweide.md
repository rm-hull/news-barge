**What does Augenweide mean?**

An *Augenweide* is something that delights you visually. Literally, it means an “eye pasture” or “eye meadow” and is used to describe anything from a lovely landscape to an elegant building or a beautifully presented meal.

"A feast for the eyes" is probably the closest English equivalent, although "easy on the eye" sometimes works too.

It’s a feminine compound noun – *die Augenweide*– combining* Augen*(eyes) with* Weide* (pasture) and conjuring the wonderfully pastoral image of somewhere your eyes can graze.

The term goes back to Middle High German *ougenweide*. While* Weide*now usually means ‘pasture’, it once had a broader meaning incorporating ideas of nourishment or refreshment – not just a place where animals grazed, but something which fed and sustained.**Why do I need to know Augenweide?**

There's a limit to how many times anyone can say *schön*or* hübsch*, and sometimes it just feels plain ridiculous to keep using the same one or two words for a house, the weather or somebody’s new curtains.

And while this word lets you admire the best of what Germany has to offer with a little more enthusiasm, it also does useful work in reverse. The phrase *nicht gerade eine Augenweide* is commonly used in German to mean “not exactly a feast for the eyes”.

For German learners with a slightly more cynical streak, it may be worth adding *Augenwischerei* to your vocabulary as well.

The term can be translated as “eyewash”, “window dressing” or “smoke and mirrors”. It describes something intended to create a reassuring impression and hide an inconvenient reality.

**Use it like this:**

*Der Marktplatz mit seinen Fachwerkhäusern ist eine wahre Augenweide.*The market square with its half-timbered houses is a true feast for the eyes.* Dein Garten ist im Frühling eine echte Augenweide.*Your garden is a real feast for the eyes in spring.* Diese Torte ist eine Augenweide – fast zu schade zum Anschneiden.*This cake is a feast for the eyes – almost too lovely to cut into.* Das Bürogebäude ist nicht gerade eine Augenweide.*

The office building isn’t exactly easy on the eye.
