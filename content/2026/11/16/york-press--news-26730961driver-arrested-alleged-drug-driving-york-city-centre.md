A driver was arrested in York city centre after testing positive for drug driving.

The arrest was made by York Outer Neighbourhood Policing Team officers and special constables during proactive patrols in response to public concerns about road safety.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "As a result of his arrest the driver has been bailed pending further enquires."




North Yorkshire Police are urging anyone with information about individuals driving under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or those involved in drug possession, to contact their Neighbourhood Policing Team.

Reports can also be made online through the North Yorkshire Police website, via web chat, or by calling 101.

Crimestoppers can also be contacted anonymously on 0800 555 111 or through their website.
