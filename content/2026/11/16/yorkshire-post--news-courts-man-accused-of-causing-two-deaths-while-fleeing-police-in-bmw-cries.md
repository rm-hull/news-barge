# Man accused of causing two deaths while fleeing police in BMW cries in court

## A man accused of causing the deaths of two people by hitting their car in a stolen BMW while fleeing police cried as he appeared in court for the first time.

Leigh Ross, 39, has been charged in connection with the crash in Leeds in which Cameron Bell, 22, and 23-year-old Jade Langton were killed.

Leeds Magistrates’ Court heard he “intends to plead guilty” to causing the deaths of Mr Bell and Ms Langton.

The court was told the chase started in the early hours of Sunday after Ross was allegedly seen “doing doughnuts” at a car meet.

Ryan Thornton, prosecuting, said police attempted to get the vehicle to stop, which it did not.

Mr Thornton said the car then drove away from police, reaching speeds of 80mph on two roads, and did a U-turn.

The court heard that the vehicle hit another car on Gelderd Road, resulting in the deaths of two people.

![Police at the scene on Gelderd Road, Leeds, where two people have died and four people have been arrested after a BMW that was being pursued by police collided with a Volkswagen](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/15/16/cs-9c29019b-bf3f-4499-bee5-2e43847d57ac.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Ross, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, sobbed in the dock as he made his first appearance at court on Monday, and his voice broke as he was asked to confirm his date of birth and address.

He is charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while uninsured, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle whilst disqualified and two counts of causing death having taken a vehicle without consent.

The court heard the offences were so serious they could only be dealt with at a crown court.

As he was leaving the dock, he looked towards the public gallery and said: “I’m sorry.”

West Yorkshire Police said a chase began at 12.44am on Sunday after a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for officers in Gelderd Road.

The BMW then collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco, which was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

The driver of the Volkswagen, a 22-year-old man from York, died at the scene.

A passenger in the same car, a 23-year-old woman, also from York, was pronounced dead in hospital a short time later, the force said.
