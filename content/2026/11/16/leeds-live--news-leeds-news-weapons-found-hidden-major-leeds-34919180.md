# Weapons found hidden in major Leeds city centre street - police statement in full

## 'Known hotspot areas' have been targeted in city-centre sweeps

Dangerous weapons were found hidden in a major Leeds city centre street.

West Yorkshire Police have been sweeping streets in the city to mark the start of 'Knife Crime Awareness Week'. The force has been targeting "known hotspot areas".

Officers managed to find a metal bar and a kitchen knife hidden in overgrown vegetation off Kirkgate on Saturday, November 14. A force spokesperson said: "During a weapon sweep on Saturday, November 14, officers recovered a metal bar and a kitchen knife hidden in overgrown vegetation off Kirkgate.

"Weapons are often concealed in familiar locations for later use. These targeted searches help us identify problem areas, remove weapons from our streets, and make our communities safer."
