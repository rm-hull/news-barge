US president Donald Trump heads to Asia this week to take part in back-to-back summits on economic cooperation, world conflicts, and nuclear weapons.

Much of the world’s focus will be on the historic, first-ever trilateral meeting between Trump, Russian leader Vladimir Putin and China’s Xi Jinping on Thursday.

However, hours before leaving the White House for the Philippines, Trump managed to raise the stakes even further while suggesting North Korean leader could also join them.

“(Xi) was here a month ago, and we're also going to meet with (Russian) President Putin, and we may meet with Kim Jong Un also,” Trump said.

“So that'll be good. We have a chance to accomplish a lot.,” while stressing the decision on Kim’s attendance was up to Beijing.

![The first-ever three-way discussion will take place between China’s Xi Jinping, US President Donald Trump, and ‌Russian leader Vladimir Putin](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/12/download.-3..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Despite the strategic importance of the issues on the agenda during the trip, it could still be overshadowed by the Nvidia scandal.

CEO Jensen Huang was forced to resign last week after it emerged he had an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. The ousted tech boss has close ties to the US president, even accompanying him on Air Force One during his last trip to China in May.

However Trump is not expected to directly raise the spy allegations with Xi, one senior US administration told Reuters on condition of anonymity.

The US president is due tol land in Manila on Tuesday for the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (Asean) summit, before flying to Shenzhen in southern China late on Wednesday for the two-day Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (Apec) forum, at the end of which the first-ever three-way discussion between Xi, Trump, and ‌Putin will take place.

“The president has made it clear that, among other topics, he will discuss the reduction of nuclear weapons in the world, whose proliferation President Trump believes is very dangerous,” said a White House spokesperson.

![A pop art painting dubbed “Shenzhen 26” by Russian artist Alexey Sergienko has been unveiled in Saint Petersburg](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/16/2026-11-12T134442Z_714129936_RC2BMOA8134S_RTRMADP_3_APEC-SUMMIT-RUSSIA-ARTWORK.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A nuclear weapons control treaty between the US and Russia expired in February. It is the first time since the early 1970s that there is no legally binding limits on US and Russian nuclear forces without another agreement being under negotiation.

Russia and the US possess the largest nuclear arsenal in the world, followed by China.

The United Nations has long urged for nuclear disarmament, warning that rising geopolitical tensions have fuelled nuclear risks around the world.

“If there is a denuclearisation program, we are ready to be fully engaged in that and contribute to make it a success,” UN ​Secretary-General Antonio ‌Guterres said on Monday in Manila, where he is attending the Asean summit.

The denuclearisation talks will also spotlight the war in Iran, with Tehran being a strategic partner of both China and Russia.

![](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/16/2026-11-16T152929Z_1949166522_RC23POAMAYNI_RTRMADP_3_USA-TRUMP-NORTHKOREA.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Leaders at Asean are likely to renew calls for peaceful resolutions of conflicts, including in the South China Sea and in Myanmar. They are expected to push for efforts to protect their economies and citizens from the continuing fallout from global conflicts, such as the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz in the Persian Gulf.

The White House has said that Trump plans to sign a trade deal with Thailand while in Manila, while US officials will also meet on the sidelines with the so-called Quad, or Quadrilateral Security Dialogue, which consists of Australia, India, Japan and the US.

Trump will be looking for an overseas success on the trip, following the disastrous midterm results that saw his Republican party lose control of Congress.

“The president will undoubtedly be seeking to use this trip to reassert leadership, authority, demonstrate his statesmanship capabilities and essentially show off on the international stage,” Kurt Tong, a former US ambassador for Apec, told Bloomberg.

![The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (Apec) China 2026 summit will be held in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, China](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/12/download.-1..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The US president will also take part in bilateral talks with Xi on Thursday.

But expectations for progress at the talks in China are not high. No agreement is expected afterward, a senior US official said, according to AP and Reuters.

“In China, the administration has been trying to lower expectations; there will be no substantive progress made between Trump and Xi,” US-based geopolitical analyst Greg Poling told PBS News.

“With Trump politically weakened after the midterm drubbing here, it is likely that the Chinese side will be even less interested in making any compromises,” he said.

Other critical issues at these summits will include AI supply chains and governance, tariff reductions and the green energy transition.
