Around 600 sheep made their way through the centre of Nuremberg, Germany, on Sunday, 15 November, as they headed towards their winter quarters.

Watched by curious onlookers, the animals crossed the city's historic old town, turning its busy streets into the setting for an unusual annual spectacle.

The 10-kilometre route is the shortest way to reach their winter home, west of the city. The yearly procession also highlights the region's shepherding tradition and the role sheep play in preserving the landscape.

Grazing helps keep open areas from becoming overgrown, making the animals an important part of local conservation efforts.
