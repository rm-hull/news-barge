# Millions of DWP claimants getting bonus cash in bank accounts from Monday

## The DWP Winter Fuel Payment 2026 is due to land in bank accounts from mid-November

Millions of pensioners will see a £300 payment arrive in their bank accounts from the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) today.

The Winter Fuel Payment typically lands in mid-November, according to the DWP, meaning approximately 15 November. As that date falls on a Sunday, it's logical that Monday, 16 November would be when payments appear, being the first working day of the mid-month period.

Close to ten million people are expected to receive assistance with heating expenses this year through the Government's winter fuel payment scheme, which aims to support pensioners during the coldest months.

The scheme is presently accessible for those born on or before 1960 who reside in England, Wales or Northern Ireland. Eligible applicants could receive between £100 and £300 towards heating bills for winter 2026-2027, reports Birmingham Live.

It's crucial to understand there isn't a single payment date. Rather, payments will be issued in phases, with eligible recipients automatically receiving their money during November or December this year.

Pensioners ought to have already received correspondence confirming how much they'll receive and which bank account will receive the payment. It's typically the same account used for State Pension or other benefits.

To be eligible for the benefit, an individual's total income must fall below £35,000. For those earning above this threshold who have already received the payment, the government has said HMRC will reclaim the funds.

There are four additional categories of people who are not entitled to the payment:.

* - If you usually live outside England, Wales or Northern Ireland.
* - If you were in hospital for a whole week in September.
* - If you need permission to enter the UK and your granted leave says you cannot claim public funds.
* - If you were in prison for the whole of the week of 21 to 27 September 2026.

Care home residents will receive £100 if they were born between September 28, 1946 and June 27, 1960. Those born before September 28, 1946 will get £150.
