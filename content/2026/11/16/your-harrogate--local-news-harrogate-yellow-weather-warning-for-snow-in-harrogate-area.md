The Met Office has issued a yellow warning for snow in the Harrogate area on Tuesday morning.

The warning runs from midnight until midday on Tuesday 17th November. Snow could cause disruption on higher ground in North Yorkshire.

The Met Office said snow is likely to affect some roads and railways, with journeys by car, bus and train taking longer than usual.

There is also a small chance of power cuts or disruption to mobile phone services.
