A Christmas market is returning to bring festive cheer and showcase local talent.

The third annual Artisan Craft Makers Christmas Market will take place at The Plough, Wombleton, on Thursday, November 19, from 6pm to 9.30pm.

Around 15 stalls will feature handmade goods including soft furnishings, candles, ceramics, clothing, jewellery and artwork, all created by makers from the Ryedale area.

Lindsey Johns said: "It's a fabulous event held at The Plough.

"A chance for locals and visitors alike to attend a cosy pub for a very different reason.

"There are some very talented craftsmen/women in this area and it should be celebrated.

"With Christmas just around the corner, it is the perfect excuse to visit the pub and buy a unique gift."

The Plough will open its bar from 5pm, serving hot and cold drinks and homemade snacks to take away.

Entry to the market is free, with parking available on-site and nearby on the street.
