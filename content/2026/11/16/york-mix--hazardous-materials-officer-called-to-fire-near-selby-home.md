A fire prompted concerns over gas supplies in Selby.

Firefighters went to a fire involving a fence behind home on Flaxley Road at 12.19am yesterday (Sunday).

Crews found a cylinder was also involved in the fire. A hazardous materials officer attended to inspect it.

#### Flaxley Road, Selby

The gas board was asked to inspect the property’s gas supply, which had been damaged by the fire.

Once the fire was fully extinguished and the cylinder showed no further hotspots, the incident was left with the gas engineer and the occupant.
