## Another UK private school suddenly closes after administrators called in

Leweston School is the latest historic UK private school to close. *(Image: Leweston School)*

Another UK private school has suddenly closed after the collapse of an historic institution in York.

Leweston School, an independent Catholic boarding and day school near Sherborne in Dorset, announced during the October half term that it would not reopen.

The closure comes one year and four months after Queen Margaret's School suddenly closed in York.

The school, which taught girls aged 11 to 18 for 125 years, announced its sudden closure on July 5, 2025.

A report published by the former Queen Margaret's School administrators, FRP Advisory Trading Limited, on August 7, showed that the company had accrued costs of £397,713 over a year-long period.

![The disused school accrued costs of £397,713 in the year after it was handed over to administrators in July 2025](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/19814914.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Queen Margaret's School in York accrued costs of £397,713 in the year after it was handed over to administrators in July 2025 *(Image: Sanderson Weatherell)*

Now, Leweston School, which was founded as St Anthony’s in 1891, has closed as well. It charged fees reaching £14,990 per term for international pupils receiving full boarding.

A parliamentary motion concerning the closure said the school shut on October 27 following an announcement giving just three days’ notice before the end of half term.

It said more than 570 pupils were left without a school place, while teaching and support staff faced unemployment and financial hardship.

Leweston School said: “We are very sorry what this means for our pupils, their families and our staff, and for how little notice we have been able to give.

“Families and staff have been contacted directly.”

Every pupil was offered a place at a school in the Inspired Learning Group from November 2, including Clayesmore School and Sunninghill Prep, both around 19 miles away.

The school had been sold to a subsidiary of Concept Education in December 2025, while it registered a debenture, a form of borrowing, from HSBC in March 2026.

Its former pupils include BAFTA-winning actor Kristin Scott Thomas and musician Zoë Schwarz.

Parents and others connected with the school have since formed an independent working group which is campaigning to reopen Leweston School.

The Leweston Working Group also established a hardship fund for former staff, which had reached £30,000.

Edward Morello, Liberal Democrat MP for West Dorset, criticised the circumstances surrounding the closure.

Mr Morello said: “I am outraged that Concept made the decision to close the school with no advance warning to staff or parents.”

The sudden closure comes

The closures have taken place during a turbulent period for parts of the independent education sector following the introduction of VAT on private school fees.

Private school education and boarding fees became subject to 20 per cent VAT from January 2025.

The Independent Schools Council said at the beginning of 2026 that 105 independent schools had ceased operating since the Government announced the VAT policy, although that total included 15 schools which had merged with others.

There has been speculation that the tax changes contributed to the closure of Leweston School, but the school itself did not attribute its decision to VAT in its public statement.

The circumstances at Our Lady’s Abingdon have meanwhile been attributed to a combination of severe financial deficits, declining pupil numbers and wider economic pressures.
