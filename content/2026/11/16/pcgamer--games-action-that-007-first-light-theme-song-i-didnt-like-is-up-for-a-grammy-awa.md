Remember that 007 First Light song Lana Del Rey surprised-dropped earlier this year that I said wasn't very good because, as I put it at the time, "it sounds like Bond karaoke"? Well anyway, it's up for a Grammy Award.

007 First light, composed by Joe Henson and Alexis Leon Smith, is in the running in the "Best Score Soundtrack For Videogames and Other Interactive Media" category, alongside four other contenders:

* **Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora - From the Ashes**, composer: Joe Paesano
* **Ghost of Yotei**, composer: Toma Otowa
* **Marvel's Wolverine**, composer : David Fleming
* **Star Wars: Zero Company**, composer: Gordy Haab

I haven't heard any of the other tracks in contention (in my defense, neither Ghost of Yotei nor Wolverine are available on PC, and I actively dislike Star Wars) but after listening to the 007 First Light theme again, and with more time to think about it, I will say this: My initial assessment was absolutely correct.

This is not a good Bond theme. Lana Del Rey may be tremendously talented, but that's lost in the all-consuming effort to hit all the notes without understanding what connects them: The end result is a pale imitation built out of a musical stereotype that's readily identifiable as someone trying *really hard* to do a Bond theme based on what they were told a Bond theme is supposed to sound like.

![Lana Del Rey - First Light (Lyric Video) - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/fA82c4YkAZ4/maxresdefault.jpg)

The Grammy Awards are a music industry award, not a game award, and that means some games that you might expect to be nominated are sometimes excluded: The rules for the videogame category state that "videogames that contain music but are not available as stand-alone audio streams/releases" are not eligible for consideration, for example. So if you don't see your favorite of the year in the list, that might be why.

The videogame soundtrack category is relatively new: The Grammy Awards only added it in 2022. Previous winners include:

* 2026 - **Austin Wintory**, Sword of the Sea
* 2025 - **Winifred Phillips**, Wizardry: Proving Grounds of the Mad Overlord
* 2024 - **Stephen Barton And Gordy Haab**, Star Wars Jedi: Survivor
* 2023 - **Stephanie Economou**, Assassin's Creed Valhalla: Dawn Of Ragnarok

Anyway, will being a bad Bond theme keep 007 First Light from winning a major music industry award? The 2027 Grammy Awards take place on February 7, 2027—we'll find out then.
