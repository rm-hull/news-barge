Dearest readers…this Christmas, Treasurer’s House in York are hosting the most anticipated social event of the season.

From 14 November to 20 December, the historic townhouse is transformed for *An Inspired Christmas* – where visitors are invited to step into the story of Nancy Lycett Green’s coming-of-age debutante ball, held at Treasurer’s House in 1906.

Through elegant decorations, historical details and handcrafted displays, guests can experience the excitement and anticipation of one of Edwardian society’s most important occasions.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Putting-the-finishing-touches-on-the-Great-Hall-tree_c_National-Trust_Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Make-sure-to-fill-out-your-dance-card_c_National-Trust_-Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)

Part of Treasurer’s House’s annual *Inspired Christmas* programme, this year’s festive transformation invites visitors to become guests arriving for the evening’s celebrations.

“We’re keen to always root the stories that we have here in the history of the house,” explained Ted Walker, visitor experience officer at Treasurer’s House. “Nancy Lycett Green’s ball, which happened 120 years ago in this very house, would have been a great big entertainment, and Frank Green, who owned the house, was somewhat of a theatrical man himself.”

As preparations unfold throughout the house, visitors will discover invitations awaiting responses, dance cards ready to be filled and rooms transformed in readiness for an unforgettable night.

One room is inspired by Hans Christine Anderson fairytales, as he used to tell stories to Queen Alexandra – who stayed at Treasurer’s House – and her sisters when they were children at the Danish Royal Palace.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/treasurers-house-2026-christmas-12.webp)

“So in this particular room, we’re alluding to a lot of the imagery of his stories throughout. We’ve got Princess and the Pea, the Snow Queen, the Emperor’s New Suit, the Red Shoes.

“But every room tells a different story, whether that’s about Frank Green, whether it’s about people who stayed here, or whether it’s about that winter ball 120 years ago.”

Throughout the house, elegant decorations inspired by the glamour, fashion and social traditions of an Edwardian Christmas will bring Nancy’s debutante ball to life. From the first preparations to the excitement of guests arriving for the evening’s festivities, each room offers a glimpse into a special moment in the house’s history, combining seasonal creativity with historical storytelling.

As with every Christmas at Treasurer’s House, many of the displays have been carefully handmade by the property’s talented volunteers, alongside contributions from local groups and organisations. Following in Frank Green’s footsteps, the team has used decoration, craftsmanship and storytelling to transform the historic interiors, creating moments of wonder and discovery throughout the visitor experience.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/You-are-invited-to-the-social-event-of-the-season_c_National-Trust_-Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Admire-elegantly-decorated-trees_c_National-Trust_-Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)

“It’s always a mammoth job to put up Christmas, particularly in a house this sort of size, and it absolutely functions because of volunteers that come in,” said Ted.

“We needed Edwardian-style props and things, so I put a call out to them, and they were unbelievable in what they were offering – I had to turn stuff down!

“They have absolutely contributed to everything you see as you go around the house, and they’re the heart and soul of it – so it’s been a real joy working with them again on this.”

Alongside the festive displays, visitors can enjoy special seasonal experiences  
throughout the Christmas programme.

Visitors can also enjoy seasonal refreshments in the café, which will be serving festive favourites and warming winter drinks throughout the Christmas period.

*An Inspired Christmas at Treasurer’s House* runs from 14 November to 20 December, Saturday to Wednesday, 11am to 4pm (last entry 3.30pm).

No booking is required and normal admission applies. House tours are paused during the festive season. Find out more here.
