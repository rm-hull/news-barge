A GP surgery will launch a new winter service to help meet rising seasonal demand.

Derwent Practice is offering same-day, urgent GP appointments for acute illnesses or health concerns as part of its new service, which runs from November 23 to March 31, 2027.

A spokesperson for Derwent Practice said: "As we approach the winter months, we know that this can be a particularly challenging time for our patients, with seasonal illnesses and increased demand for healthcare services.

"To help support our patients to stay well over the winter period, the Practice has invested in additional GP-led appointment capacity to provide further support when you need it most.

"We hope that this additional investment will help us to support our patients throughout the winter months, ensuring timely access to GP care when it is needed."

Appointments can be requested through the practice’s usual channels.
