The preview version of SteamOS 3.10.0 has just dropped, and with it comes better support for AMD and Nvidia hardware, and it's now based on Linux kernel 7.3.

For AMD graphics, the latest SteamOS update adds support for HDMI 2.1 VRR (Variable Refresh Rate), FRL(Fixed Rate Link), and ALLM (Auto Low Latency Mode). This should all contribute to a smoother gaming experience with higher bandwidth video and less input lag.

As for Nvidia graphics, it has reportedly received increased stability and performance, alongside support for Mesh Shaders on Turing and newer architectures. The latest SteamOS updates also fixed rendering corruption issues in Shadow of the Tomb Raider and The Surge 2.

Then, of course, SteamOS now uses the Linux 7.3 kernel (which launched last month).

Those are the big updates from SteamOS 3.10.0, but there are also a handful of fixes and changes. Game Mode has had the following changes:

* Added support for VK\_EXT\_present\_timing
* Added support for ASUS ROG Xbox Ally X20 HDR and brightness adjustment
* Improved frame timing when using the framerate limiter with VRR
* Improved screen flickering when VRR is enabled while navigating the Steam UI or leaving it idle
* Improved cursor movement under VRR. It should now match the game's framerate over 30 fps, and stop spiking the panel's refresh rate up to the maximum refresh rate
* Fixed scaling causing overexposure in certain HDR titles, such as Control
* Fixed an issue with certain games, such as UBOAT and The Fruit of Grisaia, where the image would flicker and present stale frames in Game Mode

SteamOS Previews are experimental by nature. You have to swap your update channel in Steam settings to get access, and there's always a chance new updates come with bugs or stability problems. If you come across a problem, Valve has a form to track SteamOS bugs.

If you don't fancy downloading a preview, SteamOS 3.10.0 is likely to come to the public channel at some point in the future, based on feedback from the preview.
