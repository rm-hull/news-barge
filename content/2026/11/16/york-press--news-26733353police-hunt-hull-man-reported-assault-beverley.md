POLICE are hunting for a man from East Yorkshire after reports of an assault earlier this month.

Humberside Police have said its officers are ‘keen to locate’ 24-year-old Aiden Wilcox from Hull after reports of an assault in Beverley.

It is said to have taken place on Spark Mill Lane on November 4.
