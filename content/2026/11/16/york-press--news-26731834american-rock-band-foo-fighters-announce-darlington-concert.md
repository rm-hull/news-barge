## Foo Fighters announce concert after cryptic Clock Tower teaser

Foo Fighters *(Image: TOM BANKS/Gigs and Tours)*

Music lovers in York and North Yorkshire have a chance to see the Foo Fighters live when they headline Darlington Arena next summer for one night, with tickets on sale soon.

The American rock band has announced a Darlington gig for September 14 next year as part of a four-date UK tour. Music lovers from York can hop on a train to Darlington which takes around 30 minutes.

The Foo Fighters will play two shows at Wembley Stadium on September 9 and 11 before coming to Darlington on September 14.

The Foo Fighters will then play in Edinburgh on September 16 and finish in Dublin at Croke Park on September 18.

It comes after fans were sent into a frenzy last week when the iconic two-F logo and the words "Monday 9am GMT" were shone onto the town's Clock Tower.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21713560.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Foo Fighters logo in Darlington *(Image: GIGS AND TOURS)*

The projection, which appeared on Wednesday, November 12, prompted speculation that the band could be heading to the town as part of a new tour.

The same logo was also projected onto Wembley Stadium in London.

Artist and album presale is set to go live in two days on Wednesday, November 18.

On Thursday, November 19 it is Gigs in Scotland presale, as well as past bookers presale and Spotify presale.

The tour will reach general sale on Friday, November 20.

The band, fronted by Dave Grohl, are behind some of rock music's best-known hits, including Everlong, All My Life and The Pretender.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21713608.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Foo Fighters *(Image: TOM BANKS/Gigs and Tours)*

The newly announced shows will run from June 9 in Reykjavik through September 18 at Dublin’s Croke Park, and will include the band’s return to London Wembley Stadium for two nights, September 9th and 11th.

Tickets for the UK dates go on general Friday, November 20 at 10am GMT via www.gigsandtours.com and www.ticketmaster.co.uk.

For further information, go to foofighters.com.

Support on various dates will include The Hives, St. Vincent, Garbage, Iggy Pop, Queens of the Stone Age, Slowdive, Violet Grohl, The Vaselines, Yard Act, Nothing But Thieves, Reclus.É', Florence Road, Fat Dog and more to be announced.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21721229.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Foo Fighters Take Cover Tour 2027 *(Image: PR)*Foo Fighters recently wrapped the 2026 North American leg of the Take Cover stadium tour, which saw the band bring*“three hours of rock mayhem and celebration*” (The New York Times) to its largest crowds ever in Toronto, Chicago, Philadelphia, Washington DC and more.

The band is currently in the midst of a blockbuster trek through Australia and New Zealand, where its Sydney stop was lauded by the Sydney Morning Herald for its *“raw energy and intensity”* and featured a rare performance of 'Ballad of the Beaconsfield Miners' dedicated to Beaconsfield miner Todd Russell on the day of his passing.

The 2027 Take Cover run will undoubtedly see Dave Grohl, Nate Mendel, Chris Shiflett, Pat Smear, Rami Jaffee and Ilan Rubin continuing to blow eardrums and expectations with exhilarating three-hour rock and roll marathons packed with Foo Fighters classics, deep cuts and surprises.

Further dates including North America will be announced shortly.
