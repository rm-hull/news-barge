# Man, 53, ruled out of Trans Pennine Trail rape investigation as police hunt for attacker continues

## A 53-year-old man arrested on suspicion of raping a woman on the Trans Pennine Trail has been eliminated from police enquiries as detectives continue their hunt for the attacker.

South Yorkshire Police confirmed the man, who was arrested on Thursday (Nov 12), has been released and will face no further action following additional enquiries over the weekend.

The investigation was launched after a woman reported being raped on the Trans Pennine Trail, near Harlington Road and Furlong Road, on Wednesday (Nov 4).

Detectives have been following multiple lines of enquiry since the attack, which has caused considerable concern among local residents.

Police have confirmed that the 53-year-old man was initially released on bail following his arrest, but subsequent enquiries have ruled him out of the investigation.

Officers are continuing their efforts to identify the person responsible and have renewed their appeal for information and footage.

The suspect has been described by the victim as a man of tall and stocky build who was wearing a dark jumper, black gloves and a black face covering.

Detective Inspector Helen Hughes, the senior investigating officer, said: “We know this horrific attack has caused considerable concern in our local communities, and we are really grateful to everyone who has contributed to our investigation so far.

“Our priorities remains clear – finding the person responsible and securing justice for the woman at the heart of this case. She has been informed of this latest update and continues to be supported by officers.

“Detectives continue to explore all investigative opportunities available to identify the man responsible and bring him to justice. We are continuing to do everything we can to identify the suspect, who the victim has been able to describe as a man of a tall and stocky build, who was wearing a dark jumper, black gloves and a black face covering.

“If you believe you have further relevant information or footage which could help us achieve this and further our investigation, then I would urge you to please get in contact with us.”
