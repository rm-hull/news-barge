Nearly 2,200 migrants arrived by boat in Spain’s Canary Islands over two days, according to the country’s maritime rescue service.

On Sunday, November 15, rescue teams assisted migrants aboard crowded boats off the island of El Hierro, helping passengers, including a baby, disembark safely at port facilities.

The Maritime Rescue Service said it had assisted 1,324 people aboard 14 boats, most of whom arrived on El Hierro. The figure was close to the daily record for the Atlantic migration route, set in November 2020.

The Canary Islands remain a major entry point for migrants attempting the dangerous Atlantic crossing from West Africa to Spain. More than 13,000 people reached the archipelago by sea between January and the end of October, according to Spain’s Interior Ministry.
