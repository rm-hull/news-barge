A 'friendly' start to the school day is bringing children and families together in York.

Stockton on the Forest Primary School has launched a new weekly community breakfast club for pupils and their families, offering a calm, social start to the morning with free food and quiet activities.

Year 5 pupil Liffy said: "Everyone is friendly, the teachers come and talk and it makes the morning feel less rushed."

Attendees can enjoy fruit, cereal, and croissants, along with activities like reading, drawing, and games.

Year 6 pupil Ethan said: "We come together for breakfast and have fun."

Parents have also praised the breakfast club, with one describing it simply as "awesome".

Stockton on the Forest Primary currently has school places available.
