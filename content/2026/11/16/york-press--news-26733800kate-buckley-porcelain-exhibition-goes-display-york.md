## York artist unveils new seasonal porcelain works in exhibition

Horizon, from the Folding Light series, 2026 *(Image: Kate Buckley)*

A new exhibition of sculptural porcelain art has gone on display in York.

York-based artist Kate Buckley is showcasing her latest work at STUDIO by Rock Garden in Scarcroft Road in a solo show running until January 3, 2027.

The exhibition features two new series; the first, Folding Light, consists of white-on-white porcelain reliefs exploring the Japanese concept of yohaku no bi, or the beauty of empty space.

The second, Between Earth & Sky, is being shown publicly for the first time and includes four oak-edged porcelain ring panels, each representing a season.

Ms Buckley said: "Everything I make starts with a fold.

"I press porcelain onto folded structures, so each piece is a fold fixed in time.

"Folding Light is the quietest work I’ve made: white on white, so it’s the light and shadow that do the drawing.

"Between Earth & Sky goes the other way, with each season as a ring of colour held in the sky."

Ms Buckley, who grew up in North Wales, left a teaching career to study art full-time.

She graduated with first-class honours in 3D Contemporary Craft from York St John University in 2019.

Her practice draws on her childhood love of origami and on Jun'ichirō Tanizaki's essay In Praise of Shadows.

The exhibition is being held at the shop, gallery, and coffee bar of Rock Garden Ceramics.

The venue is known for its handmade tableware, which is used by top restaurants including Roots and The Black Swan at Oldstead.
