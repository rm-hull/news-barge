A crash on a York road is causing slow traffic this morning (Monday).

It’s on Clifton Moor Gate. Emergency services are at the scene.

The eastbound carriageway appears to be blocked.

#### Crash location

It’s close to the junction with Water Lane. As a result northbound traffic on that road is also slow.

We have no details on casualties at the moment.
