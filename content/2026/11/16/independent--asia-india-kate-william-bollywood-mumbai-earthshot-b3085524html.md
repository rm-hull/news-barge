The Prince and Princess of Wales will visit one of the biggest film production houses in India during their three-day visit for the Earthshot Prize awards ceremony in Mumbai.

William and Kate's Mumbai visit so far has been marked by a game of gully cricket with cricketing legend Sachin Tendulkar, a metro rail ride, and will include meetings with Bollywood stars as they learn about India's hugely popular Hindi-language film industry.

The couple is expected to visit Dharma Productions in Mumbai, which is co-owned by filmmaker Karan Johar.

Founded by Mr Johar’s father, Yash, in 1976, the production house has given the country some of the cult-classic Hindi-language films that range from *Kuch Kuch Hota Hai*to the more recent* Rocky Aur Rani Ki Prem Kahaani.*

![Britain's Kate, Princess of Wales reacts as she plays gully cricket during a visit to Ballard Estate on the first day of the Prince and Princess of Wales' visit to Mumbai for the sixth annual Earthshot Prize Awards, in Mumbai, India, 16 Nov 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/11/2026-11-16T105721Z_529090146_RC2UOOAO15HI_RTRMADP_3_BRITAIN-ROYALS-WILLIAM-KATE.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The Hindi film industry, or popularly Bollywood, has long helped shape the UK's engagement with Indian culture, drawing audiences across generations through its films and music.

Following their visit to Dharma, the royal couple will meet the finalists for the Earthshot Prize awards, which will be announced on Tuesday.

Founded in 2020 by heir-to-the-throne William, the Earthshot Prize aims to find solutions to climate change, and awards five ​winners £1m each ​to scale up ⁠their projects.

![Britain's Prince William and Catherine, Princess of Wales, ride an escalator at a metro station before boarding a train in Mumbai, India, 16 Nov 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/11/download.-1..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The award ceremony will be hosted by Mr Johar.

The couple is staying in the city for two days before travelling to the capital, Delhi, for a meeting with prime minister Narendra Modi on Wednesday.

![Britain's Prince William and Catherine, Princess of Wales, board a metro train in Mumbai, India, Monday, 16 Nov 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/09/download.-1..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The ceremony will feature celebrities, including actors Alia Bhatt, Simone Ashley, Sonam Kapoor and Rami Malek, Sachin Tendulkar, and feature performances by Take That, Wild Wild Women and singer Shreya Ghoshal.

William described India's contribution to the environmental initiative as significant. In a message posted on social media, he said: “India has made an extraordinary contribution to Earthshot over the past six years and is home to more Earthshot finalists and winners than any other country."

The 15 finalists include projects addressing air pollution, biodiversity loss, waste, ocean conservation and climate change. The couple will conclude their visit with a meeting with Mr Modi.

![Britain's Prince William delivers a speech at the Earthshot Prize Impact Assembly in Mumbai, India, Monday, 16 Nov 2026](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/10/download.-3..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The meeting with Mr Modi was a late addition to the schedule, after the prime minister confirmed he would be back in time for it from a summit in the Philippines.

The meeting will reinforce the UK's ties with a key partner of growing importance, using the “soft power” of the monarchy.

William and Kate met Mr Modi during their last visit to India in 2016. Discussions are likely to touch on a range of issues, including India's work on climate change and the common link of the Commonwealth.

The visit is Kate's first long-haul overseas trip since she announced her cancer diagnosis in March 2024. Now in remission, she has gradually increased her public appearances, but had not joined William on previous foreign trips.
