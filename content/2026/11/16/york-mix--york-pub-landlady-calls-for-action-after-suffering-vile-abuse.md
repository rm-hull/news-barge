The landlady of a York pub is calling for action after she suffered ‘vile’ abuse.

Lorna Turnbull runs The Bay Horse on Blossom Street.

Earlier this month, a local man subjected her to a relentless tirade of abuse.

Among the things he said was: “I will tie you up on your own pub garden and torture you.”

Lorna told ***YorkMix***: “He proceeded to send me the vilest voice notes and photos you can even imagine for over 24 hours over nothing, just because he believed I had spoken out of turn.”

She said that this was happening too often to women in the hospitality industry.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/bay-horse-blossom-street-york-dd.webp)

Many female bar workers who have to challenge men over their behaviour in a pub have experience something similar.

“They think it gives them the right to abuse women by using the most derogatory terms for simply doing her job.

“Targeted phrases such as ‘stupid bitch’ and references to women’s weight are very common.”

“It’s simply not acceptable and nor should be tolerated,” Lorna said.

“It would be amazing if publicans and hospitality staff can really get behind a policy of zero tolerance to this in York.

“Men who repeatedly target women and/or make the most serious and vile threats to their life should be barred out the pubs in town.

“It’s not safe and it’s not acceptable. We would do the same if a person was a known racist. Why can’t we do the same for someone who clearly has issues with women with any form of authority?”

She has produced a poster which says: “We stand against violence against women in the hospitality industry”.

It says: “Our pub is a safe, welcoming and respectful place for everyone – and we will not tolerate threats, harassment of any form of abuse.”

She added: “I do not believe a man would do the same to another man, and in my case expressly and repeatedly abuse somebody in the most vile fashion for over 24 hours.

“Why? Because a bloke might have knocked them out quite frankly – so it’s just a bullying tactic as women are physically just less strong.”

Her campaign is picking up support. One person said online: “This is absolutely shocking. I hope real men stood up and defended the ladies.”

Another woman wrote: “It’s happened to me in a previous pub, we ended up wearing body cams.”
