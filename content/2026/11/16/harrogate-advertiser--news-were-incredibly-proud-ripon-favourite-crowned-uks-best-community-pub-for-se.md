A pub in Ripon has been recognised as one of the very best in the country after winning an award for the second consecutive year.

The Black Swan, run by operators Sara and Kelly Hirst, has been named ‘Best Community Pub Operator’ at the 2026 Craft Union Operator Awards, retaining the title they also won in 2025.

The annual awards, organised by Craft Union Pub Company, celebrate exceptional pub operators from Craft Union pubs across the United Kingdom who go above and beyond for their guests and local communities.

Sara and Kelly were selected from five finalists after impressing judges with their outstanding commitment to bringing people together, supporting local people and creating a welcoming pub at the heart of the Ripon community.

Judges praised the pair's “passion, dedication and genuine care” for their customers, highlighting the strong relationships they have built with locals and their constant drive to find new and interesting ways to engage the community.

The Black Swan has become renowned for its community initiatives over the years.

From creating a weekly walking group that featured on ITV's Lorraine to founding Ripon Pride, which has grown into a major annual festival supported by the local council, Sara and Kelly have consistently championed opportunities to bring people together.

Judges were particularly impressed by evidence of the real impact the pub has had on local people, including first-hand accounts from guests about the important role the Black Swan plays in the community.

Sara and Kelly said: “We are so pleased to have won this award for the second year running.

“Community has always been at the heart of everything we do and we're incredibly proud that people choose to spend their time with us, whether that's supporting local events, attending Ripon Pride, joining our activities or just popping in for a chat.

“This award really belongs to our customers and the wider Ripon community.

“The support they've shown us over the years has helped make the Black Swan what it is today, and we're excited to continue finding new ways to bring people together.”

Frazer Grimbleby, Managing Director of Craft Union Pub Company, added: “Winning Best Community Pub Operator once again is a tremendous achievement.

“To win it two years in a row is something truly special and reflects the incredible impact Sara and Kirsty continue to have on their community.

“They embody everything this award stands for.

“Their dedication to bringing people together, creating meaningful connections and ensuring the Black Swan remains a welcoming pub for everyone makes them outstanding ambassadors for both their community and Craft Union.

“I’m incredibly proud of everything they have achieved.”
