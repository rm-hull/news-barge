York City are backing one of their players after he was charged over an incident in the aftermath of last season’s dramatic promotion decider.

Hiram Boateng has been charged with affray. The first hearing was at Manchester Magistrates’ Court this morning (Monday).

It’s in connection with an alleged altercation between Boateng and a Rochdale fan during a pitch invasion in the final day decider on 25 April.

In the match, there were two pitch invasions, the first after Rochdale went ahead in the 95th minute and their fans believed they had won the title.

But then City scored in added time, taking them back up to the Football League, and a second pitch invasion followed the final whistle.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/hiram-boateng-york-city.webp)

It was then when the altercation is alleged to have taken place. At least some of what happened appeared to have been captured on a video circulated on social media.

We reported Greater Manchester Police statement on 27 April, saying: “We are aware of a video that is circulating of a possible assault involving a player and we have linked in with both clubs and the National League.

“We will be further assessing the video and reviewing CCTV of the incident.”

York City responded by saying: “It will be important in the coming days for there to be a proper reflection and assessment of the circumstances around the end of the game, what happened and what can be learned.

“As a club, we are committed to assisting with any review, and we will support all our players and staff in contributing to this.

“YCFC will be supporting Hiram through all proceedings.”

Today, a York City FC spokesperson told *The Sun*: “We are aware of developments regarding the incident involving Hiram Boateng during the game against Rochdale in April.

“We are continuing to work with him and all the appropriate authorities to address this and to understand the circumstances that led to this.

“Hiram maintains the full support of everyone at York City and is a much-valued member of the squad, both on and off the pitch.”
