## Motorist named and charged in connection with 2 deaths in horror crash

A recovery vehicle lifting a car at the scene in Gelderd Road, Leeds, where two people have died *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis/PA)*

A NORTH Yorkshire man has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving following a fatal crash at the weekend.

Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place in Harrogate, is in court today (Monday) after being charged in connection with a collision on Gelderd Road in Leeds.

A 22-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman, both from York, died in the incident, which happened at 12.44am on Sunday.

## What police have said

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "A man is appearing at court today charged over a road traffic collision in Leeds which tragically resulted in the deaths of two people.

"Leigh Ross, aged 39, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving in relation to the collision in Gelderd Road, Leeds, yesterday, which caused the deaths of a 22-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman, both from York.

"He has also been charged with two counts of causing death by driving while disqualified; two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed/uninsured; failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required by a constable; and aggravated vehicle taking.

"He has been remanded in custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates Court today.

"Two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl, who were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in relation the incident, have now been released on bail pending further enquiries.

![A police cordon near to the scene on Gelderd Road, Leeds (Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis/PA)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21722190.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police cordon near to the scene on Gelderd Road, Leeds *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis/PA)*

"Officers from West Yorkshire Police Major Collision Enquiry Team are continuing to appeal for any witnesses to any part of the incident.

"Specialist family liaison officers are supporting the families of the two people who died.

"The families have requested that people respect their privacy at what continues to be a very difficult time for them.

"Anyone with information about the incident, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online via the Live Chat facility on our website quoting reference 13260649342."
