Emergency services were called after a vehicle was involved in a crash with a wall near York.

Fire crews from Selby attended the incident in North Duffield at 5.40pm on Sunday (November 15).




A spokesperson for the service said: "Selby responded to a single-vehicle road traffic collision where a vehicle had come off the road and hit a wall.

"All persons were out on arrival with no injuries.

"Crews provided scene safety and the incident was left with police."

No injuries were reported and the incident was handed over to police after firefighters made the scene safe.
