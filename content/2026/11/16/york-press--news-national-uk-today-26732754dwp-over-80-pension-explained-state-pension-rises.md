Many older state pensioners - aged over 80 - are in line for an extra cash boost to their weekly payments from April, on top of the annual increase confirmed under the triple lock.

The additional payment is available to certain pensioners who reached State Pension age before April 2016 and are receiving the old basic State Pension.

Unlike those who qualify for the newer State Pension system, older pensioners can receive an additional payment when they reach the age of 80, provided they meet the qualifying conditions.

The change comes as millions of pensioners prepare for the next annual State Pension increase, which will take effect from April 2027.

The basic State Pension is expected to rise by 3.9% under the triple lock, taking the weekly rate from £184.90 to £192.10.

This represents an increase of £7.20 a week, or approximately £374 a year, for someone receiving the full basic State Pension.

The triple lock guarantees that the State Pension rises each April by whichever is highest of inflation, average earnings growth or 2.5%. The Government has confirmed it will remain in place for the 2027/28 financial year.

In a statement to parliament, DWP secretary Pat McFadden said: "I have concluded my statutory annual review of State Pension and benefit rates under the Social Security Administration Act 1992. The new rates will apply in the tax year 2027-28 and will mainly come into effect from 12 April 2027.

"I am pleased to announce that the basic and full rate of the new State Pensions will be increased by 3.9%, in line with the increase in average weekly earnings in the year to May-July 2026.

"This delivers on our Triple Lock commitment throughout this Parliament to increase these rates in line with the highest of growth in prices, growth in earnings or 2.5%. From April, the full annual rate of the new State Pension will increase by around £490. The full annual rate of the basic State Pension will increase by around £375."

He later added: "Other State Pension and benefit rates covered by my statutory review will be increased by 3.5%, in line with the increase in the consumer prices index in the year to September 2026.

This includes most working-age benefits and other benefits for people below State Pension age; benefits to help with additional needs arising from disability; Statutory Payments including Statutory Sick Pay and Statutory Maternity Pay; and Additional State Pension. The Pension Credit Savings Credit maximum amount will also increase by 3.5%."

## What is the Additional State Pension and who can get it?

The additional payment for people aged 80 and over is known as the over-80 pensio, or Serps. It is designed to provide extra support to older pensioners who receive a lower rate of State Pension under the previous system.

The old basic State Pension was closed to new claims in April 2016, when the new State Pension system was introduced.

People who reached State Pension age before that date generally receive the old basic State Pension, alongside any additional State Pension they built up through their National Insurance contributions.

Those reaching State Pension age after April 2016 instead qualify under the new system, which has a higher maximum weekly rate and operates under different rules.
