Residents of Harrogate and Knaresborough are invited to share their ideas for a more sustainable future at an event this Saturday.

The Mayor of Knaresborough, Shan Oakes, will host the event on Saturday 21 November at Chaloner Hall, King James’s School in Knaresborough.

Environmental campaigner and author Rob Hopkins will lead the session.

Hopkins, who co-founded the international Transition Network, has helped inspire local projects including Knaresborough Forest Park and Long Lands Common.

Drawing on his book *How to Fall in Love with the Future*, he will ask residents to imagine what they would like their communities to look like and explore how they could work together to put those ideas into practice.

Organisers Shan Oakes and Bill Rigby are asking people to register in advance to help them plan catering.

*Some places are still available and anyone wishing to attend can call Shan on 07769 607710 to book.*
