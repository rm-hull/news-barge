A vehicle crashed into a wall in a North Yorkshire village last night (Sunday).

It came off the road and hit a wall in North Duffield at 5.40pm.

Everyone was out of the vehicle when crews from Selby arrived, and no one was injured.

#### North Duffield

Firefighters provided scene safety, and the incident was then left with police.
