This latest deal from NordVPN only applies the Basic plan. The previous Black Friday deals on the higher-end Complete and Prime plans remain unchanged.

Streamers should take special note of this new deal. NordVPN has ranked as my number one streaming VPN for over 18 months now. It unblocks the likes of Netflix, Prime Video, and Disney+ libraries from anywhere first time and without any annoying buffering and it's excellent for live sports too.
