Police arrested a driver in York city centre after he tested positive at the roadside for drug driving.

He has been bailed while enquiries continue.

The arrest followed proactive patrols in York last night (Sunday) by officers from the York Outer Neighbourhood Policing Team (NPT).

A police community message said York residents have raised road safety as a concern in the force’s priority survey.

Anyone who knows of someone driving under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or who is involved in drug possession, is asked to contact their local NPT. More information is available on the police website.

Reports can be made using the force’s online reporting tools, through web chat with the Force Communications Room, or by calling 101.

In an emergency, dial 999. Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online.
