ONE of the UK’s worst-rated public toilets is in York according to a new national ranking.

St George's Field public toilets in York were ranked the fourth-worst in the UK, according to an analysis of Google reviews by bathroom experts at Bathroom Fort.

The analysis reviewed 376 UK public toilets with at least 20 Google reviews each.

St George's Field toilets received an average rating of 1.43 out of five from 28 reviews, with one reviewer writing: "Disgusting, if you have to pay it would be reasonable to have them clean."

York had three other entries in the bottom 30: St Leonard's Place (1.66), Castle Walk (1.71), and Silver Street (1.95).

One Castle Walk visitor in August wrote: "The toilets in prison are even better."

Across the four, 48 of the 68 one and two-star reviews with comments from the past year mention the 50p charge or paying, and 40 describe the toilets as dirty or smelly.

York Station’s public toilets scored the highest in the city, with a rating of 4.21.

Faz Salloo, director at Bathroom Fort, said: "A good public toilet doesn't need to be fancy. It needs to be clean, with loo roll, soap and a door that opens and locks.

"Get those right and most people are happy, whether it's free or not. What people really mind is paying to get in and finding it dirty or out of order."

Nationally, the Queen's Drive seafront toilets in Exmouth, Devon, came bottom on 1.33 out of 5, and reviewers say all 10 of the lowest-rated toilets charge to get in.

The volunteer-run public toilets in Gargrave, near Skipton in North Yorkshire, were the best-rated on 4.96.

All 10 of the worst are in England, while Scotland has seven of the 10 best.
