Skaters returned to York Designer Outlet over the weekend as Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland opened for its 21st season.

A new woodland light display surrounds the 975-square-metre outdoor ice rink, where visitors skate around a 30ft Christmas tree.

Organisers say more than a million skaters have taken to the ice since the event began in York.

New additions this year include log firepits for toasting marshmallows and a redesigned rinkside café and bar, The Chalet.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afabc46491805efec1.png)

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afabc4739f7e0b59fe.png)

Singers Beki Ann and Keira Upton performed there over the opening weekend, with more live music planned throughout the season.

Event organiser Jennifer Rojko said:

> “We’ve had a wonderful opening weekend, with perfect ice and perfect skating conditions under blue skies and sunshine.
>
> "And we’ve received such enthusiastic comments about the dazzling backdrop to the rink, and our new look alpine Chalet with its cosy fireplace and windows.”

The funfair sees the return of the Brooklands Dodgems, which date back to 1937.

Families can also follow a trail of lights to Santa’s Cabin, where each child will receive a gift.

*Reservations to visit Santa are open now either through www.yorkshireswinterwonderland.com or directly at www.bookfatherchristmas.co.uk/York.  Tickets are priced at £7 per child with £1.50 family booking fee.  A reservation is required to visit Santa but can be made on the day, subject to availability.*
