A professional basketball player from the U.S. has been arrested in Nigeria after almost 34 kilograms of cannabis was allegedly found in his luggage.

The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) said on Sunday that Greene Da'monte ​Khiree from Savannah, Georgia, arrived at Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Lagos on 11 November on an Emirates flight from Thailand via Dubai.

Officers had the 28-year-old under surveillance and arrested him in the airport car park after he left behind two bags with his name tag on them, the agency said.

![The 28-year-old said he had come to Nigeria for a holiday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/13/51/NDLEA-basketball.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The NDLEA claims the luggage contained 32 parcels of cannabis as well as a tracking device.

Khiree, who was the leading scorer with the Georgia Bombers semi-professional basketball team during the 2023 to 2024 season, told officers that he had lived and played professional ‌basketball in Thailand since October ​2025 and had come to ‌Nigeria on holiday to build networks for his basketball career

A forensic examination of Khiree’s mobile phone had found information linking him to the drug shipment, according to the NDLEA.

The International Basketball Federation (FIBA) lists a player by the name of Damonte Greene, with the most recent appearance at the Phuket Elite Cup in Thailand, on 26 September.

![Chinedu Onwude was also recently caught by the NDLEA](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/13/39/NDLEA-cereal.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The NDLEA aims to eradicate the illicit trafficking of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances by “cutting off the supply” and reducing demand.

The agency recently intercepted a 25.05 kilograms consignment of methamphetamine concealed in packs of assorted breakfast cereals imported from South Africa through Murtala Muhammed International Airport.

The suspect, Omokan Isaac Babatope, whose real name is Chinedu Onwude, was arrested on 11 November at the arrival hall of Terminal II of the Lagos airport upon his return from South Africa.

A thorough search of his two travelling boxes led to the recovery of twenty-five parcels of a white crystalline substance, which tested positive for methamphetamine. The parcels had been carefully concealed inside packs of assorted breakfast cereals and cereal-based food products in a bid to beat security screening.

In Lagos, NDLEA operatives on 10 November also arrested a 33-year-old man, Taiwo Adebayo Aderoju, in an intelligence-led operation at Ebute Metta, where 119.71kg of cannabis sativa was recovered from him.
