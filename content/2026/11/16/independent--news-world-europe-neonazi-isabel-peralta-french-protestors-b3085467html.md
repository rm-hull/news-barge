A spokeswoman for the neo Nazi group Nucleo Nacional has been arrested after allegedly saying she wanted to massacre French protestors in Paris.

Isabel Peralta, 24, was detained on 6 November after Spanish police intercepted a phone call to her boyfriend, in which she expressed regret she could not carry out a shooting spree because she had to work, according to the Spanish newspaper El País.

“I’m opening the bar, instead of grabbing an R15 [semi-automatic rifle] to shoot at everything in sight... With three magazines, I’d kill 60 of them and that would still set an example,” she reportedly said.

The call is reported to have taken place a few days after the student protests on 17 October.

![Spanish neo-Nazi Isabel Peralta at rally organised by Nucleo Nacional outside the Moroccan embassy in Madrid in July](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/11/2288498802..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Thirteen other members of Nucleo Nacional, including the leader Ivan Rico, were also arrested and the group disbanded.

Police say Peralta owned 13 rifles, three pistols, and ten boxes of ammunition at her home. One of the weapons bore the emblem of a Spanish contingent that fought alongside the Nazi army during World War II, reported El País.

Authorities also found knives marked with swastikas, a sculpture of the Third Reich eagle, and brass knuckles adorned with a Nazi emblem. A sculpture of Hitler was found in the far-right activist's living room, along with a painting of a swastika, and ten knives were seized.

A few days earlier, Peralta had reportedly shared a threat against Spain’s prime minister, posting “blow Pedro Sánchez’s head off” on social media, and calling for an “armed movement” in the country.

![Peralta was arrested along with 13 other members of the far-right group](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/09/2292654979..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Police said that after analysing the evidence, they believe Peralta is “ready to sacrifice her life to defend her ideology.”

She had previously made headlines after performing a Nazi salute during a demonstration in front of the Moroccan embassy in Madrid to protest against a surge in migrants arriving in the Spanish North African enclave of Ceuta.

Last year she was sentenced to one year in prison and a fine for provoking discrimination and hatred against the Moroccan community for remarks made in 2021, in which she called for “death to the invader” during a protest.

Her appeal was denied and her sentence upheld in July.

![Members of Spanish neo-Nazi group Nucleo Nacional perform the Nazi salute during a demonstration in Madrid in October](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/09/2297843879..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In 2023, Germany banned Peralta from entering the country for life. She had arrived at Frankfurt Airport carrying a Nazi flag and a copy of Mein Kampf in her suitcase the previous year.

The court in the northern city of Gijón issued an order on Thursday suspending the activities of Nucleo Nacional and closed it’s premises. They also blocked the group’s social media and bank accounts.

The 14 members of the group were arrested on suspicion of inciting hatred against vulnerable groups and people with opposing ideologies.

![The neo-Nazi group has now been disbanded](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/09/2292655715..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Spanish police said on X that the operation covered Madrid, Asturias, Guadalajara, Valladolid, Pontevedra, Burgos and Alicante.

The Spanish far-right and neo-Nazi political organisation emerged from far-right protests against Spain’s amnesty law in late 2023.

An unnamed Nucleo Nacional representative wearing a black shirt with the group’s logo posted a video on X saying the movement was a victim of “persecution and political repression”.

“They won’t beat us,” the post said.
