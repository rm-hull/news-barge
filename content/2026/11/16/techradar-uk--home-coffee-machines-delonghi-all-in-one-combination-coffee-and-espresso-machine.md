### De'Longhi All-In-One Combination Coffee & Espresso Machine: two-minute review

The De'Longhi All-In-One Combination Coffee & Espresso Machine brews every sort of coffee beverage you can think of. Granted, it's not fully automatic, so you'll have to be the barista, but getting started is easy and you'll be able to make a wide variety of drinks with very little practice.

Make drip coffee the traditional way, by measuring out coffee grounds into the basket. A reusable plastic filter is included, but you can use a cone #4 paper filter if you prefer. The large carafe makes 60 ounces of coffee, and the half-carafe setting brews 30 ounces. You can pre-set the machine to automatically begin brewing at a certain time. The warming plate will keep your coffee hot or reheat it. A pull-down tray holds your mug so you can brew a single serving of coffee right into it. Choose the 8oz, 12oz, 16oz, or 20oz mug/travel mug setting. There are Classic and a Bold settings for drip coffee, so you can adjust the flavor to suit your preferences.

The espresso portafilter comes with two filter baskets, one for brewing a single espresso and one for a double espresso. The machine uses 15 bars of pressure to make espresso.

The adjustable steam wand has a Latte setting, which heats your milk without scalding it, and a Cappuccino setting, which steams and foams your milk of choice. This wand also dispenses fresh hot water for Americanos or non-coffee drinks. There is a frother whisk tucked into the side of the machine that just froths your milk, which is perfect for cold foam (or hot foam if you pre-heat your milk).

As with any coffee machine, you'll need to clean everything after you're done brewing. Many of the parts can go into the dishwasher, and the parts that do need to be hand-washed are quick and easy to clean.

## Cold brew

In general coffee terms, a cold brew is brewed in the refrigerator for eight or more hours for a strong but smooth brew. Technically, a coffee machine can't make cold brew like that, but it does have a Cold Brew setting so I tested that out. I set up the coffee using a paper filter as recommended in the user guide. Brewing was slow, it took about 12 minutes to brew eight ounces of lukewarm coffee. This wasn't my favorite setting to be honest. Once I added ice, the coffee went pretty weak.

## Frother whisk

The frother whisk is different from the steam wand. It foams but doesn't add any steam to your milk. It also doesn't change the temperature. So, it's perfect if you want cold foam for your favorite iced coffee beverage. It can also make hot foam, you just have to heat your milk before using this. I decided to make an iced coffee with cold foam using some of the iced coffee I had made previously. I put a few ounces of oat milk into a small glass jar and frothed it. It quickly tripled or quadrupled in volume, though for some reason it wasn't as airy-looking as my steamed foam was. I poured what I wanted into my mug. The foam held up well to stirring.

## Buy it if

**You want both coffee and espresso** The De’Longhi All-In-One Combination Coffee & Espresso Machine makes real espresso (and double espresso) with 15 bars of pressure and six different serving sizes of real drip coffee. You can even set up the machine to make your coffee the next morning and keep it warm for you.**You like iced coffee** The De’Longhi All-In-One Combination Coffee & Espresso Machine makes iced coffee two different ways. With both a Cold Brew setting and an Over Ice setting, you're sure to find a method you like.**You want frothed milk**

This machine offers several different ways to froth milk. The built-in frother whisk makes cold foam (or hot foam if you pre-heat your milk) and tucks away when not in use. The steam wand has two different settings, Cappuccino for steamed and foamed milk, Latte for just steamed.

## Don't buy it if

**You don't want bells and whistles** If you're not going to use most of the features, it's not going to be worth the space or the expense to buy this machine.**You want to use whole beans** Of course you can buy whole beans, but you'll have to grind them yourself before using them. This machine only takes pre-ground coffee beans.**You want a one-touch machine**

While this machine lets you make just about anything, you still have to do the work of measuring the coffee into the filter and dumping out the used grounds and cleaning the filter after each use.
