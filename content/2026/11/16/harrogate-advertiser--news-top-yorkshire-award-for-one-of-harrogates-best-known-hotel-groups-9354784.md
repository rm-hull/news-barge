One of Harrogate’s best known names in hotels, restaurant and bars has won a top award at the The Yorkshire Post Business Awards.

Flying the flag for Harrogate amid the best regional companies large and small was The Fat Badger Group which scooped Best Hospitality Business of the Year for Yorkshire at the awards at New Dock Hall in Leeds.

Managed by HRH Group/Simon Cotton) The Fat Badger Group is a collection of boutique hotels, characterful traditional pubs, and AA Rosette-rated restaurants in Harrogate and across North Yorkshire.

The group is probably best known for The Fat Badger itself.

Launched by the HRH Group in 2010 inside the historic White Hart Hotel on Cold Bath Road in the town's Montpellier Quarter, the characterful bar has proven hugely popular ever since.

Highly commended on the same category was fellow Harrogate bar - Three’s a Crowd Harrogate.

Also shining in the awards was another Harrogate business - Clearpoint Recycling.

Based in Haywra Street, Harrogate, the company won Exporter of the Year while its founder and CEO Mark Garrett wonBusiness Leader of the Year.

More than 400 people were in attendance at the event in Leeds last Wednesday night, which was presented by broadcaster Christine Talbot.
