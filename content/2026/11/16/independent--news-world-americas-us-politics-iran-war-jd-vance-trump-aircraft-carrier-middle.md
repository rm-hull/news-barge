JD Vance has insisted that Donald Trump will not let Iran become a “forever war”, even as the president sent a third American aircraft carrier to the Middle East.

The vice president said the administration wouldn’t allow the conflict, which Trump launched alongside Israel in February, to reach the two-year mark because it was not what the American people wanted.

His comments came as the USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived in the Middle East with thousands of additional troops on Monday as president Trump weighs up potential military action.

For months, Trump has oscillated between launching further attacks on Iran while also insisting that the war was “effectively over”.

Vance, who has attempted to distance himself from the unpopular war after a disastrous Republican defeat in the midterms, reminded people that the administration had promised “no more forever wars”.

![JD Vance blamed the Iran war for his party’s losses at the midterm elections](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/21/download..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He told Fox News: “[Trump] is not going to let this become a forever war, and the people who want it to become a forever war, they can attack me all they want to because we signed up to not do this. We told the American people we would do no more forever wars.

“Yes, the president felt he had to destroy the nuclear program, and yes, the Iranian nuclear program is a threat to the United States of America.”

Vance added: “But we are not going to allow this to become a 10 or 15-year commitment, or frankly, even a two-year commitment despite the fact that a lot of neoconservatives and a lot of other people want us to stay there forever.

“We're not going to stay there forever. We promised we wouldn't, and I have full confidence the president will get us out of this thing with great results”.

![The USS Theodore Roosevelt arrived in the Middle East on Monday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/21/2286457897..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The USS Theodore Roosevelt left San Diego in September and joins the USS George H.W. Bush and the USS George Washington aircraft carriers, along with smaller navy ships.

Vance, who is considered a potential contender for the 2028 presidential election, has blamed the Iran war for the Republicans' devastating defeat in the midterms.

He wrote on X this month: “If you'd like to argue it was worth it, that losing the Senate for the next 6 years was worth it, then do so. Argue it was a worthwhile cost to pay.

“Agree or disagree, that's at least honest. But everyone knows why we lost. And any honest conversation about where we go from here must start there.”

A poll last week found that some 71 per cent of Americans want the incoming Democratic-controlled Congress to pass legislation ending the conflict with Iran. That figure includes 93 per cent of Democrats and 51 per cent of Republicans.
