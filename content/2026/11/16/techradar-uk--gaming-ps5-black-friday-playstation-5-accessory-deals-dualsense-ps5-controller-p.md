With just days to go before the *GTA 6*release, a lot of PS5 owners will be thinking the same thing: it's time to take advantage of Black Friday deals to upgrade your PS5 controller, headset or earbuds to make the most of your* GTA 6* day, weekend or week off.

Black Friday isn't here yet but many Black Friday deals are already live, and there are some serious savings — especially on more premium products. Interestingly, some deals have started in one country but not others, so we've seen good UK deals on the likes of SteelSeries headsets but similar deals haven't gone live in the US yet; similarly we've seen good SSD deals in the US that haven't made it to the UK so far. We'd expect that to change soon.

If you're a PlayStation Plus subscriber, the first place to go is the PlayStation Store. It has lots of Black Friday PS5 deals, and subscribers get an additional 5% off. In many cases that means the Sony Black Friday deal is cheaper than retailers' Black Friday deals, if only by a little.
