# DWP confirms new 6-month Universal Credit rule from end of November

## The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) has confirmed a new six-month job guarantee

A total of 90,000 young people are set to be offered a guaranteed job, regardless of their location, under a new initiative from the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) and the Labour government.

Every eligible 18 to 24 year old across Great Britain who is claiming Universal Credit and actively seeking employment will be guaranteed a fully-funded, six-month position matched to their skillset from the end of November, as the Government pushes ahead with a fundamental overhaul of how it supports young people, reports Birmingham Live.

Following a successful trial, guaranteed jobs for young people on Universal Credit will be rolled out nationwide across Great Britain, aiming to support over 90,000 young people who are furthest from employment into suitable roles. From April, those who qualify will also receive support into work sooner - after 12 months rather than 18 - as part of a major shake-up in provision for young people.

The scheme is intended to help more than 90,000 young people into employment by March 2029. Labour's Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden said: "The longer someone is out of work, the harder it is to get back in, and no young person should be left waiting on a scrapheap for an opportunity.

"That is why we are expanding our guaranteed jobs scheme across Great Britain and bringing it forward, so that help arrives when it matters most. Employers are crying out for talent, and there is a generation of talent ready to be unleashed. We are backing them with the support they need to build a fulfilling future."

One young person who took part in the Jobs Guarantee scheme shared their experience: "Taking part in the Jobs Guarantee scheme has had a really positive impact on my confidence with finding and being in work.

"Before starting my placement at Kenny Waste, being out of work made me feel uncertain about my abilities in settling into a workplace and being able to handle the job role after being out of one for so long. Working in a supportive team and having reassurance from my brilliant co-workers helped me overcome this.

"I now feel much more confident in my abilities to handle the workload and progress more in the workplace. Even though I have only been in the role for 6 weeks, I feel like it was the perfect placement for me and have gained so much from it already. I have the Jobs Guarantee scheme to thank for this, as the support I had there was amazing and they really do find the right role for each person."
