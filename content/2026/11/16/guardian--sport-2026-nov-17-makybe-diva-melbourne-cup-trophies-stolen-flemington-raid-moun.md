A police raid has failed to locate three Melbourne Cup trophies belonging to champion horse Makybe Diva after a smash and grab at Flemington racecourse.

A Mount Waverley home in Melbourne’s east was raided on Monday evening after thieves smashed their way into a glass display case at the racecourse about 6.30am.

A 36-year-old Malvern East man was arrested at the Solomon Street home.

He was interviewed by police and released, pending summons.

The trophies, which Victoria police says are valued at $1m each, are yet to be recovered.

The cups, won by the history-making mare in 2003, 2004 and 2005, had been on public display at the racecourse, which hosts the Melbourne Cup, since September.

The champion racehorse, which died in February at the age of 27 after a short and sudden battle with a gastrointestinal condition, was “the people’s horse’, former owner Tony Santic told 3AW.

“Hopefully we can get them back in one piece,” Santic said. “It’s more than devastating. I just can’t believe it.

“I just want to try to get them back and hopefully we get them back in one piece.”

The Victorian Racing Club said the theft was devastating.

“Victoria police is investigating a theft incident that occurred at Flemington racecourse [on Monday] morning involving three Melbourne Cup trophies,” it said.

“We are devastated by the incident and are in direct contact with Tony Santic.”
