A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been arrested after two people from York died in a crash during a police pursuit.

Police said a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for officers on Gelderd Road in Leeds at around 12.44am on Sunday.

Officers began a pursuit and the BMW collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco as it was turning from Lowfields Road onto Gelderd Road.

The Volkswagen driver, a 22-year-old man from York, was pronounced dead at the scene.

His passenger, a 23-year-old woman also from York, died in hospital a short time later.

Police arrested the Harrogate man on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving and driving while over the prescribed limit for cannabis.

Three other people in the BMW, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, were taken to hospital.

Their injuries were not believed to be life-threatening. All three were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct has opened an investigation into the incident.

Police are asking anyone who witnessed the collision or has relevant dashcam footage to come forward.
