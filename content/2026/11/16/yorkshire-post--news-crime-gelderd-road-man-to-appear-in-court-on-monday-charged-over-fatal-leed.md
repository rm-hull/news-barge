# Gelderd Road: Man to appear in court on Monday charged over fatal Leeds crash

## A man is appearing at court today charged over a crash in Leeds which tragically resulted in the deaths of a young man and woman from York.

Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving in relation to the collision in Gelderd Road, Leeds on Sunday November 15 which caused the deaths of a 22-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman.

He has also been charged with two counts of causing death by driving while disqualified; two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed/uninsured; failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required by a constable; and aggravated vehicle taking.

He has been remanded in custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates Court on Monday.

Two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl, who were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in relation the incident, have now been released on bail pending further enquiries.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police Major Collision Enquiry Team are continuing to appeal for any witnesses to any part of the incident.

Specialist family liaison officers are supporting the families of the two people who died.
