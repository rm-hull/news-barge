LAW firm Ridley & Hall has expanded its specialist legal services into North Yorkshire.

The firm has opened a new office in Harrogate, taking on more than 400 family and property cases from Berwins Solicitors after the firm filed notice of intention to appoint an administrator.

Ridley & Hall, which already has offices in Huddersfield, Leeds, Pontefract and Barnsley, acted quickly to secure the cases and hire members of the Berwins team.

Emma Pearmaine, managing director of Ridley & Hall, said: "The fact that we were able to react so quickly and approach Berwins to take on these cases, and offer immediate employment to a number of people, is a reflection of the strength and agility of our business.




"We’ve recruited some exceptional legal talent and we are delighted to welcome them to the Ridley & Hall team.

"We know they will bring a great deal of expertise to our business and ensure our Harrogate office is established on a solid footing."

She said: "Equally, whilst it is obviously sad to see any legal firm, especially one of Berwins’ history and standing, be in a position where it has to appoint administrators, our swift action has also brought reassurance to hundreds of clients who were potentially facing significant disruption.

"We have been in contact with all to inform them that their cases will now pass over to our business, and to reassure them that we have been working around the clock behind the scenes to successfully transfer their files."

The new office, located at WorkWell Offices on Copthall Bridge, is being led by six former Berwins staff members.

Residential property specialists Michelle Crowhurst, Rachael Fell, Alice Hoyle, and Jenny Spence have joined the team, along with family law experts Danielle Day and Rachael Lyon.

Ms Crowhurst will lead the Residential Property team, bringing extensive knowledge of the Harrogate property market.

Ms Day, who will lead the Family Law team, is ranked as a Band 1 lawyer in the latest Chambers and Partners legal guide.

Ms Pearmaine said: "Harrogate has long been on our radar as a potential office base and so recent events have just accelerated that plan.

"We’ve invested significantly in recent times into new IT infrastructure, a case management system and management software to put us in a position for growth, with the aim of achieving a £7m turnover for our century year.

"This is another significant step forward for our business."

Berwins, which was founded in 1986, employed around 40 staff.
