## Football club suspends players amid investigation into racism allegations

*(Image: Supplied)*

A Yorkshire football club at the centre of racism allegations has suspended a number of players while an investigation into alleged discriminatory messages continues.

Kinsley Boys FC issued an update on Friday following the emergence of screenshots appearing to show racist, ableist and sexually explicit remarks exchanged in a WhatsApp group reportedly involving individuals connected to the club.

The allegations relate to a recent match against Route One Rovers and have prompted an internal investigation.

In its latest statement, Kinsley Boys FC said it was taking the allegations "extremely seriously" and confirmed several players had been suspended pending the outcome of enquiries.

The club said: "The club takes these allegations extremely seriously and can confirm that an internal investigation is currently underway.

"As part of this process, a number of players have been suspended pending the outcome of the investigation."

## **Fixture postponement request rejected**

Kinsley Boys FC also revealed it had requested the postponement of this weekend's fixture to allow more time for the investigation to progress.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21721756.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kinsley Boys FC statement *(Image: Kinsley Boys FC / Facebook)*

The club said it believed moving forward with the match while enquiries remained ongoing would be inappropriate given the concerns surrounding those involved.

It said: "The welfare and wellbeing of all players, officials and individuals affected by this situation remain a priority for the club."

However, the club's request was rejected.

Kinsley Boys FC added: "Whilst we are disappointed that our request for postponement was not granted, we respect the league's instruction."

## **Club reiterates stance on discrimination**

The latest update comes after Route One Rovers publicly condemned racism following the circulation of the alleged messages.

In a statement posted on social media, the club said: "Racism has absolutely no place in football. No excuses. No tolerance.

"Those responsible must be held accountable, and the relevant authorities must take firm action. Silence is not an option. Enough is enough."

Kinsley Boys FC said its management committee had no prior knowledge of the alleged messages before they began circulating online and stressed that it does not condone discrimination or abusive behaviour.

The club said it remains committed to ensuring all individuals involved are treated fairly and that appropriate action will be taken once the investigation has concluded.

It added: "We ask for patience and understanding while the investigation remains ongoing. It would be inappropriate to comment further on individual cases until the process has been completed."
