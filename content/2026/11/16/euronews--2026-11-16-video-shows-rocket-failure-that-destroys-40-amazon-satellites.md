A United Launch Alliance Vulcan rocket lifted off from Cape Canaveral, Florida, on Sunday, but the mission ended in failure after an anomaly occurred around 10 minutes into the flight.

The rocket failed to deliver its payload of 40 Amazon internet satellites into orbit, the company said. The satellites were part of Amazon's planned network to provide broadband internet services.

The launch was one of several dozen flights Amazon has purchased from ULA to deploy satellites for the project.
