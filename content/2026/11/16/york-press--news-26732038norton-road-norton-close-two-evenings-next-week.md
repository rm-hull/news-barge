## Main road in North Yorkshire town to close for two evenings next week

Norton Road in Norton, known as 'blackboards' is to shut to enable the installation of illuminated signs *(Image: Haydn Lewis)*

A MAIN road in a North Yorkshire town is to close for two evenings next week.

Norton Road in Norton, known as 'blackboards' is to shut to enable the installation of illuminated signs.

Cllr Keane Duncan, who represents Norton on North Yorkshire Council said the fixed illuminated signs are due to be installed to replace the unsightly concrete barrels.

He said: "This follows the installation of more robust ‘tamper proof’ bollards and signs to replace the concrete blocks. The bollards are easily detachable in case County Bridge is closed during flooding events.

"The road will be closed evenings 7-11pm on Monday, November 23 and Tuesday, November 24 to enable the works to happen.

"All of the bollards and signs are detachable in event two-way traffic is required."
