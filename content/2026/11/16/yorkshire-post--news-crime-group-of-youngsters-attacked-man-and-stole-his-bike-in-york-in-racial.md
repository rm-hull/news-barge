# Group of youngsters attacked man and stole his bike in York in racially aggravated incident

## Police are investigating a racially aggravated robbery in York.

The incident happened at approximately 6am on Friday November 13, 2026 at the junction of Cromer Street and Crombie Avenue, York, police said.

It involved a group of six to eight young people, who assaulted a man, subjected him to racial abuse and stole his bike.

The group consisted of one girl and the remaining boys, with three of them riding pedal cycles.

They are all believed to be approximately 14 to 15 years old.

The victim did not suffer injuries despite being struck to the back of the head with a glass bottle during the incident.

Police are now looking to speak to anyone who witnessed the robbery, knows those involved, or has CCTV, doorbell, dashcam or other footage that may have captured the incident or the group leaving the area.

If you can help the investigation, contact North Yorkshire Police and quote reference 12260223437.
