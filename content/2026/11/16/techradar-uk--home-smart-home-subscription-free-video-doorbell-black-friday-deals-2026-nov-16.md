Video doorbells always get deep discounts in the Black Friday sales, but the deals aren't always what they seem. Although the doorbells themselves might be cheap, they often come with subscription fees that are unavoidable if you actually want to store footage and check it later — and you can bet your bottom dollar those subscription plans won't be any cheaper during the sales.

That's why I've put together this list of video doorbells that our reviewers have given the TechRadar seal of approval, that *don't* carry additional fees for storage. Best of all, they are all going cheap right now at Amazon. With up to 67% off, why wait for Black Friday itself?

All of these devices have earned a place in our roundup of the best video doorbells, so you can be confident you're getting a device that will be easy to use, with crisp video footage and intuitive features to protect your home — and your Black Friday package deliveries.

Swann generously provides a 32GB card in the box, but if that's not enough, you can expand the storage to 128GB.

We're not quite sure about the AI assistant feature, which can generate dynamic responses when visitors call (with varying attitudes, including 'aggressive'), but there's no obligation to use that, and the Swann Buddy4K is a superb video doorbell without it.

Doro specializes in tech for senior users, but its Hemma doorbell is a great option for anyone who wants a fuss-free video doorbell, regardless of their age. It has a simple design, and its mobile app is refreshingly straightforward, putting the most important features front and center. Unlike some smart security apps, it won't have you hunting around for your video clips, and you don't need any prior experience of smart home devices. It also comes with a loud chime as well, which is ideal if you don't have your phone to hand.

Best of all, Doro doesn't have subscription plans at all, so you're getting everything you need in one modestly-priced package, and you're in full control of all your video clips.

The downside of the Hemma is that it's only available in the UK at present, but if you're in the US, Switchbot's 4K Video Doorbell is simple to use, and comes with a separate monitor that lets you see and speak with callers without touching your phone (and you can use the Switchbot app when you're on the move).
