## On Sunday, Prime Minister Kristen Michal wrote on X that the GRU had ordered a "sabotage attempt" at the Estonian defence company Threod Systems on Saturday night.

Estonia said on Sunday it would close its border with Russia after stopping what it called an attempted arson attack by Russia's GRU military intelligence on a drone factory.

The move marked a sharp escalation in tensions between Estonia and Russia, which Tallinn sees as part of a broader campaign of hostile activity linked to Moscow's war against Ukraine.

"Estonia will close all Estonian-Russian border crossing points as of 1 December," Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna wrote in a post on X, citing "clear evidence" that the GRU had ordered the attack.

Moscow said it would verify whether Estonia was indeed planning to shut all crossings.

"We will verify all this to see if it's true," Russian foreign ministry spokeswoman Maria Zakharova told the TASS news agency.

"But if these are their (Estonia's) real plans, then it will be seen as yet another bout of aggressive and unbridled Russophobia," she added.

Earlier on Sunday, Prime Minister Kristen Michal wrote on X that the GRU had ordered a "sabotage attempt" at the Estonian defence company Threod Systems on Saturday night.

"Extensive damage was averted thanks to heightened security measures and a swift reaction by law enforcement. The attackers have been detained," he added.

The foiled attack happened in Viimsi, north of the capital Tallinn.

Tsahkna, in his later post, said: "Russia must understand that hostile actions against Estonia come with consequences.

"Attempts to intimidate, destabilise, or undermine our security will not go unanswered."

He also said he had informed his Finnish, Latvian, and Lithuanian counterparts of the decision.

Security forces arrested eight individuals who live in Latvia, chief prosecutor Astrid Asi told journalists.

Michal called the incident "part of Russia's broader hybrid war against Europe, with the aim of decreasing support to Ukraine and pressure on Russia".

"We will not be intimidated," he added.

Threod Systems manufactures reconnaissance and attack drones.

In August, the defence subcontractor Milrem Robotics, based in Tallinn, was targeted in an attempted arson attack and Latvian nationals were arrested.

Estonia, a member of the European Union and NATO, currently has three crossing points with Russia at Narva in the north and the southern Luhamaa and Koidula crossing, that allow the movement of people and goods between the two countries.

Russia suspended vehicle traffic through Narva in February 2024, although pedestrians were still allowed to pass.

The crossings are among the few remaining overland links between Russia and the European Union still operating since Moscow's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.
