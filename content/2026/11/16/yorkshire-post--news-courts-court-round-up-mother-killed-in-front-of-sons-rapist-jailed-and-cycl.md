# Court round-up: Mother killed in front of sons, rapist jailed and cyclist attacked in Leeds

## A driver who killed a mother-of-two in front of her sons, a man jailed for raping a woman in Leeds and a robber who seriously injured an elderly cyclist are among the latest offenders sentenced in Yorkshire.

A number of serious criminal cases have recently been heard in courts across Yorkshire. Here is a round-up of four of the latest sentences.

## **Driver jailed after killing mother-of-two in front of her sons in Bradford**

A driver who killed a mother-of-two by reversing over her in front of her sons following a violent argument has been jailed for seven years and two months.

Kiel Sanderson, 40, was found guilty of causing the death of Donna Smith, 48, by dangerous driving following an incident outside her home in Queensbury, Bradford, in August 2025.

The court heard that Sanderson reversed over Ms Smith following an argument, fatally injuring her in front of horrified witnesses, including her two sons.

![Kiel Sanderson was jailed at Bradford Crown Court](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/12/17/19/KIEL-SANDERSON.jpeg?trim=147,0,113,0&width=640&quality=65)


Following the incident, Sanderson deliberately set fire to his vehicle in an attempt to destroy potentially important evidence.

He had previously admitted perverting the course of justice in connection with the fire.

At Bradford Crown Court on Wednesday (Nov 11), Sanderson was jailed for seven years and two months.

He was also disqualified from driving for eight years and seven months and ordered to pass an extended driving test before being allowed back on the roads.

In a statement, Ms Smith's family described her as a much-loved daughter and mother who was deeply missed.

They said: “We know that no sentence, will bring Donna back. She was a daughter and a mum to her two sons. She is missed, so very much. It has been particularly hard to come to terms with how Donna died.”

Detective Chief Inspector Stacey Atkinson, of West Yorkshire Police, described the incident as tragic and devastating, highlighting the impact on those who witnessed Ms Smith's death.

## **Leeds rapist jailed for nine years after woman left fearing for her life**

A man who raped a woman in Leeds, leaving her fearing she would be killed, has been jailed for nine years.

Uvesh Vahora, 36, of no fixed abode, was convicted of raping the woman at a property on Autumn Avenue in the Hyde Park area on April 9, 2026.

The victim was able to identify the property where the attack took place, allowing West Yorkshire Police to arrest Vahora around five hours after the offence was reported.

![Uvesh Vahora](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/11/16/11/uvesh_vahora.jpeg?trim=168,0,169,0&width=640&quality=65)


He remained in custody while detectives gathered evidence, including witness statements, CCTV and digital material.

Following a trial at Leeds Crown Court, Vahora was found guilty of one count of rape on Tuesday (Nov 3).

He returned to the court on Wednesday (Nov 11), where he was sentenced to nine years in prison and ordered to register as a sex offender indefinitely.

In a statement following sentencing, the victim described the lasting psychological impact of the attack and encouraged others who had experienced sexual violence to speak out.

She said: “I hope that by speaking out, I can show others who have experienced something similar that their voices matter, that they deserve to be heard and believed, and that seeking justice is worthwhile.”

Detective Inspector Tom Crosfill, of West Yorkshire Police, said the woman had endured a terrifying ordeal and had genuinely feared for her life.

He praised her courage in reporting the offence and supporting the investigation.

## **Man jailed after elderly cyclist pushed off bike and seriously injured in Leeds robbery**

A 20-year-old man has been jailed for six years and six months after an elderly cyclist was pushed from his bicycle and seriously injured during a violent attempted robbery in Leeds.

Alfie Smith, of no fixed abode, was sentenced for his involvement in the attack, alongside another robbery and two burglaries.

The incident happened shortly after 1pm on August 19, when Paul Laveurt, 68, and his friend John Hoban, 74, were cycling along a canal towpath near Thwaites Lane in Stourton.

![Alfie Smith](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/11/11/24/alfie_smith.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The pair were approached by two men, including Smith, before Mr Laveurt was pushed from his bike by Smith's accomplice.

When Mr Hoban stopped to help his injured friend, Smith told the other man to steal Mr Hoban's bicycle instead.

Mr Hoban was kicked and punched as he attempted to prevent the theft.

The offenders fled with his bicycle and saddle bag, which contained tools, a sat nav and his wallet.

Mr Laveurt suffered a broken kneecap and cracked ribs.

In a victim personal statement, he described how the attack had affected his independence and left him frightened to cycle again.

Smith was also sentenced in connection with another robbery on August 10, when a 53-year-old man was threatened and had his electric bicycle stolen on the same canal towpath.

He was further linked to two burglaries at a secure car park and bicycle storage area in Whitehall, Leeds, in February and March.

PC Mo Sheikh, of Leeds District Crime Team, said the offences had fundamentally changed the victims' feelings of safety while enjoying activities they previously loved.

Police said investigations into other people involved in the incidents were continuing.

## **Man jailed for targeting vulnerable homeless woman in Scarborough**

A man who targeted a vulnerable homeless woman fleeing domestic abuse has been jailed for three years and nine months.

Abubakarr Moiba, 44, of no fixed address, approached the woman as she sat in a doorway in Scarborough town centre in the early hours of June 17.

The court heard that Moiba made serious threats and attempted to steal her mobile phone.

![Abubakarr Moiba ,44, will be deported to Sierra Leone after his sentence.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/12/15/26/download-(11).png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He was also linked to a further alleged robbery involving the same woman on June 28, when her AirPods were taken.

Police searched Moiba's address and recovered the woman's white AirPods, a black-handled knife and items matching those seen on CCTV footage.

While being transported to Scarborough Police Station, Moiba told officers that the woman had given him the headphones because she owed him money, despite police not having told him what had been stolen.

The woman, who had moved to Scarborough seeking a fresh start after fleeing domestic abuse, described how the incidents had left her frightened and struggling to feel safe.

In a victim impact statement, she said she felt “like a sitting duck with nowhere to go for safety”.

Moiba admitted attempted robbery and was sentenced at York Crown Court on Tuesday (Nov 10).

He was jailed for three years and nine months and ordered to pay a £228 victim surcharge.

Police said he would be deported to Sierra Leone after serving his sentence.

Detective Constable Alfie Thomlinson, of Scarborough and Ryedale CID, praised the victim's courage in supporting the investigation and helping secure Moiba's conviction.
