Sara and Kelly Hirst, who run the Black Swan in Ripon, have won Craft Union’s community pub award for the second year running.

The pair retained the ‘Best Community Pub Operator’ title at the 2026 Craft Union Operator Awards, which recognise operators across the company’s UK pubs.

Sara and Kelly started a weekly walking group that featured on ITV’s *Lorraine*. They also founded Ripon Pride, which has grown into an annual festival supported by the local council.

The Black Swan was one of five finalists.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afb137ec243a03ef79.png)

Judges heard first-hand accounts from customers about the role the pub plays in their lives and praised Sara and Kelly’s “passion, dedication and genuine care”.

Sara and Kelly said:

> “We are so pleased to have won this award for the second year running. Community has always been at the heart of everything we do and we're incredibly proud that people choose to spend their time with us, whether that's supporting local events, attending Ripon Pride, joining our activities or just popping in for a chat.
>
> “This award really belongs to our customers and the wider Ripon community.
>
> "The support they've shown us over the years has helped make the Black Swan what it is today, and we're excited to continue finding new ways to bring people together.”

Frazer Grimbleby, managing director of Craft Union Pub Company, said:

> “They embody everything this award stands for.
>
> "Their dedication to bringing people together, creating meaningful connections and ensuring the Black Swan remains a welcoming pub for everyone makes them outstanding ambassadors for both their community and Craft Union.
>
> "I’m incredibly proud of everything they have achieved.”
