The USS Theodore Roosevelt has arrived in the Middle East, once again bringing the total number of U.S. aircraft carriers in the Middle East to three — an unusually high number.

A U.S. official, who spoke on condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive military operations, on Monday confirmed the carrier’s arrival in the region.

It brings thousands of additional troops to the Middle East as President Donald Trump has kept his options open during the more than eight-month-old Iran war.

The Roosevelt, along with its accompanying strike force, left San Diego in September.
