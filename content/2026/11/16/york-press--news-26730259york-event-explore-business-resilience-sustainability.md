## York event to explore how businesses can cope with floods and other natural disasters

York during flooding *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A new event in York next month will explore how businesses can boost resilience and sustainability.

The conference will bring together SMEs and business leaders from across York and North Yorkshire to examine practical ways to improve resource use, prepare for future challenges, and learn from international examples, including businesses in Japan.

The event, titled Think Global, Act Local, Be Meaningful, is organised by the Meaningful Business Network in collaboration with Dr Marco Sakai, an ecological economist from the University of York, along with the York Environmental Sustainability Institute (YESI), the University of Leeds, and the York and North Yorkshire Growth Hub.

Dr Adrian Madden, a specialist in Meaningful Business research, said: "Meaningful Business is about bringing people and organisations together to share ideas, experiences and learning that can help solve the challenges businesses face.

"Many of the issues we're dealing with are shared, so there's a real opportunity to learn from each another, find common ground and explore solutions through collaboration and cooperation. By making those connections, we can turn shared knowledge into practical action."

Dr. Sakai's research compares how businesses in the UK and Japan prepare for climate-related challenges and other disruptions.

Frequent natural disasters, including devastating earthquakes and tsunamis, mean Japanese organisations have to think carefully about protecting their people, operations and supply chains.

Dr Sakai, who leads the UK-Japan SME climate action research project, said: "There are practical lessons that businesses in the UK can learn from Japan, particularly about working together to prepare for challenges and share costs.

"But it's not a one-way exchange.

"Japanese businesses can also learn from what UK companies are doing around climate mitigation and energy efficiency.

"There is a real opportunity here to share ideas and learn from each other."

The research team will present its preliminary findings at the event, with Professor Kazuhiro Manabe from the University of Kitakyushu joining via Zoom to share insights from Japan.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21719644.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dr Marko Sakai of the University of York *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The event will also feature Reusefully, the lead partner behind ReYorkshire, a project funded by the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority’s Carbon Negative Challenge Fund.

ReYorkshire aims to promote the reuse of industrial, commercial, construction, and agricultural materials, supporting the region’s community reuse sector.

An interactive World Café session will allow attendees to discuss climate action, resilience, and circular economy ideas, including reuse and materials sharing, in small groups.

Katie Wytwyckyj, programme manager for the Meaningful Business Network, said: "You don't have to be an expert in sustainability to get something out of this event.

"It's about finding out what other organisations are doing, picking up ideas you could put into practice and making connections with people who might be able to help."

The event takes place on Tuesday 8 December 2026 at the Priory Street Centre in York, with registration from 9.30am.
