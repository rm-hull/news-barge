## The next FIFA presidential election is due to take place at the 77th FIFA Congress on Thursday, 18 March 2027 in Rabat, Morocco.

French football chief Philippe Diallo is set to run for the FIFA presidency as a surprise late candidate in next year's elections.

European governing body UEFA proposed Diallo on Monday following a meeting of its executive committee.

"Diallo has accepted the invitation to stand. His candidacy is due to be formally ratified by the FFF Executive Committee at its meeting tomorrow," UEFA said in a statement, adding that Diallo had "extensive experience across all levels of the game."

Diallo is lined up to run against Japanese official Kozo Tashima and incumbent President Gianni Infantino, who is seeking to shake off a major crisis that has dominated sporting headlines in recent months.

Infantino, who was first elected as president in 2016, came under huge pressure in the summer after FIFA announced a highly controversial private investment plan that would have seen third-party investors invited to take stakes in a commercial subsidiary dubbed the FIFA Forward Enterprise (FFE).

The proposal, which was later scrapped, drew fierce pushback from across the football community, with some accusing the world governing body of looking to "sell" the sport.

"Against the backdrop of serious concerns over the direction of FIFA, the national associations called for renewed unity and trust within the global football community," UEFA said on Monday.

"They expressed their full confidence in Philippe Diallo to provide the leadership needed to achieve this ambition," it added.

FIFA confirmed in September that Infantino, who has also faced questions over his relationship with US President Donald Trump, would stand for re-election.

"The FIFA President announced in April that he will stand for re-election in 2027. Of course, nothing has changed. Mr Infantino will be standing for re-election," a spokesperson said.

The presidential election is due to take place at the 77th FIFA Congress on Thursday, 18 March 2027 in Rabat, Morocco.
