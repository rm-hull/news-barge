A gunman has been found dead after two public safety officers were shot at a New York City apartment complex.

Police rushed to the massive Co-op City housing complex in the Bronx just before noon on Monday after two officers with the Co-op City Department of Public Safety, a private security force for the complex, were shot.

The peace officers, who are armed and patrol the community, called the New York Police Department for help while responding to a report of an emotionally disturbed person inside a building at the complex, according to local Fox affiliate WNYW.

![Police and other emergency vehicles parked in front of a Co-op City building in the Bronx, New York, Monday, Nov. 16, 2026](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/16/20/23/AP26320683584318.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

NYPD officers found the suspected gunman, a 56-year-old man, dead of a self-inflicted gunshot wound after he barricaded himself in an apartment.

The two wounded peace officers were taken to the hospital with injuries that were not believed to be life-threatening.

Co-op City was built as middle-income housing in the 1960s and ’70s. It has more than 15,300 cooperative apartments in high-rises and townhomes, 50,000 residents and its own ZIP code.
