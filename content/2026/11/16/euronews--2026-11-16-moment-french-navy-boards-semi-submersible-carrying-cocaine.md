The French Navy intercepted a stateless semi-submersible vessel carrying at least 146 kilograms of cocaine in the Atlantic Ocean on Thursday, November 12.

The operation marked the first time the French Navy had intercepted this type of vessel, according to the military.

A French frigate carried out the operation with support from its onboard helicopter and a US maritime patrol aircraft operating under the control of the Joint Interagency Task Force South.

French Navy personnel boarded the vessel and seized the cocaine during the operation.
