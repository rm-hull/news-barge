Police have been brought in after two fires in York last night.

Acomb crews were called to a hedge fire in **Clifton** at**5.40pm**. It measured 2m by 1m.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Crews extinguished the fire using a knapsack sprayer and buckets of water.

“The cause of the fire is believed deliberate as crews also investigated another fire reported in the area which had been extinguished by a member of the public.

“Police were also informed.”
