Young people should not be able to consent to anything until they are 18, Kemi Badenoch has said.

The Conservative leader said it “makes sense” to stagger the age of sexual consent amid calls for a change in the law in the wake of the Russell Brand verdict and abuse allegations against David Walliams.

But she believes a law change could go further and says the legal age of adulthood should be raised to 18 across the board.

She told ITV’s Good Morning Britain: “If it was up to me personally, this is not Conservative Party policy, I would just make the age of majority 18 for everything. There’s too much randomness in the system. Let childhood be childhood, everything at 18.”

Ms Badenoch did not make it clear whether her proposal would make it illegal for 16 and 17-year-olds to have consensual sex.

![Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch said she wanted the age of consent to be 18 for everything](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/16/4f7bd421a7f5bda1e13e25d7575d953fY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk0OTMxMzcw-2.86169932.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

She told the broadcaster that it was “absolutely disgusting” to have older men preying on 16-year-olds.

She recalled being disgusted as a teenager when nightclub owner Peter Stringfellow, then 55, was dating a 16-year-old girl.

“People said, ‘Well, it's legal, it's fine’. I thought it was disgusting; I was 16, I couldn't understand why anyone would want to be with a 55-year-old man.”

Under current UK law, adults of any age can legally have sex with 16 and 17-year-olds.

Ms Badenoch added: “However, just because [a staggered age of consent] makes sense doesn't mean that it's something that we will be able to do.”

She said there was already a failure to enforce current laws, including those in place to protect children from being targeted by adults on the internet.

“We have men talking to 15-year-olds, 14-year-olds. They're using the social media pathways, the screens. We're not protecting enough.

“I also know that we spend a lot of time in parliament making laws which we cannot enforce. Let's enforce the laws that we have.

“But I do think that older men and even older women, frankly, should stay away from 16 and 17-year-olds. Let them be children.”

![Russell Brand (left) and David Walliams (right) are both facing allegations of sexually assaulting teenagers](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/11/10/download..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Ms Badenoch also criticised the government for wanting to lower the age of voting to 16, while also considering changing the age of consent, saying it was “all a big mess”.

The government has said it is “considering” a staggered age of consent amid calls from campaigners who claim the current law “legally allows adults to have sex with children”.

Pat McFadden said the government would likely consider a change in approach to consent laws.

The Work and Pensions secretary told Sky News on Sunday: “I think the whole country last week has been appalled by the abuse of power by powerful men. And a big age gap can be part of that, can be a factor on that.

“So, I think what we’ve seen over the last week has certainly raised that question. There’ll be a number of issues to work through on it.

“I can’t give you a final answer on that today, but I do think that between Russell Brand, David Walliams, what’s been put out there, people are looking at these issues in a new light.”

Calls for a law change have intensified after a woman waived her right to anonymity and claimed she was raped by Russell Brand as a 16-year-old schoolgirl.

Mia Latter, now 37, told *The Sunday Times* that the government needed to introduce a staggered age of consent.

Separately, Walliams was accused of preying on teenage girls and sexually assaulting a 15-year-old. The Little Britain star has denied any wrongdoing.
