# Finance director at Hull and East Yorkshire Combined Authority to step down

## Recruitment is underway again at Hull and East Yorkshire Combined Authority (Heyca) after the Executive Director of Finance handed in his notice.

Julian Neilson was director of finance at East Riding Council for a decade before taking the role as interim director of finance at HEYCA in February 2025.

Mr Neilson was appointed in the permanent role of Executive Director of Finance in June.

A Heyca spokesperson said Mr Neilson had supported the authority through its early days and he’d decided to take up “a new opportunity” with another employer “which better suits his personal and family circumstances”.

![Luke Campbell. Photo credit: Jacob King/PA Wire](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjVmNjdhY2U0LTQ5MmItNGQzNi1hM2EwLWZjNTcyNWJkZTI2MTo0NTg0YjAyZi02ZjQyLTQwNzMtOTFiOC05MGZhYjFlZjllZDY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Mr Neilson has given notice that he will leave on January 3. During his notice period he will continue in his role.

The spokesperson added: “The authority is grateful for Julian's contribution during its formative period and wishes him well in his new role.” The closing date for candidates is November 29.

It comes ahead of interviews on Friday for a new chief executive on a salary of £150,000 to £170,000 to replace interim CEO Alan Menzies who is on a contract until December.

It is the second time HEYCA has advertised for a CEO after failing to find a suitable candidate earlier this year.

Heyca has been through a turbulent time, with Reform Mayor Luke Campbell under police investigation after allegedly showing a female member of staff images of himself posing nude for a gay magazine 14 years ago.

Hull’s MPs have called on Reform leader Nigel Farage to suspend the Mayor until the investigation is complete.

Mr Campbell firmly denies the allegations.

Chair of Heyca’s scrutiny committee Richard Meredith said: “There is no constitutional ability to remove the Mayor from his position unless he has not attended meetings or been found guilty of committing a felony.”

An executive board meeting chaired by Mr Campbell is due to be held on November 27.
