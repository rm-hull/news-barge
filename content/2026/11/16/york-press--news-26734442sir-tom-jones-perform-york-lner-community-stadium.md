A music legend is set to make history as the first artist to headline at a major York venue.

Sir Tom Jones will perform at York’s LNER Community Stadium on Friday, May 28, 2027, as part of his Come Gather Round World Tour.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21725262.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sir Tom Jones is set to become the first music artist to headline at York's LNER Community Stadium on May 28, 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

The Welsh icon, who continues to perform internationally at the age of 86 and has sold more than 100 million records worldwide, will be joined by special guests Level 42, Heather Small MBE, and Stone Foundation.

Tickets go on sale at 10am Friday via ticketmaster.co.uk.

Sir Tom's York set will include songs from his 2021 Number 1 album Surrounded By Time, as well as classics such as It’s Not Unusual, What’s New Pussycat?, Delilah, and Green, Green Grass of Home.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21725287.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Special guests Level 42 will be in York when LNER Community Stadium welcomes Sir Tom Jones. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*The concert follows**the announcement that York band Shed Seven will play the biggest headline show of their career at the same venue on Saturday, May 29**.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21725418.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Shed Seven at York's LNER Community Stadium where they will be playing in May 2027. *(Image: (Image: Tom Oxley))*

Julian Murray, promoter at Cuffe and Taylor, said: "Coming just weeks after we announced what is now a sell out show with Shed Seven at the LNER, we are delighted to be adding a second date here with the legendary Tom Jones.

"This will be a fabulous concert with an icon so what better way to kickstart live music at this incredible venue."

The show promises an evening of timeless hits delivered with the energy and charisma that have defined Sir Tom’s career.

Knighted by Queen Elizabeth II in 2006, Sir Tom has received numerous honours, including multiple BRIT Awards, a Silver Clef Award, the Music Industry Trusts Award, and a Hitmaker Award from the US Songwriters Hall of Fame.

He will be supported by a line-up of acclaimed artists.

Level 42, who began as a jazz-funk fusion band, rose to fame in the 1980s with a sound that evolved into sophisti-pop and dance-rock.

Their 1985 album World Machine went double platinum, and their UK Top 5 hit Lessons in Love was followed by the US-charting single Something About You.

The band’s subsequent albums Running in the Family (1987) and Staring at the Sun (1988) both reached Number 2 in the UK charts.

Level 42 continue to tour and perform at festivals across Europe and South America.

Heather Small, best known as the voice of M People, is also set to take the stage.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21725266.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Heather Small will be joining the line-up as a special guest when Sir Tom Jones plays York's LNER Community Stadium. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

She helped define the sound of 1990s British dance music with hits like Moving On Up, One Night in Heaven, and Search for the Hero.

M People won multiple BRIT Awards, and their 1994 album Elegant Slumming received the Mercury Music Prize.

Heather continues to perform as a solo artist, connecting with audiences across the UK and beyond.

Completing the line-up are Stone Foundation, an eight-piece British soul collective known for their uplifting live shows and exceptional musicianship.

![Stone Foundation will be performing at York's LNER Community Stadium as guests of Sir Tom Jones.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21725277.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Stone Foundation will complete the line-up at York's LNER Community Stadium as guests of Sir Tom Jones. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

They have achieved four consecutive UK Top 40 albums and are regarded as one of the UK’s finest modern soul acts.

Sir Tom’s appearance at the LNER Community Stadium will make him the first music artist to headline at the venue.

He has captivated audiences for more than six decades and remains one of the world’s most celebrated performers.
