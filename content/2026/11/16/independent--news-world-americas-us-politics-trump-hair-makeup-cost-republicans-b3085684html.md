A leadership battle at the Republican National Committee has put an unusual expense under the microscope - hair and makeup services for President Donald Trump and other GOP officials.

Some of the committee’s 168 members, who oversee the party’s national political operation, are calling for an audit of spending decisions, including payments for those services.

The push comes amid an effort by a small group of members to remove Chairman Joe Gruters, who was handpicked by Trump, and install co-chair KC Crosbie in his place, *The Associated Press* reported.

Crosbie entered the race after the midterm elections — which saw the GOP lose control of the House and Senate — launching her challenge with a letter that infuriated the White House. Her opponents view the bid as a direct challenge to Trump’s iron grip on the party.

In her November 5 letter, Crosbie called the midterms “a serious setback” and demanded “an honest account” from the RNC of what went wrong. The organization will elect its leader in January.

![Spending on President Donald Trump’s hair and make-up services has come under scrutiny amid a leadership battle at the Republican National Committee, according to a new report](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/13/47/GettyImages-2303034953.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The RNC’s spending has become a flashpoint for some of Crosbie’s supporters, who question how the party managed to outraise Democrats yet still lose decisively.

“There’s clearly a groundswell of support for a forensic audit,” Oscar Brock, an RNC member from Tennessee, told *The Associated Press*.

Gruters responded in a letter to members last week, acknowledging their concerns but defending spending on everything from hospitality to hair and makeup for administration officials.

The Florida Republican justified a $22,500 payment for “hospitality space” at a golf event and addressed questions about a “brief contract” with Caroline Wiles, whose mother, Susie Wiles, previously served as Trump’s chief of staff.

He also defended the RNC’s decision to cover “hair and make-up services” for Trump, Karoline Leavitt, JD Vance and other top officials.

“This was a request made of us so that the expense did not fall to taxpayers,” Gruters wrote. “We felt this was a request we could accommodate.”

![KC Crosbie entered the race to become the RNC’s leader after the midterm elections, launching her challenge with a letter that infuriated the White House](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/14/2194439328..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

According to *NOTUS*, the party spent $59,000 on “media preparation” for MAGA leaders during an eight-month period in 2025. While on* The Apprentice*, Trump spent upwards of $70,000 to style his hair, per* The New York Times*.

Gruters promised “honest and direct answers to concerns raised” over the coming weeks and months.

*The Independent* has contacted the White House for comment.

For the small group supporting Crosbie, replacing Gruters would signal that the party is prepared to change course after its bruising midterm defeats.

Patti Lyman, an RNC member from Virginia, said Republicans would find it both “catastrophic” and “insulting” “to keep the same leadership and pretend that we don’t need to do anything different, that we don’t need new players and a new plan.”

The challenge reportedly angered Trump and other administration officials, who were given no advance warning of Crosbie’s letter. A day later, Trump threatened to stop helping the RNC raise money if its members defied him.

Whoever prevails will help shape the 2028 race for the White House, in which Trump is expected to play a major role.
