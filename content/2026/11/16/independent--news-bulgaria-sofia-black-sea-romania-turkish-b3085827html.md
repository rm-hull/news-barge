A Somali-flagged cargo ship sank in Bulgaria’s exclusive economic zone in the Black Sea on Monday, the transport ministry said.

All 12 crew members on board — eight Turkish citizens and four Azerbaijani nationals — were saved in the rescue operation that involved four ships, two from Bulgaria, one from Romania and one from Turkey.

There was no official information on whether the incident was caused by a drone strike, but TV footage showed the arrival of the sailors in the port city of Varna, where they told journalists that their ship had been struck by sea drones.

The vessel’s crew sent a distress call at 9:15 a.m. that the ship was sinking approximately 40 nautical miles southeast of Cape Kaliakra. The BETUL 8 was sailing from the Romanian port of Tulcea to Egypt, carrying 3,000 tons of bulk wheat, according to the ministry.

There had been several deadly drone attacks against merchant ships off the coast of Bulgaria and Romania in recent weeks, widely blamed by analysts and political leaders on Russia’s war against Ukraine.

Last week, drones struck four additional vessels near the Romanian port of Sulina. Two of the ships caught fire, although no injuries were reported.
