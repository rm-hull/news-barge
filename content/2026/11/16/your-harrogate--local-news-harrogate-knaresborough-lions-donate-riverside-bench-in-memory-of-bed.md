Knaresborough Lions have donated a bench at Conyngham Hall in memory of Martin Berry, a former runner and organiser of the Great Knaresborough Bed Race.

The bench overlooks the River Nidd near the spot where teams climb out of the water after crossing the river during the annual race.

Martin ran in the Bed Race in the 1970s and 1980s. He joined Knaresborough Lions in 2010 and remained a member until his death in 2019.

Lisa Swiers, who obtained the bench from North Yorkshire Council, said:

> “We are delighted to remember Lion Martin in this way and it is a perfect donation for a man who was such a stalwart both of the Lions and of Bed Race itself.”

Martin’s wife, Lynn Berry, joined members of the Lions at the bench.

Knaresborough Lions is a volunteer-run charity that organises the Bed Race and the Knaresborough Beer Festival.

*Anyone interested in getting involved can contact the club through its**website**.*
