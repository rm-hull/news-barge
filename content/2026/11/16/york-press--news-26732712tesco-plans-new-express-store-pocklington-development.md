A national supermarket could be opening premises on a new site in an East Yorkshire town.

Tesco has submitted a planning application to the county council to install illuminated signs, vinyls and parking displays on a building within the site of a housing development in The Balk, Pocklington.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council identifies the site as owned by BDW Trading Limited, the company behind Barratt Homes and David Wilson Homes, who are actively developing the land off The Balk and associated infrastructure close to the A1079 road outside the town.

Homes are already occupied on the companies' Wolds View and Stewarts Reach plots, out of a total of 380 houses scheduled to be built on the site.

The entrance to the proposed Tesco Express store would be just off Herring Avenue at its junction with The Balk, the plans indicated.

18 car parking spaces are dedicated for the store, according to plans, including two disabled spaces, motorcycle stores and four spaces to charge electric vehicles.

A full-size Sainsbury's operates in The Balk, opposite an Aldi, with a smaller Co-Op convenience store in the middle of the town.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has set an expiry date of Wednesday, December 9 for consultation on the proposal.
