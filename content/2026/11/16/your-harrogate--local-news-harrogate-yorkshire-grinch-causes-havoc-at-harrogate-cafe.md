The Yorkshire Grinch got up to mischief at a sold-out festive brunch at Mama Doreen’s in Harrogate on Saturday (14th November).

Malton entertainer and presenter Ryan Swain donned the green fur to play the famously grumpy Christmas character, joking with families and posing for photos during brunch.

Ryan will return to Mama Doreen’s as the Yorkshire Grinch on Sunday 13th and Friday 18th December.

Families can contact the café directly to check availability and book.

After Saturday’s brunch, Ryan headed to Doncaster to meet visitors at the town’s Christmas lights celebrations that evening.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afab9987d34e6f00fd.png)

Ryan, who is originally from Malton, began his career entertaining families at Flamingo Land, where he went on to host the park’s Party in the Park concerts.

The Yorkshire Grinch is one of several characters he’s brought to life. He’s also entertained children with his puppet, Frank Lamingo.

*His next stop is the Festive Gift Fair at Birmingham’s NEC from 19 to 22 November. Other Yorkshire appearances include The Millhouse Bistro in Holmfirth on 5 December, Rio Music Bar in Hull on 12 December and Pictureville Cinema in Bradford on 19 December.*
