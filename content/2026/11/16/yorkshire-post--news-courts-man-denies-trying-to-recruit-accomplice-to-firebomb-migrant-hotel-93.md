# Man denies trying to recruit accomplice to firebomb migrant hotel

## An alleged white supremacist has denied trying to recruit an accomplice to join him in a plot to firebomb a migrant hotel.

Mikka Anderson, 24, is on trial at Newcastle Crown Court accused of planning an attack on the Royal Hotel in Hull and to kill as many occupants as possible.

From the witness box, the kitchen manufacturer told the court such a plan would be “horrific” and “evil”.

He denies preparing an act of terrorism, including manufacturing Molotov cocktails and by conducting hostile reconnaissance on the hotel.

He also denies possessing knives and other weapons for terrorist purposes.

![Mikka Lee Anderson, a white British national of Morpeth Street, was arrested on February 26 as part of a pre-planned intelligence-led operation by Counter Terrorism Policing (CTP) North East and Humberside Police](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmMyMTFkMDE0LWY4Y2EtNDcxNS1hZmY5LWQyOTM2MTAwZTQyMjo3YzZmZWI4NC1kMmUxLTRjYmItYmMxYS0yZGE2ZjU5OGJkZGM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Jurors have heard how in February, Anderson contacted a man who had been previously involved in disorder in Hull and how he allegedly tried to recruit the man, who cannot be named by the media for legal reasons.

After discussions on social media, the pair agreed to meet in Hull city centre and spent an hour in conversation, including walking up to the hotel, jurors have heard.

Giving evidence on Monday, Anderson denied it was his plan to attack the Royal Hotel and that he introduced the plot to the would-be accomplice.

Adam Birkby, prosecuting, asked him: “You wanted to recruit (the man) to help you with your project?”

Anderson replied: “Absolutely not.”

The court has heard how two days after the meeting, the alleged accomplice told police about their meeting and told jurors last week he thought terrorism was “disgusting”.

Anderson’s cross-examination by the Crown continues on Tuesday.
