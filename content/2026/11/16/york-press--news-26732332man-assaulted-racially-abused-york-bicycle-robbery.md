## Man hit over head with glass bottle during 'racially aggravated robbery' in York

Police say a man was assaulted and racially abused in York *(Image: Staff)*

A man was reportedly assaulted and racially abused during a robbery in York.

The incident happened at about 6am on Friday, November 13, at the junction of Cromer Street and Crombie Avenue.

North Yorkshire Police said a group of six to eight young people were allegedly involved, including one girl and several boys.

The group, who are believed to have been aged around 14 or 15, allegedly stole the victim’s bicycle.

A police spokesperson said: “Thankfully, the victim did not suffer any serious injuries, despite being struck to the back of the head with a glass bottle during the incident.

“We're appealing for information following a racially aggravated robbery in York.”

Officers are keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident or who may know the identities of those involved.

They are also appealing for CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage which may have captured the incident or the group leaving the area.

Anyone with information should contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference **12260223437**.

Information can be provided by emailing PC 1064 Lily Clennett at [lily.clennett@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:lily.clennett@northyorkshire.police.uk), calling **101**, or through the force’s website.

Anyone wishing to remain anonymous can contact Crimestoppers on **0800 555 111** or through its website.
