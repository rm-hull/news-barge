Britain's Prince William and Princess Catherine joined local players for a game of street cricket in Mumbai, India, on Monday, 16 November 2026.

The couple took turns playing "gully cricket", a popular informal version of the sport played in streets and open spaces across the country. William was seen batting before Catherine took her turn. The princess then greeted young cricketers, and the couple posed for photographs with the players.

The game was part of a three-day visit to India, where William is hosting the Earthshot Prize, his environmental initiative.

The trip also includes a planned meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi. Cricket, one of India's most popular sports, gave the royal couple a chance to join local players during their visit to Mumbai, the country's financial capital.
