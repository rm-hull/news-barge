# Shop worker’s brave fightback helped police track down masked robber through DNA

## A brave fightback from a shop worker thwarted a gang of masked robbers - after he attacked one of the intruders with a crowbar.

He brave fight back against the would-be robbers left a trail of blood, allowing police to match the DNA to Christopher Windle.

With one waiting on a motorbike outside, the other two entered the shop wearing balaclavas and wielding a baseball bat and a machete.

They smashed the glass cabinets to get at the jewellery on display, but in the melee, Windle was struck over the head with the crowbar.

The pair then fled empty handed, leaving the trail of blood.

Windle, of no fixed address, was later arrested after his DNA screening confirmed the blood belonged to him.

![Windle (inset) was part of a gang who tried to rob Cash spot, but was attacked in retaliation and which led to his arrest.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/13/12/48/Windle.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He appeared at Leeds Crown Court last week over a video link from HMP Leeds, having admitted attempted robbery, possession of offensive weapon and a charge of criminal damage from a separate incident in which he damaged the door of a woman’s property.

The 26-year-old has more than 60 previous offences to his name.

Mitigating, Nicholas Leadbeater said Windle regretted his involvement and that insight he “would have made a very different decision”.

The judge, Recorder Anthony Hawks said Windle “has a shocking record” and told him: “You were armed. You don’t need me to tell you it is a very serious offence.”
