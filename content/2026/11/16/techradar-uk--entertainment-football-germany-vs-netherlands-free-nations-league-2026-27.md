Find out how to watch Germany vs Netherlands for free from anywhere in the world as respective head coaches Jurgen Klopp and Xavi clash at Berlin's Olympiastadion on Matchday 6 of the Nations League, with the hosts needing a win to guarantee a quarter-final spot.

After an inauspicious start to life as German boss – a 1-1 draw with the Dutch, followed by 2-1 home defeat by Greece – Klopp's revolution has gained traction. A 3-1 win in Serbia on Friday, with Florian Wirtz again to the fore, means Die Mannschaft guarantee quarter-final progress with a win but will otherwise need a favor from pointless Serbia against Greece if they are to avoid the relegation playoffs. Brajan Gruda impressed last time out and should keep his place in support of center-forward Kai Havertz, with Serge Gnabry and Jamal Musiala out with muscle problems.

Still unbeaten since Xavi replaced Ronald Koeman after the World Cup, the Dutch are all-but assured of a spot in last eight regardless of the result. The Oranje 7-0 gubbing of Greece on Friday means only a defeat tonight and a 14-goal swing in the Greeks' favor would deny the Dutch a quarter-final spot. Frenkie De Jong's fitness concerns have resurfaced, though, while Virgil van Dijk is already missing the fixture. Donyell Malen, in electric form for Roma all year, will start up front and Tijjani Reijnders certain to continue after his hat-trick from midfield against Greece.

One of European football's biggest rivalries – who can forget Koeman's novel use of a German shirt for personal hygiene or Frank Rijkaard's projectile at Rudi Voller's perm? – this is one not to be missed.
