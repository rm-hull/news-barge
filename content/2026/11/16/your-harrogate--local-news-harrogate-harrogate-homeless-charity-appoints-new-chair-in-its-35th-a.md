Lynne Atkin has taken over as chair of Harrogate Homeless Project from David Thomas, who is stepping down after five years.

Lynne, who lives on Leeds Road in Harrogate, has a background in human resources and has served as a trustee at Action for Children and as vice-chair of the Women’s Business Council.

Lynne said:

> “It’s tough for charities right now with rising demand and rising costs. I have been impressed with how Harrogate Homeless Project makes every pound donated count in their mission to change people’s lives.
>
> “It’s great to be a part of the team particularly as we celebrate our 35th anniversary. I look forward to shaping the future of this local charity which reflects and supports the compassion that is the characteristic of our area.”

Harrogate Homeless Project began in 1991 in response to a rise in rough sleeping in the Pinewoods.

The charity started with an eight-bed hostel and now provides 43 bed spaces across Harrogate. Its day centre receives around 10,000 visits a year.

David said:

> “Leading an organisation that helps the most vulnerable in our community is a privilege and one that I am sad to leave behind.”

People can support Harrogate Homeless Project by making a donation, attending an event or volunteering.
