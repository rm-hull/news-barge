As contest winners were finalized on Monday for President Donald Trump's third crypto gala dinner, set for November 22 at his golf club just outside Washington, the president's meme coin traded near 92 cents, a steep fall from its 2025 high of over $74.

During the height of Trump's popularity, the token’s price surged over 60% after the first contest was announced in April 2025.

Wealthy digital asset investors wagered a collective $148 million on what the president promoted as the "most EXCLUSIVE INVITATION in the world."

Holdings required to secure a dinner invitation ranged from $59,000 to $18.5 million.

Today, interest in this third meme coin dinner competition has collapsed.

The event invited 185 owners of the token, known as $TRUMP, to register and see who maintained the largest balances during the contest scoring period from September 30 to November 16.

The leaderboard at closing displayed only 130 crypto wallets, holding a combined $8.6 million, representing a 94% drop compared to the first dinner.

The top 29 wallets will earn a "Special VIP Reception" with "YOUR FAVORITE PRESIDENT," according to the official contest website, alongside VIP seating during the dinner.

This will mark the third time Morten Christensen, a crypto entrepreneur from the Netherlands, has qualified for the event, but the first where he will attend the VIP reception with the president.

He placed 26th on the leaderboard holding roughly $41,000 in time-weighted Trump token holdings.

"You can be in a room with the president of the United States, arguably one of the most powerful people in the world," said Christensen. "So why would I not go if it cost me almost nothing to go?"

His 23-year-old sister, Gabrielle Felen, is also joining him. She purchased roughly 2,000 Trump tokens. She expressed disappointment after failing to rank within the top 120, meaning she will not receive the gift bag.

## Trump crypto ventures deflate to lows

The contest’s subdued results coincide with a broader downturn across the Trump family’s other crypto-connected equities and projects, which have also dropped to record lows.

Combined, outside investors across the five primary crypto and crypto-linked ventures have lost upwards of $5 billion, according to Reuters estimates.

The bulk of those losses took place prior to the midterm elections, in which Democrats dealt a heavy defeat to Trump’s Republican Party, an outcome that sent token values down further.

Because the price of the Trump token continued to decline sharply during the holding window leading up to the dinner, contest winners have lost millions of dollars over recent weeks.

Neither the Trump meme coin project nor the Trump Organization responded to requests for comment.

The White House also did not respond to a formal request for comment, though it has repeatedly said there is no conflict of interest because the president’s assets are held in a trust managed by his children.

## Trump family earns billions

While most token buyers have suffered heavy losses, the Trump meme coin has earned the Trump family at least $635 million to date, according to the president’s June financial disclosure statement.

In total, a Reuters analysis indicated that the Trumps have earned at least $2.3 billion through April across their four main crypto ventures, including the meme coin. The president's holding in a fifth crypto-linked venture, Trump Media & Technology Group DJT.O, carries a valuation of about $500 million.

The billions accumulated by the Trump family while in office have drawn fierce opposition from congressional Democrats, who regained control of both the US Senate and US House of Representatives in the midterm elections.

They will now possess subpoena authority and the power to launch investigations and conduct hearings into what US Senator Elizabeth Warren, a Massachusetts Democrat who will lead oversight on the Senate Banking Committee, has characterized as "an orgy of corruption."

Warren stated last Monday that the Trump family crypto businesses will serve as a focus of her accountability investigations.

Crypto wallets are anonymous, identified only by strings of letters and numbers. That anonymity prompted government ethics officials to express concern regarding the initial two dinners, stating the meme coin could act as a conduit for anonymous foreign individuals seeking to purchase access to the president.

Blockchain analysis revealed that of the 220 wallets competing in the first contest, more than half were likely located entirely outside the United States.
