AN INVESTIGATION has been launched after a racially aggravated 'incident' that took place in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a man was pictured in connection with racially aggravated criminal damage in the county.

A police spokesperson said: "The incident involved a man kicking open a locked door, causing significant damage. During the incident, racially abusive language was also shouted in a public place."




* **Firefighters called to fire on the edge of York village**
* **Man arrested after 'incident' in York city centre shop today**

Officers added that they believe the man shown in the CCTV image may have information that could assist their investigation.

If you can recognise him or have any helpful information, please contact PC Sam Bradley at [sam.bradley@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:sam.bradley@northyorkshire.police.uk), through the police website, or by calling 101.

If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260184218 when providing information.
