## All 12 crew members working on board the vessel were rescued and were being flown by helicopter to Bulgaria's Chaika air base, according to authorities.

A cargo ship capsized off the coast of Bulgaria in the Black Sea on Monday following a suspected drone strike.

Maritime authorities received a distress call from the Somali-flagged Betul 8 ship in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone at around 9:15 am, reporting that the vessel was sinking, according to Bulgaria's transport ministry.

Four vessels from the Black Sea Mine Countermeasures Group were deployed to the scene of the incident, the ministry said. Two Cougar helicopters and an Su-25 jet were also mobilised to carry out surveillance.

"An aerial survey of the area has established that the vessel has capsized. A naval vessel has been dispatched to the scene of the incident to check for any marine pollution," the transport ministry said in a later update.

Video footage circulating on social media appears to show the vessel as it goes down.

The Betul 8 was travelling from Tulcea in Romania to Egypt and was carrying almost 3,000 tonnes of bulk wheat, the ministry added.

All 12 crew members working on board the vessel were rescued and were being flown by helicopter to the Chaika air base, according to authorities. They were said to be in "good overall condition" but were suffering from the cold.

The crew reportedly told journalists that their ship had been struck by sea drones.

If confirmed, it would mark the latest in a wave of drone attacks on commercial vessels in the Black Sea in recent months. Russia is suspected of being behind the strikes.

The European Union has pledged €50 million for a Black Sea Maritime Security Hub hosted by Bulgaria and Romania to help combat security threats in the region.
