For the sixth consecutive time, the IIHF Women's World Championship comes down to a showdown between arch-rivals USA and Canada. Although the side north of the border has historically dominated the rivalry, their neighbours are firmly in the ascendency, and have now won nine of their past 10 meetings. Canadaian stopper Ann-Renee Desbiens, however, has been the tournament's standout goaltender.

Eight of the 10 top scorers at the 2026 IIHF Women's World Championship are in the USA roster, with reigning IIHF Player of the Year and PWHL No.1 overall pick Caroline Harvey leading the way with six goals an nine assists. Only Hilary Knight has more goals (seven) and Taylor Fox has more assists (12). Canada's Jessie Eldridge sneaks in at No.9 with three goals and five assists.

Team Canada's progress through the tournament hasn't always been easy, Finland and Czechia having pushed them close, but like USA they're yet to be beaten in Denmark. That's some achievement, given they're right at the beginning of a rebuild under new coach Kori Cheverie, icons Marie-Philip Poulin and Jocelyne Larocque having likely departed the international stage for good, and with Blayre Turnbull out of action.

They did, however, get the better of the 2026 Winter Olympics gold medallists in the first of their pre-tournament tune-ups, Sarah Fillier and Laura Stacey scoring the winning goals in a feisty 5-3 encounter. USA's Kirsten Simms, Tessa Janecke, Hannah Bilka and Joy Dunne, however, then scored without reply in a statement 4-0 victory two days later.

While Canada have been peerless in the power play, USA's scoring efficiency is second to none.
