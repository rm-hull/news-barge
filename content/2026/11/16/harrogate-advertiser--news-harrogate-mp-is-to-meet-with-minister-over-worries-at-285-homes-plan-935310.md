Harrogate’s MP is to meet with a Government minister after raising concerns over plans for 285 homes next to the Army Foundation College.

Tom Gordon’s worries come as Redrow Homes Yorkshire seeks outline planning permission for the new development on farmland west of Oaker Bank, running between Penny Pot Lane and Beckwithshaw.

Located next to the Army Foundation College (AFC), the MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough is what is being done to protect the site in an era when many people believe that the risks and threats to the UK are greater now than at any time since the Cold War.

General anxiety has grown after a series of security threats in recent months at UK military bases used by US and British including at RAF Lakenheath, RAF Mildenhall and RAF Fairford

Speaking in the House of Commons, Mr Gordon pointed out that Luke Pollard, the Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry had already spoken about concerns over the protection of defence sites and the threats they face.

“The Army Foundation College is a relatively isolated site at the moment and that matters for its security,” said Lib Dem MP Tom Gordon.

“Any plan to build hundreds of homes right next to it needs to be thought through very carefully.

“These latest plans come on top of an additional 1,000 homes proposed for nearby.

“There are sites that are far more suitable than this one, which would limit the expansion of the AFC and pose security concerns.”

The Harrogate MP asked what steps the Ministry of Defence is taking with other government departments and North Yorkshire Council on the security and protection of places like the AFC.

In reply, the Minister said Harrogate played an important role for the armed forces and offered to set up a meeting between Mr Gordon and the Minister for Veterans and People to discuss Harrogate.

He added that communities next to bases across the country care passionately about their security and actively contribute to it.

Tom Gordon said: “The Minister himself recognised that local communities care passionately about the security of these bases and Harrogate is no different.

“The armed forces are a crucial part of our community here.

“I welcome the offer of a meeting, and I will be following this process every step of the way.”
