# Popular Leeds United fan pub's 'boss lady' celebrates major award

## Leeds United supporters absolutely love the pub in the heart of the city

A Leeds United fan pub’s ‘boss lady’ has been honoured with a major award.

The Leeds pub figure head, who has helped train more than 70 Craft Union pub operators from across the UK, has been recognised with a national award.

Claire, operator of the Moot Hall Arms in Leeds city centre, has been named ‘best centre of excellence operator’ at the 2026 Craft Union Operator Awards. The annual accolades celebrate outstanding operators from Craft Union pubs across the UK.

The best centre of excellence operator award recognises individuals who go above-and-beyond to train, mentor and support new pub operators. Claire was recognised not only for running one of Craft Unions most successful community pubs, but also for the lasting impact she has had on dozens of operators throughout her years with the business.

Over the years, Claire has welcomed more than 70 new Craft Union pub operators through the doors of the Moot Hall Arms, helping equip them with the skills, knowledge and confidence needed to succeed. Her support extends far beyond initial training, with many continuing to turn to her for advice, guidance and encouragement long after they have taken on their own pubs.

Known affectionately by customers as the ‘boss lady’, Claire has built a reputation throughout the business as someone who is always willing to help others. The Moot Hall Arms is one of Leeds city centre's best-known pubs - particularly among Leeds United supporters. Following a six-figure investment earlier this year, the venue now boasts expanded facilities including a dedicated Leeds United-themed events space, while continuing to host live sport, entertainment, fundraising initiatives and community events throughout the week.

Claire said: “I'm incredibly proud to receive this award. Training and supporting new operators is something I've always been passionate about because I know first-hand how important it is to have people around you who are willing to share their experience and knowledge.

“Seeing operators grow in confidence, develop successful businesses and put their own stamp on their pubs is hugely rewarding. I've been fortunate to work with some fantastic people over the years, and this award is really a reflection of all the operators I've had the privilege to support.”

Frazer Grimbleby, Managing Director of Craft Union Pub Company, said: “Claire is one of those rare people whose impact extends far beyond the four walls of her own pub. Over many years she has trained and mentored more than 70 Craft Union operators, helping countless people build successful pubs and careers within our business. The support she gives doesn't end when the training finishes. She is always there to offer advice, guidance and encouragement whenever it's needed.

“Claire embodies everything we stand for as a business. Her passion, generosity and commitment to helping others succeed have made a huge difference to operators across the UK, and this award couldn't be more deserved.”
