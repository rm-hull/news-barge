Match funding of up to £100,000 has been offered to a charity that has helped transform the lives of nearly 200 homeless people in Harrogate.

Lifeline Harrogate, started by husband and wife Carl Good and Georgie in 2013, provides support and housing and has now been offered a boost in a pre Christmas promise.

Carl Good, Lifeline’s CEO, said: “Like many charities each year we have a target for fundraising to meet our income needs.

“We have known the donor since we even formed the charity and they have said a primary motivation for them is for us to increase the number of our regular donors.

“This then increases our financial stability going forward.”

Whether a one-off donation or regular monthly gift, any donations received before Christmas will be matched pound for pound, until the £100,000 matching fund has been used.

Carl added: “The opportunity to double £100,000 is amazing and would offer so much potential for further improving the support we offer.

“Any regular or single donations get doubled, so for example a £1,000 donation becomes £2,000.”

Financial adviser Carl and bank worker Georgie first felt a calling to help the homeless after encountering a homeless man on the streets in Harrogate at Christmas in 2011.

With support from a national social enterprise Green Pastures, they were able to start buying houses so that homeless people had a move-on option from hostels, rehabs or prisons.

Since opening their first home in 2013, the couple has housed 180 residents, provided nearly 60,000 nights of accommodation and currently have eight three and four-bedroom houses in the town.

Now, with the nights getting colder and, in some cases, people approaching Lifeline while sleeping in cars or tents, they can offer 25 local people a warm welcoming ‘home’ in the community.

Carl added: “It has been truly amazing to see so many lives being turned around and we have seen so many people leaving Lifeline into full-time work, with a family and their own independent property.

“The basics of life which so many of us take for granted are beyond the type of person caught up in today’s homeless crisis.

“Lifeline is able to support them to navigate their way out of the net they find themselves caught up in.

“Many residents have said to us that we have literally saved their lives.

“While the work is always challenging, it is also wonderfully rewarding to see how people when given this type of opportunity grasp it and make for themselves a happy and fulfilling life”.

Carl added: “We often hear of Harrogate being voted the happiest place to live, but when you are in debt, you do not have a roof over your head, and a relationship has broken down, you need someone to give you a hand up.

“Lifeline has a tried and tested model of offering that hand of support when it’s most needed.”

A resident recently told Lifeline: “if it wasn’t for Lifeline I don’t know where I’d be. Big thanks for Lifeline and staff for everything I have today”.
