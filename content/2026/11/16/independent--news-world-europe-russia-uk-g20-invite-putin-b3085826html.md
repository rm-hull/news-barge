Russia has warned Britain it would face “serious consequences” if it attempts to curtail Moscow’s rights when London assumes the G20 presidency for 2027.

Moscow issued the warning on Monday, less than two weeks after the *Economist* cited UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham as saying that Vladimir Putin would not travel to the summit in Manchester next November due to sanctions against him related to Ukraine.

“We shall see how London actually behaves,” Marat Berdyev, the Russian Foreign Ministry's Ambassador-at-Large for G20 and APEC affairs, told local news.

“One thing is clear: any infringement of Russia’s rights will not go unnoticed. Any missteps in this regard will have serious consequences.”

![The UK will host the G20 summit next year](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/08/2026-11-15T220014Z_645483176_RC20HOAF50SA_RTRMADP_3_BRITAIN-EU-BURNHAM.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Berdye said he hoped a negative scenario could be avoided. He was also cited as saying that the country which holds the rotating G20 presidency does not actually have the right to dictate terms or decide who participates in the forum.

Russia has remained a member of the G20 despite Western efforts to isolate the country following its full-scale invasion of Ukraine.

Leaders of host nations traditionally invite all members of the bloc to attend its annual summit, which was founded back in 1999 following financial crises.

It aims to foster policy cooperation on key issues affecting the global economy. Unlike older forums such as the G7, the G20 does not only include advanced economies.

![Russia has been invited to this year’s G20 summit in Miami](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/15/16/2026-11-13T050210Z_1579645043_RC2SMOAZFXVV_RTRMADP_3_UKRAINE-CRISIS-TALKS-PUTIN.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The G20 consists of 19 individual countries, including Russia, the US, and the UK, as well as the European Union and the African Union.

The US, the current G20 president, has invited Putin to the G20 summit it will host in Miami in December.

“Under President Trump’s leadership, the United States is returning the G20 to its core mission: driving economic growth, unleashing innovation, and strengthening partnerships that benefit American workers, businesses, and allies,” the G20 Miami 2026 website states.

“This presidency is about practical solutions, real outcomes, and American leadership that delivers prosperity, security, and strength at home and around the globe.”

The Kremlin has not yet confirmed whether Putin will attend the event or whether he will send someone else in his place.
