Boroughbridge Community Charity and Nidderdale Plus will each get £75,000 from North Yorkshire Council's community health grants, starting in April 2027.

Two community charities in the Harrogate district will each receive **£75,000**
from North Yorkshire Council to help people live healthily and independently in
their communities. **Boroughbridge Community Charity** and**Nidderdale Plus**, based in
Pateley Bridge, are among 12 organisations across the county given the money
without having to compete for it.

The decision was published on 12 November. It was taken on 10 November by the council’s Corporate Director of Health and Adult Services (decision record).

## What the money is for

The grants come from the council’s **Staying Healthy, Independent and Connected**
programme, known as SHIC. The council approved a new version of it in September,
running for three years from **1 April 2027**, with the option of two more
(decision record, 10 September).

The council describes SHIC as a “crucial, trusted, community-based prevention service”. It said the new version will give local groups “crucial, multi-year funding to enable organisations to plan strategically for the long term”.

Each of the 12 organisations will get £75,000 “over the 3+2 year funding period”, the decision says. That is subject to a written grant agreement.

The council said each one was the only eligible organisation in its area and was already “well embedded” in its community. Running a contest would have wasted time for the council and the groups, it said.

## Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon

No organisation in Harrogate, Knaresborough or Ripon is on the list. The council
said the remaining SHIC areas will go through a **competitive grant application
process**, but its decision does not say which areas those are or when that
process opens.

## What the two local charities do

Nidderdale Plus works from Station Square on King Street, Pateley Bridge. It says it supports local voluntary groups and helps residents reach local services. Its aims are to promote wellbeing, foster independence and build a sense of belonging (Nidderdale Plus).

Boroughbridge Community Charity was also among 19 groups given Local Fund grants in September, for digital help sessions on the NHS App, laptops and smartphones.

## What it means for you

* **Nothing changes yet.** The new grants start in April 2027.
* **The decision can still be called in** by councillors. It takes effect on 20 November unless it is.
* **If you run a community group in Harrogate, Knaresborough or Ripon,** watch the council’s grants pages for the competitive round, which has not yet opened.
