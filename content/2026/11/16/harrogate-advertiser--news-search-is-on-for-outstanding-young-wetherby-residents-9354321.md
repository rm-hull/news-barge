Wetherby is searching for young superstars who are making a difference to the community.

The nominations deadline to the Jubilee Award is fast approaching and the town’s mayor, Coun Connor Mulhall, said he is looking forward to presenting the winner.

“The Jubilee Award recognises the hard work and dedication of a standout young person in Wetherby.

“Throughout my 18 months as Mayor, I have often said that Wetherby is not a great place by chance; it is the people who make it such a fantastic place to live and work.

“A key part of that is celebrating the achievements of our young people, who often go under the radar while making a real difference in their communities alongside their school work.”

Established in 2012 to mark the Diamond Jubilee and spearheaded by former Mayor Coun Alan Lamb, the award has been presented to 17 young people.

In 2025, Farrah Jaruga received the award in recognition of her outstanding fundraising efforts for Maggie's cancer charity.

Her work included - organising and helping to deliver a successful fashion show; baking and selling homemade cakes, creating and selling handmade jewellery.

Through her efforts, Farrah raised vital funds for cancer support services while inspiring community involvement and generosity.

Coun Mulhall added: “This is one of the highlights of my Christmas engagements.

“Each year, I look forward to reading the outstanding submissions and recognising the remarkable contributions made by young people across our town.”

To enter a young superstar, complete the nomination form on the Wetherby Town Council website before 5pm on Tuesday 1 December.

Nominees must be 19 years old or under and have made a significant contribution to the community.

Entries will be judged by a panel of community representatives and the winner will be announced in a ceremony at Wetherby Town Hall on Saturday 5 December.
