DUP leader Gavin Robinson has said there is “heartbreak” across his party after the daughter and wife of party chairman Lord Morrow died within hours of each other.

In a statement, Mr Robinson said Maurice Morrow’s daughter, Rachel Burrows, and wife, Lady Jennifer Morrow died on Sunday.

It is understood both women had been ill.

He said: “On behalf of the Democratic Unionist Party, I extend our deepest sympathy to Maurice, to Rachel’s husband John, and to the entire Morrow and Burrows families at this most difficult of times.

“Maurice Morrow has been a rock of strength and support to so many of us through difficult moments in our lives.

“Now, in his darkest hour, it is our turn to be a rock for him.

“Throughout Jennifer and Rachel’s long battles, Maurice has demonstrated extraordinary love and strength.

“I know that his deep Christian faith has sustained him through those difficult days, but I also know that today his heart is breaking as a husband, a father and a grandfather.”

Mr Robinson said there are “no words that can ease the pain of such an unimaginable loss”.

He added: “But Maurice and his family should know that they will be supported by the prayers of their many friends and colleagues across Northern Ireland.”

First Minister Michelle O’Neill described the situation as a tragedy.

She said: “I can’t imagine what they’re going through.

“On behalf of our party, to offer our sympathies to that family at this horrendous time for them.”

In a social media post, DUP deputy First Minister Emma Little-Pengelly said: “Our hearts are absolutely breaking for Maurice, her surviving daughter Victoria and the whole family circle.

“It is hard to fathom the depth of grief of the family and friends of this double tragedy and loss.”

TUV leader Jim Allister passed on sympathies from his party.

He said: “When death reaches so deeply into a family, it rightly provokes within us all, no matter what our political alignment, the deepest sense of sympathy.

“And of course, it is a reminder to us all of the mortality of us all.

“So, to the Morrow family, and indeed to the DUP, we wish to express sincere Christian condolences.”

Lord Morrow is a former Stormont minister and a former assembly member for the Fermanagh and South Tyrone constituency.
