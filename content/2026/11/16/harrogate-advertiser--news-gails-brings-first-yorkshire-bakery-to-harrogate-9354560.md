*The Harrogate Advertiser* was among the first guests invited to take a sneak peek inside GAIL’s as the bakery held a preview launch ahead of opening its doors to the public on Tuesday (November 16).

Upmarket coffee shop brand GAIL’s may once have been associated with Hampstead and the south, but recent years have seen it spread across England as far north as Manchester, leaving countless fans of its cinammon buns and sourdough loaves in its wake.

Its arrival this week at 7 James Street, one of Harrogate’s premium town centre retail addresses, represents the bakery and cafe’s first venture into Yorkshire.

The GAIL’s team was out in force to welcome its first visitors, from the counter staff in their blue aprons and bakery managers Leigh, Claudio and Sergio to members of the company’s national team.

“Its great to be in Harrogate as the first GAIL’s in Yorkshire,” said Richard Exton, the bakery’s Head of Neighbourhood Marketing, who hails from Sheffield.

“We know the town has a healthy coffee and bakery scene here already, which is great.

“Although our business now has more then 175 branches, we were only a small working bakery and neighbourhood cafe for years and we still strive to do what the very first GAIL’s did.”

First launched in 2005 in Hampstead in London, GAIL’s is renowned for being early champions of sourdough bread and acting as a wider community hub.

The craft bakery serves freshly baked sourdough, pastries, cakes, sandwiches, hand-prepared salads and breakfast pots alongside barista-made speciality coffee and iced drinks.

Since opening its first bakery more than 20 years ago, GAIL’s has built its reputation on sourdough bread, long fermentation and freshly prepared food.

However, its expansion has not been without criticism, with the chain facing questions over its pricing and the nature of its food offering

A comparison of prices suggests GAIL’s is broadly in line with other upmarket cafés in the town, while its food will now be put to the test by those visiting.

The bakery’s arrival adds another name to an increasingly competitive café scene, with its reception among residents now to be seen.

GAIL's Bakery Harrogate will open seven days a week all year-round, except for Christmas Day.

Doors open daily at 6.30am and close at 6pm, approximately.
