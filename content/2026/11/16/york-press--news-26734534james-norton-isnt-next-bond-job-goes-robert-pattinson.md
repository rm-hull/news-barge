## Fans react after James Norton loses out on 007 role

FANS of James Norton have reacted to the news that he is not going to play the next 007

Norton, who was brought up near Malton, had been in the running to play James Bond but it was announced this afternoon (Monday) that the role has gone to Robert Pattinson.

Members of the James Norton Official Fans reacted to the news on social media.

One said: "Should have been James Norton he's gorgeous."

Another said: "He’s absolutely gorgeous, but far too talented to play such a one dimensional character in my opinion

Other comments included: "James would have been the best always said it. Congratulations to him, though I think James Norton would have been ideal".

Norton, who attended Ampleforth College and Amotherby Primary School, is currently starring as Father Christmas in the Marks & Spencer Christmas advert.

His previous roles include Happy Valley, Grantchester, War & Peace, and McMafia.

Robert Pattinson played vampire Edward Cullen in the Twilight series, alongside American actress Kristen Stewart.

Before that, he was Hufflepuff's Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire.

His more recent films have included Dune: Part Three, to The Batman.

In a statement, Pattinson says: "James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime.

"I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement.

"I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team."
