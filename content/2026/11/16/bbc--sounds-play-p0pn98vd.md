Today, we discuss more details about Alan Milburn’s review into young people and work, which will be published on Tuesday. The report will call for a gradual lowering of national insurance paid by firms on employing under-25s. Adam is joined by Chris Mason and business editor Simon Jack to unpack what impact these proposed changes might have. Also, the UK’s relationship with the EU is being discussed again ahead of a UK-EU summit this week.
