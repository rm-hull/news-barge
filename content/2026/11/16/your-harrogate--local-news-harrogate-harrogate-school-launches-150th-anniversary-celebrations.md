Hundreds of pupils at Ashville College in Harrogate have formed a giant '150' to launch a year of celebrations.

Pupils gathered in the school grounds for an aerial photograph, with Ashville’s clock tower as the backdrop.

Each pupil also received a commemorative pin badge funded by the New Friends of Ashville parents’ group.

Ashville will mark its 150th anniversary in July 2027. It opened in 1877 as a boarding school for the sons of Methodist ministers, giving boys a place to continue their education when their families moved.

Its first headmaster, Dr William Richardson, opened the school with 30 boys and two schoolmasters.

Former pupils include Oscar-winning director Tony Richardson, actor Jim Carter OBE, known for playing Mr Carson in *Downton Abbey*, and former Wimbledon chief executive Sally Bolton OBE.

Rhiannon Wilkinson, Ashville’s 11th head and the first woman to hold the role, said:

> “As we look back on 150 years of rich history and educational excellence, we are reminded of the incredible legacy we carry forward.
>
> “While the world, technology, and classrooms have changed dramatically since our doors first opened, our core values remain entirely unchanged.”

A book tracing the school’s history is in production, and Ripon Cathedral will host a special anniversary service next summer.

The programme will also include performances and opportunities for former pupils, known as Old Ashvillians, to reconnect.
