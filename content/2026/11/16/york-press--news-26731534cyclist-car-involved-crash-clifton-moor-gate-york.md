Emergency services have been called to a crash in York.

North Yorkshire Police have been seen in Clifton Moor Gate at 8.40am on Monday (November 16).




Motorists have been warned of heavy traffic after reports of a crash between a cyclist and a car.

The Press has contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Service for further comment on the incident.

An AA statement said: "The road is partially blocked and busy but traffic is moving due to accident on Clifton Moor Gate. Both ways between Water Lane and Kettlestring Lane."
