People have got their skates on in their droves and headed to the North’s biggest outdoor ice rink since its gala opening.

The opening few days of Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland have been “wonderful”, according to organisers.

As ***YorkMix*** reported last week, the attraction at Designer Outlet York, which also comes with Santa’s Grotto, a funfair, and an all-new Chalet café bar, launched with great pizazz on Thursday.

Now these new pictures have been released, showing how popular the Wonderland is again, in this its 21st year.

Jennifer Rojko, event organiser of Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland said: “We’ve had a wonderful opening weekend, with perfect ice and perfect skating conditions under blue skies and sunshine.

“And we’ve received such enthusiastic comments about the dazzling backdrop to the rink, and our new look alpine Chalet with its cosy fireplace and windows.

#### Booking information

Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland is located at the South Entrance of York Designer Outlet.

Tickets for a 45-minute skating session are priced at £16.45 for adults and £15.45 for children and concessions.

Family packages are available for families of three, four or five. Tickets for parents and toddlers aged 4 and under are priced at £16.45.

Special packages are also available for parties, schools, clubs and youth groups, with discounts applying to groups of 20 or more.

Autism/quiet skating sessions are on Tuesdays at 12.15am until 8 December, and Sundays 10.15am until 13 December, in line with the Designer Outlet’s Autism Friendly Hours.

For those wishing to make the most of the season, a Frequent Skater Pass enables skaters to collect a stamp from the box office over five sessions and enjoy the sixth visit for free.

“Our first birthday party booking of the season really enjoyed the new setting. The live music nights are bringing a real buzz to evenings in the Chalet, along with our new range of cocktails and bubbles.

“The marshmallow toasting has been a particular hit, with adults loving the nostalgic experience, and the kids excited at being allowed to toast their own marshmallow over a real fire!”

Proving popular over the weekend were a new range of cocktails as well as champagne, prosecco and seasonal favourites such as mulled wine and mulled cider.

In front of the fireplace, a performance stage hosted dynamic vocalist Beki Ann singing a variety of popular songs from Motown to rock and jazz, as well as Keira Upton who brought warmth and emotion to timeless classics and feel good favourites.

A variety of further music acts are scheduled across the seven-week season, tying in with theme nights such as Fizz and Fondue, Acoustic and Aperol, and Charcuterie and Cocktails. Private parties can also be arranged, for skaters and non-skaters alike.

Outside, the grill ‘Fed From Fire’ did a brisk trade over the opening weekend with sizzling bratwurst, Yorkshire pudding wraps, loaded roast potatoes and hot chocolate.

Individual wooden picnic cabins decorated with festive touches make the ideal spot to enjoy a bite and take in the snow-sprinkled Enchanted Woodland with its illuminated gingerbread men.
