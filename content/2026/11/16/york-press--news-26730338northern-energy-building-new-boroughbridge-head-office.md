## Northern Energy building new 10,000 sq ft HQ in North Yorkshire

How the HQ interior will look *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A new HQ is taking shape in North Yorkshire.

Northern Energy, an independent, family-run business based in Hampsthwaite, near Harrogate, has operated for more than 90 years and supplies LPG to homes and businesses.

The company opened a new storage and distribution depot in Boroughbridge last year and is now building a 10,000 sq ft head office on the site, with around 60 of its 120 employees set to move in when it opens in April 2027.

James Illingworth, from Northern Energy, said: "This development marks a new chapter for us, giving us a large LPG storage and distribution facility next to the A1.

"It represents a big step towards a more sustainable future by reducing our carbon footprint and operational emissions, whilst maintaining the high level of service our customers enjoy."

The depot features three bulk LPG storage tanks with a combined capacity of 360,000 litres, one-third of which is designed for future use with renewable liquid gases.

Mr Illingworth said the development would help futureproof the business.

He said: "In addition to boosting our storage capacity, it will futureproof our operations with the infrastructure needed to blend renewable liquid gases into our fuel mix as customer demand continues to grow.

"Our new offices will give us a modern HQ designed to support our expanding team.

"It will be a place where people aspire to work, where everyone can be productive and fulfil their potential, as well as reinforcing our position at the forefront of rural energy supply in the UK."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21719787.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A Northern Energy lorry *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A number of North Yorkshire firms have been appointed to deliver the project.

York-based Ben Johnson Interiors will deliver the interior fit-out, in a scheme designed by Harrogate’s Studio 93.

Ousebank, also based in York, is acting as employer’s agent, quantity surveyor and project manager.

Emma Dodsley, director at Ben Johnson Interiors, said: "The collaborative approach between client, designer and contractor was key to securing the project.

"We’re now transforming a concrete shell into an office that reflects Northern Energy’s culture and future goals, creating an office that promotes wellbeing and business growth."

Jake Harris, senior interior designer at Studio 93, said the HQ will feature a "welcoming entrance," meeting rooms, and a mix of quiet workspaces and breakout areas.

He said: "There is also a dedicated wellbeing room, changing rooms and stunning breakout spaces that make the most of views across the surrounding countryside."

Max Colley from Ousebank said the building has been designed to offer flexibility and quality for the team.

He said: "It is a superb project to be working on, alongside a very experienced team, that have prioritised local manufacturers and craftspeople wherever possible."
