# Russell Brand’s wife shares message from disgraced comedian in new prison

## Russell Brand’s wife has revealed where the disgraced comedian is spending his sentence for rape - after reports he would be incarcerated in a Yorkshire prison.

Brand, 51, was jailed last week after being found guilty of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others during his career.

Reports last week had claimed Brand was to be transferred from HMP Wandsworth in London to HMP Wakefield, West Yorkshire.

But on Sunday, his partner Laura Gallacher revealed she had spoken to Brand from his current location after he served the first week of his sentence.

Instead of being housed at the high-security Category A HMP Wakefield, Brand is starting his prison term at Category C HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire.

![Russell Brand was jailed last week.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/15/19/cs-8a8e826f-fcef-4c4e-9665-fc7989c2dd05.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


One of Brand’s accusers said today that his move to a lower security prison is a ‘slap in the face’ for those who ‘worked so hard to get that conviction’.

Earlier this year Brand and Gallacher began releasing a weekly YouTube podcast called Sunday Service, which they carried on recording throughout his trial.

On Sunday, Gallacher released another episode which began with her reading a passage from Brand’s bible.

The 39-year-old, who is the daughter of Ryder Cup winning golfer Bernard Gallacher, said during the episode: “Russell has asked me to read something to you.”

![HMP Littlehey where Russell Brand spent his first week in prison.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/16/11/00/PA-1364645.jpeg?trim=0,32,0,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


“I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey, little as in small, and hey as in hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders’ prison in Europe.

“The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind, and they are dedicated.

“And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.

“I don’t know which other men here are not guilty, and the ones that are, they are people that Christ died to save, like you and me, let us all love, pray for, and forgive one another as God forgave us.”

![Reports last week had claimed Brand would be incarcerated at HMP Wakefield.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/27/09/v2-image-production-1210ae00c43c6247d5db1c64edb2d5c1Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzYyNDcwOTc3-2.1911325.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Gallacher choked up as she said: “Please support my treasured wife and family. I feel your prayers, your brother in Christ. Russell.”

One of Brand’s accusers, Mia Latter, said moving the rapist comedian to a Category C facility ‘probably feels like a bit of an insult’ to the women who ‘have just gone through a horrific ordeal in court’.

HMP Littlehey, which is designated a Category C prison as inmates are deemed less likely to escape, has indoor bowls, yoga, origami classes, and landline phones which prisoners can use until 11pm to ring family and friends.

Latter told LBC: “I understand that the point of prison as well is to rehabilitate people, and they should have the facilities to help them.

“I don't think they should be afforded extra luxuries like extra phone calls and extra lists of people they can call, and it's disappointing. And I feel like it's probably a slap in the face to those women that have just gone through a horrific ordeal in court and fought so hard to get that conviction.

“I think that probably feels like a bit of an insult to them. But he still has that lengthy sentence ahead of him. So, no matter how many landline phone calls he gets, he's still in prison for a considerable time.”

Brand was sentenced to a 10-and-a-half-year prison term on Monday afternoon.

He is expected to serve two-thirds of his prison sentence before being released on licence.

He will be on the sex offenders’ register for life.
