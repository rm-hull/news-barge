# Leeds households urged to get sleeping bags and '3 days of food'

## British Gas has issued a list of 10 things households should do to prepare for heavy snow expected this week

Significant snowfall is forecast to sweep across the UK this week, with a yellow weather warning issued for blizzard-like conditions in several regions this Tuesday, with the prospect of further snow to come in November.

A yellow Met Office warning for heavy snow is in effect this Tuesday from midnight until 12pm, predominantly affecting the north of England, north-west of England and the Midlands, reports The Express. Areas including Leeds, Manchester, Sheffield, Stoke, Newcastle, Carlisle and Preston are all in the firing line, along with parts of Scotland.

A separate yellow weather warning for icy conditions across Scotland remains in place until 10am today. In its 12-hour yellow snow warning, the Met Office stated: "Snow may cause disruption on high ground during Tuesday morning".

Energy provider British Gas has compiled a ten-point checklist to help households prepare for severe winter weather, which includes stocking up on three days' worth of provisions and purchasing sleeping bags.

### The checklist features the following advice:

* - Boost insulation throughout your home, including sealing any draughts around windows and doors.
* - Safeguard your pipework by fitting insulating tubing around any exposed pipes connected to your boiler, heating system or water supply.
* - Store additional logs for your log burner inside the property in case outdoor access becomes impossible, and ensure electric heaters are kept well away from furniture.

The energy giant also recommended that households inspect their smoke alarms and carbon monoxide detectors, particularly for those relying on indoor heating such as a log burner. Homeowners were further advised to locate their stopcock in the event they need to shut off the water supply should a pipe burst.

British Gas also encouraged households to make contingency plans should their heating fail, stating: "While it's unlikely that a heavy snowfall will leave you without any heating, it's always worth keeping sleeping bags, blankets and warm coats handy."

The supplier additionally recommended putting together an emergency kit, noting: "It's a good habit to have at least three days' worth of food and essentials stored in an easy-to-reach part of your home.

"That's things like medicines, drinking water, non-perishable food and snacks, and extra blankets. You should also keep a torch, batteries and a phone charger close by too."

For vehicle owners, the company recommended inspecting several essential components, stating: "Have your brakes, heater, tyres and windscreen wipers checked to make sure they're in good working order before winter."

Finally, British Gas encouraged residents to look out for those around them, warning: "When severe weather hits, you might find yourself stranded away from home. So plan how you'll stay in contact with the rest of your household and where you'll meet if you can't get home due to heavy snow.

"Check in on elderly or vulnerable relatives and neighbours to make sure they've got everything they need to get through a cold snap."
