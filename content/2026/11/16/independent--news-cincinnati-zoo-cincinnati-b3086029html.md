The Cincinnati Zoo says its beloved hippo Fiona, who became a global celebrity after her premature birth nearly a decade ago, is undergoing veterinary care after swallowing an object tossed into her enclosure.

Zoo veterinarians and staff are monitoring Fiona and working to help her pass the object, which was accidentally thrown into her habitat by a zoo visitor, the zoo said in a statement Monday. It did not immediately identify the object.

“Her care team is cautiously optimistic that she will pass it and return to her usual self,” the zoo said.

Fiona was born six weeks premature in January 2017, and her fight to survive grabbed international attention.

She initially couldn't stand on her own and weighed just 29 pounds (13.2 kilograms) at birth, 25 pounds (11.3 kilograms) less than the lowest recorded birth weight for her species.

Fans followed her progress online, and she became so popular that books were written about her. She also inspired a line of beer, all sorts of clothing and an ice cream flavor.

One couple even got engaged in front of her exhibit a few years ago, with Fiona getting into the picture while underwater in the background.
