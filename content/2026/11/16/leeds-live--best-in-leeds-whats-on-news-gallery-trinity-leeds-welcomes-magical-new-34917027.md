# Trinity Leeds welcomes magical new opening at shopping centre

## Take a look at these amazing new photos of the Trinity Leeds opening

Take a look at a magical new Trinity Leeds opening.

As the Christmas season takes shape, something rather special has opened in Leeds city centre. Trinity Leeds has seen the launch of a new restaurant and bar.

The Christmas House pop-up will run through until New Year’s Day. The restaurant and bar takes inspiration from New York’s famous Christmas venues.

You’ll find twinkling lights, decorated trees, red velvet booths and cosy candlelit tables. During the day, The Christmas House offers a festive stop for families and shoppers, with gingerbread making, letters to Santa and magical photo opportunities.

Visitors can enjoy lunch, coffee, cakes and seasonal drinks - while there’s a handy present-wrapping service also available. In the evening, the new venue transforms into a lively festive destination, serving seasonal dishes and Christmas cocktails alongside live entertainment.
