Moulin Rouge dancers performed the French cancan alongside Republican Guard musicians in Paris, France, on Sunday, 15 November 2026, to mark the launch of the Christmas lights on Faubourg Saint-Honoré.

In front of the Madeleine church, the dancers kicked their legs to the sound of brass instruments as spectators watched, filmed and applauded. The show ended with the trees along the street lighting up for Christmas.

Organised by the local merchants' association, the event aimed to bring festive cheer to the capital and improve its image following recent protests.

The association's chair said he wanted to show a more positive side of Paris, while a Moulin Rouge dancer described the occasion as special and welcomed the chance to bring some joy ahead of the festive season.
