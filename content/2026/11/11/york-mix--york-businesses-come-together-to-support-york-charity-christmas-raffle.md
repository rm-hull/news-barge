A York charity is raising vital funds through a Christmas raffle – supported by York businesses

Kyra Women’s Project aims to support women in York who are experiencing challenging circumstances, including domestic abuse, poor mental health, isolation and multiple disadvantages.

This Christmas, the charity are working to raise funds to help ensure women and children can access vital support, community activities and opportunities throughout the year ahead.

Their Advent Raffle features a wide range of prizes generously donated by local businesses across York, with all proceeds going directly towards supporting Kyra’s work.

“We have been blown away by the support from the local business community and are hoping to reach as many people as possible before the draw,” said trustee Ashley Watson.

“We have had additional prizes donated since drafting our poster and we are now up to 35 prizes from local York businesses.”

Prizes include a one month pass to York Sports Village, a gin tasting experience from York Gin, an afternoon tea for Impossible York, a sauna experience at Pool Bridge Farm, a family pass to Piglets Adventure Farm, and much more.

Every ticket purchased helps raise vital funds for the life-changing support Kyra provides to women across York, while giving you the chance to win an incredible selection of prizes worth over £2,000.

Tickets are £2 per ticket, and can be purchased up to and including 24 December, when the final prize draw takes place. The earlier you enter, the more chances you have to win as every ticket purchased is automatically entered into all remaining daily draws from the date of purchase.
