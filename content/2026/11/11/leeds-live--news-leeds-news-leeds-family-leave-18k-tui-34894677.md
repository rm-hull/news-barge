# Leeds family leave £1.8k TUI holiday early after horror hotel leaves kids in tears

## A Leeds family were left in tears after their £1,800 TUI all-inclusive holiday in Sousse, Tunisia turned into a nightmare when they discovered their hotel was 'unfit for humans'

A couple from Leeds were forced to spend £3,200 after cutting short their stay in a TUI partner hotel they had paid £1,800 for upon deciding it was "unfit for humans", with conditions so dire that their two daughters were left in tears. Clinical resourcing partner Vikki Ashton, from Leeds, had travelled to Sousse, Tunisia with her husband and their two children Brooke, 19, and Esme, 12 in July this year for the all-inclusive 14 day holiday.

However, when they arrived, the family would be shocked to find the appalling conditions at the hotel, which looked completely different to how it was advertised online. One glaring example was that the blue hotel pool they had seen in photos was in fact green. More was to come when they went to their room, where they discovered lights hanging off the walls and a consistently flushing toilet.

Vikki, 49 recalled: "While we were walking to our room [after checking in] we saw that the pool was green. It looked filthy. On the website the pool is blue. People were in it. It was in full use. We got in the room and saw it was pretty basic. The side lights were hanging off on wires, not even attached to the wall."

She added: "We went in the bathroom and the toilet was filthy and it wouldn't stop flushing. The noise was awful. The bathroom door handle just came off. It was falling apart. It wasn't fit for human habitation. The public toilets that run from the all-inclusive bar to the toilets were covered in dirt. It was filthy. We were horrified."

The family were so alarmed by the state of the room that Vikki feared she, her husband, and their children would "end up getting poorly". This was after their daughters said they had discovered ants "all over the room and all over the bed" which led Vikki to raise concerns with TUI, who, she claims, declined to move the family because it was peak holiday season.

Not settling for this answer, the family forked out £3,200 to move to a non-TUI affiliated hotel. Vikki recalled: "We told reception we need moving. We rang TUI again the next morning and they told us they couldn't move us because it was height of the season.

"But they could've moved us because we could see right now there were several hotels in the area with availability today. We said give us the money back for this hotel and we'll move ourselves. They said they wouldn't do that. We tried to work with TUI to get this resolved but they were not very forthcoming."

Vikki added: "It was either pay for another hotel ourselves or go home. The girls were in tears. The next hotel was fine and we stayed there for the remaining 13 nights. There was no way on earth we would've stayed at the first hotel. You could hear people in the hotel saying 'get me out this place'."

What's more, Vikki was surprised by TUI's response, especially as they only initially offered the family £105 in compensation, an offer the family turned down. A disappointed Vikki stated they hadn't been expecting luxury, merely "the basics".

After Vikki's case was brought to their attention, TUI subsequently claimed to have reviewed the matter, including her concerns regarding the property. TUI also claimed that a member of their team had been in contact with Vikki, and have since offered to fully reimburse the cost of the alternative accommodation the family arranged themselves. In addition, they confirmed that the original venue, which was the subject of the complaints, has been withdrawn from sale.

A TUI spokesperson said: "We're very sorry to hear about Ms Ashton's experience. We want everyone who travels with us to be able to do so with confidence and to receive the standard of holiday they expect. We have since resolved the matter directly with the customer and, following the concerns raised, taken the decision to remove the property from sale, meaning it is no longer available to book through TUI."
