# 'Bubbly, clever' Leeds woman sadly dies at just 25 in supported living accommodation

## The woman's mother described her as 'bubbly, clever and talented'

A "bubbly, clever" Leeds woman sadly died at just 25 years old. Pariss Georgia Sibson grew up in the village of Scholes, and was described as a “popular Grade A student” by her mother, in an inquest into her death at Wakefield Coroner’s Office on Wednesday, November 11.

When Pariss was a young girl, while studying at Boston Spa School, her mother Josie McGowan added she was “bubbly, clever and talented” and loved playing musical instruments, especially the keyboard, in a written statement submitted to Acting Senior Coroner Oliver Longstaff.

Tragically, Pariss struggled with mental health issues, drug abuse and an eating disorder which severely affected her life. At the time of her death, Pariss had been living in supported living accommodation Cliffe House in Yeadon. The late 25-year-old had a history of suicidal attempts, self-harm, overdosing on drugs and being hospitalised, it was heard in court.

Pariss was found at 9am deceased in bed on Saturday, August 2, 2025. The inquest into her death continues tomorrow, on Thursday, November 12.
