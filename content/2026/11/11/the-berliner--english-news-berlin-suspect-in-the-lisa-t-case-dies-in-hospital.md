Thirteen-year-old Lisa, a Berlin local, had been missing since Tuesday morning last week. According to investigators, she left her home in Berlin-Marzahn around 7:15 a.m. to go to school. She never arrived.

On Sunday, the body of the schoolgirl and the seriously injured suspect were found in an empty house in a district of Bad Freienwalde. The man received intensive care treatment, but has since died in hospital.

An autopsy was performed on the victim. However, “no clear cause of death” has been determined. Further investigations are now underway. A homicide unit of the Berlin State Criminal Police Office (LKA) is investigating.

The suspect was arrested on Sunday in a house in Altglietzen (Märkisch-Oderland), where Lisa’s body was found. He attempted suicide when police arrived at the building in this district of Bad Freienwalde. According to the German Press Agency (DPA), he is from Berlin and lived in the Marzahn district, near the girl’s apartment building.

The 22-year-old was already under investigation by Berlin investigators. The Berlin public prosecutor’s office announced on Monday that he had been under investigation since January 2025 on suspicion of acquiring and possessing child pornography.

It was revealed on Tuesday that the 22-year-old suspect has a prior criminal record. With the suspect’s death, the investigation is likely to be closed according to legal regulations. Therefore, there will probably be no criminal proceedings.
