# Bright Friday 2026 Set to Light Up Knaresborough

Knaresborough's much-loved **Bright Friday** celebration returns on**Friday 27 November 2026**, bringing an evening of festive cheer, community spirit and the official start of the Christmas season in the town.

Residents and visitors are invited to join family and friends for a magical evening of entertainment, creativity and celebration in the heart of Knaresborough.

A highlight of the event will be the popular **Lantern Workshop and Parade**, giving participants the opportunity to create their own lantern before joining a colourful procession from the Castle Grounds through the town centre. The parade will set off from the War Memorial in the Castle Grounds at** 6.00pm**, creating a spectacular trail of light as it makes its way towards the Market Place.

The festivities continue with the **Christmas Lights Switch-On**, when the Market Place will be transformed as Knaresborough's Christmas lights illuminate the town centre. Visitors are encouraged to support local businesses, enjoy the festive atmosphere and celebrate the community spirit that makes Knaresborough such a special place.

Entertainment on the **Knaresborough Town Council Stage** will begin at**5.15pm** in the Market Place, providing a lively soundtrack to the evening's celebrations.

### Event Details

**Date:** Friday 27 November 2026**Location:** Knaresborough Town Centre and Market Place**Entertainment Starts:** 5.15pm in the Market Place from the KTC Stage**Lantern Parade:** 6.00pm from the War Memorial, Castle Grounds**Christmas Lights Switch-On:** Following the parade in the Market Place

### Road Closures

Please note that road closures will be in place in and around the Market Place from **12 noon on Friday 27 November 2026** to facilitate the event set up and keep everyone safe.

Visitors are advised to plan their journeys accordingly.

We look forward to welcoming everyone for an evening of light, joy and community as Knaresborough comes together to celebrate the beginning of the festive season. Don't miss it!
