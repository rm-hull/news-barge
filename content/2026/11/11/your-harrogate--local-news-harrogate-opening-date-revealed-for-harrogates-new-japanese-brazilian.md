NIPO will open on Harrogate’s Parliament Street on Thursday 3rd December.

The team behind RIO Brazilian Steakhouse is investing almost £500,000 in the restaurant, which will create more than 30 jobs.

Invited guests will attend a launch on Wednesday 2 December, the day before it opens to the public.

NIPO takes its name from Nipo-Brasileiro, meaning Japanese-Brazilian. The team first introduced the restaurant in Newcastle.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6af467b6cfc8c161f52.png)

The Harrogate menu will feature freshly prepared sushi alongside steaks cooked over an open flame.

Cuts will include Wagyu, fillet and ribeye, as well as a côte de bœuf dry-aged in-house and served for sharing.

Rodrigo Grassi Duarte, co-founder of NIPO, said:

> “Harrogate is an exciting next step for us, and we're looking forward to introducing the concept to a new audience, whether they're joining us for lunch, a special evening out or a memorable celebration.”

A Christmas menu will be available in December, with dishes including a duck roll with cranberry hoisin and orange-miso glaze, miso-glazed black cod and a yuzu pistachio panna cotta.

The restaurant will also have a glass-enclosed private dining room and bar upstairs.
