# Family's heartbreak as 'friendly and happy' woman killed by car fleeing police in Leeds is named

## Květoslava Horvathova, 36, was on foot when she was hit in Harehills

Tributes have been paid to Květoslava Horvathova, 36, killed by a car being chased by police in Leeds.

Her family described her as a "very friendly, wise, helpful, understanding, and happy person" after detectives named her for the first time today.

In a statement released through West Yorkshire Police, they said: "Květoslava was a very friendly, wise, helpful, understanding, and happy person. Our entire family will never forget her."

Ms Horvathova was on foot when she was hit by a black Vauxhall Corsa on Beckett Street in Harehills at about 9.52am on Thursday, October 29.

She was rushed to hospital with serious injuries but sadly died.

Minutes before the crash, officers had spotted the Corsa when it failed to stop for them on Harehills Road. A pursuit was authorised, and the car then hit Ms Horvathova and failed to stop at the scene. Officers gave her emergency medical attention at the scene.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct is independently investigating the circumstances of the pursuit.

Last week, Harry Gyamsie-Perah, 19, of Coldcotes Garth, Leeds, was charged with causing death by dangerous driving, using a motor vehicle on a road without third party insurance, failing to stop after a road accident and driving a motor vehicle otherwise than in accordance with a licence.

He appeared at Leeds Magistrates' Court and was remanded in custody. He is due to appear at Leeds Crown Court on November 27.

At least two people are understood to have been in the car at the time of the crash. Detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team are urging anyone who was in the vehicle to "do the right thing” and come forward to help with their enquiries.

They are also still appealing for anyone who saw the crash, or who has relevant dashcam footage, to get in touch.

Information can be passed to the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team via Live Chat online or by calling 101, quoting crime reference 13260615046.
