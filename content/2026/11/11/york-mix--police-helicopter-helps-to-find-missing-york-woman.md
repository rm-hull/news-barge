A search for a missing York woman came to a successful end with the help of the police helicopter last night (Tuesday).

Police had issued an appeal to find the woman, who was missing from her home in Strensall.

They issued this update at 12.16am: “With support from the police helicopter, she was found by officers just before midnight.

“Thank you to everyone who shared the appeal and contacted us with information.”
