Amateur boxers are taking part in makeshift boxing matches in public parks across Bogotá, Colombia, competing for cash prizes in events organised by Santiago Abril.

On November 8, more than 20 participants gathered at a portable boxing ring in a park in the south of the capital for a tournament featuring both men's and women's bouts. Fighters competed in weight categories over two 90-second rounds as spectators watched from around the ring.

The events have attracted police attention, with officers questioning Abril about the permits required to organise sporting activities in public spaces. Bogotá's municipal authorities say such events require official authorisation and an emergency response plan.

Abril has continued organising the tournaments, promoted on social media under the name King Abril. Participants pay an entry fee and can win prizes of around $100 after securing two or three consecutive victories.
