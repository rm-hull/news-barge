Three people are dead and three others are in critical condition after a vehicle crashed into people at a busy intersection in North Hollywood on Tuesday, according to the Los Angeles Fire Department.

A white SUV hit at least five people next to a metro station just before 6 p.m., the Los Angeles Police Department reported. At least three people were taken to the hospital, including the driver, according to officials.

Photo and video posted online show a white vehicle on a sidewalk next to tents for vendors, along with emergency crews. Posts show a crew moved a person away on a stretcher.
