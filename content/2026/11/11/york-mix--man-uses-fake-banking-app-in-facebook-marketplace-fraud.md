Police investigating a fraud in North Yorkshire want to talk to this man.

The incident happened in Scarborough last month.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “He has attended an address to make a purchase of an item advertised on Facebook Marketplace and has used a fake banking app making the victim believe the item had been paid for by bank transfer.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/fraud-scarborough-october-2026-2.webp)

“The vehicle the male attended in was also found to have been on false plates.”

If you can help to identify the man in the images, you can get in touch with police on the numbers below.
