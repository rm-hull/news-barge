Two men brandished knives during a street fight in York.

It happened at **10.25pm on Sunday**(8 November) on** Tang Hall Lane**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “During the altercation, it is believed that a large knife was taken from one of the men and thrown into the street.

#### Tang Hall Lane, York

“It is understood that a second knife was then allegedly used by one of the men to threaten the other. A large knife was recovered from the scene.

“As part of our investigation, we are releasing CCTV images of two people we would like to speak to, as they may have information that could assist our enquiries.

“We appreciate the images are not of the clearest quality but hope someone may recognise them from their clothing or the bicycle shown.”

Police to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident, or anyone with dashcam, doorbell or CCTV footage that may have captured something relevant, or has any other information that could help.
