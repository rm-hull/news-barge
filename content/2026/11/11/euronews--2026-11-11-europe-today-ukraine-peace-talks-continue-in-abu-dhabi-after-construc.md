## Ukraine, Russia and the US begin a second day of peace talks in Abu Dhabi after a first round Kyiv described as constructive, with a prisoner swap and security guarantees on the table. We also look at Black Sea attacks, Europe's hybrid threats and Prince Harry's visit to the European Parliament.

## On today's show:

**Toby Gregory** reports on the second day of Ukraine peace talks in Abu Dhabi.**Sasha Vakulina** breaks down what Kyiv and Moscow have said, whether positions are moving closer, and what role European are playing.**John Stringer**, NATO's Deputy Supreme Allied Commander, tells** Shona Murray**whether he sees a chance of a genuine breakthrough. NATO is represented in Abu Dhabi by General** Alexus Grynkewich**.** Angela Skujins**, Euronews defence correspondent, explains how Brussels plans to use €70 million to support the Black Sea, where ships are being struck and seafarers are missing.** Riho Terras**, Estonian MEP from the European People's Party, asks whether Europe is doing enough to defend itself against hybrid threats.** Vincenzo Genovese**reports on** Prince Harry**'s address at the European Parliament,** Meghan**'s visit and the "Lost Screen Memorial," which features photographs of young people whose deaths have been linked to online harms.
