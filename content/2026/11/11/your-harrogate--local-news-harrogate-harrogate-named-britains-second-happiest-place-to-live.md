Harrogate has been named the second happiest place to live in Britain in Rightmove’s annual survey.

Only the London borough of Richmond upon Thames ranked higher in the property website’s 15th annual Happy at Home Index. Cirencester in Gloucestershire came third.

Homes in Richmond upon Thames have an average asking price of around £1 million.

Monmouth ranked highest in Wales, while Kirkcaldy took the top spot in Scotland.

Colleen Babcock, of Rightmove, said:

> “Even among people who would ideally choose a rural setting, access to amenities, parks and public transport remains important.
>
> “This year's results suggest that the places people rate most highly aren’t simply urban or rural, but often bring together some of the best elements of both.”

Skipton in North Yorkshire ranked seventh.

Ian Harris, president of Propertymark, said:

> “A home that meets your needs, within a community where you feel connected and have access to the things that matter to you, can be worth far more than an impressive postcode."

Around 81% of people who took part in the survey said they were happy with where they live.

People took part in the research after being emailed via Rightmove's database.
