Iranian women took part in a military training course organised by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) at a volunteer camp in Tehran on Tuesday, November 10.

The training, held at Azadi Square and named “Janfada”, meaning “Sacrifice for the Homeland”, included handling firearms, aiming with sniper rifles, rappelling down ropes and using a zipline.

Footage showed women lining up with weapons and taking part in various military-style exercises during the course.
