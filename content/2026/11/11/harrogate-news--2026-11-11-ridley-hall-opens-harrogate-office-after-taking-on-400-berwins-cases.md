Yorkshire law firm Ridley & Hall Solicitors has taken on more than 400 family and property cases from Berwins Solicitors, while opening a new office in Harrogate.

The move follows Berwins’ filing of a notice of intention to appoint an administrator. Ridley & Hall says it has taken on the cases and offered immediate employment to a number of experienced members of the Berwins team.

The new office is based at WorkWell Offices at Copthall Bridge in Harrogate.

Six solicitors have joined Ridley & Hall as part of the move. They include residential property specialists Michelle Crowhurst, Rachael Fell, Alice Hoyle and Jenny Spence, alongside family law specialists Danielle Day and Rachael Lyon.

## New Harrogate office

Emma Pearmaine, managing director of Ridley & Hall, said the firm had been able to respond quickly to the situation and take on both the cases and members of staff.

> **Emma Pearmaine, managing director of Ridley & Hall:**
>
> The fact that we were able to react so quickly and approach Berwins to take on these cases, and offer immediate employment to a number of people, is a reflection of the strength and agility of our business.
>
> We’ve recruited some exceptional legal talent and we are delighted to welcome them to the Ridley & Hall team. We know they will bring a great deal of expertise to our business and ensure our Harrogate office is established on a solid footing.
>
> Equally, whilst it is obviously sad to see any legal firm, especially one of Berwins’ history and standing, be in a position where it has to appoint administrators, our swift action has also brought reassurance to hundreds of clients who were potentially facing significant disruption.

The firm said the move reflected its ambition to continue growing and strengthen its position in the Yorkshire legal market.

The new Harrogate office will provide a base for the recruited solicitors and support the continued handling of the family and property cases transferred from Berwins.
