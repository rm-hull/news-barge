Powerful Atlantic waves crashed against the shoreline at Costa da Caparica, south of Lisbon, on Wednesday, November 11, as Storm Charles brought heavy rain and strong winds to Portugal.

The coastal resort saw waterlogged streets, overturned electric scooters and deserted seafront terraces, while a warning sign marked the closure of a pier.

Portugal’s weather agency issued red warnings for persistent heavy rain and strong winds in parts of the country. Waves along the Lisbon and Setúbal coastlines were forecast to reach up to seven metres, with maximum heights potentially reaching 13 metres.

Authorities urged residents to stay away from beaches and exposed coastal areas due to the risk of flooding and dangerous sea conditions.
