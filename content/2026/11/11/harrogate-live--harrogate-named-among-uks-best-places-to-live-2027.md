Harrogate has been named among the UK’s best places to live for 2027.

The spa town was included in Muddy Stilettos’ list of the UK’s Top 260 Best Places to Live, published at the end of October.

Published annually, the guide picks out the best places to live in each county and looks at what they offer, from schools, commuting and house prices to where to eat, shop and spend a weekend.

Harrogate is one of eight places in Yorkshire to make the list, alongside York, Ilkley, Dore in Sheffield, Hebden Bridge, Saltaire, Slaithwaite and Beverley.

## Why Harrogate made the list

“Elegant, affluent and packed with handsome Georgian and Victorian houses, Harrogate is Yorkshire’s answer to Bath,” the guide says, “with a serious shopping habit.”

It says young families “have long flocked here, drawn by excellent schools, leafy parks, an enviable food scene and one of the best independent shopping offerings in the North”.

“Frankly, it’s worth considering a move to Harrogate just for Bettys,” it adds, calling the tea room “legendary”.

For a pub meal it picks The Coach & Horses as “the gastropub everyone wishes were their local”, with the advice: “don’t miss the fish pie”.

Commercial Street, Cold Bath Road and the Montpellier Quarter are named as the heart of the town’s independent shopping.

![Tall trees casting long shadows across the grass of The Stray in Harrogate on a sunny evening, with a church spire on the skyline](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/harrogate-named-among-uks-best-places-to-live-2027-2.webp)

The guide singles out The Stray, “200 acres of protected parkland wrapping around the town centre”, and Valley Gardens, “perfect for a leisurely stroll”.

“If you can’t find relaxation in a spa town famous for its healing waters, where can you?” it asks, pointing readers to the Turkish Baths and Rudding Park Spa.

It also names the Royal Pump Room Museum, the Mercer Art Gallery, Harrogate Theatre, the Royal Hall, the Theakston Old Peculier Crime Writing Festival and the Great Yorkshire Show.

![The ivy-covered stone front of the Old Swan Hotel in Harrogate, with hanging baskets around its pillared entrance](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/harrogate-named-among-uks-best-places-to-live-2027-3.webp)

Its best kept secret is the town’s part in “a real-life whodunnit”: Agatha Christie’s 11-day disappearance in December 1926, which ended when she was found at The Old Swan Hotel, checked in under the name Teresa Neele.

She was recognised there on 14 December, 100 years ago next month.

## How much it costs to live in Harrogate

“Harrogate’s handsome period homes and sought-after postcodes come at a premium, although house prices have softened recently,” the guide says.

Properties across the town sold for an average of £385,701 over the past year, according to the guide, with semis at £364,896, terraces at £291,206 and detached homes at £669,656.

That compares with an average house price in Harrogate of £397,640 in Land Registry sales to April 2026.

For commuters, the guide puts Leeds around 35 minutes away by direct train and York about 40, with direct LNER trains taking from around 2 hours 50 minutes to reach King’s Cross.
