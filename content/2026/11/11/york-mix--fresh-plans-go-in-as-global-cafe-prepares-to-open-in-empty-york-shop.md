An international café chain is another step closer to opening on one of York’s most prominent city centre corners.

Fresh plans have gone in for 1–2 St Sampson’s Square and 1 Feasegate, the historic Grade II listed building formerly home to footwear retailer Size?.

Danish brand Joe & The Juice has submitted a listed building consent application to City of York Council to pave the way for its arrival.

The move follows initial approvals granted at the end of October for the fit-out and external changes to convert the former clothes shop into a café bar.

***YorkMix*** first revealed in July that the company had lined up the location spot for its very first Yorkshire branch.

The announcement kicked off weeks of speculation over the future of Size?, before staff were informed the trainer store would shut its doors in mid-September.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/joe-and-the-juice-st-sampsons-square-york-pd.webp)

The latest application seeks permission to remove an internal modern stud wall on the first floor.

Planning documents state this will help create a more open, functional customer seating area across the upper level. Previous plans indicated the venue would seat up to 65 visitors across two floors, with 29 covers on the ground floor and 36 upstairs.

The applicants also want to install trough lighting above the approved fascia signs to illuminate the store front during evening hours.

A heritage document submitted with the application says the proposals protect the historic character of the 18th-century townhouses.

Because the wall due for removal is modern partition fabric installed during past retail refits, it says the demolition will cause no harm to the original building fabric.

“Securing the long-term reuse of a vacant or underused listed building is identified as an important public benefit,” documents submitted with the application say.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/former-size-shoe-shop-feasegate-st-sampsons-square-york.webp)

Founded in Copenhagen in 2002, Joe & The Juice operates more than 500 locations globally across Europe, the US, Asia, and the Middle East. The chain is widely recognized for its high-energy vibe, fresh juices, health shakes, specialty coffee, and toasted flatbread sandwiches.

While the brand has multiple locations across London and major regional hubs including Manchester, Liverpool, and Birmingham, the York venue will mark its debut across the Yorkshire region.

Before Size? welcomed shoppers in 2018, the distinctive double-fronted unit housed Cloggs shoe shop and Barratts footwear before that.

The address has deep commercial roots stretching back nearly two centuries, having accommodated tailors, hatters, and grocers through the 1800s, as well as the Stylo Boot Company in the 1950s.
