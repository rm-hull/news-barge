Students at Harrogate High School have made poppies from recycled plastic bottles for a Remembrance display at the school entrance.

The students created the display to honour those who have served in the Armed Forces and remember those who lost their lives in conflict.

A spokesperson for Harrogate High School said:

> “The creation is a tribute to all those who have served, sacrificed and lost their lives in conflicts, and a reminder of the importance of remembering those who gave so much for our freedom.
>
> "Made from recycled plastic bottles students have shown creativity, kindness and care in creating this special display."
