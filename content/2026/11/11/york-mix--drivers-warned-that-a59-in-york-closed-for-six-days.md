Drivers are being warned that a stretch of the A59 Boroughbridge Road is closed for six days.

The road closed both ways from the A1237 Outer Ring Road to Beckfield Lane yesterday (Tuesday, November 10).

It’s scheduled to stay shut until Monday (16 November) at 6pm.

#### Closure location

The road closure is for waterworks. Yorkshire Water said: “We are carrying out work to either install, renew or replace assets on our water network.

“This is essential work to ensure we provide a reliable service to our customers.”
