# Met Office names six UK areas facing snow on Thursday as temperatures plummet

## Health chiefs have issued the first cold weather alert of the winter as temperatures are expected to plummet later this week

Certain regions across the UK may experience snowfall tomorrow (Thursday, November 12), based on the most recent Met Office predictions and meteorological charts. Northern Scotland could witness as much as 4mm of snow hourly tomorrow morning, according to Met Office weather mapping.

Communities in the north and north-eastern parts of the country are most likely to be impacted. Temperature readings are also forecast to decline, the same charts indicate, hovering at five or six degrees Celsius above freezing.

The Met Office UK outlook for Thursday stated: "A noticeably chillier day under northerly winds, breezier towards coasts. Wintery showers continue to feed into the far north, but elsewhere it will remain largely dry with plenty of sunshine."

The national weather service additionally noted that this evening (11 November) rainfall and powerful gusts will continue, particularly across northern Britain, reports the Express.

The complete forecast states: "Turning drier in the south, though in the far north, frequent wintry showers persist with strong winds. Elsewhere, despite a few light showers, clear spells allow for widespread frost."

Medical authorities have announced the winter's inaugural cold alert as temperatures are anticipated to tumble later this week.

Weather experts have forecast temperatures dropping beneath seasonal norms by Thursday and Friday, with icy conditions and bitter winds anticipated across various parts of the nation. The UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) announced a yellow cold health alert for the North West and West Midlands from 9am on Thursday until noon on Saturday.

The Met Office has also indicated that Wednesday will mark the arrival of colder conditions moving into northern regions, with snow showers anticipated across northern Scotland overnight from Wednesday into Thursday.

This colder air mass will then progress southwards across the country throughout Thursday and Friday, forecasters added. Meteorologists predict temperatures will drop below average across most areas by Thursday, with Friday seeing particularly cold conditions and widespread frost expected.
