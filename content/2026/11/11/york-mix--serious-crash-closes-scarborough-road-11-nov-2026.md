Emergency services are at the scene of a serious crash in Scarborough tonight.

It’s on on Scalby Road, between The Rosette and Coldyhill Lane.

North Yorkshire Police posted at 6.05pm: “Road closures and diversions are in place.

#### Crash location

“Please avoid the area and use alternative routes while emergency services respond.”  
There is:

* No access south on Scalby Road past Danes Dyke
* No access north of Coldyhill Lane
* No access from Hackness Road to Scalby Road.
* And large vehicles will have to use Burniston Road.
