# Third man charged with murder of Raheim Jackson after Leeds weapons attack

## Darnell Anderson-Browne, 29, was charged overnight

A third man has been charged with the murder of Raheim Jackson, who was attacked with weapons in Leeds.

Darnell Anderson-Browne, 29, of Holtdale Place, Leeds, was charged overnight. He was remanded into custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court today (Wednesday, November 11).

Mr Jackson, 38, was known as Reema. He was pronounced dead a short time after he was attacked outside a property on Hedley Chase in New Wortley.

Police received a report at 10.23pm on October 24 that a man was being attacked with weapons. Emergency services went to the scene but could not save him.

West Yorkshire Police said three people have now been charged in the investigation. Two other men, aged 26 and 39, were arrested on suspicion of murder and have been bailed pending further enquiries.

Detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have been following a number of lines of enquiry.

Speaking after an arrest on Monday (November 9), Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates, from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, said: “Since this attack, we have been carrying out a thorough investigation that has already seen us charge two men and arrest two others who were subsequently bailed.

“Last night’s arrest is another step forward in ensuring we get Mr Jackson’s family the answers they need. We would still urge the public to come forward with any information that may be helpful to our investigation.”

Anyone with information that can help the investigation is asked to contact police on 101 or use Live Chat on the West Yorkshire Police website, quoting reference 13260606745. Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
