Thieves have stolen Lego sets from a shop in Malton.

It happened between 5.30pm on Saturday 7 November and 9.30am on Sunday 8 November.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Entry has been gained to the front display window of Hoppers and a number of Lego sets have been stolen.

#### Crime location

“We are appealing for any witnesses to this incident or any suspicious behaviour in the area during this time frame.”
