Time to dust off the sequins and get the tinsel out, as Sophie Ellis-Bextor brings her Christmas Kitchen Disco tour back to York next year.

The show will include festive disco favourites as well as Sophie’s own hits, with a 2027 Christmas Disco album announced closer to the time.

*Sophie Ellis-Bextor’s Christmas Kitchen Disco Tour*is at York Barbican on**Wednesday 8 December 2027**.

Sophie’s previous Christmas Kitchen Disco tour in 2023 sold out across the UK.

“I absolutely love Christmas and so it makes me very happy to announce a UK Christmas tour in 2027 where the band and I will once again be bringing the seasonal cheer to a venue near you,” said Sophie.

“Gloriously, that is not all! I am also releasing my Christmas Disco album which I am thrilled about. As someone who wears tinsel all year long anyway, this all feels very me and very exciting. So much to share with you all!”

Sophie Ellis-Bextor is a beloved pop voice and a disco icon. With a musical career that spans over 25 years, Sophie has now achieved six top 10 albums and eight top 10 singles.

Her hits include *Take Me Home, Get Over You*and* Murder on the Dancefloor*. The latter finding a new audience after featuring in the film* Saltburn*, giving her a first Billboard Hot 100 entry, a performance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, her own BBC One New Year’s Eve Disco and a live set on Dick Clark’s New Year’s Rockin’ Eve, and a return to No.2 in the UK charts.

Her Kitchen Disco livestreams turned a nation of living rooms into dancefloors, and she now presents Sophie Ellis-Bextor’s Kitchen Disco on BBC Radio 2, alongside hosting her podcast Spinning Plates, which is now in its 19th series.
