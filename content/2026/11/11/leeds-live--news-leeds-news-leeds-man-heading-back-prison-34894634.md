# Leeds man heading back to prison - if you see him let police know

## Officers are on the hunt for Kyle Bucknall

Detectives in Leeds are attempting to locate a man who is heading back to prison.

Kyle Bucknall, 35, is wanted on suspicion of affray, multiple thefts from shops and a prison recall. Officers in the city say "a number of arrest enquiries have been carried out but at this time his whereabouts are unknown".

The public are urged to contact police on 101 if they see him or know where he is. A West Yorkshire Police spokesman said: "Police in Leeds are appealing for the public’s help in reporting any information about a man who is wanted on suspicion of affray, multiple thefts from shops and re-call to prison.
