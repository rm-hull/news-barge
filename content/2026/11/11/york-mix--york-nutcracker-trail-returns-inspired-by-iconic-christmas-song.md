York’s much-loved Nutcracker Trail is returning – and this year is inspired by an iconic Christmas song.

From a partridge in a pear tree to twelve drummers drumming, The Twelve Days of Christmas is coming to the streets of York as the city’s Nutcracker Trail returns.

From Thursday 12 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, the York BID trail will bring Christmas magic to the city centre, with 12 giant nutcrackers waiting to be discovered.

Two new statues join the collection this year, with designs and artwork by York artist MarcoLooks, inspired by The Twelve Days of Christmas. Funded by Costa Coffee, and delivered in partnership with Make It York, the trail invites families to wrap up warm and set off on a festive adventure.

And that’s not all – there’s a £250 York Gift Card up for grabs on the trail too. Find all 12 nutcrackers and collect their names on a trail map to enter the prize draw.

With pipers piping and drummers drumming, there’s music to look out for along the way. Count the musical notes painted across all 12 nutcrackers and answer the bonus question for a chance to win an art hamper filled with goodies from MarcoLooks.

Artist Marc Godfrey-Murphy, who was commissioned to design and paint the Nutcracker Trail, said; “This year’s Nutcracker project has been the most challenging so far. Everyone knows the Twelve Days of Christmas, so we were keen to present something a little bit different and unexpected.

“The song is full birds, but looking closer at the lyrics, we we able to draw on other animals that also make appearance – look out for the cow! I’ve been working on the Nutcrackers for the last six months, so hopefully the hours of love and care that have gone into them, will make you smile.”

Rachel Bean, project manager at York BID, said; “For lots of families, finding the nutcrackers has become part of Christmas in York. With two new statues joining the trail, we’re looking forward to seeing people discover this year’s designs and new corners of York as they go!”

You can hand completed trail maps in at the Visitor Information Centre on Back Swinegate or post them in Santa’s Post Box in Museum Gardens.

Winners of the York Gift Card and art hamper will be drawn at random by Monday 18 January 2027.
