* **Eufy’s new smart home camera features a spotlight and siren**
* **The spotlight automatically follows intruders as they move**
* **It recognizes friends and family and takes a different approach for them**

Many of the best home security cameras are equipped with spotlights that switch on at the merest hint of movement, and you might even have one yourself. But finding a device that combines recorded footage with a light that autonomously moves and follows intruders is a different proposition entirely — and one that takes a more proactive approach to protecting your home and encouraging potential trespassers to go fish.

That setup is exactly what you get with Anker’s new $299.99 Eufy TrackLight Cam S1. Anker claims that it is “the world’s first outdoor security track light camera with an auto-tracking light designed to actively deter intruders.” That active deterrence comes in the form of a light beam that follows unidentified personnel on your property, hopefully spooking them into turning around and leaving.

The camera can sense targets within 12 meters and illuminates them with 20 lux of light. The beam itself can be widened or narrowed between 10 and 30 degrees, adaptively broadening as the person gets closer and responding as they move within 355 degrees of horizontal rotation.

To really scare off any unwanted presences, a siren can be activated alongside the spotlight. Additionally, to further deter, the light can be changed to red and blue flashing modes.

Everything is recorded in 4K-resolution night vision, with 10x digital zoom picking out objects further away. There’s also a separate floodlight that works alongside the tracking beam to provide extra detail in the dark.

## Recognizing friends and family

![The Anker Eufy TrackLight Cam S1 in use.](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/p5SjLzySozxvckqQ7wxynB-1138-80.jpg)

If you’re concerned about getting blinded by your own home security camera when you come home late from a night out, the good news is that the Eufy TrackLight Cam S1 can recognize friends and family members and instead activate a soft light instead of the harsh spotlight and siren. So long as the person’s phone has been added to the product’s companion app, the camera will identify them using Bluetooth and provide a much friendlier greeting.

Anker also says that the Eufy TrackLight Cam S1 comes with a 15,000mAh battery and built-in solar panels, so it should have plenty of juice come rain or shine. Its IP65 rating also means it’s resistant to dust and water ingress.
