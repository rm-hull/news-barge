The Mitte District Office announced that a building permit has been granted for the "The Wilhelm" residential development on Wilhelmstraße. The project calls for 500 apartments and commercial spaces.

Illustrative image

After years of inactivity, construction is set to resume on a vacant lot near the Brandenburg Gate in Mitte.

The development will take place on the property at Wilhelmstraße 56–59, between Behrenstraße, Wilhelmstraße, and Französische Straße. The building is planned to have eight stories, with some sections along Wilhelmstraße reaching nine stories. District Councilman Ephraim Gothe (SPD) particularly welcomed a revision of the concept. He noted that the new owner had shifted the focus from large condominiums and penthouses to "500 standard-sized units of various sizes."

Gothe also emphasized: "The imminent disappearance of a vacant lot that has dominated this area for far too long is truly good news for the Mitte district and the city of Berlin." Since the demolition of the previous buildings and the excavation of the construction pit, the open construction site has been an eyesore in the immediate vicinity of the British Embassy and the Unter den Linden boulevard. An excavation pit has been on the site since 2016. The project had stalled. In the fall of 2025, Berlin’s Hilpert Group took over the project from the Adler Group and announced that work would resume promptly. The project is scheduled for completion in 2028.
