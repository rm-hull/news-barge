With component prices soaring and discounts getting smaller, I'm expecting it'll be a lot harder to find some truly exceptional Black Friday laptop deals this year. But signs from some of the early sales are positive, with several strong offers that are worth considering if you're planning to buy a new device in the end-of-year sales. You can find all of the best ones right here.

It's still a little quieter in the US, as we're waiting for some of the big names to kick off sales. However, both Best Buy and Dell are already serving up some big savings on laptops at this early stage, and are the places to go if you're shopping right now.

It's a lot busier in the UK, where the likes of Currys, Argos, Samsung, and Dell have all already launched Black Friday sales. It's here that you can find some good savings on mid-range and high-end devices, including some powerful and modern laptops from Acer, Microsoft, Lenovo, and more.

But this is very much just the start, and I expect many more Black Friday laptop deals will drop over the coming weeks. It may be a tougher year to find the exceptional bargains, but we'll bring all the best ones we can find to you right here.
