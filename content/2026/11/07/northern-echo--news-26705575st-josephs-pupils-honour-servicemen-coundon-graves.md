Schoolchildren have honoured fallen servicemen with a heartfelt tribute ahead of Remembrance Day.

Pupils from St Joseph’s RC Primary School in Coundon, County Durham, honoured 16 fallen servicemen by marking their graves with handwritten messages, a red rose and a small wooden cross ahead of Remembrance Day.

The tribute was part of a project supported by SSAFA, the Armed Forces charity, and led by SSAFA Durham volunteers Mike Donne and Ruth Wright.

Mr Donne said: "I love to see young people in our community form a connection with the soldiers who gave their lives in service.

"The children in the Netherlands have done this for decades, and it's such a moving example of respect and gratitude."

Inspired by a Dutch tradition of adopting war graves, the project encouraged children to learn about the individuals behind the headstones and their stories.

Pupil Mia Hamlyn, aged nine, placed a cross with her own handwritten message of thanks, while classmates laid roses and crosses at other graves.

Sarah Dove, deputy headteacher at St Joseph’s, said: "We are extremely grateful for the relationship we have built with Mike and other members of SSAFA.

"These opportunities bring generations together and help our children understand the importance of remembrance, gratitude and peace."
