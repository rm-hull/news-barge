Art enthusiasts have one week left to view the Aesthetica Art Prize exhibition at York Art Gallery.

Celebrating its 20th year, the showcase features extraordinary talent from contemporary artists worldwide.

The exhibition, which closes on November 15, highlights York's status as a UNESCO Media Arts City, supporting the city’s thriving creative industries.

This year’s shortlisted artists display a visually striking array of mixed media pieces, each exploring powerful themes like the environment, relationships with nature, and modern health and wellbeing.

Some artworks combine these themes, portraying the link between people and the natural world through compelling imagery.

Others delve into identity, race, and cultural memory within the community, while some poignantly address mental health in today’s world.

The exhibition is open Wednesday to Sunday and is included in general admission to York Art Gallery.

Tickets are available online or at the door.

For more information, visit York Art Gallery’s website.
