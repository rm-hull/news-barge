## ‘Exquisite’ York home with impressive garden hits the market – have a look inside

Outside at the four-bedroom house for sale in Moor Lane, Woodthorpe, for £850,000 *(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

An “exquisite” York home with an impressive garden has hit the market.

The four-bedroom house is for sale in Moor Lane, Woodthorpe, for £850,000.

Churchills, which is marketing the 1960s bungalow, said it is an “exceptional detached home” which has been extended and modernised to “create a spacious and contemporary living accommodation”.

Inside is an entrance hallway leading to the open-plan living kitchen with full-width windows overlooking the garden. There is also dining space and a utility room.

A separate lounge with a log burner is accessed via sliding doors and “perfect for closing off for evening relaxation”, according to Churchills.

There are three more double bedrooms, as well as a four-piece bathroom and fitted storage areas.

“This aesthetically pleasing and thoughtfully designed layout combines practicality with contemporary living,” a Churchills spokesperson said.
