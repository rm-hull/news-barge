A man who attacked a paramedic lashed out at police arresting him, biting one officer's leg and headbutting another.

Mark Davison became aggressive while a paramedic treated him on Durham Road in Chester-le-Street, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

The 28-year-old bit a police officer’s leg while being restrained.

Prosecutor Rebecca Rowlands said: “Officers were trying to arrest him in relation to the assault on the paramedic.

“The officer was trying to carry out a search of him when he became aggressive.

“He headbutted the police officer when they were putting him in leg restraints. He continued to resist, he was kicking out repeatedly and banging his head on the floor.”

Davison, of Kings Lane, Pelton, Chester-le-Street, admitted assault by beating and assaulting a police officer on June 10.

His solicitor, Jaxon Taylor, urged magistrates to follow the recommendations in his pre-sentence report.
