The Kiwis will have to do without Ronaldo Mulitalo, who serves a one-match suspension after his red card last week, but Kalyn Ponga makes a miraculous return to the reserves a matter of weeks after breaking his arm against Australia. Charnze Nicoll-Klokstad starts on the wing in place of Mulitalo for the 2008 world champions, whose forward pack captained by James Fisher-Harris are the envy of any side in the world.

Tonga's thrilling 20-18 victory of Pasifika rivals Samoa was the moment the men's tournament came alive. Halfback Isaiya Katoa was superb and the Dolphins star will again partner with Tui Lolohea as coach Kristian Woolf names the same 19-man squad for the biggest game in Tongan rugby league history.
