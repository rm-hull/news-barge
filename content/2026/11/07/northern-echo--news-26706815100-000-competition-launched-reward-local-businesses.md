LOCAL businesses are being celebrated for their vital role in powering football communities and the festive season.

BLC Forklifts from Wimbledon and RSS Robinsons Site Solutions from Middlesbrough were among those recognised at events hosted by Sage and the English Football League (EFL) at AFC Wimbledon and Middlesbrough FC.

The events highlighted the impact of small businesses on football towns and communities, from supporting matchday rituals to contributing to the broader identity of their areas.

Joe Daniels, owner of BLC Forklifts, said: "For us, Christmas is about making sure our customers can get through the festive rush and enjoy a well-earned break.

"A huge amount of care goes into keeping the forklifts serviced, maintained and running reliably, because we know how important it is for businesses to keep moving during one of the busiest times of the year.

"It meant a lot to have that work recognised and to meet other local business owners who put the same care into what they do."

The event at AFC Wimbledon’s Cherry Red Records Stadium on November 3 featured guest speakers from the club and Sage, focusing on the importance of community and business in shaping the club’s identity.

AFC Wimbledon vice-captain Alistair Smith said: "One of the things I've loved most about the club is its tight-knit community and special bond with the Wimbledon area.

"Businesses around the Cherry Red Records Stadium are a huge part of that.

"It was a pleasure to meet them and show how important they are to this football club."

A similar event took place at Middlesbrough’s Riverside Stadium on November 5, further recognising the contributions of local businesses.

The celebrations form part of Sage’s wider partnership with the EFL and coincide with the launch of Sage’s Secret Santa competition, which offers EFL fans the chance to nominate a small business to win a share of £100,000.
