A 67-year-old man called Julek told Reuters that he saw a dark car appear suddenly before the driver started shooting at a group of people and drove off.

“If we had been two minutes earlier, we might have been shot,” he said.

Poland’s PM Donald Tusk wrote on X: “My deepest condolences to the families and loved ones of the victims of the tragic attack in Kraków. The highest praise for the heroic police officers who risked their lives. And a heartfelt appeal to politicians of all stripes: do not exploit this tragedy. We will establish all the circumstances of this crime.”

A man who gave his name as Konrad, and witnessed the shooting, told news website Interia: “I saw shots being fired from the driver’s side window, and smoke was coming out. I ran to the police station in the main market square; when I burst inside, it was full of people hiding on the floor.”

The gunman fired shots from a passing car near Slawkowska Street in the main square, according to local reports.

They were then shot by police officers and detained near the intersection of Dietla and Stradomska streets.

News website Onet, citing police, said: “The man had no identification documents with him. The police were able to establish his identity through operational work.”

Courtesy of my colleague Jakub Krupa. Małopolska region police told tvn24 the Krakow attacker, who is injured in hospital, wore “military style” clothing and used “military type firearms”. More weapons have been secured in his car.

He added the incident has been contained with no other suspects.

Police started to receive reports at around 3 p.m. (1400 GMT) that someone in a car had been shooting randomly people who entered the square, a police spokesperson said.

“According to information from police officers, the man was wearing military-style attire,” the spokesperson told reporters.

“During the arrest, officers used their weapons, but the man returned fire; as a result, two police officers are among the injured.”
