A play area is now open to the public following a large-scale upgrade project.

Pocklington Town Council has finished improvement works at the Broadmanor play area, funded by a £26,881.33 open space grant and £8,488.84 in match funding.

The upgrades include new play equipment and safety surfacing designed for younger children.

Roly Cronshaw, mayor of Pocklington, said: "I am delighted to see the children’s playground on Broadmanor is open again.

"As Mayor, it is particularly to see a facility like this bringing enjoyment to local children and families for many years to come.

"I would like to thank everyone who has helped make this project possible.

"I hope the children of Broadmanor and the surrounding area enjoy many hours of fun here, and that this playground remains a much-loved part of the neighbourhood."

Councillor Nick Coultish, East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s cabinet member for culture, leisure, tourism and planning, added: "We are committed to supporting community facilities across the East Riding, and it is great to see this commuted sums funding helping Pocklington Town Council carry out these improvements.

"The upgraded play area will benefit residents and visitors for many years to come."

The play area is free-to-use and open to the public.
