# 18-year-old charged after £48,500 of cannabis plants found during Hull raid

## An 18-year-old man has been charged after almost £50,000 of cannabis plants were found during a Hull raid.

A a Misuse of Drugs Act warrant was executed at an address on Hall Road in Hull on Thursday morning (Nov 5).

Upon forcing entry into the address it is reported a large quantity of cannabis plants worth an estimated £48,500 were discovered and seized.

Aldi Pecmarkaj, 18-years-old, of Hall Road in Hull has been charged with production of class B drugs and has was remanded to appear at court.
