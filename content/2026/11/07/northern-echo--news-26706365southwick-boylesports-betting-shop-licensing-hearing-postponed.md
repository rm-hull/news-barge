A crunch council meeting to decide if a gambling licence should be granted to a planned Southwick betting shop has been postponed.

Sunderland City Council’s licensing sub-committee was due to discuss an application from gambling company BoyleSports for two business units at The Green.

A planning application for the site was submitted earlier this year seeking permission to set up a betting office at the site, however no decision has been made to date.

A separate bid to the council’s licensing department was also submitted in July 2026 seeking a ‘betting (other) premises licence’, with the application assessed by the council under separate rules and regulations.

However, the application sparked an official objection from the council’s public health department, which claimed an additional betting shop in Southwick would “create unwanted harms amongst children and other vulnerable residents and add to crime and disorder in the area”.

Public health officers said that the objection was “not founded upon a general objection to gambling but on the specific characteristics of the precise area and published evidence relating to gambling harms”.

They added Southwick houses existing gambling outlets and faces “deep-rooted socioeconomic challenges” which make its population “significantly more vulnerable to gambling-related harm than other areas.”

A council report said discussions had taken place between the applicant’s legal representative, BoyleSports and council officers from public health and licensing.

Boylesports also submitted a ‘local gambling risk assessment’ as part of its licensing application.

Yet the public health department continued to object and a public hearing was scheduled for Wednesday (November 4, 2026), where councillors were due to discuss the licensing application.

It was confirmed this week that the meeting had been cancelled and that the licensing application will return to a future licensing sub-committee.

A council spokesperson said: “Meetings and agenda items can occasionally be cancelled and further details on this matter will come back to the committee later.”

BoyleSports, which was once an SAFC club sponsor, recently challenged a city council decision to refuse a planned betting shop in Sunderland’s central rail station following concerns about gambling-related harms.

A decision on the appeal is awaited from the planning inspectorate.

The company previously stressed that its stores “ensure responsible gambling” and said the proposed BoyleSports train station betting shop represented “a significant, positive investment in a prominent vacant unit.”
