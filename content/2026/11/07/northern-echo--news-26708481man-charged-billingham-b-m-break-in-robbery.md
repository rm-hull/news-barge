A man is due in court after a break-in at a B&M store.

Police were called to the Billingham branch just before 4.15am on Friday (November 6). after reports of a burglary.

Officers found a smashed window and took a description of the suspect.

A 44-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of burglary.

He was questioned about the B&M incident and a separate robbery and later charged with possessing a bladed article in a public place, robbery and aggravated burglary.

He was remanded in custody to appear at Teesside Magistrates' Court on today (Saturday, November 7).
