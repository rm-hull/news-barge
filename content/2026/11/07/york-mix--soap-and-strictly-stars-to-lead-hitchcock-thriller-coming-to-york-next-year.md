Lead casting for iconic Hitchcock thriller *Strangers on a Train* has been announced.

Lewis Cope and Jonny Labey will lead the cast for its forthcoming UK tour of Craig Warner’s stage adaptation of Patricia Highsmith’s celebrated novel, famously brought to the screen by Alfred Hitchcock.

Directed by Roxana Silbert, the production will see Lewis Cope take on the role of Charles Bruno, one of Highsmith’s most chilling and psychologyically complex characters, opposite Jonny Labey as Guy Haines, the unsuspecting stranger drawn into his deadly game.

It will arrive into Grand Opera House York from **Tuesday 2 to Saturday 6 February 2027**.

Two strangers meet on a train.

Charles Bruno has an idea: the perfect crime. Two murders. Two strangers. No motive. No connection. No consequences.

Guy Haines dismisses it as a joke. But Bruno isn’t joking. As Bruno’s obsession spirals out of control, Guy finds himself trapped in a nightmare of guilt, manipulation and fear.

Lewis Cope’s previous stage roles include Leonard Vole in the London production of Agatha Christie’s *Witness for the Prosecution*and Brahms in the UK tour of The* Legend of Sleepy Hollow.*He is widely recognised for his portrayal of Nicky Milligan in* Emmerdale*, for which he received a Rising Star nomination at the 2023 National Television Awards, and his hugely popular appearance on the 2025 series of* Strictly Come Dancing*, where he and his professional partner Katya Jones made it through to week eleven.

Jonny Labey has appeared in numerous stage productions, including originating the leading role of Scott Hastings in the West End production of Baz Luhrmann’s *Strictly Ballroom*. He is also widely known to TV audiences for his portrayal of Rex Gallagher in* Hollyoaks*and Paul Coker in* EastEnders*.

David Gilbery, CEO, Bill Kenwright Ltd commented: “We’re delighted to have Lewis and Jonny taking on two of Patricia Highsmith’s most fascinating characters in our new production of *Strangers on a Train.*

“They are both immensely talented performers, and with the Roxana Silbert directing, alongside designer Robert Innes Hopkins, and the rest of our wonderful creative team, this has all the ingredients for a truly thrilling stage production.”

Further casting to be announced.

Tickets for *Strangers on a Train* at the Grand Opera House York are on sale starting from £15 here.
