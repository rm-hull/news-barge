A much-loved County Durham tea house with celebrity links is preparing to take on businesses from across the country as it bids to be crowned the best in England for a second time.

Quarry Tea House, in Hunwick, will represent the region at the grand final of the England Business Awards in Birmingham on Monday, November 23, after being named the county's Best Café earlier this year.

The family-led business previously won the national title in 2022, and owner Julie Bell said securing the award again, amid what she described as "testing times" for independent businesses, would be particularly special.

She said: "To get it once was a dream, to be fair. We're getting a second chance at it now.

"To do it again, I don't know if I could put it into words what it would mean to us."

Julie and her team secured their place at the national final after taking the Best Café title for County Durham at the regional England Business Awards earlier this year.

Now, with the final fast approaching, Julie admitted the nerves are beginning to creep in.

She said: "We've won it before in 2022, but obviously that's a few years ago now.

"I'm getting apprehensive as we're getting nearer. It's like, 'Oh my goodness'.

"We're just doing all we can really. I'm pushing it so much on social media and with all my customers who are coming in and continuing to work hard."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21637952.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Julie Bell and Katie Butler *(Image: The Quarry Tea House)*

Quarry Tea House opened in 2021 following the Covid pandemic and has since built up a loyal customer base.

Julie said she could never have imagined when the business first opened that the team would go on to represent County Durham at a national final twice.

"Never in a million years," she said.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21637953.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Julie and Martin Bell *(Image: The Quarry Tea House)*Customers can support Quarry Tea House ahead of the final by voting through the link:**www.nominees.info/vote**

Julie saud that "every vote will count" as the business attempts to bring the national title back to County Durham.

She said: “It’s bizarre really, but it keeps happening.

“We’ve had Zoe Ball come in before and Scarlett Moffatt visits quite often. It’s really good for the business and lovely for the team.”
