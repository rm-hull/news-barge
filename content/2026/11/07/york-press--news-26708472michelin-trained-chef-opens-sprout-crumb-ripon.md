## Michelin-trained chef launches healthy deli and takeaway in North Yorkshire

Luke Mills has opened Sprout & Crumb in Ripon *(Image: Tim Flanagan)*

A Michelin-trained chef has opened a new healthy deli and takeaway in North Yorkshire.

Luke Mills is the driving force behind Sprout & Crumb, which has opened at 85 North Street, Ripon.

The chef has built up an impressive career, working with Michelin-starred chefs Herbert Berger at Lombard Street in London and Tessa Bramley at The Old Vicarage in Sheffield.




He also spent seven years as a menu development chef at Filmore & Union and worked in Australia at the Light Brigade Restaurant in Woollahra, New South Wales.

![Sprout & Crumb has opened at 85 North Street, Ripon](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21683107.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sprout & Crumb has opened at 85 North Street, Ripon *(Image: Tim Flanagan)*

Luke said customer feedback would play a key role in shaping the business.

He said: “For me, the most important ingredients are the customers, because their feedback helps me to create customised dishes which add to an ever-evolving choice, rather than just offering a set menu.

“I’m open to suggestions and if I get asked to introduce a new dish to meet a particular taste, I will give it a go.”

The deli focuses on wholesome takeaway food made with locally sourced ingredients.

Products on offer include sweet treats from Leah Wright’s Northallerton-based Wright Cake Bakery and artisan filter coffee from Lonton Coffee, whose beans are roasted in Teesdale.

Luke runs the business alongside his wife, Lucy, who operates The Sprouted Kitchen in Bedale and L Bar coffee shop at Leeming Bar.

The couple, who have two sons, Drew and Jax, said they have quickly fallen for Ripon's character and community spirit.

Luke said: “We have been welcomed with open arms by local residents, visitors and other independent business owners and it is clear that there is a real community feel here and a buzz on the streets."

The arrival of Sprout & Crumb has also been warmly received by neighbouring businesses.

![The deli focuses on wholesome takeaway food made with locally sourced ingredients](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21683104.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The deli focuses on wholesome takeaway food made with locally sourced ingredients *(Image: Tim Flanagan)*

Sarita McDermott, owner of Realitea Indian Tearoom and Bistro next door, encouraged people to support the new venture.

She said: “We’re delighted to welcome this lovely new addition to our local community, a lunchtime deli serving up fresh, healthy salads and tasty options to brighten up your lunch break.

“Why not pop in, say hello and explore what they have to offer? Remember shopping totally locally really is the way to go.

“We encourage everyone to keep supporting all our wonderful independent businesses. Help Ripon be vibrant and our high street thrive.”

Ripon BID manager Lilla Bathurst said Luke’s experience would strengthen the city’s food and drink offering.

She said: “Luke brings a wealth of experience to Ripon and the skills he has learnt in award-winning kitchens adds to the choice that the city’s food and drink sector offers visitors and local residents alike."

Sprout & Crumb is open from 11am to 3pm on Tuesdays and from 10am to 3pm Wednesday to Saturday.
