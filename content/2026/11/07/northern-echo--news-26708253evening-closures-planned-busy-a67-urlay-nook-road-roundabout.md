Drivers are being warned of road closures and traffic disruption as resurfacing work is carried out at a busy Teesside roundabout.

Highway improvements are set to take place at the A67 Urlay Nook Road roundabout in Eaglescliffe.

The work was postponed by bad weather and will now take place with temporary lights and closures on two Sundays.

On Sunday, November 8, Urlay Nook Road, Elton Lane and the Tesco entrance will close from 4.30pm to midnight.

A week later, on Sunday, November 15, Elton Lane and the Tesco entrance will close again from 4.30pm to midnight.

Signed diversion routes will be in place, while pedestrian access and access for emergency vehicles will be maintained.

Motorists should allow extra time, especially during the evening closures.

Stockton Council said the work remains weather-dependent and could be postponed again if conditions become unsafe for crews.

A council spokesperson said: “Highway improvements are due to be carried out on A67 Urlay Nook Road roundabout as part of our annual highways maintenance programme.

“We apologise for any inconvenience.”
