A four-year-old boy has died following a car crash in West Lothian, with police appealing for witnesses to come forward.

Emergency services were called to Drumcross Road, near Bathgate, at around 4.30pm on Friday, November 6, following reports of a single-vehicle crash involving a black Range Rover.

The incident happened between Limefield Road and Drumcross Farm.

The four-year-old boy, who was a passenger in the vehicle, was taken by ambulance to St John's Hospital in Livingston, where he died a short time later.

His family has been informed.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21682883.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Drumcross Road near Bathgate, West Lothian, where a four-year-old boy died following a car crash on Friday, November 6. *(Image: Google Maps)*

The 28-year-old man driving the Range Rover was taken to the Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh for treatment.

Police closed the road to allow crash investigation enquiries to be carried out.

Road policing officers are now appealing for anyone who witnessed the incident or has dashcam footage from the area to contact them.

Sergeant Dave Waddell said: “Our thoughts remain with the family and friends of the boy who died at this extremely difficult time.

"Enquiries are ongoing and I would appeal to anyone who witnessed the crash, or has any information that may assist, to contact us if they have not already done so.

“I’d also ask drivers with dash-cams, who were in the area around the time, to review their footage for any images that could assist with our investigation.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2468 of Friday, November 6, 2026.
