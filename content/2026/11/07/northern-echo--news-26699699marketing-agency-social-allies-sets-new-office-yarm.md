A growing marketing agency that has worked on a photoshoot with King Charles himself has moved to Yarm.

Social Allies, which was launched in 2019, has set up its new office space on the town’s High Street in a “really important milestone” for the firm.

The independent agency provides businesses with a marketing partner that works closely alongside them, providing social media management, branding, graphics, and website support.

Run by Jess Tucker, the firm has worked on a number of high-profile projects including a photoshoot with King Charles and celebrity Paris Fury, wife of world boxing champion Tyson.

The agency also worked on projects for the oil and gas and renewable energy industry.

Since launching seven years ago, Social Allies has grown from a social media specialist to a full-service marketing partner.

Jess said she is “incredibly proud” of the business, which is currently working on some “really exciting projects”.

![Jess Tucker, owner and managing director of Social Allies with Yarm Town Council Mayor Jake Taylor and Deputy Mayor Yvonne Wallace](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21671360.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jess Tucker, owner and managing director of Social Allies with Yarm Town Council Mayor Jake Taylor and Deputy Mayor Yvonne Wallace *(Image: SOCIAL ALLIES)*

“Moving to Yarm feels like a really important milestone,” she said.

“We needed a space that reflected where the business is now, gave our team room to grow and provided somewhere we could welcome clients and bring ideas to life.

“It’s an exciting next step, and I’m really looking forward to what 2027 will bring.”

The move comes after the firm was shortlisted for small business of the year at the Tees Businesswomen Awards and social media agency of the year at the 2026 SMM Awards.

The agency has also launched The Marketing Agenda, a platform designed to give marketing professionals access to useful information, insights and opportunities to learn and connect.

“We’ve got big plans for Social Allies,” Jess added.

“This move gives us the space to build on everything we’ve achieved so far, while continuing to evolve, challenge ourselves and do things differently.

“We’re excited about what’s next.”
