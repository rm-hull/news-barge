A popular pizza restaurant will close its doors for the final time on New Year's Eve, its owner has announced.

Simon Towers, owner of Il Pirata Pizzata on Bingley Road in Saltaire, shared the news on social media, describing it as "not a light decision" after four and years in business.

The restaurant will close when its lease comes up for renewal on December 31, although Mr Towers said the Il Pirata Pizzata brand will continue through mobile catering and pop-ups.

In a post shared with customers, he said: "After nearly five years we're closing the doors of the restaurant for the final time on 31st December as our lease renewal comes around.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/14105661.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

"It might be the end of the restaurant, but it's not the end of Il Pirata Pizzata; we'll be carrying on, just in a different form."

## Reason for closure

Mr Towers explained that when he moved from mobile catering into a permanent restaurant, the aim was eventually to leave his job and run the business full-time.

Instead, he said he has spent years balancing both careers.

"What's happened has been seven-day working weeks between the two roles, pulling 80 to 90 hours a week for 50 to 51 weeks a year," he wrote.

He also said signing a new lease would mean taking on personal financial risk again, making it harder to grow the business or step back from its day-to-day running.

## Local reaction

The restaurant, which opened in 2022, has built a loyal following locally.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/14105620.jpg?type=mds-article-620)

One regular customer said: "It's a massive shame, because the food was really delicious and it always seemed really busy whenever we would go."

A Saltaire resident added: "It's a real shame to see a local place, offering good food at reasonable prices, close down. I always enjoyed what they had to offer."

Mr Towers said the business would now return to its mobile catering roots, with weddings already booked for 2027 and the possibility of future pop-up events.

Reflecting on the restaurant's time in Saltaire, he highlighted a number of memorable moments, including hosting in-house weddings and travelling to Milan to attend a conference alongside some of the world's leading pizza makers.

He also paid tribute to former and current staff, saying he was "super proud" of seeing team members grow during their time at the restaurant.
