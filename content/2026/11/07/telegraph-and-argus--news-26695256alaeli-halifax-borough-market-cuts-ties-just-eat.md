A family-run shawarma business has cut ties with major delivery platform Just Eat.

Alaeli, run by husband-and-wife team Alin and Andreea Stoica at Halifax Borough Market, ended its Just Eat partnership after an order dispute.

The row began when Alaeli posted about a delivery problem on social media.

Alaeli claims Just Eat later asked it to remove the posts, saying they breached the partnership agreement.

Alaeli said it removed the posts but chose to end the partnership.

![Alaeli shawarma](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20601491.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fresh ingredients *(Image: Alaeli)*

The Stoicas named Alaeli after their two daughters, Alara and Elisa, combining their names to create the brand.

They serve Middle Eastern and European-inspired food, including shawarma, Lebanese bread and sauces shaped by their Romanian heritage.

Mr Stoica previously told the T&A: "We opened with a simple goal: to bring something different to Halifax – high-quality, freshly prepared shawarma with bold, unique flavours.

"Our shawarma recipe is entirely our own.

"It has been carefully developed and cannot be found anywhere else.

"Customers often describe it as 'addictive' and unlike anything they’ve tried before.

"All of our sauces are homemade, and every order is prepared fresh daily at our stall in the market.

"We focus on generous portions, strong flavours, and consistency - which has helped us build a loyal customer base."

![Alaeli shawarma](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20601493.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Fancy one? *(Image: Alaeli)*

On October 15, Alaeli posted on Facebook: "Although the order issue was eventually resolved financially, the way the situation has unfolded has changed how we feel about this partnership.

"We've now asked Just Eat to end our partnership, and we're waiting for confirmation."

![Alaeli shawarma](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/20601495.jpg?type=mds-article-620)It's very popular with locals! *(Image: Alaeli)*

## What Just Eat had to say

A Just Eat spokesperson said: "We understand that couriers not arriving, or arriving late, can cause challenges for independent restaurants.

"We will always work with partners, as we did with Alaeli Halifax, to provide suitable compensation.

"We wish the team continued success with their business."

The business continues to serve customers from its popular Halifax market location.
