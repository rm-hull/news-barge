Bradford is known as the curry capital of Britain thanks to its incredible range of curry houses dotted around the city.

However, if you are looking for a good Indian restaurant to try, it might also be worth checking out the many spots in the neighbouring towns and villages.

The small town of Guiseley is home to a range of bars, restaurants, and cafés including the Bird Restaurant.

The Bird Restaurant offers Indian food that has been made with "the freshest herbs and spices from around the world".

## What is the Bird Restaurant in Guiseley like?

The Bird Restaurant can be found in the centre of the town and offers customers both "high standards of food" and "a buzzing atmosphere".

The restaurant's menu features dishes from "every corner of India".

To start off your trip, you can enjoy a range of street chaat, choosing from different items, or have some warm starters such as kale bhaji, okra fries, paneer pakora, and Panjabi vegetable samosa.

In terms of its mains, the Bird Restaurant offers a few different curries, including butter chicken, railway lamb curry, Bird chicken masala, and Mangalorian prawn curry.

You might also want to try out the dal makni, Keralam potato and pea stew, and Dhingr idola matar.

The Bird Restaurant has plenty of side dishes to enjoy with your main, from different rices to naans.

### What do reviews say about the Bird Restaurant?

The Bird Restaurant has a rating of 4.9 out of 5 on Tripadvisor, with over 500 people leaving reviews after their trip.

Many people praise the "amazing" food and "attentive" service they receive during their visit.

One person wrote: "Such a friendly place.

"The food is brilliant and so flavourful.

"We always order too much and end up taking home our leftovers which are amazing the next day."

Another person said: "Tried a few different dishes between us and all were fantastic.

"Meat and fish seemed good quality and great flavours in abundance.

"Service prompt and friendly.

"Definitely the best Indian food in the local area.

"Will definitely return again."
