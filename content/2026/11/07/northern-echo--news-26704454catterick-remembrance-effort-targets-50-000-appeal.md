A community-led Remembrance effort is aiming to raise £50,000 for this year’s Poppy Appeal.

In Catterick, volunteers, schoolchildren and businesses have joined forces to support the Royal British Legion campaign with roadside and roundabout displays and fundraising.

Mark Milton, Catterick Poppy Appeal organiser, said: "The response has been fantastic, and none of this would be possible without the volunteers and the generosity of local people.

"It would be absolutely wonderful if we could break through the £50,000 barrier this year, but whatever we raise, it’s a real team effort."

Mr Milton is supported by assistants Graham Stewart and Becki Bulmer.

He has also helped coordinate the creation of Remembrance displays throughout Catterick Garrison, involving schools, community groups and businesses.

Fraser Brown, director of Browns of Richmond, said: "The displays are a real credit to everyone involved, and we’re delighted to have played a small part in helping create such a fitting tribute."

The Catterick and District Veterans Association Breakfast Club has also created a separate roadside tribute.

Mr Milton added: "Remembrance means so much in a place like Catterick, and involving young people ensures its importance is passed on to future generations."
