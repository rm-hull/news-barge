A cannabis grower, a thug who hit women and two who defied court orders were among those sentenced recently at Scarborough Magistrates Court.

David Wood, 35, of Cross Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to racially aggravated words or behaviour.

He was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision and six months’ alcohol treatment. He must pay £100 compensation, a £40 fine and £85 prosecution costs.

Tyler Rushton, 24, of East Lea View, Cayton, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to assaulting two men and using threatening words or behaviour in Scarborough town centre.

He was given a six-month community order with probation supervision. He must pay £50 compensation to each man, a £40 fine and £85 prosecution costs.

Michael Anderson, 67, of Aberdeen Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to two charges of breaching a restraining order.

He was given an eight-week prison [sentence] suspended for 12 months and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

John Bateman, 46, of Elderflower Road, Scalby, denied a charge of failure to provide a breath specimen when suspected of drink driving and was convicted at trial. He pleaded guilty to allowing a man to drive a car without a licence and without insurance.

He was given a community order and must wear an alcohol abstinence tag for 100 days. He was banned from driving for 17 months and must pay £650 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.

Lauren McLean, 32, of St Leonard’s Crescent, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to failure to take a breath test when suspected of drink driving and assaulting a police officer.

She was given a 12-month community order and must wear an alcohol abstinence tag for 90 days. She was banned from driving for 42 months and ordered to pay £100 compensation to the officer and £400 prosecution costs.

David Flinton, 45, of Spreight Lane Steps, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to five breaches of a criminal behaviour order by drinking alcohol in public, trespassing on the railway and was jailed for 12 months. Magistrates said he had a “flagrant disregard for court orders”

Keith Raper, 63, of Colescliffe Road, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to cultivating 16 cannabis plants.

He was given a 12-month community order with six months’ mental health treatment and 60 hours’ unpaid work and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Paul McCann, 34, of Oriel Crescent, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to assaulting two women.

He was given a 12-month community order with probation supervision, six months’ alcohol treatment and six months’ mental health treatment. He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs.

Nicole Pryke, 36, of St Hildas Terrace, Whitby, pleaded guilty to drink driving when three times the drink drive limit.

She was given a 12-month community order with six months’ alcohol treatment and probation supervision. She was banned from driving for 28 months, fined £80 and ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.
