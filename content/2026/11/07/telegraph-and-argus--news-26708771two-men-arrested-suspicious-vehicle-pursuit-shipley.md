Two men were arrested after a vehicle was pursued into Shipley following reports of suspicious activity.

According to West Yorkshire Police, officers were first called at around 10.05am on Saturday, November 7, following concerns about suspicious behaviour in the Dewsbury area.

## **Suspicious vehicle spotted**

Police said a vehicle linked to the report was later seen in the Bradford district.

Officers attempted to stop the car, but it failed to stop, leading to a pursuit.

## **Pursuit ends in Shipley**

The vehicle eventually came to a stop on Otley Road in Shipley.

Police said two men then left the vehicle and were arrested nearby following a search of the area.

## **Investigation continues**

West Yorkshire Police said enquiries remain ongoing into the incident.

The force has not released any further details about the circumstances of the arrests or any potential offences under investigation.
