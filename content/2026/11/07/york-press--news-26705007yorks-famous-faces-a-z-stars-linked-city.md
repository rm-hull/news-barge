## From Dame Judi Dench to Guy Fawkes: The A-Z of York's famous faces

Our A to Z of famous York faces - some may surprise you *(Image: NQ)*

*York's famous faces: A-Z of stars linked to the city, compiled by MAXINE GORDON*

YORK has long attracted remarkable people.

Some were born here, some studied here, some built careers here, while others have family, educational or cultural ties to the city and wider Yorkshire region.

From Hollywood legends to historical figures, musicians to sports stars, here's our A to Z of famous names linked to York.

**A - Mark Addy**

Born in York, actor Mark Addy found fame in The Full Monty, A Knight's Tale and Game of Thrones. He attended Joseph Rowntree School and remains one of the city's best-known modern success stories.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678515.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A is for York actor Mark Addy, photographed in York with an honorary degree

**B - John Barry**

The Oscar-winning composer behind numerous James Bond scores was born in York in 1933 and grew up in a grand house that is now The Pavillion hotel. His father managed several York cinemas, helping to inspire a lifelong love of film and music.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678517.jpg?type=mds-article-620)B is for John Barry famous for composing the James Bond theme music

**C - Russell Crowe** The Oscar-winning actor has spoken about his Yorkshire roots, with family connections traced to the York area: his ex-wife Danielle Spencer had family in Nether Poppleton.**D - Dame Judi Dench**

One of Britain's most celebrated actors, Dame Judi was born in York and grew up in Heworth. She attended The Mount School and her first acting experiences were in the York Mystery Plays in the 1950s.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21618052.jpg?type=mds-article-620)D is for York-born actress Judi Dench, pictured in 1985 with the Dean of York, the Very Rev John Southgate, outside York Minster

**E - William Etty** Born in York in 1787, Etty became one of Britain's most important painters. York Art Gallery houses the largest collection of his work anywhere in the world.**F - Guy Fawkes** York's most infamous son attended St Peter's School before becoming involved in the Gunpowder Plot of 1605. More than 400 years later, his name remains known around the globe and is forever associated with Bonfire Night on November 5.**G - Harry Gration**

The widely respected York-born TV journalist and broadcaster, who presented Look North for many years, lived in the city for much of his life and passed away in 2022.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/17711332.jpg?type=mds-article-620)G is for Harry Gration, pictured starting a York 10k event. The broadcaster died in 2022

**H - George Hudson** Known as the "Railway King", Hudson transformed York into one of Britain's most important railway centres and helped shape the city we know today. George Hudson Street is named after him.**I - Arthur Ingram**

Sir Arthur Ingram (1565 – 1642) was one of the most powerful, wealthy, and controversial figures in 17th-century Yorkshire. High Sheriff of Yorkshire in 1620 and a four-time MP for York, he left the almshouses on Bootham known as Ingram House to the city.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678518.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An A to Z of York's famous faces

**J - Amy Johnson**

The pioneering English aviator, who became the first woman to fly solo from London to Australia, has strong ties to the region and remains one of Yorkshire's most celebrated figures. Amy Johnson Way at Clifton Moor Business Park in York is named in her honour.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678519.jpg?type=mds-article-620)J is for aviation pioneer Amy Johnson, pictured in her Puss Moth plane,1932. Image: supplied

**K - Berwick Kaler**

For generations of York families, Christmas meant Berwick Kaler. The legendary panto star entertained audiences at York Theatre Royal for decades before finishing his career at the Grand Opera House.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678520.jpg?type=mds-article-620)K is for York panto legend Berwick Kaler. Image: Frank Dwyer

**L - George Leeman** George Leeman (1809–1882) was one of York's most influential public figures, serving as Lord Mayor three separate times and was its Liberal MP. Famous for exposing the financial fraud of his bitter political rival, George Hudson (the "Railway King"), Leeman Road is named after him and there is a large statue by the city walls in his honour.**M - Steve McClaren**

The former England football manager was born in Fulford and was a pupil at York's Nunthorpe School before embarking on a coaching career that included Middlesbrough, England, FC Twente and Newcastle United.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678522.jpg?type=mds-article-620)M is for Former England manager Steve McClaren who joined Mike Tomlinson for the York 10K at York Minster in 2014. Picture Frank Dwyer.

**N - Eille Norwood** The York-born actor (1861-1948) became one of the silent film era's biggest stars thanks to his portrayal of Sherlock Holmes. He was educated at Pocklington School, east of York.**O - Jacques O'Neill**

In 2024, sportsman Jacques O’Neill signed a one-year deal to return to rugby league with York Knights. He had already made a name for himself as a contestant on the popular reality show Love Island in 2022.

**P - Walter Porter** Walter Harold Porter was a British athlete who competed at the 1924 Summer Olympics. Born in York, he won a silver medal in the 3000m team race. He died in 1979 at the age of 76.**Q - Queen Anne** Queen Anne reigned from 1702 to 1714 as the last monarch of the House of Stuart. A street and school were named in her honour during the early 20th-century expansion of Clifton.**R - Joseph Rowntree**

Born in 1836, in Pavement – now home to Pizza Hut – Joseph Rowntree was a true York legend who helped make the city the UK's confectionery capital through the family's chocolate business. He was also a social reformer who pioneered decent working conditions for employees. The model village of New Earswick was built at his direction to provide good homes for factory workers.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20400186.jpg?type=mds-article-620)R is for Joseph Rowntree Image: Explore York Libraries and Archives

**S - Septimius Severus** The Roman emperor spent his final years in York and died here in AD211. At the time, York was one of the most important cities in the Roman Empire.**T - Emma Thompson**

Oscar-winning actress Emma Thompson has a York connection through her husband Greg Wise, who attended St Peter's School, York, before finding success on stage and screen.

![ Emma Thompson pictured with her husband Greg Wise who went to school in York. (Picture: Mark Milsome Foundation)](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21678523.jpg?type=mds-article-620)T is for Emma Thompson pictured with her husband Greg Wise who went to school in York. (Picture: Mark Milsome Foundation)

**U - William Ullathorne** Born in Pocklington in 1806, Ullathorne became a leading Catholic bishop and one of the most influential religious figures of the Victorian age.**V - Vikings** Our Nordic invaders are as much a part of the city as the Bar Walls, Bettys, and Bishy Road! Thanks to the groundbreaking discovery of a Viking settlement in the city centre back in 1976, we now have the world-renowned JORVIK Viking Centre which continues to attract hundreds of thousands of visitors to York each year.**W - Rick Witter**

The Shed Seven frontman is synonymous with modern York. The band's songs became the soundtrack to a generation and helped put York firmly on the Britpop map.

![Shed Seven frontman Rick Witter.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21024760.jpg?type=mds-article-620)W is for Shed Seven frontman Rick Witter. Image: Cuffe & Taylor

**X - Xavier Va'a** Let's be honest, X was always going to be a challenge. Thankfully, York Knights came to the rescue with the signing of Australian rugby league forward Xavier Va'a from Sydney Panthers earlier this year. He may not yet be a household name, but he's earned a place in our alphabet thanks to one of the rarest first names in professional sport, which in a slight detour from the format we are claiming as our 'X' category!**Y - Thomas Young** Served as the Archbishop of York from 1561 until his death in 1568, Young was a highly influential religious and political figure during the Elizabethan era. He is buried inside York Minster.**Z - William Zouche** A star of medieval times, Zouche was Lord Treasurer and went on to become Archbishop of York in 1340. He lead the city during the Black Death of 1349 when the bubonic plague reached York. He died in 1352 at Cawood Palace and was buried in the nave of York Minster, where the Zouche Chapel is named in his honour.***Who's missing?
