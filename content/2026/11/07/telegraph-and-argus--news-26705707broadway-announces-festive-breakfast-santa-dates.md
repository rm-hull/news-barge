SANTA is coming to town and families are in for a magical treat.

Breakfast with Santa is returning to The Broadway in Bradford, offering a festive morning filled with cheer and excitement on December 5, 12, 19, and 21.

Each session will run in the centre’s food court, with three sittings per day between 9am and 12pm.

Ian Ward, general manager at The Broadway, said: "Breakfast with Santa is one of our most cherished festive traditions, and we’re delighted to offer families four opportunities to join us this year.

"Alongside the return of our Christmas VR Sleigh experience, it forms part of a fantastic festive programme designed to create magical memories for visitors of all ages.

"It’s a wonderful way to make memories, enjoy a festive treat and celebrate the season together."

Up to 50 children can attend each session, with tickets priced at £5 per child.

The fee is fully refunded at the event in the form of a Bradford City Centre Gift Card.

Children will be welcomed by Santa’s elves, treated to a festive breakfast, and given the chance to meet Santa, share their Christmas wishes, and receive a special gift.
