A much-loved North East ice cream parlour has closed its doors for the final time after serving customers for more than 134 years.

Mark Toney which has long been a fixture of Newcastle city centre has announced its closure.

The business has been part of the city since the 19th century.

The company operated from locations on Grainger Street and Percy Street, where countless customers have enjoyed ice cream, coffees, meals and catch-ups with friends and family over the years.

In a heartfelt farewell message posted on social media, staff at the Percy Street branch thanked customers for their loyalty and support.

The team said: "To all our lovely customers,

"As we say goodbye, we just want to take a moment to thank every one of you for your support, kindness and loyalty. It's been a pleasure serving you, having a chat, sharing a smile and being part of so many people's days.

"Whether you popped in for a quick coffee, enjoyed a meal with family, met friends for a catch-up or simply stopped by to say hello, you have all been part of what made Mark Toney special to us.

"We'll miss seeing your familiar faces, hearing your stories and welcoming you through the doors. It's the people we've met along the way who have made our time here so memorable, and we're incredibly grateful for that.

"From all of us on the team, thank you for the memories, the laughs and the lovely moments we've shared. We'll look back on our time here with fondness and wish every one of you all the very best for the future."

The message was signed by staff members Gail, Debbie, Kim, Tamuna, Joanne, Katy, Joss and Andrew.

News of the closure prompted an outpouring of sadness online, with many customers sharing memories of visits stretching back decades.

One customer wrote: "I'm 72 and it's been a part of my life since childhood, teens and adulthood. I'm devastated that it's closed.

"The staff weren't just staff; they were friends, confidants and a listening ear to many of us. There will always be fond memories of them and the ice cream."
