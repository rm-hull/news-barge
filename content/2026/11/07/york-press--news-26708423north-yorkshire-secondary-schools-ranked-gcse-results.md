The highest-performing secondary schools across North Yorkshire have been revealed in the latest Department for Education GCSE performance tables.

New provisional figures for the 2025/26 academic year show how 41 secondary schools across the county performed using the Government's Attainment 8 measure, which tracks pupils' average achievement across eight GCSE qualification slots.

Ripon Grammar School emerged at the top of the rankings, recording an Attainment 8 score of 69.9.

The selective school finished ahead of Skipton Girls' High School, which achieved 67.5, while Ermysted's Grammar School, also in Skipton, completed the top three with 64.3.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21673556.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An image of a school *(Image: PA MEDIA)*

The three schools operate selective admissions arrangements, meaning pupils are admitted on the basis of academic ability.

Among non-selective secondary schools, St Aidan's Church of England High School in Harrogate recorded the highest Attainment 8 score, achieving 59.4 and placing fourth overall.

Harrogate Grammar School followed in fifth place with 56.0, while Lady Lumley's Academy in Pickering recorded 54.5.

St Francis Xavier School in Richmond was seventh with 54.4, narrowly ahead of Ryedale School, which achieved 53.5.

Outwood Academy Easingwold recorded 53.2, while St John Fisher Catholic Academy in Harrogate completed the top ten with 52.6.

The figures show that 12 schools across North Yorkshire recorded Attainment 8 scores above 50.

Tadcaster Grammar School achieved 52.0, followed by Sherburn High School on 51.3.

Elsewhere, Brayton Academy in Selby recorded 50.0, while Scalby Academy in Scarborough achieved 49.9.

Scarborough University Technical College followed with 49.6, ahead of Malton School on 49.1 and Outwood Academy Ripon on 49.0.

In the Richmondshire area, Richmond School and Sixth Form College recorded 46.7, while Stokesley School achieved 47.7.

Further down the rankings, Whitby School recorded 39.8, Harrogate High School achieved 39.5 and Nidderdale High School scored 39.2.

Filey Academy recorded 38.9, while Settle College achieved 38.7.

Graham School in Scarborough recorded 38.2, Northallerton School and Sixth Form College achieved 37.9 and Barlby High School scored 37.7.

Risedale School recorded 33.1, while George Pindar School in Scarborough had the lowest published Attainment 8 score among the 41 schools, at 32.1.

Attainment 8 measures pupils' average achievement across eight qualification slots, including English, mathematics, three English Baccalaureate subjects and three further approved qualifications.

English and mathematics are double-weighted in the calculation, provided the relevant entry requirements are met.

Higher scores indicate stronger average attainment across the qualifications included in the measure, but they do not necessarily mean a school provides a better overall education.

The measure also does not directly show how much progress pupils have made during secondary school.

This distinction is particularly important when comparing selective grammar schools with non-selective comprehensive schools, which have different admissions arrangements and pupil intakes.

Independent and special schools have been excluded from the main rankings because of differences in their educational provision and pupil populations.

The Department for Education figures are provisional for 2025/26 and remain subject to revision.
