A North Yorkshire construction installation business has entered liquidation with debts approaching £300,000.

NJW Composite Doors of 77 Brooke Street, Selby, was incorporated in 2022 and entered liquidation on September 30.

Leeds-based Smith and Barnes Insolvency Practicioners were appointed as liquidator.

Companies House records Neil Ian Wakemen as NJW Composite Doors’ as its sole company director.

A liquidators’ report known as a statement of affairs highlights the financial situation of the company.

The statement said the business had no assets and no preferential creditors – those who are paid first.

However, HMRC was a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next – and was owed £20,000 in VAT and £10,000 in PAYE.

There were unsecured creditors owed £257,144.91 in total.

There were two trade and expense creditors owed £65,098.96 between them.

Six banks and financial institutions were owed £172,045.95 between them.

HMRC was owed a further £20,000.

This gave a total deficiency of £287,144.91.

With the company having issued and called up capital of £100, this gave a final estimated deficiency of £287,244.91.
