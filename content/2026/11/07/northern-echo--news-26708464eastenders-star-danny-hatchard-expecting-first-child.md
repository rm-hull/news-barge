EastEnders star Danny Hatchard is expecting his first child with fiancée Kerry Scouler.

The 35-year-old actor, best known for his role as Lee Carter in EastEnders, shared the news on Instagram with a playful nod to the show’s iconic theme tune, writing: "Only gone and got her up the duff duff duff duff duff duff duff duff duff..."

Ms Scouler also posted ultrasound photos on Instagram, revealing they are expecting a baby girl in January.

She wrote: "And somewhere along the way, our love became someone’s whole life. Baby girl coming January ’27 [ribbon emoji] (sic)"

The announcement comes just a month after the couple revealed their engagement during a trip to Amsterdam.

Ms Scouler shared a photo of her engagement ring on Instagram at the time, writing: "You and me forever? Don't mind if 'I do' (sic)"

Earlier this year, it was confirmed Mr Hatchard would be returning to EastEnders alongside Maddy Hill, who plays his on-screen sister Nancy Carter.

Speaking about his return to the long-running soap, Mr Hatchard said: "I’m absolutely over the moon to be returning to EastEnders as Lee Carter.

"Albert Square has always felt like a home away from home, so I’m proper buzzing to be back grafting with the Enders family."

Ms Hill also expressed her excitement about rejoining the cast.

She said: "I am so excited to be returning to Enders for a short stint and stepping back into Nancy’s shoes (or basketball shorts!).
