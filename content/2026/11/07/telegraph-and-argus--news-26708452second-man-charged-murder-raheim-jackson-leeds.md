A second man has been charged with murder as detectives continue to investigate the death of Raheim Jackson in Leeds.

Louis Jolaoso, 28, of Sowod Street, Leeds, has been charged with murdering Raheim Jackson on October 24. He is due to appear before Leeds Magistrates' Court today (November 7).

The latest development comes less than a week after a first suspect was charged in connection with the investigation.

## Second suspect charged

On November 2, Michael Gyedu, 26, of Rathmell Road, Leeds, was charged with the murder of Raheim Jackson.

Detectives have since continued their enquiries, leading to further arrests and the latest charge.

A 28-year-old man was arrested in Nottingham on November 5 on suspicion of murder before subsequently being charged.

## Investigation remains ongoing

A 39-year-old man arrested on suspicion of murder on November 4 was later released on bail pending further enquiries. A separate 26-year-old man arrested on suspicion of murder on November 1 was released under investigation.

The investigation is being led by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team, which continues to appeal for information.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates said: "We continue to support Raheim's family through this unimaginably difficult time.

"Our enquiries continue but I would like to take this opportunity to thank the public for their support in this investigation so far. Their help has been incredibly valuable."

## Appeal for information

Officers are continuing to ask anyone with information that could assist the investigation to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police via 101 or its live chat service, quoting reference 13260606745. Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online.
