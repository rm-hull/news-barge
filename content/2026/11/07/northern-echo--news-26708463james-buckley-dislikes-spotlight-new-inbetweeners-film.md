James Buckley has revealed he doesn’t enjoy the spotlight that comes with filming the new Inbetweeners film.

The 39-year-old actor, who plays Jay Cartwright in the hit E4 sitcom and its film spin-offs, recently admitted he prefers working on quieter projects away from public scrutiny.

Speaking on the latest episode of his podcast At Home With The Buckleys, which he co-hosts with his wife Clair, Mr Buckley said: "I like doing stuff like this (the podcast) that a few people like but no one really gives a s\*\*\* about.

"I like sort of pottering about doing my bits and bobs.

"Doing The Inbetweeners films, the whole level of paparazzi and people turn up (saying), 'What are they doing? What are they getting up to?'

"And then (there are) lots of people online making videos going, 'I think I know what the story's about', and people speculating.

"I like it when people don't really take any notice of what I'm up to."

The cast was recently photographed filming in Magaluf.

Mr Buckley also shared that he and his co-stars — Joe Thomas (Simon Cooper), Simon Bird (Will McKenzie), and Blake Harrison (Neil Sutherland) — are feeling the effects of age on set.

He said: "People's knees are hurting these days and stuff.

"Blake was complaining about his neck the other day, bless him.

"We're all a lot older and it's become painfully aware of how much time has passed."

Reflecting on his life before the show’s success, Mr Buckley recalled working as a kitchen fitter and struggling financially, even saying he couldn’t always afford a travel card.
