Police have temporarily made it an offence for people to conceal their faces as a series of planned protests take place today.

Northumbria Police said it has been informed of pre-arranged demonstrations in Newcastle and has introduced the measure within the city.

The restriction will be in force between 10am and 5pm.

Under the order, anyone taking part in the protests who conceals their identity within the designated zone, could be committing an offence.
