Essential repairs are set to begin on a stretch of road following a landslip.

North Yorkshire Council has announced three weeks of works at Blue Bank in Sleights, starting November 16, to address the damage.

Temporary traffic lights will be in place for the duration of the repairs, with no pedestrian access permitted.

A council spokesperson said: "This work is part of an extensive works programme and therefore some adjustments to this start date or the duration of the works may be necessary should unforeseen circumstances such as significant weather events or emergencies elsewhere on the highway network arise."

The repairs follow a week-long closure of Blue Bank just weeks ago so the council could carry out "annual maintenance".

In letters sent to residents and businesses, the council said it will try to maintain access wherever possible, though there may be times when entry is restricted.

Drivers using the route are being urged to remain patient, particularly as heavy vehicles may cause longer queues when climbing the steep incline from a full stop.
