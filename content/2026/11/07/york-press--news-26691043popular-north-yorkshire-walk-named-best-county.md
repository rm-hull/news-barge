## North Yorkshire walk with England's 'finest' view named best in the county

The area has one of England's "finest" views. *(Image: Getty)*

England is a great place for walking thanks to its vast countryside that you can spend hours exploring.

The Telegraph has revealed its best walks in every county in England ranging from parks surrounding famous estates to long hikes along the coast.

North Yorkshire is lucky to have loads of untouched areas of the countryside that are open to the public such as the Yorkshire Dales and the North York Moors.

If you are struggling to decide where to take a walk, you might want to try Sutton Bank, which was named the best walk in North Yorkshire.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659825.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can also ride bikes in the area. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

## What is Sutton Bank like?

On the area, The Telegraph said: “The vet James Herriot once declared the view from Sutton Bank to be ‘the finest in England’.

“It’s certainly one of the most accessible.

“From the visitor centre, a short, wheelchair-friendly track leads to a ramped, raised lookout, from where you can gaze across the Vales of Mowbray and York.

“Alternatively, a longer accessible – if bumpier – out-and-back trail follows the edge of the 300m-high escarpment south to the head of the White Horse of Kilburn, with fine views all the way.”

You can start your walk at the Sutton Bank National Park Centre and follow one of the many trails on offer.

There are easy walking trails as well as the Sutton Bank cliff edge family cycle route and a wheelchair-accessible path to a “sensational viewpoint”.

The area is incredibly popular and has a rating of 4.3 out of 5 on Tripadvisor.

To keep children entertained on a walk, you can grab a family trail sheet from the centre for just £1.

There is also a play area where children can let off some steam with treehouses, a stone castle, and a large sandpit.

After a long walk, you can head to the In The Wilde café that serves up a range of brunch favourites as well as homemade cakes and freshly baked scones.
