Police are appealing for help to find a woman who has been reported missing from the Holmfirth area.

Denise is missing and there are concerns for her welfare. Officers are urging anyone who may have seen her or knows where she is to come forward.

## **Description released**

Denise is described as a mixed-race Caribbean woman, approximately 163cm tall with a medium build.

She has black afro hair and is believed to be wearing a cream knitted jumper, dark tracksuit bottoms and a dark blue furry coat.

## **Concerns for welfare**

Police say there are concerns for Denise's welfare and are asking members of the public to help trace her.

Anyone who has seen her since she was reported missing, or who has information about where she may be, is asked to contact officers.

Anyone with information or sightings is asked to call 101 quoting log 670 06/11/2026..
