Amanda Owen's daughter Edith has closed the family's popular cream tea van at Ravenseat Farm for the year.

The 18-year-old, who grew up in front of millions of viewers on Our Yorkshire Farm, has played a leading role in bringing back the family's much-loved tradition of serving refreshments to walkers and visitors.

However, the venture, based at the remote farm in Upper Swaledale, closed for the season last weekend, with visitors now having to wait until a future reopening to enjoy its famous cream teas.

The seasonal closure marks another chapter for the Owen family, whose lives continue to be documented in the Channel 4 programme Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21631747.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Amanda and Clive Owen's children at the cream tea van *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The farm, situated near Keld in the Yorkshire Dales National Park, lies close to the Coast to Coast walking route and has attracted visitors keen to experience the landscape made famous by the television programme.

The family's refreshments became particularly popular with walkers making their way through the remote countryside.

After the tradition was suspended, Edith helped bring it back, taking on a more prominent role in running the venture with support from her siblings.

Amanda previously spoke proudly about her daughter's efforts to revive the business, explaining how a simple idea had developed into something much bigger.

Speaking at an event in Northallerton, the Yorkshire Shepherdess said: "It's an empire built on cups of tea really, where any good business starts."

The cream tea van has also featured in Our Farm Next Door, which follows Amanda, former husband Clive and their nine children as they restore the neighbouring farmhouse Anty John's.

The programme has shown the children taking on greater responsibilities as they grow older, with Edith's work at the cream tea van becoming one of the family's more recognisable ventures.

While the refreshments business has now finished for 2026, Ravenseat remains a working sheep farm and an important part of the Owen family's life.

The closure comes as the family's television future has been secured for another two series.

On November 2, Channel 4 announced that Our Farm Next Door would return for a fifth and sixth series, comprising ten further hour-long episodes.

The broadcaster confirmed that the new programmes would continue following the family's restoration of Anty John's, alongside life at Ravenseat.

They will also feature Edith, Violet and their sisters reopening the cream tea van, while brothers Sid and Miles take on more farming responsibilities.

Channel 4 said the programme had become More4's highest-rated show for three years.
