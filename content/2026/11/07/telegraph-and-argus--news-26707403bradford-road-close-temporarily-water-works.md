LAPAGE Street, Bradford, is to close temporarily for water works.

Bradford Council stated that from 9.30am to 3.30pm on November 17, the road will be closed between No. 101 and No. 113.

An alternative route for vehicles will be in place via the unaffected section of Lapage Street, Carrington Street, Killinghall Road, and Rochester Street.

The road will remain open to pedestrians throughout.

The closure may be extended for up to 18 months if necessary.
