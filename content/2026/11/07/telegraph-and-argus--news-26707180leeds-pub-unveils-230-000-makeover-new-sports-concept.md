A pub has reopened following a £230,000 refurbishment and is now relaunched under a new sports bar concept.

The Mustard Pot in Leeds relaunched on November 4 as the next local Marston’s pub to be relaunched under the new sports concept, Grandstand.

The reopening was marked by a visit from Homeless Street Angels, a charity based in Leeds, who were invited to pull the first pint as the pub’s nominated Community Hero.

Zoe Eastwood, operations manager at Marston’s, said: "We will have some amazing new technology, food and drink offers and events, and we want as many local people to be featured on our walls as possible.

"We can’t wait to welcome all our regulars and some new faces in, to see the new and improved The Mustard Pot."

The pub is calling on the community to help decorate its walls with photographs and memorabilia, celebrating sports teams and figures from the Leeds area.

Marston’s picked The Mustard Pot as one of the pubs to receive the Grandstand makeover due to its involvement with local sports teams, community groups, and charities.

The Mustard Pot plans to screen major sporting events including football, rugby, cricket, tennis, F1, darts, and horse racing.
