A County Durham pub is preparing to welcome customers back this weekend after spending several weeks undergoing a refurbishment.

The Church Mouse, near Chester-le-Street, has announced it will reopen today (Saturday, November 7), with staff saying they "can't wait" to welcome customers through the doors once again.

Sharing the news on social media, the pub said: "The wait is nearly over."

The venue added that it had been "busy behind the scenes" during the closure and was looking forward to welcoming both regular customers and first-time visitors back when it reopens.

The Church Mouse is located on Front Street in Waldridge and forms part of the Vintage Inns chain.

Alongside food and drink, it has built a reputation as a popular meeting place for local residents and visitors to the area.

The pub currently holds a rating of 3.8 out of five on TripAdvisor, where several visitors have praised its atmosphere, food and customer service.

One recent reviewer described it as a "lovely country pub" and thanked manager Mark and his team for a "fabulous visit", adding: "We can't wait to return."

Another customer praised the venue's "pleasant ambience and varied menu", while highlighting the efforts of staff member Holly during a busy lunch service.

TripAdvisor reviewers have also highlighted the pub's welcoming atmosphere, with many citing friendly staff and comfortable surroundings among the reasons they would return.

The latest reopening announcement has already generated excitement online, with customers responding to the post saying they were looking forward to visiting once the doors reopen.
