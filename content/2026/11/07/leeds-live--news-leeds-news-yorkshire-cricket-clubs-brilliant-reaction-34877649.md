# Yorkshire cricket club’s brilliant reaction to 'getting it wrong' after almost killing animal

## 'We are holding our hands up - we got this wrong and we are truly sorry'

A cricket club has been praised for it's "amazing" reaction after a hedgehog was rescued from a net, saying they 'got it wrong'.

On Wednesday, November 4, the Prickly Pigs Hedgehog Rescue in Otley reported members of the public had rescued a hedgehog from a net at Otley Cricket Club. The rescuers cut the animal free after the netting had got wrapped around its neck and legs.

The hedgehog - named Picard - was hypothermic, exhausted and hungry, but has now started to recover after the rescuers were able to cut the remaining net from his neck and legs.

The rescue offered advice to the cricket club, stating: "This isn’t the first and won’t be the last if nothing is done.

"It doesn’t take long just to either pack away netting or pin it up after it’s been used. Hedgehogs are now an endangered animal in the UK with their status on The Red List being ‘vulnerable to extinction’. If you would like any further advice and help about making your grounds hedgehog friendly then please get in touch."

Otley Cricket Club has been praised for it's response to the hedgehog rescue after it said: "We are holding our hands up: we got this wrong, and we are truly sorry."

It said the net being left out was an "easy oversight" at the end of the season, but it had "dangerous consequences". The cricket club said good intentions "aren't enought" and said it would take action.

Otley Cricket Club said: "We have taken immediate action: all our netting is tied high off the ground for winter, and we have updated our protocols so players, coaches, and staff ensure nets are packed away or pinned up after every single session.

"We are sharing our mistake openly because we want other sports clubs, cricket grounds, and football facilities to check theirs today. It only takes two minutes to walk the pitch, lift a trailing net off the grass, or pack it away. It costs nothing, but it could save a life."

A number of people have taken to social media to praise the club for its response, with one saying: "Amazing handling of a sad story Otley Cricket Club. Please consider fundraising for them or giving a one off donation as recognition of their work. Your words are wonderful but would be even better if supported by finances".

Another said: "I’m always impressed when an organisation can hold its hand up that they made a mistake AND make steps to rectify it. Well done." One other wrote: "It’s a refreshing change when people can admit they made a mistake, apologise, make changes and risk putting themselves in the firing line to mention on social media so others do t make the same mistake.

"My goodness what a balm to this weary soul you are."

Yet another said: "Thank you so much for sharing, discussing and please do share with other cricket clubs too. Important to highlight about football nets in people’s gardens and pitches too."

After their quick action, the charity posted an update on Picard, saying: "We just wanted to say a massive thanks to Otley Cricket Club, whose response has been exemplary from start to finish. They’ve tied up all their nets for the winter and asked all their players to lift the nets after use.

"We see dozens of entanglement injuries every year from sports clubs and garden netting, so thank you for helping to reduce this."
