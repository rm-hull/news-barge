# Raheim Jackson murder probe update as second man charged - full police statement

## A 28-year-old man is due to appear before Leeds Magistrates' Court today

A second man has been charged with murder following the death of Raheim Jackson in Leeds.

West Yorkshire Police said Louis Jolaoso, 28 and of Sowod Street, Leeds, has been charged with murdering Raheim Jackson on October 24. He will appear before Leeds Magistrates' Court today, November 7.

This comes after 26-year-old Michael Gyedu, 26 and of Rathmell Road, was charged with murder on Monday, November 2.

The police investigation into Raheim's death is set to continue.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates said: "We continue to support Raheim’s family through this unimaginably difficult time.

"Our enquiries continue but I would like to take this opportunity to thank the public for their support in this investigation so far. Their help has been incredibly valuable."

If you have any information which could assist the police in their investigation you can contact the police on 101 or by using live chat online, quoting reference number 13260606745. You can also contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or on their website.
