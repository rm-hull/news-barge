**Henry Nowak's family deserve truth and justice**

THE inquiry into the murder of Henry Nowak has raised serious concerns about accountability and transparency within our institutions.

The evidence heard so far suggests that Henry was failed by those who should have protected him. Instead of being believed, he was arrested and handcuffed while in obvious distress.

This inquiry is about more than one case. It is about ensuring that public bodies are held accountable when mistakes are made and that lessons are learned to prevent future tragedies. Henry's family deserve truth, justice, and the opportunity to grieve without having to fight every step of the way.

Let us hope the inquiry delivers the accountability that is so clearly needed.

*John Jones,*

*Russet House,*

*Birch Close,*
