Two teenagers have been arrested over a sword attack near a Catholic school in Gloucester, police said on Saturday.

A 19-year-old man was arrested overnight while an 18-year-old man handed himself in on Saturday morning, Gloucestershire police said, adding that both men are local and remain in custody.

Officers were called to Chalford Road, near to St Peter’s High School, in Tuffley, shortly after 8.50am on Friday.

It was reported that two males – one in possession of a sword – had been seen chasing two 17-year-old boys.

![Officers were called to Chalford Road, near to St Peter’s High School, in Tuffley, shortly after 8.50am on Friday (file)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/07/10/01/Screenshot-2026-11-07-at-10-41-29.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

One boy sustained a “superficial wound” from the weapon while the second boy did not receive any physical injuries.

Armed police attended the scene to search for the suspects, who reportedly fled on foot.

Gloucestershire Police said a woman who had been arrested on Friday on suspicion of assisting an offender was released on police bail pending further inquiries.

Police enquiries are ongoing and Gloucestershire Police urged anyone with information who has not yet spoken with them to make contact.
