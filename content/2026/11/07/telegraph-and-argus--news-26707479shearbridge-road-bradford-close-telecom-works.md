A ROAD in Bradford will be temporarily closed for telecommunication works.

Shearbridge Road, from its junction with Great Horton Road to outside Shearbridge Depot, will be shut from 10pm on November 10, until 5am on November 11.

The closure is due to telecommunication works.

The restriction does not apply to vehicles used in connection with the works, and pedestrian access will be maintained.

Alternative routes for vehicles will be available via the unaffected section of Shearbridge Road, Great Horton Road, Horton Grange Road, and Woodhead Road.

The closure can remain in force for up to 18 months if necessary.
