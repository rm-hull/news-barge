A man has denied murdering his sister in a crash on the A1(M) near Darlington.

Elena Chiriac, 22, died at the scene of the crash between a silver VW Golf and silver Ford Transit near Barton, on October 4.

Her brother, Florin Chiriac, 31, denied murder, wounding with intent, and dangerous driving when he appeared at Leeds Crown Court on Friday (November 6).

Cristi Chiriac, 25, was also charged with murder and pleaded not guilty to the offence on Friday.

Both men, of Hampstead Road, Newcastle, are due to stand trial at Leeds Crown Court from March 22.

They will next attend court for a case management hearing on December 11.

Elena Chiriac, originally from Romania, died of head injuries, an inquest opening heard.

She was pronounced dead at the scene after a silver Ford Transit and a silver Volkswagen Golf collided shortly after 6pm on October 4.

Assistant Coroner Mark Armitage gave Elena Chiriac’s cause of death as traumatic head injuries.

A 52-year-old woman from Newcastle was arrested on suspicion of encouraging or assisting the commission of an assault offence but was later released on bail pending further inquiries.

A 24-year-old man who was arrested following the collision has been released with no further action.
