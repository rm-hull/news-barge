Police attended St Peter's High School in Tuffley, Gloucester, at 08:50 GMT on Friday

**Two men have been arrested after a teenager was injured in an attack involving a sword near two schools in Gloucester.**

St Peter's High School and Willow Primary Academy, in Tuffley, were put into lockdown after the incident at 08:50 GMT on Friday. Armed police attended the scene to search for the suspects who reportedly fled on foot.

Police said a 17-year-old suffered a wound "believed to have been caused by the sword".

A 19-year-old, from Gloucester, was arrested overnight on suspicion of assault occasioning actual bodily harm and affray. An 18-year-old, also from Gloucester, was held on suspicion of possession of an offensive weapon, assault occasioning actual bodily harm and affray.

Both men remain in police custody for questioning.

Two men, one with a sword, had been seen chasing two boys, according to police on Friday.

Police said the 17-year-old suffered a "superficial wound believed to have been caused by the sword".

The other boy involved was not injured.

The incident happened close to two Gloucester schools

Gloucestershire Police also said that a woman who had previously been arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender had been released on police bail.

At the school gates outside St Peter's High School on Friday, a pupil called Jimmy told the BBC the situation was "really scary" and that they had been told to lock doors and turn lights off.

"At the time I was scared. My heart was racing," he said.

Jimmy said pupils were locked in their classrooms for several hours.
