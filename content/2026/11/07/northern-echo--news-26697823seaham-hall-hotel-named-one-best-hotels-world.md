An “immaculate” hotel along the Durham coastline has been named one of the best hotels in the world.

La Liste has revealed the best hotels found across the world, with many being found around the UK.

La Liste describes the hotels that have made it onto its list as offering “exceptional stays”.

Found inside a Georgian country house on the clifftops of the coast, Seaham Hall made it onto the list with a score of 93.5 out of 100.

## What is Seaham Hall like?

Seaham Hall has 24 suites in the main house that “pair period character with calm, contemporary comfort”.

The nine lodges have their own private hot tubs and offer guests a bit more privacy while only being a short walk away from the spa facilities.

During your visit, you can visit Serenity Spa, which features both indoor and outdoor pools, wellness facilities, and a range of treatments.

After spending some time at the spa, you can head to Geko for a meal where you can enjoy a range of items from small plates like duck satay and scallops to mains like steak, tonyu ramen, and stir-fried udon noodles.

The Dining Room is another option for a sit-down meal with “seasonal foodie favourites to the freshest seafood” on offer.

The restaurant has plenty of dishes available, including baked potato gnocchi, charcuterie boards, and seabass.
