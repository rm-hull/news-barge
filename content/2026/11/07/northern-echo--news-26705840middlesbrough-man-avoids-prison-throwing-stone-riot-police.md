A man who threw a stone at police during the rioting that engulfed Middlesbrough two years ago has narrowly avoided prison.

Teesside Crown Court heard that CCTV showed Shabaz Vaseen picking up the stone while with counter-protesters near the junction of Derwent Street and Outram Street.

The 28-year-old also picked up a stick, but was not seen using it during the disorder.

Prosecutor Saba Shan said Vaseen became involved in the rioting that erupted in the town in the wake of the murder of three young girls in Southport in July 2024.

She said: “He appears to be with a crowd of counter protesters. He picks up something from the ground and then minutes later he throws a stone towards the police line.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18384598.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Violent thugs brought destruction and terror to the community of Middlesbrough in August 2024. *(Image: The Northern Echo, Contributor)*

Miss Shan said there was no evidence the stone hit anyone when the protest turned violent on Sunday, August 4.

Vaseen, of Abingdon Road, Middlesbrough, admitted violent disorder.

His lawyer, Sophie Johnstone, said Vaseen was walking home when he became involved in the disorder and never intended to take an active role.

She said: “He has thrown a single stone, a very minimal, perhaps an underwhelming, act of participation given the wide state of unrest.

“He accepts, nevertheless, that it was still wrong.

“He is sorry and wishes to express his remorse for getting involved.

“It’s not who he is. It was a terrible mistake which he will come to regret for some time to come.”

He said: “These courts deal with that sort of behaviour severely. The throwing of a stone at police, even if it doesn’t hit them, is an offence so serious that it crosses the custody threshold.

“I have been persuaded to suspend your sentence.”

Vaseen was also ordered to complete 100 hours of unpaid work and attend a six-month mental health treatment programme.

The wider disorder involved cars being set alight, windows being smashed and projectiles thrown at police, while officers urged the public to stay away from Middlesbrough.
