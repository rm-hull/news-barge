Step-free access is now available at Batley station.

The improvements come as part of the Transpennine Route Upgrade (TRU) and include a new footbridge with lifts, replacing the old subway and providing fully accessible journeys between platforms for the first time.

Jonathan Hepton, project sponsor for TRU, said: "Customers at Batley will now have step-free access at the station for the first time following the construction of a brand-new footbridge with lifts.

"Improving accessibility across the Transpennine route is a core benefit of the programme, so this is another major milestone for us."

The upgrades follow similar work at Morley and Mirfield stations.

Further improvements at Batley are ongoing and include new waiting shelters, environmental enhancements, and the reopening of the station car park with three accessible parking spaces.

These works are expected to be completed by spring 2027.

Carl Rowland, TRU customer lead, said: "The opening of the new lifts and footbridge at Batley station is a fantastic milestone to celebrate for both Northern and the TRU programme.

"Our customers are now able to enjoy the benefits of an accessible, step-free station at Batley, meaning that they can travel with greater confidence and independence for many years to come."
