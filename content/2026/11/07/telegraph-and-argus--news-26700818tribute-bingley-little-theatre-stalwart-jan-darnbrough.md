A beloved member of a theatre community who dedicated more than 40 years to the stage has died.

Jan Darnborough, a long-serving member of Bingley Little Theatre (BLT), died on October 28.

Ron Hardy, president of BLT, said: "Jan was born and brought up in the Midlands, moving to Bingley to study at Bingley College and pursue a career in teaching.

"She joined Bingley Little Theatre in 1980 to appear as the ingenue in the musical 'Something's Afoot!'.

"She appeared in many plays and musicals, too many to mention, for both BLT and Bingley Amateurs and has fond memories of playing in 'Barefoot in the Park' opposite George Pickles."

She became a director and led the theatre’s studio committee for 30 years.

She later became chairman of BLT and was awarded Life Membership in 2017.

Mr Hardy said: "Her appointment as president in 2020 she felt was a great honour and privilege, even though as a theatre group like the rest of a country, she was powerless in the face of the pandemic.

"The membership feels the loss of such a valued member greatly, yet that is as nothing that her loss means to her family."
