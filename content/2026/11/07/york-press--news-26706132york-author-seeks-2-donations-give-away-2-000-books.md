An independent children’s author from York is calling on the public to help her share the magic of reading with children who need it most.

Karen Langtree, an independent children’s author of 20 years from Strensall is winding down her business and hopes to donate up to 2,000 picture books to children this Christmas through a wide network of charities.

She is appealing to the public for donations of £2 per book to cover the production costs and make those gifts possible.

So far, 380 books have been earmarked for distribution to good causes including the Salvation Army Christmas toy appeal, and Karen said she has successfully approached St Leonard's Hospice, Martin House Children's Hospice and York Hospital.

Donations can be made via a gofundme page the author created.

She said: "I thought it would be a good idea to ask if anyone out there could donate the cost price of a book or books so that I can afford to donate books to children this Christmas for the price they cost me to produce.

"These books retail at £6.99 but we want to give a book for every £2 donated, to schools, Salvation Army, local hospitals, hospices, Candlelighters, food banks and other charities that can distribute them to children who will benefit from having books they might otherwise not be able to afford or books that might cheer them up through a difficult time."

The books are aimed at children under seven and include three different Christmas-themed titles.

Andrew Vertigan of York Salvation Army said he was deeply blessed by Karen's kindness as a bundle was handed over at Lidgett Grove Methodist Church in Wheatlands Grove, Acomb.

He said it would distribute the books through schools, social services and local groups.
