Two people have each won more than £377,000 in a postcode lottery.

The pair, from Keighley, took home the top prize of £377,224 each after their postcode, BD20 6JL, was drawn in the Postcode Lottery’s Millionaire Street prize on Saturday, November 7.

A total of 659 other residents in the BD20 6 area also won prizes, ranging from £335 to £2,010 depending on how many tickets they played with.

Judie McCourt, a Postcode Lottery ambassador, said: "We’re back in Yorkshire with more winners.

"Congratulations to the two players who have won the biggest share of our weekly £1m prize, especially nice with Christmas just around the corner.

"And, of course, their win means that hundreds of others in the area have also picked up prizes, too."

Keighley is located just over three miles from Haworth, the village made famous as the home of the Brontë sisters.

Ms McCourt said: "This is an area with a rich literary history and now a new chapter can be written with this £1m win."

The prize also brings a boost to nearby charities, with a portion of lottery proceeds supporting good causes in the area.

The Sangat Community Centre in Keighley has been awarded £60,000.

The centre offers a range of services including a day centre for older people, study support for children, a job club, IT and exercise classes, immigration and welfare advice, and programmes for Asian adults with learning disabilities.

It also runs a Sunday club for children aged six to 16 and holiday activity schemes.

Keighley Creative, another local charity, has received £55,000.

The organisation works with artists, communities, and businesses to deliver creative projects throughout the town, including the annual Keighley Arts and Film Festival.

The Postcode Lottery costs £12.50 a month to play and there are guaranteed winners every day.

Players are automatically entered into all draws using their chosen postcode.

So far, players of the lottery have raised more than £1.9 billion for thousands of charities and local good causes.

This prize was promoted on behalf of the Postcode Education Trust, which supports organisations including Book Aid International, Global Fund for Children and Right To Play UK.

Full details of all available prizes can be found on the Postcode Lottery website.

Players can also check if their postcode is a winner on the lottery’s results page.
