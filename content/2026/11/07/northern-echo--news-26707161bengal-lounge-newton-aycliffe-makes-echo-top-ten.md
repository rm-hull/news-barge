An “excellent and authentic” Indian restaurant has made the top ten of The Northern Echo’s Best Curry House 2026.

Bengal Lounge, in Woodham, Newton Aycliffe, first opened in the County Durham town in 2010 and has been run by Ali Bahar for the last 13 years.

The 49-year-old said the popular venue continues to have a loyal customer base due to its “good food and clean restaurant”.

And now, Bengal Lounge is preparing to take on nine of the other top curry houses in the region as part of the Echo’s Best Curry House competition.

Ali said it is nice to be nominated: “Bengal Lounge has been going for more than ten years, and I started in 2013. It is good to be shortlisted.

“Business is okay - we are just surviving, not bad and not good. One week it is quiet and one week it is busy, it is the whole industry.”

Ali said the venue’s best dishes include the chicken tikka masala and the garlic chilli chicken tikka: “We get lots of good feedback.

“We have very loyal customers, we get a lot of people coming back because our restaurant is nice and clean and the food is good as well.”

Bengal Lounge currently boasts a 4.4 rating on Google, garnering praise for its “fantastic” food with “great quality and fresh flavours”.

One person said: “Nice restaurant with very friendly staff and a nice selection on the menu. Both tried something different and enjoyed it.”

Bengal Lounge is one of ten venues nominated for Best Curry House 2026, with others including The Indian Cottage, Crook, Babul’s, in Darlington, and Spice Island, Darlington.

Readers will decide the winner in a public vote, and we will be featuring each of the businesses nominated in the next two weeks.

Votes will be counted soon after voting closes.
