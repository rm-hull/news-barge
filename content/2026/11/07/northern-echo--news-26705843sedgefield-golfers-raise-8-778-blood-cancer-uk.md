A charity golf day held in memory of a County Durham man has raised nearly £9,000 for charity.

The event, organised in tribute to Steve Dovaston from Sedgefield, was held at Knotty Hill Golf Centre and raised £8,778.41 for Blood Cancer UK.

Mr Dovaston, who died earlier this year following a battle with blood cancer, had been an active member of the Sedgefield Golf Society.

Lewis Archer, one of the organisers, said: "The support across the board was amazing and we hope that the money raised will help to one day find a cure for blood cancer.

"Thanks to everyone who sponsored the event or took part, and to Knotty Hill Golf Centre for hosting the first Steve Dovaston Charity Golf Day so well.

"We look forward to welcoming everybody back again next year."

The event was put together quickly but saw strong support from sponsors and players.

Other organisers included Adam Dovaston, John Adcock, Neville Iceton, Michael Price, Dave Holloway and John Beamson.

The winning team on the day included Stephen Flockett, Daniel Newby, Marc Jemmeson and Sean Boynton.

Second place went to Michael, Robert and Thomas Adcock, along with Karl Thompson.
