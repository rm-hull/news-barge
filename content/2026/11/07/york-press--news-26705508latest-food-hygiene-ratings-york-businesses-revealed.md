The scores are in — the latest batch of food hygiene ratings for York restaurants, cafes, takeaways and businesses have been revealed.

Environmental health officers have been visiting businesses across the city to see if they are meeting hygiene requirements.

Based on guidelines set by the Food Standards Agency, inspectors look to see how clean the facilities are; what food safety management procedures are in place and how hygienic food handling is.

Ratings are then dished out from five to zero — with the higher score the better the result.

### Five — Very Good

Dragon House at 45 Foxwood Lane, York, rated on 23 October.

Forth Avenue Shop at 73 Fourth Avenue, York, rated on 29 October.

Inner Space Stations – Hull Road Service Station at 339–341 Hull Road, Heslington, York, rated on 26 October.

Lidl at Unit 2, Monks Cross Drive, Huntington, York, rated on 23 October.

Murphys of Acomb at 25 Front Street, York, rated on 26 October.

South Bank Social Club at 12 Ovington Terrace, York, rated on 28 October.

The Blossom Cafe at 2 Odeon Buildings, Blossom Street, York, rated on 26 October.

Thomas the Baker at 153B Tang Hall Lane, York, rated on 29 October.

Ti’Africa, rated on 26 October.

Town Coffee House at 5 Acomb Court, Front Street, York, rated on 26 October.

Vintage Rose at 11 College Street, York, rated on 4 November.

Businesses may have been reinspected since then, and this will be reflected in our next round-up.
