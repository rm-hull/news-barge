A traditional tearoom located in a tiny North Yorkshire village is attracting attention for combining homemade cakes and afternoon tea with an unexpected taste of Thailand.

Coxwold Tearoom, near Thirsk, looks much like a typical Yorkshire countryside café from the outside, but it is situated in a historic former schoolhouse and offers homemade cakes, scones, hot drinks and a peaceful garden where visitors can relax.

Alongside its traditional British menu, the business has developed an unusual speciality: authentic Thai food, afternoon teas with a Thai twist and dedicated Thai cooking classes.

The business is run by Keith and Pathitta, known as Jimmy, who took over in November 2024 after moving to North Yorkshire from Bangkok.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21679292.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Coxwold Tea Room *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

Jimmy previously ran a Thai cooking school in the Thai capital, while Keith worked in education management.

However, its latest owners have expanded the food offering and introduced new experiences for visitors.

The daytime menu includes traditional light lunches, snacks and homemade cakes, alongside monthly specials featuring Thai dishes.

Customers can also enjoy traditional afternoon tea or choose an alternative inspired by Thai cuisine.

And for those wanting something more substantial, the tearoom holds monthly Thai supper evenings featuring a three-course menu.

The owners have also introduced cooking classes, allowing visitors to learn how to prepare authentic Thai dishes.

Classes began in September 2025 and typically involve preparing three dishes before enjoying the finished food.

The business has also received positive feedback from customers on Tripadvisor.

One visitor, writing in June 2026 after staying overnight, praised the couple's hospitality and described an authentic Thai meal prepared by Jimmy as "fantastic".

Another customer, reviewing the business in March, praised its selection of light bites, friendly service and attractive village setting.

A further visitor highlighted the welcoming atmosphere and dog-friendly facilities.

The tearoom has indoor seating alongside a garden which the owners have worked to restore.

They have also reinstated a bridge across the beck, providing a connection towards the village hall and playground.

The village is known for its historic buildings and peaceful rural setting, offering a contrast to the busy tourist centres elsewhere in North Yorkshire.

And while visitors can still enjoy a traditional pot of Yorkshire tea and a homemade scone, the opportunity to sample Thai food makes this countryside tearoom different.

Coxwold Tearoom is at School House, Coxwold, North Yorkshire, YO61 4AD.
