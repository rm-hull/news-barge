VOLUNTEERS at Wesleys Community Hub and Café in Baildon are celebrating after receiving the King’s Award for Voluntary Service - the highest honour for local voluntary groups in the UK.

Equivalent to an MBE, the prestigious award recognises the contribution of around 120 volunteers who help make Wesleys a lifeline for the local community.

From modest beginnings as an occasional café four decades ago, Wesleys has grown into a thriving Community Hub and Café welcoming around 1,200 visits a week and providing a home for more than 40 community groups.

Gareth James, Chair of Wesleys Community Baildon Ltd, which oversees the work of the Community Hub and Café, said: “We are delighted to have been awarded the King’s Award for Voluntary Service.

"We express our gratitude to the many volunteers that make Wesleys such a special place; to our parent-body, Baildon Methodist Church; and for the support that we receive from Baildon Town Council, Bradford Council, and local Community and NHS organisations.”

From parent and baby groups to Scouts, Guides, dance classes and music-making, Wesleys brings generations together.

It also hosts activities for people with learning disabilities, fitness and wellbeing sessions, and opportunities for those feeling lonely to find friendship and support.

Sally Marney, a regular visitor to Wesleys, said: “When you walk in the door at Wesleys you are made to feel really welcome, and everyone is greeted with a smile.

"Wesleys is a lifeline - it helped me to join activities and make friends.

"It has helped me mentally and physically.”

Behind it all is the team of volunteers who help run activities, care for the building and garden, and keep the Café buzzing.

Around 70 Café helpers prepare food, serve customers and wash up, alongside three part-time cooks.

Their efforts make the Café a welcoming spot to catch up with friends and enjoy a meal.

Debs Molwuka, Founder of ‘Move With Debs’, who runs sessions at Wesleys, said: “Many of my class members talk about how isolated they felt before they joined us…

"Every single member stays on for lunch in the Cafe afterwards…

"Eating alone can be lonely.

"Instead, they sit down together to nutritious food, cooked with real care, at a very reasonable price.

"The friendships that have formed around those tables are wonderful to see.”

As a registered Warm and Welcome space, Wesleys offers free hot drinks and lunches to people struggling with the cost of living, plus a community pantry where visitors can take what they need for the day.

The award honours everyone who has volunteered at Wesleys, past and present, helping it grow into the busy Community Hub and Café that it is today.
