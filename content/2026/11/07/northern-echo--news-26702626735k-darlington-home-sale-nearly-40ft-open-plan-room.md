Set in the Blackwell area of Darlington, 47 Romanby Drive is a property that’s sure to impress, from its attractive facade all the way through its extensive accommodation which has been enhanced by its owners to give it a truly individual feel.

The tone is set the moment you step into the spacious reception hall, where a central staircase rises to a galleried landing.

At the heart of the home is a magnificent open-plan kitchen, dining and family room stretching nearly 40ft across the full width of the rear, its bifold doors opening onto a patio.

![47 Romanby Drive, Darlington](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675278.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Conveniently located for accessing Darlington town centre, this home in Blackwell is spacious and stylish *(Image: ANN CORDEY ESTATE AGENTS)*

This creates a wonderful flow between the indoors and outdoors, making the space perfect for alfresco dining and entertaining when the weather allows.

The kitchen is home to an array of stylish traditional-style fitted units with Silestone worktops, integrated AEG appliances and wood-effect tiled flooring, with spotlights completing its modern look.

A utility room with matching cabinetry adds valuable extra space.

To the front of the house the generous lounge – almost 20ft long – has a large bay window and a real-effect flame electric fire set within the chimney breast.

![47 Romanby Drive, Darlington](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675276.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Conveniently located for accessing Darlington town centre, this home in Blackwell is spacious and stylish *(Image: ANN CORDEY ESTATE AGENTS)*

A cloakroom plus a second generous bay-windowed reception room – currently used as a bar – complete the ground floor accommodation.

Upstairs, the principal bedroom is a wonderful retreat with a dressing area lined with sliding-door wardrobes and a luxurious en-suite featuring a walk-in double shower and twin basins.

A guest bedroom also has its own en-suite, and there are three further bedrooms – one ideal for use as a home office.

These are served by a family bathroom with a double-ended bath and a walk-in shower.

![47 Romanby Drive, Darlington](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21675279.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Conveniently located for accessing Darlington town centre, this home in Blackwell is spacious and stylish *(Image: ANN CORDEY ESTATE AGENTS)*

Outside, the south-facing rear garden is laid mainly to lawn, with a large patio.

There’s a lawned garden to the front too.

This property in Romanby Drive enjoys excellent links to the A66 and A1(M), making it particularly convenient for commuters.
