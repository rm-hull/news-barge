A 21-year-old man has died following a single-vehicle crash in Aberdeenshire.

Emergency services were called to the B9119, around two miles east of Tarland, at approximately 6.25pm on Friday, November 6, following reports of a crash involving a blackToyota Yaris.

A 21-year-old male passenger was pronounced dead at the scene.

The 26-year-old female driver and two other passengers, both aged 22, were taken to Aberdeen Royal Infirmary, where they were later discharged.

The road was closed while officers carried out crash investigation enquiries.

Police Scotland's Road Policing Unit has now launched an appeal for witnesses and dashcam footage as investigations into the circumstances of the crash continue.

Sergeant Sarah Ritchie said: “I would like to thank the members of the public that stopped to help at the scene.

“Enquiries are at an early stage, and I would appeal to anyone who witnessed the crash, or has any information that may assist, to contact us.

“I’d also ask drivers with dash-cams, who were in the area around the time, to review their footage for any images that could assist with our investigation.”

Anyone with information is urged to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2985 of Friday, November 6, 2026.
