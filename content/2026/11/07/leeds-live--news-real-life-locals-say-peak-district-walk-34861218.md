# Locals say Peak District walk is 'best place' to visit for phenomenal views

## Are you in the mood for a lovely autumnal walk this weekend? This spot located in the Peak District has been receiving glowing reviews – and it's not far from Leeds

A Peak District walk so 'breathtaking' has been praised online – and it's not far from Leeds. If you love a bit of nature, whether it's seeing Highland cows up close or taking on a walk named among UK's most beautiful, then we have just the spot for you.

Weekend activities are wonderful, but once you've exhausted the local options, it might be time to go to the drawing board. So if you're considering a trip to the Peak District, there's a particular location offering truly 'phenomenal views' worth investigating. The entire area can be explored in less than half a day, making it perfect for a short excursion.

A TikTok post from one visitor explained why Thor's Cave deserves a spot on your must-see list.

Nesma explained in the video: "This is a place I have to visit every time I'm in the UK, I absolutely love Thor's Cave and the drive there is just part of the experience. I love these country roads, we even came across the cutest cafe on the way and had a little pit stop.

"When we parked up I actually timed it how long it took us to get inside of the cave and it was 23 minutes exactly which is honestly a really easy hike. It's about 10 minutes of walking and then 15 minutes of going uphill and then stairs."

She noted the trickiest bit is actually entering the cave itself, as conditions can become slippery, so visitors should exercise caution. Nesma continued: "But once you're inside, the views are absolutely phenomenal and you get the best photos.

"I also love how different the view looks throughout the year and if you're going there from the Midlands, it doesn't even take half a day which I love so much it's such a quick trip. I highly recommend this spot to reconnect with nature."

### What to do at Thor's Cave

There's plenty on offer, from scaling the enormous Cavern Arch to walking or cycling along the Manifold Way. You might also delve further into the cave's interior, though you'll need a torch to properly explore the natural limestone chambers and narrow side passages.

Finally, make sure you appreciate the viewpoints surrounding and above the site. Head beyond the cave entrance along the higher trails to reach elevated spots offering spectacular views over the crags.

### Getting there

The journey from Leeds to the Peak District typically takes between 45 minutes and two hours, depending on your mode of transport. When heading specifically to Thor's Cave, expect roughly 1 hour and 58 minutes by car, or longer if travelling by train.
