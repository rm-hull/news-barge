Two sets of demonstrators clashed in York city centre this afternoon (Saturday).

One group had organised a “Let The Flags Of Britain Be Flown” gathering, described as “a peaceful flag flying event”. It began at 1pm in St Helen’s Square.

A counter protest, billed as “Stop the Far Right, No To Racism” gathered in the same place at noon.

There was a large police presence at the event.

At one point both groups were facing one another and chanting different slogans.

A number of people were also live-vlogging the proceedings.

***YorkMix*** spoke to some of those present.

Helen Meadows, a representative of Stand Up to Racism, said: “There’s a protest that was called for today at 1pm with the banner ‘Let the flags fly’, and we know from previous experience of this group that it’s not about patriotism.

“It’s about territorial warfare, using and misappropriating our nation’s flags, so that there’s a message of hate and unwelcome to anybody of ethnic minorities or migrants, to anybody who doesn’t fit their particular sort of mould.

“So a number of groups, including Stand Up to Racism, various union groups, York Palestine Assembly, and just a huge number of normal people from York, have joined together to fill St Helen Square to say that love conquers hate, and that we show welcome, compassion, unity, and a spirit of anti-racism in York.”

Shane, part of the “Let The Flags Fly” group, told us: “Today, the purpose of our gathering is to celebrate our national flags, history, heritage, and cultural identity.

“This is not a march or a confrontation. There will be no organised disruption, and our intention is to respect local businesses, residents, and visitors to York.

“We recognise that people have different opinions and beliefs, and we fully respect their right to express them peacefully.
