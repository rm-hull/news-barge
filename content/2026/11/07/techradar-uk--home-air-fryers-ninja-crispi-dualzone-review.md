### Ninja Crispi Dualzone: 30-second review

The Ninja Crispi DualZone is a fantastic looking and performing air fryer, boasting eight cooking modes, a clever basket design, and excellent controls. It’s also one of the most expensive air fryers on the market. Is it worth the money? I’ve put it to the test to find out.

Get it out the box and place it on your kitchen counter and you’ll be impressed by how much it doesn’t look like every other air fryer on the market. Out is the black, boxy design, and in is Ninja’s CleanCrisp glassware containers, giving the Crispi DualZone an airier presence. This is an air fryer you won’t necessarily want to hide away in a cupboard.

It’s not just looks though, as there’s clever design in play here too. The plastic and rubber base on the glass containers make them easy to manoevre and allow you to set them down anywhere when hot, without the need for a heat mat.

Then there are the eight cooking modes, offering excellent versatility and cooking performance. Air Fry and Max Crisp are staples you’d expect to see, but Bake, Roast, Re Crisp, Prove, Dehydrate and Keep Warm ensure the Crispi DualZone can be used for much more than simple air frying.

And if you have leftovers, there’s no need to decant into another container. Let the glassware cool, snap on one of the lids provided in the box and slide it into the fridge.

Sure it’s expensive, and it’s too heavy to be constantly moving in and out of storage, but the Ninja Crispi DualZone offers the premium air fryer experience its price tag demands.

### Ninja Crispi Dualzone: design

* **One of the best looking air fryers**
* **Clever basket design for easy use and placement**
* **Very heavy, so difficult to move around**

The Ninja Crispi DualZone doesn’t look like your traditional black box air fryer. You’re paying a premium for Ninja’s modern styling, and this is one of the best looking air fryers on the market.

The two CleanCrisp glassware containers and clever self-aligning, slide-in mechanism gives you a view of three of the four sides during cooking, allowing you to watch your food like a hawk to ensure it’s not burning.

And the two baskets have a clip-on plastic base with handles and rubber feet, not only allowing you to safely slide them in and out of the base, but also set them down immediately on the side without the need for a heat protective mat. I loved this, as it meant I didn’t have to think where I was placing a hot basket, I could take it straight to the table and pop it down.

Initially you might think the two 3.6L glassware baskets are a little on the small size, even if they do combine for a total cooking capacity of 7.2L. But when I started using the Crispi DualZone I quickly realised the depth of these baskets means you can fit a surprising amount into them.

Another nice touch is the two included lids for the baskets. It meant if I had leftovers, I could let the basket cool, pop the lid on, and slide it into the fridge for the next day - no need to dirty another container.

I also like the controls on this air fryer. You get physical buttons and a dial (unlike touch controls on some air fryers, which can get dirty and unresponsive), all of which are clearly labelled and easy to follow. Ninja has done a good job of simplifying the controls, with easy cooking mode, temperature, and time adjustments.

You also get Smart Finish and Match Cook functions, which come in useful when using both baskets for cooking. Smart Finish allows you to set the baskets for different cooking durations, but have them finish at the same time by delaying the start of the shorter cook.

Match Cook on the other hand allows you to copy the cook mode, temperature and duration from one zone to the other. This is handy when doing a large batch of the same food, as it means you only need to enter the cooking information once and then hit Match Cook to copy it over.

![Ninja Crispi DualZone air fryer with cooking containers removed](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/79uqRwCsYzUU44neAU55NN-1200-80.jpg)

Set the Crispi DualZone cooking, and depending on the cooking mode and duration selected, it’ll work out when it needs to alert you to shake or flip the food in the basket. It’s great Ninja does this automatically, as it means I didn’t have to remember to select a shake reminder function.

And there are plenty of modes to choose from; Air Fry, Max Crisp, Prove, Bake, Roast, Dehydrate, Keep Warm and Re Crisp. You get an instruction and recipe book included in the box that walks you through the various modes, provides tables for a wide range of food showing the best mode, time and temperature for each, as well as some tasty recipes to try.

What was a bit of a surprise however was just how heavy this air fryer is. The base has significant heft to it, and when both glassware baskets are inserted you’ve got a unit that’s over 14kg. If you’re someone who likes to pop their air fryer in a cupboard when not in use, the Crispi DualZone might not be the right choice for you. Still, at least it looks smart left out on the counter.

In the UK it’s available in three colors on the Shark Ninja website — Cyberspace (deep blue), Bone (off white) and Rose Quartz (pink) - while at time of writing Amazon UK has it in Bone and Stone Gold colorways.

A quick word of warning if you do end up buying the Ninja Crispi DualZone - the air fryer comes well packed with lots of protective packaging to remove. Make sure you've got it all off before switching it on.

### Ninja Crispi Dualzone: performance

* **Excellent cooking performance across modes**
* **Glassware baskets means it’s easy to check progress**
* **Some trial and error is needed for the perfect cook**

The Ninja Crispi DualZone is a powerful 2860W air fryer offering excellent cooking performance across its various modes.

I kicked things off in the default air fry mode. Homemade fries, garlic bread, and frozen onion rings all cooked well. The fries were crispy on the outside and fluffy in the middle after 26 minutes of air frying — although my personal preference would be to do them for another three or four minutes to really crisp up the outside.

## Buy it if

**You want excellent versatility** From the easy to use controls and eight different cooking modes, to the clever basket design and dishwasher-safe accessories, the Crispi DualZone can handle most foods, and handle them well.**You want a great looking air fryer** The Ninja Crispi DualZone is a really good looking air fryer. It can happily sit on the kitchen counter and not be an eyesore. You’ll be hard-pressed to find a better looking air fryer.**You want consistent, delicious results**

You might be paying a premium for this air fryer, but Ninja is one of the best manufacturers. It means you can be sure of excellent results — once you’ve mastered the timing and temperatures to suit your preferences.

## Don't buy it if

**You’re on a budget** The Ninja Crispi DualZone is one of the most expensive air fryers on the market. If you’re on a budget, there are plenty of dual basket air fryers that cost less — they might not look as good though.**You want to cook large items** While the Crispi DualZone offers a total cooking capacity of 7.2 liters, it’s split across two 3.6-liter baskets. That means a large chicken, sizable traybake, or a pizza won’t fit here.**You want to easily move and store your air fryer**

For all its great looks and cooking performance, the Ninja Crispi DualZone is very heavy. At just over 14kg when both glassware baskets are inserted, moving this appliance isn’t for the faint of heart. There are lighter units available for easy moving and storage.
