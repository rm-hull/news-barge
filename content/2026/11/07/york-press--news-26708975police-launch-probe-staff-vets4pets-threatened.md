POLICE are investigating reports that veterinary staff were threatened in North Yorkshire.

The incident took place at Vets4Pets in Harrogate at 10.30am on October 15.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We would like to speak to the man pictured as we believe he might have information that could assist our investigation.

"If you can help our appeal, please email [steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:steven.holloway@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

"Please quote reference 12260202908 when passing on information."
