At least six people have been killed and around a dozen wounded after a gunman opened fire in the historic centre of Kraków, Poland's second-largest city, according to police.

Amateur footage shows people fleeing as shots ring out, with cars passing through the area. Other videos capture the aftermath, showing emergency services responding to the scene, broken glass and overturned chairs scattered nearby. A helicopter can also be seen flying over the area.
