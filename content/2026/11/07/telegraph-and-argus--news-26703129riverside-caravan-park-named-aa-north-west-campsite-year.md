A FAMILY-run holiday park near the Yorkshire Dales has been named AA North West Campsite of the Year for 2026.

Located in High Bentham, Riverside Caravan Park received the honour as part of the AA Caravan & Camping Awards, which celebrate the UK’s best outdoor accommodation.

The award recognises the park’s "outstanding facilities, hospitality and commitment to guest satisfaction."

John Marshall, operations director at Riverside Caravan Park, said: "As a family-run business, guest experience is really important to us, so much so that you will usually find one of us around at all times, ready to make people's holidays and trips really special.

"To receive recognition like this from the AA means so much to us.

"It's a real testament to the hard work of our team, and we're incredibly proud to see Riverside recognised among some of the best campsites in the country."

Riverside Caravan Park first opened in March 1970 and has welcomed guests for more than 55 years.

The site is now managed by the third generation of the same family.

It offers touring pitches and holiday cottages, making it a popular base for exploring the Yorkshire Dales, Forest of Bowland, and surrounding countryside.
