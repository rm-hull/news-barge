# Second man charged with murder of Raheim Jackson in Leeds

## Detectives investigating the murder of Raheim Jackson in Leeds have charged a second man with his murder.

Louis Jolaoso, of Sowod Street in Leeds is charged with murdering Raheim on Saturday, October 24.

The 28-year-old, whose self-defined ethnicity is Black Caribbean including Black British was due to appear before Leeds Magistrates' Court today ( November 7).

![Raheim Jackson.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/27/17/14/Leeds-murder-victim-named.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Enquiries into Raheim’s death remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team.

Detective Chief Inspector Dan Bates said: “We continue to support Raheim’s family through this unimaginably difficult time.

“Our enquiries continue but I would like to take this opportunity to thank the public for their support in this investigation so far. Their help has been incredibly valuable.”
