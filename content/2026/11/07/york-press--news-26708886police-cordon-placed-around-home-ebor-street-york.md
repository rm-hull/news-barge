## Police cordon placed around home in quiet residential street in York

North Yorkshire Police has been called to an address in Ebor Street, York *(Image: Newsquest)*

A POLICE cordon has been placed around the entrance of a home in York.

North Yorkshire Police are currently in attendance at an address in Ebor Street.

One officer was in attendance at the home shortly after 4.30pm today (Saturday, November 7).




It is understood that the force has been at the propert since approximately 7am this morning.

A police van remains parked in the home's driveway.

The Press has contacted North Yorkshire Police for comment.
