A Papa Johns wants to stay open until 5am, according to a licencing application.

The pizza takeaway chain at 47 Commercial Street in Brighouse has applied for a late-night refreshment licence from Monday to Sunday from 11pm to 5am.

Brighouse town centre improvement works have been underway since January, with Commercial Street re-opening to through traffic early last month.

Footfall to businesses fell through the spring and summer months compared to the previous year, but the construction work done through the £19.1 million Town Deal is set to bring long-term improvements.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21681553.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Papa Johns on Commercial Street in Brighouse wants to open through the night (The Brighouse Deal) *(Image: The Brighouse Deal)*

The Town Deal town centre works aim to result in improvements which will encourage visitors to stay in the town and shop there for longer.

But businesses have been concerned about impact on footfall and trade and some aspects of future phases of the work – three are planned, with the first approaching completion.

This led the new Reform UK administration at Calderdale Council, which won control of the authority in May’s local elections, to pause the second phase of works for re-assessment.

The latest licencing application was made by Papa Johns’ parent company Magnum Trading WF Limited.

Calderdale residents have until 16 November to make representations.
