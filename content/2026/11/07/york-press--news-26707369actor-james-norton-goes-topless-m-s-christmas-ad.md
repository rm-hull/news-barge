NORTH Yorkshire actor James Norton has swapped the Santa suit for something a little less traditional.

The Happy Valley and Granchester star appears as a topless Father Christmas in the 2026 Marks & Spencer Christmas advert, which premiered on ITV’s This Morning on November 6.

Norton, who was brought up near Malton, stars alongside Lucy Punch as Mrs Claus and singer Katherine Jenkins, who delivers a festive performance in the advert.

Set in a reimagined North Pole, the advert sees Norton’s Santa go undercover to experience Christmas as an ordinary person.

He swaps his iconic red suit for a Christmas jumper and shaves off his beard on Mrs Claus’s suggestion, prompting a comedic topless moment that leaves her momentarily flustered.

Norton said: "It’s not every day you get asked to play Santa, let alone a Santa who immediately shaves off his beard! "MandS has been such a familiar part of Christmas for as long as I can remember, so getting to be part of its Christmas campaign feels really special."

The advert follows Santa as he leaves the North Pole to discover where the real magic of Christmas comes from.

He visits homes throughout the nation, enjoying festive food, smart outfits, and time spent with loved ones.

Throughout his journey, he realises that the true spirit of Christmas isn’t exclusive to the North Pole, but is found in families and communities everywhere.

Norton said he was drawn to the unique take on a familiar character.

He said: "I loved that this story takes such an iconic character and lets us see him differently. "He gets to experience Christmas like the rest of us, discovering that the real magic is in the food, traditions, and moments we share with the people we love. "It’s warm, funny, and full of Christmas spirit, and I’m very happy to have been briefly trusted with the red suit."

Lucy Punch, who plays Mrs Claus, said she was delighted to be part of the campaign.

Ms Punch said: "M&S and Christmas just go hand in hand, so it was an absolute joy to become part of that tradition this year.

"I loved that Mrs Claus is the one who gets this story moving.

"She gives him the nudge so he can get out there incognito and experience the magic of Christmas for himself."

She said the relationship between Mr and Mrs Claus added a playful charm to the story.

Ms Punch said: "There’s something very sweet and fun about their dynamic, and something quite lovely about the idea behind it: this idea that the magic you’re looking for is actually happening all around you."

The advert is set to a new version of Belinda Carlisle’s Heaven Is a Place on Earth, performed by Katherine Jenkins.

The track will be released following the launch of the advert.

Ms Jenkins said: "For me, Christmas has always been about coming home.

"It’s about family, traditions, and creating memories with the people you love most, and music is such a big part of that.

"Certain songs instantly become part of the soundtrack to our celebrations, so I was thrilled to record a new version of Heaven Is a Place on Earth for this campaign."

Viewers have already shared positive reactions on social media.

One Instagram user wrote: "I love it," while another said: "Now it’s CHRISTMAS!!"

A third commented: "Best Ever!"
