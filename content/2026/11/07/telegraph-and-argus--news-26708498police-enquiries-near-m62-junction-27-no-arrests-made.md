Police were reported to be on a busy road near the M62 late last night, prompting discussion on social media.

Witnesses reported seeing a police presence on the A650 Wakefield Road, close to Junction 27 and near Popeyes, at around 10pm on Friday, November 6.

Photos and reports of the incident were shared online, with motorists describing officers at the scene.

## What police have said

After being approached for comment, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said officers were carrying out enquiries linked to an existing investigation.

The spokesperson said: "Officers were conducting enquiries as part of an ongoing investigation. No arrests were made."

## What happened?

Reports of police activity began circulating on social media on Friday evening, with a number of people commenting on the presence of officers on the A650 near the motorway junction.

The circumstances surrounding the incident have not been disclosed and police have not released any further details regarding the nature of the investigation.

## No arrests made

West Yorkshire Police confirmed that no arrests were made in connection with the incident.

The force has not indicated whether further police activity linked to the enquiries is expected.
