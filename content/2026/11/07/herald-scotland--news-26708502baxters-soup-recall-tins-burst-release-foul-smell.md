Shoppers have been warned to bin cans of a popular Scottish soup over fears they could swell and burst, releasing a foul smell.

Scottish food giant Baxters has issued an urgent recall of its Chef Selections Cullen Skink after a manufacturing fault raised concerns that the contents could spoil.

The problem could cause tins of the traditional smoked haddock soup to swell and potentially rupture, according to the Food Standards Agency (FSA).

Anyone tempted to open a suspect can to check its contents has been urged to think twice, with officials warning the spoiled soup could have an “unpleasant odour and flavour”.

The recall affects 400g tins of Baxters Chef Selections Cullen Skink carrying the batch code L07XC and a best before date of April 2029.

Customers who purchased the affected soup have been told not to eat it or even open the tin.

Instead, food safety chiefs have advised shoppers to carefully bag up the entire can and dispose of it outdoors.

The FSA said: “Carefully place the full can in a bag and dispose of it in an external bin.”

Anyone who has bought an affected tin and wants a replacement should contact Baxters' customer care team.
