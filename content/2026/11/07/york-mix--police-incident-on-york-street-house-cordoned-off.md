There’s an active police incident on a York street today (Saturday).

A house is taped off on **Ebor Street**.

There’s also a police guard, with a force van parked in the driveway.

The neat brick semi-detached house is in a quiet street off Bishopthorpe Road.

The curtains and blinds are closed in the downstairs room.

One resident said they saw the cordon in place at 7am this morning.

Police told them it was a crime scene, but added nothing more.

We have asked North Yorkshire Police for more details about the incident and will update when this comes through.
