A County Durham woman is launching a female-driver-only taxi service after spotting a gap in the market during her time in the industry.

Jill Donison, from Seaham, came up with the idea after working as a taxi driver herself and noticing what she believes is an untapped demand for women-only transport options.

Jill was a residential worker in children's services until an accident at work left her unable to continue in her role.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21671237.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jill Donison is launching the service in Durham and Sunderland. *(Image: JILL DONISON)*

In August this year she began driving for Uber, where she regularly encountered passengers who were surprised and delighted to see a woman behind the wheel.

Now, she is preparing to launch her own business, which will initially operate across Durham and Sunderland.

The 51-year-old said the idea had been developing in the back of her mind for some time.

She said: "There was something always in the back of my mind that there was a need for more female drivers.

"When I was driving people, it would happen up to ten times a day where women were getting in the car and saying, 'Oh my God, a woman driver, this is amazing'.

"I don't want anyone to feel I'm being disrespectful to male drivers.

"There are thousands of brilliant male drivers who ensure the safety of their passengers, but I just saw a gap in the market and I want to give women the option to have a female driver if they want one."

Ms Donison said public reaction to the venture has been overwhelmingly positive.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21671236.jpg?type=mds-article-620)SheSafe will provide women with a female-driver-only option. *(Image: JILL DONISON)*

"The response has been amazing, absolutely amazing," she said.

She is currently waiting for her operator's licence before officially launching the service and beginning the recruitment of other female drivers.

The company will initially focus on pre-booked journeys rather than an app-based model, with rides available across the Durham and Sunderland areas.
