A fly-tipper has been ordered to pay a £3,500 penalty after waste was dumped on a Shildon lane.

A 40-year-old man from the Evenwood area was found to be to blame for the rubbish left strewn across Haggs Lane in Shildon after an investigation by neighbourhood wardens

The man was interviewed under caution and immediately accepted responsibility..

He was handed a £3,500 fixed penalty notice, which has been paid.

Pictures show large amounts of waste scattered across the ground.

Durham County Council said it would continue to pursue those responsible for environmental crimes across the county.
