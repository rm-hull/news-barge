Police Scotland have confirmed that the bodies of two men have been recovered following a glider crash into Loch Leven.

Search and rescue helicopters were dispatched to the loch, situated near Kinross, as part of the emergency response following reports that a microlight glider had crashed there shortly after 11am on Friday.

The Scottish Gliding Centre, based at the nearby Portmoak Airfield, confirmed that a "serious incident" had taken place involving a two-seater Perkoz glider.

A statement from the police said: “Around 11.05am on Friday, Police Scotland received a report of a glider having crashed into Loch Leven in the Kinross area.

“Emergency services attended and the bodies of two men have been recovered. Their next of kin have been informed.

“The deaths are not being treated as suspicious and inquiries to establish the full circumstances are ongoing.”

The Air Accidents Investigation Branch (AAIB) said a team of investigators has been sent to the site.

Footage on social media showed a helicopter flying over the loch on Friday.

![The Air Accidents Investigation Branch (AAIB) said a team of investigators has been sent to the site](https://static.independent.co.uk/2023/09/08/11/iStock-475001282.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A spokesperson for the coastguard confirmed two of its helicopters had been involved in the emergency response.

The Scottish Gliding Centre said a Perkoz aircraft had “crashed whilst on a local training flight”.

It added: “The incident is currently under investigation by the Air Accidents Investigation Branch of the Department for Transport and the police in conjunction with the British Gliding Association.

“Our club is co-operating fully with these organisations.

“At the present time there is no indication as to the cause of the accident.”

On Friday afternoon, the fire service said its response had come to an end.

A Scottish Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: “We were alerted at 11.01am on Friday November 20 to an incident at Loch Leven.

“Operations control mobilised one fire appliance and specialist resources to the area to assist emergency service partners.

“Crews have now left the scene.”
