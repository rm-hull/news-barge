The scores are in — the latest batch of food hygiene ratings for York schools and catering businesses have been revealed.

Environmental health officers from York have been visiting businesses across the city to see if they are meeting hygiene requirements.

Based on guidelines set by the Food Standards Agency, inspectors look to see how clean the facilities are; what food safety management procedures are in place and how hygienic food handling is.

Ratings are then dished out from five to zero — with the higher score the better the result.

### Five — Very Good

Hutchison Catering at Park Grove Primary School, Park Grove, York, YO31 8LG, rated five on November 10.

Impact Foods @ Ralph Butterfield Primary School, Station Road, Haxby, York, YO32 3LS, rated five on November 10.

Acomb Primary School at West Bank, York, YO24 4ES, rated five on November 5.

Hutchison Catering at St Mary's CE Primary, School Lane, Askham Richard, York, YO23 3PD, rated five on November 5.

Businesses may have been reinspected since then, and this will be reflected in our next round-up.
