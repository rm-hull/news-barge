Harrogate’s Christmas lights have been switched on, bringing festive sparkle to the town centre ahead of the Christmas period.

Harrogate BID has confirmed it has fully funded the town’s festive lights this year.

A spokesperson for Harrogate BID said:

> “We’re proud to fully fund the town’s festive lights, helping to create the magical atmosphere that makes Harrogate such a special place to visit, shop and spend time during the festive season.”

People are being encouraged to explore the town and discover the festive displays, with BID saying there are many more lights to see around Harrogate.

**No official switch-on event**

There is no official Christmas lights switch-on event in Harrogate this year, continuing the absence of a large-scale celebration since the Covid-19 pandemic.

Harrogate BID previously said staging an event could cost between £15,000 and £20,000, with the money potentially better spent on other festive activities supporting town centre businesses.

A business consultation also found that 60% of respondents did not believe a switch-on event would increase footfall or customer spending.

Harrogate Town Council considered funding an event for 2026 but voted against the proposal in July, instead agreeing to contribute £15,000 towards the wider festive programme.
