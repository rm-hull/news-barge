An apprentice has been highly commended for their achievements at a recent awards ceremony.

The recognition was given to Will Vickers, a Mechatronics Maintenance Apprentice. maintenance apprentice from Rillington-based manufacturer Ellis Patents, who was honoured at the Derwent Training Association Annual Awards Ceremony for their work on a Mechatronics Apprenticeship.

The ceremony celebrates apprentices in engineering and technical training and included the Class of 2023–2024.

Richard Shaw, chairman of Ellis Patents and Derwent Training Association, said: "We’re extremely proud to see one of our own apprentices recognised for his achievements.

"Apprenticeships play an important role in developing the practical skills, technical knowledge, and experience that engineering and manufacturing businesses like ours need for the future.

"It was particularly rewarding to see so many young people recognised on the evening and to celebrate the hard work they have put into their training."

Thirsk and Malton MP Kevin Hollinrake also attended the event.

The event brought together apprentices, employers, and representatives from Derwent Training to recognise achievement across its apprenticeship programmes.

Ellis Patents is based on High Street in Rillington, North Yorkshire.
