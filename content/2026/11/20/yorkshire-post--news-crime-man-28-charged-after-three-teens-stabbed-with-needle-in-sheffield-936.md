# Man, 28, charged after three teen girls ‘stabbed with needle’ in Sheffield

## A man has been charged with attempted wounding after three teenagers were allegedly stabbed with a needle in Sheffield city centre.

South Yorkshire Police said that a man was walking through High Street at about 4.45pm on Wednesday, and reportedly stabbed three people, aged 16, 18 and 19, with a needle.

They attended hospital to be treated for their injuries and were later discharged.

![Police were called to Sheffield city centre yesterday (November 18) after three young girls were stabbed with a needle.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/15/48/image0-(14).jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Keegan Hodgson, 28, of Rolleston Road, Sheffield, has been charged with three counts of attempted wounding with intent in connection with the incident.

The force said he has been remanded in custody and was due to appear at Sheffield Magistrates’ Court on Friday.

Inspector Andrew Rimmer, from the Sheffield city centre neighbourhood policing team, said: “Following this incident, a 28-year-old man was swiftly arrested on suspicion of Section 18 GBH by officers from the city centre neighbourhood policing team, who were on patrol at the time.

“We know the nature of this incident is concerning and therefore there has been an increased police presence in the area since the incident, which will continue today.

“If you have any concerns or queries please speak to officers while they are out and about on patrols.”
