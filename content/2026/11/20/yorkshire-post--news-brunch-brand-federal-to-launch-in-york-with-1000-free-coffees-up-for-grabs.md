# Brunch brand Federal to launch in York with 1,000 free coffees up for grabs

## Brunch brand Federal is set to open its new cafe on York’s Coney Street on Friday 4 December with 1,000 free coffees up for grabs to celebrate the launch.

The big coffee giveaway will start at 7.30am with free espresso drinks, lattes, flat whites and cappuccinos up for grabs.

York will be the brand’s largest cafe to date and second in Yorkshire, following its Leeds opening in late last year.

Federal founder Claudio Ribeiro said: “Giving away 1,000 coffees is our way of saying hello to York.

![Federal is set to give away 1,000 free coffees in York.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmUxYzIwNThhLTIxNDgtNGEyNi1hZWNiLWRkYjE0NzA0MzA1ZToxYmRlMTRkMS02ZTdhLTQwZWEtOTE2NS1jYzM3NWRiZDUyODE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


"We’ve found a building with real character on Coney Street, and we want it to feel like somewhere people can make part of their day, whether they’re popping in for a coffee or settling in for brunch with friends."

Housed in a Grade II-listed building on Coney Street, the café will offer seating across two floors and a large outdoor area.

The café’s menu will feature Federal signature dishes including French toast, steak and eggs, halloumi and shrooms, as well as a rotating line-up of specials alongside seasonal food and drink specials.
