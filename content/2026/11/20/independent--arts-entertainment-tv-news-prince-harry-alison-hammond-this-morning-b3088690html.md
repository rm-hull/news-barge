Prince Harry has jokingly scolded *This Morning* host Alison Hammond for not answering his calls after he tried to get in touch with her.

Appearing on Friday’s edition of the weekday show, the Duke of Sussex told Hammond: “I got your number from one of your team, I FaceTimed you when I got home with the kids, they were so excited, no answer.

“Then I may have texted you when you were in hospital out of concern, and nothing.”

Hammond explained: “When I see a strange number, I don’t answer it. Can I just apologise? I’m sorry.”

Harry told her: “I’m the same, don’t worry.”

Later in the show, she told him to come over to her house so she could teach him some Birmingham slang, and he joked: “You won’t pick up your phone though, will you?

“You can’t invite me and not pick up my calls, it’s just rude!”

![Prince Harry on This Morning](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/20/12/13/prince-harry-this-morning.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Elsewhere, Prince Harry gave his seal of approval to Robert Pattinson’s casting as James Bond.

The British actor was announced as the new 007 earlier this week, ending years of speculation as to who would replace Daniel Craig in the iconic role.

During his appearance on ITV’s *This Morning*, Harry played a quickfire game choosing between English and American things.

One question asked him to pick between classic British spy James Bond and *Mission: Impossible*, the US franchise starring Tom Cruise.

He replied: “James Bond, 100 per cent. I want to make sure that is in good hands.”

Asked how he felt about Pattinson’s casting, he said: “I think it’s great, I can’t wait to see it.”

Another round of the game pitted UK soap *EastEnders*against* Suits*, the legal drama that propelled his wife, the Duchess of Sussex, to fame.

He replied: “Why would you put those two in the same category? Suits, obviously.”

He offered an apology to soap star Ross Kemp adding: “Sorry Ross, if you’re watching.”
