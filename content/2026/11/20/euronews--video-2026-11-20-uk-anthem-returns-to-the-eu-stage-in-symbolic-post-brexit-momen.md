**British Prime Minister Andy Burnham was welcomed to Brussels on 20 November 2026 to the strains of*God Save the King*, the first time the UK national anthem has been played at an EU institution since Britain’s 2020 exit.**European Council President** António Costa**and European Commission President** Ursula von der Leyen** welcomed the British leader before the trio posed for cameras, listened to Beethoven’s *Ode to Joy* and greeted their negotiating teams.

The ceremonial warmth underscored a broader **diplomatic push to mend fences between London and Brussels** while respecting the UK’s exit from the bloc. With economic relations and youth mobility topping the agenda, both sides are**poised to announce deals** aimed at cutting trade red tape and making it easier for young Britons and Europeans to live, work and travel across borders.
