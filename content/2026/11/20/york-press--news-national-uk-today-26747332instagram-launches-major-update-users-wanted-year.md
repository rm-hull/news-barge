Instagram has launched a new update fans have "waited decades for".

The social media platform has released a Stories update, featuring new stickers and editing tools.

Introducing the changes in a post, Instagram Creators said: "Your Stories just got a glow-up.

"We’re refreshing stickers and editing tools so you can share your creativity more easily with your audience."

## Full list of features in the new Instagram update

The new Stories update launched by Instagram features:

* Story editing (for 15 minutes after posting - without losing views, likes, or replies)
* New stickers, fonts, colours, and shapes
* Improved sticker sheet for easier browsing
* Multi-select for photo dumps
* Background selection
* Support for landscape media

## Instagram users react to new Stories update

Users have welcomed the changes, with many saying the update was long overdue.

One user commented: "It’s about time."

Another said: "Keeping your views, likes and replies while fixing a typo is the best part.

"No more delete-and-repost for one wrong word."

A third added: "We waited decades for this."

Another wrote: "GAME CHANGER."

## How long do Instagram Stories last?

Instagram Stories remain visible for 24 hours before disappearing from profiles and feeds.

Instagram said: "Stories are designed to be temporary, which makes them a low-pressure way to share without overthinking.

"This is different from a regular Instagram post, which stays on your profile until you archive or delete it."

Each Story clip can be up to 60 seconds long, with longer videos automatically split into multiple clips.

There is no daily limit to how many Stories users can post.

## How to view past stories on Instagram

After 24 hours, Instagram automatically saves Stories to your Archive.

To access the Archive, users can:

* Go to their profile
* Tap the menu icon
* Select Archive

From there, stories can be browsed by date and added to Highlights.

Instagram added: "If you want a story to stay visible on your profile beyond 24 hours, save it to your Highlights.

"They stay on your profile until you remove them."
