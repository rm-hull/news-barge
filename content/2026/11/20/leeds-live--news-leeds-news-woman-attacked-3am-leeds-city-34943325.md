# Woman attacked at 3am in Leeds city centre as man tries to rape her

## Police say woman in her 20s is being supported by officers

A woman was attacked at 3am in Leeds city centre by a man who tried to rape her.

The attack happened at 3.07am in East Parade in the city centre. West Yorkshire Police said the woman, who is in her 20s, was attacked by a man at the junction with South Parade. They said she is being supported by specially trained officers.

British Transport Police later arrested a man aged 34 and he is being questioned on suspicion of attempted rape.

A police spokesman said: "At 3.07am today, police received a report of an attempted rape of a woman in East Parade in Leeds city centre. The victim, aged in her twenties, was attacked by a man at the junction with South Parade. She is being supported by specially trained officers.

"A 34-year-old man was subsequently arrested by British Transport Police officers on suspicion of attempted rape in relation to the incident. He remains in custody and enquires by specialist officers at Leeds District Safeguarding Unit are ongoing."

Witnesses or anyone with any information can contact Leeds District Safeguarding online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting crime reference 13260659257.
