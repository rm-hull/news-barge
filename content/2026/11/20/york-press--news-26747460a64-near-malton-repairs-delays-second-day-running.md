## Delays reported for second day on North Yorkshire main road

Traffic is queueing on the A64 both ways near the B1248 York Road (Musley Bank) due to emergency repairs *(Image: Newsquest)*

DELAYS are being reported on a main road in North Yorkshire for a second day.

Traffic is queueing on the A64 both ways near the B1248 York Road (Musley Bank) due to emergency repairs.

Traffic lights are in place to allow for the works to take place.

One motorist said it had taken them about 45mins to get through the roadworks.
