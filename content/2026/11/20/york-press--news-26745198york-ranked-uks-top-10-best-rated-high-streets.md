York has claimed one of the top spots among the UK’s best-rated high streets.

According to new research by Birmingham SEO company OKPromo, shops in York city centre average 4.47 stars out of five, placing the city eighth in a ranking of 135 UK town and city centres.

The findings are based on analysis of more than four million Google reviews.

Cristian Constantin, SEO expert at OKPromo, said: "The towns at the top are packed with independent shops where the person serving you often owns the business, and you can see it in the reviews, which keep mentioning friendly, helpful staff.

"The big chains still score well, but independents edge ahead in almost every town we looked at, so a high street with plenty of independents starts with an advantage."

A recent five-star review of a York shop, left in November 2026, praised the experience, saying: "Such lovely and helpful staff when I popped in for a Christmas present for my wife."

Other Yorkshire high streets also performed well, with Sheffield ranking fourth at 4.50 stars, and Harrogate in 12th place.

Bradford was the lowest-rated in the region, landing 116th with an average of 4.23 stars.

Nationally, Lancaster topped the list with a score of 4.55.
