Fiona, the Cincinnati Zoo's famous hippopotamus, has died after swallowing a visitor's woolly hat, despite repeated attempts by veterinarians to save her.

The nine-year-old Nile hippo ingested the knitted cap last week after it accidentally landed in her habitat. She later began losing her appetite, prompting the zoo's veterinary team to provide round-the-clock care.

Vets tried several methods to help the hat pass through her digestive system, including medication, enemas and a specialised scope. They also used a vibration plate in an effort to encourage movement through her digestive tract.

However, the attempts were unsuccessful. The zoo announced her death on Thursday, saying the object had caused a medical emergency that Fiona could not overcome.
