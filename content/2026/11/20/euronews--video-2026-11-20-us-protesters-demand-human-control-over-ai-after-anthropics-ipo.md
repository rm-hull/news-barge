Armed with placards and banners, **demonstrators outside Anthropic’s San Francisco offices urged authorities to rein in the breakneck pace of AI development**. "ASI is not a race, build it at a human pace," crowds chanted, while others called out, "What's our goal? Human control." Signs reading "Ban RSI" and "RSI - Really stupid idea" took direct aim at recursive self-improvement, the mechanism allowing AI systems to upgrade their own intelligence without human intervention.

The rally took place against a backdrop of mounting debate over AI’s impact on society, and on the heels of **Anthropic’s hugely anticipated IPO**, which captivated investors despite market jitters and warnings of a tech bubble. Ahead, the company faces tougher scrutiny from a** Democratic-led US Congress next year**, while rival** OpenAI has reportedly postponed its own flotation until 2027**.

For the protesters in San Francisco, the issue went far beyond financial metrics: their demands centred on strict government regulation, public accountability and guaranteeing that human beings never relinquish control over increasingly powerful machines.
