## Britain’s Got Talent choir farmers to speak at Yorkshire conference

The Hawkstone Farmers Choir release their debut album, This Is Home on Friday 20 November 2026 *(Image: Britain's Got Talent)*

Two farmers who found fame on Britain’s Got Talent will share their story at the 2027 Yorkshire Farming Conference.

Katryna Shell from north Northumberland and Tim Robinson from the Peak District are members of The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir, which became the first choir to win the well-known competition.

They will take part in a conversation with Clare Wise, chair of the NFU’s National Livestock Board, at the conference, which takes place on Wednesday, January 27 at the Great Yorkshire Events Centre in Harrogate.

David Tite, manager of the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, said: "It’s really exciting to welcome Katryna and Tim to the line-up for the 2027 Yorkshire Farming Conference.

"The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir has achieved fantastic public profile for the industry, and it’s going to be fascinating to hear their thoughts on how agriculture can benefit from ‘ordinary’ farmers being in the media spotlight."

The pair will discuss how the choir’s victory has affected their personal and professional lives, as well as its wider impact on the farming industry.

Ms Shell said: "Joining The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir has pushed me out of my comfort zone.

"I’m passionate about championing British agriculture, raising awareness of mental health and encouraging others in the industry to find their voice."

The choir’s debut album, This Is Home, will be released today (Friday, November 20).

In addition to their appearance at the conference, the choir will perform in the Main Ring of the Great Yorkshire Show in 2027.

The 2026 Conference attracted more than 700 farmers and workers in agriculture, with a sold-out Trade Show supported by more than 60 businesses and organisations.

Other confirmed speakers for 2027 include The Farmers’ Larder, a collective of six nature-friendly Yorkshire Dales farms.

Tickets for the event are now on sale via: www.yorkshirefarmingconference.co.uk
