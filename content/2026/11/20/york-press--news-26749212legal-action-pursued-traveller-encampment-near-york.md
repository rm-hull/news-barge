Legal advice is being pursued to remove a Traveller encampment from a car park, Stamford Bridge Parish Council has said.

East Riding of Yorkshire Council has asked its legal department to prepare a formal Direction Notice for the group at Viking Road Car Park in Stamford Bridge.

Officials are waiting to learn how long the notice period will be, the parish council stated on Friday afternoon (November 20).

The council follows a structured process for handling unauthorised encampments, which includes a set schedule of contact and legal notices.

The parish council stated that while its clerk is speaking to the East Riding of Yorkshire Council daily for updates, it has no role in the legal process.

Residents are being encouraged to continue raising concerns through the council's reporting system.

The parish council said it would remain in close contact with East Riding of Yorkshire Council as the process progresses.
