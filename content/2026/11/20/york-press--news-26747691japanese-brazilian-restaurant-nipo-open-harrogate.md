## Date announced for opening of Japanese-Brazilian restaurant in North Yorkshire town

A NIPO steak on the grill *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A Japanese-Brazilian restaurant has announced its opening date for Harrogate.

NIPO officially opens its doors on Parliament Street on Thursday December 3.

The operator, which also runs the Tomohawk Steakhouse chain, which has a branch in York, announced the opening date on social media.

The company said: “Bringing Japanese precision, Brazilian fire, bold flavours and warm hospitality to Harrogate, we're so excited to bring our Japanese-Brazilian dining experience to you.”

To celebrate, NIPO added it would give one lucky winner a table for two during the opening month if they liked the post, shared the story and tagged who they would be bringing.

The winner will be announced on Wednesday 25th November.

As the Press previously reported, Tomahawk Steakhouse had originally planned to open a restaurant in Parliament Street, where Piccolino’s used to be and before that a Gino D’Acampo My Restaurant.

Tomohawk Steak House Harrogate posted on social media last month: “We're proud to have teamed up with sister brand Rio, to come together and bring the exciting new NIPO concept to Harrogate.

“Recently opened in Newcastle, NIPO brings together Japanese Precision with Brazilian fire. Expect robata-style steaks, sushi, bold flavours and dishes made for sharing.”
