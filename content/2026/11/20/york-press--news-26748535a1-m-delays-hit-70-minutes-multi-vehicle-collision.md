Delays of 70 minutes with queues five miles long have been reported on a Yorkshire motorway.

National Highways said earlier today that traffic was temporally held on the A1 (M) Northbound between junctions 36 and 37 near Doncaster following a multi-vehicle collision.

The agency said South Yorkshire Police were at the scene.

It added: "Current delays of 70 mins and 5 miles of congestion on the approach."

AA Roadwatch posted at 12.37pm that the held traffic has now been released but the road remains partially blocked.

It spoke of 'severe delays' of 14 minutes south of the location on the northbound carriageway.

And around 1.30pm, National Highways added: "Traffic has now been released and all lanes are now open following an earlier multiple vehicle collision.  
  
"Residual delays of 20 mins and 3 miles of congestion on the approach.  
  
"Have a safe onward journey."
