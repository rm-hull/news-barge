# GTA VI developers celebrate game’s release in Leeds craft beer bar

![The team at Rockstar Leeds celebrated GTA VI's release at Salt Granary Wharf in Leeds](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/13/43/Screenshot-2026-11-20-at-12-59-59.png?trim=148,229,6,55&crop=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## A Leeds bar manager has said he was ‘proud’ to host part of the team behind GTA VI as they celebrated the game’s release this week.

The smash hit video game, which was released on November 19, has links to West Yorkshire thanks to makers Rockstar and their office in Leeds.

And after 13 years in development, Rockstar staff in the US and UK were keen to let off steam once the latest instalment went on sale.

![Grand Theft Auto 6 is expected to be the most successful video game release of all time.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/19/14/53/onecms_76f5c1c1-a723-4b88-a468-973c7003bca9.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Rockstar Leeds’ parent company Rockstar Games is based in New York, but the studio traces its origins back to the British Isles with Rockstar North still being based out of Edinburgh where it began.

Around 40 members of staff from Rockstar Leeds descended on Salt Granary Wharf in the city centre on Wednesday for an unofficial 'wrap party' marking GTA VI's release.

Craig Loughlin, general manager at the craft beer venue, said Rockstar staff were 'happy to be celebrating' when they visited.

He told The Yorkshire Post: “They just came in on a whim with the office being in the city centre. They were keeping it on the down-low.

![Rockstar Leeds staff celebrate the release of GTA VI in Leeds this week.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/13/53/Screenshot-2026-11-20-at-12-59-59.png?trim=0,20,0,32&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


“My colleagues said they were all lovely and great, and happy to be celebrating. It was naturally quite a big achievement for them to finally wrap it off.

“Once they got here they told us what it was and why - it’s not regular to get 40 people turn up on a weekday afternoon, so they mentioned why they were here.”

The latest instalment in Rockstar’s blockbuster video game series became available to UK users at midnight on Thursday, ending an agonising 13-year wait since the franchise’s previous instalment.

With pre-orders and first-day sales of more than 24.5 million units, it is estimated to have made 2.36 billion dollars (£1.8 billion), according to analytics platform Sensor Tower.

Bar manager Craig said the game's link to Leeds are a 'proud' moment for West Yorkshire.

He added: “I sent a picture of the celebrations to my mum and even she'd heard about it.

“I'm looking into getting a second-hand PS5 and a copy so I can experience it myself.”
