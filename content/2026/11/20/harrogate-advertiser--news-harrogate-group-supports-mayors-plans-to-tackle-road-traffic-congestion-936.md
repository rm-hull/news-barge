Zero Carbon Harrogate says it fully supports the Mayor David Skaith in his mission to improve transport throughout York and North Yorkshire through implementation of his Local Transport Plan (LTP).

The charity, a non-political voluntary organisation which was first launched in 2016 to encourage Harrogate district to become a net zero carbon community, has submitted its response to the Mayor’s draft LTP, setting out their support, suggestions and concerns.

The plan was put out for public consultation by Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, David Skaith for ten weeks from late August.

Jemima Parker, Chair and Lead on transport at Zero Carbon Harrogate, said: “A wider range of more affordable, more reliable travel options for residents and visitors in the area is essential to ensuring our local economy thrives and enabling journeys to be a win win for our physical and mental health.”

Zero Carbon Harrogate says it will continue to co-operate with the Mayor and his staff through the Harrogate and Knaresborough Sustainable Transport Roundtable, which they convene, and draws together a wide range of voices from the voluntary and statutory sectors and includes the voices of the organisations responsible for delivering transport services in our area.

In September the group ran the "Great Harrogate Transport Fair", successfully helping a wider range of people to engage with sustainable transport solutions and comment on the Mayor's draft LTP.

ZCH is an active member of the North Yorkshire Climate Coalition, a coalition of climate and environment groups across North Yorkshire established in 2022, which engages regularly with North Yorkshire Council and the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority.
