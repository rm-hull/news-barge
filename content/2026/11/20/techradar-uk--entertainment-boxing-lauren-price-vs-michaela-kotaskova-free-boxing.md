The Lauren Price v Michaela Kotaskova boxing live stream on Friday from Cardiff's Utilita Arena is the Welsh southpaw's chance to make history. No Welsh boxer, male or female, has ever been undisputed world champion and the Tokyo 2020 Olympic gold medallist will add the vacant WBO strap to the WBC, WBA, IBF and *The Ring* magazine belts she will again defend if she beats the 35-year-old Czech.

Price made her first defense of the WBC, WBA, IBF and *Ring* titles she won against Natasha Jonas by coming through a war with Stephanie Pineiro back in April. With Mikaela Mayer having relinquished the WBO belt to rematch Chantelle Cameron next month, Price now has the chance to hold all the welterweight belts and become undisputed, a feat not even fellow unbeaten Welsh great Joe Calzaghe could manage. The 32-year-old from Newport has designs on moving up the weight categories, with fights against Cameron, Mayer and even the GWOAT Claressa Shields all possibilities, but first must take care of business here.

Kotaskova will be no mug, however. The Czech has lost only once in her pro career – in April this year to the superb Cameron, the only person to beat Katie Taylor – and is a naturally bigger fighter who has often boxed in heavier divisions. Though a significant underdog, the 35-year-old has great heart and no little skill, especially if she can get on the inside and try to disrupt Price's rhythm.
