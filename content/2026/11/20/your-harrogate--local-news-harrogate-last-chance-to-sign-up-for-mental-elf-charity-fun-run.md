There’s still time to sign up for Knaresborough’s Mental Elf charity fun run on Sunday 29th November.

Mind in Harrogate District is hoping to beat last year’s fundraising total of £6,500 when the festive 5k returns to Conyngham Hall.

Sponsorship will help fund counselling for 11 to 18-year-olds, drop-in sessions for young people and wellbeing workshops in schools and youth clubs.

**What time does the run start?**

The run starts at 11am. Registration opens at 9.30am and organisers ask everyone taking part to arrive by 10.30am.

Your Harrogate will host the build-up from 10am, with a fancy dress competition at 10.30am. Festive outfits are encouraged and there’s a category for the best-dressed dog.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/692ee3fc4769d.jpg)

**Do I have to run the full 5k?**

You can walk, jog or run at your own pace. The course follows two loops of the Conyngham Hall parkrun route, and anyone who prefers a shorter distance can stop after one loop.

There’s no time limit. Everyone who takes part receives a goody bag and a medal that doubles as a Christmas tree decoration.

**Can I bring children or a dog?**

Children of all ages can take part with an adult. Dogs are welcome too, provided they stay on a short lead.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/68f0c1e6ac60b.jpg)

**How much does it cost and how do I sign up?**

Tickets cost £15 for adults and £12 for children aged five to 15. Under-fives go free, and discounts are available for families of five and teams of eight.

Places can be booked through the Mental Elf page on Mind in Harrogate District’s website.

The charity encourages participants to collect sponsorship, with no minimum amount to raise.
