York Knights are delivering festive cheer to young patients.

Players are visiting York Hospital’s children’s ward in the run-up to Christmas to hand-deliver presents.

Matty Lewis, player welfare and team manager at York Knights RLFC, said: "It's easy for us all to focus on our own bubble at Christmas, without thinking of those less fortunate, like the children who sadly have to spend the festive period in hospital.

"So we're determined to spread some love and put some smiles on faces."

The club has launched a public appeal for new, unwrapped toys or gifts suitable for children aged from 0 to 17 years old.

Items must remain in their original packaging, and second-hand donations cannot be accepted due to infection risks.

Donations can be dropped off at York St John Sports Fields, Haxby Road, YO31 8TA, with the final day to donate being Monday, December 14.

Anyone with questions is asked to contact [m.lewis@yorkrlfc.com.](mailto:m.lewis@yorkrlfc.com.)
