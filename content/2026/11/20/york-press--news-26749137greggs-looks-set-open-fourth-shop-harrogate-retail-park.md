Another Greggs looks set to open in Harrogate.

The bakery and fast-food chain has submitted a building control notification to North Yorkshire Council concerning Unit 5 of the Hydro Retail Park on Ripon Road.

The application, received by the council on November 17, speaks of a “Fit out of vacant unit to form a Greggs shop.”

No further details are given.

The unit previously contained Stonehouse Fireplaces and is being marketed for lease.

This would be the fourth Greggs in Harrogate, with the town having branches in Cambridge Road, Market Place, as well as Plumpton Park, Starbeck, which opened in May.

In December, a branch of Greggs opened at the Morrisons service station on the edge of Boroughbridge.
