A man has died days after falling during a fight with another fan at a Dallas Cowboys game in Texas.

Mario Narro, 36, attended the November 15 Dallas Cowboys–San Francisco 49ers game at AT&T Stadium in Arlington when he got involved in an altercation with another fan around kickoff, the Arlington Police Department and Tarrant County Medical Examiner’s Office confirmed to *The Independent*.

Narro remained at the stadium and continued watching the game after the fight.

A friend who was with him said he began complaining of pain, prompting them to seek help from stadium paramedics after the game. Narro was then taken to Medical City Arlington Hospital.

Narro’s family contacted Arlington police on November 17 to file a report about the incident. Narro died at the hospital on November 18 around 11:30 a.m., according to the medical examiner’s office.

![Mario Narro, 36, died days after falling during a fight with another fan at the November 15 Dallas Cowboys–San Francisco 49ers game at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/14/2305823607..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The Tarrant County Medical Examiner’s Office is awaiting the results of a complete autopsy and will determine an official cause and manner of death.

The Arlington Police Department is now treating the case as a death investigation.

*The Independent* has contacted AT&T Stadium for comment.

The fatal fight was just one of the many altercations at the 49ers-Cowboys match. Video circulating on social media shows police officers intervening in several altercations at Texas Live, an entertainment and dining complex just outside AT&T Stadium.

In one video, two police officers restrained a fan on the ground while another fight continued nearby. A 49ers supporter appeared to throw punches at a Cowboys fan, who fought back. Other spectators attempted to break up the altercation.

The camera then captured another confrontation in which a Cowboys fan appeared to restrain a 49ers supporter who had been fighting.

The violence continued as officers escorted one fan away from the scene. Two women, one wearing a 49ers jacket and the other dressed in Cowboys gear, were also seen arguing face-to-face and appearing to shove each other.

It was unclear what sparked the fights or whether anyone was injured.

The incident marked the second consecutive week of violent confrontations involving 49ers fans. The previous weekend, a massive parking lot brawl broke out between San Francisco and Las Vegas Raiders supporters outside Levi’s Stadium in Santa Clara, California.

Videos of that fight showed fans exchanging punches, with several people appearing to be knocked unconscious.

Santa Clara police told *SFGate* that 11 people were arrested and 45 were ejected from the stadium following the Raiders game.

Seven of those arrested were cited for disorderly conduct, while four were arrested on suspicion of fighting in a public place. At least one person also faced a drug possession charge.
