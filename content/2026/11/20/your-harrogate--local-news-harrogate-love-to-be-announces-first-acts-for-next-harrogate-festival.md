Hannah Wants and Sandy Rivera are among the first acts confirmed for next year’s Love to be... Festival in Harrogate.

The house music festival returns to the Stray on Saturday 4th September 2027 for its fourth year, after selling out its first three events.

Birmingham DJ and producer Hannah Wants has previously played Creamfields and Tomorrowland. Love to be... founders Trimtone will return to the main stage, with Stuart Pilling and Aimee also on the bill.

Café Mambo Ibiza will join the festival for the first time on the Music Factory Stage, named after the Sheffield venue where Love to be... began in 1994. K-Klass, Allister Whitehead, Juanito Chanclas, Sinclair and Scott Harris will all play the stage.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b00179d37979a79b8a.png)

A new stage called The Future will showcase emerging house music acts. Organisers are due to announce more names early next year.

The festival will run from midday until 11pm, with fairground attractions, stilt walkers, street food and bars on the Stray.

*Tickets are on sale through**Skiddle**, starting at £51.50 plus a booking fee.*
