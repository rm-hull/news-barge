APhoenix man is fighting for his life after a 7-foot plank of wood flew off a pickup truck and smashed through his windshield, striking him in the face and leaving him with devastating injuries.

DJ Hughes, 35, was driving on Interstate 10 near Jackrabbit Trail in Buckeye, Arizona around 1:20 p.m. November 10 when the piece of lumber came loose from a white pickup truck hauling wood, his mother, Lisa Hughes, told Arizona’s Family.

The plank reportedly slid across the hood of Hughes’ car before crashing through the windshield and striking him in the face. Photos from the crash showed the wood board sticking out through the broken windshield.

Hughes has spent eight days in intensive care following the incident and has already undergone three surgeries, his mother told the outlet Thursday. He suffered multiple skull fractures and extensive injuries to his face, with doctors expecting that he will need additional operations.

“Broke every bone in his face, from his eyes down. Split his chin in half,” Lisa Hughes told the outlet about her son's injuries.

![DJ Hughes, 35, of Phoenix is fighting for his life after a 7-foot plank flew off a pickup truck and smashed through his windshield, leaving him with multiple skull fractures and severe facial injuries](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/16/39/Man-rushed-to-hospital-after-7-foot-plank-flies-off-a-truck-and-through-his-windshield.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Hughes’ devastated mother said the incident has been difficult on their family.

“It’s, um, tough. You know, seeing your baby. I know he’s 35, but seeing him laying like there in that pain…” Lisa Hughes said.

“In a way, I’m thankful that it was him and not a young kid or a young girl,” she added.

The Hughes family is now trying to identify the driver of the white pickup truck that was hauling the lumber. They are asking anyone who witnessed the crash or may have information about the vehicle to come forward.

![The Hughes family is searching for the driver of the pickup truck and asking witnesses to come forward with information](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/16/50/109967179_179442230510276_r.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A GoFundMe campaign has been created to help support Hughes as he faces a lengthy recovery and additional medical treatment.

It remains unclear why the plank came loose from the truck or if the lumber had been improperly secured. Lisa Hughes is also urging drivers to take extra precautions when transporting lumber and other materials.

“Just take the extra two seconds to secure it. It’s not that much time to save a life,” she said.
