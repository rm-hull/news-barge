A digital specialist is launching a new SEO service to help boost visitor numbers at York attractions.

Helen Reynolds, also known as The Ink Gardener, created the Visitor Boost package after hearing of a 30 per cent drop in visitors at some sites.

The package is to retrofit high-converting content and Search Everywhere Optimisation (SEO) into a website’s existing design.

Ms Reynolds, a York resident, said: "I love visiting the city’s museums, quirky historic gems and immersive experiences."

"So I was really concerned when I saw the headlines surrounding Make It York’s report.

"However, my cogs started whirring when I read Gen X – 45- to 60-year-olds - made up the largest group of visitors.

"They are keen users of search engines, which is my speciality."

It draws on her 25-plus years' experience of helping to improve the digital presence of the Lake District National Park, York Minster and other prestigious destinations.
