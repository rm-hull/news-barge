Around 20 members of the isolated Awá Indigenous people have made a rare approach to a government team in Brazil’s Amazon rainforest, raising concerns about their welfare.

The group approached a government outpost in the Arariboia Indigenous Territory, in the northeastern state of Maranhão, and reportedly asked for yams. They took the food back into the forest, according to the NGO Survival International.

The encounter was documented by members of the neighbouring Guajajara Indigenous community, also known as the Forest Guardians, who work with the local government outpost.
