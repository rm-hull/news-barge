Harrogate Grammar School and Redmayne Bentley are celebrating the continuation of a partnership that is helping young athletes thrive, with the Harrogate-based firm extending its sponsorship of the school's Sports Academy for a third year.

The renewed partnership will enable the Sports Academy to continue providing students with opportunities to develop their sporting talent alongside academic achievement, while reinforcing both organisations' commitment to investing in the next generation.

Designed to complement the school's academic curriculum, the Sports Academy offers specialist coaching, strength and conditioning programmes, competitive fixtures and tailored support for aspiring athletes.

Students also benefit from workshops and guest speakers covering topics including nutrition, injury prevention, performance psychology, resilience and wellbeing.

The academy has established strong links with local sports clubs, universities, national governing bodies and professional pathways, helping students pursue opportunities beyond school while maintaining a balanced educational experience.

Support from Redmayne Bentley has also helped expand the academy's provision, ensuring more students can access the programme regardless of their financial circumstances.

Tom Ryder, Director of Sport at Harrogate Grammar School, said: "We are incredibly proud to continue our partnership with Redmayne Bentley for a third year.

“Their support has helped us develop the Sports Academy and provide opportunities that have had a genuine impact on our students' sporting journeys.

"Beyond the financial investment, it is the shared belief in nurturing ambition, resilience and personal growth that makes this partnership so valuable.

“Together, we are creating an environment where young people can flourish both academically and athletically."

Representatives from Redmayne Bentley recently visited the school to meet students and staff and see first-hand how the Sports Academy continues to support and inspire young athletes.

James Andrews, Partner and Director at Redmayne Bentley, added: "For Redmayne Bentley, good financial advice and investment management are about more than managing money.

“They are about giving people the confidence and clarity to make important decisions and pursue their long-term ambitions.

"Those principles resonate strongly with what the Sports Academy is achieving at Harrogate Grammar School.

“Over the past three years, we have seen the confidence, determination and teamwork it inspires in young people, and we are proud to continue supporting the Academy and investing in the potential of the next generation."

As the partnership enters its third year, both Harrogate Grammar School and Redmayne Bentley say they remain committed to creating opportunities that inspire students to aim high, embrace new challenges and realise their potential.

The collaboration also highlights the positive impact local businesses can have when working alongside schools to support young people, helping students develop the confidence, resilience and skills needed to succeed both on and off the field.
