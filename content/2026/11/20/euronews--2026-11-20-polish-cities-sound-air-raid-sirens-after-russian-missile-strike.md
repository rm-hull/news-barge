Polish authorities say three Russian objects crossed into the country's airspace during a major overnight attack on neighbouring Ukraine.

Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said one was a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile, while a second was also believed to be a missile and the third a jet-powered drone. Authorities were investigating three impact sites in the south-east of the country.

Images posted on social media show people walking in the Lublin region as air raid sirens sound.
