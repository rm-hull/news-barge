## Rikers had been slated to close by August 2027 following legislation passed by the City Council in 2019. Moving the deadline will require a change to this law.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani unveiled a plan on Friday to shutter the notorious Rikers Island jail by the end of 2032, proposing to push the deadline from August 2027.

Rikers, long criticised for unsanitary conditions and violence, is due to be replaced by four borough-based jails as part of an effort to improve safety and tackle overcrowding.

The four new sites will be set in the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan and Queens, with the Brooklyn facility scheduled to open in 2029.

The plan aims to bring facilities closer to courts, families and public transportation, while also reducing detention and case delay to help bring the jail population down from around 6,600 people to no more than 4,100 by the end of 2032.

“It is time to finally close Rikers Island,” Mamdani said in a statement released on Friday.

“For decades, New Yorkers have demanded a correctional system that is safe and humane," he continued. "This plan puts us on a path to deliver just that: closing Rikers, reducing pretrial detention, tackling the case delays that deprives people of their right to a speedy trial and investing $146 million in evidence-based supports that help break the cycles of incarceration."

"We have a legal and moral obligation to close Rikers," he added.

Rikers had been slated to close by August 2027 following legislation passed by the City Council in 2019. Moving the deadline will require a change to this law.

"Year after year, Rikers has taken a toll," Mamdani said in a press conference on Friday. "It has made us less safe. Because it has hollowed out those we love and failed those who need help. And because it has endured as a blight almost since the year it was built. A place we have long turned our eyes from rather than confront head on."

"Today, we lead differently. I am proud that New York City will close Rikers Island once and for all," he added.
