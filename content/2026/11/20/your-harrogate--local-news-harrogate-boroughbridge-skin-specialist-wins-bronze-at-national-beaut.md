A skin specialist at The Collaborative in Boroughbridge has won bronze at The Beauty Full Awards.

The Collaborative, owned by Gemma Carlisle, was recognised in the Skin Specialist of the Year 2026 category last night (Thursday).

It is the clinic’s fifth award this year and comes ahead of its first birthday.

Gemma has spent more than 17 years studying skin, beauty and wellbeing.

She told Your Harrogate:

> "I’ve always been passionate about continually learning, researching and developing my knowledge.
>
> "I believe there is always more to discover, and I’m constantly striving to bring the very best treatments, knowledge and expertise to my clients."

She added:

> "The Collaborative is the culmination of that passion and my dream of creating a space where skin health and holistic wellbeing work hand in hand.
>
> "I’m incredibly proud to receive this recognition and so grateful to everyone who has supported us throughout our first year. It really does mean so much."
