A woman has been arrested after a drugs raid in Malton today (Friday).

Assisted by a force drone, officers from Ryedale Neighbourhood Policing Team executed a warrant at a property this morning.

They searched a vehicle parked outside and found a bag containing white powder, and equipment including digital scales.

A woman in her 30s was arrested on suspicion of possessing Class A drugs with intent to supply. She remains in custody.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Information provided by the public plays a vital role in helping police identify and disrupt drug dealers.

“Community intelligence helps us build a clearer picture of where drugs are being supplied, identify those involved, and target our enforcement activity where it will have the greatest impact.

“Every piece of information, no matter how small it may seem, can help develop evidence, safeguard vulnerable people, and support action against those responsible for bringing drugs and the harm associated with them into our communities.”
