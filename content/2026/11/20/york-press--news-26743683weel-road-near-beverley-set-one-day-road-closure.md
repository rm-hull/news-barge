A single-track road near Beverley is set to close for one day to allow essential highway work to take place.

Weel Road, which connects Tickton to the village of Weel, will be shut today (Friday, November 20), from 9am to 3pm for surface investigations ahead of planned improvement works in the new year.

The closure will enable East Riding of Yorkshire Council’s engineers to study the road surface and prepare for future upgrades.

Councillor Paul West, the council’s cabinet member for environment and transport, said: "Thank you in advance to residents and businesses, as well as people using the recycling site, for bearing with us while we close the road for one day for essential investigations."

The Beverley Household Waste Recycling Site, located in Weel Road, will remain open during the closure.

It will be accessible via Weel Bridge and Grovehill Road in Beverley.

Motorists are reminded that Weel Bridge has a three-tonne weight limit and a maximum height restriction of 3.3 metres.

Access for residents and properties within the affected area will be maintained where possible throughout the day.
