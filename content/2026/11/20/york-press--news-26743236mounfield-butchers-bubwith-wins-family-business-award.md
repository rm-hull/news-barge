A family-run butcher has won a regional award for its dedication and excellence in retail.

Mounfield Butchers, based in Bubwith, took home the Small Retailer & E-Tailers award at the Yorkshire, Humberside and North East Family Business Awards 2026.

The ceremony, at The Majestic Hotel in Harrogate and organised by The Family Business Community, celebrated the achievements of family-owned businesses across the region.

The team said: "Winning this award is a testament to the hard work and passion of our entire team.

"We are thrilled to be recognised among such an inspiring group of family businesses."

A fifth-generation business established in 1890, Mounfield Butchers employs 13 people and operates its own on-site abattoir.

The business supports local farmers and uses renewable energy as part of its commitment to sustainability.

It is also known for its traditional pork pies, which have been named the best by Deliciously Yorkshire for seven consecutive years.

The awards evening was hosted by food writer and broadcaster Nigel Barden, alongside event co-founders Dave Clarkson and Sue Howorth.

The pair said: "We always aim to create an unforgettable experience, and hosting the awards in Harrogate this year added something special.

"We’re so grateful to everyone who joined us to celebrate the achievements of these truly fantastic businesses."
