WHITBY businesses could soon be asked whether they support reviving a Chamber of Trade after a proposal was put forward to re-establish the organisation in the town.

Councillors will explore whether there is enough local support to create a new business-led organisation in Whitby, following a proposal from Town Mayor Sandra Turner for the creation of a new business-led organisation.

A re-established Chamber of Trade could provide a collective voice for businesses, help improve communication between traders and public bodies, and create a forum for businesses to work together on common issues, according to a report prepared for councillors.

The proposal follows what council officers describe as encouraging early feedback from local businesses and residents.

According to the report, the mayor has already undertaken informal discussions and has received a positive response to the idea.

Councillors are now being asked to authorise an initial consultation exercise that would involve writing to businesses across Whitby to assess interest in the proposal.

Businesses would be invited to explain what they would want a future Chamber of Trade to achieve and whether they would be interested in becoming involved.

The council said the intention is not to create a new town council committee or council-controlled body.

Instead, any future Chamber of Trade would be developed as an independent, business-led organisation representing a wide range of enterprises across the town.

The report states that a Chamber of Trade could:

* improve communication and cooperation between businesses;
* help coordinate events and promotional activities;
* identify shared priorities and challenges;
* and strengthen the collective voice of Whitby’s business community.

If sufficient interest is identified, the council could help bring interested businesses together to discuss how such an organisation might operate.

The report says the town council’s role would initially be limited to facilitating discussions and gauging support rather than running the organisation itself.

Councillors are expected to decide next week whether to proceed with the consultation exercise.
