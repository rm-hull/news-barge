Harrogate Town have confirmed the return of their award-winning Christmas Soccer Camp, where children will get the opportunity to take part in a host of festive activities at Harrogate Town.

On Thursday, December 10, Town will be teaming up with Enchantica’s for a special festive evening at the Marcy Fitness Stadium, where young fans will be able to meet the players and work on their football skills on the pitch.

They will also get the chance to go behind the scenes in the changing rooms.

The evening will then finish with festive food, games and a meeting with Father Christmas himself!

Children will also take part in Harry Gator’s Christmas Penalty Shootout and leave with a special festive goodie bag.

Parents and guardians can enjoy hot drinks and watch the action from the LWC Hospitality Suite Stand.

**Event details**

* Date: Thursday 10 December 2026
* Time: 5:30pm – 7:30pm
* Venue: The Marcy Fitness Stadium, Wetherby Road
* Price: £30 per child
* Includes: Football skills session, first-team players meet-and-greet, changing room tour, festive food and refreshments, and a Christmas goodie bag

Spaces are limited, so early booking is recommended. The event is open to children aged 7-13 and if you have any further questions, please email [dave.riley@harrogatetownafc.com.](mailto:dave.riley@harrogatetownafc.com.)
