## The EU and UK have struck a major post-Brexit reset deal in Brussels. In tonight’s edition of Europe Tonight, we bring you a live interview from the summit, assess security alarms after Russian missiles hit Poland, and examine the final-day standoff at COP31.

### EU and UK sign Brussels summit deal

At today’s summit in Brussels, EU and UK leaders agreed to ease barriers on food exports, link carbon emissions systems, and launch a youth mobility scheme. Both sides also pledged to debate their future relationship, with Prime Minister Andy Burnham signalling a potentially closer arrangement after the next election.

### Russian missiles hit Poland amid reports of US peace trade-off

Two Russian missiles crashed in southeastern Poland during deadly strikes on Ukraine overnight an incident Warsaw condemned as an intentional provocation. The attacks came hours after Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin jointly vowed to pursue global peace, and amid reports that Washington is considering NATO membership for Ukraine in exchange for Kyiv ceding the Donbas region. We bring you full analysis on what this means for European security.

### COP31 runs into weekend overtime over four sticking points

Finally, negotiators at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya are pushing talks into the weekend after failing to reach a deal on its final scheduled day. Talks remain deadlocked over four key issues: a concrete roadmap to phase out fossil fuels, ensuring the 35% by 2035 electrification goal relies on clean energy, delivering climate finance, and guaranteeing a just transition that leaves no one behind.
