A repetitive noise that has been driving residents of one part of York ‘nuts’ has been identified.

People living in and around Osbaldwick say they have been driven to distraction by the sound.

It’s been variously described as a tapping or popping sound.

One resident posted: “It’s driving me mad. Sounds like it’s coming from inside of the wall, adjoining to my neighbour.”

Another said, only today: “It’s just started again and it’s louder than usual! It’s driving me nuts!”

Osbaldwick Parish Council took up the issue and has now reported back with an answer.

It posted on Facebook: “It’s to do with some piling work on Outgang Lane.

“Presumably the noise/vibration being felt over large areas is down to the vibration being transferred along underground pipes like sewer pipes that run along Murton Way/Osbaldwick Link Rd at the entrance to Outgang Lane.”

This is what City of York Council environmental health officer Ian Gray wrote in his reply to the parish council enquiry.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/heritage-house-murton-way-york-gsv.webp)

“I have been out to Osbaldwick today and confirmed that the source of the noise is works that are taking place at Heritage House, Murton Way.

“There is a property there which is undergoing work to combat subsidence and it is requiring some internal piling so that they can underpin the property and make it safe for use

“The problem is that immediately adjacent to the building are a number of commercial premises and offices which are in use during the day, so they have been trying to get the work done such that it would not impact those businesses.

“They have agreed that there will be no further works on a weekend and that they will try and get piling started earlier at 2pm and finish earlier by around 7pm so as to reduce the noise impact for nearby residents.

“The works should be completed within the next week.”
