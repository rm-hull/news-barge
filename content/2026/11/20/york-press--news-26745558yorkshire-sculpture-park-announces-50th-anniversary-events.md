Yorkshire Sculpture Park (YSP) will celebrate its 50th anniversary in 2027 with a year-long programme of exhibitions, commissions and new initiatives.

Located within 500 acres of historic Yorkshire landscape, YSP has spent five decades bringing modern and contemporary sculpture to broad audiences through both outdoor displays and four indoor spaces.

Highlights for 2027 include a new commission by Andy Goldsworthy, a major solo exhibition by Cornelia Parker, and the addition of Henry Moore’s monumental bronzes to the landscape.

Joe Hill, director and chief executive of YSP, said: "I’m incredibly excited by the programme we’re announcing for YSP’s 50th anniversary.

"It brings together extraordinary artists and ambitious new projects to celebrate the Park’s remarkable legacy while looking firmly to the future.

"From major exhibitions and new commissions to opening our doors for free to every teacher in the UK, it reflects our belief in the importance of art and nature in all our lives, and sets the tone for an exciting new chapter in YSP’s history."

An exhibition by US sculptor John Chamberlain and Fifty, a show featuring 50 UK-based artists born since YSP’s founding, will also be featured.

YSP will offer free annual admission throughout 2027 to every teacher, teaching assistant and trainee teacher in the UK.
