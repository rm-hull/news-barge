The local family behind a new intimate wine and drinks bar which is to open in Harrogate town centre next week have talked about their aims for the new indie.

Located at 38 Beulah Street, which was previously occupied by The Disappearing Chin until it closed in December 2024, the new bar promises to be relaxed and characterful.

Co-owner Robert Thompson said: “My wife and I wanted The Grail to feel like a little escape from the bustle of the town: somewhere you can come in for a glass of wine, beer, graze on some small plates and make an evening of it.

“We’ve had a lot of fun creating its character, and we’re looking forward to welcoming people through the door.”

The Grail is the latest opening from Appetite For Life, the local family run company behind Harrogate venues The Mucky Duck, Tap on Tower Street, The Devonshire Tap House and So! Bar & Eats. Each has its own character, and The Grail will add a more intimate wine bar to the group’s collection.

The intimate new venue will bring together wine, small plates and craft drinks in a setting inspired by the idea of a sanctuary. In the heart of town, it is designed as a place to settle in, discover something new and spend time with friends.

The interior takes cues from old monastic spaces, while the name plays on the search for a great drink and a place worth returning to. Its guiding line is: *“*Your search ends here.”

The Grail will join the likes of the neighbouring Ake & Humphris Wine Bar & Shop at 20 Oxford Street and The Silver Fox at 1 Royal Parade, as well as the long-standing founding fathers of the town’s wine bar heritage - William & Victoria at 6 Cold Bath Road and The Tannin Level at 5 Raglan Street.

The new bar is preparing to open on Thursday, November 26.

In terms of wine selection, The Grail will offer approximately 40 wines by the bottle and glass, plus occasional premium wines by the glass.

There will be craft beer, speciality world lagers, premium spirits, and a curated cocktail list.

In terms of food, The Grail will offer small plates and pintxos tapas, served alongside drinks.

Opening hours

Monday to Thursday: Noon to 10pm.

Friday and Saturday: Noon to 11pm.

Sunday: Noon to 8pm.
