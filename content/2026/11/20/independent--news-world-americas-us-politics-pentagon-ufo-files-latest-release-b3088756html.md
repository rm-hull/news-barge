The Trump administration has released mysterious footage of an unknown object passing over a U.S. warship as part of the Pentagon’s latest document dump of “unidentified anomalous phenomena.”

Grainy black-and-white footage was taken from an infrared sensor aboard the ship in the Middle East in 2020, the Pentagon said.

The 20-second clip released Friday showed the circular light moving past the ship from right to left.

A Pentagon official told *The Independent* that no additional information about the sighting was available. “We are not providing any comment or assessment on the files overall or on any specific file, so that the American people can make up their own minds regarding the files,” the official said.

It was the eighth release of declassified files released by the Pentagon since May after President Donald Trump instructed the department to declassify records related to UFOs and alien encounters.

![The Trump administration has released mysterious footage of an unknown object passing over a US warship as part of the Pentagon’s latest document dump of ‘unidentified anomalous phenomena’.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/14/03/Screenshot-2026-11-20-at-9-37-31-AM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Friday’s release also included a 1953 report by Lockheed engineer Clarence “Kelly” Johnson, who documented an incident he witnessed from his California ranch. He wrote that he saw a dark blue “saucer” in December 1953 and another object in November 1951 with a “light blue flame or emanation” moving west in the sky.

He drew sketches of the alleged sightings.

Trump first ignited interest in extraterrestrial matters in February.

“We’re going to be releasing a lot of things that we haven't,” the president said earlier this year at a White House event celebrating NASA astronauts. “I think some of it’s going to be very interesting to people.”

Videos purporting to show alien technology tend to have mundane explanations, according to Sean Kirkpatrick, a physicist and former career intelligence officer who led the Pentagon’s All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office until 2023.

The agency has been working with the White House to release the “never-before-seen UAP information.”

![Trump, pictured at NASA Lyndon B. Johnson Space Center in Houston, has taken an interest in extraterrestrial matters and instructed the Pentagon to declassify the files](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/14/19/GettyImages-2291892820.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Modern infrared cameras used by the U.S. military often capture jet engines and other hot objects in a long thermal bloom, which, Kirkpatrick said, explains viral videos of speedy, pill-shaped objects.

“Readers should not get their hopes up that there’s going to be some document with photos, interviewing the aliens when they came down,” he said. “Because that just doesn’t exist.”

Trump is hardly the first president drawn to UFO mysteries.

Former President Bill Clinton has said he once ordered a review of the Roswell Incident — something had crashed in 1947 at a New Mexico ranch and officials later said the debris was the remnants of a high-altitude weather balloon — around its 50th anniversary in 1997.

Presidents Jimmy Carter and Ronald Reagan claimed to have seen UFOs before their time in the White House.
