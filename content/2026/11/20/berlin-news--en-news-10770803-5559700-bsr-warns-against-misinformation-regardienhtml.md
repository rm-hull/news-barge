Accordingly, the BSR asks that you not place bulky waste, electronic waste or other trash on the street. According to the BSR, it offers bulky waste disposal exclusively as part of so-called "neighborhood days" in cooperation with the district offices. Smaller quantities of bulky waste can also be dropped off free of charge at BSR recycling centers. For larger quantities of bulky waste, residents can use the BSR bulky waste pickup service, which is subject to a fee. In general, the illegal disposal of bulky waste, electronic waste and other types of waste constitutes an administrative offense and may result in heavy fines.
