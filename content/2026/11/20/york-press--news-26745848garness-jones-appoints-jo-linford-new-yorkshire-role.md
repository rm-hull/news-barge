An expanding business has created a new role to focus on its Yorkshire-wide commercial property market.

Hull-based commercial property specialists Garness Jones have appointed Jo Linford as Executive Assistant & Business Support, a newly created position designed to help the company’s directors and teams.

Ms Linford said: "My job is to review the demands on each department and identify the opportunities to introduce new efficiencies, because ultimately that will result in better working practices for our team, and a better service to our clients."

Garness Jones, which opened a second office in York at the beginning of 2025, is marking 30 years in business.

David Garness, managing director, said: "We’re delighted to welcome Jo to the team.

"We’ve enjoyed another successful year in 2026 despite a challenging economy, and she joins at a time when we are reflecting on where we are as a business."

Ms Linford has previously held roles at the Employee Ownership Association and Swift Group.
