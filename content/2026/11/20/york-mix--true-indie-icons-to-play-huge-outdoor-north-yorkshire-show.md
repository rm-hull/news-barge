Much loved indie band Two Door Cinema Club have announced a huge outdoor show at Scarborough Open Air Theatre for summer 2027.

The Northern Irish trio – Alex Trimble, Sam Halliday and Kevin Baird – will play the historic Yorkshire coast venue on **Friday 6 August 2027**.

The show will be a special celebration of the band’s 2010 debut album *Tourist History* which they will play in full, alongside hits from across their career.

Two Door Cinema Club will be joined on the night by Indie dance electronic band Friendly Fires, while rising singer-songwriter Danny Griffiths will open the show.

Tickets will go on sale at 10am on Thursday 26 November via the Scarborough Open Air Theatre website.

Hailing from Bangor and Donaghadee, Two Door Cinema Club first gained widespread attention with debut offering *Tourist History*, which included the standout singles* What You Know, Something Good Can Work*and* Undercover Martyn*.

The album, which mixed guitar-driven, post-punk hooks with dancey, electronic polish, went on to be a globally-lauded smash hit which took the band from the garage where they wrote the album in Bangor to the main stages of festivals around the world. *Tourist History*clearly still resonates with* What You Know* now topping 1 billion streams globally on Spotify.

In 2024, the band embarked on a sold out 30-date amphitheatre tour of the United States, ending the year in style with a sold-out Australian arena tour, which culminated in an incredible performance at the iconic Sydney Opera House.

They returned to celebrate 15 years of *Tourist History* with a massive outdoor show at London’s Crystal Palace Park.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “Two Door Cinema Club are true indie icons – and we are delighted to add them to the line-up for here at Scarborough Open Air Theatre next summer.

“‘Tourist History’ remains a stunning piece of work and this is going to be a very special night as the band play the album live, in full, alongside many other brilliant songs from their incredible catalogue.”

Two Door Cinema Club is the latest artist announced for 2027 at Scarborough OAT which will also see headline shows from Mariah Carey, Courteeners, Shania Twain, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison, Simply Red as well as a four show run of the smash hit musical Six.
