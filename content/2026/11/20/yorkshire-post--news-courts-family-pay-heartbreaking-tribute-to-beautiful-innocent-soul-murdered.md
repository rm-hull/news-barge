# Family pay heartbreaking tribute to ‘beautiful, innocent soul’ murdered in Bradford home

## The devastated family of a woman murdered in Bradford have paid an emotional tribute to their loved one, describing her as a “beautiful, innocent soul” who was taken far too soon.

Tahira Hussain, 46, died following an incident at a property on Carr Bottom Road in Little Horton, Bradford, on Tuesday (Nov 17).

A second woman, aged 20, suffered serious injuries in the incident and was being treated in hospital.

In a heartbreaking statement, Tahira's family spoke of their devastation and urged people to respect their privacy amid speculation surrounding her death.

![Carr Bottom Road in Little Horton](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/17/35/Carr-Bottom-Road-in-Little-Horton.png?trim=0,7,0,104&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


They said: “Our family has been deeply affected by this tragic event. We have seen all manner of speculation about our loss and would ask people to remember that behind all the speculation and online commentary, there is one heart breaking reality: a beautiful, innocent soul has been taken far too soon. That is what deserves to remain at the forefront of everyone's thoughts.

“To everyone who has reached out with genuine kindness, love, prayers and support, thank you. It has meant more than you will ever know.

“At an incredibly difficult time, we would kindly ask that everyone respects the privacy of our family and allows us the space to grieve, come to terms with what has happened and begin to heal in peace."

Bilal Ajmal, 26, of Carr Bottom Road, Bradford, appeared before Bradford Magistrates' Court on Thursday (Nov 19), charged with Tahira's murder and the attempted murder of the 20-year-old woman.

He was remanded in custody and is due to appear before Bradford Crown Court on Monday (Nov 23).
