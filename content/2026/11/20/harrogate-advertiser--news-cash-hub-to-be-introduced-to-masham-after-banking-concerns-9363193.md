A new cash hub is to bring banking services back to Masham next month.

The new facility - opening at the Mashamshire Community Office on December 11 - comes following growing concerns about access to cash in the market town following the removal of the ATM in Masham Market Place and reduced banking facilities.

Recognising the impact this was having on the community, MCO Trustee Sue Palin requested a Cash Access Review, which concluded that the town needed a Cash Hub, providing assisted services.

A spokesperson for the Community Office said: “Many residents and businesses across Mashamshire continue to rely on cash, while digital and online banking solutions are not suitable or accessible for everyone.

“The new facility will, therefore, provide an important service, helping to ensure that people can continue to access essential banking facilities locally.”

Based at Mashamshire Community Office, the Cash Hub will be open Monday to Friday, from 9am to 5pm.

The service is contracted by Cash Access UK and will be staffed daily by two people.

Customers will be able to deposit cash and cheques, withdraw cash, check bank balances, pay utility bills and top up gas and electricity.

Registered businesses will also be able to access change-giving services.

The announcement has been welcomed by local businesses, with one business owner highlighting the continued importance of cash to the local economy.

They said: "Business is all about flexibility and having the ability to access/deposit cash easily at a cash hub here in Masham is still fundamental in an increasingly 'cashless' society.

“Having access means our guests that prefer dealing in cash don't skip out on treating themselves and that we retain full face value for these sales – not eroded by card handling fees.

“Thank you to MCO for advocating and bringing this facility to Masham."

MCO has expressed its thanks to Sue Palin for the considerable time and effort she has dedicated to championing access to local services, particularly banking facilities and cash access.

Installation of the new Cash Hub will take place over the coming weeks, with Mashamshire Community Office sharing updates on progress through its social media channels and newsletters.

The facility is expected to be a welcome addition to Masham, supporting residents, visitors and businesses alike and helping to strengthen the town's local services in the run-up to Christmas and into the New Year.
