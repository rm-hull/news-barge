Very high gas readings raised concerns at a York flat last night (Thursday).

Crews were called to **Ambrose Street** at**11.20pm**, after Northern Gas asked for help to get into premises where the very high gas readings were coming from.

A crew from Harrogate first responded to the request. A York crew then used a door breaker to open a flat, allowing gas engineers to get inside.

#### Ambrose Street, York

The incident was then left with the gas engineers.
