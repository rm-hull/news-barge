A two-year-old boy was taken to hospital after receiving a facial injury in an alleged incident with a dog on Skipwith Common near Selby, police said.

North Yorkshire Police said it happened at around 2pm on Saturday, November 14 on Common Road, near the car park at Skipwith Common.

The injury required medical treatment, according to the force.




A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "The dog was described as a brown, wire-haired terrier.

"It was being walked by a group of three men and two women, all described as white and believed to be aged between their mid-20s and mid-30s."

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for the owner of the dog to come forward, as well as anyone who witnessed the incident, including members of the group.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police online by visiting their website.

Alternatively, call 101 and quote reference NYP-14112026-0320.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through their website.
