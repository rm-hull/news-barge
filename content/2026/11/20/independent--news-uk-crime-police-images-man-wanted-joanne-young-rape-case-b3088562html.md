Police have made a fresh appeal for a man wanted over the drug-facilitated rape of Joanne Young after four men were convicted of sexually assaulting her.

Wiltshire Police released a series of images of an unknown man, which they are asking to be “shared as widely as possible” after fears he may have travelled abroad following the offences, which took place in April to May 2022.

Former Tory councillor Philip Young drugged and raped his wife Joanne at their home in Swindon, Wiltshire, over a 13-year period, and used sites such as Craigslist, Grindr and Fab Swingers to advertise her for sex. On Thursday, four men were found guilty of sexually abusing Ms Young following a trial at Winchester Crown Court.

In a post on social media following the convictions, Wiltshire Police said they were renewing their efforts to identify an unknown man following the verdicts.

“We are re-issuing our appeal to identify this man in connection with the drug-facilitated rape case involving Philip Young against his ex-wife, Joanne, in Swindon,” the force wrote. “These images were taken around April-May 2022.”

![Police issued images of the man they want to trace](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/16/01M7T6WRGF1D6GTQ89FNTPHSQY.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

They described the man as “of a large overweight build, hairy, with scarring on the back of his head, prominent lips, short or shaved hair, and a distinctive tattoo on the left side of his chest”.

The force added that although the offences took place in Swindon, it is unknown whether the man lives locally or may have travelled from elsewhere.

Senior investigating officer Detective Superintendent Geoff Smith said on Thursday: “Following today's verdicts, I want to again appeal for the public’s help in identifying this man.

“I appreciate that these are graphic images and that they may be distressing or triggering to some people, however it is really important that we identify him and for that we need your help.

![The man was described as of a large overweight build, hairy, with scarring on the back of his head](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/10/15/916302033_1568672268640647_6642299317138263798_n.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“We don’t know whether he lives in Swindon or outside the county, which is why we’re asking that this is shared as widely as possible to assist our bid to identify him.

“If you think you recognise this man, please email DC Rogers at [2651@wiltshire.police.uk](mailto:2651@wiltshire.police.uk) and quote reference number 54240080286.

“Information can also be left anonymously with Crimestoppers on 0800 555111.”

On Thursday, Norman Macksoni, 48, of Sharnbrook, Bedfordshire, was found guilty of one charge of rape. Richard Wilkins, 62, of Toothill, Swindon, was found guilty of one count of rape and one of assault by penetration.

![The man also has a distinctive tattoo](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/10/05/913710689_1568672315307309_2742179462467842693_n.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32, of Swindon, was convicted of sexual assault by penetration and sexual touching, and Mohammed Hassan, 38, of Swindon, was found guilty of one count of sexual touching.

Young has pleaded guilty to a total of 56 charges made up of 14 counts of voyeurism, 11 of rape, 11 of administering a substance with intent, seven of assault by penetration, four of sexual assault and one of publishing obscene articles in relation to his wife.

The other charges are three charges of making indecent images of children, one of possessing prohibited images of children in July 2024 and four counts of possessing an extreme pornographic image between January 2019 and January 2024.

A sixth defendant, Dean Hamilton, 47, of Street, Somerset, admitted one charge of assault by penetration, two of sexual assault and one of rape.
