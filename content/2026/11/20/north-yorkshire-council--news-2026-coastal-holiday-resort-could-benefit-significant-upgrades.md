A coastal holiday destination could be given a new lease of life as part of a significant investment aimed at boosting tourism and helping to fund essential services across North Yorkshire.

Plans have been drawn up to expand and upgrade Filey Brigg Caravan and Camping Park, which attracts thousands of visitors to the Yorkshire coast each year.

Situated in Filey Brigg Country Park, to the north of the town centre, the nine-acre holiday park provides a popular base for people exploring Filey, the coastline and surrounding countryside.

The site currently offers 145 caravan, motorhome or camper pitches, 14 dedicated grass pitches for tents, and a range of visitor facilities.

To help secure the long-term future of the holiday park, a range of improvements has been proposed. This includes the creation of six new glamping lodges, additional hardstanding pitches, upgraded changing facilities, enhanced Wi-Fi and investment in the electrical infrastructure.

The proposals, which could represent an investment of up to £1.6 million, are intended to modernise the site, attract more visitors and maximise the amount of income, as well as support local businesses through increased footfall to the area.

![Filey Brigg holiday park building](https://www.northyorks.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-11/FileyBrigg1.jpg)

The plans – which will be subject to planning permission - remain at an early stage and will be progressed over the coming months, guided by feedback from councillors and members of the community.

Executive member for finance and property, Cllr Peter Wilkinson, said: “Filey Brigg is a popular holiday destination for families who come to enjoy the spectacular scenery and everything the Yorkshire coast has to offer.

“The proposals being brought forward would explore opportunities to invest in and modernise the holiday park, helping to safeguard its long-term future as a valuable council-owned asset.

“If approved, the investment could enhance the facilities, broaden the accommodation on offer and improve the visitor experience – which in turn would help the local economy and support local businesses.

“The proposals form part of our wider commitment to support North Yorkshire's coastline, with significant investment in major regeneration and development projects underway in coastal towns including Scarborough and Whitby.

“We want Filey Brigg to remain a high-quality visitor destination for years to come, and I look forward to receiving more details on the proposals early next year.”

The programme of works would include essential infrastructure improvements, such as upgraded electrical systems, alongside improvements to the site’s main changing facilities. They are intended to enhance the visitor experience and reduce disruption during poor weather, helping to extend the site's operating season.

The business case is due to be presented to members of the council’s executive in 2027. The proposed new glamping lodges are likely to generate additional annual revenue and recover their initial investment over approximately six years.

The upgraded hardstanding pitches could also help reduce weather-related closures and increase occupancy.
