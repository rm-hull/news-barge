Wallace and Gromit have taken up residence in the Christmas window at Weetons on West Park.

Store manager Jess Upson spent more than 350 hours making the display for the Harrogate food hall opposite the Stray.

It features nine handmade characters, with Feathers McGraw, Bitzer and Shaun the Sheep joining Wallace and Gromit.

Jess has designed and built every Weetons Christmas window since 2021.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b00243c934a03267e6.png)

The shop won Harrogate’s Christmas Shop Window Competition in 2022 and again last year with its Grinch-themed display.

Jess said:

> “Winning the Christmas Window Competition last year was such a proud moment, and I wanted to create something equally special for 2026.
>
> “Wallace & Gromit are characters that so many generations have grown up loving, so bringing them to life has been a real joy. From the first sketches to the finishing touches, it's been a huge project, but seeing families stop to admire the window makes every hour worthwhile.
>
> "I can't wait to see everyone's reactions, especially the little ones searching for our hidden Weetons bear!”

Families can look for the Christmas bear tucked among the characters in the West Park window.

It’s a nod to the new Santa’s grotto at Weetons’ Leeds Road store, where each child can choose a bear to take home.

The Weetons Christmas Journey to Santa’s Grotto runs from 5th to 24th December and includes cookie decorating with Mrs Claus and storytelling with Santa.
