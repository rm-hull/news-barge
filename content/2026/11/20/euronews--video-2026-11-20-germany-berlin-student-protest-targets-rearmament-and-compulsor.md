Young protesters took to the streets of Berlin beneath the city’s television tower to **oppose soaring defence budgets** and**the possible return of compulsory military service**, chanting *"Never again conscription"*** as smoke flares billowed above the march. Carrying signs emblazoned with ***"Hands off my future"*alongside* GTA VI*-inspired posters proclaiming Generation Z’s**readiness to push back, demonstrators warned that** Germany’s**drive to bolster its armed forces threatens their personal freedom and life choices.***"We’re trying to resist this because it’s our future,"*** one participant said.

The protest coincided with a broader day of youth-led demonstrations across **Europe**, with rallies also unfolding in** Rome**,** Frankfurt**and** London**. While local grievances differed, young crowds voiced shared anger at political leaders whom they accused of sidelining their generation’s priorities amid** rising military expenditure** and mounting global challenges.
