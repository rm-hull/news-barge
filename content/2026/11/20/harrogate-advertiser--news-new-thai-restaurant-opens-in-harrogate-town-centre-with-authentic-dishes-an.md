A new destination for authentic Thai cuisine has arrived in Harrogate, with Sila Thai now welcoming customers to its restaurant in the town centre.

The new restaurant has opened on Station Bridge in the former Dino’s premises, bringing authentic Thai cuisine, traditional recipes and warm hospitality to the heart of Harrogate.

Sila Thai aims to offer customers a true taste of Thailand, with a menu inspired by classic dishes and the vibrant flavours that have made Thai cuisine popular around the world.

The restaurant’s menu features a range of traditional favourites, including fragrant curries, stir-fries, noodle dishes and other Thai specialities, prepared using carefully selected ingredients.

A spokesperson for Sila Thai said: “We've put so much love and care into creating Sila Thai, a place where you can enjoy authentic Thai food, beautiful surroundings and the warmth of traditional Thai hospitality.

“We've absolutely loved meeting so many of you this week, welcoming you into our new restaurant and seeing you enjoy the food we've been so excited to share.

“It's been wonderful getting to know the local community, and we really appreciate everyone who's popped in, booked a table or simply wished us well.

“We're only just getting started, and we can't wait to meet even more of you over the coming weeks.”

The opening adds another independent dining option to Harrogate’s thriving food scene, giving residents and visitors a new place to enjoy Thai cuisine in the centre of town.
