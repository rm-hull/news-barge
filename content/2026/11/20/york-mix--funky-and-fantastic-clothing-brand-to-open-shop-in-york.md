A highly-rated fashion brand is preparing to open in a beautiful York shop.

The unisex clothing brand Goose & Gander is nearly ready to open its doors in the city centre.

It will be the bird-themed brand’s first bricks-and-mortar shop in the North.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/goose-and-gander-york-1.webp)

Goose & Gander will be opening at 27 Stonegate, in what was previously home to ladies wear shop Molly Browns.

An exact opening date is yet to be announced, the signs in the window indicate it’s ‘coming soon’.

Goose & Gander was first established in 2013, and has gone from a single high street shop to eight stores across the UK, in locations such as Cardiff, Bristol, and St Ives, as well as a thriving online store.

The clothing brand specialises in clothes for all genders, including knitwear, t-shirts, hoodies, and jackets – all embellished with their iconic goose designs.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/goose-and-gander-3.webp)

Each collection is designed by the in-house team in Chipping Sodbury, and the brand aims to create fresh, comfortable, and quality clothing.

Goose & Gander has a 4.6/5 rating from more than 3,700 reviews on Trustpilot. Customers “overwhelmingly had a great experience with this company” the website says.

One reviewer wrote: “Absolutely fantastic clothing ! They are funky cute and stylish and they keep their shape wash after wash.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/goose-and-gander-york-4.webp)

Another raved: “I just wish I had more money so I could buy everything.”

The shop front on Stonegate is an eye-catching blue, and the opening mustn’t be far off as clothing displays can be seen inside, tempting passersby who are out Christmas shopping.

Find out more about Goose & Gander at their website, and watch this space for more details on the opening.
