A York school says the catering company that provides its meals has responded to issues raised during a recent food hygiene inspection.

City of York Council awarded a one-star rating – meaning major improvement necessary – to Aspens Services at Huntington secondary school following an inspection on October 6.

The Food Standards Agency website says that for hygienic food handling, it was a matter of improvement necessary.

The cleanliness and condition of the facilities and the building was good.

However, for the management of food safety, inspectors decided it was a case of major improvement necessary.

The Press approached both Huntington School and Aspens Services for comment.

Aspens, who provide the catering at the school, supplied the school with the following comment.

The statement said: “The safety and wellbeing of Huntington School's students is always our top priority. The issue raised during the October 6 inspection was an isolated incident that was resolved the same day with immediate action taken.

“Our caterers have requested a re-inspection, and we are confident this will restore our five-star rating.”

Aspens Services was awarded the contract to provide catering services at the school in 2023.

The previous food hygiene rating, awarded in 2024, was five stars, meaning very good.
