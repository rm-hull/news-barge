More tools have been stolen from the back of a van in North Yorkshire.

Police report of a theft in Swarcliffe Road, Harrogate, overnight from November 15 into November 16.

They report a van was broken into and some power tools stolen.

In a statement, police ask: "Did you see anything? Do you have any home cameras?

"Were you driving in the area with an active dashcam? Do you have any information to give? If so, please email: [mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:mark.burrows@northyorkshire.police.uk)

Alternatively, people can call North Yorkshire Police on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.

Please quote reference 12260224919 when passing on information.

As the Press reported earlier today (FRI), North Yorkshire Police also said tools were also taken from a van between 8pm on Wednesday and 7am yesterday (Thur) on Church Lane, Ripon.
