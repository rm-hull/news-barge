A community food project is calling for new leadership to help continue its mission of reducing waste and supporting those in need.

Malton Free Fridge, based in the Wesley Centre, is seeking an unpaid volunteer chair to lead its efforts in distributing surplus food to the community.

Since its launch eight years ago, the project has saved 184 tonnes of waste food and fed over 134,000 people.

A spokesperson for the Free Fridge said: "Help us keep Malton’s Free Fridge flourishing!

"We are looking for a new unpaid volunteer Chair to help lead and support our friendly community project, who reduce food waste so good food is available to everyone.

"This is your opportunity to make a practical difference locally, while shaping the future of a worthwhile volunteer-led project."

The chair will support volunteers, build relationships with local food suppliers, and help create a welcoming environment for all involved.

The role includes chairing volunteer and committee meetings, liaising with suppliers, monitoring the Malton Free Fridge email account, and attending other meetings, on an ad hoc basis to share ideas and good practices.

The ideal candidate will be organised, approachable, and committed to reducing food waste and supporting the community.

They should also feel confident communicating with volunteers, suppliers, and community organisations.

The expected commitment is between two and six hours per week.

Malton Free Fridge is run by a working committee that shares responsibilities and offers practical support.

Anyone interested in the role or wanting to learn more can email [ryedalefreefridge@gmail.com.](mailto:ryedalefreefridge@gmail.com.)
