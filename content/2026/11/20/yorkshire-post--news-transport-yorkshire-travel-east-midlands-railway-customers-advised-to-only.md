# Yorkshire travel: East Midlands Railway customers advised to only travel if essential end of November amid major engineering work

## East Midlands Railway is advising customers to only travel if it is necessary at the end of this month as there will be major engineering work taking place.

East Midlands Railway (EMR) is operated by Transport UK and will undergo a major overhaul on Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29, 2026.

During the affected weekend, there will be no LNER trains between London King’s Cross and Peterborough as part of digital signalling upgrades by Network Rail.

![East Midlands Railways train at Sheffield Midland station.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmE2ZGE4MGExLTAyOWYtNGExYy04NjkzLTU0MmU5MjYyODhkMzoxZDcyYzQzZC0yNGQ4LTRmMjYtODBlNy00ZTNjNDhkNzZlODc=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


As a result, EMR services to and from London St Pancras are expected to be very busy.

EMR customers are advised that: a queuing system will be enforced at London St Pancras and Sheffield for EMR services; it may not be possible to board your intended service or get a seat; customers should allow extra time at the station and consider travelling on a weekday if possible; customers who have already purchased their tickets and whose journey is essential should follow the journey itinerary shown on their ticket or booking confirmation; and EMR tickets will not be valid on LNER rail or coach replacement services.

LNER customers who haven’t already booked a ticket with EMR and need to travel to or from London St Pancras should: travel on Thameslink services to/from Bedford; change at Bedford for the dedicated rail replacement coach services to/from Peterborough; and continue their journey from Peterborough on LNER service.

LNER tickets will not be valid on EMR rail services and LNER customers planning journeys to or from London St Pancras International or London Terminals are advised to use the ‘Change at: Bedford’ filter when searching for travel options.

Customer experience director at East Midlands Railway, Philippa Cresswell, said: “These upgrades are essential for improving the reliability of the railway, but they do mean services will feel different for customers travelling on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November 2026.

“Our Intercity services are expected to be extremely busy over the weekend, and customers should only travel if it’s essential.

“They should also allow extra time for their journey, particularly at St Pancras International, where a queuing system will be in place. Customers should also consider making their journey on Friday or Monday, if possible.

“We also strongly encourage customers to check their journey before travelling. Customers who normally travel via LNER should continue to do so and follow any alternative travel advice provided, so we can keep everyone moving as smoothly as possible.”
