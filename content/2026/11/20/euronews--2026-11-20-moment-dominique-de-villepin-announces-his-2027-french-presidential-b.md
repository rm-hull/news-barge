Former French Prime Minister Dominique de Villepin has announced he will run for president in 2027, returning to the political frontline with a warning about what he describes as the country's decline.

"This decay of France, this instinct for defeat, this spirit of death that is taking hold of us, these are all reasons that lead me to stand before you today and say: I am a candidate in the 2027 presidential election," he said at a press conference in Paris on Friday.

The 73-year-old former prime minister and foreign minister is best known internationally for his passionate 2003 speech at the United Nations Security Council, when he opposed the US-led invasion of Iraq.

De Villepin served as prime minister under former president Jacques Chirac between 2005 and 2007. He is now running with his political movement, La France humaniste.
