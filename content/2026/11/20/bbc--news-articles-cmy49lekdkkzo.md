**A man has been found guilty of the kidnap and murder of a woman who he groomed on a fetish website.**

Dwain Hall, 54, convinced 32-year-old Sonia Exelby from Southsea, Hampshire, to travel to the United States where he stabbed her to death.

On Friday he was found guilty following a trial at a court in Ocala, Florida, of kidnap and first-degree murder. He was then sentenced to life in prison.

Exelby's body was found buried in Marion County, Florida, on 17 October 2025 after she was reported missing from her home five days earlier.

The trial of Hall, whose online username was Alpha Sadist, was told he had been the "executioner" of Sonia Exelby and had demanded she pay $4,000 (£3,000) for him to kill her.

Hall was impassive as the unanimous verdict of the Florida jury was read out.

The court in Florida heard that the 32-year-old, who had struggled with mental illness, had wanted to die.

Assistant state attorney Amy Berndt said on the first day of the trial on Tuesday that Sonia Exelby met the defendant on a now-defunct website and "he manipulated her for years".

"He targeted her because of her mental illness and he exploited her for his own sadistic pleasure," she said.

"And when he finally got her alone, thousands of miles away from home, completely incapable of making any rational decisions, he stabbed her to death and buried her body."

Berndt said Exelby had been "trying to find someone who would kill her," adding: "Unfortunately there was no shortage of depraved men willing to kill Sonia in the most torturous and vile ways possible."

## 'Beautiful soul'

Sonia Exelby's sister, Gemma Isaj, who gave evidence during the trial, said following the guilty verdict: "A vulnerable person was preyed upon. A beautiful soul was taken.

"Our lives will never be the same again. We must learn a new way of being.

"When this tragedy began to unfold, Sonia really believed that she could stop others from getting hurt. And I believe that today's justice has done that.

"Furthermore, she shone the spotlight on the darkness of such sites, like Motherless.com.

"In some capacity, we will continue to fight for mental health in her name."

Her brother, Barry Wilton, said: "I know Sonia would be so distraught to see the aftermath of her death.

"Dwain, you pretended to care for Sonia. You didn't at all. You used her to fulfil your sick fantasies.

"You had her life in your hands. You had the opportunity to save her, but instead you preyed on her vulnerabilities and you took her from us."

Sonia Exelby was reported missing from her home on 12 October last year, by which time police established she had left the country.

After her body was found by US investigators Hall was traced by police through the attempted use of the victim's credit card by the roadside assistance company he owned.

Earlier, the jury heard that Exelby's consent was not a defence to the crime of first-degree murder.

They were also told consent meant intelligent, knowing, involuntary consent, and did not include coerced submission, through the use of exploitation, threats of force, or intimidation.
