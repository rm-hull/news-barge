# What the Brussels summit could mean for the UK-EU Brexit deal

Andy Burnham is in Brussels attending a UK-EU summit alongside other European leaders, as the prime minister looks to reshape Britain's relationship with the bloc.

The BBC's political editor Chris Mason looks at the key topics and which agreements could be made around food, energy and youth mobility with the EU.
