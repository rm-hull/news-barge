Archive photos of Harrogate and Knaresborough fill two 2027 calendars on sale at the towns' libraries for £6. Last year's sold out and raised £750.

Two 2027 calendars of archive photographs of Harrogate and Knaresborough are on sale at the towns’ libraries for **£6** each.

Library staff in both towns chose the pictures with the county’s archives team, North Yorkshire Council said on Thursday. The money goes back into the library service.

## What is in them

The Harrogate calendar includes:

* the old Town Hall, now the Mercer Gallery
* the inside of the former Winter Gardens
* old shopfronts
* a roller-skating rink in the Royal Hall Gardens

The Knaresborough edition shows the Market Place and the High Street, the castle and the River Nidd.

## Get one early

This is the third year the two libraries have made calendars. Last year’s sold out quickly at both and raised **£750**, the council said.

A third calendar this year, of Selby, has already sold out. The Harrogate and Knaresborough editions are on sale “while stocks last”.

“There is something special about seeing familiar landmarks from years gone by, giving us a chance to reflect on how our towns have changed over time,” said Cllr Simon Myers, the council’s executive member for culture and arts, whose brief includes libraries.

“Whether purchased as a gift or for yourself, every calendar sold helps support our libraries to provide the essential services we all rely on.”

## What it means for you

* **Where:** Harrogate Library and Knaresborough Library, over the counter.
* **Price:** £6.
* **Timing:** there is no reprint promised, so go soon if you want one as a Christmas present.
