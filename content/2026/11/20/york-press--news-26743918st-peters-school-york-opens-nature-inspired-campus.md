A nature-inspired transformation of an Early Years campus is already making a difference to pupils’ wellbeing.

Designed by architects brown + company, the project forms part of a wider redevelopment at St Peter’s School, one of the oldest schools in the UK, founded in AD 627.

The new campus supports the school’s shift from a three- to a two-school model and reflects a growing emphasis on nature-first learning environments.

Phil Hardy, head of St Peter’s Junior School, said: "Children's wellbeing is at the heart of the new Early Years Campus, designed by brown + company, and we are already seeing the positive effect this environment is having on the children.

"The architects have created an inspiring ‘inside-outside’ learning environment to strengthen connections with nature, encourage movement and exploration, and provide spaces for calm and creativity.

"The result is a nurturing, stimulating environment that offers new opportunities to further support children's emotional wellbeing and positive mental health."

Rachel Crooks of brown + company added: "The design approach for the new Early Years Campus has been rooted in nature from project outset, where teachers had emphasised the positive impact natural daylight and blended indoor/outdoor learning have on the mental health of pupils."
