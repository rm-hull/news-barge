MAGA Congressman Derrick Van Orden, who was at the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021, has berated a freshman Democrat for taking a photo that he says “disgraces” the historic building.

Van Orden railed against Rebecca Cooke — who defeated him in the midterms and will take his seat representing Wisconsin’s third congressional district in January — after she posted a photo of herself lying on the floor of the Rotunda and holding her phone up.

“That's a wrap on freshmen orientation! Commemorated my time here by taking a photo of the Capitol dome. No one yelled at me,” she wrote on X Thursday.

But Van Orden didn’t take kindly to the post, sarcastically writing, “Great way to start.”

“This disgraces the memory of the wounded Union Soldiers that died on this floor during the Civil War. Petulant children need harsh words,” he said.

![MAGA Congressman Derrick Van Orden — who was at the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021, but says he was not part of the violence — has berated freshman Democrat Rebecca Cooke for taking a photo that he says ‘disgraces’ the historic building](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/23/19/GettyImages-2227055264.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Van Orden was ruthlessly mocked for his comments, with people on social media bringing up what the MAGA base did at the Capitol nearly six years ago.

“If you think this is bad, wait until you find out what happened here on January 6th and who pardoned the people responsible,” one X user wrote.

“Spare us!” another wrote alongside photos of the violent mob.

A third commented: “You should probably shut the f\*\*\* up considering you allowed a violent mob to destroy the place.”

“Shut up and take the L. Talk about a sore loser,” someone else wrote.

“Wait til you hear what MAGA did on that floor on J6,” another said.

!['This disgraces the memory of the wounded Union Soldiers that died on this floor during the Civil War,' Van Orden said in response to Cooke lying on the floor of the Rotunda](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/23/00/HXft28rWEAECwKw.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

President Donald Trump’s supporters stormed through the Capitol on January 6 in a failed attempt to stop the certification of Joe Biden’s 2020 presidential victory. Tear gas filled the Rotunda as rioters waved pro-Trump flags. Trump went on to pardon more than 1,500 people charged with crimes connected with the riot.

Van Orden said in an op-ed for Wisconsin’s *La Crosse Tribune* published days after the attack that he was at the Capitol that day, but left “when it became clear that a protest had become a mob.”

“I left the area as to remain there could be construed as tacitly approving this unlawful conduct. At no time did I enter the grounds, let alone the building,” he wrote.

![Tear gas filled the Rotunda as rioters waved pro-Trump flags during the January 6 attack](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/23/51/GettyImages-1230453460.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

With Trump’s endorsement, Van Orden was elected to Congress in the 2022 midterms. He remained loyal to Trump in the lead-up to this year’s midterms, despite the president’s record-low approval ratings.

“Donald Trump is always welcome in my district,” Van Orden told *The Washington Post*. “He won this district three times in a row, and he’s been doing great things for America.”

Cooke then flipped the seat with more than 52 percent of the vote. The race was one of many races that the Democrats won as they gained control of the House and the Senate.
