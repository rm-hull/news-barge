Watch Grand Slam of Darts 2026 live streams as an all-star cast fight for one of the PDC's eight ranking majors at Aldersley Leisure Village, in Wolverhampton. The draw has been blown wide open by the shock second-round exits of defending champion Luke Littler and No.2 seed Luke Humphries, so it is all to play for as we reach the quarter-finals.

Sixteenth seed Jermaine Wattimena saw off a Littler comeback to triumph 10-8 on Wednesday, denying the world No.1 a shot at winning the title for a third successive year. He will take on fellow Dutchman Kevin Doets for a place in the semi-finals after his compatriot beat Cameron Menzies 10-5.

Friday's other quarter-final sees No.4 seed Gerwyn Price face Germany's Niko Springer. Three-time champion Price overcame Ryan Searle 10-7 in the second round and is looking to move one step closer to lifting the Eric Bristow Trophy for the first time since 2021. However, 'The Iceman' will need to stay cool against 26-year-old Springer, who beat former world No.2 Rob Cross 10-8 to reach this stage.

The other two quarter-finals take place on Saturday evening, with unseeded Tom Sykes facing another three-time champion, Michael Van Gerwen, after he edged past Humphries 10-9 in a Thursday-night thriller. Van Gerwen beat James Wade 10-8 in his second-round match. His fellow Dutchman Gian Van Veen is the highest-ranked player left in the draw after the third seed saw off Martin Schindler 10-7, setting up a last-eight clash with Nathan Aspinall, who beat Gabriel Clemens by the same margin.

The winners return for the semi-finals on Sunday afternoon before the two remaining players contest the final that evening, with the champion pocketing £200,000 in prize money. This year's edition had an unusual format, with 48 players divided into 16 groups of three. The winner of each group progressed to the second round, where the competition moved to a traditional knockout format.
