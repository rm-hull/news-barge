**The Pinewoods Conservation Group is seeking formal involvement in the planning appeal over Harrogate Spring Water’s proposed factory extension into Rotary Wood, as preparations begin for a public inquiry expected to start in February.**

Harrogate Spring Water is appealing against North Yorkshire Council’s refusal of its plans to extend its factory into the woodland. The appeal is expected to be considered at a public inquiry, provisionally scheduled to begin on 16 February 2027 and potentially lasting up to six days.

The Pinewoods Conservation Group says it has the opportunity to become a formal party to the appeal under what is known as Rule 6 status, or as an interested party.

This could allow the group to present evidence and question evidence submitted by other parties. It is currently liaising with the Planning Inspectorate about its involvement, working alongside Save Rotary Wood.

However, the group says preparing and presenting an effective case will require specialist planning and legal support, bringing significant costs.

## Council appoints external consultants

North Yorkshire Council has engaged external consultants to represent it at the appeal, defending the two reasons for refusal relating to landscaping and design.

The conservation group has welcomed the council’s decision, but says the consultants will be representing the council’s case and that separate professional support will still be needed for the community’s participation.

The current appeal timetable includes a deadline of 14 December 2026 for statements from the council and any Rule 6 parties, along with the statement of common ground and any further public comments.

A case management conference is expected by 17 December, with detailed evidence and witness information due by 19 January 2027. The provisional inquiry start date is 16 February 2027.

## Group asks members not to submit separate comments

The group says members who have received a letter from North Yorkshire Council inviting further comments should not submit separate individual responses at this stage.

It says this follows legal advice and is intended to allow the community’s evidence to be coordinated and presented clearly. Original objections have already been forwarded to the Planning Inspectorate and will be considered as part of the appeal.

Save Rotary Wood is also collecting personal accounts from people about how they use and value the woodland. These can be sent to [saverotarywoodagain@proton.me.](mailto:saverotarywoodagain@proton.me.)

The group is asking supporters to contribute to Save Rotary Wood’s crowdfunding appeal, which it says will help fund specialist legal advice and representation.

## Community meeting planned

A community update meeting is due to take place on Sunday 29 November, from 6.30pm to 7.30pm, at Friends Meeting House, 12A Queen Parade, Harrogate, HG1 5PP.

The meeting will discuss progress, the appeal process and the views of supporters.

The Pinewoods Conservation Group says it will provide further updates as its participation and the arrangements for the inquiry are confirmed.
