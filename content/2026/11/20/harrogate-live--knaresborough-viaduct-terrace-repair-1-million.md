Rebuilding the collapsed Viaduct Terrace wall in Knaresborough will cost over £1 million, a council report says. Ground tests start in December.

Rebuilding the collapsed retaining wall at Viaduct Terrace in Knaresborough is now expected to cost **more than £1 million**.

The figure is in a council report for the Harrogate and Knaresborough Area Committee, which meets on **Thursday 26 November**. In September the council awarded a contract with an estimated value of £350,000 for the same wall. The new report does not explain the difference.

## What happens next

The report sets out this timetable:

* **Week beginning 7 December 2026:** a ground investigation starts on the site.
* **July 2027:** construction is “currently programmed to commence”, subject to what the investigation finds and the approvals needed.

The council is working with the Environment Agency, Network Rail and Yorkshire Water on the scheme. The terrace was closed on safety grounds after the collapse and stays shut until the work is done.

## Why it costs so much

The wall collapsed in February 2026. The report says investigations found several causes:

* historic structural problems
* river scour and erosion around the viaduct’s foundations
* difficult ground conditions
* weaknesses in the wall built in 2010

The current plan is to rebuild the whole retaining wall rather than patch it. The council calls the project “unusually complex”. The site sits beside the River Nidd, beneath a working railway viaduct, and among listed buildings, homes, businesses and one of the town’s busiest visitor spots.

## The petition

The update follows a residents’ petition debated by the committee on 24 September. It asked the council to treat the landslip as an urgent priority and start stabilising the bank before another winter. Councillors then asked for a written progress report at every meeting until the repairs are finished. This is the first of those.

The report is for noting only. It asks the committee to make no decision, and says the full costs, risks and other details will go through the council’s normal approval process as the scheme develops.

## What it means for you

* **If you walk the riverside at Waterside**, Viaduct Terrace remains shut through this winter and, on the council’s timetable, until at least summer 2027.
* **If you live or trade nearby**, expect contractors on site for the ground investigation from the week of 7 December.
* **If you want to follow it**, the committee meets at 10am on 26 November. The agenda and papers are on the council’s website.
