German Socialists and Democrats MEP Tobias Cremer says a majority of Britons believe Brexit "was a mistake" and caused "economic damage" to the United Kingdom.

As the European Union and the United Kingdom hold a summit this Friday to deepen ties, Cremer argues that reopening the Brexit debate is the "democratic" and "honest" thing to do.

Asked about the possibility of the UK rejoining the EU, the MEP said he hoped Britons would vote in favour of returning to the bloc.
