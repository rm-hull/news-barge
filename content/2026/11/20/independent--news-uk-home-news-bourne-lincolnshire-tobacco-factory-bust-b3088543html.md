A cigarette factory capable of producing more than 200,000 illegal cigarettes a day has been shut down following a major raid.

Described as a “sophisticated processing plant” by HM Revenue and Customs, the factory was uncovered at an industrial site in Bourne, Lincolnshire, during a series of raids across England involving around 200 officers.

More than 1.4 million illegal cigarettes and around 5.5 tonnes of tobacco were seized from industrial premises searched during the operation, worth an estimated £4.5 million in duty.

HMRC says officers also seized £645,000 in cash from residential properties in Willenhall, West Midlands and Greenwich, London. Four gold bars worth around £213,500 were also seized from residential properties in Greenwich and Newham in east London.

The fully operational factory in Bourne contained large-scale machinery for cutting and drying tobacco and producing and packaging cigarettes.

![Four gold bars worth about £213,500 were also seized from residential properties in Greenwich and Newham, east London](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/10/55/01-Gold-bars-recovered-during-residential-searches.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Officers seized 808,840 cigarettes and around 4.8 tonnes of tobacco in various stages of processing and production. Upon inspecting the factory and its machinery, HMRC officers believed it could produce more than 200,000 cigarettes a day.

Twenty-one people were arrested during the operation on suspicion of offences relating to the fraudulent evasion of excise duty. They were later released pending further enquiries.

Further searches and seizures were carried out at industrial premises in the Brentwood area of Essex, Kirkby-in-Ashfield in Nottinghamshire, Grantham in Lincolnshire and Northampton in the East Midlands.

![Loose cigarettes recovered from the Bourne facility which was capable of producing more than 200,000 illegal cigarettes a day](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/10/09/08-Loose-cigarettes-recovered-from-the-Bourne-facility.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Officers seized 485,560 cigarettes, 104.35kg of tobacco and packaging materials from a unit in Grantham, 168,000 mixed-brand cigarettes and packaging materials were collected from an industrial premises in Kirkby-in-Ashfield, and 616kg of raw tobacco was seized from a unit in Brentwood.

Margaret Mousley, deputy director at HMRC’s Fraud Investigation Service, said: “This was a major operation which dismantled a huge cigarette factory, disrupting the organised criminality that sits behind it. Our investigation into that criminality is ongoing.

“The sheer scale of what our officers uncovered shows this was a well-established operation capable of putting millions of illicit cigarettes onto UK streets.

![Cigarette packaging machinery found at the Bourne facility in Lincolnshire](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/10/00/09-Cigarette-packaging-machinery-found-at-the-Bourne-facility.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Illegal tobacco funds organised crime, harms legitimate businesses and deprives our public services of vital funding.

“We encourage anyone with information about the illegal manufacture or sale of tobacco to report it to HMRC online.”

HMRC added that in 2025/26, record levels of seizures of around 1.9 billion cigarettes, worth around £1.1 billion, were made across the UK.
