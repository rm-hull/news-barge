# Police seize £84,000 worth of drugs and weapons in major Barnsley crime crackdown

## Police have seized more than £84,000 worth of drugs and multiple weapons during a major crackdown on crime across Barnsley.

On Wednesday (Nov 18), officers from across South Yorkshire Police joined forces for a day of action targeting serious acquisitive crime, antisocial behaviour, environmental offences and the protection of vulnerable people.

The operation began with early morning warrants at multiple addresses, where officers discovered more than £84,000 worth of Class A and Class B drugs, alongside several weapons.

Specialist teams, including the Tactical Support Group, Fortify team, Roads Policing Group, Roads Proactive Policing Team and neighbourhood officers, took part in the operation.

Acting on intelligence from local communities, officers also carried out high-visibility patrols, property visits and stop searches, focusing on suspected offenders linked to domestic assaults and other crimes.

![Suitcase with marijuana in ziplock bags](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/17/42/media.jpg?trim=366,2,320,2&width=640&quality=65)


Police mounted units were also deployed across the district as part of efforts to increase visibility and engage with residents.

Road policing teams carried out speed enforcement checks in Mapplewell, Athersley, Staincross and Royston, with 54 driving and speeding offences recorded.

Later in the day, officers joined representatives from Barnsley Council to visit fly-tipping hotspots and discuss ways to tackle environmental crime.

Inspector Michael Sheedy, who led the operation, said: “Barnsley is built on strong partnerships and shared pride.

“This week has brought officers and local partners from Barnsley Council together with a clear purpose: to protect vulnerable people from harm, disrupt those who target people's homes and livelihoods, and clean up anti-social behaviour and environmental issues that blight daily life.

“Everyone in Barnsley deserves to feel safe and this is at the heart of what we do. We are committed to visible, proactive policing that puts the community first. Today shows what we can accomplish when we stand together with our community to tackle the issues that matter the most to our town.

“Several arrests have been made for offences related to serious acquisitive crime. We've seized high value Class A and B drugs and weapons from our communities and various fines were issued for offences related to anti-social behaviour and environmental crime.

“I would ask, as our eyes and ears; please keep talking to us and reporting your concerns. Together, we will continue to make Barnsley a safe space for all.”
