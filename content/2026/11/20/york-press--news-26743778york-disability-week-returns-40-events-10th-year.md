## York Disability Week marks 10 years with packed programme

Walking football *(Image: Supplied)*

York Disability Week is returning for its 10th year with a packed programme of events.

The week will run from November 28 to December 5, offering 40 in-person and online, mostly free events, including sport, music, talks, workshops and more.

An information fair will feature 14 organisations offering advice on topics including benefits, fire safety, mobility equipment, and council services.

Marilyn Crawshaw, chair of the planning group, said: "Whether you’re disabled, a family member, PA or carer, a health or social care professional, a business keen to become more accessible, or just interested in learning more and meeting new people, York Disability Week is for you."

Sports sessions will include wheelchair rugby, inclusive football, table tennis, boxing, and adapted cycling.

Other activities will include singing, dancing, Pilates, poetry, and arts and crafts.

A panel discussion will focus on living with invisible disabilities, and a peer support group will also be available.

Talks will explore a range of topics including the representation of disabled people in stained glass, the history of D/deaf people in radical politics, and disability in post-First World War Britain.

York Castle Museum will also offer special openings with enhanced access.

More information and the full programme are available at www.yorkdisabilityweek.org.
