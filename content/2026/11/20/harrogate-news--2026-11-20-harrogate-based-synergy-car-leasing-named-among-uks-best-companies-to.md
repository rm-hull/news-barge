**Harrogate-based Synergy Car Leasing has been named one of the UK’s Best Companies to Work For, ranking 79th in a national list of 100 small and medium-sized businesses. The recognition comes as the company celebrates its 20th anniversary, having grown from a Harrogate start-up into a national vehicle leasing business.**

The results follow an employee engagement survey conducted earlier this year by Best Companies, which uses anonymous surveys to measure how employees feel about their workplace, with results benchmarked against organisations across the UK.

Synergy also achieved 39th place in the regional list of Best Companies to Work For in Yorkshire & The Humber and 18th in the sector ranking of Best Financial Services Companies to Work For in the UK. The business was additionally awarded a Best Companies ‘Ones to Watch’ accreditation.

The survey results reflect the work Synergy has undertaken to create a positive and supportive working environment, which forms part of its wider Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) strategy.

According to the survey, 93% of staff agreed that what is expected of them in their work is made completely clear, while 90% agreed that their team is fun to work with.

Employee wellbeing is supported through an Employee Assistance Programme, while the volunteer-led Synergy People’s Forum gives employees a voice in shaping the company’s culture. The forum also organises awareness events and charity fundraising, including support for a local football team’s junior grassroots initiative.

## Founded in Harrogate in 2006

Synergy Car Leasing was established in 2006 by entrepreneur Paul Parkinson, who founded the business as Synergy Automotive.

Parkinson brought considerable experience of the automotive industry to the venture, building a vehicle leasing and finance brokerage in Harrogate that would go on to develop a national customer base.

The company’s growth was already attracting attention a decade after its formation. In 2016, Synergy had a workforce of 23 and was reporting vehicle leasing volumes of more than £29 million in the previous financial year, with plans to increase that figure to £42 million.

In September 2016, the company announced six new appointments in six months, increasing its workforce in Harrogate to 26. The recruitment formed part of a wider expansion programme, which also included £100,000 of investment and new product offerings.

Synergy subsequently developed its position in the UK vehicle leasing market, supplying cars, vans and fleet management solutions to both business and personal customers.

The business has also adapted its services to changes in the automotive market, including the growth of electric and hybrid vehicles, alongside developments in business leasing, personal contract hire and salary sacrifice arrangements.

## Newable acquisition

A significant milestone came in 2020 when Newable acquired an initial majority stake in Synergy Automotive Limited.

Newable, which provides finance, advice and support to small and medium-sized businesses, subsequently completed its acquisition of the remaining shares in October 2023.

Paul Parkinson, who founded the business, continued as Executive Chairman following the acquisition.

The acquisition was intended to support the next phase of growth, with particular attention to the SME market, the development of fleet management services and the expansion of salary sacrifice schemes.

The Harrogate Informer previously reported on Newable’s completion of the acquisition in October 2023.

Synergy has continued to receive industry recognition, having been named Broker of the Year in both 2024 and 2025. The company also holds a Feefo 10 Years of Excellence Award.

## 20 years of experience

The company’s latest workplace recognition follows its 20th anniversary celebrations earlier this year, when it highlighted the contribution of long-serving employees.

Ten members of staff had each completed at least ten years with Synergy, representing a combined 117 years of service.

Client Services Director Gemma Manning was marking 18 years with the business, while Managing Director Will Voisey, senior account manager Jonathan Kyle, IT and Technical Services Director Jean-Paul Levahn and sales account manager Craig Denison had accumulated a combined 48 years of service.

A further five employees were celebrating ten years with the company.

Will Voisey joined Synergy in 2013 as a sales account manager before progressing through the organisation to become Managing Director.

The anniversary milestones were reported by the Harrogate Informer in June 2026.

The company says its approach to employee engagement forms part of its wider ESG commitments, with staff involvement, personal development and wellbeing central to its workplace strategy.

The latest recognition adds another milestone for a Harrogate business that has grown over two decades from its original foundations under founder Paul Parkinson into a national vehicle leasing specialist.
