## 'Affordable fast fashion' store opens in prime York site

The exterior of Fabrik in Coney Street. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

A new store has opened in York offering “affordable fast fashion.”

Fabrik is operating on the first floor of the former Next in Coney Street.

That closed last November, before re-opening in Coppergate.

Fabrik took the empty site and began selling its wares two weeks ago.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739549.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The store interior. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Manager Bindy Singh says the York store is just ‘temporary’ at the moment but it hopes to stay ‘long term’ but this was up to the landlord.

Bindy says Fabrik was keen to try and bring something different to York and the opportunity arose to take the former Next site.

He said: “There was an opportunity. This site became available”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739550.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The store interior. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

Fabrik has other stores in London, he continued.

“We offer fast affordable fashion basically, nothing too expensive.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739556.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There is plenty of choice. *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

“It’s what everybody really wants considering the times.”

When the Press called on Thursday lunchtime, there were a few customers.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739605.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Another view of the clothes on offer *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

You could see bags, dresses, perfumes, jackets, jumpers, trackpants, nightwear, the lot.

It all looked well-priced for value.

But Bindy says business has been quiet since opening two weeks ago.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739616.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christmas jumpers in the store *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

He continued: “It’s quiet everywhere. Christmas isn’t what it used to be. You just have to keep trying.”

How well trade is, Bindy says, won’t be known until January or February when the novelty of the Coney Street store has worn off.

He added: “Just come to the high street rather than the retail park.”
