Harrogate favourite Weetons has unveiled its annual Christmas window, bringing the much-loved world of Wallace & Gromit to life at its West Park store front.

The magical new display is the result of an lot of work by Store Manager Jess Upson, who spent more than 350 hours handcrafting 11 characters inspired by the iconic Aardman animations, including Wallace, Gromit, Feathers McGraw, Shaun the Sheep and Bitzer, transforming the West Park storefront into another spectacular display.

Renowned for its elaborate handmade Christmas displays, the unveiling of this year’s stunning effort follows Weetons’ win in the 2025 Harrogate Christmas Shop Window Competition.

“Winning the Christmas Window Competition last year was such a proud moment, and I wanted to create something equally special for 2026,” said Jess Upson, Store Manager at Weetons West Park.

“Wallace & Gromit are characters that so many generations have grown up loving, so bringing them to life has been a real joy.

“From the first sketches to the finishing touches, it's been a huge project, but seeing families stop to admire the window makes every hour worthwhile.

“I can't wait to see everyone's reactions, especially the little ones searching for our hidden Weetons bear!”

Situated opposite The Stray, Weetons West Park has become a must-visit destination during the festive season, with its imaginative window displays attracting visitors year after year.

Having previously won the competition in 2022, Weetons claimed the top prize once again in 2025 with its spectacular handmade Grinch-themed display.

There is also a special surprise for younger visitors at Weetons this year, with a hidden Weetons Christmas bear linking the display to the brand-new Weetons Christmas Journey to Santa's Grotto at its flagship Leeds Road store.

Running from December 5-24, Christmas celebrations at Weetons' flagship Leeds Road store also include festive workshops, Breakfast with Santa and the launch of The Weetons Christmas Journey to Santa's Grotto.

Weetons' Christmas Hamper Collection is also returning for 2026, featuring premium Yorkshire produce and artisan treats, with nationwide delivery available.
