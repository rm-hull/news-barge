## West End musical hit coming to Scarborough for outdoor showing

Six, the West End musical sensation reimagining the lives of Henry VIII’s wives, will be performed at the Scarborough Open Air Theatre *(Image: Supplied)*

Pop royalty will take centre stage in Scarborough next summer.

Six, the West End musical sensation reimagining the lives of Henry VIII’s wives as modern pop icons, will be performed at the Scarborough Open Air Theatre from June 4 to 6.

Part of its first-ever outdoor tour, Six Outdoor – The Homecoming Tour will visit 10 UK locations in celebration of the show’s 10th anniversary.

Kenny Wax, producer of the tour, said: "We are delighted to have partnered with Live Nation’s concert division Cuffe and Taylor on this exciting season of outdoor performances in 10 truly stunning and individually unique venues to celebrate 10 extraordinary years of Six."

The show follows the success of Mamma Mia! at the Scarborough venue in 2023.

Peter Taylor, co-founder of Cuffe and Taylor, said: "This is exactly what Scarborough Open Air Theatre was built for back in the 1930s, so we are thrilled to be kicking off this run of dates in this incredible venue."

The tour will also visit venues including The Piece Hall in Halifax, Lincoln Castle, Cardiff Castle, and Hampton Court Palace.

Tickets go on sale at 12pm Thursday, November 26. An exclusive 24-hour presale is available from 12pm Wednesday by signing up at SixTheMusical.com/outdoor
