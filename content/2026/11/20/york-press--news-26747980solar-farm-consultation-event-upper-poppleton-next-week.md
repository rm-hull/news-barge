## Consultation event next week on proposed solar farm near York

A public consultation event takes place next week *(Image: Soonthorn - stock.adobe.com)*

A public consultation event takes place next week concerning a planned solar farm near York.

British Renewables is staging the event on Tuesday November 24 in the Poppleton Centre, Upper Poppleton, from 3.30pm to 7.30pm.

The Scagglethorpe Solar Park is planned for land west of Newlands Lane, Upper Poppleton.

The company says the scheme would generate enough electricity to power approximately 17,878 homes a year over a 40-year lifetime.

It has posted online: “Alongside clean energy generation, we’re designing the project to support nature, work closely with local communities and create positive outcomes that last beyond the lifetime of the solar park.”

Scagglethorpe Solar Park is currently in development.

British Renewables further posted online: “We are progressing environmental surveys, technical assessments and project design work ahead of a planned planning application submission in March 2027.

Subject to planning approval, a decision is anticipated in March 2028.”

As part of its proposals, the company says it would also provide a Community Benefit Fund of up to £220,000 for local projects.

The company also posted: “We’re committed to working openly with local communities throughout the development of Scagglethorpe Solar Park.

Community feedback helps shape our projects and informs the decisions we make as proposals evolve.”

For details and to give feedback, go to britishrenewables.com/scagglethorpe.
