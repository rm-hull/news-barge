North Yorkshire Council is proposing new parking and speed restrictions across the region.

The council aims to amend the existing "North Yorkshire County Council (Prohibition of Waiting & Loading & Provision of Parking) - District of Selby - Consolidation Order 2013" to introduce new parking restrictions on Main Street in Thorganby, Myrtle Avenue in Brayton, and Main Street in Appleton Roebuck.

In Thorganby, the changes propose prohibiting waiting on Main Street, on the west side from 30 metres before the driveway entrance to the park to the north for 122 metres, at all times.

Meanwhile, in Brayton, Myrtle Avenue's north side, from 20 metres from the eastern radius curve junction with Langdale Road to the east for 30 metres, will also see new parking restrictions.

The council plans to introduce 'no waiting' rules between 7.30am and 9.30am and 2.30pm and 4.30pm.

Appleton Roebuck's Main Street will also be affected, with no waiting between 7.30am and 9.30am and 2.30pm and 4.30pm on weekdays on the east side from the eastern kerb radius at the junction of Daw Lane to the north-east for 18 metres.

Residents are encouraged to review the draft orders, maps showing the affected roads, and the council's reasons for the proposed orders.

These are available for inspection at County Hall, Northallerton, and at Selby Library during normal office hours, or online at northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map or northyorks.gov.uk/traffic-regulation-orders.

Those wishing to object to the proposed orders can send their objections in writing to the Area 7 Selby Highways Office, Canal Road, Selby, North Yorkshire, YO8 8AG, or via email to [Area7.Selby@northyorks.gov.uk](mailto:Area7.Selby@northyorks.gov.uk), or through the website link, by December 10.

In addition to the parking restrictions, North Yorkshire Council is also proposing a new speed limit on Busk Lane, Church Fenton.

The proposed order aims to enforce a 30mph speed limit, starting 81 metres from the southern kerb radius for the entrance to the Royal Air Force cadets training base, to 31 metres north from the northern side of the junction for Skelf Street.

The draft order and a map showing the affected roads, along with the council's reasons for the proposed speed limit, can be inspected at County Hall, Northallerton, and at Selby Library during regular hours.

They can also be viewed online at northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map or northyorks.gov.uk/traffic-regulation-orders.
