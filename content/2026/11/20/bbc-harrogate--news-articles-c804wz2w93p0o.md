**A driver who broke an elderly man's skull when he knocked him down in a hit-and-run has been jailed.**

Luke Scott, 34, was jailed for 26 months at Teesside Crown Court on Thursday after pleading guilty to causing serious injury by careless driving.

The 81-year-old victim was crossing Northallerton High Street just after 20:00 GMT on 12 March when he was struck by the black Ford Fiesta Scott was driving.

In a statement, the victim's wife said: "All I can say is that his life, my life, the lives of our children and our grandchildren have been ruined."

Hayley Richardson, 34, who was a passenger in the car with Scott, received a nine-month sentence suspended for 18 months for perverting the course of justice.

The collision was such that the elderly man was thrown into the air, North Yorkshire Police said.

He was knocked unconscious and suffered a fractured skull and brain haemorrhage.

The man also had fractures to his face, pelvis and legs.

Police traced the pair through the car's registration after finding the partially burnt Fiesta the following day in Northallerton Rigg.

## 'Destroyed my life'

The elderly man's wife said: "Since that day, our lives have changed completely and bears no resemblance to what it was before.

"We are no longer able to do any of the things that we previously enjoyed as a couple or as individuals and the disruption that has been caused to us cannot be measured."

She added: "Two complete strangers have taken away any enjoyment of life that he might have once had, taken away his hobbies, his friends, his ability to think.

"Through doing this to him they have also destroyed my life and my enjoyment of it."

Scott, of Brickside Way in Northallerton, was also handed a driving ban for three years and eight months.

Richardson, of Berry Close in Great Yarmouth, must also do 80 hours of unpaid work.
