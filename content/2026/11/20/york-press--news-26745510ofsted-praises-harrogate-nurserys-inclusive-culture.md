![Partou Playaway Day Nursery and Pre-school in Harrogate has achieved ‘expected standard’ in all six key evaluation areas following an Ofsted inspection](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21739865.jpg?type=mds-article-962)

A nursery has been praised for its culture of inclusion and high expectations for every child.

Partou Playaway Day Nursery and Pre-school in Harrogate received a glowing report from Ofsted, which highlighted its “safe, secure and welcoming environment.".

The inspector praised the nursery’s ambitious curriculum, noting that children benefit from a calm and purposeful atmosphere.

Joanna Waller, nursery manager, said: "We are absolutely delighted with our Ofsted report and so pleased that it reflects what makes Playaway so special.

"It captures the warm, welcoming and inclusive environment we strive to create every day, where children and families feel valued, supported and truly part of our nursery family."

The report commended the nursery’s support for children with special educational needs and/or disabilities, describing its transition planning as “especially successful".

Ofsted also recognised the nursery’s “very thorough” handover process between staff and families, ensuring that important information is shared so that children's needs are met as effectively as possible.

Sam Sleeman-Boss, head of early years for Partou, said: "It is an impressive report which reflects the excellent work and professionalism of the Playaway team.

"I am particularly pleased to see the culture of inclusion highlighted, with tailored support for children who speak English as an additional language deemed to be particularly effective."
