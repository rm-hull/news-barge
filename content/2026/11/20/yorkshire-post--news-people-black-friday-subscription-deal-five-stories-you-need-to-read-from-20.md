# Black Friday subscription deal: Five stories you need to read from 2026

## Our Black Friday deal is now on and there has never been a better time to become a subscriber of The Yorkshire Post.

My name is Daniel Sheridan and I am the Head of Engagement for the paper.

As a teenager, I used to deliver The Yorkshire Evening Post around West Leeds homes.

Now, I feel very privileged to work here alongside award-winning regional journalists.

With our £12 annual Black Friday offer now live, I wanted to point out five of our premium stories from this year I don’t think you can miss.

\_\_\_\_\_

Back in March, Catherine Scott put together a series on some of the best restaurants across our region according to food reviewers for The YP. There are some amazing eateries in Yorkshire.

Earl of Harewood David Lascelles released a book about his father’s experience as a prisoner of war. He spoke to Laura Reid about the remarkable story in October.

The return of Karl Bushby to Hull after a 28 year around the world trip was national news. Rachael Grealish provided our subscribers with an exclusive story with Karl’s son in the run up to his return. Read it above.

Search and Trends writer Liana Jacob often reports on historical figures. This was no exception and went down very well with our subscribers - check it out above.

Another firm favourite of a topic with our subscribers, anything airport related performs very well. This story by Eibhlis Gale-Coleman about the cafe bar alongside Leeds Bradford Airport went down a storm.
