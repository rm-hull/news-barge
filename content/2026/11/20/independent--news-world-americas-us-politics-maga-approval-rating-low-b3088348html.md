Public support for the Make America Great Again movement has dropped to its lowest net favorability rating on record, leading high-profile allies to distance themselves from the brand.

Among those pulling back is tech billionaire Elon Musk, who recently rejected the MAGA label after losing nearly $500 million backing candidates aligned with President Donald Trump in the 2026 midterm elections.

Speaking at *The New York Times* DealBook Summit, Musk said he felt “foolish” for spending hundreds of millions of dollars on the midterm races.

“American elections are not just about money,” Musk said.

During the same summit appearance, Musk sought to separate himself from the movement altogether.

![Prominent allies of President Donald Trump are beginning to distance themselves from his Make America Great Again movement after polling data showed public support for the brand dropping to a record low](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/0/07/GettyImages-2176995336.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“I’m not MAGA,” Musk told *The New York Times*. “Well, at least I’m not, like, I’m not sure what MAGA means, but I don’t think I’m, like — if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies of the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that.”

Trump addressed Musk’s remarks on Thursday in a phone interview with NBC News while returning from a trip to Asia, calling the SpaceX and Tesla CEO “a friend of mine” with “very good intentions.”

“MAGA, to me, means something very specific, and when people veer from it, they don’t do very well,” Trump told NBC News, noting that Musk “was with me the whole — he traveled with me.”

The comments mark a shift for Musk, who previously led the administration's Department of Government Efficiency — overseeing major reductions across federal agencies — and appeared alongside Trump at the 2025 Conservative Political Action Conference, where he held a chainsaw onstage.

During a broadcast segment featuring footage from the DealBook Summit, CNN host John Berman pointed out the contrast between Musk’s recent remarks and his past support.

“I mean, he’s basically wearing a MAGA hat here!” Berman said. “So, why might he be saying now he’s not that way?”

![Tech billionaire Elon Musk says he feels ‘foolish’ after spending nearly $500 million on Trump-backed midterm candidates, distancing himself from the movement as its net favorability drops to 41 points underwater](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/0/50/GettyImages-2217853041.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

CNN senior data reporter Harry Enten attributed Musk’s pivot to declining public sentiment toward the movement, which he said reached its lowest net favorability rating on record in October 2026 polling at 41 percentage points underwater, including a net rating of minus 61 points among independent voters.

“I’m not the puppet!'” Enten said on air. “Why is he taking the ball and trying to run to the other side of the field? I’ll tell you why he’s taking the ball and trying to run to the other side of the field! MAGA’s Lowest net favorability rating ever, ever, ever, ever! In October of 2026, the latest poll we have available, 41 points underwater! Forty-one points! My goodness gracious.”

Enten added: “Down there in the Gulf of Mexico! With independents, it’s -61 points. No wonder he doesn’t want to be associated with MAGA! It is a brand that is failing amongst the American public. We saw it in the 2026 midterm elections. We saw it in those races that Elon Musk spent hundreds of millions of dollars in. Simply put, he’s a businessman. He knows MAGA at this point is failing amongst the American public, and that’s why he wants to take his ball and go home.”

The polling slump coincides with broader challenges for the White House, with Trump's overall approval rating sitting between 32 percent and 38.3 percent, as reported by *The New York Times*.
