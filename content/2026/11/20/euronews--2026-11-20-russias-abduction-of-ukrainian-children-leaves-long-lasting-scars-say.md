The abduction of Ukrainian children by Moscow is among the most harrowing consequences of Russia’s invasion of Ukraine. According to the humanitarian initiative Bring Kids Back UA, at least 20,000 children have been abducted, although the true scale of the operation remains difficult to establish.

Taken from their homeland and dispersed across the Russian Federation, these children are not only separated from their families and communities but are also subjected to efforts to erase their Ukrainian identity. Some are reportedly being raised to reject their heritage and even taught to hate their own country.

Maksym Maksymov, director of Bring Kids Back UA, warns that this “systematic brainwashing” will leave “long-lasting scars on the self-perception of these young people”.

His organisation has helped bring more than 3,000 children back to Ukraine. Many of those rescued had suffered medical neglect or had been fed false narratives that their parents had abandoned them.
