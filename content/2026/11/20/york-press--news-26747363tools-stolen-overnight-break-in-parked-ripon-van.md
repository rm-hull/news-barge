Tools were stolen from a van during an overnight break-in in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police say the break-in took place between 8pm on Wednesday and 7am yesterday (Thur) on Church Lane, Ripon.

The parked van was broken into and the tools were taken from the back.

A police statement said today (Fri): "If you witnessed anything suspicious in the area during this time, or have any CCTV, Ring doorbell, dashcam, or other video footage that may assist with enquiries, please contact North Yorkshire Police quoting incident number 12260226976."

North Yorkshire Police has given crime prevention advice following the break-in.

"Unfortunately, tools are often targeted by thieves due to their high value and ease of resale," the statement continued.

Vehicle owners are encouraged to:

• Remove tools from vehicles overnight whenever possible.

• Store tools securely in a locked building when not in use.

• Use tool marking or forensic security marking products to help identify stolen items.

• Fit additional vehicle security such as upgraded locks, tool safes, alarms, or tracking devices.

• Park in well-lit areas covered by CCTV where available.

• Keep an up-to-date record of tool serial numbers and photographs.

The statement added anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police via 101 or through the North Yorkshire Police website, quoting incident 12260226976.
