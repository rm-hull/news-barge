# Panorama's extreme weather programme failed to explain full extent of climate emergency: Yorkshire Post Letters

## Graham Wroe, Sheffield.

BBC’s Panorama focused on the increase in extreme weather in this country, featuring more drought, wildfires, winter rainfall, storms and floods. It’s worth watching, but fell short of what people calling for a National Emergency Briefing have been demanding.

Time was dedicated to setting out how our climate has changed since the 1970s, using the Climate Stripes to illustrate this, the need for investment to make school buildings more resilient and the increased demands on the NHS during heatwaves. The importance of reducing carbon emissions to achieve Net Zero by 2050 was emphasised.

Miatta Fahnbulleh MP, the Secretary of State for Energy Security and Net Zero, indicated that reducing people’s bills was more important than the climate crisis.

But drilling for more oil and gas in the North Sea won’t bring down our bills, as it will be sold on international markets.

We can’t afford the extra emissions that Jackdaw (and possibly Rosebank) will create.

So what was missing from the programme?

No one argued to end our addiction to fossil fuels according to the short time scale scientists are recommending.

Threats to our food supplies were only briefly mentioned.

Panorama failed to examine the global problems of mass displacement and increased geopolitical tensions, like disputes over water supplies.

Most importantly, climate feedbacks and devastating tipping points were completely ignored.

The BBC censors the news that around 1 billion people will be killed by 2C of global heating, and 4 billion people could be killed by 3C of heating. Collapse of the circulation system that brings us hot water from the southern Atlantic will render most of the UK uninhabitable, but this was not mentioned.

Jon Fuller, Founder of Climate Genocide Act Now, is attempting to take the BBC to a Judicial Review for failing to tell the truth about the climate emergency.

He wrote on Facebook: “The science is now clear—global heating has accelerated, and the 'energy imbalance' data strongly indicates that we will eventually reach 8C of global heating.

"We face a sixth mass extinction of wildlife and the death of billions of people.

“If the public were to be told the full horrifying facts, the people would demand emergency action and lifestyle changes to attempt to save our young.”

The National Emergency Briefing is being screened all over the country. Look out for a showing near you.
