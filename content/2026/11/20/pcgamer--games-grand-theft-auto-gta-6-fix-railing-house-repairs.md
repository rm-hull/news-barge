One of the last tasks in GTA 6's home repair mission for Jason is to fix the railing. These tasks are important if you want Lucia to have a positive welcome when she gets out of jail, so they're well worth doing if you plan to foster a romance between the two criminal lovers.

You have to fix the rooftiles, put up the panels in Jason's bedroom and paint them, repair the stairs up to the decking at the front, and finally, fix the railing. This one is slightly less obvious, but you just need to:

* Open the sliding door and head out onto Jason's back decking.
* Go down the stairs into the area with the BBQ and workout equipment.
* You'll spot the paintbrush interact icon on the gap in the railings just between where the ladder and weights are.

You can also just watch the clip above if unsure. The other tasks are relatively simple if you haven't found them yet also. Climb the ladder outside the front door to repair the roof tiles, interact with the stacked panels at the foot of Jason's bed to put them up, and you can repair the stairs at the front by interacting with the pile of planks at the bottom.
