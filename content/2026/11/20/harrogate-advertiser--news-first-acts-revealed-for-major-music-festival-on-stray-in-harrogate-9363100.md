The first phase of big name acts has been revealed for next year’s major music festival on the Stray in Harrogate.

Gearing up for its fourth sell-out year, this first wave is only a hint of what's to come at Love to be... Festival 2027 with more major names still to be revealed early next year.

The next edition will also see the introduction of a brand-new Café Mambo Ibiza stage partnership.

Taking place on Saturday, September 4, organisers will once again transform Harrogate’s beautiful town centre parkland into a vibrant playground for house music lovers, complete with colourful entertainers, stilt walkers, fairground attractions and immersive performers.

Following three sold-out editions, the legendary house music brand reveals house music heavyweights Hannah Wants and Sandy Rivera, legendary outfit K-Klass, Allister Whitehead, Trimtone and Juanito Chanclas, alongside a host of Love to be... favourites, ahead of some major additions to be announced in early 2027.

Heading up the first wave of artists for 2027 is Hannah Wants, the Birmingham-born DJ, producer and broadcaster whose high-energy house sound has earned her a global following and appearances everywhere from Creamfields and Tomorrowland.

The Music Factory Stage, named after Love to be’s original Sheffield home, welcomes a special collaboration with Café Mambo Ibiza and features pioneering house act K-Klass, legendary DJ Allister Whitehead, Balearic favourite Juanito Chanclas, alongside Sinclair and Scott Harris.

Co-founded in Sheffield in 1994, Love to be... has spent more than three decades at the heart of UK club culture.

From hosting house music pioneers including Frankie Knuckles, Masters At Work and Basement Jaxx through to today's global dance music stars Claptone, Prunk and Beave, the brand has built a reputation for championing quality, community and unforgettable dancefloor experiences around the globe.

Under the stewardship of Tony Walker and Marc Dennis, Love to be... continues to unite generations of clubbers while remaining firmly focused on the future.

Tickets are already on sale now, priced from £51.50 plus booking fee.
