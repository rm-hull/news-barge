Below-freezing temperatures are forecast for much of the UK overnight ahead of a widespread frost this weekend.

The Met Office is expecting possible lows of -9C in parts of Scotland, with temperatures set to plummet widely across the country.

Throughout the day on Friday, temperatures will reach highs of 4C in areas of Scotland, 8C in Northern Ireland, and 10C in parts of southern England and Wales.

In its forecast for Friday night, Met Office meteorologist Claire Nasir said: “It will be severe in the north, so we could see temperatures around -8C or -9C, and further south we’re expecting some sub-zero temperatures.

She said there could be “some freezing fog patches, particularly across in central southern areas and in Northern Ireland”.

![Showers are expected on Saturday and Sunday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/09/2305014558..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

On Saturday, dry and fine conditions are forecast for most of the UK, with some isolated showers expected in the northeast of Scotland.

Temperatures will reach highs of around 12C in southern parts of England and Wales, but stay in the single figures for the rest of England and Wales.

Widespread frost is forecast again on Sunday and showers will become “increasingly heavy” in parts of southern England.

Parts of Northern England will experience highs of around 7 to 8C, while temperatures are due to hit 11C in Northern Ireland.

Much colder conditions are forecast in Scotland, where highs of 4C are forecast widely.

Meteorologist Aidan McGivern said: “It will be a frost start for Scotland and Northern England. Perhaps not quite as cold as Saturday morning, but another widespread frosty one.

“A breeze from the east and northeast will bring some showers into the east of Scotland and northeast England.”

## Met Office five-day forecast

**Today** Cloud and rain clears the south through the morning, with some sunny spells developing later. Chillier, but brighter further North, with a few scattered showers along North Sea coasts, turning wintery in the far north. A chillier day for most.**Tonight** Largely dry, though some showers in the far north. Clear skies allow for widespread frost development. With light winds, fog patches develop across central and southern England and Northern Ireland.**Saturday** A crisp start to the weekend, with long sunny spells through the day. Skies turn cloudier in the southwest with rain later and strong winds developing towards the coast overnight.**Outlook for Sunday to Tuesday**

Rain persists across the southwest with strong winds on Sunday, drier elsewhere with sunny spells. Frosty overnight. Winds ease Monday, before strengthening again as rain arrives from the west Tuesday.
