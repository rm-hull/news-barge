# White supremacist guilty of plot to firebomb Hull migrant hotel

## A white supremacist has been convicted of plotting to firebomb a migrant hotel and kill as many occupants as possible.

Mikka Anderson, 24, tried to recruit an accomplice to his plan, telling him he would fire petrol bombs at the Royal Hotel in Hull, which had been the scene of previous protests.

But that man went to the police and informed them of Anderson’s scheme, telling jurors he thought terrorism was “disgusting” and that innocent people should not be killed.

![Mikka Anderson was convicted at Newcastle Crown Court (Counter Terrorism Policing North East/PA)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/16/v2-image-production-9a33e0901f673e6ddd5f1f9a908e3fa3Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1Mjc3MTIz-2.86551133.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The defendant, from Hull, denied preparing an act of terrorism including by manufacturing Molotov cocktails and by conducting “hostile reconnaissance” on the hotel.

He also denied possessing knives and other weapons for terrorist purposes, but was convicted of both counts following a trial at Newcastle Crown Court.

![The Royal Hotel in Hull](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJlY2FjN2E2LTRlZjAtNGNiYS1hOTQ1LWJkZjkxMzAyMDFhYTozMmFhMDI5Zi1iNjE1LTQ4YTQtYjlhMi0wOTBmMTdlYTQ5N2Y=.png?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Mrs Justice Lambert told the defendant he will be sentenced next year once a psychological report is completed and an assessment of his dangerousness is made.

She told him: “It is very likely indeed, if not inevitable, that I will be imposing a very significant custodial sentence.”
