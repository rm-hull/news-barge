**A volunteer who has spent almost a decade supporting young runners at Ripon Runners has been recognised with an Unsung Hero of the Year award from England Athletics.**

Ripon Runner Helen Cox received the award at England Athletics’ 2026 Yorkshire and Humberside Regional Volunteer Awards, held at Oulton Hall in Leeds.

Cox has been at the heart of the junior section at Ripon Runners for almost a decade, serving as the club’s Minis and Juniors Coordinator. Her role involves coaching, administration and supporting young athletes and fellow volunteers.

In its award citation, England Athletics said:

> **England Athletics:** For almost a decade, Helen has been at the heart of the junior section at Ripon Runners. As Minis and Juniors Coordinator, she gives countless hours to coaching, administration and supporting both young athletes and fellow volunteers. Her inclusive and caring approach has helped create a welcoming environment where children can develop confidence, skills, and enjoyment in athletics.

England Athletics is the membership and development body for grassroots athletics and running. It works with a network of around 1,800 affiliated clubs and member organisations, supporting athletes and runners of all abilities and backgrounds.

The organisation’s Regional Volunteer Awards were established in 2008 to recognise the contribution made by individuals and clubs to athletics and running. Categories include Club of the Year, Unsung Hero of the Year, Unsung Project of the Year and Services to Athletics and Running.

## Further recognition for Helen and the club

The England Athletics award follows further recognition for Cox earlier this year at the St Wilfrid’s Stars Awards, organised by Ripon Together. She received the Special Award for Individual Supporter of Youth, recognising her “commitment to junior running, and her work to make it fun and enjoyable for all young participants”.

Ripon Runners also received a Sports and Activity Group award at the same event. The club was recognised as a “friendly, inclusive club welcoming people across age groups and abilities”.

> **Helen Cox:** It was such a lovely surprise to receive these awards, and I’m still feeling very emotional about it. Ripon Runners means such a lot to me, and supporting the junior members is so rewarding.
>
> Of course, it wouldn’t be possible without the fantastic support that I’ve received from club members and volunteers, so I’d like to thank them for all the help that they have given me over the years.

## About Ripon Runners

Ripon Runners was founded in 1985 by local milkman Cameron Weatherburn. The club has grown into an established part of the city’s running community, welcoming runners of different ages, genders and abilities, with a dedicated junior section.

Based at Ripon Rugby Club, the club meets on Tuesday and Thursday evenings throughout the year, alongside regular weekend runs and social events.

The club caters for a range of running interests and abilities, providing opportunities for people to take up running, improve their fitness and take part in organised events. Its junior section gives younger members the opportunity to develop their running skills in a supportive club environment.

Ripon Runners also organises the Jolly Holly Jog, Ripon 10 Mile and Ripon Ultra. These events are open to runners whether or not they are club members, with the income helping the club to keep membership fees affordable.

Further information about Ripon Runners and its activities is available at www.riponrunners.org.uk.
