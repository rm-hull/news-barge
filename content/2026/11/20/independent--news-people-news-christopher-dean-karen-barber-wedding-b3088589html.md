Christoper Dean has reportedly married his partner of 15 years, Karen Barber – the best friend of his ice skating partner Jayne Torvill.

Dean, 68 and fellow former ice skater Barber, 65, tied the knot in a “low-key affair” in Gretna Green in March, according to reports.

The pair have been together for some 15 years, having first started dating when they both served as judges on *Dancing On Ice*.

A friend of the couple told the *Daily Mail*: “They are both over the moon and very much in love.

“They have said that they are now ‘Mr and Mrs’. Karen was wearing a big engagement ring for years but with no sign of making it official.”

![Dean and Barber in 2023](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/10/1825407576..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The publication also notes that Dean was wearing what appeared to be a gold wedding ring when he received his knighthood at Windsor Castle in June.

*The Independent* has contacted Dean’s representative for comment.

Dean and his skating partner Torvill became household names in the UK when they won gold at the 1984 Winter Olympics, where they performed their now world-famous *Boléro* routine. The pair then won bronze at the 1994 games, as well as being named European champions four times and British champions six times.

Meanwhile, Barber and her ice partner Nicky Slater skated for Team GB at the 1980 and 1894 Winter Olympics, finishing in 12th and 6th, respectively.

Dean has two previous marriages, the first to French-Canadian ice dance champion Isabelle Duchesnay. The pair got hitched in 1991 but split two years later.

![Torvill and Dean at their Investiture Ceremony in June](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/11/2279414196..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Dean then married American skater Jill Trenary in 1994 and the pair welcomed two sons before parting ways in 2010.

The following year, Dean was spotted kissing *Dancing on Ice* co-star Barber outside a restaurant. "Karen and Chris's closeness has been an open secret among everyone on the show,” a source said at the time. “They obviously care about each other a great deal.”

Barber then told *Closer* magazine: “Chris and I found ourselves at the end of our marriages and a friendship turned into something else. It took us both by surprise and we’re taking things slowly, but yes I’m very happy.”

Barber had split from her husband, fellow former ice skater Stephen Pickavance, a year prior. The pair were married for 21 years and had two daughters together.
