## China’s robotaxis now operate commercially in Guangzhou and Shenzhen while drones deliver coffee, medicine and defibrillators, as companies race to take autonomous vehicles from test zones to scale.

Robotaxis now operate commercially in Guangzhou and Shenzhen, in zones expanded to busier districts, with scale the next challenge. “China’s car industry and new energy vehicles lead the world – a strong hardware base for future large-scale autonomous driving,” says Li Yifan, Vice President, WeRide.

In Shenzhen, drones also deliver coffee to a park kiosk. “I think it’s very convenient,” says tourist Tyler Ng. LowAlt Technologies tests drones delivering defibrillators and medicine, while Mengtai Aerolink serves remote villages and islands.
