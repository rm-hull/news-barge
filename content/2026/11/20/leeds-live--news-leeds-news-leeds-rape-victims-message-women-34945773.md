# Leeds rape victim's message to other women after attacker jailed

## Evalds Saksons has been locked up

A rape victim has given a strong message to other women after seeing her attacker jailed.

Evalds Saksons, 32, of Lea Park Grove in Leeds has been jailed for 13 years after he was found guilty at Leeds Crown Court of rape, assault by penetration and two counts of assault occasioning actual bodily harm. A victim personal statement was read out in court, which included a message for other victims.

She said: “When I finally found the courage to call the helpline and ask for help, I was believed. I received help from the police, my family, friends and organisations that help women like me. They did not judge our experiences, feelings, trauma or suffering.“

Detective Sergeant Dan Bottomley, of Leeds Adult Safeguarding Unit, said: “I hope that anyone else who may be in a similar position to this victim listens to her words. We do not underestimate how hard it can be to report sexual or physical abuse offences, but taking that first step and contacting the police means that you are no longer alone.

“Please know that you will be listened to and supported by specially-trained police officers and other support services from first report throughout the criminal justice process and beyond.

“We are committed to tackling violence and sexual violence against women and girls and ensuring that perpetrators like Saksons are held accountable for their crimes.”
