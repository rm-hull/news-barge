Critics panned President Donald Trump’s 90-minute apperance at the White House as “the most boring press conference” the commander-in-chief has ever given.

Following a whirlwind four-day trip to Southeast Asia and China, Trump was back on familiar territory Friday, giving a long and wide-ranging speech to the White House press corps, flanked by Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Vice President JD Vance for the duration of what was supposed to be a press conference.

He touted the enormity of being personally greeted by Chinese President Xi Jinping on the trip, repeated a claim that his “friend” Russian President Vladimir Putin would never have invaded Ukraine if Trump had won the 2020 election, and veered off on a tangent about potholes.

After surpassing the 25-minute mark of his speech, without taking a question, Trump said, “I don't ramble. I don't ramble at all.” But his critics begged to differ.

Some noted on social media that the president appeared to be “crashing hard,” while Rubio looked like he was struggling to stay awake at times.

![President Donald Trump’s 90-minute speech was ‘the most boring press conference’ the commander-in-chief has ever given, according to critics](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/20/2305508404..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Trump has now been speaking for over half an hour without taking a single question,” said *Meidas Touch* White House correspondent Luke Radel.

“Watching Rubio watching Trump … lot of fidgeting, looking down, looking up…,” noted *The New Yorker*’s Susan Glasser.

“Trump is a half hour into ranting and raving, flitting from topic to topic incoherently, and he hasn't taken a single question yet,” commented journalist Aaron Rupar.

One of Rupar’s X followers then chimed in. “Rubio is going to jump out of his skin listening to grandpa lie his head off!”

Someone else posted that Rubio “looks like a hostage,” while another said that he “looks like he would like to be anywhere but here.”

Others observed that Rubio and Vance appeared to smirk at one another when Trump repeated the questionable claim that he inherited his IQ from his late uncle, an MIT professor.

![Critics observed Secretary of State Marco Rubio looking as though he was struggling to stay awake at times](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/20/download..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Rubio suppresses a smile and glances at Vance, who grins back when Trump repeats ‘genetically I’m blessed’ because of his uncle,” a user posted.

“Vance and Rubio exchange BIG SMIRKS because even THEY know he didn’t inherit his uncle’s IQ,” another said.

Another critic summed up the speech as “the most boring press conference Trump has ever given.”

The White House was more complimentary about the president’s remarks.

“In four days, President Donald J. Trump met with more than 20 world leaders, secured two new trade deals, with more on the way,” the official White House X account posted while Trump was still speaking. “Putting America FIRST on the world stage.”

Also in the room watching from stage right was Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller and special assistant Natalie Harp.

Miller gave brief remarks and said “the next two years are going to be the greatest two years in American history.”
