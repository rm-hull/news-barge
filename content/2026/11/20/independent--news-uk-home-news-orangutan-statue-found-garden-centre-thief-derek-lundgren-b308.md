A large statue of an orangutan has been recovered by police officers, more than two months after a cyclist stole it from a garden centre.

Cambridgeshire Police stated in a press release earlier this month that a "prolific thief" was jailed after taking the £799 animal figure from Scotsdales Garden Centre in Horningsea.

Derek Lundgren was captured on CCTV footage leaving the store without paying, while balancing the ornament across the handlebars of his bicycle.

The 61-year-old, of New Road, Sawston, then rode off with the statue on 7 September, Cambridgeshire Police said, before being jailed.

In an update issued on Friday, the force confirmed that "thanks to a member of the public, the giant animal figure has been found".

Writing in a social media post, Cambridgeshire Police stated that "following coverage of Lundgren’s conviction, information was received that an address in Teversham would be worth a look".

![Derek Lundgren was jailed after stealing the statue](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/15/01M781DVZH4N1HAKN8PG0SPBHX.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"Sure enough, the stolen orangutan was there and a woman was arrested," the force reported.

"She has since been handed a conditional caution after admitting to receiving stolen goods.

"The ape, nicknamed ‘Bruce’ by officers, will now be returned to the garden centre."

Lundgren admitted shop theft and breaching three suspended sentences, receiving a sentence of one year and 11 months in prison at Ipswich Magistrates’ Court earlier this month, police said.

Following Lundgren’s sentencing, PC Lundgren, who investigated the case, said: “Lundgren is a prolific thief who has continued to offend despite being given previous opportunities by the courts.

“His repeated offending has now caught up with him and he will spend time behind bars.”
