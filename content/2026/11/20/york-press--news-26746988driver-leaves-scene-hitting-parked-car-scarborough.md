The driver of a car is reported to have left the scene of a crash after hitting a parked vehicle in a North Yorkshire town.

The county's fire and rescue service said a crew from Scarborough was called to Dale Edge in Eastfield at 1.48am this morning (Friday, November 20).

A service spokesperson said there were reports of a car leaking fuel after the crash, with the driver making off.

Police confirmed there was no fuel leak and no further safety concerns at the scene, the service said.
