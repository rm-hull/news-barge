## Why we recommend it

In our review, the IT13 Max impressed us with its performance and connectivity. This PC’s Intel Core Ultra 9 185H processor has 16 cores and can reach 5.1GHz in boost mode, and it also includes integrated Intel Arc graphics and an AI NPU.

The IT13 Max supports up to four 4K display outputs, and it comes locked and loaded with dual USB4, Wi-Fi 7, and 2.5Gb Ethernet. It’s got Bluetooth 5.4 for connecting wireless accessories, too. For most people, this Windows mini PC ticks all the boxes they'll need.

However, we also noted its soldered, non-upgradeable memory and the fan noise under heavy workloads - opt for the 16GB DDR5 version if you're looking for one that allows you to expand the RAM. Also note that its second SSD slot uses the smaller 2242 NVMe configuration, so many drives won’t fit.

## Price context and historical value

The $799 configuration we’re highlighting here comes with 24GB of LPDDR5 memory and a 512GB SSD. That memory is soldered, though, which limits future upgrade potential. Alternatively, there’s an $849 model available that comes with 16GB of memory installed into conventional and accessible SO-DIMM memory slots- that’s less memory from day one, but with an expansion route available if you want it.

For comparison, the 1TB model is priced at $805 at Amazon, with the 512GB model matching Geekom's own price of $699.
