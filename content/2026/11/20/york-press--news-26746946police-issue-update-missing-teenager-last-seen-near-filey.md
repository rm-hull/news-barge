Police have issued an update after appealing for information about a missing 16-year-old girl last seen in a North Yorkshire village.

The teenager was reported missing after being last seen in Hunmanby around 3pm yesterday afternoon (Thursday, November 19).

The county's police force said she may have travelled to Scarborough and officers said they were becoming "increasingly concerned" for her welfare and issued her description.

In an updated statement issued this morning, North Yorkshire Police said the missing 16-year-old girl had been found safe and thanked everyone who had supported the appeal.
