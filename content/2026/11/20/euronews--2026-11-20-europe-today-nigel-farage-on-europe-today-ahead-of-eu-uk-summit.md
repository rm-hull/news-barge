## The European Union and the United Kingdom are holding a crucial summit in Brussels today to patch up ties after the bitter Brexit divorce. We speak with Reform UK leader Nigel Farage. We also debrief Trump, Putin and Xi's meeting in China with Ben Hodges, former General Commander of the US.

## On today's show:

Brussels hosts this Friday a key summit between the European Union and the United Kingdom a decade after Brexit. Brussels and London aim to unveil several deals on trade, defence industry cooperation, agrifood standards, youth mobility, economic security and access to the EU electricity market. We speak with Reform UK leader and prominent Brexiteer **Nigel Farage.** We also analyse this Thursday meeting between**US President Donald Trump, Russia President Vladimir Putin and China President Xi Jingping.** We discuss the implications of their meeting with**former US Army Europe commander, General Ben Hodges and Ian Lesser, Distinguished Fellow at the German Marshall Fund.**##** When and where to watch Europe Today?**You can join Euronews’ Stefan Grobe live on TV and Euronews' website as well as digital platforms (YouTube, Facebook, X, and Instagram),** every weekday at 8 am, 9 am and 10 am Brussels time.**In just** 28 minutes**, our new format brings you the day's key events plus crucial analysis of all the stories shaping the EU and beyond.
