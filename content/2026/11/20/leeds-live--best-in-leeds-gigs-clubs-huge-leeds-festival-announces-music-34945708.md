# Huge Leeds festival announces music megastars for 2027 editon

## Thousands of people will enjoy the fun next summer

A massive festival has announced a big line up of music megastars for its 2027 edition.

Goodlife has revealed the first wave of artists for next year’s edition. The 2027 fun will be happening on Saturday, June 12.

Faithless and Groove Armada will both play DJ sets, with Sophie Ellis-Bextor, Dimitri From

Paris, Eats Everything and Horse Meat Disco all forming the bill. You can buy tickets from 10am on Friday, November 27 - tickets will be available from SeeTickets.

Other acts to look forward to include Melvo Baptiste, Todd Terry and K-Klass. For 2027 festival organisers New Vision have expanded the music programme across three stages with investment in stage production.

Improvements will also be made across the site with the focus on retaining the intimate, welcoming atmosphere that has helped it build a loyal following. New Vision managing director, Liam Hardy, said: “We are very excited to reveal the first of our DJs and performers for 2027. A lot of thought has gone into shaping a line up that will deliver an eclectic range of house, disco, soul and funk music, and the calibre of DJs we’ve confirmed gives a sense of the day we’re putting together.

“Seeing the Goodlife crowd more than double in a year was a real vote of confidence and we want to build on that by making the experience even better for everyone who comes, whether they’ve been with us since the first festival or are joining us for the first time.

“Harewood House is a big part of why the day feels so special and we remain committed to our ethos of ‘bold, beautiful, boutique’, carefully curating an experience where music, atmosphere and community come first.”

Further artists and programme details are expected to be announced in the coming months. We’ll bring you further details when we know more.
