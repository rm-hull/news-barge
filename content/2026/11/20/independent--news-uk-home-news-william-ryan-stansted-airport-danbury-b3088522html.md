A 79-year-old man has been sentenced after racially abusing security staff at Stansted Airport.

William Ryan was found guilty of two counts of racially aggravated intentional harassment, alarm, distress at Chelmsford Magistrates’ Court on 28 October. At the same court last Monday (9 November) Ryan, of Hyde Lane, Danbury, was ordered to fined £1,600, ordered to pay £300 compensation, and costs of £1,300.

The incident began when Ryan was stopped by security staff while going through a scanner at the airport on 21 June last year.

The victim reported that he became hostile and abusive, including making monkey noises.

A witness confirmed they had also heard the comments.

Inspector James Southwell said: “There is absolutely no excuse for racially abusing people just going about their job.

“William Ryan reacted badly to being stopped by security staff and his behaviour was completely unacceptable.

“No-one should be abused because of who they are and we treat reports of this nature seriously.”

Anita Harrison, London Stansted’s Customer Operations Director, said:

“Any threatening, racist or abusive behaviour towards anyone working at London Stansted is completely unacceptable and will not be tolerated, and we thank Essex Police for the actions they took to deal with this individual.”

“There is absolutely no excuse for racially abusing people just going about their job”
