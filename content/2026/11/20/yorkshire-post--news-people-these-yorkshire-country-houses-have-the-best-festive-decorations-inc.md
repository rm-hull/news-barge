# These Yorkshire country houses have the best festive decorations - including Castle Howard

## As December approaches, Yorkshire is preparing to “deck the halls” – and these country houses have some of its most impressive decorations.

Every household has its own traditions. From Castle Howard to Harewood House, here are the most festive places to visit this winter.

**Castle Howard, York**

![One of the Dickensian-themed rooms. Photo: Emma Atkins](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJkMTgxNmU3LTVmMWUtNDZmZS1hZTlkLTI2NGRiNjUxZTJmZDowYWU5NDIyZi1jN2NhLTQ1YWEtODAyMy01ZjdhYThkZDk2YTA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Castle Howard is hosting literary-themed celebrations with its event: ‘A Dickens of a Christmas.’ From November 12 to January 3, there will be Victorian festivities, including storytelling sessions and costumed appearances by characters like Ebenezer Scrooge. Its traditional decorations are just as impressive, including over 80 trees, 30,000 baubles, and 2000 metres of light. Adult tickets start at £24.

**Burton Agnes Hall, Driffield**

![In 1173 Roger de Stuteville built the Norman manor-house, the lower chamber of which still remains today. Since then the property has never changed hands by sale, though it has passed from family to family on occasion when the male line has ended.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmRhNWI1ZTAyLTYyZGEtNDQ5Yi05NTVjLWI3MTI3MjU2NGZlZTo4YTU3YzkxNy0xMWViLTQ4OWItYjI3MS1lODM3ZTcwNmQzZTY=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Burton Agnes Hall is celebrating Christmas from November 14 to December 23. Its 12-century rooms are adorned with handmade decorations, and outside, visitors can explore a woodland trail of giant candy canes. On specific dates, ‘late night openings’ offer carol singing and mulled wine. General admission starts at £16.81 per adult.

**Harewood House, Leeds**

![Harewood House Trust is celebrating its 40th Anniversary with a host of special events:  Picture James Hardisty.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjJjMmU1NTE3LTI3OTQtNGZiNS05NDAzLTE4YjA5ODkwMjMyMzpiZWE5NTUwZS04ZDVlLTQxNWMtOWM1MS01NWFiNTIyNWNjNWQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


From November 6 to January 3, Harewood House is hosting its ‘Magic at the Mansion’ event. The estate has been decorated with over 100 paper flowers, 50 metres of garlands, and a 12-foot Christmas tree in the Yellow Drawing Room. Its Elf Village is a family-friendly addition for 2026. Adult tickets start at £24.50.

**Lotherton Hall, Aberford**

![Lotherton Hall.
Picture Bruce Rollinson]([https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/24/10/2026_10_24_09_05_36_718_ASH_42E08661405246C38E2A98CBEA1363F7.JPG?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/24/10/2026_10_24_09_05_36_718_ASH_42E08661405246C38E2A98CBEA1363F7.JPG?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65))


From November 21 to January 3, Lotherton Hall is putting an Edwardian spin on Christmas cheer with a collection of antique ornaments. In its gardens, there is also a themed ‘12 Days Walk,’ plus a North Pole and Fairy Dell. Adult tickets start at £12.50 for estate entry, with optional upgrades for Santa Claus meet-and-greets.
