# Bradford councillor stabbed ‘had intervened to protect a colleague’

## A Bradford councillor stabbed yesterday while canvassing had intervened to protect a colleague, his family have said.

Coun Ibrar Ali was stabbed on Thornbury Avenue in Bradford while out canvassing on Thursday morning, sustaining serious injuries which saw him rushed to hospital.

His attacker, who fled onto a nearby roof before being brought down and arrested on suspicion of attempted murder, remains in police custody.

![Councillor Ibrar Ali sustained serious but not life-threatening injuries](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/11/46/Salt-and-Pepper-Portrait-Against-Beige.png?trim=107,299,213,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Coun Ali remained in hospital on Friday, in a stable condition. A friend of the long-serving councillor said yesterday he was ‘awake and talking’.

His family have now released a statement through The Leap community group in Bradford, providing more details on the incident.

It said: ‘Councillor Ibrar Ali was supporting council wardens with community clean-up activities in the ward when an incident occurred.

‘During the incident, Councillor Ibrar intervened to protect a member of council staff and, in doing so, sustained multiple stab wounds.’

![Scene on Thornbury Avenue, Bradford, where a councillor was stabbed](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjQ1YmE3NzBkLWExOGItNGU1Ni1iZmY4LWE0ZGFjY2Y1Zjk2NToxYWYzN2M3Yy0yMjEyLTQ0ZmItYWUxZC1iNjFkNWVhZTg3M2Y=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Coin Ali’s family went on ‘express their heartfelt gratitude’ to police, paramedics and the public who helped with first aid at the scene.

The statement continued: ‘Councillor Ibrar is grateful for the overwhelming kindness shown and hopes to personally thank everyone once he has recovered.

‘For now, the family kindly requests privacy and understanding to allow Councillor Ibrar the time and space he needs to rest, heal and recover.’

West Yorkshire Police said they did not have information to suggest the attack was politically motivated.

Coun Ali was named in parliament on Thursday, in wake of the stabbing.

Local government minister Baroness Taylor of Stevenage named Mr Ali in the House of Lords.

She said: “I am afraid I have to mention a very tragic incident that’s occurred in Bradford… where an independent councillor who was out canvassing has been stabbed.

“I would want to send the wishes, that I’m sure noble Lords would agree, of the whole House to councillor Ibrar Ali for a speedy recovery, and our thoughts are with all of those in Bradford who are affected by this awful incident.”
