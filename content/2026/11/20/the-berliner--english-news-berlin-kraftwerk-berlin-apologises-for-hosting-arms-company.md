Following an event hosted by the arms manufacturer Helsing at Kraftwerk Berlin, the venue’s operators have distanced themselves from the event. Kraftwerk Berlin said the decision to accept Helsing’s booking was “a serious mistake,” in a statement posted on Instagram.

Kraftwerk admitted that the implications of the decision were fundamentally misjudged. “An arms company should never have been given the opportunity to hold an event at the power plant. This must never happen again.” The power plant is an established event venue in Berlin, which also includes the techno club Tresor.

Helsing hosted a presentation of a new AI platform for military use at the power plant a week ago. Projections and screens in the event venue displayed maps, target markers, and impact trajectories. Weapons were also on display in the room.

The techno club Tresor, located in Kraftwerk Berlin and sharing the same managing director as the event venue’s operating company (Schaltraum Berlin GmbH), had already distanced itself in an Instagram statement.

The statement said the club operates separately from the power plant’s rental business. Tresor condemned the decision: “The event should not have taken place,” it said. Tresor also mentioned in its statement that artists, promoters, staff, and visitors had contacted them regarding the event.

According to Tresor’s statement, the club receives no revenue from the power plant’s rental business. The companies are separate entities, even though they share the same managing director.

The joint managing director, Dietmar-Maria (Dimitri) Hegemann, has already acknowledged that approving the Helsing event was unacceptable. He has announced his intention to review the booking process and restructure responsibilities.
