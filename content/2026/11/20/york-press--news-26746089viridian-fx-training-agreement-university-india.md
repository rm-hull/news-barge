A new international partnership will support the next generation of creative talent through hands-on training and global industry experience.

Viridian FX, a visual effects company based in York, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the University of York and the University of York Mumbai to expand its talent academy and enhance opportunities for students in the UK and India.

The agreement will see students benefit from guest lectures, live projects, internships, and research and knowledge exchange across both locations.

David Skaith, Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, said: "India's visual effects industry is a global powerhouse, and our trade mission has successfully placed York right at the heart of that exchange.

This partnership proves that our businesses like Viridian FX can lead the world.

By fostering global knowledge sharing and hands-on apprenticeships, we are developing and retaining top-tier creative talent right here in York and North Yorkshire."

The MOU was signed during a recent trade mission to India, which is home to the largest visual effects industry in the world.

At the centre of the partnership is the Viridian Academy, Viridian FX’s training studio based at the University of York.

The academy has recently chosen its first cohort of four apprentices, who are employed on 18-month contracts and enrolled on a Level 4 Visual Effects apprenticeship.

Heather Niven, Associate Pro-Vice-Chancellor for India and Middle East at the University of York, said: "Partnerships like this help us in closing the gap between academic study and professional production in the creative industries, and ensures that our graduates have the skills needed to drive growth across our region as well as contribute to the film and television sector globally."

The initiative will also enable students from York to work alongside international talent, while students studying on the University of York Mumbai’s Business of the Creative Industries programme will be given the opportunity of international work experience.

Kit Monkman, founder of Viridian FX, said: "Visual effects is at the sharp end of change, and the companies that blossom will be the ones with allies and a willingness to reimagine the future.

India has the largest visual effects industry in the world, and a real appetite for British education.

Our partnership with the University of York and the University of York Mumbai puts York at the heart of that exchange, and through the Viridian Academy we aim for young people from York and North Yorkshire to be the ones who gain most from it."

Viridian FX is the north of England's leading visual effects company, and began life in a small studio in York.

It has since delivered projects for major clients including HBO, Netflix, Amazon, Apple, Paramount, Universal, and ITV.
