A York company that offered postal-based services has entered liquidation with debts topping £100,000.

Nuxdo Limited operated at 13 Micklegate and traded as Mail Boxes Etc York, offering parcel delivery, courier, printing, and mailbox rental services.

The company was incorporated eight years ago and entered liquidation on October 29, with Maxim IP Ltd of Sheffield appointed liquidator.

Companies House records the directors as Salman Cetin and Sanem Esra Koyupinar.




* **AG Construction and Developments Ltd of York in liquidation**
* **York building company liquidated with debts over £60,000**
* **North Yorkshire firm in liquidation owing £746,391**
* **Quickfix@home (York) and Quickfix (York) in liquidation**

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs said the business had assets comprising £15,134.51 cash in a bank account, plus £50 in stock.

However, it had preferential creditors – those paid first – who were owed £1,661.52 in staff wages.

In addition, as a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next – HMRC was owed £29,147.24 in VAT and PAYE.

Furthermore, there were unsecured creditors – those paid last – who were owed £84,768.62 between them.

These were made up of trade and expense creditors owed £49,200; employees owed £2,076.93 for redundancy and lack of notice; banks and financial institutions owed £11,337.56; HMRC owed £2,154.13 and £20,000 owed to directors’ loan accounts.

This gave a total shortfall or deficit of £100,392.87.

With the business having issued and called up capital of £100, this gave a total shortfall or deficit of £100,492.87 outstanding, the statement of affairs added.
