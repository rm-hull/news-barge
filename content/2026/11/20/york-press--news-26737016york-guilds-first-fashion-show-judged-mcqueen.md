A HISTORIC York guild is set to launch its inaugural fashion show later this week - with judging from a very special guest.

The Company of Merchant Taylors - a guild with roots tracing back to 1273 - will host the Golden Thread Awards on Sunday, November 22, 2026, at the Merchant Taylors’ Hall in York.

This inaugural fashion show will celebrate a mix of established and emerging designers, reflecting the guild's longstanding heritage in tailoring and cloth.

The awards were first introduced in 2026 to foster a new generation of designers, ensuring the guild's rich history is carried forward.

The fashion show will see designers compete for a range of awards, including the prestigious Designer of the Year award, which recognises excellence in design, material understanding, and craftsmanship.

Other categories include the Textile Innovation Award, the Emerging Designer Award, and production awards for hair, makeup, and styling teams and models.

The jury, which will have the final say on the winners, includes some of the most respected names in the industry.

Gary James McQueen, the chair of the jury, is a revered designer and artist, known for his distinctive voice in contemporary British fashion.

He spent seven years as the head textile designer at Alexander McQueen, working alongside his uncle, Lee Alexander McQueen, before founding his own practice in 2012 which moves between fashion, digital art, and limited-edition collaborations.

Other jury members are Liz Mawbey - who leads Personal Shopping across Selfridges’ flagship sites - and Cherie Federico, the founder and director of Aesthetica, a York based art and culture brand.

Full details of the event can be found online at goldenthreadawards.com
