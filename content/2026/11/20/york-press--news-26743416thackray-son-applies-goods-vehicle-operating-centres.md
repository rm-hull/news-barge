A York-based company is seeking permission for new goods vehicle operating centres.

Richard Grahame Thackray, trading as RG & EA Thackray & Son, has applied for a licence to use Tumbletwist Cottage, Main Street, Hayton, as an operating centre for 25 goods vehicles and 30 trailers.

Additionally, the company wants to use Hughes Mushrooms, Howden Road, Holme-on-Spalding Moor, as an operating centre for four goods vehicles and four trailers.

* **For more information about this public notice, click here.**
* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal at publicnoticeportal.uk/york-press**

Owners or occupiers of land (including buildings) near the operating centres who believe that their use or enjoyment of that land would be affected should make written representations to the Traffic Commissioner at Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds, LS2 7UE stating their reasons, within 21 days of this notice.

A copy of the representation must also be sent to the applicant.

Further guidance on making representations is available on the government website.
