# What the new UK-EU deal could mean for future post-Brexit relations

The United Kingdom and European Union reached a deal on Friday strengthening post-Brexit ties following a summit in Brussels.

The deal covers British food exports, emissions trading and a youth experience scheme.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham said it was a "good day" for Britain and Europe, while European Council President António Costa said the EU remains "open to have the closest possible relationship" with the UK.

The BBC's political editor Chris Mason and Europe editor Katya Adler analyse the new agreement and what it could mean for future relations between the UK and EU.

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch said the deal "isn't fair" - and Reform's Nigel Farage called it a "betrayal of the British people".
