A driver hit a parked car and drove off in Scarborough early this morning (Friday).

Police asked for help from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service after a report of a car leaking fuel at **Dale Edge, Eastfield** at**1.48am**.

A car had struck a parked car, and its driver made off.

#### Dale Edge, Eastfield, Scarborough

The Scarborough crew was stood down while on its way to the scene. Police at the scene confirmed there were no leaks.
