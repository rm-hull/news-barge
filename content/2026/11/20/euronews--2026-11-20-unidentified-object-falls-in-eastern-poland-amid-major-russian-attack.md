## Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russian forces had fired more than 400 attack drones and over 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight.

Two missiles and one jet-powered drone crashed in Poland on Friday amid a major Russian attack on Ukraine, Polish authorities said.

Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed in a press conference that a Kh-101 cruise missile had landed around the village of Nowy Lipowiec in the Lublin province of eastern Poland.

Another missile and a jet-powered drone fell at two other locations, he said, adding that there were so far no reports of injuries or of damage to critical infrastructure.

“We are dealing with yet another provocation by Russia. In my view, the date is not accidental. Today, the Coalition of the Willing is conducting exercises. Several countries are located near the areas where our air forces were operating,” he told reporters.

It came as Russian forces carried out large-scale strikes on Ukraine overnight, using what the Kremlin said were "high-precision land-based, naval, and air-based weapons," as well as drones.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia had fired more than 400 attack drones and over 110 missiles during the assault, which he called "one of the largest attacks" of the war.

"Due to the Russian Federation’s continued airstrikes on targets in western Ukraine, military aircraft are operating in Polish airspace," the Operational Command of the Polish armed forces said in a statement on Friday morning.

It added that land-based air defence systems had also been activated but stressed that the measures were "preventive in nature" and aimed at "securing and protecting Polish airspace."

Prime Minister Donald Tusk said he had convened a meeting of ministers, representatives of the armed forces, and intelligence services heads "in response to a violation of Polish airspace.
