A driver who ploughed into an elderly man leaving him with catastrophic injuries before speeding away has been jailed.

His passenger has also been sentenced for the cover-up that followed.

At about 8.14pm on Tuesday 12 March 2024, the now 81-year-old victim was crossing Northallerton High Street after an going out for the evening with his wife.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/northallerton-high-street-gsv.webp)

He was struck by a black Ford Fiesta driven by Luke Scott. The force propelled the man into the air.

He was left unconscious and with catastrophic injuries, including a brain haemorrhage, multiple skull and facial fractures, and fractures to his pelvis and legs.

Scott then sped away from the scene with his passenger Hayley Richardson next to him.

## Drinking at a pub

The following day, police received reports of a partially burned Ford Fiesta abandoned in Northallerton Rigg.

Officers linked the vehicle’s registration and insurance to Richardson.

During the investigation, it was found that prior to the collision, the co-defendants had visited The Buck Inn where they were seen drinking alcohol.

Scott and Richardson were arrested and charged.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-buck-inn-high-street-northallerton-gsv.webp)

Scott, 34, of Brickside Way in Northallerton, pleaded guilty to causing serious injury by careless driving and perverting the course of justice.

Richardson, 34, of Berry Close in Great Yarmouth, pleaded guilty to perverting the course of justice.

At Teesside Crown Court yesterday (Thursday), Scott was jailed for 26 months and handed a driving ban of three years eight months.

Richardson received a nine-month sentence suspended for 18 months and 80 hours of unpaid work.

## ‘They ruined our lives’

The victim’s wife told the court: “Since that day, our lives have changed completely and bears no resemblance to what it was before.

“We are no longer able to do any of the things that we previously enjoyed as a couple or as individuals and the disruption that has been caused to us cannot be measured.

“This is not way that either of us anticipated spending our retirement. He is not capable of making his own statement about how his life has been ruined by the callous actions of others. He no longer has the mental ability to do so.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/teesside-crown-court-middlesbrough-wk.webp)

“I have tried to speak on his behalf as best as I can but I don’t believe I could ever do it justice.”

She added: “All I can say that is that his life, my life, the lives of our children and our grandchildren have been ruined. Two complete strangers have taken away any enjoyment of life that he might have once had, taken away his hobbies, his friends, his ability to think.

“Through doing this to him they have also destroyed my life and my enjoyment of it.”

Detective Sergeant Ed Drake, of North Yorkshire Police’s Serious Collision Investigation Unit, said: “The selfish and callous actions of these two individuals has caused irreparable damage to not only the victim but to his family and all those who knew and loved him and their lives will never be the same again.

“This case demonstrates our determination to pursue every available line of enquiry to establish the truth and ensure those responsible are held accountable.

“We will continue to do everything in our power to seek justice for victims and their families, and to bring offenders before the courts, no matter how long it takes.”
