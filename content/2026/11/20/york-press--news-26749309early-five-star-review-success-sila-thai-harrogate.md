## Five-star reviews for new North Yorkshire Thai as it officially launches tonight

Sila Thai is enjoying five-star ratings, even before its official launch tonight *(Image: Troy Walker)*

A new Thai restaurant in North Yorkshire is already earning five-star reviews, as it prepares for its official launch night tonight (Fri).

Sila Thai opened a few days ago at 7 Bridge Street, Harrogate, where Dino’s Restaurant Bar and Grill was until closing in January this year.

The restaurant has posted on social media: “We've had such a lovely first few days meeting everyone, and we're so excited to welcome even more of you for our official opening.

“To celebrate, we're offering 10% off all food and drinks from 7pm to 10pm!

“Come along, enjoy some authentic Thai food and help us celebrate the beginning of Sila Thai in Harrogate!”

The restaurant’s first Google review gave five stars and said: “Fantastic meal, staff friendly and helpful. Slight delay on getting all the main courses out together but everyone was happy when it all arrived. Great addition to the Harrogate food scene.”

Sila Thai has also been receiving five stars reviews on Facebook as well.
