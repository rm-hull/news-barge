Smoke billowed over **Kyiv** and flames tore through apartment blocks and offices after Russia unleashed**more than 400 drones and over 110 missiles** across**Ukraine** overnight,**killing at least eight people, six of them in the capital, and injuring 15**, according to Ukrainian authorities and emergency services.

Alongside residential and commercial buildings, strikes hit hospitals, essential infrastructure and **three bridges spanning the Dnipro River**, leaving streets littered with shattered masonry, twisted metal and wrecked cars.

Across the battered capital, firefighters battled blazes while rescue teams searched ruined structures for **survivors**, police combed the debris for forensic evidence and utility crews worked to restore vital services.** Some fires had been brought under control**, but responders continued working to extinguish remaining blazes, clear rubble and assess damage across the capital.

The devastating barrage, coming just a day after an earlier wave of attacks damaged diplomatic premises in **Kyiv,** made good on Russian President**Vladimir Putin’s** warning of a "**painful" retaliation** for Ukraine’s strike on the**Crimean Bridge** earlier in the week.
