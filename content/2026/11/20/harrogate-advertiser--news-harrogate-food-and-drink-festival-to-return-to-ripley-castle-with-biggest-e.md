The Harrogate Food and Drink Festival will return to the grounds of Ripley Castle next summer for its eighth annual celebration, with organisers promising a packed weekend of food, entertainment and live music.

The popular festival will take place on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 August 2027, bringing together independent traders, chefs, performers and visitors from across Yorkshire and beyond.

Following seven successful years of the event, organisers have announced plans for an expanded entertainment programme featuring four live stages, interactive competitions and activities.

The Main Stage will host live music from local bands and musicians, while the Cookery Theatre will feature demonstrations from regional chefs and culinary experts.

The Entertainers Stage will bring family-friendly performances including magicians, circus acts and performing arts, while the Sizzling Showdown Arena will host interactive shows and competitions.

Alongside the stage programme, families will also be able to take part in a variety of activities including the Viking Heritage Kitchen, Ian’s Mobile Petting Farm, live bubble shows, the Foodie Clue Quest scavenger hunt, and children’s colouring activities.

Roaming entertainers, including stilt walkers, comedians and colourful performers, will also bring surprises throughout the festival grounds.

Michael Johnston, Director of Harrogate Food and Drink Festival, said: “Over the last seven years, thousands of festival-goers from across Yorkshire have joined us, and their continued support is what allows us to return year after year.

“As a testament to that support, we're packing as much as we can into the weekend so that there's plenty going on for friends and families of all ages.

“Visitors can take it easy in a deckchair or throw themselves into the activities dotted around the festival grounds.

“There's going to be a little bit of something for everyone.”

The festival will continue its focus on supporting independent businesses, with more than 100 traders expected to feature across the Street Food Arena, Artisan Markets and Streets of Independent Bars.

Visitors will be able to sample global cuisines, discover small-batch products, browse handmade goods and artwork, and enjoy speciality drinks from local producers, makers and artisans.

Organisers say the event aims to provide a platform for independent businesses while creating a weekend celebration for the wider community.

Limited early bird tickets for the 2027 Harrogate Food and Drink Festival are now available.
