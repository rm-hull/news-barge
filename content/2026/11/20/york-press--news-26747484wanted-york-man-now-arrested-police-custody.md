North Yorkshire Police have arrested a 28-year-old York man.

The arrest follows an appeal to find the 28-year-old who was wanted on recall to prison after breaching the terms of his licence.


  
**Police hunt wanted man believed to be in York area**

Police said today he has now been arrested and is in police custody.

North Yorkshire Police also thanked everyone who shared their appeal.
