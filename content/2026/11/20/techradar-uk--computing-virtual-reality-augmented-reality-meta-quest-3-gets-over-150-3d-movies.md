* **150 3D movies are heading to the Meta Quest 3/3S**
* **They'll be available through the TV app**
* **More 3D movies can be found in the Disney+ app**

Your Meta Quest 3 or Meta Quest 3S just got access to a wide selection of 3D movies (if you're in the US, at least): Meta has announced that more than 150 titles are now available on these headsets, including *Avatar*and* Jurassic World Rebirth*.

Meta's announcement leads with the *Hit Me Hard and Soft* tour film from Billie Eilish, which will apparently let you "stand alongside Billie, look out at the crowd, and feel like part of the show" – and is only available in 3D on Meta Quest until December 31.

The road movie was shot by James Cameron's Lightstorm Vision 3D tech outfit, which has a long-term deal with Meta. The Eilish shows were recorded with a 3D camera on stage and others filming at long range to give a genuine 360-degree view. $24.99

Many other movies, including *Spider-Man: Far From Home*,* Wicked: For Good*, and some without a colon in the title are 2D films converted to 3D, and can be bought or rented through the TV app on Horizon OS. The titles generally cost $16.99 to buy (around £13 / AU$26) and $6.99 to rent (about £5 / AU$10), but pricing does vary.

## Disney upgrades too

![HIT ME HARD AND SOFT | James Cameron Director's Message - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/nmoTUNts0Y4/maxresdefault.jpg)

Meta is also introducing a new Theater view for the TV app, which adds an extra level of immersion. The announcement says the view projects a big screen in front of your vision, and dims the surroundings to create a "cozy, personal cinema experience".

As well as the 3D upgrade for the TV app, Meta's has also highlighted the addition of 3D movies to the Disney+ app, which rolled out a few days ago. You can take advantage of these too, if you're a Disney+ subscriber.

Titles available through the Disney+ portal include *The Avengers*,* Black Panther*, and* Deadpool & Wolverine*, and there are over 60 in total. As with the movies in the TV app, availability may vary by location, Disney says.
