Multiple roads across North Yorkshire are due to face temporary vehicle restrictions for utility and drainage works.

North Yorkshire Council has issued or intends to issue traffic orders covering roads in Wighill, Harrogate, Spofforth and Ripon.

Church Lane, Wighill, will be closed to vehicles, subject to access to premises, between November 23 and November 27.

The restriction will apply outside and opposite number 8, LS24 8BG.

The closure is covered by the Temporary Prohibition of Traffic, Church Lane, Wighill, Order 2026, No. 677.

The maximum duration of the order is February 23, 2027.

St Clements Road, Harrogate, is due to be closed between November 23 and December 1.

Access to the premises will remain subject to the restriction.

The work is for Northern Powergrid service alterations.

The restriction is set out in Temporary Prohibition of Traffic, St Clements Road, Harrogate, Order 2026, No. 678.

Although the listed closure period ends on December 1, the order can remain in force until February 23, 2027.

Euclid Avenue, Harrogate, will have a vehicle restriction between November 23 and November 27.

The work is intended to improve road drainage and falls to prevent standing water.

Pedestrian access will be maintained during the restriction.

The measure is covered by Temporary Prohibition of Traffic, Euclid Avenue, Harrogate, Order 2026, No. 679.

Its maximum duration is April 22, 2028.

East Park Road, Spofforth, is due to be closed to vehicles between November 23 and November 29, subject to access to premises.

Northern Powergrid works to provide a new connection are listed as the reason.

The closure is covered by Temporary Prohibition of Traffic, East Park Road, Spofforth, Order 2026, No. 684.

The order can remain in force until February 23, 2027.

Two further restrictions are planned for December, with Nydd Vale Terrace, Harrogate, due to be closed between December 11 and December 17 for NPG fuse upgrade works.

The proposed restriction is covered by Temporary Prohibition of Traffic, Nydd Vale Terrace, Harrogate, Order 2026, No. 709.

The order is intended to remain available until March 11, 2027.

Vehicles will be restricted, subject to access to the premises.

Holmefield Road, Ripon, is due to be closed between December 7 and December 15 for NPG fuse upgrade works.

The restriction is covered by Temporary Prohibition of Traffic, Holmefield Road, Ripon, Order 2026, No. 715.

Its maximum duration is March 7, 2027, and access to the premises will be subject to the restriction.

For all six roads, the closure periods will be shown by traffic signs.

A local alternative route will be signed on site where appropriate.

Enquiries about Church Lane should be directed to OCU Group on 0113 322 7545.

Questions about St Clements Road, East Park Road, Nydd Vale Terrace and Holmefield Road should be directed to OCU Utility Services Ltd on 0333 200 0011.

For Euclid Avenue, North Yorkshire Council’s Customer Service Centre can be contacted on 0300 131 2 131.

Details are also available through North Yorkshire Council’s roadworks map at northyorks.gov.uk/roadworks-map.
