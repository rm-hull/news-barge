A draft plan for Harrogate's next 10 to 15 years lists 27 projects and goes to public consultation in January 2027. Knaresborough's follows in February.

A draft investment plan for Harrogate lists **27 projects** for the next 10 to 15 years. Residents will be asked for their views in**January 2027**.

Knaresborough’s plan goes out to consultation a month later, in **February 2027**. Both are described in a council report for the Harrogate and Knaresborough Area Committee on Thursday 26 November.

The plans are not a pot of money. The report calls them “investment-readiness tools”: evidence to support future funding bids. Putting a project in a plan “does not commit the Council or any partner to delivery”, it says.

## Harrogate

The draft vision reads: “Harrogate will be a modern spa and wellness town, celebrating its heritage, floral character, green spaces and vibrant town centre.”

The 27 projects sit under five themes:

* spa town, heritage and the visitor economy
* transport, accessibility and getting around
* business, skills and jobs
* parks, water, climate and growth
* communities, health and young people

The report does not name the 27 projects. It says the plan rests on 585 online survey responses, nine themed workshops and work with the Youth Council and schools. People told the council they were proud of the spa heritage, parks and independent shops. They also raised traffic, accessibility, the state of some streets, affordability and the lack of things for young people.

After consultation, the council aims to endorse the plan in **spring 2027**.

## Knaresborough

Knaresborough’s draft groups its ideas around nature and water, heritage and culture, prosperity, movement, and community wellbeing. Ideas under consideration include:

* a joined-up signposting and wayfinding network
* better links between the town centre, the castle and the riverside
* support for the market and evening activity
* river and green space projects
* transport and accessibility studies

The report says residents preferred “practical, phased interventions over large-scale transformation”. The February consultation will include an informal session at Knaresborough Market and a public session at Knaresborough House, plus work with young people and material online.

## Boroughbridge

Boroughbridge, about seven miles north east of Knaresborough, finished its first round of engagement over the summer. It drew more than 500 pieces of feedback, which officers are still analysing.

## What it means for you

* **If you live in Harrogate**, watch for the consultation in January. It is the point at which you can comment on individual projects.
* **If you live in Knaresborough**, the market and Knaresborough House sessions in February are the places to have your say in person.
* **If you run a business or group**, the council says the plans will be the evidence behind future funding bids and business cases.
