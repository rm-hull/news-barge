A woman was arrested following a drug raid in Malton.

Officers from Ryedale Neighbourhood Policing Team, aided by a police drone, executed a warrant under Section 23 of the Misuse of Drugs Act.

The raid took place this morning (FRI).

A vehicle parked outside the property was searched, leading to the discovery of a bag containing white powder.

The police also found digital scales and bags.

The woman, aged in her 30s, was subsequently arrested on suspicion of possessing Class A drugs with intent to supply.

She remains in custody.

The police emphasised the importance of community intelligence in their efforts to combat drug trafficking.

A spokesperson said: "Every piece of information, no matter how small it may seem, can help develop evidence, safeguard vulnerable people, and support action against those responsible for bringing drugs and the harm associated with them into our communities."
