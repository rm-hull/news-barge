# West Yorkshire Police officer charged with sexually assaulting colleague while on duty

## A West Yorkshire Police officer has been charged with sexually assaulting a colleague while on duty.

PC Kieran Williams, 29, who is based in Leeds District, has been charged with one count of sexual assault following an alleged incident in July 2026.

![police](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/16/15/53/Police-officer-stock-image.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=420&quality=65)


The charge relates to an allegation that Williams grabbed a colleague's buttock over their clothing while on duty.

West Yorkshire Police confirmed that Williams has been suspended from duty.

He is due to appear at Bradford Magistrates' Court on Thursday (Nov 26) in connection with the charge.
