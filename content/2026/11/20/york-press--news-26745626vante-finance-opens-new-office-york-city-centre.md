A specialist finance broker has opened a new office within York’s city walls.

Vante Finance, a Yorkshire-based specialist in asset, vehicle, and business finance, has moved into Colonia House in Toft Green - a recently refurbished building also home to Ardent Financial Planning.

The company, founded in 2025 by directors Dominic Boulton and Liam Eccles, now has a six-strong team based in the centre of the city.

Mr Boulton said: "Moving into Colonia House is a real statement of intent for us in York.

"We've grown the team because local businesses want a finance partner they can sit down with face to face, someone who knows the area and can move quickly.

"Being inside the city walls, a few minutes from the station, makes that easier for everyone."

The city centre office will help Vante grow its network and support small and medium-sized businesses in the area.

The company works with nearly 100 lenders to help businesses secure funding for vehicles, machinery, equipment, and expansion.

Vante’s York team serves clients across a range of sectors, including manufacturing, construction, hospitality, and professional services.

Mr Eccles said: "We're committed to York for the long term and expect to keep growing here over the years to come, so we are looking forward to welcoming our partners and wider business community."

To mark the office opening, Vante will host an open event for referral partners, introducers, and local business contacts on Thursday, November 26, from 2pm to 5pm.

The office is located a short walk from York Station and beside the Hudson Quarter development.

The open event will take place on the third floor of Colonia House, 16 Toft Green, York, YO1 6JT.
