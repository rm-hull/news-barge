## Boozed-up driver left ex-mayor with catastrophic injuries after ploughing into him

Luke Scott, left, has been jailed following a hit and run in Northallerton. His co-accused, Hayley Richardson, was sentenced for perverting the course of justice *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A drink-fuelled driver left a pensioner with catastrophic injuries after ploughing into him at speed in a town centre.

Luke Scott admitted driving the car, abandoning it and trying to burn it to destroy evidence.

The 34-year-old left the pensioner with serious injuries following the crash on Northallerton High Street at around 8.15pm on Tuesday, March 12, 2024.

His co-defendant Hayley Richardson admitted perverting the course of justice by helping destroy evidence.

Prosecutor Paul Newcombe said John Southwell, known as Josh, was thrown through the air and landed on his head in the road.

Teesside Crown Court heard Scott and Richardson drank 13 drinks between them at the Buck Inn before driving home.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21740504.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Luke Scott *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

Mr Newcombe said the pensioner was crossing the road after getting out of his car when Scott hit him and drove off.

He said: “The impact forcibly propelled the victim into the air and it must have been immediately obvious to both defendants what had happened.

“They struck the victim so hard that he left bullseye damage on the windscreen and dents in the front bumper and bonnet.”

Mr Newcombe said the defendants fled the High Street, then returned shortly afterwards to inspect the crash scene.

Mr Southwell, a former Mayor of Northallerton, suffered a bleed on the brain, fractures to the skull, jaw and bones of the face, and multiple fractures to his pelvis and both legs.

He spent months in hospital and now needs round-the-clock care from his wife and family because of his head injuries.

Police traced the Ford Fiesta to Richardson, while CCTV inside Scott’s home captured the pair planning to burn it to destroy evidence.

Scott was seen with the number plates in his hands inside the house before they set off to torch the car.

Mr Newcombe said the Ford Fiesta was found partially burned out on the A167 to the north of Northallerton the following morning.

The court heard how police were unable to breathalyse Scott due to the time elapsed from the crash and his arrest.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21740505.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hayley Richardson *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

In powerful victim impact statements, Mr Southwell’s wife of 56 years and daughter described how his injuries devastated the family.

His wife Susan said he could no longer look after himself.

“I cannot describe how it feels to have the relationship with the man I have been married to for 56 years reduced to having to keep an eye on him through a baby monitor at night,” she said.

His daughter Donna said: “These two people have destroyed my mum and dad’s lives and it has had a knock-on impact on everyone around them.

“They have taken away any of the life he knew and anything that he was able to do.”

Scott, of Brickside Way, Northallerton, admitted causing serious injury by careless driving and attempting to pervert the course of justice.

His lawyer Rachel Webster said the father-of-one had shown remorse and bitterly regretted his actions.

Richardson, 34, of Berry Close, Great Yarmouth, admitted attempting to pervert the course of justice.

Recorder Paul Reid said: “This is the worst case of careless driving, and the results thereof, that I have seen.

“You were originally charged with dangerous driving and you were fortunate that was not proceeded with.

“The aggravating factors were the speed you were travelling along Northallerton High Street and the enormous amount of alcohol you had consumed that day.”

Scott was jailed for 26 months and banned from driving for three years and eight months.

The judge added: “He thought you would be able to slow and avoid him but you didn’t – you went straight on at the same speed and hit him.

“This is all captured on CCTV and it is horrific”
