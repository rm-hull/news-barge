# Met Office -9C freeze warning as cold snap hits UK - full list of areas affected

## The Met Office has warned freezing temperatures could plunge as low as -9C on Friday night and into Saturday morning

Temperatures across certain regions of the UK could drop to as low as -9C on Friday evening through to Saturday morning, the Met Office has cautioned.

In a daily forecast video on the UK weather agency's website, meteorologist Alex Burkill outlined the weather conditions from 6pm onwards on Friday, explaining: "Later on as we go through the evening, cloud across southern areas will continue to break up and across the north it will stay largely clear. The cloud across those southern areas will continue to break up and across the north it will stay largely clear.

"The showers in the east should push away towards the North Sea, so it's really just across the far north of Scotland, and a couple of showers elsewhere where there may be a little bit of wet weather around. Most places, though, will have a dry and largely clear night, and with cold air coming down from the north, you know, perfect ingredients for temperatures to plummet."

He cautioned that the mercury dropped as low as 8.4C on Thursday night, and Friday night "could be even colder".

"Minus eight, minus nine, possibly negative double figures in some sheltered parts of Scotland," he said. "Later on as we go through the evening, cloud across southern areas "So a fairly widespread harsh frost here and even further south.

Yes, it's a bit milder, but it's still going to be chilly, and temperatures could be low enough for some frost in places too. Also need to watch out for some mist, some fog, some freezing fog patches, which will be slow to clear through tomorrow morning, he added.

Looking ahead to Saturday, the forecast promises a "crisp, frosty start for many places, but lots of sunshine on offer", he said. "Similar to today across northern areas, lots of blue sky, further south, it's going to be much brighter, with yes, some cloud at times, but also some decent sunshine, as well. A couple of showers across the far north of Scotland, a couple of showers elsewhere, but generally it is going to be dry.

"Later on as we go through the evening, cloud across southern areas. If anything, temperatures in the north tomorrow are a little bit lower than today, so a chilly feel for sure, despite the sunshine further south again. High single figures or just about low double digits, but more sunshine, brighter than it will have been through today."

He indicated little change is anticipated on Saturday evening, though conditions will remain fairly chilly and cold once more, with the meteorologist recommending those venturing out to dress warmly "because the clear skies will allow temperatures to really drop again after dark". "And whilst it might not be as cold a start on Sunday morning, there will still be some frost in places," Mr Burkill added.

Sunday is forecast to stay "pretty wet across these areas, so southern parts of Wales, the West Country, some southern counties of England as well, seeing persistent rain and some strong winds", with the potential for significant rainfall accumulations over Dartmoor.

"We could see perhaps in excess of 100 millimetres building up, so that could cause some localised issues," he warned.

"Other places though, having another fine day, lots of sunshine, but a slight shift in our wind means we're going to bringing in a few showers into some eastern parts, perhaps the best of the weather will be maybe to the west of the Pennines, something like that."

"Still a chilly-ish feel across northern areas, but temperatures up a fair few degrees compared to Saturday, so high single figures for many places here, and again, low double digits towards the south and south west."

The latest national forecast for this evening and tonight on the Met Office website is of a "few light showers in southwest, the north of Scotland and for Irish Sea coasts".

"Very cold for most with a sharp frost developing, severe in places, but milder in the far southwest. Some patches of mist and freezing fog."

On Saturday forecasters anticipate that fog will lift to "bring long spells of autumn sunshine in the north, but cloudy intervals further south and over Northern Ireland. Becoming breezy in the west this evening. Feeling cold."
