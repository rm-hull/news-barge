A train station on a well-used LNER route was forced to evacuate in the early hours of Friday morning after bomb disposal experts were called to deal with an emergency incident.

Emergency services were called to Edinburgh Waverley train station at around 1am on Friday, with reports of bomb disposal units spotted on Waverley Bridge, just outside the Christmas Markets, according to The Scotsman.

The station is on the LNER route from London King's Cross to Edinburgh via York but there is no disruption to York services this morning.

Yesterday, there was widespread disruption across the LNER network due to multiple incidents, including damage to overhead electrical wires at York, and the emergency services dealing with an incident between Stevenage and London.

Network Rail, which operates the Edinburgh station, confirmed that the station was later reopened around three hours after it was evaluated.

Officers from the British Transport Police attended the incident.

A Network Rail spokesperson told the paper at the time: “The police are dealing with an incident at Edinburgh. As a result, some lines are blocked and the station has been evacuated.”

In an updated statement, the spokesperson for Network Rail added: “Edinburgh Waverley station was closed overnight due to a police incident.

“Services are returning to normal, though we’re asking passengers to check journeys before travelling.”

British Transport Police has been approached for comment.
