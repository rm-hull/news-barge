Back in 2021—which feels like an age ago now—Rockstar added a weird picture to a house in San Andreas in its Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy—The Definitive Edition. It captured a small UFO hovering over a cream coloured bungalow with two palm trees in its front garden. It stood out because unlike some of the other pictures, players weren't really sure where it came from, but now we have an answer: It came from the future—bet you didn't see that one coming.

Well, actually some people did see it coming as they took it to be a tiny easter egg for a future GTA's Florida-setting, which we now know is correct. The picture wasn't just hinting at the location of Grand Theft Auto 6, though, it actually depicts a real location that players can go visit.

I saw this thanks to xGarbett's easter egg video, but quickly sent our own Mollie Taylor after the scent. After driving around Belville for a little while we found it: the UFO house. Mystery solved—or was it? Well as it turns out you can go into this house during the day and inside is yet another mystery.

Walking into the garage of this home, players will be met by a sprawling mind map plastered across the wall with "IT'S A SIMULATION" written in big red letters in the middle. Oh dear, an NPC has gained sentience.

There are other phrases across the cork board such as:

* "There's nothing behind closed doors."
* "Why can't I leave Leonida???"
* "Identical People" with a count of 140 scribbled beside it.
* "Number **6** - what does it mean?"
* "Who is L, who is J?"
* "I can't take it anymore"
