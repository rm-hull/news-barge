The Great Yorkshire Show in Harrogate will make history in 2027 as the Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir becomes the first choir ever to perform in the event’s iconic Main Ring during its 189-year history.

The choir, which made history itself as the first musical group to win Britain’s Got Talent, will take centre stage twice a day at the Great Yorkshire Show from Tuesday 13 to Friday 16 July 2027.

Made up of farmers and agricultural workers from across the country, the Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir has become known for celebrating rural life and sharing the story of farming with audiences nationwide.

Their appearance at the show comes as they prepare to release their debut album, *This Is Home*, on Friday 20 November.

Rachel Coates, Show Director and farmer from West Yorkshire, said the choir was a perfect fit for an event that celebrates the countryside and the people who work within it.

She said: “We’re always looking for ways to make a visit to the Great Yorkshire Show even better and so we have made a few forward-thinking changes this year, which we are excited to be announcing as we reveal our Main Ring attraction for 2027.

“The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir is doing a fantastic job of reaching the public about farming and so it’s a perfect fit for the show which celebrates and shares the important story of agriculture to thousands of people every year.”

Tickets for the 2027 Great Yorkshire Show have gone on sale with a new digital ordering system designed to make booking easier and more environmentally friendly.

Visitors can now add tickets and Yorkshire Agricultural Society membership cards directly to Apple and Google wallets, allowing quicker and simpler entry using a smartphone.

The show will continue to operate with advance ticket sales only, with visitor numbers capped at 150,000 across the four-day event.

A new family of four ticket option has also been introduced to provide greater flexibility for families attending the show.

Visitors will also have more time to enjoy the show in 2027, with the showground opening 15 minutes earlier each day at 7.45am.

The change has been introduced to give visitors extra time to explore the event, with many show ring competitions beginning at 8am.

Planning is already underway for the 2027 show, which will showcase the best of British livestock, the latest agricultural machinery, food, entertainment and countryside displays.

The Great Yorkshire Show, organised by the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, will once again celebrate farming, food and rural life, welcoming thousands of visitors to Harrogate.
