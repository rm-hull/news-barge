## Community health project celebrates first year supporting patients

The Ryedale Leg Club celebrated its birthday milestone on Monday 16 November 2026, marking a successful year of excellent lower-limb care, social connection, and community spirit. *(Image: Freelancer)*

A community health project is celebrating its first year of supporting people with lower-limb conditions.

The Ryedale Leg Club, based at Pickering Recreation Club, offers a non-medicalised, social model of care for individuals with leg and foot conditions, bringing together clinical treatment and community support in a welcoming, informal setting.

Members receive nursing care while enjoying conversation, tea, and social activities in a relaxed environment.

Barbara Harpham, chair of the Lindsay Leg Club, said: "It is a joy to come here today and see people being treated in such a welcoming, inviting atmosphere which has led to better healing rates as well as a chat and cuppa tea.

"Here at the leg club, they are not patients, they are people."

The club celebrated its one-year anniversary on Monday, November 16, with members, volunteers, nursing staff from Pickering Medical Practice, the Rotary Club of Pickering, and members of the South Hambleton and Ryedale Primary Care Network in attendance.

Dr Nicola Buchan, from the South Hambleton and Ryedale Primary Care Network and Pickering Medical Practice, said: "The Ryedale Leg Club has created a community of support that looks beyond just the medical need and provides holistic support for each individual."

The club meets every Monday from 9am to 11am at Pickering Recreation Club.
