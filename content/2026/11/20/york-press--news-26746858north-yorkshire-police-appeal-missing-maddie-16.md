*Update: In a statement issued this morning (Friday, November 20), North Yorkshire Police said the missing 16-year-old girl has been found safe.*

Police are searching for a missing 16-year-old girl last seen in a North Yorkshire village.

The teenager was last seen in Hunmanby at 3pm on Thursday, November 19, and may have travelled to Scarborough, North Yorkshire Police said.

Officers from the force say they are growing "increasingly concerned" for her welfare.

She is described by the force as white, 5ft 2in tall, of slim build, with shoulder-length brown hair.

Anyone who sees the 16-year-old or knows where she is should call 999.

Those with other information that could help the search are asked to call 101 and quote reference number 12260227489.
