## Speaking at a White House press conference on Friday, Trump defended his ties with Xi and Putin, called for a "real deal" with Iran and claimed the Russian leader wanted to end the war in Ukraine through diplomacy.

US President Donald Trump praised his recent trip to Asia during a White House press conference on Friday, highlighting his meetings with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, as well as trade discussions during the visit.

Trump described the historic trilateral meeting as a success, praising his relationship with both leaders.

"We have a very great relationship. That's a positive, by the way," Trump told reporters. He also called critics of his relationships with Putin and Xi "stupid", responding to backlash over his description of Putin as his "friend".

Turning to Iran, Trump said Tehran wanted an agreement with Washington but had yet to offer terms he considered acceptable. He insisted the US was seeking a "real deal" rather than a "fake" agreement.

“We want to make a real deal, not a fake deal,” adding that Iranian government has been “bull-----ing people for 51 years.”

On Ukraine, Trump said Putin had expressed a desire to end the war "by diplomatic means", but blamed tensions between the Russian leader and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy for complicating peace efforts.

"The problem is President Zelenskyy and President Putin have tremendous hatred, and that's never easy," he told reporters.

Trump also addressed artificial intelligence, warning that US companies building data centres overseas could face heavy taxes, arguing that the facilities should remain in the United States.
