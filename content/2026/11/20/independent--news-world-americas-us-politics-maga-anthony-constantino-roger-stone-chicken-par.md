Incoming New York Republican Rep. Anthony Constantino missed part of his congressional orientation in Washington, D.C., in order to go out for dinner with the infamous political “dirty trickster” Roger Stone.

The congressman-elect, 44, who will succeed Elise Stefanik in New York’s 21st district, told *Punchbowl News* he considered dining out with Stone in Florida “more important” than choosing office space on Capitol Hill, a task he was happy to leave to his aides.

“I let my staff pick the office so I can work on more important things such as eating chicken parmesan with Roger Stone while planning how to defend us from Radical Democrats,” he said.

Stone, 74, also confirmed the story, posting a photo of an enormous portion of the dish on X with the comment: “I not only had chicken parmesan with Anthony Constantino but it was the biggest best chicken parm from Zito’s of Fort Lauderdale.”

The representative-elect, who is set to take office on January 3, appeared to object to receiving further questions about the dinner from social media and reporters.

“Losers in the media can’t understand that it’s possible for me to let my staff pick my office while I plan to save America from Radical Democrats with Roger Stone,” he vented in a post of his own on the same platform.

“Time is finite and picking an office is not something I needed to personally do. BTW, I got a great office that was formerly occupied by Brandon Gill, another Republican star!”

He went on to call a CBS6 journalist a “creepy stalker” for asking about which stages of the orientation he missed and responded to another critic by asking: “Why do some unsuccessful people try to give advice to successful people instead of learning from them?”

Ultimately, he chose to make light of the media interest, posting: “Constantino ditched part of orientation to eat chicken parm with Roger Stone is now part of American history.”

Constantino is the co-founder and CEO of the custom printing company Sticker Mule and has boxed professionally.

![New York Republican representative-elect Anthony Constantino ducked out of part of his congressional orientation to have dinner with storied GOP 'dirty trickster' Roger Stone](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/16/48/GettyImages-2303150362.jpg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

He has previously explained that his decision to enter politics, which saw him narrowly beat Democrat Blake Gendebien earlier this month, was inspired by Stone, the veteran Republican operative and Donald Trump ally.

“Politically, I didn’t expect to be running for office,” Constantino told WAMC in June.

“I just wanted to fight for the president. And then people asked me to run, I talked to a guy named Roger Stone about it, he encouraged me to do it. This was during the special election.”

Stone went on to advise Constantino’s campaign and, according to *Mother Jones*, “told him to be nicer.”

In addition to his business ventures and burgeoning career in MAGA politics, Constantino is also an aspiring hip-hop artist and released an album entitled *Thank You President Trump* in January this year.

Incredibly, the record features Stone rapping on its final track about his gratitude to the 47th president for making America great again and exposing “scheming” Democrats.
