The first Black Friday AirPods deals have started to roll out, and there's a split between the US and the UK in terms of what has good deals so far. If you're in the US, you can get two good deals: the AirPods Max 2 for $419 at Amazon (was $549) or grab the AirPods Pro 3 for $179 at Amazon (was $249).

In the UK, the new (only released in September) AirPods 5 have a nice early discount. You can get the AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging for £119 at Amazon (was £139), and while it's not a huge price cut, it's great that such a new product has any money off, really.
