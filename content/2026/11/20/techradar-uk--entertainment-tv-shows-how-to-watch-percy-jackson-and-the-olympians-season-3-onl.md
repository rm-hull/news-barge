Dealing with heavier themes – literally, in the case of Atlas – *Percy Jackson and the Olympians* season 3 sees the threat posed by Kronos loom ever larger over the Olympian gods. There's trouble from the outset, and Annabeth throws herself into harm's way to rescue Nico and Bianca di Angelo, the vulnerable children of Hades.

She's duly whisked away by Dr. Thorn, acting on behalf of Kronos, and Percy and Grover don't hesitate to rush to her aid, paying little heed to the Oracle of Delphi's prophecy dictating that a child of Zeus, Poseidon or Hades could pave the way to victory for the Titan.

Based on *The Titan's Curse*, the third instalment in Rick Riordan's series, it brings to the screen their run-in with colossal man of bronze Talos at the Junkyard of the Gods, their encounter with the Ophiotaurus Bessie, a snake-bull hybrid creature, at the Hoover Dam and, of course, the climactic showdown with Atlas, who's being helped by Luke.

It's also been foretold that not everyone will survive the quest – a sacrific worth making, according to Zeus.
