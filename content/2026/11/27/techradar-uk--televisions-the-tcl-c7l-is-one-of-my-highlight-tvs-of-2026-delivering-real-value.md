## Real value for money

![TCL C7L with shot of Racer family kitchen from Speed Racer on screen. C7L shows punchy colors](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/rAwuLY4TCQ8Mbm9fUEPVea-1200-80.jpg)

TCL's mid-range mini-LEDs have been going from strength-to-strength as of late, with improving picture quality each year. The TCL C7L is yet another step in the right direction, now using TCL's new Super Quantum Dot (SQ) mini-LED panel to deliver sensational picture quality.

What impressed me the most during my C7L testing was its truly deep blacks and rich contrast, with very little signs of blooming (where light from bright objects against a dark background raises black levels and creates a 'halo' effect around said bright object) — something that has historically been mini-LED's kryptonite. But this isn't an issue for the C7L.

Elsewhere, the C7L has truly vibrant colors that shine in movies I tested like *Speed Racer*and* The Super Mario Galaxy Movie*, with both of these films getting plenty of pop when I tested them on the C7L.

If you're looking for a gaming TV, the C7L is well spec'd too. It supports 4K 144Hz, FreeSync Premium Pro VRR, Dolby Vision gaming and ALLM, as well as registering a measured 9.8ms input lag time (we consider anything below 10ms fantastic). When playing *Battlefield V*, I found that the C7L handled the chaotic and fast-paced motion with ease.
