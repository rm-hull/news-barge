Police have released CCTV images of a couple they want to identify after a parcel was stolen from a doorstep on Valley Road in Harrogate.

The couple were seen on the resident’s CCTV at around 5:35pm on Saturday 7th November.

They were with two young children who have been cropped from the images.

North Yorkshire Police said the woman appeared to spot the parcel on the doorstep and draw it to the man’s attention.

The man was then seen returning to the property and taking the parcel, which reportedly contained around £66 worth of Kiehl’s products.

*Anyone who recognises the couple can email [peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:peter.henderson@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101 and ask for PC 1668 Henderson, quoting reference 12260221077.*

*Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*
