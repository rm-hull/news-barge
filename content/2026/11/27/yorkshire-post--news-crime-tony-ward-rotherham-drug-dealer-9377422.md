# Yorkshire drug dealer jailed after being caught with £60,000 of drugs in his home

## A Yorkshire drug dealer has been jailed after being caught with drugs worth £60,000 and weapons in his own home.

South Yorkshire Police said officers had built up “substantial evidence” against 34-year-old Tony Ward before raiding his home on Maple Road, Kiveton, near Rotherham, on April 9 this year.

As well as the haul of Class A and B drugs, officers also found drug paraphernalia, cash, several mobile phones and a zombie knife.

Ward was arrested at the scene and appeared at Sheffield Magistrates' Court the following day.

At Sheffield Crown Court on November 19, Ward pleaded guilty to four counts of possession with intent to supply and possession of an offensive weapon.

![Tony Ward, 36, of Maple Road, Kiveton, was caught in his own home with £60,000 of drugs including ketamine, crack cocaine and cannabis.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/25/18/48/Untitled-Design-(67).jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He was sentenced to four years and one month in prison.

Detective Sergeant Matthew Jackson, from our Rotherham Fortify team, said: "Ward was involved in a highly sophisticated drugs line and thought he could get away with his crimes.

“We have no tolerance for illegal drugs in Rotherham - they damage lives and our communities, and we are fully committed to tackling the ongoing issue of drug supply.

"We will continue to identify and arrest those who are involved in the supply of controlled drugs, and we have a team of dedicated officers who will target those people to ensure they are brought to justice.

"I am pleased with the sentence handed to Ward, and I hope this serves as a warning to others who are involved in this type of criminality."
