# Woman hit by car and carried on bumper while trying to restrain loose horse

## Police are appealing for witnesses and footage after a woman was hit by a car in Horbury while trying to restrain a loose horse.

Wakefield CID of West Yorkshire Police would like to speak to anyone who can assist their investigation into an incident on Bridge Road in Horbury on Monday September 10, 2026, in which a woman in her 30s was struck by a white Audi car.

The incident took place between 4pm and 4.30pm after the woman was struck by a white Audi A6 while trying to restrain a loose horse she had seen, police said.

The victim was briefly carried on the bumper of the car, which had hit her after overtaking a vehicle in front, before it made off.

She was left with non life threatening injuries as a result of the collision, police confirmed.

Following enquiries police arrested a 22-year-old woman on suspicion of attempted section 18 assault in connection with the matter.

She was released on bail pending continuing investigation.

Anyone who can assist the investigation with phone , dashcam or CCTV footage is asked to contact Wakefield CID on 101 or online at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat)

Information can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111.
