TWO people from York who died in a crash in Leeds earlier this month are said to have died from injuries to their head, neck and chest.

An inquest into the deaths of Cameron Bell, 22 of Yew Tree Close, York, and Jade Langton, 23 of Cranbrook Road, York, opened on Thursday (November 26).

The pair were killed after a vehicle, allegedly a stolen BMW, fled from police - reaching speeds of 80mph on two roads and doing a U-turn, before hitting into another vehicle containing Cameron and Jade.

According to reports from the BBC, Wakefield Coroner's Court was told that Bell had been driving the car and Langton was a passenger when the collision happened.

Senior coroner Oliver Longstaff said Bell was pronounced dead at the scene while Langton was taken to Leeds General Infirmary where she later died.

“The inquests have been adjourned until the outcome of criminal proceedings,” said a spokesperson for the Coroner’s Office.

Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place in Harrogate, appeared before Leeds Magistrates' Court last week (November 16) after being charged in connection with the collision in Gelderd Road in Leeds.

He has been remanded in custody until his next appearance at Leeds Crown Court on December 17.

In tributes made in The Press, Jade's mum Emma Langton shared a statement on behalf of herself, her husband Andy, and their daughter Keelie – saying that the family were ‘completely devastated by the loss of our beautiful Jade.’

She said: "We are struggling to come to terms with what has happened and the reality that she has been taken from us far too soon.

"Jade had a big personality and loved being with people, chatting, giggling, going to live music, traveling and other adventures. She left an impact wherever she was.

"Whilst she always loved a bit of drama, Jade did not deserve to have her life cut short in this way. There is a big hole in our family and she is already hugely missed.

"Thank you to the emergency services and everyone who did everything they could to help.

"The kindness, support and condolences we have received from family, friends and the wider community is appreciated.

"We share our deepest condolences to Cam’s family and friends too.

"As we try to navigate this unimaginable loss, we respectfully ask people to be mindful about what they share and comment on social media.

"We kindly ask that our family's privacy is respected while we grieve together and try to come to terms with losing our Jade.

"If anyone had anything negative to say, please - just scroll by. Love you more Jade xx"
