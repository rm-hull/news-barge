A **Levski Sofia** supporter has been filmed appearing to perform a**Nazi salute** in the stands during**Marseille’s Europa League** tie against the Bulgarian side, in footage that has circulated widely online. The clip emerged amid chaotic scenes at the**Stade Vélodrome**, where flares and other projectiles were thrown and rival supporters clashed before and during** Thursday’s**match.** Around 500 Levski fans**were housed in the away section. About** ten minutes before kick-off**, several** Marseille supporters**ran from a home stand and breached the away enclosure, apparently targeting the Bulgarian fans. Projectiles and flares were thrown between the two groups, and further disturbances broke out during the half-time break.** Five Levski Sofia supporters**were taken to hospital with injuries including eye injuries, head trauma and a dislocated shoulder, according to the** Marseille police prefecture**.** Around a dozen**Bulgarian fans were reportedly injured in total. Despite the disturbances, the match kicked off as scheduled, with** Marseille**securing a** 1-0 victory** over the Bulgarian side.

The violence drew condemnation from French President **Emmanuel Macron** and his Bulgarian counterpart,**Iliana Iotova**. Levski Sofia coach** Julio Velázquez**also condemned the incidents, describing the scenes as**“unacceptable for football”**. The disturbances overshadowed the European fixture and renewed concerns over spectator safety at international club matches.
