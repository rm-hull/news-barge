Andy Farrell's side narrowly missed out on the final by a lack of bonus points, while the Wallabies are looking for revenge for that 33-31 defeat back in July.

The Irish are one of three Northern Hemisphere teams to finish on five wins and one defeat from their six games, but it was the narrow size of those victories and the wide 40-21 loss to New Zealand that meant they trailed France and England in third.
