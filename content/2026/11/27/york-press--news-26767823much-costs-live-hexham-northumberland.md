A market town in Northumberland was recently named one of the happiest places to live but how much does it cost to live there?

Rightmove has revealed its annual list of the happiest places to live in the UK - and Hexham has taken one of the top spots.

Residents from 221 locations were asked about what it is like to live in their area and had to rate them based on things like access to green spaces, amenities, and employment opportunities.

Alongside Hexham, spots like Hove, Skipton, Cirencester, and Richmond upon Thames featured on the list.

## How much it costs to live in Hexham

The national average asking price for properties in the UK is £371,015 as of October 2026, according to Rightmove.

The average price of houses in Hexham sits at just below the national average with properties costing around £337,642 over the last year.

The majority of properties sold in the area were detached houses that cost an average of £470,571.

Terraced properties sold for an average of £287,825 while semi-detached properties cost around £311,376.

Rightmove said: “Overall, the historical sold prices in Hexham over the last year were 10 per cent up on the previous year and 10 per cent up on the 2022 peak of £307,000.”

If you are thinking about renting in the area, the average monthly rent in Hexham costs around £1,329.

### **What makes Hexham one of the happiest places to live?**

In the survey, Hexham residents ranked access to nature and green spaces as the number one reason why they love the area.

Access to essential services like doctors and schools was second while non-essential amenities like restaurants and shops ranked third.

Residents also said the people that live there are “friendly and polite”.

Hexham can be found along the River Tyne with the Tyne Green Country Park being a great spot for riverside walks.

The area has a few primary schools and high schools as well as good transport links to Newcastle by train.

There are also lots of restaurants and cafés in Hexham such as Wentworth Café, Muro Lounge, Danielle’s Bistro, and Bouchon Bistrot.
