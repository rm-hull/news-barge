Sir Julian Smith MP met Claire McKeown and Chris Smith, who took over Storehouse Bar & Eatery in Ripon this year with no previous hospitality experience.

Two neighbours who took over Storehouse Bar & Eatery in Ripon earlier this year, with no previous hospitality experience between them, have shown their MP round the business.

Sir Julian Smith, the MP for Skipton and Ripon, met Director Claire McKeown and Co-owner Chris Smith, along with members of their team, to talk about the challenges and opportunities facing independent hospitality businesses in North Yorkshire.

## Overview

* **Who:** Sir Julian Smith MP, Storehouse Director Claire McKeown, Co-owner Chris Smith and their staff.
* **What:** a constituency visit to hear how the new owners have got on since taking over.
* **Why they took it on:** to preserve what Claire called a much-loved venue at the heart of the local community.
* **Who helped:** the existing staff, who the owners say have played a vital role in helping the business thrive.

## Taking over with no hospitality background

Claire explained that she and her neighbour Chris took over Storehouse Bar & Eatery determined to keep a much-loved venue going at the heart of the community, even though neither had worked in hospitality before.

Since then, the staff have played a vital role in helping the business thrive, according to the MP’s office, supporting the new owners and keeping the place welcoming for customers.

![Sir Julian Smith in a suit talking to Claire McKeown in front of a blue-tiled bar with hanging lights](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/julian-smith-storehouse-bar-eatery-ripon-2.webp)

## What Sir Julian said

“It was great to visit Storehouse Bar & Eatery and meet Claire and Chris,” he said. “Independent businesses like this play an important role in Ripon’s economy and community life.

“I was pleased to learn about the staff who have helped guide the business through a major transition and ensured it remains such a welcoming place for customers. I wish Claire, Chris and the whole team every success for the future.”

![A seating area with leather sofas, tables, exposed brick walls, a staircase and a Christmas tree inside Storehouse](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/julian-smith-storehouse-bar-eatery-ripon-3.webp)

The visit was part of Sir Julian’s programme of constituency visits to local businesses, community groups and employers across Skipton and Ripon. He also recently met politics students at Ripon Grammar School.
