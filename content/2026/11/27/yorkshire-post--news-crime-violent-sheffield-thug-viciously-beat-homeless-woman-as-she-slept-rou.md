# Violent thug viciously beat homeless woman as she slept rough on city centre street

## A violent thug has been jailed after punching and kicking a homeless woman as she lay on the pavement in Sheffield.

Lee Robertson attacked the woman as she tried to sleep under a blanket outside Hallam Business School in Sheffield city centre on January 14 this year.

Debbie Speight, prosecuting, said a probation report showed Robertson had demonstrated “no remorse” or “empathy for his victim”, even “justifying” his actions.

The motive for the attack were not made clear however Ms Speight said the violence lasted around two minutes.

![Lee Robertson was jailed for 16 months](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/26/11/48/Screenshot-2026-11-26-105801.jpg?trim=1707,15,808,0&width=640&quality=65)


The 51-year-old was arrested three months later after verbally abusing a parking warden in Hartshead Square while “intoxicated”.

Robertson had 89 previous offences on his record, the court heard, including battery and public disorder.

Ed Moss, defending, said his client was now clean of drink and drugs since being remanded into prison and apologised to the victim.

Recorder Brian Whitehead jailed Robertson for 16 months, noting the “nasty” assault was “prolonged and persistent” against a vulnerable woman.

The defendant, of Blackstock Road, Gleadless Valley, admitted actual bodily harm and threatening behaviour.
