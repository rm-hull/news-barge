## Investigation into high value thefts from York and Selby supermarkets one day apart

North Yorkshire Police believe these two men may be able to help with their investigation *(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

AROUND £1000 of goods have been stolen from supermarkets in York and Selby.

North Yorkshire Police has released CCTV images of two men they want to speak to following thefts from supermarkets in Haxby and Selby last month.

The first incident took place on October 14 at the Sainsbury’s store in Haxby, where around £550 worth of stock was stolen.

The following day (October 15), two men entered the Sainsbury’s store on Portholme Road in Selby and stole approximately £450 worth of goods.

A police spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the men pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation."
