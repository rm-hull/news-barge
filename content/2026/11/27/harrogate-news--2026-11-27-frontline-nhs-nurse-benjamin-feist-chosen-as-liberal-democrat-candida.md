**Experienced emergency medicine nurse Benjamin Feist will contest the Skipton and Ripon seat to prioritise health services, tackle the cost of living crisis, and oppose contentious local transport policy changes.**

The Liberal Democrats have selected Benjamin Feist, a senior NHS nurse specialising in emergency medicine, as their parliamentary candidate for the Skipton and Ripon constituency ahead of the forthcoming General Election. Mr Feist, aged 35, brings more than ten years of frontline NHS experience and currently serves as an Advanced Clinical Practitioner in an Emergency Department as well as a Trust Education Lead for Advanced Practice.

Drawing on his medical background, Mr Feist has highlighted improving local health services as a central part of his campaign priorities. He also intends to focus on addressing the ongoing cost of living crisis affecting households in Skipton and Ripon, particularly in light of recent announcements of rising energy bills.

Furthermore, Mr Feist has voiced opposition to the Conservative-led North Yorkshire Council’s controversial home-to-school transport policy, which has reportedly imposed additional costs and uncertainty on rural families in the area. He plans to work with local Liberal Democrat councillors and campaigners to seek the reversal of this policy.

> **Benjamin Feist:**  
> I’m proud to have been selected as the Liberal Democrat parliamentary candidate for Skipton and Ripon. People across the constituency deserve a strong voice in Parliament, and I’m looking forward to making the case for the changes our communities need
>
> Having spent more than a decade working on the frontline of our NHS, I have seen first-hand the consequences of years of underinvestment and political failure. I see the challenges facing patients, their families and NHS staff every single day. I am determined to use my experience to fight for the improvements to local health services that people across Skipton and Ripon deserve.
>
> Today’s announcement of rising energy bills is yet another blow to families already struggling with the cost of living. Far too many people are finding it increasingly difficult to make ends meet, and tackling the cost of living crisis will be at the heart of my campaigning.
>
> I am also appalled by the Conservatives’ ill-thought-out changes to home-to-school transport. At a time when families are already struggling financially, the Conservatives are piling further costs onto rural households. I will be working closely with our Liberal Democrat councillors and local campaigners to get this unfair policy overturned.
>
> The Liberal Democrats have shown, by winning the neighbouring Harrogate and Knaresborough constituency, that we can defeat the Conservatives in North Yorkshire.
>
> I am determined to fight for a fair deal for our communities, protect our public services, support our local farmers and ensure that rural communities are no longer taken for granted.

Mr Feist’s commitment to public service extends beyond his NHS career. Motivated by the loss of his cousin to a brain tumour during childhood, he has raised more than £20,000 for children’s hospice care and received the Diana Award in 2009, recognising his contributions to social action and humanitarian work. He was also a torchbearer in the 2012 London Olympic Torch Relay.

In addition to his NHS work, Mr Feist has volunteered with St John Ambulance, provided medical support at the London Olympic and Paralympic Games, and served as an international aid volunteer in rural Uganda.

The Skipton and Ripon constituency covers a predominantly rural area of North Yorkshire, including the market towns of Skipton, Ripon, Settle, and Masham, as well as smaller communities like Pateley Bridge and Grassington. It is currently represented by Conservative MP Sir Julian Smith, who retained the seat with a majority of 1,650 votes at the July 2024 General Election.
