# Watch: What has happened to online pension and benefits services?

Millions of people have been left without access to online benefits and pensions services after the government said its system had been hit by a "cyber incident".

Downing Street has not ruled out an attempted hack but said there was "currently no evidence" personal data had been lost, adding an investigation was taking place.

The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) said it had taken the digital services down as a "precaution", and pensions and benefits payments would be paid as normal.

BBC senior technology reporter Shiona McCallum has been taking a look at what happened.
