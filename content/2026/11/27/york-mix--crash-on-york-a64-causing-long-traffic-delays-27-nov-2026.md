A crash on the A64 in York has caused long delays this morning (Friday).

It happened on the Outer Ring Road close to the turn off to Fulford.

As a result, eastbound traffic is backing up all the way to the Pike Hills Golf Club.

#### Crash location

Traffic is also very heaving on the A19 coming into York and along Fulford Road leaving York.
