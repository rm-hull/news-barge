**Police have confirmed that a body found in woodland in southern Scotland has been identified as missing 20-year-old Kelsey Hunter.**

She had last been seen in Castle Douglas at 17:00 on Saturday and a major search was carried out around the nearby village of Springholm where she lived.

On Tuesday, Daniel McKnight, 21, appeared on petition at Dumfries Sheriff Court charged with murder and two other charges, including defeating the ends of justice. Kelsey's body was found at about 19:15 on Wednesday.

McKnight made no plea and was remanded in custody.

![A map viewed from above showing the area where a search operation took place near Springholm](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/5a9f/live/16f80d50-ea7e-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.png)

The search operation around the Dumfries and Galloway village concluded when the body was found but police said extensive inquiries remained ongoing with a number of resources being used - including forensic specialists.

Det Ch Insp Stephen McGrath said: "Our thoughts remain with Kelsey's family at this incredibly difficult time, and we are continuing to provide them with support.

"They have asked that their privacy is respected.

"Extensive work remains ongoing and a team of officers are continuing to carry out inquiries."

![Three officers in white forensic suits next to a police van. They are next to a bus stop and a road leading out to a rural area. ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/96ec/live/5b026f40-e82b-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

Police said forensic specialists were still involved in their inquiries

He thanked everyone who had come forward with information so far and said officers would remain in the area while the investigation continued.

Door-to-door and CCTV inquiries are still being carried out.

Local area commander Emma Dodds said: "We understand how upsetting this has been for the local community, but we continue to have a significant amount of resources dedicated to this investigation.

He asked anyone with any concerns, or information to contact police, external.

![A policeman stands outside police tape in a roadway with cars parked behind him and 30mph signs](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/100f/live/36987d40-ea81-11f1-a739-457c2295eaba.jpg)

Support has been offered at the primary school near where a cordon has been in place

A large police presence has been in the village - which sits next to the A75 that runs from Gretna to Stranraer - since Sunday evening.

Dogs, a helicopter and specialist officers were involved in the search while officers made door-to-door inquiries.

Dumfries and Galloway Council has been offering support to the village primary which is close to where a police cordon was in place.

It said a message had been sent to the community to reassure them that the school continued to be a "safe and nurturing space".

The local authority's principal education psychologist has also been working with the primary and Castle Douglas High School to "provide support to learners and staff".
