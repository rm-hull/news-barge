M&S shoppers have given a chocolate and clementine sandwich a mixed reception.

The Japanese-style sandwich is part of the retailer's food on the move range and was created as a limited-edition festive treat.

According to the M&S website, it contains a creamy filling and pieces of clementine, with Belgian dark chocolate sauce between slices of cocoa-enriched brioche-style bread.

The 160g sandwich is suitable for vegetarians.

Shoppers shared their reactions on social media, with some keen to try the combination and others less convinced.

One user said: "Oh for goodness' sake, how ridiculous! (As I frantically run to M&S)."

Another said: "I need to try this."

A third said: "Sounds delicious? Looking forward to trying it."

Another said: "That's right up my street."

Other commenters questioned whether the sweet snack was more like cake than a sandwich.

One user said: "Each to their own, but not for me."

Another said: "If that is a cake it looks yummy, but it's made with bread!"

A third said: "They are really scraping the barrel."

Another said: "This is probably the only 'no' from me regarding the M&S food hall items."

An M&S Food spokesperson said: "We wanted the clementines to be the real star of this sandwich, so they make up nearly a third of the recipe, ensuring there’s a generous amount of juicy fruit in every bite.

"M&S is known for its clementines at Christmas, so it felt completely natural to pair them with rich chocolate, which perfectly balances their zesty flavour without ever feeling too heavy.

"We can't wait for our customers to try it."
