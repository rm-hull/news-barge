**A man who was once described as "the most swiped-right man on Tinder" is due in court charged with rape following an alleged attack at The Groucho Club in central London.**

Stefan-Pierre Tomlin, 36, who in 2017 was said to be the most coveted man on the dating app, is due at Southwark Crown Court on Monday also charged with two counts of assault by penetration.

It follows a Met Police investigation after a woman reported she had been raped inside the private members' club in Soho on 13 November 2024.

Tomlin, of Brentwood, was arrested in Hertfordshire on suspicion of rape in 2024 and charges were authorised in September this year. He appeared at Southend Magistrates' Court on 2 November.

The Met Police said the woman continued to be supported by specialist officers.

Tomlin, who has 454,000 followers on Instagram, describes himself as a pilot, actor and "most right swiped man on Tinder".

The Groucho Club was opened in 1985 as a more relaxed alternative to traditional gentlemen's clubs and has a long association with celebrities.

The venue was temporarily closed in late 2024 after Westminster City Council suspended its licence following a request from the Met Police but reopened a month later.
