## Snow in York's wintry mix but how long will it last?

Flashback to snowy York last year *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

Look hard and you just might see some snow.

Big wet flakes formed part of a wintry mix that descended on York around 10am.

However, forecasters said the wintry mix would not last long.

The Met Office forecast sleet at 10am, with temperatures of 3C.

The BBC predicted light rain and a gentle breeze, with temperatures of 3pm.
