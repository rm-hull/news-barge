A BBC Christmas special will see Father Christmas “caught doing some burglaries”, as cast hint there may be a few "unexpected moments".

The festive episode of the BBC drama, Beyond Paradise, which launches today (November 27), follows DI Humphrey Goodman as he investigates a spate of Santa Claus robberies.

Kris Marshall, who plays DI Goodman, said: "Santa Claus has been caught doing some burglaries, but everything is not as it seems.

"As Humphrey always says, there's no such thing as a coincidence, so, it's suspicious that there's a spate of Santa Claus robberies throughout the festive period."

## BBC's Beyond Paradise Christmas special

Beyond Paradise is a spin-off of Death in Paradise and is set in the fictional town of Shipton Abbott.

Last year’s Christmas episode saw Don Warrington reprise his role as Commissioner Selwyn, appearing as Humphrey’s best man.

Barbara Flynn, who plays Anne Lloyd, hinted there may be further surprises in the 2026 special.

She said: "Beyond Paradise is always full of surprises and that's one of the reasons audiences enjoy it so much.

"The Christmas special brings together mystery, humour and plenty of festive warmth, and there are certainly a few unexpected moments along the way.

"It wouldn't be Christmas in Shipton Abbott without a surprise or two."

The series has run for four seasons since 2023, with a fifth confirmed for 2027.

Beyond Paradise follows DI Goodman as he solves crimes in Shipton Abbott, having left the Caribbean island of Saint Marie to be with his partner Martha.

The original series, Death In Paradise, has aired since 2011 and is expected to continue until at least 2028 with its own Christmas special being broadcast this year.

The Beyond Paradise Christmas special will be available on BBC iPlayer from November 27.

The show is just one part of the BBC's Christmas offerings with many family favourite shows returning with festive editions.

EastEnders will be bringing drama over the festive period while fans can enjoy Christmassy versions of shows like Strictly Come Dancing, The Wheel, and The Weakest Link.
