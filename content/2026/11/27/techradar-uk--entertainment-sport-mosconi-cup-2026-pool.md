Since successfully defending their crown in Las Vegas seven years ago, the US side haven't really come close to regaining it. Perhaps that's why they've gone back to the last man to captain them to a win, reappointing the legendary Johan Ruijsink. He has two world champions at his disposal, in 2024 champion Fedor Gorst and – making a record-breaking 20th consecutive appearance – Shane Van Boening, and brings back Justin Bergman to the fray as a wildcard after his own seven-year absence.

It's hard to imagine, however, that Europe captain Niels Feijen will be too concerned. David Alcaide, Joshua Filler and Jayson Shaw all played in the winning quintet last year, while the form of US Open winner Francisco Sanchez Ruiz and Serbian debutant Aleksa Pecelj propelled each of them into the side.
