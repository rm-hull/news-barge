**The Granby Care Home will welcome older local residents for a complimentary Christmas meal prepared and served by Harbour Healthcare’s Board of Directors on 9th December 2026.**

Harbour Healthcare’s Board of Directors is set to give something back to the local community this Christmas by hosting and serving a complimentary festive lunch for older people at The Granby Care Home in Harrogate. The event will take place on Wednesday, 9th December 2026, with up to 40 guests invited for an afternoon of festive food, friendship, and celebrations.

Most attendees are expected to be members of the local charity Supporting Older People. In a distinctive show of community spirit, Harbour Healthcare’s Board members will step away from their usual boardroom duties to prepare and serve a three-course Christmas lunch, dedicating their time to making the occasion memorable.

The initiative has been organised to give back to the communities where Harbour Healthcare operates and to provide an opportunity for older people to experience companionship during a time when loneliness can be particularly hard. The Granby team has collaborated closely with Supporting Older People to connect with older residents who might otherwise have limited social interactions.

Harbour Healthcare emphasises that care homes should serve as community hubs, fostering meaningful relationships beyond care provision. Ashley Worsley, Chief Executive Officer of Harbour Healthcare, commented:

> **Ashely Worsley:**  
> As a Board, we wanted to do something meaningful this Christmas and give a little something back to the communities that are so important to us. Christmas should be a time for people to come together, enjoy good company and feel included.

We’re delighted to be able to bring people together at The Granby and, importantly, for our Board to play such a hands-on part in making the day special. It’s about more than a Christmas lunch; it’s about showing that we care about the wider community and recognising the importance of connection, kindness and companionship.

Harbour Healthcare and The Granby expressed thanks to Supporting Older People for their support in facilitating the event and everyone who helped organise the community celebration. This Community Christmas Lunch demonstrates Harbour Healthcare’s ongoing commitment to supporting older people and fostering community bonds beyond the festive season.
