A primary school has won this year’s prestigious York Primary School Public Speaking Competition.

Poppleton Road School won the city-wide contest, held on November 17.

Pupils from across York took part, debating topics including "11 is the wrong age to own a smartphone", "Life in York in 100 years will be…", and "There should be a statue in York for…".

The event is organised by a steering group led by City of York Council, and the venue was provided by the Merchant Adventurers’.

Councillor Bob Webb, executive member for children, young people and education at City of York Council, said: "Being able to present ideas and make a reasoned argument is such an important skill that adults use on a regular basis, but it takes practice and confidence to speak in front of others.

"The competition is a great way to encourage young people to start honing the skills they will need in the future.

"I’m so impressed by the skills the young people have shown here this evening.

"I’d like to thank the school staff, parents, carers, and young people who’ve contributed to making this event such a success."

The competition is held annually.
