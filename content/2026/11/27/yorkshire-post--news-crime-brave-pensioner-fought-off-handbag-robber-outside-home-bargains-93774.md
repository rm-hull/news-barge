# Brave pensioner fought off drug-addicted handbag robber outside Home Bargains store in Yorkshire

## A “cowardly” attack on a elderly woman by a desparate drug addict backfired when the woman bravely fought back, a court has heard.

Marie McBride picked on the elderly woman assuming she would be an easy target, but the determined pensioner put up a fight, forcing McBride to flee empty handed.

The 47-year-old was handed a prison sentence at Leeds Crown Court after admitting attempted robbery and a burglary in which she stole medication from Boots.

Prosecutor Victoria Ball said McBride had been in the Boots shop in Morley on June 23, when she was caught on CCTV leaning over the counter and grabbing epilepsy medication worth £69 and putting it into her pocket before leaving.

She was later arrested and denied the offence during her police interview, before refusing to answer subsequent questions.

Then on the afternoon of July 8, the 76-year-old victim had been walking past Home Bargains in Morley when she was spotted by McBride who was sat on the pavement outside the shop.

![McBride (inset) tried to rob the elderly lady outside Home Bargains.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/25/12/23/McBride.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The elderly woman was walking slowly with a stick, Ms Ball said.

McBride got up and walked towards her before trying to rip the bag from the woman’s shoulder.

But the victim fought back and refused to let go, shouting and screaming at McBride.

Members of the public heard the commotion and came to the woman’s aid as McBride then ran off.

Again, she was identified by police and arrested, telling officers it was a “stupid in-the-moment” decision to attempt to rob the woman’s bag.

McBride, of Jubilee Street, Morley, said she was “rattling” - withdrawing from heroin at the time and needed money to buy the drug.

Mitigating, Martin Morrow said that she was “realistic and understands the gravity” of her offending, realising she faced jail.

She appeared in court over a video link from HMP New Hall, where she has been held on remand since her second arrest.
