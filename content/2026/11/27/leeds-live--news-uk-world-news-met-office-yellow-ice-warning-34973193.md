# Met Office yellow ice warning for Yorkshire and Leeds as freezing rain looms

## The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for ice for Friday (November 27)

The Met Office has issued an alert specifically targeting over-65s on Friday, as a new ice warning comes into force. The forecaster released a yellow weather warning for ice covering Friday (November 27) late on Thursday evening.

The 10-hour warning kicks in at 4am and remains in place until 2pm, affecting millions of residents across the north of England and the Midlands, encompassing major cities including Manchester, Sheffield, and Bradford.

Plummeting temperatures are expected to produce freezing rain, described as "one of the less common forms of winter precipitation". While the likelihood of this occurring is considered "small", the Met Office has nonetheless issued a specific advisory to all over-65s residing within the warning zone.

Experts said: "During icy and cold weather there are some simple ways you can support elderly relatives or neighbours: Check to see if they need any practical help and that they are well.

"Make sure they have enough food supplies in case they cannot go out. Offer to clear their paths when you clear yours and spread salt. You can use ordinary table or dishwasher salt - a tablespoon for each square metre you clear.", reports the Express.

Should the freezing rain materialise, it is anticipated to have a considerable impact on the daily lives of the millions of people living beneath the warning zone. Roads and pathways are set to become treacherous as ice develops across a broad stretch of the nation.

The warning continued: "A band of rain is expected to move slowly east, and could fall as freezing rain for a time down to around 300m.

"Should this occur, it could lead to widespread icy surfaces, with a particular risk for roads and pathways that rise to higher elevations over the Pennines. The rain could also briefly turn to snow before easing and clearing on Friday afternoon, with temporary accumulations of 1-3cm."

Snowfall may also occur on Friday, extending as far south as Northamptonshire.
