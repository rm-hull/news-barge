One of Harrogate’s most historic and successful stops says its key sponsorship of this year’s Harrogate Theatre’s 2026 pantomime is a “really special moment”.

The tie-in between Smiths of Harrogate furniture store and the town’s leading arts hub for the current run of Sleeping Beauty involves 126 years of Harrogate history coming together.

Harrogate Theatre first opened its doors in 1900, while family-owned Smiths of Harrogate was established just six years later in 1906.

Located on Dragon Road, Smiths will be supporting Harrogate Theatre throughout the run of its magical family panto.

It will also be bringing some of the magic of the production into its own showroom, with panto themed Christmas window displays and in-store decorations, created by local artist India Smith.

Oliver Smith, who represents the fifth generation at Smiths of Harrogate, said: “There is something really special about two organisations that have been part of Harrogate for well over a century coming together like this.

“We’ve both seen the town change enormously over the years, but we’ve also both remained very much part of the community.

“The Harrogate pantomime is an important part of Christmas in the town for so many families, including ours, and we’re incredibly pleased to be supporting it this year.”

The partnership will also be celebrated with a special “120 years of Christmas magic” weekend at Smiths from Friday, December 4 to Sunday, December 6:

The sponsorship reflects Smiths commitment to supporting the town in which the family business has traded and grown for so many years.

Oliver Smith said: “Sleeping Beauty couldn’t be a more appropriate pantomime for us.

“Beds have been at the heart of Smiths for generations – although we can’t promise that any of ours will keep you asleep for a hundred years!

“We’re going to have a lot of fun with the theme in our Christmas windows and throughout the showroom.

“India Smith (no relation!) has done a fantastic job, creating a magical display at our store on Dragons Road.

“And we can’t wait to see what mischief Tim Stedman gets up to as he brings the character of Silly Sidney to our beds department.”

Natalie Rawel, Head of Marketing and Development at Harrogate Theatre, said: **“** We’re really excited to be working with Smiths of Harrogate on this year’s pantomime.

“There’s such a lovely connection between the theme of the show and what Smiths do, and it’s been a really fun partnership to be part of.”

Sleeping Beauty runs at Harrogate Theatre from now until January 17, 2027.
