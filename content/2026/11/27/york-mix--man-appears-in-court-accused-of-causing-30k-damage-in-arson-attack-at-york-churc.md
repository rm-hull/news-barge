A man appeared in court today charged with the arson attack on a York church.

Michael Robert Peter Evans, 42, was at York Magistrates’ Court over the fire at Holy Trinity Church on Micklegate on Wednesday.

He has been charged with arson to a large altarpiece, the reredos, to the value of £30,000 belonging to Holy Trinity Church.

Evans, of Lowther Terrace in Holgate, did not enter a plea.

The case has been sent to York Crown Court on 29 December.

Two fire crews were called to Holy Trinity at 12.12pm on Wednesday after fires were spotted inside.

These were put out before firefighters arrived.

A man was arrested in connection with the incident on Blake Street about two hours later.
