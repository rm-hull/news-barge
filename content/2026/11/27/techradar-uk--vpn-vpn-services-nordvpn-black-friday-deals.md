## Is it the best VPN deal this Black Friday?

The short answer is, not necessarily.

If you are looking for the cheapest possible option for a standalone VPN service, Private Internet Access (PIA) is definitely your provider. Its Black Friday offering lowers the price to the equivalent of $1.49 per month for a 28-month-plan, down from $2.19. But be prepared for less impressive speeds.

I also found Windscribe's Black Friday offering a great opportunity to finally say goodbye to the annoyance of price renewal hikes. The Toronto-based provider lets you lock in $29 per year for life (down from $69), which still works out cheaper than NordVPN Basic.

If a wider security suite is what you are really after, I cannot recommend Surfshark One enough. Thanks to its last-minute improved Black Friday deal, you'll get the VPN alongside extra security tools like antivirus, Scam Protection, and dark web alerts, for just the equivalent of $2.29 a month.

Keep in mind that, while Surfshark is planning to improve its anti-scam tools, NordVPN's next-gen capabilities are at the moment superior. So, if scam protection is what you care about most, NordVPN Complete is then the best Black Friday VPN deal for you.
