**Ventrolla’s restoration of sash windows at Canons Ashby House is honoured by the British Woodworking Federation’s Heritage Project of the Year Award 2026.**

Harrogate-based Ventrolla has been recognised for its skilled conservation work, winning the British Woodworking Federation’s (BWF) Heritage Project of the Year Award 2026. The award celebrates the company’s careful restoration of sash windows at Canons Ashby House, a Grade I listed property managed by the National Trust in Northamptonshire.

Sponsored by Impra Wood Protection, the award highlights Ventrolla’s sensitive approach that prioritised repair and restoration over replacement, respecting the original materials of the historic windows. Judges noted the seamless integration of traditional craftsmanship and materials in the project, commending the workmanship that honours the property’s heritage.

> **Steve Thomas, Heritage Sales Manager at Ventrolla, commented on the award:**  
> We are delighted to receive this award for our work at Canons Ashby House. It’s an absolute privilege to work on historic properties. Our team took great pride in carefully repairing and restoring the house’s beautiful sash windows, and it’s so rewarding to see the team’s skills, talent and meticulous attention to detail recognised in this way.

> **Helen Hewitt, Chief Executive of the BWF, added:**  
> Our Awards celebrate the people and businesses whose skills, ideas and dedication are moving woodworking and joinery manufacture forward. As we mark the 18th anniversary of the Awards and the BWF’s 50th anniversary, it is particularly rewarding to come together to recognise and celebrate the very best our sector has to offer.  
> Historic joinery always calls for careful judgment and superb craftsmanship. Ventrolla’s respect for the original materials at Canons Ashby House was central to an approach that prioritised repair and restoration over replacement. This is a deserving win for a team whose skill and care have shone through in the finished work.

The award ceremony was held on Friday 20 November at the Kimpton Fitzroy London hotel. The British Woodworking Federation represents over 500 UK companies in the woodworking and joinery industry, a sector valued at £3.8 billion.

For more details on this year’s winners visit: [https://www.bwf.org.uk/bwf-awards-2026/](https://www.bwf.org.uk/bwf-awards-2026/)
