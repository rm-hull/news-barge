This Black Friday, Icedrive is fighting back by offering a 1TB lifetime storage deal for a one-off payment of £249.

## Why we recommend Icedrive

There’s no confusion or complication when it comes to the biggest attraction here: pay £249 once, you don’t have to mess around with monthly or annual renewals, and you’ll get 1TB of cloud storage space.

If you want a long-term option for your photos, documents, projects, and backups and you don’t want to have to think about it, then that’s a fantastic option.

It’s not just about ease of use, either, because Icedrive takes privacy seriously. Icedrive offers zero-knowledge encryption and uses Twofish encryption systems to protect files before they leave your device. The service also includes file versioning, password-protected sharing links, and handy collaboration tools.
