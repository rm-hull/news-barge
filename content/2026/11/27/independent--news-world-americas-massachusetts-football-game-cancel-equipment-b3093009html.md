A Massachusetts high school had to cancel its Thanksgiving week game because of “contaminated” equipment.

It was not immediately clear what the substance was, but it led Ayer-Shirley Regional High School to cancel its Thanksgiving Eve game just hours before kickoff, according to Boston.com.

School officials were alerted to the problem around 3:30 p.m. Wednesday.

“While this contamination does not pose a present health risk, it would be unsanitary for the players to use the equipment,” Adam Renda said, according to Boston.com. “We immediately made efforts to secure replacement equipment but were unable to do so in time for the game. Without the necessary equipment, our players cannot safely participate.”

Ayer-Shirley was set to play Lunenburg High School on Wednesday evening.

![Ayer-Shirley Regional High School in Massachusetts had to cancel its football game this week over 'contaminated' equipment](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/19/09/Screenshot-2026-11-27-at-2-15-52-PM.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

School officials said they would provide an update on rescheduling the game as needed, but no official date had been set.

“We feel terrible that we are unable to hold tonight’s game,” he added. “Our players have worked hard and were looking forward to taking the field, and we know their families and our community were looking forward to supporting them. We understand how disappointing this news is, especially with such short notice.”

Local police have been notified and are investigating.

*The Independent* has reached out to Ayer-Shirley for comment.
