## Five Yorkshire camping and caravan parks named among UK's best

Castle Howard *(Image: Supplied)*

Five Yorkshire parks have been named among the UK's best following recent site inspections.

The sites, all located in Yorkshire and spanning North, West and South Yorkshire, have been listed in the Premier Parks collection, which highlights top campsites and touring parks across the country.

Castle Howard Lakeside Holiday Park, Golden Square Caravan and Camping Park, York Naburn Lock Caravan and Camping Park, Holme Valley Caravan and Camping Park, and Waleswood Caravan and Camping Park all earned the recognition following site inspections.

Jack Hart, a spokesperson for Premier Parks, said: "These five Yorkshire parks are a really good fit for Premier Parks.

"They have their own characters, strong settings and give people a real reason to stay in the area, which is exactly what we look for when putting the collection together."

Each park showcases different parts of the region.

Castle Howard Lakeside sits on the Castle Howard estate, while Golden Square is located in the North York Moors National Park near Helmsley.

Holme Valley, set on a former mill site, marks its 40th anniversary this year.

Waleswood overlooks Rother Valley Country Park, and York Naburn Lock, an adults-only site beside the River Ouse, has received the AA’s five-star award for 2026.
