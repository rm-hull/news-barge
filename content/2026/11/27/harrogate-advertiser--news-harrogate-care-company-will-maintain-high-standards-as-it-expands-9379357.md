Two successful Harrogate entrepreneurs in the social care at home sector have launched the new Page Care Group as part of ongoing expansion plans.

The new umbrella company is the work of Tom and Kirsty Page and will see not only the uniting of their highly-regarded West Park Care and JC Home Care firms but will also serve as the foundation for future strategic acquisitions across Yorkshire.

The new development follows West Park Care being named one of the Top 20 Home Care Providers in Yorkshire and the Humber for three consecutive years.

Based at 38 Freemans Way in Harrogate, the new Page Care Group executive board has been established which includes Lindsay Austin, who previously held senior roles in Mark's & Spencer and Topshop.

Managing Director Tom Page said the company would retain its complete commitment to providing high standards of care in people’s own homes.

“Page Care Group marks the next stage of our growth,” he said.

“It gives us the structure and expertise to pursue acquisitions and expand organically while keeping outstanding private care in people’s own homes at the heart of the business.”

“We will build on what already works, bring strong care businesses into the group and create lasting value for clients, colleagues, and the communities we serve.

“Growth will be carefully managed, with clear standards, consistent delivery, and continuity of care at every stage.”

The launch of the Page Care Group follows West Park Care’s acquisition in 2024 of Jane Caress, the Masham-based agency operating as JC Home Care, which enabled the expansion of the business’s reach and capacity to meet local demand for high-quality care at home.

The newly-appointed board comprises Lindsay Austin, Martin Cheek, Richard Parker, Ian Pattinson, Billy Graham, and Lee Harris.

Lindsay Austin will act as an independent Non-Executive Director.

She is Group Managing Director of Leeds-based Bayford Group, where she oversees all functions and investments and her broader board responsibilities include serving as Chair of JumpTech, a Non-Executive Director for RAW Charging and E Gas and Power, and she is CEO of Fulcrum Group. Lindsay previously held senior regional and national leadership roles with Marks & Spencer and Topshop.

Independent Non-Executive Director Lindsay Austin said: “Page Care Group gives Tom and Kirsty the structure to turn their growth plans into a disciplined acquisition strategy while protecting the standards that have built West Park Care’s reputation.”

“The board will bring independent challenge and disciplined decision-making to every stage of the group’s growth, from identifying the right opportunities to integrating acquired businesses effectively.”
