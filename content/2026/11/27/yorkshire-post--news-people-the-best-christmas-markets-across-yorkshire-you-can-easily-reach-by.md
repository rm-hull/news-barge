# The best Christmas markets across Yorkshire you can easily reach by train

## Skip the stress of frosted windscreens and limited parking.

December has almost arrived, and Christmas markets are opening across Yorkshire. For a relaxed evening of mulled wine and artisanal stalls, the most practical solution is arriving by train.

From Whitby to Leeds, these are Yorkshire’s easiest Christmas markets to reach by rail.

**Sheffield Christmas Market** Festivities are already in full swing in Sheffield. In 2026, its Christmas market is scheduled from November 12 to December 24. Shoppers can test their skills on the ice rink, ride the Big Wheel, or snack on cinnamon churros while browsing artisanal stalls. From Sheffield Station, the market is just a 10-minute walk.**York Christmas Market**

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/16/21/York-Christmas-Market-2026-Opening-Day-36.jpeg?trim=0,1,0,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Between November 12 and December 21, Parliament Street and St Sampson’s Square will be covered with alpine chalets. In 2026, traders include Filey Bay (Yorkshire’s original whisky distillery), Herbie’s (serving stacked hot chocolates), and various street food, including Irish spice bags. The market is only a 10-minute walk from York Station – a convenient addition to an afternoon along The Shambles.

**The Piece Hall Christmas Market**

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/16/07/DSC_1641.jpeg?trim=156,0,477,4&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


With its columned exterior, The Piece Hall is a stunning backdrop for Christmas festivities in Halifax. From November 13 to December 20, its Christmas market runs every weekend, Friday to Sunday. Browse independent stalls, selling anything from handmade candles to brownies, or catch a ride on the vintage carousel. The market is less than five minutes from Halifax Station.

**Harrogate Christmas Market** From December 4, Harrogate will open its Christmas market on Cambridge Street. Aside from browsing handcrafted goods, visitors can order decadent hot chocolates or steaming mulled wine. The market is only a few minutes from Harrogate Station, and will run each weekend until December 19.**Skipton Christmas Market** Skipton is hosting a special Christmas market on December 6 and December 13. Nicknamed the ‘Gateway to the Yorkshire Dales,’ this market town is celebrating with over 100 stalls, serving festive specials like mulled wine and roasted chestnuts. There are regular trains to Skipton Station, which is a 15-minute walk from the High Street.**Made in Whitby Christmas Market** From December 12 to 13, Made in Whitby Christmas Market will return to Whitby Brewery. Expect festive music and handcrafted goods with a ‘Whitby twist,’ including folklore-inspired candles and jewellery forged from sea glass. Following the famous 199 steps, the market is an approximate 15-minute walk from Whitby Station.**Leeds Christmas Market**

![](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/24/16/44/21-11-26-Leeds-Christmas-Market-(18-of-319).jpeg?trim=1098,1,1680,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


From November 19, Leeds Christmas Market is back. Visitors can browse dozens of ‘alpine chalet’ stalls, with vendors including Stump Cross Caverns, Crumble Pots, The Savvy Baker, and Herbie’s. Skate at the ice rink on Millennium Square, ride the Big Wheel, or order a mulled cider at THOR’s Tipi Bar. The main market is less than a 10-minute walk from Leeds Station and will run until December 23.
