York has been named one of the UK’s most driver-friendly university cities in a new study.

The research, carried out by MoneySuperMarket, compared university locations across the country and gave York a 'Commended' rating with an overall score of 7.12.

The study considered factors such as parking costs, vehicle crime, road safety, fuel prices, and student insurance premiums.

Alicia Hempsted, car insurance expert at MoneySuperMarket, said: "Where a car is kept can influence insurance costs, as insurers consider factors such as traffic levels, claims history, and local risk.

"Students who move away for university and keep their car at a new address for much of the year may need to update their policy details, which could affect their premium."

York recorded the lowest number of vehicle crimes in the study, with just 1,633 incidents in the past year.

The city also had the second lowest average annual insurance premium at £1,064.38, and petrol prices were among the cheapest at 149.93p per litre.

Ms Hempsted: "Students should also consider the full cost of running a car, including parking permits, fuel, and insurance, particularly in cities where parking may be limited or expensive."
