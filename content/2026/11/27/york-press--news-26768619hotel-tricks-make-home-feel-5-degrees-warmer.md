## Simple tips and tricks used by hotels to make your home feel 5 degrees warmer

Hotels use these tricks to keep their rooms warm. *(Image: Getty)*

The rising costs of energy bills have a lot of people searching for ways to heat their homes for less money.

There are a few cheaper ways to heat your home without having to touch your thermostat and turn on your heating.

Experts have revealed the simple tips and tricks hotels use to prevent heat from escaping rooms that you can use to keep your home five degrees warmer.

## How to keep your home warm through winter

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21610038.jpg?type=mds-article-620)These tricks will keep you warm without having to turn up your heating. *(Image: TANYA LEAF/Getty)*

Hotels have been using these hacks for years to keep the heat in their buildings.

Property specialists from Luxury Serviced Apartments have revealed a few tricks that will help stop heat from escaping from your property.

Windows are the main area that allows heat to seep out of your home with hotels coming up with a method to prevent this that you can try.

Hotels make the most of its bedding and curtains to protect their buildings from losing heat.

You should draw your curtains before the sun sets, as heat loss increases through windows when it is dark.

You can pick out thicker curtains to help trap in the heat, but regular curtains will still work.

Hotels also have a technique for layering bedding that helps make it feel warmer even if the temperature of the room has not been increased.

Rather than using a single thicker blanket, hotels use multiple thin layers to help capture warm air between each layer and provide better insulation.

You can recreate this at home by using a lightweight thermal blanket under your duvet and then add a throw made of wool or fleece.

Choosing to use flannel sheets will also make you feel warmer.

If you also struggle with your flooring feeling cold, placing a rug next to your bed will make it feel cosier and also trap heat in much more effectively.

It can also be worth spending some time rearranging your furniture to maximise the circulation of heat in your home.

You should move large items of furniture away from radiators, as they can obstruct the heat flow.
