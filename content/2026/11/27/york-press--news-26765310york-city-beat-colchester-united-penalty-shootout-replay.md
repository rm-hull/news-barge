## On this day: Alan Fettis is York City's shootout hero in last meeting with Colchester

York City's former ground Bootham Crescent *(Image: Archive)*

In today's series of subscriber-only nostalgia deep dives, we look back at a time when a York City beat Saturday opponents Colchester United in dramatic circumstances.

York travel to Essex this Saturday to face Colchester, with over 800 City supporters making the trip down to the Jobserve Community Stadium.

The Minstermen are facing a Colchester side that they haven’t locked horns with in 25 years – which happens to fall on the same weekend that their upcoming trip does.

York last faced the U’s on this day in 2001, in what was an FA Cup replay held at Bootham Crescent.

The previous cup fixture had ended in a goalless draw down in Essex, despite City having the better of the chances.

On a cold November evening in York, 2,014 supporters made up the attendance at Bootham Crescent with almost half of that tally set to visit Colchester this Saturday.

Steve Baines was referee on the night and commanded a City team that boasted some famous names.

Alan Fettis started between the sticks, and was protected by a defence consisting of Darren Edmondson, Matt Hocking, Chris Smith and Neville Stamp.

Ahead of them was Chris Brass, Richard Cooper, Nick Richardson and Graham Potter, who would go on to manage the likes of Brighton, Chelsea and West Ham.

Leading the line together were Lee Nogan and Michael Proctor, whilst City had Alex Mathie, Mark Maley and Lee Bullock all come off the bench.

Colchester didn’t have too many recognisable names in their squad, but modern-day football fans might know the name of Andy Woodman between the sticks – who has recently left Bromley after guiding them to League One football.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21768649.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alan Fettis, pictured during his time at York City, helped the Minstermen to penalty shootout glory in their FA Cup win over Colchester United 25 years ago. *(Image: Archive)*

In a first round replay, York started the stronger of the two teams as Brass swept City ahead after just eight minutes.

They looked to hold onto that 1-0 lead but as the game progressed, Colchester grew into the fixture and late on in the second half they drew level.

An explosive final nine minutes first saw the U’s equalise with Scott McGleish scoring Colchester’s first.

Potter, playing in midfield on the day, then fired City back ahead just three minutes later.

But the Minstermen’s FA Cup progression was delayed as Karl Duguid bagged a stoppage-time leveller that forced extra time at Bootham Crescent.

In the following 30 minutes, there would be no further breakthrough as both teams failed to grab an extra-time winner.

That delivered a dramatic penalty shootout.

Colchester went first, but Fettis came out on top as he denied McGleish from 12 yards.

However, York didn’t show any form of improvement on their visitors as Woodman kept out Maley on City’s first attempt.

Fortunately, Gavin Johnson scuppered his penalty and missed the target for Colchester, as Potter slammed home his spot-kick to give the Minstermen the advantage.

Both Scott Fitzgerald and Brass bagged from each team’s next spot-kick, while Dean Morgan and Richardson also finished from 12 yards.

Fettis just needed to keep out Duguid, the man who was responsible for helping the visitors reach this stage.

And the City icon stepped up, parrying Duguid’s penalty to clinch a shootout win for York in the FA Cup.
