A petition signed by nearly 290,000 Canadians calling on Mark Carney’s government to oust Pete Hoekstra as US ambassador to Canada was formally presented to the House of Commons on Thursday, a sign of the strained relations between the neighboring countries.

Elizabeth May, a Canadian Green party member, originally opened the petition for signatures in July 2026 after a woman from Calgary sponsored it. The petition requests that the Canadian government declare Hoekstra “persona non grata” due to his “US” diplomatic interference in Canadian domestic affairs”.

“He has been repeatedly abusive and insulting to Canadians,” May said as she formally introduced the petition, according to Politico.

She called Donald Trump’s envoy “the most undiplomatic diplomat” Canada had ever seen.

The petition accuses Hoekstra of “undermining” Canada-US diplomatic relations through repeated statements about Canadian politics that its signatories say are “inconsistent with diplomatic protocol”.

The US state department did not immediately return a request for comment.

Hoekstra is a deeply polarizing figure in Canada, particularly for his response to Trump’s desire to annex the country and make Canada the 51st US state.

Speaking to a Michigan radio station last year, he described the president’s rhetoric as a “sign of affection” and questioned why Canadians were offended by what he characterized as a “generous offer”.

Hoekstra also previously described Canada’s 2025 federal election that saw a remarkable come-from-behind victory for the prime minister’s Liberal party as “anti-American”.

The US ambassador had suggested Canada could be blamed if Republicans performed poorly in this month’s midterm elections.

“That’s an interesting way of framing it,” Carney replied, when asked about the comments.

Democrats swept the November elections, including key Senate races in border states such as Michigan and Maine, where the bitter tariff war between Washington and Ottawa has hit residents hard.

Last month Carney said American tariffs hurt both American and Canadian households. He added that auto exports to the US had so far fallen 10%, and US exports to Canada have fallen by more than 20%.

“US tariffs have upended a trade bargain that’s existed for decades,” Carney said. “They’ve raised costs for American families while putting Ontario workers and businesses at a disadvantage and under a cloud of uncertainty.”

Hoekstra had also accused Canada of potential “political interference” in the US election, a claim Carney said held “no merit”. Speaking to reporters in Turkey, the prime minister said he was “disappointed that their envoy sees that as his role, to make these suggestions”.

Even though the Canadian government is required to provide a formal response to the petition presented by May, the petition is nonbinding.

“I think the government will quite likely say, ‘No, we’re not prepared to declare Pete Hoekstra persona non grata and strip him of his diplomatic credentials,’”, May said in an interview with CTV.

Despite this possibility, May expects the petition to signal to Trump that the Canadian government has the support of its constituents, and she hopes the government can use it as leverage against Trump with Democrats holding power in Congress for the second half of Trump’s term.
