## The European Union’s 27 leaders met in Brussels yesterday with the aim of trying to find common ground on finalising the bloc’s budget. But red lines seemed to have deepened between the budget’s main opposing camps, we debrief the summit with our correspondents Angela Skujins and Sasha Vakulina.

## On today’s show

An EU summit specifically convened to find a breakthrough on the European Union's next budget ended in disarray. The 27 heads of state and government, split in opposing camps, refused to abandon their red lines and openly questioned whether a deal could be reached by December. Our **correspondent Angela Skujins** brings us the latest news.

During the summit, EU leaders did found an agreement: over who will be the next European Central Bank president. Spain's Pablo Hernández de Cos, former governor of the Bank of Spain and current head of the Bank for International Settlements, is set to take over from Christine Lagarde in February. We speak with **Francesco Papadia, senior fellow at the economic think tank Bruegel.Hosuk Lee-Makiyama,** director of the European Centre for International Political Economy, explains the impact of Trump's tariffs.**Javier Martínez-Cantó,** Spanish political scientist, brings us the latest news on the Spanish election campaign ahead of Sunday's vote.
