# Yorkshire Post clocktower cleaned up amid future uncertainty

## The iconic Yorkshire Evening Post clocktower has been cleaned up six months after a pledge from developers.

The tower, which has fallen into a state of disrepair, is set to be demolished as part of a new office development if plans are given the green light.

Planning permission is currently being sought to build two new office buildings on the land where the clocktower currently stands in the city.

![The clocktower before and after the clean ](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/27/15/03/MixCollage-27-Nov-2026-03-26-PM-7611.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Developer Urbanite, which is backed by private equity firm Prescient, initially got planning permission to build three multi-storey buildings, one office block and two student flats, in September 2023. But the scheme never came to fruition and the condition of the clocktower worsened.

It led in April to a joint campaign by The Yorkshire Evening Post and sister title The Yorkshire Post calling for a clean-up operation..

And now pictures from the site show the clocktower has been cleaned, more than six months Doug Friend, director of Prescient Capital, said a “cleaning exercise” would take place shortly.

Leeds civic leaders and councillors have called for the clocktower to be saved, after the city council confirmed it could be knocked down if the new plans by Urbanite are approved.

![The tower is set to be demolished as part of a new office development if plans are given the green light](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/27/15/2026_11_27_15_38_57_618_ASH_1E6FAE3560ED4F9DBB440A8FAD6A3603.JPG?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Leeds Civic Trust director Martin Hamilton said: “Our feeling is, if at all possible, it should remain on site.”

Mr Hamilton said options could include moving it elsewhere on site or the tower being integrated into one of the planned buildings.

He said: “I think to lose it completely would be a shame.”

A decision on the planning application is scheduled for next month.
