## Traffic warning - key York junction closed for emergency work

St Helen's Road is closed due to a burst water main *(Image: Kevin Glenton)>*

**A BURST water main has caused ‘traffic chaos’ after it closed a junction with a main road in and out of the city.**

St Helen's Road, off Tadcaster Road, is currently closed after a burst water main – something which has caused queues on roads across the city centre.

As reported by The Press yesterday, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Huntington was first called to St Helen's Road, off Tadcaster Road, at 6.29pm on Wednesday night (November 25).

A service spokesperson said pumps were used by its crews to divert the water away from properties – with Yorkshire Water engineers called to the scene by the service.

A spokesperson for the water company said: “Last night, our colleagues repaired a burst main on St Helens Road.

"Unfortunately, the burst has caused significant consequential damage.

"We are meeting with the York City Council this afternoon to assess the situation and devise a plan and timeline for the reinstatement and eventual reopening of the road.

Later that evening, AA Traffic News reported the road was closed both ways from the A1036 Tadcaster Road to Dringhouses Primary School – a closure which is still in place on Friday morning.

Last night saw traffic gridlocked across York city centre with many buses cancelled or running upwards of 20 minutes behind schedule.

The number 4 bus – which runs down St Helen’s Road – has been diverted whilst repairs are completed.

A spokesperson for First Bus said: “Buses will operate via Tadcaster Road, Moor Lane, Chaloner's Road and Eason View in both directions before returning to the normal route.

“Please allow extra time for your journey while this diversion is in place. We apologise for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience.”
