Skaters glide past monumental **Soviet-era pavilions** and ornate fountains at**Moscow’s largest ice rink**, where a leisurely circuit feels more like a winter promenade than a sporting exercise. Stretching across** more than 20,000 square metres**, the rink can accommodate** almost 5,000 people**at once. Its route follows the exhibition park’s main avenue, passing** Pavilion No. 1**and winding around the** Friendship of Peoples**and** Stone Flower** fountains.

First opened in **2015** and billed as**Europe’s largest artificial ice rink**, the complex covers** some 68,300 square metres**when its surrounding facilities are included. After nightfall, floodlit buildings and fountains transform the setting, accompanied by music drifting across the ice.** Adult admission**starts at** 500 roubles**(** around €4.70**),** skate hire**costs** around 800 roubles**(** around €7.50**), and the season continues until** 28 February**.

Beyond its architectural splendour, the rink has become a **meeting place for generations of Muscovites**. Some return each winter to reconnect with friends, while others enjoy the physical challenge of perfecting their technique.
