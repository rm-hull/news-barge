### When does the Nations Championship Finals 2026 take place?

The inaugural Nations Championship Finals weekend runs from Friday, November 27 to Sunday, November 29.

Sunday will be the Grand Final, France vs South Africa.

The competition itself will take place every two years, avoiding years in which a Rugby World Cup or British & Irish Lions tour takes place.

### How does the Hemisphere Challenge work?

Over the three days of Finals weekend, there will be the concurrent Hemisphere Challenge.

The 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th place finals are all worth one point to whichever of the Northern or South Hemisphere side wins. The Grand Final is worth two points.

The points are totted up and whichever Hemisphere has the most after the six matches is crowned the winner and lifts the Hemisphere Shield.

Any matches level at full-time, except the final, will go to a 10-minute golden-score period. Matches still level will go to a place-kicking contest to decide the winner.

If the final is level at full-time, there will be two 10-minute periods of extra time, followed by a place-kicking shootout if the teams still can't be separated
