# Met Office verdict as 10 areas facing snow on Saturday and Sunday - full list

## Forecasters warning of a wet and windy start as temperatures drop on Saturday and Sunday.

The Met Office has disclosed which regions of the UK could be bracing themselves for snowfall this weekend, as bitter winter conditions are expected to send temperatures plummeting on both Saturday and Sunday.

The latest charts from the Met Office indicate that by 4.30pm on Saturday, snow will blanket much of Scotland, encompassing Glencoe, Fort William, Cairngorms National Park, Fort Augustus, Aviemore, Inverness and Aberfeldy. By 8:45am on Sunday, the maps reveal further grey and white coverage across areas where snowfall is anticipated. Many of the aforementioned locations will once again experience snow, alongside Ullapool, Rannock and Stornoway.

The Met Office has issued its assessment of the weekend's snowfall prospects as freezing conditions sweep across the nation. Forecasters warn of a "wet and windy start to the weekend" as a slow-moving weather front continues to exert its influence into Saturday, bringing challenging conditions for many.

"Rainfall totals could build, particularly over higher ground and south-facing hills in northern England, Wales and the southwest, where isolated totals of 70-80mm are possible," the Met Office stated in its forward-looking forecast. It urged Britons to remain updated with the latest predictions and any warnings issued, while cautioning motorists to exercise care when travelling, reports the Express.

A Met Office forecast for Saturday reads: "A wet day for most, with bands of heavy rain sweeping up through the UK, strong winds over high ground and near coasts, with gales in the northeast. Feeling milder."

The Met Office has issued the following forecast for Sunday to Tuesday: "A brighter day on Sunday, with blustery showers towards the northwest. Wet and windy weather is likely to sweep across the UK through Monday and Tuesday. Turning milder for all."

Full list of areas to see snow:

* Glencoe.
* Fort William.
* Cairngorms National park.
* Fort Augustus.
* Aviemore.
* Inverness.
* Aberfeldy.
* Ullapool.
* Rannock.
