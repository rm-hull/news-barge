The family of a grandmother who was fatally hit by a lorry have said she “will always be our hero” after saving her grandson’s life.

Barbara Barganska, 62, was with her two-year-old grandson in Trowbridge, Wiltshire, when she was fatally injured in the crash just after 10am on Monday.

The two-year-old did not sustain any serious injuries. Wiltshire Police said Ms Barganska was a pedestrian when the lorry collided with her at traffic lights in Stallard Street.

A force spokesperson confirmed that the driver of the HGV, a 58-year-old man from Cardiff, was arrested on suspicion of causing death by careless driving and has since been released under investigation.

In a tribute issued through police on Friday, her family described the grandmother’s “extraordinary act of bravery” in saving the life of her grandson.

“We are heartbroken to be writing this tribute. Barbara, who was killed in Trowbridge on November 23 2026, was our son’s beloved Nana,” they said.

“She was so caring and selfless and would do anything for anyone. Her loss leaves a space in our family that no-one else can fill.”

They continued: “You meant so much to each of us, and we will carry you in our hearts and in the memories we share. We love you more than words can say.

“You saved your grandson’s life, and we will never have the chance to thank you for that extraordinary act of bravery, but we will make sure he knows how deeply you loved him. You will always be our hero.

“We have been overwhelmed by the kindness shown to us, even by people we have never met. Thank you for helping us feel supported at such a painful time.

“In Barbara’s memory, we ask everyone who reads this to do one kind thing, accept an invitation or try an experience they might otherwise have passed up. It does not need to be anything grand.

“A small act of care for someone else, or simply saying yes to an invitation, would be a beautiful way to honour her and the kindness she gave so freely.

“You never know when telling someone you love them might be the last chance you have. You will be forever in our hearts and minds.”
