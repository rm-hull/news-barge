A 34-inch UWQHD (3440 x 1440) 1500R curved VA monitor featuring a 180Hz refresh rate, 1ms (MPRT) response time, 119% sRGB colour coverage, and an ergonomic adjustable stand.

## Why we recommend it

If you're looking to maximise office productivity or need a bigger display for content editing, a 34-inch ultrawide panel effectively does the job of two standard displays without having a distracting bezzle in the middle. Text and image clarity is kept sharp by the 3440 x 1440 (UWQHD) resolution and the 1500R curve keeps the entire screen within your field of vision.

The display's 3000:1 native contrast keeps colour crisp while deepening blacks with excellent visual separation that most budget IPS monitors are lacking, especially in dimmer environments or while viewing high-contrast media.

Connectivity is kept simple through a single DisplayPort 1.4a input alongside two HDMI 2.0b ports, with switching between devices made easy thanks to HDMI CEC Profile Sync. Keep in mind that the 180Hz refresh rate is only available through the DisplayPort input, with the duo HDMI ports capped at 100Hz.
