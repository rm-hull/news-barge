Five fan favourite stars will not be returning for the new series of a popular BBC show, it has been confirmed.

Athena, Comet, Bionic, Phantom and Cyclone will not appear in the fourth series of the revived show, Gladiators.

The reboot, hosted by father-and-son duo Bradley and Barney Walsh, features elite athletes competing in high-octane physical contests against civilian challengers.

## Five Gladiators stars will not be returning for new series

A Gladiators spokesperson said: "We would like to thank our incredible superhuman athletes for their contribution to the success and power of the Gladiators TV show.

"While they will not be appearing in Series 4, they remain part of the wider Gladiators family, and you can currently see them across the remaining shows in the UK Gladiator Arena Tour 2026."

Among those departing is Athena, real name Karenjeet Kaur Bains, a powerlifter and former track and field athlete who has appeared on the show since its first BBC season.

Comet, real name Ella-Mae Rayner, a former elite-level gymnast and fitness model, also joined in season one.

Bionic is the Gladiator persona of Matty Campbell, a bodybuilder and former sprinter and footballer, while Phantom is played by 39-year-old sprinter and Olympic bobsledder Toby Olubi.

Cyclone, known off the arena as Lystus Ebosele, is a 25-year-old Irish powerlifting champion who joined in the second series.

The upcoming fourth season will introduce the show’s first-ever Celebrity Gladiator, with Celebrity Traitors star Joe Marler joining under the name Mauler.

A special celebrity episode will also air, featuring Big Brother presenter and I’m A Celebrity campmate AJ Odudu, TV host Chris Ramsey, social media personality George Clarke and comedian Sara Pascoe.

The new series will include an additional event alongside established favourites like Duel, where contestants face off with Gladiators on elevated podiums and attempt to knock each other off using large padded batons.

In April, another Gladiator, Giant—real name Jamie Bigg—announced he would not return to the show after facing a "choice" that did not align with his personal values.

Mr Bigg said his exit was linked to his intention to publicly acknowledge his relationship with his then-partner, who is a creator on the adult content platform OnlyFans.

Gladiators originally aired on ITV from 1992 to 2000 before being revived by Sky One for a brief run from 2008 to 2009.

The current BBC version launched in 2024 and has since completed three series and two celebrity specials.
