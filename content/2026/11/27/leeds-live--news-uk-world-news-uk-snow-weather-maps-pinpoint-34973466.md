# UK snow weather maps pinpoint date 14 counties could be blanketed

## Weather maps show that 14 counties across the UK are forecast to see a dumping of snow

The Arctic conditions appear set to persist as stark weather charts reveal that 14 counties throughout the UK will shortly experience significant snowfall. Sections of Britain are predicted to encounter snow during the forthcoming days as the bitter, volatile weather continues into the weekend.

Meteorologists indicate that substantial rainfall travelling eastwards today may transform into up to 3cm of snow across higher ground in Scotland and northern England. The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for ice today covering a significant portion of northern England and the Midlands. Taking effect at 4am this morning, it will persist until 2pm this afternoon.

Judging by the most recent WXCharts weather maps, it appears the freezing conditions won't be departing imminently. Weather projections for Saturday 12 December have adopted shades of purple and white, signalling that substantial snow is approaching.

Extensive areas of Scotland are scarcely visible on midday charts, with widespread snowfall anticipated to blanket much of the nation. Argyll and Bute and Stirling appear destined to bear the brunt as snow accumulation could approach 10cm, reports the Express.

A substantial band of snow is anticipated to sweep across the Highlands, with the eastern sections of the region forecast to witness snow depths reaching 5cm, while elsewhere snowfall rates of 0.6mm per hour are projected. Meanwhile, Aberdeenshire, Perth and Kinross and Angus are forecast to experience snow depths of between 0.5cm and 0.7cm.

Across Scotland, the snowy weather is predicted to reach as far south as the Scottish Borders and Dumfries and Galloway regions. Meanwhile, northern English counties are also braced for wintry conditions.

Light snow is anticipated across Cumbria, Northumberland and North Yorkshire. While the remainder of the country won't experience snowfall, millions can expect a soggy day ahead.

The East Midlands and Humberside are set for substantial rainfall, with Lincolnshire facing the most sodden conditions - approximately 1mm of precipitation expected per hour. Rain showers will also affect Norfolk and Suffolk. Much of Wales will experience widespread rainfall, with northern regions bearing the brunt of the heaviest downpours. Southern and western parts of England are forecast the most favourable conditions, with neither precipitation nor snow on the cards.

Meanwhile, the Met Office weather outlook for 11 to 25 December states: "High pressure may continue to be quite dominant across the UK at first, with weather systems weakening or slow moving over or near the UK. Later in December, there may be more influence from frontal systems moving in from the west.

"Strong winds are possible at times, but windy weather overall is less likely than normal for winter. Temperatures look likely to be close to normal overall, but perhaps lower than normal at times in the north and northeast where there is a chance of some snow and ice at times. Milder interludes are perhaps more likely towards the south and southwest."
