Dan Carney, who cofounded Pizza Hut alongside his brother almost seven decades ago, has died at age 95.

His death was announced by Wichita State University, his alma mater, which stated that he died on Nov. 13.

Alongside his younger brother Frank, Carney established the inaugural Pizza Hut location in Wichita, Kansas, back in 1958. According to the university, both were students when they used a $600 loan from their mother to open a single small restaurant.

"The sign out front only had room for eight letters and, just like that, Pizza Hut was born," Pizza Hut's website notes.

Rapid commercial success followed as franchised locations expanded Pizza Hut's reach. The chain introduced its familiar red roof in 1969 and became the world's leading pizza chain by sales in 1971. The company website listed nearly 20,000 locations worldwide as of the end of last year.

![The first Pizza Hut was established in 1958](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/17/2260782984..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The International Pizza Hut Franchise Holder’s Association credited Carney with much of the company's overriding ethos.

"Dan believed in doing things the right way, taking care of guests, looking out for people and giving back to the community. Those values helped build Pizza Hut into the brand it is today, and they are just as important to us now," the association said in a statement sent to The Associated Press. "We’re grateful for what he and Frank started, and we’re proud to carry that legacy forward."

In addition to cofounding the company, Carney devoted much of his life to charitable work and advocacy, serving as board chairman of the Cerebral Palsy Research Foundation for 50 years. The CPRF said it was "deeply saddened" by his passing, calling Carney a "devoted champion" of its mission.

Wichita State noted that Carney remained a loyal supporter of the university for decades, providing scholarship funding for students and helping establish the on-campus Pizza Hut museum.

"Pizza Hut is known worldwide, but Dan’s legacy at this university is just as lasting," Wichita State President Rick Muma said in a statement.

Carney's younger brother and Pizza Hut co-founder, Frank, passed away in 2020.

Pizza Hut is currently based in Texas. Amid increasing marketplace competition, the enterprise has seen multiple owners through the decades.

PepsiCo purchased Pizza Hut in 1977 before spinning off its restaurant arm — which became Yum Brands — in 1997. Just earlier this year, private equity firm LongRange Capital purchased Pizza Hut for around $1.5 billion, excluding its mainland China business.
