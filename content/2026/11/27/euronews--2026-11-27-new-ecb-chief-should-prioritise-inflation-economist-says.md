Spanish economist Pablo Hernández de Cos has been named as the next president of the European Central Bank, replacing Christine Lagarde from February 2027. Leaders of euro zone countries agreed to back him at a summit in Brussels on Thursday. He still needs to be formally appointed after the European Parliament and the bank's top officials give their views

In an interview with Euronews, Francesco Papadia, a senior fellow at the Brussels-based think tank Bruegel, said Hernández de Cos would bring central banking expertise and an ability to build consensus. He said the ECB should keep raising interest rates to bring inflation back under control.
