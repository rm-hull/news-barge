Footage shows clashes between Besiktas supporters and police in the Georgian city of Batumi on Thursday during the Turkish club's Europa League match against Israel's Hapoel Beer-Sheva.

Besiktas fans unfurled a pro-Palestinian banner in the stands. Play was suspended in the 25th minute and resumed 30 minutes later. Georgia's Interior Ministry said 16 people were arrested and three officers were injured.

It accused fans of throwing seats at police. Besiktas said police used force after the banner was displayed, and it filed a complaint with UEFA. Georgia denied the claims.
