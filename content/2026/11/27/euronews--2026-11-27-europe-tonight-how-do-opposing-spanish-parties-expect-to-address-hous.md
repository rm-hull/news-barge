**Felicia Pochhammer & Lucy Davalou**

## Euronews' new weekly evening show, Europe Tonight, welcomed Javier Martínez-Cantó, a Research Fellow at the Spanish National Research Center, to discuss issues such as housing and immigration, which are dominating Spain's upcoming general elections.

Spain is getting ready to vote in snap general elections on Sunday. According to the latest polls, the results could unseat Socialist Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez, and a new right-wing coalition could be installed in Spain, a change that could significantly impact the balance of power in the EU.

Our Euronews presenter Felicia Pochhammer welcomed Javier Martínez-Cantó, a Research Fellow at the Spanish National Research Center, on our new weekly evening show to discuss major campaign issues in Spain, such as how different parties are proposing solutions to issues that have dominated the election campaign, including housing and immigration. As well as, in the case that Pedro Sánchez actually manages to stay in office, what signal would that send out to the rest of the Union?
