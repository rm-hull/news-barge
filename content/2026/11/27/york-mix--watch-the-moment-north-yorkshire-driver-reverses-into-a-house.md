This was the scary moment a driver accidentally reversed their car into a house.

The Range Rover driver loses control of the vehicle and rockets into the bay window of the home opposite, scattering debris everywhere.

Fortunately the driver wasn’t badly hurt and no one in the house was injured.

The collision happened last week and the doorbell video footage, not surprisingly, has gone viral.

The *Daily Mail* has since named the driver as Nicola Dodsworth, with the collision happening outside her home in Ainderby Gardens, Northallerton.

The report says she escaped from the wreckage of the car with little more than a small cut to her cheek.

She told the paper she was “just trying to reverse out” last Friday morning (20 November) when her car went into the home of neighbours Sarah and David Holland.

Ms Dodsworth runs the long-established Cutting It hair and sunbed salon on Northallerton High Street.

She said she was furious that footage from her own doorbell camera had found its way online.

“When I find out who’s leaked it, they’re in trouble,” she reportedly told the *Mail*.

#### Ainderby Gardens, Northallerton

“I don’t even have the video. I only happened to watch it last night because it was all over social media. I didn’t want to watch it.”

Asked whether she could explain how the freak accident occurred, Ms Dodsworth replied: “I don’t know. I can’t remember.”

A North Yorkshire Police spokesman said: “At around 9am on November 20, police in Northallerton were called by the ambulance service to an incident in the Romanby area of the town after a Range Rover collided with a residential property.

“It is believed the vehicle was initially parked before it collided with the nearby home.

“There were no serious injuries reported to the police but the driver was treated at the scene for face and head injuries.

“Structural engineers were called to assess the damage to the property.”
