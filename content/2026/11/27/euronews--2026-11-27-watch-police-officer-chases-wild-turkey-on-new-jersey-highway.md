A police officer chased a wild turkey along a busy highway in New Jersey in an unusual pre-Thanksgiving encounter.

Footage released by New Jersey State Police on 26 November shows an officer pursuing the bird on the Garden State Parkway.

The animal had wandered onto the roadway, prompting the officer to try to steer it to safety. "Happy Thanksgiving," an officer can be heard saying over the radio during the incident.

According to state police, the turkey eventually managed to get off the road unharmed.
