Harrogate Town have confirmed that midfielder Ben Fox has left the club by mutual consent.

Fox joined Town from Northampton Town in February 2025 and made 34 appearances for the club.

The 28-year-old had yet to feature this season.

Town said Fox “leaves with the best wishes of everyone at Harrogate Town AFC”.

Harrogate sit second in the National League with 38 points from 19 games after Wednesday night’s 3-1 home win over Kidderminster Harriers.

They host third-placed Yeovil Town tomorrow (Saturday), with kick-off at 3pm. Three points separate the sides.
