**A special celebratory event at Leeds recognised decades of dedication from bus drivers and staff across northern England, including teams from Harrogate and Keighley.**

Local transport operator Transdev Blazefield marked a significant milestone this November by honouring 26 of its long-serving colleagues in a special “Celebration of Service” event at the Directors Suite of Headingley Stadium in Leeds. The award ceremony recognised a collective 750 years of dedicated public service provided by employees helping to keep Lancashire and Yorkshire moving since 1986.

The invite-only event last week brought together drivers, duty managers, and various support staff from across Transdev’s region, including the Harrogate Bus Company, Keighley Bus Company, and Yorkshire Coastliner. These colleagues – spanning career milestones of 25, 30, 35 and 45 years in the transportation industry – received awards, gifts, and an evening of fine dining and drinks, with their partners also presented with gifts from Betty’s Tea Shop in Harrogate.

Among those honoured for 35 years or more were:

– Martin Bower, driver at Burnley Bus Company (40 years)

– David Minnock, driver at Keighley Bus Company (40 years)

– Anthony Kirby, duty manager at Blackburn Bus Company (35 years)

– Chris Stotan, driver at Blackburn Bus Company (35 years)

– Shaun Sutton, duty manager at Burnley Bus Company (35 years)

– Edward Walters, duty manager at Keighley Bus Company (35 years)

– Timothy Alway, driver at Keighley Bus Company (35 years)

> **Edward Walters:**  
> During Covid, as part of Clap for Carers, I took a bus to the nursing home where my wife worked as a nurse. I also have good memories of driving out to the seaside and the countryside.  
> In terms of changes, the new bus stations and the modern buses. I started on minibuses and moved up to the big buses. The uniform has changed many times, along with the move from cash to contactless and the app, things I would have never imagined.

> **Anthony Kirby:**  
> I would probably tell myself to do everything exactly the same. I have four lovely children, three grandchildren and my partner and best friend Natalia who has shown me what true loyalty and commitment is for nearly 25 years. I would not risk changing anything, and I am proud of my family exactly as they are.  
> I would also say don’t be so hard on yourself, with the many tasks you undertake and standards you set yourself. Life is like a ride on a bus; it is as much about the journey as it is about the destination!

> **Henri Rohard:**  
> We are incredibly proud of all our colleagues. Celebration of Service is a fantastic opportunity to show our gratitude to those who have spent most of their working lives with us. Thank you to everyone who attended and made it an unforgettable evening.

Transdev Blazefield operates over 500 buses across nine bases in northern England, serving communities in Harrogate, Bradford, Keighley, Blackburn, Burnley, and surrounding areas. More than 1,400 employees support an annual 32 million passenger journeys, enabling the daily travel needs of students, families, and commuters.

Further information about Transdev’s services is available online at [https://www.transdevbus.co.uk](https://www.transdevbus.co.uk) and via the ‘Transdev Go’ mobile app. The company is part of the wider Transdev group, which operates in 19 countries and transports over 12 million customers daily.
