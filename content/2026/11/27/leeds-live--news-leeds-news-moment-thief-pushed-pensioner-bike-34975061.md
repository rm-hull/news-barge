# This is the moment thief pushed pensioner from bike causing serious injuries

## Alfie Smith has been jailed and terrifying footage shows exactly why

Terrifying footage shows the moment a prolific bike thief knocked a cyclist from his vehicle before it was taken from him.

The man in his 60s was seriously injured when he was targeted by Alfie Smith and another male as he cycled along a towpath in Leeds.

Paul Laveurt, 68, and John Hoban, 78, were riding their bikes in August this year as part of a cycle club along the canal near Thwaites Lane in the Stourton area of Leeds when they were approached by two men, one of them being Smith.

LeedsLive previously reported details of the attack as Smith was sentenced at Leeds Crown Court.

Prosecutor Syed Khukon told the court at the time: "The defendant and another male appeared on the pathway and moved towards one of the men and rode around him. The other male pulled a balaclava over his face and stood in his way.

"The man tried to get around him and was struck to the chest and fell off his bike. The other man stopped, getting off his bike and helped him. The defendant shouted at the other male, ‘Leave that f\*\*\*\*\*\* bike, get that.’ The defendant tried to punch the man and they took the bike and went off in the direction of Leeds city centre."

One of the men used the bike that was left to chase after the men, to no avail.

Mr Laveurt suffered a broke kneecap and cracked ribs and was taken to hospital for treatment.

It was not the first time Smith had offended, and was to be sentenced for stealing another bike from another cyclist earlier the same month. The man targeted in that crime considered jumping into the River Aire to escape.
