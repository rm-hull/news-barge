10-year-old Tiffany Taylor from Harrogate has won an Unsung Hero Award for helping care for her younger brother and supporting her best friend through leukaemia.

Tiffany, a registered young carer, helps look after her brother, who has nocturnal epilepsy and complex health conditions.

She is often the first to respond when he has a seizure.

When her best friend, who has Down’s syndrome, was diagnosed with leukaemia and began losing her hair, Tiffany decided to donate 11 inches of her own hair to the Little Princess Trust.

She had her hair cut during a three-hour dance-a-thon organised to help her friend’s family cover the cost of frequent hospital trips.

The haircut raised £1,500 for the Little Princess Trust, which uses donated hair to make wigs for children and young people.

Tiffany later joined her family to walk a mile a day for 40 days to raise money for Young Epilepsy.

She was named Unsung Hero at the 32nd Yorkshire Young Achievers Awards last night (Thursday).

*More than 400 guests gathered at Leeds United’s Centenary Pavilion for the ceremony, where Tiffany won the award sponsored by Jamboree Entertainment.*
