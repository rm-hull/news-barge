A man in his 30s has been arrested on suspicion of manslaughter over the death of a man in Knottingley, West Yorkshire Police has said.

Kevin Walkden, aged 58, died after he came into contact with a live, exposed electricity cable while walking in a field near South Moor Lane on Sunday 22 November.

Detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team are investigating how the cable came to be exposed and whether any crimes have been committed.

The man arrested has been bailed pending further enquiries.

Detective Chief Inspector Stacey Atkinson, Senior Investigating Officer, said: “This is a tragic incident in which Mr Walkden has lost his life after coming into contact with a live, exposed electricity cable whilst out walking.”

She added: “At this stage, there is nothing to support Mr Walkden was involved in any attempt to interfere with, steal or damage the cable.”

Police are appealing for anyone who regularly walks in the area, or who was near South Moor Lane in the last four to six weeks, to come forward. They want to hear from anyone who saw people acting suspiciously, handling cables, or using excavation equipment, tools, vehicles or machinery.

Anyone with information is asked to contact police via 101 or use the Live Chat facility here quoting 13260663472.

Information can also be given anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
