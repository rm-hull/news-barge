Harrogate-based Ventrolla has won a national woodworking award for restoring sash windows at a Grade I listed house.

The company won the British Woodworking Federation’s Heritage Project of the Year award for its work at Canons Ashby House, a National Trust property in Northamptonshire.

Judges praised the team for keeping the original materials, saying:

> “It is fantastic to see the seamless integration of old into new with this project.
>
> "The workmanship truly respects the original materials and chooses to restore and repair rather than replace.”

Steve Thomas, heritage sales manager at Ventrolla, said:

> “We are delighted to receive this award for our work at Canons Ashby House.
>
> "It’s an absolute privilege to work on historic properties.
>
> "Our team took great pride in carefully repairing and restoring the house’s beautiful sash windows, and it’s so rewarding to see the team’s skills, talent and meticulous attention to detail recognised in this way.”

*The team received the award at the Kimpton Fitzroy London on Friday 20th November.*
