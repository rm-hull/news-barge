## 'It's a wonderful venue': York Christmas cinema team reveals big plans for city site

Live music at York Museum Gardens - now more events are in the pipeline for the historic York site. Image: Provided

*York Christmas cinema team plans new city events for 2027, reports MAXINE GORDON*

Fresh from the success of York's near sold-out Enchanted Christmas Cinema, the team behind the popular attraction are already looking at how they can bring more unique experiences to the city.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21768642.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Live music at York Museum Gardens - now more events are in the pipeline for the historic York site. Image: Provided

Organisers say strong demand for this year's event has encouraged them to explore a range of new ideas that could see some of York's best-known historic venues used in unexpected ways throughout 2027.

Several screenings have sold out, with around 4,000 tickets snapped up for the Enchanted Christmas Cinema festive experience at Tempest Anderson Hall, Yorkshire Museum, York Museum Gardens in December.

Now, the company behind the event says it wants to build on that momentum by creating more immersive attractions designed to encourage residents and visitors to rediscover some of York's hidden and historic spaces.

🏛️ **Historic venue**- Organisers are exploring more events at** Tempest Anderson Hall**, Yorkshire Museum's little-known 300-seat auditorium.

🎃 **Future plans**- Ideas being considered include** Halloween horror screenings, outdoor cinema events and live performances** at York landmarks.

📅 **Looking ahead**- The team is already planning an expanded programme for** Christmas 2027**, with more screenings and possible new festive attractions.

Speaking exclusively to The Press, Dominic Davies, co-founder of the events company behind Enchanted Christmas Cinema, said the response from audiences had exceeded expectations.

"Our approach is to put on really cool events and bring people out to a venue that perhaps they haven't been to for a while," he said.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21768643.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dominic Davies - Enchanted Christmas Cinema

Among the ideas being explored are outdoor film screenings, themed seasonal events and live performances within York's historic Museum Gardens site.

One proposal would see the soundtrack of Baz Luhrmann's Romeo + Juliet performed by a top-rated choir against the dramatic backdrop of St Mary's Abbey ruins.

The company is also investigating opportunities at Tempest Anderson Hall, the little-known 300-seat auditorium next to Yorkshire Museum.

Described by organisers as a "secret auditorium", the venue has a rich history and was once home to City Screen before the independent cinema moved to its purpose-built premises on Coney Street.

Dominic said Tempest Anderson Hall was discovered while the team was searching for a venue for this year's Christmas cinema.

Initially, organisers had considered using the Hospitium in Museum Gardens before learning more about the historic auditorium.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21768645.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Evenings in York Museum Gardens where more outdoor events could be staged next year. Image: York Museums Trust

That discovery could now play a major role in future plans.

Alongside film screenings, the company is considering a programme of cult classics and horror favourites, particularly around Halloween.

Ideas under discussion include immersive experiences that would make use of both indoor and outdoor spaces, creating themed journeys before audiences even reach their seats.

Research carried out by the company suggests there is strong interest in films such as Scream, 28 Days Later, The Shining, Jaws and The Rocky Horror Picture Show.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/16099868.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jack Nicholson in the iconic 'Here's Johnny' scene from horror movie The Shining. Image: Submitted

Meanwhile, organisers are already preparing for Christmas 2027.

Following this year's success, additional festive screenings are expected to be added, while those who missed out this year may be given an opportunity to secure tickets earlier.

The company is also considering expanding the Christmas experience further, potentially including additional festive attractions such as a Santa's Grotto.

Summer events are also being explored, with plans ranging from outdoor cinema experiences to premium screenings featuring picnic blankets, strawberries and champagne.

"Our goal is to use the venue to the maximum," Dominic said. "It's a wonderful venue."
