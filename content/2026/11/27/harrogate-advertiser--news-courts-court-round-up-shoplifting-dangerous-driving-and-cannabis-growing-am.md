**The following was heard at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on November 19:** Martin Gilham, 56, of Roche Avenue, Harrogate, received a 12-month community order for committing a further offence while subject to a conditional discharge. The discharge was imposed on August 13 for shop theft. Gilham admitted breaching that order. As part of the community order, he will be electronically monitored during an eight-week doorstep curfew which prohibits him leaving his home between 7pm and 7am daily. He was also given a probation requirement which stipulates that he must comply with any instructions of the responsible officer to attend appointments or participate in activities.**The following were heard at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on November 23:**

Oliver Cargill, 36, received a 12-month community order and one-year motoring ban after he admitted producing cannabis and dangerous driving in Harrogate. Cargill grew 20 cannabis plants at his home on Hookstone Chase, Harrogate. He also admitted driving dangerously in Forest Lane, Sherwood Drive and on the A661 on Hookstone Chase on March 12 last year, the same day the cannabis plants were discovered. Along with the 12-month driving ban, Cargill was fined £120 and ordered to pay £85 costs.

Jordan McGuire, 25, of Sandhill Close, Harrogate, was fined for failing to comply with the requirements of a community order. He breached the order, made by Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on September 3, by displaying “unacceptable behaviour” on October 15, failing to attend a planned rehabilitation-activity appointment on October 29 and failing to attend an unpaid-work appointment on November 1. McGuire admitted the breach and was fined £60.

Rhys Sullivan, 35, of Flying Scotsman Way, Knaresborough, admitted breaching a community order by failing to attend rehabilitation-activity appointments on September 25 and October 9. Sullivan wasn’t sentenced for the breach but was instead re-sentenced for the original offences which brought about the community order, which was imposed on July 3 for threatening behaviour towards three named women and a man in Scarborough, causing them harassment, alarm or distress. For those offences, Sullivan was made subject to a new 12-month community order with a probation requirement including further appointments or rehabilitation activities.

**The following was heard at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court on November 24:**

Matthew Yare, 44, of Bower Street, Harrogate, received a 24-week prison sentence following a series of shop thefts in the town in breach of a suspended sentence. Yare pleaded guilty to about 20 different shop thefts which occurred between July and November. The thieving spree began on July 18 when he stole four mixer cans of alcohol from Sainsbury’s Local in Cambridge Street. He went back on the same day and stole another four mixer cans. Only July 21, he stole two bottles of Jack Daniel’s whisky, a bottle of Smirnoff Lemon and Lime and a Smirnoff Tropical Crush from Tesco Express in Harrogate, as well as four packs of meat worth £17.28. Three days later, he stole a four-pack of Mahou beer from Sainsbury’s. On July 27, he stole several items worth £31.60, including three Azera Coffee jars, a kitchen towel and a Crunchy Nut bar from Tesco Express. On October 14, he stole £41 of alcohol. On October 28, he stole meat and alcohol mixer cans worth £17.55 from Sainsbury’s. He then went back to the store on the same day and stole the exact same products. On October 31, he stole a four-pack of Stella lager from the same store. On November 10, he was back at Sainsbury’s where he stole yet more alcohol. As well as the jail sentence, Yare was ordered to pay more than £200 compensation for the shop thefts.
