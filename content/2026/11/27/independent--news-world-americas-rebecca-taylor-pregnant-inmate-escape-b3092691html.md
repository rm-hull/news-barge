A pregnant woman in her second trimester serving time in connection with vehicle thefts escaped by stealing a corrections transport vehicle, New Mexico authorities said.

Rebecca Taylor, 26, fled Wednesday while being transported from a prenatal appointment in Albuquerque back to Western New Mexico Correctional Facility in Grants, authorities said.

Taylor was sentenced to six years in prison after pleading guilty to four counts stemming from a series of vehicle thefts dating to 2024, according to court records. Her other charges include possession of burglary tools, larceny and conspiracy to commit a burglary.

![Inmate Rebecca Taylor, 26, is serving a six-year sentence on various vehicle theft convictions](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/10/04/Rebecca-Taylor.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

She escaped while three correctional officers accompanying her were stopped at a Whataburger.

Taylor was not restrained during the drive because of her pregnancy, said Brittany Roembach, a spokesperson for the department. Taylor climbed from the back seat into the driver’s seat and drove away in the Dodge Journey while an officer was standing near the vehicle, Roembach said.

A second inmate exited the vehicle after Taylor took control and is still in custody.

Police found the vehicle about an hour later, 3 miles (4.8 kilometers) away, but Taylor was not inside. Police found her orange prison jumpsuit and a transport cellphone issued by the Corrections Department in the same area Wednesday afternoon.

The New Mexico Corrections Department is investigating how Taylor escaped, Roembach said.

The spokesperson added that authorities are concerned for the safety of Taylor’s baby and hope that she turns herself in for her own sake.

Several law enforcement agencies searching for Taylor said she has ties to the Houston area, Cleveland, Texas and Albuquerque.

Officials say that investigators have been searching Greyhound buses and Amtrak trains. They are also reviewing her prison phone calls and interviewing inmates.

As a result of the escape, all state prisons in New Mexico were placed on lockdown that extended until 6:00 am Thursday.

They asked anyone who sees her to call 911.
