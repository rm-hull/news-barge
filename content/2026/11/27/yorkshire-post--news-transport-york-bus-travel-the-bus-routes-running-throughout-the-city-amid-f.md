# York bus travel: The bus routes running throughout the city amid First Bus strikes

## Ahead of the First Bus strikes, there will be key bus routes operating in York during industrial action.

First Bus has announced plans to operate key routes across York alongside industrial action taking place on Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29, 2026.

However, some services will be amended, and customers are advised to check their journey before buying their ticket.

![First Bus.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/05/28/11/36/Doncaster-bus-strike-First-Bus-confirmed-that-drivers-voted-overwhelmingly-to-accept-its-pay-offer.jpeg?trim=0,55,0,95&width=640&quality=65)


General manager of First Bus, Steve Moyle, said: “Our message is clear: ‘Get to the Park and Ride and we will get you into the city.

“Our team is doing all we can to keep our customers moving, but there will inevitably be some disruption and inconvenience. We apologise in advance to all our customers and appreciate their patience when travelling with us over the coming weekend.”

**Park and Ride**

* The York Park and Ride network will operate its normal weekend frequency but with hours restricted to 8am-7pm.
* Sites will remain open until 11pm for users who return later to collect vehicles.
* Local bus company Connexions will provide the number 3 Park and Ride service at Askham Bar.
* First Bus staff will be available at all Park and Ride sites to support customers and assist with travel planning.

**City services**

* Services 10/10A/10L will be replaced with a shuttle operating between Nether Poppleton and Poppleton Park and Ride for connections into the city centre.
* York Pullman will provide buses for the number 4 and university services into the city centre and York train station.
* Service 1 will continue but with reduced frequency and operating hours 9am-7pm.
* Services 5/5A, 6 and 11A will continue but with reduced frequencies and operating hours 8am-7pm.

First Bus advises that anyone looking to buy a ticket for a day or longer period should purchase the ‘All-York’ mult-operator ticket, which allows travel on any bus operator in the city.

Customers are encouraged to check beforehand if a service is operating as First Bus tickets purchased on a strike day will not be refunded.

Customers with pre-purchased tickets valid for strike dates will be issued with replacement tickets for future travel. Customers who would prefer a refund will be provided with details of how to apply once the period of industrial action has concluded.
