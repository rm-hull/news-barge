Police are linking two shop thefts in York and Selby.

They have now released CCTV pictures as part of their investigation.

In the first theft, on Wednesday 14 October, two men entered the Sainsbury’s store in The Village, Haxby, and stole about £550 worth of stock.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-haxby-and-selby-shop-thefts-oct-2026-2.webp)

The following day, Thursday 15 October, two men entered the Selby Sainsbury’s store on Portholme Road and stole about £450 worth of stock

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the men pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”
