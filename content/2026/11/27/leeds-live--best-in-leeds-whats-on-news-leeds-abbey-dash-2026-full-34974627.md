# Leeds Abbey Dash 2026 full road closures list and parking suspensions

## Leeds Abbey Dash 2026 will take place on Sunday, November 29

Leeds Abbey Dash 2026 is going to be a massive event.

It is expected that around 16,000 participants will form the festivities on Sunday, November 29. Leeds Abbey Dash, starting and finishing in Leeds city centre, will take runners out to the historic Kirkstall Abbey as part of its route.

The event will welcome runners of all abilities. To ensure the safety of participants, spectators and the wider public, event organisers, Jane Tomlinson's Run For All, are advising residents and visitors that a number of roads across the city will be closed or subject to traffic restrictions on race day.

Tristan Batley-Kyle, operations director at Run For All, said: "The safety of participants, spectators and everyone attending the Leeds Abbey Dash is our top priority. As with any major road race, a number of temporary road closures are required to ensure the event can take place safely.

"We would like to thank residents, businesses and visitors for their patience and understanding while these restrictions are in place. We apologise for any inconvenience caused and will work to reopen roads as quickly as possible once it is safe to do so."
