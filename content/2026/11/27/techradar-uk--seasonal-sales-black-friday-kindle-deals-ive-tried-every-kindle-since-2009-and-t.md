There's nothing in the digital world like reading a hard-cover book, unless, of course, you're reading on an Amazon Kindle e-reader. Reflective e-ink displays have delivered book-like experiences to avid readers for almost 20 years. Another thing as dependable as that E Ink experience is Kindle Black Friday sales. You can get started on your near-perfect digital reading experience (or gift the readers in your life) with the standard Kindle (2024) for just $119.99 (was $149.99), while those in the UK can get the latest Kindle for £119.99 (was £149.99).

That unvarnished experience appeals to many, including me, because of the 300ppi sharp display, lightweight frame, and weeks-long battery life. You can, though, level up with bigger or even more colorful models.

Adding color and pen support, the powerful and big (11-inches!) Kindle Scribe Colorsoft is on sale for just $449.99 (was $629.99) at Amazon in the US, while in the UK, the same model is down to £399.99 (was £569.99).

You can also dial it back a bit and get the original Colorsoft (2024), which is now on sale for **$169.99**(was ~~$289.99)~~ in the US and**£144.99 (was** ~~£259.99~~) in the UK.

I know, 2024 may sound ancient in tech terms, but E Ink technology has long developed at a slower pace, and Kindle models from 2024 are just as recommendable as fresh (and not on sale) models from 2026; I especially like the on-sale and waterproof 2024 Paperwhite edition.

Heck, Kindles have been good and dependable for almost two decades. Even my Kindle 2 (circa 2009) still functions — though the battery no longer holds a charge. I've honestly loved every Kindle ereader I've owned and reviewed and can't imagine reading for pleasure without one.

You'll find these deals and more below. Spin your scroll wheel, check out the Black Friday deals, and get ready to give the gift of reading to yourself or someone you know.
