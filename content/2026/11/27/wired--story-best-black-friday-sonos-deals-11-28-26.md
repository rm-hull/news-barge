## Sonos Headphones Deals

The new Sonos Ace Ultra headphones are not on sale, but the capable originals are. The Sonos Ace don't connect or sync to other Sonos products like the brand's speakers do, but they are capable stand-alone headphones, with clear sound, a comfortable fit, and a surprisingly good transparency mode.
