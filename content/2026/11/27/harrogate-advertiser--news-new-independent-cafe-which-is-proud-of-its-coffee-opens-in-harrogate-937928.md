Another week, another exciting new cafe and bakery in Harrogate.

The latest edition to what is becoming a crowded market place in this sector is called Meraki Artisan and it’s an independent artisan bakery and cafe and sandwich takeaway.

This independent business takes over a spot which has been empty since November last year when Decoris, a preloved goods shop, closed down after 18 months.

Located just a little off the beaten track from Harrogate town centre at 26 Mayfield Grove, though it isn’t really far at all, Meraki Artisan is open for business now and offers artisan and traditional handmade breads, baguettes, club and sub sandwiches, and specialty coffee.

If anything, the new bakery and cafe is most proud of its bread and its coffee, even posting on Facebook that it offers: “Traditional handmade breads and ,in our opinion, the best coffee you'll have ever tasted"

Coffee and bakery fans will agree that the arrival of Meraki Artisan Baker adds more strength to the independent bakery sector in Harrogate which also includes Bakeri Baltzersens and The Secret Bakery.
