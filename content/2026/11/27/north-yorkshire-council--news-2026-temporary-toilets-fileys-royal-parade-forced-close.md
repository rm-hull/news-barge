Temporary toilets in a popular North Yorkshire coastal town have closed due to an urgent issue with the site’s sewage pumping system.

The facilities were introduced in March this year at Royal Parade in Filey after serious structural issues were identified at the existing toilet block.

The pumping station supplying the temporary units is owned by Yorkshire Water and has had operational problems in recent months, resulting in regular call-outs.

Representatives from Yorkshire Water visited the site this week and advised that repairs could not currently be undertaken due to safety concerns about access to the existing building.

We are now working to try and put a fix in place to reinstate the temporary facilities as quickly as possible.

Council leader, Cllr Carl Les, said: “We know that this will be disappointing news for residents, businesses and visitors in Filey and understand how important public toilets are.

“Our focus is now on finding the best temporary solution possible. Despite ongoing issues with the pumping station, we worked hard to keep the facilities open throughout the main tourism season and are now doing everything we can to reinstate the temporary toilets as soon as possible.

“At the same time, we are continuing to progress our long-term plan to provide upgraded, modern facilities that enhance Filey as one of our most popular coastal towns.”
