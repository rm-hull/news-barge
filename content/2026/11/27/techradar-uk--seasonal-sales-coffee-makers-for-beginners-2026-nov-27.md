If you want to brew your coffee with fresh beans but you find the idea of using a manual espresso machine a bit daunting, the Baristina is the machine for you — and I've been heartily recommending it to anyone who'll listen since I tested it in February this year.

This is a bean-to-cup coffee machine, meaning it does all the work for you, but it's a lot more affordable than most, and because there's no case covering everything up, it's much easier to clean. Just push the portafilter handle into place and slide it to the right, hit the brew button, and the Baristina will grind the ideal dose of coffee into the filter basket, tamp it down, then slide it over to the left automatically and brew your espresso using perfectly heated water at the optimum pressure.

I've become quite a coffee snob during my time as TechRadar's Homes Editor, and I was seriously impressed by the Baristina's performance. The only catch is that you can't adjust the grind size, so you may have to experiment with a few different coffees until you find one that works well with the machine. I was lucky enough that my usual beans (Honduran Swiss Water Decaf from Wogan Coffee, if you're curious) were ideal.

Lavazza's Tabli machine, which launched a few months ago, promises the convenience of coffee pods without the plastic and metal waste — and after thorough testing, I'm happy to report that it delivers. It uses pucks of compressed ground coffee with no individual packaging, which you insert into the top of the machine before selecting your brew size (single or double). The used coffee drops into a bin in the bottom of the machine, which you can empty directly into your household food recycling or compost heap. Easy.

Most importantly, the coffee tastes delicious. In fact, I used three full boxes of tabs while testing the machine, and found myself using it rather than my usual espresso machine.

You can control the Lavazza Tabli through a mobile app if you like, and it has some smart home connectivity features, but in my experience it works best as a no-frills espresso machine that's beginner-friendly, affordable, and really delivers on flavor.

For the slightly braver home barista, the De'Longhi Dedica Duo is a superb little manual espresso machine that will have you brewing like a pro in no time — and is surprisingly affordable. Usually, espresso machines in this price bracket are pretty poor quality, but De'Longhi has a reputation to maintain, and although the little Dedica Duo is cheap, it's just as well-made as the brand's full-size coffee makers.

That means it maintains steady heat and pressure, so you get consistent results with every brew. Once you've found the right grind size for your beans, you will be able to make great-tasting coffee every time with this little wonder. Its steam wand is excellent as well, and every component from the tamper to the milk pitcher feels reassuringly robust.

The 'Duo' in the name refers to the fact that this little machine also offers cold extraction, bypassing the boiler and making espresso using water directly from the tank. It takes a little longer than brewing hot, but the result is a smooth, rounded shot that's a great foundation for an iced latte or an espresso martini. Cheers!
