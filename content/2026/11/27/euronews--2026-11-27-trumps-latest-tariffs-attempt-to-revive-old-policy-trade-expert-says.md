US President Donald Trump has announced a fresh round of tariffs. The import taxes are set to hit some of America’s biggest trading partners, including the European Union, China and Japan.

Hosuk Lee-Makiyama, a Brussels-based trade economist and director of the European Centre for International Political Economy, told Euronews the measures were an attempt to bring back earlier tariffs under a different law. "We are not looking at a new shock here," he said.
