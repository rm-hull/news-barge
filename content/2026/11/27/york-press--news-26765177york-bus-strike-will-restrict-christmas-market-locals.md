## York bus strike will 'give tourists free run' of Christmas Market

How thoughtful of the York bus drivers and their plans to strike on November 28 and 29.

With the York Christmas Market closed to tourists on Tuesdays, the bus strike will restrict the access to locals and effectively give the tourists a free run over the weekend.

And, of course, the traders will benefit, no whingeing moaning locals complaining of the crowds and overpriced goods on sale. Well thought out York bus drivers.

## York Disability Week has something for everyone

It was great to see your November 24 full page coverage of ‘Art gives a voice to York disability stories’ but its focus on what’s happening at Explore library meant the many, many other mostly free events right across the city and online didn’t get covered or tell people where to find out more.

There are a great range of talks, workshops, a virtual pub quiz, taster events in sport, music and exercise, a chance to try out mobility equipment or find out how to improve your balance, learn about using direct payments for social care or digital exclusion, join a support group, and much more.

There’s also a big Information Fair on December 1 at St Sampson’s Centre.

Something for everyone!

Printed programme copies are in all libraries and community centres and elsewhere and online copies are at www.yorkdisabilityweek.org.

## What happened to same-day appointments?

As each decade passes, whatever Government is in office claim the NHS is more efficient than previously.

I wish to contradict that claim, particularly at local level, years past one could ring to see a doctor and be given a same day appointment, not in 3 weeks as now.

Also you could request a home visit which to my knowledge is no longer available.
