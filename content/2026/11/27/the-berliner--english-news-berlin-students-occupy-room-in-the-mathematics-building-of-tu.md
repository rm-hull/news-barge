Students at the Technical University of Berlin (TU) are protesting against planned budget cuts and the dilapidated state of university buildings. They occupied an unused room in the mathematics building on Thursday evening. The students intend to renovate the partially empty building themselves. Plans include repairing tables and chairs and reconnecting the lights to the electricity supply.

Before the action, students held an assembly in the building to discuss the poor condition of university buildings. The assembly participants decided to join the occupiers. At their general assembly, the students decided to establish a committee to coordinate the protest against the planned budget cuts. They are also demanding more funding for the university from the Berlin Senate. Furthermore, they are calling on the university’s executive board to commit to a civilian clause that would prohibit military research at the TU.

The general assembly also expressed its solidarity with the occupation of previously unused rooms in the mathematics building. The occupation aims to renovate and make the vacant rooms usable again. The participants refer to the action as a “repair occupation”.

According to one participant, the students plan to stay on site for several days. However, they do not intend to prevent anyone from entering or leaving the building during that time.

The mathematics building is one of the largest buildings at the TU (Technical University). It is located on the main campus on Straße des 17. Juni in Charlottenburg. Several buildings at the TU have been closed due to serious deficiencies. This also affects the university’s main building, it has been closed since May.
