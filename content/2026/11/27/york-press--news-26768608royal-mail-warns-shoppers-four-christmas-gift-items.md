As we get closer to the festive period, you might be thinking of getting some shopping done and sending off presents to friends and family.

If you are sending a Christmas present using Royal Mail this year, you might want to be careful and check what items you can and can’t post.

Royal Mail has a range of items that cannot be sent via its delivery service, so it is worth buying any of these things as gifts.

Royal Mail has revealed four items that are commonly sent as presents that you cannot be posting to people over the Christmas period.

## Four items you cannot deliver using Royal Mail

On its website, Royal Mail said: “To comply with national and international regulations governing the carriage of mail, and ensure that mail in transport does not present a danger to the general public, we restrict or prohibit certain items from our network.

“We want to ensure the mail is safe for everyone.

“Many everyday items, such as aerosols, nail varnish and perfumes are considered dangerous goods under transport legislation.

“You are responsible for checking whether or not an item is prohibited or restricted.”

### **Christmas crackers**

Christmas crackers are one of the items Royal Mail says you cannot send unless the snap or banger has been taken out of them.

This includes Christmas crackers that are already made and Christmas cracker making kits.

### **Nail polish**

Any nail polish, gels or varnish that you post must not exceed 30ml.

You also cannot post more than four nail polishes in one parcel.

### **Perfume**

Any perfume bottles that are bigger than 150ml cannot be sent and you cannot send more than four at a time.

If you are sending a small bottle of perfume, it must be sent in original retail packaging and be cushioned to prevent it from breaking.

### **Power banks**

Royal Mail will not deliver any power banks, as they are classed as standalone lithium batteries.

Lithium batteries can only be sent if they are contained within equipment.

A full list of all of the items Royal Mail cannot post can be found on its website that is linked above.

### **Royal Mail's last posting dates for Christmas 2026**

The company's last posting dates for UK inland services are:

* Friday, December 18: Second Class and Second Class Signed For.
* Saturday, December 19: Royal Mail Tracked 48.
* Monday, December 21: First Class, First Class Signed For and Royal Mail Tracked 24.
* Wednesday, December 23: Special Delivery Guaranteed.

Saturday, December 19 is also the last posting date for Royal Mail Tracked Returns 48 to arrive back with the sender before Christmas.

The Post Office says parcels must be received before a branch's final acceptance time, which varies between branches, and advises customers to check before posting.
