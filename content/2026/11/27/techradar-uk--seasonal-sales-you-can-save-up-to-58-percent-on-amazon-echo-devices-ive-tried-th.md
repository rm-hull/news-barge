Amazon's Black Friday deals are well underway, and if you're stuck for a gadget or two to round off your shopping basket, consider an Echo speaker. I've tried every gadget on this list, and while I think there are a few things Amazon could learn from its major smart home competitors, there's a lot it gets right; in particular, its approach to pricing.

Right now, last year's Echo Show 8 is $99.99 (was $199.99) at Amazon US or £99.99 (was £199.99) at Amazon UK, for example; a solid $100 / £100 discount. This is a prime example of the advice I gave to Amazon Echo shoppers last year coming into fruition; in the first year of a product's lifespan, the deals are mediocre at best. Give it a year and they quadruple; though that logic flies out of the window with some newer devices on this list like the Amazon Echo Time, which was $199.99 now $99.99, while in the UK it's dropped from £184.99 to £94.99.
