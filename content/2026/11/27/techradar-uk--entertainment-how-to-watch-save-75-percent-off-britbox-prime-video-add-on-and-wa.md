If you're after something cozy to watch this holiday season, *The Other Bennet Sister*should be top of your list. The BBC period drama boasts an impressive 8.3 rating on IMDb, and puts a fresh spin on* Pride and Prejudice* by centering overlooked middle sister Mary Bennet (Ella Bruccoleri).

The series follows her from her family home to Regency London and the Lake District in search of independence, love, and self-acceptance.

All 10 episodes are free to watch on BBC iPlayer in the UK, while viewers in the US and Canada can stream the series on BritBox – home to the very best of British TV, from period dramas to gripping crime thrillers.

The best part? BritBox is part of Prime Video's Black Friday add-on sale, dropping to **just $2.75 per month for two months– that's 75% off** its usual $10.99.
