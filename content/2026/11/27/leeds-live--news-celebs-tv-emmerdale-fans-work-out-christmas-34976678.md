# Emmerdale fans 'work out' Christmas death as legend 'killed off' 30 years after debut

## Steve Marchant has only just returned to the ITV soap but has vanished again

Emmerdale viewers are speculating that Steve Marchant may have met his demise - merely a week after his comeback to the ITV programme. The dodgy entrepreneur, portrayed by Paul Opacic, first graced the soap between 1996 and 1999, during which time he was wedded to Kim Tate (Claire King).

His unexpected return to the Yorkshire village this week saw Kim attempting to persuade him that DS Thea Ramsden was their long-lost daughter together.

Steve, however, knew this couldn't possibly be true, revealing his infertility, though Kim continued to allow Thea to believe he was her biological father. It later emerged that Graham Foster, who made a shocking return from the dead earlier this year, is actually her dad, prompting Kim to attempt bribing her former spouse with a substantial sum of money.

Before she could complete the transaction, viewers witnessed Graham pushing Steve to the floor earlier in the week, with his condition left uncertain.

Graham simply informed Kim that he had "dealt with" Steve, leaving it at that. During Friday evening's instalment, Thea challenged her biological mother following the abrupt silence from the man she believed to be her father.

Attempting to mislead her, Kim remarked: "Knowing Steve, it's about money. Or lack of. He's probably hoping for a pay-out. He's a criminal. Well, he was. So trust me when I say that you're better off without him."

Subsequently, Thea was spotted chatting to Serena Sugden at the pub after thinking she'd worked it all out, concluding that Kim's stepson Joe Tate had bribed Steve to vanish. She declared: "If he offered Steve a load of money to leave, of course he's gonna take it. He's paid him off to disappear."

Several viewers are speculating that Steve may have actually met his demise, with the reality set to emerge later on - most probably through another flashback sequence.

A fan commented on Reddit: "We never saw his conversation with Steve, and only have his [Graham's]word Steve is fine. We don't know whether Steve is even alive. I'm wondering whether it'll be shown in a flashback later on, like Steve blabbed."

Meanwhile, others believe Steve is still alive and will make a dramatic Christmas return to expose the truth about Thea's biological father. One viewer predicted: "Immediately puts money on Steve coming back on #ChristmasDay just to tell everyone about Graham being Thea's father! #Emmerdale."

Another commented: "Am I the only one disappointed that Steve ain't the Dad. I'm delighted he's back after all these years and wanted him to be the Dad."

A third posted: "I feel there's quite a lot more to come, not necessarily with paternity but battle lines have been drawn!".
