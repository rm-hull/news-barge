## Science labs at Askham Bryan College to undergo £350,000 upgrade

Askham Bryan College and University Centre Askham Bryan has secured more than £350,000 to upgrade its science centre laboratories *(Image: Darren Casey)*

A college has secured more than £350,000 to upgrade its science centre laboratories.

Askham Bryan College and University Centre Askham Bryan received the capital funding from the Office for Students (OfS).

The investment will support the refurbishment of two laboratories used for teaching agriculture, animal biosciences, ecology, and related land-based subjects.

Mark Gent, director of estates and facilities at Askham Bryan College, said: "This investment represents a significant enhancement to our scientific teaching facilities and demonstrates our ongoing commitment to providing first-class learning environments for students.

"The refurbishment will enable us to create modern, flexible laboratory spaces equipped to support current and future curriculum delivery while ensuring the facilities remain fit for purpose for many years to come."

The funding forms part of a wider £84.5 million capital investment programme by the OfS, designed to modernise specialist teaching facilities across England.

Libby Clements, head of land-based higher education at University Centre Askham Bryan, said: "The upgraded laboratories will have a hugely positive impact on the student experience.

"Access to modern facilities and specialist equipment allows students to develop the practical and analytical skills that employers increasingly expect, while also supporting the scientific research and investigation that underpin sustainable land management, ecology and animal biosciences."
