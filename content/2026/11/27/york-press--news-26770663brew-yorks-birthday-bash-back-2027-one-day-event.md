## Major change announced for Brew York Birthday Bash in 2027

Always a popular event, well worth attending. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A major change has been announced for Brew York’s annual Brewery Bash when it returns in 2027.

The popular event will take place at the Handley Tap in Osbaldwick, York, on Saturday, April 17.

It marks the brewery’s eleventh edition of the festival-style celebration, but there will only be one day of fun and beer instead of the usual two.

Wayne Smith, co-founder and managing director of Brew York, said: "We're incredibly excited to welcome the community back to Brew York for our eleventh Brewery Bash.

"It promises to be a fantastic celebration of great beer, good company and everything that makes Brew York so special."

This year’s event will run for one day instead of the usual two, but organisers promise it will be packed with activities and experiences for all ages.

The festivities will be split into two sessions, afternoon and evening, with the option of a full-day ticket for those who want to enjoy the entire programme.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21774789.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Co founders (L-R) Lee Grabham and Wayne Smith *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Guests can expect a wide range of craft beers, including Brew York favourites, special releases, and rare brews from guest breweries.

There will also be guided tours of the brewery, which are not usually open to the public, giving visitors a behind-the-scenes look at the brewing process.

Food will be a big part of the event, with street vendors offering a variety of options to suit all tastes.

There will also be family-friendly games and activities to keep younger guests entertained throughout the day.

Lee Grabham, co-founder and production director, said: "We're thrilled to be bringing everyone together once again to celebrate everything we love about beer and brewing.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21774800.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An excellent range of beers will be available, not just from Brew York. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

"I feel like we've just finished putting things back in place after our 10th birthday, and the next celebration is coming round already.

"Time certainly flies when you're enjoying it and that's exactly what this event is about; enjoying what we've collectively created and celebrating another fantastic year."

Brew York was founded in 2016 by Mr Smith and Mr Grabham, and the Brewery Bash has since become a highlight on the calendar for craft beer fans.

Super Early Bird tickets are now on sale at £12.50 for adults, £4 for children, and free entry for under-4s.

Tickets and more information are available at brewyork.co.uk/11th-brewery-bash.
