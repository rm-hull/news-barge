Robin Hood’s Bay is preparing to welcome a new festive tradition this December.

The picturesque North Yorkshire village is launching a magical month-long Advent Festival, combining three themed weekends of festive fun.

The new festive festival is building on the legacy of the much-loved Victorian Weekend, which came to an end in 2025 after 30 successful years.

The Advent Festival marks an exciting new chapter, bringing together local businesses, residents and community groups for an exciting mix of new events, live music, entertainment, market stalls and seasonal experiences.

The celebrations begin with the **New Victorian Weekend from 5-6 December**, followed by a** Pirates and Smugglers Weekend on 12-13 December**and will culminate with a** Family Weekend on 19-20 December**.

With more than 100 events taking place throughout December, there is something for all ages to enjoy.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/festival-singers-2048x1431.jpg)

Highlights include Smugglers Guided Walks, Crafts and Cocktails, Craft Coffee Morning, Country Crafts and Produce Fayre, Christmas Movie Nights, Karaoke and Disco, Community Carol Service, Nativity in the Barn, Vineyard Tour and Wine Tasting, Outdoor Cluedo and a Victoria Murder Mystery.

Brian Allanson, event promoter, explained: “Inspired by the success and heritage of the Victorian Weekend, we decided to create a new festival and spread the celebrations across December.

“The introduction of themed weekends reflects the village’s history, character and community spirit.

“It will add a new dimension to the village’s Christmas celebrations, and we hope visitors and locals alike will join in, enjoy the festivities and help make it something special for years to come.”
