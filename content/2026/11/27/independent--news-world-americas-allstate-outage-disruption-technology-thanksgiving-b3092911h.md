Insurance firm Allstate has become the latest U.S. company to be hit by a tech outage.

​The organization began experiencing service issues this week, with customers nationwide unable to file claims, pay bills or review policy information.

​Disruption to tech services in the United States has become international news in recent weeks, after Microsoft, JPMorgan Chase, and Apple were all affected by unrelated outages. The outages come as fears of AI and tech continue to mount.

​Reports of disruption to Allstate’s website and mobile app first appeared Monday morning, with customers reporting concerns on the website Downdetector, according to a Newsweek review of the page. But problems have continued throughout the holiday week.

​“We’re just taking inbound calls,” an Allstate employee told *Gizmodo* earlier this week. “We can process payments and make limited changes. But we can’t access the actual policies.”

![Allstate has become the latest firm to experience tech outages after Microsoft, Apple and JPMorgan Chase also experienced disruption](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/16/05/https___www-allstatenewsroom-com_wp-content_uploads_2023_04_Allstate_OutsideAllstateSign.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​A spokesperson for the company - which insures 8 million homeowners and 26 million drivers, according to a review of securities filings by *The Wall Street Journal* - acknowledged the disruption in a Wednesday statement.

​“Allstate customers can reach us in many ways, including online, by phone and through local offices,” the statement reads. “Some access has been restricted over the last 48 hours, but we continue to be available for customers and respond to and settle claims.

​“To the extent service interruptions impact a customer’s ability to make a payment, they won’t lose coverage or be charged late fees.”

​The disruption has coincided with thousands of Americans having their travel plans thrown into chaos by severe weather affecting much of the country throughout the Thanksgiving weekend. People on both coasts have battled king tides, while the East Coast has been battered by a powerful storm.

![The outage comes as thousands of Americans have had their Thanksgiving travel plans thrown into chaos by severe weather. Pictured: Flooding in Rutherford, New Jersey, on November 26](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/08/2026-11-26T201044Z_1823814425_RC2WVOAF3R29_RTRMADP_3_USA-WEATHER.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​The outage has also unfolded amid widespread anxiety over recent technological disruption affecting major U.S. firms.

​Earlier in November, Microsoft was hit with a global outage that affected services including Copilot and Outlook. The company blamed the incident on a “technical issue.”

​A day later, a rare nationwide ground stop on U.S. flights was issued due to what the Federal Aviation Administration described as a “technical problem.” That issue was blamed on a router and an aging air traffic control system. However, thousands of fliers were left stranded as planes were grounded for hours.

​In the days that followed, JPMorgan Chase, Apple, United Airlines and Delta Air Lines all experienced service issues.

​*The Independent* has contacted Allstate for further comment on the outage.
