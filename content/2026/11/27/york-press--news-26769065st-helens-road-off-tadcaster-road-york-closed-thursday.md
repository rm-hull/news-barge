A KEY junction in York is set to be closed until next week after a burst water main.

St Helen's Road, off Tadcaster Road, is not expected to be reopened until next Thursday according to the latest update from Yorkshire Water.

**As reported by The Press yesterday**, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Huntington was first called to St Helen's Road, off Tadcaster Road, at 6.29pm on Wednesday night (November 25).

At the time, pumps were used by its crews to divert the water away from properties – with engineers called to the scene by the service.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: “On Wednesday evening (25 November), our colleagues repaired a burst main on St Helens Road.

“Unfortunately, the burst caused significant consequential damage, including damage to carriageway, which means that we need to excavate the road further before we can begin reinstatement, resurfacing, and relaying the kerb.

”As a result, we are currently working towards reopening the road, which is currently closed for the safety of colleagues and road users, on Thursday 3 December. There will be a signed diversion in place.

“We apologise for the inconvenience and thank everyone in advance for their patience and understanding.

The number 4 bus – which runs down St Helen’s Road – has been diverted whilst repairs are completed.

A spokesperson for First Bus said: “Buses will operate via Tadcaster Road, Moor Lane, Chaloner's Road and Eason View in both directions before returning to the normal route.

“Please allow extra time for your journey while this diversion is in place. We apologise for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience.”
