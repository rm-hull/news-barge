A burglar has struck at a popular York hotel and bar.

North Yorkshire Police said a man stole employees' personal belongings after entering the staff room at The Judge's Lodging in Lendal between 4.30pm and 6.30pm on Tuesday, November 10.

In an appeal to the public, the force issued a CCTV image of a man its officers said they would like to speak to and want to identify, saying they believe he may have information that could help their investigation.




A force spokesperson said: "If you witnessed any suspicious behaviour or have any other information that can help the investigation, please contact North Yorkshire Police online in the first instance.

"Information can be provided online in the first instance or by emailing [alisha.brickett1@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:alisha.brickett1@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

"If you are unable to use these options, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101, quoting reference 12260221763."

Alternatively, information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
