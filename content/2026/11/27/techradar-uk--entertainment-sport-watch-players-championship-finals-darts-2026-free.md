After 34 non-televised events, the top 64 players from the 2026 PDC Players Championship series will compete over three days for a chance to win the title and £130,000 in prize money.

Defending champion Luke Littler will not return after failing to play in enough of the behind-closed-doors events, while Josh Rock and Nathan Aspinall also missed out. However, there is still an extremely strong field, led by Dutchman Wessel Nijman, who won six Players Championship tournaments this year.

He’ll face competition from the likes of world No.2 Luke Humphries, seven-time champion Michael van Gerwen and Gerwyn Price, who won the Grand Slam of Darts title last weekend. Also in action are former world champions Gary Anderson and Roc Cross, as well as women’s world No. 1 Beau Graves.
