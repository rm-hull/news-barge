Black Friday is here, but I've already covered dozens of deals on wearable tech in the lead-up to the big day. As TechRadar's resident fitness tech guru, I've looked at Black Friday deals on all sorts of devices — but in the UK, nothing's beaten the Apple Watch Ultra 3 discounted to £495 (was ~~£749~~) at Argos.

The Apple Watch Ultra 3 was rated (by us) as the best smartwatch of last year. It's quite simply a wonderful piece of kit to use, with a liquid retina OLED display, pinpoint-accurate heart rate tracking (that we tested extensively against a heart rate monitor and other wearables) and tons of fitness features. It's a shame there's not a smaller option, but that's really my only quibble here — that, and the £749 asking price.

Fortunately, that last one is solved here, with a £254 discount bringing the Ultra 3 down to around the price of a mainline Apple Watch Series 12, which doesn't have the premium titanium construction, Action button or increased battery life. A £254 discount like this is rare for any Apple product, and I would seriously recommend you snapping it up. Below you can find the deal in full, along with a full choice of your preferred bands.
