Families looking for a touch of Christmas magic can step into a winter wonderland this festive season as Mother Shipton’s Cave welcomes back its popular Christmas Experience.

The Knaresborough attraction will be transformed into an enchanting Christmas village, with twinkling woodland lights, festive activities and the chance for children to meet Santa in his cosy grotto.

The event, which has previously been recognised as one of Yorkshire’s leading Christmas experiences, invites visitors to follow a magical trail through the historic woodland setting.

Young visitors with a Santa Experience ticket will receive a festive activity pack before meeting Santa and receiving a special soft toy gift.

Families can also enjoy a range of seasonal attractions, including storytelling sessions with Holly Berry, making reindeer food to take home, and sending Christmas wishes to Santa through the Snowy Owl Sorting Office.

The festive journey also includes opportunities to explore the illuminated woodland, discover the famous Cave and Petrifying Well, and leave a wish on the Christmas wishing trees.

Visitors can warm up along the way with winter treats including hot chocolate, mince pies and toasted marshmallows.

The Magical Christmas Experience runs on selected dates from late November until Christmas Eve, with advance booking recommended.
