## Former Northallerton nurse writes children’s book to help tackle anxiety

Anthony Degnan, from Northallerton, has written a children’s book to help young readers manage anxiety *(Image: Anthony Degnan)*

A former NHS nurse has written a children’s book to help young readers manage anxiety.

Anthony Degnan, from Northallerton, has drawn on his experience in mental health to create The Invisible Painter, a story designed to support children aged eight to 12 as they prepare for the transition to secondary school.

The book follows Leo, an 11-year-old boy facing worries about starting "big school," including fears of getting lost, sitting alone at lunch, and navigating life after his parents' separation.

Mr Degnan said: "I sat with too many young people who believed their feelings were facts, and that the storm they were in would never pass.

"I wanted to give them a simpler way of seeing it, in a story they'd actually want to read."

Set in the Yorkshire countryside, the story also explores his bond with his Gran, a wild-swimmer and artist.

Chapters end with practical "missions" for children, such as the Silly Banana test and creating a glitter jar to show how a busy mind can settle.

The book also includes resources for parents and carers to help discuss worries and identify when further support might be needed.

The Invisible Painter is available now on Amazon in paperback for £7.99 and on Kindle for £3.99.
