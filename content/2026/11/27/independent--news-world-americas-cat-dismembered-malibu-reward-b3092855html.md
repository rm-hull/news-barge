A dismembered cat was found at Malibu Bluffs Park, prompting a $10,000 reward offer for information leading to a conviction.

Park visitors reported the discovery, and the Mountains Recreation and Conservation Authority was notified on Nov. 18.

“As described by park visitors, rangers and staff who responded found a dismembered domestic cat, ritualistically placed with candles and other imagery,” the Santa Monica Mountains Conservancy said in a Facebook post on Wednesday.

![A view of the Pacific Ocean seen from Malibu Bluffs Park](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/14/17/Malibu-Bluffs-Park.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Rangers recovered evidence at the scene, the MRCA said.

The animal's remains were transferred to the Los Angeles County Department of Animal Care and Control for examination.

The authority is offering $10,000 for information leading to the identification, arrest and conviction of the person or persons responsible for what it called the atrocity.

The reward offer expires on Jan. 31.

A sergeant with the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department's Malibu/Lost Hills Station confirmed to City News Service on Thursday that the MRCA was handling the investigation.
