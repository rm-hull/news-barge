## Post Office replacement claims are ‘rubbish’, says MP

TGJOnes and Post Office, Westborough, Scarborough. Courtesy Numminen, LDRS *(Image: LDRS)*

A NORTH Yorkshire MP does not believe assurances that a new town centre Post Office is being lined up amid the upcoming closure of Scarborough’s main branch.

Alison Hume MP said the Post Office should not be allowed to “walk away” from Scarborough town centre after the planned closure of the branch inside TGJones on Westborough.

The member of parliament for Scarborough and Whitby said she had been told the Post Office was in talks with potential replacement operators, but she questioned those claims.

Speaking at a meeting on Friday, November 27, she said: “I’ve been told that they’re in conversations with two or three businesses securing a new central location, but I don’t believe them.

“We need to keep the pressure on them to make sure that they don’t walk away.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21771899.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TG Jones And Post Office, Westborough, Scarborough. Courtesy Anttoni James Numminen.

While the Post Office has identified its Falsgrave Road branch around 0.7 miles away as an alternative, Ms Hume warned many constituents rely on the town centre branch for banking, bill payments and identity verification services.

She described suggestions that the Falsgrave Road branch was a suitable alternative as “rubbish” and announced plans to launch a public petition within days.

She said: “I’m very concerned about the Post Office closure and I am launching a petition that should be going live on my website in the next couple of days so that everybody can sign that.

“I’ve also written to the Post Office and had a conversation on that, and my view is simply that although they might say that there is a Post Office in Falsgrave that is just technically close enough, we know that’s rubbish and we’ve got to keep it in the town centre.”

A Post Office spokesperson previously said: “The provision of a Post Office service to our customers in the area is important to us, and we are working hard to find a location nearby to continue offering Post Office services to the local community in Scarborough.”

The branch is due to close when the TGJones store on Westborough shuts next January as part of a nationwide restructuring programme.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21771897.jpg?type=mds-article-620)TGJ Ones And Post Office, Westborough, Scarborough. Courtesy Numminen, LDRS

In a letter sent earlier this year, Ms Hume asked Post Office Ltd to explain what temporary arrangements would be put in place if there was a gap between the closure of the existing branch and the opening of a replacement.

According to the Post Office, a similar range of services will be available at Falsgrave Road, while Newlands Park does not offer Passport Check and Send services.

The store, previously known as WH Smith, is expected to close on Saturday, January 23, 2027, following a closing-down sale.

A spokesperson for TGJones acknowledged the announcement would be “disappointing” for staff and customers and said it was focused on “supporting those affected”.
