## Mobility scooter users injured or killed rises to 52 in Yorkshire

Recent data have revealed that more mobility scooter users were injured or killed in Yorkshire and the Humber in 2025 than anywhere else in Britain *(Image: Supplied)*

More mobility scooter users were injured or killed in Yorkshire and the Humber in 2025 than anywhere else in Britain.

According to the latest Department for Transport (DfT) data, 52 mobility scooter users were injured or killed in the region, marking its highest total in a decade.

The figures were published by insurance intermediary Surewise as part of its Safer Mobility campaign and follow analysis of DfT data by Agilysis.

Richard Hannan, director at Surewise, said: "We’re extremely concerned to see mobility scooter user casualties reach a 10-year high in Yorkshire and the Humber at a time when we should be doing more to protect some of the most vulnerable people using our roads.

"We need to look seriously at the infrastructure people have to navigate every day, and we urge local authorities and the government to investigate how roads, pavements and crossings can be made safer and more accessible."

In total, 63 road users were injured or killed in incidents involving mobility scooters in the region.

Of the 52 mobility scooter users affected, 35 were aged 60 or over.

Mr Hannan added: "For many people, a mobility scooter is an essential part of maintaining their independence.

"The fact that around two-thirds of the mobility scooter users injured or killed were aged 60 or over reinforces why mobility scooter users need to be properly recognised and considered in road safety policy, infrastructure and by other road users."
