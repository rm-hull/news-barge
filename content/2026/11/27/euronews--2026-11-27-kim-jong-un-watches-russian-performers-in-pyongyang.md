North Korean leader Kim Jong Un has attended a performance by a Russian artistic troupe in Pyongyang, in another public demonstration of strengthening ties between North Korea and Russia.

Kim was accompanied by his wife, Ri Sol Ju, and Alexander Kozlov, Russia's Minister of Natural Resources and Ecology, at the event on 26 November at the East Pyongyang Grand Theater.

The performance featured Russian singer Larisa Dolina and musicians from the Osipov State Academic Russian Folk Orchestra, according to reports by North Korean state media.
