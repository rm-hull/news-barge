### When did 'The Great British Sewing Bee' first air?

"The Great British Sewing Bee" first aired on BBC Two in 2013, with May Martin and Patrick Grant judging the contestants' creations.

The competition has since become a BBC favorite, with amateur sewers taking on challenges designed to test their dressmaking skills. Celebrity specials bring famous faces into the sewing room for a more lighthearted spin on the format.
