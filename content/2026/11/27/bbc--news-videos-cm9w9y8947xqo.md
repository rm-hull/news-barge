# 'It's impossible': Spain's young people on housing ahead of Sunday's election

Housing has been the biggest issue for voters ahead of Spain's general election on Sunday, with millions of people protesting across the country in recent weeks over a lack of affordable homes.

Final opinion polls suggest the conservative People's Party will receive the most votes and that the right-wing Vox party is set to enter the national government for the first time. It would mean an end to Socialist prime minister Pedro Sánchez's eight years in power.

Europe correspondent Nick Beake speaks to a young couple from Valencia, who have been kept apart due to high rents in their city.
