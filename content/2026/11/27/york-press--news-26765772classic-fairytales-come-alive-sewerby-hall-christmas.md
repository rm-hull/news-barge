## Inside Sewerby Hall's magical new Christmas fairytale experience

Fairytales at Christmas is now open at Sewerby Hall and Gardens as a daytime experience *(Image: East Riding of Yorkshire Council)*

A historic house has been transformed into a festive fairytale wonderland, bringing classic stories to life.

Fairytales at Christmas is now open at Sewerby Hall and Gardens as a daytime experience, transforming the historic house into a magical fairytale wonderland.

Visitors can explore themed rooms inspired by beloved tales such as The Nutcracker, Snow White, Cinderella, Peter Pan, and The Snow Queen.

Councillor Nick Coultish, Cabinet Member for Culture, Leisure and Tourism at East Riding of Yorkshire Council, said: "Fairytales at Christmas is a wonderful new festive experience that brings together the magic of classic storytelling and the unique heritage of Sewerby Hall.

"The themed displays throughout the house are truly enchanting and provide something for visitors of all ages to enjoy.

"It's a fantastic addition to our Christmas programme and a great opportunity for families and friends to make special memories together this festive season."

Guests can follow a trail through the house, take part in activities, and enjoy interactive fun for all ages.

The attraction is open on weekends and also on Monday, December 21, Tuesday, December 22, and Wednesday, December 23.

It remains open until Sunday, January 3, but will be closed on Saturday, December 26, and Sunday, December 27.
