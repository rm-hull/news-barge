A year of creativity and collaboration has raised thousands for a youth homelessness charity.

RARE Collective, based in York and founded by artists Sharon McDonagh and DJ Sola, raised £4,696 through a series of exhibitions, events, and fundraising activities in support of SASH, a charity helping 16- to 25-year-olds who are homeless or at risk of homelessness across York and surrounding areas.

Ms McDonagh has raised nearly £45,000 for SASH over the past three years through various creative projects.

Sharon McDonagh, founder of RARE Collective and SASH's creative charity champion, said: "We're incredibly proud to have raised another £4,696 for SASH.

"Supporting the charity has been close to my heart for several years, and setting up RARE Collective with Sola has given us a new platform to bring artists and the community together while continuing to make a difference.

"For me, there's a real connection between street art and the work SASH does."

Aimee, fundraising lead at SASH, said: "We're hugely grateful to Sharon, Sola and everyone who has supported RARE Collective.

"It's fantastic to see the creative community coming together to support SASH, and their continued commitment means so much to us."

RARE Collective’s fundraising began in March with solo exhibitions at WET York.
