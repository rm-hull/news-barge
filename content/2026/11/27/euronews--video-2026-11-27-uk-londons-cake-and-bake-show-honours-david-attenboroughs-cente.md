Visitors admired an extraordinary collection of **wildlife-inspired cakes** in**London** on**27 November 2026**, featuring remarkably lifelike gorillas, snow leopards, hedgehogs, badgers and squirrels. Several creations depict** Sir David Attenborough** himself, sculpted in sugar alongside wild animals or dressed for a polar expedition. Among the most striking entries is a mother gorilla cradling her newborn, recreated with astonishing attention to detail.

The exhibition forms part of **The Cake & Bake Show’s** annual competition, which celebrates**Attenborough’s centenary** this year under the theme**Inspired by Nature**. Paying tribute to the broadcaster behind***Planet Earth*and* The Blue Planet***, the competition brings together **amateur bakers and professionals competing** forprizes**including £1,000 in cash** and**Kenwood appliances**.

Judges praised the **artistic and technical expertise** required to create elaborate cakes capable of retaining their shape for several days in a warm exhibition hall. Visitors can vote for their favourite creation, with the**public vote** contributing to the final result alongside the judges’ scores. The winner will be announced on**Sunday 29 November**, the closing day of the event at** London’s Olympia exhibition centre**.
