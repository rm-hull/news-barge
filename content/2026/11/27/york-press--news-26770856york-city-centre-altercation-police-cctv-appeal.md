North Yorkshire Police has issued a fresh appeal for information following a fight which took place in York city centre more than a year ago.

The county's force said the incident took place outside the Slug and Lettuce near Ouse Bridge at around 7.45pm on Saturday, July 26, 2025.




A force spokesperson said its officers were called to reports of an altercation, which led to a large police response in the area at the time, and had since made further enquiries.

North Yorkshire Police issued a CCTV image of a man it said it wanted to identify and speak to, saying it believed he may have information that will assist its investigation.

The force spokesperson said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured - anyone who recognises him or has information that could help the investigation is asked to contact police by emailing [david.scott@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:david.scott@northyorkshire.police.uk), or alternatively, visit the North Yorkshire Police website.

"Please quote reference 12250138659 when providing information."
