**The 32nd annual Yorkshire Young Achievers Awards, held in Leeds, recognised outstanding young talent from across the region, including three winners from North Yorkshire.**

The 32nd annual Yorkshire Young Achievers Awards took place on the evening of Thursday 26 November 2026 at Leeds United’s Centenary Pavilion, celebrating the achievements of young people from across the Yorkshire region. The event attracted more than 400 guests, recognising talents in fields ranging from sport and education to enterprise and community service.

Among the winners were three exceptional young people from North Yorkshire: ten-year-old Tiffany Taylor from Harrogate, sailor and rower Jasmine Harrison from Thirsk, and entrepreneur Lyes Berkache from Cropton.

Tiffany Taylor received the Unsung Hero Award for her compassionate support as a Young Carer. Living with her younger brother who has nocturnal epilepsy and complex health conditions, Tiffany is often the first to respond during his medical emergencies. Her caring spirit extends beyond her family; she fundraised £1,500 for the Little Princess Trust by donating her hair to help young people with hair loss due to cancer treatment and participated in a three-hour dance-athon to support a friend’s family during a cancer battle. Tiffany also joined her family in a 40-day, mile-a-day walk raising funds for Young Epilepsy.

Jasmine Harrison was honoured with the Achievement in Sport Award. The 27-year-old from Thirsk became the youngest female to row solo across the Atlantic Ocean in 2021 and the first female to swim from Land’s End to John O’Groats a year later. She also completed a round-the-world yacht race in her 19-foot yacht, making her the first British woman to sail solo around the world in a vessel of that size. During that journey, she swam around St Helena, completing the 30-mile swim in 18.5 hours. Jasmine continues to expand her ambitions and is training to become an on-call firefighter.

Lyes Berkache, aged 25, was awarded the Achievement in Management and Enterprise Award for his success as a young businessman. Growing up on a family farm near Pickering, Lyes qualified as a quantity surveyor before founding a portable sanitation business that services the north of England. The company has expanded significantly, acquiring an established Yorkshire portable sanitation provider and branching into waste management. Lyes has built a reputation for exceptional customer service across various sectors, including construction and events.

> **Peter McCormick OBE, Chairman of the Yorkshire Young Achievers Foundation and the Awards, reflected on the evening:**
>
> We had a terrific night hearing about some of Yorkshires young shining stars in a variety of fields.
>
> This recognition for their achievements would not be possible without the support of our sponsors and our guests, and I am very proud that with their help, not only can we reward these young people but we can continue our grants programme which helps other young people across Yorkshire to reach their full potential. In the last month alone, we have made grants totalling 12,000. The Awards are our main fundraiser of the year and since their inception have raised nearly 2 million.

Several grants have recently benefited North Yorkshire organisations, including AMP, a charity delivering music activities for children in challenging circumstances; Downs Syndrome North Yorkshire, supporting trips for young people and their siblings; and the Yorkshire Dales Millennium Trust for rural apprenticeships.

Other winners at the event included:

* Personality of the Year: Dominic Calvert-Lewin, Leeds United footballer
* Youngster of the Year: Dexter Sol Ansell, young actor from Leeds
* Achievement in Education: Maryam Kapree, Bradford
* Achievement in the Arts: Fred Kettle, actor from Oxenhope
* The Martin Gerrard Award: Peter Gilman of Leeds, honoured for longstanding support of the Foundation

The Yorkshire Young Achievers Awards were founded in 1993, and the accompanying Foundation formed in 2010 to support charitable projects across Yorkshire. The event is sponsored principally by McCormicks Solicitors, based in Harrogate.
