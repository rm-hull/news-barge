# Man arrested after arson attack inside historic Grade I-listed Yorkshire church

## A man has been arrested following a suspected arson attack inside a Yorkshire church which damaged historical artefacts.

The fire service were called to Holy Trinity Church on Micklegate in York at around 12noon on Wednesday (Nov 25).

When they arrived at 12.12pm, the fires had already been put out.

When police officers arrived on the scene, the fire service informed them they believed the blaze had been started deliberately.

Investigations and CCTV footage from council operators located a 42-year-old man in nearby Blake Street.

He has since been charged with arson.

A statement from North Yorkshire Police said: “The suspect has been charged with arson to a large altarpiece, the reredos, to the value of £30,000 belonging to Holy Trinity Church. He has also been charged with possession of cannabis.

“He has been remanded into custody and will appear at York Magistrates’ Court on Friday morning (November 27).”
