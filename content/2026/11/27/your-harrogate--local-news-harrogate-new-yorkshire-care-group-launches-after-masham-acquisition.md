Tom and Kirsty Page have launched Page Care Group, bringing West Park Care and JC Home Care together.

The launch follows West Park Care’s acquisition of Masham-based Jane Caress in 2024, which allowed it to provide care to more people in their own homes.

The founders now plan to acquire other care businesses across Yorkshire.

They have appointed a six-member board to assess potential acquisitions and oversee how businesses join the group, with a focus on continuity for clients and staff.

Tom Page, managing director of Page Care Group, said:

> “Page Care Group marks the next stage of our growth. It gives us the structure and expertise to pursue acquisitions and expand organically while keeping outstanding private care in people’s own homes at the heart of the business.”

West Park Care has been named one of the Top 20 Home Care Providers in Yorkshire and the Humber for three consecutive years.

Lindsay Austin, group managing director of Leeds-based Bayford Group, has joined the board as an independent non-executive director.

She said:

> “For owners considering the future of their care business, Page Care Group offers a clear transition plan, continuity for clients and colleagues, and confidence that standards of care will be protected.”
