# Redcar rapist took advantage of teenage girl then tried to flee area

## A Redcar rapist who ‘took advantage’ of a teenage girl before attempting to flee the area has been jailed.

Gareth Fletcher raped the child in the early hours of September 12, then immediately fled the area in his white BMW.

The 42-year-old was caught near the child minutes later after the teenager reported him to police.

Fletcher admitted rape, assault by penetration and sexual activity with a child and was sentenced on November 26 at Teesside Crown Court.

He was jailed for 12 years and nine months and is also subject to a Sexual Harm Prevention Order (SHPO).

![Gareth Fletcher was jailed at Teesside Crown Court.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/27/14/29/gareth-fletcher.jpeg?trim=22,0,218,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Detective Constable Nicky Wright, from Cleveland Police’s Protecting Vulnerable People team, said: "Fletcher took advantage of a young girl, leaving her distressed and traumatised by what he had done.

"He showed no thought or concern for the devastating impact his actions would have on her both mentally and physically.

"When the incident was reported, officers acted swiftly to locate and arrest him, ensuring the victim was safeguarded.

"By pleading guilty, he has spared the victim the ordeal of giving evidence at trial and ensured he will now spend a significant period behind bars.

"The victim has shown tremendous courage throughout this investigation. After experiencing something no child should ever have to endure, she has displayed remarkable strength and resilience. What happened to her was not her fault, and I hope today's sentence provides some reassurance that she was listened to, supported and that the person responsible has been held accountable for his actions.

"I hope this outcome offers her a measure of closure and helps her to look towards the future with confidence."
