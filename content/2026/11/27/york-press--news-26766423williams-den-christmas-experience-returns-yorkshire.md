## Award-winning Yorkshire attraction brings back Christmas experience

Christmas at Williams Den includes a memorable visit to Father and Mother Christmas *(Image: The Wild Child Photographer)*

An award-winning play attraction in Yorkshire is promising a magical family day out this festive season.

William’s Den is bringing back its popular Christmas experience this year, filled with festive activities and immersive storytelling.

The event includes a visit to the woodland lodge to meet Father and Mother Christmas, a stop at the North Pole-inspired toy shop, and unlimited indoor and outdoor play.

Tor Carver, co-founder and director of William’s Den, said: "When we created our Christmas experiences, we kept coming back to the memories that made Christmas feel so special when we were growing up.

"It wasn’t about having the most extravagant celebration, it was about spending quality time together, believing in magic, and making memories that stayed with us for years."

The programme also features a new Christmas show titled The Elf Who Forgot How to Play, gingerbread decorating, marshmallow toasting, and festive treats for adults.

The Christmas experience will run every weekend from November 28, including daily from December 21 to 24.

William’s Den is also offering 'Christmas Fridays' for the first time, which include a festive family feast before meeting Father and Mother Christmas.

Tickets are on sale now at www.williamsden.co.uk/christmas-at-williams-den.
