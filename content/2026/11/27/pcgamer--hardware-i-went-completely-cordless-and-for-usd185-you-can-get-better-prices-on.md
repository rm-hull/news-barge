I made the commitment this year: no cords on my desk. I already had a wireless mouse and keyboard so I got myself a controller charging stand (no more USB-C running to the top of my PC) and finally made the switch to wireless earbuds (no more 3.5mm cord to my speakers). I feel free at last! The only clutter left on my desk is... all my other clutter (not pictured). But no cords!

You can make the same commitment to a mostly clean desktop during Black Friday with some of the best deals on our highest rated wireless kit. I'll tell you what I've got in my real desk setup pictured above but I'm recommending some better products and better deals for you. I went back through my old order details and found out I spent a total of $354.93 on this setup (not including the Xbox controller itself) but you can get something similar for way less.

## What I have: Nuphy Halo 96

## You should get: one of these Keychron keyboards

I got myself a Nuphy wireless mechanical board a couple years ago because I wanted to dip my toes into hot-swappable switches and custom keycaps. I love it, but that's a bigger project than just getting yourself a decent wireless keyboard. To get the cordless life yourself, look instead to one of these Keychron wireless boards that our PCG hardware experts rate highly.

I like mine! The Viper V4 Pro is in fact on our list of best wireless gaming mice, but the Corsair Sabre is there too and has a much bigger discount on an already lower price.

I do, but stepping into a pair of the best wireless gaming earbuds that my coworkers actually recommend sounds so pricey.
