A York music venue is celebrating tonight after a development that threatened its future was thrown out.

The Crescent Community Venue responded to the news with the words: “Christmas has come early!”

It comes after City of York Council planners rejected an application to build nine flats, a 20-unit aparthotel and offices next door.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-crescent-community-venue-york-entrance.webp)

The Crescent said that would have posed two major challenges to its continued existence. Firstly, the interruption to its business caused by the noise and mess of the construction – the historic York malt house would have been demolished before the large block was built.

Secondly, the future residents of the apartments and guests at the aparthotel could have made noise complaints against the Crescent’s live music performances.

The council gave this reason for refusing the application at 9 The Crescent: “The Local Planning Authority is not satisfied that future occupants of the proposed development would be protected from unacceptable levels of music noise and vibration, particularly in relation to low-frequency noise, nor that the development would avoid placing unreasonable restrictions on the lawful operation of the neighbouring venue.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/york-crescent-aparthotel-and-flats-impression-1.webp)

In response, the venue said: “We would like to say a heartfelt thank you to everyone who sent a letter of objection, the people who shared our story and, of course, the people who made the decision.

“It has been over a year of waiting for an outcome and we can now return our focus to the venue! If you fancy a drink to celebrate, we will be celebrating too!”

One of those welcoming the news is Bob Mitchell, the music photographer and big supporter of The Crescent.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crescent-community-venue-york-posters.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crescent-community-venue-york-stage.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crescent-community-venue-york-games.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/crescent-community-venue-york-entrance.webp)

He told ***YorkMix***: “it’s fantastic, and let’s just hope that it sticks because obviously there’s going to be appeals.

“Independent venues, grassroots venues have been struggling, and there’s been a lot closing down over the last few years due to these sort of things happening.”

He described The Crescent as “just such a great venue. Joe and and the team there do fantastic job.

“They get some fantastic people playing there, and it’s one of the bigger venues in York – the independent venue that just gives a great service.”
