A historic site in Ripon is to get pride of place on a national radio broadcast across a whole day in the build-up to Christmas.

As part of BBC Radio 3’s now traditional Carols Across the Country, the station will be broadcasting live from the UNESCO-listed Fountains Abbey in Ripon and the guests will include a well-known group of Harrogate musicians.

The once-a-year festive special of live broadcasts from all four nations in the UK will run from 6.30am to 6.30pm on Sunday, December 6 and marks the start of the Christmas countdown.

The programme, which is now in its third edition, the event will be hosted by presenter Mark Forrest, whose radio career includes Classic FM, Scala Radio, BBC Radio 4, BBC Radio 5 Live, BBC Local Radio, and Virgin Radio.

Among his BBC Radio 3 roles is the Sunday Breakfast where he hosts classical music and morning features from 6.30am to 9pm.

Carols Across the Country: A Seasonal Journey will feature live music and explores a much-loved carol, including O Come, O Come, Emmanuel, Wassail! Wassail! and I Saw Three Ships. Julie Hesmondhalgh, Celebrity Traitor turned Oh Come All You Faithful, will be popping in throughout the day to read some Christmas poetry favourites.

Ripon will be the focus nationally of the festive show from 6.30am to 9am when Mark Forrest will broadcast live from Fountains Abbey.

For the occasion, Mark will be joined by special guests, including the Harrogate Brass Band, performing Christmas favourites in a 12th century vaulted cellarium as the sun rises over the 900-year-old abbey ruins.

Mark will keep warm inside the late Elizabethan manor house, Fountains Hall, now owned by the National Trust, stepping into a scene from Dickens's Christmas Carol.

Following Mark Forrest's morning broadcast from Yorkshire, the live festive journey will continuesacross the UK.

• 9:00am – 11:00am. Sarah Walker from the vintage barge Sabina 5 at the National Waterways Museum in Gloucester.

• 11:00am – 1:00pm. Kate Molleson from the Royal Observatory in Edinburgh.

• 1:00pm – 3:00pm. Shân Cothi from St Garmon's Church in Wrexham County Borough.

• 4:00pm – 6:30pm. John Toal from Belfast Harbour
