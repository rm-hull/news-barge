## Wanted man arrested in North Yorkshire

A wanted Harrogate man has been arrested by police *(Image: Supplied)*

A wanted Harrogate man has been arrested, North Yorkshire Police reported today.

The arrest follows an appeal announced on Monday (Nov 23) to find the 34-yearold from Harrogate, who was wanted for theft and fraud by false representation.
