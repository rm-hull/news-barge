**Sunflower Bakery hosted a free Christmas cake decorating class at Boroughbridge Manor, with plans to return early next year following the successful event.**

Residents at Boroughbridge Manor had the chance to get into the festive spirit by taking part in a Christmas cake decorating class led by the local Sunflower Bakery. The free event was organised by the care home’s activities team and proved popular among participants. Following its success, the bakery is due to return to Boroughbridge Manor early next year to host another session. Activities Lead Jodie Muir expressed optimism about future collaborations with the bakery to provide enjoyable events for residents. The cake decorating class not only offered a creative outlet but also helped foster a warm community atmosphere during the holiday season at the care home.
