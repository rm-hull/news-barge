Police are searching the river in York today after a report of a possible body sighted in the water.

Officers were contacted at 8.36am this morning (Friday) by a member of the public about the potential sighting near Millennium Bridge.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Officers attended immediately and extensive search efforts are under way.

“Specialist resources, including police drones and the York Rescue Boat, have been deployed, and further specialist teams will continue to assist throughout the day as necessary.”

They ask anyone with information that could assist the investigation to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101.

If the sighting is current or urgent, please call 999. Please quote reference 12260232219.
