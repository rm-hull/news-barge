York-based Abingdon Health has reported a strong start to its financial year.

At its Annual General Meeting (AGM) on Wednesday (Nov 25), the rapid diagnostics manufacturer confirmed revenues of more than £1 million for October.

Dr Chris Hand, executive chairman of Abingdon Health, said: "I am pleased to report that the second quarter of FY27 has begun well, with October revenues exceeding £1 million.

"We expect revenue for the second quarter (October to December 2026) to be significantly improved on the first quarter of the current financial year (“Q1 FY27”).

"This reflects the recommencement of a number of customer programmes that, as reported in our final results on 13 October 2026, experienced short-term delays in Q1 FY27.

"These were due principally to the availability of speciality reagents and to specification updates.

"Trading remains in line with expectations for the year ending 30 June 2027, and we look forward to updating shareholders further in due course."

Held at the company’s offices on York Biotech Campus in Sand Hutton, the AGM follows a period of financial growth for Abingdon Health.

In October, the company reported a 32 per cent increase in total revenue to £11.3 million for the year ending June 30, 2026 (FY26), up from £8.6 million the previous year.

The company also returned to profitability at the adjusted EBITDA level in the second half of the year.

Adjusted EBITDA losses narrowed to £1.5 million from £2.6 million the previous year.

Contract development revenue rose by 56 per cent to £5 million, while regulatory services and medtech consulting revenue grew by 21 per cent to £4.1 million.

Abingdon Health also expanded its US operations, opening a manufacturing hub and performance evaluation facility in Madison, Wisconsin, on September 24, 2026.
