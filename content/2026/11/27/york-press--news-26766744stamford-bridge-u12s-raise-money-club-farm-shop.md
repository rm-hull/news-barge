Stamford Bridge Under-12s FC have been helping out at a farm shop to raise funds for the club.

Lending a hand at the Balloon Tree Farm Shop and Café on Saturday 5th December, the team will swap the football pitch for netting Christmas trees, carrying them to customers' cars, and spreading cheer with carols.

This is the second year the club has supported the Balloon Tree's Christmas season.

Last year, they raised money by helping with bag-packing for festive shoppers.

This year, they've taken on a new festive role, ensuring customers' Christmas trees make it home safely.

The Balloon Tree, just outside Stamford Bridge, has been sourcing its Christmas trees from Dalby Forest for nearly two decades.

This year, customers can choose from Nordmann and Fraser Firs, ranging from 5ft to 12ft, all selected for their excellent quality and minimal needle drop.

Stamford Bridge Under-12s and the Balloon Tree team are ready to welcome shoppers.
