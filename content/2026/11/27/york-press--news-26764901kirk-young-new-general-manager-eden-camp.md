A lifelong love of history has led to a dream job for one museum’s new general manager.

Kirk Young has taken up the role at Eden Camp Modern History Museum in North Yorkshire, bringing with him a diverse background in hospitality, security, and logistics.

Mr Young said: "When they offered me the position, I was absolutely ecstatic.

"I had planned to take some time out but, when this job was advertised, it was something I couldn’t refuse.

"I’ve come to a place of work where the history I love and all my hobbies come together.

"It’s a dream come true."

Located near Malton, the museum tells the story of wartime Britain through immersive exhibits and sensory experiences, welcoming around 125,000 visitors each year.

Mr Young’s interest in military history has long shaped his personal and professional life.

For the past 14 years, he has also explored military heritage sites across Europe as a member of a paranormal club.

He said: "I thought I knew my stuff before I came here but no I don’t.

"The team we have eat and breathe living history and the stories we tell.

"I’ve learned so much talking to the team and I can’t wait to learn more and lead the site into what will be a really exciting chapter - with the 40th anniversary of Eden Camp coming up in 2027."

Eden Camp is based on the site of a former prisoner of war camp and has operated as a museum since 1987.

Earlier this year, it was acquired by York-based Continuum Attractions, which plans to invest in the site while preserving its historical significance.

Andrew Pawson, CEO of Continuum Attractions, said: "We’re delighted to welcome Kirk to Eden Camp.

"His enthusiasm for history, passion for the guest experience and breadth of management experience make him a fantastic addition to the team.

"As we look ahead to the attraction’s 40th anniversary in 2027, we’re excited to have him at the helm, building on Eden Camp’s remarkable legacy and helping shape an exciting future for a much-loved Yorkshire attraction."

Mr Young is now working closely with the Eden Camp team as they prepare to mark the museum’s milestone anniversary and continue their mission of education and remembrance.

To find out more about Eden Camp, visit edencamp.co.uk.
