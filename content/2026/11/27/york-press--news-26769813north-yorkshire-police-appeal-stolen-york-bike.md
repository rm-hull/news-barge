A bicycle was stolen from a property in York.

The incident happened around 9.20pm on November 11, 2026 at St Mary’s House on Ramsay Close, where a grey Cutklass bike, valued at around £80, was taken.

The bike had been secured with a lock and padlock.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "Officers have obtained CCTV which is believed to show the offence taking place.

"However, due to the time of night and image quality, it has not been possible to identify those involved."

Police are appealing for sightings of suspicious activity in the area or anyone who may have seen the stolen bike.

They are also keen to hear from anyone who has been offered a grey Cutklass bike for sale.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference 12260222616.
