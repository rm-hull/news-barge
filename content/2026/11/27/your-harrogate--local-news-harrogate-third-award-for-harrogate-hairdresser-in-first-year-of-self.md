Harrogate hairdresser Robyn Mackin has won silver at the 2026 Beauty Full Awards.

Robyn placed in the global top 10 in the Best Newcomer: Hair category.

It’s her third award in her first year of self-employment, following bronze awards for Blonde Specialist of the Year and Hair Extensionist of the Year.

Robyn said:

> “I honestly can’t quite believe it.
>
> "To have now received three awards at the Beauty Full Awards in one year is something I’m incredibly proud of, and something I never expected to achieve, especially so early on in my self-employed journey.”

The town centre hairdresser has spent the past year building her own client base and developing her skills in blonding, colour work and hair extensions.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b09326650a481c0669.png)

She hopes to train other hairdressers in the future.

Robyn added:

> “Taking the step into self-employment was a huge decision for me.
>
> "Building my own clientele, establishing my business and finding my feet in an industry that I’m so passionate about has come with its challenges, but it’s also been one of the most rewarding things I’ve done.”

She also thanked those who have supported her since she went self-employed.

She said:

> “Above all, I’m so grateful to every client who has booked an appointment, recommended me, supported my business or trusted me with their hair.
>
> "I’m also incredibly thankful to my friends and family who have encouraged me along the way.
>
> "Without that support, none of this would feel quite as special.”
