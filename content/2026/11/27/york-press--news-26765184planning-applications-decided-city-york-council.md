A number of planning applications have been recorded by City of York Council, covering extensions, solar panels, and changes of use across the area.

### Proposal for a rear extension in Galtres Avenue

An application has been made for a single-storey extension at 46 Galtres Avenue, York, YO31 1JT.

The extension would extend six metres beyond the rear wall of the original house, with an eaves height and total height of 2.65 metres.

The status is listed as unknown.

### Roof dormers and rooflights planned in Earswick

An application covers three dormers on the rear roof slope and three rooflights on the front of 50 Earswick Chase, Earswick, York, YO32 9FY.

The work is described as part retrospective, meaning some of the proposal relates to work already carried out.

The application is listed as decided.

Ref. No: 26/01775/FUL | Received: Not provided | Validated: October 1, 2026 | Status: Decided

### Alterations and electric gates planned in Clifton

Plans have been submitted for alterations to the existing rear single-storey projection at 127 Clifton, York, YO30 6BL.

The proposal also includes changes to the rear and side boundary walls, raising them to 1.8 metres, along with electric gates for the parking area.

The application is listed as decided.

Ref. No: 26/01771/FUL | Received: Not provided | Validated: September 29, 2026 | Status: Decided

### Extensions and roof changes proposed in Bishopthorpe

An application has been made for a two-storey side extension and a single-storey front extension at Byro, 1 Appleton Road, Bishopthorpe, York, YO23 2RF.

The plans also include a front rooflight and a rear dormer.

The application is listed as decided.

Ref. No: 26/01772/FUL | Received: Not provided | Validated: September 29, 2026 | Status: Decided

### Tree work planned at Kings Cloisters

An application covers work to one lime tree and one horse chestnut at Kings Cloisters, Driffield Terrace, York.

Both trees would be crown reduced by about 30 per cent, equal to about five to six metres, while deadwood would also be removed.

The trees are protected by Tree Preservation Order CYC130.

The application is listed as decided.

Ref. No: 26/01740/TPO | Received: Not provided | Validated: September 24, 2026 | Status: Decided
