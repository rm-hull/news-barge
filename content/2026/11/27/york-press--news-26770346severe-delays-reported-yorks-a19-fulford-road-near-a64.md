There are severe delays on a main road in York this afternoon (Friday, November 27).

Bus passengers travelling on Fulford Road on the service to Selby were reporting gridlocked traffic around 4pm today.




Further down the A19 at the Fulford Interchange junction with the A64, "severe delays" of 11 minutes are reported by AA Traffic News, southbound after St Ann's Court.

Average speeds are as low as five miles per hour in the area, it is reported.

The news comes as road users are also warned of delays to the west of the A19 on the A1036 Tadcaster Road, after a water main burst at the junction with St Helen's Road on Wednesday evening.

Traffic approaching the Tadcaster Road from the direction of Foxwood and Dringhouses is using the Moor Lane roundabout close to the Tesco Extra supermarket increasing congestion in the area as vehicles filter across the two lanes there.
