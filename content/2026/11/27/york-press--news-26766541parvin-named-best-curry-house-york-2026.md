## 'A fantastic feeling' - beloved York restaurant named best in the city

The team at The Parvin Indian Restaurant in Haxby *(Image: The Parvin)*

A POPULAR restaurant has been named this year's Best Curry House in York.

The Parvin Indian Restaurant, in Haxby, was crowned as the 2026 winners after hundreds of Press readers voted it as their favourite dining spot.

Safiqur Rahman, who has been at the forefront of his beloved restaurant in York Road for over 18 years, said winning the award was a "fantastic feeling".




He paid tribute to customers, staff and the local community who helped secure the accolade.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21768979.jpg?type=mds-article-620)"This award is not just a reflection of the food we serve, but also the hard work and dedication of our fantastic team, who work tirelessly behind the scenes to deliver the experience our customers have come to expect" *(Image: The Parvin)*

Safiqur added: "There were many great nominees, so to come out on top is a real achievement and something we are incredibly proud of.

"I want to thank every single customer who took the time to vote for us.

"Without your continued loyalty and support, none of this would be possible.

"To win this award for a third time is something we are immensely proud of and it means so much to everyone involved with The Parvin."

The restaurant has been at the heart of the Haxby community for decades and said its focus has always been on serving high-quality food while making customers feel welcome.

Safiqur added: "This award is not just a reflection of the food we serve, but also the hard work and dedication of our fantastic team, who work tirelessly behind the scenes to deliver the experience our customers have come to expect.

"We are also extremely proud of the relationships we have built within Haxby and the surrounding areas.

"The support we receive from our local community allows us to continue supporting local charities and fundraising initiatives through our popular quiz nights."

![The Parvin has been named the winner of the 2026 competition, as voted by readers of The Press](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21768978.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Parvin has been named the winner of the 2026 competition, as voted by readers of The Press *(Image: The Parvin)*

As well as celebrating its latest success, The Parvin is looking ahead to a busy festive season.

The restaurant will be open on Christmas Day, with limited availability remaining for its first sitting between noon and 1.45pm.

Bookings are also being taken throughout the festive period and into the new year, with both small and large parties welcome.

The Parvin continues to host its popular Sunday buffet every week, priced at £15.90 per adult, and runs monthly quiz nights in support of local charities and fundraising causes throughout 2027.

Safiqur said: "To everyone who voted, supported us, recommended us and continued to visit us over the years, thank you.

"Whether you have been coming to us for 18 years or have only recently discovered The Parvin, we truly appreciate every single person.

"We never take the support for granted, and this award gives us even more motivation to keep pushing forward and delivering the very best we can for many more years to come."

For more information, visit The Parvin's website.
