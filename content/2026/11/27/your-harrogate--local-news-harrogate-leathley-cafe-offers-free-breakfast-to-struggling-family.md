Leathley Farm Shop & Café is asking local people to nominate someone for breakfast on the house this Christmas.

The offer is open to families, couples and individuals, with drinks and cakes included.

The family-run café, operated by Rebecca and Chris Blunstone, would like to hear about anyone facing financial difficulties, coping with illness or bereavement, or simply in need of a treat.

The café team said:

> “There's absolutely no judgement and this will be a private gesture.
>
> "It’s just a genuine wish to put a smile on someone's face and hopefully make their day that little bit brighter.”

*To nominate someone, send the café a private message on social media or email [info@leathleyfarmshopandcafe.co.uk](mailto:info@leathleyfarmshopandcafe.co.uk) by Friday 11th December.*
