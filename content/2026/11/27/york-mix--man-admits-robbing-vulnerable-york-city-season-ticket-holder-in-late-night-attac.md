A man has finally admitted robbing a vulnerable York City fan of his season ticket during a late-night attack in the city centre.

Gary Walls, 42, was one of two men who robbed the City fan of his season ticket, bank card, house keys, mobile phone and wallet after preying on him in Little Shambles on his way home from the pub, York Crown Court heard.

Walls’ sidekick Robert Elkes, 32, was jailed for over seven years in November last year after he admitted robbing the City supporter – who has learning and communication difficulties – and stabbing another man in a separate incident in the city centre.

Walls, whose last registered address was a homeless shelter in Union Terrace, York, had admitted fraud in that he used the victim’s bank card to buy alcohol and cigarettes, but denied playing any part in the robbery.

He was due to face trial at the crown court on Wednesday (25 November) but pleaded guilty to robbery at the last minute.

Walls, who admitted taking the victim’s mobile phone, keys and bank card, had his sentence postponed for a probation report.

Judge Sean Morris granted him bail until sentence on 8 January but warned him that he faced an inevitable jail sentence.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/york-shambles-night-time-police.jpg)

At Elkes’s sentence hearing last year, the court heard that he and Walls pounced on the named victim in the city centre shortly before midnight on 6 April, 2024.

The victim was punched, manhandled and robbed of his bank card, mobile phone, wallet, house keys and even his York City season ticket.

Prosecutor Graham O’Sullivan said that shortly before the robbery, Elkes and Walls had chased him from Shambles Market as he made his way home from a pub at about 11.30pm.

The victim had watched his beloved team play that afternoon, then went to a pub near Shambles for post-match drinks.

It was after leaving the pub and making his way home, using a short-cut through Shambles Market, that Elkes and Walls pounced.

“He was chased from the market onto the Shambles,” said Mr O’Sullivan.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/the-stonebow-pub-york.jpg)

“One of the two men punched him, one put his hand around him and one went through his pockets.”

The victim suffered a cut above his eye during the attack in which the robbers also took £25 cash, his provisional driving licence and Santander bank card.

Shortly after the robbery, the card was used by the robbers in city-centre shops to buy items such as alcohol and cigarettes.

The victim sought refuge in The Stonebow pub in Fossgate, near the scene of the robbery, where he was helped by staff and two friends who happened to be passing by and called police.

Elkes, of Fossgate, York, was arrested after being identified from one of the shop’s CCTV footage which showed him trying to buy alcohol and cigarettes.

Four months after the robbery, just after midday on 3 June, Elkes – who had a massive criminal record – stabbed another man in the chest in broad daylight, causing a collapsed and punctured lung.
