FastestVPN is nowhere close to the very best VPNs, such as NordVPN, Surfshark, and Proton VPN. But at $27 for life, it's hard to pass up if you're on a tight budget. Lifetime VPN deals are rare enough as it is, and one at this price is even rarer.

The included password manager supports six users and is also more capable than you'd expect from a freebie. It gets the basics right, generating strong passwords, saving and auto-filling your logins, and syncing your passwords across devices.

Of course, we wouldn't be recommending FastestVPN if we didn't believe it keeps your data private. It's headquartered in the privacy-friendly Cayman Islands plus its no-logs policy has been independently audited. You also get AES-256 encryption, WireGuard, a kill switch, Double VPN, and an ad blocker.

It's decent for streaming, unblocking Netflix US, UK, and Japan, along with Disney+ and BBC iPlayer in our tests. But it's a far cry from the best streaming VPNs like NordVPN, which unblocks just about everything. Despite its name, it’s not the fastest VPN either. That said, it’s still quick enough for everyday browsing and streaming.
