Music icon Marc Almond is set to perform at Scarborough Spa's Grand Hall.

The one-off headline show will take place on Thursday, September 9, 2027, at the Scarborough Spa Grand Hall.

Almond, known for his career spanning more than four decades, rose to fame as the frontman of Soft Cell, where he helped define the synth-pop era with hits like the global classic Tainted Love.

Hannah Morris from the Spa said: "We’re absolutely thrilled to welcome Marc to our 2027 programme. As one of the UK’s most iconic and recognisable performers, we can’t wait to see him take to the stage and perform the songs that have made him such a beloved artist. While we’re already looking ahead to another fantastic year of live entertainment, our team is continuing to work hard behind the scenes to bring some more big names next year - and we’re hopeful of sharing a couple more announcements very soon!"

Beyond his work with Soft Cell, Almond has built a distinguished solo career, blending pop, cabaret, chanson, and alternative music into a unique and influential sound.

His powerful voice and artistic vision have earned him critical acclaim and a loyal global following.

With more than 38 million records sold worldwide, Almond’s live shows are known for their theatricality and emotional depth.

The Scarborough Spa concert is expected to feature a mix of classic hits and fan favourites from across Almond’s extensive catalogue.

Tickets for the show go on sale at 10am on Friday, November 27, via the Scarborough Spa website or by calling the box office on 01723 376774.

Strong demand is expected from fans.

Platinum Lounge upgrades will also be available, offering an enhanced concert experience within the venue.
