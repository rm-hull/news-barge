A famous British name has opened a new store in York.

Known as one of Britain’s most distinguished tailoring houses, Gieves & Hawkes has officially opened its doors at Designer Outlet York.

It brings more than 250 years of British tailoring heritage, craftsmanship and style to the centre.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Gieves-and-Hawkes-ydo.webp)

Established in London in 1771 and located at the iconic No.1 Savile Row, Gieves & Hawkes has dressed generations of distinguished clients and built an international reputation for exceptional craftsmanship, design and enduring British style.

Rooted in a history of military, ceremonial and civilian tailoring, the house continues to draw on its Savile Row expertise for the modern wardrobe, offering everything from refined tailoring and formalwear to elevated everyday essentials.

The new store opened on Saturday, joining the centre’s growing line-up of premium and designer brands.

Guests can discover Gieves & Hawkes’ Autumn/Winter 2026 collection, *Echoes of a Landscape*; inspired by the British countryside, natural fibres and the country’s longstanding textile-making traditions, the collection combines traditional tailoring techniques with a contemporary approach to menswear.

To celebrate its arrival in York, Gieves & Hawkes is offering shoppers an additional 20% off outlet prices until 30 November 2026.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Gieves-and-Hawkes-ydo-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Gieves-and-Hawkes-ydo-4.webp)

Paul Tyler, centre manager at Designer Outlet York, said: “We’re absolutely delighted to welcome Gieves & Hawkes to Designer Outlet York.

“It is an iconic British name with an incredible heritage, and its arrival is a fantastic addition to our premium brand mix. To bring a little of Savile Row to York is really exciting, giving our guests the opportunity to discover more than 250 years of British tailoring expertise and craftsmanship right here at the centre.

“The timing couldn’t be better as we head into our busiest shopping period of the year, and we’re looking forward to seeing the new store become a firm favourite with our guests from York, Yorkshire and further afield.”

The opening comes just ahead of one of the biggest shopping weekends of the year, with Designer Outlet York extending its opening hours across Black Friday weekend to give shoppers even more time to make the most of the offers available across the centre.

On Black Friday, 27 November, and Saturday, 28 November, the centre will open from 8am until 10pm, followed by extended opening from 9.30am until 6.30pm on Sunday, 29 November.
