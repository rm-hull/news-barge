**Since opening in 1996, independent cocktail bar Montey’s has become a beloved part of Harrogate’s evening scene, celebrating three decades of live music, loyal staff and dedicated customers.**

Independent cocktail bar Montey’s in Harrogate is celebrating its 30th anniversary this November, marking three decades since it first opened its doors in 1996. Over the years, Montey’s has become a familiar fixture in Harrogate’s nightlife, known for its rock ‘n’ roll atmosphere, live music events, and a sense of community among generations of customers.

When Montey’s launched nearly 30 years ago, the hospitality landscape was very different. Mobile phones were still a novelty, cocktail menus more modest, and a drink could cost as little as £2.75. Despite such changes, Montey’s remains independently owned and operated, a testament to its resilience in a fluctuating industry.

> **Jay Smith, Montey’s founder, reflected on the journey for The Harrogate Informer:**
>
> I certainly didn’t dare look that far into the future! In those early days, and even the early years, it was very much one month at a time. Keep plugging away, try to grow and, above all, stay afloat!

He emphasised the responsibility felt in providing customers with a memorable night out:

> The sudden realisation that people had worked hard all day and were now trusting you with their leisure money. That’s an enormous honour, but also an enormous responsibility.

Jay also noted the significant evolution of Harrogate’s nightlife scene:

> At last count, over sixty venues have opened in Harrogate since Montey’s first opened its doors. Not all of them survived, but they all left their mark.

He highlighted the strength of the town’s independent businesses:

> What’s great about Harrogate is the sheer number of independents — brilliant bars, pubs, restaurants and cafés with their roots right here in town. National chains may snag the prime locations, but we should be incredibly proud of our local entrepreneurs going toe to toe with them.

However, Jay acknowledged the challenges faced over the years, particularly from financial pressures:

> Small businesses are being taxed to extinction. The combined burden of VAT, Alcohol Duty, National Insurance and business rates is terrifying.  
> We’ve survived recessions, countless competitors and even a pandemic, but the high street needs support from government now, not more taxes.

**For Jay personally, this milestone is very meaningful:**

> Montey’s has been part of my life since I was 25, and I’ve been lucky enough to share that journey with my best pal, Colin. He’s the engine of Montey’s, and we’ve been on one hell of a ride together!  
> There’s something incredibly special about owning a place that means so much to so many people. The friendships, the memories and the generations of customers and staff who’ve made Montey’s their own.  
> Every milestone makes me proud, but this one feels different.  
> Thirty years. I still can’t quite believe it!

> **Colin Speirs, Montey’s first employee and still working at the bar, shared his own reflections:**
>
> My memories of those early days are somewhat hazy! But I remember the excitement of being part of a young, enthusiastic team creating something new, at a time when the hospitality industry was really starting to flourish.

Although some customers might think the bar has not changed over the years, Colin explained the ongoing efforts to keep Montey’s fresh:

> People often walk in and say, “Wow, nothing ever changes in here!” That used to frustrate me because we’re constantly evolving our menus, décor, music and offers. But I eventually realised they meant it still feels the same.  
> Whether you last visited two weeks or two years ago, the welcome, warmth and atmosphere are still there.

Colin pointed to live music nights as some of the most memorable moments, recalling performances from both famous and local acts:

> There have been so many moments when I’ve felt incredibly lucky to be there — and I’ve had the best seat in the house, behind the bar!

He explained what has kept him at Montey’s for 30 years:

> It’s never boring! I feel valued at Montey’s, and there’s something incredibly rewarding about seeing people enjoy what we do.  
> As long as our guests and staff are telling us we’re getting it right, we’ll keep doing what we do best, whilst keeping Montey’s fresh and relevant for future generations.

For Colin, the relationships formed at Montey’s are what make it truly special:

> I’ve experienced pretty much every emotion imaginable within those four walls, and the people I’ve shared them with — customers, staff and even suppliers — are the foundation of who we are.  
> Great drinks, great tunes and a fabulous night out are what we strive for, but it’s the people who make Montey’s special. We work really bloody hard to create that shared experience.  
> The relationships and friendships that have come out of Montey’s over the past thirty years are incredible, and I feel privileged to be part of it.

As Montey’s celebrates this anniversary, it stands not just as a bar but as a significant contributor to Harrogate’s vibrant and evolving nightlife. Its story echoes the dedication of local entrepreneurs and the importance of community in sustaining independent businesses through challenges and changes.

## Anniversary weekend in Harrogate

Montey’s will celebrate its 30th birthday with a weekend of events from Friday 27 to Sunday 29 November.

Josh Ozturk will provide live music on Friday, followed by Desperado and Captain & The Bear on Saturday.

The celebrations will conclude on Sunday with an invitation-only birthday party featuring a surprise band.
