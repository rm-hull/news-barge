# Arrest made after walker killed when touching electrical cable in field

## Detectives investigating the circumstances leading up to the death of a walker in Knottingley have arrested a man on suspicion of manslaughter.

Kevin Walkden 58, died after he came in to contact with a live exposed electricity cable whilst walking in a field near South Moor Lane on November 22, West Yorkshire Police said.

Officers from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have been carrying out enquiries to establish how the cable became exposed and if any crimes have been committed.

A man in his 30s, was arrested on suspicion of manslaughter and has been bailed pending further enquiries.

Enquiries remain ongoing by HMET, who are continuing to appeal for any information around suspicious activity in the area over the last four to six weeks according to police.

There is nothing to support Mr Walkden was involved in any attempt to interfere with, steal or damage the cable.

Anyone with information that could help the investigation is asked to contact police via 101 or use the Live Chat facility at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat quoting 13260663472

Information can always be given anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111
