# Emmerdale legends spotted with Leeds United icon at very special event

## The glam event supported a very good cause

Emmerdale legends and a Leeds United icon were among the famous faces celebrating some very special people.

They were in attendance at the annual Yorkshire Children of Courage Awards (YCCA) as part of an event with more than 600 guests - a record for the festivities. The awards ceremony, which celebrates Yorkshire’s bravest children and supports charities across the region, was held on earlier this month Centenary Pavilion, based at Leeds United’s Elland Road ground, to raise vital funds for charities supporting children and families across the region.

Founded in 2011 by the St James’s Place (SJP) Charitable Foundation, the YCCA’s have become a highlight of Yorkshire’s social calendar. The evening raised an incredible £170,000 thanks to the support and generosity of guests, sponsors, local businesses, and matching from St James’s Place. All monies raised will help the foundation to continue its vital work supporting charities across Yorkshire.

The 2026 awards were hosted by Radio presenter Rich Williams and TV personality Abbie Dewhirst. Guests included boxing champion Josh Warrington, rugby legend Sam Burgess, Leeds United legend Brian Deane and Emmerdale legends Mike Parr and Gaynor Faye.

Entertainment featured a live gospel choir and dancers including the Rhinestones Dance Team before the night closed with a DJ set from Leeds’ very own Radio 1 DJ Danny Mylo.

Ewan Dowes, chair of the Yorkshire Children of Courage Awards committee, said: “Every year, we are humbled by the extraordinary stories of courage and resilience shared at these awards. It is a privilege to celebrate these remarkable young people and recognise the families, carers and charities who support them every step of the way. We are incredibly grateful to everyone who has contributed to this year’s fundraising total. Thanks to the generosity of guests, sponsors, local businesses and supporters, the SJP Charitable Foundation can continue its vital work supporting charities across Yorkshire.”

One of the evening’s most poignant moments was the presentation of the Rob Burrow dare to dream award, which honours the legacy of the much-loved Leeds Rhinos legend and celebrates young people who continue to show extraordinary courage, determination and ambition in the face of adversity. The award was presented by Lindsey Burrow and the Burrow family, who returned to support the awards and celebrate the young people whose stories embody Rob’s remarkable spirit.

Lindsey Burrow said: “Rob always believed in making the most of every moment and never giving up on your dreams. It means so much to our family to be part of these awards and to celebrate such incredible young people. Their courage, positivity and determination are truly inspiring, and we hope the dare to dream award helps them continue to believe in what is possible.”
