## Designer Outlet York welcomes luxury store arrival with 250 years of history

Designer Outlet York has announced a new luxury arrival. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A luxury British tailoring brand has opened a new store in York

Gieves & Hawkes launched at Designer Outlet York, bringing more than 250 years of heritage and craftsmanship to the centre.

Established in 1771 and based at No.1 Savile Row in London, the brand is known for its exceptional tailoring, formalwear and refined menswear.

The York store officially opened on Saturday, November 21, and offers the Autumn/Winter 2026 collection, Echoes of a Landscape.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770838.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The entrance to Gieves and Hawkes *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Paul Tyler, centre manager at Designer Outlet York, said: "We’re absolutely delighted to welcome Gieves & Hawkes to Designer Outlet York.

"It is an iconic British name with an incredible heritage, and its arrival is a fantastic addition to our premium brand mix.

"To bring a little of Savile Row to York is really exciting, giving our guests the opportunity to discover more than 250 years of British tailoring expertise and craftsmanship right here at the centre.

"The timing couldn’t be better as we head into our busiest shopping period of the year, and we’re looking forward to seeing the new store become a firm favourite with our guests from York, Yorkshire and further afield."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770845.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Luxury for more than 250 years. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The collection is inspired by the British countryside, featuring natural fibres and traditional textile techniques reimagined for modern menswear.

To mark the opening, Gieves & Hawkes is offering an extra 20 per cent off outlet prices until November 30, 2026.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770854.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A huge range of clothes are in display *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Designer Outlet York is extending its opening hours for Black Friday weekend to give shoppers more time to enjoy the offers.

The centre will open from 8am to 10pm on Friday, November 27, and Saturday, November 28.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21770853.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The store opened last Saturday *(Image: Pic supplied)*

On Sunday, November 29, doors will open from 9.30am to 6.30pm.

"With a huge range of Black Friday savings available across participating designer and premium brands, alongside Gieves & Hawkes’ opening offer, shoppers can make the most of an extended weekend of Christmas shopping and savings."

Gieves & Hawkes joins a growing list of premium brands at the outlet.
