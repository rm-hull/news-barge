With Spain heading to the polls on Sunday, voters in Madrid are expressing contrasting views about the country's political future.

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez is seeking another term, but faces challenges from conservative Alberto Núñez Feijóo and far-right Vox leader Santiago Abascal.

Among those interviewed, opinions reflect the political divisions running through the country.

The election campaign has been marked by concerns over housing costs and living conditions, as well as debate over immigration.
