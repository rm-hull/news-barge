RHS Garden Harlow Carr has been transformed into a spectacle of light and colour as its popular Glow trail opened for its 10th anniversary season last night.

The festive attraction invites visitors to explore the garden after dark, with illuminated installations, interactive displays and soundscapes along the route.

This year's trail features several new additions, including the first chance to see the garden's champion trees along the Winter Walk illuminated.

A striking neon tree has also been installed beside the Queen Mother’s Lake, while visitors can enjoy views of the Doric Columns from the top of the Main Borders.

The much-loved Bubble Man has returned and will perform every evening throughout the Glow season, filling the garden with giant bubbles that catch the colours of the light displays.

A new installation, Common Ground, has also been created in partnership with Light Night Leeds. The 30-metre illuminated sculpture, by Yorkshire artists Are You Playing Out?, uses sound-reactive LED lights and invites visitors to share their personal connections with nature.

Glow is celebrating its 10th anniversary this year, having become a festive fixture at the Harrogate garden.

Liz Thwaite, head of RHS Garden Harlow Carr, said: “Glow has become a treasured festive tradition for so many families across Yorkshire and beyond, and we're delighted to be celebrating its tenth anniversary this year.

“The garden looks completely different after dark, and visitors can expect an unforgettable experience filled with light, colour, creativity and moments of wonder at every turn.”

Visitors can enjoy festive refreshments along the trail, including bratwurst, jacket potatoes, hot chocolate and mulled wine, as well as the chance to toast marshmallows over an open fire.

There will also be opportunities to browse Christmas gifts from regional craftspeople in the garden's historic Bath House and visit the garden centre for decorations and UK-grown Christmas trees.

A special adults-only Glow Premium Experience will take place on Sunday, December 13, combining the illuminated trail with a hot chocolate, followed by a festive meal at Bettys Café Tea Rooms, live music and a gift bag.

A dedicated SEN evening will be held on Sunday, December 6, offering a quieter experience with reduced noise and fewer sensory distractions. Up to two accompanying carers can attend free of charge.

Glow runs on selected dates until December 30. Tickets must be booked in advance, with prices starting at £9.50 for adult RHS members and £15.25 for non-members.

For details and tickets, visit the RHS website.
