EMERGENCY services were called after a crash close to a North Yorkshire beauty spot.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service says both of its Harrogate crews were called to Summerbridge – close to Brimham Rocks - at 5.32pm on Thursday (November 26) after a crash involving two vehicles.

“Both drivers were out the vehicles on the arrival of the fire service. Crews made the scene safe, disconnected the batteries and washed the road down,” said a service spokesperson.

The incident was left with the police on scene.
