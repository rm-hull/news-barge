The Liberal Democrats have selected NHS nurse Benjamin Feist to stand in Skipton and Ripon at the next general election.

Feist, 35, has worked on the NHS frontline for more than a decade. He is an advanced clinical practitioner in an emergency department and leads training in advanced practice for his NHS trust.

He said improving local health services would be a priority for his campaign, alongside the cost of living and the impact of North Yorkshire Council’s home-to-school transport policy on rural families.

Feist said:

> “I see the challenges facing patients, their families and NHS staff every single day.
>
> "I am determined to use my experience to fight for the improvements to local health services that people across Skipton and Ripon deserve.”

He also pointed to his party’s win in neighbouring Harrogate and Knaresborough.

He said:

> “The Liberal Democrats have shown, by winning the neighbouring Harrogate and Knaresborough constituency, that we can defeat the Conservatives in North Yorkshire.”

Feist began fundraising for children’s hospice care after losing his cousin to a brain tumour in childhood. He raised more than £20,000 and received the Diana Award in 2009.

He went on to carry the Olympic torch in 2012 and provided medical support at the London Olympic and Paralympic Games.
