Police investigating a reported sexual assault at a Nottingham city centre nightclub have released a CCTV image of a man they want to trace.

Nottinghamshire Police said the man, captured on camera on 11 October, could hold vital information regarding the incident at a venue on Talbot Street, a busy nightlife strip in the heart of the city.

Detectives have already reviewed extensive CCTV footage and carried out local inquiries in a bid to piece together what happened that night.

![Police have released a CCTV image of a man they believe can assist their investigation](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/27/17/44/Screenshot-2026-11-27-at-17-27-55.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

PC Sasha Bellaby, of Nottinghamshire Police, said: “This was an extremely upsetting incident for the woman involved and we are determined to track down the person responsible.

“We have reviewed a large amount of CCTV footage and are now asking for the public’s help to identify the person in this image, who we believe can assist the investigation.”

Anyone who recognises the man or has information that could assist the inquiry is urged to contact Nottinghamshire Police on 101, quoting crime reference 26\*615592.

Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
