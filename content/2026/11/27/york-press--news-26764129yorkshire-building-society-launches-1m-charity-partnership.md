A building society has launched a major new charity partnership to support survivors of economic abuse.

Yorkshire Building Society, which has branches in York, Malton, Northallerton and Richmond, has announced a three-year collaboration with Surviving Economic Abuse (SEA), aiming to raise £1 million to help victim-survivors across the UK.

The funds raised through the partnership will support SEA’s peer-to-peer online forum and specialist training for frontline professionals, with a goal to improve outcomes for 125,000 victim-survivors.

Susan Allen, chief executive at Yorkshire Building Society, said: "A good home should be somewhere people feel safe, secure and able to build for the future.

"But for people experiencing economic abuse, their financial security and ability to live independently and safely can be taken away."

The support will enable SEA to assist 5,000 individuals directly and train 10,000 professionals to identify and respond to economic abuse.

Sam Smethers, CEO at Surviving Economic Abuse, said: "The right support at the right time can be life-changing.

"Through our partnership with Yorkshire Building Society, we will be able to reach and support more victim-survivors than ever before by equipping thousands of professionals with the knowledge and resources to recognise and respond to economic abuse."
