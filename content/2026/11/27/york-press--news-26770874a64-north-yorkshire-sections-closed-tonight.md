Several sections of the A64 are due to be closed tonight.

The closures are due to start at 8pm on Friday, November 27, and will continue until 6am the following morning.

The eastbound carriageway between Headley Bar and Tadcaster will be closed for parapet replacement works.

A diversion will be in place via the A64 and A659.

Additionally, the eastbound carriageway from Crambeck to Pickering will also be closed for general cleaning and maintenance works.

The westbound carriageway of the same stretch will also be closed for the same reason.

Diversions will be in place via the local authority.

There will also be a number of slip road closures, including the eastbound Tadcaster entry slip road (A162) and the Musley Bank exit slip road, both from 8pm to 6am.

These closures are part of the ongoing maintenance works on the A64.

As a result of these planned closures, motorists are advised to plan their journeys in advance and to allow for extra time.
