A CAR has crashed into a driving range in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is investigating an incident after a vehicle crashed into a driving range and caused damage before fleeing the scene earlier this week.

The incident took place at Ripon Driving Range on Park Lane, North Stainley, between 9pm and 9.30pm on Monday (November 23).
