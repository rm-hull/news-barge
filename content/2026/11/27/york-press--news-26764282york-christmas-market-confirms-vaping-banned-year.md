Vaping has been banned at one of the UK’s most popular Christmas markets.

Organisers of the York Christmas Market have confirmed that vaping is not allowed this year.

David Phillips, a vaping expert at Vape Superstore, said: "Whilst you can vape outdoors, there are a few caveats about where you can vape.

"You should always consider your surroundings and the people you are with.

"Before you decide to vape, you may wish to ask if others would mind.

"Although vaping is not covered by the same laws as smoking, many public venues and local authorities enforce their own policies, such as York Christmas markets."

Mr Phillips also reminded visitors about restrictions on public transport.

He said: "If you’re planning on taking public transport to or from Christmas markets, be cautious.

"Vaping is strictly prohibited across the UK’s public transport network, including trains, trams, buses, and stations.

"Operators like Network Rail, Great Western Railway, Stagecoach, Arriva, and TfL enforce consistent bans to prioritise passenger comfort and safety.

"While bus passengers may not face fines, they could be asked to stop, disembark, or face bans for repeated violations, with penalties on other services reaching up to £200."
