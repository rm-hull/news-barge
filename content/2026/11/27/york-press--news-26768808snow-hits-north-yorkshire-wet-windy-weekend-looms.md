HIGHER parts of North Yorkshire have seen a dusting of snow this morning (Friday).

Light snow has fallen over Blakey Ridge and Rosedale with temperatures dropping to minus five degrees.

The Met Office has forecast a wet and windy weekend for Malton.

Saturday will start off cold, with temperatures around 2°C at 6am, feeling like 0°C.

The chance of rain is 50 per cent, with winds at 6mph, gusting to 19mph.

Between 7am and 10am, temperatures will rise slightly to 3°C, with a lower rain risk of 20 to 30 per cent.

By 11am, it will feel like -1°C, with a 20 per cent chance of rain.

At midday, the temperature will remain at 3°C, feeling like 0°C, but the rain risk will increase to 30 per cent.

Winds will stay between 5 and 8mph, with gusts strengthening to 24mph.

The best time to be outside will be between 7am and 11am, before the weather worsens.

In the early afternoon, from 1pm to 3pm, the temperature will stay at 3°C, feeling like -1 to 0°C.

The chance of rain will jump to 50 per cent, with winds increasing to 8 to 9mph, and gusts of 25 to 27mph.

By late afternoon, from 4pm to 5pm, the weather will worsen further.

The temperature will rise to 4°C, feeling like 0°C, with a 70 per cent chance of rain at 4pm, increasing to 90 per cent at 5pm.

Winds will reach about 10mph, gusting to 28mph.

The evening, from 6pm to 9pm, will be the worst period of the day.

Temperatures will stay around 4°C, feeling like 0°C, with a 90 per cent chance of rain at 6pm, increasing to at least 95 per cent from 7pm onwards.

Winds will strengthen from 10 to 13mph, with gusts reaching approximately 29 to 31mph.

Late in the evening, from 10pm to 11pm, the temperature will be 5°C, feeling like 1°C, with at least a 95 per cent chance of rain at 10pm, dropping to 90 per cent at 11pm.

Winds will remain strong at 11 to 13mph, gusting around 30 to 31mph.

After 11pm, the temperature will rise to around 6 to 7°C, with feels-like values improving to around 2 to 5°C.

The chance of rain will drop substantially, eventually reaching 10 to 20 per cent.

Winds and gusts will also ease, with gusts falling from around 27mph towards 17mph.

Sunday will start off cold but dry, with temperatures around 3°C, rising to 5°C by 10am.

It will feel like 0 to 2°C, with a rain probability mostly below 5 to 10 per cent.

Winds will be around 7 to 8mph, gusting around 14 to 17mph.

By late morning, conditions will become milder, with temperatures rising to 7°C by midday, feeling like 4°C.

The rain probability will remain at only 10 per cent.

In the afternoon, temperatures will stay around 7°C, feeling like 4 to 5°C, with a rain probability of just 10 per cent.

Winds will reach around 10mph, with gusts peaking at approximately 22mph around early afternoon before easing slightly.

In the evening, the temperature will gently decline from around 7°C to 6°C, feeling like 3 to 4°C.

The rain probability will remain at 10 per cent initially, then drop below 5 per cent later.

Winds will hover around 9 to 10mph, with gusts generally 17 to 20mph.
