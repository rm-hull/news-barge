The owner of a York takeaway says she is frightened by the escalating attacks on her business.

The Dragon House Chinese takeaway on Foxwood Lane has been hit by eggs thrown by youths.

Now a gang of teenagers have thrown stones at and into the business.

The owner, who doesn’t want to be named for security reasons, told ***YorkMix***: “We’ve had eggs thrown at the shop many times before. This week it happened three nights in a row.

“What really worries me is that it’s getting more violent. Last night, as well as throwing eggs, they threw stones into the shop. I’m frightened someone could get hurt.

“All three incidents were reported to the police. Someone in the community called for us on the first night, and I called the police myself on the next two nights.

“Last night I arrived at around 8.45pm and saw two lads who looked about 10 to 14 years old. I called 999 and described them.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/dragon-house-foxwood-lane-york-egging-2-fb.webp)

“I was told officers would come, so I waited at the shop until around 11pm, when the police rang back to say they couldn’t attend.

“I felt really let down. After three nights in a row, I’m worried about what can actually be done to stop this happening again.

“We just want to feel safe at work. We’re really grateful to the neighbours who’ve been looking out for us. Their kindness means a lot to our family.”

She posted on Facebook: “I can wash the eggs off the shopfront. What’s harder to shake is the thought that one of those stones could have hit someone who’d only popped in to collect their tea.

“It’s a horrible feeling to stand behind your own counter and wonder if it’s going to happen again.”

## Targeted before

The Dragon House has been targeted before. In September 2025 it was daubed with racist graffiti, which police treated as a hate crime.

Responding to the egg and stone throwing at the takeaway, North Yorkshire Police said they have received two reports of eggs being thrown at the property and a third report of stones being thrown.

The first report involving eggs was made by a member of the public at around 9.50pm on Sunday (22 November).

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/york-foxwood-estate-police-patrols-1000.jpg)

The second was received from the business at around 7.20pm on Monday (23 November) when around four or five youths, believed to be in their mid-teens and dressed in dark coloured clothing threw eggs at the window.

A third report was received at around 8.50pm on Wednesday (25 November) when a group of youths threw stones at the window.

Officers are carrying out CCTV and local enquiries and are urging anyone who may have captured the incidents on dashcam, CCTV or doorbell cameras and to anyone who witnessed the incidents or has any information that could help identify the suspects, to get in touch.

A police spokesperson said: “This is a completely unacceptable targeting of a local business that provides services to the community.

“We urge anyone with information about those responsible to come forward.

“The suspects as described as youths in their early to mid-teens and we urge all parents in the area to ensure they know where their children are at night and what they are doing.

“Additional high-visibility patrols will be deployed to the area.”
