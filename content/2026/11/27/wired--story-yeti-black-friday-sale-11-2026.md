Everyone loves Yeti. Its coolers and insulated cups are built to last and keep beers ice cold, even on the hottest days. They cost a bit more than the run-of-the-mill coolers you’ll find at a gas station, but they’re made to last a lifetime, and discounts are rare. Yeti products hardly ever go on sale, so when they do, they tend to sell out quickly. Below are several rare Yeti discounts live right now on WIRED-tested Yeti items we loved. Grab ’em before they're gone.

For more discounts on more WIRED-tech gadgets, check out our roundup of the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals.

## Our Top Pick
