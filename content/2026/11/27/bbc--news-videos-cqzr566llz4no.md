# Student recalls moment passenger stepped in during Huntingdon attack

A student who survived the Huntingdon train attack has said one of the passengers who was stabbed "saved her life" when he ran towards attacker Anthony Williams, giving her time to escape.

On Thursday, Williams was found guilty of 14 attempted murders, including trying to kill Stephen Crean.

Astrid told the BBC Stephen's actions had allowed her to reach safety in the buffet car.

The BBC's Lucy Manning spoke to them after the verdicts, as they met for the first time since the attack.
