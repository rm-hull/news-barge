An award-winning Yorkshire fish and chip shop has been praised for its “iconic” spice bags.

If you are looking for great fish and chips, you might want to pop over to Millers Fish and Chips, which can be found in York’s Shambles or just a short drive away in Haxby.

While the shop offers incredible fish and chips, many people love its Irish spice bags that are “well worth a go”.

## What is Millers Fish and Chips like?

Millers Fish and Chips has been run by three generations of Millers and has racked up awards over the years.

The fish and chip shop has a range of classic dishes such as cod, haddock, and Whitby scampi.

However, the spot is known for its Irish spice bags that come with chips, fried chicken, onions, and bell peppers that are all coated in salt and pepper seasoning.

The fish and chip shop was visited by content creator The Yorkshireman, who praised the shop for its “massive” portions and variety.

He said: “One of our favourite parts is their iconic spice bags and their incredible fried chicken.

“Think whoppers of chicken strips with their very own homemade sauces.”

Alongside the chicken, those who do not like fish can have either a plain or battered sausage.

There are plenty of sides to add on as well, from baked beans and mushy peas to scraps and bread buns.

You can get a range of homemade sauces as well, including Mills sauce, buttermilk ranch, sweet barbecue, and sriracha mayo.

### What do reviews say about Millers Fish and Chips?

Millers Fish and Chips has a rating of 4.6 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with over 550 people leaving reviews.

One person said: “Well deserved 5 star rating, sublime fish and chips.

“First time I have visited this chippie, but it won’t be the last.

“I ordered haddock, chips and mushy peas to takeaway.

“My haddock was cooked perfectly, batter crisp and very tasty, as was the fish, chips well proportioned and again cooked perfectly - none of the hard, toenail like offerings served by some local chippies!

“I also had mushy peas and these were also delicious and tasted spot on.

“So many fish and chip establishments disappoint, not Millers.”

Another person said: “It’s seriously a better spice bag than I've had on the Emerald Isle!

“Well worth a go, with or without the Irish curry sauce on top.”
