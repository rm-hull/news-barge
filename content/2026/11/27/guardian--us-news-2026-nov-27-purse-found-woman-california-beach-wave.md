The purse of a 24-year-old who was swept into the ocean at a notorious California beach three weeks ago was found on Wednesday, as the west coast saw record high king tides.

The Santa Cruz county sheriff’s office confirmed that Se Nah Park’s purse was found by a beachgoer at 4 Mile Beach, south of Panther Beach, where she was swept away into the ocean on 8 November.

According to a GoFundMe started by Park’s friends, Park and five others had gathered around a bonfire beyond the “keyhole” archway at Panther Beach that night.

Four members of the group, including Park, attempted to make their way back through the keyhole when a powerful wave struck, sweeping Park into the ocean.

The three individuals managed to get back up the cliffslide, but tragically, Park did not.

Three weeks later, Park’s body has not been located yet, but the discovery of her purse may lead her community closer towards locating her body. .

The coast guard suspended the active search efforts to find Park on 9 November.

“Given the extensive search effort of the surrounding area and environmental conditions, we have suspended active search efforts pending further developments,” Lt Max Holloway, Sector San Francisco search and rescue mission coordinator, said at the time.

“We worked closely with our partner agencies and exhausted all available resources in our attempt to locate Ms Park. Our deepest condolences are with her family and loved ones,” the statement concluded.

In the weeks since, Park’s family and friends have not given up on their search efforts.

Her family and friends continue to “hold on to the hope that Senah can be found and brought home to rest”, her friends shared in a message on GoFundMe.
