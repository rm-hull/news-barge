An elderly woman has been accused of defying the season of goodwill after appearing to cut down her neighbour’s Christmas lights.

CCTV footage showed her approaching a property on a mobility scooter brandishing scissors.

After several seconds, the Christmas lights at Beth Wilson-Croft’s house in Middlesbrough, North Yorkshire, which is surrounded by lights with an inflatable Father Christmas and Rudolph on the roof and two snowmen in the garden, went out.

The woman then fled the scene on her scooter at about 11pm on Wednesday.

Wilson-Croft said most of her neighbours enjoyed her festive display and she had never seen the alleged perpetrator before, the Daily Mail reported.

![A house decorated with festive lights and large inflatable Christmas figures behind a fence at night](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/31002f7557ad88f90058fb5bc878bbf4f6900edd/4_0_844_675/master/844.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

The 31-year-old said she had reported the vandalism to the police.

In an effort to find the woman, Wilson-Croft posted the footage online and wrote: “If you don’t like my Christmas decorations that’s an absolute shame for you because they’ll be back up today alongside a singing Santa and a snow machine and anything else I can get my hands on.”

After seeing the video of the incident, a man offered to help and fixed the lights by soldering them back together.

Wilson-Croft, who has a six-year-old child, has subsequently shared photographs showing her lights restored.

The footage triggered outrage from neighbours who accused the perpetrator of flouting the festive spirit and described her as a “Grinch” and a “Scrooge”.

The family’s seasonal decorations draw visitors from around the area.
