The free York Ice Trail will return next year with more than 30 ice sculptures across the city.

Organised by Make It York, the event runs from 10.30am to 4pm on on Saturday 6 and Sunday 7 February 2027, under this year’s theme, ‘Legends Unleashed’.

A new interactive launch sculpture will appear on Parliament Street on Saturday 6 February only. Visitors can explore it and take photographs before heading to the main trail.

A new hero sculpture, made from eight large blocks of ice, will be a centrepiece of the trail.

For the first time, a Saturday Night Market will be held at Shambles Market, extending the event into the evening of Saturday 6 February.

Titled ‘Legends of the Shambles’, it will combine ice sculptures with York’s artisan makers, market traders, food and drink.

The popular ice carving sessions return with more places.

Run by professional ice sculptors Icebox, they let participants carve their own polar bear, with no previous experience needed.

Each session costs £48 for up to two participants, with additional children’s tickets at £12. Spaces are limited, so visitors are encouraged to book in advance.

Icebox will also carve a live ‘Ice Watcher of York’ in St Sampson’s Square each day from 12pm. Three photo installations return: Snow Block, The Ice Wall and Ice Ice Baby Neon.

Trail maps are available from the Visit York Visitor Information Centre at 28 Back Swinegate, and online from 1 February.

The new York Ice Trail pen is also available from the centre, and visitors who complete the trail can collect a commemorative sticker.
