Police are hunting a attempt who tried to fire a crossbow bolt towards a PSNI station in Belfast.

Detectives have launched an appeal for information following sightings of a male carrying a crossbow in the city's Durham Street area during the early hours of Friday morning.

The PSNI stated that officers were alerted at roughly 2.40am to reports of a man brandishing the weapon.

A spokesperson said: “Armed response units were deployed alongside local policing officers, however the suspect had fled the area prior to their arrival.

“The suspect is described as approximately 5ft 8in tall, dressed all in black and had dark hair.

“The crossbow has since been recovered along with a crossbow bolt and a face covering.

“It would appear an attempt was made to discharge the bolt at the police station on Grosvenor Road.”

The PSNI said it treats all reports of this type “extremely seriously in order to protect the public and our officers”.

The spokesperson added: “When such reports are received, officers must respond quickly and take appropriate action to ensure that any potential threat is safely contained.

“Such actions cause fear within the community and present significant risks.

“Thankfully on this occasion there were no injuries, however this was a reckless act using a weapon that could have injured either a police officer or a member of the public.”
