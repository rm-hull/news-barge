## York festive pop-up returns with Christmas cards and gifts for charity

The Cards for Good Causes charity Christmas shop is now open in York at the Church of St Martin le Grand *(Image: Cards for Good Causes)*

A festive pop-up is inviting shoppers to support good causes while preparing for the season.

The Cards for Good Causes charity Christmas shop is now open in York at the Church of St Martin le Grand on Coney Street.

The shop offers a wide selection of charity Christmas cards, Advent calendars, festive gift wrap, decorations and gifts.

A shop manager said: "We're delighted to welcome shoppers back to our pop-up Christmas shop in St Martin's.

"Our Christmas cards and Advent calendars are proving particularly popular, so we'd encourage everyone to pop in soon and pick out their favourites while stocks last.

"There's plenty more to discover too, from festive gift wrap and decorations to gifts for family and friends.

"Every purchase helps support some fantastic charities and good causes, and we'd love to see you."

All proceeds from sales go directly to support charities and good causes.

The shop also offers a Braille printing facility, allowing customers to print their festive greetings in Braille.

Cards for Good Causes is encouraging shoppers to 'Make Christmas Mean More' by supporting its charitable efforts through everyday festive purchases.

The York shop is open daily from 10am to 4pm, and the full range is available online at cardsforcharity.co.uk.
