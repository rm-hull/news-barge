The scores are in — the latest batch of food hygiene ratings for York restaurants, cafes, takeaways and businesses have been revealed.

Based on guidelines set by the Food Standards Agency, inspectors look to see how clean the facilities are; what food safety management procedures are in place and how hygienic food handling is.

Ratings are then dished out from five to zero — with the higher score the better the result.

### Five — Very Good

Hutchison Catering at Park Grove Primary School in Park Grove, rated 5 on 10 November 2026.

Impact Foods @ Ralph Butterfield Primary School in Station Road, Haxby, rated 5 on 10 November 2026.

Hutchison Catering at St Mary's CE Primary in School Lane, Askham Richard, rated 5 on 5 November 2026.

Acomb Primary School in West Bank, rated 5 on 5 November 2026.

Vintage Rose in College Street, rated 5 on 4 November 2026.

Haxby Bakehouse in Ryedale Court, The Village, Haxby, rated 5 on 3 November 2026.

Totally Caked in Golden Grove Cottage, Sheriff Hutton Road, Strensall, rated 5 on 3 November 2026.

Businesses may have been reinspected since then, and this will be reflected in our next round-up.
