## North Yorkshire pub wins Best Community Pub Operator award for second year running

The awards night *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A North Yorkshire pub has been named one of the best in the country.

The Black Swan in Ripon has been awarded 'Best Community Pub Operator' for the second year running at the 2026 Craft Union Operator Awards.

Run by Sara and Kelly Hirst, the pub was praised for its community spirit, support for residents and creating a welcoming environment at the heart of Ripon.

The judges highlighted their "passion, dedication and genuine care" for customers, as well as their efforts to engage and connect people through new ideas and events.

Sara and Kelly said: "We are so pleased to have won this award for the second year running.

"Community has always been at the heart of everything we do and we're incredibly proud that people choose to spend their time with us, whether that's supporting local events, attending Ripon Pride, joining our activities or just popping in for a chat.

"This award really belongs to our customers and the wider Ripon community.

"The support they've shown us over the years has helped make the Black Swan what it is today, and we're excited to continue finding new ways to bring people together."

The Hirsts have been recognised for a range of community initiatives, including a weekly walking group that was featured on ITV’s Lorraine and the founding of Ripon Pride, now a major annual event.

![The awards night](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21773273.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The awards night *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Judges were particularly impressed by evidence of the real impact the pub has had on local people, including first-hand accounts from guests about the important role the Black Swan plays in the community.

Frazer Grimbleby, managing director of Craft Union Pub Company, said: "Winning Best Community Pub Operator once again is a tremendous achievement.

"To win it two years in a row is something truly special and reflects the incredible impact Sara and Kirsty continue to have on their community.

"They embody everything this award stands for.

"Their dedication to bringing people together, creating meaningful connections and ensuring the Black Swan remains a welcoming pub for everyone makes them outstanding ambassadors for both their community and Craft Union.

"I’m incredibly proud of everything they have achieved."

The annual awards, organised by Craft Union Pub Company, celebrate exceptional pub operators from Craft Union pubs across the UK who go above and beyond for their guests and local communities.
