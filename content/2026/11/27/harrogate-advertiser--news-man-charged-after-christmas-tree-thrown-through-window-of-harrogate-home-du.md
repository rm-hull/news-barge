A 36-year-old man has been charged after allegedly throwing a Christmas tree through the window of a Harrogate home before assaulting an occupant and stealing items from inside.

Police were called to Ashfield Terrace in the early hours of Wednesday, November 25, following reports of an incident.

Following enquiries, officers arrested a 36-year-old man, who has since been charged with burglary and assault.

The defendant appeared before York Magistrates' Court on Thursday, November 26, where he was remanded into custody.

He is due to appear before York Crown Court on December 21.
