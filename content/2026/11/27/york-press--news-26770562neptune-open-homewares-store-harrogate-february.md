A new home and lifestyle showroom is opening its doors in the heart of a North Yorkshire town.

Neptune, a British interiors brand, will launch its latest store on Princes Street in Harrogate on February 11, 2027, where ProCook used to be.

The 3,520 sq ft, two-storey showroom will feature a curated selection of lighting, tableware, textiles, and decorative accessories, alongside the brand’s first immersive Kitchen Lab.




Michael Hope, regional head of retail at Neptune, said: "Following on from our Fenwick concession that opened earlier this year in October, Neptune Harrogate is the next step in our Northern expansion journey, bringing to life our promise to invest in this key region.

"Home to our new Kitchen Lab concept, the showroom will give customers a chance to experience Neptune in a fresh way. The opening reflects our continued belief in creating inspiring physical spaces and our commitment to bringing Neptune closer to new and existing customers."

The Kitchen Lab will allow customers to visualise kitchen designs before they are built, offering a real sense of scale, layout, and flow.

Shoppers will work with Neptune’s in-house design team to refine every detail of their kitchen projects, from cabinetry and colours to finishes and materials.

The store will also offer Neptune’s whole-home design service, with interior room sets for living, dining, bedroom, and kitchen spaces.

Throughout the year, the showroom will host events in partnership with local businesses and brands.

Neptune Harrogate joins the brand’s other northern locations in Malton Road, York, in the former Tanglewood public house; Knutsford, Southport, Chester, and Newcastle (within Fenwick).

The showroom will officially open to the public on February 11, 2027 at 7–11 Princes Street, Harrogate.
