Whether you're planning magical family days out, searching for the best Christmas markets, or looking to get into the festive spirit with seasonal shows and light displays, there's plenty happening across the Harrogate district this December.

From Harrogate and Knaresborough to Ripon, Wetherby, Nidderdale and beyond, we've rounded up some of the biggest and best festive events taking place in the run-up to Christmas.

So wrap up warm, grab a hot chocolate, and discover what's on near you this festive season with our ultimate guide to Christmas events across the district...

**Harrogate Christmas Market**

The Harrogate Christmas Market, organised by Harrogate Business Improvement District (BID), will bring a selection of independent traders, street food vendors and artisan producers to Cambridge Street, offering visitors the chance to browse for Christmas gifts, seasonal treats and handmade goods.

The market will run over three festive weekends:

**Weekend One:** Friday 4 December to Sunday 6 December**Weekend Two:** Friday 11 December to Sunday 13 December**Weekend Three:** Friday 18 December and Saturday 19 December

Opening hours will be:

**Fridays:** 10am to 5pm**Saturdays:** 9am to 5pm**Sundays:** 10am to 5pm

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said: “We're delighted to welcome the Harrogate Christmas Market back to the town centre this December."

"The market will offer a fantastic mix of festive food, gifts and artisan products, creating a vibrant atmosphere for residents and visitors to enjoy while supporting traders and local businesses during the festive season."

**Little Bird Artisan Christmas Market**

Little Bird Markets will return to the Valley Gardens with its popular festive artisan market on Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December, from 10am till 3pm.

Browse a wide range of stalls from independent makers and small businesses selling everything from handmade gifts, jewellery and artwork to homeware, festive decorations and seasonal treats.

Set against the picturesque backdrop of Valley Gardens, the market is the perfect place to pick up unique Christmas presents while supporting local and independent businesses.

**Harrogate Ice Pavilion**

The Harrogate Ice Pavilion is set to bring festive fun to the Valley Gardens with a covered real ice rink for skaters of all ages.

Open from Thursday 17 December till Sunday 3 January, the attraction will offer public skating sessions, family and toddler skates, disco evenings and learn-to-skate experiences, making it an ideal day out for families, couples and groups of friends.

The covered rink means the fun can continue whatever the winter weather.

**Christmas at Valley Gardens**

A brand-new festive marquee is coming to the Valley Gardens this December, bringing a packed programme of entertainment in the run-up to Christmas.

Running from 11 to 24 December, the marquee will host Christmas parties, live tribute acts, stand-up comedy and family-friendly shows.

Visitors can also enjoy street food stalls and festive drinks from the on-site bar, while children's entertainment will include magic shows, comedy, a Christmas trail and superhero-themed events.

Paul Kennedy, Director of Zoom Events, the company behind the new festive attractions, said: “We're really looking forward to bringing something a little different to Valley Gardens this Christmas.

“We've put together a varied programme of entertainment so there's something for all ages to enjoy in the run-up to Christmas.”

Located alongside the Harrogate Ice Pavilion, it's set to create a lively festive hub in the heart of the town centre this Christmas.

**RHS Glow at RHS Garden Harlow Carr**

RHS Garden Harlow Carr will be transformed into a magical winter wonderland this Christmas as its popular Glow light trail returns for another year.

Visitors can explore the gardens after dark, following a spectacular illuminated trail featuring colourful light installations, glowing displays and immersive experiences set among the garden's trees, landscapes and waterways.

This year's event will also include brand-new installations, including a 30-metre illuminated sculpture created in collaboration with Light Night Leeds.

Alongside the trail, guests can enjoy festive food and drink, warm up by fire pits with toasted marshmallows, and browse Christmas gifts at the garden shop.

Running on selected evenings from November 26 to December 30, Glow offers a memorable festive night out for families, couples and visitors of all ages.

For more information and to book, visit [https://www.rhs.org.uk/gardens/harlow-carr/whats-on/harlow-carr-glow](https://www.rhs.org.uk/gardens/harlow-carr/whats-on/harlow-carr-glow)

**Santa’s Polar Kingdom**

A brand-new festive experience is coming to the Great Yorkshire Events Centre this Christmas, with Santa’s Polar Kingdom bringing a touch of North Pole magic from 11 to 23 December.

Families can step into a magical festive world featuring the Elf Forest Light Trail, Santa’s Workshop, the North Pole Post Office and the Ice Kingdom.

Visitors can also enjoy a Christmas market, festive food and drink, live entertainment, a fun fair and the chance to hop aboard the Santa Express for a journey through the Polar Kingdom.

With plenty to explore for all ages, the experience promises to bring a new highlight to Harrogate’s Christmas calendar, offering families the chance to make festive memories without having to travel far.

**Knaresborough Christmas Market**

The Knaresborough Christmas Market returns this December, bringing a traditional Yorkshire festive atmosphere to the town's historic Market Place on Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December.

Set around the town's Christmas tree, the market will feature almost 80 stalls selling seasonal gifts, handmade crafts, decorations, jewellery, homeware and locally produced food and drink.

Visitors can soak up the festive atmosphere with live performances from local choirs, brass bands and dancers, while enjoying mulled wine and browsing the town's independent shops.

The weekend will come to a spectacular close on Sunday evening with a fireworks finale overlooking the famous viaduct and River Nidd.

Organised by volunteers from local community groups including the Rotary and Lions Clubs and Knaresborough & District Chamber, the event aims to celebrate the town’s independent businesses while supporting local charities and organisations.

Bill Taylor, Chair of Knaresborough Christmas Market, said: “Last year’s market was a huge success and we were thrilled with the feedback from both traders and visitors.

“The 2026 market promises to be even bigger and there has been a really high demand from traders.

“Whilst a lot of our visitor favourites will be returning again this year, we’ve worked hard to attract some new stallholders too.”

**Magical Christmas Experience at Mother Shipton’s Cave**

Mother Shipton’s Cave will be transformed into a festive wonderland this Christmas, with its Magical Christmas Experience returning from 28 November till Christmas Eve.

Families can follow a trail of twinkling woodland lights into an enchanted Christmas Village, where children can meet Santa in his cosy grotto and enjoy festive activities including storytelling, making reindeer food, sending wishes to Santa through the Snowy Owl Sorting Office and hanging messages on the Christmas wishing trees.

Visitors can also explore the magical woodland surroundings, discover the famous Cave and Petrifying Well, and enjoy seasonal treats including hot chocolate, mince pies and toasted marshmallows while soaking up the festive atmosphere.

With its mix of Christmas magic, history and family-friendly activities, it’s set to be a popular addition to Knaresborough’s festive calendar.

**Knaresborough Christmas Tree Festival**

St John’s Church in the heart of Knaresborough will be filled with festive colour as the annual Christmas Tree Festival returns from Saturday 5 to Sunday 20 December.

The popular community event will see dozens of Christmas trees decorated in creative and imaginative designs by local groups and businesses, transforming the historic church into a festive display for visitors to enjoy.

Entry to the festival is free, with visitors able to browse the displays, enjoy seasonal refreshments and soak up the Christmas atmosphere.

**Ripon Tinsel Tractor Run**

The Ripon Tinsel Tractor Run returns this December, bringing a spectacular convoy of decorated tractors to the streets and villages around the city in support of Yorkshire Cancer Research.

Taking place on Saturday 5 December, the festive procession will see tractors dressed with Christmas lights and decorations set off from Tates Garden Centre before travelling through Ripon, Bishop Monkton, Roecliffe, Boroughbridge and surrounding villages, finishing in Ripon Market Place.

Visitors can line the route to watch the colourful convoy pass by, or head into Ripon to see the tractors arrive in the Market Place.

The event has become a much-loved community tradition, raising money for Yorkshire Cancer Research while bringing plenty of festive cheer to the area.

**Carols around the Christmas Tree**

Get into the festive spirit with Carols Around the Christmas Tree at Fountains Abbey & Studley Royal, with the popular event taking place on Sunday 6, Sunday 13 and Sunday 20 December.

Set in the courtyard, visitors can gather around the glittering Christmas tree and sing along to a selection of traditional Christmas carols.

Guests can enjoy live performances from local choirs while soaking up the atmosphere of this beautiful historic setting, with hot drinks and festive treats available to enjoy during the event.

For more information, visit [https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/yorkshire/fountains-abbey-and-studley-royal-water-garden/events/a16d6872-4c29-49f8-9c9c-af2e39685f2a](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/yorkshire/fountains-abbey-and-studley-royal-water-garden/events/a16d6872-4c29-49f8-9c9c-af2e39685f2a)

**Illuminated Winter Wonderland**

Ripon Spa Gardens will be transformed into a festive wonderland this December as the annual Illuminated Winter Wonderland event returns from Friday 18 to Sunday 20 December.

Visitors can enjoy a magical weekend of seasonal entertainment, with sparkling lights, music, hot drinks and snowball throwing adding to the Christmas atmosphere.

The free event offers a chance for families and visitors to explore the gardens, soak up the festive surroundings and enjoy refreshments while taking in the winter scenes.

**Christmas Carol Concert at Ripon Cathedral**

Ripon Cathedral will host a spectacular Christmas Carol Concert this December, bringing together the renowned Ripon Cathedral Choir and St Cecilia Orchestra for an evening of festive music on Sunday 20 December.

The seasonal concert will feature much-loved Christmas carols alongside selections from Handel’s *Messiah*, with the second half inviting audiences to join in with a collection of festive favourites.

Led by Director of Music Ronny Krippner, the event celebrates Ripon’s rich musical tradition in the stunning surroundings of the cathedral.

With its beautiful setting and traditional Christmas atmosphere, the concert offers a memorable way to celebrate the festive season and enjoy an evening of live music in the heart of Ripon.

For more information and to book, visit [https://riponcathedral.org.uk/events/christmas-carol-concert-featuring-the-ripon-cathedral-choir-st-cecilia-orchestra/?occurrence=2026-12-20](https://riponcathedral.org.uk/events/christmas-carol-concert-featuring-the-ripon-cathedral-choir-st-cecilia-orchestra/?occurrence=2026-12-20)

**Christmas Light Switch On**

Wetherby will officially welcome the festive season when its annual Christmas Lights Switch On returns to the town centre on Saturday 5 December.

The evening will feature a packed programme of festive entertainment, including live performances, a lantern parade, Christmas activities, and the chance to see the town’s lights illuminated for the season.

The celebrations will also include the announcement of the Jubilee Awards and Christmas Window Dressing Awards, recognising the contributions of local people and businesses.

The lights switch-on will be followed by a community carol service, bringing residents and visitors together to enjoy traditional Christmas music in the heart of Wetherby.

A spokesperson for Wetherby Town Council, said: “We have a packed schedule with lots of activities, entertainment and photo opportunities.

“We are thrilled to be holding the Shop Window Dressing Awards and Jubilee Awards on the same evening so we can celebrate our wonderful businesses and young people as a community.”

**The Christmas Adventure at Stockeld Park**

Stockeld Park will be transformed into a magical winter wonderland this Christmas, with its festive celebrations running from 21 November to 24 December.

Families can enjoy a full day of Christmas activities, including a magical Santa’s Grotto experience, a festive theatre show, winter illuminations through the Enchanted Forest, ice skating and the Snowflake Maze.

As darkness falls, the Enchanted Forest comes alive with dazzling lights, projections and soundscapes, creating a spectacular festive trail for visitors of all ages.

**Dickensian Christmas Market**

The Dickensian Christmas Market returns this December, bringing a traditional festive atmosphere to the town centre on Sunday 13 December.

The popular market will feature a mix of indoor and outdoor stalls, with artisan gifts, handmade crafts, seasonal treats and unique Christmas presents on offer.

Visitors can enjoy festive music from local choirs and performers, browse the stalls and soak up the Christmas atmosphere, while families can meet Santa and have their photo taken on his sleigh.

Organised by Wetherby District Lions Club, the event also supports local community causes, making it a chance to enjoy a festive day out while helping to raise funds for good causes.

**Christmas Jumper Raceday**

Wetherby Racecourse will get into the festive spirit with its Christmas Jumper Raceday taking place on Saturday 5 December.

Visitors can enjoy an afternoon of thrilling racing alongside plenty of seasonal entertainment, with racegoers encouraged to don their best Christmas jumpers and embrace the festive atmosphere.

Gates open at 10am, with the first race due to take place at 12.18pm.

For more information and to book, visit [https://www.wetherbyracing.co.uk/events/christmas-jumper-raceday/](https://www.wetherbyracing.co.uk/events/christmas-jumper-raceday/)

**Nidderdale Christmas Festival**

Pateley Bridge will be filled with festive cheer as the Nidderdale Christmas Weekend returns from Friday 4 to Sunday 6 December, bringing three days of seasonal celebrations to the town.

The weekend will begin with a lantern parade and Christmas tree lighting on Friday evening, followed by a packed programme of festive activities including artisan markets, late-night shopping, live music and family entertainment.

Visitors can browse independent stalls, discover handmade gifts and enjoy the traditional Christmas atmosphere throughout the town.

Across the weekend, visitors can also enjoy street performances, a festive treasure trail, community events and performances of A Christmas Carol around Pateley Bridge.

Julie Stead-Connor, of Nidderdale Business Association, said: “Knowing how ‘tight’ it is for everyone at the moment - we are trying to keep the festival affordable, so many of the venues and activities are either free or very low cost.

“It’s another way that our businesses and community organisations are helping to support trade across the Dale and our community.”

With its mix of local makers, entertainment and seasonal activities, the Nidderdale Christmas Weekend offers a charming Yorkshire Christmas experience in the heart of the Dales.

**Magical Christmas Experience at Birchfield Ice Cream Farm**

Birchfield Farm in Nidderdale will be filled with festive magic this Christmas as its Magical Christmas Experience takes place on selected dates until Tuesday 22 December.

Visitors can enjoy a magical sleigh ride through the farm before arriving at Father Christmas’ woodland grotto, where children can share their Christmas wishes.

The experience also includes meeting friendly elves, festive crafts, ginger biscuit decorating, writing letters to Santa and posting them in the giant post box.

Families can also enjoy the farm’s festive atmosphere, with the chance to explore the Christmas activities and warm up with seasonal treats during their visit.

A new addition for 2026 is the option to toast marshmallows and create festive s’mores, adding another special touch to the Christmas experience.
