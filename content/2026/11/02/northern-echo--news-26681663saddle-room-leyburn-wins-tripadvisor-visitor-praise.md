A restaurant tucked away inside converted racing stables in North Yorkshire has won one of Tripadvisor's top awards for 2026, after attracting more than 1,400 customer reviews.

The Saddle Room Restaurant is situated at Tupgill Park, near Leyburn, and combines traditional Yorkshire food with an unusual countryside setting.

Despite being located away from a major town centre, the restaurant has built a substantial reputation among visitors.

The restaurant has also received a Travellers' Choice Best of the Best award for 2026, one of the review platform's highest distinctions.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646858.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Saddle Room Restaurant is situated at Tupgill Park, near Leyburn *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

However, it is the combination of food, setting and the building's unusual history which helps make The Saddle Room stand out.

One of its most distinctive features is its collection of stable booths, which provide an unusual setting for customers enjoying a meal.

The venue also includes a bar, outdoor courtyard and spaces used for private dining.

The restaurant describes its approach as "Grand Honest Food", with an emphasis on traditional dishes and locally sourced ingredients.

According to its Tripadvisor listing, much of its produce is sourced from within a ten-mile radius.

The menu focuses on British and Yorkshire food, with Sunday lunches forming an important part of its offering.

And recent reviews suggest the food is proving popular with customers.

One reviewer praised the choice of dishes and the restaurant's dog-friendly atmosphere.

The customer was particularly impressed by the fillet steak, describing it as "the best I've ever had".

Another visitor, who ate at the restaurant in September, praised the beef and cauliflower soup.

The reviewer described the food as "exceptional" and said the service had been quick and polite.

A customer celebrating a family birthday in August was similarly impressed.

The reviewer said the food and drinks were outstanding, while praising the warm service and comfortable surroundings.

The Saddle Room is situated near to The Forbidden Corner, one of North Yorkshire's most unusual visitor attractions.

The attraction is known for its maze of tunnels, chambers, passages and unexpected features, drawing families and visitors to the Tupgill Park Estate.

Customers can visit the restaurant separately without needing admission to The Forbidden Corner.

The estate also provides accommodation, allowing visitors to extend their stay in the surrounding countryside.

Nearby Middleham is known for its horse-racing connections, while Leyburn offers independent shops and access to Wensleydale.

The Saddle Room Restaurant is located at Tupgill Park, Coverham, near Leyburn, DL8 4TJ.
