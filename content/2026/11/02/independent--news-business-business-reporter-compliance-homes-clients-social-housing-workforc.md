*McIntyre Compliance Services is a Business Reporter client*

In the highly regulated and ever-evolving world of social housing, compliance is far more than a statutory requirement. It is a critical responsibility that directly affects the safety, wellbeing and trust of residents and communities.

McIntyre Compliance Services has established itself as a trusted nationwide life safety compliance partner, operating at the forefront of the UK social housing sector to ensure homes and communal environments remain safe, resilient and fully compliant with current and emerging legislation.

Working in close collaboration with housing associations, local authorities and property managers, McIntyre Compliance Services provides the assurance that essential safety systems are not only installed and maintained correctly, but are robustly managed over the long term. Its work supports strong governance, reduces risk exposure and helps clients meet increasingly complex regulatory obligations with confidence.

## **Simplifying compliance in a complex regulatory landscape**

The regulatory environment governing social housing continues to grow in complexity, with heightened scrutiny of electrical safety, fire safety, building risk management and resident protection. McIntyre Compliance Services responds to this challenge with a fully integrated service model designed to simplify compliance without compromising quality.

By uniting electrical, fire, passive fire and security compliance under one co-ordinated delivery structure, the company offers clients a genuinely seamless end-to-end solution. From initial inspection, testing and surveying through to remedial works, ongoing maintenance and full system installations, every stage of the compliance lifecycle is carefully managed and documented.

This integrated approach eliminates the inefficiencies and risks associated with managing multiple contractors, ensuring consistency of standards, clearer accountability and greater visibility across entire property portfolios. Clear reporting, structured data capture and accessible audit trails empower clients to take a proactive, portfolio-wide approach to compliance and risk management – supporting both operational delivery and strategic decision-making.

## **National scale, local responsiveness**

Operating at significant scale, McIntyre Compliance Services is recognised as one of the UK’s largest compliance contractors working within the housing sector. Each year the organisation completes more than 50,000 electrical and fire safety visits, supporting clients with both routine compliance activity and urgent reactive works.

This national capability is underpinned by a highly skilled workforce of over 230 qualified engineers and support staff, enabling consistent service delivery across the UK. The business operates on a 24/7/365 model, ensuring that safety-critical issues can be addressed swiftly, regardless of location or timing. For residents, this translates into a professional, respectful and resident-focused service that prioritises clear communication, minimal disruption and safety at every touchpoint.

Despite its scale, McIntyre Compliance Services remains acutely aware of the importance of local knowledge, responsiveness and trust. Its operational model balances national coverage with regionally deployed teams, allowing for rapid attendance, strong relationship management and a deep understanding of local housing challenges.

## **Quality, competence and independent assurance**

Quality and assurance are embedded throughout every aspect of McIntyre Compliance Services’ operations. The organisation holds accreditations from leading industry bodies including NICEIC, BAFE and SSAIB, reflecting independently assessed competence across electrical, fire, security and passive fire disciplines.

These accreditations provide clients with tangible assurance that all works are delivered in line with recognised industry standards, best practice and legislative requirements. They also demonstrate McIntyre’s commitment to continuous improvement, rigorous training and the maintenance of consistently high performance across a diverse and technically demanding service portfolio.

The business has a longstanding track record within the sector, having been founded in 1989 and evolving into a dedicated compliance specialist through its rebrand to McIntyre Compliance Services in 2021. This heritage brings both depth of experience and a forward-looking perspective, enabling the organisation to anticipate regulatory change and adapt proactively.

## **A collaborative, future-ready partner**

Guided by its motto “Safe Places, Safe People”, McIntyre Compliance Services adopts a partnership-led approach built on accountability, transparency and shared objectives. Rather than acting solely as a contractor, the company works alongside its clients as a trusted compliance partner supporting long-term planning, risk mitigation and continuous improvement across housing portfolios.

Ongoing investment in people, systems and technology ensures the business remains resilient and future-ready. From workforce development and digital reporting tools to structured governance and performance management frameworks, McIntyre Compliance Services is committed to evolving in step with the needs of the sector.

This commitment enables clients to navigate complex compliance frameworks with clarity, strengthen internal controls and deliver demonstrably safer outcomes for residents, stakeholders and communities nationwide. With consistency, reliability and long-term value at its core, McIntyre Compliance Services continues to set the benchmark for integrated life safety compliance within UK social housing.
