# Rahiem Jackson murder detectives charge Leeds man - police statement in full

## Michael Gyedu has been charged with murder

A man has been charged with murder following the death of Rahiem 'Reema' Jackson in Leeds.

West Yorkshire Police said Michael Gyedu, 26 and of Rathmell Road, Leeds, has been charged with the murder of Rahiem Jackson. Mr Jackson died on Saturday, October 24.

Michael Gyedu will appear at Leeds Magistrates' Court later today (November 2). A second man, also aged 26, was arrested yesterday on suspicion of murder and he remains in police custody. The investigation into Rahiem's death continues.

The police said: "Detectives investigating the murder of Rahiem Jackson in Leeds on Saturday (24 October) have charged a man with his murder. Michael Gyedu aged 26, of Rathmell Road, has been charged with murder. Gyedu, whose self-defined ethnicity is listed as 'Any Other Black', will appear at Leeds Magistrates' Court today (November 2).

"Yesterday morning (November 1), a second man, aged 26 was arrested on suspicion of murder and remains in police custody. Enquiries into Rahiem’s death remain ongoing by the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team."

If you have any information, you can contact the police online using live chat or by calling them on 101, quoting reference 13260606745. You can also contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or on their website.
