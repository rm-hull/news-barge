A man was taken to hospital with serious injuries following an assault involving a glass bottle near a curry house's car park.

Two 25-year-old men were arrested on suspicion of wounding with intent.

West Yorkshire Police said officers were called to Leeds Road, Bradford, at 11.23pm on Saturday.

A 31-year-old man was taken to hospital with injuries described as serious but not believed to be life-threatening.

A police cordon was seen near the Akbar's car park during the early hours of Sunday morning.

When the T&A visited the scene at around 8am on Sunday, the cordon had been removed.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “At 11.23pm on Saturday (31 October) police received a report of an assault with a glass bottle on Leeds Road, Bradford.

"Officers attended and a man aged 31 was taken to hospital with serious injuries that are not thought to be life threatening.

"Two men, both 25 years old, were arrested on suspicion of wounding with intent and have since been bailed pending further enquiries.

"Enquiries are ongoing."
