Leeds Bradford Airport contributes £737 million to the region's economy each year and supports more than 9,100 jobs, according to a new report.

The figure comes from a new independent economic assessment launched at a major stakeholder event in Leeds, highlighting the airport's role as a driver of jobs, investment, tourism and business growth.

The event was attended by West Yorkshire Mayor Tracy Brabin and other regional leaders from business, government, and academia.

Vincent Hodder, CEO of Leeds Bradford Airport, said: "This report demonstrates that Leeds Bradford Airport is far more than a transport hub.

"It is a vital piece of economic infrastructure that supports thousands of jobs, connects Yorkshire businesses to global markets and helps attract investment, visitors and talent into our region.

"The evidence is clear that improving connectivity to the airport is one of the most important opportunities we have to unlock further economic growth for West Yorkshire and Yorkshire as a whole.

"That is why we remain unequivocally supportive of the proposed LBA Parkway Station."

The report, titled Driving West Yorkshire's Economy, describes the airport as one of Yorkshire's most significant economic assets and highlights its role in supporting business productivity, international trade, and tourism.

In 2025, LBA handled nearly 4.5 million passengers, with ambitions to grow to around seven million in the early 2030s.

The airport generates an estimated £129 million in business productivity and supports around 750 jobs in West Yorkshire.

Tourism linked to LBA contributes £75 million and supports 2,840 jobs.

Mr Hodder said improved connectivity could unlock even greater economic benefits.

He said: "A dedicated rail gateway serving the airport would be transformational for the region.

"It would improve sustainable access, strengthen connections between our towns and cities, support inward investment, boost productivity and help ensure Yorkshire remains competitive on a national and international stage."

The proposed LBA Parkway Station is seen as a critical project for future growth, despite ongoing funding and delivery challenges.

West Yorkshire Mayor Tracy Brabin said: "Leeds Bradford Airport is West Yorkshire's gateway to the world, and this report demonstrates the important role it plays in supporting jobs, growing our economy and connecting our businesses and communities.

"We are working hard to realise ambitions for a parkway station to serve the airport and will continue working with all partners to find the best solution to improve access."

The airport is currently investing more than £200 million in facilities and infrastructure to support future growth and improve passenger experience.

Phil Ward, chief operations officer at Jet2, said: "As this report very clearly demonstrates, the airport is a critical economic asset, and its continued success will deliver positive benefits that are felt right across West Yorkshire and the wider region."
