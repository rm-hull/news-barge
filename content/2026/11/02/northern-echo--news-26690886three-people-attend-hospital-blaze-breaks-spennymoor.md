Three people attended hospital after a fire broke out at a house in Spennymoor.

Emergency services were called to St David's Close at around 5.15am.

County Durham and Darlington Fire and Rescue Service (CDDFRS) confirmed they sent three fire engines to the scene with police saying three people received medical attention.

![Emergency services attending the incident on St David's Close, Spennymoor](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21659488.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services attending the incident on St David's Close, Spennymoor *(Image: SUBMIT)*

A force spokesperson said: "Emergency services responded to reports of a fire at a property on St David’s Close, Spennymoor, this morning (November 2) at around 5.15am.

"Three individuals inside the address received medical attention from paramedics and later attended hospital as a precaution.

"An investigation into the circumstances of the fire is currently underway."

A spokesperson for CDDFRS added: “At 5.15am today (Monday), we received a report of a fire at a house on St David’s Close, Spennymoor.

“Two crews from Spennymoor and a third from Durham attended to put out the fire and have since left the scene.”

Images from the scene show smoke coming from the house, with blue lights from police vehicles and fire engines captured.
