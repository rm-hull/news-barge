Killer, sex offenders and drug dealers among those sentenced at Teesside Crown Court last month.

Here is a round up of some of the people facing justice in October.

### Dad killer

A man who killed his father by pushing him over in a pub beer garden on Christmas Eve has been jailed for six years.

Martin Greensmith became angry when he learned Steven Shevels was his father and violently pushed him over in the Queen’s Head beer garden in Willington.

The 37-year-old was convicted of manslaughter after a trial at Teesside Crown Court last month.

Judge Jonathan Carroll jailed Greensmith for six years and said Mr Shevels posed no physical threat inside the pub.

Jurors heard how the 61-year-old was asked to leave the pub after becoming a nuisance to the Greensmith family.

Within minutes, Mr Shevels had been pushed over and cracked his skull on the concrete floor in the garden.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21403324.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Martin Greensmith *(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Judge Carroll said: "The jury, rightly, rejected your claim of self-defence.

“Mr Shevels was not a violent man and was presenting absolutely no threat to you.

“You walked right up to him and delivered an aggressive and powerful push to his upper body. He immediately fell backwards with no meaningful chance to avoid your push or break his fall.

“He struck the back of his head against the concrete floor with considerable force. It was so loud it was heard from inside the pub.”

The fall, rather than an underlying heart condition, caused Mr Shevels’ death, pathologist Jennifer Bolton told the court.

Greensmith, of Rockingham Road, Sunnybrow, Willington, was convicted by a 10-2 majority after almost six hours of jury deliberations.

### Drug dealer

A drug dealer who moved £100,000 through two bank accounts has narrowly avoided jail.

Vayeed Khan was carrying cash and drugs when police stopped him in Middlesbrough.

Police found three mobile phones, while checks showed £97,533.54 had been moved through Khan’s bank accounts, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Shaun Dryden said the Crown Prosecution Service had accepted Khan’s account of the offences.

He said police stopped Khan after spotting him driving his Audi A3 along Maddren Way in Middlesbrough.

“Three mobile phones were recovered from the vehicle,” he said. “When he was searched, six wraps of cocaine and £2,770 in cash were recovered from his trouser leg.”

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21642936.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Vayeed Khan *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Recorder Simon Kealey KC sentenced Khan to three years in prison, suspended for two years.

He said: “You have been sentenced on your basis of plea and the fact that your family have reported pressure and threats being made to you and them.”

Khan was also given a nine-month drug rehabilitation requirement.

### Stolen Audi

A man paid to ferry a stolen Audi crashed into a police car after speeding off from officers as he tried to avoid arrest.

Connor Head was paid to drive the RS4 from Scotland to South Yorkshire when police tried to stop him in County Durham.

Police spotted the 24-year-old driving towards West Auckland in the early hours of September 5 last year, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Ben Sayers said Head sped away on the A68 before crashing into a police car near Oakley Service Station.

Head drove the wrong way around a roundabout at speed as he tried to escape arrest, with a passenger in the car.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21570843.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Connor Head *(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Head, of Thornton Street, Rotherham, admitted dangerous driving.

Recorder Taryn Turner jailed Head for 15 months and banned him from driving for two years and five months.

“You knew the car was displaying cloned number plates and had been stolen,” she said.

### Young rapist

A County Durham child rapist has been jailed for abusing a girl when he was a teenager.

Kurtis Stead sneaked the girl into his County Durham care home through a back door to avoid CCTV cameras.

Stead, now 21, was banned from being alone with children after he was given a sexual harm prevention order following his conviction for raping a boy.

Teesside Crown Court heard that Stead knew the girl was underage when he abused her.

Prosecutor Nick Peacock said the girl’s relative dropped her at a park near Stead’s home before he sneaked her inside.

Mr Peacock said Stead was convicted of raping a boy under 13 when Stead was 14.

He said Stead received a 12-month detention and training order after breaching his supervision order.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21622746.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kurtis Stead *(Image: Durham Constabulary)*

Judge Edward Legard jailed Stead for 15 months.

“A troubling feature of this case is that when you committed the offence at 17, you were already a convicted rapist and subject to a sexual harm prevention order,” he said.

### Brutal thugs

Two thugs have been put behind bars after a fight opposite a social club left a man with a serious head injury.

Kane Walker and Christopher Watson clashed with a group that included a mother, her daughter and their partners before fleeing and hiding in a nearby house loft.

The pair were arrested after Walker fell through the ceiling, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Zaiban Alam said both men turned on police, with Walker spitting in an officer’s face and kicking officers in the chest.

She said most of the violence was captured on CCTV, but it was difficult to see exactly what each defendant did.

“There was a struggle in which the victim was punched in the face and fell to the floor, unresponsive,” she said.

Neighbours near Roseworth Social Club in Stockton came out to intervene before the defendants and another man ran away.

The pair were hiding in the attic of a Rochester Road house when police arrived, before Walker fell through the ceiling.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21545953.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christopher Watson, left, and Kane Walker, right, have been jailed after a man suffered a fractured skull *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Walker, 22, of Rosthwaite Avenue, Stockton, admitted affray and two counts of assaulting an emergency worker after his arrest on November 20, 2022.

Watson, 32, of Miers Avenue, Hartlepool, admitted affray for his role in the disturbance.

Watson was jailed for 25 months and Walker for 24 months.

### Alcoholic neaighbour

An alcoholic left his neighbour terrified when she woke to him hurling rocks at her home and smashing a window after calling her a 'f\*\*\*\*\*\* grass'.

Lee Green targeted the woman after she gave police a statement about an unrelated public order offence, a judge heard.

The 44-year-old shouted ‘f\*\*\*\*\*\* grasses’ and hurled rocks at the woman’s home in the early hours.

Prosecutor Saba Shan said the woman looked out of her bedroom window after hearing it smash and saw Green sitting on a bike beyond her hedge.

The woman said in a victim impact statement that she struggles to sleep and fears Green has returned whenever she hears a noise outside.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21560114.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lee Green *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

His lawyer, Andrew Turton, said the father-of-four had a long-standing alcohol problem and would welcome help with his addiction.

Judge Timothy Stead jailed Green for 18 months and imposed a five-year restraining order.

“Drunkenness appears to be the hallmark of your offending and that has been acknowledged,” he said.

### Domestic abuse

A woman has been jailed for 12 weeks for breaching a domestic abuse protection order (DAPO).

Danielle Parkes was arrested when police were called to a disturbance at a block of flats in Middlesbrough.

Parkes, 40, was issued with the six-month DAPO in January to protect her brother, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Shaun Dryden said: "The police received a call from a security guard that there was a disturbance at the flat.

"When the police got there, she told them that she shouldn't have been there and held her hands up and said - 'I know I'm not allowed there'."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21540880.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Danielle Parkes *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Parkes, of Almond Court, Middlesbrough, admitted breaching the order on May 13.

He said: "You were made subject to the order to prevent you having any contact with your brother.

"You were with your brother and involved in a disturbance. You were initially charged with a much more serious offence which has been discontinued recently."

### Sex offender

A sex offender was caught with an unregistered mobile phone when it rang in his pocket while he was speaking to police.

Matthew Hodgkiss had only recently left prison when officers visited his home and caught him lying, Teesside Crown Court heard.

The 49-year-old was issued with a ten-year sexual harm prevention order in 2021 after he was convicted of attempting to incite a child into sexual activity.

Hodgkiss was released from prison on September 7 after serving time for a previous breach of his SHPO, a judge heard.

Just over a week later, he was arrested for failing to register another mobile phone.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21581807.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Matthew Hodgkiss *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Judge Deborah Sherwin jailed Hodgkiss for two years because he posed a substantial risk of committing further sexual offences.

His 2021 conviction followed online messages to a decoy he believed was a 14-year-old girl, including requests for photographs and a sex act.

### Child rapist

A child rapist who repeatedly abused a girl has been jailed for 20 years.

Liam Martin, 33, raped the girl on countless occasions during a campaign of sexual abuse.

Prosecutor Jonathan Walker told Teesside Crown Court the victim reported the attacks years after they ended.

In a victim impact statement, she said: “He has stolen my childhood from me and I will never get that back.”

She said the abuse left her unable to leave home after she spoke to police because she feared flashbacks and struggled to talk about what happened.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21602664.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Liam Martin *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Defending, Paul Newcombe said Martin had been held on a segregated wing while on remand and would find prison difficult.

Recorder Andrew Dallas sentenced Martin to 20 years in prison, followed by a one-year extended licence period.

He told Martin that his abuse had severely affected the victim’s life.

### Rape plot

A pervert who urged a man to rape his granddaughter and livestream it has been jailed.

Christopher Lumley was already under FBI investigation after exchanging messages with a child sex offender in the US. He then made the sick request while unknowingly chatting online with an undercover police officer.

The 38-year-old also shared hundreds of images and videos showing child sexual abuse, Teesside Crown Court heard.

Prosecutor Caoimhe Bicknell said Lumley committed several offences while on police bail for possessing and distributing child abuse images.

Cleveland Police were alerted to Lumley by the National Crime Agency in March after an FBI investigation, she said.

When police raided his home, Lumley said he had ‘messed up’ and warned officers they would find indecent images on his devices. More than 700 images and videos were recovered.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21579677.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Christopher Lumley *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

The HGV driver, of The Pastures, Hartlepool, admitted offences including arranging the commission of a child sex offence and making and distributing indecent images of children between January 2023 and August this year.

Lumley must sign the sex offenders’ register and comply indefinitely with a Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

### Balaclava-clad thug

A teenager wearing a balaclava has been jailed for smashing a police van’s windscreen with a rock.

Cameron Coxall targeted a Cleveland Police scenes-of-crime vehicle travelling along Kings Road towards Ormesby Road in Middlesbrough on Tuesday, August 26.

The 19-year-old was a passenger on an unregistered motorbike that crossed to the wrong side of the road and headed straight for the vehicle.

Coxall, of Peirson Street, Redcar, threw a brick through the windscreen, leaving a large crack as the driver sat at the wheel.

Still wearing the balaclava, he rode away along the pavement towards Longlands Road and Cargo Fleet Road.

He was arrested during an operation targeting suspected members of serious and organised crime groups.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21547971.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cameron Coxall *(Image: Cleveland Police)*

Coxall appeared at Teesside Magistrates’ Court last week and was jailed for four weeks for criminal damage.

He received a further four-week sentence for assaulting an emergency worker on Monday, June 15.

Coxall refused to go to his cell. As officers tried to return him, his jacket fell off; when it was handed back, he spat in the man’s eyes.
