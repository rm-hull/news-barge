A man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder after a woman suffered life-threatening injuries in a stabbing incident.

West Yorkshire Police has confirmed officers were called to an address on Reevy Crescent, Buttershaw, at around 10.08am on Sunday.

A 40-year-old woman was taken to Leeds General Infirmary with life-threatening injuries.

A 42-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of attempted murder and possession of an offensive weapon.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656634.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

## The two cordons

The incident prompted a major emergency response and saw police establish cordons at two locations in the Wibsey area.

When the Telegraph & Argus visited on Sunday afternoon, officers and detectives remained at a property on Reevy Crescent carrying out enquiries.

A second cordon was in place on nearby Wibsey Park Avenue, where part of a pavement had been taped off.

![The second cordon ](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656639.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The second cordon *(Image: T&A)*

Police have confirmed the two scenes are linked as part of the same ongoing investigation, although no further details have been disclosed.

## The scene on Sunday afternoon

Residents reported seeing a number of police vehicles arriving and leaving the area throughout the day as investigations continued.

One resident living close to the Reevy Crescent scene said: "We heard some shouting this morning and that was it really.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656635.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

"There's always screaming and shouting around here anyway.

"I didn't know what had happened until I looked out the window and saw the cordon."

Residents also reported hearing an air ambulance.

Flight tracking data from Flightradar24 suggests the Yorkshire Air Ambulance landed at nearby Fairfield Recreation Ground at 10.29am before departing a minute later.

![The cordon in place.](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656637.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: T&A)*

## Statement in full

In a statement, a spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to reports of a stabbing at around 10.08am yesterday (Sunday 1 November) at an address on Reevy crescent, Bradford.

"A man aged 42 has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder and possession of an offensive weapon.

"A woman aged 40 has been taken to Leeds General Infirmary to be treated for life threatening injuries.

"Enquires remain ongoing at this time. "
