A CHIP pan fire broke out in a semi-detached house in Bradford, prompting a response from three fire engines.

Emergency services were called to the scene on Sycamore Close at around 3.15pm this afternoon.

Firefighters extinguished the blaze using a hose reel jet and breathing apparatus, and safely isolated the gas and electricity supply.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "Crews completed a safe and well assessment on the property as there were no smoke alarms fitted."
