Harrogate litter pick – twelve volunteers took part in a Halloween-themed litter pick in Oatlands on Saturday, 31 October 2026, marking the first in a planned series of community clean-ups organised by the Green Party in Harrogate.

The litter pick began at 2pm at Oatlands Playing Fields, with volunteers collecting rubbish from the playing fields, the woods near Hornbeam Park Station, surrounding footpaths and the woods behind Pannal Ash junior football club.

The event was organised and supported by the local Green Party and was open to residents and supporters.

> **Jo Webb, Green Party campaigner for Oatlands and organiser of the event, said:**
>
> I’m chuffed with the level of support we had for today’s litter pick. It was wonderful to see so many of us wanting to look after our local area and clear rubbish from the woods and paths to make them better for people and local wildlife. I’m also really pleased that this is the first in a series of Green litter picks in Harrogate. Next Saturday’s litter pick will start at 12.30 along the footpath beside the railway line between Asda and Bilton. Later in the Winter we’re planning a Merry Green Christmas litter pick in December and a Green Beat the Winter Blues litter pick in the New Year. Everyone is welcome to come along and help, and we’d love to see you at the next one.

The next litter pick is planned for Saturday, 7 November, starting at 12.30pm along the footpath beside the railway line between Asda and Bilton.

Further events are also being planned for December and the New Year, under the titles Merry Green Christmas and Green Beat the Winter Blues.

The organisers say the clean-ups are open to everyone who wants to help keep local paths and green spaces clear of litter.
