INDIE giants Courteeners will headline a major outdoor concert in Halifax as part of a summer music series.

The Manchester band will perform at The Piece Hall on Friday, July 9, as part of the Live at The Piece Hall 2027 concert series.

They will be supported by special guest Ellur, a rising indie artist from Halifax.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "Twenty years in the making, it’s easy to see why Courteeners are so popular with their legions of loyal fans.

"With their relatable lyrics, catchy riffs and honest energy, they’re everything a proper Northern indie band should be.

"It’s also fantastic to welcome Halifax’s own Ellur back to The Piece Hall stage, shining a spotlight on the incredible homegrown talent we’re lucky to have right here on our doorstep."

Known for their energetic live shows and anthems, Courteeners have built a loyal following since the release of their debut album St. Jude in 2008.

They have released seven studio albums and sold more than one million albums in the UK, with their most recent releases reaching the UK Top 5.

Earlier this year, the band marked their 20th anniversary with the release of God Bless The Band, a greatest hits album that reached number two in the UK Albums Chart.

Their homecoming show in Manchester at Wythenshawe Park sold out, and they have embarked on a nationwide headline arena tour.

Joining them in Halifax is Ellur, who released her debut album At Home In My Mind earlier this year.

She has previously performed at The Piece Hall as a special guest for Supergrass and has shared stages with artists including Robbie Williams and Blossoms.

Tickets for all shows go on sale at 10am on Friday, November 6, via ticketmaster.co.uk.
