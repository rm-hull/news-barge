**'Empty Sainsbury's and Go Outdoors could house homeless '**

In his letter, Matthew Laverack asks why, with a bit of foresight, the City of York Council couldn't have used an existing building (Willow House) to house rough sleepers temporarily rather than leaving it empty for so long.

I, like Matthew, have wondered why so many buildings hadn't been used on a temporary basis for refugees or homeless individuals.

A classic case being the closed Sainsbury's/ Go Outdoors buildings at Foss Bank, York.

Now closed for 21 months, they are newish buildings, weather proof, and an ideal large building that could easily accommodate temporary structures undercover and protected from the weather.

Why couldn't the council have come to some temporary arrangement with the site owners?
