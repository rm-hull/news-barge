A man was arrested after a woman had to be rescued from a vehicle following a crash.

Emergency services were called at around 1am on Sunday to a report of a two-car crash on Sandy Beck, which is in the Sandy Lane area of Bradford.

Police reported one of the cars in the crash had "failed to stop" for police in Bingley.

The driver of that car was arrested on suspicion of driving a motor vehicle dangerously.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Following a short pursuit, the Audi A3 was in collision with another vehicle.

"A 40-year-old woman suffered injuries that are not believed to be life threatening.

"A 20-year-old man was arrested for driving a motor vehicle dangerously and has been bailed pending further enquiries.

"Enquiries into the collision are ongoing."

The fire service have confirmed that a woman extricated from a vehicle involved in the crash.

A spokesperson for the West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "On arrival this was two-car road traffic collision.

"One female mechanically trapped extricated by crews and left in care of ambulance service."
