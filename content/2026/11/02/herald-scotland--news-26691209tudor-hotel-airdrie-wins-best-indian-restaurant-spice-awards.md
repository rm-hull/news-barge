*Discover the Tudor Hotel in Airdrie, an award-winning South Lanarkshire venue serving Indian-European fusion food, 'The Big Boy' mixed grill, events, rooms and coffee*

Glaswegians are massive curry fans and as the city has long claimed to be the birthplace of Chicken Tikka Masala, curry is imbued in Glasgow’s cultural identity – so much so, researchers named the city Britain’s spice capital in 2017.  
  
However, savvy spice seekers are discovering the best place to go is in neighbouring South Lanarkshire, where the Tudor Hotel in Airdrie - now owned and run by the AMA Hotels - has been scooping up awards since taking over the 80-year-old landmark hotel in November 2024, including the title Best Indian Restaurant, run by restaurant manager, Suraj Aujla.

Evidence of this hotel’s remarkable achievements in under two years is prominently displayed on the reception desk in the form of trophies, which also include Best Restaurant Manager, Best Social Media Marketing, and Best Multiple Venue Operator – a triple whammy awarded at the 2026 Spice Awards, while the Scottish Funeral Awards has added one for Scotland’s Best Funeral Tea.

Situated in the town’s Alexander Street only a few minutes’ walk from the train station, one visit is all it takes to see why this family-run, three-star hotel and dining venue is becoming a hospitality hotspot, following an extensive refurbishment programme that included new furniture, upgraded function rooms, bedrooms (currently 20 but set to increase to 33) along with a new chef and extensive new menu.

“The Tudor Hotel is a longstanding institution within Airdrie and Coatbridge and a favourite venue for weddings and funeral teas,” says Suraj. “Weddings are a speciality and we have a team in place that handles everything smoothly and efficiently and the largest of our two function rooms can take up to 180 people.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21510228.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The 220-seat restaurant's new signature dish is The Big Boy was sampled in March this year by food critic Matt Binge *(Image: Supplied)*

“Since the purchase of the business, we have introduced a very different Indian/European fusion menu in the restaurant that’s attracting spice fans and curry connoisseurs not only from the surrounding area but further afield, which also helps to boost the local economy as wherever possible food is locally sourced and freshly made on site.”

Another favourite in the hotel is the barista Bar and Bistro, which has been given a fresh new look and new furniture. Hugely popular as a daytime venue for morning coffee and cake, it also caters for light lunches until 3.30pm, while stylish surroundings, friendly staff, and an extensive coffee menu (hot and iced) add to its appeal, while for warmer weather there is also an attractive beer garden.

Other foodie options include a two-course lunch for £13.95; pre-theatre menu from 2- 6.00pm Monday to Sunday for £16.95; and regular dining deals such as Fish Friday, Sunday Roasts, and Cremo Fish and Chips (in the style of Cremo Coffee, a renowned café and fast-food outlet in Sylhet, Bangladesh).

Later in the day, the 220-seat restaurant comes into its own - and its new signature dish is The Big Boy, sampled in March this year by top food critic, Matt Binge, the founder and face of the Food Review Club whose popular UK social media platform and travelling roadshow reviews restaurants across the country.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21458098.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This is a hotel for all seasons and reasons, be it a private event or celebration, Christmas, memorial service, funeral tea, wedding, business meeting, accommodation, or a casal drop-in for a drink with friends *(Image: Supplied)*

“The Big Boy is an Indian take on a mixed grill,” explains Suraj. “It’s a platter of food cooked on iron rods in a tandoori oven and includes Chicken Tikka, Lamb Tkka, Shish Kebab, King prawns, and chicken-on-the-bone.

Matt managed to finish the whole lot and said the lamb was the most flavoursome he’s ever tasted. Priced at £19.95, it’s become our most talked about – and ordered – dish. We are in the process of creating another menu and some of the dishes planned for that are exciting,” he adds.

This is a hotel for all seasons and reasons, be it a private event or celebration, Christmas, memorial service, funeral tea, wedding, business meeting, accommodation, or a casual drop-in for a coffee or drink with friends. The hotel’s 20 bedrooms are all clean, warm, comfortably furnished and equipped with a flat-screen television, free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, private bathroom and work desk.

Guests also have the benefit of free on-site parking, complimentary breakfast, room service, and a smoke-free environment. The hotel scores highly with Tripadvisor and Booking.com, which both carry excellent reviews for food, comfort, amenities and welcoming staff.x

People make places and the Tudor’s recipe for success is undoubtedly the enthusiastic team that brings all the ingredients together – and won the hotel a Best Team Award in 2025.

While you can’t keep a good thing secret and great food always gets talked about, Ama Hotels has taken an innovative approach to marketing by using social media to showcase its people and behind-the-scenes activity across the coffee shop, bar, reception and restaurant.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21660330.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The hotel recently enjoyed an extensive refurbishment programme that included new furniture, upgraded function rooms, bedrooms (currently 20 but set to increase to 33) along with a new chef and extensive new menu *(Image: Supplied)*

“We are in this business for the long term and will continue to adapt and invest to make the Tudor a happy place for all ages – a family-run, value-for-money hotel, restaurant and events venue for families to enjoy,” sums up Suraj.

Looking to spice up your life? The Tudor Hotel in Airdrie will be happy to oblige.
