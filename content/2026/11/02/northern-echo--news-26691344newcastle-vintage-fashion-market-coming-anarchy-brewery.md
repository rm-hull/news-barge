A vintage fashion market is set to bring retro style to a city brewing hotspot.

The NE Vintage Takeover will arrive at Anarchy Brewery’s Taproom in Newcastle on Sunday, November 15, offering a nostalgic shopping experience with styles from the 1990s and early 2000s.

Around 20 stallholders will showcase vintage clothing and accessories at the Heaton venue, including football shirts, band tees, military wear, upcycled pieces, and designer labels.

Simon Miles, co-founder of Anarchy Brewery, said: "It’s probably fair to say we’re not exactly Dedicated Followers of Fashion, but we are definitely about showcasing North East talent, creativity and entrepreneurship.

"We’re really excited to welcome the NE Vintage Takeover’s Fashion Market for what promises to be a fantastic afternoon celebrating fashion, cultural identity and artistic expression."

The event follows successful editions in Sunderland and Middlesbrough and marks the first time the market will be held in Newcastle.

Stalls will feature streetwear, sportswear, festival outfits, trainers, caps, and more, while Anarchy Brewery will serve its range of beers and Rebel Crust pizzas throughout the day.

Anarchy Brewery, known for its Blonde Star, Wor Beer, and Shearer’s Beer, has built a reputation as a versatile event space, hosting theatre shows, film screening and live bands.

The Taproom features a proscenium arch stage, full lighting rig, onsite bar, and parking, making it suitable for a variety of events.

The market will run from 12pm to 5pm and entry is free.
