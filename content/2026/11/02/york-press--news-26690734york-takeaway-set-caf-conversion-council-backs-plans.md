PLANS to transform a York takeaway have got the go ahead.

An application to convert Pizza Base, in Acomb's Beckfield Lane, into a café has been approved by City of York Council.

Plans from Ibrahim Kayran stated the Beckfield Café would allow the building to continue to be used for commercial purposes.

Council planning officials stated there would be no adverse effects on local amenities and the new café would not have a detrimental effect on neighbouring homes.

Plans approved by the council would see the ground floor of 147, Beckfield Lane converted to house the café.

It would open from 8am to 5pm, every day, with the takeaway currently open from 3.30pm to 11pm all week.

Plans showed the business would feature a customer seating area, a serving counter and back-of-house facilities including a food preparation area.

The application stated: “The principal design approach is to adapt the existing premises for café use without unnecessary changes to the building's scale or form.”
