AN investigation is underway following a two-car crash which left a main road in North Yorkshire closed for several hours.

The crash happened at around 3.05pm on Friday, October 30, on the A64 at Rillington, and involved a grey Ford Focus (travelling west) and a white Citroen DS4 (travelling east).

Three occupants from the vehicles were taken to hospital with minor injuries, and discharged a short time later.

The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

A police spokesperson said: "We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, email [alyssa.upton@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:alyssa.upton@northyorkshire.police.uk)

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

"Please quote reference 12260213789 when providing information."
