A YORKSHIRE company is a step closer to opening a new working distillery next to Whitby Abbey.

Founders of Whitby Distillery, Luke Pentith and Jess Slater, are busy transforming two derelict barns within the grounds of Whitby Abbey into a visitor centre, featuring the distillery, shop, tasting bar and events space.

The area will serve as the Whitby Distillery headquarters, offering year-round distillery tours and hosting weddings and events.




Work on the new site, which is leased from English Heritage, began three years ago and will have cost almost £3m by the time it is complete, much of it raised from crowdfunding.

Luke Pentith, founder and Director, said: “This is a very exciting time for the business.

“The new centre will be one of the best distillery sites in the UK and will provide a platform to showcase the business and boost its growth.

“By opening up access to the public, it will also give people the opportunity to experience the previously unseen views and spectacular sunsets.”

The loan of £500,000 which is managed by Mercia as part of the Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II, will enable Luke and Jess to complete the construction in the run-up to the opening of the HQ next spring.

Whitby Distillery, which currently employs ten staff members, was founded in 2017 and initially operated from Luke’s parents’ utility room.

The products began selling in two local shops – the first batch of which sold out in days.

Within a year, Luke and Jess had given up their jobs in management to focus on the business full-time.

The business now offers eight varieties of rum and vodka – all made using hand-foraged botanicals from the Yorkshire coast and moors.

Its newest venture is expected to create ten more jobs.
