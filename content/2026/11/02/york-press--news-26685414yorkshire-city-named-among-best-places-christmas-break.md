If you are looking for somewhere to escape to for a winter break in Yorkshire, you are spoilt for choice.

From cosy city breaks to escapes in the Yorkshire countryside, there are plenty of places to visit to get into the Christmas spirit.

Condé Nast Traveller has released a list of its best UK Christmas breaks that are "full of festive magic".

The city of York has made it onto the list thanks to its iconic Christmas market and range of cosy pubs to explore.

## What makes York so great for a Christmas break?

On the city, Condé Nast Traveller said: "With its cobbled streets, medieval buildings and cosy pubs, York feels like it was made for Christmas.

"Come December, the historic centre is taken over by twinkling lights and wooden chalets, with York Christmas Market filling Parliament Street and St Sampson's Square with more than 80 per cent Yorkshire-based traders selling gifts, crafts and plenty of festive food and drink.

"Browse the stalls before exploring the independent shops along the Shambles, warming up with a mulled wine or escaping the cold in one of the city's centuries-old pubs."

No Christmas trip to York is complete without visiting the iconic Christmas market that will be running from Thursday, November 12, to Monday, December 21.

The market will be filled with plenty of unique gifts and handmade items that you can take home to remember your trip.

You can also fuel yourself with hot chocolate, sweets, and seasonal treats ranging from Yorkshire pudding wraps to hot dogs.

If you need to escape the cold, York has loads of pubs dotted around, such as the cosy Eagle and Child, which can be found in a Grade II listed timber-framed building and offers Sunday roasts with all of the trimmings.

The Three Tuns is a traditional English pub in the city centre and is incredibly characterful, making it another spot that could be worth a visit during a Christmas break.

The Castle Museum also gets involved in the festive spirit by decorating its famous recreated Victorian street with Christmas garlands, snow-covered cobbles, and a tree.

There are also a lot of events happening at the museum, including live performances by Victorian carol singers and the Scrooge: A Christmas Carol show.

Condé Nast Traveller recommends booking a stay at the Middlethorpe Hall, which can be found just outside of the city's centre.

Over Christmas, the hotel offers a three-night Christmas House Party that involves an afternoon tea, carols and champagne, a four-course Christmas lunch, and a black-tie dinner on Boxing Day.
