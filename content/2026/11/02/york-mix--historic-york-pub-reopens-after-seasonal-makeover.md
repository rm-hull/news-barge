One of York’s oldest pubs has reopened following an autumn makeover.

Doors to the Golden Lion on Church Street in York are open again after a transformation, marking a new chapter for the historic pub.

The makeover includes new signage and décor outside, while the pub’s inside has been redecorated with refreshed flooring, lighting and furnishings.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Golden-Lion-exterior.webp)

The Greene King pub is popular with local residents and tourists to the city.

Speaking after the official reopening, Jo Wray, general manager of the Golden Lion said: “This is such a brilliant pub that has been serving customers for hundreds of years, and it is great to be open again ready for the festive season which is just around the corner.

“The works have sympathetically blended its age-old features with modern-day comforts, and we continue to serve good food and drinks for all occasions, diets and appetites.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Golden-Lion-l-r-Ailsa-Antwis-Assistant-Manager-Jo-Wray-General-Manager-and-Sam-Chambers-Team-Leader.webp)

“We are here for customers looking for a refreshing, much-needed drink after sightseeing or after-work drinks and catch ups, to a leisurely meal or enjoying one of our not-to-be-missed Sunday roasts.

“We have plenty of entertainment lined up with live music and DJ nights as well as all the action on Sky Sports and TNT Sports. Come in and see us and make the most of the Golden Lion’s welcome and hospitality!”
