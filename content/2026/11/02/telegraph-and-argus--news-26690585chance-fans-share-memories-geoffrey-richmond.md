EVERYONE had an opinion on Geoffrey Richmond, who has died at the age of 85.

City’s brash and ambitious chairman between 1994 and 2002, his promise to take the club into the Premier League within five years appeared ridiculous when he took charge.

Yet he delivered on that, overseeing some of the greatest days in City’s modern history.

The Wembley play-off final in 1996, a top-flight return sealed at Molineux in 1999, David Wetherall’s winner to keep City up in 2000 and more.

But Richmond’s financial extravagance and ambition to better City, not helped by the collapse of ITV Digital, brought the club to its knees and administration in 2002 nearly saw the club go under.

He soon left under a cloud and it took the Bantams years to recover, while some might argue they never fully have.

There was also Richmond the man, with many City supporters having a story to tell about their former chairman, whether that be a five-minute catch-up over a cigarette, or amid celebrations in Centenary Square.

Ultimately, we want to know all about your memories of the former Bantams chairman, whatever they may be…

Tell us below the line or over on Facebook what you recall of the Richmond reign and Richmond himself?
