Morrisons shoppers can get their hands on Disney character toys, festive decorations and collectible stickers this Christmas - with the chance to win a family holiday to the country that inspired Frozen.

The supermarket has launched its new Disney Christmas collection, featuring seven classic character plush toys, a sticker album and 12 Christmas bells.

There are also extra rewards up for grabs through the Morrisons More app, including free Disney products, More Points and entry into a prize draw to win a family trip to Norway.

The Disney collection is available in Morrisons stores and online now, with More Card members able to get selected products at discounted prices.

## Disney toys, stickers and Christmas decorations at Morrisons

Families can choose from seven Disney character plush toys, each priced at £10, or £4.99 for More Card members.

The collection includes:

* Mickey Mouse
* Minnie Mouse
* Donald Duck
* Daisy Duck
* Goofy
* Pluto
* Winnie the Pooh

Shoppers can also start collecting Disney stickers, with a free pack of four available when they spend £15 in-store or online.

Each pack features much-loved Disney characters or iconic items from their stories, which can be added to the Disney Sticker Album.

The album costs £2.50, or £2 for More Card members.

For an extra festive touch, Morrisons is also selling 12 Disney Christmas Bells. These cost £2 each, or £1 for More Card members, and are designed to add a Disney-inspired touch to the Christmas tree.

The collection comes with extra chances to win through the Morrisons More app.

From today, November 2, customers can scan their Disney stickers in the app to unlock rewards, including More Points, free Disney products and an entry into the prize draw to win a family holiday to Norway – the country that inspired Disney's Frozen.

## Morrisons brings back Disney Christmas collection

Gareth Lloyd, Director of Loyalty at Morrisons, said: “Following the fantastic response to our Disney Christmas campaign last year, we’re bringing it back bigger and better, giving families the chance to collect their favourite characters, unlock rewards and get involved in the fun.

“From picking up a free pack of stickers and filling your Disney Sticker Album, to collecting character plush toys and festive bells, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.”

More Card prices apply to selected products. Free Disney stickers are available when customers spend £15 in-store or online.
