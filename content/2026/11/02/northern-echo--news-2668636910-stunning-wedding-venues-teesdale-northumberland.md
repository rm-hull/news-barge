The North East has become one of the UK's most exciting wedding destinations, with venues increasingly moving beyond traditional country house celebrations to offer everything from Ibiza-inspired farm weddings to ultra-bespoke woodland retreats.

While established favourites continue to attract couples seeking classic elegance, newer venues are proving popular with a generation of couples looking for something a little different.

Here's a closer look at the North East sites that best capture the imagination of newlyweds, earning glowing reviews from couples and guests alike.

### **Lartington Hall, County Durham**

Near Barnard Castle, Lartington Hall is a Grade II listed, 17th-century country house set in about 72 acres of stunning County Durham scenery.

The venue includes an early 19th-century chapel dedicated to St Lawrence, an 1836 ballroom and a grand French-inspired archway.

It now hosts civil ceremonies and receptions, with accommodation, reception rooms and gardens available for wedding parties. One guest praised the venue as "amazing" online, adding that dedicated staff meant "the whole day ran like clockwork."

They added: "The venue itself was top quality with great bedrooms, reception rooms and gardens.

"The food for the wedding breakfast and night-time do were exceptional. Every guest I spoke to was extremely impressed and grateful for the whole day."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/10057770.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Near Barnard Castle, Lartington Hall is a Grade II listed, 17th-century country house set in about 72 acres of stunning County Durham scenery.

### **Wynyard Hall, Stockton**

Wynard Hall sits on a private 400-acre estate near Billingham, and offers couples its 18th-century Grade II listed country house for their special day.

Its facilities include a spa, guest cottages, home-grown dining and one of the largest rose gardens in the country, making it the perfect destination for the more luxury-minded planners.

One newlywed wrote online: "We recently had our wedding at this venue, and it was absolutely incredible from start to finish.

"The atmosphere was magical, every corner felt picture-perfect and romantic. Our guests couldn’t stop commenting on how stunning the venue looked."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18472595.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Wynard Hall sits on a private 400-acre estate near Billingham, and offers couples its 18th-century Grade II listed country house for their special day.

### **Lumley Castle, Chester-le-Street**

630-year-old Lumley Castle castle overlooks the River Wear, offering views of some of the North East's most beautiful landscapes.

The castle's opulent accommodation offers a fairy-tale experience in one of County Durham's oldest buildings, available for both exclusive-hire and smaller celebrations within its function rooms.

One online reviewer said their Lumley Castle went "perfectly from start to finish," and specifically praised the staff for their attention to detail.

They added: "Everything was so well organised, the setting was absolutely beautiful, and all of the little details were taken care of."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21374365.jpg?type=mds-article-620)630-year-old Lumley Castle castle overlooks the River Wear, offering views of some of the North East's most beautiful landscapes. *(Image: Google Maps)*###**Beamish Hall Hotel, Stanley**

Set in 24 acres of County Durham parkland, Beamish Hall Hotel has been described as "the jewel of the North East."

The 17th-century listed hall is another venue which offers a fairy-tale experience, with woodland ceremonies and enchanted function suites for couples to enjoy.

A guest said their experience at Beamish Hall for their daughter's wedding was "magical," writing: "Everything was perfect from arriving at the hotel to be greeted by staff who were friendly, organised and extremely helpful.

"The food both in the stables and the wedding breakfast was excellent. All the staff were professional but also couldn't do enough for you."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/19355053.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Set in 24 acres of County Durham parkland, Beamish Hall Hotel has been described as "the jewel of the North East." *(Image: DJ Aerial Photography)*###**Ellingham Hall, Northumberland**

Ellingham Hall is an exclusive-use Georgian manor with 18 bedrooms and seven stone cottages.

Boasting "old world charm interwoven with modern day luxury," the 19th century country house's two night wedding experience has received industry recognition for its rural charm and proximity to Northumberland's beautiful heritage sites.

A couple who got married at Ellingham Hall said family and friends described their wedding as "the best they have ever attended," and praised the hard work of the team to ensure their day was perfect.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653638.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Ellingham Hall is an exclusive-use Georgian manor with 18 bedrooms and seven stone cottages. *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*###**Le Petit Château, Northumberland**

This 30-bedroom boutique hotel in Otterburn offers antique French-inspired interiors, including chandeliers, gold furniture and a marble fireplace.

The dog-friendly venue is described as "shabby chic, blended with elegance," and offers a range of packages across its stunning grounds which lie just south of Northumberland National Park.

One couple who married in 2018 visited the venue's hotel for their anniversary, and said the staff had surprised them with an upgraded room and served the cocktails they had enjoyed on their wedding day.

"They were beautifully presented and tasted delicious," the reviewer added, "we will definitely be back soon,”

### **Charlton Hall, Northumberland**

Near Alnwick, Charlton Hall is surrounded by a private 150-acre estate. Popular with younger couples looking for a different style of wedding, the hall offers indoor and outdoor weddings, including ceremonies beside a lake.

Charlton Hall has received regional and national awards for its "magnificent" and "elegant" ceremonies.

One reviewer wrote that the luxury venue's staff went "above and beyond," and hailed the venue as "exceptional."

They added: "The cabin itself was stunning, but the standout has to be the outdoor spa bath – a real highlight.

"The prosecco and chocolates waiting for us were a lovely touch and just topped off the whole experience."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653657.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Charlton Hall is popular with younger couples looking for a non-traditional style of wedding. *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*###**Runa Farm, County Durham**

Couples can choose a more intimate venue near Barnard Castle in Runa Farm, with its chic, natural celebrations.

The rustic venue boasts "White Isle style and glamour," offering a range of celebrations from festival-style live music to a more elegant, Mediterranean-inspired approach.

One online reviewer said: "The perfect boho-farm getaway for a wedding weekend! The property is gorgeous, beautifully curated, and incredibly peaceful.

"It made an already special occasion feel even more magical."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21621909.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Couples can choose a more intimate venue near Barnard Castle in Runa Farm, with its chic, natural celebrations. *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*###**Brinkburn Priory, Rothbury**

Brinkburn's appeal sits with its medieval priory beside the River Coquet, south of Cragside.

Perfect for couples seeking a truly unique celebration, Brinkburn stages just 20 bespoke weddings each year, for between 22 and 52 guests.

Between three locations around the historic manor house, no two Brinkburn weddings are the same, and can feature more unorthodox celebrations including forest walks, wild swimming, sound baths and sunset yoga.

One online reviewer wrote: "The grounds were not only beautiful for the photographs but also to visit.

"On the grounds of the priory there are also cottages to let, which are lovely. The whole day was quite wonderful."

Whinstone View is one of the North East's most popular outdoor wedding venues.

In the countryside of the North York Moors, the modern-rustic barn venue can host up to 120 guests, with indoor and outdoor spaces and Scandinavian-inspired accommodation.

Whinstone View offers a secluded celebration surrounded by beautiful landscapes, perfect for couples planning a more private occasion.
