The tournament from the Plenitude Arena in the French capital represents the best chance for players to make it to the season-ending ATP Finals in Turin later in November.

Top three seeds Alcaraz, Zverev and Ben Shelton have already guaranteed their spots at the eight-man Finals, but beyond that trio it's anyone's guess. After reaching the Shanghai Masters final, Rafael Jódar is holding the final spot with Alex De Minaur his nearest rival. Novak Djokovic, who has won a record seven Paris titles, needs to reach at least the semi-finals after a second-round loss last week in Basel, while American duo Tommy Paul and Taylor Fritz need to go deep to stand any chance of making it to Turin.

With 2025 champion Jannik Sinner joined by Félix Auger-Aliassime and João Fonseca on the injured list, Alcaraz will look to put a third-round exit in China behind him as he continues his own return from the treatment room. Flavio Cobolli, the fifth seed, is also struggling with injury after leaving the court in tears in Vienna when withdrawing against Matteo Berrettini – his absence could mean an extra spot in Turin. Zverev, already guaranteed the year-end world number one ranking, will also want to get back to winning ways after struggling in Shanghai.

There won't be a dry eye in the house when Gaël Monfils takes to the court – albeit without his racket – for the final time after one of the first-round matches. The 40-year-old Frenchman, whose separation from Elina Svitolina was recently announced, retired in October and will be honored with a special presentation to commemorate one of the most popular player on tour's superb career.

The top eight seeds – Zverev, Alcaraz, US Open runner-up Shelton, Daniil Medvedev, French Open runner-up Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe and Alex De Minaur – have byes to the second round.
