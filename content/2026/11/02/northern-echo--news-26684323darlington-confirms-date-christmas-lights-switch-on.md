Preparations are underway for Darlington's annual Christmas lights switch-on, with families invited to a free evening of entertainment in the town centre next month.

The event will start in Market Square on Sunday, November 15, at 4pm, with live music, performances and special guests before the lights are switched on.

Darlington Round Table's Santa will lead the countdown to the switch-on shortly before 5.30pm, followed by a celebratory fireworks display.

The event is being sponsored by Costa Coffee in Darlington, Cityheart and Taylor Wimpey.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21650711.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Festive lights illuminate Darlington town centre ahead of the annual Christmas lights switch-on event. *(Image: Darlington Borough Council)*

An accessible viewing area will be available for wheelchair users and people with physical impairments, with further information available through the Enjoy Darlington website.

The switch-on marks the beginning of a series of festive events planned across the town centre in the run-up to Christmas.

These include sensory switch-on sessions designed for those who prefer a quieter experience, the annual Christmas tree trail featuring decorations created by schools and community groups, and the return of the popular ice sculpture trail on December 12.

Council leader Steve Harker said: "The Christmas light switch-on marks the start of the festive season.

"It is a fantastic, family fun and free event, and I hope as many people as possible come along to soak up the fabulous atmosphere."

Kelly Daniels, Costa Coffee’s area manager for Darlington, said the company was pleased to support the event.

The council's lighting team is currently installing festive decorations throughout the town centre, including the town's Christmas tree ahead of the switch-on.
