A five-time Grammy Award-winning global icon is set to launch a major UK and Ireland tour with her first show on the Yorkshire coast.

Shania Twain will open with a performance at Scarborough Open Air Theatre (OAT) on Wednesday, June 16, 2027.

It marks the start of an eight-date run across the UK and Ireland.




Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor said: "Shania Twain is a global icon and someone we are delighted to be welcoming to Scarborough.

"She has achieved the kind of success that people dream of and as one of the best-selling female country pop artists of all time, that success and influence is infinite.

"We have welcomed some of the biggest female artists to Scarborough over the years – Kylie, Britney, Alanis Morissette and Christina Aguilera to name a few – and now to add Shania Twain to that legacy is something quite special.

"This is going to be a big night."

![Shania Twain](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21657586.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The tour announcement comes after the release of Little Miss Twain - The Places I'll Go, the deluxe edition of her seventh studio album *(Image: Contributed)*

The tour announcement comes after the release of Little Miss Twain – The Places I’ll Go, the deluxe edition of Twain’s seventh studio album.

The album explores her early influences growing up in Canada and features collaborations with artists including Tanya Tucker, T Bone Burnett, Ronnie Wood, and Josh Homme.

Twain also recently released an audiobook version of her 2011 memoir, From This Moment On, narrated by the singer herself and including a new foreword.

The year 2027 will mark two major milestones in Twain’s career: the 30th anniversary of her breakthrough album Come On Over and the 25th anniversary of Up!.

Both albums redefined the country and pop genres and cemented her status as a global superstar.

In addition to the tour, a biopic chronicling her life and career is currently in development.

Twain joins a packed line-up of artists announced for Scarborough OAT in 2027, including Kings of Leon, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy and The Conspirators, Bananarama, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

Tickets for Shania Twain’s Scarborough show go on general sale at 9am on Friday.
