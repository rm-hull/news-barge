A County Durham village has been getting into the Halloween spirit, with a brilliant collection of pumpkins transforming Cotherstone into an autumnal attraction.

The Cotherstone Pumpkin Trail has been giving visitors plenty to look at this Halloween, with a host of creative displays providing a colourful backdrop to the village.

And there is still one last chance to take part, with today (Monday, November 2) the final day of this year's trail.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654953.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Spooky surprises awaited visitors following the pumpkin trail through the village *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

From carefully carved creations to eye-catching decorations, the trail has been showcasing the imagination and effort that goes into making Halloween special for the local community.

Halloween itself brought an especially atmospheric backdrop, with mist hanging over Cotherstone as families explored the displays during the day and headed out trick or treating after dark.

There were even children completing the trail on ponies while dressed up for Halloween.

Writing on their Facebook page, organisers said: "The mist hanging over the village gave our Pumpkin Trail a rather magical, slightly spooky feel, and it was lovely to see families out exploring and enjoying the displays during the day and trick or treating by night!"

They added that the "real stars" of the trail were the 33 households which had given their time, imagination and enthusiasm to creating the displays.

Organisers said: "Whether big or small, simple or elaborate, every single one has helped make Cotherstone look so special.

"It's their efforts that have brought so many smiles to people's faces, and they deserve a huge round of applause!"

And with pumpkins of all shapes and sizes taking centre stage, there has been no shortage of photo opportunities for those exploring the displays.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654955.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A colourful display spotted on the Cotherstone pumpkin trail *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

This year, hidden somewhere among 33 pumpkin displays around the village were ten very special golden pumpkins.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21654956.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Halloween has arrived in Cotherstone with pumpkins appearing around the village *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Each golden pumpkin holds a letter, with visitors challenged to collect the ten letters, unscramble them and solve a secret two-word puzzle.

Visitors can also vote for their favourite display in the trail's People's Choice Award.

Although Monday is the final day to explore the trail, completed trail maps, Golden Pumpkin Word Puzzle entries and People's Choice votes can be returned until 4pm on Wednesday, November 4.

Entries can be handed in at The Fox n Hounds, The Old Chapel Cafe, The Red Lion or Cotherstone School.

Organisers thanked everyone who had visited and supported the event, adding: "You've truly made Cotherstone shine this autumn!"

They have also encouraged visitors to support the village's hospitality businesses while completing the trail, including The Fox n Hounds and The Old Chapel Cafe, although visitors are advised to check opening times and food availability before travelling.

Cotherstone, a village in picturesque Teesdale near Barnard Castle, has a history of bringing people together through community events.

Its annual fun weekend has been a fixture in village life for decades, with the 40th anniversary celebrated in 2023.

While the pumpkins have provided a different seasonal spectacle, they are another example of the creativity and community spirit that make Cotherstone such a characterful place.
