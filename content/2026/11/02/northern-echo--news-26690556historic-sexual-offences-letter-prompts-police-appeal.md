A 'brave' woman who left a letter detailing an historic sex attack is being urged to get back in touch with detectives.

The handwritten letter was left at Loftus Police Station, with officers believing it was dropped off sometime between September 28 and October 2.

Cleveland Police want to speak to the woman, who officers say took a "brave" first step by putting her experiences into writing.

Detective Inspector Louise Sproson said: "The woman has bravely taken the first steps in writing down what occurred to her all those years ago, and I would like to appeal directly to her to please contact us again so we can talk about it further.

"We have specially trained officers who can talk about her options and provide her with support. We can also refer her to support agencies with her consent."

Officers are asking the woman to make contact by visiting the Cleveland Police website, calling 101 or attending a police station and quoting reference number 199623.
