Two people have been taken to hospital after a three-vehicle crash on the A19.

Emergency services were called to the A19 northbound just before the Moor Farm roundabout, Cramlington shortly after 8am on Monday (November 2).

The crash involved a coach, a HGV and a car, Northumbria Police have confirmed.

Two people were taken to the nearby Cramlington Hospital, while a third person was treated and discharged at the scene. Police have confirmed only minor injuries were reported.

The incident led to the closure of the A19 northbound between the A1056 Killingworth Way junction and the A189 Moor Farm roundabout, with motorists facing delays of more than an hour.

One lane of the southbound carriageway was also closed to allow emergency services to the scene. All lanes reopened by 11.15am.

A Northumbria Police spokesperson said: "Shortly after 8am today, we received a report of a three-vehicle collision on the A19 northbound just before the Moor Farm roundabout in Northumberland.

"It was reported that a coach, a heavy goods vehicle and a car had collided.

"Emergency services attended the scene, and only minor injuries were reported.

"The A19 northbound from Killingworth was blocked for a period of time to allow for vehicle recovery. It has since been reopened."

A North East Ambulance Service spokesperson added: "We were called to a road traffic incident on the A19 near Seghill in Cramlington at 8am.

"We dispatched two ambulance crews, one duty officer and three crews from our hazardous area response team. Two patients were taken to Northumbria Specialist Emergency Care Hospital and one patient was treated and discharged at the scene."

Police are now appealing for witnesses and anyone with dashcam footage of the collision to come forward to call 101 quoting reference: NP-20261102-0125.
