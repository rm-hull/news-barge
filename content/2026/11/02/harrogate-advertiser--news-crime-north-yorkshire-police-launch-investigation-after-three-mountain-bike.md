North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses following the theft of three mountain bikes from an address in Knaresborough.

The bikes were stolen and have not yet been recovered.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “We are asking anyone who has seen these bikes being ridden or offered for sale, been approached to buy any of them, captured the bikes on CCTV, dashcam or doorbell footage, or has any information that could assist our enquiries, to get in touch.”

If you can help, you should email [jess.harby@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:jess.harby@northyorkshire.police.uk)

You can also call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively if you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers by calling 0800 555 111 or by visiting [https://crimestoppers-uk.org/](https://crimestoppers-uk.org/)

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260175709 when providing any details regarding the incident.
