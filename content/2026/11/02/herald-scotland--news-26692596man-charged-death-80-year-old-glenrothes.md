A man has been charged in connection with the death of an 80-year-old man in the early hours of Saturday morning.

The 22-year-old was due to appear in court on Monday after being arrested following the discovery of James Suttie's body in the Dunecht Court area of Glenrothes.

Officers were called to a report of a "disturbance" around 3.20am.

Emergency services attended and Mr Suttie was pronounced dead at the scene.

**Read more**

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "A 22-year-old man has been arrested and charged in connection with the death of an 80-year-old man in Glenrothes on Saturday, 31 October, 2026.

"Around 3.20am, officers were called to a report of a disturbance in the Dunecht Court area.

"Emergency services attended and the 80-year-old man was pronounced dead at the scene.

"He can be named as James Suttie. His family is aware and they have asked that their privacy is respected at this very difficult time.

"A 22-year-old has been arrested and charged in connection with the death. He was due to appear at Kirkcaldy Sheriff Court on Monday, 2 November, 2026.

"A report will be forwarded to the Procurator Fiscal."
