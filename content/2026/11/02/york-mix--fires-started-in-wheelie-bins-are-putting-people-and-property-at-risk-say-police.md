Fires in Wheelie Bins in Eggborough appear to have been started deliberately and police say they are putting residents and property at risk.

On Wednesday 28 October at approximately 10:40pm, two separate fires were discovered within a short distance of each other.

**Westfield Close** Two wheelie bins were set alight, causing damage to a nearby tree and bush and a vehicle parked adjacent to the bins.**Rear alleyway off Westfield Road**  
Three wheelie bins were damaged by fire, with two bins completely destroyed.

A further bin fire was reported and extinguished on Sunday evening (**1 November)** in the rear alleyway of**Westfield Close**.

Fortunately, no injuries have been reported, however these incidents had the potential to cause significant damage.

Anyone with any information is asked to get in touch with officers.
