A car fled the scene after crashing into a house on a Bradford street, police have confirmed.

Officers were called to Enfield Drive in Wibsey at around 10.25pm on Saturday night after receiving reports that a vehicle had collided with a property before leaving the area.

Emergency services attended, but no injuries were reported.

West Yorkshire Police said enquiries into the incident remain ongoing.

![The scene on Sunday morning](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656252.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Sunday morning *(Image: T&A)*

## What residents told us

The force's update follows concerns raised by residents who were left shaken after hearing loud bangs late on Saturday evening.

People living on the street told the T&A they heard what sounded like a vehicle striking a property shortly after 10pm.

One resident described hearing "two very loud bangs".

![The scene on Sunday morning](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656251.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Sunday morning *(Image: T&A)*

Several neighbours expressed concern for the occupant of the property, who they said was an elderly lady.

A resident said: “It’s a little old lady that lives by herself.

“It was horrible. I looked out of the window and didn’t see anything, I just heard two bangs.”

## The scene on Sunday

The T&A visited the scene on Sunday morning.

Police vehicles remained at the address while crime scene investigators carried out enquiries.

![The scene on Sunday morning](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656253.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene on Sunday morning *(Image: T&A)*

Detectives and CSI officers were seen examining the area outside the property and speaking with residents.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police were called to Enfield Drive, Bradford at about 10.25pm on Saturday 31 October to a report a car had collided with a property, before then making off.

"Emergency services attended and no injuries were reported.

"Active enquiries remain ongoing into the incident."
