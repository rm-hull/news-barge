Residents at The Granby Care Home in Harrogate enjoyed an afternoon of Halloween magic, games and spooky surprises as the home celebrated the season with a special Halloween party.

The home, on Granby Road, was transformed for the occasion, with residents enjoying an afternoon of Halloween-themed entertainment, activities and treats.

The celebrations included Halloween games, a magic show and special appearances from The Granby’s very own Oompa Loompas, who helped bring plenty of fun and laughter to the event.

Granby Care Home Halloween – residents were also treated to a Halloween-themed afternoon tea, giving everyone an opportunity to relax, socialise and enjoy the festivities together.

> **Pam Clark, Home Manager at The Granby Care Home, said:**
>
> Our residents had such an amazing Halloween with our Lifestyle Team ensuring lots of fun was had. We particularly enjoyed the magic show and our Halloween cake!

The afternoon provided an opportunity for residents to come together, celebrate with friends and create lasting memories.

The Granby Care Home provides residential and nursing care and says it is committed to creating a welcoming environment where residents can enjoy meaningful experiences, build friendships and take part in a varied programme of activities and events.
