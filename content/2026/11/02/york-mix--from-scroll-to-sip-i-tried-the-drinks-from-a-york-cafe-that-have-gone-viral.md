Cookie butter lattes, banana bread matcha and whipped sweet cold foam are filling Tik Tok feeds from all over the globe, and now York is serving up its own versions.

Long known for traditional tea rooms like the historic Bettys, York has started to develop a lively new coffee identity.

Cafés are creating their own aesthetically-pleasing beverages, perfect for sharing on our social feeds, taking inspiration from across the pond.

One of the top exponents is Rise, the independent and highly popular brunch spot, with its original venue on Fossgate recently joined by a branch on Museum Street, which has become a staple for all day breakfast.

Videos of their concoctions have gained thousands of views, mirroring the success of US-based Nitro Bar, which has gone viral with its specialist drinks.

Now, I don’t have the budget to fly to New England and compare their version of this famous coffee – but why would I when we now have our own here in York?

Curious about the hype, I visited Rise’s newest location on Museum Street and ordered the acclaimed Iced Cookie Butter Latte and Banana Bread Iced Matcha.

Let’s just say that aesthetically they nailed the social brief.

I was met with their whipped cold foams, flavoured with cinnamon and vanilla, crumbled cake and cookie pieces – rather sickly but that’s what you expect from a viral sweet treat.

These popular beverages show how food and drink trends can now travel across the world within days. But what’s interesting is that these locations are rivalling long-established York favourites for popularity.

Rise now can boast a queue to get in, just like Bettys.

More independent, contemporary places have opened in the city, like Avocado Envy on Blake Street, Drift In on Nessgate and Kynd on Clifford Street enticing visitors with their matchas and all-day brunches.
