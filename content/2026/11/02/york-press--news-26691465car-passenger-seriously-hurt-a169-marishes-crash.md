A CAR passenger is in hospital with serious injuries following a crash with left a main road in North Yorkshire closed for over nine hours last week.

It happened at around 3.14pm on Friday, October 30 on the A169 near the junctions to Low and High Marishes.

The incident involved three vehicles - a blue Mazda and a red Seat travelling south towards Malton and a white Hyundai travelling in the opposite direction.

Four people in the Seat received treatment for injuries.

A police spokesperson said: "The road was closed to allow emergency services to tend to the casualties and for officers to examine the scene of the collision. It reopened at around midnight.

"We are appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision or saw the vehicles prior to the incident to get in touch, including anyone with dashcam footage of the vehicles or the collision.

"Anyone with information is asked to email [Jeremy.Dean@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Jeremy.Dean@northyorkshire.police.uk) Or pass information via our website, select ‘Tell us about’, then choose ‘An existing case or report’ - link below in the comments. Or call us on 101.

"If you wish to remain anonymous, you can pass information to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via their website.

"Please quote reference 12260213690 when providing information."
