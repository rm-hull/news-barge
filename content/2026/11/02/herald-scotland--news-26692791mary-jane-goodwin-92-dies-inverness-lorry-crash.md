**The 92-year-old woman who died following a fatal crash involving a pedestrian and a lorry in** Inverness**city centre has been named and pictured by police.Emergency services** were called to the incident on the city's Academy Street around 9:50am on Thursday, October 29.

The 92-year-old woman was pronounced dead at the scene; she can now be named as Mary Jane Goodwin.

Police closed Academy Street at Crown Road and Chapel Street for seven hours while officers carried out enquiries. The road reopened at 5pm.

**Read more**

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "The woman who died following a crash in Inverness has been named as 92-year-old Mary Jane Goodwin.

"Emergency services were called to a report of a crash involving a pedestrian and a vehicle on Academy Street around 9.50am on Thursday, 29 October, 2026.

"Mrs Goodwin was pronounced dead at the scene.

"Academy Street was closed at Crown Road and Chapel Street for seven hours while officers carried out enquiries."

Sergeant Calum MacAulay, of the Roads Policing Unit, said: “Our sympathies remain with the family and friends of Mrs Goodwin, who have requested that their privacy be respected at this difficult time.

“Our enquiries are continuing to establish the full circumstances, and we are continuing to appeal for anyone with information to please come forward.”

Anyone with information is urged to contact Police Scotland, quoting the reference 0879 of Thursday, 29 October, 2026.
