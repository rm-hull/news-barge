A 19-year-old entrepreneur has opened a new greengrocer in Darlington Market after spotting a gap for fresh fruit and vegetables in the town.

Fatih Zirih has opened Darlocal Fruit & Veg inside the market, selling a selection of fresh fruit and vegetables seven days a week.

The young trader only moved to Darlington two months ago after previously living in London, where he worked alongside his cousin in an organic shop.

After moving to join his mum, who has lived in Darlington for around 20 years, Fatih decided he wanted to bring his experience of the trade to his new home.

He said: "I was living in London before I came to Darlington. In London, I was working with my cousin in an organic shop.

"I moved here to my mum and, actually, when I came to town, I didn't see any fruit and vegetable market here, and I said, 'Why can't I do this?'"

Before taking a permanent spot inside Darlington Market, Fatih initially traded outside to see how shoppers would respond.

He said the trial went "very well", with customers encouraging him to move inside the market.

Darlocal Fruit & Veg began trading from its indoor stall on Sunday, October 25.

Fatih said the response since opening has been positive, with shoppers commenting on the appearance and freshness of the stall.

He said: "They like the style, actually. It looks very fresh and everyone says it looks very fresh and very nice.

"My plan is to sell just the best fruit and vegetables, best quality. I'm not selling bad quality or anything."

The produce is sourced from suppliers including in Middlesbrough, with Fatih saying he uses different suppliers depending on the quality and availability of the produce.

Despite having only recently arrived in Darlington, the 19-year-old is already hoping to establish his business in the town for the long term.

Asked what he thought of Darlington after his first two months, he said: "It's a good, beautiful town."

Fatih hopes Darlocal Fruit & Veg will eventually grow beyond the market stall, with ambitions to supply businesses across Darlington.

He said: "I'm planning to stay here for a long time, if everything goes well, and supply coffee shops and restaurants, all around Darlington.

"I'm would love to start making some fresh juice as well in the future."

The arrival of the new greengrocer comes shortly after JJ Blair and Sons closed its Darlington Market stall earlier this month after around 150 years of trading.

The closure followed the death of long-standing owner Robin Blair, who died aged 81 in July following a short illness.

Now, Fatih is hoping to establish his own future in the market at just 19.

He said: "So far it's been very nice, actually. The customers have been really positive. I like trading, not just fruit and vegetables. I like everything to do. I like working."

For now, Fatih is running Darlocal Fruit & Veg by himself and said he even put the stall together himself.

The stall is currently open seven days a week, generally from around 8am or 8.30am until 4pm, with Fatih still considering its longer-term opening hours.
