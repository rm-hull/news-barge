Fireworks Displays in Harrogate and the surrounding area.

Fireworks displays and bonfire celebrations are taking place across Harrogate, Knaresborough, Ripon, Wetherby and surrounding villages this November, with events planned for the weekend following Bonfire Night.

From the major charity display on Harrogate’s Stray to the annual event at Ripon Racecourse, there are several opportunities for families to enjoy fireworks, bonfires, food and entertainment.

Events are also planned in Knaresborough, Wetherby, Boston Spa, Walton and Pateley Bridge. However, one familiar local event will not be taking place this year, with Harrogate Railway Sports & Social Club confirming the cancellation of its annual Starbeck fireworks display.

## Harrogate

### Stray Charity Bonfire and Fireworks

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** The Stray, opposite Oatlands Drive, Harrogate**Entertainment:** From 5pm**Bonfire:** 6.30pm**Fireworks:** 7pm**Admission:** Free, with a suggested donation of £5 per person

Harrogate’s major annual charity fireworks display returns to the Stray on Saturday 7 November.

Organised by Harrogate Round Table, the event will feature music and entertainment, with the bonfire due to be lit at 6.30pm before the fireworks begin at 7pm.

Harrogate band UK Foo Fighters, led by Jay Apperley, will perform at approximately 7.25pm, with the event expected to finish at 9pm.

The event is free to attend, but visitors are encouraged to make a donation. This year’s fundraising will support Mind in Harrogate and Samaritans Harrogate.

Organisers say the event costs around £15,000 to stage, including the fireworks, traffic management, first aid, equipment and licensing.

Further information is available from Harrogate Round Table.

### Rudding Park Bonfire Night

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** Rudding Park, Follifoot, near Harrogate**Gates open:** 5pm**Family Fire and LED Show:** 5.45pm to 6.15pm**Bonfire:** 6.30pm**Fireworks:** 7pm

Rudding Park will hold its annual Bonfire Night event on Saturday 7 November, featuring a family fire and LED show before the bonfire and fireworks display.

The entertainment will include walkabout characters and face painters, followed by a fireworks display set to music.

Standard tickets are advertised at £15 for adults and £7 for under-18s. A VIP experience is also available, subject to availability.

Further information and booking details are available from the Rudding Park website.

### Starbeck fireworks cancelled for 2026

**Location:** Harrogate Railway Sports & Social Club, Starbeck

Residents in Starbeck will not have the club’s traditional annual fireworks display to attend this year.

Harrogate Railway Sports & Social Club confirmed on 26 October that it had made the decision not to hold the event in 2026.

> **In a statement, the club said:**
>
> After careful consideration, we have made the difficult decision not to hold our annual fireworks display this year. We understand this event has become a much-loved tradition for many families and members of our community, and we sincerely apologise for any disappointment this may cause.

The club did not give a specific reason for the cancellation, but thanked residents for their continued support.

## Knaresborough

### Knaresborough Cricket Club Firework Display

**Date:** Wednesday 4 November 2026**Location:** Knaresborough Cricket Club, Aspin Lane**Gates open:** 6pm**Fireworks:** 7pm**Tickets:** £5 adults and £2.50 under-18s

Knaresborough Cricket Club and Knaresborough Revolution are joining forces to stage a fireworks display on Wednesday 4 November.

The event will include music, hot and cold refreshments, a bar and a tuck shop. There will also be an opportunity for a visitor to take part in the launch of the display through a ‘Push the Button’ competition.

Organisers have confirmed there will be no bonfire and sparklers are not permitted. There is no parking available at the venue.

Tickets can be purchased in advance or on the door, subject to availability. Proceeds will be shared between Knaresborough Revolution and Knaresborough Cricket Club.

Tickets are available through Knaresborough Cricket Club Fireworks Display 2026.

## Ripon

### Ripon Rowels Charity Bonfire and Firework Display

**Date:** Friday 6 November 2026**Location:** Ripon Racecourse, Boroughbridge Road**Gates open:** 5.30pm**Bonfire:** 7pm**Fireworks:** 7.30pm**Tickets:** £10 adults and £5 children

One of the area’s major Bonfire Night events, the Ripon Rowels Charity Bonfire and Firework Display returns to Ripon Racecourse on Friday 6 November.

Organised by the Rotary Club of Ripon Rowels, the event will bring together families and visitors for an evening of traditional bonfire celebrations and fireworks.

Gates open at 5.30pm, with the bonfire due to be lit at 7pm and the fireworks display scheduled for 7.30pm.

Advance online tickets are required. Visitors are advised to book before travelling and check the organisers’ information for the latest arrangements.

Tickets and further information are available from the Ripon City Bonfire website.

## Wetherby and surrounding villages

### Wetherby Bonfire and Fireworks

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** The Ings, Wetherby**Time:** 5pm to 10pm**Tickets:** £5 per person; under-twos attend free but still require a ticket

Wetherby Town Council’s annual Bonfire and Fireworks event returns to The Ings on Saturday 7 November.

The event will feature a bonfire, fireworks, funfair rides and games, alongside street food stalls offering hog roast, pizza, burgers, hot dogs, pies and sweet treats.

Visitors can find further information and ticket details through Wetherby Town Council.

### Walton Village Hall Bonfire Event

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** Walton Village Hall, School Lane, Walton**Gates open:** 6pm**Bonfire:** 6.30pm**Fireworks:** 7.15pm

Walton, near Wetherby, will also hold its community bonfire event on Saturday 7 November.

The ticketed event will take place at Walton Village Hall, with tickets advertised at £9 for adults and £3.25 for children. Children under four are free.

### Boston Spa Community Bonfire and Fireworks

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** Primrose Lane Primary School, Westwood Way, Boston Spa**Gates open:** 5pm**Fireworks:** 7pm**Advance tickets:** £6 adults and £4 children aged four to 15

Boston Spa’s community Bonfire and Fireworks event takes place on Saturday 7 November, organised by Primrose Lane PTA.

The evening will include a licensed bar, hot food, mulled wine, children’s rides and activity stalls.

Proceeds from the event will support the school. Family tickets are also available, with different prices applying for tickets purchased on the gate.

Tickets are available through the Boston Spa Community Bonfire and Fireworks booking page.

## Nidderdale

### Pateley Bridge Bonfire and Fireworks

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** Nidderdale Showground, Pateley Bridge**Time:** From 6pm**Admission:** Entry by donation

Pateley Bridge will also host a community Bonfire and Fireworks event at Nidderdale Showground on Saturday 7 November.

The event is listed as starting at 6pm, with entry by donation. Visitors are advised to check local event information for any further details before travelling.

## Masham

### Masham Annual Bonfire and Fireworks

**Date:** Saturday 7 November 2026**Location:** Shooting Holme, near the bridge, Masham**Refreshments:** From approximately 5.30pm**Bonfire:** 6.15pm**Fireworks:** 7pm

Masham’s annual community bonfire and fireworks celebration is also listed for Saturday 7 November.

Organised by local volunteers, the event is expected to include food, refreshments and a bar. Donations on the evening help support the cost of staging the display.

## Planning your visit

Event organisers may change timings or arrangements, particularly in the event of poor weather. Visitors should check the relevant event or venue website before setting off.

Some displays require advance tickets, while others operate on a donation basis. Visitors should also check individual event rules concerning parking, sparklers and other items.

Harrogate Railway Sports & Social Club’s Starbeck display will not take place in 2026.
