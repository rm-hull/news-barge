# Volvo driver arrested after boy, 13, left seriously injured in Leeds crash

## A 27-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of driving while over the prescribed limit

A 13-year-old boy has been seriously injured after being hit by a car in Leeds.

West Yorkshire Police said they were called at 6.24pm on Friday, October 30, to a crash at the junction of Simmons Court and Easy Road in Leeds. There, a blue Volvo XC40 had hit a teen pedestrian, who was left with serious injuries.

A 27-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of driving while over the prescribed limit and causing serious injury by dangerous driving. He has been bailed as the police continue their investigation.

The police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a collision in Leeds involving a car and a pedestrian.

"At 6.24pm on Friday (30 October), officers were called to a report of a collision involving a blue Volvo XC40 and a pedestrian at the junction of Simmons Court and Easy Road in Leeds.

"The pedestrian, a 13 year old boy, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

"A 27-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of driving whilst over the prescribed limit and causing serious injury by dangerous driving and has since been bailed pending further enquiries."

If you have any information, you can contact the police using live chat online or by callling 101, quoting reference number 1290 of October 30. You can also contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
