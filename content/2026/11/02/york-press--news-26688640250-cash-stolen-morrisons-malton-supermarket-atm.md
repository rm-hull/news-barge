Cash has been stolen from an ATM at a North Yorkshire town supermarket after a bank card was left in the machine.

North Yorkshire Police said £250 was taken from the cashpoint at Morrisons in Castlegate, Malton, on Sunday, September 27.

The force has released an image of a man it said may have information which could help officers investigating the theft and has asked for the public's help in identifying him.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police quoting reference 12260189948.
