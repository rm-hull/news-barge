Bollards installed around Bradford's City Park to protect against hostile vehicle attacks were put to the test after a vehicle crashed into them overnight.

Videos circulating on social media show a vehicle striking the barriers near the junction of Sunbridge Road and Aldermanbury, causing significant damage to the car.

Moments later, a number of police cars can be seen surrounding the vehicle.

West Yorkshire Police has not yet confirmed the circumstances surrounding the incident.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21659325.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The damaged bollards *(Image: City Park)*

## City Park provide update

However, City Park said in a social media post that its PAS68-rated hostile vehicle mitigation bollards had been "tested last night" and had successfully protected the popular public space.

City Park said the security measures were installed as part of wider improvements made in preparation for Martyn's Law, officially known as the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21659327.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The damaged bollards *(Image: City Park)*

In a statement, City Park said: "Over the past three years, we have been working towards making City Park as safe as we could, including installing two defibrillators, a bleed kit, changing lighting to bright, modern LED fittings, increased CCTV coverage and surrounding City Park in PAS68 rated Hostile Vehicle Mitigation Bollards.

"These bollards were tested last night, and, safe to say, they did their job."

The statement added that a contractor would be carrying out a survey of the bollards this week to ensure they continue to protect the site.

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for information.
