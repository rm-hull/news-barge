Legendary Led Zeppelin musician Robert Plant to headline huge North Yorkshire show next summer.

He will headline with roots-folk band Saving Grace and Suzi Dian.

Following the release of the acclaimed *Saving Grace: All That Glitters*… EP last month and fresh back from a run of US dates, they will bring the* Roots & Branch*tour to Scarborough Open Air Theatre on**Saturday 26 June 2027**.

They will be joined by special guests award-winning folk stars Kate Rusby and Seth Lakeman.

Tickets go on sale at 10am Friday via Ticketmaster.

This group of distinguished musicians – vocalist Suzi Dian, drummer Oli Jefferson, guitarist Tony Kelsey, banjo and string player Matt Worley, and cellist Barney Morse-Brown – explore the evolution of roots music both vintage and modern.

Saving Grace began at Plant’s home on the Welsh borders in 2019 – when the pandemic intervened and any formal plans were temporarily shelved. Once protocols permitted, Plant and Saving Grace began to record informally in a barn setup or outdoors, then booked themselves in small venues without fanfare.

Earlier this year Robert Plant was honoured with the Lifetime Achievement Award at the Americana Music Association Awards.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “Robert Plant is one of the most influential and distinctive voices of British music so to be welcoming him to Scarborough to perform with Saving Grace and Suzi Dian presents a very special opportunity for live music fans.

“2027 is shaping up to be another exciting year on the Yorkshire coast and we can’t wait.”
