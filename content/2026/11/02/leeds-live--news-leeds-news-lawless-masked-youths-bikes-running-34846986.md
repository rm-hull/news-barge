# 'Lawless masked youths on bikes running riot lighting fireworks' in Leeds area hit with police order

## Leeds locals say scary youths treat the area like a 'racetrack'

Lawless young lads are “running riot lighting fireworks off bikes while doing wheelies” in an embattled Leeds neighbourhood.

West Yorkshire Police issued a temporary dispersal order in Belle Isle after numerous reports of anti-social behaviour involving youths terrorizing the area with fireworks. It was in force over the weekend, expiring at 8.30pm on Saturday night, October 31.

Six youths were told to leave the south Leeds area on Thursday night under the threat of prosecution. The force made a call to parents to highlight the impact of their behaviour.

Locals have spoken out about the horrors the youths are inflicting on the area. Locals spoke of scary youths clad in black and face coverings racing around the area on motor and quad bikes, doing tricks and firing off fireworks and even vandalising a local graveyard.

Concerned couple Lucas Davis and Ella Kirkbright, 24 and 22, who live in Belle Isle are raising a six-month-old baby. They spoke of the endless bikes whizzing around the street as if it were a “racetrack”.

Amazon delivery driver Lucas said: “There’s too many kids around here thinking they can do what they want. On motorbike speeding around and young kids not giving an F. They’re like 14/15 and most of them are into drug-dealing. You’d think it would get better but it’s getting worse.

“Some of them have fireworks strapped to their bikes and when they’re doing wheelies, and they’re pointed up towards the sky, they fire fireworks off their bikes. They do what they want.”

NHS care worker Ella added: “This road is like a racetrack, you can hear them speeding up. They were firing fireworks at cars. It’s getting worse. It just puts a bad name on the area. Half the time their bikes will be stolen, so if they get taken [by police], they’ll just go and steal another one.”

Carer and gran-of-four Siobhan Hewitt, 55, spoke of the terrors she’s seen just looking out of here window. Siobhan said: “You can hear people arguing, shouting at each other, threatening each other and cars in the middle of the street. I’ve witnessed one person trying to drag another person out of a car, literally from my bedroom window. Just swearing and threatening each other. I don’t really go out.

“There is bikes going up and down illegally and they’re all dressed in black with no numberplates or identification on them, whatsoever. It’s dragging the area down.”

Cleaner Selina Toska has lived in the area for 20 years. She said: “The kids are bleeders around here. Bikes and motorbikes coming down the road and following cars. They need sorting out. Somebody vandalised the graveyard. A car ran over some of the graves. It’s disgusting. Bloody young lads running riot.”
