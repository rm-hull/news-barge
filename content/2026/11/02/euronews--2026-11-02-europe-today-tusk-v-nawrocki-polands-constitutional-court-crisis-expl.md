## Poland's President Karol Nawrocki entered the Constitutional Tribunal to demand the return of its suspended conservative chief judge, as Donald Tusk's government seated new judges and riot police clashed with protesters. We hear both sides, plus Ukrainians braces for winter and the Ebola crisis.

## On today's show:

**Michał Wawrykiewicz**, MEP from the European People's Party, the party of Polish PM** Donald Tusk**, discusses Poland's Constitutional Court crisis.** Marcin Przydacz**, Polish secretary of state and adviser to President** Karol Nawrocki**, gives the presidential camp's side and explains why the president entered the Tribunal in person.** Mary Robinson**, former Irish president and member of The Elders, a group of former world leaders founded by Nelson Mandela\*\*,\*\* tells**Stefan Grobe** why the world is not doing more as Ebola escalates in the Democratic Republic of Congo.
