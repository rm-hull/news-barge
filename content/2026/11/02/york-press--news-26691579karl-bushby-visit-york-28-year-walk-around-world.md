## Karl Bushby set to visit York this month after 28-year trek - here's how to see him

Angela Bushby welcomes her son Karl Bushby as he arrives back home in Hull following his 28 year walk from Chile. *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis)*

A BRITISH adventurer who returned home today after setting out to walk the entire globe will visit York later this month to tell his story.

Karl Bushby will appear at York Theatre Royal on Sunday, November 22 - just 20 days after returning home from his 28-year trek from Punta Arenas, Chile, to Hull which was taken entirely on foot.

Starting his journey in 1998 with the goal of walking home to Yorkshire, Karl made himself two promises when he set off- he would use no transport to advance his journey, and he would not return home until he had completed the entire expedition on foot.

He arrived home to Hull today (November 2) and was greeted by his mother.

A spokesperson for the Theatre Royal said: "Along the way, Karl has crossed some of the world’s most hostile landscapes, faced life-threatening dangers and endured immense physical and psychological challenges, pushing himself to limits few could comprehend.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21660445.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Angela Bushby welcomes her son Karl Bushby as he arrives back home in Hull following his 28 year walk from Chile. *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis)*

"From navigating the perilous Darién Gap between Panama and Colombia, to tackling the frozen expanse of the Bering Strait, between Russian and Alaska, his journey is a remarkable testament to courage, resilience, and the indomitable human spirit.

"Now York audiences are invited to step inside an adventure spanning continents, cultures, and generations.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21660442.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Karl Bushby speaking at a civic welcome in Queen Victoria Square as he arrives back home in Hull following his 28 year walk from Chile. *(Image: Annabel Lee-Ellis)*

"Through first-hand storytelling, Karl will reveal the experiences behind the headlines: the moments when survival hung in the balance, the obstacles that threatened to derail his mission, the sacrifices demanded by a life on the move, and the extraordinary determination that kept him heading towards home."

Tickets are available online and through the York Theatre Royal Box Office.
