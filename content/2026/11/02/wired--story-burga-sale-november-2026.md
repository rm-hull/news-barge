I’ve never met a phone case from Burga that I didn’t like, and right now its cases and tech accessories are all on sale at Shop. You can get $25 off an order of $50 or more, and it applies automatically at checkout. Cases are around $50 apiece, which is the most straightforward way to save, but with dozens of items to choose from, it’s worth browsing what else you might want.

Most of Burga’s phone cases use the same material across models. It’s mostly a matter of choosing your favorite printed pattern. You can coordinate or mix and match with Burga’s other accessories. The cases are thin and inflexible—not necessarily rugged but still more protective than going without. Every one I’ve tried has been precisely cut to fit my phone’s buttons and camera bump, and it is easy to install. When you have the option, I recommend picking a MagSafe case since it’ll be compatible with so many accessories.

I also like the brand’s magnetic phone ring holders, which are available in coordinating patterns with the phone cases. They fold down flat and have a slimmer profile than PopSockets or other phone grips you might be familiar with. They stay on well and are comfortable to use if you like ring-style grips.

The sale on Shop mostly has iPhone cases for various models, but there are a few exceptions for Samsung phones. If you need an easy upgrade or want a gift for a tech-savvy person in your life, this sale is worth checking out before it ends.
