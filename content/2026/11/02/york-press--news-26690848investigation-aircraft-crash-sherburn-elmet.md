## Latest on investigation into aircraft crash that killed 70-year-old man

A man was killed in the crash *(Image: Newsquest)*

AN update has been issued after an investigation into an aircraft crash that left a 70-year-old man dead in North Yorkshire.

The Air Accidents Investigation Branch (AAIB) has said that its investigation into a light aircraft crash that took place during take-off in Sherburn in Elmet last November is ‘well advanced’.

As reported by The Press at the time, North Yorkshire Police said the crash happened shortly before 1pm on Sunday, November 2, 2025 at Sherburn Aero Club.

In today’s update (November 2), a spokesperson for the AAIB said: “Immediately after takeoff, the aircraft pitched up into a high nose up attitude. The aircraft departed controlled flight and struck the ground on the airfield.

“The investigation into this accident is well advanced and the final report will be issued in due course.”

The man – a 70-year-old from Leeds - is yet to be named, but police have confirmed that he was the sole occupant of the aircraft and was pronounced dead at the scene after the plane landed on the runway.

At the time, a spokesperson for the police said: “His family is currently being supported by specially-trained officers and our thoughts are with them at this difficult time.

“The Air Accident Investigation Branch has been made aware.

"We are not currently able to release any further information about the incident or deceased."

The AAIB also investigated a separate July 2024 crash near Sherburn-in-Elmet airfield, in which the two occupants suffered minor injuries.
