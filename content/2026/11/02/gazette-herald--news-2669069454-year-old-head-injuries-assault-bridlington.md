A MAN has received head injuries after being attacked outside a takeaway in a coastal Yorkshire town.

Humberside Police has released an image of people that it would like to speak to after reports of a serious assault on Flamborough Road in Bridlington in July.

It happened on Wednesday, July 15, at around 11pm, and saw a 54-year-old man assaulted outside a takeaway.

He received head injuries as a result of the assault.

“Since receiving the report, extensive enquiries have been ongoing including speaking to witnesses, and reviewing CCTV from in and around the area leading up to and following the report, which have so far been unsuccessful,” said a police spokesperson.

They added that if you recognise either of these individuals, or have any information that could assist their enquiries, please call 101 or report information anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
