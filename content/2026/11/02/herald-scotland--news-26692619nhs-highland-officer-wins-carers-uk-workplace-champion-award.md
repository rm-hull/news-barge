An NHS Highland project officer has been recognised nationally for her work supporting unpaid carers after winning a UK-wide award.

Ayasha Wood, who works in adult social care, was named Workplace Champion at the Carers UK Awards ceremony in London on Thursday, October 15.

The award recognises people who raise awareness of unpaid carers within their organisations, help colleagues identify as carers, connect them with support and ensure their experiences are heard.

As lead of the NHS Highland Staff Unpaid Carers Network, Ms Wood has worked to improve recognition and support for unpaid carers across the workforce.

Her work includes raising awareness of carers’ rights, supporting EPiC Carer Aware training and developing *The Wee Unpaid Carers Book of Support*. She also helped ensure carers’ voices were reflected in the Highland Carers Strategy 2025.

In June 2026, she led the Highland Carers Drop-In Event, which brought together more than 160 carers and representatives from a range of organisations.

The award recognises her work to make NHS Highland a more supportive workplace for staff with unpaid caring responsibilities.

Ayasha Wood, NHS Highland project officer, said: "I was delighted just to be shortlisted, so winning was a real surprise.

"I'm incredibly proud, but this has never been a solo effort.

"I've had brilliant support from our wider leadership team, and especially from my line manager, Michelle Keir.

"I hope this award shows colleagues who are unpaid carers that there is support for them within our workforce.

"For anyone who wants to be more carer aware, whether that's in how we support staff or in the services we deliver, there's a point of contact, and I'd really encourage them to get in touch.

"I hope this helps us keep building positive change across NHS Highland."

Arlene Johnstone, chief officer of the Highland Health and Social Care Partnership, said: "We are incredibly proud of Ayasha and delighted to see her work recognised at a UK level.

"This is a fantastic and very well-deserved achievement."
