Two people were injured in a crash on a major road.

Emergency services were called to Kings Road, Bradford, just before 3.30pm on Sunday.

A person had to be extricated from one of the vehicles, and two people were taken to hospital.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "This was a two-vehicle road traffic collision.

"One casualty was extricated by fire crews and handed into the care of Yorkshire Ambulance Service."

A spokesperson for the Yorkshire Ambulance Service said: "We received an emergency call at 3.24am on Sunday, November 1, to a report of a road traffic collision on Kings Road in Bradford.

"Two ambulances and a team leader were dispatched to the scene and two patients were conveyed to hospital."

The Telegraph & Argus has contacted West Yorkshire Police for further details.
