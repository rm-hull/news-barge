One person was seriously injured in a three-car crash on a main North Yorkshire road.

It happened on the A169 between Malton and Pickering on Friday (30 October) at around 3.14pm.

A blue Mazda and a red Seat travelling south towards Malton and a white Hyundai travelling in the opposite direction collided near the junctions to Low and High Marishes.

#### Crash location

A passenger from the Hyundai remains in hospital with serious injuries.

Four people in the Seat also received treatment for injuries.

The road was closed to allow emergency services to tend to the casualties and for officers to examine the scene of the collision. It reopened at around midnight.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We are appealing to anyone who witnessed the collision or saw the vehicles prior to the incident to get in touch, including anyone with dashcam footage of the vehicles or the collision.”
