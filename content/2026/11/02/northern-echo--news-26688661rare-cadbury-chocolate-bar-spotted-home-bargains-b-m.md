A rare Cadbury chocolate bar has caused a frenzy among fans.

The limited-edition Cadbury Dairy Milk PB&J, spotted in Home Bargains stores, has shoppers rushing to get their hands on it before it sells out.

Produced by Cadbury Australia and manufactured in Tasmania, the 170g bar is inspired by the classic American peanut butter and jelly sandwich, but swaps the bread for creamy Dairy Milk chocolate.

It features peanut-flavoured crème, crunchy peanut pieces, and raspberry-flavoured jelly, all wrapped in Cadbury’s iconic milk chocolate.

Shoppers described the bar as "delicious" and said they "need to try" it.

Not everyone was won over by the flavour.

One social media user said: "It's a bit heavy on the jam for me, needs more peanut."

Others were more blunt, calling it "yucky," while many said they "needed" to get their hands on it "immediately."

The bar is also reportedly available in B&M, which frequently stocks Dairy Milk imports.

## Cadbury chocolate back in UK supermarkets after 18 years away

Astros, space-themed chocolate and candy balls with a biscuit centre, are now available at B&M stores for £1.30.

The popular chocolate brand first launched the treat in the UK in 1997 as a competitor to Smarties and M&Ms.

They were discontinued in 2008, but have now been spotted on UK supermarket shelves.

Astros are still sold in South Africa, and B&M has imported the 40g and 150g packs from the country.

The sweets are currently being sold in 40g and 150g boxes at B&M.

The Cadbury Dairy Milk Winter Mint Crisp bar is also back on UK supermarkets for the festive period.
