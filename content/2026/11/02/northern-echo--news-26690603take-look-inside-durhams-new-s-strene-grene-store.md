Here's a look inside the newest addition to Durham city centre as shoppers flocked to the opening of Søstrene Grene.

The retailer launched its new Riverwalk shop on Friday morning with queues stretching along the street and around the corner.

Ballerinas entertained visitors at a ribbon-cutting ceremony before the first shoppers entered the store.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21659096.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: ADAM SHANLEY)*

The Durham branch expands Søstrene Grene's North East presence, offering its distinctive Scandinavian-inspired shopping experience.

Opening-day images show the shop's extensive range, including home décor, furniture, kitchenware, craft supplies, gifts, toys, seasonal collections and speciality food.

Founded in Denmark in 1973, Søstrene Grene has built a loyal following with constantly changing ranges and its focus on “hygge” — creating comfort, warmth and enjoyment in everyday life.

Søstrene Grene group chief executive and co-owner Mikkel Grene said: "It's always special to open a Søstrene Grene store in a new city, and we're particularly pleased to be bringing our universe to Durham.

"We hope the new store will become a destination for those looking for something a little different, whether they're searching for a new craft project, something for the home, or simply enjoying an afternoon of browsing."

The opening follows the retailer's decision to make Durham a key location in its UK expansion, bringing affordable Scandinavian-style homewares and lifestyle products to city-centre shoppers.

The retailer now operates more than 400 stores across 19 countries and plans to reach 100 UK stores within the next few years.
