Three people were hurt in a crash on the A64.

It happened at about 3.05pm on Friday (30 October) on the A64 at Rillington, near Malton.  
A grey Ford Focus travelling west and a white Citroen DS4 travelling east collided.

#### Crash location

The three casualties suffered minor injuries and were discharged from hospital a short time after being admitted.  
A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.
