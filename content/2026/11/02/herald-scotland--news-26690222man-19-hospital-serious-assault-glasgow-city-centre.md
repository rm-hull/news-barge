A teenager has been taken to hospital after being attacked in Glasgow city centre.

Police said it received reports of a 19-year-old man having been seriously assaulted on Gordon at around 10.15pm on Sunday, November 1.

Police said enquiries are ongoing to establish the full circumstances.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "We received reports of a 19-year-old man having been seriously assaulted in the Gordon Street area of Glasgow around 10.15pm on Sunday, 1 November, 2026.

"The man was taken to hospital for treatment and enquiries are ongoing to establish he full circumstances."

The incident saw Gordon Street closed between Union Street and West Nile Street shortly after 11pm on Sunday evening. It reopened this morning.
