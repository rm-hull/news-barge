BASEMENT Jaxx are set to headline a summer concert series with a high-energy live show.

The iconic dance duo will perform at The Piece Hall in Halifax on Friday, June 25, as part of the Live at The Piece Hall 2027 line-up.

Their set is expected to include classic hits such as Where's Your Head At, Red Alert, and Do Your Thing.

Felix Buxton and Simon Ratcliffe of Basement Jaxx are known for their vibrant stage shows, featuring a full cast of singers, MCs, musicians, and dancers.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: "I've been hoping for a 'rendez-vu' with Basement Jaxx for a long time now – so I'm really excited for this gig.

"They've got that incredibly rare combination of having had huge chart success without ever losing any of their club cool.

"I love it when our hugely versatile building sees the courtyard turn into a dancefloor.

"With so many massive tracks, this will be a high energy, hands in the air kind of show and I'm so here for it.

"Dancing shoes at the ready."

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, November 6, via ticketmaster.co.uk.
