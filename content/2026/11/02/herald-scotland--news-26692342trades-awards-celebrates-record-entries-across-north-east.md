The Trades Awards 2026 celebrated a record number of entries as almost 400 guests gathered to recognise construction talent across the North of Scotland.

The 14th annual ceremony took place at Ardoe House on October 30, recognising apprentices, tradespeople, businesses and major construction projects.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21661794.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Trade Awards. *(Image: Supplied)*

Two new categories were introduced this year: Construction Supplier of the Year and Inspiring Women in Construction. The latter attracted more individual applications than any other category in the awards’ history.

Nicola Fraser of KR Group won the inaugural Inspiring Women in Construction award, with Michelle Forth-Burnett of Safety Scotland highly commended.

John Ogston of Robertson Construction Eastern was named Apprentice of the Year, while Jack Torrance of JKR Contractors received a Highly Commended mention.

The Outstanding Contribution Award went to Doug McLeod of Barratt Scotland, recognising more than 50 years of service to the construction industry.

Launched in 2013, the awards have become an established event in the region’s construction calendar, recognising the people, projects and businesses contributing to the sector.

Mike Wilson, managing director of Mearns & Gill, said: "Every year the Trades Awards remind us just how much talent we have within the construction industry here in the North-east, but the response this year was particularly special.

"We received more applications than ever before and, importantly, they came from right across the industry.

"From apprentices at the very beginning of their careers to people who have given decades to construction, as well as fantastic businesses and some incredible projects being delivered here in the North of Scotland, there was so much to celebrate."

Doug McLeod of Barratt Scotland said: "After more than 50 years in the industry, it is a real honour to receive this recognition.

"Construction has been a huge part of my life, and I have had the pleasure of working with many great people in the industry and Barratt Homes throughout my career."
