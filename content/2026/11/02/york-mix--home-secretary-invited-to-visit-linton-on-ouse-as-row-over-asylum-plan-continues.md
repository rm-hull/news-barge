Home Secretary, Shabana Mahmood, has been challenged to accept an invitation to visit the village fighting plans to house asylum seekers at an old RAF Base

No decision has been made yet but the Government has said it would make a better place to house up to 12 hundred men as they work to end the use of hotels.

The Member of Parliament for Easingwold, **Sir Alec Shelbrooke**, has invited the Home Secretary meet the Linton-on-Ouse Action Group.

He made his request in the commons and she did not indicate she would accept in her reply

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/sir-alec-shelbrooke-mp-commons-ldrs.webp)

This was how the exchange went: Sir Alec said:

“It’s been weeks since I wrote to the immigration minister and not had a response. But out of generosity of spirit, I will say that they have been busy.

“So I’m glad to take this opportunity to invite the Home Secretary herself to come to the Linton-on-Ouse proposed site.

“This is an integrated village, and I think that the Home Secretary herself would be quite surprised and shocked if she was able to come to see it for herself. I don’t even mind if I can’t be there that day, Madam Deputy Speaker, but I really want the Home Secretary to come and see for herself with the Linton on News Action Group, just how inappropriate that site is.

The Home Secretary replied:

“I hear the passionate point that he makes as a constituency member of Parliament. He’ll know that **no decisions have been made yet**. I will ensure that his letter is responded to promptly.

“He will also note that this government has saved a billion pounds a year from our closure of asylum hotels, and he also knows that we will go further to close all asylum hotels in this in this Parliament.

“And it is important as we do so that we move to a new model of providing asylum accommodation that does include more use of military sites, but as I say, in relation to the specific one that he mentions, no decision has yet been made.
