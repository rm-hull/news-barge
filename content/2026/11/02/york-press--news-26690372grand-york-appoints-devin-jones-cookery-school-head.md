A chef who helped win a top award for afternoon tea is bringing his culinary expertise to the classroom.

Devin Jones, the new head of the Cookery School at The Grand, York, has plans to introduce hands-on classes inspired by the hotel’s five-star dining experiences, including a class in afternoon tea.

Mr Jones has spent nearly four years at The Grand, playing a key role in refining its pastry offering and helping the team secure Condé Nast Johansens’ Best Afternoon Tea for 2026.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21658767.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Devin Jones is the new head of the Cookery School at The Grand, York. *(Image: Submitted)*

Devin said: "I love interacting with guests and sharing my passion for food, so taking on The Cookery School feels like the perfect next chapter for me.

"I want people to come here and experience the incredible breadth of talent we have across The Grand, whether that’s learning techniques from our chefs, exploring different cuisines or recreating dishes they’ve enjoyed elsewhere in the hotel.

"More than anything, I want people to leave feeling confident.

"Cooking can sometimes seem intimidating, but it doesn’t have to be.

"Food should be fun, creative and personal, and if someone leaves excited to experiment at home and make a recipe their own, then we’ve done our job."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21658874.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Chef Devin Jones, of The Grand, York, offering tips on pastry at York Restaurant Week in 2023. *(Image: Rory Buccheri)*He now plans to bring that same energy to the Cookery School, with classes designed to reflect the hotel’s culinary talent and signature dishes.**Recommended reads:**

New additions will include an Afternoon Tea class, where guests can learn to make elements of The Grand’s award-winning experience, and a Beef Wellington class.

Mr Jones is working closely with culinary director Kevin Bonello and The Grand’s team of chefs to turn the Cookery School into an extension of the hotel’s restaurants.

Born and raised in South Africa, Mr Jones developed his love for food early on, inspired by his mother, a self-taught cook and baker.

His father introduced him to barbecue cooking, a style he still enjoys today.

He has worked in large-scale hotels, fine dining, and boutique hospitality, and now brings a philosophy of generosity and enjoyment to the Cookery School.

His appointment comes as part of a broader evolution of The Grand’s culinary leadership.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21658971.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Grand, York has new classes planned as Devin Jones is announced as the new head of the Cookery School. *(Image: The Grand)*

Kevin Bonello has been promoted to culinary director, and Manoj Nair is now executive head chef.

The Cookery School is now fully integrated within the hotel’s food and beverage division, creating greater opportunities to draw on expertise from across its kitchens and restaurants.

Further Cookery School experiences will be revealed later this year.
