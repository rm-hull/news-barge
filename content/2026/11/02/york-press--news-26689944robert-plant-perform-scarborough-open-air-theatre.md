A legendary British rocker is set to take the stage on the North Yorkshire coast.

Robert Plant will perform at Scarborough Open Air Theatre next June 26 as part of the 2027 Root & Branch tour, appearing alongside roots-folk group Saving Grace and vocalist Suzi Dian.

The show which is part of a wider tour that will also visit Lincoln, Halifax, and Dublin, will feature special guests and award-winning folk artists Kate Rusby and Seth Lakeman.

**Recommended reads:**

Julian Murray, of Cuffe and Taylor, promoters and venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: "Robert Plant is one of the most influential and distinctive voices of British music so to be welcoming him to Scarborough to perform with Saving Grace and Suzi Dian presents a very special opportunity for live music fans.

"2027 is shaping up to be another exciting year on the Yorkshire coast and we can't wait."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21658237.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Award-winning folk artist Kate Rusby will be returning to her Yorkshire roots to play at Scarborough Open Air Theatre in 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

The tour follows the recent release of the critically-acclaimed Saving Grace: All That Glitters... and a series of US tour dates.

The EP, available digitally via Nonesuch Records, features traditional and Americana-influenced tracks including The Blackest Crow and Two Coats, Gillian Welch’s Orphan Girl, and Bert Jansch’s Poison.

Saving Grace is made up of vocalist Suzi Dian, drummer Oli Jefferson, guitarist Tony Kelsey, banjo and string player Matt Worley, and cellist Barney Morse-Brown.

The band formed in 2019 at Plant’s home near the Welsh border and spent their early days performing as an unannounced opening act for Fairport Convention before the pandemic put plans on hold.

Once restrictions eased, the group began recording in informal settings and later booked a series of low-key shows.

Earlier this year, Robert Plant was honoured with the Lifetime Achievement Award at the Americana Music Association Awards.

Kate Rusby, one of the UK’s most celebrated folk singers, will return to her Yorkshire roots for the Scarborough show.

Her crystal-clear voice and storytelling have earned her numerous BBC Radio 2 awards, and her music has featured in film and television.

Folk artist Seth Lakeman was propelled into the spotlight with his Mercury Prize-nominated album Kitty Jay in 2005.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21658245.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Seth Lakeman will be performing at Scarborough Open Air Theatre in 2027. *(Image: Submitted)*

His follow-up album, the gold-certified Freedom Fields, marked its 20th anniversary this year.

Mr Lakeman released his latest album, The Granite Way, in 2025.

Mr Plant’s Scarborough show joins a packed 2027 lineup at the Open Air Theatre, which will also host Shania Twain, Kings of Leon, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy and The Conspirators, Bananarama, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday.

They will be available via scarboroughopenairtheatre.com and ticketmaster.co.uk.
