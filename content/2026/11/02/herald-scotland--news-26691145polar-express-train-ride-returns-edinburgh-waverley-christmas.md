*From hot chocolate and sweet treats to golden tickets and a visit from Santa himself, one of the world's most loved festive experiences is returning to Scotland's capital*

For many families, Christmas traditions are priceless. Whether it's decorating the tree together, visiting the Christmas markets or watching festive films for the hundredth time, these annual rituals become the moments children remember most.

THE POLAR EXPRESS™ Train Ride has become exactly that sort of tradition for thousands of families across the world.

Inspired by the beloved Warner Bros. film and Chris Van Allsburg's classic book, the experience has delighted almost 1.8 million passengers across 58 locations in the UK, US, Canada and Australia, and now returns to Edinburgh Waverley from 27 November to 30 December 2026.

From the moment guests arrive, the experience has been designed to bring families into the heart of the story. This year, visitors will first enter a themed departure area before boarding a specially decorated train bound for the North Pole.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21659781.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Tickets are on sale now and popular departures are selling quickly. *(Image: Supplied)*

Once onboard, the adventure begins. Talented performers bring the story to life through singing, dancing, storytelling and audience interaction, creating an experience that feels as though passengers have stepped directly into the film itself.

As the Conductor makes their way through the train punching golden tickets, dancing chefs serve hot chocolate alongside a delicious sweet treat supplied by iconic Scottish bakery Walker’s Shortbread. Families then enjoy a reading of The Polar Express before Santa Claus and his helpers join the train to present each guest with the First Gift of Christmas.

Every passenger receives a keepsake sleigh bell and souvenir golden ticket to take home, while families are encouraged to wear their pyjamas and fully embrace the festive spirit.

It's these details that have helped make the experience such a hit with families. One previous visitor described it as "an amazing, magical experience", adding: "From the second you step onto the platform to the second you leave it is nothing but FUN."

Another family called it a "wonderful, magical Christmas experience", praising the cast for creating "so many happy memories, lovely pictures and golden tickets with our names punched on them."

New for 2026, guests can also enjoy an exciting photo experience delivered in partnership with Image Insight. Families can capture memories from their journey and take home an additional reminder of their day at the North Pole.

![.](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21659783.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Every passenger receives a keepsake sleigh bell and souvenir golden ticket to take home *(Image: Supplied)*

The experience will also showcase some of Scotland's finest food and drink brands. Alongside the iconic Walker’s Shortbread, founded in Speyside in 1898, award-winning Perthshire chocolatier Iain Burnett Highland Chocolatier will supply the rich hot chocolate served during the journey, adding a distinctly Scottish flavour to the festivities.

Located in the heart of Edinburgh, THE POLAR EXPRESS™ Train Ride provides the perfect addition to a festive day out in the capital.

Tickets are on sale now and popular departures are selling quickly. Standard and First Class tickets are available, with prices varying by date and departure time.

**For full details and ticket availability, visit EdinburghThePolarExpressRide.com**
