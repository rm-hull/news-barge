A TikTok creator attempting to visit every Wetherspoons in the UK has given a County Durham pub his seal of approval.

Ross, known as Its Ross on social media, showcased The Bishop’s Mill, in Durham to his 117,000 followers in a social media video posted yesterday (November 1).

The visit comes as part of his mission to enjoy a pint of beer at every single Wetherspoons location in the UK.

His Durham appearance marks 525 pubs visited in total.

Ross praised the location of the Bishop's Mill as "unique," adding that he enjoyed the distinctive entrance and modern decor before ordering a pint of Stella.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21659698.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The viral Tiktoker's journey began in Eastbourne in February 2025, and he has so far visited over half of the roughly 800 Wetherspoons in the UK. *(Image: @itsr0ss - Tiktok)*

He said: "I'm a fan of this one," adding, "overall, it's pretty good in here."

Viewers were quick to celebrate the Wetherspoons journeyman landing back in the North East, with many praising the Bishop's Mill in the comments.

One commented: "This is the best spoons, becomes a nightclub at night."

"The best thing about this one is that the toilets aren't up three million stairs," another said.

One other said: "They push the tables to the side and it becomes a ‘club’ at weekend evenings."

The viral Tiktoker's journey began in Eastbourne in February 2025, and he has so far amassed more than 117,000 followers and over 4 million likes on his page.

Ross has now visited over half of the roughly 800 Wetherspoons in the UK, including ticking off the Newcastle Wetherspoons in January.

While in Newcastle, he said he was a "big fan" of the Quayside location, and praised the Mile Castle despite how busy it was.

Ross is yet to visit Sunderland, Darlington or many of County Durham's more rural Wetherspoons, but fans are hopeful that his review of the Bishop's Mill will mark a wider exploration of the North East's pubs.
