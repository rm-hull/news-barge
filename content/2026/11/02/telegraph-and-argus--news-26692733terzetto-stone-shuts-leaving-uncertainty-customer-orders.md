A FAMILY-run tile retailer with a showroom in Ilkley has ceased trading and is set to enter liquidation.

Terzetto Stone & Porcelain, which operated a shop in Bridge Lane, Ilkley, alongside its headquarters and showroom in Tockwith, near Wetherby and other stores across the country, has announced it has stopped trading and instructed insolvency specialists to assist with placing the company into a Creditors' Voluntary Liquidation on November 18.

In a statement issued to customers, the company said: "We regret to advise that the Company has ceased trading with immediate effect and BTG Begbies Traynor have been instructed to assist with the formalities to place the Company into Creditors Voluntary Liquidation, on 18 November 2026.

"Any customers with outstanding orders, or anyone with general enquiries, should contact BTG Begbies Traynor via the email address: [Terzetto@btguk.com.](mailto:Terzetto@btguk.com.)

"We appreciate that customers may have concerns regarding outstanding orders and the uncertainty this situation may cause. Every possible effort is being made to fulfil outstanding orders; however, this may ultimately not be possible in all cases. Further updates regarding individual orders will be provided directly to customers in due course."

The company has traded since 2003 and grew from its North Yorkshire base into a national retailer of stone and porcelain tiles, opening additional showrooms in Cheshire, Warwickshire and Oxfordshire.

It's Ilkley shop opened in 2025.

One Ilkley customer, who asked not to be named, said: "It's a family firm that has been around for 21 years. I believe around 20 staff have lost their jobs.

"We had ordered and paid for some tiles which were being stored by them. Fortunately, we've picked them up before the cut-off point.

"Apparently, one customer had ordered and paid for £14,000 worth of tiles which are currently stuck in Italy."

Affected customers should contact: [Terzetto@btguk.com](mailto:Terzetto@btguk.com)
