A lit firework was posted through a letterbox in a "reckless" act of anti-social behaviour over Halloween.

Police say the incident happened on West View Road in Hartlepool at around 7.20pm on Saturday, October 31.

It was reported to Cleveland Police, along with alleged damage to the windows of a car.

A spokesperson for the force said: "At 7.20pm on Saturday 31st October police received reports of a firework put through a letterbox of a property on West View Road in Hartlepool and damage to the windows of a car.

"There were no reports of any injuries."

Doorbell footage showing the incident circulated on social media later that evening.

It showed somebody, wearing a Halloween mask, lighting and then placing the firework through the door's letterbox before fleeing.

Anyone with information or footage is urged to call Cleveland Police on 101 or visit the force’s website quoting reference number 218199.
