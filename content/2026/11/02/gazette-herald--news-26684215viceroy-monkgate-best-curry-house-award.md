A FAMILY-run York restaurant which has been serving authentic Indian cuisine for more than three decades has been shortlisted in The Press Best Curry House 2026 competition.

The Viceroy, in Monkgate, is among the top 10 restaurants chosen by readers as voting enters its final stage.

The popular curry house, a longstanding favourite with customers and critics alike, is now in the running to be crowned York's best curry restaurant.




Speaking to The Press in 2025, a spokesperson for the restaurant said: "We have a great team of staff, some of whom have been with us for 20 years or more.

"They all work hard in providing great service, a great atmosphere, as well as amazing food."

The restaurant's roots stretch back more than 30 years, having started as a small family business selling meat and vegetables before opening its restaurant in Monkgate.

Today, it forms part of the Jinnah Group, which operates restaurants across Yorkshire, including sites in Bradford, Harrogate, Selby and Shipley.

Asked what sets The Viceroy apart from its competitors, the spokesperson pointed to the quality and freshness of its ingredients.

"The beauty of all our dishes is that The Viceroy of India purchases all its meats and spices from within the family," they said.

"All the dishes are made fresh on site by our dedicated team of staff who have worked for us since we opened."

The restaurant also prides itself on tailoring dishes to individual tastes.

"Our aim is to listen to what and how customers would like their dish to be prepared," the spokesperson said.

"Everyone has a different threshold of what they call mild to spicy and therefore we need to get it right."

The Viceroy is open seven days a week, from 5.30pm to 11pm Monday to Thursday and from 5pm to 11pm Friday to Sunday.

Readers can vote for their favourite restaurant in the Best Curry House 2026 competition using voting slips included in The Press.
