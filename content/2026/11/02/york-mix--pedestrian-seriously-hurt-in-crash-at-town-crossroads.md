A pedestrian was seriously hurt in a crash in Sherburn in Elmet on Saturday.

It happened at 7.35pm on the crossroads of Low Street and Moor Lane.

A silver Toyota Yaris collided with a pedestrian.

#### Crash location

The casualty suffered serious injuries and was taken by ambulance to hospital.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene.

“We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage to contact us.”
