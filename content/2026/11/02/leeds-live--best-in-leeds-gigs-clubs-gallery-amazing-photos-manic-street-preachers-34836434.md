# Amazing photos from Manic Street Preachers and Suede's co-headline Leeds First Direct Bank Arena show

## We had photographer Matt Shaw at Leeds First Direct Bank Arena on Friday evening

Take a look at these amazing photos from two iconic bands performing as part of a co-headline tour in Leeds.

On Friday evening, we saw Manic Street Preachers and Suede take to the stage at Leeds First Direct Bank Arena. The date being their second night on their run of UK arena shows this autumn.

The night, at the Leeds city centre venue, saw the two legendary bands play sets packed with hits. Suede’s set included airings of Animal Nitrate, So Young and Beautiful Ones among many other hits. While Manic Street Preachers delivered a 18-song set which featured performances of Motorcycle Emptiness, A Design for Life and If You Tolerate This Your Children Will Be Next.

We had photographer Matt Shaw (@mattshawphotography) at Leeds First Direct Bank Arena who captured great shots from both performances.
