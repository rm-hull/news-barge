Police were searching for a North East man who had not been seen since the early hours of Sunday morning.

Karl Molnar, 54, was last spotted on Redcar High Street at around 4am on Sunday, November 1, before he went missing.

Cleveland Police had issued an appeal as officers continued their efforts to trace Mr Molnar.

The force said he was known to spend time in several areas across Teesside, including Lazenby, Ingleby Barwick and Dunsdale.

A spokesperson for Cleveland Police said earlier on Monday, November 2: "Can you help officers find Karl Molnar who has been reported missing?

"The 54-year-old was last seen on Redcar High Street at 4am on Sunday, November 1.

"He is known to visit Lazenby, Ingleby Barwick and Dunsdale."

In an update issued at 3.30pm on Monday, Cleveland Police confirmed Mr Molnar has been found.

The force said: "Karl, 54, who was circulated as missing earlier today, has now been located.

"Thank you to everyone who shared our post to help find him."
