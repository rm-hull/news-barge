The Red Arrows’ training area activations for the month have been revealed.

They have flying in the Waddington low-level danger area on Monday, November 2 from 9am to 5pm.

The famous team will also be flying on Tuesday, November 3 from 8.30am to 5pm, on Thursday, November 5 from 7am to 5pm, and Friday, November 6 from 9am to 5pm.

On Wednesday, November 4 the area will be closed to all aircraft movements between 8am and 6pm.

The Red Arrows warn these times are subject to change depending on weather and other circumstances.

Based at RAF Waddington in Lincolnshire, the Red Arrows had flown almost 5,000 displays in 57 countries, by the beginning of 2024 – the team's 60th season.

The Red Arrows are part of the wider Royal Air Force.
