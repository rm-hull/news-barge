Richard Hammond has ruled out presenting any show other than a motoring programme with his fellow Top Gear hosts.

Hammond said should he, Jeremy Clarkson and James May ever reunite on screen, it would be unlikely that they would present another genre of show.

“We wouldn’t be very good at a fashion show,” he said in a video for the High Performance podcast, “or a cooking show, or a sports show ... We only do cars.

“The only show you get when you get us three – well, four with [producer Andy] Wilman, and you need him – is what you see. That’s the only show we can do.

“We can’t do anything else, or else we would do it.”

The presenter, 56, told the Telegraph it was “not a definite no” about the trio appearing together on screen in the future.

Hammond told the High Performance podcast he “would’ve carried on” with the Grand Tour, which the trio presented after Top Gear, after admitting the show ending left a “big hole”.

"Had the others said, 'Shall we carry on?', I would've carried on,” he told the podcast.

Hammond, who now presents Richard Hammond’s Workshop on Discovery Plus, was brought up in North Yorkshire and left Ripon Grammar School in the middle of his A levels before studying visual arts at Harrogate.

His first broadcasts emanated on BBC Radio York. “It took me about a week just to put one piece together,” Hammond once told an interviewer. “And I only got paid about £10, so I worked part-time in a bookshop and a petrol station and collected chicken eggs on a farm.”
