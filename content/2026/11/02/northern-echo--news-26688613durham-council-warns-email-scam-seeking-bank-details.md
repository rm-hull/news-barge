People across County Durham have been warned of a council tax email scam.

Durham County Council has issued a statement, saying that a fraudulent email has been doing the rounds, asking for people's bank details in order to process a refund.

Individuals are receiving emails from [no-reply@plantabird.com](mailto:no-reply@plantabird.com) claiming that they can have a council tax refund.

The email prompts people to click a link or button and then provide their bank details.

In a statement, the council said: "Durham County Council have been made aware of a new council tax scam.
