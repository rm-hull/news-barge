Harrogate's Sunday Series 2027 tickets go on general sale at 10am on Thursday 5 November, from £21. Five Old Swan coffee concerts, January to April.

Tickets for the 2027 **Harrogate International Sunday Series** go on general sale
at **10am on Thursday 5 November**. The five Sunday morning coffee concerts run
from January to April at the **Old Swan Hotel**, with tickets from**£21**.

Harrogate International Festivals says it is **freezing standard ticket prices**
for the series this year, “despite rising costs across the sector”
(announcement, via Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce).
Friends of the Festival have been able to book since 29 October.

## The five concerts

Every concert starts at **11am** at the Old Swan
(festival listing).

| Date | Who | Programme | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunday 24 January | Emily Sun, violin, and Alim Beisembayev, piano | Violin sonatas by Beethoven, Strauss and Franck | £21 |
| Sunday 7 February | Kleio Quartet | Haydn, Saariaho, Webern and Schumann | £21 |
| Sunday 28 February | Callum McLachlan, piano | Gibbons to Ravel, ending with Rachmaninoff’s Études-Tableaux | £21 |
| Sunday 14 March | Jess Gillam Trio | Music from Jess Gillam’s album Shine, plus Corelli, Telemann and Gershwin | £23 |
| Sunday 4 April | Maxim Calver, cello, and Kumi Matsuo, piano | Beethoven’s Cello Sonata No 5 to Brahms’s Cello Sonata No 2 | £21 |

A few things worth knowing about the line-up:

* **Alim Beisembayev** won first prize at the Leeds International Piano Competition in 2021.
* **Kleio Quartet** are BBC New Generation Artists.
* **Jess Gillam** became the youngest soloist ever to play at the Last Night of the Proms. It is her trio’s first Harrogate International Festivals concert.
* **Maxim Calver** was a BBC Young Musician grand finalist in 2018 and won its strings category.

## Free talks after three of the concerts

Ticket holders can stay for a **free post-concert talk** with Kleio Quartet,
Callum McLachlan and Jess Gillam. The Kleio Quartet talk is listed at 1pm on
7 February.

## How to book

* **General sale:** 10am, Thursday 5 November, on the festival website or by phone on**01423 562 303** (box office open Monday to Friday, 10am to 5pm).
* **All five concerts:** a series subscription saves**10%**.
* **Prices:** £21 for four of the concerts and £23 for Jess Gillam Trio.

The series has run every year since 1991. Sharon Canavar, chief executive of Harrogate International Festivals, said: “What makes these concerts so special is the opportunity to experience extraordinary musicianship in such an intimate setting.”

## What it means for you

If you want a particular date, book on the morning of 5 November rather than later: Friends of the Festival have had a week’s head start. If you plan to go to all five, the series subscription takes 10% off.
