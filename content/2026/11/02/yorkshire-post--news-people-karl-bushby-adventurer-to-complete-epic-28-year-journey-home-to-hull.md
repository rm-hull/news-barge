# Karl Bushby: Adventurer to complete epic 28-year journey home to Hull today

![Karl Bushby in Nevada, USA, in 2003 (tiktok.com/@karlcbushby/PA)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/31/13/v2-image-production-c6771b09b3e32e6aa362867efa239274Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkzNTQwNTM4-2.86314938.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## Adventurer Karl Bushby is to make his final stop in his home city of Hull on Monday morning following a 28-year walk from South America.

Mr Bushby, now 57, set off from the southern end of Chile in 1998, when he was 28, vowing to walk all the way home without using any form of motorised transport.

After years of dangerous escapades, including crossing the notorious Darien Gap in Central America and traversing the Bering Strait between Alaska and Russia on the frozen sea, he arrived back in England earlier this month, announcing that he had swum the Channel.

![Karl Bushby walking across Chile](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/10/31/14/cs-52469b15-8938-424e-aaec-4d910c96d38a.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


He is expected to make his final stop at Queen Victoria Square in the centre of Hull at around 11am before returning to his mother’s house – officially completing a 31,000-mile (50,000km) journey.

He will later return to Hull City Hall for a press conference in the afternoon.

A spokesperson said on Saturday Mr Bushby’s return home is bound to be an “emotional” day, adding: “He’s feeling like it’s the end of his life as he knows.

“It’s quite emotional.

“He doesn’t really know what’s out there for him for the rest of his life because this has been 28 years of focusing on getting to the next step, and that whole journey will be over on Monday.

“He says that it’s going to be interesting to see how he feels and what happens after, what happens next.”

The Lord Mayor of Kingston upon Hull and Admiral of the Humber, Maria Coward, has previously encouraged residents and visitors to come out and welcome Mr Bushby home.

She said: “So many people have followed Karl on his epic journey.

“We have all felt inspired by his spirit, tenacity and dogged determination to carry on in challenges faced by the most fearless of explorers.

“We all share pride in his incredible achievement.

“We also know what it’s like to come home to Hull, that feeling as your car or train, or feet in Karl’s case, crosses or goes under the Humber Bridge and over the city boundary.

“As the world’s media and documentary crews gather in our city, we can show them how proud we are of Karl’s achievement and how proud we are to be from Hull.”
