AN historic York pub has reopened its doors following an autumn makeover.

The Golden Lion, an 18th-century Greene King pub on Church Street, has been refreshed inside and out, with new signage, redecoration, and updated flooring, lighting, and furnishings.

Located near The Shambles, the pub is a favourite with both locals and visitors to the city.

Jo Wray, general manager of the Golden Lion, said: "This is such a brilliant pub that has been serving customers for hundreds of years, and it is great to be open again ready for the festive season which is just around the corner.

"We have plenty of entertainment lined up with live music and DJ nights as well as all the action on Sky Sports and TNT Sports.

"Come in and see us and make the most of the Golden Lion’s welcome and hospitality."

The pub also features an all-weather roof garden for alfresco dining and drinks.

Customers can browse menus and book for drinks, dining, sports and Christmas via the Greene King app.
