Plans have gone in to expand a York car wash after its popularity caused queues to back up near to a main road.

Inner Space Stations has applied to City of York Council for permission to install a second automatic car wash at its service station on Hull Road.

The sci-fi themed forecourt, famous for its Daleks, Tardis, Cyberman and Transformers, invested more than £600,000 into its state-of-the-art “Planet Wash” valeting centre in 2024.

It has proved popular, with drivers “travelling from miles around” to use it.

Drivers waiting to use the automatic car wash have brought traffic challenges, according to planning documents submitted by PB Planning on behalf of the operator.

“Due to the popularity of the car wash queuing can sometimes overflow into the wider forecourt area and also a resulting knock-on effect closer to the Hull Road access point,” the application states.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/york-fuel-petrol-2-apr-2026-inner-space-stations.webp)

The scheme seeks to solve the congestion by adding a second car wash directly to the west of the existing bay, effectively doubling capacity and potentially cutting queue times by 50 per cent.

To make space for the new unit, the applicant plans to demolish an existing domestic garage serving 337 Hull Road, a neighbouring property owned by the business.

Two existing plant rooms will be replaced by a single, smaller plant room positioned to the rear of the garden.

“The applicant acknowledges that the proposed car wash will be located closer to existing residential properties and has undertaken a Noise Impact Assessment to identify what the impact will be and the required mitigation measures,” the application states.

The mitigation measures include fitting acoustic roller shutters at both the existing and new car wash.

A new 2.4-metre-high acoustic fence will also be erected along the western boundary.

The Noise Impact Assessment says this will cut sound levels by around 15 decibels at the closest neighbouring property.

“As such, the development will result in a betterment in relation to noise emissions over the existing operations,” documents say.

The application adds that the investment would deliver crucial commercial benefits to help sustain operations and jobs across Inner Space Stations’ various sites throughout the city.
