A teenager has been charged with attempted rape and serious sexual assault after an alleged incident in York last week.

Mohammed Abdullahi, of Farnham Road, Slough, appeared at York Magistrates’ Court today (November 2).

The incident allegedly took place in the early hours of last Thursday (October 29) in Union Terrace, not far from the city centre.

Mr Abdullahi - who North Yorkshire Police said is an Iranian national - was remanded into custody and is due to appear at York Crown Court on November 30, 2026.

A spokesperson for the police confirmed that the alleged victim is continuing to receive support from specially trained officers.
