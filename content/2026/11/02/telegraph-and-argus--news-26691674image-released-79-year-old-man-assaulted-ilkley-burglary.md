Police have issued an image of a man they wish to identify following an assault and burglary at a property in Ilkley.

The incident occurred at an address on Wyvil Crescent at around midnight on Sunday, November 1.

A 79-year-old man suffered injuries during the incident. Police said the injuries are not believed to be life threatening.

![](https://www.ilkleygazette.co.uk/resources/images/21660483.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A CCTV image of a man police want to identify following a report of assault and burglary in Ilkley *(Image: WYP)*

Officers are now appealing for information and have released images of a man they would like to speak to as part of their investigation.

Anyone who recognises the man pictured, or who has information that could assist enquiries, is being urged to contact West Yorkshire Police.

Information can be passed to officers via the force's Live Chat facility, quoting crime reference number **13260620415**.

Alternatively, information can be provided anonymously through the independent charity Crimestoppers on **0800 555 111**.
