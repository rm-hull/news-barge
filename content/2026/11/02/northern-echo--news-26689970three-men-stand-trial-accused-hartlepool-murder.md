Three men will stand trial accused of murdering a man in the North East in 2019.

Hemewand Ali Hussain was found dead after being shot dead within minutes of arriving in Charterhouse Street, Hartlepool, on September 14, 2019.

Daniel Nerim Kadiu will stand trial at Teesside Crown Court alongside Armando Marku, 26, and Sajmir Dodoveci, 40, accused of murdering the 32-year-old.

Judge Francis Laird KC, the Recorder of Middlesbrough, remanded the three defendants, all of no fixed address, in custody until their trial on February 15, next year.

Four people have already been jailed over the gangland-style execution of the 32-year-old father-of-two in two separate trials.

In 2022, jurors found Eugert Merizaj played a key role in the killing team by persuading Mr Hussain to go to the address, and he was jailed for at least 32 years.

Noza Saffari, 39, of Park Lane, Middlesbrough, was jailed for 15 years after being convicted of manslaughter and cleared of murder.

Qazim Marku, 25, of Maxwell Road, West Drayton, London, and Dorian Pirija, 33, of Trillo Avenue, Bolton, were each jailed for 19 years after being convicted of manslaughter and cleared of murder.
