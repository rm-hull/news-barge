A Newcastle woman 'ragged' her Darlington girlfriend around by the hair after threatening her with a knife.

Ashleigh Walker pushed and shoved her partner when she tried to call the police.

The 27-year-old had travelled to Darlington to visit her girlfriend when an argument broke out, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Syed Ali said: “When the complainant has told the defendant to leave, she has picked up a knife and made threats.

“At one point, she said she was calling the police because of the behaviour of the defendant.

“The defendant has put down the knife and launched herself at the complainant trying to rip the phone out of her hand.

“She has grabbed hold of her hair and ragged her around.”

Walker was arrested nearby when police arrived, still carrying the knife, the court heard.

Walker had previously been convicted of assaulting the same woman, the court heard.

Walker, of Yew Croft Avenue, Benwell, Newcastle, admitted assault by beating and possessing a knife on February 18.

Walker admitted pushing and shoving the woman but denied pulling out any of her hair extensions, her barrister Elziabeth Aisbitt said.

“This happened in February and there has been no further contact between Walker and the woman,” she said.

“Walker has no interest in restarting the relationship.”

Walker was ordered to pay the woman £100 compensation and £554 in court costs.
