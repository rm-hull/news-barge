They drive through the city offering warm drinks, sleeping bags and rides to emergency shelters. According to a spokeswoman, three "cold-weather buses" operated by the City Mission will be on the road daily through March 31. The German Red Cross (DRK) warmth bus will also be in service through March 31, according to a spokesperson for the Berlin branch of the German Red Cross. During particularly cold spells, additional buses could also be mobilized. This was the case, for example, at the beginning of the year, when freezing temperatures persisted for an extended period.
