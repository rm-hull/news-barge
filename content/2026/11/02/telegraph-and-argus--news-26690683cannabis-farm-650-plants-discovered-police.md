A large cannabis farm has been uncovered in the Bradford district following reports of suspicious activity.

West Yorkshire Police officers attended Park Road Tannery on Clyde Street in Bingley on Sunday after receiving community intelligence about activity at the site.

Police forced entry to the premises and discovered a large-scale cannabis cultivation spread across two floors of the building.

A total of 650 cannabis plants were seized during the operation.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21659174.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The farm uncovered by police *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

## Two arrests made

Two men were found inside the property and were arrested at the scene, the force has said.

They have since been charged and remanded in custody.

Police have confirmed further enquiries remain ongoing.

The force thanked members of the public for providing information that led to the discovery and urged anyone with concerns about suspicious activity to continue reporting it.

Anyone with information can contact police via 101, or 999 in an emergency.

Information can also be passed anonymously through Crimestoppers.
