## PICTURES: Top chippy near York back open to 5-star reviews

Phil Phillipou outside Bish'N'Chips *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A recently re-opened York fish and chip shop is already proving to be the taste of the town again.

Owner Phil Philippou from York has re-opened the highly-rated Bish’N’Chips in Sim Balk Lane, Bishopthorpe, which had closed in early September for renovations following a change of ownership.

Phil has worked in the fish and chip industry for almost 40 years, having started when he was just 16. He acquired Bish’n’Chips from Tom Gibbons, who had ran it for six years.

Phil has previously worked at venues including Drakes York, Knaresborough and Leeds.




![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659186.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Phil Philippou outside Bish'N'Chips, which recently reopened after a major renovation. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

He told the Press: “Fish and chips have been a huge part of my working life, and despite all those years in the industry, I still have the same passion and enthusiasm for it today.”

Phil was attracted to Bish’N’Chips as it is in a ‘lovely’ village and “I felt that with some investment, fresh ideas and the right approach, we could create something really special.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659187.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Phil Philippou outside Bish'N'Chips, which recently reopened after a major renovation. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

He continued: “I’ve always believed that a good fish and chip shop should be an important part of its local community, somewhere customers know they’ll receive consistently good food and genuinely friendly service.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659228.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of Bish'N'Chips *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The business, which operates Wednesday to Saturday, has seen a full refurbishment with new branding, refreshed interior with improvements ‘behind the scenes.’

Phil said since the business re-opened last month: “It’s been a busy few weeks, but we’re extremely pleased with the transformation.

“We’ve refreshed the menu, keeping all the traditional fish and chip shop favourites while introducing additional choices, including scampi, chicken bites, salt and pepper squid and children’s meals.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659239.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new interior of Bish'N'Chips *(Image: Pic supplied)*

“We also offer a selection of gluten-free options, prepared in a separate pan using separate utensils to minimise cross-contact.

“We’re introducing both a delivery service and online click-and-collect ordering very shortly.

“We originally intended to launch these services immediately, but we’ve decided to delay them by a couple of weeks to give our new team time to settle in and ensure everything runs smoothly.

“For me, it’s important that we get things right rather than rush into doing too much too soon.”

Phil is delighted with an ‘excellent start’ despite one or two 'teething problems', and he reports an ‘encouraging’ response from customers.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659250.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new interior of Bish'N'Chips *(Image: Pic supplied)*

He said: “We’ve already received a couple of fantastic 5-star Google review from a local customer who praised the transformation of the shop, describing the fish and chips as amazing and giving us top marks for food, service and atmosphere.

“Receiving feedback like that so soon after reopening means a great deal to me and the entire team. It’s particularly rewarding after all the hard work that’s gone into getting the business ready.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21659251.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Opening hours *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Phil wants to thank customers for their support, saying he wants to serve fantastic fish and chips, in an atmosphere where customers feel welcome, offering good value in a fish shop Bishopthorpe can be proud of.

He added: “Our priority is simple: consistently excellent food, friendly service and making sure every customer leaves happy.

“Having spent almost four decades in this industry, one thing I’ve learned is that successful fish and chip shops aren’t just about the food. They’re about the people, the relationships and the sense of community.

“Our motto is “Great Food, Great People”, and that really sums up everything we’re trying to achieve."
