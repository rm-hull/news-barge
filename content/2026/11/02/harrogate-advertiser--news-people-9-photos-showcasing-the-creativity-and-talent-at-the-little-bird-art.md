The Little Bird Artisan Market brought a vibrant autumn atmosphere to the Valley Gardens in Harrogate on Sunday, November 1.

Visitors enjoyed browsing a wide range of handmade crafts, jewellery, artwork, homeware and local produce from talented independent makers.

The event offered a perfect opportunity for early festive shopping while supporting Yorkshire’s small businesses.

With delicious food, warm drinks and a welcoming community spirit, the market created a memorable day out in one of Harrogate’s most beautiful settings.

Visitors enjoy exploring the wide variety of stalls at the market, discovering handmade crafts, delicious treats and unique creations from local talented independent makers | Gerard Binks

Laura Sheard showcases her beautiful Smitten With Flowers stall at the market, displaying a stunning selection of floral creations and handmade arrangements for visitors to enjoy | Gerard Binks
