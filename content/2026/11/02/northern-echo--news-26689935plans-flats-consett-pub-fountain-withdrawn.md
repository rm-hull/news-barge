Plans to create three flats above a County Durham pub have been withdrawn.

An application had been submitted to convert the first floor and attic of The Fountain, on Medomsley Road in Consett, into three flats in December last year.

Proposals also included a two-storey extension at the back, new windows, roof windows and a raised outdoor area behind the pub.

But, Durham County Council records show the application was withdrawn on Tuesday, October 27.

Work carried out at the venue included changes inside and outside, while its drinks offering was revamped and new pub games were introduced.
