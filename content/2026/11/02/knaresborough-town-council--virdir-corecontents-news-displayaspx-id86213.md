# Time Travel Comes to Knaresborough

*Published: 02 November 2026*

The Mayor of Knaresborough, Councillor Shan Oakes, invites you to attend a rather special day when Time Travel Comes to Knaresborough

Saturday, 21st November 2026

10.00 a.m. - 4.00 p.m.

Chaloner Hall, King James’s School, Knaresborough.

This community event will be led by Rob Hopkins (co-Founder of the Transition Network, Public Speaker and Author – his most recent book being “How to Fall in Love with the Future: a time traveller’s guide to changing the world”). Rob will transport us to a future that works, a future which has – increased self-sufficiency, a resilient thriving community, and a future in Knaresborough that we can all look forward to.

This is a free event and refreshments will be served throughout the day and lunch (a variety of culinary delights from various cultures) will be provided.

To book your place please email: [deputyclerk@knaresboroughtowncouncil.gov.uk](mailto:deputyclerk@knaresboroughtowncouncil.gov.uk)

by Thursday 12th November.

Please let us know if you have any dietary requirements.

Car parking will not be available at King James’s School on that day; the nearest public car park is York Place
