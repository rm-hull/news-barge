Let’s go girls! Global icon Shania Twain will kick off a major run of outdoor shows across the UK and Ireland next year – with a date on the North Yorkshire coast.

The eight-date run begins at Scarborough Open Air Theatre on **Wednesday 16 June 2027** before heading out across the country.

The announcement follows the release of *Little Miss Twain – The Places I’ll Go*, the deluxe edition of Shania’s acclaimed seventh studio album,* Little Miss Twain*.

Tickets go on general sale at 9am Friday via Scarborough Open Air Theatre and Ticketmaster.

After more than three decades as one of the most successful and influential artists in music, the global superstar turned back to the beginning, revisiting the experiences, sounds and memories that shaped her before the world knew her name.

The album also features an exciting lineup of collaborators including Tanya Tucker, T Bone Burnett, Ronnie Wood, Josh Homme, Ray Parker Jr., and Toto’s David Paich.

In 2027, she will celebrate the 30th anniversary of *Come On Over*and the 25th anniversary of* Up!,* two era-defining albums that transformed country and pop music

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “Shania Twain is a global icon and someone we are delighted to be welcoming to Scarborough. She has achieved the kind of success that people dream of and as one of the best-selling female country pop artists of all time, that success and influence is infinite.

“We have welcomed some of the biggest female artists to Scarborough over the years –, Kylie, Britney, Alanis Morissette and Christina Aguilera to name a few – and now to add Shania Twain to that legacy is something quite special. This is going to be a big night.”

With a huge run of live dates in 2027, and a biopic chronicling her life and career also in development, Shania continues to look ahead while celebrating the music and milestones that have defined her extraordinary career.

Shania Twain is the latest artist announced for 2027 at Scarborough OAT which will also see headline shows from Kings of Leon, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy and The Conspirators, Bananarama, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison and Simply Red.
