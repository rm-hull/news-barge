ONE of Manchester's best loved bands are set to return to a popular North Yorkshire venue next summer.

Manchester indie rock band The Courteeners have announced that they will headline Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Saturday, June 12, 2027.

Promoters Cuffe and Taylor said it will be "another fantastic night" with the show also set to feature special guests The Coral and Seb Lowe.

Julian Murray of Cuffe and Taylor said: "We’re really excited to be welcoming Courteeners back to Scarborough next summer.

"They were a huge hit when they played to a sold-out crowd here in 2021, so it’s great to have them back and give fans another chance to see them live.

"Courteeners are one of the UK’s most beloved live bands, and we know this will be another very special show.

"With The Coral and Seb Lowe joining as special guests, we can’t wait to welcome fans from far and wide for what promises to be another fantastic night and a real highlight of our summer season."

This year marks 20 years since the band formed, with the group releasing seven studio albums and selling more than one million albums in the UK since their 2008 debut album St. Jude.

Their last five albums all reached the UK Top 5, and in 2023, St. Jude hit Number One on the charts 15 years after its original release - with their back catalogue including fan favourite songs such as Not Nineteen Forever, Cavorting, and Are You In Love With A Notion?

The Courteeners also played a sold-out homecoming show at Manchester's Wythenshawe Park in August and embarked on a nationwide headline arena tour.

Joining them in Scarborough are The Coral, known for their psychedelic pop sound and the hit single Dreaming of You, who recently released their 13th studio album, 388.

Seb Lowe, a fast-rising indie artist known for his witty and socially conscious songwriting, will also perform.

Courteeners join a growing list of artists set to play Scarborough OAT in 2027, including Shania Twain and Robert Plant with Saving Grace - both of whom were announced today.

Tickets for Courteeners and Robert Plant go on sale at 10am on Friday, via ticketmaster.co.uk and scarboroughopenairtheatre.com - with Shania Twain tickets available at 9am the same day.
