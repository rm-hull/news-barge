Helinox introduces Nike ACG x Helinox, a limited-edition collection combining ACG’s distinctive outdoor identity with Helinox’s expertise in lightweight, packable furniture.

Built around a shared appreciation for purposeful design and time outdoors, the collection includes the Chair Zero LT, Table Zero LT and a 17L Camp Sack.

Signature Nike ACG orange, contrasting materials and co-branded detailing connect all three pieces, designed for easy carrying and comfortable stops along the way.

The chair and table feature Helinox’s innovative Ghostgrid™ material: a translucent monofilament mesh with a reinforced ripstop grid, developed to balance low weight with abrasion resistance and durability.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21659721.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The collection is built around a shared appreciation for purposeful design and time outdoors *(Image: Supplied)*

Nike ACG x Helinox Chair Zero LT weighs in at just 534g packed and supports up to 120kg. Its shock-corded frame combines DAC TH72M aluminium alloy with nylon resin hubs, delivering strength with minimal weight. Reinforced corners support the Ghostgrid™ seat, while an X-Strap increases surface contact for improved stability on soft or uneven ground.

Then there’s the Nike ACG x Helinox Table Zero LT, the latter’s lightest table weighing in at just 254g packed and supporting up to 10kg. Designed for backpacking, bikepacking and adventures where space and weight matter, it assembles in seconds.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21659722.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Signature Nike ACG orange, contrasting materials and co-branded detailing connect all three pieces *(Image: Supplied)*Finally there’s the 17L Camp Sack, offering a spacious main compartment and two exterior stash pockets, packing flat when empty. Made from bluesign-certified 50D nylon ripstop and 300D ripstop, it weighs just 134g. The ACG edition comes in white with orange accents and a co-branded badge, completing the collection with a practical carry option for outdoor essentials.* The full Helinox x Nike ACG collection officially lands on November 2, 2026, and will be available exclusively from**SportShoes.com** in the UK.*
