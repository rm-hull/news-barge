A global tour of *William Shakespeare’s Romeo + Juliet Film in Concert* is coming to York in a cinematic live-to-film event.

The event will celebrate the 30th anniversary of Baz Luhrmann’s groundbreaking 1996 film.

*William Shakespeare’s Romeo + Juliet Film in Concert*is coming to York Barbican on**Thursday 27 October 2027**.

The world tour is produced by Communion One and GEA Live, in collaboration with Disney Concerts and Baz Luhrmann.

Presented on a cinema screen with the original dialogue and action intact, *William Shakespeare’s Romeo + Juliet Film in Concert* features a hybrid ensemble performing the film’s score and music live in sync with the full movie.

Blending rock and orchestral musicians from with vocals, electronics, and a DJ, they channel Luhrmann’s signature fusion of classic storytelling and contemporary sound.

“One of the greatest surprises over the past three decades has been watching *Romeo + Juliet* find new audiences again and again. That’s the enduring magic of Shakespeare and cinema,” said Luhrmann, a two-time Academy Award nominee for Best Picture.

“This concert celebrates both, while inviting audiences around the world to experience the film’s emotion, music, and theatricality in a completely new way.”

Originally released on 1 November, 1996, and opening number one at the US box office, *Romeo + Juliet* became one of Luhrmann’s signature works, transforming Shakespeare’s tragedy into a maximal, MTV-era pop opera.

“*Romeo + Juliet* has influenced generations of filmmakers, musicians, and audiences, and its impact continues to be felt 30 years later,” said Floris Douwes, producer and managing director at GEA Live.

“Our goal was to create a live experience that honours that legacy while giving audiences a fresh reason to fall in love with the film all over again.”

Romeo + Juliet’s defining ’90s music identity helped cement its status as a generational classic, powered by instantly recognizable tracks including ‘Kissing You’ by Des’ree, ‘Lovefool’ by The Cardigans, ‘Talk Show Host’ by Radiohead, ‘#1 Crush’ by Garbage, ‘Everybody’s Free (To Feel Good)’ by Quindon Tarver, ‘Young Hearts Run Free’ by Kym Mazelle, and ‘You and Me Song’ by The Wannadies.
