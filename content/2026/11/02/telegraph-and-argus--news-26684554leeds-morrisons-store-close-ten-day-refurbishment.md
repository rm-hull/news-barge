A supermarket is set to close for ten days to allow for a major refurbishment.

The Morrisons store in Kirkstall, Leeds, will temporarily shut its doors from Sunday, November 15, and will reopen on Thursday, November 26.

The refurbishment will include upgraded fridges and freezers and other improvements to the shopping environment.

Brad Wadsworth, store manager at Kirkstall Morrisons, said: "Our Kirkstall store is getting an exciting makeover and we can't wait to reveal the fresh new look to our customers, complete with new fridges and freezers.

"We apologise for having to temporarily close our doors while work takes place, but the wait will be worth it.

"We really appreciate the Leeds community's patience and look forward to re-opening on 26th November."

During the closure, customers are encouraged to shop at nearby Morrisons stores in Horsforth or Swinnow Road.

They can also use the Click & Collect service or order online at morrisons.com, with first-time online shoppers able to use the code 'new20' for £20 off a £60 spend.

The petrol station and Click & Collect services at the Kirkstall site will remain open throughout the refurbishment.
