Two vehicles have crashed on a main road in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said crews from Malton were called to the B1248 Beverley to Malton Road at 1.05am (Monday, November 2).

A service spokesperson said when the crews arrived everybody was out of the vehicles and the scene was left with the police who were also in attendance.

The B1248 is a busy cross-Wolds route linking Beverley with Malton and Ryedale.
