A celebration of culture, style, and tradition is coming to the city this autumn.

The Sunderland Asian Wedding Expo 2026 will take place on Sunday, November 22, from 2pm to 6pm at the Roker Hotel, bringing together more than 50 wedding suppliers from across the North East for an afternoon of inspiration and cultural celebration.

The event will feature a luxury bridal showcase and live runway, including designs by Alankar and Tushita Roy.

Hafeez Akram, event organiser, said: "We’re excited to bring the Asian Wedding Expo to Sunderland and create an event that celebrates Asian weddings, fashion, and culture.

"With more than 50 suppliers and a luxury bridal runway showcase, we want to offer couples and families something special while giving wedding businesses the opportunity to showcase their talents.

"We’ve made admission free so everyone can enjoy the experience.

"Whether you’re planning a wedding or simply interested in Asian wedding traditions, you’re welcome to join us."

Visitors will have the chance to explore ideas and connect with businesses offering services in fashion, beauty, photography, entertainment, décor, catering, and more.

The expo is open to everyone, regardless of background, offering a look into Asian bridal fashion and wedding traditions.

Organisers hope the event will not only help couples with their wedding plans but also celebrate cultural diversity and support independent businesses across the region.

They aim to attract visitors from Sunderland, Tyne and Wear, County Durham, Teesside, and beyond, while highlighting the city as a destination for weddings and cultural events.

A spokesperson for the event said: "Beyond wedding planning, our aim is to celebrate cultural diversity, support independent businesses, and bring communities together, while encouraging visitors from across the North East to discover Sunderland."

Entry to the expo is free, but advance registration is required via Eventbrite.
