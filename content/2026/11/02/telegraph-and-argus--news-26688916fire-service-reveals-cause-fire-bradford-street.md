A car fire prompted an emergency response on a Bradford road on Sunday evening.

Firefighters were sent to Horton Park Avenue just after 7.15pm following reports of a vehicle blaze.

A fire crew attended the scene and brought the fire under control using specialist equipment.

Nobody was injured in the incident.

West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said the cause of the fire was later identified as overheating.

## Statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue said: "We were called to Horton Park Avenue at 1916 yesterday to reports of a vehicle on fire.

"One crew attended and extinguished the car using a hose reel and breathing apparatus.

"No injuries logged, and the cause has been recorded as overheating."
