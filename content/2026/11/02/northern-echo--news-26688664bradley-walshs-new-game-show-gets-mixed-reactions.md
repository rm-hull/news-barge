Viewers have been reacting to the newest Channel 4 game show hosted by Bradley Walsh with many branding it as “ironic”.

Million Pound Shot was broadcast on Channel 4 yesterday (Sunday, November 1) at 9pm after gaining lots of interest on social media.

The show was described as having a “huge twist” and would offer viewers a “night of drama unlike anything he’s [Walsh] hosted before”.

Million Pound Shot gave three contestants the opportunity to win up to £1 million but by gambling their own money.

## Fans share mixed reviews on Bradley Walsh’s new game show

One of the show’s contestants, Reece, said he had been struggling financially and could only spend £1,700.

As the game progressed, he ended up gambling over three times that amount.

It was soon revealed that Million Pound Shot was not a real game show but rather a show about the dangers of gambling.

Many people took to social media after the big reveal and praised the show by calling it “brilliant”.

One person said: “Wow that gambling show on C4 with Bradley Walsh was brilliant but scary.

“I smelt a rat straight away and it put the point over perfectly.

“Online gambling virtual/casino & slots, the biggest con of all.”

Another person wrote: “Absolutely brilliant”.

Other people have branded the show “ironic”, as Bradley Walsh’s other game show, The Chase, is sponsored by a bingo site.

One person wrote: “The irony of Channel 4's Million Pound Shot being an elaborate anti-gambling stunt.

“This is the same channel that signed a 14 month sponsorship deal with Paddy Power for First Dates.

“Perhaps the biggest gamble is trying to explain that one.”

Another person said: “The million pound shot.

“Wonder if Bradley felt hypocritical considering he hosts a quiz show sponsored by a bingo/gambling company and their current incentive is deposit £10 and get 100 free spins.”
