*(Image: Getty Images)*

The Premier Inn hotel chain has launched a free breakfast deal lasting until February 2027 at around 600 of its sites.

To claim the deal, guests must book a standard rate stay through the Premier Inn website or iOS app by Monday, November 9, using the promo code 'FREEBREAKFAST'.

Stays must take place between November 13 and February 28, 2027, to qualify for the free breakfast.

The breakfast offer is not valid for bookings made through the Android mobile app or other booking channels, and guests must stay at the hotel to redeem the offer.

A Premier Inn breakfast usually costs between £10 and £14 per person, depending on location.

The menu includes cooked options such as bacon, eggs, sausages, and hash browns, as well as continental choices like pastries and cereals.

Unlimited Costa coffee and juices are also included.

Up to two children aged 15 years old and under eat for free per paying adult guest.

Breakfast is served from 6.30am to 10.30am on weekdays and from 7am to 11am on weekends.

## What Premier Inn hotels can you not use the free breakfast deal at?

Premier Inn has around 800 hotels in the UK, but the free breakfast deal won't be available at 196 of these venues.
