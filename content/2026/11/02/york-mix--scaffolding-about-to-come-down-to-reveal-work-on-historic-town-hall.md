This week residents in Ripon will see their town hall on show again after repair work.

Earlier, an agreement was reached between North Yorkshire Council and Ripon City Council and some of the key buildings, including the Town Hall, were returned to local control for the first time since local government reorganisation back in the 1970s.

The latest refurbishment programme is the first for 20 years and it revealed that the condition of the front of the building is so bad in places that rendering could have fallen onto the pavement below, putting people at risk. The City’s Mayor, Cllr Andrew Williams, says it wouldn’t have taken much for it to fall away.

The restoration work has also uncovered evidence that the façade was once GREEN.

Cllr Jackie Crozier, who represents the Ripon Spa Ward said: “I can’t wait to see our beautiful Town Hall fully on show again for the city. It’s been wonderful to see the work progressing, and I’m sure everyone will be as proud as I am when we get to see the finished result.”

All will be revealed this week, but here are some photos of what can be seen behind the covers taken today (Monday 2nd November).
