A HANDS-ON festive workshop is offering adults the chance to unwind and get creative this season.

The Broadway shopping centre in Bradford will host a Christmas wreathmaking session on Thursday, November 19, designed to support wellbeing and promote mindfulness in the heart of the city.

Ian Ward, general manager at The Broadway, said: "Our workshops have been a brilliant way to support Bradford Hospitals Charity while bringing people together.

"We’ve already welcomed 60 attendees, including a few familiar faces who’ve joined us for every session, and we’re looking forward to greeting 60 more as they craft their festive wreaths ahead of Christmas."

The event is the final session in a trio of adult-only workshops held at the centre.

Each ticket costs £5 and includes a complimentary refreshment provided by Krispy Kreme.
