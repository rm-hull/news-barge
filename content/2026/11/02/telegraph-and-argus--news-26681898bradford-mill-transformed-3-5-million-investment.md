A Bradford mill has been transformed into a self-storage centre following a multi-million-pound investment.

Space Buddy has created an 800-unit facility covering 60,000 sq ft at St Stephens Mill on Ripley Street in West Bowling.

Nadeem Aslam, the property specialist leading the project, said in total it has cost around £3.5 million, including £1.3 million spent buying the building.

Mr Aslam said the project has personal meaning, as his father worked in Bradford’s cotton mills after arriving in the UK in the early 1970s.

## 24-hour access a key selling point

He said: "We've installed a lot of tech inside the building.

"It enables customers to come in and out 24 hours a day.

"That's a big feature for our business across the board.

"We offer free 24-hour access to all our customers without additional charges.

"This is where we're covering a gap in the market."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21651641.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside the Space Buddy Storage Facility *(Image: Nadeem Aslam)*

## Strong demand after opening

Space Buddy soft-opened the site in early October with around 200 storage rooms and has already seen strong demand.

Mr Aslam said: "We had 18 or 20 people who signed up on the waiting list and now eight of those customers have already signed up."

Five of the first eight customers are local businesses, including builders and gardeners who value early-morning access.

Mr Aslam said: "A lot of people like to make an early start.

"It's not optimal for them to wait for a business to open to collect their tools and equipment."

## Self-storage as cheaper alternative

Mr Aslam believes demand for self-storage will continue to rise as people seek affordable alternatives to moving.

He said: "Often people think when they need more space they need a bigger house.

"The self-storage cost can be as little as one tenth of the cost."

## How much do they cost to use?

This is Space Buddy’s first site in Yorkshire, though Mr Aslam’s sister company, Bank Self Storage, operates five locations in Lancashire and Greater Manchester.

The units are priced at:

* 25 sq.ft £10/wk
* 50 sq.ft £20/wk
* 75 sq.ft £30/wk
* 100 sq.ft £40/wk

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21651640.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Space Buddy Storage Facility at Steven Mills *(Image: Nadeem Aslam)*

## Heritage features preserved inside building

The project was developed with his brother, Billy Aslam, who is based in Singapore, and a group of investors from Southeast Asia.

The company has preserved original elements of the mill, including a working industrial weighing scale and a restored hoist.

Mr Aslam said: "There are a lot of these in Bradford.

"People want to buy a piece of English heritage.

"If you look at an average self-storage site, it looks like a steel box.

"With the mills, it's a bit different and we tend to keep some of the old features."
