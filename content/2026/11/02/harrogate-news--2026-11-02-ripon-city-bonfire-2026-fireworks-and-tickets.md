**Ripon City Bonfire 2026: Ripon’s annual City Bonfire returns this Friday (6 November), with a bonfire and fireworks display planned at Ripon Races.**

Organised by the Rotary Club of Ripon Rowels, the event, also known as the Ripon & District Community Gunpowder Plot, will run from 5.30pm until 9pm.

Gates will open at 5.30pm, with the bonfire scheduled to be lit at approximately 7pm. A fireworks display is expected to follow at around 7.30pm.

The event is being held at Ripon Races on Boroughbridge Road and is aimed at families and visitors looking for an evening of traditional Bonfire Night celebrations.

## Tickets must be purchased in advance

Organisers have confirmed that tickets must be bought in advance, with no indication that tickets will be available on the gate.

Prices are:

* Adults: £10
* Children under 16: £5
* Children under three: Free

Tickets are available through Ticketebo.

## Event information

* **Date:** Friday 6 November 2026
* **Time:** 5.30pm to 9pm
* **Bonfire:** Approximately 7pm
* **Fireworks:** Approximately 7.30pm
* **Location:** Ripon Races, Boroughbridge Road, Ripon, HG4 1UG
* **Organiser:** Rotary Club of Ripon Rowels

Further information about the event is available from Visit Ripon.
