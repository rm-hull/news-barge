An immersive dining experience that combines art, theatre and fine food is set to arrive in Durham this month, with organisers now revealing the full seven-course menu guests can expect.

7 Paintings opens at the Radisson Blu Durham on November 28, combining projection technology, storytelling and interactive activities with a tasting menu.

The event, which has already been promoted on social media ahead of its launch, has been described as an evening in seven acts, with each course inspired by a different world-famous artist.

Guests will be taken through the worlds of artists including Michelangelo, Banksy, Pablo Picasso, Jackson Pollock, Andy Warhol, Salvador Dalí and Vincent van Gogh, with 3D projections appearing on dining tables and throughout the room.

Guests will also solve Da Vinci-inspired riddles, identify famous artists and create their own Pollock-style edible artwork during the seven-course evening.

Stuart Lloyd, general manager at Radisson Blu Durham, previously said: "When we first saw 7 Paintings, the whole team had the same reaction: Durham needs this.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651866.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Guests take part in a Jackson Pollock-inspired interactive course during the 7 Paintings dining experience. *(Image: Malte Reiter)*

"People don't just want a nice meal anymore; they want an experience they can share.

"7 Paintings is part dinner, part theatre and part art gallery, and it gives our kitchen team the chance to create seven courses inspired by some of the world's best-known artists."

The experience lasts around two-and-a-half hours and will run on selected dates until February 2027.

### What's on the menu?

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651865.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A theatrical course served during the 7 Paintings immersive dining experience, which combines fine dining with artistic storytelling and interactive elements. *(Image: Malte Reiter)*

The classic menu starts with Michelangelo-inspired salmon rillette in a shell crostini, followed by Banksy-themed charcuterie with truffle mayonnaise, olive soil and toasted olive bread.

A sea bream fillet with lemon-celery salad reflects Picasso’s cubist world, followed by an interactive Pollock course using vegetables and colourful purées as the canvas.

The fifth course is Andy Warhol-inspired grilled beef tenderloin with sweet potato, glazed snow peas and balsamic jus.

Vegetarian alternatives include tomato praline, beet carpaccio, baked falafel and grilled mini peppers. Children can choose mini burgers, vegetable chips and strawberry ice cream.

Organisers warn that menu options may vary by location.

The attraction launches at Radisson Blu Durham on November 28, and bookings are available through the 7 Paintings website. The hotel can arrange group bookings and cater for dietary requirements.
