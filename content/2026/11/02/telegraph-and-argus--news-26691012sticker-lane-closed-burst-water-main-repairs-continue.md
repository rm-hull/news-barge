A busy Bradford road has been fully closed in one direction after a burst water main caused disruption throughout the weekend.

The eastbound carriageway of Sticker Lane, outside Java Joe's coffee shop, is closed today -November 2 - as Yorkshire Water continues repair work at the scene.

The closure marks an escalation from the weekend, when one lane remained shut while crews worked to fix the damaged main.

## Statement from Yorkshire Water

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson, said:

“Due to the complexity of the burst on Sticker Lane we had to put a partial road closure in place on the inbound carriageway, with a signed diversion route, via Wakefield Road and up Bowling Back Lane.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21659624.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sticker Lane earlier today *(Image: T&A)*

"Outbound traffic remains unaffected.

“Whilst the burst has now been repaired, this partial closure will remain in place to keep everyone safe until the consequential damage and full reinstatement of the carriageway is completed.

“We’d like to apologise for any inconvenience this has caused and our team is working as quickly as possible to get the route back to normal.”

## Burst pipe first reported on Saturday

When the Telegraph & Argus visited the scene on Sunday, large amounts of water had collected in the area, with barriers positioned to prevent it spreading onto a nearby residential street.

Reports of the burst pipe first emerged on social media on Saturday afternoon, with repair crews spending several hours working at the site.

Yorkshire Water remains at the scene today, with the entire northbound carriageway now closed while repairs continue.

The T&A has approached Yorkshire Water for an update on the work and when the road is expected to reopen.
