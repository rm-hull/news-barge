A crash involving two vehicles has shut a major route in Leeds.

The A6120 ring road has been closed eastbound from the Otley Road Weetwood roundabout to Long Causeway. It is shut following a crash between two vehicles at about 5am.

An update on our traffic partner site Inrix said: "A6120 Ring Road Weetwood Eastbound closed due to accident, two vehicles involved from A660 Otley Road (Weetwood roundabout) to Long Causeway."

UPDATE: The road reopened at 7.20am on Monday morning (November 2).
