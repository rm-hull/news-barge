Ripon City Bonfire returns to Ripon Racecourse this Friday (6th November).

Organised by the Rotary Club of Ripon Rowels, the event will bring families and friends together for a bonfire and fireworks display.

Gates open at 5:30pm, with the bonfire due to be lit at approximately 7pm. The fireworks display will follow at around 7:30pm, before the event finishes at 9pm.

The evening is presented as the Ripon & District Community Gunpowder Plot and takes place at the racecourse on Boroughbridge Road.

Tickets must be purchased in advance, with organisers encouraging visitors to book ahead.

Entry costs £10 for adults and £5 for children under 16, while children under three go free.

*Tickets are available online through**Ticketebo**.*
