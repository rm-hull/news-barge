Country music superstar Shania Twain will kick off her UK and Ireland outdoor tour next summer with a huge North Yorkshire show.

Scarborough Open Air Theatre will host the Canadian country singer next June, starting an eight-date run of outdoor shows across the UK and Ireland.

Twain, a five-time Grammy Award winner, will perform some of her biggest hits on the North Yorkshire coast before travelling to venues across the country such as Glasgow, Dublin and Exeter.

Tickets for Twain's June 16 show at the Open Air Theatre go on sale this Friday (November 6) at 9am.

The 61-year-old superstar will also headline State Fayre festival in Chelmsford, Essex, on June 19 as part of her tour, in a festival which celebrates American culture.

The announcement follows the release of Twain's latest album, Little Miss Twain – The Places I’ll Go, released this summer.

The album draws from Twain’s childhood and teenage years growing up in Canada, tracing influences across country, pop, rock, soul and bluegrass.

It includes collaborators Tanya Tucker, Ronnie Wood, Ray Parker Jr and Toto’s David Paich.

Twain has also recently released an audiobook of her 2011 memoir, From This Moment On, which features narration from Twain herself.

The bestselling book charts her rise to stardom and has a new foreword with personal reflections on her journey.

In 2027, Twain will mark the 30th anniversary of Come On Over and the 25th anniversary of Up!, which were milestone projects in her career which helped cement her status as a global icon.

Twain is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, and the best-selling female country artist ever. Her pop-country influence continues through a major run of live dates next year, while a biopic about her life and career is in development.

Twain's Scarborough date will be the first stop on her announced run of outdoor concerts, and places North Yorkshire at the centre of Twain’s 2027 live plans.

Scarborough Open Air Theatre has most recently announced Twain, adding to their promising list of upcoming superstar performers.

Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe are among those who will perform at the North Yorkshire venue in 2027, marking a promising year for Scarborough's music scene.
