A man has raised almost £30,000 for Yorkshire Air Ambulance by displaying his handmade wooden tractor models.

Paul Harper has supported the charity since 2016, showcasing his collection at events around Hornsea.

His fundraising began after he and his wife saw an air ambulance crew attend an incident while they were on holiday.

His wife suggested using his wooden models to raise money for the charity, sparking a fundraising effort that has continued for a decade.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6ae87016e62d6cfab75.png)

The tractors regularly attract offers from people hoping to buy them, but Paul plans to gift his collection to Yorkshire Air Ambulance in the future.

To recognise his support, the charity’s East Regional Manager, Rob, recently joined the Mayor of Hornsea at Floral Hall to surprise Paul with a special thank you.

A spokesperson for Yorkshire Air Ambulance said:

> “Paul, thank you for your continued support and for helping us keep our crews flying!”

Alongside a selection of his tractors, Paul was presented with a Yorkshire Air Ambulance model helicopter.
