The line up for the 2027 Music Showcase Weekend at York Racecourse is now complete – as McFly have been announced today.

McFly will perform at York Racecourse on **Saturday 24 July 2027**.

A band known for their upbeat chart hits and energetic performances, fans can look forward to hits from their number one albums such as the chart topping, ‘All About You’, ‘Obviously’ and ‘5 Colours In Her Hair’.

With seven UK number-one singles, six top-ten albums, sell-out tours, plus millions of gig tickets and albums sold worldwide, McFly are one of the most significant British pop acts of the twenty-first century.

Since hitting the big time as the youngest band ever to have a debut album go straight to number one — beating The Beatles’ long-standing record — Tom, Danny, Dougie and Harry have become one of our best-loved bands, not to mention major stars in their own right.

It promises to be a fun filled weekend for racing and music fans with Madness already confirmed for the previous evening, Friday 23 July.

“Upbeat, energetic, fun that’s what McFLY bring to every show and is exactly what these special racedays are all about, making proper memories with friends hopefully in some summer sunshine,” said James Brennan, head of marketing and sponsorship.

“Everyone will have a McFLY song they love to sing along to, I’m expecting the Knavesmire will rock to them.”

A price freeze till the end of the current year, sees admission to the main Grandstand and Paddock enclosure, start at just £42 per person for a group of six and racegoers aged 5-17 enjoy a special rate of just £15 (subject to limited availability).

On the racecourse itself, The Sky Bet York Stakes will be the £165,000 Group Two contest at the heart of a seven-race card that will precede the concert.

The tickets for McFly and racing officially go on sale at 10am on Friday 6 November at the York Racecourse website.
