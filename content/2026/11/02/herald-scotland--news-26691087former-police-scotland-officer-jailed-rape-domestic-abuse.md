A former police officer has been sentenced for raping one woman and subjecting another to prolonged domestic abuse.

Derek McNee, 42, from Glasgow, admitted degrading and controlling behaviour towards both women – raping one victim on multiple occasions after giving her drugs which rendered her unconscious.

In relation to a second woman, he pled guilty to domestic abuse charges which described a course of abusive behaviour spanning a five-year period.

He further admitted being in possession of an electronic stun gun - described as an ‘ultra-high voltage’ device – which was hidden in his car.

McNee left Police Scotland in 2016 and became a dog groomer, running a busy pet salon in Paisley.

At the High Court in Glasgow on Monday, November 2, McNee was sentenced to a total of eight-and-a-half years in prison.

His name was added to the sex offenders register indefinitely and he was made subject of non-harassment orders.

Prosecutors from the Crown Office and Procurator Fiscal Service (COPFS) outlined offending against women which began in 2021.

Procurator Fiscal Faye Cook of COPFS said: “Derek McNee subjected two women to serious abuse and exploitation.

"He used drugs to facilitate the rape of one woman, rendering her incapable of consenting to sexual activity. He also subjected another woman to controlling, abusive and degrading behaviour.

"These offences had a profound impact on both victims, who should have been safe in his company.

"His conviction reflects the courage of the women who felt able to report their experiences, enabling prosecutors to seek justice on their behalf.

"COPFS is committed to listening and responding to such reports and holding perpetrators to account. Prosecuting offenders such as McNee helps make Scotland a safer place for all.”

Detective Inspector Mairi MacDonald said: "Derek McNee subjected these women to horrific abuse, and I would like to commend their courage in coming forward and speaking to us.

"They played an instrumental role in bringing this despicable individual to justice and I hope this outcome provides them with some closure and comfort.

"Police Scotland does not tolerate violence or sexual abuse in any form, and I hope this outcome gives other victims the confidence to come forward and make a report to us.

"We are here to listen and to help, and our specially trained officers will support you throughout the process, alongside our partner agencies."
