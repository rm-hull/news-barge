ROCK legend Robert Plant is heading to Halifax next summer for a headline show at Piece Hall.

The former Led Zeppelin frontman will perform with roots-folk band Saving Grace and Suzi Dian on the Root & Branch Tour.

Following the release of the acclaimed Saving Grace: All That Glitters... EP last month and back from a run of US dates, they will be joined by special guests, folk stars Kate Rusby and Seth Lakeman.

Robert Plant’s Saving Grace: All That Glitters...EP follows his 2025 album, Saving Grace; both feature singer Suzi Dian. The EP’s four tracks further explore the folk and Americana songs loved by Plant and the band love.

Saving Grace is comprised of vocalist Suzi Dian, drummer Oli Jefferson, guitarist Tony Kelsey, banjo and string player Matt Worley, and cellist Barney Morse-Brown.

Earlier this year Robert Plant was honoured with the Lifetime Achievement Award at the Americana Music Association Awards.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21658979.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Barnsley folk queen Kate Rusby is returning to her Yorkshire roots

Kate Rusby, returning to her Yorkshire roots for the gig, is one of England’s finest folk singers, renowned for her beautifully crafted interpretations of traditional songs alongside her own compositions. She has captivated audiences worldwide with her warmth, humour and unmistakable voice, with her albums winning a string of awards and her music featuring in films and TV productions.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21658980.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Seth Lakeman is a special guest

Seth Lakeman was nominated for the Mercury Music Prize in 2005 for his album Kitty Jay. His follow up was the gold-selling Freedom Fields, which celebrated its 20th anniversary this year. Multiple albums, tours and high-profile collaborations later, the acclaimed folk artist released his latest album The Granite Way in 2025, referring to ancient stories that inspired his early West Country storytelling.

Nicky Chance-Thompson, CEO of The Piece Hall Charitable Trust, said: “Another absolute icon is heading to our iconic venue! We strive to be world class and they don’t get much bigger than the lead singer of one of the most influential rock bands of the last century.

"Robert’s current musical journey has seen him evolve and embrace a range of genres working alongside the absolute cream of the crop when it comes to roots and folk. There’s an extra treat at this show with the appearance of Yorkshire’s very own queen of folk Kate Rusby. As Live at The Piece Hall expands its musical repertoire once again, as we look to deliver more variety for all tastes.”

Other acts announced for Live at The Piece Hall 2027 include Sophie Ellis-Bextor, Kraftwerk, Rudimental, Bananarama, Slash, Rick Astley, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison and Simply Red.

\* Robert Plant is at Piece Hall on Sunday, June 27, 2027. Tickets go on sale on Friday, November 6 at 10am at www.ticketmaster.co.uk
