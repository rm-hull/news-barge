Thought Bubble Harrogate 2026 celebrates its 20th anniversary with more than 600 comic creators, international guests and a week-long Yorkshire festival.

The two-day comic convention takes place at Harrogate Convention Centre on Saturday 14 and Sunday 15 November, forming the centrepiece of a week-long festival running across Yorkshire from 9 to 15 November.

This year’s event will feature talks, exhibitions, workshops and signing sessions, alongside hundreds of artists and comic creators showcasing their work.

Among the headline guests are Canadian cartoonist and author Kate Beaton, British comic book artist Simon Bisley, and Alice Oseman, the author and illustrator behind the *Heartstopper* series.

The convention will also welcome Tula Lotay, also known as Lisa Wood, who co-founded Thought Bubble in 2007.

The programme includes discussions covering the influence of superhero comics on film, the 50th anniversary of 2000 AD, comics and mental health, and romance comics.

## Two days at Harrogate Convention Centre

The convention will be open from 10am to 6pm on Saturday 14 November and 10am to 5pm on Sunday 15 November. Visitors are advised to use Entrances 4 and 5 at Harrogate Convention Centre.

The event has been based at the venue since 2019 and has grown into one of the UK’s major celebrations of comic art and culture.

More than 600 comic creators are expected to exhibit across four halls, with thousands of visitors anticipated over the weekend.

## Week-long festival across Yorkshire

Alongside the main convention, the wider Thought Bubble Festival will run from 9 to 15 November, with events taking place at venues and locations across Yorkshire.

The programme includes an academic conference, artist and industry discussions, an LGBTQ+ book club, social events and free comics for young readers. Most festival events are free to attend.

Thought Bubble was founded in Leeds in 2007 by comic artist Lisa Wood, working under the name Tula Lotay, with support from Nabil Homsi, director of comic book retailer Travelling Man.

What began as a one-day event in the basement of Leeds Town Hall has developed into an internationally recognised celebration of comic art, bringing together established names, emerging artists and fans.

## Tickets and visitor information

Final tickets for the 2026 convention are on sale, with organisers warning that the event is expected to sell out.

Ticket options are available for students, while children under 12, carers and visitors aged over 65 can attend free.

Harrogate Convention Centre is within walking distance of Harrogate railway station and bus station. The railway station is approximately eight minutes away on foot, while the bus station is around five minutes away.

The venue also has an underground car park, including Blue Badge parking spaces.

The full convention programme, festival events and visitor information are available from the Thought Bubble website.

> **Jamily Bray, Head of Venue Sales and Marketing at Harrogate Convention Centre, said:** We are thrilled to join Thought Bubble in celebrating its 20th anniversary milestone this month. Since making Harrogate its home in 2019, Thought Bubble Comic Convention has seen impressive growth, welcoming more comic creators and fans from all around the UK each year.

> **Thought Bubble Festival Director Chloe Green said:** We work so hard behind the scenes to make Thought Bubble special, and it’s all worth it when we see our guests, exhibitors and ticket holders come together as one joyous community of comic lovers.
