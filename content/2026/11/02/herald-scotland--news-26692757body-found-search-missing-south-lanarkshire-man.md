**A body has been found in the search for a 63-year-old man who went missing in South Lanarkshire during Storm Boelo.**

Police Scotland conducted an extensive search for Tom Allan, including helicopter and diving units in the Stonehouse area.

Officers have now confirmed that a body was found in the Canderside Toll area on Monday afternoon.

Formal identification has yet to take place, but the family of Tom Allan, who was reported missing from the area, have been informed.

**Read more**

Mr Allan had last been seen in the Westmains Farm area of Stonehouse at around 8.15am on Friday, October 23.

An extensive search had been launched following his disappearance, involving specialist resources including a police helicopter, dive and marine unit, police dogs and mountain rescue teams.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "Around 1.15pm on Monday, 2 November, 2026, we received a report of the body of a man having been found in the Canderside Toll area of Stonehouse, Larkhall.

"Formal identification has yet to take place, however the family of Tom Allan, aged 63, who was reported missing from the area, have been informed.

"The death is not believed to be suspicious, and a report will be submitted to the Procurator Fiscal in due course."
