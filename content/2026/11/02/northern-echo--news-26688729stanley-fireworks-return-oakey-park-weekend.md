A popular County Durham firework display will return this weekend, as thousands of people are expected to attend.

Stanley Fireworks will take place on Saturday, November 7, at Oakey Park, with the free event featuring a fireworks display choreographed to music from family favourites.

The event will include entertainment for all the family with DJ/Host DJ Lew and live music from Flat Cap Kev, Amelia Rose and Charlotte Forman entertaining the crowd before the display begins.

There will also be children’s funfair rides, food vendors, refreshments and a fully licensed bar.

Organisers, Stanley Events, are looking forward to hosting the event again.

Carl Marshall, Director of Stanley Events, said: “We can’t wait to welcome everyone back to Stanley Fireworks this Saturday. It’s been a fantastic annual event in the town for over 14 years now, bringing thousands of people together for a great family night out.

"Events like this are what community is all about, bringing families, friends and neighbours together and giving everyone something to look forward to.

"Thanks to the financial support of Stanley Town Council and Stanley Events, there’s no admission charge, making it an inclusive event that everyone can come along and enjoy.”

Stanley Fireworks is supported by funding from Stanley Town Council and Stanley Events.

Entry to the event is free. The event takes place on Saturday, November 7, at Oakey Park, Stanley and starts at 5pm, with the display beginning at 7pm.
