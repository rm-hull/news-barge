President Donald Trump rambled about the damage to the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool for 20 minutes before he mentioned the midterm elections in what was billed as his “closing argument” to voters.

Standing in front of the iconic Washington, D.C. landmark Monday, Trump called this year’s elections “the most important midterm I believe in our lifetime” after ranting about the pool for the first part of his speech. He awkwardly referred to the landmark as “a massive piece of pool” and held up before and after photos of the “magnificent renovation.”

The monument was officially reopened Monday after being largely sealed off to the public this year. The first phase of the $14 million renovation caused the pool’s “American flag blue” coat to peel, with pieces of the blue coating seen floating in the water, while algae blooms turned the pool an unsightly green in the summer.

Trump took the opportunity to tout his other D.C. beautification projects on the day before Americans head to the polls, including his plans for a “world-class golf course.”

“We have a lot of great things planned for Washington, and I could get into the nation, but for that you don't have enough time,” he said. “But I can only say that we had a war with Iran. We won.”

## Major news networks, including Fox News, cut away from Trump's speech

Major networks, including Fox News, cut away from the president’s speech before he finished, pundits noted.

“This Trump event has gone on for so long that CNN, MS NOW, Newsmax, and Fox News have all cut away at various points,” said journalist Aaron Rupar, who noted the networks stopped showing the speech once Interior Sec. Doug Burgum appeared.

## Trump says 'we're building a world class golf course' in final midterms pitch to voters

Trump has finished his speech, which lurched between touting his renovation projects and reeling off his usual campaign claims about winning wars, closing the borders and reducing crime.

Toward the end, Trump gave an update about his renovation to a public golf course in Washington, D.C.:

*We're going to make it a world championship course for the U.S. Open, for the PGA Championship, for the Ryder Cup, Presidents Cup...It'll be in shape to play that on one day's notice, it's going to be built to the highest standards of golf, and it'll be unbelievable. And you know, you have views of the different monuments, and it's going to be open to the public.*

*And we're going to give a discount to people that live in Washington D.C. Substantial discount, so they can play it. But it's in very bad shape right now. It's had it. It's a mess, and we will be building a a world class golf course open to the public, and it will be something really great.*

*So we have a lot of great things planned for Washington, and I could get into the nation, but for that you don't have enough time.*

Meanwhile, affordability - the issue voters are consistently ranking as their greatest concern - was barely mentioned.

## Trump hands over to Interior Sec. Doug Burgum, who repeats vandalism claims

Trump has handed over to Interior Secretary Doug Burgum, who has repeated the president’s baseless claims that the Reflecting Pool was vandalized.

“People that would vandalize this pool is [sic] no different than throwing a bucket of red paint on the statue of Lincoln itself,” Burgum said.

## Trump: 'We gave this a very strong shot'

Trump claimed that if everyone who voted for him in 2024 voted tomorrow, “we will win in a landslide.”

He went on to explain that the sitting president historically doesn’t fare well in the midterm elections.

“Now that doesn't happen because usually whoever the sitting president is, Republican or Democrat, good or bad, good president, great president, bad...For some reason, the sitting president doesn't win the midterms,” he said.

“But we gave this a very strong shot.”

## 20 minutes in, Trump finally mentions the midterms

Trump has finally mentioned the midterm elections more than 20 minutes into his closing pitch to voters.

He spent the first part of the speech ranting about the damage to the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool and touting his administration’s repair job.

“As you know, this is the last day before the midterm elections, the most important midterm I believe in our lifetime,” Trump said. “The Democrat Party has nominated the most extreme and dangerous slate of candidates in American history.”

The president ventured into familiar territory, including a warning about the “transgender mutilation of your children.”

## Trump moves on to touting federal takeover of D.C.

After 12 minutes on the Reflecting Pool, Trump has moved on to touting his federal takeover of D.C.

“We are making D.C. safe and beautiful again,” he said. “And our National Guard and other law enforcement partners have made over 16,000 arrests, including over 3,000 narcotics arrests, hundreds of gang members, and dozens of murderers... crime is down.”
