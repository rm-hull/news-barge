**Motorists have been warned to expect delays after a section of Scotland’s busiest motorway was restricted due to a crash.Traffic** has slowed on the**M8** , with drivers facing significant delays while the road is cleared.

The accident happened on the eastbound carriageway close to Junction 4 (Whitburn) in West Lothian, as traffic headed from **Glasgow** towards**Edinburgh** .

Drivers travelling in the area have been urged to approach with caution as disruption continues.

No further details about the incident or any injuries have yet been confirmed. Motorists are advised to allow extra time for their journeys while the situation is dealt with.
