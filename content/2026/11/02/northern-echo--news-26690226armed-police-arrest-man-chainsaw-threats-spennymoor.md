A man reportedly carrying a chainsaw and making threats to people in Spennymoor was arrested by armed police.

Officers were called to the town centre around 2pm on Sunday (November 1)

A man reportedly had a chainsaw was making threats to members of the public.

Armed police were called, and a man in his 30s was arrested on suspicion of affray, possession of an offensive weapon, and possession of a controlled drug.

A Durham Police spokesperson said: "He remains in custody while further inquiries are carried out."
