## Black Friday website builder deals: FAQs

### When do Black Friday deals for website builders start?

Officially, Black Friday starts on November 27, with Cyber Monday following on November 30. However, many website building services have already begun discounting plans ahead of Black Friday.

### How can I purchase Black Friday website builder deals?

We're constantly price-checking and updating this page with the best deals on website builders. All you need to do is select one from this page to get the best price.

Most will apply the promotional discount direct from the page. Where services require a promo code, you'll find it in the blocks above.

### What if I don't like the website builder service after I've signed up?

Just like when you sign up for a website builder service on any other day of the year, most offer money back guarantee or 7-14 days free trial for you to test it out.

Signing up on Black Friday or Cyber Monday is no different. We always recommend checking the trial option first to make sure the service is right for your business.

### Will I get these Black Friday deals when I renew the contract?

The most honest answer is no. Typically, these deals are reserved for the Black Friday/Cyber Monday period, which is the reason why all the deals listed above are time sensitive.

When the offer ends, should you pick a 12-month website builder plan that's been discounted, you'll pay the provider's renewal rate. So, it's worth checking if a longer plan may make better sense for your site. Many providers offer yearly and three-yearly plans, and the discount will apply to these.
