A PEDESTRIAN has been seriously injured after a crash which closed the main road in the centre of a North Yorkshire town.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after a person was seriously injured in a crash in Sherburn-in-Elmet on Saturday evening (October 31).

The crash happened at around 7.35pm at the crossroads of Low Street and Moor Lane.

A police spokesperson said: "The pedestrian suffered serious injuries as a result of the collision and was taken by ambulance to hospital.

"The road was closed for a number of hours while we investigated the scene."

Officers said they are now appealing for witnesses, dashcam footage, or anyone who may have seen the vehicle prior to the crash to get in touch with PC 1809 Joshua Tulip by email at [Joshua.Tulip@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Joshua.Tulip@northyorkshire.police.uk), through the website, or by calling 101.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Please quote reference 12260214717 when providing information.
