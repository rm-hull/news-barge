Sky TV customers can expect to see several changes to channels throughout November.

While the changes to the Sky TV Guide, as well as Sky+, Sky Q and Sky Glass and Stream have been announced, customers should know they are updated weekly and could change during the month.

In November, there will be name changes to a variety of channels.

Let's take a look at how your Sky TV will change over the coming days.

## Sky confirms TV changes for November 2026

Sky changes begin early in the month, with the first amendments being made on November 2.

On Monday, November 2, there will be a handful of temporary changes to the names of some channels.

These include Nick Jr. Too (Satellite 612 - Glass/Stream 208), which will be renamed to Nick Jr. Peppa, and Nick Alvinnn!!! (Satellite 605 - Sky Glass/Stream 206) which becomes Nick SpongeBob.

Sky Sci-Fi (Satellite 311 - Sky Glass/Stream 311) will temporarily be renamed to Sky ScFi/HorHD as it reverts back from a temporary change.

Ahead of the festive period, SkyAnimationHD (Satellite 302 - Sky Glass/Stream 302) will become SkyChristmasHD and Sky Halloween (Satellite 303 - Sky Glass/Stream 303) is changing to Sky Bourne HD.

On Saturday, November 7, Sky Bourne (Satellite 303 - Sky Glass/Stream 303) will change its name to Sky Batman HD temporarily.

This confirmed list is updated throughout the month, so viewers can expect more changes to come.

## 5 new free Sky TV upgrades coming soon

The news comes as Sky TV will offer five new upgrades to its customers for free.

It said the changes will make using its service easier, with things like a new menu bar and a live TV button being made available.

Customers will also notice a new dedicated sports section, and the Sky Voice search function is expected to be improved.

Sky Stream and Glass customers will also be given the opportunity to record their favourite programmes.
