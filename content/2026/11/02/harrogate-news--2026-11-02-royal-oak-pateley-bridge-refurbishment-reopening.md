The Royal Oak Pateley Bridge pub is set to undergo a major refurbishment following its acquisition by pub operator Trust Inns.

The acquisition of the Royal Oak Pateley Bridge was completed by commercial property specialists Everard Cole on behalf of Trust Inns, for an undisclosed sum.

The pub is expected to close while refurbishment work is carried out, with a proposed reopening in the New Year.

The Royal Oak is a traditional public house in the Nidderdale town, featuring exposed timber beams, a bar area and traditional seating.

James Boshier, Director (North) at Everard Cole, handled the transaction.

> **James Boshier, Director (North) at Everard Cole, said:**
>
> We’re delighted to have completed the acquisition of The Royal Oak on behalf of Trust Inns. Pateley Bridge is a fantastic location, and it’s encouraging to see an established operator committing to the future of a traditional Yorkshire pub.

He said the licensed property market continued to present opportunities for operators looking to expand their portfolios, with Everard Cole working with clients seeking suitable properties across the region.

> **James Boshier, Director (North) at Everard Cole, added:**
>
> We currently have a number of fully retained clients looking to acquire similar properties across the region, so we’d be very interested to hear from pub owners who may be considering selling, or agents who have suitable opportunities available.

Everard Cole advises clients on the acquisition and disposal of pubs, restaurants and hospitality businesses.

The company said it expected to announce further transactions in the coming weeks.
