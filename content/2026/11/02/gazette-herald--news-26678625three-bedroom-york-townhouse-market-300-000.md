A three-storey townhouse with views towards York Minster is on the market for £300,000.

The three-bedroom property, located in Monkgate, York, is situated within a gated development close to the city walls.

Ashtons, the selling agent, states that it is ready to move into, with the house spread over 928 square feet and an EPC rating of E.

The ground floor consists of an entrance hallway, cloakroom, and an integral garage.

A versatile third bedroom, study, or snug is also on this level, featuring French doors that lead to an enclosed courtyard garden.

The first floor is dedicated to the main living accommodation, including a lounge and dining room with a large south-west facing window.

The separate fitted kitchen at the rear includes wall and base units, an integrated oven and hob, and space for additional appliances.

The second floor houses two double bedrooms and a house bathroom with a three-piece suite and a bath with shower over.

The principal bedroom offers views through the trees towards York Minster.

Externally, the property has an enclosed courtyard garden, visitor parking within the development, and an integral garage for secure parking or storage.

The property is chain-free, with a share of the freehold and a service charge of £550 per annum.

No ground rent is applicable, with the listing stating that the property allows for holiday lets.

Ashtons estate agent can be reached at 01904 595167 for further details or to book a viewing.
