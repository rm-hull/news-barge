A celebration of light, colour and South Asian culture is returning to Durham as families are invited to mark Diwali with live music, traditional food and activities.

The annual Diwali event will take place at Durham University’s Oriental Museum on Saturday, November 14, from 6pm to 8pm.

The museum will be transformed for the evening to celebrate the Festival of Lights, offering visitors a chance to explore South Asian traditions through music, food, prayer and art.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653764.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Oriental Museum will be transformed for a family-friendly evening celebrating light, hope and togetherness *(Image: Supplied)*

Charlotte Spink, learning coordinator at the Oriental Museum, said: “Diwali is a celebration of light, hope and togetherness, and we are delighted to welcome visitors to the Oriental Museum."

“It has been lovely to work with members of the Hindu Society this year and develop the programme.”

Guests will be able to take part in diya (lamp) painting, enjoy Indian snacks, and watch a traditional Rajasthani marionette performance inspired by the goddess Saraswati.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653765.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Durham’s Oriental Museum will light up for Diwali with live music, traditional food, prayers and family activities *(Image: Supplied)*

The event is being organised in partnership with Durham University’s Hindu Society.

Shruti Sharma and Palak Shah, co-presidents of the society, said: “University is all about exploring new ideas, experiences and cultures, and our Diwali celebration is a great way to do just that."

“We encourage everyone to join us for a welcoming and vibrant evening of activities, learn about Diwali traditions and celebrate the diversity of our community.”

Visitors will also be able to hear traditional prayers to the goddess Lakshmi and enjoy live music from guest artists.

The evening is designed to be family-friendly.

Tickets cost £3 for adults and children, while under-fives can attend free of charge.

To ensure smooth service visitors will be asked to choose a food slot upon booking.

Advance booking is required and tickets can be reserved via Eventbrite.
