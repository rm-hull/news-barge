**Police are looking for witnesses after a firework was thrown at a woman and her children, injuring a child in a pram, as officers say "it could’ve ended much worse".**

The incident took place in the Linkwood Place area of Drumchapel on Saturday evening.

Officers say the child sustained "minor injuries" and the attack "left the woman and her children understandably shaken".

They're looking for anyone with information to come forward.

**Read more**

A Police Scotland spokesperson: "Officers are appealing for information after a child was struck by a firework in Glasgow.

"Around 6.35pm on Saturday, 31 October, 2026, a firework was thrown in the direction of a woman and her children in the Linkwood Place area.

"One of the children in a pram sustained minor injuries as a result."

Detective Constable Ashley Convery said: “This was a completely reckless act that left the woman and her children understandably shaken. The child sustained minor injuries as a result of this incident, but it could’ve ended much worse.

“I am appealing to anyone who witnessed people in the area that evening in possession of fireworks to please come forward with information.

“I would also ask those with private CCTV or dash-cam footage to review this to see if it has captured anything that could assist with our investigation.

“This type of behaviour will not be tolerated, and additional officers will be out across the community ahead of Bonfire Night. Anyone who engages in this type of reckless behaviour will be identified and brought to justice.”

Anyone with information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2804 of 31 October, 2026.

Alternatively, information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
