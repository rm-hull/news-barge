A man has today appeared in court charged with attempted rape.

It follows an incident in the early hours of Thursday (29 October) on Union Terrace, York.

Mohammed Abdullahi, 18, an Iranian national from Farnham Road, Slough, appeared before York Magistrates’ Court today

#### Union Terrace, York

He was charged with attempted rape and serious sexual assault.

Abdullahi was remanded into custody and is due to appear before York Crown Court on 30 November.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The victim is continuing to receive support from specially trained officers.”
