*IMAGO / ZUMA Press Wire*

After the peaceful end of the “Gen Z regelt” protest at the Brandenburg Gate, another demonstration took place in Berlin-Mitte. This was stopped early by the organisers in consultation with the police. The atmosphere was “very tense” from the outset, a police spokeswoman said. Many of the demonstrators were dressed in dark clothing and masked.

Eggs, stones, bottles, and fireworks were thrown at police officers, which led to the early end of the demonstration, stated Julia Scholz, a police spokeswoman. During the night, police reported that 27 officers sustained minor injuries and a total of 56 arrests were made.

The protest march, which began in Mitte, was stopped early, shortly before Diesterwegstraße in Prenzlauer Berg, in consultation with the organiser, according to the police spokeswoman. It was originally supposed to continue a few hundred meters further to the Prenzlauer Allee S-Bahn station.

At the start of the demonstration, titled “Gen Z has had enough!”, around 700 people gathered at Rosenthaler Platz near the Hackesche Höfe courtyards in the early evening, according to police. From there, the demonstrators marched towards Prenzlauer Berg. More and more people joined them along the way, and the police eventually estimated the number of participants at 3,500.
