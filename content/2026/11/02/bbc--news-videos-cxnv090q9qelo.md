# Watch: King Charles III caught in torrential downpour at Antigua and Barbuda parade

King Charles III was left drenched and without an umbrella when a torrential downpour interrupted celebrations of Antigua and Barbuda's independence day on Sunday.

Queen Camilla was swiftly given an umbrella when the rain started, while the King was seen gesturing to be given one of his own.
