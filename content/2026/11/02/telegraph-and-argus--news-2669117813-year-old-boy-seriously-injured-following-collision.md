A 13-year-old boy was taken to hospital with serious injuries following a collision involving a car in Leeds.

The incident happened at the junction of Simmons Court and Easy Road at around 6.24pm on Friday.

Officers were called to reports of a collision involving a blue Volvo XC40 and a pedestrian.

A 27-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of driving whilst over the prescribed limit and causing serious injury by dangerous driving.

He has since been released on bail.

The teenage boy suffered serious injuries and was taken to hospital for treatment.

West Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage as enquiries into the incident continue.

## Police statement in full

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: "Police are appealing for witnesses following a collision in Leeds involving a car and a pedestrian.

"At 6.24pm on Friday (30 October), officers were called to a report of a collision involving a blue Volvo XC40 and a pedestrian at the junction of Simmons Court and Easy Road in Leeds.

"The pedestrian, a 13 year old boy, was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

"A 27-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of driving whilst over the prescribed limit and causing serious injury by dangerous driving and has since been bailed pending further enquiries.

"Any witnesses including anyone who may have footage of the incident or saw the collision is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police by reporting it online using our Live Chat facility quoting reference 1290 of 30/10.

"Alternatively, call independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111."
