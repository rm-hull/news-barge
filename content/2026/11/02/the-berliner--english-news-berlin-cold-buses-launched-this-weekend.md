Several buses have been operating in Berlin since Sunday to help people in need during the cold season. They travel throughout the city, offering hot drinks, sleeping bags, and rides to emergency shelters. Three buses from the Berlin City Mission’s winter aid program will run daily until March 31, a spokesperson said.

The Berlin winter aid program also announced it is expanding its services further. The number of overnight beds for the homeless will be increased from 700 to nearly 1,000. Despite moderate temperatures, the existing emergency shelter beds were recently operating at approximately 90% capacity.

Last season, the cold-weather bus teams contacted approximately 3,800 people and brought about 1,800 of them to warm accommodations. “The trend is rising,” the city mission stated.

According to the German Red Cross (DRK), they also received nearly 1,900 calls for assistance last season, twice as many as the previous year. This demonstrates the extent of the need for help, said Mario Czaja, president of the Berlin DRK. “We expect our mobile warming bus to be needed even more frequently this winter.” Therefore, support from the public is crucial.

Anyone who sees people who might need help should approach them and ask if they would like assistance. “A call to our warming bus can save lives,” Czaja emphasised. In medical emergencies or situations of acute danger to life, please call 112.

The German Red Cross (DRK) warming bus will be operating daily from 6 p.m. to midnight for the next five months and can be reached at 030-600 300 1010.

The City Mission’s cold weather buses can be reached daily from 8 p.m. to 1:30 a.m. at 030 690 333 690.
