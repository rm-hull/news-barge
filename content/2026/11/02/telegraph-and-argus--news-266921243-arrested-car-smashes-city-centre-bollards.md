Three people were arrested after a car crashed into a set of city centre bollards.

The force said a Volkswagen Touareg had failed to stop for officers on Aldermanbury in Bradford before the collision which happened at 11.07pm on Sunday.

A 37-year-old man, a 38-year-old man and a 30-year-old woman were taken to hospital with injuries that are not thought to be life threatening.

They were all arrested and have been released under investigation.

Videos show a vehicle striking the barriers near the junction of Sunbridge Road and Aldermanbury, causing significant damage to the car.

Moments later, a number of police cars could be seen surrounding the vehicle.

In a social media post on Monday morning, City Park said that the hostile vehicle mitigation bollards had been "tested last night" and successfully protected the popular public space.

They added that the security measures were installed as part of wider improvements made in preparation for Martyn's Law, a terrorism protection of premises act.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21661275.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A car crashed into bollards in Bradford city centre last night *(Image: Newsquest)*

In a full statement, City Park said: “You may have heard of Martyn's Law, officially known as the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025.

“It is UK legislation designed to improve public safety and preparedness against terrorist attacks at public venues and events.

“The law is named in tribute to Martyn Hett, one of 22 people killed in the Manchester Arena bombing in May 2017, and follows a campaign led by his mother, Figen Murray.

“It received Royal Assent on April 3, 2025, with the legislation expected to officially come into force in spring 2027.

“Over the past three years, we have been working towards making City Park as safe as we could, including installing two defibrillators, a bleed kit, changing lighting to bright, modern LED fittings, increased CCTV coverage and surrounding City Park in PAS68 rated Hostile Vehicle Mitigation Bollards.

“These bollards were tested last night, and, safe to say, they did their job.

“The contractor will be on site this week to undertake a full survey to ensure that they continue to protect City Park.”

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "At 11.07pm yesterday (November 1) a car (reportedly) failed to stop for officers on Aldermanbury, Bradford.

"Moments later the car, a black Volkswagen Touareg was in collision with multiple bollards.

"A 37-year-old man, a 38-year-old man and a 30-year-old woman were taken to hospital with injuries that are not thought to be life threatening."
