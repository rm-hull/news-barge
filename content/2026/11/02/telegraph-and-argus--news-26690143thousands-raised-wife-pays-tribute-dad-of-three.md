Heartfelt tributes have been paid to a "larger-than-life" father-of-three who died following a motorbike crash.

Nick Akeroyd, 32, passed away after the collision on Moor End Road in Halifax last Wednesday.

In the wake of the tragedy, family members and friends have launched fundraising appeals to help cover funeral costs and memorial items, with supporters already donating thousands of pounds.

## Thousands of pounds raised

A GoFundMe page set up by Chloe Carter has raised more than £3,000 towards a £5,000 target, while a separate JustGiving appeal organised by Nick's wife, Ceejay Akeroyd, has raised more than £3,600.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21658609.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Nick and Ceejay Akeroyd on their wedding day, just two years ago. *(Image: Ceejay Akeroyd)*

## 'A massive shock'

Paying tribute, Chloe described Nick as "the best man anyone could know" and said his death had come as a huge shock to everyone around him.

She wrote: "Nick sadly passed away in a motorbike accident leaving behind three children, a wife, family and many friends.

"As this came to us as a massive shock and something so sudden we are trying to piece everything together.

"This is to help myself and his children and family arrange a funeral for the best man anyone could know.

"He was the life and soul of any party and his sense of humour would make you laugh your socks off."

## Devoted husband and father

Nick leaves behind his wife, Ceejay, whom he married in November 2024, 15-month-old daughter Autumn, and sons Reggie, nine, and Renley, seven.

Family members said Nick's world revolved around his children and that he loved spending time creating lasting memories with them.

In her JustGiving appeal, Ceejay said Nick's sudden death had "shattered" family and friends and left "a massive hole" in their lives.

She wrote: "As many are aware Nick suddenly died in a motorbike accident. He left behind a wife, three children, family and many friends.

"This has shattered us all and left a massive hole in all our hearts and lives.

"With this happening suddenly and unexpectedly it's left so many things for us to arrange and plan and now leaves a massive job of planning a funeral he deserves and making sure all three children have something of their dad to remember him by."

The fundraising page describes plans to celebrate "the man, the myth and the legend" of Nicholas "Nicky G" Akeroyd.

## 'Forever loved, forever missed'

Alongside photographs showing treasured moments with his children and memories from the couple's wedding day, Ceejay shared an emotional tribute to her husband.

She said: "A larger-than-life character, full of mischief, laughter and a cheeky smile.

"Never one to miss an opportunity for a laugh, Nick lived life his own way and made memories that will last forever.

"A loving dad to his three beautiful children, Autumn, Reggie and Renley, a devoted husband, family man and a friend to many.

"Taken far too soon, but his stories, laughter and love will live on through everyone who knew him, especially his children.

"Forever loved, forever missed, never forgotten."
