A 'lovely' restaurant in York has been named as having one of the best Sunday roasts in the UK.

As the weather gets colder, there is nothing better than heading out for a Sunday roast to help warm you up.

Enjoy Travel has released a list of the top 25 Sunday roasts in the UK featuring cosy pubs and restaurants across England, Wales, and Scotland.

Impossible in York made it onto the list at number five thanks to its Sunday roast with a 'quirky twist'.

## What is Impossible in York like?

On the restaurant, Enjoy Travel said: "For a Sunday roast with a quirky twist, head to Impossible Restaurant & Wonderbar in York.

"Impossible has made a name for itself for its iconic Sunday roast fondue.

"Yes, you read that right – Sunday roast fondue.

"Aimed at a minimum of two people sharing, the Sunday roast fondue gives diners the chance to try a sharing board of roast meats, served with all the trimmings, and featuring a huge pot of rich, thick gravy in the middle of it all – the ultimate dipping bowl, in other words."

Found in a Grade II listed building, Impossible describes itself as a 'hidden gem' and an 'unforgettable destination'.

Alongside its Sunday roast fondue, you can have a normal roast if you are not a fan of sharing.

You can choose between roast beef rump, chicken supreme, and butternut squash.

The roasts are served with potatoes, vegetables, a Yorkshire pudding, and gravy, and you can add some sage and onion stuffing on the side for an extra £1.50.

To finish off your meal, you can enjoy a dessert, choosing from things like a chocolate orange brownie, sticky toffee pudding, and cheesecake of the day.

### What do reviews say about Impossible in York?

Impossible in York has a rating of 4 out of 5 on Tripadvisor with 702 people leaving reviews.

One person said: "Second time visiting the Impossible and we had a fantastic experience.

"The food and drinks were excellent and the whole place had such a great vibe.

"A special thank you to Natalia, she was lovely, friendly, and accommodating, making sure we had everything we needed.

"Would highly recommend Impossible to anyone visiting York."
