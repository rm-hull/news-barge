A member of staff wrestled a 'gun' from an individual attempting to rob a convenience store in Sunderland earlier today.

Police were called to Mill Terrace in the Shiney Row area, near Houghton-le-Spring, Sunderland, at around 11.45am on Monday (November 2) following reports of an attempted robbery.

Officers taped off the Shiney Row shop, and several police vehicles and forensics were spotted at the scene.

Northumbria Police said a man entered the store while in possession of what was believed to be a firearm.

However, a staff member was reportedly able to wrestle the weapon from the suspect before he fled the scene.

A second man, who was reported to have been outside the premises at the time, also made off from the area. Both were last seen heading towards South View.

No injuries were reported during the incident.

A Northumbria Police spokesperson said: "At 11.45am today (Monday), we received a report of attempted robbery at a premises on Mill Terrace in the Shiney Row area of Houghton-le-Spring.

"It was reported that a man had entered the premises while in possession of a suspected firearm."

They added: "A member of staff was then able to wrestle the weapon out of the man's hands.

"The offender, and another man who was reported to be outside the premises at the time, are then reported to have made off in the direction of South View.

"No-one was injured in the incident, and a suspected imitation firearm has since been recovered by officers at the scene."

Police said they had launched a full investigation and that enquiries were ongoing.
