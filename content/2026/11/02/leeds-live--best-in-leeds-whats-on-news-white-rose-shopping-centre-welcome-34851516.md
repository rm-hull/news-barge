# White Rose Shopping Centre to welcome exciting new restaurant with big name hiring

## Work is ongoing ahead of the exciting new launch at the popular Leeds city centre

The White Rose Shopping Centre will soon welcome an exciting new restaurant.

Work is ongoing ahead of the new launch. Prezzo Italian has announced the opening of a new restaurant at the Leeds shopping centre.

The addition will create more than 20 new jobs for the area. Prezzo Italian is actively seeking local talent for all roles from chefs to front of house team members, including management. You can find out more here.

The White Rose venue will feature Prezzo Italian’s updated aesthetic. They state the new launch will combine stylish, contemporary interiors with a warm, welcoming atmosphere, alongside a state-of-the-art kitchen equipped with the latest culinary technology.

Emily Curtis, people director at Prezzo Italian, said: "White Rose is one of the busiest dining destinations in Yorkshire, and we’re really excited to be part of it. We want to find local people to build this restaurant with us.”
