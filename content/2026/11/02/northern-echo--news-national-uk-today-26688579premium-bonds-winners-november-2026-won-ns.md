Another two lucky Premium Bonds holders have each won the £1 million jackpot this month.

National Savings and Investments (NS&I) has announced the November 2026 winners.

Only two lucky winners claimed the top £1 million prize this month, with the first being claimed by a winner from Shropshire with bond number 652GW962089.

The owner's winning bond was valued at £48,990 and was purchased in November 2028 with an overall holding of £50,000.

## What is a Premium Bond?

The second Premium Bonds winner to claim £1 million this month is from Outer London, with the bond number 175WW854599.

The winner has an overall holding of £30,000, with the winning bond worth  
£5,000, purchased in December 2010.

## **Premium Bond prizes announced in** November**2026**

Every month, only two winners take home £1 million, but plenty of other prizes are available, with Bonds also able to win £100,000, £50,000, £25,000, £10,000 and more prizes.

You can check out the big winners for November via the NS&I website here.

You can check your account via the NS&I website.

Prize draws are conducted every month, and prizes of up to £1,000,000 are given away.

To find out if you have ever won a Premium Bonds prize, you will need to dig out your holder's information and head over to the prize checker.

You will need your holder’s number, which you can find on your bond record or in the app.

You can also use your NS&I number, which you should be able to find on any communication about your bonds.
