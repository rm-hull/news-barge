Amanda Owen's popular Yorkshire Dales television programme Our Farm Next Door has been renewed for two more series, with viewers set to follow the family through another chapter of their farmhouse restoration.

The commission will be divided into two five-part series, taking the programme through its fifth and sixth instalments.

The announcement follows the fourth-series finale, scheduled for Monday, November 2, which features Amanda and her daughters preparing for their first overnight stay at the restored Anty John's farmhouse.

The programme has been More4's highest-rated show for three years, according to Channel 4.

## Will there be a fifth series of Our Farm Next Door?

Yes. Channel 4 has officially commissioned both a fifth and sixth series of Our Farm Next Door.

Leeds-based production company Wise Owl Films, which has produced the programme since its launch in 2024, will continue following Amanda, former husband Clive Owen and their nine children.

The broadcaster has ordered ten hour-long episodes, split into two series of five.

The renewal means viewers will be able to follow the family's restoration of Anty John's beyond the milestone of their first sleepover at the property.

## When will Our Farm Next Door series five be released?

An official broadcast date for series five has not yet been announced.

However, Channel 4 has revealed that the new series will pick up immediately after the fourth-series finale.

It will document life with the Owen family from early summer 2026 through to spring 2027.

The filming period does not necessarily indicate when the episodes will be broadcast, and viewers will need to wait for Channel 4 to confirm a transmission date.

A release date for series six has also not been announced.

## What will happen in series five?

The fifth series will continue documenting the restoration of Anty John's, the historic farmhouse near the family's Ravenseat home in Swaledale.

After years of renovation work, the building is gradually evolving from a derelict property into a family home.

The new episodes will follow Amanda and Clive as they continue the project alongside their nine children.

Edith, Violet and their sisters will be seen reopening the family's cream tea van at Ravenseat, while Sidney and Miles take on increasing farming responsibilities.

Amanda will also explore more of the history of Anty John's, including the life of former owner Anthony Clarkson, a 19th-century Swaledale mapmaker.

Revelations from Clarkson's diaries will provide further insights into the building's past and the parallels between rural life then and now.

## Will Amanda and Clive Owen both return?

Yes. Channel 4 has confirmed that all 11 members of the Owen family will feature in the new series.

Amanda and Clive, who announced their separation in 2022, have continued working together on the farmhouse restoration and co-parenting their children.

The programme will continue to combine the renovation project with the everyday demands of farming and family life.

## What has Channel 4 said?

Jayne Stanger, commissioning editor for Channel 4, said: "Our Farm Next Door is a joy of a series that holds a special place in the hearts of our More4 viewers.

"I am thrilled we're continuing to work with the team at Wise Owl to follow Amanda, Clive and the whole family on their renovation journey, and I can't wait to see whether Anty John's might ever be finished!"

James Knight, creative director at Wise Owl Films, said the combination of farming, family life and the renovation's gradual progress was central to the programme's success.

He added: "It might seem like chaos at times, but we are so pleased that those who watch the series are finding comfort and pleasure in the chaos."
