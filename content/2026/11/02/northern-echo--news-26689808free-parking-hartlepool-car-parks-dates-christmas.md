Christmas shoppers in Hartlepool can park for free on weekends in the run-up to the big day.

Free parking will be available at the council-managed Basement, East Side, Waldon Street and West Side car parks until Sunday, December 20, in a bid to boost trade.

Councillor Dave Bruce, leader of Hartlepool Borough Council, said: "While the council continues to face significant financial challenges, we remain committed to supporting our local economy and helping our town centre to thrive."

"The return of free weekend parking is a practical way of encouraging more people to visit the town centre, particularly during the important Christmas trading period.

"We're backing our retailers this Christmas, and I would encourage residents to take advantage of the scheme and support local businesses.

"By shopping locally, people can help support jobs, investment and the continued success of Hartlepool town centre."

The multi-storey car park will stay closed throughout the scheme.

The Middleton Grange multi-storey car park temporarily closed earlier this year due to 'maintenance issues' at the site.

It also covers Remembrance Sunday and events in Victory Square on Sunday, November 8.
