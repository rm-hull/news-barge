A rapist who thought he could evade justice is beginning a 22-year prison sentence after his victim’s voice was finally heard.

Jailing Mohammed Riaz on Monday, Judge Richard Gioserano referred to the woman’s impact statement in which she said she was proud of the justice system in the UK.

“I never imagined I would have the strength to stand in court and tell my truth, but the justice system gave me that opportunity,” she said.

## Man found guilty after 2 trials

The complainant, who must not be identified for legal reasons, had to go through two trials before her abuser was finally found guilty of all his offending against her.

But Bradford Crown Court heard on Monday that the 47-year-old, of no fixed abode, still maintained his denials despite his final convictions at a re-trial in September.

Fiaz, who had no previous convictions, had originally been found guilty by a jury of some offences at an earlier trial in February this year.

On Monday, he was jailed for offences including rape, stalking, exposure and assault.

## Man jailed

Judge Gioserano said Riaz would have to serve two-thirds of the 22-year sentence before being released on licence and he would now have to register as a sex offender for the rest of his life.

The judge also imposed an indefinite restraining order which bans Riaz from contacting the complainant in the future.

Judge Gioserano said because of the victim standing up for herself the defendant now had to answer for what he had done to her.

In addition to the rapes, the court heard that Riaz subjected the complainant to physical violence and threats to kill and would turn up at her home uninvited.

## 'You still deny doing anything wrong to her'

Judge Gioserano noted that it was only after Riaz was arrested and remanded in custody that the complainant had the courage to reveal everything to the police.

He said the woman had described how the justice she had received had given her relief, pride and empowerment.

“There is no remorse in you,” the judge told Riaz.

“It's clear from the pre-sentence report that you still deny doing anything wrong to her.”

The judge referred to the fact that the severity of some cases could result in jail terms of more than 20 years and said Riaz’s overall offending required just that.
