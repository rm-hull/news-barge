An employment law expo is coming to Harrogate this month to help businesses understand changes affecting employers.

Organised by Doodle HR, the event will take place at Cedar Court Hotel on Thursday 19th November, from 10am to 2pm.

The expo will bring together guest speakers, exhibitors and event partners to offer insights into employment law changes and help small and medium-sized businesses prepare.

Visitors will have opportunities to meet local businesses, network and access advice, with discussions also planned around changes affecting landlords and pensions.

Tickets cost £5 per person and include tea, coffee and a light lunch. Proceeds from ticket sales will be donated to the British Thyroid Foundation.

Local businesses are also being invited to exhibit, with two packages available.

A £150 package includes eight feet of exhibition space, a six-foot table, space for pop-up banners and two exhibitor passes with refreshments and lunch.

A £50 package includes space for pop-up banners and one exhibitor pass with refreshments and lunch.

*Attendees can book through Cedar Court Harrogate’s events**website**. Businesses interested in exhibiting can email [admin@doodlehr.co.uk](mailto:admin@doodlehr.co.uk), or call Doodle HR on 01423 425575 for more information.*
