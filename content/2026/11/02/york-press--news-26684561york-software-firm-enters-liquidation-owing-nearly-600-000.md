A York software company has gone into liquidation with debts approaching £600,000.

Paperless Construction Ltd was incorporated in January 2019 and was registered at Askham Fields Lane, Askham Bryan, York.

The company went into a creditor’s voluntary liquidation on October 12, with Lancashire-based Business Guardian UK appointed liquidator.

Companies House lists its sole director as Raymond Castelyn.




* **Andante Group of York in liquidation owing more than £12,000**
* **Quickfix@home (York) and Quickfix (York) in liquidation**
* **York dental business in liquidation owing more than £400,000**
* **York employment agency liquidated owing more than £31,000**

A liquidator’s report known as a statement of affairs outlines the financial situation.

The statement says the business had assets of £3,840 cash in Lad.

It had no preference creditors - those who are paid first.

However, HMRC was owed £15,000 as a secondary preference creditor - someone paid next.

The statement says there were 15 unsecured trade creditors, who were owed £568,123.24 plus HMRC owed a further £15,000 as an unsecured creditor, giving a total of £583,123.24 in unsecured claims.

The unsecured creditors included Catelyn and Associates of Weston-super-Mare, which was the largest creditor, owed £404,293.24.

Funding Circle of London was owed £30,000 and Lloyds Bank was owed £40,000.

Nikivox (Pty) Ltd was owed £47,029.10.

Altogether, this meant a deficit of £594,283.24.

With Paperless Construction having issued and called up capital of £200, this gave a final shortfall or deficit of £594,483.24.
