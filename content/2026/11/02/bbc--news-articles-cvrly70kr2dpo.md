![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mirror reads: 'Duty of care.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/f777/live/d20e3520-d709-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood's revision of settlement plans for migrant care workers features prominently in Tuesday's papers. Mahmood had planned to make foreign care workers already in the UK wait 15 years to qualify for permanent settlement. Instead she has "softened plans" to help the struggling sector, the Daily Mirror reports.

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: 'UK immigration crackdown: Mahmood will exempt 280,000 people in U-turn.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/68dc/live/5cf91020-d709-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The i Paper headlines on Mahmood's "U-turn" to "exempt 280,000 people" by allowing care workers already in the country to wait five years, instead of 15 years, to apply for indefinite leave to remain. But anyone "seeking settled status needs four 'non negotiables", including a spotless criminal record, the paper explains.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Mail reads: 'Mahmood's £10 billion migrant climbdown.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/8944/live/19aa0bf0-d70c-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

The Daily Mail headlines on "Mahmood's £10 billion migrant climbdown", describing it as the home secretary abandoning "tougher residency rules after backbench revolt". Meanwhile, the Duke and Duchess of Sussex are pictured as they "move into new Cotswolds home", the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Express reads: 'Labour's "cave in" over migrant right to stay.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/f409/live/1c037e90-d707-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

The Daily Express describes the home secretary's announcement as a Labour "cave in" over migrant settlement rights in the UK. The move comes after "furious backlash from her own cabinet colleagues and trade union barons", the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Telegraph reads: 'Mahmood migrant U-turn to cost £10bn.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/6c60/live/8f091290-d709-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

The home secretary has "watered down" her migration policy, the Daily Telegraph says. Shabana Mahmoo's move comes despite her previously warning that doing so risked costing the taxpayer £10bn the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Guardian reads: 'Mahmood softens immigration plans in face of cabinet backlash.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/2a97/live/20daa1c0-d70a-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

The Guardian says Shabana Mahmood has "rowed back" on her "most hardline plans" for migrant care workers. The paper notes that MPs were warned by the home secretary that some migrants would still face "retrospective rule changes to make them wait a decade" before granting them leave to remain, the paper reports.

![The headline on the front page of the Times reads: 'Mahmood backs down on curbs to immigration.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/cd5a/live/3fd3f4e0-d710-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

"Hundreds of thousands of foreign care workers and their families will be spared" as a result of Shabana Mahmood's "back down on curbs to immigration", the Times reports. The paper quotes government sources saying the reforms were still "very significant" and a "total transformation" of the system.

![The headline on the front page of the Metro reads: 'The new cold war.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/9723/live/8f249bc0-d707-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

A cyber-attack targeting dozens of supermarkets across several European countries has put Britain on high alert, Metro reports under the headline "The new cold war". Huge amounts of food has been thrown away after store freezers were reportedly turned up to as high as 50C (122F), the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Sun reads: 'The Cold War.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/6426/live/aefeaec0-d709-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

The Sun similarly headlines on "The cold war", and points to the UK being on alert as "hackers heat up supermarket fridges across Europe". Elsewhere on the page, the I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! lineup is revealed.

![The headline on the front page of the i Paper reads: 'Petrol price record deals big blow to Trump on eve of midterm elections.' ](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/23b9/live/55b60090-d707-11f1-b441-2b33b063138f.jpg)

Petrol prices are predicted to hit a record high in the US ahead of the midterm elections, the Financial Times reports. As per a nationwide survey from the FT, nearly 70% of voters "disapprove of how Trump has handled inflation and the cost of living", the paper says.

![The headline on the front page of the Independent reads: 'Jails are in crisis... and there's no plan to fix them.'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/b178/live/4a0188f0-d70c-11f1-93e1-3b657b665bcf.jpg)

The Independent leads on an exclusive into a crisis in UK jails following a warning from a prisons inspector. "Inmates languish in bed, high on drone-delivered drugs", it says.

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: 'Wazza's wonga of Oz!'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/800/cpsprodpb/4e85/live/f3282d10-d709-11f1-a701-fddedf244c16.jpg)

And English footballer Jude Bellingham is pictured lounging in underwear for an ad campaign at the top of the Daily Star. Meanwhile, former England football star Wayne Rooney has been tipped to win the upcoming season of I'm A Celeb, the paper reports.
