A group of rugby players have swapped their kits for some rather more glamorous outfits to raise money in the fight against motor neurone disease.

Members of Ryton Rugby Club have posed as female icons for a fundraising calendar, with all proceeds supporting the Motor Neurone Disease Association and the My Name’5 Doddie Foundation.

The project was inspired by David Knowles, who was diagnosed with motor neurone disease in January 2025.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653170.jpg?type=mds-article-620)December - Marilyn Monroe *(Image: (Joe Costigan Photography))*

Mr Knowles said: "Some of my best friends at Ryton RUFC have shown their true colours, dressing as iconic stars for the calendar – proving that fundraising can be brave, brilliant and a little bit glamorous."

His son Daniel previously ran the London Marathon to raise funds for the MND Association.

Photographer Joe Costigan explained how the idea came together.

Mr Costigan said: "In February 2026, I was asked by my friend Duncan if I’d be interested in creating a calendar with some guys he used to play rugby with.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653172.jpg?type=mds-article-620)May *(Image: (Joe Costigan Photography))*

"This led to an online meeting and chat with David, and I suggested that rather than going for the nude calendar format, we try something different.

"We decided to recreate iconic female images, using the rugby guys and having a bit of fun.

"To keep costs down and have a bit of fun, we never planned to be too slick, no AI was used and the guys mostly costumed themselves from charity shops and Vinted."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653173.jpg?type=mds-article-620)September - Pamela Anderson in her famous Baywatch swimsuit *(Image: (Joe Costigan Photography))*

He hopes the calendar will be well-received and make a real difference.

Mr Costigan said: "I hope you agree – a tremendous effort for a worthy cause.

"No egos were harmed in the production of this calendar."

Each month features rugby players recreating iconic female images.

Every calendar sold helps raise vital funds for the MNDA, supporting research, care and those affected by MND.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653174.jpg?type=mds-article-620)October *(Image: (Joe Costigan Photography))*

A spokesperson for Ryton Rugby Club said: "With the rugby connection, we decided to also raise money for the My Name’5 Doddie Foundation.

"100% proceeds of the calendar sales will be split 50/50 between the two charities to continue the fight against this horrible disease."

Each month has been generously sponsored, to cover the printing costs, so that every penny spent on calendars goes to the charities.
