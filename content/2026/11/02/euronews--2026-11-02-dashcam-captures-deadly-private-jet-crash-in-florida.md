Dashcam footage captured the moment a private jet crashed near a busy roadway in Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, 1 November, killing all nine people aboard, authorities said.

The Bombardier Learjet 60 went down near Military Trail and 10th Avenue North in the Greenacres area at around 12:50 p.m., erupting into a fireball and sending thick black smoke into the air.

According to the Federal Aviation Administration, the aircraft had departed Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was heading to the Bahamas.

The dashcam video captures the moment the plane falls from the sky and the person recording reacts in shock.
