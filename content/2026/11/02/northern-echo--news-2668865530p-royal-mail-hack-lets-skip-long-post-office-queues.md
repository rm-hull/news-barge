If you regularly are sending parcels for things like Vinted or just to friends and family, you might spend a lot of time standing in queues to drop off your deliveries.

Many people still opt for using Royal Mail despite the popularity of other delivery companies such as Evri, DHL, and Yodel.

Posting items using Royal Mail can cost from £2.70 to £3.25 for small standard parcels that are sent using Second Class to £30 for large packages or using next-day Special Delivery.

If you are busy and do not want to be spending time in lengthy posting queues, few people know that you can pay just 30p for Royal Mail to collect a parcel directly from your doorstep.

## 30p Royal Mail hack that cuts lengthy queues

On the MoneySavingExpert website, it says that if you are sending a parcel or prepaid return using Royal Mail, the delivery company will collect it for just 30p.

The website reads: “If you’ve checked the ‘send it yourself’ options and found that Royal Mail is the best choice for you – or not far off – it will collect parcels for 30p.

“Using it means a postie will collect the parcel you’re sending from your doorstep or a nominated ‘safe place’, rather than you having to take it to a Post Office.

“It’s particularly useful if you’re sending a bulky item, several parcels at once or would have difficulty getting to the Post Office.”

This hack is useful for anyone who regularly uses Vinted or Depop to sell things as well as those with packages that might be difficult to get to your Post Office.

To do this, you need to pay for postage using the Royal Mail website or app and add a collection on for 30p per parcel.

You can then choose a day and time for collection, which is usually a four-hour window.

You receive an email as proof of postage once your parcel has been collected.
