A man who breached the terms of his sexual risk order by having three Instagram accounts has appeared in court.

Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard that Bobak Beckensall was given the order despite never being charged with or convicted of a sex offence.

The 27-year-old told police about the accounts during a routine check of his mobile phone.

Prosecutor Syed Ali said Beckensall had failed to register the three accounts with the police as he was required through his SRO.

Beckensall, of Eden Lane, Peterlee, admitted three breaches dating from July 21.

His barrister, Neil Taylor, said Beckensall’s mental health and neurodivergent conditions affected his ability to understand the order fully.

He said: “The SRO was put in place as a result of a police investigation. They didn’t need to prove conviction and he has never been charged with anything.

“He has used an old account and he used some accounts connected to his artwork.
