A POPULAR 2000s band are set headline a night of racing and music at York Racecourse next year.

Pop band McFly, known for hits including All About You and 5 Colours in Her Hair, will perform on Saturday, July 24, as part of the 2027 Music Showcase Weekend.

Their performance will follow an afternoon of racing, with the £165,000 Sky Bet York Stakes taking centre stage.

James Brennan, Head of Marketing and Sponsorship said, “Upbeat, energetic, fun that’s what McFLY bring to every show and is exactly what these special racedays are all about, making proper memories with friends hopefully in some summer sunshine.

"Everyone will have a McFLY song they love to sing along to, I’m expecting the Knavesmire will rock to them. On sale with a price freeze for next year, so it makes sense to lock in the date.”

Since being formed in 2003, McFly have enjoyed seven UK number-one singles, six top-ten albums, and millions of tickets and albums sold worldwide.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21070313.jpg?type=mds-article-620)McFly *(Image: Lee Blanchflower)*

Their debut album made them the youngest band ever to go straight to number one - beating a record previously held by The Beatles.

The band will join Madness - who are set to perform at the racecourse on July 23, 2027 - to round out the Music Showcase weekend.

Tickets go on sale at 10am on Friday, November 6, via yorkracecourse.co.uk, with a price freeze in place until the end of the year.
