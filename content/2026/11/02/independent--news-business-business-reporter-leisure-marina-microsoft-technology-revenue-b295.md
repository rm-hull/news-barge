*Elite Dynamics is a Business Reporter client*

The leisure industry is evolving at pace.

Customer expectations are rising. Multi-site portfolios are expanding. Revenue streams are diversifying. Yet many operators remain constrained by fragmented systems and siloed data structures that limit visibility, slow reporting and restrict scalable growth.

Elite Dynamics delivers intelligent, Microsoft-powered platforms purpose-built for holiday park and marina operators. Built entirely within Microsoft Dynamics 365 and the wider Microsoft ecosystem, the platform connects finance, bookings, CRM, owner management, sales, operations and analytics into one unified environment.

The objective is clear: replace fragmentation with connected intelligence.

## **Supporting multi-entity and intercompany operations**

Across the leisure sector, growth often brings structural complexity.

Many park and marina groups operate through multiple legal entities, separate trading companies for different sites, brokerage arms, property holding vehicles or international subsidiaries. Managing intercompany transactions, consolidated reporting and shared services across these structures can become increasingly complex.

Without consolidated visibility, leadership teams struggle to make timely commercial decisions – particularly in an environment where demand fluctuates seasonally and consumer confidence can shift rapidly.

Elite Dynamics provides native intercompany functionality within Microsoft Dynamics 365.

This enables benefits such as automated intercompany postings, consolidated financial reporting across entities, multi-site performance visibility, shared services finance structures, multi-currency capability and clear audit trails and compliance.

Rather than stitching together separate accounting systems, leadership teams gain a single, consolidated view of performance across the entire portfolio – essential in an increasingly competitive market.

This capability is particularly attractive to larger operators and acquisitive groups looking to scale efficiently.

## **Connecting holiday parks: owners and sales**

For holiday parks, commercial performance extends well beyond holiday bookings.

Owner management and structured sales processes are fundamental drivers of long-term value, particularly as customer demand becomes more experienced led and digitally influenced.

Prospective buyers expect responsive communication, clear availability, and streamlined purchasing processes. Existing owners expect digital access to site fee statements, compliance documentation, and maintenance updates.

Elite Dynamics connects the full owner lifecycle within one platform.

From pitch availability and stock control through to lead management, sales pipeline tracking, contract documentation and deposit management, the system provides end-to-end visibility of the sales journey.

Post-sale, the platform transitions seamlessly into:

* Site fee billing and renewals
* Utilities and compliance tracking
* Owner communications
* Maintenance scheduling
* Digital owner engagement

By integrating sales, finance and owner administration, operators gain clearer forecasting, stronger retention management and improved commercial control, while delivering a more professional and transparent experience to owners.

Bookings, ownership revenue and operational delivery sit within one connected commercial engine.

## **Integrating marina operations end-to-end**

Within the marina sector, customers increasingly expect transparency, digital communication and clarity about renewals, brokerage listings and service schedules.

Operational fragmentation has historically limited scalability and responsiveness.

Elite Dynamics integrates all the key aspects of marina management, from mooring allocation, berth management and brokerage sales to boatyard services and work orders, financial management, inventory and stock control and customer engagement.

Recurring mooring fees, brokerage commissions and service revenue streams are visible within one consolidated platform. For multi-site marina groups operating across jurisdictions, intercompany consolidation and multi-currency functionality provide group-level clarity without manual reconciliation, while enabling a smoother customer journey from enquiry to renewal.

## **From business intelligence to artificial intelligence: turning data into revenue**

True transformation is not powered by data alone, but by actionable insight. In an environment where demand patterns can shift quickly, operators need real-time visibility of occupancy, sales pipelines, owner retention and revenue performance.

Elite Dynamics embeds Microsoft Power BI at the core of its platform, enabling real-time dashboards across finance, occupancy, sales pipelines and operational performance. That means leadership teams can move from reactive reporting to proactive decision-making.

The progression does not stop business intelligence. Through Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft’s integrated AI interface embedded within Outlook, Excel, Teams, Word, and Dynamics 365, operators move from BI to AI.

Copilot enables users to interrogate data in natural language, generate forecasts and scenario analysis, summarise operational performance, draft communications based on customer engagement history and identify emerging commercial trends.

This creates a seamless evolution: operational Data to business intelligence to AI-driven insight to revenue.

In a market shaped by changing consumer behaviour and competitive pressure, this progression enables faster decisions, improved forecasting accuracy and greater commercial agility.

## **A Microsoft-native platform with global reach**

As a Microsoft partner and Microsoft Partner of the Year Finalist (2023), Elite Dynamics builds exclusively within the Microsoft ecosystem.

Hosted securely within Microsoft Azure, the platform provides enterprise-grade resilience, security, and scalability.

Under CEO Jamaine Campbell, who brings over 25 years of experience across technology and hospitality, the company has focused on unlocking the full power of Microsoft’s stack for leisure operators navigating both structural and customer-driven change.

The platform integrates:

* Dynamics 365
* Power BI
* Power Automate
* Microsoft Teams
* Outlook, Excel, Word and PowerPoint
* Copilot

This unified environment allows operators to scale confidently across locations and international markets while responding to evolving customer demand.

## **Industry collaboration and sector leadership**

Elite Dynamics works closely with industry bodies including British Marine, ICOMIA, AMI and the Holiday and Residential Parks Association (HARPA) to ensure its solutions align with sector-specific priorities.

The company is currently developing a membership management platform for HARPA, supporting digital modernisation at both association and operator level. Designed as a scalable model, this framework can be extended to similar trade bodies worldwide, enabling global associations to enhance member engagement, improve operational efficiency and unify their digital infrastructure.

## **Scaling globally in a changing market**

Founded in the UK and now operating with offices in the United States and Canada, Elite Dynamics is actively expanding its footprint globally through strategic partnerships and Microsoft co-sell programmes.

Marina and park groups worldwide face the same dual pressures: structural system fragmentation and rising customer expectations.

By delivering intercompany-capable, AI-enabled platforms built on Microsoft technology, Elite Dynamics enables operators to modernise with confidence, respond to demand shifts and scale without complexity.

## **Redefining the art of the possible**

Digital transformation in leisure is no longer optional. Operators require clarity across entities, visibility across revenue streams, responsiveness to customer demand and intelligent decision-making.

By combining deep industry expertise with Microsoft-native architecture and embedding Copilot-driven AI directly into operations, Elite Dynamics is redefining the art of the possible for the global leisure industry.
