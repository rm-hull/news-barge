A sex predator who lied about his age groomed and abused a teenager, leaving her feeling "disgusted and humiliated".

Lewis Bradley targeted the teenager on social media and invited her to his home in 2023 before he subjected her to multiple sex attacks.

He told her that he was 18 years old, but he was actually 28.

Bradley claimed he believed the girl was at least 17-years-old and denied telling her he was 18.

The offences came to light after the girl's mum alerted authorities.

Bradley was arrested and officers seized Bradley's mobile phone, on which they discovered images of children.

The now-32-year-old was charged with grooming, multiple counts of sexual activity with a child, rape and possession of indecent images of a child.

On the first day of his trial in August, he pleaded guilty to all charges except rape, which he denied.

Following discussions with the Crown Prosecution Service, the rape allegation was amended to a further count of sexual activity with a child, which Bradley, formerly of Wyndham Way in North Shields, then admitted.

He returned to court on October 19, where he was sentenced to four years and six months in prison.

The victim said: "For a while I blamed myself for what happened to me.

"Day after day I cried, wondering if I would ever feel like myself again - I felt disgusted and humiliated.

"But I soon realised that it's not my fault that he took advantage of me.

"What he did changed me in so many ways, and now I'm scared to trust people.

"Sometimes it replays in my head and keeps me awake at night."

Bradley was also made subject to a 15-year Sexual Harm Prevention Order and will remain on the Sex Offenders' Register for life.
