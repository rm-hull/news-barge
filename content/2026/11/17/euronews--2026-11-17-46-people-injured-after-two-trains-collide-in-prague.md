## Ivan Bednárik, the Czech Republic's minister for transport, thanked paramedics and firefighters for their "professional and swift response" and said authorities had launched an investigation into the incident.

Two trains collided in Prague on Tuesday morning near the city's main train station, leaving 46 people injured.

The incident occurred on an overpass at Seifertova street in the Prague 3 district.

Prague's emergency medical service said four people sustained severe injuries in the accident. A further 12 people suffered "moderate" injuries, while 30 had minor injuries, it added.

Video footage posted on social media by police showed emergency crews working at the scene and people being taken away on stretchers. The trains can be seen crumpled together, with damage to both.

"The trauma response was concluded at 9:40 a.m. The first crew arrived on the scene within four minutes of the incident being reported," the emergency service said in a post on social media.

"More than 10 emergency response crews, four crews with a doctor, an inspector, and a special evacuation vehicle gradually arrived on the scene," it added. "All patients with priority levels 1 and 2 have already been transported to medical facilities. We will provide the exact numbers later."

Ivan Bednárik, the Czech Republic's minister for transport, thanked paramedics and firefighters for their "professional and swift response" and said authorities had launched an investigation into the incident.

Operations at the main station were restored by around 1 pm local time.
