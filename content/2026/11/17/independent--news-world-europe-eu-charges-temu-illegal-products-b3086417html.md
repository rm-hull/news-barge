* The European Commission has charged e-commerce giant Temu following preliminary findings that it failed to prevent the sale of illegal and unsafe products on its platform.
* Regulators found that traders could commit numerous serious infringements before Temu suspended any of their online stores.
* Temu, owned by PDD Holdings, stated it disagrees with the preliminary findings and expressed confidence in reaching an “amicable resolution”.
* If the findings are confirmed, the company could face a fine of up to six per cent of its annual global turnover.
* The charges represent the second major action against Temu under the EU Digital Services Act, following a €200 million fine in May.
