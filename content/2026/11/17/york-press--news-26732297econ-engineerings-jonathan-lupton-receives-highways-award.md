A lifetime of service in the highways industry has been formally recognised.

Jonathan Lupton, of North Yorkshire-based Econ Engineering, received a long service medal at the Highways Champion Awards in recognition of more than 40 years of dedication to the sector.

The award was presented by TV personality Vernon Kay at the ceremony on November 4.

Mr Lupton said: "It's a real honour to receive this award.

"I've been fortunate to work alongside fantastic colleagues and customers throughout my career, and I'm proud to have spent more than 40 years supporting an industry that plays such an important role in keeping the country moving."

Mr Lupton joined Econ Engineering in 1985 and took ownership of the business in 2003 alongside his late brother, Andrew.

Under his leadership, Econ has become the UK's leading manufacturer of gritters, snowploughs and winter maintenance vehicles, supporting local authorities and contractors in keeping roads safe throughout the year.
