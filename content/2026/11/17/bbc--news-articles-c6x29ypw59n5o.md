![The front page of the Daily Telegraph with the main headline "No savings for a decade in Milburn's benefits plan" and the main photograph of actor Robert Pattinson with a gun in a suit for his role as the new James Bond.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/3358/live/263a2a20-e216-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

Alan Milburn's long-awaited report into youth unemployment, to be unveiled on Tuesday, will call for sweeping reforms from the classroom to the NHS to turn the "lost generation" into the "work generation", writes the Daily Telegraph. Labour's plan aims to claw back £200m a year by 2036 and reject "crude benefit cuts". Like most UK newspapers today, the front page also reveals actor Robert Pattinson has been announced at the new James Bond.

![The Daily Mail front page headline says "Labour is to blame for youth jobs crisis" and the main photograph is actor Robert Pattinson dressed in a suit with a gun and the headline "We weren't expecting you, Mr Pattinson!"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/e2c6/live/b484c000-e217-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Daily Mail also covers the release of the Milburn review, reporting that the landmark investigation warns Labour's tax hikes, rises in the youth minimum wage and new workers' rights charters have made the job market worse.

![The Metro Newspaper front page headline says "Can we fix it? YOS we can". It also features a picture of Robert Pattinson in a suit with a gun and the headline "We've been expecting you... R-Patz is Bond".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/b7b4/live/bb888e90-e217-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The Metro focuses on one of the major reforms published in the 250-page Milburn report, launching a new Youth Opportunities Service across the country to give jobless young people a better future. The recommendation would provide a dedicated helper for career, health and practical support and a personalised plan.

![The iPaper front page headline reads "UK firms demand tax cut to hire more under-25s - at the price for supporting Burnham's big plan".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/c99a/live/c09ce930-e217-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The i Paper says British businesses are set to demand tax cuts in exchange for supporting the new plan to get more 16- to 24-year-olds into work. It reports government insiders fear businesses wanting the national insurance cuts for young workers immediately - quicker than it will be offered- will derail the proposal.

![The front page headline of The Sun says "The name's Patz... R-Patz" with a photograph of actor Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/9c46/live/6e7a2170-e21e-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Sun highlights the unveiling of the seventh James Bond as 40-year-old Robert Pattinson, who said it was the "honour of a lifetime" to play the iconic spy role.

![The Mirror front page headline reads "Outside lands 007 role, 'We've not been expecting you, Mr Bond'.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/c826/live/274ae7c0-e21f-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

"We've not been expecting you, Mr Bond": The Mirror features the Batman and Twilight star's shock landing of the role of 007.

![The Times front page with the headline "UK will face penalties if Brexit reset is scrapped".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/8b6e/live/bde14010-e221-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

British and EU negotiators have agreed a clause in the food and agricultural deal due to be signed during Prime Minister Andy Burnham's trip to Brussels on Friday requiring either side to pay compensation for withdrawal, according to the Times. The clause is designed to ensure the deal survives if there is a change of government.

![The Daily Express front page headline reads "Ditch Brexit reset talks and get on with real work".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/7f33/live/079ddb60-e221-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Daily Express covers Tory leader Kemi Badenoch urging the prime minister to "stop obsessing about Europe" and focus on rebuilding the economy, slashing welfare and boosting defence after Burnham's speech about his EU ambitions on Monday night.

![The Guardian front page main headline says "Police 'retreating from streets' as funding crisis grows, warns chiefs"](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/8b57/live/99a113d0-e21f-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

Nine police forces across England and Wales are seeking exceptional financial support from the government to remain "operationally viable", writes the Guardian. The government argues it has already given forces extra money in real terms, but police chiefs say demands on them have risen, as have population numbers. A Home Office spokesperson said police funding was at a historic high and a review of the funding formula continued to prioritise public safety.

![The Financial Times front page with the headline "Nvidia rejects bid to reinstate Huang despite Trump backing for reprieve".](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/6894/live/b537b3f0-e225-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The Financial Times reports people familiar with the matter say Nvidia's board is not considering reinstating Jensen Huang despite US President Donald Trump confirming he was open to the move after the tech boss's resignation over an infidelity scandal.
