A woodland fire in a suburb of York is believed to have been started deliberately.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said a crew from Acomb was called to Clifton at 8.35pm last night (Monday, November 16).

A service spokesperson said: "The fire measured one metre squared was extinguished with a bucket of water and small tools.

"Police were informed as the cause of this fire is believed to have been deliberate."
