# Teenager who tried to have sex with pony spared jail to get help

## A teenager who tried to have sex with a Shetland pony must work with the authorities to stop it from happening again, a judge has ruled.

The defendant, 19, admitted attempting to be penetrated by a pony in a late-night visit to a farm in Almondbury, West Yorkshire.

He was sentenced at Leeds Crown Court to a two-year community order during which he must work with the Probation Service to get help with controlling his sexual urges.

The defendant must also pay £300 prosecution costs.

Robert Galley, prosecuting, said the son of the farm owner was carrying out vermin control at 1.55am on August 24 last year and had an air rifle and night sights.

He saw a light and at first thought someone was trying to steal from the farm but, Mr Galley said, “to his shock and surprise he saw that the defendant had his trousers down and his lower half exposed”.

The prosecution said the defendant was trying to be penetrated by the pony.

The farmer’s son whistled, said he had a night vision and a rifle and began to record on his phone, the court heard.

Mr Galley said the defendant pulled up his trousers, covered his face and ran off.

It was only when the defendant returned on November 12 at around 11.40pm that he was caught by the farm owners, who had since installed motion-activated CCTV cameras.

They shut him in a stable and called the police who arrested the defendant.

Analysis of the defendant’s mobile phone and internet search terms showed he had an interest in bestiality, the court heard.

The owner of two Shetland ponies at the farm made a victim impact statement, summarised by the prosecution, as saying she had been left “stunned, upset and angry”.

She said her ponies were part of her family, Mr Galley said, and both had previously been friendly, but one of them was “less forthcoming to greet her” after the incident.

The Crown said she found it difficult to sleep and remained concerned that someone would molest her animals.

Rebecca Randall, for the defendant, said: “He is genuinely sorry.”

Judge Kate Rayfield said the incident must have been frightening for the owner, saying: “She must have been distraught at what her ponies have been through.”

The judge took into account the defendant’s age – 18 when the offence was committed – as well as his guilty plea and his previous good character.

Most important was the need for the “isolated” teenager to get help to avoid reoffending, she said.

Intensive supervision from the Probation Service should address his admitted interest in bestiality, the judge said.

“I am imposing this sentence to make sure that you get that help,” she said.

“You have to take it, this is not optional.”

The judge also imposed a restraining order to keep him away from the farm and the ponies’ owners.

Before the sentencing, the judge and counsel agreed that a reporting restriction published on the court list preventing the victim from being identified in the media did not apply to the ponies’ owner, or the Shetland itself.

Mr Galley also noted that a Sexual Harm Prevention Order was not applicable as the victim in this case was an animal.

A second charge of reckless trespass to commit a relevant sexual offence – relating to the defendant’s revisit to the farm last November – will lie on the file.
