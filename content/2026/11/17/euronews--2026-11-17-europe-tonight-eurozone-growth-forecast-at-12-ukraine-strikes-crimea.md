## The EU forecasts 1.2% Eurozone growth amid inflation and debt concerns, analysed live tonight by Valdis Dombrovskis. Meanwhile, Ukraine strikes a key Russia-Crimea bridge ahead of talks with Norway's Foreign Minister, and Spain's Pedro Sánchez slams EU Rwanda asylum plans.

## On today's show:

The European Commission has upgraded its Eurozone growth forecast to **1.2% of GDP**, citing economic resilience despite ongoing geopolitical turmoil. However, inflation, public spending, and rising government debt remain major concerns across the bloc. EU Economy Commissioner** Valdis Dombrovskis** analyses.

Ukrainian forces have damaged a vital bridge connecting Russia to occupied Crimea in what President **Volodymyr Zelenskyy** called an “unprecedented strike.” As attention turns toward potential peace talks involving European negotiators talking with Norwegian Foreign Minister**Espen Barth Eide.** Spanish Prime Minister**Pedro Sánchez** has criticised five EU member states over their plans to send rejected asylum seekers to Rwanda. Hailing Spain as a “great country that respects human rights,” the Socialist leader continues to break ranks with Europe’s tightening stance on migration.
