A coach breakdown couldn’t stop one band from playing their way to victory.

Swinton & District Excelsior Band was named Best 4th Section Band for the third year in a row at the NEMBBA autumn brass band contest, despite being stranded on the M1 for more than an hour.

The band’s coach broke down just an hour into their journey, leaving members waiting on a slip road for 90 minutes until a replacement vehicle from Fletchers Coaches arrived.

A spokesman for the band said: "It was certainly a contest day to remember for the Swinton musicians, although things didn't quite go to plan before they even reached the venue.

"After arriving, unloading instruments and making their way straight to the contest stage, the band had to perform as best they could under rather unusual circumstances, with cold instruments and little preparation time."

With no time for a warm-up, the band went straight on stage and performed with little preparation.

Soprano cornet player Janella, who stepped in at short notice to cover for an absent member, was named Best Instrumentalist in the section.

The spokesman said: "Having overcome so much just to get there, the players were simply pleased to have made it onto the stage and taken part. The results were almost an afterthought.

"However, there was to be a wonderful surprise when the results were announced."

The band went on to win the Best 4th Section Band title for the third consecutive year.

The spokesman said: "The result is a fantastic achievement for the band and a testament to the commitment, resilience and team spirit of its members.

"The band would like to thank Fletchers Coaches for their help in getting everyone to the contest, the organisers for their understanding, and, as always, its members for pulling together and making the best of whatever circumstances come their way.

"Three years running – and certainly one contest day that won't be forgotten in a hurry."
