A Bloomberg report says Civilization studio Firaxis has laid off roughly 60 people, representing about one-third of its total headcount. Heather Hazen, who took over as studio head in 2023 after more than 25 years with Firaxis, is also leaving.

A 2K Games spokesperson didn't comment on the reported number of employees let go, but confirmed that layoffs had occurred in a statement provided to PC Gamer. "Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength — designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years," the rep said.

"As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis."

The rep also confirmed that Hazen has stepped down as Firaxis studio head: "With the studio entering this reset, she feels this is the right, deliberate moment to hand over the reins to ensure the next studio leader has full ownership of this new era from the start."

Firaxis has enjoyed great success as the developer of the Civilization and XCOM games, but it's struggled in recent years. Marvel's Midnight Suns was critically acclaimed but sales were middling, while Civilization 7 was just middling overall: Despite a raft of updates and changes since its 2025 release, its user rating on Steam remains stubbornly "mixed" while player numbers are still well behind that of Civilization 6.

That's resulted in previous rounds of layoffs: Roughly 30 people were let go in 2023, while an unknown number were cut in 2025. The Bloomberg report says a new XCOM game in development at Firaxis has also struggled and missed a major milestone earlier this year; work on the game will reportedly continue but may face changes as a result of the layoffs.
