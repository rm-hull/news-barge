## Five EU countries have struck a deal with Rwanda to host the first return hub for migrants outside the bloc, and we're live from the Vienna Migration Conference. Also: another attack on the Crimean Bridge, new EU sanctions on Russia, US-Iran tensions and the Commission's economic forecast.

## On today's show:

**Vincenzo Genovese**, Euronews correspondent at the Vienna Migration Conference, explains the Rwanda return hub deal, what is still being negotiated, and the reactions.** Charlie Weimers**(ECR) and** Abir Al Sahlani** (Renew Europe) clash over the return hub on *Europe Tonight*, the new prime-time show hosted by**Mared Gwyn Jones**, which premiered last night.** Sasha Vakulina**reports on the overnight attack on the Crimean Bridge and the EU's new sanctions package against Russia.** Mateusz Cholewa**, disinformation expert at Demagog.pl, asks whether the sanctions can disrupt Russia's hybrid operations, and how vulnerable Europe's elections are.** Aadel Haleem**, Euronews correspondent in Doha, assesses the rising US-Iran tensions and Tehran's conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz.** Verena Ross**, former chair of the European Securities and Markets Authority, discusses what is holding the European economy back, hours before the Commission's economic forecast.
