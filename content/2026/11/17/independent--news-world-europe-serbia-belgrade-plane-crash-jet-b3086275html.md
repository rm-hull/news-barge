A pilot has been killed after a Serbian military jet crashed on Tuesday northwest of Belgrade, the defence ministry has confirmed.

The MIG-29 jet crashed around 8:45am local time into a field near the town of Stara Pazova, around 30 miles from the Serbian capital.

Local media have published photos of what appears to be the burning wreckage in a field with an ambulance and police cars parked nearby.

A special commission will work to determine the cause of the crash.

Some local Serbian media published photos of the burning wreckage in the field and an ambulance and police cars parked nearby.

The 53-year-old pilot, Maj. Goran Bulajic, was born on 25 March in 1973 and had joined the army in 1996, the defence ministry said in a statement.

No other details were immediately available.
