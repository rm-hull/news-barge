Drivers faced delays on the southbound A1(M) near Marton cum Grafton this morning after a vehicle caught fire.

Firefighters were called at 6.18am to the stretch between junction 48 at Boroughbridge and junction 47 at Allerton Park.

Police also attended.

The AA reported:

> “Delays increasing on A1(M) Southbound between J48 A6055 (Boroughbridge) and J47 A59 (Allerton Park).
>
> "Average speed 15 mph.”
