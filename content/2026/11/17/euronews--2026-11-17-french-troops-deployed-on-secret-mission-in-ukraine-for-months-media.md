## Russia said on Tuesday it was aware of French troops deployed to Ukraine, calling it "direct participation" in the war and saying a message had been issued to Paris.

French troops have been deployed on a secret mission in Ukraine for several months, Radio France Internationale (RFI) reported on Monday.

The broadcaster did not say how many French soldiers are involved in the deployment, which has been operating in Ukraine since at least the summer.

But it did say the troops were there to train Ukrainian forces and help maintain military equipment.

Russia said on Tuesday it was aware of French troops deployed to Ukraine, calling it "direct participation" in the war and saying a message had been issued to Paris.

"Not only French ones, we know this. We have conveyed our concern...this is no longer support for Ukraine, but direct participation in the war," Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov told reporters.

His remarks come a day after a source told the AFP news agency and other media that French soldiers had been operating in Ukraine for several months to train Ukrainian personnel and help maintain equipment.

The source said the mission of a small contingent of French troops excludes any combat role.

They spoke to AFP on condition of anonymity.

"The rotation of French trainers supporting Ukraine in France, Poland, or occasionally in Ukraine is a reality," the source told AFP.

"But this is not a military operation. There is absolutely no intention of engaging in coercive actions."
