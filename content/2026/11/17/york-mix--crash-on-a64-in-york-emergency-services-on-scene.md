There’s been a crash on the York Outer Ring Road this morning (Tuesday).

It was on the A64 eastbound between Fulford and Grimston Bar.

Emergency services went to the scene.

#### Crash location

Drivers reported heavy traffic between the junctions for the A19 and the A1079.

The crash has now cleared.
