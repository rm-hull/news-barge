A dispute over the sale of David Beckham perfumes has struck a sour note with the soccer star turned style icon.

Beckham’s commercial company is suing a beauty firm after his “luxury” fragrances were allegedly found for sale in discount stores and a gas station bargain bin.

DB Ventures has filed a claim in the New York Supreme Court against German beauty group Coty, which makes and distributes a range of Beckham-branded fragrances and toiletries. They include the Classic and Instinct lines and the eaux de toilette Intimately and Exquisite Leather.

The claim argues that Beckham’s “world-renowned global brand is synonymous with excellence and refinement,” and “David Beckham products are a symbol of everyday luxury.”

“Imagine, then, the shock and dismay experienced when DB Ventures discovered that David Beckham products were being offered for sale in a gas station,” the legal document says. “How could this possibly have happened? Desperation and greed.”

Lawyers for DB Ventures allege that Beckham-branded products “ended up in the bargain bin inside a gas station” and at British budget retailer Poundland, “allowing the product to be sold for dollar store prices and associating the David Beckham brand with cheap, discount products.”

DB Ventures is seeking at least $41 million in damages for breach of its licensing agreement.

Coty has asked a judge to dismiss the lawsuit, which was filed in April. The company argues that New York courts have no jurisdiction in the case, and says DB Ventures is using the claim to “manufacture its early exit” from its licensing deal and hand over the Beckham contracts to another firm, Interparfums.

Coty and DB Ventures did not immediately respond to requests for comment on Tuesday.

Beckham, the former soccer star for Manchester United and Real Madrid and captain of the England men’s national team, parlayed success on the pitch into a lucrative business career that includes co-ownership of soccer team Inter Miami. He was knighted last year by King Charles III, becoming Sir David.

He also has had a very public persona as a fashion model, “Bend it Like Beckham” movie muse and is husband to Spice Girl-turned-fashion designer Victoria “Posh Spice” Beckham. The couple have four children.

The Sunday Times Rich list earlier this year estimated the Beckhams' wealth at almost 1.2 billion pounds ($1.58 billion).
