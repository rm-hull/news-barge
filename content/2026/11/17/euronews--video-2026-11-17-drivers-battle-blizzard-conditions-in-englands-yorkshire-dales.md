Heavy snow swept across the Yorkshire Dales in northern England on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, making driving treacherous.

Aerial footage from Kidstones Pass, near West Burton in North Yorkshire, shows cars navigating snow-covered roads as blizzard conditions sharply reduce visibility. At times, swirling snow almost completely obscures the surrounding hills.

The early snowfall blanketed the landscape in white, with strong winds blowing snow across the exposed mountain pass.

A farmer on a quad bike was also seen travelling through the wintry conditions near the summit.
