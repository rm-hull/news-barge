Thought Bubble Comic Convention returned to Harrogate at the weekend, bringing thousands of comic book fans, artists, writers and cosplayers to the town

Visitors packed the Harrogate Convention Centre to browse stalls, meet creators, attend talks and admire an array of impressive costumes inspired by comic books, films, television and video games

The annual festival once again transformed Harrogate into a colourful hub for creativity and fandom, attracting people of all ages from across the country

Here are some brilliant photos from an action-packed weekend, thanks to our photographer Gerard Binks...
