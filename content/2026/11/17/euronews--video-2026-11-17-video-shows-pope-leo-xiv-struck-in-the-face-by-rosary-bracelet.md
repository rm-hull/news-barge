Pope Leo XIV was struck in the face by a rosary bracelet thrown from the crowd as he travelled through Lima in his popemobile on Monday.

The pontiff briefly touched his face before continuing to greet the crowd and blessing a baby.

The incident happened ahead of the final Mass of his Latin American tour, which brought him to several countries across the region over 12 days.

The bracelet, known as a single-decade rosary, features 10 beads used to keep track of prayers.
