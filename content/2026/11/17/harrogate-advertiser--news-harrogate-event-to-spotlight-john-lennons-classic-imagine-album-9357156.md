A long-running Harrogate music night which has raised nearly than £26,000 for Harrogate Hospital & Community Charity in recent years is to go ‘valve’ for the first time to celebrate the most popular album by the legendary John Lennon.

For its final event of the year, Harrogate’s Vinyl Sessions will present John Lennon's 27 million selling album Imagine on an authentic vintage 1960/70s valve audio system.

Released in 1971, just a year after the break-up of The Beatles, Imagine captures Lennon at a fascinating point in his life, combining beautiful melodies, thoughtful songwriting and some occasionally uncompromising observations on the world around him.

Few albums have left quite the same mark on popular music as this unforgettable album, most famous for its title track which, when released as a single, sold more than 1.6 million copies in the UK alone.

Recorded at his home studio in Tittenhurst Park, with the legendary Phil Spector sharing production duties, Lennon assembled a top quality ensemble of musicians around him, including fellow Beatle George Harrison, Klaus Voormann, future Yes Alan White and the Rolling Stones pianist Nicky Hopkins.

The bestselling album also includes classic tracks such as Jealous Guy, Gimme Some Truth and How Do You Sleep?

Taking place at Starling Independent Bar Cafe Kitchen in Harrogate at 7.30pm on Wednesday, December 2, the latest Vinyl Sessions will throw in a bonus ball in the form of a selection of Lennon’s greatest hits.

Helmed by Vinyl Sessions founder Colin Paine, the event will also feature an in-depth introduction and album back story by resident music historian, Graham Chalmers of the Harrogate Advertiser, a former artistic director of Harrogate International Festivals Fringe and organiser of Charm gigs and events.

Colin Paine said: “John Lennon was taken from us on December 8, 1980 and, as we approach the 46th anniversary of his untimely death, this seems a fitting opportunity to revisit an album that continues to resonate across generations.”

The vintage hi fi for the forthcoming event will be made up of a Garrard LAB 95, Sony TA1130 pre-amp, 2x LEAK TL25 Monoblock valve power amplifiers and Celestion Ditton loudspeakers.

The night will feature a presentation of relevant video footage to accompany the sounds on vintage hi fi plus a prize raffle with Jill and co.

Every single penny of revenue goes to Harrogate Hospital.
