Ukraine has released footage of its latest attack on the Kerch Bridge, a key road and rail link connecting the Crimean Peninsula to mainland Russia.

The Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) said the operation was carried out overnight in coordination with Ukraine's defence forces. According to the agency, the strike destroyed one of the bridge's arches and a section of the road.

The bridge is a vital route for Russian military supplies to Crimea and a major symbol of Moscow's 2014 annexation of the peninsula.
