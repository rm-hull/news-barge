A WOMAN was assaulted at a popular York event last month.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for a 'key witness' after a woman was assaulted to the point of requiring hospital treatment whilst attending Hallowscream on the outskirts of York.

The assault took place at York Maze in Dunnington, York, on Friday, October 23.

It took place at around 10.40pm and left the victim with facial injuries that required hospital treatment

A police spokesperson said: "It is understood that the suspect is a woman, and may have been in the company of others.

"It has come to light that there may be a key witness to the incident who left before leaving their details, please get in touch if this may be you."

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing [william.robertson@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:william.robertson@northyorkshire.police.uk), making a report on the police website, or by calling 101.

Please quote reference 12260209015 when providing information.
