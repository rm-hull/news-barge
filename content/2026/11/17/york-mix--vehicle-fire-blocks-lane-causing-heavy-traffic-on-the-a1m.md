A vehicle fire to a blocked lane is causing problems for drivers on the A1(M) this morning

Firefighters were called to the fire on the southbound carriageway near Marton cum Grafton south of Boroughbridge, between junctions 47 and 48, at 6.18am.

A lane remains blocked on the carriageway. Police are on the scene.

#### Incident location

There is queuing traffic on the motorway.

The AA reported: “Delays increasing on A1(M) Southbound between J48 A6055 (Boroughbridge) and J47 A59 (Allerton Park). Average speed 15 mph.”
