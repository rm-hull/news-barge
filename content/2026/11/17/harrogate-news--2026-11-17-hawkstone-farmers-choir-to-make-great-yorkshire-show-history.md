**The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir will become the first choir in the 189-year history of the Great Yorkshire Show to perform in the Main Ring.**

The choir will perform twice daily at the 2027 Show, which takes place from Tuesday 13 to Friday 16 July.

Made up of farmers and agricultural workers from across the UK, the choir became the first choir to win Britain’s Got Talent, bringing farming voices to a much wider audience.

Their appearance at the Great Yorkshire Show will see them take centre stage in one of the event’s most prominent arenas, which traditionally features displays and performances celebrating agriculture and rural life.

The choir is also preparing to release its debut album, *This Is Home*, on Friday 20 November 2026.

> **Rachel Coates, Show Director and a farmer from West Yorkshire, said:**
>
> We’re always looking for ways to make a visit to the Great Yorkshire Show even better and so we have made a few forward-thinking changes this year, which we are excited to be announcing as we reveal our Main Ring attraction for 2027. The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir is doing a fantastic job of reaching the public about farming and so it’s a perfect fit for the Show which celebrates and shares the important story of agriculture to thousands of people every year.

The 2027 Great Yorkshire Show will be held at the Great Yorkshire Showground in Harrogate from 13 to 16 July 2027.
