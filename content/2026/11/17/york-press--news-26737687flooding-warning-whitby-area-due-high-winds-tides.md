High tides and strong winds are set to cause flooding.

The Environment Agency has issued a flood warning for properties on Sandside, around the Old Harbour, on Tuesday, November 17.

The warning is in place until 9.30pm.

The agency said the risk of flooding should reduce once the tide has passed.

Residents are advised to avoid walking, cycling, or driving through the flood water.

The situation is being monitored closely.
