York police investigating an incident have released this image of a man they want to speak to.

A post on the North Yorkshire Police York Facebook page said: “We’re appealing for information and asking people to check whether they recognise the man pictured as part of an investigation into a racially aggravated criminal damage incident.

“The incident involved a man kicking open a locked door, causing significant damage. During the incident, racially abusive language was also shouted in a public place.

“We believe the man shown in the CCTV image may have information that could assist our investigation.”

Police haven’t released a location or time for the incident.
