The Israeli parliament, or Knesset, elected a new speaker on Tuesday, a key step toward rolling back policies passed by the outgoing government led by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

The newly-elected speaker is Matan Kahana, an ally of Gadi Eisenkot, the party leader currently working to form the next government and replace Netanyahu. Eisenkot’s bloc has pledged to undo some of Netanyahu’s most contentious policies following Kahana's election.
