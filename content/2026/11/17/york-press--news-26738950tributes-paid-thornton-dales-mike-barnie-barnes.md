TRIBUTES have been paid to a grassroots sporting legend.

Mike 'Barnie' Barnes, of Thornton Dale, died on Thursday, and was honoured across the Beckett Football League with a minute’s applause at all weekend fixtures.

Mr Barnes was widely known across Ryedale for his long-standing dedication to community sport, particularly football and cricket.

A Beckett League spokesperson said: "It is with the greatest of sadness that I begin this week’s Beckett Football League report with devastating news, the passing on Thursday morning of Thornton Dale’s Mike 'Barnie' Barnes.

"A minutes applause, in memory of Mike 'Barnie' Barnes, was observed throughout all Beckett League fixtures at the weekend, such was Barnie’s popularity across Ryedale grass roots football.

"This gentleman was the complete volunteer when it came, not just to football, but to grass roots sport in Ryedale.

"He took great pride in ensuring, in particular, the players both young and old, involved in both football and cricket, had the best turned-out facilities possible to enjoy the sport they loved."

Mr Barnes was a regular supporter of the Beckett League and was known for attending finals nights regardless of which teams were playing.

The spokesperson said: "A husband, father, grandad, and friend to so, so many, a tower of the village community, Barnie, as he will forever be remembered, will quite frankly be irreplaceable."

With the support of Mr Barnes’ wife Janet, sons Craig and Stephen, and their families, Thornton Dale manager Arthur Coote led his team across the moors to play against Whitby Fishermen’s Academy Development in tribute.

Mr Barnes’ contributions extended beyond football.

He was deeply involved in cricket and worked to ensure that facilities were always of the highest standard for everyone involved.

His commitment and community spirit made him a much-loved figure in Thornton Dale and the wider Ryedale area.
