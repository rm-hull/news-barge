POLICE are investigating a crash which closed a North Yorkshire road for five hours.

The county police said it is appealing for witnesses and dashcam footage after a two-car crash which took place close to Ripon on Monday (November 16).

It happened on the B6265 - Boroughbridge Road - near Bridge Hewick, Ripon at 6.20pm.

A police spokesperson said: “It involved a red Vauxhall car travelling towards Ripon and a silver Volkswagen Passat car travelling in the opposite direction.

“Occupants from both vehicles were taken to hospital by ambulance after the collision, and have since been discharged following treatment.”

The road was closed for five hours whilst officers investigated the scene and to allow the vehicles involved to be recovered.

If you have any information that could assist the investigation, please contact PC Brown at [harry.brown@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:harry.brown@northyorkshire.police.uk), using the police website, or by calling 101.
