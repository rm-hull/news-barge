A Leeds airport owner with connections to Gloucestershire has been charged in relation to an alleged assault on a woman in Cheltenham last week.

Christopher Makin, aged 68 and from Micklefield in Leeds, has been charged with assault by beating.

Gloucestershire Police confirmed the charge relates to an incident which supposedly occurred "at a premises on the Promenade" on Thursday evening (November 12).

A spokesperson stated: "Makin has been released on conditional bail and is due to appear at Cheltenham Magistrates' Court on Tuesday 2 February 2027."

Makin, who holds a partial ownership stake in Leeds East Airport through Makin Enterprises, disclosed last month that he had submitted a joint offer to purchase the council-owned Gloucestershire Airport, reports Gloucestershire Live.
