A family-owned Harrogate hotel has as been crowned Best Spa in the North East at the Good Spa Guide Awards 2026 after a competition which attracted more than a quarter of a million votes.

The prestigious award was presented to Rudding Park Spa at a black-tie ceremony at Sopwell House in St Albans hosted by TV presenter Ben Shephard.

In total, more than 266,000 public votes were received in the awards for The Good Spa Guide, which is celebrating its 20th anniversary.

Recognised for its exceptional approach to relaxation, wellbeing and thermal spa experiences, the three floors of the glass-walled Rudding Park Spa boast an indoor pool, juniper log sauna and ‘mind and sense zones’.

The Roof Top Spa and Garden – with its hydrotherapy infinity-edge pool and Brass Monkey ice baths – remains a Spa Spy favourite.

Lesley Bacon, Spa & Wellness Director at The Good Spa Guide, said: “This year, more than 266,000 votes were cast for the spas you love most – and our Spa Spies and judges did the rest.

“From a rooftop infinity pool in Leicestershire to a subterranean hammam beneath the streets of York, these 20 winners are the very best of British and Irish spa-ing.”

The Best Spa in the North East title for Rudding Park Spa was sponsored by Spa Well and further reinforces the North East’s position as a destination for outstanding wellness and spa experiences.

Other finalists for the Best Spa in the North East category were: Turkish Baths Harrogate, Forest Spa at Middleton Lodge, Serenity Spa at Seaham Hall, SubRosa at The Grand, York, The Farmhouse Treatment Rooms at Holdsworth House, The Sanctuary at Saltmoore, The Spa at No. 1 by GuestHouse, York, Thorpe Park Hotel and Spa, Titanic Spa and Yorkshire Spa Retreat.

The Good Spa Guide Awards celebrate excellence across the UK and Ireland, with this year's 20 awards spanning ten regions, five spa types and five specialist categories.

A panel of nine industry experts judged the finalists alongside the public vote, with The Good Spa Guide's Spa Spies also contributing to the process.
