**England could host the Nations League finals this season after Football Association officials registered their interest.**

The semi-final and finals of the competition, due to take place in June, are held in one country.

As well as England, it is understood that Spain and France have also contacted Uefa to register their interest in hosting the final four games, including the bronze medal match, which will run between 9-13 June.

The FA, it is understood, is still discussing potential venues with some doubt over whether Wembley would be available. The only publicly announced Wembley events in June are two concerts by singer Sam Fender, although these are for after the Nations League.

Since the tournament began in 2019, England have never held the finals, with Portugal, Italy, Netherlands and Germany the hosts to date.

Generally, only nations who reach the last four of the Nations League are considered to host the latter stages.

Thomas Tuchel's team will be seeded in Friday's draw for the quarter-finals following their euphoric 3-2 victory over Spain in Madrid, which secured top place in the group.

They could face Belgium, Netherlands, Denmark or Norway in March in the next round before June's finals.

If the FA's bid is successful, it will provide the governing body with an opportunity to host a mini-tournament before Euro 2028, which will be held in England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland.
