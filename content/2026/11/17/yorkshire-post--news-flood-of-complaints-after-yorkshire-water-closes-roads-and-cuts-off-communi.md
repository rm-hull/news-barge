# Flood of complaints after Yorkshire Water closes roads and 'cuts off communities'

## A councillor has asked for an urgent meeting following a “flood of complaints” from villagers after they got cut off by Yorkshire Water.

At one point on Monday no one could get in or out of the villages of Ousefleet and Whitgift or to RSPB Blacktoft Sands, the reserve said.

Contractors have been working on a pipeline on the road from Whitgift, but following a burst main, the road from Adlingfleet was also closed. People were without water for around five hours.

Yorkshire Water insisted resident access “has been maintained at all times” on the road where the pre-planned work was taking place.

East Riding councillor Liz Sargeantson said: "We support essential infrastructure upgrades but closing roads at both ends and effectively cutting communities off is unacceptable. Residents deserve better planning, better communication and immediate action to resolve the issue."

![RSPB Blacktoft Sands](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjNmMmJjMjFhLTkyYmMtNDY5NS04OGMxLTZmZmZkMjk5ZjEyNjplOTZiZWI4Mi00MTQ4LTQwYzYtYTRlYS02ZDNlN2Q4YjY5OGQ=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


A local was left fuming after ringing YW concerned about watering animals and was told he could go and buy bottles and he would be reimbursed. "It was just their attitude, that it wasn't a problem", he said, adding that he then rang Defra to make a formal complaint.

YW said partial lane closures were in place as part of a £5.8m scheme to renew 18km of clean water network in Adlingfleet, Garthorpe, Reedness, Ousefleet, Whitgift and Fockerby. The burst water main at Swinefleet was repaired on Monday and water restored to impacted homes.

The affected road will reopen on Wednesday following repairs.

The spokesperson said: “We understand that this has caused significant disruption locally, but residents still have access. We would like everyone for their patience and apologise for any inconvenience caused whilst this repair is completed." They’ve been in touch with the RSPB reserve to assure them that they will have access during the ongoing work.
