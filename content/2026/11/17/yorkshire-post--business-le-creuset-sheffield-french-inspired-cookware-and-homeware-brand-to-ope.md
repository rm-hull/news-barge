# Le Creuset, Sheffield: French-inspired cookware and homeware brand to open its first Yorkshire boutique at Meadowhall

## Le Creuset, the French cookware and homeware brand, has announced it will open its first Sheffield boutique at Meadowhall this month.

Le Creuset is celebrated for its premium, colourful cookware and timeless design since 1925 and has become a household name for generations of foodies and home cooks.

The new boutique will be located on The Avenue - Lower Level and will bring Le Creuset’s full collection of premium cookware, bakeware and kitchen accessories to Yorkshire shoppers.

![British Land has sold its stake in Sheffield's popular Meadowhall shopping centre.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2024/05/20/9/14/Meadowhall.jpg.jpg?trim=151,0,182,0&width=640&quality=65)


Customers can explore the brand’s renowned cast iron range, colourful stoneware collections and everyday kitchen essentials, all designed to inspire cooking, dining and entertaining at home.

Bringing a Sheffield feel to the opening, Le Creuset has partnered with local artist Reyt Good to create a bespoke window display celebrating the shared heritage of cast steel in Sheffield and cast iron at Le Creuset.

Inspired by the craftsmanship and industrial traditions that connect the two, the installation will feature the brand’s original and iconic colourway, Volcanic, creating a tribute to creativity, workmanship and tradition.

There will also be something special for shoppers on launch day, with 50 Le Creuset goodie bags when you spend £100 or more.

Each bag will also include a taste of Sheffield, featuring local favourite Henderson’s Relish and Henderson’s crisps.

Centre director at Meadowhall, Darren Pearce, said: “Le Creuset is one of those brands that so many people know and love, so it’s fantastic to be welcoming its first Sheffield boutique to Meadowhall, especially in the run-up to Christmas.

“Cooking and spending time around the table is such a big part of Christmas, so the timing feels particularly fitting, especially when its items also make for great Christmas gifts! We’re looking forward to opening the doors this week.”

Retail director at Le Creuset, Paul Frost, said: “We’re delighted to bring Le Creuset to Sheffield with the opening of our new boutique at Meadowhall.

“It’s wonderful to be expanding our presence in a city with such a rich heritage and a genuine passion for food, design and quality craftsmanship.

“We’re excited to become part of the local community and welcome shoppers into the world of Le Creuset, offering them the opportunity to discover our iconic collections and invest in pieces that will be treasured and enjoyed for generations.”
