Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon has urged ministers to publish cost estimates for potential asylum accommodation sites.

The Liberal Democrat MP said communities in North Yorkshire wanted to know whether the former RAF base at Linton-on-Ouse was suitable for asylum accommodation, how much it would cost and who would pay.

Tom Gordon spoke during an urgent question in the House of Commons about plans for MOD Bicester.

He called on the Government to back amendments to the Immigration and Asylum Bill tabled by fellow Liberal Democrat MP Calum Miller, who secured the urgent question.

The amendments would require ministers to publish site suitability assessments, cost estimates and a financial breakdown for asylum accommodation sites.

Tom told the Commons:

> “This is a really emotive issue, and one that’s becoming a political football.
>
> "The way to cut through that would be to take up those amendments when they come back at report stage tomorrow.”

Minister Anna Turley said the amendments should be debated in full, but warned that the Government “may not be able to publish everything at every stage” because of commercial sensitivities.

Tom said:

> “Whether it’s Bicester or Linton, communities are asking the same basic questions. Is the site suitable? What will it cost? Who pays? They deserve answers. Tomorrow the Government can back these amendments and put transparency into law.”
