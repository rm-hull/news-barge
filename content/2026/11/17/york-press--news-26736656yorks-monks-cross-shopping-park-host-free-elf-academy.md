York's Monks Cross Shopping Park has announced a free 'Elf Academy' this weekend.

The shopping park in Huntington is inviting families to join in the festive fun on Saturday, November 21, and Sunday, November 22, with an interactive event designed for children of all ages.

Young visitors can train as Santa's official helpers in preparation for the busy holiday season.

The Elf Academy journey starts at 'Elf HQ', where children will receive an official Elf Passport.

They will then visit three interactive workshops inside Monks Cross stores, teaching the essential elf skills of kindness, teamwork, and creativity.

After collecting stamps at each station, the young elves can return to Elf HQ to graduate, receiving an official Elf Certificate and a festive surprise.

The shopping park is home to various popular brands, including Primark, TK Maxx, Boots, Pizza Hut, and Hobbycraft, offering a festive day out for families looking to kick off the holiday season while also getting some shopping done.

The event is free of charge, and no advance booking is required, allowing families to drop in anytime between 11am and 3pm.

Monks Cross Shopping Park is known for its convenient free parking, making it an accessible destination for a festive day out.

The park has encouraged attendees to begin their holiday season with the Elf Academy, promising a day filled with festive cheer and fun.

For more information, visit Monks Cross Shopping Park's website.

The Elf Academy will be open opposite the Customer Information Hub and Costa.
