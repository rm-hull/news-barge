# Councillor wants national anthem played at start of every meeting

## A Reform UK councillor hoping to have ‘God Save The King’ played at the start of every City of Doncaster Council meeting has said doing so would be the ‘first step’ in making the authority ‘more patriotic’. v.1

A Reform UK councillor hoping to have ‘God Save The King’ played at the start of every City of Doncaster Council meeting has said doing so would be the ‘first step’ in making the authority ‘more patriotic’.

Balby South ward’s Councillor Ioan-Emanuel Craciun is calling for the first verse of the national anthem to be played ‘as the mace bearer enters the chamber’ for the start of full council meetings.

“I believe that identity needs to be shown at full council,” he said.

![November 2026 LDRS photo available for all partners.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmVjMzU0ZmI3LTJiM2ItNDQ2YS1iY2M2LWE0YjlhYTJiODdkMzpmOTFhNDRjNS1kZmVhLTQ1YTMtODRmMi0zNWE0MWM1Y2VjMDE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“I sang it for the first time when I became a citizen, so it brings that sense of belonging… I am so proud to be British,” he added.

Coun Craciun’s idea has been submitted as a motion to full council on November 19, 2026, which asks for the possibility of the anthem being played to form part of the ongoing review of Doncaster Council’s constitution.

The Reform UK councillor said he hopes playing the national anthem will allow all councillors to align their priorities, adding: “I hope that the anthem will lead councillors to think about the country a bit more.

“We had [Nigel Farage] say we would be unashamedly patriotic and I was pleased to hear him say that.

![Councillor Ioan-Emanuel Craciun, Reform UK Doncaster. Photo from Cllr Craciun and is good for all partners. “This will be the first step, I would say, to making the council more patriotic.”

Coun Craciun’s motion, seconded by fellow Reform UK councillor Oliver Bloor, states members of the meeting and anyone else present would be ‘invited to stand’ whilst the anthem plays.

Though it adds that standing and attending would be ‘voluntary’ and no one is ‘required to take part’.
