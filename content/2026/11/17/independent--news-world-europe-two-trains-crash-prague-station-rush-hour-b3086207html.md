At least 15 people have been injured after two trains collided near Prague’s main railway station.

All emergency services responded on Tuesday morning to the collision on the overpass at Seifertova Street near Prague’s Main Railway Station in Prague 3.

![Footage shared by the police of the crash](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/9/49/Screenshot-2026-11-17-at-08-56-26.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Investigators are on the scene, working with railway inspectors to establish all the causes and circumstances of the accident and determine who is responsible for the train collision, Czech Police said.

The force said on social media: “Rescue operations have ended, and investigators are now on the scene, working with railway inspectors to establish all the causes and circumstances of the accident, and above all to determine who will be held responsible for the train collision.”

Footage on social media from the scene showed rescuers attending the scene where two passenger trains had crashed into each other. Both carriages had crumpled from the impact damage.

Another video shared by police showed emergency services carrying away someone injured in a stretcher.

The state-run train operator CzD reported service disruptions across at least six different lines which they predicted would take place until midday. The train operator reported delays of 60-120 minutes and cancellations across the lines with a number of diversions in place.

Lines from the main station to Prague-Vysočany, Prague-Libeň, Prague-Holešovice, were impacted due to “consequences of a train collision with a person”, while lines from the main station to Milovice, Lysá nad Labem, Kolín, and Ústí nad Labem were impacted following the “aftermath of a train collision.”
