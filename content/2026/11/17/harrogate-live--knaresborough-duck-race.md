Knaresborough's duck race is listed for Friday 1 January 2027 from 1pm: over 5,000 ducks from High Bridge to Marigolds Cafe, for Henshaws. Where to watch.

Knaresborough’s New Year’s Day duck race is listed for **Friday 1 January 2027, from 1pm to 3.30pm**. More than 5,000 rubber ducks are launched into the River Nidd at High Bridge and race down to the finish at Marigolds Cafe on Waterside, with prizes for the winners and the money going to the local charity Henshaws (Visit North Yorkshire).

## Overview

* **When:** Friday 1 January 2027, 1pm to 3.30pm.
* **Start:** High Bridge, next to the entrance to Mother Shipton’s Cave and Petrifying Well.
* **Finish:** Marigolds Cafe, 2 Waterside, Knaresborough, HG5 9AZ.
* **What:** over 5,000 rubber ducks, prizes, and a family afternoon by the river.
* **For:** the local charity Henshaws.
* **Ducks and updates:** the race’s own Facebook page, run under the Knaresborough Cricket Club name.

## Is it on this year?

It is listed. North Yorkshire Council’s tourism service, Visit North Yorkshire, gives New Year’s Day 2027 at 1pm, and points to the race’s Facebook page for details. As a river event it depends on the state of the Nidd, so check that page on the morning. We will add duck prices and any change to this page as they are published.

## The course

The ducks go in at **High Bridge**, next to the entrance to Mother Shipton’s Cave and Petrifying Well, and float down the Nidd to the finish line at** Marigolds Cafe, 2 Waterside**. The listing calls it a fun family day out and a highlight of Knaresborough’s festive calendar.

## Parking and getting there

Waterside car park by the river has about 70 spaces; York Place, with about 240, is the largest of Knaresborough’s council car parks. Both, with their charges, are on our parking guide. Check our Christmas trains page and the roadworks and travel page for New Year’s Day services and closures.

## More over New Year

For the night before, our New Year’s Eve page has Ripon’s Market Square party and what else is on. On Boxing Day the same stretch of river hosts Knaresborough’s Boxing Day tug of war at Low Bridge.
