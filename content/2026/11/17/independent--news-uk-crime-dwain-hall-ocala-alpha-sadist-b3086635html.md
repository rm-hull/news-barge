A vulnerable British woman was manipulated for years by a twisted predator she met on a fetish website who went on to murder her “for his own sadistic pleasure”, a US court has heard.

The Florida trial of Dwain Hall, 54, whose online user name was Alpha Sadist, was told he had been the “executioner” of Sonia Exelby and had demanded she pay 4,000 dollars (£3,000) for him to kill her when she travelled to the US last October.

The proceedings in Ocala heard that the 32-year-old from Southsea, Portsmouth, had wanted to die.

The jury was told Ms Exelby had had a difficult childhood and struggled with mental illness throughout her life, had self-harmed and also attempted suicide.

She was reported missing from her home on October 12 last year, by which time police established she had left the country.

Her body was found by US investigators in Marion County, Florida, on October 17.

Hall was arrested on charges of first-degree murder and kidnapping.

He had already been detained over the alleged fraudulent use of Ms Exelby’s credit cards.

![Sonia Exelby was reported missing from her address in Southsea, Portsmouth, Hampshire, on October 12](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/07/27/21/40/01KYJGAJEC8610JJKA705KSQZ5.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Opening the trial in Ocala, assistant state attorney Amy Berndt told the jury Ms Exelby was “brutally murdered by a predator, a man who called himself Alpha Sadist, a man who was sexually aroused by the rape and torture of women”.

“That man is the defendant Dwain Hall,” she said.

“Sonia Exelby was a beautiful, kind woman who was deeply loved by everyone who knew her. But sadly, she suffered from mental illness for most of her life.

“She met the defendant online and he manipulated her for years. He targeted her because of her mental illness and he exploited her for his own sadistic pleasure.

“And when he finally got her alone, thousands of miles away from home, completely incapable of making any rational decisions, he stabbed her to death and buried her body.”

Referring to Ms Exelby’s mental health, Ms Berndt said she had been trying to find “some substitute” for the self-harm, “some outlet that would make her feel alive and feel something again”.

The prosecutor added: “Unfortunately, she found another outlet… about two years before her death, and ultimately that outlet would lead to her murder.”

The court heard that Ms Exelby had communicated “with many different men” through a fetish website which has since been shut down.

Ms Berndt said: “She was trying to find somebody that would kill her. Her mental health was declining. She wanted to die, and she knew that she couldn’t do it herself.

“And unfortunately, there was no shortage of depraved men willing to kill Sonia in the most torturous and vile ways possible, and they would send her messages and describe to her how they would kill her.”

She added: “Even though there were many men competing for this job, one man won out.

“One man would manipulate and groom Sonia until he won the job of being her executioner. That person was the defendant Dwain Hall.”

His biography for his Alpha Sadist online identity said he would “love to find a domestic pain slut for complete control”.

Hall and Ms Exelby went on to communicate through an encrypted app, where messages were deleted automatically.

The court was told a first attempt by the pair to meet and Hall to kill her in March 2025 had failed.

Mr Berndt said: “After that fell through, the defendant realised, ‘Hey, not only can I kill her, but I might as well get some money from it as well’. And he told her that she had to bring 4,000 dollars with her when she actually came here for him to kill her.”

A meeting was rearranged for October 2025 and Ms Exelby booked a plane ticket and a rental property close to Ocala.

Before her arrival, a receipt showed Hall had bought a shovel and rope.

He was traced by police through the attempted use of her credit card by the roadside assistance company he owned.

When he was questioned by officers Hall lied for four hours and made up “a wild, nonsensical story”, before recanting and admitting he knew Sonia and had been communicating with her for years, the court was told.

He said he had sex with her, during which he hit and choked her.

Ms Berndt said: “Ultimately, he says he helped Sonia get what she wanted.”

A search of Hall’s house found the shovel with Ms Exelby’s blood on it.

They also recovered a knife from a package Hall had sent to a friend in Ohio.

Ms Exelby’s semi-naked body was found on Friday October 17, buried in woodland where Hall’s phone had been located for an hour and 20 minutes on the morning of October 12.

She had been stabbed four times in the left side, which a post-mortem examination found to be between four and seven inches deep and had “all hit vital structures”.

The court also heard that Hall had tried “to erase every trace of Sonia on his phone”, but a forensic search of the device found nude pictures of her as well as “numerous images of women being raped, tortured and murdered”.

Ms Berndt said: “Evidence that the reason the defendant beat, stabbed, and murdered Sonia was for his own sadistic pleasure.”

Hall’s defence lawyer told the jury: “We believe that when you listen to all the facts and evidence in this case, you will arrive at the decision that Dwain Hall did not murder.”
