**Harrogate and Knaresborough MP Tom Gordon is calling on the Government to publish site suitability assessments, cost estimates and financial details for proposed asylum accommodation, including the former RAF Linton-on-Ouse.**

Tom Gordon, Liberal Democrat MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough, has urged the Government to support amendments to the Immigration and Asylum Bill which would require ministers to publish more information about proposed asylum accommodation sites.

The amendments would require the publication of site suitability assessments, cost estimates and a financial breakdown of asylum accommodation proposals.

Mr Gordon raised the issue during an Urgent Question secured by Liberal Democrat MP Calum Miller concerning plans for the Ministry of Defence site at Bicester.

He said similar questions remained unanswered about the former RAF Linton-on-Ouse in North Yorkshire.

The amendments are due to return at Report Stage of the Immigration and Asylum Bill on 18 November.

## Calls for greater transparency

Speaking in the House of Commons, Mr Gordon said the issue had become politically contentious and argued that greater transparency would help address concerns.

> **Tom Gordon MP said:**
>
> This is a really emotive issue, and one that’s becoming a political football. The way to cut through that would be to take up those amendments when they come back at report stage tomorrow.

Minister Anna Turley said the amendments should be debated fully, but warned that the Government might not be able to publish all information at every stage because of commercial sensitivities.

Mr Gordon said communities affected by proposed asylum accommodation needed clear answers about the suitability and cost of sites, and who would be responsible for paying.

> **Tom Gordon MP said:**
>
> Whether it’s Bicester or Linton, communities are asking the same basic questions. Is the site suitable? What will it cost? Who pays? They deserve answers. Tomorrow the Government can back these amendments and put transparency into law.

The amendments will be considered when the Bill returns to the House of Commons for Report Stage.
