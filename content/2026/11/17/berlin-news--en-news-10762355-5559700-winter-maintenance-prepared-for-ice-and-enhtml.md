According to the report, approximately 2,400 employees with 540 snowplows and salt-spreading vehicles will be responsible for winter road maintenance in the capital in the event of slippery roads, snow and ice.

Illustrative image

Winter maintenance crews are gearing up for the cold season in Berlin. According to the Berlin City Cleaning Authority (BSR), it considers itself well-prepared for the task.

The storage facilities for deicing materials are also full. According to the report, a total of 13,000 metric tons of salt, 2,100 cubic meters of brine, and 5,000 metric tons of grit are on hand. Normally, the winter maintenance crew operates in two shifts—a third shift is added during extreme weather conditions. According to its own information, BSR is the largest municipal street cleaning and waste management company in Germany. In addition to winter road maintenance, its core responsibilities include street cleaning, garbage collection and waste treatment.

According to its own statements, the BSR is responsible for winter maintenance on the capital's numerous roads, bike paths and crosswalks. In addition, this includes approximately 6,300 bus and tram stops, certain squares such as Alexanderplatz and Breitscheidplatz, as well as specific pedestrian zones. According to the BSR, the top priority is clearing the approximately 260 working kilometers of urban highways. In addition, major thoroughfares and streets with local public transit routes are also given special priority.

However, when sidewalks are icy or covered with snow, Berliners must also do their part to ensure safety. Residents are generally responsible for clearing the sidewalks in front of their homes. Exceptions include paths in front of public green spaces such as parks - in these cases, the BSR is responsible. These responsibilities are governed by the Street Cleaning Act. According to the law, residents must also clear ice and snow from the access points to trash cans. In addition, the BSR urged all road users to adapt their behavior to the weather conditions and allow winter maintenance vehicles to pass quickly.
