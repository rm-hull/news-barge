Police are investigating an incident in Selby which left a teenager with injuries to their face.

Officers have issued this CCTV image of a man they would like to speak to following the assault.

The incident took place on Finkle Street, Selby, between 8.50pm and 9.15pm on Thursday 6 August 2026. Details have only just been released.

The victim was tackled off his bike and pushed into a shop window, resulting in injuries to his face.

**Selby Police say:** “We appreciate the image is not of the clearest quality but hope someone may still recognise the man, as he may have information that could assist our enquiries.

“We are also keen to hear from anyone who witnessed the incident, or anyone with dashcam, doorbell or CCTV footage that may have captured something relevant, or has any other information that could help.

“If you recognise the man shown in the CCTV image, or witnessed the incident, please contact us online in the first instance.”
