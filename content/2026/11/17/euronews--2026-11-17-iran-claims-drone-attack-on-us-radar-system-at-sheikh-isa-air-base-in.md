## Bahrain was heavily targeted earlier in the war, which started in February, but had been peaceful for weeks until an Iranian drone attack on Friday. A second followed on Sunday.

Blasts echoed over the Bahraini capital Manama on Tuesday, as Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they launched a drone attack, the third in five days after a weeks-long lull.

Bahrain's armed forces intercepted "Iranian aerial attacks in the kingdom's skies", according to state television, while Iran's Guards said they targeted a US-operated radar system.

Bahrain's Interior Ministry, in a post on X, warned of a "possible threat" and called on the country's residents to remain calm, move to the nearest safe place and follow only official sources for information on developments.

Bahraini officials also said the country's air defence systems had intercepted attacks from Iran.

No confirmed report has yet been released on any impact of Iranian drones on the radar system at Sheikh Isa base or on the extent of any possible damage.

The public relations arm of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps described the pre-dawn attack on Tuesday as a response to what it called "the continued violation of the airspace of the Islamic Republic of Iran" by US military drones.

It said the operation formed part of ongoing air-defence and electronic warfare measures to track, destroy and capture US drones of the MQ and MQM-170 series.

"Combat drones belonging to the Revolutionary Guards carried out a precision operation this morning, targeting a radar system operated by US forces at the Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain," the Guards' Sepah news website said.

The Guards also warned that continued use of regional bases and military facilities for actions against Iran would not go unanswered.

Bahrain was heavily targeted earlier in the war, which started in February, but had been peaceful for weeks until an Iranian drone attack on Friday. A second followed on Sunday.

Friday's strike was the first Iranian attack against a Gulf country since early September.

The US military said it had shot down two drones launched Friday by Iranian forces, while the Bahraini army said it had intercepted Sunday's attack.

Bahrain, which hosts a major US naval base, has experienced numerous Iranian attacks since US-Israeli strikes on Iran triggered the Middle East war in February.

Since September, the conflict has mainly been restricted to the area around the strategic Strait of Hormuz, with the US blockading Iranian ports and Tehran attempting to control the oil shipping route.

The Middle East war will be on the agenda during an unprecedented trilateral summit between US President Donald Trump and his Chinese and Russian counterparts, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin, scheduled this week in China, according to the White House.

As talks aimed at ending the war appear to be deadlocked, a third US aircraft carrier, the USS Theodore Roosevelt, is operating in the Middle East, the US military said on Monday.
