*Update: The A1237 Outer Ring Road in York between the B1224 Wetherby Road and the A59 is now running as expected with the obstruction reported to have been removed.*

An obstruction has blocked a major road in York causing queuing traffic.

AA Traffic News is reporting the hold-up on the northbound A1237 Outer Ring Road between the B1224 Wetherby Road and the A59 for the A1(M).

Traffic is said to be moving slowly as far south as the Askham Bryan Lane and Askham Lane roundabout following an incident which was first reported just after 7.30am this morning (Tuesday, November 17).

A statement issued by the AA said: "The road is partially blocked and queueing traffic due to an obstruction on the road on A1237 northbound between B1224 Wetherby Road and the A59."
