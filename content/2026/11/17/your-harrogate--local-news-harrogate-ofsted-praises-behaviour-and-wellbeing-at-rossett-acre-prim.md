Ofsted inspectors have praised pupils’ behaviour and the support for their wellbeing at Rossett Acre Primary School in Harrogate.

Under Ofsted’s new framework, the school achieved the ‘strong standard’ in two areas – attendance and behaviour, and personal development and wellbeing. It met the expected standard in all other areas.

Inspectors found that pupils behave well across the school and feel welcome, valued and included.

Staff know the children well and offer individual support to help them feel ready to learn.

Attendance is consistently above the national average.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afc268bd2a645534f5.png)

Inspectors also noted a significant fall in persistent absence among disadvantaged pupils.

The report highlighted the school’s personal development programme.

Pupils can learn a musical instrument, take part in sporting events and take on roles as pupil voice ambassadors or sports leaders.

Daily sessions help children explore the link between their thoughts, emotions and actions and learn how to manage their feelings.

In the early years, inspectors found that children settle quickly and benefit from opportunities to learn through play.

They also praised the teaching of early reading.

Headteacher Kate Woodcock said:

> “I am incredibly proud that this report reflects the happy, caring and ambitious school community we see at Rossett Acre every day.
>
> "It is particularly rewarding to see the strength of our work in attendance, behaviour and personal development recognised, as well as the kindness, respect and confidence our children demonstrate.
>
> "Above all, I am delighted that inspectors saw what matters most to us: children who feel safe, included and happy, alongside staff who know them well and are committed to helping every child thrive.
>
> “This report is a testament to the hard work of our children, staff, families, governors and the wider Red Kite Learning Trust community.
>
> "We are rightly proud of the many strengths recognised, while remaining ambitious about our next steps. We will continue to build on these strong foundations so that every child at Rossett Acre receives the very best education and opportunities we can provide.”

Rossett Acre is one of 16 schools across North and West Yorkshire that make up Red Kite Learning Trust.

Ian Tucker, the trust’s chief executive, said:

> “We are proud to see Rossett Acre recognised for its strong approach to attendance, behaviour and personal development.
>
> "The school's success reflects the positive contribution it makes to our wider Trust and the importance of creating an environment where pupils feel safe, valued and supported to achieve their best.
>
> "Congratulations to Kate and the whole school community.”
