## "Russia's war threatens all of Europe," President Maia Sandu wrote in a post on social media, adding that "several Russian drones" had violated Moldovan airspace on Tuesday.

Moldova closed its national airspace on Tuesday after an attack drone was detected heading towards the capital Chișinău.

Airspace was closed "for security reasons" at 4:27 pm local time after reports that a Shahed-type drone had been detected "moving from the south towards the Botanica sector of Chișinău," the ministry of defence said in a statement.

The eastern European nation, which borders Ukraine, has seen a growing wave of drone incursions this year, believed to be spillover from Russia's invasion.

President Maia Sandu said "several Russian drones" had violated Moldovan airspace on Tuesday, including one over Chișinău.

"Russia's war threatens all of Europe," she wrote in a post on social media, adding that she would continue to push for sanctions on Moscow while standing by Ukraine.

The defence ministry advised locals not to approach or touch any "suspicious flying objects" or debris as they could contain "explosive components."

Mihai Popșoi, Moldova's foreign minister, called the latest incident a "serious breach of our sovereignty" and a "direct threat to the safety of our citizens."

"Russia must stop actions that endanger Moldova" and "regional security," he added.

Police said they had received calls from residents in Chișinău and Florești reporting the sound of explosions or loud bangs on Tuesday.
