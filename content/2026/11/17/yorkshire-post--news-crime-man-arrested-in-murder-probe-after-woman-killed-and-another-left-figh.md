# Man arrested in murder probe after woman killed and another left fighting for life

## A murder investigation has been launched after a woman was killed and another left fighting for her life following a serious assault at a house in Bradford.

At around 1.40am on Tuesday (Nov 17), police were called to an address on Carr Bottom Road following reports that two women had been seriously assaulted by a man inside the property.

Emergency services attended and found two women, aged 46 and 20, who had suffered injuries.

The 46-year-old woman was pronounced dead at the scene, while the 20-year-old woman was taken to hospital, where she remains in a critical condition.

Following enquiries, a 27-year-old man was arrested elsewhere in Bradford on suspicion of murder and attempted murder. He remains in police custody for questioning.

![Police chase ](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/16/15/53/Police-officer-stock-image.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=420&quality=65)


Detective Chief Inspector Michael Cox, who is leading the investigation, said: “Significant enquiries remain ongoing today into what has clearly been a very serious incident in Bradford in which a woman has lost her life, and another has been critically injured.

“I fully recognise the concern this incident will cause in communities and want to reassure residents all necessary resources are being employed to investigate what took place at this address.

“We are holding a man in custody on suspicion of murder and attempted murder and can advise that at this stage, we are not looking for anyone else in connection with the incident.

“Specialist officers are working to support family members today, and local Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team officers are also conducting reassurance patrols in the area.”

DCI Cox added: “We would like to speak to anyone who has information or footage which could assist the investigation or who saw suspicious behaviour on Carr Bottom Road at about 1.40am today.
