More than 40 gamers gathered in Copenhagen, Denmark, on 15 November 2026 for the Classic Tetris European Championship.

Held at the Enigma Museum, the tournament saw players race to stack falling blocks as spectators cheered them on. Dutch competitor Eve Commandeur, 22, known as 'Sidnev', claimed the title after defeating Britain's Sam Vaughn, 21, by five matches to two. The pair battled through a tense final before Commandeur celebrated her victory and lifted the trophy.

First released in 1984, Tetris continues to attract new generations of players. The European championship, held annually since 2015, grew in popularity after moving online during the pandemic.
