A woman has told a court she was sexually assaulted in a cricket club toilet by a former England rugby league player.

The incident is alleged to have taken place at Horsforth Cricket Club in Leeds on July 22, 2022, involving ex England rugby league player and TV pundit Danika Priim, of Keighley.

The woman, who gave evidence from behind a screen, told Leeds Crown Court that Priim put her hand up her skirt and grabbed her underwear and private parts.

She said she confronted Priim at the time.

The complainant said: "What the f\*\*\* are you doing?"

She told the court that Priim repeated the action, grinned, and then walked out of the toilet.

A witness told police she thought Priim said something like "wahey" before leaving, the jury heard.

Priim, 42, denies a single charge of sexual assault.

The complainant described the events leading up to the alleged assault.

She said she had just used the toilet, washed her hands, and was applying lipstick while talking to two friends when Priim approached.

She said: "She walked straight up to me and put her hand up my skirt and grabbed hold of my pants and my private parts.

"I said: 'What the f\*\*\* are you doing?'

"Then she did it again, grinning at me, and walked off."

She described Priim's demeanour as emotionless.

The complainant said: "She was cold. There was no emotion.

"She was just staring at me."

She estimated that Priim was about the length of an A4 piece of paper away from her during the incident.

The woman told the jury she felt "a bit shocked and violated."

She said: "My reaction was: 'What on earth has she done?'

"'Why did she think it was OK to do that?'"

She said she and her friends talked about the incident afterward, expressing disbelief.

She added: "I just froze. I didn’t know what to do."

## 'I was sexually assaulted'

During cross-examination, Chloe Fairley, defending, suggested to the woman that Priim was not in the toilet with her.

The complainant replied: "Yes. She was."

She told the jury: "I’m saying I went to the toilet and that’s where I was sexually assaulted."

## Defendant says it never happened

Earlier in the trial, prosecutor Chloe Hudson told the jury the complainant did nothing to "encourage, ask for, or imply that she wanted to be sexually assaulted by this defendant."

Ms Hudson said the woman reported the incident to police in November 2022.

She told the court that the assault was witnessed by two of the complainant’s friends.

One of them told police she thought the defendant said something like "wahey" before leaving the toilets.

The jury also heard that the complainant discussed the incident with a friend on WhatsApp the following day, and the friend encouraged her to report it to the police.

Ms Hudson said Priim denied the allegation during a police interview.

She said: "The defendant said this incident never happened at all because she was never in the toilet with this complainant and her two friends."

The trial continues.
