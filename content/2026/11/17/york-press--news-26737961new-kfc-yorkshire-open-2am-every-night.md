A new KFC restaurant in Northallerton could stay open until 2am every night under plans submitted to North Yorkshire Council.

A premises licence application has been lodged for the new fast-food outlet being built on North Moor Road, on the northern edge of the town.

The application, submitted by Alderforce North Limited, seeks permission to serve hot food for consumption both on and off the premises between 11pm and 2am, seven days a week.

The proposed opening hours for the restaurant are 9am until 2am the following morning, Monday to Sunday.

The application would allow the restaurant to serve customers into the early hours throughout the week, including Sundays.

The new KFC is part of the ongoing commercial development in the North Northallerton area.

An Aldi supermarket, a Starbucks coffee shop and a Greggs are already open on the development.

A blue licensing notice displayed at the premises confirms that the operator has applied to North Yorkshire Council for permission to provide late-night refreshment under the Licensing Act 2003.

The application relates specifically to the sale of hot food and drink between 11pm and 2am, which requires a premises licence.

The notice does not specify when the new restaurant is expected to open, although signs at the site at the site say it will open in December.

KFC declined to comment on the application when approached by the Local Democracy Reporting Service.

Residents and other interested parties have until Thursday, December 10, to submit representations about the proposals.

Comments must be made in writing to North Yorkshire Council's licensing team by emailing [licensing.west@northyorks.gov.uk](mailto:licensing.west@northyorks.gov.uk) or by post to County Hall, Boroughbridge Road, Northallerton, DL7 8AD.
