The US has carried out further strikes on Iran despite Donald Trump’s claim that the war is “effectively over” following his humiliating defeat in the midterm elections.

An official told Reuters that a fighter ⁠aircraft ‌targeted a missile launch site ‌on Qeshm ‌Island in retaliation ⁠for Iran’s targeting of US personnel and assets.

Just last week, the US president declared the conflict “effectively over” and played down concerns that he would escalate after the elections.

Mr Trump had signalled Washington would hold off on ordering fresh strikes on Iran, provided there were no new attacks from Tehran or Yemen’s Houthi rebels.

“At this point, taking the military action doesn’t seem to be a very necessary thing – but we’re locked and loaded, ready to go for any reason,” he told reporters at the White House.

Washington carried out limited strikes on Qeshm earlier ⁠this month.

Asked for comment, the US military’s Central Command said it had no updates.

Meanwhile, Bahrain issued an all-clear early on Wednesday after warning of a potential ⁠threat and urging residents to move to the nearest safe location.

The ⁠Bahraini Interior Ministry did ​not ⁠specify the nature of the threat in its post on X. It ⁠had also issued an alert on Tuesday, ​two ⁠days after Bahrain said ‌it had intercepted an Iranian aerial attack on its territory that Tehran said had targeted ‌a US military base.

![Qeshm Island was targeted in retaliant for Tehran’s actions against the US](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/03/8/57/6816227-library.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Diplomatic efforts to end the war and open the Strait of Hormuz have been at a standstill despite the efforts of mediators from Pakistan and Qatar.

But Qatari and Iranian officials were meeting on Tuesday, Qatar said, as part of efforts to mediate an end to the nearly nine months of war that have destabilised the Middle East and pushed up global energy prices.

On Sunday Tehran launched drone attacks on a US military base in Bahrain, following a similar strike on Friday.

Earlier this week, the US deployed a third aircraft carrier, the USS Theodore Roosevelt, to the Middle East, signalling a potential resumption of hostilities.

The American midterm election results proved devastating for the Republican Party, as voters expressed anger over soaring prices sparked by the unpopular conflict, which was prompted by joint US and Israeli airstrikes on Iran on 28 February.

The US president said officials were taking seriously the possibility that Iran could be planning an attack involving a so-called dirty bomb in Washington, after being asked about a CNN report citing US intelligence assessments.

Iran’s foreign ministry spokesperson said the report was part of a US campaign “to lay the media groundwork for their sinister designs” using “baseless allegations”.

Trump has also said he will maintain a US naval blockade on Iran because it has continued to disrupt shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, which is a sticking point in negotiations.

Attacks on tankers have increased this month in the strait, and the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency said the crew of a tanker passing through the waterway was unaccounted for after being attacked north of the Omani port city of Khasab.
