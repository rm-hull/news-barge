In Italy, a 53-fingered robot pianist took on concert pianist Roberto Prosseda in Prato, Tuscany, on 12 November 2026.

The pair performed pieces by Chopin, taking turns before playing together. TeoTronico also tackled works by Mozart and Rimsky-Korsakov, boasting of its perfect timing and precision.

But Prosseda argued that technical perfection cannot replace the emotion and human connection at the heart of music.

Created in 2008 by Italian engineer Matteo Suzzi, TeoTronico uses electromagnets and computer instructions to play the piano. Integrated with Google's Gemini chatbot, it can also hold conversations.
