Some women in Kabul teach and study in secret, risking punishment under Taliban rules that have restricted their daily lives since the group seized power in August 2021. Some also run hidden salons or play sports, hoping to inspire others. The Taliban have barred women from most jobs and education, while morality police enforce strict dress codes and rules requiring male relatives to accompany them outside the home.
