Department of Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin is facing criticism for jetting out to watch a college football game over the weekend as millions of Americans were left stranded in airports waiting for delayed flights, according to a report.

Mullin and Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy had spent Friday dealing with the consequences of a five-hour nationwide ground stop, ordered by the Federal Aviation Administration in response to a technical glitch, which caused almost 7,000 flights to be canceled and another 8,000 delayed.

Once the issue had been resolved and journeys began to resume, the secretary flew into Baton Rouge, Louisiana, Saturday to catch the game between the LSU Tigers and the Texas Longhorns at Tiger Stadium, *The New York Post* reports.

There, Mullin was seen with Republican House Majority Leader Steve Scalise and in the private box of Bollinger Shipping CEO Ben Bordelon, who holds numerous contracts with the DHS.

Hollywood actor Matthew McConaughey, NFL greats Peyton Manning and Eli Manning and influencer Alix Earle were also at the game and saw the home side narrowly lose 21-22 after the visitors overcame an 11-point deficit to seize victory.

![DHS chief Markwayne Mullin is facing criticism after flying out to Louisiana hours after a nationwide ground stop saw thousands of commercial flights canceled or delayed](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/16/2026-11-14T015224Z_1804302534_RC2DNOANCJ2I_RTRMADP_3_USA-TRUMP.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Challenged about his travel arrangements by Joe Kernen during an appearance on CNN’s *Squawk Box* Tuesday, the Oklahoman said: “We would’ve never went if the airways weren’t completely open.

“They’d been completely open for 21 hours and we actually had an issue we had to deal with at the same time but I would have never taken airspace if airspace wasn’t open.”

Asked about his encounter with Bordelon, Mullin said he had never met the executive before and did not discuss business with him, that his department’s contracts with DHS predated his own tenure and that he had obtained his tickets from Scalise, not the shipping magnate, and had had to pay for them out of his own pocket as usual.

A DHS spokesperson meanwhile told *The Independent*: “Secretary Mullin is required by law to travel on a government airplane. This is to ensure access to secure communications and to comply by his constitutional duty of continuity of government should a national emergency arise.

“Any flight not related to his service as DHS secretary he pays for personally. Secretary Mullin abides by all ethics requirements.”

By Sunday, Mullin was back in Washington, D.C., and appeared on Fox News’s *The Sunday Briefing*, where he told Peter Doocy that he did not believe anyone would be fired as a result of the mass grounding.

It was subsequently determined that the disaster had been caused by a contractor replacing old copper wiring with fiber-optic cable, which accidentally overloaded a router that supported air traffic control systems and caused intermittent disruptions to communications and radar feeds, ultimately forcing the FAA to place the pause on flights “out of an abundance of caution.”

![Mullin and Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy address reporters in the White House briefing room Friday about the five-hour pause on flights and ensuing chaos](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/17/06/GettyImages-2305027510.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

“This was an issue with the equipment, Peter,” the secretary told Doocy. “What we were trying to do – and I’m not trying to get ahead of the FAA – I deal with terrorist threats and the whole of government organization, which is what we did Friday night.

“But what it appears to be is that during the process of trying to make an old, 50-year-old system – which is basically an old dial-up system – work with the new technology we have today, it’s caused problems.”

Mullin subsequently returned to Fox Monday evening and attracted ridicule by telling Sean Hannity a “guy that’s very smart with tech” had suggested to him that AI could be behind the recent spate of IT outages.

The questions raised about the DHS chief’s travel follows similar reporting about Secretary of State Marco Rubio last week, which revealed he regularly flies in to Gainesville, Florida, to see his son Anthony play football for the Florida Gators.

FBI Director Kash Patel has also been scrutinized over his use of government planes to fly around the country to watch his country singer fiancee Alexis Wilkins in concert.
