Paul Forster will lead his final Harrogate ghost walk of the season next month to raise money for a friend who has been diagnosed with cancer.

All proceeds will go to Mark Godfrey, his wife Laura and their two sons, four-year-old Teddy and two-year-old Lando.

Mark, a self-employed actor, was diagnosed with bowel cancer in February.

The cancer has since spread despite chemotherapy, and he is unable to work during treatment.

Laura, a self-employed singer, has also had to step back from work to care for Mark and their sons.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afc7070ada129c33ba.png)

Money raised from the walk on Friday 11th December will help the family cover their household bills.

Paul said he would usually have finished his tours for the year by now. He’s hoping people who have already joined him on a ghost walk will come along again in December.

Paul said:

> “I ask anyone, even if you’ve been on the tour before, to come again and help me raise as much as possible.
>
> "All proceeds will be donated to Mark and his family who are struggling financially.
>
> "He is self-employed and cannot work and he really needs support. I’d love to raise as much money as I can to help see them through these terrible times."

Ghost walk guests will meet at the Royal Pump Room Museum on Friday 11th December from 8.15pm.

Tickets cost £10 for adults and £5 for children, with all proceeds going to Mark, his wife Laura and their two sons, four-year-old Teddy and two-year-old Lando.
