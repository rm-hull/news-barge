LNER is set to operate a few special trains away from its usual route to help keep some of its passengers connected between London, York and Northallerton during engineering work.

On Saturday 28th and Sunday 29th November the line between King’s Cross and Peterborough is closed again.

However, **a limited number** of LNER trains will divert to Bedford on the Midland Main Line again after a successful trial earlier this year. Passengers can then connect into ThamesLink trains to London St Pancras.

This means that you won’t need to use a bus if you book these special services.

There will be **two trains** each way on Saturday 28 November, and**one train** each way on Sunday 29 November.

Other arrangements apply for Grand Central (Thirsk) and Hull Trains (Selby) services.

|  |
| --- |
| *At other points during the weekend, LNER services will start and end at Peterborough. From there, modern, accessible rail replacement coaches will connect customers to Bedford where Thameslink trains will operate to London St Pancras. Customers who would usually travel with LNER are asked to use the diverted services or rail replacement coaches rather than travelling via Sheffield, Derby, Nottingham, and Cambridge. This is because trains on other routes are expected to be very busy.* |

LNER has created a dedicated web page to help customers book tickets these via Bedford services more easily and to find all the information they need.

Details can be found at: [https://www.lner.co.uk/news/book-your-tickets-to-and-from-london-via-bedford/](https://www.lner.co.uk/news/book-your-tickets-to-and-from-london-via-bedford/)
