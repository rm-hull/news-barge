## Norwegian Foreign Minister Espen Barth Eide told Euronews that Moscow must not be allowed to dictate Europe's security arrangements while also warning against a return to spheres of influence.

Norwegian Foreign Minister Espen Barth Eide has warned that Russia cannot have any say over NATO's military activities within its own territory, pushing back against Moscow's demands to reshape Europe's security order as diplomatic efforts to end the war in Ukraine gather pace.

Speaking to Euronews, Barth Eide insisted that Europe's existing security architecture did not need to be renegotiated and that sovereign countries must remain free to choose their own alliances.

“We're not happy about this language about architecture,” he said. “We believe that we have a European security architecture. There was nothing wrong with it until Russia violated it.”

Barth Eide added that no deal should be imposed on Kyiv without Ukraine’s consent. “There should be nothing about Ukraine without Ukraine."

He said discussions with Moscow following a ceasefire could address arms control, verification mechanisms and confidence-building measures, drawing on precedents established during the Cold War.

The minister also warned against any agreement between the world's major powers to carve up the globe into spheres of influence, invoking the legacy of the 1945 Yalta Conference.

“The last thing we want would be an agreement between great leaders to divide the world between them,” he said. “These kinds of spheres of interest should be a thing of the past.”
