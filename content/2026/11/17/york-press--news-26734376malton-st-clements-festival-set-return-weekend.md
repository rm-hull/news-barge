## Festival celebrating winter, community and craftsmanship returns

The Malton St Clement’s Festival will take place on Saturday, November 21, , bringing a full day of celebration to Ryedale. *(Image: Freelancer)*

A POPULAR festival is set to bring seasonal cheer to a North Yorkshire town this weekend.

The Malton St Clement’s Festival will take place on Saturday, November 21, , bringing a full day of celebration to Ryedale.

Centred around Malton’s Market Place, the event will feature hands-on workshops, traditional crafts, live blacksmithing, street entertainment, artisan food, and a twilight Son et Lumière.

A spokesperson for the Malton St Clement’s Steering Group said: "St Clement’s Festival is all about bringing our community together to celebrate local craft, culture, and fantastic food.

"This year, we’re thrilled to expand the footprint of the festival with the new Town Trail, pick up your map from Kemps in the marketplace, and to offer a truly special dining experience at The Cook’s Place to close out the evening."

Named after St Clement, the patron saint of blacksmiths, the festival has become a highlight of the winter calendar.

In addition to its established programme, the 2026 event will introduce new features.

These include a self-guided town trail to help visitors explore Malton’s lesser-known attractions.

A festive tree competition will see local businesses decorate sponsored trees for public voting.

A pop-up café will open at The Parish Project on Spital Street for the day, serving a specially created menu of warming hot drinks, and unique paired creations.

The evening will conclude with a ticketed long table dinner hosted by The Cook’s Place, beginning at 8pm.

This intimate dining experience will showcase seasonal Yorkshire produce and must be booked in advance.

More artists and workshops will also be on offer.

Visitors can try their hand at crafts in the Milton Rooms or browse displays by regional makers.

The festival promises something for all ages.

Malton’s Market Place will be filled with artisan food stalls, street performers, choirs, and interactive craft sessions.

Entry to the main daytime events is free.

Town trail maps can be collected from Kemps General Store & Bookshop.

The long table dinner at The Cook’s Place will take place at The Parish Project on Spital Street and requires booking in advance through The Cook’s Place website.

For more information, visit the event page on Visit Malton or contact the organising committee at [maltonstclements@gmail.com.](mailto:maltonstclements@gmail.com.)
