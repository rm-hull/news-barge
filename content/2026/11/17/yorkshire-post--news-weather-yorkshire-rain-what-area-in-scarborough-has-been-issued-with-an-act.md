# Yorkshire rain: What area in Scarborough has been issued with an ‘act now’ flood warning and where will be most affected?

## Various areas in Yorkshire are hit with heavy rain this week and a flood warning has been issued for some coastal areas in the country, including Scarborough.

The Met Office has predicted unstable weather conditions across Yorkshire this week with a cloudy, cold and wet day with persistent rain and some strong winds today (November 17).

Throughout the day it is expected that sleet or snow will fall across the hills until late morning and tonight it will be wet and cold with rain and some sleet over the hills, gusty winds will gradually east though it will be blustery along the coast.

It is set to be raining the rest of the week with some sunny intervals and drier and brighter from Thursday, November 19, onwards, although there may be showers in coastal areas on Friday and Saturday.

![A number of flooding warnings have been issued by the Environment Agency for the Yorkshire Coast including areas of Scarborough and Bridlington.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjVhNDRiNjgzLWQ3MjctNGExMy04NjIwLWFkOTUzYzdhNDhjYzphMzIwMTU2Yi1mNjVhLTQ5Y2YtOTI5Mi04N2YyODgwZjQxMWE=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The Environment Agency has issued a flood warning for North Sea at Scarborough - Sandside with high tides, strong winds and large waves expected to cause flooding today.

Flooding is expected from 11.30am to 9.30pm and locations that are most affected will be properties on Sandside around the Old Harbour.

The risk of flooding should reduce once the tide has passed and the Environment Agency is closely monitoring the situation.

Advice from the agency is to take care and avoid walking, cycling or driving through flood water.
