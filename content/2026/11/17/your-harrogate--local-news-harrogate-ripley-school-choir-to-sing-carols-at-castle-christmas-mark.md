Pupils from Ripley Endowed CE Primary School will sing carols at Ripley Castle’s Christmas market next weekend.

The choir will perform at noon to raise money for the school’s Learning Outside the Classroom initiative on Sunday 29th November.

The school plans to redevelop its gardens so pupils can spend more time learning outdoors and exploring nature.

The children will sing traditional carols including ‘Away in a Manger’ and ‘O Little Town of Bethlehem’, alongside festive favourites such as ‘The Twelve Days of Christmas’ and a selection of worship songs.

Victoria Kirkman, headteacher at Ripley Endowed CE Primary School, said:

> “At Ripley Endowed CE Primary School, we believe some of the most memorable learning takes place beyond the classroom walls.
>
> "By enhancing our outdoor learning spaces, we can provide even more opportunities for children to develop confidence, curiosity and a love of learning in the natural environment.
>
> "We are incredibly grateful to Real Markets and the local community for supporting this important project.”

Market organiser Real Markets will also contribute to the school’s fundraising efforts.

Lucy Allen, director of Real Markets, said:

> “We are thrilled to have Ripley Endowed CE Primary School back to sing carols at our Christmas Market and delighted to contribute to their fundraising efforts.
>
> "What could be more perfect at Christmas than a little shopping and food with loved ones, a walk around a beautiful estate and gardens, and the chance to meet Father Christmas to the sound of carol singers!”

The market runs from 10am to 3pm and will feature around 40 independent Yorkshire makers, producers and artisan businesses.

Entry to the market, castle and grounds is free.
