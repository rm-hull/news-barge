## Guns and sketches marking specific locations were reportedly seized as part of the operation, which was carried out in cooperation with Greece's national intelligence agency.

North Macedonia's National Security Agency (ANB) announced on Tuesday that it had prevented a "terrorist" assassination plot targeting a person in Greece and that it had dismantled an Iranian-linked network.

The ANB said it had brought down "a wide network of proxy actors" linked to Iranian Edris Jahangiri Mavaneh, who it described as a "collaborator of the Iranian security and intelligence services".

The agency said the network "undertook preparatory logistical activities to carry out a terrorist act/assassination against a person in Greece" while "under the direct instructions of the Iranian national."

Guns and sketches marking specific locations were reportedly seized as part of the operation, which was carried out in cooperation with Greece's national intelligence agency.

An anti-terrorist force also took part, police said. Raids were carried out at four locations.

The operation began in early 2026, the security agency's head, Bojan Hristovski, said, adding that the group had held "conspiratorial meetings on the territory of third countries, including Iran."

"As for the Macedonian network, it involves individuals embedded within criminal structures in the country," Hristovski told a press conference.

Four suspects have been taken into custody, while another has been placed under house arrest, an official said.

One person was detained in Greece and transferred to authorities in North Macedonia.

A sixth suspect was still being sought.
