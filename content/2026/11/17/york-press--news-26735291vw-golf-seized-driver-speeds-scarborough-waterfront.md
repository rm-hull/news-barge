A car has been seized in a North Yorkshire town after a driver was reported to have been speeding and overtaking along the seafront.

North Yorkshire Police said the suspected incident happened in Royal Albert Drive, Scarborough at around 9.40pm on Sunday (November 15) when officers stopped the driver of a VW Golf for driving in a careless and anti-social manner.

A force spokesperson said the driver, from Bridlington, was reported for the offences and the vehicle was seized.




Recent changes to the law were reflected in the vehicle being taken off the streets immediately, the force said.

The spokesperson said: "The seizure of a vehicle prevents anti-social driving from continuing at that time, with the driver facing a financial penalty to retrieve their vehicle from the pound.

"Previously, a driver would be issued with a warning, which would remain valid for 12 months.

"If they then received another warning within that period, their vehicle would be then seized."
