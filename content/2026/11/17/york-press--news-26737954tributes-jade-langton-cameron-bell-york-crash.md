## ‘I will miss you forever’ – moving tributes after deaths of two York crash victims

‘Miss and love you always’ – tributes have been paid to young York crash victims Jade Langton, pictured, and Cameron Bell. *(Image: Photo: Facebook)*

FLOWERS have been laid at the scene of a crash that took the lives of two people from York.

Jade Langton, aged 23, and Cameron Bell, 22, were killed in the early hours of Sunday, November 15, after a BMW 123D hit the white Volkswagen Scirocco they were travelling in.

As well as a floral display at the site of the collision which happened in Gelderd Road in Leeds, friends and supporters have been paying tribute on social media and sending messages of support to the grieving families.

One close friend, still reeling from the tragedy, shared that Jade was "the most caring loving and craziest person I have ever met" who "never failed to cheer me up".

"I love you endlessly Jade and I never said that enough - I will miss you forever my beautiful angel. Rest easy," she wrote about the former All Saints RC School student.

Another friend wrote: "Miss and love you always Jade and Cam."

Well-wishers have also sent messages to the families of Ms Langton and Mr Bell who are being supported by specialist family liaison officers.

One said: "Poor Jade and Cameron. My heart goes out to their parents, family and friends. Absolutely tragic circumstances."

"A life unnecessarily cut short," wrote another.

Leigh Ross, 39, of Prospect Place in Harrogate, was charged in connection with the collision and appeared before Leeds Magistrates' Court on Monday, November 16.

Ross is alleged to have caused the deaths of Mr Bell and Ms Langton while fleeing from police in a blue BMW 123D, reaching speeds of 80mph on two roads and doing a U-turn, before crashing into another vehicle, the court heard.

Ross sobbed in the dock with his voice breaking as he confirmed his date of birth and address.

He is charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while uninsured, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle whilst disqualified and two counts of causing death having taken a vehicle without consent.

Mr Bell, who was driving the Volkswagen, died at the scene.

Ms Langton, a passenger in the vehicle, died from her injuries in hospital shortly afterwards.

"The poor families," said another supporter. "It’s ok sobbing in the dock but the actions taken have cost two families their loved ones, who now have to live with that loss forever.

"My heart goes out to them both. Jade was my sister's friend and so young at 23 - it’s no age."

Another wrote: "So sad, they were both so young. Sending love to both their families; they must be heartbroken", while someone else said: "My sympathy is with them and to their bereaving family and friends."

This brief but poignant message read: "Two families in grief forever."

Ross was remanded in custody and is due to appear at Leeds Crown Court on December 17.

Looking towards the public gallery as he left the dock, he said: "I’m sorry."

Following the incident, three other occupants of the BMW, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a 16-year-old girl, were taken to hospital with injuries, not considered life-threatening.

West Yorkshire Police said they arrested the three on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in relation to the incident.

They were then released on bail pending further enquiries while officers from West Yorkshire Police Major Collision Enquiry Team continued to appeal for witnesses to any part of the incident.

Anyone with information, or relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact officers via 101 or online via the Live Chat facility on the website quoting reference 13260649342.

West Yorkshire Police referred the incident to the Independent Office for Police Conduct, which declared an independent investigation.
