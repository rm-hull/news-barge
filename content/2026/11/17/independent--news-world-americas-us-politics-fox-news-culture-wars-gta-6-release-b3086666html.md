As gamers anxiously await this week’s release of *Grand Theft Auto VI*, Fox News hosts are already starting the culture wars over the crime-fueled video game series.* Fox & Friends Weekend* hosts Rachel Campos-Duffy, Charles Hurt and Griff Jenkins recently looked at a preview of the new game, featuring characters Jason Duval, an Army veteran working for local drug runners, and Lucia Caminos, who was previously jailed after fighting for her family.

“I’m gonna push back a little,” Campos-Duffy, wife of Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy, said. “I’m not thinking that this is a good thing for our culture.”

Jenkins then suggested Campos-Duffy could play as Luicia, prompting her to ask, “Is she the hooker?”

“No, she’s a partner-in-crime,” Jenkins said. Campos-Duffy then laughed it off. “You can maybe play and get her to go straight,” Jenkins added.

![Fox & Friends Weekend hosts Rachel Campos-Duffy said in response to the forthcoming Grand Theft Auto VI, ‘I’m not thinking that this is a good thing for our culture’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/18/46/Screenshot-2026-11-17-at-1-00-02-PM.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Conservatives and liberals have long fought over cultural beliefs, typically when it comes to civil rights, including diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives and LGBTQ+ issues.

Fox News is known to promote the conservative view, railing against DEI and trans rights. And now, the network is coming after your favorite video game.

In *Grand Theft Auto VI*, set to be released at midnight on Thursday, Jason and Lucia will find themselves in the middle of a criminal conspiracy in the fictional state of Leonida, where they will need to rely on each other to make it out alive.* Grand Theft Auto*, or* GTA*for short, is one of the most popular gaming franchises of the century.* GTA* fans have been waiting more than a decade for a new game in the series, making Thursday’s release even more exciting for them.

![Fox News is known to promote the conservative side of culture wars, railing against DEI and trans rights. And now, the network is coming after your favorite video game](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/18/10/GettyImages-2305026330.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Roughly 15 million copies of *Ground Theft Auto VI* have been preordered, CNN Business reported, citing Sensor Tower data.

Strauss Zelnick, the CEO of GTA’s publisher, Take-Two Interactive, told *Variety* that presales of the new game are “running far in excess of our expectations.”

Violent games like *GTA*,* Call of Duty*and* Mortal Kombat* have faced scrutiny over the years for potentially leading to bad behavior in players, such as aggression.

But a study published in *Molecular Psychiatry*in 2018 found no changes in behavior between participants who played* Grand Theft Auto V*, the non-violent video game* The Sims 3* and those who played no game at all.
