* **Proton VPN sign-ups from Jordan rose 110% amid national Telegram ban**
* **Jordan's security services cited extremism, cybercrime, and blackmail**
* **Citizens are being urged to choose their VPN carefully**

People in Jordan are turning to VPNs after authorities blocked Telegram across the country, with Proton VPN reporting a 110% surge in sign-ups above its usual baseline.

Jordan's Public Security Directorate (PSD) announced the block on Monday, November 16 — Reuters reported. It said the move aims to bolster digital security and stop the app being used to spread extremism and radicalization following a deadly church attack in October. Authorities have not said how long the restriction will last.

A VPN can get around this kind of block by routing your traffic through a server in another country. That's why demand for the best VPN services tends to climb whenever a government cuts off a popular app.

## Proton VPN sign-ups more than double

![Graph showing a spike in Proton VPN usage in Jordan in November 2026](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/8kgo4PaeyfAf33m3jezHZ9-1200-80.png)

Proton's Internet Censorship Observatory logged the spike in Jordan starting on November 15 and lists it as ongoing.

Proton says it regularly sees sign-ups jump after protests, contested elections, and government crackdowns. Jordan is a familiar name on that list. For example, when Discord stopped working in the country in October 2025, Proton VPN sign-ups peaked at 250% above baseline. Jordan also banned TikTok in December 2022.

The current surge is smaller than neighbouring Iraq's for now, where Proton recorded a 1,200% rise in sign-ups when Telegram was blocked in April.

## Why has Jordan blocked Telegram?

As reported by Al Jazeera, the block follows a security crackdown after an October 23 attack on a Greek Orthodox church in Karak governorate that killed nine people.

Jordan then carried out airstrikes in Syria that it said killed 20 Islamic State militants. This month, a security officer was killed in what authorities called a terrorist stabbing in Amman.

According to reports, Islamic State militants allegedly used Telegram to issue statements. The PSD has urged citizens to report unlawful content through official channels.

## Where else is Telegram blocked?

Jordan is far from alone. Around 30 countries have disrupted Telegram at least once, often during political unrest. Recent examples include:

* **Russia**: Moved to fully block the app in March 2026, prompting Telegram CEO Pavel Durov to urge Russians to stock up on VPNs.
* **Iraq**: Blocked Telegram in April 2026 amid tensions between the government and armed groups.
* **India**: Temporarily blocked the app in June 2026 to stop cheating ahead of a medical entrance re-exam.
* **Vietnam**: Ordered a block in May 2025, accusing Telegram of failing to cooperate on crime.
* **Nepal**: Banned the app in July 2025, citing online fraud and money laundering.

China, Iran, and Cuba have long-standing bans in place.
