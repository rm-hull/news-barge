A hands-on hospitality showcase in York is set to inspire the next generation of industry talent.

The event will take place on November 23, at The Grand Hotel, and is aimed at young jobseekers from York and North Yorkshire.

It is organised by York Jobcentre in partnership with the Hospitality Junior Board, and will welcome around 15 attendees for an immersive introduction to the hospitality sector.

Wendy Mangan, recruitment and partnership manager at York Jobcentre, said: "Hospitality is one of York's flagship industries and offers so much more than many people realise.

"It is a sector full of opportunity, where young people can develop professional skills, build confidence and progress into rewarding long-term careers."

Unlike a traditional job fair, the showcase will focus on practical, hands-on experiences designed to give participants a real feel for the industry.

Activities will include bedmaking challenges, setting function tables to five-star standards, and creating mocktails.

These exercises aim to demonstrate the skills, teamwork, and professionalism required in hospitality roles.

Attendees will also hear from members of the Hospitality Junior Board, a group of young professionals working in leading hotels across York and North Yorkshire.

They will share their career journeys and highlight the opportunities for growth, development, and leadership within the industry.

Ms Mangan said: "This event gives young jobseekers a chance to experience the industry first-hand, meet professionals who have successfully built careers in hospitality, and gain practical insight into what employers are looking for.

"We hope it inspires attendees to see hospitality as an exciting career choice with real opportunities for growth and development."

In addition to learning about the sector, participants will receive expert advice on applying for vacancies, highlighting transferable skills and standing out during the recruitment process.

Ms Mangan believes the showcase will also benefit employers.

She said: "Events like this are also incredibly valuable for employers.

"By engaging with the next generation of talent, businesses can showcase the fantastic opportunities available within their organisations and help attract enthusiastic young people who are ready to learn, develop, and contribute to the sector's future success."

The Hospitality Showcase is part of a wider effort by York Jobcentre and its partners to support youth employment and help build a sustainable workforce for the hospitality sector.

The initiative comes as the number of 18-24s on the claimant count has increased by more than 10 per cent across our region over the past year.

Government figures show York had an overall claimant count of 2,520 in October, an increase of 120 or 5 per cent. 18-24s made up 565 of this, an increase of 60 or 12 per cent on a year ago.

Ryedale reported a claimant count of 615, an increase of 60 or 11 per cent on last October. The 18-24s made up 85 of this, an increase of 10 or 13 per cent.

Selby reported a claimant count of 1165 in October, an increase of 50 or 4 per cent. The 18-24s made up 245 of this, an increase of 15 or 7 per cent, the Department of Work and Pensions added.
