# Exciting new Irish bar serving 'proper pint's opens in the heart of Leeds city centre

## Lots of people have been eager to see the new venue open

Take a look inside an exciting new Leeds city centre bar.

Love, Jackie has launched in the city. The Irish bar, serving ‘proper pints’ is based in Merrion Street.

The new opening comes from Leeds-based Professionals at Play - the group behind Roxy Ball Room and King Pins. Visitors can expect ‘the holy trinity of Irish stouts’ - Guinness, Beamish and Murphy’s.

There’s lots of fun to be enjoyed during a visit. There is crazy pool, interactive darts, pool, shuffleboard and two karaoke rooms - while there will be live music every Thursday, Friday and Saturday. You can also enjoy live sports events being screened.

Matt Jones, chief executive officer of Professionals at Play, said: “Everyone knows a Jackie. She’s the one who gets everyone together, convinces you to stay for another and makes sure there’s never a dull moment. We wanted to create a pub with that same personality. Somewhere with brilliant drinks, live music, plenty of games and the kind of atmosphere that makes you forget you were only popping in for one.

“Merrion Street is coming back to life, and we’re excited to be part of its next chapter. We think Jackie will fit right in.”

The new venue opened earlier this month. You can see photos of Love, Jackie by scrolling below.
