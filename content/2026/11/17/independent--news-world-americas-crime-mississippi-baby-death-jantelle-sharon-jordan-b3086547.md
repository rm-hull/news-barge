A Mississippi mom has been accused of murder after her baby died from blunt force injuries just days after he was born, according to authorities.

​Jantelle Sharon Jordan was arrested on Friday, almost a month after her baby Jaiden Shane Jordan-Burkes was born at an address in Polkville, a small town in Smith County.

​“This is a horrific case for everyone involved in working on this investigation,” Smith County Sheriff Joel Houston said in a statement. “These are the types of cases that are difficult for anyone to comprehend, especially when they involve the loss of an innocent child.”

​First responders and representatives from the sheriff’s office responded to a call about a woman in labor at the Polkville address on October 18.

Upon arriving, they found that Jordan had given birth around an hour earlier and had contacted the emergency services to have Jordan-Burkes examined.

![Jantelle Sharon Jordan’s son showed “medical complications” soon after birth](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/17/15/23/Screenshot-2026-11-17-at-15-55-29.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​The infant was taken to the hospital, where he began to show “medical complications” of an unknown cause. Medics later determined that he had become injured shortly after being born, with the youngster dying two days later, according to the sheriff’s office.

​An autopsy revealed that his cause of death was blunt force trauma, with the boy’s death being ruled a homicide. The sheriff’s office did not detail how the boy came to be injured.

​Jordan was interviewed by authorities on November 13 and was arrested and charged with capital murder at the conclusion of the meeting. ​Authorities say that the investigation is ongoing.

“The Smith County Sheriff’s Office recognizes the public’s interest in this case and remains committed to transparency while protecting the integrity of the investigation,” a spokesperson wrote in a statement. “

Cases of this nature require time, careful examination of evidence, and discretion from those involved to ensure that all facts are properly established and that appropriate charges can be pursued.”

*The Independent* has contacted the sheriff’s office for further comment.

![The Smith County Sheriff's Office (office pictured in center-right) says that the infant was born at an address in Polkville](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/17/16/03/Screenshot-2026-11-17-at-16-00-42.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=122%2C1%2C42%2C0&auto=webp)

​A visitation and funeral for Jordan-Burkes were scheduled to take place on November 1, according to an obituary published by Ott & Lee Funeral Homes.

​Included in the obituary was a passage describing the family’s love for the late infant.

​“Jaiden was born on October 18, 2026, in Smith County, Mississippi, and although his time here was short, he made a lasting impact on the family that loved him so dearly,” the passage reads.

“He would have loved racing and riding four-wheelers with his father and grandfather.”
