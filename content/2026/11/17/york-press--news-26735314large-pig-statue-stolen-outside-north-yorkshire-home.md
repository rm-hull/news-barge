Police are appealing for information after a large pig statue was stolen from outside a property in a North Yorkshire village.

The county's force said the theft took place in Mowbray Terrace, West Tanfield at 5.09pm on Saturday, October 24.




North Yorkshire Police released an image of a man they would like to speak to and are asking for the public's assistance in identifying him, saying he may have information that could help in its investigation.

Anyone with information for the force is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by emailing PC1570 Coulthard at [richard.coulthard@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:richard.coulthard@northyorkshire.police.uk.)

Alternatively, information can be submitted online by visiting the North Yorkshire Police website.

Those unable to use these options can call 101, quoting reference 12260210816.
