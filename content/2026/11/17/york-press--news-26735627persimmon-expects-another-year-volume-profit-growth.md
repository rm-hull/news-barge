Major housebuilder Persimmon expects to deliver another year of volume and profit growth.

In a trading update, the York-based company has reported a 3 per cent increase in private forward sales to £2.15 billion and a 1 per cent rise in total forward sales to £2.82 billion.

Planning approvals reached 8,722 plots in the first nine months of the year, up 12 per cent from 7,753 in the same period last year.

Dean Finch, group chief executive, said: "We are pleased to have sustained our resilient performance in a challenging market, with our market share continuing to grow.

"Our clear strategy has allowed the Group to increase both sales and planning approvals at a time when the overall sector is declining.

"We expect to deliver another year of volume and profit growth, reflecting continued progress against our strategy and the strength of our three brands, product mix, national footprint, and low-cost operating platform.

"We remain on track to deliver our 2026 performance in line with market expectations."

It has a strong pipeline of more than 100 outlets planned for 2027 to support future growth.

Since July 1, the sales rate rose to 0.79 net private sales per outlet per week, compared to 0.76 during the same period last year.

Excluding bulk sales, the rate stood at 0.65.

The build-to-rent sector remains active, with 77 per cent of planned 2026 deliveries already exchanged.

The average private selling price in the forward order book was approximately £301,900, up 2 per cent from £295,150 a year earlier.

Land expenditure in the third quarter totalled £107 million, bringing the year-to-date figure to £378 million.

The company held around 81,300 plots as of September 30, down from 83,800 the previous year.

Mr Finch continued: "We acted quickly to identify operational efficiencies which are expected to substantially mitigate the additional cost headwinds by 2028.

"As set out in August, we anticipate a modest restructuring charge in the current year."

Persimmon has opened 57 new outlets so far in 2026 and expects to reach around 100 by year-end.

The group operated from an average of 263 outlets during the reporting period, compared to 272 last year.

It plans to grow its outlet network further in 2027.

Looking ahead, Persimmon expects to continue increasing volumes despite ongoing market challenges, including affordability pressures and higher borrowing costs.

The company has already identified measures to offset these headwinds by 2028 and continues to invest in long-term value creation.
