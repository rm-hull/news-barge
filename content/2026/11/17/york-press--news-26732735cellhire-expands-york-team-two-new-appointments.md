A connectivity company has strengthened its team with two new appointments.

York-based Cellhire, a specialist in IoT connectivity, has welcomed Josh Corcoran as IoT account manager and Matthew Worrall as business development executive.

Duncan Griffiths, head of IoT sales at Cellhire, said: "Josh and Matthew join us at a time when there is more and more demand for IoT connectivity that can be relied on, regardless of where devices are.

"Bringing in new experienced people like Josh and Matthew means we can keep pace with rapid IoT growth in the UK and internationally."

Mr Corcoran brings experience from roles at EE, BT and Tela Technology.

He said: "Connectivity only adds value when it’s built around what a business actually needs.

"My focus will be to help customers by demonstrating what connectivity can achieve for their specific requirements."

Mr Worrall added: "We’ll both be helped by the capabilities of Cellhire’s SIM management platform and multi-network connectivity."
