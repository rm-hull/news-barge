## Motorists advised not to visit famous pub due to heavy snow

Heavy snow has been falling near the Tan Hill Inn, in Swaledale, near Richmond. *(Image: Tan Hill Inn)*

MOTORISTS are being advised not to travel to a world-famous pub due to the weather.

Heavy snow has been falling near the Tan Hill Inn, in Swaledale, near Richmond.

The Inn is the UK’s highest pub, standing 1,732ft above sea level with uninterrupted star views and little light pollution.

Over the years, the Tan Hill Inn has become notorious for "snow-ins" due to its isolated location in the Yorkshire Dales.

In November 2021, about 60 people got trapped by heavy snow at the pub - which is 1,732ft (528m) above sea level - after going to see an Oasis tribute band.

In January 2025, a snow plough, snow blower and gritters had to be brought in to get through 6ft (2m) drifts so 23 pub-goers - including a four-year-old child - and six workers could get home after being stuck at the inn for five days.

Posting on social media the Tan Hill Inn said: "Due to snowfall, we’re advising against travelling to Tan Hill at the moment. We’ll keep you updated here as conditions change."
