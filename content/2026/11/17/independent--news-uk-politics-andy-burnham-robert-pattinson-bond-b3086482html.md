Prime Minister Andy Burnham says he’s "absolutely delighted" for Robert Pattinson after the actor was announced as the next James Bond.

It was announced on Monday that the 40-year-old Batman star would be replacing Daniel Craig in the role, ending years of speculation about who will be the next 007.

Craig began his tenure as Bond in 2006 with *Casino Royale*and stepped away after his fifth film,* No Time to Die*, in 2021. Actors Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner were also reportedly considered for the part before Pattinson was landed the role.

Asked if Mr Burnham was pleased, the Prime Minister's official spokesman said: "He’s obviously delighted for Robert Pattinson. It’s an amazing opportunity."

Asked whether the news left him "shaken or stirred", Burnham replied, saying: "I’d love to answer that question, but I think any one of you could be working for Blofeld."

![Andy Burnham has thrown his support behind Pattinson](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/14/7a38c551152f6166ece4299914fa6356Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1MDA4OTY4-2.86510610.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The royal family marked the news on Instagram, writing: "Welcome to His Majesty’s Secret Service, Mr Bond."

Former Bond star George Lazenby, the sole Australian to play the spy, posted on Instagram: "Robert, welcome to the most exclusive club in the world.

"But remember to enjoy yourself, let’s see if you have ‘this never happened to the other fella’ moment."

He signed off: "All the best mate, George."

Elsewhere, Ralph Fiennes, who has previously played M in the Bond franchise, did not sound overly enthused about Pattinson’s appointment after being asked about it during an interview with Andy Cohen on *SiriusXM Front Row.*“I’m sorry. I’m in love with Daniel Craig still,” said the actor, who worked with Pattinson’s 007 predecessor in five films from 2006’s* Casino Royale*to 2021’s* No Time to Die*.

He went on, however, to clarify that he believes Pattinson is up to the task. “No, he’s a great actor,” he said. “I’m sure he’ll be great.”

![Pattinson will be the seventh actor to play Bond](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/10/2284931307..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Pattinson follows in the footsteps of six other big-screen Bonds: Sean Connery, Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Craig.

The next film, currently dubbed Bond 26, is in development, with *Peaky Blinders*creator Steven Knight announced last year as screenwriter.* Dune* filmmaker Denis Villeneuve will direct, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman as producers.
