## Brit Award winners to play York Museum Gardens next year - here's how to get tickets

Live at York Museum Gardens *(Image: Provided)*

A BRIT Award winning band are set to headline a major outdoor concert next summer in York.

Bastille has been announced as the latest headliner for the 2027 Live at York Museum Gardens concert series on Friday, July 2.

They have joined the concert series which will take place over two weekends for the first time in its history, joining Olly Murs and The Human League.

The group - whose debut album Bad Blood entered the charts at Number 1 - last played in York at the Barbican in 2014.

A spokesperson for the concert series said: "Chart-topping, Brit Award-winning pop titans Bastille will play York Museum Gardens on Friday 2nd July 2027 as the latest headliner revealed for next year’s Live at York Museum Gardens concert series.

"The botanical gardens of the historical city’s much loved landmark are set to host more unforgettable performances from musical legends and rising stars than ever before as the series extends to run across two weekends for the very first time."

Their debut album included the international hit "Pompeii," which helped them become the biggest-selling global breakthrough act of the year, with over a billion streams in 2013 alone.

Since then, the band has released two more studio albums and numerous hit singles, including their 2018 collaboration with Marshmello, "Happier."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21728522.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bastille are set to play York's Museum Gardens *(Image: Provided)*

The track spent a record-breaking year at Number 1 on Billboard’s Hot Dance/Electronic chart.

Their latest album, the sci-fi-inspired Give Me The Future, was released in 2022 and became their third Number 1 record.

Recent collaborations have included work with To Kill A King frontman Ralph Pellymounter, US indie pop band Joywave, and Grammy-nominated electronic artist ILLENIUM.

The Live at York Museum Garden series, now in its fourth year, has previously featured sold-out performances from Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark (OMD), Self Esteem, and Super Furry Animals.

Comedians Russell Howard, Ross Noble, and Joanne McNally also headlined as part of the sister event, York Comedy Festival.

For the first event, York’s Shed Seven headlined Museum Gardens in 2024, leading the way for other acts to follow with the event being the first time that the park had staged a major rock gig since the 1970s and 80s.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/20439057.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The crowd during the Live at York Museum Gardens series 2025 *(Image: Andy Hughes)*

Jocelyn Palmer, senior events manager at York Museums Trust, said: "We are so excited to bring Live at Museum Gardens back for another year, continuing our wonderful partnership with the team at Futuresound.

"Watching the concerts go from strength to strength each year has been so rewarding for us at York Museums Trust and the gardens are a wonderful space where we can blend its rich heritage and iconic scenery with fantastic seasonal events that gives the city centre a variety of entertainment, including these amazing concerts.

"Every penny helps us as a charity to continue to invest in our spaces and put on more unique events and experiences. We can't wait to share more details soon and welcome everyone into the gardens next summer."
