Take That star Gary Barlow says that Prince William has taken his father’s passion for the environment to the “next level” with the Earthshot Prize.

The pop group will perform two songs at the Prince of Wales’s glittering Earthshot Prize awards ceremony, which recognises innovative green initiatives and is being staged in Mumbai in front of a 5,000-strong audience.

Barlow, Howard Donald and Mark Owen have met William around 20 times over the course of their career, which spans more than 30 years, according to Barlow, who has written many of the band’s hits.

Speaking ahead of the gig, the band recalled performing for royalty in the past, including Diana, Princess of Wales.

![The Prince and Princess of Wales are currently in India to attend the sixth annual Earthshot Prize Summit.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/11/01M7MH4651YE2ZFRM436MQWYE9.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

They also remembered getting the royal family dancing to Never Forget at the King’s Coronation Concert at Windsor Castle three years ago.

Take That have performed at the Royal Variety Performance four times.

They played the 1993 Concert for Hope for World Aids Day at Wembley Arena, which Princess Diana attended, and they closed the King’s Coronation Concert.

Asked about the Earthshot Prize, now in its sixth year, Donald admitted he first heard of William’s project six months ago, but said: “It’s super-inspiring to have people trying to help climate and to help the Earth. I find it fascinating.

“I mean, for me personally, anything at home, anything that’s recyclable, anything that’s reusable, anything that doesn’t pollute rivers, I’m there all the way.”

The band are playing in India for the first time.

They will perform their track “The Greatest Day” as well as a song from their new album *Dreamers*, which is released on Friday.

Barlow said their most memorable royal performance was the Concert for Hope.

A week before that event, they were invited to spend an evening with Diana.

“What came out of it was her passion to do something that mattered in the position she was in, and so of course we were thrilled,” he said.

![Gary Barlow, Howard Donald and Mark Owen of Take That perform during a special one off show for their new album 'Dreamers' at London Palladium](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/11/2305024296..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Speaking about the royal family’s reaction to their performance at the King’s Coronation Concert, Donald said: “We saw them literally directly in front of us, waving their arms to Never Forget.”

Asked about William and his involvement in the Earthshot Prize, Barlow said: “What you want in a leader like him is someone who turns up and engages and is passionate about what they’re doing.

“Whenever I’ve been around anything that he’s been involved in, he’s there leading everybody. He’s not letting other people lead him to the floor. He’s the one leading everybody.”

He said: “William’s dad, in the 70s, used to talk about microplastics, and people thought he was crazy.

“He’s really following on (from) his dad’s passion, I think.

“It’s wonderful when dads pass down things (and)… they take the baton, taking on to the next level.”
