A caravan park in North Yorkshire has been taken over by a holiday company with plans for future investment and improvement.

High Moor Farm Caravan Park, located west of Harrogate and en route to the Yorkshire Dales, is now part of Larkham Leisure’s portfolio of holiday parks across Yorkshire.

The park, which spans 90 acres and has welcomed guests since the 1970s, was previously developed by the Kershaw family.

Rolson Larkham of East Yorkshire company Larkham Leisure said: "We are pleased to welcome High Moor Farm Caravan Park into the Larkham Leisure family.

"With its fantastic location and outstanding potential, the park is an excellent addition to our growing portfolio across Yorkshire.

"We look forward to building on its established reputation, investing in its future and creating an elevated visitor experience that guests can enjoy for many years to come."

High Moor Farm currently offers static holiday caravans, touring pitches, a bar and function room, an indoor swimming pool, and fishing lakes.

Larkham Leisure said it plans to invest in the site while maintaining the character that has made it popular with visitors for decades.

A spokesman for Larkham Leisure said: "Over the coming months, our team will be getting to know the park, its guests and the local area as we begin planning for its future.

"We’re looking forward to building on these foundations and exploring how thoughtful investment can make the experience even better for holidaymakers and holiday home owners."

We’ll be sharing more news about our plans and progress as we move towards an exciting 2027 for High Moor Farm.
