# Wakefield crash: Man, 48, fighting for life after Audi crashes into tree and railings

## A 48-year-old man has been left fighting for his life after a car crashed into a tree and railings in Wakefield.

At around 8.19pm on Monday (Nov 16), police were called to the junction of Belle Vue Road and Agbrigg Road following reports that a blue Audi Q7 had crashed into a tree and railings.

The driver, a 48-year-old man, suffered a serious head injury believed to be life-threatening and remains in hospital receiving treatment.

![The junction of Belle Vue Road and Agbrigg Road](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/17/11/40/The-junction-of-Belle-Vue-Road-and-Agbrigg-Road.png?trim=0,51,0,56&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


Police are now appealing for witnesses to the crash and are keen to speak to anyone who may have seen the vehicle or the circumstances leading up to the incident.

Officers are also urging anyone who was in the area at the time to check CCTV or dashcam footage for anything that could assist their investigation.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “Anyone with information is asked to contact the Roads Policing Unit online via www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat or by calling 101 quoting log 1545 of 16 November. “
