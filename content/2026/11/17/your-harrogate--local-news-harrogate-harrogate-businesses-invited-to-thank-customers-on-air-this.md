Local businesses can send their customers a Christmas message on Your Harrogate Radio this Christmas.

Your Harrogate's Christmas Greetings campaign gives businesses a 10-second slot to wish listeners a 'Merry Christmas' and thank customers for their support over the past year.

Businesses can also use their greeting to tell listeners about a festive event, share their Christmas opening hours or give people a reason to visit in the new year.

**Getting involved**

Three packages are available, depending on how often businesses would like their greeting to be heard.

Prices are £250 for 25 plays, £350 for 50 plays and £400 for 80 plays.

If businesses would like to feature across the Your Media Group network - Your Harrogate, Your Ilkley and Your Skipton - ads are priced at £375 for 25 plays on each station, £425 for 50 and £600 for 80.

*Businesses interested in taking part can find out more on the Christmas Greetings page**here**.*
