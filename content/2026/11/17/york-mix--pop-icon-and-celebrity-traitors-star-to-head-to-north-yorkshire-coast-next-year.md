It’s been announced today that pop icon James Blunt is heading to the North Yorkshire coast next summer.

The multi-platinum-selling artist will play Scarborough Open Air Theatre on **Sunday 20 June 2027**, as part of his *Airplane Mode* European tour.

He will be joined on the night by special guests Nathan Evans & The Saint PHNX Band.

The announcement follows James’s hugely popular appearance in the second series of *The* * Celebrity Traitors*, where the faithful won over a whole new audience.

Tickets go on sale at 10am this Friday via Ticketmaster.

James Blunt has enjoyed a career spanning more than two decades, establishing himself as one of Britain’s most successful singer-songwriters. His multi-million-selling debut album *Back To Bedlam*became a global phenomenon, spawning the worldwide smash hit* You’re Beautiful*alongside much-loved tracks* Goodbye My Lover*and* Wisemen*.

His achievements have earned him two BRIT Awards, two Ivor Novello Awards and five GRAMMY nominations.

Earlier this month, James released his eighth studio album *Airplane Mode,*which soared straight to Number 1 on the UK Album Chart. Heavily influenced by the sounds of the 90s Britpop era,* Airplane Mode* combines James’ signature anthemic hooks with his nostalgic storytelling.

Fans can look forward to a night packed with hit songs, crowd favourites and tracks from James’s latest album, as the singer-songwriter brings his acclaimed live show, humour and unmistakable voice to the North Yorkshire coast.

Julian Murray of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough OAT, said: “James Blunt is one of those artists who seems to connect with audiences everywhere he goes.

“Whether it’s the big hits, new music or his trademark humour, he always puts on a great show, and we’re delighted to welcome him to Scarborough next year.”

James Blunt is the latest artist announced for 2027 at Scarborough OAT which will also see headline shows from Mariah Carey, Courteeners, Shania Twain, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti and James Morrison, and Simply Red.

For more information please visit the Scarborough Open Air Theatre website.
