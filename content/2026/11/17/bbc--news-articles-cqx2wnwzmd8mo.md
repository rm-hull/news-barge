British Airways has apologised to the customers who were affected

**A British Airways plane became stuck in the grass at Heathrow Airport after it veered off the taxiway.**

The Boeing 787 Dreamliner bound for Lagos became stuck after its front wheel entered the grass shortly after 10:00 GMT.

A spokesperson for the west London airport said they were continuing to assist the plane back to the aircraft parking area, adding that there were no injuries and no impact on wider operations.

A spokesperson for BA said: "We're very sorry to our customers for the disruption to their flight. Our teams are working to get them on their way as quickly as possible and are in touch with them to put things right."
