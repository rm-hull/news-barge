A brewery has struck gold at a recent awards ceremony for two of its beers.

Wold Top Brewery, based in Wold Newton, scooped two top honours at the Indie Beer Awards North East in York, with its Scarborough Fair IPA and Intuition taking gold in their categories.

Alex Balchin, brewery director at Wold Top Brewery, said: "Winning Gold at the Indie Beer Awards North East is a fantastic achievement and a real testament to the breadth of talent, skill and expertise within our brewing team.

"To be crowned category champion for both our alcohol-free beer and our strongest beer demonstrates the versatility of our brewers and their ability to produce outstanding beers across the full spectrum."

The awards, organised by the Society of Independent Brewers (SIBA), are judged by brewers and industry experts.

Both winning beers now progress to the national finals at BeerX in Liverpool, scheduled to take place in March 2027.
