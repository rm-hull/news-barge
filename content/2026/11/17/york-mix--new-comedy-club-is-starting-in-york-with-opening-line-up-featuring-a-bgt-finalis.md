A new grassroots comedy club is starting in York.

Founded by James Perham and Kiri Cheesman, The Hermit Comedy is set to host mixed-bill stand-up shows with comedians from around the UK at the Micklegate Social in York.

James and Kiri both started comedy at the University of York as members of the York Comedy Society, where they spent four years producing comedy in the city.

Now they’ve started their own comedy club – with the first event taking place on **Friday 20 November**.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/alex-mitchell-bgt.webp)

The inaugural line-up features Eva Peroni, John Pease, and *Britain’s Got Talent* finalist Alex Mitchell.

“Many comedians are excited about the prospect of performing at The Hermit Comedy,” James told ***YorkMix***.

“York’s live comedy scene has been growing over the years, and they want to help develop it with us.”

The Hermit Comedy have partnered with the Micklegate Social to host their events.

“Cool people go to the Micklegate Social, and cool people are coming to The Hermit Comedy. Coolness relies on coolness. After all, you wouldn’t put ice in an oven.

“The room itself works so well for comedy. The intimate, rustic vibe always results in a great gig, making it our perfect hermitage.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/yp1.jpeg)

With the first event taking place this week, James said that “it’s literally impossible to guess what may go on. We’ve invited people who could do anything.

“The only thing we can somewhat guarantee is there will be a mic, mic stand, and a humorous element to the evening.”

The second Hermit Comedy event will take place on Friday 4 December, with the line-up of Fab Goualin, Jake Stogden, Corin Bertrand, and Lianna Holston.

As for their biggest ambition for The Hermit Comedy? “We want the Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, David Skaith, Lord Mayor of York and Ceremonial First Citizen, Councillor Margaret Wells, and York MP Rachel Maskell all to attend The Hermit Comedy.

“We hope they will be so impressed they bring back court jesters just so they can make us the official ones in the York council and Westminster respectively. We want them to be proud of what young people are achieving in their city.”

To find out more and book your tickets for a Hermit Comedy night, visit their website here. Tickets are £12. Stay up to date with all things Hermit at their Instagram page.
