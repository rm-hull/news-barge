A new bank has opened in an East Yorkshire town offering face-to-face services.

The Barclays branch, located in the centre of Beverley at 39 Saturday Market Place, opened its doors at 9.30am yesterday (Monday, November 16).

A spokesperson said it aims to provide a modern and accessible banking experience for residents across the wider East Riding.




Claire Goforth, branch manager at Barclays Beverley, said: "We are delighted to open our new branch.

"We know how much our customers value being able to speak to someone face to face, and this branch gives people across Beverley and the wider East Riding a convenient place to do so.

"Whether customers need help with their everyday banking, would like to speak to our Premier Financial Guide or are looking for support with their finances, our team is here to give them the time and guidance they need.

"We look forward to serving the local community and supporting our customers."

The branch was officially opened by Beverley businesswoman Shirley Pearson, who has run a shop in the town for more than 50 years, with a ribbon cutting.

She was joined at the opening by Mayoress Councillor Alison Healy and community representatives.

The spokesperson said the new branch features Barclays’ latest banking technology, including self-service machines for deposits, withdrawals, and cheque payments.

Customers can visit without an appointment or book ahead to ensure they have dedicated time with a member of staff.
