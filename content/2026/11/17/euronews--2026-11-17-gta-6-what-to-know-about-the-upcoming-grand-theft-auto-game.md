Grand Theft Auto, better known as GTA, is one of the world's most successful and controversial video game franchises. Its sixth instalment is due to launch on 19 November, with excitement spreading across Asia despite censorship, restrictions and a gaming market dominated by mobile phones.

Created by Scottish studio Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games, GTA is an open-world action-adventure series. Players take on the role of criminals in fictional versions of American cities, where they can explore freely, steal cars, take part in high-speed chases and complete missions involving organised crime.

The games combine action and storytelling with satire of American society. Their depictions of violence, crime and sexual content have also made the franchise controversial.

The previous instalment, GTA V, was released in 2013 and became one of the best-selling video games of all time, selling more than 230 million copies, according to its publisher.

Now, more than a decade later, anticipation is building for GTA VI. The new game returns players to Vice City, a fictional city inspired by Miami, Florida.
