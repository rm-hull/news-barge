Wetherby is charging up for the big Christmas lights switch on.

The anticipated showcase, on Saturday December 5, marks the start of the festive season and shines a light on businesses and young people.

A spokesperson for organiser Wetherby Town Council, said: “We have a packed schedule with lots of activities, entertainment and photo opportunities.

“We are thrilled to be holding the Shop Window Dressing Awards and Jubilee Awards on the same evening so we can celebrate our wonderful businesses and young people as a community.”

The event will be officially opened by town Mayor Coun Connor Mulhall at 4pm.

At 4.30pm the winner of the Jubilee Awards, celebrating a young person who has made a special contribution to the town, will be announced.

And at 5.30pm, the town’s best dressed shop window award will be declared.

Entrants will have been judged on the best Christmas impact.

A highlight of the evening will be a lantern parade of creations by residents.

People can get creative to make their own festive lantern in the Town Hall, from 4pm, and then join the parade ready at 7.15pm for the lights switch on at 7.30pm.

The festive glow will continue with a community Carol service at 7.45pm.

Just days later, the famous annual Wetherby Lions Dickensian Christmas Market, will take place.

Starting at 9am and running until 3pm on Sunday December 13, there will be artisan stalls based in and around the Town Hall.

“We work hard to make the Dickensian Christmas Market the one event that really brings Wetherby people of all ages together in the most festive setting," said Wetherby Lions President, Ruth Holt.

“Thank you to Adele of Beecrafty Markets for curating the Artisan Craft Fayre in the Town Hall on our behalf and to Littlebird Made for curating the external market. “

Entertainment will include music, choirs, and an appearance from Santa on his sleigh.

Wetherby Lions will be presenting their annual Townsperson of the Year Award at the event.

The winner, voted for their outstanding contribution to Wetherby, will be announced on the day.
