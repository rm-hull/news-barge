A Harrogate school has been recognised for its commitment to helping pupils thrive both in and beyond the classroom in a new Ofsted report.

Following a recent inspection under Ofsted’s new framework, Rossett Acre Primary School also achieved a strong standard in both attendance and behaviour, and personal development and wellbeing. It also met the expected standard in all other areas.

Ofsted describes the expected standard as a high standard, meaning schools are providing the education and support children need.

The strong standard recognises practice that consistently goes beyond these expectations.

Headteacher Kate Woodcock said: “I am incredibly proud that this report reflects the happy, caring and ambitious school community we see at Rossett Acre every day.

”It is particularly rewarding to see the strength of our work in attendance, behaviour and personal development recognised, as well as the kindness, respect and confidence our children demonstrate. “

Based at Pannal Ash Road, Rossett Acre Primary School is part of Red Kite Learning Trust.

The school's personal development programme was recognised by Ofsted inspectors as a key strength.

Centred on its vision of ‘excellence and happiness for all’, it gives pupils opportunities to develop their interests, confidence and independence.

These include musical instrument tuition, sporting events and leadership roles such as pupil voice ambassadors and sports leaders.

Inspectors were also impressed by pupils' understanding of mental wellbeing.

Through daily sessions, children explore the connection between their thoughts, emotions and actions, and learn how to manage their feelings.

They also develop a secure understanding of how to keep themselves safe, both physically and online.

Attendance at Rossett Acre is consistently above national averages. Inspectors noted improvements for disadvantaged pupils, including a significant reduction in persistent absence over time.

Pupils' behaviour was praised by inspectors, who found that they behave well across the school and feel welcomed, valued and included.

Staff get to know children and their individual needs and inspectors recognised the work of the skilled team who provide support to help pupils overcome barriers to their wellbeing and feel ready to learn.

Headteacher Kate Woodcock said: “I am delighted that inspectors saw what matters most to us: children who feel safe, included and happy, alongside staff who know them well and are committed to helping every child thrive.

“This report is a testament to the hard work of our children, staff, families, governors and the wider Red Kite Learning Trust community.

“We are rightly proud of the many strengths recognised, while remaining ambitious about our next steps.

“We will continue to build on these strong foundations so that every child at Rossett Acre receives the very best education and opportunities we can provide.”
