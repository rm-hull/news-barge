# Tributes flood in for 'unforgettable' friends Jade Langton and Cameron Bell after tragic Leeds crash

## Friends have shared emotional tributes to two young lives lost in a devastating Leeds crash

Tributes have flooded in for two young friends who lost their lives in a devastating collision on a road in Leeds. Cameron Bell, 22, and Jade Langton, 23, both from York, died following a crash on Gelderd Road in the early hours of Sunday, November 15.

The pair were travelling in a white Volkswagen Scirocco when it was involved in a collision with a blue BMW 123D, which had failed to stop for a police vehicle. West Yorkshire Police said the incident happened at 12.44am, with Cameron pronounced dead at the scene and Jade sadly dying in hospital a short time later.

A 39-year-old man has been charged in connection with their deaths, including two counts of causing death by dangerous driving. He has been remanded in custody.

Friends and loved ones have been sharing heartfelt tributes to the pair, remembering them as kind, caring and much-loved young people whose lives were tragically cut short. One close friend, Ewan Mcpherson, penned an emotional tribute to Jade and Cameron, admitting he never expected to find himself writing about losing his friends.

He began by saying: "Not the kind of thing I would ever expect to have to talk about. We are always reminded to spend as much time as possible with those who won’t be part of our lives forever, but I never expected to be writing this about friends as opposed to family."

Paying tribute to Jade, Ewan described her as an "amazing person at heart", saying he had grown to know her well through her friendship with Skye.

He wrote: "She was quite possibly the most outgoing and caring person I've ever had the pleasure of knowing, and she was always ready to help anyone in need however she could.

"I can only speak for myself when I say that she helped me more than I could have ever repaid her for, but I know many people who would say exactly the same. We both had difficult situations to deal with while I knew her, it goes without saying hers much more difficult than mine, but I hope she knew that there was nowhere we wouldn't have gone, nothing we wouldn't have done to help had she needed it."

Ewan also remembered Jade's resilience, describing how she managed to bounce back from difficult experiences that would have derailed many people's lives, while still keeping a smile on her face. He added: "I will miss the endless night drives, the adventures, the new places, the poor decisions that always seemed to yield good memories, and most of all the bond you create with a person who you could trust with anything."

He also took time to remember Cameron, admitting he had not been fortunate enough to get to know him as well as others had. Ewan wrote: "However, from the few times I met him, I could see just how kind and genuine he was. He always said exactly what he thought, and he never held anything back.

"It was so amazing to watch him open up to us all over time and become a part of our lives. I wish I had more time to get to know him properly."

Turning his thoughts to the families affected, Ewan said his heart went out to everyone grieving the two young people, particularly Jade's family. He wrote: "No parent should ever have to grieve the loss of their child. And in the same vein, nobody should ever have to lose their sister, their friend, their auntie at such a young age."

Concluding his heartbreaking tribute, he said: "Two innocent lives lost. Two people with their whole lives still to live who will never find out where they might have ended up. Two families ripped apart in a matter of seconds. Rest in peace Jade and Cameron."
