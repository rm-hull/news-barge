A murder investigation has been launched following the death of a woman in Yorkshire.

A man has been arrested and detectives are appealing for witnesses after an incident in Carr Bottom Road, Bankfoot, this morning.

Police were called to the address at about 1.40am after reports that a man had seriously assaulted two women inside, as reported by our sister title The Telegraph & Argus.

## Two women found by police

Emergency services found two injured women, aged 46 and 20.

The 46-year-old woman was pronounced dead at the property.

The 20-year-old woman was taken to hospital, where she remains critically ill.

A 27-year-old man was arrested elsewhere in Bradford on suspicion of murder and attempted murder.

He remains in custody for questioning.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21729147.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A police van on Carr Bottom Road this lunchtime *(Image: Newsquest)*

Detective Chief Inspector Michael Cox, who is leading the investigation, said: “Significant enquiries remain ongoing today into what has clearly been a very serious incident in Bradford in which a woman has lost her life, and another has been critically injured.

“I fully recognise the concern this incident will cause in communities and want to reassure residents all necessary resources are being employed to investigate what took place at this address.

“We are holding a man in custody on suspicion of murder and attempted murder and can advise that at this stage, we are not looking for anyone else in connection with the incident.

“Specialist officers are working to support family members today, and local Bradford South Neighbourhood Policing Team officers are also conducting reassurance patrols in the area.

He added: “We would like to speak to anyone who has information or footage which could assist the investigation or who saw suspicious behaviour on Carr Bottom Road at about 1.40am today.

“Information can be given to the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team on 101 or online at www.westyorkshire.police.uk/livechat referencing crime number 13260652937.

“It can also be given anonymously to the independent Crimestoppers charity on 0800 555 111.”
