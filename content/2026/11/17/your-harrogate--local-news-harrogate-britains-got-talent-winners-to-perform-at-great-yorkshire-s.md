The Hawkstone Farmers' Choir will be the first choir to perform in the Main Ring in the Great Yorkshire Show’s 189-year history.

The choir will perform twice a day in Harrogate from Tuesday 13 to Friday 16 July 2027, giving visitors eight chances to see the group.

Made up of farmers and agricultural workers from across the UK, the group was the first choir to win Britain’s Got Talent.

Its debut album, *This Is Home*, is due out on Friday 20 November.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afc2be6b161373cb1a.png)

Tickets for the show went on sale today. Visitor numbers are capped at 150,000 across the four days and all tickets must be booked in advance.

Rachel Coates, Great Yorkshire Show Director and a West Yorkshire farmer, said:

> “We’re always looking for ways to make a visit to the Great Yorkshire Show even better and so we have made a few forward-thinking changes this year, which we are excited to be announcing as we reveal our Main Ring attraction for 2027.
>
> "The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir is doing a fantastic job of reaching the public about farming and so it’s a perfect fit for the Show which celebrates and shares the important story of agriculture to thousands of people every year.”

The showground will open at 7.45am each day, 15 minutes earlier than before, giving visitors more time to reach the rings for competitions starting at 8am.

Adult **tickets** cost £38 and child tickets are £13.50. A new family ticket for two adults and two children costs £91.28, while a family ticket for five costs £93. Under-fives can enter free with a ticket booked in advance. Under-16s must be accompanied by an adult.
