A visitor needed hospital treatment after being attacked by a woman at a leading attraction.

The woman victim was assaulted at **York Maze** on Elvington Lane at**10.40pm** on**Friday 23 October**.

This was during the maze’s popular Hallowscream event.

#### York Maze

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “It involved a woman being assaulted and required hospital treatment for face injuries.

“It is understood that the suspect is a woman, and may have been in the company of others.

“It has come to light that there may be a key witness to the incident who left before leaving their details, please get in touch if this may be you.

“If you have any other information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
