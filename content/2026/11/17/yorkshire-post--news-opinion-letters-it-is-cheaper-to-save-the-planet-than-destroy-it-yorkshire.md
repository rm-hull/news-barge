# It is cheaper to save the planet than destroy it: Yorkshire Post Letters

## Geoff Petty, Orchard Terrace, Totnes, Devon.

Renewables are much cheaper than fossil fuels now. For example, electricity from new renewable sources is generally 41 per cent to 53 per cent cheaper than the lowest-cost fossil fuel alternatives globally.

Electric cars are generally 65 per cent cheaper to run per mile compared to petrol cars. Those huge savings persuade people to go green, and the cost of renewables are getting cheaper every year.

Also, politicians want to ensure that their country’s energy supply is secure, despite wars caused by people like Putin, Trump and Netanyahu. It is much better to use your own renewables than to rely on expensive oil exports disrupted by wars.

For example, solar has saved the EU €37 billion since the war on Iran began, because oil prices surged when oil tankers were stopped from going through the Straits of Hormuz.

It is cheaper to save the planet than to destroy it.

Deloitte, the top business consultantcy, found that unchecked, climate change could cost the global economy $178 trillion over the next 50 years.

By contrast, it could gain $43 trillion over the next five decades by rapid decarbonisation.

Worldwide investments in renewables, grids, storage, and electrification reached $2.2 trillion, double the $1.1 trillion directed toward oil, natural gas, and coal.

The only politicians who defy this logic are the ones funded by fossil fuel interests.

Fossil fuel lobbyists, polluting industries and climate deniers have donated £8.4 million to the Conservative Party since December 2019.

Reform UK has received £24 million from oil and gas interests, more than two thirds of its total income. It looks like these parties are up for sale.

**Malcolm Parkin, Kinnesswood, Kinross-shire.**

You can't talk someone out of a belief they have arrived at by dispensing with the process of reasoning.

So people who don’t think are drawn into the welcoming climate cult. Then look at the CND; then the people who joined pyramid-selling schemes; then the converts who suddenly see God at a Billy Graham concert, then the Greenham Common women.

Looking at them tells you something, which is that they all need the company of similar unthinking people, in the pursuit of pretty much anything.

They need to belong.

Several steps up from that hole in the ozone layer, we now have harmless and beneficial CO2 – the food of crops and everything that grows – renamed as a killer gas in the atmosphere.

Previously sensible people now believe that CO2 will destroy the planet by means of the weather.

So billions we don’t even have are being borrowed and spent on schemes to rid us of CO2, while our economy collapses under onerous climate legislation.

But the unreasoning people are all together in their comforting delusion. And that’s the real purpose of it all.
