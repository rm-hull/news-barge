The Alexandra in Harrogate has been named among the UK's best pubs for dog owners to visit this autumn.

The pub on Prospect Place is the only Yorkshire venue on the shortlist of ten, compiled by pub and restaurant guide Eat Drink Meet.

The guide highlighted nearby walking spots including Valley Gardens and the Pinewoods.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6afc0cf331e60278957.png)

A spokesperson for Eat Drink Meet said:

> “The Alexandra is ideal for dog lovers looking for somewhere to rest after a long stroll through Harrogate with their pet this autumn.”

The pub serves traditional British dishes alongside cask ales and a selection of low- and no-alcohol drinks.

Other pubs on the list include The Curlers Rest in Glasgow, The Hanging Gate in Derbyshire and The Bears Head in Cheshire.
