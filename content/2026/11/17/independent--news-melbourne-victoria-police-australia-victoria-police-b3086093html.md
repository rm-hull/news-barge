Makybe Diva’s three Melbourne Cup trophies are missing after a heist at Flemington Racecourse.

Victoria state police on Tuesday said officers raided a home in Mount Waverley, in the southeastern suburbs of Melbourne on Monday night as part of investigations into the burglary and arrested a 36-year-old man, who was later released pending summons to appear in court.

“It is alleged two men attended the racecourse ... and smashed a cabinet inside the venue at 6:30 a.m.” Monday, Victoria Police said in a statement.

Makybe Diva won the Melbourne Cup in 2003, 2004, and 2005 and the trophies had been on public display since September at Flemington Racecourse, where the southern hemisphere’s most prestigious horse race is held annually on the first Tuesday of November.

Police said the trophies stolen from the glass display case were each valued at 1 million Australian dollars ($675,000).

Makybe Diva, known widely in local racing circles as “the people's horse,” died in February at the age of 26. She was the only horse to have won the Melbourne Cup three years in a row in a race which was first held in 1861.

“Hopefully we can get them back in one piece,” former owner Tony Santic told Melbourne radio 3AW. “It’s more than devastating. I just can’t believe it. Hopefully we get them back in one piece.”

The Victorian Racing Club said it was “devastated” by the theft and it was in contact with Santic.

Point Of Law won the 166th and most recent edition of the 2-mile classic on Nov. 3.
