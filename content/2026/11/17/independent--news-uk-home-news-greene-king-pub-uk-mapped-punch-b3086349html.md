Dozens of Greene King pubs are set to be taken over by a major UK hospitality firm after the company announced it was putting up to 150 of its venues on the market.

Punch Pubs & Co is expected to snap up 24 of Greene King’s establishments across England and Scotland in what it said showed a “commitment to great pubs and thriving communities across the UK”.

Earlier this year, Britain's second-largest pub group Greene King announced plans to sell up to 150 of its managed sites and convert another 150 into tenanted pubs amid soaring costs and a tougher trading climate.

The company said the pubs will be phased into its fast-growing portfolio and refurbished to “position each pub for future success”.

![The Greene King pubs will be taken over by Punch Pubs](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/02/12/ac747aa8d86bb68bd7ec5331b70ce0a0Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkzNzA4NDY5-2.10354172.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Andy Spencer, chief executive Ooficer of Punch Pubs, said: “This is an exciting opportunity for our business and represents another important step in our acquisition strategy. We know that former managed pubs make fantastic additions to our estate, and this portfolio is a collection of brilliant pubs in excellent locations across the country.

“We have a strong track record of investing in pubs, empowering talented partners and building social connections at the heart of communities. We look forward to welcoming these pubs and their teams to Punch.”

Several of the pubs have closed under Greene King, but are set to get a new lease of life under the new owners.
