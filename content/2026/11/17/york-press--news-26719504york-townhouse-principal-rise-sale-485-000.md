## Four-bedroom York townhouse with park views for sale at £485,000

A four-bedroom townhouse in Principal Rise, York, is on sale for £485,000 *(Image: Linley & Simpson)*

A four-bedroom townhouse with a balcony overlooking neighbouring parkland is for sale in York.

The freehold property in Principal Rise is listed at £485,000 with estate agents Linley & Simpson - York.

It contains two reception rooms, a dining kitchen, four bedrooms, three bathrooms and a separate WC.

The house is arranged over four floors and includes a utility room, integral garage and off-street parking.

At ground level, the entrance hall has a storage cupboard, central-heating boiler and stairs to the first floor.

The hall leads to a utility room with a sink and space for a washing machine and dryer.

A garden room has a bay window and French doors opening onto the garden.

On the first floor, the lounge has French doors opening onto a balcony with views towards parkland.

A separate WC and a cupboard containing the hot-water tank are also on this level.

The dining kitchen contains wall and base units, work surfaces, a sink and a built-in double oven.

It also has a gas hob, extractor, fridge, freezer, dishwasher and a French door with a Juliet-style balcony.

On the second floor, one bedroom has a built-in wardrobe and a three-piece en-suite shower room.

Another bedroom has windows facing the rear and could be divided into two rooms.

The family bathroom includes a bath, sink, WC and heated towel rail.

A third-floor bedroom has two skylight windows and its own en-suite shower room.

This level contains the fourth bedroom, which has two skylights and a storage cupboard.

Outside, the west-facing rear garden has a lawn, paved area, planted beds and gravelled path.

Front parking leads to the garage, which has an electric roller door, lighting and power.

For more information, or to book a viewing, contact Linley & Simpson - York on 01904 409538.
