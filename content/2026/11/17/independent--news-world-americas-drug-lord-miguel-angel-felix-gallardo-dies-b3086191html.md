Notorious Mexican drug lord Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo has died in a hospital in the western city of Guadalajara aged 80.

Known as ‘The Godfather’ and the ‘Boss of Bosses’, Félix Gallardo was one of the founders of the Guadalajara cartel, and is considered to be one of the biggest drug smugglers in history.

The Jalisco state security department said in a statement that he died on Sunday at 2 pm local time due to multiple organ failure.

![Félix Gallardo had been in hospital since Friday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/08/2026-11-15T235207Z_1206314268_RC2NOOAPWTIM_RTRMADP_3_MEXICO-CRIME-GALLARDO.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The 80-year-old kingpin had been hospitalised since Friday, when he was rushed from Puente Grande, a maximum-security prison complex.

He had been held at the prison since 1989, and was serving a combined 77-year prison sentence for drug trafficking offenses and for the 1985 killing of US Drug Enforcement Administration agent Enrique “Kiki” Camarena.

Together with Rafael Caro Quintero and Ernesto Fonseca Carrillo, Félix Gallardo founded the Guadalajara cartel, also known as The Federation, in the late 1970s.

The cartel dominated drug trafficking in Mexico during the 1980s cocaine boom, entering into partnership with the Colombian cartels like Escobar’s Medellín Cartel to pool production in the Andes with Mexican transportation across the border into the US.

It is reported that at one point, an estimated 80 per cent of all cocaine that entered the US came from the Medellín Cartel.

![Félix Gallardo was serving a combined 77-year prison sentence](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/08/2026-11-15T235420Z_212325964_RC2NOOAGX58F_RTRMADP_3_MEXICO-CRIME-GALLARDO.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

However, the Guadalajara cartel eventually splintered into several criminal organisations in the country’s north, including the Sinaloa cartel.

All three founders were convicted in Mexico in connection with Camarena’s killing.

The DEA agent was kidnapped and tortured before his body was found in 1985.

Caro Quintero was released on procedural grounds in 2013, but was recaptured in July 2022.

In February 2025 he was extradited to the United States along with 28 other prisoners wanted by US authorities on drug-related charges.

Félix Gallardo had sought house arrest since 2019, citing his advanced age and deteriorating health.

While courts initially rejected his requests, in September 2022, a judge granted him house arrest, but the order was never carried out because of legal challenges by Mexico’s federal attorney general’s office.
