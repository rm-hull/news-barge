It’s a tale as old as time, as York Theatre Royal announce their pantomime for next year.

Their 2027 pantomime will be family favourite *Beauty and the Beast*, running from**3 December 2027 to 3 January 2028**.

And York favourite Robin Simpson has been announced to be returning for his eighth year as Dame, as he gears up to delight audiences in this year’s festive offering *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*.* Beauty and the Beast* is written by Paul Hendy and directed by Juliet Forster, and York Theatre Royal are renewing their collaboration with Evolution Productions for this magical fairytale panto.

York Theatre Royal members will have access to 5-day priority booking with *Beauty and the Beast*– and all the new 2027 shows going on sale – from 1pm on Wednesday 18 November. General sale will be open at 1pm on Monday 23 November.* Beauty and the Beast* is the latest announcement in York Theatre Royal’s diverse line up of co-produced work for 2027.

Next year, theatregoers can look forward to a new production of Arthur Miller’s timeless masterpiece *Death of a Salesman*, a co-production with Glasgow Citizens Theatre;* Tristan & Yseult*, a co-production with the Emma Rice Company, Alexandra Palace Theatre and Nottingham Playhouse; and a bold new production of Andrew Lloyd Webber’s* Sunset Boulevard* with Octagon Theatre Bolton and Royal & Derngate.

Paul Crewes, chief executive of York Theatre Royal said: “With a new year comes another exciting period of collaboration for York Theatre Royal.

“We are building relationships with brilliant partners, artists and collaborators up and down the country, producing fresh approaches to some beloved titles including Arthur Miller’s Death of a Salesman, Andrew Lloyd Webber’s Sunset Boulevard and working with the Emma Rice Company once again on the landmark production Tristan & Yseult.

“We’re proud to continue building a bold and varied dance programme and to curate an eclectic programme of presented work on our stage. The diversity on offer – from breathtaking literary adaptations to opera, comedy, music to fun and fabulous nights of drag – is a joy to share with audiences and we can’t wait to welcome friends old and new to the theatre in 2027.”

To find out more and to see the full programme of 2027 shows that have been announced, please visit the York Theatre Royal website.
