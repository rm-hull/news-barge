A new family-run Indian restaurant is getting ready to open – and has a delicious opening week offer.

Tanvi’s Indian Cuisine will open its new York restaurant at 26 Swinegate on **Friday 20 November**.

The independent restaurant, which first opened in Wakefield in 2024, will bring authentic North and South Indian cooking and popular British Indian favourites to the city centre.

To celebrate the launch, Tanvi’s will offer **50% off food** from Sunday 22 November to Monday 30 November 2026. The offer will be available during lunch and dinner. To claim it, each guest must order at least one starter and one main course, with naan or rice ordered with curry dishes.

The premises, formerly The Indian Lounge, will reopen under Tanvi’s ownership with a new management team and experienced chefs trained in India.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Team-lunch-3.png)

The menu will include regional curries, tandoori dishes, biryanis, Indo-Chinese specialities and lunchtime thalis, alongside popular British Indian classics.

Popular Wakefield dishes coming to York include Tanvi’s Mixed Sizzler, Dragon Chicken and Hyderabad Lamb Curry, alongside vegetarian and non-vegetarian choices.

Kiran, founder of Tanvi’s Indian Cuisine, said: “York has already made us feel incredibly welcome. Our opening offer is an invitation to meet the team and experience the food and hospitality that our Wakefield customers know us for.”

Tanvi’s name comes from the owners’ daughters, Tanya and Anvi.
