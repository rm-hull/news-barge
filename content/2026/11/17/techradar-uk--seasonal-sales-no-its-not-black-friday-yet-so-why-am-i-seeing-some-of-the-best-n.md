I've had about four friends (and a few relatives) message me for the past two Fridays — and I'm sure it'll happen again this Friday — asking whether or not Black Friday is this weekend, because they've seen a lot of ads and a few deals.

The answer is officially no: according to our calendars and yours too, Black Friday falls on November 27. And Cyber Monday follows on November 30. This is the date when, historically, products got *super* cheap to clear retail store shelves of older items before the influx of Christmas stock (although it has kind of gone the way of fighting in Toys 'R' Us for a Happy Holidays Barbie or Teddy Ruxpin, because these days we have The Internet).

In the US, people know these dates because Thanksgiving — on Thursday, November 26th this year — is always the day before Black Friday, so it's a big week before we get into any deals extravaganzas.

My point is, there are so many discounts live now in the UK, ahead of Black Friday, I want to tell you about them. It seems retailers here are going rogue — or at least going earlier and earlier with their Black Friday-branded discounts, to beat the competition.

Will prices drop yet further as we approach the *real* Black Friday? I honestly don't think so, not when the deals are this good right now.

Top of the deals pile for me would be the first item in my little list, the CMF Headphone Pro for just £49 (was £79) and I loved them at their full RRP. For that money, someone's sub-£50 gift just got sorted. I gave them a glowing 4.5-star review, and at this price they're a steal.

But it's not just headphones! My daily buds, the Technics EAH-AZ100, have also dipped to a new low price of just £179 (was £259) in my pick of the Black Friday deals. Think stunning premium build, excellent audio, triple-device multipoint, great ANC and strong stamina — scroll down to earbuds deals (there's a button to click on the left of this text) if they sound like a bit of you.

*Didn't come here for earbuds or headphones? That's OK. See TechRadar's pick of the* * best Black Friday deals**.*
