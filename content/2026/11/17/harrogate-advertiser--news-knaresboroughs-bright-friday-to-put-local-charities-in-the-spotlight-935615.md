Local charities have been given exclusive control of outdoor catering for Knaresborough’s Bright Friday celebrations after town councillors opted against bringing in outside commercial vendors.

Knaresborough Town Council confirmed that only two community-run refreshment stalls, operated by the Knaresborough Lions and Inspire Youth, will trade in the Market Place during the festive event on Friday, November 27.

The decision is aimed at keeping charity funds within the town while protecting trade for the town’s permanent cafes, pubs, and restaurants.

On the night, the Lions will run its traditional mulled wine wagon, while Inspire Youth will sell sweets, popcorn, and candyfloss to support its work with local young people.

A town council spokesperson said: “Following feedback from local businesses, we have decided to trial a new approach to refreshments.

“Rather than inviting external catering vendors, we will be welcoming just two Knaresborough-based charity refreshment vans, offering festive favourites.

“The aim is to encourage all residents and visitors who come to Bright Friday to explore and support Knaresborough's wonderful cafés, pubs and restaurants during the evening, while also helping to raise valuable funds for local charitable causes.”

The charity stalls will sit at the heart of a packed evening marking the official start of the town’s Christmas season.

Festivities begin with a family lantern-making workshop at Gracious Street Methodist Church at 4.30pm, before stage entertainment kicks off in the Market Place at 5.15pm.

Performances will include the Knaresborough Silver Band, Knaresborough Choral Society, Harrogate Spa Town Ukes Band, and a light dance by Orb Community Arts.

Led by the Town Crier, the annual Lantern Parade will leave the Castle Grounds War Memorial at 6pm, arriving in the Market Place for the official Christmas lights switch-on at 6.30pm.

Visitors can also expect carols, an appearance by Father Christmas on his sleigh, and late-night shopping across independent businesses.

Castlegate, the Market Place, and Silver Street will be closed to traffic from 12pm until 10pm.

This year’s event is supported by David Skaith, Mayor of York and North Yorkshire, and funded by the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority through the Vibrant and Sustainable High Streets Fund.
