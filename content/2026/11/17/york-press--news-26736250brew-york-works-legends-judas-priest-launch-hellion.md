## Rock legends inspire new beer being brewed in York

Rock Legends Judas Priest performing in Scarborough in 2025 *(Image: Dave Lawrence)*Brew York, have collaborated with British heavy metal pioneers, Judas Priest, to launch a new beer -*‘Hellion’*.

The band, who formed in Birmingham in 1969, have sold over 50 million albums and are often ranked as one of the greatest heavy metal bands of all time. Their 19th album, *Invincible Shield*, was released in 2024 to critical acclaim.

Since it was founded in 2016, Brew York has released more than 500 beers. As well as supplying beer to its native Yorkshire, the brewery also exports to many European countries.

Lee Grabham, co-founder and Production Director explains: "Music, especially heavy metal, is a huge part of my life – so to brew a beer to carry the name of the iconic Judas Priest is an absolute honour for me as a fan and as a co-founder of Brew York.

"It’s not only introducing the Brew York name to a whole new audience but is also creating great pride amongst many of us in the Brewery. It’s beers like this which remind me how great this industry is.”

The brewery, whose previous beers’ artwork have paid homage to legends of screen, stage, music, and myth - recognised the importance of creating something visually authentic to honour and respect the band and their legions of fans.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21727625.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new Hellion beer from Brew York *(Image: Pic supplied)*

This task fell to a former musician, roadie, and lifelong metalhead, Ant Piercey – one of the Brew York in-house marketing team.

Ant said: “Priest are absolutely legends and their album artwork is as instantly recognisable as it is iconic to the heavy metal audience. Only someone who knew the band’s music and influence could work on a project like this, and luckily for me, my name was the first to come up to deliver the goods. It was an absolute pleasure to work on this project and a real labour of love to put everything into paying back the decades of joy the band have given me.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21727631.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The new Hellion beer from Brew York *(Image: Brew York)*Hellion is named after the opening track of Priest’s 1982 album,* Screaming for Vengeance,* as well as the name of the ‘eagle’ which adorns the LP cover - and now represents the beer: a metallic, dive-bombing raptor on top of a blood-red sun which combines Art-Deco and retrofuturistic geometric patterns. Development of the visual, originally conceived by the band and collaborator Doug Johnson, includes metallic effect lightning behind the Hellion giving the can a dynamic visual appeal.

The beer is a pale ale using Cascade, Mosaic and Citra hops. Available in can, on keg and on cask, it has been brewed at a sessionable 4.3% abv – enough alcohol to give depth of flavour but without being over the top “to allow you keep rockin’”, according to Brew York’s Grabham.

The beer officially launches on TuesdayDecember 1 and will be available for customers to pre-order from the Brew York website (www.brewyork.co.uk) from 10:00am today.  For any trade customers looking to stock Hellion in their bar email [sales@brewyork.co.uk](mailto:sales@brewyork.co.uk) or call 01904 848448.
