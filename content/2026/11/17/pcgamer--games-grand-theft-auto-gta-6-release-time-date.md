### What's the "New Zealand trick"?

Okay, this is decades old console lore stuff. As explained above, a rolling launch that happens at midnight local time means that people in New Zealand will unlock GTA 6 first. For big launches of years past, some folks have changed the region setting on their console to spoof being in a different time zone. That means they can unlock the ability to start playing the game before midnight in the time zone where they're actually located, get it?

Is the New Zealand trick going to work for GTA 6? I'm not sure, I only know that people will definitely try it. Some launches of years past have put measures in place to block the trick. Here's Sony's guidance on changing your PlayStation account region versus Microsoft's explainer on updating your Xbox account country, which you shouldn't do unless you're actually moving to New Zealand this month, of course. Don't tell Rockstar you heard anything from me.

All the cloak and dagger stuff is sort of thrilling, maybe, but I'd take a global launch as is standard on PC any day.
