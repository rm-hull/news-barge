# Watch: Predatory Met detective's behaviour became increasingly extreme

BBC Panorama can reveal the Metropolitan Police missed opportunities to stop predatory detective, Warren Arter, from sexually exploiting vulnerable victims of crime, whose cases he investigated.

Warren Arter used his position to target the very women it was his job to protect, including his future wife, Rebekah, whose sexual abuse he filmed and distributed before she died in mysterious circumstances in Barbados two years ago.

A former detective inspector who began an investigation into Arter in 2016 claims senior bosses tried to interfere with his inquiry despite him finding multiple potential victims.

Arter's abuse spanned almost 20 years until his death on remand in HMP Wandsworth in 2024. The Met has not reviewed all the cases he was involved with.

The Met told the BBC it was deeply sorry - and encouraged anyone who believes they were abused by him to come forward. A spokesman said that intelligence reports it received were acted upon, but it acknowledges Arter could have been arrested sooner.

The Met also said it is working to examine his digital devices to try to identify if there are further victim-survivors or evidence of criminality.
