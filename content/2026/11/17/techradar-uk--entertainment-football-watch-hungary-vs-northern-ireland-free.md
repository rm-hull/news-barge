Watch Hungary vs Northern Ireland in the Nations League, with promotion to League A up for grabs.

Hungary would have been expectant of a return to the top tier of the Nations League when they were drawn into this group, but after a slow start, it all comes down to this final game against Northern Ireland. Three wins in a row against Ukraine and Georgia mean that Michael O'Neill's men are all that stand between them and their goal. Lose or draw and it will be the play-offs; win and they will be facing the cream of Europe's crop next time around.

For Northern Ireland, a similar chance is at hand, but their sturdiness at the back means that only a point is required in Budapest. O'Neill's men are still yet to concede a goal and, while they have only scored five, a clean sheet today will see them promoted to League A. The Northern Irish will be without O'Neill on the touchline, though, after racking up bookings, and it's a similar story for Luton's Jamie Donley, who misses out after picking up a second yellow card of the tournament on Saturday.
