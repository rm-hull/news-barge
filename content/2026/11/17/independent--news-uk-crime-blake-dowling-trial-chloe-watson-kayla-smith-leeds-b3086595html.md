The boyfriend of a 16-year-old girl who was murdered by a love rival was “laughing” as she was stabbed to death, and “seemed to find it funny”, a Jury has been told.

Chloe Watson was taken by her boyfriend, 18-year-old Blake Dowling, to the home of 18-year-old Kayla Smith for a pre-planned fight in the early hours of 28 March.

Leeds Crown Court has heard that Smith walked into the street holding a knife and stabbed Chloe five times in the neck and back.

Jurors have been told that Dowling had been in “on-off” relationships with both girls, and they would regularly argue over him, although they had never met in person before the fatal attack.

Dowling’s mother said Smith was “jealous and obsessed” over her son’s relationship with Chloe, who was also known as Chloe Dransfield.

Smith has pleaded guilty to murder.

Dowling and Archie Rycroft, 20, a friend of Smith’s who followed her to the fight and is accused of recording it on a phone, both deny murder and manslaughter.

![Leeds Crown Court has heard that Smith walked into the street holding a knife and stabbed Chloe five times in the neck and back](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/12/12/7e796025f0bc3a98a875e53cffb4a3cbY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk0NTcxNDU3-2.12240644.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

On Tuesday the trial heard that when Rycroft was arrested and interviewed by police, he gave officers a prepared statement in which he said he had been with Smith in the summer house of her grandfather’s garden, where she lived, on the night of Chloe’s death.

The statement, read to jurors as part of a list of agreed facts, said: “(Smith) went outside and she mentioned something about a fight.

“I followed her out as I was being nosy.

“I saw Kayla raise her arm up above her head and brought it down.”

Rycroft went on to say: “The victim’s boyfriend was laughing and seemed to find the incident funny so I didn’t think anything of it.”

Rycroft’s statement said he did not see anything in Smith’s hand and “was not aware anyone had been injured, let alone died”.

Prosecutors said that on the night of her death, Chloe was with Dowling at his mother’s house.

When Smith found out she was there, the two girls argued over the phone and agreed to fight.

Jurors heard that in the hours leading up to the fight, Smith repeatedly threatened to stab Chloe.

Dowling booked a taxi to take Chloe to Smith’s home in the Cross Gates area of Leeds.

CCTV and Ring doorbell footage shown in court showed Smith emerging from the summer house holding a knife.

She could be heard shouting: “Come on big girl”, and was then seen running back to the summer house with Rycroft after the stabbing.

CCTV footage taken at 5.12am, about one minute after the stabbing, shows Smith and Rycroft with Smith’s grandfather Geoff Smith outside the summer house.

Mr Smith is heard saying “I’m ringing the police,” and Smith replies: “You can’t ring the police, she’s dead.”

The trial continues.
