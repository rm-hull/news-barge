Following animals on the railway between Peterborough and Grantham all lines have reopened. As service recovers, trains may still be delayed by up to 30 minutes.

Disruption is expected until 13:30.

This affects LNER at York and Northallerton, Grand Central at Thirsk and Hull Trains at Selby.

You can check your journey using the National Rail real-time Journey Planner.
