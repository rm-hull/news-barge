Charities are being urged to apply for the chance to receive up to £10,000 in grant funding.

Harrogate Brigantes Rotary is encouraging groups in the area to apply for a share of its £50,000 Legacy Fund.

The funding was created following a generous bequest from local resident and friend of Harrogate Brigantes Rotary, Dennis Smith.

Guy Wilson, president of Harrogate Brigantes Rotary, said: "We have been delighted with the response we have had since opening the second round.

"We want to make sure that as many organisations as possible apply for the funds, which could help them make a real and lasting difference in the community."

This is the second round of funding from the scheme, with £50,000 already awarded to 14 organisations in 2024.

The fund focuses on capital projects where a grant would have a significant and lasting impact, though applications for ongoing running costs will also be considered if they support sustainable development.

Applications opened in October and nearly 20 entries have already been received.

Charities and community organisations based in Harrogate are eligible to apply, with the deadline for submissions set for November 30, 2026.

More information and application details are available at www.rotaryharrogatebrigantes.org.uk/project/legacy.
