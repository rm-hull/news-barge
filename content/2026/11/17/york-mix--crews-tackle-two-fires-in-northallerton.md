There were two serious fires in Northallerton late last night and early this morning (Tuesday).

Both of the town’s crews were called to a fire at a house on the **High Street** at**11.44pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The occupant was out on arrival after following advice from fire control to evacuate via the back door as the fire was located in the front porch and hallway area.

“No injuries were reported at this incident and fire crews extinguished the fire wearing breathing apparatus and using a hose reel jet.”

Crews were then called to a large building fire in Northallerton at **2.44am**.

The building was destroyed, with two attached buildings smoke logged.

The spokesperson said: “Crews used four breathing apparatus to extinguish the fire once the electricity board attended to isolate the electrics.

“The incident was left with police remaining on scene and a fire investigation will be carried out during daylight hours to determine the cause.”
