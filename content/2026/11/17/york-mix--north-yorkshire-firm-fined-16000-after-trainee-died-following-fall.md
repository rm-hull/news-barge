A North Yorkshire scaffolding firm has been fined £16,000 following the death of a trainee who fell five metres on a building site.

Michael Swales was working for Pickering-based Ask Scaffolding Ltd on 5 July 2023 when the tragedy happened at a new residential housing development near Ripon.

He and a colleague were putting up scaffolding at the front of a two-storey house under construction.

Mr Swales stood on an unsecured scaffolding board and fell five metres to the ground.

Emergency services rushed to the scene, but Mr Swales sadly died from his injuries.

An investigation by the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) uncovered major safety failings by the company, which is based at Thornton Road Industrial Estate in Pickering.

Inspectors found that the work on the day of the accident was unsafe and broke industry guidance:

* No safe zones: The firm failed to set up a ‘Scaffolder’s Safe Zone’ to prevent falls.
* No harnesses: Neither Mr Swales nor his colleague was wearing a harness or lanyard while working at height.
* Ongoing issues: A post-accident check found missing guardrails on previous work, and witnesses said staff regularly worked without following safety rules.

At Leeds Magistrates’ Court on 4 November 2026, Ask Scaffolding Ltd pleaded guilty to breaching the Work at Height Regulations 2005.

Alongside the £16,000 fine, the company was ordered to pay £8,000 in costs and a £2,000 victim surcharge.

HSE Inspector David Beaton said: “This tragic accident underlines the importance of those in control of scaffolding work making sure that it is being done safely and in accordance with industry guidance, and the potential consequences where this is not done.

“Our thoughts remain with Michael’s family and friends.”
