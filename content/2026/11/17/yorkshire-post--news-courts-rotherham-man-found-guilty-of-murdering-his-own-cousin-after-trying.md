# Rotherham man found guilty of murdering his own cousin after trying to cover up killing

## A Rotherham man has been found guilty of murdering his own cousin after attempting to cover up his involvement in the killing.

Shortly after 6am on Sunday (Jun 1), 2025, emergency services were called to Waddington Way in Parkgate, Rotherham, after Wayne White reported that his cousin, 54-year-old Garry Claydon, was injured and unresponsive.

The pair had been camping together before the incident.

Despite the best efforts of ambulance crews, Garry was pronounced dead at the scene.

Paramedics alerted police due to the suspicious nature of his injuries.

Officers spoke to White, who claimed Garry had been assaulted the previous night.

![Wayne White](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/17/17/59/waynewhite.jpg?trim=16,0,81,0&width=640&quality=65)


However, police noticed that White had minor injuries to his hands and face, and he was arrested that morning.

White was initially released pending further investigation but was rearrested on suspicion of murder on May 13, 2026, after further evidence came to light.

The 54-year-old, of Shakespeare Road, Rotherham, was subsequently charged with murder.

He pleaded not guilty at Sheffield Crown Court on May 18 and was remanded into custody ahead of his trial.

Following a seven-day trial at Sheffield Crown Court, White was found guilty of murder on Tuesday (Nov 17).

He is due to be sentenced on Wednesday (Nov 18).

Detective Chief Inspector Simon Cartwright, who led the investigation, said: “White’s cowardly attempts to cover up the fact that he killed his own cousin are almost beyond belief.

“He called the emergency services after he had left Garry with fatal injuries, and denied his own involvement.

“I am glad the jury were not convinced by his denials, and that he now faces a life sentence.”
