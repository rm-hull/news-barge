A teenager suffered facial injuries after being tackled off his bike and forced into a shop window in North Yorkshire.

Police have released a CCTV image of a man they want to speak to following an assault on a teenager in Selby over the summer.

The incident happened on Finkle Street between 8.50pm and 9.15pm on Thursday, August 6, police said.

A teenager was tackled off his bike and into a shop window, suffering facial injuries, the force added.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We appreciate the image is not of the clearest quality but hope someone may still recognise the man, as he may have information that could assist our enquiries."

Officers are appealing for witnesses, anyone with dashcam, doorbell, or CCTV footage from the area at the time of the incident, or with further info to get in touch.

Anyone who recognises the man in the CCTV image or who witnessed the incident should email Leah Thomas at [Thomas.Leah1@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Thomas.Leah1@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101.
