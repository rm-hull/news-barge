## Major York employer continues to 'deliver strong, profitable growth'

Aviva CEO Amanda Blanc *(Image: Pic supplied)*

A major York employer has reported a 19 per cent rise in general insurance premiums amid " strong, profitable growth."

For the first nine months of 2026, Aviva said in a trading update it was seeing "continued momentum" across the Group.

Amanda Blanc, group chief executive officer of Aviva, said: "Aviva is in great shape.

"Our performance has been excellent in the first nine months of 2026, demonstrating once again the benefits of scale across our diversified business.

"We continue to deliver strong, profitable growth, particularly in the capital-light areas of general insurance and wealth.

"We grew general insurance premiums by 19 per cent and, at the same time, improved profitability.

"In the UK, premiums are up 28 per cent, boosted by our home insurance partnership with Nationwide and the great progress we are making with Direct Line."

Group undiscounted combined operating ratio (COR) improved by 0.6 percentage points to 93.8 per cent, reflecting strong rate adequacy and favourable prior-year development.

Wealth net flows rose by 31 per cent to £10.9 billion, with assets under management growing by 19 per cent to £267 billion.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21018200.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Aviva's York office *(Image: Pic supplied)*

Ms Blanc said: "In wealth, we are the number one UK player and have grown assets under management by 19 per cent to £267 billion.

"Our strong momentum continues with flows up 31 per cent to £10.9 billion and we now have over six million wealth customers."

Health sales were down 31 per cent to £81 million, while protection sales fell by two per cent to £260 million.

Retirement sales reached £3.5 billion, including a seven per cent increase in individual annuities and an 11 per cent rise in equity release sales.

Aviva Investors reported total net flows of £1.5 billion, with around 65 per cent of workplace net flows allocated to Aviva Investors' funds.

The company ended October 2026 with centre liquidity of £1.3 billion, down from £1.5 billion in July.

Aviva expects its Solvency II cover ratio to reach the high-180s by the end of the year, including more than seven percentage points from remaining Direct Line capital synergies.

Ms Blanc said: "We are confident about the remainder of 2026 and expect earnings per share to grow broadly in line with our target of 11 per cent this year.

"Aviva’s continued strong trading, our strengths in artificial intelligence, and our leading market position, reinforce our confidence that we will meet our three-year financial targets and grow earnings over the longer term."

The insurer says it remains on track to meet its three-year group targets, including an operating EPS CAGR of 11 per cent between 2025 and 2028, and cash remittances of more than £7 billion over 2026 to 2028.
