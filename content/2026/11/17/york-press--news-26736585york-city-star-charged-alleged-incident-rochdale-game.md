## York City footballer charged over alleged incident

Hiram Boateng in action for York City *(Image: Richard Parkes)*

A York City men’s footballer has been charged over an alleged incident which took place at the dramatic finale of the promotion winning 2025-26 league season.

Hiram Boateng, 30, was charged with the use of threatening, abusive or insulting words or behaviour with intent to cause fear of or provoke unlawful violence at Manchester Magistrates’ Court on Monday (November 16).

The charge is connected to an alleged incident which took place following an extraordinary conclusion to the National League season on Saturday, April 25 in a game against Rochdale AFC at the Crown Oil Arena.




A 1-1 draw was enough for the Minstermen to secure the title of champions thanks to a Josh Stones equaliser in the 13th minute of time added on at the end of the game.

The goal prompted a second pitch invasion after fans had entered the playing surface after Rochdale went ahead in the 95th minute of a nail-biting game.

It was after this second incursion by supporters that an altercation is alleged to have taken place.

As reported in The Press at the time, Greater Manchester Police said it was aware of videos circulating on social media of the reported incident and it had contacted both clubs and the National League authorities.

On April 27, York City issued a statement on X in which it said it would be “supporting Hiram through all proceedings” and that it was committed to assisting with any review, and we will support all our players and staff in contributing to this.”

A York City spokesperson said: “We are aware of developments regarding the incident involving Hiram Boateng during the game against Rochdale in April.

“We are continuing to work with him and all the appropriate authorities to address this and to understand the circumstances that led to this.

“Hiram maintains the full support of everyone at York City and is a much-valued member of the squad, both on and off the pitch.”

![York City largely won the midfield battle in a 1-0 victory over Rochdale.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21719975.jpg?type=mds-article-620)York City largely won the midfield battle in a 1-0 victory over Rochdale at the weekend *(Image: Tom Poole)*

The midfielder was part of The Minstermen's first away win of the 2026-27 EFL League Two season, which came on Saturday (November 14) at the Crown Oil Arena in a 1-0 against The Dale thanks to forward Ollie Pearce's goal in the 56th minute.

York City lie fourth in the league table which is headed by Salford City.

Manchester Magistrates’ Court said Hiram Boateng was remanded on conditional bail until a hearing to be heard there at 10am on June 23 next year.
