Fiona, the hippo who became famous worldwide after being born prematurely at Cincinnati Zoo, is receiving veterinary care after swallowing a knitted hat that found its way into her enclosure.

The zoo said in a statement Monday that staff and veterinarians are keeping watch over her while they try to help her pass the item.

“Her care team is cautiously optimistic that she will pass it and return to her usual self,” the zoo said.

According to zoo spokesperson Michelle Curley, the cap found its way into Fiona's habitat after being tossed into the air by a visitor and caught by a ceiling fan, which then catapulted it through a small opening above the area's viewing windows.

The Zoo added: “Fiona will remain off habitat until this is resolved. We will share updates as we have more information.

“Please help us keep our animals safe by securing your belongings and taking extra care near habitats. Ingesting foreign objects can cause serious health problems for animals. If something falls into a habitat, please notify a Zoo staff member immediately.

“Thank you for your concern for Fiona and for keeping her dedicated care team in your thoughts.”

![Fiona the Hippo](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/22/Fiona_the_Hippo_97442.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Fiona's story began in January 2017, when she was born six weeks premature. Her struggle to survive drew attention around the world.

At birth, she weighed just 29 pounds (13.2 kilograms), which was 25 pounds (11.3 kilograms) below the lowest birth weight ever recorded for her species.

She was unable to stand on her own at first.

Her progress was followed online by fans, and her popularity grew to the point that books were written about her.

She also inspired a beer line, an ice cream flavour and all kinds of clothing.

One couple even became engaged in front of her exhibit, with Fiona appearing in the photo from underwater behind them.
