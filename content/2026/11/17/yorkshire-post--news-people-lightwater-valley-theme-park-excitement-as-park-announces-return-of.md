# Lightwater Valley Theme Park: Excitement as park announces return of popular rollercoaster

## Theme park enthusiasts have been thrilled by the announcement of the return of a popular rollercoaster at Lightwater Valley.

Award-winning Spinning Racer rollercoaster is set to return to the park for the 2027 season, the park said.

More announcements are expected soon, the park said.

A post by the theme park said: “SPINNING RACER IS BACK FOR 2027!

“It's official! Our award-winning Spinning Racer rollercoaster will be returning to Lightwater Valley for the 2027 season!

“Spinning Racer marked the beginning of a new era for Lightwater Valley in 2026, and it's already become a firm favourite.

“This is just the beginning…

“Keep your eyes peeled for more exciting updates as we get ready for another year of new rides and adventures!”

Theme Park Insanity provided on-ride video footage to the Yorkshire Post last year.

Of the return, the enthusiast said: “We can't wait to ger back on this and more next year when the park finally reopens! “
