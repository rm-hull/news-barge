**A 39-year-old man from Harrogate has been charged following a collision in Leeds in which a 22-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman died.**

Leigh Ross, of Prospect Place, Harrogate, has been charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving following the collision on Gelderd Road, Leeds, on Sunday 15 November 2026.

He has also been charged with:

* Two counts of causing death by driving while disqualified.
* Two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed or uninsured.
* Failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required to do so by a constable.
* Aggravated vehicle taking.

The two people who died were a 22-year-old man and a 23-year-old woman, both from York.

Ross was remanded in custody and was due to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court on Monday 16 November.

## Three teenagers released on bail

Two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl, who were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving in connection with the incident, have been released on bail pending further enquiries.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police’s Major Collision Enquiry Team are continuing to appeal for witnesses to any part of the incident.

Specialist family liaison officers are supporting the families of the two people who died. The families have asked for their privacy to be respected during what remains a very difficult time.

Anyone with information about the incident, particularly anyone with relevant dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, is asked to contact West Yorkshire Police via 101 or the Live Chat facility on its website, quoting reference 13260649342.
