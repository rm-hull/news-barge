## Celebrity Traitors favourite announces huge North Yorkshire show

Crowds at Scarborough Open Air Theatre. Photo Cuffe & Taylor *(Image: Photo Cuffe & Taylor)*

A familiar face from The Celebrity Traitors is heading to North Yorkshire.

James Blunt, the multi-platinum-selling artist who charmed viewers with his wit on the hit TV show, will headline Scarborough Open Air Theatre on Sunday, June 20 as part of his Airplane Mode European tour.

The show follows a recent career milestone, with James earning his first UK number one album in 19 years with Airplane Mode.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21726783.jpg?type=mds-article-620)James Blunt is to play at Scarborough Open Air Theatre in June 2027 as part of his European tour. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

Julian Murray, of promoters Cuffe and Taylor, venue programmers at Scarborough Open Air Theatre, said: "James Blunt is one of those artists who seems to connect with audiences everywhere he goes.

"Whether it’s the big hits, new music or his trademark humour, he always puts on a great show, and we’re delighted to welcome him to Scarborough next year."

**Recommended reads:**

He will be joined on the night by special guests Nathan Evans & The Saint PHNX Band.

Tickets for the Scarborough date go on sale at 10am Friday via ticketmaster.co.uk.

With a career spanning more than 20 years, James Blunt is known for hits including You're Beautiful, Goodbye My Lover, and Wisemen from his multi-million-selling debut album Back To Bedlam.

He has earned two BRIT Awards, two Ivor Novello Awards, and five Grammy nominations.

His latest album, Airplane Mode, marks his third UK chart-topper and is described as heavily influenced by 90s Britpop.

The album features tracks including Circles, Oh Daisy, Weight Of It All and Modern Love.

Fans can expect a setlist filled with classic hits and new material.

Supporting act Nathan Evans & The Saint PHNX Band have found success with their collaboration Heather On The Hill, which has surpassed 100 million streams globally.

![ Nathan Evans & The Saint PHNX Band will be joining James Blunt at Scarborough Open Air Theatre in June 2027.](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21726784.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Nathan Evans & The Saint PHNX Band will be joining James Blunt at Scarborough Open Air Theatre in June 2027. *(Image: Cuffe & Taylor)*

The Scottish group released their debut joint album Angels’ Share earlier this year.

They are known for their energetic, folk-pop sound.

Scarborough Open Air Theatre has already announced a packed programme for 2027, with headline shows from Mariah Carey, Courteeners, Shania Twain, Robert Plant with Saving Grace, Kings of Leon, Bananarama, Slash featuring Myles Kennedy & The Conspirators, Alex Warren, Kaiser Chiefs, Alfie Boe, Ronan Keating, Jack Savoretti, James Morrison, and Simply Red.

James Blunt’s appearance on The Celebrity Traitors introduced him to a wider audience, earning praise for his humour and directness on the show.

His live performances are known for mixing sharp humour with his distinctive voice and crowd-pleasing anthems.

Additional information and full event listings can be found at scarboroughopenairtheatre.com.
