A spectacular new evening experience is set to bring one of Nidderdale’s best-known attractions to life this winter, as How Stean Gorge unveils its Glow Wild after-dark light trail.

The immersive event will see the dramatic gorge and surrounding woodland transformed into a world of light, colour and sound, offering visitors the chance to explore the landscape in a completely new way after sunset.

Visitors will be invited to follow a magical trail through the gorge, with glowing trees, illuminated wildlife and hidden surprises waiting around each corner.

The route is designed to take around an hour to complete, creating an atmospheric winter walk suitable for families, friends and those looking for a unique festive experience.

Tony Liddy, Director of How Stean Gorge, said: “We’re really excited to be launching Glow Wild at How Stean Gorge this winter.

“How Stean Gorge is a pretty spectacular place during the day, but we wanted to create something that allows people to experience its natural beauty in a completely different way after dark.

“Glow Wild brings the gorge to life in a way it’s never been seen before.

“We have added some unique installations along the way too, as well as interactive fun fair games, light-up disc golf and a few other surprises.”

As part of the evening, guests can warm up beside a fire pit with hot chocolate and toasted marshmallows, while the on-site cafe will also be serving hot drinks to help visitors keep cosy during their visit.

How Stean Gorge, located near Lofthouse, has become a popular destination for outdoor adventures, but organisers say Glow Wild will offer a different side to the attraction — allowing people to discover the natural surroundings under the glow of thousands of lights.

Tony added: “The winter months can be particularly challenging for tourism and hospitality businesses in rural areas like Nidderdale.

“Rather than seeing the darker evenings as a disadvantage, we wanted to embrace them and create something really special.

“By running the event from December through to the end of March, we hope to encourage more people to visit Nidderdale during what is traditionally a much quieter period, with the wider benefits that brings to local pubs, cafes, accommodation providers and other businesses.

“We’re passionate about making Nidderdale a year-round destination.

“We are really looking forward to welcoming both our local community and visitors from further afield, and hopefully establishing Glow Wild as an annual event that people look forward to.”

The event will run from 4 December 2026 until 28 March 2027, with sessions available on selected evenings throughout the winter season.

Entry slots will be available between 3.30pm and 7pm, with advance booking required.

Organisers are encouraging visitors to wrap up warm and wear suitable footwear, as parts of the trail may become muddy during the winter months.

Glow Wild will offer visitors a different way to experience one of Nidderdale’s most popular attractions, as How Stean Gorge is transformed for an evening of lights, trails and winter fun.
