**UPDATE**: She has now been found.

Shortly after the appeal was shared, a police spokesperson said: "A short while ago, we issued an appeal to find a missing 11-year-old girl from Scarborough.

"We are very pleased to report that she has been found. Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal."

POLICE are searching for a missing child who was last seen this morning.

North Yorkshire Police say its officers are urgently appealing for information and sightings as they search for an 11-year-old who was last seen at around 11am this morning (November 17) in the Woodlands Drive area of Scarborough.

A police spokesperson said: “Extensive enquiries are ongoing to locate her and as part of our enquiries, we are asking for anyone who may have seen her, or who has information about her whereabouts, to contact us immediately.”

They added that she may be wearing black New Balance trainers, a black handbag, maroon jumper, grey pleated skirt and black tights.
