Several friends (and a few relatives) have messaged me over the past two weeks asking whether Black Friday is this weekend and whether they can recommend a turntable for under £400.

As it happens, that's all part of my job. No, it's not officially Black Friday yet — but yes, because the deals over this side of the pond are dropping like flies, I can.

Top of the pile for me is **Sony's PS-LX5BT,** now £291 (was £399) in a never-before-seen 27% off saving. Why has it never seen a discount like this? Because it's a January 2026-release deck. It also has Bluetooth, it's from a trusted name in affordable turntables (the follow-up to the excellent PS-LX310BT, which launched in 2019), and I called it "a winner eight days a week" in an in-depth 4.5-star review.

And I'd pair it with the **Edifier MR5** active speakers, which were £279.99 and are now just £189.99, both on Amazon. The amplification's onboard, so you could stick with wired playback or use Bluetooth if you prefer. Simple. Done.

Want something with built-in speakers so you can plug in and play vinyl straight away? I've got you too, friend. I also love the **Majority Folio.** It comes with speakers in the base, an Audio-Technica cartridge, the option to rip your vinyl to USB and Bluetooth in *and* out (so essentially, the speakers are Bluetooth speakers too, so you could ping music from your phone to those, but you can play your vinyl on standalone Bluetooth speakers too, should you wish). And right now it's just £141 (was £169.95) as long as the ply finish suits — and it's the one we awarded four sweet stars to in a full review, so you can see a lot more of it there if you wish.

I feel like I should clear the whole dates thing up. Officially, Black Friday falls on November 27 and Cyber Monday follows on November 30. This is the date when, historically, products got *crazy* cheap to clear store shelves before the seasonal influx of Christmas stock (although it has kind of gone the way of fighting in Woolworths for a specific BMX bike or Cabbage Patch Kid, because these days we have a thing called online shopping).

In the US, people know these dates because of Thanksgiving, which is on Thursday November 26th this year, the day before Black Friday. Basically, it's a massive week Stateside before we get into any huge deals events, so everyone's diary is meticulously planned out.

Not so here and my point is, there are tons of discounts live now in the UK, ahead of Black Friday. Basically, retailers here are taking matters into their own hands — or at least going earlier with their Black Friday-branded discounts, to beat the online competition.

Will prices drop yet further as we approach Black Friday proper? I don't think so, not when the deals are already this good — it's just as early Black Friday this year.
