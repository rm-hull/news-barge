# Former England rugby league star accused of putting hand up woman’s skirt in cricket club toilet

## A woman has told a jury how she felt “violated” when a former England rugby league player and TV pundit put her hand up her skirt and grabbed her underwear in a cricket club toilet.

The complainant told a court how Danika Priim assaulted her at Horsforth Cricket Club, in Leeds, on July 22 2022.

Giving evidence from behind a screen, the woman said she told Priim: “What the f\*\*\* are you doing?” before the defendant put her hand up her skirt for a second time.

She said the defendant then grinned and walked out of the toilet.

A witness told police she thought Priim said something like “wahey” before she left, jurors heard.

The defendant went on trial on Tuesday at Leeds Crown Court, where she denies a single charge of sexual assault.

The complainant told the jury of seven women and five men how she had used the toilet and then washed her hands and put on some lipstick just before the incident happened, as she was talking to two of her friends.

She said Priim “walked straight up to me and put her hand up my skirt and grabbed hold of my pants and my private parts”.

The woman said: “I said: ‘What the f\*\*\* are you doing?’

“Then she did it again, grinning at me, and walked off.”

Asked by Chloe Hudson, prosecuting, about the defendant’s body language, the complainant told the jury: “She was cold. There was no emotion.

“She was just staring at me.”

The woman said Priim was about the length of an A4 piece of paper away from her when she was staring.

She told the jury she felt “a bit shocked and violated” by what happened.

The complainant said: “My reaction was: ‘What on earth has she done?

“‘Why did she think it was OK to do that?'”

The woman said she and her friends discussed what happened.

She said they were saying: “I can’t believe she’s done that,” and: “Who does she think she is?”

The woman said: “I just froze. I didn’t know what to do.”

Chloe Fairley, defending, said to the woman: “She wasn’t in the toilet at all with you, was she?”

The woman said: “Yes. She was.”

She told the jury: “I’m saying I went to the toilet and that’s where I was sexually assaulted.”

Earlier, Ms Hudson opened the prosecution case, telling jurors the complainant did nothing to “encourage, ask-for or imply that she wanted to be sexually assaulted by this defendant”.

She said the woman reported the incident to police in November 2022.

The prosecutor said the incident was witnessed by two of the complainant’s friends.

One of them told police she thought the defendant said something like “wahey” before leaving the toilets.

The jury heard how the woman and one of her friends discussed the incident on WhatsApp the following day.

In the messages, her friend encouraged her to report it.

Ms Hudson said that Priim was interviewed by police.

“The defendant said this incident never happened at all because she was never in the toilet with this complainant and her two friends,” the prosecutor said.

Priim, 42, of Keighley, West Yorkshire, played for Bradford Bulls, Leeds Rhinos and represented England, including at the 2017 World Cup.

She retired from the game in 2021 and has appeared on TV as a pundit.

The trial, which is expected to last three days, continues.
