## York city centre business closes after power outage

The signs in the window of Caffè Nero *(Image: Newsquest)*

A YORK city centre café has closed its doors after a power outage.

Caffe Nero in Spurriergate has shut today (Tuesday, November 17) after losing power, with staff members turning away customers at the door and redirecting them to nearby locations.

It is not yet clear when the café will reopen.
