# Watch: What's in the Milburn review?

A government-backed review by former minister Alan Milburn has proposed wide ranging measures to help get young people into work.

Almost one million people aged 16 to 24 are currently not in education, employment or training (known as Neets).

The review suggests changes to tax, welfare and education to help reduce that number over the next decade.

Branwen Jeffreys has been reading the report.
