**Puppies being trained to work as police dogs have been named in memory of two officers killed in a head-on car crash.**

PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough died when a Volkswagen Passat travelling the wrong way on the A66 in South Bank, near Middlesbrough, crashed into their marked police car on 22 August.

In their honour, two five-month-old cocker spaniels that have joined the Dog Support Unit at Cleveland Police have been named Bladesy and Tommy.

"We are certain they will live up to their namesakes in making wonderful colleagues to work alongside," Acting Sgt Chris Lambert said.

"Dogs are an invaluable asset to policing and so when we took on the two pups it was only right that we honour the memory of our late colleagues by naming them PD Bladesy and PD Tommy."

PC Matthew Blades and PC Tom Clough were killed in a crash near Middlesbrough

PC Blades, 37, and PC Clough, 38, had been among officers looking for the Passat after its cloned registration plates triggered a camera.

Other Cleveland Police vehicles had been pursuing the car until shortly before the crash, although the two killed officers were not among them according to the Independent Office for Police Conduct.

The five men in the Passat also died and events linked to the crash prompted a series of arrests as police investigated suspected organised crime in the Teesside area.
