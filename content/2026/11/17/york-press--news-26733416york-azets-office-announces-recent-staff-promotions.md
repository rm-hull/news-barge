*(Image: Azets)*

A slew of promotions has been announced at the York office of accountancy and business advisory firm Azets.

Eleven team members have advanced to new positions, including Chris Woodroffe, who has been promoted to audit partner, and Ian Drakes, who now takes on the role of associate director in private client tax.

The promotions are part of 21 new roles created in York, which also include 10 recently announced graduates and school leavers.

Claire Needham, regional managing director for Yorkshire and the North East, said: "Congratulations to all of our colleagues on their richly deserved promotions.

"They reflect Azets’ continued investment in developing its people and strengthening its leadership across key service lines and offices in Yorkshire."

The promotions span the firm’s offices across Yorkshire, including Leeds, Bradford, and York, and range from senior and assistant manager roles to partner and director positions.

Chris Blackburn, York office managing partner, said: "These promotions, along with recent recruitment of graduates and school leavers, represent further growth for our York office as well as Azets’ continued confidence in its people and the business communities it serves.

"I am looking forward to seeing our newly promoted colleagues settle into their roles in the city."

Other promotions in the York office include Sarah Pugh to senior manager in technical accounting, Adam Sanders and Charlotte Grainger to managers in audit, and Sam Grewer and Sophie Marshall to assistant manager positions in audit and ABAS respectively.

Natalie Penswick has been promoted to assistant manager in tax, while Jack Clasper, Jess Kilner, and Tom Taylor have all advanced to senior roles.

Azets is based at Triune Court on Monks Cross Drive in York.

Latest figures from the Office for National Statistics show there were 2.79 million VAT and/or PAYE businesses in the UK as of March 2026, with 194,000 of those in Yorkshire and the Humber.
