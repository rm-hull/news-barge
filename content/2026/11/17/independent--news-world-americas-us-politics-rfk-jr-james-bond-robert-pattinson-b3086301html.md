Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F Kennedy Jr is being ridiculed after posting a meme of himself as James Bond, brandishing a carrot in place of a gun.

The 72 year-old was reacting to the news that British actor Robert Pattinson has been cast as the next iteration of 007, succeeding Daniel Craig in the role, after an extensive search. Pattinson – who shot to fame in the *Twilight* franchise and more recently starred as Batman – called the role “a dream come true”.

The secretary attempted to capitalize on the excitement over the announcement to highlight his “Make America Healthy Again” agenda, adding the quote “Food, real food” instead of the character’s trademark “Bond, James Bond” catchphrase.

But the post on X was soon met with ridicule, with social media users offering up their own version of the image, with one replacing the carrot with a glass of raw milk.

![Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F Kennedy Jr is posted a meme of himself as James Bond on X, brandishing a carrot in place of a silenced pistol to promote 'real food'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/17/10/48/RFK-Jr-Bond.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=11%2C2%2C218%2C2&auto=webp)

Others edited it to include a brain worm writhing out of the secretary’s ear, an allusion to a bizarre episode from his personal life.

One person captioned the image “Measles, shaken not stirred,” drawing on Bond’s famous cocktail preference to parody the worrying advance of that disease on Kennedy’s watch.

Others made the same point by asking how the administration’s decision to encourage the production of glyphosate chimed with Kennedy’s chemical-free food messaging, a sore subject for some MAHA activists who feel betrayed.

Critics also invoked the secretary’s celebrated father Robert F Kennedy Sr and uncle John F Kennedy, with one posting a GIF of the former weeping in heaven as the latter told him: “Bobby, your son is a disgrace.”

![Robert Pattinson in the first-look image as the new James Bond](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/17/20/HXQR9HKWsAADsY6.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“This shows exactly why Americans voted out MAGA Republicans,” one user wrote in a scathing comment on this month’s devastating midterms results. “The secretary cosplaying as James Bond instead of doing his job says it all.”

Democrats regained the majorities in both the House of Representatives and the Senate at the midterms and have already signaled their intention to have RFK Jr’s “head on a platter” next year.

Maryland Democratic Sen. Angela Alsobrooks was among those who called for the secretary’s removal when the extent of her party’s electoral triumph in the midterms elections was becoming clear.

“The time is now,” she posted on X. “RFK Jr must resign or be fired. End of discussion.”
