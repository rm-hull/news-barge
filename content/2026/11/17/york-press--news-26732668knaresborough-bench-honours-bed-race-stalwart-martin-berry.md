A new riverside bench has been installed in memory of a dedicated community volunteer in Knaresborough.

The bench, placed near Conyngham Hall, honours the late Martin Berry, a long-serving member of the Knaresborough Lions and one of the organisers of the Great Knaresborough Bed Race.

Lisa Swiers, who obtained the bench from North Yorkshire Council, said: "We are delighted to remember Lion Martin in this way and it is a perfect donation for a man who was such a stalwart both of the Lions and of Bed Race itself."

Mr Berry joined the Lions in 2010 and remained a member until his death in 2019.

He was closely involved with the Bed Race for many years, and even competed in the event during the 1970s and 1980s.

Knaresborough Lions described him as "fully committed" to the Bed Race.

The bench is located by the river, near the Bed Race’s popular Nidd crossing point.
