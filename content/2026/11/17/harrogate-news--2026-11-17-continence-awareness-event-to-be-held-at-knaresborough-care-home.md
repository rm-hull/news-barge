**A community event aimed at raising awareness of continence issues and helping to break down the stigma surrounding them is being held in Knaresborough later this month.**

Thistle Hill Care Centre, on Thistle Hill at Chadwick Park, will host the event on Thursday 26 November at 10am.

Sarah Welburn from TENA and Essity will lead the session, which will cover the resources, advice and practical support available to older adults and their families.

The event is intended to provide an opportunity for people to discuss continence issues in a supportive environment and feel more confident about seeking help when needed.

Local people are invited to attend.

> **Pratheesh Thankachan, General Manager at Thistle Hill Care Centre, said:**
>
> The event will be a fantastic opportunity to bring people together and raise awareness of an issue that affects many individuals and families. Continence can often be a difficult topic to discuss, but through events like this we can help break down barriers, provide valuable information and reassure people that support is available. We are extremely grateful to Sarah and the team from TENA and Essity for sharing their expertise with our home and the wider community.

Thistle Hill Care Centre provides residential, nursing and dementia care for up to 93 residents.

For further information about the event, contact Thistle Hill Care Centre on 01423 869200 or email [thistlehill@barchester.com.](mailto:thistlehill@barchester.com.)
