## York theatre reveals its 2027 pantomime more than a year early

Are You Even Indian will be one of the shows in 2027. *(Image: Deven Ahire)>*

A York theatre has already set the stage for its pantomime in 2027 which will form part of a packed programme of drama, dance and family entertainment.

York Theatre Royal revealed the show as part of its 2027 line-up, confirming a return to the stage for Beauty and the Beast, renewing their collaboration with Evolution Productions and written by Paul Hendy.

Long-time favourite Robin Simpson will once again take on the role of Dame in the production, which will run from December 3, 2027 to January 3, 2028, with an early bird ticket offer available until January 3, 2027.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21724348.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Football Freddie at York Theatre Royal. *(Image: Submitted)*

Paul Crewes, chief executive of York Theatre Royal, said: "With a new year comes another exciting period of collaboration for York Theatre Royal.

"We are building relationships with brilliant partners, artists and collaborators up and down the country, producing fresh approaches to some beloved titles including Arthur Miller’s Death of a Salesman, Andrew Lloyd Webber’s Sunset Boulevard and working with the Emma Rice Company once again on the landmark production Tristan & Yseult.

"The diversity on offer – from breathtaking literary adaptations to opera, comedy, music to fun and fabulous nights of drag – is a joy to share with audiences and we can’t wait to welcome friends old and new to the theatre in 2027."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21724298.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Death of a Salesman at York Theatre Royal. *(Image: Submitted)*

The theatre’s 2027 programme features a mix of newly produced and touring shows, including a co-production of Arthur Miller’s Death of a Salesman with Glasgow Citizens Theatre, running from March 3 to 27.

Tristan & Yseult, directed and adapted by Emma Rice, will be staged from April 6 to 17 in collaboration with the Emma Rice Company, Alexandra Palace Theatre, and Nottingham Playhouse.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21724159.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sunset Boulevard Landscape *(Image: Submitted)*

York Theatre Royal will also co-produce Andrew Lloyd Webber’s Sunset Boulevard for the first time, in partnership with Octagon Theatre Bolton and Royal & Derngate.

Directed by Lotte Wakeham, that musical will run from June 9 to 26.

Theatre Royal members can access five-day priority booking from 1pm on November 18.

General booking opens at 1pm on November 23.

Theatre fans can expect a wide range of performances, including dance, opera, comedy, drama, and cabaret.

Highlights include Phoenix Dance Theatre’s Sincerely, The Breakfast Club, Northern Rascals’ Reviving Her, and English Touring Opera’s productions of Orpheus & Eurydice and Tosca.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21724155.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mog The Forgetful Cat. *(Image: Submitted)*

Family audiences can look forward to Mog the Forgetful Cat and Pirates Love Underpants in the Main House, as well as Football Freddie and Doris The Dormouse in the Studio.

The theatre will also welcome back local amateur companies, with York Light Opera staging 9 to 5 The Musical, York Settlement Community Players presenting Blithe Spirit, and York Opera performing Carmen.

Tickets can be booked by calling the box office on 01904 623568 or by visiting yorktheatreroyal.co.uk.
