# Six men in court after thieves allegedly drill through warehouse wall to steal valuable Pokémon cards

## Three Yorkshire men are among six charged in connection with the alleged theft of high-value Pokémon cards after thieves reportedly drilled through a warehouse wall.

A group allegedly drilled through the wall of a business premises in Aurillac Way, Retford, before gaining entry and stealing 26 boxes of the collectable cards, Nottinghamshire Police said.

Liam Duce, 25, and Liam Parker, 29, both of HMP Lancaster Farms in Lancashire, and Daniel Milligan, 27, of Wharfe Street in Otley, West Yorkshire, have been charged with conspiracy to commit burglary between December 25 and 28 last year.

Brandon Coates, 20, of Broom Terrace, Leeds; Matthew Middleton, 44, of Pawson Street, Leeds; and Mohammed Hussan, 32, of Whiteacre Road in Newcastle-under-Lyme, Staffordshire, also face the same charge.

![The cards related to the hugely popular Japanese animation franchise have soared in value over recent years.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjFmODI0ZTljLWM5NzAtNDBmMS05ZmRlLTJhMjIyNDk2Yzc4Zjo4NjdkOTBhMS1lODQwLTQ2NjYtODYxNS02YjkxMjBlOGQwZGI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


All six defendants appeared at Mansfield Magistrates’ Court on Tuesday where they were told to appear at Nottingham Crown Court on December 15 for a plea hearing.

Duce and Parker appeared in court via video-link from HMP Lancaster Farms and were remanded in custody until the next hearing.

Coates, Middleton and Hussan attended court in person where they were granted bail with conditions, including not to contact their co-defendants and not to go to Obsidia Ltd, a trading card business in Retford.

Milligan, who appeared in court virtually, was also granted conditional bail.
