A 19-year-old teen was arrested after beating a disabled homeless man to death — leaving shoe prints on his face in the horrifying attack, cops say.

New Orleans police arrested Antonio Myles in connection to the November 8 murder of 68-year-old David Hyde, a New Orleans Police Department spokesperson confirmed to *The Independent.* Myles allegedly approached the victim just before 1 a.m. and began to argue with him before taking his belongings from a backpack and destroying them, police said.

He then began to brutally beat up the victim, who was unable to defend himself due to health issues, police said.

Authorities found Hyde outside a Walgreens just before 7 a.m., “with severe contusions to his face” as well as shoe prints left on his face, police wrote in court documents obtained by *The Guardian.*

There were also numerous prescription medication bottles left near Hyde. He was pronounced dead at the scene.

![Antonio Myles, 19, was arrested in connection with the fatal beating of David Hyde, a 68-year-old homeless man in New Orleans](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/17/16/52/New-Orleans-Teen-fatally-attacks-homeless-man.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Surveillance video from a nearby store showed Hyde sitting near the pharmacy’s front entrance when his attacker grabbed his backpack, dumped its contents on the ground and beat him up, according to the court documents.

According to the report, most of the fatal beating occurred out of the camera’s view. However, investigators said that the attacker could be seen using Hyde’s shirt to wipe his shoes before fleeing the scene.

Police said they received a call the following day reporting that Myles had been “bragging to unknown subjects that he was responsible for beating a homeless man to death.”

While Myles was interviewed by police, he was released pending further investigation. Days later, police released footage of a man in a parking lot, noting that he was a “suspect wanted in connection with a brutal beating that left a disabled man dead.”

![Myles was charged with second-degree murder and faces life in prison if convicted](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/17/16/00/Antonio-Myles.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

An informant told police the person in the video was Myles. During an interview with investigators, they said that Myles had called hours after Hyde was found dead and told the person “he loved them, and that he killed a homeless man, and continued to kick him after he was already dead.”

“The known subject also advised … that Mr. Myles advised a known subject that he killed the homeless man and took his prescription medication that was inside his backpack,” the police statement said.

In the police statement, investigators noted that they believed the information the subject was giving them was true since “no specific details of the investigation were released” to the public and news media.

“Only a person who was there when the homicide occurred would know that the victim was kicked to his head repeatedly, and that the victim was in possession of prescription medicine inside of a backpack,” the statement added.

Myles was arrested Friday on a count of second-degree murder. He would receive a life sentence in jail if convicted on the charge.
