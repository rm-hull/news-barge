President Donald Trump is reportedly spending millions of dollars for more renovation work on the White House grounds, despite suffering major losses in the midterm elections and polling indicating that his construction projects are unpopular with the public.

Work has already begun on a walkway outside the West Wing that would lead to the south of the White House via the Rose Garden, *The Washington Post* reported.

The administration is also spending $3 million to reset the bricks in Lafayette Square, the public park opposite the White House mansion, according to documents obtained by the outlet. The park has been closed for most of the year.

The latest renovation work follows Trump’s demolition of the historic East Wing to make way for a new ballroom, which could cost as much as $600 million, and his controversial plan to erect a 250-ft triumphal arch near the Arlington National Cemetery.

The president is also seeking to overhaul a public golf course as part of his plans to transform the nation’s capital, and has spent more than $16 million on repairs to the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool.

Trump is pursuing his pet projects even though they are unpopular with Americans. Two-thirds oppose the president dedicating time to remodeling Washington, D.C., according to a recent Reuters-Ipsos poll.

The funds for the brickwork in Lafayette Park will reportedly come from the National Park Service in an effort to make it “safe and beautiful,” officials told the *Post*. There are concerns that the existing brickwork could come loose and be used by protesters.

“Not only is President Donald J. Trump using his expert builder experience to make the White House beautiful now, he’s improving and restoring the grounds to greatness for future presidents to enjoy,” the Interior Department, which oversees the National Park Service, said in a statement to the outlet. “President Trump should be thanked for making our nation’s capital better than how he found it.”

The White House posted a map of D.C. Monday listing the statues, monuments and memorials it claims have been fixed and cleaned by Trump. “Decline was a choice, and @POTUS chose differently,” Interior Secretary Doug Burgum said. “After years of neglect, our monuments have never looked better and Americans once again have a capital they can take PRIDE in.”

![Two-thirds oppose the president dedicating time to remodeling Washington, DC, according to a recent Reuters-Ipsos poll](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/11/08/2303244258..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Trump lashed out at a reporter who questioned why the administration is spending tens of millions on construction projects while also demanding billions from Congress to repair the country’s antiquated air traffic control systems following Friday’s meltdown.

“You’re so pathetic. I have a responsibility to make sure the symbol of our country is in decent shape,” Trump rebuked NBC News correspondent Yamiche Alcindor on Monday. “It takes 10 minutes a day. This doesn’t cost money. This is peanuts. And I pay for it. This doesn’t cost money.”

“You don’t know what a broken column is,” he continued. “I spent very little time to do it and losers like you come out and say, ‘Why are you focused on the White House?’”

The day after Trump and the Republican Party suffered a Democratic wipeout in the midterm elections, he retreated to the construction site of the White House ballroom for a press conference. For the first 20 minutes of his remarks, he failed to mention the losses.

“I love construction,” he said.
