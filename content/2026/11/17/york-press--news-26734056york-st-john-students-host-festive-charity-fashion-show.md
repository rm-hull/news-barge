## York students to stage festive fashion show with pre-loved clothes

York St John University students are taking part in Frozen in Time *(Image: York St John University)*

A festive fashion show focused on upcycled and second-hand style is happening next month.

York St John University students are taking part in Frozen in Time, a charity event raising funds for York-based Community Bees.

The show will take place at the university’s Creative Centre and has been organised by second-year Fashion Marketing students who have curated and styled outfits using pre-loved clothing from Community Bees' store.

Evie Houghton, one of the students involved, said: "The idea for Frozen in Time came from wanting to create timeless pieces that could be frozen in time and last forever.

"Even though clothes might have been put away and almost forgotten, they can be brought back to life.

"It's about giving old clothes a new lease of life."

The event aims to both raise money and promote awareness of Community Bees, a charity supporting people affected by hoarding disorder, social isolation, and mental health challenges.

Ted Saunders, another student on the project, said: "Before starting this project, I didn't really know that much about Community Bees or even hoarding.

"Learning about what Community Bees does has made me realise how much of an issue it can be and how it affects people."

A spokesperson for Community Bees said the event was a welcome opportunity to highlight important issues, saying: "We're absolutely delighted to be working with the Fashion Marketing students at York St John University.

"Hoarding disorder is still widely misunderstood, and many of the people we support experience shame, judgement and isolation.

"Events like this are so important because they give us an opportunity to raise awareness, challenge some of those misconceptions and encourage more understanding and kindness in our community."

Frozen in Time takes place on Thursday, December 3, with doors opening at 5pm and the runway show starting at 6pm.
