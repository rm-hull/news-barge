## Renovated three-bedroom period home in York for sale at £450,000

A three-bedroom period home in Woodlands Grove, York, is on the market for offers over £450,000 *(Image: Ashtons)*

A three-bedroom period home in York is on the market for offers over £450,000.

Ashtons estate agents said the semi-detached house in Woodlands Grove has been completely refurbished following a programme of renovation.

This included a rear extension, full rewire, new gas central heating system, and replacement windows.

The house has an energy performance certificate (EPC) rating of C.

The front entrance hallway leads to a bay-fronted lounge.

At the rear of the property, there is an open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area, with a utility room, ground floor WC, and garage/store.

The kitchen has integrated appliances, an AEG induction hob with built-in extractor, and a double oven, as well as a central island.

On the first floor, there are two double bedrooms, which both have fitted wardrobes, and a third single bedroom, which could also be used as an office or nursery.

There is also a three-piece family bathroom with a shower over the bath.

Outside, there is a front garden and driveway, as well as a substantial south-west-facing rear garden, which is currently being landscaped and will have a large lawn.

The estate agents said the beautifully presented accommodation retains the charm expected from a period home.

Woodlands Grove is just off Stockton Lane, which Ashtons say is a highly sought-after location.

For more information, or to arrange a viewing, call Ashtons estate agents on 01904 918389.
