The ex-boyfriend of an “anti-woke” American influencer racially abused and threatened two sisters with what he claimed was pepper spray outside a London Tube station, a judge has found.

Philipp Ostermann, 38, was convicted of two counts of causing racially aggravated harassment and one count of using threatening or abusive or insulting words or behaviour after a trial at City of London Magistrates’ Court on Tuesday.

The managing director, from Dusseldorf, Germany, lost his job after the row and spent months unemployed before securing a new role paying 150,000 euros a year, the court heard.

District Judge Louisa Cieciora said the sisters’ evidence was “consistent with each other” and “they had no reason to lie”.

She said Ostermann’s words “did in fact cause distress” and she described him as a “tall imposing man” confronted by “two short, unimposing-in-stature women”.

The judge said it could not be proved that the canister contained pepper spray but noted deodorant could also cause harm and that an “aggravated feature” was that young children were present.

The confrontation began near Bond Street Underground station at around 7.30pm on 11 October last year after Ostermann’s then girlfriend, Melissa Rein Lively, 41, stumbled over a pram carrying one of Sabba Javed’s children, the court heard.

![The case was heard at the City of London Magistrates’ Court](https://static.independent.co.uk/2023/12/19/18/5890a3869bc44ac84804211821878a36Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzAzMDkxNDA2-2.58304547.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Prosecutor Rosalind Earis said Ostermann shouted at Sabba and her sister Marium: “You bloody Indians, watch where you’re going, you shouldn’t be here.”

Rein Lively, founder of America First PR, then grabbed Sabba’s hair, prompting her to grab the American’s hair in return, the court heard.

Sabba said: “He got aggressive very quickly. He went from zero to 60 straight away.”

She said Ostermann later pointed a small black canister with a yellow nozzle towards them, shouting: “I’ve got pepper spray, come forward, come now.”

She claimed he pressed the nozzle, releasing a “mist”, although neither sister was struck.

Sabba said: “I thought it would get sprayed in my face and I’ve never had anything like that pointed in my direction.”

Describing the aftermath, she said: “I just felt like I wasn’t welcome in my home and it really frightened me.”

![Rein Lively previously accepted a conditional caution for common assault over the hair-pulling incident and paid £910 compensation to Sabba](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/06/02/13/2245408034..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Marium said: “I was shocked because I was born and brought up in this country all my life and no one’s ever spoken to me like that.”

Ostermann denied making racist remarks and insisted the canister contained Axe deodorant, sold as Lynx in the UK.

He admitted claiming it was pepper spray but said he wanted to “create distance” between himself and the sisters.

He said Rein Lively “extremely overreacted” after stumbling over the pram and chased the sisters, prompting him to intervene.

“I tried to get Melissa out of the situation. I wanted to stop everyone fighting so then I yelled, ‘F\*\*\* off, you idiots’,” he said.

His barrister Orla Daly suggested Sabba had mistaken “idiots” for “Indians”, but she rejected this.

Ostermann said he and Rein Lively had been drinking wine and beer before the confrontation and revealed they split up shortly afterwards because he disapproved of her behaviour.

“I couldn’t agree with this behaviour,” he said. “It isn’t what I tolerate in my life.”

Rein Lively previously accepted a conditional caution for common assault over the hair-pulling incident and paid £910 compensation to Sabba.

Ostermann was spared a community order because he lives abroad but was fined £2,000 for each offence, totalling £6,000.

He was also ordered to pay a £2,000 surcharge, £600 in prosecution costs and compensation of £1,000 to Sabba and £500 to Marium, bringing the total to £10,100.
