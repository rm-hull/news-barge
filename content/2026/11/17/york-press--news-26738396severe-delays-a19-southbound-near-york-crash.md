TRAFFIC was mounting in a York road after reports of a crash earlier this afternoon.

There were ‘severe’ delays on the A19 southbound being reported, just to the south of York, after a road traffic accident in the area.

It was first reported at around 3.25pm today (Tuesday), with unconfirmed reports online stating that it is believed to be a crash involving a van and a skip lorry.

Delays was reported to be ‘severe’ – currently at 18 minutes and increasing.

It has since cleared.
