Harrogate Town beat Altrincham 4-0 to stay second, Harrogate RUFC beat Fylde 39-31 and the Rail won 3-0; Knaresborough lost at Frickley. Results and fixtures.

Harrogate Town beat Altrincham 4-0 at home on Saturday and stay second in the National League. Harrogate RUFC and Harrogate Railway Athletic also won; Knaresborough Town lost at Frickley.

## Harrogate Town

All four goals came in the second half, the club’s match report says. Will McGowan scored after 53 minutes, then Morgan Williams, Michael Craig (his first for the club) and Rohan Vaughan in stoppage time. Altrincham had a player sent off after 13 minutes. The crowd was 2,665, including 372 from Altrincham.

The win followed a 4-1 FA Cup defeat at home to AFC Fylde on 7 November. Simon Weaver’s side have 35 points from 17 games, 10 behind unbeaten Boreham Wood and 3 clear of Southend United and Fylde.

The FA Trophy third round draw sends Town to Darlington, the club says, with the tie due over the weekend of 12 December.

## Harrogate RUFC

Harrogate beat Fylde 39-31 at Rudding Lane in National League 2 North, a week after losing 34-33 at Heath. The promoted side are seventh of 14 with 27 points from 10 games, the club’s league table shows. Next is a trip to leaders Sedgley Park, who have won all 10.

## Harrogate Railway Athletic

The Rail beat LIV 3-0 at Station View in front of 134, the league’s match list shows. Alex Burton scored twice, one a penalty, and Harry Croft added the third. It was a second home win in a row after a 3-2 comeback against Club Thorne Colliery. They are 14th in NCEL Division One with 27 points from 20 games, all eight wins at home.

## Knaresborough Town

Knaresborough lost 3-0 at Frickley Athletic, with Keelan Grist scoring all three, the league’s match list shows. They are seventh in the NCEL Premier Division with 31 points from 19 games. Their home game with Pickering Town on Tuesday 17 November has been postponed.

The cricket season is over. Harrogate Cricket Club are back in the Yorkshire Premier League North in April.
