## Three-bedroom period cottage in York village on sale for £450,000

A three-bedroom period cottage in Heworth Village, York, is on the market for £450,000 *(Image: Hudson Moody)*

A three-bedroom period cottage in a quiet York village is on the market for £450,000.

Pear Tree Cottage, in Heworth Village, is freehold and arranged over three floors, with a reception room, a dining kitchen, one bathroom and an attic bedroom.

At ground-floor level, a reception hall leads to the living room and kitchen.

Exposed ceiling beams and a fireplace are included in the living room.

Quarry-tiled flooring, timber units and a Belfast sink feature in the dining kitchen, which has space for a freestanding cooker and other appliances.

Two double bedrooms and the house bathroom are on the first floor.

One bedroom has fitted wardrobes, while the bathroom has a white suite and a shower over the bath.

Roof lights and a walk-in storage cupboard are provided in the second-floor attic bedroom.

Outside, the cottage has a front garden enclosed by iron railings and a rear garden reached through a shared pathway.

The rear garden is predominantly laid to lawn, with a patio seating area, planted borders and established trees.

A brick-built storage shed has power and a water supply.

The property also has an EPC rating of D and is described as being close to local shops, amenities and healthcare.

Its location provides access to York city centre, the A64 outer ring road and Monks Cross Retail Park.

Viewings are available by appointment with Hudson Moody on 01904 595232.
