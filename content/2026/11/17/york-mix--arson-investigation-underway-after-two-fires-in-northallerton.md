A joint police and fire investigation is underway in Northallerton following two suspected arson incidents.

Witnesses and anyone with CCTV, doorbell, and dashcam footage are urged to come forward.

At approximately 11.44pm on Monday 16 November, firefighters were called to a residential property on the High Street near to All Saints Church following reports of a fire at that address. The occupant was alerted by the fire alarm and was able to get to safety unharmed.

At 2.44am today (Tuesday 17 November) fire crews attended a further fire at the rear of business premises on the High Street.

At this time it is not known if the incidents are connected.

Police patrols have been increased to provide reassurance to residents and the local business community while the investigation continues.

Police in Northallerton say: “We are appealing for witnesses and any CCTV, doorbell, and dashcam footage that might capture the incidents or people involved.

“If you can help in any way, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“If you would prefer to remain anonymous, contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or make an online report.

“Please quote reference number 12260225561 when providing details.”
