Pope Leo XIV's South American tour was marked by a series of memorable moments.

In Peru, the pope travelled by military helicopter and jeep to reach the remote Andean town of Santa Cruz de Succhabamba.

During a visit to Lima, an enthusiastic devotee accidentally struck him in the face with a rosary bracelet thrown from the crowd. The pontiff rubbed his eye but continued greeting people before stopping to bless a baby.

In the Peruvian Amazon city of Pucallpa, Leo called for justice for Indigenous leaders killed while defending their territories. He also condemned deforestation and river pollution, warning against the irresponsible exploitation of natural resources.

During a youth vigil in Buenos Aires, Argentina, where 1.3 million people attended the event, the pope warned young people against lying, betraying or humiliating others to gain approval on social media.
