Police have released an image of a man they would like to speak to after a large pig statue was stolen near Ripon.

The statue was taken from outside a property on Mowbray Terrace in West Tanfield at 5.09pm on Saturday 24th October.

Police believe the man pictured may have information that could help their investigation.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/682accec7ce38.jpg)

*Anyone who recognises him or has information about the theft is asked to email PC Coulthard at [richard.coulthard@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:richard.coulthard@northyorkshire.police.uk), quoting reference 12260210816.*

*Information can also be submitted through the ‘Tell us about’ section of the North Yorkshire Police website by selecting ‘An existing case or report’. Live chat is available on weekdays from 9am to 5pm.*

*Anyone unable to use the online options can call 101. Information can also be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.*
