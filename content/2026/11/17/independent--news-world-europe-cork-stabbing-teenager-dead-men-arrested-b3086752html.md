A murder probe is under way in Cork after a teenager died from wounds suffered in a stabbing attack in the city.

Two males, an adult teenager and a juvenile, have been arrested in connection with the investigation.

Gardaí said the fatal assault took place on the Lower Glanmire Road at around 1.30pm on Tuesday.

The victim, an adult male aged in his late teens, suffered critical wounds in the attack. He was taken to Cork University Hospital (CUH) for urgent medical attention, but subsequently died in the hospital.

The two males arrested are currently detained at Garda stations in Cork under the provisions of Section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act, 1984.

![Gardaí are appealing for witnesses to come forward](https://static.independent.co.uk/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/2018/01/03/16/gardai-ireland.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Gardaí in Cork have commenced a murder investigation into all the circumstances of the incident. The investigation is being coordinated from an incident room at Mayfield Garda Station under the direction of a senior investigating officer.

A family liaison officer has been appointed to the family of the deceased.

A post-mortem will be carried out on the teenager who died on Tuesday at CUH by Assistant State Pathologist Dr Margot Bolster.

Gardaí said the results of the post-mortem will assist in the course of the murder investigation.

Gardaí are appealing for witnesses to come forward, particularly those who were travelling or driving in the Lower Glanmire Road area of Cork on Tuesday 17 November between 1.15pm and 1.45pm.

They have also appealed for anyone who may have video footage including dash-cam to make it available to investigating Gardaí.
