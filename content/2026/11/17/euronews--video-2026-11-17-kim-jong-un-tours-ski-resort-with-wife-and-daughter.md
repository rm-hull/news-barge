North Korean leader Kim Jong Un visited a ski resort under development in Samjiyon, near the Chinese border, on Sunday and Monday.

Photos released by state media on Tuesday show him smiling in sunglasses and smoking a cigarette, with his wife Ri Sol Ju nearby. His daughter Ju Ae was also pictured skiing down a slope.

According to the Korean Central News Agency, Kim toured hotels and ski facilities and praised the construction and surrounding mountain scenery.

The visit came as US President Donald Trump suggested he might meet Kim during his trip to China.

Beijing said it had no information about such a meeting, while analysts saw no indication that the resort tour was linked to possible talks.

Trump and Kim met three times between 2018 and 2019, but no new meeting had been confirmed.
