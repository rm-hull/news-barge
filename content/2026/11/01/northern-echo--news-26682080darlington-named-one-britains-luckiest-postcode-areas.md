Darlington has been named one of Britain's luckiest areas for People's Postcode Lottery wins, ranking sixth in the country for prizes per household.

Research found the DL postcode area recorded 433 Daily Prize wins between January 2019 and September 2026, putting it ahead of 114 other postcode areas across England, Scotland and Wales.

The area recorded 26.29 wins per 10,000 households, well above the UK average of 19.78.

The study, carried out by crypto gaming experts Cryptocasino.se, analysed all 53,972 Daily Prize wins announced during the period and compared them with Census household figures.

Shrewsbury topped the rankings with 30.49 wins per 10,000 households, followed by Lancaster on 29.53 and Galashiels in Scotland on 28.47.

Darlington was the highest-ranked postcode area in the North East of England listed in the top ten.

However, the research suggests the figures do not mean residents in certain areas are more likely to win with an individual ticket.

Jonas Lindén, Crypto Gaming Analyst at Cryptocasino.se, said: "There's no such thing as a lucky postcode. What a table like this picks up is a quirk of the rules: the Daily Prize postcode is chosen at random regardless of how many tickets are playing in it, so an area split into lots of small postcodes gets more entries in the draw, and more wins, for each household. For any single ticket, the chance of winning is the same right across the country."

The research also examined repeat winners dating back to January 2011.

It found 1,630 postcodes had won the Daily Prize at least twice, while 31 had won three times. No postcode had won four times.

The findings were based on People's Postcode Lottery results and Census household counts, with data captured on October 7, 2026.

The full research is available from Cryptocasino.se.
