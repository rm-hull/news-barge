# Leeds Christmas Market 2026: dates, opening hours, locations, traders and travel information

## The market has a lot of fun and festive cheer to offer

As the festive season approaches, the city will be attracting a lot of visitors keen to visit Leeds Christmas Market.

The market has a lot of fun and festive cheer to offer and will be located in City Square, along with more options spilling over into Millenium Square and Victoria Gardens. The market will open at 10.30am on Thursday, November 19 and will close on Christmas Eve.

It will once again feature a variety of quality Christmas cuisine alongside a mix of handcrafted goods with 60 independent and artisan traders. There will be lots of local produce and merchandise from traders based in Leeds and the wider Yorkshire area.

Below, we've put together a guide with opening and closing dates, opening hours, locations, an overview of the traders, road closure information and handy places to park. Scroll below to take a look.

### Opening and Closing Dates

Opening date: Thursday, November 19

Market closing date: Wednesday, December 23 (The main market stalls pack up right before Christmas Day).

Ice Cube and rides closing date: Thursday, December 31 (The ice rink and select family attractions on Millennium Square remain open until New Year's Eve).

### Opening Hours

The market and its various entertainment bars operate across different times throughout the week:

* Trader Village and Street Food Stalls: Sunday to Wednesday: 10:30am – 7:00pm Thursday to Saturday: 10:30am – 8:00pm
* Thor's Highland Lodge and Folié Ski Bar (City Square): Sunday to Tuesday: 11:00am – 9:00pm Wednesday to Saturday: 11:00am – 11:00pm
* Ice Cube Rink and Millennium Square Rides: Monday to Thursday: 12:00pm – 9:30pm Friday: 12:00pm – 10:00pm Saturday: 11:00am – 10:00pm Sunday: 11:00am – 7:00pm

### Locations Guide

All locations are in the city-centre with three distinct areas

**City Square** The main Evergreen Winter Wonderland with traditional wooden chalets, street food, and the heated Alpine lodges.**Millennium Square** The family and entertainment hub, with Ice Cube skating rink, the high-flying Starflyer ride, and a Bavarian-themed market.**Victoria Gardens**

This area will host the 110ft Ferris Wheel of Light and Thor’s Tipi bar.

* Cookridge Street (near Millennium Square) will be closed to traffic to accommodate the Golden Gallopers carousel.
