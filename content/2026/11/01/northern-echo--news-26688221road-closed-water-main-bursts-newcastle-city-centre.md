Water has been sent pouring into the streets of Newcastle after a water main burst.

Emergency services were called to Claremont Road and Queen Victoria Road today (Sunday, November 1) after a large water main burst.

Water has flooded onto Claremont Road, near Exhibition Park and outside the Royal Victoria Infirmary, causing the road to be closed and disruption for drivers as a result.

Pictures taken from the scene show how the street has been taped off by firefighters while crews get to work on fixing the issue.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656828.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services at the scene on Queen Victoria Road, in Newcastle, where a burst water mains has flooded the road outside the Royal Victoria Infirmary *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd northnews.co.uk)*

Northumbrian Water said the damage, which was caused by a third party, has left some homes in nearby areas of Newcastle with low pressure or no water.

It said the problem has been isolated to stop flooding, with hopes water will be restored to properties “as quickly as possible”.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656829.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services at the scene on Queen Victoria Road, in Newcastle, where a burst water mains has flooded the road outside the Royal Victoria Infirmary *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd northnews.co.uk)*

A spokesperson said: “Our teams are currently on site at Claremont Road, near Exhibition Park, following third party damage to a large water main.

“After being made aware of the damage, our teams reacted quickly and currently working on isolating the main in order to stop any further flooding to the area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656828.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Emergency services at the scene on Queen Victoria Road, in Newcastle, where a burst water mains has flooded the road outside the Royal Victoria Infirmary *(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd northnews.co.uk)*

“Some properties in the areas of Jesmond, Elswick and central Newcastle may be experiencing low pressure or no water, however we are aiming to restore this as quickly as possible.

“We can confirm that there has been no impact to supplies at the Royal Victoria Infirmary.

"Traffic management is in place, and due to the location of the burst, traffic in the area is slow, so please allow extra time for travel or plan alternative routes.

“We will continue to provide updates, and thank you for your patience.”
