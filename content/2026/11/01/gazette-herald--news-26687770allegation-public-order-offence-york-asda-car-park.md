Police are appealing for information following an allegation of a "public order offence" in York.




In a statement issued last night (Saturday, October 31), North Yorkshire Police said the alleged offence happened in the car park of the Asda supermarket in Layerthorpe, on Wednesday, October 21, at around 4pm.

The statement included an image of a man North Yorkshire Police said it would like to speak to, saying he may have information which could assist the force's investigation.

Anyone with information about the alleged public order offence is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police by phone calling 101, with the reference number 12260207257.
