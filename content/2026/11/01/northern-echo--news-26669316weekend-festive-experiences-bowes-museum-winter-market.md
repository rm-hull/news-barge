**From December 11 to 13, 2026, The Bowes Museum will transform its galleries and parkland for its annual seasonal market bringing together more than 100 artisan stalls, delicious food and drink, live entertainment and creative activities for all ages.** Combining a bustling unique shopping experience with access to the museum's galleries and grounds, The Winter Market offers a festive day out for visitors of all ages.

The Bowes Museum in Barnard Castle is preparing to welcome visitors from across the region for its annual Winter Market, taking place from December 11 to 13.

Established by John and Joséphine Bowes to share art and culture with the public, the landmark building provides an impressive historic backdrop to the seasonal celebration.

More than 100 independent artists, designers, makers and regional food producers are scheduled to take part across the three-day festive event.

The market transforms the galleries into a bustling winter gathering, offering visitors a chance to find handmade presents, homewares, jewellery and festive decorations.

![]()

Stalls will stand alongside the museum's renowned permanent displays of fine art, ceramics and historic textiles, creating an engaging atmosphere where shopping and culture meet.

Visitors can browse the stands while admiring celebrated European paintings that have made the venue one of northern England's most distinguished cultural destinations.

Regional food and drink traders will also feature prominently, serving seasonal delicacies, artisan produce and hot refreshments to keep shoppers warm throughout the day.

Families attending the market can explore the building through interactive gallery trails and creative hands-on workshops tailored for younger visitors.

The surrounding parkland and landscaped gardens offer space for a winter stroll between browsing sessions, with views across the distinctive façade and grounds.

For those wanting a relaxing sit-down break, Café Bowes offers daytime dining, serving refreshments until 4.30pm with last orders taken at 4pm.

The main museum galleries and grounds remain open from 10am to 5pm daily, giving guests plenty of time to enjoy both the market and the permanent exhibitions.

The annual market has become a treasured winter tradition for Teesdale, bringing together local makers, regional visitors and festive community life under one roof.

It offers an uplifting seasonal experience that celebrates northern craftsmanship against the backdrop of one of the county's finest architectural treasures.

The event provides a warm, welcoming start to the Christmas season for shoppers and culture lovers of all ages.
