## Meet the Yorkshire artist who spent 30 years drawing strangers on paper bags

Mik Artistik and one of his Bags of Life drawings

*Mik Artistik brings Bags of Life art and stories to York Explore, reports MAXINE GORDON*

A cult Yorkshire artist, musician and storyteller whose work has won praise from music legends and festival audiences is bringing his unique Bags of Life project to York next month.

Leeds creative Mik Artistik will appear at York Explore Library on Friday, November 6, for a special afternoon combining art, storytelling and music.

The event, running from 3pm to 4.30pm, will showcase portraits, anecdotes and experiences gathered over more than three decades of drawing people on ordinary brown paper bags using only a biro.

Known affectionately as "the brown paper bag man", Mik has built a devoted following through his artwork and performances with Mik Artistik's Ego Trip, appearing at major festivals including Glastonbury, Latitude and Leeds Festival.

During the York event, visitors will hear stories from his book, Bags of Life, which chronicles his encounters with people from all walks of life.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21648378.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Bags of Life project

Mik first discovered the idea during a trip to Ireland in 1983, shortly after teaching himself to draw portraits. What began as a simple artistic exercise soon developed into a lifelong project.

"The bag held secrets. It was a container. It had mystery. As we do," he said.

Over the following 30 years, Mik estimates he created more than 20,000 portraits.

"Everyone ended up on the bag. Judges, butchers, babies, housewives. It was the great leveller," he said.

The project took him into workplaces, pubs, offices, garages and funeral parlours, introducing him to people whose lives and experiences later inspired both his visual art and music.

Among the stories featured in the book is one about a young father named Steve, whom Mik met in 1990. The encounter remains one of many vivid memories collected during decades spent sketching strangers and listening to their stories.

Visitors to York Explore will have the chance to see original artwork from the collection, hear behind-the-scenes tales and enjoy a few musical moments, alongside a special guest appearance.

The event forms part of Mik's efforts to find a publisher for Bags of Life and introduce the project to new audiences.

Organisers say it offers a rare opportunity to spend time with one of Yorkshire's most distinctive creative figures and gain an insight into a project that has connected thousands of people through art and conversation.

Bags of Life takes place at York Explore Library and Archive on Friday, November 6, from 3pm to 4.30pm.

Tickets cost £7.50 and can be booked through the Explore York ticket website: tickettailor.com/events/exploreyorklibrariesandarchives/2463246
