A burger restaurant is on the market as its owners prepare to relocate.

Meltdown Bradford, currently based on Otley Road and owned by brothers Shamas and Tayyib Khan, will move to Great Horton Road in the coming weeks.

The business, which specialises in burgers, fries and sandwiches, has put its existing premises up for sale as part of the relocation.

## 'The place is getting a bit too small for us'

Shamas Khan said the decision was driven by the need for more space and a desire to be closer to the city centre.

Mr Khan said: "We've found a new location on Great Horton Road, right in the heart of Bradford.

"That’s what compelled us to sell.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21648047.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of Meltdown Bradford *(Image: T&A)*

"We’re planning to go in the following few weeks but we’re still open at the moment.

"We have started moving our things.

"We have got more space in the new building, and we are ready for a bigger place.

"This place is getting a bit too small for us at this point."

## 'We are very excited to be making this move'

He described the move as a new chapter for the business, which was launched three years ago and has developed a loyal following.

Mr Khan said: "We are very excited to be making this move.

"Lots of people have been telling us that, where we are currently, we’re too far out of town."

## 'We felt we are too far out of the way'

While the business attracts customers from across the district and beyond, the brothers believe a more central site will improve accessibility.

Mr Khan said: "We are in BD2 at the moment.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21648048.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The exterior of Meltdown Bradford *(Image: T&A)*

"A lot of our customers come from further away, and so we felt we are too far out of the way."

The Great Horton Road premises will offer additional space and a more central location, which the owners hope will help them reach new customers, Mr Khan said.

Meltdown Bradford remains open on Otley Road while preparations for the move continue.

## 'It has shaped us into the business we are today'

Mr Khan expressed that he would like to see another independent business take over the Otley Road premises once Meltdown moves out.

He said: "It would be nice for someone to take the old shop on.

"It has shaped us into the business we are today."

The new restaurant is expected to open in around six weeks.
