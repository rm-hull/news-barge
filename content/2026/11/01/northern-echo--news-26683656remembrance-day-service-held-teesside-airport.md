A Remembrance Day service will be held at Teesside International Airport to honour those who served and lost their lives in conflict.

The Middleton St George Memorial Association will hold the service at the airport’s Memorial Garden at 10.45am on Wednesday, November 11, with members of the public invited to attend.

Those wishing to lay a wreath or personal tribute are being asked to speak to a committee member before the service begins.

![A picture of a memorial service](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18199225.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Memorial service to Andrew Mynarski and Canadian airmen at the former St. George Hotel in Middleton *(Image: Picture: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The location has particular significance because the modern-day airport occupies the site of the former RAF Middleton St George, a major wartime airfield.

The base became home to RAF 76 and 78 Squadrons in January 1941, before three Royal Canadian Air Force units, 419 “Moose”, 420 “Snowy Owl” and 428 “Ghost” Squadrons, were stationed there.

After the war, the airfield remained in RAF use before becoming Teesside International Airport in 1964.

![A picture of a memorial service](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/18199270.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An earlier memorial service to Andrew Mynarski and Canadian airmen at the former St. George Hotel in Middleton *(Image: Picture: THE NORTHERN ECHO)*

The memorial garden commemorates those who served with the RAF and Canadian squadrons based at Middleton St George.

It includes a memorial stone to the Canadian squadrons and a bronze statue of Canadian airman Pilot Officer Andrew Mynarski VC, who was posthumously awarded the Victoria Cross for his actions in 1944.

This year's Remembrance Day gathering comes shortly after another important addition to the site.

In October, a new memorial stone was unveiled to honour 76 and 78 Squadrons, 85 years after they arrived at Middleton St George.

The two squadrons lost 311 RAF personnel during their time at the airfield.

The Middleton St George Memorial Association was formed in 1977 and works to preserve the memory of those who served at the former RAF station, as well as maintaining the memorial garden.

The association also holds an annual service at the site in June.

Previous commemorations have remembered the 1,266 airmen who died while flying from the wartime airfield, with RAF representatives, veterans' organisations, cadets and families among those attending.

Wednesday’s service will begin at 10.45am on November 11 at the Memorial Garden, Teesside International Airport, with visitors advised to arrive in good time.
