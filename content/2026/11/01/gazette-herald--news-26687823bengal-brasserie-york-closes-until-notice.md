An established York restaurant has closed its doors unexpectedly.

Bengal Brasserie in Goodramgate, York, had handwritten signs on three windows this morning (Sunday, November 1) which read: "Due to unforeseen circumstances, the restaurant will remain closed until further notice.

"Sorry for any inconvenience."

![Bengal Brasserie in Goodramgate, York](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21656058.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Handwritten signs on the windows of Bengal Brasserie in Goodramgate indicate the closure *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

There were no tables in the dining area of the ground floor of the premises when The Press attended earlier this morning and a number of chairs are stacked against the restaurant window.

![Bengal Brasserie in Goodramgate, York](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21656056.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Handwritten signs on the windows of Bengal Brasserie in Goodramgate indicate the closure *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

The restaurant has operated in York since 1992, according to its website.

It adds: "As our founding location, York holds a special place in the Bengal Brasserie story.

"Every recipe, every tradition, and every aspect of our service philosophy was first perfected here."

The Press has attempted to contact the owner via another of the Bengal Brasserie restaurants in West Yorkshire and will include their response when we receive it.
