A nostalgic glimpse of Teesside in decades gone by is back in print following renewed local demand.

Memories of Stockton in the 1970s and 1980s, written by Teesside-born historian Dr Tosh Warwick, has been reprinted following renewed demand from readers keen to look back at an era which brought huge changes of its own.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651743.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Hitting the shops in the 1980s *(Image: Teesside Archives)*

The book charts changes to Stockton High Street and the River Tees waterfront, as well as the arrival of the Castlegate Shopping Centre, over two decades that shaped the town we know today.

The book’s return comes as Stockton enters another period of change, with a Waterfront Urban Park opening on the former Castlegate site and work under way on the High Street.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651744.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Castle Centre, later Castlegate Centre, in the 1970s *(Image: Teesside Archives)*

Dr Warwick said: "The changes in recent years have reignited a sense of nostalgia and reminiscence about Stockton and the wider borough in bygone decades.

"Memories of Stockton-on-Tees in the 1970s and 1980s relives some of the debates of the time and also highlights how in reality, there have been a number of changes over the years that have brought the loss of Teesside institutions but also breathed new life into old historic buildings such as the Stockton Globe."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651745.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Redevelopment work alongside the Tees at Stockton in 1970 *(Image: Teesside Archives)*

The book features more than 200 photographs, many published for the first time, alongside contributions from people across the community.

Using collections from Teesside Archives, Preston Park Museum, Stockton Borough Libraries and private collectors, the book captures everyday life and the era’s biggest moments.

The 1970s brought huge changes to Stockton town centre, with historic buildings and shops demolished as the High Street was transformed.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651741.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A bustling High Street in 1986 *(Image: Teesside Archives)*

Among them was the Castle Shopping Centre, later known as Castlegate, which became a familiar part of Stockton's retail landscape.

More than 50 years later, the shopping centre has itself been demolished, making way for the Waterfront Urban Park as Stockton’s town centre undergoes another transformation.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651746.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An artist impression of what would become the Castle Centre *(Image: Castlegate Centre)*

The book also ventures beyond Stockton, with photographs and memories from Billingham, Eaglescliffe, Thornaby and Yarm.

Dr Warwick said: "The photographs and memories in the book feature Stockton, Billingham, Eaglescliffe, Thornaby and Yarm and have prompted lively debate among readers and Heritage Unlocked followers. That response led bookshops and the wider public to demand a reprint of the book.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651742.jpg?type=mds-article-620) Mecca Leisure and Social Club in 1983, now home to the Stockton Globe *(Image: Teesside Archives)*

He added: "We hope the book will help share the area’s heritage with new audiences, bring back fond memories, and encourage constructive debate on the area’s past, regeneration and new visions for the area."

Among the moments captured are the Queen’s visit in 1977, Christmas celebrations through the years, Margaret Thatcher’s "Walk in the Wilderness" and the 150th anniversary of the Stockton and Darlington Railway.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21651740.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Memories of Stockton-on-Tees in the 1970s and 1980s book cover *(Image: Heritage Unlocked)*

There are also memories of Stockton's famous market and the traditions and everyday scenes which will be familiar to generations of people from the town and surrounding area.

Together, the photographs and stories capture Stockton and the wider borough during a time of huge change, as the town looks to its future once again.

Memories of Stockton-on-Tees in the 1970s and 1980s is available from Heritage Unlocked, bookshops, museums and selected independent retailers.
