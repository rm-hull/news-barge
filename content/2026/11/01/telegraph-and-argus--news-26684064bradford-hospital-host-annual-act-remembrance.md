AN annual Act of Remembrance will bring together hospital staff, patients, and the wider community.

Hosted by Bradford Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (BTHFT), the service will take place at Bradford Royal Infirmary on Wednesday, November 11, beginning at 10.55am.

The event will include a two-minute silence at 11am to mark Armistice Day and honour those who have served and sacrificed in past and present conflicts.

Fiona Mattison, veterans lead at BTHFT, said: "Remembrance is an opportunity for us all to pause and reflect on the sacrifices made by so many in the service of our country.

"As a Veteran Aware organisation, we are proud to recognise the contribution that veterans, serving personnel and military families make to our communities and our workforce.

"We hope that colleagues, partners and members of the public will join us as we come together in remembrance."

The service will feature readings, an Act of Remembrance, the exhortation, a two-minute silence, and multi-faith prayers.

As a Veteran Aware accredited trust, BTHFT is committed to supporting veterans, serving personnel, reservists, and their families.

Following the service, people will be invited to stay for refreshments, provided by Bradford Hospitals Charity, and a selection of homemade baked goods will be available, with donations gratefully received in support of charitable activities across the Trust.
