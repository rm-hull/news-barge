Police seized a luxury Lamborghini after its driver was caught driving anti-socially on a major Bradford road and found to have the wrong insurance cover.

Officers from West Yorkshire Police's Bradford East neighbourhood policing team stopped the vehicle on Shipley Airedale Road after seeing it accelerate aggressively from traffic lights.

Police said on social media that the driver appeared not to notice officers nearby as he tried to launch the Lamborghini “like a NASA craft” from the traffic lights.

## Driver believed he had insurance coverage

The driver told police he believed his own policy covered him to drive the Lamborghini under its third-party cover.

However, checks revealed the policy did not provide the cover he believed it did.

As a result, the Lamborghini was seized under Section 165 of the Road Traffic Act, which gives police powers to remove vehicles being driven without valid insurance.

The driver was also given a Section 59 warning for anti-social driving and reported to court.

## Police statement in full

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The driver of this Lamborghini thought it would be a good idea to try launching like a NASA craft from the traffic lights on Shipley Airedale Road.”

"Clearly he hadn't spotted us.

"On speaking to the driver about this anti-social driving, we entered into the merry-go-round discussions of insurance.

"He was very confident that he was able to drive this expensive motor on his own policy under third party rights.

"Sadly for him, his policy did not have such cover on it.

"Vehicle seized under S.165 RTA with a side serving of a S.59 warning. Driver reported to court."
