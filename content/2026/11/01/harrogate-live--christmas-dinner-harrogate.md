Christmas Day lunch in Harrogate from £89 at the Crown Hotel to £300 at FIFTY TWO, Rudding Park: 12 places with 2026 prices, sittings, deposits and child rates.

For Christmas Day in Harrogate, most people look first at the **Old Swan Hotel**(£105 for three courses), the** Fat Badger**on Cold Bath Road and the** Pickled Sprout**on Prospect Place (both £135), and** Hotel du Vin**(£144.95 with a glass of Champagne). The cheapest Christmas Day menu here is the** Crown Hotel’s** at £89. Every entry was checked against the venue’s own site on 1 November 2026.

We have not eaten at any of these for this guide, and nothing here is a review. Each entry gives the 2026 price from the venue’s own Christmas page or menu, what it includes, sitting times and booking rules where published, and the Food Standards Agency hygiene rating. Christmas Day 2026 falls on a Friday. Almost every venue asks for a deposit and a pre-order, so book early.

## Harrogate town centre

### 1. The Old Swan Hotel, Swan Road

The Old Swan is the ivy-covered hotel on Swan Road where, as the Harrogate Hydropathic, Agatha Christie was found after her disappearance in 1926 (Wikipedia). Its Christmas 2026 page offers Christmas Day lunch as “a three-course meal with tea, coffee & mince pies”.

It also sells three-night residential Christmas packages from £550 a person, which include the same lunch, and a festive “Host a Roast” group menu from £27.95 a head on weekdays (£29.95 at weekends, minimum 12 guests).

### 2. The Fat Badger, Cold Bath Road

The Fat Badger is the pub at the White Hart Hotel on Cold Bath Road. Its Christmas Day page calls the meal a “Christmas Day feast” and adds that diners also get £10 off their tab at the bar.

Bookings start with an enquiry form on the same page.

The Pickled Sprout is the restaurant at the Yorkshire Hotel on Prospect Place. Its Christmas Day page promises “exceptional food, great company, and absolutely no washing up”, and its price, sittings and payment rules are the same as the Fat Badger’s.

### 4. Hotel du Vin, Prospect Place

Hotel du Vin’s Harrogate hotel is on Prospect Place in the town centre. Its Christmas page and 2026 brochure offer a four-course Christmas Day lunch with a glass of Champagne: a pear, endive and Stilton salad to start, then free-range turkey with “all of the traditional trimmings” or a pumpkin, beetroot and chestnut Wellington, and figgy pudding or a chocolate terrine.

Festive lunches with a French market table run through December, including Boxing Day and New Year’s Day.

The Crown is the large hotel on Crown Place by the Royal Pump Room Museum. Its festive events page offers Christmas Day lunch at two sittings and says the menu features “seasonal favourites and festive treats”.

Groups of ten or more can book one of its suites, Elgar, Byron or Brontë, for a private three-course Christmas Day lunch, with half a bottle of wine a head as an option.

It also sells three-night Christmas breaks from 24 to 27 December, which include the lunch with a live pianist. We could not find a Food Standards Agency record under the hotel’s name, so no rating is shown.

Champagne is served at 1pm and lunch at 1.15pm. The page carries Michelin 2026 and AA Rosette 2025 badges.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I have Christmas dinner in Harrogate on Christmas Day 2026?

These Harrogate-area venues publish a Christmas Day 2026 price on their own websites: the Crown Hotel (£89), the Kestrel near Knaresborough (£89.95), the Old Swan Hotel (£105), the Fat Badger and the Pickled Sprout (both £135), the Inn at South Stainley (£140), Hotel du Vin (£144.95), the Majestic and Cedar Court (both £145), the West Park Hotel (£150), Grantley Hall (£235) and FIFTY TWO at Rudding Park (£300).

### How much is Christmas dinner for children in Harrogate?

Child prices on Christmas Day run from £37 at the Crown Hotel (ages 4 to 11, half portions) and £40.50 for the Kestrel’s kids’ menu, through £60 at the Old Swan and West Park Hotel and £67.50 at the Fat Badger and Pickled Sprout, to £95 at Grantley Hall. Hotel du Vin charges £74.95 for ages 5 to 11 and nothing for under-fives.

### Is there a cheaper festive menu in Harrogate before Christmas?

Yes. The Barologist’s three-course Christmas menu is £29.95 from 27 November to 24 December, the West Park Hotel’s festive menu is £29 for two courses or £35 for three from 23 November to 24 December (not Sundays), the Crown Hotel’s festive lunches start at £28 and Hotel du Vin’s festive lunches are £34.95.
