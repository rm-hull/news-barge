Two men have been arrested after officers pulling over an uninsured van led to a cash, cannabis, and drug bags discovery.

Officers stopped the white transit on Wilton Street, in Middlesbrough, after the vehicle was found to be driven without insurance.

The passenger, a 59-year-old man, was detained and searched along with his property leading to drugs and drug paraphernalia being found.

He was arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply class A and class B drugs.

A search of the driver found cannabis and the 42-year-old was arrested on suspicion of conspiring to supply a class A drug and driving a vehicle without insurance.

Cleveland Police has confirmed that both men have been released on bail while enquiries are ongoing.

Sergeant Sproson, from Middlesbrough’s proactive team, said: “This stop is part of our continuing work against drug dealing that often contributes to serious violence and other crime such as robbery.

“If you believe drug dealing is taking place in your neighbourhood, please report it by calling 101 or visiting our website.”
