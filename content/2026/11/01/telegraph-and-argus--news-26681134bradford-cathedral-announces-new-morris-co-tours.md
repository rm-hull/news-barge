GUIDED tours celebrating the stained glass legacy of William Morris are set to take place at Bradford Cathedral.

The Morris & Co. Stained Glass Window Tours, led by an expert historian, will run on Saturday, November 14, 2026, and will explore the history, design, and meaning of the cathedral’s Morris & Co. windows.

William Morris, the renowned Victorian artist and designer, founded Morris & Co. and worked alongside figures such as Dante Gabriel Rossetti, Edward Burne-Jones, and Ford Madox Brown to create stained glass artwork.

Maggie Myers, director of education and visitors at Bradford Cathedral, said: "We are delighted to be able to offer these new tours exploring our Morris & Co. stained glass here at Bradford Cathedral.

"We are fortunate to have an extensive collection of the early work of Morris & Co. in 4 locations in the building, with designs by D G Rossetti, Edward Burne-Jones, Ford Madox Brown, Philip Webb, Peter Paul Marshall, Albert Moore, William De Morgan and William Morris himself."

The tours will also feature the cathedral’s West Window, a Pre-Raphaelite piece designed by Heaton, Butler and Bayne.

Tickets are priced at £9 each, plus a booking fee.

The tours will take place at 11am and 1pm.
