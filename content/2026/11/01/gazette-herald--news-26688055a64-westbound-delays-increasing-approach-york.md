Motorists are being warned of severe delays increasing on a major road affecting York traffic this lunchtime (Sunday, November 1).




The A64 westbound currently has 14-minute hold-ups between the Barton Hill crossroads and Towthorpe Moor Lane on the approach to the city.

Speeds are limited to just 15 miles per hour on the section after Barton-le-Willows, and also Flaxton Moor, with additional delays approaching Common Lane.

The A64 is a major road for traffic between York and the North Yorkshire coast and also connects with the A1237 York Outer Ring Road.
