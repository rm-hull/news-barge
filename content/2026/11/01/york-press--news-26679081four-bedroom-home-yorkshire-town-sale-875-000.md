## ‘Bespoke’ home with panoramic views of Yorkshire town hits the market

Views from the four-bedroom house for sale in Western View, Pocklington, for £875,000 *(Image: Hudson/Zoopla)*

An “architecturally bespoke” home with panoramic views over an East Yorkshire town has hit the market.

The four-bedroom house is for sale in Western View, Pocklington, for £875,000.

Hudson Moody, which is marketing the property, said it is an “exceptional, turnkey property” in an exclusive development on the edge of the Yorkshire Wolds.

The home’s best views can be found from the sun terrace, accessed from the ground floor living room.

Also on the ground floor is the open plan kitchen diner, a bedroom and a toilet.

Upstairs on the first floor are three bedrooms, with the main and guest bedrooms served by en-suite bathrooms.

The basement, meanwhile, features a large media or games room and a separate gym area, as well as access to the double garage.

Outside, the home has a resin driveway and a rear landscaped garden.
