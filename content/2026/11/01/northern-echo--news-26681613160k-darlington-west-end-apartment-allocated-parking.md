Set within a handsome period property in Darlington’s West End, and reached via a sweeping staircase sure to impress, this first floor apartment marries timeless character with the practicalities of modern living... and does so with style.

The entrance hall, home to a handy storage cupboard, provides access to the apartment’s beautifully presented accommodation where high ceilings, generous proportions and large windows come together to create a wonderful sense of space and light.

At the heart of the home is the dual aspect lounge with built-in storage and a feature fireplace that makes a natural focal point in the room.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21647126.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This apartment in Darlington's West End is perfect for buyers looking for an elegant home where they don't have to worry about the upkeep of the building *(Image: w)*

It’s an elegant space equally suited to quiet evenings in or entertaining friends.

The kitchen blends good looks with everyday practicality.

It is home to a range of fitted units and integrated appliances including a dishwasher and fridge freezer and benefits from plinth heating.

The main bedroom is a real highlight of the apartment – generously sized and beautifully proportioned, and with a large east-facing window through which light pours in.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21647128.jpg?type=mds-article-620)This apartment in Darlington's West End is perfect for buyers looking for an elegant home where they don't have to worry about the upkeep of the building *(Image: w)*

The second bedroom, also east facing, is a good size and versatile enough to serve as a guest room, home office or extra living space.

Completing the accommodation is a smart modern shower room.

The apartment is set in well-maintained private grounds and comes with access to a beautifully kept shared garden and – a rare advantage in this location – an allocated off-street parking space.

The West End is one of Darlington’s most desirable addresses, making this a superb choice for professionals, downsizers or anyone looking for a home with genuine period charm.
