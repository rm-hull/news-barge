Bradford City legend Chris Kamara led tributes to Geoffrey Richmond tonight.

Kamara - known as Kammy- arrived at Valley Parade as a player in 1994, but it was as manager that he made his biggest impact. Taking charge in 1995, the charismatic former midfielder guided City to promotion from the Second Division through the play-offs in 1996. The achievement included a memorable semi-final comeback against Blackpool before City beat Notts County 2-0 at Wembley.

He told the T&A he was “sorry to hear the sad news” and said Richmond would always be remembered for the success he brought to Valley Parade.

Kammy, who Richmond appointed City manager in November 1995, said: “My thoughts go to his family and friends at this difficult time.

“Geoffrey gave me the honour to be the manager of Bradford City so I will always be grateful to him for that.

“The success he had as chairman of the football club means he will always be remembered.”

Kamara left the managerial job in 1998, with Paul Jewell eventually completing Geoffrey Richmond’s Premier League dream when Bradford won promotion in 1999.
