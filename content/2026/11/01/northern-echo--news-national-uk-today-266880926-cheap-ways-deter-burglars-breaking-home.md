A home expert has shared a number of cheap methods to deter burglars, with one costing as little as £11.

Research from Checkatrade found that 86% of UK regions polled saw a consistent jump in residential burglaries in the months immediately following the autumn clock change compared to the spring season.

This was based on Freedom of Information requests to police departments across the UK calling for the number of residential burglaries in the year 2025.

In response to this, the company has shared some cheaper burglar deterrents households could implement.

Emma Grant, Director of Trade at Checkatrade, said: "As the nights draw in, dark and unlit homes can unfortunately become an easy target for opportunistic thieves.

"Our analysis shows a clear link between longer, darker evenings and an uptick in break-ins, yet fewer homeowners today are building security checks into their routine home maintenance.

"The good news is that securing your home doesn't have to mean major overhauls – simple, effective steps like these can help to deter burglars, while ensuring we all feel more secure in our own homes."

## 6 cheap ways to deter burglars

### Indoor light timers

One of the most effective ways to prevent burglary, according to Checkatrade's analysis, is simulating occupancy through internal light timers.

Grant added: "These can be bought cheaply online, for example from as little as £11 on the Checkatrade Shop.

"You simply plug existing lamps into the device before they activate, turning lamps on and off randomly to realistically simulate someone being home.

"This might sound simple, but it works incredibly well, and is a great method for homeowners to feel safe quickly."

### Window lock fitting

Securing windows is another important step in burglary prevention.

All external doors should feature British Standard (BS3621) approved locks, and cylinder locks on uPVC doors should be anti-snap certified.

Grant added: "Window locks can be purchased for under £10 online, and can be fitted by a Checkatrade Express locksmith for under £100."

### Lock your gates

Locking side gates with heavy-duty padlocks, ideally closed shackle padlocks, is a simple and cost-effective way to restrict access to the back of your property.

### Install a gravel path

A noisy gravel path can act as a deterrent by drawing attention to anyone approaching the house.

An 800kg bag of gravel costs around £60 and covers approximately five square metres.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656580.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A noisy gravel path can act as a deterrent to burglars *(Image: Getty Images)*

### Outdoor motion detector lights

Outdoor motion-activated lights are another effective deterrent.

Grant said: "These are hard to spot and give any unscrupulous visitors a fright, which will likely scare them off.

"Installation starts at around £150, and can prove an effective deterrent."

### Wireless home security systems

Wireless security alarms are among the most affordable home security systems.

They use Wi-Fi and battery-powered door sensors, making them cheaper than wired alternatives.

Grant said: "Basic systems generally start from around £250, although it’s worth noting that they may require installation separately."

Simple, affordable upgrades can help keep homes safe and reduce the risk of burglaries, especially during the darker months of the year.
