## Yorkshire coastal town transformed into Gothic haven

The town of Whitby is turned into a haven for fans of Goth culture twice a year, as captured by members of The Press Camera Club *(Image: Michelle Dunnington Edwards)*

Gothic glamour has taken over the streets of a North Yorkshire coastal town.

Whitby Goth Weekend, the bi-annual celebration held every spring and autumn, transforms the town into a haven for fans of gothic culture, with visitors arriving from across the UK and beyond.

It has been running since Friday (October 30) and the curtain comes down today.

![Images from Whitby Goth Weekend, October 30 - November 1, 2026](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21656287.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The town of Whitby is turned into a haven for fans of Goth culture twice a year *(Image: Contributed)*

First held in 1994, the event began with a simple advert in NME magazine by founder Jo Hampshire, who invited fellow goths to gather in Whitby around Halloween.

She invited 40 of her pen pals to The Elsinore Inn, close to the centre of the town.

Since then, it has grown into a major international event and is now regarded as one of the most famous gothic gatherings in the world.

![Images from Whitby Goth Weekend, October 30 - November 1, 2026](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21656286.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The town of Whitby is turned into a haven for fans of Goth culture twice a year, as captured by members of The Press Camera Club *(Image: Michelle Dunnington Edwards)*

The event is a haven for photographers, and members of The Press Camera Club have included their images from the weekend.

A dedicated Facebook group is also set up to capture the inventiveness and style showcased on the streets of Whitby.

In York yesterday morning, The Press caught up with mother and daughter Emma and Sophie Richardson, waiting to catch the bus to Whitby.

Emma was wearing the outfit she made for Sophie for last year's event, with her daughter turning to a Dr Frank-N-Furter theme straight out of The Rocky Horror Picture Show.

![Images from Whitby Goth Weekend, October 30 - November 1, 2026](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21656291.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Mother and daughter Emma and Sophie Richardson were heading to the Whitby Goth Weekend when The Press caught up with them yesterday morning *(Image: Kevin Glenton)*

Visitors fill the streets in elaborate gothic costumes and take part in a packed programme of talks, workshops, parties, art exhibitions, and alternative markets.

Organisers said: "Whitby Goth Weekend has become a dark jewel in the crown of the global goth scene, drawing attendees not only from across the UK but from every corner of the globe.

"You will find that there is no single venue or stage during Whitby Goth Weekend, for the spirit of WGW is untamed & weaves itself into every shadow and side street of Whitby."

Whitby’s gothic connections run deep, with Bram Stoker drawing inspiration from the town for his iconic novel Dracula after visiting in 1890.

The latest Whitby Goth Weekend runs from October 30 to November 1.
