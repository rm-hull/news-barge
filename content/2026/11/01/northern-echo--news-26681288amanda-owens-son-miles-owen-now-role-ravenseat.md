Amanda Owen's son Miles has been playing an increasingly visible role in the family's farming and renovation work as the latest series of Our Farm Next Door approaches its conclusion.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess and her nine children have become familiar faces to television audiences through Our Yorkshire Farm and its successor, Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids.

The fourth series has shown him helping Amanda with the restoration of Anty John's, the derelict farmhouse the family has spent years transforming.

During the opening episode, Miles joined his mother in painting the newly plastered walls after they had finally dried.

However, the building project is far from Miles's only responsibility.

The series has also shown him working alongside his father Clive to tackle problems on the family's land.

In one episode, the pair dealt with a waterlogged field, highlighting the challenges that come with farming in the Yorkshire Dales.

Miles has also been involved in the family's efforts to rescue livestock.

During the third episode of the latest series, he joined Amanda and Sid in helping a pregnant sheep stranded on a hillside.

The incident offered another example of the practical skills and responsibilities associated with growing up at Ravenseat.

For viewers who remember Miles from the family's earlier television appearances, his continued involvement in farming reflects how the older Owen children are taking on different roles as they grow up.

The family's renovation of Anty John's has also created new opportunities for the children to contribute.

The property has required years of work, including repairs, interior improvements and the installation of essential services.

As the fourth series approaches its final scheduled episode, the farmhouse is beginning to resemble a home, with furniture being moved into the building.

However, Miles's involvement in the renovation should not be taken as confirmation that he will eventually inherit or manage Ravenseat.

The family's long-term succession plans have not been publicly established.

There is also uncertainty over whether the family's television journey will continue.

The final scheduled episode of Our Farm Next Door series four will air on More4 on Monday, November 2.
