A landmark York pub is looking for new managers.

The Heworth Inn on Heworth Green closed suddenly in September. It has been empty ever since.

Locals said the pub had been visited a number of times by police before its closure.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-heworth-inn-york-closed-4.webp)

One said the previous landlord had been evicted and the locks changed.

There were concerns in the community that the Heworth Inn could soon be turned into flats or holiday lets.

But owner the Stonegate Group says it is looking to reopen the pub.

A Stonegate Group spokesperson said: “We are currently looking for a new publican to take on the Heworth Inn, York and hope to find the right person soon.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-heworth-inn-york-closed-2.webp)

Anyone interested in finding out more about the opportunity can visit the Stonegate Pub Partners website.”

According to the post on that website, the forecast annual turnover at the Heworth Inn is £502,363.

The month rent is estimated at £615 a week.

It says: “The pub offers three distinct trading areas, including a main bar, a cosy snug and a separate restaurant space that can also accommodate private functions and small events.

“Internally, the pub combines traditional character features with a comfortable and welcoming atmosphere, creating broad appeal for both local residents and visitors.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/the-heworth-inn-york-closed-5.webp)

“In addition to its wet and food trade, the business benefits from seven en-suite letting rooms, providing valuable additional revenue opportunities and the potential to grow overnight stays within one of the UK’s most popular tourist destinations.”

It adds: “The ideal operator will be a community-focused publican who enjoys building relationships with local customers while maximising additional revenue streams from food, functions and accommodation.”
