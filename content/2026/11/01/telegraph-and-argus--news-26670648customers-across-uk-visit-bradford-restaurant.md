A Bradford restaurant which customers visit from across the country is up for a national award.

Mezza Restaurant is in the running to retain its title as the UK’s Best Lebanese Restaurant at the 15th British Kebab Awards.

The family-run restaurant, based at Legrams Mills, is liked by soap stars and is planning on opening a second site.

## Defending national recognition

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21637795.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Mezza restaurant *(Image: Newsquest)*

Owner Nabil Gorji said the latest recognition was another proud moment for the team.

Mr Gorji said: “It's really good. It makes us proud because it shows that we're doing something right and the restaurant is being well received by our customers.”

He said: “We're going to push hard to try and win it again.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21637796.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Mezza restaurant *(Image: Newsquest)*

## What winning last year meant

Mezza was named Best Lebanese Restaurant in the UK at last year’s ceremony in London.

Describing the awards event, Mr Gorji said: “When we went down for the award, we didn't know what to expect.

“It was at the Hilton in Westminster. We got there and I was like, 'Oh my God, this is a massive event.'

"There were around a thousand people there and we were sat on a table with MPs."

He added: “Just to be recognised and to get there was overwhelming. We were really proud of what we'd achieved.”

## Family feel behind success

He believes the restaurant’s success is based on quality, fresh food, consistency and its family atmosphere.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21637963.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the Lebanese cuisine at Mezza Restaurant *(Image: UCG)*

Mr Gorji said: “It's the food. The quality, the freshness and the consistency are always top-notch.

“It's a family-run business. People like coming back to us, maybe because of that family feel of the restaurant.”

Mezza is known for its grilled dishes, particularly its mixed grill.

Its menu also includes fattoush, tabbouleh and traditional dips.

Mr Gorji said: “Our grills are what we're known for really. The mixed grill is the go-to dish that everyone goes for.”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21637798.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Mezza restaurant *(Image: Newsquest)*

## From Covid launch to national recognition

The business opened during the Covid pandemic, after its planned March 2020 launch was delayed.

Mr Gorji said: “People were queuing up and down the road and from there people just kept coming back and coming back.”

He said customers come from across the country travelling from Newcastle, London and Birmingham.

The restaurant has also gained attention through celebrity visitors, including TV star Ryan Thomas.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21637797.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Mezza restaurant *(Image: Newsquest)*

Mr Gorji said: “We've gone viral on TikTok”

He said Bradford's TV Dr Amir Khan had recreated some of the restaurant’s dishes on social media.

Mezza is planning to open a second site in Leeds in 2027, despite rising costs across the hospitality sector.

He said: “It's been a massive struggle.

“The raw materials keep going up, utilities keep going up and staff costs keep going up. Everything just keeps going up.”
