He's used to making sure everyone else has a good night out – but one Darlington club steward has been given a taste of the VIP treatment after being personally invited to see a sold-out show by a UK theatre star.

Paul Gill, steward at Harrowgate Club in Darlington, was invited by performer Gary Ryan to see his show at the Fire Station theatre in Sunderland.

The visit comes ahead of Mr Ryan’s upcoming appearances at the Darlington club this December.

Mr Gill said: "It was brilliant to see Gary live in front of a packed full house.

"There was a fantastic atmosphere, and it was great to see how much the audience enjoyed him and had travelled from all parts of the region to be part of it.

"Seeing him perform for myself has made me even more excited about bringing him to Darlington.

"Gary is a current UK theatre star who has played some of the best-known venues, so having him join us at Harrowgate Club for two intimate Christmas nights is a real scoop."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21449683.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Gary will appear at Harrowgate Club on Saturday, 12 December and Saturday, 19 December for two special Christmas party nights. *(Image: Goffy Media)*

He said the club is working hard to make the club a place where people can enjoy a great night out.

Mr Gill said: "Tickets are selling really well, there is a great buzz and we are looking forward to welcoming everyone."

Mr Ryan said he was equally pleased to meet Mr Gill and learn about the club’s efforts.

He said: "It’s so good to see Paul and the team at Harrowgate Club are doing so well, especially with the many challenges clubs face today.

"I'm really looking forward to bringing my more intimate show to Darlington, I have been so lucky to have played some of the very best venues in the world."

Mr Ryan will perform at Harrowgate Club on Saturday, 12 December, and Saturday, 19 December, for two special Christmas party nights.
