Residents on a Bradford street have spoken of their shock after a house was rammed on Saturday night.

People living on Enfield Drive, in Wibsey, said they heard “two loud bangs” at around 10pm on October 31.

Locals told the T&A they believed a vehicle had deliberately struck the property, which they said was home to an elderly woman who lived alone.

One resident said: “I heard a couple of bangs last night after 10. I think a car has gone into the house deliberately.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656250.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene this morning *(Image: T&A)*

“It’s a little old lady that lives by herself.

“It was horrible. I looked out of the window and didn’t see anything, I just heard two bangs.

“I’ve heard metal and I just thought it was some lads on the road.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656249.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene this morning *(Image: T&A)*

“I thought it was a van because it was two very loud bangs.”

Residents speculated the incident may have been a case of mistaken identity, although these claims have not been confirmed by police.

Another resident said: “I hope she’s OK. It’s just horrible. What was the need?”

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656253.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene this morning *(Image: T&A)*

Locals also said that the woman who lives at the property had been taken to hospital following the incident.

The T&A has been unable to independently verify this information, and her condition is not known.

## The T&A visit the scene

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656252.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The scene this morning *(Image: T&A)*

When the T&A visited the scene on Sunday morning, two police vans were present and crime scene investigators were carrying out enquiries.

Detectives and CSI officers were examining the area outside the property and speaking to residents.

The T&A has contacted West Yorkshire Police for comment.
