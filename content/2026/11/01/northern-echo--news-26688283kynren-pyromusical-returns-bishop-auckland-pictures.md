The skies of Bishop Auckland were lit up with a fireworks extravaganza as Kynren’s Pyromusical returned.

Thousands flocked to the County Durham town on Halloween for a night full of dazzling fireworks - and a few “spooky surprises”.

The event, which is now in its eighth year, is one of the most atmospheric nights out of the year, where colour-popping fireworks meet dramatic staging and choreography.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656946.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kynren Pyromusical *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Fire pits glowed as darkness fell on Bishop, with families gathering around the flames to keep warm while others popped to the witches’ village or went trick or treating.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656948.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kynren Pyromusical *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

The event culminated in a fantastic 25-minute display, with thousands in the audience watching the display light up the night sky.

Lucky visitors were this year able to enjoy a sprinkle of ghoulish fun with the event falling on Halloween too, with spooks - and pumpkins - scattered across the park.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656950.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kynren Pyromusical *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Families were able to enjoy an autumn bite to eat or drink too, with hot dogs, hot chocolates, and mulled wine all on the menu.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656952.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kynren Pyromusical *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

Pyromusical has become a staple in Kynren’s calendar outside its summer season, drawing crowds of 8,000 and regularly selling out.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656951.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Kynren Pyromusical *(Image: SARAH CALDECOTT)*

"It has become one of those events that people come back to year after year and we regularly sell every seat," a spokesperson for the park said.

"The setting makes a huge difference.

"Sitting in the Tribune, looking across the Kynren stage and then seeing the entire night sky come alive in time with the music is difficult to replicate anywhere else.

"Having so many people experiencing that finale together creates a pretty special atmosphere."
