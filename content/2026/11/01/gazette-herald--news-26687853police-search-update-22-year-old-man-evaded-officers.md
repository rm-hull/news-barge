A 22-year-old man who absconded from police during a hospital visit has been located and is back in custody, police have confirmed.

North Yorkshire Police issued an appeal on Saturday, October 31, to help find the man, who had evaded officers while receiving treatment at a hospital.

He has since been found and returned to police custody, the force said in a statement issued this morning.

A police spokesperson said: "Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal.

"It has now been removed."

The man had been arrested on Friday on suspicion of breaching a Domestic Violence Protection Order, police said earlier.

Police said he managed to leave the hospital grounds and believed he may have been in the Harrogate area at the time of his disappearance.
