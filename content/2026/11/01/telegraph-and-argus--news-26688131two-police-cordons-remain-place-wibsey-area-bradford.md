Two police cordons remain in place in the Wibsey area of Bradford this afternoon.

One cordon is on Reevy Crescent in Buttershaw, where officers remain at the scene around a property.

A second has been established nearby on Wibsey Park Avenue, outside Wibsey Park, where a section of pavement has been taped off.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656634.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cordon in place this afternoon. *(Image: T&A)*

The T&A understands the two cordons may be linked, although this has not been confirmed by West Yorkshire Police.

## The T&A visits both scenes

The cordon on Reevy Crescent was first reported by residents earlier this morning and remained in place when the T&A visited both locations on Sunday afternoon.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656635.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Officers at the scene this afternoon. *(Image: T&A)*

A local resident said: "We heard some shouting this morning and that was it really.

"There's always screaming and shouting around here anyway.

"I didn't know what had happened until I looked out the window and saw the cordon."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656638.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The cordon in place this afternoon. *(Image: T&A)*

At Reevy Crescent, a number of police vehicles were seen arriving and leaving the scene while officers and detectives carried out enquiries.

The cordon on Wibsey Park Avenue was smaller, with a single officer patrolling the area.

## Air ambulance heard in the area

Residents also reported hearing an air ambulance in the area.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656639.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The second cordon in place this afternoon. *(Image: T&A)*

Flight tracking website Flightradar24 suggests the Yorkshire Air Ambulance landed at nearby Fairfield Recreation Ground at 10.29am.

Tracking data suggests it departed a minute later, at 10.30am.

West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Ambulance Service have been contacted for comment.
