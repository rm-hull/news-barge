A man caught with a 20-inch kitchen knife after threatening to cut his own head off has narrowly avoided jail.

Police were called after Brian Wilson was spotted carrying the kitchen knife to a shop to buy more alcohol.

The 57-year-old is now receiving treatment to tackle his alcohol addiction, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Syed Ali said Wilson was heading to the Premier store in Willington with the knife by a member of the public who called the police.

He said: “Police found the defendant in the store and he has been taken outside where they have found the large knife, it measured 20 inches, in the front of the defendant’s coat.

“He told the officers that he would ‘cut his head off’ when he had finished his drink.”

Wilson, of Norwich Gardens, Willington, admitted possessing the knife after his arrest on May 11.

His lawyer, Elizabeth Aisbitt, said Wilson had self-harmed in the past when drinking heavily.

She said: “He has no intention of causing injury or distress to anybody else but he accepted he took the knife out of his home.

“He is undergoing a managed reduction programme which will eventually achieve abstinence.”
