An award-winning and highly-regarded village pub near York pub is back on the market freehold.

The White Bear Inn in Stillington, a two-storey property dating back to the 1700s, is being offered for sale at £625,000 freehold.

The pub came to market in November 2025 at £985,000 freehold nearly two years ago, before it was taken off the market, leaving just a leasehold option available for £59,950.

Now, Barry Crux & Co of York are marketing the freehold for £625,000, but this does not include a two-bedroomed bungalow at the back, which is available separately.

The sale follows owners Phil and Sue Robinson taking on the lease of The White Bear Inn in 1995 and purchasing it freehold in 1998. They have decided it is time to retire.

In their 30-odd years, the popular couple have built up a thriving venue noted for delicious pub classics and Sunday lunch. It is a regular CAMRA award winner, which this year saw it declared Town and Country Pub of the Year for 2026.

TripAdvisor awards The White Bear 4.7 stars out of five, based on 342 reviews, ranking it first out of two restaurants in Stillington. Google also awards 4.7 stars out of five, based on 406 reviews.

Barry Crux estate agents said: "A historic end of terrace two storey property with colour washed elevations under a pitched tiled roof.

"The ground floor accommodates the main trading spaces, which are served by a central bar server to the public bar and restaurant areas.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21653026.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The interior of The White Bear at Stillington *(Image: Barry Crux & Co)*

"This is fitted in a traditional country inn style and offers warm and inviting surroundings."

Originally a stopping point for coaches travelling between Leeds and Helmsley via York, the pub remains a long-established business with a strong net turnover despite limited trading hours.

The property also includes three to four-bedroom owner's accommodation.

The agents added: "At the rear there are further outside ancillary spaces including the upland beer cellar, cold store, dry store and freezer room."

Attached to the external ancillary areas is a single one-bedroom ensuite letting room. Separate from this is a two-bedroom bungalow, which may be available to purchase by separate agreement at market value.
