TG Jones is set to close its store at Teesside Park.

The national chain, formerly WHSmith, will be bringing down the shutters at its store at the popular shopping park, new signs have confirmed.

A closing down sale has been launched, with huge yellow signs on the glass windows of the store saying it is closing down and that “everything must go.”

The closure means that TG Jones will no longer have a presence in Stockton, with the nearest branch being Middlesbrough’s Cleveland Centre.

The firm is also set to shut its store at the Cornmill Shopping Centre in Darlington, as well as the branch’s Post Office service, in a matter of weeks on November 28.

The news comes months after TG Jones confirmed it would be closing around 150 stores after securing court approval for a major restructuring plan.

At the time, it said the closures were part of a wider plan to rescue the business from insolvency, with hundreds of jobs affected as a result.

Tom Smith KC, representing TG Jones in court, said at the time that the company had suffered from "long-term sales decline" due to high inflation, increased online shopping, lower consumer spending, and rising labour costs.

Mr Smith said the rebranding from WHSmith to TG Jones also negatively impacted sales.

Chief executive of TG Jones, Alex Willson, said it would make TG Jones a “stronger, more sustainable business."

Teesside Park, which is currently celebrating its 35th anniversary, is currently operating at full occupancy following the recent openings of Office and Oliver Bonas.

The two brands took over from Charles Clinkard and Pro Cook respectively, which both pulled down the shutters earlier this year before the units were revamped and reopened.
