One person has been taken to hospital after a stabbing in Stockton.

Police and paramedics were called to Hawthorne Road, in the Newtown area, on Saturday (October 31) after a person was injured.

Pictures online showed a fleet of police cars parked on the street following the incident, which took place just before 6.30pm.

Reports suggest armed police were also in attendance, although this has not been confirmed and Cleveland Police has been contacted for more details.

The North East Ambulance Service (NEAS) has confirmed that it took one person to the James Cook University Hospital in Middlesbrough. Their condition remains unknown.

One 77-year-old, who lived close to the street, said she saw a police van go down the road at around 6pm yesterday evening but didn’t know what had taken place.

The woman, who has lived in her home for 26 years, said: “I just saw the police van, I didn’t see anything else going on.

“Day to day it is not that busy around here, you get the teenagers, the naughty boys, but that is about it.”

The resident argued that it “used to be nice” to live in the area but that it has changed in recent years: “It has changed because of the drugs.

“They sit at a lamppost dealing when kids are around. They used to do it on my corner [of the road] but I would ring the police.”

While other residents remained tight lipped, one man said he saw the 999 activity: “I saw the blue lights, it was last night at around 8pm. I didn’t see what was there.”

A NEAS spokesperson said: "We were called to an incident on Hawthorne Road, Sunderland at 6.30pm on October 31.

“We dispatched a double crewed ambulance, a clinical team leader, the medical car from Great North Air Ambulance and the medicar - a car which is staffed by Cleveland Police officers and a paramedic.

“One patient was taken to James Cook Hospital."
