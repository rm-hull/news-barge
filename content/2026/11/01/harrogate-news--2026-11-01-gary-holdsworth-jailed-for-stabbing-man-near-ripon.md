Gary Holdsworth has been jailed for six years and six months after stabbing a man in the neck outside the George and Dragon Inn in Melmerby, near Ripon.

Gary Holdsworth, of Melmerby, was sentenced at Leeds Crown Court on 30 October 2026 after being found guilty of wounding with intent and possessing a knife in a public place.

The incident happened at around 11.41pm on Thursday 4 December 2025 at the George and Dragon Inn in Melmerby.

A verbal argument over payment became physical and continued outside the pub, where Holdsworth stabbed the 46-year-old victim in the neck.

The victim’s injuries were not life-threatening, but he required surgery.

Holdsworth fled the scene but was arrested at his home shortly afterwards.

He pleaded not guilty on 28 April 2026 and was found guilty by a jury on 4 August 2026. He remained on remand until sentencing.

## Impact on the victim and his family

In a statement to the court, the victim’s partner described him as kind, loving and caring, and said he had nearly lost his life in the attack.

She explained that the incident had changed her life, leaving her experiencing fear, anxiety and worry. The effects had extended to the whole family, affecting their sense of safety, peace and ability to live normally.

## Police response

Detective Chief Inspector Cheryl Quinn, of North Yorkshire Police, said the sentence held Holdsworth accountable for his actions.

She highlighted the risk of knife violence causing life-threatening harm and its devastating consequences, adding that police continued to work to prevent knife crime.

A 41-year-old man who was also charged in connection with the incident was found not guilty of affray.
