A North Yorkshire chippy has been shortlisted as one of the top 20 in its class in a UK wide competition.

The Fish Bank in Low Street, Sherburn-in-Elmet has been announced as one of the semifinalists in the category of Takeaway of the Year the National Fish and Chip Awards 2027.

Organised by the National Federation of Fish Friers (NFFF), the competition highlights excellence in the industry, and this year’s semi-finalists are featured on a newly-released interactive map to help fans locate their nearest contender.




Andrew Crook, president of the NFFF, said: "Whittling down the longlist of 40 to our Takeaway of the Year top 20 has been a very difficult task.

"Every one of them brings their own unique qualities, strengths, and something extra special to the table.

"They should all be immensely proud of what they have achieved."

Organisers have put up an interactive map of all the remaining chippies in this category on their website.

The judging process examines product knowledge, food sourcing, preparation, hygiene, customer service, and dedication to promoting Britain’s iconic takeaway.

Mr Crook said: "All those who make it through each stage of the competition are setting the benchmark for what can be achieved through passion, commitment and the determination to do things to the highest possible standards."

The final 10 chippies will be revealed in December, ahead of the awards ceremony on 24 February at the Park Plaza Westminster Bridge, London, where the new winner will take centre stage as Takeaway of the Year.
