An East Yorkshire pub is undergoing a £250,000 refurbishment and will reopen with a packed weekend of entertainment.

The Tiger Inn, a Grade II listed pub in Driffield, is being revamped by Punch Taverns and will relaunch under the management of Charles Barber-Peat and his husband Simon, who together have more than 20 years of experience in the pub trade.

Mr Barber-Peat said: "We have been in Driffield for 1 year and currently love the town, the dynamics of its people and the community spirit where we have already seen so many events happening throughout the year.

"The Tiger Inn is currently having a £250,000 refurbishment through the brewery Punch Taverns, which will bring a new lease of life to the well established, grade 2 listed building, enticing colours that bring back the old school pub with a new modern twist including colours of burgundy, pastel greens and burnt oranges."

The plans also include a revamped beer garden to the rear, designed to be more accessible and suitable for events year-round.

The couple hope to turn the Tiger Inn into a true community hub.

Mr Barber-Peat said: "We plan to bring new ideas to the town, allowing people of all walks of life, ages, backgrounds and cultures to enjoy and be part of what we want to create as a community hub.

"New ideas include: Weekly live entertainment, fundraising events for both local and national charities, sporting events and weekly clubs to be a part of.

"We want to bring new ideas and some innovation to the pub that will both entice old and new customers to the pub."

The Tiger Inn will focus solely on drinks, with no food offering.

The pub is set to reopen on Monday, November 2, with a grand opening weekend planned for November 6, 7, and 8.

Mr Barber-Peat said: "On the Friday we will have a live singer from 8pm until late.

"On the Saturday there will be a fireworks display, fire breathers, stilt walkers and chances for people to win free prizes throughout the day.

"On Sunday, to commemorate Remembrance Sunday, we will also be hosting a live 1940s war memorial show with a live singer also."
