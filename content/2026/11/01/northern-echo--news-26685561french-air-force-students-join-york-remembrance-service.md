A wartime bond between North Yorkshire and France will be remembered when French Air Force students and officers join hundreds of people for a special Remembrance service.

They will take part in ceremonies at the Yorkshire Air Museum and in Elvington village on November 8.

The museum stands on the former site of RAF Elvington, a Second World War bomber base that was home to two squadrons of French airmen, and the village has marked their sacrifice for nearly 70 years.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653198.jpg?type=mds-article-620)French Air Force students and officers will join Remembrance services at Yorkshire Air Museum and Elvington, honouring RAF Elvington’s wartime bond. *(Image: Supplied)*

French students and officers from the Ecole des pupilles de l’air in Grenoble will be joining the contingent of relatives of French airmen making the journey this year.

The event also marks the 80th anniversary of the Association des Anciens et Amis des Groupes Lourds, formed in December 1946 by veterans of the French squadrons stationed at RAF Elvington.

The day's events will begin in Elvington village at around 10.15am with a parade of Scouts, Guides, Cubs, and Brownies assembling on the village green.

After a short address from the parish clergy, the parade will proceed through the village to the French Memorial, where the ceremony will take place.

Between 200 and 300 people are expected to join the procession, including families, residents, and visitors.

Dignitaries attending will include Chris Blundell, Deputy Lieutenant of North Yorkshire; Colonel Emmanuel Emond, Air Attaché at the French Embassy; Colonel David Holmes, Air Advisor at the Canadian High Commission; and Flight Lieutenant Michelle Forrester of the Australian Air Force.

A spokesman said: "We are honoured to be represented at these important ceremonies once again."

Representatives from RAF Leeming and RAF Fylingdales are also expected to attend, and the Standards of the RAF Association, York, and Royal British Legion, York, will be presented.

The Yorkshire Air Museum’s service will begin at 2pm in the Station Chapel.

It will be led by the museum’s honorary chaplain, the Reverend Charles ‘Taff’ Morgan MBE RAF (Retd), and assisted by the Reverend Mike Beresford, curate of the Elvington Three Rivers Parish.

A wreath-laying ceremony will follow in the Memorial Garden.
