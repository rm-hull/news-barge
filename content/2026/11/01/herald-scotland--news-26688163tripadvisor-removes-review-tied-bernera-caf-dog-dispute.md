**A Tripadvisor review posted by an American woman involved in the "Bernera café dog dispute" in the Western Isles has been removed after the platform ruled it breached its hate speech policies.**

The review was left by Claire Kellerman, who has become a controversial figure locally through her campaign to take her dog into premises where animals are not permitted and a £10,000 legal action connected to a community-run café in Bernera.

Ms Kellerman is currently pursuing legal action against Bernera Community Association, which operates the café at Bernera Community Centre. The dispute follows a long-running disagreement over access rules relating to her dog.

Her latest dispute involved Stornoway's Blue Lobster Café, where owner Jayne Macritchie was among a small number of business owners willing to publicly discuss Ms Kellerman's "repeated attempts to enter the premises with her dog".

Ms Kellerman later allegedly posted a highly critical review of the café on Tripadvisor, alleging discrimination and making several other complaints. The post drew a swift reaction online, including criticism from comedian Stuart Kennedy, and prompted a wave of responses from supporters of the café.

Rather than damaging the business's reputation, the controversy appeared to generate further backing for the Blue Lobster. The café, which already had a strong rating on Tripadvisor, received a surge of five-star reviews from customers praising both its food and hospitality.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21656716.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Part of the review on Tripadvisor *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

Tripadvisor has now taken down the review, deeming it "hate speech", according to the Stornoway Gazette.

Comedian Kennedy says in his video on the review that "using the owner's name goes against Tripadvisor policy", which hints at a possible reason for its removal.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21656717.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A message now states the review has been removed *(Image: Tripadvisor)*Ms Kellerman, adopted daughter of Hollywood M\* A\*S\* H actress Sally ‘Hot Lips’ Kellerman, says being told to take her Australian cattle dog, Tarkina, out of the Bernera Community Association’s café, amounted to discrimination against a disabled person.

She claims she has disabilities linked to years of trauma which, although not immediately visible, mean she must have her dog - which she says has undergone extensive training - with her at all times.

Her action, accompanied by allegations of bullying, raised voices across the café floor and suggestions of a culture clash between her American background and Hebridean neighbours, has ruffled feathers right across the tight-knit community of just 250 people and far beyond.

Firstly, it targeted the wrong organisation – originally Ms Kellerman’s writ claimed £10,000 in damages from Great Bernera Community Development Trust, wrongly treating them as operators of the small café.

Only after the Trust racked up around £7,000 in legal expenses did it accept it had nothing to do with running the café.

It then transpired that although the Trust was awarded expenses in their favour, because **Ms Kellerman has been given Legal Aid,** they would receive nothing in return.

A procedural hearing relating to the legal case is due to take place at Stornoway Sheriff Court in February.
