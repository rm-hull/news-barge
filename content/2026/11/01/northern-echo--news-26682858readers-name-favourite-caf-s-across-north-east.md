Whether it's a perfectly poured flat white, a towering slice of cake or a hearty breakfast, North East residents are passionate about their favourite cafés.

We asked readers of The Northern Echo to tell us which café deserves a spot at the top of the region's must-visit list, and plenty were quick to share their recommendations.

From farm shops and independent coffee houses to seaside favourites, these are the North East venues readers say are among the best for a drink and bite to eat.

## Broom Mill Farm Restaurant & Café

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21582216.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

Among the most popular recommendations was Broom Mill Farm Restaurant & Café.

Near Durham, Broom Mill Farm is known for its countryside setting, homemade food and farm shop, drawing customers for breakfast, lunch and afternoon treats.

## Echo 3 Coffee House & Roastery

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/20274342.jpg?type=mds-article-620) *(Image: ECHO3)*

Coffee lovers were quick to praise Echo 3 Coffee House & Roastery, with readers highlighting its quality coffee and impressive selection of cakes.

The independent business has developed a loyal following thanks to its freshly roasted coffee and wide range of sweet treats.

Echo 3 is Star Wars-themed, with memorabilia displayed throughout its cosy coffee shop in Clark's Yard, which can be accessed from Skinnergate or High Row.

## Peggottys, Wolsingham

Another favourite among readers was Peggottys in Wolsingham.

Situated in the heart of Weardale, the café is well known for its welcoming atmosphere and menu of homemade favourites.

## The Auckland Cupcake Co.

Those with a sweet tooth may want to take note of The Auckland Cupcake Co., which was recommended for its cakes and baked treats.

The County Durham business has become a popular stop for customers looking for a dessert or a special indulgence.

## Rising Café, Hexham

Over in Northumberland, Rising Café in Hexham also received a mention.

The café has earned a strong reputation for its food, drinks and friendly atmosphere, attracting both locals and visitors to the historic market town.

## Rachie's, Hartlepool Headland

Representing the coast was Rachie's at Hartlepool Headland.

The café is a popular stop for residents and visitors exploring the area, with its seaside location helping to make it a firm favourite.

## Have we missed your favourite?

These are just some of the cafés highlighted by readers, with many more suggestions continuing to be shared online.

Whether you prefer artisan coffee, homemade cakes, a traditional breakfast or a scenic setting, the North East is packed with independent cafés worth discovering.
