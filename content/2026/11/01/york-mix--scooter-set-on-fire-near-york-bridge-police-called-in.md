Police are investigating an apparent arson attack on a scooter in York today (Sunday).

Fire crews from Acomb were called to Clifton Bridge, Water End, at 1.20pm after smoke was reported in the area.

On arrival, they found a petrol scooter on fire. The blaze was put out using knapsack sprayers and small tools.

#### Clifton Bridge, York

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “It is believed to be deliberate and was left in the hands of the police on scene.”
