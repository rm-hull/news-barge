## Alfie puts his paw in it as meeting with editor takes unexpected turn

Alfie the Cavapoo in the board room at the YO1 Radio office. *(Image: Wayne Chadwick)*

**This is the first of a new column Wayne's World written by Wayne Chadwick, managing director of YO1 Radio and owner of The York Roast Co.**

There are some things in life that come as a bit of a surprise.

Being asked to write a column in a local newspaper with such a distinguished history is certainly one of them.

I’m so grateful to The Press for giving me the opportunity and hope they don’t regret it, particularly given my first meeting with the new editor.

YO1 Radio and Media and The Press have been on great terms for over four years now and we thought it would be a good idea to invite the editor and commercial director for a catch-up at our studios on James Street.

These days I am hardly apart from my 10-month-old Cavapoo Alfie.

On weekdays I often take him with me to work, and the rest of the team love his visits.

When I’m on-air, he sits on my feet in the studio.

When the microphone is on Alfie sometimes contributes to the show with a few timely yaps depending on where his attention has taken him.

Listeners are now used to hearing Alfie in the background and frankly I don’t think it matters.

So, Alfie was with me for our meeting.

There were five of us there, plus Alfie.

He was in his usual position on the table, something that has become regular practice in my own office where he will jump on a guest chair and onto my desk to lie down whilst occasionally resting a paw on my PC keyboard to remind me that he is still there but, otherwise, he’s good as gold.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21638915.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Alfie the Cavapoo in the board room at the YO1 Radio office. *(Image: Wayne Chadwick)*

In our meeting with The Press, though, Alfie decided to up his game!

No lying down, just strutting around on the meeting room table, and occasionally licking the face of the new editor.

I wasn’t sure how Mr Hardy would react.

There was a brief smirk and then when Alfie went back for seconds a full-blown laugh out loud by all.

But it didn’t end there.

Alfie soon became bored with the meeting and decided to climb down from the table and unceremoniously drop a ‘parcel’ on the floor right next to the commercial director.

There are no words.

That could have been the end – but here we are.

Some relationships are clearly made to last!
