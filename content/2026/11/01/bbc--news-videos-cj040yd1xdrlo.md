# Watch: Heidi Klum as giant severed hand for Halloween bash

The supermodel and TV host Heidi Klum continued her pose as the queen of Halloween by transforming into a giant, bloodied and severed hand for her annual costume party on 31 October.

Klum, 53, was completely unrecognisable arriving at her event in what she described as her own chopped-off "left hand".

This year was the 25th anniversary of the party, which has provided viral pop culture moments over the years, like when Klum dressed up as a worm in 2022.
