Buying a house is a huge thing to do so it is important that you pick the right place to call home.

The Telegraph, with some help from estate agent Strutt & Parker, has released a list of the most desirable places to buy a home across the UK.

From city spots to seaside resorts, the list features something for everyone depending on what they prioritise in where they live.

North Tyneside was revealed as the best place to buy a home in the North East.

## Why is North Tyneside a desirable place to buy a home?

On the area, The Telegraph said: “Sandwiched between Newcastle upon Tyne and the North Sea, North Tyneside offers the best of both worlds.

“The coastline is blessed with sandy Blue Flag beaches, so, unsurprisingly, water sports are a big part of life there, from surfing and paddleboarding to open-water swimming, according to Jason Roberts, of Strutt & Parker.

“Yet the bright lights of Newcastle upon Tyne are less than 30 minutes away by metro.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21645575.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The area has lots of coastal spots to visit. *(Image: Getty)*

“Good schools, more affordable prices than Newcastle’s suburbs and proximity to Northumberland also help make this area suitable for families looking to settle, Roberts adds.

“The seaside communities of Whitley Bay, Cullercoats and upmarket Tynemouth are firm favourites, while North Shields, a fishing town on the River Tyne, has an up-and-coming feel.

“It’s a family-friendly borough with a strong community spirit.

“It’s the kind of place where you say ‘hello’ to people when you pass them on the street,” says Roberts. “It’s very neighbourly as well. You’ll find that most people are very willing to muck in.”

The average price of a house in North Tyneside was £251,340 over the last year, according to Rightmove.

The majority of properties sold in the area were terraced houses that cost an average of £244,704.

Semi-detached properties cost around £259,594 while flats cost around £143,765.

North Tyneside is made up of different areas to explore, from North Shields and Wallsend to Tynemouth and Whitley Bay.

Both Whitley Bay and Tynemouth are home to beautiful beaches that are not only great for sunbathing in the summer but also for family walks in the autumn and wintertime.

For those who love history, you can visit the Tynemouth Priory and Castle and learn more about the area’s maritime connections.
