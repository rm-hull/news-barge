THE Bradford Bulls have confirmed that MNA Group will continue their sponsorship into the 2027 season.

The company's branding will be prominently displayed on the rear of the Bulls' playing shorts, replica shorts, and training wear, as well as on the back of the club's Las Vegas strip, which will be officially unveiled in January.

The partnership aligns with the club's efforts to expand its commercial connections.

MNA Group has been an active supporter of the Bulls and the wider Bradford community, assisting in various local initiatives.

These include their role in establishing the club's community hub, Harry's Bar.

Ian Stafford, the Bulls commercial manager, said: "We’re thrilled to be continuing our relationship with MNA Group into the 2027 season.

"Richard and the team have been fantastic supporters of the club, and we're delighted to be expanding their presence across our playing shorts, replica range, and training wear.

"Their support of the wider club and community, including their contribution towards Harry’s Bar, has been greatly appreciated.

"We’re looking forward to another exciting year working together."

Richard Bloodworth, operations director at MNA Group, shared his enthusiasm for the continued partnership.

He said: "We’re extremely proud to continue our partnership with Bradford Bulls for the 2027 season.

"Since joining the Bulls family, we’ve developed a fantastic relationship with the club and have enjoyed being part of its journey.

"Supporting a club with such a rich history and strong connection to its community is something we value enormously.

"We’re excited to see our branding feature across the playing and replica ranges, training wear, and the upcoming Las Vegas strip, and look forward to another successful season together."

This ongoing partnership is a testament to the shared commitment of supporting the Bulls on and off the field.
