A historic County Durham castle estate famous for its roaming deer, sweeping parkland and centuries of history offers the perfect setting for an autumn walk.

Set on the outskirts of Staindrop, Raby Castle attracts visitors from across the North East throughout the year, but the arrival of autumn transforms the estate into one of the county's most picturesque destinations.

As leaves turn shades of gold, amber and red, the vast grounds provide a spectacular backdrop for a leisurely stroll, with visitors able to enjoy views of the medieval castle, ancient trees and open parkland.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653192.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The entrance to Raby Castle near Staindrop, where visitors can explore historic grounds, parkland walks and one of County Durham's most famous landmarks. *(Image: AFPHOTOGRAPHY\_2025)*

Dominating the landscape is Raby Castle itself, one of the best-preserved medieval castles in England.

Built by the powerful Neville family in the 14th century, the fortress remains one of County Durham's most recognisable landmarks.

Surrounding the castle is a sprawling estate of parkland and woodland, where visitors can explore a network of walking routes while taking in the changing colours of the season.

One of the highlights is the historic Deer Park, home to red and fallow deer that roam freely across the estate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653150.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Dog walkers pass in front of the historic Raby Castle near Staindrop, with the estate attracting visitors throughout the year for its scenery and walking routes. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

The animals can often be seen grazing beneath mature trees or silhouetted against the castle's stone walls, creating scenes that draw photographers and wildlife lovers alike.

Autumn is regarded by many as the best time to visit, with the seasonal colours adding another dimension to an already impressive landscape.

Woodland areas become awash with colour while open stretches of parkland offer uninterrupted views across the estate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653076.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The formal gardens at Raby Castle offer visitors picturesque views across the historic estate near Staindrop. *(Image: Stuart Boulton)*

Families can enjoy accessible routes through the grounds, while visitors looking for a longer walk can spend hours exploring the wider estate and its historic features.

In recent years, Raby has also undergone significant investment through The Rising, a major development that has introduced new gardens, visitor facilities, food and drink venues, helping to cement its position as one of the region's leading visitor attractions.

The combination of history, wildlife and natural beauty has long made the estate a favourite with generations of County Durham residents.

Whether it's a chance to admire medieval architecture, spot deer among the autumn leaves or simply enjoy a peaceful walk through some of the county's finest scenery, Raby Castle offers plenty of reasons to visit this season.

For those searching for an autumn walk that combines heritage, wildlife and stunning views, few places in County Durham can rival the grounds of Raby Castle.
