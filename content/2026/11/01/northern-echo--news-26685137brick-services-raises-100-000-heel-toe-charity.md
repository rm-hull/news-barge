A North East business has raised more than £100,000 for a children’s charity after its latest annual fundraising ball brought in almost £12,000.

Brick Services, a Brickability Ltd business and part of BRCK Group, reached the milestone with its seventh charity event in support of Heel & Toe Children’s Charity.

This year’s ball raised £11,729, pushing the company’s total donations to the charity past the £100,000 mark.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21653006.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Brick Services at the ball in aid of the Heel & Toe Children’s Charity *(Image: RYAN LIM)*

Chris Millican, managing director at Brick Services, said: "Reaching more than £100,000 for Heel & Toe is something the whole team at Brick Services is incredibly proud of.

"This charity is very close to our hearts because we’ve seen first-hand how important the work they do is.

"Every year, we’re amazed by the generosity of our guests, customers and suppliers, and this year was no exception.

"To raise £11,729 in one evening and take our total beyond £100,000 is a fantastic achievem

ent.

"We’re delighted to be able to play our part in supporting Heel & Toe and the children and families who rely on the services they provide."

A further contribution from the BRCK Foundation helped the team surpass the six-figure total.

The charity receives no government funding and relies on donations and fundraising to continue its vital work.

Brick Services works with architects and specifiers, as well as national and regional house builders across the North of England, providing expert advice on brick, stone, blocks and cladding, alongside additional product guidance and material matching.

The company’s annual charity ball brings together customers, suppliers, colleagues, and friends for a night of entertainment and fundraising.

It has supported the charity every year since it began.

The event has become a highlight of the company’s annual calendar.

Brick Services said it plans to continue supporting Heel & Toe through future fundraising efforts.
