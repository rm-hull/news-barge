A CAREER adviser has completed his 21st humanitarian aid trip.

Nazim Ali, from Bradford, travelled to Malawi in October as part of a self-funded trip with the Nazim Ali Foundation.

He worked in partnership with Keighley-based charity Green Crescent Aid UK to deliver critical support to some of the country’s most vulnerable communities.

Mr Ali said: “It was heartwarming to see the villages come out to greet us in a parade to show their happiness and gratitude.

“Witnessing this was a poignant reminder of why I do what I do, taking annual leave from work and self-funding my travel.”

During the nine-day visit, Mr Ali and his team distributed 900 food parcels across 12 villages, delivered more than 2,900 hot meals, and handed out children’s treat bags and baby food sachets.

They also donated live goats and cash to beneficiaries.

He raised £17,010 for the trip, exceeding his original target of £10,000.

Each food parcel, valued at £35, included essentials such as maize flour, rice, sugar, oil, and soap.

He has raised more than £1.6 million for charitable causes both in the UK and abroad.
