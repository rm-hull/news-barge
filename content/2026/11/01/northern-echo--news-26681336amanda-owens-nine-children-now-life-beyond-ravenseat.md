Amanda Owen's nine children have grown up in front of television audiences, but their lives are increasingly taking different directions as the family enters another chapter.

The Yorkshire Shepherdess became a household name through Channel 5's Our Yorkshire Farm, which followed her life with Clive Owen and their children at Ravenseat in Swaledale.

Now, the family continues appearing in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, with the fourth series approaching its conclusion.

Their childhoods have been shaped by the responsibilities of living on a working hill farm, with animals, changing weather conditions and family life central to the programmes.

However, as the older siblings grow up, the latest series has highlighted how their interests and responsibilities are evolving.

Raven, the eldest daughter, has returned to Ravenseat during the fourth series, helping with work around the stables.

Her younger sister Edith has also been involved in the family's activities, while balancing her studies and preparing for examinations.

The programme has shown the two sisters working together to prepare the family's seasonal cream tea van, which serves walkers visiting the area.

Miles has continued to play a prominent role in farming and renovation work.

He has been seen helping Amanda restore Anty John's farmhouse, while also working alongside Clive on practical problems around Ravenseat.

Other members of the family have taken part in livestock rescues and the daily responsibilities that come with running a farm in the Yorkshire Dales.

Meanwhile, the younger children continue to enjoy the distinctive upbringing which first attracted viewers to the Owen family.

The fourth series has featured Easter celebrations, new puppies and the children caring for animals.

Although the siblings share a farming background, their different interests demonstrate that they are not necessarily all destined to follow the same path.

The restoration of Anty John's has also raised questions about the family's long-term living arrangements.

The neighbouring farmhouse has undergone extensive work over several years, with the aim of creating additional space for the family's future.

As the latest series reaches its final scheduled episode, the building is beginning to take shape as a home.

However, the precise plans for which family members might live there have not been fully confirmed.

There is also uncertainty surrounding the future of the family's television programme.

Series four of Our Farm Next Door concludes its scheduled run on Monday, November 2, but it remains unclear whether another episode or fifth series will follow.
