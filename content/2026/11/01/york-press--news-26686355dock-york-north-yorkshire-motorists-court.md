*(Image: Newsquest)*

Five York and North Yorkshire motorists are serving driving bans after their cases were among those heard at different magistrates courts.

Four cases were heard at Bradford Magistrates' Court.

Kate Alderson, 40, of Crossfield Crescent, Fulford, did not respond to a court summons and was convicted in her absence of failure of two offences of failure to tell police who was driving her car when it allegedly committed two traffic offences in West Yorkshire.

She was banned from driving for six months, fined £210 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £84 statutory surcharge.

Kieran O'Donnell, 51, of Oxmoor Lane, Biggin, did not respond to a summons and was convicted in his absence of speeding at 49mph in a 40mph zone in Leeds.

He was banned from driving for six months, fined £166 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs.

Stuart Lilly, 64, of Ascot Way, Acomb, pleaded guilty to driving at 43mph and 37mph in a 30mph zone at Walton near Tadcaster on separate days.

He was banned from driving for six months, fined £333 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £133 statutory surcharge.

Kieran O'Donnell, 51, of Oxmoor Lane, Biggin near Selby, was convicted in his absence of speeding at 49mph in a 30mph zone in Leeds.

He was banned from driving for six months, fined £166 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs.

Two cases were heard at Harrogate Magistrates' Court.

Vanessa Green, 43, of Doncaster Road, Whitley, near Selby, did not respond to a court summons and was convicted in her absence of failure to tell police who was driving her car when it allegedly committed an offence.

She was fined £660, ordered to pay £120 prosecution costs and a £264 statutory surcharge and given six penalty points.

Natalie Troke, 48, of Coney Hill, Kelfield, pleaded guilty to speeding on the A19 at Barlby.

She was banned from driving for six months, fined £126 and ordered to pay £120 prosecution costs and a £50 statutory surcharge.

White Rose Parkhomes, of Micklegate, York, did not respond to a court summons and was convicted in its absence at Lincoln Magistrates' Court of failure to inform police who was driving its vehicle when it allegedly committed a traffic offence in Lincolnshire.

It was fined £1,000 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £400 statutory surcharge.
