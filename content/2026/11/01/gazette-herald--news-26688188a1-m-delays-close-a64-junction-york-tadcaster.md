Long delays have been reported on a Yorkshire motorway.

The A1(M) southbound is partially blocked at junction 44 for York and Tadcaster this afternoon (Sunday, November 1) causing queuing traffic.

Delays of up to 11 minutes and increasing are reported southbound between junction 45 north of the A64 intersection with average speeds as low as 15 mph.

Beyond this junction, five-minute delays are affecting traffic going in the same direction up to junction 47 for the M1.

There are also delays approaching the A1(M) from Tadcaster and York from the A64, just after the turn off for the A162 and Sherburn-in-Elmet
