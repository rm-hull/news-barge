A tearoom located inside a former Victorian school in one of Northumberland's most picturesque villages has attracted hundreds of positive reviews, with its homemade food and cakes winning particular praise.

The White Monk Refectory and Tearoom is situated in the centre of Blanchland, a historic village surrounded by the North Pennines countryside.

Despite its relatively remote setting, the tearoom has developed a big following among visitors exploring the area, holding a rating of 4.4 out of five on Tripadvisor.

The venue has also received a Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice award for 2026, recognising businesses which consistently receive positive feedback from travellers.

However, it is the unusual location and traditional food which make the tearoom particularly distinctive.

The White Monk occupies the village's former Victorian school, which closed during the 1980s.

The building has since been transformed into a café while retaining much of its historic character.

Inside, visitors can enjoy breakfast, lunch or afternoon refreshments. The menu includes homemade soups, pies, sandwiches, quiches, cakes and traybakes.

Recent reviews suggest the food and setting are among the main attractions.

One customer, who visited with friends in June, discovered the tearoom after walking around Derwent Reservoir.

The reviewer described Blanchland as a "beautiful village" and praised the "warm welcome at the White Monk."

They enjoyed quiche, chicken pie and homemade orange cake, saying the visit had been so enjoyable that they also purchased cakes to take away.

Another reviewer was particularly impressed by the savoury dishes.

The customer described the corned beef pie as "to die for" and praised the locally sourced food and staff.

Another visitor, who stopped for lunch, praised the selection of meals and the attention received from staff.

They particularly enjoyed a cheese and ham panini, followed by a Belgian bun, and bought a slice of coffee and walnut cake to take home.

For some customers, the tearoom has become a regular stop when visiting the area.

One reviewer described it as a "must not miss" destination when visiting family.

They praised the selection of sandwiches, toasties, pies, quiches and homemade cakes.

The tearoom also offers outdoor seating, allowing visitors to enjoy refreshments in the village during warmer weather.

Dogs are welcome, making it a stopping point for people exploring the surrounding countryside.

But the café is not the only reason to visit Blanchland.

The village has a history stretching back to the 12th century, when Blanchland Abbey was established.

Much of the village's distinctive appearance comes from its historic stone buildings, narrow streets and traditional square.

Visitors can also explore the surrounding North Pennines landscape, with countryside walks and scenic routes close to the village.

The White Monk is based at The Old School, Blanchland, Northumberland, DH8 9ST.
