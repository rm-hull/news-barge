A runner has raised hundreds of pounds for charity after completing a 100km ultra marathon.

Sean Waller, 41, from North Shields, took on the St Cuthbert’s Way Ultra to support The People’s Kitchen in Newcastle, raising more than £750 - over £900 with Gift Aid - for the volunteer-led charity.

The ultra marathon begins in Melrose, Scotland, and ends on Holy Island, Northumberland, following the historic St Cuthbert’s Way and crossing the border between Scotland and England.

Mr Waller said: "My family and I think that the work The People’s Kitchen does is phenomenal and hugely important."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648951.jpg?type=mds-article-620)THE PK Runners (The Peoples Kitchen) *(Image: Provided)*

"I recently ran the 100km St Cuthbert’s Way Ultra to raise money for this amazing charity, and I hope this provides some support for the crucial work they do."

The People’s Kitchen runs 365 days a year, serving 300 hot meals daily and offering clothing, conversation, and a safe, welcoming environment.

The charity receives no government funding and relies entirely on public donations and the dedication of volunteers.

Mr Waller’s JustGiving page described how the basics of warmth, cleanliness, food, and companionship are luxuries that many of the charity’s "Friends" cannot take for granted.

He said: "When I was running, I kept thinking about why I was raising funds for the The People's Kitchen, it does such important work to support some of the most vulnerable people in our community,"

The St Cuthbert’s Way Ultra is a demanding physical challenge, featuring climbs and descents over its 100km distance.

Runners must reach the finish line on Holy Island before the tide comes in, within a strict time limit.

Mr Waller is one of many who have taken on endurance challenges to support The People’s Kitchen.

In the recent Great North Run, 35 volunteers raised £20,000 for the charity by completing the 13.1-mile course.

The organisation describes its guests as "Friends".

Mr Waller’s fundraising can be found on his JustGiving page, with donations supporting The People’s Kitchen.
