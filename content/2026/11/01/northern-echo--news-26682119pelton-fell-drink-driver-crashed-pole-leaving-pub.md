A drink-driver crashed into an electricity pole before fleeing the scene.

Amanda Harvey admitted drink-driving for the third time after being caught behind the wheel of her black Nissan.

The 37-year-old was arrested when police traced the car to her home address and she failed a breathalyser test, Newton Aycliffe Magistrates’ Court heard.

Prosecutor Syed Ali said Harvey had 113 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath after the crash on the A693 at High Handenhold, near Chester-le-Street, on January 25.

He said Harvey had two passengers in her car when she crashed into the pole after leaving a pub.

“The defendant has lost control, hit the electricity pole causing the airbags to go off,” he said.

A rear-seat passenger said he ‘felt the car come to a horrendous stop’ before the front airbags went off.

Mr Ali said Harvey had two similar convictions, the latest in 2022.

Harvey, of Little Burn Way, Pelton Fell, County Durham, admitted drink-driving and leaving the crash scene.

Harvey, who represented herself, was banned from driving for four years after admitting she was more than three times over the legal limit.

Chairman of the bench Colling Morris said: “This is the third offence for the same thing.”

Harvey was also given a 12-month community order and ordered to pay £199 in court costs.
