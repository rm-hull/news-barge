A new jewellery shop is set to open in York.

Charm After Charm is set to open its doors at 8-10 Grape Lane on **Saturday 7 November** from 10am.

The building was previously home to vintage clothing shop The Vintage Works.

Founded by Millie and her mum, Emma, Charm After Charm will be the perfect place to create your own personalised pieces of jewellery by browsing hundreds of different charms and accessories – to make something that’s unique to you.

“Our aim was to create something that wasn’t just about buying a piece of jewellery that somebody else had already designed for you,” said Millie, on the Charm After Charm Instagram page.

“The idea is really simple. You come in, you pick your base, then you choose your charms, and together we create something that’s completely yours.”

With Millie moving to York for university, she and her mum thought it would be the perfect time to open their shop in the city.

“We want Charm After Charm to be somewhere you can come in and have a look around, mix and match, make something for yourself, or create a gift for somebody else that showcases their story,” said Emma.

And for opening weekend, Charm After Charm are holding a special launch event!

Over the two days, visitors to Charm After Charm can get a first look at the York store, build your own personalised jewellery with the team, and enjoy free goodie bags when you spend a certain amount, a wheel of fortune with extra discounts, and commemorate your visit with the pink photo wall and phone booth.
