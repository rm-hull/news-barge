Thousands of residents have turned to a North East community hub for help with energy bills, household finances and other pressures as it celebrates its fourth anniversary.

The Utilita Hartlepool Energy Hub celebrated with customers, staff and community partners at an event on Thursday, October 29.

Since opening in 2022, the hub has offered residents free face-to-face help with issues including energy bills, benefits and budgeting.

Kristine Gorman, hub supervisor at the Utilita Hartlepool Energy Hub, said: "We're incredibly proud to be celebrating four years of supporting the Hartlepool community.

"The hub was created to be more than just a place where people can get help with their energy account; it's a safe, welcoming environment where people can access a wide range of support when they're facing challenges.

"Every day we see the difference that a friendly conversation, practical advice or a referral to additional services can make.

"I'd like to thank our customers, community partners and my fantastic team for helping us reach this milestone.

"We look forward to continuing to support local residents for many years to come."

A regular customer at the anniversary event described the hub as a “lifeline”.

They said: "The staff are always welcoming, they listen without judgement and they genuinely care about helping people find solutions.

"Whether it's advice about energy bills, support with forms or simply knowing there's somewhere to turn when things get difficult, the hub has made a real difference.

"Hartlepool is lucky to have this service in the heart of the community."

The Hartlepool hub is one of 12 Utilita Energy Hubs across the country providing practical support in local communities.

Alongside energy advice, the hub helps with benefits, budgeting and digital skills, and refers residents to partner organisations.

Staff work with charities and other groups to support residents’ wider wellbeing.

The hub also supports the wider community through partnerships and fundraising.

It has raised funds and awareness for organisations such as Hartlepool Ambulance Charity and Alice House Hospice.

The team also runs partnership events and volunteering initiatives.

By working with charities, the team aims to improve support for the most vulnerable residents.

Utilita said its energy hubs are open to everyone — not just its customers.

In December 2025, the network won the Customer Support Award at the Utility Week Awards, recognising its wider community role.
