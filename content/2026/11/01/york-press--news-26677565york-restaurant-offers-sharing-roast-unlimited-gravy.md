As the weather starts to get colder, you might be thinking of heading out on a Sunday for a cosy roast dinner.

The city of York is lucky to have loads of pubs and restaurants offering great roast dinners for you to try out.

The Corner Grill House has a range of menus to try out but is known for its sharing roast dinner.

If you do not fancy sharing your meal, you can also opt for a normal Sunday roast.

## What is the Corner Grill House like?

The Corner Grill House promises to use only the “best ingredients” available with its a la carte offering a range of starters and mains featuring fresh fish, chicken skewers, and steaks.

However, it might be worth visiting the restaurant for its Sunday roast where you can choose from beef, pork, and a meat-free roast.

If you cannot pick which meat you want to try, the sharing roast allows you to have both and is served with roast potatoes, honey-glazed carrots, tenderstem broccoli, Yorkshire puddings, pigs in blankets, and unlimited gravy.

Alongside the sharing roast, you also get a glass of wine or any bottle of beer.

The restaurant was visited by the food content creators, York Only Pans, who said: “Firstly, the value for money is incredible – a huge sharing board, unlimited gravy and a drink each for under £60.

“We’d normally be a little sceptical of a sharing platter because they can sometimes feel like quantity over quality, but that definitely wasn’t the case here.

“Every element was packed with flavour, from the honey-roasted carrots and garlic-buttered tenderstem to the crispy, fluffy roasties.”
