Greggs fans have been left disappointed after a popular menu item was dropped to make way for the Christmas range.

The Steak and Stilton Roll, which returned for the autumn menu in September after being last seen in 2019, has been removed once again as the festive selection launches.

Fans took to social media to express their disappointment.

One customer wrote: "Why can't we have festive bakes and steak and stilton?"

Another said: "Dropped to ma knees outside Greggs like I’d just been told ma van had failed its MOT."

Others simply said they were "gutted" by its removal.

The North East's most popular bakery chain, known for its sausage rolls and other baked goods, has not confirmed when the Steak and Stilton Roll will return to shops.

However, the Daily Express reported that a Greggs spokesperson said: "Our Steak and Stilton Bake is currently taking a break, but fans can rest assured it will return at a later date.

"In the meantime, we've welcomed our Christmas menu into shops this week, including the iconic Festive Bake."

The Steak and Stilton Roll features diced beef in a rich gravy with blue stilton cheese and grilled onions, encased in puff pastry.
