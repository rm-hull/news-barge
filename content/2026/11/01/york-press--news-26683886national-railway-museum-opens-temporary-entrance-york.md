**National Railway Museum opens temporary entrance in York**

CAN the Press claim a success? In August, a Press article described the long walk visitors faced reaching the National Railway Museum from the station with no shade or shelter en route.

Letter writers were equally critical.

One correspondent from the Wirral hoping to reach the museum said he'd given up part way.

Yet the Museum said they weren't considering providing an entrance nearer the station.

This week the NRM opened, guess what, a new temporary entrance!

Still a bleak and shelterless walk but a welcome five minutes shorter.

Surely it could have been done earlier?

So perhaps The Press feature encouraged this change of heart.

If so, well done.

NRM visitors will be grateful to you.
