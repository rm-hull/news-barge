# ‘My vote still matters’ - Americans weigh in on the power of their ballot

Do Americans still believe in the power of their vote?

As part of our Voter Voices series, the BBC has been speaking to Americans across the country about Tuesday's midterm elections. We asked voters how they feel about casting a ballot and whether they think their vote matters.
