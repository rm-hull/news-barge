As summer is truly over, you might be thinking of ways to entertain yourself during the autumn.

Exploring the UK’s countryside and enjoying a pub lunch in a cosy spot after could be the perfect way to spend the colder days.

Condé Nast Traveller has released its list of the most beautiful autumn road trips across the UK.

The publication says, “the beauty of the British countryside is that there’s something for everyone” and has picked spots across the UK to suit everyone’s tastes.

The Yorkshire Dales made it onto the list thanks to its “dramatic” landscapes and many famous spots to see.

## Why are the Yorkshire Dales perfect for an autumnal road trip?

On the Yorkshire Dales, Condé Nast Traveller said: “I may be biased as I live nearby but Yorkshire is gorgeous to visit all year round, especially during the autumn.

“It’s my favourite time of the year in the Yorkshire Dales as you get shimmering golden woodlands, dramatic open moorland landscapes, and the promise of spotting leaping salmon and perhaps even a rare red squirrel.

“The sunsets are breathtaking as the light filters through the foliage which creates a warm, fiery glow on the hills.

“Start at Bolton Abbey to explore Strid Wood, then take the winding roads towards Malhamdale, where you can walk Malham Cove, Gordale Scar, and Janet's Foss – ending at The Lister Arms in Malham for dinner and a pint.

“Stay in Grassington for the night, which is where the fictional Darrowby in All Creatures Great & Small is set.

“The next day, head north to the villages of Burnsall and Kettlewell, cross the Wensleydale Valley and stay in the market town, Hawes.

“For the final third day, head east towards Aysgarth Falls, then Cauldron Falls for an afternoon spent chasing waterfalls.”

The Yorkshire Dales has plenty of beauty spots to see and impressive natural places to explore.

Alongside the many waterfalls found in the area, there are a few castles to explore including Bolton Castle and Pendragon Castle that are perfect for history lovers.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21643123.jpg?type=mds-article-620)There are lots of restaurants and pubs to visit as well. *(Image: Getty)*

You might also want to stop off at the Bolton Abbey where you can enjoy riverside and woodland trails as well as looking at the abbey ruins and warming up with a coffee in the site's café.

The National Trust site, Fountains Abbey, is also worth putting on your road trip plan thanks to its beautiful gardens and ruins.
