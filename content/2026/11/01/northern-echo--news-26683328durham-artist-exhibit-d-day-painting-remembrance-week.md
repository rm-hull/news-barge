A County Durham artist has brought one of the most dramatic episodes of D-Day to canvas as part of a poignant tribute being displayed for Remembrance Week.

Denis Harry Fox, a military history artist from Durham City, will exhibit Liberating Europe at The Story from November 6 to 15, depicting 6th Airborne Division troops parachuting from Dakota aircraft during the airborne assault of June 6, 1944.

Mr Fox said: "The 12th (Yorkshire) Parachute Battalion formed part of the 6th Airborne Division and captured Ranville despite its scattered drop on D-Day, it held it against fierce German attacks as well as relieving glider borne British troops at Pegasus Bridge."

The painting also pays tribute to Brigadier Nigel Poett KCB, DSO & Bar, who commanded the 5th Parachute Brigade during the Normandy landings.

A Durham Light Infantry officer, Brigadier Poett was tasked with leading the airborne assault to capture two vital bridges over the River Orne, securing the left flank of the Allied invasion.

He was among the first senior officers to reach Pegasus Bridge and link up with the glider troops.

Mr Fox’s work honours the bravery and sacrifice of those involved in the operation.
