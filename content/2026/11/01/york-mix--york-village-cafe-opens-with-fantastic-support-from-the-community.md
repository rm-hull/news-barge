A new cafe has opened in Copmanthorpe this week – and it’s a hidden gem worth finding.

The Hidden Cafe opened at 1b Church Street, at Copmanthorpe in York, on Tuesday (27 October), and is serving up breakfasts, lunches, hot drinks and sweet treats.

The building was previously home to cafe and bistro Anteiku, which the Hidden Cafe owner, Ruth Green, used to work at.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hidden-cafe-copmanthorpe-4.webp)

Ruth has lived in Copmanthorpe for over 40 years, and used to be a makeup artist on Look North – working closely with the likes of Harry Gration, Christa Ackroyd, Amy Garcia and Keely Donovan – before being made redundant during lockdown.

That didn’t stop Ruth though; after being made redundant Ruth started her own baking business called Yummy Treats which has been hugely successful in and around Copmanthorpe.

But Ruth missed the sociable aspect of talking to people, which is when she started working at Anteiku – when she heard the cafe was up for sale, she took the plunge and purchased it.

It took five months to get the keys, and then followed a week of makeover, and the cafe is now open for business with “fantastic support” from the local community.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hidden-cafe-copmanthorpe-3.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hidden-cafe-copmanthorpe-2.webp)

Hidden Cafe is open Monday to Saturday, 8:30am to 3pm with food served between 9am and 2pm.

The varied menu includes toasted sandwiches like Honey Glazed Ham (£9) and Maple Bacon and Brie (£9.50), jacket potatoes (from £6.50), classic cold sandwiches (£4.75), pancake stacks (from £5.50), full English (£9.95), and of course tasty bakes made by Ruth.

Hot drinks include coffee, using Ueshima Japanese roasted beans, tea, hot chocolate, and soft drinks.
