**A crowd of thousands gathered for the Samhuinn Fire Festival in Edinburgh on Halloween.**

Described as a “modern re-imagining of an ancient Celtic festival”, organisers said it marks the transition from summer to winter.

The event on Calton Hill featured “fire-play”, performance and drumming as 5,000 spectators turned out to watch the spectacle.

Organisers said it tells the story of winter overthrowing summer, with a “dramatic stand-off” between the Summer and Winter Kings.

At the heart of this year's festival stood the Cailleach, the ancient Crone and Goddess of Winter, guiding audiences through a celebration of darkness, transformation and the wisdom found in letting go.

As the Summer and Winter Courts came together, the performance invited audiences to consider Winter as a necessary time of rest, reflection and regeneration rather than something to fear or overcome.

Under the theme Walk Beside the Darkness, this year's festival explored the courage it takes to embrace change, the sacrifices that make renewal possible, and the importance of holding life and death with equal reverence. As the wheel of the year turned, audiences were reminded that even in the darkest season, the seeds of new life wait beneath the earth.

Jenny Bloom, Chair of the Beltane Fire Society, said: “We’re delighted to once again welcome Samhuinn to Edinburgh, and thank our wonderful witnesses and volunteers for an incredible night!

"Though the current climate crisis continues to be of deep concern to ourselves and the natural world, we take the wisdom and strength of the Cailleach as we step into the darker months, and come together as a community. We look forward to meeting again at Beltane!"

Cailleach, a Celtic representation of the Goddess, or Divine Hag, decides each King's fate and ushers in the coming winter months.

The festival was held at Calton Hill for the first time in 2018 after previously taking place on the Royal Mile.

Organisers preparing for the event on Saturday morning told The Herald that they had called off rehearsals because of Storm Boelo last weekend.
