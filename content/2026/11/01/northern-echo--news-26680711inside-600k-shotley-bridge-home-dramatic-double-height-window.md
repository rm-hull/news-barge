Timeless elegance meets modern comfort in this detached home with stylish interiors and a private, low-maintenance garden that’s tucked away in a small exclusive cul-de-sac in the sought-after village of Shotley Bridge.

Stone flooring and a striking stone feature wall ensure the porch – which has ample space for the whole family’s shoes, boots and coats – makes a characterful first impression.

From here glass doors open into a 28ft hallway which is flooded with light from a dramatic double-height window.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646749.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Features like a double-height window in the entrance hall and a stylish family bathroom make sure this Shotley Bridge home stands out from the crowd. *(Image: Supplied)*

Another set of double doors opens into the dual-aspect lounge with oak flooring and a focal point brick fireplace housing a log burner perfect for creating a cosy atmosphere on winter evenings.

Also on the ground floor is a traditional-style kitchen with marble-topped fitted units, a range cooker, an integrated fridge/freezer and a breakfast area.

Alongside the kitchen sits a practical utility room.

Completing the ground floor are a dining room with French doors on to a patio; a cloakroom and a further reception room currently used as a home office but which could become a fifth bedroom if needed.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646744.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Features like a double-height window in the entrance hall and a stylish family bathroom make sure this Shotley Bridge home stands out from the crowd. *(Image: Supplied)*

A modern oak staircase rises to the first floor where there are four spacious bedrooms, including the principal bedroom with a dressing room and an en-suite shower room.

The family bathroom is a real treat, its large bath set within mirrored recesses.

There is also a walk-in shower, stone flooring and a generous cupboard.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21646743.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Features like a double-height window in the entrance hall and a stylish family bathroom make sure this Shotley Bridge home stands out from the crowd. *(Image: Supplied)*

The insulated boarded loft offers potential to extend.

To the front of the house the garden is laid mainly to lawn with a gravel path and stairs leading up to the house.

To the rear is a private garden, also laid mainly to lawn and with mature borders and a gravelled patio area plus a log store.
