A thief has struck at a supermarket in North Yorkshire and made off with more than £200 worth of stock.




North Yorkshire Police said it took place at the Sainsbury’s store in High Street, Northallerton on Friday, October 2.

North Yorkshire Police have released a CCTV image of a man it said may have information that will assist the force's investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to email [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) or visit the force website.

Alternatively, contact can be made by calling North Yorkshire Police on 101, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website  
Please quote reference 12260193608 when passing on information.
