A train-loving 12-year-old was given a trip to remember when he travelled in a railway cab from Darlington to Shildon.

Jack Jones then went behind the scenes at Shildon signal box, where he saw how signallers help keep trains running safely.

The Bishop Middleham youngster's passion for railways began when he visited Heighington signal box aged just three, almost a decade ago.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648222.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jack at Heighington aged three *(Image: Network Rail)*

Chris Thomas, operations manager for Network Rail, said: "It was a real pleasure to help arrange this very special railway experience for Jack after his visit all those years ago.

"It’s clear he has a real passion for the railway, so we wanted to make the day as memorable as we could.

"Being able to arrange the cab ride to Shildon and then show him around the signal box was fantastic, and it was even more special to have Mick there too.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648223.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jack with Mick *(Image: Network Rail)*

"We all hope it’s another railway experience that Jack will remember for many years to come."

Northern invited Jack to ride in the cab of the 11.23 service from Darlington to Shildon, where he watched driver team manager Adam Keech at work.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648224.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jack Jones, Northern driver team manager Adam Keech, Network Rail operations manager Chris Thomas *(Image: Network Rail)*

In Shildon, Jack toured the signal box, learned about a signaller’s duties and signed the train register, the official record of train movements.

His grandparents, Beryl and John Bell, joined Jack, who was reunited with former route operations manager Mick Hodgson, his first signal-box guide nine years ago.

Mr Hodgson retired two years ago but plans to stay in touch with the family.

Beryl Bell, Jack’s grandmother, said: "Jack has never forgotten that first visit when he was three, so for everyone to make such an effort to arrange another special day for him nine years later means a great deal to us.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21648221.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Jack signs the train register book at Shildon *(Image: Network Rail)*

"We're incredibly grateful to everyone at Network Rail and Northern who helped make the day possible."
