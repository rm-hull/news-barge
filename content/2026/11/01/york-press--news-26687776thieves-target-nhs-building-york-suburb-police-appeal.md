Thieves have targeted an NHS building in a York suburb.




North Yorkshire Police said a moped was stolen from the health service facility in Oak Rise, Acomb, between 4pm and 4.10pm yesterday afternoon (Saturday, October 31).

The force said the vehicle was recovered within 30 minutes in Fishponds Wood after officers responded.

Police are now appealing for information and are asking anyone who witnessed the theft, saw someone riding or pushing the moped, or has relevant CCTV, doorbell, or dashcam footage to come forward.

Anyone with information is asked to call 101 quoting job number 12260214488.
