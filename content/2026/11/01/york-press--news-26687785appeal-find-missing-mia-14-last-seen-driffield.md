**Update: In a statement issued this morning, Humberside Police said the missing 14-year-old girl has been found.The force thanked everyone who shared the earlier appeal.**

A 14-year-old girl has been reported missing after last being seen in Driffield.

Humberside Police said the teenager was last seen at around 11.30pm on October 31 in the Scarborough Road area of the East Yorkshire town.

Humberside Police said anyone who has seen the girl or has information about her whereabouts is asked to call 101, quoting log reference 17 of November 1, 2026.
