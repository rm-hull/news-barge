A County Durham man has appeared in court for stealing stealing £300 worth of scrap lead and copper.

Kenneth Yorke, of Tomlin Street, Shildon, appeared at Peterlee Magistrates' Court on October 26 after the theft at Sedbergh.

The court heard the offence took place on March 19.
