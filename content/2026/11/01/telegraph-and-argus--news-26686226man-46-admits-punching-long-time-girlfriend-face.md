A 46-year-old man punched his girlfriend in the face at her home, a court heard.

Ian Berrisford, of Goose Eye in Keighley, and his girlfriend had been in a relationship for three years after meeting on Facebook, heard Skipton Magistrates Court.

On the afternoon of August 12 this year, she had felt tired and gone to bed, leaving Berrisford downstairs and "not very happy".

He later came into her room where she was sitting on the bed and "out of nowhere" punched her once in the face, the court heard.

The punch caused her nose to bleed excessively and her left cheekbone was bruised and swollen.

Berrisford is said to have immediately apologised.

## Man arrested

A police officer called to the house on King Edward Street, Sutton-in-Craven, saw the woman's injured face and a blood stain on the bed.

When arrested, Berrisford told police "she was smacking me, so she got one back", the court heard.

Prosecutor, Leigh Morgan, said Berrisford had one previous conviction for assault when he was a youth.

There was no application for a restraining order, she added.

## 'Spontaneous single punch'

Berrisford, who admitted assault occasioning actual bodily harm, was remorseful for what he had done and accepted it had fallen outside acceptable self-defence, the court was told by his legal advocate.

The court heard in mitigation that the couple had argued and that he had reacted with a "relatively spontaneous single punch".

Berrisford's advocate added the woman had chosen not to go to hospital for treatment.

Berrisford was on methadone, was addressing substance misuse and wanted to get his life back on track.

He is due to be sentenced at Harrogate Magistrates Court on December 4.
