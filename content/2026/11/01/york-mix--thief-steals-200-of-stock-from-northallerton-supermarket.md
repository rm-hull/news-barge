Police have released this image after a theft from a Northallerton supermarket.

On Friday 2 October, stock worth more than £200 was stolen from Sainsbury’s on the High Street.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured on CCTV, as he may have information that will assist our investigation.”
