A Scottish Borders tartan specialist is expanding into Canada after acquiring a historic Highland dress business based in Toronto.

ScotlandShop, headquartered in Duns, will complete its acquisition of William Glen & Son today, November 1, extending its North American presence and adding two Canadian employees to its workforce.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21625389.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anna White, founder of ScotlandShop. *(Image: Supplied)*

Founded in 2002 by Anna White, ScotlandShop has grown from its Borders roots into an international retailer specialising in tartan, Scottish textiles, Highland dress and contemporary Scottish clothing.

The acquisition follows the launch of the company’s US operation in Albany, New York, and its recent purchase of House of Tartan.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21625388.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anna White, founder of ScotlandShop. *(Image: Supplied)*

ScotlandShop now employs 34 people across three countries, with shops in Edinburgh and Crieff alongside its Duns headquarters and international operations.

William Glen & Son has served customers in Canada since 1998, building a reputation for made-to-measure kilts, Highland dress, kilt hire and Scottish-themed clothing and gifts.

The business traces its Scottish origins to 1869, when it began as a small drapery shop in Callander.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21625390.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Anna White, founder of ScotlandShop. *(Image: Supplied)*

In a connection between the two businesses’ histories, White first met William Glen owner Mike Cantlay more than a year ago at the company’s registered offices in Callander, where the business originally began.

The existing William Glen team, including Highland dress specialists Darren Purse and Peter Campbell, will remain in place following the takeover.

Anna White, founder of ScotlandShop, said: “There is something particularly special about bringing William Glen into the ScotlandShop family.

“Both businesses have strong Scottish roots and a real passion for Highland dress, textiles and, most importantly, people.

“William Glen has built an exceptional reputation in Canada and we are delighted to be able to continue that tradition while bringing more of the ScotlandShop collection to Canadian customers.

“ScotlandShop has always been about people and communities.

“We are proud to continue the William Glen story and to help connect even more people in Canada with Scotland, its tartans and its heritage.”
