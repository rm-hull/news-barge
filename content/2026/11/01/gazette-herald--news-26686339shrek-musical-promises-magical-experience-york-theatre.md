A VOLUNTEER-RUN theatre company is bringing a beloved fairy tale to life on stage.

NETheatre York will present Shrek The Musical at Joseph Rowntree Theatre from November 20 to 28, promising audiences a magical, inclusive experience.

The company plans to transform the venue into a woodland setting, fully immersing audiences in the world of Shrek.

Steve Tearle, director of NETheatre York, said: "We are turning the JRTheatre into a forest for a total inclusive experience.

"You will be transported into the world of Shrek.

"This awesome musical gives everyone the chance to shine creating some fabulous moments."

The company, which recently won a YorkMix award for its production of Les Miserables, is entirely volunteer-run and prides itself on being inclusive and diverse.

Mr Tearle said: "We build confidence and inspire our cast to be the best they can be with no judgments.

"We have been overwhelmed with so many people got their chance to shine."

The production will feature 11 performances, with West End costumes and sets.

NETheatre York has also staged Les Miserables School Edition and Priscilla Queen of the Dessert this year.

The group will once again be supporting a number of charities through the production.
