# 'Six crashes' on Leeds roundabout in one day after signal failure

## Leeds roundabout blasted worried drivers

A Leeds roundabout has been blasted by worried drivers after "six separate collisions" in one day after a signal failure.

Drivers have hit out at the ELOR roundabout connecting the A64 York Road and the A6120 Leeds Outer Ring Road. A motorist took to an online community group warning the neighbourhood to be more cautious.

Matthew Davis wrote on Facebook group 'Crossgates - Yesterday Today & Tomorrow' about the incidents on the busy roundabout, on Saturday, October 31. He said: "A64 York Road/A6120 Leeds Outer Ring Road Signal Failure. Just be aware we’ve had six separate collisions at this roundabout today after a signal failure.

"Reminder be considerate, SLOW DOWN! and remember the A6120 through the centre of the roundabout must STOP and cross the A64 York Road only when clear!"

Other drivers were quick to chime in on his concerns on the recently constructed roundabout. Natalie Elizabeth hit out at the layout, she said: "I can’t believe this road layout was ever approved. Many times we’ve all said, if the lights ever go out. Drivers are screwed. Too many lanes inter-twining. Accidents waiting to happen...surely a roundabout would have been suffice??"

Leutha Blissett agreed, she said: "Fully grade separated would have been better where can just fly through the straights and peel off and merge of turns."

Helen Yardley said: "Awful layout, no need for the road to go through the middle at all." Nick James added: "They’ve been off most of the day and they fail quite regularly. Do we have to wait until Monday for them to be fixed and when are LCC going to do something about the regular failures?

"Half the roundabout is working and half isn’t so no wonder people are crashing - hopefully no one gets seriously injured though the night."
