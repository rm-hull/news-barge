EVERY day, critical decisions are made behind the scenes to keep the public safe.

For 22 years, Craig Chippindale has played a vital role in West Yorkshire Police’s contact centre. He currently works as a dispatch supervisor, overseeing a team that manages incidents and supports officers on the streets.

While call handlers are often the first point of contact for the public, dispatchers work behind the scenes to assess incidents, deploy officers, and maintain communication with teams in the field.

Mr Chippindale said: "One of my priorities is making sure my staff are looked after.

"If I can look after the team and make sure they’ve got everything they need to do their job, that ultimately helps them look after people in the best way, including officers and members of the public."

Dispatchers must juggle multiple tasks at once, responding to incidents on their screens while fielding requests from officers over the radio.

It can be a high-pressure environment, with exposure to distressing situations and fast-changing demands, but Mr Chippindale finds the work deeply meaningful.

He said: "Every report that comes to the police pretty much these days comes through the Contact Centre, so you will be involved in anything.

"There’s no two days the same."
