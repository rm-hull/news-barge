Bank customers are being warned about more branch closures taking place this month.

A total of 17 Lloyds and Halifax sites across the UK will close in November as part of ongoing plans to reduce physical branches in response to changing banking habits.

Two branches in the North East are among those affected.

Halifax and Lloyds, along with Bank of Scotland, are all under Lloyds Banking Group.

Lloyds said on its website: "We regularly review how our customers choose to bank with us.

"And, with most now using our app, online services or phone banking, we're closing our Wallsend branch."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21656418.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Lloyds, High St E, Wallsend *(Image: Google Maps)*

Halifax similarly said on its website: "We regularly review how our customers choose to bank with us.

"And, with most now using our app, online services or phone banking, we're closing our Redcar branch."

The full list of closures, including dates, is as follows:

### Lloyds Bank

* Accrington: November 3
* Wallsend: November 3
* Ilkeston: November 5
* Leicester, Melton Road: November 9
* Liverpool: November 9
* Wakefield: November 10
* Leicester, Leicester Road: November 11
* Worksop: November 11
* Harrogate: November 19
