Tributes have been paid to a “remarkable” York composer and musical director after his death aged 71.

Simon Webb, who lived in Haxby for several years, died on October 11 from natural causes.

Leading tributes, Kevin Short, a friend and colleague of Simon, said the musician was "one of the UK’s best".

“There are so many lasting memories of Simon, and I shall treasure them forever," he said.

Kevin said Simon, a father of two and grandfather of four, who lived in Suffolk before his death, studied music at the University of York then became musical director and arranger of Kevin’s first musical, Cloud Nine, at Perth Theatre in Scotland.

It was in Perth that Simon met and later married Thea Ranft who Kevin described as Simon’s “soulmate in life”.

“He was a remarkable human being,” Kevin said, “and I send my most heartfelt condolences to Thea and all the family, and all his friends. RIP dearest Simon.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21630686.jpg?type=mds-article-620)'He was a remarkable human being' ... Simon Webb *(Image: Supplied)*

Simon wrote and directed scores for York Theatre Royal, for everything from Shakespeare to Berwick Kaler's pantomimes.

He and Kevin also worked on the first stage production of The Who’s Tommy at London’s Queen’s Theatre in 1979.

“Assuming punk personas, we were signed to EMI [the first label the Sex Pistols signed to] and later to DJM records, culminating in the band show Dustbin Man at the Arts Theatre, London,” Kevin said.

Simon and Kevin continued to work together during the Eighties, including on Cats at the New London Theatre and Abbacadabra at Lyric Theatre in Hammersmith.

Kevin praised Simon’s achievements including his work on films such as Robert Towne’s Tequila Sunrise and Horace Ové’s Playing Away and theatre productions like Nicholas Hytner’s The Winter’s Tale and Henry V.

Simon’s scores and theme tunes for television go into the hundreds, Kevin said, and include Delia Smith’s Summer and Winter Collections, Hale and Pace, Mary Berry at Home and the British Comedy Awards.

Simon also produced arrangements and musical direction for Wogan, Friday/Saturday Night Live, TFI Friday and Last Night at the Proms.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21630687.jpg?type=mds-article-620)'There are so many lasting memories of Simon' *(Image: Supplied)*

Kevin said Simon’s work was “executed alongside his life passions for cricket, boatbuilding, sailing and his prolific Music for Sport library music company which has produced near 200 albums over the years”.

“All this, and at the same time, bringing up two wonderful daughters, with his wife Thea, and becoming the best grandad and family man anyone could wish for. A jolly good friend too, to boot.”

Kevin said he and Simon collaborated again over the past two decades, including on two feature films, Punk Strut – the Movie and Speed Love featuring Elaine Paige.

They also worked together on a forthcoming album and feature film, Christmas Dreams Are All We Need, which Kevin said will be dedicated to Simon.

Simon is survived by his wife Thea, daughters Gabi and Lisa, and grandchildren Albie, Grayson, Millie and Frankie.

* Simon Webb, composer and musical director, born March 12, 1995; died October 11, 2026.
