A lane remains closed on a busy Bradford road after a burst water main continues to cause disruption over the weekend.

One lane of Sticker Lane, outside Java Joe's coffee shop, remains shut today, November 1, as Yorkshire Water continues repair work.

A Yorkshire Water spokesperson said: "We are attending a burst main on Sticker Lane, Bradford.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656403.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sticker Lane this morning *(Image: T&A)*

"Our team will work through the repair as quickly as they can.

"We have maintained supply through a number or rezones in the area, and a lane closure is currently in place for the safety of colleagues and road users.

"We would like to apologise for any inconvenience this may be causing and thank everyone in the area for their patience."

## The scene this morning

When the T&A visited the scene this morning, the situation appeared largely unchanged from the previous evening.

A large amount of water had collected in the area, with barriers in place to prevent it spreading onto a nearby residential street.

A steady stream of water was still running down Sticker Lane.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21656405.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sticker Lane this morning *(Image: T&A)*

The lane closure has caused some disruption, with traffic backed up onto the nearby roundabout.

Reports of the burst pipe first emerged on social media on Saturday afternoon, with crews working at the scene for several hours.

The lane closure remains in place while repairs continue.
