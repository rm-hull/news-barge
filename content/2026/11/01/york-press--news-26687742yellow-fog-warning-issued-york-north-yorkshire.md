The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for York and parts of North Yorkshire.




The UK weather agency said fog could limit visibility down to 100 metres in places, causing delays and making for difficult travel conditions.

The warning, issued at 11.18pm yesterday (Saturday, October 31), covers the city, as well as parts of North Yorkshire and the East Riding of Yorkshire and is expected to persist through Sunday morning before slowly lifting and clearing.

It remains in force until 10am this morning.

York is forecast to become cloudier later, with patchy light rain possible.
