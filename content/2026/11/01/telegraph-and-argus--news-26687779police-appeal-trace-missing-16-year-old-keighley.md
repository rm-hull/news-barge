West Yorkshire Police are appealing for help to find a 16-year-old girl who has been reported missing from the Keighley area.

Abigail was last seen in the early hours of Saturday morning, October 31, and officers say they are concerned for her safety.

Anyone who has seen Abigail or knows where she may be is urged to contact police.

Information can be passed to West Yorkshire Police by calling 101 and quoting log 0036 of October 31, 2026.
