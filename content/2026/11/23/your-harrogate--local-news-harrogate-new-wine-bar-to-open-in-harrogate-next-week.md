A new wine bar serving pintxos is set to open in Harrogate town centre next week.

The Grail is expected to open at 38 Beulah Street on Thursday 3rd December.

It’s the latest venue from Appetite For Life, the local family-run company behind The Mucky Duck, Tap on Tower Street, The Devonshire Tap House and So! Bar & Eats.

The interior takes inspiration from old monastic spaces, with the family aiming to create a quiet spot for a glass of wine or a beer and a few small plates.

Robert Thompson, of Appetite For Life, said:

> “My wife & I wanted The Grail to feel like a little escape from the bustle of the town: somewhere you can come in for a glass of wine, beer, graze on some small plates and make an evening of it.
>
> "We’ve had a lot of fun creating its character, and we’re looking forward to welcoming people through the door.”

*The Grail will share opening updates on**social media.***
