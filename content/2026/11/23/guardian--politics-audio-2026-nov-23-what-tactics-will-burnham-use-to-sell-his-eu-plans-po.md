# What tactics will Burnham use to sell his EU plans? – podcast

Andy Burnham has set out his plans to win parliamentary backing for a new EU deal. But the various options for how Britain could rejoin the EU, or ‘Breturn’, will not be outlined until December. Pippa Crerar has been speaking to the prime minister about how he hopes to convince the public that reversing Brexit is worth the headache
