# Emmerdale fans say 'its obvious' as they 'work out' Kim Tate and Steve Marchant twist

## Emmerdale viewers have voiced concern for Kim Tate after her ex-husband Steve Marchant returned to the village, unaware that DS Thea Ramsden is their long-lost daughter

Steve Marchant made a dramatic return to Emmerdale during the latest episode, coming face-to-face with Kim Tate for the first time in nearly three decades. The dodgy businessman, portrayed by Paul Opacic, was a fixture on the ITV soap between 1996 and 1999, during which he was notably wed to Kim (Claire King).

Their relationship ended catastrophically when Steve attempted to sabotage Kim by stealing a horse from a competitor. Things spiralled further when he knocked down Kathy Bates (Malandara Burrows) in a hit-and-run, leaving her for dead.

He ultimately received a 10-year prison sentence for attempted murder after Kim stitched him up, allowing him to carry the can for their joint wrongdoings. After years apart, he rocked up in the village in the most recent instalment of the Yorkshire soap, casually greeting a stunned Kim with: "Hello, darling. Miss me?" Kim pressed him on his unexpected arrival, to which he responded: " Weirdly, I'm not after an apology - you know, for sending me to prison.

"I want an explanation. You know full well what I'm talking about. I was contacted by a solicitor. Something about a windfall inheritance.", reports the Daily Star.

Steve revealed his doubts about the entire affair, as he couldn't fathom who would bequeath him money. When he investigated further, the trail led straight back to Kim, prompting him to demand answers about the solicitor's true intentions.

Kim revealed: "Well, I can tell you that she's not a solicitor. She's a police officer. Detective. Turned up and tried to frame me for my step-daughter's death. That failed, but it's obsessed her. She wants me put away badly. Now she's digging up everything she can to get to me. And that appears to include you."

Kim proceeded to caution Steve that the detective, DS Thea Ramsden, might soon discover any "shady dealings" from his past. She then handed him a sum of money with one condition - vanish and avoid any future contact with the officer.

Steve accepted the money and departed, unaware that Thea is actually Kim's long-lost daughter, making him her biological father.

Kim appeared satisfied that her bribe had succeeded in removing Steve permanently from the picture, but doubts emerged as he listened to a voice message at the episode's close. It was from Thea, who stated: "'Hi, it's Thea. 'Look, I hate to do it like this, but... I lied.

"I'll explain everything if you decide you want to meet, 'but for now, you need to know - I'm your daughter. 'So text me. Call me. Whatever."

Following the dramatic revelation, one viewer took to X to share their thoughts: "I might come to regret this, seeing as this is the first time I've 'met' him. But I think I actually like Steve Marchant."

Another viewer remarked: "I wasn't a big fan of Steve originally but he had some charisma today. Maybe it was nostalgia? I really don't know but I enjoyed him today."

A third observer noted: "Steve hasn't changed at all [giggling emoji]." One fan also pondered whether this surprising return signals the end of Kim's reign in the village.

Writing on Reddit, they commented: "It's so weird for him to return all of a sudden. Can't help but worry that this might finally be it for Kim. She's obviously got such a reputation as one of the biggest bitches in soap but I think it's obvious Steve is out for revenge after all this time and I don't know how she's going to get out of this one."
