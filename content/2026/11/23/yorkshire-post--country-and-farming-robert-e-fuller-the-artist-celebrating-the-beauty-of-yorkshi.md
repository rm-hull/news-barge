# Robert E Fuller: The artist celebrating the beauty of Yorkshire in winter

## He is an artist who caught the eye by capturing the beauty of Yorkshire.

Now artist and filmmaker Robert E Fuller is launching a new exhibition of paintings and films celebrating the beauty of British wildlife in winter.

It will run until January 10 at The Robert Fuller Gallery at Fotherdale Farm, in the Yorkshire Wolds village of Thixendale.

The event features paintings of foxes diving into snow drifts, barn owls hunting over frozen fields, and hares hunkering down in blizzards alongside the films that inspired them.

Visitors will also be able to watch live wildlife cameras where owls are already beginning to secure their nests for the coming spring.

Mr Fuller’s wildlife documentaries regularly feature on BBC1’s The One Show and his films and paintings have earned him more than two million followers on social media.

“I wanted to capture how beautiful it is when the world freezes over and the landscape turns white,” said Mr Fuller.

“My films show how even in the most extreme conditions, animals just get on with it. They even seem to have the energy to be quite playful – I’ve watched hares boxing in blizzards!”

He added: "When the world turns white, I like to wrap up warm and get outside, because this is one of the best times to spot animals as they forage for food or even just jump and play in the snow drifts."

The artist uses surveillance cameras hidden in his Thixendale garden and the surrounding countryside.

Among the documentaries on show is one featuring swan cygnets breaking through ice on an East Yorkshire lake and one showing the beauty of garden birds feasting on frosted berries.

Mr Fuller, who also travels the globe to film and paint animals in their natural habitats, will also exhibit films and paintings of penguins, recorded after an intrepid journey to the Antarctic regions.

He works in both oils and acrylics, applying fine brushstrokes to record each feather, hair and glint of eye.

Also a sculptor, his bronze animal sculptures have been described as breathtaking.

Although his scope is global, he is best known for his renditions of British wildlife, many of them observed close to his home in Yorkshire.

The gallery is a beautifully restored farm building just a few miles from his childhood home.

His film work, revealing the secret world of animals, is on his YouTube channel and social media platforms. He travels the world to film wildlife but is best known for his deep understanding of British wildlife.

The exhibition features “a collection of paintings, art prints and films capturing the magic of wildlife when the world turns white”.

His wildlife art and film exhibition runs daily from 9.30am-4.30pm weekdays and 10.30am-4.30pm weekends at the artist’s gallery.
