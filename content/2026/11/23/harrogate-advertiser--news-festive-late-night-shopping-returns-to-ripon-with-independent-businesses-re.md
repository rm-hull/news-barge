Independent businesses in Ripon are getting ready for a special evening of festive shopping, with the city’s late-night shopping trail inviting visitors to enjoy a magical Christmas atmosphere across its historic streets.

With Christmas fast approaching, shoppers will have the chance to explore Ripon’s wide range of independent businesses, discover unique gifts for loved ones and enjoy the welcoming atmosphere.

The event on Thursday, November 25 comes as the city continues to build its reputation as a destination for festive days and evenings out, following a popular Christmas lights switch-on that brought crowds of all ages into the centre.

Ripon BID manager Lilla Bathurst said the city’s combination of heritage, independent businesses and hospitality venues have helped to create a special Christmas experience for both residents and visitors.

She said: “It’s beginning to look a lot like Christmas all over our city, which has been celebrating the joyous Yuletide period for more than 1,300 years.

“The holly and the ivy that can be seen at our magnificent cathedral and in the beautiful Spa Gardens, can be enjoyed alongside a centre packed with heritage buildings including three fine museums and other historical attractions, such as the Market Square with its Grade I listed obelisk.

“All add to the magic of a festive shopping trip, while our wide range of hospitality businesses provide visitors and local residents alike with a great choice of food and drink all served with a very warm welcome.”

This year’s late-night shopping evening will see businesses across the city opening their doors with special offers, refreshments and festive activities.

Visitors can expect plenty of inspiration for Christmas gifts, from fashion and homeware to books, toys, food and locally created products.

Independent favourites including boutiques, gift shops and specialist retailers will be showcasing their ranges, giving shoppers the opportunity to find something different from the high street norm.

Among the highlights will be artisan stalls at Café in the Park, where local makers will offer handmade gifts, while The Little Ripon Bookshop will host a book signing event with multi-talented author Jess Kidd, alongside festive refreshments.

Jane Aksut, Owner of Café in the Park, said: “We’re going to have an artisan market inside the cafe for local artists and small businesses, who will have stalls where they can sell gifts including crochet items, magic tricks, leather work, candles and soaps.

“The market will run from 3pm until 8pm and all stall holders have agreed to include some goods suitable for those with a pocket money budget.”

Newer businesses will also be joining in the celebrations.

Sprout & Crumb, which recently opened its doors in the city, will stay open late after receiving a warm welcome from customers and fellow traders.

Owner Luke Mills added: “Having received such a warm welcome from customers and fellow business owners, I have decided that I will be opening late on Thursday as enjoy my first taste of Christmas in this lovely city.”

The evening will also provide an opportunity to support local causes.

Saint Michael’s Hospice Shop will be taking part, with festive refreshments and a special sale to raise funds for the hospice, while The Portly Pig will be supporting Dementia Forward through fundraising activities during the event.

With its independent businesses, festive displays and welcoming atmosphere, Ripon’s late-night shopping trail promises to offer a relaxed alternative to the busy high street and a chance to support local traders in the run-up to Christmas.
