A man has been charged after a 21-year-old man died in a hospital car park.

Police said the 21-year-old was found with serious injuries near Ninewells Hospital in Dundee at 3.45am on Sunday, but investigations have revealed he was injured in Arbroath.

Officers said it is not clear how the man made it to the hospital, where he later died.

A 25-year-old man has been arrested and charged in connection with the death.

He is due to appear at Dundee Sheriff Court on Tuesday. A report will be sent to the Procurator Fiscal.

![Police said the 21-year-old was found near Ninewells Hospital in Dundee at 3.45am on Sunday, but investigations have revealed he was injured in Arbroath.](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/08/01M83QXCQNE9VDZBNV90XDMHSD.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Detective Inspector Mark Wilson previously said police activity remains ongoing at Ladyloan in Arbroath while officers work to establish the full circumstances.

“A police presence will remain at these locations and if anyone has any concerns or information, I would encourage you to speak to one of our officers,” he said on Sunday.

“Anyone with information can call 101, quoting reference 0523 of November 22, or call Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.”
