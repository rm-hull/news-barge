Leeds Bradford Airport is adding seven new destinations for summer 2027, including Santorini, Tivat and Tirana. Ryanair's four new routes start on 29 March.

Harrogate, Knaresborough and Ripon travellers flying from Leeds Bradford next summer will have seven destinations to choose from that the airport has never served before. The airport says its Summer 2027 programme is the largest range of routes in its history, at more than 100.

The seven new destinations are Tivat in Montenegro, Bari in Italy, Hurghada in Egypt, Tirana in Albania, Enfidha in Tunisia, and Preveza and Santorini in Greece.

## Overview

* **New for Summer 2027:** Tivat, Bari, Hurghada, Tirana, Enfidha, Preveza and Santorini.
* **Ryanair:** Bari, Budapest, Corfu and Tirana, twice a week each from 29 March 2027, on sale now.
* **Jet2:** Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh from February 2027; Preveza, Santorini, Tivat and Enfidha from May 2027.
* **Network:** more than 100 routes across Europe and North Africa, the most the airport has offered.

## Which airline flies where, and when

Ryanair is adding four routes, all launching in March 2027:

* Tirana, Albania
* Bari, Italy
* Budapest, Hungary
* Corfu, Greece

Jade Kirwan, Ryanair’s Director of Comms, said: “Although we have not yet launched our full Summer 2027 schedule, Ryanair is giving our UK customers in the North a sneak peek at what new routes we will be offering with the announcement of these 4 exciting new Summer 2027 routes from Leeds Bradford to Bari, Budapest, Corfu and Tirana. Each of these new Ryanair routes will operate twice per week from 29 Mar 2027, giving our Yorkshire customers even more low-fare choice and regular connections for their Summer 2027 getaways. Tickets are available to book via Ryanair’s app/website now.”

Jet2 says it will run its largest programme yet from Leeds Bradford, adding:

* Hurghada, Egypt, from February 2027
* Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt, from February 2027
* Preveza, Greece, from May 2027
* Santorini, Greece, from May 2027
* Tivat, Montenegro, from May 2027
* Enfidha, Tunisia, from May 2027

Phil Ward, Chief Operations Officer at Jet2, said: “We’re delighted to continue expanding our programme from Leeds Bradford Airport. The introduction of so many exciting new destinations gives customers and travel agent partners even more choice for Summer 2027 and responds directly to continued demand for our award-winning flights and holidays from Yorkshire.

“As always, we’re working closely with Leeds Bradford Airport to deliver fantastic travel experiences for our customers, and we’re pleased to play our part in this record-breaking growth story.”

## Long-haul connections

For journeys further afield, the airport points to connecting flights via Dublin with Aer Lingus and via Amsterdam Schiphol with KLM.

## What the airport says

The airport says it is adding more new destinations for Summer 2027 than most other UK airports.

Declan Maguire, Aviation Director at Leeds Bradford Airport, said: “This is a hugely significant milestone for Leeds Bradford Airport. For the first time in our history, we are offering more routes than ever before, providing customers across Yorkshire and the North with unprecedented choice and connectivity.

“Adding seven brand-new destinations for Summer 2027 is a fantastic achievement and demonstrates the confidence our airline partners have in both the airport and the region we serve. Whether customers are looking for a relaxing Mediterranean escape, a cultural city break or an exciting new destination to explore, there has never been a better time to fly from Leeds Bradford Airport.

“This growth reflects the strength of demand across our catchment and our commitment to delivering greater choice, convenience and value for our customers.”

The full announcement is on the Leeds Bradford Airport newsroom.
