The White House has announced the names of the two turkeys that will take part in Tuesday’s Thanksgiving presidential tradition, going with the safer option of “Freedom and Feathers” - over the internet’s more risque suggestions.

Americans had three options to choose from before voting closed Sunday: Commander & Chief, Freedom & Feathers and Honey & Ham.

But that didn’t stop them from submitting their own suggestions on social media, alluding to scandals and controversy that have dogged President Donald Trump and his administration.

Popular submissions included “Jeffrey and Epstein,” referencing the controversy surrounding the administration’s handling of the late sex offender’s case that plagued Trump for much of 2025. “‘Epstein’ and ‘Files’ are my picks,” one person suggested.

Another suggestion was “Melania and Natalie” in a nod to the first lady Melania Trump and the president’s 35-year-old special assistant Natalie Harp, who is rarely away from his side.

![The White House has announced the names of the two turkeys that will take part in Tuesday’s Thanksgiving presidential tradition, opting for ‘Freedom and Feathers’ over the internet’s suggestions (picture from 2025 turkey pardoning)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/19/2247862074..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Someone on X floated the names “Lame and Duck,” alluding to the president’s status following devastating Republican losses in the midterm elections and with two years left in office with a Democrat-controlled Congress.

“Jeffrey and Jensen” was suggested by a user on X the day Jensen Huang was reinstated as Nvidia CEO by the board following pressure from Trump.

Other Trump officials were also suggested, with many putting forward two of the most senior Trump Cabinet members and potential 2028 Republican rivals.

“Marco and JD,” an account suggested.

A less popular recommendation was “Pete and JD,” referring to Vance and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth.

At last year’s pardoning of 2025 turkeys Gobble and Waddle, Trump turned the lighthearted holiday tradition into a partisan rant about his predecessor, former President Joe Biden.

![Most of the names from the public on social media alluded to scandals that have embroiled President Donald Trump and his administration but in the end, the White House announced the winning names were ‘Feathers and Freedom’ (pictured)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/19/2306526009..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He claimed that a “thorough and very rigorous investigation” by the Justice Department had determined that Biden had used an autopen to pardon 2024’s turkeys, Peach and Blossom.

“I have the official duty to determine, and I have determined that last year’s Turkey pardons are totally invalid,” Trump said.

“They’re hereby null and void, the turkeys known as Peach and Blossom last year have been located, and they were on their way to be processed. In other words, to be killed.”

But Trump added that he’d “stopped that journey” and claimed he was now “officially pardoning” last year’s turkeys, saving them “in the nick of time.”

While Presidents John F. Kennedy and Ronald Reagan pardoned turkeys at Thanksgiving, and there are stories of pardoned turkeys dating to the Lincoln administration, the ritual didn’t become an annual tradition until the George H.W. Bush administration, according to the White House Historical Association and the National Archives.
