Robert Jenrick has been grilled over Nigel Farage’s response to a female Reform UK MP raising complaints about sexism within the party.

Reform UK’s treasury spokesman Mr Jenrick was criticised live on air for his “rather extraordinary explanation” of Mr Farage’s claim Sarah Pochin was “downwind of a few drinks” when she made the comments to an undercover journalist.

She was filmed as part of an ITV News investigation which also found councillors and candidates within Reform UK had used racist language.

Runcorn and Helsby MP Ms Pochin described the party as “so f\*\*\* ing chauvinistic”, telling an undercover reporter: “I go out on all the shows, and go: ‘Oh no, it’s lovely, oh they’re all so welcoming.’ Shit… they just exclude you if you’re a woman… It’s really tough being a girl with all of this alpha male shit going on.”

![Sarah Pochin lambasted Reform UK ‘chauvinism’ in the footage (Stefan Rousseau/PA)](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/16/08/17039fa20edda6a4c73adad8f35fc23dY29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzkyMjIxNzI2-2.85740076.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Speaking to broadcasters, Mr Farage said Ms Pochin did not “stand by her views”, adding her comments were “simply not true”.

“This was late at night and I think, it would be fair to say that it was a Christmas party and she might have been downwind of a few drinks,” Mr Farage told ITV.

Mr Jenrick was confronted about the comments on ITV on Monday, claiming Good Morning Britain presenter Susanna Reid was “overthinking” the exchange.

“OK, fine. It's perfectly reasonable if Nigel Farage had said on Friday exactly what you've just said: 'Sarah Pochin has said she doesn't believe that,’, Ms Reid said.

![Robert Jenrick has been grilled over Nigel Farage’s response to a female Reform UK MP raising complaints about sexism within the party](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/10/18/jenrick.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

"He didn't say that. He said she was 'downwind of a few drinks'. He basically said you can't rely on what she's saying because he's alleging - the clear implication of what he's saying is that she's drunk."

Mr Jenrick went on to tell her she was “overthinking” the comments and said Mr Farage was frustrated he was not being asked about other issues, such as the government’s EU reset deal.

Ms Reid replied: "We also interviewed him about what was going on in the EU as well. I think that's also a rather extraordinary explanation for what he said, with respect."

Mr Jenrick also admitted he had not spoken to Ms Pochin over the weekend, prompting Ms Reid to say: “If someone had said that about one of my colleagues publicly, I would definitely be in touch to say, 'Is that okay? Do you want to speak out? Do you want me to speak out on your behalf? Do you want me to have a word with Nigel?’”

Mr Jenrick has previously insisted the party was not chauvinistic and also denied Reform’s vetting processes were biased, but said: “All political parties have bad apples.”

“I’ve spoken to Sarah many times. We are colleagues and friends and she knows that she can always rely on me,” he said previously.

“But it’s not my experience that the party has problems of that kind.”
