# Let’s Sushi: Cockroach infestation discovered at popular Meadowhall food outlet

## A popular restaurant at Sheffield’s Meadowhall shopping centre was closed to the public following the discovery of a cockroach infestation.

The restaurant was shut with boards placed around the kiosk of a sushi outlet while the situation was dealt with to prevent the pests spreading.

![Cockroaches at Meadowhall](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/12/36/Untitled-Design-(75).png?trim=0,200,0,0&crop=&width=640&quality=65)


The issue was first reported at Let’s Sushi, in the Oasis Dining Quarter, at the start of November.

The boarding around the restaurant was reportedly removed last weekend (November 14), with the business now open and trading as normal.

It is understood other businesses on the ground floor of the food hall were also visited by Environmental Health officers following the incident.

![Let's Sushi, in Meadowhall, earned a food hygiene rating of five on January 21, 2022. Hygienic food handling: Good. Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Good. Management of food safety: Good.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjdjOWFiMjZiLWIwOTUtNDVmOC05NWM0LWNjODlhZDcxZWUzYjowZTJiNDQ0ZS1jNTkxLTQ0ZTItOWM1OS04ZGIwY2JjZjQ0Y2Q=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The businesses were reportedly given cleaning advice by officers during the visits.

A spokesperson for Meadowhall said: “Let’s Sushi is open and trading as normal in our Oasis Dining Quarter. We work closely with Environmental Health to ensure we provide a clean, safe and hygienic environment.”

Meadowhall said it became aware of the situation a few weeks ago and had been working closely with Let’s Sushi and Environmental Health since then to remedy the issue.

Let’s Sushi is a Sheffield-founded chain with three outlets currently in the city. Alongside their Meadowhall branch, there’s others on Division Street and the West One complex.

When Let’s sushi were contacted, they directed enquiries to Meadowhall.

They say on their website they are “dedicated to delivering a delightful culinary experience rooted in the artistry of Japanese cuisine”.

![Environmental health worker spraying stalls at The Moor Market where cockroaches have been detected.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/07/06/16/04/MixCollage-06-Jul-2026-05-00-PM-387.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


This isn’t the first time cockroaches have closed a prominent Sheffield location in recent times.

Moor Market was closed for almost three weeks in June this year after a cockroach infestation was discovered.

A deep clean was required following the discovery with local traders speaking of their anguish at the situation.
