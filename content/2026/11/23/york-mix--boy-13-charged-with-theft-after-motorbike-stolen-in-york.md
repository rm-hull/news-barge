A 13-year-old boy is due to appear in court charged with theft of a motorcycle following an incident in York on Friday evening.

At around 4.40pm, officers received a report that a Yamaha motorbike had been stolen from the Holgate area of the city.

CCTV showed a person tampering with the vehicle prior to pushing it down the street.

A short time later, the vehicle was abandoned in the St Paul’s Square area as officers arrived at the scene.

Police identified the suspect and arrested the 13-year-old boy from York.

He was charged with theft of a motor vehicle and is due to appear at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court in January.
