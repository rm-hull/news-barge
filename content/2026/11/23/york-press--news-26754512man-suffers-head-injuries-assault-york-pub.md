POLICE have launched an appeal after an alleged assault in a popular York city centre pub.

The incident happened in The Roman Bath pub in St Sampson's Square on October 10.




North Yorkshire Police said: "The victim, a man in his 50s, suffered head injuries after being assaulted by another man.

"We believe the person in the image will have information that could help our investigation."
