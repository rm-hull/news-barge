A York club which has reopened after a rebrand and revamp is bringing “fresh energy” to the city’s nightlife, the owners say.

Popworld on George Hudson Street reopened as Replay York last month following a two-week refurbishment and a change in management.

The revamp included new signs, new booths and new flooring. Managers say they’re not finished rebranding just yet.

The legendary revolving dance floor was retained.

Replay owner Connor Haywood and operations Manager Liam McGroarty told ***YorkMix***: “Replay brings a fresh energy to York’s nightlife.

“We’re all about bringing people together for a night out.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/inside-replay-club-york-hannah-graba-ysj-2.webp)

They said the relaunch had proved popular, with around 1,400 clubbers at the venue for Halloween.

DJ Liam Marshall said the reopening mattered at a time when the nightlife economy faces challenges.

“Nightlife has been in decline in recent years, not just in York but across the country. Thankfully, York’s not lost another venue,” he said.

Liam encouraged people to experience the new venue, saying: “Come down, give us a try for yourself!”
