There are lots of different smart lights on the market, but many people consider the Philips Hue range to be the best. Granted, it's not the cheapest. But the lights work well, there are plenty of options to choose from, and the products themselves are good quality and built to last.

However, there is one problem. Because as the range has grown in popularity, Philips has added more and more Hue products. So, how do you know which one is right for you while you're shopping in the sale?

We've created a simple quiz to help you work out which Philips Hue lights you should consider buying this sales season. We've included basic smart bulbs for those on a budget who want to experiment with adding smart lighting to their homes, all the way through to more premium options for people with specific needs, like making their gaming setup feel more immersive.

I own several Hue lights myself, and they're among my most recommended bits of tech. That’s because smart lights are a great way to add a bit more personality to your home and make everyday life more comfortable and convenient. For example, I love adding a warm glow in my bedroom to help me wind down at night. But I also love having the choice of bright white lighting in my kitchen for cooking or concentrating. Philips Hue also has a fantastic range of colorful lights that you can control from your phone to change the atmosphere in your living room, ideal for gamers or movie lovers.

We haven't included every type of Hue light here, there are also larger wall-mounted lights and outdoor lighting if you have something really specific you’re hunting for. But if you're unsure where to start, you should find something that suits you here. Just bear in mind, especially if you're shopping during the sales, that models and specifications can vary depending on your region. Some Hue products also require a Hue Bridge, which acts as a smart hub, so it's worth checking what you need before buying.
