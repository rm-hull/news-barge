Selby Bypass Swing Bridge will be closed between 7:30pm on Thursday (26 November) and 6am on Friday (27 November).

The closure is required to allow diagnostic works on its movable components. These investigations will help progress repairs to the damaged transition plates and move towards removing the current temporary speed restriction.

Please allow extra time for your journey and follow the signed diversions. North Yorkshire Council added: “Sorry for any inconvenience this may cause.”
