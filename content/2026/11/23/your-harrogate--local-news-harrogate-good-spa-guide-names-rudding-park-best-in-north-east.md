Harrogate’s Rudding Park has won Best Spa in the North East for the fourth time.

The Harrogate spa was one of 11 finalists at the 2026 Good Spa Guide Awards, held at Sopwell House.

The shortlist included Forest Spa at Middleton Lodge, Serenity Spa at Seaham Hall and SubRosa Spa at The Grand.

Meg Ainsworth, Rudding Park Spa Manager, said:

> “We are thrilled to have won this award particularly against such strong competition.
>
> "The fact we’ve won for the fourth year really is testament to the consistent quality of the produce and service from the amazing team.”

Rudding Park’s Roof Top Spa and Garden was designed to change with the seasons.

Its facilities include a hydrotherapy pool, ice baths and a garden cabin sauna.
