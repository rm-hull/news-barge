Montey's, Harrogate's independent rock cocktail bar, turns 30 with live bands on 27 and 28 November and a Pick Your Decade cocktail menu from the 1990s on.

Montey’s, the independent rock ‘n’ roll cocktail bar in Harrogate, is 30 this month, and it is marking the birthday with live music on **Friday 27** and**Saturday 28 November** and a cocktail menu that runs through three decades of drinks.

The bar opened in November 1996, when, it says, a cocktail cost £2.75. Founder Jay opened it, and Col, his very first employee, still works beside him thirty years later.

## Overview

* **What:** Montey’s 30th birthday weekend.
* **Friday 27 November:** live music from Josh Ozturk.
* **Saturday 28 November:** Desperado and Captain & The Bear, both bands the bar calls “legendary Montey’s bands”.
* **Sunday 29 November:** an invitation-only birthday party with what the bar calls “a very special surprise band”.
* **Drinks:** a Pick Your Decade anniversary menu, with cocktails from the 1990s, 2000s and 2010s.

![Two men in white shirts and black waistcoats inside Montey's bar in 1996](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/monteys-harrogate-30th-birthday-2.webp)

## Thirty years of rock and cocktails

Montey’s describes itself as “fiercely independent” and says it has become “a Harrogate nightlife institution, known for its rock music, legendary Lemon Drops, live bands, late nights and an atmosphere that’s always been unmistakably its own.”

“There have been recessions, a pandemic, changing drinking habits and plenty of challenges along the way,” the bar says. “But while other venues have come and gone, Montey’s has remained proudly independent, continuing to evolve and welcome new generations without losing its rock ‘n’ roll soul.”

Jay said: “When I opened Montey’s in 1996, I don’t think anyone imagined we’d still be doing this thirty years later. We’ve been incredibly lucky to have brilliant staff, talented musicians and generations of customers who’ve made Montey’s their own. This birthday is really a celebration of everyone who’s ever been part of it.”

## Romances, reunions and second-generation staff

The bar says it has seen “friendships, romances, break-ups and reunions” over the years. Two former members of staff fell in love and got married, and some of today’s team have parents who were drinking in Montey’s in the nineties.

“And there are still customers propping up the bar who remember those early days,” it says, “although they might prefer not to be reminded quite how long ago that was.”

![A group of young customers smiling at a table under a canopy with festoon lights, with a crowd standing behind](https://harrogatelive.co.uk/img/monteys-harrogate-30th-birthday-4.webp)

## The Pick Your Decade menu

For the anniversary the bar has put together a menu of nine cocktails, three from each decade:

* **1990s:** Yellow Bird, Killer Punch and Long Island Iced Tea, £7.95 each.
* **2000s:** Pink Fluffy Bunny, Woo Woo and Purple People Eater, £7.95 each.
* **2010s:** Smokey Old Fashioned, Siesta Margarita and Dreamy Eyes, £9.75 each.

Those are the prices printed on the menu card, which is headed “Legends of the Decades”. The menu itself says: “Some are sophisticated. Some are nostalgic. And some probably seemed like a very good idea at the time.”

## A Sunday party, on purpose

The invitation-only party falls on Sunday 29 November, and Jay says that is deliberate.

“And in true Montey’s tradition, we’re holding the big party on a Sunday,” he said. “We’ve always liked to see just how many people are prepared to take Monday off work!”
