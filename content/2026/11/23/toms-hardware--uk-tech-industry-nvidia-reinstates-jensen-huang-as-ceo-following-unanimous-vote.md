Nvidia has reinstated CEO Jensen Huang by a unanimous vote from its board of directors, the company announced Monday.

"Nvidia today announced that effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board," a statement reads. "The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead NVIDIA. In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk."

Shares of the company surged more than 30% ahead of the Nasdaq's open, erasing all of the losses incurred during Huang's leave of absence. In a Truth Social post, President Donald Trump said Nvidia "has made the correct and proper decision" in reinstating Huang, stating the USA needs his leadership and Nvidia to ensure "the success and prosperity of not only the company, but the American economy." He further stated that the U.S. Attorney General has no evidence Huang engaged "in any unlawful conduct."

The board said that it is confident that Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk, and part of this reassurance seems to have come from a senior level of the United States government.

“The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Harvey C. Jones, Chair of the Board, stated. "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as NVIDIA’s Chief Financial Officer."

Huang said he was "honored" to return to Nvidia, but took the responsibility "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment."

Huang remains under investigation following reports he was entangled in an extramarital affair with a Chinese intelligence asset, with the board stating the investigation will proceed to conclusion. Reports emerged some weeks ago that Jensen Huang was having an affair with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, a company under criminal investigation for the export of illicit AI GPUs into China. Meanwhile, a second affair with VP of health care Kimberly Powell was brought to light, prompting Huang's resignation.

Nvidia has also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors "to oversee trade compliance and national security risks." The committee will continue its work, and the company is also hiring a Chief Compliance Officer "to oversee compliance across Nvidia and report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee."
