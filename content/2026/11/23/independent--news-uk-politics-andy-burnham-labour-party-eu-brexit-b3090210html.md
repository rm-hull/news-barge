Andy Burnham has denied that his deal with the EU is prelude to a referendum on the UK’s return to the bloc, but it has meant that Brexit is firmly back at the forefront of the political agenda.

The prime minister, who has previously said he hoped the UK would return to the EU in his lifetime, on Friday reached agreements with Brussels on food and agriculture exports, emissions trading and a youth experience scheme at last week’s Brussels summit.

Mr Burnham had earlier said he would dismiss the advice of former prime ministers, including Rishi Sunak and Baroness May of Maidenhead, who have urged him not to reignite the Brexit debate.

Addressing MPs on Monday, Mr Burnham did confirm the UK would not rejoin the EU without a referendum and said there would be no “banging on about Europe” from Labour, echoing the words of former prime minister David Cameron at the Tory party conference in 2006.

However, ten years on from the referendum that took the country out of the bloc, the topic has the potential to cause rifts within the Parliamentary Labour Party.

Some senior MPs, including members of Sir Keir Starmer’s front bench, have said the country should return, while others disagree, and some have warned that reopening the debate would cause more trouble than it is worth.

Here we will keep tabs on which of the party’s MPs have spoken in favour of rejoining and those who would rather stay out - or not have the conversation. This article will be updated as more MPs speak out.

**Rejoin**

* David Lammy - “Burnham’s Labour should go into the next election clear that its intention is to rejoin the EU.”
* Peter Kyle - “The only way forward I see is for Labour to have a commitment to rejoin the EU in its next manifesto.”
* Emily Thornberry - “We ought to get back into the European Union.”
* Rachel Reeves - “Britain's future lies in the European Union”
* Stella Creasy - “Everything must be on the table.”
* Jo Stevens - “I want the UK to be an EU member again.”
* Daniel Zeichner - “We should be in the EU. We should never have left.”
* Marsha de Cordova - “Rejoining the EU would significantly boost the UK’s GDP.”
* Helen Hayes - Said every option should be explored, “including the potential for us to rejoin the European Union.”
* Rachael Maskell - “Polling in York shows a clear aspiration from my constituents to rejoin the EU, which I support.”
* Tony Vaughan - “I urge the Minister to make sure that membership is on the list of possible options.”
* Ben Coleman - “For me, the logic points inexorably towards joining.”

**Stay out**

* Jonathan Hinder - Rejoining “dissolves on contact” with reality.
* Graham Stringer - “Completely wrong to reopen the most toxic debate in our democratic history.”
* Brian Leishman - Warned against rejoining the "pro-capitalist, pro-imperialist" EU.
* Gareth Snell - Reopening debate would “monopolise an awful lot of time inside parliament, inside government."
* Jon Trickett - “Going back in would be bad for British democracy, our economy and our sense of fair play.”
* David Smith - “This shouldn’t include a return to EU membership.”
