Donald Trump has told holders of his $TRUMP meme coin that he supports the cryptocurrency to boost the United States' economic competitiveness.

The president, who has made hundreds of millions from the token, was speaking at a Sunday dinner for its biggest holders. “I’m doing it for the United States ... If we don’t do it, China’s going to do it,” Trump told attendees at his golf club outside Washington.

It was the third gala the president has hosted for the meme coin's top owners. The token once traded as high as $75 but has since fallen to a record low of under $1. Attendees said this year's private dinner was more low-key than last year's initial event, with a smaller crowd and far less money at stake.

![President Donald Trump attends the White House Crypto Summit at the White House in March 2025](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/07/2026-11-22T222811Z_2022032574_RC2ATOARB96Q_RTRMADP_3_TRUMP-CRYPTO-CLUB.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Qualification for the dinner was based on time-weighted holdings of the $TRUMP coin between September 30 and November 16. Organizers indicated that 185 crypto wallets met the criteria for Sunday's dinner. When the contest concluded on November 16, however, the leaderboard on the $TRUMP coin club website listed just 130 wallets.

Those wallets held only $8.6 million in total. That figure is a shadow of the $148 million listed for the dinner in May 2025, which 220 wealthy crypto investors attended. The contrast with the first gala was visible from the gate.

Contest winners in tuxedos pulled up to the club in luxury sports cars, while protesters chanted and stomped outside as the presidential Marine One helicopter descended.

This year's participants stood in the rain as they waited to enter the Trump National club in Sterling, Virginia. Attendees described the scene inside as subdued. The biggest $TRUMP holder at the May 2025 event was China-born crypto billionaire Justin Sun, who held $18.5 million of the token.

![Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D-CT) speaks during a forum to discuss U.S. President Donald Trump's official and personal dealings with crypto currency in July](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/18/14/34/GettyImages-2287897726.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Sun didn't sign autographs this time. Guests dined on grilled filet mignon and pan-seared halibut. Speeches came from Kevin O'Leary of the "Shark Tank" television show and NBA veteran Tristan Thompson. According to the contest's website, the top 29 winners would join a VIP reception with the president and receive a gift bag with Trump swag.

The top four would also get an 18-karat gold Trump-branded watch. Morten Christensen, founder of cryptocurrency platform Airdrop Alert, said he qualified for the top 29 with $41,000 of time-weighted $TRUMP holdings.

He did so using a hedging strategy – buying and selling the token using perpetual futures – that ensured he did not lose any money.

But Christensen said he was denied a VIP band at the event. "I asked like five people from ​the staff" and was told to email customer support, he said on Sunday night. "Obviously, ⁠no one is going to reply right now," Christensen said, adding that he was kicked out of the event around 7 p.m. for "sharing with the press."

He waited outside in the rain for his sister, who was still inside. The Trump Meme Coin project and the White House did not respond to requests for comment.

## Meme coin tumbles

More than half of the digital wallets publicly listed on the contest leaderboard for Sunday's dinner are tied directly to crypto exchanges that bar US-based users, indicating they are linked to foreign nationals.

![Among those who spoke at the event was former NBA star Tristan Thompson](https://static.independent.co.uk/2021/11/30/18/newFile-3.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The finding comes from an analysis of blockchain data that the nonpartisan, nonprofit consumer advocacy group Public Citizen prepared for Reuters. The Trump family's other crypto-connected equities and ventures have also fallen in value to record lows.

Outside investors in the family's five main crypto and crypto-linked projects have lost more than $5 billion in total, according to a Reuters analysis.

The $TRUMP token has earned the Trump family itself at least $635 million, according to the president's June financial disclosure statement.

In total, the Trumps had earned at least $2.3 billion through April from their four main crypto ventures, including the meme coin.
