A long-established North Yorkshire grower and supplier of plants is reported to have filed an application for administration.

Earley Ornamentals, LTD which is based in Thirsk, produces more than 180 million plants a year across 1,700 varieties.

The company operates from a 12-acre purpose-built site on York Road, Sowerby, where it has operated since 1992, according to its website.

Companies House records that the independent company was incorporated in 2009.

This followed a successful management buy-out from international ornamental breeding giant Sakata. It is headed and owned by managing director Simon Earley.

The business maintains strong links with Sakata, Earley’s website says, with it being one of its preferred distributors in the UK.

Earley’s range, range, which it supplies to professional growers, includes bedding, basket, patio and perennial products.

The business was the subject of a winding up petition issued by HMRC on October 30.

The company’s latest published accounts, which cover the period up to November 30 2025, reported the business had on average 36 staff.

The accounts showed the company had creditors due to be paid within a year that were owed almost £2million. In addition, a further £1.8 million was due to be paid after a year.

However, the figures also show total assets, including land and property, exceeding £5million.

No sales or profit figures were given in the accounts.

The Press has approached Earley Ornamentals for comment.
