An accident on a slip road of the M62 in West Yorkshire is causing congestion at a major interchange.

The traffic issues centre on the eastbound slip road of the M62 at junction 29 which is Lofthouse, traffic monitor Inrix is warning.

Inrix states: "M62 Eastbound exit slip road partially blocked, slow traffic due to accident at Junction 29 M1 Junction 42 (Lofthouse).

"The accident has spilled over onto the Lofthouse interchange roundabout, so there is congestion on all approaches to the interchange."
