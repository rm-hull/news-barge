A stately home has been named among the world’s must-see destinations alongside the Great Pyramid of Giza.

Castle Howard, located in North Yorkshire, was included in The Telegraph’s list of The 20 Buildings You Must Visit in Your Lifetime and was the only UK stately home to make the list.

The list, compiled by author and researcher Nicholas Boys Smith, placed Castle Howard alongside iconic landmarks such as Rome’s Pantheon and the Great Pyramid of Giza in Egypt.

Abbi Ollive, visitor attraction director at Castle Howard, said: "To be named as a bucket list destination in this way is a huge honour.

"We know visitors from places like China and the USA make special trips to see Sir John Vanbrugh’s magnificent architecture and stand against the backdrop of Brideshead Revisited and Bridgerton, so it’s a real delight to have that global recognition."

The accolade comes just weeks after Castle Howard received a VisitEngland Gold 2025–2026 Visitor Attraction Accolade for excellence in customer experience.

The award is based on VisitEngland’s annual Visitor Attraction Quality Scheme assessment, which evaluates all aspects of the visitor journey, including welcome, accessibility, online presence, and programming.

Ms Ollive said: "We are thrilled to receive this national recognition from Visit England.

"Castle Howard and the Arboretum welcome 350,000 visitors annually from the UK, Europe and the rest of the world.

"We pride ourselves on a fantastic welcome, from the very start of the visitor journey on the website, to the face-to-face service our team deliver on site."

The stately home was one of 73 attractions across England to receive the accolade, joining fellow Yorkshire sites such as the Jorvik Centre, Yorkshire Sculpture Park, and Dalby Forest Park.

Andrew Stokes, director at VisitEngland, said: "England’s visitor attractions are first-class, and these accolades highlight why they deserve this recognition.

"The recipients of these awards provide an outstanding welcome, unparalleled food and drink and magical storytelling making them integral to the high-quality tourism offer provided to domestic and international visitors."

Castle Howard is currently open daily for its Christmas events until January 3.

The Gardens and Playground remain open year-round, while the House and Arboretum will reopen in spring 2027.

Castle Howard was designed by Sir John Vanbrugh and is known for its appearance in film and television, including Brideshead Revisited and Bridgerton.
