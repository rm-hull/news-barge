## Fire crews and police rush to York incident after blaze breaks out

Firefighjters were called to the scene in Boroughbridge Road *(Image: Supplied)*

EMERGENCY services have been called to an incident in York.

Two fire engines from North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and six vehicles from North Yorkshire Police were seen in Boroughbridge Road at around 8am today (Monday, November 23).

It comes after reports of a fire at an address in Sandacre Court, next to Acomb Fire Station.

At 11.20am, a representative for the fire station said that the fire had been extinguished.

Traffic is currently moving at normal speeds.
