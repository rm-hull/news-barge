# Amazing new Leeds United fan shop opens in the heart of the city

## There are lots of brilliant Christmas gift ideas for the Leeds United supporter in your life

Leeds United fans will be delighted to know an exciting new store dedicated to the club has launched in the city.

We have previously brought you details of plans for the new opening. The popular Burley Banksy has launched a new shop in Leeds city centre.

It stocks dedicated Leeds United memorabilia with lots of Christmas present gift ideas available to be purchased. The new shop, from the Burley Banksy, opened on Tuesday, November 17 at the Merrion Centre.

In a post, stating the new launch will be open everyday ahead of Christmas, the Burley Banksy posted: “Officially licenced by Leeds United - a percentage goes to the club. Next to Jumbo Records on the ground floor. Up the Peacocks.”
