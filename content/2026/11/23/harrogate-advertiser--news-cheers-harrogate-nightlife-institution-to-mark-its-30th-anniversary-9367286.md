A legendary Harrogate bar which has prospered and remained proudly independent through the decades will celebrate its 30th anniversary this weekend.

Since opening in November 1996, Montey’s has become a Harrogate nightlife institution, known for its rock music, legendary lemon drops, live bands, late nights and an atmosphere that’s always been unmistakably its own.

For Jay, who founded Montey’s in 1996, and Col, his very first employee who remains by his side thirty years later, the milestone is about much more than cocktails and music.

Over the years, Montey’s has witnessed friendships, romances, break-ups and reunions.

Two former members of staff even fell in love and got married, while some of today’s team have parents who were drinking in Montey’s back in the nineties!

“When I opened Montey’s in 1996, I don’t think anyone imagined we’d still be doing this 30 years later,” said Montey’s founder Jay.

"We’ve been incredibly lucky to have brilliant staff, talented musicians and generations of customers who’ve made Montey’s their own.”

When Montey's first opened its doors in 1996. Oasis were playing their legendary Knebworth gigs, The Prodigy were topping the charts with Breathe, and mobile phones were still the size of bricks.

A cocktail would set you back just £2.75.

For a few short months at the very beginning, Montey’s acted as a jazz club with its wooden floorboards and Ronnie Scott’s feel.

But it soon revealed its true rock n roll self and has been rocking down the generations ever since.

Over the years Montey’s has become not only legendary for those cocktails - and beers, wines, spirits and shots - it has hosted a who’s who of great music acts, not only from Harrogate, including UK Foo Fighters, now Britain's most successful Foo Fighters' tribute band, but from across the country.

It also kindly played a key role in Harrogate Fringe Crawl Day. which ran for three years under Graham Chalmers of Charm magazine during the time of the Harrogate International Festival Fringe.

Montey’s will celebrate its 30th anniversary from Friday, November 27 to Sunday, November 29, with live music from Josh Ozturk on Friday, followed by legendary Montey’s bands Desperado and Captain & The Bear on Saturday.

The weekend will culminate in an invitation-only birthday party on Sunday, featuring a very special surprise band.

"This birthday is really a celebration of everyone who’s ever been part of Montey’s story,” said Jay.

“In true Montey’s tradition, we’re holding the big party on a Sunday.

“We’ve always liked to see just how many people are prepared to take Monday off work!”

To mark the occasion, Montey’s has created a special Pick Your Decade anniversary cocktail menu, taking customers on a liquid trip down memory lane.

The 1990s bring back big, bold classics like the Yellow Bird, Long Island Iced Tea and Killer Punch.

The 2000s revisit the wonderfully named Pink Fluffy Bunny, Woo Woo and Purple People Eater, while the 2010s showcase a more sophisticated era with the Smokey Old Fashioned, Siesta Margarita and Dreamy Eyes.

There have been recessions, a pandemic, changing drinking habits and plenty of challenges along the way for Jay, Colin and the team.

But while other venues have come and gone, Montey’s has remained proudly independent, continuing to evolve and welcome new generations without losing its rock ’n’ roll soul.
