## The sheriff's department said it believes the shooting "may be gang-related" but gave no further detail.

Nine people were shot at a concert in central California, the United States, on Saturday evening, one of them critically, local authorities said, a night of violence that comes as the state reports a decline in killings.

The shooting broke out around 7:30 p.m. local time in Strathmore, a town of about 3,000 people in Tulare County, in California's Central Valley.

Roughly 150 people were at the event, including performers from neighbouring states, when an unspecified number of suspects opened fire on the crowd, according to the Tulare County Sheriff's Department. No arrests have been announced.

The most seriously injured victim is a 26-year-old man shot in the chest, who underwent surgery and remains in critical condition. Two others, aged 19 and 22, also needed surgery. The remaining six victims had minor injuries.

The sheriff's department said it believes the shooting "may be gang-related" but did not elaborate.

Officials said the public is no longer in immediate danger. The department did not immediately respond to further questions.

The attack comes despite a recent fall in violent crime across California. The state reported 1,374 homicides in 2025, a 17.5% drop from 1,666 in 2024, according to the California Department of Justice.

State officials caution that not every agency reported its data, so year-over-year comparisons should be made carefully.
