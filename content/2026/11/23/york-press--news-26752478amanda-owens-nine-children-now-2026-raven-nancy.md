Amanda Owen's nine children became familiar faces to millions of television viewers as they grew up on one of the most remote farms in the Yorkshire Dales.

But life has changed considerably for the family since they first appeared on Our Yorkshire Farm, as the children have grown older, taken on new responsibilities, and developed interests of their own.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21340602.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Owen family *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

The family originally found fame through the Channel 5 programme, which documented their lives at Ravenseat Farm in Upper Swaledale.

They have since returned to television in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, after ambitiously restoring neighbouring farmhouse Anty John's.

**But what are the nine Owen children doing now?**

## Raven Owen

Raven is the eldest of Amanda and Clive's children and was one of the first to begin building a life beyond the family farm.

Viewers have followed her journey from childhood at Ravenseat to pursuing opportunities away from the remote Yorkshire Dales.

Despite growing older and developing her own interests, Raven remains part of the family's television story, returning to Ravenseat in the fourth series of Our Farm Next Door.

An October 2026 episode showed her working alongside younger sister Edith in the farm's stables.

## Reuben Owen

Having developed an interest in machinery and engineering from a young age, he went on to establish a business working with heavy machinery.

His career was documented in the Channel 5 programme Reuben Owen: Life in the Dales, which followed his work and the challenges of running a business in rural Yorkshire.

## Miles Owen

Miles has continued to feature in the family's farming life.

He has been seen working with livestock and helping with the demanding jobs required to keep Ravenseat running.

Channel 4 has confirmed that Miles will take on further farming responsibilities in the next series of Our Farm Next Door.

## Edith Owen

Edith has become an increasingly prominent member of the family on television.

Alongside her involvement with animals and farming, she has been helping prepare the family's cream tea van, which serves visitors to the Yorkshire Dales.

Her responsibilities have been balanced with her education, including preparations for exams.

## Violet Owen

Violet has also been involved in the family's expanding activities at Ravenseat.

Channel 4 has confirmed that she will feature alongside Edith and her sisters as they open the family's cream tea van in the forthcoming series.

## Sidney Owen

The fourth series featured his preparations for a tractor test, while future episodes will follow him taking on additional farming duties alongside Miles.

## Annas Owen

Annas continues to appear in the family's television adventures, with viewers seeing her involvement in everyday life at Ravenseat.

One episode of the fourth series featured Annas dealing with the challenges of looking after puppies.

## Clementine Owen

Clementine, often called Clemmie, is among the younger Owen children who have grown up in front of television cameras.

The fourth series finale featured Clemmie and Nancy enjoying a horseback ride through a wildflower meadow.

## Nancy Owen

Nancy is the youngest of the nine children and was just a small child when the family became television favourites.

As well as helping with life on the farm, she has developed interests outside agriculture.

The fourth series of Our Farm Next Door included her preparations for a ballet examination.

## How life at Ravenseat has changed

While the children are growing up, the family's connection to Ravenseat remains central to their story.

Amanda and Clive announced their separation in 2022 but have continued working together on their farming and restoration projects.

Their latest programme has introduced viewers to another part of the family's future through the renovation of Anty John's.

Channel 4 announced in November 2026 that Our Farm Next Door would return for two more series, with all nine children continuing to feature in the family's story.
