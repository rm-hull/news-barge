It’s only 48 hours until the launch of this year’s magical panto at Harrogate Theatre.

Sleeping Beauty will see the return of panto legend Tim Stedman to Harrogate for an incredible 26th time.

The much-loved and multi-talented comic actor will play the role of Silly Sidney in Sleeping Beauty which runs from Wednesday, November 25 to Sunday, January 17, 2027.

This year’s cast in what is one of the biggest selling single arts event in Harrogate each year will include the sensational Michael Lambourne who will be lending the dark but hilarious tones of his inimitable voice to The Vampire King.

The brilliant Harry Wyatt will also be back, playing the role of Dame Nanny Annie Nidderdale.

Also back for her second year is the fab Elouise Warboys, playing the role of Princess Aurora while the fantastic Ellen Carnazza plays the role of the evil Carabosse.

The terrific Colin Kiyani is back once again, this time playing the role of Tom Nidderdale.

The talented cast of Sleeping Beauty also includes Christina Harris, who will be playing the role of Fairy Forget Me Not, Zach Atkinson who will be the male ensemble swing, and Olivia Salmond, for her first Harrogate panto, will be the female ensemble swing.

Rounding off the cast will be Andy Cryer who will be playing the role of King Canute of K-naresborough.

A Harrogate Theatre spokesperson said: “Sleeping Beauty is proudly sponsored by Bob Partridge of Vet Dentist, whose generous support extends well beyond the pantomime.

“A valued supporter of Harrogate Theatre, Bob has also sponsored this year’s Drama Season and Comedy Festival, helping us bring an exciting and diverse programme of entertainment to Harrogate audiences.”
