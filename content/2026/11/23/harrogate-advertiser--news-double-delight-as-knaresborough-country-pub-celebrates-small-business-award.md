A country pub near Knaresborough is celebrating after being recognised with two major accolades at the Corporate Vision Small Business Awards 2026.

The Royal Oak in Staveley has been named **Best Country Pub & Restaurant 2026 – North Yorkshire** and has also received the**Hospitality Excellence Award 2026 – North Yorkshire**.

The Small Business Awards, organised by Corporate Vision Magazine, celebrate the achievements of businesses across a wide range of industries, recognising the passion, resilience and dedication of small businesses and the teams behind them.

Now in their ninth year, the awards aim to highlight organisations that demonstrate excellence, innovation and outstanding service.

Businesses recognised in the programme are selected through a judging process focused on merit, with the awards designed to shine a spotlight on the contribution small businesses make to their communities and industries.

The team at The Royal Oak said they were thrilled with the double award win and thanked their staff and customers for helping make the achievement possible.

A spokesperson for The Royal Oak said: “We are absolutely delighted to announce that we’ve won Best Country Pub & Restaurant 2026 and the Hospitality Excellence Award 2026 for North Yorkshire.

“A huge thank you to our wonderful team and all our lovely customers for your continued support - we couldn’t do it without you.”

Nestled in the picturesque village of Staveley, the pub has become a favourite destination for locals and visitors looking to enjoy traditional Yorkshire hospitality, with a cosy atmosphere and a reputation for great food and drink.
