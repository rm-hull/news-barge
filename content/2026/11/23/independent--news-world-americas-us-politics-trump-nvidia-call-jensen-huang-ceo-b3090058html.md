After admitting that he called Nvidia’s board of directors to urge them to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, Donald Trump spent nearly an hour on the phone with CNBC to celebrate the decision.

Then the president posted an image on Truth Social that compared Huang’s resignation and return to the artificial intelligence giant to Trump’s assassination attempt in Butler, Pennsylvania.

An AI-generated image places Huang with the president in a famous image that was captured moments after gunfire erupted at a campaign rally in July 2024, grazing the president’s ear and killing a rally attendee. Another person shot during the attack died of complications from their injuries this year.

But in Trump’s mock up of the attack, Huang poses with blood on his cheek and a raised fist that holds a red bra while he stands in front of a waving Nvidia flag, which replaces the red, white and blue.

Huang resigned less than two weeks ago following news of his affair with suspected Chinese spy Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer that the Commerce Department had been investigating over allegations that it breached U.S. export restrictions.

![Donald Trump posted an image from his 2024 assassination attempt that replaces him with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang after the president pressured the board to reinstate the tech giant](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/17/22/trump-nvidia-truth-social.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

*The New York Times* previously reported that U.S. intelligence determined Alice Huang had been recruited to influence Jensen Huang to push Nvidia toward selling Beijing more of its advanced chips, raising critical national security concerns.

Trump called into a meeting of Nvidia’s board of directors to “convince” them to reinstate Jensen Huang as the company’s CEO, the president admitted Monday.

“I spoke to them and I convinced them,” he told CNBC during a nearly hour-long on-air call Monday morning. “And it was a lot easier to convince them yesterday than it was a week ago.”

The president said he believes his unprecedented intervention was “very important” in the wake of the scandal, which has opened the door for congressional and federal investigations into national security threats.

![Trump called into Nvidia’s board of directors meeting Monday to lobby for Huang’s return less than two weeks after he resigned in the wake of an affair with an alleged Chinese spy](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/11/19/10/GettyImages-2297774940.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Trump, however, told CNBC that he believes Huang “did nothing wrong” and claimed he is “exonerated.”

Nvidia’s press release announcing Huang’s return Monday claimed that the decision was based on “input from senior levels of the United States government.”

“We gave him assurance, at least with everything we know, and we know a lot,” Trump told CNBC. “People say, ‘is it a threat to have him?’ No, it’s a threat to lose him.”

The president said that, if he were Chinese President Xi Jinping, “I’d say, ‘Hey, let’s get him over here real fast. He can have whatever he wants. We’ll give him Beijing.”

Tech stocks were dragging in the aftermath of Huang’s November 12 resignation. Nvidia, which has been the world’s most valuable company for roughly two years, lost roughly $1.5 trillion of its market value in that time.

According to financial records, the president holds between $13 million and $62 million in Nvidia stock, drawing criticism from watchdogs and lawmakers who say he has prioritized his own financial interests over national security concerns.

Trump has repeatedly publicly urged the company to bring Huang back to the fold in the days that followed news of Huang’s resignation.

After he spoke to board members directly on Monday morning, Trump announced on Truth Social that “the country needs Jensen’s leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the success and prosperity of not only the company, but the American economy.”

Shares of Nvidia then skyrocketed more than 30 percent, effectively recovering nearly all its losses since Huang disclosed the affair two weeks ago.

![Nvidia and the NASDAQ both shot up on news of Huang’s return](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/15/2026-11-23T152106Z_57808544_RC2PTOA6YEFN_RTRMADP_3_USA-STOCKS.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Asked whether falling stocks had anything to do with Trump lobbying for Huang’s return, Trump said: “Probably so, but the bigger impact was the long term.”

“I’m very very happy that the company and the board had the courage to do the right thing,” Trump told CNBC. “They suffered for two weeks. The company suffered greatly.”

But asked whether the move makes Huang “too big to fail,” Trump said “no” and called him “a brilliant guy, and we want him on our side.”

“Investors should be very happy,” said Trump, who repeated his spurious allegation that Democratic victories in midterm elections contributed to Wall Street losses.

“I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” Huang said in a statement Monday.

“I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment,” he added.
