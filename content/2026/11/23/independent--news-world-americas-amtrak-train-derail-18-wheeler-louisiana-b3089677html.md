A train carrying almost 100 passengers struck an 18-wheeler in Louisiana early on Sunday night, police say.

A number of the Amtrak train’s cars derailed amid the incident, with four people being rushed to an area hospital.

​Monique Blanco, the mayor-president of Lafayette Parish, confirmed that the incident unfolded at 6.05 p.m. just outside of Broussard - a city in southern Louisiana.

![An Amtrak train derailed in Louisiana after crashing into an 18-wheeler](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/8/48/Screenshot-2026-11-23-at-08-46-18.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=43%2C0%2C110%2C1&auto=webp)

​Blanco said in a statement that the 18-wheeler was hauling a bulldozer when the train - carrying around 95 people - crashed into it.

​Speaking to reporters, Lafayette Police Sergeant Ashley Wood explained more about the moments leading up to the crash.

“The train appears to have attempted to slow but was unable to stop prior to impact,” Wood told LiveNOW from FOX. “The lead locomotive and two baggage cars derailed.

“All passenger cars remained upright.”

Police told Acadiana News First that the 18-wheeler had been trying to cross the tracks when it had gotten stuck. Minutes later, the crossing guard arms closed and the crash unfolded.

![Amtrak has confirmed that all of the train's passenger carriages remained upright during the incident](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/9/21/Screenshot-2026-11-23-at-09-10-01.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=0%2C4%2C77%2C0&auto=webp)

​Wood told reporters that four people have been taken to the hospital.

The Lafayette Fire Department said that at least five passengers had sustained minor injuries and that firefighters had helped people to exit the train.

“Hazmat and Rescue personnel ensured the scene was safe for first responders to assist the injured and check on the other passengers,” a fire department spokesperson said. “Once it was safe for passengers to exit, firefighters assisted them to a safe location along with their luggage.”

Twelve ambulances were initially sent to the crash site on Sunday evening, Acadian Ambulance confirmed in a statement. Later, seven crews remained on the scene to triage the remaining passengers.

Amtrak told ABC News that passengers aboard the train had been provided with alternative transportation.

![The Lafayette Fire Department says that firefighters helped passengers exit the train](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/9/21/Screenshot-2026-11-23-at-09-24-51.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=70%2C4%2C0%2C0&auto=webp)

“All passenger cars remained upright,” a spokesperson for Amtrak said. “There were 95 customers onboard. Customers are being provided alternate bus transportation to their destinations.”

Blanco says that Amtrak is working with the Lafayette Police Department, the local fire department, and the Office of Emergency Preparedness amid an investigation into the incident.

The mayor-president added that the right southbound lane of travel at the 3000 block of U.S. 90 E had been closed but that the center and left lanes remained open.
