**Three decades after opening its doors, independent Harrogate cocktail bar Montey’s is celebrating its 30th anniversary. From £2.75 cocktails in 1996 to generations of customers, live music and a reputation built around rock ‘n’ roll, the venue has become a familiar part of the town’s evening economy.**

When Montey’s opened in November 1996, the hospitality landscape looked very different. Mobile phones were still a novelty, cocktail menus were rather less elaborate than they are today, and a drink at the new Harrogate bar could cost as little as £2.75.

Thirty years later, the business remains independently owned and operating in an industry that has undergone considerable change.

![JThe Montey’s Team – January 2026The team behind the bar, pictured earlier this year. Some of today’s staff have parents who were drinking in Montey’s back in the nineties!](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/mont3.webp)

Recessions, the pandemic, changing drinking habits and the pressures facing independent venues have all been part of that journey. Yet Montey’s has continued to trade, retaining its distinctive rock music identity while welcoming successive generations of customers.

For founder Jay Smith, reaching the 30-year milestone is as much about the people who have passed through the doors as it is about the business itself.

![The Next Generation of Montey’s CustomersCustomers enjoying Montey’s today, showing that thirty years on, the bar continues to attract new generations.](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/montey1.webp)

## Three decades of people and memories

One of the most striking aspects of Montey’s history is the continuity among its own staff. Colin Spiers, the bar’s very first employee, is still working alongside Jay 30 years after they started out together.

That shared history has seen the pair witness the changing face of Harrogate nightlife, as well as the many personal stories that have unfolded around the bar.

Over the years, Montey’s has been the setting for friendships, relationships, break-ups and reunions. Two former members of staff even met at the venue and subsequently married.

There is also a connection between generations of customers. Some members of the current team have parents who were customers at Montey’s during the 1990s, while familiar faces from the early days continue to return.

It is a reminder that long-established hospitality venues can become more than simply places to eat or drink. For some customers, they become part of their personal history, associated with friendships, occasions and different periods of their lives.

![The Legendary LemondropMontey’s iconic Lemondrop shots, photographed beneath the instantly recognisable Lemondrop neon sign.](https://www.harrogate-news.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/lemon.webp)

## Keeping an independent venue going

Montey’s anniversary comes at a time when the hospitality sector continues to face significant challenges. Rising costs, changes in consumer behaviour and pressure on the traditional night-time economy have made operating an independent venue increasingly demanding.

Against that background, reaching three decades in business is a notable achievement.

Montey’s has retained its focus on rock music, cocktails, live bands and late-night entertainment, while introducing new ideas to keep the venue relevant to customers who may not have been born when it first opened.

The anniversary celebrations reflect that mixture of history and continuing activity, with the business looking back at its origins while also welcoming a new generation of customers.

## A cocktail journey through the decades

To mark its 30th birthday, Montey’s has introduced a special Pick Your Decade cocktail menu, revisiting drinks associated with the 1990s, 2000s and 2010s.

The 1990s selection includes the Yellow Bird, Long Island Iced Tea and Killer Punch. The 2000s are represented by drinks including Pink Fluffy Bunny, Woo Woo and Purple People Eater, while the 2010s selection features the Smokey Old Fashioned, Siesta Margarita and Dreamy Eyes.

The menu reflects how cocktail culture has evolved over the past three decades, from colourful, fruit-led drinks to more contemporary combinations and techniques.

## Anniversary weekend in Harrogate

Montey’s will celebrate its 30th birthday with a weekend of events from Friday 27 to Sunday 29 November.

Josh Ozturk will provide live music on Friday, followed by Desperado and Captain & The Bear on Saturday.

The celebrations will conclude on Sunday with an invitation-only birthday party featuring a surprise band.

Reflecting on the milestone, Jay, founder of Montey’s, said:

> **Jay, founder of Montey’s, said:** When I opened Montey’s in 1996, I don’t think anyone imagined we’d still be doing this thirty years later. We’ve been incredibly lucky to have brilliant staff, talented musicians and generations of customers who’ve made Montey’s their own. This birthday is really a celebration of everyone who’s ever been part of it.
>
> And in true Montey’s tradition, we’re holding the big party on a Sunday. We’ve always liked to see just how many people are prepared to take Monday off work!

For a business that began in 1996, the anniversary is an opportunity to recognise the customers, employees and musicians who have contributed to its history.

Thirty years on, Montey’s remains an independent presence in Harrogate’s nightlife, with its founder celebrating the milestone alongside the generations who have made the venue part of their own stories.
