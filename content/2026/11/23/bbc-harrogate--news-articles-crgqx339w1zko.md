A 1980s Pacer was the target of vandalism in Bedale

**A train on a heritage railway in North Yorkshire has been targeted by vandals, its manager said.**

Vandals are thought to have trespassed on railway tracks on the Wensleydale Railway line on Thursday night and graffitied a 1980s Pacer, which was parked near Bedale.

While the damage was painted over by the railway's operations staff the following day, the incident was described as "incredibly frustrating" and "so dangerous".

Nick Keegan, marketing and fundraising manager, said: "We're very busy running our Christmas train services, our Polar Express services, and really the last thing our small team of staff and volunteers need is to have to deal with this sort of issue."

The railway was mostly operated by volunteers who are "extra busy" at this time of the year due to maintenance taking place, Keegan said.

"There's umpteen other jobs that need their attention, that they have to be taken away from to tidy up an act of needless vandalism," he said.

![A man wearing a T-shirt reading "Wensleydale Railway" looks at the camera and smiles. He is standing inside an empty 1980s railway carriage.](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/4baa/live/2a49e030-e75f-11f1-b407-33329b1ec67a.jpg)

Nick Keegan described the incident as 'frustrating' for volunteers

Keegan also raised concerns about the vandals' safety.

"Trespass at any point is incredibly dangerous but especially at this time of year," he said.

"We're not running regular passenger services but the railway can be used at any time for infrastructure trains and engineering trains."

Christmas trains could also be running during dark evenings, he added.

Keegan said the carriages hit by vandalism were out of service and would undergo planned maintenance before returning to carrying passengers.

It is not the first time the railway, which operates between Leeming Bar and Leyburn, has been the victim of vandalism.

In 2019, seats were pulled out of a carriage, causing hundreds of pounds-worth of damage.
