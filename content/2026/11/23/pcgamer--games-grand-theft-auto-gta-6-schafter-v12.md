Stealing the Schafter V12 in GTA 6 is one of the car theft missions you'll have to complete to gain Raul's trust, but similar to the BF Bandito or Declasse Vamos, it's a little tricky. Make sure to grab the silenced pistol from the trunk of the car outside—not necessarily because you'll need it, but because it's a handy weapon to have.

Now, as you can see in the video above, you can get into the chop shop by climbing up the van around back and then onto the roof. I'd then suggest dropping down on the right side, where there are few enemies, knocking out the guy sitting by the gate, then opening it by hitting the left button.

Sneak back towards the centre of the chop shop and turn left to find the Declasse Vamos, a black unassuming car. Hop inside, start it up, and drive out of the gate you just opened. Easy, right? Now simply bring it to Randy's garage as directed. You also kill everyone inside if you wish, but my way above will get you that non-lethal Criminal Profile rating.
