A Harrogate school is extending its equestrian programme at the gallop - with a new club for its younger pupils.

Ashville Prep School is adding an to its co-curricular activities for its pupils aged between five and 11 by launching a Pony Club.

Rhiannon Wilkinson, Head of Ashville said: “We’re thrilled to add this new club to the extraordinary number of co-curricular activities and sports we already offer.

"As well as increased self-confidence and fitness, riding offers young people a sense of responsibility as well as a discipline that serves them well for life.

“We look forward to our younger pupils experiencing the benefits of spending time with horses and making new equine friends.

"Hopefully they will follow in the successful footprints, and hoofprints, of our senior school riders in the years to come.”

Aimed at pupils who are riding beginners or have little experience with horses and ponies, the club combines sessions at school with visits to Follifoot Park Riding Centre.

Its launch comes as two members of Ashville Senior School’s Equestrian Team excelled on the national stage at the prestigious Horse of the Year Show at the NEC in Birmingham.

Christina Cotton finished in fourth place in the Foxhunter class, riding Jet Set des Champs ("Pepper") after producing a double-clear round.

Meanwhile, Poppy Pollard marked her debut at the show by securing 5th place in the Search for a Star working pony class, riding her 12.3 hand Cob, Fair View Barney Bear (“Charlie”).

She is now working on her qualification for the 2027 season and has launched an Instagram profile Poppy and pony for people to follow her progress and support her.

Ashville Prep School pupils hoping to emulate their success will learn about pony care, grooming, safe handling, stable management and equipment, as part of the new Pony Club. They will also have the opportunity to ride.

The new venture is led by Senior School Classics and Latin Teacher, Mrs Suzy Styles, who has over 25 years’ experience with horses.

The programme follows British Horse Society (BHS) accredited learning, and each child receives an activity book to record their progress.
