Police have been cracking down on vehicle-related anti-social behaviour at the coast.

The operation focused on the illegal use of off-road bikes, quad bikes, electric bikes and scooters.

Officers seized a quad bike after it was being ridden in an anti-social manner on The Dell, Eastfield.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The rider will now be reported for a number of road traffic offences and will be required to attend court to answer for their actions.

“We understand the frustration that irresponsible vehicle use causes to local residents, and we will continue to carry out targeted patrols and enforcement activity to identify offenders and take positive action where offences are committed.”
