The largest house on Micklegate in York has gone on sale.

Micklegate House, a Grade I listed townhouse dating from 1752, is on the market for offers in excess of £1,750,000.

Possibly designed by John Carr – and certainly built in the famous architect’s style – it is three storeys high and seven bays wide.

Found at 88–90 Micklegate, the building sits almost opposite Holy Trinity Church.

For nearly 12 years, it has been operated as the travel hostel Safestay York. The Safestay group acquired the freehold in May 2014 and opened the hostel in January 2015.

It has 154 beds in 24 bedrooms and a cafe bar & breakfast room. The business makes a yearly turnover of more than £700,000.

The hostel was previously called Ace York. From the 1960s, Micklegate House was home to University of York departments.

Agent Christie & Co is marketing the property. It says: “The property has traded under the Safestay brand successfully but also offers a new owner a variety of options.

“With a rich history and superb period features Micklegate House is a fantastic opportunity.”

The particulars says Micklegate House has many original features “including a grand ornate staircase, attic rooms with beamed ceilings and large spacious rooms with high ceilings”.

They add it is available free of the Safestay branding.

It was built as a townhouse for John Bourchier of Beningbrough Hall.

Micklegate House is only the latest grand townhouse on York’s ‘royal street’ to go on sale in recent years.

Grade I listed Garforth House, at 54 Micklegate, was on the market at £3.5 million and sold in August.

And Bathurst House, next door to Micklegate House at 86 Micklegate, went on sale for £2,950,000 in October 2024.
