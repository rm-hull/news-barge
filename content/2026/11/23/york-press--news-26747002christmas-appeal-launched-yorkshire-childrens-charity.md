## Yorkshire Christmas appeal aims to help 3,235 children in poverty

Yorkshire Children’s Charity is appealing for donations to support 3,235 children in poverty this Christmas *(Image: Yorkshire Children's Charity)*

Yorkshire Children’s Charity is urging the public to help children in poverty this Christmas.

The charity is appealing for donations to support 3,235 children living in severe poverty across the region.

Charlotte Farrington, founder and CEO of the charity, said that the generosity shown to BBC Children in Need recently has inspired hope for the children in Yorkshire.

She said: "Watching the extraordinary generosity shown towards BBC Children in Need over the weekend gave me real hope for the 3,235 Yorkshire children we are trying to support this Christmas."

Although the BBC Children in Need appeal raised less money than last year, Ms Farrington said it was "even more remarkable", given the current financial climate.

She added: "People are still finding it in their hearts to give when life is becoming increasingly expensive for everyone."

Ms Farrington is now appealing for the same generosity to be shown in Yorkshire, where one in three children are growing up in poverty.

She said: "Around one in three children in Yorkshire are growing up in poverty, and behind that statistic are families making impossible decisions about heating, food, clothing and other everyday essentials.

"Many are working families whose wages simply do not stretch far enough, while others are facing circumstances that have left them struggling to provide even the basics.

"We hear from schools about children sleeping in their coats because they have no adequate bedding, and siblings sharing shoes because their parents cannot afford a pair each.

"These are things happening in communities across our county, often far less visibly than people might imagine, and as Christmas approaches, the pressure on those families becomes even greater."

The charity’s Yorkshire Together Christmas campaign is aiming to provide Christmas gift support for over 3,000 children, a 62 per cent increase in demand compared to last year.

The charity works with parents and carers to provide selected gifts for their children, with the presents being delivered through teachers, family support workers, social workers, and other trusted professionals.

This allows parents to wrap and give the presents to their children themselves, ensuring the children can enjoy Christmas without knowing a charity was involved.

The Yorkshire Together Christmas campaign needs to raise £194,100 to fulfil Christmas wish lists for 3,235 children across Yorkshire.

Ms Farrington said: "Just £60 provides one child's chosen presents, although donations of any amount are welcome and will be combined to help families."

People are being encouraged to donate at www.yorkshirechildrenscharity.org/christmas-2026/ and encourage their workplace to get involved.

Businesses with vans or suitable vehicles can also help deliver gifts across the region.

The charity hopes that the spirit of generosity will continue to shine through this Christmas, helping to give more Yorkshire children something to unwrap on Christmas morning.
