# Gunman jailed for life after shooting young man in targeted street attack

## Two men have been jailed for their roles in a violent street attack which saw a young man shot and two people assaulted as they tried to intervene.

Reece Barnes, 32, of Claremont Street, Rotherham, and Micahiah Lewis, 29, of Vikinglea Close, Sheffield, were sentenced at Sheffield Crown Court on Monday (Nov 23) following the shooting in September 2024.

Barnes was handed a life sentence with a minimum term of 18 years and 292 days, while Lewis was jailed for 11 years.

On Wednesday (Sep 11), 2024, emergency services were called to reports of a firearms discharge on Ferham Road in Rotherham.

During the incident, Barnes and Lewis assaulted a 20-year-old man before chasing him, with Barnes then shooting the victim.

A second man who witnessed the attack attempted to intervene but was assaulted by the pair, who struck him with a firearm and beat him.

A woman who emerged from a nearby shop to help the victims was also assaulted by Barnes and Lewis.

The 20-year-old man was taken to hospital with injuries that were not life-threatening or life-changing, while the other two victims suffered minor injuries and did not require hospital treatment.

Following an extensive investigation, officers identified the two men through CCTV footage of the attack and gathered witness statements to build their case.

Barnes pleaded guilty to possession of a firearm with intent to cause fear of violence at Sheffield Crown Court on May 5.

He also admitted inflicting grievous bodily harm with intent and assault occasioning actual bodily harm on May 7.

He was subsequently found guilty at trial of attempted murder and possession of a firearm with intent to endanger life.

Lewis pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis on December 12, 2025, and was later found guilty of possession of a firearm with intent to endanger life, grievous bodily harm with intent and assault occasioning actual bodily harm.

Barnes was found not guilty of threats to kill, while Lewis was acquitted of attempted murder at Sheffield Crown Court on May 26.

Detective Inspector Claire Moss, the senior investigating officer, said: “This was a sustained and targeted attack on a young man who could easily have been killed if it wasn’t for the intervention of two people who put themselves at risk by attempting to stop Barnes and Lewis from causing even greater harm.

“When someone discharges a firearm towards another person there is no question that they are putting lives at risk.

“I am glad they both now face a lengthy prison term which reflects the seriousness of their actions, and I am thankful to the officers who worked with determination to bring the pair to justice.

“There is no excuse to illegally carry a firearm, and I would urge anyone with information about firearm crime to get in touch with us.”
