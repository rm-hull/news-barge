One of the nine people shot at a concert in central California remained in critical condition on Sunday after being wounded in the chest, the local sheriff said.

The 26-year-old man needed surgery for his injuries and was still in a critical condition, according to the Tulare County sheriff's department.

Two other victims, aged 19 and 22, also required surgery. Six people sustained minor injuries, and some of those wounded were able to drive themselves to the hospital.

![The scene outside the area where Saturday's shooting took place](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/23/7/05/Tulare-County-Sheriffs-Office.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The shooting broke out at around 7.30 pm on Saturday at a concert attended by about 150 people in Strathmore, a small town in Tulare County, the sheriff's department said in a statement.

Strathmore is a town of roughly 3,000 people in a mountainous region in the center of the state. An unspecified number of suspects opened fire on the crowd. No arrests were announced. The concert featured multiple rappers, some of whom had traveled from out of state, officials said.

![Officials have described the shooting as being "gang related"](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/11/23/7/21/Tulare-County-Sheriffs-Office1.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The department said the show featured performers from neighboring states. The show was associated with the Norteno gang, *The Fresno Bee* reported. The Nortenos are a primarily Mexican-American gang network affiliated with the prison-based criminal organization known as Nuestra Familia, Spanish for "our family."

Authorities said that they believe the shooting “may be gang-related” but didn't elaborate. The department didn't immediately respond to emailed questions seeking further details but said there was no longer any immediate threat to the public.
