A MUCH-LOVED restaurant on the North Yorkshire coast is hoping to bring its customers back to the 1980s as it celebrates four decades in business.

Florios, an Italian restaurant in Aberdeen Walk, Scarborough, has revealed that it will be hosting a one-off promotion to celebrate 40 years on December 20.

It will be offering pizzas and pastas for just £1.75 for one day only - bringing back its original happy hour price from four decades ago as a thank you to customers.

Within less than an hour of posting the promotion, the restaurant’s website received over 400 bookings from customers hoping to take advantage of the deal – which has now sold out.

In a statement, the owners said: "We knew our 40th Birthday Special was going to be popular, but we NEVER expected this.

"Within the first 20 minutes, the demand was so overwhelming that our booking system simply couldn’t cope.

"Unfortunately, it started overbooking, so we had no choice but to switch it off. So here we are… back to good old pen and paper. Just like the old days

“From our family to yours… THANK YOU, SCARBOROUGH! And now… let’s celebrate like it’s 1986!!!”

In the comments, customers called for the restaurant to extend the promotion over the full weekend and shared their experience of visiting the restaurant over the decades.

As part of the celebration, Florios will also be supporting a local charity, with customers invited to suggest causes and vote in a public poll.

For more information, visit Florios Ristorante Scarborough on Facebook.
