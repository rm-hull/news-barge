## Leeds Bradford Airport adds 7 new holiday destinations for summer 2027

Leeds Braford Airport has unveiled seven new destinations as it expands its summer 2027 network *(Image: LBA)*

Leeds Bradford Airport has unveiled its largest-ever network of routes, including seven new destinations for summer 2027.

Next year, the airport will offer more than 100 routes across Europe and North Africa, including seven new routes: Tivat (Montenegro), Bari (Italy), Hurghada (Egypt), Tirana (Albania), Enfidha (Tunisia), Preveza (Greece), and Santorini (Greece).

Ryanair has also announced new routes to support the airport’s expansion.

Declan Maguire, aviation director at Leeds Bradford Airport, said: "This is a hugely significant milestone for Leeds Bradford Airport.

"For the first time in our history, we are offering more routes than ever before, providing customers across Yorkshire and the North with unprecedented choice and connectivity.

"Adding seven brand-new destinations for summer 2027 is a fantastic achievement and demonstrates the confidence our airline partners have in both the airport and the region we serve.

"Whether customers are looking for a relaxing Mediterranean escape, a cultural city break or an exciting new destination to explore, there has never been a better time to fly from Leeds Bradford Airport.

"This growth reflects the strength of demand across our catchment and our commitment to delivering greater choice, convenience and value for our customers."

Passengers travelling through Leeds Bradford will also be able to access long-haul connections via Dublin through Aer Lingus and via Amsterdam Schiphol with KLM.

Phil Ward, chief operations officer at Jet2, welcomed the news.

Mr Ward said: "We're delighted to continue expanding our programme from Leeds Bradford Airport.

"The introduction of so many exciting new destinations gives customers and travel agent partners even more choice for Summer 2027 and responds directly to continued demand for our award-winning flights and holidays from Yorkshire.

"As always, we're working closely with Leeds Bradford Airport to deliver fantastic travel experiences for our customers, and we're pleased to play our part in this record-breaking growth story."

Ryanair has not yet launched its full programme but is planning to introduce new routes.

Jade Kirwan, Ryanair’s Director of Comms, said: "Although we have not yet launched our full summer 2027 schedule, Ryanair is giving our UK customers in the North a sneak peek at what new routes we will be offering with the announcement of these four exciting new summer 2027 routes from Leeds Bradford to Bari, Budapest, Corfu and Tirana.

"Each of these new Ryanair routes will operate twice per week from March 29, 2027, giving our Yorkshire customers even more low-fare choice and regular connections for their Summer 2027 getaways.

"Tickets are available to book via Ryanair’s app/website now."

A spokesperson for the airport said the expanded route network supports its ambition to connect Yorkshire to an ever-growing range of destinations in Europe and beyond.
