Panto season is beginning – oh yes it is!

It’s one week to go until the Ebor Players present *Mother Goose*at Bishopthorpe Village Hall, from**Monday 30 November to Saturday 5 December**.

It’s a fun, feathery and fabulous family show with all the traditional cheering and booing, songs and laughter, nonsense and romance, colourful costumes and slapstick surprises, peril and villainous skulduggery you could wish for.

Who will win the battle of good versus evil: Fairy Aurora or Smorg, the wicked troll? Can Mother Goose and her beloved Heidi escape the treacherous clutches of the Baron and his sidekick, Gonk? Will Freya and Sven find love amidst the mayhem? What will the Grand High Snow Goose have to say about it all?

The panto will be at Bishopthorpe Village Hall, as it has been since the Ebor Players’ first show in 1994.

*Mother Goose* is rected and produced by Philippa Parrish, written by Ben Crocker and sponsored by Martin & Co.

The start time for each evening is 7:30pm, but with an earlier start of 7:00pm on Monday, and a matinee on Saturday at 2pm. The Wednesday and Saturday night shows are adults only, with cabaret-style seating.

Tickets start from £10. Find out more and book your tickets here.
