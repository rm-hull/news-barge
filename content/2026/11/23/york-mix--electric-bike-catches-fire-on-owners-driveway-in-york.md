Firefighters were called in after an electric bike caught fire in York this afternoon (Monday).

It was on **Water Lane** at**12.59pm**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “The Acomb crew responded to an electric bike which had ignited on the driveway outside a house.

#### Water Lane, York

“Crews extinguished the fire using a hose reel jet and gave advice to the occupants regarding its safe disposal.”
