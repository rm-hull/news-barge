## After days of negotiations, COP31 meeting in Antalya, Turkey ended without reaching consensus. We explore the consequences for the planet with Ciarán Cuffe, co-chair of the European Green Party. We also speak with General Seán Clancy, Chairman of the EU Military Committee.

## On today's show:

The COP31 climate summit in Antalya has ended in a diplomatic failure. After two weeks of negotiations and nearly 50 hours of overtime, almost 200 countries walked away without an overarching agreement. The main sticking points? Fossil fuels and climate finance. The final draft did not even mention fossil fuels, prompting the EU to reject it. We speak about it with **Ciarán Cuffe, co-chair of the European Green Party.** We also talk with**General Sean Clancy, Chairman of the EU Military Committee** about how ready are the European armies to face a major military confrontation two days ahead of the next meeting of EU Chiefs of Defence.

And we welcome **Bulgaria's Deputy Prime Minister, Atanas Pekanov,** as we explore how the war in Ukraine is reshaping Europe's security landscape. In Bulgaria and the Black Sea the risks and threats to maritime traffic are putting pressure on NATO's southeastern flank.
