A MAN has been found dead at his home in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police is assisting the Coroner’s Office to locate the next of kin of Rob Ballard, 75.




Mr Ballard was sadly found dead at his home in Marton Court in Whitby over the weekend, police said.

A force spokesperson said: “There are no suspicious circumstances to his death.

“If you have any information that would help the Coroner to locate any members of Mr Ballard’s family please email [coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:coroner@northyorkshire.police.uk.)”
