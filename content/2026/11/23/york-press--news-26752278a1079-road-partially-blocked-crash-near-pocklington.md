## Main Yorkshire road partially blocked after crash

There has been a crash on Hull Road near Pocklington. *(Image: Contributed)>*

A MAIN road in Yorkshire has been partially blocked after a crash.

Motorists are being reminded of slow traffic after a crash on the A1079 Hull Road, near Pocklington, this morning (Monday, November 23).




AA Traffic confirmed: “Partially blocked and slow traffic due to accident on A1079, from Feoffee Common Lane to Carr Lane.”

The Press has approached Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
