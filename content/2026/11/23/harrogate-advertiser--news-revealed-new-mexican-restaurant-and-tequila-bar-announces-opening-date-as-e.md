A new authentic Mexican restaurant and tequila bar is set to bring a taste of Mexico to Harrogate town centre, with Casa Del Alma officially opening its doors this December.

The new venue, located on Princes Square, has announced that it will welcome its first customers on Friday, December 4, promising a vibrant Latino-inspired dining experience featuring Mexican food, cocktails and tequila.

Casa Del Alma said it was excited to finally reveal its opening date after months of anticipation, inviting diners to experience “delicious Mexican food, incredible cocktails, tequila and a vibrant atmosphere” in the heart of Harrogate.

The restaurant aims to bring the flavours and spirit of Mexico to the town, offering a colourful setting for everything from casual meals and date nights to celebrations with friends and family.

A spokesperson for Casa Del Alma said: “Harrogate, the wait is finally over - Casa Del Alma is opening on Friday 4 December.

“Get ready for delicious Mexican food, incredible cocktails, tequila and a vibrant atmosphere as we bring a taste of Mexico to Harrogate.”

Whether it is a festive gathering, a night out with friends or a special occasion, Casa Del Alma is inviting locals to be among the first to experience its Mexican-inspired menu and atmosphere.

The new venue will become Casa del Alma's third venture following the success of its restaurant in Darlington and its popular street food venture in Saltburn.

The opening marks another addition to Harrogate's growing independent food and drink scene.
