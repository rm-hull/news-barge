National Rail now expects disruption until 1.30pm after a track inspection blocked lines near Harrogate. A replacement bus is running between Leeds and York.

National Rail now expects disruption on the line through Harrogate to last until 1.30pm, half an hour later than it first said, while a track safety inspection continues between Horsforth and Harrogate.

* **What:** Some lines are blocked. Trains “may be cancelled, delayed or revised”.
* **Where:** Horsforth to Harrogate, on Northern’s Leeds to York line and LNER’s London Kings Cross route.
* **When:** Reported at 10.53am. National Rail expects disruption until 1.30pm.

## What National Rail and Northern said

“A safety inspection of the track between Horsforth and Harrogate means some lines are blocked,” National Rail says.

In its customer advice on that page, Northern says: “Train services are unable to run between Leeds and York via Harrogate in this direction.” It adds: “Network Rail staff have been dispatched to site.”

## What it means for you

Northern says its services between Leeds and York via Garforth are not affected. It says ticket restrictions, “including Advance and Peak restrictions”, have been lifted in the affected areas during the disruption.

A replacement bus run by J&B left Leeds for York at 11.10am, calling at Burley Park, Headingley, Horsforth, Weeton, Pannal, Hornbeam Park, Harrogate, Starbeck, Knaresborough, Cattal, Hammerton and Poppleton. Northern says journeys by road “may be extended by up to 60 minutes”. Bicycles, e-bikes and dogs, except service dogs, are not allowed.

If your train is cancelled, National Rail says you can use another participating train company within two hours of your original departure, or claim a full refund.
