Russia has threatened “painful consequences” after Ukraine claimed devastating strikes on two Russian ships at a port in Libya.

Ukraine sank a Russian naval corvette using naval drones in Benghazi and also hit the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which president Volodymyr Zelensky claimed had been used to transport weapons.

A Russian Ka-27 helicopter was also destroyed along with the corvette, Zelensky said.

Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said on Monday: “These actions result in highly painful consequences for the Kyiv regime.”

“I would like to recall a statement made by Putin in Shenzhen, in which he affirmed that this practice, this course of action by our military will continue,” he said, adding that a full response would come from the defence ministry.

Zelensky said meanwhile that Ukraine continues its operations in designated waters. The attack came only a day after a drone strike on a Russian-military linked cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland.

## Top story: Zelensky says Ukraine sank Russian warship in Libya

Ukraine sank a Russian naval corvette in a Libyan port using seven naval drones, President Volodymyr Zelensky said on Monday, showing Ukraine’s growing ability to strike Russian assets far from the frontlines.

Zelensky said a second target was also struck, identified as the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which he said Russia had used to transport weapons.

“We continue operations in designated waters,” Zelensky posted on Telegram. He said a Russian Ka-27 combat helicopter had been destroyed along with the corvette.

Libya's eastern-based government said the corvette, the Merkury, was docked at the Benghazi naval base on Sunday when it was hit in a suspected drone attack. It said the ship had stopped to refuel and resupply on its way back to Russia under a cooperation agreement.

After a civil war, western Libya is controlled by an internationally recognised Government of National Unity based in Tripoli, while the east is controlled by military commander Khalifa Haftar, whose Libyan National Army has ties with Russia.

## Russia ‘planning to place satellite with nuclear weapon in space by end of the year’

Russia is planning to put nuclear weapons in space by the end of this year, according to a disturbing new report.

The German military and Federal Intelligence Service have reportedly warned other Nato states that a satellite carrying a nuclear warhead could be in orbit by December, a report by Germany’s NDR, WDR and Sueddeutsche Zeitung said.

Western services exchanged intelligence showing that the satellite, named Sputnik-S, was assembled several years ago and is stationed in the Moscow region, the report said.

Such a weapon detonated in low orbit would have devastating consequences for both military and civilian infrastructure on Earth, the head of US Space Command warned earlier this year, referencing similar intelligence.

## Zelensky says Ukraine carried out 'exemplary' strikes in Libyan port

Volodymyr Zelensky has given the first confirmation that Ukraine targeted vessels in Libya's Benghazi port on Sunday, identifying the targets as a Russian corvette warship called Merkury and a Russian dry cargo vessel called MV Angara.

Zelensky said the strikes involved Ukrainian-made naval drones and called it "an exemplary Ukrainian operation".

He said the Merkury, which sank following the attack, was "one of their newest ships, commissioned in 2023, during the full-scale war", and that a combat helicopter was also destroyed.

"We continue our operations in designated waters. We are responding to Russia’s aggression," he wrote on X.
