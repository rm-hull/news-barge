The Yorkshire Museum is set for a spectacular festive transformation as it becomes a magical immersive cinema experience this Christmas.

The Enchanted Christmas Cinema is heading to Yorkshire for the first time, turning the historic York venue’s Tempest Anderson Hall into a cosy forest cinema.

The rarely-seen Hall will be adorned with real trees – plus snow fall before every screening!

Guests will step into a winter woodland setting with a bar serving mulled wine, hot chocolate, popcorn and festive treats before settling in to watch some of the best-loved Christmas films, including *Home Alone, Elf*and* Love Actually*.

The event will run from **21 to 23 December,** with screenings from 12pm to 8pm.

The Christmas films include:

* The Muppet Christmas Carol
* Home Alone
* Elf
* Love Actually
* The Holiday

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Enchanted-Christmas-Cinema-York3.jpg)

Dominic Davies, co-founder of Enchanted Christmas Cinema, said: “Christmas films are made to be watched together, and where better than some of the most beautiful venues around the country?

“We’re thrilled to be working with the Yorkshire Museum. It’s a building with so much heritage, and the perfect setting for a Christmas movie.”

Jocelyn Palmer, senior events manager at York Museums Trust said; “It is wonderful to be hosting the Enchanted Christmas Cinema at the Yorkshire Museum this year and bringing more festive entertainment into the museum.

“Visitors can get cosy, watch some classic favourites whilst enjoying the beautiful surroundings of the Yorkshire Museum and historic York Museum Gardens.”

Tickets are £18 for adults, and £14 for children under 12. Carers are free. It’s recommended to book sooner rather than later as tickets are selling fast with some screenings already sold out.
