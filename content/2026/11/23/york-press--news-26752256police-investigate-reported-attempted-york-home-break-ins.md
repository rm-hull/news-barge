POLICE have launched an investigation into reports of a man attempting to break into homes in York.

North Yorkshire Police said the man was spotted trying to break into addresses in the Beagle Ridge Drive area at around 3am on November 20.




A force spokesperson said: "We would like to speak with him in connection with a number of reports received from residents.

"If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us via email at [sylvia.matla@northyorkshire.police.uk.](mailto:sylvia.matla@northyorkshire.police.uk.)"

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference 12260227839.
