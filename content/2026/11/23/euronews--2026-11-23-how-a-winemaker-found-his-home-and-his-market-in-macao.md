## From Portuguese heritage and independent restaurants to luxury resorts and quiet hilltop runs, winemaker Eddie McDougall explains how Macao shaped both his career and his life since he moved there for his wife’s work.

“Macao has been an amazing place for me professionally because I work in wine,” says McDougall, Oenologist & Founder, E.J. McDougall & The Flying Winemaker.

He says the city’s push to become a gastronomic hub “plays perfectly” for his work, and its luxury venues help him seal deals. “Meeting clients in a luxury environment of course helps get the deal done,” he says. He also created the Wynn Signature Chinese Wine Awards, which he describes as the biggest of their kind in the world.

Macao’s Portuguese and Chinese heritage runs through his view of the city. “Take the Portuguese heritage, plant it in China, grow it there, bottle it in Macao,” he says.
