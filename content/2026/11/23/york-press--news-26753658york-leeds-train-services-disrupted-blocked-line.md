Rail services between York and Leeds face disruption after reports of blocked lines.

Northern services between the two cities will be disrupted until 1.30pm today (Monday, November 23).




A National Rail spokesperson said: “A safety inspection of the track between Horsforth and Harrogate means some lines are blocked.

“As a result, trains between these stations may be cancelled, delayed or revised.

“Services between Leeds and York via Harrogate are being disrupted due a safety inspection of the line.

“Train services are unable to run between Leeds and York via Harrogate in this direction.

“Northern services between Leeds and York via Garforth are not affected.”

If your train is cancelled, you can use another participating train operator's service up to two hours before or after your original departure time to complete your journey.

If you choose not to travel, or cannot finish your journey because of cancellations, you are entitled to a full refund.
