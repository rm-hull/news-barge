**Felicia Pochhammer & Lucy Davalou**

## Henri Vanhanen, a senior fellow with the Transatlantic Security Initiative, joined Euronews’ new show Europe Tonight to discuss growing security concerns and the prospects for peace in Ukraine.

The NATO Parliamentary Assembly has been holding its annual session in Berlin amid a fresh wave of security incidents across Europe, including hybrid attacks and airspace violations.

Speaking at the meeting, NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte warned of "intensifying malign activities targeting our nations."

Henri Vanhanen, a senior fellow with the Transatlantic Security Initiative at the Atlantic Council’s Scowcroft Center for Strategy and Security, joined Euronews’ new show Europe Tonight to discuss the growing security concerns and the prospects for peace in Ukraine.
