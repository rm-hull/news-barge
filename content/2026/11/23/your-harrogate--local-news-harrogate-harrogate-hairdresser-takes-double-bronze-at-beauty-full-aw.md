A Harrogate town centre hairdresser has picked up two bronze awards at the Beauty Full Awards 2026.

Robyn Mackin, who recently went self-employed, took bronze in both the Hair Extensionist of the Year and Blonde Specialist of the Year categories.

Both categories are open to freelance, mobile and salon-based hairdressers from around the world.

An independent panel of industry professionals assesses the entries.

For hair extensions, judges look at how safely the extensions have been fitted and how well they blend with the client’s own hair.

In the blonde specialist category, they assess the shade the stylist achieves and the condition of the hair after colouring.

Robyn said:

> “It’s been a really exciting journey so far, and I’m so grateful to all my wonderful clients who have supported me along the way.
>
> "To receive this recognition so early on in my self-employed journey makes it even more special.”
