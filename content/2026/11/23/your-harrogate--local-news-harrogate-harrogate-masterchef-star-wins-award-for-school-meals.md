A Harrogate chef who cooks lunch for up to 100 children a day at Great Ouseburn Primary School has won a school catering award.

Owen Diaram won the Customer Experience Award from The School Food People.

It was one of two awards for North Yorkshire Education Services on the night.

Owen, who reached the quarter-finals of BBC’s MasterChef in 2023, has seen the number of pupils choosing school meals more than double since he took over the kitchen.

He said:

> “When I took on the role I was cooking for 40 kids, and now I regularly cook for 90-100 children every day - alone.
>
> "I've more than doubled the take up of school meals and I ensure the taste and value for every child in school.”

Outside the school kitchen, Owen runs his private dining business, Cook with Owen, sells his own spice mixes and gives cookery demonstrations at food festivals across Yorkshire.
