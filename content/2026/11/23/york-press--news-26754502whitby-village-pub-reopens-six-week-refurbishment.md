## Much-loved village North Yorkshire pub reopens after six-week revamp

Imagine dining in such lovely surrounds *(Image: Facebook)*

A much-loved village pub near Whitby is back open after a six-week refurbishment.

The Board Inn at Lealholm in the North York Moors National Park is a freehold pub which dates to 1746.

Until several years ago, it was run by Alistair Dean and his partner Karen, but they decided to sell the business, whilst still owning the property.

In October, the pub was taken over by Susan Agnew, who for the past four years has had the Weatsheaf in nearby Egton.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21753291.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An elegant interior *(Image: Facebook)*

Susan said the venue needed some ‘tlc’ to get it back to what it was.

The refurbishment has seen a complete overhaul, with new furniture inside, with new soft furnishings and the interior also being decorated.

The outside river terrace has been overhauled with new seating and planting.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21753288.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The bar and its beers *(Image: Facebook)*

The lounge features a new log-burning stove.

The bar has been overhauled, and a new cellar has been fitted.

The toilets have also been revamped, along with a new kitchen being fitted.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21753321.jpg?type=mds-article-620)An elegant and stylish interior *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The menu has also been revamped, with main meals made from scratch using locally-sourced produce.

The bedrooms will also receive a refurbishment in the coming week, with completion in a month or so.

Susan said: “The Board Inn has always been a good pub in its heyday. The locals loved it. It had a good village feel. When Alistair and Karen came out it just wasn’t the same. It needed a bit of tlc and to give the village its pub back.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21753322.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sunday lunch *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The opening night was on Friday, which Susan says was a success.

“I am grateful the locals have their pub back. We are grateful for the support of the community and long may it continue.”

Customer reaction confirms this.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21753297.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The magnificent venue to enjoy *(Image: Facebook)*

One posted: “Just had a lovely Sunday Lunch at the Board. Food was plentiful and tasty. A varied wine list and good beer. A great atmosphere, warm and cosy. Definitely worth a try and for us will be a regular visit.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21753336.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Board Inn from the outside *(Image: Pic supplied)*

“Sue and her team have worked really hard to make this a worthwhile venture bringing the local pub back to Lealholm in a way that Alistair, Karen and the villagers will be pleased with. Long May it continue.”

One more said: “Just been to meet up with friends what a fabulous evening. Lovely, tasteful refurbishment, maintaining the local pub feel. Great menus. Long may it continue. Well done everyone.”

Another added: “Excellent meal tonight. Can highly recommend. Lovely to get our village pub back. Well done.”
