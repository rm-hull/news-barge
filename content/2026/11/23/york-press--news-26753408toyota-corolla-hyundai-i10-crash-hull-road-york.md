POLICE are appealing for witnesses after a crash involving two cars.

The incident took place in Hull Road at the junction with Grimston Bar Park and Ride on November 14, at 1.32pm.

It involved a blue Toyota Corolla and a grey Hyundai I10.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us."

You can do this by emailing Alex Kemp at [alexandra.kemp@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:alexandra.kemp@northyorkshire.police.uk), or by calling 101.

Anyone with information is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police, quoting reference number 12260223969.
