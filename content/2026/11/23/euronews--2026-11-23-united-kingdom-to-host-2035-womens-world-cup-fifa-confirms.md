## The chief executives of the English, Northern Irish, Scottish and Welsh football associations released a joint statement on Monday calling it a "landmark moment."

The 2035 FIFA Women’s World Cup is set to be hosted by England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales, FIFA confirmed on Monday, marking the first time a World Cup will have been held in the UK since the 1966 men's tournament.

The bid - the only one for the tournament - was approved by acclamation during a virtual meeting of the world governing body's member associations on Monday.

The meeting also saw the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica awarded hosting duties for the 2031 edition, which will see 48 teams take part for the first time.

The 2035 "All Together" bid proposed holding fixtures across 19 grounds, including London's Wembley and Emirates stadiums, Windsor Park in Belfast, Hampden Park in Glasgow and Cardiff's Principality Stadium.

The chief executives of the four associations released a joint statement on Monday calling it a "landmark moment for England, Scotland, Northern Ireland and Wales."

"What follows now is eight and a half years of work; on facilities, on coaching, on officiating, on the pathways that take a girl from her first game to a professional contract," they said. "A tournament of this scale is an opportunity for the whole of the women’s game, and we want its benefits felt in the places that need them most."

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has said that the 2031 and 2035 women's tournaments "should reach far beyond the pitch."

"More women player(s), more girls inspired, more women coaches, more women referees, more women administrators, more women leaders. Our priorities for the women's game are very, very simple: we have to expand the game globally, we have to close the gaps that still, of course, exist and we have to build the future together," he added.

## 2027

The next FIFA Women's World Cup is due to kick off in Brazil in June 2027, running from the 24 June to 25 July.

Host cities include Belo Horizonte, Brasília, Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo, with matches set to be held in iconic stadiums such as the Maracanã.
