Scratch cards were allegedly stolen from a Sainsbury’s store in York city centre.

Police have released CCTV images of a man they wish to speak to following the reported theft which took place on October 10 at the Piccadilly branch in York.

A spokesman for North Yorkshire Police said: "Please contact us if you recognise the man pictured on CCTV, as he may have information that will assist our investigation."

**Recommended reads:**

Anyone with information is asked to email [ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:ben.hepworth@northyorkshire.police.uk) or call 101, quoting reference 12260199235.

People can also contact police through "Live Chat" via its website, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, or contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
