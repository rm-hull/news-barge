Santa and his Lion Elves will be back on the streets of Knaresborough this week.

The first stop on Thursday (26th November) is still under wraps, but families can find out when Santa is heading their way by following the sleigh updates on WhatsApp.

With help from Piccadilly Motors, the sleigh will make its way around town on weeknights, playing Christmas music as Santa waves to families along the route.

Knaresborough Lions' Santa said:

> "I can't wait to see all those wonderful Knaresborough smiles again.
>
> “My sleigh is guaranteed Whamageddon-friendly. You're safe with me!”

Knaresborough Lions are also collecting donations for their Christmas appeal to support their work in the local community.

*Anyone wishing to contribute can donate via PayPal.*
