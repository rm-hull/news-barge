Shops across Ripon will stay open late this Thursday (26th November) for the city’s Christmas shopping trail.

An artisan market at Café in the Park in Spa Gardens will run from 3pm to 8pm.

Local artists and makers will sell gifts including crochet, leatherwork, candles and soaps.

Café owner Jane Aksut said:

> “The market will run from 3pm until 8pm and all stall holders have agreed to include some goods suitable for those with a pocket money budget.”

On Westgate, author Jess Kidd will sign copies of her latest novel, *Little Spark*, at The Little Ripon Bookshop from 6.30pm.

Owners Gill and Simon Edwards will serve complimentary cocktails.

On North Street, The Castle will offer £5 off purchases of £30 or more on the night, along with a glass of mulled wine and free parking behind the shop.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b03f7ed98f79aa578b.png)

Saint Michael’s Hospice Shop will hold a 10% off sale and open a pop-up Christmas shop across the road.

The GreenHouse will also reveal the first of its daily Advent calendar offers in its shop window.

Shoppers can stop for a glass of wine and a mince pie at This Other Eden on Fishergate, while Majestic Wine on North Street will host a Christmas wine tasting until 8pm.

On Kirkgate, Liz and Kevin Hill, who opened Karma ten years ago, will unveil their Christmas gift display.

Further along the street, The Portly Pig will sell raffle tickets in aid of local charity Dementia Forward.
