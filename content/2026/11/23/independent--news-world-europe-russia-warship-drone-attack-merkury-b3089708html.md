Satellite images have revealed the fate of a Russian warship that was hit by seven drones in a Libyan port on Sunday, as part of an “exemplary operation” from Ukraine.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said on Monday that the country sank a Russian naval corvette called the Merkury shortly after a drone strike on a Russian military-linked cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland.

The Merkury, believed to have been one of Russia’s newest ships that was commissioned in 2023, had docked in the Libyan port of Benghazi to refuel and take on supplies.

![Before and after images reveal the warship sunk following the attacks](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/09/2026-11-23T090225Z_382853772_RC28TOA9O2SW_RTRMADP_3_UKRAINE-CRISIS-SHIPS.JPG?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Images following the drone strike show the vessel had started to sink, with only its bow visible above the water.

Zelenskiy said a second target off Libya that was also hit on Sunday was the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which he said Russia had used to transport weapons.

He added that a Russian Ka-27 combat helicopter had been destroyed along with the corvette.

Russia's Defence Ministry did not immediately comment on either incident.

![The drone attack has been criticised by the UN mission in Libya](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/09/download..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Libya's eastern-based government said that the Merkury was docked at the Benghazi naval base ⁠on Sunday when it was hit in a suspected drone attack.

The UN mission in Libya expressed alarm at the attacks, declaring its “grave concern” and stressing that Libya “should not be allowed to become an arena for external conflicts”.

The government also condemned the attack as a violation of the country's sovereignty and a threat to maritime safety and warned that it would take legal and diplomatic measures against anyone found responsible.

"We continue our operations in designated waters,” Zelenskiy wrote on X (formerly Twitter).

“We are responding to Russia’s aggression.”

On Sunday, Russian forces struck energy facilities and infrastructure in the Ukrainian port city of Izmail, according to the defence ministry.

Russia also ‌struck a ⁠vessel near Odesa, the ministry said, claiming it was carrying military cargo. Those claims could not be immediately verified.

Meanwhile, Zelensky also confirmed that a seven-year-old boy had been pulled alive from the rubble 14 hours after a Russian airstrike flattened a residential building in the Ukrainian city of Dymer, killing at least 11 people.
