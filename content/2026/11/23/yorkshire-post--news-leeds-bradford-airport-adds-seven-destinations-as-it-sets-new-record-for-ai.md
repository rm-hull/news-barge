# Leeds Bradford Airport adds seven destinations as it sets new record for airline routes

## Leeds Bradford Airport has unveiled seven new routes to towns and cities across Europe and northern Africa, saying it is the “largest range of routes ever offered in the airport's history”.

The airport said the announcement that they are now offering over 100 routes, highlights growing demand for international travel across the region.

The new destinations include Tivat (Montenegro), Bari (Italy), Hurghada (Egypt), Tirana (Albania), Enfidha (Tunisia), Preveza (Greece) and Santorini (Greece).

LBA’s aviation director Declan Maguire said: “This is a hugely significant milestone for Leeds Bradford Airport.

"For the first time in our history, we are offering more routes than ever before, providing customers across Yorkshire and the North with unprecedented choice and connectivity.

“Adding seven brand-new destinations for Summer 2027 is a fantastic achievement and demonstrates the confidence our airline partners have in both the airport and the region we serve.

"Whether customers are looking for a relaxing Mediterranean escape, a cultural city break or an exciting new destination to explore, there has never been a better time to fly from Leeds Bradford Airport.”

In 2025, LBA saw a record 4.45 million passengers fly, nearly six per cent over 2024.

According to Cirium data, Dublin will remain the most-served market from LBA in the first half of 2027. LBA will now offer flights to 11 destinations in Greece.
