If you've looked around the apartment you inherited from Vanessa in GTA 6's third chapter, you might've noticed her abandoned laptop in the bedroom requires a password. You can't manually enter it, you have to choose from a series of guesses.

Rather than just endlessly inputting random options, you can find the password by investigating two objects located in the apartment:

* Look at the drawing of the dog wearing a crown on the left bedside table and flip it around to read "Class of '15 reunion".
* Pick up Muffins' certificate on the table to the left just as you enter the apartment, opposite where the cache is kept.

Now head back to the laptop, interact with it, and choose these three words in succession:

* Muffins - Queen - 15

This is because Muffins is her dog, she's a queen (illustrated by the drawing), and 15 because… well, I guess she needed a number and chose her class reunion.

If you tab across in the laptop, you can read Vanessa's messages, which give a clue as to what happened to her. You'll also find a mysterious message that mentions "Auntie's Place in La Perle" and gives you a code to use.

## Where to find Auntie's Place in La Perle

The garage this code unlocks is just around the corner from Aunt Zola's Soul Food in the La Perle district of Vice City. Keep going down the street, turn right onto the main road where the Twa Ponya tattoo shop is on the left side. Drive a little way down, but before you reach Aline's Thrift Store, turn right into a little parking area with a take-out restaurant labelled as Auntie's Place, with a little awning.

The garage is over on its right. Enter the safe code just below the graffiti bow on the right side and you'll unlock the Del Rio Boulevard Garage and Vanessa's Pink Growler.
