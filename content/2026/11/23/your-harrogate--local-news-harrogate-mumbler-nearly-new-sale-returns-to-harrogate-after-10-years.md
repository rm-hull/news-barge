The Mumbler Nearly New Sale is returning to Harrogate for the first time in a decade.

Families can buy and sell second-hand baby and children’s clothes and toys at Saltergate Primary School Hall on Saturday 5th December.

The sale runs from 10.30am until noon. Entry is £1 per adult and free for children.

Adults can pay by cash or card at the door, but shoppers should bring cash for purchases.

Sellers can book a table for £6.50, half the usual £13 fee.

Each table measures 4ft by 2ft and includes storage space underneath. An extra space alongside the table for larger items such as prams costs £5.

Spaces are limited, and anyone interested in selling can **contact Mumbler to book.**

*The school hall is on Newby Crescent. Parking is available, and the number 3 bus runs from Harrogate bus station to Jennyfield.*
