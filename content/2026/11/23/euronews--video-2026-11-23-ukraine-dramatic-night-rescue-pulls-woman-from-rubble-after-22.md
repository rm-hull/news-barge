After spending **22 hours trapped beneath concrete slabs**, 79-year-old** Valentyna**was** pulled alive from the wreckage**of a five-storey apartment building in** Dymer, north of Kyiv**, devastated by a** Russian drone strike**. Found on the first floor, she was handed to paramedics and taken to hospital, offering** a rare moment of relief amid the destruction**.

Under the glare of floodlights, rescuers **searched through mountains of broken concrete and twisted metal**, while sniffer dogs combed the ruins for survivors. Emergency workers carried injured residents to ambulances, recovered bodies from the rubble and even brought a cat to safety.

Ukraine's State Emergency Service announced the rescue on Monday, as **the death toll climbed to 11 and two people remained missing**. The attack, which struck overnight on 22 November, destroyed part of the residential block, leaving emergency crews working through the night in search of** anyone still buried beneath the rubble**.
