# Leeds United's Elland Road to play part in UK staging of 2035 Women's World Cup

![Leeds United's Elland Road to host fixtures in the 2035 Women's World Cup (Picture: Molly Darlington/Getty Images)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOmQ5ODE3YmY5LThjMzItNGMxOC1iNTQwLWMwYzUwNDA5NTcwMzpiYjI3MzI4NC05OWM1LTQ4OWItYWQyMy0zYWQwYWE3MWFlMWU=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=990&quality=70&enable=upscale)


## England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales have been confirmed as the joint hosts of the 2035 Women’s World Cup.

The four-nation bid was given the official seal of approval in a vote by acclamation at an Extraordinary FIFA Congress on Monday, having been the sole valid bid in contention for the event.

Leeds United's Elland Road is one of the 19 stadiums proposed to host games across the four countries.

Bramall Lane, Sheffield, and New York Stadium in Rotherham staged games at the 2022 European Championships which England’s women won.

It will be the first men’s or women’s World Cup finals to be staged in the UK since the 1966 men’s tournament.

Nineteen stadiums are proposed, with 14 in England, one in Northern Ireland, two in Scotland and two in Wales in a tournament which will feature 48 teams. One of those venues – Old Trafford – has been included in its current guise but any rebuilt stadium is envisaged to be a host.

A further three stadiums could be added – Birmingham’s new Powerhouse Stadium, Wrexham’s redeveloped Racecourse Ground and a potential new Chelsea stadium – which would take the tournament to 16 cities and 22 stadiums across the four nations.

The bid team projected the tournament would attract 3.5 billion viewers worldwide, and that 4.5 million tickets would be sold.

FIFA’s evaluation of the bid, published earlier this month, rated it 4.3 out of five.

For England this represents another chance to host a major women’s tournament, having hosted the European Championship in 2022 which the Lionesses won.

The Congress also approved the joint bid by Mexico, the United States, Costa Rica and Jamaica to host the 2031 finals, which will be the first to feature 48 teams.

Responding to today's announcement by FIFA, Leeds City Council deputy leader and executive member for economy Councillor Salma Arif said: "It is hugely exciting news that the FIFA Women's World Cup 2035 is to be staged here in the UK. To see the best teams and players playing in the biggest tournament in the world will be fantastic to watch and will inspire people of all ages to get involved and be part of it.

"We are delighted that Leeds and Elland Road is a candidate city and stadium to host matches in the tournament, and we will be doing everything we can as part of the selection process to ensure the eyes of the world are on our city and region when this incredible event comes to our country."
