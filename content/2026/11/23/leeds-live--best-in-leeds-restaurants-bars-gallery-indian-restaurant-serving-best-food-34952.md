# Indian restaurant serving the 'best food in Leeds' with 'impeccable' service

## We paid a visit to the stylish restaurant serving some wonderful dishes

Take a look at the wonderful food you can feast on visiting a restaurant serving ‘the best food in Leeds’.

Tamatanga, based at The Light, has earned an army of fans since opening in the city back in 2023. The restaurant group, which has venues in Leicester, Nottingham and Birmingham, serves a packed menu of Indian cuisine with a modern twist.

Menu highlights include a variety of meat, veggie, vegan and gluten friendly dishes. There’s a vibrant children’s menu alongside daily cocktails deals.

You can sip on two-for-one cocktails all day Sunday to Thursday. The incentive is also available until 7pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

Additionally, there’s also a bottomless thali offer which gives diners the chance to explore Indian cuisine with an unlimited feast. Tamatanga’s thali includes a delicious range of curries alongside a colourful selection of freshly prepared side dishes - all created by the restaurant’s expert all-Indian kitchen team.

Dating back thousands of years, a thali is an Indian set meal served on a round platter or tray. The meal typically features an assortment of small dishes, rice and breads and is an easy way to sample authentic Indian food and explore new flavours without ordering multiple separate dishes. Tamatanga managing director, Aman Kular, said: “A thali is all about variety, generosity and sharing the joy of Indian food, so it’s the perfect way for guests to discover new flavours alongside the dishes they already love.

“Our kitchen team puts a huge amount of care into every plate, creating fresh, flavour-packed food from homely Indian recipes. It's fantastic value and a brilliant excuse to gather friends, arrive hungry and enjoy a proper Indian feast.”

The restaurant is serving the thali feast deal Sunday to Friday - from 2pm until 5pm for £19.95 per person. The bottomless experience will run for an hour-and-a-half.

On Google, lots of satisfied customers have left reviews following visits to Tamatanga in Leeds city centre. Michelle Wiscombe described the restaurant as ‘fabulous’.

While Maksuda Hussain added: “A great spot for Indian food.” Paul Stark wrote: “Fantastic. I loved the food, the drinks and the service was impeccable.”

And Dalal Fahad commented: “Best food in Leeds. Best service. Best staff. We love the food here.”

We were kindly invited to sample the very appealing menu in the stylish setting. Vinny and the Tamatanga team looked after us wonderfully.
