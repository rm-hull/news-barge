# Who is taking part in France's recent street protests?

The streets of France have been rocked by protests and violence in recent weeks.

Student demonstrators demanding better conditions have been joined by yellow vest activists and others, angry about the rising cost of living.

Images of young, masked protesters dressed in black, clashing with police and smashing up shop windows, have made headlines around the world.

President Macron has blamed the unrest on the radical left. But who are the protesters? What drives them to use violence? And what are they really fighting for?

Our Europe Editor Katya Adler investigates.
