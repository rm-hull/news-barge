# West Pier Scarborough: Councillors asked to approve extra £4m for revised plans

## Councillors are being asked to approve an extra £4m towards revised plans to regenerate Scarborough’s West Pier.

Commercial fishermen in Scarborough use West Pier and the adjacent 180-metre fish quay as a working base to land catches, access supplies, and sell seafood. It also houses toilets and public car parking.

A report to North Yorkshire Council's Executive says the revised scheme, which will cost £18.25m “focuses on bringing dilapidated buildings into good condition, enhancing existing office space, providing better facilities for the fishing industry and sea food related businesses and improving the public realm and kiosks at the entrance to the Pier.”

The latest scheme doesn’t yet have planning permission.

The report warns of “key risks” including the time-sensitive nature of the Local Regeneration Fund, formerly Towns Fund, grant, getting planning permission and “potential delays caused by a Judicial Review of planning approval”. The extra £4.2m will be made up of £1.8m from the Scarborough Harbour Reserve with the rest coming from the Capital Contingencies Reserve.

A previous scheme was deferred by councillors last January after concerns that the project was “all about tourism” and wouldn’t help the harbour benefit from opportunities from the development of massive offshore wind farms. These included a boat lift and enhanced energy supply.

The report says that the York & North Yorkshire Combined Authority had awarded £700,000 for development work around the boat hoist. It added: “We are seeking approval to redirect these funds to the West Pier Regeneration Scheme and initial conversations with the Combined Authority have proven positive.”

Deputy leader Mark Crane said proposals had been re-scoped and re-costed “following extensive engagement as we look to protect the unique heritage while providing modern facilities that meet future needs”.
