Casa del Alma will open its doors in Harrogate next week.

The Mexican restaurant and tequila bar will welcome its first customers to 10 Princes Square on Friday 4th December.

The team behind Casa del Alma already runs a restaurant in Darlington and a street food unit in Saltburn.

Chef Matei Baran, who reached the quarter-finals of MasterChef: The Professionals in 2016, is behind the business.

A spokesperson for the restaurant said:

> "We’re so excited to officially announce our opening date!
>
> "Get ready for delicious Mexican food, incredible cocktails, tequila and a vibrant atmosphere as we bring a taste of Mexico to Harrogate!"

The Harrogate restaurant has also shared its ‘Taste of Mexico’ tasting menu.

Diners will start with a welcome tequila shot, nachos and guacamole, followed by three-cheese croquettes served with red onion and jalapeño jam.

Other dishes include sea bream ceviche and chipotle-glazed pork belly, with mojito cheesecake for dessert.
