# Cop31 collapses: why did climate talks fall apart? - The Latest

Countries at the Cop31 climate summit failed to reach a keystone agreement for the first time in 17 years. The talks, co-chaired by Turkey and Australia, were intended to produce a pact that would have injected urgency into climate action and financing for vulnerable states. Instead, the deal failed after a standoff over fossil fuels and support for countries exposed to the climate crisis.
