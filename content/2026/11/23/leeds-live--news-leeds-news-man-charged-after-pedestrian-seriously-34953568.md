# Man charged after pedestrian seriously injured in Leeds hit-and-run

## A pedestrian remains in hospital with likely life-changing injuries after being struck by a car on Easy Road earlier this month

A 40-year-old man has been charged after a pedestrian was seriously injured in a collision involving a car which failed to stop for police in Leeds.

John Collett, of Station Road, Cross Gates, has been charged following the incident on Easy Road, in East End Park, on November 11.

The collision happened at around 12.35pm after a silver BMW X1 failed to stop for West Yorkshire Police. The vehicle struck the pedestrian before the driver left the scene.

The pedestrian, a man in his 40s, remains in hospital and is said to have life changing injuries.

The BMW was later recovered by police from an underground car park on Goodman Street, in Hunslet.

Collett has been charged with causing serious injury by dangerous driving, failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required to do so by a constable, driving while disqualified by order of a court, using a motor vehicle without third party insurance and fraudulent use of a registration mark.

He has been remanded in custody to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court today (Monday, November 23).

Two further arrests have also been made as part of the investigation. A 47 year old man was arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender and drug supply offences before being released on bail.

A 29 year old woman has also been arrested on suspicion of committing an act or series of acts with intent to pervert the course of public justice. She remains in custody.

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said: “A man has been charged after a pedestrian was seriously injured in a collision in Leeds earlier this month.

“John Collett, aged 40, of Station Road, Cross Gates, Leeds, has been charged with causing serious injury by dangerous driving, failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required to do so by a constable, driving whilst disqualified by order of a court, using a motor vehicle without third-party insurance and fraudulent use of a registration mark.

“He has been remanded to appear at Leeds Magistrates’ Court today (Monday 23 November). The charges relate a collision on Easy Road, Leeds, on 11 November. The injured pedestrian, a man in his 40s, remains in hospital and is likely to have life-changing injuries.

“Two other arrests have been made as part of this investigation. A 47-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of assisting an offender and drug supply offences and released on bail. "A 29-year-old woman has been arrested on suspicion of committing an act or series of acts with intent to pervert the course of public justice and is currently in custody.”
