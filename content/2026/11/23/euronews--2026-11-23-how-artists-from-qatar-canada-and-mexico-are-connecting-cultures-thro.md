## Artists from Qatar, Mexico and Canada explore shared traditions, personal histories and new creative connections through the 2026 Year of Culture.

What happens when artists from different corners of the world come together to share ideas, traditions and creative techniques?

As part of the Qatar–Canada–Mexico 2026 Year of Culture, artists and designers are crossing borders to explore unexpected connections between their cultures.

Mexican designer Fernando Laposse is bringing traditional materials and agricultural knowledge to Doha. His exhibition, *The Sower*, combines Mexican natural dyes with Qatar's pearls, while exploring how ancient farming practices could help communities adapt to a changing climate.

Qatar-based designer Yasamin Shaikhi travelled to Mexico, where she collaborated with Mexican artisans to blend traditional craftsmanship with experimental design. What began as an exchange between two seemingly different cultures revealed surprising similarities in their creative approaches.

Meanwhile, Canadian photographer Joanna Johnston is exhibiting deeply personal images of Alberta's prairies at MIHAD, a new contemporary art and design gallery in Doha. Captured during a journey with her late father, the photographs offer a window into family, memory and belonging.

Through their work, these artists are discovering that creativity can bridge cultural and geographical divides. And while the exhibitions may be temporary, the relationships, knowledge and inspiration gained through these exchanges could endure long after the Year of Culture ends.
