## Much-loved North Yorkshire pub set to reopen tomorrow after 'transformation'

The Tapp on the Tutt in Boroughbridge re-opens tomorrow (TUE). *(Image: Darren Greenwood)*

**A MUCH-LOVED North Yorkshire town pub re-opens tomorrow (Tue) after a “full transformation.”**

The Tap on the Tutt in Boroughbridge will be offering food as well as an extensive range of drinks.

The refurbishment, which followed a five week closure from Sunday October 18, follows the pub being bought by Punch Pubs and Co in May.

In recent days, the Tap on the Tutt has been posting its progress with the refurbishment.

It said on Facebook staff have been trained and have tried a taster of the food menu that will be served. New staff have been taken on for the relaunch.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21752213.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the food available at the Tap on the Tutt. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The post continued: “We’ve got some really good proper pub grub for you and we can’t wait for you to dig in for a great deal.”

Events are also promised with the pub staging a Fabric Lantern workshop on Thursday December 10 from 7pm. It will also host a late-night Christmas Market on Wednesday December 2 from 4pm to 8pm.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21752217.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Some of the food available at the Tap on the Tutt. *(Image: Pic supplied)*

The Tap on the Tutt was previously owned by Simon Wade who has other pubs in the area.

They are the Fox and Hounds at Langthorpe, the Blue Bell Inn at Arkendale, the Grantham Arms at Milby and the Punch Bowl Inn at Marton cum Grafton.

Prior to Simon taking over the Tap on the Tutt in 2023 and giving it a lavish refurbishment, the Grade II-listed pub was known as The Three Horseshoes.

In recent weeks, the Tap on the Tutt announced it has appeared in the 2027 Good Beer Guide from CAMRA, which followed it being named by Harrogate CAMRA as the 2026 Country Pub of the Year.

The Press has approached Punch for more photos and details, which were promised by the pubco when the ‘transformation’ was announced.
