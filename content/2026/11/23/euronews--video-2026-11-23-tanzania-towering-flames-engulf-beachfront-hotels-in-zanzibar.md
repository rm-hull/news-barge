Thick black smoke rolled over the white sands of **Nungwi Beach** on**Zanzibar’s** northern tip after an afternoon fire at**Makofi Hotel**, whipped along by strong winds and thatched roofs, swept into adjacent resorts, restaurants and shops. Eyewitness mobile footage recorded** buildings roaring with flames as tourists and locals scrambled down the beach,**some hauling suitcases, others pitching in to help, until firefighters drawn from** three stations**subdued the blaze after** approximately three hours**. Although** several hotel staff**sustained injuries, officials confirmed that every one of the** 183 tourists**escaped unharmed, with** more than 20 hotels**in** Mchangani**and** Matemwe** stepping in to house displaced guests free of charge.

Across the **six affected hotels**(** Makofi**,** Z Hotel**,** Nungwi Garden**,** BR**,** Pasha**and** Sand Beach**),** four restaurants**and** about 40 shops**, the scale of the damage poses an acute challenge for local businesses and workers in the semi-autonomous** Tanzanian**archipelago, where tourism is an economic mainstay. On** Monday**, investigators were still working to confirm whether the** fire started in a bread-baking oven**, as early accounts suggested, while** Tourism and Antiquities Minister Nasra Nassor Omar** urged patience ahead of an official inquiry and promised a rapid assessment to guide the government’s response.
