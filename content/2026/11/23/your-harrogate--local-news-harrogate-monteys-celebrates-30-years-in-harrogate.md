Harrogate rock bar Montey's will mark its 30th birthday with a weekend of live music.

Josh Ozturk will open the celebrations on Friday 27th November, followed by Desperado and Captain & The Bear on Saturday.

A surprise band will play at an invitation-only birthday party on Sunday 29th November.

Cocktails cost £2.75 when Jay opened Montey’s in November 1996. His first employee, Col, still works alongside him today.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b03fe6f2d6aa2b5f3e.png)

Known for its live bands and Lemon Drops, the independent bar now employs people whose parents drank there in the 1990s.

Two former members of staff met at Montey’s and later married.

Jay said:

> “When I opened Montey’s in 1996, I don’t think anyone imagined we’d still be doing this thirty years later.
>
> "We’ve been incredibly lucky to have brilliant staff, talented musicians and generations of customers who’ve made Montey’s their own.
>
> "This birthday is really a celebration of everyone who’s ever been part of it.
>
> “And in true Montey’s tradition, we’re holding the big party on a Sunday. We’ve always liked to see just how many people are prepared to take Monday off work!”

The bar has also created a ‘Pick Your Decade’ birthday menu, bringing back cocktails including the Yellow Bird from the 1990s, the Pink Fluffy Bunny from the 2000s and the Smokey Old Fashioned from the 2010s.
