## York market which hopes to 'make everyday shopping feel a little more special' opens

Laura with an employee inside the new MAE Market *(Image: Provided)*

A MUCH-LOVED York coffee shop has opened the doors on its newest venture, which hopes to offer an independent take on a supermarket giant.

MAE Market, a sister business to MAE Café, opened its doors on Saturday (November 21) to a new deli and supermarket that hopes to 'make everyday shopping feel a little more special'.

The new shop – which is located on Holgate Road – is just down the street from the mother-and-daughter-owned café which was opened just over a year ago by Laura Mae Bowes, then 25, and her mum Joanne Bowes.

The name, which was that of Joanne’s mother, was chosen as a tribute and brings together three generations of the same family – with Joanne previously adding that it was 'really special' to work with her daughter.

On social media, the duo called the market a 'considered new chapter from MAE - created to bring together everything you need for the everyday, all in one place'.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21749673.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The coffee counter *(Image: Provided)*

They added: “From fresh produce, quality meats, household essentials, cheeses to breads, MAE Meals, juices, coffee and carefully chosen pantry staples, MARKET is designed to make everyday shopping feel a little more special.

“Thoughtful, seasonal and beautifully sourced; whether you’re picking up dinner, stocking the fridge or simply calling in for something good.”

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21749669.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Freshly baked bread from local bakers *(Image: Provided)*

Speaking to The Press previously, Laura also likened the market to an independent version of M&S - adding "We have had so much success with the café that we decided to build on our brand.".

Inside, a range of different produce is offered from independent and local suppliers including fresh fruits and vegetables, locally sourced pasta, bread from Cosgriff and Sons bakers in Tower Street, meat, and premade meals.

The cornerstone of MAE however comes in its freshly-prepared meals, which are made by an in-house chef and hope to provide a 'convenient, healthy alternative' to ready meals.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21749668.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The fridge at MAE Market *(Image: Provided)*

There are also non-perishable items and a coffee counter for people opting for a takeaway instead of visiting the nearby café.

Further information can be found at meetatmae on Instagram.
