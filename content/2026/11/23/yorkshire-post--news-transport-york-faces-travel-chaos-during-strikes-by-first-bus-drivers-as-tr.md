# York faces travel chaos during strikes by First Bus drivers as travellers warned that bus services including park and ride will be severely disrupted during Christmas period

## York is facing travel chaos during strikes by First Bus drivers over pay, Unite said and travellers are warned that services will be severely disrupted.

The transport union, Unite, has announced that there will be strikes by First Bus drivers over pay and this has led to travel chaos.

The 200 workers, who are on £15.58, are demanding pay parity with the city’s Yorkshire Coastliner drivers, who are paid £16.90 per hour.

Last week, First Group reported a seven per cent increase in its operating profits, taking them to £110 million in the six months to September 2026.

![First Bus.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/08/18/9/33/Five-drivers-with-First-Bus-in-South-Yorkshire-reach-finals-of-Bus-Driver-of-the-Year.jpeg?trim=0,75,0,75&width=640&quality=65)


Unite general secretary, Sharon Graham, said: “First’s profits continue to rise yet its hard-working York drivers are expected to put up with second class wages.

“First can afford to put forward a reasonable offer but is choosing not to out of greed.”

The workers will take initial strike action on Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29, 2026, with industrial action set to escalate severely during the Christmas period if the dispute is not resolved. Bus travel, including vital park and ride services, will be severely impacted.

Unite regional officer, Darren Rushworth, said: “First will be entirely responsible for the disruption caused to York residents, visitors and businesses during the Christmas period.

“There is still time to avoid industrial action but that will require First tabling a deal our members can accept.”
