Ukraine has released footage showing what it says are strikes targeting two Russian vessels off the Libyan port of Benghazi.

The images, shared by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky on 22 November 2026, appear to show explosions near the vessels off Libya's eastern coast. "It all took place off the coast of Libya. Our warriors deployed seven Ukrainian-made naval drones. Despite all the enemy's efforts to counter the attack, the Russian corvette Merkury was sunk," Zelensky wrote in a post on X.

The alleged strikes come as Russia's war against Ukraine continues into its fifth year, with the conflict increasingly extending beyond Ukrainian territory.
