Richard Hammond has revealed he sold the most expensive car he ever owned after getting divorced.

The former Top Gear presenter, 56, said he sold his Porsche 911 Turbo S Cabriolet (the latest model costing upwards of £209,500) following his divorce from Mindy Hammond last year.

Asked what the most expensive thing he had ever bought besides property was, Hammond told the Guardian: “You won’t be surprised to hear it’s a car.

“It was the Porsche 911 Turbo S Cabriolet, which cost an eye-watering and embarrassingly large amount of money. Have I still got it? No – I got divorced last year.”

Hammond also told the newspaper the brain injury he endured after a crash at RAF Elvington while filming Top Gear was one of the closest times he had come to death.

The presenter crashed a jet-powered dragster called Vampire at nearly 320mph while filming for Top Gear at the former RAF Elvington airbase in 2006.

Although the incident left him with serious head injuries, he recovered and returned to the show in early 2007.

He told the High Performance podcast he fears the brain injury could lead to early onset dementia but he is “too scared” to get tested for the condition.

Hammond, who was brought up in North Yorkshire and left Ripon Grammar School in the middle of his A levels before studying visual arts at Harrogate, now presents Richard Hammond’s Workshop on Discovery Plus.

His first broadcasts emanated on BBC Radio York. “It took me about a week just to put one piece together,” Hammond once told an interviewer. “And I only got paid about £10, so I worked part-time in a bookshop and a petrol station and collected chicken eggs on a farm.”
