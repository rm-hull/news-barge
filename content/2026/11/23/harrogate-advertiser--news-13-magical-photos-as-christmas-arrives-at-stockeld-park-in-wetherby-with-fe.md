The attraction has been transformed for the festive season, with visitors exploring a range of Christmas activities designed to bring some seasonal magic

Guests can enjoy a journey through the Enchanted Forest, where woodland paths are brought to life with colourful lights and displays, as well as meeting Santa in his grotto and taking part in a host of festive activities

The park’s Christmas experience also includes ice skating, the Snowflake Maze and festive entertainment, with plenty of opportunities for visitors to make memories during the run-up to Christmas
