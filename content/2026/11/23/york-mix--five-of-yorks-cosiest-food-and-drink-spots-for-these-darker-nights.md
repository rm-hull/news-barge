York is well-known for its atmospheric venues and beautiful streets.

And many of them are perfect to relax on these darker nights.

Here are my recommendations for food and drink spots in the city centre that are cosy havens at this time of year.

## Omoni House, Shambles

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-cosy-places-omoni-house-shambles.webp)

Located on York’s most famous street, Omoni House serves a traditional Korean menu and offers an authentic dining experience. The portions are large, full of flavour and great value for money. You can tuck in to a bowl of warming Korean stew or a hearty bowl of sweet and spicy ramen in a historical venue to escape the cold weather.

## Black Wheat Club, Fossgate

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-cosy-places-ysj-black-wheat-club.webp)

From daytime bakes to laid back bistro-plates this independent venue offers something for everyone. The Black Wheat Club menu is Euro-comfort food inspired and has been carefully crafted using local ingredients. Everything from sourdough to sausages and preserves are made from scratch in-house.

## Le Speciality, Fossgate

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-cosy-places-ysj-le-specialty.webp)

Le Speciality has become a local hotspot for speciality coffee, artisan tea and all-day brunch since its opening in 2024. This independent coffee shop opens seven days a week, closing at 4 every afternoon. They have created a warm, peaceful space perfect for a slow weekend brunch or a mid-week lunch time take away. They also have plenty of vegan items on the menu and are dog friendly.

## Sophie, Lendal Bridge and Skeldergate Bridge

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-cosy-places-ysj-sophie-cafe-lendal-bridge-2.webp)

Across two quirky, city centre locations this female owned coffee shop and cocktail bar really does have it all: bespoke coffee, seasonal cocktails, cosy interiors and terrace seating with views of the River Ouse. Sophie is a great place to stop for autumnal lunches that are made fresh. Their autumn/winter food and drinks menu which features the ‘fancy ham and cheddar toastie’ made with fresh sourdough and serrano ham.

## The Habit, Goodramgate

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/york-cosy-places-ysj-the-habit.webp)

Last but not least is The Habit. This is an atmospheric bar located right around the corner from York Minster – so close you can see it from the roof terrace! This bar is small but has so much to offer, notably live music performances four nights a week. Serving craft beer, cocktails and a stand out whiskey selection makes The Habit an ideal spot to enjoy a casual drink with others or to take yourself on a solo date with one of the best views in the city.
