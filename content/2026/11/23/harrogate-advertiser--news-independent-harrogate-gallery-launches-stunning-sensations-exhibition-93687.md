An independent, family-run art gallery in Harrogate which has been impressing visitors since 1997 is presenting a double exhibition in the run-up to Christmas.

Running until December 19, 108 Fine Art at 16 Cold Bath Road has launched the Sensations exhibition.

Although this stunning show sees both artists exploring the relationship between sound, movement, light and stillness, it also presents two distinct collections of paintings, each with its own interpretation of the world around us.

The work by Northern England-based abstract painter Rachel Sedman may contrast with that of Scottish landscape painter Calum McClure but the end result is a symphony of colour between the two artists in stunning harmony.

108 Fine Art has a reputation of not only being the home of the Joash Woodrow Archives but also for developing long-running partnerships with artists working in all areas of the visual arts, in particular, talented female artists.

This family-run Harrogate gallery is also is pleased to announce the return of the Andrew Stewart Award, established in 2023 in memory of Andrew Stewart, co-founder of the gallery.

The award celebrates artistic talent and provides an opportunity for selected artists to exhibit their work at 108 Fine Art in Harrogate in March 2027.

Applications are now open to artists at all stages of their careers, with submissions closing in early January 2027.
