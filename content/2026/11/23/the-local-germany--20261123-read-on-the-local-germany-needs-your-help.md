"The Local has given me significantly more value over the years than the membership fee," Amanda told us. Kelly emailed to say: "I appreciate all you do. I truly hope the publication continues; it's so valuable."

These were just two of the many, many supportive messages we received from readers in response to our autumn call for donations.

So far more than 500 members have generously donated to The Local. I would like to sincerely thank every one of you who contributed, as well as all of you who have become members.

Global tech giants and AI companies don't prioritise the interests of our community of international readers, but they have made it harder and harder for us to do just that.

Without paying members as well as kind donations, The Local could not provide the service so valued by international residents and visitors.

We wouldn't be able to fight your corner and raise issues with German authorities on your behalf. We wouldn't be able to bring you insightful information and expert advice on life in Germany or provide you with all the latest news you need to know.

If you still want to donate to The Local, just click on the link below, or if you're not a member, maybe now is the time to join? **You can take advantage of our half-price introductory offer.**

Thank you from the whole team.

If you want to help us in other ways, you can always recommend us to others, share our articles with friends and family, review our app, or post our news on social media.

Every little act of support helps us to continue our mission.
