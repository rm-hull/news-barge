School officials in California waited more than six minutes to provide a 14-year-old student with CPR after he collapsed in gym class, a lawsuit claims.

​Daniel Christopher Padilla Jr., 14, collapsed while playing basketball on March 9 at Fresno High School. He was pronounced dead later that same day, the boy’s mother states in a wrongful death lawsuit filed on Thursday and reviewed by *The Los Angeles Times*.

​Kenia Lizette Ruiz Suarez, Padilla’s mom, claims that 911 was not called and that, when emergency workers arrived, they were sent to an unattended gate that was locked.

Hailey Valdovinos, Padilla’s sister, described her younger brother as a “silly kid” in a heartbreaking tribute accompanying a GoFundMe campaign.

​“Danny was a 14-year-old silly kid who loved playing video games, cracking jokes and enjoyed spending time with his family whenever he got the chance,” Hailey wrote. “He enjoyed playing basketball, going to the movies and hanging out with his siblings.”

![Daniel Padilla died after collapsing in gym class. His mother has claimed in a lawsuit that medical care was not provided to him fast enough](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/16/29/Screenshot-2026-11-23-at-16-18-18.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

​The lawsuit describes the moments leading up to and during Padilla’s collapse, detailing how he stumbled backward and fell to the ground at 12:18 p.m., *The Times* reports. Moments later, blood was pouring from the boy’s nose, and he had urinated on himself.

Nchaeeia Ramos, the gym teacher, did not call 911 or immediately perform CPR, the lawsuit claims. Instead, district health personnel arrived three minutes after the collapse with a defibrillator shock being delivered after nine minutes.

An EMS crew arrived at 12:30 p.m., with the boy being pronounced dead in the hospital at 1:24 p.m. - less than an hour later.

In a statement given to *PEOPLE*, a Fresno Unified School District spokesperson addressed the March 9 incident.

“We can confirm that the student who collapsed during P.E. has tragically passed away at the hospital,” the spokesperson said. “Our hearts are with the student’s family, friends, and school community during this incredibly difficult time.

​“At this time, we do not have any additional information to share.”

![The boy collapsed at Fresno High School on March 9 and was pronounced dead in the hospital](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/16/33/Screenshot-2026-11-23-at-16-29-45.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=87%2C0%2C152%2C1&auto=webp)

*​The Independent* has contacted the Fresno Unified School District for further comment.

​“A minor collapsing and going into cardiac arrest is totally foreseeable, and in fact Fresno Unified had recently distributed materials to students and families called ‘Heart in the Game,’” Benjamin Drake, the founder of Drake Law Group, told *The Times*. “The district told families that in these situations the plan was: Call 911, begin CPR, use a defibrillator.

​“It should have been obvious that Danny’s life depended on fast action.”

The lawsuit seeks damages for Padilla’s mom and names the school district, Ramos, school nurse Tracy Phillips, Principal Amy Smith, Vice Principal Rafael Torres, and health worker Ka Vang. It alleges wrongful death, negligent supervision, and negligent hiring, training and retention, *The Times* reports.
