A North Yorkshire spa retreat has been named Wellness Experience of the Year at the 2026 Hoseasons Diamond Awards.

Yorkshire Spa Retreat in Nawton is the first to win the new national award. It also retained its Lodge Escape Small Park title for the North of England.

The awards recognise holiday parks, lodge resorts and boating locations across the UK, based on feedback from guests who stayed during the 2026 season.

A spokesman for Yorkshire Spa Retreat said:

> “We are very proud of this award. Our team always goes above and beyond to make sure our guests have a fantastic break when they stay with us, so it’s brilliant to see that hard work recognised in this way.”

The retreat is set in more than 11 acres of parkland in Nawton, with lodges and holiday homes. Some lodges have their own outdoor kitchens and hot tubs.

Comedian Tom Allen and Hoseasons Group Chief Commercial Officer Simon Altham presented the awards at the Celtic Manor Resort in Newport, South Wales.

Ryan Day, account director at Hoseasons, said:

> “The team at Yorkshire Spa Retreat are richly deserving winners.
>
> “These awards recognise the very best in UK holiday accommodation, so it's a fantastic achievement and one they should be very proud of.
>
> “They always go the extra mile to delight our guests, something which is clearly reflected in their excellent feedback scores.
>
> “We are grateful for their continuing partnership and wish them all the very best for another great year in 2027.”
