A man who drugged and raped his wife for over 20 years advertised online “looking for some extra fun and games”, a court has been told.

The victim’s husband, who cannot be identified to protect his wife’s anonymity, pleaded guilty earlier this year to multiple offences dating back to 2004, including rape, sexual assault and administering a substance with intent to commit a sexual offence.

The man in his 60s took images and videos of his offending as she lay “out of it” on their bed, before sharing them online.

From 2023 onwards at the latest, the Crown says he began inviting other men to their home in Stockport, Greater Manchester, where it is said they too sexually abused her.

He is due to give evidence later this week against nine men on trial at Manchester Minshull Street Crown Court over their alleged roles in the offending.

Several of the defendants initially met the husband through Fabswingers.com, a website for those interested in swinging, before they moved contact to different messaging apps.

![Jordan Wallace, one of the alleged contact offenders, arrives at court](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/15/2298082728..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

On Monday, jurors were told details of the husband’s Fabswingers page, where he described himself as a “mature confident male”, and had a profile picture of his naked torso.

On his profile he wrote: “Male from Stockport looking for some extra fun and games. Want to find out what I have been missing to see if as much fun as they say it is.

“Looking to try new stuff. Also would love some sex buddies – females and couples. I want to try it all.

“Married guy. Wife is hot and cold these days so will rarely be joining me. Want to try new and varied things.

“My ideal would be to find a genuine friendly couple for me to join them from time to time for some good satisfying fun.

“Would love to find a mature lady who is looking for a friendly mature guy to have regular meets. Just for chats and friendship but with no permanent relationship.

“If more than friendship would be great but not essential, or only when ready.”

The court heard the husband also looked online for men on fabguys.com – a website associated with fabswingers.

On his fabguys profile page he wrote: “Not much experience with guys. Like showing off my wife pics to other men. Looking to meet mature guy who is willing to teach me the techniques of bi pleasure.

“I would be a willing learner. Would be a teacher/apprentice relationship, not a master/sub one. Looking for couples, men, TV, TS, aged 18-99.”

Earlier, one of the defendants changed some of his pleas.

Robert Stewart, 70, of Stockport, pleaded guilty to two counts of rape and one count of conspiracy to administer a substance with intent. He continues to deny a single count of conspiracy to rape.

![Alan Keelan, another alleged contact offender, outside Manchester Minshull Street Crown Court](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/03/10/01M6GF8E1E02GT69SPNDJR0WYV.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Alleged contact offenders said to have visited the couple’s home to commit sexual offences including rape, are: taxi driver Philip Wild, 59, of Stockport; recruitment agency worker Mohammed Sabir, 28, of Stockport; building supplies employee Jordan Wallace, 31, of Wythenshawe; industrial cleaning firm worker Alan Keelan, 42, from Wythenshawe; sheet metal worker Richard Townsend, 38, of Heywood, Greater Manchester; and senior paramedic Jonathan Kirk, 43, of Stockport.

The Crown says non-contact offenders, who “hid behind a screen” as they allegedly conspired to administer substances to the wife and for her to be raped, are self-employed mechanic Daniel Rayner, 42, of Whitstable, Kent, and kitchen firm sales rep David Graves, 60, from Ilkeston, Derbyshire.

The prosecution says the defendants knew the wife was not a willing participant, was not consenting, and they did not reasonably believe she was consenting. The alleged offences are said to have taken place over a 10-year period between 2015 and 2025.

Another man, Tommy Golden, 59, pleaded guilty at a separate hearing at Manchester Magistrates’ Court on Monday to sexually assaulting the wife in 2025.

He was remanded in custody.
