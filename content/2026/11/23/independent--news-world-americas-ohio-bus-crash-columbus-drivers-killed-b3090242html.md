Two bus drivers were killed and nine other people were injured when the two buses collided head-on during morning rush hour in Ohio's state capital on Monday.

One of the Central Ohio Transit Authority buses was hit by a third vehicle during the crash on the city's north side at around 8 a.m.

“Early indications are that one of the buses, for reasons unknown, crossed a double yellow line and struck the other bus head-on,” Columbus Police Sgt. Joseph Albert said.

One of the bus drivers died at the crash site and the second was rushed to the hospital in critical condition but died of their injuries, according to Albert.

![First responders at the scene of the crash](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/21/50/AP26327673871161.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

He said nine other people, either bus riders or passengers in the third vehicle, were taken to area hospitals.

They were all in stable condition with injuries that were not considered life-threatening. No pedestrians were injured.

Columbus City Council President Shannon Hardin and council member Lourdes Barroso de Padilla, who chairs the public service and transportation committee, called the accident “heartbreaking.”

“Our hearts go out to all those impacted by Monday’s accident, especially to the loved ones of those killed,” they said in a statement.

“While we try to understand how this tragedy happened, we must not forget the lives lost and the families that will be mourning them just as we enter the holiday season.”
