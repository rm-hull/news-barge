## Filey holiday park could get £1.6m makeover under new plans

Plans have been drawn up to expand and upgrade Filey Brigg Caravan and Camping Park *(Image: North Yorkshire Council)*

Up to £1.6 million could be invested in major improvements at a popular holiday park under new proposals.

Plans to expand and upgrade Filey Brigg Caravan and Camping Park in North Yorkshire aim to boost tourism, support local businesses, and help to fund essential services across North Yorkshire.

The site currently offers 145 pitches for caravans, motorhomes, and campers, as well as 14 grass tent pitches.

Peter Wilkinson, executive member for finance and property at North Yorkshire Council, said: "Filey Brigg is a popular holiday destination for families who come to enjoy the spectacular scenery and everything the Yorkshire coast has to offer.

"The proposals being brought forward would explore opportunities to invest in and modernise the holiday park, helping to safeguard its long-term future as a valuable council-owned asset.

"If approved, the investment could enhance the facilities, broaden the accommodation on offer and improve the visitor experience – which in turn would help the local economy and support local businesses."

Plans include six new glamping lodges, upgraded changing rooms, improved electrical infrastructure, better Wi-Fi, and additional hardstanding pitches.

A business case for the scheme is expected to be presented to the council’s executive in 2027, with the proposals currently at an early stage.
