## Budget supermarket beating Cathedral City, Waitrose and M&S with £2.99 Christmas buy

Aldi's £2.99 extra mature cheddar has beaten Cathedral City, M&S and Waitrose in a Which? taste test, scoring 78% and earning Best Buy recognition. *(Image: Aldi)*

Aldi has been crowned the winner of a major Christmas cheese taste test, beating household names including Cathedral City, M&S and Waitrose with an extra mature cheddar costing just £2.99.

The supermarket's Specially Selected West Country Extra Mature Cheddar scored an impressive 78% in a blind taste test conducted by consumer champion Which?, earning its coveted Best Buy and Great Value awards.

Experts praised the cheese for its smooth, firm and creamy texture, with Aldi's offering coming out ahead of 10 other popular supermarket and branded cheddars.

It even managed to beat Cathedral City's Extra Mature Cheddar, which scored 74%, while M&S's Cornish Cruncher Extra Mature Cheddar received 70%.

At £2.99 for a 350g block, Aldi's award-winning cheese could be a cheaper way for shoppers to stock up ahead of Christmas and add a crowd-pleasing option to their festive cheeseboard.

The cheddar is available in Aldi stores nationwide now, ahead of the supermarket's wider Christmas cheese range arriving on November 25.

## Aldi beats Cathedral City and M&S in Which? cheese test

Which? blind-tested 11 extra mature cheddars, assessing them with a panel of experts and consumers.

Aldi came out on top with 78%, ahead of several well-known brands and supermarket own-label alternatives.

The full rankings were:

* Aldi Specially Selected West Country Extra Mature Cheddar – 78%
* Pilgrims Choice Extra Mature Cheddar – 76%
* Waitrose Davidstow Cornish Extra Mature Cheddar – 75%
* Cathedral City Our Extra Mature Cheddar – 74%
* Morrisons The Best Extra Mature Farmhouse Cheddar – 74%
* Sainsbury's Taste The Difference Extra Mature Cumbrian Cheddar – 74%
* Lidl Deluxe Somerset Crunchy Extra Mature Cheddar – 73%
* Asda Exceptional Wyke Farms Somerset Extra Mature Cheddar – 72%
* M&S Cornish Cruncher Extra Mature Cheddar – 70%
* Castello Tickler Extra Mature Cheddar – 69%
* Tesco Finest Cave Aged Cheddar – 66%

Julie Ashfield, Chief Commercial Officer at Aldi UK, said: “We’re delighted to see our Specially Selected West Country Extra Mature Cheddar recognised by Which? as a Best Buy.

“We know how important quality and value are, particularly at Christmas, and this award is a fantastic result for our team.

“At just £2.99, shoppers can enjoy an award-winning cheddar that delivers on both taste and value, making it a great addition to any festive cheeseboard.”

## Are some supermarket cheeses made in the same factory as branded ones?

According to the Coupon Kid, Jordon Cox: "Aldi & Catherdral City cheese are made in the SAME factory - here’s how you can tell.

"On the back of all dairy & meat items in the UK, you’ll find an oval code - that tells you the factory that it was made from. All products are required to have this. The Cathedral City factory is an award winning creamery in Davidstow - with the code NU007.

"And guess what code Aldi’s own brand cheese also has on the back..? Yep. NU007."

But, he adds: "Now - before you all start coming at me in the comments, I’m FULLY aware that this doesn’t mean it’s the same cheese. And they may use different ingredients.

"But, if the quality is so high at that factory (as Cathedral City themselves say), then the Aldi cheese should be a good alternative, and save you £1 every time you buy some cheese!"
