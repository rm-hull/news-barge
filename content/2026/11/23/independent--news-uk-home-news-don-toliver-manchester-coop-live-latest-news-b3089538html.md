Manchester’s Co-op Live arena has been evacuated tonight after tear gas was reportedly let off during US rapper Don Toliver’s show.

The incident is reported to have taken place while the 32-year-old rapper, whose real name is Caleb Zackery Toliver, was performing on stage and is understood to have occurred towards the end of the gig.

Emergency services including ambulance and police officers are currently at the scene following the incident with those affected being supported by authorities.

A major incident has been declared as thousands have been evacuated. No serious injuries have been reported so far.

In a statement on Sunday night (22 November), a Greater Manchester Police spokesperson said: “Emergency services are currently in attendance following an incident at Co-op Live this evening.

![Rapper Don Toliver was reportedly performing when the incident occurred](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/23/2283789920..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“It is understood that a substance believed to be tear gas was let off in the crowd towards the end of a concert. All attendees are currently being safely evacuated as a precaution.

“Those showing signs of being impacted are being supported by emergency services, with thankfully no serious injuries reported.”

Police advised members of the public to “please stay away from the arena whilst emergency services help those that have been at the concert safely make their way to their transport home.”

The spokesperson added: “A major incident has been declared to ensure emergency services and partner agencies can work together quickly and effectively to coordinate a safe response.”

Footage circulating on social media appears to show crowds of concert-goers appearing to look distressed while coughing.

![The Co-op Live arena can hold up to 23,500 people](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/23/2263545946..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Other images show groups of fans in the crowded arena while the issue was being investigated.

An organiser is reported to have addressed the crowd and explained that the gig would have to be cut short to which many fans booed before it was confirmed what had taken place.

The arena is a relatively new venue, having opened in 2024 has the capacity to hold 23,500 people.

Fans told the *Manchester Evening News* that they saw what appeared to be smoke canisters thrown into the crowd.

The rapper is one of the world’s biggest musicians with over 45 million listeners per month, according to Spotify and ranks as the 86th most popular artist in the world. His top song on the streaming service, “No Pole”, has over 1 billion plays.

He is set to continue his tour with a performance at both the O2 Arena and OVO Arena in Wembley in London.
