# Huge Leeds band to headline outdoor summer 2027 homecoming show with support coming from major acts

## Fans will absolutely love this big Leeds city centre event

A huge Leeds band will play an outdoor summer 2027 city centre show.

It will be a packed line up for the Millennium Square party. Leeds indie heroes The Pigeon Detectives will headline on Saturday, July 10.

The festivities will celebrate 20 years of their critically-acclaimed debut album Wait For Me. Tickets will go on general sale at 9am on Friday, November 27 - you can buy tickets here.

Formed in Rothwell, just 15 minutes from Millenium Square, The Pigeon Detectives burst onto the UK indie rock scene in 2006 with their very first performance at Leeds Festival earning the band an army of fans. It is going to be an event to remember with the band tops the bill.

Support will be coming from some very big names. The line up being completed by Reverend and the Makers, The Twang, The View and The Holloways.
