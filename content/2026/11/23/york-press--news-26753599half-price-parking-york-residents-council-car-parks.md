York residents will be able to park in car parks for half the standard price as part of council measures to make accessing areas of the city more affordable.

The announcement follows York council facing backlash from residents and traders across the city after hikes to parking charges.

More than 8,000 people signed a petition calling on the council to rethink parking charges at the Bishopthorpe Road car park, which initially saw parking fees rise by more than 500 per cent.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21751960.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A poster against the initially 500 per cent hike in fees at the Bishopthorpe Road car park last year *(Image: Dylan Connell)*

Changes to the Minster Badge scheme, providing parking discounts to York residents at council-run car parks and in on street parking areas, mean the parking pass will now cost £15 annually.

Under the new measures, Minster Badge holders will have a 50 per cent discount when parking in areas in Bishopthorpe Road, East Parade, Micklegate, The Groves and Rowntree Park. The previous discount was 30 per cent.

It means parking in Bishopthorpe Road car park for Minster Badge holders is now £1.50 for one hour, £3 for one to two hours and £4.50 for two to three hours.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21751962.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Bishopthorpe Road car park *(Image: Dylan Connell)*

The pass currently costs £45 for two years and the council said the new £15-a-year fee is for people buying a new Minster Badge or those renewing existing passes.

Those who already have a Minster Badge do not have to change their pass and will automatically get access to the new discounts when they next park, the council said.

The council’s executive member for transport, Cllr Kate Ravilious, said the changes to the Minster Badge scheme would help people “save money while making it easier to access local shops, services and community facilities”.

“For residents who regularly use participating community parking areas, those savings can add up quickly,” she said. “These changes mean the Minster Badge is now more affordable and offers greater benefits than ever before.

“With a lower annual fee and a bigger parking discount, this is another way we're making it easier and more affordable for you to get around your city be it as a pedestrian, cyclist, bus passenger, and for drivers."

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/19384696.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cllr Kate Ravilious, City of York Council's executive member for transport *(Image: Dylan Connell)*

The Bishopthorpe Road Traders Association welcomed the changes to the Minster Badge scheme.

“After 18 months of campaigning the council for affordable parking in the Bishy Road shoppers car park we now have in place half price parking for York residents (with a £15 Minster Badge),” a group spokesperson said.

“Over 8,000 of you signed a petition against the huge prices (increase of 500 per cent) we saw in 2025 and we thank you for all of your support to get us to this point.

“We hope to see you all shopping on Bishy Road soon.”
