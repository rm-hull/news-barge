A British actor hailed as a “national treasure” has won the hearts of fans on a rather special visit to Ripon.

Star of Downton Abbey and Paddington, Hugh Bonneville visited Ripon on Saturday evening, bringing charm, charisma and fabulous storytelling to a special fundraising event in aid of Ripon Theatre Festival.

Hugh was interviewed on stage by Anna Wallace about his latest children's book, Rory Sparkes and the Spy Who Loved Tea.

The star of TV comedy shows Twenty Twelve and W1A also entertained the packed Holy Trinity Church in Ripon with tales of his acting career and life behind-the-scenes of stage and screen.

Many happy audience members met Hugh afterwards at the book signing.

Festival Director, Katie Scott said, “We were truly honoured and grateful that Hugh shared time with us this weekend, speaking of his love of theatre and showing his support for small arts organisations like us.

“Funds raised will help us continue to bring high-quality theatre to the area, to keep prices low and to develop our community outreach work.”
