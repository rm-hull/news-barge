Temperatures are set to drop below freezing in York as the city prepares for a cold snap.

York is set to see cold and frosty weather overnight this week with temperatures sitting between 4C and 6C before dropping to -2C on Friday morning (November 27).

Met Office forecasters have predicted overcast weather throughout the week turning to fog on Saturday.

The fog is due to lift by Sunday with sunny spells by Sunday afternoon.

It comes as parts of the UK brace for the “early arrival” of winter this week as snow, frost and sub-zero temperatures are forecast in areas for the coming days.

Met Office meteorologist Tom Morgan said: “We’ve got one more week of meteorological autumn to go but winter will be making somewhat of an early arrival for some of us through the week ahead.

“It’s all courtesy of a large area of high pressure sitting to the north-east of the UK across Scandinavia trying to bring in this much colder eastly flow.

“But out towards the west we’ve got much milder systems trying to push in from the Atlantic and it’s going to be a case of which of those two air masses win through the week ahead.

“Generally though, for most of us, it will be staying on the cold side and there’s even the potential for some snow, mainly over the high grounds in the north.”
