President Donald Trump has repeated his call for Nvidia to rehire Jensen Haung and attacked Minnesota’s Somali-American community in his latest Truth Social posting spree.

Huang, 63, stepped down from the board of the chipmaker he has led since 1993 earlier this month after it emerged he had engaged in a string of extramarital affairs, one of which was with Megaspeed boss Alice Huang (no relation), whom U.S. intelligence has warned is a Chinese spy.

“I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang,” the president wrote on his social media platform Sunday. “It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.”

Trump has consistently expressed sympathy for the fallen tech boss and lobbied for his reinstatement several times last week, telling Fox News during his trip to China that he is “a brilliant man” and that “we must take care of our geniuses.”

“We’re a country of second chances,” he reminded the White House press corps on his return Friday, again pushing for Huang’s professional resurrection.

![President Donald Trump has again urged Nvidia to reinstate its scandal-hit former CEO Jensen Huang](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/15/21/2026-11-14T002154Z_1599363360_RC27ZNA5MQUU_RTRMADP_3_NVIDIA-CEO-RELATIONSHIPS.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

Nvidia, whose hardware is currently playing a pivotal role in powering the global AI boom, has appointed Colette Kress, its executive vice president and chief financial officer, as interim CEO while a full-time replacement for Huang is sought, promoting a “business as usual” message.

The president also lashed at Somalis in the North Star State in a separate post, writing: “It’s incredible that Somalians have been able to completely take over the once great State of Minnesota. They own it, ‘lock, stock, and barrel’ and, almost 95 percent are on some form of welfare.”

The community was the focus of MAGA ire a year ago after conservative YouTuber Nick Shirley posted a 42-minute documentary attempting to expose an alleged welfare fraud scam in Minneapolis centered around day care centers, which he claimed had cost the state’s taxpayers “upwards of $100 million.”

Trump himself has also regularly clashed with the outspoken Minnesota Democratic Rep. Ilhan Omar, born in Mogadishu, whose D.C. home was raided by Homeland Security Investigations agents in October.

Elsewhere on the president’s feed, he posted a video of himself praying with members of the public in a diner, along with praise from the hardly-impartial Trump War Room account, which hailed him as, “A President who isn’t ashamed to pray.”

His predecessor in the Oval Office, Democrat Joe Biden, was a devout Catholic and other recent occupants like Barack Obama, George W Bush and Bill Clinton were also practising Christians, so who the line was directed at is unclear.

Trump also claimed that the Democrats’ recent midterms elections triumph would only embolden Iran, writing: “Iranian leaders consider the Democrats weak, ineffective, and Country destroying, but I won’t let that happen!

Ilhan Omar, with whom he has regularly sparred](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/22/09/2026-10-16T001826Z_1713319104_RC27FJABUSZ6_RTRMADP_3_USA-ELECTION-MINNESOTA-OMAR.JPG?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&auto=webp)

“Both the Dems and Iran fought like hell to keep the Oil price up as long as possible and, since the Election, the Oil price has dropped by a record level,” he continued.

“Too bad the Voters didn’t understand this before they pulled the trigger, but the whole Election was very ‘strange.’ In the end, America will win!”

There is no evidence that the president’s domestic political opposition conspired with Tehran to inflate oil prices in time for November 3 but that did not stop the president typing in a conspiratorial follow-up message: “Isn’t it interesting that Oil prices have dropped precipitously after the Midterm Elections?

“I said that was going to happen. The whole thing is a Giant Hoax!”

There was also a video of two men expertly miming to a spat between himself and Biden over their rival golf games from the 2024 presidential debate, a checklist of his “wins” since returning to office and a video alleging that Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer walks “hunched over,” implying he is too old to serve.

Schumer is 76 years old. Trump is 80.
