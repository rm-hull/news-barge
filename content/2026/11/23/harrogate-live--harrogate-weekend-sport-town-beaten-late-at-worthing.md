Harrogate Town lost 2-1 at Worthing to an 88th-minute goal but stay second. Harrogate RUFC lost at Sedgley Park and the Rail lost at South Leeds. Results and fixtures.

Harrogate Town lost 2-1 at Worthing on Saturday to a goal two minutes from time, but they are still second in the National League. Harrogate RUFC and Harrogate Railway Athletic were also beaten away from home, and there is no result for Knaresborough Town.

## Harrogate Town

Will McGowan put Town ahead after 20 minutes, his third goal in three games, the club’s match report says. Ciaran McGuckin set it up and later hit the post. Jordan Young equalised for Worthing in first-half stoppage time and scored again after 88 minutes. McGuckin had come into the side for Michael Craig, who was ill. The crowd was 2,602, with 118 from Harrogate.

Simon Weaver’s side have 35 points from 18 games. Boreham Wood won again to reach 48, so the gap at the top is now 13 points, and AFC Fylde are two behind Town in third. Four clubs, Worthing among them, sit on 32.

Two home games come this week: Kidderminster Harriers on Wednesday night and Yeovil Town on Saturday.

## Harrogate RUFC

Harrogate lost 52-31 at Sedgley Park, the unbeaten National League 2 North leaders, who have now won all 11 games. Harrogate drop one place to eighth with 28 points from 11 games, the club’s league table shows. There is no league game next Saturday; the next is at Wharfedale on 5 December.

## Harrogate Railway Athletic

The Rail lost 1-0 at South Leeds, James Law scoring on 45 minutes in front of 118, the league’s match list shows. They are still waiting for a first away win of the season and stay 14th in NCEL Division One with 27 points from 21 games. They have no game this Saturday and are home to Leeds UFCA on 5 December.

## Knaresborough Town

Knaresborough were due to play Bottesford Town at Manse Lane on Saturday, but no result has been published and the game no longer appears on the league’s match list. They slip to eighth in the NCEL Premier Division with 31 points from 19 games as others played.

The cricket season is over. Harrogate Cricket Club are back in the Yorkshire Premier League North in April.
