A Mexican restaurant and tequila bar has announced its Harrogate opening date.

Casa del Alma will open its doors at 10 Princes Square on Friday December 4.

The team behind Casa del Alma already runs a restaurant in Darlington and a street food unit in Saltburn.

Chef Matei Baran, who reached the quarter-finals of MasterChef: The Professionals in 2016, is behind the business.

He recently told the Press that Casa del Alma, which means ‘house of soul,’ will be a traditional Mexican restaurant and his Harrogate business would be an extension of his Darlington venue.

The Darlington restaurant is highly rated, with it achieving 4.8 stars out of five on Google and 4.6 stars out of five on Tripadvisor.

Casa del Alma posted on Facebook: “We’re so excited to officially announce our opening date!

“Get ready for delicious Mexican food, incredible cocktails, tequila and a vibrant atmosphere as we bring a taste of Mexico to Harrogate!”
