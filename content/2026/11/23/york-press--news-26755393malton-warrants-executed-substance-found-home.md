A woman in her 30s has been released under investigation while police enquiries continue after a warrant was executed at a house in Malton.

Supported by a police drone, officers from the Ryedale Neighbourhood Policing Team executed a warrant under Section 23 of the Misuse of Drugs Act at a property on Friday.

A vehicle parked outside the address was searched, and officers found a bag containing white powder, along with digital scales and bags.

The woman was arrested and later released under investigation.

A police spokesperson said: "Information provided by the public plays a vital role in helping police identify and disrupt drug dealers.

"Community intelligence helps us build a clearer picture of where drugs are being supplied, identify those involved, and target our enforcement activity where it will have the greatest impact."

The spokesperson encouraged the public to continue sharing information, no matter how small, to help tackle drug-related crime.
