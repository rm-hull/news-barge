Castle Howard have been awarded a prestigious VisitEngland Gold accolade.

The North Yorkshire stately home received a Gold 2025-2026 Visitor Attraction Accolade, awarded by Visit England for customer experience excellence.

The accolades were presented to 73 attractions across England at a celebratory lunch at the British Motor Museum in Warwickshire on 10 November.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Lady-Victoria-Borwick-and-Abbigail-Ollive.-Credit-VisitEngland.webp)

Castle Howard received the accolade based on the scores they obtained following their annual VisitEngland Visitor Attraction Quality Scheme assessment, which evaluated the whole visit, from visitor welcome to accessibility, online reviews, website, programming and more.

Other attractions in Yorkshire that achieved the recognition include Dalby Forest Park, Jorvik Centre, National Coal Mining Museum for England, RSPB Bempton Cliffs Nature Reserve, Streetlife Museum Hull and Yorkshire Sculpture Park.

Castle Howard visitor attraction director, Abbi Ollive, said: “We are thrilled to receive this national recognition from Visit England.

“Castle Howard and the Arboretum welcome 350,000 visitors annually from the UK, Europe and the rest of the world. We pride ourselves on a fantastic welcome, from the very start of the visitor journey on the website, to the face-to-face service our team deliver on site.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Photo-by-Charlie-Kirkpatrick-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Castle-Howard.-Photo-by-David-Lindsay-1.webp)

VisitEngland director, Andrew Stokes, said: “England’s visitor attractions are first-class, and these accolades highlight why they deserve this recognition. The recipients of these awards provide an outstanding welcome, unparalleled food and drink and magical storytelling making them integral to the high-quality tourism offer provided to domestic and international visitors.”

Castle Howard was the only UK stately home featured, alongside some of the world’s most extraordinary architectural landmarks, including the Great Pyramid of Giza and Rome’s Pantheon.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Castle-Howard.-Photo-by-David-Lindsay.webp)

Abbi continues: “To be named as a bucket list destination in this way is a huge honour. We know visitors from places like China and the USA make special trips to see Sir John Vanbrugh’s magnificent architecture and stand against the backdrop of Brideshead Revisited and Bridgerton, so it’s a real delight to have that global recognition.”

Castle Howard is currently open daily for its Christmas events until 3 January. The Gardens and Playground are open daily throughout the year. The House and Arboretum are open from Spring 2027.
