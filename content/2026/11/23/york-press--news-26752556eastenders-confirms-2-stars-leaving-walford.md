Two young stars are leaving Albert Square as EastEnders says goodbye to a family.

Aaliyah James and Juhaim Rasul Choudhury, who play Avani Nandra-Hart and Nugget Gulati, will be departing the BBC soap, marking the end of the Nandra-Hart family’s time in Walford.

An EastEnders spokesperson said: "We can confirm that Aaliyah James and Juhaim Rasul Choudhury will be leaving EastEnders, and we wish them all the best for the future."

The family’s storyline is coming to a close following the recent exit of their on-screen mother, Priya (Sophie Khan Levy), in September and the upcoming departure of their father Ravi (Aaron Thiara).

Ravi’s exit was confirmed in August, but his final scenes have not yet aired.

Ms James joined the cast in October 2023, while Mr Choudhury has appeared on the show since August 2022.

The family has been at the centre of several major storylines, including Ravi’s mental health struggles, Avani’s pregnancy plot and strip search, Nugget’s use of anabolic steroids, and Priya’s affair with Max.

Their departure comes just days after Francesca Henry, who plays Gina Knight, announced she would also be leaving the show.

In a statement shared on EastEnders’ social media, she reflected on her time in Walford and thanked fans for their support.

Ms Henry said: "EastEnders is the best team on telly and I have been so lucky to be a tiny part of it for the past three years.

"I've decided to say goodbye to Walford.

"I can't imagine my life now without the friends I've made at Elstree while playing Gina, and I want to say thank you to the fans.

"I am and always will be the biggest fan and champion of our show."

EastEnders airs Monday to Thursday at 7.30pm on BBC One, with most episodes available earlier from 6am on BBC iPlayer.
