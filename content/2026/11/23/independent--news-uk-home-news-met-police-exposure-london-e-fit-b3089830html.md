The Metropolitan Police has released two e-fit images of man wanted in connection with a series of sexual offences.

Detectives are currently probing incidents involving “a man exposing himself to women” between July and October this year.

The suspect has been described as a Black man around 5ft 10ins to 6ft, with a slim build and aged between 20 and 30-years old.

The police said the reported incidents occurred in areas with the postcodes SE14 and SE8. They are as follows:

* Thursday, 16 July at 20:25hrs in Goodwood Road, SE14
* Wednesday, 5 August at 20:40hrs in Sterling Gardens, SE14
* Monday, 10 August at 19:53hrs in Brocklebank Road, SE14
* Tuesday, 11 August at 15:00 in Hatcham park Road, SE14
* Tuesday, 1 September at 18:41hrs in Auburn Close, SE14
* Wednesday, 9 September at 20:00hrs in Tarragon Close, SE14
* Sunday, 13 September at 12:10hrs in Margaret Macmillan Park, SE8
* Tuesday, 20 October at 22:00hrs in Sterling Gardens, SE14
* Friday, 23 October in Gosterwood Street, SE8.

![One of the e-fits issued by the Metropolitan Police](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/12/45/FIMS-Image-ASJ1A-(003).png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=98%2C4%2C171%2C0&auto=webp)

Detective Inspector Steven Andrews, leading the investigation, said: “I’d like to thank the women who have reported these incidents and are supporting our investigation.

“Understandably, there will be concern throughout the community – particularly among women and girls – so I would like to reiterate our unwavering commitment to pursue offenders, build the strongest cases and ensure victim-survivors receive the support they need throughout the criminal justice process.”

DI Andrews added: “Anyone with information – this includes doorbell, dashcam footage, or if you recognise the man in the e-fit – is asked to contact police as a matter of urgency.”

![The second e-fit issued by the Metropolitan Police](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/23/12/43/FIMS2.png?quality=75&width=640&crop=3%3A2%2Csmart&trim=192%2C4%2C152%2C0&auto=webp)

Members of the public with information have been asked to call the police on 101 quoting 01/8058159/26, or to share details anonymously via Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

The police said “the victim-survivors continue to be supported by specialist officers”.
