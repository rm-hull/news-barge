Girls Aloud star Kimberley Walsh and Emmerdale actor Lewis Cope will return to Strictly Come Dancing for this year’s Christmas special.

The 75-minute show will feature six previous Strictly contestants competing in a festive dance extravaganza to be crowned the 2026 Christmas champion.

Walsh, from Allerton, Bradford, will return to the ballroom floor and be paired with Cristian Priori.

She was a finalist in the 2012 series and most recently appeared on Strictly in 2017, when she returned for that year’s Christmas special.

Walsh said: “I’m absolutely thrilled to be returning to the Strictly dance floor for the Christmas Special.

“Strictly and Christmas – two of my favourite things coming together! What makes it even more magical is knowing I’ll get to watch it with my boys on Christmas Day.”

Meanwhile, Cope and his partner Katya Jones were eliminated in the quarter-final of the BBC One dance competition last year.

He will be partnered with Lethabo Monametsi.

Cope said: “I’m absolutely buzzing to be returning to the Strictly ballroom. It’s been a whole year, so let’s hope I can remember a few of those steps!”

They join the previously announced returning celebrities broadcaster Angela Rippon and Pussycat Dolls star Ashley Roberts.

Strictly hosts Emma Willis, Johannes Radebe and Josh Widdicombe will present the festive episode, which will also feature Shirley Ballas, Craig Revel Horwood, Motsi Mabuse, and Anton Du Beke on the judging panel.

Further names for the Strictly Christmas Special will be announced on It Takes Two in due course.

The festive episode will air on Christmas Day on BBC One and iPlayer.
