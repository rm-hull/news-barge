Police investigating a York shop theft want to talk to these three men.

Stock worth £479.96 was stolen from the Nike store at the Designer Outlet York on Saturday 17 October.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’ve issued this CCTV of three men we would like to speak to, following a shop theft in York.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-nike-store-designer-outlet-york-17-oct-2026-3.webp)![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-nike-store-designer-outlet-york-17-oct-2026-2.webp)![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-nike-store-designer-outlet-york-17-oct-2026-1.webp)

“Please contact us if you recognise the men pictured on CCTV, as they may have information that will assist our investigation.”
