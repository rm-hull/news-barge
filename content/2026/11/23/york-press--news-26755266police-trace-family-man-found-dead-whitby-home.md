## Update after man, 75, found dead at North Yorkshire home

North Yorkshire Police issued an appeal to find the next of kin after a 75-year-old man was found dead at his home in Whitby *(Image: Supplied)*

Police have traced the family of a man found dead in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Police issued an appeal earlier today, Monday, November 23, to trace relatives of Rob Ballard, 75, who was discovered at his home in Marton Court in Whitby over the weekend.

The force confirmed there were no suspicious circumstances surrounding his death.
