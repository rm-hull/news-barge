## Woman celebrates 30 years at North Yorkshire equine hospital

Lorraine Colgan is celebrating 30 years at the Malton-based Rainbow Equine Hospital *(Image: Malton-based Rainbow Equine Hospital)*

A WOMAN who joined a North Yorkshire vets when it operated from an old farm building is celebrating 30 years with the business.

Lorraine Colgan joined the Malton-based Rainbow Equine Hospital in November 1996 when it was a small veterinary practice with just four vets, two nurses and two office team members.

Today, it is a purpose-built equine hospital with a 70-strong team and some of the most advanced veterinary facilities and diagnostic technology available, including CT and MRI scanners, mobile digital X-ray equipment and two operating theatres.

Having witnessed the hospital's growth and development first-hand, Lorraine, who is now practice manager, has seen huge changes in both the scale of the practice and the care it can provide for horses, ponies and donkeys from all over the north and further afield.

When she first joined, her responsibilities included answering the telephone, general office administration and bookkeeping from an old, converted farm building.

Her contribution to the smooth running of the practice led to her promotion to practice manager in 2010.

Lorraine's role now spans managing the veterinary and support teams, client relations, marketing, budget management and helping ensure the hospital continues to deliver the highest standards of care.

Rainbow Equine Hospital is one of VetPartners’ premier equine practices and two years at the group’s annual awards, Lorraine was crowned Practice Manager of the Year.

Lorraine said: “When I started, there were just eight of us working in what was essentially an old farm that had been turned into a veterinary practice.

“To see how much it has grown, both in terms of the team and the facilities, is incredible.

“We now have a purpose-built hospital, with technology and equipment that we could only have dreamed of when I first started.

The CT and MRI scanners, mobile digital X-rays and two theatres mean we can offer so much more to our patients.

“It is exciting to have witnessed all of this and to have been part of all the changes.

I feel very proud of what Rainbow has become and of the people who have helped make it happen.”

Rainbow Equine Hospital has developed into one of the premier equine hospitals in the UK, providing advanced diagnostic, surgical and intensive care facilities for horses referred from across the country.

The hospital is part of VetPartners, a veterinary group which supports continued investment in facilities and equipment, alongside opportunities for professional development, continuing professional development (CPD) and training for colleagues.

Lorraine said: “Being part of VetPartners brings additional support and opportunities for the practice and its people, helping us continue to develop and invest in what we do.

“Although so much has changed over the past 30 years, what remains the same is the commitment of the team to doing the very best for every horse in our care.

"That is what makes Rainbow such a special place to work.”
