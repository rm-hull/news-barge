Manchester’s Co-op Live arena was evacuated on Sunday night after an “unknown substance” was let off during US rapper Don Toliver’s show.

The incident is reported to have taken place while the 32-year-old rapper, whose real name is Caleb Zackery Toliver, was performing on stage and is understood to have occurred towards the end of the gig.

Emergency services including ambulance and police officers were called following the incident, with those affected being supported by authorities.

A major incident was declared as thousands were evacuated. One person was taken to hospital for treatment.

![Rapper Don Toliver was reportedly performing when the incident occurred](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/23/2283789920..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Footage circulating on social media appeared to show crowds of concert-goers looking distressed while coughing.

GMP Assistant Chief Constable Steph Parker said more than 12,000 people had been safely evacuated from the arena.

“Our priority was to ensure the safety of every person that was attending the concert,” she said in a statement.

“Several people were treated on site, and one has been taken to hospital at this time. Anyone who has left the event and since become unwell is advised to attend their local hospital.

“We understand the concern that this incident will have raised – particularly those with loved ones at the venue last night – and we’d like to thank everyone for their patience as we dealt with it.

“There were over 12,000 people in attendance during this concert who were safely escorted from the arena so they could make their way home.”

She continued: “Our declaration of a major incident has allowed us to ensure emergency services and partner agencies can work together quickly and effectively to coordinate a safe response.

“We will now begin to fully understand what this substance was and how this incident came about.”

![The Co-op Live arena can hold up to 23,500 people](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/23/2263545946..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Other images showed groups of fans in the crowded arena while the issue was being investigated.

The arena is a relatively new venue, having opened in 2024 has the capacity to hold 23,500 people.

Fans told the *Manchester Evening News* that they saw what appeared to be smoke canisters thrown into the crowd.

The rapper is one of the world’s biggest musicians with over 45 million listeners per month, according to Spotify and ranks as the 86th most popular artist in the world. His top song on the streaming service, “No Pole”, has over 1 billion plays.

He is set to continue his tour with a performance at both the O2 Arena and OVO Arena in Wembley in London.

Co-op Live said in a statement that venue bosses were working with police to identify the cause of the incident.

“Following an incident in the crowd at last night’s show, we are working closely with Greater Manchester Police to identify the cause and action appropriately,” the statement said.

“Our priority remains the safety and security of all visitors to Co-op Live, and we will provide more information once we have an update from GMP.”
