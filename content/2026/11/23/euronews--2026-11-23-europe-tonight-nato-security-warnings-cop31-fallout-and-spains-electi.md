## NATO chief Mark Rutte warns of escalating hybrid threats as Ukraine pushes for membership, COP31 collapses without a climate deal, Spain faces a pivotal right-wing electoral shift, IG Metall workers strike across Germany, and Paris illuminates the Champs-Élysées for the holidays.

NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte warned in Berlin that malign activities targeting Alliance members (including sabotage, cyberattacks, and airspace violations) are expected to intensify. "We are all targeted, we are all affected," Rutte stressed, calling on allies to bolster defense spending and industrial production. Meanwhile, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has renewed his call for Ukraine to join NATO, following a proposal by US negotiator Jared Kushner for security guarantees modeled on NATO’s Article 5.

**COP31 Ends in Disappointment Over Fossil Fuels and Finance** The UN climate conference in Antalya, Turkey (now remembered as both the longest and one of the most disappointing in history) concluded without a major new agreement after deep divisions emerged over fossil fuel phase-outs and climate financing. Tonight, Green MEP Michael Bloss joins us to break down the key takeaways from the summit and discuss what Europe’s role should be moving forward.**Spotlight on Spain elections** Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez, a prominent standard-bearer for European left-wing politics, faces the growing threat of being unseated in Spain’s parliamentary election. With the center-right conservatives leading most opinion polls, a potential alliance with the far-right Vox party could secure a governing majority, raising broader questions about whether Europe’s political firewall against the far right is eroding. Chief Correspondent Maria Tadeo joins us with the latest analysis from the ground.**Industrial Tensions Escalate as German Workers Walk Out** Labor disputes in Germany’s vital metal and electrical sectors are intensifying. The country’s largest trade union, IG Metall, called on more than 23,000 workers across 19 northern sites—including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Siemens Gamesa, and shipbuilder Meyer Werft—to stage a 24-hour warning strike. The walkouts follow Friday’s nationwide strikes that mobilized 151,000 workers, affecting major hubs such as Audi’s main plant in Ingolstadt and three BMW plants in Bavaria.**Paris Kicks Off the Festive Season on the Champs-Élysées**

Finally, we turn to Paris, where the holiday season officially began on Sunday evening with the illumination of the Champs-Élysées. Joined by Paris Mayor Emmanuel Grégoire, Oscar-winning actress and 2026 event patron Cate Blanchett flipped the switch on 52 kilometers of lights and two million LEDs spanning 400 trees.
