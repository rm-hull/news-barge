The festive month of December is rapidly bearing down on us and you know what that means: It's time for another round of the Steam Awards, the annual exercise in which we hand out *awards*to our favorite games on* Steam,* hence the name. First, though, comes the nomination process, which kicked off today and will run until November 30.

This year's event is a little different from those of years past, because 2026 is the 10thanniversary of the Steam Awards. To mark the big birthday, Valve has added a few new categories inspired by previous years:

* **Just Five More Minutes**
* **Defies Description**
* **This is My Job Now**

For even bigger excitement, there's also a new "Hall of Fame" category that pits the Game of the Year winners from the previous decade against one another in a final fight for supremacy. The contenders are:

![2026 Steam Award Hall of Fame contenders](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/ZPNSbATd974mHu9xfaVf5k-1200-80.png)

* 2016 - **Grand Theft Auto 5**
* 2017- **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt**
* 2018- **PUBG Battlegrounds**
* 2019 - **Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice**
* 2020 - **Red Dead Redemption 2**
* 2021 - **Resident Evil Village**
* 2022 - **Elden Ring**
* 2023 - **Baldur's Gate 3**
* 2024 - **Black Myth: Wukong**
* 2025 - **Hollow Knight: Silksong**

Apart from the Hall of Fame category, which is locked to those 10 games, Steam Award nominations are wide open: For each category, Steam will provide you with a list of games you've played as well as "popular and recommended games" you haven't, but you can throw in whatever game you want in whatever category you like, as long as it was released in 2026.

Serious Sam Shatterverse for "Outstanding Story-Rich Game Award?" *Yes you can*.

![Steam Awards - Serious Sam Shatterverse for Outstanding Story-Rich Game](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/wrceWJxKmFBT7g5iQsAcEj-1200-80.png)

Not that I recommend you actually *do*, to be clear.

As always, nominating games will not only put your support behind your favorite games of the year, it will also earn you coveted Steam badges. Nominations will close on November 30—the nominees will be revealed, and voting will begin, with the start of the Steam Winter Sale on December 17.
