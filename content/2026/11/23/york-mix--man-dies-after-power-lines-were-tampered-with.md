It is believed that a 58-year-old man had been electrocuted, after he was fatally injured in a field in Knottingley.

The circumstances surrounding his death are currently being treated as unexplained.

Police were called around 2.26pm on Sunday (22 November) to South Moor Lane. West Yorkshire Police said: “Initial enquiries suggest that the man might have made contact with an exposed live cable.”

According to engineers from Northern Powergrid, lines in the area had been tampered with and it’s suspected that there was an attempt to steal the cables.

A representative said: “We are very sad to learn that the injuries proved fatal and must stress that we do not know why the injured person was in the vicinity of the damaged power lines.”
