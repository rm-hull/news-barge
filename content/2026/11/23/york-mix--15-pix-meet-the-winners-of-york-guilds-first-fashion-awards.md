The winners have been revealed for the Golden Thread Awards – founded by The Company of Merchant Taylors in York to champion new talent in fashion, textiles and craft.

Marina Rose, the self-taught designer behind House of White Rose in York, has been named Designer of the Year.

She received The Golden Thread Award from Gary James McQueen, Chair of the Jury, at The Thread of Hope Charity Dinner & Awards at The Milner York on Sunday 22 November.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/golden-thread-awards-c-Mareks-Strods-2.webp)

The evening followed an afternoon Runway Showcase at Merchant Taylors’ Hall in Aldwark, where thirteen finalists, chosen from eighty designers across eleven regions of the UK, presented their work beneath the hall’s medieval timbers.

Speaking on the runway, Ms Rose said she had no formal training. “I am completely self-taught. I’ve taught myself everything,” she said.

“I have learned so much, I’ve made so many mistakes, and here I am.”

The three Design Awards were decided by an independent jury: Gary James McQueen, for seven years head textile designer at Alexander McQueen; Liz Mawbey of Selfridges; and Cherie Federico, founder of Aesthetica.

A cash prize accompanies each Design Award.

“Every one of the finalists was remarkably talented,” said Mr McQueen. “Deciding the winners was genuinely difficult.”

The Textile Innovation Award went to Liubov Brovarna of BROVARNA, Alton, presented by the Master of the Company, Sally Hobbs CBE. Daniel Zak Roberts of Birmingham City University received The Emerging Designer Award from Anna Pownall, winner of The Great British Sewing Bee 2026.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/golden-thread-awards-c-Paul-Jones.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/golden-thread-awards-c-Paul-Jones-2.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/golden-thread-awards-c-Paul-Jones-4.webp)

The two Production Awards, curated from the runway, went to Emilio Pugliese and team for The Look of the Night, presented by Yani Lin, Founding Creative Director; and to Brigida Gilma Amos Vunge, The Model of the Night, presented by Kevin Winkley, Model Director.

Anna Pownall, who won The Great British Sewing Bee on BBC One this year, watched the showcase before presenting the evening’s Emerging Designer Award.

“What an incredible day,” she said. “Such an inspiration to see all the designers, and how they’ve approached the fabrics differently. Each one is so unique.

“I’ve got a real interest in historical fashion, so it was really interesting to see the nods to different periods of history, and the structures and corsetry in the designs.”

Sally Hobbs CBE, Master of The Company of Merchant Taylors in the City of York, said: “Our Company has been part of York’s making since the fourteenth century.

“On Sunday that tradition met the next generation, and it is in very good hands. We are proud to have given these designers a stage in York, and we look forward to doing so again.”

The evening was hosted by Ben Fry, York Town Crier, with remarks from the Lord Mayor of York, Councillor Margaret Wells.
