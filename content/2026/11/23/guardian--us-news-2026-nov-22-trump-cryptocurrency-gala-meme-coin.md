Donald Trump is scheduled to headline his third cryptocurrency gala on ⁠Sunday night at his ​country club in Virginia, rewarding top holders of his own meme coin as Democrats accuse the president of improperly profiting from his crypto ventures.

The dinner, set to take place at the Trump National Golf Club in Sterling, Virginia, will include meet-and-greets with Trump supporters including the boxing champion Floyd Mayweather, Shark Tank investor Kevin O’Leary, NBA champion Tristan Thompson and mentalist Lior Suchard, according to the event’s website.

The gala’s access and perks are divided by tiers for the 185 holders of $TRUMP, the president’s meme coin. A Trump fragrance, a poster, a “commemorative” ticket, and a “Trump watch” are reserved for 120 attendees. Meanwhile, the top 29 investors are invited to a VIP reception, and the top four will receive a “TRUMP 18K Gold Watch”.

Trump has hosted two similar galas since he took office last year, bringing a flurry of criticism as Democrats accuse Trump and his family of monetizing his brand through an array of equities and crypto ventures while in office.

![Gold statue of Trump holding bitcoin in front of green lawn](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/b53c207f441a6a471f7951372042cbab50c6cccd/0_0_6000_4000/master/6000.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

After sweepingwins in the midterms, Democrats have vowed to investigate conflicts of interest involving Trump and his personal wealth during his presidency. They also say they plan to push for legislation that would ban activities such as the meme coin gala dinner.

“This dinner and scoring mechanism appear carefully constructed to increase investment in $TRUMP, allowing the President to abuse the power of his office to personally enrich himself, as he has throughout this term,” reads a letter sent by Adam Schiff and eight other US senators last month.

Since returning to the White House, Trump has made more than $1bn from his crypto businesses, according to financial disclosures released in June. Trump received more than $500m from his World Liberty Financial business selling new crypto products, and raked another $600m from sales of souvenir-type “meme” coins stamped with Trump’s face. Trump launched his $Trump meme coin days before his inauguration.

The White House did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Interest in the $TRUMP coin dinner has plummeted, with contestants’ combined holdings dropping from $148m in May 2025 to $8.6m in this month’s gala.

More than half of the digital wallets publicly listed on the contest leaderboard for Sunday’s dinner are tied directly to crypto exchanges that bar US-based users, indicating they ‌are linked to foreign nationals, according to an analysis of blockchain data prepared for Reuters by the nonpartisan, non-profit consumer advocacy group Public Citizen.

![People holding up signs of gold coins with cartoon images of Trump that say 'stop crypto corruption'](https://i.guim.co.uk/img/media/f088ca4b674a9afb66a26c05ca00b1815bf9d37b/0_0_6000_4000/master/6000.jpg?width=445&dpr=1&s=none&crop=none)

“Neither the President nor his family ⁠has ever engaged — or will ever engage — in conflicts of interest,” White House spokesperson Anna Kelly told Reuters ​earlier this year. “President Trump proudly made ​the United States the crypto capital of the world ​through executive actions.“

The Trump family’s other crypto-connected equities and ventures have also fallen in value to record ​lows. Together, outside investors in their ‌five main crypto and ​crypto-linked projects have lost more than $5bn​, according to a Reuters analysis.

The Trump meme coin has earned the Trump family at least $635m, according to the president’s June financial disclosure statement. In total, the Trumps had earned at least $2.3bn through April from their four main crypto ventures, including the meme coin.

The president’s stake in a fifth crypto-linked venture, Trump Media & Technology Group, is worth about $430m.
