Passengers to and from North Yorkshire are being reminded to plan ahead as engineers prepare to work on the East Coast Main Line this weekend.

There will be no trains between Peterborough and London on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November, with rail replacement buses in operation for many journeys.

Passengers should check before travelling and allow extra time for their journey.

During the closure, engineers will carry out further upgrades to digital signalling technology, renew track at New Southgate and near Potters Bar, improve overhead line equipment.

Journeys to and from London from Yorkshire and the North-East will take longer than usual. When searching for tickets, passengers should specify travel via Peterborough or Bedford, as alternative routes via Sheffield, Nottingham or Cambridge are expected to be extremely busy.

A small number of direct **LNER** services will run between Newcastle, Northallerton and York to/from Bedford, connecting with Thameslink services to London St Pancras and allowing passengers to complete their journey by train.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/LNER_Azuma_at_Bedford_3.webp)

**Travel arrangements by train operator:Grand Central:** (Thirsk, Northallerton, York)

No services will run all weekend. On Monday 30 November, the 07.48 London King’s Cross to Sunderland via York and Thirsk, will not run.

**Hull Trains:** (Selby and Howden)

An amended timetable will run between Hull and London St Pancras.

Two trains will run in each direction on Saturday, and three trains will run in each direction on Sunday.

Trains will not call at Beverley, Cottingham, Doncaster, Retford or Grantham.

**LNER:** (York, Northallerton, Harrogate and once a day from Selby)

Most services will start and terminate at Peterborough, with rail replacement coaches connecting customers to Bedford where Thameslink trains will operate to London St Pancras.

On Saturday 28 November and Sunday 29 November, there will also be some return train services from Newcastle to Bedford, where customers can connect onto Thameslink services to London – allowing them to complete their journey by train.

For more information and to book tickets, customers should visit: [https://www.lner.co.uk/news/book-your-tickets-to-and-from-london-via-bedford/](https://www.lner.co.uk/news/book-your-tickets-to-and-from-london-via-bedford/).
