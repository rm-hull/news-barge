Darts fans in York can see some of the world’s top players up close on the North Yorkshire coast next month.

**A Night at the Darts** returns to**Scarborough Spa** on**Friday, 4 December**, marking the event’s fourth visit to the venue since March 2023.

World number two Gian van Veen and world number nine Nathan Aspinall headline the 2026 bill, alongside world number 15 Chris Dobey and Karl Coleman.

It will be a second consecutive appearance at the Scarborough event for Aspinall. Previous tours across the country have featured big names including Phil Taylor, Luke Littler and Ricky Evans.

Unlike standard ranked PDC tournaments, **A Night at the Darts** blends competitive exhibition matches with live entertainment and audience participation.

Fans are encouraged to turn out in fancy dress, with the evening featuring player meet-and-greet sessions and interactive stage games – including a nine-dart challenge against a professional.

Doors open from 4.30pm for platinum ticket holders, with standard entry at 7pm ahead of the main event starting at 7.20pm.

To find out more, go to the Scarborough Spa website.
