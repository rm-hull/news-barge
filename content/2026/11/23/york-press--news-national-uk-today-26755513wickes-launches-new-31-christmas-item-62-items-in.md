## Wickes launches new £31 Christmas item with 62 items inside and a 'robust' extra

Wickes has released a new DIY advent calendar allowing buyers to complete a 62-piece toolkit *(Image: Getty Images)*

Wickes has released a new DIY advent calendar for £31, which allows buyers to build a complete toolkit.

The calendar features 24 doors and contains 62 tools, building up to a full set by Christmas Eve.

The retailer describes it as being "ideal" for DIY and motor enthusiasts, and gift shoppers.

Inside, buyers will find 36 precision screwdriver bits (25mm), 10 metric sockets (4–13mm), eight hex keys (1.5–6mm), adapters, and extensions.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21754888.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The advent calendar also comes with a storage case *(Image: Wickes)*

It also includes a soft-grip T-handle, a bottle opener, and a 3/8-inch universal socket that fits fasteners from 7 to 19mm.

The set also comes with a storage case to keep tools organised throughout the holidays and beyond.

In a statement, Wickes shared: "This year’s calendar kit also comes with a robust storage case to keep everything safe and organised, perfect for any DIY jobs over the holidays, at-home or even on-the-go.

"These clever extras make it a perfect gift for home improvers looking to add utility and festive cheer to their tool collection."

## The history of Wickes

Wickes launched in the UK in 1972, when US-based Wickes Companies joined forces with British builders' merchant Sankey's to open the retailer's first store in the country.

The business had grown to 41 locations by 1987, when it was floated on the London Stock Exchange under CEO Henry Sweetbaum.

Wickes was bought by Focus Group, backed by Duke Street Capital, in September 2000.

In December 2004, Focus Group agreed to sell the company to Travis Perkins, with the acquisition completed in February 2005.

In July 2019, following reports that Wickes' sales had risen by 9.7%, Travis Perkins announced plans to demerge the business.

It said Wickes was "well positioned to thrive as a stand-alone business" and would gain greater autonomy over its strategy and investment.

The demerger was confirmed in April 2021, with Wickes separating from Travis Perkins by the end of the month.

Discussing its mission, Wickes shared: "At Wickes our mission is simple: to give the nation the confidence to tackle any task, no matter the size.

"We believe that everyone should feel house proud, that's why our products are designed to inspire you to create the perfect home.

"We're committed to helping everyone get that 'job well done' feeling and achieve project perfection."
