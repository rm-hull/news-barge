Ellie Kildunne suggested she had planned a try celebration involving the drawing of a watch on her wrist, but opted against it after two relatively simple run-ins

**England and Bristol Bears full-back Ellie Kildunne marked her return to former club Harlequins with two tries, victory and a pointed reference to reports that her poor time-keeping was behind her Stoop exit.**

Kildunne, with a watch drawn on taping around her wrist, ran in two first-half tries in a 48-21 victory at the Stoop in the first round of the PWR on Sunday.

"You've all seen the headlines. Why would I have a watch on my wrist?" the 27-year-old told TNT Sport after the match.

She later posted a photo of the watch drawing on Instagram, external with the caption: "Right on time."

Kildunne, who was not quoted in Quins' announcement of her departure in June, subsequently said she was "gutted" to be leaving and that it was "as much a shock to me as everyone else".

She signed for Bristol two weeks later.

The Bears, who also signed Wales back row Bethan Lewis in the summer, finished eighth in the nine-team top flight last season.

Kildunne was not the only England star to enjoy success against the former club, with Tatyana Heard, who was part of big-spending Sale's summer recruitment drive, enjoying a 31-17 win over Gloucester-Hartpury.

Gloucester-Hartpury, who won the title three times in a row between 2023 and 2025, are without a head coach after Dan Murphy left on the eve of the season.

Champions Saracens and Ealing Trailfinders, who lost to Saracens in June's final, best Leicester and Loughborough Lightning respectively.
