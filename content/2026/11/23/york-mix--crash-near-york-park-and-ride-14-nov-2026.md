A two-car crash on a main York road is being investigated by police.

It happened at 1.32pm on Saturday 14 November on the York-bound carriageway of Hull Road at the junction with Grimston Bar Park & Ride.

A blue Toyota Corolla and a grey Hyundai i10 collided.

#### Crash location

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re appealing for any witnesses to the collision, anyone who may have seen the vehicles prior to the collision, or anyone with relevant dashcam footage, to contact us.

“If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
