## South Africa vs Bangladesh 2nd Test Preview

The 1st Test looked closely contested for the opening few days. Bangladesh started well, scoring 400 in their first innings, with centuries from Towhid Hridoy and wicketkeeper Litton Das. The Proteas, however, responded with a massive 557, taking a 157-run lead. A maiden Test century from opener Marques Ackerman, another hundred from Ryan Rickelton, and Kagiso Rabada's career-best 72 at No. 10 helped the hosts turn things around.

For a moment, it looked like the match could be heading for a draw. But Simon Harmer ripped through Bangladesh's batting order on the final morning, picking up six wickets as the visitors were bowled out for just 168. Kagiso Rabada also chipped in with three wickets in the second innings to finish with eight for the match and earn Player of the Match honours. South Africa then needed just 12 runs to seal a nine-wicket victory.

The Proteas will now be without captain Temba Bavuma for the 2nd Test. He has been ruled out after sustaining a right calf strain while batting in the first innings. Tony de Zorzi is expected to come in as his replacement at No. 4, while Aiden Markram is likely to lead the side.

While Bangladesh have never won a Test against South Africa, their first-innings performance will give them some confidence. They showed they can score big runs even in challenging overseas conditions, with Hridoy, Litton and Tanzid Hasan all making valuable contributions.

However, captain Najmul Hossain Shanto admitted that missed chances and too many no-balls cost his side dearly. If Bangladesh can address those issues and avoid another batting collapse, they could make life difficult for the hosts and potentially pull off an upset, just as they did against Australia earlier this year.
