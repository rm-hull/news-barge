# Police appeal after man exposes himself to woman on Aldi car park in Yorkshire

## Police in Yorkshire have issued an appeal after a man was reported to have exposed himself to a woman in a supermarket car park.

South Yorkshire Police said the incident haqppened at around 1.25pm on November 2 in the Aldi car park in Swallownest in Rotherham.

The force has issued a photo of an image of a vehicle, and said officers would like to speak to the occupant as they believe they can help with the investigation.

A statement from the force said: “Following numerous enquiries, we are now releasing a CCTV image of a vehicle, the occupant of which we would like to speak to as we believe they may be able to help us with our investigation.

![South Yorkshire Police released an image of a vehicle following the offence](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/23/10/33/Aldi-flashing.png?trim=0,2,83,0&width=640&quality=65)


“The driver of the vehicle is described as white, of a medium build, around 5ft 10ins tall, with short grey hair and aged between 60 and 70 years old.”
