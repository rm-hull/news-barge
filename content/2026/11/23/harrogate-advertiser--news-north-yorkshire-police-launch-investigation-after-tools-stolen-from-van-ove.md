North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information and witnesses after a van was broken into overnight in Ripon.

The incident happened between 8pm on Wednesday 18 and 7am on Thursday 19 November when a van parked on Church Lane was broken into and a quantity of tools was stolen.

A spokesperson at North Yorkshire Police said: “If you witnessed anything suspicious in the area during this time, or have any CCTV, Ring doorbell, dash cam, or other video footage that may assist with enquiries, please contact us.”

If you can help, you should call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

You should quote the North Yorkshire Police reference number 12260226976 when providing any details regarding the incident.
