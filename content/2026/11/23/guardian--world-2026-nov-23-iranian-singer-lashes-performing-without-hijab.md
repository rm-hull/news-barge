An Iranian singer and four members of her production team ⁠have each received 74 lashes after being sentenced for “offending public decency by producing and ⁠publishing obscene and ⁠immoral ​content online”, an Iranian news website said on Monday.

Parastoo Ahmadi and the four ⁠others were also handed two-year bans on artistic and musical activities and travel, the Emtedad website ⁠quoted lawyer Mostafa Nili as saying.

The case followed the ​release of a ‌video on YouTube ‌in December 2024 showing Ahmadi performing without the mandatory hijab alongside ‌male musicians in Qom, south of the capital, Tehran, the Norway-based human rights group Hengaw said.

Western governments and human rights groups have criticised flogging as torture and a violation of international law. Iran rejects accusations over ‌its human rights record, saying western countries should focus on their own human rights ​problems.

Rules requiring women to wear the hijab were a flashpoint during protests in 2022 after the ⁠death in custody of a Kurdish Iranian woman, Mahsa Amini, ​who was ​arrested for allegedly violating ​the dress code.

The rules have increasingly been ​flouted on ‌the streets of Tehran ​and ​beyond, and enforcement of the rules has also become more selective.
