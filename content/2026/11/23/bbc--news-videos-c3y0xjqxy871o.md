# How Ukraine drone attacks on Russian ships unfolded

Two Russian ships have been hit by Ukrainian drones in Libya on Sunday. Verified videos show huge fireballs, smoke plumes rising and sea drones attacking the vessels.

Experts say they believe this is the first time Ukraine's President has publicly acknowledged an attack outside the Black and Baltic seas. Merlyn Thomas reports.
