Emergency services were called to an incident off Boroughbridge Road in York this morning.

Police, ambulance and fire crews went to reports of a fire at an address in Sandacre Court.

A stretch of Boroughbridge Road was closed for a while shortly after 9am while they responded.

#### Sandacre Court, York

North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service says the fire has now been put out.
