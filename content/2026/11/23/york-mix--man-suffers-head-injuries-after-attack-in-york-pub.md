Police investigating an assault in a York pub released this image today (Monday).

It follows an incident at the Roman Bath on St Sampson’s Square.

The victim, a man in his 50s, suffered head injuries after being attacked by another man on Saturday 10 October.

#### Roman Bath pub, York

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-roman-bath-pub-york-10-october-2026-full.webp)

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We believe the person in the image will have information that could help our investigation.

“If you can help us identify the person shown, please get in touch.”
