# Reform councillor facing court hearing over alleged assault and drunk and disorderly offences

## A Reform UK councillor facing charges for alleged offences of assault and being drunk and disorderly has been suspended by the party.

Wayne Beddoes, who represents Wakefield East ward on Wakefield Council, is due to appear in court next month in relation to two separate incidents alleged to have taken place in the city centre earlier this year.

Wakefield Council has also confirmed Coun Beddoes now sits as an Independent and is no longer the authority’s Armed Forces Champion.

According to West Yorkshire Police, Coun Beddoes has been summoned to appear before Leeds Magistrates’ Court on December 11 in relation to an offence of assault, said to have taken place at Wakefield Exchange, on Union Street, on July 18.

![Councillor Wayne Beddoes was elected to represent Wakefield East ward on Wakefield Council in May this year.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjEwYjBmOGIyLTI0OWUtNDg5YS1iYjE4LWRmNTBkODI3ZDljNzo0NDQxYmI3Mi00OTYyLTQ1NGItYTkzZC01YjY0ZDVmMTcwNzA=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


The Local Democracy Reporting Service understands the drunk and disorderly offence is alleged to have occurred in the early hours of August 30 in The Bull Ring.

Coun Beddoes was elected for the first time in May this year, when Reform took control of the council from Labour by winning 58 out of 63 seats at the local elections.

He was elected to serve a one-year term of office after coming third in the ballot with 1,161 votes, a 9.6 per cent share of the overall turn out.

A West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “A Wakefield man has been summoned by postal requisition to appear before magistrates next month.

“Wayne Beddoes, 54, will appear before Leeds Magistrates’ Court on December 11 after being summoned for an offence of assault reported to have occurred at Wakefield Exchange on July 18 this year.

“He will also appear after being summoned for an offence of drunk and disorderly behaviour reported to have occurred on August 30.”

It is understood Coun Beddoes was suspended after Wakefield’s local Reform group referred the matter to the national party.

A party spokesperson said: “Coun Beddoes has been suspended from Reform UK pending investigation.”

A council spokesperson said: “We have been informed that Coun Wayne Beddoes now sits as an Independent councillor.

“Coun Ian Fletcher has replaced him as the council’s Armed Forces Champion.”

Coun Beddoes has been approached for comment.
