Deciding whether to take the cut or pure drugs in GTA 6 is a decision you'll make during the Dealer's Choice mission in Chapter 3.

The proposal is that if you can pass off the drugs cut with baby powder, you can keep the profit, but the deal might go south if they realise, while bringing the pure stuff should make the dealers happy. Here's what happens if you choose either:

* **Cut drugs**: If you choose the cut drugs, the dealers will immediately realise the stuff is cut with baby powder. They'll confront you, at which point you can either offer to give them half off (which reduces your profit to the same as it would have been if you'd taken pure), or you can tell them to take it or leave it. This second option will cause a gunfight, but assuming you survive, you can grab the money from their car and make maximum profit. This also doesn't have any other consequences.
* **Pure drugs**: If you choose this option, you'll make less money, but the deal will go off without a hitch after they test it. That means no gunfights.

With that in mind, I'd say the cut drugs are the best to pick. You get maximum profit, a cool shootout that makes the mission less boring, and it doesn't have any significant consequences afterwards.
