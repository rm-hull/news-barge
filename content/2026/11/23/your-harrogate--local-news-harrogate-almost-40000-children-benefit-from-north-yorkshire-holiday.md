Almost 40,000 children and young people have taken part in North Yorkshire’s FEAST holiday programme since it launched five years ago.

The scheme has provided more than 200,000 free meals in that time, with a healthy meal included at every session.

Bookings opened today (Monday 23rd November) for the Christmas holidays.

More than 40 local providers will run around 80 sessions across the county from Monday 21st December, including sports, outdoor adventures, creative arts, science and technology.

![](https://mmo.aiircdn.com/409/6b04535f068e4bb5ff2.png)

Nine-year-old Jaxon Pitt, from Harrogate, tried filmmaking, 3D printing and smoothie-making at Art Camp over the summer.

Jaxon said:

> “I just love everything about Art Camp. We do fun things that I wouldn't get to do at home.”

FEAST is open to children aged five to 16, with free places available to those who receive benefits-related free school meals.

Parents and carers can browse activities and request a place through the FEAST section of the North Yorkshire Together website.

*Providers will start confirming bookings on Wednesday 25th November.*
