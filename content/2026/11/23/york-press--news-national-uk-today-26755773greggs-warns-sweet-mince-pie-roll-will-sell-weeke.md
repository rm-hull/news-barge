Greggs has warned customers that one of its festive items is predicted to sell out by this weekend, and it won't be available in shops again until next year.

The retailer unveiled its new Christmas menu back in October, and one of the items included was the Sweet Mince Pie Roll.

This is filled with sweet mincemeat made from vine fruits, Bramley apples and candied orange and lemon peel, wrapped in puff pastry and finished with a light dusting of icing sugar.

Greggs says the sweet treat, which costs £1, has quickly become a fan favourite, but it is predicted to sell out by this weekend (Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29).

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21755177.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Sweet Mince Pie Roll was introduced along with other Greggs Christmas menu items back in October *(Image: Greggs)*

It has been confirmed that once the Sweet Mince Pie Roll sells out, it will not return until the winter season of 2027.

Zoe Harris, Customer Director at Greggs, said: "The Sweet Mince Pie Roll has quickly become one of the stars of our Christmas menu, but they’ve proven so popular that we’re set to sell out before December.

"So, consider this a public service announcement, this is the last week to get your hands on one before it disappears until next Christmas.

"As for Santa, if he misses out this week, our classic Sweet Mince Pies are still in stock and waiting for him."

## Greggs launches luxury hotel suite with sausage roll service

Earlier this month, Greggs opened a luxury hotel suite in partnership with Hotel Gotham in Newcastle.

The Greggs Suite, designed for two guests, offers year-round stays.

Decorated in Greggs’ signature midnight blue and gold, the suite features a 'ring for a sausage roll' concierge service.

Guests can use a dedicated rotary phone to order Greggs favourites, which will be delivered to the room on a silver platter.
