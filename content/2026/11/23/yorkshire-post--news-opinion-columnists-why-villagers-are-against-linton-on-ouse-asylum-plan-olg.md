# Why we villagers are against the Linton-on-Ouse asylum plan - Olga Matthias

## Olga Matthias, of the Linton-on-Ouse Action Group, sets out the village’s view on the asylum camp plans.

Plans to repurpose the decommissioned RAF base at Linton-on-Ouse as accommodation for asylum seekers have reignited debate. Sir Alec Shelbrooke, MP for Wetherby and Easingwold, submitted questions in Parliament.

Tom Gordon, Liberal MP for Harrogate and Knaresborough, calls for transparency and publication of site risk assessments. Yet Anna Turley, Minister for Border Security and Asylum, persists with the argument of closing hotels.

Regional MPs, including pro-immigration Rachael Maskell (Labour, York Central), have stated in Parliament that the plan is unsuitable - for residents, asylum seekers, and taxpayers. North Yorkshire councillors and the mayor and deputy mayor of York and North Yorkshire warn of substantial practical, financial, social and resourcing challenges.

![Olga Matthias.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/20/19/35/onecms_bf8ac60d-12d5-4630-ad82-4d2edb0140d2.jpg?trim=0,252,0,148&width=640&quality=65)


Yorkshire Water advises that significant new water infrastructure is required, and the currently inadequate sewage system needs completely overhauling.

The NHS, already over-stretched in the region, and North Yorkshire police and fire Services, having some of the fewest numbers of officers per square mile of any force in the UK, have cautioned that increased demand for routine and unplanned activity - alongside the need for specialist services such as mental health support and language-accessible provisions - will compromise services for both locals and newcomers.

Estimates suggest housing asylum seekers at the site could now cost at staggering £853 per night per person, costing circa £403m over 3 years.

Had the electorate known closing hotels would mean spending five times more than the average hotel room would they agree with the policy?

Isn’t utilising lower cost rented houses or HMOs which are truly dispersed across all geographies better?. It would better meet Andy Burnham’s “fair share” statement, given costs circa £40-50 per night per person not £853.

The site’s physical readiness and availability is a problem. Planning and installation of utilities complicates any immediate reuse of the facility and make the plan fiscally ridiculous given the site is only available from the Ministry of Defence until July 2029.

If the current Cabinet chooses to move ahead with portacabins, shipping fuel, water and sewage in and out of the site, the cost would be huge, both financially and environmentally.

Do taxpayers want the government spending their contributions on transportation of human waste rather than NHS cancer treatments or defending our nation?

The village is home to roughly 600 adults and 100 children; proposals for an asylum centre would more than double the local population.

This scale of the influx risks overwhelming the community. Mr Burnham’s “fair share” would have young single males of different cultural beliefs and norms being up to 80 per cent of the population.

Is this the right decision for a democratic government to make in 2026 – will they remove the vote from the women of Linton next?

Anyone with half a brain would not inflict this demographic ratio on either men or women in the free world.

The Government states the sites will be self-sufficient as much as possible – the previous proposal to house 1,500 male asylum seekers in 2022, was canned given clear infrastructure issues but also operator staffing issues, along with the cost and logistics of transportation for asylum seekers to local cities and towns given the narrow roads and local winter flooding.

Environmental and security considerations round out the list of issues remaining unanswered.

Significant site modification, the use of portacabins, generators, tank storage would all affect the environment, biodiversity and the scale of the facility would necessitate robust, transparent security arrangements to safeguard asylum seekers and residents.

*Professor Olga Matthias is the spokesperson for the Linton-on-Ouse Action Group*
