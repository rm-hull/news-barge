A person has been taken to hospital after being rescued from a burning flat in York.

Three fire crews, six police cars and paramedics were on the scene in Sandacre Court, off Boroughbridge Road, at 8.30am on Monday (November 23).

One person was inside the flat and was rescued by firefighters, North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said.

The fire service said its crews rushed to the scene after an alarm was activated inside the flat.

“On arrival, crews found a fire inside the property with the occupant unaccounted for,” a fire service spokesperson said.

“Under rapid deployment, crews entered the property and rescued one occupant from inside the property.

“The occupant was taken to hospital with paramedics and a fire investigation officer attended to determine the cause of the fire.”
