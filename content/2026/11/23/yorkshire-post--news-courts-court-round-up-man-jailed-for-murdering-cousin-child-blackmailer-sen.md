# Court round-up: Man jailed for murdering cousin, child blackmailer sentenced and elderly pedestrian seriously injured

## A man who brutally murdered his own cousin during a camping trip, a Bradford offender who blackmailed nine young girls and a driver who left an elderly pedestrian with catastrophic injuries are among the latest offenders sentenced in Yorkshire.

A number of serious criminal cases have recently been heard in courts across Yorkshire. Here is a round-up of four of the latest sentences.

## **Rotherham man jailed for life after brutally murdering cousin during camping trip**

A Rotherham man who beat his own cousin to death during a camping trip before lying to police about what happened has been jailed for life.

Wayne White, 54, was found guilty of murdering his cousin, Garry Claydon, 54, following an attack in the early hours of Sunday (Jun 1), 2025.

![Wayne White, 55, has been found guilty by a jury, of murdering his cousin, Garry Claydon, after the pair went camping on wasteland in Rotherham last year.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/17/16/01/waynewhite.jpg?trim=22,0,75,0&width=640&quality=65)


Sheffield Crown Court heard that White subjected Mr Claydon to a vicious beating while the pair were camping in Rotherham.

Following the attack, White called the ambulance service and claimed his cousin had been assaulted by an unknown person during the night.

He told emergency services that Mr Claydon had left their camping spot and returned saying he had been beaten up.

When police arrived, White repeated his account and denied being responsible for his cousin's injuries.

However, extensive CCTV footage covering the area showed no evidence of Mr Claydon leaving during the night, contradicting White's claims.

The court also heard that White had attacked his cousin two nights earlier, on May 30, fracturing his eye socket.

He returned to Sheffield Crown Court on Wednesday (Nov 18), where he was sentenced to life imprisonment with a minimum term of 18 years.

In a statement following sentencing, Mr Claydon's family said: “Garry had a difficult life, but no one deserves to have their life taken away by another person. He has been deprived of his opportunity to turn his life around and that is incredibly sad.”

## **Bradford man jailed after blackmailing nine young girls over intimate images**

A Bradford man who blackmailed nine young girls into sending intimate images and money has been handed an extended 17-year sentence.

Musaib Hussain, 24, of Bridgwater Road, Bradford, targeted victims through social media platforms including TikTok and Snapchat.

Bradford Crown Court heard that Hussain persuaded girls to send intimate photographs before threatening to share the images online unless they gave him money.

![Musaib Hussain](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/21/12/01/MUSAIB-HUSSAIN.jpeg?trim=32,0,228,0&width=640&quality=65)


One victim described how Hussain threatened to kill her and her family if she refused to send further images and videos.

She said: “When I found out that my intimate images and videos had been shared on social media my sense of safety was destroyed.”

Hussain admitted 18 charges, including blackmail, causing a child to engage in sexual activity and making indecent images of children.

At Bradford Crown Court on Friday (Nov 20), he was handed an extended 17-year sentence and made subject to a lifelong Sexual Harm Prevention Order.

Detectives believe there may be further victims who have not yet come forward.

Detective Constable Gemma Goodwin, of Bradford's Online Child Abuse Team, described Hussain as a dangerous individual who exploited his victims for financial and sexual gain.

She praised the bravery of the girls who reported his crimes.

## **Driver jailed after elderly pedestrian left with catastrophic injuries in Northallerton crash**

A driver who struck an elderly pedestrian on Northallerton High Street before fleeing the scene and attempting to conceal evidence has been jailed for 26 months.

Luke Scott, 34, of Brickside Way, Northallerton, was driving a black Ford Fiesta when he hit the man, now aged 81, at around 8.14pm on Tuesday (Mar 12), 2024.

The pedestrian had been crossing the road after an evening out with his wife when he was struck and thrown into the air.

![Luke Scott](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/21/11/18/luke-scott-16121991.jpg?trim=0,0,0,0&width=640&quality=65)


He suffered devastating injuries, including a brain haemorrhage, multiple skull and facial fractures, and fractures to his pelvis and legs.

Rather than stopping to help, Scott and his passenger, Hayley Richardson, 34, drove away.

The following day, police discovered a partially burned Ford Fiesta abandoned in Northallerton Rigg.

Investigators linked the vehicle to Richardson and established that both defendants had been drinking alcohol at a pub before the crash.

Scott pleaded guilty to causing serious injury by careless driving and perverting the course of justice.

At Teesside Crown Court on Thursday (Nov 19), he was sentenced to 26 months in prison.

Richardson, of Berry Close, Great Yarmouth, admitted perverting the course of justice and received a nine-month prison sentence suspended for 18 months, alongside 80 hours of unpaid work.

In a victim personal statement, the pedestrian's wife described how the crash had destroyed their retirement.

She said: “We are no longer able to do any of the things that we previously enjoyed as a couple or as individuals and the disruption that has been caused to us cannot be measured.”

Detective Sergeant Ed Drake, of North Yorkshire Police, described the defendants' actions as selfish and callous.

## **Castleford man convicted of sexually assaulting and strangling woman**

A Castleford man has been convicted of sexually assaulting, strangling and physically abusing a woman who was left suffering nightmares and multiple broken bones.

Martin Longstaff, 51, was found guilty of five offences following an investigation by Wakefield's Adult Safeguarding Unit.

The offences took place in Castleford in April 2026, with police launching an investigation after emergency services were called following an assault on the victim.

![Martin Longstaff](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/18/18/25/906681589_1666940388809834_2312962102097571178_n.jpeg?trim=294,0,43,0&width=640&quality=65)


Longstaff was convicted of sexual assault by digital penetration, non-fatal strangulation, two counts of actual bodily harm and controlling and coercive behaviour.

Longstaff was also made subject to an indefinite restraining order and placed on the Sex Offenders Register for life.

In a victim impact statement, the woman described suffering nightmares and continuing physical pain from broken fingers and ribs.

She said: “Since the attacks, I suffer with bad nightmares which keep me awake and I keep re-living what happened to me.”

Detective Constable Adele Bossons, of Wakefield's Adult Safeguarding Unit, praised the woman's courage in seeking help and supporting the investigation.
