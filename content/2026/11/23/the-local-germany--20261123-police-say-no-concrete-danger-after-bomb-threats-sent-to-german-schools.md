Bomb threats were emailed to schools in Germany but there appeared to be no "concrete danger" at present, police said Monday, after a wave of hoax bomb warnings at schools across Europe in recent weeks.

More than 450 schools in the southwestern state of Baden-Wuerttemberg were sent the threats, police said. Police in the eastern state of Thuringia also said "numerous" schools there received bomb threats, which were not considered to be genuine.

A report by Tagesschau clarified that threats were received via email at schools natiowide, including in Jena, Erfurt, Nordhausen, Gotha, Rudolstadt, and Greiz in Thuringia, and also in several schools in Lower Saxony.
