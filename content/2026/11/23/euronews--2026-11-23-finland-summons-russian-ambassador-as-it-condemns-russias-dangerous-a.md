## It comes after a Russian helicopter was suspected of having violated Finnish airspace on Saturday afternoon, off the coast of Loviisa.

Finland announced on Monday that it had summoned the Russian ambassador as it condemned Moscow's strikes on Ukraine and hit out at what it called Russia's "dangerous and reckless actions" in Europe.

"We strongly condemn Russia's illegal war of aggression and its escalating strikes against civilian targets in Ukraine. We stand in solidarity with Denmark, Poland and our other European partners," the foreign ministry said in a post on social media.

It comes after a Russian helicopter was suspected of having violated Finnish airspace on Saturday afternoon, off the coast of Loviisa.

Finnish Defence Minister Antti Häkkänen said the incident may have been related to "rescue or monitoring operations" involving the Russian cargo ship Sparta II, which caught fire after being struck by drones in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday morning as it was sailing to Kaliningrad.

"Ukraine has the right to defend itself against Russia's illegal aggression. Russia must end its war of aggression against Ukraine," the foreign ministry added on Monday.

The Finnish Border Guard confirmed on Sunday that the Sparta II was being towed away by a Russian rescue ship.

"The vessel is slightly listing, but the situation is stable," it said in an update on the situation, adding that a Border Guard helicopter was monitoring the Sparta II and any potential risks to the local marine environment.

The United States announced sanctions against the Sparta II in 2022, shortly after Moscow launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Ukraine has also sanctioned the vessel.

The Gulf of Finland is a crucial maritime passage for Russia, connecting Saint Petersburg with the wider Baltic Sea.
