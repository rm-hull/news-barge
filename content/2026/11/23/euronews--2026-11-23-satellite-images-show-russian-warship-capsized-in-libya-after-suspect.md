Satellite images released by US-based intelligence company Vantor show the aftermath of an explosion that left a Russian naval vessel capsized at the port of Benghazi in eastern Libya.

Images show the ship docked at the port before the incident and largely submerged afterwards.

Libya's eastern-based government said the explosion happened at dawn on Sunday, while the vessel was stopping for fuel and supplies on its way back to Russia. Officials suspect a drone attack.

According to Vantor's satellite analysis, the vessel appears to be the Russian navy's *Merkury.*
