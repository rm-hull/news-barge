A TEENAGE boy has been charged following the alleged theft of a motorcycle in York.

The 13-year-old, from York, was arrested after a Yamaha motorbike was reported stolen from the Holgate area on Friday (November 20), at around 4.40pm.




A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said: "CCTV showed a person tampering with the vehicle prior to pushing it down the street.

"A short time later, the vehicle was abandoned in the St Paul’s Square area as officers arrived at the scene."

He has been charged with theft of a motor vehicle and is due to appear at Harrogate Magistrates’ Court in January.
