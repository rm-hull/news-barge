# 'Life is not infinite': Catherine talks about 'mortality' in new documentary clip

The Princess of Wales has said cancer made her confront her own vulnerabilities, in a new clip from her upcoming documentary, Heal.

"When you're faced with your own mortality, you have to accept that life is not infinite," Catherine says.

In March 2024, Catherine revealed that she had a cancer diagnosis and was receiving chemotherapy.

In a previously released trailer for the documentary, Catherine is seen hugging her children and her husband, the Prince of Wales, as she completed the Three Peaks Challenge earlier this year.

Heal will air on BBC One on Sunday, 29 November.
