British Paralympic silver medallist Lee Fryer has been charged with 11 counts of rape.

Wheelchair basketball player Fryer has been remanded in custody and will face trial in March 2027.

The 26-year-old from West Yorkshire faces 17 charges in total, including voyeurism, intentional strangulation, assault, controlling or coercive behaviour and possession of a class A drug, namely cocaine.

He appeared at Leeds Crown Court on Thursday via video link from prison, where he pleaded not guilty to all the charges except voyeurism, for which no plea was entered.

The allegations concern one woman and are alleged to have taken place between May 2021 and May 2026 in England, Spain and South Africa.

British Wheelchair Basketball (BWB) formally suspended Fryer and removed hum from the GB programme he was arrested in July.

A BWB spokeperson said: "We are aware of the criminal charges brought against Lee Fryer and recognise the seriousness of the allegations.

"At the point of his arrest, he was removed from the GB Programme and all British Wheelchair Basketball activities, pending the outcome of the external investigation.

"As criminal proceedings are ongoing, it would be inappropriate to comment further at this time."

Fryer was part of the ParalympicsGB that won a silver medal at the Paris Games in 2024, narrowly missing out on gold when beaten 73-69 in the men’s wheelchair final.

It was nevertheless the first time Great Britain’s men had reached a final since 1996, equalling their best-ever finish.

ParalympicsGB said: "We are aware there are charges against Lee Fryer.

"As the matter is now subject to ongoing legal proceedings we will not be commenting further at this stage."

Fryer was also part of the British teams that collected world silver and European gold, winning both in 2023.
