Prince Harry and Meghan Markle arrived at the European Parliament in Brussels on Tuesday for an event focused on online child safety.

Harry is due to speak at the opening of the Lost Screen Memorial, an art installation honouring children who have died following online harm.

The appearance marks Meghan's first public outing since her father, Thomas Markle, was charged in the US with possessing child sexual abuse material. The couple's visit is part of their campaign supporting families affected by online harm.
