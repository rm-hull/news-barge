A thug bit his girlfriend’s finger down to the bone, leaving her nail hanging on by a thread, after she confronted him about cheating.

Samuel Snell inflicted the horrific injury when she tried to stop him fighting her friend’s partner.

Teesside Crown Court heard that the 29-year-old had been drinking in Newcastle before returning home, where the victim’s daughter overheard him speaking to another woman.

Prosecutor Jon Harley said the woman was being driven through Darlington by her friend and the friend’s boyfriend when she spotted Snell.

He said: “She got out of the car to confront him and was shouting at the defendant, who responded by grabbing her by the shoulders and pushing her towards the car.

“This prompted the man to get out of the car and intervene, and the pair started fighting.

"The man pinned Snell to the floor, but the defendant was angry and intoxicated and continued to struggle.

“The woman tried to intervene to protect the man, and when she did so, the defendant bit her index finger with such force that it left a gash through to the bone.”

Mr Harley said Snell got up, ripped the car’s door mirror off and damaged its windscreen wipers.

Snell, of Lynton Gardens, Darlington, admitted causing grievous bodily harm, possessing cannabis and criminal damage after his arrest on April 13, 2024.

His barrister, Robert Mochrie, said Snell had since started a new relationship and fathered a child with his partner, more than two years after the incident.

He said: “His life has changed completely since he committed this offence.

“He is now in a stable relationship, with a one-year-old son and a stepson.

“He is very remorseful for what happened.”

Judge Amanda Rippon told Snell that images of the injury were among the worst she had seen in her career.

“You bit her with such force that you bit off the top of her finger,” she said. “The photograph shows it was utterly grotesque what you did to her.

“You removed flesh. You bit her down to the bone. Her nail was hanging onto practically nothing.
