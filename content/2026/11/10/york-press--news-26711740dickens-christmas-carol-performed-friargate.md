## One-man Christmas Carol show coming to Friargate Theatre

A one-man performance of Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol is coming to Friargate Theatre next month *(Image: Vintage Verse)*

A one-man performance of A Christmas Carol is coming to Friargate Theatre next month.

It will be held on December 11 and 12, with Mat Jones performing A Christmas Carol, straight from Charles Dickens' own reading text, first performed publicly in 1853.

Unlike modern adaptations of the story, the production preserves Dickens' exact words and structure.

This presents A Christmas Carol as the author himself wrote it to be performed.

There will be no staging, props, or embellishments, just voice, language, and imagination.

The result is a performance that is said to be "intimate, immediate, and unlike anything audiences will have seen before".

Mr Jones is an experienced performer and storyteller specialising in one-man productions that honour classic literature in its original form.

Alongside his performances of A Christmas Carol, now in its ninth year of touring, he has created and performed an authentic solo adaptation of The Wind in the Willows.

The solo performance remains true to Kenneth Grahame's beloved words.

Mr Jones is also currently developing a one-man performance of a Sherlock Holmes story in the same spirit.

He is hoping to bring timeless tales to life through faithful storytelling, rich characterisation, and a deep respect for the authors' original voices.
