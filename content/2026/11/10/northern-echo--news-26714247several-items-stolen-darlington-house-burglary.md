A Darlington home was broken into and items stolen in a burglary in broad daylight.

Police were called to a home on Eastbourne Road just before 3.30pm on Monday (November 9) after reports of a burglary.

Several items were reported stolen during the break-in.

Police and crime scene investigators (CSI) were seen at the property, and it is understood a window or door at the rear of the house had been broken into.

Police want witnesses or anyone who saw somebody acting suspiciously nearby to come forward via durham.police.uk, quoting crime reference CRI00718534.

Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or at www.crimestoppers-uk.org.
