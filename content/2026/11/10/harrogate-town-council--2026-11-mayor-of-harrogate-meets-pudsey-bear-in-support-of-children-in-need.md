The Mayor of Harrogate, Cllr Viv Poskitt met a very special guest last night while supporting Push for Pudsey in aid of BBC Children in Need.

The Mayor was delighted to meet Pudsey Bear and show his support for the campaign, which is helping to raise funds and awareness for Children in Need.

It was great to see the community getting behind the campaign and supporting such a worthwhile cause.

And, of course, there was time to get a photo with Pudsey!
