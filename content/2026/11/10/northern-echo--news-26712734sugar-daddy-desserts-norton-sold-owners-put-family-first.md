A Teesside dessert shop has changed hands months after opening.

Paul Dawson and his partner opened Sugar Daddy Desserts on Norton Road, Stockton in July.

But the pair said the venture became difficult to balance as they juggled their jobs as a barber and nail tech, while working busy evenings in the shop until after 10pm.

The couple, who are now expecting a baby, said they had taken on more staff to cover the hours, which dented profits.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21691358.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sugar Daddy Desserts in Stockton has changed hands *(Image: Sugar Daddy Desserts)*

Announcing the decision to sell, they said: "With great sadness we are officially closing the shop.

"This isn’t a decision we have took (sic) lightly, between both of our mental health and my physical health with the pregnancy complications I’m dealing with, it’s becoming increasingly difficult to continue running the business.

"I want to give a big thank you to our loyal customers for their support and also a huge apology as I know there will be a lot of unhappy people who really enjoy our treats."

The couple had spent nearly £40,000 fitting out the shop.

Paul estimates he is still £20,000 out of pocket, but says he has no regrets and believes the experience was worthwhile.

Sugar Daddy Desserts will now continue to operate under its new owner.
