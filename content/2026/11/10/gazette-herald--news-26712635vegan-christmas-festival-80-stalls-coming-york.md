York is set to host the Vegan Christmas Festival featuring food, talks, and workshops.

The festival will be held at York Railway Institute on Sunday, December 13, 2026, offering a mix of world street food, ethical brands, and community spirit.

Organised by Vegan Events UK, the festival is open to all, with free entry for under-16s accompanied by an adult.

Visitors can explore 80 stalls featuring local and artisan produce, sustainable lifestyle brands, cruelty-free beauty products, and ethical fashion.

The festival is a hub for Christmas shopping, boasting a festive plant-powered atmosphere.

Local stallholders include York’s Soap Out Of The Garden, Drink Me Designs, Dolly’s Vegan Bakery, and Ripon’s Patisserie Gal.

The event also features a range of talks and workshops, including sessions on gut health and hormone balance by Anne Davies, and a vegan dinner party guide by Jessica Goldthorpe.

Cookery demonstrations will be held in the afternoon by the Vegetarian Society.

The festival runs from 10.30am to 4.30pm.

Standard entry is £5, while VIP tickets are £15, including a goody bag of vegan products, samples, discounts, and offers.

Volunteers are sought for the festival, with shifts from 9am to 1pm or 12pm to 4pm, or all day - and will receive free entry and a food voucher.
