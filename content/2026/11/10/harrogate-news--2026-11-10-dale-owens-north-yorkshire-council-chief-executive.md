Dale Owens, currently chief executive of Gateshead Council, has been recommended as the next chief executive of North Yorkshire Council.

The recommendation follows a recruitment process and will be considered by Full Council at its meeting on Wednesday 18 November. The appointment remains subject to statutory procedures and pre-employment checks.

If approved, Mr Owens is expected to take up the role in May 2027, replacing Richard Flinton, who announced his retirement in August after a public service career spanning around 40 years.

Mr Flinton will remain in post until May next year, overseeing the local elections in North Yorkshire in his capacity as returning officer.

## Dale Owens’ career in local government

Mr Owens has a background in local government, particularly in health, adult social care and supported housing. Before joining Gateshead Council, he worked for both North Yorkshire County Council and Hartlepool Council.

He spent around seven years working for North Yorkshire County Council, where his roles included head of accommodation for supported housing and assistant director of health and adult services.

He moved to Gateshead Council in August 2022, initially taking up the role of strategic director of integrated adult social care services. He was subsequently appointed chief executive, with the appointment approved by councillors in May 2024 and taking effect on 1 June that year.

His experience therefore includes responsibility for adult social care services, housing-related provision and wider local authority leadership.

Cllr Carl Les OBE, leader of North Yorkshire Council and chair of the chief officer appointment committee, said the committee was recommending Mr Owens following a thorough and competitive recruitment process.

> **Cllr Carl Les OBE, leader of North Yorkshire Council:**
>
> Following a thorough and highly competitive recruitment process, I am delighted that the committee is recommending Dale as the next chief executive of North Yorkshire Council.
>
> Dale demonstrated outstanding leadership qualities, a clear strategic vision and a strong commitment to public service. We were also impressed by his understanding of the opportunities and challenges facing our county.

Cllr Les said the council faced significant pressures from reduced national funding and increased demand for public services.

He added that the council was confident in Mr Owens’ ability to lead the organisation through its next stage of development.

## New chief executive appointment follows council reorganisation

Richard Flinton led North Yorkshire Council through the reorganisation of local government in April 2023, when eight councils were brought together to form a single unitary authority.

The council says the reorganisation has already delivered more than £69 million in savings.

Responding to the announcement, Mr Owens said he was honoured to be recommended for the appointment and described North Yorkshire as a special place, with ambitious communities and strong partnerships.

> **Dale Owens:**
>
> North Yorkshire Council has made a sound start as a unitary authority thanks to the dedication and focus of its workforce and elected members.
>
> Subject to approval, I am looking forward to working with councillors, colleagues, partners and residents to build on these achievements and help deliver the best possible outcomes for communities across the county.
>
> I recognise the strong foundations established under Richard Flinton’s leadership and would be proud to lead the organisation into its next chapter.

North Yorkshire Council is England’s largest unitary authority by geographical area and the third largest by population.

The recommendation will be considered by Full Council on Wednesday 18 November 2026. If approved and all appointment requirements are completed, Mr Owens is expected to begin the role in May 2027.
