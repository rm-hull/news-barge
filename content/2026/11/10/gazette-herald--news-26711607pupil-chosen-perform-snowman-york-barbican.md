A young pupil has earned a starring role in a national tour coming to York.

Charlotte, a student at Duchy College Prep in Harrogate, will perform Walking In The Air at York Barbican as part of The Snowman Tour, which features a screening of the classic 1982 film accompanied by a live orchestra.

Charlotte has taken on leading roles in school nativities and regularly participates in Duchy College Prep’s performing arts events.

Charlotte said: "When I found out that I’d got the part, I was really, really shocked.

"I thought I’d just give it a go for the experience, so finding out I’d been successful was amazing.

"This school is amazing for opportunities.

"There’s always a chance to do something, and if you get the opportunity to do something big, the school will always support you.

"That support makes you feel more confident and willing to give things a go."

James Savile, head teacher of Duchy College Prep, added: "This is an incredible achievement.

"Charlotte is an outstanding member of our community, and we are exceptionally proud of her."

The Snowman Tour will take place in 13 other venues across the country, with soloists performing in venues in Birmingham, Liverpool and Durham Cathedral.
