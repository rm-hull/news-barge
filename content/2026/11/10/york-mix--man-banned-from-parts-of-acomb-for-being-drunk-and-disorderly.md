A man has been banned from parts of Acomb and prohibited from drinking alcohol in public in York.

Stuart Tether, 44, of Tudor Road, appeared in court following repeated incidents of antisocial behaviour and being drunk and disorderly.

His behaviour, along with that of other adults gathering on Front Street in Acomb, prompted North Yorkshire Police to launch an operation to tackle persistent troublemakers in the area.

Despite increased police patrols and offers of support from partner agencies, Tether’s poor behaviour continued.

In response, the York Community Safety Hub, comprising police, City of York Council and partner organisations, successfully applied for a criminal behaviour order at York Magistrates’ Court on 28 October.

The order lasts for five years and prohibits Tether from:

* Having an open container of alcohol in any public place within the area bounded by York Outer Ring Road, unless on licensed premises
* Being in charge of, walking or otherwise controlling a dog while under the influence of alcohol or drugs
* Entering Heron Foods on Front Street, Acomb, or the Front Street/Green Lane area covered by the exclusion zone on the below map.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/stuart-tether-banning-order-map-nyp.jpg)

Breaching the order could lead to criminal proceedings, imprisonment and/or a fine.

Sergeant Charlotte Gregory of North Yorkshire Police said: “Stuart Tether’s behaviour had a significant impact on local people and businesses.

“Despite being offered support, he continued to engage in antisocial behaviour that affected the community’s quality of life.

“He was initially issued with a Community Protection Notice, but breached its conditions on numerous occasions, leading to the court granting a Criminal Behaviour Order.

“We will take robust action where any breaches of the order are reported and evidenced. Civil orders such as these are an important tool in tackling antisocial behaviour and protecting our communities.”
