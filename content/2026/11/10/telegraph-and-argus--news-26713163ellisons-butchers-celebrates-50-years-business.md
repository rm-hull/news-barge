A butchers is celebrating 50 years in business with a week of fundraising and community events.

Ellison’s Butchers, of Cullingworth, is marking the milestone with sausage-themed fundraising and a special pork pie event as part of the celebrations.

The events, taking place from November 16 to 22, aim to thank customers, staff and suppliers who have supported the family-run business over the years.

John Ellison said: "Reaching 50 years is something we're very proud of.

"We couldn't have done it without our loyal customers, our hardworking staff and the support of the local community."

Throughout the week, the business will also be raising money for Manorlands.

Mr Ellison said: "Some families have shopped with us for generations, and that means a great deal.

"We wanted to celebrate by bringing everyone together and supporting a local charity that means so much to so many people."
