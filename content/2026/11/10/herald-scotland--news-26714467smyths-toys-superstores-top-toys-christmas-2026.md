Christmas magic has arrived at Smyths Toys Superstores, with its Top Toys for Christmas 2026 offering plenty of inspiration for festive wish lists. This year’s selection brings together much-loved characters, interactive toys, creative play and family favourites, with something to excite children of all ages.

Featuring some of the biggest names in toys and entertainment, including Pokémon, Disney Toy Story, PAW Patrol, LEGO, Barbie and more, the 2026 collection is packed with gift ideas to make Christmas morning extra special.

From imaginative and creative play to interactive toys, games and action-packed fun, there’s plenty to help parents, grandparents and gift-givers find the perfect present.

For more details on these wonderful toys and many more, pick up your FREE 300 page Smyths Toys Superstores catalogue in-store!
