Three men have been charged with theft following an alleged incident at Sainsbury’s on Wetherby Road in Harrogate, involving goods valued at almost £1,500.

North Yorkshire Police said officers were called to the store shortly before 2pm on Monday 9 November after staff reported a suspected high-value theft.

Three men had been seen acting suspiciously in the alcohol aisle before attempting to leave the store without paying for the goods.

Two of the men were stopped by Sainsbury’s staff, while the third got away after becoming aggressive towards employees.

Car keys were found on one of the men. Officers subsequently located the vehicle in a nearby car park and found the outstanding man asleep inside.

A 25-year-old man, a 36-year-old man and a 56-year-old man, all from the Nottingham area, were arrested on suspicion of theft.

All three have since been charged with theft and remanded in custody. They were due to appear in court on Tuesday 10 November.

The police reference number is 12260220506.
