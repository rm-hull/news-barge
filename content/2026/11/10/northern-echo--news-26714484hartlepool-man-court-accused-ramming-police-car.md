A man has appeared in court accused of ramming a police car before driving the wrong way along a dual carriageway.

Rhys Robinson, 20, of Kipling Road, Hartlepool, was arrested and charged with dangerous driving, driving while disqualified, having no insurance and possessing a knife or bladed article in public.

He appeared at Teesside Magistrates' Court on Monday, November 9, and was remanded in custody until his next hearing at Teesside Crown Court in December.

It follows an incident in the Galsworthy Road area of Hartlepool at around 1.45am on Sunday, November 8,

Officers reported pursuing a Citroen C3, travelling the wrong way along the A689. The allege it went off-road and drove head-on towards members of the public on a dual carriageway.

The force says vehicle reportedly rammed a police car.

Superintendent Emily Harrison said: “We recognise that this incident would have been extremely frightening for any motorists who encountered it and we want to reassure the public that swift and decisive action was taken by officers to bring the incident to a safe conclusion and to secure an arrest, ensuring no ongoing risk to the community.”

Anyone with information or dashcam footage that could help the investigation should contact Cleveland Police on 101, quoting incident reference 223595.

Footage can also be submitted directly online through the force's appeal page.
