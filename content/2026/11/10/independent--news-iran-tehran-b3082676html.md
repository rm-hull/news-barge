Women carrying assault rifles scaled down a wall and rode a zip line as Iran opened a military training camp for women volunteers in Tehran’s Azadi Square on Tuesday. Authorities say the camp aims to prepare volunteers to defend against a possible U.S. ground assault. It is part of a nationwide campaign that includes military training, emergency relief and logistical support.
