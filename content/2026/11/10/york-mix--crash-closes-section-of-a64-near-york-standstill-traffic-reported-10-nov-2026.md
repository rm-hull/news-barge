A crash is causing problems for drivers on the A64 near York today (Tuesday).

It’s on the eastbound carriageway near Bilbrough Top.

The AA reports: “Severe delays of 16 minutes and delays increasing on A64 Eastbound In North Yorkshire. Average speed five mph.”

#### Crash location

Drivers in the area are reporting standstill traffic.
