Harrogate-based interior design business Roomsmiths has been shortlisted for a Northern Design Award 2026 for its work on a basement bar in London’s West End.

The business is a finalist in the Commercial Awards Restaurant & Bar Design category for BOX BELOW, a venue designed for Leeds-based hospitality group Arc Inspirations.

The winners will be announced at the Northern Design Awards ceremony on Thursday 12 November, with the event taking place at Chester Cathedral following a drinks reception at Chester Town Hall.

Roomsmiths said the BOX BELOW project involved transforming the structural constraints of a central London basement into a sports bar designed for high-volume hospitality.

## Roomsmiths’ relationship with Arc Inspirations

Roomsmiths has a longstanding working relationship with Leeds-based Arc Inspirations. The Harrogate business has also worked with Simon Mideley on the extension and design of Starling Bar in Harrogate.

The relationship with Arc Inspirations provides a local connection to the award-nominated project in London, with Roomsmiths continuing to work with the hospitality group on its venues.

## Recognition at the Northern Design Awards

> **Cheryl Duerden, Director at Roomsmiths:**
>
> Being named a finalist in the Northern Design Awards is a wonderful recognition of the creativity and hard work that went into BOX BELOW.

> **Jen Danks, Director at Roomsmiths:**
>
> We thank our clients Arc Inspirations for their concise and clear brief, collaborating with such a visionary client made designing this space an absolute pleasure, and we’re looking forward to celebrating alongside so much design talent in Chester this Thursday.

The Northern Design Awards recognise work across residential and commercial design, with categories covering interiors, architecture, restoration, hospitality, kitchens, bathrooms and bespoke design.

## Interior design business based in Harrogate

Roomsmiths is based on Albert Street in Harrogate and provides interior design services for hospitality venues and residential properties.

The business says it has 20 years of experience in interior design, offering a service from initial concepts through to the completion of projects.

The Northern Design Awards ceremony takes place on Thursday 12 November, when the winners will be announced.
