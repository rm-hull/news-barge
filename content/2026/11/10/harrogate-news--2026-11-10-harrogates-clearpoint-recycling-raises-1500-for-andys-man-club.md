Harrogate-based waste management broker Clearpoint Recycling has raised £1,500 for men’s mental health charity Andy’s Man Club, following a fundraising challenge involving 27 employees.

The company, which has named Andy’s Man Club as its Charity of the Year, organised a challenge called ‘Miles to Vilnius’, encouraging colleagues to virtually travel from its Harrogate headquarters to its office in Lithuania.

Employees logged miles through walking, running and other physical activities. A total of 27 colleagues recorded 252 activities, covering 1,601 miles – around 70 miles further than the original target.

The money raised will support Andy’s Man Club, which provides free peer-to-peer support groups for men across the UK and online. The groups offer a safe, non-judgemental environment where men can talk openly about their mental health and experiences.

## Supporting men’s mental health

Clearpoint Recycling said the fundraising reflects its commitment to supporting men’s mental health both in the wider community and within its own workplace.

> **Mark Garrett, managing director at Clearpoint Recycling:**
>
> We are proud to have Andy’s Man Club as our Charity of the Year. Men’s mental health is something we take seriously, and we want to help challenge the stigma that can prevent men from talking about how they are feeling.
>
> That commitment starts in our own workplace. It is important that our colleagues know they can speak openly, that support is available and that looking after our mental wellbeing is just as important as looking after our physical health.
>
> The team, led by our customer services manager, Laurie O’Connor did a fantastic job raising £1,500 for the charity. We hope the money will help Andy’s Man Club continue its vital work, while also reinforcing the importance of having open conversations about men’s mental health.

Joseph Doyle, area lead for Leeds and North Yorkshire at Andy’s Man Club, thanked the employees for their efforts.

> **Joseph Doyle, area lead Leeds and North Yorkshire at Andy’s Man Club:**
>
> We are extremely grateful for the effort and commitment the team has shown to raise money for the charity.
>
> The funds raised will help us to continue our support for men, help remove the stigma surrounding men’s mental health and provide safe and supportive environments for men to talk openly to one another.

Andy’s Man Club was founded in Halifax and has developed a network of peer-support groups across the UK, alongside online support.

Clearpoint Recycling was founded in 2012 and specialises in the sourcing and sale of recyclable materials, connecting suppliers and buyers internationally. The company employs 25 people and is based in Harrogate.

More information about Andy’s Man Club is available at andysmanclub.co.uk.
