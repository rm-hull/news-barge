A Harrogate healthcare clinic has introduced a saliva-based test for women with suspected endometriosis, offering results within 25 days without the need for surgery or invasive sample collection.

Cocoon Healthcare, based on East Park Road in Harrogate, is offering the EndoTest service at a cost of £1,100. The clinic says the test has an accuracy rate of 97.5%, although results need to be considered alongside a woman’s symptoms, medical history and appropriate clinical assessment.

The service is aimed at women who may have been experiencing symptoms for some time without receiving a diagnosis.

## Long waits for an endometriosis diagnosis

Endometriosis is a condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the womb grows elsewhere in the body. Symptoms can include severe pelvic pain, heavy periods, chronic fatigue and difficulties with fertility.

The condition affects approximately one in ten women and those assigned female at birth of reproductive age in the UK, according to Endometriosis UK.

Research published by the charity in March 2026 found that the average time between a woman first consulting her GP about symptoms and receiving an endometriosis diagnosis was nine years and four months.

Separate research involving Cysters found that respondents from ethnically diverse communities waited an average of 11 years for a diagnosis.

During that time, women can face repeated medical appointments and investigations while dealing with symptoms that may affect their work, relationships and everyday lives.

## How the saliva test works

EndoTest involves an appointment at Cocoon Healthcare’s Harrogate clinic, where a saliva sample is collected and sent for specialist analysis.

The clinic says results are available within 25 days and reports an accuracy rate of 97.5%. The £1,100 fee is approximately £100 less than comparable providers, according to Cocoon Healthcare.

Consultant gynaecologist and endometriosis specialist James Tibbott has joined the clinic to oversee results and provide specialist clinical input when women are considering their findings and possible next steps.

The clinic emphasises that receiving a test result within 25 days does not mean a confirmed diagnosis is guaranteed within that period. Results should be interpreted alongside symptoms, medical history and appropriate clinical assessment.

## Cocoon Healthcare expands women’s health services

Cocoon Healthcare was established in 2024 by founder Sam Naughton, following her own experiences of baby loss and pregnancy-related anxiety.

She said those experiences had influenced her decision to create a healthcare environment where women felt listened to, supported and taken seriously.
