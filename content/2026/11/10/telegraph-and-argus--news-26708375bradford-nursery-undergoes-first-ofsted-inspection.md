A Bradford nursery has been praised for its nurturing environment and ambitious curriculum following its first Ofsted inspection.

Raising Explorers Day Nursery, based in Manningham, provides places for children aged between one and five years old.

The nursery was found to be meeting the expected standard across all areas during an inspection carried out in July.

Inspectors also confirmed that safeguarding arrangements were effective.

The nursery, which opened in 2025, received positive feedback for the way children are supported to settle into the setting and develop confidence in their learning.

The report said: "Children are supported to quickly settle with their key person, showing they feel happy, safe and secure in this inclusive and nurturing setting."

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21683004.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raising Explorers Day Nursery, based on Carlisle Road in Manningham. *(Image: Google Maps)*

## Ambitious curriculum praised

Ofsted highlighted the nursery's approach to learning and said leaders had created a clear vision for children's education.

Inspectors said: "Leaders have a clear and ambitious vision for the curriculum."

The report found that staff support children's physical development, communication skills and independence through a range of activities designed to help them explore and learn.

Children were observed behaving well, following routines and building positive relationships with staff and their peers.

Inspectors also praised the nursery's support for children with special educational needs and disabilities.

## Areas identified for improvement

While the report was largely positive, inspectors also highlighted areas where the nursery could improve.

Ofsted found that staff did not always consistently support children's language and mathematics development during activities.

The report said staff did not regularly model a wide range of vocabulary or consistently build mathematical concepts into everyday learning opportunities.

Inspectors recommended that leaders provide further support to ensure all staff deliver the curriculum consistently and help children make the strongest possible progress, particularly in language and maths.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21685924.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Raising Explorers Nursery's outdoor learning and play space. *(Image: Raising Explorers)*

## Nursery celebrates milestone

The nursery described the outcome as a significant achievement following its first inspection.

A nursery manager at Raising Explorers said: "We're incredibly proud to have successfully completed our first Ofsted inspection.

"Our aim has always been to create a nursery where children feel happy, safe and excited to learn. It's lovely to see Ofsted recognise the nurturing environment our team works so hard to provide.

"This is a special achievement for everyone involved, and we're looking forward to continuing to grow and build on this success."

The nursery forms part of the wider Raising Explorers group, which also operates sites in Listerhills, Thornbury, Leeds and Rochdale.
