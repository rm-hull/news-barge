A charity supporting struggling families in the North East has received a £1,500 grant to provide essential equipment for 167 babies and young children living in poverty.

The Sunderland-based charity, Love, Amelia, will use the funding from EMG Solicitors’ Charitable Fund to support its Safe Starts Project, delivering vital items such as cot beds, Moses baskets, baby baths, changing mats, and toiletry packs to 167 children aged up to four.

Steph Capewell, who founded Love, Amelia in 2019 in memory of her daughter, Amelia, who died shortly after birth in 2018, said: "We don’t want any child to go without and that is what drives us to do what we do.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21686946.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The grant from EMG Solicitors’ Charitable Fund will help Love, Amelia provide essential equipment to 167 children aged up to four *(Image: Supplied)*

"We see the positive impact this charity has on families in the area and that is at the heart of everything we do.

"Every year, we seem to be getting busier and busier.

"This all started with me handing out 12 boxes and to date we’ve supported over 42,000 people."

Ms Capewell said: "The money from EMG will go towards helping us buy and distribute essential equipment to babies and young children who are living in poverty."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21686947.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The grant from EMG Solicitors’ Charitable Fund will help Love, Amelia provide essential equipment to 167 children aged up to four *(Image: Supplied)*

Love, Amelia has supported more than 42,000 people across the North East since it began, providing pre-loved clothes, toys, nappies, and other essentials.

Families are referred to the charity by health visitors, midwives, and social workers to ensure support reaches those most in need.

The charity's wider work includes improving children's safety, health, and wellbeing; reducing parental stress; and preventing issues from escalating.

Claire Hewitt, who leads EMG’s Social Impact Team, said: "It’s clear to see the great work Love, Amelia is doing for children and families in the local area.

"From its Safe Starts Project to its back-to-school backpack collections and Christmas campaigns, the charity provides practical help to families when they need it most.

"Supporting local charities and grassroots organisations is a really important part of EMG’s commitment to making a positive difference in our communities.

"We hope this grant will help Love, Amelia continue its vital work and make a real difference to babies and young children who might otherwise go without essential items during those crucial early years."

In addition to its Safe Starts Project, Love, Amelia also runs a back-to-school scheme providing children with school bags filled with notepads, pens, pencils, and water bottles.

During the festive season, the charity creates Christmas Eve boxes for families, packed with treats such as biscuits, hot chocolate, and reindeer food.

The charity relies on donations of high-quality pre-loved items, which are then distributed to families in need.

EMG Solicitors established its Charitable Fund in 2019 with Point North.

Since then, the fund has supported more than 50 charities and grassroots projects, awarding over £300,000 in grants, donations, and sponsorships.
