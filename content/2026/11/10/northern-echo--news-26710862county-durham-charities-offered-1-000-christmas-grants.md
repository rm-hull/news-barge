Charities across County Durham could receive a festive £1,000 funding boost as part of a £240,000 Christmas giveaway.

The funds are being distributed through the Benefact Group’s Movement for Good Awards, part of the annual 12 Days of Giving campaign.

Running in the lead-up to Christmas, the scheme will see 20 charities selected each day to receive £1,000, with 240 charities set to benefit in total.

Mark Hews, group chief executive of Benefact Group, said: "We’re delighted to be launching our 12 Days of Giving campaign again this year, giving away £240,000 in donations to causes nationwide.

"We know how much charities need support, particularly during the festive season, so we’re asking the people of County Durham to take a few minutes to nominate a cause that is particularly close to their hearts.

"We know that £1,000 can have a huge impact on incredible work that charities do and we’re looking forward to seeing how this financial boost will change lives for the better."

Nominations are now open, and County Durham residents are being encouraged to nominate a cause they care about at www.movementforgood.com.

Last year, County Durham charities including the If U Care Share Foundation in Chester-le-Street and Northumbria Blood Bikes in Durham each received £1,000 through the programme.

Winners are selected at random, with all nominated charities in with a chance of receiving the donation.

The Benefact Group has donated more than £250 million to charities since 2014 and is the UK’s third-largest corporate donor over the past decade.

The scheme is funded by EIO plc, part of the Benefact Group.
