FIREFIGHTERS have been called in after a tumble dryer fire in North Yorkshire.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said its crew from Tadcaster were called to Station Road in Church Fenton at 5.12pm yesterday afternoon (November 9) after smoke started coming out of a tumble dryer.
