A DRUNK woman has attacked people outside a York city centre shop.

North Yorkshire Police is appealing for information after an ‘incident’ that occurred outside Tesco Express on Piccadilly in York at the end of last month.

It happened at around 7pm on Friday, October 30 and involved members of the public trying to help a drunk woman who lost her friends and had no charge left on her phone.
