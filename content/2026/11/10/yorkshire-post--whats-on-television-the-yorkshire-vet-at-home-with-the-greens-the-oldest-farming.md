# The Yorkshire Vet: At Home with the Greens: The oldest farming couple in Britain return to screens as Steve Green is inspired to write a love poem for Jean

## The oldest farming couple in Britain return to Channel 5’s The Yorkshire Vet: At Home with the Greens - and Steve Green is inspired to write a poem for his wife Jean.

The Yorkshire Vet spin off series ‘At Home with the Greens’ follows the farming life of Jean and Steve Green who have been working on their farm since they were married 45 years ago.

They are considered the oldest farming couple in Britain with a collective age of 170 years and they renewed their vows in March 2019 on the farm surrounded by their family and friends.

At Home with the Greens returns to screens this year for its third series and the couple have no intention of slowing down, as their work on the farm continues.

![Rob and Dave visit the Greens at their farm for a Whitby on the farm celebration for Steve's birthday.](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/10/12/43/NLYP-10-11-2026-Rob-and-Dave-visit-the-Greens-at-their-farm-for-a-Whitby-on-the-farm-celebration-for.jpg?trim=120,55,122,58&width=640&quality=65)


However, during the first episode of the series, the warning signs are there when Jean has to go to hospital for a gallbladder operation.

As Steve struggles on alone, good friend Peter Wright steps in to lend a hand with the farm work before turning his attention to another poorly patient - the Greens’ beloved 13-year-old greyhound cross, Reuben, who needs serious dental treatment.

Peter also rekindles Steve’s love of writing and poetry at a special event in Thirsk, inspiring him to pen a love poem for Jean.

Once Jean is finally up on her feet and back home but unable to travel, Peter hatches a grand plan: if the Greens can’t go to Whitby, Whitby must come to the Greens.

With the help of Matt, Rohin and Cannon Hall Farm’s Rob and Dave, he turns Stoneybrough Farm into Stoneybrough-on-Sea.

The news of the show returning for another series, which was posted on The Yorkshire Vet’s Facebook page, has provoked a positive reaction from viewers. The post got more than 7,000 reactions and 248 comments.

The new series of The Yorkshire Vet: At Home with the Greens starts on Thursday, November 19, 2026, at 8pm on Channel 5.
