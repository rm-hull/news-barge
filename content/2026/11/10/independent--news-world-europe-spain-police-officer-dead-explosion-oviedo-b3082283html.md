A Spanish police officer was killed and another was seriously injured after a backpack containing an explosive device detonated as they responded to a shooting, local authorities said.

The officers had been responding to a violent dispute between two families on Tuesday, during which a young woman was shot and injured by a man on the outskirts ‌of Oviedo at around 7:30am, local authorities said.

The device exploded at around 8:30am, killing a member of the National Police’s canine unit, and leaving an officer of the bomb disposal unit in a very serious condition after losing a leg, police union ‌JUPOL said in a statement.

The union said that upon ‌arriving at the scene of ​the shooting on Rafael Urrusti Street outskirts ‌of Oviedo, police ⁠found a backpack at the building's ⁠entrance containing the explosive device.

It detonated as ‌officers took ​it away from ‌the building to ​defuse it.

The area has now been cordoned off as National Police deploy a large operation, according to Spanish outlet *El Pais*.

The *EFE* news agency reports that Alfredo Canteli, the mayor of Oviedo, is heading now to the scene to gather information on what happened.
