Clive Owen became one of Yorkshire's best-known farmers through Our Yorkshire Farm, appearing alongside Amanda Owen and their nine children.

But long before the family's television success, Clive had already been married and was raising a family of his own.

The farmer, who turned 70 in 2026, was divorced and had two children when he first met Amanda in 1996.

It means Clive is the father of 11 children in total, although his two older children have largely remained outside the public spotlight associated with the family's television programmes.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21670603.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Clive Owen *(Image: CHANNEL 4)*

Clive's early life was very different from the celebrity profile he would eventually develop.

Before settling at Ravenseat, he had farmed at Stainmore.

He moved to Ravenseat in 1989, attracted by its location in the heart of Swaledale and its association with the traditional Swaledale sheep breed.

Seven years later, his life changed when a young contract shepherd arrived at his remote farmhouse.

Amanda, then 21, had been sent to collect a ram.

The encounter marked the beginning of their relationship, with the couple later marrying and having nine children together.

Their family life became the focus of Our Yorkshire Farm, which launched on Channel 5 in 2018.

Viewers watched as Raven, Reuben, Miles, Edith, Violet, Sidney, Annas, Clementine and Nancy grew up surrounded by the demands of a working hill farm.

Clive and Amanda separated in 2022 after 22 years of marriage, but continued working together and raising their children.

They subsequently returned to television in Our Farm Next Door: Amanda, Clive and Kids, documenting the restoration of historic farmhouse Anty John's.

While the programme has made Clive's younger children familiar faces, considerably less is publicly established about the lives of his two children from his first marriage.

The family has largely kept those relationships outside the programme, and there is no basis for claiming a particular career, relationship or current location for his eldest daughter without further confirmation.

Clive's earlier marriage remains a lesser-known part of the history of the farmer whose life at Ravenseat eventually became a television phenomenon.
