**Now found safe and well**

North Yorkshire Police has launched an urgent search for a 73-year-old woman who has gone missing in Harrogate.

Carole Watson was last seen in the Verity Walk area of the town at approximately 12.45pm on Tuesday 10 November 2026.

She is described as being around 5ft 3ins tall, with brown bobbed hair containing some grey.

Carole may be wearing a dark purple puffer jacket and possibly slippers. She may also have her handbag with her.

Police say they are very concerned for her welfare and are appealing to the public for help in locating her.

## Police appeal for information

Anyone who sees Carole is asked to call 999 immediately.

Anyone with other information that could help the search is asked to contact North Yorkshire Police on 101.

When sharing information, quote reference number **12260221413**.
