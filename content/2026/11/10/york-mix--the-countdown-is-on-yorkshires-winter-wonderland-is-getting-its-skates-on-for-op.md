Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland is getting its finishing touches, as the festive attraction counts down to opening night.

With three days to go, the event is almost ready to welcome visitors, with a few finishing touches taking place around the site – from stacking up logs for the marshmallow firepits, to hanging alpine-themed curtains in the rustic rinkside Chalet, to stacking skates in the skate-hire marquee.

The north of England’s largest ice rink is ready to welcome skaters and non-skaters alike, as Bobo and Buddy have already been for a test-skate!

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Buddy-and-Bobo-1.jpg)

Winter’s first snowfall has also been reported at York Designer Outlet! Once again Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland has been sprinkled with festive magic, with snow dusting the hundreds of Christmas trees, and a colourful light display illuminating the woodland backdrop and glistening surface of the ice rink.

The award-winning Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland has welcomed over one million skaters since its debut in York two decades ago, and opens for its 21st season on 13 November, with a VIP Preview Evening on Thursday.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Snowfall-at-Yorkshires-Winter-Wonderland.jpg)

Invited guests will enjoy a special skating performance before taking to the ice themselves, skating under the stars in a stunning illuminated woodland setting. The funfair will be offering thrills galore for all ages, and inside the re-designed alpine Chalet guests can enjoy festive refreshments from the bar, while cosying up on sheepskins by the fireplace.

Producer James Cundall says the new team behind the event this year has been outstanding. “Everything has gone really well and we’re actually ready well ahead of schedule, with just the last few festive sprinkles to be done here and there!

“The ice is looking pristine and we’re all very excited to unveil this year’s new attractions – an amazing light show as a backdrop to the rink and the Chalet’s cute alpine makeover which looks wonderfully authentic and Swiss.

“We now have a performance stage in front of a fireplace for live music nights during the season which along with special lighting will bring a lovely après-ski, après-skate vibe to evenings in the Chalet. And outside we have two marshmallow firepits with real fire for that cosy festive experience with wafts of woodsmoke.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/chalet-fireplace.jpg)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/chalet-exterior.jpg)

There’s free parking at York Designer Outlet, so visitors can enjoy a full day of festive activities and of course Christmas shopping at more than 100 designer and premium brands, with savings of up to 60% off the recommended retail price.

Yorkshire’s Winter Wonderland will open from **Friday 13 November 2026 until Sunday 3 January 2027**. Tickets are now on sale here.
