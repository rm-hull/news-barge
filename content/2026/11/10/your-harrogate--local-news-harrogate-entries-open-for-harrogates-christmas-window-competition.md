Harrogate town centre businesses are being invited to enter this year’s Christmas window competition.

Harrogate BID has teamed up with the Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce for the annual competition, which has four categories – shops, pubs and restaurants, hotels, and professional services.

Judges will visit the displays from 4pm on Thursday 3rd December and assess each window on its illumination, innovation and spirit of Christmas.

The winners will be announced at an awards evening on Thursday 10th December.

Alongside the four category winners, judges will name an overall champion and present a number of special awards.

Bethany Allen, Operations Manager at Harrogate BID, said:

> “The Christmas Window Competition has become a much-loved part of Harrogate's festive celebrations, and we're delighted to launch it once again for 2026.
>
> “Every year we're incredibly impressed by the creativity and effort that local businesses put into their displays. The competition not only helps create a magical atmosphere throughout the town centre, but also encourages residents and visitors to explore Harrogate and support local businesses during the festive season.
>
> “We're pleased to be partnering with the Harrogate District Chamber of Commerce this year and look forward to seeing businesses across all sectors bring festive cheer to the town.”

*Businesses can enter by completing the online form on Harrogate BID’s website.*

*For more information, email [info@harrogatebid.co.uk.](mailto:info@harrogatebid.co.uk.)*
