A 19-year-old man from York is recovering in hospital after being shot during a mass shooting in Krakow, Poland, which left six people dead and 15 others injured.

Harry Marchant suffered a gunshot wound during the attack in Krakow’s Main Market Square on Saturday 7 November. His family has confirmed that he is now in a stable condition and receiving ongoing hospital treatment.

North Yorkshire Police said it is assisting Harry’s family following the incident.

## Six people killed in Krakow shooting

The shooting happened shortly after 3pm on Saturday, when a 24-year-old man drove a car onto Krakow’s Main Market Square and opened fire on people nearby.

According to an account published by Krakow’s city authorities, the gunman fired at random pedestrians. Six people were killed and 15 others were injured.

The suspect was shot during a police intervention and taken to hospital in a critical condition. Polish police confirmed that he died in hospital in the early hours of Monday 9 November.

The victims included four women and two men. Those injured were taken to hospitals across Krakow, with two police officers among the wounded.

Polish police have since provided further details of the investigation. Officers said the suspect was a 24-year-old man from Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski. Three people were also found dead at a property connected to him in that town.

Poland’s National Prosecutor’s Office has opened an investigation into the Krakow killings and attempted murders, treating the attack as an act of a terrorist nature. A specialist police team has been established to examine the circumstances and the suspect’s possible motives.

## Harry Marchant’s family issue update

Harry’s family has released a statement confirming that he is in a stable condition and thanking those who came to his assistance.

> **Harry Marchant’s family:**
>
> We would like to confirm that our son, Harry Marchant, was injured during the incident in Poland over the weekend. We are pleased to say he is now in a stable condition, receiving ongoing hospital treatment.
>
> We can’t express how grateful we are to the first responders, medical team, and the people of Poland for their help and care for Harry.
>
> We ask for our privacy to be respected as we support Harry with his recovery.

North Yorkshire Police is assisting the family. No further details have been released about the circumstances in which Harry was injured.
