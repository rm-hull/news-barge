BRADFORD City have already lost Graham Alexander and Chris Lucketti to Preston.

And now a key member of their backroom staff has been lured to Deepdale as well.

City’s head of analysis Scott Dyer has left Valley Parade after six years to take up the same role with North End.

Speaking to the Preston website, Alexander said: “Scott worked closely with us at our previous club and played a significant role in our success there.

“He’ll work exceptionally hard to support the players and provide everything we need from an analysis perspective.

“We’re delighted to bring him in.”
