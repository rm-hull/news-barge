An award-winning County Durham restaurant renowned for its South Asian cuisine is celebrating after being named the best in the region.

Multi-award-winning Babul’s, which has restaurants in Darlington and Barnard Castle and is preparing to open a new venue in Durham, has won Regional Restaurant of the Year at the North East Bangladeshi Awards.

The much-loved restaurant shared the news with customers on social media following the awards, saying the accolade meant “everything” to its team.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689259.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Babul's won the regional award *(Image: Sozo Marketing Ltd)*

A spokesperson said: “Babul’s has won Regional Restaurant of the Year at the North East Bangladeshi Awards and we are absolutely over the moon.

“We are honestly still taking it in. Thank you to every single person who has supported us, eaten with us and cheered us on.

“This one is for you.

“We could not do any of this without our incredible team and our amazing community, and this means everything to us. Thank you.”

The North East Bangladeshi Awards were launched in May 2022 to recognise the achievements and contributions of British Bangladeshi individuals, communities and businesses.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689260.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Babul's won the regional award *(Image: Sozo Marketing Ltd)*

The awards aim to celebrate the role British Bangladeshis play in shaping towns and cities across the region, while encouraging people to take pride in their identity, culture and roots.

Owner Shuhel Ahmed said: "Winning Restaurant of the Year at the North East Bangladeshi Awards for the third year running is an incredible honour for all of us. NEBA plays a vital role in celebrating the talent, hard work, and rich heritage of our community across the region, so being recognised on this stage means everything.

"This award is a testament to the dedication of our unbelievable staff, who bring their best every single shift, and to our amazing customers who continue to support us day in, day out. This win belongs to every single one of them."

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689259.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Babul's won the regional award *(Image: Sozo Marketing Ltd)*

Khloe Connor, Babul’s Manager, added: "We've just won the NEBA award again, and as a team, we're all collectively absolutely buzzing. We've got a lot of new exciting things coming - new sites opening, new ventures launching - and we're all really excited to share it with you guys. Onwards and upwards for Babul's.”

While Babul’s has not yet announced an official opening date, customers can make bookings from November 27 onwards, including for the busy festive period.

News of the award was met with congratulations from customers, who praised the restaurant’s food and staff.

One person said: “Excellent. Still waiting for my curry in a Yorkshire pudding to become a reality. You all totally deserve the win.”

Another added: “Very well deserved, food and cocktails are always outstanding, and the staff are simply the best.”
