BBC Look North presenter Amy Garcia and Lindsey Burrow began their Push for Pudsey challenge in the Harrogate district on Monday (November 9), setting off with the famous red sofa on their fundraising journey.

![We take a look at 15 fantastic photos as Amy Garcia and Lindsey Burrow bring the Push for Pudsey challenge to Harrogate district](https://www.connachttribune.ie/jpim-static/image/2026/11/10/12/58/PUDSEY.jpeg?trim=0,0,0,0&crop=&width=640&quality=70&enable=upscale)


The pair began their route in Ripon, the pair travelled through Bishop Monkton and Burton Leonard before arriving in Ripley, where they were greeted by crowds of well-wishers and pupils from Ripley Endowed Church of England Primary School.

Supporters gathered to cheer them on, take photographs and show their support for the fundraising challenge.

After leaving Ripley, Amy and Lindsey continued their route through Harrogate, Starbeck and Knaresborough, where more supporters gathered to show their support for the Push for Pudsey challenge.

You can follow Amy and Lindsey’s progress using the live tracker, find out more about the challenge and donate via the BBC Children in Need website.
