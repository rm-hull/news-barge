A BRADFORD primary school is inviting families to explore its Early Years provision.

Thornbury Primary Leadership Academy, located on Dick Lane, will host a Reception Open Day on November 18 from 3.30pm to 5pm.

The event will offer families the chance to meet pupils and staff, tour the school, and learn about the curriculum and enrichment activities available to children starting their school journey.

A spokesperson for the school said: "Visitors can also look around the school’s modern learning and leisure facilities and get a feel for the welcoming school community."

The academy runs an 80-place nursery for children aged three and four, and its Early Years provision was rated 'Outstanding' by Ofsted.

Part of the Star Academies network, Thornbury Primary benefits from the support and expertise of one of the UK’s leading education providers.

The admissions deadline for Reception places in September 2027 is January 15, 2027.

For more information, contact [info@thornbury.staracademies.org](mailto:info@thornbury.staracademies.org) or call 01274 665 812.
