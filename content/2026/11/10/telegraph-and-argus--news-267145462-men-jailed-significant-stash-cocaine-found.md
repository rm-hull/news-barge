Two men have been jailed after police found a "significant stash" of cocaine during dawn raids.

Bradford men Alfie Leahy, 21, and Connor Broadbent, 26, have been jailed for a total of over eight years.

## Drugs worth £22,000 found

Following warrants executed just after 5am on July 17, officers found a large amount of cash, gold jewellery, multiple vehicles and a significant stash of cocaine at an address on Dawson Street in Dudley Hill.

Cannabis, a baton and a replica firearm were seized at an address on Grayswood Crescent in Holme Wood.

Drug paraphernalia, knives, and multiple mobile phones were also found at both addresses.

The total street value of the drugs was estimated at over £22,000.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21694276.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Items seized during the raids *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

## How long was each men jailed for?

Both men were sentenced at Bradford Crown Court on Monday after pleading guilty at Bradford Crown Court on Thursday, August 20.

Leahy, of Dawson Street, was sentenced to five years imprisonment for two counts of being concerned in the supply of crack cocaine and two counts of being concerned in the supply of heroin.

He was also sentenced for possession with intent to supply cocaine, crack cocaine and heroin.

Broadbent, aged 26, of Wycombe Green in Holme Wood, was sentenced to three years and four months imprisonment for possession with intent to supply cocaine and possession of criminal property.

![](https://www.thetelegraphandargus.co.uk/resources/images/21694275.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Cash and drugs belonging to Alfie Leahy *(Image: West Yorkshire Police)*

## What police have said

Anyone with information about drug offences or concerns about suspicious activity at a premises in their community is asked to report it via Live Chat | West Yorkshire Police.

There is also a specific online reporting form for reporting drug use or drug dealing which is available here at [https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/report-it/report-drug-use-drug-dealing](https://www.westyorkshire.police.uk/report-it/report-drug-use-drug-dealing)

Sergeant Charlie Smallwood, from the Bradford district team, said: “I welcome today’s sentences and hope they will provide some reassurance to the public and serve as a warning to others who aim to profit from criminality.

“Drug dealing blights our communities and is a direct cause of violent crime and county lines offending.

“If anyone has information about drug offences or concerns about suspicious activity, I urge them to report it.”
