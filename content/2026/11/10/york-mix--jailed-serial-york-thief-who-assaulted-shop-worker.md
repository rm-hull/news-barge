A prolific York shoplifter has been sent to prison.

Michaela Allan, 36, of no fixed address but from the York area, appeared at York Magistrates’ Court yesterday (Monday).

She was sentenced to 50 weeks’ imprisonment for numerous shop thefts and an assault on a retail worker.

On 23 October, Allan received a suspended sentence for a shop theft back in September.

But she continued to steal throughout October and the beginning of November and has now been imprisoned.

Police staff investigator Jamie Brownhill said: “Allan’s offending, committed over a sustained period, caused significant disruption to local businesses and retail staff.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/york-magistrates-court-close-doorway.webp)

“Retail staff should be able to go to work without facing the impact of persistent criminality.

“We take reports of shop theft seriously and will continue to work with businesses to identify offenders, put them before the courts and protect our communities.

“This sentencing sends a clear message that we will relentlessly pursue prolific offenders and take robust action against those who commit theft and violence within our communities.

“North Yorkshire Police remains committed to working alongside retailers to tackle shop theft, protect staff and bring repeat offenders to justice.”
