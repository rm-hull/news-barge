## Ukraine, Russia and the United States resume peace talks in Abu Dhabi, with a joint EU team and officials from France, Germany and the UK on site. We speak to Eurogroup president Kyriakos Pierrakakis about the race to succeed Christine Lagarde and report on new talks over Cyprus's division.

## On today's show:

Eurogroup president and Greece's finance minister **Kyriakos Pierrakakis** sits down with**Stefan Grobe** for an exclusive interview on rising energy prices and the race to succeed**Christine Lagarde** at the helm of the European Central Bank.**Maria Tadeo**, Euronews Europe Editor, speaks exclusively to Spain's Deputy PM and Economy Minister** Carlos Cuerpo**, as Madrid pushes** Pablo Hernández de Cos**for the ECB job.** Toby Gregory**reports from Abu Dhabi on the preparations, the negotiating format and the agenda for US-mediated peace talks between Ukraine and Russia.** Sasha Vakulina** explains what else is on the table beyond security guarantees, and what role Europeans will play.

Our correspondent in Athens **Fay Doulgeri** explains why Cyprus reunification talks are back in the news after Monday's meeting of the two leaders with the UN envoy.
