North Yorkshire-based Johnsons Nurseries has renewed its Plant Healthy certification for a sixth consecutive year, as the business continues to invest in plant health standards and employee training.

The family-run nursery, based in Whixley between York and Harrogate, supplies between five and six million plants and trees each year to the landscape and garden centre sectors.

The Plant Healthy Certification Scheme recognises businesses that have plant health management systems in place to help reduce the risk of introducing and spreading harmful pests and diseases through the movement of plants.

Alongside renewing its certification, Johnsons Nurseries has announced that ten members of staff completed specialist Plant Health training during the autumn.

The employees who completed the training are Wayne Atkinson, Jill Rose, Tyler Lonergan, Ruby Waldron, Ashley Robinson, Nigel Robinson, Freya Lamacq-Wigham, Levi Riley, Jordan Riley and Alex McCutcheon.

The training covered plant health, pest and disease identification, preventative measures and biosecurity procedures throughout the growing and distribution process.

## Investment in plant health and staff training

> **Jonathan Whittemore, Commercial and Business Manager:**
>
> Plant health is fundamental to everything we do, from growing and caring for millions of plants each year to ensuring they reach our customers in the best possible condition.
>
> Renewing our Plant Healthy certification is an important achievement for the business and reflects the hard work and dedication of our teams across our nursery sites.
>
> We’re equally proud of the employees who have completed their latest Plant Health training. Investing in our people and developing their knowledge is essential to maintaining high standards, protecting our plants and supporting the long-term sustainability of the horticultural industry.

Johnsons Nurseries was established in 1921 by Eric Johnson. The business was taken over by John Richardson in 1964 and became known as Johnsons of Whixley Ltd in 1993, before rebranding as Johnsons Nurseries Ltd in 2023.

The company supplies plants and trees to the amenity, landscaping and garden centre sectors, with experience growing plants for projects including the Forth Road Bridge, the Royal Parks, HS1 and the Athletes’ Village for the Glasgow Commonwealth Games.

Johnsons Nurseries also holds ISO 9001:2015 quality management and ISO 14001:2015 environmental management certifications.
