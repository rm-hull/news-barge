A major project to restore a wall with dates back to the beginning of the 17th century will be the focus of a special event this weekend.

The lime mortar repair study day will be held at the Grade II\* listed Old Lodge Hotel in Malton, on Saturday, November 14, from 9.30am to 4.30pm.

Hosted by the Building Limes Forum in partnership with Build & Restore Ltd and the Earth Stone & Lime Company, the event will focus on the ongoing restoration of the 1604-08 curtain wall—part of a Scheduled Monument and the remains of the Eure Prodigy House.

The deteriorating wall, constructed largely from calcareous sandstone quarried nearby, is currently being repaired by local specialists Build & Restore.

A spokesperson for the organisers said: "The wall has been in distress over many decades, is heavily eroded and locally decayed.

"Its repair raises numerous conservation and repair issues and dilemmas.

"The event will include presentations of work, past and present craft practices with lime in the region, as well as demonstration mixing of the repair mortars to be used at the lodge.

"There will also be a tour of the scaffolded works and hopefully a good deal of discussion."

Plasterer Jay Sumner Brown will discuss the repair of Whitby Town Hall’s columns, while Nigel Copsey will explore traditional mortar use in north-east Yorkshire and provide a brief history of the Old Lodge site.

Mason Steve Murray will examine whether modern finishing techniques align with historic practice.

Martin Craggs and Eric Bowie from Build & Restore will deliver a talk titled "The Wall."

Afternoon sessions will include mixing demonstrations, a guided tour of the scaffolded wall, and open discussions during afternoon tea.

The mortar used in the repair project is a hot-mixed, non-hydraulic lime mortar, following the original specification identified by Bill Revie: two parts non-hydraulic lime to one part aggregate, including sharp sand, oolitic limestone dust, and calcareous sandstone dust.

Morning coffee, a sandwich lunch, and afternoon tea will be provided.

Attendees are advised to wear sturdy clothing and bring a hard hat if possible, though some PPE will be supplied on the day.

Tickets cost £35 for Building Limes Forum members and £45 for non-members.

For more information go to www.buildinglimesforum.org.uk/events/lime-mortar-repair-study-day/
