# Police Leeds Asda House evacuation update as verdict on package confirmed

## Asda House was evacuated and multiple roads were closed

Police have issued an update after Asda House in Leeds was evacuated.

West Yorkshire Police have said the suspicious-looking package, at the centre of the evacuation, was "non-suspicious and non-viable" after army bomb disposal units were sent to the Asda HQ on Great Wilson Street, in Leeds city centre, yesterday (Monday, November 9). Multiple roads were closed and a cordon was in place.

In an update, a West Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "A package reported to officers that was found at Asda House on Great Wilson Street, Leeds city centre yesterday (9 November) has been confirmed to be non-suspicious and non-viable. Multiple roads were closed and the Army EOD were called to attend following the incident that saw a building evacuated as a precaution and a cordon put in place.

"The EOD carried out a controlled explosion and following enquiries, the package is not being treated as suspicious and the investigation has been closed. Officers would like to thank motorists and members of the public for their cooperation."
