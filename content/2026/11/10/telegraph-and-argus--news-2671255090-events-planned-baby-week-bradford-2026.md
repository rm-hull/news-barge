A WEEK of baby raves, literacy adventures, and sensory activities is set to take over Bradford.

Baby Week Bradford 2026 will run from November 14 to 20, featuring more than 90 events designed to support families and celebrate early childhood.

Cllr Stephen Place, leader of Bradford Council, said: "Baby Week is all about bringing people together to celebrate 0-5s and the vital role that families and communities play in helping them help them grow, learn and thrive.

"We are committed to helping families understand how important the first 1001 days to age 5 are, and how they lay the foundations for lifelong health and wellbeing."

The event programme includes everything from baby raves and nature play to sensory sessions, storytelling, and chatty tea parties.

Most activities are free of charge.

Two flagship family events will take place at Darley Street Market from 1pm to 4pm on Saturday, November 14, and at Airedale Shopping Centre in Keighley from 10am to 1pm.

Pam Essler, chair of the Keighley and Worth Valley Community Partnership, said: "It's always exciting to be part of Baby Week Bradford.

"It's fantastic to see Baby Week returning to Keighley, bringing with it a real buzz and plenty of opportunities for families to chat, play and read together."
