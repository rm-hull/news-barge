# Put women prisoners in ex-military camps and place asylum seekers in jails instead: Yorkshire Post Letters

## Dave Croucher, Pinfold, Doncaster.

Anyone entering the UK should have their fingerprints taken, their photo taken, and their DNA taken. If they are not prepared to do this, they should be deported on the next plane going out. Anyone having no ID will automatically be deported.

If we are so short of prison places, why not take the women who are in jail for minor offences and put them in the ex-forces barracks and use their jails for illegal migrants?

If you take the migrants straight to jail, the thousands on the other side of the Channel will soon get the message.

Who came up with the bright idea to just put them in a holiday camp, give them money and a free bus service?
