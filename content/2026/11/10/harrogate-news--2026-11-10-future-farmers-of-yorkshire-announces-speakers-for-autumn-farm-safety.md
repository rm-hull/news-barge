Three speakers will share practical advice on improving farm safety at the Future Farmers of Yorkshire’s Autumn Debate later this month.

The event will focus on low-cost measures that farmers can introduce into their day-to-day work to reduce accidents and improve safety on farms.

Farmer and farm safety campaigner Jane Gurney MBE, farmer and agricultural software developer Sam Hoste, and agricultural health and safety manager Rhiannon Wilson will form the panel.

The debate will take place at Pavilions of Harrogate, at the Great Yorkshire Showground, on Monday 23 November. It is open to everyone working in agriculture, with a free supper and networking from 6pm.

## Farm safety speakers

Jane Gurney is the sixth generation of her family to farm on the Bedfordshire-Cambridgeshire border. She founded the Tilly Trailer Pass scheme, which provides inspection, servicing and certification to ensure agricultural trailers meet the legally required standard.

The initiative was established to give young farmers and operators a voice during harvest work and has developed into a UK-wide scheme, with thousands of trailers submitted each year through authorised dealerships.

Sam Hoste farms in North Yorkshire and secured an Innovate UK ADOPT grant to develop Agrillo, a software platform and app designed to help farmers manage on-farm plant protection products.

The system aims to reduce paperwork, improve compliance and provide greater visibility of products stored on farms. Agrillo is now used by farmers and agronomists across the UK.

Sam also owns Quantech Solutions, a consultancy supporting start-up agri-tech companies, with a focus on livestock innovations.

Rhiannon Wilson is Health and Safety Office Manager at CXCS Group Ltd, which advises farmers on agricultural regulations. She comes from a dairy farming background in Herefordshire and has a particular interest in agricultural health and safety.

## Practical steps for safer farms

The panel will discuss the theme ‘Heads Up: Practical Steps for a Safe and Productive Farm’, exploring everyday actions that can make a difference to the safety, wellbeing and efficiency of farm businesses.

The event is being delivered alongside the Yorkshire Rural Support Network and sponsored by McClarrons.

Sally Conner, a farmer and Business Development Executive in McClarrons’ Farm Team, will chair the discussion. Attendees can also claim two BASIS points for attending.

> **Joe Seels, Chair of Future Farmers of Yorkshire:**

It’s well-worth coming along to hear what they have to say.
