CONSTRUCTION sites are being targeted by thieves across Yorkshire.

An investigation by security specialists Nothing But Padlocks has revealed more than 5,200 incidents of theft and burglary at construction sites across Yorkshire and the Humber between 2020 and 2025.

Of these, 2,670 were recorded in West Yorkshire, with Leeds and Bradford among the most affected areas.

Rich Quelch, managing director at Nothing But Padlocks, said: "Our research highlights just how often construction sites across Yorkshire are targeted by thieves, with tools, machinery and materials disappearing at a rate of more than two incidents a day.

"For contractors managing tight margins and rising material costs, each theft means delays, replacement costs, and higher insurance premiums.

"While it is encouraging to see recorded incidents falling since 2020, more than 700 crimes a year is still a sizeable and concerning problem and the higher number of thefts in West Yorkshire shows the risk hasn’t gone away."

The highest number of incidents in West Yorkshire was recorded in 2023, with 523 reported cases.

Of the 2,670 incidents in West Yorkshire, 1,131 were in Leeds, 649 in Bradford, 375 in Wakefield, 303 in Kirklees and 212 in Calderdale.
