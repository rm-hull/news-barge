A care worker is marking 20 years of service and a remarkable journey from frontline roles to the finance team.

Kasia Serwatka began her career with the Czajka Care Group in 2006 after moving to the UK from Poland.

She initially worked in the laundry department at Fairmount and Staveley Birkleas Nursing Homes but soon progressed through a variety of roles including kitchen assistant, senior carer, and training officer.

Ms Serwatka said: "I love working at Czajka Care Group and the last 20 years have flown by.

"It’s a family business where people genuinely care about you and look after you.

"I have had the opportunity to try different roles, learn new things and develop professionally throughout my time here."

Her professional background in accountancy, along with her care experience, led her to transition into the company’s finance department, where she now handles invoicing, part of the payroll, and financial administration for Czajka’s retirement communities.

Konrad Czajka, managing director of Czajka Care Group, said: "We are incredibly grateful to Kasia for her 20 years of loyalty, hard work, and commitment.

"She is a fantastic example of the versatility and dedication we value within our business."
