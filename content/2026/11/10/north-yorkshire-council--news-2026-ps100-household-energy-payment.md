The government recently announced it will be providing a £100 household energy payment for households in Council Tax bands A to D as a one-off payment during this financial year.

We are currently awaiting detailed guidance from the government and expect to begin making payments from January 2027.

You do not need to contact us about the payment, we will let everyone know as soon as further information becomes available.

You can see which Council Tax band your property is in on the government's website.

If you pay your Council Tax by direct debit, you are likely to receive the rebate more quickly because we already have your bank details.

If you do not currently pay your Council Tax by direct debit, you can sign up today. We offer a range of flexible payment options, including six monthly payment dates, as well as fortnightly and weekly payment plans.

For more information about the government's Autumn Budget announcement on energy bills, visit the what the Autumn Budget 2026 means for your energy bills page on the government website.
