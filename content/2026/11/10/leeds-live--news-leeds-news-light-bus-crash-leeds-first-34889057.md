# The Light bus crash in Leeds as First Bus issues update on injuries

## First Bus has apologised 'for any inconvenience'

Passengers received "minor injuries" after a bus crashed into The Light entrance in Leeds city centre.

The First Bus crashed into the building's entrance, on Albion Street, at 4.37pm yesterday, on Monday, November 9. The road was closed while emergency crews dealt with the scene. First Bus has said there were minor injuries to some customers but did not elaborate on why the crash occurred. The bus company apologised for the crash and says it's assisting police.

A spokesperson for First Bus said: “There was an incident involving one of our route 5 buses on Albion Street in Leeds yesterday evening. We are assisting the police with its enquiries and apologise for any inconvenience to pedestrians and other road users.”
