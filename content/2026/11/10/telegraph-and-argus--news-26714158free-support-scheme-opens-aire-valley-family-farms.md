A SCHEME is offering family farms free support to build resilience and plan for the future.

The Farm Resilience Programme (FRP), from The Royal Countryside Fund, is now open to farmers in the Aire Valley and surrounding areas.

Up to 20 farming families will be offered £2,000 worth of free business and environmental support through the scheme.

Beth Owen-Smith, Senior Programme Manager at The Royal Countryside Fund, said: "Family farms are operating in an environment of constant change, but many tell us the biggest challenge is finding the time and space to step back and plan for the future.

"The Farm Resilience Programme has helped farmers understand their business, explore new opportunities and make informed decisions with confidence for ten years.

"We know that resilient businesses and resilient environments go hand in hand, and that's why the programme takes a whole-farm, whole-family approach."

Now in its tenth year, the programme has supported more than 1,500 family farms since 2016.

It is particularly suited to small family livestock farms and crofters but is open to any farm business that could benefit.

The programme is delivered through in-person workshops in partnership with The Yorkshire Dales Farmer Network.

Each farm receives a business health check, guidance on improving financial performance, practical business planning, and advice on working with nature to lower input costs and improve resilience.

Steve, a fourth-generation upland beef and sheep farmer from Lancashire who took part in the programme last year, said it helped him focus on profitability, succession planning, and future opportunities.

He said: "I like to know what's going on and what's working and what's not, and looking at figures and trying to increase profitability and efficiencies.

"The benchmarking gave me the confidence to keep going forwards with it and made me think that what we're doing is right."

Farmers also gain the chance to connect with peers facing similar challenges and build support networks that last beyond the programme.

The Aire Valley group will be supported by The Yorkshire Dales Farmer Network, ensuring the help provided is tailored to the local environment.

The programme is free to attend.

Farmers can find out more and sign up by visiting www.royalcountrysidefund.org.uk/farmresilience, emailing [yorkshire@thefarmernetwork.co.uk](mailto:yorkshire@thefarmernetwork.co.uk), or calling 07300 371523.

The Farm Resilience Programme is funded by Aldi UK, the Esmée Fairbairn Foundation, Hilton Foods, Morrisons, the Postcode Lottery, and the Rothschild Foundation.
