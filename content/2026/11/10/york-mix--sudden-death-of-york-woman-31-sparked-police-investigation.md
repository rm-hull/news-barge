The sudden death of a York woman sparked a police investigation which saw a house cordoned off and one person arrested.

North Yorkshire Police today issued details about the incident, which saw the terraced home on **Ebor Street** taped off on Saturday.

The Major Crime Unit was investigating, and forensic crime scene investigators were seen at the house.

#### Incident location

Today, a North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “An investigation involving the sudden death of a 31-year-old woman from York has determined that she died of natural causes.

“She was admitted to hospital on Thursday (5 November) evening, and she sadly passed away on Saturday morning.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/police-incident-ebor-street-york-7-nov-2026-1.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/police-incident-ebor-street-york-7-nov-2026-3.webp)

“Due to the initial reported circumstances, a 24-year-old woman was arrested in connection with the investigation.

“An address in York was also cordoned off and searched by the police on Saturday.

“However, following extensive enquiries by officers and from the findings of a pathologist, it was determined that the death was not suspicious.

“A report is being prepared for the coroner.

“The woman’s family have been supported by officers throughout this time.”
