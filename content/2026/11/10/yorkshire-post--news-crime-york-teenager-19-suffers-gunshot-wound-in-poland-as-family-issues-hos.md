# York teenager, 19, suffers gunshot wound in Poland as family issues hospital update

## A 19-year-old man from York is recovering in hospital after suffering a gunshot wound in a shooting in Poland that left six people dead and 16 others injured.

Harry Marchant was injured during the shooting in Krakow's Main Square on Saturday (Nov 7), with his family confirming he is now in a stable condition.

Four women and two men were killed in the attack, while 16 others were injured, including two police officers.

The death of the suspect, a 24-year-old Polish national, was confirmed by Krakow Police in a post on X on Monday (Nov 9).

![Harry Marchant](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/10/15/23/harry-marchant.jpeg?trim=29,0,279,0&width=640&quality=65)


In a statement released through the force, Harry's family said: “We would like to confirm that our son, Harry Marchant, was injured during the incident in Poland over the weekend. We are pleased to say he is now in a stable condition, receiving ongoing hospital treatment.

“We can't express how grateful we are to the first responders, medical team, and the people of Poland for their help and care for Harry.

“We ask for our privacy to be respected as we support Harry with his recovery.”

Harry is a supporter of York City FC, who paid tribute to him in a statement on Tuesday.

The club said: "Everyone at York City Football Club would like to send their love and support to young supporter Harry Marchant, who was caught up in the horrific gun attack in Kraków, Poland, on Saturday.

"Harry is in a stable condition in hospital in Poland following surgery. We all would like to wish Harry a speedy recovery, and we hope to see him back at the LNER supporting us very soon."
