York’s well known and much respected Street Ranger, Scott Sinclair, has announced that he is moving on.

Scott works for Eboracum which was founded by Carl Nickson in 2011.

The company has expanded over the years and now provides services across a diverse range of sectors in Yorkshire and beyond.

Scott has been a key member of the team for more than ten years and says he has enjoyed his work helping to keep residents, businesses and visitors safe in a city that he loves.

Street Rangers are a welcoming face for visitors and residents, and work with partners to help tackle complex crime and disorder problems.

​

Now he says he is ready for a new job and a new challenge and this week he will be getting ready to move to the security department at York Hospital.

Scott told *YorkMix Radio*: “I’ll miss the relationships that I built up over the years on the streets, getting to know each individual in every business, and being able to work out and about in my home town.

“I never thought I’d be as as well known as I have been. I find it a lot when I go around the city centre, even on my days off.

“I’m six foot seven. I’m quite a noticeable person, anyway. But I won’t let changing jobs change that for me. Like I say, I’ll still make time for the people of the city centre that I’ve known and made friends with over the years.”

Scott will leave Street Rangers just as the Christmas Market gets underway.
