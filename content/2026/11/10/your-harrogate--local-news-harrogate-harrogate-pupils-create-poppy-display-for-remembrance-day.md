Every pupil at Grove Road Community Primary School in Harrogate has made a poppy for a school display marking Remembrance Day.

The children each painted, drew or decorated their own poppy to remember those who have served and those who have lost their lives in conflict.

Grove Road teacher Mrs Ella Tinsley-Harrison told Your Harrogate:

> “The display is a lovely reflection of the children's creativity, thoughtfulness and understanding of the importance of remembering.
>
> “Each poppy is unique and has been made with care, making the whole display something we are incredibly proud of.”
