A North Yorkshire hotel and restaurant is celebrating after being ranked among the top 10 per cent of properties on Tripadvisor.

The Wainstones Hotel, in Great Broughton near Stokesley, has received the Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice Award 2026 following a refurb of its restaurant.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689022.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Wainstones restaurant *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

At the foot of the North Yorkshire Moors, the Wainstones is a base for visitors exploring the surrounding countryside.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689023.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Wainstones Hotel and Restaurant *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

The refurbishment blends the building’s original character with modern comforts, while focusing on a “luxurious yet homely” feel and sustainability.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689021.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Wainstones restaurant cocktail bar *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

The Wainstones has also introduced The Stones Café, serving locally sourced food and drinks and offering another place for guests and visitors to stop and refuel.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689019.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Stones Café *(Image: TripAdvisor)*![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689018.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The Wainstones is well-known for a range of classic breakfast dishes*(Image: TripAdvisor)*

Guests can eat in the bar, book for the restaurant or choose private dining in the Bait Box.

The menu combines classic favourites and contemporary dishes, including beer-battered haddock with curry sauce and chips, plus minted lamb shoulder pie.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689020.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Sunday roasts are highly rated among guests at the Wainstones *(Image: TripAdvisor)*

Other options include a double smash burger, 12-hour-braised beef shin lasagne and pot-roasted chicken breast.

The hotel itself has 20 individually styled rooms, including the 'Tip Top' and 'Top Notch' twins, 'Divine Double' and 'Swanky' suites.

Those travelling with dogs can book one of the 'Pawsome' or 'Pawfect' rooms for £20 per visit.

Four-legged visitors receive a welcome pack with a treat, blanket, food and water bowls, and a guide to dog-friendly walks and pubs nearby.

Visitors can find The Wainstones Hotel at 31 High Street in Great Broughton.
