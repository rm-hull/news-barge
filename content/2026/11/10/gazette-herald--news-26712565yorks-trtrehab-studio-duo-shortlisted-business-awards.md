A health and rehabilitation studio has announced two of its team members as finalists at the UK Women’s Business Awards 2026.

York-based TRTrehab Studio’s founder Iwona Michalska and manager Marie Elcock have been recognised for their work at the national event.

Ms Michalska said: "I am incredibly proud, but this recognition isn't just about me.

"TRTrehab has always been about people.

"Every person who walks through our doors has their own story, their own challenges and their own turning point."

Ms Michalska is shortlisted in three categories: Health and Wellbeing Business Woman of the Year, Inspirational Woman in Business, and the Lifetime Achievement Award.

Ms Elcock, who is also a senior therapist at the studio, is a finalist for Manager of the Year.

She said: "For me, management has never been about being the person in charge.

"It is about being someone others can rely on, learn from, and grow alongside."
