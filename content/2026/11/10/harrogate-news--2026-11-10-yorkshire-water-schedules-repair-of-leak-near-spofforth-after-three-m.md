Yorkshire Water has scheduled a repair to a water leak near Spofforth for 24 November, following concerns that water has been escaping from the leak for almost three months.

The leak is at the junction of Deighton Road and Harrogate Road and was first reported to Yorkshire Water on 13 August 2026 by local resident James Monaghan.

Mr Monaghan said he initially received reassurance that the water company was aware of the problem and was working to resolve it. However, the leak was still running when last observed on 2 November.

> **James Monaghan:**
>
> I spotted the leak in the middle of the road and reported it a couple of days later. At first, I was reassured that Yorkshire Water said they were aware of the issue and were working to fix it. However, almost 3 months later the leak remains unrepaired. It is exasperating to think how much water has been wasted with the leak running 24 hours a day for so long now.

Mr Monaghan had raised concerns about the length of time taken to arrange the repair, particularly following the drought conditions experienced earlier in the year.

> **James Monaghan:**
>
> After the drought we have faced this year, I would have expected repairing leaks like this to be a priority. I am calling on Yorkshire Water to repair it promptly and give residents a clear timescale for the work.

## Yorkshire Water confirms repair date

Following an enquiry from The Harrogate Informer, Yorkshire Water confirmed that the repair has been scheduled for Tuesday 24 November.

> **Yorkshire Water spokesperson:**
>
> We have planned to repair the leak at the junction of Deighton Road and Harrogate Road on the 24 November. We understand that the length of time taken to plan the repair will be frustrating for residents and would like to thank them for their patience – our leakage team works 24/7, prioritising the leaks that are losing the most water.

The leak was reported under Yorkshire Water case reference X436529.

The repair is scheduled for 24 November 2026.
