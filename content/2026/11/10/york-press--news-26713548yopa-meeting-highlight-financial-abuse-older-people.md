An assembly will spotlight the hidden financial abuse of older people in York.

The York Older People's Assembly meeting is set to be held on Monday, November 16, at the Friargate Quaker Meeting House from 2pm to 4pm.

The session will introduce a new campaign, led by the City of York Council, with Independent Domestic Abuse Services and Age UK York.

The campaign aims to help people recognise the signs and understand how this hidden form of domestic abuse affects wellbeing and quality of life.

Pamela Gourlay from the City of York Council will address the topic.

Carl Wain from the charity will then discuss how Age UK York involves older volunteers in various roles.

The meeting will also provide details about a new free prescription delivery service in its "Did you know...?" segment.

The event is said to be "an important and informative social afternoon".
