Just when you thought the celebrations for Grand Theft Auto 6 couldn't get any more extravagant, Rockstar has announced that it will be hosting a huge music festival in Miami just a few days after its release. But that begs the question: who's going to turn up? I thought everyone was going to be playing GTA 6?

"Experience the music of Vice City and Leonida at Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a special live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami to celebrate the launch of GTAVI, featuring a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack," an official blog post reads.

The event is in partnership with PlayStation and will feature "two stages of nonstop music performances" with the following artists:

> Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a special live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami to celebrate the launch of GTAVI, featuring a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack. More info: [https://t.co/XRlfYFOk0H](https://t.co/XRlfYFOk0H) [https://t.co/fQyOU7I4gUNovember](https://t.co/fQyOU7I4gUNovember) 9, 2026

Tickets will be available via a lottery system for those aged 17+ with exclusive codes being sent out to those who managed to win a spot when the entries are drawn on today at 4 pm ET/ 9 pm GMT. But there are a couple things to keep in mind. There's no word on how expensive the tickets will be, with prices likely being revealed later today in the lottery, and the festival is a mere two weeks away, as it takes place on November 21, 2026.

So, unless you're a super fan with money to spare on not only a festival ticket but also a last-minute plane ticket to Miami, this event will likely be for locals or, at the very least, those already in the US. My only issue is that if you're so big a fan that you're willing to drop everything last minute and head over to Florida then why aren't you actually playing GTA 6? The festival is happening just two days after GTA 6, I'm pretty sure we're all busy around then.
