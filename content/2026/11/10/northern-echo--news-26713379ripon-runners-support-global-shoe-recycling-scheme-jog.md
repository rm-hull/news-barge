Ripon Runners have donated more than a hundred running shoes to support a global shoe recycling scheme.

They collected the shoes for Jog On, an initiative that aims to keep one million pairs of trainers out of landfill by redistributing them to people in need around the world.

Jog On, founded in the UK by Tony Piedade, estimates that 18 billion pairs of running shoes are sold each year globally, with more than 30 million ending up in landfill — many of which are still usable.

Sarah Beldon, ladies’ captain of Ripon Runners, said: "I was inspired by reading about Jog On and realised that lots of us at the club have unwanted running shoes ready to pass on to others.

"So, I decided to co-ordinate a collection at the club and was delighted with the response — which resulted in 54 pairs of shoes being donated.

"I’d like to offer a huge 'thank you' to all my club colleagues who got involved."

Jog On has partnered with logistics companies to collect, sort, and distribute shoes to 15 destinations worldwide.

By August 2026, the organisation had already processed 60 tonnes of shoes.

Ms Beldon added that she hopes the club can organise another collection in the future.
