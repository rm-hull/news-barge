A 21-year-old has denied robbing another man of a Rolex watch and designer bag at York Railway Station.

Alfie Fowler was arrested following an incident on October 11 that led to two people being taken to hospital and police closing Park Street for some hours. He was arrested at Hull Railway Station later the same day.

Fowler, of Ripley Close, Hull, was appearing before York Crown Court for a plea and trial preparation hearing via a video link from the prison where he is on remand.

He denied that he had robbed a man of a Rolex watch, a designer bag and cash on October 11. The machete offence was committed on the same day.

His trial was fixed for March 31 at York Crown Court.

He did not apply for bail and was remanded in custody until then.
