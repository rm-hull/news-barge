Police are hunting vandals who damaged a car at a North Yorkshire holiday park.

It happened between 10.30pm on Saturday 9am on Sunday (7-8 November) in the Blue Dolphin Holiday Park in Filey.

A car’s front and rear windscreen wipers were snapped off.

#### Blue Dolphin Holiday Park, Filey

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We’re particularly appealing to anyone who saw this incident happen, or saw any suspicious behaviour during this time frame.”
