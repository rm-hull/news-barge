A drunk woman assaulted people who were trying to help her, police said today (Tuesday).

It happened outside Tesco Express on Piccadilly in York at about 7pm on Friday 30 October.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “Members of the public have tried to assist a drunken female at the location who lost her friends and had no battery power left in her phone to contact them.

#### Incident location

“She has then become aggressive towards the members of public offering assistance and assaulted them.”

The woman was wearing beige outfit with a black shoulder bag. It s believed other members of the public also stopped to help her.

Police are asking witnesses to come forward.
