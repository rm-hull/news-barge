A North East wedding planner who has spent years creating other couples’ dream days turned to a familiar County Durham venue when it was finally time to plan his own.

Ben Carter, who runs Party Forever from a shop in Coxhoe, has spent 15 years transforming Bowburn Hall Hotel into a wedding wonderland for clients.

So when it came time to marry his partner Ryan, there was no doubt where the celebration would take place.

Mr Carter said: "There was a lot of pressure on me to create something very special for my wedding.

"If that’s your day job then people will expect it to be spectacular and that’s exactly what we tried to do.

"I have created weddings at Bowburn Hall Hotel for the past 15 years and absolutely love it, so it was definitely the place that Ryan and I wanted to have our special day."

The couple hosted 140 guests during the day and 250 in the evening at the country house hotel, which can be booked for exclusive use.

The ceremony took place in Bowburn’s stunning garden, with a surprise rainbow firework display.

Mr Carter said: "The fireworks were set to go off when the registrar said we could have our first kiss.

"It was a fantastic surprise for everyone and really got the day off to a brilliant start."

Guests were treated to entertainment during the day by a brass band, a saxophonist and then in the evening by a full Ibiza Symphony show.

Bowburn’s main function room was transformed with white flowers and candles.

Mr Carter said: "The beauty about Bowburn is that the staff just can’t do enough for you.

"I’ve worked at so many venues where you don’t see the same faces twice because they have such a high turnover of staff, but at Bowburn, people clearly love working there and have stayed for years.

"I have had such a fantastic relationship with them and I knew it would be the perfect place to bring our vision to life."

Tom Pallister, general manager at Bowburn Hall Hotel, said he was delighted to host the couple’s big day.
