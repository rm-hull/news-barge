A series of footpaths in a North East town are set to close for around five months while essential gas main repairs are carried out.

The paths, all located in Hall Lane Estate, Houghton-le-Spring, will be shut by Sunderland City Council under a Temporary Traffic Regulation Order.

The council has announced the closures, which will come into effect on November 30, and are estimated to be in operation for five months from commencement of the works.

The paths to be closed are:

* Balmoral Crescent from the extended north-western property boundary of 59 Balmoral Crescent to the extended south-western property boundary of 61 Balmoral Crescent.
* Gillas Lane the south-east side, from its junction with Meadow Close to the extended south-western property boundary of 25 Warden Grove.
* Gillas Lane, the footpath linking Gillas Lane to Meadow Close.
* All footpaths on Kingsway.
* All associated footpaths linking numbers 16 Leeholme to 46 Leeholme.
* Marlowe Place from the extended eastern property boundary of 9 Marlowe Place to the extended south-eastern property boundary of 12 Waller Terrace.
* All associated footpaths linking numbers 13 Meadow Close to 34 Meadow Close.
* Houghton Footpath 26, Woodlea from the extended party property boundary of 46 and 47 Leeholme for a distance of 74 metres in a south-westerly direction.
* Footpath linking Waller Terrace to Shakespeare Street from the extended north-eastern property boundary of 12 Waller Terrace for a distance of 152 metres in a south-westerly direction.

* **For more notices affecting YOUR area, visit our Public Notice Portal - Public Notices from The Northern Echo ( publicnoticeportal.uk). More information on this application can be found here.**

The council has stated that diversions for pedestrians will be provided via the adjacent highway network, and the paths will remain closed until the works are completed, or as indicated by traffic signs.

The only individuals who will have access to the closed paths will be those directly involved in the repair works.

The maximum duration of the closure under the Order will be 6 months, however it is estimated that the closures will be in operation for 5 months from commencement of the works.
