The royal couple joined victims' relatives for a **solemn funeral Mass** in Toledo's 13th-century cathedral, held nearly two weeks after one of Europe's deadliest rail disasters this century.

Residents of Urda placed **69 candles near the altar**, one for each person who died, and a 16th-century sculpture of Christ was brought from the town especially for the ceremony. Mourners gathered inside, where candles were burning in tribute to the dead.

Spain's second fatal high-speed rail accident this year has **left 69 people dead** after a**Renfe train collided with a stationary Iryo service** near Urda on 28 October. The disaster**prompted the resignations of the transport minister** and the head of the railway operator, while European prosecutors have launched a**fraud investigation into EU safety funds**.

Citing his refusal to attend religious ceremonies, **Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez did not attend** the funeral Mass, drawing criticism from opponents who accused him of dodging an angry public during an election campaign.

With a **general election scheduled for 29 November**, the tragedy has intensified pressure on the government, while rail services were further disrupted by a** train drivers' strike**.
