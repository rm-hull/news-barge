Dale Owens, currently chief executive at Gateshead Council, is being recommended as the next chief executive of North Yorkshire Council.

The recommendation follows a “robust” recruitment process, the council said.

It will go before a meeting of full council on Wednesday 18 November. Subject to approvals, Mr Owens will replace Richard Flinton, who retires next year.

Mr Flinton led the council through the reorganisation that saw eight councils become a single unitary authority in April 2023. The council says the change has delivered more than £69 million in savings for the county.

He announced his retirement in August, after a public service career spanning around 40 years. He will stay until May next year to oversee the local elections in North Yorkshire as returning officer, and Mr Owens is expected to take up the role at that point.

Cllr Carl Les, leader of the North Yorkshire Council, said: “Following a thorough and highly competitive recruitment process, I am delighted that the committee is recommending Dale as the next chief executive of North Yorkshire Council.”

Mr Owens previously worked for the former North Yorkshire County Council as an assistant director in health and adult services. He later became director of integrated health and adult services at Gateshead Council before becoming chief executive there in 2024.

Responding to the recommendation, Mr Owens said: “I am honoured to be recommended for this appointment. I know firsthand what a special place North Yorkshire is with ambitious communities and strong partnerships.”

North Yorkshire Council is England’s largest unitary authority by geography and the third largest by population.
