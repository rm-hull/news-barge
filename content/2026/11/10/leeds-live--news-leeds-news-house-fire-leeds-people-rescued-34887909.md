# House fire in Leeds caused by ‘ricocheting firework’ as neighbours alerted by ‘people screaming’

## Firefighters found a ground floor flat ablaze with two people at the window in need of rescue

Firefighters rushed to the scene of a house fire caused by a 'richocheting firework' after people could be heard inside 'screaming'.

Emergency services - including three fire engines and two 4x4s - were called to the blaze in Cross Green Avenue in Cross Green just before 1am this morning (Tuesday).

Calls to 999 came in reporting a 'house on fire and that people could be heard screaming', according to West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service.

Firefighters found a ground floor flat ablaze with two people at the window. The occupants were rescued by the fire service, and the neighbouring homes were evacuated as a precaution, said the service.

Firefighters went into the burning building with oxygen tanks, needing three hoses to extinguish the flames, along with a ventilation fan.

The two people who were rescued suffered smoke inhalation and were given a check up by paramedics, but did not need to go to hospital.

After the police had reviewed cctv the incident was deemed accidental due to a ricocheting firework.

Hard wired smoke detection was fitted and actuated.
