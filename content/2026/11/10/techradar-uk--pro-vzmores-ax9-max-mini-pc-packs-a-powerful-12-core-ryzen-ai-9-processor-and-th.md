## Why we recommend it

The VZMORE AX9 Max has some impressive hardware packed inside its tiny frame. The AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 processor combines 12 cores and 24 threads, and Radeon 890M integrated graphics can handle creative applications, high-resolution media, and 1080p gaming. It offers up to 86 TOPS of combined AI performance.

The AX9 Max uses a vapor chamber to spread heat across a larger area and three selectable performance modes operate at 45W, 54W, and 65W, allowing you to prioritize quieter operation or extra processing performance.

Connectivity includes two USB4 ports, dual HDMI 2.1, six USB-A connections, dual 2.5Gb Ethernet, and Wi-Fi 7.

The supplied 32GB of DDR5 RAM and 1TB SSD provide a useful starting configuration, with expansion support for up to 256GB of RAM and 5TB of storage. Windows 11 Pro comes preinstalled.

In his four-and-a-half star review, our expert Alastair said: "At the top end of standard form-factor mini PCs, the AX9 Max, with its Ferrari-styled logo, promises speed, luxury, and exceptional performance. Sure enough, the full metal casing, powerful processing engines, and detailing all add up."

He added that "This is a machine with potential, and while undoubtedly expensive for a mini PC with a 1TB SSD and 32GB RAM, that money is invested in that powerful CPU, GPU and NPU combo and the ability to expand the storage to a far greater degree than I have seen on the vast majority of other mini PCs of this size and type."
