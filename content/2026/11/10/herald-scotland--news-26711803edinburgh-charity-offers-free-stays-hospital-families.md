An Edinburgh charity has partnered with Airbnb.org to provide free accommodation for families who need to stay close to their children during hospital treatment.

Edinburgh Children’s Hospital Charity (ECHC) has joined Airbnb.org’s Medical Stays programme, which has launched in the UK with a £1 million commitment to provide free accommodation for patients and families travelling for medical care.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21689758.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Airbnb has partnered with an Edinburgh children's hospital charity. *(Image: Supplied)*

Airbnb.org is a non-profit organisation founded by Airbnb that provides free temporary housing during times of crisis.

The partnership is intended to ease the emotional and financial pressure on families who have to travel long distances for treatment.

Research commissioned by Airbnb.org and carried out by Censuswide found that people in Scotland travel an average of 34 miles to reach a hospital or treatment centre – the highest distance in the UK.

The research also found that around one in 10 people in Scotland had turned down hospital treatment because of the cost or difficulty of travel and accommodation, while one in three had slept in a hospital waiting room.

A further 12% said they had borrowed money from friends or family to cover travel costs, while eight per cent had gone without essentials such as food or heating.

The family had to leave their hotel the morning after the procedure and faced the prospect of paying hundreds of pounds for alternative accommodation.

![](https://www.heraldscotland.com/resources/images/21689759.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Airbnb has partnered with an Edinburgh children's hospital charity. *(Image: Supplied)*

Since 2021, families have been able to use the 26-bedroom Ronald McDonald House on the top floor of the hospital, but demand often exceeds capacity.

The Airbnb.org partnership provides an additional option, allowing ECHC to identify families needing urgent accommodation and arrange immediate check-in.

The charity supports around 500 families visiting the hospital every day and says the additional accommodation will help reduce the hidden costs associated with having a child in hospital.

Pippa Johnston, deputy chief executive of ECHC, said: "Every day we see families coming through the doors of the hospital who are struggling to cope with their child’s illness and are overwhelmed.

"Adding the financial pressure of having a child in hospital can be their breaking point.

"That is why we are delighted to announce our new partnership with Airbnb.org.

"We are very lucky to benefit from Ronald McDonald House here in Edinburgh but this service is often full to capacity so being able to offer families somewhere else close to stay, and at no cost, is invaluable."

Nathan Blecharczyk, Airbnb co-founder and chief strategy officer, said: "Airbnb believes everyone deserves a home, and this is especially true when going through some of the hardest moments of life.

"Through Airbnb.org’s Medical Stays programme, we’re working with NHS trusts and non-profit organisations to provide free overnight stays for patients and families near hospitals and committing £1 million so patients can be supported by their loved ones without the financial pressure of paying for accommodation."
