More than 100 new homes in a County Durham village have been given the green light, months after councillors backed the plans.

Durham County Council has formally approved 104 homes on land east of Daleside and Cross Lane in Sacriston on November 2.

A meeting in March saw councillors back the development.

Sixteen of the homes will be classed as affordable - 11 for rent, while five will be sold for no more than 75 per cent of their market value.

As part of a legal agreement with the authority, Persimmon will pay more than £200,000 towards education in the surrounding area. Other funding will go towards open spaces, public footpath signs and increasing capacity at Sacriston Medical Centre.

When councillors considered the plans, concerns were raised about the pressure more housing could place on local GP services and schools.

Councillor Tim Robson, who represents Sacriston and Witton Gilbert, previously said residents had "deep concerns" about the number of new homes being built locally.
