A “beautiful” York hotel steeped in history has been named one of the best in the world.

La Liste has released its list of the best hotels around the world including many spots found around the UK.

York’s own Grays Court Hotel and The Bow Room Restaurant made it onto the list with a score of 91.5 out of 100.

## What is the Grays Court Hotel and The Bow Room Restaurant like?

Described as a “beautiful boutique hotel”, the Grays Court Hotel can be found right next to the York Minster and is surrounded by some of the city’s must-visit spots.

The hotel features 12 individually designed en-suite rooms with views ranging from the cobbled courtyard to the gardens surrounding it and the York Minster.

During your stay, you can enjoy a walk around the gardens or relax in the Long Gallery while enjoying the history around you.

The hotel's building dates back centuries and is thought to be the oldest continuously occupied building in Britain.

The hotel has its own restaurant onsite called The Bow Room that offers “an exceptional experience” for its guests.

The dishes from the restaurant are made using ingredients from the “best” local suppliers as well as fruits, vegetables, and herbs sourced from the Bow Room’s kitchen gardens.

The restaurant has a range of dishes on offer from sea bass and salt aged lamb saddle to barbecued hispi cabbage.

If you want to make your stay even more special, you can test out the Bow Room’s tasting menu.
