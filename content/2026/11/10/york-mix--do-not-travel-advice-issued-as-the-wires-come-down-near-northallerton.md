There are long delays to train services on the East Coast Main Line this evening.

LNER said: “We are experiencing route wide disruption due to overhead line damage in the Northallerton area.”

Customers are being advised **NOT TO TRAVEL**.

Due to the severity of the disruption, LNER cannot guarantee that they will be able to get you to your destination. Trains that are able to run are also expected to be extremely busy.

Disruption is affecting LNER, Grand Central, CrossCountry and TPE services.

Hull Trains at Selby is not affected.

It’s expected that there will be delays on services until the end of the day.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/HWxHjBQWUAERhzl-2048x1189.jpg)

Rail replacement transport has been organised to operate between:

* Northallerton and York, operated by Abbotts and York Pullman
* Northallerton and Darlington/Newcastle, operated by DMMS
* You can find the location of the rail replacement bus stops here. Please speak to station staff for further assistance.

Tickets for travel on Tuesday 10 November will be valid for use on Wednesday 11 November, so passengers who have not yet started their journey should postpone their travel until tomorrow where possible.
