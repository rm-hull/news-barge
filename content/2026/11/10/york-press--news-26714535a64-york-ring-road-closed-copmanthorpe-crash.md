* The A64 is currently closed at the Copmanthorpe roundabout after a crash.
* This is affecting traffic on the dual carriageway and the nearby ring road.
* If you've been caught up in this, please email us at [newsdesk@thepress.co.uk](mailto:newsdesk@thepress.co.uk)

## Road reopens

The A64 eastbound has now reopened, however 'residual delays remain' with queues still currently at around 10 minutes.

The AA has stated that they are increasing.

## 'Standstill traffic' in the area

Reports of the incident state that traffic is at a standstill, with delays currently at 16 minutes according to AA Traffic News.

"Affecting traffic between Tadcaster and York," said a spokesperson for the AA.
