A class trip has inspired pupils in Selby to see the future in bricks and mortar.

Year Six children from Hambleton Primary School visited Banks Homes’ Pastures development, off Gateforth Lane, where more than 60 new homes are being built.

The hour-long visit included a site tour, equipment demonstrations, and hands-on activities at the customer centre.

Ellie Waterton, a teacher at the school, said: "Being able to get a close-up look at something that’s happening so close by made everyone feel really involved, and it helped to put a lot of the classroom learning we’ve been doing around coding and materials into context.

"Everyone on the site team was absolutely amazing with the children and shared so much interesting information about what they did and how they did it – they could all be teachers.

"The children were really upbeat when we got back into class and had lots of things to say and questions to ask about everything we’d seen on site, with several of them already picking out the housebuilding jobs they want to do in the future."

The Pastures is Banks Homes’ first residential development in North Yorkshire.

It includes three-, four-, and five-bedroom homes, and two-bedroom bungalows, alongside public open space and new wildlife habitats.
