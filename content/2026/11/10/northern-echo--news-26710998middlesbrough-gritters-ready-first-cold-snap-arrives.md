Gritters are ready to roll in a North East town as the first cold snap of the season arrives.

Middlesbrough Council has confirmed that its fleet is fully stocked and prepared, with 4,800 tonnes of road salt used during 71 planned gritting operations last winter.

The council has 4,000 tonnes stored at Cargo Fleet, with more available from Boulby if needed.

Councillor Peter Gavigan, Middlesbrough Council’s executive member for environment, said: "Year in, year out our fantastic gritting crews keep our vital road network clear and safe over the winter period.

"Temperatures are expected to drop over the coming week, so they’ll be on standby around the clock to ensure we’re ready for whatever the weather can throw at us."

The council has six gritters: five cover priority routes and one is held back for breakdowns or extreme weather.

Two more grit-capable vehicles are due to arrive this season, boosting the council’s capacity.

Salt bins will return for minor roads, while extra resources will target town-centre roads and pavements when needed.
