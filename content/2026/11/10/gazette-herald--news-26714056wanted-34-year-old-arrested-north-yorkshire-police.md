A WANTED man has been arrested after a search by the county's police.

A 34-year-old man who was wanted in connection with a serious assault in Masham has now been arrested after an appeal by North Yorkshire Police published on Sunday (November 8).

As reported by The Press, the man is described as mixed race, in his 30s, with bald or very closely shaved hair and dark eyebrows.

His name was also reported at the time, but has since been removed by North Yorkshire Police.

A police spokesperson said: “The man has since been arrested. Thank you to everyone who shared our appeal and helped us reach the community.”
