From minor mishaps to full-blown disasters, here’s how to choose the right word in German for the accident you’re describing.

**What does*Missgeschick* mean?**

*Das Missgeschick* is a noun describing a minor mishap or unfortunate slip-up – generally something which is embarrassing or inconvenient, but usually not disastrous.

In German, the prefix *Miss-*usually signals that something has gone wrong. For example, the verb* missverstehen*means “to misunderstand” and* missbrauchen*means “to misuse”.* Das Geschick,*on the other hand, generally refers to skill or dexterity, as in* handwerkliches Geschick*(craftsmanship) or* ein Geschick für Sprachen*(an aptitude for languages). In more elevated language, it can also mean fate or destiny.**Why do I need to know*Missgeschick*?**

Who doesn’t suffer from the occasional mishap? *Missgeschick* is a handy word to have in your vocabulary whenever you select the wrong ticket on a machine, put salt in your coffee or confidently join the wrong queue.

It acknowledges an error without suggesting that emergency services should be called.

It can also be helpful to know the difference between *ein* * Missgeschick, ein Unfall*and* ein Unglück.*

*Ein Unfall*is a sudden physical incident, often involving injury or damage. It readily forms compounds such as* Autounfall*(car accident),* Verkehrsunfall*(traffic accident) and* Arbeitsunfall*(workplace accident).* Ein Unglück*is weightier still. The term describes a serious misfortune, tragedy or disaster such as a* Zugunglück*(train disaster),* Grubenunglück*(mining disaster) and* Schiffsunglück*(shipping disaster).**Use it like this:**

*Mir ist beim Abendessen ein kleines Missgeschick passiert.*I had a little mishap during dinner.* Durch ein dummes Missgeschick habe ich meine Schlüssel im Auto eingeschlossen.*Through a silly mishap, I managed to lock my keys in the car.* Solche Missgeschicke können jedem einmal passieren.*Mishaps like that can happen to anyone.* Ein Missgeschick in der Küche setzte den Rauchmelder in Gang.*

A kitchen mishap set off the smoke alarm.
