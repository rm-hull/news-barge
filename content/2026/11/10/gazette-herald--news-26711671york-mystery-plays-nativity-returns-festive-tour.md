The York Mystery Plays Supporters Trust is set to tour its Nativity production in November and December.

The plays, which depict the birth of Jesus from the York Cycle of Mystery Plays, will be performed at four venues.

These include St Chad's Church in Campleshon Road, St Giles' Copmanthorpe, St Olave's Church in Marygate, and All Saints North Street.

The York Mystery Plays, dating back to medieval times, consist of 48 plays originally performed by the city's guilds, narrating the Biblical story from Creation to Judgement Day.

The Nativity, a part of this heritage, is now being brought to life by the Trust for the fifth time, following successful tours over the past two years.

The Trust, a registered charity run by volunteers, is dedicated to preserving the legacy of the York Mystery Plays, which are considered a unique part of the city's cultural heritage.

Performances are scheduled for November 21 (St Chad's), November 28 (St Giles'), December 4 (St Olave's), and December 5 (All Saints North Street).

Tickets for the performances are now available for purchase via TicketSource.

The Trust hopes to engage both local residents and visitors with this piece of historical storytelling.
