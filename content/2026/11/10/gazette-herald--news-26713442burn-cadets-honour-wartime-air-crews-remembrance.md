Cadets joined residents in Burn for the village's Remembrance Service.

For the fourth consecutive year, cadets from the Combined Cadet Force at the Read School, Drax, attended the service on Sunday, November 8.

The service was led by Martin Pascoe on behalf of Burn Methodist Chapel.

Burn Parish Council chairman Alice Houlden thanked the cadets and families for their participation, saying: "Their presence adds a poignant sense of continuity and respect for those who gave so much."

Cadets laid a wreath honouring the Royal Canadian Air Force 431 "Iroquois" Squadron.

They were accompanied by Flight Lieutenant Annette Oliver, OC RAF Section, and family members.

Other wreaths and memorial crosses for RAF 578 Squadron and others were placed by members of Burn Chapel, Burn Parish Council, and the families of former RAF veterans and servicemen and women.

Burn is home to an airfield used by RAF and Royal Canadian Air Force for bomber missions during the Second World War.
