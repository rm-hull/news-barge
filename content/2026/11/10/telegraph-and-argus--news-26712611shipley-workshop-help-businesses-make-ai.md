BUSINESSES are being invited to explore how artificial intelligence can improve their daily operations.

A workshop titled How AI-Ready Is Your Business? will be held on November 20 at Shipley Enterprise Hub to help organisations understand and implement AI effectively.

Behshad Mousavi, AI trainer, consultant and founder of Digital Epix Ltd, will lead the session.

Councillor Andrew Judson, Bradford Council’s executive member for economy, regeneration and planning, said: "Artificial Intelligence offers incredible potential to drive efficiency and growth.

"This practical workshop is a great opportunity for local businesses to assess their digital readiness, and unlock the true potential of these emerging technologies."

The event will cover what successful AI adoption looks like, why some businesses struggle to gain momentum, and the practical steps that can make AI more effective.

It will also include a 60-Second AI Readiness Check, an interactive assessment that allows participants to discover which of five AI readiness levels best describes their business.

Mr Mousavi is an experienced AI trainer who helps businesses and non-technical teams use AI tools confidently.

The workshop will begin at 10am and is free to attend, but spaces are limited and booking is recommended.

The Shipley Enterprise Hub was funded by the Shipley Local Regeneration Fund, a government-funded regeneration programme that supports the delivery of economic growth and enhanced opportunities for the local community.
