A FESTIVE shopping event is set to bring seasonal cheer to Halifax this weekend.

Dean Clough’s Design Shop Christmas Shopping Sunday will take place on Sunday, November 15, from 11am to 3pm at D Mill Reception and Crossley Gallery.

Melanie Hall of Dean Clough said: "Our Christmas Shopping Sunday is designed to celebrate the exceptional talent of our local creative community while offering a fun and gentle start to the festive season.

"Whether you are looking for the perfect unique gift, wanting to participate in a creative workshop, or simply hoping to soak up the festive atmosphere with live music and great food, Dean Clough is the place to be next weekend."

Visitors can enjoy 10 per cent off all shop items, festive drinks and nibbles, and a programme of creative workshops including wreath and candle making.

Dean Clough’s resident artists will also be selling original artwork, prints, and cards.

Admission is free, though some workshops require booking.
