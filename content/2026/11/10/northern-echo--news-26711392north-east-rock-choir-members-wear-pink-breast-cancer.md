Choir members across the North East have turned rehearsals pink to raise awareness of breast cancer and show their support for a national charity.

Rock Choir members in Chester le Street, Durham, Hexham, Newcastle, Jesmond, Ponteland and Sunderland donned pink clothing as part of Breast Cancer Now’s Wear it Pink campaign, which raises awareness and funds for breast cancer research and support.

Rob Slater, Rock Choir leader, said: "‘Wear it Pink’ is something we do every year.

"Many members are affected by breast cancer and they ask me about this every October if we can support this.

"We do something simple and it starts conversations about breast cancer."

Mr Slater said: "I just think we can have a massive impact as Rock Choir and this is something small we can do to help.

"We join together for this one cause and everyone gets behind it to support Breast Cancer Now."

Breast Cancer Now is the UK’s leading breast cancer charity. It has a bold aim and hopes that by 2050, everyone with the disease will live and live well.

Breast Cancer Now doesn’t receive any government funding and relies on the generosity of supporters to carry out its life-changing work.

The money raised by ‘Wear It Pink’ could help fund a potentially life-saving research breakthrough, provide even more life-changing support, and help the charity campaign even more effectively for better services and care.

Rock Choir is an award-winning contemporary choir experience with 34 locations across Yorkshire and the North East.
