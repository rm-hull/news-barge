A young man from York was shot in a ‘terror’ attack in Krakow that killed six people.

Harry Marchant, 19, suffered a gunshot wound in the incident on Saturday (7 November).

A British man was among six people shot dead in the Polish city attack by a rampaging gunman, who is also suspected of killing three members of his own family.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/harry-marchant-york-full.webp)

Four women and two men were killed, with local officials saying three of the women and one man were Polish.

National reports said that another Briton was among 16 people injured, including the perpetrator, after he opened fire in Krakow’s Main Square on Saturday afternoon.

That person has now been revealed to be Harry.

His family today issued this update.

“We would like to confirm that our son, Harry Marchant, was injured during the incident in Poland over the weekend.

“We are pleased to say he is now in a stable condition, receiving ongoing hospital treatment.

“We can’t express how grateful we are to the first responders, medical team, and the people of Poland for their help and care for Harry.

“We ask for our privacy to be respected as we support Harry with his recovery.”

The gunman suspected of the killings died in hospital, Polish police revealed yesterday morning (Monday).

As well as Harry, four foreign nationals from Portugal, Russia, Germany and Austria were among the wounded.

Authorities also found three dead bodies at the 24-year-old suspected gunman’s family home in Ostrowiec Swietokrzyski, a city about 93 miles north-east of Krakow.

Police said on Sunday they were the bodies of his sister, mother and father, and they are believed to have been shot.

Footage on social media shows an armed police officer approaching the gunman, who appears to be wearing tactical gear and lying on the ground with a rifle beside him.

Sirens can be heard as the officer appears to fire six shots towards him before dropping to his knees.

The suspected gunman, a Polish national, was believed to have been inspired by “far-right ideologies” and was admitted to hospital after being shot by police.
