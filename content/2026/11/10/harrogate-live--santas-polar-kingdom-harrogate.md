Santa's Polar Kingdom opens at the Great Yorkshire Showground on 11 December. Daytime entry from £22.99, evenings up to £39.99, parking free. Every price.

Santa’s Polar Kingdom is a new Christmas attraction at the **Great Yorkshire
Events Centre on the Great Yorkshire Showground, HG2 8NZ**, open on** 11 days
from Friday 11 to Wednesday 23 December 2026**. Tickets are on general sale now,
from **£22.99** plus a booking fee, and the price depends on the day and on your
arrival time: anything from 3.30pm costs more. Every price below is from the
organisers’ own site and ticket pages, read on 10 November.

## The quick answer

* **Dates:** Friday 11 to Sunday 13 December, then Wednesday 16 to Wednesday 23 December.**Closed Monday 14 and Tuesday 15 December.**
* **Times:** entry from 10am, last entry 7.30pm, closes at 9pm. You book a half-hourly arrival time, but there is no time limit once you are in.
* **Cheapest days:** Friday 11 and Wednesday 16 December, from**£22.99**. Most other days start at £24.99; Monday 21 to Wednesday 23 December start at £29.99.
* **Evenings cost more:** arrivals from 3.30pm were £2 to £10 dearer on every day we checked.
* **Under-ones go free:** children aged 12 months and under. Everyone older needs a ticket, adults included.
* **Parking:** free. VIP parking next to the entrance is £6.99 a car.
* **Extras:** the Santa Express and ice skating are add-ons (skating is included with VIP), and funfair rides are paid for on the day.

A booking fee of about 10% goes on every ticket: **£2.50** on a £24.99 ticket,**£3.30** on £32.99 and**£4** on £39.99. Up to 16 tickets go on one booking.

The organisers’ FAQ page still says standard tickets start “from £19.99”. No ticket at that price appeared in the booking calendar or on any ticket page we read; the lowest was £22.99 (FAQs).

So a family of two adults and two children over a year old pays about **£110
for a daytime visit**on most days (four £24.99 tickets plus fees), and about**£176 for an evening
arrival on 23 December**. Daytime on Wednesday 16 December, about £101 for the same family, is the
cheapest way in.

## What the ticket includes

A standard ticket covers the **Elf Forest Light Trail**, the** North Pole Post
Office**,** Santa’s Workshop**, the** Santa and Mrs Claus Christmas Show**, the
Candy Cane Forest, the Ice Kingdom, the Christmas market, the après ski bar and
live shows and music
(Santa’s Polar Kingdom).

Three things cost extra:

* **The Santa Express**, the train ride into the Kingdom. Add it when you book.
* **Ice skating**, 20-minute sessions with skate hire, for ages 4 and over. Add a session when you book; it is included with VIP. Spectators watch free. Skates run from children’s size 6 to adult size 12.
* **Funfair rides**, paid for on the day.

The ticket pages we could read did not show the add-on prices for the train or the skating before checkout, so we cannot give them here yet.

**VIP** adds a queue-jump entrance, ice skating at any time and VIP parking
closest to the entrance, on top of everything in standard.

## Getting there and parking

**By car:** use HG2 8NZ. For the halls, turn off Wetherby Road onto Railway Road
at the junction by Sainsbury’s, then follow the Polar Kingdom signs. **Parking is
free**; VIP spaces by the entrance are £6.99 a car and limited each day
(plan your visit).

**By bus:** the 7 and X70 serve the showground; for the halls, use the stop on
Hookstone Drive. **By train:** from Harrogate station take the 7 bus or a taxi.
Hornbeam Park station is about 1.5 miles away, linked by the Showground Greenway
(FAQs).

Wetherby Road is the main route in from the town centre. Check our Harrogate roadworks page before you set off, and our parking guide if you are combining the visit with the town centre.

## Before you go

* **Arrive late and you still get in.** The arrival time is there to spread the queues; you may wait a short time.
* **No refunds**, but you can move your date on the organisers’ support page for a £5 fee per booking. If the new slot is cheaper, the difference is not refunded.
* **Weather:** the Kingdom is mostly indoors. It stays open in ordinary rain and cold, and not going because of normal winter weather does not qualify for a refund.
* **Food:** no outside food or drink, except baby food and milk, and food needed for medical or dietary reasons.
* **Pushchairs** are welcome but have to be left outside some attractions, including the Santa Express.
* **Dogs:** assistance dogs only.
* **Essential companions go free.** Book the rest of the group, then email the booking reference and a DLA or PIP letter or a Nimbus Card.
