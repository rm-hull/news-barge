# Raheim Jackson murder detectives quiz FIFTH man - police statement in full

## Detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have been following a number of lines of enquiry

A fifth man was being quizzed by murder detectives last night (Monday) following an "attack with weapons" in Leeds that led to the death of Raheim Jackson.

Mr Jackson, known as Reema, 38, was pronounced dead after being attacked outside a property in the New Wortley area. Several 999 crews attended following a report at 10.23pm on October 24.

Detectives from the Homicide and Major Enquiry Team have been following a number of lines of enquiry.

So far, two men have been charged and two men aged 26 and 39 have been arrested on suspicion of murder and bailed pending further enquiries. On Monday (November 9), a 29-year-old man from Leeds, was also arrested on suspicion of murder. He remains in custody at this time.
