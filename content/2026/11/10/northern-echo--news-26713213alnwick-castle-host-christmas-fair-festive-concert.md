Alnwick Castle is set to host a two-day Christmas fair and a live festive concert.

The programme begins with the Christmas Fair in the castle’s Guest Hall on Tuesday, November 24, and Wednesday, November 25.

Visitors can browse handcrafted gifts, enjoy refreshments, and soak up the festive atmosphere.

The fair opens with a preview evening on November 24, where guests receive a welcome drink from Ad Gefrin and have the first opportunity to explore the stalls, before returning on November 25 for a daytime market.

All ticket sales go to Smile for Life, the Northumberland Estates Charity of the Year, which supports disabled and disadvantaged young people.

The festive programme continues on Friday, December 4, with Christmas Greetings LIVE!

This evening of live music and ballet features violinist Matt Glossop and the Bloomsbury Soloists.

The performance offers a mix of Christmas film melodies, classical pieces, and seasonal favourites.

Two ballerinas, trained at the Bolshoi Ballet Academy, will join the musicians.

Audiences can choose between two performances, beginning at 6pm and 8.30pm.

The programme includes music from Home Alone, Love Actually, The Snowman, and Tchaikovsky’s The Nutcracker, as well as Christmas favourites associated with Nat King Cole, Bing Crosby, and Michael Bublé.

Matt Glossop has performed at the BBC Proms and worked with ensembles including the Royal Northern Sinfonia.

He has appeared on Strictly Come Dancing, the Eurovision Song Contest Final, The X Factor, and Songs of Praise.

Tickets and more information are available on the Alnwick Castle website.
