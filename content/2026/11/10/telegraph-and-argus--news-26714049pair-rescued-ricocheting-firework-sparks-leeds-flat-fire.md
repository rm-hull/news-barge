A ricocheting firework has been blamed for starting a blaze at a ground-floor flat.

The fire broke out at a property on Cross Green Avenue in Leeds this morning (Tuesday).

Fire crews were called to reports of screaming at around 12.55am.

Two people were rescued from a window by firefighters and treated at the scene for smoke inhalation.

### Full fire service statement

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service said: "Calls reporting a house on fire and that people could be heard screaming.

"On arrival this was a ground floor flat on fire.

"After the police had reviewed CCTV, the incident was deemed accidental due to a ricocheting firework.

"The two occupants that were rescued suffered with smoke inhalation and were given a check-up by the ambulance service.

"They did not attend hospital.

"Hard wired smoke detection was fitted and actuated."

Six breathing apparatus, three hose reels and a positive pressure ventilation fan were used to extinguish the fire.

Neighbouring properties were evacuated as a precaution.

West Yorkshire Police have been contacted for comment.
