A new restaurant is set to open at a popular hotel and spa following a major transformation of the venue.

The Mulberry Restaurant is preparing to welcome diners at The Parkmore Hotel and Leisure Club, on Yarm Road in Eaglescliffe, on November 20.

It comes after the Parkmore underwent what bosses described as a “complete transformation,” with work covering its bedrooms, restaurant and bar, communal areas, spa and function spaces.

The hotel, which was originally built as a home in 1896, previously announced plans to breathe new life into the venue while retaining the character and hospitality it has become known for.

Now, the hotel has unveiled The Mulberry, a new restaurant which it says will combine heritage-inspired dining with modern elegance.

Announcing the restaurant on Facebook, the hotel said: “The wait is finally over. The Mulberry has arrived.

“A new chapter. A new menu. A whole new dining experience.

“We’re opening our doors and inviting you to join us for an unforgettable launch weekend.

“Discover the charm of The Mulberry Restaurant, where heritage inspired dining meets modern elegance.”

The opening will be marked with a three-day launch weekend, with customers offered 25 per cent off all food and drinks on the Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

“Three days of incredible food, beautiful wines and a completely new dining experience.

“Whether it’s a romantic dinner, a long overdue catch up or simply an excuse to indulge, there’s a seat waiting for you at The Mulberry.

“Be amongst the first to experience The Mulberry.

“Book your table and celebrate with us.”
