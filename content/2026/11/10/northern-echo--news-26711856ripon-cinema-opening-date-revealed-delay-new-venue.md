A luxury cinema bringing the big screen back to a North Yorkshire town has revealed when it expects to welcome its first customers after its planned pre-Christmas opening was delayed.

The eagerly awaited three-screen cinema is expected to open in Ripon in February or March 2027 as refurbishment continues on Fishergate.

The 10,000 sq ft venue will have three screens, a lounge serving food and drink, blockbusters, independent films and live theatre and opera screenings.

With 157 seats and eight to ten screenings a day, the cinema aims to become a major attraction for Ripon and the surrounding area.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689763.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A mock-up of how the Ripon cinema will look *(Image: Ripon BID/Visit Ripon)*

John Tate, who is developing the cinema with business partner John Hewitt, has confirmed the new timetable for the venue.

He said: "We had originally intended to have a pre-Christmas opening, but as is often the case with large reconfiguration and refurbishment works of this nature, there have been a couple of issues that we needed to resolve."

Despite the delay, the pair remain confident the cinema will open in early 2027.

Mr Tate said: "We are now on schedule for an opening in early 2027 and are looking forward to welcoming filmgoers from Ripon and neighbouring towns and villages to come and experience the wow factor of watching the latest releases and screenings from the National Theatre and Royal Opera House in the sumptuous surroundings that we are creating in Fishergate."

Alongside mainstream releases, the venue will show independent films and event screenings for audiences seeking something different.

Screenings are planned from 10.30am to 8pm, targeting families with young children as well as theatre and opera fans.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21689764.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A poster for the Ripon cinema *(Image: Ripon BID/Visit Ripon)*

A lounge will serve food and drinks before or after screenings.

The project is the latest expansion by Mr Tate and Mr Hewitt, who already run successful independent cinemas elsewhere in Yorkshire.

Their Ilkley Film Theatre won Cinema of the Year at the Big Screen Awards in 2022, seven years after they opened it.

The pair bought Wetherby’s historic Caxton Street cinema in 2018 and added a screen and stylish lounge to the 1915 building.

Now they hope to repeat that success in Ripon.

Mr Tate said: "We have been extremely encouraged by the warm welcome our plans have received in this wonderful and historic city and we hope that the cinema's opening will provide a boost to the local economy."

Ripon BID manager Lilla Bathurst welcomed the investment, saying businesses across the city had backed the plans from the start.

She added: "The investment by two seasoned and award-winning cinema operators is a further sign of the confidence that investors like them and Sterne Properties Limited have in our magnificent Cathedral City of the Dales."

The cinema is expected to announce its opening date, first screenings and ticket details in the coming months.
