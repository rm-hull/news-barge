A £15m investment has delivered new facilities at an RAF base in North Yorkshire as a key military unit responsible for helping protect UK airspace relocates there.

The Air Command and Control Force, which moved from RAF Scampton, will now be based at RAF Leeming in Northallerton.

The unit is responsible for 24/7 air surveillance of the UK and assigned NATO airspace.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692397.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The handover marks the first completed scheme under a major Ministry of Defence contract to modernise the Defence estate *(Image: Crown Copyright)*

Nicola Bond, project manager at the Defence Infrastructure Organisation (DIO), said: "Air C2 Force can now complete their move to RAF Leeming, where we have delivered new sustainable infrastructure to support their vital work.

"Since relocating from RAF Scampton in 2022, Air C2 Force has been based at RAF Leeming, RAF Boulmer and RRH Staxton Wold, and it is wonderful to be bringing this journey to a successful conclusion."

The relocation marks the completion of the first project under a new Ministry of Defence contract intended to modernise and consolidate the Defence estate.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692400.jpg?type=mds-article-620)New sustainable facilities at RAF Leeming include office space and a 32-bed accommodation block for Air C2 Force personnel *(Image: Crown Copyright)*

Construction began in September 2025, delivering new office space and a 32-bed single living accommodation block.

The scheme was led by the DIO, with Galliford Try delivering the design and construction.

Arcadis supported the project with design assurance.

The offices and accommodation buildings were constructed using pre-cast concrete for improved air tightness and energy efficiency.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692402.jpg?type=mds-article-620)he handover marks the first completed scheme under a major Ministry of Defence contract to modernise the Defence estate *(Image: Crown Copyright)*

They are heated by air source heat pumps, partly powered by solar panels, and use harvested rainwater to reduce environmental impact.

The Air C2 Force directs military air systems to protect the UK and its NATO allies.

The project was delivered under the Defence Estate Optimisation (DEO) Portfolio, the MOD’s largest estates transformation programme.

![](https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/resources/images/21692403.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Galliford Try also refurbished RAF Leeming’s Cinebowl, which provides a cinema and ten-pin bowling for service families *(Image: Crown Copyright)*

The scheme was the first to break ground and reach completion under the 2023 contract, which groups multiple regional projects to encourage best practice and consistent, sustainable building standards.

A handover ceremony was attended by Air Vice-Marshal Anthony Lyle, Air Officer Commanding 2 Group, and Air Commodore Simon Blackwell, Commander Air Bases.

Ms Bond said the project faced challenges but credited its success to strong collaboration.

She said: "This project, like any, has brought challenges but the collaboration between the RAF, DIO and external partners has been key to a successful build completion and handover, and I am very proud of the infrastructure my team has delivered."

In addition to the core facilities, Galliford Try refurbished the site's Cinebowl as part of a social value initiative.

The Cinebowl offers entertainment and recreation for service families, including a cinema and ten-pin bowling.
