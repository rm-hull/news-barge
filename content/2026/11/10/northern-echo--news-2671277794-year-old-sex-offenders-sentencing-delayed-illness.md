A 94-year-old sex offender will have to wait for his sentence after falling ill in court.

Donald Gates was convicted of five charges involving the sexual abuse of a young girl at Teesside Crown Court.

Recorder Andrew Haslam KC adjourned the hearing over concerns about Gates’s ability to take part.

Gates, of New Park, Newfield, Bishop Auckland, was convicted of two charges of sexual activity with a child, two of causing a child to engage in sexual activity and one of sexual assault by touching.

The judge apologised to the victim and adjourned sentencing after Gates fell ill.

Gates will be sentenced on January 26.
