## EastEnders character leaves life behind to start afresh

EastEnders star Ellie Dadd is set to depart the show with her character, Amy Mitchell, leaving for Norfolk *(Image: BBC iPlayer)*\* This article contains spoilers \*

An EastEnders character at the heart of a gripping storyline has relocated.

Amy Mitchell, played by Ellie Dadd in the BBC soap, has been dealing with the aftermath of escaping from abuser Craig Ingram, whom she left for dead.

Struggling with the belief she killed him, Amy was initially supposed to be taken to Ibiza by Sean Slater.

But in Monday's episode, Jack Branning intervened and arranged for her to stay with Bianca Jackson and Sonia Fowler in Norfolk.

They had both returned to the UK after travelling since their exit last year.

The emotional storyline saw Jack promising to track down Craig to ensure Amy's safety.

Dadd, who has been in the soap since 2022, is taking a short break but will reportedly return later this year, according to The Sun.
