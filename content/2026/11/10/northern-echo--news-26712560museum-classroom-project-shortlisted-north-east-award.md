A pioneering project bringing museum objects into the classroom to inspire young readers and writers has been shortlisted for a North East culture award.

The Museums In Your Classroom programme is a partnership between Middlesbrough Museums and Beech Grove Primary School, and has been nominated in the Best Arts and Education Partnership category at the North East Culture Awards.

Launched in 2025 and supported by the National Literacy Trust, the project uses real museum artefacts to support the development of reading, writing and storytelling skills.
