Melania Trump is selling digital pictures of herself — and one could be yours for $150.

The first lady made the announcement of the “exclusive collection” of snaps shot by German photographer and former model Elle von Unwerth in a statement on social media over the weekend.

The pictures are digital, so there are no physical copies to hang around the home or keep in a wallet with the family; no, these are “visual portraits.”

It’s not as random as it may seem — the pictures are a new business venture intertwined with the debut of the first lady’s Amazon MGM series, a two-part documentary titled *Melania, The Series*.

![The first lady made the announcement of the “exclusive collection” of snaps shot by German photographer and former model Elle von Unwerth](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/16/2306006982..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Melania walked the red carpet for the premiere of the series at Lincoln Center’s Alice Tully Hall in New York on Thursday, but she had to go stag, since President Donald Trump was still on his way back from China.

The *Melania*series arrives on the heels of her film documentary,* Melania*, which was directed by disgraced director Brett Ratner, who was blacklisted in Hollywood during the #MeToo movement when he was accused of sexually harassing multiple women. Ratner vehemently denied the allegations.

The doc was a hit, and now it seems Ratner is set to make big-budget flicks again, since it was announced that he would direct the long-awaited *Rush Hour 4*— news that was made shortly after President Trump said via social media that he missed old-school buddy cop films, like the* Rush Hour* series.

Jackie Chan and Chris Tucker are expected to return for the film, which will be distributed by Paramount, which is run by the Trump-friendly David Ellison.
