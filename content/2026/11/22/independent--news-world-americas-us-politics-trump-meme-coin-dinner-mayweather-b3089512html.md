President Donald Trump has lined up a roster of celebrity guests for a gala dinner Sunday celebrating his slumping meme coin.

Canadian entrepreneur and Shark Tank cast member Kevin O’Leary and former five-division world boxing champion Floyd Mayweather are among those expected to attend, *The Wall Street Journal* reported.

Former Cleveland Cavaliers star Tristan Thompson and Israeli mentalist Lior Suchard are also slated to join the event.

The dinner at Trump’s golf club in Virginia is for 185 holders of $TRUMP, the meme coin he launched shortly before his inauguration.

Invitations are awarded through a competition that ranks participants by the size of their holdings and how long they hold them. Securing a seat at the first gala, in May 2025, required at least $55,000 in $TRUMP holdings. This time, one participant qualified with just $518, *The Washington Post* previously reported.

![President Donald Trump has lined up a roster of celebrity guests for a gala dinner Sunday celebrating his slumping meme coin](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/20/48/GettyImages-2305531745.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

The sharply reduced threshold comes amid the coin’s plunge to a record low. After surging to about $74 within hours of its launch in January 2025, the token fell to roughly $17 a month later. By Sunday, it was trading at about 89 cents, according to Coinbase.

Hundreds of thousands of buyers have lost a combined $3.8 billion, *The New York Times* reported in July.

Trump has long brushed aside concerns about his meme coin, which reportedly netted him $635 million in royalties last year. Asked last May whether he was profiting from cryptocurrency, he said, “I haven’t even looked.”

In July, he said: “There’s nothing illegal. There’s nothing wrong with it.”

![Canadian entrepreneur and Shark Tank cast member Kevin O’Leary is slated to attend the gala in Virginia Sunday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/20/03/GettyImages-2304647412.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)
![Former five-division world boxing champion Floyd Mayweather, who has publicly backed Trump, is among those expected to attend Sunday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/20/31/GettyImages-2300243196.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

But his crypto businesses could soon face greater scrutiny. After midterm victories fueled in part by voters’ concerns about corruption, Democrats have vowed to investigate his ventures.

A group of Democratic senators has described the latest gala as an apparent “pay-to-play scheme.”

“This dinner and scoring mechanism appear carefully constructed to increase investment in $TRUMP, allowing the President to abuse the power of his office to personally enrich himself, as he has throughout this term,” they wrote in a letter to the White House.
