## Popular business plans 'Bridgerton' makeover for Grade II listed York building

Stonegate today *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

A POPULAR bakery is set to bring some ‘Bridgerton magic’ to York city centre as it gets ready to open the doors in one of the city’s most historic streets.

Qream T's House – a new bakery run by Turner and Co. specialising in cakes and sweet treats – has revealed that it will be ‘embracing the history’ of Grade II listed 22 Stonegate with a goal of opening before Christmas.

The business, which currently has two locations in Mirfield and Beverley, offers a range of handmade cakes, hot puddings, sandwich options, brunch dishes, and afternoon tea – alongside various hot and cold drinks.

In a post to social media, owner Tommy said that he was hoping to take visitors ‘back in time’ with his new location – taking inspiration from Downton Abbey, Bridgerton, and hoping to create an atmosphere that is ‘old-fashioned, classic, elegant and a little bit Manor House’.

He also added that he would be paying tribute to his mother, renovating the shop floor room into ‘Hilary’s Library’.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21748007.jpg?type=mds-article-620)The building that Qream T's will be moving into *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

He said: “Think floor-to-ceiling bookcases, rich dark greens, antique golds, beautiful blacks and elegant floral touches. A little bit of old-world charm, a little bit of Bridgerton, and plenty of character.

“We’re going to embrace the history of this beautiful Grade II listed building, keeping its original charm and adding our own little Manor House magic.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21748006.jpg?type=mds-article-620)A sign in the window of the cakery *(Image: Alice Kavanagh)*

“And of course, this room has a special name for a very special reason. Named after my mum, Hilary.

“It’s currently looking a little more renovation than romance, but just wait until you see what we have planned… Every room at Qream T’s House will have a story to tell.”
