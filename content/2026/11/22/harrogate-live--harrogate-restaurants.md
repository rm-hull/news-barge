We checked 12 Harrogate restaurants, from Michelin-starred Fifty Two to the Drum and Monkey's seafood, for prices, hours and booking. Updated November 2026.

Harrogate has 12 restaurants worth booking in 2026, and this guide puts them in one place with prices, opening hours and what each kitchen is known for, from two Michelin stars to a seafood restaurant that has been in Montpellier Gardens for half a century.

## Overview

* **Michelin stars:** FIFTY TWO at Rudding Park, starred for the first time in February 2026, and Shaun Rankin at Grantley Hall, starred since 2021. Both serve tasting menus only.
* **In the Michelin Guide without a star:** Paradise Café at Killinghall, The General Tarleton at Ferrensby, the Grantley Arms and Where there’s Smoke in Masham.
* **Town centre stalwarts:** the Drum and Monkey for seafood, William & Victoria for classic British cooking, The Orchid for pan-Asian food and Sukhothai for Thai.
* **Cheapest good lunch:** William & Victoria’s set lunch, two courses for £23.
* **Closed on Sundays:** the Drum and Monkey and Paradise Café.

Every entry was checked against the restaurant’s own website on 22 November 2026.

## Restaurants in Harrogate town centre

### 1. The Drum and Monkey, Montpellier Gardens

The Drum and Monkey is a seafood restaurant in the Montpellier Quarter, where it has been, it says, for more than 50 years. The menu follows what is in season and what the boats land, from clams to lobster, and the Carter family, who took it over in 2013, own and run it themselves.

It is a short walk from the Royal Hall and the conference centre, and there is a separate lunch and early bird menu.

**Open** lunch Mon to Fri 12pm to 1.45pm, Sat 12pm to 2pm; dinner Mon to Sat 5.30pm to 9pm; closed Sun**Booking** by phone or online**Call** 01423 502650**Address** 5 Montpellier Gardens, Harrogate, HG1 2TF**Website** drumandmonkey.co.uk**Good for** seafood, lunch and early bird menu, free on-street parking in the evening

### 2. William & Victoria, Cold Bath Road

William & Victoria describes itself as “Harrogate’s longest running wine bar”, opened in the 1980s as the town’s first, with a restaurant serving “simple, classic dishes celebrating Yorkshire produce”. The main menu runs from devilled lambs’ kidneys and moules marinière to Lamb Henry and steaks, and the set lunch and early bird menu, served alongside it, is the best-value meal in this guide.

Sunday lunch runs all afternoon, and there is a smaller tapas menu in the bar.

**Food** set lunch Mon to Sat 12pm to 2.30pm; early bird Mon to Sat 5pm to 6.30pm; Sunday lunch 12pm to 6pm**Price** mains £16.95 to £39.95; set lunch and early bird two courses £23, three £28; roast sirloin £22.95**Booking** online for up to eight; call for larger groups**Call** 01423 506883**Address** 6 Cold Bath Road, Harrogate, HG2 0NA**Website** williamandvictoria.com**Good for** set lunch, Sunday lunch, wine list, private dining

### 3. The Orchid, Swan Road

The Orchid is the pan-Asian restaurant of the Studley Hotel, next to the Valley Gardens, and has been open since 2001. Upstairs is The O Bar for a cocktail before dinner, and the restaurant also does takeaway.

It opens for lunch and dinner seven days a week and all afternoon on Sunday. The building is Grade II listed: the restaurant says it has some step-free access and asks you to ring to check what suits.

**Open** Mon to Thu 12pm to 2pm and 5pm to 9pm; Fri and Sat 12pm to 2pm and 5pm to 10pm; Sun 12pm to 8.30pm**Booking** online**Call** 01423 560425**Address** 28 Swan Road, Harrogate, HG1 2SE**Parking** three-hour disc zone; discs from the restaurant**Website** orchidrestaurant.co.uk**Good for** pan-Asian food, cocktail bar upstairs, takeaway, open Sunday

### 4. Sukhothai, Cheltenham Parade

Sukhothai is a small Yorkshire Thai group, founded by chef and owner Ban Kaewkraikhot, who opened the first one in Chapel Allerton, Leeds, in 2002; there are now three, in Chapel Allerton, Leeds city centre and Harrogate. The Cheltenham Parade dining room is a short walk from the town centre, and the menu is the same as in Leeds, with a wine list and signature cocktails.

It is evenings only, and it does click and collect.

**Open** Mon to Thu 5pm to 10pm; Fri and Sat 5pm to 11pm**Booking** online**Call** 01423 500869**Address** 17-19 Cheltenham Parade, Harrogate, HG1 1DD**Website** sukhothai.co.uk/harrogate**Good for** Thai food, cocktails, click and collect

## Rudding Park, Follifoot

### 5. FIFTY TWO

FIFTY TWO won its first Michelin star in February 2026, which made it the only starred restaurant in Harrogate itself. There is no menu: guests sit at five tables of shared seating facing an open kitchen and are served a run of snacks, bread and plates built on what is growing in the 52 raised beds of the hotel’s kitchen garden.

The Michelin inspectors single out the desserts, and during pudding each table picks a track for “Dessert Island Discs”. Dietary requirements need 48 hours’ notice.

**Open** lunch Fri and Sat, arrive 12.45pm; dinner Wed to Sat 7pm, Sun 6pm**Price** lunch six courses £75; dinner ten courses £135; drinks pairings £50 to £65**Booking** online, for set sitting times**Call** 01423 871350**Address** Rudding Park, Follifoot, Harrogate, HG3 1JH**Website** fiftytworuddingpark.co.uk**Good for** Michelin star, tasting menu, open kitchen, kitchen garden walk before dinner

### 6. Clocktower, Rudding Park

The Clocktower is Rudding Park’s everyday restaurant: a brasserie, a bar and a conservatory built around a 400-year-old olive tree, with a terrace outside. The all-day menu is brasserie dishes and what the hotel calls “seasonally inspired Yorkshire favourites”; dinner runs from scallops and steak tartare to roast cod and duck breast.

Sunday lunch is a fixed price, served family style with Yorkshire puddings, and there is live acoustic music on Friday evenings.

**Food** 12pm to 9pm Mon to Sat; Sunday lunch 12pm to 5pm**Price** dinner mains £18 to £31; Sunday lunch two courses £36, three £43.50**Booking** online; the hotel says tables must be booked**Call** 01423 871350**Address** Rudding Park, Follifoot, Harrogate, HG3 1JH**Website** ruddingpark.co.uk/clocktower**Good for** Sunday lunch, terrace, children's menus, live music Fridays

## Near Knaresborough and Killinghall

### 7. The General Tarleton, Ferrensby

The General Tarleton is an 18th-century coaching inn on the Harrogate Road at Ferrensby, reopened in August 2025 by the chef Tommy Banks’s Jeopardy Hospitality, and added to the Michelin Guide in April 2026. The executive chef, Aled Williams, came from Northcote in Lancashire, and the menu reworks pub classics: the Yorkshire Post singled out the steak and Black Sheep ale pie, and The Times the sourdough treacle tart.

There are eight bedrooms upstairs and a monthly pub quiz.

**Food** lunch Wed to Sat 12pm to 2.30pm; dinner Mon to Sat 5.30pm to 8.30pm; Sunday roasts 12pm to 6pm**Price** festive set menu three courses £55, from 16 November**Booking** online or by phone**Call** 01423 297050**Address** Harrogate Road, Ferrensby, Knaresborough, HG5 0PZ**Website** generaltarletonferrensby.co.uk**Good for** Michelin Guide, Sunday roast, terrace, rooms, private dining in The Hayloft

### 8. Paradise Café, Killinghall

Paradise is a café at Daleside Nurseries on the Ripon Road, run by Frances Atkins, Roger Olive and John Tullett, who describe themselves as a former Michelin-starred team that has worked together for more than 20 years. It serves breakfast, brunch and lunch from menus that change with the season, with a cake counter for the rest of the day, and opens for supper on Friday nights.

The Michelin Guide lists it, noting the terrace over the lake behind the garden centre.

**Open** Tue to Sat 9am to 5pm; supper Fri 6pm to 8.30pm; closed Sun and Mon (ring to check, as the home page also gives other hours)**Booking** by phone; recommended for lunch**Call** 01423 877109**Address** Daleside Nurseries, Ripon Road, Killinghall, Harrogate, HG3 2AY**Website** paradisewithfrj.co.uk**Good for** Michelin Guide, brunch and lunch, dogs welcome, wheelchair accessible, chef's table

## Ripon, Masham and Nidderdale

### 9. Shaun Rankin at Grantley Hall, near Ripon

Shaun Rankin’s restaurant at Grantley Hall has held a Michelin star since 2021, awarded 18 months after it opened. Dinner begins with drinks and snacks in the drawing room before the move into what was the house’s music room, and there is only the “Taste of Home” tasting menu, built on Yorkshire produce, most of it, the hotel says, from within 30 miles, plus the estate’s kitchen garden.

Vegetarian and pescatarian versions are offered; there is no vegan menu.

**Open** Wed to Sat, dinner 6.30pm to 8.30pm**Price** six courses £165; ten courses £210; wine pairings from £80, non-alcoholic from £55; 12.5% service added**Booking** online**Call** 01765 620070**Address** Grantley Hall, Ripon, HG4 3ET**Website** grantleyhall.co.uk/bars-restaurants/shaun-rankin-at-grantley-hall**Good for** Michelin star, tasting menu, special occasions, over-12s only, smart casual dress

### 10. Grantley Arms, Grantley

The Grantley Arms is Grantley Hall’s village pub, a restored 17th-century inn that went into the Michelin Guide in April 2026; the inspectors mention the beef and ale pie and the trifle. The kitchen does a full à la carte, from fish and chips and a steak and Hambleton ale pie to halibut and lobster, and a Sunday roast.

Walk-ins are welcome, though the pub suggests booking for meals at weekends.

**Food** Mon to Sat 12pm to 2.30pm and 5.30pm to 8.30pm; Sun 12pm to 6pm; light bites Thu to Sat 2.30pm to 5pm**Price** mains £26 to £42; Sunday roasts £29 to £36**Booking** recommended at weekends**Call** 01765 699946**Address** Grantley, Ripon, HG4 3PJ**Website** grantleyarms.co.uk**Good for** Michelin Guide, Sunday roast, dogs in the bar, terrace and garden, children welcome

### 11. Where there’s Smoke, Masham

Where there’s Smoke, known as WTS, cooks everything over coals in a small dining room on Silver Street, and was in the Michelin Guide six months after it opened. The chef-owner, Jon Atashroo, is also a potter, and the Michelin inspectors note that he made the tabletops too.

There is one set menu, changed weekly with what is in season, and the restaurant asks for three days’ notice to cook around a dietary need.

**Open** Thu to Sat 6.30pm to 10pm**Booking** essential, online; email or call for six or more**Call** 01765 689000**Address** 7 Silver Street, Masham, HG4 4DX**Website** wts.restaurant**Good for** Michelin Guide, cooking over fire, set menu, rooms

### 12. SHK, North Street, Ripon

SHK is what Storehouse Kitchen on North Street has become. Iain and Kirsty Wilkinson took it over in November 2025 and have turned it into what they call “Ripon’s home of smokehouse, country and damn good food”: smoked meats, burgers, steaks and cocktails, with bottomless brunch and live music nights.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Harrogate restaurants have a Michelin star?

Two in the district. FIFTY TWO at Rudding Park, Follifoot, won its first star in February 2026, and Shaun Rankin at Grantley Hall, near Ripon, has held one since 2021. The Michelin Guide also lists Paradise Café at Daleside Nurseries in Killinghall, The General Tarleton at Ferrensby, the Grantley Arms and, in Masham, Where there’s Smoke.

### Where is good to eat in Harrogate town centre?

The Drum and Monkey, a seafood restaurant in Montpellier Gardens for more than 50 years; William & Victoria on Cold Bath Road, which calls itself Harrogate’s first wine bar; The Orchid on Swan Road for pan-Asian food since 2001; and Sukhothai on Cheltenham Parade for Thai.

### Where can I get a set lunch in Harrogate?

William & Victoria serves a set lunch and early bird menu, two courses £23 or three for £28, Monday to Saturday from 12pm to 2.30pm and 5pm to 6.30pm. The Drum and Monkey has a lunch and early bird menu, and FIFTY TWO’s six-course Friday and Saturday lunch is £75.

### Which Harrogate restaurants are open on Sunday?

William & Victoria (Sunday lunch 12pm to 6pm), The Orchid (12pm to 8.30pm), the Clocktower at Rudding Park (Sunday lunch 12pm to 5pm), The General Tarleton (Sunday roasts 12pm to 6pm), the Grantley Arms (food 12pm to 6pm) and FIFTY TWO for dinner. The Drum and Monkey and Paradise Café are closed on Sundays.
