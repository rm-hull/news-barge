# What it is like behind-the-scenes at Emmerdale as legend teases Christmas 2026 spoilers

## This comes after 2027 Emmerdale Village Tour plans have been revealed

Emmerdale is loved by millions and we have a little look at what it is like behind-the-scenes which is sure to impress fans.

We always look to bring you the latest gossip and features from the ITV soap. Sometimes, we get this by paying a visit to the show’s studios for special press days.

In this feature, you can enjoy a glimpse from our latest visit to the show’s purpose-built Yorkshire village. Taking in some of the sets and speaking to some of Emmerdale’s biggest stars.

While some of the sets can’t be visited by the public, the village itself can be. The Emmerdale Village Tour regularly welcomes devotees of the legendary programme - you can find out more about its 2027 tour dates here.

And, do you want a little bit of Emmerdale spoilers and gossip? Of course you do.

Well, we recently spoke to a true icon about what fans can expect this Christmas. Lisa Riley, 50, who is known to millions of Emmerdale fans as Mandy Dingle, has provided a tease of what is coming for Christmas 2026 for the Dingle clan.

The star, who first joined Emmerdale back in 1995, informed us that she has recently been filming festive scenes. When asked if we can expect a dramatic Christmas for the Dingles, Lisa replied: “Yeah, it is. There’s a massive clip doing the rounds from the red carpet (at the Pride of Britain Awards) and it is true.

“The Christmas Day episode - when I read it my jaw fell. When we shot it, my jaw fell.

“It is so true. I played it as Mandy (when finding out). It is all about the jaw drop - not the mic drop.”
