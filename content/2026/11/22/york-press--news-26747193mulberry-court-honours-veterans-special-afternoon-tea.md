## Care home honours veterans and serving Armed Forces personnel

Mulberry Court, run by Barchester Healthcare, hosted a special afternoon tea, bringing together serving members of the Armed Forces *(Image: Barchester)*

A care home has honoured Armed Forces personnel and veterans.

Mulberry Court, run by Barchester Healthcare, hosted a special afternoon tea bringing together serving members of the Armed Forces, veterans and residents for an afternoon of conversation, friendship and shared memories.

The event also welcomed representatives of the Association of Wrens (WRNS) and provided an opportunity for residents to hear stories from different generations of service personnel.

Jane Hughes-Cook, general manager at Mulberry Court, said: "We were incredibly proud to welcome members of our Armed Forces community to Mulberry Court.

"It was a truly special afternoon, filled with conversation, friendship and the sharing of memories.

"Our residents thoroughly enjoyed the opportunity to meet those who have served our country, and we hope everyone left knowing how much their service and sacrifice are appreciated."

Ms Hughes-Cook said the tea was organised as a gesture of thanks to those who have served, reflecting the home’s belief in "celebrating people, bringing generations together and recognising the remarkable contributions individuals have made to our community and our country".

Mulberry Court provides nursing and residential care and is part of Barchester Healthcare, one of the UK’s largest care providers.

Barchester Healthcare is committed to delivering high-quality care across its network of homes and hospitals.
