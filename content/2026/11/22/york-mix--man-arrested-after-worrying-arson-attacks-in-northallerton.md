Police today (Sunday) issued an update on their investigation into arson attacks in Northallerton.

As we reported last week, there were two fires on the town’s High Street, one at a residential property at 11.44pm on Monday, 16 November, and one at the rear of a business premises at 2.44am the next morning.

They are now being linked along with a third incident, involving a skip outside a business at Standard Way Business Park, which happened during the same period.

This was reported to the police at a later time.

Investigating detectives then released CCTV images of a man they wanted to talk to.

In their update, police say they arrested a 49-year-old man at a property in the Bradford area yesterday (Saturday).

He’s in custody for questioning.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “We thank residents and the business community in Northallerton for their support to the investigation.

“We know this has been a worrying time for the town.

“We will keep you updated as the investigation progresses.

“The appeal for witnesses and information is continuing.”
