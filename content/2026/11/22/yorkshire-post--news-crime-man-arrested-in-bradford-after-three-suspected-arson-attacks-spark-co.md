# Man arrested in Bradford after three suspected arson attacks spark concern in Yorkshire town

## A man has been arrested in West Yorkshire following three suspected arson attacks in Northallerton, including a fire at a home where a resident was forced to escape.

At around 11.44pm on Monday (Nov 16), firefighters were called to a residential property on Northallerton High Street, near All Saints Church, following reports of a fire.

The occupant was alerted by a fire alarm and managed to escape unharmed.

Just hours later, at around 2.44am on Tuesday (Nov 17), fire crews attended a second fire at the rear of business premises on the High Street.

A third suspected arson incident, involving a skip outside a business at Standard Way Business Park, is believed to have taken place during the same period but was reported to police at a later date.

North Yorkshire Police launched an investigation and released CCTV images of a man they wanted to speak to in connection with the incidents.

The images showed a man wearing distinctive clothing and carrying what appeared to be a large backpack.

Officers believed the same man appeared in all the images, although he appeared to have changed his clothing.

![Four men charged](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/16/15/53/Police-officer-stock-image.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=420&quality=65)


Following extensive enquiries, police arrested a 49-year-old man at an address in the Bradford area on Saturday (Nov 21) evening.

He was being held in police custody for questioning.

Police thanked residents and businesses for their support during the investigation, acknowledging the concern the incidents had caused in the town.

Increased police patrols have also been taking place to reassure the local community.

Officers are continuing to appeal for witnesses and anyone with CCTV, doorbell or dashcam footage that may have captured the incidents or those involved.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Police said:”If you can help in any way, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

“Please quote reference number 12260225561 when providing details. “
