# Man, 62, dies in hospital after serious street assault as murder probe launched

## A 62-year-old man has died in hospital following a serious assault in Halifax, with detectives now investigating the circumstances surrounding his death.

Haji Asghar Ali died on Saturday (Nov 21), four days after he was seriously injured in an assault on Gibbet Street in Halifax.

The incident took place on Tuesday (Nov 17), with Mr Ali taken to hospital in a critical condition.

The circumstances leading to his death are now being investigated by the force's Homicide and Major Enquiry Team.

![Haji Asghar Ali](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjA3OTE5ZWEwLTUxNzgtNGE3YS04MzA5LTQ2ZmQwMzliZTI4NjoxY2FhZWZkYS0xZWY1LTQ5YTctYjg2Mi03OTdmNGU5Y2IyM2U=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


Awais Haroon, 35, of Halifax, was charged on Thursday (Nov 19) with wounding with intent in connection with the assault on Mr Ali.

He was also charged with racially or religiously aggravated common assault on a police officer, threatening to damage or destroy property, criminal damage, racially or religiously aggravated harassment, alarm or distress, possession of a Class B drug and possession of a Class C drug.

Haroon appeared at Bradford Magistrates' Court on Thursday (Nov 19), where he was remanded in custody.

He is due to appear at Bradford Crown Court on Thursday (Dec 17), when Mr Ali's death will be addressed by the court.

Haroon remains in custody ahead of the hearing.

Police enquiries into the circumstances surrounding Mr Ali's death are continuing.
