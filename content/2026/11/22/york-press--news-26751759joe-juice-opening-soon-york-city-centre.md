**A POPULAR drink, coffee, and sandwich chain is taking shape in York city centre.**

Joe & The Juice in Parliament Street, York city centre, looks to be one step closer to opening its doors after new decals have appeared in the shop windows stating that it will be opening soon.

The popular chain has also listed two roles, for assistant store manager and a supervisor job, on their website.

The listing states that successful applicants will become a part of their “international network of Juicers who support each other, celebrate wins together, and push each other to grow”.

New signs put up in the windows read ‘Ouse Up for a Juice?’ and state it will be opening soon, with workers spotted coming in and out of the building.

The opening of Joe and the Juice follows the closure of Size? in September, which was in the site for eight years before the Local Democracy Reporting Service revealed that plans had been submitted to convert the store in early June.

The chain, which was founded in Copenhagen in 2002 and now has over 500 stores across the world, will offer juices, shakes, coffees, and sandwiches – wrapped in ‘iconic’ pink packaging.
