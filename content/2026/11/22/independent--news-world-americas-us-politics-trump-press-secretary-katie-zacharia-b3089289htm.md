President Donald Trump suggested in a Fox News interview Saturday that he was considering “four or five” finalists for White House press secretary.

Trump also defended Katie Zacharia, who was fired after just five days in the role following a widely panned debut briefing. The position has remained vacant since her October 16 ouster.

During the interview, host Kayleigh McEnany asked, “Do you think we’ll know who the new press secretary is before the new year?”

“Well, I have four or five people,” Trump, speaking by phone, responded. “I can tell you I have a hundred people that want that job as you can imagine. OK? A hundred people.”

He added: “We had one who’s a very good person, Katie. She’s a fantastic person ... But I saw the way she was being treated. I said, ‘Katie you don’t need this.’”

![President Donald Trump hinted that he has 'four or five' finalists to become the next White House press secretary during an interview with Fox News on November 21](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/20/17/download.-3..jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Zacharia, a former Newsmax commentator, drew criticism from Democrats and Republicans after her 14-minute debut briefing, during which she took questions from just three journalists and mistakenly referred to her husband as “my president.”

She also faced scrutiny for describing herself as a lawyer despite never passing a bar exam, and is now under investigation by the State Bar of California.

“She might’ve been able to fight her way out of it,” Trump continued. “But why should she be put in that position? ‘Cause I think she might’ve actually been very good. But I said ‘what do you need this for?’”

He continued: “They really came at her. I was surprised to see it. Nasty. So maybe that’s over with now and somebody else would come in and they’ll be a little bit more normalized.”

After her ouster, Zacharia defended her performance, saying she was not given enough time to prepare. Asked by *The Free Press* this week whether she would take the job again, she responded: “There’s just no way that I would say no to that man.”

![Katie Zacharia was fired after just five days in the role following a widely panned debut briefing. The position has remained vacant since her October 16 ouster](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/10/31/17/58/GettyImages-2299988078.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Names floated among political analysts as potential new hires in recent weeks include Danielle Alvarez, a former senior adviser to Trump’s 2024 campaign, and Deputy Press Secretary Anna Kelly.

Earlier this month, Trump also offered the job to UFC president and CEO Dana White, who declined. Trump later said he had been joking.

Zacharia’s predecessor, Karoline Leavitt, served as the president’s chief spokesperson for 19 months before leaving in August to spend more time with her family.

Trump had four press secretaries during his first term: Sean Spicer, Sarah Huckabee Sanders, Stephanie Grisham and McEnany, who is now a Fox News personality.
