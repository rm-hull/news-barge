Ngumoha stars as Liverpool thrash 10-man Man Utd

**"Sacked in the morning, you're getting sacked in the morning..."**

Fourteen minutes in, arms folded, two goals down at Liverpool and with his Manchester United team reduced to 10 men, Mauricio Pochettino could not ignore the cruel taunts echoing around Anfield.

What on earth must Pochettino have been thinking as he stood at the edge of his technical area?

In distance terms, he was 3,800 miles from his last managerial assignment against Haiti in Cincinnati in the early hours of Wednesday morning UK time.

In difficulty levels, it must have felt significantly further than that.

By the final whistle, as Liverpool's gleeful supporters alternated between singing about the notion of Pochettino's immediate dismissal, United "going down" and celebrating "Poch is at the wheel", the Argentine head coach would have been starting to understand the scale of the task he has taken on by quitting his United States job.

He had just watched United be dismantled 4-0 by their oldest rivals, with Leny Yoro's eighth-minute red card and two quickfire goals soon after representing just about the worst possible start imaginable to his reign as manager.

His team sit 19th in the 20-team Premier League after 11 games, already 20 points behind leaders Arsenal.

"Animals of the grass, we like to smell it and feel it," was the memorable phrase Pochettino came out with during Friday's pre-match media conference, explaining how he has missed the day-to-day nature of club football.

Listen to former United skipper Roy Keane's post-match summary of the loss on Sky Sports and a very different smell comes to mind.

For the record, it was the joint-worst result for any new United boss, matching the 4-0 loss Lal Hilditch suffered against Bolton in October 1926.

Hilditch lasted 30 games and never managed another team.

## 'Rubbish' or 'fight' - how views differed on Man Utd performance

Keane is not one for holding back, so his response to his old side's latest setback was predictable.

"Fans are applauding the players [at the end]. Applauding what? Applauding rubbish," he said. "When do the players take responsibility for some of the decision-making?"

There were enough images of chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox broadcast during the game by Sky Sports to suggest there is a wider debate to be had about the quality of the players at Pochettino's disposal.

On Friday, Berrada said this season's squad was "fundamentally better" than the one which finished third last season. Few would agree with that, and presumably Keane is among the group who would challenge Berrada's assertion.

Pochettino, 54, shed tears of emotion when he explained his difficulty in deciding to quit the United States job and take up an opportunity at a club he has flirted with for the best part of a decade but, only now, has been given the chance to coach.

It was easy to imagine him being reduced to tears again as Sunday's debacle unfolded, a nightmare afternoon in Liverpool coming at the end of a chaotic week of travel.

He arrived to begin his new role as United head coach on Wednesday after agreeing a two-and-a-half-year contract.

The USA beat Haiti 2-0 in Cincinnati in his final game in charge on Tuesday, with Pochettino flying directly to Manchester on a private jet after the game. He landed at about 11:30 GMT before heading to the club's Carrington training ground - and his feet have barely touched the floor since.

Mauricio Pochettino gives his post-match verdict on Liverpool v Man Utd

Pochettino was bullish when speaking to the media after this 4-0 defeat.

Although he began his first answer by saying he went to referee Sam Barrott after the final whistle to "present his respect", he became increasingly more forceful in his view Leny Yoro should not have been sent off.

After dismissing the sorry statistic that United have conceded 19 goals in their latest six games, because he didn't want to talk about results and performances that occurred before he replaced Michael Carrick, he then used Yoro's red card as the prism through which his side's entire performance at Anfield should be judged.

"What I learned from my players [today] is that they like to fight. They are committed with the team, with the club, and of course they want to win games," Pochettino said.

"Today we made an unbelievable effort. Today was a test. After eight minutes, 10 men, red card, concede the goal in this action. Do you think that is easy?

"Even the best team in the world playing like this in this situation, in our circumstance, maybe concede the four goals or maybe more."

## We were punished and they were not - Pochettino

It is a basic truth that Pochettino won six games this season as United States boss, twice as many as United have managed in 16 matches across all competitions. His new team have kept one clean sheet, against Azerbaijanis Sabah FK in the Champions League in September.

Pochettino's biggest selection call was to alter his defence by handing Matthijs de Ligt his first Premier League start in almost 12 months.

It was a De Ligt misread of a bouncing ball that led to Yoro having to decide whether to foul Alexander Isak before he entered the penalty area.

"It was clearly a foul and he was clearly the last man," said home boss Andoni Iraola, giving his reading from a Liverpool perspective. "Will he score? We will never know."

Pochettino had a different opinion.

"If you go a minute and a half before, [Jeremy[ Jacquet and [Bryan] Mbeumo and compare it..." he said, suggesting his team should have been awarded an earlier penalty.

"Yes, it's painful because it's 4-0. But for us to play 80 minutes with 10 men is unfair. We need to be critical but the action of Liverpool [player] was also a mistake, no? But we were punished and they were not. That is the truth."

Pochettino takes his team, and his grievance, to Lisbon on Wednesday for a Champions League encounter with Sporting.

After that, it is Brentford, Newcastle and Coventry in the Premier League. This, to an extent, was a game United and their fans, and even Keane probably, expected little from.

If they are still in the relegation zone after Coventry have left Old Trafford on 5 December, United really are in trouble.
