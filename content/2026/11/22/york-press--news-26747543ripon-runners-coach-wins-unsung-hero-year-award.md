## Runninng coach named Unsung Hero after decade supporting youngsters

Helen Cox of Ripon Runners *(Image: Supplied)*

A running coach has been honoured for her dedication to supporting young athletes.

Helen Cox, from Ripon Runners, received the Unsung Hero of the Year award at the 2026 England Athletics Yorkshire and Humberside Regional Volunteer Awards.

The award was given in recognition of her long-standing commitment to the club’s junior section.

A spokesperson for England Athletics said: "For almost a decade, Helen has been at the heart of the junior section at Ripon Runners.

"As Minis and Juniors Coordinator, she gives countless hours to coaching, administration and supporting both young athletes and fellow volunteers.

"Her inclusive and caring approach has helped create a welcoming environment where children can develop confidence, skills, and enjoyment in athletics."

Ms Cox said: "It was such a lovely surprise to receive these awards, and I’m still feeling very emotional about it.

"Ripon Runners means such a lot to me, and supporting the junior members is so rewarding.

"Of course, it wouldn’t be possible without the fantastic support that I’ve received from club members and volunteers, so I’d like to thank them for all the help that they have given me over the years."

The England Athletics award follows her recent recognition at the 2026 St Wilfrid’s Stars Award.
