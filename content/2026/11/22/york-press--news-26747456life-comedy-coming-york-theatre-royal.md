A new comedy from playwright John Godber is set to open on stage in January.

This Is The Life, described by Mr Godber as "one of the funniest plays" he's written in the last twenty years, will run at York Theatre Royal from January 27 to 30.

The play tells the story of Dave and Sapphire, two longtime backstage workers at the Old Forge theatre who step into the spotlight when their beloved workplace faces closure.

Mr Godber said: "I started to wonder what life would be like if these stalwarts of our theatrical art were to be given a voice, and they became successful.

"If all the world is a stage, then this is the life that lands right in the middle of it, and I can’t wait to get started."

The two-hander stars Ciara Morris as Sapphire and Tom Gallagher as Dave.

Ms Morris is known for her role in The Play That Goes Wrong on the West End, while Mr Gallagher appeared in Do I Love You?

The show will feature a post-show discussion with Mr Godber and the company following the evening performance on January 28.

Tickets start at £15, and the show carries a 14+ age guidance.
