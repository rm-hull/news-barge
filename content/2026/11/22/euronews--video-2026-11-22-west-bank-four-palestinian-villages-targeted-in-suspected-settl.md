In **Deir Abu Mashaal**, northwest of** Ramallah**, footage showed residents inspecting the** charred remains of their vehicles**outside homes marked with** graffiti**. Similar scenes unfolded in** Kafr Malik**, east of the city, where Palestinians gathered around** burnt cars**and examined** Hebrew slogans** sprayed on walls.

Local activist **Jihad al-Qaq** told**AFP** that**settlers** had entered the outskirts of**Kafr Malik** at around**2 am**, setting fire to approximately** eight vehicles**and leaving** racist messages**on houses.** Israeli police**confirmed that an** investigation had been launched**, while the** military**said soldiers and officers had been deployed to** four affected villages**and** condemned the attacks**.

The incidents also affected **Deir as-Sudan** and**Al-Tuwani**, amid continuing tensions in the** occupied West Bank**. Violence in the territory has intensified since the** Hamas-led attacks on Israel**on** 7 October 2023**and the subsequent** war in Gaza**. According to an** AFP**tally based on** Palestinian health ministry**figures, at least** 1,119 Palestinians have been killed**by** Israeli forces or settlers**in the** West Bank** since then.

Official **Israeli figures** indicate that at least**51 Israelis**, including soldiers,** have been killed**in** Palestinian attacks or clashes**over the same period. More than** 500,000 Israeli settlers**live alongside approximately** three million Palestinians**in the territory, where** settlements are considered illegal under international law**. The latest incidents follow new** British and Australian sanctions**targeting** eight Israeli companies** linked to settlement activity.
