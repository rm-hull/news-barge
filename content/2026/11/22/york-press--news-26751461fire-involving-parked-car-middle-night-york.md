EMERGENCY services were called to a York street in the middle of the night after a fire involving a parked car.

North Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service say its York crew were called to a residential street at 3.24am on Sunday morning (November 22) to extinguish a fire involving a parked car.




* **Urgent search for missing York man - last seen in Malton McDonald’s**
* **Major road in East Yorkshire to be closed until 'some time' next week“** One hose reel jet used to extinguish the fire. Thermal image camera used for checks,” said a service spokesperson.

They added that the crews liaised with North Yorkshire Police at the scene and gave advice to owner of vehicle.
