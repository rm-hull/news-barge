Police have launched a search for a York man who is missing.

David Boulton is from the Bootham area.

The 31-year-old was seen outside his home address at 10.45pm on Friday (20 November).

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/david-boulton-full.jpg)

The last confirmed sighting of him was in McDonald’s on Edenhouse Road in Malton at 2am yesterday (Saturday).

David is described as white, 5ft 5in tall, stocky build, and he has very long brown hair and a moustache.

He was last seen wearing a black puffer-style jacket, beige trousers, and carrying a bag for life.

While David lives in York, he was last seen in Malton and has links to Pickering.

Police say if you see David, do not approach him directly. Instead, call 999 immediately.

If you have any other information that could help them to find him, call 101 and ask for the Force Control Room.
