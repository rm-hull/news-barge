# Murder arrest ruled out after woman, 58, found dead in South Yorkshire home

## A man arrested on suspicion of murder after a woman was found dead at a Rotherham home has been released without further action after police confirmed her death was not suspicious.

A woman has tragically been found dead at a South Yorkshire home - with a man arrested on suspicion of murder before later being released.

The death of the 58-year-old woman is no longer being treated as suspicious following a forensic post-mortem examination.

South Yorkshire Police were called to Great Park Road in the Kimberworth Park area of Rotherham at 5.20am on Saturday (November 21) by the ambulance service after reports of a sudden death at the address.

A man was arrested on suspicion of murder but has since been released with no action taken.

![The cordon remains in place on Great Park Road](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/jpim-static/image/2026/11/21/13/08/WhatsApp-Image-2026-11-21-at-1-27-48-PM.jpeg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


A police statement says: “At 5.20am yesterday (Saturday 21 November), we were called by the ambulance service to reports of a sudden death at an address on Great Park Road in Rotherham.

“Officers attended and a 58-year-old woman was sadly deceased at the scene.

“A man was arrested on suspicion of murder and has since been released with no further action taken following a forensic post-mortem examination.

“Officers have completed their enquiries and the woman’s death is no longer being treated as suspicious. A file has been prepared for the coroner.”

Police tape could be seen going across the steps and path that lead to a side door of the property, attached to a hand rail, on Saturday morning.
