A sex offender, company director and three motorists from York and North Yorkshire have been sentenced at magistrates courts from Peterlee to Cardiff.

Maryam Habib, 25, of Heslington Lane, York, pleaded guilty to failure to provide a breath test in Rochdale.

Tameside magistrates in Greater Manchester banned her from driving for 18 months.

She was fined £432 and ordered to pay £400 prosecution costs and a £173 statutory surcharge.

Sezgin Dereli, 25, of Burns Court, Woodthorpe, did not respond to a summons and was convicted at Peterlee Magistrates' Court of failure to tell police who was driving his car when it was allegedly being driven in a careless way on the A1(M) in County Durham.

He was fined £660, ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and six penalty points.

Paul Davey, 57, of Howard Street, Scarborough, pleaded guilty to breaching a sexual risk order and harassment.

He was jailed at Harrogate Magistrates' Court for 29 weeks and ordered to pay £450 prosecution costs.

Joe Thorpe, 41, of Claxton, north-east of York, did not enter a plea and was found guilty at Cardiff Magistrates' Court of failure to send a copy of the accounts of Impact1 (Yorkshire) Ltd, a company of which he is the sole director, to Companies House.

He was fined £220 and ordered to pay £75 prosecution costs and an £88 statutory surcharge.

Simon Winstanley, 59, of Northfield Lane, Riccall, pleaded guilty to speeding in a 70mph zone on the A66 near Barnard Castle, County Durham.

He was banned from driving for six months at Peterlee Magistrates' Court, fined £415 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £166 statutory surcharge.
