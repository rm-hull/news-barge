A pick-up driver has been banned from driving for 12 months and given a community order for causing a crash that left a motorcyclist with life-threatening injuries.

Barry Lofthouse, 62, of Doe Park, Rawcliffe, admitted causing the woman serious injuries by careless driving of a Ford Ranger at 7.47pm on March 15, 2024.

York Magistrates Court heard that he didn’t give way at the junction of Howard Drive and Shipton Road, Rawcliffe.

The road, the main route north from the city centre towards Thirsk and Northallerton, was closed for several hours by the crash.

Following the crash, the motorcyclist, who was on a Yamaha bike, was taken to hospital with what police described at the time as “life-threatening injuries”.

York magistrates gave him a 12-month community order with 200 hours’ unpaid work.

He was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and a £114 statutory surcharge.
