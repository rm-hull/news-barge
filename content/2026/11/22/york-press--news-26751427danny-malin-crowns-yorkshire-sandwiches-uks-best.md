A GLOWING review has put a York sandwich shop firmly on the foodie map.

Food reviewer Danny Malin, who hosts the Danny Vs Food YouTube channel, visited Salt Pig, based on Fossgate in York, and declared it home to "one of the best sandwiches I've ever tasted.

He said: "I've been hunting for the UK's best sandwich and this is one of the best sandwiches I've ever tasted.

"I come to York with my wife quite regularly and now I know where I'm coming for sandwiches when I get here because they are absolutely amazing."

Danny added: "This is what food reviewing is about.

"It's about finding places like this that have a dedication and a love for food, and putting a spotlight on them."

### 'Mate, that is fantastic'

He sampled three options during his visit: the Hot Lips, the Greedy Get, and the Peppa Pig.

The Hot Lips – made with salami, cheese, rocket, balsamic glaze and 'nduja – made an immediate impression.

Danny said: "Oh my God.

"That is absolutely fantastic.

"You've got the saltiness from the salami, the beautiful creamy cheese, the peppery rocket, the sharpness of the balsamic vinegar and then this peppery, sweet sauce.

"Mate, that is fantastic."

### 'It's just packed with meat, packed with cheese'

Next up was the Greedy Get, a focaccia sandwich filled with salami, ham, turkey, cheese and herb slaw.

He said: "It's just packed with meat, packed with cheese.

"It's tasty."

Danny also praised the shop’s freshly baked focaccia bread, calling it "fantastic" with a "nice crunch on top" and a soft interior.

### 'Every sandwich was great'

The third sandwich, the Peppa Pig, featured ham, cheese, jalapeño relish, sun-dried chilli sauce and pickled chillies.

Danny said: "The cheese is amazing, the ham's amazing and the sauce gives it a slight little tingle which lifts the sandwich."

Despite trying three different sandwiches, he said he couldn’t pick a favourite.

He said: "Every sandwich was great."

### Ten stars awarded

Danny awarded Salt Pig a full 10-star rating, praising both the food and the customer service.

Speaking before the taste test, he said he had struggled to find sandwiches in the UK that matched some of those he had enjoyed in the US and Italy.

He said: "I love a good sandwich and the fact they're baking their own bread.

"It smells amazing."
