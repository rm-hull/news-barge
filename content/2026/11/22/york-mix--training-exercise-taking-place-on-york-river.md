If you see a lot of emergency service crews near and on the river this morning (Sunday), don’t worry.

There’s nothing untoward happening, it’s a training exercise.

York Rescue Boat explained the situation under a banner saying ‘Don’t Panic’ on social media.

“This morning we are taking part in a joint training exercise with York and Acomb North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service in the Blue Bridge area,” the post read.

“You will see a lot of our crew in the area. But rest assured it is training.”
