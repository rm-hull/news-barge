The Cincinnati Zoo hippo that made national headlines after she became sick and died from eating a hat someone threw into her enclosure is going to be honored at Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

Macy’s said in a recent statement, "Fiona brought joy to millions, including so many in the Macy's community in Cincinnati. This year, as Macy's welcomes our own beloved Blue Hippo back to the Parade, we wanted to mark Fiona's memory.”

The company added, "With the Cincinnati Zoo's blessing, Macy's has made a donation to the zoo in Fiona's memory, and Blue Hippo's handlers will wear a purple Fiona patch during the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade."

Fiona, the world-famous hippo who won hearts across the globe after surviving a premature birth, died at Cincinnati Zoo 11 days after swallowing a visitor’s knit hat.

The zoo’s animal care team had been caring for Fiona around the clock, attempting to help her digest and pass the hat. Staff kept Fiona hydrated, offered her special treats and closely monitored her movements.

Surgery was not an option for Fiona, as Jack Kottwitz, assistant professor at Michigan State University’s College of Veterinary Medicine, explained, noting that adult hippopotamuses’ complex digestive systems make such procedures extremely complicated.

“It is with extreme sadness that we share with you the devastating news that Fiona did not make it after multiple attempts from our amazing vets and keeper team to help her through what ended up being a medical emergency,” the zoo wrote on Thursday afternoon.

Cincinnati Zoo director Dave Jenike added: “We are in shock at the moment. We did not expect this outcome. She fought until she couldn’t anymore.”
