Emergency workers rushed to the scene in **Dymer, near Kyiv**, where a Russian drone strike had destroyed a section of a** five-storey residential building**from the first to the fifth floor. Footage released by** Ukraine’s State Emergency Service**showed rescuers using an extended fire ladder and an aerial platform to help residents escape from the damaged structure, while other** teams searched through piles of debris for possible survivors**.

According to Ukrainian authorities, **nine residents, including one child, had been evacuated**. The State Emergency Service initially said that up to** seven people could be trapped beneath the rubble. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy**later said that** 14 people were unaccounted for.**The attack was part of a wider wave of** Russian drone strikes**reported across Ukraine overnight. Ukraine’s Air Force said** 186 attack drones were launched, of which 161 were shot down.**In** Kharkiv**, a strike drone hit the Kyivskyi district and started a fire. In** Chernihi**v, a Russian strike set a wood-processing plant ablaze, while in** Zaporizhzhia** three people, including two children, were injured in an evening attack.

The extent of the destruction in **Dymer** raised fears for residents who**might still be beneath the collapsed sections of the building**. Rescue workers remained focused on removing debris and** reaching potentially trapped people**, with no confirmed outcome for those still missing at the time of the initial reports. The latest strikes once again exposed the dangers faced by** civilians in residential areas** as Russian aerial attacks continue across Ukraine.
