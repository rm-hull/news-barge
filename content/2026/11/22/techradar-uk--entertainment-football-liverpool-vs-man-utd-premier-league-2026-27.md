Find out how to watch Liverpool vs Man Utd from anywhere in the world as the Premier League's two bitterest rivals meet at Anfield in Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of the visiting Red Devils.

Andoni Iraola's Reds ran out 3-1 winners against Crystal Palace two weeks ago to take hold of fourth place and put memories of their limp 1-0 defeat by Arsenal behind them. Florian Wirtz was on the scoresheet in that one and the 23-year-old playmaker bagged three in two Nations League games for Germany as a sign of his improving form. Virgil van Dijk missed the international break but should be fit enough to feature against their bitter rivals – the Merseysiders have won only one of seven since the famous 7-0 win at Anfield in March 2023.

Pochettino's first day in charge at Old Trafford was only on Wednesday as he completed duties with the US national team, but the former Tottenham and Chelsea boss will hope to have an instant impact on a beleaguered squad that has delivered the worst start to a United season in four decades. One win in 10 and languishing in the bottom three, United will be without Manuel Ugarte, Benjamin Sesko and Carlos Baleba, while Luke Shaw, Marcus Rashford and Harry Maguire are doubts. If Pochettino can get a tune out of Argentine compatriot Lisandro Martinez, available again after suspension, then he'll earn every second of his two-and-a-half-year contract.
