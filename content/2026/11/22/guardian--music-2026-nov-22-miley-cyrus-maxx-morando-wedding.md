Grammy-winning pop star Miley ⁠Cyrus married her longtime partner, drummer Maxx Morando, ⁠in California on ⁠Saturday, ​People magazine and other US entertainment news outlets ⁠reported.

Cyrus and Morando reportedly tied the knot ⁠at a private ceremony in Palm ​Springs, ‌a month ‌after performing Let’s Get Married together at ‌the Hollywood Bowl. The song appears on Cyrus’s latest studio album, Bass Persuades.

Representatives for both Cyrus and ‌Morando did not immediately respond to a Reuters’ request ​for comment.

Morando is the drummer for Liily, a Los Angeles-based alternative rock band ⁠affiliated with Atlantic Records, according to ​the ​band’s website.

The Flowers singer ​was previously married ​to Australian actor ‌Liam ​Hemsworth, ​who filed for divorce in Los Angeles in 2019.
