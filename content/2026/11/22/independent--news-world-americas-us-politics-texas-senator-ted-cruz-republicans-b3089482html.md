Outgoing Sen. John Cornyn, a Texas Republican, took a parting shot at fellow Texas Senator Ted Cruz, calling him a “fragile thing.”

The jab marked the latest escalation in their feud over Elliot Gaiser, President Donald Trump’s nominee for the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas. Cornyn, who lost his GOP primary to Trump-endorsed Ken Paxton, has blocked Gaiser’s nomination.

The dispute erupted into a shouting match on the Senate floor Tuesday, *The Washington Sun* reported, with Cornyn yelling at Cruz: “I don’t find you credible. I’m mad at you!”

In a Fox News interview Saturday, Cruz offered his account of the confrontation. “I came to him, and I asked him to please let this judge go,” he said. Cruz claimed Cornyn had refused even to meet with Gaiser and argued that blocking the nomination effectively handed the seat to Democrats, who will retake control of the Senate in January.

“Unfortunately, he got very angry. He began screaming; he began cursing at me,” Cruz said. He suggested Cornyn’s reaction stemmed from frustration over his primary defeat.

![Outgoing Senator John Cornyn took a parting shot at Ted Cruz, a fellow Texas senator, amid their feud over a judicial nominee](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/18/2289050168..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“It is tough in politics when you lose an election,” he said. “Sometimes when they’re angry, they lash out. I just wish it wasn’t the people of Texas that were paying the price.”

Cornyn, who will leave Congress in January after nearly 24 years in the Senate, fired back in an X post Saturday afternoon.

“Everyone knows that I am a notorious cusser and screamer and that Ted is a fragile thing,” he wrote. “But enough about us.”

Cornyn then outlined one of his objections to Gaiser’s nomination. As a senator representing Texas, Cornyn can block the nomination by withholding a so-called blue slip signaling his support.

“If a President can appoint someone from another state, no Senator will have a say in their state, notwithstanding Article II, Section 2, Clause 2 of the U.S. Constitution,” Cornyn wrote. “It is called ‘advise and consent.’”

![‘Unfortunately he got very angry. He began screaming; he began cursing at me,’ Cruz said of Cornyn during his Fox News interview](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/18/2294045214..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

In an interview with CBS News this week, Cornyn cited Gaiser’s limited Texas ties, lack of trial experience and the White House’s handling of the nomination.

“I think his nomination is no longer viable," the senator said of Gaiser. “He's not a Texan, has no trial experience, and the White House tried to jam the Senate and wouldn't allow us to do our due diligence that we need to do on lifetime appointments.”

Gaiser, an assistant attorney general who leads the Justice Department’s Office of Legal Counsel, is an Ohio native. He previously worked on Trump’s efforts to overturn the 2020 election.

His ties to Texas include a yearlong clerkship for a federal appeals judge in Houston. Defending his nomination, Cruz said that Gaiser’s wife is from the Lone Star State and that his family lives there.
