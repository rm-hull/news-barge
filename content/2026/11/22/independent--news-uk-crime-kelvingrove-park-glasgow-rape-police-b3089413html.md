Police are appealing for witnesses after a woman was raped in Kelvingrove Park in Glasgow.

Officers say the woman was attacked by a man on the Kelvin Walkway at about 11.50pm on Friday November 20.

He is described as white, with an English accent, between the age of 30 and 40, about 5ft 10ins and of heavy build.

He was wearing a black hoodie, black trousers and a black face covering.

Police have increased their presence in the area and appealed for witnesses.

Detective Superintendent Peter Sharp said: “This was a terrifying experience for the woman and extensive inquiries are ongoing to trace the individual responsible.

“From our inquiries so far, we know that there were people in and around the park near the time of the incident, and we are appealing for you to come forward with any information, no matter how insignificant it may seem.

“Think back, did you see someone matching this description or acting suspiciously in the area? Any information may be able to assist our inquiries.”

Local area commander Chief Inspector Karen Cameron said: “We understand this is a concerning incident for the local community and I’d like to reassure the public that there will be an increased, visible police presence in the area whilst we continue our inquiries.

“Officers will be carrying out dedicated pro-active patrols and will look to engage with visitors and locals.

“Anyone with concerns is encouraged to speak to these officers.”
