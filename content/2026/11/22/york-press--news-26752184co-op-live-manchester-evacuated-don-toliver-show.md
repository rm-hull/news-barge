The Co-op Live music venue in Manchester was evacuated tonight as an emergency situation developed at a performance by rapper Don Toliver.

A major incident was declared by police amid reports that a concert-goer had let off a tear gas canister during the show, causing a stampede.

The venue, sited in the Etihad Campus next to the City of Manchester Stadium, is popular with music fans across the north of England, with many from Yorkshire frequenting the arena.

In a statement late on Sunday, a spokesperson for Greater Manchester Police said: "Officers alongside other emergency services personnel are currently in attendance following an incident at Co-op Live this evening.

"It is understood that a substance believed to be tear gas was let off in the crowd towards the end of a concert.

"All attendees are currently being safely evacuated as a precaution.

"Those showing signs of being impacted are being supported by emergency services, with no serious injuries reported.

"Please stay away from the arena whilst emergency services help those that have been at the concert safely make their way to their transport home.

"A major incident has been declared to ensure emergency services and partner agencies can work together quickly and effectively to coordinate a safe response."

Don Toliver, whose real name is Caleb Zackery, is a 32-year-old American rapper, singer, and songwriter from Texas.
