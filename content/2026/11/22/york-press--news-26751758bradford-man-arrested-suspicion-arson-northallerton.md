A 49-year-old man has been arrested in connection with three suspected arson incidents in a North Yorkshire market town.

Police confirmed that following "extensive enquiries" a man was detained at an address in the Bradford area on Saturday, November 21, and remains in custody for questioning.

Two of the incidents took place on Northallerton High Street in Northallerton, the first at a residential property at 11.44pm on Monday, November 16, and the second at a business premises at 2.44am the following day.

A third incident involved a skip outside a business at Standard Way Business Park, which was reported to police at a later time.

## 'This has been a worrying time'

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: "We thank residents and the business community in Northallerton for their support to the investigation.

"We know this has been a worrying time for the town."

The investigation is ongoing and officers are continuing to appeal for witnesses and information.

Enquiries remain ongoing.

If you can help in any way, please call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Alternatively, contact Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111 or via their website.
