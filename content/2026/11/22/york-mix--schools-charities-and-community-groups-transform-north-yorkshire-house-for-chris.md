One of North Yorkshire’s most picturesque manor houses are celebrating sustainable making this Christmas.

*Nature’s Noel: A Community Christmas*is at Nunnington Hall from**19 November to 20 December**.

This year, local schools, charities and community groups have been invited to create festive displays inspired by Nunnington Hall itself, transforming the historic house with more than 20 unique Christmas trees celebrating its architecture, collections, gardens, wildlife and surrounding landscape.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Getting-ready-for-Father-Christmas-in-the-Panelled-bedroom_%C2%A9National-Trust_Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Discover-a-community-Christmas-at-Nunnington_%C2%A9National-Trust_Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)

Continuing Nature’s Noel’s focus on thoughtful and sustainable making, decorations have been crafted using natural, recycled and reused materials wherever possible.

Handmade decorations, garlands and wreaths created by a dedicated team of volunteers also help transform the Hall for the season, adding warmth and festive touches throughout the historic rooms.

Sarah Nolan, visitor experience officer at Nunnington Hall, said: “It’s been wonderful to see the variety of ideas people have brought to this year’s displays.

“We asked groups to take inspiration from Nunnington Hall, and the results have been incredibly creative. Some have focused on the house itself, others on the gardens, landscape or wildlife.

“Together they’ve created a festive experience that’s full of character, surprises and details for visitors to discover.”

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Dedicated-teams-of-volunteers-work-all-year-to-create-handmade-decorations_%C2%A9National-Trust_Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)
![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Decorations-have-been-inspired-by-Nunnington-Hall_%C2%A9National-Trust_Anthony-Chappel-Ross.webp)

Beyond the decorated interiors, visitors can continue their festive day out with a family trail through the gardens before warming up in the tea-room with seasonal cakes, festive bakes and hot drinks.

Nunnington Hall will also host a series of Festive Evening openings, giving visitors the chance to see the decorated house illuminated after dark and browse a festive mini market. Families can also book a special visit with Father Christmas, where children can share their letters, listen to festive stories and receive a book to take home.

*Nature’s Noel: A Community Christmas* offers plenty of opportunities to make Christmas memories together at Nunnington Hall.
