## 'Fantastic' North Yorkshire farm shop that 'won't disappoint' visitors

The farm shop is known for its great local produce. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

A farm shop in North Yorkshire has been praised for its “absolutely lovely” staff, with visitors saying you “won’t be disappointed”.

While you are likely to do your weekly shop at a big supermarket, it can be nice to take a trip to a farm shop to pick up some local produce.

Alongside a great farm shop, the Beadlam Grange Farmshop also has a restaurant for guests to enjoy.

Found just outside of Helmsley, the farm shop is worth a visit if you are looking for some high-quality food.

## What is the Beadlam Grange Farmshop like?

The Beadlam Grange Farmshop is family-run and offers “good, real, honest, tasty, local feel good food”.

The farm shop’s butchery offers a range of freshly prepared sausages and burgers as well as lamb, poultry, bacon, and gammon.

You can also find lots of fresh fruits and vegetables, dairy products, homemade cakes and bread, and even wines, gins, ciders, and beers.

If you need your produce to last a bit longer, there is a freezer section with frozen fruit and vegetables, patisseries, and savoury products as well as ready meals for a convenient dinner.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21733171.jpg?type=mds-article-620)You can get gifts as well as food. *(Image: Tripadvisor)*

After exploring the shop, you might want to stop off for a meal at the Bistro, the on-site restaurant.

The restaurant offers breakfasts, lunches, and even cream teas to keep you going throughout the day.

For those who like sweet treats, you can enjoy a slice of homemade cake or try one of the traybakes.

If you visit the restaurant on a Sunday, you can try the roast.
