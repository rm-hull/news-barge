# Great Yorkshire Show: Clarkson’s choir poised to be stars

## A choir, originally put together by television presenter turned farmer Jeremy Clarkson, will literally take centre stage at next year’s Great Yorkshire Show.

The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir will be the first to take to the Main Ring in the Great Yorkshire Show’s 189-year history, performing twice a day at next year’s event which runs from Tuesday July 13 to Friday July 16, 2027. The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir - made up of farmers and agricultural workers from across the UK - was the first choir to win ITV’s Britain’s Got Talent. Formed last year, the choir yesterday (Friday) released an album of original music called This Is Home, which was recorded in just five days with the entire choir earlier this summer. The choir was put together as part of an advertising campaign for Jeremy Clarkson’s Hawkstone drinks brand. It used its platform to raise awareness about rural isolation and mental health struggles in the British farming industry. Winning the hearts of the public, and Britain’s Got Talent judges, the Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir took the Golden Buzzer at their audition, won their semi-final and were ultimately crowned champions in May. It also secured them a spot performing at this year’s Royal Variety Performance. The choir is taking to the road for a major 2027 UK tour, performing their own songs and stories, and last month won the NFU Farming Champion of the Year Award at the Farmers Weekly Awards. Meanwhile, tickets for The Great Yorkshire Show 2027 also went on sale this week with changes announced to the show format and the ways to book tickets. Tickets are usually snapped up weeks in advance of what has now become the largest agricultural show in England, seeing more than 13,500 competitive entries each year. Rachel Coates, show director and a farmer from West Yorkshire said: “We’re always looking for ways to make a visit to the Great Yorkshire Show even better and so we have made a few forward-thinking changes this year, which we are excited to be announcing as we reveal our Main Ring attraction for 2027. “The Hawkstone Farmers’ Choir is doing a fantastic job of reaching the public about farming and so it’s a perfect fit for the Show which celebrates and shares the important story of agriculture to thousands of people every year.” There is a new digital ordering system for show tickets which, since the Covid pandemic, have only been available to buy in advance with visitor numbers capped at 150,000 over the four day show. The new digital online ordering system for purchasing show tickets and membership of the Yorkshire Agricultural Society, is more sustainable and environmentally friendly. Tickets and membership cards can now be added to Apple and Google wallets for ease of use on a smartphone. A new Family of 4 ticket option has also been introduced to better cater for families. Membership of YAS, which organises the Great Yorkshire Show and is a registered charity, goes on sale from Tuesday. YAS members enjoy access to the Show across its four days.
