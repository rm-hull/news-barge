A WARNING has been issued after deer have been spotted.

North Yorkshire Police has urged drivers to take care on Wheldrake Lane towards Crockey Hill after a number of deer were seen in the area.

A police spokesperson said: "Deer are most active in the early morning and evening, but can appear on roads at any time. They may move unpredictably, especially in groups, and can run suddenly into the path of vehicles."

To help deal with the issue, the force said that motorists should reduce their speed, particularly at dawn and dusk, and watch for warning signs and movement at the roadside as when one deer crosses, others are often close by.

Drivers should use full-beam headlights where appropriate and safe, while dipping them for oncoming traffic. They should also allow extra stopping distance and avoid sudden swerving, which can increase the risk of a collision.

The spokesperson added: "This matter has been passed to the parish council to raise the question on getting deer warning signs installed. Thank you for keeping our roads safe."

Anyone finding injured wildlife after a crash should put their own safety first and report the incident through 101, or call 999 in an emergency.
