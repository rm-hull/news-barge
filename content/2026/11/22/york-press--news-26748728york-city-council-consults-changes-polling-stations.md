A review of polling districts, places and stations is underway to ensure voting remains accessible and convenient.

City of York Council is carrying out the review following changes to ward boundaries made by the Local Government Boundary Commission.

It aims to ensure all polling places are reasonably located and accessible, especially for voters with disabilities.

Richard Webb, chief executive and returning officer, said: "We want to hear your views on our proposed new arrangements in response to the Boundary Commission's changes.

"We are also keen to hear any suggestions for improving polling district boundaries or polling places.

"In particular, we are interested in feedback from disabled people about access to polling stations and any support that may be needed."

The public consultation is open and will run until Sunday, December 20.

Full details of the proposals and information on how to take part in the consultation are available at www.york.gov.uk/PollingReview.

Paper copies of the survey, including Easy Read versions, are available from libraries and West Offices.
