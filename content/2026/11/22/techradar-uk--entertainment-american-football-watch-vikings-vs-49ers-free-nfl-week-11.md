The final international game of the 2026 NFL season is a potential dress-rehearsal for the NFC Championship, with the 7-2 Vikings going up against the 8-1 49ers in a heavyweight showdown at the legendary Estadio Banorte (formerly Estadio Azteca) in Mexico City.

Five days after sealing his sensational return to Minnesota, Kirk Cousins showed enough to suggest that he can provide the steady hand that Kevin O'Connell needs. The 38-year-old threw for 207 yards, two touchdowns and an interception on his second Vikings debut, but most promisingly of all he took no time re-establishing his connection with Justin Jefferson, who had a season-high nine catches for 74 yards.

That could be the deciding factor in a game that's likely to be dominated by a Vikings defense that's surrendered the fewest points per game and the second-fewest yards per game, and a 49ers offense that's put up the most yards per game and the second-most points per game. That said, Brian Flores likes to blitz as often as possible, a tactic that doesn't tend to work against quarterbacks that get rid of the ball quickly, like Brock Purdy.

Christian McCaffrey ran riot with a 74-yard touchdown and a 66-yard score against the Cowboys last weekend, but conceding 40 points will likely prompt a reaction from Kyle Shanahan.

San Fran have done their homework too. Estadio Azteca sits 2,241m above sea level, so the 49ers have prepared by training at high altitude at Colorado Springs. The Vikings, on the other hand, are winging it.
