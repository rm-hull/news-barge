The wife of convicted rapist Russell Brand has responded to the backlash over her decision to share a message from her husband in prison proclaiming his innocence.

Laura Brand, 39, read out a message from the disgraced comedian – who is behind bars at HMP Littlehey – last week. The choice to air the message sparked anger and backlash and Downing St ordered an urgent investigation into the matter.

Russell Brand, 51, is currently serving a 10-and-a-half year prison sentence after he was convicted of rape and three counts of sexual assault, following a trial earlier this month.

In a new video released on Sunday, Laura Brand implied that she had not understood the rules around sharing messages from prison and vowed to follow regulations going forward.

![Laura Brand said she had been under intense ‘scrutiny’](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/15/45/Laura-Brand.png?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“Last week’s episode drew a considerable amount of attention,” she said. “And now that I understand the parameters set by the brilliant and professional staff at HMP Littlehey, I will absolutely honour that.”

In last week’s message, Brand had read her husband’s emphatic denial of the crimes of which he was convicted, saying: “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support.

![Russell Brand was convicted of rape and sexual assault (PA).](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/12/53f8c5fbaf2040f4b135a96b9512ae49Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk0OTE4MDI1-2.86369922.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

“As you know, I am not guilty for the offences I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening. The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do.

“I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey, little as in small, and hey as in hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders’ prison in Europe.”

On Sunday, Brand said she would be continuing with the YouTube show, dubbed Sunday Sermons.

Speaking about her experience of “grief” and “desolation” in the night, she added that she had made the decision to continue the segments as she found them personally helpful.

She commented on the intense “scrutiny” she had been under and the debate around “what kind of woman I am”.

“It’s also a place where I will continue to be proud to be a wife, a mother, and a woman defending her family at a time when the media's image of what kind of a woman I am is under so much scrutiny.”

![Russell Brand had claimed his innocence in a video posted by his wife, Laura](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/19/18/84cd9022c629223585913f1887d7adc5Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk1MTk5OTEw-2.86369927.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Reading from the Book of Jonah, she said she had found “hope” in the verses.

Closing the show she shared a prayer for Brand, saying: “Please God, look after my husband. Care for him and bring him comfort. Please look after my children and bring them courage and joy and keep my heart pure and keep help to keep me focused on you.”

The couple share three children aged between 10, eight and three-years-old.

![Russell Brand was convicted of rape and sexual assault](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/16/12/53f8c5fbaf2040f4b135a96b9512ae49Y29udGVudHNlYXJjaGFwaSwxNzk0OTE4MDI1-2.86369922.jpg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Brand is expected to serve two-thirds of his 10-and-a-half year prison sentence before being released on licence. He will be on the sex offenders’ register for life.

The Metropolitan Police is conducting further investigations into additional allegations against him that had emerged through the course of the trial.

A Met spokesperson told the BBC at the time: "Detectives are assessing this information as part of their ongoing enquiries."
