# Jungle return to 'f\*\* king incredible' Leeds with huge First Direct Bank Arena show

## Jungle took to the stage at Leeds First Direct Bank Arena on Saturday evening in support of 2026 album Sunshine

Jungle headlined the perfect weekend party when they made their return to Leeds.

Jungle in support of latest release Sunshine played to a very appreciative audience at Leeds First Direct Bank Arena on Saturday evening. The thousands packed into the Leeds city centre venue presenting something very different from the London band’s debut appearance in the city back in 2013 - when they played to ‘around 100 people’ at Belgrave Music Hall.

The dance funk collective are now five records in and are something of a marvel to behold live. Support, fitting with the theme and sound of the night, came from California collective Rio Kosta. The rhythmic ensemble, having spent the last two months accompanying Jungle around the globe, brought something of a festival feel to the chilly November day.

Kosta Galanopoulos, from Rio Kosta, declaring he was ‘chuffed’ to make his first visit to Leeds - despite the climate. Later asking, “Who the f\*\* k is ready for Jungle?”

As it happened, a packed arena full of people were with Platoon opening the energetic proceedings as the clock hit 9pm. Big hitters The Heat, Heavy, California and Beat 54 soon followed as part of a confidently frenetic start.

Originally formed by Tom McFarland and Josh Lloyd-Watson, on Saturday, Jungle were seen as an eight-piece with the latest recruits joining the originals on-stage. The ever-impressive vocalist Lydia Kitto, very much a full-fledged Jungle member, commanding the stage when taking the lead for Candle Flame and Back On 74.

Loud cheers followed as Kitto asked: “What’s going on Leeds?” She added: “Everyone up north is always up for it.”

It seemed so, as casting an eye across the arena’s tiers there appeared a plethora of fans up on their feet, throughout, perhaps wishing they were in the standing section below.

Sunshine, from the 2026 album release of the same name, fitting in well alongside classic Jungle offerings. Lloyd-Watson enthusing: “Leeds! It’s Saturday night!”

The conclusion of Time saw a mass of huge, multi-coloured bouncing balls released into the audience. Kitto often styling out kicking the odd one or two alongside keeping her vocals firmly in check.

Devotees even got to experience some territorial posturing as drummer George Day revealed he has a tattoo stating ‘WY’ representing West Yorkshire as he hails from Hebden Bridge.

All of The Time and Good Times brought the main set to a close before the hugely-talented troupe returned for a two-song encore. Opening with Keep Moving before Jungle calling card anthem Busy Earnin’ brought the curtain down.

Lloyd-Watson declaring Jungle’s supporters present to be ‘f\*\* king incredible’.
