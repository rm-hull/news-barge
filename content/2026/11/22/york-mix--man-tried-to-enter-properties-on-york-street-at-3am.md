Police are trying to trace this man following suspicious behaviour in York.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cctv-beagle-ridge-drive-york-20-nov-2026-full.webp)

They want to speak with him in connection with a number of reports received from residents in the Beagle Ridge Drive area.

A man was seen attempting to gain entry to people’s homes at 3am on Friday (20 November).

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “If you have any information that could assist our investigation, please contact us online in the first instance.”
