Christmas magic has returned to a historic York house – as it has once again been overtaken by festive and well-dressed mice.

Fairfax House’s much-loved Christmas display returns for 2026 with a brand-new  
theme: *A Christmas Tail: The Town Mouse and The Country Mouse*, promising more mice than ever before.

Inspired by Aesop’s beloved fable, this year’s Christmas experience follows a family of  
country mice as they join their city cousins for a season of feasting, finery, parlour games  
and festive merriment.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/A-mouse-hanging-about.webp)

Many mice are dressed in hand-crafted and exquisitely miniature Georgian finery, custom-made by Fairfax House’s volunteers.

*A Christmas Tail is at Fairfax House*from now until**Sunday 3 January**, with three atmospheric candlelit evenings on** 16, 18 and 21 December**.

Through the House, there are all sorts of Georgian amusements brought to life, including huge feasts, fabulous fashion, tempting bargains at the Frost Fair and a lavish ball.

Curator Dr Sarah Burnage said, “We drew inspiration from our amazing stuccowork  
ceilings in the House, which includes in the Library a portrait of the famous eighteenth century author Alexander Pope.

“Being represented on the ceiling suggests Pope was one of the Fairfaxes’ favourite literary figures. Pope wrote an interpretation of Aesop’s classic fable ‘The Town Mouse and the County Mouse’ so it seems a fitting nod to the House’s former residents and their tastes.

“We absolutely love this time of year when the house is dressed for a Georgian Christmas  
and over-run with our mischievous mice. We really hope visitors enjoy what we’ve created  
this year – we’ve certainly enjoyed making it.”

Tickets for *A Christmas Tail: The Town Mouse and The Country Mouse* are £9.50 for adults and £8.50 for concessions and children (under 16) can visit for free.
