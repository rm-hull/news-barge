Conservative commentator Ann Coulter has floated a pick for the 2028 Republican presidential nomination that could raise eyebrows.

Rather than rally behind Vice President JD Vance or Secretary of State Marco Rubio — both widely viewed as leading contenders — Coulter urged Republicans to back White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller.

“Instead of the JD/Rubio debate over our next presidential candidate, let's make it The People's Choice, Stephen Miller,” she wrote on X Friday.

Coulter’s post quickly drew mockery, with commenters pointing to Miller’s polarizing role as an architect of Trump’s hardline immigration agenda. Others predicted he would hand Democrats a landslide victory, eclipsing even their midterm gains.

“Because your party is full of crazy people, I can't tell if you're serious, or joking. Stephen Miller is probably disliked by 92% of this country,” wrote one X user.

![Conservative commentator Ann Coulter said that White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller should be the GOP nominee in 2028](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/17/50/GettyImages-2226726979.jpeg?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

A YouGov poll this month found that Americans viewed Miller unfavorably by nearly a 2-to-1 margin, with 37 percent expressing an unfavorable opinion and 19 percent a favorable one.

“With his calm demeanor and magnetic charisma, I would say he’s every bit as electable as Ken Paxton,” another quipped, referring to the Texas Senate candidate who lost to Democrat James Talarico.

The pushback also came from conservatives, who dismissed the suggestion from Coulter, a longtime advocate of stricter immigration policies who has compared non-white immigration to the U.S. to genocide.

“Let’s really not. Some people have learned literally nothing during the Trump era and it shows,” wrote Jenna Ellis, a former legal adviser to Trump’s 2020 campaign who broke with him in 2023.

Conservative commentator S.E. Cupp also weighed in.

“I love that this sentiment still exists,” she wrote in response to Coulter. “It’s like the flat earthers and the people who believe Avril Lavigne is a body double and Stevie Wonder can see. It’s so dumb, it’s funny.”

Still, Miller had supporters among the commenters. “I'm just about at the point where I would happily make Miller dictator for life,” one wrote.

![Trump asked Miller if he wanted to run for president during a Friday White House press conference](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/11/22/17/2306095700..?quality=75&width=640&auto=webp)

Coulter’s post came in response to a clip of Miller speaking at a White House press conference on Friday, several weeks after President Donald Trump promoted him chief of staff. During the briefing, Trump invited Miller to the podium and introduced him as “the most powerful man in the world.”

After he ticked through a lengthy list of Trump’s second-term accomplishments, the president pointed to Vance and Rubio and offered a comparison.

“He was better than these two guys,” Trump said, before asking Miller: “Do you wanna run for president?”

Though the 2028 presidential race is still a long way off, most polls of potential Republican nominees put Marco Rubio and JD Vance ahead of the field, with Florida Governor Ron DeSantis, podcaster Tucker Carlson and Donald Trump Jr. among those trailing behind.

Miller is not listed in most polls.
