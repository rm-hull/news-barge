# Yorkshire under cold weather health alert as freezing temperatures grip region

## Yorkshire is facing another bitterly cold night as temperatures are forecast to fall below freezing, with a yellow cold health alert remaining in place until Tuesday morning.

Flooding is expected as a slow-moving band of heavy rain breaks out across parts of the UK, with forecasters urging caution amid “treacherous” conditions.

A yellow rain warning is in force covering areas in the South West, including Exeter, Plymouth and Cornwall, for the rest of Sunday.

There is a chance that as much as 100mm of rain could fall over Dartmoor in particular through the day.

More than 25 areas in England have been identified as at risk of possible floods by the Environment Agency, with flooding expected in the south Devon coast at Beesands, Torcross, Slapton and Dawlish.

![Temperatures are expected to drop in Yorkshire](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjc3NTk0OWUwLTg0NmYtNDZkZi1iMGZiLTY3Nzg0YzVjMDFkZjphMWNkZTU3NC02NmYyLTRhZGUtOWU0ZS1kZjJhYzZjNTllZjM=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)


“Take extra care if you are travelling today as conditions will be treacherous at times,” the forecasting body added.

Meteorologist Greg Dewhurst said: “We’re in a bit of a slow-moving weather pattern at the moment so this weather front isn’t going to go anywhere very quickly.

“We’re expecting some very heavy rain across parts of Devon and Cornwall in particular, for a Met Office yellow rain warning in force for much of the day.

“So, there could be some localised disruption as we move through the day ahead.

“Looking at the rainfall totals for today, widely across Devon and Cornwall we could see 40mm or so, locally in the south 60mm and perhaps as much as 80mm to 100mm across Dartmoor in particular.

“This is likely to lead to some flooding disruptions, so it is worth staying up to date to our warnings as we go through the day ahead as we are expecting some local flooding over the course of the day.”

For the rest of the country, a frosty start should give way to a dry day with some sunny spells.

Overnight, a widespread frost is expected across northern and eastern areas, with temperatures in rural parts potentially dropping to minus 5C.

The band of deluge over the South West will start to ease in the early hours of Monday and it should go on to be a drier day for the region.

The UK saw its coldest night since January 9 overnight on Friday as a low of minus 10.6C was recorded at Tulloch Bridge in the Highlands.

It comes after the first snow warning of the season, with more than an inch recorded in parts of northern England earlier in the week.

A yellow cold health alert is in force for the North West, North East and Yorkshire and the Humber until 9am on Tuesday.

The alert means weather conditions could lead to an increased use of healthcare services and a greater risk to life for vulnerable people, the UK Health Security Agency said.
