## York pupil’s winning design becomes new eco-friendly school library

Woodthorpe Primary School officially opened the new library, designed by student Archie *(Image: Excel Learning Trust)*

A pupil’s winning design has been transformed into a new eco-friendly school library.

Woodthorpe Primary School officially opened the new library, designed by student Archie, after he won a national competition to imagine a dream reading space.

His “Eco Library” design was selected from 1,901 entries in the Inspiring Libraries competition and brought to life by modular building specialist Portakabin.

Helen Elsworth, vice principal at Woodthorpe Primary School, said: "The opening of our new library is a significant milestone for our school, providing pupils with a calm, welcoming environment where they can develop a lifelong love of reading, nurture their curiosity and reach their full potential.

"We are excited to see the lasting impact this inspiring space will have on their learning and wellbeing for many years to come."

Archie’s winning design featured solar panels, a living wall, grass-effect flooring, cloud-shaped lights and softly shaped, natural bookshelves.

Wendy Jones, regional manager at Portakabin, said: "Knowing that Archie’s winning design has now been brought to life is deeply rewarding for everyone at Portakabin, and it’s fantastic to see how excited the children at Woodthorpe Primary are about their new library."

The library is now open to pupils as a dedicated space for reading and learning.
