**AN air ambulance was seen setting off from York city centre this afternoon.**

A Yorkshire Air Ambulance helicopter was seen taking off from Bootham Park Hospital in York on Sunday lunchtime (November 22).

It was spotted at 1.30pm, later landing at RAF Leeming - suggesting the incident may be a training exercise.

We have contacted Yorkshire Ambulance Service for comment.
