Over the last couple of weeks, some Nvidia users noticed their SSDs were taking a hammering, reporting their driver's log file was being overwritten constantly, sometimes to the tune of 30 GB every hour. Which obviously isn't great for the lifespan of the valuable SSD you've got it installed on.

Given the problem was caused by recent driver updates, rolling back to version 621.01 was the best solution until Nvidia could do something about it. Which they did, releasing a hotfix as version 621.35. It's not available in the GeForce app yet, but you can download it directly from Nvidia's site. Not that you should.

As users on the Nvidia subreddit are reporting, the problem persists. While the hotfix reduces the peak write rate and prevents it for a short while after reboot, it eventually creeps back in. One user reported that overnight the log file was still being rewritten by approximately 4.5 GB every hour.

So yeah, the best solution remains to roll back to version 621.01 and, as always with Nvidia drivers, to hold off on updating to the latest driver until it's been confirmed safe. If this ongoing saga sounds familiar, it's because the same thing happened to AMD's GPU drivers earlier this month. Maybe vibecoding driver updates and then vibecoding fixes for their inevitable bugs isn't the best way of doing things.
