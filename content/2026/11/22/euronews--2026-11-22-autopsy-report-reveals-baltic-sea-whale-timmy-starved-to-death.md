## A tragic end to a media spectacle: New findings into the death of humpback whale 'Timmy' reveal that the animal had gone a prolonged time without food.

A Danish report has revealed that Timmy, the humpback whale that stranded several times off Germany's Baltic Sea coast last spring, died of starvation.

For months, Timmy struggled to swim with a heavy fishing net trapped in its mouth. By the time he died, the whale was severely emaciated and ultimately drowned.

Timmy was given its nickname in reference to the beach where the whale first stranded, on Timmendrofer beach.

The whale soon became a global sensation after multiple sightings attracted widespread attention. However, the case also sparked strong criticism from animal rights activists, who questioned how local authorities handed the situation.

An investigation by the German weekly newspaper Die Zeit has shed new light on the whale's fate and the controversial rescue attempts.

According to the report, Timmy had previously become entangled before being stranded in a gillnet of the type legally used for cod fishing at depths of around 50 metres between Denmark and Sweden. Two experts who analysed fragments of the net for the newspaper ruled out the possibility that it had been used by fishermen in the Baltic Sea.

This suggests that the net was not a so-called ghost net drfiting freely in the water. Fisheries biologist Daniel Stepputtis told Die Zeit that Timmy had probably torn the net from its anchors, dragging a section measuring about 150 metres long and three metres wide behind her.

"And that costs an awful lot of energy," he said.

Fishing-net dealer Eike Peters added that the obstruction in the whale's mouth had likely made it impossible for the animal to feed.

The report found that Timmy's stomach was completely empty during the post-mortem, with lead pathologist Tim Kåre Jensen claiming the animal had not eaten in months.

It is unclear when exactly Timmy became entangled in the net, and the report found no underlying illness that could have been the cause of death.

## **Rescue team "chaotic and divided"**

Die Zeit also revealed serious mistakes in the rescue operation, and indications that Timmy endured considerable suffering during its rescue operation.

Local authorities approved a private rescue operation under growing public pressure, despite experts warning that the mission could cause more harm than good to the humpback whale.

Voice messages, documents and video material reportedly reveal that members from the private rescue operation team had acted in a "divided, unqualified and chaotic" manner.
