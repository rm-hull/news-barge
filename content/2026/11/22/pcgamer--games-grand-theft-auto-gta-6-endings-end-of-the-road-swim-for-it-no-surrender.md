**Please note, this guide contains massive spoilers for some of the endings of Grand Theft Auto 6**. If you don't want to know what happens, please stop reading!

There are multiple endings to GTA 6, and not all the endings take place in the same location. One of the potential final scenes during the GTA 6 story mission, The End of the Road, takes place on a bridge in the Leonida Keys.

After discovering the cops are onto them, Jason and Lucia drive away from Cal's house and head to Brian's marina to find an airplane. The police quickly trap them on a bridge with a roadblock ahead and multiple cars behind them. Lucia wants to keep going but Jason thinks it's all over for them. The player is then given a choice, "Swim for it" or "No Surrender."

**AGAIN, MASSIVE SPOILERS BELOW! STOP NOW IF YOU DON'T WANT TO KNOW THE ENDINGS**!

## No Surrender

Jason and Lucia decide Tomas is better off without them in his life. They drive through the roadblock, shooting at the cops. They're both shot to death and we see them dead in the front seat of the car, still holding hands. During the end credits, we see Tomas living happily with Lucia's sister and her husband and children.

## Swim for it (A, B, and C)

Jason and Lucia decide to jump off the bridge and swim to freedom.

However, **a second choice appears** once you've chosen 'Swim for it.' As Jason gets out of the car, a prompt for Lucia to 'Exit' the car appears. You can choose for Lucia to follow through with the plan by pressing the button, or change your mind and let the prompt expire.**A: 'Swim for it' and 'Exit'**: The couple will get out of the car together and walk to the edge of the bridge. Lucia jumps but Jason does not, letting himself get arrested by the cops. During the credits, Lucia signs the deal which keeps her free and sends Jason to prison. We see her bring Tomas to visit him, so Jason finally meets his son, though it's through glass.** B: 'Swim for it' and don't 'Exit'**: If you choose to swim for it but don't choose the 'Exit' prompt, Jason will get out of the car but Lucia will remain in the car. The police will open fire and kill them both. As with the No Surrender option, we see Tomas living with Lucia's sister's family.** C: 'Swim for it' and 'Exit' (with $2 million in Buck Me savings)**: If you delayed the Protective Custody mission of GTA 6 until you had deposited $2 million in savings, the Swim for it and Exit choices will show the same results, but during the credits, Lucia uses the money to get Jason released from prison. Lucia picks him up at prison, and Jason meets Tomas and holds his hand.

Note: These are not the only endings to GTA 6. I've seen several more endings that do not take place on the bridge at all and don't involve these particular choices.
