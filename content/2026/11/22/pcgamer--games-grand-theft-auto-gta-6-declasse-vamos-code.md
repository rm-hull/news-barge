The Declasse Vamos is one of the many cars Moses asks you to steal for him in GTA 6, but like the BF Bandito, it's a little trickier than just grabbing it. When you reach the map marker, you'll realise the car is locked in a garage that requires a specific code to open it.

The code to the door is 2727 and this is fixed. The way you discover this is by sneaking inside the trailer and looking at the number plate on the wall to the left of the window, just above the guy snoozing in the chair.

Enter the code to open the garage, break in with the slimjim, then hit the button to open the door. I personally incapacitated all of the people behind the trailer, so chances are they will pursue you or start shooting if you don't, once you try to drive out of there.

Now take the car back to Moses, being careful on the way, to earn $4,100ish for top condition.
