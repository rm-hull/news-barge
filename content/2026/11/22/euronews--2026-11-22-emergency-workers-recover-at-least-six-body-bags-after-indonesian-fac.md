## Video footage posted on social media by Indonesia's National Search and Rescue Agency (Basarnas), showed emergency crews looking through the charred remains of the building.

Emergency workers in Indonesia have recovered at least six body bags after a major factory fire in western Java.

Indonesian police said the blaze broke out at a candy factory in the Serang Regency of Banten province on Saturday.

A joint emergency team removed the six body bags from the site, police said.

A further 42 out of 54 recorded employees remain unaccounted for, "while 11 returned home and one is receiving medical treatment," the force added.

"We are also continuing to coordinate with families and the company to verify the whereabouts of employees suspected to have been inside the factory area when the fire broke out," senior superintendent Maruli Ahiles Hutapea said in a statement.

"Meanwhile, search and evacuation operations inside the production and office buildings will be carried out once the cooling process is complete and the site is declared safe by personnel."

The fire began at around 4:30 pm on Saturday and was extinguished by 6:30 am on Sunday, according to police.

Video footage posted on social media by Indonesia's National Search and Rescue Agency (Basarnas), showed emergency crews looking through the charred remains of the building.

The candy company, Fonusa Agung Mulia, describes itself online as a subsidiary of Indonesia's UNIFAM food company.
