Emergency services were called to a car fire in York early this morning (Sunday).

They went to the incident on **Etty Avenue** at**3.24am**.

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “York crew attended a residential street and extinguished a fire involving a parked car.

#### Etty Avenue, York

“One hose reel jet was used to extinguish the fire. A thermal image camera was used for checks.

“The crew liaised with police on scene and gave advice to the owner of the vehicle.”
