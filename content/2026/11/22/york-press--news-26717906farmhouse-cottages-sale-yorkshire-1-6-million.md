## Farmhouse and cottages hit the market on Yorkshire coast – for £1.6 million

Island Farm Cottage which is for sale in Staintondale, north of Scarborough, for £1.6 million *(Image: Carter Jonas/Zoopla)*

A farmhouse and three cottages have hit the market on the North Yorkshire coast – for £1.6 million.

Island Farm Cottage is for sale in Staintondale, north of Scarborough.

Three self-contained cottages, two of which are currently used as holiday lets, are included in the sale, as well as a range of equestrian facilities.

![](https://www.yorkpress.co.uk/resources/images/21699734.jpg?type=mds-article-620)Inside Island Farm Cottage which is for sale in Staintondale, north of Scarborough, for £1.6 million *(Image: Carter Jonas/Zoopla)*

Carter Jonas is handling the sale and said Island Farm Cottage is an “attractive property of stone construction under a pantile roof which has been sympathetically refurbished throughout to the highest of standards.”

Inside the main farmhouse is an open plan kitchen, dining and living area.

There is a home office and a lounge with doors leading to an outdoor living area.

Also on the ground floor is a bedroom with access available directly from the courtyard which Ashtons said could also be incorporated into the adjoining holiday let.

Three more bedroom suites are on the first floor, each with their own en-suite accommodation.

Bee’s Cottage adjoins the farmhouse and comprises a self-contained property with an open-plan kitchen, dining and living area and one bedroom.

Orchard and Thistle Cottages, a pair of self-contained holiday lets, are also connected to the farmhouse. Each cottage has an open plan kitchen/living area together and a bedroom with en-suite facilities.

Also included in the sale is a workshop, stables and 18.99 acres of land.
