## National Trust property to be decked with more than 20 Christmas trees this year

A 12 foot tree greets all visitors on their arrival *(Image: National Trust - Anthony Chappel Ross)*

A historic home is inviting visitors to step into a festive celebration of nature and community this Christmas.

Nunnington Hall, managed by the National Trust, is hosting its annual Christmas programme, Nature’s Noel: A Community Christmas, from November 19 to December 20.

The event features more than 20 Christmas trees and seasonal displays throughout the manor, with displays created by schools, charities, and community groups using natural, recycled, and reused materials.

Sarah Nolan, visitor experience officer at Nunnington Hall, said: "It's been wonderful to see the variety of ideas people have brought to this year's displays.

"We asked groups to take inspiration from Nunnington Hall, and the results have been incredibly creative.

"Some have focused on the house itself, others on the gardens, landscape or wildlife.

"Together they've created a festive experience that's full of character, surprises and details for visitors to discover."

The handmade decorations, garlands, and wreaths have been crafted by a team of dedicated volunteers.

Each display is inspired by the manor’s architecture, collections, gardens, and surrounding landscape.

Visitors can enjoy a family trail through the gardens, followed by seasonal treats in the tea room.

The gardens remain open throughout the season, providing a peaceful setting for a winter stroll.

For those looking for something extra special, Nunnington Hall will host Festive Evening openings on December 5 and 12, with extended hours until 8pm.

These evenings offer the chance to see the house illuminated after dark and browse a mini festive market.

Families can also book a visit with Father Christmas, where children can share their letters, listen to seasonal stories, and receive a book to take home.

A spokesman for the National Trust said: "Whether exploring the community-created displays, following the family trail through the gardens, enjoying a festive evening opening or gathering with family and friends over seasonal refreshments, Nature's Noel: A Community Christmas offers plenty of opportunities to make Christmas memories together at Nunnington Hall."

Other National Trust properties across York and North Yorkshire are also inviting visitors to enjoy festive experiences.

At Treasurer’s House in York, an Inspired Christmas transports guests to Nancy Lycett Green’s 1906 debutante ball, with the house dressed for the season.

Beningbrough Hall offers a Father Christmas adventure where young visitors help save the season.

In addition to the pre-bookable experience, guests can enjoy seasonal shopping, refreshments, and a winter day out exploring the hall and gardens.

The spokesman said: "Together, the three places offer a range of Christmas experiences, from community-created displays and Edwardian elegance to family adventures, festive food and seasonal shopping."

Nunnington Hall, its gardens, and tea room are open Thursday to Sunday from 10.30am to 4pm, with last entry at 3.30pm.

Festive Evening openings run from 10.30am to 8pm, with last entry at 7.30pm.

Visits with Father Christmas must be booked in advance.

Normal admission applies, but entry is free for National Trust members.

For more information and to plan your visit, go to www.nationaltrust.org.uk/nunnington-hall.
