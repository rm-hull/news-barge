# Castle 30 minutes from Leeds is turning into the ultimate winter wonderland

## A castle 30 minutes from Leeds is set to open its magical Christmas Kingdom on Friday, November 27, complete with a light trail, Santa's grotto, woodland funfair and more

If you are a Leeds parent desperately searching for a fresh, festive experience to keep the kids entertained this winter, you might want to look just outside the city. A spectacular 11th-century Yorkshire estate is currently undergoing a massive transformation to become a magical winter wonderland, and it's right on our doorstep.

Tucked away deep in the natural woodland of Hazlewood Castle, near Tadcaster, the brand-new 'Christmas Kingdom' is set to open its doors to the public on Friday, November 27.

Taking to TikTok, early years teacher and content creator @earlyyearsteachermum recently shared a behind-the-scenes "sneak peek" of the construction, detailing exactly what local families can expect from the highly anticipated event.

The main attraction is a dazzling, handcrafted light trail that winds along the natural paths beneath the estate's trees. Designed to enchant visitors of all ages, the 'Secret Forest Light Trail' takes around an hour to complete.

Alongside the illuminated walk, the woodland will be brought to life by roaming actors, allowing little ones to fully immerse themselves in the festive storyline. Kids can also write their hopes for the season and peg them onto the magical 'wishing trees' before heading over to enjoy a traditional family funfair.

Whatever the unpredictable Yorkshire winter weather brings, visitors will be able to warm up in a purpose-built, sheltered food and drink area named the 'Forest Feast'.

As well as offering seating and the chance to toast marshmallows over an open fire with the whole family, the cosy area will serve up a great variety of seasonal food prepared by Hazlewood Castle's own chefs.

### Tickets and pricing

If you are planning to make the trip, the TikToker urged parents to book their time slots well in advance to avoid disappointment.

Tickets for the Christmas Kingdom's Secret Forest Light Trail start from £15. If you want to make the visit extra special for the little ones, you can add optional tickets for Santa's Grotto to your booking for £15 per child.

### How to get there from Leeds

Hazlewood Castle is incredibly easy to reach from Leeds, making it an ideal evening or weekend excursion.

Located just off the A64 heading east towards York, the drive takes roughly 30 minutes from Leeds city centre depending on traffic.

Simply follow the A64 past the A1(M) junction near Aberford, and look out for the signposted turning for Hazlewood Castle and Paradise Lane just before you reach Tadcaster.
