**Tennessee Titans linebacker Jaylen Harrell is in hospital after a car crash in Florida, with police investigating whether he assaulted a rideshare driver moments before the collision.**

The 24-year-old was a back-seat passenger in his hometown of Tampa on Friday morning "when he reportedly assaulted the driver while the vehicle was in motion", Tampa Police said.

According to police, the driver, a man in his mid-30s, swerved and hit another vehicle before crashing into a utility pole.

Harrell was thrown forward into the windscreen and lost consciousness.

The rideshare driver sustained minor injuries and was treated at the scene, while the driver of the second vehicle was uninjured.

Tennessee Titans said in a statement, external they are in "constant communication" with Harrell's family and are working to gather further information.

"At this time, Jaylen remains in the hospital and continues to receive care," the Titans added. "We ask that Jaylen and his family's privacy be respected at this time. We will provide an update when appropriate."

Harrell has not featured for the Titans during the 2026 NFL season after tearing his anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) in pre-season.

His father, James Harrell, played eight seasons in the NFL.
