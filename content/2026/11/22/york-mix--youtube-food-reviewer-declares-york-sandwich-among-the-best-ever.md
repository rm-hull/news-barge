A popular YouTube food reviewer has visited a York sandwich shop and declared it “absolutely fantastic”.

Danny Malin travels the world in search of great food. His Danny vs Food channel has 187,000 subscribers.

Two days ago he posted his video about a visit to **Salt Pig Sandwiches** on Fossgate in York.

And he was blown away.

In his video review, which has been viewed more than 215K times, Danny tried three of Salt Pig’s sandwiches, the Hot Lips, the Greedy Get and the Pepper Pig.

He declared the Hot Lips (nduja, fennel salami, provolone, rocket, hot honey, balsamic – £6.50 / £9.50) as “absolutely fantastic”

He praised the saltiness from the salami complemented by “the beautiful bit of creamy cheese”.

Danny was equally effusive about the Greedy Get (celeriac remoulade, turkey, fennel salami, ham, gouda – £10).

“It’s just a beautiful bite of a beautiful sandwich,” Danny said.

![](https://wp.theyorkmix.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/danny-vs-food-salt-pig-york-1.webp)

Finally it was time for the Pepper Pig (ham, jalapeno relish, provolone, sun dried chilli mayo –£5.75 / £8.50).

“I’ve been hunting for the UK’s best sandwich,” he told viewers. “I liked Granny G’s but I think this has just knocked Granny G’s off the top.

“This is one of the best sandwiches I’ve ever tasted.”

Tim Jervis launched Italian-inspired Salt Pig Sandwiches in October 2025. It has since won rave reviews.
