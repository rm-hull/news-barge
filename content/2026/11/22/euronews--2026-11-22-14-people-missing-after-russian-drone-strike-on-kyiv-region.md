## Timur Tkachenko, the head of the Kyiv Regional Military Administration, said the bodies of two people had been recovered from the rubble and that at least four people had been injured.

Fifteen people are missing after a Russian drone strike destroyed part of a five-story apartment building in the Kyiv region, Ukrainian police said on Sunday.

Search and rescue operations are underway at the scene of the attack in Dymer, north of the Ukrainian capital.

"Fifteen people are currently listed as missing, including two children: an 11-year-old girl and a seven-year-old boy," Ukraine's National Police wrote in a post on Telegram.

Images from the scene show emergency crews picking through the debris as they work to find survivors.

Timur Tkachenko, the head of the Kyiv Regional Military Administration, said the bodies of two people had been recovered from the rubble and that at least four people had been injured.

He added that a command centre had been set up to help coordinate a response to the attack, with social services and psychologists working out of the site.

It comes after Russian forces fired more than 400 attack drones and over 110 missiles at Ukraine from Thursday night into Friday morning. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called it "one of the largest attacks" of the war so far.

At least seven people were killed in Kyiv in those strikes, while key bridges across the Dnipro River were also damaged.

On Sunday, Zelenskyy said Russian forces had launched around 2,700 drones, close to 1,460 aerial bombs, and 165 missiles at 20 Ukrainian regions this week.

"A significant number were ballistic missiles, including North Korean ones, which the Russians are constantly helping Pyongyang modernize to improve their accuracy," Zelenskyy said, as he renewed calls for allies to speed up deliveries of interceptor missiles.

"It is particularly difficult to understand the delays in supplying anti-ballistic interceptors to Ukraine," the Ukrainian leader added, urging Washington to authorise transfers of interceptors already paid for through NATO's PURL initiative.

Ukrainian forces reportedly also carried out their own strikes overnight, targeting a Russian National Guard facility in an attack on Krasnodar.

Five people are reported to have been injured in the strikes on the region, including four in Afipsky, a settlement home to an oil refinery that has previously been targeted by Ukraine.
