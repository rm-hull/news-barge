A North Yorkshire furniture retailer has gone into liquidation with debts topping £50,000.

Bed Guru Ltd of Kirkgate, Knaresborough, was incorporated in 2015 and went into a creditors' voluntary liquidation on November 6.

Leeds-based Breese Business Recovery were appointed liquidator.

Companies House records Bed Guru's directors as Jane Helen Slimming and Carl Peter Walsh.

A report into the company’s finances known as a statement of affairs has been produced by the liquidators.

It reports Bed Guru Ltd had estimated realisable assets of £26,493.67.

When it came to liabilities, the company owed £629.36 to two employees, who are classed as preferential creditors – someone paid first.

In addition, HMRC was owed £52.40 as a secondary preference creditor – someone paid next.

This left £25,811.91 available for distribution to unsecured creditors – those who are paid last.

The report reveals unsecured creditors were owed £80,541.90 between them.

This was made up of trade and expense creditors owed £60,920.00; consumer creditors owed £985.70; and two employees owed £18,636.20 between them.

This gave a shortfall or deficit of £54,729.99.

With Bed Guru Ltd having issued and called up capital of £200, this gave an overall deficit or shortfall of £54,929.99, the statement of affairs added.
