The footage, recorded by a driver travelling through Lviv on Friday 20 November, shows the **moment a projectile struck a building directly ahead of the vehicle**. The impact sent debris across the road and forced the driver to reverse sharply before driving away.** Two people were killed and at least 10 others injured in Lviv’s Zaliznychnyi district**as a large-scale Russian missile and drone barrage tore across the western Ukrainian city and its surrounding region, igniting fires in residential blocks and garages. According to Ukrainian authorities, the bombardment left a trail of destruction spanning homes, vehicles, a dormitory, a warehouse and critical energy infrastructure, while also damaging a ticket terminal at** Sknyliv railway station**.

The fallout extended beyond the city limits into the nearby **Zolochiv district**, where debris from a downed Russian drone damaged** three houses**and a vehicle, leaving** one further person injured**.

Ukrainian **President Volodymyr Zelenskyy** said Russia had launched more than**400 attack drones and over 110 missiles**, including 27 ballistic missiles, in the wider assault. The strikes also affected** Kyiv and other parts of Ukraine**, underscoring the scale of the bombardment and the continuing threat to civilians and essential infrastructure.
