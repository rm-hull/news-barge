**Carrying umbrellas and banners**, demonstrators filled the capital's main avenues, chanting**"Higher wages, better pensions"** and denouncing the growing gap between household expenses and salaries. Placards reading**"Everything increases except salaries"** highlighted concerns over housing, food, energy and fuel prices. Organised by Portugal's main trade union confederation, CGTP, the nationwide demonstration brought together workers from across the country.**Housing affordability** emerged as a central concern, with protesters warning that rising rents were making it increasingly**difficult to cover basic expenses**. The demonstration took place ahead of the** final parliamentary debate**on Portugal's** budget for 2027**, with a vote scheduled for** Tuesday 24 November**. Further protests were announced as unions sought to increase pressure on the government over wages, pensions and living standards.
