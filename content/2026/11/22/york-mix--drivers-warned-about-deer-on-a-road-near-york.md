Police are warning drivers to be careful due to the sighting of deer near a busy York junction.

A number of deer have been spotted around **Wheldrake Lane** towards**Crockey Hill**.

In a community message, police said: “Deer are most active during the early morning and evening hours, but can appear on roads at any time.

#### Wheldrake Lane near Crockey Hill

“They may move unpredictably, particularly if travelling in groups, and can suddenly run into the path of vehicles.

“Drivers, motorcyclists and cyclists are all asked to remain vigilant when using this route. This matter has been passed to the parish council to raise the question on getting deer warning signs installed.”

To help prevent collisions, police say:

* Reduce your speed, especially during dawn and dusk.
* Stay alert for deer warning signs and movement at the roadside.
* Be aware that where one deer crosses, others are often close behind.
* Use full-beam headlights where appropriate and safe to do so, but dip them for oncoming traffic.
* Allow extra stopping distance and avoid sudden swerving, which can increase the risk of a collision.

If you encounter injured wildlife following a collision, ensure your own safety first and report the incident via 101 or 999 in an emergency.
