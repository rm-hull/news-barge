![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Express reads: Kate: My road to recovery](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/0f4f/live/98316c00-e5f5-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

"Brave princess reveals how cancer battle changed her life," reads the Sunday Express's front page this morning. It is one of several papers to picture Catherine, Princess of Wales completing the Three Peaks Challenge in June after it was announced that a documentary about her experience with cancer would air next week. The paper also covers "superb England" beating the All Blacks by a record margin on Saturday.

![The headline on the front page of the Mail on Sunday reads: William's plan to strip Harry of his titles when he's the king](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/1531/live/a6772390-e5f5-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

Royal news also dominates the Mail on Sunday's front page. Alongside Kate's documentary, the paper reports that a "bombshell book" will claim that the Prince of Wales "plans to strip Harry of his titles" when he becomes king. Non-working royals would lose their "HRH" titles under Prince William's planned "radical shake-up", according to the Mail. A spokesman for the Prince and Princess of Wales told the paper that they did not comment on books or claims made within them.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Telegraph reads: EU reset 'will cost families £281 a year'](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/3a5b/live/798518b0-e5f5-11f1-99b6-a35a62fa4264.jpg)

The Sunday Telegraph's front page likewise features an image of Catherine ahead of the documentary, Heal, which will be broadcast on the BBC. In other news, the paper reports that the Conservatives have warned Andy Burnham's "EU reset deal" will cost the average British family "an extra £281 a year in energy bills". The prime minister has called the agreement a "win-win" which will help families with the cost of living. The Telegraph also covers England's 37-12 rugby union triumph over New Zealand.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Times reads: Killed after police incompetence](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/3291/live/86762ad0-e5f7-11f1-99a1-253766e7ad8b.jpg)

The Sunday Times also continues to cover reaction to Friday's UK-EU summit. The head of the University of St Andrews has warned of "unintended consequences" should tuition fees be cut for European students, the paper reports, while its sketch shows a man holding a "Brexit reset" pamphlet and telling his doctor: "I'd like to reopen the old wound from when I shot myself in the foot." The Times also reports on the "catalogue of failures" which saw three people killed by a man released from prison "in the midst of a psychotic episode" in 2019. The local police force said it was addressing "areas of learning".

![The headline on the front page of the Observer reads: How Jilly Cooper plagiarised the works of other novelists](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/0798/live/b6c8ef10-e606-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.png)

"Andy wants to rejoin", reads the Observer's front page, as it reports Burnham has been "absolutely clear" in meetings with European leaders that he "wants to be the prime minister who takes the UK back into the EU". The prime minister plans to discuss possible options for even closer ties with the bloc, but has said the public "will be the boss of what happens next". The paper also claims that the late author Dame Jilly Cooper "plagiarised the works of other novelists".

![The headline on the front page of the Sun on Sunday reads: Joanna's sex bully torment on ITV drama](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/5dc8/live/bc8a6250-e5f5-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The Sun on Sunday reports that Joanna Page earlier this week said she had been "sexually bullied" while working on an ITV show. The paper reports that it happened on the drama Making Waves. An ITV spokesman told the Sun it was "completely committed to creating an environment where everyone is treated with respect". They said it would always investigate complaints or serious concerns and take action where required, and encouraged people to report inappropriate behaviour.

![The headline on the front page of the Sunday Mirror reads: Tyson will keep on fighting](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/966f/live/d85d7df0-e5f5-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

"Tyson will keep on fighting" is the Sunday Mirror's headline, as it carries its interview with Paris Fury. She tells the paper that her husband, boxer Tyson Fury, would continue fighting "because he needs it for his mental health".

![The headline on the front page of the Daily Star reads: Katy who?!](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/2952/live/c9ed33f0-e5f5-11f1-a094-5fdc692f3e33.jpg)

The Daily Star meanwhile reports that jailed comedian Russell Brand "could not remember the dates of his marriage to pop superstar Katy Perry", citing US court documents. The paper calls it a "bizarre claim".
