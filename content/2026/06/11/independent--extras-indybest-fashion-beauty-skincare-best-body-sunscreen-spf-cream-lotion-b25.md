If you’re jetting off to sunnier climates, sunscreen is as essential for packing as your passport. And it’s not just for abroad holidays, even sunny UK days in the city or along the coast warrant the application of SPF. Trust us, the right body sunscreen and face SPF can be the difference between fun, sun-soaked days and painful sunburn.

Yet, as a country, our application habits remain far from perfect . According to a 2023 study by Melanoma Focus, only around 32% of adults in the UK say they regularly use sunscreen, despite the well-known risks of UV exposure.

To find out more about SPF, I enlisted the expertise of Dr. Conal Perrett. He told me: “Consistent sunscreen use significantly reduces the risk of skin cancer. In the UK, skin cancer is now the most common form of cancer, with melanoma rates having more than doubled over the past few decades.” In fact, just one severe sunburn during your adolescence can raise the risk of melanoma later in life, he says. Also, people under 35 who experience UV rays face “up to a 75% higher chance of developing the cancer.”

However, Dr. Perrett also told me that, if used as directed, sunscreen can “protect the skin from the harmful effects of ultraviolet radiation”. Of course, it’s also important to choose a formula that suits your skin’s specific needs, whether that’s dryness or sensitivity, so I set about testing a range of creams, mists and sprays to find the standout options. Below, you’ll find my tried-and-tested pick of the best body sunscreens for every budget and skin concern.
