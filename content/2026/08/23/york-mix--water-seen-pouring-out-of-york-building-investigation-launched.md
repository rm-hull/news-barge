An investigation was launched after was was seen “seen pouring out” of a building in York.

Firefighters from Acomb went to the incident on **Shipton Road** at**8.51pm last night** (Saturday).

A North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service spokesperson said: “Acomb crews investigated a report of water seen pouring out of a disused building.

#### Shipton Road, York

“The crew was unable to gain access to the building. Yorkshire Water was requested to isolate the external supply.”
