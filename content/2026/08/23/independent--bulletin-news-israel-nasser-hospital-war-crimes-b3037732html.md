# Calls for UK to suspend trade with Israel over hospital attack that killed journalists

- A joint investigation by *The Independent* and*Independent Arabia* uncovered evidence that an August 2025 Israeli double-tap attack on Gaza's Nasser Medical Complex may constitute war crimes.
- The strikes killed at least 20 people, including *Independent* correspondent Mariam Abu Dagga and Reuters cameraman Hussam al-Masri, as journalists and first responders gathered following an initial strike.
- International law experts concluded that a prosecutor could reasonably find the Israeli military committed war crimes by intentionally targeting civilians and launching a disproportionate attack.
- The investigation refuted Israeli military claims that a Hamas camera was targeted, confirming that the equipment belonged to Reuters and that civil defence workers were falsely labelled as terrorists.
- British MPs have urged the UK government to take strong measures, including suspending trade with Israel and expanding sanctions, while demanding accountability through international courts.
