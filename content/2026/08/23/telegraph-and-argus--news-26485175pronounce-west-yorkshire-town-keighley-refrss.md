From tourists to people from the south of England, many people will completely mess up the pronunciation of Yorkshire places they have never been to before.

If you are from Yorkshire, it can be quite frustrating when people pronounce different towns and villages incorrectly.

Keighley is a market town found in West Yorkshire and is only a short drive away from the city of Bradford.

If you know someone who struggles with pronouncing places in Yorkshire like Keighley correctly, you might want to send them this guide.


## How do you pronounce Keighley?

While it might look easy to pronounce, Keighley often catches people out.

It looks like it is pronounced like Keeley or Kaylee, but if you want to impress the locals, do not say it like that.

It is actually pronounced keeth-lee.

Under a TikTok video talking about places in the UK that everyone pronounces wrong, one commenter mentioned Keighley and said: “Keighley in West Yorkshire always gets me.

“It’s pronounced Keith lee.”

One person replied: “I work in Keighley and it always makes me cringe when someone out of area pronounces it keelee.”

Another person responded: “I came here looking for Keighley.”

## 
  **What can you do in Keighley?**

Keighley is home to the Cliffe Castle Museum and Park, which is completely free to look around.

The museum has different rooms to explore, with paintings, furniture and art showcasing a range of styles.

After walking around the museum, you can take a break in the gardens surrounding it.

You can also take a trip on the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway, which takes you through five miles of West Yorkshire countryside.

You can visit a range of villages along the way at each of the stations.

In terms of food, Keighley has a range of cuisines available, from great Italian food at Amici to award-winning Indian dishes from Azeem’s.

There are also lots of bars and pubs where you can relax, such as Taylor’s on the Green, where you can sit in the beer garden with a pint or cocktail.
