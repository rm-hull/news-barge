Andrew Garfield has opened up about his desire to escape the Hollywood spotlight one day.

The *Amazing Spider-Man* star has spent much of the past year working on back-to-back projects, including the new Enid Blyton adaptation*The Magic Faraway Tree*, the action period drama* The Uprising*and Luca Guadagnino’s Sam Altman biopic,* Artificial.*

In a new interview with *Variety*, Garfield, 43, was asked if he ever thought about leaving his career in the public eye behind.

“All the time,” Garfield admitted.

“I’ve worked a lot this year. I’ve done like 11 months, 12 months, straight back-to-back projects. I want people to see those projects, so I’m gonna have to tell people about them. I get that. But if I had my druthers, people would just see them, and I could go whittle wood in the Somerset countryside and think about what the birds might be doing.”

![Garfield currently stars in ‘The Magic Faraway Tree’ alongside Claire Foy](https://static.the-independent.com/2026/03/23/8/35/251002_the_magic_far_away_tree_reel_1_grade_RECUT_no_logos_007_0013902_Claire_Andrew.jpg)

Earlier in the interview, Garfield spoke about fulfilling his dreams as an actor. “I still have dreams, but they’re slightly more earthbound than my previous ones,” he said.

“They’re maybe a bit more philosophical and existential than achievement-based. Now I think it’s more I want a certain quality of life. I want a certain quality of relationships with my family and friends. I want a certain quality of relationship with myself.”

The *Hacksaw Ridge* star does have form when it comes to taking a break from the spotlight. In 2022, following the release of three major films (*The Eyes of Tammy Faye, tick, tick… BOOM! and Spider-Man: No Way Home*), Garfield revealed he was taking a career hiatus.

“I need to recalibrate and reconsider what I want to do next and who I want to be and just be a bit of a person for a while,” he told *Variety* at the time.

“Because as you know, that is a washing machine, that awards season. I need to just be a bit ordinary for a while.”

His absence from the screen lasted roughly two years following the release of Hulu’s true-crime series *Under the Banner of Heaven* in 2022 before he officially returned to lead the romantic drama*We Live in Time*, which premiered in late 2024.

*The Magic Faraway Tree* is out now in theaters.
