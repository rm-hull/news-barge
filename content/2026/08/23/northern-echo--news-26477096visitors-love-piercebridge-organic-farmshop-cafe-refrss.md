A family-run organic farm shop near Darlington has won praise from visitors for its high-quality meat, fresh vegetables and friendly service.

Piercebridge Organic Farmshop and Cafe is based on a 300-acre organic farm, which has been run by the same family since 1964.

The farm produces much of the meat sold in its shop, including beef from Aberdeen Angus cattle, pork from Landrace Duroc pigs, and lamb from a mixed flock of sheep.

It also keeps hens for eggs and rears birds for the table.

At the heart of the business is its on-site butchery, where staff prepare meat from animals reared on the farm.

The team makes its own burgers, sausages, ham, bacon and cooked meats, alongside more traditional joints and cuts.

Piercebridge describes its approach as that of an “old-fashioned butcher”, with the farm overseeing the process from rearing animals through to preparing the meat for customers.

Visitors have regularly praised the quality of the produce.

One recent customer described it as a “fabulous organic farm shop”. At the same time, another said it was a “fantastic farm shop for high quality organic meat and stock cupboard groceries and vegetables”.

Fresh vegetables have also impressed shoppers.

One visitor said it was “so nice to have local organic produce” and added that the vegetables tasted noticeably better than supermarket alternatives.

They acknowledged customers could expect to pay more for organic produce but said: “You get the quality.”

The traditional butcher’s counter is another major attraction.

One reviewer praised the “great quality meat” and described the shop as a “lovely traditional butcher's” with a good range of organic products.

Another customer, who bought lamb from the deli, called it “delicious” and also praised the cheese and coleslaw.


Christmas produce has earned strong feedback too.

One visitor gave the shop five stars after buying chicken and gammon for Christmas, describing both as “outstanding”.

They also praised the “very friendly helpful staff” and said Piercebridge was somewhere they planned to keep returning to.

The adjoining cafe has also proved popular.

A recent visitor simply described it as a “lovely cafe, shop and staff”, while another customer singled out the coffee as “excellent”.

For some shoppers, Piercebridge is the sort of place they discover after driving past its signs for years.

One visitor said they had passed the signpost “on many occasions” before finally calling in.

They were impressed by the selection of meat, vegetables, cheese, and other products and said they would “definitely be back”.

Alongside food, the farm has also developed a strong reputation for conservation.

It has planted miles of hedgerows, field margins and beetle banks as part of environmental stewardship work, helping increase wildlife across the land.

Birds recorded on the farm include skylarks, curlews, lapwings, barn owls, tawny owls and grey partridge.

Piercebridge has also received recognition for its conservation efforts, including a Silver Lapwing award.

The farm shop opens Monday to Saturday from 9am until 3.30pm, and on Sundays from 9.30am until 1pm, although it closes on bank holidays.
