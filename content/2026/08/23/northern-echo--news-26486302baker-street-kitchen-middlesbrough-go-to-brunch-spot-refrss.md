Finding a restaurant where you'll struggle to choose between a traditional full English and a good stack of pancakes is no easy task - but this Teesside brunch spot has spent years doing exactly that.

In the heart of Middlesbrough, the independent Baker Street Kitchen has built a loyal local following with its quality coffee, generous portions and an all-day menu that keeps breakfast lovers, brunch fans and lunchtime diners full.

With no rush to meet the usual breakfast cut-off, customers can order everything from a Full English to pancake stacks, avocado on toast or steak and eggs whenever they fancy.

  Avocado on toast at Baker Street, Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: TRIPADVISOR)*

The stylish but relaxed interior has makes it the perfect go-to spot for everything from breakfasts spent catching up with friends and family, to a quick on-the-go coffee break.

On the main menu, traditional cooked breakfasts sit alongside omelettes, waffles and pancake stacks topped with everything from bacon and maple syrup to Nutella and banana.

For those who like to mix up their sweet and savoury breakfasts, Baker Street has crispy chicken waffles and pancakes on the menu to spice things up.

  Baker Street pancakes. 

*(Image: Baker Street Kitchen)*

  Baker Street Kitchen, Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: Baker Street Kitchen)*

While breakfast may be the headline act, the lunch options can hold their own too.

Burgers, toasted sandwiches, jacket potatoes and salads all feature on the menu, and halloumi fans will be pleased to know that there are a few options that put the squeaky cheese centre stage.

A separate dedicated menu for vegan diners has lots of choice, including a vegan Full English, breakfast bagels and vegan pancake options such as Biscoff, maple and sausage, and berry and yoghurt.

To go alongside your meal, Baker Street has everything from freshly brewed teas and the café's own coffee blend, to iced coffees, vegan smoothies and milkshakes that are particularly popular during the warmer months.

Those looking for something more indulgent can even pair brunch with a glass of Prosecco.

  Baker Street Kitchen, Middlesbrough. 

*(Image: Tees Valley Combined Authority)*

If you're simply in for a quick coffee and cake break, the selection of sweet treats behind the counter changes day-to-day, so make sure to take a look at what's on offer before you take your seat.

Everything on the menu is available to take away, with collection and delivery options helping customers enjoy their favourites across the region with just the click of a button.

In a region packed with cafés and coffee shops, competition for the title of Teesside's best brunch destination is fierce.

But with all-day breakfasts, towering pancake stacks, quality coffee, vegan options and a menu that caters for almost every craving, Baker Street Kitchen has certainly put itself firmly in the conversation.
