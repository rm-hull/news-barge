Somehow I've blinked and half of *Reacher* season 4 has already passed us by. The bad news is that we've only got four episodes left, but the good news is that the political espionage plot at its heart is far from being solved.

As we've seen over the first four episodes, the eponymous hero is embroiled in a CIA conspiracy after suspecting a woman on a New York City subway train is actually a suicide bomber. By the second, it's snowballing into something more complex than anyone could have expected.

But, can Reacher and the gang get to the bottom of things before anyone else dies? And when does *Reacher* season 4 episode 5 arrive on Prime Video?

## What time can I watch Reacher season 4 episode 5 on Prime Video?

![REACHER Season 4 - Official Trailer | Prime Video - YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/PCbp1FfipPg/maxresdefault.jpg) 

We're expecting episode 5 of *Reacher* season 4 to be available on**August 26 at 12am PT / 3am ET in the US**.

Internationally, look out for the below times:

- **US** – 12am PT / 3am ET
- **Canada** – 12am PT / 3am ET
- **UK** – 8am BST
- **India** – 12:30pm IST
- **Singapore** – 3pm SGT
- **Australia** – 5pm AEST
- **New Zealand** – 7pm NZST

## When do new episodes of Reacher season 4 come out?

- **Episode 1:** out now
