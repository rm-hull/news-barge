# La La Land and Gilmore Girls actor Bunny Levine dies in California

![Bunny Levine in 'Jury Duty'](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/23/9/54/Screenshot-2026-08-23-at-10-45-02.png)

- Actor Bunny Levine, who appeared in *Gilmore Girls**, Law & Order*, and* La La Land* , has died aged 97 at her home in Tarzana, California.
- Her representative confirmed her death to NBC Los Angeles, with police saying that she appeared to have died from natural causes.
- Levine began her Hollywood career in her fifties, having previously worked for more than two decades as a school librarian after raising her three children.
- She became a prolific television actor throughout the 2000s and 2010s, featuring in hit shows such as *Everybody Loves Raymond**,** Ugly Betty**, Community*, and* Jury Duty.* 
- Predominantly cast in comedic roles, Levine's final film appearance was in the 2026 indie horror project *The Cure.*
