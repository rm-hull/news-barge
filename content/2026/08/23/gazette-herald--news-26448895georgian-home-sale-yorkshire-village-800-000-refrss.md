A Georgian home located in an “outstanding village setting” in North Yorkshire has hit the market – for £800,000.

The four-bedroom Grade II-listed house is for sale in Sutton-on-the-Forest.

Churchills, which is marketing the property, said it has been “beautifully preserved and thoughtfully enhanced”, resulting in the home being “rich in period character yet seamlessly adapted for modern living”.

  Inside the four-bedroom Georgian home for sale in Sutton-on-the-Forest for £800,000 

*(Image: Churchills/Zoopla)*

On the ground floor is an elegant dining room, along with a sitting room and a study.

The orangery kitchen to the rear of the house is a “standout feature”, according to Churchills.

“This exceptional space is flooded with natural light via a dramatic lantern roof with electric sun blind, alongside composite windows and French doors opening onto the terrace and gardens beyond,” a spokesperson for the estate agent said.

Upstairs, the main bedroom and two more double bedrooms are on the first floor, as well as the family bathroom.

Another bedroom is on the second floor with a rear-facing window and roof light.

Outside is a large enclosed garden with raised vegetable beds, a green house and more, including an outbuilding which was once stables.
