## Where are Man City in midfield search?

Manchester City’s midfield rebuild is not yet complete, Enzo Maresca has said, with **Enzo Fernandez** and**Adam Wharton** both linked with the club in recent days. City are poised to sign**Ayyoub Bouaddi** but the departure of Rodri has still left Maresca short. 

"I'll try to find a new Rodri - it is what it is,” Maresca said on Friday. “Rodri, before he arrived here, he didn't win the Ballon d'Or, he didn't win the Champions League, he didn't win the Club World Cup. Then he joined the club and then after he achieved many important things.”

It would take around £120m for City to sign Fernandez while it would also take a huge fee to convince Palace to sell Wharton at this stage of the window. But City are set to sell **Savinho** and**Omar Marmoush** to Tottenham in a double swoop that could reach around £150m. They are in talks over signing Brazilian winger**Allan** from Palmeiras. The 22-year-old has a release clause of £85m. 

City kick off their Premier League campaign at home to Bournemouth at 2pm this afternoon.

## Will Ollie Watkins join Aston Villa exodus?

Aston Villa begin their Premier League campaign at Brighton this afternoon but will **Ollie Watkins** be part of their squad? 

Aston Villa have turned down four bids for the England international from Saudi club Al Hilal amid reports that the 30-year-old wants to move away.

Unai Emery said on Friday: "We have an issue with him, it is clear. We had offers for him but he is our player. I don't want to sell him but it can change if it is a good option for him, the club and I can replace him in the squad.”

Villa could turn to Chelsea striker **Nicolas Jackson** if Watkins follows Youri Tielemans, Morgan Rogers, Ezri Konsa and Lucas Digne in leaving this summer. 

## Man Utd target Alejandro Balde wants to fight for Barcelona place

Manchester United target **Alejandro Balde** wants to remain at Barcelona this summer, according to*Mundo Deportivo* in Spain. Signing a left back remains a priority for United this summer, following the 2-0 opening day defeat at newly promoted Hull yesterday, and there has even been contact between the clubs.  But the 22-year-old Spain international Balde wants to remain at this boyhood club and fight for his place in the team this season. 

## Liverpool continue to be frustrated in search for a winger

Liverpool continue to be frustrated in their approach for a new winger. The Reds reportedly enquired over the availability of Real Madrid forward **Endrick** in the past few days, but the Spanish giants have made clear that the Brazilian is not for sale, or available to join on loan.

Barcola, however, has been left out of PSG’s squad to play Rennes on Sunday evening. It’s the third game in a row where the France international has been left out of the PSG squad, following the Super Cup against Aston Villa and the French Super Cup against Lens.

## Today's top story: Alvarez would welcome Arsenal move?

**Julian Alvarez** would welcome a move to Arsenal this summer – if his dream move to Barcelona falls through. That’s according to the*Standard*, who report that the Argentina international’s stand-off with Atletico Madrid is beginning to turn sour as he continues to push for a move away. Alvarez is not for sale this summer, according to Atletico, who play Villarreal this afternoon. 

Alvarez has been in contact with Mikel Arteta and Arsenal sporting director Andrea Berta, according to reports in Spain. *AS* report that Alvarez has come round to the idea of joining the Gunners if Atletico refuse to sell to Barcelona and the door to joining the Premier League champions remains open. 

Arsenal want to a sign a marquee attacker this summer, but Juventus have closed the door on forward **Kenan Yildiz** joining the Premier League champions. Luciano Spalletti told reports that the Turkey international is not for sale and Juventus want to keep the 21-year-old for the upcoming season.
