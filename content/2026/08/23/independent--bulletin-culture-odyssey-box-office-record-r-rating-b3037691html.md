# The Odyssey smashes major box office record

- Sir Christopher Nolan's adaptation of *The Odyssey* has become the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time.
- The epic movie has reached $1.35bn (£990m) at the global box office, surpassing *Deadpool & Wolverine* 's $1.34bn (£980m).
- An R-rating in the US requires under-17s to be accompanied by an adult, which typically limits box office potential.
- In the UK, the film was rated 15 for strong violence, while Australia granted it an M-rating.
- The Odyssey has become Nolan’s best-reviewed movie to date and made $264m in its opening weekend.
