A serious crash has closed a North Yorkshire road this afternoon (Sunday).

Emergency services are at the scene of the collision on the **Great North Road** that runs parallel to the A1 between**Ledsham** and the**Selby Fork interchange**.

A North Yorkshire Police spokesperson said: “The road is expected to remain closed for some time and motorists are advised to avoid the area and use and alternative route.
