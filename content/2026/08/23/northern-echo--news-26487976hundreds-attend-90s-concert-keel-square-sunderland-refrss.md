Hundreds of music fans packed Keel Square as 90s pop stars brought a day of nostalgic hits to Sunderland.

Love the 90s, part of the city’s One Big Summer programme, saw performances from Eternal, Steps singer Claire Richards, Atomic Kitten’s Natasha Hamilton, Amelle from the Sugababes and DJ Sammy.

  Hundreds of music fans gathered in Keel Square to enjoy Love the 90s, one of the highlights of Sunderland's One Big Summer programme. 

*(Image: North News & Pictures Ltd)*

Classic dance tracks and pop singalongs rang out across the city centre as families, groups of friends and visitors gathered for the event.

The square was transformed into a lively outdoor party, with fans of all ages singing along to well-known songs from the decade.
