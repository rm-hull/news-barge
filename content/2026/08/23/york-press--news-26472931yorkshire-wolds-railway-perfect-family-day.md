If you are struggling to think of things to do in the summer holidays with your children, you might want to check out a heritage railway.

For train-obsessed kids or those who just want an adventure, taking a ride on a heritage railway can be a fun day out.

As East Yorkshire’s only heritage railway, the Yorkshire Wolds Railway is very popular with families.

The railway is found on the old Malton to Driffield line and will take you on a short but sweet journey through the countryside.


## What is the Yorkshire Wolds Railway like?

The train will take you along a part of the old Malton to Driffield line from Fimber Halt, with hopes to expand the railway in the future.

Train rides cost £7.50 for adults and £3.75 for children aged between four and 17 years, with family tickets available.

The Visitor Centre, which can be found in an MK1 coach, explores the history of the old train line.

After enjoying a trip on the train line, you can head to the shop with railway gifts available to remember your trip by and tea and coffee to sip while sitting in an old BR MK2 carriage.

The railway also has lots of events on throughout the year, including Halloween and Christmas-themed train rides.

The site is only open on certain days so you might want to check the Yorkshire Wolds Railway website before planning a trip.
