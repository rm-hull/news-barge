Police are appealing for the public's help to find a 43-year-old man who has been reported missing from Halifax.

West Yorkshire Police issued an appeal on Sunday after Lee was reported missing, with officers expressing concerns for his welfare and urging anyone with information to get in touch.

A photograph released by police shows Lee with short-cropped hair and a closely trimmed beard. In the image, Lee is wearing a light grey sweatshirt and is facing the camera against a plain background.

## Public Asked to Check for Sightings

Officers are asking residents across Halifax and the wider Calderdale area to remain vigilant and report any possible sightings.

Anyone who may have seen Lee, spoken to him recently, or knows where he may be is being urged to contact police.

## How to Help

West Yorkshire Police said: "Have you seen Lee? Lee is 43 years old and is missing from Halifax. Police are concerned for Lee's safety.

They added: " If you have seen him or know where he is, please call 101 and quote log 137 of 23rd August 2026"
