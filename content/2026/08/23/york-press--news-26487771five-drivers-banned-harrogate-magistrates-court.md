Five motorists who committed driving offences in York and North Yorkshire have been banned.

A sixth now has nine penalty points on their licence. All cases were heard at a specialist court at Harrogate Magistrates Court.

Jake Newell, 25, of Little Avenue, Clifton, pleaded guilty to driving without insurance and without a valid licence.

He was banned from driving for six months, fined £120 and ordered to pay £120 and a £48 statutory surcharge.

Adam Endersby, 44, of Foxwood Lane, York, was banned from driving for six months after he pleaded guilty to careless driving on Bootham, York.

He was fined £133 and ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £53 statutory surcharge.

Dylan Jackson, 23, of Hawthorn Close, Malton, pleaded guilty to driving without insurance and without a licence on the B1257 in Malton.

He was banned from driving for six months, fined £600 and ordered to pay a £240 statutory surcharge and £120 prosecution costs.

Michael James Sams, 58, of Low Street, Brotherton, pleaded guilty to speeding in a 40mph zone on the A162 in Barkston Ash.

He was fined £146, ordered to pay a £58 statutory surcharge and £120 prosecution costs and banned from driving for six months.

Audrina Daadipour, 52, of Ouseburn Avenue, York, was convicted of careless driving on Moor Lane, York.

She was fined £1,100, ordered to pay £130 prosecution costs and a £440 statutory surcharge and given nine penalty points.

Jack McKenna, 29, of Glastonbury Drive, Huddersfield, pleaded guilty to speeding at more than 70 mph in a Mercedes on the A64 at Bramham Moor.

He was banned from driving for six months, fined £80 and ordered to pay a £32 statutory surcharge. Harrogate magistrates did not order him to pay prosecution costs after deciding he lacked sufficient income.
