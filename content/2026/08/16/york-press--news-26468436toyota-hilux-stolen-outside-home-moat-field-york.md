*(Image: North Yorkshire Police)*

A black Toyota Hilux was reportedly stolen from outside a home in York.

Police say the vehicle was taken from Moat Field in Osbaldwick, sometime between 7.20pm on Thursday, August 6, and 5.40am on Friday, August 7.

It was parked outside a residential address and was described as being in excellent condition at the time of the alleged theft. Its registration number is YY24 GJJ.

  **Recommended reads:**

Police are now appealing for information from the public to help locate the vehicle.

They are keen to hear from anyone who may have seen the vehicle in the Osbaldwick area during the stated times, witnessed any suspicious activity in Moat Field that night, or has dashcam, CCTV, doorbell footage, or other recordings that could assist.

Additionally, anyone who has seen the vehicle since it went missing is urged to come forward.

Information can be provided online by emailing PC Matla at [Sylvia.Matla@northyorkshire.police.uk](mailto:Sylvia.Matla@northyorkshire.police.uk), or through the North Yorkshire Police website.

The police's live chat service is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Alternatively, people can call North Yorkshire Police on 101.

Those wishing to remain anonymous can contact Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through their website.

People are asked to quote reference 12260152069 when providing information.
