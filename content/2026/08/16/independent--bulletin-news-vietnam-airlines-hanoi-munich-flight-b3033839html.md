# Vietnam Airlines flight forced to return to airport after overshooting runway

![Vietnam Airlines Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner airplane prepares for landing](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/16/15/2026-08-16T014518Z_503505605_RC2DZMAGJ2SW_RTRMADP_3_VIETNAM-AIRLINES-MUNICH-INCIDENT.JPG)

- A Hanoi-bound Vietnam Airlines flight was forced to return to Munich Airport on Saturday following technical difficulties shortly after take-off.
- The Boeing 787 reportedly overran the runway during departure, sending up a large dust cloud captured on video footage.
- The aircraft was required to circle for more than two hours to burn off fuel before landing safely back in Munich.
- Following the landing, the plane was unable to taxi under its own power, resulting in the temporary closure of the airport's north runway.
- Vietnam Airlines confirmed that all passengers and crew members are safe and that an investigation into the incident is ongoing.
