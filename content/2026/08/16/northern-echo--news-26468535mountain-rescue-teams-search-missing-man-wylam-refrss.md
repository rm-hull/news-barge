Police and mountain rescue teams are searching for a missing young man whose family is growing “increasingly concerned”.

Liam was last seen heading towards Wylam from Wylam Bridge, in Northumberland, shortly after midnight in the early hours of Saturday morning (Aug 15).

The 22-year-old, from Northumberland, is described as a white man, 6ft 3ins tall, of medium build with dark hair. He has tattoos down his right arm.

He was last seen wearing black joggers, black Nike trainers, a black T-shirt with large text and a diamond logo on the back, and a gold chain.
