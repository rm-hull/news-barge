# Arsenal dominate Manchester City to win season-opening Community Shield

- Arsenal won the Community Shield with a comfortable 3-0 victory over Manchester City.
- Riccardo Calafiori opened the scoring for the Gunners following a precise pass from Myles Lewis-Skelly, which became the competition’s fastest-ever goal.
- Kai Havertz doubled the lead within 30 minutes after Christos Tzolis set him up and Gianluigi Donnarumma fumbled.
- Martin Odegaard sealed the victory shortly after half-time, finishing a move involving Tzolis and Lewis-Skelly.
- The result marked a disappointing start to Manchester City's new era under manager Enzo Maresca.
