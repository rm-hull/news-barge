This semi-detached home on Wellfield Road in Wingate may not look very different from its neighbours at first glance, but step inside the front door and you’ll soon see that it’s definitely something special.

Extended to offer a wealth of family-friendly accommodation, this home is centred around the breakfast kitchen.

Contemporary in design, it is perfect for both everyday family living and entertaining, with the well-equipped galley-style kitchen being home to modern fitted units with luxurious quartz worktops, a range of integrated appliances, a Belfast-style sink, a peninsular breakfast bar, and double doors into the rear garden.

Alongside this is a dining area with ample room for a large table, plus a door into the garden.

Off the kitchen is a useful utility room incorporating a cloakroom.

Also on the ground floor are the entrance hall with an understairs cupboard; the lounge, with a large bay window to the front plus a focal point feature fireplace housing a stove, and a snug perfect for less formal family gatherings.

Upstairs are a stylish family bathroom with a walk-in shower and five bedrooms, including a single bedroom ideal for use as a home office or nursery.

The property boasts a spacious south-facing rear garden perfect for enjoying sunny afternoons and outdoor gatherings in.

It comprises lawns, a paved patio right across the rear of the house, planted borders, a gravelled seating area, and a second gravelled seating area with a pergola.

To the front of the property are planted borders and an area of hardstanding offering parking for several vehicles.

As well as being located in a highly regarded part of Wingate, this home is close to schools and well placed for those who commute throughout the region.
