An off-road vehicle was “completely destroyed” by a blaze that spread 100 square metres across a forest floor.

Firefighters were called to woodland off South Town Lane, near Loftus, after a dog walker reported seeing smoke last Sunday (August 9).

Crews were sent from Loftus and Saltburn and on arrival found a 4x4 off-road utility vehicle that had been ravaged by flames.

Although the vehicle was no longer alight, the fire had spread into the forest floor, leaving deep-seated hotspots across an area of around 100 square metres.

*(Image: CLEVELAND FIRE BRIGADE)*

The remote location presented additional challenges for firefighters, who worked together to extinguish the fire, tackle hidden hotspots and make the area safe.

In the week following this incident, firefighters had to tackle a surge in blazes amid a scorching heatwave across the country.

The Government even sent out an emergency alert on Friday evening warning millions to not light anything that could spark a wildfire, including barbecues and garden incinerators.
