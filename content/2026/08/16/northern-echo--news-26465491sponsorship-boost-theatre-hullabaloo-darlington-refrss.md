A children’s arts charity in Darlington has secured a new sponsorship deal which it says will help expand its programme and bring more world-class cultural experiences to families across the region.

Theatre Hullabaloo, which is focused on delivering creative experiences for young people, has announced financial support from Northumbria Financial Management (NFM), a Durham-based financial planning firm.

Emma Rees, executive director and joint CEO at Theatre Hullabaloo, said: "We are thrilled to welcome Northumbria Financial Management as a sponsor.

  Andrew Teasdale, managing director and chartered financial planner at Northumbria Financial Management at Hullabaloo 

*(Image: Supplied)*

"Their support will make a real difference to our work, helping us connect with more people and continue delivering memorable and beneficial creative experiences for our audiences.

"It is especially meaningful to work with a business that shares our commitment to nurturing long-term relationships and values positive impact for families and communities in our region."

Theatre Hullabaloo operates The Hullabaloo in Darlington, the only purpose-designed theatre for children and families north of London.

  Andrew Teasdale, managing director and chartered financial planner at Northumbria Financial Management with Emma Rees, executive director and joint CEO at Theatre Hullabaloo 

*(Image: Supplied)*

The venue offers a programme of ticketed performances alongside free creative activities throughout the year.

This autumn’s line-up includes shows such as After Party, a performance for children aged 0-2, and Not Now, Bernard, based on the popular children’s book by David McKee.

The centre will also stage two Christmas productions: The Yule Tree by HurlyBurly, making Christmas magical for little ones through the beauty of opera, and a festive twist on the classic tale of Little Red Riding Hood by Lyngo Theatre in association with Theatre Hullabaloo, where a wardrobe is a magical portal to the wild snowy wood.

  Andrew Teasdale, managing director and chartered financial planner at Northumbria Financial Management at Hullabaloo with 

*(Image: Supplied)*

Northumbria Financial Management (NFM) provides financial planning and wealth management advice to individuals, families, businesses, trusts and charities.

Founded in 1994, the family-run firm focuses on building long-term relationships and holistic financial support.

Their sponsorship will help Theatre Hullabaloo continue to grow its programme, reach new audiences, and create opportunities for people to engage with inspiring, world-class cultural activity in Darlington.

Andrew Teasdale, managing director and chartered financial planner at Northumbria Financial Management, said: "We are delighted to be working with Theatre Hullabaloo.

"As a local business, we want to give back to our local community.

"Upon arriving at the Theatre, we felt a real synergy with the team and their aim of giving young people across the region the space to be creative, imaginative, and most importantly, to make it inclusive for everyone, no matter your background."
