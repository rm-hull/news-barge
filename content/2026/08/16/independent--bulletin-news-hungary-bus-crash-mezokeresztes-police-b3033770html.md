# 12 dead and multiple injured as tourist bus crashes off motorway

![Emergency services attend the scene after a passenger bus went off the road and overturned in a ditch along a highway between Mezokeresztes and Mezonagymihaly in Hungary,](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/16/08/Hungary_Bus_Crash__6318.jpg)

- Twelve people have died and at least 10 others were injured after a Polish tourist bus overturned on a motorway in eastern Hungary.
- The bus was carrying 59 people towards Nyiregyhaza when it veered into a ditch near the town of Mezokeresztes early on Sunday.
- Hungarian police stated that preliminary information suggests the driver likely fell asleep at the wheel, and he is being taken into custody.
- Hungarian Prime Minister Peter Magyar and Polish President Karol Nawrocki expressed their condolences following the tragic crash.
- Poland's Foreign Minister Radoslaw Sikorski confirmed consular services are working alongside Hungarian authorities to support those involved.
