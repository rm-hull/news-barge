### Spider-Man: Brand New Day becomes second-fastest film to make $2 billion at box office

# ‘Spider-Man’ achieves another impressive milestone at the box office

- Marvel blockbuster *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* , starring Tom Holland, has officially surpassed $2 billion at the global box office, grossing $785 million in North America and $1.23 billion internationally to reach a total of $2.022 billion.
- The superhero film achieved this milestone just three weekends after its release, making it the second-fastest movie in history to cross the $2 billion threshold behind 2019's *Avengers: Endgame.* 
- Directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, the film becomes only the eighth movie ever to hit $2 billion worldwide, outperforming its 2021 predecessor, *Spider-Man: No Way Home* , which finished at $1.9 billion.
- The film has earned strong critical acclaim, with *The Independent*'s Clarisse Loughrey awarding it four stars and praising it as the best* Spider-Man* movie in more than 20 years.
- The release features an unusual post-credits scene with no actors on screen, showing a device tracking the superhero and dropping a pin in outer space to hint at his next destination.
