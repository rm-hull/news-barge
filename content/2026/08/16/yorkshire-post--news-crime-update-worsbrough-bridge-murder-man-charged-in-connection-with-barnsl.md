# Update: Worsbrough Bridge murder: Man charged in connection with Barnsley murder

## A man has been charged in connection with a murder on on George Street, in Worsbrough Bridge, Barnsley.

Today (August 16), Andrew Fowler, 60, of George Street, Barnsley, has been charged with murder and has been remanded into custody.

He is due to appear at Barnsley Magistrates’ Court tomorrow (Monday, August 17).

On Friday, August 14, 2026, at 5.45pm, South Yorkshire Police received reports of a missing 62-year-old man from Sheffield. Police officers conducted missing-from-home enquiries following the report.

![A police cordon surrounds crime scene. (Pic credit: Christopher Furlong / Getty Images)](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/webimg/b25lY21zOjhjNTU5OTI3LWU3MDUtNDlmMy05NDcxLWYwN2RjZWQ3ZDM4ZDpkM2YzMTY1MS1iZWYwLTRjYjMtOWY4NC0zYzk4MWM3ZDFkMTI=.jpg?crop=3:2,smart&trim=&width=640&quality=65)



Later that day at 10.16pm, they received reports of a body inside a property in Worsbrough Bridge, Barnsley and police officers, alongside the ambulance service, attended, and located a man. He was pronounced dead at the scene.

A spokesperson for South Yorkshire Police said: “His family are being supported by our Family Liaison Officers. They have asked for their privacy during this difficult time.

“A murder investigation was promptly launched by detectives in our Major Crime Unit, and a 60-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of murder a short time later. He remains in police custody at this time.

“Three scenes are currently in place while officers conduct their work. Two are currently in place on George Street, and one on the TransPennine trial near George Street.”

Detective chief inspector Aneela Khalil-Khan, who is currently leading the investigation, said: “This news will be shocking to the local communities, and not something they expect to be waking up to on a Saturday morning.

“Our officers have moved fast, and have made an arrest. They are now working hard to determine the circumstances around this tragic incident.

“Our officers will be around the Worsbrough area of Barnsley throughout the day and into the evening, conducting several lines of enquiries.

“This is distressing news, so please stop and talk to them if you have some concerns - they will listen and help where they can.”

Anyone with information that could assist with police enquiries is asked to please either call 101 or report it to them online. Please quote incident number 1238 of 14 August 2026 when you get in touch.

As with standard protocol, as the man had previously been reported missing, a self-referral has been made to the Independent Office of Police Conduct (IOPC).
