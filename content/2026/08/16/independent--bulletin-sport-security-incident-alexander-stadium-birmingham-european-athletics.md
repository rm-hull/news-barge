# Arrest made after security incident disrupted European athletics championships

![The security concern sparked chaos at the European Athletics Championships in Birmingham, England on Saturday](https://static.independent.co.uk/2026/08/16/16/49/AP26227705106702.jpg)

- West Midlands Police arrested a man in his 30s on suspicion of fraud after a security incident occurred at Alexander Stadium during the European track-and-field championships in Birmingham on Saturday night.
- The security concern arose around 8:15 p.m. when the suspect was found in an area of the stadium holding the wrong accreditation, prompting authorities to suspend transport for athletes, staff, and fans as a precautionary measure.
- The temporary shutdown resulted in major travel delays for spectators leaving the venue, while scheduled medal ceremonies were postponed until Sunday.
- Organizers and police said there was no immediate threat to the public and apologized for the inconvenience while emphasizing that safety remained their highest priority.
- Governing body European Athletics confirmed that all security issues have been fully resolved and reassured fans that it was safe to attend the concluding session of the championships on Sunday night.
