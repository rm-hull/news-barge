A Teesside takeaway has foodies drooling over their 48-hour dough pizzas, smash burgers and dirty fries.

Dohboys in Norton is putting its own spin on comfort food, serving everything from 48-hour dough pizzas - topped with everything from pepperoni to chilli - to hand-fried chicken burgers and halloumi.

The takeaway spot opened its doors in April 2026 and has quickly established itself as a go-to spot for locals looking for a quick bite.

  Dohboys in Norton is serving up 48-hour pizza dough. 

*(Image: Dohboys)*

Dohboys specialises in pizzas made using 48-hour Italian dough to create a light and airy crust with bubbly charring - then topped with homemade marinara sauce and a freshly grated three-cheese blend.

Pizza lovers can choose from classic favourites like Margerhita and pepperoni pizzas, or they can opt for a more creative blend like the Veggie Deluxe, Chicken Tikka, or the Hotshot

If you're a meat-lover looking for something a little heartier, Dohboys recommend the Bolognese Badboy or Mr Meats pizza.

If you're a little indecisive - or there is too much to choose from - diners can make up a half-and-half pizza so you can get the best of both worlds.


While pizza is at the heart of the menu, Dohboys isn't shy about other favourites on the menu like their smash burgers or stir-fried donner.

There's a range of sides to enjoy alongside your pizza or burger, including dirty fries and chilli cheese bites.

The takeaway prides itself on using fresh ingredients and making their food by hand - creating flavour-packed food and a quick, convenient experience for their customers.

Since opening earlier this year, Dohboys has become one of the latest additions to Stockton's growing food scene.

Dohboys is open daily from 4pm until 11pm on Norton Road.
