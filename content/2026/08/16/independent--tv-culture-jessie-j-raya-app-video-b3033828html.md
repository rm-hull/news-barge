# Jessie J responds to dating rumors as she tells fans ‘it’s time to tell you the truth’

Jessie J has responded to speculation about her love life in a tongue-in-cheek video.

The singer announced she and partner Chanan Safir Colman had split after five years together, in a statement last month.

The 38-year-old has now responded to reports she has been spotted on dating app Raya, in an Instagram video, posted on Saturday (16 August).

Telling fans, “it’s time to tell you the truth”, the mother-of-one said: “I updated and reactivated my Raya account on a dating app. I did. For two days, I surrendered. I just got excited and I updated my pictures,” she said, before adding that she has deactivated her account.

“I won’t do it again.”
