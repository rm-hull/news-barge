“Concerned” police believe a missing woman who vanished more than two weeks ago may have travelled to London.

Tracey Smith was last seen in the Stokesley area on Friday, July 24.

North Yorkshire Police said it remains "very concerned” for the welfare of the 57-year-old, who is 5ft 5in tall, of slim build with brown shoulder length hair.

It is not known what Tracey, who also has a small mole above her lip, was wearing when she was last seen.

And now, the force has said that it believes there's a possibility she may have travelled to the London area.

She may be travelling in her car, a black Fiat Panda, registration number WR10 ETK.

A force spokesperson said: “We are appealing to anyone who may have seen Tracey or her vehicle to get in touch as a matter of urgency.

"If you have seen Tracey or her car, or know where she is now, please call us on 999. If you have any other information that could help us find her, please call us on 101.

“Please quote reference 12260154127 when passing on information.”
