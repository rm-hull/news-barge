## Stunning locations within easy reach of York captured in 11 great photos

Isaac Savage captured Holgate Windmill. *(Image: Isaac Savage)*

Our gallery celebrates the variety and beauty of days out within driving distance of York.

With summer in full swing, we chose #outandabout for this month’s theme for The Press Camera Club and members have been sharing some of their favourite places from fresh perspectives.

  Helmsley Walled Garden by Judith Ness. 

*(Image: Judith Ness)*

From the peaceful paths and colourful planting of Helmsley Walled Garden to Knaresborough Viaduct rising dramatically above the River Nidd, this selection also features the bracing coastal beauty on the walk from Thornwick Bay to North Landing at Flamborough, where wide skies meet chalk cliffs and the North Sea.

  Sandra Michelle Davies captured Knaresborough Viaduct when darkness fell. 

*(Image: Sandra Michelle Davies)*

  **Recommended reads:**

  Christine Hainsworth took this photo of North Landing on a walk along the coastal path from Thornwick Bay to Flamborough. 

*(Image: Christine Hainsworth)*

Closer to home, York Minster appears twice: its remarkable Five Sisters Window offers a quiet moment of historic detail, while a view from Exhibition Square shows the cathedral dominating the city skyline.

  The Five Sisters Window in York Minster by Sandra Michelle Davies. 

*(Image: Sandra Michelle Davies)*

Elsewhere, a high vantage point frames Holgate Windmill against a spectacular sunset, turning one of York’s best-known landmarks into a striking silhouette.

Finally, a wooden carved feature at Askham Bog, celebrating its 80th anniversary, provides a reminder of the wildlife-rich green space preserved just beyond the city centre.

  Sue Gabbatiss was at Askham Bog which has celebrated its 80th anniversary. 

*(Image: Sue Gabbatiss)*

## Join our Camera Club

Would you like to see your photographs in The Press and online?

More than 3,000 readers have joined The Press Camera Club, which launched in June 2017 and brings together talented photographers from across York and North and East Yorkshire to share their work, swap tips and take part in themed monthly prize competitions.

To join the free club, simply search for ‘The Press Camera Club’ on Facebook - we’d love to see what our region looks like through your lens.

We will feature pictures from our camera club in The Press and online regularly.
