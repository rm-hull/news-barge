A crash is causing jams on a coastal road this afternoon (Sunday).

It’s on the A171, close to Fylingdales and the Robin Hood’s Bay turn-off.

Emergency services are on the scene.

#### Crash location

North Yorkshire Police said at 4.44pm: “Please avoid the A171 whilst we deal with a collision near the Robin Hood’s Bay turn off on Robin Hood’s Bay Road.

“Please use alternative routes where possible while emergency services work at the scene.

“Thank you for your understanding.”
