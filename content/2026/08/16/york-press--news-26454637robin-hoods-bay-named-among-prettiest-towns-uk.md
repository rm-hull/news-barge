A popular seaside town in North Yorkshire has been named one of the prettiest towns in the UK.

World Atlas has named the 12 prettiest small towns across the UK, with Robin Hood’s Bay making it onto the list.

Other areas, including Castle Combe and Rye, alongside other seaside towns like St Ives, were mentioned.

The "vibrant" area has a sandy beach, cobbled streets, and many pubs and cafés to enjoy.


## Why is Robin Hood’s Bay a great place to visit?

On the town, World Atlas said: "Robin Hood's Bay appears almost as if it has been folded into the hillside above the North Sea.

"Narrow passages wind between old stone cottages before dropping toward the shoreline, reflecting the village's origins as a fishing community where buildings were packed closely together for protection from coastal weather.

"The steep streets and changing views make even a short walk feel different depending on the direction, with small courtyards, hidden corners, and glimpses of the sea appearing between the houses.

"At low tide, the beach reveals rock pools and fossils beneath the cliffs, while the surrounding coastline attracts walkers following the Cleveland Way National Trail, one of England's best-known long-distance walking routes.


"The village's old fishing cottages now house cafés, pubs, and independent shops, including The Old School House, a café set inside a converted historic building, and The Laurel Inn, a traditional pub overlooking the winding streets.

"Nearby, Robin Hood's Bay Museum explores the village's maritime past and smuggling history, adding context to the narrow lanes and old stone buildings.

"By evening, many residents and visitors make their way toward the shoreline, where the sound of the waves and village lights create a quieter side of this dramatic coastal community."

The sandy beach is well worth a visit, with rock pools to explore and fossils to hunt.

The beach has an interesting history, as it used to be used by smugglers, with it being believed that there are underground passages beneath some of the sold houses in the area.

Reviewers on Tripadvisor have said the beach is "secluded" and "beautifully quiet," making it a nice spot for a picnic.


For food, you could visit the Cove, which is a little café with wonderful views of the sea that you can enjoy while eating a stacked ciabatta or slice of cake.

If you want the full seaside experience, you can go to the Fish Box, an "express style" food spot where you can get classic fish and chips to eat outside on the picnic tables overlooking the sea.
