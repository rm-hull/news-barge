A North Yorkshire farm shop praised for its free-range meat, local produce, and friendly staff has been described as a "hidden gem" by customers who say it is well worth making a detour for.

Farm Gate Farm Shop and Free Range Butchery, near the A1(M) and A19, holds a 4.9-star rating on Google from dozens of reviews.

Visitors regularly praise the shop for its selection of locally sourced produce, including free-range meat, British cheeses, bread, milk, and other Yorkshire products.

One recent customer described the farm shop as "superb" and said its range included cheeses that could be difficult to find elsewhere.

They said: "The meat is free range and you know what you get is quality.

"A wide selection of different cheeses, some of which are difficult to find elsewhere.

"Steve and all the team are so welcoming and accommodating to our requests.

"Well worth a detour if you are visiting the area."

Another visitor praised the knowledgeable staff after picking up cheese and steaks, describing it as a "great little farm shop".

The business has also won fans for its focus on local and organic food.

One customer said: "Fresh, organic and tasty food.

"And they have the nicest staff!

"Locally sourced produce."

Another called Farm Gate an "absolutely fantastic local artisan and organic shop", praising its selection of organic flour, British cheeses, free-range meat, and locally produced drinks.

They added that the staff were friendly, knowledgeable, and clearly cared about the products they sold.

The welcoming atmosphere is another recurring feature in reviews.

One customer who visited with her sister and young son said staff even showed the youngster the lambs during their trip.

She wrote: "Well, what can I say other than 'amazing'.

"The staff were exceptionally welcoming and helpful."

For some customers, an unexpected first visit has turned the shop into a regular destination.

One reviewer said: "What a find this little place was.

"Having happened upon it by chance a few months ago, it is now one of our go-to places to find the very best authentic locally produced food."

Another customer said they had been shopping there for more than a year and praised its granola, coffee, cheese, bread, flour, and meat, as well as a Christmas chicken they described as "gorgeous".

Its approach to animal welfare and sustainability has also been highlighted by visitors.

One reviewer described the farm shop as a "gem" and said a lamb joint bought there at Easter had been "unsurpassed in flavour and tenderness".

Long-standing customers have also praised the family-run business.

One reviewer said they had been using Farm Gate Farm Shop for more than ten years, while another said they had received weekly milk and butter deliveries for eight years without being let down.

The latter wrote: "Reliable, consistent, and genuinely helpful whenever you get in touch.

"The kind of local business that does everything right."

Other products mentioned by visitors include locally produced milk, bacon and pancetta cured by the business, seasonal venison, and a selection of Yorkshire goods.

One reviewer said they had followed signs from the Malton road to reach the shop and found the journey worthwhile.

Another customer, who discovered it while on holiday, said they planned to return despite living around 50 minutes away.

They said they had been particularly impressed by the farm shop's own meat and its extensive cheese selection.
