## ‘Beautiful’ York home that’s been ‘thoughtfully’ upgraded for sale – look inside

The home for sale in Newborough Street, Clifton, York for £280,000 *(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

A “beautiful” York period home that has been “thoughtfully” upgraded has hit the market.

The three-bedroom end-terrace house is for sale in Newborough Street, Clifton, for £280,000.

Ashtons, which is marketing the property, said the home's end-terrace location means it has “wider proportions than many neighbouring properties”.

The home also comes with a separate single storey annexe that is currently operated as a popular Airbnb, the estate agent said.

  Inside the three-bedroom end-terrace house for sale in Newborough Street, Clifton, York, for £280,000 

*(Image: Ashtons/Zoopla)*

Inside the main house, a living room is to the front of the property with a dining room and modern kitchen to the rear.

Two double bedrooms and family bathroom are on the first floor.
