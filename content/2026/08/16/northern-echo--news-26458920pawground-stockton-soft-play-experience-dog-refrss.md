Dog owners looking for a unique day out with their four-legged friends are flocking to a Teesside attraction that offers something a little different.

The Pawground, on Portrack Lane in Stockton, has become a hit with pet owners thanks to its obstacle-filled soft play packed with slides, nets, wobble bridges and climbing equipment created especially for canine visitors.

The venue combines an indoor soft play area with an outdoor playground, giving dogs plenty of opportunities to run, climb and explore in a secure environment.

Even more pet owners are jumping on the soft play train after TikTok influencer @newgirlintoon shared her experience with the Pawground last month.


Each 56-minute session gives owners exclusive use of the space, allowing dogs to play without interruptions from unfamiliar pets - making it perfect for those with dogs that aren't too keen on socialising.

The soft play has proved so popular that bookings are snapped up months in advance, with facilities currently fully booked until February 2027.

Owners can bring up to three dogs as part of the standard £25 booking fee, with additional dogs welcome for extra - making it a popular option for friends looking to arrange playdates with their pets.

The fully enclosed play area means dogs can roam freely while owners relax, and every visit ends with a complimentary pup cup as a reward for all that exercise.

In the heart of Stockton, the Pawground is one of several attractions getting attention from dog lovers across the North East that are eager to find new ways to keep their pets active and entertained.

The Pawground is open on weekends between 9.00am and 5.00pm, with online booking essential due to the high demand.

For owners looking to make a full day of it, dog-friendly restaurant Chadwick & Co, located inside Barker and Stonehouse at Teesside Park, is just a short drive away.

With its combination of private play sessions, colourful obstacles and secure surroundings, The Pawground has become one of the more unusual attractions for dog owners and their pets in the region.

To find out more about the venue, visit: The Pawground Ltd.
