Four people got stuck in a lift in a property near York city centre just after midnight triggering an emergency call for help.

The alarm was raised at 12.29am this morning, Sunday, Sunday 17, and York firefighters went to the scene on Redeness Street.

A spokesperson for North Yorkshire Fire & Rescue Service said all the people were free from the lift on the crew's arrival.
