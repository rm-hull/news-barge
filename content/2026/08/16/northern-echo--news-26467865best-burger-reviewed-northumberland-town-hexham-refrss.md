A viral food reviewer claims to have discovered 'the best burger' in the centre of a Northumberland town.

Kyle V Food, a content creator who makes honest reviews and completes food challenges, visited Hexham to try The Smashing Burger on Priestpopple.

He reviewed the £17.95 milkshake meal deal, which includes any burger, any milkshake or ice cream sundae, fries and sauce.

It's named 'The Smashing Burger' as they use the technique of searing the outside of the meat, which locks in all the juices and flavour.


Kyle got the classic smash burger, mango milkshake, fries with sauce, and some cookie dough to finish.

He described the smash burger as having a 'lovely caramelisation' and was a 'solid burger'.

  The smashing burger was 'really good, simple and classic' 

*(Image: Kyle V Food)*

"A very good, simple, classic smash burger," Kyle said, moving onto the 'lovely' mango milkshake, which he said tasted like a 'more creamy, thick mango lassi'.

He added it 'might have been one of the best milkshakes' he'd ever tried in his life.

The Oreo cookie dough dessert was not part of the meal deal, but was another 'really good' item on the menu.

"Honestly guys, a top-notch burger spot here in Hexham. Get yourself down. It's a lovely smashed burger, can't go wrong," he added.
