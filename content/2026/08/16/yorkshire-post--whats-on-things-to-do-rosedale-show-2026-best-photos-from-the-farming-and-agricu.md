The show was founded in 1871, held in Milburn Arms Field at Rosedale Abbey and run by the Agricultural, Horticultural and Industrial Society.

The Rosedale Show was held on August 15, 2026, and includes cattle, goats, heavy horses, ponies, local produce, crafts, vintage tractors and more.
